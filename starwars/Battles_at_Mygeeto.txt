By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] fought a series of '''battles''' at<ref name="Battlefront" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Mygeeto">{{Databank|mygeeto|Mygeeto}}</ref> [[Mygeeto]].<ref name="Battlefront" /> The Alliance's [[Riot Squadron]] also fought there, during which the [[squadron]] earned its name.<ref name="Alphabet" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY">{{BTCdate}}</ref> the 61st [[Rebel trooper|soldiers]] [[Roja]] and [[Beak (individual)|Beak]] reminisced of the fighting at Mygeeto in the presence of [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]], who had joined the [[company]] after Mygeeto, while the three were stationed at a [[Echo Base|rebel base]] on the [[Water|ice]] planet [[Hoth]].<ref name="Battlefront" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="4 ABY">{{AlphabetSquadronDate}}</ref> members of Riot Squadron recounted the fighting at Mygeeto during their [[time]] on the planet [[Jiruus]].<ref name="Alphabet" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battles at Mygeeto were first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
