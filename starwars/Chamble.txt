'''Chamble''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="TLF" /> in the [[Chamble system]] in the [[Mytaranor sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]], in grid square Q-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> A part of the [[Galactic Republic]], it was represented by a [[senator]].<ref name="TLF" /> In [[33 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{InsiderCite|224|Launchpad}} dates the events of ''[[The Living Force]]'' to a [[Standard year|year]] prior to {{Film|I}}, which equates to [[33 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]] discussed with [[Naboo]] [[Senator]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and [[Jedi Master]]s [[Yoda]] and [[Adi Gallia]] an ongoing uprising on Chamble deemed to have been caused by a local police strike. When Gallia mentioned sending [[Jedi]] negotiators there, Palpatine mentioned that Chamble's senator only wanted protection for local businesses. When Yoda pointed out that the Jedi were not [[police officer]]s, Valorum stated his belief that only a visit was required, with Palpatine suggesting a training mission for show. Gallia promptly made a note before the group moved on to other business.<ref name="TLF" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Chamble was mentioned in ''[[The Living Force]]'', a [[2024]] novel written by [[John Jackson Miller]].<ref name="TLF" />
