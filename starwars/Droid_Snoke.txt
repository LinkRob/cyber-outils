'''Droid Snoke''' was a [[humanoid]] [[Gender|male]] [[Cloning|clone]] of [[Snoke]] who was created by the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Religion|cult]] on [[Exegol]]. His cloning process was unsuccessful which left him believing that he was a [[droid]].<ref name="TSS" />

==Biography==
{{Quote|I had hoped to perfect the cloning practices of our dearly departed Emperor, but the only thing I have perfected is&hellip; FAILURE! My clones of clones are nothing but cheap imitations! Cheap, dangerous and—perhaps most importantly—running amok in our fair citadel!|A Sith scientist, to an individual|<ref name="TSS" />}}
Created on Exegol, Droid Snoke was a failed clone of Snoke. The cloning process caused him to think he was a droid, a defect labeling him a reject in the eyes of a [[Sith cultist|Sith]] [[scientist]]. The scientist later spoke with an individual who sought out and defeated Droid Snoke at the scientist's request.<ref name="TSS" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Frustration reaching maximum capacity! You have beeped your last boop!|Droid Snoke|<ref name="TSS" />}}
Droid Snoke shared his physical appearance with his clone template, [[Supreme Leader]] Snoke, including blue eyes and white eyebrow hair. Droid Snoke maintained a belief he was a droid, often integrating the words "beep" and boop" into his sentences.<ref name="TSS" />

==Powers and abilities==
{{Quote|Initiating "spilt-in-half" protocol! Nghhh&hellip; NGHHH! ERROR. Action not supported by flesh based chassis.|Droid Snoke|<ref name="TSS" />}}
Droid Snoke was capable in melee combat. As an organic being, he was unable to split in half.<ref name="TSS" />

==Equipment==
Droid Snoke wore [[Color|gold]] robes identical to those worn by the original Snoke.<ref name="TSS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Droid Snoke first appeared in the [[2022]] [[canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', in the side mission "Attack of the Clones (Again)".<ref name="TSS" />
