A '''unit''' of [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] served under<ref name="Legacy 7" /> [[Jedi General]]<ref name="Note" /> [[Grandea]] and the [[Jedi]] [[Alivar]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]].<ref name="Legacy 7" /> At the end of the war in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the two Jedi and their unit were stationed on a terrestrial astronomical object engaging entrenched Separatist forces. From their headquarters, Alivar and Grandea planned on how to break through those lines when [[Order 66]],<ref name="Legacy 7" /> an [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|executive order]] from the [[Supreme Chancellor]] marking the Jedi as traitors to be executed. The unit managed to eliminate Alivar, though they suffered several losses, while Grandea managed to escape after using the [[mind trick]] on two clones to escape. Members of the unit were equipped with [[phase II clone trooper armor]] with [[Color|blue]] markings and dual [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster]]s on [[holster]]s. [[Unidentified clone trooper (Grandea)|One clone officer]] donned additional [[armor]].<ref name="Legacy 7" />
