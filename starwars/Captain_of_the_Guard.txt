'''Captain of the Guard''',<ref name="USW" /> also written as '''captain of the guard''',<ref name="CVD" /> was the title held by the [[captain]] of the [[Black Sun Guard]],<ref name="USW" /> the captain of the [[Zygerrian Royal Guard|Zygerrian]]<ref name="Databank" /> and [[Royal guard (Pijal)|Pijal Royal guard]]<ref name="M&A">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> and captain of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Senate Guard]],<ref name="HC 30" /> as well as being held by some individuals who served as the [[commanding officer]] of a military unit.<ref name="DepthsofDark" /> 

==History==
The [[Pyke]] [[Captain]] [[Kalt]] served as the Captain of the Guard for the [[Pyke Syndicate]]<ref name="PaV">{{MSL|Chapter 4: Pride and Vengeance}}</ref> at some point between [[17 BBY]] and [[13 BBY]].<ref name="ShadowLordDate">{{ShadowLordDate}}</ref> After [[Maul]] [[Assassination of Marg Krim|murdered]] Pyke [[Crime lord|crime boss]] [[Marg Krim]], the former [[Sith Lord]] promoted Kalt to leader of all the Pyke forces.<ref name="PaV" />

The [[mercenary]] [[Lang]] served as Captain of the Guard for [[Magistrate]] [[Morgan Elsbeth]]'s forces, commanding the defense of the walled city of [[Corvus]].<ref name="DB Captain Lang" /> The [[Night Trooper]] [[Enoch]], who was further identified by the rank of captain itself,<ref name="Part Seven">{{Ahsoka|Part Seven: Dreams and Madness}}</ref> served as Captain of the Guard for [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]]<ref name="Part Six">{{Ahsoka|Part Six: Far, Far Away}}</ref> by [[9 ABY]].<ref name="AhsokaDate">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> In that capacity, Enoch served as the [[commander]] of Thrawn's [[Night Trooper]]s,<ref name="DepthsofDark" /> the remaining [[stormtrooper]]s who survived in [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] Thrawn and the personnel aboard his [[flagship]], the ''[[Chimaera]]'', had found themselves in after being banished to a [[Far galaxy|distant galaxy]] by a pod of [[purrgil]] commanded by [[Ezra Bridger]].<ref name="Part Six">{{Ahsoka|Part Six: Far, Far Away}}</ref>

When aboard a [[starship]] of the [[First Order Navy]], [[First Order stormtrooper]]s would follow a captain of the guard as their ultimate authority. Despite this special chain of command, stormtroopers would follow the commands of any [[officer]], regardless of their [[First Order military|military]] branch, during an alert.<ref name="CVD" />
