'''Distilon''' was a [[Planet|world]] that was the site of a [[Battle of Distilon|battle]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="FACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=Rendezvous Point (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> between [[2 BBY]] and [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">"[[Rendezvous Point (short story)|Rendezvous Point]]" establishes that the [[Battle of Distilon|battle]] at Distilon took place after the [[Escape from Skystrike Academy|defection]] of [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Derek Klivian]] and prior to Klivian's [[death]] during the [[Battle of Hoth]]. {{HelmetCollectionCite|57|Highlights of the Saga}} dates their defection to [[2 BBY]], while ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the Battle of Hoth in [[3 ABY]]. The battle at Distilon must have therefore taken place between those [[Standard year|years]].</ref> [[Rebel pilot]]s [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Derek Klivian]], who had previously [[Escape from Skystrike Academy|defected]] from the Empire, participated in the fighting at Distilon.<ref name="FACPOV" />
