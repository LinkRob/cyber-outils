'''Bounty Lime Soda''' was a type of [[Color|green]]-colored [[soda]] produced by the [[Mandalorian Bottling Co.]] It was advertised as being stowed in [[Carbonite]] for freshness and described as having an "out of [[The galaxy|this galaxy]] taste." The bottle featured the skull of a [[Mythosaur]] and a [[Mandalorian]] [[Mandalorian armor/Legends|helmet]].<ref name="SWCT" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bounty Lime Soda featured in the [[2023]] [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]] set ''Star Wars: Card Trader Illustrated - 2023'' on the card named "Bounty Lime Soda."<ref name="SWCT" />
