'''Bev Kranti clams''' were a [[species]] of [[clam]] from the [[planet]] [[Chandel]] that produced valuable [[Bev Kranti pearl|pearls]] over the course of 150 [[Standard year|years]].

==Description==
Bev Kranti clams could be found buried beneath the [[ocean]]s<ref name="Outlaws" /> on the [[planet]]<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/the-future-of-the-force-trivia-gallery|text="The Future of the Force" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> [[Chandel]]. They were small with a [[Color|grey]] pitted shell. A clam could produce several [[Bev Kranti pearl|pearls]] at once but it would take 150 [[Standard year|years]] which made the pearls very valuable to [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==History==
In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Danka Orsato]] [[contract broker|brokered]] the sell of a Bev Kranti clam from the [[Pyke Syndicate]] to the [[Ashiga Clan]]. When Danka got word that the [[Pyke]]'s were instead going to sell the clam to [[Crimson Dawn]] she recruited the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] to retrieve it from the Pykes. However the Pykes had not actually intended to sell the clam to the Dawn and instead were trying to con them.<ref name="Outlaws" />

Vess and her companion [[ND-5]] approached the meeting which occured in the [[Mirage (Toshara)|Mirage]] region of the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] as it was starting and stated that they were there to collect on behalf of Danka. Realizing the Pyles were trying to double deal, the Crimson Dawn representatives began a fire fight. The fight resulted in the [[death]] of all the members of the Pykes and Crimson Dawn that were present. After surviving the fight Vess and ND-5 searched the speeders which directed them to the location of the clam, which was in a [[Pyke Syndicate workshop|starship workshop]] nearby.<ref name="Outlaws" />

Vess proceeded to enter the workshop and retrieve the clam. After which she contacted Danka who directed her to meet at [[Daruda Diner]] in the [[city|town]] [[Jaunta's Hope]] on Toshara. Once there she proceeded down stairs to the diner's [[cantina|speakeasy]] and discovered that Danka was selling the clam to an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] officer. Vess was able to palm a single [[pearl]] while the officer inspected the clam. The officer demanded a bribe from Vess otherwise she would call in an alert for her. After the exchange Danka asked to meet her at [[Makal's Gambling Parlor]] in the [[capital]] city [[Mirogana]]. When Vess arrived, Danka revealed that she knew that Vess had taken one of the pearls and she could broker a sale to Crimson Dawn or the Ashiga Clan.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bev Kranti clams first appeared in [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' which was published by [[Ubisoft]] and developed by [[Massive Entertainment]]. The quest has two choices for the player to decide which changes how things progress. The first is whether to pay the bribe to the Imperial officer. If the player doesn't pay it, they will be assigned a wanted level of four and will have to evade the Empire. The second choice is if and who to sell the pearl to. The player can choose to keep the pearl which sells for 2000 credits, or give it to one of the two syndicates in exchange for increased reputation with the chosen syndicate.<ref name="Outlaws" />
