'''''Star Wars Rebels: Battle to the End''''' is the fourth entry in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' [[Star Wars Rebels chapter books|chapter book series]] from [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]. It was released on [[June 9]], [[2015]]. The book is an adaptation of the finale of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of ''Star Wars Rebels''.

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''This action-packed chapter book retells the exciting final arc of Star Wars Rebels' first season. As the stakes rise higher than ever before, the Rebels must work together to thwart the Empire. If they fail, the consequences are unthinkable. Stuffed with full-color images of all the characters, this book's eye-catching design is sure to stand out at retail.''<ref name="Disney" />

==Plot summary==
{{Plot|adaptation}}

==Continuity==
Michael Kogge's ''Battle to the End'' comprises elements of the ''Star Wars Rebels'' season-one finale episodes "[[Call to Action]]," "[[Rebel Resolve]]," and "[[Fire Across the Galaxy]]." While the action largely follows material presented on screen, the title occasionally provides insights into the minds of the characters, such as [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] imagining himself in the place of either the executed [[Cumberlayne Aresko]] or [[Myles Grint]], and [[JJR-579]] questioning the loyalty of his fellow stormtrooper [[JTN-303]].
