'''Alkin Neret''' was a [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]] who served under [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] and studied the powers of the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]].<ref name="Dark Tremors" />

When Palpatine was still a [[Senator/Legends|senator]], Neret and him discovered a lost [[Sith tower of Alkin Neret|Sith tower]] on [[Rimk]]. Palpatine allowed Neret to use the tower as a home in exchange for the dark secrets it contained.<ref name="Dark Tremors" />

Some years later, the captured [[Mon Calamari/Legends|Mon Calamari]] [[Sileski]] was handed over to Neret by Emperor Palpatine.<ref name="Dark Tremors" />
