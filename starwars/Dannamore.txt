'''Dannamore''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Age of Rebellion" /> in the [[Saijo sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where the [[orgo-harvest facility]] was located. Natives of the [[Unidentified planet (Fantanines)|planet]] where the [[Fantanine]]s operated a [[Fantanine mining colony|mining colony]] were to be shipped to the orgo-harvest facility on Dannamore, but [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], who had been offered ownership of the [[mining]] colony by [[Roz Fantanine]], freed them, refusing the offer.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
