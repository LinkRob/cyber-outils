'''Aridus''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> located in the [[Aridus system]], a [[star system]] in the [[Narvath sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> along the [[Gamor Run]].<ref name="Gamor" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], it was under [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] control and [[Republic High Command]] sent at least one [[commando]] [[squad]] to Aridus in hopes of coordinating with [[Aridus freedom fighters|local freedom fighters]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" />
