"'''Best of Hyperspace'''" originally titled '''''Leap Into Hyperspace''''' (in [[Star Wars Insider 70|''Star Wars Insider'' 70]], [[Star Wars Insider 71|71]], [[Star Wars Insider 73|73]]-[[Star Wars Insider 76|76]]), was a regular column appearing in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]''. It featured highlights of material first published online on [[StarWars.com]]'s [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]].

It was later replaced by ''[[Best of StarWars.com]]'' starting with [[Star Wars Insider 93|''Star Wars Insider'' 93]].
