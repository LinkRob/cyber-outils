'''Burrow-crawlers''' were a type of creature.<ref name="ACS">{{StoryCite|story=A Coruscant Solstice|book=Life Day Treasury}}</ref> In around [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/life-day-treasury-exclusive-reveal|text=Celebrate the ''Star Wars'' Galaxy's Greatest Holiday with ''Life Day Treasury'' - Exclusive Reveal}} states that "A Coruscant Solstice" takes place during the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' project, which {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-high-republic|text=Lucasfilm to Launch ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Publishing Campaign in 2021}} places 200 [[Standard year|years]] before [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places in [[32 BBY]]. Therefore, "A Coruscant Solstice" takes place around the same time.</ref> the [[thief]] [[Nittel Karkson]] hesitated and froze like a burrow-crawler in headlights when he realized he was being pursued by [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] through the alleyways of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="ACS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Burrow-crawlers were mentioned in "[[A Coruscant Solstice]]," a short story written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in the [[2021]] ''[[Life Day Treasury]]'' anthology.<ref name="ACS" />
