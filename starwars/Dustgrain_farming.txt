'''Dustgrain farming''' was a method of [[farm]]ing carried out by the [[Aki-Aki]], the [[Homeworld|native]] [[species]] of the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Pasaana]]. The Aki-Aki used [[electro-sifter]]s to catch [[seed]]s and spores blown by the planet's desert winds. The sifters also used [[moisture vaporator]] technology to irrigate the arid ground, making it suitable for farming.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
