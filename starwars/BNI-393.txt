'''BNI-393''' was an [[LEP-series service droid]] who served within the [[Republic Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Nicknamed '''Bunny''' by her [[BNI-393's master|master]], BNI-393 was stationed aboard a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] the ''[[Renown]]'' at some point in the war, where she served alongside several other Republic [[droid]]s in the warship's maintenance.<ref name="PoNR" />

An [[LEP-series service droid]],<ref name="PoNR" /> stood at 1.26 [[meter]]s in height.<ref name="DB" /> The droid also retained blue-colored plating alongside yellow sensors. She cared about her companions like the [[WED Treadwell repair droid]] and tried to save the unit when B1 battle droids shot him despite WAC stopping her.<ref name="PoNR" />
