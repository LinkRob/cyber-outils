'''Delphidians''' were a group of related [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] from the [[Delphidian Cluster]], a [[Star|stellar]] nursery on the fringes of [[Wild Space]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. The Delphidian all had finely striated,<ref name="TFA" /> [[Color|dark gray]],<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> or midnight-black<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> skin with a leathery texture<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> and black [[eye]]s<ref name="VE" /> but varied in their other physical characteristics. As such some [[geneticist]]s thought that they shared a long-lost common ancestry, while others believed they were bioengineered as [[Slavery|slaves]] by some vanished master, though the Delphidians themselves were silent on the subject. The species were part of a brawling confederation founded [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Galactic Republic]] discovered their stars. The individual Delphidian species spoke a vast number of dialects and relied on a [[Delphidian (language)|common tongue]] that evolved for trade within the Delphidian Cluster.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jorinda Boffrey]] was a Delphidian [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Out of the Shadows">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref> [[Sidon Ithano]] was a Delphidian [[pirate]] [[captain]] who hid his heritage by wearing a bright red [[Kaleesh]] [[helmet]]. Ithano and another Delphidian, [[Durteel Haza]], were both present in the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]] shortly before it was [[Battle of Takodana|attacked]] by the [[First Order]] around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Delphidian species first appeared in the form of Sidon Ithano in the short story "[[The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku]]," which was written by [[Landry Q. Walker]] and released in [[2015]]. Ithano went on to appear in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], released later that year. The species was not identified in either source, but received its name in ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="TFAVD" /> Hidalgo later stated on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that Durteel Haza, another character who also appeared in Maz Kanata's castle in the film, was also a Delphidian.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/719622799183253504|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=@FN1017 yes! That's him.|archivefile=File:Delphidian confirmation.png}} {{C|In response to a fan asking if a screenshotted character from Maz Kanata's castle is another Delphidian.}}</ref>
