'''Brandon Wainerdi''' is a writer who is a contributor to ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' and [[StarWars.com]]. He also created a ''[[Star Wars]]'' podcast dedicated to interviews with the cast and crew of ''Star Wars'': Talking Bay 94.<ref name="SW.com" />

He is one of the authors of the reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' published by [[Dorling Kindersley]].<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> He is also the author of Dorling Kindersley's second edition of [[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles'']].<ref name="Penguin">{{Penguin|url=books/787846/star-wars-encyclopedia-of-starfighters-and-other-vehicles-by-brandon-wainerdi|text=Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles}}</ref>
