'''Celestials''' were a group known for their singing. During a [[job on Kullgroon]], in [[10 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/solo-a-star-wars-story-books-and-comics-revealed|text=''Solo: A Star Wars Story'' Books and Comics Revealed}} places ''[[Star Wars: Lando - Double or Nothing]]'' right before ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', which ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' places nine [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|founding]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, ''Star Wars: Lando - Double or Nothing'' takes place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] compared the singing voice of [[Naaruu]], a individual who threatened Lando, who Lando subsequently [[Death|killed]] and took his hide as a [[cape]], to the sweetest of Celestials.<ref name="Double or Nothing II" />

==Behind the scenes==
Celestials where introduced into [[canon]] with a mention in the [[2018]]<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/69611/star_wars_lando_-_double_or_nothing_2018_2|text=Star Wars: Lando - Double or Nothing (2018) #2}}</ref> comic [[Lando - Double or Nothing 2|''Lando - Double or Nothing'' 2]],<ref name="Double or Nothing II" /> written by [[Rodney Barnes]]<ref name="Announcement">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/movies/solo-star-wars-book-connections/solo-but-far-from-alone|text=Here's how a galaxy of new books will connect to Solo: A Star Wars Story|archivedate=20180815233216|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2018-02-16|nolive=1}}</ref> and pencilled by [[Paolo Villanelli]].<ref name="Artist">{{Twitter|HeatherAntos|status/964535523388874752|[[Heather Antos]]|quote=That's right, folks, we've got a BRAND-NEW #StarWars mini coming out! LANDO - DOUBLE OR NOTHING by Rodney Barnes & Paolo Villanelli coming to your local comic shop this MAY!|archivedate=20200814212104}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Celestial/Legends|Celestials]] were first mentioned, albeit unnamed, in the [[1983]] novel ''[[Lando Calrissian and the Mindharp of Sharu]]'', written by [[L. Neil Smith]].<ref name="LCatMoS">''[[Lando Calrissian and the Mindharp of Sharu]]''</ref> Celestials were first named by [[Troy Denning]] in his [[2005]] novel ''[[Dark Nest I: The Joiner King]]''.<ref name="Dark Nest">''[[Dark Nest I: The Joiner King]]''</ref> In the ''Legends'' continuity, [[the Ones]], who made their first appearance in [[Overlords]],<ref name="Overlords">{{TCW|Overlords}}</ref> a [[2011]] episode of [[Season Three]] of the TV series [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]],<ref name="Overlords Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode315.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Overlords}}</ref> were identified as Celestials in the [[Star Wars Blog]] article "[[Galactic Architecture 101 and the History of Centerpoint Station]],"<ref name="Centerpoint">{{SW|url=news/the-world-puller-the-history-of-centerpoint-station-needs-author-entry|text=Galactic Architecture 101 and the History of Centerpoint Station|int=Galactic Architecture 101 and the History of Centerpoint Station|nolive=1}}</ref> but no canonical connection has been made between the Ones and Celestials.
