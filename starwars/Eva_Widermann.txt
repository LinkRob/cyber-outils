'''Eva Widermann''' is a German [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] artist and concept artist. She was an illustrator for the magazine series [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine]], the comic strips [[Secrets of Sienar]], [[The Line of Duty]], [[Academy Cadets]] and [[A Day's Duty]]. She also designed the character, [[Reann Tomvig]].{{Mediacat|imagecat=Images by Eva Widermann}}
