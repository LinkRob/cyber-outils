'''Dean''' was a title used at universities, such as [[Reena University]],<ref name="QueensBloom" /> the [[Shadow University]],<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12" /> and the [[University of Bar'leth]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 19" /> [[Ry Harket]] served as dean of student life at Reena University<ref name="QueensBloom" /> around [[396 BBY]].<ref name="Bloom Date">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' establishes that [[Axel Greylark]] was eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old thirteen years prior to its events. As ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''Convergence'' to [[382 BBY]], he must have been [[Reproduction|born]] in [[413 BBY]]. Greylark is seventeen years old in "[[The Queen's Bloom]]," dating its events to around [[396 BBY]].</ref> A [[Unidentified Shadow University dean|human]] was the dean of the Shadow University<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12" /> around [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{Aphra2016Date|12}}</ref>
