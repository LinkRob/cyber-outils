'''Beldons''' were a large [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]]. They lived in the [[planet]]'s lower [[atmosphere]] and could be found in large herds. 

==Biology and appearance==
They used the long tendrils on their bodies to gather atmospheric [[plankton]] and chemicals. These became [[tibanna]] [[gas]], a valuable commodity that formed the basis of Bespin's economy, through a metabolic process.<ref name="USW" />

Beldons measured anywhere from 0.8 to 10 [[kilometer]]s in width, and they had bodies filled with [[Color|orange]] gas bladders that allowed them to float. These creatures used their fleshy fins for propulsion. They were preyed upon by [[velker]]s, and their remains often served as food for [[crab glider]]s.<ref name="USW" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beldons first appeared in ''[[Galaxy Guide 2: Yavin and Bespin]]'', a ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' reference book written by [[Jonatha Ariadne Caspian]] that was released in [[1989]] by [[West End Games]]. The species was confirmed to exist in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which was released in [[2015]].
