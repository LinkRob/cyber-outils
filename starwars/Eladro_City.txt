'''Eladro City''' was the biggest settlement on [[Basteel]]. It was subterranean and constructed out of tunnels that had been carved over the [[Standard century|centuries]]. The entrance to the [[city]] was located in a narrow canyon on the surface of Basteel. The main landing pad was surrounded by intricate carvings that resembled a cathedral. The tunnels near the entrance to the city were full of kiosks made from shipping containers. It had had a large [[Eladro City bazaar|bazaar]] that was home to many shops and stores.<ref name="Moving Target" />
