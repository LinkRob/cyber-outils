The '''''Freedom Fighter''''' was a [[GR-75 medium transport]] that served in the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Following the [[Battle of Yavin]], the ''Freedom Fighter'' was one of multiple [[transport]]s that evacuated Alliance personnel on the surface. When the [[Galactic Empire]] launched an attack on the rebel base, the last Alliance personnel escaped on the ''Freedom Fighter'' and several other accompanying [[starship]]s.<ref name="Legends" />

Eventually, it was discovered that one of the ship's crew was a [[spy]] for the Empire, and after briefly being captured by a [[Victory-class Star Destroyer|''Victory''-class Star Destroyer]], the ''Freedom Fighter'' managed to escape. The ''Freedom Fighter'' was under the command of [[Captain]] [[Darpik]]. [[Commander]] [[Ares Ginn]] served as the senior [[Alliance Army]] [[officer]] onboard, and [[Corporal]] [[Calle Tharandon]] served as the ship's [[quartermaster]].<ref name="Legends" />
