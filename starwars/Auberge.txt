The '''''Auberge''''', formerly known as the '''''Glitterstim Shine''''', was a [[war cruiser]] owned by a [[Unidentified spice dealer|spice dealer]] from [[Kijimi]] until it was confiscated by the [[Nihil]] after the [[Deployment of the stormseeds|formation]] of the [[Stormwall]]. It eventually became the [[flagship]] of [[General]] [[Abediah Viess]], with the [[heavy cruiser]] serving at the [[Battle of Eriadu]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
