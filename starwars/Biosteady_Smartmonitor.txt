The '''Biosteady Smartmonitor''' was a model of [[Bio-Support Dispenser]] produced by [[Arakyd Industries]]. It could be integrated into [[armor]] and enhanced the effects of in-combat treatments through carefully regulated and distributed dosages of medicine.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Biosteady Smartmonitor was mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] sourcebook for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series.<ref name="Rise" />
