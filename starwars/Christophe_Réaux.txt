'''Christophe Réaux''', otherwise known as '''CROC''', is a French game designer and author. He wrote a number of articles for the French roleplaying magazine [[Casus Belli (magazine)|''Casus Belli'']], edited ''[[Backstab (magazine)|Backstab]]'' magazine<ref name="BS28">[[Backstab 28|''Backstab'' 28]]</ref> and later worked for  [[Edge Studio]] from [[2020]].
