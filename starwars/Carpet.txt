'''Carpet''' as a floor covering used in [[the galaxy]]. On the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]], a type of [[Amberwoven carpet|carpet]] was made with woven strands of [[amberine]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s quarters on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] had a carpet made of [[Coruscant fiberweave]].<ref name="Of MSE-6 and Men">{{StoryCite|story=Of MSE-6 and Men|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Carpet was first mentioned in the 1977 film {{Film|IV}} by [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]] as an derogatory term for the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]].<ref name="ANH" />
