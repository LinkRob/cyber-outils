The '''Caf-Colored Robes''' was an outfit owned by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Salvagers|standard costume]]. This outfit consisted of the upper Jawa sporting a [[Color|reddish-brown]] [[robe]] with pale blue sleeves, while the lower Jawa's robes were almost entirely pale blue, though both featured golden detailing, wrapped in a gray [[bandolier]]. Secured to their back was an attachment consisting of various metallic golden, silver, and bright blue [[droid]] parts, including the domed head of an [[astromech droid]] and the hand of a [[B1-series battle droid]].<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Caf-Colored Robes was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Utooni, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. Unlike most Costumes in the game, they possessed no direct Weapon Wrap counterpart, thus not being part of an in-game cosmetic set. The Caf-Colored Robes is one of the only Epic Costumes in the game not to have a distinct flavor text, but instead share the same unattributed text with Utooni's "Base," "Uncommon," and "Rare" rarity Costumes.<ref name="Hunters" />
