'''Cinnabar''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[fathier]] that [[Reproduction|mated]] with the male fathier [[Crimson Pavane]], resulting in the birth of the fathier [[Vermilion]]<ref name="Rose Tico" /> around [[31 ABY]]. By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The Last Jedi: Rose Tico: Resistance Fighter]]'' establishes that [[Vermilion]] is three [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of {{Film|VIII}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]], therefore meaning they must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[31 ABY]].</ref> Vermilion had participated in a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]] in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[casino]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. Prior to the race, a racing guide that listed Cinnabar as Vermilion's [[Parent|dam]] was distributed; a copy of which was later collected by the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance technician|mechanic]] [[Rose Tico]] and added to her [[Rose Tico's journal|diary]] while she was on a [[mission to Canto Bight]]<ref name="Rose Tico" /> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Date" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cinnabar was mentioned in the [[2018]] replica journal ''[[The Last Jedi: Rose Tico: Resistance Fighter]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Rose Tico" />
