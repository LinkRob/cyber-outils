Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> an '''attack on Eyo-Dajuritz''' was carried out by the [[Partisans|guerillas]] of the [[Onderonian]] [[Saw Gerrera]]. Secretly, clone troopers were dispatched on [[Eyo-Dajuritz]] to destroy a contingency supply cache of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and those with knowledge of it, which included Imperial [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] and [[Imperial Intelligence]] [[supervisor]] [[Haki Zeophrine]]. The troopers instead encountered members of Saw's guerrillas, who coincidentally were on the planet to arm themselves with the weapons in the supply cache, and gave chase.<ref name="MOF" />

Eventually cornering Saw's fighters and Bail Organa, Haki revealed her allegiance as an Imperial Intelligence agent as troopers openeed fired, although she was hit due to them being unaware of her true allegiance. Saw's guerrillas bomb the center of the cavern, causing a great flood that wiped out some of the clone troopers but separated Haki from the group, with [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]] and Organa paired and the rest of Saw and his guerillas. While Saw, his fighters, Soujen, and Bail escaped, Haki was found by a [[Unidentified Clone Commander (Eyo-Dajuritz)|Clone Commander]], who after listening to her plea that she was on their side, ordered his men to execute her shortly to keep the existence of the cache a secret.<ref name="MOF" />
