'''45-x-920p''' was a date in which the [[Contract of the Common Ownership Agreement]] was signed. The document, signed between [[Lawyer/Legends|attorney]] partners [[the Wavering Oeklass]], [[Lilac Nani]], [[Medypsh]], and [[Zot Effervescence]], set rules for multi-use property on [[Mistar-5]],<ref name="MAGL">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref> a location in the [[Mistar system/Legends|Mistar system]], within the [[Tragan Cluster/Legends|Tragan Cluster]].<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The date 45-x-920p was introduced in a one-page [[Wikipedia:cease and desist|cease and desist]] letter addressed to "the Wavering Oeklass," written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that combines creature designs and photographs from various projects of [[George Lucas]], creator of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, with original text by Carrau.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]] with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
