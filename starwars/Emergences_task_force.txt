In the aftermath of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] during the [[High Republic Era]], a '''task force''' was created by the [[Galactic Republic]] in order to discover the cause of the disaster and subsequent [[Emergences]], and find a way to deal with them. Operating largely from the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[cruiser]] ''[[Third Horizon]]'', the task force was led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] and included [[Jedi Knight]] [[Elzar Mann]], [[Admiral]] [[Pevel Kronara]], and [[Marlowe San Tekka|Marlowe]] and [[Vellis San Tekka|Vellis]] of the [[San Tekka clan]] as members.<ref name="LOTJ" />
