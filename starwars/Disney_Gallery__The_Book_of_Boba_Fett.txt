'''Disney Gallery: The Book of Boba Fett''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' feature that premiered on [[Disney+]] on [[May 4]], [[2022]]. The feature explored the behind-the-scenes story of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] and his return to [[Tatooine]] with [[Fennec Shand]]. The special revealed never-before-seen footage, technology, and practical effects by the filmmakers, cast, and crew.<ref name="D23" />
