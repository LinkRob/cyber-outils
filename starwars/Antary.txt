An '''antary''' was a type of nocturnal, [[Diet|predatory]] [[bird]] found on<ref name="Divine Intervention" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Endor]].<ref name="Divine Intervention" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Divine (?) Intervention]]" takes place concurrent to {{Film|VI}}, the events of which are dated to [[4 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Ewok]] [[shaman]] [[Logray]] [[Force sense|reached out]] with his senses while in a [[forest]] and heard the sounds of thousands of different birds [[Homeworld|native]] to the moon, including the distant hoot of an antary as it was waking up for a [[Local day|night]] of [[hunting]].<ref name="Divine Intervention" />

==Behind the scenes==
An antary was mentioned in "[[Divine (?) Intervention]]," a short story written by [[Paul Crilley]] and published in the [[2023]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="Divine Intervention" />
