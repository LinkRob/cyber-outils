In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date note" /> the final [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Clone Wars]], a '''battle''' took place<ref name="Battles" /> on the [[planet]]<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> [[Metalorn]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Fought during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]], the Confederacy's [[Confederacy military|military forces]] were led by the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] [[General]] [[Linwodo]], who also served in the Republic's [[Siege of Boz Pity|siege]] of the planet<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> [[Boz Pity]] and later the Confederacy's [[Battle of Kashyyyk|assault]] on the planet [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="Battles" />
