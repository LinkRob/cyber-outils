The '''Aricho system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Siannach sector]], a subsector of the [[Danjar sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square N-18 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the planet [[Aricho]].<ref name="Origin" />
