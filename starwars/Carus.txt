'''Carus''' was a [[Mon Calamari]] from the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]]. According to legend, Carus ignored the warnings of his friends and family to explore the [[Great Ungeness Trench]]. While exploring he awoke a [[Unidentified squid-like creature|squid-like creature]] at the bottom which [[Death|killed]] him and shook the ground around his [[Unidentified Mon Calamari city|city]] for almost a month.<ref name="Myths & Fables" />
