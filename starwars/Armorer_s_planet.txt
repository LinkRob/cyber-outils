The "'''Armorer's planet'''",<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> also known as '''the Covert''',<ref name="MVG" /> was an unknown [[planet]]<ref name="Graphic Novel">[[The Mandalorian – The Graphic Novel of Season 3|''The Mandalorian'' – The Graphic Novel of Season 3]]</ref> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="MVG" /> that hosted a [[Mandalorian secret covert|Mandalorian covert]]<ref name="Chapter 17" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|S3}}</ref> The planet was discovered by a [[scout]] from the [[Children of the Watch]].<ref name="MVG" /> When the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] saved the [[Children of the Watch]] [[covert]] using his [[Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter|N-1 starfighter]] while [[Paz Vizsla]]'s son, [[Ragnar Vizsla|Ragnar]]<ref name="Chapter 20" /> took the [[Mandalorian Creed]];<ref name="Chapter 17" /> Djarin and fellow Mandalorian [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] traveled to the planet after Kryze's [[Kryze Fortress|castle]] on [[Kalevala]] was destroyed by an [[Imperial Remnants|Imperial remnant]] after they left [[Mandalore]].<ref name="Chapter 19">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 19: The Convert}}</ref> When Ragnar was taken by a [[canyon raptor]] on the planet, he was rescued by Djarin, Kryze, Vizsla, and the [[Shriek-hawk Training team]].<ref name="Chapter 20" />

The planet had no name, was uncharted, and was only identified by a catalogue number in the [[Imperial registry]]. The Children of the Watch referred to it as their latest '''''Veheh'yaim''''', [[Mando'a]] for a temporary settlement.<ref name="MVG" />
