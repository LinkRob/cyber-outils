The '''Defender-5 sporting blaster''' was a model of [[Defender sporting blaster pistol]] manufactured by the [[Drearian Defense Conglomerate]] and favored by the nobility of the [[planet]] [[Gatalenta]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:Amilyn_Holdo_TLJ.png|thumb|left|180px|Amilyn Holdo with a Defender-5]]
The Defender-5 was a model of [[Defender sporting blaster pistol]] that had both a lethal and a [[stun]] setting, with the power setting control on the left side of the weapon. It could show signs of [[Oxygen|oxidation]] around the tip of the [[barrel]] due to the high [[temperature]] it produced. It was [[Color|black]] and silver in [[color]].<ref name="TLJVD" />

==History==
By the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. As [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] carries on immediately from the events of ''The Force Awakens'', it can be assumed to take place in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the Defender-5 [[blaster]] was considered a venerated classic. The and [[Resistance]] [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]], who hailed from [[Gatalenta]], favored using [[Holdo's blaster|one]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> [[Gender|She]] used the blaster to stun several mutineers in the [[hangar]] of the<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']]<ref name="TLJVD" /> during an [[Battle of Oetchi|attack]] on the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] by the [[First Order]].<ref name="TLJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Defender-5 sporting blaster first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ" /> which was released on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-viii-to-open-december-15-2017|text=''Star Wars: Episode VIII'' to Open December 15, 2017}}</ref> The weapon was not named in the film,<ref name="TLJ" /> but received identification in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref> Its design is based off the [[Wikipedia:MCM_pistol|Margolin pistol]], albeit with some modifications.
