'''Duggan''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who served as an [[Imperial officer|officer]] in the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Security Bureau|Security Bureau]]. In [[2 BBY]], he was sent to an [[Unidentified underground mining colony|underground mining colony]] on an [[Unidentified airless moon (Outer Rim)|airless moon]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. While there, he was alerted by a [[miner]], [[Corbin]], who had joined the Empire, that a [[Marriage|married]] couple, [[Ryland]] and [[Fiona]], were rebel [[Spy|spies]]. This was not true, as Corbin was framing them out of jealousy. Duggan arrested Ryland and Fiona, and ordered them to confess. When they refused, as they were completely innocent, Duggan had Fiona [[Death|executed]]. Ryland and his daughter, [[Laina]], eventually escaped to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] with the help of relatives.<ref name="Laina" />
