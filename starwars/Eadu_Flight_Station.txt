'''Eadu Flight Station''' was a [[shuttle]] depot located at the [[Eadu Energy Conversion Laboratory]] on the [[planet]] [[Eadu]] during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Description==
On the main landing pad for the flight station, four large spotlights lit up space where at least five [[Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle|''Zeta''-class cargo shuttles]] could be landed.<ref name="Rogue One" />

==History==
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="ROdate">{{FilmDate|RO}}</ref> [[Bodhi Rook]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[pilot]] who defected from the facility, alongside the reprogrammed [[KX-series security droid|KX series]] Imperial security [[droid]] [[K-2SO]], stole shuttle ''[[SW-0608]]'' from this facility, a ''Zeta''-class cargo transport. The shuttle was later used to infiltrate the [[Shield Gate]] at [[Scarif]].<ref name="Rogue One" />
