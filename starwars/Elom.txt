The '''Elom''' were a mysterious [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that were [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Elom (planet)|Elom]] who lived in frozen [[Cave|grottos]].<ref name="VE" /> They were short and stocky, and covered in thick, oily [[Hair|fur]]. As a result of living in darkness, Eloms had exceptional [[eye]]sight, but found natural or bright lights intolerable. They had two tusks covering the opening of their [[mouth]] and small, [[Color|black]] [[eye]]s located on the far ends of their wide faces. Though as a species that rarely ventured outside their caves, one Elom did make a name for himself offworld. [[Tanus Spijek]] was a [[spy]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Alien Archive" /> Most, though not all, members of this species were sympathetic to the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>
