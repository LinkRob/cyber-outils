"'''Ewok'''" is the second episode of the webseries ''[[Star Wars Galactic Pals]]''. The episode sees [[Safari droid]] [[M1-RE]] attempt to help [[Unidentified Ewok youngling|a]] [[Youngling|baby]] [[Ewok]] overcome its shyness, and feel at home. It was released alongside the first episode, "[[Wookiee (episode)|Wookiee]]," on [[StarWarsKids.com]] on [[April 12]], [[2022]].<ref name="Announcement" />

==Official description==
''Ewoks love a celebration. Miree helps a shy Ewok feel right at home in a new place by throwing a party!''

==Plot summary==
[[M1-RE|Miree]] introduces an [[Unidentified Ewok youngling|Ewok youngling]], who is attending his first day at the [[Youngling Care Space Station]]. She has configured the room to simulate his homeworld of [[Endor]] with forests and fountains. The Ewok youngling is shy so Miree and [[CAM-E]] entice him with snacks. The Ewok swings down and snatches the food.

Miree and CAM-E also bring drums and trumpets but the Ewok child is not interested. After seeing the [[Unidentified Wookiee youngling|Wookiee youngling]] playing with a [[Unidentified Rodian youngling|Rodian youngling]] who is riding a swing, Miree invites the Ewok youngling to play with the other children. When the other children including the [[Jawa]] take an interest in the musical instruments, Miree realizes that Ewoks like celebrations especially those with music.
