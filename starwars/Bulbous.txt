The '''bulbous'''<ref name="Manual">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> was a creature that was used as a [[dejarik]] holomonster.<ref name="Solo" /> They were [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Dorin]] and could project [[Poison|toxic]] [[gas]]ses to [[Death|kill]] enemies.<ref name="Ewoks" />

==Behind the scenes==
This creature was going to be featured on the dejarik table in ''[[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|A New Hope]],'' but it was cut alongside another [[Scrimp|creature.]]<ref name="Solo easter egg">''{{SW|url=news/tippett-studio-solo-easter-egg|text=How Tippett Studio (and Some Serendipity) Created a ''Solo'' Easter Egg}}''</ref>

[[File:Bulbous.jpg|thumb|Bublous dejarik piece in ''Solo: A Star Wars Story'']]
Decades later, during the production of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' the original puppets of the creatures would be digitally scanned and added to the film. At one point during the film [[Chewbacca]] hits the dejarik table in the [[Millennium Falcon]], and both pieces of the creatures fizzle out, explaining why they aren't present later on in ''A New Hope.''<ref name="Solo easter egg" />

The creature was nicknamed "Bulb" by the production crew of ''A New Hope,''<ref name="Solo easter egg" /> and it was first identified as bulbous in the [[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]].<ref name="Manual" />
