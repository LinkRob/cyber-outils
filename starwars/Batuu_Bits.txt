'''Batuu Bits''' was a snack food served at [[Oga's Cantina]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />

==Behind the scenes==
Batuu Bits is a snack available at Oga's Cantina bar in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. It is made up mostly of dried spicy snacks like tortilla chips, dried fish, and wasabi peas.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> The menus at [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] describe it as "crispy galaxy snack bits with spiced chermoula dip,"<ref name="Menu FL">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/ogas-cantina/menus/dinner|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Oga's Cantina: Dinner Menu}}</ref> while the Disneyland menu calls it "a light crisp Snack Mix from the galaxy."<ref name="Menu CA">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/dinner|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Dinner Menu}}</ref> When it was initially announced, it did not yet have its final name and was referred to as "Cantina Mix."<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=galaxys-edge-announcements|text=Everything You Need to Know About ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref><ref name="Nerdist">{{WebCite|url=https://nerdist.com/article/star-wars-galaxys-edge-food-drinks-cocktails-menu-disney-parks|text=What You Can Eat and Drink in STAR WARS: Galaxy's Edge|work=[[Wikipedia:Nerdist Industries|Nerdist]]|archivedate=20190228074254|author=[[Amy Ratcliffe]]|date=2019-02-27}}</ref>
