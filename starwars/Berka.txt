{{Quote|Before the First Order, it was the Empire. Before the Empire, it was the pirates. You young people will learn that all this conflict works itself out eventually.|Berka, to Bakkar|<ref name="Datapad" />}}
'''Berka''' was a [[Nautolan]] resident of [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], Berka privately denounced the First Order's presence on the [[planet]]. [[Bakkar]], owner of the [[Ronto Roasters]] [[restaurant]], advised Berka to keep quiet about the First Order, who monitored Batuu's communication frequencies, but Berka, who saw the First Order as no threat, saw no need to, insisting that the conflict would eventually settle.<ref name="Datapad" />
