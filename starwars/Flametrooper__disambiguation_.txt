*'''[[First Order flametrooper]]''', of the [[First Order]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]].

==[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]]==
*'''[[Fire trooper]]''', of the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Sith Empire's]] [[Imperial Army (Sith Empire)|Army]].
*'''[[Flame trooper/Legends|Flame trooper]]''', of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic's]] [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]].
*'''[[Clone blaze trooper]]''', in the Grand Army of the Republic, which was an advanced type of [[Clone jet trooper/Legends|clone jet trooper]] with thick [[Armor/Legends|armor]].
*'''[[Incinerator stormtrooper/Legends|Incinerator stormtrooper]]''', of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] [[Stormtrooper Corps/Legends|Stormtrooper Corps]].
