'''Adon Fox''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[starfighter]] [[pilot]] who served in [[Rebel Alliance Navy|the fleet]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. He fought in the [[Battle of Hoth]], and was the [[Wing Commander]] of [[Blade Squadron]] in the [[Battle of Endor]].

==Biography==
===Hoth===
Adon Fox joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] at some point prior to the [[Battle of Hoth]]. He was stationed at [[Echo Base]], the Alliance headquarters on [[Hoth]], along with [[Adon Fox's wife|his]] [[Marriage|wife]]. When the [[Galactic Empire]] invaded Echo Base, Fox had less than one hundred [[Standard hour|hours]] of flight [[time]], but was still asked to fly his single [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] to escort a [[transport]] during the necessary evacuation. Fox managed to survive the mission, but his wife was [[Death|killed]] when the transport she was on was destroyed by a [[blockade]] of [[Star Destroyer]]s.<ref name="BS" />

===Endor===
Later in the war, Fox reached the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Wing Commander]] and was placed at the helm of [[Blade Squadron]], the Alliance's [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing starfighter]] squadron. He worked with [[Lieutenant]] [[Braylen Stramm]] and [[Cadet]] [[Gina Moonsong]]. When the Alliance decided to [[Battle of Endor|attack]] the Empire's unfinished [[battle station]], the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]], Fox and his [[squadron]] were charged with guarding the [[fleet]]'s rear and safeguarding its lines of communication. Once the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Rebel fleet]] arrived at [[Endor]], where the Death Star was located, Fox was shocked to discover that a large flotilla of Imperial Star Destroyers had been deployed. His squadron's rearguard mission was quickly aborted, and Blade Squadron was reassigned to the kill-zone. Fox soon noticed the presence of the ''[[Devastator]]'', the Imperial ship which had destroyed his wife's transport at Hoth. He ordered an attack run on the Star Destroyer, but was met with two dozen [[TIE/IN interceptor]]s.<ref name="BS" />

[[File:AdonFox-SWZ99.png|thumb|300px|right|Fox piloted a B-wing during the Battle of Endor.]]
Blade Squadron engaged the ''Devastator'' and its TIEs. Imperial [[Commander]] [[Gradd]], of whom had a grudge on Fox, attempted to kill the man but only damaged his B-wing before being destroyed by one of the other members of Blade Squadron. The ''Devastator'' soon fell to Blade Squadron's bombs but not without heavy casualties. Fox's B-wing was severely damaged and only Lieutenant Stramm and Cadet Moonsong remained to be by his side. His pod couldn't eject leaving him to perish shortly before the Death Star's shield went down.<ref name="BS" />

==Personality and traits==
Fox was described as being rotund and red-faced. He made up for his lack of warrior physique with reflexes and mental agility. He was also known to be a first-rate leader across the Alliance fleet.<ref name="BS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Adon Fox first appeared in the two-part short story "[[Blade Squadron (short story)|Blade Squadron]]" written by [[David J. Williams]] and [[Mark S. Williams]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 149|''Star Wars Insider'' 149]] to [[Star Wars Insider 150|150]] in [[2014]]. There is a discrepancy concerning what type of starfighter Fox piloted during the Battle of Endor: Part One explicitly states that Fox piloted an [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wing]] during the battle, while Part Two and some sections of Part I explicitly state that he was piloting a [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing]]. Details in story (heavy shielding, escape pod, referring to the squadron as an "elite B-wing unit") suggest that Fox was canonically piloting a B-wing,<ref name="BS" /> which was later shown to be the case in the ''[[Battle Over Endor Scenario Pack]]''.<ref name="Adon Card">{{FFGXW2|set=Battle Over Endor Scenario Pack|cardname=Adon Fox}}</ref><ref name="Endor Preview">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/star-wars-x-wing-battle-over-endor-rebel-preview|text=Star Wars: X-Wing &ndash; Battle Over Endor Rebel Preview}}</ref>
