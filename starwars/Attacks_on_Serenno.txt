In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Galactic Republic]] launched a wave of '''attacks''' against the [[planet]] [[Serenno]], the [[homeworld]] of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[Head of State|leader]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]], during their [[D'Astan campaign|campaign]] in the [[D'Astan sector]] amid the [[Outer Rim Sieges]] offensive of the [[Clone Wars]] against the Confederacy. However, despite their waves of attacks alongside concentrated efforts within the [[sector]] and [[Serenno system]], the Republic was unsuccessful in capturing the planet from the Confederacy.<ref name="BMF 70" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attacks on Serenno were mentioned in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" section of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 70|seventieth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine.<ref name="BMF 70" /> The section was written by [[Mark Newbold]],<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> and the magazine was published around [[May 4]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|70}}</ref>
