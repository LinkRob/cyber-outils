A '''dark-side reservoir''' was a wellspring of [[Dark side of the Force|evil]] energy. One such reservoir emanated from the many [[Sith]] [[crystal]]s and [[Sith artifact|artifacts]] that [[Darth Sidious]] kept in the [[Grand Imperial Medical Facility|Grand Republic Medical Facility]]. At the center of that reservoir hung a massive Sith-imbued [[kyber crystal]], slivers from which were to be used in Sith [[lightsaber]]s. When [[Anakin Skywalker]] was reconstructed into [[Darth]] Vader following his [[Duel on Mustafar|duel]] on [[Mustafar]], his recovery was aided by an "immersion" in that wellspring.<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
