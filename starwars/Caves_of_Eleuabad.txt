The '''Caves of Eleuabad''' were a network of water-filled underwaterways within the porous structure of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]].

==History==
Located beneath the [[city]] of [[Theed]] and the [[Great Grass Plains]], these caves were a notorious location for [[sando aqua monster]]s. Some of its underwaterways led into the [[Solleu River]] which ran through Theed city.<ref name="SWCL" />

While attempting to reach Theed and warn [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of the [[Trade Federation]] [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]], [[Jedi]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] treated with the [[Gungan]]s of [[Otoh Gunga]] to utilize a [[Tribubble bongo|bongo]] to travel through the planet's core. Along with navigator [[Jar Jar Binks]], the Jedi passed through the Caves of Eleuabad, encountering a sleeping sando aqua monster which they barely escaped before following the underwaterways into Theed.<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>
