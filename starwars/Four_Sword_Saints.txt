{{Quote|You must be one of the Four Sword Saints, Master Okinaa, The One Who Shatters the Galaxy with Nothing But a Name.|The Ronin, to Okinaa|<ref name="Visions" />}}
The '''Four Sword Saints''' was a group that the [[Sith Master]] [[Okinaa]] belonged to. Okinaa lived in a [[Unidentified village (Okinaa)| village]] on a [[Unidentified planet (Okinaa)|planet]] with their [[Astromech droid|droid]]<ref name="Visions" /> [[R5-D56]]<ref name="Payback">{{Visions|The Duel: Payback}}</ref> and other villagers. Over time, many of the villagers [[Death|died]] until Okinaa and RD5-56 became the [[City|village's]] only surviving members. Ten [[Standard year|years]] later, while visiting a [[Unidentified holy place|holy place]] on the [[planet]], Okinaa was challenged to a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] by<ref name="Visions" /> the former [[Sith]]<ref name="Payback" /> known as [[the Ronin]]. While issuing the challenge, the Ronin mentioned that Okinaa was one of the Four Sword Saints and referred to them as "The One Who Shatters the Galaxy with Nothing But a Name". Okinaa was then killed by the Ronin during their [[Lightsaber combat|duel]].<ref name="Visions" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The Four Sword Saints appeared in the form of Okinaa and were identified in the [[Canon|non-canon]] one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Visions – Takashi Okazaki 1|''Visions – Takashi Okazaki'' 1]], written and illustrated by [[Takashi Okazaki]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Visions" /> on [[March 20]], [[2024]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/108450/star_wars_visions_-_takashi_okazaki_2024_1|text=Star Wars: Visions - Takashi Okazaki (2024) #1}}</ref> Prior to the comic's release, the Four Sword Saints were first pictured, also in the form of Okinaa, in a [[StarWars.com]] article published on [[September 14]], [[2023]].<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-visions-takashi-okazaki-1-preview|text=New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Comic Explores the Ronin's Sith Origins — Reveal}}</ref> In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Kensei (honorary title)|sword saint]] was a title given to the greatest [[Wikipedia:Swordsmanship|swordsmen]] during the [[Wikipedia:Edo period|Edo period]] in [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japan]].<ref name="Art of Visions">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]''</ref>
