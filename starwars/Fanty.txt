'''Fanty''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who served as a [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing]] [[starfighter]] [[pilot]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. He was part of the [[Blade Squadron]], under the leadership of [[Wing Commander]] [[Adon Fox]]. When his squadron participated in the [[Battle of Endor]], Fanty flew under the call sign [[Blade Four]].<ref name="BS" />
