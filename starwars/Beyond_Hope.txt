"'''Beyond Hope'''" is a short story that was published in the [[2020]] anthology book ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''. It was written by [[Michael Moreci]] and focuses on [[Private]] [[Emon Kref]].

{{Plot|story}}

==Development==
{{Quote|So it fascinates me, like I love what Alexander opened up, and I kind of always&mdash;you know&mdash;I love a good military story so, I was like what is it am I thinking. Like what are these guys&mdash;yeah, you know&mdash;these people thinking in these trenches of Hoth.|Michael Moreci, on his inspiration on "Beyond Hope"|<ref name="MoreciPod" />}}
According to the author [[Michael Moreci]], he took inspiration from [[canon]] war-based stories by [[Alexander Freed]], ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'' being Moreci's favorite as it gave a look into "[[Soldier|grunts]]" on the ground who had different perspectives of the events of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga. The author apparently tabled these ideas with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] trench warfare on [[Hoth]].<ref name="MoreciPod">{{Spotify|url=episode/62Bfnw0YA5VMi43njz4GSm?si=IoCSiWxMRHm0EdmKY-Y9DA|text=Episode 88 &ndash; Michael Moreci talks From A Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back and more|quote=On November 10, 2020, DelRey released From A Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back and Michael Moreci is on of the 40 contributing authors.}}</ref>
