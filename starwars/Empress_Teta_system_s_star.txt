The '''star''' of the [[Empress Teta system]] was<ref name="NOP" /> located in the [[Koros sector]] within the [[Deep Core]]<ref name="Corr" /> and [[the Interior|Interior]] regions of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Interior" /> The [[star]] was set in the grid square L-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Map" /> The sun was [[orbit]]ed by the [[planet]] [[Empress Teta]], Empress Teta's [[Color|red]] [[moon]] [[Atale]], and eight other planets. Like the rest of its system, the star was placed on the [[hyperspace route]]s known as the [[Byss Run]], the [[Carbonite Run]], and the [[Koros Trunk Line]].<ref name="NOP" />

It took Empress Teta 359 [[Standard day|days]] to complete an orbit around the sun, with each day taking 23.5 [[Standard hour|hours]]. In the [[Royal District]] of Empress Teta's [[capital]] [[city]], [[Cinnagar]], nine [[gold]]en spires represented the planets within the Empress Teta [[Star system|system]]. The spires were placed along the [[equator]]ial axis so that the morning sunlight would illuminate them according to their orbit order about their sun.<ref name="NOP" />

==Behind the scenes==
The star was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Nexus of Power]]'', a [[2016]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] sourcebook for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]''.<ref name="NOP" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the sun was named [[Koros]] and was first mentioned in the article "Byss and the Deep Core Part 2: Empress Teta," which was authored by [[Rodney Thompson]] and posted to [[Wizards.com]] on [[April 14]], [[2005]].<ref name="Legends">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20050414planet2|series=Byss and the Deep Core|text=Part 2: Empress Teta}}</ref>
