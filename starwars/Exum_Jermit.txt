'''Exum Jermit''' was a [[Muun]] who served as [[Second Vice Chair]] of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] and frequented the [[Malastare Hunting Club]], who would treat them lavishly for their loyalty to them.<ref name="BH 11" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date note" /> Jermit refused to do business with [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] in favor of doing business with a mysterious competitor of Jabba's. In response, Jabba sent his [[majordomo]], [[Bib Fortuna]], to try to persuade Jermit to reconsider. Jermit spoke with Fortuna during the [[Great Hunt of Malastare]], but still refused. In response to Jermit's refusal, Fortuna allowed the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] to [[Death|kill]] Jermit, as he had been hired to do by Jabba.<ref name="BH 11" />
