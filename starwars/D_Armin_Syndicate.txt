The '''D'Armin Syndicate''' was an organization that operated during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Aphra 6" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 6]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the syndicate [[Attack on a Tagge Corporation supply ship|attacked]] a [[Unidentified supply ship|supply ship]] of the [[Tagge Corporation]]. By the following [[Standard day|day]], news of the attack led to a drop for the Tagge Corporation in the [[Hadi system]], which the corporation's [[Executive officer|leader]]&mdash;[[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]] of the [[House of Tagge]]&mdash;noticed and questioned her<ref name="Aphra 6" /> [[secretary]]<ref name="Boushh">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]]</ref> [[Lapin Tagge]]<ref name="Aphra 28">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 28|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 28]]</ref> about. In response, Lapin informed Domina of the attack, to which she ordered her secretary have a [[battleship]] cut the syndicate off at the celestial body [[Feriae Junction]]. Domina then instructed to have their [[engine]]s and [[weapon]]s taken out before pushing them into the [[Star|sun]] the next day.<ref name="Aphra 6" />

==Behind the scenes==
The D'Armin Syndicate was mentioned in flashbacks in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|sixth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 6" /> on [[November 25]], 2020. Prior to the release of the comic, the syndicate was mentioned in a preview article of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[November 18]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-doctor-aphra-6-exclusive-preview|text=There's a New Bounty in Marvel's ''Doctor Aphra'' #6 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
