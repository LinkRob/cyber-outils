'''Dome of the Anointed''' was a location in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] of [[Jedha]], that was used as a secondary base by the [[Partisans]] to stage missions in the city. The Partisans launched their [[Battle on Jedha|final onslaught]] from the Dome of the Anointed.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" />
