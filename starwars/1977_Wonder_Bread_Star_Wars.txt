'''1977 Wonder Bread ''Star Wars''''' was a trading card set manufactured by the [[Wikipedia:Continental Baking Company|Continental Baking Company]] and released in specially marked packages of their [[Wikipedia:Wonder Bread|Wonder Bread]], sold from Fall [[1977]] through early [[1978]].<ref name="Original Topps" />

One card came in each of the bags, with a total of 16 cards in the set. Each card featured a photo of a character or vehicle from {{Film|IV}}, with a small description of the subject on the back.<ref name="Original Topps" /> The complete set was also made available as a mail-in offer from Wonder Bread.<ref name="Original Topps" /> The cards were designed by [[John Van Hammersveld]],<ref name="SWCA Blog">{{WebCite|url=http://blog.theswca.com/2018/01/wonder-no-more-star-wars-bread.html|text=Wonder No More: The Star Wars Bread Promotion of 1977|archivedate=20260208142247}}</ref> who also created the artwork used on promotional store displays.<ref name="Original Topps" />

==Continuity==
Luke Skywalker's card erroneously states his age in the film as 20 years old, as opposed to 19 years old as later revealed in {{Film|III}}. It also places Chewbacca's age at 100 years old instead of the official age of 200.<ref name="Original Topps" /> Grand Moff Tarkin is referred to as the "Governor of the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Imperial Outlands]]," which would later go on to be known as the Outer Rim Territories.<ref name="TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Legacy==
Sets of the cards were sold in limited quantities by the "[[The Jawa Trader|Jawa Trader]]" section of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' in [[1998]].<ref name=SWI 41">{{InsiderCite|41|The Jawa Trader|Jawa Trader}}</ref> Images of the 16 cards were reproduced in the afterword of ''[[Star Wars: The Original Topps Trading Card Series, Volume One]]''.<ref name="Original Topps" />
