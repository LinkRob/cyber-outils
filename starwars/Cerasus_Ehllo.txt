'''Cerasus Ehllo''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Imperial officer]] of the [[Ehllo family]]. [[Cerasus Ehllo's father|His father]] personally knew [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. Cerasus rose to the rank of [[captain]] in the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Sana Starros 1" />

At some point between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SanaStarrosDate}}</ref> he [[Marriage|married]] [[Aryssha Starros|Aryssha]] of the [[Starros Clan]], and she eventually became pregnant with [[twin]]s. Aryssha traveled to the [[Starros family homestead|Starros family ancestral home]] on [[Hosnian Prime]] to give [[Reproduction|birth]] and informed Ehllo she would meet him in a fortnights [[time]]. Ehllo grew impatient of waiting for Aryssha to return, claiming he missed her, and had a birthing suite created for her on his [[King's Ransom|ship]]. He then [[Attack on the Starros family homestead|stormed]] the clan's home with<ref name="Sana Starros 1" /> a [[Unidentified stormtrooper squad (Starros family homestead)|squad]]<ref name="216 Launchpad">{{InsiderCite|216|Launchpad}}</ref> of [[stormtrooper]]s to retrieve Aryssha and took her back to his [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="Sana Starros 1" />

Once on-board, he escorted her to their suite and left her there as he attended to his own matters. She snuck out and connected a [[datapad]] to the ship's [[computer]]. When she was found by a pair of stormtroopers, Ehllo returned and took her to get some food. He served her [[Nosna fish]] that he got on [[Naboo]] since he knew it was her favorite. Ehllo was asked by Aryssha about their destination, but he refused to divulge the information. She asked him if they could visit [[Neral's moon]] so she could get some [[shoshak]]. They were interrupted by [[Gabe Bean]] who told Ehllo that they had made a breakthrough in their research. He left the room after promising his wife he would consider the trip to Neral's moon.<ref name="Sana Starros 2">[[Sana Starros 2|''Sana Starros'' 2]]</ref>

Ehllo traveled to the [[Corellian sector]] and brought his wife to the bridge of his ship. He apologized for forcing her to leave her family, confessing that he was worried he might lose her. They returned to their quarters but Aryssha left to use the [[refresher]]. She followed a pair of crewmembers thinking they might lead her to [[Avon's journals]]. Ehllo and [[Phel Starros]] caught her and threaten to punish her.<ref name="Sana Starros 3">[[Sana Starros 3|''Sana Starros'' 3]]</ref>

The Imperial officer was on his ship above [[Hynestia Prime|Hynestia]] while Phel, with the assistance of [[Marl Jibs]], captured [[Thea Starros]] and [[Mevera Starros]].<ref name="Sana Starros 4">[[Sana Starros 4|''Sana Starros'' 4]]</ref> After [[Lanitra]] and several of her allies snuck onto the ''King's Ransom'' and disabled most of the crew with gas, Ehllo and a [[Unidentified fire team|fire team]] of stormtroopers confronted them. He was captured by [[Deva Lompop]] when Sana broadcast [[Chadra-Fan opera]] into the [[Stormtrooper armor|helmets]] of the stormtroopers at loud volume, disabling them. She forced him to lead her to the [[data cube]] that contained Avon's journals. He was then forced into an [[escape pod]] and jettisoned into space with Phel. Ehllo vowed to get his family back.<ref name="Sana Starros 5" />
