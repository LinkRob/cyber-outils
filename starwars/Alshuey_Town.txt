'''Alshuey Town'''<ref name="Star Wars 1">[[Star Wars (2025) 1|''Star Wars'' (2025) 1]]</ref> was a [[City|town]] and trading post on the [[planet]] [[Guat'a]]. The town was small with old and worn down buildings, including a social club called [[Rugova's]], which served as the base of operations for the [[warlord]] [[Smaatku]]. It was described as being half town, half market, half garbage dump. The outpost consisted of stalls, shops and workshops, which were all arranged around one central building.<ref name="Free Fall" />
