'''Daedus''' was a small [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. After the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s created their [[Stormwall]], the planet became trapped within Nihil territory known as the [[Occlusion Zone]].<ref name="Eye of Darkness" />

[[Creighton Sun]] and [[Porter Engle]] flew a [[shuttle]] to Daedus and were shot down by pirates. He managed to crash-land the ship and fought the marauders who had downed them. Years later, Engle returned to the planet to search the wreckage in hopes of finding an intact [[EX communication droid|EX droid]].<ref name="Eye of Darkness" />
