'''Danger Squadron''' was a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] that served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps|Starfighter Corps]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] under the [[Danger Leader|command]] of the [[Duros]] [[Shriv Suurgav]]. During the [[Battle of Theed]], three [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] defectors&mdash;[[Iden Versio]], [[Del Meeko]] and [[Dio]]&mdash;temporarily joined Danger Squadron to prevent the Empire from completing [[Operation: Cinder]] on [[Naboo]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==History==
===Battle of Naboo===
Sometime after the [[Battle of Endor]], Danger Squadron was stationed on the [[MC80 Liberty Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80 ''Liberty'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]] ''[[Restoration]]'' which was commanded by [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. At that time, the Empire had initiated [[Operation: Cinder]], which sought the destruction of many loyal Imperial [[Planet|worlds]] using experimental [[climate disruption array]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

One of the Empire's first [[Skirmish on Vardos|targets]] was [[Vardos]], which inspired the defection of [[Iden Versio]] and [[Del Meeko]], two [[agent]]s and their [[ID10 seeker droid]] [[Dio]] of the [[Imperial Special Forces]] team known as [[Inferno Squad]]. The two turncoats contacted the ''Restoration'', pledging their surrender to the [[Alliance officer|officers]] there. [[Danger Leader]], [[Shriv Suurgav]], ordered for them to be taken onto the ''Restoration'' for questioning.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

[[File:SWBF2_Battle_of_Naboo_Space.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Danger Squadron arrives at Naboo with the ''Restoration'']]
Meanwhile, a [[Raid at the Wretch of Tayron|raid]] at the [[Wretch of Tayron]] revealed that [[Naboo]] was the next target for Operation: Cinder. General Calrissian was informed of this<ref name="Shattered Empire 3">[[Shattered Empire 3|''Shattered Empire'' 3]]</ref> and reached out to Versio and Meeko, asking for their assistance in stopping the Imperials. The pair agreed to help and were taken into Danger Squadron. Danger Squadron's [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] then jumped into range of Naboo alongside the ''Restoration'', the ''[[Mellcrawler II]]'' and other assisting [[starfighter]]s including [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wings]],<ref name="Battlefront II" /> [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wings]], and [[BTL-S3 Y-wing starfighter|Y-wings]]. The starfighters began [[Battle of Theed|engaging]] the climate disruption arrays, destroying all that were in their path. Danger Squadron then turned their guns onto the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Torment]]''<ref name="Shattered Empire 3" /> and defended the Y-wings from [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] as they began bombing the large vessel.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

Once the ''Torment's'' [[Deflector shield|shields]] were down, Suurgav gave the ''Restoration'' the all-clear to open fire on the ''Torment''. The ''Restoration's'' cannons devastated the ''Torment'' and its ground forces evacuated and mobilized onto the streets of [[Theed]], [[Naboo]]'s capital. The ''Restoration'' responded by sending its own forces down to defend the [[city]]. Shriv, Iden and Del were among them and were sent to bring the [[Theed Royal Palace|palace's]] defense systems online. They were successful, and an ion blast rendered the Imperial forces defenseless.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
