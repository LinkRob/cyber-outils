'''Felucian Falafel and Hummus Garden Spread''' was a [[plant]]-based dish served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Felucian Falafel and Hummus Garden Spread is an entrée available at the [[In-universe|real-world]] at Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]]<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> starting in [[June]] [[2024]].<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2024/05/two-new-dishes-docking-bay-7-dhs|text=Two New Dishes Coming to Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at Disney's Hollywood Studios|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20240531162806}}</ref> The dish is described as "Chickpea Falafel, Roasted Beet Hummus, and Beet-Tomato-Cucumber Relish topped with Garlic Toum Sauce and served with Grilled Pita." The dish is distinct from the similarly named [[Felucian Garden Spread]], which had faux [[meatball]]s instead of falafel. The Felucian Garden Spread was not seen on the Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo menu upon the debut of the Felucian Falafel dish.<ref name="DHS Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/dinner|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Dinner Menu}}</ref>

In celebration of [[May 4|May the 4th]] in [[2025]], [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney Eats]] released a recipe for the dish. Ingredients not previously confirmed to be in the dish include the seasonings [[Wikipedia:Onion|onion]], [[Wikipedia:Parsley|parsley]], [[Wikipedia:Coriander|cilantro]], [[Wikipedia:Allspice|allspice]], [[Wikipedia:Cumin|cumin]], coriander, [[Wikipedia:Cardamom|cardamom]], [[cinnamon]], [[Wikipedia:Clove|cloves]], black pepper, and plant-based [[Wikipedia:Mayonnaise|mayonnaise]].<ref name="Recipe">{{DPB|url=disney-experiences/disney-eats-exclusive-plant-based-star-wars-day-recipe|text=Disney Eats: Exclusive Plant-Based Star Wars Day Recipe|author=Wilson, Katie|date=2025-05-02}}</ref>
