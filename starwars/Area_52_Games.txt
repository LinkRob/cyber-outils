'''Area 52 Games''' was a Seattle-based mobile and PC game development studio. The company was founded in [[2013]], [[June 4]].<ref name="Crunchbase" />

The development studio partnered with [[Disney Interactive#Disney Interactive Worlds|Disney Interactive Worlds]] to develop the now-cancelled browser-based PC game [[Star Wars: Attack Squadrons]]. While development has ceased, Area 52 and Disney Interactive have stated that they plan to release other Star Wars games in the future.<ref name="Cancelled">{{WebCite|url=http://www.starwarsattacksquadrons.com|text=Cancellation note|quote=We want to thank all of the fans who participated in the closed beta of Star Wars: Attack Squadrons. After much consideration, we have decided to cease development so that we can focus on other Star Wars game experiences. We truly appreciate the time you spent engaging in the beta.|archivedate=20140614103417}}</ref>
