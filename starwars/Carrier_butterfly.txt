'''Carrier butterflies''' were tiny [[insect]]s native to the [[planet]] [[Maridun]].<ref name="SWAEYNTK" /> They could also be found on [[Saleucami]]<ref name="Cut and Run" /> and [[Daro]].<ref name="War-Mantle" /> They were often used as messengers as they were able to listen to their owners' instructions and deliver messages to allies.<ref name="SWAEYNTK" />

Carrier butterflies had two front wings and two trailing ones. Their wings could be many different [[color]]s, such as blue, pink, white, or black.<ref name="Cut and Run" /><ref name="Defenders">{{TCW|Defenders of Peace}}</ref>

[[Tub]] had a carrier butterfly, which he used to warn his village of a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Defense of Maridun|invasion]].<ref name="Defenders"/>
