'''Bokken sabers''',<ref name="Part Three">{{Ahsoka|Part Three: Time to Fly}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> also known as '''lightsaber training staffs''' and '''Jedi training sabers''',<ref name="A Test of Strength Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/a-test-of-strength-concept-art-gallery|text="A Test of Strength" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref> were wooden [[sword]]s<ref name="Trials of the Darksaber Episode Gallery" /> used for [[lightsaber combat]] training.<ref name="A Test of Strength" />

Bokken sabers were an essential part of any [[Jedi]]'s training. [[Padawan]]s used them to learn basic fighting techniques and combat moves in a safe setting. After a student moved comfortably with a Bokken saber, either against sparring opponents or [[lightsaber]] training technology, they could progress to actual lightsabers. Jedi could turn to Bokken sabers at any point for additional training drills for either themselves or their Padawan.<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> 

==History==
===High Republic Era===
[[File:JeckiBokkenSaber-DressingAcolyte.png|thumb|200px|left|Jekki Lon holding a Bokken saber.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], several [[Jedi]] including [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] used Bokken sabers during a lesson with [[Jedi Master]] [[Lakshay]].<ref name="Day">{{Acolyte|Day (episode)}}</ref>

===Clone Wars===
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Petro]] practice with a training staff while he was on board the [[Paladin-class corvette|''Paladin''-class corvette]] ''[[Crucible]]'', deflecting shots from a [[Marksman-H training remote]] and then showing his fellow trainees an original move he had created named the "[[Whirlwind of Destiny]]."<ref name="A Test of Strength" />

===Imperial Era===
During the [[Imperial Era]] [[Sabine Wren]] and [[Ezra Bridger]] used training sabers made by [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] to help the [[Mandalorian]] learn how to wield the [[Darksaber]].<ref name="Trials of the Darksaber" /> 

===New Republic Era===
[[File:SabineWren-Platinum2025.png|thumb|200px|right|Sabine Wren trained with Bokken saber under Ahsoka Tano.]]
During the [[New Republic Era]], Wren would later use a Bokken when sparring against the [[droid]] [[Huyang]] wielding [[hologram target hilt]]s and also [[Ahsoka Tano]], after agreeing to resume training as Tano's Padawan.<ref name="Part Three" /> Tano's training of Wren with Bokken sabers included using them for [[zatochi]] training.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />
