An '''anx''' was a type of tool used by [[spacer]]s during the [[High Republic Era]]. An abandoned [[spanner]] and anx lying on still-[[Color|green]] [[moss]] in the central globe of the [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine space station]] were noted by [[Affie Hollow]] as proof that the ancient [[space station]] was not abandoned after she and many others were stranded there after the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]].<ref name="ITD">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref>
