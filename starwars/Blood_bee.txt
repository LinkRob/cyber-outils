A '''blood bee''' was a type of [[bee]].<ref name="Crown" /> In [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> shortly after [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] [[Mission to Siline|arrived]] on the [[planet]] [[Siline]], she was brought to a room within a [[Unidentified crystalline tower|crystalline tower]], where she concentrated on [[Force sense|sensing]] the flow of [[the Force]]. Lon felt that the planet's geology or [[atmosphere]] seemed to vibrate on a fundamental level, and although it did not clash with her sense of the Force, she mentally compared the distraction it caused to a blood bee buzzing around her head.<ref name="Crown" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blood bees were mentioned in ''[[The Acolyte: The Crystal Crown]]'', a [[2025]] young-adult novel by [[Tessa Gratton]].<ref name="Crown" /> In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Sphecodes|''sphecodes'']] is a [[Wikipedia:Genus|genus]] of bee also known as the blood bee.<ref name="bees">{{WebCite|url=https://www.buzzaboutbees.net/sphecodes-blood-bees.html|text=Sphecodes - Blood Bees|work=[https://www.buzzaboutbees.net buzzaboutbees.net]|archivedate=20250416163431}}</ref>
