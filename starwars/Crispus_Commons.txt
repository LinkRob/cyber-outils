'''Crispus Commons''', also known as simply '''Crispus''', was a housing project in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Gorse]]'s [[Gorse City]] built for [[Clone Wars]] veterans around [[19 BBY]]. At some point, the demolitions expert [[Skelly]] moved into an [[Skelly's apartment|apartment]] in the complex and began renovating Crispus's [[Crispus Commons bomb shelter|bomb shelter]] for his personal use. In [[11 BBY]], the [[Twi'lek]] [[Hera Syndulla]] tracked Skelly to the complex's [[bomb]] shelter to discuss combating [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] overreach.

==Description==
[[File:Gorse_-_sw_galactic_atlas.png|thumb|left|250px|Crispus Commons was built on the planet Gorse.]]
Crispus Commons, also known as simply Crispus, was a housing project built in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Gorse]]'s [[Gorse City]]. The complex featured several residences&mdash;including at least one [[Skelly's apartment|apartment]]&mdash;and was sandwiched between two of Gorse's industrial districts, placing it within easy commuting distance of most of the planet's major work sites by the [[time]] of the [[Imperial Era]]. The facility had an exercise yard for residents, at the far edge of which housed its trash bin. Behind the trash bin, a rusted-out grate led to the area's [[permacrete]] [[Crispus Commons bomb shelter|bomb shelter]].<ref name="AND" /> By [[11 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']] to [[11 BBY]].</ref> residences in Crispus Commons had become dilapidated.<ref name="AND" />

==History==
Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Construction" /> Crispus Commons was constructed as a retirement [[Colonist|colony]] for [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] war veterans of the [[Clone Wars]] conflict fought between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The project's builders erected a [[bomb]] shelter on its premises for its residents to use if the Separatists' [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] or [[General]] [[Grievous]] decided to destroy the facility. After the war's [[Mission to Mustafar|conclusion]]<ref name="AND" /> that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Construction" /> the complex fell under the control of the nascent [[Galactic Empire]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|formed]] from the Republic. Over the following years, the Empire continued sending residents to live at Crispus Commons. However, the Imperials neglected to invest in improving the complex, leading to the project becoming increasingly dilapidated.<ref name="AND" />

[[File:HeraSyndulla-AND.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Hera Syndulla tracked Skelly to Crispus Commons's bomb shelter.]]
At some point, the [[Corellian]] demolitions expert [[Skelly]] moved to Crispus Commons and lived in an apartment. Though not fond of the complex's conditions, he resolved to continue residing there due to its location on Gorse being close to the world's industrial districts. While a resident, he cleaned out [[mold]] growing in the facility's bomb shelter and began using the space to work on and display his manifesto about the adverse effects of [[mining]] on Gorse's [[moon]], [[Cynda]]. The [[Gender|man]] eventually came to consider the room "his world" and used different parts of the area to display his interests. At some point, Skelly's file at Gorse's [[Transcept Media Solutions]] began listing his last known address as Crispus Commons.<ref name="AND" />

In 11 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Skelly [[Gorse Conflict|attacked]] [[Count]] [[Denetrius Vidian]]&mdash;an "efficiency expert" in service of the Empire&mdash;trying to draw attention to mining activity that he felt could lead to Cynda's destruction. The assault resulted in Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s searching Skelly's Crispus Commons apartment for his whereabouts and information about him. Later, [[Hera Syndulla]], a [[Twi'lek]] seeking to form a resistance against the Empire, became interested in Skelly after learning of his actions against Vidian. Shortly after, she tracked him to the bomb shelter in Crispus Commons and conversed with him about fighting Imperial overreach. However, the Twi'lek's impression of his erratic demeanor during their conversation eventually dissuaded her from working with him in the future and she left him in the compound.<ref name="AND" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It was more an echo of Saint Crispin/Crispinus, on whose day a famous battle was fought.|John Jackson Miller on Crispus Commons's naming|<ref name="Naming">{{Twitter|jjmfaraway|status/508486824571183105|[[John Jackson Miller]]|date=2014-09-06|quote=@TheTrekspert It was more an echo of Saint Crispin/Crispinus, on whose day a famous battle was fought. {{C|In response to: @jjmfaraway Was Crispus Commons named for AC Crispin, by at chance?}}|archivedate=20230817032812|archivefile=File:JohnJacksonMiller-CrispusCommons-Twitter.png|nolive=1}}</ref>}}
Crispus Commons appeared in [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']], a [[2014]] novel written by [[John Jackson Miller]].<ref name="AND" /> The complex was named in reference to [[Wikipedia:Crispin and Crispinian|Saint Crispin]], also known as Crispinus,<ref name="Naming" /> a [[Wikipedia:Patron saint|patron saint]] of shoemakers, saddlers, and tanners.<ref name="catholiccrispus">{{WebCite|url=https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04491a.htm|text=CATHOLIC ENCYCLOPEDIA: Sts. Crispin and Crispinian|author=Meier, Gabriel|work=[[Wikipedia:New Advent|''New Advent'']]|quote=Crispin and Crispinian are the patron saints of shoemakers, saddlers, and tanners.|archivedate=20221201000712}}</ref> Miller took inspiration from [[Wikipedia:St Crispin's Day|Crispin's Day]], on which the [[Wikipedia:Battle of Agincourt|Battle of Agincourt]] took place, and also presumes that Crispus was the name of some battleground or war hero from the Clone Wars. The author's conception of Crispus Commons reflected that no actual [[Clone trooper|clones]] were housed at the facility, but rather, various planetary volunteers and draftees.<ref name="dawnproduction">{{Faraway|url=fiction/starwarsanewdawn|text=''Star Wars: A New Dawn''}}</ref>
