The '''Edusa system''' was located in the [[Morshdine sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> Situated in grid square O-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was linked by a [[Axxila–Tangrene hyperlane|hyperspace route]] to the [[Tangrene system|Tangrene]], [[Vandyne system|Vandyne]],<ref name="TFABG" /> and [[Thabeska system]]s.<ref name="TSWGalaxy">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Edusa [[Star system|system]] was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' boxed set.<ref name="TFABG" /> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The [[Edusa system/Legends|Edusa system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> ''[[The Clone Wars: Act on Instinct]]'', a [[Star Wars comic books|webcomic]] authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on StarWars.com<ref name="AoI">''[[The Clone Wars: Act on Instinct]]''</ref> over the span of the 2009<ref name="AoI announcement">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/news20091001comics/index.html|text=New ''Clone Wars'' Web Comic Tells Original Tale}}</ref>&ndash;[[2010]]<ref name="Lethal Trackdown Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode222.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Lethal Trackdown}}</ref> [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series,<ref name="AoI announcement" /> featured the first appearance of the Edusa [[Star system/Legends|system]].<ref name="AoI" />
