A '''transport shuttle'''<ref name="SWGC Overview">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/experience-highlights/#itinerary|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Itinerary}}</ref> operating out of [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]] carried passengers from the surface to ships in space and back, including the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''.<ref name="New Details">{{DPB|url=2021/07/new-details-revealed-disney-imagineers-talk-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-opening-spring-2022-at-walt-disney-world-resort|text=New Details Revealed: Disney Imagineers Talk ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser, Opening Spring 2022 at Walt Disney World Resort}}</ref> The vessel was piloted by [[RX-series pilot droid|RX-Series pilot droid]] [[J6-F8]].<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge]]''</ref> The transports were provided by [[Oga Garra]], and could be boarded at the cruise ship's [[Transport Dock]] on Deck 6.<ref name="SWGS Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)|''Star Wars'': Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BSO_transport_from_Halcyon_interior.jpg|250px|thumb|The transport's interior]]
This transport was the transportation that brought guests of the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel to the nearby [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]].<ref name="YouTube">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@DisneyParks|name=Disney Parks|video=QsyH2sayDEs|text=Behind The Scenes: Disney Imagineers Talk Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|date=2021-07-30}}</ref> In reality the transport was a bus, which was themed on the inside to maintain the illusion guests are on a space vessel traveling back and forth from the ''Halcyon'' to [[Batuu]].<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2019/08/concept-art-first-look-at-immersive-guest-transport-system-for-the-star-wars-hotel-at-disneys-hollywood-studios|text=CONCEPT ART: First Look at Immersive Guest Transport System for the Star Wars Hotel at Disney's Hollywood Studios|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20201207180216}}</ref> Concept art of the transport's interior and its fictional exterior was released by Disney, and later fully identified in a video discussion featuring [[Walt Disney Imagineering|Disney Imagineers]].<ref name="YouTube" />
