'''Fish and grits''' was a type [[Foodstuff|dish]] that the [[Zabrak]] [[Bram Shano]] made for [[Kay Vess]] and [[Nix]] while they were on [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Kay Vess mentioned how much better [[Che Mosskà]] was while eating a plate of it at a [[Irio|food stall]] in [[Market District]] of [[Mirogana]] with Nix.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
