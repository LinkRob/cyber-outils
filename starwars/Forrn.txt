'''Forrn''' was a [[Planet|world]] located in the [[Forrn system]] within the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Xan sector]]. The world was ruled by [[Empress]] [[Eliaora]] by [[4 ABY]]. At one point, the [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] visited Forrn and established a connection with Eliaora.

That [[Standard year|year]], knowing Forrn had a [[Forrn's moon|moon]] with deposits of the energy-rich material [[carnium]], Organa reached out to the world in the hopes they would side with the forming [[New Republic]]. The [[Gender|princess]] was reasonably confident she could convince Forrn to side with the Republic due to her connection with the empress.

==Description==
Forrn was a [[Planet|world]]<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> located in the [[Forrn system]] within the [[Xan sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> The world had a [[Forrn's moon|moon]] with deposits of the energy-rich material [[carnium]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> It was situated in grid square Q-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> A [[Manda–Mon Gazza hyperlane|hyperspace route]] connected Forrn to the [[Ando system|Ando]] and [[Leritor system]]s.<ref name="Mandalorian Visual Guide" />

==History==
[[File:Leia-wedding-dress.png|thumb|right|200px|Leia Organa (pictured) visited Forrn and established a connection with Empress Eliaora.]]
By [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'' to [[4 ABY]].</ref> Forrn was ruled by [[Empress]] [[Eliaora]]. During a visit she undertook to Forrn,<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> also by the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[human]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] established a connection with Eliaora.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />

After the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> in 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Organa requested that [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Rebel Intelligence]] [[Major]] [[Nioma]] send her data on Forrn, as she planned to reach out to worlds with energy-rich resources in the hopes they would side with the forming [[New Republic]]. Organa discovered that the world had either remained independent or, at minimum, had been forced into a financial agreement with the [[Galactic Empire]]. Reasonably confident that she could convince Forrn to side with the Republic, the [[Gender|princess]] sent a [[holomessage]] to Eliaora and used her established connection with the empress to speak to them at a personal level.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> Forrn was part of the New Republic's territories.<ref name="Mandalorian Visual Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Forrn was first mentioned in ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'', a [[2022]] novel written by [[Beth Revis]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />
