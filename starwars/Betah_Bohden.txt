{{Quote|She's the bounty hunter who found the elusive Betah Bohden. Slaughtered him and all his guards single-handedly. And may or may not, have eaten their remains.|Gwi, describing Bohden's demise at the hands of Honnah|<ref name="Target Vader 1" />}}
'''Betah Bohden''' was an individual who had the reputation of being elusive.<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Bohden was [[Hunting|hunted]] down by the [[Gamorrean]] tracker [[Honnah]], who [[Death|killed]] both him and his guards. Bohden's death helped cement her reputation as one of the best trackers in the [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunting]] field.<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> In 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Gwi]],<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> the leader of the [[Hidden Hand]] criminal syndicate,<ref name="Target Vader 6">[[Target Vader 6|''Target Vader'' 6]]</ref> described Honnah's reputation to the [[cyborg]] [[bounty]] hunter [[Beilert Valance]] while attempting to recruit him for a mission to hunt down Vader, speculating that the Gamorrean may have [[Diet|eaten]] Bohden and his guards' remains.<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Betah Bohden was mentioned in the [[Target Vader 1|first issue]] of the [[2019]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]''. The issue was written by [[Robbie Thompson]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> on [[July 3]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/77664/star_wars_target_vader_2019_1|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #1}}</ref>
