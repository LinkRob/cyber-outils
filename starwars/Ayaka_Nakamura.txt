'''Ayaka Nakamura''' is a digital painter and color designer<ref name="TMDB" /> who works for [[WIT Studio]]. They drew concept art of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Sevn]], [[Jin-Sim Canbelon]] and members of the [[Unidentified insurgent group (Genya)|resistance group]] for the non-[[canon]] short film "[[The Bounty Hunters]]," which released as part of ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/why-we-love-star-wars-visions|text=Why We Love the Unique Stories of ''Star Wars: Visions''}}</ref>
