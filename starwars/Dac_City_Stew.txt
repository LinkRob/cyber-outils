'''Dac City Stew''' was a traditional seafood [[stew]] on the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]]. The [[foodstuff|dish]] became a signature of the [[Quarren]]/[[Mon Calamari]] [[hybrid]] prep cook [[Jom Jarusch]]. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] found that none of the versions that he tried while visiting [[Dac City]] on Mon Cala were as good as Jom's recipe. The chef later included a recipe for the dish in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].

==Behind the scenes==
Dac City Stew was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish makes use of [[Wikipedia:Butter|unsalted butter]], [[Wikipedia:Flour|all-purpose flour]], a chopped [[Wikipedia:Onion|yellow onion]], a chopped [[Wikipedia:Bell pepper|green bell pepper]], dice [[Wikipedia:Celery|celery ribs]], minced [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic cloves]], cut [[Wikipedia:Andouille|Andouille sausage]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic powder|garlic powder]], [[Wikipedia:Onion powder|onion powder]], [[Wikipedia:Oregano|oregano]], [[Wikipedia:Paprika|paprika]], [[Wikipedia:Thyme|thyme]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], [[Wikipedia:Cayenne|cayenne]], [[Wikipedia:Bay leaves|bay leaves]], [[Wikipedia:Chicken|chicken]] [[Wikipedia:Broth|broth]], stewed [[Wikipedia:Tomato|tomatos]], [[Wikipedia:Filé powder|gumbo filé]], [[Wikipedia:Shrimp|shrimp]], and fresh [[Wikipedia:Parsley|parsley]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
