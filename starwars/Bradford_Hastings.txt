'''Bradford Hastings''' is an [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice actor]] and audiobook narrator. Hastings narrated the [[2018]] audiobook ''[[Join the Resistance: Books 1-3]]''<ref name="Join Resistance" /> and would go on to narrated the [[2019]] audiobook ''[[Star Wars Storybook Collection]]''.<ref name="Storybook Collection">''[[Star Wars Storybook Collection]]''</ref>
