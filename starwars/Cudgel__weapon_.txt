'''Cudgels''' were a type of [[melee]] weapon. The antique dealer and undercover rebel [[Luthen Rael]] had an [[Utapaun monk cudgel]] displayed at his [[Coruscant]] art gallery [[Galactic Antiquities and Objects of Interest]] in [[5 BBY]].<ref name="Andor 4">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> By [[2 BBY]],<ref name="WttR date">{{AndorDate|209}}</ref> Rael had acquired another ancient cudgel, one that featured a hidden [[beskar]] blade within its shaft.<ref name="Welcome to the Rebellion">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref><ref name="Script">{{Disney|subdomain=assets.debut|url=documents/Andor%20209%20--%20POST%20FINAL%20--%203.3.24%5B1%5D.pdf|text=Episode #209 "Welcome To The Rebellion" Script|format=PDF}}</ref>
