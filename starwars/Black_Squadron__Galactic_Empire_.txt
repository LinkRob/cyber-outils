'''Black Squadron''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] [[squadron]].<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:Black_Squadron_TCGGA.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Vader flanked by Black Squadron at the Battle of Yavin.]]
During the [[Battle of Yavin]], TIE fighters from [[Color|Black]] Squadron launched from the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] to engage [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[starfighter]]s that were assaulting the Imperial [[Space station|station]]. Led by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in his [[TIE Advanced x1]], Black Squadron shot down many rebel fighters, though several were able to begin their attack runs on the [[Death Star]]'s [[thermal exhaust port]], a small opening on the [[battle station]] that led directly to the main [[reactor]]. Ultimately, [[Rebel pilot|rebel]] [[pilot]] [[Luke Skywalker]] was able to fire two [[proton torpedo]]s directly down the exhaust port, which triggered a chain reaction that destroyed the station.<ref name="Episode IV" />

[[Senior Lieutenant]] [[Iden Versio]], a [[Gender|female]] pilot in the [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]], served as a member of Black Squadron as [[Sigma Three]], and was one of the few Imperial survivors of the battle. Versio's TIE fighter was damaged by debris from the Death Star, and she crashed on [[Yavin 4]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
