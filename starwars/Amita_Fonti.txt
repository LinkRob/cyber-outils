'''Amita Fonti'''<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode310.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref> was a [[Gossam]]<ref name="Concept Art Gallery" /> [[senator]] that represented the [[Commerce Guild]]<ref name="Collapse of the Republic" /> in the [[Separatist Senate]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> Alongside [[Separatist Congress Leader|Speaker]] [[Bec Lawise]] and Senator [[Voe Atell]] of the [[Corporate Alliance]], Fonti guided talks in the legislative body.<ref name="Collapse of the Republic" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides Date">{{TCWcanondates|310}}</ref> they were present on the Separatist [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Raxus Secundus]] during Senator [[Mina Bonteri]]'s proposal to open [[Confederate–Republic peace initiative|peace talks]] with the Republic.<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="A Friend in Need Date">{{TCWcanondates|414}}</ref> Fonti, Atell, and Lawise participated in a [[Confederate–Republic peace conference|peace conference]] with Republic representatives on the neutral world [[Mandalore]], though the negotiations ultimately collapsed.<ref name="A Friend in Need">{{TCW|A Friend in Need}}</ref>

Like other corporate-allied senators in the Separatist Senate, Fonti had little interest in peace.<ref name="Collapse of the Republic" /> They had [[Color|light blue]] skin and blue [[eye]]s<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> and wore a set of four golden [[neck ring]]s<ref name="A Friend in Need" /> and a brown [[Hat|cap]], as well as a long red, white, and black gown on Raxus<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> and a nearly identical one on Mandalore that swapped the red for blue.<ref name="A Friend in Need" />

==Behind the scenes==
Amita Fonti first appeared, albeit unidentified, in "[[Heroes on Both Sides]]," the tenth episode of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]],<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> which originally aired on [[November 19]], [[2010]]. They were identified in the [[The Clone Wars Episode Guides|episode guide]] for "Heroes on Both Sides" available on [[StarWars.com]], though it did not specify which Gossam was Fonti.<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides Guide" /> A Gossam senator<ref name="A Friend in Need" /> later confirmed to be Fonti<ref name="Fonti">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-disappeared-part-i-concept-art-gallery|text="The Disappeared, Part I" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> appeared in "[[A Friend in Need]]," the fourteenth episode of ''The Clone Wars''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]],<ref name="A Friend in Need" /> which originally aired on [[January 13]], [[2012]].<ref name="Insider 130: Launch Pad">{{InsiderCite|130|Launchpad}}</ref> Their model was later retextured as a Gossam [[Gender|female]] [[Merchant|vendor]]<ref name="Concept Art Gallery" /> for use in "[[The Disappeared, Part II]]," the ninth episode of ''The Clone Wars''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth season]],<ref name="The Disappeared, Part II">{{TCW|The Disappeared, Part II}}</ref> which originally aired on [[March 1]], [[2014]].<ref name="German TV Schedule">{{WebCite|url=http://www.fernsehserien.de/star-wars-the-clone-wars/episodenguide/staffel-6/9660|text=Star Wars: The Clone Wars Staffel 6 Episodenguide|work=[https://www.fernsehserien.de/ https://www.fernsehserien.de/]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/lRTqV}}</ref>
