'''Bertie Carvel''' (born [[September 6]], [[1977]]) is an [[Wikipedia:British people|English]] voice actor who voiced the male Imperial Agent and additional voices for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' and its four expansions.

==Works==
===Gameography===
*''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' &mdash; Imperial Agent (male) and additional voices
*''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Rise of the Hutt Cartel]]'' &mdash; Imperial Agent (male)
*''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Shadow of Revan]]'' &mdash; Imperial Agent (male)
*''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Fallen Empire]]'' &mdash; Imperial Agent (male)
*''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Eternal Throne]]'' &mdash; Imperial Agent (male)
