'''Boglings''' were small [[Hair|furry]] [[Species|creatures]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Bogano]]. They dug a number of tunnels and nests throughout the rocky plains and [[Swamp|marshes]] of Bogano, working in large packs. They were preyed upon by both the [[bog rat]]s and [[splox]], with the latter often luring boglings out of the burrows to be attacked by bog rats, allowing the splox to enter the burrow and eat the bogling's eggs.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> Boglings could be considered valuable on the black market.<ref name="Adventures 8" /> The [[Latero]] [[Greez Dritus]] accidentally brought a group of boglings to the planet [[Koboh]].<ref name="Jedi Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Bogling.jpg|thumb|left|200px|A bogling]]
Boglings reproduced by laying [[egg]]s, which they hatched in underground nests.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

While their bodies were generally [[Hair|furry]], boglings had bare feet and [[Limb|leg]] which they evolved to run on, allowing them to navigate [[Bogano]]'s rocky and marsh-like terrain, as well as to quickly jump, change direction, and escape predators. The fur on the rest of their body compensated for heat loss though the feet.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==Behavior==
{{Quote|Poor bogling was killed. Was it trying to defend its family?|Cal Kestis discovers a dead bogling in the lair of an [[Oggdo Bogdo]]|<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />}}
Bogling behavior centered around defense against the many predators on Bogano. Some were even found [[Death|dead]] fighting against an [[Oggdo]], desperately trying to defend their families. Boglings spent around one-third of their lives digging a tunnel network beneath Bogano's surface, that connected their nests underground.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> These tunnels were often hidden from view and contained traps created as a defense against predators and intruders.<ref name="Bogling">{{GoC|Bogling}}</ref> They collaborated in large packs called "heckles," with each bogling able to dig up to five [[meter]]s a [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
