The '''Bleuf system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]]. It was located in grid square L-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the [[planet]] [[Bleuf]].<ref name="Origin" />
