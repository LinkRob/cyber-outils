'''Erinya''' was a [[human]] [[gender|female]] who was a member of the [[4040s]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] activist group active during the first [[Standard year|year]] of the Empire's reign. To [[Chemish Runazha]], a member of [[Imperial Intelligence]] [[spy|spying]] on the group, Erinya and her [[marriage|partner]] [[Jayu]] appeared to be more interested in socializing than [[politics]], though Erinya would talk about her work with the [[Coruscant Underworld Police]].<ref name="MoF" />
