'''Drya Thornne''' was a [[humanoid]] [[lieutenant]] of the [[Bedlam Raiders]] who was skilled enough to be gifted an [[Color|orange]]-bladed [[Drya Thornne's lightsaber|lightsaber]] with retractable [[Crossguard lightsaber|crossguards]] by the Raiders' leader, [[Rayvis]].<ref name="Survivor" />

==Biography==
[[File:YellowBladedIndividual.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Drya met their end dueling Jedi Knight Cal Kestis.]]
In [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> Thornne was with Rayvis during his search for [[Dagan Gera]], who they found after his initial defeat against [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]]. Thornne was among those sent to the [[Republic Koboh lunar satellite facility|facility]] on the [[Shattered Moon]] of [[Koboh]] to [[Mission to the Shattered Moon|search]] for an [[Abyss compass]]. While on the station, the lieutenant encountered Cal Kestis in the upper levels of the foundry and attacked him.<ref name="Survivor" />

Kestis ultimately won the [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] and [[Death|slew]] Thornne, taking the custom [[Emitter matrix|emitter]] from [[Drya Thornne's lightsaber|Thornne's lightsaber]] and using it to modify [[Cal Kestis' second lightsaber|his own]].<ref name="Survivor" />

==Equipment==
Drya Thornne wore a blue [[Clothing|outfit]] with a brown tunic and gray [[armor]] over it, along with a helmet that covered their entire head. They also wore a gray [[helmet]] and used a [[wrist blaster]], [[grenade]]s, and a portable [[cloaking device]] in combat. Their primary weapon, however, was an orange [[crossguard lightsaber]] gifted to them by [[Rayvis]] from his personal collection.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drya Thornne was created for the [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'', serving as a boss that the player must defeat to progress the story.<ref name="Survivor" /> Prior to the game's release, Thornne was revealed in the teaser trailer for the game that was shown at the 2022 Video Game Awards on [[December 8]], [[2022]].<ref name="Jedi: Survivor Trailer">{{EASWYouTube|VRaobDJjiec|Star Wars Jedi: Survivor &ndash; Official Reveal Trailer}}</ref> The trailer used the same lightsaber model as Cal's, but was later changed.
