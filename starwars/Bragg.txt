'''Bragg''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[Captain]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] following the end of the [[Clone Wars]] in [[19 BBY]]. Tasked with promoting cooperation from the uneasy population of [[Raxus Secundus|Raxus]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]’ [[capital]], she liaised with its [[Senator]], [[Avi Singh]], to get the residents onboard. Bragg addressed the people of Raxus during a gathering, and brought forth Singh to help her. However, the senator defied the Empire, leading the captain to have him arrested and scatter the crowd.

Bragg had Singh [[Prison|imprisoned]] for [[Torture|interrogation]] at his [[Avi Singh's residence|residence]]. But as she began the interrogation, the rogue [[clone commando]] unit known as the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]] intervened and [[stun]]ned her and her colleagues. After regaining consciousness, Bragg located the Bad Batch to a captured [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] and reported it to her forces, ordering them to pin the rogue [[Cloning|clones]] down. The Bad Batch ultimately escaped with Singh and his [[RA-7 protocol droid]] [[GS-8]].

==Biography==
===Enforcing Imperial rule===
{{Quote|To deal with the rise in incursions, a mandatory curfew will be put into effect for your protection. Such measures were approved by your esteemed senator, Avi Singh, who would like to say a few words about the importance of cooperation as we build a unified galaxy.|Bragg addresses the people of Raxus|<ref name="Common Ground" />}}
In the wake of the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Common Ground" /> between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], which ended in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Bragg served in the [[Imperial Military|Military]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Republic's successor, with the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[captain]].<ref name="Common Ground" /> As a loyal enforcer of the Empire, she was tasked with securing local support during the Imperial [[Occupation of Raxus Secundus|occupation]] of [[Raxus Secundus|Raxus]],<ref name="Bragg DB">{{Databank|captain-bragg|Captain Bragg}}</ref> which served as the Confederate [[capital]] during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Raxus DB">{{Databank|raxus|Raxus}}</ref> Bragg attempted to work with Raxus' [[Senator]] [[Avi Singh]] to ensure the cooperation of the world's population, which had shown resistance against the Empire, and a speech was arranged at the Confederacy's [[Separatist Senate Building|Senate Building]] in the [[city]] of [[Raxulon]].<ref name="Common Ground" />

[[File:BraggSpeech-CG.png|thumb|left|300px|Bragg speaks before the people of Raxus]]
With residents of Raxus gathered before the senate building, Bragg came forward to address them from a balcony, assuring the people that the Empire was not their enemy. The Captain added the Separatist planets would be treated fairly in exchange for loyalty, which she stressed had not been displayed by Raxus' population. As her speech continued, she announced a curfew to deal with incursions that had taken place, much to the dismay of the crowd. Promising that Singh had approved of this, she gave the que for the senator to speak about cooperation. Singh implored his people to follow the Empire's law but then had a change of heart, choosing to then condemn the Imperial occupation. Bragg had her [[clone trooper]]s arrest the senator and ordered for the crowd to be scattered.<ref name="Common Ground" />

===Failed interrogations===
{{Quote|Defying Imperial law and openly inciting civil discord. I thought we had an understanding, Senator.''"<br />"''I refuse to be your puppet. My loyalty is to the people not the Empire.''"<br />"''An unwise choice. Regardless, we will acquire what we need from you.|Bragg and Avi Singh|<ref name="Common Ground" />}}
[[File:AviSinghCellar-CG.png|thumb|right|300px|Bragg visits Avi Singh for interrogation]]
Singh was [[Prison|imprisoned]] in a bar at his [[Avi Singh's residence|residence]], where Bragg brought an [[IT-O interrogation droid]] in to [[Torture|interrogate]] him. After the captain poured herself a drink and expressed disappointment in his actions, Singh proclaimed that his loyalty was to his people. Bragg called it an unwise choice and brought forth her [[interrogation droid]] to get information from her captive. That was when the door to the room opened and a [[Smoke grenade|smoke bomb]] was thrown inside. The attackers, the rogue [[clone commando]] unit known as the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]], [[stun]]ned Bragg's two [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s before stunning the captain herself and her [[droid]].<ref name="Common Ground" />

The rogue clones rescued Singh, assisted by his [[RA-7 protocol droid]] [[GS-8]], and escaped the building onto an [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] which the unit captured. After regaining consciousness, Bragg and her two troopers followed the Bad Batch's trail to a balcony which they had ziplined from and watched as the captured [[walker]] fled from the building. The captain reported the captured All Terrain Tactical Enforcer to her forces and ordered them to pin it down. However, despite the efforts of Bragg's forces, the Bad Batch was able to escape Raxus with Singh and GS-8.<ref name="Common Ground" />

===Demobilization of the clones===
{{Quote|We're soldiers. What are we supposed to do?''"<br />"''Please direct all your questions and concerns to the Imperial Information Bureau.|A clone trooper and Captain Bragg's response|<ref name="TBB">{{TBB|The Outpost}}</ref>}}
Months later with the passage of the [[Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill]], a piece of legislation which would [[Project War-Mantle|phase out]] the clone troopers and replace them with recruited [[TK stormtrooper]]s, Captain Bragg escorted a trio of clones out of the [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|Center for Military Operations]] on [[Coruscant]] as clones were being decommissioned. She told them that the Empire thanked them for their years of service and wished them well on their retirement. When a trooper sarcastically said it was forced retirement and asked what the clones were supposed to do now, Captain Bragg answered that they had to direct all their questions and concerns to the [[Imperial Information Bureau]].<ref name="TBB" />

===Skirmish on Wayland===
{{Quote|Governor Tarkin, I have completed my assessment. Various wings of the base sustained heavy damage. Doctor Hemlock and several troopers perished in the attack.''"<br />"''Was any of his research salvageable?''"<br />"''No, Governor. The databanks were destroyed.''"<br />"''Hemlock's failings have cost us enough. Shutter this facility indefinitely and redestribute all funding to Project Stardust.|Bragg informing Governor Tarkin|<ref name="The Cavalry Has Arrived">{{TBB|The Cavalry Has Arrived}}</ref>}}
After the partial destruction of [[Tantiss Base]] by a [[Specimen A|Zillo Beast]] and the [[Clone trooper|clone]] insurgents who saved Senator Singh, Captain Bragg accompanied [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and his fleet to the ruins on [[Wayland]]. After Bragg completed her assessment, she informed Tarkin about the state of the facility and the deaths of [[Chief Scientist]] [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]] and several of his men and reported the loss of all his research due to the databanks' destruction when he asked if it was salvageable. Since Tarkin felt Hemlock's failings had cost the Empire enough, he posthumously punished him by ordering Bragg to close the secret base indefinitely and redistribute all funding and the remaining assets to [[Project Stardust]] so she agreed with a nod of her head.<ref name="The Cavalry Has Arrived" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|The Empire is not your enemy. We promise to treat all planets under the Confederacy of Independent Systems fairly. All we asked in exchange was for your loyalty. Yet that has not been forthcoming here on Raxus.|Bragg calls for cooperation from the people of Raxus|<ref name="Common Ground" />}}
Bragg was a [[human]] female with [[Color|light]] skin, black [[hair]], and blue [[eye]]s. She promoted loyalty for the Empire, stressing the importance of cooperation to the uneasy residents of Raxus. While she preferred to do things the simple and peaceful way, if this failed however, Bragg had no qualms about using force and more ruthless methods to achieve her goals. The captain also expressed confidence in her alignment and methods, jibing at Singh's arrogance against the Empire and her interrogation droid.<ref name="Common Ground" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|They've commandeered one of the walkers. Pin them down.|Bragg speaks over her comlink|<ref name="Common Ground" />}}
Bragg [[Clothing|wore]] the standard grey [[Imperial officer's uniform|uniform]] of an Imperial officer, including an [[Imperial kepi]] and a rank plaque which displayed four red squares in a line to define her rank of [[captain]]. She also used a [[comlink]] to contact her forces.<ref name="Common Ground" />
