The '''EndTimes trilogy''' was a trilogy in the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'' campaign]] that was released in [[2006]] and written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] and [[Jae Walker]]. It focused on the fate of the [[Darkstaff]] and [[Len Markus]], a major villain from the campaign.
