A '''black market hauler''' was operated by the [[pilot]] [[Argomon]] and the [[droid]] [[B-333]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. The [[black market]] hauler made deliveries for the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] within their [[Occlusion Zone]] territory behind the [[Stormwall]], including to the [[prison ship]] [[Korvix Vorn]]. The [[starship]] secretly served as a method of gathering information from within Nihil space, which was reported to the [[Jedi Order]] by B-333. When the [[Jedi]] planned to make use of the Korvix Vorn's imminent emergence from the Occlusion Zone and required a [[Homing beacon|tracker]] to be placed aboard the vessel, Argomon made a delivery of [[Mycopram]] to the [[scientist]] [[Niv Drendow Apruk]] aboard the prison ship.<ref name="THRA 3" />
