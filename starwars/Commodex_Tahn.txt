'''Commodex Tahn''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] from [[Naboo]].<ref name="V10" /> During the last [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], he worked as a military clerk. At some point before the last [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Commodex Tahn's father|his]] [[Parent|father]] [[Death|died]], prompting Tahn to leave Republic service and enter the family trade as a [[mortician]].<ref name="V9">[[Darth Vader (2015) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 9]]</ref> It was he who, at the conclusion of the Clone Wars, prepared the body of [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], [[senator]] and former [[Monarch of Naboo|queen]] of Naboo, for [[Padmé Amidala's funeral|burial]]. Shortly before her death, Amidala was pregnant with [[twin]]s, whose father was none other than [[Anakin Skywalker]], a [[Jedi Knight]] who had turned to [[Dark side of the Force|evil]]. Amidala had managed to give birth to her children, who were spirited away so Skywalker would never find them. Tahn, as a mortician, was tasked with doctoring the corpse to make it appear that Amidala was still pregnant so Skywalker did not suspect that his children were alive.<ref name="V10" /> Thereafter, he decided to retire.<ref name="V9" />

Nearly twenty years later, Skywalker, who went by the [[Sith]] moniker of [[Darth]] Vader, learned that he had a [[Luke Skywalker|son]]. Commodex Tahn received a [[Assault on Commodex Tahn's villa|visit]] from [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]], an agent of Vader, who [[torture]]d him into confessing. Tahn, however, only told Aphra what she already knew, that Amidala had given birth to a boy. The mortician, however, resisted mentioning the existence of a second child, a [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|girl]]. Despite his confession, Tahn was killed by [[0-0-0|Triple Zero]], a sadistic [[droid]] who worked with Aphra.<ref name="V10" />
