A [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] '''species''' [[Homeworld/Legends|hailed]] from the high-[[Gravity/Legends|gravity]] [[Mid Rim/Legends|Mid Rim]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Kaump III]]. Its members had a short stature and [[Limb/Legends|limbs]] as well as a dense body structure and a flat face. The [[Mercenary/Legends|mercenary]] and [[Pirate/Legends|pirate]] [[Chanchaz Iryt]] was a member of the [[Species/Legends|species]] who grew up on Kaump III. Initially studying [[Repulsorlift/Legends|repulsorlift]] mechanics, he eventually turned to a life of crime and committed a series of high-profile [[Thief/Legends|robberies]] before leaving his homeworld aboard a [[Luxury liner/Legends|luxury liner]]. Iryt subsequently went on to become a member of [[the Fatal Visionaries]] pirate gang.

==Biology and appearance==
The [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]] of which the [[Mercenary/Legends|mercenary]] and [[Pirate/Legends|pirate]] [[Chanchaz Iryt]] was a member was [[Homeworld/Legends|native]] to [[Kaump III]], a high-[[Gravity/Legends|gravity]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="Polyhedron68" /> situated in the [[Mid Rim/Legends|Mid Rim's]] [[Bruanii sector/Legends|Bruanii sector]].<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Kaump system/Legends|Kaump system]]</ref> Due to the gravity of their homeworld, the species had a short stature and a dense body structure. Its members also had short [[Limb/Legends|limbs]] and a flat face. Iryt was 1.1 [[Meter/Legends|meters]] tall and had [[Color/Legends|brown]] [[Eye/Legends|eyes]] and [[Hair/Legends|hair]].<ref name="Polyhedron68" />

==Society and culture==
The species' homeworld, Kaump III, featured at least one [[City/Legends|city]] with an [[Currency/Legends|affluent]] neighborhood as well as several rough [[Nightclub/Legends|nightclubs]].<ref name="Polyhedron68" />

==History==
Chanchaz Iryt was a physically strong&mdash;as well as violent and sadistic&mdash;member of the species who was [[Reproduction/Legends|born]] on Kaump III. During his early life, Iryt studied all he could about [[Repulsorlift/Legends|repulsorlift]] mechanics while also spending the [[Local day/Legends|nights]] learning about brawling and bullying at some of his homeworld's nightclubs. He worked on [[Engine/Legends|engines]] and also [[Thief/Legends|robbed]] [[Alcohol/Legends|intoxicated]] people and lost tourists for drink money. Soon, however, Iryt decided that he was destined for more in life and committed a series of highly lucrative and highly publicized robberies in a wealthy district of a local city. Thereafter, at some point by the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], Iryt left Kaump III aboard a [[Luxury liner/Legends|luxury liner]] traveling to<ref name="Polyhedron68" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]] planet [[Bespin/Legends|Bespin]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>

==Chanchaz Iryt's species in the galaxy==
In Bespin's [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City]] settlement, Iryt met the [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-sensitive]] [[Zhen Mirat]] and eventually became the chief [[Lieutenant/Legends|lieutenant]] of the latter's pirate gang, [[the Fatal Visionaries]].<ref name="Polyhedron68" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chanchaz Iryt's species was mentioned in "[[Iron Hands, Captive Hearts]]," a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] source article written by [[Joe Littrell]] for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' and published in the [[Polyhedron 68|sixty-eighth issue]] of the ''[[Polyhedron]]'' magazine in [[February]] [[1992]]. Since that article was released outside of the [[Lucas Licensing]] process,<ref name="Polyhedron68" /> it was considered non-[[canon]]ical with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="UnlicensedStatus">{{UnlicensedStatus}}</ref>
