"'''A Guide to the Vehicles in ''Shadows of the Empire'''''" is a large fold-out poster included in [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 9|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 9]]. The poster features artwork and text detailing nine key [[Starship/Legends|starships]] and vehicles prominently included in the [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire|''Shadows of the Empire'']] multimedia project.
