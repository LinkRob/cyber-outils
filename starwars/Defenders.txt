'''''Defenders''''' was a [[Resistance]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] created by the [[Alderaanian]] [[artist]] [[Janray Tessime]]. It depicted two Resistance soldiers standing firm, as well as a [[lurmen]] kneeling down. Up at the top, it stated "Join the Resistance. Defend the [[New Republic|Republic]]." In the middle, in big letters with the Starbird in the background, it said "Protect Those Who Can't Defend Themselves."<ref name="Propaganda" />
