Extending their own lives, as well, so that they could live for centuries beyond their intended expiration?|Yupe Tashu, to [[Wedge Antilles]]|<ref name="Aftermath" />}}
The ability to '''drain''' life [[The Force|Force]] energy from other beings was utilized by various [[Force-sensitive]]s throughout [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]].

==Description==
The draining of [[The Force|Force]] energy from an individual into oneself was an ability associated to the [[dark side of the Force]] and practiced by various figures. This process consisted of the summoning of life energies from others by the practitioner, who then absorbed it.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Users==
===Ancient Sith===
According to the [[Emperor's adviser|Imperial adviser]] [[Yupe Tashu]], some [[Sith Lord]]s of old drained Force energy from other individuals. By siphoning life from others, the ancient [[Sith]] strengthened their connection to the dark side of the Force and extended their own lives by [[Standard century|centuries]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

===Nightsister magick===
{{Quote|You ''feel'' that, Count? Mother draws your very ''life force'' from your withered bones&hellip; draining you will give her all she needs to fully take form.|Darth [[Maul]] to Count Dooku, as the latter succumbs to the Talzin's magick|<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Four" />}}
[[File:Talzin_sacrifices_Dooku.jpg|thumb|left|224px|Dooku succumbs to Talzin's magick]]
The [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[shaman]] [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]]'s spirit was able to emerge from the [[spirit world]] and into a stone monolith to directly [[Possession|possess]] the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]], allowing her to drain his life energy and regain her physical form in the process. Green-[[color]]ed [[Gas|mist]] emmitted from Dooku's body initially, and at a later phase, the substance flowed from the Count's oral and nasal cavities, as the life force was forcefully taken by Talzin. The victim collapsed screaming in pain.<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Four" />

The ritual was intended to restore Talzin's body permanently, although the witch was interrupted by the arrival of [[Darth Sidious]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]]. The latter punctured her body with two [[lightsaber]]s, reducing the Nightsister's physical form to a dried husk and ending Talzin's life.<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Four" />

===The abersyn queen===
{{Quote|Oh, it has been ''too long'' since I last tasted Jedi.|The Queen of Ktath'atn|<ref name="Screaming1" />}}
[[File:FeedingTheQueen-TheScreamingCitadel1.png|thumb|right|224px|The Queen of Ktath'atn fed on her subjects]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Queen of Ktath'atn]], a [[Sentience|sapient]] [[abersyn symbiote]] who ruled her [[Queen of Ktath'atn's hive|realm]] as its [[queen]] from a [[humanoid]] host body, could "feed" on others, empowering herself through mass-feeding from her [[human]] subjects in their [[sleep]]. This procedure was done on a [[Ktath'atn village]] some distance away from the [[Citadel of Ktath'atn]], on a balcony of which the symbiote fed from. Those subject to her powers were discomforted as red, glittering energy was extracted from their bodies through the mouth, and flew into that of their leader.<ref name="Screaming1" />

The [[Force-sensitive]] [[planet]]ary [[Monarchy|sovereign]] took particular pleasure from feeding on Jedi who were strong in the Force. She welcomed guests into her citadel for only a [[Standard day|day]] each [[Standard year|year]]. On such an event, the Queen of Ktath'atn received strange life forms from throughout the galaxy in exchange for various favors and gifts given out to guests. Subsequent to this, the ruler then fed upon the life forms.<ref name="Screaming1" />

===Sith Eternal===
{{Quote|The life force of your bond&hellip;a dyad in the Force. A power like life itself. Unseen for generations. And now, the power of two restores the one&hellip; true&hellip; Emperor!|Darth Sidious, before draining Rey and Ben Solo|<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>}}
Under the use of the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, leader of the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Religion|cult]], the act of Force drain allowed for the appearance of phantom white streams stemming from the chests of those being deprived of their life force and whirling into the [[Limb|palms]] of the practitioner. Those who suffered from this ritual found themselves greatly weakened.<ref name="Episode IX" />

During the [[Battle of Exegol]] of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], the [[Transference|resurgent]] [[Darth]] Sidious drained the Force energy of the Jedi [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and [[Ben Solo]] to increase his strength, rejuvenating himself to a state of power<ref name="Episode IX" /> comparable to his at the [[time]] of his [[Duel in Palpatine's office|triumph]] on [[Coruscant]]<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> and his [[Battle of Endor|defeat]] over [[Endor]].<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> But Rey, stood up, ignited her lightsabers, and destroyed Sidious once and for all when he tried to kill her with Force lightning.
