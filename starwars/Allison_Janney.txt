'''Allison Janney''' is an American actress best known for her roles in [[Wikipedia:The West Wing|''The West Wing'']] and [[Wikipedia:Mom (TV series)|''Mom'']]. She provided the voice of the [[Droid/Legends|droid]] [[Aly San San/Legends|Aly San San]] in the [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney Parks]] attraction ''[[Star Tours: The Adventures Continue]]''.<ref name="Countdown">{{Disney|domain=go.com|url=disneyfans/insider/article/20110510|text=Countdown to an All-New Star Tours|date=2011-05-10}}</ref>
