'''''Escape from Jakku''''' is a [[canon]] young-readers storybook that collects the stories ''[[Finn & Rey Escape!]]'' and ''[[Han & Chewie Return!]]'' from author [[Michael Siglain]], which adapt portions of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']]. It was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] in [[December]] [[2015]] and was available exclusively through the [[Wikipedia:Disney Store|Disney Store]].<ref name="Disney" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''When Finn and Rey find themselves on the run, they must work together to escape from the evil First Order. Han Solo and Chewbacca return in a heroic new adventure set in a galaxy far, far away.''<ref name="Disney" />
