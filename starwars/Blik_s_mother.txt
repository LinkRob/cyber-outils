During the [[Republic Era]],<ref name="Date">{{TalesDate|U4}}</ref> a [[Gender|female]]<ref name="TOTU 104 AD" /> [[Duros]]<ref name="TOTU 104" /> was the '''mother''' of the [[youngling]] [[Blik]] and the partner of [[Blik's parent|another]] of its [[parent]]s.<ref name="TOTU 104 AD" />

Between c. [[62 BBY]] and [[33 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the woman was present on the [[planet]] of [[Duro]] alongside their partner and their child. As the trio strolled the streets, Blik wandered away from<ref name="TOTU 104" /> its<ref name="TOTU 104 AD" /> parents and attempted to chat up the Duros younglings [[Cad Bane|Colby]] and [[Niro]] as they caused trouble on the sidewalk. Blik's parents caught up with it before any conversation could unfold, with its mother telling it to get away from the "street [[rat]]s" as they approached their child. The woman also reminded their offspring that that they and their partner had instructed it not to hand around with such individuals. As the individual and their partner led their child away, Blik looked back at its peers, and its mother&mdash;also turning back&mdash;rushed it up with a pat on its shoulder.<ref name="TOTU 104" />

A [[Color|blue]]-skinned female Duros with red [[eye]]s and a stocky build, the mother wore close-fitting, [[Clothing|garments]] with a belted waist. They had objections to their son hanging around with street children.<ref name="TOTU 104" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blik's mother appeared in the "[[The Good Life]]," an episode of the animated miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Underworld]]'',<ref name="TOTU 104" /> which was released on [[May 4]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|GkywNANueew|Tales of the Underworld &#124; Official Trailer &#124; Available May 4 on Disney+}}</ref> The character was voiced by [[Vanessa Marshall]], and was only identified as "Mother" in the credits.<ref name="TOTU 104" />
