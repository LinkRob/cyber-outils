'''Fluorescent plankton''' was a type of [[Bioluminescence/Legends|bioluminescent]] [[Plankton/Legends|plankton]] that dwelled in [[Cave/Legends|caves]]. They were cited in a song called "[[If Only I Could Let Go and Cry]]."<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fluorescent plankton was mentioned in ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a book published in [[1993]].<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]] within the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
