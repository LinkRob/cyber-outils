'''Aviral Anil Vineeta Kumar''' is an [[Wikipedia:Indian people|Indian]] musician operating out of [[Wikipedia:Mumbai|Mumbai]], [[Wikipedia:India|India]], who also engages in voice acting for various [[Wikipedia:Hindi cinema|Bollywood]]-related projects.<ref name="Instagram Profile" /> Kumar voiced an unnamed [[stormtrooper]] in the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|Volume 2]] episode "[[The Bandits of Golak]]", written and directed by [[Ishan Shukla]], and produced by [[88 Pictures]],<ref name="Bandits">{{Visions|The Bandits of Golak}}</ref> with his voice line also being featured in one of the volume's official trailers.<ref name="Instagram Post">{{Instagram|username=aviral_kumar|url=p/CrlrfDvtjzj|name=[[Aviral Kumar]]|date=2023-04-28|quote=I recently played a character of a Storm Trooper in Star Wars Vision 2, releasing on 4th May on @disneyplushotstar . @me_rajr @treasuringvoice thank you for having me on board for this project ✨ .
