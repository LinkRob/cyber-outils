A [[lightsaber]] '''auxiliary''' was a [[Color|red]]-bladed, fan-shaped bespoke [[Sith lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber auxiliary|auxiliary]] used during the [[Sith rebellion]] by the [[Sith]] [[Kouru]]'s [[Kouru's master|master]]. The [[holographic blueprint]]s for the construction of the auxiliary were designed and transmitted by the leader of the rebellion (later known as [[the Ronin]]) from cell to cell. Kouru, who struggled with the construction of her [[lightsaber parasol]] auxiliary, saw the promise of crafting it upon seeing her master's reforged lightsaber fan auxiliary. Her master told her he did not enjoy crafting his auxiliary.<ref name="Ronin" />
