'''Cliff worms''' were a type of [[worm]] that could be found on the [[planet]] [[Skako Minor]].<ref name="Vader 28" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Darth Vader (2020) 28|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 28]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> [[pirate]]s [[Jul Tambor's forces|affiliated]] with [[Jul Tambor]], the grandson of former [[Techno Union]] [[Foreman]] [[Wat Tambor]], crashed their [[Starship|ship]] into a hive of cliff worms after they boarded a [[transport]] from [[Corellia]] and exposed the passengers to the [[poison]]ous [[atmosphere]] of the planet. After the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Mission to Skako Minor (Darth Vader and Sabé)|arrived]] on world to stop Tambor from overthrowing the [[Skakoan Council|Imperial regime]], he [[Death|killed]] the worms that were attacking the pirates.<ref name="Vader 28" />
