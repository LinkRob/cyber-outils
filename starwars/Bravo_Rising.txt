'''[[Bravo Rising]]''' was a united resistance movement on the [[planet]] [[Atterra Bravo]], dedicated to fighting the [[First Order]]. During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the First Order imposed a [[blockade]] on the [[Atterra system]], plundering [[fuel]] and [[mineral]]s and cutting off all travel and communications between the twin worlds Atterra Bravo and [[Atterra Alpha]]. Bravo Rising had a small [[fleet]] of [[starfighter]]s and [[transport]] [[Starship|ships]] at their disposal and were making supply runs between the two planets. However, the rebels were short on fuel, food, weapons, drinking [[water]], and medical equipment, so they turned to the [[Resistance]] for help.<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" />
