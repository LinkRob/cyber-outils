'''Dynia Reekeene''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] fighter in the [[Resistance]]. She was the daughter of two [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[commander]]s who had led<ref name="TROS VD" /> the [[Fakir Roughnecks]]<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> cell in the [[Fakir sector]], and joined the Resistance in honor of her [[parent]]s.<ref name="TROS VD" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dynia Reekeene first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], and was first identified in the accompanying [[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary|visual dictionary]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> She shares her last name with [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] characters [[Lens Reekeene|Lens]] and [[Mikka Reekeene]], who were [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] commanders who led a campaign in the [[Fakir sector/Legends|Fakir sector]].<ref name="SWCP">''[[Star Wars Campaign Pack]]''</ref>
