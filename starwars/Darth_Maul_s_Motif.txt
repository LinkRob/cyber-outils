'''Darth Maul's Motif''' is a minor theatrical theme used in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] to represent the character [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]]. It was written by [[John Williams]] and performed by the [[London Symphony Orchestra]] in [[1999]].
