'''Alderaanian puff cake''' was a type of [[puff cake]] that [[Homeworld|originated]] from<ref name="A Valiant Vow" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], Alderaanian puff cake was sold at [[Menaya's Sweet Shop]] in [[CoCo Town]] on<ref name="A Valiant Vow" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="A Valiant Vow" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: A Valiant Vow]]''|nearly two years after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> when the [[human]] [[Gender|girl]] [[Zenny Greylark]] traveled to Menaya's Sweet Shop to meet with the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Tep Tep]], she mentally noted the [[cake]] was one thing available at the shop.<ref name="A Valiant Vow" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alderaanian puff cake was mentioned in the [[2025]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: A Valiant Vow]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="A Valiant Vow" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref>
