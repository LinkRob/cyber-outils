The '''bombing of Mennar-Daye'''<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> was carried out by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] on [[Mennar-Daye]] prior to the [[Battle of Endor]]. Whilst [[Pilot|flying]] over [[Pandem Nai]] during the [[Battle of Pandem Nai|attack on Pandem Nai]], the mind of [[Lieutenant]] [[Yrica Quell]] flashed to the [[Orbital bombardment|bombardment]] of Mennar-Daye, which she had dutifully joined.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
