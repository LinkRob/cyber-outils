The '''2025 ''Gift Box''''' is a gift set for the ''[[Star Wars: Unlimited]]'' card game that collects two booster packs from several of the game's sets<ref name="Asmodee" /> and was released on [[November 7]], [[2025]].<ref name="Target" /> The gift box collected two booster pages from ''[[Twilight of the Republic]]'', ''[[Jump to Lightspeed (Star Wars: Unlimited)|Jump to Lightspeed]]'', ''[[Legends of the Force]]'', and ''[[Secrets of Power]]'',<ref name="Asmodee" /> which released the same day.<ref name="SSU">{{UnlimitedWeb|url=products/set-6-secrets-of-power|text=Set 6 Secrets of Power}}</ref> The gift box also featured two exclusive cards of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. These cards were illustrated by artist [[Vincent Dutrait]] and depict both characters as they appeared during the [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="Asmodee" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Launch into a galaxy of endless possibilities with the Star Wars™: Unlimited gift box! Each box contains a total of eight booster packs: 2 packs each from Twilight of the Republic, Jump to Lightspeed, Legends of the Force, and Secrets of Power. Also included are two exclusive variant cards and an oversized leader, neither of which are available anywhere else! This gift box is the perfect opportunity to start your Star Wars: Unlimited journey or grow your collection.''<ref name="Asmodee" />
