'''FN-2198'''<ref name="Poe Dameron: Flight Log" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> who served in the [[First Order]]'s [[FN Trooper Corps|FN Corps]]<ref name="BTA" /> as a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]]. After [[Resistance]] [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] was [[Attack on Tuanul|captured]] by First Order forces in the [[Sacred Villages|village]] of [[Tuanul]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]<ref name="Poe Dameron: Flight Log" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> FN-2198 and fellow stormtroopers [[FN-3156]], [[HX-1072]], and [[JY-834]] performed a scan of Dameron's [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]. The [[trooper]]s reported to their superior, [[Captain]] [[Phasma]], that they found nothing of significance. A copy of Phasma's report on the stormtroopers' scan results eventually came into the possession of Dameron, who added it to his [[Poe Dameron's flight log|flight log]] which he compiled after the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]]<ref name="Poe Dameron: Flight Log" /> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" />

==Behind the scenes==
FN-2198 was mentioned in the replica journal ''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]'', which was written by [[Michael Kogge]] and released in [[2016]].<ref name="Poe Dameron: Flight Log" />
