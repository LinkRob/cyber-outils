The '''Chief Architect''' was a non-permanent position that had the duty of representing builders and [[Merchant|traders]] of [[Naboo]]. Once its holder's term was finished, it was expected of them to return to their previous careers.<ref name="Altaya" /> [[Hugo Eckener]] held this position<ref name="CVDNE">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>
