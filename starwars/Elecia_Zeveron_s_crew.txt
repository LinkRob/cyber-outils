The [[human]] [[Elecia Zeveron]] led a '''crew''' of [[Thief|thieves]]<ref name="QueensBloom" /> around [[396 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' establishes that [[Axel Greylark]] was eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old thirteen years prior to its events. As ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''Convergence'' to [[382 BBY]], he must have been [[Reproduction|born]] in [[413 BBY]]. Greylark is seventeen years old in "[[The Queen's Bloom]]," dating its events to around [[396 BBY]].</ref> One member of the crew, the [[Kilotowan]] [[Leyli Romero]], was attending [[Reena University]] as a student to spy for them.<ref name="QueensBloom" />
