'''''Dark Forces: Rebel Agent''''' is the second volume in the [[Star Wars: Dark Forces (novellas)|''Dark Forces'']] trilogy of novellas written by [[William C. Dietz]]. It was released [[March 11]], [[1998]] by [[Dark Horse Comics]] and [[Berkley Boulevard]], and both the hardcover and trade paperback contain twenty-five full-color illustrations by [[Ezra Tucker]].

==Publisher's summary==
===Hardcover===
====Back cover====
''Secret [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] Agent [[Kyle Katarn]] will do anything to avenge his [[Morgan Katarn|father's]] untimely death at the hands of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]]. When Kyle is threatened by [[8t88/Legends|8t88]] in a [[Nar Shaddaa/Legends|Nar Shaddaa]] bar, he tracks down the [[Droid/Legends|droid]] and secures a mysterious disk that could put him on the path to learning why his father was killed. Kyle soon finds that the disk contains information about a map to the [[Valley of the Jedi]], a place his father had stumbled upon and kept secret. Now it's a race between Kyle and the Empire to see who can get to the map&mdash;and the Valley&mdash;first.''

''Based on the popular [[Star Wars: Dark Forces|''Star Wars: DARK FORCES'']] and [[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II|''Star Wars: JEDI KNIGHT'']] interactive games, ''Rebel Agent'' is part two of a trilogy written by acclaimed author William C. Dietz (''The Final Battle'') and illustrated by the award-winning artist Ezra Tucker (the Adventures of Little Nettie Windship children's book series).''

====Inside flap====
''After Kyle's father, [[Morgan Katarn]], discovers the Valley of the Jedi, Morgan passes along a description of it to a [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] named [[Qu Rahn/Legends|Rahn]], who falls into the hands of [[Jerec (Inquisitor)|Jerec]] the [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]]. From Rahn's mind, Jerec extracts information that leads back to the Katarn farm. Meanwhile, the droid 8t88 propositions Kyle in connection with a mysterious disk&hellip;and a chase ensues. Kyle secures the disk and learns of his father's discovery&mdash;but arrives too late to save the map. Or ''does'' he? The answer lies with 8t88.''

''This brand-new adventure trilogy provides background to, and incorporates elements of, the [[LucasArts]] ''DARK FORCES'' and ''JEDI KNIGHT'' interactive games. ''Rebel Agent'' follows [[Dark Forces: Soldier for the Empire|''Soldier for the Empire'']] and prepares the way for [[Dark Forces: Jedi Knight|''Jedi Knight'']]''.

===Trade paperback===
''Following [[Dark Forces: Soldier for the Empire|''Dark Forces—Soldier for the Empire'']], part two of this graphic-story-album trilogy chronicles the adventures of Kyle Katarn as he learns of the mysterious Valley of the Jedi, discovered by his father years ago. Trapped in the Valley is an enormous reservoir of energy, waiting to be released. Who will reach the Valley&mdash;and its power&mdash;first, Kyle, or the Dark Jedi known as Jerec? Written by William C. Dietz and accompanied by 25 painted illustrations by artist Ezra Tucker.''

==Plot summary==
[[1 BBY/Legends|THE PAST]]: [[Morgan Katarn]] transports a colony of [[Farmer/Legends|farmers]] from [[Sulon/Legends|Sulon]] to the distant uncharted world of [[Ruusan/Legends|Ruusan]] to escape the scrutiny of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]]. While on a trek through the wilderness, he encounters several native [[Bouncer|bouncers]] – a [[Species/Legends|species]] of orblike beings – who describe to Morgan a prophecy of a [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] who will fight a battle on their world and free the [[Torture/Legends|tortured]] [[Force ghost/Legends|spirits]] found there. The bouncers lead Morgan to the fabled [[Valley of the Jedi]], lost site of an ancient [[Seventh Battle of Ruusan|battle between the forces of light and darkness]]. 

[[5 ABY/Legends|THE PRESENT]]: [[Standard year/Legends|Years]] later, a band of [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]] led by [[Jerec (Inquisitor)|Jerec]] subjugates the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Dorlo]] and captures [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi]] survivor [[Qu Rahn/Legends|Qu Rahn]]. Jerec, eager to find the Valley of the Jedi, interrogates Rahn. Probing Rahn's mind, Jerec discovers that the late Morgan Katarn knew the Valley's location and passed this information to his son, [[Kyle Katarn|Kyle]]. Jerec slays Rahn and tasks [[8t88/Legends|8t88]], an [[Information broker/Legends|information broker]] [[Model 88-series administration droid|droid]], to continue the search. 8t88 finds an encoded [[Hologram disc/Legends|holodisc]] in Morgan's old residence on Sulon, but he cannot decrypt it. The droid later encounters Kyle Katarn on [[Nar Shaddaa/Legends|Nar Shaddaa]] and attempts to have his thugs exhort information on the hidden Valley from him, but Kyle frees himself from the goons and steals the disc. In his frenzied escape from Nar Shaddaa, Kyle discovers that his latent [[Force power/Legends|Force abilities]] are growing. 

The holodisc reveals that Morgan has left a [[Map/Legends|map]] at his Sulon farmhouse, as well as [[Qu Rahn's lightsaber|Rahn's lightsaber]]. With the blessing of [[Mon Mothma/Legends|Mon Mothma]], [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], and [[Princess/Legends|Princess]] [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia]], Kyle and [[Jan Ors]] undertake the quest for the Valley. On Sulon, Kyle finds the lightsaber, but the map, hidden in the tilework of the farmhouse ceiling – is missing. Jerec's lackeys have already found it, and 8t88 is on Sulon to decipher it. The droid destroys the map after recording an analysis of it and transmits the data to Jerec. Jerec's Dark Jedi minions betray the droid by severing its head, keeping the droid's [[Memory module|memory module]] intact for their use. On the trail of the map, Kyle fights Yun, one of Jerec's Dark Jedi followers. He defeats [[Yun]], but spares his life. The Dark Jedi brothers [[Gorc]] and [[Picaroon C. Boodle|Pic]] attack Kyle, and he slays them. Retrieving 8t88's head, Kyle uncovers the data inside and locates the map to the Valley.

==Continuity==
The storyline of the second and third [[Dark Forces/Jedi Knight|''Dark Forces'']] novellas expand upon the narrative of the [[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II|''Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II'']] from [[LucasArts]]. The story features a coterie of [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]] villains – individual "bosses" who must be defeated to complete the game.<ref name="TERC" />
