'''Force-sculpture''' was a form of art which consisted in producing three-dimensional figures, manipulating matter through [[the Force]]. Towards the end of the [[Republic Era]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Veleckra]] taught Force-sculpture at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> The [[Duros]] Jedi Master [[Wishan]] was also a Force sculptor, using dark [[Color|red]] [[clay]] to fashion a variety of figures, such as a [[greater krayt dragon]], a tower, a replica Jedi Temple, and even a statuette representing the young [[Jedi]] [[Jabe Rann]], complete with a [[lightsaber]].<ref name="JTC-E1">{{JTC|1}}</ref>
