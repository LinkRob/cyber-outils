A '''Forward Air Controller''', sometimes referred to as '''FAC''', was an individual who directed air support to targets during a battle. The most notable individual who did this was [[Cait]], who was [[Death|killed]] directing [[X-wing starfighter]]s to their targets during the [[Battle of Allst Prime|fighting]] on [[Allst Prime]].<ref name="Inbrief" />

==Behind the scenes==
The position of Forward Air Controller first appeared in the [[2015]] short story "[[Inbrief]]," written by [[Janine K. Spendlove]] and illustrated by [[Jose Cabrera]].<ref name="Inbrief" />
