'''Beetoo''' was an [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]] who accompanied [[Jedi Master]] [[Jocasta Nu]] in the [[Standard day|days]] following the [[proclamation of the New Order]]. The duo [[Pilot|flew]] a [[Jocasta Nu's Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|gold Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] from [[Jocasta Nu's cache|Jocasta Nu's school]] to the [[Coruscant Underworld]] for Nu to infiltrate the former [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] to [[Hunt for Jocasta Nu|retrieve]] a list of [[Force-sensitive]] children across [[the galaxy]].

Beetoo was left to await Jocasta Nu's return, of which was promised by the [[Chief Librarian]] to be soon, alongside the [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]]. However, a group of [[Coruscant underworld police]] officers approached the area; as per a prior agreement with Nu, the [[droid]] notified the Jedi [[librarian]] of the intrusion before detonating the [[starship]] with the intention of protecting the [[Jedi]]'s quest. This was not to be, however, as the remnants of Beetoo and the Delta 7B were recovered by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] after a [[Jedi Order]] [[Jedi crest|emblem]] originally a part of the starfighter was found by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces. Once brought to the [[ISB Central Office]] on [[Coruscant]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was notified of the occurrence, and was invited to inspect the wreckage. After a short conflict with an [[Unidentified Imperial Security Bureau major|Imperial Security Bureau major]], of which briefly brought back the general forms of the [[Jedi starfighter]] and Beetoo through Vader's use of [[telekinesis]], the [[Sith Lord]] triggered a search for a lone Jedi on the [[planet]].<ref name="Dying Light I" />
