An '''exterminator backpack''', also known simply as an '''exterminator's pack''', was a type of [[backpack]] worn by some residents of the [[Coruscant Underworld]] to offer them a measure of protection from the animal pests found there. They featured probes that allowed them to detect currents affected by [[conduit worm]]s.<ref name="CVD" />
