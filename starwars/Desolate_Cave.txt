The '''Desolate Cave''' was a rock formation in the [[Lost Steppe]] region on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the materials required to apply a shadow order [[Color|paint job]] to a [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]] were placed in a sealed portion of the cave<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|canon}}
During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] used a modification to her [[blaster pistol]] to blow open the sealed portion of the cave and retrieve the materials.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Desolate Cave appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The location with the loot was only accessible after the player get the power upgrade to Vess's blaster. The cave contains one of the paint themes that players could used to customize their speeder bike.<ref name="Outlaws" />
