'''Belial''' was a [[Devaronian]] [[Gender|male]] who held the rank of [[Cloud (rank)|Cloud]] in the [[Pirate|marauder]] organization known as the [[Nihil]]. Serving under [[Lourna Dee's Tempest]], Belial worked at the monitoring station aboard his [[Lourna Dee|commander's]] eponymous [[Lourna Dee (cruiser)|cruiser]]. During the [[39th Emergence]], Belial detected that the debris leaving [[hyperspace]] did not contain the [[Legacy Run]]'s flight recorder. Dee considered Belial to be smart, capable, unemotional, and cool in a crisis. She also predicted that he would soon rise up the ranks of the Nihil to become a [[Storm (rank)|Storm]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
