'''Cularin's Lady Senator, Lavina Wren''' is an article written by [[August Hahn]] and [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] adventure ''[[Oblivion's Kiss]]''. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[December 6]] [[2001]] and was written as a broadcast by [[Eye on Cularin]] on the [[Cularin/Legends|Cularin]] senator [[Lavina Durada-Vashne Wren|Lavina Wren]].
