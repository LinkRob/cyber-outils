{{Quote|They're dekamites, kid! The boys used to call them "swamp spiders". Nasty little things. But I've beaten them before, and I can beat them again.|Rex|<ref name="ATW">{{RebelsAnimationCite|1|A Trooper's Worth}}</ref>}}
'''Dekamites''', also known as '''swamp spiders''', were large, [[crab]]-like, [[swamp]]-dwelling creatures that used discarded pieces of metal as shells. They attacked in swarms, and their [[Queen (biology)|queens]] were so enormous that they could occupy a whole [[720 light freighter]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[clone trooper]] [[Rex]] and his men had at least one encounter with those aggressive creatures. [[Standard year|Years]] later, during the [[Galactic Civil War]], Rex had to face them again during a mission to retrieve a drive unit from an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] junkyard.<ref name="ATW" />
