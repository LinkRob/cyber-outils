'''Blagg''' was an individual who served as a [[lieutenant]] in the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="Of MSE-6 and Men" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> the lieutenant was stationed in [[Detention Block AA-23]] on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], the [[Galactic Empire|Empire's]] ultimate weapon. Blagg was responsible for preparing the [[IT-O interrogation droid|IT-O Interrogator]] [[droid]] for the [[Torture|interrogation]] of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and grew anxious when a necessary part had not arrived by the time [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] came to personally check that everything was on schedule. When Blagg ordered the delivery droid, an [[MSE-6 series repair droid]] designated [[MSE-6-G735Y]], to leave quickly, the droid collided with Tarkin and accidentally activated a [[Holorecorder|holorecording]] of the [[stormtrooper]] [[TK-421]]. Tarkin was briefly distracted by the recording, then resumed telling Blagg not to make excuses or cast blame for the delays. Blagg asked if they should begin the interrogation, but Tarkin stated that it would be conducted by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Of MSE-6 and Men" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blagg appeared in the short story "[[Of MSE-6 and Men]]," written by [[Glen Weldon]] for the [[2017]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''. After the release of 2017 [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], Weldon addressed a theory that the "broom boy" [[Temiri Blagg]] had a connection to Lieutenant Blagg. According to Weldon, he made up a Star Wars-y sounding name and had not contacted or been contacted by [[Lucasfilm]] about it.<ref name="GW Tweet Blagg">{{Twitter|ghweldon|status/942957520326418433|[[Glen Weldon]]|quote=I don't comment on my A CERTAIN POINT OF VIEW story because the story IS my comment. I will say only this: "Blagg" was a name I just made up, because I thought it seemed kinda STAR WARS-y. I heard from no one at Lucasfilm about it at any point.|archivedate=20210424092739}}</ref>
