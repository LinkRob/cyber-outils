'''Forb''' was a [[human]] who resided in [[Lonisa City]] on the [[planet]] [[Valo]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. After [[the Warden]], a [[Nihil]] [[commander]], attempted to pull in troops from Valo to aid in his [[Invasion of Eriadu|invasion]] on [[Eriadu]], Forb and several other members of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s [[Ram Jomaram]] and [[Gavi Takitaken]]'s [[Scarlet Skulls]] resistance movement led a [[Revolt on Valo|revolt]] against the marauders, with Forb helping [[Breebik]] cause an explosion which engulfed numerous Nihil.<ref name="THRA 11" />
