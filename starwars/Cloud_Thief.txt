The '''Cloud Thief''' was a [[clothing|costume]] worn by the [[Kubaz]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Glikkin]]<ref name="Cloud Thief" /> during the competitive sporting show [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]].<ref name="Hunters" /> This outfit resembled the one worn by the leader of the [[Cloud-Riders]], [[Enfys Nest]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cloud Thief was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Glikkin, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 5.0.0 alongside Season 5 on [[January 27]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cloud Blades]]. This costume could be unlocked as one of two grand prizes within the "Cloud Rogue Vault."<ref name="Hunters" />
