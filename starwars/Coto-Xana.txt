'''Coto-Xana''' was a terrestrial celestial body<ref name="Convergence" /> located in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and [[the Interior]],<ref name="TheInterior" /> It was situated in grid square L-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], within the [[Coto-Xana system]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Coto-Xana was connected to<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> the [[Uviuy Exen system|Uviuy Exen]] and [[Brentaal system]]s via the [[Hydian Way]] [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> a [[Skirmish in the Eiram system|skirmish]] in the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]] resulted in the creation of a belt of debris in [[orbit]] around [[Eirie]], a [[moon]] situated between the [[Double planet|twin planets]] [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]], that resembled Coto-Xana's rings of [[Water|ice]].<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coto-Xana was first mentioned in ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'', a [[2022]] novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' publishing initiative's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" />
