"'''A Different Path'''" is a [[canon]] short story that appears in ''[[The Prequel Trilogy Stories]]''. It was written by [[Elizabeth Schaefer]].<ref name="TPTS" /> It adapts the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] from the arrival on [[Coruscant]] to [[Darth Sidious]]'s command to wipe out the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]].
