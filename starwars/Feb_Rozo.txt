'''Feb Rozo''' was a [[Rodian]] [[musician]] active during the [[High Republic Era]]. He played guitar as part of the band [[Feb Rozo and the Sap Tap Trio]], and often performed at [[Hap's Sap Tap]], a [[cafe]] in the town of [[Kublop Springs]] on the planet [[Tenoo]]. Feb was a friend of the cafe's owner, [[Hap]].<ref name="BftB" />
