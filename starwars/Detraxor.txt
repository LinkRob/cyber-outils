'''Detraxors''' were devices that the people of [[Parnassos]] used to collect the nutrients from a [[Death|dead]] body. The device had a bulb that connected to tubes and a needle like siphon. They needed to be fitted with a fresh leather skin every time they were used for extraction. The reclaimed "essence" would then be used to create an oily substance called [[oracle salve]], which was a useful substance that was given great reverence in [[Scyre]] society. Similar moisture detraxors also existed on [[Otomok system|Otomok]], but only for beasts.<ref name="Phasma" />
