{{Quote|You came all the way to the statues of prophecy. How pointless. But now I find that they are connected, not unlike us. Come. Give yourself over to the dark. Enjoy it.|Bichan, to Toul|<ref name="JDH" />}}
A [[human]] [[Sith Lord]] active during a [[Unidentified Jedi–Sith war|war]] between the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Sith]], '''Bichan''' wore a [[Color|red]] face-concealing mask and wielded a [[Bichan's lightsaber|lightsaber]] with an attached whip. At one point, he [[Duel between Bichan and Jedi|fought]] and [[Death|killed]] a group of eight [[Jedi]], including the [[Unidentified human Jedi (Toul)|teacher]] of the [[Padawan]] [[Toul]]. The Sith Lord became interested by the anger displayed by the Jedi apprentice and spared his life, with Toul being left with a lingering sense of fear by the confrontation. Later, Bichan was noticed at a [[Unidentified asteroid city|city]] on a [[Unidentified marketplace asteroid|marketplace asteroid]] by Toul, who was acquiring supplies there for a [[Mission to Dolgarak|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Dolgarak]].

The Sith followed Toul and the [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Ara]] there, shooting down their [[Ara's starship|starship]] with his own [[Bichan's starship|craft]]. Subsequently, Bichan engaged Toul in a [[lightsaber duel]] atop a [[Ara's speeder|speeder]] that Ara drove up one of the two [[statues of prophecy]] located on Dolgarak. Their confrontation continued atop a [[stone]] ring connecting the heads of the two statues, where Bichan eventually disarmed Toul and demanded that he either surrender to the [[dark side of the Force]] and join him or die. At that point, however, Ara, who realized that she played a part in a prophecy that had foretold the duel, distracted Bichan through the use of explosives, allowing Toul to [[Telekinesis|summon]] his own [[lightsaber]] and decapitate the Sith Lord.

==Biography==
===Deadly encounter===
{{Quote|I'll kill you. I swear, I'm going to kill you!''"<br />"''Your rage. It befits a Sith more than a Jedi. Come find me when you're ready to embrace the dark.|Toul and Bichan|<ref name="JDH" />}}
[[File:BichanVsToulRound1-JourneyToTheDarkHead.png|thumb|right|330px|Bichan facing the immobilized Toul during their first confrontation]]
Bichan was a [[Sith Lord]]<ref name="StoryGallery3">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-story-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Story Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> who was active during a [[Unidentified Jedi–Sith war|war]] waged between the [[Sith]] tradition of [[Force-sensitive|Force-users]] and the [[Jedi Order]], their ideological antithesis,<ref name="JDH" /> at some point by [[1032 BBY]].<ref name="Death" /> During that conflict, Bichan [[Duel between Bichan and Jedi|fought]] a group of at least nine [[Jedi]] on a platform enveloped in a red-[[color]]ed mist. The Sith Lord [[Death|killed]] seven of his opponents before his attention was drawn to [[Toul]], a Jedi [[Padawan]] facing him with his [[lightsaber]] drawn. Bichan rushed to strike the young Jedi down, but his attack was [[Lightsaber combat|blocked]] by Toul's [[Unidentified human Jedi (Toul)|Jedi teacher]], who [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to [[Force push|repulse]] their enemy. The Jedi proclaimed the Sith&mdash;whom they knew by name&mdash;to be "nothing but a disgrace" and launched a fierce attack on him, with the latter also leaping to engage in [[Lightsaber duel|combat]]. Bichan, however, then used the whip attached to [[Bichan's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] to fatally impale Toul's [[teacher]].<ref name="JDH" />

In response, the enraged young Jedi apprentice himself attempted to attack the Sith, only for Bichan's whip to wrap around Toul's [[Limb|wrists]] and pin him to the ground. In response to the Padawan swearing he would kill him, Bichan goaded Toul to embrace his anger and the [[dark side of the Force]], lifting the Jedi into the [[Atmosphere|air]] and inviting him to find him later. Proclaiming his belief in the inevitable triumph of the Sith over the Jedi, Bichan then used his [[Sith lightsaber|lightsaber]] to create a scar on Toul's face before dropping the Padawan to the ground and walking away from the site of the carnage.<ref name="JDH" />

===Pursuit by fear===
{{Quote|I sense a familiar presence.|Bichan, on Toul|<ref name="JDH" />}}
[[File:BichanAsteroidCity-JourneyToTheDarkHead.jpg|thumb|left|330px|Bichan (pictured) encountered Toul in an asteroid city.]]
Some [[time]] later, Toul was [[Meditation|meditating]] aboard<ref name="JDH" /> a [[Unidentified warship|warship]]<ref name="StoryGallery3" /> housing the [[Jedi High Council]]. During his meditation session, the young Jedi experienced a flashback to the duel with Bichan&mdash;an event that had left him with a lingering sense of fear. Toul was subsequently dispatched on a [[Mission to Dolgarak|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Dolgarak]], accompanying<ref name="JDH" /> the [[Technician|mechanic]]<ref name="Highlights">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-highlights-journey-to-the-dark-head|text=''Visions'' Revisited: 5 Highlights from "Journey to the Dark Head"}}</ref> [[Ara]] with the aim of "cutting off the dark head," that is, decapitating one of the two mystical [[statues of prophecy]] located there&mdash;specifically the one associated with the dark side&mdash;in order to try and affect the course of the ongoing war.<ref name="JDH" />

In order to acquire supplies for the mission, the pair traveled to<ref name="JDH" /> a [[Unidentified marketplace asteroid|marketplace asteroid]]<ref name="StoryGallery5">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-story-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Story Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> housing a [[Unidentified asteroid city|city]]<ref name="AD">{{Visions|Journey to the Dark Head}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref>&mdash;with Bichan also being present at the [[City|settlement]] at the time. While observing a passing group of individuals bearing red-smoking<ref name="JDH" /> [[Censer|incense burners]],<ref name="AD" /> Toul experienced an uncertain [[Force sense|sensation]]<ref name="StoryGallery5" /> and suddenly saw Bichan, who was standing atop a building on the far side of the city.<ref name="AD" /> While the Jedi was gazing transfixed at the Sith, the latter in turn became aware of Toul's presence<ref name="JDH" /> and turned<ref name="AD" /> to watch him in return.<ref name="StoryGallery5" />

===Arrival on Dolgarak===
{{Quote|What an interesting place for our reunion. Have you been waiting for this moment? Picturing it over and over in your mind?|Bichan, to Toul|<ref name="JDH" />}}
[[File:SpeedertopDuel-JourneyToTheDarkHead.jpg|thumb|right|330px|On Dolgarak, Toul and Bichan engaged in a lightsaber duel atop a speeder piloted by Ara.]]
Ara and Toul subsequently departed for Dolgarak, with Bichan following the pair aboard a personal [[Bichan's starship|starship]]. While the former's [[Ara's starship|craft]] approached the statues of prophecy, Toul was experiencing a sense of physical discomfort that at one point was punctured by him sensing the presence of Bichan behind them. The Sith Lord then opened fire with<ref name="JDH" /> the [[laser cannon]]s<ref name="AD" /> of his [[Starship|ship]], landing a hit on the other vessel's wing that sent it falling toward the planet's surface. Wondering as to Toul's purpose in traveling to Dolgarak, the Sith followed the other's ship, continuing to fire upon it until it ultimately crash-landed<ref name="JDH" /> in the courtyard<ref name="AD" /> of a local [[Dolgarak temple|temple]] situated at the base of the statues.<ref name="JDH" />

In his ship, Bichan then briefly hovered over the crashed vessel before<ref name="JDH" /> arming<ref name="AD" /> his [[cannon]]s for another salvo, but at that point Ara and Toul emerged from the wreckage aboard a [[Ara's speeder|speeder]] with which they rammed the Sith's ship. Bichan struggled to control his spinning craft, yet it, too, crashed into a ball of [[fire]]. The Sith Lord himself, however, [[Force jump|jumped]] onto the top of his quarry's [[speeder]], which had begun to traverse the spiraling pathway leading up along one of the statues. With Toul also climbing atop the speeding vehicle to face Bichan, the Sith once again taunted him by inquiring whether he had been visualizing their upcoming confrontation, at which point the two engaged in a lightsaber duel.<ref name="JDH" />

===Cutting off the dark head===
{{Quote|You're out of options. Choose. I told you, our fates are entwined. Only one outcome can save you now. Join me.|Bichan, to Toul|<ref name="JDH" />}}
The confrontation&mdash;during which Bichan further claimed that the Jedi Order had known that he was searching for Toul but had regardless sent the latter to Dolgarak without any Jedi backup&mdash;continued until Ara drove the speeder off the statue and above the [[cloud]] cover engulfing the statues' heads. Toul and Bichan both leaped to the large [[stone]] ring connecting the heads; there, the Sith discarded his mask and [[cloak]] in preparation for the final face-off with the Jedi.<ref name="JDH" />

[[File:ToulVsBichan-JourneyToTheDarkHead.jpg|thumb|left|330px|The final duel between Toul and Bichan]]
When the two Force-users once again engaged in combat, the intensity of the blue-, red-, and purple-colored energy discharges pulsating throughout the ring and the statue heads rapidly increased. The duel saw the combatants crash into the [[crystal]]line interior of the ring, where they fought each other before once again emerging atop the structure. At that moment, [[Star|sunlight]] pierced through the clouds, and the colored energy inside the [[Light side of the Force|light side]]&ndash; and dark side&ndash;representing heads began to mix, leading Bichan to comment on the supposed pointlessness of Toul's visit to Dolgarak. Launching another attack on Toul, the Sith Lord eventually gained the upper hand over the Jedi by disarming him, after which Bichan used his whip to hold his opponent aloft over the edge of the ring.<ref name="JDH" />

While the Sith demanded that Toul choose between joining him or perishing, Ara, who was observing the confrontation while preparing to blow up the neck of the "dark" head, realized that she had witnessed it before&mdash;in a prophecy foretold<ref name="JDH" /> [[Standard year|years]]<ref name="StoryGallery2">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-story-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Story Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> earlier, when she was participating in the recording of prophecies at the temple situated at the base of the statues. Further realizing that, aside from Toul and Bichan, she herself was playing a central role in the prophecy&mdash;and giving up on her mission&mdash;Ara then redirected the explosives to fly from the statue's head toward Bichan, who used the Force to deflect them upward into the sky. When Ara subsequently detonated the charges, the distracted Sith Lord struggled against the resulting aftershock, giving Toul the opportunity to [[Telekinesis|telekinetically]] summon his lightsaber and behead Bichan.<ref name="JDH" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|The Jedi will fall, and the Sith will rise.|Bichan|<ref name="JDH" />}}
[[File:Toul_Visions.png|thumb|right|285px|Bichan was interested in persuading the Jedi Toul (pictured) to become his dark side student.]]
Bichan was a [[human]] with light skin, dark [[eye]]s, and long dark [[hair]]. He sported a gray [[tattoo]] that marked his chin and cheeks and covered his neck. Bichan, who believed in the inevitability of the triumph of the Sith over their opponents, the Jedi, became intrigued by the rage displayed by the Jedi Padawan Toul during their first encounter. The Sith Lord was interested in the dark side potential in Toul, as evidenced by the latter's anger and fear, and wished to become Toul's Sith teacher, considering his destiny as a Jedi to be "meaningless" and claiming that together they could conquer [[the galaxy]]. In order to goad Toul into surrendering to his anger and joining him, Bichan taunted the Jedi in both of their confrontations.<ref name="JDH" />

Bichan eventually came to view himself and Toul as "connected," believing that their destinies were linked. When Toul traveled to the planet Dolgarak, the Sith Lord was curious as to the purpose of the journey. Bichan ultimately viewed the Jedi's voyage to the statues of prophecy&mdash;as well as the latter's efforts at affecting the ongoing Jedi&ndash;Sith war&mdash;as "pointless." The Sith also referred to the ring connecting the statues' heads as the "stage" that Toul had supposedly chosen for their confrontation.<ref name="JDH" />

==Powers and abilities==
{{Quote|And after all this, you will need a new master.|Bichan, to Toul after the former has killed several Jedi, including the latter's teacher|<ref name="JDH" />}}
[[File:BichanDisarmsToul-JourneyToTheDarkHead.png|thumb|left|330px|Bichan was skilled at both lightsaber combat and the use of the Force.]]
Bichan was skilled in the use of the Force, both for non-combat applications&mdash;such as telekinesis or sensing another person's presence across a large distance&mdash;and to augment his performance in battle, such as by rapidly closing the range to an enemy or by leaping across large distances. He was proficient with a lightsaber, using it while single-handedly killing at least seven Jedi&mdash;though not without letting out grunts and panting due to exertion in the process. The Sith Lord utilized a style of lightsaber combat that incorporated both a two-handed&ndash; and a single-handed grip of the [[weapon]] as well as acrobatic leaps. In combat, Bichan also used to lethal effect a whip attached to his lightsaber.<ref name="JDH" />

In his last duel against the Jedi Toul, the Sith displayed superior timing when telekinetically manipulating a stone slab in order to disarm his opponent&mdash;however, a distraction also cost Bichan his own life, allowing Toul to deal him a mortal blow. Bichan was also adept enough at [[pilot]]ing an armed starship to continuously attack another vessel while maneuvering during an atmospheric encounter&mdash;and subsequently escape the fiery crash-landing of his own craft alive.<ref name="JDH" />

==Equipment==
Bichan wore a dark gray [[Clothing|tunic]] with black and red accents over layered white and red undertunics. His attire also included a black and red [[Belt|sash]], dark gray pants, black [[boot]]s, and black and red [[vambrace]]s. The Sith Lord also wore a black and dark gray cloak and a dark metallic [[helmet]] that included a mask. The mask was red in color, completely obscured Bichan's face, and featured a freely hanging band of textile that extended down to his knees. Bichan wielded a Sith lightsaber with a red blade that extended from a dark [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] with brown and tan accents. The hilt was fitted with an extended guard, and at its pommel was attached a long, segmented, variable-rigidity whip with a sharp tip. Bichan also piloted a dark-colored, armed single-person starship.<ref name="JDH" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Portrayal===
{{Quote|Have you been waiting for me? Was I always in the back of your mind?|Alternate dialogue by Bichan, as presented in ''Star Wars: Visions Filmmaker Focus''|<ref name="Focus">{{VisionsFocus|Journey to the Dark Head}}</ref>}}
[[File:JourneyToTheDarkHead_Visions_Vol_2_Poster.png|thumb|right|300px|Bichan (seen in the background) serves as the antagonist in "Journey to the Dark Head."]]
Bichan was created as the antagonist of "[[Journey to the Dark Head]],"<ref name="JDH" /> the fifth short film of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' non-[[canon]] animated anthology television series, which was produced by<ref name="SWCE 2023">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-visions|text=SWCE 2023: ''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Trailer, Key Art, and Cast Revealed}}</ref> the South Korean<ref name="Studios">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Release Date, Studios Revealed}}</ref> [[Studio Mir]] and premiered on [[Disney+]] on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="SWCE 2023" /> Prior to the film's release, Bichan was first depicted in the [[April 10]], 2023 official trailer for ''Visions''{{'}} second volume,<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|SSXqU92cymY|Star Wars: Visions Volume 2 &#124; Official Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/star-wars-visions-volume-2-official-trailer}}</ref> and the character's name was introduced at a panel at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]] held on the same day.<ref name="Celebration2023">{{SWYouTube|vf7D4MWJofM|Star Wars Celebration LIVE! 2023 - DAY 4}}</ref> Bichan's name in [[Wikipedia:Korean language|Korean]] is written as 비찬,<ref name="JDHsubtitles">{{Visions|Journey to the Dark Head}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> with the [[Wikipedia:Hangul|Korean alphabet]] character [[Wiktionary:비|비]] meaning "rain."<ref name="CollinsRain">{{WebCite|url=https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/korean-english/%EB%B9%84|text=English Translation of &ldquo;&#48708;&rdquo;|work=[https://www.collinsdictionary.com Collins Online Dictionary]|archivedate=20240107191648}}</ref>

The character was voiced by [[Yun Yong Sik]] in the Korean and [[Daniel Dae Kim]] in the English voice cast<ref name="SWCE 2023" />&mdash;with the latter noting that they always enjoyed portraying villains. The installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions Filmmaker Focus]]'' series of featurettes accompanying "Journey to the Dark Head" presents an English translation of several lines of the film's Korean dialogue spoken by Bichan that differs from the lines in the English dub of the film.<ref name="Focus" /> According to<ref name="StudioMirRadioTalk2">{{YouTube|video=jfDISQiPye4|text=(ENG) Studio Mir Radio Talk (ENG) Studio Mir Radio Talk With Star Wars Visions Volume 2 Crew Part. 2|channel=@StudioMIR2010|name=Studio Mir|date=2023-05-26}}</ref> the film's producer [[Choi Go Un]],<ref name="StudioMirRadioTalk1">{{YouTube|video=N8ZGEgeqZbU|text=(ENG) Studio Mir Radio Talk With Star Wars Visions Volume 2 Crew Part. 1|channel=@StudioMIR2010|name=Studio Mir|date=2023-05-19}}</ref> when the crew of "Journey to the Dark Head" listened to the first [[Wikipedia:Scratch vocal|scratch vocal]] lines of Bichan's dialogue that were recorded off of the film's [[Wikipedia:Storyboard|animatic]], to them the character sounded more "[[Wikipedia:Emo subculture|emo]]" than "cool." The film's director [[Hyeong Geun Park]] also performed some of the scratch vocal lines for Bichan.<ref name="StudioMirRadioTalk2" />

===Design===
{{Quote|We designed Bichan to make him look inhumane and terrifying. Consequently, we made his silhouette look vague and ambiguous, so the distinction between his face and body would be vague. For the helmet, we used the curves typically seen on a Hahoe mask. We used vertical lines for his long cloak and the cape attached to his helmet to make him look intimidating. As for Bichan's helmet, we used the patterns we see on Korean traditional artifacts like Haetaes, and Hahoe masks.|Kang You Tae|<ref name="Focus" />}}
[[File:BichanConceptArt-SWEpisodeGuide.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Concept art of Bichan]]
Concept art of Bichan was made by the artists [[Kim Hyun Min]] and [[Choi Ji Hyeon]], with a piece by Choi depicting a scene not present in the released film&mdash;an unmasked Bichan leaning in close to Toul with the former's whip wrapped around the latter's neck.<ref name="ConceptGallery">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-head-concept-art-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slides 2, 4, 6, 9}}</ref> An earlier concept art illustration by [[Kim Il Kwang]] from [[2022]] depicted Bichan pointing his weapon at a bound, kneeling captive while a [[soldier]] stood nearby. Instead of a helmet, the Sith Lord was wearing a black [[scarf]] that covered his lower face.<ref name="KimIlKwang">{{Twitter|username=@gimigam2|url=status/1658105539736248322|name=[[Kim Il Kwang]]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/ttBoz}}</ref> Another piece of concept art explored Bichan's appearance with either black facial hair or various extents of red facial markings.<ref name="StudioMirRadioTalk1" />

Per [[Lee Soo Kyung]], the production manager of "Journey to the Dark Head," Bichan was intended to hail from the same culture of the planet Dolgarak as the character Ara, as evidenced by their similar facial tattoos. Studio Mir's Art Director [[Kang You Tae]] wanted to add a "unique, [[alien]]-like feature" to Bichan and Ara to distinguish their cultural background, and the tattoos' look was inspired by that of Haechi<ref name="StudioMirRadioTalk1" />&mdash;the mascot of [[Wikipedia:South Korea|South Korea]]'s capital city [[Wikipedia:Seoul|Seoul]] that represents<ref name="Haechi">{{WebCite|url=https://english.seoul.go.kr/seoul-views/seoul-symbols/3-symbol/|text=Icon of Seoul, Seoul Haechi|work=[https://english.seoul.go.kr/ Seoul Metropolitan Government]|archivedate=20241207163505}}</ref> the mythological creature [[Wikipedia:Xiezhi|haetae]]<ref name="TriviaGallery4">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-head-trivia-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Trivia Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref>&mdash;though Bichan's markings were made different from Ara's more rounded shapes to fit his character better.<ref name="StudioMirRadioTalk1" /> According to Kang, Bichan's apparel was designed to render the character's appearance "inhumane and terrifying," and to achieve that, the character's silhouette was made to appear "vague and ambiguous," including a vague distinction between his face and body. The clothing also incorporated vertical lines so as to give an intimidating impression to the viewer.<ref name="Focus" />

[[File:Haetae.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Bichan's design was inspired by the Korean mythological creature haetae.]]
Bichan's helmet adapted the curved shapes characteristic of the traditional Korean artifacts known as the [[Wikipedia:Hahoetal|Hahoe masks]], and it features patterns resembling those on both Hahoe masks and<ref name="Focus" /> depictions of the haetae.<ref name="TriviaGallery4" /> While designing Bichan, giving him weapons such as a [[Wikipedia:Tonfa|tonfa]] or<ref name="Focus" /> a long-handled<ref name="KimIlKwang" /> "[[Wikipedia:Trident|trident]] lightsaber" was initially considered. However, the lines and characteristics of the character reminded the creative team of a snake, and consequently the design of the weapon morphed into the "snake-like"<ref name="Focus" /> whip attachment of the lightsaber seen in the film<ref name="JDH" />&mdash;a weapon that, according to Kang, reflected Bichan's character.<ref name="Focus" /> Another early design for the lightsaber depicted it with two blade emitters attached to cords that ran along the length of Bichan's arms, potentially hidden by the long sleeves of his attire.<ref name="StudioMirRadioTalk1" /> [[Malcolm Wope]] was in charge of layout and key animation for scenes of the final duel between Bichan and Toul.<ref name="Wope">{{ArtStation|subdomain=malx|username=malx|url=projects/YBrKB6|name=[[Malcolm Wope]]|text=STAR WARS VISIONS - JOURNEY TO THE DARK HEAD ANIMATION (STUDIO MIR)}}</ref>

Initial screenplay drafts for "Journey to the Dark Head" explored Bichan's backstory, something that had to be cut due to the short film's runtime constraints.<ref name="StudioMirRadioTalk1" /> [[Chung Se Rang]], the writer of the film, expressed their belief that the Sith Lord's interest in Toul stemmed from the latter's increased sensitivity to the light and dark sides of the Force.<ref name="Focus" /> The [[StarWars.com]] episode guide for "Journey to the Dark Head," released on [[July 28]], 2023,<ref name="EpGuideDate">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2-episode-guides|text=Explore ''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 with New Episode Guides}}</ref> also noted that the prophetic image that "Journey to the Dark Head" establishes to have depicted, among others, Bichan, was also meant to show the silhouettes of several iconic characters<ref name="TriviaGallery2">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-head-trivia-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Trivia Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> from the [[Star Wars saga|''Star Wars'' saga]] of films<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref>&mdash;[[Dark Lord of the Sith]]<ref name="SWT">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Amidala]]<ref name="TriviaGallery2" /> of the planet [[Naboo]], and their<ref name="SWT" /> [[twin]] [[Skywalker family|children]],<ref name="TriviaGallery2" /> [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]].<ref name="SWT" />
