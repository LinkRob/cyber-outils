'''Calys''' was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] that [[death|died]] during the [[Battle of Scarif]]. Her death led her [[gender|brother]], the [[Twi'lek]] rebel named [[Asara Deyn]], also joining the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Biography==
{{Quote|She was my sister. And she died at the Battle of Scarif.''"<br/>"''I'm sorry. Never heard of it.''"<br/>"''The Empire made sure of that.|Asara Deyn to Kay Vess|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
===Life of a rebel===
Calys grew up on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]] with her [[gender|brother]] [[Asara Deyn]] and their [[Asara Deyn's parents|parents]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Calys eventually began organizing a [[Calys's rebel cell|rebel cell]] to counter the Empire on Akiva. As the cell grew, Asara began helping the cell by spying on Imperial patrols. After making contact with a representative of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> around [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Death date" /> Calys decided that she wanted to leave Akiva and join the Rebel Alliance. When she told Asara her plans he became scared and told their parents about Calys plans. However that did not stop her and she later slipped away during the night and joined the alliance anyway. [[Standard month|Month]]s later she [[death|died]] during the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Legacy===
Calys death left Asara with immense guilt and later inspired him to join the Rebel Alliance himself.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] compiled a report on Asara in which they classified him as a class two threat and detailed Calys death and its impact on Asara. The report went on to state that an evaluation of Asara's psychological profile suggested that the trauma of his sister would be a strong pressure point that can be exploited in the event of his capture.<ref name="Outlaws" />

In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> Asara shared his admiration for Calys as well as some of the details of her death to a [[thief]] named [[Kay Vess]] while she was helping him rescue a number of members of [[Typhoon Squadron]] from the [[Hutt Poaching Station]] on Akiva before they could be sold to the Empire.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During the same [[Standard year|year]], Vess found a copy of the ISB's report on Asara at the [[Hutt]]'s poaching station which included references to Calys.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Calys was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. Asara's ISB report initially stated that Calys died at the [[Battle of Trevini]] instead of the Battle of Scarif which is what Asara states during the "Legacy" main story quest. However the text of the report was updated in a later version of the game which corrected the mistake and confirm that Calys died during the Battle of Scarif.<ref name="Outlaws" />
