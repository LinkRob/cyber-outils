An '''ambassador''', or a '''representative''', was a title for someone who was sent by a government or organization to represent that faction's interests or to resolve some kind of conflict. Ambassadors usually held a diplomatic status allowing them special privileges. In the waning days of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Jedi Order]] was called upon to act as ambassadors representing the [[Galactic Senate]] or the [[Supreme Chancellor]]. During the [[Invasion of Naboo]], the [[Jedi]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] were sent as ambassadors to attempt to resolve the dispute aboard the [[Trade Federation]] [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|cargo freighter]] ''[[Saak'ak]]'', but were forced to [[Escape from the Saak'ak|flee]] when the Trade Federation ambushed them.<ref name="Episode I" />

During the [[Imperial Era|rule]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Telvar]] served as the Imperial ambassador to [[Mon Cala]] during heated trade negotiations until his [[death]].<ref name="Dark Lord 13">[[Darth Vader (2017) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 13]]</ref> A [[consular ship]] was expected to have an ambassador on board it. When the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' did not have such an ambassador on board, [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] challenged [[Captain]] [[Raymus Antilles]]'s claim that the [[Starship|vessel]] was a consular ship.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[Senator]]s of the Galactic Republic were also sometimes known as ambassadors.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>
