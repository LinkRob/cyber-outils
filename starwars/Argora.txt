An '''argora''' was a type of [[bird]] that lived on [[Xendek]]. The [[Xendekian]]s believed that the argora was the responsible for all the good fortune on the [[planet]].<ref name="Endangered 1" /> In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Endangered, Part 1]]" to [[2 BBY]].</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] stole the bird from the Xendekians to transport it to the [[Emperor's personal zoo]], and the [[Spectres]] were sent to [[Rescue of the argora|retrieve]] it.<ref name="Endangered 1" />
