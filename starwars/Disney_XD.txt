'''Disney XD''' (launched on [[February 13]], [[2009]]) is a cable and satellite television channel owned by [[The Walt Disney Company]] through their Disney-ABC Television Group subsidiary. It was created to replace the Toon Disney channel. Disney XD currently airs action-oriented children's programming, with its primary demographic being boys ages 6 through 14.

Disney XD, along with [[Disney Channel]], aired ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'', a non-[[canon]] ''[[Star Wars]]''-themed crossover special.<ref name="phineas">{{WebCite|url=http://www.ign.com/articles/2014/07/18/phineas-and-ferb-star-wars-special-exclusive-image-reveal|text=IGN - Phineas & Ferb: Star Wars Special Exclusive Image Reveal|dateaccessed=2018-06-25|archivedate=20160603065901}}</ref>

On [[October 13]], [[2014]], Disney XD began airing episodes of the animated series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="Rebels">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-rebels-spark-of-rebellion-premieres-friday-october-3-on-disney-channel|text=''Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion'' Premieres Friday, October 3 on Disney Channel}}</ref>
