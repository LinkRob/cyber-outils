'''42X-58939''' was a [[BX-series droid commando]] that served the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Sometime prior to [[9 BBY]], the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Lucrehulk]] ''Primacy'' crashed on the planet [[Koboh]], where they, along with the rest of the droid army, were reactivated to serve the [[Bedlam Raiders]].

==Biography==
===The Clone Wars and the Koboh Abyss===
42X-58939 was constructed by [[Baktoid Combat Automata]] during the [[Clone Wars]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> At some point before [[9 BBY]], the [[droid]] was assigned to the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Lucrehulk]] ''[[Primacy]]''.<ref name="Survivor" />

The ''Primacy'' exited hyperspace near the [[Koboh Abyss]] which began to tear the ship apart, the Lucrehulk proceeded to crash into the [[planet]] of [[Koboh]], where the separatist droids, including 42X-58939 were shut down.<ref name="Survivor" />

The [[Gen'Dai]] [[Rayvis]] discovered and reprogrammed the dormant droid army within the ship to serve the [[Bedlam Raiders]].<ref name="Survivor" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Survivor}}
42X-58939 was [[Death|destroyed]] by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]]<ref name="Survivor" /> in 9 BBY.<ref name="Date" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
42X-58939 first appeared in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'', released on [[April 28]], [[2023]]. The droid's plating colors and location of the droid are randomized but retains the voice of a standard [[BX-series droid commando]].<ref name="Survivor" />
