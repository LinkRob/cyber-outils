'''Brugiss''' was a company that produced the [[C/ln Ion cannon]] utilized on the ''[[Slave I]]'', a [[Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft|''Firespray-31''-class patrol and attack craft]] owned by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]].<ref name="75060SlaveI" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brugiss was mentioned in the non-[[canon]] [[2015]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] [[LEGO Ultimate Collector's Series|Ultimate Collector's Series]] set [[w:c:brickipedia:75060 Slave I|75060 ''Slave I'']].<ref name="75060SlaveI" /> They were originally introduced into the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity in the [[2007]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Complete Cross-Sections]]''.<ref name="CrossSesctions">''[[Star Wars: Complete Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
