'''Adnama'''<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|url=status/1486538168409083905|name=[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2022-01-27|quote=the third Nalle triplet is behind Anakin in this shot; you can see her blonde braids. On the right hand side of the screen is @amandalucasmma as Adnama.|archivedate=20220130100712}}</ref> was a [[human]] with light skin and [[Color|pink]] [[hair]].<ref name="AOTC" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[22 BBY]].</ref> they attended the<ref name="AOTC" /> [[Outlander Club]] in the [[Uscru Entertainment District]]<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and stood with a drink in [[Limb|hand]] talking with another human when the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] entered the club. While at the club, Adnama wore a dark, high-collared [[Clothing|outfit]].<ref name="AOTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Adnama appeared in the [[2002]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']],<ref name="AOTC" /> in which the character was portrayed by [[Amanda Lucas]], daughter of director [[George Lucas]].<ref name="DB">{{DB|character|terrtaneel|Taneel, Terr}}</ref> The club scene was filmed on Stage 3 at [[Fox Studios, Sydney]] on [[July 17]], [[2000]].<ref name="365Days">''[[Creating the Worlds of Star Wars: 365 Days]]''</ref>

In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Adnama's name was confirmed by<ref name="Twitter" /> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member<ref name="SW Show Interview">{{StarWarsShow|GvT2F1j-SvA|Pablo Hidalgo Extended Interview|sw_url=video/pablo-hidalgo-extended-interview}}</ref> [[Pablo Hidalgo]] on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] on [[January 27]], [[2022]].<ref name="Twitter" /> The name, which is Amanda [[Tuckerization|spelt backward]], originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in a [[Databank (original)|Databank]] entry for the character [[Terr Taneel/Legends|Terr Taneel]]&mdash;another Amanda Lucas cameo&mdash;on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="DB" /> on [[May 11]], [[2005]].<ref name="Unrest">{{SWArchive|url=databank/updates/news20050511.html|text=Unrest in the Senate|date=2005-05-11}}</ref>
