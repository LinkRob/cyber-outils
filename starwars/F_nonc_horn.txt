'''F'nonc horns''' were a type of [[music]]al wind instrument consisting of a gas bag at the end of a cylindrical tube. The three [[Palandag]] [[musician]]s [[Dhuz]], [[Hhex]] and [[Jhat]] played f'nonc [[horn]]s through tentacular embouchures while at the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Casino]] in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
F'nonc horns first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> which was released on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-viii-to-open-december-15-2017|text=''Star Wars: Episode VIII'' to Open December 15, 2017}}</ref> The props were not named in the film,<ref name="TLJ" /> but received identification in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref>
