'''Attack Pattern Delta''' was an attack formation used by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] against the [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Battle of Hoth]]. [[Luke Skywalker]] called for [[T-47 airspeeder|snowspeeders]] of [[Rogue Squadron|Rogue Group]] to use this strategy during the battle.<ref name="TESB" />
