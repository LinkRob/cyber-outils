The '''Force Priestesses''' were five beings that resided on the [[Wellspring of Life]], the home of the [[midi-chlorian]]s.<ref name="Force Planet">{{Databank|force-planet|Force Planet}}</ref>

The priestesses were representative of the connection between the [[Living Force]] as experienced by living beings, and the [[Cosmic Force]] in its role in the greater scheme of the universe. Existing within the [[Wellspring of Life]], the five were a form of [[Changeling|shape-shifter]], and would mutate their forms to manifest as [[Serenity]], [[Joy]], [[Anger]], [[Confusion]], and [[Sadness]].<ref name="Databank-Force Priestesses">{{Databank|force-priestesses|Force Priestesses}}</ref>

[[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] encountered the Force Priestesses during the [[Clone Wars]]. They knew the secret to transforming into a [[Force spirit]].<ref name="Destiny SW.com" /><ref name="Sacrifice">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sacrifice-episode-guide|text="Sacrifice" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Though installments from [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|The Lost Missions]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' implied that [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] had learned from the Force Priestesses and directed Kenobi to them as well, [[canon]] material from the official [[Memory bank/Legends|Databank]] and the now-defunct [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|Encyclopedia]] on [[StarWars.com]] still refer to Jinn as having learned from a "[[Shaman of the Whills]]," the [[Ancient Order of the Whills]] having first been referenced in the [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|novelization]] of the [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|original ''Star Wars'' film]].<ref name="Qui-GonSWE">{{SWE|characters|quigonjinn|Qui-Gon Jinn}}</ref><ref name="QuiGonBioGallery">{{Databank|qui-gon-jinn-biography-gallery|Qui-Gon Jinn Biography Gallery}}</ref>

In an interview, [[Dave Filoni]] indicated the Force Priestesses may be one being who became five over a long period of time, saying, "The way that I reconciled that being is actually one being. It's one ancient being separated over a time that for our perception to be able to see her, she is these many different iconic things presented to us. But she died a long, long, long time ago, she is conscious in the Force, and she has a limited ability to manifest."<ref name="Q&A">{{SWYouTube|QdcWbbG8omw|Force Priestesses - The Lost Missions Q&A &#124; Star Wars: The Clone Wars|sw_url=video/the-lost-missions-q-and-a-force-priestesses}}</ref>
