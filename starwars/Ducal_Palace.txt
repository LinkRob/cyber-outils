The '''Ducal Palace''' was located on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Battlefront" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Heroes of Rogue One">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Heroes of Rogue One}}</ref> [[Bamayar]]. During the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Mid Rim Retreat|Mid Rim offensive]],<ref name="Battlefront" /> which began around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] [[Battle of Bamayar|occupied]] the palace. [[Hazram Namir]], a [[First Sergeant]] in the 61st, remembered the capture of the Ducal Palace while on the [[planet]] [[Haidoral Prime]]<ref name="Battlefront" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Ducal Palace was mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
