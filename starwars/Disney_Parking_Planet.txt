'''Disney Parking Planet''' was a terrestrial [[planet]] with a breathable [[atmosphere]], typically used by visitors to the [[Disney Playground Planet]] as parking space. Heavily built-out for [[Starship|spacecraft]] storage, the planet was [[orbit]]ed by at least five variously-[[color]]ed [[moon]]s, which were used for wayfinding. While mostly [[City|urbanized]], the planet featured [[mountain]]tops and [[Plant|greenery]], though to a limited extent.

The [[human]] [[Parent|mother]] [[Marge Simpson]] at one point brought several [[Gender|mothers]] and their [[Youngling|children]] to the planet on the [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s [[T-6 1974|T-6 shuttle]] in preparation for a visit to the Disney Playground Planet, an occasion during which the [[alien]] [[Mickey]] skipped in line ahead of them to park in the human [[Bob Iger]]'s spot.

==Description==
{{Quote|Remember which moon you're under!|A sign orbiting the planet|<ref name="May12th" />}}
Disney Parking Planet was a gray-[[color]]ed, somewhat [[Stone|rocky]] [[planet]]<ref name="May12th" /> with a breathable, Type I [[atmosphere]].<ref name="Atmosphere" /> While most of its landscape was [[City|urban]], it also featured natural [[mountain]]s. As a first-stop parking space for [[Disney Playground Planet]] visitors, the planet was extensively covered in [[Starship|spacecraft]] storage infrastructure featuring little [[Plant|greenery]] between its parking aisles.<ref name="May12th" />

[[File:MickeyParking.png|thumb|300px|Mickey parking their vehicle on the planet]]
It was [[orbit]]ed by at least five [[moon]]s of various colors, including red, yellow, green, purple, and a lighter shade of blue. These natural satellites were used by individuals on the planet to aid in remembering where they parked their vehicles. Also orbiting the planet was a large informational sign in the [[High Galactic]] [[Writing|script]] of [[Galactic Basic Standard]] visible from afar, to instruct visitors to use the moons as navigational aid when returning to their starships.<ref name="May12th" />

Visiting starships formed a single, straight line when entering the heavily utilized planet, in which they needed to wait for preceding ships to then look for their parking spot. While some honked their horns in the queue, other individuals were known to skip the line. Vehicles that could be housed in the planet's sprawling parking facilities included starships such as [[T-6 shuttle]]s and [[VCX-100 light freighter]]s. It also featured dedicated spaces for [[speeder]]s such as [[T-47 airspeeder]]s and [[X-34 landspeeder]]s.<ref name="May12th" />

==History==
During a [[Mother's Day]] outing, the [[human]] [[Marge Simpson]] led a number of other [[Gender|mothers]] and their [[Youngling|children]] on [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s [[T-6 1974|T-6 shuttle]] to Disney Playground Planet, although, as noted by an individual known as the [[Evil Queen]], they first needed to park Tano's ship on the Disney Parking Planet beforehand. They entered the line of starships waiting to land on the planet, while the [[alien]] [[Mickey]] skipped the line and took the human [[Bob Iger]]'s reserved parking spot, for which a sign indicated an exception existed allowing for Mickey's use.<ref name="May12th" />
