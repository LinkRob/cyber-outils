'''Conspiracy''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie-in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[June 2]], [[2005]] and took the form of an in-universe article written by [[Nia Reston]] and [[San Herrera]] on how the use of [[Clone trooper/Legends|Clone troopers]] by the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] was potentially dangerous.
