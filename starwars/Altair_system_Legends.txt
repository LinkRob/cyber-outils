The '''Altair system''' was a [[Star system/Legends|star system]] located within the [[Thrasybule sector/Legends|Thrasybule sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]] region of [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]]. It consisted of at least two [[Planet/Legends|planets]], [[Altair 3]] and [[Altair 9]], and the [[Star/Legends|star]] [[Altair (star)|Altair]].
