'''Ascendant Record Keeper''' was a position in the [[Ascendant]]<ref name="Aphra 29" /> [[Religion|cult]]<ref name="Inside Intel">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-inside-intel-the-ascendant|text=''Star Wars'' Inside Intel: The Ascendant|int=Star Wars Inside Intel}}</ref> that were active [[Standard century|centuries]]<ref name="Aphra 22" /> before [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> specifically within the [[Bar'leth Group]] that operated<ref name="Aphra 29" /> out of the cult's main temple, the [[Tomb of the Spark Eternal]], on<ref name="Inside Intel" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Bar'leth]]. The [[Trandoshan]]<ref name="Aphra 29" /> [[Ghess]]<ref name="Aphra 22">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 22|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 22]]</ref> held the position and was tasked with the cult's documentation, which they wanted to sound mysterious and exciting to ensure that it would be read. Ghess was also allowed by [[Miril]]&mdash;a [[Chadra-Fan]] co-founder of the Ascendant&mdash;to come up with names for everything within the group. Eventually, the [[Darkseekers]]&mdash;a group of [[Sith]] that [[Hunting|hunted]] threats to themselves<ref name="Aphra 29" />&mdash;[[Slaughter of the Ascendant|wiped out]] the Ascendant at the temple on Bar'leth, including Ghess<ref name="Aphra 22" /> and the position they held.<ref name="Date" />

==Behind the scenes==
The position of Ascendant Record Keeper first appeared, albeit unidentified, in flashbacks in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 22|twenty-second issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Natacha Bustos]] and [[Minkyu Jung]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 22" /> on [[July 27]], [[2022]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/98323/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_22|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #22}}</ref> It was later identified in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 29|twenty-ninth issue]], also written by Wong, illustrated by Bustos, and published by Marvel<ref name="Aphra 29" /> on [[February 22]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel 2">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103074/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_29|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #29}}</ref>
