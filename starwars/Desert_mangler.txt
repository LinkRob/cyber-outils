'''Desert manglers''' were a [[Species|creature]] that could be found in the [[desert]]s of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Sergia]]. The [[stormtrooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]] used the creatures as mounts for their patrols. While on a [[Mission to Sergia|mission]] to Sergia, [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Warba Calip]] were pursued in a [[landspeeder]] by a desert mangler-mounted stormtrooper patrol.<ref name="Star Wars 74" />
