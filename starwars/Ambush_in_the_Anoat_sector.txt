==Prelude==
{{Quote|The Emperor has made a critical error, and the time for our attack has come.|[[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] of the Rebel Alliance|<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>}}
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], the [[Emperor|ruler]] of the  of the [[Galactic Empire]], lured the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] into a trap over the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]] to destroy the rebellion once and for all using the firepower of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]] as well as the [[Imperial Navy]]'s [[Moddell sector fleet]] and [[Death Squadron]]. However, despite the odds being stacked against them, the Rebel Alliance emerged victorious from the decisive [[battle of Endor]], with the DS-2 Mobile Battle Station being destroyed, and with it, Emperor Palpatine and his [[Emperor's Fist|right hand man]] [[Darth Vader]],<ref name="Episode VI" /> the [[Supreme commander of the Imperial fleet]]. In the aftermath of their defeat at Endor, the Empire was left without a clear line of command<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> as the Rebel Alliance began the process to reform themselves as the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Insurgency Rising 1" />

==Ambush==
[[Standard hour|hours]] after their [[Battle of Endor|victory]] over [[Endor]], a [[squadron]] including [[Rebel pilot]]s [[Rynn Zenat]], [[Preeti Delmin]],<ref name="Insurgency Rising 1" /> and [[Eiro]] were assigned to attack [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] in the [[Anoat sector]]. After the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] squadron engaged [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s,<ref name="Insurgency Rising 1" /> over [[Darlyn Boda]],<ref name="Insurgency Rising 2" /> the Imperial forces appeared to go on the run , making the [[pilot]]s believe the tide was turning in their favor. However, a swarm Imperial [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter|TIE strikers]] soon arrived and began opening fire, catching the rebel squadron off guard. As a result, the rebel squadron split, with Rynn piloting her [[B-wing heavy assault starfighter]] in one direction to draw the TIE strikers away, while the other rebels went in another direction.<ref name="Insurgency Rising 1" />

However, systems in Rynn's B-wing began failing from blaster damage, causing Rynn to request backup from the rest of the squadron and report to Preeti that her ship loosing power, she was trying to gain altitude, and that the TIE strikers were closing in. However, Preeti informed Rynn that the Imperial forces had formed a perimeter around the [[sector]] and were closing in on the rest of the squadron. Left with no other options as TIE strikers closed in, Rynn turned her B-wing towards the TIE strikers, damaging one of the fighters and knocking the wing off another, before ejecting the ship's [[cockpit]] from the rest of the ship and escaping to [[Endor]].<ref name="Insurgency Rising 1" /> Meanwhile, the rest of the squadron was shot down over [[Darlyn Boda]].<ref name="Insurgency Rising 2" />
