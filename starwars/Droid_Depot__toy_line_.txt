The '''''Droid Depot''''' toy line is a series of toys sold at the [[Droid Depot]] shops found in the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] area at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] starting in [[2019]]. The line includes various models of customizable [[droid]] toys made in the shop, as well as other toys and merchandise. A number of products in the line were previewed at [[Celebration Chicago|Star Wars Celebration Chicago]] in [[April]] 2019.
