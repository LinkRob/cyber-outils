Between around [[170 BBY]] and around [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[High Republic Era]], '''Avon's funeral''' took place on the [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] [[Ingae]]. The [[funeral]] was held for the [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale]], who had [[Death|passed away]], and was attended by the [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] and the [[Shani]] [[Deva Lompop]] who were both close friends of Sunvale. After the funeral, Lompop told Rwoh to forget about her and the two parted ways.<ref name="Wayseeker" />

Around 160 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the Mirialan sought out the Shani as she was in need of her help [[Investigation into the production of 47 lightsaber nullifiers|finding]] the scientist [[Florinda Jackard]]. After Rwoh arrived at [[Deva's asteroid]], Lompop's [[alarm system]]s were set off and the Shani threatened to kill whoever had come to her home, unaware that it was the Mirialan. Once Rwoh told Lompop it was her, the Shani reminded the Mirialan what she had told her after the funeral, to which Rwoh stated she remembered, but informed Lompop she needed her help.<ref name="Wayseeker" />
