A '''battle''' was fought on<ref name="The High Republic 9" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> astronomical object [[Golrath]]<ref name="The High Republic 9" /> in [[231 BBY]], as [[Nihil conflict|part of]]<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]]'s<ref name="The High Republic 9" /> [[Operation: Counterstrike|Operation Counterstrike]]<ref name="Timelines" /> against the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s in response to their [[Valo Atrocity|attack]] on the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] on<ref name="The High Republic 9" /> the Outer Rim planet<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> [[Valo]]. The event resulted in a major victory for the [[Jedi]] and Republic's [[Unified Republic Task Force]].<ref name="The High Republic 9" /> That same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic (2021) 9|''The High Republic'' (2021) 9]] to [[231 BBY]].</ref> the [[task force]]'s victory at Golrath&mdash;as well as [[Battle on Aris|victories]] [[Battle on Port Haven|on]] the celestial bodies [[Aris]] and [[Port Haven]]&mdash;was reported by [[Jedi High Council]] member [[Stellan Gios]] to the Jedi stationed aboard the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]].<ref name="The High Republic 9" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle on Golrath was first mentioned, in flashbacks, in the [[The High Republic (2021) 9|ninth issue]] of the [[2021]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']], released as part of<ref name="The High Republic 9" /> the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 1]], 2021.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/94642/star_wars_the_high_republic_2021_9|text=Star Wars: The High Republic (2021) #9}}</ref>
