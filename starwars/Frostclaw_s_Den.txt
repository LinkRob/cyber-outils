'''Frostclaw's Den''' was a cave system used by the [[Skel]] [[Frostclaw]] that was located underneath the [[Big Sink]] on [[Hoth/Legends|Hoth's]] [[Clabburn Tundra]]. The entrance to the den was a volcanic sinkhole spanned by a bridge, and a tunnel connected the bridge to the [[Buried Field]].
