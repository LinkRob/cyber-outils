An '''outpost''' of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] was established on [[Aeos Prime]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] with the [[Galactic Empire]]. Decades after, during the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the [[First Order]] [[Attack on Aeos Prime|destroyed]] the abandoned outpost to prevent the [[Resistance]] from using it.<ref name="New World" />
