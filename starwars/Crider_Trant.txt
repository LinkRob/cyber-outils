'''Crider Trant''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] who stayed on [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="AED" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crider Trant appears in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']]. According to these files, Trant would have been on [[W:c:swg:Naboo|Naboo]]. However, Trant is not known to have appeared on the [[Star Wars video games|video game]]'s live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]]. Nevertheless, an emulated depiction pulled from this character's Interchange File Format reveals what he would have looked like if he were added to the game.<ref name="AED" />
