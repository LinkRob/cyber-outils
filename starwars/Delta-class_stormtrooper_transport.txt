The '''''Delta''-class stormtrooper transport''' was a class of [[transport]] that was used for the transportation of [[stormtrooper]]s. An [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] could have fifteen such transports within its complement.<ref name="BMF 20" />
