'''Foundry''' was a location<ref name="Tales from Vandor" /> in the [[Foundry system]] of the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was under control of the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the [[operation at the Berullian Checkpoint]], Imperial [[Grand General]] [[Ormeddon]] was dispatched to deal with the situation. With Ormeddon in less restrictive security, [[Beckett's gang]] stole Ormeddon's [[shuttle]] and returned to Foundry. They landed in Ormeddon's personal docking bay and stole experimental [[deflector shield generator]] technology.<ref name="Tales from Vandor" />
