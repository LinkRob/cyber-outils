'''Bren Quersey''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Rebel pilot]] who was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He was a member of [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]], though one of numerous [[pilot]]s forced to stay behind at the [[Great Temple]] on [[Yavin 4]], due to the lack of available [[starfighter]]s, and observed the ongoing [[Battle of Yavin]] alongside the other stationed pilots.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Don't think they're planning to evacuate. Most of the essential equipment left with the fleet. It's all in the hands of the pilots upstairs.|Quersey, during the ongoing Battle of Yavin|<ref name="DR" />}}
Bren Quersey was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] within the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]'s [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps|Starfighter Corps]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="DR" /> Prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]] in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">{{FilmDate|IV|1|Bren Quersey's appearance in "[[Duty Roster]]"}}</ref> Quersey was a member of [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]] and at one point flew under the callsign [[Red Eight|Red 8]].<ref name="SWCT" /> In the moments immediately prior to the Battle of Yavin, Quersey entered a [[T-65 X-wing starfighter]], and was handed a flight helmet from a [[Rebel technician|ground technician]] on his port-side. He subsequently commenced the take off process.<ref name="ANH" /> Ulimately however, Quersey became one of the many [[pilot]]s who stayed behind at the [[Massassi Group]]'s [[Great Temple|base]] on<ref name="DR" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="DByav4">{{Databank|yavin-4|Yavin 4}}</ref> [[Yavin 4]] that included fellow squadron members; [[Elyhek Rue]], [[Wenton Chan]], and [[Col Takbright]], after Red Squadron's leader [[Garven Dreis]] selected the pilots who would participate in the engagement, due to the shortage of available [[starfighter]]s.<ref name="DR" />

[[File:Yavin_4_Temple.png|thumb|left|250px|Quersey was forced to stay behind at the Great Temple alongside other pilots due to a lack of starfighters.]]
After the squadron took off to destroy the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], Quersey and the other stationed pilots followed the battle from the base's war room. He commented that the rebels did not plan on evacuating the base, as most of the essential equipment was already on board the fighters that took off. As the battle progressed, the [[technician]] [[Kelemah]] stated that the fighter that [[Wedge Antilles]] was flying had a misaligned port [[stabilizer]], and Takbright pointed out that Antilles was taking a big risk. Quersey laughed and replied that everyone in the base was part of the "biggest risk in the history of [[the galaxy]]." When the Death Star was finally destroyed, the base was filled with joy, and Quersey was immediately hugged by Takbright.<ref name="DR" />

==Personality and traits==
A human male, Quersey had [[Color|brown]] [[hair]] and light skin.<ref name="SWCT" /> He observed the battle hoping that the rebel pilots would emerge victorious, despite more of them [[Death|dying]] as the battle progressed. Col Takbright thought of Quersey as a talented and volatile pilot.<ref name="DR" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BenQuerseyCard-SWCT.png|thumb|200px|Bren Quersey identified in ''Star Wars'': Card Trader.]]
The [[2015]] digital card trading app [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]] identified a previously unnamed pilot within the film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] as Bren Quersey. It simultaneously establishes Quersey serving as [[Red Eight]] during the Battle of [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]],<ref name="SWCT" /> which is depicted in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH" /> Within the film,<ref name="ANH" /> and the image featured on the front of the card&mdash;a still frame derived from the scene&mdash;Quersey enters a [[X-wing starfighter]] [[cockpit]].<ref name="SWCT" /> While this matches his background in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity,<ref name="Bren Quersey Legends">{{CCG|set=Special Edition Limited|cardname=Bren Quersey}}</ref> the [[2017]] [[canon|canonical]] short story "[[Duty Roster]]," published in the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]'', introduced a new version of his story, establishing that he was stationed in the Great Temple on Yavin 4 while the battle took place in [[Realspace|space]] and was not selected to join Red Squadron and enter a starfighter. The story also revealed that [[Zal Dinnes]] flew as Red Eight instead.<ref name="DR" /> This article follows the short story's version of his biography, however regards the card image in Card Trader as identifying Quersey as that particular pilot within the ''A New Hope''.
