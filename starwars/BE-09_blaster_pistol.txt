The '''BE-09 blaster pistol''' was a model of [[blaster pistol]] owned by the [[moisture farmer]] [[Beru Whitesun Lars]]. Her BE-09 had minor carbon scorching on its barrel as a result of minimal use.<ref name="DawnofRebellion" /> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="KenobiDate">{{KenobiDate|VI}}</ref> Lars used her BE-09 to [[Assault on the Lars Homestead|protect]] her nephew [[Luke Skywalker]] from the former [[Inquisitor]] [[Reva Sevander]].<ref name="Kenobi6">{{Kenobi|Part VI}}</ref>

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The BE-09 blaster pistol appeared in "[[Part VI]],"<ref name="Kenobi6" /> the sixth episode of the [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'', which was released on [[June 22]], [[2022]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-june-2022|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in June 2022}}</ref> It was first identified in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="DawnofRebellion" />
