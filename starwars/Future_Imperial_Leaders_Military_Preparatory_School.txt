The '''Future Imperial Leaders Military Preparatory School''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Military|Military]] school located on the [[planet]] [[Vardos]], in the [[Jinata system]], that both [[Iden Versio]] and [[Gideon Hask]] attended during their youth. Versio studied at the school from a young age, and despite being bloodied, graduated at the top of her class with honors.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
