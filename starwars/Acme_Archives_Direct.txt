'''Acme Archives Direct''', a subsidiary of '''Acme Archives''', is an artwork production company that has created several ''[[Star Wars]]''-themed "[[SpecPlate]]s" and "TechPlates" based on ''Star Wars'' vehicles. They also created another line of art prints known as [[Sketchplates]].
