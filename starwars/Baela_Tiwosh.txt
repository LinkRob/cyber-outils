'''Baela Tiwosh''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Zabrak]] who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Shortly before the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]], Tiwosh and two other Rebel operatives entered the [[sector]] but were unable to leave when the [[blockade]] began. Tiwosh was secretive about her true mission in the [[Anoat]] sector, and she was skillful at wielding [[blaster]]s.<ref name="Tiwosh" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
During the blockade, Tiwosh joined the crew of a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]], assisting them on various missions.<ref name="Tiwosh" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Baela Tiwosh was created for the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was released by [[Kabam]] in [[2015]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> In ''Uprising'', Tiwosh is a Tier II Crew Member available through the Premium Crane.<ref name="Tiwosh" />
