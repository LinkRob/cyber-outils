"'''All I Want For Life Day'''" is a [[Canon|non-canon]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short that aired online on [[November 5]], [[2020]].

==Official description==
''What Obi-Wan Kenobi really wants this holiday season is to catch General Grievous. Can Anakin Skywalker help grant his master's wish?''<ref name="YouTube" />

==Plot summary==
[[File:Alliwantforlifeday2.png|thumb|left|250px|Anakin Skywalker brings a gift to Obi-Wan Kenobi]]
[[Jedi Order|Jedi]] [[General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] are studying a [[hologram]] of General [[Grievous]] exiting his [[Belbullab-22 heavy starfighter]], the ''[[Soulless One]]'', where he is greeted by four [[B1-series battle droid]]s. Kenobi discusses these events with two [[clone trooper]]s of the [[501st Legion]] as Skywalker uses [[the Force]] to put up decorations for [[Life Day]]. One such decoration gets out of his [[Telekinesis|control]] and lands on Kenobi's head. Kenobi is displeased, but Skywalker gifts him with a new [[Color|red]] sweater, which Kenobi puts on over his [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]].

[[File:Alliwantforlifeday3.png|thumb|right|250px|Obi-Wan Kenobi is surprised by a second lightsaber blade]]
As Kenobi and the [[Soldier|troopers]] continue to discuss the hologram, Skywalker brings another gift to Kenobi, this one being a blue [[double-bladed lightsaber]]. Excited, Kenobi turns it on, but the bottom blade mistakenly slices the hologram table in half. Kenobi becomes frustrated with Skywalker, who walks away dejectedly. Skywalker then brings another gift&ndash;a [[Marksman-H training remote]], which flies around, firing at the two Jedi as well as the clones. It continues to wreak havoc until Skywalker jumps on top of it with the gift box.

[[File:Alliwantforlifeday4.png|thumb|left|250px|Obi-Wan Kenobi duels General Grievous]]
Kenobi is once again frustrated, but then Skywalker receives an idea whilst looking at the hologram of Grievous. While Kenobi paces around the room, Skywalker brings yet another gift, this one being the biggest one yet. He tears open the box to reveal that it is the [[cyborg]] General Grievous, who has a red bow on top of him. Grievous shakes off the bow and ignites his four [[lightsaber]]s. Skywalker tosses the double-bladed lightsaber to Kenobi, who begins to duel with Grievous. Skywalker uses the Force to push Grievous away, who then sees a pair of shiny [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s. He jumps in one and rides away, laughing all the while. Skywalker and Kenobi are left standing alone in a room that is now very much destroyed. Kenobi then gifts Skywalker with a present of his own&ndash;the very same sweater Skywalker had given him. The pair laugh together, enjoying the holiday.

==Continuity==
The [[clone trooper]]s seen in the short resemble the [[clone jetpack trooper]]s from the [[2020]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set titled 501st Clone Troopers. However, these troopers are not wearing jetpacks. The All Terrain Recon Transports also appear to be taken from the set.<ref name="501st battlepack">{{LEGOCite|tcw|75280|501st Legion Clone Troopers}}</ref> Additionally, Anakin Skywalker wears a sweater with the [[Starbird (symbol)|starbird]] symbol on it, even though he was never affiliated with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].
