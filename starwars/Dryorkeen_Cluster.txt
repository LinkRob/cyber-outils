{{Quote|There're probably more corrupt governors out there than there were ten years ago, and that can't ''all'' be blamed on the Rebellion. The famines in the Dryorkeen Cluster are real.|Yrica Quell, Soran Keize|<ref name="Alphabet">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>}}
The '''Dryorkeen Cluster''' was a<ref name="Alphabet" /> [[star cluster]] in the [[Parmic sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where a famine occurred during the [[Imperial Era]]. [[Yrica Quell]], a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], believed that the Empire failed to help with the famine. She voiced this opinion to [[Major]] [[Soran Keize]] when expressing her distaste in what the Empire's inefficiency and excessive blaming of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] in the process.<ref name="Alphabet" />
