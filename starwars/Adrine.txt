'''Adrine''' was the [[Gender|sister]] of [[Marsa]] and [[Tay Kolma]], a [[Chandrilan]] [[human]] associated with the [[Bank of Kolma|Bank Of Kolma]]. The sisters at one point met Chandrilan [[senator]] [[Mon Mothma]]'s daughter, [[Leida Mothma]], on her [[homeworld]], [[Chandrila]]. When Leida and Kolma encountered one another on<ref name="Announcement" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]]<ref name="Announcement" /> in [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|107}}</ref> Leida was reminded by Mon that she had previously met Kolma and his sisters, though Leida was not sure that she remembered the previous encounter.<ref name="Announcement" />
