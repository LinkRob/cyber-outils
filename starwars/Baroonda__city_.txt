'''Baroonda''' was a [[city]]<ref name="GEStaff" /> where [[podracing]] took place. When [[Syril Karn]] attempted to gain entry to the [[Imperial office complex]] on [[Ghorman]] after hours, the [[Unidentified Imperial office complex guard 1|two]] [[Unidentified Imperial office complex guard 2|guards]] were watching a podrace on a [[datapad]] screen, with [[Aurebesh]] text at the bottom indicating that the race took place in Baroonda, at [[Grabvine Gateway]].<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman?">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}}</ref>

The last race of [[The Rawani Cup]] [[podracing]] event was held in Baroonda. The event was broadcast from the city by [[alien]] [[Bakk and Zico]] on radio station [[BSO 401.72]]. The broadcast was played in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="GEStaff">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] has official backstory elements that, though not directly published for the public, are officially canon details that can be obtained verbally from cast members working in the land.</ref> It shared its name with the [[Baroonda system]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The city Baroonda was first created for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at Disneyland Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="GEStaff" /> It shares its name with the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Baroonda/Legends|Baroonda]], which first appeared in the [[1999]] video game [[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']], in which it featured as a prominent [[Podracing/Legends|podracing]] location.<ref name="Racer">[[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']]</ref>
