'''FN-3188''' was the designation of<ref name="GE" /> a [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who was a member of the [[First Order]]'s [[FN Trooper Corps]]. In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the First Order intercepted intelligence of a supposed gathering of [[Resistance]] recruits near a [[Spaceport (Batuu)|spaceport]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. FN-3188, along with fellow stormtrooper [[FN-2627]] held up Resistance [[scout]]s at [[blaster]]-point with [[F-11D blaster rifle]]s. After the [[Jedi]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] showed up, the troopers were instructed to watch over the crowd of potential Resistance recruits. Soon, FN-3188 was disarmed and apprehended by the Resistance scouts.<ref name="GE" /><ref name="2023 SWNite Show" />

==Behind the scenes==
FN-3188 appeared in the interactive [[Lightsaber]] Meetup show for [[Star Wars Nite]]s at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disneyland]] in [[2023]], [[2024]], and [[2025]]. The designations of troopers FN-3188 and FN-2627 were revealed in the 2023 version of the show,<ref name="2023 SWNite Show" /> but their designations were cut in the 2024 version.<ref name="2024 SWNite Show">{{YouTube|video=XjSNnfnQ-UU|text=Disneyland After Dark: Star Wars Nite 2024 &#124; Lightsaber Meet-Up with Rey|channel=@laughingplace|name=Laughing Place|date=2024-04-17}}</ref> Their designations were returned in the 2025 version.<ref name="2025 SWNite Show">{{YouTube|video=TwsxbVa2s2M|text=The Force is STILL Strong with This One!!! &#124; STAR WARS NITE 2025 &#124; Disneyland|channel=@nerdydaydreams|name=Nerdy Day Dreams|date=2025-04-19}}</ref>
