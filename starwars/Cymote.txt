'''Cymotes''' were [[Color|purple]] [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[Species|creatures]] that were thought extinct<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11" /> for eons<ref name="Solicit">{{SW|url=news/marvel-june-2021-star-wars-comics-exclusive-preview|text=A General, a Jedi, and a Wookiee Plot a Rescue as ''War of the Bounty Hunters'' Begins and More from Marvel's June 2021 ''Star Wars'' Comics - Exclusive Preview}}</ref> by the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11" /> Considered a nightmarish and horrifying threat,<ref name="Solicit" /> they laid [[egg]]s within the [[mouth]] of a living host and ate their way out of the host when hatched. Because their bodies were delicate under their shells, they were edible. According to the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], they were a popular decoration for the dinnerware of the ancient [[Merelithic]] culture.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 11|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 11]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> a number of cymotes, despite their supposed extinction, were present aboard the [[Luxury cruiser/Canon|luxury cruiser]] ''[[Opal Empress]]'' as it [[transport]]ed [[Ebann Drake]] to what he described to his cousin [[Domina Tagge]] as an [[Auction for Han Solo|incredible opportunity]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11" />

Sometime later, Aphra and the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] were sent by Tagge to locate Drake and discover what the opportunity was. By the time they arrived at the ''Opal Empress'', however, the cymotes had [[Death|killed]] all the passengers of the ''Opal Empress'', including Drake. As they made their way through the [[Starship|ship]], Aphra and Starros discovered that the dead bodies of the passengers, though they were unsure of what had caused all the deaths. The two headed to a cabin to find Drake, and Aphra heard noises that came from the vents, that unbeknownst to her came from the cymotes, Aphra asked Starros if she had also heard them, and when she questioned what Aphra had heard, the noises stopped. Eventually they discovered Drake's corpse and what his big opportunity was, an invitation to a [[Crimson Dawn]] [[Auction for Han Solo|auction]] where they planned to sell off the smuggler [[Han Solo]]. As Starros and Aphra discussed the auction, they were fired at by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Durge]], who had arrived to claim a [[bounty]] on Drake.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11" />

As Aphra and Starros spoke with Durge, the cymotes came out of the vents and attached themselves to Durge, although he was able to get them off him, and the trio made their way into a room. Aphra realized what the creatures were and informed Starros of what they were up against. After Aphra mentioned that they were supposed to be extinct, Durge claimed he could fix that. Starros asked if the cymotes had any weaknesses and Aphra revealed their sensitivity to [[Atmosphere|atmospheric]] changes. With this, Starros suggested sending them out of an [[Airlock|air lock]] and they made their way towards one. Durge lured the cymotes into the air lock, though he was also jettisoned him into [[Realspace|space]] alongside the cymotes by Aphra. Because of the rapid decompression, the cymotes exploded when they were exposed to space.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11" />
