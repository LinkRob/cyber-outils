'''Specialists''' of the [[Coastal Defense Division]] were used by the [[Galactic Empire]] in tropical environments. [[Coastal defender stormtrooper]] specialists wore similar [[armor]] to their counterparts but with [[Color|red]] markings on their chest and shoulders. They usually served as [[sniper]]s, supporting forward units and picking off targets at range using the [[DLT-19x targeting blaster]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Behind the scenes==
This variant of the shoretrooper appeared in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' as part of the Galactic Empire's specialist class.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
