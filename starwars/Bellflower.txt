'''Bellflowers''' were a type of large [[flower]] that grew on [[tree]]s on a number of [[planet]]s. They tempted [[insect]]s to come close, and when they were, the bellflowers trapped the insects for [[Foodstuff|food]].<ref name="Into the Light" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{IntotheLightDate}}</ref> while the [[Nihil]] [[scientist]] [[Doctor]] [[Zadina Mkampa]] tended to saplings of the [[Drengir]]<ref name="Into the Light" />&mdash;[[Sentience|sentient]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[carnivorous plant]]s&mdash;she thought about bellflowers and how they acted as an example of the [[Diet|predatory]] side of [[plant]] life in comparison to the common perception of them as harmless.<ref name="Into the Light" />

==Behind the scenes==
