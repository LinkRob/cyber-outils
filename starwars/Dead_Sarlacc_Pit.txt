A [[Unidentified sarlacc (Western Dune Sea)|sarlacc]] died [[Standard year|years]] before [[3 ABY]] leaving behind its '''pit''' in the [[Western Dune Sea]] region of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. By 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], scavengers had become aware of the pit and visited it to search for treasure.

==Description==
The pit was located in the center of several small rock formations in the [[Western Dune Sea]] region of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

==History==
A [[Unidentified sarlacc (Western Dune Sea)|sarlacc]] lived in the pit and had died a number of [[Standard year|years]]<ref name="Outlaws" />  before [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> leaving behind its pit as part of the desert landscape.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Over time, the sarlacc's insides merged with rock and sand<ref name="ArtOfOutlaws">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]''</ref> and by 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> [[scavenger]]s had begun to explore the pit in search of treasure.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] visited a [[Teeka's tribe|group]] of [[Jawa]]s in the [[South Jundland Wastes]] region of Tatooine while looking for someone to add a [[laser turret]] to the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]]. Negotiations quickly halted due to Vess's inability to speak with [[Teeka]], one of the Jawa [[merchant]]s in their native [[language]] of [[Jawaese]]. However a nearby [[protocol droid]] designated [[22-X]] interrupted to [[Translator|translate]], informing Vess that Teeka was willing to install a [[TU-B4 laser turret|laser turret]] in exchange for a [[tooth]]. The [[droid]] went on to clarify that Teeka wanted a tooth from a sarlacc's second [[mouth]]. After agreeing to get the tooth, Vess contacted her crew who informed her that sarlacc's were found in the Dune Sea.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

While Vess traveled to the dead sarlacc pit, her companion, the [[BX-series droid commando]] [[ND-5]] warned her that pristene [[sarlacc tooth|sarlacc teath]] were worth a lot on the [[black market]] and that she should be wary of other scavegers. Before Vess arrived, a group of bandits had setup at the edge of the pit and had sent a [[hired gun droid]] in to the pit to search for teeth. The bandits were still waiting on their droid when Vess arrived, who after handling the bandits slid into the pit past the dead sarlacc's first mouth. As she made her way though the insides of the pit she discovered a wrecked [[bantha-II cargo skiff]] that the sarlacc had eaten before its death. Vess eventually found the bandit's droid which had been deactived while still holding a pristine sarlacc tooth. She then procceeded to climb out through a side passage that led to a narrow opening in the nearby rock formations.<ref name="Outlaws" />

As she reached the opening she discovered an active [[grenade]] thrown by a member of the bandit group. She managed to dive out of the way of the explosion but was knocked brieft unconsious while the bandit searched her wand stole the sabacc tooth. She came too as the bandit got on their [[speeder bike]] and speed away, however she managed to catch up to the bandit and recover the tooth. When she returned to the Jawa's to finalize the agreement, she discovered that the group had a whole crate full of sarlac teeth. Later the same year, Vess accepted a contract from a [[Contract broker|broker]] that involved retireving cargo lost by the client near the sarlacc pit.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}
