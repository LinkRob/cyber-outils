The '''''Book of Hondo''''' was a [[book]] written by [[Hondo Ohnaka]],<ref name="Force Collection" /> a notorious [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] active during the [[Clone Wars]] and [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|hondo-ohnaka|Hondo Ohnaka}}</ref> It was published a few [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Helmet Collection 41">{{HelmetCollectionCite|41|Databank A-Z}}</ref> The book outlined Ohnaka's philosophy regarding his nefarious lifestyle,<ref name="Force Collection" /> and it included advice such as "[[Thief|stealing]]" an opportunity rather than waiting for one.<ref name="Crimson Corsair" /> Considered to be Ohnaka's most infamous contribution, the ''Book of Hondo'' was rumored to have inspired other well-known criminals, such as the "[[Sidon Ithano|Crimson Corsair]]."<ref name="Force Collection" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Book of Hondo'' was first mentioned in the epigraph of "[[The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku]]," a short story written by [[Landry Q. Walker]] and released as an [[eBook]] in [[2015]] under the [[Journey to Star Wars: The Force Awakens|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']] publishing label.<ref name="Crimson Corsair" /> In [[2016]], the short story was republished in the book ''[[Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I]]'', although the epigraph which mentioned the ''Book of Hondo'' was not included.<ref name="Tales">''[[Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I]]''</ref>
