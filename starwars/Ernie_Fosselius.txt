For Poggle, I had this old idea that I'd first tried for Martians in a story I made on tape back in high school: You record some dialogue, play it backward, imitate what it sounds like in reverse, and record it again. Ernie was superb in imitating his own voice backward; we both laughed ourselves into tears as we tried to produce those sounds.|Ben Burtt|<ref name="Sounds">''[[The Sounds of Star Wars]]''</ref>}}
'''Ernie Fosselius''' is a filmmaker who wrote and directed the first high-profile Star Wars fan film, [[Wikipedia:Hardware Wars|''Hardware Wars'']], released in [[1978]]. He later provided the voice of [[Malakili]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="Red Five">{{InsiderCite|150|Red Five}}</ref> Fosselius also recorded dialogue for the [[Geonosian]] [[Poggle the Lesser]] for the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. He imitated the sounds of his dialogue played backwards and recorded [[Wikipedia:Swahili language|Swahili]]-type clicking noises and the sound of him blowing on the edge of a piece of paper, which were all used by sound designer [[Ben Burtt]] to create Poggle's [[Geonosian (language)/Legends|Geonosian language]] dialogue. Fosselius replaced<ref name="Sounds" /> actor [[Marton Csokas]] in the role after he had been previously cast as the character.<ref name="NZHerald">{{WebCite|url=https://www.nzherald.co.nz/lifestyle/marton-csokas-swimming-with-hollywood-sharks/LEFFTUWT7F2G5DOAYLHGKTX3YQ|text=Marton Csokas, swimming with Hollywood sharks|author=Russell Baillie|work=[[Wikipedia:The New Zealand Herald|The New Zealand Herald]]|archivedate=20201113204338}}</ref> Both actors were uncredited in the finished film.<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref>
