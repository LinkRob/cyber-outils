The '''bluevev glider''' was a [[species]] of large [[Color|blue]] [[fish]] found in the [[ocean]]s of the [[planet]] [[Corellia]], where they could be purchased in [[Coronet City]]'s fishmarkets.<ref name="Solo" /> They could also be found in the [[water]]s of [[Nur]],<ref name="J:FO" /> an ocean [[moon]] in the [[Mustafar system]].<ref name="Part IV">{{Kenobi|Part IV}}'</ref> They possessed [[poison]] barbs on their sides<ref name="Solo" /> and were notable for being longer than a [[human]] was tall.<ref name="Expanded">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
