"'''From the Trees'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year three of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was a stand-alone adventure and was written by [[Ed Gibson]] and edited by [[Jae Walker]] and [[Margaret van Poelgeest-Heintz|Margaret Heintz]].

==Plot summary==
The [[Wyrd]] attack a [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] settlement on [[Cularin/Legends|Cularin]].
