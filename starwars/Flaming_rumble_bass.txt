The '''flaming rumble bass''' was a [[music]]al instrument and weapon, combining the function of a [[rumble bass]] and a [[flamethrower]]. The [[Hutt]] [[Musician|guitarist]] [[Geezer Odajion]] of the band [[Star Waver]] performed with one, which he also used in combat.<ref name="Tatooine Rhapsody" />
