'''Christine Crabb''' worked as an editor for several ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Roleplaying game|RPGs]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]], such as ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', and ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''. Crabb also worked as a proofreader for ''[[Starships and Speeders]]'' published by Fantasy Flight Games.
