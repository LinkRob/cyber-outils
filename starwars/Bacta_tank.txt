A '''bacta tank''', also known as '''bacta pod''',<ref name="Stranger" /> was a cylindrical tank into which a being could be submerged for extensive exposure to [[bacta]], a fluid that could accelerate healing and treat major injuries.<ref name="explV" />

==History==
Bacta tanks existed during the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> by [[231 BBY]],<ref name="231 note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> during which time bacta was new.<ref name="Light">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

When [[Jedi Knight]] [[Dagan Gera]] betrayed the [[Jedi Order]] after feeling betrayed when the [[Jedi]] gave up on his discovery, the planet [[Tanalorr]], his right arm was severed by the [[Santari Khri's lightsaber|lightsaber]] of his former Jedi colleague, [[Santari Khri]]. He was placed in a bacta tank for treatment on [[Koboh]], but an [[Emergences|emergence]] that threatened the planet after its [[Shattered Moon|moon]] was shattered left him unconscious in the tank for 224 years.<ref>[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]</ref>

After a gruesome [[Sabotage of Grievous' shuttle|shuttle crash]]<ref name="Galaxy at War">''[[Star Wars: A Galaxy at War]]''</ref> that nearly [[Death|killed]] him,<ref name="HC 8">{{HelmetCollectionCite|8|Helmets}}</ref> the [[Kaleesh]] [[warlord]] [[Grievous]] was fatally injured<ref name="Galaxy at War" /> and was put into a bacta tank,<ref name="German Build X-Wing 47">{{BuildXWingCite|47|Starfighter Aces}} (German edition)</ref> resulting in his transformation into a [[cyborg]].<ref name="lair">{{TCW|Lair of Grievous}}</ref>

During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] used them to treat its injured [[Grand Army of the Republic|soldiers]].<ref name="SoM" />

[[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] was placed into a bacta tank after the injuries she sustained in the [[attack on the Executive Center]], having been caught of the blast of the former [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[assassin]] [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]] when she activated his self-destruct mechanism. Between her time in the bacta tank, which erased any visible scars or signs of the surgery, and being operated on atop surgical cots, she was not fully conscious for the first few days after the attack. After finally regaining consciousness, the confused Mothma did not understand that the bacta had erased any possible scars and started to panic, only to be put back to sleep by a droid for, in its words, "her own safety."<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

A bacta tank was located in a chamber in [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[Fortress Vader|castle]] on [[Mustafar]], where the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] would relax and [[Meditation|meditate]] outside of his [[Darth Vader's armor|life support armor]] with a modicum of comfort.<ref name="ROASWS" /><ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

[[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was put in a bacta tank to heal from burns inflicted by Darth Vader on [[Mapuzo]].<ref name="Part III">{{Kenobi|Part III}}</ref>

After being attacked and severely injured by a [[wampa]] ice creature on the frigid [[planet]] of [[Hoth]], [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]] was put into a bacta tank so he could recover from his wounds.<ref name="ESB" />

During the [[Scourge crisis|scourge of the droids]], two [[DT-series sentry droid]]s infected by the [[Scourge]] attacked Vader's bacta tank with the element of surprise, killing the two [[Emperor's Royal Guard]]s standing vigilant and destroying the tank. However, Vader harnessed the [[Force power|power]] of the [[The Force|Force]] to crush both units.<ref name="Dark Droids 3">[[Dark Droids 3|''Dark Droids'' 3]]</ref> The tank was later repaired, from which Vader reached out to his [[Sith Master|master]] through the Force after another [[Attack on Vader|skirmish on Mustafar]].<ref name="Vader 39">[[Darth Vader (2020) 39|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 39]]</ref>

[[File:Boba_Fett_Bacta_Pod.png|thumb|left|200px|Boba Fett lies in his bacta pod]]
A [[Boba Fett's bacta pod|portable format of the bacta tank]]<ref name="Stranger" /> was installed within [[Boba Fett's Palace]] on [[Tatooine]] by [[Boba Fett]],<ref name="World Building">{{InsiderCite|227|World Building (Star Wars Insider 227)}}</ref> who bathed in his bacta pod at many points in [[time]].<ref name="Stranger" /><ref name="Tribes">{{BOBF|Chapter 2: The Tribes of Tatooine}}</ref><ref name="Streets">{{BOBF|Chapter 3: The Streets of Mos Espa}}</ref> He used it to heal his burned scars<ref name="The Gathering Storm">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref><ref name="Chapter 4 audio description">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> from the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]]'s stomach.<ref name="World Building" /> After a failed assassination [[Assassination attempt on Boba Fett|attempt]], Boba put [[Unidentified Gamorrean guard 1|one]] of his [[Gamorrean]] guard in his bacta tank, after being bitten by [[Krrsantan]] in the left shoulder.<ref name="Streets" /> After the [[Battle of Mos Espa|battle for Mos Espa]] Fett's bacta tank was used for [[Cobb Vanth]], after he was [[Showdown in Freetown|shot]] by [[Cad Bane]], while a [[The Modifier|cyborg modifier]] was taking care of him.<ref name="Name of Honor">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|While I was in the bacta tank fully submerged in water, I had to hold my breath for all that too. For some reason I couldn't breathe through the regulator. Although when I saw Ewan [McGregor] inside of it, it looked like he was actually getting oxygen through his because you can see the bubbles going. But no, I had to hold my breath, and I really enjoyed it. I mean, I found it very peaceful in the bacta tank and they're sort of meditative.|Hayden Christensen|<ref name="Christensen bacta">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cinemablend.com/interviews/star-wars-hayden-christensen-on-holding-his-breath-and-performing-as-darth-vader-in-the-obi-wan-kenobi-bacta-tank|text=Star Wars' Hayden Christensen On Holding His Breath And Performing As Darth Vader In The Obi-Wan Kenobi Bacta Tank|author=Hurley, Laura|date=2022-06-21|work=[https://www.cinemablend.com CinemaBlend]|archivedate=20220621210932}}</ref>}}
Bacta tanks first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], the second installment in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]].<ref name="ESB" /> They were first identified in "[[Shadow of Malevolence|Shadow of ''Malevolence'']]," the third episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated series.<ref name="SoM" />

[[File:HaydenChristenseninBactaTank.png|thumb|right|220px|Hayden Christensen in a bacta tank]]
In the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' television series, [[Hayden Christensen]] played a [[helmet]]less [[Darth]] Vader in a bacta tank. The crew knew the actor would have to hold his breath while submerged in the [[water]], so he worked with a dive instructor who helped him build up his breath holds. Christensen wasn't allowed to breathe through the regulator, but [[Ewan McGregor]], the actor who played [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], was getting oxygen during his bacta tank scene.<ref name="Christensen bacta" /> A team of make-up artists spent four to five hours applying make-up on Christensen for him to play Vader in those scenes. He worked very closely with executive creative director of [[Lucasfilm]], [[Doug Chiang]], who gave input into the overall look.<ref name="Christensen Hollywood">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/obi-wan-kenobi-hayden-christensen-darth-vader-anakin-sand-feelings-1235162358|text='Obi-Wan Kenobi' Star Hayden Christensen Explores His Latest Darth Vader Incarnation and Talks Anakin's (Much-Memed) Sand Feelings|author=Parker, Ryan|date=2022-06-13|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20220613130155}}</ref>
