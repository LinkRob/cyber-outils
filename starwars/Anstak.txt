'''Anstak''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi Master]] of an [[Water|aquatic]] [[species]]. According to the [[Qel-Droma Epics]], Anstak was in charge of a temple within the [[Imhar Canyon]] on the [[Jedi Order]] [[planet]] of [[Ossus]]. The temple was situated in a system of submerged tunnels, where Anstak trained numerous aquatic [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s. Anstak kept a number of [[The Force|Force]] artifacts, often related to underwater endeavors. According to the Epics, Ossus was devastated by the [[Cron Supernova]]e, but some believed Anstak and his students could have survived within the deep [[cave]]rns.<ref name="Nexus of Power" />
