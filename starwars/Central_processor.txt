A '''central processor''' was a piece of technology that was used in a [[droid]] or [[mech]] that functioned like a [[brain]] for the device. [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] repaired one for [[Pran]] of the [[Open]] during the [[mission to Carnelion IV]].<ref name="Part III">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 3|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 3]]</ref> [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] considered the central processor of [[L3-37]] to be [[light-year]]s more advanced than that of any droid he had ever met.<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref>
