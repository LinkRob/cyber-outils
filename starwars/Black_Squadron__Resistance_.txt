'''Black Squadron''' was a [[Resistance]] [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] founded by [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]]. Black Squadron played a key role during several engagements in the [[Cold War|conflict]] between the Resistance and the [[First Order]]. The squadron mainly fielded [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s, but flew other craft such a [[BTL-S3 Y-wing starfighter|BTL-S3 Y-wing Starfighter]]. They notably participated in the [[Hunt for Lor San Tekka|search]] for [[Lor San Tekka]] traveling to [[planet]]s such as [[Ovanis]] and [[Megalox Beta]].

==History==
===Mission to Ovanis===
[[File:BlackSquadronAssembled-PoeDameron1.png|thumb|left|Black Squadron at D'Qar]]
After [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] recruited [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] to [[Hunt for Lor San Tekka|search]] for [[Lor San Tekka]],<ref name="Before the Awakening">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref> Dameron selected a [[squadron]] of four [[Resistance pilot|pilots]] and a [[technician]] known as [[Color|Black]] Squadron. The squadron's members included the [[pilot]]s [[Captain]]<ref name="BuildXwing26" /> [[Temmin Wexley]], [[Karé Kun]], [[Jessika Pava]], [[L'ulo L'ampar]], the [[technician]] [[Oddy Muva]], and several [[astromech droid]]s including [[BB-8]]. For their [[Mission to Ovanis|first mission]], Black Squadron with the exception of Muva traveled to the [[mountain]]ous [[Planet|world]] of [[Ovanis]]. Dameron encountered the [[Crèche]] cult and questioned their [[Unidentified Crèche elder|leader]] about Tekka's whereabouts. Dameron learned that Tekka had visited Ovanis a long [[time]] ago.<ref name="PD1" /> However, Muva had been blackmailed by the [[First Order Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Terex]] into spying on Black Squadron.<ref name="PD11">[[Poe Dameron 11|''Poe Dameron'' 11]]</ref>

Black Squadron was subsequently involved in a skirmish with [[First Order]] [[First Order military|forces]] led by Terex, who had been sent to recover the information that Dameron had acquired during [[Operation: Sabre Strike]]. While Dameron and BB-8 tangled with Terex and his landing party, Wexley and the other pilots engaged in a dogfight with First Order [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s in the skies above Ovanis.<ref name="PD2">[[Poe Dameron 2|''Poe Dameron'' 2]]</ref> The skirmish was interrupted when the [[Crèche egg]] hatched and released two [[Unidentified Crèche creature|large flying creatures]], which fought each other. Dameron and BB-8 managed to overpower Terex and forced him to call off his forces. After the Crèche cultists departed with one one of the flying creatures, Black Squadron refueled and departed. Unwilling to risk a [[Cold War|war]] between the [[New Republic]] and the First Order, Black Squadron allowed Terex and his surviving man to leave.<ref name="PD3">[[Poe Dameron 3|''Poe Dameron'' 3]]</ref>

===Rescuing Grakkus===
Continuing their search for Tekka, Black Squadron [[Mission to Megalox Beta|traveled]] to [[Megalox]] [[prison]] on the high [[gravity]] world of [[Megalox Beta]] to question the [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] the [[Hutt]]. With Organa's help, Black Squadron managed to bribe [[Warden]] [[Luta]] into allowing them to enter the prison. However, Terex learned they were coming and bribed Luta into abandoning Black Squadron to the prisoners. Despite this setback, Black Squadron managed to fight their way into Grakkus' [[Fortress of Grakkus the Hutt|fortress]]. Grakkus granted Dameron and Terex an audience but refused to divulge the information on Tekka until one of them had freed him.<ref name="PD4">[[Poe Dameron 4|''Poe Dameron'' 4]]</ref>

In response, Dameron got BB-8 and the other astromech [[droid]]s [[O-R10N]], [[R2-HA]], and [[R4 (Black Squadron)|R4]] to disable the [[Megalox space station]]'s gravity shield generator. Black Squadron also fought off an attempt by [[gangster]]s hired by Terex to storm the fortress and [[Death|kill]] Grakkus.<ref name="PD5">[[Poe Dameron 5|''Poe Dameron'' 5]]</ref> After disabling the gravity shield, Dameron managed to force Luta to let them and Grakkus leave the prison in exchanging for restoring the gravity shield. Before they could leave the system, Terex's [[starship]] ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' attacked Megalox station. Black Squadron drove off the ''Carrion Spike'' and obtained the data from Grakkus. In return, the Hutt was given [[hyperspace]] coordinates and allowed to leave. After returning, Organa instructed Dameron to suspend his search for Tekka to investigate the presence of a [[spy]] within Black Squadron.<ref name="PD6">[[Poe Dameron 6|''Poe Dameron'' 6]]</ref>

===Heist of the Alderaanian data archive===
[[File:HeistArchive-PoeDameronAnnual2.jpg|thumb|407x407px|Black Squadron using Clone Wars-era starfighters to acquire the data archive from Nu'Tiv's cruiser]]
Poe and the rest of Black Squadron were assigned by Organa to [[Heist of the Alderaanian data archive|acquire]] a [[Archive of the Great Library of Alderaan|data archive]] containing what was left of the [[Royal Library of Alderaan|Royal Library]] of [[Alderaan]], which the trader [[Mek Nu'Tiv|Mek Nu’Tiv]] was planning to sell to the First Order. The four pilots used old [[Clone Wars]]-era starfighters so that their identities would not be discovered. When Black Squadron arrived at [[Mek Nu'Tiv's cruiser|Nu’Tiv’s cruiser]], she ordered her men to fire on the pilots and sent starfighters to intercept them, as she believed that they were associates of smugglers [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]], who had posed as First Order officers. Poe managed to land his [[Clone Z-95 starfighter|Clone Z-95 Headhunter]] on the cruiser while the others were engaging the starfighters, but was cornered by Nu’Tiv herself. BB-8 managed to stun Nu’Tiv and Dameron managed to acquire the archive. Unbeknownst to him, Solo and Chewbacca had found the archive first, but upon realising its importance to Organa, decided to let the Resistance take it and secretly cleared the way for Poe to make his escape. By the time he escaped the cruiser, the rest of Black Squadron had destroyed all of Nu’Tiv’s fighters and the four escaped with data archive to return it the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar|D’Qar]].<ref name="PDA2" />

===Test of trust===
Following the events on Megalox Beta, Poe undertook a [[Skirmish on Pheryon|solo mission]] to [[Pheryon]] to meet with the ''[[Galaxy Beacon]]'' [[journalist]] [[Suralinda Javos]], who had information on the First Order's militarization efforts. After escaping First Order forces, Javos decided to join the [[Resistance]].<ref name="PD7">[[Poe Dameron 7|''Poe Dameron'' 7]]</ref> Still unable to trust his fellow pilots due to the presence of a spy, Dameron brought Muva, BB-8, and Organa's spymaster [[C-3PO]] on a [[Mission to Kaddak|mission]] to recover one of the [[Resistance spy droid network|Resistance's droid spies]] on [[Kaddak]].<ref name="PD8">[[Poe Dameron 8|''Poe Dameron'' 8]]</ref>

Dameron and his team managed to rescue the [[BX-series droid commando]] [[N1-ZX]], who was purportedly carrying vital First Order intelligence including the location of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]].<ref name="PD10">[[Poe Dameron 10|''Poe Dameron'' 10]]</ref> In truth, this was a trap set by Terex to use Dameron and the droid to track down the [[Resistance base]]. Terex wanted to destroy the Resistance with his [[Ranc Gang]] forces.<ref name="PD11" /> Dameron and the droids managed to flee offworld on Dameron's [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] ''[[Black One]]''.<ref name="PD10" /> Terex gave chase in his flagship ''Carrion Spike'' and a [[fleet]] of "[[Ugly|Uglies]]."<ref name="PD11" />

During the journey, Dameron realized that Muva was the spy and suspected that Terex had planted the information in order to find the location of the Resistance base. Dameron exited hyperspace above an [[Unidentified desert planet|uncharted desert planet]]. Terex and the Ranc Gang followed and forced Dameron to ground on the [[desert]] planet. Meanwhile, Muva infiltrated the ''Carrion Spike''<ref name="PD11" /> and managed to reunite with his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Sowa Chuan]], who had been taken captive by Terex to exert leverage over her husband. Muva also encountered Terex's harem of [[Slavery|slaves]].<ref name="PD12">[[Poe Dameron 12|''Poe Dameron'' 12]]</ref>

Dameron and the droids fled into a cave. BB-8 and C-3PO managed to delay Terex long enough for the rest of Black Squadron to [[Battle over a desert planet|enter]] the desert planet's [[Star system|system]].<ref name="PD12" /> During the ensuing dogfight, Muva managed to reprogram the ''Carrion Spike''{{'s}} battle [[computer]]s to target Terex's "Uglies." Black Squadron provided cover for Muva and the escaped slaves' [[escape pod]]s while fighting with Terex's Ranc fighters. L'ulo shot down one of the "Uglies" that destroyed an escape pod. However, he was shot down by another fighter. While Dameron and BB-8 fought with Terex, First Order forces led by Commander [[Malarus]] exited hyperspace and wiped out the Ranc Gang forces and Terex's flagship but did not target the Resistance. Dameron's Black Squadron mates flew over Malarus' landing party while Dameron met with the First Order commander. After learning she had come to apprehend Terex for disobeying Snoke's directive to avoid open conflict with the Resistance, Poe handed the rogue First Order agent to her.<ref name="PD13">[[Poe Dameron 13|''Poe Dameron'' 13]]</ref>

===Gathering allies===
Following the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], General Leia Organa sent Black Squadron on a mission to gather allies and bring new [[Star system|systems]] into the Resistance.<ref name="PD28" /> The squadron was also accompanied by its newest member, Suralinda Javos (Black Four) in an [[RZ-2 A-wing interceptor]].<ref name="PD29" />

===Battle of Ikkrukk===
As part of the mission to gather allies, Black Squadron went to [[Grail City]] on the planet [[Ikkrukk]]. However, when they arrived there, the city was under [[Battle of Ikkrukk|attack]] from a First Order heavy cruiser, ''[[Fortitude]]'', which diverted all necessary TIEs it had to engage Black Squadron in an aerial battle over the city's shield, which was slowly weakening under the bombardment. Grail City had a [[FreiTek Incorporated|FreiTek]] planetary defense grid with an integated central processor with multi-hemispheric tracking, which Pava had previously trained on in the navy. Pava suggested that if she could get to the command center, she could bring it online. A tiny section of the shield was opened, so Pava and Javos could slip down to the surface, while Wexley and Kun stayed outside to battle the TIEs. However, as they made the attempt, Pava clipped her X-wing and crash landed on the surface below. As Pava and Javos battled their way on foot to the command center, things turned for the worse and Javos was shot.<ref name="PD30">[[Poe Dameron 30|''Poe Dameron'' 30]]</ref>

[[File:SuralindaJavos-SWZ.png|thumb|right|250px|Suralinda Javos and Jessika Pava fly through the shield protecting Grail City]]
Pava administered medical aid to Javos and convinced her to continue fighting. Outside the shield, Wexley and Kun continued their struggle with the seemingly endless number of TIEs, so Wexley suggested getting as close as they could to the heavy cruiser, so the TIEs chasing them would be less likely to shoot them for fear of damaging their own ship. Kun took a hit to her stabiliser, locking her heading and losing her weapons, and ended up tracking towards an enemy turret that had a targeting solution on her. Out of nowhere, Commander Poe Dameron arrived and destroyed the turret, saving Kun, who was able to restore full control to her ship. Dameron ordered their ships to pull away from the ''Fortitude'' and as they did so, the shield over the city disappeared and the planetary defense grid activated, destroying the heavy cruiser. For saving the city, Prime Minister [[Grist]] promised to provide support the Resistance,<ref name="PD31">[[Poe Dameron 31|''Poe Dameron'' 31]]</ref> although she went back on her promise mere hours later.<ref name="Resistance Reborn">''[[Resistance Reborn]]''</ref>
