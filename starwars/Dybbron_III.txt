'''Dybbron III''', or just '''Dybbron''', was a [[planet]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]' [[Dybbron system]] which hosted a breakaway faction of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the later [[Galactic Civil War]]. In [[5 ABY]], it was [[Operation: Cinder|targeted]] by the loyalist [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], which [[Attack on Dybbron III|engulfed]] the planet as part of its campaign against Imperial breakaway worlds. After learning of the attacks, [[New Republic]] [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] sent some of her [[Barma Battle Group|forces]] to Dybbron III to check for survivors.

==Description==
{{Quote|There are civilians on those planets.|Wyl Lark|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
Dybbron III, or simply Dybbron, was a [[planet]] located in the third [[orbit]]al position<ref name="Victory's Price" /> of the [[Dybbron system]], within the intersection of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing" /> It was situated in the [[Nythlide Array]] [[sector]] at the coordinates L-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> Dybbron III had its own [[Plant|vegetation]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==History==
{{Quote|But what about Fedovoi End, and the others?''"<br />"''The ships we left in Nythlide were scheduled to rendezvous with us after operations there were wrapped up. I'm rerouting them to look for survivors.|Wyl Lark and Hera Syndulla|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
[[File:MaryElizabethWinsteadasHeraSyndulla-ToppsChoice2025.png|thumb|left|250px|General Hera Syndulla rerouted some of her forces to search for survivors at Dybbron III.]]
During the later [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[New Republic]], the Imperial forces at Dybbron III broke away from the [[Imperial Military|loyalist forces]] of the Empire,<ref name="Victory's Price" /> which had been splintering since the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] [[death]]<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> As a result, the world was targeted by the loyalist aligned [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] in its [[Operation: Cinder|campaign]] to purge Imperial breakaway worlds<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="5 ABY">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> When the 204th [[Attack on Dybbron III|confronted]] the forces on Dybbron III, it gave the rogue Imperials there an offer to re-join the loyalist side. At least two took the offer, [[Heirorius]] and [[Starzha]], who joined the 204th's ranks before the planet was engulfed by the [[fighter wing]]. Some vegetation was also collected from Dybbron III before the world's devastation, and a [[Unidentified pirate gunship|pirate gunship]] from the local evidence yards.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

The 204th's campaign continued with the fall of the planets [[Kortatka]] and [[Fedovoi End]], which together formed a near straight path from Dybbron III. After catching onto the fighter wing's trail, [[Barma Battle Group|New Republic forces]] led by [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] learned of the 204th's attacks on Dybbron III and the other two worlds, which had all gone silent for the New Republic. The general redirected other [[starship]]s she had placed in the Nythlide Array to look for survivors at each fallen planet. When Syndulla discussed the situation with members of the New Republic's [[Alphabet Squadron]], [[Captain]] [[Nath Tensent]] questioned using resources to aid Imperial strongholds, with [[Starfighter Commander]] [[Wyl Lark]] arguing that there were civilian populations on them.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
