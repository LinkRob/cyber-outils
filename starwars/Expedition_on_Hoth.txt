No earlier than [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> a six-[[Standard month|month]]-long '''Expedition on Hoth''' was undertaken by the [[New Republic]]'s [[Department of Artifacts and Archival Goods Retrieval]] on<ref name="Chaos Agent 3" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Hoth]]. After the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]]&mdash;who worked for the department&mdash;was informed by her boss<ref name="Chaos Agent 3" /> [[Director]] [[Meteora Gray]]<ref name="Chaos Agent 1">[[Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent 1|''Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent'' 1]]</ref> that she had been assigned onto a [[Mission to Felucia (New Republic)|different mission]], Aphra asked the director if<ref name="Chaos Agent 3" /> she<ref name="Chaos Agent 1" /> was sure she did not prefer to have Aphra go on the Hoth expedition instead. The archaeologist was ultimately unsuccessful in her efforts to escape her assigned mission.<ref name="Chaos Agent 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Expedition on Hoth was mentioned in the [[Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent 3|third issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent]]''. The issue was written by [[Cherish Chen]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Chaos Agent 1" /> on [[August 27]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/122234/star_wars_doctor_aphra_-_chaos_agent_2025_3|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra - Chaos Agent (2025) #3}}</ref>
