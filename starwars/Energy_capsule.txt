'''Energy capsules''', also known as '''food capsules''', were a type of [[Foodstuff|food]]. Members of the [[Jedi Order]] often carried energy capsules on their [[utility belt]]s. Each contained tiny pellets with enough energy to sustain a [[Jedi]] on distant [[planet]]s and long [[Realspace|space]] journeys.<ref name="VE" /> The patrons of a [[cantina]] on [[Enujut]] were once compelled by the [[Telepathy|telepathic]] [[Zemerin]] into arguing over what type of food capsule tasted best.<ref name="JTC-E2">{{JTC|2}}</ref>
