'''Ann Nocenti''' is a journalist, writer and editor who worked in several ''Star Wars'' publications from [[Marvel Comics]] starting in 1984. After being listed as an editor of [[Star Wars (1977) 84|''Star Wars'' (1977) 84]] along with [[Louise Simonson]], she became the sole editor of the [[Star Wars (1977) 85|next issue]] and continued to edit the series until its [[Star Wars (1977) 107|final issue]] in 1986. She was also the writer of [[Star Wars (1977) 89|''Star Wars'' (1977) 89]].

Nocenti was serving as editor when future ''Star Wars'' [[Dark Horse Comics]] writer and editor [[Randy Stradley]] wrote [[Star Wars (1977) 86|''Star Wars'' (1977) 86]] ([[1984]]). After this, she asked Stradley to write another story, this time involving [[Hoojib/Legends|Hoojibs]]. Stradley found this task to be too difficult and stopped working for Marvel.<ref name="Wookiee World">Foreword to ''[[Star Wars: A Long Time Ago... Volume 6: Wookiee World]]'', written by Randy Stradley</ref>

In recent years, Nocenti has worked primarily with DC Comics; her latest work was on the company's ''Catwoman'' title.
