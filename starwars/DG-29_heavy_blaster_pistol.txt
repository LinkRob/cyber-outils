The '''DG-29 heavy blaster pistol''', also known as the '''DG-29 sideloader''', was a model of [[heavy blaster pistol]] produced by [[BlasTech Industries]], and was the preferred weapon of [[Tobias Beckett]] for his dominant left hand. While fighting [[Enfys Nest]], it was kicked out of his hand. But he manage to used a black DG-29 on [[Kessel]]. His weapon had a rainbow-ring caused by heat-exposure oxidation.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> The DG-29 had a similar grip and power ampule socket as the [[RSKF-44 heavy blaster|RSKF-44]] but was side-loading and sported an image magnifying [[macroscope]] for long range shots.<ref name="Databank" />
