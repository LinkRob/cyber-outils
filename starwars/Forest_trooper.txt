'''Forest troopers''' were [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s trained to operate in [[forest]]ed environments. To that end, they wore special, camouflaged [[stormtrooper armor]].<ref name="ALD" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a number of forest troopers were [[Imperial territory G5-623|stationed]] on [[Kashyyyk]] as part of its garrison.<ref name="ALD" />
