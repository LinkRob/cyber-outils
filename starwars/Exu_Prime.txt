'''Exu Prime''' was an [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] astronomical object located in the [[Tolonda sector]]'s [[Exu Prime system]]. In [[230 BBY]], it became stranded behind the [[Stormwall]] [[hyperspace]] barrier that defined the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]]-controlled region of [[Realspace|space]] known as the [[Occlusion Zone]].

Between 230 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[229 BBY]], [[Ikkrukkian]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Porter Engle]] [[Ambush on Exu Prime|stole]] the [[Torment (shuttle)|shuttle]] of a [[Unidentified Nihil patrol (Exu Prime)|Nihil patrol]] on Exu Prime. In [[228 BBY]], the Occlusion Zone was dissolved following the [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|Stormwall's fall]].

==Description==
Exu Prime was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> located in the [[Exu Prime system]] within the [[Tolonda sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> and [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing Sectors" /> It was situated in grid square R-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==History==
[[File:Porter-PhaseIII.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Porter Engle ambushed a Nihil patrol on Exu Prime.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], Exu Prime was located<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> within a section of the Outer Rim designated as the [[Galactic Frontier]], which contained locations considered removed far enough from [[the galaxy]]'s center for the [[Galactic Republic]] to send [[Republic Pathfinder|explorers]] there.<ref name="Chronicles" /> After the [[Deployment of the stormseeds|formation]] of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders']] [[Occlusion Zone]] region of [[Realspace|space]]<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'' establishes that the [[ambush on Exu Prime]] took place between the [[Deployment of the stormseeds|formation]] of the [[Occlusion Zone]] and the one-[[Standard year|year]] anniversary of the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to between [[230 BBY]] and [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> Exu Prime became trapped behind the [[Stormwall]] [[hyperspace]] barrier that prevented travel into Nihil territory.<ref name="Eye of Darkness" />

Between 230 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> while the [[Ikkrukkian]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Porter Engle]] was stranded in the Occlusion Zone, he [[Ambush on Exu Prime|ambushed]] a [[Unidentified Nihil patrol (Exu Prime)|Nihil patrol]] on Exu Prime and stole their [[Torment (shuttle)|shuttle]]. Many [[Standard month|months]] later,<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> around 229 BBY,<ref name="Date 2">The chapter of ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'' that mentions Exu Prime takes place not long after the one-[[Standard year|year]] anniversary of the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to around [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> Engle&mdash;still trapped in the Occlusion Zone&mdash;mentally recalled his ambush of the Nihil on Exu Prime while on the [[planet]] [[Daedus]].<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="TrialsDate">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|23|the [[Nihil]]'s surrender}}</ref> the Nihil surrendered to the Republic following the [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|Stormwall's fall]], which dissolved the Occlusion Zone. The worlds of that region were welcomed back into the Republic if they wished to rejoin. However, some of those worlds instead opted to retain their independence, while others remained under the control of individual Nihil [[warlord]]s.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref>
