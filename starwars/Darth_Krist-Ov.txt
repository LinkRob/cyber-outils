'''Darth Krist-Ov''' was a [[Sith Lord]] featured in one of [[Actor|actress]] [[Risha Synata]]'s tragic plays. Performed in the [[Cirius system]] for [[Galactic Republic]] [[senator]]s during the [[Clone Wars]] fought between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the play featured a [[humanoid]] [[Unidentified actor|actor]] portraying Krist-Ov. In the play, Krist-Ov was confronted by his lover, a [[Unidentified Jedi (Darth Krist-Ov)|Jedi]] who left him [[heart]]broken. Seeing that Krist-Ov had fallen to the [[dark side of the Force]], the [[Jedi]] aimed to make the [[Sith]] face his destiny. While the two lovers [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]], Krist-Ov took the upper hand, poising to take his lover's heart and bidding farewell. As he did so, the woebegone Jedi took the opportunity to [[Death|kill]] him. The Jedi then laid down on the ground, Krist-Ov in hand, grieving with the question of why he had to make her kill him.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Farewell, my love.''"<br />"''Farewell.|Krist-Ov and his lover's parting words, as portayed in a play by Synata|<ref name="Intermission" />}}
[[Darth]] Krist-Ov was a [[Sith Lord]] featured in a play that the legendary [[Frenk]] [[Actor|actress]] [[Risha Synata]] produced. Synata's play was set at a [[Color|black]]-and-red [[Sith]] locale, where Krist-Ov was confronted by his [[Unidentified Jedi (Darth Krist-Ov)|Jedi]] lover. She produced a blue [[lightsaber]], and was determined to help realize Krist-Ov's destiny. Krist-Ov pulled out his crimson lightsaber and [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] her, asking what had happened to them and proclaiming that they could have shared a destiny. While the Jedi exclaimed that Krist-Ov took abode in the [[Dark side of the Force|shadows]], the Sith told her that she had shattered his [[heart]] and flung aside her lightsaber. As the tip of Krist-Ov's was lowered and poised to take her heart&mdash;and the Jedi told the Sith to do so&mdash;the Jedi [[Telekinesis|summoned]] her blade while Krist-Ov bid his love farewell. Blade in hand, she closed her [[eye]]s in sadness and [[Death|struck down]] the Sith, bidding Krist-Ov farewell in turn. The Jedi then wallowed in grief, kneeling down on the ground to hold Krit-Ov's body, questioning why he had to force her hand.<ref name="Intermission" />

[[File:TragedyOfLove.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Darth Krist-Ov is confronted by his Jedi lover, as portrayed in a play by Risha Synata.]]
Synata, who based her plays on events in [[the galaxy]] and turned them into tragedies without regard for historical accuracy, performed the Jedi in the play aboard her [[Risha Synata's ship|cruiser]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Soon after the [[Senate hostage crisis]]<ref name="Intermission" /> in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Republic [[senator]]s were invited by Synata to her [[cruiser]] near [[Caamas]] in the [[Cirius system]]. Amongst the guests was [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], who took her secret lover, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], for a two-[[Standard day|day]] vacation aboard the [[Starship|ship]]. However, the pair were late, and thus missed the play of Darth Krist-Ov, which was performed in a theatre packed full of guests.<ref name="Intermission" />

==Personality and traits==
Darth Krist-Ov was portrayed in Synata's play by a [[humanoid]] [[Unidentified actor|actor]]. The humanoid's face was painted a pale white, and he had brown hair and red eyes and [[tattoo]]s. Krist-Ov wished to share a destiny with his lover, and was heartbroken by the Jedi after he himself fell to the dark side of [[the Force]]. Later, the Sith Lord attempted to claim her heart after besting her in a lightsaber duel. Before striking the killing blow, Krist-Ov spoke his farewells to his lover, which left an opening for her to kill Krist-Ov.<ref name="Intermission" />

==Equipment==
In Synata's play, Krist-Ov wielded a red lightsaber and wore black [[robe]]s, which included a hood that concealed his face.<ref name="Intermission" />
