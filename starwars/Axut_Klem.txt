'''Axut Klem''' was a [[Praelion]] who served as a [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[High Republic Era]], representing<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[Japrael sector]]. A stern individual, throughout his career, Klem had little direct connections to the [[Jedi]] &mdash; a monolithic order that worked alongside the Republic as peacekeepers beneath its rule &mdash; despite the presence of two [[Jedi outpost|outposts]] within the Japrael sector, where Axut was an influential leader.<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> however, he had been brought round to Senator [[Worus Rayencourt]] and his proposed external review of the [[Jedi Order|Order]], which included contentious sentiments that Klem had begun to echo, being willing to place the Jedi installations within his [[sector]] under surveillance.<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" />
