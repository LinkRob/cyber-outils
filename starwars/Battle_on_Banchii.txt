In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''battle on Banchii''' occurred when the [[Nihil]], led by [[Minister of Advancement]] [[Baron]] [[Boolan]] attacked the [[planet]] [[Banchii]]. Boolan initially traveled there in hopes of recruiting the various [[Jedi]] there, including [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Lily Tora-Asi]] and [[Reath Silas]], fallen Jedi [[Azlin Rell]], and [[Jedi Master]]s [[Savina Besatrix Malagán]] and [[Arkoff]] for his experiments, with the promise that he would not harm the people of Banchii if they complied. When the Jedi in charge, Tora-Asi, refused, Boolan sent his horde of Nihil [[pirate]]s to attack the planet and the Jedi. As Tora-Asi fought the Nihil, the [[plant]]s of the planet began to attack them too, as members of the [[Drengir]] [[species]] had triggered the plant life to protect Tora-Asi in their stead after the Jedi helped them flee Banchii.<ref name="Edge of Balance 4" />

As the Baron started to flee, not believing he could win the battle, he released [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures, which preyed on [[Force-sensitive]]s such as the Jedi and inhibited them with only their presence. However, the Jedi were assisted by a large group of non-Force-sensitive Banchii natives as well as a group of [[Banchiian]]s led by [[Shosho]], a Nihil defector named [[Maktan Plynt]], and Malagán's friend [[Dexter Jettster]], who defeated the Nameless. Meanwhile, Boolan made one final plea to Rell to join him and study the Nameless, but Rell refused and chose to protect [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Viv'Nia Nia'Viv]] and [[Nima Allets]] instead. With the Nameless defeated, the Jedi drove the Nihil away, winning the battle and successfully protecting the planet.<ref name="Edge of Balance 4" />
