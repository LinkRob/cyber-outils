The '''AT-ST Raider'''<ref name="Force Friday">{{SW|url=news/triple-force-friday-products|text=Check Out New ''Star Wars'' Products Just Revealed for Triple Force Friday}}</ref> was a warpainted and heavily modified variant of the [[All Terrain Scout Transport]] used by a [[Klatooinian]] raider [[Klatooinian raiders|tribe]] after the [[Battle of Jakku|fall]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The AT-ST was later destroyed by [[Din Djarin]] and [[Carasynthia Dune|Cara Dune]] when they drew it to the [[Unidentified village (Sorgan)|village]] that had been raided regularly and it stepped in a man-made [[Water|lake]], rendering it useless. Djarin attached a [[grav charge]] to the wrecked walker and blew it up.<ref name="Chapter 4" />

{{Veh-stub}}

==Description==
The AT-ST Raider had two viewports, with [[Color|red]] light shining out of them at night, and a twin [[laser cannon]]. Its knee joint had nonstandard plating, and its legs were painted by the [[Klatooinian raiders]]. The Raider had not been repaired to [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] standards.<ref name="Handbook" />
