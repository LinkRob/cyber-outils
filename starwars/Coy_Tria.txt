'''Coy Tria''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[tea]] [[farmer]] and [[pilot]] from [[Myoca]]. He joined the [[Citizens' Fleet]] during the [[Battle of Exegol]], shortly after the battle, participated in the hunt for the [[First Order]] [[Major]] [[Chetachi Gohl]].<ref name="TLO" />

==The Battle of Exegol==
In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> he joined the [[Citizens' Fleet]] during the [[Battle of Exegol]] in his family's ship, the [[Harvest(freighter)|''Harvest'']] although he didn't get to take a single shot during the battle. Afterwards, he returned to [[Ajan Kloss]] with the [[Resistance]] fleet and stayed to help as a messenger as the Resistance took in many refugee ships.<ref name="TLO" />

==The Hunt for Major Gohl==
On one such ship, he met [[Niila]] who convinced the Resistance command that Major Chetachi Gohl of the First Order was still alive and training children on [[Sardich]]. General [[Finn]] and [[Jannah]] joined Niila and him on the ''Harvest'' to [[Second mission to Sardich|fly to Sardich]] and eliminate Gohl. During their time together, Coy and Niila developed a close bond with Coy developing feelings for the supposed refugee.  When Niila was discovered to be the former First Order officer Lieutenant Shroud who sought revenge for the death of her brother, Coy was able to convince her to break the cycle of violence and to come live with him on Myoca in peace instead.<ref name="TLO" />

==Equipment==
Coy wore a [[respi-vap]] near his waist that helped his weak lungs breathe. While on Ajan Kloss, he wore a [[jumpsuit]].<ref name="TLO" />
