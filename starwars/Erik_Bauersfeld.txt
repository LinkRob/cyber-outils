'''Erik Bauersfeld''' ([[June 28]], [[1920s|1922]]<ref name="SWYT 2-7-2020" /> &ndash; [[April 3]], [[2016]])<ref name="Hollywood Reporter" /> was the voice of [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] and [[Bib Fortuna]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. It only took one hour for Bauersfeld to record Ackbar's lines and thirty minutes for Fortuna's. He also read for the part of [[Yoda]], but lost the role to [[Frank Oz]].<ref name="SFGate" /> He later went on to reprise his role as Ackbar in the [[Star Wars video games|computer game]] [[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']]<ref name="Hollywood Reporter" /> and [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="Hollywood Reporter" />
