The '''Abrion sector''' was a [[sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]],<ref name="USW" /> located at the confluence of several [[hyperspace route]]s.<ref name="Rise" /> It contained the [[Rishi system]],<ref name="USW" /> where a [[Defense of Rishi Station|battle]] took place during the [[Clone Wars]] on the [[Rishi Moon|moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Rishi]],<ref name="Rookies" /> as well as the planet [[Scarif]],<ref name="RO World Locations" /> which was the site of a [[Battle of Scarif|battle]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> The sector was also home to several [[agriworld]]s, including [[Abrion Major]], [[Gibad]], [[Tieos]], and [[Ukio]].<ref name="Rise" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], Abrion Major was represented by [[Esu Rotsino]] in the [[Galactic Senate]], who led the sector in defecting from the [[Galactic Republic]]. In the [[Separatist Senate]], Rotsino represented the entire sector.<ref name="Rise" />
