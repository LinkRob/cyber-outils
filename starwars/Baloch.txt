'''Baloch''' was a [[Rodian]] during the early days of space travel. According to legend, Baloch was the [[captain]] of [[Unidentified Galleon (Baloch)|star-faring galleon]] that participated in a [[Mission to Batuu (Pre-Republic period)|competition]] to map a safe route to the rumored [[planet]] of [[Batuu]]. During the competition, the Galleon was attacked by pirates and Baloch was taken by the pirates for ransom.<ref name="Skiff & Galleons" />
