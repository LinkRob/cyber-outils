The '''Enhanced T2-B Repulsor Tank''' was a variant of the [[T2-B Repulsor Tank]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. The enhanced model was upgraded with performance modifications that were built around existing systems.

==Characteristics==
The Enhanced T2-B Repulsor Tank was a modified variant of the [[T2-B Repulsor Tank]] that was outfitted with upgraded [[power coupling]]s and sub-systems, allowing its [[quad laser cannon]] to fire high powered blasts at a faster rate of fire than average. The performance modifications were built around existing systems, and it had an altered [[hull]] and exterior than the standard model, such as the addition of dark [[color]]ed [[armor]] plates. The [[tank]] utilized [[repulsorlift]]s for propulsion.<ref name="Commander" />

==History==
[[File:SWC T2-B Tank.png|thumb|right|240px|The standard T2-B Repulsor Tank model]]
The Enhanced T2-B Repulsor Tank was deployed by the [[Tatooine rebel cell]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], participating in [[Operations on Tatooine|operations]] on [[Tatooine]], the [[Dandoran Campaign]], the [[Er'Kit Campaign]], a [[campaign on Yavin IV]], and other conflicts shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Commander" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Enhanced T2-B Repulsor Tank debuted in [[canon]] in the [[2014]] mobile [[video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''.<ref name="Commander" />
