The '''Alvadorjia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Andirma sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was located in grid square L-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the [[Homeworld|home]]<ref name="Planetnamia" /> of the [[Alvadorjian]] [[species]].<ref name="Origin" />
