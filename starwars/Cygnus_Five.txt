==Behind the scenes==
Cygnus Five appeared in the [[The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone 3|third issue]] of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone]]''. The comic was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Lucas Marangon]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="DftOZ 3" /> on [[December 18]], 2024.<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3016-088/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III--Dispatches-from-the-Occlusion-Zone-3|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III--Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone #3}}</ref>
