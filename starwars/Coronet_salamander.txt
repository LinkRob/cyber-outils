'''Coronet salamanders''' were [[fly]]-[[Diet|eating]] [[salamander]]s that partially shared their name with [[Coronet City]], a [[city]] on<ref name="CC" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="TravelersGuide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. The [[Corellian]] former [[scrumrat]] [[Qi'ra]] mentally noted the [[Crimson Dawn]] operative [[Corynna]]'s quick reflexes when the latter caught the transceiver linked to the former's [[Slavery|enslavement]] [[tracking chip]] in the [[Atmosphere|air]], comparing the swift movement to a Coronet salamander catching flies.<ref name="CC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coronet salamanders were mentioned in ''[[Crimson Climb]]'', a [[2023]] young-adult novel written by [[E. K. Johnston]].<ref name="CC" />
