'''Argus Tanzer''' was a young, [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[bureaucrat]] on board the [[Alderaan Flotilla]], a group of [[starship]]s carrying the surviving natives of his [[homeworld]] [[Alderaan]]. In the [[Standard year|year]] [[5 ABY]], Tanzer was part of a meeting on board the flotilla [[Alderaanian escort frigate]] ''[[Sunspire]]'' to discuss the future of the flotilla and witnessed the delivery of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]'s remains by the [[New Republic]] to the Alderaanians.

==Biography==
{{Quote|It's pieces of that damnable Death Star. Isn't it?|Argus Tanzer recognises the Death Star wreckage|<ref name="Life Debt" />}}
Argus Tanzer was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] from the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] who survived the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of his [[homeworld]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]<ref name="Life Debt" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]] to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Following the loss of his homeworld, Tanzer at some point joined up with the [[Alderaan Flotilla]], a group of [[starship]]s carrying surviving [[Alderaanian]]s that had been initially established by [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and the [[Rebel pilot]] [[Evaan Verlaine]].<ref name="Life Debt" />

In the year [[5 ABY]],<ref name="date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> Tanzer was serving as a [[bureaucrat]] on the [[flotilla]], which was floating amongst the [[Alderaanian Graveyard|remains]] of Alderaan in the [[Alderaan system]]. He attended a meeting with the [[Regent Administrator]] [[Eglyn Valmor]] on board the Alderaanian [[frigate]] ''[[Sunspire]]'' where they and several other leading members of the flotilla discussed their people's future. Valmor wished to remain in the Alderaan [[Star system|system]], but others like the former [[teacher]] [[Icar Orliss]] and the [[diplomat]] [[Janis Pol]] wished for the surviving Alderaanians to re-settle a new world. When Orliss produced a list of suitable planets they might choose from, Tanzer interjected, arguing that re-settling a new world was not as simple as picking from a list, since they would need to follow the process put in place by the [[New Republic]], although he admitted no one knew what that process was.<ref name="Life Debt" />

The arguing within the meeting continued until it was interrupted by reports of several large vessels approaching the flotilla. Tanzer and the others were then shocked to see a number of New Republic vessels hauling the remains of the Death Star toward the flotilla, which fellow flotilla member [[Teven Gale]] then revealed he had worked with Organa and Verlaine to acquire for the flotilla as a resource. Valmor and Gale then suggested building a new [[space station]] from the superweapon's remains, although Orliss still remained stubborn on resettling elsewhere.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Personality and traits==
Tanzer was a young man in 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], and possessed a natural handsomeness that seemed as though he did not need to cultivate it. While speaking at the meeting he gestured with his hands, thrusting up his [[Limb|finger]] to emphasize a point. He smirked upon realizing the nature of the Death Star wreckage.<ref name="Life Debt" />
