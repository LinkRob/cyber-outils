'''Este Meza''' served as the Senior Events Manager for [[Lucasfilm]]<ref name="One Day Book" /> from [[2015]] to [[2020]],<ref name="LinkedIn" /> where he led the team that planned and executed various in-person ''[[Star Wars]]'' events including [[Celebration Chicago]], [[Celebration Orlando]], and other convention appearances. In his role, he also performed remote control work for [[BB-8]] and [[R2-D2]] in real-life appearances, such as the [[droid]]s meeting children with life-threatening illnesses in hospitals.<ref name="One Day Book" /> Meza ideated the [[Millennium Falcon Experience|''Millennium Falcon'' Experience]], a walk-through of the ship which toured five U.S. cities during the press tour for ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="MFE">{{SW|url=news/7-things-we-learned-inside-the-millennium-falcon-experience|text=7 Things We Learned Inside the ''Millennium Falcon'' Experience}}</ref> Meza left Lucasfilm in early [[2020]],<ref name="LinkedIn" /> shortly after being featured in [[Este Meza: Lucasfilm Events|an episode]] of the [[Disney+]] series [[Wikipedia:One Day at Disney Shorts|One Day at Disney]]<ref name="One Day Short">[[Este Meza: Lucasfilm Events]]</ref> and its accompanying book.<ref name="One Day Book" />
