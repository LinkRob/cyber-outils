The '''Alderaanian Diplomatic Corps''' was a [[diplomat]]ic group from the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. Prior to the ''[[Tantive IV]]''&mdash;a [[CR90 corvette]] used by the [[House of Organa|royal house]] of Alderaan&mdash;being assigned a [[Secret mission to Tatooine|mission]] to the planet [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> the corps assigned the [[protocol droid]] [[U-3PO]] to the [[droid]] pool of the [[corvette]]. U-3PO had secretly been altered by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] agents and acted as a sleeper agent for them onboard the [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Alderaanian Diplomatic Corps was mentioned in "[[The Rise of the Rebellion and the Battle of Yavin]]," a book published as part of the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series by [[De Agostini]] on [[March 9]], [[2021]].<ref name="De Agostini">{{DeAgostini|lang=es|url=libros-comics/enciclopedia-star-wars|text=Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref> The [[Alderaanian Diplomatic Corps/Legends|corps]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned on a card in the [[1995]] ''[[A New Hope Limited]]'' set of the ''[[Star Wars Customizable Card Game]]''.<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=A New Hope Limited|cardname=U-3PO}}</ref>
