The '''Bogano Vault''', also known as the '''Ancient Vault''', the '''Zeffo Vault''', or simply the '''Vault''', was a vault constructed by the [[Zeffonian]] [[species]] on [[Bogano]]. The Vault was secured using ancient Zeffo technology and required both a Zeffo artifact known as an [[astrium]] and strength in [[the Force]] to open.

==History==
===Ancient history===
Built centuries before the [[Clone Wars]], the Vault of Bogano was constructed out of [[karnite]], a mineral-rich stone mined from the planet's surface. The Vault's interior was filled with ancient, fragile offering vessels, left by pilgrims in the distant past. The interior walls of the monolithic structure were decorated with inscriptions etched into the metallic plating which lined the walls. Depictions of [[Miktrull]] and [[astrium]]s could be found in murals in the vault, having survived [[Zeffo Sage|Sage]] [[Kujet]]'s campaign to destroy all depictions of astriums.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

===Republic era===
[[File:Within_the_Vault.png|thumb|left|270px|The central chamber of the Vault]]
[[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] discovered the Vault while studying the [[Zeffonian]]s, who were extinct. The Vault required [[Force-sensitive|someone strong in]] [[the Force]] and equipped with a Zeffo artifact called an astrium to open. Cordova used the Vault to hide a Jedi [[Eno Cordova's holocron|holocron]] containing a list of Force-sensitive children in the event the [[Jedi Order]] needed to be rebuilt.

===Imperial era===
[[File:CereCalBoganoVault.png|thumb|250x250px|Together, former Jedi Cere Junda and Jedi Padawan Cal Kestis went to the Vault with the hopes of rebuilding the Jedi Order.]]
Several [[Standard year|years]] after [[Order 66]], [[Cere Junda]] brought [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] to the Vault, hoping to use the holocron to [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|rebuild]] the Jedi Order.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

Once Cal returned to the planet with an astrium from [[Dathomir]], he placed it in the Vault, giving him a vision of Padawans he was training. The Empire then attacked, and Cal appeared to be an [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitor]] with the younglings being tortured to surrender. Cal awoke and grabbed the holocron only to see the [[Second Sister]] before him. They dueled, with Cal eventually stealing [[Second Sister's lightsaber|her lightsaber]]. This gave him an [[Psychometry|echo]] of her past trauma. Trilla made off with the holocron, leaving behind a detachment of [[stormtrooper]]s to deal with Cal.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
[[File:StarWarsAdventuresBoganoVault.png|left|thumb|250x250px|Princess Leia Organa and aspiring Jedi Luke Skywalker visited the Vault when they went to Bogano.]]

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Bogano Vault was visited by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who were investigating planets that could potentially host a rebel base. The discovery of stormtroopers on the planet alerted the pair to the fact that the Empire discovered the planet first.<ref name="Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=7|story=The Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 1}}</ref>

===New Republic era===
By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="BoglingDate">{{GOCDate}}</ref> [[Bogling]]s had burrowed into the ancient ruins of the Bogano Vault and made a home within the central chamber. This fact was discovered by [[SF-R3]] and [[CAM]] when they visited Bogano to find a Bogling. The pair ran through a cave which led to a hole in the wall of the central chamber. They found the central chamber filled with Boglings.<ref name="Bogling episode">{{GoC|Bogling}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
===Conception and appearances===
The Bogano Vault was created for the [[Wikipedia:Single-player video game|single-player]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]] and published by [[Electronic Arts]] (EA)&mdash;the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars Jedi]]'' multimedia series<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> which was released on [[November 15]], [[2019]].<ref name="YBY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> The location was first revealed in the [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nfcAHfDuNzw "Cal's Mission" trailer] of the game on [[September 26]], 2019<ref name="Cal's Mission trailer">{{EASWYouTube|nfcAHfDuNzw|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order &mdash; "Cal's Mission" Trailer|sw_url=video/cal-s-mission-trailer-star-wars-jedi-fallen-order}}</ref> and was first identified as the "Bogano Vault" in the game itself.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> Concept art was done by artist [[Gabe Yeganyan]].<ref name="Art JFO">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> Like with the rest of the game, the location is heavily influenced by [[Wikipedia:Metroidvania|3D Metroidvania type design]]&mdash;an [[Wikipedia:Action-adventure game|action-adventure]] subgenre defined by large, interconnected maps containing paths that could be unlocked as the player progresses through the game.<ref name="GameSpot">{{GameSpot|url=articles/e3-2019-star-wars-jedi-fallen-order-didnt-begin-as/1100-6467511|text=E3 2019: Star Wars: Jedi Fallen Order Didn't Begin As A Star Wars Game}}</ref>

===Inconsistencies===
[[File:StarWarsAdventuresBoganoVault.png|thumb|340px|The Bogano Vault, as depicted in the "The Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale Part 1" comic story]]
The vault later made an appearance in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 7|seventh]]<ref name="Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 1" /> and [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 8|eighth]] issue<ref name="Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 2">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=8|story=The Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 2}}</ref> of the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] comic series, as part of "The Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale" [[The Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 1|Part 1]] and [[The Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 2|2]] comic stories written by [[Sam Maggs]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]].<ref name="Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 1" /><ref name="Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 2" /> Part 1 of "The Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale" was released on [[July 28]], [[2021]]<ref name="Preview 1">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-adventures-7-exclusive-preview|text=The Skywalker Twins Explore the Galaxy in IDW's ''Star Wars Adventures'' #7 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref> and Part 2 was released on [[August 18]], 2021.<ref name="Preview 2">{{SW|url=news/idw-star-wars-adventures-8-exclusive-preview|text=Blasters Blaze on Bogano in IDW's ''Star Wars Adventures'' #8 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref> In both comic stories, the bottom part of the vault is pictured as a mound, and the vault is placed on a hill. The comic stories also picture the top of the vault as being shorter. 

Inside the vault, two murals of Zeffonians are pictured next to a semicircular mirror.<ref name="Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 1" /><ref name="Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 2" /> These all contradict ''Star Wars: Jedi Fallen Order'', which pictures the bottom part of the vault as a hemisphere and places the vault on the edge of a cliff. The game also pictures the top part of the vault as being longer and the inside as having a mural of Zeffonians next to two semicircular mirrors.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> In the "Bogling" episode, the vault's interior is missing a slot where an astrium could be placed to "perceive the mysteries of the vault," and the vault is not shown as partially submerged in water.<ref name="Bogling episode" /> These all contradict the video game;<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> however, the vault not being partially submerged in water can be attributed to the passage of time, as the game and the episode are set in different times.<ref name="BoglingDate" /><ref name="JFO Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref>
