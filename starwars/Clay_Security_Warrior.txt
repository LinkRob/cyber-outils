The '''Clay Security Warrior''' was a body configuration owned by the [[KX-series security droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[K-A0S]] that she could swap for her standard [[KX-Series Security Droid (K-A0S)|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" /> This frame resembled the large [[stone guardian]] [[droid]]s utilized by the [[Frangawl Cult]] on the [[moon]] [[Zardossa Stix]].<ref name="The Disappeared">{{TCW|The Disappeared, Part II}}</ref>

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Clay Security Warrior was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter K-A0S, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 2.0.0 alongside Season 2 on [[July 30]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Clay Grafted Shields]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing the Super Security Bundle within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
