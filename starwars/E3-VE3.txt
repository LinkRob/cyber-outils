'''E3-VE3''' was a custom-modified [[Droideka]] found on the [[planet]] of [[Koboh]]. The droid was modified to have enhanced offensive capabilities and heightened belligerence algorithms. Instead of serving the [[Bedlam Raiders]], this droid was reprogrammed a second time with new logic and loyalty protocols, which overrode [[Rayvis]]' programming.<ref name="Survivor" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Survivor}}
E3-VE3 was [[Death|destroyed]] by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]]<ref name="Survivor" /> in [[9 BBY]], after Kestis destroyed E3-VE3's B-1 Battle droid guards.<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref>
{{EndGame}}
