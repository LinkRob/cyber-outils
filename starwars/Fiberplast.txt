'''Fiberplast''' was a substance sold in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a member of the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime [[syndicate]] order a shipment of fiberplast from a [[human]] [[merchant]] named [[Hen Puon]] from their [[Hen Puon's general goods|store]] in the [[Pyke market]] of the [[city]] of [[Mirogana]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. He had Puon deliver the shipment to the [[Hot Pot]] [[soup]] kitchen in the [[Market District (Mirogana)|Market District]] of Mirogana and paid Puon in [[Forger (profession)|counterfeit]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Puon was confronted by a [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] named [[Kay Vess]] when she sold something to Puon and was paid in counterfiet credits. Admitting the credits seemed too good to be true Puon told Vess about the fiberplast shipment.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fiberplast was first mentioned in the [[canon]] continuity in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It is mentioned during the "Counterfeit Credits" intel quest.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Fiberplast was originally introduced in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity in the [[1997]] novella "[[Side Trip]]" written by [[Timothy Zahn]] and [[Michael A. Stackpole]].<ref name="Side Trip">"[[Side Trip]]"</ref>
