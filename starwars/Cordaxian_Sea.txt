The '''Cordaxian Sea'''<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> was a [[Ocean|sea]] located on the [[planet]] [[Odona]]. After being stranded on Odona, the [[Kajain'sa'Nikto]] [[Frax]] spent four [[Standard month|months]] alone on the Cordaxian sea.<ref name="Annual 3" /> In addition to the [[water]]s of [[Naboo]], the [[colo claw fish]] could also be found in the Cordaxian sea.<ref name="Complete Visual New" />

==Behind the scenes==
The initial version of the [[2025]] [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]] appendix of [[star system]]s introduced a Cordaxia system as an origin point for the Cordaxian Sea and [[Cordaxian ore]],<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> but the [[October 13]] iteration of the appendix removed the system as erroneous due to the Cordaxian Sea's placement on Odona.<ref name="Substack 2">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref>
