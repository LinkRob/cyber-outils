'''AA-1 VerboBrain''' was a model of [[verbobrain]] manufactured by<ref name="New Edition" /> [[SyntheTech]]<ref name="HC3" /> that was able to store tremendous amounts of information in a [[droid]]'s active [[memory bank]]s<ref name="New Edition" /> and calculate complex scenarios. It was used in high-end [[protocol droid]]s,<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> including [[Cybot Galactica]]'s [[3PO-series protocol droid]].<ref name="New Edition" /> While the [[L-1g general purpose droid]] was most commonly used for basic operational tasks, it was also equipped with an AA-1 VerboBrain.<ref name="Droidography" />
