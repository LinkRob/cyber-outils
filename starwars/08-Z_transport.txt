The '''08-Z transport''' was a model of [[hovertrain]] [[transport]] manufactured by [[Marmoth Factories]]. 08-Zs were used as low-cost transportation for workers to move to and from their job sites. They consisted of two cars, one of which carried the passengers,<ref name="DRVG" /> with seating for up to sixteen individuals,<ref name="KenobiI" /> and the other the train operator and control room. The [[engine]] and [[stabilizer]]s were located in the front of the passenger compartment, with a large [[exhaust vane|grille]] leading air inside to prevent the mechanism from overheating.<ref name="DRVG" />

One 08-Z transport was used to ferry [[tibidon sand whale]] harvesters working on [[Tatooine]]'s [[Tibidon Station]] by [[9 BBY]], the only amenity provided to them by their employers. Among those using the train daily was [[Jedi Master]] in hiding [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], on one of his many temporary jobs.<ref name="KenobiI" />
