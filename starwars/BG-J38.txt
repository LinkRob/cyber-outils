'''BG-J38''' was a [[Insect|bug]]-headed,<ref name="SWCT" /> [[Color|yellow]]-plated<ref name="ROTJ" /> [[J9 worker drone]]<ref name="By the numbers" /> who served in the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba's criminal empire|Jabba Desilijic Tiure's criminal empire]]. Within [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]], the droid was stationed near the entrance to the throne room.<ref name="SWCT" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
BG-J38 first appeared in the [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']],<ref name="ROTJ" /> released on [[May 25]], [[1983]].<ref name="YBY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> The Jabba's Palace scenes including BG-J38 were shot in [[January]] [[1982]] at [[Elstree Studios]] in [[Wikipedia:Borehamwood|Borehamwood]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="The Making Of">''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]''</ref> BG-J38 was created as a puppet with limited articulation. The character was identified in [[canon]] in [[2020]] by the [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]] app.<ref name="SWCT" />
