'''Doni's High Quality Ship Parts and Installments''' was a [[starship]] store operated by [[Doni Laro]] in the [[City|town]] of [[Jaunta's Hope]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The store consisted of a single building next to the town's landing pads. The building was open along a curved corner of the building that left to a counter in front of a door leading further into the shop. Laro conducted business from a counter under an awning in front of the curved section of the building. A sign written in [[Aurebesh]] which read "Doni's High Quality Ship Parts and Installments" was a fixed to the awning above Laro's counter.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], a [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] named [[Kay Vess]] utilized the shop's services to upgrade the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Doni's High Quality Ship Parts and Installments appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was one of several shops that the player could use to upgrade their starship.<ref name="Outlaws" />
