The '''B2 Super Battle Droids Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ35''') is an expansion pack for the miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]''. It is an updated release of the [[2020]] [[B2 Super Battle Droids Unit Expansion|expansion of the same name]], published on [[May 23]], [[2025]] as part of [[Atomic Mass Games]]' overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
''The B2 Super Battle Droids are the powerful shock troops of the Separatist army. Making their debut in Star Wars™: Episode II - Attack of the Clones, these iconic battle droids bring a formidable force to the tabletop in Star Wars™: Legion.''

''SWQ35 Unit Expansion provides a corps unit for Separatist army players, with the B2 Super Battle Droids bringing mid range firepower support and heavy armor. This unit pack will be included in many army lists and is supported by many character expansions and unit expansions to build an engaging and thematic Separatist army.''

==Contents==
*9 Miniatures
*1 Unit Card
*5 Upgrade Cards
