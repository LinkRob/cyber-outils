'''Fazlee''' was a [[Rodian]] [[gambler]] that resided on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During which they played [[Kessel Sabacc]] against a [[Quarren]] named [[Aquesh]] at [[The Alcazar]] [[cantina]] in [[Myrra]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of Akiva. Fazlee had [[Color|orange]] skin, black [[eye]]s and spoke [[Huttese]]. While playing [[Sabacc]] at The Alcazar they wore a dark [[jacket]] over a dark [[clothing|shirt]] and pants along with blue [[scarf]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> a [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] named [[Kay Vess]] played [[Kessel Sabacc]] against Fazlee and Aquesh.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fazlee appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, they were at one of several sabacc tables that the player could choose to play against earn [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
