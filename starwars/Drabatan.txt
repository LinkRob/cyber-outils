'''Drabatans''', {{Pronounced|Drabatan|file=Drabatan-Pronunciation-RogueOneNovelization.flac}} also known as '''Drabatas''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Pipada]]. Their homeworld was harvested for materials by the [[Galactic Empire]], displacing much of the native population, including the Drabatans. [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek|Pao]], a [[commando]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], was a Drabatan. During the [[Cold War]], one [[Unidentified Drabatan|Drabatan]] served as a [[Technician|mechanic]] on staff at the [[Resistance base]] on the planet [[D'Qar]].

The Drabatans primarily spoke a language called [[Drabatese]]. However, as this language was unusually loud, the Drabatans also used a second form of speech that was based off of passing air through a [[vocal pouch]].

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Pao-ROUSE.png|thumb|250px|left|Drabatans had large mouths that could be opened extremely wide.]]
Drabatans,<ref name="Dawn" /> also known as Drabatas,<ref name="Pao" /> were [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[humanoid]]s<ref name="Dawn" /> with tough<ref name="Sticker">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Ultimate Sticker Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[leather]]y [[color|gray]],<ref name="Rogue One Novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> pink,<ref name="Commander" /> green,<ref name="Star Wars 28">[[Star Wars (2015) 28|''Star Wars'' (2015) 28]]</ref> or green-gray skin. They had small dark [[eye]]s with large [[mouth]]s and stood slightly shorter than average [[human]]s. These amphibians excelled at swimming in [[water|freshwater]] but strongly disliked [[salt]] water. Being [[Diet|omnivorous]], Drabatans ate [[insect]]s, [[fish]], and [[algae]].<ref name="Dawn" />

To [[reproduction|reproduce]], Drabatans laid [[egg]]s in silty [[Lake (disambiguation)|pond]] soil where they hatched into [[tadpole]]s. Over the course of a [[standard year]], the tadpoles grew and obtained bipedal features before becoming child-size Drabatans. They also developed large [[lung]]s and diaphrams that helped release powerful vocals that could be used for [[Music|sing]]ing or yelling.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Society and culture==
[[File:Alderaan-2025ToppsSWHyperspace.png|thumb|right|200px|Drabatan politics aligned with that of Alderaan (pictured) and Chandrila.]]
Drabatans were passionate by nature, being dedicated to the performing [[Art Group|arts]], especially [[music]]. Many individuals claimed that Drabatan [[song]]s required no [[Translate|translation]] to appreciate the ethereal, melancholy tones. Some even considered seeing a performance by the [[Pipada Symphony Chamber Chorus]] to be life changing. Song was incorporated into most areas of Drabatan lives, from entertainment to [[education]]. Drabatan families had unique songs that detailed their lineage and history, these songs were often performed at family gatherings by the family's best [[musician|vocalist]] and having the song rearranged for the occasion. [[School (disambiguation)|School]] curriculums taught everything through the form of song, students would use jingles as mnemonic devices to help with memorization.<ref name="Dawn" />

Drabatan culture also heavily valued free artistic expression and heavily opposed censorship. It was said that the only thing a Drabatan loved more than a song; was a cause. Drabatans were often found leading chants at protests across the galaxy. Their [[Politics|political]] views were aligned with [[Alderaan]] and [[Chandrila]], which created tension while under [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] rule.<ref name="Dawn" />
