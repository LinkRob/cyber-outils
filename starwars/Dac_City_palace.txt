During the [[Imperial Era|rule]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[King of Mon Cala]] [[Lee-Char]] maintained a '''palace''' in [[Dac City]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] [[Mon Cala]]. The palace was a target by the [[Inquisitorius]] during the [[Battle of Dac City]], and, along with Dac City and the rest of the surface [[City|cities]], was destroyed by [[Unidentified Mon Cala creature|enormous sea creatures]].<ref name="Dark Lord 14">[[Darth Vader (2017) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 14]]</ref>
