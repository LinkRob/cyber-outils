As the flashbacks in [[The High Republic — The Blade 1|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 1]] take place twenty-five [[Standard year|years]] before the present day events, they must take place in [[407 BBY]].</ref> they [[Reproduction|had]] a Kage [[Barash Silvain's family|daughter]]&mdash;[[Barash Silvain]]&mdash;and at least two [[Barash Silvain's siblings|other children]].<ref name="The Blade 3" /> Also by that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Youngling|infant]] Silvain's [[Force-sensitive|Force-sensitivity]] was discovered and she<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was taken by the [[Jedi Order]] to be [[Jedi training|trained]] as a [[Jedi]] at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="The Blade 3" /> the [[Core Worlds]] planet<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="The Blade 3" />

In [[397 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">The present day events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic — The Blade]]'' take place between the [[wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran]] and [[Porter Engle]] [[Siege of Bardotta|earning]] his title the "Blade of [[Bardotta]]," both of which are dated to [[382 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As the flashbacks in [[The High Republic — The Blade 3|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 3]] take place fifteen [[Standard year|years]] before the present day events, they must take place in [[397 BBY]].</ref> Silvain told her close friend&mdash;the [[Ikkrukkian]] Jedi [[Porter Engle]]&mdash;that because she had been found by the Order at an older age than usual, and because of her<ref name="The Blade 3" /> eidetic memory,<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> she had continued to remember her [[parent]]s and family. Silvain noted she recalled how it felt to be with her parents, and although she did not regret being brought to the [[Jedi temple|temple]], she remembered what she had lost. She then told Engle that she had found family again with him, and asked if he would be her brother, which he accepted.<ref name="The Blade 3" />
