The '''Coastal Defense Division''' was a [[division]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]]. The [[trooper]] division consisted of [[coastal defender stormtrooper]]s, who were responsible for the defense of littoral zones, the coasts, and the beaches of [[planet]]s with large maritime zones.<ref name="Imperial Troops" /> Unlike other specialized units of the [[Imperial Army]]'s Stormtrooper Corps, the Coastal Defense Division's shoretroopers did not require additional training as any [[trooper]] could join the unit due to its temporary and cyclical function. The only prerequisite for stormtroopers to join the division was to have the rank of [[sergeant]], and if conflict arose they were required to lead [[squad]]s of ordinary stormtroopers.<ref name="Rogue One Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
