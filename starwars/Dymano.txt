'''Dymano''' was a [[planet]] on which a [[circus]] was held. The planet was habitable for [[human]]s.<ref name="BattleOfDarkSide" /> At some point between [[19 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="BotDSDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the start of the [[Imperial Era]] to [[19 BBY]] and the [[death]] of [[Maul]] to [[2 BBY]]. As such, "[[Battle of the Dark Side]]" must take place at some point between those years.</ref> the former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]]&mdash;disguised as the Sith Lord [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]&mdash;took a squad of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s to the planet. On Dynamo, the squad stole string lights and [[Color|red]] lipstick from the circus while Maul held a [[clown]] with [[pie]] on its face in the air using [[the Force]]. One of the stormtroopers, who desired to visit the planet [[Felucia]], commented in disappointment that Dymano was not Felucia.<ref name="BattleOfDarkSide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dymano appeared in "[[Battle of the Dark Side]]," a comic story written by [[Martin Fisher]] and illustrated by [[Bob Molesworth]], which was published in [[LEGO Star Wars 130|issue 130]] of the magazine [[LEGO Star Wars (magazine)|''LEGO Star Wars'']].<ref name="BattleOfDarkSide" /> The issue was published in the [[Wikipedia:United Kingdom|United Kingdom]] by [[Immediate Media]] on [[December 17]], [[2025]].<ref name="jedinews">{{WebCite|url=https://www.jedinews.com/lego/articles/immediate-medias-lego-star-wars-magazine-130-out-now-in-the-uk/|text=Lego Star Wars Magazine 130 Out Now in the UK|archivedate=20251227164953}}</ref> In the [[Wikipedia:Polish language|Polish]] translation of the comic, the planet is named "Damano".<ref name="BattleOfDarkSide" />
