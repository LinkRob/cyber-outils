A '''Drone barge''' was an automated [[starship]] used for [[hyperspace]] travel. During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], the [[Dug]] criminal [[Gronson Takkaro]] smuggled himself on a drone barge to the planet [[Lothal]]; a journey that took nine [[Standard day|days]]. His escape was recorded on several datapads that were used by the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], who was sent by the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] to collect a bounty on Takkaro.<ref name="Ezra's Gamble">''[[Ezra's Gamble]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A Drone barge first appeared in ''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]'', a [[1978]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel written by [[Brian Daley]] as the first entry in the [[Star Wars: The Han Solo Adventures|''The Han Solo Adventures'']] trilogy.<ref name="Han Solo at Stars' End">''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]''</ref> They became [[canon]] after one was mentioned in ''[[Ezra's Gamble]]'', a junior novel written by [[Ryder Windham]]<ref name="Ezra's Gamble" /> that was released on [[August]] [[August 5|5]], [[2014]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-rebels-ezras-gamble|text=Star Wars Rebels: Ezra's Gamble}}</ref>
