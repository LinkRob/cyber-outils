'''G21''' was a [[B2-series super battle droid]] manufactured by [[Baktoid Combat Automata]] and used in the [[Separatist Droid Army]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. At some point, the [[droid]] delivered a report to a superior that a [[planet]] and its population were under control. Like all B2 super [[battle droid]]s, G21 was armed with built in dual [[laser cannon]]s<ref name="USW" /> and stood 1.91 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="SWE" />

==Behind the scenes==
G21 first appeared in the [[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (novelization)|novelization]] of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], which was written by [[Matthew Stover]] and released in [[2005]]. In the novel, G21 greeted Separatist [[General]] [[Grievous]] on the [[planet]] [[Utapau]] and informed him that the planet was secure.<ref name="Novel">[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (novelization)|''Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith'' novelization]]</ref> The droid was then identified by ''[[The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book released in [[2009]].<ref name="CSWE">''[[The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia]]''</ref> G21's name and its single quote were confirmed to be [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which was released in [[2015]]. As G21 did not appear in the film itself,<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> and the canon status of the film novelizations is currently unclear following the [[2014]] establishment of all Expanded Universe material as Legends,<ref name="EUNewPage">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> this article makes no assumption about the canonicity of the context for G21's quote.
