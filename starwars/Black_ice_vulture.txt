The '''black ice vulture'''<ref name="Databank" /> or simply the '''ice vulture'''<ref name="Outpost AD">{{TBB|The Outpost}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> was a type of [[bird]] native to the icy [[planet]] [[Barton IV]].<ref name="The Outpost" />

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Ice_vulture.jpg|left|thumb|250px|A black ice vulture in flight during a clear day on Barton IV]]
Black ice vultures were large carnivorous [[bird]]s that were at home in the icy conditions of [[Barton IV]].<ref name="The Outpost" /> The vultures were one of the few living things who could survive the cold and windy temperatures of the [[planet]] for an long periods of time.<ref name="SWECG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

Their [[Color|blue]] plumage helped blend in with the landscape.<ref name="Databank" /> With massive wingspans, ice vultures could swoop and sail through the air with ease, screeching. Black ice vultures would appear solitary or, if prey was present, sometimes several would appear together.<ref name="The Outpost" /> The vultures would feast on any creatures unable to withstand the freezing conditions, or who were just unlucky.<ref name="SWECG" /> The birds were equipped with sharp beaks and razor-sharp talons.<ref name="Databank" />

==History==
[[Clone Commander]] [[Mayday]] warned [[Crosshair]] about the vultures' danger when he arrived on Barton IV. At the same time, Mayday also admired the vultures' ability to survive there.<ref name="The Outpost" />
