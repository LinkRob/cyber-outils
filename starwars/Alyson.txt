'''Alyson''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]]. Around [[4 BBY]] she, the [[Lasat]] [[Garazeb Orrelios]], [[Lexim]], [[Wad]], and [[Greve (hunter)|Greve]] were almost attacked by [[gundark]]s at [[Lexim's hunting ground]].<ref name="Becoming Hunted" />

== History ==
Around [[4 BBY]], [[Greve (hunter)|Greve]] brought his friends Alyson and [[Wad]] to [[Lexim's hunting ground]] on a [[Unidentified planet (Lexim)|forest planet]] in the [[Lothal sector]] to hunt [[Gundark|gundarks]]. However, after arriving [[Lexim]] found he could not control the animals and soon the gundarks overran the facility. Lexim and the three patrons were forced into hiding within the facility while Lexim sent off a [[distress signal]]. [[Garazeb Orrelios]], aboard the shuttle ''[[Phantom]]'' responded to the distress signal. However, he found the door locked and busted it down. Having unintentionally opened the door for the gundarks, the group soon came under attack by the creatures. Orrelios ordered the group deeper into the facility, finding shelter in the galley.<ref name="Becoming Hunted" />

There Orrelios ordered the group to find weapons to arm themselves with, but instead they only found a broken rifle and a pair of spoons.  Greve revealed that he had only brought Wad there to combat his fear of animals, which had not been helped by what they had been through. Orrelios confronted Lexim about not having prepared the facility for the gundark threat, but they were interrupted by the gundarks which broke through the floor. The group had to defend themselves again, with Alyson battling the gundarks with the broken rifle and was quickly overwhelmed. Lexim, the last one of them standing, took Orrelios' [[bo-rifle]] and took out the gundarks, freeing the group.<ref name="Becoming Hunted" />

The group then decided to flee using the tunnel to the landing pad where Orrelios had left the ''Phantom''. Greve, Alyson, and Wad all made it to the shuttle safely. They rendezvoused with the ''[[Ghost]]'' where [[Hera Syndulla]] suggested to Greve he take up other hobbies than hunting.<ref name="Becoming Hunted" />

== Personality and traits ==
Alyson was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] with brown eyes, hair and a light skin complexion. She wore a vest, brown pants and shirt and long brown gloves.<ref name="Becoming Hunted" />
