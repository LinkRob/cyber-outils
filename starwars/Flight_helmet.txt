'''Flight helmets''', also known as '''starfighter helmets''', were part of the [[flight suit]] worn by [[pilot]]s. There were many different [[helmet]] styles throughout the various factions that fielded pilots, but they all had several design elements in common, in particular a visor protecting the [[eye]]s.

Flight helmets could also be repurposed for [[Jedi training]] as Luke’s helmet had [[blast shield]]s<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> and used a [[KSE-H44 A-wing pilot helmet]] while training his [[Skywalker family|sister]] on [[Ajan Kloss]].<ref name="Rise of Skywalker Expanded">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

==The Republic era==
[[File:WarthogFlightHelmet-TVE.jpg|thumb|left|150px|Clone pilot [[Warthog]]'s Phase I helmet]]
Before the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] had no central military. Various [[planet]]s had their own forces; however, and some of those, such as [[Naboo]], had [[Starfighter Corps|starfighter corps]]. [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]] [[pilot]]s had [[Color|brown]], [[leather]]-like [[helmet]]s with visors.<ref name="The Phantom Menace">{{Film|I}}</ref> Early in the Clone Wars, [[Clone trooper pilot|clone pilots]] wore white helmets similar to [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I]] [[clone trooper]] helmets, but with air tubes.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence">{{TCW|Shadow of Malevolence|Shadow of ''Malevolence''}}</ref> By the [[time]] [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]] [[Escape from Tipoca City|escaped]] from [[Tipoca City]], a new model of helmet for clone pilots had been introduced, and this helmet left some of their face exposed.<ref name="Fugitive">{{TCW|Fugitive}}</ref> This type of helmet was used by [[starfighter]] pilots like [[Clone Commander]] [[Davijaan]] "Odd Ball" during the [[Battle of Coruscant]] and [[Jag]] during [[Order 66]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>

==The Rebellion and Resistance==
[[File:LukeSkywalkerHelmet-MF43.png|thumb|right|200px|[[Luke Skywalker]]'s K-22995 light flight helmet]]
[[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] pilots were not allowed to remove their helmets during flight, something that recruits were taught at the [[Imperial Academy]].<ref name="Double" /> [[Rebel pilot|Pilots]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] often wore the [[Rebel Alliance starbird|starbird]] symbol on their helmets,<ref name="A New Hope" /> which often were the [[K-22995 light flight helmet]].<ref name="RO Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> There were several different designs of Rebel flight helmet. [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] and [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing]] pilots, during the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] and [[Galactic Civil War]], usually wore a rounded helmet with a chinstrap.<ref name="A New Hope" /> [[A-wing starfighter|A-wing]] and [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing]] pilots, however, usually wore a different design with cheek pieces and no chinstrap.<ref name="Return of the Jedi">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

By the time of the [[Cold War]] thirty years later, the [[Resistance]]'s X-wing pilots wore a helmet design that was an updated version of the type from the Galactic Civil War.<ref name="The Force Awakens">{{Film|VII}}</ref> In all cases, modified helmets were available for those whose [[species]] prevented them from fitting into [[human]]-standard helmets. [[Ryloth]]ian pilot [[Yendor Brethen|Yendor]] had a custom helmet with holes for his [[lekku]],<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> and fellow [[Twi'lek]] [[Hera Syndulla]] wore a pilot's cap and custom visor.<ref name="Wings of the Master">{{Rebels|Wings of the Master}}</ref>

==The Empire and First Order==
[[File:Oxixo_ANH.png|thumb|left|190px|TIE fighter pilot [[Oxixo]]]]
[[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilots]] wore black helmets similar in design to those of [[stormtrooper]]s. They had breathing tubes that connected to a [[control box]] worn on the [[Armor|chestplate]] of an [[Environment suit|atmospheric suit]], as [[TIE series|TIE fighters]] typically did not have fully pressurized [[cockpit]]s. Some helmets had gray stripes.<ref name="A New Hope" /> Thirty years later, the TIE pilots of the [[First Order]]'s [[First Order starfighter corps|starfighter corps]], in keeping with the mimicking of Imperial aesthetics, wore similar-looking black flight helmets, some with red stripes.<ref name="The Force Awakens" />

[[Z'Vk'Thkrkza|Aunt Z]] had a shelf of pilot helmets from across several decades in her [[Aunt Z's Tavern|tavern]], including [[Mandalorian]], clone, Imperial, and rebel pilot.<ref name="Bucket's">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-high-tower-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 9 Fun Facts from "The High Tower" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2018-10-29|quote=Aunt Z has a shelf of pilot helmets from across the decades, including Mandalorian, Imperial, clone, and rebel pilot.}}</ref>
