[[File:EndorShieldGeneratorBunker-TCSBRBD2.png|thumb|right|250px|The Forest [[Moon]] of [[Endor]]]]
A '''forest''' was a large tract of land area covered with [[tree]]s (in which case it was also called '''woodland''' or '''timberland'''), [[Fungus|fungi]], or [[coral]]s.<ref name="EV">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> A humid, overgrown forest was referred to as a [[jungle]].<ref name="Felucia">{{Databank|felucia|Felucia}}</ref>

The [[Wookiee]] [[Homeworld|home]] [[planet]] of [[Kashyyyk]] was covered with dense forests of [[wroshyr tree]]s.<ref name="sith">{{Film|III}}</ref>

The [[Zoess]] was a large forest on the planet [[Radhii]].<ref name="Insider 169">{{InsiderCite|169|Turning Point (Jason Hough)}}</ref>

The [[Galactic Empire]] razed the forests of [[Lothal]], replacing them with [[Mining|mines]] and machines.<ref name="secret">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref>

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Forests first Appeared as a whole in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi]] in [[1983]] as the forest moon of [[Endor]]. The scenes showing the forest of the moon where shot in [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redwood_National_and_State_Parks Redwood National Park] forests in [[California]].
