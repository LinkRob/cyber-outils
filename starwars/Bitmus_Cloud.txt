{{Quote|The San Tekkas had always been a bit hardscrabble, but they suddenly ran into a bit of luck, mapping new routes through places like the Relgim Run and the Bitmus Cloud, high-value routes.|Chancey Yarrow|<ref name="Out of the Shadows">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref>}}
The '''Bitmus Cloud''' was a [[nebula]]<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> located in the [[Wazta sector]] of<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> and [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western Reaches" /> It was situated in grid square J-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. The Bitmus Cloud lay between<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Rex Strata]] and the nebula known as [[Nefitifi]]<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> on the [[Koda Spur]] [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="Koda Spur" />

After the [[Great Hyperspace Rush]]<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> of [[383 BBY]],<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> the [[San Tekka clan]] of [[hyperspace]] [[prospector]]s [[map]]ped valuable new hyperspace routes through the Bitmus Cloud using the abilities of one its members&mdash;the [[Youngling|child]] [[Mari San Tekka]].<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the event of ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> as the [[scientist]] [[Chancey Yarrow]] explained San Tekka's abilities to the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] [[Nan Hague|Nan]], she mentioned San Tekka's mapping of routes through the Bitmus Cloud.<ref name="Out of the Shadows" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bitmus Cloud was first mentioned in the [[2021]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
