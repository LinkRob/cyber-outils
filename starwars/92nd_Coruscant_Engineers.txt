The '''92nd Coruscant Engineers''' were a military unit. One of the unit's [[squad]]s accidentally received a shipment of [[LJ-50 concussion rifle]]s. The squad's [[Unidentified 92nd Coruscant Engineers armorer|armorer]] saw the potential for the [[blaster rifle]]s to be effective at breaching walls and scattering enemy [[trooper]]s and stripped them down into [[blaster carbine]]s.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

The weapons were undeniably effective and impressed the squad's [[Unidentified 92nd Coruscant Engineers officer|superior officer]]. Word then reached [[Arakyd Industries]], who began manufacturing the carbines as [[LJ-40 concussion carbine]]s alongside the LJ-50s.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 92nd Coruscant Engineers were first mentioned in the [[2018]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] sourcebook [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational]].''<ref name="Fully Operational" />
