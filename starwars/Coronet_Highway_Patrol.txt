The '''Coronet Highway Patrol''' was an [[Imperial Military]] [[law enforcement agency]] in [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]], an area considered important enough by the [[Galactic Empire]] for law enforcement. It consisted of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[patrol trooper]]s that utilized [[C-PH patrol speeder bike]]s.<ref name="TOG" />

In [[13 BBY]], a patrol trooper of the Coronet Highway Patrol was involved in a police chase with [[Han Solo|Han]] as he and [[Qi'ra]] escaped from the [[White Worms]] and later crashed.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Coronet Highway Patrol was named and based on the 1977-1983 TV series ''CHiPs'', which featured members of the real-life California Highway Patrol.<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
