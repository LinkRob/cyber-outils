The '''Fabrin system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Circarpous sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was located in grid square O-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Fabrin]].<ref name="Origin" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Fabrin system originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, and was recanonized when it was included in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
