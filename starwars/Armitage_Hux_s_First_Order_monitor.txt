The [[human]] '''monitor''' of [[First Order]] [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] was on the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]'' during the [[Battle of D'Qar]]. They were the one who intercepted [[Poe Dameron]]'s message as the [[pilot]] contacted Hux from ''[[Black One]]''. The [[Rank insignia of the First Order|cuff-title]] on their left sleeve said "[[Ro]],"<ref name="TLJ" /> designating them as a [[sergeant]].<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
