The '''Carnage E-Web''' was an [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]] owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Prototype E-Web|standard E-Web]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Carnage E-Web was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Sentinel's Prototype E-Web, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Carnage Armor]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching Sentinel Hunter Fame level 22.<ref name="Hunters" />
