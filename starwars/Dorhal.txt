'''Dorhal''' was a [[gender|male]] individual that worked with a smuggler named [[Jet Kordo]] when they organized a heist against the [[Hutt Clan|Hutts]]. 

==Biography==
Kordo and their [[droid]] companion, [[H1-0N]], vetted Dorhal before recruiting him to participate in the heist. However he later met with members of the [[Hutt Clan]] on [[Nar Shaddaa]] where he made a deal to sell out Kordo and their crew to the hutts. The day of the heist they sent a message to the Hutts to confirm the deal and review the location of the crew's [[overgrown vault]] on Akiva.<ref name="Outlaws" />

Following the heist, Kordo discovered Dorhal's betrayal which led to a confrontation between him and Kordo in [[Jet Kordo's vault]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. The confrontation resulted in Dorhal's death and H1-0N discovering Kordo standing over his body. The resulting fallout of Dorhal's betrayal and death lead to infighting between the crew and the eventual destruction of H1-0N.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dorhal's appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game they could be heard in the collectible "Jet Kordo's Legacy part 3" audio journal entry.<ref name="Outlaws" />
