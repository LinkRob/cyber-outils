'''Anta''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[merchant]] who sold items in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. The [[First Order]] prevented Anta from importing anything she could sell, and as a result her business fell. Anta spoke with fellow resident [[Bathari]], worrying about failing to make [[Oga Garra]]'s quota.<ref name="Datapad" />
