How wonderful it feels to be returned to ever-dramatic Geonosis, harsh but ever welcoming, scoured by the rays of our crimson sun and emboldened by them, blessed to have this corner of space to ourselves so that the Geonosian society has been able take charge of its own evolution and fulfill its destiny.|translated=1|Poggle the Lesser|<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>}}
The '''Creator''' was a [[Deity|being]] spoken of by the [[Geonosian]]s<ref name="Catalyst" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> When [[Archduke]] [[Poggle the Lesser]] returned to Geonosis at some point after the [[Vallt Rescue Operation|rescue of Galen Erso]]<ref name="Catalyst" /> in [[21 BBY]]<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[wedding of Lyra and Galen Erso]] to [[25 BBY]], and ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]'' states that the [[Erso family]] moved to [[Vallt]] three [[Standard year|years]] later, placing that event in [[22 BBY]]. ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'' establishes that the [[Vallt Rescue Operation|rescue]] of the Ersos occurred after they spent one year on Vallt, placing the rescue in [[21 BBY]].</ref> and spoke at the [[Eve of Meckgin Festival]] in the [[Petranaki Arena]], he proclaimed in [[Geonosian hive-mind|Geonosian]] that he had been blessed by the Creator to have returned to Geonosis in [[time]] for the [[Meckgin]] holiday. He also proclaimed that the [[Geonosian hive]]s were blessed to have their area of [[the galaxy]] to themselves, stating it ensured they were free to chart their own destiny and evolution.<ref name="Catalyst" />
