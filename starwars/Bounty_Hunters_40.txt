'''''Bounty Hunters'' 40''' is the fortieth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Davide Tinto]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 22]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE SHOCKING BETRAYAL! &ndash; A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! The Galaxy's deadliest BOUNTY HUNTERS must stop a corrupted VALANCE! Even if it means his death!!! ENEMIES become desperate ALLIES! And who is the wolf in the fold in the BOUNTY HUNTERS' MIDST?''
