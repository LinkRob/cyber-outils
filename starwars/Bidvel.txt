'''Bidvel''' was a [[Xot]] who operated a [[Bidvel's scrap pile|scrap pile]] on a [[Unidentified planetoid (scrap pile)|remote planetoid]] with the help of [[Davil]].<ref name="EtSP" />

==Biography==
In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{Twitter|tronprogram2|status/751843791565381632|[[Martin Fisher]]|quote=@LelalMekha it takes place between The Call and Homecoming|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/wg5Mq}} places "Escaping the Scrap Pile" between "[[The Call]]" and "[[Homecoming]]." Both of these episodes are dated to [[3 BBY]] in ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Bidvel operated on a [[Unidentified planetoid (scrap pile)|remote planetoid]], taking in destroyed [[droid]]s and scrapping them for parts. At some point, [[C1-10P]], also known as Chopper, ended up stranded in the [[Bidvel's scrap pile|scrap pile]] after a fight just happened involving [[Hera Syndulla]], [[Sabine Wren]], and [[Garazeb Orrelios]], who were on the hunt to find him. When Chopper woke up, Bidvel welcomed the droid to the last day of his life. Chopper tried to shock Bidvel, but was unable to do it as he was kicked to the ground by the [[Xot]]. Bidvel remarked at how he disassembled his shock pods before he awoke, and asked another fellow employee to take Chopper to the cataloging.

Restrained on a table, Chopper tried to bargain [[Davil]] into getting him out of the situation by telling him how he and his [[Spectres|friends]] steal [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer|ships]], but Davil laughed at his sense of humor and continued cataloging. Davil, to his dismay, realized that Chopper might have been telling the truth, as he told Bidvel about how he has locations to [[Chopper Base]] as well as other useful stuff the Empire would appreciate. As Bidvel was giving Davil his gratitude, Chopper used his rocket to bash the two into the wall and escape, which Bidvel shouted at Davil to capture the droid. Davil then caught up with Chopper, and tried to grab the droid, but Chopper hit a side in the junkpile, causing it to collapse on top of Davil. Chopper then used the activation codes to awake all the other droids, but was stunned by Bidvel in revenge for hurting Davil.

After Bidvel extracted all the information and recovered Davil, he asked his friend to start to incinerate Chopper in one of the scrap piles incinerators. But before Davil could incinerate the [[C1-series astromech droid]], numerous [[Monster droid|junk droids]] started to awaken and tried to stop Bidvel and Davil. Bidvel was then held gunpoint by a [[Unidentified B2-series super battle droid|B2-series battle droid]], asking him to surrender, but Bidvel yelled at Davil to shoot every droid attacking him. Davil managed to shoot a droid, but was stunned by the B2-series battle droid. Bidvel then used the opportunity to activate the crane and send Chopper falling, but was saved when the doors shut after a [[Droideka]] closed the doors.

Bidvel was then succumbed by the droids, as Syndulla entered his scrap pile. Chopper told Syndulla how Bidvel had stolen some of the information he had stored in him, and was then taken away to recover the info from him.<ref name="EtSP" />

==Personality and traits==
Bidvil was a male [[Xot]] with gray-blue skin and yellow markings. He had pink eyes. He wore a yellow jacket and brown pants. He often carried a pad around from which he could control machinery around the scar pile. He disliked droids, enjoying dismantling them and physically assaulted them. He also enjoyed inflicting pain on those droids that crossed him. He did respect hard workers like Davil.<ref name="EtSP" />
