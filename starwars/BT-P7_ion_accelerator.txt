A '''BT-P7 ion accelerator''' was a [[particle accelerator]] that could be used to increase the range of ion blasts fired from an [[ion blaster]]. The accelerator utilized a [[laser actuator]] that fired through [[Quad-helix prismatic crystal]]s to achieve its increase range.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Jawa]] [[Entip]] had come into possession of a BT-P7 ion accelerator and offered it as a trade item while traveling in a [[sandcrawler]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] traded a [[fusion engine core]] and two thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to Entip in exchange for the ion accelerator which she later used it to the modify her [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]]'s ion module.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A BT-P7 ion accelerator appeared as an upgrade item in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The player must obtain the trigger in order upgrade their blaser's surge ion module to level four.<ref name="Outlaws" />
