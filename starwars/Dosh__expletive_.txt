'''Dosh''' was a vulgar [[List of phrases and slang|expletive]]. The derived word '''doshing''' (or '''doshin'''') was also used as a derogatory modifier, as in "No doshin' way!" or "Take your doshin' hands off." [[Dec Hansen]] and [[Mattis Banz]] were both known for using those terms frequently.<ref name="Join the Resistance">''[[Join the Resistance]]''</ref>
