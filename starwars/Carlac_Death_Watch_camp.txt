The '''Carlac Death Watch camp'''<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/episode-guide-a-friend-in-need|text="A Friend in Need" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> was erected by the [[Mandalorian]] splinter group [[Death Watch]] on the [[planet]] [[Carlac]].<ref name="Carlac">{{Databank|carlac|Carlac}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], Death Watch leader [[Pre Vizsla]] met with [[Lux Bonteri]], who came to Carlac with the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], to discuss Bonteri's plan for revenge on [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]], who had murdered Bonteri's mother [[Mina Bonteri|Mina]]. However, after witnessing the atrocities committed by the Death Watch against the native [[Ming Po]], Tano revealed herself and fought Vizsla, eventually escaping with Bonteri.<ref name="A Friend in Need" />
