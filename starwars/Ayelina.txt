The '''Ayelina''' were a faction that resided in the [[Malaga Cluster]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Jedi]] [[Mission to the Malaga Cluster|negotiated]] a trade agreement between the Ayelina and the [[Ludmere]], another group which lived in the Cluster, averting a diplomatic crisis.<ref name="First Duty">{{InsiderCite|issue1=201|issue2=202|Starlight: First Duty}}</ref>
