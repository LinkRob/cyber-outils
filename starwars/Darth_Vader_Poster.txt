"'''Darth Vader Poster'''" was a fold-out poster article in [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 11|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 11]]. It featured an in-depth montage of the history, weapons, and vehicles of [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] as well as a large "chase-card" artwork by artist [[Walter Simonson]].
