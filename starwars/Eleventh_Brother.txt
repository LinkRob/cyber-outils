The '''Eleventh Brother''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Gender|male]] individual who served the [[Galactic Empire]] as [[Brother (Inquisitorius)|part]] of its [[Jedi hunter|Jedi-hunting]] [[Inquisitorius]]. Once a [[Jedi|member]] of the [[Jedi Order]], which [[Order 66|fell]] in [[19 BBY]], he became the Eleventh Brother shortly after and was present at the [[Inquisitor]]'s [[Fortress|base]] [[Fortress Inquisitorius]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Nur]] where he met his Master, the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].

Between [[17 BBY]] and around [[13 BBY]], the Eleventh Brother was sent to the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Janix]] after the Inquisitor known as the [[Marrok|First Brother]] confirmed the survival of the former Sith [[Maul]]. After the Eleventh Brother arrived on Janix, he worked with the First Brother to [[Hunt for Maul|hunt]] Maul and confronted him and his [[Shadow Collective|allies]]. The Inquisitors [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] with the former Sith, ultimately defeating him and rejecting his surrender, but was forced to retreat when Maul brought a [[cave]] down on them.

Later, when Maul, his allies, and other fugitives including [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]] attempted to escape the Imperial [[Operations on Janix|lockdown]] of [[Janix city]], the Eleventh Brother and the First Brother dueled them underneath the [[city]]. Unable to stop them from fleeing, the Eleventh Brother informed Vader they were headed toward him and the Dark Lord engaged the group. The Eleventh and First Brothers again fought the two Jedi, with the Eleventh Brother fleeing after being wounded by Izara.

By [[5 BBY]], the Empire was informed about the presence of a Jedi at a [[Unidentified farm village|farming village]], and the Eleventh Brother was [[Great Jedi Purge|dispatched]] to investigate. The Inquisitor [[Attack on a farm village|destroyed]] the village to lure out the Jedi, rounding up two survivors of the destruction, including the [[Unidentified village brother|individual]] who had tipped the Empire off. Shortly after, the supposed Jedi, actually former Padawan [[Ahsoka Tano]], returned to the village and prevented the Eleventh Brother from killing the informant [[Telekinesis|with]] [[the Force]]. Upon his realization the individual was Tano, the Inquisitor went to strike her down, but the former Jedi evaded his attacks before taking his [[Eleventh Brother's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and swiftly [[Death|killing]] him.

==Biography==
===Imperial Inquisitor===
{{Quote|Brothers, sisters. Enter.|The Grand Inquisitor, welcoming the Inquisitors|audio=BrothersSistersEnter-Devoted.flac|<ref name="Devoted" />}}
[[File:BrothersSistersKneel-Devoted.png|thumb|left|300px|The Eleventh Brother, First Brother, Barriss Offee, and Fourth Sister kneel before the Grand Inquisitor and Darth Vader.]]
A [[Force-sensitive]] [[humanoid]], the individual who would later be known as the Eleventh Brother<ref name="Countdown" /> was once a [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Countdown" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="19BBY">The events of "[[Devoted]]" take place during and immediately after [[Operation: Knightfall]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]].</ref> the Jedi Order was [[Order 66|eliminated]]<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> and the individual was no longer a Jedi.<ref name="Countdown" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="19BBY" /> he<ref name="Companion" /> was present in the partially constructed [[Fortress Inquisitorius]]<ref name="Devoted" /> on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Nur]].<ref name="SWE" /> Now the Eleventh [[Brother (Inquisitorius)|Brother]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s<ref name="Devoted" /> [[Jedi hunter|Jedi-hunting]]<ref name="SWE" /> [[Inquisitorius]], he accompanied the [[Marrok|First Brother]] and [[Fourth Sister]] through a hallway flanked with [[Purge Trooper]]s into the [[fortress]]'s [[Fortress Inquisitorius audience chamber|audience chamber]] after being welcomed in by [[the Grand Inquisitor]],<ref name="Devoted" /> the leader of the [[Inquisitor]]s.<ref name="SWE" />

Within the audience chamber, the group joined fellow Inquisitor [[Barriss Offee]]. At the [[Grand Inquisitor]]'s command, the First Brother and Eleventh Brother took a [[Limb|knee]], with the Fourth [[Sister (Inquisitorius)|Sister]] and Offee following suit. The Grand Inquisitor then ushered in<ref name="Devoted" /> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]<ref name="SWE" /> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the Inquisitors' new Master. After<ref name="Devoted" /> he<ref name="SWE" /> took his [[throne]], the Grand Inquisitor ordered those gathered to stand and declared "[[Long live the Empire]]."<ref name="Devoted" />

===Hunting the Shadow Lord===
====Arrival on Janix====
{{Quote|Sir, a transport is en route to our sector. Your presence is requested to brief the&mdash;|A stormtrooper, to the First Brother|<ref name="Oblivion">{{MSL|Chapter 7: Call to Oblivion}}</ref>}}
Sometime between [[17 BBY]] and around [[13 BBY]],<ref name="ShadowLordDate">{{ShadowLordDate}}</ref> the First Brother [[Hunt for Maul|discovered]] the former [[Sith]] [[Maul]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]], and [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]] on<ref name="Hunted">{{MSL|Chapter 6: Night of the Hunted}}</ref> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Shadow of Maul 1">[[Shadow of Maul 1|''Shadow of Maul'' 1]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Janix]], which he reported to a superior as he requested guidance on how to proceed.<ref name="Hunted" /> Shortly after, the Eleventh Brother traveled to the planet aboard a<ref name="Oblivion" /> [[Phi-class shuttle|''Phi''-class shuttle]],<ref name="SWE" /> with a [[stormtrooper]] informing the First Brother of his arrival.<ref name="Oblivion" /> Later in the [[Time|day]],<ref name="OblivionAD">{{MSL|Chapter 7: Call to Oblivion}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> the Eleventh Brother arrived at the [[Janix Civil Defense HQ]] where the First Brother awaited him. He stood at the [[entry ramp]] of the [[Shuttle|ship]] as it lowered and then descended it to meet his companion.<ref name="Oblivion" />

Together, the Inquisitors traveled aboard an Imperial [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]] and intercepted Maul and his [[Shadow Collective|companions]] attempting to flee the planet atop a bridge. After the [[gunship]] cut Maul's route off, the Inquisitors leaped from its hold, [[lightsaber]]s in hand, and confronted the former Sith. Maul struck at the pair first, though they blocked his attacks. Maul then fled to a bridge below, with both Inquisitors following. The former Sith drove his opponents away, with the Eleventh Brother rolling to dodge Maul's thrown [[Maul's lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="Oblivion" />

====Confronting Maul====
The Inquisitors fled to a nearby rooftop, though Maul chased after,<ref name="Oblivion" /> ferociously<ref name="OblivionAD" /> attacking them. The Eleventh deflected the former Sith's attack and, after being shoved back by Maul, [[Force push]]ed his opponent to a lower point of the roof. Maul fled, with the Inquisitors following, through a nearby [[water]]fall and into a [[cave]]. The Eleventh Brother stalked the cave in search of the former Sith, while the First Brother departed to search another section of the cave. The Eleventh Brother<ref name="Oblivion" /> anticipated<ref name="OblivionAD" /> an attack from behind and whipped around, blocking Maul's strike. The two clashed blades as the [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] continued until Maul eventually Force pushed the Eleventh Brother away, with the First Brother taking his place in the duel.<ref name="Oblivion" />

[[File:TheCrowAndMarrokDuellingMaul.png|thumb|250px|right|The Eleventh Brother damaged Maul's legs during their duel]]
The former Sith buckled as his [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] leg fritzed, after being shoved into a [[Stone|stalagmite]] by the First Brother using the Force. The Eleventh Brother took the opportunity to strike, but Maul was able to dodge the attack. On the bridge, a member of Maul's team, [[Scorn]], noticed the red flashes of the lightsaber duel and ran towards the cave. As the duel continued, the Eleventh Brother struck Maul's cybernetic thigh with his lightsaber, forcing the former Sith to the ground. The Inquisitor switched his lightsaber to disk mode and began spinning the blades around their disc-hilt as he approached his enemy. As he raised the lightsaber to strike, Scorn entered the cave and shoved the Eleventh Brother aside. As Scorn swung a fist at the First Brother, after the Brother destroyed<ref name="Oblivion" /> his<ref name="Scorn">{{Databank|scorn|Scorn}}</ref> [[weapon]], the Eleventh Brother impaled Scorn from behind, before pushing him with the Force out of the cave.<ref name="Oblivion" />

The two Inquisitors then pressed forward to Maul, who jumped to a lower level of the cave, with both Brothers jumping down after him. The First Brother engaged Maul and kicked him to the ground. As the Brothers approached him with their lightsabers drawn, Maul put his lightsaber away and attempted to surrender. The First Brother responded to the surrender by stating that there was no surrender for him, and that the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]]<ref name="Oblivion" />&mdash;[[Darth Sidious]], who was also Maul's former [[Sith Master]]<ref name="SWE" />&mdash;wanted him dead. To this, Maul noted that he wanted the [[Emperor]] dead and began using the Force to bring down the cave. As stones began to hit the ground, the two Brothers retreated from the cave.<ref name="Oblivion" />

====A new approach====
{{Quote|He questioned me. He wanted information about you and Master Daki. I didn't know anything, but who knows what I would have said if I did.|Rylee Lawson, to Devon Izara, on the Eleventh Brother|<ref name="Creeping Fear">{{MSL|Chapter 8: The Creeping Fear}}</ref>}}
The Eleventh Brother then returned to the defense HQ. While there, the Inquisitor planned to interrogate [[Rylee Lawson]], the son of [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]] who had served as part of<ref name="Creeping Fear" /> Janix's [[Tactical Defense Force]]<ref name="Shadow of Maul 1" /> before going rogue. After questioning Rylee and doubting that he did not know information of value, [[Lieutenant]] [[Blake]] brought the boy to an interrogation room, noting that if<ref name="Creeping Fear" /> he<ref name="Blake">{{Databank|lieutenant-blake|Lieutenant Blake}}</ref> was not convinced, then "he"&mdash;referring to the Eleventh Brother&mdash;would not be either, wishing Rylee luck. The Inquisitor then opened a door,<ref name="Creeping Fear" /> causing Rylee to cringe at his sight, with the Eleventh Brother staring at the boy through his mask.<ref name="CreepingFearAD">{{MSL|Chapter 8: The Creeping Fear}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> The boy questioned who the Inquisitor was and asked what he wanted, repeating his claim of not knowing anything<ref name="Creeping Fear" /> and cowering<ref name="CreepingFearAD" /> as the Inquisitor entered the room. The Inquisitor then questioned Rylee for information about the two Jedi, but as he did not know anything, Rylee did not give him what he sought.<ref name="Creeping Fear" />

Later, the Eleventh Brother, the First Brother, Blake, and a number of [[TK stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] were present in a building after forcing [[Rheena Sul]]&mdash;an [[Information broker|informant]] of Brander who was helping him, his son, the [[police droid]] [[2B0T|Two-Boots]], and the two Jedi escape Janix&mdash;into setting a trap for them. As the group descended down the levels of the building, Izara began to [[Force sense|sense]] that something was not right, with her Master also having the same sense. After Brander contacted Sul, he knew she had been compromised and the group escaped their [[turbolift]] before it reached the [[hangar]]. With the lift approaching, the two Inquisitors and three stormtroopers made their way toward it, expecting to find their targets. The Brothers both ignited their lightsabers, only to find that the lift was empty, looking up to its ceiling. Sul then grabbed a stormtrooper's [[DC-15A blaster carbine|blaster]] and began killing the troopers before firing on her [[Starship|ship's]] [[Engine|thrusters]], which caused it to detonate and setting off an explosion that ripped through the hangar toward the two Inquisitors.<ref name="Creeping Fear" />

===Dispensing Imperial "favor"===
{{Quote|You claim a Jedi was here, and yet, I find no one.|The Eleventh Brother, to the informant|audio=YouClaim-Resolve.flac|<ref name="Resolve" />}}
[[File:AttackOnFarmVillage-Resolve.png|thumb|left|300px|The Eleventh Brother holds two hostages in the burning village.]]
By [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Death" /> the Eleventh Brother was [[Great Jedi Purge|dispatched]] to a remote<ref name="Who">{{SW|url=news/who-is-ahsoka-tano|text=Who Is Ahsoka Tano?|author=[[Kelly Knox|Knox, Kelly]]|date=2023-08-15}}</ref> [[Unidentified farm village|farming village]]<ref name="Who">{{SW|url=news/who-is-ahsoka-tano|text=Who Is Ahsoka Tano?|author=[[Kelly Knox|Knox, Kelly]]|date=2023-08-15}}</ref> on an [[Unidentified astronomical object (Ahsoka Tano)|astronomical object]] to investigate a tip from an [[Unidentified village brother|informant]] about the presence of a Jedi there. He [[Death|killed]] most of the inhabitants of the [[City|village]] and,<ref name="SWE" /> in order to find the Jedi, [[Attack on a farm village|burned]] the village;<ref name="Who" /> however, no Jedi came. Believing he had been lied to, the Eleventh Brother rounded up the survivors&mdash;the informant and an [[Unidentified old man (Ahsoka Tano)|old man]]&mdash;and held them within the burning village.<ref name="Resolve" />

The Eleventh Brother dragged his [[Eleventh Brother's lightsaber|lightsaber]] through the dirt as he paced near the two hostages before raising it to the informant's neck. He then confronted the informant about his information, stating that while he had claimed a Jedi had been at the village, he had found there was none. In response, the [[Gender|boy]] promised the Inquisitor a Jedi would arrive back at the village, and added that he should be rewarded for turning her in. The Eleventh Brother then asked the informant if he expected to be rewarded by the Empire despite lying, to which<ref name="Resolve" /> former Padawan<ref name="SWE" /> [[Ahsoka Tano]], the supposed Jedi in question, made her presence known by affirming the boy's claim.<ref name="Resolve" />

===Facing Ahsoka Tano===
{{Quote|And who might you be?|The Eleventh Brother, to Ahsoka Tano|audio=AndWhoMightYouBe-Resolve.flac|<ref name="Resolve" />}}
Turning to face Tano as she strode toward him, the Eleventh Brother inquired as to who she was, but she simply told him to let the hostages go. The boy then called out that she was the Jedi he had reported, and reminded the Inquisitor of his earlier statement that she would return to the village, which the Eleventh Brother agreed with. Walking behind the informant, the Inquisitor told him he would have his reward and then raised his [[Double-bladed spinning lightsaber|lightsaber]] to strike down the boy, but was thwarted by Tano's [[Telekinesis|use]] of [[the Force]].<ref name="Resolve" /> Immediately recognizing that it was Tano,<ref name="Highlights">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-tales-of-the-jedi-highlights|text=15 Highlights from ''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi''|author=[[Brandon Wainerdi|Wainerdi, Brandon]]|date=2022-12-02}}</ref> the Eleventh Brother walked toward her while igniting the second blade of his [[Double-bladed lightsaber|weapon]] and told the former Padawan she was supposed to be dead.<ref name="Resolve" />

[[File:EleventhBrotherDeflates-Resolve.gif|thumb|right|300px|The Eleventh Brother's helmet deflates after he is decapitated.]]
As he drew closer to Tano, the Inquisitor noted that he would be the one rewarded after all, and then stated her name. The Inquisitor struck at Tano with both blades, but the former Jedi avoided each attack. On the fourth swing, Tano grabbed the lightsaber and deactivated it, before taking the lightsaber from his hands by kicking the Inquisitor's chest, separating the two. As the Eleventh Brother staggered back, she reactivated the saber and decapitated the Imperial. His [[helmet]] rolled back and deflated as his [[Color|red]] [[eye]]s went dark and his body collapsed.<ref name="Resolve" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You lie to me and expect Imperial favor?|The Eleventh Brother intimidates his hostage|audio=ImperialFavor-Resolve.flac|<ref name="Resolve" />}}
[[File:EleventhBrotherThreatens-Resolve.png|thumb|left|300px|The Eleventh Brother threatens the young man with his lightsaber.]]
The Eleventh Brother was a humanoid male<ref name="Countdown" /> with tiny, red eyes.<ref name="OblivionAD" /> The Inquisitor was ruthless, burning down an entire village and killing most of its inhabitants after receiving a tip that a Jedi was hiding among them.<ref name="Resolve" /> When he was unable to find the Jedi, he was frustrated<ref name="Highlights" /> and accused the informant of lying, threatening him with his lightsaber. After Tano revealed herself to him, he still planned to kill the informant. Upon realizing the supposed Jedi was Tano, he was<ref name="Resolve" /> eager to kill her,<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2023 Topps Finest Star Wars|cardname=Inquisitor|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/394545/cid/23204404}}</ref> expecting a reward for himself.<ref name="Resolve" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a former member of the Jedi Order, the Eleventh Brother was Force-sensitive and trained to [[Force power|wield]] the Force.<ref name="Countdown" /> He was also capable of lightsaber dueling.<ref name="Oblivion" />

==Equipment==
{{Listen3|EleventhBrothersSabersSecondBlade-Resolve.flac|the Eleventh Brother igniting his lightsaber's second blade}}
The Eleventh Brother wore a black helmet<ref name="DevotedAD">{{TOTE|Devoted}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> with a white, [[demon]]ic<ref name="Countdown" /> mask resembling the [[Bone|skull]] of a [[crow]].<ref name="OblivionAD" /> He wore a black<ref name="Resolve" /> [[cloak]]<ref name="OblivionAD" /> with a silver clasp in the front. Both shoulders of the cloak were adorned with a large [[Imperial crest]]. The Inquisitor wielded a double-bladed spinning lightsaber with red blades. He wore black clothes under his cloak, with a black [[utility belt]] and black [[boot]]s. The Eleventh Brother's black [[glove]]s featured imagery of bones along the fingers and talons at the tips.<ref name="Resolve" />

===Eleven is greater than six===
{{Quote|It's based on the same outline I gave publishing for the novel. It was always the same story.|Dave Filoni, on "Resolve"|<ref name="Nerdist">{{WebCite|url=https://nerdist.com/article/tales-of-the-jedi-dave-filoni-interview-ahsoka-tano-count-dooku-star-wars|text=Dave Filoni on How TALES OF THE JEDI Explores Choices Between Light and Dark|author=[[Amy Ratcliffe|Ratcliffe, Amy]]|date=2022-10-26|work=[[Wikipedia:Nerdist|Nerdist]]|archivedate=20221026155354}}</ref>}}
The [[2016]] young-adult novel [[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']], which was written by [[E. K. Johnston]], features Ahsoka Tano battling and killing an Inquisitor known as the [[Sixth Brother]] during an [[Raada Uprising|uprising]] on the Outer Rim moon [[Raada]].<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> After Johnston watched the ''Tales of the Jedi'' trailer, she assumed the Inquisitor&mdash;at that point not identified&mdash;was new and therefore not an adaption of the Sixth Brother,<ref name="EK tweet 1">{{Twitter|username=ek_johnston|url=status/1568673474046226432|name=[[E. K. Johnston]]|date=2022-09-10|quote=A new Inquisitor, because it's only fair that Ahsoka gets to beat like 12 of them. 🤣🤣🤣|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/2JkCp|archivefile=File:EKJohnston-EleventhBrother-Twitter.png|nolive=1}}</ref> stating the individual he was seen threatening was not [[Kaeden Larte]],<ref name="EK tweet 2">{{Twitter|username=ek_johnston|url=status/1568675342424096768|name=[[E. K. Johnston]]|date=2022-09-10|quote=Well, that's certainly not Kaedan he's got.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/5fRvM|archivefile=File:EKJohnston-EleventhBrother-Twitter.png|nolive=1}} {{C|In response to: So totally new Inquisitor and not a redesign of the Sixth Brother?}}</ref> a character from her ''Ahsoka'' novel.<ref name="Ahsoka" /> When a fan replied that the Inquisitor in the trailer seemed like the Sixth Brother, she replied that she appreciated their optimism.<ref name="EK tweet 3">{{Twitter|username=ek_johnston|url=status/1568748377474740225|name=[[E. K. Johnston]]|date=2022-09-10|quote=I like your optimism.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/NuQQT|archivefile=File:EKJohnston-EleventhBrother-Twitter.png|nolive=1}} {{C|In response to: I don’t think this guy is a new inquisitor. He seemed like the one from your novel tbh}}</ref>

[[File:DaveFiloni-CelebrationEurope23.png|thumb|right|250px|According to Dave Filoni, "Resolve" was intended to be the same story as the ''Ahsoka'' novel.]]
When Johnston addressed the possibility of contradictions between her novel and ''Tales of the Jedi'', she noted her frustration upon seeing fans declare her novel non-[[canon]], stating that Dave Filoni had approved the novel years before and had since continued to work on her story.<ref name="EK contradictions tweet">{{Twitter|username=ek_johnston|url=status/1553725660459417602|name=[[E. K. Johnston]]|date=2022-07-31|quote=There isn't a big conspiracy behind the changes in Ahsoka's story. Dave approved the book six years ago and then kept working on her journey. It's frustrating to see people gleefully declare my book non-canon instead of just&hellip;using their imaginations. Like we did.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/YNtZn|archivefile=File:EKJohnston-TalesOfTheJedi-Twitter.png|nolive=1}}</ref> After the release of ''Tales of the Jedi'', Filoni confirmed that the episode was based on the same outline provided for the ''Ahsoka'' novel, and that it was intended to be the same story.<ref name="Nerdist" /> Both ''Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy'' and "Inquisitor Countdown" separate the two Inquisitors, though neither source states that the Sixth Brother was killed by Tano.<ref name="Countdown" /><ref name="SWE" /> This article assumes the events of ''Ahsoka'' and "Resolve" are separate.
