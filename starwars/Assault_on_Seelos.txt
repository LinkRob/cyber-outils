In [[4 BBY]], the [[Galactic Empire]] launched an '''assault''' against rebel and former [[Clone trooper|clone]] forces on the [[planet]] [[Seelos]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|I hope you brought a better class of soldiers than those&hellip;stormtroopers.|Rex's response to Agent Kallus' threat|<ref name="Relics" />}}
In an attempt to find a planet to host [[Phoenix Cell]]'s new base, [[Ahsoka Tano]] suggested to the ''[[Ghost]]''{{'s}} crew to search for her fellow [[Clone Wars]], [[Captain]] [[Rex]], who knew of useful locations for the rebels to utilize. The [[Spectres]] traveled to [[Seelos]], the planet on which the clone had probably retired after the war. There [[Kanan Jarrus]], [[Ezra Bridger]], [[Sabine Wren]], and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] reached the surface on the shuttle ''[[Phantom]]'', leaving [[Hera Syndulla]] on the ''Ghost'' orbiting the planet.<ref name="The Lost Commanders">{{Rebels|The Lost Commanders}}</ref>

The rebels found the clone riding a [[Rex's AT-TE|modified]] [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]], a Clone Wars-era [[walker]], together with two comrades, [[Commander]]s [[Wolffe]] and [[Gregor]]. After some adventures and debates, the group seemed to get along, but Wolffe, who was unhappy with the rebels and Tano, alerted the [[Galactic Empire]]. Despite realizing his folly, the rebels' position was now exposed to the Empire. Shortly later, an Imperial [[probe droid]] discovered the ''Phantom'' and inflicted damage on the shuttle before the droid was destroyed by Rex.<ref name="The Lost Commanders" />

Tracking Wolffe's signal, the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] reached Seelos aboard the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] ''[[Relentless]]''. Shortly later, Agent Kallus contacted the clones by intercom and demanded that they surrender the rebels. However, Rex rejected Kallus' demands, and he and his fellow clones resolved that they would fight in order to allow the rebels to escape offworld. Before leaving, Rex handed Bridger a list of [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] military bases in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and [[Mandalore]].<ref name="Relics" />

==The battle==
{{Dialogue|Wolffe|Enemy contact point two four. How many legs they got?|Gregor|Looks like four.|Wolffe|Four? Well, how are they not falling over?|Gregor|Will you look at the size of those things!|Rex|Reinforced armor plating, heavy cannons and antipersonnel blasters.|Ezra|I'm glad you guys are so excited, but those giant death machines are on their way to blow us to bits!|attr=Wolffe, Gregor, Rex, and Ezra Bridger on AT-ATs|src=<ref name="Relics" />}}
Instead of an [[orbital bombardment]], Kallus decided to deploy three [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|AT-AT walkers]] to destroy enemy forces, represented just by the clones' AT-TE. To gain an advantage, Wolffe commanded the walker into an approaching [[sandstorm]], causing all the vehicles to be blinded. However, the [[Force-sensitive]] rebels Jarrus and Bridger were able to use [[the Force]] to guide the clone crew through the sandstorm.<ref name="Relics" />

The AT-ATs and AT-TE attempted to outmaneuver each other until Kallus was able to surround the clone walker with an enveloping [[pincer maneuver]]. Rex soon sent Bridger to use the Force to destroy one of the AT-ATs. This took the Imperials by surprise since they were not aware that they were so close with the enemies. Jarrus pointed Bridger in the right direction, and the young rebel centered one an AT-AT with the AT-TE's main gun. Then the clone walker left the sandstorm, pursued by the remaining two walkers.<ref name="Relics" />

At this point in the battle, [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]] was ordered by [[Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] to meet with his [[shuttle]], forcing him to abandon the battle. The ''Ghost'' was finally able to descend to Seelos without having to contend with the Imperial Star Destroyer. Meanwhile the rebels left the AT-TE aboard the ''Phantom'' to rendezvous with Syndulla and Chopper on the ''Ghost'' while Rex and the clones charged the two remaining walkers in a suicide attack.<ref name="Relics" />

Despite taking several hits, the clones' AT-TE was able to ram the legs of Kallus' AT-AT. However, Kallus' more powerful and taller AT-AT soon gained the advantaged over the vintage AT-TE walker. In spite of the odds against them, Rex and his fellow clones resolved to continue fighting. At the urging of Bridger, Jarrus agreed to help the clones. While Wren flew the ''Phantom'', Jarrus, Bridger, and Orrelios entered the head of the second remaining AT-AT and hijacked the walker. They then used the walker's guns to attack Kallus' walker.<ref name="Relics" />

Kallus attempted to call for reinforcements, but Admiral Konstantine was unable to render any help since he was preoccupied with hosting the [[Fifth Brother]], another [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitor]]. With Kallus' AT-AT walker weakened, the clones' AT-TE walker was able to regain power. This allowed Rex to fire the AT-TE's [[laser cannon]] into the AT-AT walker's neck, destroying the Imperial walker. Kallus and two surviving [[AT-AT pilot]]s escaped the battlefield in a speeder. Out of gratitude to the rebels, Rex along with Wolffe and Gregor saluted Bridger and Jarrus.<ref name="Relics" />

==Aftermath==
{{Dialogue|Rex|Looks like we live to fight another day.|Wolffe|Don't we always?|Gregor|Well, we do when we've got a Jedi leading us.|attr=Captain Rex and Commanders Wolffe and Gregor|src=<ref name="Relics" />}}
While Wolffe and Gregor chose to stay behind on Seelos,<ref name="Rebels Recon #2.06">{{RebelsRecon|Jdj4teY5gik|#2.06: Inside "Wings of the Master" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-wings-of-the-master}}</ref> Rex decided to aid Phoenix Squadron and traveled with the crew of the ''Ghost''. The group met with the rebel flotilla, where Ahsoka Tano saw her old friend and had a brief talk with him.<ref name="Relics" /> Since the events on Seelos, Rex often helped the rebels in their missions, such as in the [[Blockade of Ibaar]],<ref name="Wings">{{Rebels|Wings of the Master}}</ref> in the [[rescue on the Imperial Interdictor]],<ref name="Stealth">{{Rebels|Stealth Strike}}</ref> the [[Battle of Garel]],<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref> an [[Ambush on an Imperial construction module|ambush]] in the [[Geonosis system]],<ref name="The Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref> and the establishment of [[Chopper Base]] on the planet [[Atollon]].<ref name="Mystery">{{Rebels|The Mystery of Chopper Base}}</ref>

Wolffe and Gregor then took the last AT-AT and turned into their new home, [[Joopa Base]].<ref name="Fool's Hope">{{Rebels|A Fool's Hope}}</ref> The battle had also demonstrated, once and for all, that clone troopers were indeed superior to the soldiers who had replaced them.<ref name="Rex Roll">{{InsiderCite|227|Rex & Roll}}</ref> Seelos again became a battleground of the early Galactic Civil War when another former clone trooper&mdash;[[Wildfire (clone trooper)|Wildfire]], who had actually served under Wolffe during the Clone Wars&mdash;was dispatched by the rebellion on a [[Mission to Seelos|mission]] to secure intelligence from Joopa Base.<ref name="Tyrants">{{SWIA|set=Tyrants of Lothal}}</ref>
