The '''Ebaq system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Deep Core]]. It was located in grid square M-11 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Ebaq was mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 36|thirty-sixth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine, published in [[2015]].<ref name="Falcon 36">{{BuildFalconCite|36|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> The [[Treskov system]] that contained [[Ebaq/Legends|Ebaq]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2002]] novel ''[[The New Jedi Order: Destiny's Way]]''.<ref name="NJO">''[[The New Jedi Order: Destiny's Way]]''</ref>
