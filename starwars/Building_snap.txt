'''Building snaps''' were a type of toy used by [[Youngling|children]] in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Chiss [[sky-walker]] [[Ab'begh]] had a number of building snaps in her quarters on the [[Chiss]] [[warship]] [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']], significantly more than fellow sky-walker Che'ri. The [[Gender|girls]] began to play with the snaps in Ab'begh's quarters but were interrupted by her [[Ab'begh's caregiver|caregiver]], who instructed them to read for half an [[Standard hour|hour]] before continuing to read. Che'ri, who had begun to create a design with her snaps that she feared she would forget, protested, though Ab'begh put down her snaps, causing Che'ri to accept her assignment.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref>

In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Che'ri]] kept building snaps in her quarters on the Chiss warship ''[[Springhawk]]'', which her [[caregiver]], [[Mitth'ali'astov]], suggested that she play with during her downtime. Che'ri, however, was uninterested in playing and instead wanted to meet members of the [[Paccosh]] [[species]] along with adult members of the ''Springhawk''{{'s}} crew.<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Behind the scenes==
Building snaps first appeared in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
