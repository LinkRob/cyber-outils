'''Double planets''' were pairs of [[planet]]s of approximately the same size and mass that [[orbit]]ed each other and together shared an orbit around that [[Star system|system's]] [[star]] or stars. The moon [[Cynda]] was almost large enough to be classified as a double planet with [[Gorse]], which it [[orbit]]ed.<ref name="A New Dawn">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref> The [[gas giant]]s [[Nixus (Haileap system)|Nixus]] and [[Neralus]] in the [[Haileap system]] were a true double planet. Although Nixus and Neralus were both orbited by their [[moon]] [[Wevo]],<ref name="AToC" /> [[Eirie]], the moon of the double planets [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]], instead hung suspended between them,<ref name="Convergence">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]''</ref> leaving the twin worlds' inhabitants unaware of which planet the moon may have originally orbited.<ref name="ITD">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref>
