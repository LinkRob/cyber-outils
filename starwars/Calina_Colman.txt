'''Calina Colman''' was a [[Gender|woman]] who ran a [[Ryden Colman's bakery|bakery]] on the [[planet]] [[Estarvera]] with her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Ryden Colman]]. When the plague known as the [[Blight]] appeared on Estarvera, Calina, Ryden, and their children [[Shanna]] and [[Davet Colman]] tried to evacuate the planet. Although the [[Colman family|family]] was unable to escape, they survived due to the Blight being [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|stopped]] by the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
