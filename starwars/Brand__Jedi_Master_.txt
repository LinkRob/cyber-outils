'''Brand''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] individual who served as a [[Jedi Master]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="TCC" /> Around [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> he served as a delegate of the [[Jedi Order]] present on the [[Siline]] during the diplomatic talks between the [[Silinese government]] and the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[diplomat]] [[Darren Abysina]] about the [[planet]] joining the Republic. During the high ranking member o the [[Silinese War Council]], [[Rap Arcon]], repeatedly challenged Brand to a ritualistic duel, which Brand refused. The [[Silinese]] were offended and saw Brand's refusal as a disrespect of their traditions as a former warrior culture and questioned his ability to appropriately negotiate with them, leading to a standstill in the diplomatic negotiations. In response the [[Jedi High Council]] ordered Brand from the planet and sent Jedi Masters [[Elishe]] and [[Sol]] to clear up the misunderstanding.<ref name="TCC" />
