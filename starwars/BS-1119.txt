'''BS-1119''' was a [[droid]] [[Bouncer (occupation)|bouncer]] of the criminal group known as [[The Family]]. Resembling a modified [[HK-model gladiator droid|HK assassin unit]], it oversaw the Family's [[Family tracks|illegal racetracks]] within the [[Coruscant Underworld]], where it encountered [[Marriage|newlyweds]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] during a secret rendezvous. Despite being designed as an enforcer, BS-1119 came across as particularly polite, a quirk that Skywalker attributed to it being programmed for both security and business. The droid wielded twin [[blaster pistol]]s that it stored in [[holster]]s on its hips.<ref name="Brotherhood" />

==Behind the scenes==
BS-1119 was introduced in the [[2022]] novel [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] by author [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />
