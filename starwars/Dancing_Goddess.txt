The '''Dancing Goddess''' was an idol of the [[Godoan]] people<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files" /> and was said to give great fortune to its owner.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> At one point, [[Barpotomous Drebble]] acquired it. However, it was [[Thief|stolen]] from him<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files" /> and wound up in the [[Dryden's study|collection]] of [[Dryden Vos]], the public face of the crime syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]].<ref name="TOG" /> The idol was smashed to bits during a confrontation between Vos, [[Qi'ra]] and [[Han Solo]],<ref name="Solo" /> but was later repaired.<ref name="CR 3">[[Crimson Reign 3|''Crimson Reign'' 3]]</ref> A similar artifact was in the possession of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who kept it in his [[Fortress Vader|castle]] on [[Mustafar]].<ref name="TFVC 3">[[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 3|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 3]]</ref> Around [[34 ABY]], the Dancing Goddess was kept by the [[Ithorian]] [[collector]] [[Dok-Ondar]] in his [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|shop]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dancing Goddess, albeit unidentified, first appeared in the theatrical film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo" /> which was released on [[May 25]], [[2018]].<ref name="Solo Release">{{SW|url=news/christopher-miller-and-phil-lord-to-helm-han-solo-anthology-film|text=Christopher Miller and Phil Lord to Helm Han Solo Anthology Film|nolive=1}}</ref> The artifact was identified in the tie-in visual dictionary ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TOG" /> and released on the same day as the film.<ref name="Guide Release">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/567426/solo-a-star-wars-story-the-official-guide-by-pablo-hidalgo|text=Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide}}</ref> According to Hidalgo, the Dancing Goddess was added to the background of the film after he was asked for a shortlist of potential trophies by the filmmakers.<ref name="Shortlist">{{Bluesky|infinata.bsky.social|post/3kx6olkfca32p|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2024-07-13|quote=This issue also features the debut of the Dancing Goddess, a precious jade statue that I was able to get into the background of Solo: A Star Wars Story in Dryden Vos' yacht (the filmmakers asked me for a shortlist of potential trophies, and the Dancing Goddess made the cut).|archiveurl=https://archive.is/SmQHO}}</ref>
