An '''electroshock stun device''', or simply '''electroshock device''', was a piece of equipment fitted to [[KE-8 Enforcer Ship]]s. These patrol vehicles, used in the [[Tipoca City Military Complex]] on [[Kamino]], sought out and isolated aberrant [[clone trooper]]s during their training, and were also equipped with powerful [[manipulator arm]]s.<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
