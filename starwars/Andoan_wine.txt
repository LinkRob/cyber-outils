'''Andoan wine''' was a type of [[wine]] produced on [[Ando]],<ref name="Lost Stars" /> the [[homeworld]] of the [[Aqualish]] [[species]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="AOTCDate">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}} to [[22 BBY]].</ref> Andoan wine was being served at the<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref> [[Outlander Club]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and was kept in a bottle on the counter behind the club's central bar.<ref name="AOTC" /> By the [[Standard year|year]] [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">[[Thane Kyrell]]'s taste for [[Ando]]an [[wine]] is mentioned during a section of ''[[Lost Stars]]'' depicting events that occur concurrently with those of {{Film|V}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[human]] [[pilot]] [[Thane Kyrell]] had developed a taste for the drink over [[time]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />

==Behind the scenes==
Andoan wine was first mentioned in the [[2015]] [[canon]] novel ''[[Lost Stars]]'', which was written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="Lost Stars" /> The [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: How Not to Get Eaten by Ewoks and Other Galactic Survival Skills]]'' written by [[Christian Blauvelt]] [[Retroactive continuity|retconned]] one of the drinks<ref name="HNTGE" /> visible in the [[2002]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']],<ref name="AOTC" /> to be Andoan wine.<ref name="HNTGE" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the beverage previously appeared in "[[The Great Herdship Heist]]," a [[1997]] short story written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 15|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 15]].<ref name="The Great Herdship Heist">{{JournalCite|15|The Great Herdship Heist}}</ref>
