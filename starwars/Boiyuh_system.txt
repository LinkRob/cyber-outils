The '''Boiyuh system''' was located in the intersection of the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Galaxy" /> and [[the Interior]] regions,<ref name="Interior" /> situated at the coordinates J-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Galaxy" /> It contained the [[planet]] [[Boiyuh]], the [[homeworld]] of the [[human]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[twin]]s [[Lyttan Dree|Lyttan]] and [[Tamu Dree]]. Facing starvation on Boiyuh, the twins left the [[Star system|system]] to join the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Imperial Cadet 1">[[Han Solo - Imperial Cadet 1|''Han Solo - Imperial Cadet'' 1]]</ref> by [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Han Solo - Imperial Cadet 1|''Han Solo - Imperial Cadet'' 1]], including the mention of [[Boiyuh]], to [[10 BBY]].</ref>
