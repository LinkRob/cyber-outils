'''Credit bricks''' were a physical form of the [[Galactic Credit Standard]] used during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. 

==Descriptions==
A credit brick<ref name="DarkRevengeAD" /> was a heavy triangular prism that were used as a physical form of the [[Galactic Credit Standard]] used during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="DarkRevenge" />

==History==
The [[Gran]] [[crime lord]] [[Nico Deemis]] owned a [[bank]] that stored credit bricks in the [[Banking District]] in [[Janix city]] on the [[planet]] [[Janix]]. One [[Local day|night]]<ref name="DarkRevenge" /> between [[17 BBY]] and around [[13 BBY]],<ref name="ShadowLordDate">{{ShadowLordDate|1}}</ref> a shipment of credit bricks were being transported to Deemis's bank on Janix when a group of [[Mandalorian]] and [[Zabrak]] criminals [[Raid on a bank tower|attacked]] the bank. During the attack, a [[bank guard droid]] managed to activate the alarm and seal off the vault. The criminals loaded several [[repulsorcart]] of credit bricks on to a [[bank transport]] and departed the bank. Shortly after members of the [[Janix Civil Defense]]'s [[Tactical Defense Force]] surrounded the criminals as they were loading the stolen credit bricks onto the ''[[Hex (Kom'rk-class)|Hex]]'', a [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport]]. In the ensuing firefight, the criminals used the bricks as cover to protect themselves from [[blaster]] bolts. The criminals won the engagement and returned to their [[Maul's safe house|safe house]] where  the criminal's leader, [[Maul]], examined the stolen credit bricks while talking with [[Rook Kast]], one of the Mandalorains, about how Deemis believe he could take what was his away from him.<ref name="DarkRevenge" /> 

Later, the criminals [[Raid on the Janix Civil Defense HQ|captured]] [[Looti Vario]], a [[Aleena]] crime lord, and brought him back to their hideout. There Maul used [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|lift]] a cart of credits brick above Vario. Successfully intimidated, the Aleena agreed to provide information on the [[Pyke Syndicate]]. With the information, Mauls criminal's attacked a [[Unidentified starship (Pyke Syndicate)|Pyke cruiser]] that was transporting two carts of credits bricks from Janix.<ref name="SinisterSchemes">{{MSL|Chapter 2: Sinister Schemes}}</ref>

When Vario contacted [[Marg Krim]], he claimed the shipments were stolen by rogue [[lieutenant]]s of [[Nico Deemis]] and offers to make amends by delivering the lost payments to Krim, including the value of the destroyed Janix cargo.<ref name="WhispersintheUnknown">{{MSL|Chapter 3: Whispers in the Unknown}}</ref>

Upon arriving at the [[Pyke Palace]] on [[Oba Diah]], [[Vario's guard droid]]s unloaded the two credit brick carts, before Vario informed the [[Captain of the Guard]] [[Kalt]] that Maul was aboard the [[Lau cargo ship]].<ref name="PrideVengeance">{{MSL|Chapter 4: Pride and Vengeance}}</ref>
