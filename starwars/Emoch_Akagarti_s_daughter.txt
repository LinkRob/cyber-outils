The '''daughter''' of [[Emoch Akagarti]] and his [[Emoch Akagarti's wife|wife]] was a [[Gender|female]] individual. She was held hostage with [[Emoch Akagarti's wife|her mother]] and her [[Taryai Akagarti|brother]] when the Dreamer's took Emoch to the [[Affadar water purification plant]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
