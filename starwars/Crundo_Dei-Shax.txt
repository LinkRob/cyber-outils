'''Crundo Dei-Shax''' was a [[Weequay]] [[Technician|mechanic]] who in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> ran a [[speeder]] business in the outskirts of the [[city]] of [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. Dei-Shax had [[Color|blue]] [[eye]]s, light skin and were capable of upgrading a [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]] when they had access to the appropriate materials. While working the shop<ref name="Outlaws" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> they wore a [[Color|brown]] [[Clothing|vest]] over a brown shift, black pants, and a brown [[belt]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crundo Dei-Shax appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, they were one of several speeder mechanics that could upgrade the player's speeder bike.<ref name="Outlaws" />
