'''Chief supervisor''' was a title and position used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] and during the [[Imperial Era]] by the ruling [[Galactic Empire]].

==Description==
[[File:ISB-Chief-Supervisor-rank-plaque.png|thumb|left|200px|An Imperial rank plaque used to denote a chief supervisor in the ISB.]]
The title and position of chief supervisor was used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] and was used by the individual who oversaw [[B1-series battle droid]] production within [[droid]] production plants.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Subsequently, the [[Galactic Empire]] utilized the title within the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], the secret intelligence and [[law enforcement agency]] of the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]]. In the organization, ISB [[Imperial officer|officers]] with the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[commander]] could use the title, and were placed in command of ISB installations and operate secret agents. The Imperial [[rank insignia plaque]] for an ISB commander who served as chief supervisor was a single row plaque with four [[Color|blue]] tiles.<ref name="Survivor" /> Another version of a chief supervisor plaque was a single row of one blue tile followed by three red tiles.<ref name="Topps">{{SWCT|set=Imperial Ascension|cardname=Imperial Security Bureau Chief Supervisor}}</ref>

==History==
During the pan-[[Galaxy|galactic]] Clone Wars between the Confederacy and the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> that lasted from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the individual [[Poreni]] served as a chief supervisor in the [[Akiva production plant]] and announced to [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] that the latest batch of B1 [[battle droid]]s was completed ahead of time and that per their agreement, would deliver them to [[Hakara]] for immediate deployment.<ref name="Outlaws" />

In [[9 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> during the Imperial Era, Commander [[Lank Denvik]] served as chief supervisor of the [[II-0810 Satellite Station]], a clandestine listening station in the [[Nova Garon system]].
the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Commander]] [[Lank Denvik]] held the title of chief supervisor. Commander Denvik also handled [[Bode Akuna]], a fallen [[Jedi]] survivor of the [[Great Jedi Purge]] who began to operate against remnants of the Jedi Order. Denvik [[Death|perished]] when Akuna betrayed him to [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and killed Denvik for his failures.<ref name="Survivor" />
