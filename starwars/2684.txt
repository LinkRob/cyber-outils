'''2684''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] that served the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] as part of a [[Geonosian droid army|droid army]] on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Episode II" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[B-series battle droid]] was among those deployed to the [[Petranaki Arena]] to [[First Battle of Geonosis|combat]] a [[Jedi assault team]] that had infiltrated the arena to rescue three [[prisoner]]s due for [[Death|execution]]: [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Episode II" />

As fighting broke out on the floor of the arena, 2684 was destroyed when [[Cloning|clone]] troopers fired at the droid in their [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]]s.<ref name="Episode II" />

The B1's designation was written on the back of their [[comlink booster pack]].<ref name="Episode II" />

==Behind the scenes==
2684 first appeared in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]]. While the [[droid]]'s designation can be spotted in film,<ref name="Episode II" /> the numbers could not be identified until ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'' gave a translation for B1 numerals.<ref name="Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
