'''Alastair Murden''', also known as '''Alastair James Murden''' and '''AJ Murden''', is a [[Wikipedia:British people|British]] [[Wikipedia:Voice Actor|voice actor]] who voiced [[Lieutenant]] [[Blake]] in the [[2026]] [[Disney+]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]''.

==Biography==

Alastair Murden, who is also known as Alastair James Murden<ref name="Facebook">{{Facebook|username=alastairjamesmurden|name=Alastair James Murden|archivefile=File:AlastairMurden-Profile-Facebook.png}}</ref> and AJ Murden,<ref name="Podcast"/> is a [[Wikipedia:British people|British]] born<ref name="TikTok Bio"/> [[Wikipedia:Voice Actor|voice actor]],<ref name="Podcast"/> professional [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]], [[Wikipedia:Singing|singer]], and [[Wikipedia:Improvisational_theatre#Comedy|improv comedian]] who lives in [[Wikipedia:Los Angeles|Los Angeles]].<ref name="Facebook"/> He<ref name="Podcast"/> voiced [[Lieutenant]] [[Blake]] in the [[2026]] [[Disney+]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]''.<ref name="Blake"/> Murden found out about being cast in ''Maul - Shadow Lord'' whilst working at [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] in [[2024]]. The voice actor shared his excitement at being able to finally reveal the casting in a [[Wikipedia:TikTok|TikTok]] post on [[April 21]] of 2026,<ref name="Blake"/> a day after the first two episodes in which his voice appeared, [[Chapter 5: Inquisition]]<ref name="MSL 105"/> and [[Chapter 6: Night of the Hunted]],<ref name="MSL 106"/> premiered on Disney+.<ref name="Air Date">{{Twitter|starwars|status/2033951658485682588|''[[Star Wars]]''|date=2026-03-17|archivefile=File:MaulShadowLord-EpisodeTitles-Twitter.jpg}}</ref> During the show's development, Murden's character's name was changed, having originally being "Lt. Tavich".<ref name="MSL Trivia">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-trivia|text=Inside the Making of ''Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord''}}</ref> Murden has had [[Sam Witwer]], a prolific fellow voice actor who voiced the [[Maul|titular character]] of ''Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord'', on his podcast:<ref name="TikTok Bio"/> "Actor Behind the Voice".<ref name="Podcast"/>
