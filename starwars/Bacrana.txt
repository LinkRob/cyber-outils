'''Bacrana''' was an astronomical object located in the [[Bacrana system]] in the [[Brak sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> along the [[Corellian Run]].<ref name="TFABG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The map included in the [[2016]] roleplaying sourcebook [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], which recanonized Bacrana, incorrectly swapped the locations of Bacrana and the [[Milagro system]] along the [[Corellian Run]].<ref name="TFABG" /> Later maps, such as [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]], placed Milagro in the correct location at the junction of the Corellian Run and the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Harrin Trade Corridor]] route.<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref>
