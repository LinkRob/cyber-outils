The '''Abektofah''' also known as the '''common abektofah''' was a winged [[species]] found on [[Toshara]], a [[moon]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It had a similar bone structure but a wider wingspan than its ancestor species the [[Abekdodo]]. <ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[Rodian]] explorer was examining a abekdodo specimen and making observations about it in comparison to the abektofah while in the [[Market District (Mirogana)|Market District]] of [[Mirogana]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of Toshara. A near by Rodian freelancer commented that they were pretty sure it was a fake, to which the explorer claimed to already know that.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The Abektofah were first mentioned in ambient dialogue that the player can over hear in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
