'''Aneta Graf''' was a member of the [[Graf family]] who was born on the [[planet]] [[Siline]] during the [[High Republic Era]] as the child of<ref name="Crystal Crown" /> [[Yrlan Graf]].<ref name="Gratton" /> She served as a co-director of [[Graf-Siline Industries Consortium]] along with her husband, [[Xan Graf]]. Their child, [[Lionine Graf]] was deeply connected with the [[Silinese]] people and culture, resulting in familial conflict, as Aneta and Xan wished to integrate Siline with the [[Galactic Republic]], which would allow their branch of the family to leave Siline and move to [[Coruscant]]. Despite being [[human]], Aneta and her husband were relatively well-respected in Siline due to their fortune, which they maintained through [[iflisalt]] mining, particularly their synthetic and extraction processes, which were key in Siline's place in the [[the galaxy|galactic]] economy.<ref name="Crystal Crown" />
