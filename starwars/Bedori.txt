The '''Bedori''' ([[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]: ベドリ) was a type of [[Frog/Legends|frog]] native to the [[Mountain/Legends|mountains]] of [[Kessel/Legends|Kessel]].<ref name="FamiMaga">{{BookCite|author=|container=Star Wars|title=Family Computer Magazine|number=1987 #21|publisher=Tokuma Shoten|date=December 4, 1987|issn=|link=https://archive.org/details/famimaga-dec-1987}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
These creatures first appeared in the [[1987]] [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] [[Star Wars (1987 video game)|''Star Wars'']] video game for the [[Wikipedia:Nintendo Entertainment System|Famicom]] home console.<ref name="Star Wars (1987 video game)" /> Their name was revealed in the [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese language]] guide of the game serialized in Family Computer Magazine, in which it was rendered as ベドリ (''Bedori'') in [[Wikipedia:Katakana|katakana]] syllabary.<ref name="FamiMaga" />
