'''Corellian Ryshcate''' was a type of pastry from the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] that was traditionally served at celebrations and commonly eaten by [[Corellian]]s who had left the planet. The pastries were consumable by [[Artiodac]]s<ref name="Cookbook" /> and [[human]]s,<ref name="Human" /> and were made with [[Corellian whiskey]] and [[nut]]s in layers of pastry. After the [[Battle of Takodana]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] began to serve Corellian Ryshcate in his traveling [[restaurant]], [[Tuggs' Grub]], while stationed in [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet Batuu.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian Ryshcate was introduced to the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was authored by [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> [[Ryshcate/Legends|Ryshcate]] from [[Corellia/Legends|Corellia]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, in [[Michael A. Stackpole]]'s [[1996]] novel ''[[X-Wing: Rogue Squadron]]''.<ref name="Rogue Squadron">''[[X-Wing: Rogue Squadron]]''</ref> The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] ingredients are [[Wikipedia:Puff pastry|puff pastry]], [[Wikipedia:Walnut|walnuts]], [[Wikipedia:Raisin|golden raisins]], [[Wikipedia:Pistachio|pistachios]], [[Wikipedia:Brown sugar|brown sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Butter|butter]], [[Wikipedia:Honey|honey]], [[Wikipedia:Cinnamon|ground cinnamon]], [[Wikipedia:Cardamom|ground cardamom]], [[Wikipedia:Flour|flour]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], [[Wikipedia:Whisky|whiskey]] or [[Wikipedia:Brandy|brandy]], and [[Wikipedia:Cream|heavy cream]] for glazing.<ref name="Cookbook" />
