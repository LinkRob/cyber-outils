The '''Ajara system''',<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> also known as the '''Ajan Kloss system''',<ref name="Systems">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref>  was a [[star system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Situated in the [[Cademimu sector]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> it was located in a partially explored region of [[Realspace|space]] off the [[Celanon Spur]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Ajan Kloss]]</ref> It contained the [[gas giant]] [[planet]] [[Ajara]], which was [[orbit]]ed by the [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />
