"'''Assignment: Decoy'''" was a three-episode adventure outline for the ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. It was published in [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 7|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 7]] and written by [[Bill Smith]], with illustrations by [[Shawn Martinbrough]].

The plot was intended as a tie-in to [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'' novel]], and is presented as happening "behind the scenes" during the events of that novel.
