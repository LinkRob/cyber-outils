{{Quote|If not for your general, my family would not have been tortured and killed under the yoke of Imperial oppression!|Chadkol Gee, to [[Rose Tico]], speaking about Leia Organa|<ref name="Allegiance 2" />}}
The [[Quarren]] [[Chadkol Gee]] was a member of a '''family''' from the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]]. During the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[occupation of Mon Cala]], members of Gee's family was subject to [[torture]] and were eventually [[Death|killed]] by the Empire.<ref name="Allegiance 2" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Allegiance]]'' takes place between [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] and [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' places the [[Battle of Batuu]] from ''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge'' in the same year as ''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'', which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places in [[34 ABY]]. Therefore ''Star Wars: Allegiance'' takes place in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] as well.</ref> when [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] [[Mission to Mon Cala|traveled]] to Mon Cala to acquire [[Starship|ships]] for the [[Resistance]]'s [[First Order-Resistance War|fight]] against the [[First Order]], she was greeted by Gee, [[General]] [[Nossor Ri]], and several other Quarren and [[Mon Calamari]] at [[blaster]]point. Gee threatened to kill Organa as a result of the anger he felt towards her, blaming her for what had happened to his family.<ref name="Allegiance 2" />
