'''Evelyne Bolling''' is credited as the materials manager for ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''. She also worked on ''[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron II: Rogue Leader]]'', ''[[Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided]]'', ''Galaxies''<nowiki>'</nowiki> many expansion packs, ''[[Star Wars: Battlefront II]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War: Forces of Corruption]]'', ''[[LEGO Star Wars II: The Original Trilogy]]'', ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]''.
