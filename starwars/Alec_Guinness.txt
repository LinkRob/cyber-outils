[[Wikipedia:Knight Bachelor|Sir]] '''Alec Guinness''' [[Wikipedia:Order of the Companions of Honour|CH]] [[Wikipedia:Order of the British Empire|CBE]] ([[April 2]], [[1910s#1914|1914]] &ndash; [[August 5]], [[2000]]) was an English actor who played [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in the [[original trilogy]].

Guinness won an Academy Award with the role of Lt. Col. Nicholson in [[Wikipedia: The Bridge on the River Kwai|''The Bridge on the River Kwai'']] in [[1950s#1957|1957]].<ref name="Year By Year" />

==Biography==
===Original trilogy===
[[File:HamillGuinness-MOSW.png|left|thumb|250x250px|Guinness enjoys a lighthearted moment with [[Mark Hamill]] on the set of ''Star Wars''.]]
Alec Guinness was born on [[April 2]], [[1910s#1914|1914]].<ref name="This Week! Ep10" /> During the casting process for the part of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] for [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], director [[George Lucas]] was looking for an actor who brought a certain authority to the role and could be powerful and gentle. Lucas found these qualities in Guinness,<ref name="Remembered" /> who joined the cast because of the film's sense of moral good and the studio's doubling of his initial salary offer.<ref name="Cancer Today">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cancertodaymag.org/Pages/Spring2012/alec-guinness-liver-cancer.aspx|text=Alec Guinness, Reluctant Intergalactic Icon|work=[https://www.cancertodaymag.org Cancer Today]|archivedate=20200722153701}}</ref> Lucas wanted Kenobi to die, however, Guinness objected to his character's death. Lucas then explained that Kenobi would have little to do after the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' escaped the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], which conviced Guinness to allow the death.<ref name="Making of">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref> Guinness was nominated for an [[Wikipedia:Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor|Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor]] for his role in the film.<ref name="Oscars">{{WebCite|url=https://www.oscars.org/oscars/ceremonies/1978|text=The 50th Academy Awards &#124; 1978|work=[[Wikipedia:Academy Awards|Academy Awards]]|archivedate=20241229053923}}</ref> He reprised his role as a [[Force spirit|force ghost]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref> and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> Guinness' contract gave him 2.25% of the director's cut. He viewed ''A New Hope'' as "fairy-tale rubbish," and he disliked being associated with the role of Kenobi.<ref name="Cancer Today" /> Despite this, [[Brian Blessed]], an actor who met Guinness many times, stated that Guinness was "madly in love" with the ''Star Wars'' films.<ref name="Blessed">{{YouTube|video=wTJ3DSNP6Dg|text=Boss Nass Warned George Lucas about Jar Jar Binks (Brian Blessed Interview)|channel=channel/UCKhZplFZ1QaLqA_fCTVlU_g|name=The Geeknd [Jamie Stangroom]|archivedate=20201103070721}}</ref>

===Legacy===
{{Quote|I always have the acting challenge of playing a younger Alec Guinness, and that's my great joy in doing this, is to feel like I'm moving towards becoming Alec Guinness, you know. That's always where I'm trying to do.|Ewan McGregor discussing playing Obi-Wan Kenobi in ''Obi-Wan Kenobi''|<ref name="7 Questions">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@VanityFair|name=Vanity Fair|link=Wikipedia:Vanity Fair (magazine)|video=kWpCRYBq6kM|text=Ewan McGregor, Hayden Christensen & Moses Ingram Answer 7 Star Wars Questions|archivedate=20220518174951}}</ref>}}
On [[August 5]], [[2000]], Guinness died from [[Wikipedia:Liver cancer|liver cancer]] at [[Wikipedia:King Edward VII's Hospital|King Edward VII Hospital]] in [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="Cancer Today" /> When [[Ewan McGregor]] played Kenobi in the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> he came up with the idea for he and the crew to look at pictures of Guinness in ''A New Hope'' to bridge the gap between the two actors. Also, his hair and beard were grayed a little bit to get closer to Guinness's portrayal.<ref name="Hair Breakdown">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@entertainmentweekly|name=Entertainment Weekly|link=Wikipedia:Entertainment Weekly|video=nYRcBE17jks|text=Ewan McGregor on Obi-Wan Kenobi's Ever Changing Hair Styles|archivedate=20220622010438}}</ref>

Edited audio of Guinness saying the word "afraid" was used for Kenobi to say "[[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]" in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="GuinnessTFA">{{WebCite|url=http://www.ew.com/article/2015/12/20/jj-abrams-reveals-obi-wan-and-yoda-are-star-wars-force-awakens|text=Obi-Wan and Yoda are secretly in ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''|author=Anthony Breznican|date=2015-12-20|work=''[[Wikipedia:Entertainment Weekly|Entertainment Weekly]]''|archivedate=20180319042421}}</ref>

When [[Joby Harold]] was writing the [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi|''Obi-Wan Kenobi'']] television series, he wanted to understand the story of what happened to Kenobi between McGregor and Guinness' portrayal of him.<ref name="17 year">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/tv/obi-wan-kenobi-ewan-mcgregor-hayden-christensen-cover-story|text=Inside the 17-year journey to reunite Ewan McGregor and Hayden Christensen for ''Obi-Wan Kenobi''|author=Dalton Ross|date=2022-03-10|work=''[[Wikipedia:Entertainment Weekly|Entertainment Weekly]]''|archivedate=20220310151626}}</ref> When McGregor was playing Kenobi, he made it seem like the character would grow up to be Guinness by trying to sound and feel like Guinness, but he didn't want to do an impersonation. To relearn his Kenobi voice, McGregor listened to a sound file that has all of Guinness' lines from ''A New Hope''.<ref name="Rotten Tomatoes TV">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@RottenTomatoesTV|name=Rotten Tomatoes|link=Wikipedia:Rotten Tomatoes|video=K-BiOMIR6NM|text='Obi-Wan Kenobi' Star Ewan McGregor Explains Why He Sounds So 'Alec Guinness-y' &#124; Rotten Tomatoes TV|archivedate=20220527155120}}</ref> McGregor imagined how Guinness would say the lines before filming a scene.<ref name="Kino Plus">{{YouTube|video=xQ7OZxXNgKA|text=Star Wars Spin-Off OBI-WAN KENOBI: Ewan McGregor, Hayden Christensen, Moses Ingram & Deborah Chow|channel=@KinoPlus|name=Kino Plus|archivedate=20220522190720}}</ref>
