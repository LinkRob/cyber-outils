A '''burn kit''' was a small [[medkit]] that [[Jedi Initiate]]s and [[Padawan]]s were given to treat minor burns caused by [[lightsaber]] training accidents. It included a soothing gel that could be applied to an inflamed patch of skin. In [[41 BBY]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] used his burn kit to treat his wrist injured by a [[demon squid]].<ref name="Padawan" />
