'''Artisan's Row''' was an alley of the [[capital]] [[city]] [[Myrra]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. It was located next to Myrra's [[Market District (Myrra)|Market District]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[human]] [[Temmin Wexley]] operated a [[Temmin's|junk shop]] in the alley.<ref name="Outlaws" />
