The '''Arg'garok''' was a type of two-handed [[vibro-ax]] wielded by [[Gamorrean]] [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], warriors and other [[species]].

==Description==
Based on the traditional [[Gamorrean war ax|Gamorrean war-ax]], the Arg'garok was a massive vibro-ax capable of inflicting horrific wounds.<ref name="GaG" /> Its presence alone was able to make a [[Gamorrean Warrior]] seem even more fearsome.<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|230|Launchpad}}</ref> The weapon required two hands to wield. Gamorrean warriors, especially those hired out to [[crime lord]]s throughout [[Hutt Space]] and surrounding areas, favored these heavy and ungainly weapons. Measuring a [[meter]] and half in length, they required tremendous strength to properly wield in combat. In spite of the weapons' association with the Gamorreans, Arg'garoks were valued as prestige weapons and were often found in the hands of [[Barabel]] and [[Houk]] [[bodyguard]]s in the employ of the [[Hutt]] [[kajidic]]s. More physical and belligerent Hutts were thought to have brandished such weapons themselves.<ref name="GaG" />
