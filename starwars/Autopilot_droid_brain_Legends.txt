'''Autopilot droid brains''' were a more advanced and specialized upgrade compared to standard [[Autopilot/Legends|autopilot]] systems. They became popular among [[Smuggler/Legends|smugglers]] and others, as they allowed vehicles or ships to be piloted competently. Additionally, users could communicate with the autopilot droid brain remotely using a [[Beckon call/Legends|beckon call]], enabling them to summon the vehicle or ship from several hundred [[Kilometer/Legends|kilometers]] away.<ref name="Fly Casual">''[[Fly Casual]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The autopilot droid brain was first mentioned in ''[[Fly Casual]]'', a [[2015]] [[roleplaying game]] sourcebook by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fly Casual" />
