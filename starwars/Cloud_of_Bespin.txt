'''Cloud of Bespin''' was an [[alcohol]]ic specialty [[cocktail]] served in the [[Sublight Lounge]] on board the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''<ref name="Cruiser" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The drink could be consumed by [[human]]s and was [[Color|yellow]] with a foamy white topping. The beverage shared its name with<ref name="Cruiser" /> the [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]], home of [[Cloud City]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cloud of Bespin was an alcoholic beverage available in the [[in-universe|real world]] at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> It was last served during the [[September 28]][[September 29|&ndash;]][[September 30|30]] voyage of [[2023]], the final voyage before the closure of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser.<ref name="Official website">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref> The out-of-universe beverage used [[Wikipedia:Rum|rum]], [[Wikipedia:Lemon|lemon]], [[Wikipedia:Orgeat syrup|almond orgreat]], [[Wikipedia:Passiflora edulis|passion fruit]], and [[Wikipedia:Falernum|falernum]]-lemon foam.<ref name="Cruiser" />
