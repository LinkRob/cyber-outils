'''Ciption Lingsnot''' was an [[Adnerem]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] and member of the [[Lingsnots|Lingsnot family]]. He was seen as a joy to his parents, although they expressed concern for him when he occasionally escaped into [[Virulent Reality]]. At every school quarter, Ciption made it into the Dean's List, a pride for the family&mdash;despite the fact that his brother [[Ussto Lingsnot|Ussto]] was the major donor to [[Ciption Lingsnot's school|the school]]&mdash;and was named [[All-Quadrant Detonator]] on [[Iron Filing Field]]. He planned to get a [[Transport License]] during [[Conception Day]].<ref name="MAGL" />
