'''Brene''' was one of several [[Ubese]] [[Exile (punishment)|exiles]] who were part of a [[bounty hunter]] [[Boushh's crew|crew]] led by [[Boushh]]. After Boushh rescued her from a [[rancor]], she was the first member to join him.

==Biography==
Brene was a [[Ubese]]<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" /> [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Tweet" /> [[bounty hunter]] who was a member of the [[Boushh's crew|crew]] led by fellow Ubese [[Boushh]]. By the [[time]] of the [[War of the Bounty Hunters]],<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" /> she<ref name="Script notes" /> had been [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] from [[Uba IV]] for seven [[Standard year|years]] and a member of Boushh's crew for six. Boushh and Brene met when he rescued her from a [[rancor]] and told her to run;<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" /> she then became the first to join him.<ref name="Script notes" /> Fellow exiles [[Ilini]], [[Hareck]], and [[Tans]] also joined the group.<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" />

Boushh's crew, including Brene, were hired by [[Silas Tagge]], the [[House of Tagge|brother]] of [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]], to kill the [[Tagge Corporation]]'s [[Executive Board]] in an attempted coup. Domina and her [[Honor Guard (Domina Tagge)|Honor Guard]] successfully thwarted her brother and she hired the crew for herself.<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" /> Brene and the others protected their employer from another [[Assassination attempt on Domina Tagge|assassination attempt]] by [[Crimson Dawn]] and her nephew, [[Ronen Tagge]]; Lady Domina also defended herself with the [[Tagge protoblade]]. Tans remarked that their employer was terrifying and Brene enthusiastically agreed, leading Ilini to comment that the latter had bad taste.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 28" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I swear, ''Brene'', if your stupid jokes get us killed''&mdash;&raquo;<br />&laquo;''What are you gonna do, ''Hareck? Die mad'' about it?|translated=1|Hareck and Brene|<ref name="Doctor Aphra 28" />}}
Brene had a fondness for puns and telling jokes in the middle of a firefight.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 28" /> She also enjoyed sharing the story of how she met Boushh when he rescued her from a rancor.<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" />

==Skills and abilities==
In addition to [[Ubese (language)|Ubese]], Brene could understand and speak the [[Galactic Basic Standard]] [[language]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 21">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 21|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 21]]</ref>

==Equipment==
Brene wore a [[Color|green]] [[poncho]] and a gray and green [[helmet]] with a matching visor. She armed herself with a pair of small blaster pistols.<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|She will steal your snack and eat them just to see how you'll react|Alyssa Wong describes Brene|<ref name="Script notes" />}}
Brene first appeared in one-shot issue [[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]], part of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] crossover ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'', which was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and illustrated by [[David Baldeón]].<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" /> Wong shared further details from their script notes via [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], including observations about Brene's personality and she/her pronouns. Her name is a reference to the [[W:c:startrek:Breen|Breen]] from [[W:c:startrek:Star Trek|''Star Trek'']].<ref name="Script notes" /> The author later confirmed Brene's gender and shared additional script notes from a scene in the series' [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 28|twenty-eighth issue]].<ref name="Tweet" />
