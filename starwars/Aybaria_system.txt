The '''Aybaria system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Steniplis sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square L-19 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Aybarian]] [[species]].<ref name="Empire's End" />
