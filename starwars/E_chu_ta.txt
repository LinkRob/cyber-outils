'''E chu ta''' was a rude [[Huttese]] phrase<ref name="So You Want">''[[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?]]''</ref> used to curse someone.<ref name="Tribes" />

==History==
{{Quote|E chu ta!''"<br />"''How rude!|E-3PO and C-3PO|<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref>}}
In [[3 ABY]], [[C-3PO]] encountered fellow [[protocol droid]] [[E-3PO (Bespin)|E-3PO]] while at [[Cloud City]] on [[Bespin]]. The former commented how he found it nice to see a "familiar face", only for the latter to respond with "e chu ta" and walk away, to which C-3PO exclaimed was rude.<ref name="Episode V" />

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The phrase "e chu ta" was created by sound designer [[Ben Burtt]] as a [[Huttese]] expletive for the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film, {{Film|V}}. In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity reference book ''[[Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide (real-life book)|Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide]]'', Burrt noted that it is not really appropriate to translate.<ref name="GPBTG">[[Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide (real-life book)|''Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide'']]</ref> The canon junior novel ''[[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?]]'', an adaptation of ''The Empire Strikes Back'' wherein the narrator speaks directly to the reader, states that "It is absolutely not appropriate to translate. Maybe when you're older."<ref name="So You Want" />
