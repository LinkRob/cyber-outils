The '''Cityworld Prototype''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Bounty Hunter (Imara Vex)|standard costume]]. The [[Clothing|outfit]] and [[armor]] featured a [[Color|cream]], dark gray, and yellow color scheme, with a glowing yellow visor on the helmet, as well as a triangular [[shoulder-mounted armament]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cityworld Prototype was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Imara Vex, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.18.0 within the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Fortress Dustbowl]]. Unlike most Costumes in the game, Cityworld Prototype did not share the same rarity as its cosmetic set counterpart, with Fortress Dustbowl instead being an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap." This costume could be unlocked by purchasing the Bounty Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" /> It was initially planned to be called "Megacity Phantom."<ref name="Hunters Panel">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-star-wars-hunters-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: Hunters'' Panel}}</ref>
