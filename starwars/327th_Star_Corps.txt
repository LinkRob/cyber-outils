The '''327th Star Corps''' was a [[Republic Military|military]] [[corps]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], composed of [[clone trooper]]s [[Cloning|bred]] to fight for the [[Galactic Republic]]. Led by [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]] and her trusted [[clone Commander]], [[Bly]], the 327th fought against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Separatist Droid Army|droid armies]] on multiple [[planet]]s across [[the galaxy]]. On the eve of the [[Clone Wars]]' conclusion, a group of 327th [[trooper]]s [[Death|executed]] Secura in accordance with [[Order 66]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:327thStarCorpsEliteArmorPilots-SWL.png|thumb|left|250px|The 327th had elite pilots trained to operate the highly maneuverable Infantry Support Platform.]]
The 327th Star Corps was a [[corps]] within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]] and [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Bly]].<ref name="BFM 21" /> At the start of the [[Clone Wars]], the 327th Star Corps was equipped with the standard [[Phase I clone trooper armor]].<ref name="Jedi Crash">{{TCW|Jedi Crash}}</ref> Later, they discarded their first [[armor]] in favor of the more advanced [[Phase II clone trooper armor|Phase II]] model. The clones customized the new armor with distinctive [[Color|marigold]] markings that identified their affiliation with the 327th. In addition to the colorized pattern design, the 327th soldiers wore [[pauldron]]s and [[kama]]s with their armor.<ref name="Episode III" /> The 327th also comprised of [[clone jetpack trooper]]s.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />

==History==
===Mission to Escander===
After the [[Clone Wars]] began at the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the 327th [[Star]] [[Corps]] became a part of the newly formed [[Grand Army of the Republic]] and was assigned to [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]] and led by [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Bly]].<ref name="Bly" />

During the war, the [[Jedi]] [[Quinlan Vos]] discovered intelligence regarding a new Separatist [[droid]]. Aayla Secura and a [[Aayla Secura's company|company]] of the 327th were sent to the muddy [[planet]] of [[Escander]], where a [[Unidentified Separatist base (Escander)|Separatist base]] was located. Shortly after arriving, Separatist [[battle droid]]s attacked them, resulting in [[Commander]] Bly getting injured. Due to Bly's injuries, [[Unidentified clone trooper (327th Star Corps)|another clone trooper]] took command of the company. Secura ordered her forces, among them the trooper and [[Flash]], to remain in place and proceeded to the Separatist base alone.<ref name="Choose Your Destiny" />

After having no contact with their [[general]], the [[Cloning|clones]] decided to contact Aayla, which resulted in Aayla being detected. Going against orders, they decided to enter the base and attempt to rescue Aayla. Eventually, the clones reach the base and discovered Aayla being interrogated by [[Asajj Ventress]], who revealed that she had leaked the intelligence to lure and capture the Jedi. The clones then activated the remaining droids to distract Ventress and the clones and Aayla Secura escaped to safety.<ref name="Choose Your Destiny" />

===Battle of Quell===
Early during the Clone Wars, members of the 327th Star Corps were aboard three [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]], including Secura's [[flagship]] the [[Liberty (Venator-class)|''Liberty'']], when they engaged multiple Separatist [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]], culminating in the [[Battle of Quell]].<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

[[File:Aayla_Secura_SWZ_Jake_Murray.png|thumb|left|284x284px|Aayla Secura and Bly during the Battle of Quell.]]
During the battle, Republic forces were ultimately overwhelmed and called Jedi General [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his forces for reinforcements. By the time Skywalker's flagship, the ''[[Resolute]]'' arrived, Secura's flagship was the only remaining vessel, and was being boarded by [[B2-RP battle droid|B2-RP super battle droids]]. During the Separatist boarding action, Secura ordered Commander Bly to repel enemy forces. Ultimately, Secura, Bly, and Flash would be rescued by Skywalker, who was joined by his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], and 501st troopers [[Cameron]] and [[Lucky]]. Together, they managed to escape to a [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|''Consular''-class cruiser]], although Skywalker was critically injured in the escape. As the [[cruiser]] attempted to dock with the ''Resolute'' during the battle, it came under attack by [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droid starfighters]] which accidentally activated the ship's [[hyperdrive]], forcing the cruiser to take evasive action to avoid a catastrophic collision with the ''Resolute'' as it jumped into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

===Defense of Maridun===
[[File:Cameron.png|thumb|right|306x306px|Bly leans over a dead Cameron, a soldier from the 501st who helped in the escape from Quell.]]
After avoiding a collision with [[Maridun's star]], the cruiser crash-landed on the planet of [[Maridun]]. After escaping the wreck and establishing a camp, [[Captain]] Rex kept watch over the camp and Skywalker while the others, including [[Cameron]], Flash, and [[Lucky]], sought aide from any possible inhabitants. After encountering [[Mastiff phalone]]s which resulted in the [[death]]s of Cameron, Flash, and Lucky, the group managed to locate a [[Lurmen]] [[Lurmen village|village]] and appealed for help. The leader, [[Tee Watt Kaa]] reluctantly dispatched his son, [[Wag Too]] to accompany Bly and Tano back to the campsite and bring back Skywalker and Rex.<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

Sometime later, a Separatist [[C-9979 landing craft]] arrived, forcing the Republic team to escape and hide in the tall [[grass]]. Separatist General [[Lok Durd]] and his forces ransacked the Lurmen village in search of any Republic contraband.<ref name="Defenders of Peace">{{TCW|Defenders of Peace}}</ref> The 327th later returned to Quell<ref name="Quell card">{{Destiny|set=Way of the Force|cardname=Quell|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/07150}}</ref> between [[20 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Quell card date note">{{Destiny|set=Way of the Force|cardname=Quell|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/07150}} shows the [[327th Star Corps]] with [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], which makes its first chorological appearance in "[[Water War]]," while [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] establishes that [[armor]] was used for the rest of the [[Clone Wars]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of "Water War" to [[20 BBY]] and those of ''Revenge of the Sith'' to [[19 BBY]], the events pictured on the Quell card must occur between those two [[Standard year|years]].</ref> for a new battle, which was fought on Quell's surface under the command of Secura.<ref name="Quell card" />

===Battle of Felucia and Order 66===
[[File:327thStarCorps-ROTS.png|thumb|240x240px|Commander Bly (center) and Lieutenant Galle (second from left) and the 327th execute Order 66.|left]]
In 19 BBY,<ref name="Quell card date note" /> the 327th was [[Battle of Felucia (Outer Rim Sieges)|deployed]] to the planet [[Felucia]] in order to capture [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] member [[Shu Mai]].<ref name="SWCEUE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> The 327th was armed with several vehicles, including [[All Terrain Attack Pod]]s, [[All Terrain Open Transport]]s, and [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s. While advancing through the Felucian jungle towards a [[Confederacy military|Separatist military]] force, the 327th received [[Order 66]] from [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], directing them to execute their [[Jedi]] leader. As a result, Secura was [[Death|fatally]] shot in the back multiple [[time]]s by her own [[trooper]]s, including Bly and [[Clone Lieutenant|Lieutenant]] [[Galle]].<ref name="Episode III" /> The battle went onto end as a victory for the Republic,<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> which was transformed into the [[Galactic Empire]] at the decree of Palpatine shortly thereafter.<ref name="Episode III" /> Felucia was left a ruined husk of its former self, however.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>
