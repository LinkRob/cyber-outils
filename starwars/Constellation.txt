'''Constellations''' were recognizable, arbitrary groupings of [[star]]s as viewed from the surface of a [[planet]]. When [[Ezra Bridger]] underwent a mystical experience in the [[Lothal Jedi Temple]], he apparently entered a [[cave]]rn so massive it had its own night sky, sprinkled with constellations unknown to him.<ref name="Duel with Danger">''[[Ezra's Duel with Danger]]''</ref> Over long periods of [[time]], the configuration of a constellation would appear to change from a viewer's perspective.<ref name="TLJ-TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

[[Amilyn Holdo]]'s [[bracelet]]s were designed after the constellations as seen from the ground of her [[homeworld]] of [[Gatalenta]].<ref name="TLJ-TVD" />
