'''Cheedoan whiskey''' was a type of beverage that was available in the [[Standard year|years]] following the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The [[New Republic]] [[senator]] [[Count]] [[Jogurner]] was quite fond of Cheedoan [[Whiskey|whiskies]], to the point that they were the only subject he could converse about at length.<ref name="Bloodline" /> It shared a name with the [[Cheedoa system]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref>
