An '''arcade machine''' was a type of device that allow an individual to play [[arcade game]]s and could be found in [[cantina]]s and [[arcade hall]]s across [[the galaxy]]. There controls were designed to accommodate a variety of [[species]] with different body sizes and [[limb|hand]] types.<ref name="Art Of Outlaws">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Arcade machines first appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in part two of the two-part episode "[[The Recruit]],"<ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}} </ref> the first episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[October 7]], [[2018]].<ref name="Disney release">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> 

Arcade machines later appeared as optional activities in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. [[Wikipedia:Concept Art|Concept art]] for the arcade machines that appeared in ''Star Wars Outlaws'' was done by [[Adrien Girod]]<ref name="Girod ArtStation">{{ArtStation|subdomain=kurobot|url=projects/98E6Da|name=[[Adrien Girod]]|text=Arcade Games|date=2024-09-30|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/zhGQ2}}</ref> and later was later published in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]''. The design was inspired by real-life [[Wikipedia:Arcade machine|arcade machines]] with the added element of needing to support a variety of [[species]] with different hand shapes and body types.<ref name="Art Of Outlaws" /> The machine was realized in the game was done by Level Artist [[Julien Payen]], with visual effects done by [[Simon Romain]] and the machine's UI design done by [[Yoann Carré]] under UI lead [[Séverine Mastroleonardo]].<ref name="Payen ArtStation">{{ArtStation|subdomain=jpayen|url=projects/2B9arJ|name=[[Julien Payen]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws - Kessel Sabacc|quote=This two fully playable arcade games were an absolut blast to work on! I was responsible for creating the two arcade console, and most of what you see inside the minigame, level art and material. Séverine Mastroleonardo - UI Lead. Yoann Carré - UI Design. Adrien Girod - Lead Concept Artist. Simon Romain - VFX Artist |date=2024-09-30|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/AklRJ}}</ref>
