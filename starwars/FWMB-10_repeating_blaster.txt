The '''FWMB-10 repeating blaster''', also known as the '''megablaster''',<ref name="Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was a [[repeating blaster]] manufactured by the [[Sonn-Blas Corporation]].<ref name="Visual" />

==Characteristics==
[[File:FWMB10RepeatingBlaster-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|300px|The repeating blaster set]]
Produced by the [[Sonn-Blas Corporation]], the FWMB-10s used by [[First Order]] [[First Order heavy trooper|megablaster heavy assault troopers]]&mdash;who earned their name from their use of the blaster<ref name="Visual" />&mdash; featured integrated stands that could fold out from the blaster,<ref name="Episode VII" /> as well as [[Barrel-shroud|barrel cooling shrouds]]. [[Light Infantry Utility Vehicle]]s also used pintle-mounted megablasters as their primary armament.<ref name="Visual" /> These forward-mounted<ref name="Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> FWMB-10s had steadying grips and trigger levers, data and power feeds, calibration adjustment, elevation gear, a collimator ring, and collimator sleeves.<ref name="Visual" /> It was powered by an [[Eksoan]] [[Class-5B1 duplex power generator]].<ref name="LJ:VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The [[FWMB-10K]] was a smaller variant of the FWMB-10.<ref name="BF">{{EA|url=games/starwars/battlefront/star-wars-battlefront-2/classes/heavy-trooper|text=Heavy Trooper Class - Star Wars™ Battlefront™ II}}</ref>
