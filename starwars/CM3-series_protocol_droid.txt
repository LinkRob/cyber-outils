The '''CM3-series protocol droid''' was a model of [[protocol droid]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> with a humanoid frame. [[TAY-0]] was a refurbished CM3-series protocol droid equipped for racing who was destroyed and rebuilt<ref name="Faster" /> sometime between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|204}}</ref>
