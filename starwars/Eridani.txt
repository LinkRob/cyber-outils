'''Eridani''' was a major [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] [[City/Legends|establishment]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Sylvia IV]]. In [[1125 BBY]], the [[Disaster of Arrakad]] led to the massacre of the [[Human/Legends|Human]] population of Eridani, forcing the Republic to launch a massive evacuation of [[Jedar]] and [[Lorik (city)|Lorik]] before quarantining the planet.<ref name="LVN" />
