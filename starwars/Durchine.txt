'''Durchine''' was one of several individuals sheltered by [[Yahenna Laxo]] of the [[Gray Syndicate]] due to being wanted by the [[Galactic Empire]] for petty crimes. The Gray Syndicate was a minor player in the crime scene of the<ref name="justice" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="datalothal">{{Databank|lothal|Lothal}}</ref> [[planet]] of [[Lothal]], though Laxo liked to exaggerate its importance. He would only shelter individuals so long as they had the [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to pay him.<ref name="justice" />

During a crackdown of criminal activities on Lothal, the Empire struck one of Laxo's warehouses and two of his [[gambling]] clubs. As a result of this, Laxo had [[Merei Spanjaf]] visit several of his charges. She was to tell Durchine along with [[Apapaba]], [[Holshef]], [[Kinlo]] and [[Marhas]] that they needed to be packed and ready, as [[Rosey]], another member of the group, would soon move them to new locations.<ref name="justice" />

==Behind the scenes==
Durchine is mentioned ''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]'', the third title of the [[Star Wars Rebels: Servants of the Empire|''Servants of the Empire'']] quartet by [[Jason Fry]], as criminals hidden by Yahenna Laxo. His exact crimes were not stated, but the title describes that those sheltered by Laxo other than Holshef were facing gambling debts or other self-inflicted troubles.
