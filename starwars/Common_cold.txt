The '''common cold''' or simply '''cold''' was an illness that could be caught by [[wampa]]s on [[Hoth]]. Snot would come out of the sick wampa's [[nose]] and it would start sneezing.<ref name="Wampa" /> During or after [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{GOCDate}}</ref> the [[Safari droid]] [[SF-R3]] "Aree" of the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] was dispatched to Hoth in order to treat a wampa which had been sickened with the common cold. After first taking the wampa's [[temperature]], Aree tried to expose the wampa to heat in various ways, as this was the treatment for most species. Eventually, Aree lit a [[fire]] in the Wampa's [[cave]], causing the wampa to sneeze, which in turn caused [[Water|snow]] to fall from the ceiling onto the wampa and Aree. The cold temperatures of the snow instantly cured the wampa, causing Aree to realize that exposure to cold was the treatment for a Wampa with the common cold.<ref name="Wampa" />
