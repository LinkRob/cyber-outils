{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
CZ-D16 appeared in "[[The Duel: Payback]]", a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kamikaze Douga]] and [[ANIMA (company)|ANIMA]], was released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. Before the short's release, they were first mentioned in a Japanese [[StarWars.com]] article revealing the cast of the series. They were voiced by [[Tokuyoshi Kawashima]] in the original Japanese and by [[Gary Littman]] in the English dub.<ref name="JPN Cast">{{Disney|subdomain=starwars|domain=co.jp|url=news/20250926_01|text=日本語オリジナル版＜本予告＞が全世界解禁！『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』Volume3|date=2026-09-26}}</ref>
