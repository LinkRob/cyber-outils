'''Cosdra''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a loyal member of the [[White Worms]] gang on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. She worked as a sentry for the gang's [[Den of the White Worms|den]] in [[Coronet City]], and in [[13 BBY]], she was present when the [[scrumrat]] [[Han Solo|Han]] escaped from the gang's leader, [[Lady]] [[Proxima]].

==Biography==
Cosdra was [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] from the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] who served as a [[scrumrat]] in the [[White Worms]] gang of [[Coronet City]]. By the time that she was one of the older scrumrats in the gang, Cosdra's loyalty had earned her a number of perks, including sentry duty near the surface of [[Den of the White Worms]], which allowed her access to slightly fresher air then most other gang members.<ref name="SoloOG" />

In the [[Standard year|year]] [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' that take place on [[Corellia]] to six years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]]; therefore, the events on Corellia in ''Solo'' must take place in [[13 BBY]].</ref> Cosdra was present in the den when fellow scrumrat [[Han Solo|Han]] was brought before [[Lady]] [[Proxima]], leader of the gang. Han, who had returned from a trade with no goods for Proxima, claimed to be holding a [[Thermal detonator]] above his head, at which point Cosdra got down on her knees for cover. However, Han actually only held a rock, which he used to break a shutter behind the gang leader and bathe her in light, which burned her skin. Using the distraction, Han then pushed through the other scrumrats and escaped.<ref name="Solo" />

==Personality and traits==
Cosdra was a loyal member of the White Worms<ref name="SoloOG" /> with [[Color|orange]] skin and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="Solo" />

==Equipment==
Cosdra wore dark gray [[Salt|brine]]-stained [[Clothing|robes]] with gray pants and wrist wraps as well as gray and red head wrappings. She made use of a [[Breath mask|breather mask]] and used a white pipe as a makeshift weapon.<ref name="SoloOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cosdra first appeared in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> The character was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" />
