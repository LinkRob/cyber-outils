The '''Cylo Applicator'''<ref name="Hunters" /> was a long-range [[dart gun]]<ref name="Reveal" /> owned by the [[Rodian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Skora]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Underworld Applicator|standard applicator]]. This ornate applicator featured a [[Color|gold]], blue, and gray color scheme, with a patterned stock and red cloth wrapped around certain components.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cylo Applicator was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Skora's Underworld Applicator, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cylo Chemist]]. Unlike most cosmetic sets in the game which are added at the same time, the Cylo Chemist was added within the 0.18.0 update, while this Weapon Wrap was not.<ref name="Hunters" />
