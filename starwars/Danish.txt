'''Danish''' was a type of circular dough-based dish.<ref name="PnF" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{PnFDate|1}}</ref> while on board of the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' spacecraft, stormtrooper [[Buford]] commented on [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]'s hairstyle, with two large distinct circular buns on the sides of her head, comparing them to the dish, by calling her the "Danish-head".<ref name="PnF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Danish pastry is a multilayered, laminated dough-based dish originating from Denmark and Austria. It was first mentioned in the non-canonical television special ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'', which aired in 2014.
