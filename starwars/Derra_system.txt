{{Quote|One of the greatest Imperial successes of the Galactic Civil War came via the destruction of a significant rebel convoy in the Derra system and this was a direct outcome of the Ubiqtorate operations.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
The '''Derra system''' was an [[Expansion Region]] [[star system]] in the [[Mikaster sector]] that contained [[Derra IV]]. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] moved supply [[convoy]]s through the system during the [[Galactic Civil War]] until [[Vice Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] led a [[Ambush of a rebel convoy at Derra|military action]] against them near Derra IV that destroyed a significant supply convoy in one of the greatest Imperial successes of the war.

==Description==
{{Quote|It was Vice Admiral Rae Sloane&mdash;a figure of huge importance in the Empire's future&mdash;onboard the Star Destroyer ''Vigilance'', in cooperation with remnants of the 7th Fleet, who were able to establish the likeliest candidates for rebel shipping routes in the Expansion Region and narrow the list down to Derra.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
The Derra system was a [[star system]]<ref name="Rise" /> in the [[Mikaster sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Rise" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]]<ref name="TrailingSectors" /> that contained the terrestrial celestial body<ref name="RisingStorm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> [[Derra IV]].<ref name="Rise" /> It was located at the coordinates N-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="TFABG" />

==History==
===High Republic Era===
{{Quote|Master Sinube of the Derra Outpost has dispatched reinforcements, a drift of Vectors led by Porter Engle.|Stellan Gios|<ref name="RisingStorm" />}}
In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="RisingDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], an [[Derra Outpost|outpost]] of the [[Jedi Order]] on Derra IV was [[marshal]]led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Tera Sinube]]. After being contacted by Master [[Stellan Gios]], Sinube dispatched a [[drift]] of [[Jedi Vector|Vectors]] led by Jedi Master [[Porter Engle]] in order to reinforce [[Jedi]] and [[Galactic Republic]] forces being [[First Battle of Cyclor|attacked]] by [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s at the [[Cyclor Shipyards]].<ref name="RisingStorm" />

===Supply hotspot===
{{Quote|For some time, the rebels had been moving supplies through the Derra system on the Outer Rim. These convoys contained a variety of weapons, equipment, food, and medicines that were required for various rebel cells across the galaxy. More importantly, over eighty percent of the stockpiles were allocated to equipping the hidden rebel base.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:Admiral_Sloane_SWArmada_Tiziano_Baracchi.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Wearing a gray Imperial officer's uniform and cap, Rae Sloane stands with her arms cross infront of a ship's viewport. Through the viewport, B-wing starfighters and TIE fighters can be seen in a dogfight over a gray world with an explosion behind one ship near to Sloane's vessel.|Rae Sloane led a military action in the Derra system.]]
By [[3 ABY]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] had been moving supply [[convoy]]s carrying a variety of [[weapon]]s, equipment, [[Foodstuff|food]], and medicines through the Derra system and onto the various rebel cells that required them across [[the galaxy]]. Eighty percent of these stockpiles were specifically allocated to equipping the hidden rebel headquarters at [[Echo Base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. [[Vice Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy]] and the remnants of the Imperial [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|7th Fleet]] were able to establish the Derra system as the likeliest candidate for the Alliance's shipping route through the Expansion Region. Both Sloane and the [[fleet]] had been partially sidelined from mainstream Imperial operations at the [[time]].<ref name="Rise" />

As a direct outcome of operations carried out by the Empire's [[Ubiqtorate]], Sloane led Imperial forces, including the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] ''[[Dark Omen]]'' and the [[Interdictor]] cruiser ''[[Retention]]'', from aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] [[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|''Vigilance'']] in a [[Ambush of a rebel convoy at Derra|military action]] against the rebels near Derra IV.<ref name="Rise" />

===A win for the Empire===
{{Quote|Though the Rebel Alliance were not able to ascertain exactly what happened at Derra IV, we know now that the ''Vigilance'' took the lead&mdash;given it was Sloane's plan&mdash;but was ably supported by the ''Dark Omen'' and, most importantly, the Interdictor cruiser ''Retention'' that helped isolate the convoy and enable its destruction.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:Imperial_Inderdictor_DB.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=An Imperial Interdictor cruiser sits against the black of space. Shaped like an arrow, a low, flat command tower sits at its rear with four domes around it on the ship's surface.|The ''Retention'', an Interdictor cruiser (pictured), played a significant role in the action in the Derra system.]]
The Imperials ambushed the rebel [[Renegade Squadron]] and then, using the ''Retention'', they isolated a significant supply convoy and destroyed it. Although the location of Echo Base was not uncovered, the Imperial fleet considered the action a near-perfect victory entirely of their own merit. For her part in the ambush, Sloane was promoted and given a place on the [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military]], with the 7th Fleet also reincorporated back into mainstream Imperial operations as a result.<ref name="Rise" />

A file on Sloane and the 7th Fleet's work to narrow down the convoy's location to the system was stored in the "Military Operations" section of the [[Imperial Archives]] and was later recovered from the damaged [[computer]] core of the ''Vigilance''.<ref name="Rise" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Starcruiserdate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> a dot representing the Derra system was displayed as part of a partial [[Star map|map]] of the [[galaxy]] on several [[Viewscreen|screens]] aboard [[Chandrila Star Line]]'s [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''.<ref name="GS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref>

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned the destruction of the convoy in the Derra system in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' and called it one of the greatest Imperial successes of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He noted that the rebels were not able to ascertain exactly what had happened during the battle but cited what information he did have to a file titled [[Interrogations 143]] in the "Imperial Prisoners" section of the [[New Republic Archives]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Derra system was first mentioned as a point labeled simply "Derra" on a map of the galaxy included in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' line.<ref name="TFABG" /> Its first [[in-universe]] appearance then came as an unlabeled dot on a map viewable in [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]], an immersive roleplaying experience at [[W:c:disney:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]] in Florida<ref name="GS" /> that opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]],<ref name="31 Things">{{SW|url=news/31-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=31 Things We Learned in an Exclusive Preview of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}}</ref> and had its final voyage from [[September 28]] to [[September 30|30]], [[2023]].<ref name="StarcruiserClosure">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=destinations/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser}}</ref> The system was then first identified fully in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]]. The book erroneously mentions the Derra system as being in both the Expansion Region and Outer Rim Territories.<ref name="Rise" /> This article assumes the former to be correct due to the placement of Derra in the Expansion Region in prior material.<ref name="TFABG" />

[[File:BattleDerraIV.jpg|thumb|right|300px|alt=A chaotic mess of ships battle around an Imperial Star Destoryer in space, with explosions engulfing several X-wing starfighters near various TIE fighters. In the background the brown striped surface of a planet is visible.|The Derra system's ''Star Wars'' Legends depiction in ''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' 25]]
The [[Derra system/Legends|star system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared, albeit unnamed, in the first installment of the [[The Empire Strikes Back (radio)|''The Empire Strikes Back'' radio drama]], written by [[Brian Daley]]<ref name="RadioDrama">[[The Empire Strikes Back (radio)|''The Empire Strikes Back'' radio drama]]</ref> and broadcast on [[February 14]], [[1983]].<ref name="Princess Leia and Han Solo's Wedding">{{ThisWeek|gaaZjNoO5d4|Princess Leia and Han Solo's Wedding, The Book of Boba Fett Finale, and More!|sw_url=video/princess-leia-and-han-solo-s-wedding-the-book-of-boba-fett-finale-and-more}}</ref> It was then identified in the fourth part of the novella "[[Side Trip]]," which was written by [[Timothy Zahn]] and published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 13|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 13]] in [[May]] [[1997]].<ref name="Side Trip">{{JournalCite|13|Side Trip}}</ref>

The [[Star system/Legends|system]] then appeared visually for the first time as part of flashbacks in the [[X-Wing Rogue Squadron 25|twenty-fifth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Rogue Squadron]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series. The issue was written by [[Michael A. Stackpole]], illustrated by [[Steve Crespo]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Xwing25">[[X-Wing Rogue Squadron 25|''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' 25]]</ref> on [[December 3]], 1997.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54670/star_wars_x-wing_rogue_squadron_1995_25|text=Star Wars: X-Wing Rogue Squadron (1995) #25}}</ref>
