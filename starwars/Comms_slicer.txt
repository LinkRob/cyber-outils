A '''comms slicer''' was a bespoke device created by [[Luuda Fluxion]] to eavesdrop on [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] audio transmissions. Hiding the device within a [[auction art piece|piece of art]] smuggled into an art auction during a [[podracing|podrace]] on [[Nar Shaddaa]], Luuda's [[Sebulba's crew|crew]], led by the [[Dug]] [[Sebulba]], managed to get the device close to the Empire's local communications nexus. Although [[Hutt colosseum heist|their heist]] failed, the device worked.<ref name="SWBV" />
