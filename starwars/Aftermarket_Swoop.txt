The '''Aftermarket Swoop''' was a bodily configuration owned by the [[droideka]] [[Team Slingshot droideka|member]] of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]] that she could swap for her standard [[Beloved Paintwork|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This configuration featured a dark metallic [[Color|gray]] frame, with plating bearing an orange-to-red gradient, as well as custom [[Limb|arm]] [[blaster]]s, shaped to resemble the [[engine]]s of a [[swoop]]. Both the components on these blasters, as well as the [[sensor]]s on the droideka's head, featured an orange glow.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aftermarket Swoop was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Aftermarket Racer]]. Unlike most Weapon Wraps in the game, Aftermarket Swoop did not share the same rarity as its cosmetic set counterpart, with Aftermarket Racer instead being an "Epic" rarity "Costume."<ref name="Hunters" />
