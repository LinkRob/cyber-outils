'''Amy Beth Christenson''' is a senior concept designer at [[Lucasfilm Animation]]. She worked as a concept artist for the video games ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II]]'',<ref name="Website" /> [[Topps]] trading cards, and other things.<ref name="Website" /> In [[2011]], she started working on ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' also as a concept artist, designing concepts for the [[Pyke/Legends|Pyke species]] and the [[Pyke blaster pistol/Legends|Pyke blaster pistol]] for the episode "[[Eminence (episode)|Eminence]]."<ref name="EminEG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/eminence-episode-guide|text="Eminence" Episode Guide &ndash; ''The Clone Wars''}}</ref>
