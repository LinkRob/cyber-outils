'''Forest nymphs''' were a type of magical creature. The [[Jedi]]-in-hiding [[Kanan Jarrus]] considered some [[Gender|women]] similar to forest nymphs.<ref name="AND">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Forest nymphs were first mentioned in the novel [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']], which was written by [[John Jackson Miller]] and published in [[2014]].
