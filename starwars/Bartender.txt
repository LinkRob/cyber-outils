A '''bartender''', also known as a '''tavern-keeper''',<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> '''barman''',<ref name="LCZA">{{InsiderCite|156|Last Call at the Zero Angle}}</ref> '''barkeep''',<ref name="LF">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> or '''mixologist''',<ref name="Survivor" /> was an individual who ran or owned a [[cantina]] or a bar in some other kind of establishment.

==History==
{{Quote|If you have a drink in your hand, you don't need me for nothing.|Wuher|<ref name="We Don't Serve Their Kind Here">{{StoryCite|story=We Don't Serve Their Kind Here|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>}}
[[File:Cabuck-2025ChromeSapphire.png|thumb|180px|left|Cabuck was a bartender on Ueda in 132 BBY.]]
During the Imperial Era on [[Lothal]], the [[Ithorian]] [[Jho]] ran [[Old Jho's Pit Stop]], a [[cantina]] and repair shop. Jho worked the bar, and some of his frequent guests were the [[Spectres]], a rebel cell made up of [[Kanan Jarrus]], [[Hera Syndulla]], [[Garazeb Orrelios]], [[Sabine Wren]], [[Ezra Bridger]], and the droid [[C1-10P]].<ref name="Empire Day">{{Rebels|Empire Day (episode)}}</ref> Jho ran the bar until his execution, upon which it was given to [[Valen Rudor]]. While under Rudor's management, the bar was popular among Imperials.<ref name="The Occupation">{{Rebels|The Occupation}}</ref>

Also during the Imperial Era, the [[Wookiee]] [[Chalmun]] owned a cantina in the [[Tatooine]] spaceport [[Mos Eisley]] named [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]]. He employed three bartenders, [[Wuher]], [[Ackmena]], and [[Husklf]].<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> The [[human]] [[Gender|male]] Wuher, who ran the bar during the day, had a gruff personality and a strong dislike for droids.<ref name="A New Hope" /> The night-shift bartender was the human female Ackmena, whose friendly manner made her more popular than Wuher with the patrons. Husklf was a [[Nimbanel]], and unlike Wuher and Ackmena, who worked the public bar, Husklf was staffed in Chalmun's private bar.<ref name="Complete Locations" />

Around the time of the [[Cold War]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[First Order]], the female [[Blutopian]] [[Oga Garra]] owned and operated [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. Oga's Cantina sold a wide array of alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks.<ref name="Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]</ref>

[[Z'Vk'Thkrkza]], also known as Aunt Z, was a female [[Gilliand]] who lived on the refueling platform [[Colossus]] on the planet [[Castilon]]. She owned a [[Cantina|tavern]] called [[Aunt Z's Tavern|Aunt Z's]] on the Colossus during the time of the Cold War.<ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref>
