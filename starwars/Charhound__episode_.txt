"'''Charhound'''" is the tenth episode of the webseries ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Creatures]]''.<ref name="SWKids" /> The episode was released on [[StarWarsKids.com]] on [[November 11]], [[2021]],<ref name="Episode" /> alongside the series' ninth episode, "[[Nexu (episode)|Nexu]]."<ref name="SWKids" />

==Official description==
''Aree has to out-wit the fiery breath of a charhound in order to play a friendly game of fetch.''

==Plot summary==
The [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] sends [[SF-R3]] ("Aree") and [[CAM]] to [[Elphrona]] to find out whether [[charhound]]s play fetch. Aree and CAM walks into a cave where they meet a charhound, who plays fetch with a stick that Aree throws. The creature grabs the stick and burns it with its breath. Aree throws more objects which the creature burns with its fiery maw.

After [[Aree's ship|SF-R3's starship]] breaks down, Aree laments that they are out of ideas. After urinating fire, the charhound returns to its cave where Aree discovers heat resistant crystals. Aree plays fetch with the charhound using the heat resistant crystals, gaining the creature's affection. Aree lists several fun facts including that charhounds are quite friendly despite their dragon-like breath.
