'''Clone gunners''' were [[clone trooper]]s that manned vehicle-mounted weapons or [[artillery]], wearing specialized [[clone trooper armor]] throughout their service. Clone [[gunner]]s participated in multiple battles against Separatist forces, including in the [[Battle of Christophsis]] and the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]]. [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Stew (clone gunner)|Stew]] served aboard the ''[[Endurance]]'' and at one point, participated in the instruction of a group of [[clone cadet]]s from the [[Clone Youth Brigade]].

==Overview==
===Description===
[[File:Clone-Gunner-P2-Pauldron.png|thumb|left|170px|A clone gunner in Phase II armor aboard a HAVw A6 Juggernaut.]]
Clone gunners were specialized [[clone trooper]] operators that were trained in the usage of [[artillery]],<ref name="Clone Trooper Armor">{{Databank|clone-trooper-armor|Clone Trooper Armor}}</ref> other heavy ground weapons,<ref name="Padawan Lost" /> and [[starship]] armament.<ref name="Death Trap" /> [[Gunner]]s relied on their specialized training to anticipate enemy tactics and effectively target their opponents.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> They wore a variant of the [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] early during the war, which contained reinforced chest [[armor]] and [[helmet]]s with additional noise insulation to protect against the recoil and sound of the weapons they operated. Additionally, standard shoulder armor was replaced with reinforced shoulder pieces. Commonly, clone gunners had [[Color|dark green]] reinforced armor, with brownish helmet protection,<ref name="Clone Trooper Armor" /> however, clone gunners were also known to use [[Color|orange]] reinforced armor, with orange helmet protective gear.<ref name="Beam Cannon">{{Legion|set=TX-130 Saber-class Fighter Tank Unit Expansion|cardname=TX-130 Beam Cannon Turret}}</ref>

This armor was usually modified to represent unit affiliation. The clone gunners of the [[7th Sky Corps]] could customize their armor in [[Color|orange]] and brown [[desert]] camouflage.<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> Clone gunners of a clone [[tank]] [[division]]'s [[Unidentified tank squadron|ground squadron]] that operated alongside the [[104th Battalion]] customized their armor with dark colors and the stylized mouth of a creature on their helmet.<ref name="Padawan Lost" />

Once the [[Republic Military]] transitioned to [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], clone gunners were also provided with the upgraded [[clone trooper armor]]. One variant of the armor included helmet modifications, as well as a large chest and right shoulder [[pauldron]]. [[Clone major]]s were equipped with this Phase II clone gunner armor.<ref name="Complete Vehicles NE">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> Additionally, another type of Phase II armor was used by clone gunners, which was a direct upgrade of the previous set of armor. This armor set included the additional helmet insulation equipment, as well as reinforced chest and shoulder armor.<ref name="TX-130">{{Legion|set=TX-130 Saber-class Fighter Tank Unit Expansion|cardname=TX-130 Saber-class Fighter Tank}}</ref>

Clone gunners were trained in armed and [[unarmed combat]], as well as military tactics.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Like most clone troopers,<ref name="clonetroopers">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref> clone gunners were 1.83 [[meter]]s, or 6 [[Foot|feet]] tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Role==
[[File:Clone-gunner-P2-tank.png|thumb|right|250px|A clone gunner in Phase II armor mans a turret aboard a TX-130 ''Saber''-class fighter tank.]]
In the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], clone gunners manned the main [[turret]] [[cannon]] of [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s,<ref name="Padawan Lost" /> which were designated as [[Firefont 04 mass driver cannon]]s.<ref name="AT-ACT Deluxe Book">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: AT-ACT Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]''</ref> Clone gunners also operated [[AV-7 Anti-vehicle Artillery Cannon]]s in many battles,<ref name="TCW film" /> as well as the light [[laser cannon]] turret<ref name="Laser Turret">{{Legion|set=TX-130 Saber-class Fighter Tank Unit Expansion|cardname=TX-130 Twin Laser Turret}}</ref> or the [[KB-50a beam cannon]] that were situated on [[TX-130 Saber-class fighter tank|TX-130 ''Saber''-class fighter tanks]].<ref name="Beam Cannon" />

Clone gunners also crewed [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]]s, serving under the Juggernaut's clone [[major]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles NE" /> In the [[Republic Navy]], clone gunners operated [[capital ship]] weapon [[turret]]s to defend their starship against enemy [[starfighter]] attacks.<ref name="Death Trap">{{TCW|Death Trap}}</ref>

==History==
===Christophsis, Teth, and Bothawui===
Clone gunners saw action within the [[Galactic Republic]] early during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], participating in the [[Battle of Christophsis]]. There, four clone gunners, each operating an AV-7 artillery piece, held Separatist forces at bay with the support of ground troops and tanks from the [[501st Legion]] and the [[7th Sky Corps]], respectively under the command of [[Jedi General]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Gunners subsequently participated in the [[Battle of Teth]] with the 501st's [[Torrent Company]], as they manned the [[cannon]]s of two [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s. Both gunners were [[Death|killed]] during the engagement, the first when a [[walker]] was destroyed, and the second was shot out of the cannon and fell to his death.<ref name="TCW film" />

During the [[Battle of Bothawui]], the [[Navy|naval]] [[Skywalker's fleet|task force]] of [[General]] Skywalker deployed a large contingent of AT-TE walkers on the [[Fervse'dra asteroid belt|asteroid field]] surrounding the [[planet]] [[Bothawui]]. There, the walkers and its gunners ambushed the Separatist fleet under the command of General [[Grievous]], allowing Republic forces to win the battle.<ref name="Downfall">{{TCW|Downfall of a Droid}}</ref>

===Ryloth, Felucia, and Devaron===
Clone gunners once again took part in the [[Battle of Ryloth]], when the AT-TE walkers of the [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]] were deployed to liberate the [[Twi'lek]] [[homeworld]].<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref> Gunners later participated in a [[First Battle of Felucia|battle]] on [[Felucia]], where Republic forces separated into two tank groups, which included AT-TE walkers and a [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]], and were overwhelmed and trapped by Separatist ground forces. Surviving Republic forces were evacuated by [[gunship]]s from the [[104th Battalion]].<ref name="Holocron Heist">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref> During the [[Battle of Devaron]], three gunners manned the cannons of AT-TE walkers that were deployed atop a Separatist [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]] in an attempt to rescue [[Jedi Master]] [[Bolla Ropal]]. During the topside boarding action, one walker was destroyed, killing its gunner in the process.<ref name="Cargo of Doom">{{TCW|Cargo of Doom}}</ref>

===Second Battle of Geonosis===
Clone gunners notably participated in the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], manning the cannons of the multiple AT-TEs deployed by the 7th Sky Corps, the 501st Legion, and a [[Jet's battalion|special forces battalion]]. The gunners assigned to the 7th's [[212th Recon Division]] fielded [[desert]] camouflage, and provided fire support from their walkers as they defended [[Point Rain]] from being overrun. The 501st's walkers were destroyed, while the [[special forces]] [[battalion]]'s tanks were diverted from the main battlefield after encountering obstacles. Once all forces regrouped at Point Rain and pushed back the advancing Separatist forces, the encircling tanks were deployed to destroy a nearby [[deflector shield generator]]. Once the 501st's troops created an opening, the gunners on several AT-TEs fired upon the structure, destroying it.<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref>

After some time, Republic forces besieged the Separatist [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|primary droid foundry]], and advanced with troop columns and AT-TEs, and several AV-7s were called to the front lines of battle. Once the area was temporarily cleared, the gunners engaged Separatist [[Separatist super-tank prototype|super tank]]s, which were being deployed from the foundry. The gunners and their cannons were unable to impact the tanks, and were all destroyed and killed by Separatist forces. Eventually, the Republic forces emerged victorious.<ref name="Weapons Factory">{{TCW|Weapons Factory}}</ref>

===Subsequent deployments===
