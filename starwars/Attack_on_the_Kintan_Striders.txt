In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Boba Fett]] and [[Fennec Shand]] '''attacked''' a group of the [[Kintan Striders Gang]]. The gang was riding in the open desert with more than fourteen [[Kintan Strider speeder bike|speeder bikes]] when Fett, flying ''[[Slave I]]'', opened fire, leaving no survivors and taking revenge for the [[Massacre of a Tusken Raider tribe|massacre]] of the [[Tusken Raider]] [[Unidentified Tusken Raider tribe|tribe]] that had taken him in.<ref name="The Gathering Storm" />
