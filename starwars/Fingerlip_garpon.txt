The '''fingerlip garpon''', also referred to as '''Finger-Lipped Garpon''' or simply '''garpon''', was a [[species]]<ref name="Jedi: Survivor" /> of [[scalefin fish]]<ref name="SoloOG" /> found in the [[ocean]]s and [[lake]]s of the [[planet]]s [[Ahch-To]],<ref name="TLJVD" /> [[Corellia]],<ref name="SoloOG" /> and [[Koboh]].<ref name="Jedi: Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> The fish had [[Color|silver]]-scaled bodies with long thin lower [[Mouth|jaws]] that extended far beyond their broader upper jaws below their [[Nose|nostrils]] and black [[eye]]s. The species possessed a pair of fins extending from just behind their gills and a tail fin.<ref name="TLJVD" /> The fisherman of [[Coronet City]] on Corellia,<ref name="SoloOG" /> the fishman [[Skoova Stev]],<ref name="Jedi: Survivor" /> and members of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Lanai]] species on Ahch-To caught fingerlip garpons for [[Foodstuff|food]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. As [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] carries on immediately from the events of ''The Force Awakens'', it can be assumed to take place in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the Lanai [[caretaker]] [[Clan Matron|matron]] [[Alcida-Auka]] prepared a garpon with a fish scaling [[knife]] in the [[Jedi village]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> where [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] were residing.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A fingerlip garpon first appeared in the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']]. The prop was not named in the film,<ref name="TLJ" /> but received identification in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="TLJVD" /> The prop was then reused in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="SoloOG" /> A fingerlip garpon prop used in the [[Andor Season 1|first]] season of the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' was made of dense foam with a fish scale and flesh tone finish. It measured seventeen inches by four inches by three inches.<ref name="Auction">{{WebCite|url=https://entertainment.ha.com/itm/movie-tv-memorabilia/props/andor-disney-2022-2025-aquatic-alien-creature-prop-from-season-1/a/7456-11070.s|text=Andor (Disney, 2022-2025), Aquatic Alien Creature Prop From Season 1|work=[[Wikipedia:Heritage Auctions|Heritage Auctions]]|archivedate=20260406195222}}</ref>
