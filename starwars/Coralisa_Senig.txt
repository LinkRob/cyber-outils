'''Coralisa Senig''' was an [[Urubaian]] from the [[planet]] [[Urubai]] who cared for the [[Mining|miners]] known as the [[Unseen]] and their families during the [[Clone Wars]]. She was the main point of contact on the world for [[Jedi Master]] [[Kelleran Beq]], who wished to aid the Unseen, and approached him after learning that two [[bounty hunter]]s had been sent to [[Death|kill]] the creator of the Unseen's preliminary [[fractal radio]] network: [[Mira Bridger]]. She then led Beq and [[Junior Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] of the [[Galactic Senate]] to Bridger, who they convinced to accompany them off planet to safety.

As the group headed for Binks' [[H-type Nubian yacht]], the ''[[Heyblibber Duck]]'', they came under fire from the bounty hunters, [[Aurra Sing]] and [[Rum Sleg]]. Although Beq defended them with his [[Kelleran Beq's lightsaber|lightsaber]], Senig was hit in the [[Limb|leg]] by a bolt and was grounded. She returned fire on the hunters, hitting Sleg in the shoulder, and then told the others to leave without her, as the people of Urubai still needed her. Beq and Binks then escaped the bounty hunters with Bridger and got her to safety.

==Biography==
===Carer of the Unseen===
{{Quote|Coaxium isn't the only resource being mined on Urubai. Our planet is actually home to ''dozens'' of rare and important ''minerals''. But we've recently ''discovered'' a ''new'' one. A potential galaxy changer. We've been calling them ''cyphristal''&mdash;a special type of ''crystal'' that can form the basis of a ''fractal radio network''.|Coralisa Senig|<ref name="JarJar" />}}
[[File:BeqAndSenig-JarJar1.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Kelleran Beq kneels beside a wounded Coralisa Senig holding her hand after she has been shot in the knee.|Coralisa Senig became the main point of contact for Kelleran Beq on Urubai.]]
Coralisa Senig was an [[Urubaian]] from the [[planet]] [[Urubai]]. During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the [[Urubaian government]] began working a banished group of trapped workers known as the [[Unseen]] as hard as they could to [[Mining|mine]] [[coaxium]] on Urubai, no longer restricted in the treatment of its laborers by Republic law due to the [[Emergency Powers Act]] made at the start of the war. Senig began caring for the miners and their families, becoming the main point of contact on Urubai for [[Jedi Master]] [[Kelleran Beq]], who wished to aid the Unseen. The pair at one point played [[sabacc]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], leaving Beq in Senig's debt.<ref name="JarJar" />

When the Unseen discovered a new type of mineral in the mines, which they named [[cyphristal]]s, they used it to set up a preliminary network of [[fractal radio]]s that could be used for highly encrypted and secure communication. Through their new network, they learned that its very existence had upset powerful individuals, including Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], and that a pair of [[bounty hunter]]s had been hired to [[Death|kill]] the network's creator, [[Mira Bridger]]. Despite this knowledge, Bridger told Senig that there was no need to make a fuss and that she needed to stay with her work on Urubai.<ref name="JarJar" />

===Beq and Binks===
{{Quote|We have a preliminary version of the network up and running. And it alerted us to a new ''threat''.|Coralisa Senig|<ref name="JarJar" />}}
[[File:SenigMines-JarJar1.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=Coralisa Senig leads Jar Jar Binks and Kelleran Beq through mine tunnels on Urubai.|Coralisa Senig sought aid from Kelleran Beq and Jar Jar Binks in keeping Mira Bridger safe.]]
Beq bought [[Junior Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]]&mdash;who had been the one to propose the Emergency Powers Act to give additional powers to Palpatine&mdash;to Urubai in the hopes of convincing him to speak to represent the interests of the Unseen and speak to Palpatine about their plight and the fact that the mining might lead to a planetary core meltdown on Urubai. As the pair toured one of Urubai's [[Unidentified mine|mines]], Senig raced up to them, calling out to the [[Jedi]]. Beq introduced her to Binks, but Senig warned him about their new problem and led Binks and Beq deeper into the mine to meet Bridger.<ref name="JarJar" />

As she led them through the tunnels, Senig brought them up to speed on the fractal radio network, which Beq had to explain the importance of to Binks. They found Bridger working, and she repeated that she did not want a fuss and needed to stay with her work, but upon learning about the bounty hunters, Beq convinced Bridger to flee to safety with them and continue her work elsewhere. Bridger begrudgingly agreed and Senig, Beq, Binks, and Bridger set out for Binks' [[H-type Nubian yacht]], the ''[[Heyblibber Duck]]''.<ref name="JarJar" />

===Urubai shootout===
{{Quote|It's not that bad. I can patch you up on the ''Duck''.''"<br />"''I'm ''not'' leaving. Too many people here are counting on me. Get Mira to safety.''"<br />"''Coralisa&hellip;''"<br />"''Go! I'll be fine.''"<br />"''I'll be ''back''.''"<br />"''You better be. You still owe me credits from that sabacc game in the Outer Rim.|Kelleran Beq and Coralisa Senig|<ref name="JarJar" />}}
[[File:SenigShot-JarJar1.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Coralisa Senig cries out as she is hit in the left knee by a blaster bolt mid-run.|Coralisa Senig was hit by blaster fire while trying to reach the ''Heyblibber Duck''.]]
As the group approached the [[yacht]], they suddenly came under fire from the bounty hunters sent for Bridger: [[Aurra Sing]] and [[Rum Sleg]]. Beq used his [[Kelleran Beq's lightsaber|lightsaber]] to deflect the bolts and ordered the others to make a run for the [[starship]] while he covered them. Senig and the others did as he ordered, but the Urubaian was hit in the left [[Limb|knee]] by one of the hunter's shots, knocking her to the ground. Beq cried out for her, and Binks bent to help her up, but Senig told them to get to the ship without her, rolling onto her front and opening fire with a [[blaster pistol]], hitting Sleg in the shoulder.<ref name="JarJar" />

Beq ignored her command and rushed to her, trying to help her up and promising to treat her wound on the ''Heyblibber Duck'', but Senig refused to leave Urubai, as there were too many there who needed her still. The Jedi then promised to return, and she reminded him that he still owed her from their sabbacc match. Beq then joined Bridger and Binks on board the yacht, but Sing also managed to board before it took off and left Urubai.<ref name="JarJar" />

[[File:SlegShot-JarJar1.png|left|thumb|300px|Holding Bridger's attackers at bay, Senig allowed them to escape.]]
Sleg managed to make it to his own [[Rum Sleg's starship|starship]] and gave chase, but Beq and Binks ultimately managed to escape the hunters and get Bridger to safety on [[Coruscant]],<ref name="JarJar" /> [[capital]] of the Republic.<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Binks then managed to convince Palpatine to open a formal inquiry into the conditions on Urubai and name Binks as the voice of the world. He then asked Beq to be his [[eye]]s and [[ear]]s out in [[the galaxy]] as they started forming a network using the fractal radios but told him he could not be his [[agent]] on Urubai, as Palpatine was aware of their work together there.<ref name="JarJar" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I ''told'' you not to make a fuss.''"<br />"''And I told ''you'' that the fuss has already been made.|Mira Bridger and Coralisa Senig|<ref name="JarJar" />}}
Coralisa Senig cared for the Unseen on Urubai and made sure to find aid for Mira Bridger when she was in danger despite Bridger's insistance on not causing a fuss. After being wounded, she refused to leave Urubai as the people there still needed her. The Urubaian had [[Color|pink]] and blue skin, blue eyes, and black [[hair]].<ref name="JarJar" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Get to the ship&hellip; I'll cover you. ''Go''!|Coralisa Senig draws her blaster pistol|<ref name="JarJar" />}}
Senig wore a white [[Clothing|shirt]], brown pants, brown [[boot]]s, a brown sash [[belt]], a brown [[cloak]], white wrist wraps, and brown hair ties. She used a black blaster pistol that she kept [[holster]]ed on her right leg.<ref name="JarJar" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:CoralisaConceptArt.png|thumb|right|150px|Concept art by Kieran McKeown]]
Coralisa Senig appeared in [[Jar Jar 1|''Jar Jar'' 1]], a one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] written by both Jar Jar Binks actor [[Ahmed Best]] and [[Marc Guggenheim]], illustrated by [[Laura Braga]], [[Kieran McKeown]], and [[Michael Atiyeh]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="JarJar" /> on [[February 11]], [[2026]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/133299/star_wars_jar_jar_2029_1|text=STAR WARS: JAR JAR (2029) #1}}</ref> On [[February 16]], 2026, Ahmed Best revealed in a [[Wikipedia:Instagram Reels|Reel]] on [[Wikipedia:Instagram|Instagram]] that they had based the character's name and design off of his friend Coralisa Delaforce, who had lost her fight with cancer, to honor her fight and memory and revealed that he was able to show her the design before her passing. The teal parts of the character's skin represent [[Wikipedia:Ovarian cancer|ovarian cancer]] while the pink parts represent [[Wikipedia:Breast cancer|breast cancer]]. Concept art of Senig was created by Kieran McKeown. Best also said in the post that he promised Delaforce and others that Senig would reappear.<ref name="Inspiration">{{{Instagram|username=bestahmed|url=reel/DUz1vNZkirR|name=[[Ahmed Best]]|date=2026-02-16|quote=The best thing about @starwars for me is watching characters from different parts of the galaxy choosing to become family. I wanted to honor her and celebrate her for the badass she was. One of my proudest moments in #starwars was when I told her that I created a character in this comic for her. The color that represents ovarian cancer is #teal, and for #breastcancer the color is pink. We honored those colors on Coralisa's skin in the comic. Coralisa, the character, is a celebration of the life of my chosen sister and to honor all of those #unseen warriors of both ovarian and breast cancer. If you have someone in your family who is fighting or has fought cancer, drop their name in the comments. #jarjar 1 is out everywhere you get your comics. I promised her, and I promise you, this is only the beginning for #coralisasenig. @9oa____ and #ivandelaforce my ohana, I love you both and I hope I made her proud. #kelleranbeq #jedi #jarjaristherkeytoallofthis|archivefile=File:Ahmed Best IG Coralisa Senig.png}}</ref>
