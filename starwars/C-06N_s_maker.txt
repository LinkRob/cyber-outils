At some point, the [[protocol droid]] [[C-06N]] was constructed by a '''maker'''.<ref name="Family Ties" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">According to ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'', the [[Guardian Protocols]] were implemented in [[229 BBY]]. According to [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|''Shadows of Starlight'' 1]], this happened one week after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places in [[230 BBY]]. The events of ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'', including the [[Expansion of the Stormwall]], take place exactly one year after the Fall of [[Starlight Beacon]]. The events of "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Friend or Foe?]]" and "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Survivors]]" take place one month after the Expansion of the [[Stormwall]], so they must be set in [[228 BBY]]. "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Pathogen]]" occurs immediately after those stories, while "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Family Ties]]" then immediately follows "Pathogen."</ref> while attempting to resuscitate the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Ghal Tarpfen]], the [[human]] [[Joss Adren]] questioned in C-06N understood anything in relation to being a [[medical droid]]. C-06N responded by confirming they could be helpful, furthermore mentioning they could communicate with other programs, but was mainly developed by their creator to assist the [[Tarkin family]] with [[Species|interspecies]] communication. Before the [[droid]] could continue, C-06N was interrupted by the human [[Elping Tsoo]], who ordered for them to cross reference any information regarding medical programming in the [[starship]] ''[[Aurora III]]'' from a plug in the wall. Eventually, Tarpfen was brought to consciousness by Adren, Tsoo and other individuals with some assistance from C-06N.<ref name="Family Ties" />
