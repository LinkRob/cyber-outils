'''Droideka Sentinels''',<ref name="Commander" /> also known simply as '''Sentinels''', were an advanced<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> variation of a [[droideka]] modified by [[smuggler]]s to protect contraband. Like its predecessor, the Sentinel was equipped with two [[blaster]] [[cannon]]s, but had better firepower due to combat improvement. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], both the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] utilized it as a [[security droid]]. To turn the sentinel into a de facto [[turret]], both the Imperials and rebels could base a sentinel atop a [[Droideka Platform]].<ref name="Commander" />
