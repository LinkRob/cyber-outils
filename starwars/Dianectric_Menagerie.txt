The '''Dianectric Menagerie''' was a display of [[Species|creatures]] that,<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> in [[90 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Dooku]]'s first visit to [[Serenno]], which corresponds to the [[celebration on Serenno]] according to ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', to [[90 BBY]].</ref> was set up at a [[Celebration on Serenno|celebration]] of [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] cultures on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]] hosted by the [[Count of Serenno]], [[Gora]]. Creatures visible in the Dianectric Menagerie included [[blatterborn]] and [[hunanetre]]. The visiting [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Dooku]] and Gora's daughter and the former's sister, [[Jenza]], were delighted by the Menagerie.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

Dooku later recounted the incident in a [[holo-letter]] to Jenza.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="Date 23">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku displayed a [[Hologram|holographic]] [[Datacard|data disk]] that detailed the events around meeting his sister, including their visit to the Dianectric Menagerie, to his recently-recruited [[Sith assassin]], [[Asajj Ventress]]. The pair listened to the recording, after which Dooku gave Ventress several more disks for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into Jenza's location.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dianectric Menagerie was mentioned in a flashback sequence in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin-Download">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The spelling of Dianectric Menagerie was confirmed by the audio drama's [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin-Download" />
