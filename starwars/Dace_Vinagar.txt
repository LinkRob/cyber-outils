'''Dace Vinagar''' was an apprentice to the [[Jedi archivist/Legends|Jedi archivist]] [[Orb Telorn]], and lived during the [[Great Sith War/Legends|Great Sith War]].

==History==
Vinagar was [[Brotherhood of the Sith|one of the Jedi]] who joined [[Exar Kun/Legends|Exar Kun]], in search for power and knowledge, but didn't participate to the [[Sith uprising|uprising]] nor implied himself in the war, instead staying with his master.<ref name="Le facteur X" />

Sometime after the [[Battle of Foerost (Great Sith War)|Battle of Foerost]], Vinagar and his [[Jedi Master/Legends|master]] encountered a group of adventurers, said to be [[Time travel/Legends|time travelers]]. He helped his master to search for information about a legendary [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]], [[Ku'ar Danar]], whose tomb was found by [[Sli'Lon Tahar]]. The now deceased Jedi Master tried to prevent the return of Danar.<ref name="Le facteur X" />

The Jedi order, however, was not inclined to trust those adventurers and jailed them for interrogation. But Telorn, with the help of his apprentice, managed to free them. Vinagar then participated to an expedition in the search of the Sith tomb, in the [[Nilrebmah system]].<ref name="Le facteur X" />

On [[Nilrebmah XIII]], Vinagar helped the adventurers to fight the Dark Side creatures, but as they noticed, the beast just ignored him most of the time. He finally tried to kill his comrades when they found the monolith, stole their ship and went to the tomb by himself to explore it.<ref name="Le facteur X" />

Ku'ar Danar, sensing the presence of Vinagar, lured him in the depth of the planet. Danar slowed the adventurers by reanimating the skeletons of the civilization he destroyed, gaining time for Dace Vinagar to come inside the monolith. He then empowered the apprentice with his own Force abilities, allowing him to unleash dark side beasts on his pursuers. The Fallen Jedi then revealed to his comrades that he belonged to the [[Brotherhood of the Sith]] and that he would used the power of the monolith to overthrow [[Exar Kun/Legends|Exar Kun]]. The beasts gave Vinagar just enough time to finish an old ritual and activate the monolith, liberating the spirit of Ku'at Danar. Cursing the [[Force ghost/Legends|Force spirit]] of the Fallen Jedi when his body started to consume itself, Vinagar let his rage amplify the power of the ritual and was finally changed in a giant [[Hydra]] moved by pure energy.<ref name="Le facteur X" />

The entire planet started to collapse, as it moved toward [[Nilrebmah XII]], and the hydra was shot down by a [[Krath/Legends|Krath]] starship commanded by [[Ulic Qel-Droma/Legends|Ulic Qel-Droma]], effectively ending the life of Vinagar.<ref name="Le facteur X" />

==Personality and traits==
Dace Vinagar was an ambitious, opportunistic Jedi apprentice who searched power through the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|Dark side of the Force]]. Contrary to other members of the [[Brotherhood of the Sith]], he didn't killed his master but rather stayed with him.
He enjoyed battle and took great pleasure in killing, but was clever enough to maintain his good image. However, his treacherous nature led him to his doom, as his attempt to betray [[Exar Kun/Legends|Exar Kun]] was punished by another [[Treason/Legends|treason]], that of Danar.<ref name="Le facteur X" />

==Power and abilities==
Dace Vinagar was proficient in [[Meditation/Legends|meditation]] and competent with a lightsaber. He obtained, during his last moments, great powers from [[Ku'ar Danar]], as he was able to summon beasts and protect himself with [[Force deflection/Legends|Force deflection]].<ref name="Le facteur X" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dace Vinagar was created by [[Philippe Rat]] and [[Mehdi Sahmi]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] scenario "Le Facteur X" which appeared in the French magazine ''[[Casus Belli 115]]''.<ref name="Le facteur X" />
