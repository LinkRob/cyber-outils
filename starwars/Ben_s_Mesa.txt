'''Ben's Mesa''' was a flat-topped [[mountain]] located on the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]]. It was named in tribute to [[Ben Neluenf]], the first great Tatooinian [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]], who [[Death|died]] while attempting to scale the mesa. The dangerous [[Boonta Eve Classic]] racetrack mostly turned around Ben's Mesa.<ref name="CL" />
