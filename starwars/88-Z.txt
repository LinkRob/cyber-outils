'''88-Z''' was a remotely activated<ref name="Remote">{{SWIA|set=Heart of the Empire|cardname=Remote Activator|link=https://imperial-assault.fandom.com/wiki/Remote_Activator|archiveurl=https://archive.is/eEXSU}}</ref> [[droid]] used in combat missions<ref name="Scour the Streets" /> and for surveillance by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Surveillance">{{SWIA|set=Heart of the Empire|cardname=Active Surveillance|link=https://imperial-assault.fandom.com/wiki/Active_Surveillance|archiveurl=https://archive.is/Ej3Lm}}</ref> It was equipped with [[infrared]] scanners<ref name="Infrared" /> and targeting [[sensor]]s.<ref name="Targeting" /> The droid was armed with [[blaster]] emplacements<ref name="Blasters" /> and electromagnetic disruptors.<ref name="Disruptors" /> It also had the ability to deploy [[Ray shield|energy shields]]. 88-Z was a round droid with [[Color|gray]] and yellow plating, and a black sensor. The droid also had two antennas on top of its body, and a dish extending from its side. While on<ref name="Scour the Streets" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Planet">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Coruscant]], during the [[Galactic Civil War]], 88-Z encountered a [[Coruscant rebel cell|rebel cell]].<ref name="Scour the Streets" />

==Behind the scenes==
88-Z was first pictured with it's name visible, but illegible, in the news post [https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/news/2017/5/5/heart-of-the-empire Heart of the Empire] on [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/index official website] on [[May 5]], [[2017]], which depicted<ref name="HotA">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/5/5/heart-of-the-empire|text=Heart of the Empire}}</ref> the [https://imperial-assault.fandom.com/wiki/88-Z 88-Z] card<ref name="88-Z">{{SWIA|set=Heart of the Empire|cardname=88-Z|link=https://imperial-assault.fandom.com/wiki/88-Z|archiveurl=https://archive.is/GNpgo}}</ref> that would eventually be released in the ''[[Heart of the Empire]]'' expansion set for ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Assault]]'' on [[October 12]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/9/22/i-am-no-jedi|text=I Am No Jedi}} indicates that ''Heart of the Empire'' was scheduled to be released in conjunction with the ''[[Maul Villain Pack]]'', the ''[[Emperor Palpatine Villain Pack]]'', and the ''[[Ahsoka Tano Ally Pack]]'', which were published on [[October 12]], [[2017]], according to {{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/10/12/my-ally-is-the-force|text=My Ally Is the Force}}.</ref> The droid was clearly identified as 88-Z in the news post [https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/news/2017/7/26/scour-the-streets-of-coruscant Scour the Streets of Coruscant] also on Fantasy Flight Games' official website on [[July 26]], [[2017]].<ref name="Scour the Streets" /> 88-Z was designed by artist [[Cristi Balanescu]]<ref name="ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=cristib|url=artwork/3wY6J|name=Cristi Balanescu|text=Propagandist Specialization|quote=For Star Wars: Age of Rebellion from FFG}}</ref> and originally appeared as an unidentified droid in the [[Star Wars Legends]] sourcebook ''[[Desperate Allies]]'' for Fantasy Flight Games' ''[[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|Star Wars: Age of Rebellion]]''.<ref name="Desperate Allies">''[[Desperate Allies]]''</ref>
