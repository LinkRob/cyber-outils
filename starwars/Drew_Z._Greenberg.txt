'''Drew Z. Greenberg''' is a writer for the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series. His work includes the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|season one]] episodes "[[The Hidden Enemy]]" and "[[Hostage Crisis]]," as well as the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|season two]] episodes, "[[Children of the Force]]," "[[Senate Spy]]," "[[Landing at Point Rain]]," "[[Weapons Factory]]," "[[Legacy of Terror]]," "[[Brain Invaders]]," "[[Grievous Intrigue]]," "[[The Deserter]]," "[[Lightsaber Lost]]," "[[The Mandalore Plot]]," "[[Duchess of Mandalore]]," and "[[Cat and Mouse]]."
