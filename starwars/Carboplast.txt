'''Carboplast''' was a material that existed in [[the galaxy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. '''Carboplast-composite'''<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> was used in [[KX-series security droid]]s<ref name="Generalization">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'' state that carboplast-composite was used in the body of K-2SO. As [[K-2SO]] is a reprogrammed [[KX-series security droid]], it can be assumed that the material is also used in other KX droids.</ref> in the shell that encased their [[Droid brain|cognitive module]]<ref name="ROUVG" /> as well as their fists.<ref name="GOH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>
