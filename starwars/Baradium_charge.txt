'''Baradium charges''' were explosives that made use of the [[Radiation|radioactive]] chemical [[baradium]]. The charges cost 750 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] per unit. Though their blast radius was rather small, the radiation produced by the charges could affect entire [[planet]]s.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> During the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="R2 Come Home">{{TCW|R2 Come Home}}</ref> Bounty hunter [[Boba Fett]] made an [[Mission to Vanqor|attempt]] on [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]]'s life utilizing a [[Shaped charge|shaped]] baradium charge concealed within his [[Jango Fett|father's]] [[Boba Fett's armor|helmet]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A baradium charge first appeared in [[R2 Come Home]],<ref name="R2 Come Home" /> the twenty-first episode of the animated television series [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]' [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]], which originally aired on [[April 30]], [[2010]],<ref name="Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode221.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: R2 Come Home}}</ref> though was only later identified as such by ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'',<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> released [[October 23]], [[2018]].<ref name="Quarto">{{Quarto|url=books/9780760362051/Star-Wars-Scum-and-Villainy.html|text=Star Wars: Scum and Villainy}}</ref>
