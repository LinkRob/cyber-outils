'''Adria Arjona''' is an actress who played [[Bix Caleen]] in the [[2022]] ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series. She is known for playing [[W:c:emeraldcity:Dorothy Gale|Dorothy Gale]], the lead in the fantasy drama series ''[[Wikipedia:Emerald City (TV series)|Emerald City]]'', and for supporting roles such as [[W:c:goodomens:Anathema Device|Anathema Device]] in the Amazon series ''[[Wikipedia:Good Omens (TV series)|Good Omens]]'', [[W:c:pacificrim:Julia Reyes|Julia Reyes]] in the [[2018]] movie ''[[Wikipedia:Pacific Rim Uprising|Pacific Rim Uprising]]'', and [[W:c:marvel-movies:Martine Bancroft|Martine Bancroft]] in the [[2022]] film ''[[Wikipedia:Morbius (film)|Morbius]]''.

==Biography==
Adria Arjona played [[Bix Caleen]] in the ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series. Arjona's ethnicity was not part of any of the conversations about her casting.<ref name="Potent Moment" /> Executive producer and creator [[Tony Gilroy]] cut everyone else from the casting process. He then told her she had the role and welcomed her into ''[[Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Tricky lines">{{YouTube|video=0q25e6uuFwA|text="Star Wars means so much!" Diego Luna and the Andor cast on auditions, tricky lines and falling over|channel=@bbcradio1|name=BBC Radio 1}}</ref> Arjona and Gilroy discussed Caleen's character arc at length.<ref name="Spiral">{{SW|url=news/adria-arjona-andor-interview|text=Losing Bix: ''Andor'''s Adria Arjona on the Downward Spiral of Bix Caleen}}</ref> Arjona was immediately drawn to her character's tough exterior, which protects her vulnerable core. She liked how Caleen is fearless, bold, deep inside, loyal, compassionate, and cares too much for others.<ref name="Potent Moment" /> She described her character as a hardworking and complicated woman who is underestimated by people.<ref name="ProductionBrief">{{Getty|url=thewaltdisneystudios/allaccess#!asset/gppr5wpqgb4t74974f8s|text=ANDOR Production Brief V3 FINAL 8-04-22|author=[[Gina McIntyre|McIntyre, Gina]]|date=2022-08-04}}</ref>

[[File:Andor_Bix_Caleen_poster.png|thumb|left|130px|Adria Arjona as Bix Caleen in her character poster]]
Arjona assumed that she would film in front of a [[Wikipedia:Chroma key|green screen]] or a virtual set, and she was surprised that an entire city was built for scenes on [[Ferrix]].<ref name="Real People">{{WebCite|url=https://variety.com/2022/tv/news/star-wars-andor-tony-gilroy-diego-luna-1235348148|text=How ‘Andor' Became the First ‘Star Wars' TV Series for Grown-Ups: ‘I Wanted to Do It About Real People'|author=Vary, Adam B|work=[[Wikipedia:Variety (magazine)|''Variety'']]|archivedate=20220824140042}}</ref> Scenes on the [[planet]] were filmed in an intricate set in [[Wikipedia:Marlow, Buckinghamshire|Little Marlow]], [[Wikipedia:Buckinghamshire|Buckinghamshire]].<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> According to Gilroy, everything on Ferrix was filmed first.<ref name="Good day">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/andor-season-2-post-credits-scene-tony-gilroy-1235268962|text=‘Andor' Creator Tony Gilroy Talks Luthen's Good Day, That Post-Credit Scene and Season Two|author=Davids, Brian|date=2022-11-23|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20221123185515}}</ref> In Arjona's apartment, she kept notepads everywhere, which she used to plot and connect the dots that would take Caleen to where she ended up.<ref name="Spiral" /> Arjona was obsessed with the [[droid]] [[B2EMO]] whom she thought was endearing, funny, and "badass."<ref name="B2EMO Twitter">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1582800891706454016|[[Star Wars]]|date=2022-10-19|quote=The cast of Andor shares their thoughts on the newest droid to join the galaxy, B2EMO.|archivedate=20221020141047}}</ref> During the first take of a scene in which Caleen and [[Luthen Rael]] spoke at a bar on [[Ferrix]], Arjona tripped on the pebbles and fell on her face. She then insisted on doing the scene again.<ref name="Tricky lines" /> Arjona believed that the betrayal of [[Timm Karlo]] completely broke Caleen and took her down a spiral where she lost herself. She thought that Gilroy made an interesting choice in having Caleen be [[torture]]d by an auditory device. Arjona said that just thinking about it messed with her head. She asked Gilroy if she could make Caleen unable to take care of herself in a childish way towards the end of the series.<ref name="Spiral" /> In [[September]], [[2022]], Arjona attended the ''Andor'' launch event in [[Wikipedia:Hollywood, Los Angeles|Hollywood]], [[Wikipedia:California|California]].<ref name="Launch event">{{SWYouTube|Fv6hHyIToC0|Diego Luna and the Cast and Crew of Andor}}</ref>
