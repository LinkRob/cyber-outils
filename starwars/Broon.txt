'''Broon''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[scrumrat]] of the [[White Worms]] gang on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. Around [[13 BBY]], they and the fellow human scrumrat [[Fixit]] helped prepare for a [[Heist on a Corellian shipyard|raid]] on a Corellia [[Unidentified Corellia shipyard|shipyard]] by [[Scout|monitoring]] the [[Shipyard|facility]] in shifts, often taking turns with the [[Rodian]] scrumrat [[Oggro]].

In one instance, when [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s exited a [[hangar]] and began scanning the area around the shipyard, Broon and their companion fled back to the [[den of the White Worms]] to tell someone, not waiting for Oggro to replace them at their posting. The pair told the older scrumrat [[Qi'ra]] of the situation, with Broon indicating they had not yet told the Rodian what they had learned. After promising to pass on the information, she dismissed the boys for [[Foodstuff|dinner]].

==History==
{{Quote|I have an important update about the shipyard.''"<br />"''What is it?''"<br />"''Two of our scouts reported a heavy Imperial presence. They must have come to pick up the control boards earlier than we expected.|Qi'ra, to Proxima, about Broon and Fixit's shipyard report|<ref name="CC" />}}
The [[human]] [[Gender|male]] Broon lived on<ref name="CC" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="GalaxyMap">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Corellian system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Corellia]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. They eventually joined the [[White Worms]] criminal gang as a [[scrumrat]] [[thief]] and lived within their [[Coronet City]] [[Den of the White Worms|den]] under [[Head]] scrumrat [[Ema]]'s management, alongside several other orphans.<ref name="CC" /> Around [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The chapter of ''[[Crimson Climb]]'' in which Broon appears takes place a few [[Standard week|weeks]] after [[Han Solo|Han]] escaped [[Corellia]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates his escape to [[13 BBY]], the chapter's events must have taken place around that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the White Worms prepared to [[Heist on a Corellian shipyard|raid]] a Corellia [[Unidentified Corellia shipyard|shipyard]] to steal control boards under the orders of their leader, [[Lady]] [[Proxima]], who hoped the endeavor would result in the [[death]]s of several of her older scrumrats so she could thin her ranks.<ref name="CC" />

[[File:QiraScrumrat-SoloOMG.png|thumb|left|195px|After discovering stormtroopers at the shipyard, Broon informed Qi'ra of what they had seen.]]
As one of the group's younger scrumrats, Broon paired off with the fellow human scrumrat [[Fixit]] to monitor the [[shipyard]] as a [[scout]]. The boys took shifts at a watching post during the [[Standard day|day]], while the [[Rodian]] scrumrat [[Oggro]] would come to relieve the boys and take the [[Local day|night]] shift. One [[time]], while waiting for the Rodian to take their place, they and their partner were surprised to see a large group of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s scanning the nearby area as they marched out of the facility's [[hangar]]. Broon and Fixit fled their post and returned to the White Worms' den to inform someone of the information, not waiting for Oggro to replace them.<ref name="CC" />

Broon and Fixit explained the pair's situation to the older scrumrat [[Qi'ra]], with the former explaining that they had not told Oggro what they had learned. Qi'ra thanked them and sent the boys to acquire [[Foodstuff|dinner]], offering to pass the information on to Proxima. Though she then reported the boys' report about Imperial stormtroopers, Proxima berated her for questioning their plan and dismissed the scouts' claims. Not long afterward, the White Worms executed the raid and took heavy scrumrat losses. Afterward, Qi'ra informed [[Sarkin Enneb]]&mdash;an [[enslaver]] who had purchased her&mdash;about Broon and Fixit's warning of Imperials at the shipyard, indicating that Proxima had ignored the information.<ref name="CC" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Did you tell Oggro?''"<br />"''We didn't see him. We thought it was more important to get back and tell someone.|Qi'ra and Broon|<ref name="CC" />}}
Thinking that not all scrumrats would have made that decision, Qi'ra mentally commended Broon for reporting the Imperial presence despite having to leave their post to do so. By the time of the shipyard raid, the scrumrat had a young appearance.<ref name="CC" />
