'''Edgehawks''' were an [[Bird|avian]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[grass]]lands of [[Lothal]]. They were known to harass [[Loth-cat]] young. [[Ezra Bridger]] once rescued a group of young Loth-cats from a number of Edgehawks, an event he recorded in his [[Rebel Journal|journal]].<ref name="Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger" />
