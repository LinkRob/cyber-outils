The '''Fylar Freedom Fighters''' were a resistance group that fought in a [[Battle for Ontotho|battle]] on [[Ontotho]]. They opposed the [[Fylar region]]'s incorporation into Ontotho's [[Ontothon government|planetary government]] and, by extension, incorporation into the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Dark Temple 1" /> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cere Junda]] worked with the freedom fighters while on the [[planet]].<ref name="Dark Temple 2">[[Jedi Fallen Order - Dark Temple 2|''Jedi Fallen Order - Dark Temple'' 2]]</ref>
