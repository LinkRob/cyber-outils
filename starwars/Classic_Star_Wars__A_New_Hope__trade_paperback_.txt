'''''Classic Star Wars: A New Hope''''' was a reprint trade paperback collection, collecting both issues of the [[Classic Star Wars: A New Hope (comic series)|''Classic Star Wars: A New Hope'']] series.

==Publisher's summary==
''Luke Skywalker: a boy on his way to becoming a man, backed against the wall, his identity and his very soul at stake. With the entire Empire out to disarm him, he can give in to rage and despair or choose instead to apply a little Force. Thrill to the adventures of Luke and Leia, the swashbuckling Han Solo and his trusted companion Chewbacca, the dastardly Darth Vader, and the faithful droids Artoo-Detoo and See-Threepio in ''Classic Star Wars: A New Hope''&hellip;''
