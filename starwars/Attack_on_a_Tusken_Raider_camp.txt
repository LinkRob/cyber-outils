A [[Tatooine rebel cell|gang]] of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] led by the [[human]] [[Saponza]] and his [[Saponza's partner|partner]] aided the [[Galactic Empire]] in '''attacking''' a [[Tusken Raider]] [[Unidentified Tusken Raider camp|camp]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The mercenaries used [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s provided by the Empire to destroy the camp, after which the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] congratulated them and requested they work with Empire again in future.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|I'm picking up an Imperial transmission. It's Darth Vader! What would the Emperor's champion want on Tatooine?|Saponza receives Vader's transmission|<ref name="Star Wars: Commander" />}}
At sometime between the [[Battle of Yavin]] and the [[Battle of Hoth]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[mercenary]] [[Saponza]] returned to his [[Saponza's homestead|homestead]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] and met there with an [[Saponza's partner|old friend]]. Shortly after they were finished constructing a new [[autoturret]], the [[Tatooine rebel cell|mercenaries]] were [[Attack on Saponza's homestead|attacked]] by a group of outlaws working for the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]. The homestead's autoturrets managed to [[Death|kill]] the criminals, after which Jabba's [[majordomo]] [[Bib Fortuna]] contacted them and informed them that the Hutt was angry. The [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] then contacted the pair by [[hologram]] and ordered that they aid the local Imperial garrison in destroying a nearby [[Tusken Raider]] [[Unidentified Tusken Raider camp|camp]]. The [[Sith]] provided the mercenaries with three [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s for the battle.<ref name="SWC" />

==The attack==
{{Quote|I've mobilized several AT-ST's for this mission. In light of that, failure will not be tolerated.''"<br />"''With Jabba on our tail, we could use some friends. Let's take those walkers for a spin and see what they can do.|Darth Vader and Saponza as forces are mobilized for the attack|<ref name="Star Wars: Commander" />}}
The mercenaries deployed the three AT-STs, causing a group of seven Tuskens to emerge from the camp and attack. A pair of Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s then began firing on buildings in the camp, completing multiple attack runs. The walkers and [[starfighter]]s proved superior in combat though and destroyed the entire camp, including three [[autoturret]]s the Tusken's controlled.<ref name="SWC" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Your actions were noted, mercenary. The Empire would find your skills useful.|Vader congratulates Saponza's partner|<ref name="Star Wars: Commander" />}}
Once the camp was destroyed Vader contacted the mercenaries again and congratulated them, telling them that they should work with the Empire again in future. Saponza considered the request, but before he could decide [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] contacted him and his partner and asked for their aid in recovering medical supplies from a [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] [[Unidentified Cartel den|den]] on the planet [[Dandoran]]. The mercenaries [[Mission to recover medical supplies|obliged]], and were offered allegiance by the Alliance as well. The mercenaries chose to work with the rebellion.<ref name="Starwars.com">In [[Kekit]]'s Databank entry, it is stated that Saponza and his partner played an integral part in the campaign that liberated a Jawa clan from the Tusken Raiders. It also states that the plan was orchestrated by Jennica Pierce of the Rebel Alliance. This scenario does not occur if the player aligns with the Empire, so it can be deduced that Saponza's gang joined the Rebellion.</ref><ref name="SWC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'', a mobile strategy game released in [[2014]] by [[Disney Interactive]]. The attack acts as part of the game's tutorial and always has the same outcome. Shortly after the battle players must chose to either side with the Empire or the Alliance.
