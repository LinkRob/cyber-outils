The '''Catacombs of Cadera''' were a series of ancient ruins on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]]. The Catacombs were once an ancient monastery known as the '''Cadera Monastery''', and were located a half-day's walk from the [[Jedha City|Holy City]].<ref name="Rogue One novel" />

==History==
[[File:CatacombsOfCadera-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|270px|The Partisans made their base in the catacombs.]]
It served as a burial ground<ref name="DoR" /> for the [[Skeleton|skeletal]] remains of<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> thousands of generations of [[NiJedhan monk]]s,<ref name="DoR" /> adherents of a vanished faith,<ref name="DB">{{Databank|catacombs-of-cadera|Catacombs of Cadera}}</ref> whose name and history have been long forgotten.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> Their [[humanoid]] skulls<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> lined the catacomb walls, a revered but morbid presence.<ref name="DoR" /> The site had a reputation for being haunted by spirits of the departed and was generally avoided by the locals.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" />

At some point, the Catacombs were inhabited by the [[Church of the Contained Crescent]]. While inhabiting the ancient monastery, they added a latticed window. The Contained Crescent eventually left the Catacombs years prior to [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[Partisans]], who repurposed the Catacombs into a base which he named "'''the Gut'''."<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> It was destroyed when the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[Destruction of Jedha City|tested]] its laser on Jedha's Holy City, which not only wiped the city off the map but also caused the surrounding surface of Jedha to peel off and destroy anything in its path, including the Catacombs themselves.<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Behind the scenes==
An earlier iteration of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''{{'s}} story involved Saw Gerrera hiding on a jungle planet by the name of Cadera. [[Pablo Hidalgo]] reused the name to refer to Gerrera's hideout on Jedha.<ref name="Cadera Tweet">{{Twitter|@@pabl0hidalgo|status/1701648106230575568|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=An earlier iteration of the story had Saw hiding out, Colonel Kurtz-style, on a jungle planet named Cadera. When it came time to name his otherwise nameless hideout on Jedha, I dusted that off and said he's in the Catacombs of Cadera in the Guide.|archiveurl=https://archive.vn/qWBdk}} &mdash; [[Pablo Hidalgo]] indicates that the origin of the name Cadera came from an unused concept regarding a jungle planet named Cadera where Saw Gerrera was hiding in.</ref>
