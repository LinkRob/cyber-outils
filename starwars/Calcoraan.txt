'''Calcoraan''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Calcoraan system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]. It was entirely covered by an [[ocean]] made out of [[chromyl chloride]], which gave the planet its [[blood]] red [[color]], mixed with several other [[acid]]s, making it uninhabitable. However, substances in both the planetary ocean and the ocean floor had uses in manufacturing, resulting in the construction of multiple [[space station]]s in Calcoraan's orbit. [[Count]] [[Denetrius Vidian]] had a [[Calcoraan Depot|storage facility]] orbiting this planet.<ref name="A New Dawn" />
