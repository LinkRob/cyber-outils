The '''''Aspirant''''' was a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class transgalactic military assault ship]] active in [[19 BBY]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="TCW">The third arc of ''[[Star Wars: Yoda]]'' features [[Agen Kolar]] on the [[Jedi High Council]]. "[[A Seat on the Council]]" establishes that Kolar replaced [[Eeth Koth]], who served on the Council during [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|''The Clone Wars'': The Lost Missions]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of "[[The Wrong Jedi]]" to [[19 BBY]]. ''Galactic Atlas'' also dates the beginning of [[Great Jedi Purge]], which includes the events of "[[Victory and Death]]," to 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}} places ''The Lost Missions'' between "The Wrong Jedi" and "Victory and Death", dating the events of ''The Lost Missions'' to 19 BBY. Kolar was later seen on the Council during {{Film|III}}, the events of which ''Galactic Atlas'' also dates to 19 BBY, including the [[Operation: Knightfall|destruction]] of the [[Jedi Order]], limiting Kolar's tenure, and ''[[Star Wars: Yoda: Size Matters Not]]'', to that year.</ref> Following a [[Mission to Golatha|mission]] to [[Golatha]] to destroy a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Golatha factory|factory]] attempting to produce [[megadroid]]s, in which [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] sustained minor injuries when he got trapped inside of a megadroid launching into [[realspace|space]], the diminutive Jedi received medical treatment from [[2-1B surgical droid]] [[TB-5]] aboard the ''Aspirant''. The megadroid was brought to the [[starship|ship's]] cargo bay where [[engineer]]s examined the specimen, and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] was debriefed.<ref name="Yoda 9" />
