A '''skirmish''' occurred above the [[planet]] nicknamed "[[Sunrise]]" between the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] and forces allied with the [[Grysk]]s,<ref name="Greater Good" /> in approximately [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Date" />

During the skirmish, the ''[[Springhawk]]'' was attacked by three formations of five [[Unidentified missile boat|missile boats]]. Soon thereafter, the ''[[Grayshrike]]'', commanded by Senior Captain [[Irizi'in'daro]], arrived in the system to deliver a message from [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] to Senior Captain [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]]. Lakinda ordered her ''Grayshrike'' to join the battle to protect the ''Springhawk''.<ref name="Greater Good" />

During the battle, the ''Springhawk'' and ''Grayshrike'' were attacked by the ''[[HopeBreaker]]'' apparently accompanied by a repair ship which turned out to be a [[freighter]]. The [[Battle Dreadnought class|Battle Dreadnought]] kept the [[Chiss]] forces occupied while the freighter escaped to [[hyperspace]]. Thrawn disabled or destroyed all of the missile boats as well as the armaments and sensors on the Battle Dreadnought's port side. After the freighter escaped into hyperspace, the Battle Dreadnought also fled, but first destroyed all the intact missile boats so the Chiss could not learn anything about their design or schematics.<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Prelude==
[[Haplif]] of the [[Agbui]] orchestrated a [[Sunrise's civil war|brutal war]] on the [[planet]] that came to be known as [[Sunrise]]. That war killed most of the planet's population.<ref name="Greater Good" />

After encountering a group of [[refugee]]s led by [[the Magys]] on [[Rapacc]], [[Mitth'ali'astov]] sought to prevent the refugees' mass suicide by taking their leader to [[The Magys's people|their people's]] homeworld. Despite [[Caregiver]] Thalias not having the authority to order the ''[[Springhawk]]'' to make such a journey, [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] was pleased with Thalias' initiative and ordered his officers to act as though Thalias had command whenever the Magys was present.<ref name="Greater Good" />

Senior Captain [[Irizi'in'daro|Lakinda's]] ''[[Grayshrike]]'' joined the battle, as she was tasked with delivering Thrawn a message from their commanding officer [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]]. She traced the ''Springhawk''{{'s}} likely vector to Sunrise, where she encountered the ''Springhawk'' under attack by unknown forces.<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Aftermath==
After seeing the destruction wrought on her homeworld, [[the Magys]] ordered the [[Death|suicide]] of her companion, and attempted to take her own life as well. However, Senior Captain Thrawn arrested the Magys for causing the death of an alien aboard his vessel, and held her in a [[hibernation chamber]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

[[Second skirmish over Sunrise|Another skirmish]] would take place over Sunrise two [[Standard week|weeks]] later as part of the [[Nikardun campaigns]]. Lakinda's ''Grayshrike'' and Ar'alani's [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']] fought the Unidentified Battle Dreadnought, which self-destructed.<ref name="Greater Good" />
