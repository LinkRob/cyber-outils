'''''Fate of the Jedi: Outcast''''' is a novel by [[Aaron Allston]] released on [[March 24]], [[2009]]. It is the first novel in the [[Star Wars: Fate of the Jedi|''Fate of the Jedi'']] series and published as a hardcover<ref name="Continue">{{SWArchive|url=vault/books/news20080912.html|text=Del Rey ''Star Wars'' Books Continue Through 2013}}</ref> and audiobook. The book features an excerpt of the subsequent novel ''[[Fate of the Jedi: Omen]]''. The audiobook is narrated by [[Marc Thompson]], who narrated the audiobook versions for the ''[[Star Wars: Legacy of the Force]]'' series. The paperback was released on [[April 27]], [[2010]].

==Plot summary==
The novel starts off [[43 ABY|two years]] after the [[Battle of Uroro Station|end]] of the [[Second Galactic Civil War]]. [[The galaxy/Legends|The galaxy]] is still steadily recovering from the fallen [[Darth/Legends|Darth]] [[Darth Caedus|Caedus's]] brief but brutal reign as [[Chief of State (Galactic Alliance)|Chief of State]] of the [[Galactic Federation of Free Alliances|Galactic Alliance]]. The partnership between the GA, [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial Remnant]] and the [[Confederation]], headed by GA Chief of State [[Natasi Daala]], is working well enough to bring the Galactic Alliance back to its previous status. Unfortunately, [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Knight]] [[Valin Horn]] suddenly [[Force psychosis|believes that everyone around him is an imposter]] as he detects through their [[The Force/Legends|Force]] presences. He then goes on a rampage on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] trying to find where the real people are. After two extended chases throughout the upper levels of the GA capital planet, with several Jedi in pursuit of Valin, he is caught and brought into the medical custody of the [[New Jedi Order]], despite the resentment presented towards the Jedi by the others chasing Valin, such as [[Galactic Alliance Security]], led by [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Oric Harfard]], and the [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]] hired by Daala to chase down the rogue Jedi. However, as [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Cilghal]] tries to examine Valin, she finds that he is somehow blocking out her brain sensors from discovering what is going on in his mind, a trick harnessed by Jacen Solo prior to his fall to the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side of the Force]].

Meanwhile, [[Grand Master/Legends|Grand Master]] [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] is arrested by Daala under the charge of dereliction of duty in lieu of Darth Caedus's reign of terror, having failed to prevent his fall to the dark side. Daala's plan is to hold the Jedi accountable for their actions as ordinary superhumans, for not bringing Jacen under their control during the Second Galactic Civil War and for abandoning the GA at the [[Battle of Kuat (Second Galactic Civil War)|Battle of Kuat]]. Luke arranges to go into a ten year exile in an effort to learn how and why Jacen fell to the dark side. Should he effectively demonstrate that Jacen's fall was not his fault to a board of Galactic Alliance members of Daala's choosing, he will be pardoned of all charges. Luke's son, [[Ben Skywalker|Ben]], decides to go with him to also gain some closure from Jacen's machinations toward him during the previous war, such as nearly apprenticing him as a [[Sith/Legends|Sith]], having him do many horrible deeds as he served in the [[Galactic Alliance Guard]], Jacen killing Ben's mother, [[Mara Jade Skywalker|Mara]], etc. Luke leaves Jedi Master [[Kenth Hamner]] as acting Grand Master, as Luke cannot be within five light years of a Jedi Order Temple or Academy, nor can he advise in any Jedi affairs. The Order gives him a warm send off as a reminder of his achievements and contributions to the New Jedi Order he founded. Meanwhile, [[Han Solo/Legends|Han]] and [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa Solo]] sneak off of Coruscant with their granddaughter, [[Allana Djo Solo|Allana]], [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]], and [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]], to respond to [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando]] and [[Tendra Risant Calrissian]]'s help in the [[Glitterstim/Legends|glitterstim]] mines of [[Kessel/Legends|Kessel]]. Han is reluctant to go due to his experiences on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] with [[Kyp Durron]] and the late [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] decades earlier, but Leia convinces him to go help a friend, as they can't do much else. Not much later, the New Jedi Order members are given individual observers to ensure what happened with Valin Horn and [[Seff Hellin]] doesn't occur again. [[Jaina Solo Fel|Jaina Solo]] is assigned [[Dab Hantaq]], who was given reconstructive surgery to look like Jaina's dead brother, [[Anakin Solo|Anakin]], during the [[Yuuzhan Vong]] [[Yuuzhan Vong War|War]] by the late treacherous [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] [[Viqi Shesh]]. This was in an attempt to distract Han and Leia with the loss of their son so that Shesh could kidnap an infant Ben Skywalker for the Yuuzhan Vong. [[Tahiri Veila]], meanwhile, is freed from observation in court, due to [[Nawara Ven]]'s legal savvy. She meets Dab again since the war against the Vong, and is visibly shaken.

Luke and Ben arrive at [[Dorin/Legends|Dorin]], where the [[Baran Do]] Sages' Temple is situated. Luke requests a meeting with Mistress [[Tila Mong]], who later allows an audience after Ben defeats the student apprentice, [[Tistura Paan]]. They discover that Jacen came to the Sages for their own mastery of the Force and techniques. Luke easily masters what Jacen had learned. At a ceremony for [[Chara|Charsae Saal]], Luke and Ben realize the Master had not died, but merely concealed himself in the Force, and transported himself underground to assume a new identity and die in obscurity. Apparently, none of the Masters of the Baran Do Sages&mdash;not even [[Koro Ziil]], the Master in charge when Jacen was present&mdash;have died, and the Skywalkers invade a transport for Saal. They go with him to where he goes, and they are informed that they have to choose new names, for Luke and Ben Skywalker are dead. The reasoning for the underground [[Kel Dor/Legends|Kel Dors]]' plan is that in case if another Jedi purge were to occur, and the Baran Do Sages on the surface of Dorin were wiped out, then the underground Sages would be safe from such an onslaught, resurface, and then teach what they learned for the future of their order.

On Kessel, Han and Leia are informed by Lando and Tendra that there are groundquakes occurring throughout the planet. The Solos find out that these quakes are resulting from groups of machines, guarded by strange phantom-like beings, interspersed across the globe that are exerting gravitic anomalies that Leia calculates would completely destroy the world very soon. So they get the help of [[Rogue Squadron/Legends|Rogue Squadron]], with [[Wedge Antilles/Legends|Wedge Antilles]] once again leading the team, to destroy the machines as Han and Leia help in the [[Millennium Falcon/Legends|''Millennium Falcon'']]. Eventually, the machines are destroyed, and Kessel is safe. Lando, Tendra and [[Nien Nunb/Legends|Nien Nunb's]] glitterstim enterprise continues, and Han, Leia, Allana, C-3PO and R2-D2 prepare to go home back to Coruscant. However, Allana is visibly shaken from an [[Abeloth|unknown dark side presence]] that she feels is above Kessel.

After convincing the Kel Dors present in the underground caverns that their mission here would backfire on them and showing them that their leader, the Hidden One (Koro Ziil), was keeping secrets from them, the Skywalkers orchestrate their escape. Ben bests Chara (Charsae Saal) in combat to prove the underground organization's lack of efficiency in terms of Force-using and combat, as Luke does to the Hidden One when the latter refuses to let his followers go. Most of the other Baran Do Sages down with the Skywalkers leave after the family's victory. The Skywalkers then decide to continue their journey to find out what Jacen learned through his travels via information from the Hidden One who told them of Jacen's next location prior to the quiet revolution that Luke provoked.

On Coruscant, Jaina and her [[Darkmeld]] organization secretly capture Seff Hellin for medical study, believing him to be afflicted with the same condition as Valin Horn, who was frozen and detained by the government in [[Carbonite/Legends|carbonite]]. Soon after, thanks to Nawara Ven again, the Jedi Order is reprieved of their observers.

==Continuity==
Taking a deliberate departure from the arch darkness that dominated the last two epic novel series, [[Star Wars: Fate of the Jedi|Fate of the Jedi]] intersperses scenes of relative domestic tranquility amid a growing sense of dreadful confrontation brewing between the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]] and the [[Galactic Federation of Free Alliances|Galactic Alliance]]. The series also set out to mend the relationship between [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke]] and [[Ben Skywalker|Ben]], sending them off on an interstellar journey together inspired by [[Wikipedia: Homer|Homer's]] [[Wikipedia: Epic poetry|epic poem]] [[Wikipedia: Odyssey|''The Odyssey'']].<ref name="TERC" />
