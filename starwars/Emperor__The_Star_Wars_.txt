The '''Emperor''' is the title of the [[Head of State/Legends|Head of State]] of the [[Galactic Empire (The Star Wars)|Galactic Empire]]. Until the [[Great Jedi Rebellion]] and the rise of the [[Knights of Sith]], the position had been closely guarded by the [[Jedi-Bendu]] for at least 100,000 years. The Emperor in the aftermath of the rise of the Knights of Sith was [[Cos Dashit]], although the [[Supreme Tribunal]] members [[Darth Vader (The Star Wars)|Darth Vader]], [[Crispin Hoedaack]], and [[Vantos Coll]] planned to usurp the position.

==Style of Office==
The Emperor, at least during the time [[Cos Dashit]] ruled the Empire, was identified as "Your Highness." In addition, a common phrase for the Emperor when celebrating a speech was "Long Live the Emperor!"

==Behind the scenes==
This incarnation of the [[Emperor]] first appeared in [[George Lucas]]'s [[1974]] [[The Star Wars rough draft|rough draft]] of ''[[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|A New Hope]]''. In this script, the Emperor had been a position used since at least 100,000 years prior to the events of the story and was originally benevolent. This was changed by the final draft to the [[Galactic Emperor]] being a position created when the [[Galactic Empire]] replaced the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]], although hints of its original conception were retained in the [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|novelization of A New Hope]], including [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s mention of there being multiple Emperors, with the later ones becoming corrupt over time. Similarly, the same novelization also stated that [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] was a puppet ruler.

In [[2013]]-[[2014]], a [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] adaptation of the rough draft, titled ''[[The Star Wars]],'' was published by [[Dark Horse Comics]].
