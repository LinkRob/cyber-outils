The '''Doogs''' were creatures from the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Morda]] and shared the planet with the [[Morling]]s. The Morlings used their [[Leather/Legends|leather]] to produce clothes.

==Behind the scenes==
The Doogs were briefly mentioned by [[Jean-Michel Ringuet]] in the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] gamemaster aid "[[Providence (Avalon)|Providence]]," which appeared in the French magazine ''[[Avalon 1]]''.
