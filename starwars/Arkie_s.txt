'''Arkie's''' was a store situated along the [[beach]] on [[Pier Nine]] at [[Niamos]], run by a [[Gender|male]] individual named [[Arkie]]. [[Windi]], a [[Human]] who was in a relationship with [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] when the latter was at Niamos, asked Andor to drop by Arkie's when he went out and get the new flavors of an unspecified [[Foodstuff|food]] product, as well as [[peezo]]s and [[Revnog]], as they were out of both. However, before he was able to complete his errand, Andor was apprehended by a [[Unidentified coastal defender stormtrooper (Niamos)|shoretrooper]] right outside the store.<ref name="Andor7" />
