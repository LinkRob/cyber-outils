The '''Council of First Knowledge''' was one of the four [[Jedi Council|councils]] of the [[Jedi Order]] in operation during the rule of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Its role was to procure advice on matters requiring ancient Jedi wisdom, including the curriculum of the Temple, and the [[Jedi Archives]].<ref name="Graphics" /> It also managed the Archives themselves, safeguarding manuscripts, data-tapes, and the [[Holocron Vault]], while acquiring new knowledge from Jedi explorers. The Council was responsible for safely storing or destroying [[Sith]] [[Sith artifact|artifacts]].<ref>[[Rise of the Separatists|''Rise of the Separatists'']]</ref> Its councilors convened in the [[Tower of First Knowledge]].<ref name="CL 2016" />
