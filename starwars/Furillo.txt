'''Furillo''' was an individual that served as a [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] in the [[Resistance]] during its [[First Order-Resistance War|conflict]] with the [[First Order]]. The [[pilot]] fought against the First Order in the [[Battle of Starkiller Base|assault]] on [[Starkiller Base]]. Furillo was [[Death|killed]] when the pilot's [[T-70 X-wing starfighter|X-wing starfighter]] was hit by enemy [[fire]] from Starkiller Base's ground defense emplacements.<ref name="Episode VII" /> During a [[Funeral of Han Solo|ceremony]] held at the Resistance base on [[D'Qar]] for [[Han Solo]] after his death on Starkiller Base, [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] read out the names of the pilots who'd never returned from Starkiller Base.<ref name="VIII Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
