'''Cloud City Bread''' was an airy [[bread]] that was originally made on the [[tibanna]] [[gas]] [[mining]] colony [[Cloud City]] on the [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]]. Due to the rarity of tibanna, Cloud City became quite wealthy and as such the airy bread became associated with wealth and became quite popular on the [[HoloNet]]. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included the recipe for bread in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', in 35 ABY.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cloud City Bread were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish makes use of three large [[Wikipedia:Egg white|egg whites]], [[Wikipedia:White sugar|granulated sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Vanilla extract|vanilla extract]], [[Wikipedia:Almond extract|almond extract]], [[Wikipedia:Food coloring|pink food gel dye]], and [[Wikipedia:Cornstarch|cornstarch]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
