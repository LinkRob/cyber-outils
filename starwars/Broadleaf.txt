'''Broadleaf''' [[forest]]s grew on the [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]].

==Description==
Broadleaf [[forest]]s were the primary terrain of the overgrown [[Ajan Kloss]], which, when seen from afar in [[Realspace|space]], was almost completely covered in [[Color|green]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==History==
[[File:Ajan-Kloss-TROS-GG.png|thumb|left|160px|Broadleaf forests thrived on the lush green world of Ajan Kloss]]
A [[gas giant]]-[[orbit]]ing [[moon]] once inhabitated by a civilization came to house broadleaf forests. Long before the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] on [[Exegol]] between forces of the [[Sith]] and [[Jedi]], some of the densely [[Plant|vegetated]] world's forests were cleared by its people, [[Standard century|centuries]] afterwhich the world was [[Map|charted]] by [[Alderaanian]] [[scout]]s.<ref name="TROS VD" />

Having [[Homeworld|originated]] from the faraway [[Core Worlds|Core World]] of [[Alderaan]]&mdash;a world sympathetic with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]&mdash;and to have made their discovery<ref name="TROS VD" /> shortly prior to the [[Battle of Scarif|outbreak]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="Ajan Kloss discovery">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that the discovery of [[Ajan Kloss]] was made prior to the [[Galactic Civil War]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the begin to [[1 BBY]], and news of it was withheld from the [[Imperial Senate]]. As, per {{Film|III}}, the Imperial Senate only existed during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire|First Galactic Empire]], and the discovery of Ajan Kloss must have occured between [[19 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]].</ref>&mdash;waged between the Alliance and the ruling [[Politics|government]] known as the [[Galactic Empire]]&mdash;the scouts withheld news of their discovery from the [[The galaxy|wider galaxy]], and the forested world was considered as a place for their [[Great Temple|main base of operations]] by the Alliance.<ref name="TROS VD" />

However, it would not be until [[Standard decade|decades]] later that another group would find a home on the new-found world. Amidst the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], the latter organization&mdash;led by the Alderaanian [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], hero of the Alliance to Restore the Republic&mdash;settled in a [[jungle]] area of the world, then known as Ajan Kloss, which they named the [[Klosslands]]. During their stay, Resistance members found cleared areas of forests, which they believed were evidence of ancient inhabitants on the world, who they named the [[Kloss]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
