'''Aragu Kato''' was a [[Chagrian]] criminal who lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Han Chewie 6" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> Kato was [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] at a [[Gulhadar prison|prison]] on the prison [[planet]] [[Gulhadar III|Gulhadar]]. After the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] was also imprisoned in the prison, the two got into a [[Unarmed combat|fight]] after Kato claimed that Chewbacca looked at them funny. During their fight, Kato impaled the Wookiee with their [[lethorn]]s, to which Chewbacca responded by punching the Chagrian in the face. In response, Kato declared their intent to finish both the fight and Chewbacca, and drew a [[knife]]. Kato was then approached by another prison, [[Phaedra]], who attempted to use a [[mind trick]] to get Kato to drop the knife and return to their cell. Kato saw through the fake attempt and called Phaedra out for not being a [[Jedi]], which she admitted, but added that she had heard their tricks worked on the weak minded, and that she thought Kato would qualify as. Phaedra then revealed that she had only intended to distract Kato, and when Kato asked from what, Chewbacca appeared and punched Kato again, ending the fight.<ref name="Han Chewie 6" />
