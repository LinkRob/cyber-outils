'''Coma-bloom''' was a type of [[flower]]ing [[plant]] with deleterious properties. Shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]<ref name="Inquisitor" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] was shocked to discover coma-bloom and other deleterious plants growing innocently alongside plants with beneficial properties in the flower beds of a small walled [[garden]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] that was accessible from the [[Jedi Archives]]. She recognized coma-bloom and the other plants and remembered their names from readings that had been assigned to her by her [[Jedi Master]], [[Sember Vey]].<ref name="Inquisitor" />
