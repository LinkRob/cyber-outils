'''Blastocrete''' was a material that could be used when constructing a building. The [[Observatory]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] included a spare room that had walls made of blastocrete that was dark in [[color]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blastocrete appeared in the [[2017]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', written by [[Chuck Wendig]].<ref name="Empire's End" />
