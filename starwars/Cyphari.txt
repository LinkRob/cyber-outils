The '''Cyphari''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Cyphar]]. Cyphari were described as resembling large stick [[insect]]s with snouts similar to that of a [[Rodian]]. They were covered in neat rows of short [[Color|red]] [[Hair|fur]]. One group of Cyphari was known as the [[Afe clan]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Afe Clan was led by a Cyphari named [[Chief]] [[Joko (Cyphari)|Joko]].<ref name="Thrawn" />
