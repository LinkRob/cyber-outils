'''Clutterbombs''' were a type of explosive which were used during the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Tassahondee Station]]'s passages were riddled with clutterbombs when the [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] [[Yrica Quell]] once flew through them.<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Clutterbombs were mentioned in ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the second novel in [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' which was published in [[2020]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
