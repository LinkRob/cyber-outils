The '''broadsaber''' was a type of specialized [[lightsaber]] model. Due to specialized focusing lenses and emitters, the broadsaber's hilt made a blade that was broader and flatter than a standard lightsaber's cylindrical blade, and as such, gave it a profile that was closer to that of a traditional metal [[sword]]. The broadsaber provided a larger overall surface for the blade, while losing the advantages of an omnidirectional cutting edge.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />

Because of this, broadsabers were not popular among the members of the [[Jedi Order]], with many [[Jedi Master]]s believing that it was less elegant than standard lightsabers. Despite this, broadsabers were well suited for [[Form I]] techniques, so the form had some masters with the broadsaber.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
