'''Chris Scarabosio''' is a veteran sound designer of [[Lucasfilm]]'s [[Skywalker Sound]] who has worked on the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]], including providing voice talent for a [[Neimoidian]] [[Senator]] in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. He worked on the sound mixing for the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']], receiving an [[Wikipedia:Academy Awards|Oscar]] nomination, and served as the sound designer and provided additional voices for ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.
