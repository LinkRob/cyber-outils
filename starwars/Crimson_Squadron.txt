'''Crimson Squadron'''<ref name="FFG" /> was a [[Resistance]] [[Bomber]] [[Squadron]] comprised of [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17s]]. They most notably took part in the [[Battle of D'Qar|Evacuation of D'Qar]] during the Resistance's [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] with the [[First Order]].<ref name="Episode VIII Teaser">{{SWYouTube|zB4I68XVPzQ|Star Wars: The Last Jedi Official Teaser|sw_url=video/star-wars-the-last-jedi-official-teaser}}</ref>

==History==
Crimson Squadron participated in the [[Atterra campaign]], delivering supplies to the [[Atterra system]].<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" />

During the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation of D'Qar]], two [[bomber]]s<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|crimson-squadron|Crimson Squadron}}</ref> of Crimson Squadron were called in to bomb the [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought]] that was targeting the [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']], along with [[Cobalt Squadron]]. All of Crimson Squadron were destroyed, but Cobalt Squadron member [[Paige Tico]] managed to release their bombs and destroy the dreadnought.

==Behind the scenes==
Crimson Squadron was first seen in the first teaser trailer for [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] on [[April 14]], [[2017]].<ref name="Episode VIII Teaser" /> The name was revealed on [[September 8]], 2017 on the [[Fantasy Flight Games]] website.<ref name="FFG" />
