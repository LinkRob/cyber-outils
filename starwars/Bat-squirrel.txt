A '''bat-squirrel''' was a type of [[Species|creature]] that was capable of gliding through the air. Members of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Tevellan]] species, who had thin membranes stretched between their [[Limb|arms]] and torsos, were able to glide like bat-squirrels.<ref name="Choose Your Destiny" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bat-squirrels were first mentioned in the [[canon]] junior novel ''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Finn & Poe Adventure]]'', which was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Choose Your Destiny" />
