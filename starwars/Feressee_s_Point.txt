'''Feressee's Point''' was a rock formation in the [[Starship Graveyard]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. [[Starship]] wrecks and corpses dating back to the [[Battle of Jakku]] in [[5 ABY]] could be found near the formation. By [[34 ABY]], the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] mentioned Feressee's Point in her [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]] and it was listed as an interesting locale in a planetary capsule of data on Jakku included in [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]]'s [[Poe Dameron's flight log|flight log]] at some point after the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]].

==Description==
Feressee's Point was a rock formation near the [[Carbon Ridge]] [[mountain]]s<ref name="CLNE" /> within the [[Starship Graveyard]] in the [[Goazon Badlands]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. The Graveyard was littered with [[starship]]s brought down in the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="RSG" /> in [[5 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> and the bodies of their crews, with the corpses found all the way from Feressee's Point to the ship known as the [[Spike (wreckage)|Spike]].<ref name="RSG" />

==History==
{{Quote|There's a Ghtroc 720. You know the one? Out by Feressee's Point?|Strunk|<ref name="Before">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref>}}
[[File:NakanoLashs720Freighter-BountyHunters4.jpg|thumb|right|300px|alt=A flat gray freighter with a large cockpit in the center of its front end sits on a landing platform.|A Ghtroc 720 light freighter was one of several freighters crashed near Feressee's Point.]]
By [[33 ABY]],<ref name="ReyTimelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Rey Skywalker|Rey's]] story in ''[[Before the Awakening]]'' to [[33 ABY]].</ref> a [[Ghtroc Industries|Ghtroc]] [[720 light freighter]] had crashed near Feressee's Point, splitting into pieces upon impact.<ref name="Before" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="ReyTimelines" /> the [[scavenger]] [[Strunk]] mentioned the point when discussing the wrecked [[freighter]] with his fellow scavengers [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and [[Devi]].<ref name="Before" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' includes an addendum at the end describing some of the events of {{Film|VII}}, written several [[Standard day|days]] after they take place. The addendum is the final entry in the guide and is included chronologically after the entry mentioning Feressee's Point. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the film to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Rey had written about Feressee's Point in a [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]]<ref name="RSG" /> she<ref name="Before" /> created while living on Jakku.<ref name="RSG" />

Around 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="ScavengerDate">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Scavenger Mission]]'' states that the [[Battle of Jakku]] took place thirty [[Standard year|years]] earlier and depicts [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] living on [[Jakku]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the battle to [[5 ABY]], but also dates Rey leaving Jakku to [[34 ABY]], meaning the events of ''A Scavenger Mission'' cannot take place a full thirty years after the battle. This article assumes the statement to be an approximation, meaning it took place around 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> [[Jaylin]] and another [[Jaylin's friend|scavenger]] lived together in a battered old [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Unidentified Alliance freighter (Jaylin)|freighter]] that had crashed near Feressee's Point during the Battle of Jakku.<ref name="Scavengers">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Scavenger Mission]]''</ref> Feressee's Point was also included on a list of interesting locales on Jakku in a planetary capsule of data on the world that [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] included in his [[Poe Dameron's flight log|flight log]] following the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]]<ref name="FlightLog">''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]''</ref> in 34 ABY.<ref name="Timelines" />

==Behind the scenes==
Feressee's Point was first mentioned simultaneously in ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'', a replica journal written by [[Jason Fry]],<ref name="RSG" /> and ''[[Before the Awakening]]'', an anthology written by [[Greg Rucka]],<ref name="Before" /> both of which released on [[December 18]], [[2015]].<ref name="Studio">{{StudioFun|url=book-display.php?isbn13=9780794435691|text=Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Rey's Survival Guide}}</ref><ref name="Disney">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/before-the-awakening|text=Before the Awakening}}</ref> The point was then depicted for the first time in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
