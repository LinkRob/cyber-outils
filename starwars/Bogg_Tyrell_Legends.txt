'''Bogg Tyrell''' was an [[Aleena/Legends|Aleena]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] who represented her [[Homeworld/Legends|homeworld]] of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Aleen/Legends|Aleen]] in the [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]].

==Biography==
[[File:Seboca_Bogg.png|thumb|left|220px|Bogg and Seboca during the chase between Anakin Skywalker, Obi-Wan Kenobi and Zam Wesell]]

The [[Aleena/Legends|Aleena]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] Bogg Tyrell represented her [[Homeworld/Legends|homeworld]],<ref name="DB" /> the [[Mid Rim/Legends|Mid Rim]]<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="FF" /> [[Aleen/Legends|Aleen]], in the [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]].<ref name="DB" /> She was occasionally mistaken for the Aleena [[Podracer/Legends|podracer]] [[Ratts Tyerell/Legends|Ratts Tyerell]].<ref name="FF" /> 

Bogg was known to enjoy wealth and the high life during her time on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. She even dated [[Dug/Legends|Dug]] holostar [[Seboca/Legends|Seboca]]; the two were joyriding in an [[Airspeeder/Legends|airspeeder]] in [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]] when they were almost hit by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] dangling from [[ASN-121/Legends|ASN-121]], after the failed attempt to assassinate [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Amidala]]. Afterwards, Bogg went to get a drink at [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]], coincidentally leaving the café&mdash;apparently inebriated&mdash;shortly after Obi-Wan entered the establishment looking for information on the weapon that had killed [[Zam Wesell/Legends|Zam Wesell]].

==Personality and traits==
Tyrell bared a passing resemblance to Ratts Tyerell, causing her to be often mistaken for him. She was upset that she, a senator, was mistaken for someone she considered a mere Podracer. Tyrell also took issue with the fact that Tyerell was male, and she was female. The Aleena had [[Color/Legends|blue]] [[Eye/Legends|eyes]], tan and blue skin, and stood at a stature of 0.79 [[Meter/Legends|meters]].<ref name="FF" /> She also had numbed taste buds.<ref name="VD">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DermotPower-BoggTyrell-Instagram.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Bogg Tyrell concept art labeled "dp479"]]
Bogg Tyerell first appeared in the [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']],<ref name="aotc" /> which was released on [[May 16]], [[2002]].<ref name="Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> The computer-generated model of Ratts Tyerell that had appeared in<ref name="DB2" /> the [[1999]] prequel trilogy film<ref name="Year" /> [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] was reused and modified to create the character.<ref name="DB2">{{DB|location|dexsdiner|Dex's Diner}}</ref> Concept artist [[Dermot Power]] made a design of Tyrell's costume in [[August]] of [[2000]], labeled with the production code "dp479."  He was not the designer of the character, however.<ref name="IG">{{Instagram|username=powerdermot|name=[[Dermot Power]]|url=p/COzOKivDA3y|date=2021-05-13|quote=Costume design for Ep 2 creature from a long time ago ... etc. Did not design Bogg Tyrell .|archivefile=File:BoggTyrell-DermotPower-Instagram.png}}</ref> 

Prior to the film's release, she was first pictured in the "[[Prequel Update]]" department of the [[Star Wars Insider 59|fifty-ninth]] issue of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'',<ref name="Insider59">{{InsiderCite|59|Prequel Update}}</ref> published on [[April 2]], 2002.<ref name="SW.com">{{SWArchive|url=community/fanclub/mag/news20020308.html|text=25 Years of ''Star Wars'' in New Insider}}</ref> The character was later identified in an Episode II [[Depth Commentary]] available on [[StarWars.com]] that could be accessed through the DVD-ROM home release of ''Attack of the Clones''<ref name="Depth">{{HomeVideoCite|film=2|featurette=Depth Commentary}}</ref> on [[November 12]], 2002.<ref name="DVD">{{SWArchive|url=episode-ii/news/2002/08/news20020801.html|text=Episode II On DVD & VHS}}</ref> According to [[Holocron continuity database]] manager [[Leland Chee]], unused text from the [[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Scrapbook|''Attack of the Clones Scrapbook'']],<ref name="UnusedScrapbook">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=1125#16098316|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Tasty Taste]]|date=2006-12-28|time=4:01 PM|quote=They're unrelated. Unused text for the ''Attack of the Clones Scrapbook'' had intended to point out this fact.}}</ref> authored by [[Ryder Windham]],<ref name="Scrapbook">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Scrapbook]]''</ref> was going to establish her as having no relation to [[Ratts Tyerell/Legends|Ratts Tyerell's]] [[Tyerell|wife]].<ref name="UnusedScrapbook" />
