An '''attempted hijacking''' took place aboard the [[Regal Voyager]] passenger [[transport]] ''[[Regal Zephyr]]'' within [[the Slice]]<ref name="The Living Force" /> in [[33 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> While the [[starship]] traveled along the [[Ootmian Pabol]] [[hyperspace route]] to the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], a [[Idiot Three|group]] of criminals&mdash;[[The Lobber]], [[Ghor]], and [[Wungo]]&mdash;attempted to take control of it for one of the [[pirate]] gangs in the region, though they could not agree on which. [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who were traveling aboard the ''Regal Zephyr'', intervened and talked the criminals out of hijacking the vessel. The [[Jedi]] let the criminals live, throwing them off the ship at its next stop on [[Randon]]. Although the hijacking was unsuccessful, it proved to be a rarity&mdash;unknown to the three criminals, [[Riftwalkers]] leader [[Zilastra]] had ensured none of the Slice's pirate gangs attacked Regal Voyager as part of a [[Assassinations of the pirate leaders|scheme]] to [[Death|assassinate]] the other pirate leaders.<ref name="The Living Force" />
