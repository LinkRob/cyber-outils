In the [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Ahsoka Tano]] used two '''white lightsabers''' as her personal weapons. The [[lightsaber]]s, which had [[Color|white]] blades and [[Curved-hilt lightsaber|curved hilts]], replaced the [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|lightsabers]] she used before leaving the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Clone Wars]], which Tano had abandoned in order to fake her [[death]] to the [[Galactic Empire]] after the [[Siege of Mandalore]].

Tano obtained the [[kyber crystal]]s used in these lightsabers [[18 BBY|one year]] after the end of the Clone Wars, retrieving them from [[Sixth Brother's lightsaber|the]] [[Double-bladed spinning lightsaber|lightsaber]] of the [[Sixth Brother]], an [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitor]] she had killed during the Imperial [[Raada Uprising|occupation]] of [[Raada]]. Though the [[crystal]]s originally emitted a red blade, she [[Purification|purified]] them with the Force, turning them white. The hilts of the lightsabers were originally created from pieces of scrap metal that Tano had acquired over the year since the [[Republic Era|fall]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]], but were later modified to be more functional and decorative.

She first used the lightsabers in battle on Raada just after she had constructed them, rescuing her friend [[Kaeden Larte]] and covering the escape of the population of Raada onto arriving evacuation ships. Later, after introducing herself to the [[Spectres]] rebel group and [[Phoenix Cell]], Tano fought various Inquisitors with the weapons, before [[Mission to Malachor|duelling]] former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] and her former master [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on [[Malachor]]. After the events on the [[Sith]] world, Ahsoka Tano continued to carry her two lightsabers throughout the [[Galactic Civil War]] and beyond.

==Description==
[[File:AhsokaTanoWhite-CGSWG.png|left|thumb|315x315px|Tano's curved-hilt lightsabers emitted white blades.]]
[[Ahsoka Tano]]'s second pair of [[lightsaber]]s were both [[curved-hilt lightsaber]]s with a rectangular profile,<ref name="Fire Recon">{{RebelsRecon|xggYEpemcck|#1.14: Inside "Fire Across the Galaxy" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-fire-across-the-galaxy}}</ref> although upon their initial construction, the hilts were much rougher due to having been assembled in a hurry in the middle of battle out of pieces of scrap metal.<ref name="Ahsoka" /> As with her [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|original pair]] of lightsabers, they consisted of one regular saber and a [[shoto lightsaber]].<ref name="TFOTF" /> The blades were white, powered by two [[kyber crystal]]s that Tano had retrieved from the [[Sixth Brother's lightsaber|double-bladed lightsaber]] of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitor]] known as the [[Sixth Brother]]. She purified them of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] taint they had been imbued with via [[bleeding]] before assembling her lightsabers, initially using various mechanical components she had picked up on her journeys and some specialized components she recovered from the Sixth Brother's lightsaber. Tano believed the kyber crystals had initially been looted from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] after the [[Great Jedi Purge|downfall]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Ahsoka" />

==History==
===Building new weapons===
{{Quote|They used to be red. When the creature had them, they were red. But I heard them before I ever saw him on Raada, and knew that they were meant for me.|Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="Ahsoka" />}}
[[File:AhsokaFlames-OFNF.png|thumb|right|250px|Ahsoka wielding her original lightsabers during the Siege of Mandalore]]
Ahsoka Tano had grown up in the [[Jedi]] Order and, during her time as a [[youngling]] and [[Padawan]] before and during the [[Clone Wars]], constructed two lightsabers.<ref name="TCWmovie">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref><ref name="Ahsoka" /><ref name="Heroes on Both Sides">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref> However, after being framed for [[treason]] by her former friend [[Barriss Offee]], leading to her [[Trial of Ahsoka Tano|trial]] and near-execution, Tano lost faith in the Order and left, leaving her weapons behind.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref> Late in the war, after joining up with [[Mandalorian]] warrior [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] and her [[Nite Owls]],<ref name="Together Again">{{TCW|Together Again}}</ref> Tano received her lightsabers back from her former master [[Anakin Skywalker]] upon temporarily rejoining the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Republic Military|military]] to lead forces in the [[Siege of Mandalore]].<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> After winning the Siege and capturing the renegade [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]], Tano and [[Clone Commander]] [[Rex]]'s journey back to [[Coruscant]] with their prisoner was [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|disrupted]] by [[Darth Sidious]]' issuance of [[Order 66]], which initiated the [[Great Jedi Purge]]. After freeing Maul as a distraction for the attacking [[clone trooper]]s, Tano ambushed Rex and had his [[Behavioral modification biochip|control chip]] removed.<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> However, Maul's subsequent destruction of the [[hyperdrive]]<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> of the ''[[Tribunal]]'',<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] they were traveling on, caused the stricken [[starship]] to crash on a [[Unidentified moon|moon]], with only Tano, Rex and Maul making it off beforehand. Afterwards, Tano abandoned her lightsabers by the graves she and Rex had dug for the clones who died in the crash<ref name="Victory and Death" /> to fake her death, before she and Rex parted ways to go into hiding.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

Tano spent the next [[Standard year|year]] in hiding on the [[planet]] [[Thabeska]], working as a mechanic for the [[Fardi clan]]. After fleeing the planet on the first [[Empire Day]], Tano, under the alias "Ashla," wound up on the remote farming [[moon]] [[Raada]]. As she befriended various locals, including sisters [[Kaeden Larte|Kaeden]] and [[Miara Larte]], Tano began to collect various mechanical bits and pieces, not quite sure why she was doing so. After the Empire arrived on Raada, Tano began to organize a [[Raada resistance|resistance]], but was forced to leave with her friends in hiding after exposing herself as a [[The Force|Force-user]] to rescue them from a botched plot cooked up by the farmer [[Hoban]] behind Tano's back. Tano returned to Thabeska briefly, but was eventually contacted by agents of [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], who had noticed a pattern of good deeds being done in the area and suspected a Jedi (or former Jedi) might be in the area and sought to recruit her for his burgeoning [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebellion]]. After joining Organa, and learning that a Force-using agent of the Empire — an Inquisitor called the Sixth Brother — had been sent to Raada and was holding Kaeden hostage, Tano decided to build herself new lightsabers in earnest, having already realized what the components she had been collecting would fit together to make.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

[[File:Ahsoka-rain-art.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Tano crafted a pair of white lightsabers using crystals from an Inquisitor's dual blade]]
Needing new kyber crystals, Tano traveled to the sacred [[Unknown Regions]] planet of [[Ilum]], where the Jedi had harvested their kyber crystals for millennia. However, she found that the Empire had begun strip-[[mining]] the planet, and was forced to hide further out in the [[Ilum system|system]] so she would not be spotted. Meditating into the Force to look for where she might find new crystals, Tano heard a song that pointed her back to Raada. Having arranged for Organa to send aid to [[Raada Uprising|evacuate]] the moon's population, Tano returned there, finding Miara, who had been in hiding after the Inquisitor had attacked the caves where she, her sister and their surviving friends had been sheltering, with her as the only survivor after Kaeden's capture. Miara was sent to begin arranging the evacuation, while Tano followed the song of her crystals to a confrontation with the Sixth Brother. Despite being unarmed, Tano easily defeated the unprepared Inquisitor, killing him by accident when she pulled on the crystals in his [[double-bladed spinning lightsaber]] with the Force and caused the weapon to explode.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

Tano took the kyber crystals housed within the deceased Inquisitor's lightsaber, which had been calling out to her. She noted that the crystal had been turned [[Color|red]] through the practice known as [[bleeding]]. Realizing that the crystals were meant to be hers, she purified them back to the [[light side of the Force]], the [[Purification|process]] turning the crystals a pure white. Tano then assembled herself two new lightsabers from the components she had collected, as well as the specialized crystal mountings from the Inquisitor's lightsaber. She noted that the hilts were rather rough, but had no time to make them more functional. Assisting in the evacuation of Raada, Tano leapt into battle with her new weapons, rescuing Kaeden from where she had been imprisoned and covering the locals' retreat into the evacuation ships sent by Organa. Afterwards, during a meeting on the ''[[Tantive III]]'', Tano showed the Senator her new weapons, before agreeing to take up the position of [[Fulcrum]] in order to improve the rebels' communications.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

===Fulcrum===
[[File:Duel_in_Hammertown.png|thumb|right|250px|Ahsoka battled two Inquisitors on Takobo.]]
Over the years that followed, Tano rebuilt the lightsabers' hilts to make them more functional and decorative, with new curved hilts and her recently acquired white blades.<ref name="Ahsoka" /> Fifteen years after the rise of the Empire, Tano met the rebel group known as the [[Spectres]], who included Jedi Padawan [[Kanan Jarrus]] and his apprentice [[Ezra Bridger]].<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> Tano used her weapons to give Bridger a combat lesson.<ref name="Disarming">{{FOD|A Disarming Lesson}}</ref> During a [[Mission to rescue Janard|mission]] to rescue a [[Janard|contact]], Ahsoka and [[Sabine Wren]] traversed a submerged [[freighter]] that had crashed on the [[ocean]] world [[Quila]]. Tano used her lightsabers to cut through several pieces of large debris, giving her and Sabine a path through the ship.<ref name="Ocean Rescue">{{RebelsMagCite|12|Ocean Rescue}}</ref> Later, during a [[Duel in Hammertown|mission]] to rescue [[Force-sensitive]] infants from the Empire, Tano fought Inquisitors [[Fifth Brother]] and [[Seventh Sister]] in [[Hammertown]] on [[Takobo]], defeating them easily but sparing their lives as she was covering Jarrus and Bridger's escape with the two children, [[Alora]] and [[Pypey]], as well as Pypey's mother [[Oora]].<ref name="TFOTF" />

Sometime after, Tano accompanied Bridger and Jarrus to the [[Lothal Jedi Temple]]. Inside the temple, Ahsoka experienced a Force vision while meditating. The vision, of Anakin, expressed anger at Ahsoka's departure from the order, calling her selfish and accusing her of abandoning him. He asked if Ahsoka knew what he had become, changing into the form of [[Darth]] Vader. Refusing to accept the truth, Ahsoka lashed out with her primary lightsaber, hoping to destroy the vision with tears in her eyes. Tano was subsequently left alone in the dark to grapple with the revelation.<ref name="Shroud of Darkness">{{Rebels|Shroud of Darkness}}</ref>

After the visit to the [[Lothal]] [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple]], on the advice of Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]],<ref name="Shroud of Darkness" /> Tano, Jarrus, and Bridger [[Mission to Malachor|traveled]] to the forbidden planet of [[Malachor]] searching for answers on how to destroy the [[Sith]]. There, they encountered an [[Eighth Brother|Inquisitor]] who had been searching for [[Maul]], who Tano had last seen during her escape from Order 66, and who had been stranded in the ruins of the [[Malachor Sith Temple]] for some time. When the Eighth Brother summoned backup, Tano and Jarrus reluctantly teamed up with Maul in order to fight the three Inquisitors. However, after Maul's inevitable turn against the Jedi party, Tano declined a rematch, instead tasking the injured Jarrus with dealing with him as she could sense the approach of a greater threat: Darth Vader.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref>

[[File:Forestall-CM.png|thumb|left|250px|Ahsoka dueling her former master Darth Vader on Malachor.]]
Tano arrived to fight Vader just in time to save Bridger's life, engaging in a fierce battle with the Sith Lord. Vader eventually knocked her off the top of the temple before returning to deal with the two Jedi, but Tano recovered and ambushed him from behind, cutting open part of his helmet to reveal his right [[eye]]. As the two resumed their fight, Jarrus and Bridger escaped in the ''[[Phantom]]'' before the Sith Temple exploded.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice" /> A few years later, Bridger entered a portal to the timeless [[World Between Worlds]] from the Lothal Jedi Temple<ref name="Wolves and a Door">{{Rebels|Wolves and a Door}}</ref> and discovered a portal through which he saw Tano's continued fight with Vader. Tano was being forced back, beginning to lose her ground in the duel, so Bridger pulled her into the timeless Force realm just before the destruction of the temple.<ref name="A World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref>

After spending some time in the World Between Worlds, Tano and Bridger were forced to retreat after being confronted by [[Sith alchemy]] cast through another [[portal]] by the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]], Tano using her lightsabers to break it off the [[fiery energy]] from pulling Bridger to make him coming through the portal as she and Bridger fled. Separating, they headed back through the portals from which they had come, with Tano promising to find Bridger again someday.<ref name="A World Between Worlds" /> Back on Malachor, Tano headed beneath<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> the ruins of the Sith Temple<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice" /> and embarked on a spiritual journey that changed the course of her life before making her way off-world. Years later still, in the aftermath of the [[Galactic Civil War]], Tano continued carrying her blades.<ref name="USWNE" /> While traveling with [[Professor]] [[Huyang]], Tano also began training Bridger's friend [[Sabine Wren]] as her apprentice, but their relationship ended on bad terms.<ref name="Part Two">{{Ahsoka|Part Two: Toil and Trouble}}</ref>

===Hunting the Grand Admiral===
{{Quote|Now tell me, where is your master? Where is Grand Admiral Thrawn?|Tano to Magistrate Morgan Elsbeth|<ref name="Chapter 13">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref>}}
[[File:PabloOlivera-AhsokaTanoprint.png|thumb|right|300px|Tano wields her lightsabers on Corvus in 9 ABY]]
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="The Mandalorian Date">{{MandoDate|13}}</ref> Tano was still using her white lightsabers while [[Quest to find Ezra Bridger|searching]] for [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn." Having learned that<ref name="Chapter 13" /> a former [[Nightsisters|Nightsister witch]],<ref name="Part One">{{Ahsoka|Part One: Master and Apprentice}}</ref> [[Magistrate]] [[Morgan Elsbeth]], was a servant of the Grand Admiral, Tano traveled to the [[forest]]ed planet [[Corvus]]. She became a threat to Elsbeth's rule of the city of [[Calodan]] and used her lightsabers to defeat Elsbeth's [[scout guard]]s. The following day, after mistaking him for an enemy and attacking him with her white sabers, Ahsoka gained the help of [[Din Djarin]], and she was able to infiltrate the magistrate's sanctuary. Armed with a [[Beskar spear|spear]] of pure [[beskar]], Elsbeth engaged Tano in combat, and even managed to knock one of her lightsabers into a pond. Tano, left with only her [[Shoto lightsaber|shoto]], still gained the upper hand and disarmed Elsbeth, and demanded the whereabouts of Grand Admiral Thrawn. In defeating Elsbeth, Ahsoka ended the magistrate's rule of Calodan, and set free its oppressed villagers,<ref name="Chapter 13" /> but Elsbeth was later [[Attack on the Vesper|freed from New Republic custody]] by two hired mercenaries, former [[Jedi]] [[Baylan Skoll]] and his apprentice, [[Shin Hati]].<ref name="Part One" />

Tano continued to wield her lightsabers as she reunited with Wren and continued her search for Thrawn with Huyang and her Padawan.<ref name="Part Two" /> In that time, Tano used her lightsabers against Elsbeth's [[HK-87 assassin droid]]s<ref name="Part One" /> and scout guards, the former [[Inquisitor]] [[Marrok]], and Skoll.<ref name="Part Three">{{Ahsoka|Part Three: Time to Fly}}</ref> In the end, Tano and Wren would discover Thrawn and Bridger on the [[extragalactic]] planet [[Peridea]], where Tano engaged in a second duel against Skoll<ref name="Part Seven">{{Ahsoka|Part Seven: Dreams and Madness}}</ref> and, together with Bridger and Wren, fought Thrawn's [[Night Trooper]]s in an effort to prevent his [[Battle of Peridea|escape from Peridea]]. In that battle, the Night Troopers were slain but risen from the dead by Thrawn's allies,<ref name="Part Eight">{{Ahsoka|Part Eight: The Jedi, the Witch, and the Warlord}}</ref> the Nightsisters<ref name="Aktropaw Cutout">{{WebCite|url=https://advancedgraphics.com/aktropaw-nightsister-life-size-cardboard-cutout|text=Aktropaw (Nightsister) Life-Size Cardboard Cutout|archivedate=20231005002437}}</ref> known as the [[Great Mothers]], which left the Night Troopers able to rise after lightsaber strikes or deflected blaster bolts. Tano also fought and killed a [[Blade of Talzin]]-wielding Elsbeth, whom had been formally welcomed into the Nightsister order by the Mothers, although her shoto lightsaber was sliced in half and destroyed during the duel. While Bridger was able to escape back to the galaxy about Thrawn's [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]'', Tano, Wren, and Huyang were left stranded on Peridea.<ref name="Part Eight" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ahsoka's white lightsabers first appeared in the TV episode "[[Fire Across the Galaxy]]," the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|season one]] finale for ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> According to [[Dave Filoni]], one of the show's creators, the lightsaber blades are white to reflect her non-affiliation with the Jedi or Sith.<ref name="Fire Recon" /> During production of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' television series, property master [[Josh Roth]] noticed that Tano's lightsabers kept getting bigger because of the batteries, receivers, and wireless gak they put inside them.<ref name="Ahsoka transformation">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-mandalorian-guide-to-season-two-exclusive-excerpt|text=Explore Ahsoka Tano's Transformation in ''Star Wars: The Mandalorian: Guide to Season Two'' – Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref> Director<ref name="Chapter 13" /> Filoni said the lightsabers could not look like that, so they ended up being powered by an external battery.<ref name="Ahsoka transformation" />
