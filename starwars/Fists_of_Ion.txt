"'''Fists of Ion'''" is a [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]]-exclusive short story written by [[Edward M. Erdelac]] and illustrated by [[Cat Staggs]]. The story was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 31]], [[2008]]. On October 1, 2012, it was republished on [[Unbound Worlds|Suvudu]], a subsidiary website of [[Random House]] publishing.

"Fists of Ion" is told as an excerpt from "Chapter 7: Hard Raine" of the [[in-universe]] novel ''[[Fists Of Ion: Memoirs Of A Champion Shockboxer]]''. The excerpt was published in ''[[Transgalactic Perspectus]]'' 41:4:34.

==Plot summary==
This story is presented as a magazine excerpt from an [[Autobiocron|autobiography]] of champion [[Shockboxing/Legends|shockboxer]] [[Lobar Aybock]]. Lobar recounts the night when en route to a shockboxing match with a [[Barabel/Legends|Barabel]] champion, he is intercepted by a van full of [[New Republic Intelligence Service|New Republic Intelligence]] [[Agent/Legends|agents]]. The agents inform the boxer that a local [[Gangster/Legends|gangster]] is rigging the fight so that Lobar will lose and he can sell the [[Athlete/Legends|athlete's]] valuable organs. The NRI offers to counter-rig the match, so Lobar can fight fairly. He wins the match, inspired by the opportunity to break the criminal hold on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] that has kept so many impoverished.

==Continuity==
The main character's name, [[Lobar Aybock]], was an anagram of [[Wikipedia: Rocky Balboa|Rocky Balboa]].<ref name="TERC" />
