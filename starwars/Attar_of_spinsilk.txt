'''Attar of spinsilk''' was a type of [[liquor]] that was green in [[color]]. It had a soft yet spicy flavor that left a tingling sensation on the [[tongue]]. Attar of spinsilk was a favorite of the [[human]] [[Vellis San Tekka]]. In the aftermath of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> a [[Service droid|servitor droid]] served the drink to the [[Jedi]] [[Avar Kriss]] and [[Elzar Mann]] in a stemless [[glass]] bowl when the pair met with San Tekka and his [[Marriage|husband]], [[Marlowe San Tekka]], at their [[Varykino]] [[San Tekka compound|home]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. Both Jedi found the drink delicious, with Kriss noting its finery, and discussed their consumption of it with Vellis afterward.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
