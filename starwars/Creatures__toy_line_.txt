'''Creatures''' is the name for the toy line sold at the [[Creature Stall]] at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. The line mostly features both plush and interactive, electronic animal toys which customers can "adopt." Most of the products feature red and gray labels with the word "Creatures" in both English and in Aurebesh.<ref name="WDWNT Every" />

Other product lines are dedicated to some specific shops such as [[Droid Depot]] and [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]], while the [[Black Spire Outpost (toy line)|Black Spire Outpost toy line]] is available in various locations.
