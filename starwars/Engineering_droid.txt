==Behind the scenes==
Engineering droid was first mentioned in the current [[Canon|''Star Wars'' canon]] via "[[The Mutiny]]," the fourteenth episode of the [[Disney Channel]] animated series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''{{'s}} [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]], which aired on [[January 5]], [[2020]].<ref name="Air date">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> The droid type later appeared in the [[2021]] [[Del Rey]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> In [[2025]],<ref name="Tenoo's Fastest Release">{{SW|url=news/young-jedi-adventures-season-2-and-nubs-shorts|text=''Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures'' Returns for Season 2}}</ref> the engineering droid received its first visual depiction in "[[Tenoo's Fastest]]," the first part of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|second season]]'s [[Tenoo's Fastest / Home Sweet Temple|fifteenth episode]] of the [[Disney+]] and [[Disney Junior]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''.<ref name="Tenoo's Fastest">{{YJA|Tenoo's Fastest}}</ref> For the animated series, the droid was designed by [[Wikipedia:Artist|artist]] [[Chris Blackstock]].<ref name="Blackstock">{{WebCite|url=https://blackstockart.com/young-jedi-adventures|text=Young Jedi Adventures|work=[[Chris Blackstock]]'s [https://blackstockart.com official website]|archivedate=20250306020751}}</ref>
