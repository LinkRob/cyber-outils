The '''draped overcloak''' was an elegant style of full-body [[clothing]] worn by the [[Alderaanian]] [[senator]]ial [[aide]] [[Stonroy Soma]] during the [[Invasion of Naboo|Naboo Crisis]]. Stonroy's draped overcloak was created from [[Color|mauve]] fabric woven with ornate square motifs and [[gold]]en lining, under which he wore an ankle-length white tunic.<ref name="VE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Stonroy Soma was to appear as a background character wearing the draped overcloak in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], in which he was portrayed by an unidentified extra.<ref name="TPM cut">{{Film|I}}, deleted scene featured on the Blu-ray edition.</ref> The draped overcloak was first identified in ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="VE" />
