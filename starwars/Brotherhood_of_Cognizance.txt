The '''Brotherhood of Cognizance''' was a [[Religion|religious]] order based on [[Naboo]], that consisted of 20 [[hermit]]s and built a secluded abbey on the shores of the [[Lake Country]]. It was a contemplative order seeking enlightenment from studying and worshipping art. [[Standard century|Centuries]] prior to<ref name="BMF54" /> [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Brotherhood's [[Pontifex]] would regularly legalise [[marriage]]s, a law that was still in order during the last [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. The Pontifex [[Maxiron Agolerga]] legalised the marriage between [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]]<ref name="BMF54" /> in 22 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> after the practice hasn't been performed in 300 [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="BMF54" />
