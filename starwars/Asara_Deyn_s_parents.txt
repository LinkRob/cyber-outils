{{Quote|The ISB's investigations into Asara suggested that he grew up on Akiva with his parents and his sister Calys|ISB report on Asara Deyn|<ref name="Outlaws" /> }}
The '''parents''' of the [[Twi'lek]] [[Asara Deyn]] and his [[Gender|sister]] [[Calys]] raised the pair on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Calys started a [[Calys's rebel cell|rebel cell]] on Akiva however when she made contact with a representative of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] she decided to leave Akiva and join the alliance. After Calys told Asara of her plans, he became scared and told his parents of her plans. However that did not stop her and she later slipped away during the night to join the alliance and she [[death|died]] [[Standard month|month]]s later during the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Asara Deyn's parents were mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They were mentioned in Asara's ISB report collectible as well as the game's character databank.<ref name="Outlaws" />
