A '''clone major''' was a [[Military rank|rank]] that could be held by [[clone trooper]]s in the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. [[Major]]s were able to serve in the Grand [[Army]]'s [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]]s.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clone majors were first mentioned and pictured in the [[2018]] release of [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']], an updated [[canon]] version of the<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> previously released [[2013]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2013)|edition]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2013">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2013)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']]</ref>
