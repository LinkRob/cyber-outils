'''Brel''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Tsevuka system]]<ref name="TLO" /> in the [[Cademimu sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="SWSystems" /> The [[Merchant Uprising]] was massacre that took place on the planet during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="TLO" />

{{Planet-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Brel appeared in a memory in flashbacks in the [[2025]] young-adult novel ''[[The Last Order]]'', written by [[Kwame Mbalia]]. The novel places Brel within the Tsevuka [[Star system|system]] and [[Wild Space]].<ref name="TLO" /> However, the system is actually located in the Outer Rim. This article treats the placement in Wild Space as incorrect.<ref name="SWSystems" />
