"'''A Servant of the Empire'''" was to be a short story written by [[James L. Cambias]] that was scheduled to appear in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 16|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 16]]. However, due to the cancellation of issue 16, the short story never made it into the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal|''Adventure Journal'']].

It was later published on the Star Wars Expanded Universe Timeline website, along with other canceled stories from the ''Adventure Journal''.<ref name="SWEUT">{{WebCite|url=http://www.starwarstimeline.net/AdventureJournal16/ServantoftheEmpire.htm|text=Servant of the Empire|author=[[James L. Cambias|Cambias, James L.]]|work=[http://www.starwarstimeline.net Star Wars Expanded Universe Timeline]|archivedate=20200209101102}}</ref>
