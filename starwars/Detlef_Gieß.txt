'''Detlef Gieß''' is a [[Wikipedia:German|German]] [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice artist]]. Gieß played [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] in the [[German Rebels audio dramas]] which adapted a number of episodes of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' animated television series.<ref name="German Rebels" />
