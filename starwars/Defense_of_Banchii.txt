The '''Defense of Banchii''' was carried out in [[231 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> when a [[Unidentified settlement (Banchii)|farming settlement]] on the [[planet]] [[Banchii]] was attacked by the [[Drengir]]. The Drengir were able to kill several farmers before being stopped by members of the [[Jedi Order]] under the command of [[Jedi Master]] [[Arkoff]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Lily Tora-Asi]].<ref name="Edge of Balance 1" />
