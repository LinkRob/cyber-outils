The '''bubse tree''' was a type of bulbous [[tree]] [[Homeworld|indigineous]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Saleucami]]. [[Plant|Root]] segments and [[mineral]] [[water]] harvested from the plant fetched high [[Currency|prices]] as specialized medicinal exports with the aesthetes of the [[Zealots of Psusan]],<ref name="BMF44" /> a [[Religion|religious]] cult.<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], bubse trees were first mentioned in "Visiting Saleucami," an article published by [[De Agostini]] in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the magazine [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 44|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 44]]<ref name="BMF44" /> around [[November 4]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|44}}</ref> The [[Bubse tree/Legends|trees]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
