The [[Ashiga Clan]]'s '''field report for Toshara''' was a report that detailed the local government operates, commerce, wildlife, and terrain of the [[savanna]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> It was compiled in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>  by an [[Unidentified Ashiga scout (Toshara)|Ashiga scout]]  to examine the potential for the clan to expand its interest to the moon.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That year,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> while in the [[Ashiga Clan District]] of [[Kijimi City]], the [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] was hired by a [[myrmitrix]] [[Unidentified Ashiga myrmitrix (Ashiga scouts)|member]] of the Clan to pick up the scout's intelligence report and return it to [[Kijimi]]. She later meet with the scout inside [[Daruda Diner]] in the Tosharan [[city|town]] [[Jaunta's Hope]] where the pair exchanged the field report for the nutrient pack. Vess returned to the myrmitrix in Kijimi City later that year to delivered the report and [[Field report for Akiva|another]] about the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. After thanking the [[human]] for her service to the clan, the myrmitrix transferred Vess five hundred [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The field report for Akiva appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, the report appeared as a quest item for "Ashiga Scouts" optional intel quest.<ref name="Outlaws" />
