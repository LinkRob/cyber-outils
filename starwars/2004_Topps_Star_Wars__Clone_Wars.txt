'''2004 Topps ''Star Wars: Clone Wars''''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] in [[April]] [[2004]] which covers the events of the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' animated series. It had a total of 90 cards, featuring stills from the show.<ref name="SW" />
