The '''corkscrew maneuver''' was a type of [[Navy|naval]] tactic that [[starship]]s could used to evade enemies while in battle. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[human]] [[pilot]] [[Pikka Adren]] used one such maneuver during the [[Battle of Eriadu]] in a last-ditch effort to elude three [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] starships.<ref name="Out with a Bang" />

==Behind the scenes==
The corkscrew maneuver first appeared in the short story "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Out with a Bang]]," written by [[Lydia Kang]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Out with a Bang" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref> The story was published in the [[Star Wars Insider 231|231st issue]] of the magazine ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' as the eighth installment in the [[The High Republic: Tales from the Occlusion Zone|''Tales from the Occlusion Zone'']] serialized fiction on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Insider">{{TitanComics|url=m/179-star-wars-insider-231|text=Star Wars Insider #231}}</ref>
