The '''DC-17m Interchangeable Weapon System''',<ref name="CVD" /> also known as the '''DC-17m Repeating Blaster Rifle''',<ref name="EA Community Transmission">{{EA|subdomain=battlefront-forums|url=discussion/143163/community-transmission-a-look-ahead-at-what-s-coming-in-2019|text=Community Transmission &ndash; A look ahead at what's coming in 2019|author=F8RGE}}</ref> '''DC-17m ICWS Blaster Carbine''',<ref name="Characters page">{{AtomicMassGames|url=character/clone-republic-commandos|text=SWL118 Clone Republic Commandos}}</ref> or '''DC-17M ICWS''' for short,<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> was a type of [[Reconfigurable blaster|reconfigurable]] [[Repeating blaster|repeating]] [[blaster rifle]] used by [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[clone commando]]s during the [[Clone Wars]] and the early days of the Empire.<ref name="CVD" /> It could change between a repeating blaster rifle and [[grenade launcher]] by switching the weapon's [[barrel]] module.<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> Other configurations included a [[sniper]] mode identified as the "Sniper Config" and an anti-[[armor]] mode known as the "Anti-Armor Config."<ref name="Scene to Stats" />

==History==
The DC-17m was used during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II" /> waged between the [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> but was highly similar in design to a blaster seen during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 1">[[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 1|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 1]]</ref> During the Clone Wars, the DC-17m was used by [[clone commando]]s in service of the [[Galactic Republic]], using it in combat against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II" /> The weapon was also used by [[pirate]]s from [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[Ohnaka Gang|gang]].<ref name="A Test of Strength" /> The weapon continued to be used in the early [[Imperial Era]] by the clone commandos who served the new [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="War-Mantle">{{TBB|War-Mantle}}</ref>

The DC-17m Interchangeable Weapon System made its first prominent canon appearance in "[[Missing in Action]]," a [[2013]] episode for the TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]],''<ref name="Missing in Action">{{TCW|Missing in Action}}</ref> though it was previous seen held by an [[Unidentified Weequay pirate (Crucible)|unnamed pirate]] in the episode "[[A Test of Strength]]"<ref name="A Test of Strength" /> and a background clone trooper on board the ''[[Negotiator]]'' in "[[Bound for Rescue]]". The blaster was identified in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''.<ref name="CVD" /> The blaster first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[2005]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]''.<ref name="Republic Commando">''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]''</ref>
