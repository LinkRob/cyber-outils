'''Amanda Serino''' was the set decorator for eight episodes of the live-action [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', as well for two episodes of the series ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'', and two episodes of the series ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]''.
