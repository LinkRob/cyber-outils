{{Quote|One of the greatest Imperial successes of the Galactic Civil War came via the destruction of a significant rebel convoy in the Derra system&hellip;|Beaumont Kin, ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
The '''ambush of a rebel convoy at Derra''' was a clash between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and [[Galactic Empire]] that occurred at the<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> [[Expansion Region]] astronomical object<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[Derra IV|Derra]] during the Empire's [[Project Swarm|search]] for the rebel base after the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Although the ambush did not provide the Empire with the location of the base, it fit together with chatter about increased rebel operations in the region that had been tracked by [[Imperial Intelligence]]. Following the ambush, the Imperial search for the rebel base intensified.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> According to the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] in his book ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', the battle saw [[Rae Sloane]] in command of the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|Viligance]]'' and resulted in her promotion from [[Vice Admiral]] to the full [[admiral]] rank.<ref name="Rise" /> In reality, however, Sloane was still a [[commodore]] until the aftermath of the [[Battle of Hoth]]. She was granted command of the ''Vigilance'' in the post-Hoth period,<ref name="Lord Vader">{{StoryCite|story=Lord Vader Will See You Now|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> although it was true that she already had a connection to that vessel.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ambush of a rebel convoy at Derra was mentioned within the [[canon]] source ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> The battle comes from [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] material, which depicted the engagement as the [[Battle of Derra IV]].<ref name="XWRS25">[[X-Wing Rogue Squadron 25|''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' 25]]</ref> This Legends battle had previously been referenced in canon through [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 3|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 3]] as an [[ambush on Red Squadron]].<ref name="HC3">{{HelmetCollectionCite|3|Databank A-Z}}</ref> However, no canon source has linked the ambush of a rebel convoy and the ambush on Red Squadron at this time.

More detail was provided about the battle in canon in the book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' by Doctor [[Chris Kempshall]], which is framed as an in-universe text by historian Beaumont Kin. According the book, the battle saw Rae Sloane promoted from the rank of Vice Admiral to a full admiral.<ref name="Rise" /> However, the short stories "[[The Final Order]]"<ref name="Final Order">{{StoryCite|story=The Final Order|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> and "[[Lord Vader Will See You Now]]" establish she was still a commodore in the aftermath of the Battle of Hoth,<ref name="Lord Vader" /> which occurs after the ambush. Further, the book alleges that Sloane was in command of the ''Vigilance'' during the battle,<ref name="Rise" /> which she only received command of in the short story "Lord Vader Will See You Now."<ref name="Lord Vader" /> However, she did already have a history with the ship according to the video game ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''.<ref name="Squadrons" />
