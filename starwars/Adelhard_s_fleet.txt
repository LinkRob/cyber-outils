'''Adelhard's fleet'''<ref name="Ewoks 1">[[Ewoks (2024) 1|''Ewoks'' (2024) 1]]</ref> was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[sector fleet]] was assigned to the defense of the [[Anoat sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It counted a number of [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]], including the ''[[Dread Omen]]''.

Following the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] at the [[Battle of Endor]] in the [[Standard year|year]] [[4 ABY]], the [[sector fleet]] became the backbone of [[Grand Moff]]<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]], enabling him to set up the [[Iron Blockade]]&mdash;the lockdown of the entire Anoat [[sector]]. However, a resistance group known as [[Uprising]] fought the [[blockade]], even infiltrating the ''Dread Omen''.<ref name="Uprising" /> Around the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]], the blockade had been defeated.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

==Description==
The [[Anoat sector]] [[fleet]] consisted of at least eight [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]], including the fleet's [[flagship]], ''[[Dread Omen]]''<ref name="Uprising" /> and [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s flagship, the [[Scepter (Imperial-class)|''Scepter'']].<ref name="Under Siege 4" />
