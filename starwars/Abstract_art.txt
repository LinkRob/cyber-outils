'''Abstract art''' was an art movement characterized by nonrepresentational qualities. Abstract scultpures were often strange, but sophisticated and beautiful. In the [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]], [[Viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] was a notorious collector of abstract art. [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] also possessed similar works of art.<ref name="EV">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
