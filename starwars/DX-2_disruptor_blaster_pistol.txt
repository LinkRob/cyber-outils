The '''DX-2 disruptor blaster pistol''' or the '''DX-2 disruptor pistol''' was a type of [[disruptor]] [[blaster pistol]] capable of disintegrating organic beings. Although the DX-2 was prohibited by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], some members still used the weapon, including [[Brand]] of the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]].

==Description==
The DX-2 disruptor blaster pistol<ref name="Destiny">{{Destiny|set=Spark of Hope|cardname=DX-2 Disruptor Blaster Pistol|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/11153}}</ref> was a [[disruptor]] [[blaster pistol]] that could disintegrate organic beings. The DX-2 disruptor pistol was also powerful enough to open a [[metal]] hatch with just two shots.<ref name="Twilight Company" /> Gray in [[color]],<ref name="Destiny" /> it could be wielded with one [[Limb|hand]]<ref name="Twilight Company" /> but was also able to be gripped with two hands.<ref name="Destiny" />

==History==
Disruptor [[weapon]]s were banned throughout [[the galaxy]] during the [[Imperial Era]],<ref name="Droids in Distress">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref> and the DX-2 was banned by the regulations of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] due to its destructive potential. Despite this, some operatives used the weapon anyway.<ref name="Destiny" /> [[Brand]], a member of the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]], owned a modified DX-2 disruptor as her sidearm during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Alliance and the [[Galactic Empire]]. She took great care to maintain the rare and valuable weapon and was willing to wield it publicly when necessary despite the regulations against it.<ref name="Twilight Company" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> when the 61st Infantry's [[CR90 corvette|corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'' was [[Infiltration of the Thunderstrike|infiltrated]], Brand used the weapon to re-enter the [[Corvette|ship]] and disintegrate several invading Imperials as the rebels reclaimed the [[Starship|vessel]]. Shortly after the conflict, Brand sold her DX-2 disruptor for a hefty [[Currency|price]] on the [[planet]] [[Ankhural]], an action she regretted having to take.<ref name="Twilight Company" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the disruptor first appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'' by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Twilight Company" /> It was later pictured and identified as the DX-2 disruptor blaster pistol on a [[Star Wars: Destiny|''Star Wars'': Destiny]] [https://swdestinydb.com/card/11153 card], illustrated by [[Greg Bobrowski]] and released as part of the expansion pack ''[[Spark of Hope]]''<ref name="Destiny" /> on [[July 5]], [[2019]].<ref name="Release Date">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/7/5/available-now-july-5|text=Available Now &mdash; July 5}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[DX-2 disruptor pistol/Legends|DX-2]] was first mentioned in the [[1997]] sourcebook ''[[The Essential Guide to Weapons and Technology]]'', written by [[Bill Smith]].<ref name="EGWT">''[[The Essential Guide to Weapons and Technology]]''</ref> It then first appeared in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']], developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment.<ref name="SWGED">{{SWGcite|exp=AED|type=item|DX2 Pistol}}</ref>
