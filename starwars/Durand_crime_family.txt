The '''Durand crime family'''<ref name="Isa Durand">{{Databank|isa-durand|Isa Durand}}</ref> was a crime family<ref name="Infested" /> which existed in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of [[The Bad Batch Season 1|''The Bad Batch'' Season 1]] in [[19 BBY]].</ref> The organization was led by [[Isa Durand]] and among its members was her son, the [[Devaronian]] [[Roland Durand]]. The Durands lived by the philosophy of "You take what you want," a tactic of which Roland stated that his mother had perfected.<ref name="Infested" />
