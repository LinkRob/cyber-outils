'''Agruss''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Zygerrian]] [[enslaver]] and [[keeper]] of the [[Kadavo slave processing facility]] during the time of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Slaves of the Republic" /> After [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] were captured on Zygerria, Agruss became their slave master and oversaw the slave work at the Kadavo facility. When the [[104th Battalion]] arrived to [[Battle of Kadavo|liberate]] the slaves, he was [[Death|killed]] by Rex when he impaled him with an [[electrostaff]].<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" />
