'''Ardis San Tekka''' was a member of the [[San Tekka clan]]. She was a creature [[cartographer]] and [[artist]] as a member of the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]]. She, along with her [[safari droid]] [[B8-T5]], complied a [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]] about the various creatures of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Bestiary" />
