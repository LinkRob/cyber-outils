'''Feather corals''' were a type of [[coral]] from<ref name="LOTJ" /> the [[waterworld]]<ref name="TUC">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref> [[Glee Anselm]] that grew in large spindly structures. While serving as a [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[captain]] aboard the [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] ''[[Aurora IX]]''<ref name="LOTJ" /> in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> Glee Anselm [[Homeworld|native]] [[Finial Bright]] compared the shape of [[Solar Array 22-X]]&mdash;a [[space station]] in the [[Hetzal system]]&mdash;to the feather corals on his homeworld.<ref name="LOTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Feather corals were mentioned in [[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi|''Light of the Jedi'']], a [[2021]] novel written by [[Charles Soule]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="LOTJ" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
