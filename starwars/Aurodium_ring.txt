'''Aurodium rings''' were [[ring]]s fashioned with [[aurodium]], and were worn by many different being across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an aurodium ring could be sold for thirty two [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aurodium rings appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game, the ring could only be sold for credits, and did not have another function.<ref name="Outlaws" />
