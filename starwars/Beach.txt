A '''beach''' was a landform made of sand that was normally located on the edge of a body of [[water]] and land. The [[planet]] [[Scarif]] contained numerous beaches, many of which connected to jungle canopies.<ref name="RO UVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> An [[Island Four|island]] on the [[moon]] [[Wasskah]] had a large beach which sloped into the surrounding water.<ref name="Padawan Lost">{{TCW|Padawan Lost}}</ref> In addition, the [[Wookiee]] [[city]] of [[Kachirho]], located on the planet [[Kashyyyk]], was built along a sandy beach.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith">{{Film|III}}</ref>  In some cases, [[planet]]s known for their beaches opened to tourism, such as planets [[Iloh]]<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> and [[Niamos]],<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> and [[Sesid]].<ref name="Moving Target">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref>

During the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]], a specialised branch of the [[Stormtrooper Corps]], [[Coastal defender stormtrooper|Coastal defender Troopers]], would patrol beaches to enforce imperial rule<ref name="Rogue One: A Star Wars Story">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref><ref name="Announcement" />, sometimes with the help of [[KX-series security droid|KX-series droids]].<ref name="Announcement" />
