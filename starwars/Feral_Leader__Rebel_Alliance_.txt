A '''pilot''' served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during [[Galactic Civil War]] with the [[Galactic Empire]]. This [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] served under the [[callsign]] [[Feral Leader]] in the [[Battle of Cawa City]], where they flew a [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]] along with other members of [[Feral Group]]. They were tasked with destroying an [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] with the support of [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wings]] from [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]]. The Y-wings dropped [[magnetising cluster bomb]]s that magnetized to the hull and destroyed the AT-AT.<ref name="SE2" />
