'''Charhound treats''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|treat]] that shared their name with<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> [[charhound]]s, a type of [[Diet|carnivorous]] [[canine]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Planet|world]] [[Elphrona]].<ref name="Charhound GoC">{{GoC|Charhound}}</ref> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> after [[Living Force|Force]]-consuming [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures escaped from containment aboard the [[Elite-class vessel|''Elite''-class vessel]] ''[[Innovator]]'', the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Kildo]] suggested getting the Nameless into the [[Starship|ship's]] [[escape pod]]s. [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] responded by questioning how Kildo intended to do so, asking if he would use charhound treats. The Jedi ultimately did not use charhound treats to lure the Nameless.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Charhound treats were mentioned in the [[2024]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref>
