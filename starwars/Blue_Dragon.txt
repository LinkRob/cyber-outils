The '''Blue Dragon''' was a [[pirate]] organization based in the [[Aakaash system]] of the [[Oplovis sector]] and were the most powerful crime syndicate in the [[Star system|system]], the Blue Dragon had ties to high-ranking officials of the [[Land & Sky Corporation]], the company that ruled the Aakaash system. The criminal organization was led by an individual known as the "[[dragon head]]," and was hostile towards the [[Silver Knights]], [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] who worked for profit as well as to supply their [[Home of the Silver Knights|community]] on the [[planet]] [[Wind]], an [[orbit]] of the Aakaash system.<ref name="Vow" />
