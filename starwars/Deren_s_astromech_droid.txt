An '''astromech droid''' served the [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] [[captain]] [[Deren]], who was in charge of protection for the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] of the [[Pijali monarchy]]. When a [[Pijali religion|ceremonial]] [[Fanry's Grand Hunt|Grand Hunt]] was hosted prior to Fanry's [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]]<ref name="MA" /> in [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' to [[40 BBY]].</ref> Deren's [[astromech droid]] cycled through the sentry data from the event to ensure its security, with the captain further double-checking the former's work. Despite this, the [[Grand Hunt|event's]] [[Hunting|prey]]&mdash;a [[LM-432 crab droid|crab droid]]&mdash;[[Attack on the Grand Hunt|attacked]] the princess, leaving her emotionally shaken but otherwise unharmed. Afterward, Deren reported to Pijal's [[Jedi]] [[lord]] [[regent]], [[Rael Averross]], in the [[regent's audience chamber]], explaining the preventative work he and his astromech had done, albeit admitting it had not been enough to prevent the attack.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The astromech droid appeared in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
