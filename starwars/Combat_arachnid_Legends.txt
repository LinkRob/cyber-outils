The '''combat arachnid''' was a [[Species/Legends|species]] of arachnid similar to [[Acklay/Legends|acklays]]. They possessed twelve legs, were covered in spikes and thorns, and were very fierce. They had a mouth full of razor sharp teeth, roughly fifteen eyes, and vocalizations in the ultrasonic range.<ref name="A Boy and His Monster">{{StoryCite|story=A Boy and His Monster: The Rancor Keeper's Tale|book=Tales from Jabba's Palace}}</ref> They were native to the [[Planet/Legends|world]] of [[Carida/Legends|Carida]] prior to its destruction, however a small population of these creatures lived in the ruins of [[Kuar]]. Their carapaces had a gem-like quality, and the arachnids were sometimes raised purely for that purpose, though even the damage incurred during a battle left plenty for trophies.<ref name="A Boy and His Monster" />

Combat arachnids were renowned for their prowess and fighting capability; [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] ordered a trio of them to fight his pet [[Rancor/Legends|rancor]]; the rancor ultimately prevailed, however it was badly wounded in the fight.<ref name="A Boy and His Monster" /> They were often used as opponents in training exercises for the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] [[Stormtrooper Corps/Legends|Stormtrooper Corps]].
