'''379 BBY''', or '''379<ref name="JediDate" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Around that year,<ref name="ShieldDate" /> the [[Barabel]] [[Petrish Imglor]] ascended to the rank of [[Jedi Knight]], and the [[City|village]] [[Peka]] on the [[Planet|world]] [[Batuu]] became subject to [[Local day|nightly]] raids by a [[Unidentified salmaca|salmaca]] creature. One of those raids resulted in the [[death]] of the villager [[Tuntis]]. [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] passed her [[Rooper Nitani's Jedi Trials|Jedi Trials]] by helping solve the issue with the [[salmaca]] and retrieving the [[Silandra Sho's shield|shield]] of her [[Jedi Master]], [[Silandra Sho]], and ascended to the rank of Jedi Knight.<ref name="Shield" />

Around the same year,<ref name="JediDate" /> [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the [[Evereni]]<ref name="Closed Fist">{{StoryCite|story=A Closed Fist Has No Claws|book=The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref> founder of a group of [[Pirate|raiders]] who would eventually become the [[Nihil]]<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref>&mdash;ran into a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi]] at a [[Unidentified marketplace|marketplace]] during<ref name="Closed Fist" /> her<ref name="GrattonInterview" /> [[Marda Ro's hunt|quest]] for other members of her [[species]] and assaulted him. Afterward, she [[Diet|fed]] him to a [[Living Force|Force]]-consuming [[Shrikarai|beast]] called the [[Great Leveler]] aboard her [[starship]], the ''[[Gaze Electric]]''.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

==Behind the scenes==
379 BBY simultaneously appeared in the [[George Mann]] short story "[[Shield of the Jedi]]"<ref name="Shield" /> and in a flashback in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> Both stories were published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Shield" /><ref name="Closed Fist" />
