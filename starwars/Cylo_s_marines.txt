The [[scientist]] [[Cylo|Dr Cylo]] maintained his own army of '''marines''' aboard [[Cylo's whale fleet|his whale fleet]]. Several marines were killed when [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Dr Aphra]], and her [[Chelli Lona Aphra's squad|squad]] breached [[Cylo's base|Cylo's flagship]].<ref name="Darth Vader 5" /> These soldiers saw action against [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces during Cylo's [[Cylo Hijacking|attempted hijacking]] of the [[Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Executor]]'' as part of a plot to overthrow [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Darth Vader 23">[[Darth Vader (2015) 23|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 23]]</ref> Cylo's remaining marines were killed by Lord Vader aboard [[Cylo's second flagship]], which crashed into a [[star]], killing Cylo.<ref name="Darth Vader 25">[[Darth Vader (2015) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 25]]</ref>
