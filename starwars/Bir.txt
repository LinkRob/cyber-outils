'''Bir''' was a terrestrial astronomical object with [[water]]falls<ref name="A Valiant Vow" /> that shared its name with the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[ocean]]ic [[moon]] [[Kef Bir]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: A Valiant Vow]]''|nearly two years after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], as the [[Hutt]] [[Churo]] was traveling aboard his [[Viperi-class cargo shuttle|starship]], the ''[[Lucky Tortle]]'', to<ref name="A Valiant Vow" /> the Outer Rim<ref name="Defy the Storm">''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Aricho]] with his mentor&mdash;the [[Shani]] [[biologist]] [[Glenna Kip]]&mdash;the [[Shuttle|ship]] was attacked by [[Nihil]] [[scav droid]]s.<ref name="A Valiant Vow" />

Upon seeing their destination was still thirty [[Standard minute|minutes]] away and knowing that the journey would be long, Churo thought about the things he had wanted to do but had yet to do, including exploring the waterfalls on Bir, which left him feeling that he had achieved nothing in life. The Hutt then came up with an idea to get the [[droid]]s off the ''Lucky Tortle''&mdash;a plan that, while successful, ultimately ended with the ship crashing on Aricho.<ref name="A Valiant Vow" />
