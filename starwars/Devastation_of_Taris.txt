At some point prior to the [[rebellion on Akiva]], the [[Ecumenopolis|city planet]] of [[Taris]] underwent a massive '''devastation''' that left much of the [[Planet|world]] in ruins. Although large chunks of the [[city]] were ultimately rebuilt, in which the ruling elite resided, much of it remained a wasteland, polluted by the fallout that the devastation left behind.<ref name="Aftermath" /> Wrecked [[starship]]s littered the surface, and ruined buildings sprawled across the landscape of regions such as the [[Talinn district]]. Over [[time]], the devastated landscape became overgrown with [[three-fingered vines]] and [[slime-slick fungus]]. Those who resettled the planet later on made makeshift homes out of old shipping containers.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The devastation of Taris was first mentioned in ''Star Wars'' [[canon]] in the novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']].<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Legends'']], the devastation in question was the [[Destruction of Taris|orbital bombardment]] of [[Taris/Legends|Taris]] by the [[Sith armada (Jedi Civil War)|naval forces]] of the [[Darth Revan's Sith Empire|Sith Empire]], as depicted in the video game ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''.<ref name="KOTOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref>
