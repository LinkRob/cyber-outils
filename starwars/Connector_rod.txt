'''Connector rods''' were rods sold in the [[Office of Acquisitions]] on the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]''. In [[34 ABY]], the [[First Order]] operative [[Teroj Kee]] asked the [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Kazuda Xiono]], who was working there that [[Standard day|day]], to deliver a crate of connector rods and other items to [[loading dock 23]]. Though Xiono did so, Kee trapped him in the storage locker, having intended to lure the former out of the store so he could [[Thief|steal]] a [[phase connector]] from its back room.

[[File:TerojKazudaLoadingDock-DangerousBusiness.png|thumb|right|300px|Teroj Kee tricked Kazuda Xiono into leaving the Office of Acquisitions to make a delivery&mdash;which included a crate of connector rods&mdash;so that he could steal from the establishment.]]
Connector rods were a type of rod sold in the [[Office of Acquisitions]]&mdash;owned by the proprietors [[Flix]] and [[Orka]]&mdash;on the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]''.<ref name="Dangerous Business" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="DBDate">{{ResistanceDate|1|13}}</ref> the [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] agreed to watch over the Office of Acquisitions for a [[Standard day|day]] to repay a debt to Flix and Orka. While posing as a representative from the [[Mining Guild]], the [[First Order]] operative [[Teroj Kee]] tried to buy a [[phase connector]] [[mining]] tool from the establishment, though Xiono informed him the only one in stock was already reserved. Afterward, the [[gorg]] [[Bitey]] bit Kee's [[Limb|hand]], prompting the annoyed First Order operative to ask Xiono to deliver a crate of connector rods, three [[tool kit]]s, and ten [[laser lens]]es to his storage locker at [[loading dock 23]] in the ''Colossus''{{'s}} [[Colossus loading docks|loading docks]]. Though Xiono was excited, believing the sale to be genuine, Kee instead intended to draw Xiono away from the shop so he could [[Thief|steal]] the phase connector device from the establishment's vault.<ref name="Dangerous Business" />

Xiono gathered the requested parts on a pallet and proceeded to deliver them despite his [[BB-series astromech droid|astromech droid]], [[BB-8]], finding the situation suspicious. Upon arrival, the mechanic was held at [[Teroj's blaster|blaster]]-point by Kee in the locker and trapped inside of it. Though Xiono escaped, the locker&mdash;including the connector rods and other parts inside&mdash;were dropped into the [[Ocean|waters]] of<ref name="Dangerous Business" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Castilon]] below.<ref name="Dangerous Business" />
