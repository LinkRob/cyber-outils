"'''Ahsoka's Ark'''" is a short comic story published in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic 6.36|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic'' 6.36]], and later in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Magazine 14|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars Magazine'' 14]].

==Publisher's summary==
''In this issue's all new ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' comic adventure ''Ahsoka's Ark'', Ahsoka is stuck on a babysitting duty aboard a ship carrying farm animals to terraformed planets. It may seem a strange job for a Jedi, but when the ship is overtaken by a group of angry farmers, can Ahsoka protect her noisy, smelly cargo?''

==Plot summary==
[[Ahsoka Tano]] undertakes a mission to the planet [[Felucia]]. The planet is facing an ecological crisis due to a drought, which has severely affected the local wildlife and the native [[Jungle Felucian|Felucians]]. Ahsoka discovers a group of trapped and endangered animals in a canyon. Determined to save them, she devises a plan to lead the creatures to safety. Using her skills in [[the Force]], Ahsoka guides the animals through the harsh terrain, ensuring they reach a more hospitable environment where they can survive. Throughout the mission, Ahsoka demonstrates her compassion and resourcefulness, highlighting her growth as a [[Jedi]] and her commitment to protecting all forms of life.
