The '''Earl of Vis''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[earl]] associated with [[Vis]].<ref name="Falcon" /> They were the cousin of a [[Monarch of Naboo|king]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] who ruled prior to the reign of [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]],<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> which began in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The earl built a [[banqueting rotunda]] in [[Theed]], Naboo's [[capital]] [[city]].<ref name="Falcon" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Earl of Vis was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 24|24th issue]] of the [[De Agostini]] magazine ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'',<ref name="Falcon" /> published around [[June 17]], [[2015]]<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|24}}</ref> and written by [[Tim Veekhoven]].<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:Credits-TimVeekhoven-Twitter.png|creator=[[Tim Veekhoven]]}}</ref> The [[Earl of Vis/Legends|Earl]] was originally mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'' by [[Kristin Lund]].<ref name="Insides">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
