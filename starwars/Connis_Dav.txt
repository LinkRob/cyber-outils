'''Connis Dav''' was a [[Firrerreo]] [[Jedi Initiate]] who was part of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Brotherhood" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] is set between the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] and the [[Battle of Christophsis]], both of which are dated to [[22 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> Dav was the youngest of a group of five [[Jedi]] [[youngling]]s who accompanied [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[D'urban Wen-Hurd]] on an [[Aid mission to Langston|aid mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Langston]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />
