A '''flexorcord''' or '''flexor cord''' was a [[rope]]-like material that had a number of uses. [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]]'s [[prod pistol]] had a bowstring made of flexorcord; several flexorcords were also used on [[HS-19 cargo loading transport skimmer]] to connect the steering vane with the front of the speeder.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
