'''Benyah Melax's ship parts''' was a storefront owned by [[starship]] [[Technician|mechanic]], [[Benyah Melax]]. It was located in the [[Kijimi Spaceport]] in [[Kijimi City]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Benyah Melax's ship parts first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. Melax was one of several ship mechanics in the game that could upgrade [[Kay Vess]]'s [[EML-850 light freighter]], the [[Trailblazer]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
