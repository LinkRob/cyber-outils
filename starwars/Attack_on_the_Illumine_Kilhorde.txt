In [[18 BBY]], the [[Unknown Regions]] [[Paataatus Hiveborn]] nation carried out an '''attack''' on the [[Illumine Kilhorde]], the battle [[fleet]] of the [[Kilji Illumine]] nation, after the fleet conquered several small nations to the north and northwest of the Hiveborn's territory as part of the Illumine's [[conquests of enlightenment]]. The Hiveborn deemed the Kilhorde's operations near their borders unacceptable, tasking a multipronged [[Paataatus attack fleet]] with destroying the Kilhorde after the [[Chiss]] [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] shared data on the Kilhorde's design and battle doctrine with the [[Paataatus]] [[species]].

The Hiveborn obliterated the Kilji battle fleet and freed the nations the Illumine's forces had conquered. The destruction of the Illumine's battle fleet prompted the [[Grysk]] "[[Jixtus]]," who had allied himself with the Illumine's ruler, [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]], to [[Death|murder]] the generalirius, as the Kilhorde's destruction rendered Nakirre useless to his ally.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Answer my question, and I'll tell you why it was a mistake to send the Nikardun against the Paataatus. You sent your Kilhorde against several of the small nations to the south and southeast of the Ascendancy, unfortunately for them, that also put them to the north and northwest of the Paataatus Hiveborn. Having a large military force operating that close to their borders was deemed unacceptable.|Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo, to "Jixtus" and Generalirius Nakirre|translated=1|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
[[File:GreaterGood-OOP-Art.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The Chiss Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo (pictured) shared data on the Kilhorde with the Paataatus Hiveborn.]]
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date-CR">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' in [[19 BBY]].</ref> the [[Grysk]] operative known as "[[Jixtus]]" tasked his [[Nikardun]] associate [[General]] [[Yiv]]<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> and his [[Nikardun Destiny]] hegemony with [[Nikardun conquests|conquering]] the nations surrounding the [[Unknown Regions]]' [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation, including the [[Paataatus]] species' [[Paataatus Hiveborn]]. Yiv conquered the Hiveborn; however, the Ascendancy [[Battle over Primea|reduced]] the<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> Destiny to remnants, one of which controlled the Hiveborn. The [[Chiss]] [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," [[Skirmish above Nettehi|freed]] the Paataatus [[homeworld]] of [[Nettehi]] from the remnant's control a [[Standard year|year]] later,<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> in [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Date-GG">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref>

That year,<ref name="Date" /> Jixtus allied himself with the ruler of the Unknown Regions' [[Kilji Illumine]] nation, [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]]. The Grysks gave the Illumine's battle [[fleet]], the [[Illumine Kilhorde]], permission to move against the small nations to the south and southeast of the Ascendancy to prevent any Chiss [[refugee]]s from fleeing to those nations as Jixtus and Nakirre instigated a [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|civil war]] in the Ascendancy. However, Thrawn [[Sunrise incident|retrieved]] data on the Kilhorde's design and combat doctrine from the [[Kilji picket cruiser]] [[Hammer (Kilji picket cruiser)|''Hammer'']] and shared the data with the Hiveborn. As the Kilji conquests took place south and southeast of the Ascendancy, they were positioned to the north and northwest of the Paataatus nation, which the Hiveborn found unacceptable. As a result, in gratitude for Thrawn's assistance in liberating their homeworld, the Paataatus sent a multipronged [[Paataatus attack fleet]] to attack the Kilhorde.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==The attack==
{{Quote|The mistake was that the Paataatus were quite unhappy at his attempt to conquer them. That anger was matched with an equal degree of gratitude for my assistance in breaking the last Nikardun stranglehold on them.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''And so&hellip;?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''And so they were happy to do me a favor.|Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo and "Jixtus"|translated=1|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
During the attack, the Hiveborn's fleet annihilated the Kilhorde and freed the conquered nations.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|You did this! Your arrogance&mdash;your refusal to accept the Kilji Illumine as true allies&mdash;your failure to provide adequate support for our conquests of enlightenment!|Nakirre, to Jixtus|translated=1|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
[[File:Colonial_Station_Chaf.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Nakirre speculated that his generals had extended their conquests to Colonial Station Chaf (bottom left).]]
Nakirre was alarmed when he stopped receiving reports of victory from the Kilhorde's [[Field Commander|field commanders]], prompting him to speculate that his generals had "foolishly" extended their conquests to the Ascendancy's [[planet]] of [[Colonial Station Chaf]]. He feared they had attacked the Chiss world, which would have disrupted the operation of the planet's [[long-range triad transmitter]]. The generalirius speculated that his commanders had not contacted him because they had disrupted the transmitter's operation, the only link between them and Nakirre.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

After Nakirre's [[Kilji war cruiser]] ''[[Whetstone]]'' arrived at the Unknown Regions [[Sunrise system|"Sunrise" system]], it encountered a [[Thrawn's fleet|fleet]] of [[Starship|vessels]] under Thrawn's [[Commanding officer|command]]. The senior captain told Jixtus, who was traveling aboard the ''Whetstone'', that he had made a mistake when tasking Yiv with conquering the Paataatus, stating that he had freed the Hiveborn and that the Paataatus were thankful for his assistance. After the Chiss told Nakirre that the Hiveborn had destroyed the Kilhorde as a favor to him, the generalirius snapped at Jixtus, prompting the Grysk to murder his ally, as the Kilji fleet's destruction had rendered Nakirre useless to him.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack was mentioned in the final novel of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''Ascendancy Trilogy'']], ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', which was published in [[2021]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
