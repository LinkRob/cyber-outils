'''Cantonican golden silk''',<ref name="Family Affair 1" /> also known as '''Cantonican gold silk''',<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was a type of [[silk]] woven into a brocade and was the [[color]] of [[gold]].<ref name="Family Affair 1" /> The silk originated from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[desert]]<ref name="Canto Bight DB">{{Databank|canto-bight|Canto Bight}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. It was used in the [[cape]] of former outlaw, [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. When an old acquaintance of Lando's, [[Clariah]] sat down to talk to him, she tried to enlist his help to stop her [[Jiandy|son's]] recent bad behavior, she offered to return his cape that he dearly missed. Lando became very excited and shouted out the names of various materials in his cape, including the Cantonican golden silk. He then agreed to help end her son's bad ways.<ref name="Family Affair 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cantonican gold silk appeared in the story "[[Tales from Wild Space: Family Affair, Part 1]]", which was part of the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 10|tenth issue]] of the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|Star Wars Adventures]] anthology [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series. The story was written by [[Pierrick Colinet]] and [[Elsa Charretier]], who also served as the colorist. The story published with the rest of the comic issue on [[May 9]], [[2018]].<ref name="Comixology">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2017-2020-10/digital-comic/652799|text=Star Wars Adventures #10|nolive=1}}</ref>
