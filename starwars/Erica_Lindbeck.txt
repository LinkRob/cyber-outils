'''Erica Lindbeck''' is an [[Wikipedia:America|American]] voice actress best known for being the official voice of the last generation of [[Wikipedia:Barbie|Barbie]] dolls. She has also done voice work for many [[Wikipedia:Anime|animes]] and provided the voice of [[Wikipedia:Black Cat (Marvel Comics)|Black Cat]] in [[Wikipedia:Insomniac Games|Insomniac Games]]' popular [[Wikipedia:Spider-Man (2018 video game)|''Spider-Man'']] video game. Lindbeck is also best known for voicing Daki in the anime Demon Slayer. 

In [[2019]], Lindbeck had a voice role in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''.<ref name="J:FO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> She later provided additional voices in the game's [[2023]] sequel, ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>
