The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''29 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3248 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7948''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] undertake a [[mission to Carnelion IV]] to investigate a [[distress signal]] on [[Carnelion IV]]<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin 1" />
**This ended the [[Open-Closed War|Open-Closed war]], because the [[Galactic Republic]] forces all hostilities to cease.<ref name="obiwanani5">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 5|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 5]]</ref>
*The [[Yam'rii]] of [[Huk]]<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> sought the Galactic Republic for assistance,<ref name="The Separatists">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Separatists}}</ref> and the [[Jedi]] intervened catastrophically in a [[Kaleesh-Yam'rii war|conflict]] between the [[Kaleesh]] and the Yam'rii,<ref name="Date">{{HelmetCollectionCite|8|Helmets}} places the [[Kaleesh-Yam'rii war]] [[Standard decade|decades]] earlier than the event of the [[Battle of Coruscant]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places in [[19 BBY]]; therefore, the conflict must take place in 29 BBY.</ref> supporting the [[insect]]oid [[species]]<ref name="Rise" /> against the invaders.<ref name="The Separatists" />

==Births==
*[[Nax]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Dates">{{KenobiDate|II|the appearance of the [[clone trooper]] veteran}} {{SW|url=obi-wan-kenobi-part-ii-trivia-gallery|text=Part II Trivia Gallery &#124; Obi-Wan Kenobi}} states that [[Nax]] is "20 or so" years old. As such, he would have been born in or around 29 BBY.</ref> on [[Kamino]]<ref name="Clone Troopers">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref>
*[[Hera Syndulla]] on [[Ryloth]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
*[[Zett Jukassa]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Zett">The script of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], as seen in ''[[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith Illustrated Screenplay]]'', states that [[Zett Jukassa]] is 10 [[Standard year|years]] old. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''Revenge of the Sith'' to [[19 BBY]], Jukassa must have been born around 29 BBY.</ref>
*[[Bom Vimdin]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Vimdin birth">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' states that [[Bom Vimdin]] was 28 [[Standard year|years]] old in 7977 [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]], also giving that date for the [[Battle of Scarif]]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the battle to [[1 BBY]], Vimdin must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around 29 BBY.</ref>
*[[Che'ri]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Cheri">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' states that [[Che'ri]]'s tenth [[starday]] occurred between the [[Battle over Primea]] and the [[Mission to Rapacc]]. The battle over [[Primea]] was portrayed in ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', which took place in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. ''Greater Good'' took place around [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, Che'ri must have had her tenth star day between those two [[Standard year|years]]. A star day celebrated a Chiss' first sight of the stars, which required them to already be born. Therefore, Cheri must have been born by around 29 BBY.</ref>
