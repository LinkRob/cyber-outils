'''Flimsiplast''', sometimes shortened to '''flimsi''', was a substance formed into flat thin sheets suitable for [[writing]] on. During the [[Imperial Era|age]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], flimsiplast was a more common physical writing medium than [[paper]].<ref name="TRB">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref> Nonetheless, it was regarded as an archaic technology compared to [[datapad]]s.<ref name="Any Work">{{StoryCite|story=Any Work Worth Doing|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref>

During the [[High Republic Era]], the library on [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Orlen Mollo]]'s ship the ''[[Paxion]]'' contained a small shelf of ancient flimsiplast books among a larger collection of [[holobook]]s.<ref name="The High Republic: Convergence">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]''</ref>

[[Tiaan Jerjerrod]] preferred using flimsiplast ever since his days as an [[engineer]]ing student. He would spend his discretionary funds on antique reams of flimsiplast to maintain his habit. Even when he spearheaded the Empire's construction of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], he maintained schematics for the [[battle station]] on flimsiplast.<ref name="Any Work" />

By the end of the Empire's reign, flimsiplast maps were regarded as a curiosity.<ref name="Aftermath" />
