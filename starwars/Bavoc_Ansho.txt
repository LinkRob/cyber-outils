'''Bavoc Ansho''' was a [[Nautolan]] [[Jedi Knight]] who was a member of the [[Jedi assault team]] during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]. His [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] was [[Tualon Yaluna]]. Both survived the battle and Yaluna was promoted to [[Knighting Ceremony|Jedi Knight]] shortly thereafter. Now a [[Jedi Master]], Ansho died at some point during the [[Clone Wars]].

== Biography ==
Bavoc Ansho was born sometime before the fall of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]]. As a [[Force-sensitive|Force sensitive]], he was discovered by the [[Jedi Order]] and trained to [[Knighting Ceremony|Knighthood]]. Ansho's [[Foraging mission to Naboo|first mission]] as a [[Jedi Knight]] involved leading a group of [[Padawan|Padawans]] in foraging on Naboo; a [[Unidentified Jedi Master (Naboo)|Jedi Master]] accompanied them just in case. After watching the performance of [[Twi'lek]] [[Initiate]] [[Tualon Yaluna]] in the [[Jedi Tournament]], the young Ansho took him as his [[Padawan]]. Master and apprentice had a good relationship and<ref name="Inquisitor" /> by [[22 BBY]],<ref name="DiplomaticDate">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'' establishes that Bavoc Ansho and [[Tualon Yaluna]]'s [[Unidentified diplomatic mission|diplomatic mission]] took place prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> they were tasked with escorting a [[Unidentified senator (Tualon Yaluna)|Senator]]. Bandits attacked during the [[Unidentified diplomatic mission|mission]] and the two [[Jedi]] were successful in defending the [[Senator]] from them.<ref name="Inquisitor" />

== Personality and traits ==
Bavoc Ansho was a [[Nautolan]] with [[Color|black]] [[Eye|eyes]] and blue-gray skin.<ref name="Inquisitor" />
