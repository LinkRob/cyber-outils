'''G4-S8'''<ref name="Toy">{{DisneyToyCite|set=Droid Depot (toy line)|pack=Droid Factory Mystery Crate|altlink=https://www.endorexpress.net/site-news/2022/01/droid-factory-mystery-crate-series-1}}</ref> was an [[R4 astromech droid]],<ref name="GEDD" /> manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]].<ref name="CEUE" /> During the [[First Order]]'s [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> G4-S8 was displayed amongst a variety of [[droid]] parts upon one of the upper shelves of the [[Droid Depot]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on Batuu.<ref name="GEDD" /> The depot also sold action figures of the droid.<ref name="Toy" /> It had [[Color|green]] and white paneling and a black [[sensor]].<ref name="GEDD" />
