In the years following the [[Galactic Civil War]], the reclusive culture known as the [[Crèche]] guarded a '''giant egg''' hidden deep in caverns on [[Ovanis]]. The Crèche believed that the egg would one day hatch the savior of [[the galaxy]]. The egg was destroyed by [[First Order]] [[First Order flametrooper|flametroopers]] under the command of the [[First Order Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Terex]].

==Description==
The Crèche egg was a large [[egg]] that emitted a [[Ghost (folklore)|ghostly]] [[Color|blue]] light. It was suspended from the ceiling of the [[Cave of the Crèche]] by a series of tubes.<ref name="PD1" /> The Crèche egg could be damaged by sustained [[fire]] from [[flamethrower]]s.<ref name="PD3">[[Poe Dameron 3|''Poe Dameron'' 3]]</ref>

==History==
For generations, members of the [[Crèche]] cult devoted their lives to protecting and nurturing the egg. They believed that the egg held their unborn savior. When the egg hatched, the Crèche believed that their savior would reward them for keeping it safe for many generations. Following [[Operation: Sabre Strike]], the [[Resistance]] [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] dispatched the [[starfighter]] [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] and his [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] to [[Hunt for Lor San Tekka|search]] for the explorer [[Lor San Tekka]]. Leia believed that Lor San Tekka could help her find her lost brother [[Luke Skywalker]]. When Poe [[Mission to Ovanis|arrived]] on [[Ovanis]], the [[Unidentified Crèche elder|Crèche elder]] told her that Tekka had visited them a long [[time]] ago.<ref name="PD1" />

She then proceeded to brief Poe about the Crèche egg and its importance to her community. When [[BB-8]] found a [[Homing beacon|tracker]] on [[Black One|Poe's]] [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]], Poe confronted the Crèche. Mistaking the tracker for a [[detonator]], the Crèche elder ordered her people to attack Poe. Poe managed to reassure the Crèche that he was not a threat.<ref name="PD1" /> Shortly later, the [[First Order]] [[agent]] [[Terex]] arrived with a contingent of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] and demanded the Crèche hand over Poe. The Crèche elder feigned ignorance but one of Terex's stormtroopers sensed the presence of Poe's tracker.<ref name="PD2">[[Poe Dameron 2|''Poe Dameron'' 2]]</ref>

In retaliation, Terex threatened to torch the Crèche egg. To save the egg, Poe surrendered himself. When Poe revealed that Black [[Squadron]] was attacking Terex's [[First Order military|forces]], Terex ordered his [[First Order flametrooper|flametroopers]] to torch the egg.<ref name="PD2" /> Ultimately, the egg cracked open and released two [[Unidentified Crèche creature|large winged creatures]], who fought each other. The black creature ultimately triumphed over the blue winged creature. After the First Order forces were defeated by the [[Resistance pilot]] Dameron and Black Squadron, the grateful Crèche cultists regarded the black winged creature as their savior and departed with it.<ref name="PD3" />
