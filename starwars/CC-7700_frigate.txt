The '''CC-7700 frigate''' was a class of [[frigate]] used by the [[Imperial Navy]] that was an updated version of the earlier [[Detainer CC-2200]]. The CC-7700 was an [[Interdictor]] vessel, due to its [[Gravity well projector|ability]] to emit an [[interdiction field]], which prevented other [[Starship|ships]] from entering [[hyperspace]] and could be used to maroon vessels traveling at lightspeed. In [[14 BBY]], one CC-7700 formed part of a [[task force]] [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and was used in conjunction with other Interdictor vessels in a failed [[Incident in the Obroa-skai system|attempt]] to capture the [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel]] [[corvette]] ''[[Carrion Spike]]''.

==Characteristics==
The CC-7700 [[frigate]] was a type of [[Interdictor]] vessel. Like its predecessor, the [[Detainer CC-2200]], the CC-7700 featured thick [[armor]], a downsloping bow, and stubby winglike lateral projections that housed quartets of [[gravity well projector]]s. These allowed the [[Starship|ship]] to emit an [[interdiction field]]. The frigate also had [[sublight engine]]s and could communicate with nearby [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] ships via a [[battle net]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Role==
As an Interdictor vessel, the CC-7700 frigate utilized its [[gravity well]] projectors to generate an interdiction field, which could be used to pull enemy ships out of [[hyperspace]] or to prevent them from entering it in the first place.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==History==
[[File:CarrionSpike-PoeDameron2.png|thumb|left|250px|A CC-7700 attempted to capture the rebel corvette ''Carrion Spike'' in 14 BBY.]]
In the [[Standard year|year]] [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the CC-7700 frigate was used by the [[Imperial Navy]]. At this [[time]], a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] was able to [[Mission to Murkhana|steal]] the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s prized [[corvette]], and use it in a [[Berch Teller campaign|series of attacks]] on Imperial facilities. In an attempt to stop the campaign, Tarkin [[Commanding officer|assembled]] a [[task force]] to capture the vessel, which consisted of the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class star destroyer]] ''[[Executrix]]'', an [[Unidentified Immobilizer 418 cruiser|Immobilizer 418 cruiser]], a Detainer CC-2200, a CC-7700 frigate, and several other pickets, frigates, and [[gunboat]]s. Based on fake coordinates transmitted by the rebels, the Imperial group traveled to the [[Obroa-skai system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]], where Tarkin hoped to [[Incident in the Obroa-skai system|intercept]] the rogue corvette.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Tarkin contacted the [[Unidentified task force liaison officer|task-force liaison officer]] aboard the CC-7700 by battle net just before the ''Carrion Spike''{{'s}} estimated arrival time. The [[Imperial officer|officer]] informed Tarkin that the Interdictor vessels' [[gravity]] well projectors were powering up to high gain and that the field would be disabled soon after it was initiated, so that civilian vessels would not be pulled out of hyperspace too. However, the [[commander]] also explained that such a result may be unavoidable, due to the high traffic in the [[Star system|system]] and because the ''Carrion Spike''{{'s}} speed meant that the gravity well projectors had to be set at a higher power setting. Regardless, just as the corvette was expected to arrive, the [[Immobilizer 418 cruiser|Immobilizer 418]] malfunctioned, causing the interdiction field to fluctuate and pull several civilian ships from hyperspace. The Detainer and a [[Stellar Vista|luxury liner]] then collided, causing chaos and branding the mission as a failure.<ref name="Tarkin" />
