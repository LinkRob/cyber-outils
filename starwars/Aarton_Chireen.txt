'''Aarton Chireen''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[lieutenant]] in the [[Resistance]] from [[Jaymir]], where he and his sister [[Nimi Chireen]] were recruited by [[pilot]] [[Ello Asty]]. The siblings were held in reserve until after the [[Battle of Crait]], when they joined the core Resistance group.

In [[35 ABY]], he was stationed at the [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|Resistance base]] on the [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]] as a [[quartermaster]]. When the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' returned to the base from a [[Mission to Sinta Glacier Colony|mission]] on fire, Chireen ordered fire crews to attend to the flames. He then attended a meeting in which it was revealed that the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] had returned and that a [[Final Order|Sith fleet]] was going to reinforce the First Order from the [[planet]] [[Exegol]].

==Biography==
Aarton Chireen was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] from [[Jaymir]] who served in alongside his sister [[Nimi Chireen]] in the [[Resistance]] during its [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]]. After their initial recruitment by the [[pilot]] [[Ello Asty]], the siblings were held in reserve by the Resistance and Aarton was asked to gather intelligence about his local area of operation. This changed following the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]] and [[Battle of Starkiller Base]] in [[34 ABY]], after which Aarton and Nimi were called up for active duty and increased their involvement in the movement, joining the core group following the [[Battle of Crait]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

By [[35 ABY]], Aarton held the rank of [[lieutenant]] and was part of the command staff for the [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|Resistance's base]] on the [[jungle]] [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]]. Working as the base's [[quartermaster]], he was responsible for allocating resources to the [[pilot]]s, [[soldier]]s and [[technician]]s stationed there.<ref name="TROS VD" /> When the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' returned to the base ablaze with fire from an [[Mission to Sinta Glacier Colony|intelligence mission]] to the [[Sinta Glacier Colony]], Chireen rushed toward it with other personnel and ordered fire crews to front and rear of the damaged vehicle.<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

Chireen later attended a meeting of the base personnel to discuss the intelligence that the mission to the colony had recovered. Chireen stood behind the adviser [[Maz Kanata]] as [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] revealed that the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] had returned and that the First Order would soon be reinforced with a [[Final Order|Sith fleet]] from the [[planet]] [[Exegol]].<ref name="TROS" />

==Personality and traits==
Aarton Chireen had tan skin, [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s and black [[hair]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Equipment==
On Ajan Kloss, Aarton Chireen wore a khaki [[Clothing|shirt]] with a brown [[belt]], pants and [[glove]]s. He had a gray [[scarf]] knotted around his neck and used a brown [[Hat|cap]] with a pair of black [[goggles]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aarton Chireen first appeared in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']],<ref name="TROS" /> and was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> He was played by British actor [[Aaron Neil]], who was credited as "Resistance Officer"<ref name="TROS" /> and had his first name incorporated into the character's.<ref name="TROS VD" />
