"'''Below the Belt: Philanthropy'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year two of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the first scenario in the [[Below the Belt trilogy]] and was written by [[August Hahn]].

==Plot summary==
[[Nirama]] opens up the [[Cularin asteroid belt]] for exploration, and many [[Starship/Legends|starships]] flock to participate. The heroes are invited to take part, and they soon discover a strange asteroid.
