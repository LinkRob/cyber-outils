'''Foerost''' was a [[Core Worlds]] astronomical object where the [[Battle of Foerost]], an engagement that the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[officer]] and later [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Tabor Seitaron]] participated in, took place in the vicinity. A travel service ran between the celestial body and the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] in [[5 BBY]]. Later on, the [[Drabatan]] [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek|Pao]] carried out his [[Demolitions job on Foerost|first mission]] with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] on Foerost.

==Description==
Foerost was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> located in the [[Foerost system]] within an intersecting portion of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Interior]] regions.<ref name="Interior" /> It lay in the coordinates L-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" />

==History==
[[File:Coruscants_spaceport.png|thumb|left|300px|Coruscant Eastern Spaceport (pictured) ran a service to Foerost.]]
By around [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Battle date">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'' establishes that [[Tabor Seitaron]]'s active service predated the [[Star Destroyer]] design that the ''[[Herald]]'' was part of, which ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' established to be an [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the introduction of the ''Imperial II''-class to around [[5 BBY]], meaning Seitaron must have therefore retired by then. As the [[Battle of Foerost]] took place during Seitaron's active service, it must have also taken place by around 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> Foerost was in the vicinity of the [[Battle of Foerost]], in which [[Tabor Seitaron]], an [[officer]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and later its [[Proclamation of the New Order|successor]], the [[Galactic Empire]], served, considering his participation a triumph of his career.<ref name="TC" /> In 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Andor date">{{AndorDate|104}}</ref> a travel service was set to run from [[Coruscant Eastern Spaceport]] on<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="TC" /> [[Coruscant]] to Foerost the [[Standard day|day]] the disgraced [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces|Corporate Authority]] [[Police officer|lawman]] [[Syril Karn]] arrived at that [[spaceport]] from<ref name="Aldhani" /> the planet<ref name="Kassa audio">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> [[Morlana One]].<ref name="Aldhani" />

The [[Drabatan]] [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek|Pao]] carried out a [[Demolitions job on Foerost|demolitions job]] on Foerost,<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> his first known mission with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]],<ref name="Heroes of Rogue One">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Heroes of Rogue One}}</ref> which involved taking out Foerost's [[Foerost communications tower|communications tower]]<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> sometime by [[1 BBY]].<ref name="1 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Battle of Scarif]] to [[1 BBY]]. Since [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek|Pao's]] [[Demolitions job on Foerost|job]] on Foerost was mentioned on the [[Standard day|day]] that the Battle of Scarif took place, it must have occurred by 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> The rebel [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] remembered that job shortly before the [[Battle of Scarif]]<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="1 BBY" /> where the captain's [[Rogue One|team]] carried out a similar operation. Andor thought to himself that he would ask Pao about the job before the mission began.<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY">{{BTCdate}}</ref> Captain Seitaron was summoned aboard the<ref name="TC" /> [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> ''[[Herald]]'' by [[Prelate]] [[Verge]]. Upon the captain's arrival, an [[Unidentified Imperial ensign (Herald)|Imperial ensign]] gave him a tour of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] and commented on how the ''Herald''{{'s}} ten percent increase in efficiency would have been useful at the Battle of Foerost.<ref name="TC" />

==Locations==
A communications tower was located on Foerost during the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the Empire.<ref name="Rogue One novel" />

==Behind the scenes==
Foerost was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via a mention of the Battle of Foerost in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="TC" /> It was subsequently first described as an astronomical object in Freed's [[2016]] [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|novelization]] of the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Foerost/Legends|Foerost]] originally appeared in the [[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 1|first issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries. The issue was written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]], illustrated by [[Dario Carrasco, Jr.]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="TJ:SW1">[[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 1|''Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War'' 1]]</ref> on [[August 15]], [[1995]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54595/star_wars_tales_of_the_jedi_-_the_sith_war_1995_1|text=Star Wars: Tales Of The Jedi - The Sith War (1995) #1}}</ref>
