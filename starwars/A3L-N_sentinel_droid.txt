The '''A3L-N sentinel droid''' was a model of [[security droid]]. The [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate used modified A3L-N sentinel droids in bulked-up chassis, despite the fact that the model had been recalled [[Standard decade|decades]] prior. A number of the droids patrolled a [[prison]] area on Crimson Dawn's [[fortress]] [[flagship]] [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']]. During Crimson Dawn's [[Auction for Han Solo|auction]] of the [[carbonite]]-frozen [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] aboard the ''Vermillion'', the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and the smuggler [[Sana Starros]] encountered one of the droids while attempting to explore a restricted area of the [[starship]]. The droid informed them that the area was restricted before incapacitating the two and taking them to one of the prison cells.<ref name="Aphra 14" />
