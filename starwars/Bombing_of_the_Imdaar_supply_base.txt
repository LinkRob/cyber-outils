We've bombed the shipyards at Kuat and the supply base on Imdaar. ''[&hellip;]'' I'm not sure what else we could possibly be doing.|Leia Organa and Gial Ackbar|<ref name="Star Wars 4" />}}
In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> after the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Battle of Yavin|destroyed]] the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> [[superweapon]],<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> the Alliance carried out a '''bombing''' of an Imperial [[Imdaar supply base|supply base]] on the [[Planet|world]] [[Imdaar]]. The [[bomb]]ing was one of over a dozen attacks undertaken by the Rebel Alliance during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the Empire in an attempt to capitalize on a wounded Empire following the [[Death Star]]'s destruction.<ref name="Star Wars 4" />

Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while in a meeting of [[Alliance High Command|Rebel Alliance leaders]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] stated her belief the Rebel Alliance had not been doing enough against the Empire. In response, [[Admiral]]<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> [[Gial Ackbar]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> used the Imdaar bombing as an example of the Alliance's actions after the [[Battle station|Death Star's]] destruction, noting<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> he<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> was not sure what more they could be doing.<ref name="Star Wars 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bombing of the Imdaar supply base was mentioned in the [[Star Wars (2015) 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']]. The issue was written by [[Jason Aaron]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> on [[April 22]], 2015.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52655/star_wars_2015_4|text=Star Wars (2015) #4}}</ref>
