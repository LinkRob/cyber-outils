'''''Darth Vader and Son''''' is a series of non-[[canon]] picture books by [[Jeffrey Brown]]. The [[Darth Vader and Son|first book of the same name]] was published on [[April 18]], [[2012]] and featured [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] parenting [[Luke Skywalker]]. Later books in the series would feature similar scenarios, such as Vader parenting [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].

''[[Rey and Pals]]'', while not focusing on Darth Vader, was also written by Brown and features the same art style and book structure.
