The '''Denudia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Grohl sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square R-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained at least one terrestrial astronomical object; the [[Hutt]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] possessed an ancient tapestry depicting a Denudan sunset in his treasury.<ref name="Escape">''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Escape Mission]]''</ref>

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Denudia system was first mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The system originates from the audiobook ''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Escape Mission]]'', which mentions a "Denudan sunset".<ref name="Escape" />
