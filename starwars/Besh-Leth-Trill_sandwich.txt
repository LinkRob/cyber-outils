'''Besh-leth-trill sandwich''' was a type of [[Sandwich/Legends|sandwich]] served at [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. It consisted of crispy [[nuna bacon]], [[revwien lettuce]] and [[Topato/Legends|topatos]] all on either toasted of therm-zapped [[Pseudograin bread]] and cost 6.7 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]].
