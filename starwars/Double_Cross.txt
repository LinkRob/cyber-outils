'''Double Cross''' is an article written by [[August Hahn]] and [[Cynthia Hahn]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[July 8]], [[2004]] and described an encounter in a [[Tolea Biqua]] bar where a [[Human/Legends|Human]] received some stolen data chips from the [[Rodian/Legends|Rodian]] [[Kletoo]]. The chips were stolen from [[Nirama]] and were to be used by [[Riboga]] to get his revenge; however, Riboga's agent was actually double-crossing him alerting a third party to the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt's]] impending move.

In the context of the Living Force campaign, it marked the availability of the smuggling compartment as a starship upgrade, as well as causing any player characters with certificates for "Favor of Nirama" to encounter trouble in the Cularin system.
