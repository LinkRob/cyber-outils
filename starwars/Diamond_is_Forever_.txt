"'''Diamond is Forever!'''" is an article written by [[Chris Spitale]] that appeared on [[Star Wars Insider 113|''Star Wars Insider'' 113]]. The article discussed how Diamond Select, an offshoot of Diamond Distributors that manufactures comic-inspired toys and collectibles, began a new line of ''[[Star Wars]]'' figures.<ref name="sw13" />
