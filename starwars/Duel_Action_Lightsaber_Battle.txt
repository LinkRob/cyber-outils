'''Duel Action Lightsaber Battle''' was a free online browser [[Star Wars video games|game]] hosted on the Hasbro website in [[2010]] where players fight as a [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] through various missions.

==Gameplay==
Players have to use the mouse to make attacking moves with their [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] and use the arrow keys on the keyboard to either block, evade or move with their character. In the first mission the player has to rescue [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] from the [[Separatist]]s. To do this, the player has to battle waves of [[B1-series battle droid/Legends|B1-series battle droid]]s. In the second mission the player has to hold off the enemy forces on [[Christophsis/Legends|Christophsis]] until reinforcements arrive. For the third mission players have to rescue [[Rotta/Legends|Rotta]] and bring him back to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba the Hutt]]. In this mission in addition to [[B1-series battle droid/Legends|B1-series battle droid]]s also [[B2-series super battle droid]]s have to be fought. The fourth mission then takes the player to the planet of [[Maridun/Legends|Maridun]] were they have to defend the [[Lurmen/Legends|Lurmen]] colony. In the fifth and last mission players have to protect a strategic planet from [[Dooku/Legends|Count Dooku]]'s assassin [[Asajj Ventress/Legends|Asajj Ventress]] and her droid army. Upon victory the player character is then promoted to a [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]].
