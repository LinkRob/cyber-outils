'''Dobak leg''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|dish]] that could be prepared in various ways. The dish was available for purchase at [[Market District (Mirogana)|Market District]] of [[Mirogana]], the [[capital]] [[city]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an announcer speaking [[Huttese]] advertised over the market's announcement system that the leg was available roasted, dipped or pan&ndash;fried.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dobak leg was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
