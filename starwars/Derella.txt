'''Derella''' was an astronomical object<ref name="LPOA" /> located in the [[Derella system]] of the [[Core Worlds]], situated in grid square M-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of [[Senator]] [[Cinderon Malpe]], who represented it in the [[Imperial Senate]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="LPOA" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] became familiar with the homeworlds of her [[House of Organa|father]]&mdash;[[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa's]]&mdash;colleagues.<ref name="LPOA" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Leia mentally noted the senator's homeworld as Derella on two occasions: during her [[Day of Demand]] ceremony, upon spotting Malpe in attendance, and later at a banquet hosted by her [[parent]]s in the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan]] on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]].<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Derella was mentioned in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="LPOA" /> The astronomical object was not mentioned in the original excerpt for ''Leia, Princess of Alderaan'' published in the paperback editions of ''[[Lost Stars]]'', a [[2015]] young-adult novel also authored by Gray. Instead, the celestial body [[Osk-Trill]] was denoted as the origin of Senator [[Gall Trayvis]].<ref name="LS paperback">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' excerpt in the [[Egmont|Egmont UK Ltd.]] and [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] paperback editions of ''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
