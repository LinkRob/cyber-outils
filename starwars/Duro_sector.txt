The '''Duro sector''' was a [[sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]] region of [[the galaxy]]. During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], [[Slim Jek]] of the [[Zenowai Crime Society]] acted as an adviser to criminals all over the Duro sector.<ref name="SWU" /> It contained the [[Duro system]],<ref name="Appendix" /> which in turn contained [[Duro]], the homeworld of the [[Duros]] [[species]].<ref name="Duro" />
