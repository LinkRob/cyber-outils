'''''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand'' 4''' is the fourth and final issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] limited series ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Last Stand]]''. The issue was written by [[Alex Segura]], illustrated by [[Jethro Morales]] and [[Jim Campbell]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 22]], [[2025]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE BATTLE OF JAKKU RAGES ON! THE BATTLE OF JAKKU &mdash; as never seen before! THE NEW REPUBLIC must make a painful choice that puts ACKBAR's forces at risk! LUKE SKYWALKER faces off against the deadliest menace to the Republic &mdash; and the Empire! A historic character makes their first appearance ever!''

==Plot summary==
After rogue [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] joins the [[Battle of Jakku]], various factions work to change the tide of the battle. [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] moves on foot to stop [[Counselor to the Empire|Counselor]] [[Gallius Rax]] from destroying the remaining Imperial forces as part of his [[contingency]]. Rax works works with [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] leader [[Yupe Tashu]], a [[Sentinel (Contingency)|Sentinel]] [[droid]], and [[Brendol Hux]] at the [[Jakku Observatory]]. [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] member [[Tomasso]] gets permission from leader [[Zeva Bliss]] to turn their forces against Adelhard. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[New Republic]] [[pilot]] [[Rynn Zenat]] rush through the [[Anoat sector]] towards Jakku, all the while hoping Tomasso can get the Spice Runners to stop Adelhard. 

When Luke and Rynn arrive to Jakku, they are greeted by the [[pirate]] [[Eleodie Maracavanya]], who is using zher [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Liberty's Misrule]]'' to keep [[Adelhard coalition|Adelhard's forces]] at bay. Luke and Rynn are also helped by [[Therisa Alern]], who has commandeered a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]]. Using access codes to Adelhard's [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] provided by New Republic [[spy]] [[Kith Alaytia]], Rynn opens the [[hangar|bay doors]] to the [[Scepter (Imperial-class)|''Scepter'']]. 

As they move their ship in, however, Therisa is [[death|killed]] in battle. This shatters Rynn, and she starts to hastily vie for revenge. Luke is able to calm her down and get her to focus on the mission. Outside the ''Scepter'', the bombardment of ''Liberty's Misrule'' grows too much, and Maracavanya decides to retreat. Luke and Rynn are on their own.

On the New Republic's provisional [[capital]], [[Chandrila]], Kith Alaytia updates [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and [[Han Solo]] on the many losses the Republic is facing at Jakku and that Luke and Rynn have boarded the ''Scepter''. Solo feels they cannot just sit back and decides he is going to help Luke wherever he can.

Meanwhile, the main New Republic fleet is facing a chaotic rout after their [[Starhawk-class Battleship Mark I|Starhawks]] start to penetrate the imposing Imperial [[blockade]]. Even so, the battle is turning into a stalemate as the New Republic is unable to effectively get past the ''[[Ravager]]'' and the ''[[Punishment]]''. [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] orders [[Commodore]] [[Kyrsta Agate]] to evacuate the ''[[Concord]]'', but she remains hopeful they can find a way to break through. 

Adelhard takes this opportunity to announce his presence as a third party in this conflict. He announces on an open channel that his goal is total annihilation of everyone. Unbeknownst to the Moff, this inspires Zeva Bliss to call for her forces to turn on Adelhard. This signal also activates [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] mystic [[Reyna Oskure]] to plunge a [[dagger]] into [[Commander]] [[Bragh|Bragh's]] chest, killing him. She prepares to kill Adelhard too, but he slices the [[Anzati]] in her abdomen with his own [[vibro-ax]]. The wounded mystic speaks to the governor's imminent demise at the hands of the underworld, but he still believes the Spice Runners to be at his side. Just then, [[Lieutenant]] [[Brabaum]] announces to Adelhard that the Spice Runners have turned their ships against him. They cause massive damage to the ''Scepter''.

Rynn moves through the burning corridors of the ''Scepter'' and runs into Reyna Oskure. The mystic is heading towards the docking bay to escape, and Rynn reluctantly lets her live since she has to complete her mission with little time to spare, especially as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' arrives to support [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. Han gets his friends to help him clear the way to the ''Scepter''. 

On the ''Concord'', Commodore Agate asks for permission from Admiral Ackbar to use her ship to take out the ''Ravager''. Ackbar wishes she could live, but he relents. This action changes the tide of the battle and ultimately allows the New Republic to win the [[Galactic Civil War|war]]. 

Back on the ''Scepter'' Luke finds his way to Adelhard. His goal is not to kill the governor, but to try and save him. This evokes laughter from Adelhard, who then swings at Luke with his vibro-ax. Adelhard cannot be reasoned with, and hints that the victory will be short-lived. Before he can elaborate, he is shot in the shoulder by Rynn. Luke and Zenat rush to the ''Millennium Falcon'' as time grows short. They get clear before the ship explodes, and Han feels like things might really be done this time. 

Following the battle, Rae Sloane is tasked by the dying Rax to follow through with [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] Contingency in the [[Unknown Regions]]. The New Republic and [[Mas Amedda]] sign the [[Galactic Concordance]], officially ending the war. Leia and Han's son, [[Ben Solo]], is born.

Later, somewhere in the [[Fenril sector]], a mysterious Praetor speaks with [[Prime Minister]] [[Kezrin Almovar]] of [[Pirros]] in a [[cantina]]. The figure offers to Almovar a chance to gain immense power and freedom from the New Republic at a high price...
