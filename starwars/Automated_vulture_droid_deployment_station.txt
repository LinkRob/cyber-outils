The '''automated vulture droid deployment station''',<ref name="Bounty hunters EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/bounty-hunters-episode-gallery|text="Bounty Hunters" Episode Gallery}}</ref> also referred to as the '''Automated Droid Deployment Station''',<ref name="Starfighters">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> was a type of [[space station]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]] to carry [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class starfighters]]. It could launch a total of six [[droid starfighter]]s when hostile [[Starship|vessels]] approached in its territory.<ref name="Bounty Hunters" />

During the Clone Wars in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places "[[Children of the Force]]" and "[[The Zillo Beast]]" in [[21 BBY]]. As the events of the episode "[[Bounty Hunters (episode)|Bounty Hunters]]" takes place between these two episodes according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, this episode must also take place in 21 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> after the [[Destruction of the Felucia medical station|destruction]] of [[Felucia Medical Station HCTFF2]] at the hands of Separatist forces, the Confederacy placed an automated vulture droid deployment station above the [[planet]] [[Felucia]]. The station was later encountered by [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], to which it deployed all six [[Starfighter|fighters]] against their [[T-6 shuttle]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
The automated vulture droid deployment station first appeared in "[[Bounty Hunters (episode)|Bounty Hunters]],"<ref name="Bounty Hunters" /> the seventeenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which originally aired on [[April 2]], [[2010]], and was first identified in the episodic guide for "Bounty Hunters".<ref name="Bounty Hunters Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode217.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Bounty Hunters}}</ref> It was also alternatively identified as the Automated Droid Deployment Station in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]'', written by [[Landry Q. Walker]].<ref name="Starfighters" />
