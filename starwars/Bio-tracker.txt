{{Quote|As you have no doubt deduced, since we last spoke, I had Admiral Motti install bio-trackers in the helmets of every gunner on this station. Each of you hesitated at key points during the firing of the Death Star.|Grand Moff Tarkin to a group of Death Star gunners|<ref name="Grand Moff" />}}
A '''bio-tracker''' was a device used to analyse reactions.<ref name="Grand Moff" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Age of Rebellion - Grand Moff Tarkin 1|''Age of Rebellion - Grand Moff Tarkin'' 1]] featured the [[Destruction of Alderaan]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref> [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Admiral]] [[Conan Antonio Motti]] installed bio-trackers in the [[Helmets (department)|helmets]] of every Death Star [[gunner]] at the request of [[Grand Moff|Grand]] [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]. After the [[destruction of Alderaan]], Tarkin requested to have the mission report reaction data of the operation and discovered through the bio-trackers' results that several gunners, including [[Chief Gunner]] [[Endo Frant]], had hesitated before firing [[Alderaan]]. As a result, Tarkin decided to punish the gunners for their failure by shutting them in an airlock and jettisoning them into [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Grand Moff" />
