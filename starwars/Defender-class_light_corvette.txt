The '''''Defender''-class light corvette''' was a class of [[light corvette]] manufactured by the [[Rendili Vehicle Corporation]] that was used by the [[Jedi Order]]. Although there was not official evidence presented, the [[Paladin-class corvette|''Paladin''-class corvette]] was likely influenced by the ''Defender''-class, which was an ancient design. It was introduced long before the [[Clone Wars]], and the visual similarities between both the ''Defender''-class and the ''Paladin''-class were not likely to be a coincidence. Similar to the ''Defender''-class, the ''[[Crucible]]'', a ''Paladin''-class corvette, featured a front section that was shaped as an anvil.<ref name="BMF 69" />
