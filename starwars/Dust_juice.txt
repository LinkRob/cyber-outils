'''Dust juice'''<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> was an [[alcohol]]ic beverage that, despite having a disgusting taste, was allegedly the best of what a [[Unidentified planet (Eeth Koth)|planet]] had to offer. The drink was [[Color|orange]] in color. After the [[hunt for Eeth Koth]],<ref name="Dark Lord 19" /> the [[Thirteenth Sister]] and [[Tualon Yaluna]]<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> drank the beverage to celebrate his death.<ref name="Dark Lord 19" />
