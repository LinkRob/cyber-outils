'''Evans Basch''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[engineer]] who served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. He was aboard the refugee vessel ''[[Dorlanna]]'' before it was destroyed, and later stationed on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="HS12" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Evans Basch first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], as an unnamed crew member aboard the ''Millennium Falcon''.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> He was first identified in the comic [[Hyperspace Stories 12|''Hyperspace Stories'' 12]].<ref name="HS12" />
