An '''enforcer''' was an individual who worked as [[soldier]] for a criminal organization, such as the [[Hutt Clan]], [[Ashiga Clan]], [[Crimson Dawn]] and [[Pyke Syndicate]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> or corporate organization, such as the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]].<ref name="BDOWD">{{EncyclopediaCite|Battle Droids and Other Droids of War}}</ref> The [[human]] [[Qi'ra]] was an enforcer for Crimson Dawn,<ref name="221 Launchpad">{{InsiderCite|221|Launchpad}}</ref> with her fighting abilities making her a valuable resource for the syndicate.<ref name="OSWM 117">{{GermanFanCite|117|Database}}</ref> A specific type of Dawn enforcer were the [[Hylobon Enforcers]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

After the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]]<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> and the [[Jedi]] intervened in the [[Kaleesh-Yam'rii war|fight]] between his people, the [[Kaleesh]], and the [[Yam'rii]], [[Grievous]] held an intense grudge toward them<ref name="HC 8">{{HelmetCollectionCite|8|Helmets}}</ref> and allied with the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]]<ref name="Separatists">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Separatists}}</ref> and became its enforcer.<ref name="BDOWD" />
