'''David Glen Russell''' is a composer who provided music for the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Recon Missions]]'' as well as multiple ''[[Star Wars]]'' television series, including ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'', [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (television series)|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'']], and [[Star Wars: Ahsoka|''Ahsoka'']].<ref name="Credits">{{WebCite|url=http://davidglenrussell.com/credits|text=Credits - David Glen Russell|archivedate=20230903182317}}</ref>
