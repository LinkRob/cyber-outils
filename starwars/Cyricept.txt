'''Cyricept''' was a company that manufactured the [[Mk IX Personal Mimetic Concealment Suit]], a type of [[mimetic suit]].<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cyricept was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Cyphers and Masks]]'',<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Accept Your Mission">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/8/16/accept-your-mission|text=Accept Your Mission}}</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity where it first appeared in [[Knight Errant (novel)|''Knight Errant'']], a [[2011]] novel written by [[John Jackson Miller]] and published by [[Del Rey]].<ref name="Knight Errant">[[Knight Errant (novel)|''Knight Errant'']]</ref>
