'''2021 Topps ''Star Wars'' Chrome Legacy''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] on [[October 20]], [[2021]]. It included 200 base cards, 15 concept art cards from ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', 15 concept arts from ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'', 20 from the "Wielders of the Lightsaber" subset, and a number of artist sketch cards. It was later released on the [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]] app as "Chrome Legacy - 2021" in [[September]] [[2022]].
