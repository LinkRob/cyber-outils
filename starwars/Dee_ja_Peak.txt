'''Dee'ja Peak''' was a [[farm]]ing community located in the [[Gallo Mountains]] on [[Naboo]]. It was founded by a nomadic tribe of [[human]]s from [[Grizmallt]] who came to colonize the [[planet]] many [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Crisis" /> Information from the [[Naboo Planetary Tourist Bureau]] indicated that Dee'ja Peak was a major arts hub, including a [[Unidentified Dee'ja Peak theatre|theatre]]. The historian [[Beaumont Kin]] noted this information in<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> book ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' in [[35 ABY]]<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>, suggesting that [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] may have gotten his interest in opera and ballet from spending time in the area during his youth.<ref name="Rise" />
