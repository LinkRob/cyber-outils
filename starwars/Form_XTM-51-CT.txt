'''XTM-51-CT''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] form mandated by [[Imperial Naval Regulation 132.CAT.ch(22)]] that required the [[gunnery captain]]s of Imperial vessels to explain why they shot at [[escape pod]]s during a [[Category V]] engagement. It existed in order to appease the [[Imperial Senate]] regarding the [[Imperial Military]]'s doings. While it was in his character to avoid having to fill out such a form, gunnery captain [[Bolvan]] of the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]'' confirmed that, when he ordered his subordinate [[Hija]] not to fire at an escape pod with no lifesigns jettisoned from the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' during its [[Secret mission to Tatooine|capture]] over [[Tatooine]], he was not thinking of the form. Instead, he did not want to hurt his shot-to-kill ratio due to its lack of life forms. However, Bolvan later realized the pod had likely had the stolen [[Death Star plans]] hidden inside it and came to [[fleet logistics liaison]] [[Arvira]] for help covering up his mistake.<ref name="TSOD">{{StoryCite|story=The Sith of Datawork|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Form XTM-51-CT was first mentioned in the short story "[[The Sith of Datawork]]" from the [[2017]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''.<ref name="TSOD" />
