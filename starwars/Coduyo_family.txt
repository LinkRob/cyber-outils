The '''Coduyo family''' was a [[Chiss family]] belonging to the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Great Families]], although it once belonged to the [[Ruling Families]]. It possessed a [[Standard century|centuries]]-old [[Coduyo fleet|fleet]]. The [[Chiss]] mid-ager [[Coduyo'po'nekri]] and his betrothed [[Yomie]] were members of this family.<ref name="Greater Good" />

The Coduyo were allied with fellow Great Family the [[Xodlak family]] and the Ruling [[Irizi family]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Coduyo family first appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', the second book of [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''The Ascendancy Trilogy'']] by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Greater Good" />
