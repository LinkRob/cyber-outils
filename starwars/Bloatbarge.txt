'''Bloatbarges''' were floating craft used by the [[Galactic Empire]] on [[Milvayne]] to remove the foul air from the upper levels of [[Milvayne City]] and dump it into its [[Underworld (Milvayne)|underworld]]. The criminals [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] and [[0-0-0]] hitchhiked on one with [[Vulaada Klam]] and the [[qaberworm]] [[Gurtyl]] until the [[Milvayne Authority]] intercepted them.<ref name="Aphra 28" />
