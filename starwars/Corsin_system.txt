The '''Corsin system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Greater Plooriod sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was located in grid square M-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Corsin]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> It was located along the [[Hydian Way]], and was the western endpoint of the<ref name="Map" /> [[Vaathkree Trade Corridor]].<ref name="Vaathkree" />
