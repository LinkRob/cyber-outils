The '''121st Regiment''' was a mobile armored unit in the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].

==History==
The 121st Regiment utilized [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut/Legends|HAVw A6 Juggernauts]], [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer/Legends|AT-TE]] walkers and [[All Terrain Attack Pod/Legends|AT-AP]] walkers, with green markings painted on their armor panels to designate their [[Regiment/Legends|regiment]] affiliation. One of the earliest conflicts the 121st participated in was the [[Battle of Herdessa (Clone Wars)|Battle of Herdessa]].
