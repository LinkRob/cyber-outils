"'''Dreaming Of Thala'''" was a [[music]] track by the [[band]] [[Jagged Hope]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it could be heard on various [[jukebox]]es on the [[moon]], [[Toshara]], including at [[Makal's Gambling Parlor]] in [[Mirogana]] and at [[Daruda Diner]] in [[Jaunta's Hope]]. The song was also on the jukebox in [[The Broken Hoof]] [[Cantina|bar]] in [[Canto Bight]] during the same time period.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Dreaming Of Thala" was created by the [[Cody Matthew Johnson]] for the [[2024]] [[Ubisoft]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" /> It was also released as part of the game's soundtrack, ''[[Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)]]''.<ref name="Songs from the Underworld">{{OST|Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)}}</ref>
