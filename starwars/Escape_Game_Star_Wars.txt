'''''Escape Game Star Wars''''' is a board game created by [[Nicolas Bonnefoy]] and published on [[October 5]], [[2022]] by [[Hachette Heroes]].<ref name="Hachette" />

{{Game-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Face the Empire to free your allies!''

''You play Saruya, the leader of a team of smugglers taken prisoner by the Empire. From Tatooine to Mimban, it's up to you to rescue each member of the team with the help of the Rebels. Only with your entire crew can you accomplish the ultimate mission entrusted by the Rebel Alliance...''

''Will you be up to the task?''<ref name="Hachette" />
