{{Quote|The Empire's time has come. You don't have to go down with it.|The captain of the ''Invincible Faith'' to Commander Iden Versio|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Commander]] [[Iden Versio]] of [[Inferno Squad]] '''escaped''' from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[MC80 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Invincible Faith]]'' with the help of an [[Dio|ID10 seeker droid]] after allowing herself to be taken [[Prisoner|captive]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Captain She's highly trained. Deception. Infiltration. Extraction. She's special forces.|A rebel, to the captain of the ''Invincible Faith''|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
[[File:Invincible_Faith.png|thumb|left|220px|A U-wing, carrying Iden Versio, approaches the ''Invincible Faith''.]]
Sometime before, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] managed to retrieve a message from [[Moff]] [[Derrek Raythe]], which contained orders for [[Death Squadron]] to move to the far side of the planet [[Endor (planet)|Endor]] for the [[Galactic Empire]]'s trap during the upcoming [[Battle of Endor]]. Needing to keep the information secretive, [[Inferno Squad]] [[Commander]] [[Iden Versio]] was assigned to retrieve the message before the Rebel Alliance managed to decode it. In order to infiltrate the [[MC80 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Invincible Faith]]'' where the message was being decoded, Versio feigned her capture.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

Aboard the ''Invincible Faith'', the [[Unidentified Invincible Faith captain|ship's captain]] attempted to interrogate Versio in order to get the activation code for her [[droid]]. He attempted to coax her by offering her a spot in the Rebel Alliance as he believed that the Galactic Empire would soon be toppled during the [[Battle of Endor]]; however, she refused his offer. Once he left, Versio spoke the activation code into her [[helmet]], which was linked to her droid, activating it.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==The escape==
{{Quote|Attention, an Imperial prisoner has escaped. She is extremely dangerous! Find her!|A rebel making an announcement on the ''Invincible Faith''|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
After being awakened with the activation code, the [[ID10 seeker droid|ID10]] began to scan the room while a [[Unidentified Sullustan Rebel technician|Sullustan]] [[technician]] attempted to hack its memory core. While working, the [[Sullustan]] was shocked by the ID10's [[electro-shock prod]]. Having discovered Versio's location by hacking into the [[Starship|ship's]] [[computer]]s, the ID10 headed for the ventilation system. Exiting the ventilation system, the ID10 came across many rebel [[trooper]]s just outside Versio's cell, and was ordered to head back into the ventilation system and find another way through. Once more exiting the ventilation system, the ID10 came across a large staging area. Avoiding several rebels, the ID10 managed to reach a console which allowed it access to another ventilation system outside, which led directly to Versio's cell. Finally reaching her cell, the ID10 freed Versio of her shackles.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

[[File:IdensID10SeekerDroid-2025EmpireUnit.png|thumb|right|250px|Versio's ID10 was essential to the success of the mission.]]
Freed, Versio placed her helmet back on, once again gaining access to communications with the [[Corvus (Raider II-class)|''Corvus'']] and [[Agent]] [[Gideon Hask]]. She informed Agent Hask that her mission was going smoothly, while her ID10 opened her cell door. She began to move through the ship, knocking out several rebels along her way with a [[baton]], before discovering a weapons cache and stealing a [[DH-17 blaster pistol]]. With her weapon, she began to move through the ship once more, eventually reaching the communications center, shooting and killing the [[officer]] working on decrypting the message. She quickly played the message before deleting it. However, deleting the message triggered the ship's alarm, and now the rebels were on high alert. Moving to take an [[A280C blaster rifle]], she ordered Hask to begin moving in for extraction.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

[[File:The_Corvus_Comes_Out_of_Hyperspace.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Corvus'' comes out of hyperspace to extract Iden Versio.]]
Heading toward an [[airlock]], Versio killed many rebel soldiers during her escape. Finally, Versio made it to a heavily secured door, which would take longer to slice than the others. Pinned down, Versio did her best to defend against the oncoming waves of rebel soldiers before the droid finally finished slicing the door. At the airlock, Versio sealed herself inside and informed Hask that she was in position for extraction. As Hask counted down, the airlock opened and Versio was jettisoned into the vacuum of [[Realspace|space]] before the ''Corvus'' arrived, allowing her to land in its hangar bay. While the ''Invincible Faith'' fired [[concussion missile]]s, the ''Corvus'' made its escape into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Today, the Rebellion dies.|Iden Versio shortly after she escaped the ''Invincible Faith''|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
On the [[Bridge (starship)|command deck]] of the ''Corvus'', Versio congratulated her crew on a successful rescue. Hask questioned her on the successful deletion of the message, which was met with approval. The crew then contacted [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]] to tell him of the mission's success.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
[[File:InfernoSquadEndor.png|thumb|250px|right|Inferno Squad witnesses the destruction of the second Death Star.]]
With the retrieval of Raythe's message,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> the Rebel Alliance was caught off-guard at the [[Battle of Endor]], unknowingly walking into the trap the Empire had set for them. Even so, the Rebel Alliance managed to disable the [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] around the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II]] and destroy its [[reactor]] core from the inside, striking a critical blow against the Empire.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> Eventually, the Empire was toppled during the [[Battle of Jakku]]. With Iden repeating the words given by the captain of the Invincible Faith to save her father. Which he declined and urged his daughter to live and survive in peace.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
