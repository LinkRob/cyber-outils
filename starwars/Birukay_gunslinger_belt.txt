A '''Birukay gunslinger belt''' was a light simple [[belt]] that was reminiscent of<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Aphra 25">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 25|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 25]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Birukay]]. The belt was [[Color|tan]] and connected in the front by a silver-colored buckle. On its right side, it had a [[holster]] that could carry a [[blaster pistol]]. Its front had space to store two [[blaster module]]s. The back of the belt had an attached pouch as well as space to store [[bacta vial]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Twi'lek]] [[merchant]] [[Sato Bizaz]] had a Birukay gunslinger belt for sale from their [[Bizaz|stall]] on the Outer Rim planet [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the human [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] purchased an Birukay gunslinger belt from Bizaz.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
An Birukay gunslinger belt appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When equipped the belt restored the player's health for each enemy defeated while performing the "[[Adrenaline]] Rush" ability.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Wikipedia:Concept Art|Concept Artist]] [[Iuliia Misiul]] created concept art of the Birukay gunslinger belt and other outfits used by Vess.<ref name="Art Station">{{ArtStation|username=Iuliia Misiul|url=/artwork/4N9Ve4|name=[[Iuliia Misiul]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws &mdash; Kay's Gearsets}}</ref>
