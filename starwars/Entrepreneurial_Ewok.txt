'''Entrepreneurial Ewok''' was an [[Ewok]] of the [[Bright Tree tribe]] on the forest [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. They borrowed [[Currency|money]] from the [[Village Doctor|village doctor]] to start a [[Stone|pebble]] business.<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
Entrepreneurial Ewok first appeared in the [[2022]] [[canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', in the side mission "Ubbla the Weather."<ref name="LEGO" />
