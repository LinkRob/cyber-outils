'''Atticus Cale''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Abednedo]] who served as a [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. [[Standard decade|Decades]] after Jedi Master [[Barnabas Vim]] went missing, Cale, his [[Padawan]] [[Jadia Brink]], and a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Outer Rim Exploration Team|Pathfinder team]] went to search for him. He wielded a yellow-bladed lightsaber.<ref name="Echoes of Fear 3" />
