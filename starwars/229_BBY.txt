The following events took place in or around the year '''229 BBY''', aka '''3048 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]], '''210 BFE''' according to the [[Imperial calendar|Imperial Calendar]], and '''Year 7748''' according to the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.

==Events==
*The [[Occlusion Zone]] is ruled by the [[Nihil]].<ref name="THR 1" />
*[[Jedi Master]] [[Harli Cogra]] publishes the ''[[Chronicles of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="Chronicles">{{ChroniclesoftheJediDate}}</ref>
*[[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Pra-Tre Veter]] is captured by the Nihil on [[Asternin]]<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 3">[[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 3|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 3]]</ref> and tortured for 2 months, before eventually being [[Pra-Tre Veter's execution|executed]] aboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]''.<ref name="Eye of Darkness" />
