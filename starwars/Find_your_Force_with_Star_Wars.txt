{{Quote|Earlier this week, our friends at Headspace brought us another moment of zen with a kid-friendly journey featuring Chewbacca and BB-8. The animated series Find your Force with ''Star Wars'' teaches younglings ages five to twelve the basics of mindfulness and meditation to learn how to manage emotions and navigate through asteroid fields of feelings like anger, fear, and frustration, as well as the Jedi-like power of gratitude.|[[Kristin Baver]]|<ref name="TWISW" />}}
'''''Find your Force with Star Wars''''' is a [[Canon|non-canon]] animated web series created by [[Headspace]] that focuses on mindfulness,<ref name="TWISW" /> and began on [[August 8]], [[2022]].<ref name="Meltdown" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Official description==
''Help kids find their force, anytime. In this four-part animated series, we'll follow the journeys of Chewbacca™ and his sidekick BB-8™ as they travel across the Star Wars™ galaxy to visit Chewbacca's™ home planet of Kashyyyk™. Of course, there's no adventure without its bumps along the way. Luckily, Headspace kids and family mindfulness expert Samantha Snowden is here to guide these two explorers through every challenge and tough emotion through breathing and mindfulness exercises. With these tools, they'll learn that the force is truly always with them — all it takes is a little mindfulness.''<ref name="Playlist" />
