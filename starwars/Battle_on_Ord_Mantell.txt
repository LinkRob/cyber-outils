The '''Battle on Ord Mantell''', also known as the '''Battle of Ord Mantell''', was fought between the [[Shadow Collective]] of [[Maul]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] on and in orbit of [[Ord Mantell]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The battle was devised by Maul and [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]], who suspected that the [[Sith]] and Separatists were trying to draw Talzin out into the open to destroy her. The battle was designed to capture [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]] by luring them into a trap on Ord Mantell.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 2" />

Maul planned the trap along with his Shadow Collective allies, including [[Death Watch]], the [[Black Sun]], and the [[Pyke Syndicate]]. Directly before the arrival of the [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]], a group of [[Nightbrothers]] lead by [[Viscus]] arrived in a [[Talzin's shuttle|transport]], which had been sent by Talzin as reinforcements. When the battle began, the Shadow Collective forces engaged the [[battle droid]] [[army]] on the surface, while Maul made his way into orbit and boarded the [[Grievous's Providence-class Dreadnought (Campaign against the Shadow Collective)|command ship]], Grievous disabled the battle droid army after Maul gave him the choice to do so or die, and the [[cyborg]] general was taken into custody. Dooku, meanwhile, had engaged and defeated the Nightbrothers in battle, before being captured by Shadow Collective forces.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 2" />

With the battle over, Maul contacted Talzin, who told him they would soon capture [[Darth Sidious]] and have their revenge against the Sith.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 2" />
