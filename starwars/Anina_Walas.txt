'''Anina Walas''' worked as a Associate Producer on the television series ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Temple Challenge]]'' and various other [[StarWarsKids.com]] productions, and behind the scenes web series such as ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Show]]''. Walas also was a visual effects production manager in ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'', ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''. She is the niece of [[Chris Walas]].
