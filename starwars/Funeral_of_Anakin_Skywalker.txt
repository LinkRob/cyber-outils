In the aftermath of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s defeat against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] held a '''funeral''' for his [[Parent|father]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], on the [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]]. During the battle, Anakin, who had been known as the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Vader, was redeemed by Luke and brought balance to [[the Force]]. However, the redemption cost Anakin his life, having been mortally wounded by the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]], [[Darth Sidious]], while [[Death|killing]] his former [[Sith Master|Master]].

After his death, Anakin became one with the Force. Luke escaped the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]], where Anakin had died, just before the [[battle station]] was destroyed by the Rebel Alliance. Once on Endor, Luke built a [[funeral]] pyre and lit his father's empty [[Darth Vader's armor|armor]] on [[fire]]. After the funeral ended, Luke joined the Rebels and the native [[Ewok]]s for a victory celebration, where he saw the [[Force spirit|spirit]] of his father&mdash;along with the spirits of [[Jedi Master]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Yoda]]&mdash;proudly watching the celebration.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|I'll not leave you here. I've got to save you.''"<br />"''You already have, Luke. You were right. You were right about me.|Luke and Anakin Skywalker, in the moments before Anakin's death|audio=Luke-Vader-NotLeave.flac|<ref name="Episode VI" />}}
[[File:Vader_Grabs_Palpatine.png|thumb|left|225px|Anakin Skywalker grabs Darth Sidious, preparing to throw the Emperor to his death.]]
During the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> a climactic engagement of the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and his son, [[Luke Skywalker]], faced one another in a final [[lightsaber duel]] aboard the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], a [[battle station]] capable of destroying an entire [[planet]]. Luke defeated his [[Parent|father]] and proclaimed himself a [[Jedi Knight]] but was attacked by the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]], [[Darth Sidious]], who used [[Force lightning]] in an attempt to [[Death|kill]] Luke. Refusing to watch his son die, Vader&mdash;who still had good within him&mdash;threw his [[Sith Master|Master]] down a [[reactor]] shaft. This act redeemed Vader from the [[dark side of the Force]], and he once again became Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="Episode VI" /> With Sidious dead, and the persona of Vader also destroyed, the [[Sith]] were defeated.<ref name="Episode VI SW.com">{{SW|url=films/star-wars-episode-vi-return-of-the-jedi|text=''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi''}}</ref>

The choice to save Luke was ultimately a sacrifice, as Sidious' lightning damaged Anakin's [[Life support|life-support]] systems when the redeemed [[Jedi]] threw the Emperor down the shaft. Anakin's redemption and Sidious' death brought balance to [[the Force]], and Anakin fulfilled his destiny as the [[Chosen One]].<ref name="Luke Bio Gallery">{{Databank|luke-skywalker-biography-gallery|Luke Skywalker Biography Gallery}}</ref> Luke attempted to save Anakin by bringing him to a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]], where they could escape the Death Star, but Anakin knew that his death was imminent. At his father's request, Luke removed Darth Vader's [[Darth Vader's armor|helmet]] from Anakin's head, allowing the former [[Sith Lord]] to look upon his son with his own [[eye]]s for the first and last [[time]]. In his final moments, Anakin comforted Luke, reminding his son that there had always been good in him&mdash;just as Luke had believed. Anakin asked Luke to tell his sister, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]] that he was right about him having a good side. Anakin then passed away,<ref name="Episode VI" /> and his body disappeared into the [[light side of the Force]].<ref name="The Story of Darth Vader">[[Star Wars: The Story of Darth Vader (2015)|''Star Wars: The Story of Darth Vader'']]</ref>

==The funeral==
[[File:Anakinfuneral.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Vader's armor burns on the funeral pyre.]]
Luke escaped the Death Star with his father's [[armor]] moments before the battle [[Space station|station]] was destroyed by the Rebel Alliance at the battle's end.<ref name="Episode VI" /> The death of the Emperor and the destruction of the Death Star led to the defeat of the Empire and the restoration of freedom to [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Episode VI SW.com" /> Luke brought Anakin's armor to nearby [[Endor]], where the Rebel Alliance and the native [[Ewok]]s were celebrating the Emperor's defeat, and constructed a funeral [[fire]] near the [[Bright Tree Village|Ewok village]]. When the pyre was constructed,<ref name="Episode VI" /> Luke lit it aflame and burned his father's armor,<ref name="Luke Bio Gallery" /> leaving behind the twisted remains of Darth Vader's helmet.<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

The funeral held importance for Luke, but he knew that for anyone else, the sight of Vader's helmet in flames would be cause for rejoicing. As such, Luke was careful to hold the funeral well away from others.<ref name="Beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref>

==Aftermath==
[[File:LeiaVader-BattleOfJakkuInsurgencyRising1.png|thumb|left|225px|Leia visited the remains of her father shortly after the Battle of Endor, unable to forgive him.]]
As Vader's armor burned, the [[droid]]s [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] found Luke in the [[forest]]. C-3PO expressed his joy at seeing Luke alive and suggested that the young Jedi join the celebration with his fellow rebels in the Ewok village.<ref name="LINE">[[Star Wars (LINE Webtoon)|''Star Wars'' LINE Webtoon]]</ref> Once there, Luke reunited with his friends, including his twin sister, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. Luke briefly stepped away from the celebration and saw the [[Force spirit|spirits]] of his [[Jedi Master]]s, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Yoda]], quietly observing the evening's festivities.<ref name="Episode VI" /> Anakin's spirit, which had been preserved by Kenobi and Yoda,<ref name="Anakin Bio Gallery">{{Databank|anakin-skywalker-biography-gallery|Anakin Skywalker Biography Gallery}}</ref> materialized beside the two Jedi Masters, in the form of his younger self, before his fall to the dark side of the Force. Together, the three watched as Luke looked on. Luke bid them farewell and rejoined the celebration,<ref name="Episode VI" /> his journey to becoming a Jedi Knight complete.<ref name="Luke Databank">{{Databank|luke-skywalker|Luke Skywalker}}</ref>

Later that night, Leia visited the funeral pyre and said goodbye to her father. She knelt to the ground, finding the charred mask of Vader and picked it up. She wished she could forgive Anakin like Luke had, but all she felt was anger at all the pain and suffering he had caused. She threw the mask back into the wooden grave, set her anger aside, and returned back to the celebration.<ref name="BoJ-IR1">[[Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising 1|''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 1]]</ref>

As Vader's body was ablaze, the [[stormtrooper]] [[Karenza Masus|TK-111]] witnessed the event. Reporting back to [[Captain]] [[Enric Pryde]], she led him and fellow trooper [[FS-451]] to the pyre the next morning. Although Pryde did not believe her when she insisted that it had been Vader's body being burned, he would personally investigate and discover that TK-111 had been telling the truth. Pryde briefly dug through the remains of the pyre in search of [[Darth Vader's lightsaber]], wanting the weapon as a symbol of power, but eventually fled the scene after hearing noises of either Rebels or Ewoks coming from the forest nearby.<ref name="The Steadfast Soldier">{{StoryCite|story=The Steadfast Soldier|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref>

[[File:HelmetWarrantyVoided-TFAT2.png|thumb|right|225px|The charred remains of Darth Vader's helmet, scavenged from the funeral pyre on Endor]]
The night before her [[Wedding of Han Solo and Leia Organa|wedding]] to [[Han Solo]], Leia visited the remains of Vader's funeral pyre once more and inspected the remains of his helmet before returning it. It made her glad that, while they both became dust, her adoptive father, [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], was "stardust" while Vader was nothing but "sooty mud," thinking that Bail would drift in the heavens forever while Vader was scum on the bottom of her shoe. She also loudly proclaimed her hatred towards Vader for all he had done before returning to [[Bright Tree Village]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>

Sometime later, Vader's helmet was scavenged from his funeral pyre. Thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the funeral,<ref name="TFA VD" /> the charred and melted helmet was in the possession of Vader's grandson, [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]. He spoke to the twisted helmet for guidance,<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> and the helmet acted as a symbol of the power of the dark side<ref name="TFA VD" />&mdash;as well as its weaknesses, as Ren believed that it was Darth Vader's sentiment in saving Luke that brought down the Empire.<ref name="TFA novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:FuneralStoryboard-MakingofROTJ.png|thumb|left|180px|Storyboard concept art of Anakin Skywalker's funeral]]
Anakin Skywalker's funeral is a scene from the ending of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. When the film was first shot, the funeral scene was not part of the movie. Only after the conclusion of principal photography did [[George Lucas]] and his creative team decide to add the scene. Anakin's death had ended with the character in Luke's [[Limb|arms]], which created concern that the audience might think Anakin was still alive<ref name="MakingOfJedi">''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]''</ref> or, according to editor [[Duwayne Dunham]], that Luke had left his father's body aboard the second Death Star.<ref name="100 Scenes">''[[Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]''</ref> Producer [[Howard G. Kazanjian]] was opposed to the scene at first but changed his mind after viewing an animatic version of it. Once the decision was made to include the funeral pyre, the scene was scripted, given a storyboard treatment by [[Joe Johnston]], and filmed<ref name="MakingOfJedi" /> at [[Skywalker Ranch]] in Marin County, California.<ref name="100 Scenes" />

The events of the funeral were briefly expanded upon in the [[Star Wars (LINE Webtoon)|''Star Wars'' LINE Webtoon]], a [[Star Wars comic books|web comic]] retelling of the [[original trilogy]] from Luke Skywalker's point of view.<ref name="SWcom">{{SW|url=news/a-new-take-on-the-original-trilogy-line-webtoons-digital-star-wars-comic-arrives|text=A New Take on the Original Trilogy: LINE Webtoon's Digital ''Star Wars'' Comic Arrives}}</ref> The LINE Webtoon and its storyline are approved by [[Lucasfilm]], though cultural interpretation affords the storyline some leeway in terms of strictly following [[canon]].<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|jenniferheddle|status/604430151309365249|[[Jennifer Heddle]]|quote=It is approved but its canon status is grey area. We gave them leeway for cultural interpretation|archivefile=File:LINE Webtoon Tweet.png}}</ref> The comic's depiction is similar to that of the [[1996]] [[Return of the Jedi (radio)|''Return of the Jedi'' radio drama]], in which C-3PO finds Luke at Darth Vader's funeral pyre.<ref name="Jedi radio">[[Return of the Jedi (radio)|''Return of the Jedi'' radio drama]]</ref>

On [[April 16]], [[2015]], Lucasfilm released the second teaser trailer for [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']], the seventh live-action installment of the franchise. The teaser included a brief shot of the scorched and twisted mask of Darth Vader,<ref name="Trailer 2">{{SWYouTube|ngElkyQ6Rhs|Star Wars: The Force Awakens Official Teaser #2|sw_url=video/star-wars-the-force-awakens-official-teaser-2}}</ref> a shot that was present in the final cut of the film.<ref name="Episode VII" />
