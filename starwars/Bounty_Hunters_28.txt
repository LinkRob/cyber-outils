'''''Bounty Hunters'' 28''' is the twenty-eighth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 2]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''PANIC AT THE ACCRETION DISCO! The bounty hunters are locked in a desperate attempt to escape the pull of a black hole! To make matters worse, the Pykes are out to kill them first! Meanwhile, what will Valance have to sacrifice to serve the Empire?''

victories he and his team have achieved against Crimson Dawn.

==Development==
The scene in which the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] is seemingly about to [[Force choke]] [[Beilert Valance]], only to instead adjust his collar, is one of writer [[Ethan Sacks]]'s favorite moments in ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. Sacks later shared textless art of the moment when discussing it on Twitter.<ref name="Sacks Collar">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1748121186066718947|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=As I look back at the Bounty Hunters run, I had so many beats, but I think my two favorite are probably: 1.) The old Darth Vader looks like he's about to Force choke Valance, but is just adjusting his collar. (The Sith are neatness freaks, apparently) (Art by @paolovillanelli)|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/k1ias}}</ref>
