The '''Elders''' of the [[Oswaft]] were the leaders of the [[Vacuum/Legends|vacuum]]-breathing species dwelling in the [[ThonBoka]]. Due to their age and wisdom, they were called upon to make the decisions concerning the entire population. The meeting place for the council was the [[Cave of Elders]], located in the center of the [[ThonBoka]] Nebula.

In [[3 BBY/Legends|3 BBY]], they numbered three, with the chief Elder being [[Senwannus'gourkahipaff]], and his second was [[Feytihennasraof]]. [[Bhoggihalysahonues]] was the third and youngest of the group.
