'''D.C. Douglas''' (born February 2) is an American actor, voice actor, writer, director, and editor known for his extensive work in the entertainment industry, including in the science fiction genre. While he is fairly new to the Star Wars franchise, his career spans a wide range of media, including film, television, video games, and cartoons.

Douglas's journey in the Star Wars universe began with providing additional voices in the  MMORPG "[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]." He later lent his voice to the character [[2MED2]] in the popular "[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]" video game. Most recently, Douglas portrayed the complicated character [[Rayvis]] in the highly regarded action-adventure game "[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]],<ref name="Credits">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'' credits</ref>" solidifying his place in the Star Wars canon.

Beyond the Star Wars franchise, Douglas has made contributions to various other science fiction projects. In the realm of video games, he is renowned for his portrayal of the iconic Albert Wesker in the "[https://dcdouglas.com/voice-over-male-talent-los-angeles/albert-wesker/ Resident Evil]" series, appearing in nine games from 2007 to 2019. He also voiced Legion in "Mass Effect 2" and "Mass Effect 3," leaving a lasting impact on fans of the franchise (''"Does this unit... Have a soul?"'').

Douglas's science fiction credits extend to the world of anime and cartoons as well, including "Transformers: Rescue Bots" and "DC Super Hero Girls."

On the silver screen, Douglas has appeared in several science fiction films, including "Sharknado 2" and the SyFy original movie "Isle of the Dead." His television credits include roles in sci-fi shows like "Star Trek: Enterprise" and "Charmed."

Apart from his acting career, Douglas is also a writer, director, and editor. He has written and directed [https://dcdouglas.com/actor-film-television-videos-demo-reel/hit-the-fan/ several short films] and web series.
