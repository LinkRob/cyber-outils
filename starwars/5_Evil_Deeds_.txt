"'''5 Evil Deeds!'''" is an article appearing in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' [[Star Wars Insider 113|113]] that ranks the five most evil deeds in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]], [[prequel trilogy]], and [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]], with each of the three lists being distributed throughout the issue.<ref name="SWI 113" />

==Contents==
*Original Trilogy
**[[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Vader]] cuts off [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke's]] hand.
**[[Destruction of Alderaan/Legends|Destruction of Alderaan]].
**[[Torture/Legends|Torture]] of [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]].
**The [[Palpatine/Legends|Emperor]] tricks the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebels]].
**Death of [[Owen Lars/Legends|Owen]] and [[Beru Whitesun Lars/Legends|Beru]].
*Prequels
**[[Order 66/Legends|Order 66]].
**[[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin]] maims and kills [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]].
**Dooku decrees death in the arena.
**[[Invasion of Naboo/Legends|Invasion of Naboo]].
**[[Sebulba/Legends|Sebulba]] sabotages Anakin's [[Anakin Skywalker's Podracer/Legends|podracer]].
*Expanded Universe
**[[Stormtrooper/Legends|Stormtrooper]] throwing.
**[[Destruction of Taris|Bombardment of Taris]].
**[[Padawan Massacre|Evil]] [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]]!
**[[Hosk Trey'lis]] is killed by the [[Darth Krayt|Sith]].
**[[Mara Jade Skywalker|Mara Jade]] is [[Skirmish on Kavan|murdered]]!
