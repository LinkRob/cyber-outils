[[Admiral]] '''Ers'ikaro''' of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Chiss Defense Force|Defense Force]] was a [[Chiss]] [[flag officer]] who [[Commanding officer|commanded]] the [[Firewolf man-of-war]] [[warship]] [[Bellicose (Firewolf man-of-war)|''Bellicose'']] during a [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|crisis]] in the Ascendancy<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> in [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Lesser Evil Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> During the conflict, the flag [[officer]] stopped a [[Battle at Csaus|battle]] between the [[Tahmie]] and [[Droc family|Droc]] [[Great Families|families]] before it could further escalate.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

The same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Lesser Evil Date" /> Ers'ikaro was present in a meeting room where [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," was sentenced to [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] from the Ascendancy due to his [[Non-intervention policy|preemptive strikes]] against various nations. Ers'ikaro, along with four other admirals, delivered the verdict to Thrawn himself.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> During his exile, Thrawn strongly remembered Ers'ikaro and the other admirals rising from their chairs to announce their decision.<ref name="IMO">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Ers'ikaro was first mentioned, albeit indirectly, in the [[2017]] novel [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']], the first volume of the [[Star Wars: The Imperial Trilogy|''Thrawn'' novel series]] by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="IMO" /> Ers'ikaro later appeared, and was also identified, in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final volume of the [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''Star Wars: Thrawn Ascendancy Trilogy'']] written by Zahn.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
