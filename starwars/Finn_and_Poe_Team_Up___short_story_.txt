"'''Finn and Poe Team Up!'''" is a [[2017]] short story published in the anthology ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories Strike Back]]'' that is a partial adaptation of the [[Star Wars saga|''Star Wars'' saga]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].

The story re-used illustrations by [[Pilot Studio]] previously published in the [[2016]] [[Finn and Poe Team Up! (book)|''Finn and Poe Team Up!'']] of the same name by [[Nate Millici]],<ref name="FaPTU">[[Finn and Poe Team Up! (book)|''Finn and Poe Team Up!'']]</ref> with new text for more advanced readers written by [[Calliope Glass]].
