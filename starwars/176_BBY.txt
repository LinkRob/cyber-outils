'''176 BBY''', or '''176<ref name="SolDate" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Around that year,<ref name="SolDate" /> the [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Sol]] left his [[Sol's family|family]] to join the [[Jedi Order]] and become a [[Jedi]].<ref name="Destiny" /> Sol eventually ascended to the rank of [[Jedi Knight]]<ref name="EPG">{{SW|url=series/the-acolyte/destiny-episode-guide|text="Destiny" Episode Guide &#124; The Acolyte}}</ref> by [[148 BBY]]. That year,<ref name="ConvoDate">The events of "[[Destiny (The Acolyte)|Destiny]]" take place sixteen [[Standard year|years]] before the present day events of [[Star Wars: The Acolyte|''The Acolyte'']], which occur in [[132 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:TheAcolyteDate|here]].</ref> while on a [[Mission to Brendok|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Brendok]], Sol and [[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]] tested the [[Aniseya family|twins]] [[Mae-ho Aniseya|Mae-ho]] and [[Verosha Aniseya]] for their ability to use [[the Force]]. While testing Verosha, Sol told her about his own experience with joining the Jedi Order, mentioning he joined it at the age of four.<ref name="Destiny" />

==Behind the scenes==
176 BBY was first indirectly mentioned in "[[Destiny (The Acolyte)|Destiny]],"<ref name="Destiny" /> the third episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] series [[Star Wars: The Acolyte|''The Acolyte'']], which released on [[June 11]], [[2024]].<ref name="EPG" />
