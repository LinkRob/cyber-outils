'''Christian Simpson''' is an actor, writer, and composer.

==Biography==
Christian Simpson portrayed several characters including starfighter pilot [[Bravo Six/Legends|Bravo Six]], Lieutenant [[Gavyn Sykes/Legends|Gavyn Sykes]], in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Blog: The Force">{{Blog|bravo|22/comments|Making Episode III: 31 Jan 2005 - 'The Force binds the galaxy together'|nobackup=1}}</ref><ref name="Star Wars: The Official Starships & Vehicles Collection 62">[[Star Wars: The Official Starships & Vehicles Collection 62|''Star Wars: The Official Starships & Vehicles Collection'' 62]]</ref> Simpson's likeness was reused for Sykes in the MMORPG ''[[Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided]]'' and the game [[Star Wars: Episode I: Battle for Naboo|''Battle For Naboo'']].

Simpson served as [[Hayden Christensen]]'s stand-in during the filming of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref>{{SW|url=news/from-fandom-to-phantom-dominate-your-destiny-yoda-quotes-can|text=From Fandom to ''Phantom'': Dominate Your Destiny, Yoda Quotes Can|nolive=1}}</ref><ref>{{Blogspot|subdomain=starwarsinterviews1|url=2010/02/christian-j-simpson-interview-star-wars.html|name=Star Wars Interviews|text=Christian Simpson Interview}}</ref> as well as playing the actions of the female [[BD-3000 luxury droid]].

In [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] Simpson voiced aliens and stormtroopers,<ref>{{SW|url=news/from-blast-that-x-wing-to-traitor-the-voices-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=From "Blast That X-wing!" to "Traitor!": The Voices of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''}}</ref><ref>{{Twitter|JATactor|status/677964176338235392|[[James Arnold Taylor]]|quote=Familiar voices are in that new movie! @MattLanter @cattaber @matthewood @MeredthSalenger @SamWitwer @DavidWCollins|archivedate=20181030001026}}</ref> as well as co-producing [[Nien Nunb]]'s dialogue with [[Matthew Wood]].<ref name="Road to Rotich">{{SW|url=news/the-road-to-rotich-finding-nien-nunb-part-1|text=The Road to Rotich: Finding Nien Nunb, Part 1|nolive=1}}</ref><ref>{{Facebook|username=christianjsimpson|url=christianjsimpson/posts/10153874518182764|name=Christian Simpson|nobackup=1}}</ref>

Christian Simpson is the voice of Resistance X-Wing pilot [[C'ai Threnalli]], whose appearances include ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'' and ''The Force Awakens''.<ref>{{Twitter|chrisimpsonline|status/866687686198923264|[[Christian Simpson]]|nobackup=1}}</ref><ref>{{Facebook|username=chrisimpsonline|url=chrisimpsonline/photos/a.124024102763.103495.16384802763/10155206873437764|name=Christian Simpson|quote=A year ago today I recorded my part as X-Wing pilot C'ai Threnalli for LEGO Star Wars: #TheForceAwakens (though I couldn't tell you about it or they'd ship me to Abednedo) 🤐👽🚀 #MTFBWY!|archiveurl=https://archive.is/2amiH}}</ref>

He also voices the member of [[Saw Gerrera]]'s Rebels reclaiming [[Jedha City|Jedha]] [[Kyber crystal]]s, as well as [[Death Star]] Imperials, in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref>{{Facebook|username=chrisimpsonline|url=chrisimpsonline/photos/a.124024102763.103495.16384802763/10154891474117764|name=Christian Simpson|quote=A long time ago aged 7, I dreamt of being in A New Hope. I'm giddy to say that I'm now 10 minutes before it!|archiveurl=https://archive.is/Cw1Tl}}</ref>

In [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker]] he co-scripted & directed [[Nien Nunb]]'s dialogue, performed by Simpson's client and Nunb voice actor since 1983, [[Kipsang Rotich]].<ref>{{Facebook|username=chrisimpsonline|url=chrisimpsonline/photos/wow-rise-of-skywalker-is-everything-you-wanted-a-new-star-wars-movie-to-be-every/10157974639592764|name=Christian Simpson|quote=Wow Rise of Skywalker is everything you wanted|nobackup=1}}</ref>

In 2014 Simpson also became a contributor for [[StarWars.com]].<ref>{{SW|url=news/contributor/csimpson|text=Christian Simpson}}</ref>
