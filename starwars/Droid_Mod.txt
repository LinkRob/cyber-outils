A '''Droid Mod''' was a type of [[droid]] intelligence which could be installed in a [[starship]], acting as an integrated [[Pilot|co-pilot]].<ref name="EpisodeGuide4">{{SW|url=news/andor-episode-4-aldhani-highlights|text=''Andor'' Analyzed: 5 Highlights from Episode 4, "Aldhani"}}</ref><ref name="EpisodeGuide2">{{SW|url=series/andor/andor-episode-4-aldhani|text="Aldhani" Episode Guide &#124; Andor}}</ref><ref name="ThatWouldBeMe">{{SW|url=series/andor/andor-season-1-episode-2-episode-guide|text="That Would Be Me" Episode Guide &#124; Andor}}</ref>

==History==
[[Luthen Rael]] made use of a [[Fondor Droid Mod]] on his heavily modified [[Luthen Rael's Fondor Haulcraft|Fondor Haulcraft]].<ref name="Ferrix" /><ref name="Aldhani" /> This droid mod had a swiveling set of [[Photoreceptor]]s installed in the cockpit, was capable of speech (though with a somewhat neutral personality), and was capable of calculating hyperspace jumps, flying and landing the ship with skill, arming weapons, analyzing sensor readings, and many other ship functions.<ref name="Aldhani" /><ref name="ThatWouldBeMe" /><ref name="Ferrix" /> Ultimately the [[Fondor Droid Mod]] proved invaluable during [[Luthen Rael]]'s confrontation with a [[Cantwell-class Arrestor Cruiser]], the ability to multi-task and divide ship functions giving him a potent edge in combat and his subsequent escape.<ref name="Ferrix">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref>
