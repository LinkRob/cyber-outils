A '''communications team''' or '''comms team''' was responsible for receiving messages via [[EX communication droid|EX droids]] from [[Republic Pathfinder]]s who were far away in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Inheritance" /> They would then set up a string of [[relay buoy]]s to keep the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] in contact with the frontier worlds.<ref name="QftHC">''[[The High Republic: Quest for the Hidden City]]''</ref>
