'''Droid boxing'''<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was an activity that involved sending two [[droid]]s to duel in [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]]. Aboard the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[starship]] [[Marauder (Weequay pirate ship)|''Marauder'']], Weequay pirates sent droids to box&mdash;one such droid being the modified [[ASP-series labor droid]] [[K0-5D]].<ref name="Nomad Droids">{{TCW|Nomad Droids}}</ref> K0-5D's boxing skills earned him the nickname "Maximo."<ref name="K05D databank">{{Databank|k0-5d|K0-5D}}</ref>
