==Behind the scenes==
Deeta Kardon appeared in the [[2026]] audiobook ''[[Terror on Mustafar]]'', which was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and narrated by [[Marc Thompson]]. As the story is only available in audio format, no official spelling for the character's name is available.<ref name="Terror" />
