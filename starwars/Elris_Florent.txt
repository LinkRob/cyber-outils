'''Elris Florent''' was a [[Gender|female]] member of the [[Disciples of the Whills]] [[religion]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. In [[382 BBY]], Florent was the Disciples' [[representative]] on the [[Convocation of the Force]] council on the holy [[moon]] [[Jedha]]. She stood by the [[Yacombe]] religion when they sent an [[Yacombe Delegate|envoy]] to apply for membership onto the Convocation.

Later, Florent supported the [[Temple of the Kyber]]'s protectors, the [[Guardians of the Whills]], in their decision to arrest the [[thief]] [[Tey Sirrek]] for the temple's [[Bombing at the Temple of the Kyber|bombing]]. She also defended herself, her religion, and the Convocation to [[Werth Plouth]], the [[Herald (title)|Herald]] of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] cult, when he petitioned for the disbandment of the Convocation. Florent was caught up in a riot sparked by Plouth at the beginning of the [[Battle of Jedha]] after his petition was rejected.

==Biography==
===Convocation matters===
{{Quote|Then why don't they speak for themselves?''"<br />"''They don't speak at all, Representative ''Waran''. Only through their proxies. You ''know'' that.|Waran Val and Elris Florent on the Yacombe|<ref name="Peace and Unity" />}}
Elris Florent<ref name="Art of THR" /> was [[Reproduction|born]] between around 441 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 432 BBY<ref name="Birth" /> and was in her fifties<ref name="Art of THR" /> in [[382 BBY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">{{PeaceandUnityDate}}</ref> she was the [[representative]] for the [[Disciples of the Whills]] [[religion]] on the [[Convocation of the Force]], a council of [[The Force|Force]]-affiliated groups that operated on<ref name="Peace and Unity" /> the holy [[moon]]<ref name="CotJ">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Jedha]]. Florent was present at the [[Convocation Chambers]] when the [[Yacombe]] organization sent an [[Yacombe Delegate|envoy]] to apply for membership onto the Convocation.<ref name="Peace and Unity" /> During the application, Florent remained calm and open to hearing all sides,<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> but the [[Fallanassi]] religion's representative, [[Sirené Taran]], went to attack the [[envoy]], forcing the [[Lonto]]<ref name="Peace and Unity" /> [[species]]'<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> representative, [[Susalee]],<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> to intervene and stop the violence. In response, Florent urged the Convocation's<ref name="Peace and Unity" /> [[Chairman|chair]], [[Kilan T'Dara]],<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> to intervene and stop Taran. T'Dara complied, but the chair's plea for the Fallanassi to stop failed.<ref name="Peace and Unity" />

[[File:Convocation-PeaceAndUnity.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Florent and the other members of the Convocation watch as Susalee holds back Sirené from attacking the Yacombe envoy.]]
Florent spoke with the [[Matukai]] religious group's representative,<ref name="Peace and Unity" /> [[Waran Val]],<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> who was also antagonizing the envoy.<ref name="Peace and Unity" /> She<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> reminded Val that the Yacombe did not speak and only communicated through their proxies before adding that Val should have known that fact already. Val expressed disbelief that out of all the representatives, Florent and the Disciples of the Whills would vouch for the Yacombe, to which Florent responded that she felt the Convocation should at least listen to the Yacombe's application.<ref name="Peace and Unity" /> [[Jedi Knight]]<ref name="PoV">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> [[Oliviah Zeveron]] then stood in to defend the Yacombe envoy, forcing the attackers to stand down. Because of the incident, the Yacombe envoy withdrew their application and left.<ref name="Peace and Unity" />

Later, a meeting of the Convocation discussing the [[Bombing at the Temple of the Kyber|bombing]] of the [[Temple of the Kyber]] was held with<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> Jedi Knight<ref name="The High Republic 2">[[The High Republic (2022) 2|''The High Republic'' (2022) 2]]</ref> [[Vildar Mac]] and<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Matthea Cathley]].<ref name="PoV" /> When Mac suggested that the [[Guardians of the Whills]], the protectors of the temple, might have jumped to conclusions in arresting the [[thief]] [[Tey Sirrek]] for the crime due to Sirrek's history with the organization, Florent insisted that the Guardians,<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> who she worked closely with,<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> did not make assumptions and that their [[Captain]] [[Oklane Viss]] was dealing with the situation personally. Despite Cathley's continued protests, T'Dara revealed that the official story the Convocation was presenting was that a buildup of [[gas]] had caused the explosion.<ref name="The High Republic 4" />

===The Herald of the Open Hand===
{{Quote|Representative Kilan, I ''refuse'' to be addressed in this manner. The Disciples of the Whills&mdash;''"<br />"''Are one of the oldest and most respected orders on Jedha&hellip; Yes, we are aware of that, Elris.|Florent and Kilan T'Dara after Werth Plouth insulted Florent|<ref name="The High Republic 4" />}}
When [[Werth Plouth]], the [[Herald (title)|Herald]] of the [[Path of the Open Hand]], a cult that believed [[Force power|using]] the Force was wrong, petitioned the Convocation for the council's disbandment and claimed that they were all abusing the Force by using it, Florent called Plouth arrogant and insisted his claims were preposterous. In response, Plouth asked Florent if their [[robe]]s were [[Color|crimson]] to cover up the [[blood]] on their [[Limb|hands]]. Feeling insulted, Florent told Representative T'Dara that she refused to be addressed in such a manner and started to proclaim the legitimacy of the Disciples of the Whills before T'Dara cut her off and told her they were all aware the Disciples were one of the oldest and most respected orders on Jedha.<ref name="The High Republic 4" />

[[File:JedhaRiot-2022THR5.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Florent is caught up in the riots at the beginning of the Battle of Jedha.]]
After Plouth stormed out of the Convocation Chambers into<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> the [[Square of the Supplicants]]<ref name="PoV" /> and attempted to rile up a crowd that had gathered there against the Convocation and the Jedi, Florent and T'Dara followed the [[Sorcerers of Tund]] Force group's representative [[Tarna Miak]] outside to confront Plouth. However, Plouth successfully incited a riot,<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> which Florent was caught in<ref name="The High Republic 5">[[The High Republic (2022) 5|''The High Republic'' (2022) 5]]</ref> and many of the Convocation members took part in.<ref name="PoV" /> In the riot, which evolved into the [[Battle of Jedha]], Florent was part of a group knocked down by members of<ref name="The High Republic 5" /> the order called the [[Brotherhood of the Beatific Countenance]].<ref name="CotJ" /> Florent became one of many attacked by Susalee, who had been psychologically affected by<ref name="The High Republic 5" /> the [[Great Leveler|Leveler]], a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature utilized by the Path that preyed on [[Force-sensitive]]s and caused them to [[Hallucination|hallucinate]].<ref name="PoV" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|The Guardians of the Whills do ''not'' make assumptions.|Florent defends the Guardians of the Whills to Vildar Mac|<ref name="The High Republic 4" />}}
Elris Florent was a respected and somewhat regal [[Gender|woman]]<ref name="Art of THR" /> who had brown [[eye]]s, light skin,<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> and was 1.73 [[meter]]s (5 [[Foot|feet]], 8 inches) tall. During the Yacombe's petition for membership, she was able to remain calm and was open to hearing both sides.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> She believed strongly in the Guardians of the Whills,<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> who she worked closely with,<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> and defended the organization's integrity to Vildar Mac. Florent also took pride in the Disciples of the Whills and their history and did not like when someone disrespected her as Werth Plouth did.<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> She performed [[meditation]] to try and hear the [[Cosmic Force|will of the Force]], and because of this she and the other Disciples of the Whills believed the [[dark side of the Force]] should not be shunned, but instead held in balance with the [[Light side of the Force|light side]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Tell me, representative, are your robes ''crimson'' to hide the ''blood'' that stains your hands?|Werth Plouth, to Florent|<ref name="The High Republic 4" />}}
Florent wore crimson [[Clothing|robes]]<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> that reflected her high status,<ref name="Art of THR" /> gold chains around her body, and a brown [[belt]]. On her face, she wore gold jewelry, including a [[Star|sun]]-shaped headpiece<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> that represented the will of the Force,<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> a nose chain, and spangles dotted across her cheeks, nose, and chin. She also carried a gold [[staff]].<ref name="The High Republic 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:The-High-Republic-2022-4-final-cover.jpg|thumb|right|200px|''The High Republic'' (2022) 4 marked the first use of Elris Florent's surname.]]
Elris Florent was created by [[Cavan Scott]] and designed by [[Andrea Broccardo]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia initiative.<ref name="Art of THR" /> She first appeared in Scott's [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story "[[Peace and Unity]]," which was illustrated by Broccardo and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Peace and Unity" /> on [[October 12]], [[2022]],<ref name="Marvel Issue 1">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/100736/star_wars_the_high_republic_2022_1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic (2022) #1}}</ref> in the [[The High Republic (2022) 1|first issue]] of the 2022 comic series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2022)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']]<ref name="Peace and Unity" /> as part of [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]] of the initiative.<ref name="Art of THR" /> The character, only identified as "Elris" in the story,<ref name="Peace and Unity" /> had her last name revealed in the [[The High Republic (2022) 4|fourth issue]] of the series,<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> which was released on [[January 11]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel Issue 4">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/100739/star_wars_the_high_republic_2022_4|text=Star Wars: The High Republic (2022) #4}}</ref>

Scott's original brief and artist notes for the character called for Florent to resemble an [[Wikipedia:Indian people|Indian]] woman and for her robes to look like a more tailored version of the Disciples of the Whills' red robes from<ref name="Art of THR" /> the [[2016]]<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]].'' In the brief, Scott called Florent the leader of the Convocation,<ref name="Art of THR" /> but Kilan T'Dara was established as its leader in "Peace and Unity" instead.<ref name="Peace and Unity" />
