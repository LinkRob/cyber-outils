'''''Darth Vader'' 6''' is the sixth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. It was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 14]], [[2020]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE PUNISHMENT! DARTH VADER failed to turn his son, Luke Skywalker, to the dark side of the Force. No&hellip;Luke was too weak to join his father in overthrowing EMPEROR PALPATINE. Vader sought revenge against those who hid Luke from him for all of these years&hellip; and in doing so nearly betrayed his master. It did not go unnoticed by Palpatine&hellip; time for Vader's next lesson.''
