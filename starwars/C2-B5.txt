'''C2-B5''' was a [[Color|black]]-plated [[astromech droid]] in the service of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[droid]] was frequently subjected to [[memory wipe]]s to keep it subservient, causing the astromech to have no distinctive personality. It was capable of deploying a large [[personal energy shield]], [[Scan pulse|scanning]] the area for hostiles, and releasing a [[poison]]ous [[dioxis]] [[gas]].

The astromech was stationed in an [[Imperial base (Mustafar)|Imperial facility]] near [[Fortress Vader]] on the planet [[Mustafar]] when agents of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Mission to Mustafar (Rebel Alliance)|infiltrated]] the base in [[1 BBY]]. Later that [[Standard year|year]], C2-B5 was deployed in the [[Imperial Center of Military Research|Imperial security complex]] on the planet [[Scarif]], patrolling for needed maintenance and performing [[computer]] network sweeps within the [[Citadel Tower]]'s [[Scarif vault|data vault]]. The droid was present in the Citadel when the [[Battle of Scarif]] broke out against the rebels, which culminated in the destruction of the entire base by the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]].

==Biography==
C2-B5 was an [[astromech droid]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="RO:UVG" /> and was deployed in an [[Imperial base (Mustafar)|Imperial base]] located near [[Fortress Vader]],<ref name="SotE">''[[Star Wars: Secrets of the Empire]]''</ref> the personal residence of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on the [[Magma|lava]] [[planet]] [[Mustafar]]. In the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]],<ref name="SWT">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[droid]] was present at the base when a team of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] operatives disguised as [[stormtrooper]]s, along with the rebel [[spy]] [[Athex]] and ex-Imperial [[KX-series security droid|security droid]] [[K-2SO]], [[Mission to Mustafar (Rebel Alliance)|infiltrated]] the facility to recover intelligence regarding a [[Proto-saber|powerful artifact]] in Imperial possession.<ref name="SotE" />

[[File:C2-B5_Rogue_One.png|thumb|left|300px|C2-B5 approaches Jyn Erso, a rebel infiltrator disguised in an [[Imperial ground crew]] uniform.]]
Later in 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="SWT" /> the droid was stationed in the [[Imperial Center of Military Research|Imperial security complex]] on the tropical planet [[Scarif]], tasked with searching for maintenance needs,<ref name="RO:UVG" /> carrying out security tasks,<ref name="Connecting the galaxy">{{Facebook|username=starwarsrebels|url=photo?fbid=659498580873435&set=a.230754950414469|name=Star Wars Rebels|date=2016-10-12|quote=They don't trust them as much as the Rebels, but the Empire uses astromechs, too.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/7RHE3}}</ref> and sweeping the [[computer]] network to locate any electronic discrepancies within the [[Scarif vault|data vault]] of the [[Citadel Tower]].<ref name="RO:UVG" /> The Imperial astromech was present in the Citadel when a rebel [[squad]] [[Callsign|called]] [[Rogue One]] infiltrated the base to [[Thief|steal]] the [[Death Star plans|plans]] for the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]. Three undercover rebel agents&mdash;[[Jyn Erso]], [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], and K-2SO&mdash;walked past C2-B5 inside the base.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

Soon after, when [[rebel trooper]]s outside the base began their attack to facilitate the rebel trio's entrance into the [[data vault]], the astromech roamed the Citadel corridors alongside the [[Scarif Deployment|stormtrooper garrison]] rushing to intercept the rebel infiltrators. The [[Battle of Scarif]] eventually concluded with the Alliance's successful theft of the plans and the destruction of the entire Imperial complex by the [[Death Star]]'s [[Mk I Superlaser|superlaser]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Characteristics==
[[File:C2B5-ROOCE.png|thumb|right|255px|C2-B5 had gunmetal-based, black plating with matte black and silver accent panels.]]
C2-B5 was a [[Color|black]]-plated, sinister-looking astromech droid<ref name="Topps Finest" /> that stood 0.93 [[meter]]s (3 [[Foot|feet]] 1 inch) tall.<ref name="AEYNTK:UE" /> Featuring [[gunmetal]]-based plating with matte black and silver accent panels around its cylindrical body, C2-B5 contained a black [[sensor]] and a [[processor]] state indicator on the front of its dome<ref name="Sideshow" /> that produced a red light, which could alternatively switch to blue.<ref name="SWBF" /> The astromech also had another indicator on the back of its dome<ref name="Sideshow" /> that emitted light in alternating yellow and green.<ref name="SWBF" /> C2-B5's dome additionally featured an illuminated logic function and diagnostic light display ports,<ref name="Sideshow" /> and the droid also had a retractable third [[Limb|leg]].<ref name="SWBF" />

As an Imperial droid, C2-B5 was not granted independence and was routinely subjected to [[memory wipe]]s by Imperial [[technician]]s to ensure its subservience.<ref name="Topps Finest" /> For this reason, the droid did not have any sort of distinctive personality<ref name="RO:UVG" /> and was devoid of emotions such as fear, anger, and greed, but still maintained a level of unique comportment. An honorable droid,<ref name="Top Trumps">{{TopTrumps|set=Star Wars: Rogue One|cardname=C2-B5|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/146126/cid/9471032}}</ref> C2-B5 considered serving the Empire as its sole purpose until it was destroyed or its memory was wiped for a new mission.<ref name="FC" /> The Imperial droid was the opposite of the rebel astromech [[R2-D2]] in many ways.<ref name="Topps Finest" />

==Equipment==
{{Listen3|C2-B5Shield.flac|C2-B5 deploys a personal energy shield}}
C2-B5 was equipped with a [[deflector shield generator]] capable of deploying a large [[personal energy shield]], and the astromech could also release [[Poison|toxic]] [[dioxis]] [[gas]] in order to neutralize nearby opponents. The droid was additionally able to emit a 360-degree [[scan pulse]] burst that revealed enemies out of sight.<ref name="SWBF" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Development===
{{Quote|There was a lot of thought given to making changes to the R2 units while staying within the protocol of what's been established. ''[C2-B5]'' is a product of that imagination.|Neal Scanlan|<ref name="EW Guide">''[[Entertainment Weekly's Ultimate Guide to Rogue One]]''</ref>}}
[[File:CanaryWharf-SWI211.png|thumb|left|335px|C2-B5 was part of the ''Rogue One'' filming at the Canary Wharf station.]]
C2-B5 was created for ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="EW Guide" /> a [[2016]] film that marks the first entry in the ''[[Star Wars Anthology Series]]''.<ref name="Year By Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> The droid was first seen in a behind-the-scenes reel for ''Rogue One'' that debuted during the film's panel at [[Celebration Europe (2016)|''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2016]]<ref name="RO panel">{{SWYouTube|QU9556YKZnU|Rogue One: A Star Wars Story Panel &#124; Star Wars Celebration Europe 2016|date=2016-07-22}}</ref> on [[July 15]], 2016.<ref name="Year By Year" /> The character was later fully revealed in an episode of ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'', a then-weekly digital video series by [[Lucasfilm]], released on [[August 31]], 2016.<ref name="SW Show">{{StarWarsShow|GJWjotccTHg|New Rogue One Droid Revealed, Rayne Roberts Interview, and More|sw_url=video/the-star-wars-show-episode-17|date=2016-08-31}}</ref>

C2-B5 was created by [[R2-D2 Builders Club]] member [[Lee Towersey]], who initially gave the droid a different name.<ref name="Revisited">{{WebCite|url=https://droidbuilders.uk/2019/10/24/c2-b5-revisited/|text=C2-B5 Revisited|author=[[Lee Towersey]]|date=2019-10-24|work=DroidBuilders UK|archivedate=20250821202219}}</ref> According to [[Neal Scanlan]], creature effects supervisor for ''Rogue One'', C2-B5 was created upon the idea of reimagining [[R2-series astromech droid|R2 units]] while respecting the model's established characteristics.<ref name="EW Guide" /> Dubbed R2-D2's "[[Wikipedia:Goth subculture|goth]] [[twin]],"<ref name="Cineplex">{{BookCite|author=Randoja, Ingrid|container=Rebel with a Cause|title=[[Wikipedia:Cineplex Entertainment|Cineplex Magazine]]|contributor=Weisz, Marni|number=vol. 17, no. 11|publisher=[[Wikipedia:Salah Bachir|Bachir, Salah]]|date=[[December]] [[2016]]|quote=In ''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' we meet C2-B5, an Imperial astromech droid that's been dubbed R2-D2's 'goth twin.'|link=https://web.archive.org/web/20180124023048/https://mediafiles.cineplex.com/Attachments/NewItems/CineplexMagazine_DEC2016_72dpi_20161202150310_0.pdf}}</ref> C2-B5 only briefly appeared in ''Rogue One'' as a background character despite the droid's prominent presence in the film's marketing and merchandising.<ref name="SlashFilm">{{WebCite|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/548349/c2-b5-rogue-one|text=Imperial Droid C2-B5 Is The Constable Zuvio of 'Rogue One'|author=Sciretta, Peter|date=2016-12-22|work=[[Wikipedia:/Film|/Film]]|archivedate=20220815194919}}</ref> A physical model for C2-B5 was built and used for the Scarif Citadel interiors sequence,<ref name="Celebration Reel">{{SWYouTube|HUb_zpdyDpU|Rogue One: A Star Wars Story - Celebration Reel|sw_url=video/rogue-one-a-star-wars-story-celebration-reel|date=2016-07-15}}</ref><ref name="ILM">{{ILMVFXYouTube|ZBvi8opWTiQ|ILM: Behind the Magic in Rogue One: A Star Wars Story|date=2017-09-27}}</ref> which was shot at the [[Wikipedia:Canary Wharf tube station|Canary Wharf station]] on the [[Wikipedia:London Underground|London Underground]] in a single night<ref name="RO:UVG" /> on [[August 16]], [[2015]].<ref name="Featurette">{{HomeVideoCite|film=Rogue One|featurette=K-2SO: The Droid}}</ref>

The concept art for [[First Order Cargo]], a real-life store in [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] themed land, features repurposed promotional images of C2-B5.<ref name="Disneyland">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=shops/disneyland/first-order|text=First Order Cargo &#124; Disneyland Resort|oldversion=20190603170919}}</ref> While official sources use it/its pronouns for C2-B5,<ref name="FC" /><ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] members [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Matt Martin]] refer to the character as he/him.<ref name="SlashFilm" /><ref name="Twitter 1">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1304513169944014848|[[Matt Martin]]|date=2020-09-11|quote=We worked him into Secrets of the Empire just because he's so cool looking.|archivedate=20201020194959}}</ref>

===In ''Star Wars'' gaming===
{{Quote|As the director of Advanced Weapons Research, Krennic has access to C2-B5, an astromech droid, and can deploy it in battle. This droid will periodically scan the area for enemies, and will protect itself by deploying a large personal shield. Once C2-B5 detects a Rebel, it will release a dioxis gas to neutralize the enemy. This capablity to detect and dispatch enemies gives Krennic ability to impact the movement of both allies and enemies on the battlefield.|Official [[Electronic Arts]] website|<ref name="EA">{{EA|subdomain=starwars|url=starwars/battlefront/news/rogue-one-scarif-new-hero-details|text=Rogue One: Scarif - New Hero Details|date=2016-12-12|nolive=1}}</ref>}}
[[File:C2-B5_Battlefront.png|thumb|right|290px|C2-B5 deploys a large personal energy shield in ''Star Wars Battlefront''.]]
Prior to the release of ''Rogue One'' on [[December 16]],<ref name="Year By Year" /> C2-B5 made its first appearance in the ''Rogue One: Scarif'' expansion pack of the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'',<ref name="SWBF" /> released on [[December 6]], 2016.<ref name="BF date">{{SW|url=news/palm-trees-and-death-troopers-rogue-one-scarif-expansion-arrives-for-battlefront|text=Palm Trees and Death Troopers: ''Rogue One: Scarif'' Expansion Arrives for ''Battlefront'' - Exclusive Interview|author=[[Dan Brooks|Brooks, Dan]]|date=2016-12-07|quote=''Rogue One: Scarif'', the final expansion for ''Star Wars Battlefront'', arrived yesterday and it's something of a landmark release.}}</ref> In the game, the droid can be deployed into battle as a non-playable character by the player-controlled [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]]. The astromech then generates a large personal [[Deflector shield|shield]] to protect itself and its Imperial allies, releases a cloud of dioxis gas to deal damage to nearby rebel opponents, and periodically scans the area to reveal enemies hidden from view.<ref name="SWBF" />

According to Matt Martin, due to C2-B5's appealing look, the character was included in ''[[Star Wars: Secrets of the Empire]]'',<ref name="Twitter 1" /> a [[2017]] hyper-reality experience created by [[ILM Immersive|ILMxLAB]] and [[The VOID]].<ref name="D23 SotE">{{D23|url=a-to-z/star-wars-secrets-empire|text=Star Wars: Secrets of the Empire}}</ref> The droid could be physically interacted with in the experience's [[Wikipedia:Disney Parks, Experiences and Products|Disney Parks]] locations.<ref name="Twitter 2">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1304580173543534592|[[Matt Martin]]|date=2020-09-12|quote=The Disney Parks locations have him. Not sure which others.|archivedate=20200912005254}} {{C|In response to: "Which places have [a physical] one, and I'll aim for that next time&hellip;?"}}</ref> C2-B5 also appeared in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]]ical ''[[LEGO Star Wars Battles]]'' mobile game as a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] reinforcement that can be unlocked in battles on the planet [[Kamino]]. When deployed into combat, the droid repairs allied structures and zaps enemy ground [[troop]]s with an electric arm. According to the game, C2-B5 is strong against [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut|clone turbo tanks]] and good with [[turbolaser]]s and [[Flame Tower]]s but weak against [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing bombers]] in battle. While the cards menu animation for C2-B5 shows the astromech trying to drink from a red mug but later realizing it is empty, a [[Light side of the Force|light side]] emote features<ref name="LEGO SWB">''[[LEGO Star Wars Battles]]''</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]]<ref name="AEYNTK:UE" /> [[Yoda]] hitting C2-B5 with his [[Gimer stick|stick]].<ref name="LEGO SWB" />
