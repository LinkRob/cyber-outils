"'''Call to Action'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]]: "'''Aufruf zum Widerstand'''") is an episode of the [[German Rebels audio dramas]] adapting the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Call to Action]]". It was released on [[June 11]], [[2015]] by [[Universal Music Group]] as part of the album [[Star Wars Rebels Episode 6 - Call to Action / Rebel Resolve]]. The episode was written by [[Gabriele Bingenheimer]], directed by [[Marian Szymczyk]] and includes narration by [[Hanns Jörg Krumpholz]].<ref name="Kiddinx" />
