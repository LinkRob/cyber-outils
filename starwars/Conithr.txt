'''Conithr''' was a [[Jedi Master]] at the [[Coronet City temple]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. She helped [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Ruu]] to help change his name in the [[Jedi]] records and later to became his Master. After the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], Conithr refused to come back to [[Coruscant]] and chose to defend [[Corellia]] from the [[Nihil]]. She sacrificed herself to save her [[Padawan]] Ruu in a fight against the Nihil.<ref name="Rogue Element" />
