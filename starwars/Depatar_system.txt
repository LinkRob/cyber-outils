The '''Depatar system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Cademimu sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square L-6 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained a [[Unidentified planet (Depatar)|planet]] that was the site of the [[spaceport]] [[Depatar]].<ref name="Origin" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Depatar/Legends|Depatar]] was a floating city above the [[gas giant]] [[Genarius]] in the [[Cularin system]].<ref name="cp">{{LivingForce|Clouds of Genarius: Clouded Paths}}</ref> The [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix of [[star system]]s placed the Depatar system in the Cademimu sector of the Outer Rim to be near the Lost Souls asteroid field,<ref name="Appendix" /> conflicting with the Legends placement of Genarius in the [[Expansion Region]],<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> but the appendix's author [[Jason Fry]] has stated that Depatar's Outer Rim placement will be kept.<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-08-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-08-31}}</ref>
