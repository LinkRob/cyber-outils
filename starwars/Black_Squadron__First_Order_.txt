'''Black Squadron''' was a [[First Order military|military]] [[squadron]] of [[First Order]] [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] active<ref name="Ships of the Galaxy" /> about thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Set to Roll">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-vii-set-to-roll-cameras-may-2014|text=Star Wars: Episode VII Set to Roll Cameras May 2014}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Black Squadron was mentioned in the exclusive blueprint for the [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]] in ''[[Star Wars: Ships of the Galaxy]]'', a reference book released on [[Force Friday]] on [[September 4]], [[2015]].
