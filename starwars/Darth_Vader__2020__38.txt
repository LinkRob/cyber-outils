'''''Darth Vader'' 38''' is the thirty-eighth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]] and [[Federico Blee]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 13]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE SCOURGE COMES FOR THE ''EXECUTOR''! &ndash; A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! As DARTH VADER'S Super Star Destroyer flagship faces its deadliest threat ever, the DARK LORD fights the war on two fronts &ndash; against a horde of SCOURGED DROIDS&hellip;and against the forces of THE EMPIRE itself! Featuring a rare glimpse into the true character of ADMIRAL PIETT!''
