{{Quote|Well, she built him. And he built the rest of us.|Another Elthree droid, on L3-37 and her first droid|<ref name="Last Shot" />}}
A '''droid''' was constructed by [[L3-37]] in order to form the [[Elthree Assault Team]]. Once built, the [[battle droid]] manufactured several replicas of itself, waiting until a virus that L3 had placed in the [[Phylanx Redux Transmitter]] sent out a [[distress signal]]. When the [[droid]] received the signal, he and the rest of the Elthree Assault Team tracked down the Phylanx in the [[Mesulan Remnants Belt]], entering a [[Second skirmish in the Mesulan Remnants Belt|skirmish]] with [[Fyzen Gor]] and his [[Original Dozen]]. The droid managed to survive the skirmish, and along with a second droid, rescued [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] from the destruction of the Phylanx. The two Elthree droids returned Calrissian to the ''[[Chevalier]]'', explaining their purpose to Calrissian and [[Han Solo]]'s team. With their mission accomplished, the first droid planned to wander [[the galaxy]] and fight crime, leaving the [[shuttle]] with the other droid.<ref name="Last Shot" />
