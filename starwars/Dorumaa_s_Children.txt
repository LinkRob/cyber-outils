"'''Dorumaa's Children'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year three of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was a stand-alone adventure and was written by [[Rob Edwards]] and edited by [[Ronald A. Heintz]] and [[Jae Walker]].

==Plot summary==
One of the [[Dorumaan leviathan]]s is stolen by [[Rufus Trammel]], and the heroes have to try to free it.
