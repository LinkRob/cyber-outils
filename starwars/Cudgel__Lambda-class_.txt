The '''''Cudgel''''' was a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] in the complement of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Ultimatum]]''. It was dispatched to the [[Highground]] [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Gorse]] by [[Imperial Navy]] [[Captain]] [[Rae Sloane]] to pick up herself and [[Count]] [[Denetrius Vidian]] after the dissident [[miner]] [[Skelly]] blew up their first [[shuttle]], [[Truncheon (Lambda-class)|''Truncheon'']]. However, the ''Cudgel'' was also blown up by Skelly, immediately after it landed at the spaceport. Sloane was then forced to call for a third shuttle, ''[[Bastinade]]''.<ref name="A New Dawn" />
