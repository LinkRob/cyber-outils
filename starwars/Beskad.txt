A '''''beskad''''' was a traditional<ref name="Collapse" /> [[Mandalorian]] [[sword]],<ref name="BeskadDuelist" /> forged from [[beskar]]. Comprised of a broad, single-edged blade about the length of a grown [[human]]'s [[Limb|arm]]<ref name="Collapse" /> that widened toward the tip before tapering to a point,<ref name="BeskadDuelist" /> the ''beskad'' was a heavy single-handed short sword, designed to crack [[armor]] and capable of delivering short, powerful slashes&mdash;similar to [[machete]].<ref name="Collapse" /> Two metal guards protected the wielder's hand at the top and base of the hilt.<ref name="BeskadDuelist" /> The weapon was based on a design from the ancient [[Taung]]. Traditionally, it was accompanied by the [[kal]] [[dagger]] in the wielder's off hand.<ref name="Collapse" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''beskad'' was first named by author [[Karen Traviss]] in her now-[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[EBook|e-novella]] ''[[Boba Fett: A Practical Man]]''.<ref name="APM">''[[Boba Fett: A Practical Man]]''</ref>
