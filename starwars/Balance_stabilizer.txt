'''Balance stabilizers''' were a type of [[stabilizer]] that could be installed in a [[droid]] to keep the unit steady. Balance stabilizers could be seen in droids from various [[Standard century|centuries]], with units active in the [[High Republic Era]] and late [[Republic Era]] periods both making use of the technology. According to [[B1-series battle droid]] [[B1-0516]] ("Bats"), B1 droids and [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|T-series tactical droids]] could, should they feel fear, quake in their balance stabilizers.

==Description==
[[File:TI-99-Armada.png|thumb|right|290px|B1-series battle droids and T-series tactical droids (both pictured) were equipped with balance stabilizers.]]
Balance stabilizers were a [[stabilizer]] device built into [[droid]]s with the purpose of keeping them steady. Among the droids who used such devices were [[B1-series battle droid]]s and [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]]s. According to the rogue B1 unit [[B1-0516]] ("Bats"), such droids, should they be experiencing fear, could quake in their balance stabilizers.<ref name="Roger Roger" />

Finding work as a [[monster hunter]] and [[Mercenary|saber-for-hire]] during the [[High Republic Era]],<ref name="Saber for Hire 1" /> the former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ty Yorrick]] led a crew consisting of herself, the [[Administrative droid|admin unit]]<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> and [[protocol droid]]<ref name="Characters Of">{{SWYouTube|OkCRhorGlC4|Ty Yorrick &#124; Characters of Star Wars: The High Republic|sw_url=video/ty-yorrick-characters-of-star-wars-the-high-republic}}</ref> [[KL-03]] ("Klo"),<ref name="Saber for Hire 1" /> the [[astromech droid]] [[R0-VR]] ("Rover"),<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> and her [[squire]], [[Drewen Qweebjillan]], out of a [[Rancor Rider|starship]].<ref name="Saber for Hire 1" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="229 Date">The events of [[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 1|''The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire'' 1]] take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> while operating within the [[Nihil]]-controlled [[Occlusion Zone]], Yorrick and her crew came under attack from Nihil [[scav droid]]s for refusing to pay [[hyperspace route]] tolls. Unable to enter [[hyperspace]] directly for their [[Temerancé|next destination]] without changing course, Yorrick rammed into the scav swarm and ordered Klo to electrify their [[YT-750 freighter|vessel]] to wipe out the surviving droids. Reluctant to do so, Klo advised the crew to hold onto their balance stabilizers before activating the defense, allowing them to escape.<ref name="Saber for Hire 1" />

[[File:Adventures19-BalanceStabilizers.png|thumb|left|400px|The rogue B1 unit Bats wrongly believed he had scared Separatist droids to the extent they were quaking in their balance stabilizers.]]
During the late [[Republic Era]], [[Standard century|centuries]] after the [[Standard day|days]] of the High Republic,<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the B1 battle droids and T-series tactical droids of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]'s [[Confederacy military|armed forces]] made use of balance stabilizers during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. During the [[Battle of Horain]]<ref name="Roger Roger" /> around [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the rogue [[battle droid]] B1-0516 ("Bats") resisted his Separatist captors and, believing he had scared his fellow B1 units and a [[Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Horain)|T-series tactical droid]], exclaimed that he had predicted they would be quaking in their balance stabilizers. In reality, the droids were shaking due to vibrations from an approaching [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tank]] manned by [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] and [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who were arriving to rescue Bats and successfully did so.<ref name="Roger Roger" />
