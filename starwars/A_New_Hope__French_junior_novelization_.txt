'''''A New Hope''''' ([[Wikipedia:French language|French]]: '''''Un nouvel espoir''''') is a junior novel adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. ''A New Hope'' was written by [[Catherine Kalengula]] and published by [[Hachette Jeunesse]] on [[August 23]], [[2017]].<ref name="Hachette" />

==Publisher's summary==
''A long time ago, in a galaxy far, far away.&hellip;''<br />
''Luke Skywalker joined the Rebels to fight the Empire, with the help of Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi. Can they save Princess Leia and destroy the Death Star?''<ref name="Hachette" />
