The '''CR-series'''<ref name="BMF 86" /> or '''CR line''',<ref name="TLF" /> was a line of [[corvette]]s from [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]<ref name="BMF 86" /> that included the [[CR56 corvette]],<ref name="TLF" /> the [[CR70 corvette]] and the [[CR90 corvette]]. The [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvette]] was an early predecessor of the much later CR-series.<ref name="BMF 86" />

Because of the CR70 and CR90 corvettes' frequent use as [[diplomat]]ic [[cruiser]]s by the [[House of Organa|Royal House]] of [[Alderaan]], the CR-series also known as '''Alderaanian diplomatic cruisers''',<ref name="C-3PO">{{Databank|c-3po|C-3PO (See-Threepio)}}</ref> '''Alderaanian cruisers''',<ref name="The Rebel Files" /> '''Alderaanian starships''',<ref name="Black Series">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|subset=Centerpiece|pack=#01: Darth Vader|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/Darth-Vader-01-Centerpiece-The-Black-Series.asp}}</ref> or '''Alderaan cruisers'''.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>
