'''7169''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] who served in the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Trade Federation Droid Army|Droid Army]]. In [[32 BBY]], they were part of the forces deployed to the [[city]] of [[Theed]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] by the Federation during its [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] of the world. 7169 was part of a group of [[battle droid]]s led by the [[OOM command battle droid]] [[2163]] that were tasked with guarding the [[Naboo Royal Starship]] in the [[Theed Hangar]].

The [[droid]]s were approached by a group led by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], who was attempting to transport [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] off world using the royal [[starship]]. After 2163 ordered Jinn and his companions be arrested, the [[Jedi]] cut down 7169 and the other droids with his [[Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber|lightsaber]].

==Biography==
7169 was the designation of a [[B1-series battle droid]] manufactured for the [[Trade Federation]]. They served as part of the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]] and was amongst the [[battle droid]]s that the Federation brought with them to blockade the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] over a trade dispute. The blockade then escalated into full scale [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] when 7169 and the rest of the army were deployed to the surface of Naboo, which quickly resulted in the Federation successfully capturing the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Theed]] and the [[Monarch of Naboo]], [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="TPM" />

[[File:Naboo_battle_droids_in_hangar.png||left|thumb|300px|7169 with their fellow units in Theed Hangar]]
Once the city was captured, 7169 was part of a group of [[droid]]s under the command of the [[OOM command battle droid]] [[2163]] that was stationed in the [[Theed Hangar]] to guard the [[Naboo Royal Starship]] and various hangar personnel who had been captured. 7169 was stood next to unit [[1933]] and 2163 by the entry ramp to the [[J-type 327 Nubian royal starship|starship]] when a group of [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]], including the recently liberated Amidala, approached accompanied by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], his [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and the [[Gungan]] [[Jar Jar Binks]].<ref name="TPM" />

Jinn hoped to use the royal [[starship]] to transport the queen and her allies off planet, and introduced himself to 2163 as an ambassador of the [[Galactic Republic]] wishing to take those with him to the planet [[Coruscant]]. 2163 rejected the request and ordered 7169 and the other droids present to arrest Jinn and his companions, causing the Jedi Master to swiftly draw his [[Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and [[Death|destroy]] 2163 and 1933 in one motion, then cutting down 7169 and the other droids next to the ship.<ref name="TPM" />

==Characteristics==
Like all B1 battle droids, 7169 stood 1.93 [[meter]]s tall and had [[Color|black]] [[sensor]]s and tan plating. It carried an [[E-5 blaster rifle]] and was equipped with a [[comlink booster pack]] that had the droid's designation written on it<ref name="TPM" /> in [[Trade Federation Script]].<ref name="Complete Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
7169 first appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. In the film, 7169's designation is not spoken, but the numbers are visible on the droid's backpack.<ref name="TPM" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the droid was additionally identified as [[3B3-1204]] on a card in the [[2001]] "[[Theed Palace Limited]]" expansion for [[Decipher]]'s ''[[Star Wars Customizable Card Game]]''.<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Theed Palace Limited|cardname=3B3-1204}}</ref>
