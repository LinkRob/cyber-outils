==Behind the scenes==
The ''Centennial Chihuahua'' first appeared in ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]''. The ship was a reference to Isabella's pet chihuahua [[w:c:phineasandferb:Pinky the Chihuahua|Pinky]] in the [[Disney Channel]] animated series ''[[w:c:phineasandferb|Phineas and Ferb]]''.<ref name="Phineas" />
