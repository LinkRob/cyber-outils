A '''broadband antenna''' was a type of [[antenna]] that could pick up broadband frequencies. In conjunction with build-in [[comlink]]s, broadband antenna receivers allowed [[EV-series supervisor droid]]s to wirelessly link to local [[computer]]s.<ref name="R2-D2 20">{{BuildR2Cite|20|Droid Directory}}</ref> They were similarly equipped on [[3PO-series protocol droid]]s.<ref name="R2-D2 2">{{BuildR2Cite|2|Droid Directory}}</ref>
