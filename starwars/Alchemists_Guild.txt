{{Quote|Surely you remember my disappointment at receiving no response to my application to join the Alchemists Guild? Now how do you think I reacted when I learned that the Alchemists Guild hasn't actually existed in nearly a millenium?|Cornelius Evazan|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
The '''Alchemists Guild''' was an organization that had been defunct for almost a thousand [[Standard year|years]] by the [[Imperial Era]]. [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]] was not aware of this fact and sent in an application, becoming disappointed when he received no response. He later learned the Guild no longer existed, which surprised him. He recorded this story in an entry in the logbook known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' while it was in his possession.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
