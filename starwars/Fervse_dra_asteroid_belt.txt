The '''Fervse'dra asteroid belt'''<ref name="BMF 62">{{BuildFalconCite|62|Starship Fact File}}</ref> was located within [[Bothan Space]] of the regions known as the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> Located around the [[planet]] [[Bothawui]], the [[Asteroid field|asteroid belt]] was situated in grid square R-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Grid" /> and retained several passable zones, such as [[Zone six|zones six]], [[Zone five|five]], [[Zone four|four]], and [[Zone three|three]].<ref name="Downfall" />

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|106}}</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], the [[asteroid]] belt served as the site of an [[Battle of Bothawui|engagement]] a Republic [[Skywalker's fleet|task force]] led by [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and a Separatist [[fleet]] [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Kaleesh]] Separatist [[General]] [[Grievous]]. Following the battle, which led to Grievous's defeat, the remains of his fleet were left floating adrift throughout the asteroid belt, which was later scavenged by [[Trandoshan]] [[scavenger]], [[Gha Nachkt]].<ref name="Downfall" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fervse'dra asteroid belt was introduced, although only in the episode's opening newsreel, in "[[Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence'']],"<ref name="Rising">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> the second episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which aired on [[October 3]], [[2008]].<ref name="Rising Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode002.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> It later made its full appearance in "[[Downfall of a Droid]],"<ref name="Downfall" /> the sixth episode the series' first season, which aired on [[November 7]], 2008.<ref name="DoaD Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode006.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Downfall of a Droid}}</ref> In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the asteroid belt was identified in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 62|sixty-second issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 62" /> published around [[March 6]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdates">{{BMFdates|62}}</ref>
