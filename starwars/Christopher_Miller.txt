'''Christopher Miller''' (born [[September 23]], [[1975]]) is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] film producer, director, and writer who is an executive producer for ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. Miller and industry partner [[Phil Lord]] were the original directors of the film,<ref name="Directors">{{SW|url=news/a-message-from-lucasfilm-regarding-the-untitled-han-solo-film|text=A Message From Lucasfilm Regarding the Untitled Han Solo Film}}</ref> but were replaced by [[Ron Howard]].<ref name="Ron Howard">{{SW|url=news/ron-howard-to-assume-directorial-duties-on-the-untitled-han-solo-film|text=Ron Howard to Assume Directorial Duties on the Untitled Han Solo Film}}</ref>

Miller was an intern at [[Lucasfilm]] during production of ''[[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition]]'' and appeared as a [[stormtrooper]] in the Special Edition of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']].

In [[2018]], in response to if the concept of the [[Cloud-Riders]] from ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' originated from the [[1977]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] comic, ''Star Wars 9: Showdown on a Wasteland World!'', or if it was just coincidental, [[Jon Kasdan]], a writer of the film, replied on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that it was no coincidence, as when former directors Miller and [[Phil Lord]] settled on [[swoop]] bikes for the group, Kasdan found the ''Star Wars'' Legends [[Cloud-Riders/Legends|Cloud-Riders]] when browsing through [[Wookieepedia]], and the name stuck.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|jonkasdan|status/995504590337466368|Jon Kasdan|quote=Not a coincidence at all. In the earliest drafts of the script Enfys Marauders attacked the Conveyex on Bantha II skiffs. When Chris & Phil decided they wanted to do swoop bikes instead, we found the Cloud Riders on Wookieepedia, from above Marvel comic, and it STUCK.|archivefile=File:Jon Kasdan used Wookieepedia.png}}</ref>
