'''BK-298''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Sleeper Agent|sleeper operative]]. [[Hypnosis/Canon|Hypnotic]] suggestions ensured she believed herself to be a loyal [[stormtrooper]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] instead of a Rebel [[spy]].<ref name="BK-298" />

==Behind the scenes==
