"'''Fifth and Final'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]] title "'''Die Fünfte und Letzte'''") is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Jeremy Barlow]], drawn by [[Bob Molesworth]] and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. The story was published in the 37th issue of the [[Wikipedia:Germany|German]] [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]], which was released on [[October 25]], [[2017]].

==Plot summary==
[[Jinko]] was adopted by the wealthy [[Bolin Volfrham|Bolin]] and [[Willa Volfrham]] as a young child and raised by them until he was replaced by another cute child when it became convenient for Bolin and Willia. Jinko was kidnapped, taken to an uninhabited [[planet]] in the Volfrham's [[Personal Luxury Yacht 3000|yacht]] and launched towards the surface in an [[escape pod]]. Bolin repeated the feat with others he sought to get rid of, including the accountant [[Crez Lorell]] and his wife Willa. The planet's atmosphere blocked all electronic communication so the three, who all disliked each other, settled around a single hill. A fourth escape pod crashed on the planet bringing the [[E-XD-series infiltrator droid]] [[D-34-KI]].  However, after the droid's arrival, the pods stopped coming for a long period until the arrival of a fifth, bearing [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]].

As Jinko watches the arrival of the fifth escape pod, Lorell, who is cooking, throws a stone at Jinko and orders him to get the chair ready. Calrissian, being escorted by Willa Volfrham and her pet [[lizard]] is ordered to sit down in the chair (which is made from the remains of D-34-KI) while Jinko ties him to it. As Jinko restrains Lando, the lizard lunges toward Lorell's cooking pot. Lorell retreats and demands that Volfhram control the lizard. Meanwhile, Calrissian uses the distraction to try and negotiate with Jinko. Jinko questions Calrissian about the location of his escape pod, revealing that he is close to rebuilding his own but only needs a [[repulsor coil]] to complete it. He promises to take Calrissian with him if it works. Calrissian agrees to Jinko's terms and Jinko also reveals he left Calrissian's bond slack so he could escape if he wanted to. Jinko takes off into the forest while Volfrham, who had just noticed the conversation between Calrissian and Jinko, demands to know its topics, while brandishing a knife at Calrissian.

Calrissian recognizes Volfrham and who explains that D-34-KI had been eliminated by the group, believing that Bolin Volfrham had sent it to kill them. Willa demands to know why Calrissian was there and he explains to Willa and Lorell that he had been playing cards with Bolin and having just won, then woke up in an escape pod. He asks the two if they have a [[distress beacon]], but the interrogation is interrupted by the rumble of Jinko's escape pod activating. Calrissian, realizing that Jinko intended to leave him behind, breaks free of his bonds, climbs the hill to where the escape pod is located and just before it takes off, opens the hatch, throws Jinko aside and climbs into the pod by himself. The craft takes off, leaving the three marooned on the planet.

Jinko sulks as his best chance to get off the planet was taken away but spots the craft returning.  He arrives at the landing site to see Calrissian exit the craft. Calrissian reveals to the others that he had traveled outside the planet's atmosphere and sent a [[distress signal]] and that his friends were on their way to rescue them.
