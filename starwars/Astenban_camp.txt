The '''Astenban camp''' was a base located on the [[moon]] [[Astenban]].<ref name="Edge of Balance 3" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Savina Besatrix Malagán]] predicted that the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] would attempt to take control of the base, and decided to undergo a [[Mission to Astenban|mission]] there to deny it to them. Shortly after Malagán arrived on Astenban, the Nihil also arrived and took control of the base. She then successfully destroyed the base using explosives.<ref name="Edge of Balance 3" />
