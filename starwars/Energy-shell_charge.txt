The '''energy-shell charge'''<ref name="X-Wing">{{FFGXW2|set=Servants of Strife Squadron Pack|cardname=Energy-Shell Charges|reprint=1}}</ref> or '''energy torpedo''', was a type of weapon used by the [[Trade Federation]] and later the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. [[Vulture-class starfighter|Vulture droid starfighters]] were equipped with torpedo launchers that could fire energy-shell charges.<ref name="SWE vulture droid">{{SWE|technology|vulturedroid|vulture droid}}</ref>
