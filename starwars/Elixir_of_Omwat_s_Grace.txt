By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The entry in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' mentioning the Elixir of Omwat's Grace is placed chronologically prior to an entry written by the criminal [[Tobias Beckett]]. ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' depicts Beckett's [[death]] on the [[planet]] [[Savareen]], and ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on Savareen in the film to nine [[Standard year|years]] after the [[proclamation of the New Order]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the proclamation to [[19 BBY]], Beckett's death must occur in [[10 BBY]], and so his entry in the guide and those chronologically before it must all be set prior to or during 10 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> an '''Elixir of Omwat's Grace''' was part of the plunder seized from the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] when he was captured by the [[Blazing Claw]] and their leader, [[Gunda Mabin]]. The elixir was included in an inventory of the assets taken that was included in the [[book]] that would become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smugglers Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> [[Omwat system|Omwat]] was an astronomical object located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Elixir of Omwat's Grace was mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and first published in [[2018]].<ref name="Smugglers Guide" />
