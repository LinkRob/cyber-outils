The '''Emperor's throne room''' was a [[throne]] room of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] that was located in a [[Emperor's tower|tower]] on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. The tower and the throne room were located in the [[Northern Polar Command Sector]], and were both<ref name="Dawn" /> destroyed during the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Episode IV" />
