'''Anti''' was a [[Color|fair]]-skinned [[humanoid]] [[bounty hunter]] affiliated with the [[Haxion Brood]] crime syndicate during the [[Imperial Era]]. They wore white, blue, and grey [[armor]] as well as white [[boot]]s, a brown [[belt]], and a tan [[Clothing|shirt]] and pants. Anti utilized a [[jetpack]], a [[flamethrower]] and [[missile launcher]] from the armor's gauntlets, and a [[blaster rifle]]. The humanoid had several [[cybernetics|cybernetic]] enhancements, including a right [[mechno-arm]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFO Date" /> Anti fought the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]]. However, Kestis prevailed and [[Death|killed]] Anti.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Anti appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', published by [[Respawn Entertainment]]. In the game, after the player attempts to return to their [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'', after visiting the [[Zeffonian]] [[Tomb of Miktrull]] on the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]], the player may be attacked and captured by any of the Haxion Brood bounty hunters, including Anti. After Kestis wakes up in a cell on the Haxion Brood homebase of [[Ordo Eris]], if the bounty hunter to capture him was Anti, the two will fight within the [[Haxion Fight Pit]] where Kestis will kill Anti. If Anti is not the [[bounty hunter]] to capture Kestis, the player may encounter the hunter in a variety of locations after the Ordo Eris chapter of the story. The player may choose to kill Anti and scan them for the "Haxion Brood Bounty Hunter" tactical guide entry if not already previously obtained.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> In the [[2026]] [[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, Vol. 2|manga adaptation]], the hunter who captures Cal Kestis is [[Atticus Rex]].<ref name="Manga">[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, Vol. 2|''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'', Vol. 2]]</ref>
