'''Bognar''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Bith/Legends|Bith]] who lived during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="SWG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bognar appears in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']], a [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment and published by [[LucasArts]],<ref name="SWG" /> prior to its closure on [[December 15]], [[2011]].<ref name="Shutdown">{{SonyCite|url=playerinfo|text=IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT ''STAR WARS'' GALAXIES™}}</ref> According to these files, introduced with the [[2005]] "[[W:c:swg:New Game Enhancements|New Game Enhancements]]," Bognar would have been present on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] and in some way involved with the Mos Eisley part of the "[[W:c:swg:Legacy Quest|Legacy Quest]]." However, he is not known to have appeared on the game's live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]]. Nevertheless, an emulated depiction pulled from this character's Interchange File Format reveals what he would have looked like if he were added to the game.<ref name="SWG" />
