In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''bombing at Gus Talon University''' was carried out by Nihil member [[Sabata Krill]]. This led to all of the Jedi from the [[Coronet City temple]] being deployed to [[Gus Talon]]. The bombing was meant as a distraction from the Nihil's upcoming [[Battle of the Corellian Shipyards|attack on Corellia's shipyards]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
