'''Star Wars Celebration Japan''' of [[2025]] was the sixteenth [[Celebration]] convention. It was held at the [[Wikipedia:Makuhari Messe|Makuhari-Messe Convention Center]] in [[Wikipedia:Chiba (city)|Chiba]], [[Wikipedia:Greater Tokyo Area|Greater Tokyo]] from [[April 18]]&ndash;[[April 20|20]], [[2025]].<ref name="SW.com Japan" /> The event was announced on [[February 21]], [[2023]],<ref name="SW.com Announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-reasons-to-go|text=5 Reasons to Go to ''Star Wars'' Celebration for the First Time}}</ref> while the location and date were announced on [[April 10]].<ref name="SW.com Japan" />
