'''Bet'nih''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Gender|female]] [[Chiss]] who served as an [[Sky-walker|ozyly-esehembo]] within the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> During the [[Nikardun campaigns]], she served as the navigator for the [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Grayshrike]]'' under the command of [[Senior Captain]] [[Irizi'in'daro]].

While on the ''Grayshrike'', Bet'nih was assigned the [[caregiver]] [[Soomret]]. At one point during their travels, Lakinda tried to bribe Bet'nih into navigating faster by giving her a [[Unidentified cheese|dark cheese]] favored by both Chiss, but Bet'nih navigated no faster after eating the cheese.<ref name="Greater Good" />
