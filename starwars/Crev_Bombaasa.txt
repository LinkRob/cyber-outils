'''Crev Bombaasa''' was a well-cultivated [[human]] who worked as a shipping executive on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. He bribed local [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] to stop them from investigating violent attacks against his business rivals, but he faced greater scrutiny under the [[Galactic Empire]]. That prompted him to travel to [[Coronet City Spaceport|Coronet Spaceport]] in order to leave Corellia in search of a new base of operations.

While in the [[spaceport]], he witnessed another traveler being arrested, which allowed the criminals [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]] to cut in line behind Bombaasa. The executive then continued to move forward in the queue without incident.

==Biography==
Crev Bombaasa was a [[human]] who worked as a shipping executive on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. He paid enormous amounts to the local [[Law enforcement agency|police]] in order to ensure that they did not investigate violent strikes against his business rivals. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], his work became more difficult under Imperial scrutiny, so<ref name="TOG" /> in [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Corellia]] events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[13 BBY]].</ref> he traveled to [[Coronet City Spaceport|Coronet Spaceport]] in order to leave Corellia and begin scouting the [[Outer Rim Territories]] for a new location to set up his base of operations.<ref name="TOG" />

[[File:Crev_Bombassa_Spaceport.png|thumb|right|300px|Crev Bombaasa witnessed the arrest of another traveler.]]
While in the [[spaceport]], Bombaasa stood in line waiting to pass through the Imperial checkpoint while Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s dragged away another human in the line behind him. Bombaasa turned briefly to watch the commotion before returning his attention to the front of the line as the criminals [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]] used the confusion to cut into the space in the line behind him.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

The line then proceeded forward without further incident until Bombaasa had been called up to the booth, after which the checkpoint was locked down when Qi'ra and Han attempted to bribe their way through. Travelers who had already passed through the checkpoint were prevented from leaving.<ref name="Solo" />

==Personality and traits==
Crev Bombaasa was well-educated and had a refined taste in the arts. He had fair skin, [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s, and brown [[hair]].<ref name="TOG" />

==Equipment==
While in the spaceport, Bombaasa wore a gray [[Clothing|robe]] with a yellow collar under a black [[gaberwool]] [[Cape|overcloak]] with a pair of black [[boot]]s. He carried a [[cane]] containing a location [[Transponder beacon|transponder]] and wore an elite-status transit tag.<ref name="TOG" />
