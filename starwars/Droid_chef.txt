'''Droid chefs'''<ref name="Art of Outlaws">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]''</ref> was a type of [[cooking droid]] found in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> droid chef operated [[Foodstuff|street food]] stalls on the [[planet]]s [[Akiva]], [[Kijimi]], and [[Tatooine]], as well as the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]; serving [[Kurado fruit platter]], [[Duradan Soup]], [[Sketto Chuga]] and [[Che Mosskà]] respectively.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The droid chefs<ref name="Art of Outlaws" /> appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game they were part of the galactic street food optional activity though specific details about the model of droid were not supplied within the video game.<ref name="Outlaws" /> The droids were later identified as droid chefs in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]'' published by [[Dark Horse Books]].<ref name="Art of Outlaws" />
