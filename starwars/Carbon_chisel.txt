A '''carbon chisel''' was a tool used for removing [[carbon scoring]] from objects. [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], a [[desert]] [[scavenger]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]], used a carbon chisel for removing scorching on her salvages<ref name="RSG" /> around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> If the chisel proved not to be enough, she would use a [[vibro-model]] for tougher jobs.<ref name="RSG" />
