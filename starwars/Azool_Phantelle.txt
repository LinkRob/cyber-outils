'''Azool Phantelle''' was an [[Ortolan]] criminal and the [[Gender|brother]] of the [[musician]] [[Maximilian Rebo]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Phantelle once [[Thief|swindled]] several of the inhabitants of the [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. After the victims mistook the musician for his thieving brother, Phantelle had to rescue Rebo, who threw the criminal's stolen [[Currency|money]] back into the crowd chasing them. The [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] later learned of Phantelle's crimes, which had taken place within his [[Jabba's criminal empire|territory]], and punished Phantelle by forcing him to serve drinks aboard the Hutt's [[LO-KD57|sail barge]], the ''[[Khetanna]]''.

==Biography==
===Brothers reunited===
{{Quote|Azool?! What are you doing here?''"<br />"''Just having some fun, brother! Hoo hoo!|Max Rebo and Azool Phantelle|<ref name="Blue Brothers" />}}
Azool Phantelle was an [[Ortolan]] who worked as a [[thief]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. He was the [[Gender|brother]] of the [[musician]]<ref name="Blue Brothers" /> [[Maximilian Rebo]], leader of the [[Max Rebo Band]]. Rebo had a [[Maximilian Rebo's mother|mother]] and his father was the musician [[Jeph Rebo]]. Several [[Standard year|years]] before<ref name="Fancy Man" /> [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Fancy Man Date">The events of the flashback in "[[Fancy Man]]" occur several [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Rescue of Han Solo|destruction]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] [[Khetanna|sail barge]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> Rebo was glad that the wayward Phantelle laid no claim to the [[Rebo family|family's]] [[Rebo family home|home]] in [[Cheddatown]] on the [[planet]] [[Orto]].<ref name="Fancy Man" />

[[File:Azool_and_Max.png|thumb|left|300px|Azool Phantelle was the brother of musician Max Rebo.]]
After Phantelle swindled several of the inhabitants of the [[spaceport]] [[Mos Eisley]] on the planet [[Tatooine]], the victims mistook Rebo for Phantelle and began chasing the musician. Rebo then collided with two Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s while fleeing toward [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]]. Before the [[trooper]]s could punish him for the collision, Phantelle spotted Rebo while passing on a [[speeder bike]] and pulled his brother out of harm's way and onto the bike.<ref name="Blue Brothers" />

As the pair sped away from the angry stormtroopers and locals, Rebo admonished Phantelle for his life of crime and fretted that the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], who employed Rebo, would punish both brothers for Phantelle's crimes, as they had taken place within the crime [[lord]]'s [[Jabba's criminal empire|territory]]. Unconcerned, Phantelle avoided a [[missile]] fired by one of their pursuers, after which Rebo stopped the chase by grabbing Phantelle's bag of stolen [[Currency|funds]] and throwing the contents out to those chasing them.<ref name="Blue Brothers" />

===Crime never pays===
{{Quote|Perhaps this will teach you some respect, Azool! Ho! Ho! Ho! Ho!|translated=1|Jabba laughs at Azool Phantelle's punishment|<ref name="Blue Brothers" />}}
[[File:Azool_the_waiter.png|thumb|right|250px|Jabba punished Azool Phantelle by making him serve drinks aboard the Hutt's sail barge.]]
Upset by the loss of his ill-gotten goods, Phantelle stopped to watch the locals fighting over the scattered currency. As the brothers stood watching, Jabba and his entourage confronted the pair and demanded to know what was going on. Rebo informed the crime lord of what had happened, after which Jabba angrily threatened Phantelle. As the criminal was related to Rebo, Jabba chose to spare him and punished him by forcing him to work as a waiter aboard his personal [[LO-KD57|sail barge]],<ref name="Blue Brothers" /> the ''[[Khetanna]]''.<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>

Sometime later, the [[cartographer]] [[Emil Graf]] was reminded of Phantelle and Rebo's case of mistaken identity when he was falsely accused of stealing a pair of [[laser-caliper]]s from his [[BB-series astromech droid]], [[BB-00]], when the true culprit was the [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]] [[Noni]]. Graf then told the story to BB-00, Noni, and the [[droid]] [[CR-8R]].<ref name="Blue Brothers" /> During the era of the [[First Order]],<ref name="Adventures30">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=30|story=The Lost Stories, Part 1}}</ref> Graf imagined himself riding with Phantelle on the Ortolan's [[speeder]] bike while experiencing a stream of disjointed memories that were being fed upon by a [[Unidentified Tarinna|Tarinna]] on the planet [[Nelgenam]].<ref name="Adventures32">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=32|story=The Lost Stories, Part 3}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Haha! You haven't changed Max, still afraid of letting go.''"<br />"''You haven't changed either, Azool! I see you've been swindling the locals again.|Azool Phantelle and Max Rebo|<ref name="Blue Brothers" />}}
[[File:Azool_and_Jabba.png|thumb|right|200px|Jabba intimidated Azool Phantelle.]]
Azool Phantelle considered swindling civilians to be fun and believed his brother, Max Rebo, was too afraid of letting go. When Rebo hurled Phantelle's stolen currency from the back of the speeder bike they were riding to distract their pursuers, Phantelle was upset by the loss of wealth. He was unconcerned about the risk of Jabba catching him stealing on the Hutt's territory, but when he was actually confronted by the crime lord, Phantelle was intimidated and submitted to a humiliating punishment. Phantelle had [[Color|blue]] skin and black [[eye]]s.<ref name="Blue Brothers" /> He strongly resembled his brother.<ref name="Lando">{{EncyclopediaCite|Lando Calrissian and Other Characters From the Galaxy}}</ref>

==Equipment==
Phantelle wore a black [[Clothing|jacket]] and rode a speeder bike. While being forced to work on the ''Khetanna'', he wore a drinks tray on top of his head.<ref name="Blue Brothers" />

==Behind the scenes==
Azool Phantelle first appeared in "[[Tales from Wild Space: The Blue Brothers]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written and illustrated by [[Nick Brokenshire]] and published in the [[2018]] comic book [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 13|''Star Wars Adventures'' (2017) 13]].<ref name="Blue Brothers" />
