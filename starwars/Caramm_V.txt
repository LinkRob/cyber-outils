'''Caramm V''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> in the [[Caramm system]] within the [[Talcene sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square R-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

The world was ruled by the silken-tongued individual [[Ado Eemon]] and occupied the fifth [[orbit]]al position in its [[star system]]. Around the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], in which Eemon allied with the Confederacy, the planet had become ravaged as its ruler deployed a personal [[fleet]] of [[HMP droid gunship|gunships]] to eliminate threats to his power.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
Caramm V was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']].<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Caramm V/Legends|Caramm V]] was introduced in ''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', a [[2005]] reference book authored by [[Curtis Saxton]].<ref name="Incredible Cross-Sections">''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
