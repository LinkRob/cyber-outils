The '''Farlingan Collapse''' was an event that took place on the [[planet]] [[Seswenna]] during the [[High Republic Era]], in which five [[Jedi]] were trapped in the [[mines of Seswenna]]. While four of the Jedi were [[Death|killed]] instantly by rubble, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Indara]] survived and was rescued a [[Standard week|week]] later.<ref name="Wayseeker" />
