'''Aber''' was a [[doctor]] who was a member of a [[Unidentified Escalan species|species]] on<ref name="Han Chewie 6" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Han Chewie 7">[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 7|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 7]]</ref> [[Escalan]].<ref name="Han Chewie 6" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] was dumped on Escalan after he was shot by the [[Rodian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Greedo]],<ref name="Han Chewie 7" /> two younger members of Aber's [[species]] brought Solo to Aber. Although she did not have knowledge that she needed of [[human]] anatomy, she did her best to save him as he was very close to [[death]]. A [[Standard week|week]] later, Solo awoke, informing the doctor of his name, though she did not understand and considered his words to be strange. Aber requested the presence of informed [[Elder (Escalan)|Elder]] [[Sy'Son]], who arrived with another individual. As she led them to Solo's body, she informed the pair of Solo's awakening and what he had said.<ref name="Han Chewie 6" />
