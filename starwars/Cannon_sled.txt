The Rebel '''cannon sled'''<ref name="Surface Vehicles">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Surface Vehicle in Star Wars Movies|video=k7dVRY2QRV0|nolive=1}}</ref> was a ground vehicle used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Episode V" />

==History==
[[File:EchoBaseHangar-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|200px|An ice-cutter (left) in the hangar of Echo Base.]]
During the [[Battle of Hoth]] between the Rebel Alliance and the [[Galactic Empire]], several cannon sleds were present, with at least one on the front lines and another in [[Hangar 7]] in [[Echo Base]].<ref name="Episode V" />

Some of the craft had [[laser-ice cutter]]s mounted to them and were used by the [[Rebel Alliance Corps of Engineers]], alongside excavator machinery, to carve out space inside the caves of [[Echo Base]].<ref name="CL 2016" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:CannonSled-TMOTESB.jpg|250px|thumb|Cannon sled prop on the set of ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]
The vehicle appeared, unnamed, in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="Episode V" /> The original blueprints for the prop show its production name was "cannon sled".<ref name="The Blueprints">''[[Star Wars: The Blueprints]]''</ref> The [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] identified the laser-ice cutter on top of the vehicle,<ref name="CL 2016" /> and the vehicle itself was officially named in a [[Star Wars By the Numbers]] video.<ref name="Surface Vehicles" />

The ''[[Star Wars: The Blueprints]]'' book indicates the vehicle was mounted on a sled towed by an [[Wikipedia:Snow Trac|Aktiv Fischer Snow Trac]] vehicle.<ref name="The Blueprints" /> On set footage of the vehicle was included in the rare documentary by [[Michel Parbot]], ''[[The Making of "The Empire Strikes Back"]]''.<ref name="Parbot">{{YouTube|video=YeB-uXGGaEU|text=The "Lost" Empire Strikes Back Documentary by Michel Parbot (1980)|channel=channel/UCrGFZ8Ke27YWT8mfy2i0_NQ|name=Adywan revisited}}</ref> There were a number of publicity photos of [[Luke Skywalker]] manning the turret on the vehicle which, according to [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] executive [[Pablo Hidalgo]], were not deleted scenes but rather on-set photos taken with the prop.<ref name="Pablo tweet">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1432779261304258563|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=You may occasionally find photos of Luke in his snowspeeder pilot manning the guns online labeled as a 'deleted scene'. It's not. It's more like a photo opportunity, as this prop / vehicle was on set that day|archivedate=1432779261304258563}}</ref>
