"'''Boba Fett - The Bounty Hunter'''" is the thirty-third episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com]] website and YouTube on [[June 21]], [[2019]].

==Plot summary==
On the bridge of the ''[[Executor]]'', the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Admiral]] [[Firmus Piett]] reacts negatively to the presence of several [[bounty hunter]]s including [[Dengar]], [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], [[Boba Fett]], [[4-LOM]], and [[Zuckuss]]. [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] tasks the five bounty hunters with hunting down the crew of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.

The narrator explains that in a [[The galaxy|"galaxy of adventures"]], Boba Fett always gets his bounty. There is a a scene of Fett firing his blaster and capturing a green [[Twi'lek]] fugitive before jetting off in his [[jetpack]]. The video then flashes forward to Fett wrapping his [[whipcord launcher]] around [[Luke Skywalker]] at the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]]. In another scene, Fett flies the ''[[Slave I]]'' through [[hyperspace]] and then engages in a space battle.

The video then flashes forward to Fett flying the ''Slave I'' to [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] with [[Han Solo]] [[Carbon-freezing|frozen]] in [[carbonite]]. Jabba and his entourage including [[Salacious B. Crumb]] and [[Bib Fortuna]] laugh mockingly at Solo. Fett then jets off into a battle where he hurls several grenades, resulting in explosions.

Returning to the ''Executor'', Vader reminds Fett that he wants them alive, emphasizing "no disintegrations." Fett complies with Vader's orders. The video then flashes forward to Fett's last stand at the Great Pit of Carkoon.
