'''Arquebus''' were [[Slugthrower pistol]]s that were favored amongst the [[Vurk]]s. The [[pistol]] was used for long distance targets and cost 1,200 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] per unit.<ref name="Unlimited Power" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arquebus were first mentioned in ''[[Unlimited Power]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Force and Destiny|''Force and Destiny'']] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' module that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2018]].<ref name="Unlimited Power" />
