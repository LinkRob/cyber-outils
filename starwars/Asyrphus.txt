'''Asyrphus''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="BTC" /> located in the [[Perkell sector]]'s [[Asyrphus system]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]], situated at the coordinates Q-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] were deployed there<ref name="BTC" /> at some point between [[1 BBY]] and [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate|the mentioned of the [[massacre at Asyrphus]]}} In the novel, [[Hazram Namir]], who was present for the massacre, joined the [[61st Mobile Infantry]] shortly after the [[Battle of the Tower]], which occurs nineteen years after the [[Clone Wars]] in the novel, and before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Since ''Star Wars: Timelines'' places the end of the war in [[19 BBY]], the massacre at Asyrphus must have occurred sometime between [[1 BBY]] and 3 ABY.</ref> On Asyrphus, the [[company]] faced a bombardment which [[Massacre at Asyrphus|massacred]] many of its [[Rebel trooper|troopers]], including a [[Unidentified comm tech|comm tech]] and her [[droid]]. [[Hazram Namir]], a [[soldier]] in the 61st who knew the [[technician]], remembered the massacre on Asyrphus in his mind when recalling her<ref name="BTC" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> and later that [[Standard year|year]] when he compared the losses the 61st took on Asyrphus to the company's recent losses in its command structure.<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Asyrphus was mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
