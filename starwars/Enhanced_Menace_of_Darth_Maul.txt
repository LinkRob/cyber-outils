'''''Enhanced Menace of Darth Maul''''' is an expansion card set of the ''[[Young Jedi Collectible Card Game]]'', produced by [[Decipher]] and released on [[August 2]], [[2000]]. The set is based on the original [[Menace of Darth Maul]] set. This set introduced a new "wild card" mechanic, wherein you have a "white deckbuilding" dot that could replace one colored deckbuilding dot. You could only have 1 white dot per color, and limited to 6 wild cards in a 60-card deck. This set contained wild cards from various scenes in the movie, and the packs contained boosters from the [[Menace of Darth Maul]] set.
