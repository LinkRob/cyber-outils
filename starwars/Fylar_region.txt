The '''Fylar region''', also simply known as '''Fylar''', was one of the regions on the [[planet]] [[Ontotho]], inhabited by the [[Fylari]]. When an [[Ontothon government]] was formed so that the planet could join the [[Galactic Republic]], the Fylar region was the only region that refused to unite.<ref name="Dark Temple 1" />
