{{Quote|Senator Bonteri heads a committee looking into educational reform in the poorer areas of the Core planets. This bill was stalled because no one on the Core planets wanted to admit they had poorer areas.|[[Dormé]], to Padmé Amidala|<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />}}
A '''committee''' in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Galactic Senate]] dealt with educational reform in the poorer areas of the [[planet]]s of the [[Core Worlds]]. It was headed by [[Senator]] [[Mina Bonteri]] of [[Onderon]]. The bill was stalled, however, because no one on the Core planets wanted to admit that there were poor areas on their worlds. Sometime later when visiting Bonteri, [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] asked to listen to her plans for educational reform, which Bonteri agreed to. The pair then spent thirty [[Standard minute|minutes]] imagining how they'd rework the system if they had unlimited [[time]], [[Currency|money]] and personnel, before they discussed what Bonteri could do with the resources she actually had.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />
