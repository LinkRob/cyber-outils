The '''Botajef Defense Force''' was the [[Planetary defense forces|planetary defense force]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Botajef]]. It was made up of seven [[squadron]]s of [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s as well as two [[CR90 corvette]]s. It was mobilized against the forces of the [[Galactic Empire]] when [[Governor]] [[Quesl]] declared the planet's secession, however the [[Battle for Botajef|rebellion]] was brought to an end after [[Commodore]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] revealed that the entire situation was merely a ruse to facilitate the theft of a collection of extremely valuable [[Jefi]] artwork and Quesl was taken into custody.<ref name="Thrawn" />
