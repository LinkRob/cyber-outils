The '''Concession Stand''' was an awning-roofed blockhouse found within [[Niima Outpost]]. One of only a few semi-permanent structures within the village, it was from here that the [[junk boss]] [[Unkar Plutt]] ran his operation, in which, he offered up small amounts of [[Foodstuff|food]] in exchange for valuable scrap.<ref name="awakensvisual" /> The stand was heavily armored, made from a converted [[Cargo crawler/Canon|cargo crawler]]. It also featured a security system to stop desperate scavengers from stealing.<ref name="True Love">{{StoryCite|story=True Love|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref>
