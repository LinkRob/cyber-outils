'''Bog Bay''' was a collection of landing pads located in the [[Toydaria]]n [[city]] of [[Riar]]. The pads were owned by the [[Gorensla]] [[kajidic]] and were built just above the [[water]] of a nearby [[swamp]].<ref name="GoD" />

Bog Bay's administrator was a [[Toydarian]] [[Gender|female]] named [[Nel Henol]]. Docking. a ship for the day had a sticker price of 200 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], but haggling could bring it down to about 100.<ref name="GoD" />
