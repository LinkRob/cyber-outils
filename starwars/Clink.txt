'''Clink''' was an [[LE-series droid]] owned by a [[Unidentified Gran trader|Gran trader]] on [[Pantora]]. Along with the [[astromech droid]]s [[225]] and [[DK-3]], Clink was supervised by the [[protocol droid]] [[CG-67]].<ref name="Cornered" /> The small [[droid]] did not suffer fools.<ref name="Databank" />

Shortly after the end of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Echo]]&mdash;having encountered Clink and the other [[droid]]s after being sold to a [[Unidentified Gran trader|trader]] by his comrade [[Hunter]] after the [[Merchant|trader]] believed a disguised Echo was a droid himself&mdash;removed the droids' [[restraining bolt]]s, tasking them with the repair of their [[starship]], [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Marauder'']]. Though Clink attempted to take charge, CG-67 asserted her authority. When the ''Marauder'' was repaired, Echo thanked Clink and the other droids, returning them to CG-67's care.<ref name="Cornered" />
