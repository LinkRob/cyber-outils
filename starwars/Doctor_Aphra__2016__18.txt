'''''Doctor Aphra'' 18''' is the eighteenth issue of [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The comic was written by [[Simon Spurrier]] and illustrated by [[Emilio Laiso]], and it was published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[March 28]], [[2018]]. The issue contains the story "'''Remastered Part V'''," in which [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] [[Infiltration of Hivebase-1|infiltrates]] the [[Tarkin Initiative]] [[space station]] [[Hivebase-1]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Doctor Aphra is being blackmailed by Triple-Zero to run missions for his underground crime syndicate &mdash; but that doesn't mean she can't cause trouble and have fun along the way! Sometimes that fun means infiltrating Rebellion training grounds&hellip;and sometimes that trouble means crossing paths with famed rebel Hera Syndulla herself!''<ref name="Marvel" />

==Plot summary==
As they travel to [[Hivebase-1]], [[Chelli Lona Aphra]]'s crew discusses the grim work of Doctor [[Cornelius Evazan]], the "techflesh artist" responsible for one crewmate's cybernetics and the creation of the mutilated [[Decraniated]]. Upon arrival, they use the captured [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] to feign a trade with the [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]]. Hera warns the crew that following Aphra will only lead to ruin, but Aphra reveals her true plan: she intentionally activated Hera's hidden tracker, luring a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] ship into a firefight with Imperial [[TIE series|TIEs]]. Using the ensuing space battle as a distraction, she lands their ship inside the base.

They enter a chilling bay filled with the frozen rejects of the [[Tarkin Initiative]]—grotesque fusions of flesh and technology. Syndulla identifies it as an archive for experiments too costly to mass-produce but too successful to destroy. While Aphra marvels at the "science" of a [[Wookiee]] with a railgun for a head, Hera is horrified by the experimental subjects.

Meanwhile, aboard the attacking Rebel ship, Imperial Inspector [[Magna Tolvan]] seizes command. [[Commander]] [[Yewl]], the commander of Hivebase-1, orders her to initiate the vessel's self-destruct sequence to eliminate the Rebel rookies. Aphra contacts Tolvan, expressing shock that she would commit such a murder, revealing she only cares about Tolvan's safety. Citing an obscure Imperial doctrine, Tolvan cleverly defies the order, choosing instead to capture the ship while also alerting Yewl to Aphra's infiltration of the base. In response, Yewl orders the rejects to be defrosted. As the monstrous experiments stir to life, Aphra's crew is thrown into chaos when [[Tam Posla]], the lawman, refuses to attack them, viewing them as victims. The rejects advance, revealing their orders: capture General Syndulla and kill everyone else.
