A '''44 scoop''' was a tactic used by the [[Spectres]] [[Phoenix Cell|rebel]] cell which involved a [[starship]] flying by a designated area to allow individuals to jump onto it and be recovered.<ref name="Steps" /> In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> after the Spectres [[Rescue of Hondo Ohnaka|freed]] the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] from an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Naraka prison|prison]] on the [[planet]] [[Naraka]], the mission leader, [[Ezra Bridger]], contacted [[Hera Syndulla]], who was [[pilot]]ing their [[VCX-100 light freighter|ship]], the ''[[Ghost]]'', and requested a 44 scoop from the [[prison]]'s landing platform, an alteration from their original escape plan. Once Bridger and his team, namely [[Garazeb Orrelios]], [[Sabine Wren]], and [[C1-10P|Chopper]], fought their way to the landing platform, Syndulla swooped in. Bridger, Orrelios, Wren, Chopper, and Ohnaka then leapt from the platform and onto the top of the ''Ghost'' before flying away.<ref name="Steps" />

==Behind the scenes==
A 44 scoop first appeared in part one of the two-part episode ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]'',<ref name="Steps" /> the premiere episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Three|third season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' which aired on [[September 24]], [[2016]].<ref name="Premiere Date">{{SW|url=news/the-rebels-face-grand-admiral-thrawn-when-star-wars-rebels-season-three-premieres-saturday-september-24|text=The Rebels Face Grand Admiral Thrawn When ''Star Wars Rebels'' Season Three Premieres Saturday, September 24}}</ref> Prior to the episode's release, the tactic was first mentioned in an episode of the behind-the-scenes [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] series ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'' on [[July 13]] of that year,<ref name="Star Wars Show">{{StarWarsShow|Qe3LSQiMJUk|Star Wars Rebels Season 3 Clip, Gareth Edwards Interview, and More!|sw_url=video/the-star-wars-show-episode-10}}</ref> and then first pictured in the season's trailer on [[July 16]].<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|xmXp802sFgQ|Star Wars Rebels Season Three Trailer (Official)|sw_url=video/star-wars-rebels-season-three-trailer}}</ref> The term 44 scoop is a reference to the [[22 pickup]], another phrase used earlier in the series.<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=episodes/steps-into-shadow-trivia-gallery|text="Steps Into Shadow" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref>
