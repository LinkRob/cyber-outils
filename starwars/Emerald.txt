'''Emeralds''' were a type of valuable [[Gemstone|stone]],<ref name="The Escape">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Escape]]''</ref> which were green in [[color]].<ref name="The Nest">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Nest]]''</ref> [[Rhyssa Graf]] owned an emerald pendant, which was given by her [[Marriage|husband]], [[Auric Graf|Auric]], on [[Morellia]].<ref name="The Escape" /> The stone shared its name with a color featured in the names of various [[Clothing|costumes]] and [[weapon]]s used by [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]]s of the [[Grand Arena]] on [[Vespaara]] including [[Grozz]] and [[J-3DI]]<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date2">{{HuntersDate}}</ref>
