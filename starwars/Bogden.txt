'''Bogden''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Attack of the Clones" /> located in the [[Bogden system]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on the [[Hydian Way]]<ref name="TFABG" /> that was [[orbit]]ed by [[Moons of Bogden|multiple]] [[moon]]s, one of which was the location where the [[Sith Lord]] [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]] secretly hired the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] to become the [[cloning]] [[Clone template|template]] for the future [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Attack of the Clones" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bogden first appeared in "[[The Revenge of Kybo Ren]]," the eighth episode of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO]]'',<ref name="The Revenge of Kybo Ren">{{DroidsCite|The Revenge of Kybo Ren}}</ref> which originally aired on [[October 26]], [[1985]].<ref name="The Revenge of Kybo Ren air date">{{InsiderCite|27|A Star Wars CELibration|A ''Star Wars'' CELibration}}</ref> It became [[canon]] when it was mentioned in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="Attack of the Clones" />
