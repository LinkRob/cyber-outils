'''Drake''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[scavenger]] and [[Ipsium]] [[miner]] who worked for the gang boss [[Mokko]]. He was in Mokko's inner-circle and was commonly the "top earner" among the crew of scavengers under Mokko. Eventually, Drake's younger co-worker [[Benni Baro]] revealed to all the workers on a bridge that Mokko had lied and the [[ipsium]] had not degraded, and this enabled Mokko to keep the profits for himself. Baro passed [[Omega]]'s minicomputer to Drake, who reviewed the data, before confronting Mokko. Mokko defensively stated that without him, the miners would not have survived. Mokko then ordered his [[Droid mine guard|security droids]] to attack the miners with [[electrostaff]]s. In response, [[Clone Force 99]] destroyed Mokko's droids, while a few of the [[Mokko's crew|Ipsidon miners]] engaged Mokko in [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]]. Mokko soon began to fall off the bridge, but Baro caught him. As Baro struggled to pull their gang boss back up, Drake told their younger co-worker to let Mokko fall to his [[death]].<ref name="Retrieval" />
