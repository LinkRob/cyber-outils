The '''''Bellicose''''' was a [[Firewolf man-of-war]] [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Chiss]] [[Admiral]] [[Ers'ikaro]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation's [[Chiss Defense Force|Defense Force]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> the ''Bellicose'' stopped the [[battle at Csaus]] between the Chiss [[Tahmie]] and [[Droc family|Droc]] [[Great Families|families]] before it escalated. The [[Clarr family]]'s [[Captain]] [[Clarr'os'culry]] and [[Patriarch]] [[Clarr'ivl'exow]] mentioned the [[warship]]'s intervention while discussing information retrieved by the [[Grysk]] operative "[[Jixtus]]" about a [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|crisis]] in the Ascendancy.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Chiss [[Supreme General]] [[Ba'kif]] had the ''Bellicose'' stay in the Ascendancy's territories while a third of the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] traveled to [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand|capture]] [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] on the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] [[Sunrise]]. [[Mitth family|Mitth]] Patriarch [[Mitth'urf'ianico]] mentioned the [[Man-of-war|vessel's]] stay in the Ascendancy during a [[Circle of Unity]], a meeting of all the [[Chiss family|families']] patriarchs.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Bellicose'' was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final volume in ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]'' written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
