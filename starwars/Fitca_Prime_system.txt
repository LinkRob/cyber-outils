The '''Fitca Prime system''' was located in the [[Urce sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> The [[star system]] was situated in grid square L-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Fitca Prime system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Fitca system]] was introduced in the [[1995]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplement ''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]'', written by [[Bill Smith]] and published by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="AIR">''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]''</ref>
