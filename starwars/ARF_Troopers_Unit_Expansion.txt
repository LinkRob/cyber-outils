The '''ARF Troopers Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ135''') is an expansion to the asymetrical two&ndash;player wargame ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. It was released on [[May 28]], [[2025]]. 

==Publisher's description==
''The ARF Trooper Unit Expansion brings the recon specialists of the Galactic Republic armies to Star Wars™: Legion. Joining the renowned troopers is a new Jedi Knight attachment for Clone Trooper squads as a replacement leader.''<ref name="ARF" />

''The ARF troopers bring special abilities for deployment, setting up deadly ambushes and support cover. Bringing their thematic mechanics to the tabletop. Joined by a new Jedi Knight model, this ARF Trooper Unit Expansion is great for hobbyists and gamers alike.''<ref name="ARF" />

==Contents==
*8 Miniatures
**1 [[Jedi]]
**7 [[Advanced Recon Force trooper]]
*1 Unit Card
*3 Upgrade Cards
