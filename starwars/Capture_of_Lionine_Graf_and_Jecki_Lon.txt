In [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] and the [[human]] [[soldier|warrior]] [[Lionine Graf]] were '''captured''' by a [[Orisent's mercenaries|group]] of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] on the [[planet]] [[Siline]] during the [[great hunt]] of [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|that year's]] [[Crowning Convocation]]. The mercenary leader, [[Orisent]], was hired by the young Graf to sabotage the great hunt by releasing [[lylek]]s into the arena and [[poaching]] [[ericadi]] so that the [[Silinese]] traditions would lose their air of purity and help usher in a less traditional future. When the operation was discovered by Lon and her allies, sullying Orisent's potential for profiting on ericadi hunting, she pivoted to abducting Graf and Lon in hopes of making ransom money. After knocking Lon and Graf unconscious, the mercenaries brought their captives to the [[Heart of Siline]], a sacred place, as they waited for the ransom.<ref name="Crown" />

As Graf and Lon fought the mercenaries, fellow Padawan [[Yord Fandar]] protected the Silinese [[Sitia Arthyst]], who had been severely injured by a trap sprung by the mercenaries which used resonance to target Silinese crystalline [[bone]]s. Wielding [[Jecki Lon's lightsaber|Jecki's lightsaber]] and a [[blaster]] pilfered off a deceased poacher, Fandar fought off the onslaught of mercenaries. When a lylek joined the battle, attacking indiscriminately, many of the mercenaries died. Soon, the [[Silinese government]] arrived with Lon and Fandar's [[Jedi Master]]s [[Sol]] and [[Elishe]]. They supported Fandar and came to Sitia's aid. The Silinese warriors killed any mercenaries left behind after the capture of Lon and Graf, aside from the Silinese Convocation participant [[Parnix]], who begged for mercy from the Jedi, and was spared for future interrogation. The warriors then coordinated with the Jedi to put down the lylek.<ref name="Crown" />
