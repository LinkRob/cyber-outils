The '''A-wing Slash''' was a strategy used in [[starfighter]] battles that used the superior speed of [[A-wing starfighter]]s,<ref name="A-wing Model">''[[Star Wars: A-wing Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]''</ref> including the [[RZ-2 A-wing interceptor]],<ref name="Rebel Starfighter">''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> to trick and overcome enemy forces. In the maneuver, slower fighters such as [[X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] engaged an opponent to distract and confuse them. Once the enemy's attention was focused on the first wave of fighters, a group of speedier A-wings entered the battle to make a devastating surprise attack.<ref name="A-wing Model" />

==Behind the scenes==
The A-wing Slash was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2017]] reference and model book ''[[Star Wars: A-wing Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]'' by [[Michael Kogge]].<ref name="A-wing Model" /> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1993]] novel ''[[The Last Command]]'' by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="The Last Command">''[[The Last Command]]''</ref>
