'''Emerick Caphtor''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Caphtor was tasked with investigating the mysterious death of [[Loden Greatstorm]]<ref name="TOS 1" />  on the planet [[Grizal]]. He found that an old jedi nursery rhyme gave him clues where the Jedi Archives came up short. At the same time, [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] hired the [[Private investigator|private]] [[detective]] [[Sian Holt]] to look into the same mystery.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 2">[[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 2|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 2]]</ref> 

==Biography==
Emerick Caphtor was taken in by the Jedi Order at a young age. When he was a [[Jedi Initiate|youngling]], Jedi nursemaid [[Ruusstha Vidyarvrikt]] would sing a [[Shrii ka rai ka rai|nursery rhyme]] to him when he wouldn't go to sleep.<ref name="TOS 1" />

==Powers and abilities==
Caphtor could use [[the Force]] to induce a feeling of calm in another person.<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> According to Stellan Gios, Caphtor was the best investigator the Jedi Order had and he was also a capable fighter with a lightsaber.<ref name="TOS 1" /> He could dispel fog through telekinesis<ref name="Trail of Shadows 2" /> and was capable of piloting a starship in battle.<ref name="Trail of Shadows4">[[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 4|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 4]]</ref>

==Equipment==
Emerick Caphtor wielded a [[Emerick Caphtor's lightsaber|yellow-bladed lightsaber]]. He had a droid assistant called [[Q-2]].<ref name="TOS 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Emerick Caphtor first appeared in the [[2021]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]].<ref name="Out of the Shadows" />
