'''11-4D''' was a [[protocol droid]] in the service of [[Darth Plagueis]] in the [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Some [[time]] before the [[Clone Wars]], Plagueis was [[Tragedy of Darth Plagueis the Wise|killed]] by his [[Sith apprentice]] [[Darth Sidious]], who took 11-4D as his inheritance. The [[droid]] went on to faithfully serve Sidious for [[Imperial Era|many]] years as it was programmed to serve its newest master.

==Biography==
[[File:SecretsoftheSith-Darth-Plagueis.png|thumb|left|250px|11-4D served Darth Plagueis (pictured).]]
11-4D served the [[Sith Lord]]s [[Darth Plagueis]] and his apprentice [[Darth Sidious]]. After Darth Sidious killed his master, 11-4D continued serving Sidious in several guises. In public, Sidious assumed the persona of [[Senator]] Sheev Palpatine of [[Naboo]] who eventually rose to the position of [[Supreme Chancellor]] following the [[Battle of Naboo]]. Sidious manipulated the [[Separatist Crisis]] which led to the emergence of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the devastating [[Clone Wars]]. Sidious used the [[Cloning|clone]] [[Grand Army of the Republic]] to destroy the [[Jedi]] during the [[Great Jedi Purge]]. He then [[Proclamation of the New Order|appointed]] himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] and transformed the Republic into the first [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

By [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> 11-4D was stationed at the [[Imperial Palace]], which had been the former [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. 11-4D also spent time with his master inside Sidious' lair, a small rock-walled enclosure deep beneath the temple which had once been an ancient [[Shrine in the Depths|Sith shrine]] that the Jedi had built over. After the Emperor dispatched [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on a [[Mission to Murkhana|mission]] to the planet [[Murkhana]], 11-4D reported that Tarkin had departed in his starship ''[[Carrion Spike]]''. Sidious instructed the droid to alert him when the ship landed on Murkhana. Sidious subsequently returned to his meditation.<ref name="Tarkin" />

After the insurgent [[Berch Teller]] and his [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] stole the ''Carrion Spike'', 11-4D was present at a meeting between Sidious and his [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military|Joint Chiefs]] including [[Admiral]] [[Conan Antonio Motti]], [[General]] [[Cassio Tagge]], [[Rear Admiral]]s [[Kendal Ozzel]] and [[Tiaan Jerjerrod]], [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order|COMPNOR]] [[Director]] [[Armand Isard]], [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Deputy Director]] [[Harus Ison]], [[Colonel]] [[Wullf Yularen]], and [[Naval Intelligence Agency|Naval Intelligence]] [[Vice Admiral]]s [[Dodd Rancit]] and [[Terrinald Screed]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

During the meeting, 11-4D plugged one of its appendages into an interface socket that routed holofeeds to the [[Pinnacle Room|summit]] from the former [[Jedi communications suite]]. 11-4D also complied with the Emperor's orders to tint the windows. The Emperor and Joint Chiefs witnessed footage of the ''Carrion Spike'' [[Attack on Lucazec|attacking]] the [[Tagge Corporation|TaggeCo]] mining facilities on [[Lucazec]]. When the Emperor asked 11-4D if the droid puzzled out what was happening, 11-4D confirmed the affirmative and reported that the [[holovid]] was being transmitted by the ''Carrion Spike''. In response, Sidious dispatched reinforcements to aid Tarkin and Vader, including one of the latter's [[Darth Vader's black Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|black Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptors]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

[[File:EmperorPalpatine-BaseSeries3.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Sidious, 11-4D's master]]
11-4D was later present when Sidious questioned the ISB [[case officer]] [[Stellan]] and the [[Koorivar]] informant [[Bracchia]] about the discovery of the communications cache on Murkhana which had led to the theft of the ''Carrion Spike''. The two confirmed that they were acting on information which had been passed by [[Imperial Intelligence|Military Intelligence]], of which Naval Intelligence was a branch of. Satisfied with their explanation, Sidious tasked 11-4D with checking the holovid sent by Military Intelligence to ISB. When 11-4D pointed out he could find no record of the holovid, Sidious said that the video was in the ISB's archives. After 11-4D retrieved the holovid, Sidious found that the holovid was a fake which had been created by someone with access to Imperial codes and to devices capable of subverting the [[HoloNet]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

Vader and Tarkin subsequently discovered that Rancit was a [[Treason|traitor]] who was supplying intelligence and weapons to Teller and his insurgents in order to boost his own standing in the [[Imperial Ruling Council]]. While Vader executed Rancit, Tarkin defeated Teller's rebels during the [[Battle near the Gulf of Tatooine]]. The defeat of Teller's insurgency led to Tarkin's promotion to [[Grand Moff]] and allowed the Empire to continue with the construction of the first [[Death Star]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Characteristics and equipment==
11-4D served Darth Plagueis and his successor Darth Sidious, and had apparently taken several guises throughout his time of service.
The droid had many more [[Limb|arms]] than a typical protocol model, and only two of them terminated in hand-like appendages. The rest were devoted to tools of various purpose, such as [[computer]] interface and power charge extensions. 11-4D once oversaw the excavation and restoration of Sidious's private lair; an ancient [[Sith]] shrine underneath the deepest recesses of the Imperial Palace. The droid also served as Sidious' servant and assisted him with various duties including relaying holofeeds and retrieving holovids.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Behind the scenes==
11-4D first appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> Before that, [[11-4D/Legends|11-4D]] originally appeared in the [[2012]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel [[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']], also written by Luceno.<ref name="Darth Plagueis">[[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']]</ref>

In [[2012]], during a question and answer session on [[Wikipedia:Facebook|Facebook]], Luceno opined that 11-4D was there when [[Anakin Skywalker]] transformed into [[Darth]] Vader, depicted in a scene from [[2005]]'s [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Luceno">{{WebCite|url=http://www.eucantina.net/archives/11656|text=Transcript of James Luceno's Facebook Fan Chat|date=2012-01-25|archivedate=20170617182922}}</ref>
