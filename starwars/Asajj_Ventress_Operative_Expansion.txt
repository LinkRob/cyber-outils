The '''Asajj Ventress Operative Expansion''' ('''SWL108''') is a character expansion to ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]<ref name="Asmodee" /> released on [[March 17]], [[2023]].<ref name="Asmodee old" /> The release adds [[Sith assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] into the game.<ref name="Asmodee" />

==Publisher's summary==
===Online summary===
''A sinister new character enters Star Wars: Legion in this new Operative Expansion! Asajj Ventress is a talented warrior who wields two curved lightsabers in a fighting style known as Jar'Kai with deadly efficiency. This pack adds her to the ranks of the Separatist Alliance along with four new Command Cards that give her the opportunity to unleash her unique skills on the battlefield.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

===Back blurb summary===
''Taken as a young child from her home with the Nightsisters of Dathomir, Asajj Ventress is the ultimate survivor. Strong with the dark side of the Force, Ventress is a talented warrior who served as apprentice to Count Dooku himself. She wields two curved lightsabers in a fighting style known as Jar Kai and her combat prowess makes her a challenging foe to both Jedi Knights, such as Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker, as well as the Separatist General Grievous. Ventress stealth abilities, as well as her gift of deception, make her an ideal assassin.''<ref name="Operative Expansion" />
