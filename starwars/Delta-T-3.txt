'''Delta-T-3''' was the designation of a [[YV-865 Aurore-class freighter|YV-865 ''Aurore''-class freighter]] that was used by the [[Zygerrian Slave Empire]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. After a failed [[mission to Zygerria]] and the capture of [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Governor]] [[Gupat Roshti]], and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex|CT-7567]], Delta-T-3 was used to transport them along with other [[Slavery|slaves]] to the [[Kadavo system]] for conditioning at the [[Kadavo slave processing facility]].<ref name="Slaves of the Republic" />

{{Ship-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Delta-T-3 first appeared in "[[Slaves of the Republic]]" and its designation is written in [[aurebesh]] on its wings. Additionally, other writing on the starboard [[hull]] spells "Spa", and there is further writing on the port side.<ref name="Slaves of the Republic" />
