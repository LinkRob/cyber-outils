During the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the '''family''' of<ref name="Victory's Price" /> the [[human]]<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> [[Gender|male]] [[Bansu Ro]], who was [[Reproduction|born]] on<ref name="Victory's Price" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Lothal]], sided with rebels against the Empire. Ro resultantly split from his family<ref name="Victory's Price" /> and joined the [[Carida Academy|Imperial Academy]] on the<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> planet<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> [[Carida]].<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> Although the allegiance of his estranged family initially put a stain on his career prospects in the Empire, Ro was taken in by the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="4 ABY">[[Bansu Ro]] joined the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] in the [[TIE Fighter 4|fourth issue]] of the [[Star Wars: TIE Fighter (comic series)|''Star Wars: TIE Fighter'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]], which takes place between the [[mission to the Kudo system]] and the [[Battle of Endor]]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the latter two events to [[4 ABY]], Ro must have joined the 204th in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> Four [[Standard month|months]] after the fall of the [[Galactic Empire]] at the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> he was an ex&ndash;Imperial present on the planet [[Freerock]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The family first appeared in the form of Bansu Ro in the [[TIE Fighter 4|fourth]] issue of the [[Star Wars: TIE Fighter (comic series)|''Star Wars: TIE Fighter'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series, written by [[Jody Houser]], penciled by [[Rogê Antônio]] and [[Ig Guara]],<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[July 17]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/75575/star_wars_tie_fighter_2019_4|text=Star Wars: Tie Fighter (2019) #4}}</ref> Bansu Ro's family was then established to exist in ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the final novel in [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy which was published in [[2021]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
