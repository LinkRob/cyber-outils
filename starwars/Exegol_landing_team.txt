The '''Exegol landing team''' was led by [[Resistance]] [[General]] [[Finn]] during the [[Battle of Exegol]]. Their task was to destroy the [[navigation tower]] that was capable of making the [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[fleet]], the [[Final Order]], leave the hidden [[Sith]] [[planet]] of [[Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==History==
{{Quote|You're going to launch a ground invasion on a Star Destroyer?|Wrobie Tyce|<ref name="Episode 9" />}}
The ground invasion force was transported by the Resistance's [[YC-123B transport hauler|troop carrier]] the [[Fortitude (transport)|''Fortitude'']]. They were originally going to land of the surface of Exegol and take out the Sith Eternal's navigation tower so their fleet could not deploy. However, a [[Unidentified First Order officer (Exegol)|First Order officer]] onboard [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]]'s [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] realized the Resistance's plan and informed [[Admiral]] [[Frantis Griss]] and Pryde. Pryde ordered the navigation signal to be switched over to his ship so they could guide out the fleet themselves.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:Resistance-charge.png|left|thumb|199x199px|Finn and Jannah charging across the ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer ''Steadfast''.|alt=]]
As the tower shut down, Finn sensed through [[the Force]] that the navigation signal was coming from the [[command ship]], and ordered the ground invasion to target the ''Steadfast''. The troop carrier landed on the outside deck of the Destroyer, deploying [[Company 77]], mounted on [[Orbak]]s. The cavalry charged towards the navigation tower, with General Finn, [[Jannah]], and [[BB-8]] at the front. [[Commander]] [[Rose Tico]], [[Captain]] [[Beaumont Kin]], and [[Lieutenant]] [[Kaydel Ko Connix]] lead the infantry behind them with [[Sidon Ithano]]. The team succeeded in their mission to take out the tower, but was attacked by [[Sith trooper]]s. Connix was hit with a laser bolt to the leg, then Tico and Kin came to her aid. The overwhelming attack from [[TIE/dg starfighter|Sith TIE fighters]], Sith troopers, and [[Sith Jetpack Trooper]]s killed many Resistance troopers, forcing the remaining infantry and cavalry to retreat back to the troop carrier. BB-8 joined the retreating infantry with Connix and Kin. Tico contacted Finn, urging him and Jannah to leave with the surviving troops, but Finn refused and ordered her to leave with the others. After the troop carrier departed, Finn and Jannah managed to take control of a cannon and use it to target the command deck, causing the ''Steadfast'' to plummet down to the surface, which resulted with the Sith fleet to be trapped and destroyed on Exegol. Finn and Jannah held onto the ship as it became vertical, until they were rescued by General [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Chewbacca]] aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Exegol landing team was created and developed for the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="Episode IX" /> It was later given its name in the ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' that was released on the same day.<ref name="TROS Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
