The '''Fyodos system''' was located in the [[Tammuz sector]], a part of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square U-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fyodos [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Star system/Legends|star system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was introduced as the [[Tatrang system]] in ''[[Planets of the Galaxy, Volume Two]]'', a [[1992]] sourcebook by [[John Terra]] published by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. In the book, the system was also referred to as the Fyodos system.<ref name="Planets">''[[Planets of the Galaxy, Volume Two]]''</ref>
