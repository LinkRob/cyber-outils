The '''Desolous Legion''', numerically designated as the '''44th''', was a [[Sith trooper]] [[legion]] of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Sith cult]] known as the [[Sith Eternal]]. The legion had five-thousand [[trooper]]s as its composition and was active during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]] until the Sith Eternal's forces were defeated during the [[Battle of Exegol]] in [[35 ABY]]. As their history was hidden from the rest of [[the galaxy]], the significance of the legion's name, the [[Timeline of galactic history|ancient]] [[Sith Lord]] [[Desolous]], was only understood by the Sith Eternal's cultists.

==Description==
[[File:SithTroopers-TROSGG.png|thumb|right|180px|5,000 Sith troopers (pictured) made up the Desolous Legion.]]
The Desolous Legion, designated as the 44th, was a [[legion]] within the [[Sith Eternal army|army]] of the [[Sith Eternal]], a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Sith cult|cult]] that worshipped the [[Sith]] and was headquartered on the [[Unknown Regions]] Sith<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[throneworld]]<ref name="DB Sith">{{Databank|sith|Sith}}</ref> [[Exegol]]. All Sith Eternal legions were numerically designated and given the name of an [[Timeline of galactic history|ancient]] [[Sith Lord]], [[Desolous]] for the 44th. Only the [[Sith cultist|Sith Eternal cultists]] understood the significance of the names given to their legions, as their history was hidden to the rest of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

The legion consisted of five-thousand [[Sith trooper]]s, which were [[infantry]] [[soldier]]s created from the offspring of cultists,<ref name="TROS VD" /> and [[Youngling|children]] conscripted into the ranks of the [[First Order]] who were identified and disappeared due to their potential.<ref name="TROS Expanded">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> The legion's troopers were further divided into [[squadron]]s of standard infantry and aerial [[Sith Jetpack Trooper]]s of ten each.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> With the Desolous Legion comprising of Sith troopers, they were equipped with [[Color|crimson red]] [[Sith trooper armor]] and could be armed with [[ST-W48 blaster rifle]]s or [[FWMB-10B repeating blaster]]s, while Sith Jetpack Troopers were equipped with [[NJP-900 integrated jetpack]]s and [[F-11ABA heavy blaster cannon]]s.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==History==
The Desolous Legion was active by the time of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the First Order and the [[Resistance]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> which took place from [[34 ABY]] to [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The Sith Eternal and its [[Sith Eternal military|forces]] were defeated during the [[Battle of Exegol]]<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> when a joint Resistance force and a [[Citizens' Fleet]] from across the [[galaxy]] defeated [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]] and the entirety of his [[Final Order|forces]],<ref name="Episode IX" /> including the Desolous Legion.<ref name="Note" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Desolous Legion was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was released in [[2019]] and authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
