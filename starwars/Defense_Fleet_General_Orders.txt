The '''Defense Fleet General Orders''' were a set of orders in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet|Defense Fleet]] that stated that strategy, tactics, and weaponry was not to be discussed with [[Chiss]] [[sky-walker]] [[navigator]]s.<ref name="Thrawn">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref> During the [[Lioaoin skirmish]], [[Caregiver]] [[Mitth'ali'astov]], [[core name]] "Thalias," disregarded the orders when she explained the [[breacher]] [[missile]]s and [[triangulation observation area]]s aboard the Expansionary Defense Fleet [[Starship|vessel]] ''[[Springhawk]]'' to sky-walker [[Che'ri]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
