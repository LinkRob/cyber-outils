"'''Blade Squadron'''" is a two-part short story written by [[David J. Williams]] and [[Mark S. Williams]] that appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 149|''Star Wars Insider'' 149]] and [[Star Wars Insider 150|''Star Wars Insider'' 150]]. It was inspired by artwork depicting an unused battle scene from [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], and the story focuses on a [[Blade Squadron|squadron]] of [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing]] [[starfighter]]s on a suicide mission during the [[Battle of Endor]].

''Blade Squadron'' is the first ''Insider'' short story, and the first story outside of the films and ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|The Clone Wars]] ''TV series, that is part of the new [[canon]],<ref name="LR">{{WebCite|url=http://lightsaberrattling.com/star-wars-insider-magazine-fiction-is-canon|text=Star Wars Insider Magazine Fiction Is Canon|date=2014-05-30|work=Lightsaber Rattling|archiveurl=https://archive.is/nltyu}}</ref> as defined since the [[April 25]], [[2014]] reboot.<ref name="End of EU">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> The story was later reprinted on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Blade Squadron on SW.com">{{SW|url=news/blade-squadron-part-one|text=Blade Squadron: Part One Short Story}}</ref><ref name="Blade Squadron on SW.com 2">{{SW|url=news/blade-squadron-part-two|text=Blade Squadron, Part Two}}</ref> with some slight differences.

==Plot summary==
The Battle of Endor is near. In preparation for the up-coming battle, Blade Squadron has recently been refitted with the new [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|A/SF-01 B-Wing Assault Starfighter]] - more commonly referred to as the "B-Wing." Wing Commander Adon Fox must lead Blade Squadron on a suicide mission against the Devastator in support of Rebel Command's overall battle plan to engage and destroy the [[Death Star II/Legends|Death Star II]], which is currently under construction and in orbit around Endor's moon.

==Development==
The two-part story ''Blade Squadron'' was published as the monthly short fiction of the [[Star Wars Insider 149|149th]] and [[Star Wars Insider 150|150th]] issues of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine. The announcement of the new [[Canon|Lucasfilm canon policy]] on [[April 25]], [[2014]] was released between the publication of the two issues, leaving the state of the short story uncertain. In ''Star Wars Insider'' 150, it was announced that ''Blade Squadron'' did belong to the new canon, making it the first prose work and story in general to be considered as canon outside from the movies and the TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.
