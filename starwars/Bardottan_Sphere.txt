The '''Bardottan Sphere''' was an orb-shaped [[Force artifact]] that functioned as a battery, capturing and storing [[The Force|Force energy]] stolen from living beings. When certain mystical and gravitational effects were applied, the Sphere could focus its captured energy into a Force wielder.

==Description==
{{Quote|Humbah! Looganah! Humbah! Looganah! Disjenck! Now shall the demon of war, Malmourral, be sated. Now shall the Frangawl clan steal your essence!|Peteen uses the Sphere in a sacrificial ritual|<ref name="Disappeared Part 1" />}}
Large, crystalline, and milkily transparent, the Bardottan Sphere was neither mechanical nor alive, its color dependent on the light it reflected. Inert until attuned to a Force-sensitive being, the Sphere would glow when activated, directed to form a link with a nearby target by its user. The Sphere could target any living creature. Once targeted, the Sphere cut off the victim's connection to the [[Living Force]] and drained it until nothing but a lifeless husk remained.<ref name="NoP" />

Some theorized that the Sphere was an anti-Force artifact that, when inert, remained cut off from the universe and reflected the Force around it. These theorists further believed that the Sphere contained a sort of "Force vacuum" that was a natural attractor of the Force when active. More than a tool of execution, the Sphere acted as a reservoir, storing what it had stolen for a time to be tapped into to amplify numerous Force powers by the Nightsisters.<ref name="NoP" />

==History==
{{Quote|Our analysts agree that the shard is likely somewhere in the Jedi Temple. We know you wanted to recon the site, but our intel indicates there is no time. The Empire's forces are mobilizing with nearly the same information you have. You must reach the shard first! We dare not even imagine it in the hands of the Emperor.|Rebel Command|<ref name="Heart of the Empire">{{SWIA|set=Heart of the Empire}}</ref>}}
Either created or discovered by the [[Nightsisters]] of [[Dathomir]], the Sphere's origins remained unknown, although its absence from historical record suggested that it was constructed by the Nightsisters or their allies shortly preceding the kidnapping of [[Queen]] [[Julia]].<ref name="NoP" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Disappeared time note">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of "[[The Wrong Jedi]]" and the [[Battle of Coruscant]] as being in 19 BBY. {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}} puts the events of "[[The Disappeared, Part II]]" after "The Wrong Jedi," but before the events of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] which means that its events would take place in the same year as both.</ref> it was used by members of the [[Frangawl Cult]] to drain the [[Living Force]] from kidnapped members of the [[Dagoyan Order]] on the planet [[Bardotta]].<ref name="Disappeared Part 1">{{TCW|The Disappeared, Part I}}</ref> Later, the Nightsister Mother [[Talzin]] attempted to use the Bardottan Sphere and the captive Queen Julia as part of a ritual on [[Zardossa Stix]] to increase her Force powers. Talzin and the Frangawl Cult were thwarted by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] and the [[Gungan]] [[Junior Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]].<ref name="Disappeared Part 2" /> Exhaustive record searches turned up no trace of the Sphere before or after the incident.<ref name="NoP" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], a Rebel team on [[Coruscant]], the [[ecumenopolis]] capital of the [[Galactic Empire]], discovered and transmitted word that the Empire sought the Sphere as a weapon before being captured. A rescue was mounted by a [[Coruscant rebel cell|Rebel cell]] composed of operatives [[Drokkatta]], [[Jarrod Kelvin]], and [[Ko-Tun Feralo]], and they learned of Lorn, a Jedi scholar and bureaucrat whose historical records could get them one step closer to a shard of the Bardottan Sphere; the Rebels recovered these records from the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate building]]. [[Zaaryn]], an [[Imperial officer]], had knowledge of a piece of the Sphere. Initially searching for a wealthy buyer on Coruscant, he later tried to sell the information to the former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]],<ref name="Heart of the Empire" /> the son of Talzin,<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 3|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 3]]</ref> arranging a meeting on a remote, Imperial-controlled world. The Rebels desired this information.<ref name="Heart of the Empire" />

Becoming a target, Lorn implored the Rebels to protect him in exchange for information about the Sphere. The Rebels arranged a rendezvous with a group of Alliance sympathizers meant to share information with the original Rebel team. Rebel Command concluded that the Bardottan Sphere shard was located in the Jedi Temple, intending to deny it from Emperor Palpatine as the Empire mobilized with similar information. Lorn narrowed down the shard's possible hiding places but had to be on site for a final analysis, leading the Rebels to escort him. Rebel intelligence located Maul at an Imperial facility on a remote world. Unable to identify Maul's agents on Coruscant, the Rebels aimed to capture him directly. Acquiring the Bardottan shard on Coruscant, the Rebels were aided in their escape by [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Heart of the Empire" />

At Alliance headquarters, Rebel leadership examined the shard. Deemed too dangerous to use, Tano convinced the Rebel leaders not to destroy it. Instead, a task force was formed, hiding the shard in a remote corner of the galaxy.<ref name="Heart of the Empire" />

After the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="NoP date">''[[Nexus of Power]]'' takes place after the [[death]] of [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] during the [[Battle of Yavin]].</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Sphere was under guard by Dagoyan mystics. Some scholar-mystics studied the Sphere with the intentions of using its powers in a way more aligned with peaceful Bardottan philosophies, lobbying for greater access to the object as to better understand and therefore control it. However, any who wanted to activate it failed to devise detailed plans satisfactory to Bardotta's sovereign and the Dagoyan council, arguing that the Sphere was required to formulate specifics, leading to the denial of their requests due to the danger the orb posed.<ref name="NoP" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bardottan Sphere first appeared in "[[The Disappeared, Part I]]," the eighth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Disappeared Part 1" /> It was later identified in ''[[Nexus of Power]]'', a [[Star Wars Roleplaying]] sourcebook published on [[March 17]], [[2016]].<ref name="NoP" />
