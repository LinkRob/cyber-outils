The '''Force icon''' was a symbol used by the [[Path of the Open Hand]]. The symbol was three wavy [[Color|blue]] lines on top of each other. The Path of the Open Hand used [[brikal shell]]s to smear the icon onto their foreheads.<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref>
