'''Amandla Stenberg''' is an actress who portrays [[Verosha Aniseya|Verosha]] and [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'' (2024).<ref name="Announcement" /> She is best-known for playing [[W:c:thehungergames:Rue|Rue]] in ''[[Wikipedia:The Hunger Games (film)|The Hunger Games]]'' (2012), and for starring in films such as  ''[[Wikipedia:Everything, Everything (film)|Everything, Everything]]'' (2017), ''[[Wikipedia:The Hate U Give (film)|The Hate U Give]]'' (2018), ''[[Wikipedia:The Darkest Minds|The Darkest Minds]]'' (2018), and ''[[Wikipedia:Where Hands Touch|Where Hands Touch]]'' (2018).<ref name="The Film Stage">{{WebCite|url=http://thefilmstage.com/news/the-hunger-games-pulls-in-rue-and-thresh|text='The Hunger Games' Pulls In Rue and Thresh|author=Kristy Puchko|date=2011-04-18|work=[https://thefilmstage.com The Film Stage]|archivedate=20120318191638}}</ref><ref name="The Hollywood Reporter">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/amandla-stenberg-nick-robinson-star-912150|text=Amandla Stenberg, Nick Robinson to Star in 'Everything Everything' (Exclusive)|author=Borys Kit and Rebecca Ford|date=2016-07-20|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20160720220150}}</ref><ref name="Variety">{{WebCite|url=https://variety.com/2016/film/news/hunger-games-amandla-stenberg-hate-u-give-movie-1201737524|text='Hunger Games' Actress Amandla Stenberg to Star in 'The Hate U Give'|author=Dave McNary|date=2016-03-23|work=[[Wikipedia:Variety (magazine)|''Variety'']]|archivedate=20220714093743}}</ref><ref name="Darkest Minds Hollywood Reporter">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/heat-vision/amandla-stenberg-star-foxs-ya-932499|text=Amandla Stenberg to Star in Fox's YA Adaptation of 'Darkest Minds' (Exclusive)|author=Borys Kit|date=2016-09-26|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Report|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20220518234424}}</ref>
