'''Drakka's Diner''' was an eatery in a dangerous region of [[Shaketown]] on the planet [[Gorse]]. The [[Besalisk]] [[Drakka]] worked as a chef. The location was frequented by [[Kanan Jarrus]] as well as other local residents.<ref name="AND" />

==History==
When an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] landed in front of the diner, [[Drakka]] had to close it for an entire [[Standard day|day]]. The diner was destroyed when the Imperial [[shuttle]] exploded.<ref name="AND" />
