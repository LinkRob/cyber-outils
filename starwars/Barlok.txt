I figured it would be a great chance to reload my cargo pods with all the fresh fruit and veg a wanderin' chef could possibly need&mdash;from shi-shok to taba leaf.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Barlok''' was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in the [[Barlok system]] of the [[Marcol sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It fell within grid square L-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The world was known for its advanced horticultural techniques. [[Prisht-fruit]] was [[Homeworld|native]] to the world and its most prized produce. Other [[Diet|edible]] [[plant]]s like [[taba]] leaf and [[shi-shok]] could also be purchased there.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

Having had enough of [[Temperature|hot]] worlds like [[Li-Toran]] and [[Jakku]], the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited Barlok during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he made<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs used the opportunity to stock up on [[fruit]] and [[vegetable]]s, including two bushels of prisht-fruit, which he thought he would have been a fool to miss. He later mentioned Barlok as part of the recipe for [[prisht parfait]] in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Barlok was mentioned in the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> [[Barlok/Legends|Barlok]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[2006]] novel, [[Outbound Flight (novel)|''Outbound Flight'']].<ref name="Outbound">[[Outbound Flight (novel)|''Outbound Flight'']]</ref>
