The '''BTL-S3 Y-wing starfighter''', also known as the '''BTL-S3 Y-Wing''', was a two-seater variant of the [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|BTL series]] Y-wing.

==Characteristics==
{{Main|BTL Y-wing starfighter#Characteristics}}
Except for the cockpit variations, all ships in the BTL series were similar in design.<ref name="RSOWM" /> It was typically crewed by a [[pilot]] and [[gunner]], who sat back-to-back in the [[cockpit]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 17" /> An [[astromech droid]] could occupy an exterior [[Astromech socket|socket]].<ref name="RSOWM" />

==History==
It was developed for [[Galactic Republic]] service in the [[Clone Wars]], but became most famous for its use by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Weapon" /> Despite its slow speed and ponderous handling characteristics, these craft were renowned for their toughness in the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The [[Resistance]] operated at least one such craft for armed reconnaissance missions in [[34 ABY]], during the [[Cold War]]. At the time, it was the Resistance's only two-seat starfighter, although it was soon destroyed during the [[Mission to Spalex|mission to]] [[Spalex]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 17" />
