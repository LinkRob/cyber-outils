"'''Driven to the Dark Side'''" is a [[canon]] short story that appears in ''[[The Prequel Trilogy Stories]]''. It was written by [[S.T. Bende]].<ref name="TPTS" /> It adapts the events of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] from [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s perspective from after the [[Battle of Coruscant]] to the [[Duel in Palpatine's office]].
