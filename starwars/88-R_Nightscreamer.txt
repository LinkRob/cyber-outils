The '''88-R Nightscreamer''', also stylized as '''88-R "Nightscreamer"''', was a model of [[speeder bike]] manufactured by [[Ikas-Adno]]. It was high-performance, faster than some [[airspeeder]]s, and had to be equipped with forward [[deflector shield]]s to protect its [[pilot]] from debris particles that would be dangerous at its high speeds.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
