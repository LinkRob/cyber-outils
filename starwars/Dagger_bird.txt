'''Dagger birds''' were a type of [[bird]] that was native to the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]]. They were distinct by their pointed [[Color|blue-black]] wing pattern, along with their ability to dive into [[water]] to spear up [[fish]] for [[Foodstuff|food]].<ref name="Sparks" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Volume 4 (Phase III)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Volume 4'']]|one [[Standard year|year]] and a few [[Standard month|months]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}} The events of "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Sparks Fly]]" take place concurrent with ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Volume 4'', therefore meaning they must take in the same year.</ref> the [[human]] [[Pikka Adren]] had found the birds to be elegant, believing their ability to dive to be an excellent example of natural [[Engineer|engineering]].<ref name="Sparks" />
