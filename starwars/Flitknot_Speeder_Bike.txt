The '''Flitknot Speeder Bike''',<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> also known as the '''Flitknot Speeder''',<ref name="Count Dooku's Speeder" /> '''Geonosian Speeder''',<ref name="Beginnings">''[[Star Wars Journeys: Beginnings]]''</ref> or the '''Separatist speeder''',<ref name="Blue Shadow Virus">{{TCW|Blue Shadow Virus (episode)}}</ref> was a model of [[speeder bike]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Count Dooku's Speeder" />

==Characteristics==
The Flitknot [[Speeder]] was the preferred [[speeder bike]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. These bikes were fast and maneuverable, enough so that it was able to outrun a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|gunship]]. Being of simple design, this speeder had more bulk located at its rear in the [[engine]]s than anywhere else. It had dual handholds and dual footholds, making them ideal for [[humanoid]]s to use.<ref name="Count Dooku's Speeder" />

==History==
[[File:STAPs_BSV.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Three [[B1-series battle droid]]s riding [[Single Trooper Aerial Platform|STAPs]] escort [[TR-350]] riding a Flitknot speeder.]]
[[Count of Serenno|Count]] Dooku used a Flitknot Speeder Bike during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] to escape from the [[Stalgasin hive]] to his secret [[hangar]]. He abandoned the vehicle once he left [[Geonosis]] in [[Count Dooku's solar sailer|his]] [[Punworcca 116-class interstellar sloop|solar sailer]].<ref name="Attack of the Clones" /> He later used a Flitknot to meet [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Mission to Jabba's Palace (Clone Wars)|duel him]] on [[Tatooine]]. Following the [[Lightsaber duel|duel]], Skywalker stole the speeder to escape the [[Dune Sea]] and head for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]'s [[Boba Fett's Palace|Palace]].<ref name="TCWfilm">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>

At least one Flitknot speeder, painted in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[color]] scheme, was present on [[Naboo]], it was used by [[TR-350]] on a patrol prior to the [[Blue Shadow Virus]] [[Raid on Nuvo Vindi's laboratory|incident]].<ref name="Blue Shadow Virus" />

During the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], Flitknot speeders were extensively used by the [[Geonosian]]s,<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> and many [[BX-series droid commando|commando droids]] used Flitknot speeders during a [[battle on Kiros]].<ref name="Kidnapped">{{TCW|Kidnapped}}</ref> [[Clone trooper]]s used captured speeders to go to places on [[Coruscant]]. One was used by [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]] [[Fives]] when he was trying to [[Escape from the Grand Republic Medical Facility|escape]] from other clones to prove his innocence when he was framed for the attempted [[Death|murder]] of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref>

[[File:Pantoran-Security.png|thumb|right|200px|[[Pantoran Security]] in [[Ro Station]] used Flitknot Speeders.]]
The [[Pantoran Security]] rode Flitknot speeders when they responded to a public disturbance in the [[central marketplace]] after [[Fennec Shand]] and [[Clone Force 99]] [[squad leader]] [[Hunter]] fought each other. Two guards witnessed the pair chasing [[Omega]] and chase them in their own police speeders. Shand, subsequently shot and [[Death|killed]] one of the following guards, who then crashed into the second, causing both vehicles to collide with a building and explode. After Hunter rescued Omega and destroy Shand's speeder with an [[pyro denton explosive]], four more guards on police speeders arrived to the scene.<ref name="Cornered" />

[[Rose Tico]] borrows a speeder bike from a [[Mon Calamari officer]] to catch up with a [[Quarren]] who tried to kill her team. She rams the bike into him, knocking him into a crate of crustaceans.<ref name="A4" />
