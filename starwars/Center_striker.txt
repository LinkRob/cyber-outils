'''Center striker''' was one of six positions in the sport of [[grav-ball]]. It was generally considered the game's key position, as the center striker began every play and had the choice in each play of running with the ball, passing it one of the two [[Striker (grav-ball)|strikers]], or handing it off to one of the two [[fullback]]s for a carry.<ref name="edge" /> While these parameters gave the center striker a lot of importance in the game, there were certain limits. For example, the center striker was never allowed to enter the game's scoring area, even if he or she was carrying the [[Grav-ball (ball)|ball]].<ref name="gridiron">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-rebels-edge-of-the-galaxy-and-the-grav-ball-gridiron|text=''Star Wars Rebels: Edge of the Galaxy'' and the Grav-Ball Gridiron}}</ref>

[[Zare Leonis]] was chosen as the center striker for the [[AppSci SaberCats]]. Due to the center striker's key role, Leonis often had to improvise strategy on the fly and call plays.<ref name="edge" /> Only one "coach's time out" was permitted in each triad of the game, with much of the duties that would normally be given to a [[coach]] instead falling to the center striker. As such, Leonis's role as center striker made him the de facto team captain.<ref name="gridiron" />
