The '''Crusaders'''<ref name="Payback" /> were a sect of radical [[Jedi]] led by [[The Grand Master]].<ref name="ProductionBrief" />

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Crusaders first appeared in "[[The Duel: Payback]]", a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kamikaze Douga]] and [[ANIMA (company)|ANIMA]], was released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. The sect was first mentioned in a production brief document for ''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 3, published on [[October 24]], 2025.<ref name="ProductionBrief" />
