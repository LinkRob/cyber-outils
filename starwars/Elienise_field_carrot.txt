'''Elienise field carrots''' were a type of [[carrot]]<ref name="TOTF" /> that originated from<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Elien system]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Roasted Elienise field carrots were [[Diet|edible]] by [[Grand Theljian snow dog]]s, and they were the favorite treat of the Grand Theljian snow [[dog]] [[Plinka]], who was owned by the [[Graf family]] scion [[Xylan Graf]].<ref name="TOTF" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|TOTF}}</ref> Graf's [[Marriage|husband]], [[Cair San Tekka]], activated a secure frequency to contact the [[Ugnaught]] [[pilot]] [[Belin]] while at the [[San Tekka compound]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. The frequency was activated through a code that asked for Plinka's favorite treat and required roasted Elienise field carrots as the response.<ref name="TOTF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Elienise field carrots were mentioned in the [[2024]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="TOTF" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
