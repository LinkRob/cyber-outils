"'''Droid Dilemma'''" is a a mini-comic created by [[Chris Eliopoulos]] and [[Jordie Bellaire]] that was published in [[Star Wars (2015) 25|''Star Wars'' (2015) 25]]. It was in memory of [[Kenny Baker]] and features the character Baker portrayed, [[R2-D2]].

==Plot summary==
As the rebels setup a base on [[LX-Robynsun V]], pilot [[Luke Skywalker]] is ordered to find a missing [[freighter]]. He orders his companion [[droid]] [[R2-D2]] to go set up his [[T-65 X-wing starfighter/Legends|X-wing starfighter]]. Along the way, R2 gets caught up in the base's activities. When he finally arrives at the [[hangar]], Skywalker tells him the mission was canceled.
