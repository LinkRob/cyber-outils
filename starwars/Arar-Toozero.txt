'''Arar-Toozero''' was a [[probe droid]] in service to [[Itai]], a [[Ren prospect|prospect]] to the [[Knights of Ren]].<ref name="Path1" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Arar-Toozero first appeared in the [[Path of the Lightsaber, Vol. 1|first volume]] of ''[[Star Wars: Path of the Lightsaber]]'', a [[Star Wars comic books|manga series]] written and illustrated by [[Kenny Ruiz]] and published by [[Viz Media]]<ref name="Path1" /> on [[November 11]], [[2025]].<ref name="Date">{{Viz|url=manga-books/graphic-novel/star-wars-path-of-the-lightsaber-volume-1/product/8564/paperback|text=Star Wars: Path of the Lightsaber, Vol. 1}}</ref> The probe droid was first pictured in the announcement of the manga series at ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]]'s "Lucasfilm Publishing: Manga Mania!" panel<ref name="Panel">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-lucasfilm-publishing-panel|text=Phase III of ''The High Republic'' And All The Lucasfilm Publishing News from ''Star Wars'' Celebration Japan 2025 &ndash; UPDATE|author=[[Dustin Diehl|Diehl, Dustin]]|date=2025-04-18}}</ref> on [[April 18]] earlier in the year.<ref name="PanelDate">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-japan-2025-panel-schedule|text=Plan Ahead with the ''Star Wars'' Celebration Japan 2025 Panel Schedule|date=2025-03-14}}</ref> The droid was identified as "Arar-Toozero" in the first volume.<ref name="Path1" />
