'''Chief curator''' was a position that the [[Chiss]] [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," claimed to hold<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> in [[19 BBY]]<ref name="CR Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' in [[19 BBY]].</ref> while [[Primea diplomatic reception|traveling]] undercover at the [[Vak Combine]] [[homeworld]] of [[Primea]]. The [[Chiss officer]] posed as "[[Artistic Master]] Svorno," chief [[curator]] of the [[Nunech Art Collection]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
