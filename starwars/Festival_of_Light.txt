The '''Festival of Light''' was a [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] holiday held [[Standard year|annually]] since the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] joined the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Crisis on Naboo" /> in [[867 BBY]].<ref name="Crisis date">In "[[Crisis on Naboo]]," [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] was kidnapped by [[Cad Bane]] "eight-hundred and forty-seven years [after] Naboo joined the Republic." According to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', the kidnapping took place in [[20 BBY]]. Therefore, Naboo joined the Republic in 867 BBY.</ref>

During the [[Clone Wars]], in [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[Festival of Light (847th)|eight hundred and forty-seventh Festival of Light]] saw a group of [[bounty hunter]]s hired by [[Count]] [[Dooku]] attempting to [[Plan to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine|kidnap]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Crisis on Naboo" />
