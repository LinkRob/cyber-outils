The '''defense of Murkhana City''' took place<ref name="Collapse" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]] of the [[Clone Wars]], in [[Murkhana City]] on<ref name="Collapse" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[Murkhana]],<ref name="Collapse" /> which had been the site of conflict over the course of the war through a [[Battle of Murkhana|battle]] nine [[Standard month|months]] into the war<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> in [[21 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the [[mission to Mortis]] and the [[Battle of Mon Cala]] to [[20 BBY]]. As {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}} places "[[The Citadel (episode)|The Citadel]]," "[[Counterattack]]" and "[[Citadel Rescue]]" between these events, the [[Battle of Lola Sayu]] must also occur in 20 BBY. Because [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] dates the Battle of [[Murkhana]] to be a [[Standard year|year]] before the Battle of [[Lola Sayu]], it can be dated to [[21 BBY]].</ref> and a prolonged [[Bombing of Murkhana|bombardment campaign]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> Targeted by the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Corporate Alliance]] defended the [[city]], with the operations [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Magistrate]] [[Passel Argente]]. The defensive received additional support from the [[Vibroblade Brigade]], a [[mercenary]] group occasionally contracted by the corporation for protections and other operations.<ref name="Collapse" />

==Behind the scenes==
The defense of Murkhana City was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Collapse" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Battle of Murkhana (Clone Wars)|defensive]] was originally introduced in ''[[Dark Lord: The Rise of Darth Vader]]'', a novel authored by [[James Luceno]] and published in [[2005]].<ref name="Dark Lord">''[[Dark Lord: The Rise of Darth Vader]]''</ref>
