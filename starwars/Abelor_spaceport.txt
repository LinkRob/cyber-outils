A dusty '''spaceport''' was located on<ref name="ACOF" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Abelor]].<ref name="ACOF" /> At some point between [[33 ABY]] and [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]]. The novel establishes that [[Izal Garsea]] had met [[Damar Olin]] less than a [[Standard year|year]] prior to the novel's events, meaning the pair's encounter with [[Ana Tolla's crew]] must have been between [[33 ABY]] and 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Damar Olin]] encountered the smuggler [[Ana Tolla]] and her [[Ana Tolla's crew|crew]] after he and his partner [[Izal Garsea]] became stranded with no [[fuel]], [[Foodstuff|food]], or contacts in the [[spaceport]].<ref name="ACOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The spaceport was mentioned in the [[2019]] young-adult novel ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="ACOF" />
