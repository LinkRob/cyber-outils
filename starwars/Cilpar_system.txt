The '''Cilpar system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where an [[Attack in the Cilpar system|attack]] occurred between [[Draedan pirates]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="SWA 11">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=11|story=Squad Goals, Part One}}</ref> It was situated at the coordinates L-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Cilpar system first appeared in "[[Squad Goals, Part One]]", which was published in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 11|eleventh]] issue<ref name="SWA 11" /> of the [[2020]] comic book series [[Star Wars Adventures (2020)|''Star Wars Adventures'']]. Written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Manuel Bracchi]], the story was published on [[October 19]], [[2021]].<ref name="Comixology">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2020-11/digital-comic/957990|text=Star Wars Adventures (2020-) #11|nolive=1}}</ref>
