'''Bilemaws''' were large quadrupedal creatures that lived on the [[planet]] of [[Koboh]]. They were covered in a hard exterior, which makes them resistant to most conventional attacks. Additionally, bilemaws could regurgitate acidic spit, known as "[[bile]]," to any enemies within their way. Bilemaws made their homes in the many crags, [[cave]]s, and crevices on the surface of Koboh. They had a notoriously bad temper, leading to rampages that would trample, crush, or pulverize those caught in their way.<ref name="Survivor" />

Some bilemaws would frequently lounge in pools of [[Volcano|volcanic]] mud that bubbled up through fissures from deep beneath the surface of Koboh. The creatures would get coated in the substance, which would harden into a second skin, reminiscent of the [[basalt]] columns [[Basalt Forest|where the pools were found]].<ref name="Survivor" />
