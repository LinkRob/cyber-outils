During the [[Separatist Crisis]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]<ref name="Episode II" /> and his [[Padawan]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], were involved in a very important mission<ref name="Anakin Encyclopedia" /> concerning a '''border dispute on Ansion''',<ref name="Episode II" /> which left [[the galaxy]] staggered.<ref name="Tarkin" /> Upon returning from the [[Ansion]] dispute, Skywalker and Kenobi became involved in trying to stop the [[plot to assassinate Senator Amidala]].<ref name="Episode II" />

==Behind the scenes==
The border dispute on Ansion first appeared in ''[[The Approaching Storm]]'', a [[2002]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel written by [[Alan Dean Foster]] as a tie-in to the 2002 film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="The Approaching Storm">''[[The Approaching Storm]]''</ref> It became [[canon]] when it was mentioned in the aforementioned film.<ref name="Episode II" />
