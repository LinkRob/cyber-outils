The '''Enselon system''' was located in grid square L-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] as part of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="The Interior" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Enselon [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Enselon]] was the home of the [[Ensel]] [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] [[Alta Ranga]], mentioned in the [[2008]] reference book ''[[The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="CSWE">{{CSWECite|III|78|Ranga, Alta}}</ref>
