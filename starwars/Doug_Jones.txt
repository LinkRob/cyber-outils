'''Doug Jones''' was a negative supervisor at [[Industrial Light & Magic]] for the special editions of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']],<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']],<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. He also assisted with film scanning and recording on [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].
