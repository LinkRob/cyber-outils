'''Bunn''' was a [[foodstuff]] that was featured in most meals on [[Skuhl]]. It consisted of long, sticky [[grain]]s and was prepared in a steamer, cooking overnight. At breakfast time, it was sometimes enjoyed with [[egg]]s and [[caf]]. [[Hadder Ponta]] was able to make bunn. Diners on Skuhl also served bunn.<ref name="rising" />
