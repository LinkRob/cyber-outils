'''Blasty''' was the name of a [[Clone gunner/Legends|Clone tank gunner]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].<ref name="By any other name" />

==Biography==
Like all other [[Cloning/Legends|clones]], Blasty was a clone of [[Jango Fett/Legends|Jango Fett]].<ref name="SWE" /> He was a clone gunner who served in the Clone Wars. He was a very obedient trooper, and, according to clone [[1139]], a kiss up. Blasty was also the only clone in his group with a nickname. Blasty had good skills and could hit a [[OG-9 homing spider droid/Legends|spider droid]] in the eye from a distance.<ref name="By any other name" /> He also knew how to drive a tank, even if it was a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Separatist]] [[Armored Assault Tank/Legends|tank]].<ref name="Surrender">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/comicl12.aspx|text="Clones don't even know the meaning of the word surrender"|int=Clones don't even know the meaning of the word surrender|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref>

===Hunt for R2-D2===
Blasty was in the same squadron with clone troopers [[1137]] and 1139 during the hunt for R2-D2. 1139 hated Blasty. When Blasty took down a spider droid by himself, he got praise from all of his squadron except 1139.<ref name="By any other name" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the first comic he is in, Blasty has an antenna on his helmet. He is only seen with it once, and the rest of the time he has a plain helmet.<ref name="A droid in the hand" />
