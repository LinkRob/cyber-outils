'''134 BBY''', or '''134<ref name="YordDate" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> During that year,<ref name="YordDate" /> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Yord Fandar]] passed his [[Jedi Trials]], ascending to the rank of [[Jedi Knight]]. [[132 BBY|Two years later]], Fandar mentioned the event to his friend, former Padawan [[Verosha Aniseya|Osha Aniseya]], after reuniting with her.<ref name="LF" />

In 134 ABY,<ref name="MaeDate" /> the [[Force-sensitive]] [[assassin]] [[Mae-ho Aniseya|Mae Aniseya]] met<ref name="Acolyte VG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> the secret<ref name="Night">{{Acolyte|Night}}</ref> [[Sith Lord]] known only as "[[the Stranger]]"<ref name="Acolyte Companion">{{InsiderCite|229|Star Wars: The Acolyte Companion|''Star Wars: The Acolyte'' Companion}}</ref> and "Qimir" who posed as the Stranger's subordinate,<ref name="Acolyte VG" /> yet in reality was the Stranger's alter ego.<ref name="Night" /> Aniseya then began an intense training regimen under the Stranger that lasted [[132 BBY|two years]], focusing her anger into a weapon.<ref name="Acolyte VG" /> Around 134 ABY,<ref name="UedaDate">The events of ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'' take place in [[132 BBY]] per the reasoning [[:Template:TheAcolyteDate|here]]. ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' places the start of [[Indara]]'s posting on [[Ueda]] to two [[Standard year|years]] before the series, equating to around 134 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]] was assigned to [[Point Ueda]] on the [[planet]] [[Ueda]].<ref name="Acolyte VG" />

==Behind the scenes==
134 BBY was first indirectly mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="LF" /> the first episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] series [[Star Wars: The Acolyte|''The Acolyte'']], which aired on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="EPG">{{SW|url=series/the-acolyte/lost-found-episode-guide|text="Lost / Found" Episode Guide &#124; The Acolyte}}</ref> That approximate [[134 BBY/Legends|in-universe year]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in the "[[Operation Blue Star]]" adventure of [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] campaign.<ref name="LegendsDate">The adventure "[[Operation Blue Star]]" of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] campaign establishes [[Blue Star Centre]] to have been founded 103 [[Standard year/Legends|years]] prior. The ''[[Living Force Campaign Guide]]'' dates the beginning of the campaign to one year after the events of {{Film|I}}, which translates to around [[31 BBY/Legends|31 BBY]] per ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'', while "[[Below the Belt: Philology]]" shifts the setting of the campaign ten years forward, that is, to around [[21 BBY/Legends|21 BBY]]. "Operation Blue Star" is set before the events of "Below the Belt: Philology," therefore it must be set around 31 [[Galactic Standard Calendar|BBY]] and Blue Star Centre must have been established around [[134 BBY/Legends|134 BBY]].</ref> The adventure was written by [[Megan Robertson]]<ref name="OBS">{{LivingForce|Operation Blue Star}}</ref> and released in [[2001]].<ref name="LegendsReleaseDate">{{Wizards|url=rpga/livingforce/article.asp?x=lf/swlf20011115a3_release&c=swlfc|text="Law and Order" scenario schedule for September 2001 - December 2001}}</ref>
