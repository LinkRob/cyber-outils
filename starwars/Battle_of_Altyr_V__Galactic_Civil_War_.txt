The '''Battle of Altyr V''' was one of the first conflicts between the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] and the [[Dark Empire]]. It was a part of the [[Operation Shadow Hand]] campaign.

==Overview==
[[Altyr V]] was one of the battlefronts of the struggling [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]]. However, remnants of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]] decided it was an ideal target to halt the Republic's advent. They had modified two [[Asteroid/Legends|asteroids]] of the [[Altyr system/Legends|Altyr system]] as bombardiers and hoped to conquer the planet as a base.<ref name="MotS" />

New Republic heroes [[Kyle Katarn]] and [[Mara Jade Skywalker|Mara Jade]] were on-station when the attack came, practicing [[Lightsaber combat/Legends|lightsaber combat]].<ref name="MotS" />

==Battle==
The New Republic [[Commander/Legends|commander]] told the duo that two asteroids were taking up an orbit around the planet and bombarding it while [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] were being landed; more reinforcements would arrive in two hours, sealing the fate of the base. Katarn thought that if a landing craft could be stolen, it could be landed on the asteroid and the asteroids could be destroyed. Mara Jade stayed to coordinate defenses.<ref name="MotS" />

Katarn moved through the base, assisting Republic soldiers. While wandering in the base, he released a hangared [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor/Legends|A-wing]] which fell on the heads of stormtroopers, crushing them. Stormtroopers continued to pour through the base and the Republic commander ordered Kyle to shut the [[Blast door/Legends|blast doors]]. However, right before they shut, a [[TIE/sa tactical bomber/Legends|TIE/sa bomber]] blasted them. Nevertheless, Katarn kept going and defeated several stormtroopers and [[All Terrain Scout Transport/Legends|AT-STs]] and restored power to sections of the base. Eventually he got to the [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle/Legends|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] and killed the stormtroopers guarding it. Then, he flew to the asteroid base. There he battled through the personnel until he got to the firing center where he had it fire at and destroy the other asteroid. He then set the asteroid to self destruct and left.<ref name="MotS" />
