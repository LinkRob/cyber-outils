The '''black traveling gown'''<ref name="Name note">The name "traveling gown" is used in ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'' for both the [[Color|black]] gown worn by [[Sabé]] in disguise and the purple gown worn by [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]; the latter is also called "traveling gown" in ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''. While the purple one receives a more complete identification as the "dark purple traveling dress" in ''[[Queen's Peril]]'', the subject of this article has not received a more distinct name; thus, a conjectural name is used to disguish which traveling gown it is.</ref> was owned by [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] as part of her extensive [[Padmé Amidala's wardrobe|wardrobe]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> The ornate [[Color|black]]<ref name="Episode I" /> traveling gown<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was woven with enough [[Karlini silk]] to protect the wearer, as well as anyone standing behind her, from [[blaster]] bolts.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> The dress had web-like black designs layered over gold-colored fabric, and the skirt had the [[symbol of the Royal House of Naboo]] in gold as a repeated pattern around the hem.<ref name="Episode I" /> The royal gown and headdress<ref name="TCVD NE">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> were worn by Amidala's [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] [[Sabé]] when she acted as the Queen's decoy during the [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="Episode I" />

==Behind the scenes==
The costume was created for the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]], [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], where it was worn by [[Keira Knightley]] as Sabé.<ref name="Episode I" /> The finished costume was realized by costume designer [[Trisha Biggar]]. Though not worn by [[Natalie Portman]] in the film, the finished costume was photographed with Portman modeling it. Identified in ''[[Dressing a Galaxy: The Costumes of Star Wars]]'' as "Travel Gown I," the costume included handmade lace fabric and a vintage jet-beaded back panel.<ref name="Dressing a Galaxy">''[[Dressing a Galaxy: The Costumes of Star Wars]]''</ref> The spider web-like lace fabric took over a month for one person, working ten hours for five days a week, to create. Originally, the dress was sewn onto a special thin fabric backing, which was then dissolved in water to leave only the stitching on top. The dress was seamless, with each panel of the dress stitched directly onto another panel.<ref name="Costume Design">{{SWArchive|url=episode-i/feature/19990501/indexp10.html|text=''Star Wars'' Episode I: Production Notes: Costume Design}}</ref>
