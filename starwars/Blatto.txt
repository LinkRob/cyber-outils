==Behind the scenes==
Blatto was voiced by [[Christopher Corey Smith]] in ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'', a non-[[canon]] special crossover with the [[Disney Channel]] series [[:w:c:phineasandferb:Phineas and Ferb|''Phineas and Ferb'']].<ref name="Phineas" /> The special premiered on [[July 26]], [[2014]].<ref name="Reporter">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/live-feed/phineas-ferbs-star-wars-special-711433|text='Phineas and Ferb's' 'Star Wars' Special Lands Premiere Date|author=Ng, Philiana|date=2014-06-12|work=The Hollywood Reporter|archivedate=20201205100409}}</ref> [[Wikipedia:Television director|Director]] [[Robert F. Hughes]] later confirmed that Blatto was the son of [[Watto]],<ref name="Twitter" /> a Toydarian from the [[1999]] film {{Film|I}}, the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>
