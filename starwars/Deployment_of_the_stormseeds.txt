In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> organization of [[Pirate|marauders]] known as the [[Nihil]] and their leader, [[Marchion Ro]], '''deployed''' powerful weapons known as [[stormseed]]s, which had the power to destroy any [[starship]]s traveling through [[hyperspace]] in their area of deployment. With their activation in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], the Nihil successfully prevented the [[Galactic Republic]] from being able to reach ten full [[sector]]s of the Outer Rim, and thus secured the territory as their own,<ref name="Eye 2" /> which became known as the [[Occlusion Zone]].<ref name="Timelines" /> The Republic quickly learned this when their [[Republic Defense Coalition Outreach Fleet]] that was traveling to the Outer Rim to support Outer Rim worlds was destroyed in hyperspace during the stormseeds' deployment.<ref name="Eye 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
The deployment of the stormseeds appeared in the [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 2|second issue]] of the [[2022]] comic series ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Eye of the Storm]]'', written by [[Charles Soule]] for ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia initiative.<ref name="Eye 2" />
