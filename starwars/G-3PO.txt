'''G-3PO''' was a [[3PO-series protocol droid|3PO]] [[protocol droid]] aboard a [[starship]] that was attacked by [[pirate]]s. Along with several other [[droid]]s, including [[Z-3PO]] and [[B-3PO]], G-3PO discovered [[Pax Maripher]], the only survivor of the attack. The protocol droids raised Maripher until he was rescued fifteen [[Standard year|years]] later, whereupon the droids returned to their regular duties.<ref name="MA" />
