'''Chaff Korus''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who was a [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] at the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He flew as part of [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]] under the [[callsign]] [[Green Six]]. Shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]], he participated in an [[Battle of Scarif|attack]] on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]] to help [[Battle of Scarif|secure]] the [[Death Star plans|plans]] for the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" />
