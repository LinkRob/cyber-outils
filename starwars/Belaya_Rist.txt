'''Belaya Rist''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual who was the CEO of [[CryonCorp]] and was also a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] sympathizer. After witnessing the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] from [[Delaya]] nearby, she invited the Alliance to hijack several shipments of [[low-feedback scanner]]s and was a close ally of secret Alliance leader [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]].<ref name="Forged" />

==Behind the scenes==
Belaya Rist was first mentioned in the [[2016]] sourcebook ''[[Forged in Battle]]'', a supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] game.<ref name="Forged" />
