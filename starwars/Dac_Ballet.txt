The '''Dac Ballet''' was a ballet company that shared its name with<ref name="Script" /> [[Mon Cala|Dac]], the [[Waterworld|aquatic]] [[homeworld]] of two [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]: the [[Quarren]] and [[Mon Calamari]].<ref name="Mon Cala">{{Databank|mon-cala|Mon Cala}}</ref> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] was [[Battle of Galov|captured]] by [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Republic Defense Coalition|forces]] and [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] on the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]]. There, she was interrogated by the station's [[Head of Security]], the Mon Calamari [[Ghal Tarpfen]]. By observing Tarpfen's posture, Dee identified her as having once been part of either the [[Mon Calamari Guard|Mon Calamari Royal Guard]] or the Dac Ballet, and expressed doubt that Tarpfen danced ballet.<ref name="Script" /> Indeed, Tarpfen had once been a member of the Royal Guard.<ref name="Shadows Remain">{{InsiderCite|issue1=207|issue2=208|Starlight: Shadows Remain}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Dac Ballet was mentioned in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', a [[2021]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The spelling of the ballet's name was later confirmed with the release of the audio drama's [[The High Republic: Tempest Runner (script)|script]] in [[2022]].<ref name="Script" />
