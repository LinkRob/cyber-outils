'''Diva Shaliqua''' was a half-[[Theelin]] [[Gender|female]] [[Musician|singer]] enslaved by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]. She was one of his favorites and accompanied him to events. She often wore revealing [[Clothing|outfits]], which Jabba was fond of.<ref name="Geektionary" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] to [[32 BBY]].</ref> Shaliqua attended the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]] alongside Jabba and his [[Twi'lek]] [[majordomo]] [[Bib Fortuna]], where she was standing next to him and wearing a [[dancing-girl costume]] when he announced the race's beginning. After the young [[Slavery|slave]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] won the event, Jabba fell asleep, and Shaliqua left the viewing box. Shaliqua had pale skin and [[Color|blue]] [[hair]].<ref name="TPM" />
