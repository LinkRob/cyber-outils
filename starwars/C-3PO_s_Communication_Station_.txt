"'''C-3PO's Communication Station!'''" is a recurring department in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]''. The magazine's letters page, ''Communication Station'' premiered in [[Star Wars Insider 149|''Star Wars Insider'' 149]], and has appeared in every issue since.

In the feature, ''Star Wars Insider ''readers write in about ''Star Wars'' related topics.
