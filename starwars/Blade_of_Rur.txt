The '''blade of Rur''' was a [[Color|blue]]-bladed [[lightsaber]] owned by [[Rur]], leader of the [[Ordu Aspectu]]. Like its owner, who belonged to a splinter sect of the [[Jedi Order]], that lightsaber was unorthodox, as its [[blade emitter]] was pierced with holes through which smaller, spike-like secondary blades were projected, forming a star-shaped collar at the basis of the primary blade. It was utilized by [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] to defend [[Gender|herself]] against and destroy the original [[droid]] body of [[Eternal Rur]],<ref name="Doctor Aphra 6" /> and later by Eternal Rur itself in a new body in conjunction with another, more traditional blue lightsaber.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" />
