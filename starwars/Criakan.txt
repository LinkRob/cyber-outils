'''Criakan'''<ref name="Secrets" /> was a [[Southern Mustafarian]] who lived on [[Mustafar]]. He led an attack on the [[Fortress Vader|fortress]] of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], but was [[Death|killed]] by the [[Sith Lord]].<ref name="TFVC 5" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Criakan first appeared in a flashback section of the [[2018]] [[canon]] comic [[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 5|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 5]], written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Charles Paul Wilson III]].<ref name="TFVC 5" /> According to Scott, he and Wilson had discussed what the appearance of a Southern Mustafarian without their [[helmet]] would be, and had also created a short backstory for Criakan.<ref name="Secrets" />
