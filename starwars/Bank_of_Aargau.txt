The '''Bank of Aargau''' was a bank that was broken into by a crew run by [[Zabrak]] criminal [[Easy Xiukhan]].<ref name="SWU" /> It utilized gigantic [[Seventh-stage bank-courier repulsor-truck|bank-courier repulsor trucks]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Bank of Aargau was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Canon]] by the [[2015]] game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', where it was mentioned in a quote found in the description for the unique crew member Easy Xiukhan.<ref name="SWU" />

The [[Bank of Aargau/Legends|Bank of Aargau]] was originally created for the [[1981]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] comic [[Star Wars (1977) 48|''Star Wars'' (1977) 48]] by [[Marvel Comics]].<ref name="SW48">[[Star Wars (1977) 48|''Star Wars'' (1977) 48]]</ref>
