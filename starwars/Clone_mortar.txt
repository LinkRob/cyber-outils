The '''Clone mortar'''<ref name="Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/carnage-of-krell-concept-art-gallery|text="Carnage of Krell" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> was a short-range weapon utilised by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. The weapon launched explosive projectiles upon targets at a close distance along an arcing trajectory. In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Star Wars: Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> during the [[Battle of Umbara]], the [[501st Legion]] used the weapon to attack what they believed to be native [[Umbaran]]s, but they subsequently realized that they had been deceived into attacking their own men from the [[212th Attack Battalion]].<ref name="TCWS4-CoK" />
