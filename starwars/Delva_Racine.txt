'''Delva Racine''' was a [[human]] [[Fashion designer|high-fashion designer]] during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="Complete Visual New" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="III Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|III}} to [[19 BBY]].</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], they were walking up the steps of<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> the [[Galaxies Opera House]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> with another human patron during a performance of<ref name="ROTS" /> ''[[Squid Lake]]''<ref name="USWNE" /> when the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] ran past them into the [[opera]] house to meet [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="ROTS" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the planet [[Alderaan]] owned a [[Delva Racine original hanging sleeve dress]] as part of her diplomatic wardrobe.<ref name="G&G">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref> She wore the [[Clothing|dress]] during the [[Royal Award Ceremony]] on<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> the [[jungle]] [[moon]]<ref name="Y4DB">{{Databank|yavin-4|Yavin 4}}</ref> [[Yavin 4]]<ref name="Episode IV" /> in [[0 ABY]],<ref name="IV Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IV}} the take place after the [[Battle of Yavin]] to [[0 ABY]].</ref> held to commemorate the heroes of the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> Racine was fair-skinned with brown [[hair]] buns,<ref name="Card Trader" /> a hairstyle which Organa would also later wear.<ref name="Episode IV" /> While at the opera, they wore a pair of [[gold]]en-colored [[goggles]], a neckpiece, and a horn-like mask that covered their [[nose]] and [[mouth]].<ref name="Card Trader" /> They also wore a gray tunic and [[silver]]-colored raised shoes.<ref name="HC">{{HelmetCollectionCite|16|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Delva Racine appeared in the [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="ROTS" /> released on [[May 19]], [[2005]].<ref name="YBY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> She was played by real-life British fashion designer [[Alice Shreeve]], who is the creative director of the London-based women's wear label [https://www.belleandbunty.co.uk Belle & Bunty].<ref name="LifestyleTails">{{WebCite|url=http://www.lifestyletails.com/alice-shreeve-belle-and-bunty|text=Alice Shreeve and Pearl the Boston Terrier|author=Glazin, Simon|partialdate=2019|work=[http://www.lifestyletails.com Lifestyle Tails]|archivedate=20190405220402}}</ref> The Galaxies Opera House stair scenes including Racine were shot on the R Stage at [[Shepperton Studios]] in [[Wikipedia:Shepperton|Shepperton]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]] during additional photography on [[August 23]], [[2004]].<ref name="Making Of">''[[The Making of Star Wars Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref>

In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Racine's name was revealed in [[2016]] by the [[Star Wars video games|mobile application]] [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]].<ref name="Card Trader" /> The [[Delva Racine/Legends|name]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was introduced in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[James Luceno]]<ref name="RotS VD">''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and released prior to the film on [[April 2]], 2005.<ref name="Visual Dictionary Amazon">{{DK|url=us/9780756611286-star-wars-revenge-of-the-sith-the-visual-dictionary|text=Star Wars Revenge of the Sith: The Visual Dictionary|nolive=1}}</ref>
