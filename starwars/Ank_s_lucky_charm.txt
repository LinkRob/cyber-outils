'''Ank's lucky charm''' was a charm made from two twisted [[Explosive charge|explosive wires]], one [[color|red]] and one green. It was made by [[Pirrip]] for his [[marriage|spouse]], [[Ank Parako]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Parako was imprisoned by the [[Ashiga Clan]] and forced to build explosives at their [[Ashiga weapons factory|weapons factory]] on [[Kijimi]]. When the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] broke into the weapons factory in order to free Parako, Parako sent Vess to fetch the charm from the locker room she had left it in before she would leave.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ank's lucky charm appeared as a quest item in the "Breakout" main quest of the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
