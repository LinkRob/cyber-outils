'''Erebus''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Juggernaut" /> located in the [[Erebus system]] in the [[Colonies]].<ref name="Appendix" /> A [[mountain]]ous and muddy world, Erebus had valuable resources that would be extracted by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] strip mining, which left the terrain even more barren and potholed.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|erebus|Erebus}}</ref> Erebus housed an [[Imperial labor camp]] which utilized [[prisoner]]s for their mining operations.<ref name="Juggernaut" />

During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|312}}</ref> [[Clone Force 99]] landed on Erebus to infiltrate the labor camp and [[Rescue of Edmon Rampart|extract]] former [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]] in order to acquire the coordinates of [[Tantiss Base]] from him.<ref name="Juggernaut" />
