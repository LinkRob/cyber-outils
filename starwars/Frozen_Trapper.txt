The '''Frozen Trapper''' was a costume owned by the [[Trandoshan]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Charr]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Cunning Trapper|standard costume]]. This cold weather outfit consisted of layers of [[Color|white]] [[Hair|fur]], potentially the pelt of a [[tauntaun]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Frozen Trapper was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Charr, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Icy Scatter Gun]].<ref name="Hunters" />
