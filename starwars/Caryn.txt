'''Caryn''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[smuggler]] and fence who operated during the [[Galactic Civil War]] and the subsequent [[New Republic Era|time]] of the [[New Republic]]. She was invited to the [[Kijimi summit]] by [[Zeva Bliss]] of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]], who intended to [[Death|kill]] her guests. Bliss's plans were stopped by Spice Runner [[Poe Dameron]], and the criminals were saved. Once free, Caryn attempted to run to her [[starship]] alongside several other guests.<ref name="Free Fall" />
