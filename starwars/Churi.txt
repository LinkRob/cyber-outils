The '''churi''' was a large [[Bird|avian]] native to [[Endor]], a [[forest]]ed [[moon]] [[orbit]]ing a [[planet]] of the [[Endor (planet)|same name]]. [[Logray]], the [[shaman]] of the [[Ewok]] [[Bright Tree Village]], wore a headdress made from the [[Bone|skull]] of a churi<ref name="Card Trader" /> and his apprentice [[Teebo]] wore churi feathers atop a [[gurreck]] skull headdress.<ref name="Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded>''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> The ewoks used a tool known as a [[churi bird caller]] to call churi birds.<ref name="Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>
