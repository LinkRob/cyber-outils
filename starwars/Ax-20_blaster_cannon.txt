The '''Ax-20 blaster cannon''' was a model of small [[blaster cannon]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]]. The weapon was capable of firing repeated blasts without overheating and consisted of a long [[barrel]] with a widened neck around the muzzle.<ref name="Complete" />

[[Aratech Repulsor Company]]'s [[74-Z speeder bike]] was equipped with an Ax-20 that was fixed on a rotating mount underneath the chassis of vehicle and protruded out under the control vanes at the front of the vehicle. Two [[power coupling]] lines attached the rear of the [[blaster]] to a series of six [[power cell]]s that were themselves each attached to a [[power cell recharger]]. The cannon could be controlled by the [[speeder bike]]'s [[pilot]] using firing controls in the right steering handle.<ref name="Complete" />
