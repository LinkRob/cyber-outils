'''Crys-anoblue-7255''' was the name of a particular shade of [[Color|blue]] in the [[Lantillian paintworks coding system]]. When the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' belonged to [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], its outer paint scheme featured highlights in that shade.<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
