'''Daniel Davis''', born on [[November 26]], [[1940s|1945]] in [[Wikipedia:Gurdon|Gurdon]], [[Wikipedia:Arkansas|Arkansas]], is an [[Wikipedia:United States of America|American]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]].<ref name="Playbill profile" /> His best known roles include Niles the butler on the popular sitcom [[Wikipedia:The Nanny|''The Nanny'']]<ref name="The Nanny">{{WebCite|url=http://thenanny.com/nannystory.html|text=The Story of The Nanny, FAQ & Trivia|archivedate=20201130192220}}</ref> and [[W:c:memory-alpha:James Moriarty|Professor James Moriarty]] in [[W:c:memory-alpha:Elementary, Dear Data (episode)|two]] [[W:c:memory-alpha:Ship In A Bottle (episode)|episodes]] of ''Star Trek: The Next Generation''.<ref name="">{{Twitter|username=TheTrekCentral|url=status/1578853358907318272|name=Trek Central|quote=NEW - Daniel Davis As PROFESOR MORIARTY!|date=2022-10-8|archivedate=20221015001012}}</ref>

In [[2012]], Davis narrated the [[Darth Plagueis (audiobook)|audiobook]] of [[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']].<ref name="Darth Plagueis">[[Darth Plagueis (audiobook)|audiobook]]</ref> In the following years, Davis would join other Star Wars voice artist for the audiobooks in the ''[[William Shakespeare's Star Wars]]'' series.<ref name="Shakespeare Hope">[[William Shakespeare's Star Wars: Verily, A New Hope (audiobook)|''William Shakespeare's Star Wars: Verily, A New Hope'']]</ref> Davis would return to Star Wars in [[2023]] to narrate the short story "[[To the Last]]" in the [[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi (audiobook)|audiobook]] of ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="FaCPoV Return">[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi (audiobook)|''From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi'']]</ref>
