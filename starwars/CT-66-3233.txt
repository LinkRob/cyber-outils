'''CT-66-3233''' was a [[clone cadet]] who served in [[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]], a training [[Squad|unit]] on [[Kamino]], during the [[Clone Wars]]. They were marked with a one on their [[clone training armor]].

==Biography==
CT-66-3233 was a [[Cloning|clone]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] to serve in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. Early during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], CT-66-3233 was attached to [[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]], a [[squad]] of [[clone cadet]]s training on Kamino. During their [[time]] training, Bravo [[Squad]] managed to complete the practice examination in record time. When [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Colt]] arrived to watch over the final exams, Bravo Squad, along with [[Domino Squad]] and two other units, were called to a [[hangar]] in [[Tipoca City Military Complex]] for Colt's speech. During the speech, Colt selected Bravo Squad for the final test first due to their record time in completing the practice test.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

Commencing the [[Citadel Challenge]] version THX variable 1138, CT-66-3233 and [[CT-66-3232]] took the left flank while [[CT-66-3238]], [[CT-66-3339]], and [[CT-27-3555]] took the right flank. CT-66-3233 provided cover fire while the rest of their teammates moved forward, taking out the training [[battle droid]]s. As the entire squad began moving forward, CT-66-3233 took out a training [[BX-series droid commando]] in close proximity. As Bravo Squad moved forward, [[Fives|CT-27-5555]] commented that he and Domino Squad were better as they watched from above. Eventually, CT-66-3339 told CT-66-3238 to move forward to the citadel, and was able to rappel up and capture the light post, completing the exam. While exiting the arena, CT-66-3233, who was the first in line, mocked the incoming Domino Squad.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Personality and traits==
CT-66-3233, as a clone of Jango Fett, was 1.83 [[meter]]s tall, had [[Color|tan]] skin, black [[hair]], and was a [[human]] born on Kamino. As they belonged to Bravo Squad who were considered exemplary, CT-66-3233 unprovokingly mocked Domino Squad, a unit who had a rough record.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Skills and abilities==
CT-66-3233 was able to coordinate effectively with the rest of their teammates, which helped them to pass the final exam. During the exam, they were able to provide cover fire for their squadmates.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Equipment==
CT-66-3233 was equipped with [[clone training armor]], sporting the red coloring of their unit, the number one on their chest plate and left shoulder [[armor]], had their designation on their left leg, and utilized a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] with non-lethal bolts and an [[ascension cable]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Behind the scenes==
This Bravo Squad cadet's identification number was first depicted in the episode "[[Clone Cadets]]" of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''. It says 66-3233, and the prefix CT- was predetermined. This way of determining identification numbers was proved with the troopers in [[Domino Squad]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />
