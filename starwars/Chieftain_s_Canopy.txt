The '''Chieftain's Canopy''' was an area situated at the top of the [[Origin Tree]] on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]]. Long before the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Zeffonian]]s visited Kashyyyk, and left an [[Astrium]] in the Chieftain's Canopy. The [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]], guided by the [[Wookiee]] [[chief]]tain [[Tarfful]], climbed the [[tree]] and retrieved the Astrium. A number of [[Standard year|years]] later, the former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] retraced Cordova's steps.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
