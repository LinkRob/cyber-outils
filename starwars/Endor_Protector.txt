The '''Endor Protector''' was a bodily configuration equipped by the [[droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[J-3DI]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Noble Jedi|standard configuration]]. It was designed to resemble the appearance of the [[Ewok]] [[species]] native to the [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]].<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Protector was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter J-3DI, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.18.0 during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Protector Saber Design]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing either the Ewok Bundle or the Yub Nub Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
