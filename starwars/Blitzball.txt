'''Blitzball''' was a [[water]] sport. In [[22 BBY]], it was played in a [[Unidentified lake (Langston)|lake]] on the [[planet]] [[Langston]] by a group of [[youngling]]s. [[19 BBY|Three years later]], during the [[Barsha family]]'s [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] to the [[moon]] [[Gus Treta]], former [[Barsha Corporation]] executive [[Nnytyl Barsha]] began fervently watching copious amounts of blitzball played by a [[Unidentified regional blitzball circuit|regional blitzball circuit]].

==Description==
Blitzball was a type of [[water]] sport that could be played in matches.<ref name="Brotherhood" />

==History==
{{Quote|That's enough! All of this. I am sick of it. I'm going to watch blitzball and forget any of this ever happened.|Nnytyl Barsha, shortly before his death|<ref name="LRM" />}}
In the [[Republic Era]], blitzball was played by [[youngling]]s on the [[planet]] [[Langston]].<ref name="Brotherhood" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] to [[22 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Mill Alibeth]] went on a [[Aid mission to Langston|mission]] to [[Langston's mining district]]. There, they witnessed a group of younglings playing the game in a [[Unidentified lake (Langston)|lake]] as they walked through the rubble.<ref name="Brotherhood" />

[[File:ND5full-OutlawsVCG.png|thumb|right|200px|ND-5 (pictured) observed Nnytyl Barsha's obsession with blitzball while on Gus Treta.]]
During the [[Barsha family]]'s [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] to the [[moon]] [[Gus Treta]]<ref name="LRM">''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]''</ref> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">{{LRMDate|1|blitzball}}</ref> former [[Barsha Corporation]] executive [[Nnytyl Barsha]] spent copious amounts of time watching the [[Unidentified regional blitzball circuit|regional blitzball circuit]], which his [[Gender|son]], [[Jaylen Vrax]], took note of. One time<ref name="LRM" /> that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date2" /> Nnytyl cheered loudly during a blitzball broadcast. Jaylen became annoyed as the noise partially drowned out his own conversation with the [[BX-series droid commando]] [[ND-5]], a [[droid]] that, unbeknownst to the former, was secretly working for Jaylen's half-brother, [[Sliro Barsha]], to [[Death|assassinate]] the Barsha family. Not long afterward, ND-5 reviewed a record containing information on the Barsha family's activities, which included a note about Nnytyl's blitzball obsession.<ref name="LRM" />

The Barsha family eventually planned to leave Gus Treta, hosting a party to celebrate the start of their new lives following their exile. During the party, Nnytyl reflected that he had found happiness in watching blitzball all [[Standard day|day]], not realizing how much stress he had endured while running Barsha Corporation. Later during the event, ND-5 trapped the partygoers in the [[Barsha compound]]'s dining room. When Nnytyl attempted to leave, expressing his wish to forget the incident and watch blitzball, he&mdash;along with all present except for Jaylen&mdash;was killed. Jaylen managed to escape the situation and used a [[restraining bolt]] to ensure the droid would serve him loyally instead. Afterward, Jaylen viewed a copy of ND-5's corrupted logs and saw a list of Nnytyl's activities, including the results of the blitzball matches he had spectated.<ref name="LRM" />

Chen included the game due to his past work as a writer for the [[Wikipedia:NHL|National Hockey League]].<ref name="Tweet" />
