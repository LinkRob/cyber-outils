A '''felinx''' was an [[Species|animal]] that could be kept as a pet. The [[Gavana Orbital]] [[space station]] resident [[Yrica Quell]] and her [[Quell family|family]] once owned one, but Quell's [[parent]]s were forced to [[Death|put it down]] when it became sick. While [[governor]] of the [[planet]] [[Haidoral Prime]], [[Everi Chalis]] concluded that felinx were becoming feral and had many killed. The governor later defected to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]], and the 61st recruit [[Corbo]] attempted to confront her with a [[Knife|galley knife]], as his felinx had been killed under her orders.

==Felinx in the galaxy==
===High Republic Era===
{{Quote|Maybe that was your biggest mistake. You're hiding here like a cowardly felinx when there could be someone out there looking for help. But that’s what you can expect from a Hutt, isn't it?|Zenny Greylark to Churo|<ref name="BTN">''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''</ref>}}
[[File:ZennyGreylark-BewareTheNamelessCover.png|thumb|right|200px|On Palagosal, Zenny Greylark accused Churo of hiding like a cowardly felinx.]]
In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] brought a number of Republic delegates and some [[Jedi]] on a tour at [[Lonisa City]], the [[capital]] of the [[planet]] [[Valo]], led by [[Coordinator]] [[Samera Ra-oon]]. When the party used some [[Hoverbuggy|hoverbuggies]] during their tour, the sound of the vehicles reminded [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] of the gentle purring of a felinx.<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>

A joint group of [[Republic Defense Coalition]] and [[Jedi Order]] colleagues answered a distress call on the [[planet]] [[Palagosal]]<ref name="BTN" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> When the [[human]] [[Zenny Greylark]] learned that the signal's origin led to [[Churo]]&mdash;a [[Hutt]] who had overridden another distress call to boost his own&mdash;she accused him of hiding at his current location like a cowardly felinx instead of seeing if anyone else needed assistance.<ref name="BTN" />

===Imperial times===
{{Quote|How ''do'' you feel?''"<br />"''Same way I feel about the time our felinx was sick and dying. My parents were sure she was in pain, and they were probably right. But I refused to go with them when they had her put down.|IT-O and Yrica Quell|audio=ItoQuell-Felinx-SF.flac|<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>}}
[[File:YricaQuell-TIEFighter1.jpg|thumb|left|220px|The family of Yrica Quell (pictured) owned a female felinx, though they eventually put it down.]]
By the [[Imperial Era]], members of the felinx [[species]] were kept as pets. [[Yrica Quell]], a resident on the [[Gavana Orbital]] [[space station]], and her [[Quell family|family]] once owned a female felinx, which eventually became sick. Quell's [[parent]]s, sure that the animal was in pain, had the felinx [[Death|put down]] as a result, though Quell refused to witness the creature's death.<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> While [[Everi Chalis]] was [[governor]] of the planet [[Haidoral Prime]], she believed that too many felinx in the planet's capital [[city]], [[Haidoral Administrative Center One]], were turning feral and harming the city's appearance. Consequently, she ordered a culling of the creatures, which resulted in the death of one owned by the civilian [[Corbo]].<ref name="Battlefront" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY">{{BTCdate}}</ref> after the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] [[Attack on Haidoral Prime|attacked]] Haidoral Prime, Corbo joined the [[company]]. Chalis additionally defected to the 61st in fear that the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] would punish her for the company's attack. While the governor was held in a makeshift [[brig]] aboard the company's [[CR90 corvette]], the ''[[Thunderstrike]]'', Corbo attempted to confront her about his dead felinx, carrying a [[Knife|galley knife]] with him. His attempt to visit Chalis failed, and he explained the death of his felinx to [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] when questioned about his intentions.<ref name="Battlefront" />

In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> Quell, who was a [[New Republic pilot]] at that [[time]], mentioned her felinx's death in a conversation with the [[IT-O interrogation droid|IT-O Interrogation Unit]] [[IT-O]] while the two were stranded on a [[Unidentified planetoid (Cerberon system)|planetoid]] in the [[Cerberon system]]. Quell remarked that she felt the same way about the felinx dying as she did about not admitting to her [[Yrica Quell's father|father]] that she had broken one of his plates on another occasion.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
