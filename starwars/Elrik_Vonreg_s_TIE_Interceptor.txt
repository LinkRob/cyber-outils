'''Elrik Vonreg's TIE Interceptor''' or '''Vonreg's TIE Baron''' was [[pilot]]ed by [[Major]] [[Elrik Vonreg]] during the [[Cold War]]. Vonreg utilized the [[TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor]] during a [[New Republic]] [[Mission to rendezvous with the Resistance|mission]] to rendezvous with the [[Resistance]], ultimately getting driven off before returning to [[Starkiller Base]]. A higher-end model equipped with a [[hyperdrive]], the TIE/ba was armed with at least two [[laser cannon]]s and a pair of [[missile launcher]]s.<ref name="The Recruit" /> The [[TIE series|TIE]] was also equipped with an [[Experimental flight computer (TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor)|experimental flight computer]] manufactured by [[Torplex]].<ref name="USWNE" />

[[File:FirstOrderProvocateur-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|Vonreg's TIE patrolling outside Station Theta Black.]]
After a [[spider probe droid]] alerted the [[First Order]] to the presence of Resistance agents in the [[Dassal system]], Major Vonreg led a flight of TIEs to [[Mission to the Dassal system|attack]]. He was forced to retreat after being led into a [[gravity well]].<ref name="The Core Problem">{{Resistance|The Core Problem}}</ref>

After the First Order [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupied]] the ''[[Colossus]]'', Vonreg flew his TIE/ba on patrol missions.<ref name="No Escape: Part 1" />

As the crew of the station engaged the flying systems, Vonreg led the TIE garrison into [[Battle of Castilon|battle]]. He was then shot down by [[Kazuda Xiono]] in the ''[[Fireball]]''.<ref name="No Escape: Part 2" />
