'''Corran''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Gender|male]] that lived on the [[planet]] [[Daiyu]]. Corran and his [[Parent|mother]],<ref name="Part II" /> [[Nyche]],<ref name="CGttSWG" /> desperately needed to leave Daiyu as Corran's abilities put him in danger. Corran's mother contacted a supposed [[Jedi]] named [[Haja Estree]] and asked him to get them both off Daiyu. Haja, who was actually a [[con artist]], directed them to [[Gate 3-C]] at the [[Daiyu cargo port|port]] so that they could travel to [[Corellia]] in exchange for [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] as payment.<ref name="Part II" /> Corran made his way to the planet [[Jabiim]], where he resided along with other members of the [[Hidden Path]].<ref name="Part V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref>

Once the base on Jabiim was [[Attack on Jabiim|attacked]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], the innocents made a dash to [[Kawlan Roken]]'s freight transport, which would take off into space, and be followed by the ''[[Devastator]]''. The [[Youngling|young]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] would use her [[toy droid]], [[L0-LA59]] (Lola) to entertain Corran as the transport was fired upon. [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] would distract the ''Devastator'' and its [[Anakin Skywalker|captain]] by fleeing in an [[escape pod]], and through the real Jedi's sacrifice the innocents onboard were able to escape.<ref name="Part VI">{{Kenobi|Part VI}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Corran first appeared in the [[Part II|second episode]] of the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'', and was portrayed by [[Indie Desroches]]. Though the character's name was not mentioned in the actual episode, it did appear in the credits.<ref name="Part II" />
