The '''fry-market''' was a interior market on the [[Ring of Kafrene]],<ref name="Andor" /> a [[mining]] colony and deep-[[Realspace|space]] trading post<ref name="RONovel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Thand sector]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Bodhi Rook]]'s defection and [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor's]] meeting with [[Tivik]] as occuring in [[1 BBY]]. As [[Rogue One – Cassian Andor 1|''Rogue One – Cassian Andor'' 1]] takes place between these two events, it must also take place in 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[intelligence officer]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] detonated a [[blaster gas]] cartridge on one of the grills there to distract a group of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s while he collected a [[comlink]] from a drop site in the market. After he spoke with his contact [[Tivik]] using the device, Andor was attacked by [[Barger]], a criminal working for [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]]. Andor used improvised [[weapon]]s from that stalls of the fry market to fend off Barger until Andor's companion [[K-2SO]] arrived and [[Death|killed]] the criminal.<ref name="Andor" />
