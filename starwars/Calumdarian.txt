'''Calumdarian''' was a [[Volcano|volcanic]] rocky world<ref name="Age of Rebellion" /> located in the [[Calumdarian system]] in the [[Immalia sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Its [[atmosphere]] was breathable for a short [[time]] period, but was filled with smoke that made long-term exposure difficult. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] operated a secret [[surveillance outpost]] on Calumdarian. Shortly after the [[Cyrkon Extraction]], [[Luke Skywalker]] and several [[Rebel pilot]]s located [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] on [[Balnab]], requesting that the [[smuggler]]s transport several crates of cargo to the outpost in the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
