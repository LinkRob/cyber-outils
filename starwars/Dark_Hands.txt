"'''Dark Hands'''" is a short story by [[August Hahn|August]] and [[Cynthia Hahn]], released on [[August 18]], [[2005]], and set within the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] setting from [[Wizards of the Coast]]. Following the story arc of [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Darrus Jeht/Legends|Darrus Jeht]], it describes his sabotage of a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Separatist]] factory building [[DSD1 dwarf spider droid/Legends|DSD1 dwarf spider droids]] from the perspective of a fallen [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] that Jeht was sent to kill.
