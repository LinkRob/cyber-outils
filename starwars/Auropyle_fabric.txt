'''Auropyle fabric''' was a form of textile used to make [[Clothing|clothes]]. [[Musician|Singer]] [[Aurodia Ventafoli]] wore a corded auropyle dress,<ref name="SoloGuide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> while [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] wore corded auropyle fabric robes.<ref name="JediVisual">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
