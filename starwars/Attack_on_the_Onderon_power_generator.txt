During the [[Onderonian Civil War]], the [[Onderon rebels]] '''attacked''' the [[Onderon power generator]] in an effort to bring the [[Separatist Droid Army]] to a standstill. The operation was successful, resulting in the destruction of the facility and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] dispatching reinforcements as the war intensified.<ref name="Front Runners" />
