'''Domm Korgale''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who served as an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Vice Admiral]] under [[Grand Moff]] [[Lozen Tolruck]] at the time of the [[Liberation of Kashyyyk|liberation]] of [[Kashyyyk]]. He served as commander of the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Dominion]]'', until it was infiltrated by [[Han Solo]] and his team. After being captured by Solo, Korgale escaped his ship's bridge, and the [[New Republic]] didn't think he was useful enough to be pursued.

==Biography==
A [[Gender|male]] individual, [[Vice Admiral]] Domm Korgale served as the [[commander]] of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Dominion]]''<ref name="Life Debt" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> His subordinate was [[Rear Admiral]] [[Urian Orlan]]. During the [[Liberation of Kashyyyk|liberation]] of [[Kashyyyk]], Korgale and Orlan complied with [[Grand Moff]] [[Lozen Tolruck]]'s orders to [[Orbital bombardment|orbitally bombard]] the [[planet]] after the [[Wookiee]]s rose up in revolt against [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] rule. Later, he commanded the ''Dominion'' when a rebel team led by [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] boarded the [[Starship|ship]] in an attempt to knock out the ''Dominion'' and its sister ships ''[[Vitiator]]'' and ''[[Neutralizer]]''.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Despite Solo and his team unleashing a rampaging horde of [[webweaver]] [[spider]]s, [[Admiral]] Korgale and his [[stormtrooper]]s managed to capture the rebel boarders. Korgale placed the rebel boarders in shackles and then confronted Solo personally. After identifying himself, he informed Solo that he was going to deliver him to the Empire's embrace so that he could buy a seat with the Imperial leadership. When Solo responded that he was a war criminal, Korgale threatened to [[Death|execute]] his comrades.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Korgale informed his comrades that he had asked for Imperial reinforcements and voice his hope that the Empire would respond. One of his comm [[officer]]s then told him that a [[YT-1300 light freighter]] had jumped out of [[hyperspace]]. This ship turned out to be Solo's ship ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', which was being [[pilot]]ed by his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. Korgale ordered his Destroyer to launch a contingent of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. When Leia ordered him to stand down in the name of the [[New Republic]], Korgale laughed and dismissed the idea that her [[freighter]] could take on three [[Star Destroyer]]s.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Leia was accompanied by a dozen [[X-wing starfighter]]s from [[Captain]] [[Wedge Antilles]]' [[Phantom Squadron]] and [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar's]] [[cruiser]] ''[[Home One]]''. Believing that his foes were outnumbered, Korgale ordered the Imperial ships to mount a vigorous defense of Imperial territory G5-623. However, Chewbacca broke free of his captors and hurled a stormtrooper against the wall. Han and his comrades broke free and then took out Korgale's men. Chewbacca hit Korgale and knocked him to the ground. While Korgale fled the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], Han and his comrades hijacked the ''Dominion'' and used its [[turbolaser]]s and [[laser cannon]]s to destroy the ''Vitiator'' and to [[fire]] upon the ''Neutralizer''. As a result, the ''Neutralizer'' surrendered to the New Republic and Imperial rule on Kashyyyk came to an end.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Personality and traits==
Domm Korgale was an Imperial Vice Admiral who regarded the New Republic as criminals. He hoped that capturing Han Solo would improve his standing in the Galactic Empire. Korgale underestimated the ingenuity of his New Republic opponents, and particularly Solo, who ultimately tilted the balance of the battle in the New Republic's favor by hijacking the Vice Admiral's personal Star Destroyer ''Dominion''.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Behind the scenes==
Domm Korgale first appeared in [[Chuck Wendig]]'s [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', the second installment in [[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy|The Aftermath Trilogy]].
