During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Galactic Empire]] [[Minister]] [[Veris Hydan]] led an '''excavation''' of the [[Lothal]] [[Lothal Jedi Temple|Jedi Temple]]. [[Gender|He]] did this on the orders of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], who wished to gain access to a [[portal]] inside the [[Jedi temple|temple]] that gave access to the [[World Between Worlds]].<ref name="Wolves" /><ref name="A World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref> The Empire had become aware of the temple after two Imperial [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]], [[Fifth Brother]] and [[Seventh Sister]], tracked the [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Ezra Bridger]] as well as the former Jedi [[Ahsoka Tano]],<ref name="Shroud of Darkness">{{Rebels|Shroud of Darkness}}</ref> there around the [[Standard year|year]] [[3 BBY]].<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-rebels-returns-with-princess-leia|text=''Star Wars Rebels'' Returns...with Princess Leia}} indicates that "[[A Princess on Lothal]]" takes place roughly three [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. As a result, "[[Shroud of Darkness]]"&mdash;which is set after "A Princess on Lothal"&mdash;also occurs around three years before the Battle of [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]].</ref> During the excavation, one of the doors of the temple was removed, stone by stone, and transported offworld. The doorway contained one portal to the mystical [[The Force|Force]] realm.<ref name="Farewell">{{Rebels|Family Reunion &ndash; and Farewell}}</ref>

The excavation uncovered a [[Painting of the Mortis gods|painting]] of the three powerful Force-wielders, the [[Father (Mortis)|Father]], [[Daughter]], and [[Son]], which Hydan correctly deduced had a connection to the portal he sought.<ref name="A World Between Worlds" /> In the year [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Wolves and a Door date" /> the [[Spectres]], a group of Rebels, undertook a [[Mission to the Lothal Jedi Temple|mission]] to the temple. There, Ezra and [[Sabine Wren]] snuck into the excavation site disguised as [[scout trooper]]s. The two figured out how to activate the portal before Sabine was caught and unmasked, and Ezra entered the World Between Worlds.<ref name="Wolves" /> The excavation ended after Ezra, upon returning to Lothal through the portal, activated the painting of the [[Mortis]] gods in such a way that the Jedi temple was permanently destroyed, preventing the Empire from accessing that portal.<ref name="A World Between Worlds" />
