The '''Bando Gora''' was a [[The Force|Force]] cult active in [[the galaxy]]. They ended up turning toward [[assassin]]ation and formed a criminal syndicate.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> By [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> an individual named [[Luntobetha]] worked as a [[clothing]] dyer and vendor on the [[planet]] [[Olega]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]], donning a repurposed Bando Gora rebreather mask to protect her from caustic dye fumes, though the old mask made her appearance seem more threatening than she truly was.<ref name="TAVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Bando Gora was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> The [[Bando Gora/Legends|Bando Gora]] originally appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunter]]'',<ref name="Bounty Hunter">''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunter]]''</ref> a [[2002]]<ref name="Bounty Hunter release">{{WebCite|url=http://www.gamefaqs.com/ps2/557901-star-wars-bounty-hunter/data|text=GameFAQs Title Data for ''Star Wars: Bounty Hunter''|work=GameFAQs|archivedate=20160304090346}}</ref> [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed and published by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="Bounty Hunter" />
