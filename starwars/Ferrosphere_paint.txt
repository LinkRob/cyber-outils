'''Ferrosphere paint'''<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> was a paint applied to starfighters that could be used to jam enemy [[missile]] locks.<ref name="BFII">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> [[Poe Dameron]] used the paint on his [[Black One|X-wing fighter]].<ref name="TFA VD" />
