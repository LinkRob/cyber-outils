The '''CR70 corvette''' was a model of [[corvette]] used by the [[House of Organa]] during [[Order 66]] and later the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> One notable CR70 corvette was the ''[[Tantive III]]'',<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> which was utilized by [[Viceroy]] and [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] during the fall of the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Imperial Era|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Episode III" />
