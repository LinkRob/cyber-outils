'''Dunkutu Day''' was an annual celebration celebrated by the people of [[Omaka]]. It was traditionally celebrated with a big feast in the center of town.<ref name="The Harvest Feast" /> 

{{Event-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dunkutu Day first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'' episode "[[The Harvest Feast]]."<ref name="The Harvest Feast" /> Showrunner [[Michael Olson]] said the holiday was reminiscent of the real-world holiday [[Wikipedia:Thanksgiving (United States)|Thanksgiving]].<ref name="Thanksgiving">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-young-jedi-adventures-new-episodes-interview|text=''Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures'' Creators on the Growing World of ''The High Republic''}}</ref> [[Thanksgiving]] is mentioned in the [[Canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''.<ref name="Monsters">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref>
