'''Fissrit''' were a type of [[Diet|carnivorous]], many-[[Limb|legged]] [[arthropod]] that were [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Water|ice]] [[Planet|worlds]]. Also known as '''fire worms''', they could produce incredible [[Temperature|heat]] through a natural chemical reaction in their underbellies, which allowed them to thrive in frozen conditions. The amount of heat they could produce was variable, with a fissrit at rest producing just enough to survive local conditions.<ref name="Savage" />

When necessary, though, they could increase the temperature produced to melt snow or ice and to [[Death|kill]] any prey creatures that investigated the pools of liquid water the melting produced. Fissrit typically measured just under a third of a [[meter]] in length, with the largest variants typically acting as solitary hunters. Other varieties dwelt in colonies, as all fissrit possessed a strong social instinct. Fissrit could be purchased for 1,500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] but were hard to find for sale.<ref name="Savage" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fissrit were mentioned in ''[[Savage Spirits]]'', a [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement written by [[Tim Huckelberry]] and published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]].<ref name="Savage" />
