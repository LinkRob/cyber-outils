'''2391''' was the designation of an [[OOM-series security droid]] that served in the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Trade Federation Droid Army|Droid Army]]. In [[32 BBY]], it was stationed on board the modified [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|''Lucrehulk''-class LH-3210 cargo freighter]] ''[[Saak'ak]]'' while the [[starship]] was part of the [[Invasion of Naboo|blockade]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. During the blockade, 2391 was in the [[freighter]]'s hangar when the [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class space cruiser]] ''[[Radiant VII]]'' arrived carrying a pair of [[Jedi]] ambassadors from the [[Galactic Republic]].

==Biography==
2391 was an [[OOM-series battle droid]] manufactured by [[Baktoid Combat Automata]] for the [[Trade Federation]]. It served as a [[OOM-series security droid]] in the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]] and was stationed on board the modified [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|''Lucrehulk''-class LH-3210 cargo freighter]] ''[[Saak'ak]]''. In [[32 BBY]], it was on board the ''Saak'ak'' when the [[starship]] formed part of a [[Invasion of Naboo|blockade]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] over a trade dispute.<ref name="TPM" />

In order to settle the dispute, the [[Jedi]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] were sent by the [[Galactic Republic]] to negotiate with the Trade Federation and arrived on the ''Saak'ak'' in the [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class space cruiser]] ''[[Radiant VII]]''. As the [[cruiser]] entered the [[freighter]]'s hangar, 2391 was stood in the hangar with fellow security droid [[2296]], and the pair turned to watch the ship land. Not long after its arrival, the ''Radiant VII'' was destroyed by a ceiling [[turret]] in the hangar. The Trade Federation then began deploying its forces to Naboo in order to take control of the planet.<ref name="TPM" />

==Characteristics==
Like all OOM-series battle droids, 2391 stood 1.91 [[meter]]s tall<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> and had [[Color|black]] [[sensor]]s and tan plating with red markings<ref name="TPM" /> to denote its role as a security specialist.<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
2391 first appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. The droid's name is not spoken in the film, but its designation is visible on its back.<ref name="TPM" />
