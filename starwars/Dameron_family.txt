The '''Dameron family''' was a family of [[human]]s that served prominently in the military of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], [[New Republic]] and the [[Resistance]].

==History==
Around [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Kes Dameron]]'s flashbacks in [[Star Wars (2020) 12|''Star Wars'' (2020) 12]] to around [[4 BBY]].</ref> [[Kes Dameron]] traveled to [[Galator III]] before the wedding of a [[Kes Dameron's friend|friend]], they decided to put their money together and go to the [[betting houses]]. Kes bet one of his last few credits on a race, picking the driver called [[Shara Bey]] because he liked her name, after winning the bet, Kes went to thank Shara and bought her a drink.<ref name="Star Wars 12">[[Star Wars (2020) 12|''Star Wars'' (2020) 12]]</ref>

The two eventually fell in love, got married<ref name="Shattered Empire 1" /> and in [[2 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> had a son whom they named [[Poe Dameron|Poe]]. Poe rarely saw his parents as they started to serve the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in the fight against the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" />
