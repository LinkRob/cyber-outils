'''Azzameen Station''',<ref name="Timelines" /> also known as '''Azzameen Home Base''',<ref name="BMF" /> was a [[space station]] in the [[Garis sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that served as the residence of the [[Azzameen family/Canon|Azzameen family]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the station also served as the headquarters of the Azzameen family's [[Azzameen family trading company|trading company]].<ref name="BMF" />
