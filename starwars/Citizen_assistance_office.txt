'''Citizen assistance offices''' were offices that assisted and tended to the needs of citizens. Some offices focused on a specific demographic, such as the [[Lothalian citizen assistance office]]. Other offices were open to all citizens, such as the [[Bartanish Four Sector supplementary citizen assistance office]].<ref name="Thrawn (novel)">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>
