The '''Four Masters'''<ref name="SWcom">{{SW|url=news/hidden-knowledge-5-fascinating-facts-about-the-jedi-temple|text=Hidden Knowledge: 5 Fascinating Facts about the Jedi Temple|nolive=1}}</ref> also known as the '''Four Founders''',<ref name="Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> were the four [[Jedi Master]]s who founded the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. Sculptures of the founders were carved as bas-reliefs into massive pylons which were displayed at the formal entrance to the Temple. Before the entrance was the ceremonial staircase of the [[Processional Way]], atop of which were the huge statues of two [[Jedi Guardian|Warrior Masters]] and two [[Sage Master]]s,<ref name="SWcom" /> which bore some resemblance to the Four Masters' sculptures. The Four Masters consisted of a [[Twi'lek]], two [[humanoid]]s and one Master of [[Unidentified species (Four Masters)|another species]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The sculptures of the Four Masters first appeared in the [[2005]] film ''[[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith]]'',<ref name="ROTS" /> which premiered on [[May 19]], [[2005]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] they were first identified in an article on [[StarWars.com]], written by [[Kevin Beentjes]] and published in [[2015]].<ref name="SWcom" /> Their name originates in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity where it was first used in the 2005 reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="CL 2005">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'' (2005)]]</ref> They were later first identified as the Four Founders in the [[2019]] audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]].<ref name="Jedi Lost" /> The sculptures were designed by [[Aaron McBride]] and [[Ryan Church]] under the direction of director [[George Lucas]] between October and December [[2003]]. The sculptures originally depicted the Masters as different species and in different postures<ref name="Art of ROTS">''[[The Art of Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref> than in the final film.<ref name="ROTS" /> For their appearance in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' they were designed by [[Chris Glenn]] and [[Kilian Plunkett]].<ref name="TCW Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sabotage-episode-guide|text="Sabotage" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>
