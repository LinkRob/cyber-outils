'''Ba-Gonk''' was a [[droid]] that [[Haxion Brood Bounty Droid|served]] the [[Haxion Brood]] crime syndicate as a [[bounty hunter]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It had [[Color|white]] and dark blue plating and orange and cyan [[sensor]]s. In combat, Ba-Gonk used a [[wrist blaster]] mounted on its right [[Limb|arm]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFO Date" /> the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Cal Kestis]] [[Death|destroyed]] Ba-Gonk when the droid attacked him.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ba-Gonk appeared as an optional enemy in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''. The droid is one of many Haxion Brood bounty hunters that have a chance to randomly spawn and attack the player alone or in a pair at a variety of locations throughout the game. This article assumes Kestis slew all possible bounty hunters, including Ba-Gonk, as part of game completion.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

Within ''Fallen Order'', the bounty hunter who captures Cal Kestis on [[Zeffo]] is randomized.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> In the [[2026]] [[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, Vol. 2|manga adaptation]], the hunter who captures Kestis is [[Atticus Rex]].<ref name="Manga">[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, Vol. 2|''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'', Vol. 2]]</ref>
