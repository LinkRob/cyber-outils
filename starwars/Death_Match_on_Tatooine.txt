Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the '''Death Match on Tatooine''',<ref name="Bounty Hunters 42" /> also known as a '''mission to Tatooine''',<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> was initated in the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]] by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] to reveal his [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure's Megadroid|Megadrioid]] to the criminal underworld. The match was set to be between the [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] and the droid, only for Valance's allies, [[T'onga's crew]], to reveal themselves and help him destroy the unit. With the droid destroyed, the coming [[rescue of Han Solo]] would be easier.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 42" />
