The '''Blue Sector''' was a location in [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. The [[ASP-9 "Vrelt" autopistol]] manufactured by [[Talus Ironworks]] saw use within the Blue Sector by [[inspector]]s of the [[Corellian Security Forces]] at crime scenes.<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Blue Sector was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Gadgets" /> It was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'',<ref name="TOR">''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''</ref> a [[2011]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] developed by [[BioWare]] Austin and released by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="LucasArts Announce">{{LucasArts|url=company/release/news20081021.html|text=LucasArts and BioWare reveal ''Star Wars'': The Old Republic}}</ref>
