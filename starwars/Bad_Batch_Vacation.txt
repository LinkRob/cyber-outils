"'''Bad Batch Vacation'''" is a [[Canon|non-canon]] animated [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short released on [[August 5]], [[2022]] on the LEGO YouTube channel. It is part of both ''[[LEGO Star Wars Summer Shorts]]''<ref name="Playlist" /> and ''[[LEGO STAR WARS: Celebrate the Season]]''.<ref name="SWKids" />

==Official description==
''The Bad Batch and Omega enjoy some lakeside downtime until Cad Bane arrives to spoil the fun.''<ref name="Batch" />

==Plot summary==
While [[Hunter]] and [[Echo]] are racing [[speeder]]s in a lake, [[Tech]] sits on the shores of the beach reading something. He is disturbed by [[Wrecker]], who accidentally splashes water on him while entering the water. Meanwhile, [[Omega]] races behind Hunter's float on a motorized surfboard.

While [[Clone Force 99]] are enjoying themselves, they are being watched from above by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] and his droid [[Todo 360]] aboard their [[starship]] ''[[Justifier]]''. Bane gets Todo 360 to lower their starship above the water and exits the ''Justifier'' while donning [[rocket boot]]s. He pursues Omega, prompting Hunter to swerve his speeder to the left, splashing Cad Bane in water.

Bane heads to the shore but Wrecker jumps in his way, splashing the bounty hunter with water. Undeterred, Bane continues the pursuit but is knocked off balance by the cable connecting Omega's slide to Hunter's speeder. He falls back into the water again. The ''Justifier'' joins Bane's pursuit of Omega and Hunter. Watching from the shore, Tech uses the ''[[Marauder (Omicron-class)|Marauder]]'' to fire a projectile at the ''Justifier'', causing it to spin and collide with Bane.

Still undeterred, Bane pursues Omega again. Omega jumps off the slide, which Bane grabs onto. The slide hits a plastic barrier, causing Bane to crash into the lake water. Hunter then lassos Bane and ties him to the departing ''Justifier''. The clones wave as Bane is dragged away by the ascending ''Justifier''.
