The '''EV-series supervisor droid''',<ref name="Droidography" /> also known as the '''EV-series droid overseer''',<ref name="HC16">{{HelmetCollectionCite|16|Databank A-Z}}</ref> was a model of [[supervisor droid]] manufactured by [[MerenData]].<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> The [[droid]] was gangly in form.<ref name="Living Set">{{ToppsLivingSet|num=218|cardname=EV-9D9|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/205515/cid/16724061/2019-Topps-Living-Star-Wars-218-EV-9D9}}</ref> One of the droids, [[EV-9D9]], worked for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], as the chief of [[Boba Fett's Palace|his palace's]] Cyborg Operations.<ref name="Return of the Jedi" />

In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate}}</ref> EV-9D9 worked as a [[bartender]] in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]].<ref name="Connections" />

==History==
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Pyke Syndicate]] utilized several [[Color|black]]-plated [[EV-series]] [[supervisor droid]]s with orange-striped shoulders in the [[Coaxium Mines of Kessel]], employing them in the [[Kessel Control Center]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Behind the scenes==
EV-series supervisor droids have served as bartenders in several earlier ''Star Wars'' products. In the Legends [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (video game)|adaptation]] of the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], an EV unit designated [[EV-7G7]] [[Bartender/Legends|bartended]] at the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena/Legends|Mos Espa Grand Arena's]] [[7-Gee Lounge]].<ref name="TPM video game">[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (video game)|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'' video game]]</ref> Another EV unit known as Isaac bartended aboard the ''Khetanna'' in the "Fett Defeated" segment of ''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars Episode III]]'', a non-canon ''[[Wikipedia:Robot Chicken|Robot Chicken]]'' special<ref name="Robot Chicken">''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars Episode III]]''</ref> that originally aired on [[December 19]], [[2010]].<ref name="Robot Chicken air date">{{Twitter|wizmatts|statuses/7551272282497024|[[Wikipedia:Matt Senreich|Matt Senreich]]|nope.
