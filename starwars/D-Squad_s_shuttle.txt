A '''''Rho''-class transport shuttle'''<ref name="Rho-class DB" /> was used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] to load [[rhydonium]] onto a [[Renown|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Missing in Action" /> to [[Carida incident|destroy]] the [[Republic strategy conference]] taking place on the [[space station]] [[Valor (space station)|''Valor'']] above [[Carida]].<ref name="Point of No Return" /> It was stolen by [[D-Squad]] to [[Escape from Abafar|escape]] from [[Abafar]].<ref name="Missing in Action" /> The squad, upon landing on the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]], discovered that the [[Star Destroyer|capital ship]] had been turned into a flying bomb. After the Star Destroyer dropped out of [[hyperspace]] in the Carida system, all of the members of D-Squad except [[R2-D2]], and the surviving service droids from the capital ship, escaped its destruction in the shuttle.<ref name="Point of No Return" />
