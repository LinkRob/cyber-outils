The various '''farm villages''' were a series of villages found beyond the [[hovertrain]] tracks on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Golak]]. Siblings [[Rani]] and [[Charuk]] were from one such village, but were sent away by [[Rani and Charuk's pa|their father]] when the village was attacked. The citizens of their village refused to fight back against the invaders, and all of what they had was burned. After scavengers witnessed Rani using [[the Force]] and the train headed towards a [[Unidentified Golak city|city]] near the [[Golak Mahal]], they reported this to the [[Galactic Empire]], who dispatched an [[Inquisitor]] to search for her. [[Unidentified Inquisitor (Golak)|This Inquisitor]] then claimed to have burned the farm villages to the ground, leaving no survivors, before tracking Rani down. He was killed by the [[Jedi]] survivor [[Rugal]] shortly thereafter.<ref name="TBOG" />
