The '''Csaplar spaceport''' was the primary [[spaceport]] of [[Csaplar]], [[capital]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] and the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] [[Csilla]]. It was located on the southwest edge of the [[city]], and [[Tube car|tunnel cars]] could be used to leave the spaceport. A number of [[hotel]]s and [[restaurant]]s could be found in the area around Csaplar spaceport.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Before [[19 BBY]],<ref name="CRflash">The flashbacks of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' take place prior to the main events of the novel, which take place in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Chiss officer]]s [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," and [[Ar'alani|Irizi'ar'alani]], core name "Ziara," landed at the spaceport when they traveled to the [[Irizi family homestead]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Csaplar spaceport appeared in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
