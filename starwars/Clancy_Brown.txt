'''Clarence John Brown III''', known as '''Clancy Brown''', (born [[January 5]], [[1950s#1959|1959]]) is an [[Wikipedia:United States of America|American]] actor and voice actor.

Brown began his acting career in the early 1980s with an appearance in [[Wikipedia:The Dukes of Hazzard|''The Dukes of Hazzard'']]. He is mostly recognizable for his role as the protagonist's friend in [[Wikipedia:The Adventures of Buckaroo Banzai Across the 8th Dimension|''The Adventures of Buckaroo Banzai Across the 8th Dimension'']] (1984), The Kurgan in [[Wikipedia:Highlander (film)|''Highlander'']] (1986), prison guard Byron Hadley in [[Wikipedia:The Shawshank Redemption|''The Shawshank Redemption'']] (1994), a drill instructor in [[Wikipedia:Starship Troopers (film)|''Starship Troopers'']] (1997) and the preacher Meacham in [[Wikipedia:Cowboys & Aliens|''Cowboys & Aliens'']] (2011). Brown is of Scottish ancestry.

Having a deep and resonant voice, he also works as a voice actor for both animation and video games, including [[Wikipedia:Mortal Kombat (film)|''Mortal Kombat'']] (1995), [[Wikipedia:Gargoyles|''Gargoyles'']] (1995&ndash;1996), [[Wikipedia:SpongeBob SquarePants|''SpongeBob SquarePants'']] (1999&ndash;present) and [[Wikipedia:Avatar: The Last Airbender|''Avatar: The Last Airbender'']] (2005&ndash;2008; a show which featured heavy involvement from [[Dave Filoni]] and [[Giancarlo Volpe]]). He also voiced Lex Luthor in the DC Animated Universe's [[Wikipedia:Superman: The Animated Series|''Superman: The Animated Series'']] (1996&ndash;2000), [[Wikipedia:Justice League (TV series)|''Justice League'']] (2001&ndash;2004) and [[Wikipedia:Justice League Unlimited|''Justice League Unlimited'']] (2004&ndash;2006).

==Biography==
Clancy Brown provided the voice of the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Montross (bounty hunter)|Montross]] in ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunter]]'', the [[Nightbrothers|Nightbrother]] turned [[Sith Lord]] [[Savage Opress]] in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars'']], and [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Governor]] turned [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] member [[Ryder Azadi]] for ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. He reprised the role of Azadi in live-action in the ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'' television series. He also portrayed the [[Devaronian]] [[Burg]] in the [[Disney+]] live-action TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.
