'''Callosian spring vegetable soup''' was a type of soup on the menu at [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] under the Pantoran Plate Specials section. It was a daily soup at Dex's and served only on [[Centaxday/Legends|Centaxday]]. It was sold for 1.7 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]].
