The '''BD-1 Cutter vibro-ax''' was a long [[vibro-ax]]<ref name="SWCT" /> manufactured by the [[SoroSuub Corporation]].<ref name="Melee and Ranged Weapons" /> [[Pagetti Rook]], a [[Weequay]] under the employment of the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]], used a BD-1 Cutter vibro-ax to nudge [[Luke Skywalker]] into the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]] during the [[rescue of Han Solo]]. During the mission, [[Han Solo|Han]] used one to save [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] from falling into the [[Sarlacc]].<ref name="RotJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>
