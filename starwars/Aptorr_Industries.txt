'''Aptorr Industries''' was a business run on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]] by the [[Yarkora]] [[Aptorr]], whose [[Marriage|wife]], [[Varralis]], also served as his business partner. The company had a [[Aptorr's factory|factory]] on [[Brazatta Key]], manufacturing a range of products, including camping equipment. Aptorr secretly trained the [[Wookiee]] [[Gartabba]] to run his plant, but did not wish for anyone to know as his products were advertised as being purely Yarkora-made.<ref name="The Living Force" />
