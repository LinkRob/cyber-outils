'''Bruce Vilanch''' is a writer and jokesmith who served as one of the writers for [[1978]]'s ''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]''. When he learned at a development meeting that the special would center around [[Chewbacca's family/Legends|Chewbacca's family]], he became worried that it was in danger of looking like "one long episode of [[Wikipedia:Lassie (1954 TV series)|''Lassie'']]." He expressed his concerns to [[George Lucas]]&mdash;pointing out that [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiees]] didn't speak a [[Human]] language and made noises that could potentially be interpreted as sounding sexual&mdash;but he was simply met with a "glacial" look from the ''[[Star Wars]]'' creator.<ref name="Vanity Fair" />
