'''Bistan's U-wing''' was a [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]] piloted by [[Laren Joma]] and [[Taslet Colb]] during the [[Battle of Scarif]]. The U-wing arrived with the rest of [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]] to reinforce [[Jyn Erso]] and the rest of [[Rogue One]] in their mission to recover the [[Death Star plans]]. During the battle, the U-wing was shot down, [[Death|killing]] Joma, Colb, and [[Bistan]].<ref name="Rogue One" />
