'''Darren Nevin''' is a concept designer and creature FX sculptor who has worked on [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']],<ref name="TFA">{{Instagram|username=jahsonlion|url=p/BCO66PVGvbq|name=[[Darren Nevin]]|date=2016-02-25|quote=Dengue Sisters. The first full characters I got to sculpt! Conceptual designs by Martin Rezard. #denguesisters #starwars #theforceawakens #starwarstheforceawakens #lucasfilm #nealscanlan #disney #episode7 #creaturefx #mazscastle|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/u6C6C}}</ref> ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Rogue One">{{Instagram|username=jahsonlion|url=p/BOfjl3TjlGB|name=[[Darren Nevin]]|date=2016-12-26|quote=A briefly seen background alien I sculpted for Star Wars Rogue One. Concept design by Ivan Manzella with animatronics by @javiercoronilla & finishing artwork by Henrik Svensson #starwars #rogueone #lucasfilm #disney #creaturefx|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/ghQmo}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ">{{Instagram|username=jahsonlion|url=p/Bi2XSnZnyiM|name=[[Darren Nevin]]|date=2018-05-16|quote=Another shot of Pemmin Brunce that I found online! A background character of my own design that I sculpted for Star Wars TLJ. Paintwork by Henrik Svensson & animatronics by Gustav Hoegen.....dream come true to see mi work in their incredible hands😎 #starwars #TheLastJedi #tlj #creaturedesign #creaturefx #specialfx #creature #foamlatex #animatronics|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/r5DNN}}</ref> ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo">{{Instagram|username=jahsonlion|url=p/B5PlHd4DxF4|name=[[Darren Nevin]]|date=2019-11-24|quote=A few more background characters I got to sculpt for Solo that actually made the cut & a couple into The Mandalorian! #hansoloastarwarsstory #solo #soloastarwarsstory #hansolomovie #themandalorian #mandalorian #hansolo #solo #starwars #creatureeffects #creaturefx #pinewoodstudios #pinewood #nealscanlanstudio #nealscanlan #rodian #animatronics #sculpture #lucasfilm #disney #disneyplus|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/0pUrV}}</ref> and [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="TROS">{{Instagram|username=jahsonlion|url=p/CIq3vfjDjr8|name=[[Darren Nevin]]|date=2020-12-11|quote=Excited after seeing all the new Star Wars content coming soon! Here’s a character I sculpted on miself😎for The Rise Of Skywalker! Concept design by Luke Fisher @luke.d.fisher, make-up application by Lucy Sibbick @lucysibbickmakeup #starwarsriseofskywalker #starwarsepisode9 #starwars #creaturefx #sculpture #acting #cameo #lucasfilm #lucas #disney|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/LMx20}}</ref> He primarily sculpted designs made by other concept artists, though he did at least partially design creatures for both ''The Last Jedi'', the [[Ongidae]] [[Pemmin Brunce]],<ref name="TLJ" /> as well as ''The Rise of Skywalker'', where he both created and portrayed an [[unidentified pale wrinkled species]] which was ultimately cut from the film.<ref name="TROS" />
