'''''Boba Fett — Black, White & Red'' 2''' is the second issue of the [[canon]] [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Boba Fett — Black, White & Red]]''. The issue was written by [[Saladin Ahmed]], illustrated by [[Ramon Rosanas]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 29]], 2025.

==Publisher's summary==
''TIME TO PAY THE PIPER! SAMU is a down-on-his-luck criminal always running from his problems &ndash; but he can't outrun BOBA FETT! Witness one of the greatest bounty hunters in the galaxy stalk his prey &ndash; and blast a few sleemos while he's at it! Will the galaxy's most notorious hunter capture one of his sneakiest bounties yet?''

==Plot summary==
On the planet [[Meddan]], a notorious [[Zeltron]] con artist known as [[Samu Mavolak|Samu "the Scurrier" Mavolak]] attempts to evade the legendary bounty hunter [[Boba Fett]]. Samu, a man wanted in three different systems for theft, fraud, and impersonation, relies on a lifetime of experience running from creditors and ex-wives to stay one step ahead.

The pursuit begins in a crowded marketplace where Samu utilizes "dirtbombs" - shells packed with native soil and metal shavings - to scramble Fett’s sensor arrays and disappear into the crowd. Fett is initially misled by a decoy: a local businessman whose holographic identification had been "grafted" with Samu's likeness via stealth visual edits. However, Fett's meticulous nature allows him to see through the ruse. He tracks down Samu's sister, who reveals that despite official records listing his home as [[Coruscant]], Samu has returned to his actual home on Meddan.

Samu's sister strikes a deal with the bounty hunter: she will provide Samu’s location if Fett agrees to deliver him to a work colony on [[Krodos 4]] rather than executing him. Fett agrees to the terms.

Meanwhile, a group of Houks led by a man named [[Aloysius]] - whom Samu had previously swindled at [[Sabacc]] - takes Samu’s young son hostage to draw the [[Scurrier]] out of hiding. Samu, realizing his habitual fleeing has put his child in mortal danger, stops running and confronts the Houks in a scrapyard.

As Aloysius prepares to kill both Samu and the boy, Boba Fett intervenes. Refusing a bribe from the Houks to walk away, Fett declares the job is his and engages the group. Fett systematically eliminates the Houk threat, allowing Samu to reunite with his son.

In the aftermath, Samu chooses not to run from Fett. Recognizing that "facing life head-on" is the only way to protect what matters most, he allows himself to be taken into custody. As Fett leads him toward the ''[[Slave I]]'', Samu begins formulating plans for a future prison break from Krodos 4, though he acknowledges that his heart is no longer in the escape, but in seeing his son again.
