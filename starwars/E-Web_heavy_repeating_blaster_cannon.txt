The '''E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon''', also known as the '''E-Web heavy repeater''', '''Emplacement Weapon''', '''Heavy Blaster''', or simply as the '''E-Web''', was the most powerful heavy repeating [[blaster cannon]] in the [[Imperial Army|Imperial]] arsenal and commonly used by Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s and [[Clone trooper|clones]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] that came before it.

==Description==
[[File:EWeb-SWBC21.png|thumb|left|300px|The main E-Web cannon.]]
The E-Web featured rigid mounts to counteract the kinetic energy created by the weapon's formidable firepower, and was usually operated by a two-person crew. However, its set-up time limited its effectiveness. In consequence, various [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] crews pre-charged its [[Class-4T3 power generator]] for faster assembly, however doing so required careful adjustment of the power flow to prevent an overload.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The E-Web also had a scope that enabled improved accuracy for longer-range engagements.

An earlier variant of the E-Web in service near the end of the Clone Wars appeared to use a different [[Tripod (weapon attachment)|tripod]] with two fore legs and one aft leg instead of one fore and two aft.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==History==
{{Quote|Set up the cannon! Quickly!|A snowtrooper during the Battle of Hoth|<ref name="Tales of Villainy">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=1|story=Tales of Villainy: Invasion of Echo Base}}</ref>}}
The E-Web was put into [[Galactic Republic]] service by the late days of the Clone Wars<ref name="Aftermath" /> and remained in use as the Republic transitioned into the Galactic Empire.<ref name="ESB" /> Earlier in the Clone Wars, another model of heavy blaster cannon had seen use by Republic forces, with that model seeing use since at least the [[Battle of Christophsis]]<ref name="The Hidden Enemy">{{TCW|The Hidden Enemy}}</ref> and continuing to see service all the way up to, at the very least, the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]].<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref> By the late [[Outer Rim Sieges]], the E-Web had entered service. During the [[Battle of Kaller|Conquest of Kaller]], a clone trooper operated an E-Web that was deployed in one of the front line trenches where Jedi Master [[Depa Billaba]] and Clone Captain [[Grey (clone trooper)|Grey]] took cover.<ref name="Aftermath" />

An E-Web cannon was used by [[TK stormtrooper]]s on the planet [[Janix]]. Thanks to the [[Looti Vario]]'s distraction [[2B0T|Two-Boots]], a rogue [[Tactical Defense Droid]], manage to use the cannon to shoot down a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] and a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] [[gunship]] before another LAAT/i shot the cannon, making Two-Boots loose control. [[Brander Lawson]] grabbed the cannon from its tripod and used it to buy more time for both his son [[Rylee Lawson|Rylee]] and his friend to escape on a [[Dryden Vos's shuttle]] from [[Dryden Vos]], a representative of [[Crimson Dawn]].<ref name="MSL10">{{MSL|Chapter 10: The Dark Lord}}</ref>

[[File:DurgeRampage-BountyHunters36.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Durge picks up and uses an E-Web]]
When the [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] [[Battle of Hoth|invaded]] [[Echo Base]] on [[Hoth]], they used an E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon in an attempt to prevent the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' from escaping.<ref name="ESB" /> During a [[Mission to Quantxi|firefight]] on the [[moon]] of [[Quantxi]], the massive [[bounty hunter]] [[Durge]] picked up and carried a E-Web to serve as his gun. Hoping to kill his enemies faster, Durge chose to do so because he was annoyed at how long the battle was taking.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36">[[Bounty Hunters 36|''Bounty Hunters'' 36]]</ref>

After the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="Chapter 4">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 4: Sanctuary}}</ref> E-Webs were used against [[New Republic]] [[shock trooper]]s<ref name="Chapter 8" /> who were dropped from overhead ships during the hunt for Imperial warlords.<ref name="Chapter 4" /> Due to that tactic, E-Webs proved to be useful in firing upon dropping shock troopers, who would be vaporized in front of their comrades' very eyes before they had even hit the ground. A newer model of E-Web<ref name="Chapter 8" /> identified as the [[E-HOB heavy repeating laser cannon]]<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> was constructed<ref name="Chapter 8" /> by [[9 ABY]]<ref name="Date Note">{{MandoDate}}</ref> and utilized by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]'s [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|remnant]] during a [[Clash on Nevarro|skirmish]] on [[Nevarro]]. In introducing the weapon, Gideon called upon former shock trooper [[Carasynthia Dune]]'s memories of the E-Web to put fear into his enemies, further comparing the cannon to weaponry used in the [[Night of a Thousand Tears]] to scare the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]]. However, once a firefight broke out, Djarin ended up claiming the cannon and using it against Gideon's forces.<ref name="Chapter 8" /> Another successor model to the E-Web was the [[F-Web repeating blaster]].<ref name="Blasters and Firearms">{{EncyclopediaCite|Blasters and Ranged Weapons}}</ref>
