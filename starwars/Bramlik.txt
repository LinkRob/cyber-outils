'''Bramliks''', also known as '''spikers''' by the locals, were spherical creatures indigenous to [[Koboh]]. If a bramlik was approached, the spines on its back would extend, deterring most threats. Although not usually aggressive, the bramlik would attack if confronted with a dangerous situation.<ref name="Survivor" />
