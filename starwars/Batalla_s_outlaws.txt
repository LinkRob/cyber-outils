This large group of '''outlaws''' was led by [[Batalla]] and took up residence among the [[Wastelands of Kullgroon|Wastelands]] of the [[planet]] [[Kullgroon]]<ref name="Lando 3" /> by [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> They allied themselves with members of the [[Zakku Rain Cartel]] in an attempt to apprehend and execute the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]], though would ultimately fail in these endeavors. The majority of the outlaws' ranks were filled with members<ref name="Lando 3" /> of the [[Unidentified Kullgroon species|native population]],<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|username=paolovillanelli|url=status/1611659415903522816|name=[[Paolo Villanelli]]|date=2023-01-07|quote=Fun memories when creating this guy, a native of that snow planet where the story is set, i wanted to make him small but imposing so he is walking around on a small clone wars era walker|archivefile=File:KullgroonNativeSpecies-PaoloVillanelli.png|nolive=1}}</ref> though a few other [[Alien|aliens]] could also be found among their numbers.<ref name="Lando 3" />
