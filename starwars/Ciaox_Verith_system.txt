The [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] stopped in the [[star system]] on its way to the [[planet]] [[Chadawa]] in [[5 ABY]]. The [[New Republic]] refitted [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'' tracked the Imperial [[fighter wing]] to the system and launched an [[Skirmish in the Ciaox Verith system|attack]], part of its [[Deliverance's starfighter wing|starfighter wing]] being able to engage the 204th before the Imperial [[starship]]s escaped.

==Description==
The Ciaox Verith system was a [[star system]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> located within the [[Croynar sector]] in an intersecting portion of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]],<ref name="Trailing" /> situated at the coordinates M-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> A [[Unidentified gas giant (Ciaox Verith system)|gas giant]] [[orbit]]ed in the system.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==History==
{{Quote|Lark? What happened?''"<br />"''They jumped to lightspeed. Couldn't close fast enough to stop them.''"<br />"''What happened?''"<br />"''One TIE destroyed. One of ours lost, too. Lieutenant T'oknell. I think he was surprised.|Syndulla and Wyl Lark|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
[[File:Chadawa-Fedovoi-SWT.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The 204th Imperial Fighter Wing stopped in the Ciaox Verith system while traveling between Fedovoi End and Chadawa.]]
In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] stopped in the Ciaox Verith system on its journey between<ref name="Victory's Price" /> the Croynar sector<ref name="Systems" /> [[Planet|worlds]] of [[Fedovoi End]] and [[Chadawa]]. While the 204th ran a combat drill above a [[gas giant]], the [[New Republic]] refitted [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'' arrived, having followed a communications signal burst from the 204th's [[bulk freighter]] ''[[Yadeez]]'' that had been orchestrated by [[Yrica Quell]], a member of the 204th who was trying to [[Saboteur|sabotage]] the unit.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

The ''Deliverance'', [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]], had exited [[hyperspace]] in the midst of a [[Color|jade-green]] [[plasma storm]] but nevertheless found the 204th by using its scanners. Part of the [[Deliverance's starfighter wing|''Deliverance''{{'s}} starfighter wing]]&mdash;[[Starfighter Commander]] [[Wyl Lark]] and the [[X-wing starfighter]]s of [[Flare Squadron]]&mdash;moved in and [[Skirmish in the Ciaox Verith system|engaged]] the Imperial [[fighter wing]] while the refitted Star Destroyer rushed to reach firing range and deploy the rest of its [[starfighter]] wing. One 204th [[TIE series|TIE]] was destroyed and [[Lieutenant]] [[Lourgh T'oknell]] of Flare Squadron was [[Death|killed]] before the 204th's fighters were recalled to the ''Yadeez'' and the Imperial fighter wing's [[starship]]s escaped into hyperspace. The ''Deliverance'' and its wing pursued the Imperial unit across other systems in the Croynar sector, tracking it from more comm bursts.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ciaox Verith system first appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the third volume in [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Victory's Price" /> It was misspelled as "Ciaox Venith" in the first version of "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," the corresponding appendix to the [[StarWars.com]] article "[[Star Wars Galaxy Map]]"<ref name="Systems" />&mdash;both works being authored by [[Jason Fry]] and released on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The spelling was then corrected for second version of the appendix,<ref name="Systems" /> updated on [[October 13]]of that year.<ref name="Substack 2">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref>
