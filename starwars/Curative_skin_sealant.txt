A '''curative skin sealant''' was a medical cream used to seal up wounds. During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], [[Ciena Ree]] used a curative skin sealant to heal the wounds of her classmate [[Thane Kyrell]], who had been beaten up by his [[Parent|father]] [[Oris Kyrell]] the night before.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
