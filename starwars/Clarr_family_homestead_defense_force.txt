The '''Clarr family homestead defense force''', was the personal private and family defense force of the [[Clarr family]], one of the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. As [[Patriarch]] of the Clarr family, [[Clarr'ivl'exow]] commanded the force, which consisted of the [[Clarr family fleet]] of at least three [[Destroyer|Clarr destroyers]] and a [[Cruiser|Clarr escort cruiser]] named the ''[[Orisson]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
