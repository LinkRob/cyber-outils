'''Bestine''' was a [[city]] and the [[capital]] of [[Tatooine]]. Shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Luke Skywalker]] spent a night in Bestine while on their way to [[Mos Eisley]]. Imperial stormtroopers searching for C-3PO and R2-D2 intercepted a Jawa sandcrawler as it left Bestine.<ref name="CL 2016" /> The [[Tusken Raider]] [[Reirin]] encountered a hooded [[Unidentified trader (Tatooine)|trader]] selling stolen goods in the city, who offered to get her offworld in exchange for stealing a [[crystal]] from a [[Jawa]] clan.<ref name="Reirin">{{StoryCite|story=Reirin (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the city was under the control of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bestine was first mentioned in [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 1|first issue]] of ''[[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine]]'' released on [[November 16]], [[2015]].

Bestine was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends]] continuity in the [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars: A New Hope'' novelization]] which was released on [[November 12]], [[1976]] before the [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|film]].
