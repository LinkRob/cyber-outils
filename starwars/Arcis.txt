'''Arcis''' was a central company [[City|town]] on the [[planet]] [[Burnin Konn]].<ref name="OS Locations">{{WebCite|url=https://playstarwarsuprising.com/game-guide/introduction/#locations|text=Locations|work=Star Wars: Uprising Official Website|archivedate=20160624013822}}</ref> During the [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]] it was inhabited by [[Mining|miners]] and their families; however, it became overrun with hostile [[droid]]s. Aid workers were sent to provide relief aid to the families, and a [[The Smuggler|mercenary]] helped forge a path for the workers in return for payment in [[chromium]] from the miners.<ref name="SWU" /> The criminal [[Trade Spine League]] owned a [[Trade Spine League storage cave|storage cave]] within the district; however, it became overrun with vermin during the blockade. In order to retrieve the goods in the cave, the League put out a request for someone to sneak into the cave and reset the perimeter defense systems to clear the cave out.<ref name="Vermin" />
