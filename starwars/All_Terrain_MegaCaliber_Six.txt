The '''All Terrain MegaCaliber Six''' ('''AT-M6'''),<ref name="Incredible Cross-Sections" /> also known as the '''Gorilla Walker'''<ref name="SWMadEasy">''[[Star Wars Made Easy]]''</ref> or '''First Order Heavy Assault Walker''',<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2017 Topps Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi|subset=Blueprints|cardname=First Order Heavy Assault Walker|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/157503/cid/10271217/2017-Topps-Star-Wars-Journey-To-The-Last-Jedi---Blueprints-1-First-Order-Heavy-Assault-Walker}}</ref> was a mobile heavy [[artillery]] [[walker]] used by the [[First Order]] during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] with the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> As the biggest walker in the First Order's arsenal, the AT-M6 was much larger than the [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="SWMadEasy" /> and was operated by specialist [[First Order Walker Driver]]s, who were responsible for the use of the machine's most prominent feature, a single [[MegaCaliber Six turbolaser cannon]] mounted to its back<ref name="Incredible Cross-Sections" /> for which the walker was named.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/inside-two-deadly-new-first-order-vehicles-from-the-last-jedi|text=Inside Two New First Order Vehicles from ''The Last Jedi''}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
[[File:ATM6-TLJTOG.png|thumb|left|200px|Forward view of an AT-M6]]
The AT-M6 was a heavily [[armor]]ed [[walker]] utilized by the [[First Order military]] during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]]. Its design was half siege walker and half psychological weapon, with its massive height, imposing arms, and terrifying noises capable of breaking whole armies before its weapons came into range. The AT-M6 was essentially a siege platform, with the walker's entire frame based around accommodating the [[MegaCaliber Six turbolaser cannon]], a heavy [[turbolaser]] which could punch through [[Deflector shield|shields]] rated to withstand [[Orbital bombardment|planetary bombardment]] to end sieges with one swift strike. [[First Order]] researchers managed to install the giant cannon by reinforcing the front legs, giving the walker stability and the look of a giant simian predator. To power its main weapon the walker carried a dedicated [[power generator]] as well as a number of auxiliary [[fuel cell]]s that were mounted on the walker's back behind the cannon compartment to reduce the recharge time between shots.<ref name="CVNE">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref>

The AT-M6 was covered in state of the art [[Ultra-dense matrixed composite armor]] and powered by a [[Fusion generator|fusion reactor]]. Alongside the MegaCaliber cannon, it was also equipped with two forward-mounted fire-linked [[heavy laser cannon]]s, two cheek-mounted [[Anti-aircraft gun|anti-ship]] medium [[laser cannon]]s,<ref name="Incredible Cross-Sections" /> and two sets of serrated cable cutters in the front legs to deter attempts at toppling the top-heavy walker. The walker also carried a [[targeting computer]] which analyzed the input from two sets of cockpit-mounted [[targeting sensor]] banks to deliver precise shots.<ref name="CVNE" />

==History==
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> they were deployed to the [[planet]] [[Vardos]] to [[Battle of Kestro City|put down]] their [[Jinata Security|former allies]] after [[Gleb]]'s death.<ref name="SWBFII">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

<div style="float:right">[[File:AT-M6_Databank.jpg|thumb|right|200px|All Terrain MegaCaliber Six walkers were used in the Battle of Crait against the Resistance.]]</div>
Shortly after the battle on Vardos,<ref name="Timelines" /> and shortly after the [[death]] of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] and the ascension of [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] as leader of the [[First Order]], a number of these [[walker]]s led the [[Battle of Crait|charge]]<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> in a [[Veers Formation]]<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> against the [[Resistance]] [[Crait outpost|base]] on the [[planet]] [[Crait]], along with a pair of [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport]]s and [[Kylo Ren's command shuttle|Ren's]] [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|''Upsilon''-class command shuttle]], with numerous [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s and [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s providing air support.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

After the majority of Resistance [[speeder]]s and [[Resistance trooper|ground troops]] were destroyed and the remainder forced to withdraw to the heavily [[armor]]ed confines of the base proper, the AT-M6s participated in the bombardment of the [[Force projection]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]], who had come to confront Ren, his fallen [[Padawan|apprentice]]. However, the [[laser cannon]]s of the walkers had no effect on the illusion.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:AT_M6_cross_section.jpg|thumb|right|224x224px|AT-M6 cross section]]
The All Terrain MegaCaliber Six was first officially revealed on [[The Star Wars Show]] on [[August 23]], [[2017]].<ref name="SW Show">{{StarWarsShow|vTNJNRXfSIg|New The Last Jedi Vehicles Revealed, Exploring Lucasfilm's Vaults, and More!|sw_url=video/new-the-last-jedi-vehicles-revealed-exploring-lucasfilm-s-vaults-and-more}}</ref> It was created for the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Director [[Rian Johnson]] noted he aimed to have the AT-M6 resemble a gorilla.<ref>{{Film|VIII}} commentary track</ref>

A scale model of the All Terrain MegaCaliber Six, alongside several other models and props, was placed on display in the [[W:c:Disney:Star Wars Launch Bay|Star Wars Launch Bay]] attraction during the Galactic Nights event on [[December 16]], [[2017]], as part of the promotion for the then-recently released movie ''The Last Jedi''.<ref name="Orlando Business Journal 23">{{WebCite|url=https://www.bizjournals.com/orlando/news/2017/12/17/disney-reveals-new-info-and-first-looks-of-the.html|text=Disney reveals new info and first looks of the Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge expansion|author=Carchidi, Jim|date=2017-12-17|work=[https://www.bizjournals.com/orlando Orlando Business Journals]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/0qZJ4}}</ref>
