'''Aquaris Broth''' was a type of [[Diet|vegetarian]] [[soup]] that consisted of a variety of [[Color|brown]], white, and green [[Foodstuff|ingredients]] suspended in a brown liquid and served in a bowl. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] created the dish after being boarded by the [[pirate]]s known as the [[Aquaris Freeholders]] during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he made<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref>

The pirates had hoped to find the narcotic [[spice]] aboard Tuggs' [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|food freighter]], but upon learning that all he had to offer was a freezer full of [[gnooroop]] filets, they instead demanded a gourmet meal. The Artiodac then created Aquaris Broth from ingredients he had gathered on the [[planet]] [[Aquaris]] and the pirates consumed all that he made before leaving. Assuming that they must have enjoyed it, Tuggs then included the recipe for the broth in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aquaris Broth was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the soup makes use of [[Wikipedia:Water|water]], [[Wikipedia:Dashi|dashi powder]], [[Wikipedia:Miso|red miso]], cubed, medium-firm [[Wikipedia:Tofu|tofu]], [[Wikipedia:Shirataki noodles|shirataki noodles]], sliced [[Wikipedia:Shiitake|shiitake mushrooms]], a sheet of [[Wikipedia:Nori|nori]], [[Wikipedia:Kamaboko|kamaboko]], and [[Wikipedia:Scallion|green onions]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
