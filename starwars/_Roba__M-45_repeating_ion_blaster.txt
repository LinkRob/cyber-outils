The '''"Roba" M-45 repeating ion blaster''', manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]],<ref name="RSOWM" /> was a [[Heavy repeating blaster|heavy repeating]] [[ion blaster]] that saw use during the [[Galactic Civil War]], typically pintel-mounted on [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|UT-60D U-wing]] [[gunship]]s.<ref name="ROUVG" />

The M-45 could also be set to function as an ordinary blaster, and carried as a hand-held firearm. It was commonly used as such by [[Resistance]] Heavy Troopers.<ref name="BF2" />
