The '''AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport''', also known as an '''Atmospheric Assault Lander''' ('''AAL'''), '''First Order Transporter''', or '''First Order shuttle'''<ref name="The Escape" /> was an [[armor]]ed [[Troop transport|troop carrier]] operated by the [[First Order]] designed to ferry up to twenty [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] from [[orbit]]al [[Starship|vessels]] to [[planet]]ary surfaces.

==Characteristics==
[[File:AtmosphericAssaultLander-MF80.png|thumb|left|250px|Atmospheric Assault Landers with disembarkation ramp extended.]]
The [[Sienar-Jaemus Army Systems]]'s Atmospheric Assault Lander was a combat-zone rapid-deployment transport designed to ferry twenty [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] into combat in less than thirty seconds. Measuring 17.83 meters in length, it carried an additional [[gunner]] and veteran TIE pilot. It featured a disembarkation ramp at the front of the vessel, where two squads of stormtroopers in a low-light troop compartment (to preserve [[trooper]]'s night vision) would disembark as a bright spotlight above the ramp brightened the landing zone, dazzling the enemy in the process. Located directly behind the searchlight was a forward [[Deflector shield generator|deflector]] augmenter and [[sensor]] array. On top of the front section of the craft, there was a troop compartment emergency hatch. The cockpit was located near the stern, and was so narrow that the [[pilot]] was required to stand when operating the lander.<ref name="Cross" />

The transport was maneuvered by a single veteran [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|TIE pilot]] in a small, rectangular space above the vessel to provide maximum visibility, but could be controlled via consoles in the crew compartment below&mdash;albeit with less precision. Assault lander pilots were not generally concerned by the exposed vantage point, but complained of the ship's lack of maneuverability compared to that of a [[starfighter]]. The lander had two pairs of robust landing gear underneath for hard landings, and had one ramp actuator on either side of the front to stabilize the craft when the ramp was lowered. The lower sides of the craft included atmospheric ducting, heat exchanger fins, [[Atmosphere|atmospheric]] processing, and life support systems, as well as the forward [[Repulsorlift|repulsor]] array used during landing. On the outside were two pairs of terrain sensors near the stern.<ref name="Cross" /> The troop cabin was also large enough to accommodate a pair of [[125-Z treadspeeder bike]]s.<ref name="TROSVD" /> It was also possible to destroy an AAL by setting it to self-destruct, something Jarek Yeager showed during the [[Battle of Barabesh|rescue]] of [[Tamara Ryvora]].<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref>

===Armament and defenses===
<div style="float:right">[[File:AtmosphericAssaultLanderProfile-MF80.png|thumb|right|300px|The AAL's F-Z0 antipersonnel blaster cannon was located just forward of the cockpit.]]</div>
A single stormtrooper gunner protected the lander during its final approach to the drop zone, scanning the terrain from an elevated dorsal turret mounting a [[F-Z0 antipersonnel blaster cannon]]. The turret provided a 240-degree field of fire and could also be maneuvered from the crew compartment, while an elevating footplate raised the gunner into position. The blaster turret power cells were located in front of and beneath the gunner. A [[deflector shield generator]] was located on top of the craft, with a main forward deflector projector located slightly in front of it. Inside the prow was a forward deflector augmenter and sensor array for extra protection further up the front of the vessel, located behind the spotlight. Each lander had a smooth, armored hull that used voids of inert gas to dampen blaster bolt impacts, with additional external armor used to protect the engines from incoming fire.<ref name="Cross" />

Stormtroopers were deployed the moment the boarding ramp was lowered, with a first group of troopers rushing out to establish a perimeter. To do this, the soldiers often used [[FWMB-10 repeating blaster]]s, which offered a good cover fire. Two squads, consisting of twenty soldiers, would disembark in standard two-abreast formation, and could begin combat operations in as little as thirty [[second]]s, allowing the lander to evacuate the drop zone. The gunner would maintain his or her position until the drop zone was secured by the deployed troops, while the pilot acted as spotter to identify any threats.<ref name="Cross" />

===Propulsion systems===
[[File:AtmosphericAssaultLanderEngines-MF80.png|thumb|left|250px|AAL's SJAS-210 sublight ion engine]]
Each craft was powered by a [[reactor]] located in a sturdy reactor compartment in the rear, and featured four [[SJAS-210 sublight ion engine]]s that were kept powered up during landings in the event rapid extraction was required. These engines required numerous reactant ducts and pumps and an acceleration compensator to function fully. Furthermore, to give the pilot greater control of the craft, electromagnetic thrust vectoring fins were stationed over the engines' exhaust, and ion drive thrusters, an ion collision bar and ion acceleration chambers further improved the assault lander's maneuverability. Additionally, several power cells were located underneath the engines, sheltered under the frame of the vessel.<ref name="Cross" />

==History==
{{Quote|Put him on board.|Kylo Ren orders a pair of stormtroopers to put Poe Dameron on an Atmospheric Assault Lander|audio=KyloRen-PutHimOnboard.flac|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
The Atmospheric Assault Lander was used by the [[First Order]] 30 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Timeline">''[[Before the Awakening]]'' takes place shortly before [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which the ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'' notes that as taking place 30 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].</ref> They were used to deploy stormtroopers to restore order to the [[Pressy's Tumble refinery complex]] in the [[Pressylla system]].<ref name="Before">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref>

[[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] of the [[Resistance]] had access to one, labeled 226, and he used it on the mission to discover [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]]'s location after the admiral had been captured by the First Order. Dameron diverted to a planet to rescue the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]].<ref name="C-3PO Special" />

Two of the transports were deployed on an assault platform during the search for the famed explorer [[Lor San Tekka]].<ref name="Poe 1">[[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]]</ref>

[[File:WestDocks-TheDisappeared.jpg|thumb|right|250px|An AAL at the ''Colossus'']]
During the [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupation]] of [[Castilon]]'s ''[[Colossus]]'' platform, the First Order used the transports to deport residents who had been arrested for defying them. When [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza]], [[Hype Fazon]], [[Grevel]], and [[Nod]] were to be sent away in a transport, [[Kazuda Xiono]] and [[Torra Doza]] [[Rescue on the Colossus|helped]] them overpower their stormtrooper escort and steal [[Unidentified AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport|the ship]] so they could escape the planet. Aunt Z decided that she and Fazon would go to [[Takodana]], where she had a friend, and Fazon flew the transport offworld.<ref name="The Disappeared" /> The two picked up Xiono's [[Distress signal|distress call]] and later returned just in time to aid Xiono and the rest of the ''Colossus'' in escaping the planet and the First Order.<ref name="No Escape 2" /> When the ''Colossus''{{'}} [[trans-binary deflector]] began breaking down and resulting in the threat of lethal [[cosmic radiation]], Xiono, [[Neeku Vozo]], and [[CB-23]] used the same stolen First Order transport as a disguised to the same type of [[supertanker fuel depot]], the ''[[Titan]]'' controlled by the First Order to [[Infiltration of the Titan|infiltrate]] the station to get the new trans-binary deflector.<ref name="Station to Station">{{Resistance|Station to Station}}</ref> A year later, the [[Team Colossus|''Colossus'' Resistance]] used their stolen AAL to rescue [[Tamara Ryvora]] from the ocean planet Castilon. However, they were captured and taken on board Commander [[Pyre]]'s [[Thunderer|Star Destroyer]] where they escaped by crawling out from beneath the landing gear and they set the ship to [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destruct]]. Before the [[Battle of Barabesh|destruction]] of the [[Star Destroyer]], they commandeered the transport, with Kaz telling Torra to not fire on them as they escaped.<ref name="The Escape" />

[[File:Stormtroopers-raid-Jakku.png|thumb|left|250px|Four transports were deployed to Jakku.]]
Just after the mission to [[Pressy's Tumble]],<ref name="Before" /> four Assault Landers and [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren's]] [[Kylo Ren's command shuttle|command shuttle]] departed from the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]'' and arrived at [[Tuanul]], a village on [[Jakku]], to [[Attack on Tuanul|discover]] information about [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]'s exile. The landers deployed multiple squads of stormtroopers, who proceeded to engage in combat with the villagers. When Dameron was captured, two stormtroopers dragged Dameron onto one of the troop shuttles. Returning to the ''[[Finalizer]]'', FN-2187 felt significant stress from the mission, taking off his [[helmet]] inside an AAL to breathe and in order to vomit after witnessing the events on Jakku. [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] quickly noticed his distress and lack of helmet, and told him to submit his blaster to be checked, making sure he fired a shot. The landers were also used to deploy stormtroopers to [[Takodana Castle]] on Takodana in an [[Battle of Takodana|attempt]] to secure [[BB-8]], an [[astromech droid]] which had been given the information leading to Skywalker.<ref name="Episode VII" />

Several AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transports were deployed during the [[Assault on Naboo (First Order)|assault]] on [[Naboo]].<ref name="Legacy of Vader 5">{{WebCite|url=https://aiptcomics.com/2025/06/20/marvel-preview-star-wars-legacy-of-vader-5|text=Marvel Preview: Star Wars: Legacy of Vader #5|archivedate=20250620192913}}</ref>

[[File:Rey_uses_force_lightning.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Rey accidentally destroys an AAL using Force lightning.]]
During a [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|mission]] on [[Pasaana]], the First Order used two such crafts to deploy stormtroopers and to transport [[Chewbacca]], who was captured during the mission. [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] later destroyed one of the transports with [[Force lightning]], after she got into a tug of war with [[Supreme Leader]] Kylo Ren. When the First Order [[Occupation of Kijimi|occupied]] the world [[Kijimi]], they used their Assault Landers to take prisoners and "new recruits" back to the orbiting Star Destroyer.<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

===Legacy===
The [[Sith Eternal military]] employed the [[AAL-2100/9.5 atmospheric assault lander]], an updated model of the AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport, during the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="TROSVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The First Order Transporters appear in the [[2015]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. The shuttles appear in the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short series ''[[Poe to the Rescue]]''. After picking up C-3PO, Poe Dameron used it to travel to the ''Finalizer'' and rescue Admiral Ackbar.
