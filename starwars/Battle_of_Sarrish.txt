{{Quote|Sarrish? Sarrish. Wait. What was the Battle of Sarrish?''"<br />"''It was one of the Republic's most devastating losses.''"<br />"''Sarrish. Yes. Yes, I remember now. So many soldiers were dying.|Gregor and Meebur Gascon|<ref name="Missing in Action" />}}
The '''Battle of Sarrish''',<ref name="Missing in Action" /> also known as the '''invasion of Sarrish''',<ref name="Helmet Collection 21" /> was a grueling battle that took place on<ref name="Gregor">{{Databank|gregor|Gregor}}</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[Sarrish]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] during the second [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Helmet Collection 21" /> It was one of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s most devastating defeats, with many<ref name="Missing in Action" /> [[clone trooper]]s<ref name="Gregor bio 5" /> perishing in the battle<ref name="Missing in Action" /> as it turned into a rout.<ref name="Helmet Collection 64">{{HelmetCollectionCite|64|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref>

[[Clone Captain]] [[Gregor]] led [[Foxtrot Group]] into the battle before leading the retreat. Gregor managed to bring the surviving Republic forces back into [[realspace|space]], but his own [[transport]] was shot down. The only survivor on the transport, Gregor was able to reach<ref name="Helmet Collection 64" /> the remote [[Planet|world]] of [[Abafar]], where he crash-landed. He was reported missing, but a [[D-Squad|Republic team]] discovered him.<ref name="Missing in Action" />
