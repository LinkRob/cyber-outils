The '''Daughters of Ferrix''' was a citizens' society and social club<ref name="RixRoad" /> consisting of citizens of the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]]. The [[human]] [[Maarva Carassi Andor]] was a member and had once been its [[president]].<ref name="Narkina 5" /> [[Jezzi]] was also a member. Their group had a formal uniform that consisted of a long [[Color|burgundy]] robe with tan accents under a tan sash with burgundy accents.<ref name="RixRoad" />
