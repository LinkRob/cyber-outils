A '''devilfighter''' was used by [[Bly Tevin]], a [[pilot]] who served as [[Thylefire Nine]] in [[Thylefire Squadron]] of the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]. The [[devilfighter]] had originally belonged to the planet's [[prince]]s, [[Xiri A'lbaran]], though she instead gave it to Tevin.<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[starfighter]] was [[Saboteur|sabotaged]], with A'lbaran's its intended victim, though Tevin used it instead and was [[Death|killed]] during a [[skirmish in the Eiram system]].<ref name="Convergence" />
