'''''Classic Star Wars: The Early Adventures'' 4''' was the fourth issue of the ''[[Classic Star Wars: The Early Adventures]]'' series.

This particular ''Classic'' series were reformatted and colored reprints of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars (comic strip)|daily newspaper strip]] by [[Russ Manning]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Luke Skywalker is ordered by Rebel Command to abandon a reconnaissance mission. It seems that appearances of unscheduled transport capsules have coincided with the destruction of Rebel bases, and just such a capsule has landed&mdash;on Tatooine! Luke must return to his home planet, now a bustling locus of trade and a hotbed of rebellion, to discover how the Empire is finding out about the hidden Rebel bases. He finds the answer, but will it be at the cost of his life?''
