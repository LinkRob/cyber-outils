'''Beeva''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[senator]] in the [[Imperial Senate]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="One Year Later" /> he and his [[Beeva's wife|wife]]<ref name="One Year Later AD" /> attended the [[Wedding of Stekan Sculdun and Leida Mothma|wedding]] of the [[Chandrilan]]s [[Leida Mothma]] and [[Stekan Sculdun]] at the [[Mothma Estate]] on<ref name="One Year Later" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Chandrila]]. On the first [[Local day|day]] of the three-[[Standard day|day]] event, Leida's [[Parent|mother]], Senator [[Mon Mothma]], greeted Beeva and his wife while passing the couple on one of the estate's exterior stairways. Beeva greeted her back with a nod. He had dark skin and [[color|brown]] [[eye]]s. While at the [[Marriage|wedding]], Beeva wore a tan [[clothing|outfit]] with a matching [[hat]] and [[cloak]] fastened by a gold-colored loop. His [[belt]] and the loop on his hat were also golden.<ref name="One Year Later" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beeva appeared in "[[One Year Later]]," the first episode of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the live-action series [[Star Wars: Andor|''Andor'']], which was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="One Year Later" /> on [[April 22]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref> The scene in which Beeva is introduced<ref name="One Year Later" /> was filmed as a "[[Wikipedia:Long take|oner]]" and was the final visual effects shot filmed for the season, completed in collaboration between [[Industrial Light & Magic]] and [[Scanline VFX]] on [[August 5]], [[2024]].<ref name="Declassified">{{SW|url=news/andor-trivia-guide-week-4|text=Secrets from the Set of ''Andor'' Season 2: Week 4 Trivia}}</ref>
