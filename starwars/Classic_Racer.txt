The '''Classic Racer''' was a costume owned by [[Dizzy (Ugnaught)|Dizzy]], the [[Ugnaught]] member of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]], that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Wild Racer|standard costume]]. It featured a [[Color|blue]], orange, black, and silver color scheme, with silver and black goggles [[goggles]], and a red and black design on the shoulders.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Classic Racer was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.16.0 on [[March 13]], [[2023]] during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Classic Treatment]]. Unlike most Costumes in the game, Classic Racer did not share the same rarity as its cosmetic set counterpart, with Classic Treatment instead being a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap."<ref name="Hunters" /> It was initially planned to be called "Classic Suit."<ref name="Hunters Panel">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-star-wars-hunters-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: Hunters'' Panel}}</ref>
