The '''Emperor's Voice''' can refer to two positions:

*The [[Emperor's Voice (Sith Empire)|title]] used in the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Sith Empire]] to refer to the host body of then Emperor [[Tenebrae|Vitiate's]] soul; the Voice functions as the legitimate spokesperson for the Emperor and issues commands in his name.
*A [[Emperor's Voice (Galactic Empire)|high rank]] of [[Dark Side Adept]] during [[Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine's]] [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]].
