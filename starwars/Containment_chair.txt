A '''containment chair''' was a device used by [[bounty hunter]]s to secure captives. The independent bounty hunter and former [[Sith apprentice]] [[Asajj Ventress]] was known to store several containment chairs aboard the [[Banshee (starship)|''Banshee'']] during the [[Clone Wars]]. During a [[Rescue of Marg Krim's family|mission]] to rescue [[Marg Krim]]'s [[Krim|family]], Ventress secured [[Vram (Pyke)|Vram]] and [[Laalee]] in two containment chairs to prevent them from accidentally damaging her [[starship]] while she and [[Quinlan Vos]] went to rescue their [[Parent|mother]] [[Tezzka Krim]].<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>
