'''Courtsilius'''<ref name="Graphics" /> was one of the five [[planet]]s<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> that [[orbit]]ed the [[Hosnian's sun|sun]] of the [[Hosnian system]], in the [[Core Worlds|Core]] region of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Graphics" /> The [[Star system|system]], including Courtsilius, was [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroyed]] by the [[First Order]]<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" />

The planet at one point was the site of the little-remembered [[Courtsilius Wax Revolution]], an atrocity that resulted in the death of 10% of the planet's population.<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>
