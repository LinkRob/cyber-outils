'''David West Reynolds''' is an archeologist who is the author of multiple ''[[Star Wars]]'' reference books. 

==Biography==
Reynolds became fascinated with the world of ''Star Wars'' at age 13 and created a drawing of how a [[lightsaber]] worked while in middle school. After earning his PhD in archeology from the University of Michigan, Reynolds and a friend traveled to [[Tunisia]] to test his skills by discovering the shooting locations for the [[Tatooine]] sequences from the [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|original film]]. After six weeks in the Tunisian desert they had discovered "a couple dozen" locations, using archeological techniques to narrow down the vast search area, and Reynolds wrote of the findings in the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine.<ref name="Wave">{{WebCite|url=https://www.wave3.com/2023/06/30/new-albany-man-embarks-journey-find-original-star-wars-film-locations|text=New Albany man embarks on journey to find original Star Wars film locations|author=Shannon Cogan|date=2023-06-30|work=[[Wikipedia:WAVE (TV)|WAVE]]|archivedate=20230630151646}}</ref>

Around this time, [[George Lucas]] was planning the [[prequel trilogy]] and learned from his archivist that the details of the shooting locations had not been recorded. Lucas discovered Reynolds' article and realized that he was possibly the only person in the world who had the GPS coordinates. He hired Reynolds as a location scout and later as a full-time employee.<ref name="Wave" />

While writing reference books, Reynolds designed the cross section of a lightsaber, based on his previous drawing, with the addition of a [[Lightsaber crystal/Legends|lightsaber crystal]], introducing the idea that choosing their crystal is a test of the [[Jedi]]'s [[Force-sensitive|Force sensitivity]].<ref name="Wave" />

He wore the [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] armor at the opening event for ''Star Wars'' at the Smithsonian.<ref name="Boba Fett magazine">''[[Star Wars: Boba Fett]]''</ref>
