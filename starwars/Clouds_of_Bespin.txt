'''Clouds of Bespin''' was a type of [[cocktail]] that shared its name with<ref name="Topps" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]], which had an endless amount of [[cloud]]s.<ref name="Datanak">{{Databank|bespin|Bespin}}</ref> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{Topps|set=2018 Topps Star Wars: The Last Jedi Series 2|subset=Patrons of Canto Bight|cardname=#4 - Armo Malou|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/177536/cid/11396881/2018-Topps-Star-Wars-The-Last-Jedi-Series-2---Patron-of-Canto-Bight-CB-4-Armo-Malou}} describes [[Armo Malou]] during the events of {{Film|VIII}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the cocktail was developed by the professional [[bartender]] [[Armo Malou]], with the beverage gaining fame.<ref name="Topps" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clouds of Bespin was mentioned on a card for Armo Malou in the [[2018 Topps Star Wars: The Last Jedi Series 2|2018 Topps ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Series 2]] set of cards produced by [[Topps]] in [[2018]].<ref name="Topps" />
