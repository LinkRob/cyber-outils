The '''Ashiga Clan District''' was the area of [[Kijimi City]] that surrounded the [[Dai Bendu Monastery]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]]. The [[Ashiga Clan]] had full control of this area of Kijimi<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The clan owned all the properties in the district. They also operated a [[Ashiga Clan vault|vault]] in the district.<ref name="Outlaws" />

Sometime before 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> two individuals named [[Noki]] and [[Velek (Kijimi)|Velek]] had stashes valuables within the district. [[Krisk Ashiga]]'s [[keycard]] for the vault was left near Velek's stash. A copy of [[Jet Kordo]]'s journal entry about the Ashiga Clan was left by [[Noki]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> In 3 ABY,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Melitto]] [[merchant]] [[Viska (merchant)|Viska]] operated a shop within the district. A Melitto [[contract broker]] named [[Mayiu]] operated out of Viska's shop during the same year.<ref name="Outlaws" />

In 35 ABY, The Ashiga Clan district was destroyed along with the planet during the [[Destruction of Kijimi]] by the [[Sith Eternal]] with the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Derriphan]]'' of the [[Final Order]] [[fleet]].<ref name="Destruction" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ashiga Clan district first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The district was one of several areas in the game that was more accessible depending on the players reputation with the owning faction, in this case with Ashiga Clan<ref name="Outlaws" />
