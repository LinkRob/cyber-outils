'''Droid laser turrets''' were a type of [[turret-droid]]<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> armed with [[turbolaser]]s.<ref name="Databank" /> They consisted of a [[Color|gray]] oblate spheroid body with two [[barrel]]s protruding from it and a trunk ascending from the top of the spheroid to the surface that the [[turret]] was attached to. A [[droid]] [[laser turret]] was concealed within the ceiling of a [[hangar]] within the [[Trade Federation]]'s<ref name="TPM" /> modified [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|''Lucrehulk''-class LH-3210 cargo freighter]] ''[[Saak'ak]]''.<ref name="AEYTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>

During the [[blockade]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]<ref name="TPM" /> in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], including the appearance of the [[droid]] [[laser turret]], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the turret descended and fired upon the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class space cruiser]] ''[[Radiant VII]]'' on the orders of [[Viceroy of the Trade Federation|Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]]. The turret targeted the [[cruiser]]'s [[cockpit]], destroying the [[Starship|vessel]] and [[Death|killing]] its crew, [[Captain]] [[Maoi Madakor]] and [[pilot]] [[Antidar Williams]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
A droid laser turret first appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] it was first identified in the [[2018]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']].<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> The identification originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in the [[2013]] reference book of the [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2013)|same name]].<ref name="Legends">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2013)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']]</ref>
