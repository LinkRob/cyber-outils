"'''Child of Light'''" is the first ''[[RPGA|RPGA&trade; Network]]''-sanctioned tournament scenario in the [[Child of Light saga|''Child of Light'' saga]] written by [[Lee Pickler]] and released by the [[RPGA]] on behalf of [[Wizards of the Coast]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Commander Luke Skywalker wants you to locate a child who has a special gift of the Force. Of course, dark siders and the Empire may also want him. You have to get there first. Part One of the Child of Light saga.''

==Plot summary==
Four months after the [[Battle of Hoth/Legends|Battle of Hoth]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] is contacted by an individual from the planet [[Dalicron-4]] who wants their protection for a strongly [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-sensitive]] child known as the Child of Light. This boy, [[Caeleb]], cannot use [[The Force/Legends|the Force]] but strongly amplifies the abilities of any other Force user. While [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] is too busy to investigate himself, the Alliance does send a small group of agents.

The agents meet a [[Bothan/Legends|Bothan]] contact on the [[Kwenn Space Station/Legends|Kwenn Space Station]] before traveling to Dalicron-4 in the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]]. After some investigations, the agents track down the boy's family just as they are being attacked by a [[Whiphid/Legends|Whiphid]] [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] named [[Tol Skaros]]. Skaros kills both the boy's parents before he is defeated by the Rebel agents. They take Caeleb into Alliance protection, but not before an Imperial [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate/Legends|Nebulon-B escort frigate]] enters the [[Dalicron system/Legends|Dalicron system]] and places it under quarantine. With the aid of [[Thaum Rystra]], a former Rebel scout, who was on the planet at the same time, the Rebels manage to deliver Caeleb to [[Kylbar]], an aide to [[Admiral/Legends|Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar/Legends|Gial Ackbar]].
