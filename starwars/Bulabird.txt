'''Bulabirds''' were a [[species]] of [[bird]] native to the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. They had [[star]]-tipped beaks and strutted around the pebbles and sand near a [[Unidentified bar (Junari Point)|bar]] off [[Junari Point]].<ref name="ALD" /> They had good hearing.<ref name="JS">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>
