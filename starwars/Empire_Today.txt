'''''Empire Today''''' was a magazine published by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Vehicles" /> ''Empire Today'' Volume IX was published in [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> subtitled ''Let Darkness Prevail'', and celebrated the fourteenth [[Empire Day]]. ''Empire Today'' Volume IX discussed the history of the Galactic Empire and interviewed [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and a "[[Brave stormtrooper]]" about [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="FreeGalaxy" /> Another issue of ''Empire Today'' was an in-flight magazine on [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttles]]. It included articles such as "Talkin' Tarkin," "Get the Imperial look," and "10 things you never knew about superweapons". The cover of this magazine was eventually collected and published in a [[Unidentified book|book]] written by [[Poe Dameron]].<ref name="Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Empire Today'' was first mentioned in the [[2015]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Free the Galaxy]]''.<ref name="FreeGalaxy" />
