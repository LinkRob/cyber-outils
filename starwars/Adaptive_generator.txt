An '''adaptive generator''' was an experimental [[starship]] system that was capable of collecting energy from nearby exploding [[reactor]]s and channel that energy to a ship's systems.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> one was stored in a backroom at [[Jet Kordo's hideout]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Kay Vess]] talked with [[Korsaan Dwyll]] while in [[Hauler's Keep]] who told her that [[Jet Kordo]], a previous owner of her [[EML-850 light freighter]], as at a hideout at Toshara. After giving Vess the coordinates for the hideout, Dwyll told her that Kordo would probably like to see his old ship again. Vess later traveled to the hideout where she was ambushed by Dwyll and two accomplices who was hoping of [[death|killing]] Vess, taking her ship, the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', and giving it [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] to pay off a debt they had with the [[Hutt]]. After dealing with the group, Vess checked the backroom and recovered the adaptive generator<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Adaptive generators first appeared in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> They were a collectable "passive system" ship upgrade that players could find during the "Jet Kordo Hideout" intel quest. When equipped the upgrade restores some of the player's ship's [[hull]] and [[deflector shield]] when the player destroys enemy ships. When dealing with Dwyll the player has the options of fighting them, offering to pay off the debt themselves or using a positive relationship they have with the [[Hutt Clan]] to dissuade the attack.<ref name="Outlaws" />
