The '''Almas, Sweet Almas trilogy''' was a trilogy of scenarios in the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign, a supplement to the [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]. The scenarios were written by [[Adam Burns]] and released in [[2002]], in Year 2 of the campaign. The plot of the trilogy focused on thwarting the efforts of the [[Believers]] on [[Almas]].
