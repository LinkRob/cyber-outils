'''Bull rancors''' were a type of [[rancor]].<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> As a rancor, it was [[Sentience|semi-sentient]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

The [[LH75 Low Orbit Specialty Live-Haul Frigate]], a [[freighter]] manufactured by [[Rothana Heavy Engineering]], had a spatial and weight capacity equivalent to that of three fully grown bull rancors.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bull rancors were mentioned in ''[[Knights of Fate]]'', a sourcebook for [[Star Wars Roleplaying]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2018]].<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the bull rancor first appeared in the [[Sony]] Online Entertainment [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|MMORPG]] ''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'', the initial release of ''Star Wars Galaxies'', in [[2003]].<ref name="Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided">''[[Star Wars Galaxies|Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided]]''</ref> In the [[2014]] mobile strategy game ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'', a subspecies of rancor known as a [[rage rancor]]<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> shares its appearance with the bull rancor as it appeared in Legends' ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]'' video game,<ref name="TFUvg">''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]''</ref> though no connection has been made between the rage rancor and bull rancor of modern Star Wars [[canon]].
