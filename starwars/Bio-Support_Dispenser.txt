'''Bio-Support Dispensers''' were a type of medical technology which was integrated into [[armor]] during the [[Clone Wars]], and enhanced the effects of in-combat treatment of injuries sustained during battle. The system applied medication automatically when the wearer's vitals suffered. Bio-Support Dispensers included an internal [[bacta]] reservoir for sealing grievous injuries. The attachment was only able to be installed on fully enclosed suits of armor, such as [[clone trooper]] [[Clone trooper armor|armor]] or [[heavy battle armor]].<ref name="Rise" />

Models of Bio-Support Dispensers included the [[Smartmed 1000 Integrated Systems]], manufactured by [[Athakam Medtech]], and the [[Biosteady Smartmonitor]], manufactured by [[Arakyd Industries]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bio-Support Dispensers were mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" />
