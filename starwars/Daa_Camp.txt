The '''Daa Camp''' was an outpost established by [[Dylanto Daa]] and the [[Daa Corporation]] on [[Ontotho]], situated on the border of [[Fylar region|Fylar]]. While assisting the [[Fylari]], who claimed to have been attacked by the corporation, the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cere Junda]] infiltrated the camp. [[Gender|She]] discovered that her [[Jedi Master|master]], [[Eno Cordova]], who she had thought to be [[Death|dead]], had been rescued by the Daa Corporation, and believed that they were in the right.<ref name="Dark Temple 3" />
