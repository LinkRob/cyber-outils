'''Fried worrt''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] that was [[Diet|edible]] by [[Hutt]]s and was comprised of a fried [[worrt]],<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> a creature [[Homeworld|native]] to<ref name="Worrt">{{Databank|worrt|Worrt}}</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Worrt" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> in the [[Hutt]] [[Devirsivik Clan]]'s [[Devirsivik Clan's palace|palace]] on the planet [[Nal Hutta]], a [[Toydarian]] [[Unidentified Toydarian cook|cook]] served the Hutt [[Churo]] fried worrt and [[crucrarocha beetle salad]] with his [[green juice]] for breakfast, despite the fact that Churo had previously told the [[Chef|cook]] he was a [[vegetarian]]. Churo's [[Churo and Dahara's father|father]] made him eat his meal anyway and he was mocked by his cousin [[Lorko]], who shoveled an extra helping onto Churo's plate. Later that [[Local day|day]], Churo mentally recalled being forced to eat fried worrt during his breakfast.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />
