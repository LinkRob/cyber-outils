'''A Mother's Memoirs, Continued''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[April 29]], [[2004]] and took the form of the second part of [[Mother (title)/Legends|Mother]] [[Dariana]]'s memoirs. Among other remarks, she recorded an encounter she has as a young [[Tarasin]] with a [[Human/Legends|Human]] woman in the jungles.
