The '''125-Z treadspeeder bike''' was a model of [[speeder bike]] which saw service during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:TreadspeederXsection-TROSVD.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A treadspeeder cross-section]]
The [[First Order]] Treadspeeders were rugged, shielded patrol [[speeder bike]]s which had a design that granted greater traction on unstable terrain. Two of these speeders could be carried aboard an [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport]]. Its heavy laser cannons were manufactured by [[Sonn-Blas Corporation|Sonn-Blas]], its shields by [[Chempat]].  Its outer treads were made of [[plasteel]].<ref name="ROSVD" /> It was capable of defeating countermeasures that would jam traditional [[speeder]]s.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|DiscussingFilm|status/1117111974121357312|Discussing Film|quote=The first look at the First Order Treadspeeder in 'STAR WARS: THE RISE OF SKYWALKER' has been officially revealed at #SWCC.|archivefile=File:DiscussingFilm-TreadSpeeder-April-13-2019.png}}</ref> The treadspeeder bike was designed to catapult [[First Order jet trooper]]s into the [[Atmosphere|air]] during combat operations.<ref name="Episode IX" />

The treadspeeder was a hybrid design combining aspects of treaded and repulsorlift vehicles. A track impeller drove the vehicle, while low-yield, jamming-resistant repulsorlifts acted as secondary stabilizers. The forward track, known as the Ripper, wrapped around the drive wheel and grabbed at the ground. The Treadspeeder should have been much heavier and more unwieldy, but due to the repulsorlifts, the tail section was kept aloft. A rear thruster boosted both maneuverability and speed.<ref name="CVNE">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref>

[[File:125ZTreadspeederBike-TROSPromoYT.png|thumb|left|250px|Profile view of a 125-Z treadspeeder]]
Unlike most scout vehicles, the Treadspeeder utilized shielding technology. Usually, dust particles disrupted the shielding process, but First Order engineers found a solution. A refreshing cooling system let the shields remain active at a diminished state. When the stormtroopers helmet detected an incoming threat, the shields would activate fully, blocking any harm.<ref name="CVNE" />

For weapons, the Treadspeeder featured two heavy laser cannons on outriggers. These cannons relied on their own battery packs and not the main speeder cell. If those were not enough, the passengers and pilot could also supplement with small arms fire.<ref name="CVNE" />

They were [[pilot]]ed by [[First Order Treadspeeder Driver]]s, specialized [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] who, in certain instances, were encased in a variant of the [[First Order stormtrooper armor]].<ref name="ROSVD" /> Otherwise, the drivers donned the standard stormtrooper [[armor]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==History==
[[File:TreadspeederPasaana-TROS.png|thumb|right|300px|The First Order utilized 125-Z treadspeeder bikes on Pasaana.]]
Advancements in anti-repulsorlift technology made alternative scout bikes an option. The First Order had perfected high-yield repulsorlift jamming, and so it saw the advantage in having vehicles immune to such devices.<ref name="CVNE" />

One such speeder was used by a [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper sergeant|sergeant of the First Order's 709th Legion]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] to [[Battle of Batuu|take down]] two criminals who had pinned down members of the [[709th Legion]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 4" />

The First Order deployed them during the [[Blockade of Kashyyyk]], but they were all destroyed singlehandedly by the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]], who was freeing the captives.<ref name="SW Adventures 28" />

A pair of treadspeeders, each carrying a treadspeeder driver and a jet trooper, pursued Resistance [[agent]]s [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], [[Finn]], [[Poe Dameron]], [[Chewbacca]], and [[droid]]s [[C-3PO]] and [[BB-8]] during a [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|mission]] to [[Pasaana]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
