The '''Alderaan Refugee Conference''' was a diplomatic conference held by [[Senator|members]] of the [[Galactic Senate]] on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The conference was held to address the growing number of [[refugee]]s who were displaced by the [[Galactic Republic]]'s war against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the conference, [[bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing]]&mdash;under contract from the aligned Separatist [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] the [[Hutt]]&mdash; in revenge for his failure to [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and for his imprisonment; attempted to assassinate [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. The bounty hunter was stopped by [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and arrested before she could kill the Senator.<ref name="Assassin" />
