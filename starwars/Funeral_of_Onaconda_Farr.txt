{{Quote|And we are all aware that Onaconda was a flawed man. He knew better than anyone of his own mistakes, but he never compromised his principles, and what he did, he did for his people. Their needs always came first.|Silood leads Onaconda Farr's funeral|<ref name="Senate Murders" />}}
A '''funeral''' for [[Senator]] [[Onaconda Farr]] was held on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] a few days after his [[death]].<ref name="EpGuideGallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/senate-murders-episode-gallery|text="Senate Murders" Episode Gallery}}</ref>

Farr's aide [[Silood]] held the eulogy while a detachment of [[Honor Guard]]s from the [[Senate Guard]] served as the ceremonial honor guard. Among the individuals that attended the ceremony were Farr's other aide [[Lolo Purs]] and his closest friends Senators [[Padmé Amidala]], [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], and [[Mon Mothma]]. [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], [[Vice Chair]] of the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] [[Mas Amedda]], [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] of [[Mandalore]], and [[Jedi Master]]s [[Yoda]] and [[Mace Windu]] from the [[Jedi High Council]] were also in attendance as were several other senatorial colleagues including [[Riyo Chuchi]], [[Orn Free Taa]], and even Farr's political rival, [[Mee Deechi]].<ref name="Senate Murders" />

After the funeral, Silood departed for Rodia with Farr's body. It was then that Chancellor Palpatine invited several of the attendees to [[Emperor's office|his office]] where [[Lieutenant]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tan Divo]] of the [[Coruscant Security Force|Coruscant Police]] revealed that Farr had been murdered.<ref name="Senate Murders" />
