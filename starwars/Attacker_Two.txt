'''Attacker Two''' was a [[heavy cruiser]], designated as such by the crew of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[warship]] [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']], who came under [[Attack at Primea|attack]] from the [[heavy cruiser]] and three others above the [[Vak]] [[homeworld]] of [[Primea]]. The [[Lioaoin Regime]] sent the [[cruiser]]s to prevent the rescue of [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] from Primea on behalf of the [[Nikardun Destiny]]. In the attack, the cruiser designated Two provided flanking fire from a long range, along with the cruiser [[Attacker One|One]]. The ''Vigilant'' was able to escape the attack in the shadow of [[Attacker Four]], which had suffered damage from the [[Thrawn's patrol boat|Vak fighter]] [[pilot]]ed by Thrawn.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
