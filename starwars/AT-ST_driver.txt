'''AT-ST drivers''', also called '''AT-ST pilots''',<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> were [[Imperial Army]] [[Imperial combat driver|combat drivers]] specialized in operating the [[All Terrain Scout Transport]].<ref name="RotJ" />

==Training==
Largely pulled from the [[human]] [[species]], two [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] [[pilot]]s were trained to be primarily responsible for keeping an AT-ST [[walker]] moving at speed through uneven terrain, utilizing its offensive capabilities for reconnaissance and anti-personnel hunting missions.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />

==Uniform and equipment==
[[File:Atstcrew.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Two AT-ST drivers in uniform]]
AT-ST pilots wore one-piece light [[Color|grey]] jumpsuits with basic [[armor]] plating underneath<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> and [[Imperial crest]]s on both shoulders.<ref name="RotJ" /> The drivers' feet were protected by a pair of calf-high black [[boot]]s, and their hands by [[fire]]-resistant black [[gauntlet]]s. The [[belt]] that came with the jumpsuit was made of a black, [[leather]]-like material, with a metallic buckle featuring an [[Officer's disk|identity disk]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />

AT-ST pilots wore a field grey open-face helmet<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> consisting of a dome with a rim and a helmet skirt. Identity disks were positioned on both sides of the skirt, and a [[telemetry receiver unit]] was attached to the right side of the dome, aligned to the rim. A black trim covered the edge of the skirt and the forehead of the dome. A black chin strap helped holding the helmet in place.<ref name="RO Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> A pair of blast [[goggles]]<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> that had a square-like frame with angled sides and dark lenses protected the driver's [[eye]]s.<ref name="RotJ" />

Although they were not expected to replace standard ground-based infantry, AT-ST drivers carried [[blaster]]s, grenades, [[thermal detonator]]s, emergency [[flare]]s, and a [[comlink]] in case of emergency.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />
