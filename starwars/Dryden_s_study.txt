'''Dryden's study''' was the office of [[Dryden Vos]], a [[crime lord]] and the public leader of [[Crimson Dawn]]. Vos kept several artifacts and frozen trophies in his office, which was on the top level of the ''[[First Light]]''. He was ultimately [[Death|killed]] in the study by his [[Lieutenant (criminal)|lieutenant]] [[Qi'ra]], who then contacted [[Maul]], the true leader of [[Color|Crimson]] Dawn, from [[Dryden's desk|Vos' desk]].<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dryden's study appears in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Solo" /> and was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Solo Guide" />

According to script writer [[Jon Kasdan]], some of the artifacts were meant to foreshadow Maul's appearance.<ref name="Jon's Twitter">{{Twitter|JonKasdan|status/1040529517956161536|[[Jon Kasdan]]|quote=concluded|archivedate=20200114055642}}</ref>
