The '''DLT-18 laser rifle''',<ref name="IG-RM Thug Droids" /> also known as the '''DLT-18 heavy blaster rifle''', was a model of [[heavy blaster rifle]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]].<ref name="IG-RM Thug Droids" /> The [[human]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Dengar]] carried a DTL-18 during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Bounty" /> 
A rugged variation of the DLT-18 was used by the rebel leader [[Steela Gerrera]] as a [[sniper rifle]]. Steela’s sniper rifle was modified with a [[scope]] and a thinner barrel.<ref name="A War on Two Fronts" /> The bounty hunter [[Onca]] carried a DLT-18.<ref name="The Box">{{TCW|The Box}}</ref> Several members of [[Hondo Ohnaka]]’s [[Ohnaka Gang|pirate gang]]<ref name="Bound for Rescues">{{TCW|Bound for Rescue}}</ref> and former governor [[Ryder Azadi]] used a similar rugged rifle.<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref> 

[[Lothal]]-based [[crime lord]] [[Cikatro Vizago]], leader of the [[Broken Horn Syndicate]], equipped his [[IG-RM bodyguard and enforcer droid]]s with DLT-18s,<ref name="IG-RM Thug Droids" /> as did the [[Mining Guild]].<ref name="Crawler Commandeers" />
