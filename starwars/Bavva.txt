'''Bavva''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Private security">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: "Private Security"</ref> in the [[Bavva system]] in the [[Anoat sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Appendix" /> along a [[Gerrenthum—Eriadu hyperspace route|hyperspace route]].<ref name="BMF" /> Over several [[Standard month|months]] during the [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[sector]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], a [[Unidentified merchant (Bavva)|merchant]] on Bavva was robbed several times. The [[merchant]] then offered payment for an experienced individual to provide them personal security.<ref name="Private security" />

{{GameMechanics}}
A [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] working with the [[Trade Spine League]] sent a member of their crew to protect the merchant on Bavva in exchange for the payment.<ref name="Private security" /> When the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]] launched a surprise attack on an [[Imperial outpost (Bavva)|Imperial outpost]] on Bavva, the smuggler also sent some of their hard-hitting crew there to help.<ref name="Surprise Attack" />
