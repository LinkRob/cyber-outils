'''Forosnags''' were a [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[species]] with several pairs of [[Limb|arms]] and six rows of [[Tooth|teeth]]. They were immune to [[blaster]] fire due to their ability to swallow it. Forosnags were used by the [[Forosnag Attack Battalion]] of [[Sef Con]], who confronted a team led by [[Han Solo]] during a [[Blockade of Substation Grimdock|standoff]] at [[Substation Grimdock]]. The [[slicer]] [[Peekpa]] hacked into the [[prison]] and opened panels in the floor below the forosnags and the Sef Con troopers, causing them to fall into the lower levels. Some of the forosnags survived, but were eaten by [[bastak]]s.<ref name="Last Shot" />
