The '''Black Hole of Nakat''' was a [[black hole]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]] that lay in grid square N-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Black Hole of Nakat was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Black Hole of Nakat/Legends|Black Hole of Nakat]] was first mentioned in ''[[Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter]]'', a [[2001]] novel by [[Michael Reaves]].<ref name="Maul">''[[Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter]]''</ref>
