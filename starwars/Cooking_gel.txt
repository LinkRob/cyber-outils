'''Cooking gel''' was a sticky [[Fire|flammable]] [[Color|yellow]] substance.<ref name="BH6" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bounty Hunters 6|''Bounty Hunters'' 6]], in which the cooking gel appears, to [[3 ABY]].</ref> while in the [[Spur Orbiting Market]] with the [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]], the [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Cadeliah]] threw cooking gel on an [[Unidentified Arcona bounty hunter|Arcona bounty hunter]] who was fighting against Valance. Valance used his [[palm blaster]]s to set the liquid on fire and burn the [[Arcona]] to [[death]]. The cooking gel was contained in bottles that were red and white.<ref name="BH6" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cooking gel appeared in the [[Bounty Hunters 6|sixth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series, published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="BH6" /> on [[October 21]], [[2020]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80125/star_wars_bounty_hunters_2020_6|text=Star Wars: Bounty Hunters (2020) #6}}</ref> The comic was written by [[Ethan Sacks]] and illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]] and [[Arif Prianto]].<ref name="BH6" />
