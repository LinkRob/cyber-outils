'''2022 Topps ''Star Wars'' Chrome Galaxy''''' is a trading card set produced by [[Topps]]. The cards were released in hobby boxes.

The 100-card base set consists of ''[[Star Wars]]''-inspired artwork, with many of the art coming from previous Topps releases. The base set comes in various parallels, including a base Refractor, Atomic Refractor (serial numbered to 150), Wave Refractor (serial numbered to 99), Prism Refractor (serial numbered to 75), Mojo Refractor (serial numbered to 50), Purple Refractor (serial numbered to 25), X-Fractor (serial numbered to 10), Red Refractor (serial numbered to 5), and SuperFractor (serial numbered to 1).<ref name="CardCon">{{CardCon|url=2022-topps-chrome-star-wars-galaxy-trading-cards|text=2022 Topps Chrome Star Wars Galaxy Trading Cards Checklist and Odds}}</ref>

The Gameplay Galaxy insert set features Star Wars video game cover art. Parallels for the Gameplay Galaxy set consist of Green (serial numbered to 99), Purple (serial numbered to 50), Orange (serial numbered to 25), Red (serial numbered to 5), and SuperFractor (serial numbered to 1).<ref name="CardCon" />

The Original Trilogy Concept Art insert set features concept art used for the early renderings of characters from the [[Original trilogy|Original Trilogy]]. Parallels for the Original Trilogy Concept Art set consist of Green (serial numbered to 99), Purple (serial numbered to 50), Orange (serial numbered to 25), Red (serial numbered to 5), and SuperFractor (serial numbered to 1).<ref name="CardCon" />

The Retro Rewind insert set features art from the ''[[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO]]'' and [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']] animated series. Parallels for the Retro Rewind set consist of Green (serial numbered to 99), Purple (serial numbered to 50), Orange (serial numbered to 25), Red (serial numbered to 5), and SuperFractor (serial numbered to 1).<ref name="CardCon" />
