'''Dan Patrascu''' is a machinist and model maker at [[Industrial Light & Magic]]. Working alongside [[John Knoll]] and [[John Goodson]], Patrascu is responsible for creating the miniature model of the ''[[Onyx Cinder]]'' used for the [[2024]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]''.<ref name="Paintings and Models" />
