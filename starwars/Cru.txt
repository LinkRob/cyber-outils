The '''cru''' was a [[species]] of small, winged creatures. For their size, they had large noses and ears. Two front sharp teeth stuck out of the mouth. During the [[High Republic Era]], a [[Gender|male]] named [[Cham Cham]] was the pet of the [[Force-sensitive]], [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]].

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:ChamCham-2021THRA2.png|thumb|left|145px|Cham Cham attacking members of the Nihil]]
The cru were small creatures with [[Color|brown]] skin and black [[eye]]s. They were [[Diet|carnivorous]] creatures with sharp teeth. Some of their teeth stick out of their mouth. Relative to their size, the cru had two very large ears and a large nose. Black stripes were located on the back head. Cru also had jagged wings that were used for flying. On their wings, one sharp claw was located on the top of each wing. The cru also possessed a long brown tail relative to their size.<ref name="HRA 2" /> On each [[foot]], a cru has two toes.<ref name="HRA 3">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 3|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 3]]</ref> After a meal a cru would fall [[Sleep|asleep]].<ref name="HRA 2" />

==Cru in the Galaxy==
In [[232 BBY]]<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 2|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 2]] to [[232 BBY]].</ref> a cru named [[Cham Cham]] was the pet of the [[Force-sensitive]], [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]]. Cham Cham was with Zeen when the [[Nihil]] launched an [[Attack on Trymant IV|attack]] on the [[planet]] [[Trymant IV]].<ref name="HRA 1" /> During the attack [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] used Cham Cham to help locate Zeen's friend [[Krix Kamerat]].<ref name="HRA 2" />

Wild Cru could be found on the [[moon]] [[Nar Shaddaa]].<ref name="WOTBH 4&Z">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – 4-LOM & Zuckuss 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – 4-LOM & Zuckuss'' 1]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The cru first appeared in [[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 1]] which was written by [[Daniel José Older]] with [[Rebecca Nalty]] serving as the colorist.<ref name="HRA 1" />
