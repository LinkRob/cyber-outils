'''Fraser Kelly''' (born [[November 30]], [[2001]] [[Wikipedia:Scunthorpe|Scunthorpe]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]]) is an [[Wikipedia:English people|English]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]]. In [[2018]], Kelly portrayed a [[scrumrat]] in the  ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.

==Biography==

Fraser Kelly, also known as Fraser Patrick Kelly,<ref name="Full Name">{{Instagram|username=fraserpatrickkelly|name=Fraser Kelly|archivedate=20190415192747}}</ref> was born on [[November 30]], [[2001]],<ref name="Birthday" /> in [[Wikipedia:Scunthorpe|Scunthorpe]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="Grim Telegraph" /> In [[2015]], he co-starred alongside [[Richard Armitage]] in the [[Wikipedia:Drama (film and television)|drama film]] ''Urban & the Shed Crew''.<ref name="Armitage">{{WordPress|subdomain=richardarmitageblog|url=2015/10/24/scunthorpe-schoolboy-fraser-kelly-stars-with-anna-friel-richard-armitage-in-major-new-movie-urban-and-the-shed-crew|text=Scunthorpe schoolboy Fraser Kelly stars with Anna Friel & Richard Armitage in major new movie Urban and the Shed Crew|name=Richard Armitage Blog|author=Armitage, Richard|date=2015-10-24|archivedate=20161206002906}}</ref>

In [[2017]], Kelly auditioned for a role in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', but was not aware what film he was trying out for at the time. After the audition, he did not think he had landed a role, but received communication a few months later that he had been cast. Kelly then researched the film, which was then under the codename "Redcup," and realized it was a part of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise. Despite having been a ''Star Wars'' fan when he was younger, he had not seen any of the films released after [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] bought the franchise from [[George Lucas]] in [[2012]]. Kelly opted to not watch the films he had not yet seen so that he could portray the part in his own way. He and his mother traveled to [[Pinewood Studios]] to film his speaking role,<ref name="Grim Telegraph" /> and in the film's credits, he is listed as "Eager [[Scrumrat]]."<ref name="Credits">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> The film was released on [[May 25]], 2018.<ref name="Solo">{{SW|url=films/solo|text=''Solo: A Star Wars Story''}}</ref>
