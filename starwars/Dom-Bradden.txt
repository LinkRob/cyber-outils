'''Dom-Bradden''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="MVG" /> in the [[Dom-Bradden system]] in the [[Lol sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Galactic Empire]] maintained an [[Dom-Bradden Imperial outpost|outpost]] on Dom-Bradden, but abandoned the facility after the Empire's collapse. As a result, a group of [[Klatooinian raiders]] scavenged an [[All Terrain Scout Transport]] from the outpost and used it in raids throughout the surrounding region of space.<ref name="MVG" />
