'''Crash's shipyard hideout''' was a [[shipyard]] hideout that was located in [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="Heir" />

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The hideout appeared in a flashback in "[[Chronicles of Corellia: Heir Apparent]]," a short story written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published in the [[Star Wars Insider 227|227th]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="Heir" /> on [[August 27]], [[2024]], as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
