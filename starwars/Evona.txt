'''Evona''' was an entity revered by the [[Hutt]] [[Gender|male]] [[Churo]], which he considered to be "mighty."<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|BTN}}</ref> while [[Mission on Palagosal|trapped]] on the [[Arkanis sector]] [[planet]] [[Palagosal]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], Churo prayed to Evona as he attempted to send a message to [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] to request that he travel to Palagosal to rescue Churo, as well as several Jedi and [[Galactic Republic]] individuals who were also trapped with him. Jomaram received the message on the [[Elite-class vessel|''Elite''-class vessel]] ''[[Innovator]]'', which he watched via [[hologram]], and traveled to the planet afterward.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Evona was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2024]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Evona/Legends|Evona]] was one of the [[Binary star system/Legends|two]] [[Star/Legends|suns]] of the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] [[Homeworld/Legends|homeworld]] [[Varl/Legends|Varl]]. It was first mentioned in the first edition of ''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]'', written by [[Troy Denning]] and released in [[1989]] as a supplement to [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Galaxy Guide 4">''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]''</ref>
