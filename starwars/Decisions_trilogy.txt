The '''Decisions trilogy''' was a trilogy of scenarios from year four of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'' campaign]]. It focused on the the [[Thaereian military]] and the attempts to expel it from the [[Cularin system/Legends|Cularin system]].
