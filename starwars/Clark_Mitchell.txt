'''Clark Mitchell''' is an artist who created the packaging art for the [[Hasbro]] ''Unleashed'' figurines as well as the artwork for their [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] toy line. He was also commissioned to create an illustrated piece exclusively for [[Celebration IV]].
