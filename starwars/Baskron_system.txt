The '''Baskron system''' was located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> Situated within the [[Trax sector]] in grid square Q-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Baskron Pirates]].<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Baskron [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix released alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]].<ref name="Systems" /> Both works were written by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
