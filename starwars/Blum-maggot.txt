A '''blum-maggot''' was a type of [[maggot]].<ref name="Jaxxon" /> Between [[5 ABY]] and [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Hyperspace Stories Annual—Jaxxon 2023]]'' take place after the [[Galactic Civil War]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' establishes [[Battle of Jakku|ended]] in [[5 ABY]]. The issue also occurs while [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] is a [[senator]] in the [[New Republic]]. [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]&mdash;which ''Timelines'' dates the events of to [[28 ABY]]&mdash;depicts Organa's resignation as senator. Therefore, ''Hyperspace Stories Annual&mdash;Jaxxon 2023'' must take place between 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] and 28 ABY.</ref> the [[Dulok]] [[King]] [[Gorneesh]] referred to the [[Ewok]] [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]] as a "snivelling blum-maggott" when Warrick ran to attack a group of Duloks at their [[Dulok camp (Chirpa)|camp]] on<ref name="Jaxxon" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]].<ref name="Jaxxon" />
