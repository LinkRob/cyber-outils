'''Brian Lemay'''  was a layout artist for the [[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO|''Droids'']] and [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']] animated series. Having graduated from an animation program in 1980, Lemay has worked in the field for more than three decades, working on shows like ''Inspector Gadget'', ''Smurfs'', and ''Care Bears'', also branching out into drawing comic characters and sculpting. He is a teacher and has written a number of books on animation.<ref name="Resume">{{WebCite|url=http://www.brianlemay.com/Pages/aboutbrian/resume.html|text=Brian's Resume & Teaching Portfolio|work=[http://www.brianlemay.com brianlemay.com]|archivedate=20210102194101}}</ref>
