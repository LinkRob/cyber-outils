'''Darlyn Boda''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Anoat sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. During the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Iron Blockade]], Darlyn Boda had a criminal network which included the [[Trade Spine League]]<ref name="SoD">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: "Seeds of Distrust"</ref> and the [[Ivax Syndicate]].<ref name="BiDB">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: "Bodyguard in Darlyn Boda"</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
A [[black market]] dealer on the planet offered a selection of valuable materials as payment for anyone who would rough up a number of thugs and gang members who were driving away his clientele,<ref name="RT" /> and at another point during the blockade, one of the smaller gangs on Darlyn Boda attempted to discredit the Trade Spine League's integrity by spreading false rumors of their dealings. In response, a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] assigned a crew member to stop them.<ref name="SoD" />
{{EndGame}}

By the time of the Iron Blockade, a group of [[New Republic]] pilots were stranded in the planet pursued by [[Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s forces. Adelhard personally joined his [[stormtrooper]]s on the planet where he berated the Rebels' commitment to the [[New Republic]], personally executing a pilot who stood up to him.<ref name="IR2">[[Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising 2|''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 2]]</ref>
