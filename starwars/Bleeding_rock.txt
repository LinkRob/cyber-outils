{{Quote|Still, that didn't stop me from riskin' a quick pit stop on Li-Toran to pick up a special ingredient&mdash;a sweet, sticky sap harvested from an organic 'bleedin' rock' found on the planet's surface. Melittos can't get enough of the stuff (though I'm not really sure how they consume it, seein' that those weird bug faces of theirs don't seem to have any mouths on 'em).|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
The '''bleeding rock''' was a [[coral]]-like organism [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Li-Toran]]. The organism grew within the thick [[Water|steam]] of the world's underground tunnels, which contained air with much higher densities of toxic compounds than Li-Toran's already toxic surface [[atmosphere]].<ref name="Cyphers" /> Bleeding rock also grew on the surface of Li-Toran where the native [[Melitto]] [[species]] harvested<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> the [[Color|crimson]] [[Bleeding-rock sap|sap]] of the organism to make up the primary component of their [[sugar]]y liquid [[diet]]. Melittos leaving Li-Toran used [[special survival device]]s to simulate the [[sap]] by distilling local [[foodstuff]]s.<ref name="Cyphers" />

The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited Li-Toran to collect some bleeding-rock sap during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs then used the sap to make the dish known as [[Melitto Sap Cake]]s and mentioned the bleeding rock in the recipe for the [[cake]]s that he included in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bleeding rock was first mentioned in ''[[Cyphers and Masks]]'', a [[2018]] supplement for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Cyphers" />
