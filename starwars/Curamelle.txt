'''Curamelle''' was a [[city]] located on the [[planet]] [[Corulag]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> of the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Corulag system]]</ref> Eleven [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Jakku]], in which the [[New Republic]] achieved a decisive final victory over the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' takes place in [[5 ABY]] per the reasoning [[Template:VictorysPriceDate|here]].</ref> former [[New Republic pilot]] [[Chass na Chadic]] resided in Curamelle. [[Gender|She]] soon moved to [[Crullov]], where the rent would be cheaper, as her New Republic pension was on the verge of expiration.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Curamelle was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the final entry of [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Victory's Price" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Curamelle/Legends|Curamelle]] first appeared in "[[Small Favors]]," a short story by [[Paul Danner]] published in [[February]] [[1997]] in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 12|twelfth issue]] of [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'' magazine.<ref name="Leg">{{JournalCite|12|Small Favors}}</ref>
