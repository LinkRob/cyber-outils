'''Delweed''' was a [[grain]] found on the [[planet]] [[Thorum]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], it was one of Thorum's minor exports and was popular offworld.<ref name="CC" /> Around [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The chapter of ''[[Crimson Climb]]'' mentioning delweed takes place a few [[Standard month|months]] after [[Han Solo|Han]] escaped [[Corellia]], which equates to around [[13 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Crimson Dawn]] operative [[Qi'ra]]&mdash;using the name Elesa Norre&mdash;posed as a [[Plant|grain]] buyer while [[Infiltration of the Hammers|infiltrating]] Thorum's [[The Hammers (Syndicate)|the Hammers]] criminal syndicate.<ref name="CC" />

She met with the Hammers{{'s}} leader, [[Dennet Clyde]], demanding to buy his organization's delweed. Surprised by the inquiry, Clyde informed her of the Hammers's delivery limitations due to their [[transport]]s malfunctioning. After Qi'ra insisted she had her own [[Starship|ships]] and wanted to purchase the product directly, Clyde left to draw up the sale agreement. Qi'ra quickly departed with a promise to return the following [[Standard day|day]]; however, she instead returned a few [[Local day|days]] later, signing the provided delweed contracts with her false name. Shortly afterward, the strike phase of Crimson Dawn's ploy initiated, [[Death|killing]] Clyde as retribution for encroaching on offworld contracts in Crimson Dawn territory.<ref name="CC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Delweed was mentioned in ''[[Crimson Climb]]'', a [[2023]] young-adult novel written by [[E. K. Johnston]].<ref name="CC" />
