'''Ferroceramic''' was a material that could be used to make [[armor]]. The bodies of [[BT-series ordnance droid]]s were coated in [[Color|brown]] and silver colored ferroceramic armor.<ref name="FO" />

{{Substance-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ferroceramic was mentioned in the [[2018]] roleplaying supplement [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']], which was released by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] as part of the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']].<ref name="FO" /> The material originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[1999]] novel ''[[X-Wing: Isard's Revenge]]'', which was written by [[Michael A. Stackpole]].<ref name="X-Wing">''[[X-Wing: Isard's Revenge]]''</ref>
