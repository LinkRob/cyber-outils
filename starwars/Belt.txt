{{Youmay|an item of [[clothing]]|the [[Vex Asteroid Belt]], also known as "the Belt," or [[Belt (disambiguation)|other uses]] of the word "belt"|linebreak=1}}
[[File:CountDookuBelt-SWBC61.png|thumb|right|300px|[[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s leather belt]]
A '''belt''' was an article of [[clothing]] worn around one's waist. They could be simply functional or they could make an outfit more interesting. Belts often had pouches and [[Holster/Legends|holsters]]. One known material from which belts were made was rare [[Rancor]] [[leather]], known to be worn by [[Count Dooku]].<ref>[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]</ref>

One type of belt was a [[gravity belt]], which could create artificial [[gravity]] around an individual.<ref name="PD6">[[Poe Dameron 6|''Poe Dameron'' 6]]</ref> The [[cyborg]] [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] belt held some of the controls for [[Darth Vader's armor|his armor's]] life support system.<ref name="LotS">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref>
