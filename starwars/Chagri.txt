'''Chagri''' was the native [[language]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Chagrian]] [[species]]. A pleasant [[tongue]], it was rarely heard outside of the Chagrian [[homeworld]] of [[Champala]] as Chagrians that traveled offworld learned [[Galactic Basic Standard]] with ease.<ref name="LBE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chagri was mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Lead by Example]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="LBE" /> The language originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the ''[[Alien Anthology]]'', a [[2001]] roleplaying supplement for [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="LECG">''[[Legacy Era Campaign Guide]]''</ref>
