The '''All Terrain Defense Turret (AT-DT)'''<ref name="TOG" /> was a bipedal [[walker]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was a slow walker due to the size of its main [[cannon]], which fired a massive shot that exploded on impact. It was designed for long-range strikes before infantry troops were deployed.<ref name="DoRVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> While firing, it was rendered immobilized. It was vulnerable to anti-vehicle weapons as a result of its light [[armor]]. It also possessed a front armor plate with artillery flashback shielding, and like the [[All Terrain Scout Transport]] (AT-ST) had a flap to cover the peephole in case of heavy fire.

==History==
[[File:ATDTprofile-SWBC51.png|thumb|left|200px|Profile view of an AT-DT]]
In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="TOG" /> during the [[Mimban Campaign]], several [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] AT-DTs were deployed on [[Mimban]] by the [[224th Imperial Armored Division]] to fight against the forces of the [[Mimbanese Liberation Army]]. They were delivered to the battlefield by [[Y-45 armored transport hauler]]s.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

[[Time|Sometime]] prior to the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[L3-37]] met with an [[Er'Kit (species)|Er'Kit]] [[arms dealer]] and acquired a shipment of All Terrain Defense Turrets. Calrissian and L3, however, were [[Ambush in the Halthor sector|ambushed]] in the [[Halthor sector]] when an Imperial [[interdictor]] dropped out from [[hyperspace]]. As a result of the ambush, Calrissian lost the entire shipment.<ref name="Commander" />

After the [[Battle for Tatooine]], the [[Tatooine rebel cell|rebel cell]] based on [[Tatooine]] came into possession of Calrissian's holorecordings, including [[Holojournal Entry 1983712]], and received chatter from the criminal underworld of the older and heavily-armed AT-DT walkers being utilized. [[Saponza]] deduced that someone must have found Calrissian's shipment, and that people would be hunting for the rest of the cargo. Saponza and [[Saponza's partner|his partner]] embarked on a [[mission to recover Lando Calrissian's shipment]].<ref name="Commander" />

[[File:Rebel_AT-DT_SW-C.jpg|right|thumb|A Rebel AT-DT]]
During the Galactic Civil War, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] utilized stolen AT-DTs and re-purposed them to suit their needs. Rather than being standard [[Color|gray]] like the AT-DTs used by the Empire, the Rebel Alliance's had a dark gray and orange-red color scheme with a touch of white.<ref name="Commander" />

==Behind the scenes==
The first footage of the All Terrain Defense Turret was shown in the official trailer for ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' released on [[April 8]], [[2018]].<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|jPEYpryMp2s|Solo: A Star Wars Story Official Trailer|sw_url=video/official-trailer-solo-a-star-wars-story}}</ref> The AT-DT design started out as a [[Wikipedia:Nazi Germany|German]] [[Wikipedia:8.8 cm Flak 18/36/37/41|8.8 cm Flak 18/36/37/41]] [[Wikipedia:Anti-aircraft warfare|anti-aircraft gun]] on the body of an All Terrain Scout Transport, with the concept and model built by [[Mark O'Kane]].<ref name="Solo art">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
