'''Deborah Kara Unger''' is a Canadian actress. She was the voice of the SCORPIO [[Artificial intelligence/Legends|artificial intelligence]] [[Droid/Legends|droid]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.

==Biography==
Deborah Kara Unger was born in Vancouver, British Columbia, to a mother who was a nuclear specialist and a father who was a [[Wikipedia:Gynaecology|gynaecologist]]. She was the first ever Canadian to be accepted into [[Wikipedia:Australia|Australia's]] [[Wikipedia:National Institute of Dramatic Art|Institute of Dramatic Art]]. Unger now lives in [[Wikipedia:Los Angeles|Los Angeles]].<ref name="Ojumu, Akin" />
