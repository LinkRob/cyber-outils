The '''E-Web (15)''' was a [[heavy repeating blaster]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]]. It was equipped with an automatic [[deflector shield generator]] and a [[Gk7 Cryocooler]], which made it more efficient than the [[F-Web repeating blaster]]. The [[repeating blaster]] could fire for up to an [[Standard hour|hour]] at a sufficient range to discourage most [[Army|armies]].<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />

==Behind the scenes==
The E-Web (15) was first mentioned in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] as the [[E-Web(15) Heavy Repeating Blaster]] in [[The Last Command Sourcebook]], a hardcover supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', released in [[March]] [[1994]].<ref name="Roleplay">''[[The Last Command Sourcebook]]''</ref> The E-Web (15) was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Blasters and Ranged Weapons]]," a reference book published exclusively in France by [[De Agostini|Altaya]] on [[February 23]], [[2021]].<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />
