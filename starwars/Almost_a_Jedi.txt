"'''Almost a Jedi'''" is a short story that was published in the anthology ''[[The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark]]'', adapting the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[A Necessary Bond]]." It was written by [[Sarah Beth Durst]], and published on [[August 25]], [[2020]] by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]].

{{Plot|story}}

==Continuity==
In the story, [[Katooni]] mistakenly refers to [[Anakin Skywalker]] as a [[Jedi Master]], when Skywalker is in fact a [[Jedi Knight]].<ref name="Anakin databank">{{Databank|anakin-skywalker|Anakin Skywalker}}</ref>
