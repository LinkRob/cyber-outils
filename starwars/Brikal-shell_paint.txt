'''Brikal-shell paint''' was a type of [[Color|blue]] [[painting|paint]] used by the [[Path of the Open Hand]]. The pattern used denoted a member's role or rank, with three wavy lines on the forehead used for lower-ranked members, with [[Elder (Path of the Open Hand)|Elders]] having the whole top half of their face covered.<ref name="deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref>
