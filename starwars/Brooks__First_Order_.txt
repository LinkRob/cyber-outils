'''Brooks''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[admiral]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]. While speaking with [[Commandant]] [[Brendol Hux]], Hux's son [[Armitage Hux|Armitage]] was ordered to serve them drinks, but dropped the tray. Both Admiral Brooks and Commandant Hux scolded Armitage for his failure. Brooks later served as an admiral in the [[First Order]], ranking below Armitage Hux, who had risen to the rank of [[General]].<ref name="Age of Resistance" /> In [[33 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> when a [[shuttle]] transporting General Hux and [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] was sabotaged, Hux traced the attack to a [[Unidentified radar technician|radar technician]] working under Brooks, confronting the admiral. Brooks claimed he was not responsible for the sabotage, but Hux [[Death|killed]] him.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />
