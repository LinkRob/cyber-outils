The '''At Achrann civil war''' was a multi-generational conflict between the [[Troik]] and [[Hattan]] factions on the [[planet]] [[At Achrann]], one of the isolated [[Jewels of the Old Republic]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. The conflict, raging on since the [[Old Days]], meant that — at least from the Troik perspective — offworld contact was scarce beyond trading hides for ammunition for the war effort.<ref name="No At Attin" />

Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SC Date">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> a Hattan patrol stole a herd of [[eopie]]s from the Troiks, then led by [[General]] [[Strix]]. While the Troiks intended to raid to take them back and burn their granaries with the [[Skeleton Crew]] spearheading the incursion, the eopies were eventually returned by [[pirate]] [[Jod Na Nawood]] after he purchased them back from the Hattan's using [[Wim|Wim's]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|dataries]].<ref name="No At Attin" />

==Behind the scenes==
The civil war was portrayed in "[[Can't Say I Remember No At Attin]]," the fourth episode of the live&ndash;action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]''.<ref name="No At Attin" />
