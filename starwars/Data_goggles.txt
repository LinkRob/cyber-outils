'''Data goggles''' were a type of sophisticated [[goggles]] that displayed information, among other functions.<ref name="RiseVisual" /><ref name="LastJediVisual" />

==Varieties==
*[[Neimoidian]] [[pilot]]s such as [[Tey How]] and [[Mar Tuuk]] wore data goggles that allowed them to interface to their ships.<ref name="Storm over Ryloth" /><ref name="The Phantom Menace" />
*[[Bounty hunter]]s such as [[Seleno Chandro]] wore tactical data goggles that identified and magnified threats, assisting him in his hunts.<ref name="RiseVisual" />
*[[Torreb Savato]], a criminal, also wore a pair of general use data goggles.<ref name="LastJediVisual" />
