A '''battalion''' of [[stormtrooper]]s answered to the [[Fifth Brother]] [[Inquisitor]] during the [[Hunt for Obi-Wan|hunt]] for [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Combined with the support of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the battalion gave the Fifth [[Brother (Inquisitorius)|Brother]] an advantage in the hunt for the Kenobi,<ref name="Dark Side" /> whose capture would elevate the status of any Inquisitor in Vader's [[eye]]s. The battalion accompanied the Fifth Brother to the [[planet]] [[Daiyu]] during the hunt and marched behind him through the streets of [[Daiyu City]].<ref name="Part II" /> However, the clever tactics employed by another Inquisitor, the [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]],<ref name="Dark Side" /> outmaneuvered the Fifth Brother and his stormtroopers; the Third [[Sister (Inquisitorius)|Sister]] put an open [[bounty]] on Kenobi, revealing the [[Jedi]]'s location to her as he was attacked by [[blaster]] fire.<ref name="Part II" />

==Behind the scenes==
The [[2023 Topps Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi|2023 Topps Disney ''Star Wars Obi-Wan Kenobi'']] set describes the unit as both a [[legion]] and a [[squad]].<ref name="Fifth Brother Legion">{{Topps|set=2023 Topps Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi|cardname=The Fifth Brother's Legion of Stormtroopers|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/363350/cid/21461414}}</ref> As [[Star Wars: The Dark Side Pocket Expert|''Star Wars The Dark Side Pocket Expert'']] presents no contradictory information,<ref name="Dark Side" /> this article sides with its statement that the unit is a battalion.
