'''Astromech droids''', also referred to as '''astro droids''' or '''mechs''', were a type of [[Maintenance droid|repair droid]] that served as an automated [[Technician|mechanic]] on [[starship]]s. These compact [[droid]]s used tool-tipped appendages stored in recessed compartments. Many [[starfighter]]s relied on astromech [[Pilot|copilots]] to control flight and power distribution systems.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Sitting in an [[astromech socket]] exposed to [[Realspace|space]], the droid, in addition to its piloting duties, could calculate [[hyperspace]] jumps and perform simple repairs.<ref name="TCW-RM">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> The [[astromedic]] was a mix between an astromech and a [[medical droid]].<ref name="SWC">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref>

[[File:R2ToolsIcons.png|thumb|left|250px|Astromechs like R2-D2 had many functions.]]
Most astromechs were only able to communicate in writing, conveyed via another computer system, or through a special code of clicks, bleeps, and similar sounds, known as [[binary]]. However, certain other droids, such as the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]], could understand the binary code of astromechs, and often translated from binary to other languages.

Astromech droids averaged 0.93 [[meter]]s in height,<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> while [[R-series]] droids from the [[R2-series astromech droid|R2 line]] forward had a height of just over a meter.<ref name="IncrediBuilds">''[[Star Wars: R2-D2 Deluxe Book and Model Set]]''</ref> However, the astromech [[P2 (prototype astromech droid)|P2]] stood around the same height as a [[human]].<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> R-series droids had three wheeled legs.<ref name="AbEverything" /> The later [[BB-series astromech droid]]s were spherical, giving them the ability to roll.<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> Some astromechs, such as [[OC-JK14]], were moved on treadwells. Though this style was once popular in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], it had fallen out of favor by [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The R2-series unit [[R2-D2]] was perhaps the most famous astromech droid in [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]].<ref name="Backstories">''[[Princess Leia: Royal Rebel]]''</ref>
