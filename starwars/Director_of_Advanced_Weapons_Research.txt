The title of '''Director of Advanced Weapons Research''',<ref name="Dawn" /> also known as the '''Director of the Advanced Weapons Research Division''',<ref name="Complete VD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> was held by [[Commander]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] as the head of the [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]] and [[Project Stardust]]. [[Director]] Krennic oversaw the construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]] during the [[Imperial Era]],<ref name="Dawn" /> and was a member of the [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military]].<ref name="Incident Report">{{StoryCite|story=An Incident Report|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> [[Director]] Krennic was assisted by [[Colonel]] [[Brierly Ronan]], who held the title of [[Assistant Director]].<ref name="Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref>
