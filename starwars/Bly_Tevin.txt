'''Bly Tevin''', nicknamed "'''Blitz'''," was a [[Gender|male]] [[E'roni]] [[pilot]] who flew for [[Thylefire Squadron]] under the callsign [[Thylefire Nine]] during the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]]. A recent recruit to [[E'ronoh's fleet]], he gained his nickname after crashing several [[time]]s during training. Due to a shortage of [[starfighter]]s, [[Captain]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]], the [[squadron]]'s leader, gave Tevin the use of a [[Corellia]]n [[devilfighter]] which had originally been [[Bly Tevin's devilfighter|assigned]] to her. The switch led Tevin to fall [[Death|victim]] to an act of sabotage intended to kill A'lbaran, who was [[E'ronoh]]'s [[princess]], and restart hostilities between E'ronoh and [[Eiram]].<ref name="Convergence" />
