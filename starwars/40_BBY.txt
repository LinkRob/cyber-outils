==Events==
*[[Qui-Gon Jinn]] turns down an offer<ref name="Timelines" /> extended by Jedi [[Master of the Order]] [[Mace Windu]] to take the seat of retiring [[Jedi High Council]] member [[Poli Dapatian]].<ref name="Master & Apprentice" />
*[[Mission to Pijal]]<ref name="Timelines" />
*[[Galen Walton Erso]] joins the [[Republic Futures Program]] on [[Brentaal IV]].<ref name="RO Visual Guide" />
*[[Finis Valorum]] is elected [[Supreme Chancellor]].<ref name="Finis Date" />

==Births==
*[[Syril Karn]] {{C|Approximate date}}<ref name="Birth date">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' establishes that Syril Karn was 35 [[Standard year|years]] old at the [[time]] of the events of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which the book dates to [[5 BBY]]. Therefore, Karn must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[40 BBY]].</ref>
*[[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] on [[Pkori]]. {{C|Approximate date}}<ref name="Iskat birth date">According to ''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'', Iskat Akaris was eighteen [[Standard year|years]] at the [[time]] of the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]]. Therefore, Akaris was [[Reproduction|born]] around 40 BBY.</ref>
