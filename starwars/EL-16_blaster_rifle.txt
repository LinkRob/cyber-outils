The '''EL-16 blaster rifle'''<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> was a [[blaster rifle]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]]. A Heavy Field Edition of the EL-16, the [[EL-16HFE blaster rifle]], was also used by the [[Resistance]]. The EL-16 was outdated by the [[time]] of the [[First Order]]'s rise to power. One was given to [[Finn]] by [[Han Solo]], but he lost it when [[Maz Kanata|Maz's]] [[Takodana Castle|castle]] was destroyed. He got a new one of the same model at the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> Both it and the EL-16HFE were utilized during the [[Battle of Crait]].<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>
