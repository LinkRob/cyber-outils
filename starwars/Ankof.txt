'''Ankof''' was an [[admiral]] who taught courses at the [[Royal Imperial Academy]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Commendable performance in today's strategic exercise, Cadet Sydna Tarkin.|Ankof complements Sydna Tarkin.|<ref name="BFRWB4" />}}
Ankof taught at the Royal Imperial Academy. After a strategic exercise, he stopped [[Sydna Tarkin]] in the halls and congratulated her on her performance.<ref name="BFRWB4" />

==Personality and traits==
He was considered by the cadets to be very cold and emotionless. They compared him to a corpse animated by pure spite. He was a harsh teacher and his course only had a 10% pass rate.<ref name="BFRWB4" />
