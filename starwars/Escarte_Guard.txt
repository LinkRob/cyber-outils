The '''Escarte Guard''' was an elite security force from [[Escarte]], a world<ref name="Rise" /> in the [[Escarte system]] within the [[Keldrath sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Escarte system]]</ref>> Escarte was a major stronghold of the [[Commerce Guild]], and as a result the Escarte Guard was part of the [[Commerce Guild Punitive Security Forces|Punitive Security Forces]]. They offered [[bodyguard]]-based protection to the corporation's important individuals. After the [[Clone Wars]] [[First Battle of Geonosis|began]],<ref name="Rise" /> fought from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Commerce Guild sided with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] against the [[Galactic Republic]] and provided their military forces, incorporating the Escarte Guard into the [[Separatist Droid Army]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Escarte Guard was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Escarte Guard/Legends|Escarte Guard]] was introduced in ''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]'', a [[2005]] novel authored by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Labyrnith">''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''</ref>
