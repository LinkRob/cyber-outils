'''Andre's Fruit-in-a-Cup''' was a fresh [[fruit]] business based in [[Capital City]], [[Lothal]].<ref name="Recon Missions" />

==Behind the scenes==
Andre's Fruit-in-a-Cup was originally designed by [[Amy Beth Christenson]] as a [[fruit]] vendor stall at the [[Kothal market]] in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Fighter Flight]]".<ref name="Concept Art">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/fighter-flight-concept-art-gallery|text="Fighter Flight" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1, 7}}</ref> However, it did not appear in the actual episode.<ref name="Fighter Flight">{{Rebels|Fighter Flight}}</ref> The business shares its name with<ref name="Recon Missions" /> [[Andre Kirk]], one of the concept artists who worked on the episode.<ref name="Concept Art" />
