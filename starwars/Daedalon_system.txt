The '''Daedalon system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Ktilac Regions]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was located in grid square N-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Daedalon system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Daedalon system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[The Paradise Snare]]'', the first novel in [[A. C. Crispin]]'s [[Star Wars: The Han Solo Trilogy|''Han Solo Trilogy'']], which was released in [[1997]].<ref name="TPS">''[[The Paradise Snare]]''</ref>
