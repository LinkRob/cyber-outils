A '''fleet''' of the [[Galactic Empire]] that was a smaller portion of the [[Imperial Navy]] was located in [[Almagest]] shortly after the [[Battle of Endor]]. Although not as large as the [[Vulpinus Nebula Imperial fleet|contingent]] of the Imperial Navy in the [[Vulpinus Nebula]], the [[fleet]] still contained hundreds of [[Star Destroyer]]s and thousands of smaller craft, similar to five other fleets hidden in other [[nebula]]s. The fleet was called to [[Jakku]] by [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]] along with the other fleets,<ref name="Life Debt" /> and participated in the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="Armadas Note">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' establishes that [[Gallius Rax]] called the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[fleet]]s from Almagest, [[Inamorata Imperial fleet|Inamorata]], [[Queluhan Nebula Imperial fleet|Queluhan Nebula]], [[Recluse's Nebula Imperial fleet|Recluse's Nebula]], the [[Ro-Loo Triangle Imperial fleet|Ro-Loo Triangle]] and the [[Vulpinus Nebula Imperial fleet]] to [[Jakku]] prior to the Battle of Jakku. As ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' establishes that Rax was accumulating his forces and they all remained, these fleets must have participated in the battle.</ref>
