{{Quote|So what will it be? An Elysium Sunrise, maybe? Or something with a little more ''spice''? Penitent's Ruin? A splash of revnog?|Kradon Minst, to Vildar Mac and Matthea Cathley|<ref name="The High Republic 2" />}}
'''Elysium Sunrise''' was a beverage served at the [[Enlightenment]] [[Cantina|bar]] in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] of<ref name="The High Republic 2" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[moon]] [[Jedha]]. [[Kradon Minst]], a [[Villarandi]] who owned the bar, considered the beverage to have less [[Spice (seasoning)|spice]] than a [[Penitent's Ruin]] or a [[revnog]].<ref name="The High Republic 2" />

In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic (2022) 2|''The High Republic'' (2022) 2]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> when the [[Kiffar]]<ref name="The High Republic 2" /> [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Vildar Mac]] and the [[Twi'lek]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Matthea Cathley]] visited the bar in search of an individual, Minst offered to listen to their situation over a beverage and listed Elysium Sunrise among those available. The two ultimately had no drink as Mac instead spotted the individual they sought, the [[Sephi]] [[thief]] [[Tey Sirrek]], and attempted to apprehend him.<ref name="The High Republic 2" />
