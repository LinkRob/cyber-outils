'''Comet storms''' were astronomical phenomena characterized by many [[comet]]s traveling close together in [[Realspace|space]]. Comet storms could be found near [[planet]]s and could pose a risk to a [[starship]] by obstructing its path.

==Description==
[[File:AbafarComets-ASDitV.png|thumb|right|D-Squad's shuttle flies through a comet storm near Abafar.]]
[[Comet]] storms contained many comets drifting in the same general direction through [[Realspace|space]]. The larger comets in the storm had an opaque bluish-white center mass surrounded by a bluish cloud, with a translucent tail of the same substance, which appeared to be a foggy, milky gas. Light refracting through particles in a storm cast that part of space in a similar light blue sheen. These bigger comets could reach the size of a [[shuttle]] or larger, whilst smaller chunks of ice in a storm could be the size of an [[astromech droid]]. Although comet storms moved through space, they could be found near celestial bodies, such as [[planet]]s and [[star]]s.[[File:RedHyperspace.png|thumb|left|The normally blue tunnel of hyperspace turned red when a comet storm was near.|alt=]]These storms were dangerous to [[Starship|ships]] and space travelers. Comets could inflict significant damage on light starships, such as the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederacy of Independent System's]] [[Maxillipede shuttle]]. Despite being able to allow smaller ice comets to shatter or bounce off, ship [[hull]]s could be torn open, and windows cracked, by larger ones.<ref name="sunny" />

When traveling in [[hyperspace]], the presence of a comet storm nearby turned the normally blue tunnel of hyperspace into a reddish, pinkish color. Additionally, ship [[sensor]]s could pick up the presence of a comet storm before it was reached, allowing time for the [[pilot]] to exit hyperspace to avoid a collision at [[lightspeed]]. Scanners could also detect and display the size, shape and approximate positioning of ice comets in a storm.<ref name="sunny" />

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[D-Squad]] encountered a comet storm near the planet [[Abafar]] while returning from a [[mission to steal a Separatist encryption module]]. After [[D-Squad's Maxillipede shuttle|their Maxillipede shuttle]] was damaged by the storm, the [[droid]]s and [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]] [[Escape from Abafar|crash landed]] on the planet. They were stranded in the desolate [[The Void|Void]] for some time before finding refuge at the remote [[city]] of [[Pons Ora]].<ref name="sunny" />
