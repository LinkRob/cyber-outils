'''Clyss Power'''<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> was an [[admiral]] in the [[Imperial Navy|starfleet]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] who fought in the [[Galactic Civil War]]. By the [[time]] of the [[First Order]], Power's achievements had passed into history, and the [[Rank insignia of the First Order|commemorative armbands]] worn by [[lieutenant]]s of the First Order's [[First Order military|military]] bore the admiral's name in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="TFA VD" /> [[Dopheld Mitaka]] wore a [[Clothing|uniform]] with the officer's surname on it,<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> designating him as a lieutenant.<ref name="TFA VD" /> When the [[slicer]] [[DJ]] [[Battle of Oetchi|infiltrated]] the ''[[Supremacy]]'' with [[Resistance]] members [[Rose Tico]] and [[Finn]], he wore a stolen uniform with the admiral's name on it.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> Likewise, [[Lusica Stynnix]] wore a uniform with the officer's surname on it.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> [[Lodent Cowell]], a member of the [[Kijimi garrison]], also wore a uniform emblazoned with Power's surname.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
