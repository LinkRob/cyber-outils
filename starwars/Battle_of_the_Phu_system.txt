The '''Battle of the Phu system''' took place in [[22 BBY]] within the [[Phu system]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Prior to the battle, several [[Galactic Republic]] fleets had been destroyed in a series of attacks by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and its new mystery weapon, the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]'', commanded by [[General]] [[Grievous]]. To locate the [[warship]], [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]] dispatched a [[task force]] of three [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Ares Nune]]. However, while moving through the Phu [[Star system|system]], Nune and the task force were intercepted and destroyed by the ''Malevolence''.

Sometime later, the ''Malevolence'' was tracked to the [[Abregado system]] by General Koon's own [[Plo Koon's fleet|fleet]] in order to destroy the warship before it could strike once more. While Koon's [[fleet]] was [[Battle of Abregado|destroyed]] by the ''Malevolence'', Koon was able to escape the destruction and report his findings to the Republic.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|This mystery weapon has struck in a dozen systems and disappeared without a trace.|Mace Windu, on the ''Malevolence''{{'s}} strikes|<ref name="Rising Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref>}}
[[File:Malevolence_profile.png|thumb|left|300px|Due to its capabilities of destroying entire fleets, the Republic endlessly searched for the ''Malevolence'' in order to destroy it.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the confederate government, through the help of the [[Quarren]] [[engineer]]s of the [[Free Dac Volunteers Engineering Corps]] and [[Pammant Docks]], constructed the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]'', a [[starship]] capable of destroying entire [[Fleet|starfleets]] with its two [[mega-ion cannon]]s.<ref name="BMF 43" /> The Confederacy began to use the [[warship]], placed under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of the [[Kaleesh]] Separatist [[General]] [[Grievous]], in a [[Malevolence Campaign|series of attacks]] against the [[Republic Navy]] that left no survivors. The ''Malevolence'' was thus dubbed a mystery weapon by the Republic.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

In response to their losses, the [[Jedi High Council]] intended on preventing the ''Malevolence'' from inflicting further damage to their starfleet. Eager to destroy the Separatist weapon, [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]] sent a [[Ares Nune's task force|task force]] of three [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]]<ref name="BMF 43" /> commanded by [[Jedi Master]] [[Ares Nune]]<ref name="German Falcon 43" /> to locate the warship.<ref name="BMF 43" />

==The battle==
While moving through the [[Phu system]]<ref name="BMF 43" /> in the [[Colonies]] region,<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Phu]]</ref> the task force, led by Nune,<ref name="German Falcon 43" /> following their orders from Koon, was intercepted by the ''Malevolence'', which had remained one step ahead of the Republic. Following its arrival, the task force's transmissions were jammed by Grievous under orders from the Separatist [[Head of State|leader]], [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]], to keep the vessel a secret. The task force then came under intense fire from the ''Malevolence'', resulting in the destruction of all Republic forces, including the Jedi Master.<ref name="BMF 43" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|How's the hunt for the mystery weapon going?''"<br />"''We've tracked it to the Abregado system. We need reinforcements.|Anakin Skywalker and Plo Koon, prior to the Battle of Abregado|<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />}}
[[File:Battle_of_Abregado.jpg|thumb|right|270px|Following the engagements in the Phu system, the ''Malevolence'' was tracked to the Abregado system.]]
Despite the destruction of the Republic task force in the Phu [[Star system|system]],<ref name="BMF 43" /> the ''Malevolence'' was later tracked to the [[Abregado system]] by the Republic. As a result, the [[Jedi Council|Jedi High Council]], resolute in destroying the [[heavy cruiser]] before it could conduct further attacks, dispatched General Koon and his [[Plo Koon's fleet|fleet]] to the Abregado system to destroy the ''Malevolence''. Despite their attempts, Koon's fleet was destroyed by the ''Malevolence'' just as other ships were before it, but the [[Jedi Master]] was able to escape the warship and report his findings to the Republic,<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> resulting in the ''Malevolence''{{'s}} eventual [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula|destruction]].<ref name="Destroy Malevolence">{{TCW|Destroy Malevolence|Destroy ''Malevolence''}}</ref>
