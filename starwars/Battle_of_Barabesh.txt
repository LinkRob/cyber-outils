The '''Battle of Barabesh''' followed a failed mission to rescue [[Tamara Ryvora]], who had become disillusioned with the [[First Order]]. [[Jarek Yeager]] and [[Kazuda Xiono]] arranged to rendezvous with Ryvora on the planet [[Castilon]], who traveled there under the guise of leading a training mission. However, the mission was complicated when [[First Order Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Tierny]] discovered Ryvora's defection and dispatched the ''[[Thunderer]]'' after them. Despite being captured by the First Order and exposing the ''[[Colossus]]'' to First Order pursuers, the team managed to sabotage the Star Destroyer's [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] and escape the warship before it was destroyed during a skirmish in the Barabesh system.

==Prelude==
===Background===
Tamara Ryvora was an aspiring racer who left her homeworld of [[Kuat]]<ref name="Synara's Score">{{Resistance|Synara's Score}}</ref> to pursue a racing career at the ''[[Colossus]]'' refueling platform on the planet [[Castilon]]. However, her plans did not work out and Ryvora fell on hard times. Destitute, she was forced to sell her racer but found lodging and work with [[Jarek Yeager]], a former [[New Republic]] [[starfighter]] [[pilot]] who owned a [[Jarek Yeager's Repair Station|repair station]] at the ''Colossus''. Tam worked as a mechanic and hoped to earn enough money to buy the old [[racer]] known as the ''[[Fireball]]''.<ref name="The Escape" /> Ryvora's working relationship with Yeager was complicated by the arrival of undercover [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]], who was on a [[Mission on the Colossus platform|mission]] to spy on [[First Order]] activity on the ''Colossus''.<ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref> 

Ryvora grew frustrated with Kaz's unexplained offworld trips and Yeager's perceived leniency towards Kaz.<ref name="The Core Problem">{{Resistance|The Core Problem}}</ref> Furthermore, she conflicted with Xiono over their different opinions about the First Order. While Xiono regarded the presence of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] as hostile, Ryvora&mdash;the granddaughter of an [[Unidentified Imperial factory|Imperial factory]] [[Tamara Ryvora's grandfather|worker]]&mdash;took comfort from the security that the [[Color|white]]-[[First Order stormtrooper armor|armored]] soldiers offered, seeing them as a source of badly-needed protection against the [[Attack on the Colossus|ongoing]] [[Raid on the Colossus|pirate]] [[Torra Doza's abduction|raids]].<ref name="New Trooper">{{Resistance|The New Trooper}}</ref> After the First Order raided Yeager's repair station following Kaz's [[Mission to the Dassal system|intrusion]] into First Order space, Ryvora thought it was a misunderstanding and surrendered herself to the authorities.<ref name="Descent">{{Resistance|Descent (episode)}}</ref>

The [[First Order Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] managed to turn Ryvora against Kaz and Yeager by revealing that the former was a Resistance spy and claiming that the latter had taken advantage of her by withholding information from her.<ref name="No Escape 1">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 1}}</ref> Tam's anger and resentment towards Kaz and Yeager led her to join the First Order during the [[Battle of Castilon]], which saw the [[Team Colossus|Team ''Colossus'']] liberate the ''Colossus'' from First Order rule and escape into hyperspace.<ref name="No Escape 2">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 2}}</ref>

Ryvora became a [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|First Order TIE Fighter Pilot]] [[cadet]] during the First Order's [[Hunt for the Colossus|hunt for the ''Colossus'']],<ref name="Into the Unknown">{{Resistance|Into the Unknown}}</ref> training alongside former Colossus residents [[Jace Rucklin]] and [[Lin Gaava]].<ref name="Live Fire">{{Resistance|Live Fire}}</ref> Over time, Ryvora came to question her commitment to the First Order due to her [[Escape of Venisa Doza|encounter]] with Resistance pilot [[Venisa Doza]], who was the wife of [[Captain]] [[Imanuel Doza]] and mother of [[Torra Doza]], a friend of Tam from her time on the ''Colossus''.<ref name="Rendezvous Point">{{Resistance|Rendezvous Point (episode)}}</ref> Ryvora also secretly helped former friends Xiono and [[Neeku Vozo]] [[Infiltration of the Titan|steal]] a [[Trans-binary deflector]] for the ''Colossus''.<ref name="Station to Station">{{Resistance|Station to Station}}</ref> Despite rising to the rank of [[Lieutenant]], Ryvora's disillusionment with the First Order peaked after the First Order destroyed an [[Unidentified Aeosian village|Aeosian village]] during the [[Suppression of the Aeos system]]. Deciding to defect, Ryvora retrieved her [[comlink]] from [[Tierny's office]]. With the help of the [[MSE-6 series repair droid|mouse droid]] [[5-L]], she transmitted a coded transmission to Xiono with arrangements to rendezvous on Castilon.<ref name="The Escape" />

===Helping a friend===
Kaz, Neeku, and Yeager received Tamara's coded transmission. While Yeager thought it could be a trap, Kaz did not want to abandon Tam. Yeager agreed to accompany Kaz but the two needed [[Captain]] [[Imanuel Doza]]'s permission first. Though Doza was skeptical, Kaz believed that Tam had a genuine change of heart. Kaz was supported by Yeager and Doza's own wife [[Commander]] [[Venisa Doza]], who said they would rescue their daughter if she was in the same position. Doza relented and allowed Kaz, Yeager, and [[CB-23]] to borrow a former First Order [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport|Atmospheric Assault Lander]] to travel to a foregone racing ring near the former site of the ''Colossus.''<ref name="The Escape" />

Meanwhile, Tam took her squadron of three [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s, which were capable of hyperspace travel to Castilon on a training mission. She was accompanied by Rucklin, who was committed to the First Order. Upon arriving, Rucklin recognized the Lander as a stolen First Order shuttle. Despite Tam's orders not to engage, Rucklin managed to convince the other pilots to open fire on the Lander. Tam was forced to knock him out and used her TIE fighter to shoot them down.<ref name="The Escape" />

[[File:KazTamReunite-TheEscape.png|thumb|right|250px|Kazuda Xiono and Tamara Ryvora reunite atop a racing ring.]]
Tam had a reunion with Yeager and Kaz on the racing ring. Though Yeager was still angry that Tam had left them to join the First Order, Kaz managed to defuse the situation by telling them they could resolve their differences later. At Tam's insistence, Kaz and Yeager agreed to also evacuate the unconscious Rucklin. Before they could leave, the ''Thunderer'' arrived an opened fire on the racing ring and sea.<ref name="The Escape" />

===Behind enemy lines===
Though the Atmospheric Assault Lander managed to escape into [[Realspace|space]], it was trapped by the Thunderer's [[tractor beam]] projector. Before the ship's capture, Yeager deleted the shuttle's flight logs and set the ship to self-destruct. While Tam and a still loyal Rucklin surrendered to Commander Pyre, Agent Tierny, and several stormtroopers, Kaz, Yeager, and CB-23 sneaked out of the ship through the maintenance hatches. While Tam confirmed that she had abandoned the First Order, Rucklin tried to prove his loyalty by revealing that the ''Colossus'' was in the [[Barabesh system]]. Pyre was unmoved and ordered that the two cadets be executed by [[First Order stormtrooper executioner|Executioner troopers]] for [[treason]].<ref name="The Escape" />

After the shuttle exploded, Kaz, Yeager, and CB-23 took the opportunity to flee the hangar and infiltrate the ship. Despite Pyre, Tierny, and the stormtrooper's efforts, they managed to breach the Thunderer's corridors. Tierny issued a code red while Pyre ordered the Thunderer to set a course for the Barabesh system, seeking to destroy the ''Colossus'' once and for all.<ref name="The Escape" />

Later, Kaz and Yeager ambushed the stormtrooper party escorting Tam and Rucklin for execution. Kaz rejoined her friends but Rucklin was confident that the First Order would destroy the ''Colossus''. After knocking him unconscious, Tam confirmed that the First Order had discovered the location of the ''Colossus'' due to Rucklin's treachery. The three decided that they had to warn the ''Colossus'' that the Thunderer was coming after them. Tam came up with an idea of infiltrating the engineering deck and rerouting a subspace frequency to send a transmission to the ''Colossus''. Kaz contacted CB-23 and informed her about their plans.<ref name="The Escape" />

===Contacting the Colossus===
With the help of CB-23, Kaz, Tam, and Yeager managed to take a [[turbolift]] in [[G27]] into the engineering deck. After taking out two [[KXFO-series security droid|First Order security droids]] and rendezvousing with CB-23, the trio made their way down to the depths of the engine core in order to send their subspace frequency. Near the bottom of the engine, the group encountered a pair of stormtroopers, whom Yeager shot. However, one of the stormtroopers managed to report the intruders to Commander Pyre, who led reinforcements to intercept the intruders.<ref name="The Escape" />

Kaz and Tam contacted the ''Colossus'' using the subspace frequency, warning them that the First Order was coming for them and telling them to flee. The ''Colossus'' residents were initially distressed by Kaz and Tam's message but several residents including [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza|Aunt Z]], [[Synara San]], and [[Bolza Grool]] urged them to fight back. Though they managed to warn the ''Colossus'', their frequency allowed the First Order to home in on their location. Tam and her rescuers fled down a maintenance hatch which led to a hangar. They attempted to escape aboard an Atmospheric Assault Lander but were captured by Commander Pyre and his stormtroopers. However, CB-23 managed to slip away.<ref name="The Escape" />

==The battle==
{{Quote|The First Order does not tolerate the weak, Agent Tierny.''"<br />"''I understand.''"<br />"''Do you?''"<br />"''Yes, Supreme&hellip; Leader.|Agent Tierny and Kylo Ren before he kills her|<ref name="The Escape" />}}
Tam, Kaz, and Yeager were brought onto the command bridge. Agent Tierny confronted Tam about her betrayal, saying that she had made her a pilot and given her purpose. Tam responded that Yeager gave her a family. When Pyre mocked Kaz that his efforts amounted to nothing, Kaz responded that he had warned the ''Colossus'' and that the station would be gone. Shortly later, the Thunderer exited hyperspace in the Barabesh system. Kaz was horrified to discover that the ''Colossus'' had not left.<ref name="The Escape" />

Pyre deployed several TIE squadrons against the space station but the ''Colossus'' residents were ready. Commander Doza led<ref name="The Escape" /> [[Synara San]],<ref name="SW Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> and both [[Jade Squadron]] and [[Ace Squadron]] against the Thunderer. They tried to take out its thrusters but these were protected by the Thunderer's [[deflector shield]]. CB-23 managed to lower the deflector shield, allowing the starfighters to bombard the Thunderer's engines.<ref name="The Escape" />

Kaz, Tam, and Yeager took advantage of the ensuing chaos to break free of their captors. Tam told the group to disable the ship's controls and dashboards, leading the trio to open fire on them. They then fled down a turbolift with CB-23. They reached a hangar where Commander Pyre was supervising the departure of stormtroopers on assault landers to attack the Colossus. At Tam's suggestion, CB-23 lowered them onto a crane via cable so they could reach a nearby transport. However, a blast from a ruptured engine caused the grappling cable to break loose. Tam, Kaz, and Yeager fell, but CB-23 caught them.<ref name="The Escape" />

Kaz and Tam landed on several crates, attracting the attention of Pyre and his men. A blaster fight ensued and Yeager shot a crane carrying munitions, causing it to fall on Pyre's men. The munitions exploded, blowing Pyre to the ground. Pyre got up and shot a crate which caused it to blow up and knock Team Fireball to the ground. Kaz was pinned down by a large box, so Pyre attempted to kill him with his [[BL-155 Laser ax]] but CB-23 used her grappling cable to grab his ax. Pyre managed to damage the droid with his weapon but Kaz broke free and struck Pyre down with another laser ax.<ref name="The Escape" />

After alerting Torra Doza that they would be escaping aboard a stolen First Order transport, Kaz along with Tam, CB-23, and Yeager escaped the doomed ship. Meanwhile, the ''Colossus'' residents defeated the First Order boarding parties. Deeming Commander Pyre and Agent Tierny to be weak, [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] refused to send reinforcements and [[force choke]]d Agent Tierny. The Thunderer was destroyed by the combined firepower of Jade Squadron, Ace Squadron, and the ''Colossus''.<ref name="The Escape" />

==Aftermath==
Following the rescue, Tamara Ryvora returned home to the ''Colossus'' with Yeager, Kaz, and CB-23. She was greeted by several ''Colossus'' residents including Neeku, Aunt Z, the [[Doza family]], Ace Squadron, and Jade Squadron. Tam also reconciled with her friends Kaz, Yeager, and Neeku while having drinks at [[Aunt Z's Tavern]].<ref name="The Escape" />

Several ships from Team ''Colossus'' took part in the [[Battle of Exegol]]. [[Ace Squadron]]'s racers including the ''Fireball'', [[Jarek Yeager's racer]], ''[[Blue Ace]]'',<ref name="Bucket List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-escape-part-2-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 5 Fun Facts from "The Escape - Part 2" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref> [[Red Ace]], [[Green Ace]], and [[Yellow Ace]],<ref name="TROS GN">[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker Graphic Novel Adaptation|''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker'' Graphic Novel Adaptation]]</ref> as well the ''Colossus''<ref name="TSW Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> were part of the 14,000 strong [[Citizens' Fleet|allied armada]] that took on the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]].
