'''First Order snowtrooper gear''' was [[armor]] used by [[First Order snowtrooper]]s.

==Description==
First Order snowtrooper gear,<ref name="SWE" /> also known simply as snowtrooper armor, was an advancement of the [[armor]] worn by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s own [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]]<ref name="CVD" /> designed for [[Temperature|cold]]-weather warfare.<ref name="SWE" /> As such, it was issued to [[stormtrooper]]s deployed in low-temperature environments.<ref name="Datapad">{{DatapadCite|Snowtrooper armor|SnowtrooperArmor-GEDatapad.png}}</ref> The armor was made of [[betaplast]]<ref name="SWE" /> and featured fewer plates than standard [[First Order stormtrooper armor]] to allow increased movement in difficult terrain, including icy and snowy. Snowtroopers wore a dense, [[Temperature|heat]]-retaining [[body glove]] with a wind- and [[water]]-resistance fabric overtop that insulated the suit. A [[personal environment unit]] was worn on the back of the armor that monitored and regulated the [[trooper]]'s body temperature. The [[helmet]]s featured a polarized slit [[Polarizing visor|visor]] to lower glare and two [[Breath mask|breather tank inlets]].<ref name="CVD" />

Snowtrooper [[Breast|chest]] plates were made of a betaplast composite and coated with an icephobic substance to prevent frost buildup in cold or humid environments. The front of their chest plate featured an insignia for the trooper's unit and controls for the suit heater. Their [[utility belt]] was equipped with two utility pouches and featured an insulated [[kama]]. The gear's [[insulated glove]]s were equipped with adjustable [[Temperature|heating]] units. They wore ice [[boot]]s and their greaves had adjustable straps. The snowtroopers carried [[F-11D blaster rifle]]s with modifications for their unique environments. The [[J19 electroscope]] featured antifogging filaments and the barrel of the [[blaster rifle]] had cooling vanes to shunt excess heat to the interior mechanisms. The blaster's trigger was wrapped with a heated filament.<ref name="CVD" />

==History==
Sometime between [[26 ABY]] and [[34 ABY]],<ref name="AORDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Age of Resistance - Rose Tico 1|''Age of Resistance - Rose Tico'' 1]] as taking place from around [[26 ABY]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[First Order]] conquered the icey [[planet]] [[Hays Minor]] and employed Snowtroopers,<ref name="FOEncyclo">{{EncyclopediaCite|The First Order}}</ref> equipped with their standard snowtrooper armor.<ref name="AOR">[[Age of Resistance - Rose Tico 1|''Age of Resistance - Rose Tico'' 1]]</ref> By 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="TFADate">{{FilmDate|VII}}</ref> the First Order deployed First Order snowtroopers equipped with their specialized gear to protect and maintain [[Starkiller Base]]'s surface facilities.<ref name="CVD" /> During the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], after stealing a [[Light Infantry Utility Vehicle|First Order snow speeder]], the [[Treason|ex-stormtrooper]] [[Finn]] and [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]<ref name="TFANovel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|Star Wars: The Force Awakens novelization]]</ref> were pursued by six snowtroopers aboard three [[Snowspeeder|snow speeders]]. Finn incapacitated one snow speeder, and its two passengers, by trigger a mound of snow to collapse on it. The former trooper eliminated another speeder with two snowtroopers by shooting one of its [[engine]]s, causing the snow speeder to careen into the nearby [[tree]]s. The last two pursuers leapt from their vehicle into the snow to avoid following Finn and Rey over a ravine.<ref name="Pursuit">{{FOD|Perilous Pursuit}}</ref> Shortly after the Battle of Starkiller Base, because of the surface glare on the [[planet]] [[Crait]], First Order snowtroopers were deployed in the [[Battle of Crait]], utilizing their anti-glare slit visors. The heating units within their gear was deactivated for the battle.<ref name="CVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The snowtrooper armor first appeared in the [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']],<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> which premiered [[December 18]], [[2015]].<ref name="TFARelease">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-vii-to-open-december-18-2015|text=Star Wars: Episode VII to Open December 18, 2015|date=2013-11-07}}</ref> The armor was first introduced in a [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set<ref name="LEGO">{{LEGOCite|num=75100|name=First Order Snowspeeder}}</ref> and a [[Hasbro]] action figure,<ref name="Action">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Force Awakens (toy line)|pack=Snowtrooper|altlink=https://galacticfigures.com/figureDetails.aspx?id=3490}}</ref> which both released [[September 4]], 2015.<ref name="LEGORelease">{{WebCite|url=https://brickset.com/article/15421/star-wars-episode-vii-the-force-awakens-set-names|text=Star Wars Episode VII: The Force Awakens set names|author=CapnRex101|date=2015-06-03|work=[https://brickset.com Brickset]|archivedate=20251028133826}}</ref><ref name="HasbroRelease">{{Rebelscum|url=First-Order-Snowspeeder-The-Force-Awakens.asp|text=FIRST ORDER SNOWSPEEDER}}</ref> First Order Snowtrooper gear was added to the [[EA Capital Games]] [[video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''<ref name="SWGOH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>&mdash;originally debuted [[November 24]], [[2015]]<ref name="Holotable Master">{{SW|url=news/how-to-become-a-master-of-the-holotables-in-star-wars-galaxy-of-heroes|text=How to Become a Master of the Holotables in ''Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes''|author=[[Cole Horton]]|date=2015-11-24|nolive=1}}</ref>&mdash;as part of the event to unlock Galactic Legend Rey,<ref name="SWGOH" /> which was added [[March 25]], [[2020]].<ref name="GOHAdd">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/226431/title-update-03-25-2020|text=Title Update - 03/25/2020|author=CG_SBCrumb|date=2020-03-25}}</ref>

While First Order snowtroopers and their armor appeared in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'', a [[2017]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] by [[Electronic Arts]], at launch,<ref name="BF2">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> the trooper was only playable with the release of the Battle on Scarif Update on [[April 29]], [[2020]].<ref name="Scarif">{{EA|subdomain=battlefront-forums|url=discussion/158697/community-transmission-the-battle-on-scarif-update|text=Community Transmission - The Battle on Scarif Update|author=F8RGE|date=2020-04-23|nolive=1}}</ref>
