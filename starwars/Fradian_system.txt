The '''Fradian system''' was located in the [[Roche sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]] in [[the galaxy]]. The [[Star system|system]] was located in grid square Q-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fradian system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the article "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> The [[Fradian system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2009]] novel ''[[Imperial Commando: 501st]]'', written by [[Karen Traviss]],<ref name="Imperial Commando">''[[Imperial Commando: 501st]]''</ref> and was later identified in [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion]].<ref name="TEAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
