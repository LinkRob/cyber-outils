The '''Clacis sector''' was a part of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Where" /> and the [[New Territories]],<ref name="NT" /> located southwest of the [[Raioballo sector]] [[Planet|world]] [[Lah'mu]].<ref name="Where" /> It contained the [[Bisellia system|Bisellia]], [[Cezith system|Cezith]], [[Dactruria system|Dactruria]], [[Endoraan system|Endoraan]], [[Fodro system|Fodro]], [[Gwori system|Gwori]], [[Karka system|Karka]], [[Korrus system|Korrus]], [[Kwevron system|Kwevron]], [[Ord Sedra system|Ord Sedra]], [[Praya system|Praya]], [[Shramar system|Shramar]], and [[Venestria system]]s, which were situated in grid squares K-4 and J-4 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> By [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the nascent [[New Republic]],<ref name="ALD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> the Clacis sector<ref name="Appendix-Korrus">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Korrus system]]</ref> [[Planet|world]] [[Korrus]] contributed to the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="ALD" /> Korrus was connected<ref name="Galaxy Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> by the [[Braxant Run]]<ref name="Braxant" /> [[hyperspace route]] to the [[Muunilinst system|Muunilinst]] and [[Ord Trasi system]]s.<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> The [[starship]] manufacturing company [[Gwori Revolutionary Industries]]<ref name="Complete Vehicles">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> originated from the Gwori [[Star system|system]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /><ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Gwori Revolutionary Industries]]|system=[[Gwori system]]}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Clacis sector was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[StarWars.com]] article "[[Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Rogue One?|Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Rogue One''?]]." The article was written by [[Mark Newbold]] and published on [[January 31]], [[2017]].<ref name="Where" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Clacis sector/Legends|Clacis sector]] was first mentioned in ''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]'', a [[1995]] sourcebook for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="AIR">''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]''</ref> The [[Sector/Legends|sector]] was illustrated on a map designed by [[Modi]] and included in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> The StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to ''The Essential Atlas'' later placed the [[Praya system/Legends|Praya system]] in the Clacis sector,<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> thereby also establishing the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Praya]]'s appearance in an [[in-universe]] [[Tourscan|video advertisement]] in the<ref name="ST">[[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] {{C|Tokyo only}}</ref> [[1989]]<ref name="The Bad Batch Returns">{{ThisWeek|EU-glArwz-U|The Bad Batch Returns, Your Photos of The Child, and More!|sw_url=video/the-bad-batch-returns-your-photos-of-the-child-and-more}}</ref> [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] [[Wikipedia:Amusement park|theme park]] in [[Wikipedia:Tokyo|Tokyo]], [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japan]] as the de facto earliest appearance of the Clacis sector.<ref name="ST" />
