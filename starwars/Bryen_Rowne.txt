'''Bryen Rowne''' was a [[human]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] who served as a [[captain]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. One of the Alliance's top [[pilot]]s, Rowne volunteered to be part of a team of four [[Starship|ships]] to undertake an [[Assault on Coruscant|attack run]] on the Imperial [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]].

Rowne flew an [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]] alongside the [[Twi'lek]] [[Freyta Smyth]] and the [[Sullustan]] [[Nien Nunb]] as well as the human [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]], who crewed the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. The force infiltrated the [[Coruscant system]] before attempting to race past the planetary defenses. Although the mission was successful, Rowne [[Death|perished]] during the attack run.

==Biography==
{{Quote|The Emperor and Vader might be off their game, but that doesn't mean the ''Empire'' is suddenly weak. They have ''countless'' officers, pilots, and soldiers.|Bryen Rowne, to Leia Organa|<ref name="Star Wars 36" />}}
[[File:RebelBriefings-2020StarWars36.png|thumb|left|275px|Rowne attending the briefing]]
Byren Rowne was a [[human]] who came to serve the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Rebel Alliance and the [[Galactic Empire]], Rowne was both a [[captain]] and one of the Alliance's best [[Rebel pilot|pilots]].<ref name="Star Wars 36" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Rowne joined a team of elite [[pilot]]s assembled by [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] alongside the [[Twi'lek]] [[Freyta Smyth]], the [[Sullustan]] [[Nien Nunb]], the human [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], and the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]]. Aboard the rebel [[flagship]] ''[[Home One]]'', Organa revealed to the team that the Alliance had an incredible opportunity to strike against the Empire, due to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] dealing with an [[Force Wave|anomaly]] in [[the Force]], which meant they would not be attending the Empire with their usual level of focus.<ref name="Star Wars 36" />

It was then that Rowne spoke up, questioning whether the fact mattered, as the Empire still had immeasurable forces outside of<ref name="Star Wars 36" /> the [[Sith Lord]]s.<ref name="Lords">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref> Organa agreed with the captain's point but maintained that they needed to capitalize on the unique opportunity with a daring mission. She then revealed the plan, which was for the small team to carry out an [[Assault on Coruscant|aerial assault]] on the Imperial [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. For the mission, Rowne piloted a [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]] alongside Smyth and Nunb, while Calrissian and Chewbacca flew the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. The ships entered the [[Coruscant system]] and were able to sneak into the Coruscant approach lane at [[security checkpoint Delta-Three]] while hidden aboard the [[Altor-class supply ship|supply ship]] ''[[Pikka's Pride]]''. From there, the team exited the [[Supply ship|transport]] and swooped towards the planet.<ref name="Star Wars 36" />

[[File:BryenShotDown-2020StarWars36.png|thumb|right|280px|Rowne moments before they were shot down]]
As [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] intercepted them, Rowne was [[Death|killed]] by [[fire]] from an orbital platform before the rebels reached Coruscant. Smyth also perished during the mission, leaving Calrissian, Chewbacca, and Nunb as the only survivors. As Calrissian returned from the mission, he spoke with Organa over [[Comm unit|comms]] about the loss of Rowne and Smyth, mourning that the two pilots had been needlessly lost. Organa likewise lamented their deaths, saying that they were both good pilots and good people, but told Calrissian that the mission had been accomplished, as the pilots had successfully struck at the Imperial capital and damaged a statue of the [[Emperor]].<ref name="Star Wars 36" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|We lost Freyta Smyth and Bryen Rowne.''"<br />"''Ah. They were good people and incredible pilots.|Lando Calrissian and Leia Organa|<ref name="Star Wars 36" />}}
A human, Bryen Rowne had [[Color|green]] [[eye]]s, light skin, and brown [[hair]] that was graying<ref name="Star Wars 36" /> by the [[time]] between 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] and 4 ABY.<ref name="Date" /> Rowne's slicked-back hair was accompanied by a mustache and beard. General Organa regarded them as both a talented pilot and a good person.<ref name="Star Wars 36" />

==Skills and abilities==
Bryen Rowne was an elite pilot of the Rebel Alliance who was willing to volunteer for a high-risk mission.<ref name="Star Wars 36" />

==Equipment==
Rowne piloted an [[A-wing starfighter]]. The pilot wore an orange [[flight suit]], a white [[flak vest]], and utilized a white [[flight helmet]] that featured the [[Rebel Alliance starbird|starbird symbol]] of the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Star Wars 36" />
