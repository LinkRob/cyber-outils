{{Quote|I was a fully credentialled sava&ndash;a scholar and teacher&ndash;at the University of Bar'leth, smack in the middle of the bright, shining Core.|[[The Archivist]]|<ref name="Crimson Reign 3">[[Crimson Reign 3|''Crimson Reign'' 3]]</ref>}}
'''Bar'leth''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Core Worlds]], in grid square M-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[University of Bar'leth]]<ref name="Lando 1" /> and the [[Museum of Bar'leth]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 13" /> were located on Bar'leth.<ref name="Lando 1" /> 
In the time before the founding of the [[Galactic Republic]], Bar'leth was the hiding place of the [[Ascendant]], until the [[Sith]] [[Darkseekers]] tracked down and destroyed the cult in their [[Tomb of the Spark Eternal]]. In the following years, the Museum of Bar'leth was built over the site of the tomb, its secrets all but forgotten.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 19" /><ref name="Doctor Aphra 22">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 22|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 22]]</ref>

Early in the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[the Grand Inquisitor]] and [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] visited Bar'leth to retrieve a Sith artifact. Akaris, who had been to Bar'leth during her time as a [[Padawan]], noted the planet had not changed significantly under the Empire's rule.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />
