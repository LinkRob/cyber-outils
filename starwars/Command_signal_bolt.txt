A '''command signal bolt''' was a type of modified [[restraining bolt]] that could be used as a signal hub. The [[R5 series astromech droid]] [[R5-2JE]] used a command signal bolt while serving as the head of the [[droid]] pool for the [[forest]] landing pad of the [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|Resistance base]] on the [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]]. Using the bolt, R5-2JE could send similarly equipped [[Resistance]] droids commands and work orders from a distance. The bolt was [[Color|silver]] in color<ref name="TROS VD" /> and similar in appearance to an unmodified restraining bolt.<ref name="SoloOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A command signal bolt was first shown on a figure of R5-2JE released as part of a [[Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|''Star Wars: Droid Factory'']] pack of action figures released by [[Hasbro]]<ref name="Toy">{{DisneyToyCite|set=Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|packtype=Multipack|pack=D-O · R5-2JE · R6-LE5 · R2-SHP|link=star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker-droid-factory-figure-set-400911413423.html}}</ref> in [[November]], 2019.<ref name="ToyDate">{{WebCite|url=https://www.jeditemplearchives.com/content/modules.php?name=JReviews&rop=showcontent&id=3855|text=D-O/R5-2JE/R6-LE5/R2-SHP - Droid Factory|author=Harrison, Paul|date=2019-12-12|archivedate=20210116080954}}</ref> The set was tied into the [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']],<ref name="Toy" /> released on [[December 20]], [[2019]],<ref name="Jedi Redeemed">{{InsiderCite|196|The Jedi Redeemed: Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker|The Jedi Redeemed: ''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker''}}</ref> and although R5-2JE made an appearance in the film itself the bolt was not visible.<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> The bolt was however identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
