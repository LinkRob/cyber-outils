The '''B'ankor Refuge''', or '''B'ankora Refuge''', was a small sanctuary on [[Coruscant]] given by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Chasen Piian]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] in perpetuity to [[B'ankora homeworld cataclysm|surviving]] members of the [[B'ankora]] [[species]] during the waning years of the Republic.

Immediately after the [[Order 66|end]] of the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the B'ankora were forcefully removed from their reserve after an Imperial edict citing ancient laws of eminent domain was enacted. The B'ankora were brought to the lifeless [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] world [[Parau VI]], where the species became extinct. The buildings and fields that had once been home to the species were razed and the refuge was replaced by an Imperial energy research center. Some citizens objected to the refuge being replaced, but after some time, public opinion no longer thought much of the issue.

==Description==
The B'ankor Refuge was a sanctuary located between the pole and the equator in the western hemisphere of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Unlike the fully urbanized eastern hemisphere, the area did not have [[Coruscant weather control|weather control]]. The refuge was built by [[construction droid]]s and complete with small [[lake]]s, fertile soil, copses of [[tree]]s and other [[plant]]s, and even two artificial hills. All in all, the B'ankor Refuge was 5.1 square kilometers.<ref name="Catalyst" />

Following the relocation of the B'ankora species, the buildings and fields that had once been their home were razed, their surface trails were erased, while air control stations and surveillance towers were erected.<ref name="Catalyst" />

==History==
===Republic Era===
The area was originally a privately owned [[grass]]land that was neglected due to legal difficulties.<ref name="Catalyst" /> Around [[1300 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> after the [[B'ankora homeworld|homeworld]] of the [[B'ankora]] [[species]] suffered a [[B'ankora homeworld cataclysm|cataclysmic collision]] with a [[meteor]], the [[Galactic Republic]] acquired the entire area and made it a temporary sanctuary for the members of the B'ankora species. During the waning years of the Galactic Republic, the [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Chasen Piian]] granted the land that eventually came to be known as the B'ankor Refuge in perpetuity to the sole surviving members of the species.<ref name="Catalyst" />

===Imperial Era===
Immediately after the [[Order 66|end]] of the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the B'ankora were [[Relocation of the B'ankora|forcefully removed]] from their reserve after an Imperial edict citing ancient laws of eminent domain was enacted. The B'ankora were brought to the lifeless [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] world<ref name="Catalyst" /> [[Parau VI]], where the species became extinct.<ref name="Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref> The buildings and fields that had once been home to the species were razed and the refuge was replaced by an [[Project Celestial Power complex|Imperial energy research center]] affiliated with [[Project Celestial Power]]. Some citizens objected to the refuge being replaced, but after some time, public opinion no longer thought much of the issue.<ref name="Catalyst" />

During her time on the [[planet]] [[Watassay]] in the [[Five Points system]], [[Jyn Erso]] reminisced about [[Erso family|her family]], including [[Lyra Erso|her mother's]] attempts to protect [[Legacy world]]s and her research of the B'ankor Refuge.<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The B'ankor Refuge first appeared in the [[2016]] novel ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'', written by [[James Luceno]]. The novel served as a prequel to the [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Catalyst" />

The 2016 reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]], originally dated the B'ankor being granted the B'ankor Refuge to [[131 BBY]],<ref name="Rebel Dossier date">According to ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'' the [[B'ankora]] were granted the B'ankor Refuge 112 [[Standard year|years]] before the end of [[Proclamation of the New Order|the end]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. As such it could be deduced that the B'ankor Refuge was established in [[131 BBY]].</ref> but in ''Catalyst'' it is stated that they had lived in the Refuge "for more than fifty generations" and had planted a copse of gargantuan [[tree]]s "centuries earlier" by the time of the novel.<ref name="Catalyst" /> [[2023]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' later instead placed the event to [[1300 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Because ''Timelines'' is the newer source and it's date lines up better with what is established in ''Catalyst'', this article assumes it to be correct.
