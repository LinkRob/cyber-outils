'''Crystalstorms''' were a type of weather which occurred on the [[mineral]] [[planet]] [[Crait]],<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> the [[crystal]]line planet<ref name="Databank Christophsis">{{Databank|christophsis|Christophsis}}</ref> [[Christophsis]],<ref name="How Not to Get Eaten by Furry Teddy Bears">''[[Star Wars: How Not to Get Eaten by Ewoks and Other Galactic Survival Skills]]''</ref> and the [[desert]] planet<ref name="Databank Seelos">{{Databank|seelos|Seelos}}</ref> [[Seelos]], where the storms were a hazard.<ref name="How Not to Get Eaten by Furry Teddy Bears" /> On Crait, crystalstorms were known to kick up crystals from beneath the planet's surface.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> When the [[Nupayuni Mining Consortium]] first charted Crait, the [[mining]] company erected several structures, including a [[blast door]] that could withstand crystalstorms.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> At some point during or prior to [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Leia Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]]. Since the [[Crait outpost|outpost]] on [[Crait]] was constructed prior to the events of the novel, the outpost must have been constructed by then.</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] leader [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] established a [[Crait outpost|hidden outpost]] on the planet, which once bore witness to a crystalstorm.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crystalstorms were first mentioned in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'' written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TLJ VD" />
