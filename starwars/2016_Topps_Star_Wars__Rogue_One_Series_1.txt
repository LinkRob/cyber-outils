'''2016 Topps ''Star Wars: Rogue One'' Series 1''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] on [[December 16]], [[2016]],<ref name="CardCon" /> coinciding with the release of the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Year By Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> The set features a variety of cards based on the film and also includes cards with autographs from actors.<ref name="CardCon" />
