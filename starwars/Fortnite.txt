'''''Fortnite''''' is a [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed and published by [[Epic Games]]. Several of its updates have included ''[[Star Wars]]'' content, beginning with the ''[[Fortnite X Star Wars]]'' event which occurred in the ''[[Fortnite Battle Royale|'''Battle Royale''']]'' mode to promote {{Film|IX}} between [[December 14]], [[2019]] and [[January 7]], [[2020]]. Since then, new ''Star Wars'' cosmetics have been added periodically, and ''Fortnite'' has hosted entire ''Star Wars'' limited-time events such as "Skywalker Week" in [[2022]] and "Find the Force" in [[2023]]. As ''Fortnite'' has further expanded into a game platform and debuted other game modes (namely ''[[Fortnite Festival|'''Fortnite Festival''']]'', ''[[LEGO Fortnite|'''LEGO Fortnite''']]'', and ''[[Rocket Racing|'''Rocket Racing''']]),'' special ''Star Wars'' content has been added to these modes as well. Some ''Star Wars'' cosmetics are also compatible with the '''''Save the World''''' and '''''Creative''''' game modes. In [[2026]], new official game modes based on ''Star Wars''&mdash;''[[Star Wars: Galactic Siege]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Droid Tycoon]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: Escape Vader]]''&mdash;were added.

In [[2025]], ''Fortnite'' introduced the first-ever season based entirely on ''Star Wars'', titled ''Fortnite Galactic Battle''. The season included elements from the [[original trilogy|original]], [[prequel trilogy|prequel]], and [[sequel trilogy|sequel]] trilogies and featured a five-part "saga" of weekly story updates in the ''Battle Royale'' mode; ''Imperial Takeover'', ''The Pull of the Force'', ''Mandalorian Rising'', ''Star Destroyer Bombardment'', and ''Death Star Sabotage''.

In the world of ''Fortnite'', an alternate version of the ''Star Wars'' universe exists as an adjacent reality to the several realities that the game's various modes take place in, and crosses over with them. This makes the ''Star Wars'' universe one of the many realities spawned from the [[W:c:fortnite:The Zero Point|Zero Point]] and connects it to the game's larger continuity. The ''Star Wars'' reality formerly crossed over with [[w:c:fortnite:Reality Zero|Reality Zero]] before the latter's collapse during the events of [[w:c:fortnite:The Big Bang|The Big Bang]].

The crossover event was first announced on [[December 8]]<ref name="TROS Announcement">{{Twitter|fortnite|status/1203392481532039170|Fortnite|quote=Live from Risky Reels ''Fortnite'' Presents: #StarWarsTheRiseOfSkywalker exclusive scene premiere. ===Find the Force===
{{Quote|Every saga has a beginning.|Official tagline|<ref name="Find the Force">{{FortniteWeb|url=news/fortnite-battle-royale-v24-30-update-find-the-force-and-begin-your-training|text=Fortnite BR v24.30 Update: "Find the Force" and Begin Your Training}}</ref>}}
[[File:FindTheForceKeyArt-Fortnite.png|left|thumb|300px|"Find the Force" promotional image.]]
Chapter 4: Season 2, titled ''Mega'', began on [[March 10]], [[2023]] and concluded on [[June 8]], 2023. The season's ''Star Wars'' content notably included the "Find the Force" event, which ran from May 2 through May 23, 2023, at 9 AM ET. The event featured the addition of the [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[501st Legion|501st Trooper]], and [[212th Attack Battalion|212th Trooper]] cosmetic outfits to the Item Shop. Players could complete "Find the Force" Quests to gain Galactic Reputation and unlock cosmetic and in-game rewards. The free Reward Track included a basic [[Clone trooper|Clone Trooper]] skin, with an option to purchase the Premium Reward Track that included the [[104th Battalion|Wolf Pack Trooper]], [[332nd Company|Ahsoka's Clone Trooper]], and [[Maul|Darth Maul]] outfits. By purchasing the Premium Reward Track, players automatically unlocked the [[Coruscant Guard]] skin. The Quests for "Find the Force" were set up as a trilogy and epilogue, mirroring the [[prequel trilogy]]. The first set of Quests, titled "The Force Within," went live with the event, and were followed by "Begun the Clone Wars Have" on [[May 7]], "Fall of the Republic" on [[May 12]], and "The First Galactic Empire" on [[May 17]]. By completing twelve Quests in each set, players would earn a [[Sith holocron|Sith Holocron]], and after earning three of them would unlock the [[Scimitar|Sith Infiltrator]]. The event-specific gameplay in ''Battle Royale'' featured the appearances of Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin Skywalker, and Darth Maul in [[hologram]] form on the Island, where if players accepted, they would jump into each character's respective Rift Gate and be given one of three Lightsaber Mythic weapons which would also grant Force abilities. The [[DC-15A blaster rifle]] was added as a new Mythic weapon, acquirable from Republic Chests across the Island.<ref name="Find the Force" />

Chapter 4: Season 4, titled ''Last Resort'', began on [[August 25]], 2023 and concluded on [[November 3]], 2023. The Battle Pass for the season included [[Ahsoka Tano]] as an unlockable skin by completing special Ahsoka Tano Quests that were available for Battle Pass owners beginning [[September 26]] and lasted through the rest of the season. Ahsoka also appeared on the Island in hologram form as a trainer, just like Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin Skywalker, and Darth Maul during the previous "Find the Force" event. Accepting Ahsoka's training and entering her Rift Gate would grant players the Jedi Training Lightsaber Mythic weapon and allow you to use special Force abilities.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

===Star Wars Day 2024===
[[File:FNxSW-KeyArt.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Key art for the Star Wars Day 2024 event]]
A [[May 4|''Star Wars'' Day]] celebration event began across the ''Fortnite'' universe on [[May 3]], [[2024]], and will conclude on [[May 14]], 2024. Notably the first ''Star Wars'' collaboration since the launch of the ''LEGO Fortnite'', ''Fortnite Festival'', and ''Rocket Racing'' game modes on the ''Fortnite'' platform, the event brought ''Star Wars'' content to all of these modes in addition to ''Battle Royale''. Each mode had its own special ''Star Wars'' Quests which could be completed for rewards.<ref name="SWD 2024">{{FortniteWeb|url=news/star-wars-lands-in-the-fortnite-universe-the-eve-of-star-wars-day|text=Star Wars Lands in the Fortnite Universe the Eve of Star Wars Day!}}</ref>

In ''LEGO Fortnite'', a new "Rebel Adventure" was added, introducing the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] to the ''LEGO'' world. Players could assist the Rebels by building up the Rebel Village and defending it from the Empire using the new Lightsaber, [[Bowcaster]], [[DL-44 heavy blaster pistol|DL-44 Blaster]], [[E-11 medium blaster rifle|E-11 Blaster]], and [[Thermal detonator|Thermal Detonator]] weapons. The "Rebel Adventure" LEGO Pass included unlockable rewards including ''Star Wars''-themed LEGO builds and the exclusive new [[Chewbacca]] outfit obtained via the Premium Reward Track.<ref name="SWD 2024" />

In ''Battle Royale'', Chewbacca was introduced on the Island, where he was captured by the Empire. Players could save Chewbacca by going to a specific Imperial Roadblock on the Island, which would change with each match. Once freeing Chewbacca, he would join you in battle as a follower, utilizing his Bowcaster weapon and giving you your own Wookiee Bowcaster Mythic weapon as a thanks for freeing him. The Mythic was also available from Imperial Chests located at the various Imperial Roadblocks across the Island. The Darth Vader Boss encounter as well as the Darth Vader's Lightsaber and E-11 Blaster Mythic weapons also returned.<ref name="SWD 2024" />

In ''Fortnite Festival'', "[[Mad About Me|Cantina Band]]" by [[John Williams]] and the [[London Symphony Orchestra]] was added as a playable Jam Track. The Jam Stage was also changed to resemble the [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina|Mos Eisley Cantina]], and completing seven of the mode's ''Star Wars'' Quests would award players with a [[Seven-string hallikset|Seven-string Hallikset]] guitar.<ref name="SWD 2024" />

In ''Rocket Racing'', completing May the Fourth Quests and ''Star Wars'' Quests would unlock the [[Anakin Skywalker's Podracer|Anakin's Podracer]] and Darth Maul vehicle decals and the [[Energy binder|Energy Binders]] trail.<ref name="SWD 2024" />

Additionally, a variety of ''Star Wars'' cosmetics were added to the Item Shop, including the Dagobah Luke, [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], and [[Death trooper|AWR Trooper]] outfits, the [[Beskar]] car body, including decals based on Din Djarin, [[The Armorer]], Boba Fett, [[Bo-Katan Kryze]], and [[Sabine Wren]], and the Endorian Drum Kit and [[Red ball jett organ|Nalargon]] Keytar instruments.<ref name="SWD 2024" />

===First Order Stormtrooper===
Leading up to the release of ''Galactic Battle'', a First Order Stormtrooper from the ''Star Wars'' franchise was released on [[April 29]], [[2025]] as a free reward for linking your MyDisney account to your Epic Games account.

===''Fortnite Galactic Battle''===
{{Quote|A GALACTIC RECKONING IS COMING...|Quote from the ''Fortnite Galactic Battle'' announcement trailer|<ref name="Galactic Battle">{{FortniteYouTube|RKZPMyAvEXs|text=Fortnite Galactic Battle Teaser Trailer|date=2025-04-19}}</ref>}}
[[File:Fortnite_Battle_Royale_Galactic_Battle_key_art.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Key art for ''Fortnite Galactic Battle'']]
The entirety of Chapter 6: Mini Season 1 was a month-long ''Star Wars''-themed season entitled ''Fortnite Galactic Battle'', announced at [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan 2025]] with a segment featuring [[Anthony Carboni]] and [[Ahmed Best]],<ref name="Cel 2025">{{SWYouTube|83cNchT1gdA|Star Wars Celebration - DAY 3 Replay|livestream=p20GM68wyC4}}</ref>. Coinciding with Star Wars Day 2025 and including elements from the entire timeline, the season contained a ''Star Wars''-themed Battle Pass, weekly content updates, and featured both characters from ''Star Wars'' media&mdash;such as [[Darth Sidious]], [[Mace Windu]], and [[Jar Jar Binks|Darth Jar Jar]]&mdash;as well as mash-up characters based on characters from both ''Star Wars'' and ''Fortnite'' like Wookiee Team Leader.<ref name="SW GB">{{SW|url=news/swcj-2025-fortnite-star-wars-galactic-battle|text=Fortnite & ''Star Wars'' Collide Like Never Before in the new Galactic Battle Season}}</ref>

The season was divided into five chapters; the first, ''Imperial Takeover'', launched on [[May 2]], the same day as the season. It was followed by ''The Pull of the Force'' on [[May 8]], ''Mandalorian Rising'' on [[May 22]], ''Star Destroyer Bombardment'' on [[May 29]], and finally, the live in-game event ''Death Star Sabotage'' on [[June 7]], which culminated the season.<ref name="Galactic Battle" /><ref name="SW GB" />

===Zero Hour===
Chapter 6 concluded on [[November 29]], 2025, with the Zero Hour live event. Calling back to ''Galactic Battle'', the protagonists Jones and Hope contacted the [[Resistance]] to aid them in the battle against the Dark Presence, with the Galactic Empire assisting as well.

===Star Wars Day 2026 and new game modes===
As part of the celebration for [[May 4|''Star Wars'' Day]] [[2026]], new content and game modes are set to be added to ''Fortnite''. This includes new cosmetics such as a back-bling of [[w:c:fortnite:Fishstick|Fishstick]] frozen in [[carbonite]], new [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]] skins based on their appearances in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', a new [[Din Djarin]] skin with a stylized "pen and ink" design, a [[BDX droid]] sidekick, and the new games ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Siege]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Escape Vader]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: Droid Tycoon]]''. New content is also planned for ''[[LEGO Fortnite Odyssey]]'', and a watch party containing a ten-minute preview of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]'' will be shown in-game.<ref name="StarWarsStandouts">{{FortniteWeb|url=news/a-galaxy-of-new-star-wars-games-are-coming-to-fortnite-more-than-ever-before|text=A Galaxy of New Star Wars Games are Coming to Fortnite &ndash; More than Ever Before}}</ref>
