The '''grandfather''' of the [[pilot]]s [[Bly Tevin]] and [[Lina]] was an [[E'roni]] [[Gender|man]]. At some point during their younger [[Standard day|days]], they served in the [[E'ronoh's military|military]] of their [[homeworld]]&mdash;the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]&mdash;to combat the [[Eiram's military|forces]] of the neighboring planet [[Eiram]]. The man owned a [[bane blade]]&mdash;a ceremonial [[sword]] carried by every E'roni [[soldier]]&mdash;which they eventually passed down to Tevin.<ref name="Convergence" />

By [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Tevin enlisted in [[E'ronoh's fleet]] to follow in his grandfather's footsteps and fight on behalf of their [[Bly Tevin's family|family]]. While on a supply escort [[Skirmish in the Eiram system|mission]]<ref name="Convergence" /> that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> he thought about why he had joined the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|fight]] against Eiram on his homeworld's behalf, reflecting that he wanted to finish the struggle his grandfather had participated in as a young man. During the endeavor, when Tevin struggled to reset the controls of his [[Saboteur|sabotaged]] [[Bly Tevin's devilfighter|devilfighter]], the pilot used his grandfather's dully edged [[Weapon|blade]] to strike a port on his navigation panel, causing the [[Devilfighter|vessel]] to power down.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bly Tevin's grandfather was mentioned in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase II<ref name="Convergence" /> that was published on [[November 22]], [[2022]].<ref name="ReleaseDates">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-convergence-excerpt|text=A Mysterious Mission Begins in ''The High Republic: Convergence'' &ndash; Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref> Prior to its release, the grandfather's mention was included in a preview for the novel released on the official [[Wikipedia:Penguin Random House|Penguin Random House]] [https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com website]<ref name="Excerpt">{{WebCite|url=https://sites.prh.com/convergence|text=STAR WARS: CONVERGENCE EXTENDED EXCERPT|work=[[Wikipedia:Penguin Random House|Penguin Random House]]|archivedate=20221004162329}}</ref> on [[October 4]] of that year.<ref name="ReleaseDates" />
