A '''Batuu-bon''' was a sweet dessert served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. It was a spherical [[chocolate]] [[cake]] with a [[caf]]-flavored filling. The pastry was topped with a small disk-shaped chocolate treat depicting an emblem of Black Spire Outpost.<ref name="DB7" />

==Behind the scenes==
Batuu-bon is a dessert found in the [[In-universe|real-world]] Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. The menu's description reads, "Chocolate cake, white chocolate mousse, coffee custard." The dessert also includes a small chocolate coin.<ref name="DB7" />
