'''Bansee''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] member of the [[White Worms]] gang on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. She caught the [[eye]] of the gang's leader, [[Lady]] [[Proxima]], for being the best [[rat]] catcher amongst the [[scrumrat]]s her age and was made [[Third Girl]]. In [[13 BBY]], she was present when the scrumrat [[Han Solo|Han]] escaped from Proxima in the [[Den of the White Worms]].

==Biography==
Bansee was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] from the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]<ref name="USW" /> who served as a [[scrumrat]] in the [[White Worms]] gang of [[Coronet City]]. As the best [[rat]] catcher out of all of the girls her age, Bansee caught the attention of the leader of the gang, [[Lady]] [[Proxima]], who made her [[Third Girl]].<ref name="SoloOG" />

In the [[Standard year|year]] [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' that take place on [[Corellia]] to six years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]]; therefore, the events on Corellia in ''Solo'' must take place in [[13 BBY]].</ref> Bansee was present in the den when fellow scrumrat [[Han Solo|Han]] was brought before Proxima by [[Moloch]],<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> one of the gang's [[lieutenant]]s.<ref name="USW" /> Han, who had returned from a trade with no goods for Proxima, used a rock to break a shutter behind the gang leader and bathe her in light, which burned her skin. Using the distraction, Han then pushed through the other scrumrats and escaped.<ref name="Solo" />

{{GameMechanics|Unfinished Business (Outer Rim)|''Unfinished Business''}}
After an individual infiltrated the White Worms in order to retrieve a client's [[datapad]], Bansee tasked them with "sending a message" to one of the gangs enemies in order to prove their loyalty. The individual succeeded in the task, but Bansee ended up threatening them with a [[stun baton]] and questioning who they worked for. The individual managed to avoid Bansee's wrath and ultimately acquired the datapad they were after.<ref name="Unfinished" />
{{EndGame}}

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Bansee-CGSWG.png|thumb|200px|right|Bansee was ambitious, wanting to climb the ranks of the White Worms.]]
One of the older scumrats,<ref name="Junior Novel">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> Bansee was clever and a tough fighter, but mean-spirited and humorless.<ref name="SoloOG" /> The [[Corellian]]'s talent with the pipe she called the "[[rat hammer]]" caught Lady Proxima's attention, and the scrumrat did not hesitate to use it on anyone who displeased her.<ref name="Junior Novel" /> Despite holding the rank of Third Girl,<ref name="SoloOG" /> she was ambitious and eager to climb higher within the gang, possibly by becoming one of Moloch's thugs. She had [[Color|tan]] skin, brown [[hair]], and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="USW" />

==Equipment==
Bansee wore a gray [[Salt|brine]]-stained [[Clothing|robes]] with a brown [[belt]] and [[boot]]s. She carried a satchel for stolen parts, wore a red light-filter headband, and used a battered pipe known as the "rat hammer" as a makeshift weapon.<ref name="SoloOG" /> She also made use of a [[stun]] [[baton]].<ref name="Unfinished" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bansee first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> The character was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" />
