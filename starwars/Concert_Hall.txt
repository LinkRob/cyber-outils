The '''Concert Hall''' was a [[music]] venue that had been constructed in [[Theed]], [[capital]] of the [[human]] [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] culture on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]], by the [[Battle of Naboo]]<ref name="SWCL2016" /> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> The building was circular in shape with [[Color|tan]] walls and a turquoise domed roof matching the architectural style of many other structures in the [[city]].<ref name="SWCL2016" /> Featured in every tourist guide,<ref name="BMF24">{{BuildFalconCite|24|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> the hall was built beside the banks of the [[Solleu River]], with [[tree]]s growing between it and the [[water]].<ref name="SWCL2016" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Concert Hall was first mentioned in "Theed: Naboo's Capital City," an article in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 24|twenty-fourth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF24" /> which was released around [[June 17]], [[2015]]<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|24}}</ref> and written by [[Tim Veekhoven]].<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:Credits-TimVeekhoven-Twitter.png|creator=[[Tim Veekhoven]]}}</ref> The Concert Hall was then pictured in canon in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="SWCL2016" /> from the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]], illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]], and marked the first mention of the [[Concert Hall/Legends|hall]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
