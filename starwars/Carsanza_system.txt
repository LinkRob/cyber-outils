The '''Carsanza system''' was the site of a [[Skirmish at Carsanza|skirmish]] prior to the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion's]] tactics during the skirmish were later re-used&mdash;along with tactics from a [[Skirmish at Nexator|skirmish]] at [[Nexator system|Nexator]]&mdash;during the pivotal [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Rogue One novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref>
