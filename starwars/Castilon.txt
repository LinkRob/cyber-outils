'''Castilon''' was an [[ocean]] [[planet]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]], and was located close to the borders of [[Wild Space]]. It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Chelidae]] [[species]] and the ''[[Colossus]]''&mdash;an [[Starship|aircraft]] [[Fuel|refueling]] station, as well as a destination for [[racer]]s&mdash;was located on Castilon, along with several other inhabited structures. During the [[Cold War]] between the [[New Republic]] and the [[First Order]], [[Resistance]] [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] sent the [[pilot]]-turned-[[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] on a [[Mission on the Colossus platform|mission]] to Castilon. While Xiono was aboard the Colossus, the First Order forced its [[captain]], [[Imanuel Doza]], into allowing them to act as security, making way for them to [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupy the platform]] and slowly take control. The ''Colossus'' was eventually forced to [[Battle of Castilon|flee]] the planet in order to escape the First Order, rising from the ocean and jumping into [[hyperspace]].

==Description==
{{Quote|For most people, this wa-a-aterball is the other side of the galaxy.|[[Yani]]|<ref name="The Children from Tehar" />}}
A terrestrial [[planet]] covered in vast [[ocean]]s<ref name="Castilon DB" /> and dotted with isolated islands, Castilon was located in the [[Tashtor sector]]'s<ref name="The Escape" /> [[Castilon system]], within the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]],<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> also falling in the region known as the [[Western Reaches]],<ref name="Castilon DB" /> in grid square I-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Coordinates" /> The [[Star system|system]] had one [[star]],<ref name="The Recruit" /> and the planet itself was [[orbit]]ed by two [[moon]]s.<ref name="Bibo Bucket's List" /> It had an [[atmosphere]] that was breathable to the various [[species]] that inhabited it, including [[human]]s.<ref name="The Recruit" />

[[File:CastilonSurface-TheHighTower.jpg|thumb|left|240px|Castilon and its two moons during night]]
Castilon was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Chelidae]], a [[species]] of [[amphibian]] [[Sentience|sentients]] who preferred to live above the [[water]],<ref name="TCfT Bucket's List" /> and was also home to many non-sentient creatures, including both the [[Bird|avian]] [[speagull]]s<ref name="The High Tower" /> and aquatic creatures such as the [[sharvo fish]],<ref name="The Recruit" /> [[snarlfish]],<ref name="Snarlfish" /> and the massive [[rokkna]].<ref name="Bibo" /> A number of smaller creatures lived in the oceans of the planet and were often the prey of the larger creatures, such as the speagulls.<ref name="Speagull DB">{{Databank|speagull|speagull}}</ref> While most of the planet was covered in ocean, there were occasional inhabited structures above the surface of the water.<ref name="Castilon DB" />

==History==
===Home of the ''Colossus''===
Over several [[Millennium|millennia]], a number of battles were fought over Castilon, leaving [[starship]] wreckage scattered across the seafloor.<ref name="USWNE" /> The [[space station]]<ref name="No Escape: Part 1">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 1}}</ref> and [[supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]'' was stationed on the planet, having sat partially submerged in Castilon's oceans since at least [[14 ABY]].<ref name="14 ABY Date">"[[No Escape: Part 2]]" states that the ''Colossus'' has remained idle on Castilon for at least twenty [[Standard year|years]]. The episode takes place simultaneous to the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. This places the ''Colossus'' [[Legacy of Vader 1]] presence on Castilon to 14 ABY or earlier.</ref> The platform came to be owned by [[Captain]] [[Imanuel Doza]], and fostered a vibrant racing circuit that became famous throughout [[the galaxy]].<ref name="The High Tower" /> Following the [[Battle of Endor]], a [[Battle at Castilon|battle]] was fought at the planet between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[New Republic]], which resulted in a [[Star Destroyer]] being sent into the ocean.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY Date">[[Star Wars Resistance Season One|''Star Wars Resistance'' Season One]] takes place some [[time]] before, and later concurrent to the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Doza lived in the ''Colossus''{{'}} [[Doza Tower|tower]] with his daughter [[Torra Doza|Torra]],<ref name="The Recruit" /> his [[droid]] assistant [[4D-M1N]], and the [[pilot]]s of [[Ace Squadron]].<ref name="The High Tower" /> The platform was frequently attacked by nearby pirates, though after the First Order betrayed them both crews had a common enemy.<ref name="No Escape: Part 2">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 2}}</ref>

===The First Order===
{{Quote|If they're building up their military, they need fuel and supplies. Sources tell us that somebody on Castilon is helping them.|Poe Dameron, on the First Order|<ref name="The Recruit" />}}
Late in the [[Cold War]], the [[First Order]] became interested in using the ''Colossus'' as a staging area.<ref name="The Triple Dark">{{Resistance|The Triple Dark}}</ref> However, Captain Doza refused to place the platform under First Order control,<ref name="The High Tower" /> although he did try to keep relations with them cordial.<ref name="The Children from Tehar" /> The First Order began staging [[pirate]] attacks against the platform, in an attempt to covertly pressure Doza into eventually inviting the First Order to act as security on the ''Colossus''.<ref name="The Triple Dark" /> At the same [[time]], the [[Resistance]], suspecting that the First Order had a [[spy]] on the platform, sent one of their own, the newly recruited [[Kazuda Xiono]], on a [[Mission on the Colossus platform|mission to the ''Colossus'']] to identify what the First Order wanted. Xiono was accompanied by [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]]'s [[astromech droid]], [[BB-8]].<ref name="The Recruit" />

The First Order used the [[Warbird gang]] led by [[Kragan Gorr]] to [[Attack on the Colossus|attack]] the ''Colossus''<ref name="The Triple Dark" /> [[Raid on the Colossus|several times]] in an attempt to make Doza accept their proposal,<ref name="Synara's Score">{{Resistance|Synara's Score}}</ref> but he remained reluctant. By [[Torra Doza's abduction|kidnapping his daughter]], the First Order convinced Doza to allow their presence,<ref name="The Doza Dilemma">{{Resistance|The Doza Dilemma}}</ref> leading to an [[occupation of the Colossus]] platform,<ref name="The First Order Occupation">{{Resistance|The First Order Occupation}}</ref> which continued to escalate. Civilian inhabitants of the ''Colossus''<ref name="The New Trooper">{{Resistance|The New Trooper}}</ref> who were once accepting of the First Order<ref name="The First Order Occupation" /> began to protest against the occupation.<ref name="The New Trooper" /> The First Order eventually targeted residents who opposed or defied their control, placing them under arrest to be sent offworld.<ref name="The Disappeared">{{Resistance|The Disappeared}}</ref>

[[File:Descent-EpisodeGuide.jpg|thumb|right|240px|The ''Colossus'' sinks into the ocean]]
After the First Order discovered Xiono's true identity as a spy, he, along with [[Jarek Yeager]] and [[Neeku Vozo]], planned to send a message out to the Resistance. To reach the First Order's communications jammer on Doza Tower, they coordinated with Captain Doza, sinking the platform almost entirely underneath the surface of the ocean. They successfully disabled the jammer, contacting [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who informed them that the Resistance was unable to provide assistance.<ref name="Descent">{{Resistance|Descent (episode)}}</ref>

===Escape of the ''Colossus''===
{{Quote|Captain Doza, get ready to lock the station down. Neeku got the hyperdrive working.''"<br />"''We're still gaining altitude. We can't jump until we've cleared the planet's gravity.|Kazuda Xiono and Imanuel Doza|<ref name="No Escape: Part 2" />}}
After expressing concern over the ethics and legality of the First Order's activities, Captain Doza was arrested by Commander [[Pyre]], who took over the platform for himself. Xiono and Torra attempted to free the captured residents, but were interrupted when the First Order troopers on the platform stopped to witness [[Starkiller Base]]'s [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of [[Hosnian Prime]].<ref name="No Escape: Part 1" />

While the platform's stormtroopers were watching the destruction via [[hologram]], Xiono and Torra successfully freed Captain Doza, along with Yeager. With the help of Vozo, they let water into the ''Colossus'' to flush out the stormtroopers who were patrolling the corridors, sending them out into the planet's ocean. Losing control of the ''Colossus'', Pyre decided to leave the planet, along with the [[First Order Security Bureau]]'s [[Agent]] [[Tierny]], who convinced Xiono's former friend [[Tamara Ryvora]] to join them. Before they could board their [[shuttle]], Xiono ordered Vozo to activate the station's [[hyperdrive]], lifting it up from the ocean.<ref name="No Escape: Part 2" />

[[File:ColossusRises-NoEscapePart2.jpg|thumb|left|240px|The ''Colossus'' leaves Castilon]]
The First Order abandoned the ''Colossus'', sending [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] to [[Battle of Castilon|attack]] it. The Ace Squadron pilots were freed and sent to defend the station, along with Xiono and Yeager. The First Order sent a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] to assist in the battle, leading Xiono to give Vozo the coordinates to the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]]. As the ''Colossus'' was still within Castilon's [[gravity well]] and could not enter [[hyperspace]], the station's defenders continued to hold off the First Order forces until it had ascended to a suitable height. Vozo activated the hyperdrive, but was unable to enter in the entire set of coordinates, and the ''Colossus'' escaped Castilon, heading to an unknown location in the [[galaxy]].<ref name="No Escape: Part 2" />

The [[Thunderer|Star Destroyer]] remained on station above Castilon for a short time, during which Pyre and Tierny reported the ''Colossus''{{'}} escape to Captain [[Phasma]] and received new orders to [[Hunt for the Colossus|hunt down the station]] at all costs and Ryvora was formally inducted in as [[cadet]] DT-533, before departing on its search.<ref name="Into the Unknown">{{Resistance|Into the Unknown}}</ref>

In order to help some residents of the ''Colossus'' who were feeling claustrophobic during the station's journey through space, Vozo set up a [[hologram]] projection of Castilon's sky in the station marketplace.<ref name="Hunt on Celsor 3">{{Resistance|Hunt on Celsor 3}}</ref> Some time after the [[Battle of Crait]], a First Order list of most wanted targets contained a number of names with their last known location listed as Castilon.<ref name="Resistance Reborn">''[[Resistance Reborn]]''</ref>

Later, in [[35 ABY]], Kazuda Xiono and Jarek Yeager returned to Castilon to rendezvous with Tamara Ryvora, who had seemingly defected from the First Order. The two arrived on the ocean world in their stolen First Order [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport|AAL transport]], and they waited on a racing ring, something the Aces had used when the ''Colossus'' was at rest. After a while, three TIE fighters appeared in Castilon's cloudy skies. One of them was Tamara Ryvora, who, after a brief skirmish with the other fighters, joined Xiono and Yeager on the racing ring. After a reunion, Commander Pyre and Agent Tierny's ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer appeared above the planet. It bombarded the surface, its powerful blasts pounding the ocean and creating large tidal waves. Xiono, Yeager, Ryvora, and [[CB-23]] managed to get their ship out of Castilon's atmosphere, only to be captured soon after. During this, an unconscious [[Jace Rucklin]] discovered where the AAL had come from, the [[Barabesh system]], meaning that the ''Colossus'' was there. After a while, the Star Destroyer left the Castilon system for the Barabesh system. It was later destroyed.<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref>

==Inhabitants==
The Chelidae were a species [[Homeworld|native]] to the oceans of Castilon. An amphibious species, they were able to live underwater, though preferred to live on land.<ref name="TCfT Bucket's List" /> The Chelidae had [[Chelidae language|their own language]], but could understand [[Galactic Basic Standard]],<ref name="The Children from Tehar" /> which was spoken by other inhabitants of the planet. Individuals of many other sentient species resided on board the ''Colossus'',<ref name="The Recruit" /> including the Chelidae, who worked as the platform's [[engineer]]s.<ref name="The Children from Tehar" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|The ''Colossus''. It's one of the few supertanker fuel depots left from the old days.|Poe Dameron|<ref name="The Recruit" />}}
The ''Colossus'', an old supertanker fuel depot<ref name="The Recruit" /> space station<ref name="No Escape: Part 1" /> converted into a public [[Fuel|refueling]] station,<ref name="The Recruit" /> was one of the few inhabited structures on the planet,<ref name="Castilon DB" /> until it was forced to leave Castilon to escape the First Order.<ref name="No Escape: Part 2" /> A number of smaller inhabited platforms and floating vessels were also located on Castilon.<ref name="USWNE" /> The [[Karavian Trench]] was located underneath the water, and was where [[Synara San]] salvaged a [[Clone Wars]]&ndash;era [[Z-95 Headhunter]]<ref name="Bibo Bucket's List" /> that contained the young rokkna [[Bibo]].<ref name="Bibo" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Resistance-ConceptArt.jpg|thumb|right|240px|Concept art for ''Star Wars Resistance'']]
Castilon first appeared in "[[The Recruit]],"<ref name="The Recruit" /> the first episode of the television show ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[October 7]], [[2018]]. It was first shown in the trailer for the series,<ref name="Resistance trailer">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-resistance-trailer|text=''Star Wars Resistance'' Trailer Revealed}}</ref> and later identified on the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] page for the ''Colossus''.<ref name="Colossus DB">{{Databank|colossus|Colossus}}</ref>

Early concept art of the ''Colossus'' on Castilon was created by [[Polygon Pictures]] and released on the concept art gallery for "The Recruit" on [[StarWars.com]]. In the concept art, the ''Colossus'' is made up of several structures, and Castilon has landmass above the water.<ref name="The Recruit Concept Art">{{SW|url=star-wars-resistance-the-recruit-concept-art-gallery|text=The Recruit Concept Art Gallery}}</ref>

The reference book ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'', places the Barabesh system is placed in two different regions of the galaxy. According to a map in the book, the Barabesh system is located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]], but another section places it in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> This appears to be related to the placement, by the ''[[Star Wars Resistance|Star Wars: Resistance]]'' episode ''[[The Recruit]]'', of [[Castilon]] a ways out in the Outer Rim on the edge of Wild Space and its proximity to Barabesh system in which the [[space station]] [[Colossus]] hid. As the [[2022]] [[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] also places Barabesh and Castilon in the Mid Rim, this article assumes it to be the currently correct placement until clarified by [[Lucasfilm]].<ref name="SWGalaxyMap">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref>
