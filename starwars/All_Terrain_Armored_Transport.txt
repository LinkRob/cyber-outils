{{Quote|They're the most heavily armored ground vehicles in the Imperial Army.''"<br />"''...so what you're saying is that you have a thorough knowledge of just how screwed we are.|[[Thane Kyrell]] and [[Yendor Brethen]], just before the Battle of Hoth|<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>}}
The '''All Terrain Armored Transport''' ('''AT-AT''') was a four-legged combat [[walker]] of the [[AT-series|All-terrain vehicle]] line used by the [[Imperial Army|ground forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], and the [[First Order]]. Along with the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]], [[stormtrooper]]s, and the [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]], it was one of the most famous symbols of the Empire's [[Imperial Military|military]] might.

==Models==
===Early model===
{{Main|All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)}}
This model was used against [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|the early rebellion]].<ref name="Relics of the Old Republic" /> This variant featured two [[heavy laser cannon]] [[turret]]s, four anti-personnel [[blaster]]s, and a dorsal twin [[laser]] turret at the rear.<ref name="Rebels Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Visual Guide: Epic Battles]]''</ref>

===Later model===
{{Main|All Terrain Armored Transport (Galactic Civil War)}}
This model was used during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="ESB" /> This variant was shorter than the early model, and featured dual [[Piperii-Cerlurn]] [[R-90C medium blaster]]s located on the sides or "temples," and a pair of [[Taim & Bak]] [[MS-1 fire-linked heavy laser cannon]]s mounted on the "chin."<ref name="AT-ACT Deluxe Book">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: AT-ACT Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]''</ref>

===Elite AT-AT===
{{Main|Elite AT-AT}}
This was a modified version of the Galactic Civil War AT-AT: the variant featured, among other things, thicker and darker [[armor]], heavier [[laser cannon]]s, and an elite crew of [[AT-AT pilot|pilots]].<ref name="Commander" />

===First Order AT-AT===
{{Main|First Order All Terrain Armored Transport}}
This was an updated version of the AT-AT used by the [[First Order]].<ref name="TLJ" />

==History==
===Age of the Empire===
{{Quote|I heard of these. Imperial armored transports. Giant four-legged things that were used to keep the peace on conquered worlds.|[[Scoggan]], to [[Davjan Igo]] upon entering the ruins of an AT-AT|<ref name="True Love">{{StoryCite|story=True Love|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref>}}
[[File:Rebel_defence_SWC.jpg|thumb|left|280px|AT-ATs lead an Imperial assault on a rebel outpost.]]
The AT-AT walker was developed during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> and used in the [[Imperial Army]], the ground forces of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was the successor of the [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] that also saw use during the Clone Wars,<ref name="sweATTE">{{SWE|technology|attewalker|AT-TE walker}}</ref> although it constituted a far more imposing example of walker technology.<ref name="sweATAT" /> Heavily armored and awe-inducing, the AT-AT positioned itself as the standard of Imperial firepower on the battlefield.<ref name="sweATST">{{SWE|technology|atstwalker|All Terrain Scout Transport}}</ref>

[[File:ProtoATATmainhold.png|thumb|right|280px| Cal Kestis infiltrated an AT-AT and took control of the walker during a confrontation on Kashyyyk.]]
Many AT-ATs were built in the factories of the planet [[Balmorra]].<ref name="HC 4">{{HelmetCollectionCite|4|Databank A-Z}}</ref> In [[14 BBY]], the Empire used AT-ATs against the [[Partisans]] led by [[Saw Gerrera]] on [[Kashyyyk]] during an attack on an imperial refinery. During the battle, the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] stole an AT-AT and used it to provide aid to Partisans. Kestis used the walker's firepower to eliminate most of the Imperial troops on the way to the refinery, but the AT-AT eventually crashed. When Imperial reinforcements arrived, the Partisans were forced to retreat. Gerrera shot down an AT-AT during the retreat.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

===Galactic Civil War===
{{Quote|That armor's too strong for blasters. Rogue group, use your harpoons and tow cables. Go for the legs; it might be our only chance of stopping them.|[[Luke Skywalker]], during the Battle of Hoth|<ref name="ESB" />}}
In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Relics of the Old Republic date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Relics of the Old Republic]]" to [[4 BBY]].</ref> the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] led three [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|early]] AT-AT walkers on an [[Assault on Seelos|assault]] on the desert planet of [[Seelos]] against the [[Spectres]] and three retired clone officers aboard an AT-TE.<ref name="Relics of the Old Republic" />

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> AT-ATs were again used against the Spectres, when [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] arrived on the [[planet]] to give the ''[[Ghost]]''{{'s}} crew three [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvettes]]. Two walkers were deployed to patrol the transports' landing platform, which were formally on Lothal to offer supplies to citizens impacted by the rebel efforts. When the rebels [[Mission on Lothal|tried to steal]] the corvettes, the ''Ghost'' started firing on the AT-ATs. Four [[proton torpedo]]s were not powerful enough to destroy one of the walkers, and it was necessary for [[Kanan Jarrus]] to use his [[Kanan Jarrus' lightsaber|lightsaber]] to cut its legs, toppling it. The second one was knocked down by the engines of the last Hammerhead, piloted by [[Ryder Azadi]].<ref name="A Princess on Lothal">{{Rebels|A Princess on Lothal}}</ref>

[[File:Imperial_walkers_Inside_Man.jpg|thumb|left|220x220px|AT-ATs on the planet Lothal.]]
In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="AIMDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[An Inside Man]]" to [[2 BBY]].</ref> [[Lieutenant]] [[Yogar Lyste]] deployed two AT-AT walkers, [[Walker 271]] and [[Walker 414]], to combat an attack on the east gate of the [[Imperial Armory Complex]] by members of the [[Lothal resistance group]] led by Ryder Azadi. The rebels Kanan, [[Ezra Bridger]], and [[C1-10P|Chopper]] escaped in the stolen [[All Terrain Defense Pod|AT-DP]] [[Walker 216]]. However, [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] realized that the rebels had hijacked the AT-DP walker and ordered his AT-ATs to crush them. One of the AT-AT walkers proceeded to crush the AT-DP under its heavy body but the rebels managed to use their [[lightsaber]]s to cut their way aboard. [[Morad Sumar]] also fired a rocket which hit one of the walker's legs. After knocking out the AT-AT pilots, the two climbed out and escaped with Ryder's cell into the wilderness.<ref name="An Inside Man">{{Rebels|An Inside Man}}</ref>

Thrawn then deployed four of these during the [[Battle of Atollon|battle]] on [[Atollon]]. [[Jedi]] survivor Kanan Jarrus then destroyed one with his lightsaber. The [[Bendu]] then destroyed two of the AT-AT walkers. However, the last one shot Bendu down from the sky.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

[[File:StolenATAT-2015StarWars3.png|thumb|right|250px|The stolen AT-AT on Cymoon 1]]
Around the time of the [[Battle of Yavin]], a [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Galactic Civil War)|new AT-AT model]] was introduced. At this time, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] stole a number of AT-ATs and provided them to [[Tatooine rebel cell|Saponza's Gang]].<ref name="Commander" />

During the Rebel [[Assault on Cymoon|assault]] on the Imperial [[weapons factory]] [[moon]] of [[Cymoon 1]], a group of Rebels led by [[Han Solo]] and Leia Organa stole an AT-AT from [[Weapons Factory Alpha]]. They used the walker to escape from the factory after they sabotaged its main [[Reactor|power core]] to explode. During their escape, Solo fired a shot that nearly killed the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], using all the AT-AT's [[MS-1 fire-linked heavy laser cannon|powerful]] [[R-90C medium blaster|armaments]].<ref name="Star Wars 2">[[Star Wars (2015) 2|''Star Wars'' (2015) 2]]</ref> Soon after, Vader managed to overtake the Rebels in their escape and cut down the massive walker's back left leg. The AT-AT fell tremendously and its passengers barely escaped the wreckage<ref name="Star Wars 3">[[Star Wars (2015) 3|''Star Wars'' (2015) 3]]</ref> before fleeing Cymoon 1 entirely for the safety of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]].<ref name="Star Wars 4">[[Star Wars (2015) 4|''Star Wars'' (2015) 4]]</ref>

During the [[Battle of Hoth]], [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]] led a squadron of at least five AT-ATs on the icy fields of [[Hoth]] to knock out the [[Deflector shield|shield]] [[Deflector shield generator|generator]] that protected [[Echo Base]], the Rebel headquarters. The triumph of Veers' squadron allowed the Imperials to invade Echo Base and rout the Rebels, striking a major blow in the war. However, the battle also revealed that the AT-ATs could be toppled by a cable wrapped around their legs. Although that weakness had been used effectively by the Alliance on Hoth,<ref name="ESB" /> the Empire continued to use these mechanical behemoths in the later stages of the war.<ref name="ROJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

[[File:Rebel-Sabatuer.png|thumb|left|300px|A Rebel Alliance saboteur destroys multiple AT-ATs]]
Several AT-ATs were deployed by Admiral [[Kalaxo]] during the [[Battle of Ab Dalis]].<ref name="SW 2020 15">[[Star Wars (2020) 15|''Star Wars'' (2020) 15]]</ref> A number of AT-ATs marched as part of an Imperial Unity Day parade on<ref name="SW 2020 26">[[Star Wars (2020) 26|''Star Wars'' (2020) 26]]</ref> the planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Bar'leth]] in the [[Core Worlds]], where the Empire was caught by surprise by a [[Unity Day attack|rebel attack]]. Caught totally off-guard, all AT-ATs and other mechanical marchers were destroyed by the time the rebels withdrew in an attack that was transmitted to the entire galaxy, proving the rebellion had survived the Battle of Hoth.<ref name="SW 2020 26" /> During the [[occupation of Endor]], at least one AT-AT was present on [[Endor]] prior to the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="ROJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

Shortly after the Battle of Endor, AT-ATs were present for the [[Battle of Cawa City]].<ref name="Shattered Empire 2">[[Shattered Empire 2|''Shattered Empire'' 2]]</ref> The rogue agents of [[Inferno Squad]] [[Iden Versio]] and [[Del Meeko]] used an AT-AT in order to escape [[Vardos]], during [[Operation: Cinder#Target: Vardos|Operation: Cinder]]. During the Battle of [[Naboo]] between the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]], four [[AT-series|AT-AT]] were deployed with two [[Gozanti-class cruiser|Gozanti]].<ref name="BFII">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> at least one AT-AT walker was stationed at [[Camp Sardo]] on the planet Kashyyyk. During the [[Liberation of Kashyyyk]], several [[Wookiee]] guerrilla fighters, including [[Kirratha]], hijacked the AT-AT walker and threw out the crew.<ref name="Life Debt" /> Later, AT-ATs were deployed against [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Defense Force|forces]] during the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />

===Rise of the First Order===
[[Standard decade|Decades]] after the war, a young [[scavenger]] named [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] used one of the downed AT-ATs, designated [[Hellhound Two]], as her home on [[Jakku]].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

The First Order used an [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport|updated version]] of the AT-AT in its [[First Order Army|army branch]]. The walkers were deployed under the command of [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], the [[Ren|master of the Knights of Ren]] and grandson of [[Darth]] Vader, during the First Order's [[Battle against the Benathy (First Order)|brief struggle]] against the [[Benathy]] [[species]].<ref name="Age of Resistance Kylo">[[Age of Resistance - Kylo Ren 1|''Age of Resistance - Kylo Ren'' 1]]</ref> Later, during the [[Battle of Crait]], these AT-ATs escorted the newer [[All Terrain MegaCaliber Six|AT-M6s]].<ref name="TLJ" /> This model of AT-AT walker was also stored aboard [[Commander]] [[Pyre]]'s [[Thunderer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]], though the [[warship]] was ultimately destroyed during the [[Battle of Barabesh]].<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:MCQ-atat.jpg|thumb|right|260px|AT-AT concept art by Ralph McQuarrie.]]
The AT-ATs in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']]<ref name="ESB" /> and the brief appearance in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Return of the Jedi'']]<ref name="ROJ" /> were filmed via [[Wikipedia:stop motion|stop motion]].

Initially, the Empire was to attack the Rebel base on Hoth using tanks.<ref name="DB">{{DB|vehicle|turbotank|clone turbo tank}}</ref> [[Joe Johnston]] created many designs of these vehicles,<ref name="Tippett">{{SWArchive|url=episode-iv/bts/article/f20040602/index.html|text=Phil Tippett: Hands-On Effects}}</ref> one of which was the basis for the [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]] that appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III Revenge of the Sith]] and the [[HAVw A5 Juggernaut]] in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]],<ref name="DB" /> and the tanks themselves were to be portrayed by remote-controlled vehicles. However, [[Dennis Muren]] and Joe Johnston convinced [[George Lucas]] to use stop motion instead,<ref name="Tippett" /> which eventually led to the abandonment of the tank design.<ref name="DB" /> In the DVD commentary to ''The Empire Strikes Back'', George Lucas explains that he got the idea of the AT-AT from the massive tripods from [[Wikipedia:H.G. Wells|H.G. Wells]]' 1898 novel [[Wikipedia:The War of the Worlds|''The War of the Worlds'']].<ref name="Empire Strikes Back DVD commentary">{{Film|V}} DVD commentary</ref> Though it is popularly believed that dock machinery was the inspiration for the AT-AT, [[Phil Tippett]] has said otherwise, recalling that it was based on the prehistoric mammal [[Wikipedia:Paraceratherium|''Paraceratherium'']].<ref name="Tippett2">{{WebCite|url=http://www.wired.com/2012/05/phil-tippett-feature|text=35 Years After ''Star Wars'', Effects Whiz Phil Tippett Is Slowly Crafting a Mad God|author=Watercutter, Angela|date=2012-05-24|work=http://www.wired.com|archivedate=20160617001538}}</ref>

The AT-ATs featured in ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' are different models from the ones in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Original trilogy]], inspired by the early concept art drawn by [[Ralph McQuarrie]] during the production of ''The Empire Strikes Back''.<ref name="ROTOR Trivia">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/relics-of-the-old-republic-trivia-gallery|text="Relics of the Old Republic" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> Another model of the AT-AT, the [[AT-ATQ]], was featured in the non-canon ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' short "[[The Song of Four Wings]]."<ref name="Four Wings">{{Visions|The Song of Four Wings}}</ref>

''Rebels'' creator [[Dave Filoni]] purposefully let the series' characters pronounce their name as both "ATAT", prononced as one word, and as "A-T-A-T", with each letter of the acronym pronounced individually, or also simply call them "walkers". He said that he did this for both simplicity and to be less technically and to also make the galaxy sound more linguistically diverse and realistic.<ref name="Filoni-SWCE2016">{{SWYouTube|QYWHjT1n5so|Dave Filoni Interview &#124; Star Wars Celebration Europe 2016}}</ref>
