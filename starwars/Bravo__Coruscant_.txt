'''Bravo''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] that served as the [[pilot]] of the [[Stinger Mantis crew|''Stinger Mantis'' crew]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. He also served as a [[soldier]] and fought alongside [[Saw Gerrera]].

==Biography==
During their time together, Bravo and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] ran many successful missions together in their [[Stinger Mantis crew's crusade against the Empire|struggle]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. 

Bravo assisted Kestis and the ''Stinger Mantis'' crew to infiltrate [[Level 2046]] on [[Coruscant]] to steal classified [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] information aboard the [[yacht]] of [[Senator]] [[Daho Sejan]]. After capturing the [[Pau'an]] senator, the team tried to escape Coruscant, but were intercepted by the [[Ninth Sister]] of the [[Inquisitorius]], who [[Death|executed]] the senator for harboring Kestis. The crew's slicer [[Gabs]], along with the [[Klatooinian]] twins [[Koob]] and [[Lizz]], were killed during the ensuing chaos. 

After Kestis bested the Ninth Sister, he reunited with [[Bode Akuna]] and Bravo. Together, they planned to escape Coruscant using [[Z-95 Headhunter]]s and the ''Mantis'', split up, and later rendezvous using [[locator beacon]]s. Bravo, however, was shot down by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE Fighters]] during the escape, and the ''Mantis'' received damage to its gyro systems.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Personality and traits==
Bravo was a trusted ally of Kestis during their [[Stinger Mantis crew's crusade against the Empire|crusade]] against the Empire. Kestis considered him to be a close friend and kept a photograph of Bravo, alongside himself, Gabs, Koob, and Lizz onboard the ''Mantis''.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bravo first appeared in the [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'', which was developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]]. In the game he was portrayed and voiced by [[Russell Richardson]].<ref name="Survivor" />
