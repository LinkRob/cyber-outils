'''Creshik wood''' was a type of [[Color|golden]]-colored [[Plant|wood]] from the [[planet]] [[Tralus]]. [[Coronet Arms]] imported the wood from Tralus to produce the hand-carved furniture and stock of its [[Mark V "Sand Panther" hunting rifle]], a product available during the [[Galactic Civil War]] that was as much art piece as it was functional weapon.<ref name="G&G" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], creshik wood was mentioned in the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="G&G" /> The [[Creshik wood/Legends|wood]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in ''[[Suns of Fortune]]'', a [[2014]] Fantasy Flight Games supplement for ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]''.<ref name="SoF">''[[Suns of Fortune]]''</ref>
