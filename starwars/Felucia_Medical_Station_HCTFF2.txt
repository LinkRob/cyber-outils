The '''Felucia Medical Station HCTFF2''' was a [[Haven-class medical station|''Haven''-class medical station]] that served as a [[medcenter]] for the [[Galactic Republic]] and was assigned to the [[12th Sector Army]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[21 BBY]], the [[space station]], situated in [[orbit]] of the [[planet]] [[Felucia]], was [[Destruction of the Felucia medical station|destroyed]] by a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval]] force. In response to the attack, the Republic dispatched [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], as well as Skywalker's [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], to confirm the station's destruction.

==Description==
As a [[Haven-class medical station|''Haven''-class medical station]], constructed and remodified by [[VenteX Construction Yards]]<ref name="VenteX" /> as a [[medcenter]] for the [[Galactic Republic]], the Felucia Medical Station HCTFF2<ref name="BH" /> spanned 651.48 [[meter]]s.<ref name="Size" /> Equipped with several [[bacta tank]]s, the station was capable of carrying and revitalizing<ref name="Collapse" /> at least 60,000 wounded [[clone trooper]]s,<ref name="Complement" /> while also carrying supplies for emergencies. Situated in the [[Thanium sector]]<ref name="Collapse" /> of both the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="BH" /> and [[The Slice|Slice]] regions,<ref name="The Slice" /> the station was located in [[orbit]] of the [[planet]] [[Felucia]] in its [[Felucia system|eponymous star system]],<ref name="BH" /> at grid square R-6 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Coordinates" />

==History==
{{Quote|I guess we know what happened to the medical station.|Obi-Wan Kenobi, as the Jedi shuttle comes under attack by Separatist vulture droids|<ref name="BH" />}}
At some point, the Felucia Medical Station HCTFF2 was constructed by VenteX Construction Yards<ref name="VenteX" /> for usage as a medical station by the Galactic Republic, during the Clone Wars against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Established sometime later, above the planet Felucia, which had become heavily contested by the conflict due to its strategic proximity to the [[Perlemian Trade Route]],<ref name="BMF 21">{{BuildFalconCite|21|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> a major [[super-hyperroute]].<ref name="BMF 29">{{BuildFalconCite|29|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> Alongside several other ''Haven''-class medical stations, the medical station was utilized by the [[Republic Military]]'s [[Sector Army|Sector Armies]],<ref name="BMF 89">{{BuildFalconCite|89|Starship Fact File}}</ref> being assigned<ref name="BMF 21" /> to the [[12th Sector Army]],<ref name="Note" /> whose military campaign against the Confederacy had enveloped Felucia.<ref name="BMF 21" />

[[File:FeluciaMedicalStationDestroyed-BH.png|thumb|left|300px|The Felucia Medical Station HCTFF2 is destroyed by the Confederacy navy.]]
Later, in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> sometime after the Republic's [[First Battle of Felucia|defeat]] on Felucia by the Separatists, the Confederacy targeted the medical station. Attacking and destroying it with a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval force]], consisting of a lone [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] accompanied by two [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]], the Separatists then proceeded to position an [[Automated vulture droid deployment station|automated deployment station]] for [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class droid starfighters]] in Felucia's orbit. Afterward, hearing of the medical station's disappearance, the Republic dispatched [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], as well as Skywalker's [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], to investigate. The [[Realspace|space]] station was confirmed to have been destroyed when Tano was unable to detect it on the [[scanner]]s of the Jedi's [[T-6 shuttle]].<ref name="BH" />

While investigating, the Jedi trio discovered the deployment station and crashed on Felucia's surface as their [[shuttle]] was shot down by vulture [[Droid starfighter|droids]]. Afterward, the trio&mdash;despite their need to report the station's destruction to the Republic&mdash;became involved in a [[Skirmish on Felucia|skirmish]] on the planet's surface against the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] and his [[Ohnaka Gang|gang]]. The Jedi were later able to return to the Republic, reporting their discoveries to the government.<ref name="BH" />

==Commanders and crew==
The Felucia Medical Station HCTFF2 was operated by a small crew of [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s who oversaw the space station's operations.<ref name="Collapse" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Felucia Medical Station HCTFF2 was introduced in the opening newsreel of "[[Bounty Hunters (episode)|Bounty Hunters]],"<ref name="BH" /> the seventeenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which aired on [[April 2]], [[2010]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode217.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Bounty Hunters}}</ref> It was later identified in ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' product series.<ref name="Collapse" />
