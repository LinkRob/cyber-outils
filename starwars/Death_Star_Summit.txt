The '''Death Star Summit''', as designated in the [[Imperial Archives]], was a high-level assembly of the [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military]], convened in a [[Death Star conference room|conference chamber]] aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] shortly prior to the [[Destruction of Alderaan|obliteration]] of [[Alderaan]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date" />
During this pivotal summit, [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] formally announced the [[Dissolution of the Imperial Senate|dissolution]] of the [[Imperial Senate]], signaling the [[Galactic Empire]]'s shift to direct rule through military might. The meeting was notably marred by a confrontation between [[Admiral]] [[Conan Antonio Motti]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], which escalated when Vader used [[the Force]] to [[Force choke|strangle]] Motti, only relenting at Tarkin's request.<ref name="Episode IV" />
By the time [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] published his account, ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', sometime after the [[Battle of Exegol]] in [[35 ABY]], few traces of the summit remained. Official documents euphemistically cited a "difference of strategic opinion" between Motti and Vader, and only two purported images of the event survived. Additionally, a heavily redacted incident report filed by Motti was archived, offering scant insight into the altercation.<ref name="Rise" />
