The '''104th Battalion''', also known as the "'''Wolf Pack'''" '''Battalion''', or the '''Wolfpack Battalion''', was a [[clone trooper]] [[Republic Military|military]] [[battalion]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. The 104th was led by [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]] throughout the [[Clone Wars]]. A subunit of the battalion was the similarly nicknamed [[Wolfpack]] [[squad]].

==Description==
[[File:104thOfficerBattlefrontII.png|thumb|left|180px|The 104th Battalion customized their armor with special unit markings and grey-black patterns.]]
Before the end of the [[Malevolence Campaign|''Malevolence'' Campaign]], [[clone trooper]]s of 104th [[Battalion]]&mdash;a subdivision of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]&mdash;could be distinguished by the [[Color|maroon]] markings on their [[Phase I clone trooper armor]]. With so many of the battalion's [[Cloning|clones]] lost during the [[Battle of Abregado]],<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> clone [[trooper]]s of the battalion later changed to a color scheme of flint grey<ref name="Holocron Heist" /><ref name="R2CH">{{TCW|R2 Come Home}}</ref> or blue-grey as a way to honor those lost at [[Abregado system|Abregado]].<ref name="Sideshow">{{SideshowCite|set=Sixth Scale Figures|pack=Wolfpack Clone Trooper (104th)|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/Wolfpack-Clone-Trooper-104th-Star-Wars-Sixth-Scale.asp}}</ref> One subunit of the 104th was the elite [[Wolfpack]]<ref name="HC 73">{{HelmetCollectionCite|73|Databank A-Z}}</ref> [[squad]].<ref name="Hope">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref>

The [[commanding officer]] of the battalion was [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]],<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> who also flew under the [[callsign]] [[Wolf Leader]].<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" /> The second-in-command was [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]].<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> Members of the battalion included [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Sinker]], [[Clone Corporal]] [[Comet (clone trooper)|Comet]],<ref name="Holocron Heist" /> clone troopers [[Boost]],<ref name="R2CH" /> [[Wildfire (clone trooper)|Wildfire]],<ref name="Tyrants">{{SWIA|set=Tyrants of Lothal}}</ref> and [[Umbra]],<ref name="Legends">''[[Legends of the Alliance]]''</ref> and [[Clone trooper pilot|clone pilots]] [[Warthog]]<ref name="Holocron Heist" /> and [[Corvis]].<ref name="Battle Tales 2" /> The ranks of the 104th Battalion consisted of standard clone troopers, [[clone trooper officer]]s,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> clone pilots,<ref name="Holocron Heist" /> [[clone sharpshooter]]s, and [[heavy weapons clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==History==
===Mission to Quarmendy===
[[File:WolfpackFightsOnNexus-TCWBattleTales2.png|thumb|right|180px|The 104th Battalion's Wolfpack squad fought on the Nexus during the early days of the Clone Wars.]]
Early on in the [[Clone Wars]], [[General]] Koon sent [[Commander]] Wolffe and three Wolfpack troopers, Boost, [[Sergeant]] Sinker, and [[Corporal]] Comet on a [[Mission to Quarmendy|mission]] to the [[Nexus]], a floating [[city]] and trading outpost located on the [[planet]] [[Quarmendy]], famous for its elaborate defense systems, which was captured by [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]] led by [[Emir]] [[Wat Tambor]].<ref name="Battle Tales 2" />

Using [[JT-12 jetpack]]s and [[grappling hook]]s attached to their [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s, the [[squad]] scaled the wall, while being shot at by the city's defense system and Tambor's [[D1-series aerial battle droid]]s. The clones were able to reach the top of the wall without suffering a single casualty.<ref name="Battle Tales 2">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 2|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 2]]</ref>

However, when Tambor's forces were breached by their incursion, he destroyed the city as to not allow it to be controlled by the [[Galactic Republic]]. Before the destruction, the clones were able to rescue a hostage, the city's [[Custodian of the Nexus|custodian]], [[Orkle]]. Despite the loss, Koon showed gratitude to Wolffe for making sure all of them survived and for rescuing Orkle.<ref name="Battle Tales 2" /> At least one member of the battalion also took part in the [[mission on Krystar]], helping to free captured clones from a [[Unidentified compound|secret compound]].<ref name="Battle Tales 3">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 3|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 3]]</ref> Clone troopers [[Wildfire (clone trooper)|Wildfire]] and [[Umbra]] also served in the 104th. At some point, their squad was left stranded on the [[planet]] [[Felucia]] for a [[Standard month|month]], during which time they ate [[insect]]s to survive.<ref name="Legends" />

===Siege of Hisseen===
[[File:WolffeLosesContact-TCWBattleTales2.png|thumb|left|218px|Clone Commander Wolffe lost contact with Jedi General Plo Koon during the siege of Hisseen.]]
Sometime later after the missions to Quarmendy<ref name="Battle Tales 2" /> and Krystar,<ref name="Battle Tales 3" /> Wolffe, Koon, and the Wolfpack were sent as part of the Republic's [[siege of Hisseen]] alongside [[Jedi]] Generals [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and their clone commanders, Clone Commander [[Cody]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]].<ref name="Battle Tales 1">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 1|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 1]]</ref>

When the Jedi were drawn away from the battle to confront [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and his [[Sith apprentice]] [[Asajj Ventress]], Koon attempted to contact Wolffe, but his communication was obstructed by the mountain range. After Wolffe only heard garbled instructions about the [[Hisseen's parliament|Hisseenian parliament]], Koon instead sent coordinates to Boost, and Wolffe deduced that they were being sent to rescue the parliament members. Leading the charge,<ref name="Battle Tales 2" /> the Wolfpack attacked the droids while again fighting side-by-side with members of the 212th. They were able to successfully rescue the parliament members.<ref name="Battle Tales 3" />

However, as soon as they arrived back, they were ambushed by Count Dooku's forces, and were about to be taken [[prisoner]], when Jedi Generals Plo Koon, Obi-Wan Kenobi, and Anakin Skywalker arrived and rescued the clones. The Republic was able to win the siege of [[Hisseen]].<ref name="Battle Tales 5">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 5|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 5]]</ref>

===Rising ''Malevolence''===
{{Quote|We're picking up a large energy reading from the target, sir.''"<br />"''Open fire!''"<br />"''We're not in range yet, sir.|Wolffe and Plo Koon|<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />}}
[[File:Sinker-TCWCEJtB.png|thumb|right|200px|Sinker was a member of the Wolfpack part of the 104th Battalion.]]
As numerous [[Republic Navy|Republic fleets]] were destroyed due to a [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]], the ''[[Malevolence]]'', Jedi General Plo Koon aboard the ''[[Triumphant]]'' and his [[Plo Koon's fleet|fleet]] of [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] were sent to hunt down the then-unknown [[superweapon]]. The 104th Battalion managed to track the [[Starship|ship]] to the [[Abregado system]], where they discovered it was, in fact, a ''Subjugator''-class [[heavy cruiser]] with an [[ion cannon]].<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

Before the [[fleet]] could call for reinforcements, they lost contact with the Republic. Plo Koon ordered Clone Commander Wolffe to open fire at the ''Malevolence'', but Wolffe informed him that they were out of range. However, the ''Malevolence'' had already fired its ion [[cannon]] which then hit the fleet. With no power, the fleet was now vulnerable to be destroyed, and the ''Malevolence'' opened fire with its [[laser cannon]]s, destroying the entire fleet. Koon ordered his [[troop]]s to get to the [[Republic escape pod]]s before their ships were obliterated.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

When the fleet was destroyed, the ''Malevolence'' launched a [[Droch-class boarding ship|''Droch''-class boarding ship]], also known as a hunter, to search and destroy the remaining [[escape pod]]s. At the same [[time]], Anakin Skywalker and [[Ahsoka Tano]], searching for survivors, arrived in the [[freighter]] ''[[Twilight]]''. Meanwhile, the hunter had found Plo Koon's escape pod and went to take them out. Due to Koon's [[antiox breath mask]], and the [[oxygen]] system built into the clone troopers' [[Clone trooper armor|armor]], Koon, Clone Sergeant Sinker, and Boost managed to get out of the escape pod and into [[Realspace|space]] while Commander Wolffe kept the pod's signal alive. Koon and his [[troop]]s fought off the [[B1-series rocket battle droid]]s that were carried in the hunter and were then rescued by Skywalker and Tano. They managed to get away from the ''Malevolence'' and reported their discovery to the [[Jedi High Council]].<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> Koon would later help [[Shadow Squadron]] severely damage the Separatist [[superweapon]] during the [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula]]<ref name="Shadow">{{TCW|Shadow of Malevolence|Shadow of ''Malevolence''}}</ref> and was present to watch as the ''Malevolence'' was finally destroyed.<ref name="Destroy Malevolence">{{TCW|Destroy Malevolence|Destroy ''Malevolence''}}</ref>

===Battle of Khorm===
Sometime later, the 104th Battalion's ranks were replenished with new troopers, and the battalion participated in the [[Battle of Khorm]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> where they fought alongside Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="Star Wars: Force Collection">{{ForceCollection|Clone Commander Wolffe|star=4}}</ref> During the fighting, Wolffe was permanently scarred when Asajj Ventress cut his right [[eye]], leaving a long scar across the right side of his face.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> He later had a silver [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] replacement installed.<ref name="Holocron Heist" /> Despite his injury, Wolffe continued to serve and lead his troops.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

===First Battle of Felucia===
[[File:Warthog-CTRaF.png|thumb|left|180px|Warthog flew for the 104th Battalion during the Battle of Felucia.]]
Following the Battle of [[Khorm]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> the Wolfpack and Koon were deployed on the ''[[Negotiator]]'', a ''Venator-class'' [[Star Destroyer]], to break the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederacy navy's]] [[blockade]] of the planet [[Felucia]], and participated in the First Battle of Felucia. The Republic forces, led by Jedi Generals [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and Anakin Skywalker, and [[Jedi Commander]] Ahsoka Tano, had been overwhelmed by the [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]], so Koon led a group of [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] [[gunship]]s and [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s through the blockade to rescue Skywalker and his troops, while Wolffe oversaw the space battle against the Separatist [[battleship]]s and [[starfighter]]s. Wolffe directed the [[clone gunner]]s to target and destroy the Confederate [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]]. Meanwhile, Koon and the rest of the Wolfpack extracted Skywalker, Tano, Kenobi, and the clone soldiers and brought them to safety aboard the ''Negotiator''. They then returned to [[Coruscant]], having completed the rescue mission.<ref name="Holocron Heist" />

At some point after the First Battle of Felucia, where the maroon-colored markings had still been part of 104th armor,<ref name="Holocron Heist" /> the legion shifted to a grey-based coloration in memory of those lost in the Battle of Abregado.<ref name="Sideshow" /> Following Grievous's [[Capture of Eeth Koth|capture]] of the Jedi [[Eeth Koth]], Wolffe discovered a coded message from Koth that told him that Grievous was heading to the planet [[Saleucami]].<ref name="Grievous">{{TCW|Grievous Intrigue}}</ref>

===Rescue on Vanqor===
{{Quote|It's Anakin's droid, R2-D2.''"<br />"''Well, R2-D2, transmit the message you so obviously need to communicate.|Ahsoka Tano and Plo Koon|<ref name="R2CH" />}}
[[File:PlosBros-R2ComeHome.png|thumb|right|250px|The Wolfpack traveled on gunships to Vanqor to rescue Jedi Generals Mace Windu and Anakin Skywalker.]]
When an attempt on the life of [[Mace Windu]] by a young [[Boba Fett]] failed, the ''Venator''-class Star [[Destroyer]] ''[[Endurance]]'' was forced to crash over the planet [[Vanqor]]. However, [[R2-D2]] managed to escape from Vanqor and came to the Jedi Council and showed them the hologram of Skywalker and Windu inside the wrecked ''Endurance''. Plo Koon, Ahsoka Tano, and the Wolfpack were dispatched to the planet to retrieve them. They came down in [[space gunship]]s to get Skywalker and Windu out of the destroyed [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]]. While Koon and Tano used [[the Force]] to hold the collapsing bridge steady, Wolffe and Comet jumped in and got Windu and Skywalker onto the gunship.<ref name="R2CH" />

===Citadel rescue===
{{Quote|I believe you have worn out your welcome!|Plo Koon, to the rescue team on Lola Sayu|<ref name="Citadel Rescue">{{TCW|Citadel Rescue}}</ref>}}
[[File:Plo_Koon_and_wolfpack_members.png|thumb|left|250px|A Republic rescue team was rescued on Lola Sayu by Plo Koon's 104th Battalion.]]
When it was confirmed to the Jedi that Kenobi and Skywalker could not get off [[Lola Sayu]] with [[Even Piell]] and his men, [[Saesee Tiin]], Plo Koon, [[Adi Gallia]], and [[Kit Fisto]] went to the [[Citadel|prison]] planet to extract them. They gave Kenobi and the rescue team the coordinates to a small island that would act as an evac-zone. As the rescue team made its way to the evac-zone, a fleet of four Republic [[cruiser]]s [[Battle of Lola Sayu|engaged]] the Separatist blockade. General Koon and Commander Wolffe went down with a gunship escorted by Fisto, Gallia, and Tiin in their [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Jedi starfighters]]. When the gunship reached the ground, they managed to get all the survivors onto the gunship and got back to the fleet.<ref name="Citadel Rescue" />

===Return to Felucia===
{{Quote|Artillery, open fire!|Plo Koon|<ref name="Padawan Lost" />}}
[[File:BattleOfFelucia-Planning.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The leaders of the 104th and the 501st coordinated their attack strategy upon the return of Republic forces to Felucia.]]
During the [[Second Battle of Felucia]] the 104th Battalion, along with the [[501st Legion]], returned to Felucia to make a night assault on one of General Grievous' outposts in the system. Anakin Skywalker and Clone [[Captain]] Rex were leading a diversion on the front gate while Ahsoka Tano, Comet, Sinker, and Boost scaled the back wall. Plo Koon, Wolffe, and their team used [[jetpack]]s to fly over the sidewall and surprise attack the enemy inside. After the battle, they realized Tano was missing. The rest of the night they spent searching for her, until the next morning when they left the [[Star system|system]].<ref name="Padawan Lost" />

===Mercy mission on Aleen===
{{Quote|Look! Here's how it is: my orders are to deliver supplies and re-establish power and communication systems. Report back to the transport the moment that the R2 unit has completed the download.|Wolffe, to C-3PO|<ref name="Mercy Mission">{{TCW|Mercy Mission (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:Warthog-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Plo's Bros'' gunships brought the Wolfpack Aleen where they conducted a mission of mercy to help the Aleena.]]
[[Aleen]] was a planet hit by natural [[Groundquake|earthquakes]] which had killed many civilians and left their cities in ruins. The 104th Battalion was dispatched to help them rebuild under orders of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] to give them both [[Foodstuff|food]] and medical supplies. The [[astromech droid]] R2-D2 went with them to help reestablish power, and the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] went with them to help the clones communicate with the native [[Aleena]]. During their mission C-3PO and R2-D2 disappeared underground, so Commander Wolffe was unable to communicate with the Aleena without C-3PO to help translate. The two droids discovered the source of the earthquakes. The [[tree]]-like [[alien]] [[species]] [[Kindalo]], who lived in the subterranean [[cave]]s beneath the surface of Aleen, had been angered because the surface-dwelling Aleena had dug too deep and disturbed them. The two droids were then able to negotiate with them, and came to the surface in time to regroup with the 104th as they left the planet.<ref name="Mercy Mission" />

===Rescuing Adi Gallia===
After regrouping with Plo Koon's fleet, the 104th Battalion was deployed to [[Rescue of Adi Gallia|free Adi Gallia]] from General Grievous' [[dreadnought]] following Gallia's capture in the [[Battle of Patitite Pattuna]]. Koon and his forces successfully boarded the dreadnought and liberated Gallia, though Grievous fled the battle.<ref name="Nomad Droids">{{TCW|Nomad Droids}}</ref>

===Battle of Kadavo===
[[File:PloKoonLeadingHisBros-EscapeFromKadavo.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The 104th was part of a Republic strike force that rescued a group of Togruta slaves as well several Jedi and clone officers.]]
In a mission to rescue [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Togruta]] from [[Zygerrian]] [[enslaver]]s, Jedi General Obi-Wan Kenobi and Clone Captain Rex were captured. Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano launched a [[Battle of Kadavo|rescue operation]] on the planet [[Kadavo]], which was where the slaves were being transferred. Arriving in the [[YV-865 Aurore-class freighter|YV-865 ''Aurore''-class freighter]] ''[[Tecora]]'', the two Jedi called for reinforcements, in the form of the Wolfpack. As part of a [[Plo Koon's second fleet|fleet]] led by Plo Koon and [[Admiral]] [[Barton Coburn]], members of the Wolfpack launched a strike force that contained consisted of LAAT/i gunships and [[clone Z-95 starfighter]]s. The force attempted to break through, but they could not, on account of the [[ray shield]] over the facility. Skywalker, however, was able to destroy the ray shield while Kenobi and Rex rebelled and killed the Zygerrian [[Keeper]] [[Argus]]. The strike force then successfully extracted Obi-Wan, Anakin, Ahsoka, Rex, and the slaves, and then destroyed the facility.<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" />

===Investigation on Oba Diah's moon===
{{Quote|The hull shows substantial scoring and wear. Whenever it crashed, it wasn't recently. General Plo, the scans check out. It's a T-6 shuttle, all right, serial number 775519.|Wolffe, to Plo Koon|<ref name="The Lost One" />}}
[[File:PloKoon775519-TLO.png|thumb|left|300px|Koon and the 104th recovered the missing lightsaber of the late Jedi Master Sifo-Dyas.]]
When the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] received a [[distress signal]] from the [[775519|Jedi T-6 shuttle 775519]], Koon, Wolffe, and the 104th Battalion were sent to the [[Oba Diah moon|moon]] of [[Oba Diah]] to investigate. The 104th Battalion landed on the surface and discovered the [[T-6 shuttle]]'s crash site. Inside, Koon and Wolffe discovered a [[Sifo-Dyas's lightsaber|lightsaber]] that had belonged to [[Jedi Master]] [[Sifo-Dyas]]. Koon ordered the [[shuttle]] to be taken back to Coruscant.<ref name="The Lost One" />

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|Hey, Wolffe. Can you believe it?''"<br />"''What?''"<br />"''The wolves! You got your Wolfpack back!''"<br />"''Yeah. They fight just like the boys.|Gregor and Wolffe, referring to the Loth-wolves|audio=WolfPackBack-AFH.flac|<ref name="Fool's Hope">{{Rebels|A Fool's Hope}}</ref>}}
[[File:Wolffe_commanding_AT-TE.png|thumb|right|250px|Wolffe continued to wear parts of his 104th clone trooper armor years after the Clone Wars ended.]]
The Clone Wars ended shortly after [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] revealed himself as the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious and issued [[Order 66]],<ref name="E3">{{Film|III}}</ref> which forced the clone troopers to turn on and kill their Jedi [[officer]]s via [[Behavioral modification biochip|control chips]] implanted in their [[brain]]s.<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref> Wolffe had his chip removed at some point,<ref name="TLC">{{Rebels|The Lost Commanders}}</ref> and by [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Rebels date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[The Lost Commanders]]" to [[4 BBY]].</ref> he was living in a [[Rex's AT-TE|converted]] [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] on the planet [[Seelos]] with Captain Rex and the former [[clone commando]] [[Gregor]], who had also removed their chips, when the [[Spectres]] [[Assault on Seelos|arrived]], having been sent in search of Rex by Ahsoka Tano.<ref name="TLC" />

Rex left with the Spectres to join the rebellion,<ref name="ROTOR">{{Rebels|Relics of the Old Republic}}</ref> but Wolffe and Gregor stayed on Seelos in their new home, [[Joopa Base]].<ref name="Family Reunion">{{Rebels|Family Reunion – and Farewell}}</ref> Trooper Wildfire had also found his way into the ranks of the Rebellion and learned that Woffle was one of their allies, resulting in him joining the [[mission to Seelos]].<ref name="Tyrants" /> A few [[Standard year|years]] after Rex joined, Gregor and Wolffe were recruited to assist in the [[Liberation of Lothal|liberation]] of the planet [[Lothal]] from the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Fool's Hope" /> During the operation, Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] recruited the assistance of Lothal's native [[Loth-wolf|Loth-wolves]], creatures with a strong connection to the Force. After Gregor quipped that Wolffe had his "wolfpack" back, Wolffe admitted that the creatures fought much like the "boys" of his old unit.<ref name="Family Reunion" />
