In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the enforcer of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] launched the '''first attack''' on the [[Son-tuul Pride]] on the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Son-tuul]]. Imperial forces tracked an [[Twi'lek]] [[Unidentified Twi'lek informant|smuggler]] to the Pride's den, storming the base with stormtroopers. Although the Pride's leader, a [[Yonak's father|Rodian]], released a [[Rodian Reek-like creature|large horned beast]] on the Imperial forces, he was nonetheless killed with the rest of the group. In the aftermath, stormtroopers opened a vault to discover a hoard of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] that had been amassed over many [[Standard year|years]], which Vader ordered them to confiscate.<ref name="Darth Vader 7" />

After, Vader met with the Rodian's [[Hutt]] competitor [[Sutha]], who served [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]]. Vader and Sutha agreed upon a mutually beneficial relationship between the Empire and the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]], with the Empire eliminating competitors like the Son-tuul Pride to open up new markets in return for the Hutts supplying the Empire.<ref name="Darth Vader 7" />
