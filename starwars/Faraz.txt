'''Faraz''' was a [[Houk]] [[technician]] and an old friend of [[Zia Zaldor Zanna]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The Jedi and the Thief" />

{{Char-stub}}
==History==
Faraz lived on [[Tenoo]] when [[Zia Zaldor Zanna]] was a [[Jedi]] [[Youngling]], and the two eventually became good friends. Zanna would often run off to fight pirates during their time together, and one time broke his speeder. Years later, when Zanna became a [[Jedi Master]], she walked past with the Youngling [[Kai Brightstar]], when Faraz greeted her and gave her a hug. Zanna asked for a [[Cryo-spanner]], when Brightstar asked about their history together. Faraz reminisced on her past time with him, when Brightstar noticed their shuttle being stolen. Zanna asked to borrow Faraz's [[speeder]], which he agreed to but pleaded to bring it back in one piece.<ref name="The Jedi and the Thief" />
