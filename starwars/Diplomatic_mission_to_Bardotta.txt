A '''diplomatic mission to Bardotta'''<ref name="TheJediGeneral">{{InsiderCite|163|The Jedi General}}</ref> was launched during the [[Clone Wars]] when the [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] and [[Gungan]] [[Politics|politician]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] were dispatched to the [[planet]] [[Bardotta]]. There, they investigated the mysterious disappearance of the people of [[Queen]] [[Julia]],<ref name="Disappeared 1" /> eventually determining that [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] was responsible for the disappearances.<ref name="Disappeared 2" /> The mission demonstrated both Windu's wisdom and patience and<ref name="TheJediGeneral" /> the surprising<ref name="Disappeared 1" /> political cunning and capabilities of Binks.<ref name="TheJediGeneral" />
