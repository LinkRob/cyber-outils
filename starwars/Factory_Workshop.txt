The '''Factory Workshop''' was an Imperial workhouse that was located on a [[Unidentified planet (Screecher's Reach)|planet]]. The building was [[Color|dark gray]] with yellow strips of light and guarded by multiple [[droid]]s. It had multiple floors, including a room with rows of [[bunk]] beds for [[Youngling|child]] workers, a floor where workers dumped black rocks into a crucible, and a room where workers moved [[metal]] cubes out of a machine and onto a conveyor belt. Above the rooms was a walkway where workers in protective [[Clothing|uniforms]] turned wheels on steam pipes.

At one point, one of the workers&mdash;a [[human]] named [[Daal]]&mdash;caused a pipe to burst. She then stormed out of the workhouse and was followed by two of her friends, [[Baython]] and [[Quinn]]. Baython asked Daal what she was doing, to which she explained that she was leaving the workhouse. Quinn became excited that they were leaving. After Baython told Daal how long the walk would be, Quinn suggested [[Thief|stealing]] some [[speeder]]s. Another fellow worker named [[Keena]] approached the trio and asked what was happening, and Baython invited her to join them. The group then stole [[Repulsorcraft|speeders]] from the workhouse and left it behind.

==Description==
{{Quote|Why walk when we could steal some speeders?|Quinn suggesting the group steals speeders from the workhouse|<ref name="Screecher" />}}
[[File:StackedBunks-ScreechersReach.png|thumb|left|300px|The many workers slept in long rows of stacked bunk beds on a lower floor]]
The Factory Workshop<ref name="ConceptGuide">{{SW|url=screechers-reach-concept-art-gallery|text="Screecher's Reach" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref> was an Imperial<ref name="Meet">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-screechers-reach-interview|text=Meet the Visionaries: Paul Young on the Irish Folklore-Inspired Tale of "Screecher's Reach"}}</ref> workhouse located on a [[Color|gray]] mucky field on<ref name="Screecher" /> a [[Unidentified planet (Screecher's Reach)|planet]].<ref name="Meet" /> The building was guarded by many hovering [[droid]]s with [[spotlight]]s. The outside of the building was dark gray with yellow strips of lights lining the sides of the walls. Near the building, multiple [[speeder]]s were chained together. Further away from the building, a few workers used three-[[Limb|legged]] machines to walk around the muck. The workhouse released black smoke from pipes leading to the interior.<ref name="Screecher" />

Inside, the building had multiple floors<ref name="Screecher" /> where workers faced challenging conditions.<ref name="StoryGuide">{{SW|url=screechers-reach-story-gallery|text="Screecher's Reach" Story Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> A lower floor contained many rows of multi-layered stacked [[bunk]] beds where many<ref name="Screecher" /> [[Youngling|child]] workers<ref name="Focus">{{VisionsFocus|Screecher's Reach}}</ref> [[Sleep|slept]], some beds were used by more than one being. When it was time to work, a droid woke the workers up. The floor above had tracks where mine carts containing black rocks were pushed by workers to be dumped into a crucible, at least one droid guarded this floor. The next floor contained machines that dispensed glowing [[metal]] cubes that workers removed with tongs and placed on slow moving conveyor belts. The room also contained pillars with buttons connected to the floor above that were pressed by a hovering droid. Overhead, walkways above these rooms had steam [[pipe]]s with wheels that were turned by workers wearing protective black [[Clothing|suit]]s. Throughout the workhouse were many steam vents.<ref name="Screecher" />

==History==
{{Quote|What if you didn't have to go back to the workhouse? The farms. All of this. If you could just keep going, would you?|Daal, asking Baython what he would do if he could leave|<ref name="Screecher" />}}
[[File:OutsideTheFactory-ScreechersReach.png|thumb|right|300px|The four children stand outside of the factory]]
At some point, a young [[human]] worker named [[Daal]] was turning a wheel connected to the steam pipes too quickly, causing steam to erupt from the pipe and cause a red button to flash in the floor below as a hovering droid frantically pressed it. Aggravated, the young human stormed out of the workhouse<ref name="Screecher" /> for a breath of fresh [[Oxygen|air]]. In the quiet of the outdoors, she [[dream]]t of escaping.<ref name="StoryGuide" /> Followed by her friends [[Baython]] and [[Quinn]], Baython asked Daal what she was doing.<ref name="Screecher" />

She told her friends that she was leaving the workhouse, to which Quinn celebrated. Daal explained that she was headed to [[Screecher's Reach]], a [[mountain]] that was believed to be haunted by a [[Screecher (Sith)|ghost]]. Baython told Daal that the journey to the mountain was a three [[Standard day|day]] walk, and Quinn pulled out a device and suggested they could [[Thief|steal]] some [[Repulsorcraft|speeders]] instead. A younger worker, [[Keena]], asked the trio what they were doing and Baython invited her to join them in leaving. She accepted and the group stole three speeders and left the workhouse behind, speeding through fields and knocking over a worker in a machine. Daal and Baython later reflected on what they could do if they left the workhouse forever.<ref name="Screecher" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Development===
{{Quote|So we start off in a workhouse. There was workhouses in Ireland which were not a pleasant part of our history, where lots of people were made to work in very poor conditions.|Director Paul Young, describing the inspiration for the Factory Workshop|<ref name="Focus" />}}
[[File:Cartoon_Saloon_2018_logo.png|thumb|left|250px|The team at Cartoon Saloon designed the Factory Workshop to create a feeling of oppression.]]
The Factory Workshop appeared in "[[Screecher's Reach (short film)|Screecher's Reach]]," a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series directed by [[Paul Young]]. The animated short, produced by [[Cartoon Saloon]], was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Screecher" /> on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Release Date, Studios Revealed}}</ref> Prior to its appearance, the workhouse was first mentioned in an article on [[StarWars.com]] recapping the reveals at the ''Star Wars: Visions'' panel featured at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]] on [[April 10]] of the same year.<ref name="1stm">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-visions|text=SWCE 2023: ''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Trailer, Key Art, and Cast Revealed}}</ref>

The workhouse was identified as the Factory Workshop in the short's episode guide posted on StarWars.com. The episode guide also confirmed that concept art for the workhouse was created by [[Leo Weiss]], [[Maria Pareja]], [[Almu Redondo]],<ref name="ConceptGuide" /> and [[Federico Pirovano]]. Pirovano designed the workshop as an orphanage, and created multiple possible designs for the factory before one was settled on.<ref name="PirovanoWebsite">{{WebCite|url=https://federicopirovano.com/star-wars-visions-season-2|text=Star Wars Visions Season 2 &mdash; Federico Pirovano|work=[https://federicopirovano.com federicopirovano.com]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/3AoTY}}</ref> Weiss created multiple pieces of concept art showcasing the interior and exterior of the workshop, including shots of scrapped scenes.<ref name="WeissWebsite">{{WebCite|url=https://leo-weiss.com/screechers-reach|text=Screecher's Reach &mdash; Leo Weiss|work=[https://leo-weiss.com Leo Weiss]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/g1WsJ}}</ref>

Inspiration for the workshop came from the [[Wikipedia:Workhouse|workhouses]] in [[Wikipedia:Ireland|Ireland]], which were considered an unpleasant part of the country's history by Paul Young. According to art director Almu Redondo, in creating the workhouse the team at Cartoon Saloon attempted to create a huge feeling of oppression. Revealing the kids as workers in the workshop were compared by Redondo to a little colony of ants working in a huge machine city.<ref name="Focus" />
