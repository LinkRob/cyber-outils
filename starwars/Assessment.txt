"'''Assessment'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] published in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 11|eleventh]] issue of the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]] series by [[Egmont]] UK Ltd. It was written by [[Martin Fisher]], illustrated by [[Ingo Römling]] and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. The comic was released on [[November 4]], [[2015]].

==Plot summary==
[[Commandant]] [[Cumberlayne Aresko]] and [[Taskmaster]] [[Myles Grint]] are awaiting the arrival of a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]]  carrying [[Assessor (title)|Assessor]] [[Potalla]] and her aide [[Dunum]], who have been sent to evaluate their leadership of the [[Academy for Young Imperials]] on [[Lothal]]. After reviewing the myriad rebel activity at the academy, Potalla informs them that their leadership is under review. Aresko and Grint plot their response to the appraisal while Potalla questions [[Imperial cadet|Cadet]] [[Zare Leonis]]. 

Grint and Dunum are discussing Dunum's teaching suggestions, with Grint inferring that Dunum is attempting to take their place at the academy's head when an alert of a rebel intrusion into the academy interrupts them. Grint and Dunum meet up with Aresko, who orders the intrusion contained, while Dunum slips off on his own. 

[[Sabine Wren]] and [[C1-10P|Chopper]] have broken into the Academy using a [[Imperial Military Code|code]] via the main door. However, they set off the alarm having failed to take out all the [[Stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] guarding the entrance. They engage more stormtroopers as they make their way through the facility until they get to the [[Turbolift|turbolifts]]. The stormtroopers await their arrival and begin firing as the doors are supposed to open, but upon investigation, only find an armed [[grenade]] awaiting them. Wren and Chopper having escaped through the hatch at the top of the car using Chopper's [[rocket booster]]<nowiki/>s arrive at their predetermined point, the [[armory]]. The two steal supplies and then begin placing [[Explosive charge|explosive charges]]. They are come upon by Dunum, who demands their surrender. However, Dunum is [[Stun charge|stunned]] by an unknown assailant, which gives Wren time to finish placing the explosives while Chopper cuts a hole through the wall. They call for pickup and escape via a [[landspeeder]] piloted by [[Garazeb Orrelios]].

Meanwhile, Arestos arrives at the armory with stormtroopers who help the stunned Dunum leave the room before the charges explode. Potalla confronts Aresko, demanding answers as to how the intrusion happened. After an investigation by Aresko and Grint, it was found that a personal access code assigned to Dunum had been used to access the main entrance. Dunum swore his innocence, but was taken into custody. The Assessor took her leave, informing Aresko that someone else would deliver her report.

Aresko and Grint believed she was fooled by their treachery, having programmed a [[probe droid]] to stun Dunum while creating a lapse in security that allowed the rebels to access his personal access code. Potalla in fact had not been fooled and gave a negative report of the academy's leadership to her superior, [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], forcing him to change his plans and visit Lothal earlier than he expected.
