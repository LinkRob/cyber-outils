'''Borleias''' was an astronomical object located in the [[Borleias system]] of the [[Colonies]] region. Borleias sat at grid square K-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
Borleias was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], [[Borleias/Legends|Borleias]] first appeared in ''[[X-Wing: Rogue Squadron]]'', a [[1996]] novel authored by [[Michael A. Stackpole]] as the first installment of the [[Star Wars: X-Wing (novel series)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']] novel series.<ref name="Rogue Squadron">''[[X-Wing: Rogue Squadron]]''</ref>
