'''''Bounty Hunters'' 17''' is the seventeenth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]'', and is part of the ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'' event. It was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]], [[Arif Prianto]], [[Edgar Delgado]], and [[Jesus Aburtov]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 3]], [[2021]].

==Publisher's summary==
''"LAST STAND" Severely wounded, VALANCE must make a desperate escape attempt from an Imperial Star Destroyer. Will T'ONGA and her crew reach him in time? Plus: A shocking twist that will leave the survivors' lives forever altered!''
