The '''First Secretary''' was the title of the leader of the [[Combined Bothan Clans|Bothan Council]]. Also called the [[Chief Councilor]], it was the highest post in [[Bothan/Legends|Bothan]] society. The office was once held by [[Desark Fey'lya]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]],<ref>''[[The Clone Wars: The Wind Raiders of Taloraan]]''</ref> and later by future [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] [[President/Legends|President]] [[Borsk Fey'lya]]. During the [[Caamas Document]] crisis, [[Ceok Orou'cya]] served as First Secretary.
