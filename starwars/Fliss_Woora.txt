'''Fliss Woora''' was a [[Togruta]] [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Night of Sorrow]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Woora was in the [[Dalnan system]] when they received a [[Distress signal|message]] from Jedi Master [[Creighton Sun]] for help with a [[Night of Sorrow|situation]] with the [[Path of the Open Hand]] on [[Dalna]]. In response, she traveled to Dalna on a [[transport]] and landed in the [[city]] [[West Platt]] and gathered around one hundred [[Dalnan]]s who were willing to assist the [[Jedi]]. Afterwards, they all traveled to the [[Path compound]], and after departing from the transport, Woora bowed to Sun and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Aida Forte]] and introduced herself.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
