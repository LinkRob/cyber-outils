'''Aerosol inhalers''' were a type of inhaler consisting of a thin white tube with a [[Color|golden]] ring and protrusion part way along its length. The end of the tube was also golden and angled up with an opening at the tip.<ref name="TLJVD" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], which includes the aerosol inhaler's appearance, to the same [[Standard year|year]] as the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[Moldwarp]] [[Edmo Ectacle]]<ref name="Datapad">{{DatapadCite|Flight Crews Wanted|FlightCrewsWanted-IDIntro.png|EdmoEctacle-GEDatapad.jpg}}</ref> made used of an aersol inhaler while in the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Casino]] in [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> [[Gender|He]] was also using the inhaler in the image used for his identity profile when the profile was stolen by the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]].<ref name="Datapad" />

==Behind the scenes==
An aerosol inhaler first appeared in the [[2018]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ" /> and was first identified in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released alongside the film.<ref name="TLJVD" /> The inhaler was also included in concept art of Edmo Ectacle titled "Cantina Aliens 37A", which was produced by creature concept designer [[Jake Lunt Davies]] for the film.<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]''</ref>
