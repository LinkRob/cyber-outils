The '''''Bellator''-class dreadnought''' was a class of [[Imperial Navy]] [[dreadnought]] that was manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]] in limited numbers during the [[Imperial Era]], when the [[Galactic Empire]] experimented with creating larger models of [[battlecruiser]]s between [[19 BBY]] and [[3 BBY]]. Such experimentation also resulted in the creation of the [[Assertor-class Star Dreadnought|''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought]], and both dreadnoughts were considered to be in the same family of dreadnoughts as the [[Mandator-class Dreadnaught|''Mandator''-class Dreadnought]].

==Characteristics==
The ''Bellator''-class [[dreadnought]] was a class of large-scale dreadnought that was in service to the [[Galactic Empire]] during its [[Imperial Era|reign]]<ref name="Lead by Example" /> and manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]].<ref name="Timelines" /> The dreadnought was smaller than the [[Assertor-class Star Dreadnought|''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought]], which, in terms of size and power in the [[Imperial Navy]], was second only to the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]]. The ''Assertor''-class and ''Bellator''-class belonged to the same family of Imperial Navy dreadnoughts as the [[Mandator-class Dreadnaught|''Mandator''-class]].<ref name="Lead by Example" />

The command structure of the ''Bellator''-class was similar to that of the smaller [[Surveyor-class reconnaissance frigate|''Surveyor''-class reconnaissance frigate]]. The [[frigate]]'s heavily shielded broad and bunker-like command structure extended beyond the main [[Sublight engine|drives]] and rose at the aft of the [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="Lead by Example" />

==History==
Kuat Drive Yards was contracted as the exclusive [[Star Destroyer]] supplier for the Imperial Navy in [[19 BBY]], resulting in a fleet buildup during the Imperial Era. Between 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[3 BBY]], the Galactic Empire experimented with larger [[battlecruiser]] models of the Star Destroyer design family, ultimately creating ships such as the ''Assertor''-class and ''Bellator''-class dreadnoughts. Despite this, few of the large [[warship]]s were built.<ref name="Timelines" /> The ''Bellator''-class dreadnought was active at the same [[time]] as the ''Mandator''-class as well as when the ''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought underwent construction at some point prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Lead by Example" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Bellator-class_Star_Battlecruiser.jpg|thumb|left|300px|A ''Bellator''-class dreadnought, as it appeared in ''Star Wars'' Legends]]
The ''Bellator''-class dreadnought was introduced in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]''.<ref name="Lead by Example" /> The [[Bellator-class dreadnought/Legends|''Bellator''-class dreadnought]] originally appeared, albeit unnamed, in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Dark Empire 2|second issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Empire]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]],<ref name="Dark Empire 2">[[Dark Empire 2|''Dark Empire'' 2]]</ref> released on [[February 18]], [[1992]]<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54058/star_wars_dark_empire_1991_2|text=Star Wars: Dark Empire (1991) #2}}</ref> and drawn by [[Cam Kennedy]].<ref name="Dark Empire 2" />
