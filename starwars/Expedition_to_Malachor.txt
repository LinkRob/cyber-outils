Sometime before the [[Battle of Coruscant]], an '''expedition''' was sent to the [[planet]] of [[Malachor]] at the behest of [[Darth Sidious]], a [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] of the [[Sith]] whose public persona was [[Supreme Chancellor]] Sheev Palpatine of the [[Galactic Republic]]. An [[Sith artifact|artifact]] known as the [[Darth Sidious' Sith Chalice|Sith Chalice]] was retrieved on Malachor and brought to Sidious.<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
The expedition that brought back the Sith Chalice was first [[Canon|canonically]] mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="CL" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] version of the book, published in [[2005]], the Chalice was uncovered on [[Yavin 4]] rather than Malachor, during the [[Wolhanian expedition]].<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
