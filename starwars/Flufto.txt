{{Quote|''Sixty'' shrink frozen tookas. Query: Are they ''all'' explosive?''"<br />"''Naturally. In my experience, Rexa, not many problems you can't fix with a cuddle or a crater.|Rexa Go and Chelli Lona Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 16">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 16|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 16]]</ref>}}
'''Flufto''' was the nickname of a batch of [[Cloning|cloned]] and customized [[tooka]]s owned by the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]]. Aphra cloned over sixty of the creatures in a [[laboratory]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Son-tuul]]. Each clone was modified with bio explosives in its [[gene]]s and exploded in response to the codephrase "Snugglebum Oogiewoogie."

Aphra kept the tookas shrink frozen in a crate, keeping her own Flufto on her during her [[Mission to Skako Minor (Son-tuul Pride)|mission]] to the planet [[Skako Minor]]. Her first tooka was detonated to wipe out an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[squad]] that surrounded [[Aphra's crew]]. Another was then taken out, but eventually used as a projectile explosive on the [[cyborg]] creature known as a [[chthonic worm god]].

When Aphra's crew visited the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] flight school, the ''[[Lucrehulk Prime]]'', her Flufto was allowed to run into the school's control core so that she could chase it inside. The rotation [[general]], [[Hera Syndulla]], shot the tooka [[Death|dead]]. After [[Capture of Hera Syndulla|capturing]] Syndulla, Aphra and her team [[Infiltration of Hivebase-1|infiltrated]] the Imperial complex [[Hivebase-1]], where the rogue archaeologist allowed her remaining tookas to defrost and escape, spreading out through the base before they were detonated thanks to some name changing in the firing registry.

==Biography==
===Mass produced===
{{Quote|No, I ''don't'' know why the doctor has a hairy outgrowth. If you want to dissect it, you'll have to ask yourself.''"<br />"''I&hellip;I was testing a relic splicer rig, that's all. C-cloned a ''tooka''. I had one as a pet when I was little.''"<br />"''There, you see? ''Origins!'' Though I expected rather ''more'' when I gave you that lab.''"<br />"''It's ''customized''.|0-0-0 and Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 15" />}}
Around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]]<ref name="Aphra 15" /> [[Chelli Lona Aphra]]<ref name="Aphra 3">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 3|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 3]]</ref> was forced to work for the [[Son-tuul Pride]] syndicate and its most recent leader, the [[assassin droid]] [[0-0-0]]. The [[droid]] granted Aphra her own [[laboratory]] on<ref name="Aphra 15" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]]<ref name="Vader 7">[[Darth Vader (2015) 7|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 7]]</ref> [[Son-tuul]], where she used an old [[splicer rig]] to [[Cloning|clone]] one of<ref name="Aphra 15" /> the [[feline]] creatures<ref name="Tooka DB">{{Databank|tooka|Tooka}}</ref> known as [[tooka]]s, the doctor having kept one of the creatures as a [[Aphra family's tooka|pet]] when she was [[Youngling|younger]]. The new tooka was modified with<ref name="Aphra 15" /> bio explosives in his [[gene]]s<ref name="Aphra 17">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 17|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 17]]</ref> so that the feline could explode in response to the codephrase "Snugglebum Oogiewoogie,"<ref name="Aphra 15" /> and at least sixty copies of him were made and shrink frozen in a crate.<ref name="Aphra 16" />

[[File:AphraCarriesTheCatBomb-2016DoctorAphra15.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Chelli Lona Aphra carries Flufto while speaking with 0-0-0.]]
Aphra carried one of the tookas around with her, naming him Flufto. When 0-0-0 questioned what she was carrying, calling him a hairy outgrowth, the rogue archaeologist mentioned her work with the tooka cloning. The doctor was then introduced to a new [[Aphra's crew|crew]] of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] for her new mission to find the [[assassin]] droid's lost memories, being required to choose a crewmember not to bring with her. [[Rexa Go]]&mdash;0-0-0's [[cyborg]] muscle and the only other mercenary beside Aphra to know the true target of the mission&mdash;suggested leaving behind Flufto, but Aphra raised her [[blaster]] at the cyborg, telling Go that she would [[Death|die]] if she touched the tooka. Ultimately, the [[sword]]sman [[Rabael Dir Glorio]] was left behind while Aphra and her crew [[Mission to Skako Minor (Son-tuul Pride)|voyaged]] to their first destination, the planet [[Skako Minor]].<ref name="Aphra 15" />

===Huggable bomb===
{{Quote|Flufto doesn't deserve this. Won't you please look after him? He's an innocent. He didn't ask to be spliced.''"<br />"''&hellip;sssssss&hellip; I suppose if I can't liberate my people, I can at least bring them a meal&hellip;|Aphra hands Flufto to Hallio Bas|<ref name="Aphra 16" />}}
On Skako Minor, Aphra's crew was guided to the location of<ref name="Aphra 15" /> the [[Techno Union]] [[foreman]]<ref name="Wat Tambor DB">{{Databank|wat-tambor|Wat Tambor}}</ref> [[Wat Tambor]]'s [[Wat Tambor's workshop|workshop]] by the [[Skakoan]] guide [[Hallio Bas]]. However, the guide was working with the [[Galactic Empire]], and an Imperial [[squad]] laid a trap for Aphra's crew. After the lead [[Imperial officer]] ordered the deaths of the mercenaries, Aphra gave Flufto to Bas, asking that he look after the tooka. The Skakoan accepted him, believing the feline would be a worthy meal for his people. At this moment Aphra recited Flufto's detonation codephrase, causing an explosion that wiped out the Imperial squad. In the wake of the fireball, Go confirmed with the rogue archaeologist that Flufto had been a customized tooka.<ref name="Aphra 15" />

[[File:BombReplacement-2016DoctorAphra16.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Aphra brings out a new Flufto from the crate.]]
Go put down her crate for Aphra to retrieve one of the shrink frozen tookas, a new Flufto, other crew members having assumed the crate's contents were [[alcohol]]. The crew began exploring Tambor's workshop but were attacked by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] led by [[Lieutenant-Inspector]] [[Magna Tolvan]]. The skirmish activated the cyborg creature known as a [[chthonic worm god]], which attacked both parties. In the chaos, Aphra, carrying Flufto, found herself stuck with Tolvan, and she made the Imperial throw her tooka at the worm god while she shouted the codephrase to make him a projectile [[bomb]]. While the explosive tooka attack did not defeat the creature,<ref name="Aphra 16" /> it<ref name="Aphra 15" /> was ultimately killed by Aphra's crew, who looted Tambor's workshop before departing.<ref name="Aphra 16" />

===Another one bites the dust===
{{Quote|Ugh&mdash;that damn ''tooka.'' What kind of ''spacer'' keeps a pet naturally attracted to ''high-energy'' fields, I ask you?|The mercenary [[Sister Six]] pretends to be surprised at Flufto getting loose|<ref name="Aphra 17" />}}
[[File:FluftoOnTheLoose-2016DoctorAphra17.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Flufto was allowed loose by Aphra to get into the ''Lucrehulk Prime''{{'s}} control core.]]
Aphra took another Flufto out of the crate before her crew disembarked at<ref name="Aphra 17" /> the ''[[Lucrehulk Prime]]'',<ref name="Aphra 21">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 21|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 21]]</ref> a [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]] used as a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] flight school. Aphra, carrying Flufto, and her crew were given a tour of the school by the [[Alliance officer]] [[Bini]], who thought they were recruits. However, the rogue archaeologist actually intended to kidnap the ''Lucrehulk Prime''{{'s}} rotation [[general]], [[Hera Syndulla]], as part of her plan to infiltrate the Empire's [[Tarkin Initiative]] research and development complex [[Hivebase-1]],<ref name="Aphra 17" /> where the doctor had learned 0-0-0's lost memories were stored.<ref name="Aphra 16" />

During Bini's tour, Aphra allowed Flufto to get loose and run into the ''Lucrehulk Prime''{{'s}} control core, being attracted to the high energy field. Although the core was access restricted, the rogue archaeologist used her tooka's rogue behavior as an excuse to run inside after him, much to Bini's distress. The [[weapon]]s [[Alarm system|alarm]] also set off due to Flufto's bio explosive induced genes. Aphra climbed and<ref name="Aphra 17" /> pretended to try and grab the feline while she bugged the control core's systems with her [[electro-tattoo]]s.<ref name="Aphra 19">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 19|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 19]]</ref> That was when Syndulla, who had been using the control core to remotely control some of the flight school's training [[X-wing starfighter]]s, shot Flufto dead.<ref name="Aphra 17" />

===Destruction by explosive cats===
{{Quote|Hey&mdash;doc? Don't you want to bring one of your ''exploding tookas?"<br />"''Nah, they're attracted to ''energy sources,'' remember? Dumb things'd just make a mess.|Caysin Bog and Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 18">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 18|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 18]]</ref>}}
Aphra and her crew were ultimately successful in [[Capture of Hera Syndulla|capturing]] Syndulla and used their possession of the rebel leader to get close to Hivebase-1 while allowing the ''Lucrehulk Prime'' to follow them to the complex as a distraction as they [[Infiltration of Hivebase-1|infiltrated]] the base. After covertly landing in the [[hangar]], Aphra's crew began searching for Hivebase-1's [[Datacore|database]]. The cyborg mercenary [[Caysin Bog]] asked whether the rogue archaeologist wanted to bring another Flufto, but Aphra turned the option down, claiming that the tookas' attraction to energy sources might cause a mess.<ref name="Aphra 18" /> Secretly, that was Aphra's exact intention,<ref name="Aphra 19" /> as she hit the crate before she left, allowing the lid to open slightly<ref name="Aphra 18" /> and affect the shrink-freezing of the remaining tookas.<ref name="Aphra 19" />

[[File:FluftoInvasion-2016DoctorAphra19.jpg|thumb|left|350px|The tookas roam Hivebase-1.]]
While Aphra and her crew made it to Hivebase-1's datacore,<ref name="Aphra 18" /> the tookas defrosted and escaped from the crate, running free through the Tarkin Initiative complex. Several of the felines found the idle [[Dek-Nil]], a droid from Aphra's crew, who startled the creatures by springing back to life. Dek-Nil went on to use a dataport to change the names in the base's firing registry. As the tookas continued to spread out through Hivebase-1, the base's [[Commander]] [[Yewl]] ordered for its weapons to fire on ''Lucrehulk Prime'', which Aphra had used, through her bug, to rip and steal the complex's datacore using a [[tractor beam]]. However, the Imperial [[officer]] giving the orders read the tampered firing registry, which included "Snugglebum Oogiewoogie," over the [[Comm unit|comms]], detonating all of the tookas simultaneously and destroying Hivebase-1.<ref name="Aphra 19" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Say, did you ''know'' tookas are attracted to ''high energy fields?'' At least&mdash;they are if you ''customize'' 'em that way.|Aphra, to Hera Syndulla|<ref name="Aphra 19" />}}
[[File:SnugglebumOogiewoogie-2016DoctorAphra15.png|thumb|right|250px|Aphra detonates Flufto.]]
Flufto and each clone had [[Color|black]] skin and purple [[Hair|fur]]. A clone's face was white with black [[eye]]s encased in purple eye masks, and the feline was customized with bio explosives in his genes.<ref name="Aphra 17" /> When activated for detonation, Flufto clones' eye masks gave a red glow.<ref name="Aphra 15" /> The tooka clones were also customized to be attracted to energy sources<ref name="Aphra 19" /> and fields.<ref name="Aphra 17" />
