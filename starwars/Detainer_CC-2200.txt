The '''Detainer CC-2200''' was a model of [[cruiser]] and [[Interdictor]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] used a CC-2200 to try and capture the stolen [[corvette]] ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' in the [[Obroa-skai system]]. The attempt was unsuccessful, and resulted in the [[MC80 Star Cruiser|Mon Calamari star cruiser]] ''[[Stellar Vista]]'' crashing into the CC-2200.

==Description==
The Detainer CC-2200 was a model of [[Interdictor]] [[cruiser]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy]]. Like the newer-model [[CC-7700 frigate]], the Detainer CC-2200 had a downsloping bow and stubby wing like projections, each of which housed four [[gravity well projector]]s which could be used to [[Interdiction field|pull]] [[Starship|vessels]] from [[hyperspace]]. The cruisers were thickly [[armor]]ed and had downsloping bows.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==History==
{{Quote|The requested Interdictors are being developed as part of the Deep Core Security Zone, my lord.|[[Kren Blista-Vanee]] informs [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] about Tarkin's plans to use the interdictors|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> a Detainer CC-2200 was being developed for use by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Deep Core Security Zone]]. The vehicle was requisitioned by [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] for use in a [[Carrion Spike task force|task force]] that aimed to [[Incident in the Obroa-skai system|capture]] the [[corvette]] ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', which had been stolen from Tarkin by a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]]. Tarkin planned to use the CC-2200, along with a newer-model [[CC-7700 frigate]] and a newly developed and untested [[Immobilizer 418 cruiser]], to pull the ''Carrion Spike'' from hyperspace in the [[Obroa-skai system]], which the Imperials estimated the corvette would pass through based on a [[homing beacon]] they had on the ship.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Once the three cruisers activated their interdiction fields the Immobilizer's [[overcurrent resistor]]s failed to prevent the cruiser's gravitic systems from redlining, creating a field that was far stronger than necessary. Ships began to be pulled from hyperspace, and those already in the system were dragged toward the interdictors. The field was momentarily turned off, but then reactivated, pulling the large [[MC80 Star Cruiser|Mon Calamari star cruiser]] ''[[Stellar Vista]]'' from hyperspace. Out of control, the larger vessel broadsided the CC-2200, before spinning to impale its dorsal surface on the interdictor's bow. An explosion on the [[Mon Calamari]] vessel then knocked the CC-2200 ninety degrees out of position. The interdictors failed to capture the ''Carrion Spike'' and its crew, who had become aware of the homing beacon and transferred it to a [[YT-1000 light freighter|YT-1000 freighter]] named the ''[[Reticent]]'', which the Imperials did capture.<ref name="Tarkin" />
