The '''''Arkanian Dawn''''' was a [[freighter]] owned by the [[enslaver]] [[Krastic Tarta]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="SG" /> By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The entry mentioning the ''Arkanian Dawn'' in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' takes place chronologically prior to an entry written by [[Tobias Beckett]]. ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' depicts Beckett's [[death]] on [[Savareen]] and ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on Savareen in the film to nine [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the proclamation to [[19 BBY]], meaning Beckett's death must take place in [[10 BBY]], and so his entry in the guide and those chronologically before it must all be set prior to or during 10 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> Tarta had the [[Starship|vessel]] respond to a distress call from a [[privateer]]'s starship that had crashed landed on an [[Unidentified planet (Purple Ones)|unnamed planet]]. Although Tarta found the crashed vessel to be completely abandoned, the slaver discovered a native [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] he named the [[Purple Ones]]. The slaver then wrote about these events in the [[book]] known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', which he had discovered on board a [[Bloody Bones|second crashed ship]] and decided to keep safe on the ''Arkanian Dawn''.<ref name="SG" />

After capturing seven villages of the natives, Tarta left the [[planet]] on the ''Arkanian Dawn'' with the ship's holds entirely full of [[Slavery|slaves]] which he intended to sell at the [[Cyklo market|slave markets]] of the planet [[Chalcedon]]. However, upon landing on Chalcedon, his slaves revolted against him, [[Death|killing]] Tarta and seizing the ''Arkanian Dawn''.<ref name="SG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Arkanian Dawn'' was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="SG" /> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was created for the [[1993]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' supplement ''[[Galaxy Guide 9: Fragments from the Rim]]'', which was written by [[Simon Smith]] and [[Eric S. Trautmann]], with illustrations by [[Mike Vilardi]].<ref name="GG9">''[[Galaxy Guide 9: Fragments from the Rim]]''</ref>
