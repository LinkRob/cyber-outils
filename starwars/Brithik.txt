'''Brithik''' was a [[Katikoot]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Aubadas]].<ref name="QFTHC" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for the Hidden City]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Silandra Sho]]'s [[Republic Pathfinder]] [[Master Sho's Pathfinder team|team]] traveled to Aubadas to investigate what happened to [[Rok Buran]] and his [[Outer Rim Expedition Two-Five-Two|Pathfinder team]]. Sho and her [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] were confronted by Brithik and other Katikoot, but the conflict was resolved peacefully when [[Mittik]] explained the situation.<ref name="QFTHC" />
