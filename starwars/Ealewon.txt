'''Ealewon''' was a company that produced the [[A-212 Tracing Console]], a model of [[computer]] available during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="SM" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ealewon was mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Special Modifications]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="SM" /> The company originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Galladinium's Fantastic Technology]]'', a [[1995]] roleplaying supplement for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="GFT">''[[Galladinium's Fantastic Technology]]''</ref>
