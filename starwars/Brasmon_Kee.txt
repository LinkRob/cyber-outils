'''Brasmon Kee''' was an [[Abednedo]] [[Gender|male]] who served as [[senator]] for his [[homeworld]] [[Abednedo (planet)|Abednedo]] in the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Senate|Senate]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' establishes the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], including the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]], take place in the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]].</ref> Kee was present on the [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]] while it hosted the Senate,<ref name="TFAVD" /> and was [[Death|killed]] when the all of the planets in the [[Hosnian system]] were [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroyed]] by the [[First Order]]'s [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> Kee had [[Color|pink]] skin and gray [[hair]], and while on Hosnian Prime wore a brown patterned [[Clothing|robe]] with the [[Turban|Regent's turban]].<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brasmon Kee was created to appear in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']],<ref name="TFAVD" /> which was released on [[December 18]], [[2015]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> The senator is not actually visible in the final release of the film,<ref name="Episode VII" /> but appeared in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TFAVD" /> and released the same day as the film.<ref name="New Books">{{SW|url=news/a-galaxy-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens-books-coming-december-18-first-look|text=A Galaxy of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Books Coming December 18 - UPDATED!}}</ref>

Like all members of the Abednedo species named by Hidalgo in the ''Visual Dictionary'', the character was named in reference to a song by the [[Wikipedia:Beastie Boys|Beastie Boys]], as director of ''The Force Awakens'' [[J.J. Abrams]] is a big fan of the group. Kee's name is a reference to the song "Brass Monkey".<ref name="Insider 165">{{InsiderCite|165|50 Things to Notice in Star Wars: The Force Awakens|50 Things to Notice in ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''}}</ref>
