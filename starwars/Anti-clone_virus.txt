'''Anti-clone viruses''' were hypothetical [[disease]]s that the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] was rumored to be [[Biological warfare|developing]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. When [[clone trooper]] [[Tup]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Death|murdered]] [[Jedi General]] [[Tiplar]] during the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]], his apparent lack of memory of the incident baffled the [[Jedi]] and [[Cloning|clones]], causing them to speculate that it was possible that his actions could be because of a virus, a toxin, or a type of sickness. Jedi [[General]] [[Tiplee]] stated that an anti-clone virus could be a possibility.<ref name="Unknown" /> In truth, Tup's actions were caused by his malfunctioning [[Behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chip]], an organic device implanted inside all of the clone [[trooper]]s to ensure their compliance with [[Death penalty|executing]] the Jedi during [[Order 66]].<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref>
