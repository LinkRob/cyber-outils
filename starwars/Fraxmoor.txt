'''Fraxmoor''' was an [[alien]] individual who served as an [[officer]] within the [[Tactical Defense Force]] on the [[planet]] [[Janix]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Fraxmoor was a member of Brander Lawson's squad with [[Hendrix (human)|Hendrix]] and [[Jam Jam]].<ref name="SOM1" /> He was the only remaining member after Hendrix who was a mole working for [[Nico Deemis]] was killed by a sniper and Jam Jam dying of his injuries caused by a [[thermal detonator]] used by Hendrix. Being corrupted too, Fraxmoor tried to kill [[Brander Lawson]]. But the captain foils his murder and he offered to take his former squadmate back to the station to talk. Fraxmoor fled, however, and Lawson shot him in the back—not intending to kill him, as he wanted to learn whose orders he was following—and Fraxmoor stumbled into a street where he was struck by a driver in a speeder and killed.<ref name="SOM3">[[Shadow of Maul 3|''Shadow of Maul'' 3]]</ref>
