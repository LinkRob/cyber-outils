'''''Balance of the Force''''' is the second deluxe expansion for ''[[Star Wars: The Card Game]]''. It introduces two multiplayer formats to the game and two pre-built challenge decks, ''[[Tiaan Jerjerrod/Legends|Jerjerrod]]{{'s}} Task'' and ''The Hunt for Skywalker''. These decks let one player play against teams of two or three. This expansion also provides objective sets for all six affiliations.

It was released on [[December 13]], [[2013]].
