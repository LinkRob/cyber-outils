The '''Battle on Jedha''' was a battle that took place in [[Jedha City]], on the [[moon]] of [[Jedha]]<ref name="Rogue One" /> in [[1 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> when [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]] attacked a [[Galactic Empire]] convoy transporting [[Kyber crystal]]s<ref name="Rogue One" /> in [[Tythoni Square]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] operatives [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], [[Jyn Erso]] and [[K-2SO]], as well as two [[Guardians of the Whills]], [[Chirrut Îmwe]] and [[Baze Malbus]], also became involved in the battle against the Imperial forces. However, in the aftermath of the fight, Andor, Erso, Îmwe, and Malbus were all taken prisoner by the Partisans and brought to the [[Catacombs of Cadera]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

==The battle==
As the convoy passed by [[Tythoni Square]], a [[grenade]] was tossed from a balcony in front of the [[TX-225 GAVw "Occupier" combat assault tank|tank]]. Its detonation halted the vehicle's progress, and the Partisans revealed themselves, opening fire on the [[stormtrooper]]s, both from ground level and with snipers atop the nearby buildings. Once [[Jyn Erso]] entered the street to save a child and return it to her mother, her escort, [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], was forced to help her to safety, shooting the tank's weapon operator who had just destroyed a nearby tower with a missile. As Jyn hid in front of the stopped vehicle, Cassian noted a Partisan atop a nearby elevated bridge preparing some grenades and shot him to create a distraction&mdash;as upon his fall to the ground, the explosives detonated&mdash;to the sight of Partisan troop leader [[Benthic]]. As Jyn and Cassian ran to safety, another bomb was tossed at the stormtroopers' feet, and after being detonated, the Partisans raided the vehicle to collect the [[Kyber crystal]]s. They finished just as Imperial reinforcements, which included an [[All Terrain Scout Transport]], arrived.<ref name="Rogue One" />

As Jyn and Cassian fought their way through more troopers, Cassian's reprogrammed droid, [[K-2SO]], arrived and detonated a grenade upon a squad of charging troopers. The trio returned to the square, where the reinforcements checked the damages suffered by the tank and were mistaken for an actual Imperial droid directing prisoners. Jyn and Cassian were only saved by the intervention of two [[Guardians of the Whills]], [[Chirrut Îmwe]] and [[Baze Malbus]]. As K-2 returned to Cassian's ship, the Partisans took over the square and captured the rebels and Guardians, as Benthic felt they were hostile due to Cassian's attack. Once Jyn revealed she had businesses with [[Saw Gerrera]] for being the daughter of [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]], the Partisans put bags on their heads (which slightly annoyed Chirrut, who was blind anyway) and took them to their headquarters in the [[Catacombs of Cadera]], where Jyn was brought before Saw, while Cassian, Baze, and Chirrut were put in a cell.<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle on Jedha was created and developed for the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|film]] ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. The first footage of the battle was shown in the ''Rogue One'' teaser trailer on [[April 7]], [[2016]].<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|2BDyeARyIkw|Rogue One: A Star Wars Story Trailer Tease}}</ref>
