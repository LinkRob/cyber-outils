'''Fezzonk''' was a [[Dowutin]] member of the [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection|security crew]] led by [[Alys Ongwa]] on [[Corellia]]. When the crew was tasked with protecting [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] during a visit to Corellia, Fezzonk flew as [[Air Eleven]] in a [[Starship|craft]] above the gathering. The Dowutin shot down a [[scav droid]] that was released by the [[Nihil]] during Soh's visit.<ref name="THRA Annual 2021" />
