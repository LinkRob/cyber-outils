'''Cavin Cornwall''' is an [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] who worked as a creature performer on [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'' Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]<ref name="TFA" /> where he portrayed [[Sidon Ithano]]<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2019 Topps Star Wars: Journey to The Rise of Skywalker|cardname=Cavin Cornwall as Sidon Ithano|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/215324/cid/13667768/2019-Topps-Star-Wars-Journey-to-Star-Wars-The-Rise-of-Skywalker---Autographs-NNO-Cavin-Cornwall}}</ref> and [[Durteel Haza]].<ref name="Secrets">{{Film|VII}} - ''Secrets of the Force Awakened: A Cinematic Journey'' featurette</ref> He also worked on [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> where he portrayed [[Pemmin Brunce]].<ref name="Nick Kellington interview">{{WebCite|url=https://www.starwarsinterviews.com/sequel-trilogy/episode-viii-the-last-jedi/nick-kellington-snook-uccorfay|text=Nick Kellington - Snook Uccorfay (The Last Jedi)|author=Pellegrom, Dennis|work=[https://www.starwarsinterviews.com Star Wars Interviews]|archivedate=20180112193203|nolive=1}}</ref>
