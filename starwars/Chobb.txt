'''Chobb''' was a name used in the phrases "in the name of Chobb"<ref name="Andor">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> and "Chobb's Knob."<ref name="HttJ" /> In [[5 BBY]], [[Zorby]], proprietor of [[Zorby's Western Shiplot]] on the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]], invoked Chobb while trying to find out what was causing a commotion in the shiplot.<ref name="Andor" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="0 ABY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Heir to the Jedi]]'' to [[0 ABY]].</ref> the [[Bith]] [[Priban]] used the phrase Chobb's knob when he was attacked by a [[Fexian skullborer]].<ref name="HttJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
The name Chobb was first used in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Heir to the Jedi]]'', by [[Kevin Hearne]].<ref name="HttJ" />
