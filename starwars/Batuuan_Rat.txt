'''Batuuan Rats''' were a [[species]] of [[rat]] native to the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. Their calls could be heard from within [[Black Spire Outpost]]. Some residents of Batuu raised rats to become pets or for other reasons.<ref name="GEStaff" />

{{Creature-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Batuuan Rats were created for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at Disneyland Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]]. While not physically present in the park, sound clips of the rats' calls can be heard in certain areas.<ref name="GEStaff" />
