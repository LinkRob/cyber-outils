'''Clari-crystal''' was a substance that could be used to make windows. Some buildings on the [[ecumenopolis]] [[Coruscant]] had clari-crystal windows. The cleaning and upkeep of the windows was carried out by a small army of [[CLE-004 window cleaning droid]]s and [[INS-444]]s, who also maintained the [[planet]]'s [[glass]] and [[transparisteel]] windows.<ref name="BuildR2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clari-crystal was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2017]] [[De Agostini]] magazine [[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 38|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 38]].<ref name="BuildR2" /> The substance was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as [[clari-crystalline]] in the [[2006]] reference book ''[[The New Essential Guide to Droids]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="NEGTD">''[[The New Essential Guide to Droids]]''</ref>
