"'''Chapter 14'''" is the fourteenth episode of the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' animated television series, the fourth episode in [[Clone Wars: Season Two|Season Two]], and the fourteenth episode in [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume One|Volume One]]. Directed by [[Genndy Tartakovsky]], the episode originally aired on [[Cartoon Network]] on [[March 31]], [[2004]].

==Plot summary==
[[File:Luminara_and_Barriss_fighting.jpg|thumb|left|250px|[[Luminara Unduli/Legends|Luminara Unduli]] and [[Barriss Offee/Legends|Barriss Offee]] showing their twin-dueling abilities.]]

On the icy planet of [[Ilum/Legends|Ilum]], inside a sacred [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] site, [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Luminara Unduli/Legends|Luminara Unduli]] is supervising the ritual creation of a [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] by her [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]], [[Barriss Offee/Legends|Barriss Offee]]. Using crystals from the cave, Barriss finalizes her lightsaber, sealing the completed device up.

As Offee constructs her lightsaber, Unduli recites a ritualistic incantation which she tells her padawan that she must never forget:

<blockquote>The crystal is the heart of the blade.

The heart is the crystal of the Jedi.

The Jedi is the crystal of the Force.

The Force is the blade of the heart.</blockquote>

Unduli concludes by saying that, "All are intertwined: the crystal, the blade, the Jedi. You are one," and then she congratulates her Padawan for completing her training. Offee's exultation is short lived, however, as Luminara, through [[The Force/Legends|the Force]], senses something is amiss. Cloaked [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Separatist]] [[Spelunker probe droid/Legends|chameleon droids]] have infiltrated the cave, in an attempt to destroy a site necessary for the Jedi war effort.

Although they manage to stop the droids from entering the crystal-laden part of the cave, the Jedi precipitate a cave-in, sealing themselves inside. However, help is nearby&mdash;[[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]], near Ilum on the [[H-type Nubian yacht/Legends|royal yacht]] of [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Padmé Amidala]], feels them through the Force, and states that they must go to Ilum to save the Jedi.
