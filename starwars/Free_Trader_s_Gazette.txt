'''''Free Trader's Gazette''''' was a publication published during the [[Imperial Era]]. [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] was once featured on the cover of ''Free Trader's Gazette'' and was pictured wearing high-wasted [[Clothing|pants]]. The criminal [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] saw the magazine that bore Calrissian's image and, upon meeting the [[Baron Administrator]] in person, noted that Calrissian's pants resembled those that he had worn on the cover.<ref name="FITYMI">{{StoryCite|story=Fake It Till You Make It|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>
