The '''Emergences''' were a series of disasters that occurred in the aftermath of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], in which the [[freighter]] ''[[Legacy Run]]'' came apart in [[hyperspace]] while maneuvering to avoid a [[Nihil]] [[starship]], with numerous fragments of its wreckage entering the [[Hetzal system]] at near-[[lightspeed]]. A combined effort of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Jedi Order]] headed by [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] managed to prevent a majority of the damage at Hetzal. However, further fragments of the wreckage continued to emerge from hyperspace across the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

The [[first Emergence]] occurred in the [[Ab Dalis system]], with approximately twenty million people [[Death|killed]] when a fragment struck the [[planet]] [[Ab Dalis]]. As they continued,<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> entire civilizations were wiped out by the Emergences.<ref name="Brendok">{{Databank|brendok|Brendok}}</ref> As a result of the disasters, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] enacted closures of [[hyperspace route]]s throughout the Outer Rim. With a [[Keven Tarr's navidroid array|navidroid array]] created by [[Keven Tarr]], the Republic was able to predict the locations of future Emergences, recovering the [[Black box|flight recorder]] of the ''Legacy Run'' to determine what had caused the initial disaster.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

The [[Koboh Emergence]] slammed into the [[Shattered Moon|Moon]] of [[Koboh]], smashing a large part of it into debris. Most of this flew onto the surface of the planet, causing major destruction and hastening the Republic's ongoing efforts to abandon the planet after the [[Attack on Tanalorr]].<ref name="Star Wars Jedi: Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> The planet [[Brendok]] was hit by an Emergence,<ref name="Brendok" /> rendering it uninhabitable. By the following [[Standard century|century]], however, the planet was teeming with life, which was attributed to a [[vergence]] in [[the Force]].<ref name="Choice">{{Acolyte|Choice}}</ref> [[Bri-Phrang City]] was hit by an [[Eriadu system Emergence|Emergence]], which damaged a [[Lava reactor core|geothermal plant]] that provided power to [[Eriadu City]].<ref name="Trials">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Emergences were created for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' publishing initiative.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> They were first mentioned in the short story "[[STET!]]," written by [[Daniel José Older]] and published in the collection ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]'' in [[2020]]<ref name="STET!">{{StoryCite|story=STET!|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> and later appeared in the novel ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'', written by [[Charles Soule]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> and published on [[January 5]], [[2021]].<ref name="LOTJ-Date">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/622991/star-wars-light-of-the-jedi-the-high-republic-by-charles-soule/9780593157718|text=Star Wars: Light of the Jedi (The High Republic)}} {{C|Hardcover}}</ref> The first visual depiction of the Emergences was in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 1]], written by Older, illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]], and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="THRA 1">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 1]]</ref> on [[February 3]], [[2021]].<ref name="THRA1-Date">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-the-high-republic-adventures-1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures #1}}</ref>
