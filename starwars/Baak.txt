'''Baak''' was a [[Wookiee]] [[youngling]] that lived on [[Kashyyyk]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. They participated in the [[Great Tree Race]]<ref name="TToK" /> in [[232 BBY]].<ref name="Date">The events of "The Trees of Kashyyyk" take place during [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|season two]] of ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'', which corresponds to [[232 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:YJADate|here]].</ref> According to [[Jedi]] youngling [[Lys Solay]], they had won the past three races.<ref name="TToK" />
