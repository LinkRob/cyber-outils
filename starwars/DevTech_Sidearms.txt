'''DevTech Sidearms''' was a company that manufactured weapons including the [[Vilmarh's Revenge]] [[blaster pistol]], the weapon used by the [[Devaronian]] criminal [[Cikatro Vizago]] around five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="RVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
DevTech Sidearms was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Adam Bray]] and published in [[2014]].
