'''Caf Stop''' was a business that shared its name with the beverage [[caf]]. It had a location across the street from the [[Imperial Senate Building]] in the [[Federal District]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. In the first [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]] after its [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]]<ref name="MoF" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Imperial Intelligence]] was observing the Caf Stop opposite the Senate Building. The Imperial [[spy]] [[Haki Zeophrine]] watched a feed from within the business while flicking through various intelligence feeds on her monitor in the [[Imperial Inteilligence adjunct operations center]] on Coruscant.<ref name="MoF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Caf Stop was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', which was written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first instalement of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
