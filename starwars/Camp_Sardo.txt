'''Camp Sardo''', officially known as '''Imperial Work Settlement #121''', was a [[Wookiee]] [[Slavery|slave]] labor camp on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] run by the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Camp Sardo was located near the Wookiee [[city]] of [[Awrathakka]] and housed the enslaved former residents of the city. Wookiee slaves were used to grow [[Foodstuff|food]], breed, fight for entertainment, exploited as disposable slave labor force, and were subject to various Imperial experiments.<ref name="Life Debt" />

The camp was run by [[Commandant]] [[Theodane Sardo]] and was located at a considerable distance from [[Grand Moff]] [[Lozen Tolruck]]'s [[Lozen Tolruck's island fortress]]. A rebel team led by [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] managed to destroy the camp's [[deflector shield generator]]. With the help of the former Imperial [[loyalty officer]] [[Sinjir Rath Velus]], they managed to free the Wookiee inmates during a massive planetwide Wookiee [[Liberation of Kashyyyk|slave uprising]] that ended Imperial rule on Kashyyyk.<ref name="Life Debt" />
