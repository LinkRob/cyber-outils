"'''Astral Love'''" was a [[music]] track performed by the [[band]] [[Billi B and the Paradise Gang]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it was added to the [[jukebox]] in the [[Bestine]] [[cantina]] on the [[planet]], [[Tatooine]]. The song was also on the jukebox in [[The Broken Hoof]] [[Cantina|bar]] in [[Canto Bight]] during the same time period.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Astral Love" was created as an [[in-universe]] music track by [[Cody Matthew Johnson]] for the [[2024]] [[Ubisoft]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" /> It was released in the game's soundtrack ''[[Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)]]''.<ref name="Songs from the Underworld">{{OST|Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)}}</ref>
