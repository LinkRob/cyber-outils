'''Blue Eleven''' was the [[callsign]] assigned to the eleventh [[pilot]] of [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]].<ref name="SWROUVG" />

==History==
===Galactic Civil War===
[[Laren Joma]] and [[Taslet Colb]] served as Blue Eleven in [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]] during the [[Battle of Scarif]], [[pilot]]ing a [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]].<ref name="SWROUVG" /> During the battle, their ship was shot down after they delivered troop reinforcements on the ground.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Blue Eleven callsign made its first appearance in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'', which was published by [[Electronic Arts]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
