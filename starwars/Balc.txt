'''Balc'''<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sacrifice-trivia-gallery|text="Sacrifice" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> was a [[Sith]] [[language]]<ref name="Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sacrifice-episode-gallery|text="Sacrifice" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref> spoken during the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Sacrifice" /> The [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] once used an incantation spoken in the Balc tongue<ref name="Trivia Gallery" /> and the [[Force-bond]] between [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]] and [[Yoda]]<ref name="Sacrifice" /> to gain the [[Force power|power]] of [[dark illusion]],<ref name="Episode Gallery" /> hoping to break Yoda's will while the [[Jedi Master]] was on the Sith [[homeworld]] of [[Moraband]].<ref name="Sacrifice" />
