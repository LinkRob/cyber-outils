'''Fo Skoolan''' was a [[Mon Calamari]] individual who held the title of [[captain]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Navy]]. At some point, they [[Commanding officer|commanded]] the modified [[MC80 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Home One]]'' and led their forces during a raid on the [[warship]]. However, the assailants destroyed the [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s and [[X-wing starfighter]]s and entered the Mon Calamari [[cruiser]], [[Death|killing]] several [[Rebel trooper]]s and eventually reaching Skoolan's position, the ship's briefing room, where more [[soldier]]s defended them while they stayed in the "splash zone," a [[water]] container.<ref name="TSS" />

Following the Rebel soldiers' defeat, Skoolan fought the invaders themselves, first fighting them without a firearm, and later using a [[E-11/S blaster rifle]] and later a [[Flamethrower]]. Eventually, however, the assailants defeated Skoolan and captured ''Home One''. Skoolan had [[Color|brown]] skin, wore a [[Mon Calamari fleet officer uniform]] and could use [[blaster]]s, flamethrowers and could engage in physical combat.<ref name="TSS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fo Skoolan appears in the [[Canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', released in [[2022]]. In the game, the player may find ''Home One'' in [[Endor system|Endor space]], [[Yavin system|Yavin space]] or [[Hoth system|Hoth space]], where they must destroy the various [[starfighter]]s guarding it and later, infiltrate the [[Starship|vessel]]. Following the player's arrival at the vessel, they must confront hordes of Rebel soldiers as they reach the briefing room, where they confront Skoolan. If the player defeats Skoolan, they gain the ability to purchase the vessel.<ref name="TSS" />
