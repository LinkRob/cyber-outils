'''Digrenara''' was an individual who served as [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] at some point before the [[Gender|woman]] [[Lina Soh]]<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> was [[Supreme Chancellor election, 234 BBY|elected]] Supreme Chancellor around [[234 BBY]].<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> According to the [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Rhil Dairo]], Digrenara did a lot for the Republic during their [[time]] as [[chancellor]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> Dairo [[Brain|mentally]] noted her belief that Soh, who was well loved across [[the galaxy]] and presided over the Republic during what the reporter believed could be described as the [[galaxy]]'s [[High Republic Era|golden age]], was the Supreme Chancellor who had done the most for the Republic since Digrenara.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
