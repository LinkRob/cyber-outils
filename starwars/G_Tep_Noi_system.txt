==Description==

The G'Tep'Noi system was a [[star system]] located within the intersection of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing" /> Situated in the [[Croynar sector]] at the coordinates N-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Systems" /> it contained a dense [[asteroid field]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==History==
{{Quote|Looks like the freighter's recalling the TIEs. Can they jump to hyperspace already?''"<br />"''They're leaving themselves open to attack. They ''must'' be about to clear the asteroid field and jump. It's a risk, but t's not crazy. Close in! Go!|Lieutenant [[Itina]] and Wyl Lark|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
[[File:204thLegion-TIEFighter1.png|thumb|left|300px|The 204th Imperial Fighter Wing (pictured) stopped in the G'Tep'Noi system.]]
In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] traveled to the G'Tep'Noi system while traveling between the<ref name="Victory's Price" /> Croynar sector's<ref name="Systems" /> [[Fedovoi End system|Fedovoi End]] and [[Chadawa system]]s. However, [[New Republic]] [[Barma Battle Group|forces]] led by [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]], which had been hunting the [[fighter wing]] through the sector, arrived after [[Standard hour|hours]] of scouting and guesswork. After the 204th was located in the [[asteroid]] field, Syndulla sent her [[Deliverance's starfighter wing|starfighter wing]] to [[Skirmish in the G'Tep'Noi system|pursue]] the Imperials.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

As New Republic [[starfighter]]s approached the 204th's [[flagship]], the [[bulk freighter]] ''[[Yadeez]]'', its own fighters began recalling in preparation to leave the asteroid field and escape the system. Three Imperial [[TIE series|TIEs]] remained and advanced on the New Republic starfighter wing. While two of them fired and retreated, the third stayed on course. This TIE was flown by [[Lieutenant]] [[Yrica Quell]], who had formerly served with Syndulla's forces. Quell attacked and cracked asteroids around the fighters to scatter and delay them. [[Starfighter Commander]] [[Wyl Lark]] managed to close in on the ''Yadeez'' and start firing on it when Quell declared her presence over comm and fired on her former comrade. As Lark was thrown off course, Quell returned to the ''Yadeez'', which escaped into [[hyperspace]] with its escorts. The New Republic forces ultimately pursued the 204th to the Chadawa system before losing their trail.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The G'Tep'Noi system first appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the third installment in [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
