'''Alex Sanchez''' is an artist who is currently illustrating ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic, Threat of Peace]]'' series of webcomics. He has previously done work on comics such as [[Wikipedia:Marvel Age#Marvel Age.2FMarvel Adventures continuities|''Marvel Age Hulk'']], [[Wikipedia:X-Men|''X-Men'']], and various [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]] comics.
