An '''auditor''' was a financial position held by certain individuals in the [[Galactic Empire]]. Individuals with the position were evidently greatly feared by members of the Imperial hierarchy, being described by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] as the "credential equivalent of a stink bomb."<ref name="Leia2">[[Princess Leia 2|''Princess Leia'' 2]]</ref>

At one point, Organa and [[Evaan Verlaine]] posed as Imperial auditors to gain safe access to [[Naboo]] in order to rescue a group of [[Alderaanian]] refugees that were being trafficked, with the former posing as "[[First minister|First Minister]] [[Han Solo|Solo]]."<ref name="Leia2" />
