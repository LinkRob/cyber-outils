'''Bennic''' was a [[human]] [[Republic Intelligence]] officer. During the [[Clone Wars]], Bennic assisted the independent covert [[special forces]] group [[Zero Company]],<ref name="PC Gamer 4 de" /> which was tasked by [[Unidentified Republic Intelligence officer|a Republic Intelligence officer]] and the dispossessed noblewoman<ref name="PC Gamer 4">{{WebCite|url=https://www.pcgamer.com/games/strategy/star-wars-zero-company-is-more-than-just-star-wars-xcom-it-feels-like-mass-effect-but-with-turn-based-tactics-and-permadeath/|text=Star Wars Zero Company is more than just 'Star Wars XCOM'&ndash;it feels like Mass Effect but with turn-based tactics and permadeath|author=Ted Litchfield|date=2026-03-26|work=[[Wikipedia:PC Gamer|''PC Gamer'']]|archivedate=20260326143803}}</ref> [[Jae Mordant]]<ref name="PC Gamer 4 de" /> to investigate [[Dark side of the Force]]-based cult aligned with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] who sought to infest [[the galaxy]] with [[Unidentified plague (Infinite Coil)|a]] [[Disease|plague]]. Bennic and Mordant served as advisors to the Zero Company's leader [[Hawks]] in the group's base of operations, the [[Den (Ring of Kafrene)|Den]] on the [[Ring of Kafrene]].<ref name="PC Gamer 4 de" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bennic will appear as a supporting character in the upcoming [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Zero Company]]'',<ref name="PC Gamer 4 de" /> set to release some time in [[2026]].<ref name="Swcom Update">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-zero-company|text=Join the Ranks of ''Star Wars'' Zero Company}}</ref> They were first mentioned by the [[Wikipedia:German language|German]] version of [[Wikipedia:PC Gamer|''PC Gamer'']] on [[March 26]] of that year.<ref name="PC Gamer 4 de" /> The article and it's main English version also mention another Republic Intelligence officer who tasked the group with their mission,<ref name="PC Gamer 4" /><ref name="PC Gamer 4 de" /> but the articles don't specify if the two are meant to be the same character.
