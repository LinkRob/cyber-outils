In [[40 BBY]], a [[Unidentified chemical|chemical]] was '''dispersed''' by [[Pijali]] [[blackguard]] [[soldier]]s on the [[Pijal's moon|moon]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]] as part of [[Princess]] [[Fanry]]'s secret [[Blackguard campaign|campaign]] to disrupt the proposed [[Governance Treaty]]'s signing, which would strip the [[Pijali monarchy]]'s power. For a few [[Standard day|days]], the chemical made the [[moon]]'s [[atmosphere]] appear [[Color|crimson red]], panicking Pijali citizens.

The [[Local day|morning]] afterward, blackguards left a message on a [[Unidentified temple (Pijal)|temple]] in [[Pijal's capital city]], asking the public whether their act of dissent was enough for them not to be forgotten. [[Royal guard (Pijal)|Royal guard]] [[captain]] [[Deren]] later gave a copy of the [[Hologram|holographic]] recording of the the chemical dispersion to [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], while the [[Jedi]] were investigating the recent [[Mission to Pijal|upheavals]] in the [[Pijal system]].

==Prelude==
In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]'s [[Mission to Pijal (Rael Averross)|serving]] [[lord]] [[regent]] [[Rael Averross]] and the [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] collaborated on the [[Governance Treaty]] initiative&mdash;which would reorganize the reigning [[Pijali monarchy]] into a relatively powerless constitutional [[monarchy]] and cede power to a [[Democracy|representative]] [[Assembly (Pijal)|Assembly]]&mdash;by using the results of a [[Pijal system]] [[Pijal census|census]] to survey the [[Homeworld|native]] population. However, the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] believed that Czerka had been exploiting her constituents by unfairly [[Slavery|enslaving]] them and did not wish to sign the treaty upon her [[crown]]ing, hoping to retain her power as future [[queen]]. Averross, meanwhile, felt that the Governance Treaty would economically benefit Pijal if the Assembly opened the [[Pijal Hyperspace Corridor]] [[hyperspace route]] in conjunction with the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="MA" />

==Ominous moon rising==
Hoping to stall the treaty's adoption, Fanry began covertly leading a contingent of Pijali [[blackguard]] [[soldier]]s to stir [[Blackguard campaign|unrest]] throughout the Pijal [[Star system|system]], assisted by her [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] [[captain]] [[Deren]]. As one of these incidents, the blackguards seeded the [[atmosphere]] of [[Pijal's moon]] with a harmless [[Unidentified chemical|chemical]]. As a result, the [[moon]]'s appearance changed from [[Color|golden-and-green]] in color to crimson red, panicking citizens who watched it rise in the [[Local day|evening]].<ref name="MA" />

==Aftermath==
[[File:JediMasterAndPadawan-TPM.png|thumb|right|300px|Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi investigated the upheavals in the Pijal system, including the chemical dispersion.]]
On the morning after the chemical dispersion, the blackguards scrawled a painted message on a [[Unidentified temple (Pijal)|temple]] in [[Pijal's capital city]], asking the public whether their act of dissent was enough for them not to be forgotten. The vandalism was blamed on the [[Opposition]]&mdash;a [[Politics|political]] performance troupe known for protesting the Governance Treaty&mdash;and Deren kept a [[Hologram|holographic]] recording of the event stored.<ref name="MA" />

Some [[Standard day|days]] later, Deren gave the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], the recording of the chemical's usage while they were on Pijal attempting to locate the Opposition and [[Mission to Pijal|investigating]] the recent upheavals in the system. Eventually, following Fanry's unsuccessful attempt to crown herself queen during her violent [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]], the Opposition was cleared of blame for the chemical usage and other incidents.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The dispersion appeared in a hologram in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
