{{Quote|Welcome, oh, great queen, to my humble establishment. I invite you to taste a delicacy of our home planet. I present... a gorg!|Bolza Grool, to the [[Unidentified Aeosian Queen|Aeosian Queen]]|<ref name="No Place Safe">{{Resistance|No Place Safe}}</ref>}}
A '''gorg stall''' owned by the [[Klatooinian]] [[gorgmonger]] [[Bolza Grool]] was a small shop at the [[Colossus marketplace|marketplace]] on the mobile aircraft refueling [[Space station|station]] ''[[Colossus]]'' on the ocean [[planet]] [[Castilon]], where Grool made a living selling [[gorg]]s as [[Foodstuff|food]]. [[Aurebesh]] signage was used to attract customers. It read "Most <u>taste</u>" – "Best <u>flavor</u>" – "<u>guaranteed</u>" and "<u>Real good</u>" – Fresh 4 you."<ref name="Recruit" /> While the station was hiding over [[Celsor 3]], the station ran low on food, and a [[jakoosk]]'s remains were sold as meat, resulting in Bolza's old gorg signs being crossed out and advertising jakoosk meat instead.<ref name="SW-Bucket's List Extra: 9 Fun Facts from Hunt on Celsor 3 - Star Wars Resistance">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-hunt-on-celsor-3-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 9 Fun Facts from "Hunt on Celsor 3" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2019-10-28|quote=Bolza's old gorg signs were crossed out -- and now advertise jakoosk meat.}}</ref>
