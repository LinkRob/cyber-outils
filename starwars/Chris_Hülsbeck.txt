'''Chris Hülsbeck''' is a composer employed by the company [[Factor 5]] who composed and directed the [[musical score]] of the [[1998]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (video game)|''Star Wars: Rogue Squadron'']].<ref name="Rogue">[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (video game)|''Star Wars: Rogue Squadron'' video game]]</ref> The original [[Rogue Squadron Main Title|main title]] of the game is reused for the subsequent installments of the [[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (series)|''Rogue Squadron'' series]].
