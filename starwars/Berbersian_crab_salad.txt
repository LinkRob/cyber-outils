The '''berbersian crab salad''' was a dish that consisted of crisp [[Naboo lettuce]] and [[butter]]ed [[crab]] sticks. It could be served with [[dindra sauce]]. In her attempt to convince the famished [[Lina Graf]] to trust her, the [[bounty hunter]] [[Shalla Mondatha|Shalla "the Shade" Mondatha]] once made her berbersian crab salad.<ref name="The Steal" />

==Behind the scenes==
Berbersian crab salad was first mentioned in the novel ''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Steal]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="The Steal" /> and published on [[June 30]], [[2016]].<ref name="Steal Amazon">{{Egmont|domain=co.uk|url=books/star-wars-the-steal/9781405279956|text=Star Wars: The Steal}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel [[Death Star (novel)|''Death Star'']], there is a mess hall dish named [[berbersian crab]].<ref name="Death Star">[[Death Star (novel)|''Death Star'']]</ref>
