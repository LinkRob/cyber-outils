'''Durteel Haza''' was a [[Delphidian]] [[Gender|male]] who spent [[time]] watching a [[fighting droid]] match in [[Takodana Castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]] in [[34 ABY]]. He looked up from the game when [[Maz Kanata]], the owner of the castle, shouted out to the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Han Solo!|Maz Kanata yells at Han Solo, catching Durteel Haza's attention|<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref>}}
[[File:Dramatic_Wollivan.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Durteel Haza spent time at Takodana Castle.]]
Durteel Haza was a [[Delphidian]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Alien Archive" /> who spent [[time]] in<ref name="TFA" /> [[Takodana Castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]]<ref name="VE" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VII}} to [[34 ABY]].</ref> He was stood at a table with fellow patrons<ref name="TFA" /> [[Wollivan]] and [[Praster Ommlen]]<ref name="VE" /> as well as several [[human]]s watching a<ref name="TFA" /> [[fighting droid]]<ref name="VE" /> match when the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] entered the room with the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], the former [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn]], and the [[Astromech droid]] [[BB-8]].<ref name="TFA" />

When [[Maz Kanata]], owner of the castle, spotted Solo, she shouted to him across the room, causing all the patrons to go silent as Haza and the others looked up swiftly from the game. Once Kanata went over to greet Solo, everyone returned to their business, but soon after many of the patrons were drawn outside to witness the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[Hosnian system]] by the [[First Order]]'s [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]]. The castle was then [[Battle of Takodana|attacked]] and destroyed by the forces of the First Order, who were in search of BB-8.<ref name="TFA" /> Haza was mentioned in an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]], which was restored, expanded, and displayed by the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="Alien Archive" /> at some point no earlier than 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Durteel Haza had [[Color|dark gray]] skin and black [[eye]]s.<ref name="VE" />

==Equipment==
While at the castle, Haza wore a black and [[copper]]-colored [[Clothing|outfit]]. He also wore a dark gray headpiece, which included a small light that could shine blue<ref name="TFA" /> or white,<ref name="VE" /> but chose to keep his face visible.<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DurteelHaza-OnSet.png|thumb|right|250px|Cavin Cornwall (right) on set as Durteel Haza.]]
Durteel Haza was portrayed by [[Cavin Cornwall]],<ref name="Secrets">{{Film|VII}} - ''Secrets of the Force Awakened: A Cinematic Journey'' featurette</ref> who also portrayed [[Sidon Ithano]],<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2019 Topps Star Wars: Journey to The Rise of Skywalker|cardname=Cavin Cornwall as Sidon Ithano|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/215324/cid/13667768/2019-Topps-Star-Wars-Journey-to-Star-Wars-The-Rise-of-Skywalker---Autographs-NNO-Cavin-Cornwall}}</ref> in the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="TFA" /> Cornwall kept Haza's makeup on underneath the mask of Ithano's costume<ref name="Secrets" /> and the characters were later identified as members of the same species.<ref name="Alien Archive" /> The character was first identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'', which was written by [[Adam Bray]], [[Cole Horton]] and [[Tricia Barr]].<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
