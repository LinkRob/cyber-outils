The '''Chain Worlds Theorem''' theorized the existence of the [[The Force|Force]] realm known as the [[World Between Worlds]]. An illustration of the Chain Worlds Theorem was included in a [[book]] that was a part of the [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]'s [[sacred Jedi texts]].

==Description==
{{Quote|This mystical realm connects all of time and space, creating a conduit between the living and the dead.|[[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] describes the World Between Worlds, which the Chain Worlds Theorem posited|<ref name="SotS">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
[[File:World_between_worlds.png|thumb|left|250px|The Jedi Ezra Bridger accessed the World Between Worlds.]]
The Chain Worlds Theorem was a theory positing the existence of the [[World Between Worlds]],<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> a mystical plane located between [[time]] and [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="WBW Databank">{{Databank|world-between-worlds|world between worlds}}</ref> An illustration of the theorem was authored by an unknown individual and was part of a [[book]] that was a part of the [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]'s collection of [[sacred Jedi texts]]. The illustration of the theorem was accompanied by [[writing]]s in an archaic [[language]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

The realm that the Chains Worlds Theorem theorized the existence of did in fact exist. The World Between Worlds was accessed by the [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]]<ref name="World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="1 Date">{{RebelsDate|412}}</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. At the site of the ruins of the [[Lothal Jedi Temple]] on the<ref name="World Between Worlds" /> [[planet]]<ref name="LJT Databank">{{Databank|lothal-jedi-temple|Lothal Jedi Temple}}</ref> [[Lothal]], Bridger [[Mission to the Lothal Jedi Temple|entered]] the realm via a [[portal]], which he opened by using the Force to interact with the [[painting of the Mortis gods]].<ref name="World Between Worlds" />

==History==
[[File:LukeSkywalkerTreeLibrary-IGN.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Knowledge on the Chain Worlds Theorem was preserved in Luke Skywalker's collection of sacred texts.]]
When Skywalker [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] himself to the planet [[Ahch-To]] following the [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destruction of his temple]] and the students of his [[Jedi Order]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> in [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the book including information on the Chain Worlds Theorem was kept in his collection of the sacred texts located in the [[tree library]] on the planet's [[Temple Island]]. An identical illustration in a different book, the ''[[Rammahgon]]'', depicted abstracted hyperspatial waypoints to the Sith<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[throneworld]]<ref name="DB Sith">{{Databank|sith|Sith}}</ref> of [[Exegol]], with an amended insert added by Skywalker connecting it to Ahch-To.<ref name="TROS VD" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VIII}} in [[34 ABY]].</ref> the Padawan [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] took the books aboard the [[starship]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="TROS VD" /> A [[Unidentified historical book|book]] describing objects relating to [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]] contained an entry on the sacred Jedi texts, including a captioned depiction of the Chain Worlds Theorem.<ref name="100 Objects" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chain Worlds Theorem was first mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
