A [[language]] known as the '''Empire's tongue''' was spoken in the feudal [[Empire (Rei'izu)|Empire]] that ruled [[the galaxy]]. It was spoken on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Genbara]] by a [[Unidentified teahouse shopkeep|shopkeep]] and by the warrior known as [[the Ronin]].<ref name="Ronin" />
