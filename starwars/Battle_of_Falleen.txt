During the [[Skytop Station campaign]] of the [[Clone Wars]] in [[22 BBY]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Galactic Republic]] engaged in a '''battle''' on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Falleen (planet)|Falleen]]. Led by [[Count]] [[Dooku]], [[General]] [[Grievous]], and [[Commander]] [[Asajj Ventress]], the [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]] emerged victorious, taking over the planet and destroying the Republic's [[Falleen battle group|battle group]] stationed there.

==Prelude==
[[Falleen (planet)|Falleen]] was a [[planet]]<ref name="Helmet Collection 21" /> in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> which, before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], joined the latter faction, supplying the Separatists with an advantage in [[The galaxy|galactic]] trade during the [[Separatist Crisis]].<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> However, Falleen later joined the Republic,<ref name="SW Book" /> with a [[Republic Navy]] [[Falleen battle group|battle group]] being stationed there.<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" /> Despite enduring several serious setbacks<ref name="The Separatists" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the first year of the war,<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> Separatist [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|leader]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] continued his crusade against the [[Jedi Order]] and the Republic through a [[Skytop Station campaign|new campaign]].<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" />

[[File:FirstDate-ROTS.png|thumb|right|270px|After an encounter with the forces of Anakin Skywalker (center), General Grievous (left) led his fleet to Falleen and attacked the previously Confederacy-aligned world.]]
The campaign was made possible by intelligence secured through<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" /> the [[Skytop Station]] [[listening post]].<ref name="DOTD">{{TCW|Duel of the Droids}}</ref> After a [[Standard month|month]] of invasions and [[blockade]]s,<ref name="The Separatists" /> Grievous's campaign had jeopardized the Republic's powerbase in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" /> His next target was the planet [[Bothawui]], which was important to the Republic's own intelligence operations<ref name="The Separatists" /> and foothold in the Mid Rim.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> While a [[Skywalker's fleet|Republic fleet]] under [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]<ref name="The Separatists" /> and [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> [[Commanding officer|command]] made its way to Bothawui to defend the planet, Grievous was able to attack the fleet by pulling it from [[hyperspace]]. Although Skywalker's fleet ultimately escaped Grievous's attack and continued on its way to Bothawui, Grievous elected to alter his plans and, instead of chasing Skywalker further, chose to help coordinate attacks against the Republic's forces stationed near Falleen.<ref name="The Separatists" />

==The battle==
Assisted by the [[Head of State|leader]] of the Separatist state, [[Count]] [[Dooku]], and<ref name="The Separatists" /> his personal [[assassin]], [[Commander]]<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> [[Asajj Ventress]],<ref name="The Separatists" /> Grievous led an agile [[fleet]] to attack Falleen<ref name="SW Book" /> as part of his wider campaign.<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" /> After the battle began on Falleen, the [[Confederacy military|Confederate forces]] under Grievous's command emerged victorious<ref name="Helmet Collection 21" /> and wiped out the entire Republic [[battle group]], which became one of many losses the Republic sustained during Grievous's campaign.<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" /> As such, Falleen fell to the Confederacy.<ref name="The Separatists" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|The Separatist fleet commanded by General Grievous is headed your way.''"<br />"''Seems like that coward always knows where and when to attack us.|Generals Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker discuss Grievous's advance after the fall of Falleen|<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" />}}
Due to losses like those on Falleen, the Republic's operations were heavily hampered.<ref name="Downfall of a Droid Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode006.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Downfall of a Droid}}</ref> Following the victory<ref name="The Separatists" /> on Falleen,<ref name="Helmet Collection 21" /> the cyborg general and his fleet moved to [[Battle of Bothawui|capture]] Bothawui, where Skywalker was stationed with his task force. Although he was warned about the defeat on Falleen and Grievous's numbers by fellow Jedi General [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], Skywalker continued his defense, leading to a Republic victory<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" /> that stopped the Confederacy from furthering its hold on the Mid Rim. While Grievous escaped<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> and his victory on Falleen mitigated his loss at Bothawui,<ref name="Helmet Collection 21" /> Skytop Station was [[Raid on Skytop Station|destroyed]] a short while later.<ref name="DOTD" />

[[File:Skytop_station_destroyed.png|thumb|left|250px|Shortly after Grievous's victory on Falleen, the listening post that had made his wider campaign possible was destroyed.]]
Nonetheless, Grievous's attack on Falleen proved to be one of many attacks launched by the cyborg that disrupted the Republic,<ref name="SW Book" /> with his many victories making it seem like he was an almost indestructible enemy.<ref name="The Separatists" /> As Grievous and his fleet continued to attack planets such as the<ref name="SW Book" /> [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref> world of [[Dorin]], the Republic remained unable to estimate where he would strike next, which distracted the regime and enabled formerly pacified worlds to rise up once more, leading to engagements like the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="SW Book" /> Even though both had served in the battle for Falleen,<ref name="The Separatists" /> Grievous and Ventress did not come face-to-face during the operation and only met each other in person during the [[Battle of Kamino]] in [[21 BBY]].<ref name="ARC Troopers Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/arc-troopers-trivia-gallery|text="ARC Troopers" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}} establishes that [[Grievous]] and [[Asajj Ventress]] first met each other face-to-face during the [[Battle of Kamino]], which is dated to [[21 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'', seen in "[[ARC Troopers (episode)|ARC Trooperss]]." According to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, the events of "ARC Troopers" occur after the events of "[[Downfall of a Droid]]" and, therefore, the battle of Falleen. As such, Grievous and Ventress cannot have met face-to-face during the earlier battle.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The battle of Falleen was first mentioned in "[[Downfall of a Droid]],"<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" /> the sixth episode of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated series's [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]], which first aired on [[November 7]], [[2008]].<ref name="Downfall of a Droid Ep Guide" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the battle was known as the [[Fall of Falleen]], and it first appeared in "[[The Clone Wars: The Fall of Falleen|The Fall of Falleen]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|webcomic]]<ref name="The Fall of Falleen">''[[The Clone Wars: The Fall of Falleen]]''</ref> released on the same day<ref name="Archives">{{SWArchive|url=archives/index.html?c=d6de5d83-4da5-48ab-bf34-75cd3af8fbef&k=&page=9|text=''The Clone Wars'' archives}}</ref> that led into "Downfall of a Droid." The comic was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and illustrated by [[Tom Hodges|Thomas Hodges]].<ref name="The Fall of Falleen" />
