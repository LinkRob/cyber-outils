A '''credit solid''' was a payment device that could be used in lieu of carrying around a large amount of physical [[credit chip]]s. They were usually linked to various [[bank]]s. A typical credit solid was handheld and gold-striped. Untraceable credit solids were often used by agents of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebellion]] on secret missions.<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>
