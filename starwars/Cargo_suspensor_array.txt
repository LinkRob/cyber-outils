A '''cargo suspensor array''' was a system used in [[starship]] that involved mounting a number of [[Repulsorlift|repulsor fields]] in a ships hold in order to help protect fragile cargo. 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] paid a starship [[Technician|mechanics]] five hundred [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and supplied them with two [[Cargo signature scrambler]]s to install a suspensor array in the hold of the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]]. This upgrade allowed her and her companion, [[ND-5]], to take contracts [[smuggler|smuggling]] volatile cargo such as [[bacta]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cargo suspensor arrays appeared as an upgrade in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> In the expansion, players could purchase the "cargo suspensor array" upgrade to allow the player to smuggle volatile cargo.<ref name="Outlaws" />
