'''Colandrus''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Tarsunt]] who [[Senator|represented]] the [[Suntilla system]] in the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] in the period between the [[Invasion of Naboo]] and the [[Clone Wars]]. One of the most influential delegates in the Senate, he was also one of the most corrupt. A [[gambling]] addict, Colandrus frequently had losses to cover, and he extracted money from every [[planet]] within his influence, trading favors for [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. However, he did so carefully, only drawing small amounts in each transaction, so that his corruption could not be proved easily.<ref name="Part III" />

Three [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Invasion of Naboo]],<ref name="Comic Announced">{{WebCite|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/movies/2015/10/10/exclusive-star-wars-obi-wan-anakin-comic-book/73726522|text=Exclusive: Jedi get spotlight in 'Star Wars: Obi-Wan & Anakin'|author=Truitt, Brian|date=2015-10-10|work=USA Today|archivedate=20200819065457}}</ref> the [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the Republic, [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], brought the promising [[Jedi]] [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] to [[Club Kasakar]], where Colandrus used to gamble. While the Tarsunt senator was betting at a game table, the Chancellor and the Jedi, both in disguise, watched him from afar. Colandrus' last [[Chance cube|dice]] roll made him lose large sums of money. What the Tarsunt did not know was that Skywalker may have used his [[Telekinesis|telekinetic]] [[Force power|Jedi power]] to rig the dice, inspired by Palpatine's unflattering description of the senator.<ref name="Part III" />

Hearing Palpatine&mdash;whom he did not recognize under the hood he was wearing&mdash;laugh at his misfortune, Colandrus sicced his [[bodyguard]] on him. The disguised Chancellor, however, defused the situation by pretending that "his son" had just told him a joke, and promised that they would leave at once. Still unconvinced, Colandrus nevertheless allowed the cloaked individuals to go freely.<ref name="Part III" />
