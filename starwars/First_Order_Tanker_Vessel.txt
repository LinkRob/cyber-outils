The '''''First Order Tanker Vessel'''''<ref name="Poe Dameron 16">[[Poe Dameron 16|''Poe Dameron'' 16]]</ref> was a class of [[fuel]] [[tanker]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 16" /> It featured three large external fuel tanks. In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Unidentified tanker|one vessel]] intercepted a [[Phelarion-class fuel tanker]] carrying fuel for the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 15" /> It lacked a hyperdrive, requiring a [[Hyperspace transport ring]] to travel far distances.<ref name="Poe Dameron 16" />
