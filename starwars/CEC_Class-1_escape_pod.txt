The '''CEC Class-1 escape pod''', also referred to as the '''Model CEC Class-1''', was a model of [[escape pod]] made by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]. The modified [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was equipped with five Model CEC Class-1s as part of its standard factory-issued equipment. Each pod was built for a single passenger and carried [[Foodstuff|consumables]], emergency supplies, and a portable shelter. It was also equipped with a primitive [[navigation computer]], a one-way [[comlink]] transceiver, and an [[Distress beacon|emergency beacon]].

One of the Model CEC Class-1's escape pods in the ''Millennium Falcon'' had its egress hatch inscribed with [[Clynese]] text that read "PROPERTY OF [[Han Solo|HAN SOLO]], PLEASE RETURN."

==Description==
The Class-1 was a model of small [[escape pod]] manufactured by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] for use on board its [[YT-1300 light freighter]]s, which carried five as part of their standard configuration.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> To allow the passenger to escape a potentially damaged craft, the pods were carried in a storage bay on the underside of the ship next to the freight [[Turbolift|elevator]]. They could be rotated by use of an indexing conveyor to allow the passengers to board them; the freight elevator arms would then turn the pods towards the escape chute to allow them to launch from one of the two available escape ports.<ref name="YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owner's Workshop Manual">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>

They had space for a single passenger lying down inside<ref name="IncrediBuilds" /> but were too small to fit species larger than a [[human]]. The interior bedding was padded to prevent injuries and was equipped with specialized [[life sensor]]s<ref name="YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owner's Workshop Manual" /> which scanned the passenger's life signs&mdash;should a passenger lose consciousness, the pod's systems would become automated,<ref name="BTMF52">{{BuildFalconCite|52|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref> activating a [[distress beacon]] and navigating towards the nearest destination viable for their rescue. To assist the passenger in surviving whatever conditions they encountered in their destination, the pods carried a number of emergency supplies in a cargo compartment in the front, including [[Foodstuff|rations]], [[water]], a survival shelter, a [[breath mask]], [[glowrod]]s, and a portable [[comm unit]]. In addition to a navigation computer, they were also equipped with a number of course-correction [[Engine|thrusters]], [[Atmospheric controls|atmospheric control vanes]] to slow the craft's descent towards a [[planet]], and basic [[Deflector shield|shields]] to deflect space debris. Each pod was lined with soft-landing [[repulsorlift]]s to ensure a smooth landing and a sensor band to track the nearest [[starship]]s or [[space station]]s that could provide help and rescue.<ref name="YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owner's Workshop Manual" />

==History==
In [[10 BBY]], [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] piloted [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian's]] YT-1300 [[freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' on a dangerous mission, during which he was forced to eject the craft's custom-added [[YT Dart]] escape [[shuttle]] to distract a [[summa-verminoth]] inside the [[Maw Cluster]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> As a result, the ship was left with only its five CEC Class-1 pods per its standard configuration. In [[1 BBY]] while [[Rescue of Princess Leia|piloting]] [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]],<ref name="IV Date">{{FilmDate|IV}}</ref> and their two [[droid]]s, [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]], to [[Alderaan]], Solo and his passengers found themselves snared by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] space station [[Death Star]]'s [[tractor beam]]. Solo took Kenobi's advice and jettisoned the remaining escape pods to convince Imperial personnel that they had abandoned the ship.<ref name="YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owner's Workshop Manual" />

During the Empire's [[Battle of Mako-Ta|attack]] on the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]]-controlled [[Mako-Ta Space Docks]], Solo utilized one of the ''Falcon''{{'s}} Class-1 pods to evacuate C-3PO from the ship, as it was actively being hunted by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Solo needed to inform Rebel command of a way to bypass their [[MC-series star cruiser|Mon Calamari Star Cruisers']] [[hangar]] bay doors, which had been previously sealed, preventing their [[starfighter]]s from launching. In order to evacuate the droid without Vader noticing, Solo flew the ship inside one of the cruisers' exploding debris and managed to evade Vader just long enough to launch the pod towards the nearest Star Cruiser.<ref name="SW 52">[[Star Wars (2015) 52|''Star Wars'' (2015) 52]]</ref>

Decades later, during the [[Battle of Oetchi]] in [[34 ABY]], [[Rey Skywalker]] infiltrated [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]'s flagship, the ''[[Supremacy]]'', in an attempt to bring [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] back towards the [[light side of the Force]]. Solo's former first mate and current [[captain]] of the ship, [[Chewbacca]], performed a [[hyperspace]] jump close to the flagship, ejected the Model CEC-Class-1 pod Rey was in, and jumped out. The escape pod used to board the ''Supremacy'' was escorted by five [[First Order]] [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]], and Rey was held [[prisoner]] and interrogated by Snoke.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Behind the scenes==
During the making of [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], director and writer [[Rian Johnson]] wanted to ensure viewers knew the Model CEC Class-1 pod originated from the ''Millennium Falcon''. Design supervisor [[James Clyne]] was tasked with the creation of a new language that would be inscribed onto an escape pod that read, "PROPERTY OF HAN SOLO, PLEASE RETURN."<ref name="YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owner's Workshop Manual">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> Johnson emphasized his desire for the language to emulate calligraphy as opposed to a font style. Aboard a flight, Clyne studied existing languages from Thailand and the Middle East, settling on three variations to propose for use. Johnson wrote back in a email encoded in the language that applauded Clyne's "beautiful!!" work, a statement which reportedly made Clyne's day. The language is considered [[Clynese]] in the ''Star Wars'' galaxy, although there was initial discussion on defining it as [[Corellian]].<ref name="Art of the Last Jedi">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]''</ref>

The Model-CEC Class-1 was specifically designed to be reminiscent of a coffin, borrowing inspiration from C.S Lewis's [[Wikipedia:Perelandra|Perelandra]], in which the main character travels across space to Venus in a coffin.<ref name="Art of the Last Jedi" />
