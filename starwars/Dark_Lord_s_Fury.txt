"'''Dark Lord's Fury'''" is the eighth episode of [[The Empire Strikes Back (radio)|''The Empire Strikes Back'' radio drama]]. It first aired on [[National Public Radio]] on [[April 4]], [[1983]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> The episode adapts events from {{Film|V}} taking place in [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City]], centering on the betrayal and capture of [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] and the [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Princess Leia]].

==Plot summary==
Leia and Han are in the luxurious accommodations that [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando]] has provided in [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City]]. Leia still does not trust Lando, and she believes her suspicions confirmed when [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] arrives carrying [[C-3PO/Legends|See-Threepio]] in pieces. The droid has been shot with a blaster. Han still wants to trust his friend, but when Lando arrives, Han does not tell him about the incident.

[[File:ForEveryInsult-ESBRadio.flac|thumb|Vader prepares to torture Han Solo.]]
Lando invites them to join him in a banquet hall. There they are greeted by [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] himself. The dark lord easily thwarts Han's attempts to fight him, and the entrance of a squad of troops with [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] leaves the Rebels no choice but surrender. Lando tries to explain that he turned over Han in exchange for a guarantee that Cloud City would remain free of imperial interference. But Vader oversteps the limits of their deal immediately. He subjects Han to torture and will allow Fett to bring him to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba the Hutt]]; he orders Leia and Chewbacca to remain in the city indefinitely; and he threatens to occupy the city with a garrison of [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]]. Sensing the deal breaking down, Lando tells [[Lobot/Legends|Lobot]] to put the [[Bespin Wing Guard/Legends|city's guard force]] on alert.

[[File:Lobot-ESBRadio.flac|thumb|Lando tells [[Lobot/Legends|Lobot]] to stay on alert.]]
Chewbacca works to repair See-Threepio in a holding cell. The droid regains consciousness and becomes angry when he realizes his head is on backward. Han is brought in, and Threepio is able to explain to him that he was shot by stormtroopers. Leia is brought in next, soon followed by Lando. Lando explains that Vader is not after them except as bait for [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]]. He tries to justify his actions, saying that this was the only way that he could protect his people. Han tries to hit him and is subdued by guards. When Lando leaves, both Leia and Han express hope that Luke will sill be able to help them, even if he is walking into a trap.

A trooper tells Vader that Luke's [[T-65 X-wing starfighter/Legends|X-wing snubfighter]] is approaching the planet. Vader orders that Luke be allowed to land unopposed. He tells Lando that he is planning to put Skywalker into [[Carbon-freezing/Legends|carbon freezing]] to bring to the Emperor. Lando warns that this might kill him; Vader decides to test the process on Captain Solo&mdash;yet another breach of their agreement. He orders Lando to bring all the Rebels to witness Han's fate, even Threepio, though he still has to be carried in pieces. Up in Bespin's atmosphere, Luke tells [[R2-D2/Legends|Artoo]] to be ready for a battle.

In the [[Carbon-freezing chamber/Legends|carbon-freezing chamber]], Chewbacca tries fruitlessly to fight back; Han talks him down. Leia tells Han she loves him; he says, "I know." Finally Han is lowered into the machine to be frozen in a block of [[Carbonite/Legends|carbonite]].

==Continuity==
[[File:Lobot_saves_Lando.jpg|thumb|Lobot talks to Lando in [[Star Wars (1977) 57|''Star Wars'' issue 57]].]]
A conversation between Lando and Lobot is the only significant addition to an episode that otherwise closely follows the events of the film ''The Empire Strikes Back.'' Lobot had been portrayed as mute in the film,<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref><ref name="Star Wars: Behind the Magic">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref> but already the [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'' comic]] had shown him to be capable of speech.<ref name="Star Wars 1977 57">[[Star Wars (1977) 57|''Star Wars'' (1977) 57]]</ref> "Dark Lord's Fury" was the first time his voice was actually heard: he says two brief lines to Lando. Later [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] sources would explain that Lobot's [[Cybernetics/Legends|cybernetic implant]] had degraded but not destroyed the speech centers of his brain, as well as make him more inclined to communicate with computers than with people.<ref name="The New Essential Guide to Weapons and Technology">''[[The New Essential Guide to Weapons and Technology]]''</ref>
