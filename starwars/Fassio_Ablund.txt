'''Fassio Ablund''' was a [[human]] from the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] who acted as an [[Information broker|informant]] for the [[Galactic Empire]], providing information on resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[partisans]]. He was then declared a criminal by the Empire after getting caught attempting to [[Thief|steal]] [[armor]] from an Imperial [[trooper]]'s corpse and was forced to hide in [[Jedha City]] from both factions.

In [[1 BBY]], he aided the partisans in [[Battle on Jedha|ambushing]] a [[Imperial Hammers Elite Armor Unit|group]] of Imperials in [[Tythoni Square]], firing from a balcony until an [[TX-225 GAVw "Occupier" combat assault tank|Imperial tank]] destroyed the building. Jedha City was [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]] by the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] soon after.

==Biography==
Fassio Ablund was a [[human]]<ref name="ROUVG" /> who acted as an [[Information broker|informant]] for the [[Galactic Empire]] on his [[homeworld]],<ref name="Geektionary French" /> the [[moon]] [[Jedha]].<ref name="Geektionary" /> Seeking protection from the [[Partisans|partisan]] [[Jedha insurgency|insurgency]] of resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]], Ablund sold the secrets of their movements<ref name="ROUVG" /> and activities to the Empire to ensure his own survival. He then fell from grace with the Empire after getting caught attempting to [[Thief|steal]] [[armor]] from the corpse of an Imperial [[trooper]].<ref name="Geektionary" /> Declared a criminal,<ref name="ROUVG" /> Ablund's protection immediately ceased and, with no allies left, he was forced to keep a low profile in [[Jedha City]] while avoiding both groups that he had betrayed.<ref name="Geektionary" />

[[File:FassioBalcony-RO.png|thumb|left|300px|Fassio Ablund (top left corner) ambushed the Empire with the partisans on Jedha.]]
Despite this, Ablund aided the partisans<ref name="RO" /> in [[1 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> when they [[Battle on Jedha|ambushed]] a [[Imperial Hammers Elite Armor Unit|group]] of Imperials carrying [[kyber crystal]]s through<ref name="RO" /> [[Tythoni Square]]<ref name="ROUVG" /> in the [[city]]. The partisans hid in the surrounding buildings and streets before attacking once several [[stormtrooper]]s and a [[TX-225 GAVw "Occupier" combat assault tank]] carrying the [[crystal]]s had entered the square. Ablund was stood on a balcony overlooking the square, firing down on his former Imperial allies until the [[tank]] fired into the building where Ablund had been stood, destroying the balconies. The partisans succeeded in defeating the Imperials and stealing the crystals, but Jedha City was [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]] soon after by the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]].<ref name="RO" /> The blast [[Death|killed]] many partisans, although some did escape.<ref name="SW39">[[Star Wars (2015) 39|''Star Wars'' (2015) 39]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Fassio Ablund obeyed only one rule: self-preservation.<ref name="Geektionary" /> He had [[Color|tan]] skin, brown [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]].<ref name="ROUVG" />

==Equipment==
Fassio Ablund wore a black [[Hat|turban]] and [[scarf]] with a red [[cloak]] and [[Clothing|sash]] over white [[robe]]s. His outfit also included a brown [[bandolier]] and at least one white armored [[glove]].<ref name="ROUVG" /> He used a [[blaster rifle]] in combat.<ref name="RO" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fassio Ablund appeared in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film, ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="RO" /> The character was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="ROUVG" />
