'''Almakian apples''' were a type of [[Fruit/Legends|fruit]] used in cooking. They were used as an ingredient in the dish known as [[all-Almakian apple pie]], a part of the cultural cuisine of the [[Leffingite/Legends|Leffingite]] [[Species/Legends|species]]<ref name="DaD" /> of the world [[Almak/Legends|Almak]],<ref name="CSWE Almak">{{CSWECite|I|24|Almak}}</ref> who greatly enjoyed eating the [[Pie/Legends|pie]]. For use in the pie, fresh and juicy [[Apple/Legends|apples]] would be spiced with [[Kessinnamon]] and then baked into a [[Color/Legends|golden]] [[pom seed flour]] crust.<ref name="DaD" />

The [[Besalisk/Legends|Besalisk]] [[Chef/Legends|chef]] [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster's]] [[Restaurant/Legends|eatery]], [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] in the [[CoCo Town/Legends|CoCo Town]] [[district]] of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] served all-Almakian apple pie as a dessert for 2.5 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]] with the option of adding [[Denta bean/Legends|denta bean]] [[Denta bean ice cream|ice cream]] for an additional 2.2 credits, as Jettster was fond of the dish. Almakian apple [[Fruit juice/Legends|fruit juice]] was also available in the diner in large portions for 2.5 credits and small for 1.7 credits. Both items were available on the menu<ref name="DaD" /> at some point prior to diner's destruction during the first two [[Standard year/Legends|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] rule.<ref name="DexEra">''[[The Last of the Jedi: Underworld]]'' established that [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] was destroyed by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. ''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' later stated that the novel took place around the [[Standard year/Legends|year]] [[18 BBY/Legends|18 BBY]]. ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' states that the Empire was formed in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]], so Dex's Diner was destroyed either during 19 BBY or 18 BBY.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Almakian apples were first mentioned in the [[2009]] [[StarWars.com]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] exclusive article, "[[Dining at Dex's]]," which was written in the style of an [[in-universe]] menu for Dex's Diner by [[Gregory Walker]].<ref name="DaD" />
