{{Quote|I particularly recommend those included in Yim Relot's collaborative project ''Filling the Vacuum: Artistic Responses to the Missing''.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
'''''Filling the Vacuum: Artistic Responses to the Missing''''' was a collaborative artistic project led by the [[artist]] [[Yim Relot]]. The piece aimed to act as a monument to all those who had vanished under the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]] and the absence that they left.<ref name="Rise" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] recommended the works in the project in particular when discussing a number of artistic memorials to the vanished that<ref name="Rise" /> he<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> was deeply moved by in his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Filling the Vacuum: Artistic Responses to the Missing'' was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
