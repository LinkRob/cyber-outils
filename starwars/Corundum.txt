{{Quote|Steelpeckers search for vanadium, osmiridium, and corundum which they store in their gizzards so you want to ''stay away'' from parts with concentrations of those&mdash;such as gyroscopic equipment or focusing arrays that have been exposed to intense heat.|Rey in her survival guide of Jakku|<ref name="Rey's Survival Guide" />}}
'''Corundum''' was a [[Color|pink]] metal that was used to construct [[Jedi]] [[lightsaber]]s and [[gyroscope]]s. It was consumed by [[steelpecker]]s on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] and kept in their gizzards to aid with digestion.

==Description==
[[File:Steelpecker-SWChrome2024.png|thumb|200px|left|Steelpeckers of Jakku used corundum to aid in their digestion.]]
Corundum was a pink [[color]]ed<ref name="JFO" /> metal<ref name="Alien Archive" /> could be used to contruct the emitters and switches of [[Jedi]] [[lightsaber]]s.<ref name="JFO" /> It was also found in [[Gyroscope|gyroscopic]] equipment and [[focusing array]]s that had been exposed to intense [[Temperature|heat]].<ref name="Rey's Survival Guide" /> The metal, found on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]], was consumed by [[steelpecker]]s and collected in their gizzards<ref name="TFA VD" /> to aid with digestion.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> When combined in their gizzards with the materials [[vanadium]] and [[osmiridium]], corundum formed a chemical that helped break down a steelpecker's metal [[Foodstuff|food]].<ref name="DB">{{Databank|steelpecker|Steelpecker}}</ref>

==History==
{{GameMechanics}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' takes place five [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Great Jedi Purge]], which is dated to [[19 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'', in which [[Cal Kestis]] finds corundum on [[Bogano]], is set in [[14 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi Order|Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] discovered some corundum on the [[planet]] [[Bogano]] and kept it for making modifications to his [[Jaro Tapal|former master's]] [[Jaro Tapal's lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="JFO" />
{{EndGame}}

Many [[Standard year|years]] later during the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Era|Era]], the Jakkuvian [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] wrote about the steelpecker [[bird]]s' affinity for the metal in her [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]] and warned future scavengers of Jakku's [[Starship Graveyard]] to avoid parts that contained high concentrations of it so they would not encounter them.<ref name="Rey's Survival Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corundum was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'' by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TFA VD" /> and the replica journal ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' by [[Jason Fry]],<ref name="Rey's Survival Guide" /> both released on [[December 18]], [[2015]] in conjunction with the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="TFA books">{{SW|url=news/a-galaxy-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens-books-coming-december-18-first-look|text=A Galaxy of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Books Coming December 18 - UPDATED!}}</ref> It first appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]], where it was one of twenty-four materials players could collect to customize the appearance of Cal Kestis' lightsaber.<ref name="JFO" />
