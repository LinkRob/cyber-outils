The '''Bala Trix Nebula''' was a nebula where the planet [[Bespin/Legends|Bespin]] was located.<ref name="ST">[[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] {{C|Tokyo and Paris only}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A galaxy map depicting the various destinations of the [[Star Tours/Legends|Star Tours]] travel agency is seen in the queue of the [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] attraction at [[Disneyland Paris]] and [[Tokyo Disneyland]]. The map erroneously suggests Bespin is in this nebula, and is also the seventh planet in its system, which is contradicted by many [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] sources.<ref name="ST" />
