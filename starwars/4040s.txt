The '''"4040s"''' were a pro&ndash;[[Galactic Empire]] activist group based on the [[ecumenopolis]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]'s [[Level 4040|4040th level]] that were active during the first [[standard year|year]] of the [[Imperial Era|Imperial reign]]. Functioning as something of a paramilitary group in [[time]], the group was extremely supportive of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], and derisive of his foes. Similar groups flourished all over Coruscant, but the 4040s were one of the oldest. The group had some degree of connection to the Imperial administration, particularly [[Imperial Intelligence]]. The group regularly held protests outside the offices of the [[Chandrila]]n [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] and other members of the [[Delegation of 2,000]]. [[Laevido]], a man that had formerly worked in [[weather control]], was the ''de facto'' leader of the group.<ref name="MoF" />

The 4040s held meetings in [[Level 4040 dive bar|dive bars]], [[Level 4040 club|clubs]], and other areas on the [[Level 4040|4040th level]]. The group was almost entirely [[human]], though one member—[[Dakhmi]]—was an [[Omwati]]. Other members that belonged to the inner circle included the [[Kloo horn]] [[Musician|player]] [[Fowlitz]], the [[marriage|married]] couple [[Erinya]] and [[Jayu]], and [[Thalvus Agyomenidus]], who was radicalized by the group and [[Zhuna's murder|attempted]] to [[death|assassinate]] Senator Mothma and her husband [[Perrin Fertha]].<ref name="MoF" /> 

[[Chemish Runazha]], a member of [[Imperial Intelligence]], [[Spy|infiltrated]] the group as "Homish Grayline" to learn about why Thalvus Agyomenidus had murdered their cousin [[Zhuna]], who was killed in his assassination attempt on Senator Mothma. Chemish got to meet the hundreds associated with the group at meetings, fundraisers, [[speeder]] races, political rallies, and private gatherings. They questioned whether or not the 4040s were a potential source of informants and agents, or a threat to be surveilled.<ref name="MoF" />

At various political rallies, the 4040s observed the arrivals and departures of senatorial [[aide]]s and recorded speeder traffic, leading Chemish to wonder what the group was doing with the surveillance data. Listening to an exchange between Laevido and Dakhmi, they learned that the 4040s had a demonstration planned for [[Separatist Reintegration Day]] in order to make the worlds that had been aligned with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] feel unwelcome in the new Empire.<ref name="MoF" />
