==Behind the scenes==
Bigbot appeared in [[War of the Bounty Hunters Alpha 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters Alpha'' 1]], a [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]]<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 1" /> released by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 5]], [[2021]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/93642/star_wars_war_of_the_bounty_hunters_alpha_2021_1|text=Star Wars: War Of The Bounty Hunters Alpha (2021) #1}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]] and illustrated by [[Steve McNiven]].<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 1" /> Bigbot's design resembled the [[Annoo-dat Blue]] [[Species/Legends|species]] from [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], but Soule confirmed the character was not a part of that race.<ref name="Its not">{{Twitter|CharlesSoule|status/1678446576497315843|[[Charles Soule]]|quote=It’s not. {{C|In response to: "Wait just a second, I think this random dude named "Bigbot" (left) from "War of the Bounty Hunters Alpha #1" might be an Annoo-dat Blue (right) from the "Droids" animated series??? Or at least our first Canon adaptation? Could this be the case, @CharlesSoule?"}}|archivedate=20230801040832}}</ref>
