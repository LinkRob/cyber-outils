'''Celesta Kami''' was the [[human]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Carver Drow]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] during the [[High Republic Era]].

==Biography==
During the [[High Republic Era]], Celesta Kami trained with and became best friends with [[Wes Vinik]] as a [[Jedi Youngling]].<ref name="The Battle of Tenoo">{{YJA|The Battle of Tenoo}}</ref> Kami later served as the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] to [[Jedi Master]] [[Carver Drow]]. She trained at the [[Batuu Jedi temple|Jedi temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="The Tale of Short Spire" />

At some point, Kami accompanied [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], and [[Nubs]] to [[Black Spire Outpost]] in search of tools that her master requested. The Jedi ran into [[Ozon Nimbee]], a real estate developer keen on cutting down the natural stone spires of Batuu to build a more modern outpost. After Nimbee retaliated and sent out [[Pistoeka sabotage droid|buzz droids]] to cut down the spires, Kami and the [[youngling]]s inspired the locals to fight back. Kami helped destroy the buzz droids and expel Nimbee from the planet.<ref name="The Tale of Short Spire" />

Later, Kami, Solay, and Nubs, along with several villagers, watched as Brightstar trained with [[remote]]s under Draw. When Brightstar was finished with his set, Kami thanked the attendees for coming. Kami continued to watch as Crow demonstrated his advanced training set, before Brightstar attempted to copy it, but fell onto the ground. Kami ran up to Brightstar and asked if he was alright, and Brightstar responded that he had no injuries but he wanted to be perfect, and as Brightstar sulked away, Kami signaled for Drow. Draw asked Kami to continue training with Solay and Nubs, and she left with the two to go practice.<ref name="The Caves of Batuu">{{YJA|The Caves of Batuu}}</ref>

Later, at the [[Battle of Tenoo]], Kami and [[Princess]] [[Inaya]] arrived in Anaya's ship to rescue the Jedi trapped by the [[Ganguls]], where Kami subsequently reunited with Vinik.<ref name="The Battle of Tenoo" />
==Equipment==
Kami carried a [[Color|blue]]-bladed [[lightsaber]].<ref name="The Tale of Short Spire" />
