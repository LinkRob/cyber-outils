'''For Every Action trilogy''' was a '''trilogy''' in the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'' campaign]] that was released in [[2005]] and written by [[Rob Edwards]]. It focused on the heroes' efforts to stop [[Riboga]] the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]], who had just regained control of crime in the [[Cularin system/Legends|Cularin system]].
