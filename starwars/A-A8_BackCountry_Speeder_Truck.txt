The '''A-A8 BackCountry Speeder Truck''' was a [[speeder truck]] and trekking vehicle manufactured by [[Trast Heavy Transports]]. Based on the popular [[A-A5 Speeder Truck]] model, the A-A8 BackCountry came equipped with numerous systems to keep sentient beings alive and comfortable in wilderness survival situations over extended periods of time. Similar to the A-A5, its moderately spacious crew compartment seated the [[pilot]] and one passenger. The vehicle came with a modular sleeping cabin comfortable for up to six individuals.<ref name="Savage Spirits" />
