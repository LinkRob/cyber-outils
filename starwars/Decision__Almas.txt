"'''Decision: Almas'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year four of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the first adventure in the [[Decisions trilogy]] and was written by [[Ronald A. Heintz]].

==Plot summary==
The heroes join with [[Strike Force Cularin]], a military task force made up of units from the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]], [[Cularin Militia]] and [[Thaereian military]]. The task force was dispatched to the [[Ando system/Legends|Ando system]], where it captures a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Separatist]] ship and attacked a Separatist base. However, the heroes recovered a [[Datacard/Legends|datacard]] which revealed that the Thaereians had secretly joined forces with the Separatists.
