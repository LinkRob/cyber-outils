The '''Dene Gois Cluster''' was a cluster of [[moon]]s, including [[Hubin]], which orbited a [[gas giant]] [[Unidentified planet (Dene Gois Cluster)|planet]]<ref name="Star Wars 56" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> A [[hyperspace route]] opened up in its vicinity, and it was often used as a route for [[smuggler]]s.<ref name="Star Wars 56" />
