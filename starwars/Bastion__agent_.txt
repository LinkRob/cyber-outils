[[Agent]] '''Bastion''' was a [[New Republic]] operative that served during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Bastion was present with [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Prince]]ss [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] surveying the damage left on [[Chandrila]] following an [[Attack on Chandrila (Acolytes of the Beyond)|attack]] by the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] when an [[Saycos Hawn|acolyte]] ambushed the trio. Bastion was later present at a briefing reviewing the assailant and the return of [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]].<ref name="Last Stand 2" />
