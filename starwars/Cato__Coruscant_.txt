'''Cato'''<ref name="Wrong Jedi" /> was a dancing establishment<ref name="Catch" /> in the [[Coruscant Underworld]]. One Cato establishment was located on [[Level 1312]], near [[Asajj Ventress]]' dwelling. An [[Color|orange]] and yellow neon sign was used to attract customers. It showed the name of the business written in [[Trade Federation Script]], with an abstract motif below that,<ref name="Wrong Jedi" /> which contained additional text written in [[Aurebesh]], reading "DANCE".<ref name="Catch">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref> Another Cato establishment was located on [[Level 1313]], near [[Rafa's Laundry]], while a third establishment was located at [[the wharf]] on the same level.<ref name="Gone" />
