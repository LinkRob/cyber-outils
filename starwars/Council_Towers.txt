The '''Council Towers''' were the four towers&mdash;the [[Tower of First Knowledge]], the [[Reassignment Council Tower]], the [[High Council Tower]], and the [[Tower of Reconciliation]]&mdash;that surrounded the [[Palace spire|Temple Spire]] atop the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. Each tower housed one of the four [[Jedi Council|councils]] that oversaw the [[Jedi Order]]'s affairs.<ref name="Complete Locations 2016" />
