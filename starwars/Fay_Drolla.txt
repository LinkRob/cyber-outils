'''Fay Drolla''' was a [[human]] [[engineer]] that worked with with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] at its [[Great Temple|base]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]] in [[2 BBY]]. During his time at the base he was tasked with reprogramming a [[KX-series security droid]] that [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] recovered from the [[Ghorman Massacre]]. When he was ready to power it up after the reprogramming he sent a [[Unidentified rebel corporal (Yavin 4)|corporal]] to notify Andor of the test. After explaining some of the details of the reprogramming his offer of protective [[goggles]] was rejected by Andor who instead chose to grab a [[blaster rifle]] and aim it at the droid during the activation. Drolla proceeded to flip a switch to activate the droid that would become [[K-2SO]]. <ref name="Welcome to the Rebellion" />

==Personality and traits==
Drolla had light skin, [[color|brown]] [[eye]]s and [[color|grey]] [[hair]]. While reprogramming K-2SO, Drolla wore a protective goggles and a tan [[clothing|vest]] over a tan and orange [[jumpsuit]].<ref name="Welcome to the Rebellion" />
