'''Bonnard''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[Lieutenant]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]’s [[Hunter Fleet]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Bonnard served aboard [[Admiral]] [[Kalaxo]]'s [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|ISD]] ''[[Ultima II]]'', participating in a [[Battle of Ab Dalis|battle]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. She was killed during the battle.<ref name="SW 15" />
