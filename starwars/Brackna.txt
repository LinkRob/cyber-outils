'''Brackna''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Wayseeker" /> located in the [[Brackna system]] within the [[Doldur sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square P-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

During the [[High Republic Era]], [[soup]] contests took place on Brackna.<ref name="Wayseeker" /> Around [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{WayseekerDate}}</ref> after the [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] returned to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Wayseeker" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]], she recounted her travels as a Jedi [[Wayseeker]] within the past [[Standard decade|decade]] to other members of the [[Jedi Order]]. As Rwoh did so, the [[human]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Indara]] mentally noted that she spoke in a way that she could make judging a soup contest on Brackna sound interesting.<ref name="Wayseeker" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brackna was mentioned in ''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]'', a [[2025]] novel written by [[Justina Ireland]].<ref name="Wayseeker" />
