{{Quote|Banthas are common on many worlds, and bred for their meat, wool, hides, and milk.|Hondo Ohnaka, ''Galactic Explorer's Guide''|<ref name="Guide" />}}
'''Bantha hide''' also known as '''Bantha leather''' was a type of [[Leather|animal hide]] harvested from [[bantha]]s, a domesticated [[species]] [[Reproduction|bred]] for their hides as well as their [[Foodstuff|meat]], [[Bantha wool|wool]], and [[Blue milk|milk]].<ref name="Guide" /> The hide could be tanned and used to make [[clothing]] or furniture.<ref name="Absolutely" /> Bantha-hide [[boot]]s, jackets, and other wares were quiet popular,<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> with the fashion-concious [[human]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] owning a [[Color|red-and-black]] bantha-hide [[belt]].<ref name="LS">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref> Bantha hides could also be mashed with fermented [[Plant|grains]] in order to make the bitter drink [[ardees]].<ref name="Absolutely" />  The hide was mentioned in an entry on banthas in the section concerning the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] in the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[book]], ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''.<ref name="Guide" />
