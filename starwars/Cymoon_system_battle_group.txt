A '''battle group''' of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy]] that consisted of multiple [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] was active during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Recognizing that the [[moon]] [[Cymoon 1]] and the Imperial [[Weapons Factory Alpha]] on its surface were vital, the Empire maintained a strong fleet presence in the [[Cymoon system]], with the [[battle group]] remaining there at all times. [[Landing craft]] were extensively scanned, with incoming craft having to be scheduled well in advance of arriving, though there was a degree of flexibility.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

The battle group's [[Star Destroyer]]s were supplemented by a score of [[fighter wing]]s and orbital vessels. On the ground, the factory was protected by a full [[Cymoon regiment|regiment]] of [[stormtrooper]]s and subdivided into [[battalion]]s. Due to the various number of ad hoc groups, efficacy was reduced due to communications breakdowns between the units.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />
