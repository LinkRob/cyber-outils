'''Cody Matthew Johnson''' is a [[Wikipedia:Music Producer|music producer]], [[Wikipedia:Songwriter|songwriter]] and [[Wikipedia:Composer|composer]] known for working on various video games including [[Wikipedia:Bayonetta 3|Bayonetta 3]], and [[Wikipedia:Devil May Cry 5|Devil May Cry 5]].<ref name="Website" /> His first work in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise was to compose music including [[in-universe]] music tracks for ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
