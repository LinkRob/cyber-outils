The '''2nd Fleet''' was a [[fleet]] in the [[Republic Navy|naval branch]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Rise" /> formed between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Note" /> During the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the 2nd Fleet was stationed in the [[Quellor sector]], the location of the [[Neimoidian]] [[Purse world|Purse Worlds]], which belonged to the Confederate-aligned [[Trade Federation]].<ref name="Rise" />

Because of these purse-[[Planet|worlds]], the 2nd Fleet [[Blockade of the Quellor sector|blockaded]] the [[sector]], and as a result fought against the CIS [[Trade Federation Home Defense Legions]] and its [[Quellor sector fleet|enormous fleet]] of [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleships]], which kept the Republic fleet at bay. As one of the twenty mainline fleets of the Republic [[Navy]], it was attached to a [[Sector Army]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 2nd Fleet was mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Rise" />
