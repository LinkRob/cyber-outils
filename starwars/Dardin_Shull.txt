'''Dardin Shull''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="KenobiI" /> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="KenobiDate">{{KenobiDate|I}}</ref> Shull was present in a town square in [[Anchorhead]] when [[Inquisitor]]s [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]] and [[Fifth Brother]] interrogated the locals for information regarding the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Nari]].<ref name="KenobiI" />

==Biography==
Dardin Shull lives on Tatooine and gives water to the locals in a stand. One day, he was giving water to people until foreman [[Groff Ditcher]] cut in line. Reva returns to Tatooine and ask him where Owen Lars was. Dardin found Owen and his nephew: [[Luke Skywalker]] in a [[Anchorhead General Store|store]] and came to warn Owen that Reva was looking for him.
