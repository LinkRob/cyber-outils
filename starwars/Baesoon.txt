'''Baesoon''' was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] who worked as a [[tauntaun]] handler at the Alliance's [[Echo Base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]]. Due to a shortage of handlers, Baesoon's rotation kept the handler from having [[time]] to both get enough [[sleep]] and attend the mess hall. As a result, handlers like Baesoon relied on [[Foodstuff|food]] runs from other personnel. One [[Standard day|day]],<ref name="Good Kiss" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[A Good Kiss]]" take place concurrently to the events of {{Film|V}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> a runner named [[Chase Wilsorr]] brought food and [[caf]] to Baesoon and two other handlers named [[Murell]] and [[Jordan Smythe]] at the tauntaun pens. Baesoon gratefully accepted a share of the delivery. After Smythe was distracted with Wilsorr, Baesoon told off the other handler and ordered him to equip [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and his [[Riba|tauntaun]]<ref name="Good Kiss" /> for a patrol.<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Baesoon appeared in the short story "[[A Good Kiss]]," written by [[C.B. Lee]] as a part of the [[2020]] short story collection ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="Good Kiss" />
