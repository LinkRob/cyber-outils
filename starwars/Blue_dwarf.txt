'''Blue dwarves''' were a classification of [[star]] that was small and blue in [[color]]. One such blue dwarf was [[Asar]], the star of the [[Ilum system]]. Asar was considered "exceedingly bright" for its star type.<ref name="NoP" /> The [[Astrology|astrological]] signs [[Ai]] and [[Genry]], from the astrological systems of [[Shili]] and [[Coruscant]] respectively, were marked by the formation of blue dwarves nearby, and people born under them would allegedly possess wisdom, charisma and exceptional virility.<ref name="LPOA">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>
