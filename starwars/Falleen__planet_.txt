'''Falleen''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> located in the [[Falleen system]] in the [[Doldur sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Reptomammal|repto-mammalian]] [[Falleen]] [[species]].<ref name="Helmet Collection 17" /> Falleen aligned itself with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in the build up to the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. The leader of the Confederacy, [[Count]] [[Dooku]], offered [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] of the planet [[Eriadu]] the opportunity to transport his world's [[lommite]] through Falleen and other Separatist-controlled areas including [[Ruusan]] and the [[Tion Cluster]] in order to persuade him to side with the Separatists.<ref name="Tarkin" />

During the Clone Wars, Falleen eventually became a Republic world<ref name="SW Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> despite its previous alliance with the Separatists.<ref name="Tarkin" /> The Republic established a [[Falleen battle group|battle group]] to defend the planet,<ref name="Downfall of a Droid">{{TCW|Downfall of a Droid}}</ref> yet it was targeted by [[General]] [[Grievous]]<ref name="SW Book" /> during the [[Skytop Station campaign]].<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" /> Beyond disrupting Republic operations through the attack, Grievous's [[fleet]]<ref name="SW Book" /> emerged victorious during the [[Battle of Falleen|battle on Falleen]]<ref name="Helmet Collection 21">{{HelmetCollectionCite|21|Databank A-Z}}</ref> and destroyed the Republic's [[battle group]].<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" />
