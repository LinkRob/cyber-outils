'''Commodore Freylon Obituary''' was the obituary of [[Freylon]], a [[commodore]] in the [[Imperial Navy]].<ref name="Rise" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' establishes that the Battle of Ponolapo occurred after [[Crimson Dawn]]'s [[War against the Sith|war against the Empire]], which ended during the events of [[Hidden Empire 5|''Hidden Empire'' 5]]. {{SW|url=news/star-wars-hidden-empire|text=''Hidden Empire'' Revealed: Charles Soule on Marvel's Next ''Star Wars'' Epic &ndash; Exclusive}} establishes that the events of ''[[Star Wars: Hidden Empire]]'', including ''Hidden Empire'' 5, take place between the events of {{Film|V}} and {{Film|VI}}, which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. ''Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire'' also establishes that the battle occurred before the [[Battle of Endor]], which is dated to 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] by ''Star Wars: Timelines''. Therefore, the battle must have occurred between those [[Standard year|years]].</ref> Freylon was [[Death|killed]] at the [[Battle of Ponolapo]] when<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="The Steadfast Soldier">{{StoryCite|story=The Steadfast Soldier|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> [[Star Destroyer]], the [[Steadfast (Imperial-class)|''Steadfast'']], was attacked by a small [[Rebel Alliance Navy|rebel fleet]]. The obituary was stored in the "Imperial Navy" section of the [[Imperial Archives]] and was referenced by the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] when<ref name="Rise" /> he<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> mentioned Freylon and the Battle of Ponolapo in his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>
