The '''''Consular''-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)''', also known as the '''''Consular''-class frigate''', '''''Consular''-class Armed Cruiser''', '''Charger C70 Combat Refit''' or the '''Republic frigate''', was a modified variant of [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class light cruiser]] that saw service with the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Converted from [[Judicial Forces]] [[starship]]s as part of [[Project Supercharger]], the combat-oriented Charger c70 featured new battle-[[armor]]ed [[hull]] and [[laser]] [[turret]]s and proved a mainstay of the [[Republic Navy]] during the conflict.

==Characteristics==
[[File:RepublicFrigate-MF75.png|thumb|left|250px|The Charger c70 retrofit was equipped with heavy laser cannons and boasted powerful engines.]]
The Charger c70 retrofit, also known as the Republic frigate, was a product of the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]], based on the hull of the company's earlier [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class light cruiser]];<ref name="Starfighters" /> at 138.55-[[meter]]s long,<ref name="Databank Republic Frigate" /> it was slightly larger than the original Consulars (at 115 meters).<ref name="SWJ1">''[[Star Wars Journeys: The Phantom Menace]]''</ref> The most important difference between the Charger c70 and its predecessor, however, was its combat configuration.<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" /> Unlike the unarmed Judicial cruiser,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> the Charger c70 was equipped with four turret-mounted heavy [[laser cannon]]s (ventral port and starboard and dorsal port and starboard), a forward-mounted twin light laser cannon, and dorsal turret-mounted [[quad laser cannon]]. Furthermore, it was outfitted with forward twin [[concussion missile launcher]]s<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" /> as well as armored hull plating.<ref name="Starfighters" />

Like the Consular, the Charger c70 had three [[sublight engine]]s, though it could travel 1,200 [[Kilometer per hour|kilometers per hour]].<ref name="Starfighters" /> to the ''Consular''-class's 900 kph.<ref name="SWJ1" /> It featured a forward navigational sensor dish, a port and starboard docking ring, a communications and sensor dish towards the stern, and a [[deflector shield generator]],<ref name="Starfighters" /> with sturdier models introduced during the retrofit.<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" /> The vessel was also equipped with four [[Republic escape pod]]s and a detachable [[salon pod]] in the bow below the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] for diplomatic meetings.<ref name="Starfighters" />

==Role==
Republic frigates saw extensive use during the Clone Wars, and served in various roles; while they were often used as escort vessels and armored [[transport]]s,<ref name="Ambush" /> they were also considered to excel in the role of [[scout ship]] or picket.<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" /> Some instances also saw them as [[hospital ship]]s.<ref name="Unknown" />

==History==
[[File:ZaksConsularCruiser-Ambush.png|left|thumb|220x220px|A ''Consular''-class cruiser during the Ambush on Rugosa]]
With the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the newly-formed and largely-unprepared [[Republic Navy]] was forced to press existing ship classes into combat; the ''Consular''-class space cruiser, already in service with the [[Judicial Forces]] and the [[Republic Diplomatic Corps]], was selected for this purpose by the [[Department of Naval Procurement]] due to its speed and durability. In cooperation with Corellian Engineering, the Department initiated [[Project Supercharger]], with the objective to convert Consular frigates to a war footing.<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" /> As early as the [[Battle of Christophsis]]<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Charger c70s had entered service, with the ''[[Swift Return]]'' being destroyed by [[Admiral]] [[Trench]].<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> During the [[Battle of Ryloth]], [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] of the [[Jedi Order]] made use of a Republic frigate to [[Battle of Rugosa|travel]] to [[Rugosa]] with his [[clone trooper]] [[Diplomatic service|escort]] in order to negotiate a possible alliance with [[Katuunko]], the [[King]] of [[Toydaria]]. When they arrived, the vessel was ambushed by a pair of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]].<ref name="Ambush" />

Republic frigates also participated in the [[Defense of Rishi Station]] as part of the Republic [[fleet]] commanded by Kenobi and Skywalker.<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref> After Skywalker's [[astromech droid]], [[R2-D2]], was obtained by [[General]] [[Grievous]], R2-D2's datafiles were intact, and Grievous was able to glance at Republic military strategies and information, including information on Republic frigates.<ref name="Duel of the Droids">{{TCW|Duel of the Droids}}</ref> After the [[Mission to Rodia|capture]] of [[Trade Federation]] [[Viceroy of the Trade Federation|Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] on [[Rodia]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Luminara Unduli]] and [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] used a Republic frigate to ferry Gunray from the surface to the ''[[Tranquility]]'', a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] in orbit above Rodia, and docked with the vessel. After Separatist agents managed to liberate Gunray, they used the same Republic frigate to escape.<ref name="Cloak">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness}}</ref>

[[File:Jedi_Crash_frigate.png|thumb|right|250px|A Republic frigate escapes the crippled flagship.]]
When Jedi General [[Aayla Secura]]'s [[Aayla Secura's task force|task force]] were attacked by Separatist naval forces that culminated in the [[Battle of Quell]], Skywalker, Tano, and their forces arrived to evacuate Secura and her troops. During the battle, a Republic frigate docked with Secura's [[flagship]] [[Liberty (Venator-class)|''Liberty'']], and after Skywalker sustained critical injuries during the escape, planned to dock with the ''[[Resolute]]'', Skywalker's flagship that was under the command of Admiral Yularen. During the docking procedure, however, [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droid starfighters]] attacked the frigate, destroying its forward navigational dish and sensor, and damaging the bridge, killing the [[Clone trooper pilot|pilots]]. The damage inadvertently caused the vessel's [[hyperdrive]] to activate; it had to take evasive action to prevent a collision with the ''Resolute'' and jumped into [[hyperspace]] in-atmosphere. When the frigate turned out to be on a collision course with a [[star]], Secura, Tano, and others deactivated the power to exit hyperspace.<ref name="Jedi Crash">{{TCW|Jedi Crash}}</ref>
