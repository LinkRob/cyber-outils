'''Cyslin Myr''', a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Mirialan]] was the [[Jedi Master]] who [[Jedi training|trained]] [[Padawan]] [[Mace Windu]] in the ways of [[the Force]].<ref name="Mace Windu 4" />

Around [[60 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Myr and<ref name="Mace Windu 4" /> her<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> apprentice [[Mission to Mathas|traveled]] to [[Mathas]], a [[planet]] whose inhabitants were suffering from the [[Niffin plague]]. Together, they removed a man named [[Drooz]], who was pretending to be a Jedi prophet and healer to trick the inhabitants into paying him for spiritual advice. They openly confronted Drooz and arrested him, after her Padawan, Mace Windu disarmed him and planned to kill him. She talked Mace out of it, convincing him that was a matter to be taken up with the court of law.<ref name="Mace Windu 4" />
