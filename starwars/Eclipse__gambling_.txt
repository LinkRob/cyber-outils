'''Eclipse''' was a [[gambling]] [[hologame]] played with colored [[Hologram|holographic]] cards. It was played with multiple players and a dealer. The cards were in various colors and with different symbols on themPlayers could either "stand" or "stay" or could let themselves be "hit" by the dealer, who then shouted "Eclipse" and then, depending on the their cards, the players would either win or loose. Names of cards included "Twilight", "Equinox", "Nova", "Partial Eclipse" and "Supernova".<ref name="DJ-MW">[[The Last Jedi – DJ – Most Wanted 1|''The Last Jedi – DJ – Most Wanted'' 1]]</ref> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi &ndash; DJ &ndash; Most Wanted'' 1 to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[slicer]] [[DJ]] played the game in [[Canto Bight]], where he first played it with stolen money in the [[Coruscant Hotel and Casino]]&mdash;where it was played by [[Unidentified Zabrak Eclipse dealer|a Zabrak dealer]]&mdash;and later the [[Vesustrian]].<ref name="DJ-MW" />
