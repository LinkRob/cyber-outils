'''C-85''' was a fictional character in the [[holodrama]] ''[[Family of the Frontier Worlds]]''. The series released at least seventeen episodes<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> In the fifteenth episode [[Suna]] offered to help their mom, [[Malina]], repair the [[farm]], noting that they were shown how to solder by C-85 in [[Suna's school|school]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
C-85 was first mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
