'''Freight tenders''' were a type of [[starship]] with [[ion engine]]s.<ref name="Weapon" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="WeaponDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the main events of ''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]'' to [[0 ABY]].</ref> a pair of freight tenders were part of the busy traffic in the [[Realspace|space]] lanes above the [[planet]] [[Giju]]. When a [[Mission to Giju|firefight]] erupted between a [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|trio]] of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing starfighters]] and a patrol of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], the two freight tenders tried to fly away from the fighting as quickly as possible.<ref name="Weapon" />

==Behind the scenes==
Freight tenders appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Weapon" />
