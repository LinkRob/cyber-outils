{{Quote|In response to the Yomo Council's treasonous actions&mdash;its defiance of Grand Admiral Sloane's order to direct assets to the D'Aelgoth sector, its refusal to acknowledge the Empire's rightful regent on Coruscant, and its alliance with the Shiortuun Syndicate, among others&mdash;we have been sent to bring retribution to your world.|Soran Keize|<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>}}
The '''D'Aelgoth sector''' was located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]] and the [[Western Reaches]] and was home to over a dozen [[star system]]s. [[Forger (profession)|Forger]] [[Gwellis Bagnoro]] stayed on the resident astronomical object [[Ogem]] with a [[droid]] that contained valuable information, which left with [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] between [[9 BBY]] and [[5 BBY]]. The [[grand-engineer]] [[Lony Coleema]] dealt information in the D'Aelgoth sector's [[asteroid]] outpost [[Z-LOQ]], where she was then captured by the [[bounty hunter]] [[IG-88B]].

Around [[1 ABY]], the [[Galactic Empire]] sent its [[wreckage-prison]] [[Accresker Jail]] [[Skirmish aboard Accresker Jail|colliding]] with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]-sympathising [[planet]] [[Tiferep Major]] in the [[sector]]. In [[5 ABY]], [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] of the Empire ordered for Imperial assets to travel to the D'Aelgoth sector.

==Description==
[[File:Tiferep-SWT.png|thumb|right|300px|The Chroman system and Tiferep Major were located in the D'Aelgoth sector.]]
The D'Aelgoth sector was located in an intersecting portion of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western Reaches" /> Among its [[star system]]s were the [[Chroman system|Chroman]], [[Feenix system|Feenix]], and [[Tallaso system]]s at the coordinates K-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], and the [[Argul system|Argul]], [[Mima system|Mima]], [[New Cylimba system|New Cylimba]], [[Ogem system|Ogem]], [[Selenius system|Selenius]], [[Sinded system|Sinded]], [[Tarsa system|Tarsa]], [[Tiferep Major system|Tiferep Major]], [[Vandron system|Vandron]], and [[Z-LOQ system]]s at the coordinates L-17.<ref name="Systems" /> The Tiferep Major, Chroman,<ref name="Galaxy">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> and Ogem systems were linked together by a [[hyperspace route]] that eventually connected to the [[Corellian Trade Spine]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref>

The [[sector]] included<ref name="Systems" /> the astronomical object [[Feenix]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> as well as the [[asteroid]] outpost [[Z-LOQ]]<ref name="Rebel Bounty">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|annual=2020|story=A Rebel Bounty}}</ref> and the terrestrial [[planet]]s<ref name="TFAVG">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref><ref name="Aphra 23" /> [[Ogem]]<ref name="TFAVG" /> and [[Tiferep Major]],<ref name="Aphra 23" /> which were all located respectively in their eponymous star systems.<ref name="Systems" />

==History==
In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] ordered for Imperial assets to be directed to the D'Aelgoth sector. The [[Yomo Council]], a faction that had formerly been part of the Empire, refused to follow Sloane's order, and [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]] of the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] subsequently listed it among a number of "treasonous actions" by the Yomo Council before his [[Imperial Military|forces]] proceeded to [[Destruction of Fedovoi End|destroy]] the council's forces at the planet [[Fedovoi End]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|Hunters! The mighty Jabba has placed a bounty of twenty-five thousand credits on a ''grand engineer''&hellip; Last spotted on asteroid outpost Z-LOQ.|translated=1|The [[Twi'lek]] [[Bib Fortuna]], on behalf of Jabba|<ref name="Rebel Bounty" />}}
In the [[Fall of the Old Republic|late days]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], Feenix was represented by [[Senator]] [[Tanner Cadaman]] in the Republic's [[Galactic Senate]]. Shortly after the Republic [[Proclamation of the New Order|transitioned]] into the Galactic Empire in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> the new faction [[Senatorial arrests|arrested]] Cadaman and sixty-two other senators for their [[Delegation of 2,000|involvement]] in the [[Petition of 2,000]] against the powers of the ascended [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> The Empire ran [[Chroman Labor Pits|labor facilities]]<ref name="Captain">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Captain Fatulaine"</ref> in the Chroman system<ref name="Systems" /> where the Imperial [[Captain]] [[Fatulaine]] secretly began to free [[prisoner]]s from the facility after working there for three [[Standard day|days]].<ref name="Captain" />
	
[[File:DepartingOgem-SWAFCBD2019.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Han Solo and Chewbacca depart Ogem.]]
During the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the Empire, the [[Forger (profession)|forger]] [[Gwellis Bagnoro]] stayed on Ogem, keeping an [[NP-unit]] [[droid]] that held the coordinates of [[Pirate Queen]] [[Rane Mahal]]'s secret treasure, secretly Mahal herself looking to escape the<ref name="SWA 2019">[[Star Wars Adventures Free Comic Book Day 2019|''Star Wars Adventures'' Free Comic Book Day 2019]]</ref> planet<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Sakreen]]. The [[pirate]] [[Gwarm]], as well as the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]], separately arrived to take the droid<ref name="SWA 2019" /> some time between [[9 BBY]] and [[5 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Solo and Chewbacca ultimately got away with the droid after Gwarm, who had incapacitated Bagnoro, was attacked by the forger's pet, [[Izby]].<ref name="SWA 2019" /> During the [[New Republic Era|time]] of the [[New Republic]], the [[junk dealer]] [[Unkar Plutt]] sold salvage from the planet [[Jakku]] to [[spacer]]s on Ogem.<ref name="TFAVG" /> The [[scavenger]] [[Miggs McKane]] was said to have retired to Ogem after selling off a valuable [[datacore]] he found in an Imperial [[Star Destroyer]] on Jakku.<ref name="RSG">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''</ref>

Between [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="RB date">"[[A Rebel Bounty]]" takes place after [[Alexsandr Kallus]]'s betrayal in "[[Zero Hour]]" but before [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] death in {{Film|VI}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' date to [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]] respectively.</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Fulcrum|operative]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]] and the [[protocol droid]] [[Dee-Four]] met with the [[grand-engineer]] [[Lony Coleema]] on Z-LOQ and received information on some of the Empire's new technological capabilities. Meanwhile, several [[bounty hunter]]s searched for the grand-engineer, with the droid [[IG-88B]] ultimately capturing her and taking her away before two Imperial Star Destroyers arrived in search for her.<ref name="Rebel Bounty" />

Around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="1 ABY">{{Aphra2016Date|23}}</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the Alliance, the population of Tiferep Major was considered by the Empire to be sixty-seven percent sympathetic to the Alliance. The Empire subsequently sent its [[wreckage-prison]] [[Accresker Jail]] on a [[Skirmish aboard Accresker Jail|collision course]] to the world.<ref name="Aphra 23" /> Rebel forces were evacuated from Tiferep Major before the prison hit the planet, devastating it.<ref name="Aphra 25">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 25|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 25]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The D'Aelgoth sector was first mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the final installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy, which was written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> Tiferep Major's placement in the D'Aelgoth sector was established through the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," which was released alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Systems" />&mdash;both works having been written by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> This placement retconned the D'Aelgoth sector's first appearance to the [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 23|twenty-third issue]] of the [[2016]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Simon Spurrier]], illustrated by [[Kev Walker]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 23" /> on [[August 22]], [[2018]].<ref name="Marvel 23">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/69597/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_23|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #23}}</ref>

[[File:DAelgoth-EAOC.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The D'Aelgoth sector, as depicted in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]]
In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[D'Aelgoth sector/Legends|sector]] was first mentioned in the [[1997]] novel ''[[The Hutt Gambit]]'', the second volume of ''[[Star Wars: The Han Solo Trilogy]]'', written by [[A. C. Crispin]].<ref name="Hutt Gambit">''[[The Hutt Gambit]]''</ref> The StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] Legends reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry, later placed the [[Selenius system/Legends|Selenius system]] in the D'Aelgoth [[Sector/Legends|sector]],<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> thereby establishing the introduction of the celestial body [[Selenius VII]] in "[[Almost Had 'Im]]," a short companion narrative authored by [[Rick D. Stuart]] and included in the [[1994]] [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' sourcebook ''[[Galaxy Guide 10: Bounty Hunters]]'', as the de facto first appearance of the D'Aelgoth sector.<ref name="GG10">{{WEGCite|story=Almost Had 'Im|book=Galaxy Guide 10: Bounty Hunters}}</ref> In [[2011]], the sector was given its first visual depiction on a map designed by [[Modi]] and published in the Online Companion feature "Essential Atlas Extra: Sectors of the Mid Rim."<ref name="MidRimOriginal">{{SWArchive|url=atlas/midrim/index.html|text=Essential Atlas Extra: Sectors of the Mid Rim}}</ref>
