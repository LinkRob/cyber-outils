The '''''Dauntless''''' was an<ref name="Battlefront II" /> [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Note" /> that was in service of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was commanded by [[Moff]] [[Derrek Raythe|Raythe]], and carried experimental [[Climate disruption array|satellites]] crucial to [[Operation: Cinder]] following the [[Battle of Endor]]. Due to this, the ''Dauntless'' participated in the [[attack on Fondor]] while docked at an [[Imperial dockyard]], during which the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] attempted to hijack the [[Starship|vessel]] after it was bombarded with [[ion cannon]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Dauntless'' first appeared in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'', a [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] that was developed by [[DICE]], [[Motive Studios]], and [[Criterion Games]] and published by [[Electronic Arts]] on [[November 17]], [[2017]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> The ''Dauntless'' shares its name with the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Dauntless (Imperial I-class)|''Dauntless'']]<ref name="Rogue One VD">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> that appeared in the [[2016]] live-action film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' depicts the ''Dauntless'' with an image used to represent an ''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer by the "[[Starships of the Empire and the Rebellion]]" issue of [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']].<ref name="Note" />
