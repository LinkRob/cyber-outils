The '''''Fang''-class ''Protectorate'' fighter''', also known as '''Fang fighter''', '''Protectorate fighter''', or as the '''Protector Fang Fighter''', was a starfighter manufactured by [[SoroSuub Corporation]] and [[MandalMotors]] and used by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Journeyman Protector|Protectors]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:Zealous_Recruit_Protectorate_Starfighter_XWM.png|thumb|left|250px|The Fang fighter was equally lethal in atmospheric and space combat.]]
Fang fighters were fast<ref name="ProtectorateExpansion" /> and highly maneuverable<ref name="ConcordDawnVeteran">{{FFGXW|set=Protectorate Starfighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Concord Dawn Veteran}}</ref> [[starship]] designed specifically for combat. The craft's narrow attack profile<ref name="ConcordDawnAce" /> and swivel-mounted wings that provided vectored-thrust control gave the fighter unmatched agility.<ref name="ConcordDawnVeteran" />

The [[Tooth|Fang]] fighter was designed and manufactured by [[MandalMotors]],<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> and [[SoroSuub Corporation]]<ref name="Dawn" /> Possessing a frame that was considered elegant yet aggressive in design, the Fang fighter had a thin, angular silhouette and a cranked-delta platform that gave the vessel an unmistakable profile. The fighter's fuselage took the form of a small, elongated hexagon with a low-profile canopy that covered the vessel's midship-mounted [[cockpit]]; the starfighter's aft section was dominated by a single, large, high-output [[ion engine]]. Two articulated [[S-foils|S-foil]] wings were mounted to the fighter's fuselage, each capable of rotating and able to be raised or lowered, and possessed of a large, additional ion [[engine]]. The Fang fighter featured a sophisticated vectored thrust system, advanced flight controls, and automated vernier thrusters.<ref name="No Disintegrations" />

Fang fighters could accommodate a single [[pilot]],<ref name="Fearlessness" /> with no room for other passengers, and enough consumables for one<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> to three [[standard day]]s.<ref name="Dawn" /> They possessed an array of close range [[sensor]]s, a class 2 [[hyperdrive]], and an onboard [[navigation computer]]. The Fang fighter's S-foil wings housed the majority of the starfighter's weapon systems, which comprised of a pair of forward-mounted [[laser cannon]]s. Just forward of the cockpit, mounted in the fighter's underside, was a retractable [[Proton torpedo launcher|torpedo launcher]] that carried a single [[proton torpedo]], offering the vessel a degree of tactical flexibility.<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> The hidden launcher allowed a pilot to launch a devastating salvo before their quarry realized the vessel was carrying ordnance.<ref name="ZealousRecruit" /> Fang fighters typically cost between 120,000<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> to 185,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Dawn" />

==Role==
[[File:Fearlessness_XWM.png|thumb|left|250px|A Mandalorian Protector in the cockpit of a Fang fighter]]
The Fang fighter was custom-built for [[starfighter combat]],<ref name="ProtectorateExpansion" /> and the vessel's narrow attack profile<ref name="ConcordDawnAce" /> and pivot wing technology could be used to achieve unmatched agility,<ref name="ConcordDawnVeteran" /> making the Fang fighter well suited to executing [[Death|deadly]] head-on charges such as the [[Concordia Face Off]] maneuver.<ref name="ConcordDawnAce" /> The Fang fighter was designed to build upon the successes of the [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|''Kom'rk''-class fighter/transport]], a model considered to be the Fang fighter's spiritual older sibling.<ref name="No Disintegrations" />

==History==
The Fang fighter was manufactured by MandalMotors<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> and SoroSuub Corporation<ref name="Dawn" /> during the the last years of the [[Galactic Republic]], and during this time it found significant use among the forces of the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]] of [[Concord Dawn]], who purchased the majority of the line. This led to the Fang fighter becoming known colloquially as the "Protectorate fighter."<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> Favored by the [[Concord Dawn Protectorate]]'s elite pilots,<ref name="ConcordDawnAce" /> Fang fighters became a common sight throughout the [[Concord Dawn system]] and across the [[Mandalore sector]].<ref name="No Disintegrations" />

[[File:Databank-Fang-Fighters.png|thumb|right|250px|Mandalorian Protectors pilot Fang Fighters in defense of the [[Concord Dawn system]].]]
All [[Mandalorian]] Protector Fang [[pilot]]s were personally trained by [[Fenn Rau]], [[Skull Leader|leader]] of [[Skull Squadron]] during the Clone Wars and eventually leader of the Protectors during the Age of the Empire.<ref name="databank" /> The ships were deadly weapons when piloted by these Mandalorians.<ref name="Kanan 10" /> While most Mandalorians took a neutral stance the Clone Wars,<ref name="The Mandalore Plot">{{TCW|The Mandalore Plot}}</ref> Skull Squadron fought with the Republic and participated in the [[Third Battle of Mygeeto]], during which they assisted Republic forces led by Jedi Master [[Depa Billaba]] and her apprentice, [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]].<ref name="Kanan 10" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], Fang fighters piloted by the Protectors encountered and easily destroyed an entire unit of [[Phoenix Cell]]'s [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s. After Rau's capture, the Mandalorian Protectors reluctantly allowed the Phoenix Cell through their territory,<ref name="Protector" /> resulting in the [[Imperial Super Commando]]s of [[Clan Saxon]] eventually arriving and massacring all present. Afterward, Rau himself allied himself with the rebellion.<ref name="Imperial Super Commandos">{{Rebels|Imperial Supercommandos}}</ref>

[[File:ClanWrenVolunteer-SWZ93.png|thumb|250px|left|A Fang fighter in service of Clan Wren]]
After some time, a [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|civil war]] ignited in Mandalorian society between [[Clan Wren]], [[House Kryze]] and [[House Vizsla]] against Clan Saxon and the [[Galactic Empire]]. Clan Wren dispatched a contingent of Fang fighters to support the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Battle of Atollon]] against the Galactic Empire, and were vital to the Alliance's survival.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

Several Fang fighters were sold to other parties on the open market, and a number of former Protectorate fighters came into the possession of the [[Black Sun]], a criminal syndicate that subsequently resold the hard to obtain vessels to collectors and [[mercenary]] pilots.<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> Over time, two Fang fighters came into the possession of [[Kad Solus]]<ref name="KadSolus">{{FFGXW|set=Protectorate Starfighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Kad Solus}}</ref> and [[Teroch]].<ref name="OldTeroch">{{FFGXW|set=Protectorate Starfighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Old Teroch}}</ref>

[[File:SWC_Fang_Fighter_hangar.png|thumb|250px|right|Alliance Fang fighters]]
At some point following the [[Battle of Yavin]], the Fang fighter became a standard starfighter asset of the Rebel Alliance, who had acquired Fang fighters for use as powerful anti-vehicle air support during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They were prominently used during the [[Battle for Tatooine]] and similar operations launched by the [[Tatooine rebel cell]].<ref name="Commander" /> After the people of Mandalore were forced to abandon their world in the [[Great Purge of Mandalore]], Fang fighters formed part of the [[Mandalorian fleet]]<ref name="Chapter 23" /> assembled by [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] during the [[New Republic Era]] to [[Conflict on Mandalore|retake]] Mandalore.<ref name="Chapter 17">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}}</ref> Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="c. 9 Date">{{MandoDate|23}}</ref> after Kryze [[Mission to Plazir-15|reclaimed control]] of her fleet, she ordered its combined forces to join with the [[Children of the Watch]]-aligned [[The Tribe|Mandalorian clan]] on the planet [[Nevarro]], where the combined tribes met before the fleet journeyed to Mandalore.<ref name="Chapter 23" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' as taking place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must take place in [[35 ABY]].</ref> several ''Fang''-class fighters were part of a [[Citizens' Fleet|fleet]] assembled by [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] to assist the [[Resistance]] during the [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]] over the [[planet]] [[Exegol]]. At least two Fang fighters were struck with [[Darth Sidious]]' [[Force lightning]] which disabled until they were able to regain power after Sidious was distracted and killed by the [[Jedi]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

In a December [[2016]] update for the mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'', Fang fighters became available for use by players in their bases.
