'''Cora Vessora''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[witch]] from an [[Unidentified planet (Cora Vessora)|unidentified]] [[planet]]. The [[Jedi]] wished for her to join their ranks, but she refused to join them or the [[Sith]]. The Sith retaliated by [[Death|killing]] her loved ones while the Jedi idly stood by and did nothing. Vessora vowed revenge on both parties, and her story was later put into the form of an [[opera]] piece titled ''[[The Cantata of Cora Vessora]]''.<ref name="ALD" />
