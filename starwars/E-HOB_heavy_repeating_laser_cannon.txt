'''E-HOB heavy repeating laser cannon''' was a heavy repeating blaster cannon. It was based on the [[Imperial Era]] [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]]<ref name="CGttSWG" /> and acted as its successor, with [[Moff]] [[Gideon]] identifying it as part of the overall "E-Web" series. An [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] led by Gideon utilized the blaster on [[Nevarro]]<ref name="Chapter 8" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date Note">{{MandoDate|8}}</ref> During the battle [[Din Djarin]] took control of the E-HOB cannon until Gideon caused it to explode with his [[D-16 blaster pistol]]. The cannon was able to be utilized while detached from its [[turret]] body.<ref name="Chapter 8" /> Another E-HOB was used on [[Tython]] where it was destroyed by [[Boba Fett]]'s [[DB-41 sporting blaster pistol]].<ref name="Chapter 14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[E-Web(15) Heavy Repeating Blaster]] was a newer model of the E-Web that was in use during the [[Thrawn campaign]] in [[9 ABY/Legends|9 ABY]].<ref name="The Last Command Sourcebook">''[[The Last Command Sourcebook]]''</ref>
