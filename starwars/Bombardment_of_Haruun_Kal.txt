In the aftermath of the [[Clone Wars]], an '''insurrection''' took place in the highland [[jungle]]s of the [[planet]] [[Haruun Kal]]. The [[Korun]] natives mounted a guerilla campaign against the [[Imperial Army|ground forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], which struggled to cope with the harsh indigenous environment of [[fungus]] and [[fever wasp]]s. Eventually, the Imperial [[Imperial Navy|fleet]] resorted to [[orbital bombardment]] to suppress the insurrection, resulting in a decisive victory that would contribute to the reputation of the fledgling Imperial Navy.

==Prelude==
[[File:BillabaVsGrievous-Kanan11.png|thumb|right|270px|Depa Billaba and General Grievous (both pictured) led their armies and clashed on Haruun Kal during the Clone War.]]
[[Haruun Kal]] had previously been a battlefield during the [[Clone Wars]], between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The Republic [[Clone trooper|clone]] [[Depa Billaba's battalion|forces]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Depa Billaba]] had been decisively [[Battle of Haruun Kal|defeated]] by the [[Separatist Droid Army]] under [[General]] [[Grievous]], suffering ninety percent [[Death|casualties]].<ref name="First Blood III">[[Kanan 9|''Kanan'' 9]]</ref>

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Clone Wars were brought to an end with the abrupt dissolution of the Confederacy of Independent Systems, the deactivation of the droid armies, and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|reorganization]] of the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> Nevertheless, its immediate aftermath saw the continuation of several smaller conflicts, and in areas with [[Separatist holdouts]] such as the [[Western Reaches]], the Empire attempted to [[Pacification operations in the Western Reaches|pacify]] the insurrections.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> On Haruun Kal, the insurrection by the native [[Korun]] people was another conflict left over from the Clone Wars, necessitating the intervention of the might of the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="Final Order" />

==Campaign==
===Ground assault===
In an effort to suppress the insurrection, the Empire first landed [[Imperial Army|ground forces]]. Imperial orbital fire made "smoke circles" in the highland regions of the [[planet]], burning back the jungle and creating clearings for their [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]]. Accompanying the [[stormtrooper]]s was [[Imperial Navy]] liaison was [[Lieutenant]] [[Canonhaus]], of the [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]] [[Swoop (Quasar Fire-class)|''Swoop'']]. However the [[infantry]] ran into problems almost immediately, with the native [[fungus]] grounding the shuttles and [[speeder]]s within two [[Standard day|days]]. Only their armored [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]]s were left operational, with their [[blaster]]s only saved through "obsessive cleaning."<ref name="Final Order" />

The [[Unidentified CO|commanding officer]] of the Imperial ground troops ordered a march to a [[lake]] thirty [[kilometer]]s away. Canonhaus would remember this decision as being tactically unsound, but ascribed the poor judgement to the fact that the CO had [[larva]]e of [[fever wasp]]s growing in her [[brain]]. After the wasps killed her, the formation was stranded in the wet darkness of the Haruun Kal jungle. At nightfall, they would be attacked by Korun natives from the trees using crude [[slugthrower]]s and [[bomb]]s that could penetrate [[stormtrooper armor]]. Though they returned the first attacks with disciplined and accurate fire, the Imperial ground forces eventually resorted to indiscriminately mowing down the jungle with their [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon|E-Web heavy repeating blaster]] emplacements. In the aftermath of these skirmishes, Canonhaus would take it upon himself to confirm the stormtroopers' kills, in an effort to boost morale.<ref name="Final Order" />

===Bombardment===
{{Quote|Now ''that'' was an example of a bombardment well handled.|Canonhaus|<ref name="Final Order" />}}
After the difficulties faced by the ground forces, the Imperial fleet resorted to an [[orbital bombardment]] of the planet's surface, executing [[Base Gamma One]]. Those Imperials remaining on the ground sought shelter in their Juggernauts, which were themselves driven beneath a lake for additional protection; however, the ensuing firestorm boiled the lake off. As the Juggernauts were unable to cool their air fast enough, the heat generated from the bombardment had severe repercussions for many Imperials, causing some to go into convulsions. Eventually, the Empire was able to claim victory, largely because of the orbital bombardment.<ref name="Final Order" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|One of the battles that gave the Imperial Navy its dread reputation.|Canonhaus|<ref name="Final Order" />}}
The suppression of the insurrection was a major contributor to the fearsome reputation earned by the nascent [[Imperial Navy]], despite the harmful effect that the orbital bombardment had on the ground forces. Several [[Standard year|years]] later, during the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Commander]] [[Tian Karmiya]] would remember Haruun Kal as a glorious victory.<ref name="Final Order" />

As late as [[3 ABY]],<ref name="GA" /> the now-[[Captain]] Canonhaus was stricken with traumatic flashbacks to his time on Haruun Kal, including his experiences with the fever wasps and counting enemy corpses. Nevertheless, when discussing the insurrection with Karmiya, he assented to her view of the battle as a proud episode in the history of the navy, even lying that he had helped conduct the bombardment from orbit rather than experiencing it from the surface.<ref name="Final Order" />
