'''Bob Larkin''' was an artist who created the cover to ''[[The Marvel Comics Illustrated Version of Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back]]'', which reprinted Marvel Comics' adaptation of the film (the cover was reprinted numerous times for subsequent releases of the comic adaptation), and the cover to [[Pizzazz 5|''Pizzazz'' 5]] magazine.
