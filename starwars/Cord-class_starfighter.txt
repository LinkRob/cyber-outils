The '''''Cord''-class starfighter''' was a model of [[Starfighter|fighter craft]] used by the [[clone trooper pilot]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the pan-[[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. They were available to the [[Republic Starfighter Corps]] early in the conflict,<ref name="Rise" /> which [[First Battle of Geonosis|started]] in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> alongside other craft like [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Cord''-class starfighters were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[roleplaying game]] sourcebook published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]] for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series of products.<ref name="Rise" /> They first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2002]] young readers book ''[[Boba Fett: Crossfire]]'' by [[Terry Bisson]].<ref name="BF:Cross">''[[Boba Fett: Crossfire]]''</ref>
