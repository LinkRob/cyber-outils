'''Bis Refinery''' was a [[fuel]] [[refinery]] on the [[planet]] [[Savareen]]'s [[Pnakotic Coast]] that did not fall under the jurisdiction of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was damaged by the criminal syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]], but the [[Savarian]]s repaired some of the machinery. They kept their operations limited to not attract unwanted attention, but sometimes refined valuable products such as [[coaxium]] for [[smuggler]]s who stole them from [[Kessel]]. Savarians lived in the ruins of the [[factory]], using its storage silos for housing.<ref name="Solo Guide" /> In [[10 BBY]], the workers helped [[Beckett's gang]] deliver their unrefined coaxium to the refinery.<ref name="Solo novel">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
