The '''''Cantaros''''' was a [[VKCE-7100x Starcutter]] utilized by [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] during the [[High Republic Era]], named for her former [[Padawan]], [[Imri Cantaros]]. Rwoh kept the [[starship]] on [[Coruscant]] during her time as a Master in case she was ever required to leave. In [[132 BBY]], Rwoh and a group of [[Jedi]] used the starship to travel to the [[planet]] [[Khofar]] in order to investigate the aftermath of Master [[Sol]]'s [[Mission to Khofar|mission]]. After returning to Coruscant, Rwoh later traveled to Brendok in the ''Cantaros'' with another Jedi team to follow Sol's [[Transponder code|transponder]].

==Description==
{{Quote|It's time to get back to the ship.|Vernestra Rwoh, to Mog Adana|<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" />}}
The ''Cantaros'' was a [[VKCE-7100x Starcutter]] produced by [[Valkeri-Kuat Consolidated Enterprises]]. An experimental design, the [[starship]] was equipped with an integrated [[hyperdrive]]<ref name="Databank" /> as well as [[engine]]s.<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" />

==History==
{{Quote|Prepare a rescue team to depart for the planet Khofar immediately.|Vernestra Rwoh, to Mog Adana|<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" />}}
[[File:Cantaros-TeachCorrupt.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The ''Cantaros'' docked on Coruscant]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] of the [[Jedi Order]]<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" /> owned the ''Cantaros'', which was named for her former [[Padawan]], [[Imri Cantaros]]. Although Rwoh was unsettled by [[hyperspace]] travel, she kept the vessel on [[Coruscant]] for instances when she had to leave.<ref name="Databank" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> after Rwoh received a [[distress signal]] from Master [[Sol]] following his [[Mission to Khofar|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Khofar]]<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" />&mdash;during which the rest of his team was [[Death|killed]] by the [[Sith]] known as [[the Stranger]],<ref name="Night">{{Acolyte|Night}}</ref> another former pupil of Rwoh's<ref name="The Acolyte" />&mdash;she decided to travel to Khofar herself. Rwoh and a group of [[Jedi]], including<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" /> [[Padawan]]<ref name="Databank Mog">{{Databank|mog-adana|Mog Adana}}</ref> [[Mog Adana]], boarded the ''Cantaros'' at a [[spaceport]] on Coruscant and departed for Khofar. After arriving above the planet&mdash;unknowingly not long after Sol's own [[Polan GX-8|vessel]] departed&mdash;the Jedi landed the ''Cantaros'' in a field on Khofar, disembarking to investigate. With Sol having deactivated his [[Transponder code|transponder]],<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" /> Rwoh and the other Jedi returned to Coruscant.<ref name="The Acolyte" />

[[File:Cantaros-TeachCorrupt.png|thumb|right|250px|The ''Cantaros'' arrives on Khofar]]
When Sol reactivated his transponder after [[Duel on Brendok|arriving]] on the planet [[Brendok]], Adana informed Rwoh, who instructed the Padawan to gather as many [[Jedi Knight]]s as possible while remaining discreet and prepare her ship. Rwoh and the other Jedi traveled to Brendok in the ''Cantaros'', flying past the planet's [[Brendok fortress|fortress]] while Sol [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] the Stranger and the Sith's former acolyte, [[Mae-ho Aniseya]], fought her [[Aniseya family|sister]], [[Verosha Aniseya]], with the vessel's arrival being noticed by the combatants. Landing the ''Cantaros'' in a field surrounded by [[tree]]s, Rwoh disembarked behind the other Jedi before stopping next to the [[entry ramp]], having sensed her former student's presence. On Brendok, Rwoh and the Jedi found Sol dead and Mae with her memories erased. Taking Mae [[prisoner]], the Jedi subsequently returned to Coruscant.<ref name="The Acolyte" />

==Commanders and crew==
{{Quote|Are you joining us on the mission, Master?''"<br />"''I am.|Mog Adana and Vernestra Rwoh, on traveling to Khofar|<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" />}}
The ''Cantaros'' was utilized by Jedi Master Vernestra Rwoh.<ref name="Databank" /> When traveling to Khofar, Rwoh was accompanied by three other Jedi, including Mog Adana.<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" /> A group of ten other Jedi accompanied Rwoh and Adana to Brendok.<ref name="The Acolyte" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Cantaros'' first appeared in "[[Teach / Corrupt]],"<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" /> the sixth episode of the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which aired on [[July 2]], [[2024]].<ref name="T/C Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/the-acolyte/season-1-episode-6-teach-corrupt-episode-guide|text="Teach / Corrupt" - Episode Guide &#124; The Acolyte}}</ref> The vessel was identified in both the [[StarWars.com]] episode guide<ref name="T/C Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=teach-corrupt-trivia-gallery-the-acolyte|text="Teach / Corrupt" Trivia Gallery &#124; The Acolyte}} {{C|Slide 8}}</ref> and [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry<ref name="Databank" /> on the [[July 3|following day]], which confirmed its name's connection to Imri Cantaros from the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="T/C Trivia Gallery" />
