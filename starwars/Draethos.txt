The '''Draethos''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[humanoid]] species.<ref name="NoP" /> A [[Unidentified Nihil Storm|Nihil Storm]] was a member of the [[species]].<ref name="THR 7" /> They possessed massive teeth<ref name="NoP" /> and had [[Color|blue]]-colored skin, black [[eye]]s, and four-fingered hands.<ref name="THR 7" /> By the [[Imperial Era]], a Draethos skeleton still remained sprawled at the feet of his great chair in his office in the [[Chamber of Antiquities]] in the [[Great Jedi Library]] on [[Ossus]], while two [[Ossus Stone Guardian|stone guardians]] stood as silent sentinels to protect the chamber from intruders.<ref name="NoP" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Draethos were first mentioned in the ''[[Nexus of Power]]'', the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' sourcebook,<ref name="NoP" /> and first appeared in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[The High Republic (2021) 7|''The High Republic'' (2021) 7]], written by [[Cavan Scott]], penciled by [[Georges Jeanty]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="THR 7" /> on [[July 28]], [[2021]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/94640/star_wars_the_high_republic_2021_7|text=Star Wars: The High Republic (2021) #7}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Draethos/Legends|Draethos]] were a [[Species/Legends|species]] created by authors [[Kevin J. Anderson]], [[Tom Veitch]] and artist [[Chris Gossett]]<ref name="interview">{{WookCite|url=Wookieepedia:Interview/Kevin_J._Anderson|text=Interview with Kevin J. Anderson}}</ref> for the character [[Odan-Urr/Legends|Odan-Urr]] and first appeared in the comic [[Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith 3|''Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith'' 3]], published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Tales of the Jedi">[[Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith 3|''Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith'' 3]]</ref> on [[December 1]], [[1994]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/93-551/Star-Wars-Tales-of-the-Jedi---Dark-Lords-of-the-Sith-3-of-6|text=Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi - Dark Lords of the Sith #3 (of 6)}}</ref>
