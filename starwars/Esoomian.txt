'''Esoomians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] from the [[Esooma system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].

==Biology and appearance==
This species was distinguished by their [[Color|green]] skin, red [[eye]]s, facial tendrils, and large build, towering over most other sentients.<ref name="SFH 3" />

==Species in the galaxy==
Sometime after [[229 BBY]],<ref name=" SFH 3 Date">The events of ''The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire'' 1 take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> a member of this species served as a member of the [[Nihil]].<ref name="SFH 3" /> The brawler [[Bumblethunk]] was an Esoomian who served the criminal [[Razzi Syndicate]] as "Prowling Thug 36."<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Smuggler's Guide Date">The entry mentioning Bumblethunk in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' takes place chronologically prior to an entry written by [[Tobias Beckett]]. ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' depicts Beckett's [[death]] on [[Savareen]] and ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on Savareen in the film to nine [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the proclamation to [[19 BBY]], meaning Beckett's death must take place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> Bumblethunk had beatdown the [[Gambling|gambler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Landonis Calrissian]] after Calrissian took a swing at the Esoomian in order to impress a [[Trianii]] [[Unidentified Trianii debutante|debutante]]. [[Submister]] [[Twirm]] of the Razzi Syndicate later recorded this event in the [[book]] that would become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Tantor.jpg|thumb|right|200px|An Esoomian in ''Star Wars'' Legends.]]
Esoomians were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', a [[2018]] reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> This species were first pictured in the [[2024]] ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' comic issue, [[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 3|''The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire'' 3]], written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Rachael Stott]].<ref name="SFH 3" /> The [[Esoomian/Legends|species]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were created for the [[1993]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' supplement [[Wanted by Cracken (sourcebook)|''Wanted by Cracken'']], which was written by [[Louis J. Prosperi]].<ref name="WbC">[[Wanted by Cracken (sourcebook)|''Wanted by Cracken'']]</ref>
