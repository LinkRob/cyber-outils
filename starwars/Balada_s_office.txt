'''Balada's office''' was the office of [[Balada]] the [[Hutt]], who ran the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] sports competition on [[Vespaara]]. This office was where Balada stored her archive of relics and [[Clothing|costumes]], sometimes showing them off or lending them to others. The [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Rieve]] received at least two outfits from this collection, the [[Sith Lord (Rieve)|Sith Lord]] and the [[Starcrossed Punk]], and the [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as [[Utooni]] stole their costume, the [[Imperial Salvagers]], from Balada's office as well.<ref name="Hunters" />
