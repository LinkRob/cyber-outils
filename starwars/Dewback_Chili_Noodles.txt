'''Dewback Chili Noodles''' was a meat and [[noodle]] dish served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dewback Chili Noodles is an entrée available at the [[In-universe|real-world]] at Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disneyland]]<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> starting in [[January]] [[2022]].<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2022/01/review-new-dewback-chili-noodles-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge-in-disneyland|text=REVIEW: New Dewback Chili Noodles at Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge in Disneyland|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20220112133839}}</ref> At that time, the dish was not available at the restaurant's [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] location.<ref name="DHS Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/dinner|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Dinner Menu}}</ref> The menu describes the dish as "Spiced Pappardelle Noodles and Gingered Ground Pork with Brussels Sprout Leaves and Shredded Red Cabbage."<ref name="DLP Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref>
