In [[5 ABY]] as the [[Imperial Era]] ended, members of the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] staged a '''revolution''' in [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. The Acolytes attacked the [[city]] so that the new recruit [[Kiza]] and her companion [[Remi]] could steal a [[Kiza's lightsaber|lightsaber]] from the archives underneath the [[Peace and Security Headquarters|Peace and Security station]].

==Prelude==
In the [[Standard month|months]] following the [[Battle of Endor]], a sect that revered the [[Sith]] and the [[dark side of the Force]] emerged on the [[planet]] [[Taris]] known as the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]]. They began collecting [[Sith artifact]]s including what they believed was [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]] to burn in order to reunite with their former Sith owners after [[death]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> Later, other Acolytes including [[Remi]] and the [[Pantoran]] [[Kiza]] also surfaced on the [[Corellian]] [[city]] of [[Coronet City]]. Their activities including tagging walls with the [[Darth Vader's armor|Vader mask]] sigil and the warning "VADER LIVES", stealing [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], slicing into the [[HoloNet]], and attacking [[Corellia]]n [[Peace and Security|peace officers]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

The Acolytes then decided to launch a coordinate attack on Coronet City in order to steal a [[Kiza's lightsaber|lightsaber]] from the archives beneath the city's [[Peace and Security Headquarters|Peace and Security station]].<ref name="Empire's End" /> The archives were part of a shared basement with the [[Unidentified Coronet City museum|museum]] nextdoor.<ref name="Life Debt" /> As part of the plan, the Acolytes falsified [[paper]]s which allowed Kiza to infiltrate the station disguised as a dame from the secretary pool. [[Gender|Her]] lover<ref name="Empire's End" /> Remi also allowed himself to be captured by the [[detective]] [[Erno]], who intended the boy for vandalism. His task was to steal the [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Life Debt" />

==The attack==
Before Erno could interrogate Remi, the security [[officer]] [[Spob Rydel]] informed him that the Acolytes were launching attacks throughout Coronet City. The Acolytes attacked various areas throughout the city including [[Diadem Square]], where they mobbed storefronts and leapt on top of the [[air-tuk]]s to pull the [[speeder]]s down to the ground. Other Acolytes swarmed the 1-line of the [[Hovertrain|mag-lev]] subway, the [[train]] stops at the [[Juni Street Station]], and assaulted pedestrians at the city's [[casino]]s. These Acolytes were armed with red sticks and wore masks.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Erno returned to the interrogation room only to learn that Remi had disappeared. In fact, Remi had retrieved the lightsaber from the basement. After the emergency lights had come up, Erno headed into the main room to join Rydel and the detectives [[Shreen]] and [[Mursey]]. Before he could speak to Rydel, Kiza shot Rydel in the forehead. Kiza then gunned down Shreen and Mursey, killing them. Erno tried to reach for his [[blaster]]. When he asked Kiza what was going on, she replied that she was no longer his [[doll]]. She described the attack as a revolution, the revenge of the darkness, and oblivion.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Erno attempted to shoot Kiza but Remi [[Dismemberment|sliced off]] his hand with his newly-acquired lightsaber. Remi remarked to Kiza that he had told her the weapon was in the basement. After confirming that the blade was the weapon they had been looking for, Kiza clubbed Erno in the head. She then told him that Vader lived and to tell everyone that the Acolytes were coming.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Aftermath==
Following the Coronet City revolution, Kiza and Remi's Acolytes cell traveled to the [[Colonies]] world of [[Devaron]]. There, they launched an [[Attack on a New Republic outpost|attack]] on a [[New Republic]] [[New Republic outpost|outpost]] under the guidance of [[Yupe Tashu]], a former [[Advisor|Adviser]] to the late [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Coronet City revolution first appeared in an interlude chapter in [[Chuck Wendig]]'s [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''. This chapter was told from the point of view of the detective Erno. It was later mentioned in another interlude chapter in [[2017]] sequel novel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', where the events were recollected from the point of view of the Acolyte Kiza.
