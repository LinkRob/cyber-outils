''Star Wars'' is ultimately a realistic fantasy universe, which gives him a chance to design all new creatures, vehicles, etc., while still being very grounded and making it all feel lived-in. That was one of the hallmarks of the Original Trilogy, and it's always been part of Alex's style as well, so it meshes really well.|Charles Soule|<ref name="marvel">{{Marvel|url=news/comics/24460/lando_calrissian_makes_his_way_to_marvel|text=Lando Calrissian Makes His Way to Marvel}}</ref>}}
'''Alex Maleev''' is a [[Wikipedia:Bulgarians|Bulgarian]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] artist who has collaborated with [[Charles Soule]] on the [[Marvel Comics]] limited series [[Star Wars: Lando (comic series)|''Star Wars: Lando'']]. He has also drawn variant covers for the first issues of Marvel's main [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']] series and [[Star Wars: Vader Down|''Vader Down'']] crossover event, as well as for the second issue of the [[Star Wars: Princess Leia|''Princess Leia'']] miniseries.

Maleev is best known for illustrating [[Wikipedia:Brian Michael Bendis|Brian Michael Bendis]]' [[W:c:marvel:Daredevil Vol 2|''Daredevil'']] run, and the two have also collaborated on [[W:c:marvel:Spider-Woman Vol 4|''Spider-Woman'']], [[W:c:marvel:Moon Knight Vol 4|''Moon Knight'']], and [[W:c:marvel:Scarlet Vol 1|''Scarlet'']].
