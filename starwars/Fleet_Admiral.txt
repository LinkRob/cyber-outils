'''Fleet Admiral''' was a high-[[Military rank|ranked]] position in several [[Navy|navies]] in [[the galaxy]], including the [[Republic Navy]], [[Imperial Navy]] and [[New Republic Defense Fleet]]. In the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] Navy, a [[fleet]] [[admiral]] was superior to an admiral and inferior to a [[Grand Admiral]]. However, in the [[New Republic]], the rank was the highest naval position which was in command of the entire starfleet.

==Description==
The [[military rank]] of Fleet Admiral was a [[Navy|naval]] [[military rank]] conferred on [[officer]]s within multiple factions throughout different [[time]]s.<ref name="Life Debt" /> The rank existed in the [[Republic Navy|Galactic Republic Navy]]. The [[Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] also utilized the rank, with holders serving above standard [[admiral]]s<ref name="Thrawn" /> but below [[Grand Admiral|grand admirals]] within the hierarchy.<ref name="Life Debt" /> In the Imperial Navy, fleet admirals had the capacity to serve as the [[commanding officer]]s of multiple [[task force]]s,<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> a [[squadron]],<ref name="Imperial Officers and Agents">{{EncyclopediaCite|Imperial Officers and Agents}}</ref> or a mobile numbered [[fleet]].<ref name="Thrawn" /> The [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] used the rank of fleet admiral for [[Gial Ackbar]],<ref name="Life Debt" /> the leader of the navy.<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> The [[Chiss Ascendancy]] was another government that used the fleet admiral rank within its [[Ascendancy military|armed forces]], falling above a [[Chiss]] admiral, but below the [[Supreme Admiral]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==History==
[[File:AdmiralYularen-MF35.png|thumb|left|150px|Wullf Yularen was a Fleet Admiral]]
During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Rising Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> [[Wullf Yularen]] served as Fleet Admiral<ref name="Announcement EG" /> in the [[Republic Navy]]. In that capacity, Yularen commanded a [[Skywalker's fleet|fleet]] alongside [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]],<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> whom he respected<ref name="Cat & Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref> but often clashed with.<ref name="JediCrash">{{TCW|Jedi Crash}}</ref>

Prior to [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[commander]] of the Galactic Empire's [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|7th Fleet]], [[Sartan]], held the rank, as did [[Jok Donassius]].<ref name="Thrawn" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], [[Kendal Ozzel]] of [[Death Squadron]] used the rank.<ref name="Imperial Officers and Agents" /> [[Firmus Piett]], who replaced Ozzel as the commander of Death Squadron after his execution<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> served as a fleet admiral by the time of the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> The rank of fleet admiral was also given to [[Gallius Rax]], a close [[advisor]] to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. After the [[death]] of Palpatine during the Battle of [[Endor]], Rax promoted Admiral [[Rae Sloane]] to the position for her actions during the [[Rebellion on Akiva]], but later promoted her again to the superior rank of [[Grand Admiral]].<ref name="Life Debt" />

Admiral Gial Ackbar,<ref name="Life Debt" /> who also served as [[supreme fleet commander]] in both the [[New Republic]] and the Alliance to Restore the Republic before it,<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> was specifically identified as a Fleet Admiral by the time of his service in the New Republic Defense Fleet. As such, he was quick to rebuke Sloane identifying him as a "Grand Admiral" as that was an Imperial ranking.<ref name="Life Debt" />
