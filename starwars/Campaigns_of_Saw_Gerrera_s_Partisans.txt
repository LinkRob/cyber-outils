{{Quote|Or the campaigns of Saw Gerrera's Partisans? Can you condone that?|Ransolm Casterfo asks [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] if she can condone the Partisans' so-called terrorist campaigns|<ref name="Bloodline" />}}
The '''campaigns of Saw Gerrera's Partisans''' were a series of campaigns led by [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[Partisans]],<ref name="Bloodline" /> who at one time were affiliated with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] but were eventually [[Resolution of Censure Condemning Saw Gerrera of the Partisans|censured and cut off]] from the Alliance.<ref name="TRF" /> The Partisans' battles resulted in civilian deaths, with it eventually reaching a point where the [[Galactic Empire]] proceeded to make a [[propaganda]] poster [[Rebels Are Terrorists|denouncing]] the Rebel Alliance as a whole as being a [[terrorist]] group in response.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref>

Despite the many operations undertaken against the Empire by Gerrera and his fighters,<ref name="Bloodline" /> [[Agent]] [[Del Meeko]] regarded the Partisans as a minor threat in the grand scheme of things.<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> Gerrera's final campaign was [[Jedha insurgency|his fight for the control]] of [[Jedha City]] on [[Jedha]]. Gerrera and most of his Partisans were killed when Jedha City [[Destruction of Jedha City|was destroyed]] in the first test of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> The campaign was later used by [[Senator]] [[Ransolm Casterfo]] as an example of the so-called terrorist tactics of the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Bloodline" />

==History==
===Prelude===
[[File:OnderonianRebellion-WOTF.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Gerrera and fellow rebels during the Clone Wars]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Jedi]] trained various militias in fighting the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]]. One of these militias were the [[Onderon rebels|rebels]] of [[Onderon]] led by former [[Senator]] [[Lux Bonteri]], [[Steela Gerrera]] and her brother [[Saw Gerrera|Saw]].<ref name="A War on 2 Fronts">{{TCW|A War on Two Fronts}}</ref> For three [[Standard year|years]],<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> a [[Onderonian Civil War|civil war]] was fought on [[Onderon]] between the Separatist forces under [[Monarch of Onderon|King]] [[Sanjay Rash]] and the rebels. Together, with the aid from [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]<ref name="Front Runners">{{TCW|Front Runners}}</ref> the rebels were able to force the [[Droid]] [[Army]] to withdraw from Onderon and Dendup was restored to the [[throne]]. Onderon then rejoined the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Tipping Points">{{TCW|Tipping Points}}</ref>

===Imperial Era===
====First battles====
Following the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformation]] into the [[Galactic Empire]], Saw reorganized his rebels into the [[Partisans]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> His cell was among the first [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] cells formed.<ref name="Ghosts" /> When the Empire [[Onderon insurgency|occupied]] [[Onderon]], the partisans fought a [[Alexsandr Kallus' squad|squad]] under [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]], with a [[Lasat]] [[Unidentified Lasat mercenary|mercenary]] [[Death|killing]] all but Kallus.<ref name="Honorable Ones" />

Gerrera and several of his partisans then [[Salient Campaign|fought]] the Empire in the [[Salient system]] for several [[Standard week|weeks]]. Only Gerrera was able to make his escape.<ref name="Catalyst" />

====Kashyyyk operation====
{{Quote|Everyone! These have been hard years. We've lost comrades, friends, family... to the Empire. Dark times. And yet the fire... still burns. Hope still burns. The Jedi are not yet lost. We are not yet lost. Kashyyyk is not yet lost! For the cause!|Saw Gerrera|<ref name="Fallen Order" />}}
By [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Fallen Order Date">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' takes place five [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Great Jedi Purge]], which is dated to [[19 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'', in which the Mantis crew appears, is set in [[14 BBY]].</ref> Gerrera and the Partisans were engaged with Imperial forces on [[Kashyyyk]]. Using numerous [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic gunships]], Gerrera's forces assaulted an [[Imperial Refinery]] from both air and ground. With the help of [[Padawan|Jedi Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]], the attack was successful and the refinery was captured. However, an Imperial counterattack later overran the Partisans and pushed them off the planet. Though Saw believed Kashyyyk to be a lost cause, some Partisans such as [[Mari Kosan]] chose to remain and fight with the Wookiees. Unable to convince them otherwise, Gerrera and the Partisans abandoned them.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

====Rescuing Jyn Erso====
In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Galen Captured">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' places the capture of [[Galen Walton Erso]] to six [[Standard year|years]] after the end of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places in [[19 BBY]].</ref> Gerrera and his partisans took in [[Jyn Erso]] when her [[Galen Walton Erso|father]] was [[Capture of Galen Erso|taken]] and [[Lyra Erso|mother]] was killed. For the next eights years,<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> Erso became one of the best [[soldier]]s of the partisans. Together, she undertook missions [[Mission to Horuz|at]] [[Horuz]] [[Mission to Inusagi|and]] [[Inusagi]] with them. During their [[Mission to Tamsye Prime|mission]] to [[Tamsye Prime]],<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> Gerrera and his partisans abandoned her for her safety.<ref name="Rogue One" /> The [[Cloud-Riders]], who had started their own [[Campaigns of the Cloud-Riders|crusade against the Empire]], gave shipment of [[coaxium]] to Gerrera and Jyn in the aftermath of the [[showdown on Savareen]].<ref name="Solo novelization" />

====Strikes against the Empire====
Gerrera and his Partisans were hired by [[Arane Oreida]] to infiltrate the [[Sakoola blossom festival]] on [[Inusagi]]. The Partisans massacred the festival and the overcome involved the deaths of [[Maia]], [[Governor]] [[Cor Tophervin]], the [[Unidentified chieftess of Inusagi (Saw Gerrera)|Inusagi chieftess]], several Imperials and many Inusagi civilians. Some of Gerrera's members left the Partisans for less extreme rebel groups.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="3 BBY date note">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] birth in [[19 BBY]]. The events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' commence just after Organa's sixteenth [[birthday]], which places the events of the novel in [[3 BBY]].</ref> the Partisans successfully planted a bomb in the [[Quarsh Panaka's chalet|chalet]] belonging to Imperial Moff [[Quarsh Panaka]] on [[Onoam]], one of [[Naboo]]'s [[moon]]s. Naboo was the homeworld of Emperor Palpatine and Panaka had great personal loyalty to the Emperor. The bomb exploded and killed Panaka along with his household and came close to killing [[Queen]] [[Dalné]] of Naboo and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] who were standing on the steps of the chalet when the bomb went off having just left a meeting with the moff to discuss the working conditions of the [[miner]]s of Onoam. However, the Partisans successful killing of Panaka also indirectly saved Organa's life as Panaka had just figured out that she was the long lost daughter of [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and was about to inform the Emperor. Leia's adoptive mother Queen [[Breha Organa]] said that Leia was the one in the most danger, more than she could have known. Despite this, Panaka's assassination angered the other rebel leaders, including Breha, as Panaka was the highest ranking Imperial that they had hope of someday contacting and maybe even working with and with Panaka dead that option was no longer available to them.<ref name="PoA" />

[[File:Saw_Gerrera_2BBY.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Gerrera during his operation to investigate Geonosis]]
In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Gerrera and a team of his partisans were sent by Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] and [[Alliance High Command|Rebel Command]] to [[Mission to investigate Geonosis|investigate]] the [[Sterilization of Geonosis|disappearance]] of the [[Geonosian]]s, who had previously been under [[Second occupation of Geonosis|Imperial occupation]]. However, Gerrera's team was wiped out by repaired [[B1-series battle droid]]s and [[droideka]]s. Only Saw was still alive when the [[Spectres]] arrived to get him and his information on this. With the Spectres, Gerrera learned that Empire had used [[poison]] [[gas]] to wipe all but one Geonosian, [[Klik-Klak]] out because of something the Empire was [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|building]] with the Geonosians help.<ref name="Ghosts" />

At [[Mon Mothma]]'s urging, the Partisans were [[Resolution of Censure Condemning Saw Gerrera of the Partisans|censured]] from the growing Rebel Alliance.<ref name="TRF">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="1 BBY date note">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e8-trivia-gallery|text="Narkina 5" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 6}} dates the events of "[[In the Name of the Rebellion]]" to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Gerrera and his partisans had indeed broken off with the Alliance and Mothma, starting their own private war against the Empire, killing civilians as well Imperials. Gerrera and [[Edrio]] undertook a [[Mission to Jalindi|mission]] to destroy an Imperial [[Jalindi Station|relay station]] on [[Jalindi]]. Gerrera and Edrio were successful in destroying it, the command cruiser [[Marauder (Arquitens-class command cruiser)|''Marauder'']], and rescuing Spectres [[Sabine Wren]], [[Ezra Bridger]], and [[C1-10P|Chopper]].<ref name="ITNOTR" />

Gerrera, Edrio, Wren, and Bridger then went to [[Faos Station]] to [[Mission to Faos Station|investigate]] an Imperial shipment. Together, they infiltrated [[Freighter 2716]]. Gerrera, Wren, and Bridger found several technicians who were being held captive. Gerrera learned from the technicians that the other cargo came from the [[Jedha system]]. He and the spectres learned that main cargo was a [[kyber crystal]]. At first, Gerrera wanted to follow the shipment to the [[Tonnis sector]] to discover what the Empire was building. However, the crystal was to be taken aboard Captain [[Slavin]]'s [[Slavin's Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]]. Gerrera decided to destroy the crystal and make his escape with Edrio. Only Bridger, Wren, Chopper and the technicians survived the crystal's explosion.<ref name="ITNOTR" />

====War on Jedha====
{{Main|Jedha insurgency}}
Gerrera and his Partisans later traveled to [[Jedha]], and established their base in the [[Catacombs of Cadera]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> There, they [[Jedha insurgency|attacked]] several kyber shipments from the [[Jedha's kyber mines|mines]] and [[Jedha City]]'s [[Temple of the Kyber]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> A team of Partisans under [[Beezer Fortuna]] then captured a defected Imperial [[pilot]], [[Bodhi Rook]].<ref name="Rogue One Part I">[[Rogue One Adaptation 1|''Rogue One Adaptation'' 1]]</ref> During this [[time]], one of the Partisans, [[Tivik]], kept in contact with the Rebel Alliance and [[Mission to the Ring of Kafrene|informed]] [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service]], Captain [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] of the pilot's capture and rumors of a Imperial planet killer. However, Andor was forced to eliminate Tivik when stormtroopers found them on the [[Ring of Kafrene]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

Beezer, Edrio, [[Benthic]] and the other Partisans brought Rook to before Gerrera. Despite Rook's claiming he was sent by [[Galen Walton Erso]] with a message, Gerrera decided to put him with [[Bor Gullet]] and [[torture]] the truth out of him. The Partisans then [[Battle on Jedha|attacked]] a [[TX-225 GAVw "Occupier" combat assault tank|combat assault tank]] loaded with kyber [[crystal]]s. After the [[Guardians of the Whills]], [[Baze Malbus]] and [[Chirrut Îmwe]], took the last of the escort, the Partisans then took them, [[Jyn Erso]], and Andor back to their base. There, Gerrera showed Jyn the message from her father. At that moment, Governor [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], ordered the Holy [[City]] to be [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]] by the Death Star. Many of the partisans, including Gerrera, were killed in the aftershock of the Holy City's destruction.<ref name="Rogue One" />

Following the [[Battle of Yavin|Battle]] of [[Yavin 4]], the Partisans under [[Benthic]]<ref name="Star Wars 39" /> continued to fight the Empire when they continued to take the kyber crystals from Jedha.<ref name="Star Wars 38" />
