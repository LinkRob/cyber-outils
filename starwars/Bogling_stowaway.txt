A [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Battle Scars" /> '''bogling''' living on the [[planet]] [[Bogano]] encountered the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]]<ref name="JFO" /> in [[14 BBY]].<ref name="Fallen Order date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> The [[bogling]] was trapped behind a broken door below the landing area of Kestis' [[Starship|transport]], the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'', on Bogano.<ref name="JFO" /> She<ref name="Battle Scars" /> was rescued by Kestis, who gave the creature a new home aboard the vessel. She was initially a source of consternation to the ''Mantis''{{'s}} [[pilot]], [[Greez Dritus]], who frequently blamed the stowaway for mishaps aboard.<ref name="JFO" />

Eventually, Dritus warmed up to the bogling; she was adopted by the whole crew and lived in her home vent when not roaming the ship. She sometimes answered the [[droid]] [[BD-1]]'s beeps with grumbles. BD-1 enjoyed running around with her and they had their own game that involved chasing each other around the [[holotable]]. The bogling and BD-1 stuck together when there was tension between the organic crew members. Before the [[Kadas'sa'Nikto]] [[engineer]] [[Irei]] left the ship on [[Zimara]], one of her gifts was a toy for the bogling.<ref name="Battle Scars" />
