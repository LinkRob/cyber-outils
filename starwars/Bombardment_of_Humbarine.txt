During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], there was heavy fighting in the [[Humbarine sector]] including the '''bombardment''' of the [[sector]]'s [[capital]], [[Humbarine]], by [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]]. The [[orbital bombardment]] left the [[Planet|world]], which was represented in the Republic's [[Galactic Senate]], utterly seared.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the bombardment of Humbarine was first mentioned in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Emily Shkoukani]].<ref name="Dawn" /> The [[Bombardment of Humbarine/Legends|event]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2005]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', which was written by [[Curtis Saxton]].<ref name="ROTS ICS">''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
