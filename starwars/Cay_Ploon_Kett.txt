==Behind the scenes==
The name "Cay Ploon Kett" first appeared in "[[Rebel Resolve]]," the twelfth episode of [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|the first season]] of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. Strikingly similar to "[[Kilian Plunkett|K. Plunkett]]," the name was written in [[Aurebesh]] in a prisoner data file.<ref name="Rebel Resolve" />
