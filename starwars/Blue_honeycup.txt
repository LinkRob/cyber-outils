'''Blue honeycup''' was a type of [[tea]] that was served at [[Café Raduli]] in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. It was potable by [[Caskadag]]s. The Caskadag [[assassin]] [[Anglang Lehet]] considered drinking it to be one of his few indulgences.<ref name="Rules of the Game" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/4-reasons-canto-bight-is-an-essential-read|text=4 Reasons ''Canto Bight'' Is an Essential Read}} states that the events of [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']] take place a few [[Standard day|days]] before the events of {{Film|VIII}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref> he drank a cup at Café Raduli, enjoying the tea's piquant sweetness. Lehet later set the tea down when he left the café to follow the [[Wermal]] [[VaporTech]] salesbeing [[Kedpin Shoklop]].<ref name="Rules of the Game" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blue honeycup first appeared in "[[Rules of the Game]]," a short story written by [[Saladin Ahmed]] for the [[2017]] novella collection [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']].<ref name="Rules of the Game" />
