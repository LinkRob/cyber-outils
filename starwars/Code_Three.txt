In the [[First Order military|military]] of the [[First Order]], a '''Code Three''' was indicative of a security breach in its bases.<ref name="Station to Station" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|2|11}}</ref> when the [[Resistance]] infiltrators [[Kazuda Xiono]] and [[Neeku Vozo]] were caught in the [[Engineering (Titan)|engineering section]] of the First Order [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|refueling station]] ''[[Titan]]'', a Code Three was reported to [[Commander]] [[Pyre]] by an onboard [[Unidentified First Order security officer|security officer]].<ref name="Station to Station" />
