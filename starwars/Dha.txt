'''Dha''' was a [[Standard month/Legends|month]] in a nine-month [[Unidentified calendar (Raspar system)|calendar]] used on a [[Raspar system/Legends|Raspar system]] [[Unidentified planet (West Fissure)|world]]. During this month, citizens could expect incandescent [[Solar flare/Legends|solar flares]] to etch the surface of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]]. At some point, a weather forecast reminded citizens of [[mind eel]]s, which were provoked by the solar flares. Dha was preceded by [[Neevlit]] and was followed by [[Bababbb]].<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dha was mentioned in a one-page weather forecast titled "Region 29: The West Fissure," written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that collects concept designs and photographs from various [[George Lucas]] projects.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]].<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
