'''Crytan''' was a [[professor]] of [[Fyzen Gor]] at [[Prasteen Braak]], a medical school in [[Pau City]] on [[Utapau]]. Crytan often said that dire, [[blood]]y circumstances gave [[Reproduction|birth]] to innovation, something that Gor remembered after attaching an [[Limb|arm]] that had belonged to his friend [[Greesto Ftrak]] onto a [[medical droid]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
