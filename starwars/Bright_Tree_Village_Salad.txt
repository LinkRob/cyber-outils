{{Quote|One of the dishes was this colorful salad, made from Endorian carrots shaved down into little sticks. After a few bites, it had me dancin' to the drums and singin' the praises for the 'Golden One' myself|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Bright Tree Village Salad''' was a [[salad]] served in [[Bright Tree Village]] on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. It was made with [[Endorian carrot]]s that had been shaved down to small sticks. While making a culinary tour of the [[galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] ate the [[Foodstuff|dish]] with the [[Ewok]]s during an festival that celebrated the [[Golden One]]. Chef Tuggs included a recipe for the dish in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bright Tree Village Salad were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish makes use of [[Wikipedia:Rice vinegar|rice vinegar]], diced [[Wikipedia:White sugar|granulated sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Mirin|mirin]], [[Wikipedia:Sesame oil|sesame oil]], [[Wikipedia:Daikon|daikon radish]], [[Wikipedia:Carrot|carrots]], and [[Wikipedia:Sesame|black sesame seeds]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
