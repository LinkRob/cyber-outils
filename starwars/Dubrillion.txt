==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|So Gold team will execute an air-sea raid on Dubrillion.|Gial Ackbar|<ref name="Perilous Rescue">{{FreemakerAdventures|A Perilous Rescue}}</ref>}}
In ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'', Dubrillion was an [[Water|aquatic]] [[planet]]. Around [[4 ABY]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Gial Ackbar]] sent [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] on an air-[[Ocean|sea]] raid to Dubrillion.<ref name="Perilous Rescue" />
