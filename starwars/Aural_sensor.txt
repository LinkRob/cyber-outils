An '''aural sensor''' was a sensor that allowed a being to patch in through a communicator to allow to hear what others were saying discretely through a [[vacuum]]. [[Milo Graf]] utilized this sensor during his adventures on an unidentified [[planet]] in [[Wild Space]].<ref name="AiWS:N">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Nest]]''</ref>
