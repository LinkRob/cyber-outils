'''Fantafly polyhedral dice''' were a type of [[gambling]] [[Chance cube|dice]] used at the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]], in the [[Canto Bight]] [[city]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="TLJ VD" /> They shared their name with the [[casino]] dice game [[Fantafly]] that was also played in Canto Bight<ref name="Smuggler">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> by [[10 BBY]].<ref name="10 BBY">According to ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', the promotional booklet mentioning [[Fantafly]] must have been created at some point before the [[Death|destruction]] of [[L3-37]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[10 BBY]].</ref>

The dice could be tetrahedral, cubic, or eight-, ten-, twelve-, or twenty-sided in shape. They could be [[color]]ed in a [[gold]]-[[silver]]-[[bronze]] palette,<ref name="TLJ VD" /> but there also existed examples that were blue, purple, or translucent-red in color.<ref name="DJ">[[The Last Jedi – DJ – Most Wanted 1|''The Last Jedi – DJ – Most Wanted'' 1]]</ref> The different colors and shapes of Fantafly polyhedral dice made them stand out.<ref name="GeneralEquipment2">{{EncyclopediaCite|General Equipment (2)}}</ref> The dice also had various markings on their faces.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY">[[The Last Jedi – DJ – Most Wanted 1|''The Last Jedi – DJ – Most Wanted'' 1]] is set after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]] and before "[[DJ]]" [[Mission to Canto Bight|meets]] [[Finn]], [[Rose Tico]], and [[BB-8]], which translates to [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Fantafly polyhedral dice were among the gaming implements present at the casino of Canto Bight's [[Coruscant Hotel and Casino]].<ref name="DJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:SWRoleplayingDice.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Fantafy polyhedral dice share similarities with the real-world dice (pictured) used by Fantasy Flight Games' ''Star Wars'' Roleplaying products.]]
Fantafly polyhedral dice were first mentioned in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> They share similarities with the real-world dice<ref name="FDCR">[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny Core Rulebook|''Star Wars: Force and Destiny'' Core Rulebook]]</ref> used in [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars Roleplaying|''Star Wars'' Roleplaying]] series of products, including the [[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire|''Edge of the Empire'']], [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Age of Rebellion'']], and [[Star Wars: Force and Destiny|''Force and Destiny'']] lines of games, which all utilize<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> cubic and eight-, ten-, and twelve-sided dice in various colors.<ref name="FDCR" />

Fantafly polyhedral dice went on to have their first full appearance in [[The Last Jedi – DJ – Most Wanted 1|''Star Wars: The Last Jedi – DJ – Most Wanted'' 1]], a [[Marvel Comics]] one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] written by [[Ben Acker]] and [[Ben Blacker]], penciled by [[Kev Walker]],<ref name="DJ" /> and published on [[January 31]], [[2018]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/65930/star_wars_the_last_jedi_-_dj_-_most_wanted_2018_1|text=Star Wars: The Last Jedi - DJ - Most Wanted (2018) #1}}</ref> "[[General Equipment (2)]]," a booklet of [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series<ref name="GeneralEquipment2" /> that was published on [[February 18]], [[2022]],<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref> misidentified<ref name="TLJ VD" /> Fantafly polyhedral dice as [[Hazard Toss dice]]<ref name="GeneralEquipment2" /> in a section authored by [[Tim Veekhoven]].<ref name="Tim Veekhoven">{{DMCite|dm=File:Credits-TimVeekhoven-Twitter.png|creator=[[Tim Veekhoven]]}}</ref>
