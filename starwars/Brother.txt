The Nightbrothers were [[Gender|male]] [[Dathomirian]] [[Zabrak]]s who served the [[Nightsisters]]. [[Maul]] and [[Savage Opress]] were both Brothers.<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Monster}}</ref>
