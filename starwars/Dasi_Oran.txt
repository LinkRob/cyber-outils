'''Dasi Oran''' was a [[human]] [[senator]] who represented the [[planet]] [[Ghorman]] in the [[Imperial Senate]]. In [[3 BBY]], Oran conversed with fellow senator [[Mon Mothma]] about a bill aimed at bringing down the [[Public Order Resentencing Directive]]. Despite being sympathetic to Mothma, he disagreed heavily about the bill and refused to vote with her for fear of further retaliation by the Imperial authorities against the [[Ghorman (people)|Ghormans]].<ref name="Ghorman" /> As part of its secret [[Ghorman project]], the Empire had been conducting an [[Suppression of Ghorman|anti-Ghorman campaign]] via propaganda and military force in order to exploit Ghorman's [[kalkite]] ores. The Empire's suppression culminated in the [[Ghorman Massacre]] as pretext for seizing the planet's resources.<ref name="WAY?">{{Andor|Who Are You?}}</ref>

Following the massacre, Senator Oran was [[Arrest of Dasi Oran|arrested]] by [[stormtrooper]]s in the [[Imperial Senate Building]] at night on [[Coruscant]]. Oran was dragged through the building's crowded lobby filled with fellow senators who were drawn by the commotion, with someone stating that the presence of stormtroopers in the Senate was unprecedented. Oran's captors remained silent as he asked them what charge he was being arrested for, and as he was taken away, Oran begged the onlooking senators to pay attention and remember Ghorman, warning them that the same oppression would come next to their own people.<ref name="Welcome" />
