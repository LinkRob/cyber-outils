A [[Gender|female]] individual was the '''mother''' of the<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> [[Palliduvan]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|aurra-sing|Aurra Sing}}</ref> [[Aurra Sing]]. The mother was a [[spice]] addict and was too poor to raise Sing, who would leave the slums of the [[moon]] [[Nar Shaddaa]]<ref name="Chrome Perspective">{{Topps|set=2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|subset=Jedi Temple Archives|cardname=Aurra Sing|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/114595/cid/8022643/2015-Topps-Chrome-Star-Wars-Perspectives-Jedi-vs.-Sith-39-J-Aurra-Sing}}</ref> and go on to become a [[bounty hunter]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aurra Sing's mother was first mentioned in the [[2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives: Jedi vs. Sith]] trading card set<ref name="Chrome Perspective" /> released by [[Topps]] on [[August 21]], [[2015]],<ref name="Topps">{{WebCite|url=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewSet.cfm/sid/114595/2015-Topps-Star-Wars-Chrome-Perspectives:-Jedi-Vs.-Sith 2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives: Jedi Vs. Sith - Trading Card Database|text=2015 Topps Chrome Star Wars Perspectives Jedi vs. Sith|date=2025-01-28|work=[https://www.tcdb.com/ Trading Card Database]|archivedate=20211227184854}}</ref> specifically in the card titled "Aurra Sing."<ref name="Chrome Perspective" /> The mother originated in [[Star Wars Legends]] and identified as [[Aunuanna]], appearing in the [[Star Wars (1998) 17|seventeenth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Republic]]'' comic series,<ref name="SW 98 17">[[Star Wars (1998) 17|''Star Wars'' (1998) 17]]</ref> published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[April 26]], [[2000]] by [[Dark Horse Comics]].<ref name="DarkHorse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/99-116/Star-Wars-17Emissaries-to-Malastare-Part-5-of-6|text=Star Wars #17: Emissaries to Malastare (Part 5 of 6)}}</ref>
