'''Asu''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who lived in [[Meevai Village]] on [[Keelia]] sometime after the [[Clone Wars]]. He was [[Wedding of Asu and Haru|married]] to [[Haru]].<ref name="Bride" />

==Biography==
Local raider [[Lord]] [[Izuma]], who took over the [[B1-series battle droid]]s left behind on [[Keelia]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], at one point demanded his bride Haru's grandfather, the village chief, as collateral to avoid unnecessary conflict with the villagers. Haru and Asu both volunteered to go in his stead, despite repeated protests from her sister [[Saku]]. Haru and Asu's [[Wedding of Asu and Haru|wedding ceremony]] took place the night before they were to be taken.<ref name="Bride" />

On the day following the wedding, Haru and Asu turned themselves in to Izuma. Saku and others had secretly organized an ambush on the raiders, but their plans were foiled. When Izuma was ready to execute Saku for being the leader, [[Jedi]] [[F]] intervened and saved her. F's companion [[Valco]] destroyed [[Izuma's starship|Izuma's ship]], thus disabling his [[B1-series battle droid]]s. In the ensuing chaos, Izuma's associates fled. Izuma then took Haru as hostage. In a swift move, F struck down the raider, and saved Haru, Asu, and the rest of the villagers.<ref name="Bride" />

Sometime later, Asu and other villagers thanked F for her help, and said their farewell to F as the Jedi left the planet.<ref name="Bride" />
