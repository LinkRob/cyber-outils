The '''derrick major''' was an area on [[Coruscant]] that had [[Docks under the derrick major|cargo docks]] underneath it.<ref name="Senate Murders" />

==History==
The [[docks under the derrick major]] were used for loading and unloading containers and had many lifting cranes operating out of the side of a building.<ref name="Senate Murders" />
