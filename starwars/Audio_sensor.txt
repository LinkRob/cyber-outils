An '''audio sensor''',<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> also known as an '''audio pickup''',<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>, '''auditory sensor''',<ref name="TROS Junior Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>, or '''audio receptor'''<ref name="DB MG Security Droid">{{Databank|mining-guild-security-droid|Mining Guild Security Droid}}</ref> was a type of [[sensor]] that detected sound. The component was used in [[3PO-series protocol droid]]s,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> [[RA-7 protocol droid]]s,<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11">[[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 11|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 11]]</ref> and [[LOM-series protocol droid]]s<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 7">{{BuildR2Cite|7|Droid Directory}}</ref> and also featured in [[General]] [[Grievous]]'s [[helmet]]<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II" /> and [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] [[Stormtrooper armor|helmets]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />
