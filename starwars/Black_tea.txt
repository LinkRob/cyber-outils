'''Black tea''' was a type of [[tea]] that could be served [[Temperature|hot]]. On the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]], the [[Julen Rakab's mother|mother]] and [[Julen Rakab's father|father]] of [[farmer]] [[Julen Rakab]] drank [[Color|black]] tea during his childhood, on [[Standard day|days]] with pleasant weather.<ref name="ACOF" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> after both his [[parent]]s had [[Death|died]], Rakab remembered the ritual fondly.<ref name="ACOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Black tea was mentioned in ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', a [[2019]] young-adult novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="ACOF" />
