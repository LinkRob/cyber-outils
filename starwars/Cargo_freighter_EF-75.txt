{{Ship-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cargo freighter EF-75 was named by [[Steven Melching]] as a reference to the bulk carrier [[Wikipedia:SS Edmund Fitzgerald|SS ''Edmund Fitzgerald'']], which sank in [[Wikipedia:Lake Superior|Lake Superior]] in [[1975]] and was later memorialized in one of Melching's favorite songs, "[[Wikipedia:The Wreck of the Edmund Fitzgerald|The Wreck of the ''Edmund Fitzgerald'']]," by [[Wikipedia:Gordon Lightfoot|Gordon Lightfoot]].<ref name="Melching Tweet">{{Twitter|stevenmelching|status/970873998601957376|[[Steven Melching]]|quote=Useless #StarWarsRebels trivia: "Cargo Freighter EF-75" was my reference to the Edmund Fitzgerald, which sank in Lake Superior in 1975 and was later memorialized in one of my favorite songs, "The Wreck of the Edmund Fitzgerald," by Gordon Lightfoot.|archivedate=20200812084956}}</ref>
