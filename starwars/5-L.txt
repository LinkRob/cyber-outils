'''5-L''' was a [[MSE-6 series repair droid]] who served the [[First Order]] during its [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]]. When the [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|TIE pilot]] [[Tamara Ryvora|DT-533]] wished to [[Treason|leave]] the First Order, she reprogrammed 5-L to assist her.

==Biography==
{{Quote|You are doing me a huge service, 5-L.''"<br />[5-L chirping]<br />"''See? That didn't hurt at all, did it?''"<br />[5-L chirping]<br />"''Now, you're gonna help me get out of here.|DT-533 and 5-L|<ref name="Escape" />}}
[[File:Reprogram5L-TheEscape.png|thumb|left|300px|Tamara Ryvora reprogramming 5-L.]]
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">{{ResistanceDate|2|19}}</ref> 5-L served the [[First Order]] aboard the<ref name="Escape" /> [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Thunderer]]''.<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Shortly after the [[suppression of the Aeos system]], 5-L entered [[First Order barracks|barracks]] aboard the ''Thunderer'' of First Order [[pilot]] [[Tamara Ryvora|DT-533's]]&mdash;named Tamara Ryvor&mdash;to update the systems. DT-533 requested the [[droid]] perform the upgrades at another time but the droid continued their objective. When the pilot encouraged the droid to continue upgrading and began approaching the droid, 5-L attempted to flee from the pilot. DT-533 trapped 5-L by shutting the door and allowed scooped the mouse droid up. After carrying 5-L to a [[bunk]], the pilot opened up the droid's panels and reprogrammed the droid to assist her. She comforted 5-L after the reprogramming and informed 5-L that the droid would insist her with [[Treason|escaping]].<ref name="Escape" />

Later, as [[Commander]] [[Pyre]] and [[Agent]] [[Tierny]] departed [[Tierny's office]], 5-L placed their body in the doorframe of Tierny's office, preventing the door from closes. Ryvora entered the office and removed the droid from the door. She thanked 5-L as she searched Tierny's desk. After retrieving her [[Tam's comlink|comlink]] from the locked drawer before hearing Tierny returning. She commanded 5-L to hide as she took her own spot next to the door. 5-L failed to hide before Tierny entered her office. Tienry was reading a [[datapad]], allowing the pilot to slip out unnoticed. Tierny took notice of 5-L in the middle of her office and inquired if the system upgrade had been completed. 5-L chirped in response before departing the office and meeting up with Ryvora in the hall.<ref name="Escape" />

Ryvora carried 5-L back to her quarters and opened the droid's interior again. She connected her comlink with one of 5-L's inner wires and transmitted a message to [[Resistance]] member [[Kazuda Xiono]]'s comlink&ndash;to meet her on [[Castilon]] in three [[cycle]]s. The message was transmitted in a [[mechanic's code]] developed by her and [[Neeku Vozo]].<ref name="Escape" />

5-L first appeared in part one of the two-part episode "[[The Escape (episode)|The Escape]],"<ref name="Escape" /> the finale of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[January 26]], [[2020]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> Concept arts for 5-L were designed by [[Lucasfilm Animation]] artist [[Aaron Pham]] on [[August 21]], [[2018]].<ref name="ConceptArt1">{{SW|url=the-escape-part-2-concept-art-gallery|text="The Escape: Part 2" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref><ref name="ConceptArt2">{{SW|url=the-escape-part-2-concept-art-gallery|text="The Escape: Part 2" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> The droid's designation was a tribute to [[w:c:anamericantail:Fievel Mousekewitz|Fievel Mousekewitz]], the main character of the [[1986]] animated movie [[Wikipedia:An American Tail|''An American Tail'']], produced by [[Wikipedia:Amblin Entertainment|Amblin Entertainment]].<ref name="Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-escape-part-1-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 5 Fun Facts from "The Escape - Part 1" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2020-01-27}}</ref>
