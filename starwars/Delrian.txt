'''Delrian''' was a [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> which hosted an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Prison|penal]] [[factory|work camp]]. Prisoners incarcerated there during the [[Imperial Era]] were tasked with installing [[computer|electronics]] on the [[durasteel]] restraints used on the [[Oovo 4]] prison.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

During the [[New Republic Era]] the prison came under the control of the [[New Republic]], and the convicted [[alien]] criminal [[Vorg Alsum]] served a 35 [[Standard year|year]] sentence there for [[speeder]] theft. The sentence was listed on Alsum's profile, which was found on a database seen by [[Marshal]] [[Carasynthia Dune|Cara Dune]] when she searched for the profile of the criminal [[Migs Mayfeld]]<ref name="Chapter 14" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|14}}</ref>

Boucher]].<ref name="GG1">''[[Galaxy Guide 1: A New Hope]]''</ref>
