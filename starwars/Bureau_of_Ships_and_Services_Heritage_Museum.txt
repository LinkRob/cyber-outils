The '''Bureau of Ships and Services Heritage Museum''' was a museum of the [[Bureau of Ships and Services]] that was located on [[Coruscant]]. During the [[time]] it was active, it had in its possession an ancient [[Dataplaque|navigational dataplaque]] from the ''[[Permondiri Explorer]]'' which was eventually stolen and replaced with a forgery.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bureau of Ships and Services Heritage Museum was first mentioned in the [[2018]] [[canon]] reference book, ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />
