'''Erminia''' was a [[human]] individual who worked for [[De'Rruyet Industries]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 10|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 10]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Erminia was present at the [[De'Rruyet Center]] on the<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 8">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 8|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 8]]</ref> [[Midarr]] where [[Beol De'Rruyet]], the leader of De'Rruyet Industries, planned to [[De'Rruyet Industries confluence|unveil]] a replicated [[Nihil]] [[Path engine]] [[hyperdrive]]. Before the event could take place, forces of the [[Unbroken Clan]] led by [[General]] [[Vukorah]] arrived at the center and, in an attempt to claim the hyperdrive for herself, took De'Rruyet and his employees hostage.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" />

Rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]], who had been sent by [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]] to acquire the hyperdrive for the [[Tagge Corporation]], planned for Starros to rescue the employees whilst Aphra stalled Vukorah. Starros came across two Unbroken Clan [[soldier]]s who were holding Erminia and three other employees hostage and subdued the soldiers, informing the employees they were now safe and asked if the employees were okay. Erminia responded that they were fine and thanked to Starros. Starros then asked Erminia where the nearest exit was, and Erminia pointed to where it was, before they began to ask what would happen if the soldiers came back. Before Erminia could finish, however, Starros informed the employees that she would not let them be hurt, and the group then made their way to the exit. After they exited the building, Erminia reunited with her [[Erminia's mother|mother]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" />
