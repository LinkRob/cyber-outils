The '''Deadly Sharpshooter''' was a costume owned by the [[Miraluka]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Diago Velaar]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Frontier Sharpshooter|standard costume]]. This dark outfit featured [[Color|golden]] embellishments and a [[Helmet|mask]], being considered a much more intimidating appearance than his usual costume by Hunters like [[Nox]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Deadly Sharpshooter was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Diago, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 5.0.0 alongside Season 5 on [[January 27]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Deadly Slugthrower]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching Tier 70, the final tier, on the Season 5 Arena Pass' premium track.<ref name="Hunters" />
