In [[5 BBY]], during the period of [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Sabine Wren]] of the [[Spectres]] rebel cell '''attacked''' an [[Lothal airfield|airfield]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Lothal]]. The attack, targeting an Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] airfield, was designed as a diversion to allow the rebels' [[VCX-100 light freighter|ship]], the ''[[Ghost]]'', to lift off nearby without detection from Imperial forces. 

During the attack, Wren painted her crest, the [[Starbird (symbol)|starbird]], onto a TIE fighter and placed a [[paint bomb]] in the center of it. Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s who spotted the [[graffiti]] and explosive were too late, as the resulting explosion destroyed the TIE fighter and drew the attention of the Empire, allowing the ''Ghost'' to lift off undetected. As a result of her successful attack, the Empire named a person matching Wren's description as one of Lothal's [[Lothal's most-wanted list|most-wanted criminals]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|What do you think you're doing?''"<br />"''What does it look like? Art.|TK-626 and Sabine Wren|<ref name="Art Attack" />}}
[[File:StarWarsRebels-MeetSabine.png|thumb|300px|right|Sabine Wren brought her love of art to her anti-Imperial activities with the Spectres.]]
For [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|formal outbreak]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="GCW date">{{InsiderCite|203|A Certain Point of View}} states that the [[Galactic Civil War]] officially began at the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance]]. The Declaration is dated to [[2 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Art Attack]]" to [[5 BBY]], placing the attack on the Imperial airfield ahead of the official beginning of the war, during the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]].</ref> the conflict wherein the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] declared and waged open war against the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against the Empire manifested as small but growing insurgencies made up of various rebel cells and factions.<ref name="Call to Action">{{Rebels|Call to Action}}</ref> One of these cells was the [[Spectres]], who were based on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Lothal]]. Among them was [[Sabine Wren]],<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> a [[Mandalorian]] who deserted from being an [[Imperial Academy of Mandalore|Imperial Academy]] [[Imperial cadet|cadet]] on the planet [[Mandalore]]<ref name="Out of Darkness">{{Rebels|Out of Darkness}}</ref> and later found a second chance within the Spectres.<ref name="Blood Sisters">{{Rebels|Blood Sisters}}</ref> Wren and the Spectres undertook numerous operations against the Empire,<ref name="Visual Guide" /> in which Wren brought her love of [[Artist|art]] to her anti-Imperial activities.<ref name="Sabine Databank">{{Databank|sabine-wren|Sabine Wren}}</ref>

==The attack==
{{Quote|What do we got?"''<br />''"One intruder in Mando gear, still at large.|The stormtrooper commander and MB-223|<ref name="Rise of the Rebels" />}}
In [[5 BBY]], the rebels' [[Starship|ship]], the [[VCX-100 light freighter]] known as the ''Ghost'', needed a diversion in order to leave Lothal<ref name="Timelines" /> without attracting the attention of the Empire, who was familiar with their vessel. In order to take off undetected, the Spectres came up with a plan in which Wren would create a diversion at an [[Lothal airfield|airfield]] for [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s near where the ''Ghost'' was docked.<ref name="Art Attack" />

[[File:Sabine_Wren_Symbol_TIE_Fighter.png|thumb|300px|left|Wren paints her crest&mdash;the starbird&mdash;onto the wing of a TIE fighter.]]
Putting the plan into action, Wren infiltrated the [[Airbase|airfield]] and began using her [[airbrush]]es to spray [[Color|purple]] [[graffiti]] of her crest, the [[Starbird (symbol)|starbird]], on the wing of one of the [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Starfighter|fighters]] docked there. She was quickly cornered by two [[stormtrooper]]s, [[TK-626]]<ref name="Art Attack" /> and [[MB-223]],<ref name="Rise of the Rebels" /> who ordered her to stand down at [[E-11 medium blaster rifle|gunpoint]]. Undaunted and enjoying her actions at the airfield, a defiant Wren goaded the stormtroopers into shooting at her. While the stormtroopers were busy trying to make their shot, Wren took the chance to slip out of sight and lead the stormtroopers on a pursuit through the airfield, taunting them along the way. TK-626 quickly called for reinforcements. Four more stormtroopers,<ref name="Art Attack" /> including a [[Unidentified stormtrooper commander (Lothal)|commander]],<ref name="Rise of the Rebels" /> arrived for support.<ref name="Art Attack" />

While the stormtroopers were regrouping, Wren took the opportunity to finish the starbird on the wing of the TIE fighter she had graffitied. Before running off, she added a [[paint bomb]] to the center of the symbol. The stormtroopers soon spotted the graffiti, but realized too late that there was an attached [[Bomb|explosive]]. The resulting explosion destroyed the TIE fighter and sent the stormtroopers flying, all of which created enough noise to allow the Ghost to slip away into the skies&mdash;leaving behind an explosion that was big enough for [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]] to remark that the Spectres could see it from the ship. The stormtroopers survived the explosion, with their [[Stormtrooper armor|armor]] covered in paint, while Wren watched the colorful, explosive results of her actions with much delight.<ref name="Art Attack" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|We have an intruder on the north side, sector nine. The ''artist'' is back. Sound the alarm!|TK-626, when Wren returned to the airfield ahead of the action on Mustafar|<ref name="Battle to the End">''[[Battle to the End]]''</ref>}}
[[File:AirfieldAttack-FireAcrossTheGalaxy.png|thumb|300px|right|Wren and her fellow rebels re-attack the airfield to steal an Imperial transport, ahead of the action at Mustafar.]]
After the attack on the airfield, a person matching Wren’s description was placed onto [[Lothal's most-wanted list]]. This attack,<ref name="The Rebellion Begins">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref> along with a string of other attacks by the Spectres culminating in the [[mission in Capital City]], attracted the attention of [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]], who viewed the rebel activities on Lothal as a potential spark of wider rebellion<ref name="Spark">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref>&mdash;which he was ultimately proven right about. The actions of the rebels were part of a [[Bail Organa's resistance movement|wider movement]],<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref> which helped spark the beginnings of the Galactic Civil War.<ref name="Timelines" />

Shortly before<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> the [[action at Mustafar]] that became one of the earliest rebel victories of the conflict<ref name="Mustafar Databank History">{{Databank|url=mustafar-history-gallery|title=Mustafar History Gallery}} {{C|Slide 15}}</ref> in [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Wren returned to the airfield&mdash;where<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> TK-626<ref name="Battle to the End" /> recognized her as "the artist"&mdash;along with fellow Spectres [[Ezra Bridger]] and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] to destroy several parked TIE fighters and steal an [[Gozanti-class cruiser|Imperial transport]] that was used during the operation to rescue one of their leaders, former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kanan Jarrus]], over<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> the planet<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on the Lothal airfield was the central plot of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' animated television short "[[Art Attack]],"<ref name="Art Attack" /> which premiered on [[August 18]], [[2014]]. The short, the second in a four-part series, was part of the leadup to the [[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion|series premiere]] of ''Star Wars Rebels''<ref name="Insider 156">{{InsiderCite|156|Star Wars Rebels Recounted|''Star Wars Rebels'' Recounted}}</ref> on [[September 26]], 2014.<ref name="Early">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-rebels-spark-of-rebellion-to-debut-early-on-watch-disney-xd|text=Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion to Debut Early on WATCH Disney XD}}</ref> Concept art, storyboards, and early animation for the events of "Art Attack" were first seen in a promotional video introducing the character of Sabine Wren on [[February 19]] of the same year.<ref name="Meet Sabine YouTube">{{SWYouTube|-_0A2-YDexw|Meet Sabine, the Explosive Artist &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/star-wars-rebels-meet-sabine-the-explosive-artist}}</ref>
