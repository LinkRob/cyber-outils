The '''Empire's annual costume ball''' was an annual costume ball hosted by the [[Galactic Empire]]. According to ''[[The Official Stormtrooper Training Manual]]'', many [[snowtrooper]]s wore their [[Snowtrooper armor|armor]] as a [[Ghost (folklore)|ghost]] costume for the ball.<ref name="Manual">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Official Stormtrooper Training Manual]]''</ref>
