In the months following the [[Attack on Kamino|bombardment]] on [[Tipoca City]], [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart|Rampart]] ordered the '''assassination''' of several individuals with knowledge of his involvement in the event. Among the targets was [[Slip (clone trooper)|Slip]], who was followed by [[Clone X]] as he traveled to an isolated industrial area of [[Coruscant]] to meet with [[Rex]]. [[Senator]] [[Riyo Chuchi]] and two of her [[Pantoran guard|guards]] also tracked the fleeing [[clone trooper]] to his destination, ignorant of the plot against him, and approached him before his intended contact could arrive. Slip, spooked by the knowledge that he had been followed, attempted to run, prompting Clone X to open fire and [[Death|kill]] him.

However, unconvinced that he had stopped Slip's story from spreading, X chose to expand his mission parameters to include the elimination of senator Chuchi. He continued to trade fire with her guards as they escorted the senator into a nearby abandoned building, and eventually killed both of them. He then followed the terrified Chuchi as she ran onto a catwalk, but before he could fire the killing shot, he was [[stun]]ned by Rex, who had just arrived for the meeting with Slip.

Rex and Chuchi took the captured assassin to [[Martez sisters' repair shop|Trace Martez's repair shop]] to interrogate him, but rather than reveal any further information, X bit down on an implanted [[Death|suicide]] [[Electro capsule|shocker]], killing himself.<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" />
