==Behind the scenes==
Abrion Major's star first appeared in the [[Obi-Wan 3|third issue]] of the comic miniseries [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan (comic series)|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'']], which was published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> on [[July 27]], [[2022]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/101189/star_wars_obi-wan_2022_3|text=Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi (2022) #3}}</ref> The reference booklet "[[Exegol, the Unknown Regions and Wild Space]]" places the [[Abrion Major system]] in [[Wild Space]],<ref name="EURWS">{{EncyclopediaCite|Exegol, the Unknown Regions and Wild Space}}</ref> though all other systems contradict this.
