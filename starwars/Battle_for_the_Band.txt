"'''Battle for the Band'''" is the first part of the [[Battle for the Band / Uprooted|sixth episode]] of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|second season]] the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''. It was released on [[Disney+]] on [[August 14]], [[2024]].<ref name="Announcement" />

==Official description==
''Hap joins a Jedi mission.''

==Plot summary==
===The kidnapping===
One morning, the restaurateur [[Hap]] welcomes the members of the band [[Feb Rozo and the Sap Tap Trio]], whom he regards as old friends. Their reunion is interrupted by the [[Ganguls]], who wreck [[Hap's Sap Tap]]'s furniture. [[Sellaccc Orryak]] says that her gang is in need of a band and orders [[Jooro Jarrot]] and [[Toda-Joh]] to kidnap the band members which consist of a [[Bith]], a [[Rodian]], a [[Gigoran]] and an [[Ardennian]] and take them to [[Yarrum]]. Hap ponders what to do in despair and doubts his ability to rescue the band. 

Later the [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]] and [[Nubs]] bring [[Padawan]] [[Wes Vinik]] for breakfast at Hap's Sap Tap. They find that the restaurant has been wrecked. Hap emerges from the counter and tells the [[Jedi]] that the Ganguls kidnapped Feb Rozo and the Sap Tap Trio. Hap berates himself for being frightened by the pirates and not being a hero like the Jedi. Brightstar and Vinik promise to help Hap get his friends back. When Hap says that he is not a hero like them, Solay and Vinik reassure him that he can help in other ways. Vinik invites Hap along. He believes that the band are in danger. 

===The Yarrum exchange===
The Jedi and Hap get a ride aboard [[Nash Durango]]'s ''[[Crimson Firehawk]]''. The ''Firehawk'' circles [[Yarrum Tower]] but find no sign of the Ganguls. Solay spots the Ganguls' flagship ''[[Rathtar's Revenge]]'' hovering over the desert. Durango and [[RJ-83]] fly closer to have a look. Through a [[Macrobinoculars]] Brightstar, Vinik and Hap spot a [[sail barge]] and other [[speeder]]s. On the deck, Hap sees Feb and his band. Hap also sees the infamous [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Bulcha]], one of the most feared crime lords in the [[Bri'ahl sector|sector]], and numerous [[security droid]]s. Brightstar tasks Durango and RJ-83 with staying behind as getaway pilots while the Jedi infiltrate the ship disguised as guards. 

Bulcha reminds Orryak about their deal to trade the band for several armored [[security droid]]s. When Rozo asks why he and his band are chained, Bulcha reminds them that they are his prisoners. After handing the keys to a red-skinned [[Twi'lek]] guard, Bulcha orders the band to play to some music. The four aliens play an upbeat tune, which pleases Bulcha. The Jedi mingle on the deck disguised as pirate guards while Hap hides behind a stack of crates. When a green-skinned [[Kadas'sa'Nikto]] notices the strangers, a disguised Hap manages to draw his attention by offering a cold drink for a hot afternoon. 

Impressed with Feb Rozo and the Sap Tap Trio's performance, Bulcha agrees to sell the security droids to the Ganguls in return for the kidnapped band members. To celebrate the deal, Bulcha treats his guests to cold drinks. While Hap serves them, the disguised Jedi younglings approach the band members. The [[Unidentified Ardennian Sap Tap Trio member|female Ardennian band member]] is despondent but Brightstar reassures her that they are here to help. Brightstar proposes searching for the keys but Vinik ignites [[Wes Vinik's lightsaber|his lightsaber]], which he intends to use to free the kidnapped band members. 

However, this draws the attention of the pirates and Bulcha. When Bulcha asks about the uninvited guests, Orryak attempts to cover her mistake by offering both the band and the Jedi as prisoners. Vinik demands that the Hutt release their friends. Bulcha instead deploys his three security droids who wield [[vibroblade]]s. Outnumbered, Vinik convinces his fellow Jedi to deactivate the lightsabers. Bulcha's guards take the prisoners to the brig while Bulcha returns to sealing the deal with the Ganguls. Orryak orders Jarrot and Joh to bring the security droids back to their [[shuttle]]. 

===Hap to the rescue===
Hap panics about the botched rescue mission but recalls Solay's advice that he can find his own way to help. Before the guards can usher their Jedi prisoners into the brig, Hap confronts the guns and knocks them out with two beverage mugs. Solay and Vinik steal the guard's keys before locking them inside the brig. 

Outside, Bulcha gloats over his lopsided deal with the Ganguls, who have departed in their starship. He reveals that he had acquired the popular Sap Tap Trio band in return for selling two rusty security droids to the pirates. The Jedi confront the Hutt crime lord and his guards. While Hap frees the kidnapped band members, the Jedi fight against Bulcha's guards, who are armed with vibroblades. Before Hap can free the band members, Bulcha grabs his arm and attempts to assert his authority. Hap temporarily blinds him by splashing a mug on him. He then process to free Rozo and the Sap Tap Trio. 

While fighting the Twi'lek guard, Brightstar contacts Durango via [[holoprojector]] to request a pick-up. The Nikto guard knocks Vinik to the ground with his vibroblade. The guard is distracted by the ''Firehawk'' flying over the sail barge, allowing Vinik to [[Force push]] him against a sail mast. Nubs and Solay drive a guard into Bulcha, knocking them over. Durango positions the landing ramp of the ''Firehawk'' against the ship's deck. The Jedi, band members and Hap climb aboard and the ship flies away, leaving a defeated Bulcha behind. 

Back in [[Kublop Springs]], Feb Rozo and the Sap Tap Trio hold a concert at Hap's Sap Tap. Hap tells the crowd that he learnt that anyone can be a hero. He also gives credit to his Jedi friends and Durango. The band play a song.
