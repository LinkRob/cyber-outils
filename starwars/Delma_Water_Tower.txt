The '''Delma Water Tower''' was a [[Color|gray]] [[water]] tower of unknown origins located in the [[Karaba Foothills]] region of the [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. It consisted of four sections of stepped discs each increasing in size moving up the tower and towered over the wilderness around it. Some locals attributed the tower to the [[Ahia-Ko]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> an ancient [[species]] that had constructed temples in Akiva's [[jungle]]s.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] saw the tower while on Akiva and was amazed by the architecture, the likes of which she had not encountered before. She contacted her crewmate [[ND-5]] to ask about the structure and he informed her of its mysterious origins and the theories about the Ahia-Ko.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Delma Water Tower appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. It is possible to complete the game without ever encountering the tower and triggering Kay Vess and ND-5's conversation about it.<ref name="Outlaws" />
