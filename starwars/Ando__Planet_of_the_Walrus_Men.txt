'''Ando: Planet of the Walrus Men''' was a series of ''[[Planet Hoppers]]'' articles from [[July]] [[2003]] covering the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Ando/Legends|Ando]]. It was written by [[Cory J. Herndon]] and published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] on [[Wizards.com]] as an online supplement for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''.

==Contents==
===Part 1: Any Port in a Storm===
''&hellip;an escaped [[Mon Calamari/Legends|Mon Calamari]] slave finds refuge on the dangerous water-world Ando.''<ref name="Ando" />

===Part 2: Lucky Break===
''&hellip;young fugitive Mon Calamari [[Sissalik]] meets his new boss -- the Aquala captain of the trawler ''[[Nanda's Luck]]''.''<ref name="Ando" />

===Part 3: When Rocks Swim===
''&hellip;"Slick" Sissalik nets his first [[Andoan mineral-fish]] from the deck of the ''Nanda's Luck''.''<ref name="Ando" />

===Part 4: She Is the Pirate Queen===
''&hellip;the ''Nanda's Luck'' has an unlucky encounter with the infamous [[Aqualish/Legends|Quara]] [[Pirate/Legends|pirate]] [[Pyash Yopayomba]].''<ref name="Ando" />

===Part 5: Portage Moon===
''&hellip;Slick is caught up in a plot to incite a new Aqualish civil war at the annual [[Portage Moon Festival|Festival of the Portage Moon]].''<ref name="Ando" />
