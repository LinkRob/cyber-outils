'''Duralloy''' was an alloy that was used to make [[capital ship]]-class [[armor]]. Although it was only a [[light freighter]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was fitted with duralloy plates salvaged from an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[cruiser]].<ref name="IncrediBuilds">''[[Star Wars: Millennium Falcon Deluxe Book and Model Set]]''</ref>
