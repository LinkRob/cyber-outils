'''Bib'vena''' was a [[Rutian]] [[Twi'lek]] who lived during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. Like [[Bib'vena's mother|his mother]] before him, Bib'vena was a freedom fighter. A good natured being with a keen sense of ethics he also inherited from his [[Parent|mother]], he despised any form of [[slavery]]. His weapon of choice was a [[blaster pistol]].<ref name="Vena" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
Several [[Standard month|months]] into the [[Iron Blockade|blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], the Twi'lek was recruited by [[the Smuggler]] for his combat skills and expertise in defense.<ref name="Vena" />{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the [[2015]] iOS video game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', Bib'vena is one of the many crew members that can be sent on Crew Runs for helpful items. His background is in warfare, preference is defensive and he is a soldier, skilled at battle runs.<ref name="Vena" />
