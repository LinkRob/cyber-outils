== Background ==
The ''Exas Jewell'' was a [[luxury train]] that crossed over a mountainous region of the [[planet]] [[Tyegin]]. It was designed by [[Shen Kanton]]. The train moved across the planet on a magnetic field loop. The lead car was the engine car, in which the train's engineer conducted the train. The third car was the premier-class car. The train had orange and yellow livery.<ref name="Off the Rails" />

== History ==
In [[3 BBY]], the train embarked on its maiden voyage, where upon its arrival at its final destination, a large reception was planned. The conman [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] posed as Kanton in order to take [[Currency|money]] from several passengers in a high-stakes card game. However, during the voyage, the train was attacked by [[Pirate|pirates]] who stole Calrissian's credits and the valuables of other guests. The pirates also damaged the train, causing it to speed up. Calrissian, still posing as Kanton, unhooked the engine with the aid of [[Rakan]] and the train's engineer and let it speed away. Then Calrissian, Rakan, and [[Baj]] used the train's furniture to disrupt the magnetic field to slow the rest of the train. Eventually [[Tyegin's law enforcement]] responded, rescuing the passengers.<ref name="Off the Rails" />
