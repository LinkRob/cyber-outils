{{Quote|When folks think of Coruscant&ndash;one of the most historically significant worlds in the entire galaxy&ndash;they usually think of a densely populated city-planet that stretches high into the atmosphere. Me? I think of slugs. More specifically, duraslugs, a species of massive mollusk that feeds on the duracrete stone used to construct the ancient buildings on Coruscant's lowest levels.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Duracrete slugs''', also known simply as '''duraslugs''', were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[Slug (creature)|slug]] found in the [[Coruscant Underworld|underworld]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. They [[Diet|fed]] on the artificial [[stone]] [[duracrete]], then after digesting it, secreted it from their bodies to form a hard outer [[Armor|shell]]. The creatures used a series of [[mouth]] [[Limb|tentacles]] to sense their surroundings and could grow up to ten [[meter]]s in length.

Under their armored skin, duracrete slugs had tasty meat that was initially eaten by residents of the underworld out of necessity, before citizens of the planet's upper levels began using it and created [[Duraslug Roll]]s, a type of savory [[sandwich]]. [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for this in ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'' between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Duraslugs might not look all that appetizin', but once you get past that armored skin of theirs, the meat inside is mighty tasty.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref>}}
[[File:DuracreteSlug-MonstersOozeSlime.png|thumb|left|300px|Duracrete slugs secreted duracrete through their skin to form a hard shell.]]
The duracrete slug,<ref name="AEYNK" /> also known simply as duraslugs,<ref name="Dooku" /> was a [[species]]<ref name="AEYNK" /> of massive,<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[Slug (creature)|slug]] that [[Diet|fed]] on [[duracrete]], a type of sturdy<ref name="AEYNK" /> artificial [[stone]] that it could break down with its abrasive and muscular [[mouth]]part.<ref name="CVD" /> Though [[Reproduction|born]] with soft skin,<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> the [[gastropod]] [[mollusk]]s'<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> unusual diet<ref name="Ooze">''[[The Star Wars Book of Monsters, Ooze and Slime]]''</ref> allowed them to digest the duracrete, then secrete it through their skin<ref name="CVD" /> or excrete it to form a hard outer shell<ref name="VE" /> of [[armor]]-like skin.<ref name="AEYNK" /> Underneath their unappetizing armored skin, duracrete slugs had [[Foodstuff|meat]] that was edible to [[Artiodac]]s.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

As its underdeveloped [[eye]]sight was all but useless,<ref name="CVD" /> leaving the creature blind,<ref name="BMF26">{{BuildFalconCite|26|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> the duracrete slug's main sensory apparatus<ref name="CVD" /> was a series of [[Limb|tentacles]] around its mouth<ref name="AEYNK" /> that served as feelers<ref name="CVD" /> to sense its surroundings.<ref name="AEYNK" /> The slugs also used two of their powerful mandibles to devour duracrete in large quantities.<ref name="Coruscant" /> The slug's armored skin was [[Color|tan]] in color, while its two small eyes were black. The body of an adult slug could grow up to ten [[meter]]s (thirty-two [[Foot|feet]], eight inches) in length,<ref name="AEYNK" /> but they averaged between three and five meters.<ref name="Coruscant" /> Each slug was covered in a series of spiked ridges that ran from the creature's mouth to its tail.<ref name="AEYNK" />

==Behavior==
Duracrete slugs were burrowers<ref name="BMF26" /> capable of causing much more damage than [[granite slug]]s and generally could be found in the lowest levels or foundation of a building. Within a few [[Standard week|weeks]], a single slug could cause permanent damage to a large building.<ref name="Coruscant" />

==History==
===Urban pest===
[[File:Hawk-Bat-HyperspaceLounge.png|thumb|right|300px|Hawk-bats preyed upon duracrete slugs.]]
Duracrete slugs were one of several unique forms of wildlife that evolved on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] due to the thousands of [[Standard year|years]] of urbanization that resulted in the world becoming an [[ecumenopolis]]. Adapting to incorporate the artificial into their biology, they fed on the duracrete used in the construction of buildings in the [[Coruscant Underworld]] and came to pose a threat to the area's structural stability.<ref name="CVD" /> As most of the [[city]]'s foundations was filled with duracrete, the slugs could weaken the foundations of the buildings above, causing untold mayhem and destruction and making the species a dangerous nuisance.<ref name="BMF26" />

Occasionally, duracrete slug nests would be revealed when crumbling masonry fell away from buildings in the lower levels of the city. [[Hawk-bat]]s would then descend to pick over the nest and devour the slugs in a shrieking chaos.<ref name="Crisis">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Crisis on Naboo}}</ref> Exterminators on Coruscant carried pest control equipment to eradicate duracrete slugs<ref name="VE" /> and were deployed by the city authorities in constant campaigns to keep the infestations under control.<ref name="Coruscant" />

{{Quote|Look, I know you're better than him. He knows you're better than him. Even the duraslugs back home know you're better than him, but there's no need to rub his face in it.|Sifo-Dyas speaks to Dooku about their fellow Jedi Arath Tarrex|<ref name="Dooku">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>}}

During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Hutt]] [[Churo]] conducted research on many species in [[the galaxy]] and learned about duracrete slug anatomy.<ref name="BTN">''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''</ref> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> Churo was awed by the shape of the [[Viperi-class cargo shuttle|''Viperi''-class cargo shuttle]] [[Lucky Tortle|''Sarlacc's Scorn'']], reflecting that while he knew less about [[Starship|ships]] than duracrete slug anatomy, the vessel remained impressive to him.<ref name="BTN" /> In [[90 BBY]],<ref name="DookuDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Dooku]]'s trip to [[Serenno]] to [[90 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Sifo-Dyas]] mentioned duracrete slugs while trying to convince his friend [[Dooku]] not to antagonize their fellow Initiate [[Arath Tarrex]] during a trip to the planet [[Serenno]]. Dooku later recounted the incident in a [[holo-letter]] to his [[House Serenno|sister]], [[Jenza]].<ref name="Dooku" />

During a [[Mission to Metagos|mission]] to the planet [[Metagos]]<ref name="GlassAbyss">''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]''</ref> in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="AbyssDate">The events of ''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]'' take place the [[Standard week|week]] after the [[funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] encountered [[demi-slug]]s, a type of creature that he believed had once been similar to an outsized duracrete slug before being altered by the [[Great Storm (Metagos)|Great Storm]] of [[radiation]] that had effected the planet.<ref name="GlassAbyss" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="Date 23">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku displayed a [[Hologram|holographic]] [[Datacard|data disk]] that detailed the events around meeting his sister, including the conversation mentioning duracrete slugs, to his recently recruited [[Sith assassin]], [[Asajj Ventress]]. The pair listened to the recording, after which Dooku gave Ventress several more disks for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into Jenza's location.<ref name="Dooku" />

===Coruscant cuisine===
{{Quote|Folks in the planet's undercity started makin' meals outta these gargantuan gastropods by necessity back in the day, but it wasn't long before citizens in the planet's upper echelons caught on and elevated this bottom feeder, creatin's this savory slug sandwich.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
[[File:StronoCookieTuggs.png|thumb|300px|right|Artiodac Strono Tuggs considered duracrete slug meat tasty and included a recipe using it in ''The Ultimate Cookbook''.]]
Shortly after some denizens of the Coruscant Underworld began to eat duracete slugs out of necessity, citizens of the planet's upper levels learned of the duracrete slug meals and began using the mollusk's meat in their food as well, resulting in the creation of the savory [[sandwich]]s known as [[Duraslug Roll]]s, which were also popular with Underworld citizens.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

After publishing<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> his second [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'',<ref name="LDCookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]''</ref> the Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] went on a culinary tour of the galaxy, during which he tried Duraslug Rolls on Coruscant, considering the slug's meat tasty.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> Between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'' establishes that the [[in-universe]] ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'' was published following the publishing of ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'' and prior to the [[Destruction of Kijimi]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'' establishes that ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook'' was published at the same [[time]] as when the [[First Order]] was searching for the [[Resistance]] base on [[Batuu]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. ''Timelines'' also dates the destruction of [[Kijimi]] to [[35 ABY]].</ref> he then published the recipe for the rolls in his third cookbook, ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', where he claimed that duracrete slugs were the main thing he thought of when thinking of Coruscant, also stating that the quality of the rolls was one of the few things that the upper and lower residents of Coruscant could agree on.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Canon and non-canon===
{{Quote|Many buildings on Coruscant are infested with Duracrete Slugs &ndash; hardy creatures that burrow into the rock! Find as many as you can and remove them.|Orn Free Taa|<ref name="Infinity">''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]''</ref>}}
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], duracrete slugs were first mentioned in the "Coruscant Underworld" article in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 26|twenty-sixth]] issue of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF26" /> which was published around [[July 1]], [[2015]]<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|26}}</ref> and written by [[Mark Newbold]].<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref>

Later that year, on [[August 30]],<ref name="InfinityDate">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2015/05/05/june-cover-revealed-disney-infinity-3-0.aspx|text=June Cover Revealed – Disney Infinity 3.0|author=Cork, Jeff|work=Game Informer|archivedate=20191026055312}}</ref> they then first appeared in the quest "Pest Control" in the ''Twilight of the Republic'' Play Set of the non-canon [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]''. During the quest, the player encounters [[Senator]] [[Orn Free Taa]] outside the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on Coruscant and he asks them to eliminate as many duracrete slugs as they can as part of efforts to clean up the city in preparation for the [[birthday]] celebrations of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. The player must then locate and kill four duracrete slugs in the area around [[Dex's Diner]] before returning to Taa to receive further tasks.<ref name="Infinity" />

The name duraslug was then first used in the [[2019]] audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', which was written by [[Cavan Scott]].<ref name="Dooku" /> In the [[2023]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]], the [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for Duraslug Rolls uses [[Wikipedia:Lobster|lobster]] meat in place of duracrete slug meat.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

===Origins===
{{Quote|The duracrete slug is a species-shift from the slug in the ''Wildlife of Star Wars'' book by Terryl Whitlatch. The slug is based on her design, but with a twist &mdash; instead of having a soft/slimy skin, it has an armored skin developed from consuming and excreting duracrete, so that they're really tough &mdash; and big.|Robert E. Barnes|<ref name="Archive">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/ref/f20061031/index.html|text=Building a ''Complete Visual Dictionary''}}</ref>}}
[[File:Duracrete_worm.jpg|thumb|right|200px|The duracrete slug was based on a duracrete worm sketch by Terryl Whitlatch.]]
The species was originally created by concept sculptor [[Robert E. Barnes]] in collaboration with author [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]] exclusively for use in the [[2002]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]''.<ref name="Archive" /> 

Barnes created a sculpture of the creature to be photographed for the book based on a sketch of a [[duracrete worm]] created by concept artist [[Terryl Whitlatch]] for the [[2001]] Legends book ''[[The Wildlife of Star Wars: A Field Guide]]''. Barnes shifted the design to have armored skin rather than soft skin based on the idea of the creature exuding duracrete.<ref name="Archive" />
