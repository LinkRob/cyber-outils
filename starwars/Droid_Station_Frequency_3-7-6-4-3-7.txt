'''Droid Station Frequency 3-7-6-4-3-7''' was a classified radio station hosted by [[protocol droid]] [[B6-24]] where [[droid]]s helped droids. The station played in certain areas of [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="GEStaff">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] has official backstory elements that, though not directly published for the public, are officially canon details that can be obtained verbally from cast members working in the land.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Droid Station Frequency 3-7-6-4-3-7 was created for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at Disneyland Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="GEStaff" />
