The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''12,000 BBY''', also known as '''-8723 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''11981 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''-4023''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

{{Stub}}

==Events==
*[[Ord Mantell]] is colonized by settlers from [[Corellia]], becoming an [[Ordnance/Regional Depot]].<ref name="BMF 48" />
*Around this [[time]], the [[planet]] [[Ossus]] becomes the [[Jedi]]'s cultural center, and is made a near-mandatory pilgrimage site.<ref name="Ossus cultural center">According to ''[[Nexus of Power]]'', which is set in the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Jedi]] credited [[Ossus]] as having been their cultural center for a 12,000 [[Standard year|year]] period. Since the Imperial Era began in [[19 BBY]] and ended in [[5 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', it could be found that Ossus came to be the Jedi's cultural center in approximately 12,000 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>
