The '''Battle of Birthright''' was a tradition of [[Hynestian]] [[Hynestian royal law|law]], in which any individuals entitled to becoming the [[Monarchy|ruler]] of the [[Hynestian royal family|royal family]] were required to fight any blood relations to the [[death]] to secure their title. [[Forsythia Jin]] became the [[queen]] of Hynestia by finding a loophole in the Hynestian law, moving her siblings offworld and promising to enact the Battle of Birthright if they returned. Other [[Hynestian]]s believed Jin had murdered her siblings, giving her the nickname "[[Assassin]] Queen."<ref name="Lando's Luck">''[[Lando's Luck]]''</ref>
