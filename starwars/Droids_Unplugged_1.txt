'''''Droids Unplugged'' 1''' is a [[canon]] one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] which collects the previously published comic stories "[[Probe Droid Problem]]," "[[Droid Dilemma]]," and "[[SaBBotage]]." The stories were written and illustrated by [[Chris Eliopoulos]], and the issue was published on [[June 28]], [[2017]] by [[Marvel Comics]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Collecting three stories featuring everyone's favorite droids! What adventures did the probe droids in ''The Phantom Menace'' get into when Darth Maul wasn't looking? Luke's got a mission for R2-D2! But can the astromech complete it in time? And all BB-8 wants is to help&hellip;two Resistance soldiers fall in love!''
