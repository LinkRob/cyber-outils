The '''Dug Derby''' was a [[podracing]] [[Racetrack|course]] that shared its name with the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Dug]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Malastare]]<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> The [[Prae'tus]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Volo Bolus]], a resident of Malastare, had won a race on the Dug Derby by the [[time]] they participated in the [[Hydian-Hutta Podracing Circuit]]<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> around [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> The win was recorded on their downloadable [[Racer|racing]] profile.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Dug Derby was mentioned in the [[2025]] [[Industrial Light & Magic]] [[Star Wars video games|virtual reality game]] [[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]].<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> The [[Dug Derby/Legends|course]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']], a [[1999]] video game released by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="Racer">[[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']]</ref>
