'''''Disney SpellStruck''''' is a mobile word [[Star Wars video games|game]] developed and published by [[Artist Arcade]]<ref name="AppStore" /> and [[Fun + Games]], in collaboration with [[The Walt Disney Company|Walt Disney Games]]. It is a game exclusive to the [[Wikipedia:Apple Arcade|Apple Arcade]] subscription service.<ref name="SWPress" /> Gameplay is similar to other popular mobile crossword-style spelling games such as [[Wikipedia:Words with Friends|''Words with Friends'']], but with the twist of playing as and against characters, each having special power-ups that modify the rules of the game, as well as story elements.<ref name="AppStore" /> The initial game, featuring many animated Disney characters, was released on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="Release" /> On [[February 19]], [[2026]], the game released a major update featuring a ''[[Star Wars]]'' takeover of the app's content. This update was based around {{Film|IV}},<ref name="SWPress" /> but the game teased future episodic updates featuring other ''Star Wars'' media.<ref name="SpellStruckHome">{{WebCite|url=https://spellstruck.app/|text=About|work=[https://spellstruck.app SpellStruck.app]|archivedate=20260222195147}}</ref>
