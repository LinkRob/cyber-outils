The '''Crothy system''' was located in [[Seitia sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="WesternReaches" /> The [[star system]] was situated in grid square K-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the location [[Crothy]].<ref name="ITD" />

By [[232 BBY]],<ref name="ITD Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> Crothy was dealing with an outbreak of [[Disease|fever]].<ref name="ITD" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="ITD Date" /> an [[Unidentified individual (Crothy)|individual]] and their colleagues attempted to help bring fever vaccines to the location with a [[transport]]. However, they were prevented from delivering them to Crothy when the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] disrupted [[hyperspace]] traffic [[The galaxy|galaxy]]-wide. In the disaster's aftermath, the individual asked the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Reath Silas]] whether anyone else had been able to reach Crothy or if they had been able to contain the fever, questions to which Silas did not have an answer.<ref name="ITD" />
