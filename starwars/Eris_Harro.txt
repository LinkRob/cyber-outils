'''Eris Harro''' was an individual who served the [[Galactic Empire]] as [[HNN Bureau Chief|Bureau Chief]] for the [[Outer Rim Node]] of the [[HoloNet News]] agency in the<ref name="Pin" /> [[city]]<ref name="TK-462">{{InsiderCite|166|TK-462 (short story)}}</ref> of [[Phelar]], on<ref name="Pin" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[Eriadu]]. Harro, along with [[Minister of Information]] [[Pollux Hax]], dispatched messages welcoming new members into the [[Imperial Press Corps]], a news organization comprised of what Harro and Hax claimed to be [[the galaxy]]'s most esteemed [[journalist]]s. Enclosed with each message was a badge which granted the authority to publicize all transgressions against the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Pin" />

Harro and Hax also sent wanted [[Painting|posters]] depicting [[Spectres|several rebels]]<ref name="Posters">[[HoloNet News promotional posters]]</ref> who were active on the world of [[Lothal]]<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref> to Imperial Press Corps members, as well as a communiqué with instructions to spread the word. Journalists were reminded, however, to report sightings of the rebels to the local Imperial [[garrison]] rather than attempt to detain the criminals themselves.<ref name="Posters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eris Harro was first named in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a card sent to real-world journalists in [[2014]], alongside a pin of the Imperial Press Corps' emblem, as a promotion for the then-upcoming animated series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. While one face of the card represented an [[in-universe]] communiqué sent by Harro and Pollux Hax, the flip side noted ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]'', the series premiere movie, would air on [[October 3]], 2014, while the series itself would begin on [[October 13]].<ref name="Pin" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Eris Harro/Legends|Eris Harro]] first appeared in "[[The Guns of Kelrodo-Ai (short story)|The Guns of Kelrodo-Ai]]," a short story written by [[Jason Fry]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 132|the 132nd issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="Guns">{{InsiderCite|132|The Guns of Kelrodo-Ai (short story)}}</ref> on [[March 13]], [[2012]]<ref name="Titan">{{TitanMagazines|url=t/star-wars-insider/us/132|text=Star Wars Insider Issue #132}}</ref> as a tie-in to the reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'',<ref name="Guns" /> which was written by Fry and [[Paul R. Urquhart]] and released that same year.<ref name="Warfare">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref> Harro's surname in "The Guns of Kelrodo-Ai" was spelled as "Herro,"<ref name="Guns" /> while "Harro" was used in ''The Essential Guide to Warfare'',<ref name="Warfare" /> with the latter being the same spelling utilized on the ''Star Wars Rebels'' promotional cards.<ref name="Pin" />
