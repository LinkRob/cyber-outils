'''Coaxium regulators''' were devices used in [[starship]] [[engine]]s, designed to regulate the [[coaxium]] used for [[hyperspace]] travel. Such regulators were extremely cold in temperature, and shot a [[Color|green]] beam of energy into a ship's engines which was powerful enough to tear a vessel in two if the regulator was dislodged. During the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]<ref name="Into the Dark">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref> in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="SWTHR date">{{SWYouTube|xCat5fXRyyU|Star Wars: The High Republic &#124; Announcement Trailer|sw_url=video/announce-trailer-star-wars-the-high-republic}} states that the events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' take place 200 [[Standard year|years]] prior to the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the [[transport]] ''[[Vessel]]''{{'s}} coaxium regulator became unhitched from its station after the starship was sideswiped by hyperspace debris from the ''[[Legacy Run]]''. Copilot [[Affie Hollow]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Reath Silas]], one of the ship's passengers, were able to repair the regulator mounting and prevent further damage.<ref name="Into the Dark" />
