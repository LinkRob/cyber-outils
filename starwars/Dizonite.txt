The '''Dizonites''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] native to the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] [[Dizon Fray]]. At some point during the [[Imperial Era]]<ref name="Nobody's Listening" /> prior to [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The [[massacre of the Dizonites]] occurs prior to the events of "[[Nobody's Listening!]]," which takes place approximately two months after "[[Kassa (episode)|Kassa]]," which dates itself to 5 BBY.</ref> all of the Dizonites present on their homeworld were [[Massacre of the Dizonites|massacred]] by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces, and the genocide was broadcast and recorded as proof of mission.<ref name="Nobody's Listening" />

The choral dying screams of the Dizonites caused severe emotional distress to those who listened to them, leading to the audio's use as a [[torture]] method by the Imperial interrogator [[Doctor]] [[Gorst]]. Gorst curated the recordings to use what he believed were the screams of Dizonite [[youngling]]s to cause as much distress as possible. The audio recordings were used by Gorst to torture the [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Bix Caleen]] in 5 BBY, which emotionally crippled Caleen and allowed [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[lieutenant]] [[Dedra Meero]] to successfully interrogate her for information regarding her friend, [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]].<ref name="Nobody's Listening" /> Caleen later forced Gorst to listen to the same recording before she and Andor killed him.<ref name="What a Festive Evening">{{Andor|What a Festive Evening}}</ref>
