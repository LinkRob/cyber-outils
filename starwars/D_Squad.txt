'''D Squad''' was a [[squad]] of five [[B1-series battle droid]]s that were part of<ref name="Last Battle" /> [[General]] [[Kalani]]'s [[Agamar garrison|garrison]]<ref name="USNWE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> on [[Agamar]]. After a [[Mission to Agamar (2 BBY)|skirmish]] against several rebels from [[Phoenix Cell]], the Separatist holdout and the rebels ceased hostilities and fought together against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Some of the battle droids then escaped with Kalani and the rebels from Agamar.<ref name="Last Battle" />
