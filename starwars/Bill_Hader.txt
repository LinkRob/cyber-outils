'''William Thomas Hader Jr.''' (born [[June 7]], [[1978]]) is an American [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]], comedian, writer, producer, and director who provided the voice for [[BB-8]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] along with [[Ben Schwartz]]. In a [[2023]] interview, however, Hader mused that he likely did not deserve credit for the performance. He claimed that he initially "did some voice stuff that just didn't work" and was later invited back by director [[J.J. Abrams]] to operate a [[Wikipedia:Talk box|talk box]] for the character's voice. He called Abrams "very sweet" for putting his name on the final product, as he felt that operating the talk box was something that anybody could have done.<ref name="Bill Hader Variety interview" />

Hader was also a production assistant on the [[2004]] documentary ''[[Empire of Dreams: The Story of the Star Wars Trilogy]]'', for which he went to [[Mark Hamill]]'s house to pick up some pictures of the actor from his wife.<ref name="Going over to Mark's house">{{WebCite|url=https://www.interviewmagazine.com/film/mark-hamill|title=
Jedi master Mark Hamill geeks out with Bill Hader|work=[[Wikipedia:Interview (magazine)|''Interview'']]|accessdate=2025-11-21|author=Bill Hader|date=2017-11-09|archivedate=20171110000710}}</ref>

In [[2011]], he appeared in the video "Stand Up To Cancer - ''Star Wars''," with other celebrities like [[Samuel L. Jackson]], [[Jaime King]], and Emma Stone, recreating classic ''[[Star Wars]]'' scenes to raise awareness for cancer research.<ref name="Youtube">{{SWYouTube|video=UM0-A12MmVs|text=Stand Up To Cancer - Star Wars}}</ref>

In [[2017]], Hader interviewed Mark Hamill ahead of the release of [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] release, for the magazine [[Wikipedia:Interview (magazine)|''Interview'']].<ref name="Going over to Mark's house" />
