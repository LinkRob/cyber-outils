'''Force visions''', also known as '''Force premonitions''', were an [[Force power|ability]] of [[the Force]] to see into the past, the future, and other places. It was once possessed by all [[Jedi]] but grew rare in the final [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Some [[Sith]] also experienced Force visions.

==Description==
{{Quote|Jedi are known to have elaborate—and often cryptic—visions of the past, present, and future. These visions can occur at any time but are particularly common during meditation or sleep, or upon entering a location highly attuned to the Force. They can serve as grim warnings of what is to come, but they can also hold secrets and solutions that can aid the Jedi in their quests. Although they are filled with deep meaning, these visions can be just as mysterious as the Force itself.|[[Luke Skywalker]]|<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Destiny-2020StarWars4.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Luke Skywalker experiences a vision of his past, present, and future.]]
Force visions, also known as Force premonitions,<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> were an aspect of [[the Force]], an [[Force power|ability]] to see into the past, the future and other places.<ref name="Episode V" /> The power of [[psychometry]] functioned by giving the Force user a vision<ref name="THR (2022) 2">[[The High Republic (2022) 2|''The High Republic'' (2022) 2]]</ref> of the past of an object and those who had interacted with it.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

[[Sith]], as [[Force-sensitive|Force users]], were also capable of experiencing Force visions.<ref name="Episode VI" /> Despite their prophetic nature, Force visions were not absolute in their content and could be left up to much interpretation, with the emotions of the witness potentially clouding their judgement on the issue or otherwise causing them to misread the vision.<ref name="MA">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> Some Jedi attributed this to the future constantly being in motion, with the visions merely warning of what could happen.<ref name="Episode V" /> As such, visions were treated with great caution by the Order.<ref name="MA" /> Nevertheless, users of pyschometry were greatly respected as useful allies,<ref name="Hunt for Ziro">{{TCW|Hunt for Ziro}}</ref> with their visions of the past<ref name="THR (2022) 2" /> being useful in hunting down fugitives or gathering evidence.<ref name="Hunt for Ziro" />

==Users==
===Republic Era===
====High Republic Era====
During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Path of the Open Hand]], a radical anti-Force wielder cult, was led by the Force-sensitive [[Elecia Zeveron]], who was known to her followers as "the [[Mother (title)|Mother]]" and hid her own use of the Force. She claimed to receive visions from the Force but, in fact, was little more than a con artist who tried to guide the Path to her own ends.<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref>

In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="382 Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the Path undertook a [[Mission to Planet X (Path of the Open Hand)|mission]] to the so-called [[Sophros|Planet X]], where they hoped to uncover eggs of the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] [[species]] for use against Force-sensitives as part of their mission to "free" the Force from use. Despite the cult ideals, Zeveron allowed a Force-sensitive [[Setaran]] tracker named [[Calar]], who had joined the cult all the same and tried to limit his Force usage, to join the mission as their tracker. As the Path breached [[The Veil (Sophros)|the Veil]] around Planet X, however, he experienced a vision that struck fear into his heart; Calar realized that their mission would have grave ramifications for the galaxy and the Force so tried to destroy their [[starship]], only to be shot dead by [[pilot]] [[Radicaz Dobbs]]. Later in the mission, Path member [[Bokana Koss]], whose latent Force-sensitivity was unearthed by the visit to Planet X, had a fearful vision of a coming [[blight]] to be brought on from their mission, revealing to Koss that their actions were helpful to the so-called [[Under-Dweller]]s and terrifying him. However, he was able to shake off that feeling and help the mission afterward.<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />

While she was a [[Padawan]], [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] developed a tendency for having Force visions while traveling in [[hyperspace]], which she called "hyperspace visions." They stopped after her Knighting, but she later had one of a [[Nihil]] [[Attack on Tiikae|attack]] on [[Tiikae]] shortly before the [[starship]] she was travelling on was diverted to assist there. The aging Force-sensitive [[Mari San Tekka]] was drawing Rwoh to her, intending to give Rwoh her final hyperspace [[Path]] before [[Death|passing on]].<ref name="OOTS">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref>

====Late Republic Era====
[[File:KrrsishVisions-Yoda4.jpg|thumb|right|270px|Jedi youngling Krrsish has a Force vision of Trandoshans and Wookiees waring.]]
The [[Wookiee]] [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Krrsish]] had a Force vision of [[Trandoshan]]s and Wookiees fighting,<ref name="Yoda 4">[[Yoda 4|''Yoda'' 4]]</ref> leading to him becoming distant to his friend and fellow youngling [[Gheyr]], who he foresaw falling to the [[dark side of the Force]].<ref name="Yoda 5">[[Yoda 5|''Yoda'' 5]]</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]] had claimed to Gheyr should not tell anyone else of the vision nor openly act on it due to the Order's bias against believing Force visions.<ref name="Yoda 4" /> Ultimately, Krrsish's fear overwhelmed him, leading to him [[Duel on Corvair II|attacking]] Gheyr during a [[Initiate Trials|trial]] on [[Corvair II]].<ref name="Yoda 5" />

Prior to the [[Clone Wars]], the Jedi Master [[Sifo-Dyas]] had foreseen [[The galaxy|the galactic]] conflict, but his ideas of raising an [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]] in defense of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] had been dismissed as too extreme, leading to his dismissal from the [[Jedi High Council]].<ref name="The Lost One">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref><ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Undeterred, Sifo-Dyas pursued his vision anyways, secretly ordering a [[Cloning|clone]] army from the [[Kaminoan]]s.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>

Jedi Master [[Yoda]] was able to see into the future, though he understood that [[The Force|Force]] prophecy was not absolute and that the future was always in motion.<ref name="Episode V" /> His visions led him to foresee the [[Great Jedi Purge|fall]] of the Jedi in the [[Clone Wars]] and even his own [[death]].<ref name="Sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref> Yoda once even had a vision of the exact moment [[Order 66]] was issued, but factors including the brevity of the vision, the Jedi Master's wariness towards such visions, and his uneasiness about the [[clone trooper]]s' genetically-encoded desire to be obedient prevented him from realizing the true import of the brief glimpse.<ref name="Sharing the Same Face">{{StoryCite|story=Sharing the Same Face|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref>

[[File:AnakinVaderVision-GOM.png|thumb|left|250px|Anakin Skywalker had a vision of himself becoming Darth Vader.]]
As the [[Chosen One]], [[Anakin Skywalker]] had extremely powerful Force visions in his [[dream]]s when [[Sleep|asleep]], and his visions were never known to be wrong. He foresaw the death of his [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|mother]] following terrible [[torture]] by [[Tusken Raider]]s and was [[Mission to rescue Shmi Skywalker Lars|unable]] to stop it, and she died in his arms.<ref name="Episode II" /> Later, he witnessed through the Force his own [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|wife's]] death in [[Reproduction|childbirth]] and was determined to prevent it from happening. However, he neglected to remember the fact that such visions were not absolute; only that what he saw was what ''might'' happen. Skywalker fell to the dark side and became [[Darth]] Vader to try to prevent it, but instead he caused it to happen; when his [[Marriage|wife]] discovered what he had done, she was horrified and then [[Force choke|wounded]] by her husband in his rage, subsequently dying from medically unexplainable reasons after delivering her children.<ref name="Episode III" />

[[Jedi General]] [[Pong Krell]], during the Clone Wars, foresaw the [[Jedi Order]]'s destruction and the Republic being "ripped apart from the inside" to become the [[Galactic Empire]]. This caused Krell to fall to the dark side and begin sabotaging his campaigns in an effort to become [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s new [[Sith apprentice]] and forge his place in the "new order" he saw coming. However, Krell was unmasked as a [[Treason|traitor]] during the [[Battle of Umbara]] and subsequently arrested by the [[501st Legion]]. Krell tauntingly explained his vision to [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] as he assumed the [[Umbaran]]s would free him if they captured the base, but the [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s chose to execute Krell due to that precise danger, which ultimately never transpired.<ref name="Carnage of Krell">{{TCW|Carnage of Krell}}</ref>

At the end of the Clone Wars, [[Maul]] had a vision of Anakin Skywalker's fall to the dark side and transformation into the [[Sith apprentice]] of [[Darth Sidious]]. In an effort to prevent Sidious from turning Skywalker into his apprentice, Maul orchestrated a [[Mandalorian Civil War (19 BBY)|civil war]] on [[Mandalore]], hoping to draw him out and kill him along with [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref> [[Ahsoka Tano]], Skywalker's former Padawan, later had a vision of Skywalker's fall as it occurred, but only realized that "something terrible" had happened, and the issuing of Order 66 meant she had no further time to think about the vision as she was immediately thrown into a [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|fight for her life]].<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref>

[[Depa Billaba]] was able to receive visions during [[meditation]] and to pass them on through touch to her Padawan, [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]].<ref name="Kanan 2" /> Dume was taught that visions were difficult and nearly impossible to interpret; one's emotions could cloud them and it took training and discipline to see things clearly. He later passed on these [[Jedi training|teachings]] to [[Ezra Bridger]] during the [[Imperial Era]] after his Padawan misinterpreted his first vision.<ref name="Vision of Hope">{{Rebels|Vision of Hope}}</ref>

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|Through the Force, things you will see. Other places. The future... the past. Old friends long gone.|Yoda tutors Luke Skywalker in the ways of the Force|<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref>}}
In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Star Wars: Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Jedi Padawan [[Ezra Bridger]] experienced a dark Force vision when he was in the [[Lothal Jedi Temple|Lothal Jedi temple]]. Bridger fell from a cliff that was inside the temple, and he his vision made him fall in his room that was on the ''[[Ghost]]''. He walked outside and into the hallway, where he heard the [[Spectres]] talking about his lack of Jedi knowledge and training. [[Garazeb Orrelios|Zeb Orrelios]] found Bridger spying on what the Spectres were talking about, [[Sabine Wren]] questioned how long Ezra was hearing them. He crossed his shoulders and replied "long enough." As Bridger and Orrelios started an argument, Bridger began to realize he was experiencing an illusion. He became angry and ran away from the Spectres. After running away, the [[Grand Inquisitor]] appeared and the door of the Ghost went shut. The Inquisitor ignited his [[lightsaber]] and [[Death|killed]] the Spectres. The door opened afterwards, and the Inquisitor slowly chased Bridger on the Ghost. Suddenly, Bridger was then pulled into space and was back in the Lothal Jedi temple.<ref name="Path of the Jedi">{{Rebels|Path of the Jedi}}</ref>

[[File:ForceVision-EaW.png|thumb|right|220px|Luke had a Force vision of Obi-Wan Kenobi while on Hoth.]]
[[Luke Skywalker]] had a vision on [[Hoth]] after he escaped from the cave of a [[wampa]]. In it, Obi-Wan Kenobi appeared and directed him to travel to [[Dagobah]] where he would find Yoda and learn the ways of the Force from him.<ref name="EaW">{{Destiny|set=Empire at War|cardname=Force Vision|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/03129}}</ref> Once on Dagobah, he experienced another vision, one of his friends suffering in a [[Cloud City|city]] [[Bespin|in the clouds]]. Although his [[Jedi training|training]] was incomplete, he rushed to aid his friends, ignoring the warnings of both Yoda and Kenobi, who warned that such a decision was rash and that he needed further training. His [[duel on Cloud City]] ended in failure, losing his lightsaber and right hand to Darth Vader.<ref name="Episode V" />

While visiting the [[Cave of Evil]] on Dagobah, Skywalker experienced a dark side vision of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. After fighting the spectral Vader, Luke Skywalker succeeded in decapitating the sith lord. Shortly after doing so, however, Darth Vader's helmet exploded, revealing Luke's own face underneath. This was an ominous sign that Luke wasn't as safe from the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] as he would like to believe, a warning that deeply rattled Skywalker.<ref name="Episode V" />

[[Mission to Cloud City|Returning]] to Cloud City to retrieve his lightsaber, Skywalker had a force vision of events in his past and future, including seeing a hooded figure tell him to follow his destiny. The vision convinced him to abandon his hunt for his lightsaber to instead save his friend [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], whom he saw in the vision encased in [[carbonite]].<ref name="Star Wars 2020 4">[[Star Wars (2020) 4|''Star Wars'' (2020) 4]]</ref> The hooded figure, he later learned, was a former Padawan named [[Verla]].<ref name="Star Wars 2020 5">[[Star Wars (2020) 5|''Star Wars'' (2020) 5]]</ref>

Darth Sidious was able to use the dark side to peer into the future and manipulate events on a [[Galaxy|galactic]] scale to suit his ends. Despite this, he was unable to foresee his own demise when Darth Vader turned from the dark side to help his son Luke overthrow him.<ref name="Episode VI" />

===New Republic Era===
[[File:LukesMistake-TLJ.png|thumb|left|220px|Luke ignites his lightsaber after witnessing a horrifying vision of destruction, pain, and death caused by Ben Solo.]]
In [[28 ABY]] Luke Skywalker, sensing the darkness within his nephew Ben growing in glimpses during his training, decided to probe Solo's mind as he lay asleep. He learned that Snoke had turned his apprentice's heart to the dark side, and experienced a brief dark vision that Solo would bring nothing but death and destruction and leave Skywalker's loved ones in anguish. In a flash of defensive instinct, Skywalker thought he could stop that future and for a brief moment of pure instinct he raised his lightsaber to destroy him, believing that it was the only way to halt his descent into darkness and prevent the end of everything he loved. This instinct was gone almost the second that it had come, and Skywalker was immediately filled with shame.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

Solo had already awoken to see his uncle standing over him with his lightsaber raised. Confused and angry, Solo pulled his own [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|lightsaber]] to him in self-defense and attacked Skywalker, using the Force to pull down the hut on top of him. Solo emerged from the hut and screamed toward the temple, asking Skywalker why he had done it, and in his anger bringing down a lightning strike with the Force that destroyed the temple and set it on fire. When Skywalker came around, he emerged from the rubble of the hut to find his temple destroyed, with his other apprentices either killed or vanished along with Solo. This effectively left Skywalker the last of the Jedi once more as he was after the death of his own master, Yoda, decades earlier.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Resistance Era===
[[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] experienced a Force vision of her past and future upon touching the [[Skywalker lightsaber|lightsaber]] that once belonged to Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> While training with Luke Skywalker on [[Ahch-To]] she had another vision, this one of multiple mirror images of herself.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

[[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] of the [[First Order]] had visions that led him to find shards of archaic lore in the old bases of operations once owned by [[Snoke]], his master and predecessor whom he had [[Battle of Oetchi|killed]], leading him to discover that a darker force was behind Snoke's power:<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> the returned Darth Sidious.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> When Ren confronted Sidious on [[Exegol]], Sidious conjured a Force vision before Ren that allowed him to witness the rise of the [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[fleet]], the [[Final Order]], from beneath Exegol's ground surface. The image eventually disappeared and switched to the marching of [[Sith trooper]]s before Ren set aside the vision.<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

[[File:DarkReyLightsaber-TLC.png|thumb|right|220px|Rey's vision of her dark side self.]]
While in the [[Emperor's Throne Room]] amidst the ruins of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] on [[Kef Bir]],<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> Rey had a vision<ref name="Rey Bio Gallery">{{Databank|rey-biography-gallery|Rey Biography Gallery}}</ref> of a dark side phantasm<ref name="TLC">''[[Star Wars: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref>&mdash;herself as a dark side user&mdash;when she touched [[Emperor's wayfinder|the Emperor's]] [[Sith wayfinder|Sith]] [[wayfinder]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

After the death of his mother Leia, who had sacrificed her life to save her son, Ben Solo encountered a memory of his deceased father Han Solo on [[Kef Bir]]. Solo, whom Ben had slain during the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], encouraged his son to give up the dark side and fulfill what Leia had been fighting for most of her adult life. Following his deceased father's impassioned plea, Ben Solo renounced the dark side of the Force, later traveling to Exegol in order to help Rey defeat the resurgent Darth Sidious, once and for all.<ref name="Episode IX" />
