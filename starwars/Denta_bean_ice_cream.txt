'''Denta bean ice cream''' was a flavor of [[Ice cream/Legends|ice cream]] which tasted like [[Denta bean/Legends|denta beans]]. It was served in [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]], on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], in a variety of styles including soft ice cream, or in a [[Neuvian sundae]].
