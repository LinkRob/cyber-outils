'''First Order officers'''<ref name="FOTF">''[[Star Wars Adventures: Flight of the Falcon]]''</ref> were [[officer]]s who held a position of [[Military rank|rank]] and authority in the [[First Order]]<ref name="WIRED Arsenal of Weapons">{{WebCite|url=https://www.wired.com/2015/11/star-wars-force-awakens-props|text=''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Arsenal of Battle Props|work=Wired|archivedate=20190129062057}}</ref>&mdash;a military junta inspired by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Abrams Luke Skywalker">{{WebCite|url=http://www.ew.com/article/2015/08/12/star-wars-luke-skywalker-hooked-jj-abrams/2|text=''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'': J.J. Abrams explores 'Who IS Luke Skywalker &hellip;?'|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2015-08-12|archivedate=20200607192519}}</ref><ref name="Abrams First Order">{{WebCite|url=http://www.empireonline.com/news/story.asp?NID=45203|text=Kylo Ren Is Not A Sith|author=Dyer, James|date=2015-08-25|work=Empire Magazine|archivedate=20151018205158}}</ref> They wore distinctive [[Color|dark]] [[Clothing|uniforms]]<ref name="Episode VII" /> and [[hat]]s, and wielded the [[SE-44]] or [[SE-44C blaster pistol|SE-44C]] models of [[blaster]] [[Blaster pistol|pistol]].<ref name="WIRED Arsenal of Weapons" /> [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] was one such officer.<ref name="DB">{{Databank|general-hux|General Hux}}</ref> First Order officers of a particularly high rank were granted access to the [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|''Upsilon''-class command shuttles]].<ref name="Upsilon X-Wing Miniatures">{{FFGXW|set=Upsilon-class Shuttle Expansion Pack}}</ref> Because of the First Order believing in a hierarchy, it was forbidden for a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] to initiate contact with a First Order officer.<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
