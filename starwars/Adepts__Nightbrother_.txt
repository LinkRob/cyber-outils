'''Adepts''' were [[Force-sensitive]] [[Nightbrothers]] whom were trained to use [[the Force]] in order to augment their physical abilities to improve their skills as [[assassin]]s and [[bodyguard]]s in service to the [[Nightsisters]]. Unlike their Nightsister counterparts, they were forbidden from learning [[magick]].<ref name="DoR">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref>
