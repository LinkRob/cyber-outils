'''Coronet City''', also known as '''Corellia City''', or as the '''Golden City''', or the '''Unbroken Clan Territory''', was the [[capital]] [[city]] located on the prestigious [[Core Worlds|Core World]] [[planet]] [[Corellia]].

==Characteristics==
The [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Corellia]],<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> Coronet City, also known as Corellia City,<ref name="Tales from Vandor" /> or Golden City,<ref name="Crimson Climb">''[[Crimson Climb]]''</ref> had one known district, the [[Teeno Village district]], as well as [[Hovertrain|mag-lev]] subways and trains, which stopped at various stations, among them [[Juni Street Station]]. The city was known for its nightlife which included several [[casino]]s and [[Peace and Security Headquarters|Peace and Security stations]], one of which shared a basement with a [[Unidentified Coronet City museum|museum]]. [[Diadem Square]] was located in the downtown area of the city, and had stores and [[air-tuk]]s. The city also had its own [[HoloNet]] news channel, known as [[Coronet City Info]], abbreviated as CCI.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Many dignitaries were in the city at any given time which is why fierce [[Politics|political]] fighting out of greed and a lust for power were common place, opening up a market for [[bodyguard]] agencies. [[Assassin]]ations occurred from [[time]] to time.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

Located on the outreaches of the city, away from the many skyscrapers, were conveyer belts, assembly plants and cranes.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

==History==
{{Quote|Welcome to ''Coronet City''&hellip;or better known now as the Unbroken Clan Territory. Which makes it ''my'' territory.|Vukorah|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 21" />}}
By the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Jedi Order]] had established a [[Corellian Jedi temple|temple]] in Coronet City. <ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> Around [[230 BBY]],<ref name="THR Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> the [[Nihil]] staged an [[Battle of the Corellian Shipyards|attack]] on Coronet City to try to get the [[Santhe Shipyards]]. However, the attack was stopped by the [[Jedi]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

After she [[Assassination of the Unbroken Clan Grand Leader|took over]] control of the [[Unbroken Clan]], [[Grand Leader]] [[Vukorah]] had the Clan take over control of the city with it becoming known as the Unbroken Clan Territory.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 21" />

[[File:Unlimited-PrecisionFire.png|thumb|left|300px|An Imperial [[stormtrooper]] in Coronet City takes aim]]
During the late stages of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Corellian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Dengar]] and [[Mercurial Swift]] once had a [[Unarmed combat|fistfight]] on a [[holoplex]] rooftop in the city.<ref name="Aftermath" />

In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> a group known as the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] began to attract followers in the city but also harangued the locals. The Acolytes, [[Dark side adept|dark-side adherents]] and worshipers of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], organized a [[Coronet City revolution|revolution]], which involved coordinated attacks on several locations throughout the city including Diadem Square, the 1-line of the mag-lev subway, Juni Street Station, and several casinos. Acolytes also jumped on top of several air-tuks to pull them down to the ground. These attacks were a ruse to enable the Acolytes [[Kiza]] and [[Remi]] to attack a Peace and Security station in order to steal a [[Color|red]]-bladed [[Kiza's lightsaber|lightsaber]] from its basement.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coronet City first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] book ''[[Ambush at Corellia]]'', the first book in ''[[Star Wars: The Corellian Trilogy]]''. It was written by [[Roger MacBride Allen]] and published by [[Bantam Spectra]] in [[February]] [[1995]]. The city became [[canon]] in [[Chuck Wendig]]'s [[2015]] novel, [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']].
