'''Flare Ten''' was the [[callsign]] assigned to the tenth [[pilot]] of the [[New Republic]]'s [[Flare Squadron]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a [[Corellian]] [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] was designated this callsign during a [[Attack on Chadawa|battle]] with the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] over the [[planet]] [[Chadawa]], where the [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] pilot was able to get a solid hit on one of the [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvettes]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
