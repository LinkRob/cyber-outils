{{Quote|This is a warlord named ''Burnium Ro''. He is operating out of a stronghold on the Mid Rim world ''Ikkrukk''.|Wilhuff Tarkin, to Ellian Zahra when showing her a hologram of Burnium Ro|<ref name="SW7" />}}
A [[Color|gray]] '''stronghold''' was located on the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Ikkrukk]] and was owned by the [[warlord]] [[Burnium Ro]], who operated out of it. The stronghold was built inside a [[mountain]] and had several windows.<ref name="SW7" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Ellian Zahra]] was sent by [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to Ikkrukk with orders to [[Death|kill]] Ro. Tarkin lent his personal [[corvette]], ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', to Zahra to accomplish her [[Mission to Ikkrukk|mission]].<ref name="SW7" />

Once Zahra arrived on Ikkrukk, several [[probe droid]]s were dispatched to follow Ro from a social club in the local [[Grail City]] to his stronghold, unbeknownst that the subject being followed was a decoy. Once the fake Ro arrived at the base, Zahra gave the order to the ''Carrion Spike''{{'s}} crew to destroy the stronghold, killing everyone inside. After returning to Tarkin, Zahra learned that she had only eliminated a decoy and that her mission was incomplete.<ref name="SW7" />

==Behind the scenes==
Burnium Ro's stronghold appeared in a flashback in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Star Wars (2020) 7|''Star Wars'' 7]], which was published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="SW7" /> on [[October 7]], [[2020]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80516/star_wars_2020_7|text=Star Wars (2020) #7}}</ref> The comic was written by [[Charles Soule]] and illustrated by [[Ramon Rosanas]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]].<ref name="SW7" />
