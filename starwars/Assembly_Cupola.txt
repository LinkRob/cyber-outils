The '''Assembly Cupola'''<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> was a structure within [[Csaplar]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[homeworld]] [[Csilla]]. The [[Syndic]]s of the [[Chiss Syndicure]] and [[Aristocra]] of the [[Chiss]] [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] met annually in the Cupola for important government meetings. Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' in [[19 BBY]].</ref> the Syndics were in attendance of one such meeting, during which time Csilla was [[Attack on Csilla|attacked]] by [[Paataatus Hiveborn|unknown assailants]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
