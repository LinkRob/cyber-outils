An '''abandoned sarlacc pit''' on [[Tatooine]] was inhabited by a [[leviathan krayt dragon]], which the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] speculated to have eaten the [[sarlacc]]. Members of a tribe of [[Tusken Raider]]s regularly visited the pit to feed the [[Krayt dragon|dragon]], in hopes that it would [[sleep]] longer. The pit was eventually the site of an [[Mission to Tatooine (Din Djarin)|effort]] between Djarin, Cobb Vanth, the citizens of [[Freetown|Mos Pelgo]], and the tribe of Tusken Raiders to [[Death|eliminate]] the krayt dragon living inside the pit.<ref name="Chapter9" />
