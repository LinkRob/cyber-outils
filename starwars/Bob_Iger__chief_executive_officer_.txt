'''Bob Iger''' is the [[Wikipedia:Chief executive officer|Chief executive officer]] of [[The Walt Disney Company]]. In [[2012]], Bob Iger signed the deal with [[George Lucas]], the creator of ''[[Star Wars]]'', that transferred ownership of [[Lucasfilm]] and the ''Star Wars'' franchise to The Walt Disney Company.<ref name="New Movies Announced">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-movies-announced-as-disney-enters-agreement-to-acquire-lucasfilm-ltd|text=New Star Wars Movies Announced as Disney Enters Agreement to Acquire Lucasfilm Ltd.}}</ref>
