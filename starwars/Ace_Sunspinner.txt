'''Ace Sunspinner''' was a [[pirate]] who had joined the [[Rokana Raiders]] by [[3 ABY]].

==Biography==
Ace Sunspinner was a [[pirate]] who had joined the [[Rokana Raiders]]<ref name="Outlaws" />  by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> While with the raiders he became an outspoken critic of the raider's leader, [[Stinger Tash]], who he called a bad [[captain]]. So much so that [[deckmaster]] [[Offo]] sent a message to other members of the pirate gang instructing them to shutdown Sunspinner's criticism if they heard it.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> Sunspinner was present at the pirate base's landing pad in the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Sayma Nebula]] when a disguised [[Kay Vess]] transported a pretend captive [[Hondo Ohnaka]] back to the base after his previous escape. After a brief exchange with Vess and Ohnaka, Sunspinner retired to the base's [[cantina|tavern]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Vess met up with him at the tavern later on where he continued to openly complain about Tash to Vess.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}
Later that day, Tash successfully broke into the tomb's burial chamber. When Sunspiner and several other raiders joined her there, Ohnaka was there with a piece of the [[Architect's Scepter]] which was needed to access the treasure. After initially threatening to smash the section to pieces, Ohnaka proceeded to challenged Tash for leadership of the raiders. Vess, still disguise as a raider spoke for Ohnaka before the group put it up for a vote.<ref name="Outlaws" />  
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
While speaking for Ohnaka, Vess had the opportunity to repeat Sunspinner's belief that Tash was a bad captain in order to win Sunspinner's vote for Hondo. After the vote, disaster struct which resulted in activation of the tomb's [[Khepi guardian droid|sentry droids]] killing many of the raiders and causing the rest to flee the base.<ref name="Outlaws" />  
{{EndGame}}

==Personality and traits==
[[File:AceSunspinner-Voting-Outlaws.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Ace Sunspinner voting for the captain of the Rokana Raiders]]
Sunspinner was a human with tan skin and [[color|brown]] [[eye]]s. He was not shy about his criticism of the Rokana Raiders' captain, Stinger Tash.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

==Equipment==
While he was a member of the Rokana Raiders he wore a grey an purple fight [[helmet]], a purple [[clothing|vest]] over a blue shirt and brown pants with black finger-less groves and a grey double wrapped belt. His vest bore the Raiders' icon and he had metal knee protection.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Ace Sunspinner appeared in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', which released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirates Fortune">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune|text=Steal a Look at ''Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune'', Available May 15}}</ref> During the vote for leadership there are three possible outcomes depending on the players action: Tash wins and activates the sentry droids, Ohnaka wins and activates the sentry droids, or Ohnaka wins and Tash overloads the base's generator causing nebula gasses to flood the base. With each of the outcomes, Sunspinner is not shown to have died making his fate currently unknown.<ref name="Outlaws" />
