A '''bellfruit''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|edible]] [[fruit]]. At some point during the [[Imperial Era]], the residents of the [[Imperial Elderblock 26-0640]] had been served rotten bellfruit for dinner. During her [[Investigation of the murder of Armen Sim|investigation]] of the murder of her former fellow resident [[Armen Sim]], [[Urbra Procura]] negatively remembered the incident.<ref name="Elderblock">{{InsiderCite|236|Galactic Tales: A Death in Elderblock 26-0640}}</ref>
