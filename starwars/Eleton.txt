'''Eleton''' was a type of [[blaster gas]] used from the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="First Duty">{{InsiderCite|issue1=201|issue2=202|Starlight: First Duty}}</ref> until at least the [[Galactic Civil War]]. It could be compressed and carried inside small ampoules.<ref name="ROVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> 

A small amount of the [[gas]] was used by [[Ambassador]] [[Ceeril]] to stage an [[assassin]]ation attempt against himself.<ref name="First Duty" /> Decades later, [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] spy [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] carried eight ampoules of compressed eleton gas in his belt to quickly refill his [[blaster]].<ref name="ROVG" />
