'''Feral Interactive''' is a video game publisher for [[Wikipedia:MacOS|macOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Linux|Linux]] platforms. It is based in [[Wikipedia:London|London]], [[Wikipedia:United Kingdom|United Kingdom]].

Feral Interactive's first Star Wars game was a macOS port of ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Complete Saga|LEGO Star Wars II: The Original Trilogy]], ''released in [[2007]].
