'''Anthan 1''' was a [[moon]] that [[orbit]]ed the [[planet]] [[Anthan Prime]] in the [[Anthan system]]<ref name="DV11" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Albarrio sector]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> It was so close to the [[gas giant]] that it laid within the [[Thanteen Substrata]], an upper layer of the planet's [[gas]]eous [[atmosphere]], and was the site of the [[Plasma Devils]]' base.<ref name="DV12" />
