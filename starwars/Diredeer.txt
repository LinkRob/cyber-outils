'''Diredeer''' were a type of [[deer]] [[Diet|edible]] by [[Quarren]].<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The mention of diredeer in ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' takes place in between the [[Battle of Jedha]] and the [[Night of Sorrow]], both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> when Quarren [[Galactic Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Orlen Mollo]] was on the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]] to discuss their [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|conflict]] with their neighboring world of [[Eiram]], he felt that he was being too coddled&mdash;he was served candied [[cactus flower]]&mdash;and would have preferred a burnt, crispy joint of diredeer, [[oil]]y enough to stain [[clothing]].<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Behind the scenes==
