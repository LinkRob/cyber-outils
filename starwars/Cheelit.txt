'''Cheelit''' was an astronomical object<ref name="RSG" /> in the [[Cheelit system]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Semagi sector]] in grid square I-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of [[brain crawler]]s, a type of [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[worm]] that could infest [[happabore]]s, domesticated creatures found on [[Planet|worlds]] throughout the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="RSG" /> Cheelit shared its name with the [[Thulgars of Cheelit]], a group that existed<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> by [[19 BBY]]<ref name="MOFDate">{{MOFdate}}</ref> and [[Death|killed]] those who betrayed them.<ref name="MOF" />

The instructions for the slurry formulation of [[Galacti-Stok]]'s [[Happy-Bore Medicated Dewormer]] mentioned Cheelit alongside the worms while advising against the use of the [[dewormer]] on the parasites.<ref name="RSG" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="RSGDate">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' includes an addendum at the end describing some of the events of {{Film|VII}}, written several [[Standard day|days]] after they take place. The addendum is the final entry in the guide and is included chronologically after the entry mentioning Lanteeb. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the film to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] had acquired a copy of the instructions and included it in a [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]] that<ref name="RSG" /> she<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> compiled.<ref name="RSG" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Cheelit was first mentioned in ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'', a [[2015]] replica journal written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="RSG" /> The [[Cheelit/Legends|world]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in "[[Dark Lord's Conscience]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Alan Moore]], illustrated by [[John Stokes]], and first published in [[The Empire Strikes Back Monthly 155|''The Empire Strikes Back Monthly'' 155]] in [[April]] [[1982]].<ref name="DLC">{{StoryCite|story=Dark Lord's Conscience|book=The Empire Strikes Back Monthly 155}}</ref>
