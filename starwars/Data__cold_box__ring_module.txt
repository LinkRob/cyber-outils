The '''data "cold box" ring''' was a custom storage module. The [[Ozrelanso]] [[Gender|male]], [[Plesko Marno]] use the data ring to keep navigational data he stole from the [[New Republic]]'s [[Bureau of Ships and Services]]. The data was stored on the back of Marno's [[blast vest]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The data "cold box" ring was first mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
