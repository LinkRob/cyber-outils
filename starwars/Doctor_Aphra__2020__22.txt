'''''Doctor Aphra'' 22''' is the twenty-second issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]], [[Natacha Bustos]], and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[July 27]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Tainted by the SPARK ETERNAL, DOCTOR CHELLI APHRA is not herself! Trapped in her own mind, Aphra's only hope for escape lies in her own memories&hellip; &hellip;but will she find a way out before the Spark consumes her completely?''
