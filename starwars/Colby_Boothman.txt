'''Colby Boothman''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] actor and swordsman, best known for playing Leon in [[Colin Trevorrow]]'s [[Wikipedia:Jurassic World|''Jurassic World'']] and performing [[Wikipedia:Motion capture|motion capture]] work for ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''.<ref name="ProvJ">{{WebCite|url=http://www.providencejournal.com/article/20150612/ENTERTAINMENTLIFE/150619745|text=R.I. native Colby Boothman-Shepard plays raptor handler in 'Jurassic World'|author=Pelletier, Jenna|date=2015-06-12|work=[[Wikipedia:The Providence Journal|The Providence Journal]]|archivedate=20160304102503}}</ref>

He began his swordsmanship as a fencer in the [[Wikipedia:Fédération Internationale d'Escrime|Fédération Internationale d'Escrime]] and later learned [[Lightsaber combat/Legends|lightsaber combat]] from ''Star Wars'' stunt coordinator [[Nick Gillard]].<ref name="GameOverMan">{{WebCite|url=http://playtopia.com.au/gameoverman-australia-colby-boothman-interview|text=Colby Boothman about his work on Star Wars Battlefront 3, Jurassic World and Lego!|date=2015-10-23|archivedate=20151118164817}}</ref>

Boothman was the guest star of the [[Wikipedia:MythBusters (2015 season)|''Mythbusters Star Wars: The Myths Strike Back'']] special on the Discovery Channel, teaching [[Adam Savage]] and [[Jamie Hyneman]] lightsaber combat, and deflecting high-speed projectiles with his sword.<ref name="Nerdist">{{WebCite|url=http://nerdist.com/what-we-learned-from-the-mythbusters-star-wars-special|text=WHAT WE LEARNED FROM THE MYTHBUSTERS STAR WARS SPECIAL|author=Marnell, Blair|date=2015-09-08|archivedate=20180616021400}}</ref>
