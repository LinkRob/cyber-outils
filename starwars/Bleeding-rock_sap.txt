'''Bleeding-rock sap''' was the sweet and sticky [[Color|crimson]] [[sap]] of the [[bleeding rock]] organism [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Li-Toran]]. It was the primary element of the liquid [[diet]] of the [[insectoid]] [[Melitto]] [[species]] that also lived there,<ref name="Cyphers" /> who harvested the sap on the world's surface.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> Melittos who left Li-Toran used [[special survival device]]s that made sweet-smelling liquids that simulated the sap by distilling local [[foodstuff]].<ref name="Cyphers" />

During the culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited Li-Toran to collect some of the sap, requiring him to wear [[Environment suit|environmental gear]] due to the world's noxious [[atmosphere]]. Tuggs then used the sap to sweeten individual [[cornbread]]s to create [[Melitto Sap Cake]]s, a vegetarian [[cake]] that he included the recipe for in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> In the book, he professed to not understanding how Melittos consumed the sap as they had no visible [[mouth]]s.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bleeding-rock sap was first mentioned in ''[[Cyphers and Masks]]'', a [[2018]] supplement for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Cyphers" /> The [[2023]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]], uses [[Wikipedia:Honey|honey]] to represent the sap in an [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
