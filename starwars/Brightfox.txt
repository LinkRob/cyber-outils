The '''''Brightfox''''' was a [[starship]] that participated in the [[Karee Blockade Clutch]]<ref name="Last Shot" /> in [[7 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the present day events of ''[[Last Shot]]'' to [[7 ABY]]. As ''Last Shot'' establishes that the [[Karee Blockade Clutch]] took place in the same [[Standard year|year]] as the novel's present day events, it must have also taken place in 7 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> where it was beat by the ''[[Vorantis]]'', a ship owned by the [[pilot]] [[Brantis Mo Fresk]]. Later that [[Standard year|year]], Mo Fresk offered his services as a pilot for hire on [[Frander's Bay]]&mdash;a [[space station]] that [[orbit]]ed the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. When the [[New Republic]] operative [[Han Solo]] visited Frander's Bay to hire an unaffiliated pilot with his friend [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], [[Brantis Mo Fresk's barker|Mo Fresk's barker]] mentioned the pilot's beating of the ''Brightfox'' in the Karee Blockade Clutch into one of Solo's [[ear]]s.<ref name="Last Shot" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Brightfox'' was mentioned in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Last Shot]]'', written by [[Daniel José Older]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
