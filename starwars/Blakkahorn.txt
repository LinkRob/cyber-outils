A '''Blakkahorn'''<ref name="Script">[[The High Republic 1 Behind-the-Scenes Exclusive|''The High Republic'' 1 Behind-the-Scenes Exclusive]]</ref> was a type of creature known for screaming.<ref name="THR1">[[The High Republic (2021) 1|''The High Republic'' (2021) 1]]</ref> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic (2021) 1|''The High Republic'' (2021) 1]] to [[232 BBY]].</ref> while on the [[planet]] [[Shuraden]] for her [[Jedi Trials]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Keeve Trennis]] mentally noted that her awareness of [[the Force]] kept her from screaming at the top of her [[lung]]s like a Blakkahorn when she was being pestered by a [[Ximpi]] named [[Kanrii]], who kept asking her questions she found annoying.<ref name="THR1" />

==Behind the scenes==
A Blakkahorn was mentioned in the [[The High Republic (2021) 1|first issue]] of the [[2021]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']]. The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="THR1" /> on [[January 6]], 2021, as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume I]]''</ref> The comic page containing the mention of the creature was included in a preview published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[September 10]], [[2020]].<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-the-high-republic-1-exclusive-preview|text=A Padawan Faces the Jedi Trials in Marvel's ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' #1 – Exclusive Preview}}</ref> The capitalization of Blakkahorn was confirmed in [[The High Republic 1 Behind-the-Scenes Exclusive|''The High Republic'' 1 Behind-the-Scenes Exclusive]], a comic-book<ref name="Script" /> released on [[April 7]], [[2021]]<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/95456/star_wars_the_high_republic_behind-the-scenes_exclusive_2021_1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Behind-The-Scenes Exclusive (2021) #1}}</ref> that presents the unfinalized script and artwork from ''The High Republic'' 1.<ref name="Script" />
