'''Ata Walpa''' was a fictitious [[planet]] envisioned by the [[human]] [[Izal Garsea]] when she was a [[Youngling|child]] living on [[Batuu]]. As a child, Garsea wanted to be a [[senator]] of Ata Walpa and give it's citizens unlimited [[puffed candies]] and no forced [[Sleep|bedtimes]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> when they were now both nineteen [[Standard year|years]] old, Garsea's former childhood friend [[Julen Rakab]] reminded her about Ata Walpa during a conversation, which made Garsea laugh.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />
