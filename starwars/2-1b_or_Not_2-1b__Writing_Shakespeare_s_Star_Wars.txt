"'''2-1b or Not 2-1b: Writing Shakespeare's ''Star Wars'''''" is an article written by [[Megan Crouse]], published in [[Star Wars Insider 186|''Star Wars Insider'' 186]] on [[December 18]], [[2018]]. It is an interview with [[Ian Doescher]], the author of the ''[[William Shakespeare's Star Wars]]'' series.
