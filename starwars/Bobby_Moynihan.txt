'''Bobby Moynihan''' (born [[January 31]], [[1977]])<ref name="This Week! Ep1" /> is a voice actor best known for being a cast member on ''[[Saturday Night Live]]''.

==Biography==
In [[April]] [[2018]], he was announced as part of the cast of ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' as [[Orka]].<ref name="Resistance announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-resistance-set-for-fall-debut|text=''Star Wars Resistance'', Anime-Inspired Series, Set for Fall Debut}}</ref> He voiced [[Pintu Son-El]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|seventh season]] episode "[[Gone with a Trace]]."<ref name="GWAT">{{TCW|Gone with a Trace}}</ref>

===''Saturday Night Live''===
While on ''Saturday Night Live'', Monyhan was a significant part of three ''Star Wars''-related sketches in the lead-up to ''[[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens]]''. In the [[November 21]], [[2015]] episode, during a fictitious audition reel in which [[J.J. Abrams]], [[Daisy Ridley]] and [[John Boyega]] appeared as themselves, he portrayed [[George Lucas]], as well as [[Wikipedia:Danny Devito|Danny DeVito]] auditioning for the role of [[BB-8]].<ref>{{OfficialYouTube|channel=UCqFzWxSCi39LnW1JKFR3efg|name=Saturday Night Live|video=-T_pjMr7-n0|text=Star Wars Auditions - SNL}}</ref> In the [[December 12]], 2015 episode, he appeared in a fictitious ''Force Awakens'' toy commercial, playing a fan/collector who is horrified to see children using the potentially priceless collectibles for their intended purpose.<ref>{{OfficialYouTube|channel=channel/UCqFzWxSCi39LnW1JKFR3efg|name=Saturday Night Live|video=EYyuo7gm-aQ|text=Star Wars Toy Commercial &ndash; SNL}}</ref> In the [[January 15]], [[2016]] episode, hosted by [[Adam Driver]], he appeared as a [[First Order stormtrooper|First Order Stormtrooper]] in the [[Wikipedia:Undercover Boss|''Undercover Boss'']] sketch in which Driver reprised his role as [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and went undercover as the radar technician "Matt" to learn more about the employees of [[Starkiller Base]].<ref name="Undercover Starkiller">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=channel/UCqFzWxSCi39LnW1JKFR3efg|name=Saturday Night Live|video=FaOSCASqLsE|text=Star Wars Undercover Boss: Starkiller Base - SNL}}</ref>
