The '''Divine thunder-class transport''' was a [[Transport]] [[Starship]] from the [[Aakaash system]]. It was a notably fast ship which could even outmaneuver the governmental forces of the [[Land & Sky Corporation]]. [[Zhang Jincheng]] kept a ship of this
class as an escape vessel in his [[Hangar]] on [[Fire (city)|Fire]].<ref name="Vow" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the original Chinese, this class was called 神雷 (Pinyin: Shénléi).
