'''Dymek''' was a company which produced weaponry for ground [[assault vehicle]]s during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. They notably produced the [[MK 2e/w medium laser cannon]], used in the Empire's [[TX-225 Occupier]] series,<ref name="ROUVG" /> and the [[HM-6 Concussion Missile Launcher]], which was utilized by the [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]].<ref name="FlightLog" />
