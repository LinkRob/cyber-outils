'''Al'doleem''',<ref name="Darth Vader Storybook">{{StoryCite|story=Darth Vader and the Weapon of a Sith|book=Galactic Adventures Storybook Collection|audiobook=2}}</ref> also known as the '''river moon of Al'doleem''', was a [[moon]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] that the [[droid]] [[Arex]] spent many [[Standard year|years]] on with many [[Jedi]]. During the early stages of the [[Great Jedi Purge]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Kirak Infil'a]] lived in seclusion on the moon.<ref name="Dark Lord 3" />
