A '''Commissar''' was an authoritative position on<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Crisis">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/crisis-on-naboo-trivia-gallery|text="Crisis on Naboo" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> [[Grizmallt]]. During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Commissars of Grizmallt and the [[Arch-Provost]] of the planet [[Rendili]] began examine the [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class transgalactic military assault ship]] produced by [[Rothana Heavy Engineering]], a subsidiary of [[Kuat Drive Yards]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> Anxious in shadowing their rivals,<ref name="BMF 49">{{BuildFalconCite|49|Starship Fact File}}</ref> they ordered several industrial [[Spy|spies]] and [[starship]] designers to reduce their rival's lead for new Republic contracts, and with Rendili's extensive [[shipyard]]s in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] and [[Outer Rim Territories]], could gain temporary ascendancy.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
Commissars were first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' canon in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 49|forty-ninth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 49" /> published around [[December 9]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdates">{{BMFdates|49}}</ref>
