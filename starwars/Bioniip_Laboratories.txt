==Behind the scenes==
Bioniip Laboratories was created for [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, making its debut in the 1989 adventure book, ''[[Crisis on Cloud City (adventure book)|Crisis on Cloud City]]'', written by [[Christopher Kubasik]] for the first edition of ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Crisis">[[Crisis on Cloud City (adventure book)|''Crisis on Cloud City'']]</ref> Bioniip Laboratories was integrated into the [[Canon]] when it was featured as a map included in the downloadable expansion pack, Bespin, for the 2015 video game ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''.<ref name="Welcome">{{EA|subdomain=starwars|url=starwars/battlefront/news/bespin-welcome-to-cloud-city|text=Bespin &ndash; Welcome To Cloud City|date=2016-06-21|nolive=1}} {{C|"We've also added a map called the Bioniip Laboratories which, our lore-savy fans out there will know, is where cybernetics and other bio-implants were manufactured."}}</ref><ref name="Battlefront" />
