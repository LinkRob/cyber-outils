A '''battle''' was fought at the [[planet]] [[Dol'har Hyde]] between the [[Galactic Republic]], [[Jedi Order]], and a [[Krix Kamerat's cell|cell]] of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization led by [[Krix Kamerat]], who the [[Jedi]] hoped to capture. After they were informed of Kamerat's location by [[Padawan]] [[Bibs]], who had been sent to investigate the source of a [[Distress signal|distress call]], the Republic and [[Jedi]] sent a [[Unidentified Jedi task force|task force]] jointly led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Torban Buck]] and Padawan [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] to assault the [[Nihil Encampment]] and capture Kamerat. After a battle in the planet's [[atmosphere]], Padawans Talisola, [[Qort]], and [[Farzala Tarabal]], as well as Kamerat's childhood friend [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]], made their way to Kamerat's saferoom where they were able to capture him.<ref name="HRA 12" />

[[Standard century|Centuries]] later,<ref name="Aphra 8">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 8|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 8]]</ref> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Aphra 8 date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 8|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 8]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> wreckage from the battle was still present on the planet when rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] and [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] traveled to Dol'har Hyde as they sought information about a [[Path engine]] the smuggler [[Remy (smuggler)|Remy]] had sold to [[Beol De'Rruyet]].<ref name="Aphra 8" />
