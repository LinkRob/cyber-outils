"'''From Puppets to Pao: The Inside Story of a Rebel Commando'''" is an interview by [[Amy Ratcliffe]] with creature performer and puppeteer [[Derek Arnold]] mostly focused on the character [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek]] from ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.
