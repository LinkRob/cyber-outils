The '''Bryx sector''' was a [[sector]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="RO World Locations" /> that contained the [[Wobani system]]<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'': Rogue One: X-wing VR Mission''</ref> along with the [[planet]]s [[Pzandias]]<ref name="RotS" /> and [[Kijimi]].<ref name="TROSVD" /> The sector seceded from the [[Galactic Republic]] and joined the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="RotS" />
