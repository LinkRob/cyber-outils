The '''battle over Primea'''<ref name="Greater Good" /> was the ultimate event in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s intervention in the [[Nikardun conquests|conquests]] of the [[Nikardun Destiny]]. Taking place above the [[Vak]] [[homeworld]] of [[Primea]], the battle was orchestrated by [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] of the Ascendancy. During the battle, Thrawn outsmarted [[General]] [[Yiv]] and the [[Nikardun]] forces, manipulating them into turning the [[Vak Combine]], who Yiv was attempting to conquer, against them. During the battle, Thrawn captured Yiv, who became a [[prisoner]] of the Ascendancy.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

The battle followed a string of encounters and skirmishes between the [[Chiss]] and Nikardun forces, or factions subjugated by the Nikardun. Thrawn sent the [[caregiver]] [[Mitth'ali'astov]] and the [[sky-walker]] [[Che'ri]] to return a [[Thrawn's patrol boat|Vak fighter]] Thrawn had previously commandeered in an [[Attack at Primea|escape]] from Primea, along with a message to the Vak leadership. Vak forces aligned with the Nikardun organized for the capture of the two Chiss and instead delivered a false message, both of which were expected by Thrawn. Yiv sent a threat to the Ascendancy, demanding that Thrawn deliver him a [[ransom]] for the issues he had caused. The Ascendancy's [[Chiss Syndicure]] decided that the capture of a sky-walker required a military response. Thrawn traveled to Yiv's location in an [[Thrawn's freighter|unarmed freighter]] with the [[Pathfinders (Navigators' Guild)|Pathfinder]] [[navigator]] [[Qilori]], who, having been allied with Yiv, changed the arrival coordinates from above Primea to further out in the [[Primea system|system]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

As Yiv confronted Thrawn with four [[Nikardun Battle Dreadnought]]s, including the [[flagship]] ''[[Deathless]]'', five Chiss [[warship]]s, led by [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] arrived at Primea, informing the Vaks of the locations of [[orbit]]ing Nikardun vessels, as Thrawn had offered to do in his message. The Vaks allowed Ar'alani to fire on the Nikardun, and Yiv sent his three [[dreadnought]] escorts to assist his [[blockade]] of Primea. With the ''[[Springhawk]]'' taking damage, the vessel went dark, and Admiral Ar'alani requested humanitarian aid from nearby [[Patrol ship|Vak patrol boats]]. The Vak complied, but the Nikardun vessels shot down the [[patrol ship]]s, turning the Vak Combine against the Nikardun. Yiv attempted to pull Thrawn's [[freighter]] in with a [[tractor beam]], but Thalias and Che'ri released [[tava]] mist into the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the ''Deathless'', allowing Thrawn to break free of the tractor beam and instead crash his freighter into the [[viewport]], with his ship rendered unharmed due to a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Deflector shield|energy shield]] [[Mission to Mokivj|acquired]] from [[Mokivj]]. Thrawn boarded the bridge, rescuing the two Chiss prisoners and capturing Yiv. Following Yiv's disappearance, the Nikardun Destiny was left without a suitably competent successor, and their plans of conquest fell apart.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Aftermath==
After Yiv's defeat and capture at Primea, the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] undertook the [[Nikardun campaigns]] to neutralize the threat posed by remnants of the [[Yiv's battle forces|Nikardun military]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle over Primea served as the climax of the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
