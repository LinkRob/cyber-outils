A '''resistance''' movement was formed on the [[planet]] [[Elom (planet)|Elom]] during the [[Occupation of Elom|occupation]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Resistance began when the Empire subjugated the [[Elomin]] into harsh [[mining]] conditions, although some Elomin managed to escape deeper underground into the hidden [[City|cities]] of the [[Elom]]. The Elom and Elomin coordinated with [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] agents and both [[species]] cooperated to undermine Imperial efforts.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />
