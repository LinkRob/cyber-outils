The '''aerial landing platform''', also referred to as a '''sky-base'''<ref name="Dark Lord 14" /> or '''skybase''', was a type of [[starship]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]] that served as a [[staging area]] for invading [[planet]]ary forces. In [[18 BBY]], they were deployed to [[Mon Cala]] to quell an uprising, following the [[Battle of Dac City|destruction]] of all surface [[City|cities]] on the [[planet]].<ref name="Dark Lord 15" />

These platforms were defended by [[deflector shield]]s and point-defense grid capable of easily shooting down [[missile]]s.  However, during the invasion, [[Commander]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] attacked one of these platforms using a force of [[Unidentified Mon Calamari aquatic vehicle|aquatic vehicles]] by approaching it from underwater.  Once inside the range of the point-defense grid, the vehicles emerged directly underneath to fire special metallic-encased missiles that penetrated the shields and destroyed the platform.<ref name="Dark Lord 15" />

The [[Politics#Empire|Empire's]] successor, the [[First Order]],<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> also had dedicated [[First Order landing platform|landing platforms]]<ref name="PD1">[[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]]</ref> similar in design to the sky-base.<ref name="Dark Lord 15" />

==Behind the scenes==
The aerial landing platform was first mentioned in [[Darth Vader (2017) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 14]]<ref name="Dark Lord 14" /> and later appeared in [[Darth Vader (2017) 15|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 15]]. Both comics were released in [[2018]] and were authored by [[Charles Soule]] and penciled by [[Giuseppe Camuncoli]].<ref name="Dark Lord 15" />
