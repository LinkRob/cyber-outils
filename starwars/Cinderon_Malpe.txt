'''Cinderon Malpe''' was an individual who [[Senator|represented]] [[Derella]] in the [[Imperial Senate]]. Malpe had close ties to [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] and was invited to attend the [[Leia Organa's Day of Demand|Day of Demand]] held for [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] on [[Alderaan]]<ref name="LPOA" /> in [[3 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] birth at [[19 BBY]]. The events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' commence just after Organa's sixteenth birthday, which places the events of the novel in [[3 BBY]].</ref> He attended banquets hosted at the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan]] with other senators involved with resisting the Empire. Malpe expressed discontent, at one of theses banquets, with [[Saw Gerrera]]'s violence after learning he was involved with the [[Strike on Onoam|assassination of Quarsh Panaka]]. At another banquet, [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] unexpectedly attended to try to find evidence that the group was plotting against the Empire. Malpe was questioned by Tarkin about his concerns with his planet and the senate which Maple worriedly responded to but was interrupted when [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]] staged a scene in which she pretended to drunkenly accuse her husband of having an affair with his colleague [[Mon Mothma]] though Malpe did not realize that it was a scene until after Tarkin had left.<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cinderon Malpe first appeared in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]] that was released as part of [[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi|Journey to ''Star Wars'': ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="LPOA" /> In the preview chapter published in the paperback edition of ''[[Lost Stars]]'', Malpe's role was originally taken by [[Senator]] [[Gall Trayvis]], whose homeworld was revealed as [[Osk-Trill]], but Trayvis was replaced by Malpe in the full novel.<ref name="LS paperback">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' excerpt in the ''[[Lost Stars]]'' paperback</ref>
