'''Collum Woslo''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial swamp trooper|mudtrooper]] part of the [[224th Imperial Armored Division|224th]] who held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Lieutenant]] in the army and a 224thSGT. He was a known brawler in his class at [[Myomar Academy]], which earned him less desirable military deployments.<ref name="Solo Guide" />
