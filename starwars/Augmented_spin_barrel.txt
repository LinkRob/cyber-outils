Those who lived and [[Death|died]] to the performance of their weapon endlessly tinkered with it to gain an extra edge in battle. The increase of a weapon's lethality was a common modification made by such individuals, and was often accomplished through the installation of an '''augmented spin barrel'''.<ref name="Gadgets" />

Using an updated and streamlined [[galven-pattern]] heavy-duty static pulse adapter, augmented spin [[barrel]]s increased both the damage output and [[armor]] penetration of [[blaster rifle]]s and [[heavy blaster rifle]]s. The trade-off for extra stopping power consequently decreased the effectiveness of the [[blaster]]'s frame and barrel, which led to an increase in maintenance and downtime. The cost of an augmented spin barrel was 1,750 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets" />
