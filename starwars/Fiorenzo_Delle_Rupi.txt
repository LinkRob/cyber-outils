'''Fiorenzo Delle Rupi''' was the president of the Cloud City Fan Club Italiano di Guerre Stellari, the largest [[Wikipedia:Italia|Italian]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan club of its era. In [[1998]], Delle Rupi wrote [[Star Wars: The Power of the Force (comic)|''Star Wars: The Power of the Force'']] (Italian: ''Star Wars: Il Potere della Forza''), a short comic ordered by [[Hasbro]] Italy to promote the upcoming release of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. Illustrations were provided by future Cloud City Fan Club vice president [[Filippo Rossi]].
