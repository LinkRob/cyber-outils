In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the '''Beltire Liberation'''<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" /> was carried out by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] when it liberated<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] location<ref name="Galaxy Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Beltire system]]</ref> [[Beltire]].<ref name="Timelines" /> During the event, [[rebel pilot]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Shara Bey]] flew assist for [[Rampant Squadron]].<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" />

After the liberation, Bey's fellow [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]] [[pilot]] [[L'ulo L'ampar]] heard about the lieutenant's participation and mentioned it to<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" /> her<ref name="Timelines" /> after the two had taken part in the [[Battle of Cawa City|liberation]] of [[Cawa City]] on<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref> [[Sterdic IV]]. L'ampar used it as an example of how Bey had been working very hard and stated<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" /> he<ref name="Timelines" /> would try to get her assigned to lighter duties.<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Beltire Liberation was first mentioned in the [[Shattered Empire 2|second issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Shattered Empire]]''. The issue was written by [[Greg Rucka]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" /> on [[October 7]], 2015.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52680/journey_to_star_wars_the_force_awakens_-_shattered_empire_2015_2|text=Journey To Star Wars: The Force Awakens - Shattered Empire (2015) #2}}</ref>
