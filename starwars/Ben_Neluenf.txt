'''Ben Neluenf''' was the first great [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Reproduction|born]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]]. At some point prior to the [[Invasion of Naboo]]<ref name="CL" /> in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> Neluenf lost his life in a spectacular attempt to scale a broad, flat-topped [[mountain]] located between the [[Desert Plain]] and the [[Hutt Flats]] on Tatooine. As a tribute, the site of his [[death]] was christened [[Ben's Mesa]].<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Ben Neluenf was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="CL" /> from the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]] and marked the first mention of the [[Ben Neluenf/Legends|character]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
