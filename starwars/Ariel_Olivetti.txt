'''Ariel Olivetti''' (born [[November 15]], [[1960s|1967]]) is an [[Wikipedia:Argentine people|Argentine]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] artist who has drawn the covers for [[Dark Horse Comics]]' ''[[Star Wars: Darth Vader and the Ninth Assassin]]'' series, as well as the variant cover to the [[Chewbacca (2015) 1|first issue]] of [[Marvel Comics]]' [[Star Wars: Chewbacca (2015)|''Chewbacca'']] miniseries.<ref name="Marvel.com">{{Marvel|subdomain=news|url=comics/24866/sdcc_2015_chewbacca|text=SDCC 2015: Chewbacca}}</ref> His work also includes [[W:c:marvel:Daredevil Vol 1|''Daredevil'']], [[W:c:marvel:Punisher War Journal Vol 2|''Punisher War Journal'']], and [[W:c:marvel:Cable Vol 2|''Cable'']].
