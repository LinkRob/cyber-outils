"'''A droid in the hand is worth two in the asteroid belt'''" is the eleventh in a series of comics on the LEGO Star Wars website. It was first placed on the website [[March 25]], [[2009]].

==Plot summary==
The comic starts with a newsflash stating that [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] has once again gone missing. It then shows that [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano/Legends|Ahsoka Tano]] are not the only ones searching for the missing droid. [[Count/Legends|Count]] [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]] has sent a team of [[IG-86 sentinel droid/Legends|assassin droids]] and, accidentally, a squad of [[B1-series battle droid/Legends|B1-Series battle droids]] to find Artoo. Also, on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], a squad of [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]] is given orders to find the [[Astromech droid/Legends|astromech droid]]. Focusing on the clones, they are shown getting the orders to find R2-D2 before the Separatists or the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]]. The clones board a [[Nu-class attack shuttle/Legends|''Nu''-class attack shuttle]] and depart for where Artoo's last known location. There they find [[Rocket battle droid/Legends|rocket battle droids]] who inform the clones that R2-D2 had already been taken and pointed in the direction his captors took him. As soon as the clones leave however, the droids question themselves if that was that was the right direction. Arriving at a [[Planet/Legends|planet]], the clone troopers fly down to investigate. While they land, their scanners detect many droids which turn out to be Separatists battle droids.
