The '''''Bothan-5'''''<ref name="The Mandalorian Handbook" /> was a [[New Republic Correctional Transport]]<ref name="Chapter 10" /> that carried [[prisoner]]s of the [[New Republic]]. The [[Ranzar Malk's crew|mercenary crew]] of [[Ranzar Malk]] were sent to [[Bothan-Five incident|rescue]] the prisoner [[Qin]] from the [[prison ship]], utilizing the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', the [[starship]] of the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]]. Against Djarin's wishes, the crew [[Death|killed]] the sole living crewmember aboard,<ref name="Chapter 6" /> [[Lieutenant]]<ref name="Chapter 10">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 10: The Passenger}}</ref> [[Lant Davan]], who activated a [[distress signal]] before his death, leading New Republic forces to the prison ship's location. After rescuing Qin, the crew betrayed Djarin and left him trapped aboard the ship, but Djarin escaped and incapacitated the [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], imprisoning them within a cell aboard the ''Bothan-5''.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

Eventually, the ship was found derelict near the [[Dilestri system]],<ref name="Chapter 14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref> with security records showing that Djarin had risked his life to save that of Davan, and the bounty hunter left three wanted fugitives aboard,<ref name="Chapter 10" /> including [[Migs Mayfeld]], who was apprehended for his part in the rescue.<ref name="Chapter 14" /> Those records, once accessed by a pair of [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] on patrol, convinced the pilots not to arrest Djarin when they encountered him.<ref name="Chapter 10" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ship first appeared in the [[Chapter 6: The Prisoner|sixth episode]] of [[Jon Favreau]]'s [[2019]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.<ref name="Chapter 6" /> Recognizing the [[Wikipedia:Spaghetti Western|Spaghetti Western]] film influence that pervades the series, concept supervisor [[Christian Alzmann]] sought to evoke the feeling of a stagecoach or steam train robbery in his design of the prison ship. Alzmann repeated boxy shapes, like a train, on the vessel's exterior, even mimicking a cow-catcher on its front. The round prison cell doors on the interior suggested an Old West bank vault door.<ref name="Chapter 6 guide">{{SW|url=series/the-mandalorian/chapter-6-guide|text="The Prisoner" Episode Guide &#124; The Mandalorian}}</ref>
