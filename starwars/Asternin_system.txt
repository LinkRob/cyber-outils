The '''Asternin system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Tamarin sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square N-19 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the planet [[Asternin]].<ref name="Origin" />
