'''Darth Bane''' was a legendary [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] and the sole survivor of the [[Defeat of the Sith|destruction]] of the [[Brotherhood of Darkness]] at the hands of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Jedi-Sith War]] a thousand [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Clone Wars]]. As the only surviving [[Sith]], Bane recognized that Sith infighting had weakened them to the point that the [[Jedi]] could destroy them. To rectify this, Bane reformed the Sith and created the [[Rule of Two]], mandating that there could be only two Sith&mdash;a [[Sith Master|Master]] and an [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]]&mdash;at any given [[time]]. These new Sith would begin a [[Grand Plan|plot]] to destroy the Jedi and conquer [[the galaxy]] in secret. With Bane's [[Death|defeat]] at the hands of his apprentice, [[Darth Zannah]], allegedly on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Ambria]], the Jedi wrongly believed that the Sith were defeated with him, as the [[galaxy]] entered a [[millennium]]-long [[Republic Era|era of peace]].

The vendetta Bane held against the Jedi was fulfilled during the Clone Wars when [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], secretly the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious and a follower of the Rule of Two, [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] the [[Galactic Republic]] into the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[19 BBY]] and [[Order 66|destroyed]] the Jedi Order. However, the victory was short-lived, as the redeemed [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], formerly the Sith Lord Darth Vader, [[Battle of Endor|defeated]] the Sith [[4 ABY|twenty-three years later]], and the order was finally [[Battle of Exegol|destroyed]] upon Sidious's final death in [[35 ABY]].

==Biography==
===The last survivor===
{{Quote|Weakened by their own hand, the Sith were nearly wiped from existence by the opportunistic Jedi. But there was a Sith Lord who survived the culling&mdash;the great Darth Bane.|Darth Sidious, ''The Secrets of the Sith''|<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" />}}
[[File:DarthBaneAncientMaster-CM.png|thumb|right|270px|Darth Bane was an ancient Sith Lord from the time of the Old Republic.]]
Hailing from [[Moraband]],<ref name="JTB CW" /> the ancient [[homeworld]] of the [[Sith]],<ref name="Moraband-DB">{{Databank|moraband|Moraband}}</ref> Darth Bane was a legendary [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]<ref name="Dark Side">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side]]''</ref> who lived during the [[Fall of the Old Republic|dark age]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]],<ref name="SWP note">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'' places the [[Jedi-Sith War]], from which [[Darth]] Bane emerged as the sole survivor according to ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'', within the [[Fall of the Old Republic|dark age]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]].</ref> a thousand [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Clone Wars]]. The Sith Order in his [[time]] was nearly equal in number with the [[Jedi Order]]; at the height of their power, the Sith's dominion had spread across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> However, the Sith were often fueled by their own individual lusts for power, with squabbling Sith turning against their own<ref name="Dark Side" /> in combat as standard practice.<ref name="Maul Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Darth Maul and Other Followers of the Dark Side}}</ref>

While serving in the [[Sith Empire]], known as the [[Brotherhood of Darkness]],<ref name="Brotherhood" /> Bane realized that infighting and greed from other [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] warriors created a weakness. The infighting eventually led to the [[Defeat of the Sith|defeat]] of their order<ref name="Dark Side" /> at the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Ruusan]]<ref name="Helmet 29">{{HelmetCollectionCite|29|Databank A-Z}}</ref> in [[1032 BBY]],<ref name="SWT">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> as the [[Jedi]] were able to all but destroy the weakened Sith.<ref name="Bane-DB" /> Bane was the sole survivor of the [[Jedi-Sith War]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> as he organized the [[Death|eradication]] of all the other Sith.<ref name="Dark Side" />

===The Rule of Two===
{{Quote|There should only be two, no more, no less. One to embody power, the other one to crave it.|Darth Bane|<ref name="BustCollection">{{BustCollectionCite|4|Star Wars Universe}}</ref>}}
[[File:SithOrder-SOTJ.jpg|thumb|left|290px|Bane's Rule of Two had ensured the rebirth of the Sith Order.]]
Bane recognized that the old Sith traditions were a dead end<ref name="Bane-DB" /> and became determined to ensure the continuity of the Sith.<ref name="Maul Encyclopedia" /> To this end, he created a new Sith [[Philosopher|philosophy]]<ref name="Bane-DB" /> that succeeded the older [[Doctrine of the Dyad|doctrine]] of the [[Force dyad|dyad in the Force]], a rare and powerful [[Force-bond]] that existed between two individuals.<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Instead of building a new [[army]] of [[Sith Lord]]s, Bane's [[Rule of Two]] mandated that there could be only two Sith at a given time: a [[Sith Master]] to embody power and a [[Sith apprentice]] to crave it.<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> Only by slaying their master could the Sith apprentice gain that title and take on their own apprentice, or they could die in the attempt,<ref name="Helmet 14">{{HelmetCollectionCite|14|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> proving themselves unworthy of the Sith legacy,<ref name="Insider 186">{{InsiderCite|186|Secrets of the Sith: A Dark Side Chronicle}}</ref> and be replaced.<ref name="Helmet 14" /> Those Sith would favor the shady tactics of trickery and scheming to destroy their enemies rather than outright force.<ref name="Bane-DB" />

The goal of Bane's decree, and the new hierarchy that it created, was to ensure the survival of the Sith Order. The Rule of Two recognized the ambition for power inherent in Sith philosophy and utilized it as a mechanism of succession from one Sith Master to the next. The Rule of Two therefore encouraged Sith apprentices to take the power that they had been trained to covet by supplanting their masters,<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> who were obliged to anticipate signs of betrayal<ref name="Helmet 51">{{HelmetCollectionCite|51|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> while educating their apprentices through agonizing lessons.<ref name="Insider 186" />

===A cycle of death===
{{Quote|Lord Bane's own apprentice struck him down to claim Bane's place. And so began a magnificently vicious cycle. Many Sith apprentices&mdash;myself included&mdash;have supplanted their masters in order to ascend.|Darth Sidious, ''The Secrets of the Sith''|<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" />}}
[[File:Darth_Banes_tomb.png|thumb|right|270px|Darth Bane's sarcophagus on the Sith homeworld of Moraband]]
The Rule of Two's establishment coincided with the creation of a plot,<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> known as the [[Grand Plan]], for the Sith to corrupt the Galactic Republic from within, diminish and disgrace the Jedi Order, and eventually rise and conquer the [[galaxy]];<ref name="Helmet 14" /> the Sith would soften up their enemies before zeroing them in for total subjugation.<ref name="Topps GF">{{Topps|set=2018 Topps Star Wars Galactic Files|cardname=Darth Bane|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/170092/cid/11644161}}</ref> Believing that the new principle would prevent the power of the dark side from destroying the Sith Order,<ref name="Dark Side" /> Bane took on an apprentice of his own, [[Darth Zannah]],<ref name="Helmet 2" /> and began passing on his knowledge. The Jedi learned of Bane's new philosophy and falsely believed that they had destroyed the Sith forever with Bane's defeat,<ref name="Bane-SWE" /> which marked the dawn of a long-lasting [[Republic Era|era of peace]] under the rule of a reformed Galactic Republic.<ref name="TSWB" /> However, Bane's plans continued through his apprentice, who slew the Sith Master in order to claim his place.<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> Although historical records were not accurate about the identity of Bane's apprentice or how Bane died,<ref name="Dark Side" /> it was rumored that Bane was killed by Zannah in a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]]&mdash;a pattern that nearly all Sith repeated afterward&mdash;which took place on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] planet of [[Ambria]].<ref name="Helmet 2" />

After Bane's death, his remains were placed in a [[Tomb of Darth Bane|sarcophagus]]<ref name="Bane-SWE" /> adorned with [[Sith alphabet|runic inscriptions]] in the [[ur-Kittât]] [[language]]<ref name="GE1-Ency">{{EncyclopediaCite|General Equipment (1)}}</ref> in the [[Valley of the Dark Lords]] on Moraband,<ref name="Bane-SWE" /> which was said to be haunted by a [[Fire|fiery]] spectral image of the late Dark Lord.<ref name="TSWB" /> Additionally, a colossal statue of Bane in [[Ancient Sith Armor|his armor]] was erected at the far end of his massive [[Sith temple (Valley of the Dark Lords)|burial chamber]].<ref name="Sacrifice" /> Bane was the last Sith Lord to be buried on Moraband.<ref name="Bane-SWE" />

===Legacy===
====Shadowy menace====
{{Quote|An illusion. Real, you are not.|Yoda, to the specter of Darth Bane|audio=YodaVSBane2-TCW.flac|<ref name="Sacrifice" />}}
One of the most infamous of the Sith Lords,<ref name="Yoda-FC">{{ForceCollection|Master Yoda &lsqb;Defiance of Evil&rsqb;|star=5}}</ref> [[Darth]] Bane was considered the founder of the modern Sith, who continued their quest for revenge, working in secret as they plotted their return to galactic dominance. Those plans continued through [[Darth Sidious]], publicly known as [[Supreme Chancellor]] Sheev Palpatine,<ref name="Dark Side" /> who engineered the Clone Wars to gain more power over the Galactic Republic. He also manipulated the war&mdash;fought between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]&mdash;to make the Jedi compromise their ideals, as they served as [[Jedi General|leaders]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. Doing so would allow him to justify his eventual destruction of the Jedi Order.<ref name="Sith-DB" /> Sidious also had a personal [[protocol droid]] named [[11-4D]] that he inherited from his Sith Master, [[Darth Plagueis]], and the [[droid]]'s [[memory bank]] retained information on the former strongholds of the ancient Sith Lords, such as Darth Bane's tomb on Moraband.<ref name="TOM 98">{{GermanFanCite|98|Database}}</ref>

[[File:EpGuide613.png|thumb|left|285px|Grand Master Yoda encountered the specter of Darth Bane during the Clone Wars.]]
During the war, in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="SWT" /> Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] [[Mission to Moraband|ventured]] to Moraband on a journey to discover the secrets of [[immortality]]. He entered Bane's tomb and encountered a fiery vision of the ancient Dark Lord. As part of Yoda's trial to learn the [[Force spirit|power of immortality]], the apparition attempted to convince the [[Jedi Master]] to turn to the dark side. Yoda refused, remarking that Bane was dead and that what appeared before him was simply an illusion that had nothing to offer. Yoda's defiance banished the screaming specter back into the sarcophagus, while Yoda continued on his journey.<ref name="Sacrifice" /> The Jedi Master's travel to Moraband was eventually mentioned in a [[Galactic Atlas|set]] of [[Limb|hand]]-drawn [[map]]s that were believed to be created by the [[Ithorian]] [[artist]] [[Gammit Chond]], along with an illustration of Darth Bane's fiery specter. The maps were later restored and collected in the [[Graf Archive]] on the [[moon]] [[Orchis 2]] at some point no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> An updated version of the set of maps, including the illustration of Bane, was released no earlier than 35 ABY.<ref name="Galactic Atlas Updated Edition">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>

====Revenge of the Sith====
{{Quote|But this is what the Order was for me. A ''golden age''. About eight centuries after we ended the rule of Sith Lord Darth Bane.''"<br />"''Yeah, see&hellip; This is what I'm talking about. Darth ''Bane''? It's too much.|Jedi Master Elzar Mann's imprint and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="SW 20">[[Star Wars (2020) 20|''Star Wars'' (2020) 20]]</ref>}}
[[File:BaneSidiousVader.jpg|thumb|right|260px|Darth Sidious and Darth Vader were the last Sith Lords who followed Bane's Rule of Two.]]
The war's [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] in 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="SWT" /> a thousand years after Bane's death,<ref name="Bane-SWE" /> saw the culmination of the Sith's [[millennium]]-long vendetta against the Jedi. Sidious [[Proclamation of the New Order|declared]] himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] and reformed the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]]. To destroy the Jedi, he issued [[Order 66]], a [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|command]] [[Behavioral modification biochip|implanted]] in the [[clone trooper]]s of the Grand Army of the Republic to [[Death penalty|execute]] the Jedi as [[Treason|traitors]]. In addition, Sidious seduced to the dark side [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], believed by the Jedi to be the [[Chosen One]] of prophecy who would bring balance to [[the Force]] and destroy the Sith. Skywalker adopted the Sith mantle of Darth Vader,<ref name="TSWB" /> and the Grand Plan that Darth Bane had put in motion a thousand years ago had thus been fulfilled by Sidious,<ref name="Helmet 14" /> who was recognized thenceforth as the greatest Sith Lord after Bane.<ref name="Helmet 51" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="SW 20 date">{{OutlawsComicDate|Star Wars (2020) 20|''Star Wars'' (2020) 20}}</ref> [[Luke Skywalker]], a [[Padawan|Jedi trainee]] and Vader's distant son, traveled to the planet [[Gazian]] in hopes of [[Jedi training|learning]] more in the ways of the Jedi. When he reached a [[vergence]] in the Force known as the [[Living Sea]], Skywalker encountered a duplicate consciousness of a Jedi of the [[High Republic Era]]&mdash;an imprint of Jedi Master [[Elzar Mann]]'s mind. Witnessing the Republic and the Jedi Order at their zenith, Mann described the Jedi in his time as living their golden age, approximately eight [[Standard century|hundred years]] after they ended the rule of Darth Bane. Skywalker, who was already concerned about his lack of knowledge about the Force and the Jedi, interrupted Mann after the latter mentioned Bane, and complained about his struggle to move forward in his life without proper guidance.<ref name="SW 20" />

Despite achieving their goal of galactic conquest, the Sith victory proved short-lived as the [[Emperor]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]] ended within a generation after his rise to power.<ref name="Sith-DB">{{Databank|sith|Sith}}</ref> During the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], Vader sacrificed his life to save his son, Luke, killing the Emperor and dying<ref name="SWT" /> as the last Sith Lord in the lineage of Darth Bane.<ref name="TLJ:TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The destiny of the Chosen One was fulfilled, and Anakin Skywalker became one with the Force. With the Sith rule at an end,<ref name="TSWB" /> understanding Bane's tactics and rationale was seen as crucial for the Jedi to rebuild their order, and to prevent the Sith's rise to power once again.<ref name="Topps Chrome">{{Topps|set=2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|subset=Jedi Temple Archives|cardname=Darth Bane|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/114595/cid/8022636}}</ref>

====The endgame====
{{Quote|The Sith have not risen again since the fall of the Empire, but history proves that they could return when we least expect it.|Jedi Master Luke Skywalker, ''The Secrets of the Jedi''|<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>}}
[[File:DarthBane-TheDarkSide.jpg|thumb|left|265px|Darth Bane's legacy endured for a millennium.]]
Unbeknownst to the galaxy, however, Sidious was resurrected by [[Transference|transferring]] his essence through the Force to an imperfect [[Cloning|cloned]] body. He secretly created a [[Force-sensitive]] [[gene]]tic [[Strand-Cast|strandcast]] that came to be known as [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] to rule the [[First Order]], a military regime succeeding Sidious's Empire, as proxy.<ref name="TSWB" /> In 34 ABY,<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi date">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]'' establishes it was [[author]]ed by [[Luke Skywalker]] during the events of {{Film|VIII}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Luke Skywalker, the last Jedi in existence, included a mention of Bane and the Rule of Two in a [[book]] in which he chronicled the history of the Jedi Order, titled ''[[The Secrets of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi" />

That same year, [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], [[Ren|leader]] of the [[Knights of Ren]] and grandson of Darth Vader, betrayed and [[Battle of Oetchi|killed]] Snoke, who had trained Ren in the ways of the dark side.<ref name="SWT" /> Ren then usurped his late master's title and took control of the First Order in an act of ascension worthy of Darth Bane's Rule of Two.<ref name="Dark Side" /> [[35 ABY|One year later]], the resurrected Sidious made a [[Final Order|failed attempt]] to restore the Sith Order to its former glory.<ref name="SWT" /> During this time,<ref name="Secrets of the Sith date">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]'' establishes it was [[author]]ed by [[Darth Sidious]] during the events of {{Film|IX}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[35 ABY]].</ref> Sidious mentioned Bane while chronicling Sith history in his book, ''[[The Secrets of the Sith]]''. He acknowledged Bane as a great Sith Lord who survived the purge of the ancient Sith and ensured their Order's revival through the Rule of Two. Additionally, Sidious credited Bane as the founder of a lineage of Sith Lords that culminated in his own rise to power.<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> Not long after, Sidious experienced his [[Battle of Exegol|final defeat]], when his granddaughter [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] used the power of the Jedi that came before her, ultimately leading to Sidious, the Sith, and Bane's long lasting vendetta to be destroyed for good.<ref name="SWT" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I chose to pass my knowledge on to only one.|Darth Bane's specter|audio=GhostBane02-TCW.flac|<ref name="Sacrifice" />}}
[[File:DarthBane-2020DarthVader27variant.jpg|thumb|right|225px|Besides his intellectual mind, Darth Bane was a formidable warrior.]]
A visionary Sith Lord,<ref name="TSWB" /> Darth Bane was a [[human]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Bane-DB" /> who possessed [[Color|pale]] skin and yellow [[eye]]s.<ref name="Destiny" /> He believed that strength should have dominance over weakness, developing this philosophy after his personal experiences and by studying ancient [[Sith artifact|Sith documents]]. Having witnessed the destruction of the Sith and realizing the inherent loneliness that arose from [[Dark side adept|serving]] the dark side, Bane saw that their infighting and greed had led to their downfall. To preserve the Sith as its last survivor, Bane adopted the Rule of Two, believing that limiting the number of Sith to one Master and one apprentice would allow the Sith to survive in secret so they could plot their revenge against the Jedi.<ref name="Dark Side" /> Like his descendants, Bane inspired fear in the [[heart]]s of other beings.<ref name="TVE" />

==Powers and abilities==
{{Quote|Created the Rule of Two, you did.|Yoda, to the specter of Darth Bane|audio=YodaVSBane1-TCW.flac|<ref name="Sacrifice" />}}
As a former member of the Brotherhood of Darkness<ref name="Brotherhood" /> and the founder of the Rule of Two, Darth Bane was recognized in [[Timeline of galactic history|history]] for his military tactics and leadership skills.<ref name="JTB CW" /> A [[Sith warrior]]<ref name="TVE" /> renowned for his skills in [[lightsaber combat]] and the use of intimidation, Bane was an agile,<ref name="JTB CW" /> strong, and skilled fighter. While he was a powerful Force wielder and possessed high intelligence, Bane lacked [[diplomat]]ic skills.<ref name="Topps GF" />

==Equipment==
[[File:DarthBaneLightsaber-2020DarthVader27variantcover.jpg|thumb|left|355px|Darth Bane's single-bladed lightsaber]]
Darth Bane [[Clothing|wore]] ancient Sith [[armor]] in the colors of blue, gray, and red, which featured gold etching on it and covered most of his body.<ref name="Destiny 2">{{Destiny|set=Covert Missions|cardname=Ancient Sith Armor|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/12015}}</ref> [[Weapon]]s in the Sith Lord's arsenal included a red-bladed [[Darth Bane's lightsaber|lightsaber]]<ref name="Destiny" /> and a [[Darth Bane's Sith lightsaber pike|lightsaber pike]] featuring a fiery-orange blade.<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> Bane additionally owned a [[T-class long-range personal cruiser]] named the ''[[Valcyn]]'', which was also used by other Sith Lords.<ref name="Falcon 44">{{BuildFalconCite|44|Starship Fact File}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
===Creation and development===
{{Quote|One of the themes throughout the films is that the Sith lords, when they started out thousands of years ago, embraced the dark side. They were greedy and self-centered and they all wanted to take over, so they killed each other. Eventually, there was only one left, and that one took on an apprentice. And for thousands of years, the master would teach the apprentice, the master would die, the apprentice would then teach another apprentice, become the master, and so on. But there could never be any more than two of them, because if there were, they would try to get rid of the leader, which is exactly what Vader was trying to do, and that's exactly what the Emperor was trying to do. The Emperor was trying to get rid of Vader, and Vader was trying to get rid of the Emperor. And that is the antithesis of a symbiotic relationship, in which if you do that, you become cancer, and you eventually kill the host, and everything dies.|George Lucas, on Darth Bane's role in the Sith backstory|<ref name="Time">{{WebCite|url=https://content.time.com/time/magazine/article/0,9171,23298-4,00.html|text=Of Myth And Men|author=[[Wikipedia:Bill Moyers|Moyers, Bill]]; [[George Lucas|Lucas, George]]|date=1999-04-18|work=[[Wikipedia:Time (magazine)|''TIME'']]|pages=p. 4 of 4|archivedate=20221015235638}}</ref>}}
[[File:DarthBane-frontal.jpg|thumb|right|240px|The story of Darth Bane was explored at length in ''Star Wars'' Legends.]]
Created by [[George Lucas]] as part of the backstory for the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]],<ref name="Sacrifice Trivia">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sacrifice-trivia-gallery|text="Sacrifice" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> Darth Bane was first mentioned within the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|novelization]] of {{Film|I}}, written by [[Terry Brooks]]<ref name="TPMNovelization">[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'' novelization]]</ref> and published on [[April 21]], [[1999]].<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> The character was first pictured in ''[[Star Wars: Secrets of the Sith]]'', a young readers' guidebook published in [[2000]]<ref name="secrets2000">''[[Star Wars: Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref> and made his first appearance in the short story "[[Bane of the Sith]]", written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]] and published in the ''[[Star Wars Gamer]]'' magazine's [[Star Wars Gamer 3|third issue]]<ref name="Gamer 3">{{GamerCite|3|Bane of the Sith}}</ref> on [[April 17]], [[2001]],<ref name="Gamer 3 date">{{SWArchive|url=gaming/other/rpg/news20010316.html|text=''Star Wars Gamer'' #3|date=2001-03-16|quote=''Star Wars Gamer'' #3 goes on sale at newsstands on April 17, 2001.}}</ref> and Bane's story was then expanded upon in a number of Legends stories, including a [[Star Wars: Darth Bane Trilogy|novel trilogy]] authored by [[Drew Karpyshyn]] that focuses on the character as the protagonist.<ref name="TERC">''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]''</ref>

While the [[Wikipedia:French language|French]] version of the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]'' establishes that Bane hailed from [[Apatros]],<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]'' {{C|French version only}}</ref> which was the case in the ''Star Wars'' Legends storytelling,<ref name="PoD">''[[Darth Bane: Path of Destruction]]''</ref> ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side Pocket Expert]]'' &mdash; sourcebooks that were published in [[2021]] and [[2023]], respectively &mdash; list Moraband as the Sith Lord's homeworld.<ref name="JTB CW" /><ref name="TDSPE">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side Pocket Expert]]''</ref> This article assumes ''Star Wars: Geektionary'' is incorrect.

===''Star Wars: The Clone Wars''===
====The Mortis arc====
{{Quote|We are those who have come before, those who were betrayed, those who hate. We are the wielders of the dark side.|Darths Bane and Revan introduce themselves to the Son in the deleted scene for "Ghosts of Mortis"|audio=Bane_Revan_Mortis.flac|<ref name="Deleted scene">{{HomeVideoCite|set=Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Three|scene=Son Confers with Ancient Sith Lords}}</ref>}}
During the writing of "[[Ghosts of Mortis]],"<ref name="Ghosts of Mortis Featurette">{{SW|url=video/ghosts-of-mortis-episode-featurette|text="Ghosts of Mortis" Episode Featurette}}</ref> the seventeenth episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated series,<ref name="Ghosts of Mortis EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/ghosts-of-mortis-episode-guide|text="Ghosts of Mortis" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> Lucas wanted to include two ancient Sith Lords who could serve as an "uber evil influence guiding the [[Son]]," the villain of the episode. The production team decided that Darth Bane and [[Revan/Legends|Darth Revan]], the main character of the Legends [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'', would be the two Sith Lords.<ref name="Ghosts of Mortis Featurette" />

[[File:Darth_Bane_Clone_Wars.png|thumb|left|285px|Darth Bane was nearly depicted in the ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' episode "Ghosts of Mortis," based on his Legends appearance.]]
The two characters were styled and modeled for "Ghosts of Mortis" based on their appearances in ''Star Wars'' Legends, but the scene, despite being laid out, was never animated. [[Dave Filoni]], the series' supervising director, was apprehensive of the idea that Sith Lords could return as spirits, believing that Bane and Revan's appearance would have greater implications than the episode was exploring. Lucas thought about it as well and eventually agreed that the scene should be removed from the episode due to those implications on his ideas about the Force.<ref name="Ghosts of Mortis Featurette" />

The [[Content cut from Star Wars|unrealized scene]], titled "Son Confers with Ancient Sith Lords" and released in an uncompleted form in the ''Jedi Temple Archives'' feature for [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Three|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Three]], depicts Bane and Revan speaking to the Son in the [[Well of the Dark Side]] located in the ethereal realm of [[Mortis]]. The Son, who has been guided by the two Sith Lords in the past, questions why he feels pain over the events of the two previous episodes, "[[Overlords]]" and "[[Altar of Mortis (episode)|Altar of Mortis]]," and the Sith Lords tell him it is because he has not embraced his pain and resentment. When the Son demands to know their true identities, the two spirits reveal themselves as Bane and Revan and tell him that controlling Anakin Skywalker, the Chosen One, is the key to controlling the galaxy.<ref name="Deleted scene" />

===="Sacrifice"====
{{Quote|I thought, well, what a great, bizarre reflection to have Mark play, like, the ultimate side of good and light, and then the, kind of, the father of old, ancient evil.|Dave Filoni, on Mark Hamill voicing Darth Bane in ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars''|audio=DarthBaneFiloni.flac|<ref name="Bane Q&A">{{SWYouTube|BFeIKu-m6ws|Darth Bane - The Lost Missions Q&A &#124; Star Wars: The Clone Wars|sw_url=video/the-lost-missions-q-and-a-darth-bane}}</ref>}}
Despite being cut from "Ghosts of Mortis," Darth Bane would eventually make his first [[canon]]ical appearance, albeit as a specter, in "[[Sacrifice (episode)|Sacrifice]],"<ref name="Sacrifice" /> the thirteenth and final episode in the [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth season]] of ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'', which first aired on [[March 7]], [[2014]].<ref name="100 and Counting">{{InsiderCite|150|100 and Counting Expanded}}</ref> Cutting the previously planned appearance allowed the series' team to redesign the character and define what Bane would look like in the new ''Star Wars'' canon.<ref name="Sacrifice Trivia" /> The production team wanted Bane's outfit to resemble ancient warrior armor while still featuring futuristic, high-tech elements.<ref name="Bane Q&A" />

[[File:Darth_Bane_BtS.png|thumb|right|290px|Character concept of Darth Bane, as seen in ''The Clone Wars'' episode "Sacrifice"]]
The concept design for Bane, created by [[Darren Marshall]], included a full armor suit<ref name="Sacrifice Concept Art">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sacrifice-concept-art-gallery|text="Sacrifice" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slides 1 and 3}}</ref> that was influenced by both [[Wikipedia:Japanese armour|samurai armor]], which also inspired the design of [[Darth Vader's armor|Darth Vader's suit]], as well as ancient [[Wikipedia:Chinese armour|Chinese medieval armor]].<ref name="Bane Q&A" /> Although not being featured in the episode itself,<ref name="Sacrifice" /> the concept art for "Sacrifice" contained a "Sith warrior [[lightsaber pike]]" designed by [[David Le Merrer]],<ref name="Sacrifice Concept Art" /> which later appeared in the 2021 reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''.<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> Additionally, according to [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Pablo Hidalgo]], Bane's specter was conjured by the [[Force Priestesses]] in the episode.<ref name="Hidalgo Priestess">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/809169127977816064|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2016-12-14|quote=that was an illusion created by the Priestesses|archivefile=File:Darth_Bane_Priestesses_illusion.png|nolive=1}}</ref>

Bane's specter was voiced by [[Mark Hamill]], best known for his portrayal of Luke Skywalker in the [[Star Wars saga|''Star Wars'' saga]]. To create Bane's voice as heard in the episode, Dave Filoni recorded Hamill reading his lines using two different inflections, and then sound designers [[Matthew Wood]] and [[David Acord]] layered the recordings on top of one another.<ref name="Bane Q&A" /> Prior to taking on the role of Bane, Hamill did not know of the character or how Bane fit into the overall arc of ''Star Wars''. His son [[Nathan Hamill]] knew about the history of the character and was able to explain it to his father once he took on the role.<ref name="Hamill SW Weekend">{{YouTube|video=Hmo0TsB2wcI|text=Mark Hamill Conversation FULL Show Day 3, Star Wars Weekends - Talks Yoda, Joker, Stunts, Diet +|channel=@MouseStepsWeekly|name=MouseSteps / JWL Media|date=2014-06-09|quote=My son Nathan knew the history of Darth Bane, because I didn't even realize how he fit in the scheme of things.}}</ref>

''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]'', a 2021 book that retells the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' and the [[original trilogy]] in the style of [[Wikipedia:Homer|Homeric]] [[Wikipedia:Epic poetry|epic poetry]],<ref name="Odyssey Excerpt">{{SW|url=news/the-odyssey-of-star-wars-excerpt-interview|text=A Tremor Ripples Through the Force in ''The Odyssey of Star Wars'' - Excerpt|author=[[Kristin Baver|Baver, Kristin]]|date=2021-09-28}}</ref> depicts an alternate version of the scene where Vader speaks to the Emperor via [[hologram]] in {{Film|V}}. In the epic poem, the Emperor tells Vader to recall how Bane restored the Sith Order from ruin and instituted the Rule of Two,<ref name="Odyssey" /> and unlike the film,<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref> the conversation occurs before the Empire discovers the [[Echo Base|rebel base]] on the planet [[Hoth]]. ''The Odyssey of Star Wars'' also establishes that Sidious kept twin statues of Darth Bane and the [[Unidentified rogue Jedi|nameless founder]] of the Sith Order in his [[Emperor's Throne Room|throne room]] aboard the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] [[battle station]]. Both statues were made out of the hardest [[crystal]], with Bane's statue representing him in his [[iron]] armor.<ref name="Odyssey" /> According to the book's author, [[Jack Mitchell]], these statues are used as [[Wikipedia:Ekphrasis|ekphrastic]] devices, a way of giving background embedded in the narrative, and not to be necessarily considered as canonical facts.<ref name="Mitchell-Discord">{{WookDiscord|discordname=Holshef|name=Jack Mitchell|date=2021-11-22|messagelink=https://discord.com/channels/397848917036761090/492458000720592906/912207621069615104|quote=In the case of the statues in the emperor's throne room on Death Star II, that's an example of what they call 'ecphrasis,' the description of works of art as a way of giving background that's embedded in the narrative, instead of just having the narrator step back and say, 'OK, there was this guy Darth Bane&hellip;' So in this case I would hesitate to say that the poem was adding to canon by asserting that there were statues in the throne room &mdash; for one thing, none are visible in the film! Personally I would class them as neither canonical nor anti-canonical.|archivefile=File:Mitchell-Discord.png}}</ref>
