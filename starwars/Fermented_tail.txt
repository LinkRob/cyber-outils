'''Fermented tail''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|dish]] made from [[Robuma]], a [[diet|herbivorous]] [[mammal]] [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Toshara]], a [[moon]] [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> fermented tail was available for purchase at [[Market District (Mirogana)|Market District]] of [[Mirogana]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of Toshara.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fermented tail was mentioned in an ambient conversation that the player can overhear in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
