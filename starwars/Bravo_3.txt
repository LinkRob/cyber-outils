'''Bravo 3''' was a [[pilot]] [[callsign]] used within [[Bravo Squadron]], an [[N-1 starfighter]] [[squadron]] within the [[Naboo Royal Space Fighter Corps|Space Fighter Corps]] of the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]] that served the [[Monarch of Naboo]]. During the [[Battle of Naboo]]<ref name="TPM" /> in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Arven Wendik]]<ref name="BMF31">{{BuildFalconCite|31|Starship Fact File}}</ref> flew under the callsign, taking part in the attack on the [[Trade Federation]] [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]] ''[[Vuutun Palaa]]'' and ultimately surviving the battle.<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
The callsign Bravo 3 was first used in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. The callsign is not spoken in the film but is listed in the credits for Wendik's actor, [[Clarence Smith]], who is listed as "Fighter Pilot Bravo 3."<ref name="TPM" />
