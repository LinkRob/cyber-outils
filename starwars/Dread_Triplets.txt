The '''Dread Triplets''' were [[pirate]]s who operated on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. They were included in an Imperial report of piracy in the [[Akiva system]] that had been stored at Imperial [[Research Station 57]] on Akiva<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate|APF=1}}</ref> In the report, they were listed as still at large along with fellow pirates [[Silver-fin Bollas]] and [[Big Muhh]]. The report went on to blame the local [[Satrapy of Myrra|satrapy government]] for failing to prevent privacy in the Akiva [[Star system|system]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Around 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> while the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] was looking for information about the whereabouts of a different pirate named [[Qorvino]] at Research Station 57, she accessed the report mentioning the Dread Triplets.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dread Triplets was mentioned on a [[Computer|data]] entry in the "A Pirate's Fortune" story expansion of the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> Viewing the data entry that mentions the triplets is a required step of the expansion's first main quest, "A Pirate's Plight." Starting the story expansion quest requires players to have completed the game's base story. As such, this article assumes completion of the base game story and completion of the "A Pirate's Plight" quest in the "A Pirate's Fortune" story expansion.<ref name="Outlaws" />
