'''Bilbousa Spaceport''' was a [[spaceport]] located in [[Bilbousa]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 24" /> the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]]<ref name="BMF 81">{{BuildFalconCite|81|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> [[Nal Hutta]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 24" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Bounty Hunters 24|''Bounty Hunters'' 24]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> the [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']], the [[flagship]] of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate, traveled to the spaceport to rendezvous with its agents. At the same [[time]], the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] hired fellow [[bounty]] hunter [[Dengar]], who was secretly aligned with Crimson Dawn, to get her and her [[T'onga's crew|crew]] [[Raid on the Vermillion|aboard]] the ''Vermillion'' to rescue the [[Youngling|child]] [[Cadeliah]] who was being help [[Prisoner|captive]] on the [[Starship|vessel]]. Dengar led the crew to the spaceport, where they were able to get onboard the ''Vermillion'', though they realized Dengar had actually led them into a trap.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 24" />
