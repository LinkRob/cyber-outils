"'''A Necessary Bond'''" is the ninth episode of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' television series']] [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]]. It aired on [[November 24]], [[2012]], marking the final episode of a four-part story arc which began with "[[The Gathering (episode)|The Gathering]]". 

==Official description==
''When General Grievous launches a full-scale attack on the pirate base on Florrum, Ahsoka and the younglings fight side-by-side with Hondo and his pirates to turn back the Separatist forces.''<ref name="Explore: A Necessary Bond" />

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|We have a common enemy in Grievous. Together, we can get out of this.|Ahsoka Tano, to the pirates|<ref name="Bond" />}}
[[Ahsoka Tano|Ahsoka]] and her [[youngling]] rescuers haste to return to the ''[[Crucible]]'', with a team of [[Hondo Ohnaka|Hondo Ohanka's]] [[Weequay]] pirates inside a [[WLO-5 speeder tank|speeder tank]] in hot pursuit. After some frantic attempts, [[Petro]] finally manages to contact [[R2-D2]] and [[Ganodi]] aboard their cruiser, who have just restored [[Huyang]] to an operative state (minus his arms) once more. The three of them set out to aid their friends at once, and attempt to pick them up in mid-run via the cruiser's boarding ramp. But in the midst of the evac attempt, their speeder races to a ravine, forcing Petro to turn about, leaving [[Byph]] and [[Gungi]] to hang precariously from the ramp. During a second attempt, however, the pirates manage to hit the cruiser's engines critically, forcing an immediate evacuation of the ''Crucible'' before it crashed down on the [[Florrum]] plains, taking the [[Jedi]]'s ride with it. They are all then cuffed and taken prisoner on the pirate's tank.

With the Jedi in shackles, the pirates return to their base, only to suddenly find it being paid an unpleasant surprise visit by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Battle droid|droid]] army under the command of [[General]] [[Grievous]] in person. Taking the baffled band by surprise, Grievous opens a [[Hologram|holo-communication]] channel through which Count Dooku contacts Ohnaka. The Count, having neither forgotten nor forgiven Hondo's questionable hospitality during [[Dooku Captured|their first encounter]], announces that he's taking control of the planet and plans to dismantle Ohnaka's arsenal making it part of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. When Ohnaka protests against this, he is swiftly incarcerated.

In the face of this common crisis and need to escape the Separatists' clutches, Ahsoka begins to argue with her captors. Following her suggestion that the pirates must have some additional ships stashed away somewhere, the pirate leader reveals that Hondo does have a private fleet, but only he knows where it is hidden. Ahsoka argues that as with her knowledge of Grievous, she could be a valuable help for them all, whereupon the pirates release her and the younglings. Ahsoka then devises a plan to use R2 and the pirate's tank to divert the droids' attention for a bit while she and part of her entourage sneak into the pirate base and find their way to Hondo's cell. After some arguing, Hondo demands that the younglings show him their [[lightsaber]]s to demonstrate their readiness for the fight ahead. All the younglings ignite their sabers, except for [[Katooni]], who still hasn't managed to make hers work. With some encouragement from Huyang and Hondo, however, Katooni finally succeeds in correctly assembling her weapon and freeing Hondo from his electronic field shackles.

The united Jedi and pirates proceed to liberate the rest of Hondo's men, but one droid manages to alert Grievous to their presence, and Grievous decides to cut the problem short by having the prison section levelled by his [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|tanks]]. But their opposition manages to break out and flee the base with their remaining vehicles, whereupon Grievous and several [[Single Trooper Aerial Platform|STAPs]] give chase. Barely evading their pursuers, who continue to chase after Ahsoka and the other Jedi, Katooni, Hondo and his pirates reach a [[Hondo's secret ship vault|hidden hangar]] wherein Hondo's secret ships are kept. Hondo decides to fly away from the battle at first, but Katooni succeeds in appealing to his sense of honor.

In the meantime, Grievous exchanges several blows with Ahsoka and prepares to board the Jedi Initiates's speeder tank, which forces R2 to execute some risky maneuvers in an attempt to shake him off. However, this move causes the tank to crash instead. The stunned Jedi recover just as Grievous reappears, ready to continue the fight as the younglings unite behind Ahsoka, igniting their lightsabers, unafraid and ready to battle. Grievous moves in for the kill when Ohnaka's escape ship, the rebuilt ''Slave I'', arrives on the scene to pick up Ahsoka and the Jedi Initiates. Ahsoka sends the younglings to the craft and engages Grievous in combat to cover their escape. Although Ahsoka backflips, slashes, and utilizes the Force to gain an advantage, Grievous is more than a match, overpowering the Padawan through the implementation of his additional limbs and clawed feet. They fight with their lightsabers crossed until Grievous grabs Ahsoka with his clawed feet, hurls her in the air, and smashes her down as he charges, his blades spinning rapidly as she keeps rolling, before leaping onto her feet and locking blades with Grievous again. An exhausted Ahsoka shoves Grievous hard enough before jumping over him in order to flee. She then barely escapes onto the ship on her own. Before Hondo has a chance to properly repay Grievous for his cordiality, a squad of AATs forces them to withdraw for good, leaving a angered Grievous to laugh in victory.

[[File:ANB_task_force.png|thumb|right|300px|The Republic task force]]
After meeting with a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Grand Army of the Republic|rescue team]] which has already picked up [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and the remnants of his men following their [[Attack on Obi-Wan Kenobi's fleet|near-fatal defeat]] by Grievous' hands, Ohnaka boldly attempts to weasel his way out of trouble by claiming that his previous attack on the Crucible was merely a "rescue mission," and due to bemusement and Hondo's aid to the younglings, Obi-Wan decides to leave it at that. Hondo departs, but not before giving Katooni a nod of appreciation. Obi-Wan approaches Ahsoka, and expresses relief in seeing her and the younglings unharmed. Ahsoka's reply sparks Huyang to remark on their mission as being as eventful as when Master Yoda went to get his kyber crystal. After lauding the younglings for their success in the liberation mission, Obi-Wan invites them to form a wheel with their ignited lightsabers, formally welcoming the younglings into the [[Jedi Order]].

==Continuity==
"A Necessary Bond" is the conclusion to a four-part story arc,<ref name="SW Blog: Insider 137">{{InsiderCite|137|Launchpad|Launch Pad}}</ref> which was titled "Young Jedi" and previewed as a full-length 80-minute feature at [[Celebration VI]] in [[August]] [[2012]] to family viewers.<ref name="TFN Young Jedi">{{WebCite|url=http://www.theforce.net/latestnews/story/CVI_TCW_Young_Jedi_Arc_Previewed_147724.asp|text=CVI: ''TCW'' "Young Jedi" Arc Previewed on TheForce.net|author=Eric|archivedate=20190730010724}}</ref> It replaced<ref name="SW Blog: Insider 137" /> "[[Secret Weapons]]," originally reported by [[Star Wars Insider 137|''Star Wars Insider'' 137]], as the ninth episode in Season Five.<ref name="SW Blog: Insider 137" /> Some of the ideas in this story came from a scrapped pilot script for a [[Untitled Young Padawans spin-off series|spin-off TV series]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.cinemablend.com/television/1589720/the-last-star-wars-project-george-lucas-considered-before-selling-to-disney|text=The Last Star Wars Project George Lucas Considered Before Selling To Disney|author=Veneble, Nick|date=2016-11-25|archivedate=20190521211101}}</ref>

When General Grievous arrives at the pirate's base, he says to Hondo: "You can dispense with the pleasantries, pirate!" This is an allusion to Darth Vader's similar line to Moff [[Tiaan Jerjerrod|Tiann Jerjerrod]] when he arrives to inspect the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] at the beginning of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].
