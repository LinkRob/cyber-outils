A '''company''' of the [[Galactic Empire]] was stationed on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[mining]] colony of [[New Valis]]. The [[infantry]] [[company]] consisted of [[stormtrooper]]s, and was led by [[Imperial Army]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Fhaen]], who was considered inexperienced, and had little to no combat experience. During the [[liberation of New Valis]], the company fought against a [[Ette Shi'lou's platoon|platoon]] of the [[Marine Corps]] from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], and were eventually defeated and forced to retreat.<ref name="Lead" />
