'''Cousin''' was the second-highest [[Chiss family|family]] rank of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. In the [[Chiss]] hierarchy, it was situated above the rank of [[ranking distant]] but below the rank of [[Blood (rank)|blood]].<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> The children of ranking distants were born as cousins. Those who held the rank of cousin possessed some of the same rights and privileges as blood, including the right to make personal declarations of family.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>

[[Mid Captain]] [[Ufsa'mak'ro|Samakro]] held the rank of cousin of the [[Ruling Families|Ruling]] [[Ufsa family]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> [[Syndic]] [[Mitth'ras'safis|Thrass]] held the rank of cousin of the Ruling [[Mitth family]]. Before his birth, his [[Mitth'ras'safis's father|father]] was killed saving the life of the Mitth [[Patriarch]] [[Mitth'oor'akiord|Thooraki]]. As thanks, the Patriarch rematched Thrass's [[Mitth'ras'safis's mother|mother]] into the Mitth familly as a ranking distant. Therefore, Thrass was born a Mitth cousin.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
