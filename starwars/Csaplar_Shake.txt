{{Quote|Before long, we were sharin' drinks and tradin' recipes&mdash;includin' this fantastic frozen drink with a froth as blue as a Chiss's skin.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Csaplar Shakes''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|shake]] with [[Color|blue]] froth found on the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] of [[Csilla]]. During a tour of [[the galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited the [[capital]] [[city]] [[Csaplar]] on Csilla where he shared drinks and recipes at the space port. Chef, Tuggs included the recipe for the shake in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Parnassos Swirl Cake was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish makes use of [[Wikipedia:Icing (food)|frosting]], [[Wikipedia:Rock candy|rock candy]], [[Wikipedia:Vanilla|vanilla]] [[Wikipedia:Ice cream|ice cream]], [[Wikipedia:Milk|milk]], [[Wikipedia:Blue raspberry flavor|blue raspberry syrup]], [[Wikipedia:Vanilla extract|vanilla extract]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], [[Wikipedia:Cream|whipped cream]], and [[Wikipedia:Freeze dried|freeze dried]] [[Wikipedia:Raspberries|raspberries]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
