'''Berretic''' was a [[lieutenant]] who served under [[General]] [[Sotorus Ramda]]. Before the [[Battle of Scarif]], Ramda sent Berretic a request listing loaned equipment and [[troop]]s that he wanted back. In the request, Ramda informed Berretic to rewrite the request using official formatting and to send it up through the [[oversector]] chain of command until an official acknowledged the request. Ramda made it clear, however, not to send the request to [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], as Tarkin was very close to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and that they always needed to demonstrate their best towards Palpatine, and so ordered Berretic to instead send the request to one of the [[sector admiral]]s.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

In the request, Ramda detailed that he wanted two [[squadron]]s of [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter]]s loaned to the ''[[Unassailable]]'', in order to have adequate patrols and allow for proper training of [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilots]]. Ramda also requested that twelve [[All Terrain Armored Cargo Transport|AT-ACTs]] be returned in order to [[transport]] valuable [[ore]] from [[Mining|mines]] and refineries, as well two [[Company|companies]] of [[Coastal defender stormtrooper|shoretroopers]]. Ramda saw no issue in having the shoretroopers returned as he believed that [[Scarif]] was one of the few [[planet]]s to be suitable for them, and did not imagine that they could serve much aboard [[Star Destroyer]]s. Lastly, Ramda requested to be returned two [[platoon]]s of [[death trooper]]s, as the [[Death trooper training camp|training camp]] on Scarif needed more instructors. Ramda preferred veteran death troopers with body augmentations at v1.01 or earlier.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
