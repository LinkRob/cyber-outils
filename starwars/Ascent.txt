The '''''Ascent''''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]] which, along with the [[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|''Vigilance'']] and the ''[[Vanquish]]'', was present at the [[planet]] [[Akiva]] for the [[Rebellion on Akiva|battle there]]. It brought one of the Empire's greatest tacticians, [[General]] [[Jylia Shale]], to the [[Akiva Summit|emergency summit on Akiva]]. It was destroyed after the summit.<ref name="Aftermath" />
