'''Blue Four''' was the [[callsign]] of the fourth member of the [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

==History==
[[File:Kickback-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|Clone pilot "Kickback" flew as Blue Four during the Battle of Ryloth.]]
[[Clone trooper pilot]] "[[Kickback]]" served as Blue Four at the [[Battle of Ryloth]]. He was one of the few pilots to survive the battle.<ref name="Storm">{{TCW|Storm Over Ryloth}}</ref>

[[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[pilot]] [[Barion Raner]] served as Blue Four around the time of the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

During the [[Battle of Jakku]], a member of the New Republic's [[77 Squadron]] (which flew as Blue Squadron in the battle) used the callsign.<ref name="Hyperspace Mountain" />

[[Resistance pilot]] [[Pallaris Ven]] flew as Blue Four with [[astromech droid]] [[BR-BA]] and perished in the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 28" />
