{{Quote|Divo's theory, updated years after the incident, was that Farwan & Glott, the original manufacturers of Kee's podracer, arranged the hit to get their hands on Kee's customization secrets&mdash;modifications he otherwise refused to share.|Exantor Divo, ''Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious''|<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>}}
'''Farwan & Glott''' was a small [[podracer]] manufacturer who produced the unusual and advanced [[FG 8T8-T]], which sacrificed speed for [[pilot]] safety. The [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Neva Kee]] flew a modified [[Neva Kee's Custom Podracer|FG 8T8-T]] in the [[32 BBY]] [[Boonta Eve Classic]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], but refused to share his modifications. Kee vanished during the race, and [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[Inspector]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tanivos Divo]] later formed a theory that Farwan & Glott had been behind the disappearance in an attempt to learn of the modifications.

==Description==
[[File:NevaKeePodracer-SWCTP.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Neva Kee in the cockpit of a FG 8T8-T, with its large engines fused to the cockpit.|Farwan & Glott produced the unusual FG 8T8-T, which sacrificed speed for safety.]]
Farwan & Glott was a small boutique producer of [[podracer]]s and high-performance podracer parts. One of its products was the technologically advanced [[FG 8T8-T]], which was among the most unique and visually striking podracers on the market but also the slowest of the factory customs available while it was on sale.<ref name="Endless Vigil" />

It was designed with maneuverability as the focus and eschewed normal podracer construction by having its two [[LGN-19 MagnaPulse ion drive]]s&mdash;[[ion engine]]s designed for [[starfighter]]s or [[Patrol ship|patrol boats]]&mdash;fused directly onto the [[cockpit]] aft of the [[pilot]]. The enclosed cockpit itself was also unusual, as it prioritized pilot safety and luxury over speed and acceleration with advanced handling and braking and an [[ejector seat]].<ref name="Endless Vigil" /> 

==History==
[[File:NevaKeeDisappeared-ScumAndVillany.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=In a dark cave on Tatooine, Jabba the Hutt speaks with Aurra Sing as two thugs drag Neva Kee away from the burning wreckage of his podracer.|Tanivos Divo theorized that Farwan & Glott arranged the disappearance of Neva Kee.]]
The [[Xamster]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Neva Kee]] used a [[Neva Kee's Custom Podracer|FG 8T8-T]] in<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> the [[32 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}}, in which [[Neva Kee]] participates in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] in a [[FG 8T8-T]], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> [[Boonta Eve Classic]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. Kee modified the podracer but refused to share his experimental modifications and then vanished during the [[Podracing|race]]. After [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[Inspector]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tanivos Divo]] was sent an unverifiable [[hologram]] showing that [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] had paid [[bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing]] to capture Kee after he crashed, Divo formed a theory [[Standard year|years]] after the race that Farwan & Glott had organized the hit in order to learn of Kee's secret modifications to their vehicle. Divo's [[Divo family|grandson]], [[Exantor Divo]], later published this theory in his [[book]], ''[[Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'',<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{ScumAndVillainyDate}}</ref>

Spahn]] and published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Endless Vigil">''[[Endless Vigil]]''</ref> The [[Farwan & Glott/Legends|company]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[1999]] [[Star Wars: Episode I Insider's Guide|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Insider's Guide'']] reference software.<ref name="InsidersGuide">[[Star Wars: Episode I Insider's Guide|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Insider's Guide'']]</ref>
