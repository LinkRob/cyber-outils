'''A Mother's Memoirs, Concluded''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[April 14]], [[2005]] and took the form of a final memoir recorded by the [[Tarasin]] [[Mother (title)/Legends|Mother]] [[Dariana]]. It related the death of her mother and the final words she passed on. It was accompanied by role-playing statistics for Dariana.
