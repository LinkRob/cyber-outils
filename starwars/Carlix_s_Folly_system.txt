The '''Carlix's Folly system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Tharin sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The system was located in grid square S-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="map" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Carlix's Folly system was first introduced into [[Canon|Star Wars Canon]] by the [[StarWars.com]] article "[[Star Wars Galaxy Map]]."<ref name="map" /> The system was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends]] continuity in the [[StarWars.com]] [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Atlas">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
