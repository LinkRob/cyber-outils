The '''firewolf''' was a creature known to the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], the nation of the [[Chiss]] [[species]]. The [[Firewolf man-of-war]], a [[warship]] class used by the [[Chiss Defense Force]], was named for the creature. The firewolf was also represented as a piece in [[Tactica]], a strategy board game played in the Ascendancy.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The firewolf appeared as a piece of the [[in-universe]] Tactica board game in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final volume of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
