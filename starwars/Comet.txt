'''Comets''' were giant [[Water|ice]] formations floating through [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Sunny Day" />

Eons before the [[Battle of Endor]], a comet named [[Kinro]] was predicted to destroy several [[Core Worlds|Core]] [[planet]]s. The comet was destroyed before it reached the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]], and its destruction was attributed to the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>

During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]] and [[D-Squad]] encountered a [[comet storm]] near the planet [[Abafar]].<ref name="Sunny Day" />

A [[Ilum system cometary cloud|cometary cloud]] was located in the [[Ilum system]].<ref name="NoP">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>

A class of [[mining ship]]s that mined comets for [[thorilide]] were called Comet Chasers. A fleet of them returned to the [[Calcoraan Depot]] in [[11 ABY]] after exhausting a fresh supply of thorilide bearing comets.<ref name="AND">''[[A New Dawn]]''</ref>
