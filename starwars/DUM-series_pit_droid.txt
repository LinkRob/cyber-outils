The '''DUM-series pit droid''' was a model of [[Maintenance droid|repair droid]] manufactured by [[Serv-O-Droid, Inc.]]<ref name="CharEncyclo" /> which have been in use for about [[232 BBY|two centuries]]<ref name="Nash's Race Day">{{YJA|Nash's Race Day}}</ref> by the time of the [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="The Phantom Menace" /> [[WAC-47]] was a pit droid, though he was programmed to be a [[pilot]].<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|characters|wac-47|WAC-47}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|You know, it's costing me a lot of money to keep these droids even powered up.|Peli Motto, on her DUM-series pit droids|<ref name="TheMandalorian Chapter 5">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 5: The Gunslinger}}</ref>}}
[[File:DUM-series_pit_droid.png|thumb|left|200px|A reddish colored pit droid during the Clone Wars]]
Pit droids were cheap, expendable,<ref name="Pit Droid">{{Databank|pit-droid|Pit Droid}}</ref> and incredibly durable repair [[droid]]s.<ref name="Intel">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-inside-intel-podracing|text=''Star Wars'' Inside Intel: Podracing|int=Star Wars Inside Intel}}</ref> Pit droids stood at a height of 1.19 [[meter]]s<ref name="Pit Droid" /> but had the ability to fold into a compact form when not in use. This feature could be toggled by tapping their "nose."<ref name="The Phantom Menace" />Pit droids were able to lift objects several times their own weight. They were encased in hardened alloy casings that allowed them to endure the harsh weather on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

Pit droids were programmed with a sense of urgency, though their minimal logic processors prevented them from completing complex tasks. In the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]], pit droids were mostly used to [[Fuel|refuel]] vehicles, reach tight spaces, and make simple wire connections.<ref name="Intel" />

If one could not reach the system it needed to work on, the unit would get other pit droids to make an unstable and haphazard droid pyramid. Pit droids were also fearless when faced with unsafe tasks, going as far as to be happy to throw themselves into danger.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

==History==
During the [[Boonta Eve]] [[Boonta Eve Classic|Classic]] in [[Mos Espa]] on Tatooine in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], which includes the [[Boonta Eve Classic]], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> [[Ody Mandrell]] had a record-setting team of pit droids for his [[Ody Mandrell's Podracer|podracer]]. Unfortunately, one of the pit droids was sucked into one of the intakes of Mandrell's podracer. The droid survived, but Mandrell's podracer engine was shot.<ref name="The Phantom Menace" />

[[File:PitDroid-Db.png|thumb|250px|right|A pit droid inspects a piece of equipment while holding a power wrench]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]] [[Commanding officer|CO]], [[Clone Commander]] [[Neyo]] had a pit droid designated as [[WAC-47]] that served him.<ref name="DB:W-47">{{Databank|wac-47|WAC-47}}</ref> WAC-47 was also a pilot and was chosen to be [[D-Squad]]'s pilot under [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]].<ref name="SW" /> During the mission, WAC-47 received a field promotion to [[Corporal]].<ref name="ASDitV">{{TCW|A Sunny Day in the Void}}</ref>

WAC-47 later joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and reunited with [[R2-D2]] during the [[Scourge crisis|scourge of the droids]].<ref name="D-Squad 1">[[Dark Droids: D-Squad 1|''Dark Droids: D-Squad'' 1]]</ref> A number of other pit droids served aboard the ''[[Silver Arrow]]'', a vessel in the [[Rebel Alliance Navy]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The [[Scourge]], however, managed to get aboard the ship and infect all droid units aboard, including the pit droids. The infected droids then murdered all organic crew and later attacked [[Magna Tolvan's crew]].<ref name="Aphra 37" />

In [[3 BBY]], a pit droid was a member of [[Skraik's gang]] stealing ship parts in [[Garel City]] on [[Garel]].<ref>{{RebelsMagCite|29|The Size of the Fight}}</ref>

After the fall of the [[Galactic Empire]], the manager of [[Hangar 3-5]] at [[Mos Eisley]], [[Peli Motto]], used three pit droids to make repairs on docking ships.<ref name="TheMandalorian Chapter 5" />

During [[34 ABY]], several pit droids were seen on the [[space station]] known as the ''[[Colossus]]'' worked as [[labor droid]] and [[loader droid]]s. One pit droid was named [[G-LN]] who was owned by [[Flix]] and [[Orka]]. G-LN assisted them in the [[Office of Acquisitions]] by helping them retrieve items or assist customers.<ref name="The Recruit" />

==Behind the scenes==
Pit droids first appeared in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="The Phantom Menace" />In [[Canon]], the droids were first identified as DUM-series pit droids by the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Art director [[Doug Chiang]] created early design sketches of pit droids for ''The Phantom Menace''.<ref name="Old DB">{{DB|droid|pitdroid|pit droid}}</ref> Sound engineer [[Ben Burtt]] created their voice with the same principle as [[R2-D2]]'s [[Binary]], but instead of a musical synthesizer, he used the cute noises of baby toys.<ref name="guide">[[Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide (real-life book)|''Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide'']]</ref>

When it was decided that a droid would be the main character of the [[Star Wars Galaxy of Creatures]] animated micro-series, the series' crew originally wanted to use a pit droid. [[Matt Martin]], a member of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]], didn't like the concept of a droid doing something that it wasn't originally designed to do. As a replacement, the team created the [[Safari droid]], which had features similar to that of a pit droid.<ref name="Launchpad 206">{{InsiderCite|206|Launchpad}}</ref>
