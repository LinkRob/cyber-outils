'''Aaron Brown''' is a [[Star Wars video games|video game]] artist at [[Obsidian Entertainment]]. He is credited as a member of the Art team for ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]'' and made the models used for the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Hologram/Legends|holograms]] on the [[Ebon Hawk]].
