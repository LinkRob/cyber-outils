'''Fun Works Play To Learn''', simply referred to as '''Fun Works''', was a trademarked publishing imprint of the [[The Walt Disney Company]], based in [[Wikipedia:Burbank, California|Burbank, California]], used to publish children's books from [[1997]] to [[1998]].<ref name="uspto">{{WebCite|url=http://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=75182478&caseType=SERIAL_NO&searchType=statusSearch|text=Fun Works Play To Learn - United States Patent & Trademark Office|archivedate=20120107132834}}</ref> In 1997, Fun Works published the [[Star Wars Action Toy Book]] series in conjunction with production company [[White Heat, Ltd.]]
