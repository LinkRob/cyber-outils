'''Alva Brenne''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served in the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[colonel]] and [[Chief Imperial Architect]]. She was responsible for [[Jedi Temple remodeling project|transforming]] the [[Imperial Palace]] from its former identity as the Jedi Temple of the old [[Jedi Order]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the Empire, she was assigned by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] to design [[Fortress Vader|whatever]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] deemed necessary on his own [[planet]] of [[Mustafar]]. While designing a castle for Lord Vader, Brenne was murdered by [[Lieutenant]] [[Roggo]] who had been possessed by the [[Mask of Lord Momin|mask]] of the ancient [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Momin]].

==Biography==
===Serving the Emperor===
{{Quote|I will send an Imperial architect with you, to design whatever facilities you deem appropriate. Colonel Alva Brenne. She oversaw the alterations to my palace&mdash;removed all traces of the Jedi. She is very good. An appealingly brutal aesthetic.|Darth Sidious, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />}}
[[File:Imperial_Temple.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Alva Brenne oversaw the alterations that transformed the Jedi temple into the Imperial Palace.]]
Alva Brenne was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[colonel]] and [[Chief Imperial Architect]] for the [[Galactic Empire]]. In [[19 BBY]], following the [[Republic Era|rise of the Empire]] and the [[Order 66|fall of the Jedi Order]], Brenne oversaw the alterations made to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. All traces of the [[Jedi]] were wiped, and the temple was [[Jedi Temple remodeling project|transformed]] into [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] Imperial Palace. Pleased with Brenne's work on the Imperial Palace, Emperor Palpatine assigned Brenne to design [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[Fortress Vader|stronghold]] on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]], along with anything else the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] deemed appropriate.<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />

===Mission to Mustafar===
{{Quote|If this were a job just ''anyone'' could do, they wouldn't have sent Colonel Alva Brenne.|Brenne|<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />}}
[[File:RoggoBrenneAndVader-2017DarthVader21.png|thumb|right|300px|Brenne, in the wake of the Inquisitorius incident, accompanies Vader to Mustafar to design his base.]]
Following the [[Coruscant Incident|Rogue Inquisitor chase]] on [[Coruscant]], Colonel Brenne and [[Lieutenant]] [[Roggo]] accompanied Darth Vader aboard the [[Naboo Royal Starship]] on a trip to Mustafar. As they were approaching Mustafar, Vader informed Brenne that he was to take control of the ship once they entered Mustafar's orbit. Aboard the starship, Brenne and Roggo pondered what aesthetic would best suit the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord's]] fortress. Brenne initially believed Vader would prefer a minimalist approach to his castle, but the elegant design of Vader's [[J-type 327 Nubian royal starship|J-type 327 Nubian starship]] made Brenne doubt her conclusion. An [[Mask of Lord Momin|ancient, "sinister" mask]] aboard the ship caught the eye of Brenne and Roggo. Unbeknownst to the two [[Imperial officer|imperials]], the mask housed the spirit of it's once owner, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Momin]].<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />

While entering Mustafar's atmosphere, Brenne and Roggo were alerted by the ship's safety system that the external hull temperatures had reached critical levels. Upon further examination, Roggo discovered the Nubian starship was entering Mustafar's atmosphere without any [[Deflector shield|shields]]. Despite the their pleads, the pilot, Vader, refused to turn the starship shields's back on. In a panic Brenne reached for her blaster, but she was quickly disarmed by the Dark Lord, who reiterate that the Emperor had placed her and Roggo under Vader's authority. The three landed on the [[Gahenn Plains]] near a [[Sith cave]] Vader had previously visited. The once pristine mirror finished hull of the Nubian starship had melted to a crisp as a result of Vader's hazardous approach. Upon seeing the ship exterior, Brenne stated to Roggo that she now had a better understanding of Darth Vader's aesthetic.<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />

Alva Brenne immediately began her work designing Darth Vader's castle. At first Brenne took a basic, functional approuch when designing Vader's fortress. After scouting the natural landmarks, Brenne concluded she would have the initial castle designs finished in four to five hours. The natural lava flow that ran over the Sith Cave into was planned to be incorporated into the castle's design. Brenne presented her finished castle schematics to Vader, however, the Sith Lord was unimpressed with the design. Setback, Brenne retreated to retool the designs in hopes of pleasing the dark lord.<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />

===Death===
{{Quote|Looks like we're starting over, Roggo. We have officially entered the Brandy phase of the&hellip; project.|Brenne, shortly before her murder|<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />}}
[[File:AlvaBrennesDeath-2017DarthVader21.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Brenne is found murdered by Lord Vader before running into Roggo under possession of the Mask of Lord Momin.]]
While Colonel Brenne was presenting her designs to Lord Vader, the spirit of Lord Momin that was hosted within the ancient mask had taken possession of Roggo. When Brenne returned to the ship to rework her designs, Roggo, now a host for Momin, shot and murdered Brenne. Momin then began to create his own design for Darth Vader's castle. Brenne's corpse was quickly discovered by Lord Vader, who swiftly cut down murderer Roggo with his [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|My fortress will tune the energies of the locus. It is your first step toward using the power of the Dark Side to pierce the veil of time, between life and death.|Lord Momin to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 22">[[Darth Vader (2017) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 22]]</ref>}}
Despite the death of Roggo, Momin's spirit remained unharmed within his mask. Momin communed with Darth Vader, and the two Sith Lords agreed to cooperate to further their goals. Momin was to continue to design Vader's castle, which would now act as a doorway through the Force that could tune the energies of the dark side locus on Mustafar. Alva Brenne's designs for the fortress were unused by Momin, who instead opted to used his own approach when designing the castle.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> The castle went through 9 different designs before it was finalized as "Fortress Vader."<ref name="Dark Lord 23">[[Darth Vader (2017) 23|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 23]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You get clients like this from time to time. They say, "Oh, don't worry, I'm not particular, I'm sure whatever you design will be perfect." Cut to ten thousand revisions later and they're ''still'' not satisfied. The ones who say they have no opinions always manage to find them in the end.|Brenne, on designing for Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />}}
Alva Brenne was a human female that had [[Color|light]] skin, blonde hair, and blue [[eye]]s. Brenne was highly regarded by Emperor Palpatine, who described her work as "very good." She was confident in her work as an architect, but had doubts that she would be able to design a fortress that would please Darth Vader.<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />

==Equipment==
As a Colonel in the [[Imperial Officer Corps]], Alva Brenne wore the standard olive [[Imperial military uniforms|uniform]], with a [[Rank insignia plaque]] displaying 4 red squares over 4 blue. On her mission to Mustafar, Brenne carried a [[SE-14r light repeating blaster]] holstered to her hip.<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />
