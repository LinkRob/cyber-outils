'''''Bounty Hunters'' 39''' is the thirty-ninth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Davide Tinto]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 4]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''SCOURGE OF THE CYBORG! &ndash; A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! Turned into a mindless killing machine, VALANCE is out to eliminate his former crew! Do BOSSK and ZUCKUSS stand a chance? Can even the mighty DURGE stop him? And TARR KLIGSON'S SECRET PLAN is finally revealed!''
