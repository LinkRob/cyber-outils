'''Evroes'ky'mormi''', [[core name]] '''Oeskym''',<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> was a [[Chiss]] male who served the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] as the [[weapons officer]] aboard the [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']], [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]]. During a skirmish with [[Nikardun]] resistance forces following the defeat of [[General]] [[Yiv]], [[Senior Captain]] [[Kiwu'tro'owmis]] ordered Oeskym to stand by [[spectrum laser]]s as the ''Vigilant'' approached a target.<ref name="Greater Good" />
