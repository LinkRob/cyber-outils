'''Dellso Prin''' was a [[Cingulon]] who worked as a used [[starship]] seller and [[Technician|mechanic]]. He was loosely affiliated with the [[Resistance]] during its [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]], and after the Resistance established a [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|base]] on the [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]], Prin hauled cargo and supplies to it. Prin was drawn to working with the Resistance by the promise of being able to work on legendary ships such as the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' and ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="TROS VD" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref> Prin was present in the base when [[Jedi]]-in-[[Jedi training|training]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] returned from running a training course in the nearby [[jungle]]. As Rey spoke to [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], Prin stood by an entry ramp into the ''Tantive IV'', holding a [[datapad]] and speaking with another Resistance member.<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> Prin had [[Color|white]], pebbly skin and black [[eye]]s and wore a green [[flight suit]] with a yellow [[jacket]] that had two white plates on the front. His outfit also included brown harnesses and a white [[helmet]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
