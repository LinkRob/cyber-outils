'''Exonium''' was a type of [[ore]] that could produce clean energy, found in vast deposits on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Rishi]]. Considered a precious resource, exonium was used by the planet's indigenous [[Rishii]] [[species]] to cook [[Foodstuff|food]] during Rishi's [[winter]] [[season]]. Due to the ore's properties, settlers were drawn to Rishi to [[Mining|mine]] and sell the ore, as exonium could be used to power various artifacts, including the [[insectoid]] [[Geonosian]] species' [[sonic weapon]]ry. During the [[galaxy]]-spanning [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Separatist Geonosians attempted to [[Battle on Rishi|conquer]] Rishi for its exonium deposits.<ref name="Mustafar and Other Planets" />

==Behind the scenes==
Exonium was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[De Agostini]]'s [[2022]] [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] booklet "[[Mustafar and Other Planets in the Outer Rim]]."<ref name="Mustafar and Other Planets" /> [[Exonium/Legends|Exonium]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in [[The Gauntlet of Death|''The Clone Wars: The Gauntlet of Death'']], a [[2009]] comic written by [[Henry Gilroy]].<ref name="Gauntlet of Death">{{StoryCite|story=The Gauntlet of Death|book=Free Comic Book Day 2009 Special|sformatted=''The Gauntlet of Death''|smanual=1}}</ref>
