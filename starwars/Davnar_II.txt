'''Davnar II''',<ref name="Lead" /> also known as '''Davnar''', was a [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> located in the [[Davnar system]], within the [[Kanchen sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> and [[the Interior]] regions,<ref name="Location" /> in grid square K-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Coordinates" /> The planet occupied the second [[orbit]]al position in its [[star system]]. It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[kalidor]], a [[Diet|predatory]] [[Bird|avian]] [[species]],<ref name="Lead" /> as well as [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

At some point, Throno left Davnar II to study [[Cartographer|cartography]] at the [[Graf Archive]] on the [[moon]] [[Orchis 2]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> after leaving his homeworld, Throno mentioned the planet in his [[book]] ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', which detailed the planet [[Batuu]] and also included a [[map]] depicting the location of Davnar II in the work.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Davnar II was introduced to the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] by the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]''.<ref name="Lead" /> The [[2020]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', written by [[Cole Horton]], introduced the alternate name "Davnar" for the planet.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> [[Davnar II/Legends|Davnar II]] was originally first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1979]] novel ''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]'', written by [[Brian Daley]],<ref name="Stars' End>''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]''</ref> and made its first appearance in the [[1998]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']].<ref name="Rebellion">[[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']]</ref>
