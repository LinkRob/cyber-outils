"'''Ewok Ambush'''" was a scenario for [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s [[Star Wars Miniatures]] in [[2006]] which was written by [[Sterling Hershey]]. It allows for the players to conduct the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor's]] ground portion.
