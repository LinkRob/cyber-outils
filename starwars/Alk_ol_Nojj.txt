'''Alk ol Nojj''' was a [[Phydolon]] who was the significant other of [[Ark ol Loor]] and was [[Death|killed]] by the [[Devaronian]] [[Gender|male]] [[bounty hunter]] known as the "[[Executioner (bounty hunter)|Executioner]]." Loor attempted to avenge Nojj's lives but was stopped by a [[Unidentified Force-sensitive human (Ark ol Loor)|Force-sensitive human]] and offered to teach Loor in the ways of [[the Force]] as Loor was also Force-sensitive.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
