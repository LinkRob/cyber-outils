In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] and [[journalist]] [[Rhil Dairo]] were '''ambushed''' by [[Nihil]] [[pirate|marauders]] led by [[General]] [[Abediah Viess|Viess]]. The Nihil killed [[Quith Meglar]], a Nihil defector who had agreed to help smuggle Dairo and Kriss out of the Nihil's [[Occlusion Zone]]. Kriss dueled Viess, who used her [[beskar]] armor to deflect Kriss's [[lightsaber]]. Eventually, Jedi Master [[Porter Engle]] arrived and joined the fight. Knowing she could not take down both Engle and Kriss, Viess retreated.<ref name="EoD" />
