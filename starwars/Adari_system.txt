The '''Adari system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Adari sector]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square M-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the home<ref name="Planetnamia" /> of the [[Adarian]] [[species]].<ref name="Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Adari system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Adari system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned indirectly in ''[[Galaxy Guide 12: Aliens — Enemies and Allies]]'', a [[1995]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="GG12">''[[Galaxy Guide 12: Aliens — Enemies and Allies]]''</ref> It was then identified as the Adari system in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', which was written by Fry and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
