The '''Blue Focaccia and Sausage Takeaway''' was a [[sandwich]] consisting of a [[Color|blue]] [[bread]] and a [[sausage]] topped with [[Red Fruit]] Jam.<ref name="Cruiser" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the dish was one of many that could be found in the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]] on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''. The [[Foodstuff|dish]] was safe for [[human]]s to consume.<ref name="Cruiser" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Focaccia and Sausage Takeaway was a dish available in the [[In-universe|real world]] at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> The dish was last served at the hotel during the [[September 28]][[September 29|&ndash;]][[September 30|30]] voyage of [[2023]], the final voyage before the closure of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser.<ref name="Official website">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref> The sandwich, which was served at lunch on the second day of the voyage, was made with [[Wikipedia:Focaccia|focaccia bread]] and served with "[[Wikipedia:Tomato jam|Red Fruit Jam]], [[Wikipedia:Ají (sauce)|Ají]] [[Wikipedia:Aioli|Aïoli]]."<ref name="DFB">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneyfoodblog.com/2022/02/25/full-review-were-eating-everything-for-lunch-at-the-star-wars-hotel-in-disney-world|text=FULL REVIEW: We're Eating EVERYTHING for Lunch at the Star Wars Hotel in Disney World|author=Madison Owens|date=2022-02-25|work=[https://www.disneyfoodblog.com Disney Food Blog]|archivedate=20240328133519}}</ref>
