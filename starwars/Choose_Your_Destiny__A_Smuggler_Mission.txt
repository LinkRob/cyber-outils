'''''Choose Your Destiny: A Smuggler Mission''''' is a [[canon]] audiobook written by [[Cavan Scott]] and part of the ''[[Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny]]'' series. The audiobook was released exclusively on [[Wikipedia:Amazon|Amazon]] FreeTime Unlimited in [[2019]]. As of June 2025, it is available with an Amazon Kids+ subscription.<ref name="Skill">{{Amazon|url=/Star-Wars-Choose-Your-Destiny/dp/B07LCZGLD7|text=Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny|archivedate=20240615023033}}</ref> It can be accessed by saying "Alexa, start Star Wars Choose Your Destiny" or "Alexa, open Star Wars Choose Your Destiny,"  then selecting it from the list of "Mini Missions".

There is one canon ending and there are 19 non-canon endings. The path to the canon ending is AAABBBCBAABA; there is only one path to the canon ending, taking 16 minutes to reach the ending. The total duration of all 41 story segments is approximately 34 minutes and there are 44 total paths possible.
