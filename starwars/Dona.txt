'''Dona''' was an individual who served as a [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="LEGO" /> At some point during the conflict between [[20 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="WarDate">The ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Padawan Lost]]" shows [[clone trooper]]s wearing [[Phase I clone trooper armor]], while the later episode "[[Water War]]" shows clones wearing [[Phase II clone trooper armor]]. As both of these episodes are dated to [[20 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', the switch to Phase II armor must have taken place during 20 BBY. Since clone trooper [[Lock Down]] is shown wearing Phase II armor, the events of the comic must take place sometime after the switch and before the end of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]].</ref> she visited a Republic base on the [[planet]] [[Garasu]]. While there, the base was attacked by [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]]. Overrun by [[battle droid]]s, Dona escaped from the base and fled across the planet's [[Stone|rocky]] terrain. During her evacuation, a [[Imperial shock trooper|clone shock trooper]] [[squad leader]] ordered another [[trooper]], [[Lock Down]], to protect the senator by providing cover fire.<ref name="LEGO" />
