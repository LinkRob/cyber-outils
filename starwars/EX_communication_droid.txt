'''EX communication droids''', also known as '''EX droids''', '''Ex-droids''', '''Ee-Ex comm droids''', or '''Eeex''', were a model of [[communications droid]] used during the [[High Republic Era]] to communicate across [[the galaxy]]. One such [[droid]] was used by a [[Republic Pathfinder]] team that discovered treasure; however, when the team was all killed during a [[pirate]] raid, the droid's whereabouts were unknown for [[Standard century|centuries]] afterward until it surfaced again in the possession of a [[Servit|descendant]] of a [[Servit's ancestor|member]] of the expedition, becoming sought-after as it pointed to the treasure.

==Description==
{{Quote|Anyway, this is all we have left of her. A rusty old droid. It's been passed down through the family for generations like some sort of heirloom.|Servit|<ref name="Inheritance" />}}
EX droids were [[communications droid]]s with [[Color|beige]] plating along with blue- and copper-colored plating at their tops, disc-shaped chassis, and a [[photoreceptor]] on top.<ref name="SWCA" /> The [[droid]]s had several manipulator [[Limb|arms]] attached to a spindle<ref name="Inheritance" /> that could be used while they hovered as well as an [[antenna]].<ref name="SWCA" />

==Role==
{{Quote|The teams would travel out to visit worlds in the Outer Rim, then send these droids back carrying messages and coordinates to communications teams.|Dexter Jettster, to Servit|<ref name="Inheritance" />}}
The droids were used during the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="SWCA" /> by [[communications team]]s<ref name="Inheritance" /> to communicate across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="SWCA" /> Specifically utilized by teams of [[Republic Pathfinder]]s, who would travel to the [[Outer Rim Territories]], the droids would be sent back with coordinates of Outer Rim [[Planet|worlds]].

==History==
{{Quote|The story goes that Sturm's team uncovered a great treasure on an arid moon somewhere out on the frontier. A moon called Shara'Tam. The Pathfinders managed to dispatch their Ee-Ex droid with a message, informing their comms team of the prize and its location&hellip; but they were attacked by raiders and killed.|Dexter Jettster, to Servit|<ref name="Inheritance" />}}
[[File:Dex-Inheritance.png|thumb|right|270px|Dexter Jettster (pictured) taught Servit about the EX droids' history.]]
One Ee-Ex droid was used during a Pathfinder [[Outer Rim expedition oh-four-seven|expedition]], with the [[Jedi]] [[Sturm Umbrik]] recording a message to send to an individual named [[Janssen]] regarding the expedition's discovery of treasure. However, they were subsequently all [[Death|killed]] in a [[pirate]] attack, and the droid ended up as an heirloom, being passed down in the family of an [[Servit's ancestor|individual]] on the expedition.<ref name="Inheritance" />

During the [[Republic Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="Era">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-insider-galactic-tales|text=''Star Wars Insider'' to Celebrate 20th Anniversary of ''Star Wars: Attack of the Clones'' with "Galactic Tales"}} establishes that the ''[[Galactic Tales]]'' series, which includes "[[Galactic Tales: Inheritance|Inheritance]]," takes place at the same time as the [[prequel trilogy]], thereby placing it in the [[Republic Era]] per ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> a descendant of the individual, the [[Mirialan]] [[Servit]], tried to figure out if the old EX droid was worth anything. [[Gender|He]] accidentally revealed it to the [[Weequay]] [[Jarl]], who, knowing the story of Umbrik, tried to take it for himself. Servit ran off with the droid and ended up at [[Dex's Diner]], where he had his friend [[Dexter Jettster]], the owner of the [[Restaurant|eatery]], take a look at it. Jettster, who also knew of Umbrik's tale, explained its value to Servit and helped him fend off Jarl and [[Jarl's crew|his crew]] when they caught up to the Mirialan. After a short skirmish, they all listened to Umbrik's [[Holorecorder|holorecording]], and once Jettster tricked Jarl into thinking that the treasure had been destroyed, Servit set off to follow the coordinates provided by the Ee-Ex droid and find the treasure for himself.<ref name="Inheritance" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Those droids are ''really'' important.|Cavan Scott|<ref name="SWCA" />}}
EX droids were created for Phase II of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia series, revealed in a ''High Republic'' panel at [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim]] on [[May 26]], [[2022]]. According to author [[Cavan Scott]], [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Pablo Hidalgo]] suggested a "[[Wikipedia:Pony Express|Pony Express]] droid,"<ref name="SWCA" /> referencing the nineteenth-century pony-drawn mail system,<ref name="Pony">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dictionary.com/browse/pony-express|text=Pony express Definition & Meaning|archivedate=20201128075419}}</ref> which led to the droids' creation. Scott also compared the droids to how drama writers try to break cellphones early on so that they do not present an easy way out of a situation&mdash;faraway individuals would not be able to rely on high-speed communication.<ref name="SWCA" />

Prior to their ''High Republic'' debut, EX droids first appeared in "[[Galactic Tales: Inheritance]]," an installment of the ''[[Galactic Tales]]'' series of short stories. The story was written by [[George Mann]] and was published in the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine's [[Star Wars Insider 211|211th issue]]<ref name="Inheritance" /> on [[June 7]], [[2022]].<ref name="Date">{{TitanComics|url=m/102-star-wars-insider-211|text=Star Wars Insider #211}}</ref>
