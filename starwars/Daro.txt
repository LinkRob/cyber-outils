'''Daro''' was a terrestrial [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Ojoster sector]]. It was covered primarily in [[forest]]s that enveloped its [[mountain]]ous terrain, and the available [[The galaxy|galactic]] information on Daro implied that the planet was uninhabited, housing no settlements or installations. Daro was therefore perfect for housing secrets, and in [[19 BBY]], the [[Galactic Empire]] occupied the planet and established a top-secret [[Imperial Base on Daro|military base]] inside a mountain as part of [[Project War-Mantle]], aiming to replace its [[clone trooper]] [[army]] with conscripted [[soldier]]s. The facility was populated by a team of fifty [[clone commando]]s who trained the Empire's next generation of Imperial [[trooper]]s, the [[TK stormtrooper]].

Early into training his recruits, the [[Cloning|clone]] [[commando]] [[Gregor]] attempted to leave the base and [[Desertion|desert]] the Empire. While he was caught and [[Prisoner|imprisoned]], he planted a [[distress beacon]] outside the facility, and its signal reached the former [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]]. Unable to retrieve Gregor himself, Rex instead [[Extraction on Daro|sent]] the rogue commandos of [[Clone Force 99]] to Daro. When the [[squad]] arrived, they discovered and entered the Empire's facility, freeing Gregor from [[prison]]. The clones were detected by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]], with squads of [[stormtrooper]]s and commandos trying to stop them. The team eventually departed Daro with the exception of their leader, [[Hunter]], who was captured by the Empire.

==Description==
{{Quote|<nowiki />''[&hellip;]'' Daro, a terrestrial planet in the Outer Rim with no known settlements or installations.|Tech|<ref name="War-Mantle" />}}
[[File:DaroLandscape-WM.png|thumb|left|325px|Daro's verdant and mountainous landscape]]
Daro was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="War-Mantle" /> located in the [[Daro system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[New Territories]] portion<ref name="NewTerritories" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Ojoster sector]], situated in grid-square N-6 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Featuring a Type I [[atmosphere]]<ref name="Atmosphere" /> that was breathable to [[human]]s, Daro<ref name="War-Mantle" /> [[orbit]]ed a [[star]].<ref name="Audio" /> The planet's surface was covered in vast<ref name="War-Mantle" /> verdant<ref name="Audio" /> [[forest]]s<ref name="War-Mantle" /> and [[jungle]]s<ref name="Audio" /> of [[pine]] [[tree]]s, [[Fern (plant)|fern]]s, and other [[plant]]s, which were home to [[Carrier butterfly|carrier butterflies]] and [[massiff]]s. The thousands of trees engulfed Daro's landscape,<ref name="War-Mantle" /> which was peppered<ref name="Audio" /> with towering rocky [[mountain]]s<ref name="War-Mantle" /> and buttes,<ref name="Concept Art" /> the cliffs of which were in turn enveloped by the trees' expansive roots.<ref name="War-Mantle" /> The planet featured snow-covered portions at its topmost regions as well as numerous bodies of [[water]] of variable sizes that were scattered throughout the forested areas.<ref name="Concept Art" />

==History==
===Daro's secret===
{{Quote|I was sent here with other commandos&hellip;and quickly realized I wanted out.|Gregor, on his assignment on Daro|<ref name="War-Mantle" />}}
[[File:GregorOnTheRun-WM.png|thumb|right|325px|Gregor attempted to leave Imperial training on Daro.]]
In the early [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]]<ref name="War-Mantle" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the planet Daro was officially classified by [[The galaxy|galactic]] files as uninhabited, with no recorded installations or settlements. Since the data on Daro made it suitable for secret operations, the Empire occupied the planet and established a top-secret [[Imperial Base on Daro|military base]] inside one of its mountains, in the process fortifying it to nearly unimpregnable levels. The facility was used as part of a high-priority training program codenamed [[Project War-Mantle]], aimed at replacing and retiring the [[Galactic Republic]]'s grandfathered [[clone trooper]] [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]] with a more cost-effective [[Imperial Military|military]] of conscripted [[soldier]]s brought in from across the [[galaxy]]. To carry out the strategy, a contingent of fifty [[clone commando]]s<ref name="War-Mantle" /> and [[Clone trooper officer|clone officers]]<ref name="Officers">{{EncyclopediaCite|Imperial Officers and Agents}}</ref> was sent to Daro to train the first one thousand recruits, destined to become the next generation of Imperial soldiers&mdash;the [[TK stormtrooper]].<ref name="War-Mantle" />

Shortly into training, the Imperial [[Cloning|clone]] [[commando]] [[Gregor|CC-5576]], named Gregor, attempted to [[Desertion|desert]]. After breaking out of the base, Gregor was chased by [[stormtrooper]]s who used massiffs to track him. In the [[Local day|nighttime]] pursuit, the commando was incapacitated by the Imperials and dragged away into a [[shuttle]], to be summarily [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] at the facility. Before being taken away, Gregor planted a [[distress beacon]] that relayed a [[Signal beacon|signal]] to the former [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], who had been leading a [[Clone Underground|resistance network]] against the Imperial regime. Occupied by more pressing matters, Rex contacted the rogue clone commandos of [[Clone Force 99]], nicknamed the Bad Batch, [[Extraction on Daro|tasking]] them with taking<ref name="War-Mantle" /> his<ref name="Timelines" /> place in rescuing his old friend. The former [[captain]] provided the Bad Batch, aboard their [[Omicron-class attack shuttle|shuttle]], the [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Marauder'']], with the signal beacon before signing off. The group pinpointed the beacon's location on Daro and briefly argued about whether to carry out the mission due to the limited information, with Daro reported to be uninhabited. The clones ultimately decided to go to the planet.<ref name="War-Mantle" />

===A "simple" extraction===
{{Quote|We're banking a lot on a clone we don't even know.|Hunter, to Clone Force 99 on rescuing Gregor on Daro|<ref name="War-Mantle" />}}
[[File:DaroTrek-WM.png|thumb|left|325px|Clone Force 99 took Rex's place in rescuing Gregor.]]
The ''Marauder'' subsequently exited [[hyperspace]] and descended into Daro's atmosphere, flying low over the surface to avoid any potential [[Sensor|tracking scanners]]. Landing in the planet's forests, the Bad Batch disembarked from the shuttle and began searching for the beacon, with the young<ref name="War-Mantle" /> [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Timelines" /> clone [[Omega]] eventually finding it while [[Wrecker]] wondered where the missing [[trooper]] was. Using his tracking capabilities, the [[squad]]'s leader, [[Hunter]], surveyed the area and discovered the trooper had been taken captive after a pursuit, heading in the direction the latter had been dragged away in. As the squad followed him, Hunter further used his enhanced senses and realized something had been built into the mountain, leaving the clones confused due to Daro supposedly being uninhabited. Since their scanners were being [[Jammer|jammed]], Omega and Wrecker were ordered back to the ''Marauder'' while Hunter, [[Tech]], and [[Echo]] proceeded to scale the mountain, where they discovered the Imperial base.<ref name="War-Mantle" />

Moving in closer, the squad spotted a number of clone commandos leading groups of what they believed were clone troopers with [[TK stormtrooper armor|updated armor]]. Hunter nearly called off the mission as the Bad Batch was unsure whether CC-5576 was even alive and they would have to infiltrate the base without information or reinforcements. However, he recalled how they had [[Mission to Skako Minor (Clone Wars)|rescued]] Echo in a similar manner before. The squad then entered the base via [[turbolift]] and lost communication with their shuttle, sneaking through corridors to avoid Imperial troopers. They reached a control terminal, where Echo breached the new Imperial encryption and found the muster report listing the base's personnel. Further probing the base's network, Echo was able to locate CC-5576 at a [[Prison|holding cell]] in [[Cellblock 25]], four levels down from the infiltrators' position.<ref name="War-Mantle" />

[[File:ExtractionofGregor-WM.png|thumb|right|325px|Clone Force 99 attempting to leave the Imperial base with Gregor]]
The Bad Batch entered the cell block and knocked the on-duty guard unconscious, proceeding to deactivate the [[Ray shield|ray-shielded]] entrance to the cell and then return with CC-5576&mdash;learning his name in the process&mdash;to the turbolift. In an attempt to avoid other Imperial squads that<ref name="War-Mantle" /> he<ref name="Audio" /> discovered were gathering for an inspection, Tech staged a diversion to redirect them; he issued a [[Code-16]] across the facility from a central [[computer]], but since Republic codes no longer worked, it also triggered a security alert that drew stormtroopers to their location. Chased across the [[Level six|sixth level]], the Bad Batch incapacitated several stormtroopers and realized their attackers were non-clone soldiers, who Gregor explained had been recruited to replace the clone [[army]].<ref name="War-Mantle" />

===A botched escape===
{{Quote|Hunter, tell them to come back! Order them to come back!''"<br />"''Sorry, kid. I can't do that.|Omega, wanting the Bad Batch to rescue Hunter|<ref name="War-Mantle" />}}
[[File:HunterOutnumbered-WM.png|thumb|left|325px|Hunter was taken prisoner by the Galactic Empire in the rescue.]]
Fleeing more Imperial units, the Bad Batch traded [[blaster]] fire with their pursuers until they entered the turbolift. While heading up to the main level, Gregor explained that the Empire wanted to replace the clone troopers of the Republic with an endless supply of less effective conscripted soldiers. At the topmost level, the turbolift exposed the Bad Batch to another squad of stormtroopers that was led by the clone commando [[Scorch]], forcing them to retreat downward and take an alternate route while being chased by Scorch's squad. Scorch briefly stopped the Bad Batch as<ref name="War-Mantle" /> he<ref name="Audio" /> blasted Gregor before being [[stun]]ned unconscious himself. Met by yet another squad, the Bad Batch fled into a control room that they realized was connected to the outside via [[reactor]] vents, and the clones proceeded to the [[Thermal exhaust port|exhaust port]] where they could contact the ''Marauder'' for an extraction.<ref name="War-Mantle" />

At the exhaust port, the squad hailed Omega and Wrecker to be retrieved. While the infiltrators waited for the shuttle, a group of stormtroopers breached the control room and notified Imperial forces of their target's location, pursuing them as [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] [[gunship]]s and [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|V-wing starfighters]] were mobilized to intercept. Wrecker provided cover fire from aboard the ''Marauder'' to hold off the stormtroopers, giving Gregor and Tech [[time]] to board the shuttle, with the latter taking the ship's controls as aerial forces descended upon them.<ref name="War-Mantle" />

[[File:EnterCrosshair-WM.png|thumb|right|325px|Crosshair comes to Daro for Hunter.]]
Tech then steered the ''Marauder'' away in order to draw the Imperial [[starfighter]]s away from the mountain, leaving Hunter and Echo to fend off the stormtroopers and engage a clone commando in [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]] that sent the latter off the cliff. The shuttle dispatched multiple Imperial fighters in pursuit but suffered heavy damage to its [[engine]]s and began losing power and its [[deflector shield]]s. Eliminating the other fighters, the ''Marauder'' pulled back to the exhaust port, where Echo boarded the ship, but Hunter slipped and fell into the forests below after the shuttle shook from the blaster fire of another starfighter unit. After Imperial troopers took Hunter prisoner, he ordered the Bad Batch to depart Daro for hyperspace, which the group initially refused. Hunter was then brought inside the Imperial facility. Not long afterward, the Imperial [[Clone Commander]] and former Bad Batch member [[Crosshair]] received word of the capture and traveled to Daro. Although he had hoped that the whole squad had been captured,<ref name="War-Mantle" /> Crosshair nevertheless took Hunter into his custody as part of a plan to lure the other clones.<ref name="Return">{{TBB|Return to Kamino}}</ref> During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="18Date">{{TBBDate|306}}</ref> Rex brought the [[Clone X trooper]] [[CX-1]] to his network's [[Central command area|base]] in the [[B'omarr Order Monastery]] on<ref name="Infiltration">{{TBB|Infiltration (episode)}}</ref> the planet<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Teth]] and questioned the latter on where other troopers of his [[Clone X trooper division|type]] was based, including whether it was on Daro.<ref name="Infiltration" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|The muster report listed 50 clone commandos and 1,000 TK troopers.|Echo|<ref name="War-Mantle" />}}
Daro was inhabited by humans. The Galactic Empire's military facility on the planet was populated by a force of fifty clone commandos and one thousand non-clone soldiers,<ref name="War-Mantle" /> as well as other clone [[officer]]s.<ref name="Officers" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|There's something here, inside that mountain.|Hunter|<ref name="War-Mantle" />}}
While Daro was officially listed as uninhabited, the Galactic Empire established a secret military training facility inside one of its mountains early into its reign. The mountain's natural composition significantly fortified the base and made it difficult to escape, with the facility only having two exits: one upward and one through the reactor exhaust port.<ref name="War-Mantle" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DaroButteConcept.png|thumb|right|270px|Concept art of Daro's surface]]
Daro was introduced in "[[War-Mantle]],"<ref name="War-Mantle" /> the fourteenth episode of the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' animated series' [[The Bad Batch Season 1|first season]], which was written by [[Damani Johnson]] and aired on [[Disney+]] on [[July 30]], [[2021]], serving as the episode's primary setting.<ref name="Companion">{{InsiderCite|206|Star Wars: The Bad Batch Season One Companion|''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season One Companion}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Animation]]'s [[Jim Moore]] made concept art of Daro's full planetary overview titled "planetballDaro," as well as three concept illustrations of mountain buttes, dated to [[May 5]], [[2020]]. Fellow [[Lucasfilm]] Animation artists [[Jason Pichon]], [[Matthew Mossman]], and [[Colas Gauthier]] created concept illustrations of the buttes and forests of the planet in addition to the mountain butte base, respectively dated to [[May 4]] and [[May 6]] of the same year.<ref name="Concept Art" />
