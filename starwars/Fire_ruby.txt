'''Fire rubies''' were a type of [[gemstone]] which could be extremely valuable during the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s reign.<ref name="ERJ" /> In [[Lothal]] Year 3272,<ref name="RVG">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> the street urchin and pickpocket [[Ezra Bridger]] stole a fire ruby in the [[Imperial pavilion]] district of the [[planet]] Lothal's [[Capital City]]. Bridger later wrote about the haul in his [[Rebel Journal|journal]], in which he commented that the gem was probably fake.<ref name="ERJ" />

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fire rubies were first mentioned in ''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and released in [[2014]].
