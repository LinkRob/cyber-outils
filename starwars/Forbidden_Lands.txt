The '''Forbidden Lands''' was the name that the [[Zixon]] gave to the area where the [[Galactic Empire]] had set up on their [[homeworld]] of [[Minfar]]. The Empire constructed research facilities on Minfar, where they created the [[Echo Horn]], a device that could control the Zixon.<ref name="Spark of the Resistance" />
