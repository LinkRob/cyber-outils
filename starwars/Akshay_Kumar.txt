'''Akshay Kumar''' (not to be confused with his [[Wikipedia:Akshay Kumar|Bollywood namesake]]) is a British actor who plays [[Rumitar Shay]]<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|Akshay04Kumar|status/946669034916995073|Akshay Kumar|quote=If, like me, y'all have been wondering just how Finn and Rose sneaked passed the bad guy's {{C|sic}} security systems so easily...BLAME THIS GUY! 👇👇👇 What an idiot. 🤦🏻‍♂️|archiveurl=https://archive.is/0INKj}}</ref> (credited as Mega-Destroyer First Order Monitor) in the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']]. He is best known for playing hotel barman [[W:c:TheHalcyon:Adil Joshi|Adil Joshi]] in the British period drama ''[[Wikipedia:The Halcyon|The Halcyon]]'' and medical student [[W:c:Homeland:Aayan Ibrahim|Aayan Ibrahim]] on the fourth season of the spy television series [[Wikipedia:Homeland (TV series)|''Homeland'']].<ref name="Mandy" />
