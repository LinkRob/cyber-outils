'''Drums''' were a type of percussive musical instrument. [[Ak-rev]] and [[Umpass-stay]] were two of the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]'s [[bodyguard]]s who played drums in the [[Max Rebo Band]]. The [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Ewok]] [[species]] of the [[moon]] [[Endor]] used drums covered in animal skins<ref name="AEYNTK" /> and played them to celebrate the destruction of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> Drums were also used on [[Akiva]].<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>
