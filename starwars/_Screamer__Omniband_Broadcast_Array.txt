The '''"Screamer" Omniband Broadcast Array''' was a model of [[sensor]] [[baffler]] manufactured by [[Fabritech]]. It cost 8,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and used a combination of broad-spectrum transmissions and low-power targeted [[ion pulse]]s to confuse enemy [[sensor]]s.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Screamer" Omniband Broadcast Array was first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref>
