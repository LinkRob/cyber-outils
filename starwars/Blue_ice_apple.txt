'''Blue ice apples''' were a type of [[Color|blue]] [[apple]] [[fruit]] [[Diet|edible]] to [[Yoda's species|Yaddle's species]] and [[Arkanian]]s.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Cippa Tarko]] [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|move]] one of several blue ice apples and swiftly split it into four equal sections. Tarko then floated one piece toward the [[Jedi Master]] [[Yaddle]]&mdash;her assigned [[Teacher|instructor]]&mdash;though the latter waved the portion away and told several individuals via [[hologram]], who had seen their interaction, about the [[youngling]]'s tendency to show off.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Behind the scenes==
