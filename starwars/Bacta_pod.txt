A '''bacta pod''' was a long, cylindrical [[Bacta tank|capsule]] made to hold [[bacta]], in which a being could be submerged. The pod had a [[Viewport|window]] that revealed the inside of the pod and could be opened using a control panel inside the pod.<ref name="Stranger" /> On [[Tatooine]], [[Boba Fett]] bathed in his bacta pod at many points in time.<ref name="Stranger" /><ref name="Tribes">{{BOBF|Chapter 2: The Tribes of Tatooine}}</ref><ref name="Streets">{{BOBF|Chapter 3: The Streets of Mos Espa}}</ref> He used it to heal his burned scars.<ref name="The Gathering Storm">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref><ref name="Chapter 4 audio description">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> After the [[Battle of Mos Espa|battle for Mos Espa]] Fett's bacta pod was used by [[Cobb Vanth]], after he was [[Showdown in Freetown|shot]] by [[Cad Bane]], while [[The Modifier]] was taking care of him.<ref name="Name of Honor">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref>
