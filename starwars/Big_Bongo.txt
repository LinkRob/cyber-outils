{{Quote|But look at you. You brought in our biggest catch ever.|Gregor to Garazeb Orrelios upon catching the Big Bongo|<ref name="The Lost Commanders" />}}
'''Big Bongo''' was a giant [[joopa]] that lived under the crust of [[Seelos]],<ref name="The Lost Commanders" />  a [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Epic Battles">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Visual Guide: Epic Battles]]''</ref> Following his retirement, the [[human]] [[Cloning|clone]] [[Gregor]], former soldier of [[Galactic Republic]], came to live on Seelos aboard a [[Rex's AT-TE|modified]] [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer|AT-TE]] [[walker]] with two of his comrades, [[Rex]] and [[Wolffe]]. Over time, Gregor became obsessed with catching Big Bongo. When [[Spectres|the Spectres]] arrived on Seelos, Gregor convinced one of them, [[Garazeb Orrelios]], to serve as bait for the giant joopa, who was thought to enjoy the taste of [[Lasat]] meat. The Big Bongo ambushed Orrelios, causing cracks in the surface under the Lasat's [[Limb|feet]],<ref name="The Lost Commanders" /> before wrapping his slimy [[tongue]] around Orrelios. Big Bongo then then yanked his prey under the surface and into his belly.<ref name="Epic Battles" />

Two other members of the Spectres, the [[Jedi]]-in-hiding [[Kanan Jarrus]] and his apprentice [[Ezra Bridger]], used [[Joopa prod]]s to send bolts of energy along a laser line under the surface. When Big Bongo finally surfaced,<ref name="The Lost Commanders" /> with the regulator [[laser]] line leading into his gut,<ref name="Epic Battles" /> Rex [[Death|killed]] him with a direct hit from the [[Firefont 04 mass driver cannon|main cannon]] of the AT-TE. After Big Bongo's death, Gregor helped Orrelios exit Big Bongo's mouth. When Orrelios chastised Gregor for his near-death, Gregor countered, and reminded the Lasat that he brought in Big Bongo, who was their biggest catch ever.<ref name="The Lost Commanders" /> The clones carried the creature's carcass on the side of their walker for a time. However, when the [[Galactic Empire]] launched an [[Assault on Seelos|assault]] against them, Gregor and his comrades were forced to abandon it for the sake of greater mobility.<ref name="Relics">{{Rebels|Relics of the Old Republic}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Big Bongo had [[Color|gray]] and green skin,<ref name="The Lost Commanders" /> and his crown was covered in bright red [[eye]]s. He had a long slimy tongue<ref name="Epic Battles" /> that could extend beyond his mouth.<ref name="The Lost Commanders" /> Big Bongo also had hooked mouthparts, but no actual teeth, so he swallowed his prey, including people, whole.<ref name="Epic Battles" />
