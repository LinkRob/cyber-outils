'''Bakk and Zico''' was an [[alien]] who hosted a broadcast of the final [[Podracing|podrace]] of [[The Rawani Cup]] from the [[city]] of [[Baroonda (city)|Baroonda]]. The broadcast was played on radio station [[BSO 401.72]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="GEStaff" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bakk and Zico was created for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at Disneyland Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]]. The alien is not present in the park, but their voice can be heard in certain areas.<ref name="GEStaff" />
