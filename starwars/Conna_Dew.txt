'''Conna Dew''' was a [[human]] [[Elder (Polyneus)|Elder]] from the [[planet]] [[Polyneus]]. She was an Elder of [[Cliff]], the birthplace of [[New Republic]] [[pilot]] [[Wyl Lark]]. Lark had called Dew his friend since he'd been fourteen years old, ever since she had caught and scolded Lark for spying on a stranger's [[Marriage|wedding]] feast. Some time after the [[Battle of Jakku]] but before [[11 ABY]]<ref name="Date">This event takes place between the [[Battle of Jakku]] in [[5 ABY]] and the reunion of [[Wyl Lark]], [[Chass na Chadic]] and [[Yrica Quell]] six [[Standard year|years]] after the war in [[11 ABY]]. Therefore, Lark's meeting with [[Conna Dew]] has to be between 5 ABY and 11 ABY.</ref>, Lark visited Dew when he had journeyed to Cliff to consult with a [[Sun-Lamas|Sun-Lama]] about treating a [[sur-avka]]'s mangled [[claw]]. He helped her yank desiccated husks of dead creeper vines off her house as she told Lark that her old caretaker had decided to become a Sun-Lama at the age of ninety-nine. After working and talking for a while, Dew told Lark that the [[Hik'e-Matriarch]] is looking for a [[senator]] to represent Polyneus in the [[New Republic Senate]] and suggested Lark as the best candidate. Shortly thereafter, Lark did become senator of Polyneus.<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>
