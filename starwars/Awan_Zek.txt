'''Awan Zek''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[bounty hunter]] who lived in [[the galaxy]]. Awan was also known to work with two [[Rodian]] associates. He tried to settle scores with the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] but Hondo managed to escape with the help of the [[Scavenger|scavenging]] family known as the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]]. After Hondo tried to steal the Freemakers' ship ''[[StarScavenger]]'', the Freemakers handed Hondo back to Awan and his associates.

==Biography==
===Chasing Han Solo and Chewbacca===
[[File:AwanZe-All-Stars.jpg|thumb|left|209px|Awan Zek and his Rodian associates captured Han and Chewbacca on the Ring of Kafrene.]]
At some point before the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] owed Awan a [[Durasteel-transmuter coil]]. After chasing Han and Chewbacca around the [[Ring of Kafrene]], Awan and his [[Rodian]] associates caught and threatened Solo and Chewbacca to give them a new coil. Eventually Solo and Chewbacca were able to find the coil through the help of Han's old friend [[Pace Freemaker/LEGO|Pace Freemaker]].<ref name="Dealing with Lando; Han and Chewie Strike Back">{{All-Stars|Dealing with Lando / Han and Chewie Strike Back}}</ref>

===Cheated by Hondo Ohnaka===
At some point later following the [[quest for the Kyber Saber Crystals]], Awan was cheated by the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] in a game of [[sabacc]]. Hondo managed to convince the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] to give him safe passage offworld by offering to help them find a Corellian Defender on the planet [[Taul]]. [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] needed to obtain a proton suspension housing from the Defender in order to build a starship called the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']].<ref name="The Storms of Taul" />

Before Hondo could leave with the Freemakers, Awan Zek confronted him for cheating him. Hondo protested his innocence but dropped the cards, proving his guilt. Rowan managed to use his [[The Force|Force powers]] to hurl a table at Awan and his two [[Rodian]] associates. Awan and his associates gave chase but Rowan used the Force to slam the door shut. When Zek chided his associates for letting the Freemakers escape, the orange Rodian recognized Rowan and recalled that the [[Galactic Empire]] had placed a bounty on the boy's head. Awan then contacted an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] and informed them that Rowan was heading to planet [[Taul]]. The Imperial [[Hunter droid/LEGO|hunter droid]] [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]] picked up the message and traveled to Taul.<ref name="The Storms of Taul" />

The Freemakers managed to obtain the proton suspension housing using Hondo's information. However, Hondo abandoned them and tried to steal their ship, the ''[[StarScavenger]]''. Hondo was eventually tackled by their [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]] and locked inside a sealed canister. After rescuing the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Major]] [[Bren Derlin]], Roger handed Hondo in a box to Awan and his associates. Hondo tried to humor Zek and his associates with a game of sabacc but they ignored his offer.<ref name="The Storms of Taul" />

==Personality and traits==
Awan Zek was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] bounty hunter with a pockmarked face. He wanted to settle scores with Hondo Ohnaka for cheating him in a game of sabacc and was known to work with two Rodian associates.<ref name="The Storms of Taul" />

==Behind the scenes==
Awan Zek first appeared in the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season Two|Season Two]] episode "[[The Storms of Taul]]," which premiered on [[August 2]], [[2017]]. He appears to be physically based on [[Cornelius Evazan]].
