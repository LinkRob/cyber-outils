'''Chepatite impact explosives''' were an explosive substance utilized in [[explosive micro-rocket]]s. The [[Missile|rockets]] were reliant on the explosives, which provided them with their powerful kinetic force.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chepatite impact explosives were mentioned in the [[Canon]] continuity in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' tabletop game.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> They were originally introduced in "[[Tinian on Trial]]," a short story in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 4|fourth issue]]&mdash;released in [[November]] of [[1994]]&mdash;of ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'', a supplemental magazine for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="TinianOnTrial">{{JournalCite|4|Tinian on Trial}}</ref>
