The '''Bantha Brothers''' was an outfit owned by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Salvagers|standard costume]]. This garb featured [[robe]]s with a colorful design including [[Color|yellow]], green, purple, tan, and brown patterns, as well as crisscrossed [[bandolier]]s and a green satchel. A large [[bantha]] [[Bone|horn]] and a patch of [[Hair|fur]] was suspended to the back of the upper Jawa via green cords.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bantha Brothers was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Utooni, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.18.0 during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Bantha Set]].<ref name="Hunters" />
