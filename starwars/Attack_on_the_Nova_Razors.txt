During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a warrior '''attacked''' the [[Nova Razors]], the most ruthless gang on the [[planet]] [[Bestine IV]], at their headquarters in the [[Blue Garden Homes]] housing tenement in the [[Bsellis City market district|market district]] of Bestine's [[Bsellis City]]. The warrior defeated many thugs floor to floor and freed those they had enslaved to refine their [[spice]]. They then found the [[Unidentified Twi'lek gang leader|Twi'lek gang leader]] and his [[lieutenant]]s, going on to defeat the leader too and find out about the gang's protection deal with [[Bestine Security Force]] [[Sergeant]] [[Mey Kilrow]].<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
