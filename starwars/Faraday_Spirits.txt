'''Faraday Spirits''' was a beverage distribution company operated by the [[Faraday family]]. The [[Chandrilan]] [[Lady]] [[Lu-reen Faraday]] was an [[Gender|heiress]] of the company by [[382 BBY]]. At some point, a shipment of [[Chandrilan whiskey]] of the company's befell a shipping accident, resulting in not many bottles of the beverage surviving.

Upon first meeting [[Axel Greylark]]&mdash;the [[Greylark family|son]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Kyong Greylark]]&mdash;she introduced herself with Faraday Spirits' slogan, and the pair became romantically involved. However, when they later broke up in 382 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], Axel took Faraday's gift of some Chandrilan [[whiskey]] and drank it in a [[Raik's Parlor|gambling den]], briefly thinking about her and how she had first introduced herself.

During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Chandrilan]] [[Lady]] [[Lu-reen Faraday]]&mdash;the [[Gender|daughter]] of a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Lu-reen Faraday's father|senator]]&mdash;was an heiress of her [[Faraday family|family's]] company, and the organization bore her family name. At some point, Faraday met [[Axel Greylark]]&mdash;the [[Greylark family|son]] of the Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Kyong Greylark]]&mdash;and introduced herself with the Faraday Spirits' slogan. The pair eventually became romantically involved.<ref name="Convergence" />

One [[time]], the company [[transport]]ed a shipment of [[Chandrilan whiskey]], but an accident in transit resulted in much of the batch being destroyed, with only three hundred bottles left<ref name="Convergence" /> by [[382 BBY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Faraday gifted a bottle of the now-rare [[whiskey]]&mdash;which was worth a thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] a bottle&mdash;to Axel during a visit. However, he broke up with her shortly afterward and drank the beverage from his [[Axel Greylark's flask|flask]] while in the [[gambling]] den [[Raik's Parlor]] on<ref name="Convergence" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]], attempting to avoid the news [[HoloNet|holos]] reporting about their breakup. As Axel conversed with others about the drink in his [[flask]], he thought about his former paramour and her relation to Faraday Spirits, including how Faraday had first introduced herself using the company's slogan, followed by her [[Parent|father's]] title of [[senator]].<ref name="Convergence" />
