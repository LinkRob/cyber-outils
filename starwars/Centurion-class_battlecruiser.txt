The '''''Centurion''-class battlecruiser''' was a class of [[battlecruiser]]; at least one ship of this class was active during the [[New Republic Era]]. [[Zeva Bliss]], head of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] in [[18 ABY]], used one such [[Zeva Bliss's Centurion-class battlecruiser|vessel]] as her flagship. The crime leader used it to transport her daughter [[Zorii Bliss]] and [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] back to [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Free Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
A ''Centurion''-class battlecruiser first appeared in [[canon]] in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]''.<ref name="Free Fall" /> The class originated in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]''.<ref name="Kotor II">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]''</ref>

[[File:CenturionSketchfab.png|thumb|250px|right|A 3D ''Centurion'' model as designed by user [https://sketchfab.com/Kharak Kharak] on Sketchfab.]]

The appearance of the ''Centurion'' in [[Battle of Jakku — Last Stand 1|''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand'' 1]] appears to be based on a fan-created model<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sith-centurion-battlecruiser-3f2934da83cb421c87a3510ce62ceb8d|text=Sith Centurion Battlecruiser|work=[https://sketchfab.com Sketchfab]|author=Kharak|date=2022-10-28|archivedate=20241217194201}}</ref>, according to the user who commissioned the model to be created.<ref name="TracingThread">{{Reddit|url=r/StarWars/comments/1ttsebz/please_help_i_think_a_comic_artist_traced_a|thread=Please help, I think a comic artist traced a commission of mine.|user=HMS_Exeter|date=2026-06-01|quote=A few years ago I commissioned the wonderful Kharak to create a 3d model of the Centurion-class battlecruiser from KOTOR 2 (released on sketchfab on 29/10/22). We added a few details, like the side hangar bays, and while browsing wookieepedia I found this image from the comic {{'}}''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand 1''{{'}} released on 25/12/24. This canon depiction of the centurion seems to be based upon my commission, looking at those side hangars.|archivedate=20260601161021}}</ref> Several features of the ship, such as the lateral docking bays, were invented for the fan model, and had not previously appeared in official sources.<ref name="TracingThread" /> However, neither Marvel nor the issue's artists have confirmed this connection.
