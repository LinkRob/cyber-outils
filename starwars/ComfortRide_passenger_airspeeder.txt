The '''ComfortRide passenger airspeeder''' was a model of open-air [[repulsorcraft]] [[airspeeder]] manufactured by the [[Hyrotii Corporation]]. It was comparable to Hyrotii's [[EasyRide passenger airspeeder]] and was used as [[air taxi]]s on the [[ecumenopolis]] [[Coruscant]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:The_posse_escapes.png|thumb|left|250px|Cad Bane's posse escapes from the Senate Building in ComfortRide speeders]]
The ComfortRide was a model of open-air [[airspeeder]] manufactured by the [[Hyrotii Corporation]].<ref name="2018Vehicles" /> It similar in appearance to<ref name="HC" /> Hyrotii's [[EasyRide passenger airspeeder|EasyRide]] model,<ref name="2018Vehicles" /> but slightly longer. The airspeeder also lacked the EasyRide's antenna, but had an elongated rear and a tail-fin. The model had two seats in the front and space for at least four more passengers in the back.<ref name="HC" /> 

Both the EasyRide<ref name="2020Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> and the ComfortRide<ref name="2018Vehicles" /> commonly used as [[air taxi]]s on the [[ecumenopolis]] [[Coruscant]] which gave both the names '''Coruscant air taxis'''.<ref name="DB">{{Databank|coruscant-air-taxi|Coruscant Air Taxi}}</ref><ref name="2018Vehicles" /> They were piloted by trained taxi drivers to ferry their passengers from one destination to another and were allowed unrestricted "free travel" that allowed them to leave the official autonavigated skylanes that confined most other vehicles on long journeys along defined corridors to prevent unmanageable traffic chaos<ref name="2020Vehicles" /> and also utilized [[airbus]] lanes.<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

==History==
The ComfortRide was one of many models of taxi airspeeders in use during the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] and her entourage used a ComfortRide speeder to travel during their stay on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The speeder was sabotaged and crashed into a skyscraper.<ref name="DOM">{{TCW|Duchess of Mandalore}}</ref>

Two ComfortRides were used by [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] and his [[Cad Bane's group|crew]] during the rescue of [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] the [[Hutt]] from the [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center]].<ref name="HC" />

The model would continue to see use after the war's end. While chasing after [[Omega]] on [[Pantora]], bounty hunter [[Fennec Shand]] commandeered a civilian's ComfortRide, but it was destroyed by [[Hunter]]'s [[pyro denton explosive]].<ref name="Cornered">{{TBB|Cornered}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The "Coruscant Air Taxi" entry in the [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] uses an image of a ComfortRide passenger airspeeder, but lists<ref name="DB" /> the dimensions of the smaller EasyRide passenger airspeeder.<ref name="2020Vehicles" />
