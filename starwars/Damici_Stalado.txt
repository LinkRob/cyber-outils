'''Damici Stalado''' was a [[human]] who believed that physical shape was arbitrary. While undergoing deep-tissue regrafting and splice therapy in [[10 BBY]], she attended a party on board the ''[[First Light]]'', the private [[yacht]] of the [[Crimson Dawn]] [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]]. After the yacht arrived on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]], Stalado danced on the viewing deck during a performance by the [[Musician|singers]] [[Aurodia Ventafoli]] and [[Luleo Primoc]].

==Biography==
Damici Stalado was a [[human]] who<ref name="TOG" /> in [[10 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events aboard the ''[[First Light]]'' in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to nine [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Empire's formation to [[19 BBY]]; therefore, those events of the film must take place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> was undergoing deep-tissue regrafting and splice therapy to transform her features again. While in the midst of the therapy, she attended a party held by [[Dryden Vos]], a [[crime lord]] of the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate, on board his personal [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]''.<ref name="TOG" /> Stalado was on board when the [[Starship|vessel]] arrived on<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> the [[planet]] [[Vandor]],<ref name="TOG" /> and during a performance by the [[Musician|singers]]<ref name="Solo" /> [[Aurodia Ventafoli]] and [[Luleo Primoc]],<ref name="TOG" /> she stood by a table of [[Foodstuff|food]] on the viewing deck with two other humans while gesturing with her hands. Another woman then joined the trio, and Stalado began dancing as the criminal [[Han Solo]] passed by them.<ref name="Solo" />

==Personality and traits==
Damici Stalado was a social chameleon who believed physical shape to be arbitrary. She had dark skin and [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s.<ref name="TOG" />

==Equipment==
While on the ''First Light'', Stalado wore a pink [[Clothing|dress]] made of metachromatic [[trevalla cloth]] with a golden [[belt]] and neckpiece. She also wore a gold [[bracelet]], golden high-heeled sandals,<ref name="HNTGE">''[[Star Wars: How Not to Get Eaten by Ewoks and Other Galactic Survival Skills]]''</ref> a black masquerade mask, and a black parabolic headdress<ref name="TOG" /> that was good at concealing her identity.<ref name="HNTGE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Damici Stalado appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo" /> and was identified in the accompanying reference book, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TOG" />
