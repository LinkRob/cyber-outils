'''Coovern''' was a [[moff]] in the highest [[Military rank|ranks]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] who attempted to replace [[Colonel]] [[Shakara Nuress]] as leader of the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] with [[Captain]] [[Jothal Gablerone]]. After the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] began a [[Mid Rim Retreat|campaign]] into the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] around [[1 ABY]], Coovern and [[Minister]] [[Khemt]] were [[Death|killed]] by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Their deaths, reported as tragic accidents, were part of a purge of those who were blamed by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] for the [[Battle of Yavin|destruction]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Emperor Palpatine decided that incompetence at the highest ranks was to blame for the destruction of his Death Star, and from there began a culling. There were other deaths, less public.|Everi Chalis explains the reason for Coovern's death to Micha Evon|audio=ChalisDeaths-BTC.flac|<ref name="BTC" />}}
[[File:DarthVader-BlackWhiteRed1-virginvariant2-DellOtto.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Darth Vader (pictured) executed Coovern on the order of the Emperor.]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Coovern served among the highest echelons of the [[Galactic Empire]], holding the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[moff]].<ref name="BTC" /> Coovern once assigned Imperial [[Captain]] [[Jothal Gablerone]] to the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] to replace its [[commanding officer]], [[Colonel]] [[Shakara Nuress]], who ultimately refused to retire.<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> Shortly after the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] began a [[Mid Rim Retreat|campaign]] into the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="BTC" /> around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Coovern and [[Khemt]], a [[minister]] who was also among the Empire's highest ranks, were both [[Death|killed]] by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Their deaths came as part of a purge of those whom [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] blamed for the [[Battle of Yavin|loss]] of the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]].<ref name="BTC" />

Coovern and Khemt were officially claimed to have died in tragic accidents. However, [[Everi Chalis]], the [[governor]] of the [[planet]] [[Haidoral Prime]], learned how the moff had actually died through her own sources.<ref name="BTC" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY">{{BTCdate}}</ref> Chalis defected to the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] and revealed the true nature of Coovern's death to the rebel [[company]]'s captain, [[Micha Evon]].<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coovern was first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
