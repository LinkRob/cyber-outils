'''Bo-Katan Kryze's unit'''<ref name="Mando Sticker">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Ultimate Sticker Collection]]''</ref> was a team of [[Mandalorian]] [[Nite Owls]] under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]],<ref name="MandoVG" /> the former [[Mandalore (title)|Mand'alor]]. Kryze and her followers fought against [[Gideon's Imperial remnant]] and hunted for the [[Darksaber]] with the intention to one [[Standard day|day]] [[Conflict on Mandalore|retake]] the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]].<ref name="Chapter 16" />

==History==
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate}}</ref> Kryze and her unit were hijacking weapons shipments from the [[black market]] port on [[Trask]] to [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]'s [[Imperial Remnant|Imperial]] [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|remnant]]. During this time, Kryze, [[Koska Reeves]], and [[Axe Woves]] rescued Mandalorian [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] and his foundling, [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] from Quarren fishermen. At first, Djarin refused to acknowledge Kryze and her unit as fellow Mandalorians. After destroying the Quarrens' [[Quarren fishing vessel|boat]], Kryze and her unit save Djarin and from the surviving Quarren.<ref name="Chapter 11" />

Kryze told Djarin about how Gideon's remnant were using the plunder they seized from Mandalore during the [[Great Purge of Mandalore|Great Purge]] to buy weapons. She tells him that she and her unit were hijacking the shipments in order to reclaim Mandalore, despite Djarin's belief that the planet was cursed. Kryze assured him that was not true, knowing that their enemies wanted to divide the surviving Mandalorians more. Kryze asked Djarin to help her to continue her hijacking missions. At first, djarin refused stating that he was trying to find a Jedi to take in Grogu. Kryze, remembering [[Ahsoka Tano]] from the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> told Djarin where he could find one in exchange for his help.<ref name="Chapter 11" />

After showing Djarin their target, an Imperial [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class]] cruiser, Djarin, Kryze, Reeves, and Woves flew in. Together, they were able to [[Assault on the Imperial Gozanti freighter|take out]] the stormtroopers. During the hijacking, the cruiser's [[captain]], [[Tribus]], contacted Gideon and told him of his situation. He was ordered the scuttle the cruiser. However, Kryze and her unit were able to take control of the cruiser. Having captured Tribus, Kryze demanded to know if Gideon still had the [[Darksaber]]. The captain confirmed that he did before killing himself with a [[Electro capsule|suicide shocker]]. For completing the mission, Kryze told Djarin to go the city of [[Calodan]] on [[Corvus]] to [[Liberation of Calodan|find]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. Kryze and her unit left Trask and Djarin returned to the ''[[Razor Crest]]''.<ref name="Chapter 11" />

Woves later left Kryze and Reeves on [[Lafete]]. At the [[Port of Lafete|port]], they were approached by Djarin and [[Boba Fett]]. At first, Reeves fought Fett until Kryze broke it up. Djarin was able to convince her and Reeves to help them in rescuing Grogu from Gideon. In exchange, she could claim both Gideon's [[Gideon's light cruiser|light cruiser]] and the Darksaber. With help from [[New Republic]] [[Marshal]] [[Carasynthia Dune]] and assassin [[Fennec Shand]], Kryze and her unit were able to take control of Gideon's ship. However, she was unable to reclaim the Darksaber due to Djarin disarming and capturing Gideon. Kryze and her allies then received help from [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]], who finished off Gideon's [[Phase-III Dark Trooper|Dark Troopers]].<ref name="Chapter 16" />
