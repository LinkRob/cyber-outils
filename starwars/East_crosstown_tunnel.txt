The '''east crosstown tunnel''' was a tunnel located in<ref name="Ezra's Gamble" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Lothal]]'s [[Capital City]].<ref name="Ezra's Gamble" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Ezra's Gamble]]'' to [[5 BBY]].</ref> the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] traveled to Lothal and enlisted the help of local orphan [[Ezra Bridger]] after [[Lothal garrison|forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] began to pursue him. As the pair fled from Imperial [[scout trooper]]s on a [[Thief|stolen]] Imperial [[speeder bike]], Bridger told Bossk of his plan to travel down the east crosstown tunnel in an effort to lose their pursuers. However, Bridger ultimately [[pilot]]ed the [[speeder]] bike down a different tunnel by mistake.<ref name="Ezra's Gamble" />

==Behind the scenes==
The east crosstown tunnel was mentioned in ''[[Ezra's Gamble]]'', a junior novel by [[Ryder Windham]] and published in [[2014]].<ref name="Ezra's Gamble" />
