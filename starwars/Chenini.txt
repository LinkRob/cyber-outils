'''Chenini''' was one of the three [[moon]]s of the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], along with [[Guermessa]] and [[Ghomrassen]]. Chenini and its parent planet were located in the [[Tatoo system]], a [[Binary star system|binary]] [[star system]] located in the [[Arkanis sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="AbEverything" />

{{Moon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The moon is also the name of a real [[Wikipedia:Chenini|village]] near [[Wikipedia:Tataouine|Tataouine]] in [[Tunisia]], where many Tatooine scenes were filmed.
