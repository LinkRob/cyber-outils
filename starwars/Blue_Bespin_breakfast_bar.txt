'''Blue Bespin breakfast bars''' were a type of breakfast [[Foodstuff|food]]. The [[light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' held blue [[Bespin]] breakfast bars in its refrigerator unit on at least one occasion; the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] found them alongside [[blue bantha buttermilk biscuit]]s and [[Blue Puff Cube|blue puff cubes]], leading him to wonder if all the foods on the ship were [[Color|blue]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" />
