This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Solo" /> [[Ahmar]] was a notable member of this species.<ref name="Devoted" />

==Biology and appearance==
This sentient species is distinguished by the six horns found on their head, two protruding from the top, two from the side, and two from the chin. Their skin was varying shades of green, and they appeared hairless, oftentimes with yellow and then orange coloration around the ends of their horns, as well as yellowish patches surrounding some facial features.<ref name="Solo" /> The species was capable of both speaking [[Galactic Basic Standard]] and being [[Force-sensitive]].<ref name="Devoted" />

==Species in the galaxy==
During the final years of the [[Galactic Republic]], Ahmar, a member of the species, was inducted into the [[Jedi Order]]. A peer of [[Barriss Offee]], Ahmar would survive [[Order 66]] but be detained by the [[Galactic Empire|Empire's]] [[Inquisitorius]], brought to their burgeoning [[Fortress Inquisitorius|training facility]] on [[Nur]]. Here he was invited to join their ranks and compete to become an [[Inquisitor]] alongside Offee and another mutual candidate, [[Dante]], though, when showing doubt and attempting to leave the fortress, he was caught and executed, the [[Grand Inquisitor]] using his fate to deter further resistance.<ref name="Devoted" />

[[File:Ahmar-Species-Coruscant.png|thumb|left|300px|A member of this species within Coruscant's lower levels]]
Additionally, one unidentified member of this species was found among the guests aboard [[Dryden Vos]]'s [[First Light]] [[Nau'ur-class yacht|yacht]] the first time [[Han Solo]] arrived alongside [[Chewbacca]] and [[Tobias Beckett]].<ref name="Solo" /> Another was present at the [[Coruscant Eastern Spaceport]] shortly after [[Syril Karn]] lost his job with [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]] and was amid traveling back home to see his [[Eedy Karn|mother]].<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> Another could be seen dwelling in the [[Coruscant Underworld|lower levels]] of [[Coruscant]], not far from where [[Lonni Jung]] and [[Luthen Rael]] held a secret meeting.<ref name="OWO">{{Andor|One Way Out}}</ref> A few years later,<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|207}}</ref> a member of this species worked as a reporter, present on the [[planet]] [[Ghorman]] just before the [[Ghorman Massacre]].<ref name="Messenger">{{Andor|Messenger}}</ref>

Another was incarcerated by the [[New Republic]] and put to work in the [[Karthon Chop Fields]], in close proximity to fellow prisoner [[Migs Mayfeld]] when he was freed by [[Marshal]] [[Carasynthia Dune]] and recruited by [[Din Djarin]] for a dangerous mission to [[Morak]].<ref name="Believer">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 15: The Believer}}</ref>
