'''Dune zaywars''' were a [[species]] of tusked creature. The [[Church of the Force]] devotee [[Dasha Promenti]] used a [[blaster pistol]] with a handle made from a dune zaywar's tusk while she lived on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]], around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The tusk of a dune zaywar first appeared as part of a prop in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], a film released in [[2015]]. The substance was not identified in the film, but was named in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released the day after the film.
