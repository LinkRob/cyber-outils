'''Aran’s jetpack''' was a [[jetpack]] utilized by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] to assist him within the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] hosted within [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. Attaching to the back of [[Clan Tal Warrior|Aran Tal's armor]], the Mandalorian could use the dual-[[Rocket pack|rockets]] on the bottom of his jetpack to fly, boost, hover, and even slam into the ground during [[combat]].<ref name="Hunters" />
