The '''E-11D blaster carbine''' was a [[blaster carbine]]<ref name="DoR" /> manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]]. It was used by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[death trooper]]s and [[Purge Trooper]]s. Very similar in design to the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]], it featured a stock and a large-bore reinforced barrel in order to maximize its rate of fire and intensity.<ref name="UVG" /><ref name="Databank">{{Databank|death-trooper-blastech-e-11d-rifle|Death Trooper BlasTech E-11D Rifle}}</ref>

==Description==
[[File:E11DGrenadeLauncherConfig-SWL34_Mark_Molnar.png|thumb|left|200px|A death trooper carrying a E-11D with grenade launcher configuraton.]]
The [[BlasTech Industries|BlasTech]] E-11D [[blaster carbine]] was the lightweight, sturdily-built standard-issue weapon of [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[death trooper]]s. The E-11D was designed for close-quarters and urban combat situations, the type of occurrences that death troopers excel in. The carbine traded range and accuracy for stopping power and short range performance. The E-11D came with a large-bore reinforced [[barrel]] and was equipped with integrated features to improve power output, balance, and handling. It also had an integrated stun setting.<ref name="DoR" /> It could also be [[Reconfigurable blaster|reconfigured]] with a [[grenade launcher]] attachment.<ref name="SWL34" />

==History==
[[File:Sideshow-PurgeTrooper-closeup.png|thumb|right|250px|An Imperial Purge Trooper in phase II armor with an E-11D]]
Imperial death troopers wielded E-11D blaster carbines. The [[Death Squad Leader]] was armed with an E-11D.<ref name="FA">''[[Star Wars: Force Arena]]''</ref> [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] had a [[Krennic's guard squadron|squad]] of death troopers, many of whom wielded E-11Ds, at his disposal and acting as his personal guards.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> a contingent of death troopers was requisitioned from Krennic by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who led them into the [[skirmish in the Korakanni Mound]]. Several of the death troopers wielded E-11Ds during the engagement.<ref name="Annual 2">[[Darth Vader Annual 2|''Darth Vader Annual'' 2]]</ref> During the [[capture of Galen Erso]]<ref name="Rogue One" /> in [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> several of Krennic's death troopers held E-11Ds as they marched on [[Lah'mu]] in search of the [[Erso family]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> Imperial [[Purge Trooper]]s, by the time they shifted into [[Phase II Purge Trooper armor]], wielded E-11D carbines.<ref name="Part IV">{{Kenobi|Part IV}}</ref>

During his service to the Empire, [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn" was granted a [[Thrawn's guard|squad]] of death troopers by [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] himself.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref> The squad acted as Thrawn's personal security. They were armed with the standard E-11D. During the [[Lothal campaign|occupation of Lothal]], death troopers such as [[DT-L21]] were armed with the standard-issue blaster rifle.<ref name="The Occupation">{{Rebels|The Occupation}}</ref>

[[File:Databank-E11D.png|thumb|left|270px|A death trooper with their E-11D]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> members of a [[DT-F16's squad|death trooper squad]] under Commander [[DT-F16]] wielded E-11Ds during the [[mission to Faos Station]], in which they attempted to protect a [[kyber crystal]] shipment.<ref name="INR">{{Rebels|In the Name of the Rebellion}}</ref> During the [[Battle of Scarif]], Krennic ordered all but two of his personal death trooper squad onto the beaches. The majority of troops wielded the E-11D.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

After the [[Battle of Jakku|collapse of the Empire]], [[Gideon's Imperial remnant]] had a [[Gideon's guard|unit of death troopers]], all of which were armed with the E-11D blaster rifle as well.<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> The death troopers of [[Thrawn's forces]] still used the E-11D during their exile on the [[extragalactic]] [[planet]] [[Peridea]]. One of them used a variant of the blaster carbine that lacked the metallic reinforced barrel tip that it usually has.<ref name="Part Eight">{{Ahsoka|Part Eight: The Jedi, the Witch, and the Warlord}}</ref>
