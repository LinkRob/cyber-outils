A '''prison''' of [[Darth Devastator]]'s [[Galactic Empire]] was located on the [[planet]] [[Dagobah]]. [[Yoda]], [[Grievous]], and [[Mace Windu]] were held there after [[Yesi Scala]] accidentally exposed a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] base. The prison was heavily guarded by [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] [[Clone trooper|troopers]]. Sometime later, the prison was discovered by [[Sig Greebling]] during a mission to find a [[Force Builder temple]] built by the [[Force Builders]].<ref name="LEGO" />
