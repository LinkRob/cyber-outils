The '''Er'Kit''' were a [[species]] of [[humanoid]]s with large, elongated craniums and long, skinny legs. One Er'Kit was [[Ody Mandrell]], a famous [[Podracing|podracer]] who took part in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]].

==Biology and appearance==
The Er'Kit were a [[Sentience|sentient]] race of [[humanoid]]s<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> that originated from the [[planet]] [[Er'Kit]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Commander" /> Er'Kit possessed large, elongated craniums and long, skinny legs with skin that came in various shades of [[Color|blue]],<ref name="DB" /> red, and purple.<ref name="HRA 9" /> Er'Kit were well adapted to [[desert]] planets due to the environments of their [[homeworld]].<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==History==
The Er'Kit homeworld had a murky history of political scandals, [[Slavery|slave trade]], and violent militias.<ref name="Alien Archive" />
Shortly before the [[Clone Wars]], [[Danry Ledwellow]] was an Er'Kit [[Gender|male]] [[senator]] who represented the planet Er'Kit in the [[Galactic Senate]]. After a complication with [[Thalassian]] slavers, Ledwellow was arrested, and his private army of [[IG-227 Hailfire-class droid tank|IG-227 ''Hailfire''-class droid tanks]] fell into the hands of the [[Blood Dawn]] slavers.<ref name="Commander" />

==Er'Kit in the galaxy==
Some Er'Kit hailed from the planet of [[Tatooine]], located in the Outer Rim Territories.<ref name="DB" />

In the [[High Republic Era]], Er'Kit sisters [[Sabata Krill|Sabata]] and [[Bareen Krill]] were members of the [[Nihil]].<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 8">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 8|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 8]]</ref>

[[Ody Mandrell]] was a famous [[Podracing|podracer]] renowned for his team of [[DUM-series pit droid|pit droids]] that helped him to break several records. During the [[Invasion of Naboo|Naboo Crisis]], Mandrell took part in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]].<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>
