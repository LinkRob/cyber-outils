'''Shuttle ports'''<ref name="Story Gallery">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e2-story-gallery|text="That Would Be Me" Story Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> were facilities scattered around<ref name="Andor 2" /> the [[Free Trade sector]]<ref name="Andor 1">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="AD">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> [[Ferrix]]. They were used by the [[Ferrix Entry Ferry Service]] to [[transport]] passengers to and from the [[Ferrix Terminal]] station in [[Ferrix City]] via the company's [[HFY-3920 shuttle ferry|shuttle ferries]]. They could accommodate at least two ferries each<ref name="Andor 2" /> on exterior landing pads<ref name="AD" /> and were equipped with large [[antenna]]e.<ref name="Andor 2" />

One of the [[shuttle]] stations was located 2.3 [[kilometer]]s away from the [[city]] and was used by [[spy]]master [[Luthen Rael]]<ref name="Andor 2" /> in [[5 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|102}}</ref> When<ref name="Andor 2" /> he<ref name="Andor 1" /> arrived on the planet to meet with local [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], who was selling an [[Imperial N-S9 Starpath Unit]] Rael was interested in, the spy left his [[Luthen Rael's Fondor Haulcraft|personal]] [[V-21.1 Chevlex light haulcraft|Fondor Haulcraft]] near the station and picked a shuttle ferry from there in order to continue his trip inconspicuously.<ref name="Andor 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
