The '''''Elite''-class vessel''' was a class of science [[Starship|vessel]] developed by the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. The ''[[Innovator]]'' was an ''Elite''-class vessel designed by [[Vam Targes]] and constructed at the [[Cyclor Shipyards]] by the [[insectoid]] [[Cyclorrian]]s. The vessel had a long and tapered arrowhead shape, with [[sensor]]s lining a raised area at the rear that housed the operations center. The ''Innovator'' was showcased at the [[Republic Fair]] on the [[planet]] [[Valo]], but was destroyed when the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] launched an [[Valo Atrocity|attack]] on the celebration.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
