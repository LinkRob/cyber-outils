'''Dwarf vinocks'''<ref name="YouTube">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Creature in the Star Wars Movies|video=liYY0_DcgE8|nolive=1}}</ref> were a type of winged creature with [[Color|cream]] skin and black [[eye]]s. In [[10 BBY]], the [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]] kept a dwarf vinock on display in a case in his study on board his [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]''.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
A dwarf vinock first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology series]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> They were first identified in the current [[canon]] in the video "Every Creature in the ''Star Wars'' Movies", which was released by the ''Star Wars'' Kids [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel in [[2019]].<ref name="YouTube" />
