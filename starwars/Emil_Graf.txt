'''Emil Graf''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[cartographer]]. He was the [[Parent|grandson]] of [[Milo Graf]], who, along with his sister [[Lina Graf|Lina]], was involved in a series of misadventures early in the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Along with his [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]] [[Noni]], his [[BB-series astromech droid|BB unit]] [[BB-00]], and the [[monster droid]] [[CR-8R]], Graf traveled across [[Wild Space]] in the Imperial prototype [[scout ship]] ''[[Star Herald]]'', which he inherited from his grandfather and great-aunt.<ref name="Stop, Thief" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Emil_Graf_BB-00_concept_art.jpg|thumb|left|184px|Jon Sommariva's concept art of Emil Graf and BB-00]]
Emil Graf first appeared in the ''Tales of Wild Space'' back-up story of [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 1|''Star Wars Adventures'' (2017) 1]], "[[Tales from Wild Space: Stop, Thief!|Stop, Thief!]]," written by [[Cavan Scott]] with art by [[Jon Sommariva]], [[Sean Parsons]] and [[Charlie Kirchoff]]. The character was revealed to be the grandson of [[Milo Graf]], a character from the [[Star Wars: Adventures in Wild Space|Adventures in Wild Space]] chapter book series, some of which were also written by Scott. Similarly to his grandfather, Emil owns a [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]] as a pet. His starship, the ''[[Star Herald]]'' comes from the chapterbook series as well, where it was a ship originally owned by a [[Visler Korda|former]] [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]].<ref name="Stop, Thief" />

Scott named him after an editor of the Adventures in Wild Space chapter book series, [[Emil Fortune]].<ref name="Name">{{WebCite|url=http://cavanscott.com/2017/09/19/naming-bb-00-for-star-wars-adventures|text=Naming BB-00 for Star Wars Adventures|author=[[Cavan Scott]]|date=2017-09-19|archivedate=20190315161442}}</ref>
