'''Cybernetic brain implants''' were a type of miniaturized [[computer]] [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] implant designed to augment the brainpower of its user. The implant fit around the user's ears and the back of their head, and granted superior reasoning and increased calculation speed, as well as memory storage. The cost of a cybernetic brain implant was 10,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cybernetic brain implants were mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets" />
