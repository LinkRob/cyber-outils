'''Deraformine''' was a drug that was used to fake [[death]]. Agents of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Naval Intelligence Agency|Naval Intelligence]] sometimes used it as an exit strategy from an assignment. Although highly risky, that strategy worked provided that the so-called "corpse" was recovered intact and brought to safety. Smaller amounts merely rendered the ingester unconscious, posing little danger.<ref name="Inferno Squad">''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''</ref>
