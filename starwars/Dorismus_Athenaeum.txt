'''Dorismus Athenaeum''' was a location on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="Explorer's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dorismus Athenaeum was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]'', a [[2019]] reference book written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Explorer's Guide" /> Within the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, it was first mentioned in [[Death Star (novel)|''Death Star'']], a [[2007]] novel by [[Michael Reaves]] and [[Steve Perry]].<ref name="DS">[[Death Star (novel)|''Death Star'']]</ref>
