The '''''Daggadol''''' was a [[Submarine|submersible vehicle]] that was operated by the [[Draedan]] [[pirate]] [[Aurelant]] on the [[planet]] of [[Sesid]]. It was large and [[Color|dark]] and was covered with a thick layer of seaweed. It had a bow that was able to split open and let surface [[Starship|ships]] enter it. The interior had catwalks that overlooked the basin inside the ship.<ref name="MT" />
