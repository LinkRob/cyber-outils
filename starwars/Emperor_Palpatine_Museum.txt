The '''Emperor Palpatine Museum''' was a [[museum]] dedicated to [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] on his [[homeworld]] [[Naboo]]. The gift shop included mugs, Sheev Palpatine voice changers, and [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-ATs]]. Exhibits included [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Amidala]], dioramas of the [[Clone Wars]], and multiple [[starship]]s, including a rare [[N-1 starfighter]].<ref name="Crystal Chase">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Kyber Saber Crystal Chase}}</ref>
