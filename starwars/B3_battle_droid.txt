'''B3 battle droids''' were [[battle droid]]s manufactured by [[Baktoid Combat Automata]]<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> and used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> [[B2-series super battle droid]]s and B3 battle droids were favored over the expensive and complex [[E522 assassin droid]]s.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> At one point, at least one was stationed near a [[Vanqor listening station|listening station]] on the [[planet]] [[Vanqor]]. A curved piece of metal used in B3 repair was uncovered by [[Quinlan Vos]] when he and a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] strike team [[Destruction of the Vanqor listening station|attacked]] the listening post.<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> The B3 battle droid was also mentioned in an intelligence report by<ref name="Official Magazine 118">{{GermanFanCite|118|Database}}</ref> [[Corporal]]<ref name="sunny">{{TCW|A Sunny Day in the Void}}</ref> [[WAC-47]] describing E522 assassin droids, which was included in the survival compendium "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]."<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />

[[Temmin Wexley]] also found parts of a B3 while trying to sneak into the [[Satrap's Palace]] on [[Akiva]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>
