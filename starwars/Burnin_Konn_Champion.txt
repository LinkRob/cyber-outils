A [[human]] [[Gender|male]] known as the '''Burnin Konn Champion''' was one of many individuals who came to the call to defend the [[Mining Worlds|mining world]] of [[Burnin Konn]]. He rose to prominence within the defenders, becoming a champion to the people of that planet.<ref name="Uprising" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the [[2015]] iOS video game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', the Burnin Konn Champion is one of the many crew members that can be sent on Crew Runs for helpful items.<ref name="Uprising" />
