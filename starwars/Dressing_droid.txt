'''Dressing droids'''<ref name="Chapter 4" /> were a type of [[droid]]s designed to assist dressing a being up. [[Daimyo]] [[Boba Fett]] used two [[Color|white]]&mdash;colored dressing droids during his [[Fett gotra|reign]] as the daimyo of [[Mos Espa]] to put on his [[Boba Fett's armor|armor]].<ref name="Chapter 1">{{BOBF|Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref>
{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dressing droids first appeared in "[[Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land]]," the first episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'' television series,<ref name="Chapter 1" /> which was released on [[December 29]], [[2021]].<ref name="Disney+">{{DisneyPlusYouTube|t9Ca4ARSfqA|Next On Disney+ &#124; December 2021}}</ref> The droids were first identified in "[[Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm]]," the fourth episode of the series,<ref name="Chapter 4" /> which was released on [[January 19]], [[2022]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-january-2022|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in January 2022}}</ref>
