'''Dathan''' was a [[Symeong]] [[Gender|male]] [[administrator]] and [[Slavery|slave]] to the [[Sith Eternal]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]]. He was friends with a [[Dathan|Strand-Cast]] who was the [[Palpatine family|son]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The flashback events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' which feature Dathan takes place concurrently with [[Darth Vader (2020) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 11]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Dathan helped his friend to escape Exegol. After escaping from Exegol, the [[Strand-Cast]] used the name of Dathan to sign on a register to work aboard a sunscooper.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />{{Char-stub}}
