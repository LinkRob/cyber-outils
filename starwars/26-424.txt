'''26-424''' was an individual from [[Galloway Island]]. 26-424 belonged to an [[Alien/Legends|alien]] [[Species/Legends|species]] that possessed [[Color/Legends|dark]] [[Eye/Legends|eyes]], a tail, two [[Limb/Legends|legs]], at least one arm, and a horn on the head. 26-424, as usual, refused to participate in a [[Unidentified seminar|periodical seminar]] that, at least on one occasion, featured eminent figures such as [[Regalia Sub-space station-12]] director [[mOKDAR 15]], [[Manifold Mollusk Matrix and Matrimony Association]] director [[Skreen]], and [[University of Desultori]] scholar and author [[Aanxi Raanxi Laanxi]], although 26-424 had presence confirmed for the feedings.<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behind the scenes==
26-424 was introduced in a segment named "The Seminar Participants," written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that collects concept designs and photographs from various [[George Lucas]] projects.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]] with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
