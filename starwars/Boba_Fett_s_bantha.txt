A '''[[bantha]]''' was owned by [[Boba Fett]] until around [[9 ABY]] when he set her free.

==Biography==
Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|5}} The flashbacks of ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'' Chapter 4 take place, in part, at the same time as Chapter 5 of ''The Mandalorian''.</ref> [[Boba Fett]] rode the [[bantha]] to [[Boba Fett's Palace|the palace]] once occupied by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] with the intent to retrieve [[Slave I|his ship]]. After seeing the amount of guards at the palace, he decided to hold off. He later saw lights in the sky that led him to [[Fennec Shand]]. After seeing to Fennec's wounds, Boba, the bantha, and Fennec returned to the area around the palace, where Boba set the bantha free.<ref name="Chapter 4" />
