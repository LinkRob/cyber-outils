An '''entry dome''' was a domed entrance that led underground, often to a buried structure. The [[moisture farmer]] [[Cliegg Lars]] hand-built a [[pourstone]] entry dome for access to [[Lars homestead|his]] below-ground [[moisture farm]].<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
