{{Quote|My children and I can manipulate the Force like no other. Therefore, it was necessary to withdraw from the temporal world and live here as anchorites.|Father to [[Anakin Skywalker]]|<ref name="Overlords">{{TCW|Overlords}}</ref>}}
An '''anchorite''' was a type of [[hermit]] who [[Exile (punishment)|retired from society]] for somewhat religious reasons.<ref name="Overlords Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/overlords-trivia-gallery|text="Overlords" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> The powerful [[Force wielder]] known as the [[Father (Mortis)|Father]] described himself and his two [[Youngling|children]] as such.<ref name="Overlords" /> The anchorites that lived on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] were known to wear dark fancy [[clothing]] to control their body. They were also known to take in children as orphans. The faith of the anchorites had ties to the [[Light side of the Force|light side]] of [[the Force]] and a thousand [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Jakku]], the anchorites bound themselves to the [[Jedi]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> The [[Caretakers]] of [[Ahch-To]]'s [[Temple Island]] reminded the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] of the anchorites on Jakku.<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
