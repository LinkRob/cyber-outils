'''Andi Gutierrez''' is the show host of both ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'' and ''[[Rebels Recon]]''.

==Biography==
An avid ''Star Wars'' fan since age seven, when her father first showed her [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']],<ref name="SW Andi Gutierrez" /> she joined [[Lucasfilm]] in 2013. On her first day at work, she was given the task of learning the entirety of Star Wars. She initially worked as a social media representative for Lucasfilm on Facebook and Twitter.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.npr.org/transcripts/477026236|text=Meet the Expert: Andi Gutierrez|date=2016-05-06|archivedate=20240622010238}}</ref> In 2014, she began hosting ''[[Rebels Recon]]'', a YouTube show that accompanied the TV series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' where she interviewed guests and reviewed each episode.<ref name="Recon #1.01">{{RebelsRecon|jXl7GtvG8t4|#1.01: Inside "Spark of Rebellion" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-spark-of-rebellion}}</ref> Gutierrez is the [[Tuckerization|namesake]] of [[Gooti Terez]], a character designed to resemble her from "[[Iron Squadron (episode)|Iron Squadron]]," the sixth episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Three|third season]] of ''Star Wars Rebels''.<ref name="Recon #3.07">{{RebelsRecon|mlpvQhH58X4|#3.07: Inside "Iron Squadron" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-iron-squadron}}</ref> ''Rebels Recon'' aired until 2018 when the TV series ended.<ref name="Recon #4.8">{{RebelsRecon|uR8Sn_2VRBA|#4.8: Inside "A Fool's Hope" & "Family Reunion &ndash; and Farewell" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/disneyxd-rebels-recon-inside-a-fool-s-hope-and-family-reunion-and-farewell}}</ref>

In 2016, she and [[Peter Townley]] were selected to host the weekly ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'', a YouTube program which recapped news associated with Star Wars and interviewed guests and fans.<ref name="Variety">{{WebCite|url=https://variety.com/2016/digital/news/star-wars-show-digital-series-andi-gutierrez-1201770363|text=‘Star Wars Show’ to Serve Weekly Blast of News, Interviews|author=Todd Spangler|date=2016-05-11|archivedate=20160512100903}}</ref> The show aired until 2020, when it went on hiatus.<ref name="Laughing Place">{{WebCite|url=https://www.laughingplace.com/w/news/2020/12/16/the-star-wars-show-is-going-on-a-indefinite-hiatus-will-return-for-occasional-specials-on-youtube|text=“The Star Wars Show” is Going On a Indefinite Hiatus, Will Return for Occasional Specials on YouTube|date=2020-12-16|work=[https://www.laughingplace.com Laughing Place]|archivedate=20201216224710}}</ref> Along with [[Anthony Carboni]] and [[Corey Vidal]], she was a host for the [[Star Wars]] Celebration Anaheim Live.<ref name="Anaheim Live">{{SW|url=news/watch-star-wars-celebration-anaheim-live-on-starwars-com-powered-by-verizon|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim Live on StarWars.com, Powered by Verizon!}}</ref>

In July 2018, Gutierrez received online abuse on Twitter after [[Rebel Force Radio]] posted an altered photo of her posing with a mug saying, "Fanboy Tears." In response, [[StarWars.com]] severed its relationship with RebelForce Radio.<ref name="The Mary Sue">{{WebCite|url=https://www.themarysue.com/star-wars-fans-lucasfilm-harassment|text=Star Wars Fans Now Going After Lucasfilm Employees (But There's Hope for Fandom Yet)|author=Kate Gardner|date=2018-07-09|work=[https://www.themarysue.com The Mary Sue]|archivedate=20181029012814}}</ref><ref name="The Daily Dot">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dailydot.com/parsec/star-wars-last-jedi-gamergate|text='Star Wars' backlash is the new Gamergate|author=Gavia Baker-Whitelaw|date=2018-07-11|work=[https://www.dailydot.com The Daily Dot]|archivedate=20200418142007}}</ref><ref name="">{{WebCite|url=https://gizmodo.com/the-washington-posts-analysis-of-star-wars-toxic-fandom-1828856645|text=The Washington Post's Analysis Of Star Wars' Toxic Fandom Doesn't Go Deep Enough|author=James Whitbrook|date=2018-09-07|archivedate=20210802124012}}</ref>
