The '''712-AvA speeder bike'''<ref name="The Mandalorian Handbook" /> was a reliable, low to the ground [[speeder bike]] model manufactured by [[Aratech Repulsor Company]]. It was known for its speed and sturdy engineering.<ref name="2024 Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> The inexperienced [[bounty hunter]], [[Toro Calican]], [[Unidentified 712-AvA speeder bike (Toro Calican)|rode]] this model across the desert dunes of the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], while chasing down a [[bounty]] on [[Fennec Shand]], alongside [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]].<ref name="Chapter 5">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 5: The Gunslinger}}</ref>

When Djarin returned to Tatooine in search of other Mandalorians, [[Peli Motto]] loaned him Calican's speeder bike when he went to [[Freetown]], with [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] in a bag on the side of the speeder. Djarin rode the bike with [[Cobb Vanth]] when he recruited the [[Tusken Raider]]s to help kill a [[Leviathan krayt dragon]].<ref name="Chapter 9">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref> When he was returning to [[Mos Espa]], a trio of raiders set a trap by concealing a rope across the gully. As the bike passed, the raiders raised the rope, knocking Djarin off the bike. The bike was destroyed as a result, and Djarin, Grogu, and the supplies attached to the bike were sent flying.<ref name="Chapter 10">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 10: The Passenger}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The 712-AvA speeder bike first appeared in [[Chapter 5: The Gunslinger|Chapter 5]] of [[Jon Favreau]]'s [[2019]] [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="Chapter 5" /> which aired on [[December 6]], 2019.<ref name="DTCI">{{WebCite|url=https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/the_mandalorian_project_one-sheet_10_ec1e005f.pdf|text=The Mandalorian Media Kit|work=[https://dtcimedia.disney.com Disney Direct-to-Consumer & International]|archivedate=20230315150248}}</ref> The speeder bike was later identified in the [[2021]] [[Canon]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Handbook]]''.<ref name="The Mandalorian Handbook" />
