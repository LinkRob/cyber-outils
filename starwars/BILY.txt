'''BILY''' was a [[bear]]-like droid who was at one point present in [[Mos Eisley]] on [[Tatooine]] with the [[human]] [[Yuko]]. He had a microwave oven built into his chest.<ref name="Celebration" /> BILY was an acronym for "'''Baby I Love You'''."<ref name="Getty">{{Getty|subdomain=assets.dam|url=s75j56ckkw879rfqfnxr49s/v/1291165528/original/VISIONS%20SEASON%203%20Final%20Production%20Brief.pdf?Expires=1761418361&KeyName=gcs-bfldr-prod&Signature=yVNCOpLKX0CT4Ufu0FjQLOANhQs=|text=VISIONS SEASON 3 Final Production Brief|date=2025-10-22|nolive=1}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
BILY first appeared in "[[Yuko's Treasure]]", a non-[[canon]] short film directed by [[Masaki Tachibana]] released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kinema Citrus Co.]], was released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. BILY was first introduced at the ''Star Wars: Visions'' panel at [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]] on [[April 19]], 2025.<ref name="Celebration" /> He is voiced by [[Mitsuru Houfu]] in the original [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] version and by [[Harvey Guillén]] in the English dub.<ref name="Getty" />
