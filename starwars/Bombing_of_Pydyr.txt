The '''Bombing of Pydyr''' occurred in [[17 ABY/Legends|17 ABY]], when the [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]] [[Kueller]] devastated the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] of [[Pydyr]].

==The bombing==
Kueller had seized control of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Almania]] and wanted to devastate its wealthy neighbor, Pydyr. This was to be as a demonstration of his power and also for more practical reasons, so that he could gain it's wealth. He detonated bombs that been hidden inside [[Droid/Legends|droids]] that had been manufactured on [[Telti/Legends|Telti]] and 1,651,305 [[Pydyrian]]s were killed. At the same time, a coordinated operation was led by [[Hantzig]], who had about 1000 people imprisoned within their homes. The bombing sent out a massive disturbance in [[The Force/Legends|the Force]], that was felt by [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa Solo]], and her children, [[Darth Caedus|Jacen]], [[Jaina Solo Fel|Jaina]] and [[Anakin Solo|Anakin]], and some of the students in Luke's [[Jedi Praxeum]] on [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]].

==Aftermath==
Kueller had the valuables of Pydyr divided up into lots and sold off to collectors, to fund the next stage of his operation.
