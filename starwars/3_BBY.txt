The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''3 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''BBY 3''',<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman?">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}}</ref> as '''3274 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7974''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*After deploying prototypes<ref name="Dev insights">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/253434/developer-insights-tie-in-interceptor-prototype|text=Developer Insights: TIE/IN interceptor Prototype}}</ref> for years,<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> the Empire officially brings [[TIE/IN interceptor]]s into action.<ref name="Dev insights" />
*[[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] has her [[Leia Organa's Day of Demand|Day of Demand]].<ref name="LPOA Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>
*[[Thane Kyrell]] and [[Ciena Ree]] are accepted to the [[Royal Imperial Academy]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
*Princess Leia is officially [[Coronation of the Alderaanian heir|invested]] as crown princess of Alderaan following the completion of her [[Challenge of the Heart]], [[Challenge of the Mind]] and [[Challenge of the Body]].<ref name="LPOA Date" />
*[[Kongo]] the Disemboweler is employed to keep the [[dolovite]] [[Mining|mine]] tunnels of [[Mustafar]] free of [[lava flea]]s and [[xandank]]s.<ref name="GA" />
*[[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[Partisans]] [[Rhydonium heist|steal]] [[rhydonium]] with the help of [[Wilmon Paak]].<ref name="Friends Everywhere">{{Andor|I Have Friends Everywhere}}</ref>
*The [[Ghorman Front]] [[Ghorman heist|steal]] a shipment of Imperial cargo in [[Palmo]], [[Ghorman]], but one of their allies from the [[Axis network]], [[Cinta Kaz]], is killed during the operation.<ref name="Festive Evening">{{Andor|What a Festive Evening}}</ref>
*[[Davo Sculdun]] holds a [[Davo Sculdun's party|party]] for [[Senate Investiture]], during which [[Kleya Marki]] clandestinely removes a [[listening device]] planted within the [[Davo Sculdun's gallery|gallery]].<ref name="Festive Evening" />
*[[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] and [[Bix Caleen]] [[Assassination of Gorst|assassinate]] [[Doctor]] [[Gorst]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Festive Evening" />
*Princess Leia covertly delivers three [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvettes]] to the Phoenix rebels on Lothal.<ref name="A Princess on Lothal" />
*The Phoenix rebels gain safe passage through the [[Concord Dawn system]] from the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]].<ref name="The Protector of Concord Dawn">{{Rebels|The Protector of Concord Dawn}}</ref>
*The rebels [[Rediscovery of Lira San|rediscover]] the [[Lasat]] homeworld of [[Lira San]].<ref name="Legends of the Lasat">{{Rebels|Legends of the Lasat}}</ref>
*The Lothal rebels, with the help of the [[Free Ryloth Movement|Ryloth freedom fighters]] led by [[Cham Syndulla]], hijack an [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|Imperial bomber carrier]].<ref name="Homecoming" />
*[[Kanan Jarrus]] is promoted to [[Jedi Knight]].<ref name="Shroud" />
*[[Phoenix Cell]] establishes a [[Chopper Base|base]] on [[Atollon]].<ref name="The Forgotten Droid" />
*[[Maul]] is found living on [[Malachor]].<ref name="Twilight" />
*[[Barion Raner]] begins serving under [[Antoc Merrick]]'s command.<ref name="UVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
