A '''cracker''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Yoda]] combined a small cracker with [[sweetpuff jam]] to make a and roasted it over a fire to make [[sweetpuff jamwich]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> Later, during the [[New Republic Era]], [[Karr Nuq Sin]] and [[Maize Raynshi]] were served crackers, along with [[runyip cheese]] at a [[restaurant]] on [[Utapau]].<ref name="Force Collector" /> [[Fish Crackers]] were available at the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]] on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''.<ref name="SWGS">{{GSAttr|Crown of Corellia Dining Room}}</ref>
