'''Egbert Sen''' (c. [[1930s|1932]] &ndash; [[March 14]], [[2019]]) was a [[Wikipedia:Pakistani|Pakistani]] actor who played [[Willrow Hood]] in [[1980]]'s [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. The character has since attracted a cult following by ''[[Star Wars]]'' fans, inspiring "The Running of the Hoods" cosplaying event at ''Star Wars'' [[Celebration]] conventions from the 2010s onward.

==Biography==
{{Quote|The Running of the Hoods are here to honor Egbert Sen, the original Cloud City king who wouldn't leave his camtono behind. Thank you, Hoods!|[[Anthony Carboni]], at Celebration Japan in 2025|<ref name="SWCJ 2025 1">{{SWYouTube|video=vbV-4BmPmxc|text=Star Wars Celebration LIVE! 2025 - DAY 1|date=2025-04-18}}</ref>}}
Born around [[1930s|1932]] in [[Wikipedia:British Raj|British-ruled]] [[Wikipedia:Indian subcontinent|India]],<ref name="Infobox" /> Egbert Sen was a [[Wikipedia:Pakistani|Pakistani]]<ref name="Infobox" /> who emigrated to the [[Wikipedia:United Kingdom|United Kingdom]] in [[1950s|1957]], finding employment as a musician performing Indian music for the [[Wikipedia:BBC|BBC]] in [[Wikipedia:England|England]]. He contributed to the score of the [[1960s|1965]] [[Wikipedia:The Beatles|Beatles]] film [[Wikipedia:Help! (film)|''Help!'']]. Sen was contacted by an agent recruiting non-white actors, and, living in south [[Wikipedia:London|London]], he commuted to [[Elstree Studios]] for work in film and television productions such as [[Wikipedia:The Saint (TV series)|''The Saint'']] in the 1960s. For the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], Sen was a supporting cast member who played a character later named [[Willrow Hood]] in the corridors of [[Cloud City]], running amidst the [[Escape from Cloud City|evacuation]] of the [[city]] holding a [[camtono]] container.<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> Sen's daughters Dolly and Paula also appeared among a group of children running with their teacher in a scene set in Cloud City as [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] leads the [[Heroes of Yavin|rebel heroes]] through the corridors.<ref name="Delage">{{YouTube|video=l7sp9_hXYQA|text=Le Plus GROS MYSTÈRE de STAR WARS Enfin Résolu ! (après 43 ans)|channel=@NicolasDelage|name=Nicolas Delage|date=2023-08-14}}</ref> The scene was filmed between [[April 5]]&ndash;[[April 12|12]], [[1979]], on stage 2 at Elstree.<ref name="Rinzler">''[[The Making of The Empire Strikes Back]]''</ref>

[[File:RunningoftheHoods.jpg|thumb|right|300px|"The Running of the Hoods," inspired by Egbert Sen's character, Willrow Hood]]
Sen found additional work as a supporting cast member in films throughout the 1980s, including [[Steven Spielberg]]'s [[Lucasfilm]] productions [[w:c:indianajones:Raiders of the Lost Ark|''Raiders of the Lost Ark'']] of [[1981]]<ref name="Guardian">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2011/feb/26/asian-parents-care-homes-sarfraz-manzoor|text=Asian parents in care homes|author=[[Wikipedia:Sarfraz Manzoor|Sarfraz Manzoor]]|date=2011-02-26|work=[[Wikipedia:The Guardian|''The Guardian'']]|archivedate=20230201125221}}</ref> and [[w:c:indianajones:Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom|''Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom'']] of [[1984]], acclaimed British films such as [[Wikipedia:My Beautiful Laundrette|''My Beautiful Laundrette'']] of [[1985]],<ref name="Guardian" /> as well as the [[Wikipedia:Octopussy|James Bond]] and [[Wikipedia:Superman IV: The Quest for Peace|''Superman'']] film franchises. Although Sen spoke of his film career, he made no mention of his ''[[Star Wars]]'' work in a [[Wikipedia:Channel 4|Channel 4]] documentary<ref name="Asian Gracefully">{{YouTube|video=A46iG2z-7gw|text=First Cut - Asian Gracefully (4 March 2011)|channel=@japanin30seconds95|name=Japan in 30 seconds}}</ref> or in an interview with [[Wikipedia:The Guardian|''The Guardian'']]<ref name="Guardian" /> that were both published in [[2011]], and he passed away while resident at the Aashna House care home in south London in [[2019]].<ref name="Delage" />

Willrow Hood has attracted a cult following among ''Star Wars'' fans. From the 2010s onward, ''Star Wars'' [[Celebration]] conventions have featured "The Running of the Hoods," a cosplaying event of fans who, like Hood in ''The Empire Strikes Back'', dressed in orange jumpsuits and ran across the convention floor holding a container.<ref name="Celebration">{{SW|url=news/empire-at-40-6-minor-star-wars-the-empire-strikes-back-characters-you-should-know|text=''Empire'' at 40 &#124; 6 Minor ''Star Wars: The Empire Strikes'' Back Characters You Should Know}}</ref> His daughters Dolly and Paula discovered Sen's following as Willrow Hood in [[2023]], when they were approached by a ''Star Wars'' fan named Nicolas Delage investigating the identity of Hood's actor, and they were proud of their late father for the following; the sisters said that the phenomenon was the recognition that he had always wanted.<ref name="Delage" /> The Running of the Hoods at the subsequent ''Star Wars'' Celebration, [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]] in [[2025]], was dedicated to Egbert Sen.<ref name="SWCJ 2025 1" />
