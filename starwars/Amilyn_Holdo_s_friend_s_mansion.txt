A '''mansion''' owned by a [[Amilyn Holdo's friend|friend]] of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[captain]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] was located on the [[planet]] [[Spira]].<ref name="Star Wars 29" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Holdo borrowed the mansion from her friend and brought members of the Rebel Alliance there on vacation from the [[Galactic Civil War]]. After the rebels acquired a [[Nihil]] [[Path engine]] following an [[Auction on Spira|auction]], they returned to the mansion where Holdo installed it into her [[starship]], the ''[[Skyfarer]]'', though they were tracked by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. As the rebels fled aboard the ''Skyfarer'', the [[Starfighter|fighters]] destroyed the mansion and Holdo lamented the loss, knowing her friend would be upset.<ref name="Star Wars 29" />
