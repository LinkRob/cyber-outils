{{Quote|I've gotta admit, I'm impressed. Jabba ''said'' you ''Trandoshans'' had a good thing ''going'' here. But these ''corrosive charges'' of yours are ''really'' something ''special''.|Han Solo|<ref name="Life Day 1" />}}
'''Corrosive charges''' were a type of volatile chemical weapon. A group of [[Trandoshan]]s who took over a medicine production [[Gullet|facility]] on the [[moon]] [[Lessa-7]] used the chemicals from the [[stomach]] of a [[sarlacc]] to create corrosive charges. The [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] were sent to Lessa-7 by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] to purchase some of the corrosive charges, but during the process discovered that the Trandoshans had been [[Slavery|enslaving]] the [[Wookiee]]s who formerly owned the facility and freed them, ending the Trandoshans' operation. Regardless, the Wookiees, the facility once again under their control, allowed the smugglers to take the last remaining corrosive charge for free.<ref name="Life Day 1" />
