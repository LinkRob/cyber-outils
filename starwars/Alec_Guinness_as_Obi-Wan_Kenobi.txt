"'''Alec Guinness as Obi-Wan Kenobi'''" is an interview of [[Alec Guinness]] conducted by [[Michael Parkinson]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 112|''Star Wars Insider'' 112]]. The article collects a variety of quotes from the interview, which was originally conducted in [[1977]], from Guinness about his experience filming ''[[Star Wars]]''.
