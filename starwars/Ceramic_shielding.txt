'''Ceramic shielding''' was a material that could be added to a [[starship]]'s hold to protect its systems from ionized cargo. However, it could not protect computer systems from ionic discharges.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Miyuki Trade League]] installed ceramic shielding in [[Kay Vess]]'s [[EML-850 light freighter]], the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', to allow her to [[Smuggler|smuggle]] ion related cargo, such as the ion weapons favored by the [[Ashiga Clan]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ceramic shielding first appeared in the [[canon]] continuity in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref>  In the expansion, players are awarded with the "Ion dampening hold" upgrade when talking with [[Reess Passik]] after completing the quest "No Prey, No Pay". The upgrade allowed them to take smuggling contracts to transport ion weapons.<ref name="Outlaws" />
