'''Devalok''' was a terrestrial [[planet]] known for its [[garden]]s and gardeners<ref name="Eye of the Beholder" /> that was located in the [[Devalok system]] in the [[Altier sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Devalok fell under a brutal [[Siege of Devalok|attack]] from the Separatists despite having no strategic value, during which its [[City|cities]] and gardens were destroyed. [[Republic Military]] forces were forced to retreat, evacuating [[refugee]]s from the [[Amrit spaceport]]. During the assault, [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] rescued the [[Youngling|children]] [[Zohra]] and [[Dumuz]] and their [[parent]]s from the ruins of their [[Zohra's family's apartment|apartment]] and the [[Enki greenhouse]]. Devalok was also known for being the [[homeworld]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Uma Kalidi]].<ref name="Eye of the Beholder" />
