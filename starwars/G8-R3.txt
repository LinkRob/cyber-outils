'''G8-R3''', also known as '''R5-A7''', was an [[astromech droid]] which served onboard the [[Naboo Royal Starship]] used by the [[Royal House of Naboo|royalty]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. During the [[Invasion of Naboo]] by the [[Trade Federation]], [[King|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] used the vessel to try and escape from the planet, but came under fire from [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|''Lucrehulk''-class freighters]]. When the Royal Starship's [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] was damaged, the ship's astromechs attempted to repair it, but G8-R3 was destroyed in the attempt.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Hello Boyos.|Jar Jar Binks greets G8-R3 and the other astromechs|audio=Binks-HelloBoyos.flac|<ref name="TPM" />}}
[[File:AstromechsRepairingJtype327Nubian-TPM.png|thumb|left|250px|G8-R3 works to repair the Naboo Royal Starship's shield generator.]]
Also known as R5-A7,<ref name="Jedi Challenges">''[[Star Wars: Jedi Challenges]]''</ref> the [[astromech droid]] designated G8-R3 was one of six astromech units which served on board the [[Naboo Royal Starship]] used for transport by the [[Royal House of Naboo|royalty]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]].<ref name="TPM" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[32 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA" /> Naboo was blockaded and then [[Invasion of Naboo|invaded]] by the [[Trade Federation]] over a tax dispute. The Federation managed to successfully capture [[Theed]], the world's capital, and took over [[Theed Royal Palace]] During the Federation's occupation, G8-R3 remained onboard the Royal [[Starship]] in the Royal Hangar, along with five other astromech [[droid]]s.<ref name="TPM" />

After being rescued by two [[Jedi]], the reigning [[King|queen]] at the time, [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], and her entourage fought their way to the Royal Starship and boarded it, hoping to escape Naboo and travel to the planet [[Coruscant]] where the Queen could plea for help from the [[Galactic Senate]]. Once the group was onboard, the [[Gungan]] [[Jar Jar Binks]], a companion of the Jedi, was told to remain with the astromechs and not cause trouble by [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Binks greeted the droids, causing them to activate and turn to face him.<ref name="TPM" />

[[File:G8-R3_destroyed.jpg|thumb|right|250px|G8-R3 was destroyed by the Trade Federation battleships blockading Naboo.]]
As the ship attempted to escape Naboo, it was greeted by the Federation's blockade of [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|battleships]], which opened fire on the Royal Starship and damaged its [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]]. G8-R3 and its companions were alerted to the damage by an alarm, and the group quickly made its way to the ship's exterior to attempt repairs. Positioning themselves on the vessel's right wing, the droids began their work on the generator while under fire from the battleships. In turn, each droid was destroyed until only G8-R3 and [[R2-D2]] remained, and then G8-R3 too was hit, its parts being blown away into the void of space. The sole survivor, R2 managed to repair the generator and reactivate the Royal Starship's shielding, allowing it to escape the blockade.<ref name="TPM" />

==Characteristics==
G8-R3 had white and yellow [[color]]ed plating, with red and yellow [[sensor]]s. The droid possessed a small manipulator arm, which it used to try and repair the Royal Starship.<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
G8-R3 was designed for use in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], first appearing in the film's [[1999]] [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|novelization]], a part of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity. The droid was not identified in the film or novel, but received a name in the Legends ''[[Star Wars: Episode I Insider's Guide]]'' released by [[LucasArts]] in the same year as the film's release.
