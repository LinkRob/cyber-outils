'''Catalor''' was a terrestrial location in [[the galaxy]]. At some point,<ref name="Databank" /> the [[bounty hunter]]s<ref name="Shadow 4">[[Shadow of Maul 4|''Shadow of Maul'' 4]]</ref> [[Rheena Sul]] and [[Brander Lawson]] undertook a [[mission on Catalor]]. During the failed mission, Sul saved Lawson's life, and subsequently Lawson saved Sul.<ref name="Databank" /> Later during the [[hunt for Maul]], Sul mentioned the Catalor mission to Lawson.<ref name="Sinister Schemes" /> Sul later reference Catalor upon Lawson checking in if she was comprised by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Creeping Fear">{{MSL|Chapter 8: The Creeping Fear}}</ref>
