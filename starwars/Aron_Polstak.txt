'''Aron Polstak''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. After the [[Battle of Hoth|evacuation of Hoth]] in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="RPDate">"[[Rendezvous Point (short story)|Rendezvous Point]]" is set in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Hoth]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]]. As such, the events in "Rendezvous Point" must have taken place around that year.</ref> Polstak was [[Commander]] [[Wedge Antilles]]' first selection for the reformed [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]]. However, he was unavailable as he was already on special assignment for [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="Rendezvous Point" />
