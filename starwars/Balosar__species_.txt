'''Balosars''' were a [[near-human]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] which possessed two [[antennapalps]] on the tops of their heads, but were otherwise similar in appearance to [[human]]s. They were native to the ruined world [[Balosar]]. 

==Biology and appearance==
Balosars were a [[near-human]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] native to<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Balosar system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Balosar]].<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> They were visually almost indistinguishable from [[human]]s except for two small sensory enhancing [[antennapalps]]<ref name="EG" /> located between the [[hair]] on the tops of their heads near the front.<ref name="AOTC" /> They were also immune to almost any [[Poison|toxins]] in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Face of Evil">"[[The Face of Evil]]"</ref>

==Balosars in the galaxy==
Shortly before the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="AOTC" /> the Balosar [[Elan Sel'Sabagno|Elan Sleazebaggano]]<ref name="Elan Sleazebaggano">{{Databank|elan-sleazebaggano|Elan Sleazebaggano}}</ref> attempted to sell the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] [[death stick]]s in the [[Outlander Club]] on the planet [[Coruscant]], but the [[Jedi]] [[mind trick]]ed the dealer into going home and rethinking his life. At around this time a male Balosar also served in the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="AOTC" /> 

[[Ven Alman]] was a Balosar [[pirate]] and a friend of fellow pirate [[Phee Genoa]]. He was killed in the early [[Imperial Era]] by [[Cellia Moten]].<ref name="Sanctuary">''[[Sanctuary: A Bad Batch Novel]]''</ref>  During the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]] on the planet [[Lothal]], the street urchin [[Ezra Bridger]] spotted a Balosar woman wearing a [[shimmersilk]] scarf around her antennapalps, which he valued at least one thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="EG" />

[[BeeLee Amdas]] was a notorious Balosar [[thief]] who lived during the [[New Republic Era]], before her human partner [[Ryn Biggleston]] killed her by poisoning her drink with one of the only few toxins deadly for Balosars. Biggleston later tried to have her visual appearance changed by the [[Frigosian]] [[cryptosurgeon]]s [[Thromba]] and [[Laparo]] at [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on [[Takodana]]. After having found out that she intended to leave without paying them, the two surgeons instead changed her appearance to that of her partner Amdasz. Afterwards the pair contacted the local authorities and Biggleston was captured while trying to flee the planet on a [[transport]].<ref name="Face of Evil" />
