The '''Dinwa system''' was a [[star system]]<ref name="Into the Light" /> located in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> that contained the [[planet]] [[Dinwa Prime]].<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{IntotheLightDate}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] sent the [[Nihil]] intelligence operative [[Nan Hague]] to<ref name="Into the Light" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] to lure the [[Jedi Order]] into an ambush in the Dinwa system. However, while on Coruscant, Hague threatened to unleash the affliction known as the [[blight]] if she was not able to speak with [[Jedi Knight]] [[Reath Silas]]. After speaking with the Jedi Knight, she [[Desertion|deserted]] from the Nihil and accompanied him to<ref name="Into the Light" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> planet [[Kashyyyk]] to help the [[Jedi]] [[Mission to Kashyyyk (blight)|find]] the Nihil [[Doctor]] [[Zadina Mkampa]] who Hague believed was heading there.<ref name="Into the Light" />

During her time on Kashyyyk, Hague was confronted by Silas about why she had not told them about Mkampa's interest in controlling<ref name="Into the Light" /> the [[Sentience|sentient]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[carnivorous plant]]s known as the [[Drengir]] that had attacked them, then stating that she had deserted from the Nihil and told them nothing, asking if it had been worth her threat of releasing the blight on Coruscant. In response, Hague told Silas that she had not deserted by going to Coruscant, but by accompanying him to Kashyyyk, and then revealed to the Jedi Knight the reason why she had been sent to Coruscant by Ro, noting she had disobeyed the Eye's order.<ref name="Into the Light" />
