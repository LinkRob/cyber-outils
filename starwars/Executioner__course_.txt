The '''Executioner''' was a [[podracing]] [[Racetrack|course]] on<ref name="BTMF64" /> the [[prison]] [[moon]]<ref name="BXW 11">{{BuildXWingCite|11|Starfighter Aces}}</ref> [[Oovo 4|Oovo IV]],<ref name="BTMF64" /> located in the [[Oovo system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Tharin sector]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Oovo system]]</ref> It was part of the [[Galactic Podracing Circuit]], a series of seven championship races spanning [[the galaxy]].<ref name="BTMF64" /> The twin [[Rodian]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracers]] [[Staka]] and [[Raka]] had won a race on the Executioner by the [[time]] they participated in the [[Hydian-Hutta Podracing Circuit]]<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> around [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> The win was recorded on their downloadable [[Racer|racing]] profile.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Executioner was first mentioned in "Visiting Malastare," an article<ref name="BTMF64" /> written by [[Mark Newbold]]<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> and published in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 64|sixty-fourth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine series,<ref name="BTMF64" /> which was released around [[March 23]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|64}}</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where the [[Executioner (course)/Legends|course]] first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']], a [[1999]] video game released by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="Racer">[[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']]</ref>
