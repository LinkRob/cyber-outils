The '''Agrunan Spire''' was a residential building found on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|21}}</ref> [[Chemish Runazha]] climbed it, starting at [[Level 4168]] up thirty meters freehand, using only [[magnetic glove]]s and [[grip stocking]]s, while some of the spire’s residents shouted obscenities or encouragement, or threatened to call the [[Coruscant Security Force]].<ref name="MoF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Agrunan Spire appeared in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
