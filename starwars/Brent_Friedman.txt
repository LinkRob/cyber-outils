'''Brent Friedman''' was a writer on ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' and ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. He wrote the scripts for several episodes of ''The Clone Wars''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth]], [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth]], [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth]] and [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|seventh seasons]].
