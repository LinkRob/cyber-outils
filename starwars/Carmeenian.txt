The '''Carmeenian''' also refered to as just the '''Carmeen''' were the inhabitants of the [[Carmeen]]. The Carmeenians had their own code of conduct and would skin trespassers alive. Sometime before the [[Imperial Era]] they were part of the [[Battle of Carmeen]] which resulted in very few Carmeenians surviving the battle.<ref name="What a Festive Evening">{{Andor|What a Festive Evening}}</ref>

By [[3 BBY]], [[Davo Sculdun]], a [[Chandrilan]] businessman had several items pertaining to Carmeenian culture in [[Davo Sculdun's gallery|his private gallery]] on [[Coruscant]]. During a [[Senate Investiture]] [[Davo Sculdun's party|party]] hosted by Sculdun, [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Director of Advanced Weapons Research|Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] verbally sparred about the Battle of Carmeen which Mothma had been taught was a systematic execution.<ref name="What a Festive Evening" />
