'''Cincey's work hut''' was a small outbuilding located within the [[Path compound|Path of the Open Hand compound]] on the [[planet]] [[Dalna]]. The human Path member [[Cincey]] managed communications from within the hut, including collecting information that helped the [[Children of the Open Hand]] on their missions to collect [[Force artifact]]s all over the galaxy. The hut contained a table, stools, and the equipment Cincey used in their work, as well as other tools. The Children often spent time in and around the hut, and it served other purposes such as temporary storage.<ref name="POD" />
