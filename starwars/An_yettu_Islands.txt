==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the An'yettu Islands were introduced in the [[2016]] issue ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 63]]''.<ref name="BMF 63" /> The [[An'yettu Islands/Legends|islands]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[1996]] sourcebook ''[[Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook]]''.<ref name="ShadowsEmpire">''[[Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook]]''</ref>
