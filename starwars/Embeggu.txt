'''Embeggu''' was a [[Klatooinian]] [[pirate]] and brewer who served within a [[Klatooinian raiders|group]] of raiders during the [[New Republic Era]]. While on the [[planet]] [[Sorgan]], Embeggu was [[Death|killed]] by the [[human]]s [[Carasynthia Dune]] and [[Din Djarin]] when they [[Skirmish on Sorgan|infiltrated]] their [[Klatooinian wikiup|wikiup]].<ref name="Chapter 4" />
