'''''Battle of Yavin''''' was the fourth expansion card pack made for the [[Star Wars Trading Card Game]] produced by [[Wizards of the Coast]], based on [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]&mdash;in particular, the elements of the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]]. The expansion symbol is that of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|first Death Star]].

The expansion was released in [[March 31]], [[2003]]<ref name="rel" /> and contains 105 cards. It was sold in boosters containing 11 (7 common, 3 uncommon, 1 rare) cards.

==Gameplay==
*New Abilities:
**'''Retaliate''' - If a unit in the same arena as this unit is attacking this unit, this unit does X dice of damage to that unit when the attack ends
*Card Types:
**Battle: 19
**Character: 39
**Ground: 21
**Mission: 7
**Space: 19
