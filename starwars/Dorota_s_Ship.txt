'''Dorota's Ship''' was an [[N-1 starfighter]]-like ship used in the [[End-of-year family race]] at the [[Hanna City Flight Academy]].<ref name="I Am Your Mother" />

{{Stub}}

==Behind the Scenes==
Dorota's Ship appeared in "[[I Am Your Mother]]," the fourth episode of ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]{{'}}'' [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second volume]], which premiered on [[Disney+]] on [[May 4]], [[2023]]. Unlike the [[Cormorant|Space Tug]], the ship is in total contrast through elegant, slick lines, agressive and high tech within the Star Wars universe. To create the ship, Aardman used the royal Naboo ship as base from [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|Episode I: The Phantom Menace]] then after some rudimentary sketches, designer [[Aurelien Predal]] quickly pushed the design in 3D in order to lock the scale with the trickest thing to keep was a nice curve with the cockpit windscreen.<ref name="aurelien-predal" />
