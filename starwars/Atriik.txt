The '''Atriik''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] known to exist during the time of the [[Clone Wars]]. Members of the species possessed [[Horn/Legends|horns]] on the sides of their heads and mottled skin.<ref name="FotL" />

One Atriik was [[Gender|Mother]] [[Tunenep]], a follower of the [[Path of the Open Hand]]. She lived to be more than 150 [[Standard year|years]] old, an advanced age even for the Atriik.<ref name="FotL" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Atriik appeared in "[[Galactic Tales: The Failing of the Light]]," a short story by [[Adam Lance Garcia]] that was published in [[Star Wars Insider 233|issue 233]] of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="FotL" /> on [[August 19]], [[2025]].<ref name="Lunar">{{WebCite|url=https://www.lunardistribution.com/home/search?term=Star+Wars+Insider|text=Star Wars Insider|work=[https://www.lunardistribution.com/ Lunar Distribution]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/GLG8o}}</ref> According to Garcia, he deliberately avoided describing the species in detail, to allow for other creators in the future to expand upon it.<ref name="SWLit">{{Spotify|subdomain=open|url=episode/75ILnFi3EiPyegjQsVqu0Z|text=Adam Lance Garcia Wrote (Another) Star War|archivedate=20250902141011}} </ref>
