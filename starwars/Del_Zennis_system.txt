The '''Del Zennis system''' was a restricted [[star system]]<ref name="Stealth Strike" /> in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> that was used as a research testing ground for the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Interdictor (Interdictor-class)|Imperial Interdictor]]<ref name="Stealth Strike" /> in [[4 BBY]].<ref name="Timeline">"[[Stealth Strike]]" takes place in [[4 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The Imperial Interdictor was a prototype warship equipped with four [[gravity well projector]]s that were capable of pulling ships out of [[hyperspace]]. Under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Brom Titus]], the Interdictor succeeded in destroying one rebel patrol and capturing the [[CR90 corvette]] [[Liberator (CR90 corvette)|''Liberator'']], the flagship of [[Phoenix Cell]]. Among their captives were [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] and [[Ezra Bridger]], a young [[Jedi]]-in-training. During a [[Rescue on the Imperial Interdictor|rescue mission]] mounted by [[Kanan Jarrus]], [[Rex]], and [[C1-10P|Chopper]], the Imperial Interdictor and its escort [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|light cruisers]] were destroyed, leaving the surrounding space strewn with wrecked debris and starship parts. Admiral Titus survived the devastation in an [[escape pod]] and was retrieved by [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]].<ref name="Stealth Strike" />
