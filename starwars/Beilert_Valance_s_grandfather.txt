The '''grandfather''' of the [[human]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] was a [[Gender|male]] individual who was [[Slavery|enslaved]] as a [[miner]] on his<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> [[homeworld]] [[Chorin]]<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> for his whole life, like generations of the [[Valance family]] and the population of the world before him had been for [[Standard century|centuries]], until they were [[Liberation of Chorin|liberated]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] and became a free people who mined for profit. Sometime after Chorin's liberation, a young Valance asked his [[Beilert Valance's father|father]], who was also a human miner, if he would remain a miner his whole life like his ancestors. The boy's [[Parent|father]] stated that he believed Valance himself was different and was born free, and that he belonged to the [[star]]s.<ref name="Target Vader 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beilert Valance's grandfather was first mentioned in a flashback in the [[Target Vader 2|second issue]] of the [[2019]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]'', written by [[Robbie Thompson]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> on [[August 14]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel-TV2">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/78430/star_wars_target_vader_2019_2|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #2}}</ref>
