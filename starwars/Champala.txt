'''Champala''' was a [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Catalyst" /> in the [[Champala system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Located on the [[Hydian Way]],<ref name="Beginner Game" /> it was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Chagrian]] [[species]], including [[Mas Amedda]], [[Vice Chair|Vice Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and later [[Grand Vizier]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Journeys: Beginnings" /> A popular [[Time|pastime]] on Champala was [[fishing]], but, according to Amedda, by the [[Clone Wars]] its [[ocean]]s were "not what they once were." Nevertheless, the [[human]] [[architect]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] used such as an analogy to explain his plans for [[scientist]] [[Galen Walton Erso]] to Amedda.<ref name="Catalyst" /> The world had [[Champalan Embassy|its own embassy]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Complete Locations" /> 

During the Clone Wars, Champala hosted a [[Separatist base (Champala)|base]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Full panel"/> It was later [[Occupation of Champala|occupied]] by the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Lead" />
