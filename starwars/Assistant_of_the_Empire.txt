"'''Assistant of the Empire'''" is an article published in [[Star Wars Insider 147|''Star Wars Insider'' 147]]. The article, written by [[Calum Waddell]], is a spotlight on and interview with British actor [[Milton Johns]], who portrayed [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Bewil]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].
