The '''All Terrain Heavy Enforcer''', abbreviated as '''AT-HE''', was a model of [[walker]] in the [[AT-series]] that was created in the [[Standard year|years]] following<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> [[22 BBY]]<ref name="Date">The All Terrain Heavy Enforcer is mentioned in a section of [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']] covering the events of {{Film|II}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> due to the success of [[Rothana Heavy Engineering]]'s [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]. The AT-HE was heavier than the AT-TE and had a hull raised above the reach of [[mine]]s, also allowing greater vantage for its energy weapons.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the All Terrain Heavy Enforcer was mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']].<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> The [[All Terrain Heavy Enforcer/Legends|walker]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2002]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', which was written by [[Curtis Saxton]].<ref name="AOTCICS">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
