In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s carried out an '''attack''' on<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> [[Thelj]]. The attack was reported to the [[Galactic Republic]] [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] within [[Standard hour|hours]] of it taking place. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] was informed of the attack when she contacted [[Jedi Master]] [[Estala Maru]], who was stationed on Starlight Beacon. Rwoh then asked the Jedi Master if anyone had gone missing from the attack, but he did not state if there had been any reports of missing individuals from Thelj.<ref name="Mission to Disaster" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on Thelj was mentioned in the [[2022]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
