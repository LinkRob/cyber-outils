"'''Always count your clones before take-off'''" is the third in a series of comics available on the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] website. It was first placed on the LEGO website in the fall of [[2008]]. It was later placed on [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 20]], [[2009]] with some changes, notably, different sound effects and background music.

==Plot summary==
The [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] is making a hasty retreat, but aboard one [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry/Legends|Republic gunship]] a clone notices that [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]] [[1137]] and [[1139]] are not aboard. Back on the planet, the two missing troopers wave desperately at the leaving gunships, but to no avail. They are alone until a group of [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]] arrives. Hiding behind a large crate, the two overhear the droids discussing their secret weapon. Determined to find the weapon, the clones create a plan. They open the crate and construct a [[Hovertank/Legends|fighter tank]]. Using the tank, the clones set to find the weapon, leaving a trail of destroyed droids in their wake. Finding the weapon to be a large tower, the troopers fire the tank's weapons at the top of it, bringing it down. As the two look over the wreckage, a Republic gunship comes down to pick them up.
