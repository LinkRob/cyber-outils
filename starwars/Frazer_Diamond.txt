'''Frazer Diamond''' is a former production staff member, puppeteer, and child actor who portrayed a [[Jawa/Legends|jawa]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': ''Episode IV: A New Hope'']]. He is the brother of [[Warwick Diamond]], and the son of stunt co-ordinator [[Peter Diamond]].

==Biography==
[[File:FrazerDiamond-ANH.jpg|thumb|left|210x210px|Frazer Diamond, [[Warwick Diamond]] and [[Anthony Daniels]] during the filming of ''A New Hope''.]]
Diamond portrays one of the [[Jawas]] inside the [[Sandcrawler]] who interacts with [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]].

He is specifically identified as the [[Jawa]] who "freaked out" [[C-3PO]] by swinging a [[blaster]] at him, when the [[protocol droid]] asked, "Do you think they'll melt us down?". His scenes were filmed at [[Elstree Studios]] when he was roughly 7 years old. He has humorously attributed his casting to "good old-fashioned nepotism," as his father was the stunt arranger for all three films in [[The Original Trilogy]].
