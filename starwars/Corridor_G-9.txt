'''Corridor G-9''' was a corridor on the ''[[Colossus]]'' [[Fuel|refueling]] station. When the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] ran from the [[First Order]] while wearing the [[First Order stormtrooper armor|armor]] of the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[CS-515]], he escaped down Corridor G-9, placing the [[armor]] back on the unconscious [[trooper]], who was then tracked down and caught.<ref name="The New Trooper" />
