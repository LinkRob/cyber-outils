The '''Command Chair''' was the collective name of a custom [[Repulsorlift chair|repulsorlift]] [[Command Chair]] and a pair of [[Blaster Remote]]s utilized by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Command Chair was a "Base" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Sprocket's chair and remotes, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Prodigal Programmer]]. This wrap was unlocked by default when Sprocket became playable.<ref name="Hunters" />
