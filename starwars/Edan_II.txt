'''Edan II''' was the second [[orbit]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> of the [[Hythrope sector]]'s [[Edan system]] within the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[Trailing Sectors]] regions,<ref name="Trailing" /> situated in grid square M-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> By [[2 BBY]],<ref name="2 BBY">In ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', the report regarding the rebel locations that included Edan II is set between the introduction of the [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]] and the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance]], which are both dated to [[2 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> it was being used as an operations headquarters by an [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|organized rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]] secretly led by [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]]. Edan II was noted on a [[Star map|map]] of [[the galaxy]] made for Mothma that listed the rebellion's [[safe world]]s, [[starfighter]] hubs at level five or higher, operations headquarters, [[shadow planet]]s, and deep [[Realspace|space]] caches. The [[map]] was ultimately archived by [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]] in ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', a compilation of non-electronic documents.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
Edan II was introduced into the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] through its mention in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1997]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] boxed set ''[[Introductory Adventure Game]]'', which was authored by [[Peter M. Schweighofer|Peter Schweighofer]] and [[Stephen Luminati]] and published by [[West End Games]] as part of ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="IAG">''[[Introductory Adventure Game]]''</ref>
