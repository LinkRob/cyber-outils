'''Candovant''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> in the [[Candovant system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Colonies]] region of [[the galaxy]], situated along the [[Hydian Way]] and home to the [[Candovantan]] species.<ref name="Galaxy Map" /><ref name="Shattered Empire 4" /> In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Alphabet">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Attack on Candovant|targeted]] Candovant as part of [[Operation: Cinder]], attempting to destroy the world after the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Shattered Empire 4" /> The world ultimately saw the devastation of civilizations at the hands of Imperial [[Imperial Navy|fleets]].<ref name="Alphabet" /> Thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the Battle of Endor, Candovant was a member world in the [[New Republic]], where it was represented by [[Zygli Bruss]] in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]].<ref name="TFA VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Candovant was first mentioned in the comic [[Shattered Empire 4|''Shattered Empire'' 4]], written by [[Greg Rucka]], penciled by [[Marco Checchetto]]<ref name="Shattered Empire 4" /> and released on [[October 21]], [[2015]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52682/journey_to_star_wars_the_force_awakens_-_shattered_empire_2015_4|text=Journey To Star Wars: The Force Awakens - Shattered Empire (2015) #4}}</ref>
