'''Amplifier droids''' were hovering [[droid]]s used to amplify sounds. A number of them were located on the [[planet]] [[Jelucan]] about 16 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], when an Imperial [[proctor]] announced the names of the [[Imperial cadet|students]] accepted into the various [[Imperial Academy|Imperial Academies]] across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />

After the founding of the [[New Republic]], [[Senator|members]] of the [[New Republic Senate|Senate]] utilized amplifier droids when addressing their fellow Senators.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>
