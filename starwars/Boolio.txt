'''Boolio''' was an [[Ovissian]] [[Gender|male]] who worked as a [[Mining|mine]] [[overseer]] at the [[Sinta Glacier Colony]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. He was sympathetic to the Resistance and covertly sent supplies their way. After a First Order inspection team visited the colony, Boolio discovered a [[datafile]] in his office labeled as highly valuable intelligence for the Resistance from a [[Treason|traitor]] within the First Order.

Not wanting to transmit the data, Boolio contacted the Resistance and arranged for them to meet him face to face at the colony. The [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was [[Mission to Sinta Glacier Colony|sent]] to the meeting, and upon their arrival Boolio transferred the file to the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] on board the [[freighter]]. After the ''Falcon'' left, Boolio was [[Death|killed]] by the First Order and his severed head presented to the [[First Order Supreme Council]] by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].

==Biography==
===Traitor's pawn===
{{Quote|Boolio, good to see you. You got something for us?''"<br />"''From a new ally. A spy in the First Order.''"<br />"''A spy? Who?''"<br />"''I don't know.|Finn greets Boolio|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:Finn-Boolio.png|thumb|left|250px|Boolio worked with Finn and the Resistance.]]
Boolio was an [[Ovissian]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Databank" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] on [[Sinta IV]]<ref name="TROSVD" /> in [[1 ABY]].<ref name="Dates" /> He worked as a [[Mining|mine]] [[overseer]] at the [[Sinta Glacier Colony]], which extracted [[Water|ice]] cores from the [[Sinta Glacier]] in order to harvest [[malsarr]]. During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], he was sympathetic to the Resistance,<ref name="TROSVD" /> becoming one of its top [[Information broker|informants]]<ref name="Junior">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> and managing to covertly divert surplus materials<ref name="TROSVD" /> and minerals<ref name="TROSEE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> from the mines on to [[transport]] [[starship]]s friendly to their cause.<ref name="TROSVD" /> He managed to make multiple shipments to the Resistance over a period of several [[Standard month|months]], two of which were picked up by the former [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn]].<ref name="TROSEE" />

When a discrepancy in the colony's ledgers was discovered a First Order team of inspectors was sent to investigate. The inspectors left without comment, after which Boolio discovered a datafile that had been left on his office [[datapad]]. The datafile claimed to contain game-changing intelligence on the First Order for the Resistance, but Boolio was unable to decrypt the file any further. Once he had authenticated it as a message from a high-ranking [[First Order officer]] based on the ciphers used, he contacted the Resistance via intermediaries,<ref name="TROSVD" /> since as a mine overseer he did not have access to a secure frequency.<ref name="TROSEE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

===Meeting with the Resistance===
{{Quote|Finn, we're about to be cooked!''"<br />"''We're almost there!|Poe Dameron warns Finn about the incoming First Order forces|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:BoolioDateTransfer.png|thumb|right|250px|Boolio transferred a message to the Resistance from a traitor within the First Order.]]
Not wanting to risk revealing the intelligence he had been given, Boolio's message to the Resistance promised them that he could provide a [[regulator]] that they need to repair the ''[[Tantive IV]]'', the [[CR90 corvette]] of Resistance leader [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="TROSVD" /> In order to ensure they came quickly, he offered an extremely low price for the part and claimed that it was critical and would not last long.<ref name="TROSEE" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Dates" /> the Resistance then [[Mission to Sinta Glacier Colony|dispatched]] a crew from the [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]] in the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to meet with Boolio face to face at the colony.<ref name="TROSVD" />

The [[freighter]] passed through the ice tunnels of the colony before parking beneath a hatch into a dim icy corridor<ref name="TROSEE" /> where the Ovissian waited. Finn and the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] greeted Boolio through a hatch in the roof of the ''Falcon'', at which point the overseer revealed the true reason he had called them to the colony.<ref name="Episode IX" />

===Data handover===
{{Quote|Captured at the glacier colony, sir. A traitor.|Admiral Griss presents Boolio|<ref name="TROSEE" />}}
After hurling down one end of a cable for Finn to plug into R2-D2, Boolio admitted he did not know who the traitor within the First Order was but encouraged them to deliver the data to Organa. When asked how he could be repaid, Boolio told them to "win the war." Once the transfer was complete, Boolio quickly hauled the cable back up so that the ''Falcon'' could make a hasty departure, as First Order [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s and [[TIE/wi Interceptor]]s began to chase it. The crew of the ''Falcon'' successfully delivered Boolio's intelligence to Organa and the rest of the Resistance, which informed them that [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] had returned and provided the First Order with the [[Sith Eternal]]'s fleet, the [[Final Order]], that would soon attack all remaining free worlds so he can establish a new [[Sith Empire]] and rule the galaxy as [[Galactic Emperor]] again.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Providing the Resistance with this intelligence ultimately cost Boolio his life, as he was captured by the First Order and taken to the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']]. Dragged between a group of stormtroopers, Boolio was presented to [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] by [[Admiral]] [[Frantis Griss]]. Ren immediately activated his [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and [[Death|decapitated]] the Ovissian.<ref name="TROSEE" /> Boolio's severed head was then presented to the [[First Order Supreme Council]] by Ren.<ref name="Episode IX" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|We have a spy in our ranks who just sent a message to the Resistance.|Kylo Ren presents Boolio's head to the Supreme Council|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:BoolioHead.png|thumb|left|250px|Boolio was killed by the First Order.]]
Ren declared to the Council that he knew that there was a traitor within the First Order who had sent a message to the Resistance; however, he was unaware that the traitor who had used Boolio to contact the Resistance was General [[Armitage Hux]], one of the council members who sat before him.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Hux was later executed by [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]] aboard the ''Steadfast'' after he realized his intentions. Though he lost his life, Boolio's actions allowed the Resistance to hatch a plan to deal with Darth Sidious' return. His wish for the Resistance to win the war would be honored when they defeated the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]] at the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|How do we thank you?''"<br />"''Win the war!|Finn and Boolio|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
Boolio had [[Color|greenish-yellow]] skin,<ref name="TROSEE" /> golden [[eye]]s, and yellow horns, and stood 1.78 [[meter]]s tall. He was good natured,<ref name="TROSVD" /> and had a horn-to-horn grin when he greeted Finn. The Ovissian was nervous while transferring the data and was worried that if the spy within the First Order had discovered that he was aiding the Resistance, then the rest of the First Order would soon discover him.<ref name="TROSEE" /> Finn incorrectly suspected that Boolio would not have risked his life only to confirm the return of Palpatine,<ref name="Junior" /> but as the Ovissian had not been able to decrypt the datafile, he had risked it without knowing the contents of the file at all.<ref name="TROSVD" /> After he was captured, he stared up defiantly at Ren<ref name="TROSEE" /> without a hint of fear<ref name="Junior" /> before being killed.<ref name="TROSEE" />

==Equipment==
Boolio wore an orange mining thermal [[Clothing|worksuit]]<ref name="TROSEE" /> that was insulated with a lining of heat-conductive filaments. He had a personal temperature-control unit mounted on his chest for the low-temperature suit with an orange and brown ice-abseiling harness. He used a wrist-mounted [[comlink]] and data-storage unit on his left [[Limb|arm]].<ref name="TROSVD" />

===Design===
[[File:Alien_Pilot_01.png|thumb|right|150px|Alternate concept art of a red-skinned Boolio was drawn by Jake Lunt Davies.]]
{{Quote|Boolio getting decapitated factored a lot into the design. Holding a decapitated head is actually quite difficult.|Neal Scanlan|<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]''</ref>}}
According to creature workshop head [[Neal Scanlan]], the fact that Boolio would be decapitated factored heavily into the character's design, as holding a severed head could be difficult. It was decided that holding the head by the hair was too brutal, so curved horns were chosen. During sculpting, the horns were moved around so that it could be swung down and smack onto a table. Where the decapitation cut was be made, the angle and placement of the horns and the width of Boolio's jaw were also all thought about in terms of what would be needed for the shot of the head. Scanlan demoed the head for producer [[Kathleen Kennedy]], director [[J.J. Abrams]] and actor [[Adam Driver]].<ref name="Art" />

A number of pieces of concept art of Boolio were created by creature concept designer [[Jake Lunt Davies]], including a piece titled "Alien Pilot 01" that depicted Boolio as a red-skinned alien with a number of head tentacles similar to a [[Nautolan]] that Ren could hold rather than horns. Lunt Davies also created "Boolio 05" and "Boolio Gunner 05," the latter of which depicted Boolio in a gunner's seat similar to those found in the ''Millennium Falcon''. One of the unused pieces of Boolio concept art became [[Ochi]].<ref name="Art" />
