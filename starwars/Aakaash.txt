'''Aakaash''' was a [[star]] of the [[star system]] of the [[Aakaash system|same name]],<ref name="Timelines" /> which was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Oplovis sector]],<ref name="Vow" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It was situated in grid square M-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Timelines" /> and it gave off [[Color|silver]] rays of light. In the traditional folk beliefs of the system, the silver light of the star symbolized justice.<ref name="Vow" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aakaash first appeared in the [[Wikipedia:Written Chinese|Chinese]] web-novel ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''.<ref name="Vow" /> It was first named on a map created for the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' and published as part of a blog post on [[StarWars.com]] on [[May 27]], [[2022]] prior<ref name="TimelinesSWcom">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-lucasfilm-publishing-behind-the-page-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the Lucasfilm Publishing Behind the Page Panel}}</ref> to the book's release during [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-europe-2023-exclusives|text=Check Out ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Exclusives from Hasbro, the LEGO Group, and More}}</ref> of [[April 7]] through [[April 10|10]], [[2023]].<ref name="CelebrationSchedule">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-panel-schedule|text=Plan Ahead with the ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Panel Schedule}}</ref>
