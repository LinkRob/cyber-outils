[[Clone Commander]] "[[Doom]]" [[Commanding officer|led]] a '''unit''' of [[clone trooper]]s alongside [[Jedi General]]s [[Tiplar]] and [[Tiplee]]. They utilized [[M3 Bulwark Blast Shield]]s of their own variation. Troopers of this unit were also armed with [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon]]s and [[Electromagnetic pulse grenade|Electro Magnetic pulse grenades]].
==History==
[[File:Tiplar_and_Doom_marines.png|thumb|left|250px|Doom and Master Tiplar rally the unit to battle.]]
In [[19 BBY]], the Republic battled the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] on the [[Ringo Vinda space station|space station]] ring around the [[planet]] [[Ringo Vinda]]. Led by [[Jedi General]]s [[Tiplar]], [[Tiplee]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Doom]], the unit advanced and fought alongside the [[501st Legion]]. During the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda|battle]], the unit became severely depleted. Attempting to break the deadlock, the Republic made a desperate push toward the Separatists and managed to reach the communications center.<ref name="The Unknown" />

There, they discussed a battle plan to seize the Separatist command center. [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives|CT-5555]] "Fives" and [[Tup|CT-5385]] "Tup" were ordered to rally ten of their men to reinforce Doom's unit as they advanced. But as Republic troops reached the command center, Tup fell into a [[Order 66|trance]] and, before anyone could react, shot Tiplar dead.<ref name="The Unknown" />

Upon Tiplar's death, the [[clone trooper]]s lost their momentum and [[droid]] reinforcements arrived. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] ordered a retreat as Tup was apprehended and escorted away.<ref name="The Unknown" />
