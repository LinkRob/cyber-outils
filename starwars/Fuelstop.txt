A '''fuelstop''' was located in [[Milvayne City]] on the [[planet]] [[Milvayne]]. It had a [[repair rig]] where [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] repaired the [[assassin droid]] [[0-0-0]]'s [[Limb|legs]] after [[Chase on Milvayne|escaping]] [[Tam Posla]] and the [[Milvayne Authority]]. Upon leaving the fuelstop, Aphra and Triple-Zero were pursued by the [[Hunting|hunters]] [[Nokk]] and [[Winloss]].<ref name="Aphra 29" />
