A '''ship''' was owned by [[Chardri Tage]] and his partner and co-[[pilot]], [[Tamsin]], in the [[Standard year|year]] after the formation of the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] sent the pair in their [[starship]] to locate a former [[Jedi]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], though he did not tell them whom he sought. They located her on a [[Unidentified planet (Thabeska system)|planet]] in the [[Thabeska system]] when her [[Unidentified light freighter (Thabeska)|freighter]] was pursued by an [[Unidentified Black Sun agent|agent]] of the [[Black Sun]] criminal syndicate. They fired upon the other [[Unidentified Black Sun ship|ship]], forcing the agent to flee, then locked Tano's in a [[tractor beam]].<ref name="Ahsoka" />
