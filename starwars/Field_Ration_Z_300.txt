'''Field Ration Z 300''' was a type of [[ration bar]].<ref name="Trivia" /> Its name was printed in [[Aurebesh]] on the [[Color|yellow]] wrapping. [[Clone Force 99]] member [[Omega]] offered one to the younger [[miner]] [[Benni Baro]] as a thank you for helping her and the rest of the squad attempt to recover their [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Marauder (Omicron-class)|Marauder]]'', which Baro had originally stolen. Baro was taken aback by Omega's kind gesture, asking why she would give him her ration bar, but she told him that she was thankful that he was helping them. Omega had witnessed the reality that Baro and the other miners were underfed when Baro helped her sneak inside [[Mokkotown]] to retrieve their ship, and thus offered the young boy her [[Foodstuff|food]].<ref name="Retrieval" />

==Behind the scenes==
