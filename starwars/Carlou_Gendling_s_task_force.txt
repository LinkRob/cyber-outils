A '''task force''' commanded by [[Admiral]] [[Carlou Gendling]] notably participated in a [[Battle over Umbara|battle above]] the [[planet]] [[Umbara]]. The battle proved to be costly on Gendling's [[task force]] as one of his [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvettes]] was destroyed and another seriously damaged with half of its crew dead or wounded.

[[Imperial Navy]] [[Admiral]] [[Carlou Gendling]]'s [[task force]] consisted of six [[Starship|ships]], one of which was the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Foremost]]'', which served as its [[flagship]]. The task force also possessed four [[corvette]]s, at least two of which were [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvettes]]; and one [[light cruiser]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

==History==
[[File:ThunderWasp-Thrawn4.png|thumb|left|300px|The task force was assisted at Umbara by the ''Thunder Wasp''.]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], Admiral Carlou Gendling [[Commanding officer|commanded]] a task force in the [[Galactic Empire]]. At some point, the criminal [[Nevil Cygni]] instigated an uprising on the [[Expansion Region]] [[planet]] [[Umbara]], which [[Nightswan's campaign|aided]] him by allowing the Empire to seize the world's mining and refining facilities. Gendling was sent to respond to the uprising in the ''Formost'', along with two ''Raider''-class corvettes, while his other ships, two more corvettes and a light [[cruiser]], were sent off to investigate a problem in another [[Star system|system]]. Aiding Gendling's ships at Umbara was the [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] ''[[Thunder Wasp]]''.<ref name="Thrawn" />

At Umbara, Gendling, assisted by the [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] ''[[Thunder Wasp]]'', [[Battle over Umbara|fought]] against the [[Nightswan's campaign|rebellious]] [[Nightswan]]'s forces, although the admiral's ships were heavily damaged, with one of the Raiders being destroyed. However, the ''Thunder Wasp'' managed to defeat the enemy [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]], winning the battle for the Empire.<ref name="Thrawn" />
