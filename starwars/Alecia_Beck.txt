'''Alecia Beck''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who served as a [[Senior Commander|senior]] [[commander]] in the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]. In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Yavin]], she was tasked with hunting down [[Lieutenant]] [[Caluan Ematt]], a member of the enemy [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].

==Biography==
Alecia Beck was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who came to serve in the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], the largest of the two intelligence agencies of the [[Galactic Empire]]. During her first tour with the ISB, Beck, then a mere [[lieutenant]], caught her [[Unidentified training officer (Alecia Beck)|training officer]] selling secrets for [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. Although he was a full [[captain]], Beck confronted him in the maintenance bay aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Vehement]]''. The captain attempted to [[Death|kill]] her, using a [[laser cutter]] he had found on a nearby workbench. The two then engaged in a fight. Beck managed to get the upper hand, although she lost her left [[eye]] in the confrontation. Consequently, Beck received a promotion and a commendation.<ref name="Smuggler's Run" />

At some point, Beck attempted to request a meeting with [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]]. [[Hendri Underholt]], Mon Mothma's [[aide]], lied and claimed to Beck that the senator had already left on a fact-finding mission to [[Goroth Prime|Goroth]]. Underholt earned praise from Mon Mothma for her reply.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

Around the time of the [[Battle of Yavin]], Beck had been made a [[Senior Commander]] in the ISB, making her one of the few women holding high ranks in the Empire. She was tasked with hunting down the [[Shrikes]], a special recon unit of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. When word came out that the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]] had been destroyed by the Alliance in the [[Yavin system]], all senior commanders of the ISB were ordered to move on their current targets immediately.<ref name="Smuggler's Run" />

[[File:Beck_apprehends_Solo.png|thumb|left|250px|Alecia Beck encountered Han Solo when he attempted to rescue Caluan Ematt.]]
Beck and her men [[Ambush on Taanab|ambushed]] the Shrikes on [[Taanab]]. All Shrikes were killed, with the exception of their leader, [[Caluan Ematt]], who managed to escape. Ematt sent a burst transmission to the Alliance, letting his allies know of the Tanaab incident. Eventually, Beck and the ISB discovered that Ematt had made his way to [[Cyrkon]], in [[Hutt Space]]. By capturing Ematt, Commander Beck hoped she would learn where the Alliance had relocated after the Battle of Yavin. The ''Vehement'' was positioned to block all flight traffic to and from Cyrkon, while Beck herself deployed to the surface, accompanied by four squads of white-clad [[stormtrooper]]s. Upon searching the planetary starport, Beck identified the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', a battered freighter that belonged to [[Corellian]] [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]], a known member of the Alliance. When Solo, Ematt and the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] entered the docking bay that housed the ''Falcon'', Beck and her men were waiting for them. However, Beck had underestimated the sheer audacity of Han Solo. The Corellian smuggler destroyed the [[Deflector shield|magnetic shield]] that protected the docking bay from Cyrkon's polluted air. In the ensuing chaos, Solo, Chewbacca and Ematt managed to board the ''Falcon'' and escape.<ref name="Smuggler's Run" /> Commander Beck gave a report on the situation to [[Admiral]] [[J. Ras-Teker]], and immediately began to calculate their possible destinations, determined not to let Ematt escape her a third time.<ref name="SR anim" /> As a result of her failure during the mission, Beck lost a promotion.<ref name="TPatS">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>

Around the same time as the [[Escape from Cloud City|annexation]] of [[Cloud City]], which brought a major supplier of [[tibanna]] gas under Imperial control, Beck was dispatched as an ambassador to the moon of [[Madurs]], where the Empire hoped to secure [[carnium]]. However, Beck told the local government to prepare for an art expo to hide the regime's true intentions. As she encouraged [[Prime Minister]] [[Dreand Yens]] and company to build up a small city of visitor apartments, the Empire prepared for the coming mining operation, with a [[Black tower|space station]] in orbit scanning for the best place to mine and settling on a spot next to a populated city. When negotiations came, Beck revealed the Empire's true desire for the carnium, but Yens rejected the Empire's offer to buy it for much less than it was worth. Beck departed on a shuttle, and, before the day had even turned to night, the Empire attacked by dropping its space station down from orbit into the ice. The impact killed over two thousand people and destroyed the city.<ref name="TPatS" />

Beck oversaw the mining effort even as it began to destabilize Madurs, with Yens as her reluctant puppet leader. After the [[Battle of Endor]], which Beck believed to be rebel propaganda, she received orders to finish up. The Imperials gave promises that they would pay reparations before leaving, but, even if true, they would have come too late for Madurs anyway. However, the operation began to unravel when Solo and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] arrived on Madurs during their honeymoon, as Organa hoped to begin talks with Yens about joining the [[New Republic]]. Beck was eager for a chance to kill Solo, but both rebels managed to escape her grasp. As both joined the local resistance movement under Yens and attacked the mining operation, Beck and the other officers attempted to escape Madurs, leaving their stormtroopers and drill behind, on someone's orders. However, the arrival of [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]] under [[Shara Bey]] cut off Beck's escape, leaving her to fall into rebel custody. Organa was particularly interested to learn who had been giving Beck orders, and the defeat of the Imperial force on Madurs allowed the local government and New Republic a chance to begin stabilizing the water moon.<ref name="TPatS" />

During a [[mission to Vetine]] with [[Luke Skywalker]], Bey impersonated Commander Beck in an attempt to infiltrate the secret [[Imperial base (Vetine)|Imperial labs]] there. However, the two overlooked the fact that Beck was missing her left eye, thus blowing their cover and forcing them to fight their way out.<ref name="Shattered Empire">[[Shattered Empire 4|''Shattered Empire'' 4]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Alecia Beck was a human woman who was incredibly loyal to Emperor Palpatine. She possessed a cybernetic eye and wore her blond hair tied back in a bun, which appeared to be so tight Leia Organa almost thought she was bald. As Organa also observed, she walked with a confidence seen only in those with a deadly type of experience. While some people began to claim the Empire had fallen with the Battle of Endor, Beck regarded the incident as rebel propaganda. Beck had a particular hate for Han Solo, blaming him for a lost promotion and hoping for a chance to kill him. Upon her capture, she ranted about the man. Beck was smarter than the average Imperial stormtrooper. She was willing to abandon her forces on Madurs, but Organa and Solo suspected that was only because someone ordered her to.<ref name="TPatS" />
