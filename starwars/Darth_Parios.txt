'''Darth Parios''' was a [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] of the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|reconstituted Sith Empire]].

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Parios was intended to appear in Patch 1.3.0 of ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', but was ultimately cut. The character exists within the game files with a placeholder model.
