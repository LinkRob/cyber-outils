The '''Commission for a Safe and Secure Republic''' was a nonprofit think tank based on [[Level 5121]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the waning [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. One of their members was the [[Xidelphiad]] [[Gender|female]] [[Sannab Ro]], who created a [[propaganda]] [[The Military Creation Act|poster]] inciting citizens to support the [[Military Creation Act]].<ref name="Propaganda" />
