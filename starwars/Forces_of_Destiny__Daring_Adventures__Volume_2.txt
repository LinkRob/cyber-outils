'''''Forces of Destiny: Daring Adventures: Volume 2''''' is the second chapter book of the Forces of Destiny: Daring Adventures series based on the animated micro-series ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]'', which adapt select episodes of the animated micro-series, while adding scenes and dialogue not originally present. It was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[October 3]], [[2017]].

It, alongside ''[[Forces of Destiny: Daring Adventures: Volume 1]]'', was collected into an audiobook on [[December 15]], 2017.<ref name="Audio">{{Blackstone|url=star-wars-forces-of-destiny-daring-adventures-volumes-1-and-2|text=Star Wars Forces of Destiny: Daring Adventures: Volumes 1 & 2}}</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''Based on the all-new animated micro-series spotlighting some of Star Wars' greatest heroes! Three exciting adventures featuring rebel leader Princess Leia, Rogue One's Jyn Erso, and Jedi apprentice Ahsoka Tano! Includes introductions by Maz Kanata and art from the series.''

==Plot summary==
{{Plot-link|Star Wars Forces of Destiny}}

==Continuity==
The book adapts three episodes of the micro-series ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]'': ''[[The Padawan Path]]'', ''[[Beasts of Echo Base]]'', and ''[[The Stranger (episode)|The Stranger]]'', with additional scenes and introductions by Maz Kanata.
