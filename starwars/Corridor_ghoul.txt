'''Corridor ghouls''' were creatures that lived in sewers in the [[Coruscant Underworld|underworld]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Star Wars 28" /> Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The flashbacks of [[Star Wars (2020) 50|''Star Wars'' (2020) 50]] take place approximately twenty-two [[Standard year|years]] after [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|ascension]] to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]], meaning they must occur around [[3 ABY]]. The flashbacks also take place after the events of [[Star Wars (2020) 43|''Star Wars'' (2020) 43]], which occur after the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Droids]]'' crossover event, an event {{SW|url=news/marvel-dark-droids-interview|text=Charles Soule Unleashes ''Dark Droids''}} places after ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'', which is dated to 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] by ''Timelines''. As ''Star Wars'' (2020) 28 takes place between ''War of the Bounty Hunters'' and ''Star Wars'' (2020) 43, its events must take place around 3 ABY.</ref> a family of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Desertion|defectors]] encountered a corridor ghoul as they attempted to escape from [[Coruscant underworld police]]. [[Bevelyn]] shot and [[Death|killed]] the creature, though it alerted the [[police officer]]s to their location. Not long after, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Luke Skywalker]], who had [[Mission to Coruscant|traveled]] to Coruscant to rescue the family, [[Taming beasts|summoned]] a number of corridor ghouls to attack the police, which allowed for his and the family's escape.<ref name="Star Wars 28" />
