An '''automated saline pump''', known simply as a '''saline pump''', was a device used by [[Clawdite]] [[Changeling|shapeshifters]] to modify the amount of [[Salt|saline]] within their [[Brain|limbic systems]]. This allowed the [[species]] to shapeshift into a broader range of lifeforms, as the amount of saline within a Clawdite's [[Limb|body]] directly affected the size and mass of their transformations. Automated saline pumps were illegal to own, and were sold on the [[black market]] for 2,500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

==Behind the scenes==
Automated saline pumps were first mentioned in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']],<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> a [[2017]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="A Fistful of Credits">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/2/2/a-fistful-of-credits|text=A Fistful of Credits}}</ref>
