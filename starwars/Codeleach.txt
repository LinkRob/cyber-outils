The '''Codeleach''' was a jewel-covered [[skeleton]] of a [[Metellos]] [[wyrdwyrm]]. It was held together with [[kiirium]] solder and was 50 [[centimeter]]s tall. It was being stored in the [[Varcinius Repository]] when [[Gallandro]] and his crew stole it for a client who agreed to pay them 1,575,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="SG" />
