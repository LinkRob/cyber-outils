The '''Celestial Wake''' was a location in the [[Celestial Wake system]], within the [[Kriz sector]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Western Reaches]],<ref name="Western Reaches" /> in grid square K-19 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Tobias Beckett]]'s [[death]] to [[10 BBY]], therefore his actions on the fringe of the Celestial Wake took place by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the criminal [[Tobias Beckett]] and his [[Beckett's gang|crew]] spent three [[Standard month|months]] on the fringes of the Celestial Wake, waylaying [[Mining Guild]] [[oreship]]s. When [[bartender]] [[Tivoche Bilure]] learned about Beckett's crew actions near the Celestial Wake, he [[Author|wrote]] about them in his [[Midnight's book|journal]].<ref name="Solo Tales from Vandor" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Celestial Wake was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] replica journal ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Solo Tales from Vandor" /> The [[Celestial Wake/Legends|Celestial Wake]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story "[[The Brink]]," written by [[Justin Lambros]], illustrated by [[Rick Lacy]], and published in the [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures Volume 4|fourth volume]] of the comic series [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures (graphic novels)|''Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures'']]<ref name="CWAVol4">{{CWACite|4|The Brink}}</ref> on [[October 26]], [[2005]].<ref name="DH-Link">{{DarkHorse|url=Books/10-920/Star-Wars-Clone-Wars-Adventures-Vol-4-TPB|text=Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures Vol. 4 TPB}}</ref>
