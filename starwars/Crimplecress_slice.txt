'''Crimplecress slices''' were a [[Foodstuff|dish]] that the [[human]] [[moisture farmer]] [[Owen Lars]] had stored alongside [[roasted duodecipede leg stick]]s in the food storage compartment of the [[Zephyr-G swoop]] he used at his [[Lars family|family's]] [[Lars homestead|moisture farm]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="CV New" /> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}, as depicted in ''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]'', to [[22 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], crimplecress slices were first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> from the [[2002]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', which was written by [[Curtis Saxton]] and marked the [[Crimplecress slice/Legends|food's]] first mention in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Cross-Sections">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
