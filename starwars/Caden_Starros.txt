'''Caden Starros''' was a [[Gender|male]] member of the [[Starros Clan]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. According to a family story told to his great-granddaughter&mdash;the [[human]] [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]]<ref name="A Test of Courage" />&mdash;by [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> one of Caden's [[Caden Starros' enemy|enemies]] [[Thief|stole]] his [[Starship|ship]] and left him stranded on a [[Unidentified minor moon|minor moon]]. In the story, Caden then followed the man to<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> the [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Cad Bane">{{Databank|cad-bane-biography-gallery|Cad Bane Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 18}}</ref> [[Orondia]] to take his revenge.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while speaking with the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] about the [[Attack on the Steady Wing|death]] of the former's [[Jedi Master]], [[Douglas Sunvale]], Avon questioned whether the Jedi wanted to [[Gwishi|find]] [[Klinith Da|those]] who had [[Death|killed]] him. After mentally noting she could not believe neither Jedi would want revenge for Sunvale's death, the scientist added that if someone she loved had been hurt, she would only want revenge. Avon then recalled the story about Caden, and added that regardless of whether it was actually true, it sounded more sensible to her than forgetting about being wronged.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

==Behind the scenes==
Caden Starros was mentioned in the [[2021]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published as part of<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
