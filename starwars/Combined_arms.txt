'''Combined arms''' was an approach to [[Ground warfare|surface warfare]] in which multiple military types (from [[infantry]] to [[armored cavalry]]) were integrated into an operation. The [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Army|army]] favored that strategy, which forced the enemy to defend against one type of attack while leaving them vulnerable to another.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> For example, [[Major]] [[Newland|Marquand]], who led the [[Tempest Force|Imperial forces]] during the ground portion of the [[Battle of Endor]], was familiar with combined arms attacks and cohesive cavalry tactics.<ref name="Tempest Force">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/scene-to-stat-tempest-force-battle-force|text=Scene to Stat: Tempest Force Battle Force}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] also used the combined arms approach. For example, it would field [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|assault gunships]] fire on [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|enemy]] vehicles as they delivered [[clone trooper]]s to the battlefield.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> Another example came with having clones fight alongside armored vehicles, like the [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]], during battles.<ref name="Scene to Stats">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/scene-to-stats-clone-commander-cody|text=Scene to Stats: Clone Commander Cody|date=2023-3-15}}</ref>
