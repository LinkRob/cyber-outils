==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Fennesa-RebuildTheGalaxy.png|thumb|left|140px|Fennesa as seen in LEGO Star Wars: Rebuild the Galaxy]]
Fennesa was first mentioned in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[Creature Feature]] series article [[Mountain Nerf]] that was written by [[Jeff Quick]] and added to [[Wizards.com]] on [[February 6]], [[2003]].<ref name="Mountain Nerf">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20030206nerf|series=Creature Feature|text=Mountain Nerf}}</ref> In ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Rebuild the Galaxy]]'', Fennesa was a [[planet]] where the [[human]] [[nerf herder]] [[Sig Greebling]] resided. On Fennesa, Greebling was content with herding [[nerf]]s and sharing stories of the heroics of the [[Jedi]].<ref name="D23">{{SW|url=news/d23-2024-lucasfilm-reveals|text=D23 2024: All the Star Wars News from D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event}}</ref>
