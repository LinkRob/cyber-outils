The '''Arron Prime system''' was located in the [[Cassander sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in [[the galaxy]]. The [[Star system|system]] was located in grid square L-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Arron Prime system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the article "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Arron Prime]] is a location where it was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Fallen Empire]]'', the fifth digital expansion to the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', released in [[2015]].<ref name="KotFE">''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Fallen Empire]]''</ref>
