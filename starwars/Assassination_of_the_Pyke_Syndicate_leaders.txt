During the [[War against the Pykes|war]] between the [[Fett gotra]] and the [[Pyke Syndicate]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], the gotra's [[Master Assassin]] [[Fennec Shand]] undertook a mission to '''assassinate''' the enemy leadership in an attempt to stop the syndicate's [[Battle of Mos Espa|invasion]] of the [[city]] [[Mos Espa]]. The leadership was based in the [[spaceport]] [[Mos Eisley]]'s [[Desert Survey Office]]. Shand was successful, eliminating the heads of all the [[Mos Espa]] crime families, the [[Pyke Boss|Pyke boss]] and his [[Pyke sentinel|guards]], as well as the corrupt [[Mayor of Mos Espa]] [[Mok Shaiz]], who had allied himself with the Pyke Syndicate. With Fett's victory, his ally [[Din Djarin]] left Tatooine<ref name="Chapter 7" /> and eventually reunited with a [[Mandalorian secret covert|Mandalorian covert]], which was [[Skirmish outside a Mandalorian covert|attacked]] by a [[Lake monster|dinosaur turtle]].<ref name="Chapter 17" />
