'''Baktoid Fleet Ordnance''' was a [[droid]]-manufacturing company<ref name="Collapse" /> that was a [[division]] of the larger [[Baktoid Combat Automata]] firm.<ref name="BR2 45" /> Baktoid Fleet produced the [[HMP droid gunship]]. Their [[gunship]] was used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Collapse" />

==Description==
The manufacturing company Baktoid Fleet Ordnance was formed under the [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Techno Union]] corporation, as a builder of automated [[droid]] [[weapon]]ry. Alongsides its sister company [[Baktoid Armor Workshop]], it was a subisidiary to [[Baktoid Combat Automata]] and was composed of [[Geonosian]] workers.<ref name="BR2 45" /> One of their projects was the [[HMP droid gunship|HMP ''Predator'' gunship]], a heavily [[Weapon|armed]] [[airspeeder]] designed and manufactured for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in collaboration with the [[Haor Chall Engineering Corporation|Haor Chall Engineering]] companies.<ref name="Collapse" />

==History==
At some point in the [[Republic Era]], Baktoid Fleet Ordnance was founded as a subisidary to the Techno Union. When the [[Clone Wars]] occurred,<ref name="BR2 45" /> fought from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> between the [[Galactic Republic]] and Confederacy of Independent Systems, the company became one of the Confederacy's manufacturers for automated droid weaponry. Envisioning a heavily armed and automated [[repulsorlift]] airspeeder alongside Haor Chall Engineering, Baktoid Fleet Ordnance designed the HMP ''Predator'' gunship to replace the Separatists' [[pilot]]-dependant [[assault ship]]s.<ref name="BR2 45" />

[[Phlac-Arphocc Automata Industries]] helped to finalize the product, by providing Baktoid and Haor Chall with their stockpiles of exotic and superdense [[Radiation|radioactive]] [[fuel]]s. While development took [[time]], the gunships were eventually deployed directly from the production lines two [[Standard year|years]] into the Clone Wars. Baktoid and Haor Chall's products were then incorporated into the Confederacy and introduced at the [[Onderonian Civil War]]<ref name="BR2 45" /> in [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> where four nearly eliminated a [[Onderon rebels|band]] of [[Onderonian]]s rebelling against the Separatist occupation.<ref name="BR2 45" /> From that point onward, the ''Predator'' gained a terrifying reputation for its [[droid brain]] as one of the most hated assets in the [[Confederacy military|Separatist military]].<ref name="Collapse" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baktoid Fleet Ordnance was first mentioned simultaneously in the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' reference books ''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith: The Visual Dictionary]]'', both of which released on the same day in [[2005]]. The company was confirmed to be [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which was released in [[2015]].
