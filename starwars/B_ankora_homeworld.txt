The '''homeworld''' of the [[B'ankora]] [[species]] was a [[planet]] in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Catalyst" /> At some point before around [[1300 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the planet suffered a [[B'ankora homeworld cataclysm|cataclysmic collision]] with a [[meteor]]. In response the surviving B'ankora were given a piece of [[grass]] land on the [[Galactic Republic]]'s capital [[Coruscant]] and were ultimately granted the land by by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Chasen Piian]] to use in perpetuity so they could resume their sedentary lifestyle with humble homes, fields, and trails, which became known as the [[B'ankor Refuge]].<ref name="Catalyst" />

==Behind the scenes==
The B'ankora homeworld was fist mentioned in ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'', written by [[James Luceno]]<ref name="Catalyst" /> and published on [[November 15]], [[2016]]<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/7879/catalyst-star-wars-by-james-luceno|text=Catalyst (Star Wars)}}</ref> as a prequel to the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Catalyst" />

The 2016 reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]], originally dated the B'ankor being granted the B'ankor Refuge to [[131 BBY]],<ref name="Rebel Dossier date">According to ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'' the [[B'ankora]] were granted the [[B'ankor Refuge]] 112 [[Standard year|years]] before the end of [[Proclamation of the New Order|the end]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. As such it could be deduced that the B'ankor Refuge was established in [[131 BBY]].</ref> but in ''Catalyst'' it is stated that they had lived in the Refuge "for more than fifty generations" and had planted a copse of gargantuan [[tree]]s "centuries earlier" by the time of the novel.<ref name="Catalyst" /> [[2023]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' later instead placed the event to [[1300 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Because ''Timelines'' is the newer source and it's date lines up better with what is established in ''Catalyst'', this article assumes it to be correct.
