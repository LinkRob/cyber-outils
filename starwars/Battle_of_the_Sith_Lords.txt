'''''Battle of the Sith Lords''''' (internally codenamed "'''Damage'''" and given the working title '''''Maul''''') was the tagline for a ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]] once in development by [[Red Fly Studio]].<ref name="GI subscription article" /> The game was canceled on [[June 24]], [[2011]]. Red Fly employees linked the game's cancelation to ''Star Wars'' creator [[George Lucas]]' decision to sell [[Lucasfilm]] and [[LucasArts]] to [[The Walt Disney Company]].<ref name="GI article">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/04/10/video-from-the-darth-maul-game-you-will-never-get-to-play.aspx|text=Video From The Darth Maul Game You'll Never Get To Play|author=Reiner, Andrew|date=2014-04-10|archiveurl=https://archive.is/Lr3Yr}}</ref>

==Gameplay==
[[File:Battle_of_the_Sith_Lords_gameplay_storyboard.jpg|thumb|right|150px|Storyboard concepts of the core gameplay of "Maul"]]
''Battle of the Sith Lords'' would have allowed the player to switch between control of Maul's descendant and Darth Talon.<ref name="Ama" /> A [[Maul's droid|probe droid]] would act as a central guide, comprising a major portion of the game's user interface and main menu.<ref name="ArtStation" /> The droid and Talon would also act as companions when the player is in control of "Maul."<ref name="ArtStation" /> The droid would scan the environment and mark potential threats. Talon would attack when given special commands.<ref name="ArtStation" />

Several locations were planned for ''Battle of the Sith Lords'', including a [[Spaceport/Legends|spaceport]] operated by the [[Black Sun/Legends|Black Sun]] and a [[Jungle/Legends|jungle]] planet situated next to a [[Red dwarf/Legends|red dwarf]]. A central and recurring location in the game would have been a [[Maul's asteroid base|base]] built into an [[Asteroid/Legends|asteroid]]. It would have served as the base of operations for "Maul" and Talon.<ref name="GI subscription article" />

Features that game would have included were a [[Droid/Legends|droid]] mechanic where Maul could send a [[Probe droid/Legends|probe droid]] to scout or map an area for him, a reversal fear mechanic in which Maul could pull enemies into the dark to kill them or just scare them to the point of being so afraid they would fight each other or commit lethal mistakes like walking into furnaces or [[Laser gate/Legends|laser gates]] (getting their fear slider/meter up in the process), fighting mechanics inspired by [[Wikipedia:Rocksteady Studios|Rocksteady Studios]]' [[2009]] video game ''[[Wikipedia:Batman: Arkham Asylum|Batman: Arkham Asylum]]'', mechanics where Maul and Talon could tag-team (like one throwing their [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] and the other catching it like [[Wikipedia:Batman|Batman]] and [[Wikipedia:Catwoman|Catwoman]]) and a mechanic to reflect enemy [[Laser/Legends|lasers]] to armed enemies without it being automatic but demanding the player's skill.<ref name="Bombastic Dan Borth ITW" />

The game would have been different compared to other ''Star Wars'' games, as it would have been aimed for adults, include [[Reproduction/Legends|sex]] and violence, resulting in a [[Wikipedia:Entertainment Software Rating Board|M-rated]] video game had the project been produced.<ref name="Bombastic Dan Borth ITW" />

==Development==
===Inception===
[[LucasArts]] contracted [[Red Fly Studio]] to begin working on ideas, demos, and mechanics for a ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:Wii|Wii]] or [[Wikipedia:Wii U|Wii U]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] featuring [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]], with prototyping for the game beginning on [[October 9]], [[2010]]. Red Fly did not have any story direction from LucasArts other than the title ''Battle of the Sith Lords''. Red Fly originally had the idea of telling the story of Maul's origins and ending with his "[[Death/Legends|death]]" at the end of the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], which they felt it could last one or two games.<ref name="GI subscription article" /> The game would have started with a 12 years old Maul training to become a [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] and would have forced players in scenarios where they would have to take the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] way over their morals to survive and please [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] or either disobey Palpatine and die, yet side stories could depict Maul's remaining humanity. However, [[George Lucas]] met with [[Dan Borth]] and the game's lead designer and spoke with them what he wanted for the game over what they were thinking, adding all of his notes on a big notepad, such as his desire for a mass effect conversation system.<ref name="Bombastic Dan Borth ITW">{{YouTube|video=cUrTtF0edCg|text=The cancelled Star Wars Origin Story we'll never know|channel=channel/UCSPZGQNwMhWbLfaHL6VVLwQ|name=Bombastic}}</ref> LucasArts then let them know that Darth Maul survived the events of ''The Phantom Menace'', but did not let Red Fly in on the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series' then-upcoming plot, which featured Maul's survival, only details like his robotic legs and his brother [[Savage Opress/Legends|Savage Opress]].<ref name="GI subscription article" /> ''The Clone Wars'' supervising director [[Dave Filoni]] later talked to Borth and his designer about the show, with the intention of integrating the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] in the plot, which, adding to what Red Fly had already planned, had the potential of shaping the project into a trilogy, which interested LucasArts.<ref name="Bombastic Dan Borth ITW" />

===Prototyping===
Without a main storyline, Red Fly instead focused early development of ''Battle of the Sith Lords'' toward gameplay prototyping. Emphasis was placed on action-centric gameplay, with a concentration on stealth elements. Red Fly used technology from ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II]]'' as a baseline for their prototype, but at the request of LucasArts, extra care was taken to ensure the gameplay did not feel too similar to ''The Force Unleashed''.<ref name="Ama" /> The gameplay prototype featured [[Wikipedia:Ragdoll physics|ragdoll physics]] and more realistic [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] combat, which would allow decapitation and amputation.<ref name="Ama" /> Red Fly expected the game to receive a mature rating from software rating boards.<ref name="Ama" />

Footage of Red Fly's prototype was published online and featured Maul fighting members of the [[Death Watch/Legends|Death Watch]] on a [[Space station/Legends|space station]] and [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]] on a [[Desert/Legends|desert]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]].<ref name="YouTube">{{YouTube|video=oxlOoS4NmIA|text=The Canceled Darth Maul Game|channel=channel/UCK-65DO2oOxxMwphl2tYtcw|name=Game Informer}}</ref> Red Fly confirmed that these assets were made simply for demonstration purposes and do not reflect what the final plot or time period of the game would have looked like.<ref name="Ama" />

===New direction===
Later in development, during a meeting with representatives of Red Fly, George Lucas revealed his interest in Darth Maul working with [[Darth Talon]], a concept that would carry over to Lucas' first drafts of the ''Star Wars'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="SW Archives">''[[The Star Wars Archives: Episodes I–III, 1999–2005]]''</ref> When Lucas was told that this would not work since Talon lived more than 170 [[Standard year/Legends|years]] after Maul, he said the main character could instead be a [[Maul's descendant|descendant]] or [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] of Maul.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2015/10/26/the-story-of-the-darth-maul-game-that-never-came-to-be.aspx|text=The Story Of The Darth Maul Game That Never Came To Be|author=Reiner, Andrew|date=2015-10-26|work=GameInformer|archivedate=20200320000537}}</ref> Talon's purpose in the game would have been to serve as "Maul's [[Wikipedia:Lauren Bacall|Lauren Bacall]]", a smart and sexy female deuteragonist.<ref name="Bombastic Dan Borth ITW" />

Red Fly then began working with this idea, and their game's story finally started coming together. The plot would have featured this Maul proxy and Talon fighting against [[Darth Krayt]] and his [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] army, with a developer of Red Fly saying Maul could strike at [[One Sith|Krayt's Sith]] for going against the former [[Rule of Two/Legends|Rule of Two]].<ref name="GI article2">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/04/09/art-from-the-darth-maul-game-you-39-ll-never-get-to-play.aspx|text=Art From The Darth Maul Game You'll Never Get To Play|author=Andrew Reiner|date=2014-04-09|work=[[Wikipedia:Game Informer|''Game Informer'']]|archivedate=20240223064541|nolive=1}}</ref>

===Character designs===
[[File:Battle_of_the_Sith_Lords_characters.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Concept art of a variety of characters from the game]]
Red Fly initially made designs for [[Maul's descendant]] that made the character distinct from Maul himself. LucasArts was not in favor of the concept and insisted that the main character look identical to Darth Maul.<ref name="Ama" />

Outside of "Maul" himself, the characters and plot of the game would have been a departure from the designs and storylines from the ''[[Star Wars: Legacy]]'' comic series, which originated [[Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire]]. Krayt was completely redesigned, with a red and black cybernetic look. Several new [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lords]], such as [[Darth Kroan]] and [[Darth VaGhal]], were designed for the game, the majority of which ignored the heavy usage of [[Sith tattoo]]s by members of Krayt's [[One Sith]].<ref name="GI article2" />

Designs for a generic [[Sith Emperor/Legends|Sith Emperor]] and his advisor were also created, although it is unknown if this character was a revised version of Krayt or an entirely new individual.<ref name="ArtStation" /> Concepts for New Empire officers and [[stormtrooper]]s were designed as well, both also deviating from the designs defined in the ''Legacy'' comics.<ref name="ArtStation" /> In addition to many new Sith and Imperial characters, several [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] adversaries, such as [[Nexus Wan]] were created for the game, alongside multiple [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] concepts.<ref name="ArtStation" />

===Decline and cancelation===
Despite the project coming together, LucasArts would not "sign off on any of the big-ticket items that would allow development to truly get underway."<ref name="GI subscription article" />

The project became troubled when LucasArts suddenly stopped communicating with Red Fly. Red Fly found out from some members of LucasArts that something was wrong, before finally being told that the project was canceled via email on [[June 24]], [[2011]].<ref name="GI subscription article" /> According to one of the game's creators, one reason for the cancelation was [[The Walt Disney Company]]'s acquisition of [[Lucasfilm]],<ref name="Ama" /> which they didn't know back then but Lucasfilm's board had been informed of Lucas' intentions privately.<ref name="Bombastic Dan Borth ITW" /> At the time of its cancelation, the game's prototyping phase had just been finished.<ref name="GI article" />

In [[October]] [[2015]], Red Fly was working on "a full next-gen demo of all things Maul to show to the powers that be." In the event that [[Electronic Arts]] and/or Disney would be uninterested in the project, Borth expressed hope that they could reskin the game to be more in line with the companies' vision for ''Star Wars''.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.polygon.com/2015/10/25/9611182/darth-maul-video-game-electronic-arts|text=Darth Maul video game developer wants a second chance with Electronic Arts|author=Good, Owen S.|date=2015-10-25|archiveurl=https://archive.is/ywTqB}}</ref>

In [[April]] [[2020]], Dan Borth, the former head of Red Flyer was interviewed by the YouTube channel "Bombastic" where he discussed the projects development and cancellation and revealed unseen footage and story details including the depiction of [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Darth Plagueis's]] death and the [[Rise of the Empire era|rise of Palpatine]], with the game's structure consisting on Maul's origins, his [[Sith apprentice/Legends|apprenticeship]] with Palpatine, tie-ins with the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] and Maul meeting Talon in the [[Legacy era]].<ref name="Bombastic Dan Borth ITW" />

[[File:Battle_of_the_Sith_Lords_2015_prototype.png|thumb|right|250px|Red Fly's 2015 "Maul" prototype in Unreal Engine 4]]
In [[December]] 2015, Borth confirmed in an interview that Lucasfilm was "not interested," and that all ''Star Wars'' video games going forward would be developed only by Electronic Arts, as per the exclusive license granted to the company.<ref name="December 2015" /> Borth also posited that a public "Ask Me Anything" thread published by Red Fly on [[Wikipedia:Reddit|Reddit]] annoyed Lucasfilm.<ref name="December 2015" />

Since 2015, there has been no status update on ''Battle of the Sith Lords'' from Red Fly. In [[2021]], Lucasfilm ended its exclusive contract with Electronic Arts and founded [[Lucasfilm Games]] to partner with a variety of studios to develop video games under the ''Star Wars'' license.<ref name="Lucasfilm Games">{{SW|url=news/lucasfilm-games|text=Lucasfilm Games Begins a New Era}}</ref>
