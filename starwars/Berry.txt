'''Berries''' were small sweet [[fruit]]s. [[Oi-oi]]s, one variety of berry, were bright [[Color|red]] in color. [[Sinjir Rath Velus]], when he was a [[Youngling|child]], picked oi-ois with his [[Parent|mother]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> [[Salakberry|Salakberries]] were another type of berry, and when fermented, were used to create the [[alcohol]]ic beverage [[tsiraki]].<ref name="Life Debt" /> Many [[bird]]s included berries in their diet,<ref name="Creature Safari" /> and some types of berries could be used to make [[pie]]s.<ref name="Feather Frenzy" />
