'''Ewoks III''' was the working title of a third movie centered on the [[Ewok/Legends|Ewoks]], which was planned around the [[1980]]s, but it was cancelled. The film would have been a sequel to ''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]'' and ''[[Ewoks: The Battle for Endor]]'', and was to focus again on the [[Ewok]]s.

==Development==
During an interview in the [[1980]]s, [[Warwick Davis]], the actor who played [[Wicket Wystri Warrick|Wicket W. Warrick]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] and in the two [[Ewok]]s movies ''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]'' and ''[[Ewoks: The Battle for Endor]]'', revealed that a third ''Ewoks'' movie, with the tentative title of ''Ewoks III'', was in development.<ref name="10 Things">{{WebCite|url=http://www.warpedfactor.com/2016/06/10-things-you-might-not-know-about-ewoks.html|text=10 Things You Might Not Know About THE EWOKS|work=[http://www.warpedfactor.com Warped Factor]|archivedate=20230102175736}}</ref>

The film, however, was eventually scrapped and was never made, although the reason for this is not known. In [[2010]], during an interview with Star Wars Interviews, [[Daniel Frishman]], the actor who played [[Deej Warrick]] in the two previous ''Ewoks'' film, commented that he had never known anything about this project, possibly meaning that his character was not going to be involved in the film's story, which remains a mystery.<ref name="Deej Interview">{{Blogspot|subdomain=starwarsinterviews1|url=2010/02/daniel-frishman-interview-deej-ewok.html|name=Star Wars Interviews|text=Daniel Frishman interview}}</ref>
