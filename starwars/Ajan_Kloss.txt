'''Ajan Kloss''' was a [[jungle]] [[moon]] located in the [[Cademimu sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], in grid square L-5 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], [[orbit]]ing the [[gas giant]] [[Ajara]]. It was notably used by [[Luke Skywalker]] to train [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] in the way of the [[Jedi Order]] as his first [[Padawan|student]] of a new generation of [[Jedi]] and later the [[Resistance]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]], who established a [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|base]] there.

==Description==
[[File:AjanKlossLandscape-TROS.png|thumb|left|300px|Ajan Kloss was a lush, verdant moon.]]
Although Ajan Kloss was a [[moon]], it had two moons of its own. Its [[gas giant]] shielded it from harmful radiation. The [[jungle]] resonated with life and [[Resistance]] scouts would routinely check for new [[species]] to be used in medicine, though the moist environment meant [[technician]]s had to work double-time to moisture proof electronics. Other annoyances on the [[planet]] included [[Fungus|fungal]] and insect infections, but there were no large predators on the moon.<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] compared Ajan Kloss with the planet [[Dagobah]], where he trained years before with [[Yoda]]. He noted that the similarities between Dagobah and Ajan Kloss were that they were wet, warm, [[Color|green]] and overflowing with life, but the main difference was that Ajan Kloss did not smell bad.<ref name="TROSEE" /> The [[Ajara]] gas giant reflected sunlight onto the moon's surface, allowing [[plant]]s to gain sustenance even at night.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==History==
===The Kloss===
Long before the Resistance arrived on the planet, inhabitants called the [[Kloss]] lived in the [[Klosslands]] and hollowed-out [[cave]]s. [[Historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] speculated that the [[cave]] where the [[Resistance]] made their [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|base]] in [[35 ABY]] was once a Kloss refuge or storage cache.<ref name="TROS VD" />

===Imperial Era===
Prior to the [[Battle of Scarif|outbreak]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Alderaanian]] scouts charted Ajan Kloss, which was located in a patchily explored stretch of space off the [[Celanon Spur]]. However, the scouts deliberately hid it from the [[Imperial Senate]] because it was short-listed as a potential home for a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] base like [[Yavin 4]] and [[Dantooine]], but the shifting battlefront against the Empire precluded Ajan Kloss from being used during the conflict.<ref name="TROS VD" />

===New Republic Era===
{{Quote|I treasured each moment I spent with my brother. The things he taught me [....] I use them every day. Once you touch the Force, it's part of you always. Over the years, I continued to learn, to grow. There were times on the Senate floor when the meditations I'd practiced with Luke were the only thing that kept me from causing a galactic incident.|Leia, to Rey|<ref name="TROSEE" />}}
[[File:Luke-Leia-training.jpg|thumb|right|250px|After the Battle of Endor, Organa undertook Jedi training from her brother.]]
Yet it served as a training ground for [[Luke Skywalker]] and his sister, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] at the latter's suggestion sometime after the [[Battle of Endor]], where Skywalker trained Organa in the ways of the [[Jedi]] as his first [[Padawan|student]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> She utilized her [[Leia's lightsaber|lightsaber]] for [[Lightsaber combat|sparring sessions]] during her time as her [[twin]] brother's first Padawan,<ref name="Episode IX" /> and became immensely skilled with the blade.<ref name="TROSEE" /> Organa discontinued her training after she told Skywalker that she had sensed the [[death]] of [[Ben Solo|her son]] at the end of her path.<ref name="Episode IX" />

After the formation of the [[New Republic]], Ajan Kloss served as a quiet waypoint between Republic outposts.<ref name="Squadrons" />

===Rise of the First Order===
The [[Resistance]] maintained a presence on Ajan Kloss during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]]. The mission to find [[Exegol]] was launched from the [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|Resistance base]], and when the planet's location was uncovered, the Resistance forces scrambled and prepared for [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] against the [[Sith Eternal]]. They later returned with the allied fleet for the victory celebrations.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ajan Kloss was developed for the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the third and final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

The reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Galactic Guide]]'' incorrectly listed Ajan Kloss as a planet rather than a moon.<ref name="TROS TGG">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Galactic Guide]]''</ref>
