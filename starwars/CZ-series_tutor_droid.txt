'''CZ-series tutor droids''', also known as '''CZ-1 tutor droids''', were a series of [[Droid|droids]] used to teach children a number of subjects, including mathematics and history. 

One such droid, [[CZ-1 (tutor droid)|CZ-1]], was owned by the [[Kyrell family]] of the planet [[Jelucan]].<ref name="Lost Stars" /> Another tutor droid, [[CZ-7OB]], was in the employ of the [[House of Organa|Royal House]] of [[Alderaan]], serving as a teacher to [[Princess|Crown Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="Eclipse" /> A tutor droid maintained an orphanage in [[Jedha City]] alongside its owners, [[Kaya Gimm]] and [[Killi Gimm]].<ref name="Guardians of the Whills" />
