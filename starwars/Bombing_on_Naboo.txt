In [[18 BBY]], during the [[Imperial Era]], [[Galactic Empire]] [[Desertion|defector]] [[Luthen Rael]] and his companion [[Kleya Marki]] '''bombed''' [[Imperial Army|Imperial forces]] occupying a bridge on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] as their first act of [[Terrorist|terrorism]] against the Empire. The two watched the bridge from afar once they planted the [[Bomb|explosives]], with Rael imparting the significance of the act they were about to commit to Marki. After Marki proved she was making the choice to be his accomplice for herself by displaying willingness to personally set off the explosives, Rael initiated the detonation, with the two watching the [[Fire|fiery]] explosion as it engulfed Imperial Army [[Trooper|troops]] and two [[Unidentified troop transport|troop transports]]. The pair then fled the scene and avoided being caught.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Life shows us what we stand to lose. Most people look away. We can't be like that.|Luthen Rael, to Kleya Marki|audio=Luthen-CantLookAway-MakeItStop.flac|<ref name="Make It Stop" />}}
Around [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Meeting Date">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-interview-stellan-skarsgard-elizabeth-dulau|text=<i>Andor</i>'s Stellan Skarsgård and Elizabeth Dulau Break Down Luthen and Kleya}} states that [[Kleya Marki]] and [[Luthen Rael]] had known each other for 17 [[Standard year|years]] by the events of "[[Who Else Knows?]]" The episode occurs directly after "[[Make It Stop]]," which dates itself to [[1 BBY]]. Therefore, Marki and Rael's first meeting and Rael's subsequent defection occurred around the year [[18 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Imperial Era]], [[Sergeant]] [[Luthen Rael|Lear]] of the [[Imperial Army]] [[Desertion|defected]] from the [[Galactic Empire]] and took a<ref name="Make It Stop" /> [[Kleya Marki|girl]]<ref name="MISAD">{{Andor|Make It Stop}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> who had been orphaned by a [[Unidentified massacre (Luthen Rael)|massacre]] he had participated in as his companion. After Lear adopted the name Luthen Rael and the girl took the name<ref name="Make It Stop" /> Kleya Marki,<ref name="Explained">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-finale-episodes-10-11-12|text=''Andor'' Explained: Episodes 10-12 - "Make It Stop", "Who Else Knows?", and "Jedha, Kyber, Erso"}}</ref> the two began to plan revenge against the Empire, hoping to take it down.<ref name="Make It Stop" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Rael chose<ref name="Bissell Interview" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Naboo]] for the location of their first act of [[Terrorist|terrorism]] against the Empire because it was the [[homeworld]] of [[Emperor]]<ref name="Bissell Interview" /> [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> and would hopefully act as a hostile statement toward<ref name="Bissell Interview" /> him.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> In preparation, the pair planted [[Bomb|explosives]] linked to a [[detonator]] on a Naboo bridge that [[Imperial Army trooper]]s, [[stormtrooper]]s, [[Unidentified troop transport|troop transports]], and an [[IBT-2921]] were occupying.<ref name="Make It Stop" />

[[File:LuthenKleyaOnNaboo-MakeItStop.png|thumb|left|350px|Luthen Rael (center) places the detonator in front of Kleya Marki (left) in anticipation of the bombing.]]
Disguised amongst civilians, Rael and Marki sat at a table on<ref name="Make It Stop" /> a [[cafe]] terrace<ref name="MISAD" /> within view of the bridge where their explosives were planted. After Rael confirmed for Marki that there were fifty or more Imperial [[Trooper|troops]] on the bridge, Rael tried to help the young [[Gender|girl]] understand what she would leave behind as a consequence if she chose a life spent opposing the Empire. Marki accused Rael of being unnecessarily depressing and using her as an excuse to potentially back out, but Rael was only worried about how his choices were affecting her and attempting to confirm she was choosing to engage of her own volition.<ref name="Make It Stop" /> 

As Marki was adamant in her desire to fight the Empire, Rael placed the detonator on the table in front of her and told her to detonate the explosives planted on the bridge, but also gave her the option to back out. Taking Rael's words into account, Marki noticed<ref name="Make It Stop" /> a family of three at an adjacent table, happily enjoying [[tea]].<ref name="MISAD" /> Marki was frustrated by Rael requiring her to press the button, but Rael explained that he was doing it because once they performed their attack, their fight against the Empire would be real. When the girl finally reached for the detonator, Rael stopped her from pressing the button at the last second.<ref name="Make It Stop" />

==The bombing==
{{Quote|I need to know you're making a choice. I lived most of my life&hellip;without ever realizing that that was a possibility.''"<br />"''You're afraid.''"<br />"''I'm only afraid of what I'm doing to you.|Luthen Rael and Kleya Marki, as they prepare for the bombing|audio=Luthen-Kleya-MakingAChoice-MakeItStop.flac|<ref name="Make It Stop" />}}
[[File:KleyaWatchesExplosion-MakeItStop.png|thumb|right|350px|Marki watches the exploding bridge as Rael leads her away.]]
Having confirmed Marki was making the choice to participate for herself, Rael pressed the button to set off the explosives. Rael only allowed her to look at the bridge after two troop [[transport]]s had exploded, with the [[Death|deadly]] blast and its [[Fire|fiery]] aftermath engulfing many troopers within and around the transports.<ref name="Make It Stop" /> Marki was left shellshocked by the event<ref name="MISAD" /> and could not pull her eyes away from the bridge, but Rael declared that they had made their choice and started leading her away as subsequent explosions sounded.<ref name="Make It Stop" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|We'll be leaving now. We made our choice.|Luthen Rael, to Kleya Marki after the explosion|audio=Luthen-LeavingNow-MakeItStop.flac|<ref name="Make It Stop" />}}
Marki and Rael slipped out of the terrace amidst the panic and confusion created by horrified fleeing civilians<ref name="MISAD" /> and avoided being caught for their actions.<ref name="Explained" /> After the attack,<ref name="Make It Stop" /> Rael and Marki continued opposing the Empire, eventually forming an anti-Imperial [[Axis network|network]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> in [[15 BBY]].<ref name="Network Date">"[[Coruscant: Whispers of Rebellion]]" states that the [[Axis network]] was founded a [[Standard decade|decade]] before the events of [[Star Wars: Andor|''Andor'']] [[Andor Season 1|Season 1]], which takes place in [[5 BBY]] per the reasoning [[:Template:AndorDate|here]].</ref> The bombing was later mentioned on a [[map]] of Naboo made by the [[artist]] [[Gammit Chond]], which was included in an updated version of the ''[[Galactic Atlas]]'' [[book]] published by the [[Graf Archive]] at some point no earlier than [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|We knew we were going to show them have their first act of terrorism, Kleya and Luthen together, and I argued it should be on Naboo, because that's the Emperor's home planet. And if Luthen is going to make his first public statement I think he's going to stick his thumb into the eye of the guy he hates more than anyone.|Tom Bisell in an interview, on developing the scene featuring the bombing|<ref name="Bissell Interview" />}}
The bombing appeared in flashbacks in "[[Make It Stop]]," the tenth episode of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the live-action television series [[Star Wars: Andor|''Andor'']],<ref name="Make It Stop" /> which premiered on [[May 13]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref> "Make It Stop" writer<ref name="Make It Stop" /> [[Tom Bissell]] revealed in an interview that because Naboo was Emperor Palpatine's homeworld, he had argued for the bombing to be set on Naboo to<ref name="Bissell Interview" /> series creator<ref name="Trailer News" /> [[Tony Gilroy]], who liked the idea and approved it. Bissell also stated that he deliberately wrote a scene later in the episode,<ref name="Bissell Interview" /> in which Marki bombs<ref name="Make It Stop" /> [[Lina Soh Hospital]]<ref name="Secrets from Set">{{SW|url=news/andor-trivia-guide-week-4|text=Secrets from the Set of ''Andor'' Season 2: Week 4 Trivia}}</ref> during her [[Infiltration of Lina Soh Hospital|infiltration]] of it,<ref name="Make It Stop" /> to parallel the bombing on Naboo.<ref name="Bissell Interview" /> The scene including the bombing was shot at [[Wikipedia:Hever Castle|Hever Castle]] in [[Wikipedia:Kent|Kent]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]], which was previously used as a filming location for<ref name="Secrets from Set" /> the [[1999]]<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> film {{Film|I}} for an ultimately deleted scene set on Naboo.<ref name="Secrets from Set" />
