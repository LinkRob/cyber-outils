A '''court-martial''' was a military court consisting of high-ranking [[officer]]s appointed to try personnel charged with infractions of military law.<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> When they disobeyed the orders of [[Jedi General]] [[Pong Krell]] during the [[Battle of Umbara]], the [[clone trooper]]s [[Fives]] and [[Jesse]] nearly faced a court-martial.<ref name="PoD">{{TCW|Plan of Dissent}}</ref> During the [[mission to Ktath'atn]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] threatened to court-martial fellow rebel [[Luke Skywalker]] back to [[Moisture farmer|moisture farming]] if he did not obey her orders.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 7" /> After [[Dec Hansen]] and [[Mattis Banz]] broke into [[General]] [[Leia Organa's quarters]] at the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]], Banz wondered why [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] had not court-martialed them, leading him to believe that procedure was not practiced by the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Join the Resistance">''[[Join the Resistance]]''</ref> It was known among [[Imperial officer]]s that [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] often simply did not bother with a court-martial when it came to discipline.<ref name="datawork">{{StoryCite|story=The Sith of Datawork|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>
