'''Acid-spitters''' were aggressive flying creatures found in the [[Batuu Wilds]] region of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] world of [[Batuu]]. [[Acid-spitter tail]]s were rare ingredients that could be used to make exotic drinks. [[Seezelslak]] was able to concoct a beverage including acid-spitter tails, [[demlin]] [[demlin scale]]s and [[terateak pod]]s.<ref name="TFGE">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge]]''</ref>
