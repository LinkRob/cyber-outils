'''''Cinematic Classics: Star Wars: A New Hope''''' is a [[Star Wars Cinematic Classics|''Star Wars'' Cinematic Classics]] reference book written by [[Tom Huddleston]]. It was released on [[August 1]], [[2024]],<ref name="Amazon" /> alongside ''[[Cinematic Classics: Star Wars: The Phantom Menace]]''.<ref name="Amazon Cinematic">{{WebCite|url=https://uk.bookshop.org/p/books/the-phantom-menace-star-wars-cinematic-classics-tom-huddleston/7664380|text=The Phantom Menace (Star Wars Cinematic Classics)|work=[https://uk.bookshop.org Bookshop.org]|archivedate=20260126192415}}</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''A retelling of Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope featuring stunning development art from the original Lucasfilm concept artists.''<ref name="Amazon" />

''Relive the excitement of the cinematic classic A New Hope, the classic adventure that launched the Star Wars saga. As the evil Empire tightens its grip on the galaxy, Darth Vader seeks the stolen plans to a terrifying weapon - and a young man takes his first steps on the path of destiny...''<ref name="Amazon" />

''Enjoy the hit movie through this all-ages retelling accompanied by concept art from the original artists. Alongside the art, fascinating facts about each piece give an insight into the design process behind iconic characters and settings like Darth Vader and the Death Star itself.''<ref name="Amazon" />
