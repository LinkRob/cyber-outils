'''A Book Excerpt''' was a recurring feature in ''[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine]]'' that previewed upcoming ''[[Star Wars]]'' novels and media.

*[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 1|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 1]] &mdash; ''[[The Crystal Star]]''
*[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 2|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 2]] &mdash; "[[A Wind to Shake the Stars]]"
*[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 3|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 3]] &mdash; [[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']]
*[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 4|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 4]] &mdash; "[[The Clash of Lightsabers]]"
*[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 7|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 7]] &mdash; [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'']]
