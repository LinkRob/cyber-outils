The '''Blue Ghosts''' were a criminal gang that operated in the [[Anoat sector]]. By the time of the [[Iron Blockade]], they had nearly been wiped out in a territorial dispute with the powerful [[Ivax Syndicate]]. However, the surviving Ghosts worked hard to regain their favor with the Syndicate.<ref name="BG" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
A [[Gender|male]] [[Duros]] Blue Ghost ended up joining the crew of [[the Smuggler]], who sent him on a variety of missions throughout the Anoat sector.<ref name="BG" />
{{EndGame}}
