"'''Babu Frik's Repair Shop Malfunction'''," also titled "'''R2-D2 and BB-8's Quest for a New Arm!'''," is the second episode of the web series ''[[Star Wars: Droid Diaries]]''. The episode was released on [[September 12]], [[2025]] on the ''[[StarWarsKids.com|Star Wars Kids]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel.<ref name="Episode" />

==Official description==
''R2-D2 goes to Babu Frik's repair shop with BB-8 to fix his broken arm and they end up getting trapped in the disassembly line below the shop! Will they escape before they’re turned into parts?''<ref name="Episode" />

==Plot summary==
The episode opens with a wide landscape shot of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' parked inside a [[hangar]]. Inside a workshop, [[Babu Frik]] is repairing an [[R5 series astromech droid]]. [[R2-D2]] and [[BB-8]] enter the workshop. R2-D2 asks Frik to repair one of his damaged grappling appendages but Babu Frik tells him to join a queue consisting of [[D-O]] and [[R5-D4]]. While waiting outside, the two [[astromech droid]]s fall down a trap door, which leads to a factory conveyer belt network within a droid disassembling factory. 

BB-8 dodgers several dismantling arms and a crushing machine to land safely in the junk pile. R2-D2 uses his grappling cable to latch onto a [[repulsorlift]] barge but hits a pillar. The repulsorlift barge then drags R2-D2 through several factory machines including a freezer unit. As he approaches BB-8, R2-D2 attempts to reach out to him but is unable to because of his faulty appendage. As the droid carries R2-D2 away into a container full of discarded droid arms, the ground beneath BB-8 opens into a chute that leads to a fiery furnace. 

BB-8 attempts to latch onto a repulsorlift barge with his grappling cable but misses. Fortunately, R2-D2, who had acquired a red arm, pulls him to safety. The two arrive at Babu Frik's workshop just as the mechanic has found a new grappling appendage for R2-D2. R2-D2, who is now sporting a red arm, declines Frik's offer.
