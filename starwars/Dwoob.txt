'''Dwoobs''' were a type of creature with [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]].<ref name="MandoVG" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|9}}</ref> a [[Unidentified Gamorrean fighter 1|pair]] of [[Gamorrean]] [[Unidentified Gamorrean fighter 2|fighters]] wore traditional [[leather]] and dwoob-fur [[Clothing|trunks]] while brawling for sport in the arena at the [[Gor Koresh's Honky Tonk|honky tonk]] of [[Abyssin]] criminal [[Gor Koresh]] on the [[planet]] [[RTK111]].<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], dwoob-fur first appeared in "[[Chapter 9: The Marshal]],"<ref name="Chapter 9">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref> the premiere episode of the [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second season]] of the [[Disney+]] live-action series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', which aired on [[October 30]], [[2020]].<ref name="S2 Companion">{{InsiderCite|202|Star Wars: The Mandalorian Season Two Companion|''Star Wars: The Mandalorian'' Season Two Companion}}</ref> The fur was then first identified as belong to dwoobs in the [[2026]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="MandoVG" /> The [[Dwoob|creatures]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they appeared on the [[Gamorrean/Legends|Gamorrean]] [[Homeworld/Legends|homeworld]] [[Gamorr/Legends|Gamorr]] in "[[Murder in Slushtime]]," a short story written by [[Barbara Hambly]] and published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 14|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 14]] by [[West End Games]] in [[August]], [[1997]].<ref name="Slushtime">{{JournalCite|14|Murder in Slushtime}}</ref>
