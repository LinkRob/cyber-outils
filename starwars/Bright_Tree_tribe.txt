The '''Bright Tree tribe''' was an [[Ewok]] tribe from [[Bright Tree Village]]&mdash;a settlement located on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. At the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Endor]], the tribe had been led by [[Chief]] [[Chirpa]] for forty-two seasons. After the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] victory over the [[Galactic Empire]] at Endor, Chirpa was succeeded by his daughter [[Kneesaa a Jari Kintaka|Kneesaa]].<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know" />

==History==
Before [[Chief]] [[Chirpa]] led the Bright Tree Tribe, his father Chief [[Buzza]] led before him.<ref name="Night of the Gorax" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Bright Tree tribe had been led by Chief Chirpa for forty-two seasons.<ref name="CE" /> At that time, aside from the [[Council of Elders (Ewok)|Council of Elders]], other figures of authority in the [[Bright Tree Village|village]] included the [[shaman]] [[Logray]] and Chirpa's two best [[Hunting|hunters]], [[Asha Fahn]] and [[Teebo]].<ref name="beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> Meanwhile, in the wider [[The galaxy|galaxy]] and far from the preoccupations of Ewoks, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] waged [[Galactic Civil War|war]] against the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Episode VI" /> which [[Occupation of Endor|occupied]] Endor<ref name="Yub Nub 217">{{InsiderCite|217|Yub Nub! 40 Years of the Ewoks}}</ref> to build the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]].<ref name="Episode VI" />

One [[Standard day|day]], the Bright Tree [[scout]] [[Wicket Wystri Warrick|Wicket W. Warrick]] discovered a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] lying unconscious in the [[forest]]. Said woman was, in fact, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], a leading member of the Alliance. Organa had landed on [[Endor]] with a [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Rebel strike team]] whose role was to deactivate a [[deflector shield]] [[Deflector shield generator|generator]] protecting the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], a [[planet]]-[[Death|killing]] [[battle station]] developed by the Empire. Warrick quickly bonded with the stranger, and helped her to the safety of his village.<ref name="Episode VI" />

Elsewhere, some of the tribe's hunters captured two humans, one [[Wookiee]], and two [[droid]]s who were ensnared in the net of a trap. Unbeknownst to the Ewoks, their captives were [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca]], [[C-3PO]], and [[R2-D2]], the other members of Princess Leia's strike team. Upon seeing C-3PO, a [[protocol droid]] with a shiny [[gold]]en plating, the hunters mistook him for a [[Deity|god]] and started venerating him.<ref name="Episode VI" />

The Rebel captives were brought to the Bright Tree Village and, at first, the shaman Logray decided that Solo, Skywalker, Chewbacca, and R2-D2 would be sacrificed to serve as the main course at a banquet in C-3PO's honor. Despite Leia Organa's intervention and C-3PO's attempt at freeing his friends, the Ewoks refused to obey, and they only changed their minds when the so-called golden god used his magic. Actually, it was Skywalker, a [[Jedi Knight]], who used [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|elevate]] C-3PO in mid air, which compelled the Ewoks to cut the Rebels loose.<ref name="Episode VI" />

[[File:SupportOfTheTribe-SoH.png|thumb|left|250px|Warriors from Bright Tree tribe helped attack the Empire during the battle.]]
At night the Bright Tree villagers gathered in Teebo's hut, where they learned of the Empire's threat to the galaxy by listening to C-3PO, who told them the whole story of the Rebel heroes. Inspired by the droid's tale, Chief Chirpa wanted to help the Rebels. But Logray, as a shaman, thought that his Ewoks should not meddle with matters that were beyond them. With the support of Teebo and Asha Fahn, Chirpa was able to overrule Logray, and he welcomed the Rebels as honorary members of the tribe.<ref name="beware" />

Having realized that their new friends were in fact heroes determined to destroy the cold metal [[moon]] hanging in the sky over their forest, the Bright Tree villagers agreed to help them.<ref name="beware" /> The Ewoks led the Rebels to the site of the [[Death Star's shield generator|bunker]] containing the shield generator, which had to be destroyed before the attack on the Death Star could begin. They also informed the Rebels that the bunker had a secret entrance. [[Paploo]] then stole an Imperial [[speeder bike]], prompting [[scout trooper]]s to chase him, thus creating a diversion.<ref name="Episode VI" />

Although the Rebels managed to enter the bunker, they were immediately captured by Imperials who had been expecting their [[Battle of Endor|attack]]. Although the Alliance's plan seemed to have failed, the Bright Tree warriors made a timely entrance,<ref name="Episode VI" /> along with members of other tribes that had answered their call to war.<ref name="beware" /> Their primitive weapons felled the [[stormtrooper]]s and the [[All Terrain Scout Transport|scout walkers]] of the Empire. The tribe suffered losses, but its contribution allowed the Rebels to finally deactivate the deflector shield, which ultimately led to the Death Star's destruction in space above.<ref name="Episode VI" />
