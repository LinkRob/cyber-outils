'''Cli Pastayra''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Pau'an]] criminal and a member of the criminal organization [[Wandering Star]] on the [[planet]] [[Utapau]].<ref name="Last Shot" /> By around [[17 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> he found and smashed [[Fyzen Gor]]'s droid army and captured him.<ref name="Last Shot" /> Around [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> he organized the [[Auction for the Phylanx Redux Transmitter|auction]] of the [[Phylanx Redux Transmitter]] on [[Cantonica]]. During the proceedings, an unknown assailant shot Pastayra through the hand. While trying to make his escape backstage, he was [[Death|killed]] by the [[droid]] [[Fenbolt]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
