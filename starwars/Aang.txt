'''Aang''' was a [[Roonan]] [[senator]] for the planet [[Roona]]<ref name="TBoCW">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Clone Wars Begin}}</ref> who served in the [[Galactic Senate]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a [[The galaxy|galaxy]]-wide conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the war, he served on the [[Military Oversight Committee]] and was persuaded by the [[Naboo]] senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] to vote in favor of [[Reduced military spending bill|reducing]] the amount of [[Galactic Credit Standard|money]] spent on the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Evil Plans" /> Despite Aang's support, the Senate ultimately voted to increase [[Republic Military|military]] [[Republic military enhancement bill|funding]] in order to supply the [[army]] with five million additional [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="SM">{{TCW|Senate Murders}}</ref> [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Amidala]] once threw a party for him following the [[Alderaan Refugee Conference|Conference on Alderaan]].<ref name="Evil Plans" />
