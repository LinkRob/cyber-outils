'''Azure''' was an astronomical object<ref name="TFABG" /> located in the [[Azure system (Mid Rim)|Azure system]] in the [[Truum sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was positioned in grid square P-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
Azure was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] boxed set for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="TFABG" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Azure/Legends|Azure]] was introduced in [[Secrets of the Jedi (novel)|''Secrets of the Jedi'']], a [[2005]] junior novel authored by [[Jude Watson]].<ref name="Secrets">[[Secrets of the Jedi (novel)|''Secrets of the Jedi'']]</ref>
