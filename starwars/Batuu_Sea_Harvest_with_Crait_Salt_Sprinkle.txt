'''Batuu Sea Harvest with Crait Salt Sprinkle''' was a [[Foodstuff|provision]] served at [[Oga's Cantina]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> This dish containing [[salt]] from the planet [[Crait]] was edible by [[human]]s.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />

==Behind the scenes==
Batuu Sea Harvest with Crait Salt Sprinkle first appeared as a provision available to guests at the [[In-universe|real-world]] Oga's Cantina in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]], exclusively for [[Star Wars Nite]]s in [[2024]].<ref name="2024 Program">{{WebCite|url=https://www.laughingplace.com/w/disney-parks/disneyland-after-dark-star-wars-nite-guide-map-with-list-of-entertainment-characters-and-food-items|text=Disneyland After Dark: Star Wars Nite &ndash; Guide Map with List of Entertainment, Characters, and Food Items|work=LaughingPlace.com|archiveurl=https://archive.is/3D7ci}}</ref> The menu described it as "[[Rice]] Roll, [[Wikipedia:Salmon|Salmon]] [[Wikipedia:Mousse|Mousse]], [[Wikipedia:Cucumber|Cucumber]], [[Wikipedia:Red onion|Red Onion]], [[Wikipedia:Everything bagel|Everything Bagel]] Crunch, Broken [[Wikipedia:Caper|Caper]] [[Wikipedia:Vinaigrette|Vinaigrette]]."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/lunch|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Lunch Menu}}</ref><ref name="Ordinary Adventures">{{YouTube|video=RBiqQmTGmrs|text=Star Wars Nite 2024 Food Tour!|channel=@OrdinaryAdventures|name=Ordinary Adventures|date=2024-04-18}}</ref>
