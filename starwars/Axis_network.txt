The '''Axis network''',<ref name="DOR">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> as it was dubbed by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]],<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> was a secret spying operation<ref name="Kleya DB" /> and resistance network<ref name="Narkina 5" /> that was part of<ref name="SWGCE" /> the then-informal<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref> [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="SWGCE" /> Formed in [[15 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> it was led by [[Luthen Rael]] from his covert headquarters in the [[Galactic Antiquities and Objects of Interest]] gallery on the Imperial [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Narkina 5" /> Rael's network was critical to establishing collaboration in the early rebellion.<ref name="JKE">{{Andor|Jedha, Kyber, Erso}}</ref> [[Kleya Marki]] was Rael's second-in-command,<ref name="Getty Doc" /> working as his emissary, messenger, and enforcer.<ref name="Kleya DB" /> Meanwhile, Luthen sought to build and expand the network with Imperial [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]]<ref name="Aldhani" /> of [[Chandrila]],<ref name="Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> who used her family funds to help the network grow<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> and had established a [[Mon Mothma's rebel cell|rebel cell]] of her own,<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> and other contacts throughout [[the galaxy]], including [[Cassian Jeron Andor]], [[Saw Gerrera]], [[Anto Kreegyr]],<ref name="Narkina 5" /> [[Maya Pei]],<ref name="One Year Later" /> and [[Carro Rylanz]].<ref name="Friends">{{Andor|I Have Friends Everywhere}}</ref> [[Vel Sartha]] led a [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|rebel infiltration team]] for the [[Aldhani heist]],<ref name="Aldhani" /> and took later missions from Marki,<ref name="Narkina 5" /> while [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[ISB Supervisor|Supervisor]] [[Lonni Jung]] acted as a mole within the Bureau and fed intel to Rael.<ref name="One Way">{{Andor|One Way Out}}</ref>

In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="AldhaniDate">{{AndorDate|104}}</ref> operatives Vel Sartha and [[Taramyn Barcona]] led the heist on [[Aldhani]], organized by Luthen Rael, with the goal of stealing the [[sector]]'s quarterly payroll of more than 80 million [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. Assisting with the heist were network contacts Cassian Andor, [[Karis Nemik]], [[Cinta Kaz]], [[Arvel Skeen]], and the rebel [[spy]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Gorn]].<ref name="Aldhani" />

After receiving information from Lonni Jung about the [[Project Stardust|development]] of a [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|weapon]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], Rael returned to his gallery to destroy his equipment. Upon being [[Raid on the Galactic Antiquities and Objects of Interest|cornered]] by [[Dedra Meero]], who had been searching for him and his network for years, Rael attempted to take his own life with a [[Nautolan bleeder]]&mdash;a job which was [[Infiltration of Lina Soh Hospital|finished]] when Kleya Marki disconnected him from his life support at the [[Lina Soh Hospital]].<ref name="MIS" /> The Axis network's final piece of intelligence would be shared by Cassian Andor to [[Alliance High Command]] after he [[Raid on the Axis safehouse|rescued]] Marki from Coruscant, leading to the approval of a [[Mission to the Ring of Kafrene|mission]] to the [[Ring of Kafrene]] to meet [[Tivik]] to discover more about the weapon.<ref name="JKE">{{Andor|Jedha, Kyber, Erso}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Axis network was created for the TV series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', and first appeared in the episode "[[That Would Be Me]]."<ref name="That Would Be Me">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}}</ref> It was first identified in ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="DOR" /> The episode "[[Welcome to the Rebellion]]" later depicted [[Erskin Semaj]] as a member of the network during the [[escape from the Senate]] and subsequent [[Operation Handoff]],<ref name="Welcome">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref> [[Retroactive continuity|retroactively]] establishing that Semaj's appearance in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Secret Cargo]]" was an appearance of the Axis network.<ref name="Secret Cargo" />
