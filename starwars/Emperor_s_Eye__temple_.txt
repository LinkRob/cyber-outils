The '''Emperor's Eye''' was an ancient [[Sith temple/Legends|Sith Temple]] on [[Ziost/Legends|Ziost]] that fell into ruin by the time of the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]]. The temple was dedicated to the [[Tenebrae|Sith Emperor]] and contained an inscription left by his subjects, who awaited his return. By [[3636 BBY]] the [[Galactic Solutions Industries]] conducted an archaeological reconstruction of the temple, using [[HR-02 Omni Droid]]s. At the same time [[Tenebrae|Vitiate]], the former Sith Emperor, returned to Ziost and performed a [[Sith Emperor's ritual|ritual]] that wiped out all life on the planet, leaving holographic reconstruction of the temple unfinished.<ref name="SWTOR" />

==Behind the scenes==
Emperor's Eye was added to ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Shadow of Revan]]'' in game update 3.2.
