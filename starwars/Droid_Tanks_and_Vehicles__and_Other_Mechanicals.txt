"'''Droid Tanks and Vehicles, and Other Mechanicals'''" is a volume in the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series of reference books published by [[De Agostini]]. It was published on [[February 25]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Droids of every imaginable type existed in the galaxy, and it was only a matter of time before someone turned them into tools of war as well. From the fighter droids used by the Separatists during the Clone Wars, to the sentry droids that the First Order would use decades later as surveillance tools, many were the organizations that used them for war purposes. Often they were machines with almost no consciousness or intelligence; however, there were also droids capable of handling them or even piloting starships with the same expertise as an organic being. Some of these pilot droids would play very relevant roles in certain events on a galactic scale.''<ref name="De Agostini" />

==Continuity==
*The encyclopedia incorrectly identifies the [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170 starfighters]] that escorted a [[Rho-class transport shuttle|''Rho''-class transport shuttle]] carrying [[clone trooper]] [[Tup|CT-5385]] during the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]] as [[Clone Z-95 starfighter/Legends|Clone Z-95 starfighters]].
*The book incorrectly states that the [[Battle of Teth]] was fought just several [[standard day]]s after the start of the [[Clone Wars]], when it was at least several [[standard month]]s.
*The book incorrectly states that Separatist [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Fighter/Bombers]] destroyed other Separatist ships during the [[Invasion of Scipio|Battle of Scipio]], when they were [[Galactic Republic]] ships.
