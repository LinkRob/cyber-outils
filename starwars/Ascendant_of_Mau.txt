'''Ascendant of Mau''' was a title held by [[Nartun Trecim]] according to ''[[Collected Poems, Prayers, and Meditations on the Force]]'', a compilation of writings on [[the Force]] edited by the [[Disciples of the Whills|Disciple of the Whills]] [[Kozem Pel]].<ref name="GotW">[[Guardians of the Whills (novel)|''Guardians of the Whills'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
