'''Creating Mandalore''' is a featurette included in the Blu-ray and DVD release of [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Two|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Two]]. It covers the development of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series' [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|season two]] episodes "[[The Mandalore Plot]]," "[[Voyage of Temptation]]" and "[[Duchess of Mandalore]]."

The featurette explores the introduction of the [[Mandalorian]]s and [[Death Watch]] in the series, the concept and design of [[Mandalore]], [[Pre Vizsla]], the [[Darksaber]] and [[Satine Kryze]].
