The '''C-3PO Crunch Bar''' was a type of [[candy|candy bar]]. The name of the candy was written in [[High Galactic]] on the [[Color|yellow]] wrapper, which featured the head of the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]], its namesake.<ref name="Card Trader" />

==Behind the scenes==
C-3PO Crunch Bar was featured in the [[2023]] [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]] set ''Halloween 2023 - Classic''.<ref name="Card Trader" />
