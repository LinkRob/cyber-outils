{{Quote|Scum knows scum. Would you say that's accurate, Barger?''"<br />"''Entirely accurate, sir.''"<br />"''Good. That's why I hired you.|Orson Krennic and Barger|<ref name="Andor" />}}
'''Barger''' was an [[alien]] [[cyborg]] who worked for [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. In [[1 BBY]], Barger kept an [[eye]] out for the defected Imperial cargo [[pilot]] [[Bodhi Rook]] for Krennic near the [[Ring of Kafrene]] [[mining]] colony and contacted Krennic when a [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]] using falsified credentials arrived at the colony.

Krennic had Barger try and capture the [[starship]]'s pilot, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], on the Ring of Kafrene, and the cyborg grabbed him in the colony's [[fry-market]]. Andor and Barger fought until Andor's companion, the [[KX-series security droid]] [[K-2SO]], hurled Barger to the ground and crushed the alien's head with his [[Limb|foot]], [[Death|killing]] them.

==Biography==
===Keep eyes on Kafrene===
{{Quote|You told me to keep an eye on ''Kafrene''. You told me your missing cargo pilot might show up here.''"<br />"''You've identified ''Bodhi Rook?"<br />"''Unclear who's in my crosshairs. But it's a U-wing running false credentials.|Barger and Orson Krennic|<ref name="Andor" />}}
[[File:BargerKrennic-ROAndor.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Orson Krennic's face is visible on a green-tinted screen infront of Barger in the cockpit of a starship. A speech bubble from Krennic reads "And how do you know they're false credentials?," with Barger's response being "Because I sell them myself, Director Krennic."|Barger worked for Director Orson Krennic.]]
Barger was a [[Gender|male]] [[alien]] [[cyborg]] who sold falsified [[starship]] credentials during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. After the Imperial cargo [[pilot]] [[Bodhi Rook]]<ref name="Andor" /> defected in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] of the Empire's [[Imperial Security Bureau]] hired Barger to keep an [[eye]] out for Rook near the [[Ring of Kafrene]],<ref name="Andor" /> a deep-[[Realspace|space]] [[mining]] colony and trading post<ref name="RONovel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Thand sector]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> where the director thought Rook might surface. Barger waited in a starship in<ref name="Andor" /> the [[Kafrene asteroid belt]]<ref name="RONovel" /> around the colony and contacted Krennic via [[hologram]] when a [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]] using one of the cyborg's falsified credentials arrived at Kafrene.<ref name="Andor" />

Barger informed Krennic about the vessel but could not confirm if Rook was on board or not, only that the falsified credentials suggested someone who was wanted by the [[Law enforcement agency|law]]. Krennic demanded that Barger bring the U-wing's crew to<ref name="Andor" /> him<ref name="RONovel" /> and the cyborg managed to track down the vessel's pilot, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[intelligence officer]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], on the Ring of Kafrene. Barger observed Andor using the [[blaster gas]] cartridge from his [[A280-CFE convertible heavy blaster pistol|blaster pistol]] to create a distraction for Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s while the rebel was recovering a [[comlink]] from a drop site in the colony's [[fry-market]].<ref name="Andor" />

===Fatal fight in the fry-market===
{{Quote|Ten minutes, was it? Near the exhaust fan? What's the secret behind this secret rendezvous, ''eh''? I think we'll both show up and&mdash;''<br />[Andor bites Barger]<br />"HARRR! You're going to suffer for that one.|Barger grabs Cassian Andor|<ref name="Andor" />}}
[[File:BargerSkewered-ROAndor.png|thumb|left|200px|alt=Barger leaps at Cassian Andor with fists raised as the rebel drives skewers into each side of Barger's chest.|Barger fought Cassian Andor on the Ring of Kafrene.]]
Barger followed Andor further into the market and overheard him talking to his contact [[Tivik]] over the comm, planning a meeting in ten [[Standard minute|minutes]] [[time]]. The cyborg then grabbed Andor from behind and informed him that they would both be going to the meeting, but the rebel bit Barger's [[Limb|hand]], causing the alien to cry out and release him. Promising to hurt Andor, Barger charged at him and hit the rebel despite Andor driving a pair of skewers from a nearby stall into Barger's ribs.<ref name="Andor" />

Pulling out the skewers, Barger tried to stab Andor, but he rolled out of the way and then hurled an [[egg]] from a stall into the criminal's face. When Andor pulled his [[blaster]] and tried to threaten Barger, the alien mocked him, knowing that it lacked a gas cartridge and pulling out their own blaster to point at the rebel. Andor's companion, the [[KX-series security droid]] [[K-2SO]], then emerged from the crowd and grabbed Barger's wrist, crushing it before swinging the alien into the air and slamming them into the floor. The [[droid]] then crushed Barger's head with his foot, [[Death|killing]] the cyborg.<ref name="Andor" />

[[File:BargerStomped-ROAndor.png|thumb|right|200px|alt=K-2SO stamps down on Barger's head, producing a "kunch" sound from the prone alien.|K-2SO crushed Barger's head.]]
When a [[squad]] of stormtroopers later stopped Andor and K-2SO elsewhere on Kafrene, the droid impersonated an Imperial escorting Andor as his [[prisoner]] and claimed that Barger had been the [[terrorist]] responsible for the explosion Andor had caused in the fry-market. He admonished the stormtroopers for not doing their jobs and ordered that they go clean up the mess left by Barger's demise and restore order to the area. Falling for the ruse, the stormtroopers departed, leaving Andor to head to the meeting with Tivik.<ref name="Andor" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I'm done with you. You're meat.|Barger threatens Cassian Andor|<ref name="Andor" />}}
K-2SO considered Barger to be gigantic and ugly. Krennic considered the alien scum but saw this as something that would enable them to better understand other scum like Rook and locate him in ways that the Imperial Security Bureau's rigid limitations and blind spots would fail to achieve. Despite Krennic's orders to capture Andor, Barger grew angry during their fight and tried to kill Andor with skewers. The cyborg had [[Color|pink]] skin, a blond [[Hair|beard]], and a brown [[eye]].<ref name="Andor" />
