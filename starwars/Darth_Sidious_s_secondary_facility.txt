A '''secondary facility''' served as a backup for [[Darth Sidious's secret facility]] on [[Mustafar]], where the [[Sith Lord]] attempted to perform a [[conditioning procedure]] to turn [[Force-sensitive]] [[Youngling|children]] into his personal [[army]] of [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Spy|spies]]. When the [[Jedi]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]] [[Mission to Mustafar (Skywalker and Tano)|discovered]] the main facility<ref name="COTF" /> in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|203}}</ref> Sidious ordered his [[nanny droid]] [[RO-Z67]] to evacuate the children to his secondary facility. This was prevented by the Jedi, who were able to rescue the children and bring them to safety.<ref name="COTF" />
