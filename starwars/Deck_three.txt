'''Deck three''' was a deck on the [[Emissary-class Republic Cruiser|''Emissary''-class Republic Cruiser]] ''[[Third Horizon]]''. The deck was breached alongside [[Deck four]] during the [[Battle of Kur]] when a craft from [[Kassav's Tempest]] exploded against the ''Third Horizon''<nowiki>'</nowiki>s hull. It did not cause any severe damage, as the deck was a non-essential area.
