{{Quote|Before he created the Death Star, Palpatine experimented with various other super-weapons, including the Death Cloud, the Death Moon, and the Death Rainbow.|A stormtrooper in ''The Official Stormtrooper Training Manual''|<ref name="Training" />}}
The '''Death Rainbow''' was an experimental [[superweapon]] created for the [[Galactic Empire]] under the rule of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Training" /> Between [[4 ABY]] and [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Official Stormtrooper Training Manual]]'' features images of the [[Battle of Endor]] but is set before the fall of the [[Galactic Empire]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]] and [[5 ABY]] respectively. Therefore, the [[The Official Stormtrooper Training Manual|in-universe guide]] must have been [[Writing|written]] between those two [[Standard year|years]].</ref> an Imperial [[Unidentified stormtrooper (Training Manual)|stormtrooper]] [[Writing|wrote]]<ref name="Training" /> in his<ref name="Droid Tales">{{DroidTales|Gambit on Geonosis}}</ref> [[Book|handbook]], ''[[The Official Stormtrooper Training Manual]]'', that prior to the construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]], Palpatine experimented with other superweapons such as the Death Rainbow along with the [[Death Cloud]] and the [[Death Moon]].<ref name="Training" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Death Rainbow was mentioned in the [[2016]] [[Canon|non-canon]] young readers book ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Official Stormtrooper Training Manual]]'', which was written by [[Arie Kaplan]].<ref name="Training" />
