'''City Father''' was a title used in [[Coronet City]] on [[Corellia]]. It included the [[Father of Finances]], the [[Father of Chemicals]], and the [[Father of Metals]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> [[Ovus Buckell]] was the Father of Finances, [[Finmos Tagge]] was the Father of Chemicals, and [[Deklar Graf]] was the Father of Metals.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
