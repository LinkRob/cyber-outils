'''Danry Ledwellow''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Er'Kit (species)|Er'Kit]] who [[Senator|represented]] his [[species]] in the [[Galactic Senate|senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] some time before the [[Clone Wars]]. The senator took bribes from [[Thalassian]] [[Slavery|slavers]] so they could operate safely on [[Er'Kit]], and owned a private army of [[IG-227 Hailfire-class droid tank|IG-227 ''Hailfire''-class droid tanks]]. Eventually, Ledwellow was arrested, and his droid army later fell into the hands of the [[Blood Dawn]] slavers.<ref name="SWCommander" />
