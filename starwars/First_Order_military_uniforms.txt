The '''military uniforms''' worn by the personnel of the [[First Order]]'s [[First Order military|armed forces]] consisted of a high-collared tunic, flared-hipped breeches, and knee-high [[boot]]s. Accessories included [[belt]] with a polished buckle, [[code cylinder]]s, black [[leather]] [[glove]]s, and a crested [[command cap]]. Parade uniforms also included a [[greatcoat]] made of [[gaberwool]].<ref name="TFAVD" />

[[Rank insignia of the First Order|Rank]] was denoted by a braided cuff title on the uniform's left sleeve, as well as by uniform [[color]]: [[General]]s' and [[Admiral]]s' uniforms were charcoal grey, [[First Order officer|senior officers']] were teal, and junior officers' were light grey. Non-commissioned officers&mdash;including [[Petty Officer]]s, [[Chief Petty Officer|chief petty officers]], and some military [[technician]]s&mdash;wore lighter, black duty uniforms without belts.<ref name="TFAVD" />

These uniforms were updated versions of the the [[Galactic Empire]]'s own [[Imperial military uniforms|uniforms]], albeit optimized with more modern modifications and alterations that were rooted in efficiency rather than comfort.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>
