The '''defense of the southern polar regions''' was tasked to [[Admiral]] [[Raddus]] by [[King of Mon Cala|King]] [[Lee-Char]] during the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Invasion of Mon Cala|invasion]] of the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]].<ref name="Dark Lord 16" /> The defense ended when the Empire began to [[Orbital bombardment|bombard]] the region and Raddus gave orders to a [[Unidentified Mon Calamari commander (Mon Cala)|commander]] to launch all [[cruiser]]s so they could escape the [[blockade]] of the planet.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />
