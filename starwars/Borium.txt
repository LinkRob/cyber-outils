'''Borium''' was a valuable [[Ore/Legends|ore]].<ref name="CRFG">[[Cracken's Rebel Field Guide (sourcebook)|''Cracken's Rebel Field Guide'']]</ref>

Valuable for its industrial and manufacturing applications, borium was useful for, among other things, borium-tipped excavators and [[Drill/Legends|industrial drills]], and its role in [[starship]] production.<ref name="STRONG">''[[Strongholds of Resistance]]''</ref>

It was heavily present on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Contruum/Legends|Contruum]], where it played a significant part in [[Contruum]]s economy.<ref name="STRONG" />
