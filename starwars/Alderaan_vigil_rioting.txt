The '''Alderaan vigil rioting''' took place in [[Coruscant]] after the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] by the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] in [[1 BBY]]. Rumors of what occurred began reaching the [[Alderaanian]] population residing in Coruscant, most of whom lived on [[Level 3204]], and vigils were held which soon became heated and angry when images followed the rumors, confirming Alderaan had been destroyed. The vigils turned into rioting which lasted for several days until the [[Coruscant Security Force|security forces]] and Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s were sent in to restore order.<ref name="OTLD" />

However, as the troops marched into the streets, the stormtroopers opened fire with their [[blaster]]s. One of the causalities was [[Reffe]] who died during his tirade against the Empire. The crowds, frightened, dispersed. The following day, the Coruscant police arrested anyone caught out in the streets. The day after that, stormtroopers began going door to door stating that [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Spy|spies]] had recruited local residents and Alderaanians had to be taken in for questioning.<ref name="OTLD" />
