'''Bettin''' was a [[Color/Legends|yellow]]-skinned [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] who served as a [[Sniper/Legends|sniper]] and exotic weapons expert member of [[Wraith Squadron]] in [[13 ABY/Legends|13 ABY]]. He was present on a mission to [[Ryvester]] that eliminated [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Admiral]] [[Kosh Teradoc]]. Bettin tagged a gemologist, [[Mulus Cheems]], who was being held captive by Teradoc, with a transmitter that gave Cheems instructions. The Wraiths then lured Teradoc out with an offer of a sculpted gem treasure. Teradoc took the treasure after having Cheems authenticate it, but the Wraiths rescued Cheems while Teradoc fled back to his base, where the sculpture&mdash;actually an explosives package of [[anthracite]]&mdash;detonated.
