The '''fighting pits of Bendeluum''' were a [[gladiator]]ial arena located on the [[planet]] of [[Bendeluum]]. It provided significant entertainment for spectators, [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] for [[gambler]]s, and an outlet for fighters.<ref name="FPoB" />

{{GameMechanics}}
At some point during the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Iron Blockade|blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]], [[the Smuggler]] dispatched some of their crewmembers to the pits in hopes of recruiting a fighter.<ref name="FPoB" />
