'''Brian Matyas''' is a senior concept artist within [[Lucasfilm]] Concept Design Department.

==Biography==
[[File:Smugglers-SWU.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Matyas illustrated the loading screens for ''Star Wars: Uprising''.]]
Brian Matyas' earliest contributions to the Star Wars universe were the creation of character designs and loading screen art for the mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''.<ref name="kotaku">{{WebCite|url=http://kotaku.com/now-this-is-how-you-do-star-wars-art-1709196424|text=Now This is How You Do ''Star Wars'' Art|author=Plunkett, Luke|date=2015-06-04|work=[[Wikipedia:Kotaku|''Kotaku'']]|archivedate=20170811013257}}</ref> He was later recruited by [[Doug Chiang]] to be part of Lucasfilm<ref name="Designer">{{WebCite|url=https://ziro.hu/english/exclusive-interview-with-brian-matyas-designer-of-the-heavy-infantry-mandalorian|text=Interview with Brian Matyas, designer of the Heavy Infantry Mandalorian|author=István, Kádas|date=2019-11-27|work=ziro.hu|quote=When Doug Chiang asked me to join the Lucasfilm art department to design characters for The Mandalorian, I couldn't have been more thrilled. It was also an incredible challenge to design them for an entire series of content (I'll have lots of character designs to post in the future). I also did many keyframe illustrations for the show. (&hellip;) More to come!|archivedate=20191127185919|nolive=1}}</ref> Concept Design Department for the [[The Mandalorian Season One|first season]] of the [[Disney+]] show ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="MandoS1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> and continued in this position for the [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second season]],<ref name="MandoS2">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref> as well as ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]''<ref name="BoBF">{{BOBF|Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref> and ''[[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''.<ref name="ObiWan" /> He also created concepts for characters for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] and [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]].<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge]]''</ref>
