'''Filson Bood''' was a salvager who worked in the [[Jagoth system]].<ref name="Pathfinders 1" />

==Biography==
Bood was salvaging with his droid [[Exoh]] in the Jagoth system when he found a [[Atticus Cale's shuttle|shuttle]] owned by [[Atticus Cale]]. Investigating it, he discovered the corpse of the [[Jedi Master]] and called the [[Office of the Frontier]] to report it. When [[Temesh]] and his [[Pathfinder team (Lightbringer)|Pathfinder team]] arrived, they were told that Bood was a thief and under arrest. However, [[Talik]] and [[Morla]] were able to hack the [[Jagothian Dominion]] communications and discovered that Bood was actually employed by [[Petro Arglass]].<ref name="Pathfinders 1" />
