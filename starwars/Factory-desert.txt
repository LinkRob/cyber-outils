'''Factory-deserts''' were a type of terrain found on the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="BTC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> [[Phorsa Gedd]], where the evenings there saw a venting of [[Temperature|heat]] until the next [[Local day|day]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> During the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Mid Rim Retreat|campaign]] into the Mid Rim,<ref name="BTC" /> which began around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] [[Battle on Phorsa Gedd|fought]] in Phorsa Gedd's [[factory]]-[[desert]]s.<ref name="BTC" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{AlphabetSquadronDate}}</ref> [[Wyl Lark]] and [[Nath Tensent]]&mdash;who served as [[New Republic pilot|pilots]] for the Alliance's [[Declaration of a New Republic|successor]], the [[New Republic]]&mdash;told stories of their worst nights in the wilderness, with one of them mentioning how they spent evenings in the factory-deserts of Phorsa Gedd.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

==Behind the scenes==
Factory deserts were first mentioned in [[Alexander Freed]]'s [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''.<ref name="BTC" />
