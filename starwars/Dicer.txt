'''Dicer''' was a [[human]]<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base" /> [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Databank" /> [[pilot]] who flew under the [[callsign]] [[Phoenix Six]]<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base" /> in [[3 BBY]].<ref name="3 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[The Mystery of Chopper Base]]" to [[3 BBY]].</ref> She was a [[lieutenant]] in [[Phoenix Cell]], a [[fleet]] composed of [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wing starfighters]], and operating under the command of [[Jun Sato]]. During her deployment at the recently established [[Chopper Base]] on the planet [[Atollon]], Dicer was tasked with placing a [[sensor beacon]] on the eastern end of the base for protection against any unwelcome visitors. After placing the sensor, Dicer was taken by a [[krykna]], a [[spider]]-like creature native to the planet, and presumably eaten. Dicer's disappearance was reported to [[Sabine Wren]], who decided to go looking for her with the help of [[clone trooper]] [[Rex]]. When they arrived on the scene, all they found was her A-wing and her helmet.<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dicer first appeared in "[[The Mystery of Chopper Base]],"<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base" /> the twentieth episode in the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', which aired on [[March 23]], [[2016]].<ref name="Insider 169">{{InsiderCite|169|Star Wars Rebels: Season Two Episode Guide|''Star Wars Rebels'': Season Two Episode Guide}}</ref> She was voiced by [[Ashley Eckstein]], who also voices former [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] in the series. In the episode's credits, the character's name is spelled "Deiser."<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base" /> Dicer was identified as Mia Dicer in the Google Play Store page for the mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Starfighter Missions]]'', but the name wasn't included in the game.<ref name="Starfighter Missions">''[[Star Wars: Starfighter Missions]]''</ref>
