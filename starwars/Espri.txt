The '''Espri''' were a group of [[Evereni]] whose name meant "to have" in the [[Evereni (language)|language]] of their [[species]]. The siblings [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]] of the species' [[Faers|Faer family]] knew Evereni bearing the name Espri.<ref name="Closed Fist">{{StoryCite|story=A Closed Fist Has No Claws|book=The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref> At some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The segment of "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" depicting a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi's]] [[death]] onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' takes place around [[379 BBY]] per reasoning [[Template:ClosedFistJediDeathDate|here]]. [[Marda Ro]] met the [[Faers]] after the [[Jedi]]'s death; therefore, she must have met them no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the two Faers [[Marda Ro's hunt|encountered]] [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the Evereni<ref name="Closed Fist" /> founder of the [[Pirate|marauder]] group who would eventually become the [[Nihil]].<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref> Wanting to know more about<ref name="Closed Fist" /> her<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> species, she asked them to tell her more about the Evereni and learned, among other things, of the Espri.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Later, Ro mentioned them as one of the things she learned from the Faers while recounting her encounter with them for a [[Marda Ro's journal|journal entry]] she recorded for [[Ro family|her descendants]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during his youth, Ro's [[Gender|great-great-grandson]] [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to her journal, including the message where the Espri were mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Espri were mentioned in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> In an interview, Gratton revealed that<ref name="GrattonInterview" /> she<ref name="GrattonPronouns">{{Bluesky|username=tessagratton.bsky.social|name=[[Tessa Gratton]]|quote=she/he/they|archivedate=20251206043541}}</ref> finds the lack of naming conventions in ''[[Star Wars]]'' frustrating. He decided to give Evereni a naming convention, which was one of the first parts of the story they created.<ref name="GrattonInterview" />
