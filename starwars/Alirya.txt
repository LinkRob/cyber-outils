'''Alyria''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] member of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] who was out on the [[Galactic Frontier]] preaching the Path's teachings at the time of its [[Night of Sorrow|violent end]] on the [[planet]] [[Dalna]] in [[382 BBY]]. At some point between the Path's collapse and around [[381 BBY]], she was one of the few Path members tracked down by the [[Evereni]] [[pirate]] leader and former Path member [[Marda Ro]]. She was one of the few to accept Ro's offer to join her [[Nihil|group]] and Ro took Alirya aboard her ship, the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' to introduce her to a [[Shrikarai|creature]] known as the [[Great Leveler]]. Alirya quickly rose to a position by Ro's side as her [[Aide|assistant]].

Around [[379 BBY]], Alirya, Ro and two other beings in Ro's service observed the Leveler consume a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi]], and Alirya later conversed with Ro in the ''Gaze''{{'s}} [[throne hall]]. At some point no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], Alirya was present when the [[Twi'lek]] raider [[Fori Nagor]] presented captured refugees to Ro. Later, Alirya and Ro were alone in the throne hall and debated on the future of the group Ro led, and what Ro's goals were. When Ro managed to track down the Evereni siblings [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]] during her [[Marda Ro's hunt|hunt]] for others of her species, Alirya stood by Ro's side during the initial meeting and prepared [[Foodstuff|food]] for the three Evereni.

Feeling that her position was threatened by the two [[Faers]], Alirya attempted to murder Vika with a [[dagger]] during a feast thrown on the ''Gaze'' a few days later. Her attack was unsuccessful however, and she was killed by the Evereni woman instead. Alirya's death was avenged by Ro, who shot Vika with a [[blaster]]. Left without Alirya, Ro offered the human's former place by her side to Vika's brother, who hesitantly accepted. Later, Ro recounted her time with Alirya in [[Marda Ro's journal|messages]] she recorded for her [[Ro family|descendants]], which her great-great-grandson [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to by [[252 BBY]].

==Biography==
===Early life and joining Marda Ro===
{{Quote|I have collected some scattered members of the Path of the Open Hand, those who never knew the Closed Fist.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
[[File:PathOfTheOpenHandMembersSymbol-CharactersOfSWTHR.png|thumb|right|300px|Alirya was a member of the Path of the Open Hand. (members pictured)]]
Born around [[394 BBY]],<ref name="Birth" /> Alirya was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who became a member of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> at some point no later than [[382 BBY]].<ref name="PathJoin">''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' establishes that the [[Path of the Open Hand]] dissolved in the aftermath of the [[Night of Sorrow]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]]. Therefore, Alirya must have become a member of the Path at some point no later than 382 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> In her service to the Path she ventured out onto the [[Galactic Frontier]] to spread the cult's teachings and was therefore not present for<ref name="Closed Fist" /> the [[Night of Sorrow]] which took place on the Path's [[Homeworld|home]] [[planet]] [[Dalna]]<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> in 382 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]<ref name="PathJoin" /> and led to its dissolution. Oblivious to the Path's collapse, Alirya carried on with her mission.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

She was among the few scattered Path members that were tracked down by an [[Evereni]] former member of the Path called [[Marda Ro]] in the aftermath of the Path's fall<ref name="Closed Fist" /> at some point no later than around [[381 BBY]].<ref name="RecruitDate">"[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" establishes that [[Marda Ro]] visited [[Dantooine]] around a [[Standard year|year]] after taking possession of the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', an event that ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]]. Therefore, Ro's trip to Dantooine must have occurred around [[381 BBY]]. Alirya was recruited by [[Marda Ro]] into ther [[Nihil|group]] of [[pirate]]s prior to her visit to Dantooine. Therefore, Ro must have recruited her no later than around 381 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]</ref> Ro told Alirya a false story about how the Path ended in order to position<ref name="Closed Fist" /> herself<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> at the center of Alirya's faith and desires. She then offered Alirya to either join her band of [[pirate]]s<ref name="Closed Fist" />&mdash;who would eventually become the [[Nihil]]<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref>&mdash;or cease with any Path activity. Alirya was one of the few who accepted Ro's offer, becoming the sixth member of the group.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

After joining, Alirya was taken by Ro aboard her [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Gaze Electric]]''. There, Ro introduced Alirya to a [[Living Force|Force]]-consuming [[Shrikarai|beast]] called the [[Great Leveler]]. She explained that they are now the caretakers of the Leveler, keeping it safe so that one day it can return and extinguish the [[Jedi Order|abusers]] of [[the Force]] in the future. Ro also added that there was no religion to share or a story to tell [[the galaxy]] anymore, and that the only thing of importance was surviving. Alirya quickly became Ro's right hand and secretary, and unlike most other beings serving Ro did not go out and raid. She instead stayed on the ''Gaze Electric'' and focused on finding opportunities for the group and plotting the course.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

===Death of a Jedi===
{{Quote|Alirya, Milan Stor, and Rox entered, as I’d instructed on approach. They stared at the bloodied Jedi sprawled on the cold floor of the hold. Alirya covered her mouth in shock when she saw me.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
[[File:Jedi_symbol.svg|thumb|left|230px|Alirya was present when the Great Leveler turned a member of the Jedi Order (symbol pictured) into a lifeless husk.]]
Around [[379 BBY]],<ref name="JediDate">{{ClosedFistJediDeathDate}}</ref> Alirya was aboard the ''Gaze'' when Ro returned to the ship aboard a [[shuttle]] with a heavily injured [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi]] on the floor of the shuttle's hold. As instructed by her, Alirya and two other subordinates of Ro&mdash;[[Rox]] and [[Milan Stor]]&mdash;boarded the shuttle and stared at the [[Jedi]]'s body. Seeing Ro covered in the Jedi's [[Color|red]] [[blood]], Alirya was shocked and inquired whether Ro was well, which Ro confirmed. Ro ordered them to take the Jedi's body and led them to the ''Gaze's'' [[Unidentified medical observation room|medical observation room]] where the Leveler was kept behind a [[containment shield]]. Ro turned the shield off and observed as the Leveler turned the Jedi into a lifeless, [[stone]]-like husk, but Alirya looked away immediately.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Ro sent the leveler back to the medical observation room and then strode away towards the ''Gaze's'' [[throne hall]]. Alirya went after Ro and found her sitting on her [[throne]], looking at a bloodied reflection of herself in a [[holo-mirror]]. She interrupted Ro, who screamed at her to get out. Ro then ignored her until Alirya called Ro "the Mother," a title belonging to the Path's [[Elecia Zeveron|former leader]]. Angered by the comparison, Ro reached out to claw off Alirya's face, only to discover that Alirya was kneeling halfway across the hall from her.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Alirya then informed Ro that Stor had set a course for the ''Gaze'', however Ro wasn't focused on her anymore. She stayed silent and listened to voices of [[Ghost (folklore)|ghost]]s calling out her name until Alirya made her snap back to reality with an echoing scream. Walking over to Ro with teary eyes, she offered to take care of her, which Ro refused, commanding her to sit with her and Alirya then sat crouched in front of Ro's throne for some time. Eventually she left, and Ro tore down the banners decorating the hall to paint a large red [[Star|sun]] on the wall.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

===Further service to Ro===
{{Quote|What are we doing, Marda?''"<br />"''We are drinking tea, Alirya.|Alirya and Marda Ro on the pirate group's purpose|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
[[File:GreatLeveler-2022THR9.png|thumb|right|250px|During her time serving Ro, Alirya had seen the Great Leveler (pictured) several times.]]
At some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]],<ref name="RaidDate">The segment of "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" depicting a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi's]] [[death]] onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' takes place around [[379 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:ClosedFistJediDeathDate|here]]. The raid in which the [[Unidentified green-skinned man|green-skinned man]] and other refugees were captured by [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Pirate|raider]]s occurs after the [[Jedi]]'s death; therefore, it must have taken place no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the other beings who worked for Ro carried out a raid on a camp of refugees and&mdash;as the refugees had no valuable posessions to steal&mdash;took the refugees as their prize. Alirya was present when the [[Twi'lek]] [[Fori Nagor]]&mdash;one of the raiders&mdash;presented the refugees to Ro, who was angered by the act. She asked the captured refugees whether any of them were [[Force-sensitive]] and while she waited for an answer, Alirya walked over to kneel next to Ro.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Getting no answer, Ro threatened the refugees and a [[Unidentified green-skinned man|green-skinned man]] from among them confessed to being Force-sensitive. Ro announced to the other refugees that they could choose to either join her group or be dropped off at the next [[spaceport]], and then threatened Nagor to never attempt anything like this again. Nagor took all refugees except the green-skinned man away, leaving only Ro, the man, and Alirya in the throne hall. Ro then commanded the man to follow her and took him to the Great Leveler.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

At some point after the incident with refugees, Ro took the ''Gaze'' away from known systems and have her group lay low for some amount of time following the deaths of two members of Nagor's [[Fori's team|team]] in a firefight. At some point during that time, Alirya brought [[tea]] to the Evereni while she was sitting on her throne. Alirya questioned her about her goals, and the purpose of the group that she had assembled, which had significantly grown in size since Alirya joined. Ro was surprised by Alirya's questions, but explained that her plan for the group is to take whatever they want until they can do anything they wish and reminded Alirya that her personal goal is [[Marda Ro's hunt|seeking]] other Evereni. She also added that the group is not the Path anymore, but something new and different.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

===Meeting the Faers and death===
{{Quote|Your pet is sweet.''"<br />"''Alirya is not a pet. She is a part of my crew, and as important as she makes herself.|Vika Faer and Marda Ro talking about Alirya|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
Later, Ro managed to catch up to the Evereni siblings [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]], who stopped to refuel at a [[Unidentified space dock|space dock]] near the [[gas giant]] [[Siquay]]. Ro invited the [[Faers]] aboard the ''Gaze'' and met them in the throne hall. After the raiders [[Esstrop]] and [[Kortanio]] escorted the pair into the hall, Alirya walked over to Ro and stopped next to her, right by her throne, where she remained during of the encounter. Ro offered the Faers to dine with her and Alirya prepared [[Foodstuff|food]] for the three Evereni&mdash;thin cuts of meat and boiled [[grain]] with a rich souce and a chemical spice akin to [[Dalnan pepper]]&mdash;based on Ro's preferences.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

[[File:GazeElectricpaths.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Alirya died in the throne hall of the Gaze Electric. (pictured)]]
While Ro and the Faers ate, Alirya brought them a clear [[liquor]] and gave Ro concerned glances. After eating, Vika and Ro sparred. Once they stopped fighting, Alirya rushed to Ro and pressed a cloth against a wound on Ro's arm that she had suffered while sparring. The Faers brought up their interest in having a drink, but Alirya didn't acknowledge the siblings and kept taking care of Ro, reluctantly stopping only once the Evereni instructed her to do so. While she was bringing the Evereni siblings the liquor they had requested, Vika called Alirya a pet and Ro corrected her that Alirya is as important as she makes herself, which made Alirya gaze at Ro.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

The Faers stayed aboard the ''Gaze'' for the next few days and went out with Ro's marauders on a raid. When they returned, a feast was thrown in the throne hall to celebrate that everyone returned safely. Ro ate away from the others on her throne, but Vika Faer came over to sit with her. While the two talked, Alirya approached Faer from behind and stabber her with a [[dagger]]. She then backed up with the dagger in her hand and the Evereni swiftly turned around and grabbed Alirya by her neck and wrist. While Alirya kicked her in defense, Faer rose to her full height and lifted the human off the floor. She snapped Alirya's wrist and dug her claws into her neck, making Alirya cry out in pain. The Evereni then dropped her dead body onto the throne hall's floor<ref name="Closed Fist" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|All I could see was Alirya’s expression when I had said, ''As important as she makes herself''. And the old uncle telling me not to trust anyone. Everything falls away.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
Ro was shocked by Alirya's death. In response she took a [[blaster]] from a compartment hidden in her throne and shot Faer in retaliation, claiming Alirya was hers. Ro recalled what she had said about Alirya being as important as she makes herself, and realized that it was what motivated her to attempt murdering Faer. Losing the person who was always by her side, Ro then offered the position to Velya Faer. While enraged and somewhat distrustful after Ro killed his sister, Faer accepted the offer and claimed Alirya's former position by Ro's side. Soon after, Alirya's body and that of her killer were both cleaned away. Later, Ro extensively talked about Alirya in [[Marda Ro's journal|messages]] she recorded for her [[Ro family|descendants]] and stressed the importance of passing on her name.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during his youth, Ro's great-great-grandson [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to her journal, including the entries where Alirya was mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Marda. Mother?|Alirya|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
[[File:MardaRo-PathOfDeceit.png|thumb|right|Alirya devotedly served Marda Ro. (pictured)]]
Alirya was completely devoted to Ro and saw her similarly to the Mother, the Path's leader, even referring to her as "the Mother" once. She believed in Ro and wanted nothing more than to care for her, becoming concerned when she saw Ro wounded or unwell. Alirya was desperate to stay at Ro's site, turning to murder when she perceived Vika and Velya Faer as threatening her position.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Despite being young, Alirya wanted responsibility. Her request was indulged by Ro, who saw herself in Alirya and gave her what she herself never had. Alirya often became frustrated when Ro wanted to go in a different direction that the one Alirya had planned just to follow leads on other Evereni, but ultimately obeyed Ro despite her frustration.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Even though she abandoned the Path in favor of joining Ro and her pirates, Alirya struggled letting go of the Path. Even years later, she felt that Ro and the raiders were lost, as the Evereni didn't speak of the Force or the Path anymore. The human girl was thin and had hair of a red-brown shade similar to dried human blood. She had cut the hair during her time serving Ro and arranged it into spikes. She had small, blunt [[Tooth|teeth]] that&mdash;like her nails&mdash;would struggle cutting through flesh. Alirya's touch was cold, and, by the time she recorded the message for her descendants, Ro missed it.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I needed it to be violent and even, like, I got notes back the first time I sent it in and they were like 'Can you make this worse?' and I was like 'I'm so glad you asked!'|Tessa Gratton, talking about the scene in which Alirya dies|<ref name="GrattonInterview" />}}
Alirya appeared in a flashback in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> In an interview, Gratton discussed that<ref name="GrattonInterview" /> she<ref name="GrattonPronouns">{{Bluesky|username=tessagratton.bsky.social|name=[[Tessa Gratton]]|quote=she/he/they|archivedate=20251206043541}}</ref> wanted the scene in which<ref name="GrattonInterview" /> Alirya<ref name="Closed Fist" /> and Vika Faer died to feel like a climatic moment of the story. He also added that they wanted it to be brutal, and was glad when notes on the first submitted draft of the story asked if the scene could be made more violent.<ref name="GrattonInterview" />
