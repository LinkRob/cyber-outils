The '''Dewback Deceivers''' was an outfit owned by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Salvagers|standard costume]]. This garb was designed to resembled an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Desert stormtrooper|sandtrooper]] mounted on a [[dewback]], with the upper Jawa wearing a weather [[stormtrooper]] [[Armor|chest plate]], as well as a [[Color|white]] head wrapping and sleeves, as well as an orange and black [[pauldron]] commonly associated with [[Stormtrooper Commander]]s. From the waist of the upper Jawa down, including the entirety of the lower Jawa's [[robe]]s, the material resembled the scaly yellowish-green coloration of a [[Reptile|reptilian]] dewback, with various brown straps and satchels draped overtop. Strapped to their back via a large brown bag with silver buckles, was a swath of reddish-brown [[Hair|fur]], as well as part of a [[SD-48 survival backpack]].<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dewback Deceivers was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Utooni, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dewback Set]], though it may have had a secondary "Epic" rarity counterpart known as the [[Deceiver Set]] which, otherwise, had no matching Costume, making it potentially the only cosmetic in the game to be a part of two sets at once<ref name="Hunters" />
