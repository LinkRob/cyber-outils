'''Deana Kiner''' is a music composer who worked on three ''[[Star Wars]]'' animated series&mdash;''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'',<ref name="Iron Squadron">{{Rebels|Iron Squadron (episode)}}</ref> the [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|seventh season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref> and ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref>&mdash;alongside her father, [[Kevin Kiner]], and brother, [[Sean Kiner]]. She graduated from Berklee College of Music.<ref name="The Kiner Siblings Music" /> In 2023, Deana came out as transgender.<ref name="Pronouns" />
