{{Quote|Thanks to your planet's rich resources, I have created the next generation Dark Trooper suit forged from beskar alloy.|Moff Gideon|<ref name="Chapter 23" />}}
The '''Dark Trooper phase 4'''<ref name="GoH Kit Reveal">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/265853/kit-reveal-dark-trooper-moff-gideon|text=Kit Reveal - Dark Trooper Moff Gideon}}</ref> was the fourth iteration and the culmination of the [[Dark Trooper]], though it was a battle [[armor]] developed in the [[Mandalore Imperial base]] as an upgrade over the [[Phase-III Dark Trooper|third-generation]] Dark Trooper to be worn by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]] and his planned [[Gideon's clones|clone army]]. Forged from [[beskar]] alloy collected from [[Mandalore]], the fourth-generation design borrowed heavily from [[Mandalorian armor]] philosophy design, and incorporated a visored [[helmet]], [[Mandalorian vambrace|vambraces]] with integrated weaponry, and a [[jetpack]]. Additionally, the armor enhanced Gideon's strength, allowing him to deliver powerful blows and to crush the [[Darksaber]] in one hand.<ref name="Chapter 23" />
