{{Quote|We had a true ''victory'' at Elessia. Two full rebel divisions ''reunited'', and we sent ''Commander Zahra'' and her Imperials running.|[[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]|<ref name="Star Wars 9">[[Star Wars (2020) 9|''Star Wars'' (2020) 9]]</ref>}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> a '''battle''' was fought at [[Elessia]]<ref name="Star Wars 9" /> between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and [[Galactic Empire]], when Imperial [[Commander]] [[Ellian Zahra]] and her [[Hunter Fleet|forces]] intercepted transmissions between the rebel [[Fourth Division|Fourth]] and [[Seventh Division]]s, who were attempting to regroup after the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] was scattered after the [[Battle of Hoth]].<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

Zahra initially intended to send her [[Battle group|assault group]] to attack the Fourth [[Division]] in order to exact revenge on [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] for the [[death]] of her mentor, [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], but was convinced to instead send her [[Hunter Fleet's secondary strike force|second strike force]] to attack Organa's group as it was closer to the Fourth Division, and Zahra's group was closer to the Seventh Division. By the time the second force had arrived, however, the Fourth were already heading to Elessia to regroup with the Seventh, arriving shortly after Zahra's assault group began attacking the Seventh.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

As the two rebel divisions battled the Imperial forces, Zahra boarded the Fourth Division's [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] and disabled the safety controls on the [[Starship|vessels]] [[reactor]], but insisted that she would reactivate them if Organa spoke with her alone in the ships [[engine]] room. Organa complied with the request and made her way to the engine room. After Zahr informed Organa of her reasoning why she sought revenge and before she could carry it out, Zahra was informed by [[Lieutenant]] [[Gorr]] that her [[flagship]], the ''[[Tarkin's Will]]'', was taking significant fire from the rebel divisions and that he was preparing to flee and that she should return to the ship. Zahra fled the rebel ship, and made her way to the ''Tarkin's Will'' before it departed Elessia.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />
