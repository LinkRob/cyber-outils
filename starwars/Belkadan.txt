'''Belkadan''' was a celestial body<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> located in the [[Belkadan system]] of the [[Dalonbian sector]], a part of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was situated in grid square L-1 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Beyond Victory Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> a [[viewscreen]] in [[Sornah's Garage]] on<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Malastare DB">{{Databank|malastare|Malastare}}</ref> [[Malastare]] featured a job listing for a deep [[Realspace|space]] signal surveyor based at Belkadan.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

==Behind the scenes==
Belkadan's name was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' boxed set.<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Belkadan system]]</ref> The [[2025]] [[Wikipedia:Virtual reality|virtual reality]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]],<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> developed by [[Industrial Light & Magic]] and published by [[Disney Electronic Content]],<ref name="Meta">{{WebCite|url=https://www.meta.com/experiences/star-wars-beyond-victory/9460903453969753|text=Star Wars: Beyond Victory|work=[[Wikipedia:Meta Platforms|Meta]]|archivedate=20250814000239}}</ref> was the first to describe it as a distinct celestial body.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Belkadan/Legends|Belkadan]] first appeared in [[The New Jedi Order: Vector Prime|''Vector Prime'']], a [[1999]] novel authored by [[R. A. Salvatore]] as the first entry of the ''[[Star Wars: The New Jedi Order]]'' series.<ref name="Vector Prime">''[[The New Jedi Order: Vector Prime]]''</ref> It was first visually depicted in an [[:File:Luke Onslaught.jpg|illustration]] by [[Mikael Noguchi]] that was used on the cover of the [[Star Wars Gamer 8|eighth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Gamer]]'' magazine,<ref name="Gamer 8">[[Star Wars Gamer 8|''Star Wars Gamer'' 8]]</ref><ref name="Onslaught">''[[The New Jedi Order: Dark Tide I: Onslaught]]''</ref> published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] on [[December 21]], [[2001]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SWArchive|url=gaming/other/rpg/news20011210.html|text=Yuuzhan Vong Invade ''Gamer'' #8}}</ref>
