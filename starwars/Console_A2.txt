'''Console A2''' was based aboard a [[shuttle]] by the [[First Order]] while on<ref name="Breakout">{{Resistance|Breakout}}</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="The Missing Agent">{{Resistance|The Missing Agent}}</ref> [[Varkana]].<ref name="Breakout" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|2|12}}</ref> the [[Resistance]] [[droid]] [[CB-23]] was kept there by [[First Order]] [[Commander]] [[Pyre]] in an attempt to decrypt the droid's [[Memory bank|memory core]]. During her own interrogation of the Resistance member [[Jarek Yeager]], [[First Order Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Tierny]] was called by a [[tech stormtrooper]] to assist at the console, forcing her to end her questioning of Yeager. After she arrived, Pyre left the agent to continue the attempt to crack the memory core until CB was able to successfully escape.<ref name="Breakout" />
