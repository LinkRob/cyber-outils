The '''deep caverns''' were a series of caves located in [[the Tomb]], an ancient [[Prison/Legends|prison]] built on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Belsavis/Legends|Belsavis]] by the [[Rakata/Legends|Rakata]]. Accessible via [[Frozencavern Canyon]], the caverns were overrun by the [[Esh-kha]] [[Species/Legends|species]] during the [[invasion of Belsavis]], and [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Devlin (Nautolan)|Devlin's]] [[Soldier/Legends|soldiers]] were held captive in the caverns.<ref name="TOR" />

==Behind the scenes==
The deep caverns appear as a location of the Tomb on Belsavis in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', and are the site of the Republic mission "Riot."<ref name="TOR" />
