The '''GA-series servant droid''',<ref name="BR2D268">{{BuildR2Cite|68|Droid Directory}}</ref> or just '''GA servant droid''' for short, was a model of [[Service droid|servant droid]].<ref name="A Junior Novel" /> It had collapsable [[Limb|legs]] for compact storage, contained a [[power plant]], and featured [[hydraulic line]]s.<ref name="The Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> By the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> GA units were used in [[Takodana Castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]]. One such servant [[droid]] was [[GA-97]], a member of the [[Resistance spy droid network]]<ref name="GA-97">{{Databank|ga-97|GA-97}}</ref> who informed the [[Resistance]] that one of their [[astromech droid]]s, [[BB-8]], was on Takodana.<ref name="The Force Awakens" />
