During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Creighton Sun]] utilized a [[Color|blue]]-bladed [[Crossguard lightsaber|crossguard]] '''lightsaber'''. He used the lightsaber to help remove [[Morton San Tekka]]'s body from rubble that he was trapped under after the [[Attack on the peace summit]] at the [[Second Spire]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]]. He later used it during the [[Battle of Jedha]]<ref name="Battle of Jedha" /> and the [[Night of Sorrow]].<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref>
