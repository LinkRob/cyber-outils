The '''Cosmic Force''' was the wellspring from which the [[Living Force]], the energy of all life, sprang into all living things, and into which all life was feeding upon death, becoming one with it.<ref name="HRA 7" /><ref name="Voices" /><ref name=":0">{{TCW|Destiny (The Clone Wars)}}</ref> Together, the Cosmic Force and the Living Force were the two parts making up the energy field created by, and also penetrating and surrounding all living things, that was life, death and rebirth and everything in between<ref>[[The Secrets of the Jedi]]</ref> - [[the Force]]. The Cosmic Force bound all things,<ref name="Voices">{{TCW|Voices}}</ref> moving through and beyond [[Realspace|space]] and [[time]],<ref name="The Force DB">{{Databank|the-force|The Force}}</ref> and was connected to [[destiny]].<ref name="Nature">{{InsiderCite|216|Forces of Nature: The Wild Spaces and Wilder Creatures of the Star Wars Galaxy|Forces of Nature: The Wild Spaces and Wilder Creatures of the ''Star Wars'' Galaxy}}</ref> Through the Cosmic Force,<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> [[midi-chlorian]]s birthed in [[Wellspring of Life]], the foundation connecting the Cosmic Force with the Living Force,<ref name=":0" /> formed a symbiotic relationship with living beings and communicated to them the will of the Cosmic Force.<ref name="The Force DB" /> Because of this will, nothing occurred in [[the galaxy]] by accident.<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>

By clearing the mind of any thoughts and acting on instinct, [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Force-sensitive|Force wielders]] could listen to the Force's will<ref name="The Force DB" /> and become an instrument of it,<ref name="TLJ EE">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> fulfilling their predestined path. The [[Jedi]] were agents of the Force's will.<ref name="The Force DB" /> [[Let'ij]] believed the will of the Force desired the flourishing of life, harmony, and community, and was only heard by those who operated as a larger organism, a community.<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> However, all lifeforms attuned to the presence of the Force through the midi-chlorians could hear it.<ref name="TSWB" />

In [[death]], one chose to enter the Cosmic Force;<ref name="TSWB" /> [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo]] did not pass into the Force until her [[Parent|son]] [[Ben Solo]] died as a redeemed [[Jedi]],<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''<nowiki/>''</ref> where the two entered the Cosmic Force together and found peace at last.<ref name="TSWB" /> Becoming "one with the Force" upon death was not a simple expression; according to [[Jedi Master]] [[Obratuk Glii]], an individual's death served to give "life" to the Cosmic Force, which in turn generated the continued existence of the Living Force.<ref name="HRA 7" /> [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker]] [[Gella Nattai]]'s understanding of the Force taught her that one who died was not 'truly gone,' as they instead became one with the Force in a way no living being could manage. Beyond that, she could look back on and remember those who passed, which in its own way kept such people alive.<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref>

Former Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Verla]] had a very negative view of the Cosmic Force, labelling the entirety of the Force a "trap" that lured people like her in with promises of power or making a difference in the galaxy. To her, it was a force of "bizarre cosmic goals" that treated such goals as destiny, treating people like herself and [[Luke Skywalker]] as mere tools for that will. She feared that following the Force only resulted in being broken and chewed apart. While Skywalker understood her concerns, he was unable to turn his back on the galaxy and believed he was being called to help, much to her annoyance.<ref name="Star Wars (2020) 6">[[Star Wars (2020) 6|''Star Wars'' (2020) 6]]</ref>

As explained by the [[Force-Sensitive Outcast|former]] Jedi [[Ahsoka Tano]], since the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] became a part of the Cosmic Force after life, his will could manifest within a [[loth-wolf]], which went by the name of "[[Dume]]" and briefly communicated with Jarrus' [[Padawan]] learner, [[Ezra Bridger]].<ref name="A World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref> After the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Cosmic Force became dormant and was seemingly quieted to those who could sense its presence. Turbulence within the Cosmic Force coincided with the awakening of latent [[Force power|Force abilities]] in individuals such as [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and [[Finn]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Don't be too proud of this technological terror you've created. The ability to destroy a planet, indeed, a whole system, is insignificant compared with the Cosmic Force.|Darth Vader, voiced by David Prowse, in an earlier cut of ''A New Hope''|<ref name="Conference-del">{{YouTube|video=8Erf6s_wYJk|text=Rare New Hope Deleted Death Star Meeting Extended- Star Wars Celebration 2017 RARE|channel=channel/UC9_7TY9knptriw4dFDi3sww|name=Star Wars Experience Network}}</ref>}}
The Cosmic Force was first identified in<ref name="Conference-del" /> {{Film|IV}}, albeit in a scene that was deleted from the film by the time of its release in [[1977]].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> The term was mentioned by Darth Vader, voiced by [[David Prowse]],<ref name="Conference-del" /> as part of the first mention of the Force in one revised [[1976]] fourth draft of the film.<ref name="Draft4">{{WebCite|url=https://www.starwarz.com/starkiller/star-wars-the-adventures-of-luke-starkiller-revised-fourth-draft|text=Star Wars: The Adventures of Luke Starkiller (Revised Fourth Draft)|author=Bjorn Wahlberg|date=2010-03-31|archivedate=20201111224954}}</ref> The final cut of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' film reduces the mention of the Cosmic Force to the Force, and the scene of Vader, who is dubbed by [[James Earl Jones]], is instead placed after the scene on [[Tatooine]] where Obi-Wan Kenobi defines the Force to Luke Skywalker.<ref name="ANH" />

In [[canon]], the Cosmic Force was first identified in the episode "[[Voices]]" from ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Voices" /> In officially released ''Star Wars'' media, the Cosmic Force was first identified in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel ''[[Coruscant Nights III: Patterns of Force]]''.<ref name="nightsIII">''[[Coruscant Nights III: Patterns of Force]]''</ref> Author [[John Jackson Miller]] claimed that the [[Unifying Force]] and Cosmic Force are one in the same, with "Unifying Force" simply the outdated name given to the Cosmic Force concept in older works. Miller described the difference between the Living Force and the Cosmic Force as "here and now" against "the big picture."<ref name="Living Prose">{{InsiderCite|225|John Jackson Miller: The Living Prose}}</ref>
