'''Annette Miles''' was the Key Hairdresser for the [[2005]] [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']]. On [[June 5]], [[2003]], she fitted actress [[Mimi Daraphet]] with a short wig as part of a test for her to appear as [[Bultar Swan]] in Episode III.<ref name="Separatists & Swan" /><ref name="Hyperspace Wordpress" />
