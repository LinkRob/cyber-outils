"'''Everyone's a Critic'''" is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie-in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[March 31]], [[2005]] and took the form of a number of holonet posts discussing a story released depicting [[Nightwing]], a member of the [[Thaereian military|Navy of Thaere]], killing his superior officer.
