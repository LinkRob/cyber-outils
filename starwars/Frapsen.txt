'''Frapsen''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Fromprath]] [[musician]] who played a [[batanga]]. Whilst at the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of [[Maz Kanata]] on [[Takodana]]<ref name="Last Shot" /> around [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashback events of ''[[Last Shot]]'' that feature [[Han Solo]] to around [[3 BBY]].</ref> he played a [[Music|song]] about missing [[Dathomir]]. Shortly after, he took down a [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] outside of the castle.<ref name="Last Shot" />
