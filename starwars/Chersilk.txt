'''Chersilk''' was a type of fabric used in [[clothing]] worn by [[Monarch of Naboo|Monarchs of Naboo]]. When she was [[Queen]] of [[Naboo]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] wore a semitransparent, [[Color|mauve]] chersilk [[hair]] veil with her [[dark purple traveling dress]].<ref name="TCVD" /> During [[Padmé Amidala's funeral|Amidala's funeral]], Queen [[Apailana]]'s [[funeral]] attire included an opaque, pleated, purple chersilk mourning [[robe]] layered beneath her white-gold outer robe.<ref name="CEUE" />
