'''Cream tea''' was a type of [[tea]] that was [[Diet|potable]] to [[human]]s. It could be served [[Temperature|cold]] and made with [[cream]].<ref name="TOTF" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the [[Nihil]] [[Minister of Information (Nihil)|Minister of Information]] [[Ghirra Starros]] visited a [[Unidentified Coruscant café|café]] in a central area of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], drinking a cold, [[crystal]] [[glass]] of the tea. She [[Brain|mentally]] reflected that the idyllic location was ideal for her to seem carefree while enjoying her beverage, therefore representing the Nihil's image better. However, after receiving a call from the Nihil's [[Eye of the Nihil|leader]]&mdash;[[Marchion Ro]]&mdash;not long afterward, she left the establishment.<ref name="TOTF" />
