A '''bluebell squish''' was a flowering [[plant]], with [[seed]]s that could be found on [[Koboh]]. It could be [[Color|yellow]] or blue in color.<ref name="Survivor" />

The plant was commonly found in damp environments around the galaxy, drawing moisture from the air around it. When planted in a less humid environment, the bluebell squish stored liquid in its mossy root sac, which expanded as it filled.<ref name="Survivor" />
