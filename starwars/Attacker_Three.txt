'''Attacker Three''' was the designation given to a [[heavy cruiser]] by the crew of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[warship]] [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']]. The [[Lioaoin Regime]] sent the [[heavy cruiser]] and three others for an [[Attack at Primea|attack]] on the ''Vigilant'', on behalf of the [[Nikardun Destiny]], when the [[Chiss]] [[Starship|vessel]] attempted to retrieve [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] from the [[planet]] [[Primea]]. Attacker Three, along with the [[cruiser]] designated [[Attacker Four|Four]], blocked the ''Viiglant''{{'s}} optimal escape route, so [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] had the warship target Three, while Attacker Four was heavily damaged by the [[Thrawn's patrol boat|Vak patrol boat]] that Thrawn was [[pilot]]ing.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
