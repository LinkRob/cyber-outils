'''Drill ships''' were a type of [[starship]] with long, sharp noses on the prow of their ship used for ramming into other ships, and were even capable of splitting small [[asteroid]]s. They were used during the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]] as part of [[E'ronoh's fleet]].<ref name="Convergence" /> in [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> The [[Spiral-class Viz-Core Eviscerator|''Spiral''-class Viz-Core Eviscerator]] was a model of drill ship.<ref name="EFV">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref>
