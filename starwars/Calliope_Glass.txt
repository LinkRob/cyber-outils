'''Calliope Glass''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' young readers author. She wrote the book ''[[AT-AT Attack!]]'', which adapts the first act of the film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back]]. The book is part of the [[World of Reading]] series of books for the [[original trilogy]] of films, published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]. It was released on [[November 3]], [[2015]].<ref name="AT-At">{{Amazon|url=World-Reading-AT-AT-Attack-Level/dp/1484705491|text=World of Reading Star Wars AT-AT Attack! (Level 1)|archivedate=20151224210635}}</ref>
