"'''Differences of Opinion'''" was a short story published in ''[[The Last Command Sourcebook]]''.

This story relates the quarrel between [[Garm Bel Iblis/Legends|Garm Bel Iblis]] and [[Mon Mothma/Legends|Mon Mothma]] during the preparation of the [[Attack on Milvayne|attack]] on [[Milvayne/Legends|Milvayne]] that eventually leads him to break off from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]].
