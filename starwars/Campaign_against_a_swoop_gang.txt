The [[Imperial Military|forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] carried out a long '''campaign''' against a [[Unidentified swoop gang (Rishi)|swoop gang]] of [[Pirate|bandits]] wreaking havoc on<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Rishi]], ultimately neutralizing the [[Swoop gang|gang]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> The conclusion of the campaign was covered on the [[HoloNet News]] by [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Dorna Parros]], who provided an estimate of 3.5 million [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] for the cost of damages the gang had caused over the prior [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
