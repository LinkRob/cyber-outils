'''Derso-4''' was a location in the [[Derso system/Legends|Derso system]].<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> The [[Leisure Fields]] were located beyond Derso-4.<ref name="MAGL">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Derso-4 was mentioned in a one-page cooking recipe titled "Cooking a Smapp", written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that collects concept designs and photographs from various [[George Lucas]] projects.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]].<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
