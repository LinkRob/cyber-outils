'''Dathomiri clan''', also known as '''coven''' or '''witch clan''' was the name given to a group or assembly of Force-users on the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]]. By the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Nightsisters" /> [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]-wielding [[Nightsisters]] had been united into a single [[Mother Talzin's clan|clan]]<ref name="Mother Talzin card">{{Topps|set=2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|subset=Jedi Temple Archives|cardname=Mother Talzin|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/114595/cid/8022653/2015-Topps-Star-Wars-Chrome-Perspectives:-Jedi-Vs.-Sith-49-J-Mother-Talzin}}</ref> under [[Mother (title)|Clan Mother]] [[Talzin]].<ref name="Nightsisters" /> Other clans included the [[Blue Coral Divers Clan]], the [[Singing Mountain (clan)|Singing Mountain]], and the [[Howling Crag]].<ref name="Bug" />
