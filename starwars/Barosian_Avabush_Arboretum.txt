The '''Barosian Avabush Arboretum''' was a location in [[the galaxy]] that contained greenhouses of [[avabush]]. The [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] undertook a [[heist in the Barosian Avabush Arboretum]], managing to get away with an entire greenhouse.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
