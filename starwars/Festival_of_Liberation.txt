The '''Festival of Liberation''' was a seven-[[Standard day|day]] fete that celebrated the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]], which marked the end of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Standard year|24 year]] [[Imperial Era|reign]] and the conclusion of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. It was first observed in [[5 ABY]], as a replacement for [[Liberation Day (New Republic)|Liberation Day]].<ref name="AEE" />
