At least two [[Gender|male]] members of the [[Starros Clan]] were the '''uncles''' of the [[human]] girl [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> By [[235 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'' to [[232 BBY]]. ''A Test of Courage'' establishes that [[J-6]] was given to [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]] three [[Standard year|years]] before its events, and that Starros was given J-6 after the [[Kidnapping of Avon Starros|kidnapping]]. Therefore, the kidnapping must have taken place by [[235 BBY]].</ref> Starros was [[Kidnapping of Avon Starros|kidnapped]] by a [[Unidentified local group|local group]] on<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Hosnian Prime]], but was eventually found by her uncles, who then [[Death|killed]] those that had taken their niece.<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> while on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Wevo]], Starros told the [[Dalnan]] boy [[Honesty Weft]] about the [[time]] she had been captured, and of her uncles' actions in finding her and then killing her kidnappers.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />
