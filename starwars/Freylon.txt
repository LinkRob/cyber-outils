'''Freylon''' was a [[commodore]] in the [[Imperial Navy|navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] who served as the [[commanding officer]] of the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Imperial-class)|''Steadfast'']]. Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], he was [[Death|killed]] when the ''Steadfast'' was ambushed by a small [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] at the [[Battle of Ponolapo]]. Following the battle, Commodore [[Scaanos]] replaced Freylon as the commanding [[officer]] of the ''Steadfast'', infuriating [[Captain]] [[Enric Pryde]], who believed he should have received the command instead.

==Biography==
[[File:YoungPryde-2020DarthVader42cover.png|thumb|right|150px|Enric Pryde (pictured) believed he should have received command of the ''Steadfast'' after Freylon's death.]]
Freylon was a [[commodore]] in the [[Imperial Navy|navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] who served as the [[commanding officer]] of<ref name="Rise" /> the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|The First Order}}</ref> [[Steadfast (Imperial-class)|''Steadfast'']].<ref name="Rise" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Battle Date" /> the ''Steadfast'' was escorting an Imperial [[convoy]] when it was ambushed at the [[Battle of Ponolapo]] by a small [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]]. Due to the Empire's recent [[War against the Sith|conflict]] with the criminal organization [[Crimson Dawn]], the ''Steadfast'' did not have its usual accompaniment of several [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti'']] or [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens'']] [[light cruiser]]s, which allowed the rebels to destroy most of the convoy and damage the ''Steadfast'', [[Death|killing]] Freylon.<ref name="Rise" /> 

Following Freylon's death, Commodore [[Scaanos]] replaced him as the commanding [[officer]] of the ''Steadfast''<ref name="Steadfast" /> and an [[Commodore Freylon Obituary|obituary]] was created for him and stored in the "Imperial Navy" section of the [[Imperial Archives]].<ref name="Rise" /> [[Captain]] [[Enric Pryde]], the longest-serving crewmember of the ''Steadfast'', was infuriated that Scaanos had received the command instead of him. The [[Local day|day]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Steadfast" /> in 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="SteadFast Date">The events of "[[The Steadfast Soldier]]" take place the [[Standard day|day]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. As ''Timelines'' also dates the events of [[Shattered Empire 1|''Shattered Empire'' 1]], which also take place the day after the battle, to the same [[Standard year|year]], "The Steadfast Soldier" must also take place in 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> while thinking about his dislike of Scaanos, Pryde mentally recalled Freylon's death.<ref name="Steadfast" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] referenced both Freylon's obituary and a file on the Battle of Ponolapo that was stored in the "Military Operations" section of the [[Rebel Alliance Archives]] when<ref name="Rise" /> he<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> mentioned Freylon in his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />

==Personality and traits==
Pryde believed that Freylon's carelessness was the cause of his death at the Battle of Ponolapo.<ref name="Steadfast" />

==Equipment==
Freylon commanded the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''Steadfast''.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Freylon was first mentioned, albeit indirectly, in the short story "[[The Steadfast Soldier]]," written by [[Adam Christopher]] and published in the [[2023]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="Steadfast" /> He was later identified in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
