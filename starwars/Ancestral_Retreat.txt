The '''Ancestral Retreat''', also known as the '''Imperial Retreat''',<ref name="Star Wars 63">[[Star Wars (2015) 63|''Star Wars'' (2015) 63]]</ref> was a sacred site for the [[Shu-Torun royal family|royalty]] of the [[planet]] [[Shu-Torun]], and was considered one of the world's wonders. The [[adamantine shoal]]s swam in the [[magma]] surrounding the structure, which was located close to an active [[volcano]]. By the [[time]] of the [[Shu-Torun War|War on Shu-Torun]], it had been a peaceful location for [[Millennium|thousands]] of [[Standard year|years]], and [[Monarch of Shu-Torun|Queen]] [[Trios]] traveled there with her [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] allies during the fighting in order to plan her next move and remind herself of what she was fighting for. Despite its sacred status, the retreat was then [[Attack on the ancestral retreat|attacked]] by the [[Lava Leviathan]], a vessel owned by the rebellious [[Ore-duke]] [[Baron]] [[Rubix]] who hoped to [[Death|kill]] Trios. Trios attempted to flee from the retreat in a [[shuttle]], but the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] commandeered the [[Starship|vessel]] and used it to board the leviathan with his Imperial allies [[Morit Astarte]] and [[Aiolin Astarte]]. The trio then sunk the vessel and returned to the retreat with Trios.<ref name="V17" />
