'''Durkteel''' was an agricultural [[planet]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[Saurin]] [[species]].<ref name="Saurin" /> Durkteel was also the homeworld of [[Mattis Banz]], a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] who joined the [[Resistance]]. [[Lund Gourley]] and [[Lund Berlo]] were [[City|cities]] located on Durkteel.<ref name="Join the Resistance" />

==History==
This planet was liberated by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in the Mid Rim Offensive, but abandoned in the subsequent [[Mid Rim Retreat|retreat]]. By [[4 ABY]], the Empire had yet to reoccupy it.<ref name="The Rebel Files" />

In 4 ABY, the Alliance circulated rumors that [[Mon Mothma]] would make a visit to the former rebel base in the [[Tak-Beam complex]] as part of a number of diversionary operations designed to distract the Empire from their preparations for the [[Battle of Endor|attack on Endor]]. To provide weight to the rumors, Mothma decided to actually go there herself. Her aide [[Hendri Underholt]] convinced her to let her go in her place.<ref name="The Rebel Files" />

Shortly after her arrival on the planet, three Imperial Star Destroyers arrived on the planet, following the rumors. Rather than deploying to the planet to capture or otherwise engage with the possible Rebel forces, the Imperial commander simply [[Bombardment of Durkteel|bombarded the area]] from [[orbit]].<ref name="The Rebel Files" />

[[Resistance]] member [[Mattis Banz]] grew up on an [[orphan farm]] before his recruitment.<ref name="Join the Resistance" />

Around [[34 ABY]], a construction crew breaking ground to build a new communications tower discovered a sealed room containing several relics from the war, including an armored case that was later discovered to hold ''[[The Rebel Files]]''. [[Vaxvissh Kal Ness]] was the [[Planetary Governor|planetary governor]] at the time, and had sent the Alliance files to the [[New Republic]].<ref name="The Rebel Files" />
