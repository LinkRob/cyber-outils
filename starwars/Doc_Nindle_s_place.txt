'''Doc Nindle's place''' was a building in [[Ferdan]], on [[Dalna]], that served as [[Doctor|Doc]] [[Nindle]]'s clinic and surgery center. It featured windows, an examination table, a cupboard with shelves, and storage drawers. When [[Ric Farazi]] fell ill at the [[Path compound|Path of the Open Hand compound]], she was taken to Doc Nindle's place. [[Jedi]] [[Oliviah Zeveron]] and [[Matthea Cathley]] attempted to help Nindle save Farazi, but she died on the examination table. Although Nindle had [[Stimpack|stim packs]], [[anti-toxin]]s, and a defib kit, he stated that it wasn't "exactly a top-of-the-line [[medcenter]]."<ref name="Vengeance" />
