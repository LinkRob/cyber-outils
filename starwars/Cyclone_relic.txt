A '''cyclone relic''' had been found at the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Excavation Site 12]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> It was a small artifact that could spin and was believed to have been used in ceremonial dances to symbolize the cycle of Akiva's storms.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] infiltrated the excavation site and stole the cyclone relic from a storage crate.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A cyclone relic appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, the relic can be equipped as a minor charm to give the player a high change to gain a free [[bacta vial]] or [[grenade]] when they are used.<ref name="Outlaws" />
