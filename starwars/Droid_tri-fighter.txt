The '''droid tri-fighter''' was a model of [[droid starfighter]] utilized by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It was specifically designed to engage and excel in heavy [[Starfighter combat|dogfighting]]. Despite being the smallest [[starship]] in both the [[Republic Navy]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederacy navy]], the tri-fighter was heavily armed, and possessed an advanced [[droid brain]], which gave it an advantage when dealing with organic pilots. These droid brains were more advanced than those intergrated in various other droid starfighters manufactured for the Confederacy. Conceived for the total destruction of an enemy, it possessed an array of missiles and other armaments.

A formidable opponent for [[Clone trooper pilot|clone pilots]] during the [[Galaxy|galactic]]-spanning conflict,<ref name="Episode III" /> the tri-fighter saw use at major engagements, including the [[Battle of Sullust]],<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref> [[Battle of Dathomir]],<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref> and [[Battle of Coruscant]]. At the end of the war, the tri-fighters were disabled when [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] initiated a shutdown command, deactivating all operational Separatist units.<ref name="Episode III" /> Five [[Standard year|years]] after the conclusion of the Clone Wars, tri-fighters could still be seen harassing the nascent [[Galactic Empire]] under the command of dissidents, with one such [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|cell]] being led by former [[Republic Intelligence]] official [[Berch Teller]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Characteristics==
A standard tri-fighter measured 5.4 [[meter]]s in length,<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> making it the smallest [[starship]] in both the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Separatist]] and [[Republic Navy|Republic navies]].<ref name="AbEverything" /> A space-faring relative of the [[Trade Federation]]'s dreaded [[droideka]], the tri-fighter<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> was designed with a sole purpose in mind: total destruction of their adversary.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> Developed for intense dogfighting and close-range space battles, the tri-fighter, unlike [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class starfighters]], featured more advanced brains than standard [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[droid]] [[Starfighter|fighters]],<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> which made it harder for an enemy to predict the fighter's movements and tactics.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> These brains were engineered with basic [[heuristic processor]]s.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> A unit was able to analyze, anticipate, and mimic an opponent's attack. The tri-fighter's predatory programming came from the [[Colicoid]]s.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> Alternatively, a tri-fighter could further be modified and made into a vehicle for a [[pilot]].<ref name="Overview Separatists">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/hot-shots-aces-ii-separatist-overview|text=Hot Shots and Aces II Separatist Overview}}</ref> Tri-fighters were deployed in swarms, where they would usually stick in groups of three, and used to achieve space superiority.<ref name="OSWM 121">{{GermanFanCite|121|Database}}</ref>

[[File:DIS-T81.png|thumb|left|300px|A tri-fighter fires on an Eta-2 Jedi starfighter in a debris field.]]
Compact and heavily armed,<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> the tri-fighter possessed four laser cannons and [[discord missile]]s, which were located on its bottom. Support clamps were connected to its wing cannons, and one of the tri-fighter's weapons, the nose-tip [[laser cannon]], was at the front of the fighter near its [[photoreceptor]]s and port sensors.<ref name="AbEverything" /> The [[droid starfighter]] also had a payload of [[proton torpedo]]s, [[pistoeka sabotage droid]]s, or [[concussion missile]]s,<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> which were used in bombardment.<ref name="OSWM 121" /> A radiation vent and cooling radiator were on its [[repulsorlift]] wings, and these wings were for flying in a [[planet]]'s [[atmosphere]]. The droid also had three drive thrusters and a gyroscopic [[reactor]] core. To intimidate organic enemies,<ref name="CVD" /> the fighter's three-armed design mimicked the skull of a ferocious<ref name="AbEverything" /> and terrifying prehistoric predator native to [[Colla IV]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" />

==History==
===Clone Wars===
====Early deployment====
{{Quote|Trident group one, attack the starboard engines. Trident group two, knock out their port shield generators. I'll go directly for their bridge.|Asajj Ventress orders the Trident groups against the ''Resolute''|<ref name="Nightsisters" />}}
[[File:Phlac-ArphoccPrototype-SWZ.png|thumb|right|270px|A droid tri-fighter prototype being assembled at Phlac-Arphocc Automata Industries.]]
Manufactured by [[Phlac-Arphocc Automata Industries]],<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> tri-fighters served as part of the Separatist navy during the Clone Wars,<ref name="Nightsisters" /> a three [[Standard year|year]] long conflict fought between the Separatists and [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Clone Wars fun facts">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures Fun Facts|text=The Clone Wars|video=jJWkGarrAHA}}</ref> The tri-fighter was deployed by the [[Confederacy military]] during the later period of the Clone Wars,<ref name="Collapse" /> first introduced in [[20 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> At one point during the war, the tri-fighter designated [[DIS-347]] critically damaged a Republic [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]].<ref name="DIS-347 X-wing">{{FFGXW2|set=Droid Tri-Fighter Expansion Pack|cardname=DIS-347}}</ref>

In 20 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Battle of Sullust date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[Battle of Sullust]] to [[20 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Battle of Sullust]], droid tri-fighters of [[Trident group one]] and [[Trident group two|group two]] served under [[Dark Acolyte]] [[Asajj Ventress]] in [[Asajj Ventress's fleet|her fleet]]. During the fighting, Ventress, flying in her [[Ginivex-class fanblade starfighter|''Ginivex''-class fanblade starfighter]], ordered the first team of tri-fighters to attack the starboard [[engine]]s of the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Resolute]]'' and had the second team to attack the port [[deflector shield generator]]s. Ventress herself made a run for the ''Resolute''<nowiki></nowiki>'s [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]]. After a [[clone navigation officer]] aboard the ''Resolute'' reported the loss of the port deflectors, Ventress fired on the [[flagship]], and this assault on the ''Resolute'' was able to cripple the vessel. Afterwards, Ventress, who was followed by several tri-fighters, moved to attack [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="Nightsisters" />

[[File:Tri-fighters_Sullust.png|thumb|left|250px|A trio of tri-fighters during the Battle of [[Sullust]]]]
After Ventress hit [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Kenobi's Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]], the two [[Jedi]] split up, resulting in Ventress following Kenobi's starfighter, while the tri-fighters pursed [[Anakin Skywalker's Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Skywalker's fighter]], which the Jedi [[General]] joked about. As three of the droid starfighters chased him alongside a [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class Commerce Guild destroyer]], Skywalker spotted three other tri-fighters at the other side of the [[warship]]. Skywalker used this to trick the three fighters behind him to crash into the other three. Skywalker then moved to help Kenobi, allowing Skywalker to blast Ventress's starfighter. Ventress and Kenobi then both had a crash landing in the [[Unidentified Providence-class carrier/destroyer (Battle of Sullust)|Separatist command ship's]] [[hangar]]. Unfortunately for those on-board, [[Count]] [[Dooku]], the Separatist [[Head of State]], had been ordered by his [[Sith Master]], [[Darth Sidious]], to [[Death|kill]] Ventress, so he ordered that [[TJ-912]] fire on the command ship. After it was destroyed, Dooku ordered the Separatist [[fleet]] to withdraw. However, Ventress survived the attempt on her life and went to the planet of [[Dathomir]], where she planned her revenge with the [[Nightsisters]].<ref name="Nightsisters" />

====Further action====
{{Quote|Woah! What the&hellip;|Fives upon seeing starships fight at Umbara|<ref name="Plan">{{TCW|Plan of Dissent}}</ref>}}
Tri-fighters later took part in the [[Battle of Lola Sayu]] as part of the [[Lola Sayu defense fleet]]. However, the Republic forces were able to escape the planet.<ref name="Citadel Rescue">{{TCW|Citadel Rescue}}</ref> They were also involved in the [[Battle of Umbara]] and fought alongside the [[Umbaran Militia]].<ref name="Plan" /> Despite the betrayal of Jedi General [[Pong Krell]], the Republic managed to win the battle.<ref name="Carnage of Krell">{{TCW|Carnage of Krell}}</ref>

[[File:Tri-fighters_attack_Dathomir.png|thumb|right|250px|Tri-fighters and ''Hyena''-class bombers during the Battle of Dathomir]]
Sometime after her plot to use [[Savage Opress]] against her former master [[Showdown at Toydaria|failed]], Ventress returned to Dathomir once again. Unbeknownst to her, Dooku learned this and ordered General [[Grievous]],<ref name="Massacre" /> the ruthless<ref name="CIS history gallery 9">{{Databank|url=confederacy-of-independent-systems-history-gallery|title=Confederacy of Independent Systems History Gallery}} {{C|Image 9 caption}}</ref> [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander of the Droid Army]],<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> to annihilate the all of the Nightsisters. After the [[battle droid]]s under Grievous's command had landed on planet, two tri-fighters and two [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Bombers]] approached the [[Nightsister lair]] and fired. Eventually, the Separatists prevailed<ref name="Massacre" /> and wiped out [[Mother Talzin's clan]], although Ventress, [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[Merrin]],<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> [[Morgan Elsbeth]],<ref name="Path of Fear">{{TOTE|The Path of Fear}}</ref> and [[Shelish]] survived.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>

Before General Kenobi could deploy [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] to pick up a group of [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] on the ''[[Crucible]]'', a Separatist fleet under the command of General Grievous came out of [[lightspeed]] and [[Attack on Obi-Wan Kenobi's fleet|attacked]] the [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's taskforce|Jedi's fleet]]. Republic forces began to be overrun, and tri-fighters flew alongside Grievous's [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|''Sheathipede''-class transport shuttle]] during the battle. Grievous and a force of battle droids soon boarded Kenobi's flagship, the ''[[Negotiator]]'', which ultimately made the Jedi General activate the ''Venator''-class [[Star Destroyer]]'s self-destruct.<ref name="Bound for Rescue">{{TCW|Bound for Rescue}}</ref>

[[File:TriFightersOfCatoNeimoidia-TCWS05E17.png|thumb|left|250px|Tri-fighters during the defense of Cato Neimoidia]]
Later, the Separatists [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|invaded]] the planet of [[Cato Neimoidia]], prompting the Republic to launch a defense, with General Skywalker and his [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], leading a starfighter [[squadron]] and bringing a group of [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic gunships]]. However, once they were into the planet's atmosphere, several tri-fighters were launched from a [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]], and three of the droids moved to attack the Republic starfighters. After taking out at least two [[Z-95 Headhunter]]s, one of the tri-fighters launched buzz droid missiles, resulting in a number of the sabotage droids to land on [[Anakin Skywalker's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|Skywalker's Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]]. The droids forced his [[astromech droid]], [[R2-D2]], to crash land the ship on a cliff.<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref>

====Ringo Vinda and Utapau====
{{Quote|I'm picking up fighters on our tail.|Jedi General Obi-Wan Kenobi, referring to Grievous and the tri-fighters|<ref name="The Big Bang">{{TCW|The Big Bang}}</ref>}}
During the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]], droid tri-fighters were present in the Confederate held part of the [[Ringo Vinda space station]], along with vulture droids and other combat droids, just as General Skywalker, [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], and [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]] launched a rescue mission to free [[clone trooper]] [[Tup]], who was being transported on a [[Maxillipede shuttle]] to be taken for examination by the [[Sith]]. The three were able to successfully rescue the captured [[trooper]].<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref>

[[File:Grievous-and-droid-starfighters.png|thumb|right|250px|Tri-fighters and other Separatist fighters over Utapau]]
Later in the war, General Grievous and Count Dooku attempted an arms deal for a massive [[kyber crystal]] with [[Sugi]] [[arms dealer]]s on [[Utapau]]. Eventually, Jedi Generals Kenobi and Skywalker [[Mission to Utapau|arrived on Utapau]] and tried to stop Grievous, although the Separatists eventually took the [[crystal]] aboard a [[Maxillipede shuttle]]<ref name="Crystal Crisis">{{TCW|Crystal Crisis}}</ref> to bring it to a [[Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel|supply ship]]. The Jedi pursued the crystal using a [[freighter]], but Grievous, using his personal starfighter ''[[Soulless One]]'', and three tri-fighters chased after their ship. As they flew through a Separatist fleet, three vulture droids also began to pursue the Jedi. Kenobi took control of the weapons systems, however, and he managed to take out all three of the tri-fighters and one of the vultures.<ref name="The Big Bang" />

After the Maxillipede boarded the supply ship, Kenobi and Skywalker entered the ship using [[escape pod]]s, and Skywalker had their freighter crash into the supply craft's [[hyperdrive]] to prevent a Separatist escape. Eventually, the two learned the crystal could refract and intensify energy sent into. Knowing this, when they brought the crystal to the ship's hangar, Kenobi made several [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tanks]] fire at it. While they and Grievous escaped the doomed ship, the crystal overheated, ultimately sending out an energy wave that began to destroy other Separatist ships. After the wave started, three tri-fighters were flying near a ''Recusant''-class [[destroyer]], and at least two of the fighters were destroyed.<ref name="The Big Bang" />

====The final days====
{{Quote|The Seps were on us as soon as shook the dust from our wings. The V-19s punched a hole through the droid tri-fighters that tried to block our advance. Lost two pilots.|Clone pilot Oddball|<ref name="On the Front Lines" />}}
[[File:TrifighterTrioEta2-SWCVNE.png|thumb|left|200px|A trio of droid-tri-fighters pursue an ''Actis''-class Jedi intereptor]]
Tri-fighters also participated in battles of the [[Outer Rim Sieges]] campaign, including a [[Battle between a Republic and Separatist fleet|skirmish between a Republic and Separatist fleet]] that was led by General Grievous, where tri-fighters were engaged by [[Clone Z-95 starfighter|Z-95 Headhunters]] in dogfights over the ''Providence''-class Dreadnoughts,<ref name="Old Friends">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> and the [[battle on Lokori]].<ref name="Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> By this time, Republic forces were managing relatively well against ''Hyena'' bombers and vulture droids, however tri-fighters were still a considerable threat.<ref name="OSWM 121" /> Tri-fighters also participated during the [[Battle of Cato Neimoidia]], during which they took on Jedi General [[Plo Koon]] and his wingmen. Tri-fighters were also present during the [[Battle of Yerbana]], where they engaged Z-95 Headhunters and [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic gunships]] trying to provide support to Commander Cody and the [[212th Attack Battalion]], who were under fire by [[Separatist Droid Army|Separatist ground forces]]. In the air, the tri-fighters launched surprise attacks over the Headhunters and gunships, weakening them. Nevertheless, the battle on [[Yerbana]] was still won by the Republic.<ref name="Old Friends" />

Tri-fighters were also deployed as part of<ref name="Episode III" /> an [[Coruscant attack fleet|attack fleet]]<ref name="Battles that Changed">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> during the [[Battle of Coruscant]], a major Separatist strike against the Republic [[capital]] world under the command of Grievous.<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> One tri-fighter involved in the battle had actually been modified by the pilot [[Volan Das]], who used the tri-fighter as his personal starfighter.<ref name="Overview Separatists" /> During the surprise attack, Grievous was able to land in the [[Federal District|Executive District]], capture the Republic's [[Supreme Chancellor]], [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], and take the [[chancellor]] to his flagship, the ''[[Invisible Hand]]''. To rescue Palpatine, Jedi Generals Skywalker and Kenobi went to infiltrate the ''Invisible Hand''. The elite [[Clone Flight Squad Seven]] was to escort them, and their [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s were able to make a hole through the tri-fighters that attempted to stop their advance, although the [[squad]] lost two [[pilot]]s.<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> As the Jedi and [[clone trooper pilot]]s were making their way to Grievous's warship, a tri-fighter took out a [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter]]. Several tri-fighters also chased after another ARC-170, making the [[Cloning|clone]] pilot ask for help. While Skywalker wished to go back to help the pilot, Kenobi did not let him and insisted that they had a mission to do.<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:DroidTriFighterSquadron-SWM.png|thumb|right|250px|A squadron of tri-fighters over Coruscant]]
After a tri-fighter managed to blast an ARC-170, one tri-fighter flew near the vulture droid that fired buzz droid missiles at the Jedi. Skywalker and Kenobi ultimately defeated the buzz droids and boarded the ''Invisible Hand''. During their [[time]] aboard, Skywalker killed Count Dooku, Palpatine was rescued, Grievous abandoned the craft, and the starship crashed to [[Coruscant]]'s surface.<ref name="Episode III" /> Grievous ordered all Separatist ships to retreat, but some vessels, including some droid starfighters, remained. Clone trooper pilots spent hours taking out leftover tri-fighters, vulture droids, and Hyena [[bomber]]s. The battle also proved to be the Separatist's last grasp, as their fleet had been annihilated and Dooku was dead. Clone pilot [[Davijaan]] "Odd Ball" later have a postmission summary, and he mentioned that Squad Seven's V-19s punched through the tri-fighters.<ref name="On the Front Lines" />

===Age of the Empire===
A short time after the Battle of Coruscant, Chancellor Palpatine, secretly the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious, [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire|First Galactic Empire]]. Anakin Skywalker, now newly christened to be the [[Sith]] Darth Vader, [[Mission to Mustafar|had]] all Separatist droid units, and therefore their droid tri-fighters, shut down.<ref name="Episode III" />

During the ensuing [[Imperial Era]], some tri-fighters continued to see use as part of the [[Free Ryloth Movement]]<ref name="Lords of the Sith" /> and [[Berch Teller's rebel cell]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

===Age of the New Republic===
By the time of the [[Skirmish aboard a Moraysian cruiser|skirmish]] aboard a [[Unidentified Moraysian cruiser|cruiser]] stolen from the [[Pyke Syndicate]] by the [[mercenary]] [[Ledesmar]], there was a collection of droid tri-fighters onboard the ship.<ref name="Free Fall">''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'', the droid tri-fighter is in the "Interceptor" class for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] faction.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>
