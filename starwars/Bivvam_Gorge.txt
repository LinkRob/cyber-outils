'''Bivvam Gorge''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who worked for the [[Red Key Raiders]], a [[Tatooine]]-based criminal syndicate led by [[Lorgan Movellan]] which rose to power following the [[death]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]].<ref name="LD" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt" /> Gorge and a [[Unidentified Red Key Raider|fellow thug]] found the human [[Malakili]] wandering into Red Key territory, so they chased the man down. However, Gorge and his comrade were killed by [[Cobb Vanth]] and [[Issa-Or]], self-proclaimed [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcers]].<ref name="LD" />
