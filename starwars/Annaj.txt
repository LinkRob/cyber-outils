'''Annaj''' was an astronomical object in the [[Annaj system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Moddell sector]]. It was at the end of the [[Spar Trade Route]], the easiest way to get into the [[sector]], and was patrolled by the [[Galactic Empire]]. When the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] began preparing to attack the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] at the Moddell sector [[moon]] [[Endor]], rebel [[agent]]s were posted on Annaj and one scout, [[Corporal]] [[Ractivelle]] analysed the sector's geography, noting that the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance fleet]] would almost definitely find Imperials if they tried to enter the Moddell sector via Annaj, concluding that the [[fleet]] could not have an element of surprise.

==Description==
Annaj was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in the [[Annaj system]]<ref name="TLoP" /> within the [[Moddell sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Situated at the coordinates H-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Coords" /> it was at the end point of the [[Spar Trade Route]] and was connected by the [[Houche Run]] [[hyperspace route]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> to the celestial body [[Ovise system|Ovise]], which was an end point of the [[Ovise Reach]] hyperroute.<ref name="BMF 18">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> Annaj was additionally connected by hyperspace route to the astronomical body [[Kuna's Tooth]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==History==
{{Quote|Look at the line on this map. Fast, clear, and straight. This is what the Empire is calling the Sanctuary Pipeline. It runs directly between Sullust on the Rimma and Endor in Moddell. That's how the Empire is doing it. That's how they've shunted thousands of cargo barges to a construction site without our agents on Annaj seeing a thing.|Capin Harinar, in a classified document to Crix Madine|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Annaj was under Imperial patrol.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Leading up to [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Empire constructed the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] [[superweapon]] at the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]] in the Moddell sector, using a secret artificial hyperspace route known as the [[Sanctuary Pipeline]] to secret materials into the sector from the planet [[Sullust]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] then made a plan to lead the majority of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Alliance Military|forces]] into a trap using the [[Death Star]] as bait. He allowed information about the superweapon and the Sanctuary Pipeline to fall into rebel hands.<ref name="HC 14">{{HelmetCollectionCite|14|Highlights of the Saga}}</ref>

As the Alliance began preparing to destroy the second Death Star. While rebel [[agent]]s were placed on Annaj, [[Corporal]] [[Ractivelle]], a rebel scout part of [[Stardust unit]], analysed the Moddell sector's geography, producing a [[Star Map|map]] of the [[sector]] that marked Annaj and other locations there. The [[map]] was included in a special report by the scout where they discussed how the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance fleet]] could reach Endor. Ractivelle noted that the Spare Trade Route was the easiest way into the Moddell sector, followed by a traverse along the Houche Run from Annaj and then a hopscotch approach the rest of the way to Endor. The corporal predicted that an Imperial picket would almost certainly be at Annaj and ruin the [[fleet]]'s element of surprise.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

While Ractivelle concluded that a surprise attack was impossible with their findings, [[Major]] [[Capin Harinar]] chimed in with a classified document addressed to Alliance [[General]] [[Crix Madine]], in which he addressed the Empire's Sanctuary Pipeline route, how it had evaded the notice of the Annaj agents, and how it could be used by the Alliance for a surprise attack. As a result, the Alliance [[Battle of Endor|attacked]] the second Death Star using the Sanctuary Pipeline,<ref name="Rebel Files" /> destroying it in a decisive victory that saw the death of the Emperor himself&mdash;all despite the trap that had initially been laid.<ref name="Timelines" /> The documents from Ractivelle and Harinar were both collected by [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]], who compiled them in the archive of non-electronic documents known as ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' by the time of their death on the same [[Standard day|day]] as the Endor battle.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
Annaj was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', a [[canon]] boxed set and reference book first released in [[2017]] and authored by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
