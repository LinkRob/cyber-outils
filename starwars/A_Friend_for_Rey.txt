"'''A Friend for Rey'''" is a short story initially published in ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]''. It was written by [[Elizabeth Schaefer]].<ref name="5-Minute Star Wars Stories" />

The text and illustrations were slightly changed when it was republished in ''[[Star Wars Galactic Stories: 7 Stories from a Galaxy Far, Far Away....]]''<ref name="Galactic Stories" />
