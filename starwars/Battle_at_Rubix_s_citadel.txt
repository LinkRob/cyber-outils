In the final '''battle''' of the [[Shu-Torun War|War on Shu-Torun]], [[Queen]] [[Trios]] led her loyalist forces in an attack on the [[Rubix's delving citadel|delving citadel]] of [[Duke]] [[Rubix]], leader of the [[ore-duke]]s rebelling against her. Part way through the battle, the forces of the [[Galactic Empire]], led by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] joined the fray on the side of Trios and quickly claimed victory. After Rubix surrendered, Trios [[Death|executed]] him and named his daughter [[Bixene]] as successor to his position.<ref name="DV19" />
