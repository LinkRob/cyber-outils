The '''Artiod system''' was a [[star system]] located in the distant [[Morellian Commonwealth]]<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> of [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Timelines" /> that contained the [[planet]] [[Artiod Minor]], the [[homeworld]] of the [[Artiodac]] [[species]]. It was discovered by [[Zygerrian]] [[enslaver]]s while trying to escape [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] justice, who returned there over the [[Standard century|centuries]] to fill their [[Slavery|slave]] pens with Artiodacs and train them for sale.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />
