'''Epsilon 5''' was a [[Callsign/Legends|call sign]] used by the [[First Order]]. During the [[Ottegan Assault|attack]] on the [[Ottegan]]-populated [[Planet|world]] of [[Arthon]], a [[Gender|male]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] of the [[First Order]] [[pilot]]ed a [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]] under the callsign [[Epsilon 5 (First Order)|Epsilon 5]]. During the battle, a [[missile]] from an Ottegan [[siege engine]] struck Epsilon 5, making it the eighth recorded First Order casualty of the battle.
