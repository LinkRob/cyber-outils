The '''Frontier Slugthrower''', also known as a '''Diago's slugthrower''', was a [[slugthrower rifle]] utilized by the [[Miraluka]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Diago Velaar]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Frontier Slugthrower was a "Base" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Diago's slugthrower, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.17.0 update on [[July 18]], [[2023]], during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Frontier Sharpshooter]]. This wrap was unlocked by default when Diago became playable.<ref name="Hunters" />
