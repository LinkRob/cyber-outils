'''Fort Sunfire'''<ref name="Commander" />, or '''Bestine Compound'''<ref name="Outlaws" /> was one of two major [[Galactic Empire]] [[Imperial Army|Imperial military]] installations on [[Tatooine]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]&mdash;the other being [[Fort Ironhand]], near [[Anchorhead]].<ref name="Commander" /> The fort was located south of [[Bestine (city)|Bestine]] in the [[Desert Valley]].

Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the base was [[Attack on Fort Sunfire|destroyed]] by a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Saponza's partner|agent]] as part of [[operations on Tatooine]]. The destruction of Fort Sunfire started a contained [[Battle for Tatooine|conflict]] between [[Captain]] [[Kosh]] and the local Rebels.<ref name="Starwars.com">In [[Kekit]]'s Databank entry, it is stated that Saponza and his partner played an integral part in the campaign that liberated a Jawa clan from the Tusken Raiders. It also states that the plan was orchestrated by Jennica Pierce of the Rebel Alliance. This scenario does not occur if the player aligns with the Empire, so it can be deduced that Saponza's gang joined the Rebellion.</ref> 

The fort was rebuilt sometime before [[3 ABY]]. In that year, [[Kay Vess]] and [[bounty hunter]] [[Vail Tormin]] infiltrated Fort Sunfire to find an Imperial file identifying a [[Zerek Besh]] spy in the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace of]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
