'''Emperor''' was the title of a leader who wielded [[Dictatorship|autocratic power]] over an [[Politics|empire]]. Its [[Gender|feminine]] counterpart was [[Empress]].<ref name="TSWB" /> The [[Atrisian Emperor]]s, including the [[14th Atrisian Emperor]], ruled [[Atrisia|Kitel Phard]].<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The [[Sith Empire]] was ruled over by an [[Unidentified Sith Emperor|individual]] who was identified as the [[Sith Emperor]].<ref name="Coruscant" /> [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], secretly the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious, masterminded the [[Clone Wars]] and completed the [[Grand Plan]] of the [[Sith]]. He adopted the title of [[Galactic Emperor]] at the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the war, [[Proclamation of the New Order|transforming]] the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Darth Sidious DB" />

Following Sidious' [[death]], the title of Galactic Emperor fell into disuse; not even the [[Strand-Cast|strandcast]] [[Snoke]] made an effort to claim it despite his role as [[Supreme Leader]] of the [[First Order]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], [[Skywalker family|grandson]] of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], set out to [[First Order-Resistance War|conquer]] [[the galaxy]] as the First Order's new Supreme Leader.<ref name="TSWB" /> Sidious, having resurrected himself through [[cloning]], offered to help Ren become the next Emperor.<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> After Ren returned to the [[Light side of the Force|light side]] of [[the Force]] as Ben Solo, Sidious urged his [[Palpatine family|granddaughter]], the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], to give into the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] and claim the [[Throne of the Sith]] as Empress. Ultimately, Sidious discarded the idea<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> of possessing Rey's body<ref name="Inside Intel">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-inside-intel-palpatines-contingency-plan|text=''Star Wars'' Inside Intel: Palpatine's Contingency Plan|int=Star Wars Inside Intel|date=2021-08-03}}</ref> upon discovering the true nature of the [[Force-bond]] between Solo and Rey; they were a [[Force dyad|dyad in the Force]]. Although he succeeded in healing his cloned body with the dyad's power, Sidious' [[Battle of Exegol|second destruction]] ended his dream of ruling a [[Sith Empire]] as an [[Immortality|immortal]] Emperor.<ref name="TSWB" />
