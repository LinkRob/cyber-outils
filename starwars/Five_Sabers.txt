The '''Five Sabers''' was a prestigious [[pilot]]ing championship held on [[Theron]]. As its name suggested, the contest was comprised of five stages: the [[Atmosphere|atmospheric]] dash, the [[orbit]]al sprints, the [[Moon|lunar]] relays, the [[Realspace|sublight]] relays, and [[hyperspace]] orienteering. While the first two stages were mainly watched live or in full, the latter three were best watched in post-race edited footage, due to them lasting [[Standard hour|hours]], [[Standard day|days]], and [[Standard week|weeks]] respectively. A version of the Five Sabers, called the [[Junior Sabers]], was held for younger pilots.<ref name="Bloodline" />

[[Han Solo]] helped oversee the Five Sabers held in approximately [[28 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> By [[34 ABY]], [[Marcus Speedstar]] was a three-time winner of the Five Sabers.<ref name="The Platform Classic" />
