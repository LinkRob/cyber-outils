'''CC-4M''' was a [[Color|white]] [[Loadlifter droid (model)|loadlifter droid]] with fabric dust shroud on its left shoulder<ref name="SoloOG" /> that was forced to work in the criminal [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s [[spice]] [[Spice mines of Kessel|mines]] on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Before coming to Kessel, he was the leader of a team of [[construction droid]]s on an [[Water|ice]] [[moon]]. He missed his friends from his old team while he was forced to work in the mines on Kessel.<ref name="Extraordinary Droids" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on [[Kessel]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to nine years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]], therefore the event on Kessel in ''Solo'' must take place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> CC-4M was freed from [[slavery]] when its [[restraining bolt]] was removed during a [[Raid on Kessel|slave revolt]] in the [[Mining|mines]] started by the [[droid]] [[L3-37]].<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
CC-4M first appeared in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Solo" /> and was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" />
