"'''Becoming Hunted'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] published in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 10|tenth]] issue of the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]] series by [[Egmont]] UK Ltd. It was written by [[Martin Fisher]], illustrated by [[Bob Molesworth]], and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. The comic was published on [[October 7]], [[2015]].<ref name="BH">{{RebelsMagCite|10|Becoming Hunted}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
[[Greve (hunter)|Greve]] and [[Alyson]] are trying to pry open a door but are failing. They turn around to two other people awaiting the door opening, [[Lexim]] and [[Wad]]. The four of them argue over whose fault their predicament is while banging is heard on a second door in the room. Fearing the creature that might be banging on the door, they are surprised when [[Garazeb Orrelios]] busts down the door. Orrelios explains that he was responding to a distress call. Alyson and Greve explain to Orrelios that they were expecting creatures which had escaped from captivity, but Orrelios remarks that he had not seen any on his entry.

As he finishes saying that, one of the creatures tackles him from through the open door. The creature is revealed to be a [[gundark]], which then leaps at Lexim. Before the gundark can land, Orrelios shoots the creature. Orrelios then orders the four to move into the adjoining room, fearing that a horde of gundark was about to descend on them, as three more enter the facility. In the new room, Lexim explains how the gundarks came to be on the planet, having opened a [[Lexim's hunting ground|hunting facility]] specializing in exotic species for those that can pay. However, Lexim had not got the facility fully prepared before importing the gundarks from offworld. Seeking more support, Orrelios orders a search for other weapons that they could use against the gundarks, but only a broken [[rifle]] and some spoons are found.

Orrelios offers the four an escape via his [[Shuttle|shuttle,]] the ''[[Phantom]]'', which is located on the landing pad, which causes an argument amongst the four survivors. While they are arguing, the gundarks break in though the floor. The five are forced to defend themselves with Alyson using the broken rifle, Greve the spoons. Lexim and Wad try to evade the gundarks, with Orellios' timely intervention preventing their fall. However, Orrelios is soon overpowered, as are Alyson, Greve, and Wad. Lexim takes Orellios' [[bo-rifle]], which had fallen from his grasp and attacks the gundarks, eliminating the threat. Handing back the bo-rifle to Orrelios, the five flee the room and make for the landing pad via a tunnel, with Orrelios holding back the gundarks with his bo-rifle. One of the gundarks breaks into the tunnel through the roof and latches onto Lexim at the tunnel's exit. Orrelios blasts the gundark, but not before Lexim is hurt. Orrelios urges Lexim to get going, but Lexim refuses, instead shutting the exit's door and halting the gundarks long enough for the others to escape.

Orrelios, Alyson, Greve, and Wad safely board the ''Phantom'' and rendezvous with the ''[[Ghost]]''. Along with the crew of the ''Ghost'', the four ruminate over Lexim's sacrifice, with Orrelios sharing a similar tale from his youth where his [[Garazeb Orrelios' friend|friend]]'s [[Garazeb Orrelios' friend's brother|brother,]] while facing a creature, sacrificed himself so his friend could escape. Orrelios hoped that someday, he could repay Lexim's sacrifice.
