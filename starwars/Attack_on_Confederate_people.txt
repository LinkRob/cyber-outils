{{Quote|Your Republic forces have carried out a ''barbaric attack'' on our people! And among the deaths was the very ''sponsor'' of the peace accord, Senator Mina Bonteri. With her needless death, I must formally withdraw the proposal for peace offered by our Senate.|Count Dooku|<ref name="PoP" />}}
After the [[bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid]] and breakdown of peace talks, [[Count]] [[Dooku]] alleged the [[Galactic Republic]] launched an '''attack''' on the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]], [[Death|killing]] many&mdash;including prominent Separatist [[Senator]] [[Mina Bonteri]]&mdash;in the process. While Republic [[Spy|spies]] instead attributed Bonteri's death to Separatist agents, Dooku withdrew Bonteri's [[Confederate–Republic peace initiative|peace proposal]] offered by the [[Separatist Senate]].<ref name="PoP" />
