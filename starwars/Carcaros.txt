'''Carcaros''' were aggressive [[fish]] that lived in the [[Ocean|oceans]] of [[Tordus]]. A group of them entered the [[Axil Research Station]] when it was sabotaged and attacked [[Kit Fisto]] and [[Nahdar Vebb]].<ref name="Tot2" /> They had a [[mouth]] filled with sharp [[Tooth|teeth]] and several dorsal fins.<ref name="Tot3">[[Hyperspace Stories—Tides of Terror 3|''Hyperspace Stories—Tides of Terror'' 3]]</ref>
