'''''Casus Belli'' 109''' was a French roleplaying magazine from October of [[1997]]. It contained many roleplaying articles, including one adventures for the [[West End Games]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|D6]] system. The article was called "[[« Elle »]]" (in English "She") written by [[Philippe Rat]].
