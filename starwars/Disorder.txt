'''Disorder''' is the sixth cinematic trailer released by [[BioWare]] for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', and serves as a trailer for the ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Legacy of the Sith|Legacy of the Sith]]'' expansion. It was released on [[February 15]], [[2022]], alongside the expansion.<ref name="LotS" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Jedi Master Denolm Orr and his Padawan, Sa'har Kateen, travel to the Temple of Nul on Elom to retrieve a special holocron before the Sith Empire can. They seem to have the upperhand, until an unexpected enemy appears&hellip;''<ref name="YouTube" />

==Development==
{{Quote|We wanted to not only tell some of the backstory of the game, but also show what kind of game it is. It's a game about characters. It's a game about making moral decisions and facing difficult odds.|Charles Boyd|<ref name="SW.com Interview">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-old-republic-legacy-of-the-sith-trailer|text=''Star Wars: The Old Republic'' Celebrates 10 Years with ''Legacy of Sith'' Expansion and Stunning New Trailer}}</ref>}}
Unlike previous ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' cinematic trailers, which were animated by [[Blur Studio]], ''Disorder'' was created by [[Industrial Light & Magic]]. The short was directed by [[Neel Upadhye]],<ref name="Alex Backes website" /> who had previously worked with the computer animation studio on ''[[Hunted]]'', a cinematic trailer for the [[2020]] game ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''.<ref name="Squadrons">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-squadrons-hunted|text=With the ''Star Wars: Squadrons'' CG Short "Hunted," EA, Motive Studios, and ILM Tell a New Kind of ''Star Wars'' Story}}</ref> Upadhye wrote the short alongside a team of writers, including [[Alex Backes]], [[Josh Callahan]], [[Tanner Boyajian]], and [[Sam Schectman]].<ref name="Alex Backes website" />

[[Charles Boyd]], creative director of ''The Old Republic'', stated that the goal of ''Disorder'' was twofold: the short serves to give backstory for the [[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Legacy of the Sith|''Legacy of the Sith'']] expansion, but also to demonstrate the difficult moral decisions that players of the MMORPG had experienced over the previous ten years of the game's lifespan.<ref name="SW.com Interview" /> According to Callahan, ''Disorder''{{'s}} writers aimed to tell a story that did not paint the Jedi Order as perfect heroes, and instead chose to illustrate them as flawed individuals.<ref name="Gizmodo Jedi Order Sucks">{{WebCite|url=https://gizmodo.com/star-wars-the-old-republic-disorder-cg-trailer-1848542972|text=''Star Wars: The Old Republic''{{'s}} Latest Trailer Is a Gorgeous Reminder That the Jedi Order Sucks|author=Whitbrook, James|date=2022-02-15|archivedate=20220217075140}}</ref><ref name="Callahan Response">{{Twitter|Jschmuck2|status/1493699533871542274|[[James Callahan]]|quote=Hey this was a great writeup! That's all the stuff we meant to get across!|archiveurl=https://archive.is/NzrnN}}</ref>

''Disorder'' premiered on [[February 15]], [[2022]], the same day that the ''Legacy of the Sith'' expansion was released.<ref name="SW.com Interview" />

==Continuity==
{{Quote|I've always said, to prepare for the future, we must look to the past.|Denolm Orr, prior to his travel to Elom|<ref name="Mission Abstract">{{TORweb|url=info/news/article/20211001-0|text=Mission Abstract: Investigate link between Darth Malgus and the planet Elom|int=Mission Abstract: Investigate link between Darth Malgus and the planet Elom}}</ref>}}
The events of ''Disorder''{{'s}} story were teased in ''[[Mission Abstract: Investigate link between Darth Malgus and the planet Elom]]'', a short story written as an [[in-universe]] report by Jedi Master Denolm Orr. While investigating clues, Orr discovers that Malgus' plans likely point to the planet Elom, and so he plans to seek out the truth with Kateen and a small team.<ref name="Mission Abstract" /> As a trailer for ''Legacy of the Sith'', ''Disorder'' serves to set up the journeys of the expansion's major characters, namely ''Old Republic'' mainstay Malgus as well as the new character Sa'har.<ref name="SW.com Interview" />

As previous ''Old Republic'' cinematics had done, ''Disorder'' draws connections to the ''[[Star Wars]]'' Skywalker Saga films. A [[Chroon-Tan B-Machine/Legends|Chroon-Tan B-Machine]] [[Midwife droid/Legends|midwife droid]] appears in the short, having first been seen in {{Film|III}}. Another reference to Episode III appears in musical form, with the short's composer [[Blake Robinson]] reusing a portion of "[[Anakin's Dark Deeds]]" from [[John Williams]]' score for that film.<ref name="Revenge of the Sith">[[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith (soundtrack)|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'' soundtrack]]</ref> This particular piece had previously been used in the ''Old Republic'' cinematics ''[[Deceived]]'', [[Hope (cinematic trailer)|''Hope'']], and [[Return (cinematic trailer)|''Return'']].<ref name="Deceived">{{TORweb|url=info/media/trailers/deceived-cinematic-trailer|text=Deceived Cinematic Trailer|int=Deceived}}</ref><ref name="Hope">{{TORweb|url=info/media/trailers/hope-cinematic-trailer|text="Hope"|int=Hope (cinematic trailer)}}</ref><ref name="Return">{{TORweb|url=info/media/trailers/return|text="Return"|int=Return (cinematic trailer)}}</ref>
