"'''Ambush'''" is the first episode of [[The Clone Wars: Season One|Season One]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', and the first episode of the series overall. Before its television premiere, the episode was shown on the [[Wikipedia:Television Critics Association|Television Critics Association]] press tour on [[July 11]], [[2008]]<ref name="IGN">{{WebCite|url=http://tv.ign.com/articles/888/888374p1.html|text=IGN: ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' - Our First Look|archivedate=20080715050558}}</ref> and later at [[Wikipedia:Dragon Con|Dragon*Con]] in [[Wikipedia:Atlanta, Georgia|Atlanta, Georgia]] in September.<ref name="TFN">{{WebCite|url=http://www.theforce.net/latestnews/story/Star_Wars_News_From_DragonCon_117416.asp|text=TheForce.Net - ''Star Wars'' News From Dragon*Con|archivedate=20250613110216}}</ref> "Ambush" aired on [[Cartoon Network]] on [[October 3]], [[2008]]. Though it is the first episode of the series, "Ambush" is the fifth episode chronologically, being preceded by the later episode "[[Supply Lines]]" and succeeded by the next episode, "[[Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence'']]," first part of a three-part storyline.<ref name="CheeTweet">{{Twitter|HolocronKeeper|status/443106707472801793|[[Leland Chee]]|date=2014-03-10|quote=216 116 T 301 303 101-115 117-121 201-203 217-219 204-214 220-222 305-307 302 304 308 122 309-311 215 312-322 S4 502-513 501 514-520 601-613|archivedate=20190722081618|nolive=1}}</ref>

==Official description==
''Jedi Master Yoda is on a secret mission to forge a treaty with the King of the strategic system of Toydaria when his ship is ambushed by Count Dooku. Yoda and three Clone troopers must face off against Count Dooku's dreaded assassin Ventress and her massive droid army to prove the Jedi are strong enough to protect the king and his people from the forces of the war''.

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|Now is the time to face the enemy, Lieutenant. Ambush them, we will.|Yoda, to a clone trooper|<ref name="Ambush" />}}
[[File:Munificent-Ambush.png|thumb|right|250px|Yoda's ship being ambushed by the Separatists.]]
[[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] is dispatched to the dried coral moon of [[Rugosa]] to convince [[King]] [[Katuunko]] to allow the [[Galactic Republic]] to build a base in [[Toydarian]] territory. As Katuunko and his retinue await the [[Jedi]] envoy, [[Asajj Ventress]] arrives and presents Katuunko with a hologram of [[Count]] [[Dooku]], who argues the Jedi's inability to protect Toydaria in hopes of swaying the king to the Separatist's cause.

In orbit above the [[Rugosa]] moon, two [[Munificent-class star frigate|Banking clan frigates]] ambush Yoda's [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|ship]]. Yoda and three clones&mdash;[[Lieutenant]] [[Thire]], [[Jek]], and [[Rys]]&mdash;escape to the moon's surface in an [[escape pod]]. As the Republic frigate leaves the system, Asajj Ventress points to the fleeing ship as a sign of the Republic's weakness.

Yoda contacts Katuunko via hologram and informs the king that he has landed nearby. Ventress suggests a challenge: If Yoda can escape her best [[Separatist Droid Army|troops]], then [[Toydaria]] will be free to join the Republic. If he should fail and her droids prevail, however, Toydaria will join with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]]. Yoda agrees to the challenge, despite the king's uncertainty. 

[[File:TeamRepublic-Ambush.png|thumb|left|250px|Yoda leading Thire, Jek and Rys.]]
As Yoda and the clones make their way to the rendezvous point, a Separatist [[C-9979 landing craft|transport]] lands nearby and deploys a [[battalion]] of droids. The dense coral forest proves difficult to penetrate, however, and the droid commander, [[224]], orders his troops to abandon their tanks and advance on foot. As a result, Yoda and the clones are able to ambush the droids in smaller groups. Stronger forces eventually zero in on the clones' location, however, and Lieutenant Thire is wounded. The clones are forced to retreat with Master Yoda.

Yoda leads the clones to a cave to recover. The clones are demoralized over being outnumbered and low on ammo. To raise their spirits, Yoda shares his insight into their identities as [[Soul|individuals]] despite their origins as [[Cloning|clones]], and assures them they have all they need to prevail.

[[File:Yoda_droideka_faceoff.png|thumb|right|250px|Yoda faces approaching droidekas.]]
A column of droids and tanks approach, and Master Yoda decides to face them alone, telling his clone troopers to stay out of sight and that they will know when their help is needed. The enemy proves little challenge to the jedi master as he expertly cuts through them, steadily reducing their numbers one-by-one with his lightsaber. Furious, Ventress sends in ten [[droideka]]s to deal with the jedi. Suspecting Master Yoda is too distracted to notice the incoming droids, Lieutenant Thire fires his last rocket at an overhanging bluff, sending an avalanche of rocks crashing down, crushing the reinforcements and ending the combat.

Impressed by Yoda's and the clones' performance, Katuunko chooses to join with the Republic due to the fact that Ventress cheated. Count Dooku orders Ventress to kill the king. Ventress knocks the king's [[Toydarian Guard|guard]]s unconscious and moves to strike down the king, but Master Yoda arrives just in time and [[Telekinesis|seizes]] Ventress with the Force. Yoda demands Ventress' surrender, but Ventress remotely detonates pre-planted explosives and causes a landslide, allowing her to escape in her [[Punworcca 116-class interstellar sloop|solar sailer]]. King Katuunko presents his sword to Yoda, offering his services to the Republic, as [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic gunships]] arrive and retrieve both parties.

==Development==
{{Quote|So we do things far better in a late episode in Season One than we did in the movie, for the theatrical release of ''Clone Wars''. So I think there were some kind of juxtapositional episodes based on that. You know, we all liked the Yoda episode, 'Ambush in the Outer Rim', and I thought that we all felt that, you know, fans had never really seen something that was that Yoda-specific before, but um, you know, we all felt it would be a really strong way to start the series with a really fun episode about Yoda.|Director [[Dave Filoni]], during an interview|<ref name="Interview">{{WebCite|url=http://theforce.net/latestnews/story/ForceCast_March_27_2009_122037.asp|text=Force-Cast|work=[[TheForce.net]]|archivedate=20090401184043}}</ref>}}
The episode's original name was "'''Ambush in the Outer Rim'''."<ref name="Interview" /> Its title was first revealed by [[Henry Gilroy]] in an interview that featured in [[Star Wars Insider 103|''Star Wars Insider'' 103]].<ref name="Insider 103">[[Star Wars Insider 103|''Star Wars Insider'' 103]]</ref>

The original moral of this episode was supposed to be, "''Great leadership comes in all sizes.''"<ref name="Ambush Ep Guide" />

"Ambush" was originally not supposed to be aired as the first episode of [[The Clone Wars: Season One|Season One]], but the episodes' order was changed very close to the series' premiere.<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DHBoards|subdomain=www|url=Help/Boards/viewtopic.php?p=212333#212333|text=Tom Hodges on Dark Horse message boards|nobackup=1}}</ref><ref name="Rugosa in databank">{{DB|location|rugosa|Rugosa}}</ref>

Almost two years after the original airing of "Ambush," a prequel episode titled "[[Supply Lines]]" aired on [[September 24]], [[2010]] as part of [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]]. It revealed the Republic's original purpose to use [[Toydaria/Legends|Toydaria]] as a staging ground to help the Twi'leks and supply them with food and medical supplies, which intertwined with the [[Battle of Ryloth|Ryloth Trilogy]] of episodes which aired later on in Season One.

==Release and reception==
"Ambush" marked the series' debut on October 3, 2008. It brought in 3.96 million viewers,<ref name="Records">{{WebCite|url=https://variety.com/2008/tv/news/clone-wars-pulls-in-record-ratings-1117993515/|text='Clone Wars' pulls in record ratings|work=[[Wikipedia:Variety]]|archivedate=20260130222943}}</ref> and was Cartoon Network's most watched series premiere.<ref name="Force">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/clone-wars-a-force-cartoon-120493/|text=''Clone Wars'' a force for Cartoon Network|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter]]|archivedate=20260110102127}}</ref>
