'''Durasteel''' was a type of metal alloy used for a variety of purposes, ranging from combat [[armor]] to buildings. More resistant than standard [[steel]],<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> durasteel was able to protect against kinetic damage,<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> so it was often used for defensive plating on military [[starship]]s.<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> Durasteel was able to withstand blows from vibro-weapons, force pikes, and even able to briefly stand against lightsabers.

==Production and usage==
[[File:DarthVadersHelmet-SW100Objects.png|thumb|left|200px|Darth Vader's helmet was made of a durasteel and obsidian alloy.]]
[[Zersium]] was an [[ore]] critical to the making of durasteel, and was found in the bedrock of the [[planet]] [[Nag Ubdur]]. Because of this, the [[Galactic Empire]] strip-mined the planet to make use of its ore deposits.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

Durasteel could also be transformed into a pliant material. The [[Imperial weapons technician|weapons technicians]] of the Empire wore black durasteel [[glove]]s.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>

Part of the [[Calcoraan Depot]] was made of durasteel, and [[Denetrius Vidian]] was placed in durasteel [[Stuncuffs|cuffs]] when he was [[Gorse Conflict|captured]] by [[Hera Syndulla]], [[Kanan Jarrus]], [[Skelly]], and [[Zaluna Myder]]. Additionally, the starship ''[[Forager]]'' possessed a catwalk made of heavy durasteel.<ref name="A New Dawn">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref> The ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was [[armor]]ed with a form-fitted durasteel shell.<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

During the [[Republic Era]], the [[EML-850 light freighter]] was designed and prototyped. However, it had an exterior plating of durasteel which made the craft durable but very expensive. This led to the design being rejected for mass production.<ref name="Star Wars Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

Durasteel was used in the hilts of [[Aayla Secura's lightsaber]]<ref name="Lightsabers">''[[Star Wars Lightsabers: A Guide to Weapons of the Force]]''</ref> and [[Adi Gallia's second lightsaber]].<ref name="SWL">''[[Star Wars Lightsabers: A Guide to Weapons of the Force]]''</ref>

Along with his dulled-down armour, 

[[General]] [[Grievous]]'s armor was made of [[duranium]] composite durasteel,<ref name="Star Wars 100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> as well as [[armorplast]].<ref name="Build R2-D2 53">{{BuildR2Cite|53|Droid Directory}}</ref>

[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[Darth Vader's armor|armor]] was constructed from an alloy that combined durasteel and [[obsidian]].<ref name="Bust Collection 1">{{BustCollectionCite|1|Character}}</ref>

The [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]] was constructed out of [[sandrock]] and [[ditanium]] over durasteel supports.<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

During [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> durasteel was sold throughout [[the galaxy]] for seventy five [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] a unit including on the [[planet]]s [[Akiva]], [[Kijimi]], and [[Tatooine]], as well as the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
