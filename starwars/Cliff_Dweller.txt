{{Quote|''[&hellip;]'' there's always the chance unexpected company could show up, so it's a good idea to know how to mix up a delicious drink to make guests feel welcome.|Strono Tuggs, in ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''{{'s}} recipe for Cliff Dwellers|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
The '''Cliff Dweller''' was a [[fruit]]y, non-[[alcohol]]ic beverage originating from the [[planet]] [[Ahch-To]]. Variants of the beverage could be either [[Color|pink]]-hued or deep orange-colored, topped with a garnish, or served in mugs resembling the [[porg]] [[species]]. In [[34 ABY]], a recipe for Cliff Dwellers was included in the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]]'s [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', and the drink was also served in [[Oga's Cantina]] on the planet [[Batuu]].

==Description==
{{Quote|The Cliff Dweller does just that, incorporatin' simple ingredients that can easily be found on Ahch-To's archipelagos.|Strono Tuggs, on his variant of Cliff Dwellers|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
The Cliff Dweller was a frothy, [[fruit]]y, non-[[alcohol]]ic beverage that could be [[Diet|consumed]] by [[human]]s<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> and [[Artiodac]]s.<ref name="Cookbook" /> One version of the drink was [[Color|pink]]-hued<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> and originated from<ref name="Cookbook" /> the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]]<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Ahch-To]], incorporating several ingredients&mdash;including a garnish&mdash;that could be found within the world's archipelagos.<ref name="Cookbook" /> The drink's name was inspired by Ahch-To's cliffs.<ref name="Drinks" /> Another variant of the drink was deep orange-colored and could be served in mugs.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />

==History==
{{Quote|Its surprisingly tropical flair manages to make Ahch-To's rocky shores seem like paradise.|Strono Tuggs' opinion on the beverage|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
[[File:Cliff_Dweller.jpg|thumb|left|200px|A Cliff Dweller from Oga's Cantina]]
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] published his first [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', and included a recipe for a pink-hued version of the Cliff Dweller. He felt the beverage had "tropical flair" and indicated that the refreshment was one unique way to incorporate readily available local ingredients. According to Tuggs, even beings dwelling in the cliffs of the remote planet of Ahch-To needed to know a drink recipe in case they ever had unexpected company, and the Cliff Dweller was a good option.<ref name="Cookbook" />

Deep orange-colored Cliff Dwellers were<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> a popular offering<ref name="Drinks">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Drinks]]''</ref> at the [[Oga's Cantina]] establishment in the [[Black Spire Outpost]] [[City|settlement]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> the same [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="GE date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> The [[cantina]] offered the drink in [[glass]]es<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> or in mugs carved to look like [[porg]]s,<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> a [[species]] of [[bird]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to Ahch-To.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> Also in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] included Cliff Dweller in a listing of the cantina's offerings in his book ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> Around then, Tuggs also included his cookbook's Cliff Dweller recipe in a small pamphlet of recipes meant to help a Batuu visitor learn more about the planet.<ref name="Treasures">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Treasures from Batuu]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:SWGECookbook-Cover.jpg|thumb|right|210px|An out-of-universe recipe for Cliff Dwellers was included in ''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''.]]
The Cliff Dweller first appeared as a beverage sold to guests at the Oga's Cantina bar in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] upon its debut in [[Disneyland]] Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge|date=2019-05-10}}</ref> The drink was first revealed in a [[Wikipedia:D23 (Disney)|D23]] article on [[February 27]] of the same year.<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=galaxys-edge-announcements|text=Everything You Need to Know About ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge|date=2019-02-27}}</ref> The menu's description of the beverage reads, "[[Wikipedia:Citrus#Culinary|Citrus Juices]], [[Wikipedia:Coconut|Coconut]], [[Wikipedia:Hibiscus|Hibiscus]]-[[Wikipedia:Grenadine|Grenadine]], and [[Wikipedia:Seagram|Seagram's®]] [[Wikipedia:Ginger ale|Ginger Ale]] served in a souvenir porg mug."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/dinner|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Dinner Menu}}</ref>

An [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the drink is included in the recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> and released on [[November 5]], 2019.<ref name="Insight">{{Insight|url=product/star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The ingredients [[Wikipedia:Orange juice|orange juice]], [[Wikipedia:Pineapple juice|pineapple juice]], [[Wikipedia:Lime (fruit)#Culinary|lime juice]], grenadine, and [[Wikipedia:Coconut milk|coconut cream]] are shaken in a [[Wikipedia:Cocktail shaker|cocktail shaker]], then poured over chipped ice and topped with [[Wikipedia:Ginger|ginger]] [[Wikipedia:Ale|ale]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> In an interview, Sumerak noted the Cliff Dweller as one of his favorites from the recipe collection's drink selection.<ref name="ForceNet">{{WebCite|url=https://forcecast.net/story/home/The_ForceCast_Author_Series_With_Chelsea_MonroeCassel_And_Marc_Sumerak_185657.asp|text=The ForceCast Author Series With Chelsea Monroe-Cassel And Marc Sumerak|author=Steve|date=2019-11-15|format=[[Wikipedia:Podcast|Podcast]]|work=[https://forcecast.net/ ForceCast.net]|archivedate=20240327213404}}</ref> The official [[Wikipedia:Disney Experiences|Disney Parks]] Blog posted a similar recipe that uses the same ingredients as the cookbook but in different ratios, and it specifies hibiscus grenadine like the theme park version. According to the post, the measurements were converted from the restaurant's large batch recipe.<ref name="DPB recipe">{{DPB|url=2020/08/disneymagicmoments-cooking-up-magic-for-anniversary-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge-with-specialty-drink-from-ogas-cantina|text=#DisneyMagicMoments: Cooking Up Magic for Anniversary of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge with Specialty Drink from Oga's Cantina|date=2022-08-09}}</ref>
