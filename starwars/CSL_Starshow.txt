'''CSL Starshow''' was a [[fireworks]] show in space put on by the [[Chandrila Star Line]] for passengers of the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' while the ship was above the planet [[Bespin]]. Guests watched the display from in the ship's [[Atrium (Halcyon)|Atrium]] on Deck 6. The music of singer [[Gaya]] was featured during the show when she was the ship's featured performer.<ref name="SWGS Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)|''Star Wars'': Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)]]</ref>
