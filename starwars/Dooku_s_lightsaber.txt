'''Dooku's lightsaber''', also known as '''Darth Tyranus' lightsaber''', or '''Dooku's Sith lightsaber''', was the personal [[Curved-hilt lightsaber|curved-hilt]] [[lightsaber]] of [[Dooku]], the [[Count of Serenno]]. The former [[Jedi Master]], who became [[Darth]] Tyranus and the [[Politics|political]] [[Head of State|leader]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], constructed the weapon's unique hilt with a [[kyber crystal]] that emitted a [[Color|red]] blade. As the [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Darth Sidious]], Dooku used the Sith blade against his foes.

The weapon was built for the purpose of [[lightsaber combat]], and the [[Sith Lord]] carried it with him into numerous battles during the [[Clone Wars]] against the Jedi, a conflict he and his master orchestrated from both sides. In his [[Battle of Coruscant|final bout]], Dooku lost his [[Sith lightsaber]] to the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] once he was defeated in a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] aboard the [[starship]] ''[[Invisible Hand]]''. Skywalker used Dooku's own weapon to [[Death|behead]] the Sith Lord and end his life.

==Description==
{{Quote|When he became my apprentice, Count Dooku was already in possession of a lightsaber that he carried during his many years as a Jedi. His weapon was handcrafted to meet his high standards of excellence and sophistication. The lightsaber's unusual curved hilt was perfectly balanced to complement his graceful fighting style and expertise as a duelist.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="The Secrets of the Sith">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
[[File:CountDookuCrystal--LightsaberSeries.png|thumb|left|180px|Darth Sidious presented Dooku this red crystal when he became Darth Tyranus.]]
When he was a [[Jedi Master]], [[Dooku]] set aside the [[Dooku's first lightsaber|first lightsaber]] he had used as a [[Padawan]] to create one that was superior. The new [[lightsaber]] used a personalized design, and it had no connection to his master, [[Yoda]], or to the fashion of that [[time]]. Dooku studied the [[Jedi Archives]]' records to make the design, and the lightsaber was made to suit his technique, and favored long, elegant moves and deftness of hand. He continued to use the blade even after he became a [[Sith]],<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> but by then he had made minor modifications to the cosmetics of his hilt and his lightsaber had begun to generate a [[Color|red]]-colored blade as a result of Dooku utilizing a red [[kyber crystal]] provided to him by his new master, [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Expert Guide">''[[Star Wars Expert Guide]]''</ref>

Unlike any standard lightsaber, Dooku crafted his with an unusual curved shape, which helped control the weapon when in a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]]. Primarily, it allowed Dooku greater finesse and precision in combat when lunging or slashing at an opponent. This was done by Dooku as he considered himself a sophisticated duelist,<ref name="Ultimate" /> often able to use his lightsaber under the control of only one hand, and against multiple opponents.<ref name="AotC" /><ref name="RotS" />

==History==
{{Quote|That lightsaber. It's&mdash;Where did you get that?''"<br />"''I made it, of course.|[[Jak'zin]] and Dooku, shortly before the latter kills the former|<ref name="AoR Dooku" />}}
[[File:YodaDookuDuelingDemo-AORDooku1.png|thumb|right|250px|Jedi Master Dooku was a skilled lightsaber duelist who learned the Jedi arts from Grand Master Yoda.]]
As sometimes occurred in the [[Jedi Order]], [[Jedi]] Master Dooku set aside the [[Dooku's first lightsaber|first lightsaber]] he had used as a Padawan. Dooku wished to create a superior lightsaber, and, after studying Jedi Archives to make the design, the Jedi Master had his personalized design. His lightsaber had no connection to the fashion at the time or to his master, Yoda, who had a [[Shoto lightsaber|shoto]]. This lightsaber was made to suit his technique, and this weapon favored long, elegant moves and deftness of hand.<ref name="CVD" /> The lightsaber gave off a blue color, and Dooku even dueled Yoda with the lightsaber. However, Dooku would leave the Jedi Order sometime before the [[Invasion of Naboo]], where his former Padawan, [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], was killed.<ref name="AoR Dooku" /> Although Dooku offered to leave the lightsaber with the Jedi, he decided to keep it upon Yoda's urging.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>

Dooku continued to use the blade even after he became a [[Sith]]. The blade still retained its blue color when he dueled and killed Master [[Yaddle]] and reaffirmed his new allegiance to the Dark Side.<ref name="The Sith Lord">{{TOTJ|The Sith Lord}}</ref>

During the [[The galaxy|galactic]]-conflict known as the Clone Wars, the weapon now had a red color as a result of it using a red kyber crystal provided by his new master Darth Sidious.<ref name="Expert Guide" /> Ten [[Standard year|years]] after the Invasion of Naboo, Dooku used his weapon at the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] against Obi-Wan, Anakin and Yoda, which started the Clone Wars.<ref name="AotC" />

[[File:DookuVsYoda-AOTC.png|thumb|left|220px|Renouncing the Jedi Order, Count Dooku challenged his former master to a lightsaber duel.]]
In a private hangar, as he made his escape from [[Geonosis]], Dooku was confronted by the Jedi Knight [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his Padawan, [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="AotC" /> Dooku was easily<ref name="Ultimate" /> able to best his opponents with his lightsaber, wounding both Kenobi and Skywalker, the latter of which Dooku severed his arm after a brief duel. Immediately after, Dooku was confronted by his former Jedi Master, Yoda, whom Dooku was unable to best in combat with either the Force or his lightsaber, regardless of having challenged his former master to prove how powerful he had become.<ref name="AotC" />

[[File:Hondo_Dookus_lightsaber.png|thumb|right|240px|Hondo Ohnaka displayed Dooku's lightsaber as proof that the Count was his prisoner.]]
At one point during the Clone Wars, Kenobi and Skywalker tracked Dooku's starship down in an attempt to capture him, but failed. A brief conflict led to a crash landing for Dooku's [[Punworcca 116-class interstellar sloop|solar sailer]] on [[Vanqor]], where the [[Weequay]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] and his [[pirate]] [[Ohnaka Gang|gang]] offered him transport to [[Florrum]]. However, Ohnaka had no intention of aiding Dooku, instead using the loyal [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]] [[Pilf Mukmuk]] to steal Dooku's lightsaber and take the Sith Lord captive. Ohnaka demanded a ransom for Dooku, presenting the unique, curved lightsaber as proof of the Sith Lord's capture to the Republic and the Jedi&mdash;which convinced Yoda, who confirmed the weapon's authenticity.<ref name="Dooku Captured" />

Dooku continued to use his Sith lightsaber through the Clone Wars under the guise as the leader of the Separatist Alliance, leading the conflict against the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Ultimate" /> Although Dooku secretly operated under his hidden identity of Tyranus, he had no qualms about using the Sith weapon out in the open. Dooku killed the [[Gungan]] [[Treason|traitor]] [[Rish Loo]] with his lightsaber in front of Skywalker on [[Naboo]] during the war,<ref name="Shadow Warrior">{{TCW|Shadow Warrior}}</ref> and later killed the [[Pyke Syndicate]] leader [[Lom Pyke]], in front of both Kenobi and Skywalker, with his lightsaber as well. It was this [[Mission to Oba Diah (Clone Wars)|mission]] that the Jedi learned Dooku and Tyranus were one and the same.<ref name="The Lost One">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref>

[[File:Makashi2.png|thumb|left|230px|Dooku fought against both Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker on several occasions in the Clone Wars.]]
Eventually, Dooku attempted to train two [[Sith apprentice]]s of his own to overthrow Darth Sidious and become the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] himself, as was the [[Rule of Two|way of the Sith]]. However, although he successfully recruited both [[Asajj Ventress]] and [[Savage Opress]] to his cause for a time, eventually they were both failures as Sith; regardless, Dooku used both his knowledge of the Force and his dueling skill to train them both.<ref name="Ultimate" /> He also successfully trained the [[cyborg]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] in lightsaber combat; Grievous went on to kill numerous Jedi in the Clone Wars and collect their lightsabers for his collection, becoming a feared opponent. Grievous was eventually defeated in [[Battle of Utapau|combat]] by Kenobi.<ref name="RotS" />

During the [[Battle of Coruscant]]&mdash;in what would become Dooku's final bout&mdash;Dooku faced-off once again with Kenobi and Skywalker aboard Grievous' [[starship]], the ''[[Invisible Hand]]'', in the General's quarters. The two Jedi believed they were there to rescue the [[Supreme Chancellor]] Palpatine, who was in secret Darth Sidious;<ref name="RotS" /> Sidious' true agenda, however, was to make Dooku a necessary sacrifice to recruit a new apprentice in Skywalker.<ref name="Ultimate" />[[File:DookuScaredtoDeath.png|thumb|right|250px|During the Battle of Coruscant, Skywalker executed Dooku with the Sith Lord's own lightsaber.]]
After Dooku defeated Kenobi and rendered him unconscious, a vengeful Skywalker charged at Dooku during their duel.<ref name="RotS" /> Although Dooku was confident that his skills with a lightsaber would be enough to best the Jedi Knight &mdash;just like he had previously done on Geonosis&mdash;<ref name="Ultimate" /> he was unable to keep up with Skywalker's ferocity and lost both his hands in a quick motion from Skywalker. The Jedi Knight then took Dooku's own lightsaber and, at the urging of Sidious, beheaded the unarmed Count Dooku with the Sith Lord's own weapon.<ref name="RotS" />

Once Skywalker escaped with both Kenobi and Palpatine in tow, Dooku's lightsaber was left behind aside Dooku's corpse in Grievous' quarters on the ''Invisible Hand''. Shortly thereafter, the General's quarters were destroyed.<ref name="RotS" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I've probably done more sword fights on celluloid than any actor in history, and this fight is greater than anything I've ever been involved in.|Christopher Lee in the ''Attack of the Clones'' "Action Featurette"|<ref name="Story">{{Film|II}} "Story Featurette" {{C|''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''}}</ref>}}
[[File:Dooku_BTS_two_lightsabers.jpg|thumb|left|200px|The late Christopher Lee, who portrayed Dooku in the ''Star Wars'' films, on the set of Episode II filming Dooku's lightsaber duel with Yoda.]]
Dooku's lightsaber first appeared alongside Count Dooku's first on-screen appearance in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]], [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="AotC" /><ref name="RotS" /> Dooku and his weapon continued to appear in ''Star Wars'' [[canon]] through other media, including [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]],<ref name="TCW">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|television series of the same name]],<ref name="TCWTV">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' {{C|TV series}}]]</ref> and ''[[The Clone Wars Legacy]]''<ref name="Darth Maul">''[[Star Wars: Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir]]''</ref> and a few novels.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref><ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>

When creating Count Dooku, [[George Lucas]]&ndash;creator of the ''Star Wars'' saga&ndash;wanted an older Jedi who was "very good," "elegant," and "sophisticated." These traits were later represented in Dooku's portrayal and in his feats with a lightsaber.<ref name="Story" /> The late [[Christopher Lee]], who portrayed Dooku in the live-action ''Star Wars'' films, said he found the role "very rewarding" and that the action scenes from ''Attack of Clones'' were the greatest he had ever been involved in.<ref name="Action">{{Film|II}} "Action Featurette" {{C|''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''}}</ref> Lee, a renowned expert at sword fighting throughout his career, had to learn new fighting styles for the lightsaber sequences.<ref name="Making It Count">{{HBCite|62}} {{C|[[June 13]], [[2002]]}} {{C|Excerpt: ''He's done more sword-fights than any other actor. [&hellip;] '''This is a different kind of sword-fighting, so he had to learn a new kind,''' and he's very good at that''.}}</ref> [[Kyle Rowling]] doubled for Lee in both ''Attack of the Clones'' and ''Revenge of the Sith'',<ref name="AotC" /><ref name="RotS" /> in particular during some of the lightsaber sequences, where Rowling's face was replaced with Lee's using [[Wikipedia:Computer-generated imagery|CGI]].<ref name="Making It Count" /><ref name="Summons the Force">{{SWArchive|url=episode-ii/bts/profile/f20011211/index.html|text=Christopher Lee Summons the Force}} {{C|[[December 11]], [[2001]]}} {{C|Excerpt: ''We went from black-and-white movies to color, and that of course went onto yellow screen and blue screen, '''and now what they call CGI -- well, it's all magic'''''.}}</ref> The distinctive curved shape of the lightsaber handle was a result of earlier exploration into Sith villains. Art Department Assistant [[Roel Robles]] drew inspiration from various Filipino swords, knives, and spears; the [[Wikipedia:Barong (knife)|Barong]], one of them, had a curved handle which provided a firmer grip for its bearer.<ref name="old-db-bts">{{DB|character|countdooku|Dooku, Count}}</ref>

Originally, there were plans to have Count Dooku wield two lightsabers during his duel with Yoda in ''Attack of the Clones''. This was later dropped from the film as it was not needed, although scenes were filmed, parts of which can be found on the special features of the ''Attack of the Clones'' DVD release.<ref name="AotC" /> In concept art for unfinished episodes of ''The Clone Wars Legacy'', Dooku's lightsaber was the basis for the [[Oppo Rancisis's lightsaber|lightsaber]] of Jedi Master [[Oppo Rancisis]], who ultimately made an appearance in the episode "[[The Big Bang]]" without the lightsaber.<ref name="CrystalCrisisConcept">{{SW|url=tv-shows/crystal-crisis-on-utapau-concept-art-gallery|text=Crystal Crisis on Utapau Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Excerpt: '''''Oppo's lightsaber is sourced from CW308 "Dooku Lightsaber"''', and the scale is unchanged.''}}</ref> The reference booklet "[[Lightsabers and Jedi Equipment]]" mistakenly says Dooku's lightsaber used a [[synthetic kyber crystal]].<ref name="Lightsabers and Jedi Equipment">{{EncyclopediaCite|Lightsabers and Jedi Equipment}}</ref>
