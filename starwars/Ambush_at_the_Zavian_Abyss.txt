The '''ambush at the Zavian Abyss''' occurred when the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Overseer (Imperial-class)|''Overseer'']] was lured inside the [[Zavian Abyss]] and ambushed by the [[New Republic]], to prevent it from finding [[Project Starhawk]]. During the ambush, the New Republic revealed the newly completed prototype [[Starhawk-class battleship|''Starhawk''-class battleship]] ''[[Starhawk]]'' and forced the ''Overseer'', to retreat.<ref name="Squadrons" />
