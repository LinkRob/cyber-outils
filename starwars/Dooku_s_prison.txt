'''Dooku's prison''' was a [[prison]] consisting of various cells and torture chambers located deep within the rockface of a large cliff where [[Castle Serenno]] was situated on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Serenno]]. At least one known entrance to the prison was a huge metal door flanked by controls in a long hallway in the palace. One of the cells in the prison featured a [[force field]].

In [[19 BBY]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Quinlan Vos]] was held at the prison where he was [[torture]]d by [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] and various [[droid]]s. Former [[Sith assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] and [[Boba Fett]]'s [[Krayt's Claw|syndicate]] later attempted to [[Mission to Serenno (Krayt's Claw)|rescue]] Vos however Vos by then fully embraced the [[dark side of the Force]] and spurned their rescue.

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Sith assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] participated in interrogations of various prisoners in the prison below [[Castle Serenno]]. Ventress later noted that no prisoners were released from the prison. Towards the end of the Clone Wars, [[Jedi Master]] [[Quinlan Vos]] was captured and detained by [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] in one of the many cells located below the palace after he and former Sith assassin Ventress attempted to [[Mission to Raxus Secundus|assassinate]] Dooku on the Outer Rim planet [[Raxus Secundus]]. While he was detained, Vos was continually [[torture]]d by [[droid]]s who also monitored his sleep patterns and gave meals at irregular intervals. Dooku sometimes came to the cell where Vos was held and observed Vos being tortured or blast Vos with [[force lightning]] to hear his screams. Vos continually tried to press Dooku to talk in the hope that Dooku let something slip however this did not work according to Vos. At one point, Dooku interrogated Vos and sarcastically asked him about his mission. Although Vos answered truthfully, Dooku still ordered the torture droid to continue to torture Vos.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

Ventress and [[Boba Fett]]'s [[Krayt's Claw|syndicate]] later attempted to [[Mission to Serenno (Krayt's Claw)|rescue]] Vos. The [[bounty hunter]]s distracted the various [[battle droid]]s guarding Castle Serenno while Ventress sneaked into the palace. While sneaking into the palace, Ventress fought and destroyed several droids patrolling the inside of the palace. She later managed to enter into the prison where she met Vos. Vos however [[Force choke|force- choked]] Ventress, enraged that Ventress was the one who lied to Vos about who [[Death|killed]] Jedi Master [[Tholme]] after he used his [[Psychometry|psychometric]] powers when holding Tholme's [[lightsaber]]. Vos later fought against Ventress despite numerous pleas to not fully embrace the [[Dark side of the Force]]. This forced Ventress and the bounty hunters to flee the prison and the palace.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />
