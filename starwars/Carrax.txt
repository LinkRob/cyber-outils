'''Carrax''' was a [[human]] who resided on the [[planet]] [[Calior]]. Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Chief Regional Auditor]] [[Cygnus Anton]] visited Calior with an escort that included guards of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate. After seeing the Crimson Dawn troops, Carrax told Anton that they were supposed to protect Calior from the syndicate, and one of the guards then [[Death|shot]] him, killing him.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Aaah! You&mdash;you ''killed him'' before you even said what you ''wanted''!''"<br />"''So I did. Everyone in a ''listening'' mood now?.|A human and Chief Regional Auditor Cygnus Anton|<ref name="Vader 18" />}}
Carrax was a [[human]] who lived on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Calior]].<ref name="Vader 18" /> Sometime between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Bird|avian]] [[Cygnus Anton]], [[Chief Regional Auditor]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], visited Calior to claim the planet's [[southern water works]] as Imperial property and demand that Calior's inhabitants pay a [[Standard month|monthly]] tribute of five thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] if they desired continued access to the facility. As Anton's [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]] landed on Calior, Carrax and two other humans approached the [[shuttle]]. Carrax questioned Anton on their identity and told the avian that they were supposed to protect Calior from the syndicate after he saw the Crimson Dawn troops.<ref name="Vader 18" />

One of the guards [[Death|mortally shot]] Carrax, prompting one of the humans that had accompanied him to Anton's [[Starship|vessel]] to complain that Anton had murdered Carrax before the avian presented their demands, which Anton confirmed, asking if Calior's people were in a listening mood after Carrax's death.<ref name="Vader 18" />

==Personality and traits==
Carrax had [[Color|black]] [[hair]] and light brown skin.<ref name="Vader 18" />

==Equipment==
Carrax wore a [[breath mask]], brown [[clothing]], and gray trousers.<ref name="Vader 18" />

The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]] and illustrated by [[Leonard Kirk]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/89724/star_wars_darth_vader_2020_18|text=Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020) #18}}</ref>
