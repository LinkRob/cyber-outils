'''Cadet''' was a title used to refer to youthful individuals enrolled in a training or educational program. Prior to and during the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] trained [[clone cadet]]s on [[Kamino]], who would one day become [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Episode II" /> The [[New Mandalorians]] created the [[Imperial Academy of Mandalore|Royal Academy of Government]], where young [[Mandalorian]] cadets were sent and instructed how to become great leaders.<ref name="TA" /> After the fall of the [[Galactic Republic]], the newly-formed [[Galactic Empire]] set up the [[Imperial Academy]] to train [[Imperial cadet]]s to serve in the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="Breaking Ranks" />
