The '''Baroonda Circuit''' was a [[podracing]] course<ref name="Scum & Villainy" /> that shared its name with the [[city]] [[Baroonda (city)|Baroonda]], where [[the Rawani Cup]] podracing event was held.<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|BSO 401.72|park}}</ref> It also shared its name with<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Baroonda system]]</ref> the astronomical object<ref name="RacingWinner">{{LSWCite|57|The Racing Winner}}</ref> [[Baroonda]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> At the [[time]] of his disappearance during the [[32 BBY|7945]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] [[Boonta Eve Classic]] podrace, the [[Xamster]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Neva Kee]] was considered the rising star of the Baroonda Circuit according to the [[author]] [[Exantor Divo]].<ref name="Scum & Villainy" />

A [[hologram]] supposedly showing what had happened to Kee during the race was sent to [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[Inspector]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tanivos Divo]] and was later included alongside a mention of the Baroonda Circuit in the [[book]] ''[[Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was written by Exantor, who was Tanivos's [[Divo family|grandson]],<ref name="Scum & Villainy" /> and published in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{ScumAndVillainyDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Baroonda Circuit was mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Scum & Villainy" /> The circuit also shares its name with the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Baroonda/Legends|Baroonda]], which first appeared as the location of several [[Baroonda drag course 1|podracing]] [[Baroonda drag course 2|courses]] in the [[1999]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']].<ref name="SWEIR">[[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']]</ref>
