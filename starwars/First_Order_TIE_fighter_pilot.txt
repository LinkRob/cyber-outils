'''First Order TIE fighter pilots''', also known as '''First Order TIE pilots''', or '''TIE pilots''', were skilled [[pilot]]s who served the [[First Order]], hunting down enemy vessels and protecting space around installations and [[warship]]s in their advanced [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s. Elite pilots had access to [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s, two-seater [[TIE series|TIE craft]] equipped with enhanced weaponry and [[sensor]] systems.

==Equipment==
{{Quote|So which weapons are the most important? I'll make it easy for you. Take a good look at each other. Now, take a good look at yourselves. When you climb into your cockpit, and settle into your seat, and ease your finger close to the trigger on your control stick, know that the TIE fighter and its cannons are nothing without the instrument that makes them operational.|Jad Bean, to the First Order TIE pilots of Zeta Squadron|<ref name="TIE Manual">''[[TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>}}
[[File:FirstOrderTIEPilotHelmet-CGSWG.png|thumb|250px|left|A First Order TIE pilot helmet]]
First Order TIE pilots wore a uniform consisting of a [[Color|black]] [[Flight suit|jumpsuit]] with a chestplate with [[life support]] gear, [[pauldron]]s on the shoulders, [[gauntlet]]s, a [[utility belt]], [[boot]]s, and a helmet with [[goggles]]. The helmet had a flexible gas transfer hose, which connected to the pilot's life support system.<ref name="Episode VII" /> The helmet's goggles also had an interior viewplate that projected combat data to the pilots.<ref name="TROS VD" /> TIE pilot cadets were not equipped with pauldrons or gauntlets and wore a special insignia on their left shoulder.<ref name="Live">{{Resistance|Live Fire}}</ref>

Pilots were typically issued with a [[SE-44C blaster pistol]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> which could be carried in a [[holster]] on their belt when not in use.<ref name="TFA VD" /> 

==Training==
{{Quote|Our Order is built on survival of the fittest. If one pilot can't do their duty and they die serving the First Order, so be it. The Order becomes stronger by culling the weak.|[[Lieutenant]] [[Galek]], to [[Tamara Ryvora]] and [[Jace Rucklin]]|audio=CullingTheWeak-LiveFire.flac|<ref name="Live Fire" /> }}
[[File:GalekAndCadets-LiveFire.png|thumb|left|300px|Lieutenant [[Galek]] speaks to TIE pilot cadets.]]
Since the conclusion of the [[Galactic Civil War]] had seen the various flight academies under the Empire returned to local institutions, the TIE pilots of the First Order were trained directly within First Order-owned ships, whether they be newly created ships or Imperial survivors of the war. Largely because they could not afford to train an endless supply of pilots,<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> the First Order treated its fighter pilots as important parts of its war machine instead of disposable assets; unlike its [[Galactic Empire|predecessor]]. Potential pilots were identified as [[Youngling|children]] and given rigorous training, often within the corridors of warships, then tested ruthlessly throughout their careers to ensure their reflexes, visual acuity and coordination measured up.<ref name="First Order TIE Fighter Pilots" /> 

Due to the nature of their training, many of them never set foot on a planet during this time.<ref name="Epsilon Squadron Cadet card">{{FFGXW2|set=TIE/fo Fighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Epsilon Squadron Cadet}}</ref> However, the First Order was also willing to recruit regular citizens into their starfighter corps,<ref name="Into the Unknown">{{Resistance|Into the Unknown}}</ref> training them to be TIE pilots.<ref name="Live Fire">{{Resistance|Live Fire}}</ref> The First Order's pilots had a higher life expectancy than their Imperial equivalents; largely due to their not having to be burdened with a restrictive galactic budget.<ref name="Zeta Squadron Pilot card">{{FFGXW2|set=TIE/fo Fighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Zeta Squadron Pilot}}</ref> In combat, TIE pilots tended to rely on the same tactics and maneuvers, which [[Resistance pilot]] [[Jessika Pava]] assumed to be something drilled into the pilots during training. While still effective, such overreliance on their standard tactics could make TIE pilots predictable to their foes.<ref name="Poe Dameron 30">[[Poe Dameron 30|''Poe Dameron'' 30]]</ref> They were equipped with enhanced weaponry and [[sensor]] systems on their fighters.<ref name="First Order Special Forces TIE Fighter">{{Databank|first-order-special-forces-tie-fighter|First Order Special Forces TIE Fighter}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|TIE pilots don't mix up their tactics too much. You see the same maneuvers over and over. Don't get me wrong&mdash;they can be ''effective''&hellip; but not a lot of variation. Guess it's how they're trained. But over Grail City&hellip; they were ''exuberant.'' Taking risks I'd never seen them take before.|Jessika Pava recalls the performance of First Order TIE pilots during the Battle of Grail City|<ref name="Poe Dameron 30" />}}
Pilots who demonstrated both exceptional skill and unwavering loyalty were promoted to elite ranks within First Order during the [[Cold War]].<ref name="Omega Squadron Ace card">{{FFGXW2|set=TIE/fo Fighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Omega Squadron Ace}}</ref> [[Major]] [[Elrik Vonreg]] was a pilot who answered directly to [[Captain]] [[Phasma]].<ref name="The Recruit" /> [[TN-3465]] was recruited by Phasma for the [[hunt for Rivas]].<ref name="Captain Phasma 4">[[Captain Phasma 4|''Captain Phasma'' 4]]</ref> "[["Quickdraw"|Quickdraw]]" was a pilot who was once involved in an engagement amid the wreckage of another starship.<ref name="FFG">{{FFGXW|set=Special Forces TIE Expansion Pack|cardname="Quickdraw"}}</ref> During the [[Battle of Ikkrukk|Battle of Grail City]], TIE pilots displayed exuberance and took numerous risks in combat instead of relying on their typical tactics.<ref name="Poe Dameron 30" />
