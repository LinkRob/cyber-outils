'''Eroudan''' was a [[language]] that originated on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Eroudac]] and was known to the [[Hutt]] [[Churo]]. The [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]] word "quiraawa" meant "sun spear" in Eroudan, and "quiraa" could also mean "light" or "prism of light."<ref name="Heart of Eroudac" />
