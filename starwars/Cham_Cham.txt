'''Cham Cham''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[cru]] that was a pet of [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]], a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Mikkian]]. When Mrala was a young [[Youngling|child]], she relied on Cham Cham and her friend, [[Krix Kamerat]], to keep her safe and be with her. Sometime after, they lived on the [[planet]] [[Trymant IV]] when the [[Trymant system|system]] was threatened by debris from the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]. Mrala carried Cham Cham as she attempted to search for safety with her friend Krix. Being Force-sensitive, Mrala could feel through the Force that something terrible was about to happen: she speculated that Cham Cham either shared the feeling or could sense that she was scared.

A group of [[Jedi]] came to the planet's rescue after the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] launched an [[Attack on Trymant IV|attack]]. During the attack, [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] brought Cham Cham onboard a Nihil [[cruiser]] to save Mrala's friend. When Kamerat refused to join them and altered the Nihil to their location, Yoda tried to tell Cham Cham to be quiet. But Cham Cham did not cooperate and attacked a Nihil member, eating their arm. He then fell asleep after the meal and returned with Yoda to Mrala.

Shortly after, Talisola and other Padawans searched for Mrala, who had left the [[space station]] to find Kamerat. They realized that she had not permanently left because she left Cham Cham behind. Cham Cham jumped on the head of Padawan [[Qort]] and refused. He joined the young Padawans as they crammed into a [[Jedi Vector]] to go off and locate Mrala.

==Biography==
===Nihil attack===
[[File:YoungZeenAndKrix-2021THRA1.png|thumb|left|225px|Cham Cham with a young Mrala]]
Cham Cham was the [[cru]] pet to the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]], who she had kept from an early age. Mrala's [[parent]]s had left her when she was young, so she relied on Cham Cham and her friend [[Krix Kamerat]] to look out for her.<ref name="HRA 1" />

During the [[High Republic Era]], Cham Cham resided on the [[planet]] [[Trymant IV]] with Mrala and Kamerat. Cham Cham was with Mrala and Kamerat when they were evacuating during a [[Trymant IV disaster|cataclysm]] from the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]. She carried Cham Cham as they ran from a [[Nihil]] [[Spider Cruiser]]. She then quickly hid with Cham Cham from Nihil [[Pirate|marauders]]. Cham Cham then accompanied his owner as they attempted to stop the Nihil [[Attack on Trymant IV|attack]] with the help of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s.<ref name="HRA 1" />

===Helping the Jedi===
[[File:PurpleGlow-2021THRA2.png|thumb|right|175px|Yoda and Cham Cham discover an artifact.]]
As the battle raged between the Nihil and the Jedi, [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] asked Mrala to allow him to borrow Cham Cham to sneak onto the Nihil [[cruiser]] and rescue Kamerat. Cham Cham looked back on his owner with worrying [[eye]]s as Yoda took him along for the adventure.<ref name="HRA 2" />

Yoda and Cham Cham snuck past the [[Eye of the Nihil|leader of the Nihil]], [[Marchion Ro]] and aboard the Spider Cruiser. They surprised Kamerat, and handed him a [[holoprojector]] to use to track him. Kamerat was also surprised to see Cham Cham with Yoda but saw it as proof that Mrala had lied to him. He then called out to everyone on board that he had found Yoda, so the Jedi master took off with Cham Cham. As they hid behind crates, Yoda opened up a case with an [[Nameless control rods|artifact]] owned by Ro. As Nihil personnel approached them, Cham Cham attacked a Nihil and bit off their fingers and arm.<ref name="HRA 2" />

Yoda and Cham Cham managed to escape from the Nihil ship as the Jedi won the battle. Cham Cham was sleeping when Yoda returned him to Mrala, who was shocked that he ate part of a Nihil. Cham Cham continued to rest as Mrala joined the rest of the Jedi on the [[Starlight Beacon]].<ref name="HRA 2" />

===On Starlight Beacon===
[[File:ZeenOnStarlight-2021THRA3.png|thumb|left|175px|Cham Cham playing with the young Jedi]]
A [[Standard week|week]] later, Cham Cham rested with Mrala as she viewed a message from Kamerat, who was still very angry with her. During their stay, Cham Cham played with the Jedi [[youngling]]s, who were very excited to see him fly.<ref name="HRA 3">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 3|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 3]]</ref>

One [[Standard day|day]], Padawan [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] visited Mrala and snuggled Cham Cham. She alerted Mrala to the arrival of the Jedi Masters, who wanted to discuss her future. As the masters talked to Mrala, Cham Cham continued to play with the Younglings.<ref name="HRA 3" />

===Joining the Jedi rescue===
{{Quote|Do you believe her?''"<br />"''Absolutely. But even if I didn't, if she was really running off for good&hellip;There's no way she'd leave Cham Cham behind!|Farzala Tarabal and Lula Talisola|<ref name="HRA 4">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 4]]</ref>}}
[[File:ChamChamQort-2021THRA4.png|thumb|right|125px|Cham Cham jumped on Qort's head.]]
After Kamerat contacted Mrala, while the Jedi were trying to find the Nihils, telling her that he wanted to meet her, Mrala left the Jedi outpost to see Kamerat leaving Cham Cham behind. As Lula and the Jedi Padawans came into Mrala's room, they noticed that she was gone and started examining her room, finding a [[holoprojector]] with a message on it. The message explained that Mrala was gone to save her friend but that she would come back. They didn't believe she would be gone forever, even because she left Cham Cham behind. The cru then leaped up and jumped on Padawan [[Qort]]'s head. Qort tried to move Cham Cham off his head but to no avail. As the Padawans prepared to leave and help Mrala, Cham Cham squeezed in with them as they all crammed into the last remaining [[Jedi Vector]] and took off.<ref name="HRA 4" />

After the Jedi fought against the Nihils and [[Battle on Quantxi|returned]] from [[Quantxi]] with Mrala, she was reunited with Cham Cham. Cham Cham was in her arms when the Jedi asked her again if she wished to remain at the Starlight Beacon.<ref name="HRA 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 5]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Mm&hellip; Too many, there are. Escape quietly, we mu&hellip;''"<br />"''REEEAROW!''"<br />"''Oh, Cham Cham&hellip;|Master Yoda and Cham Cham|<ref name="HRA 2" />}}
Cham Cham was a male cru with [[Color|brown]] skin and black [[eye]]s. He had large sharp teeth and a [[diet]] that consisted of meat.<ref name="HRA 2" /> Cham Cham had large wings on both sides of his torso with a large claw located on each wing. He had black stripes going from his forehead and down to his back. The cru also possessed a long tail with a claw at the end.<ref name="HRA 4" />

Cham Cham was very loyal to his owner, Mrala and jouryned with her everywhere. He would protect her and comfort her. But Cham Cham would sometimes not listen to directions and go his own way.<ref name="HRA 2" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|He ate a&hellip; a Nihil?!''"<br />"''Only some fingers, I believe. An arm, perhaps.|Zeen Mrala and Yoda |<ref name="HRA 2" />}}
[[File:ChamCham-2021THRA2.png|thumb|left|145px|Cham Cham attacking members of the Nihil]]
Cham Cham had to ability to fly because of his long wings. Although he could crawl, he more often was seen flying in order to keep up with his fellow counterparts.<ref name="HRA 2" /> Mrala speculated if Cham Cham was force-sensitive like she was, but could not come to a definitive conclusion.<ref name="HRA 1" /> Cham Cham also possessed a furious bite which he demonstrated when he ate the arm of a Nihil marauder.<ref name="HRA 2" />
