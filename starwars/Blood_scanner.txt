A '''blood scanner''' was a [[sensor]]y device used to scan [[blood]] samples. Used for medical diagnostics, a blood scanner was able to provide an analysis of an individual's general well-being. In addition, the device excelled at isolating and identifying foreign elements present in one's bloodstream, such as [[disease]]s, chemicals, and [[poison]]s. Once a toxin had been identified, a blood scanner offered advice and options for treatment, detailing antidotes and remedies. Blood scanners were able to analyze the blood of nearly any [[species]] in [[The galaxy|the known galaxy]] and provided extensive information about the patient's health. Around the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], a blood scanner cost 400 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="GaG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blood scanners were mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="GaG" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Blood scanner/Legends|blood scanner]] originated in the [[2015]] Fantasy Flight roleplaying supplement ''[[Keeping the Peace]]''.<ref name="Keeping the Peace">''[[Keeping the Peace]]''</ref>
