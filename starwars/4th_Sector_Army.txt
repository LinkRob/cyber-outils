The '''4th Sector Army''' was a [[Sector Army]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[Galactic Marines|21st Nova Corps]], led by [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Bacara]], was part of the 4th [[Sector]] [[Army]]. Sector Armies from the [[1st Sector Army]] to the [[6th Sector Army]], including the 4th, were reserve commands.

==Description==
[[File:TwilightoftheRepublic-ExecuteOrder66.png|thumb|left|250px|Personnel of the 4th Sector Army included the Galactic Marines and their leader, Jedi General Ki-Adi-Mundi.]]
The 4th Sector Army, as a [[Sector Army]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], was responsible for the defense of their specific area within [[the galaxy]]. At the start of the [[Clone Wars]], the Grand Army was segmented into ten [[Systems Army|Systems Armies]] which were in turn composed of two Sector Armies each. As a result, the 4th Sector Army belonged to the same Systems Army as another Sector Army did. The 4th Sector Army served as a reserve command along with those numbered from the [[1st Sector Army]] up to the [[6th Sector Army]].<ref name="BMF 18" /> As a Sector Army, it contained a total of 147,456 [[clone trooper]]s organized into four [[corps]],<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] establishes that a [[Sector Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] contained 147,456 [[clone trooper]]s and was composed of four [[corps]].</ref> including one being the [[Galactic Marines|21st Nova Corps]].<ref name="Bacara FC" /> Like other Sector Armies, the 4th Sector Army had one of twenty [[Galactic Republic]] [[Haven-class medical station|''Haven''-class medical stations]] towed to them,<ref name="Haven" /> and was attached to one of the twenty numbered [[fleet]]s of the [[Republic Navy]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

==History==
The 4th Sector Army, through its subordinate unit the 21st Nova Corps, participated in various engagements during the Clone Wars<ref name="HC 29">{{HelmetCollectionCite|29|Helmets}}</ref> against the [[battle droid]] [[Army|armies]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="CompEncyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Famously ferocious, the 21st&mdash;or the "Galactic Marines," as the unit came to be known<ref name="BattlesChanged">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>&mdash;were led to many victories by [[Jedi General]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> who commanded the unit alongside [[Clone Commander]] [[Bacara]].<ref name="CompEncyclopedia" /> Bacara and Mundi fought together in many battles.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

[[File:FourthBattleOfMygeetoOrder66-GalaxysEdge2.png|thumb|right|350px|The 21st Nova Corps was lead against the Separatist Droid Army throughout the Clone Wars until the execution of Order 66 during the battle for Mygeeto.]]
The 21st participated in the [[Orto Plutonia campaign]] and the [[Rhen Var campaign]], both victories,<ref name="HC 29">{{HelmetCollectionCite|29|Helmets}}</ref> before participating in the [[Mygeeto Campaign]] during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]]. In that engagement, known as the [[Fourth Battle of Mygeeto]], the 21st Nova Corps, launched an assault on Separatist forces on the surface. Mundi and Bacara led their forces during the battle until [[Order 66]] was communicated to clone forces. The [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|Executive Order Code]] commanded the Republic's clone forces to eliminate their [[Jedi]] [[officer]]s. Mundi was [[Death|killed]] by the 21st Nova Corps,<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> which then proceeded to eliminate Confederate forces.<ref name="HC 15">{{HelmetCollectionCite|15|Helmets}}</ref>
