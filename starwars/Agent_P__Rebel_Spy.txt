'''''Agent P: Rebel Spy''''' was a web browser 2D platformer developed and released by [[Disney Interactive]] on [[June 20]], [[2014]]. It was released as a promotional tie-in to the crossover special ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Web" />
