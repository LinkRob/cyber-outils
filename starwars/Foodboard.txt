'''Foodboards''',<ref name="DB-Dex's Diner">{{DB|location|dexsdiner|Dex's Diner}}</ref> also spelled "'''food-boards'''," were used in [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster's]] restaurant on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]], before<ref name="Menu">{{Hyperspace|url=fans/hyperspace/source/dexdiner/index.html|text=Dining at Dex's|int=Dining at Dex's}}</ref> the establishment was destroyed in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]].<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The Last of the Jedi: Underworld]]''</ref> [[Slider]]s were served on top of grilled, trans-shipped, medium-density food-boards.<ref name="Menu" />

[[Kal Skirata]] served sliced foodboard to the young [[Null-class Advanced Recon Commando|Null ARC troopers]] in his quarters on [[Kamino/Legends|Kamino]]. In his opinion, it looked "less appetizing than the tray" it was on.<ref name="Republic Commando: Triple Zero">''[[Republic Commando: Triple Zero]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Foodboards first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]''. They were later identified in the [[Databank (original)|Databank]] on [[StarWars.com]].
