'''Bantoo''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="EoD" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AttackonBantooDate}}</ref> it fell within the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Occlusion Zone]], and the Nihil [[Attack on Bantoo|sent]] a group led by [[Minister of Protection]] [[General]] [[Abediah Viess]] to collect tithes from the planet. When they arrived at the [[city]] [[Jodzia]], Jodzia's [[Jodzia's mayor|mayor]] refused to pay the tithes, claiming that the Nihil erection of the [[Stormwall]] to create the Occlusion Zone was illegal. In response, the Nihil razed three districts of Jodzia. Feeling trapped by the supposed restrictions her [[Shryke family|family]] had placed on her, [[Melis Shryke]] raided her family's vault and paid tribute to the Nihil, and asked to join them. Viess accepted Shryke's demand.<ref name="EoD" />
