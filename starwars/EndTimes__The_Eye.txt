"'''EndTimes: The Eye'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year five of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign. It was the third adventure in the [[EndTimes trilogy|''EndTimes'' trilogy]] and was written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] and [[Jae Walker]].

==Plot summary==
The heroes learn that [[Len Markus]] has gone in search of the [[Eye of the Sun]], a Force-imbued artifact that, when combined with the power of the [[Darkstaff]], will destroy [[Cularin/Legends|Cularin]]. They confront him at the [[Ch'hala tree|Ch'hala]] [[Heart Tree]] and defeat him before his plan succeeds.
