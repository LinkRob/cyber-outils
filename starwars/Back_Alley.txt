The '''Back Alley''' was an alley in the [[Wonto District]] of [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] which led from the [[Wonto District market]] to an [[Old Junkyard]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an individual had lost a well&ndash;made [[airspeeder]] toy.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
During 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], the [[merqaal]] [[Nix]] led his companion [[Kay Vess]] through the alley and discovered the lost toy. Nix didn't play with it but liked the idea of owning his own ship.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Back Alley first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by Ubisoft. The location is is identided as the "Back Alley" on the games map. The "Tiny Ship" nix treasure found at the Old Junkyard along with other Nix treasures found on Tatooine could be traded with the [[human]] [[tailor]] [[Berek Keehro]] in [[Mirogana]] on [[Toshara]] to unlock the [[Color|red]] [[hat|headband]] cosmetic for Nix.<ref name="Outlaws" />
