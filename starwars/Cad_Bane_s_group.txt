'''Cad Bane's group''', also known as '''Cad Bane's crew''', were a group of [[bounty hunter]]s led by the [[Duros]] [[bounty]] hunter [[Cad Bane]] from before [[32 BBY]], until at least [[20 BBY]]. Although the group's members changed over time, the group was always made up of a variety of dangerous individuals, including bounty hunters such as [[Aurra Sing]] and [[Embo]].

==History==
===Working with Darth Maul===
[[File:Darth_Maul_3_Textless.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Sith apprentice Darth Maul hired Cad Bane and his crew as part of a scheme to enter the auction of Jedi Padawan Eldra Kaitis.]]
At some point before the [[Invasion of Naboo]], [[Cad Bane]], along with fellow [[bounty hunter]]s [[Aurra Sing]] and [[Vorhdeilo]] intervened with the [[Sith Lord]], [[Maul|Darth Maul's]], [[Altercation on Nar Shaddaa|brawl]] on [[Nar Shaddaa]] in [[Hutta Town]]. Bane and his compatriots then agreed to help him enter [[Xev Xrexus]]'s [[Xrexus Cartel|Cartel's]] [[Auction of Eldra Kaitis|auction]] of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Eldra Kaitis]]. Together, Maul, Bane and his group then stole a [[Haddrex Gang ship|ship]] from the [[Haddrex Gang]]. They then met up with [[Troo-tril-tek]], who was able to capture the [[protocol droid]] [[FE-B3]] and force to reveal the auction's [[Xev's hideout|location]]. Together, they went to the [[Drazkel system]]. Bane, Maul, Sing, and Fee-Bee were then able to attend the auction.<ref name="Darth Maul 2">[[Darth Maul (2017) 2|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 2]]</ref>

Bane, Maul and the rest of the group then came up with plan to take Kaitis from the highest bidder. After [[Jee Kra]] entered his bid, Bane and his group then took his [[Shekelesh-class freight gunship]] from his crew. Maul, Bane and his group then confronted Jee Kra and his remaining crew as they boarded their ship. They then allowed the [[Sith apprentice]] to kill Kra and his crew himself. Maul, Bane, and his group then left, but their [[gunship]] was rigged with explosives<ref name="Darth Maul 3">[[Darth Maul (2017) 3|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 3]]</ref> and crash-landed on [[Moon of Drazkel|Drazkel's moon]].<ref name="Darth Maul 4">[[Darth Maul (2017) 4|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 4]]</ref>

Only, Maul, Bane, Sing, Vorhdeilo, and Kaitis survived the crash. When they saw Xrexus and her guests coming to [[Skirmish on the moon of Drazkel|hunt]] them down, Maul took Kaitis away while the rest of the crew formed up to battle the hunters. As Bane, Sing, and Vorhdeilo fought the Xrexus's guests, Maul dueled Kaitis.<ref name="Darth Maul 4" /> Bane, Sing, and Vorhdeilo then planned to steal a ship from the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]]s. After Bane and Sing took out the [[Trandoshan]]s and the Weequays, they took a [[Flarestar-class attack shuttle|''Flarestar''-class attack shuttle]]. Bane then destroyed the other ships, rendezvousing with Sing and Vorhdeilo, and finally with Maul, who had killed Kaitis.<ref name="Darth Maul 5">[[Darth Maul (2017) 5|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 5]]</ref>

===Clone Wars===
====Work for Darth Sidious====
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name=" 21 BBY Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places "[[Liberty on Ryloth]]" and "[[Children of the Force]]" in [[21 BBY]]. As "[[Holocron Heist]]" takes place between these two episodes according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, this episode must also take place in 21 BBY.</ref> Bane was hired by [[Darth Sidious]] to [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|steal]] a [[holocron]] from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple's]] [[Holocron Vault]] on [[Coruscant]]. To carry out this job, Bane recruited the [[Clawdite]] [[Cato Parasitti]] to assist him. He also had his [[Techno-service droid]] [[Todo 360]] accompany him on the job. Though Parasitti was captured and Todo destroyed, Bane was able to escape with a holocron.<ref name="Holocron Heist" />

====Work for the Hutts====
Bane was then hired by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] to acquire the plans for the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]]. For this job, Bane recruited the [[assassin droid]] [[HELIOS-3E]] and his newly rebuilt droid, Todo 360, to kidnap Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s protocol droid, [[C-3PO]] and [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Knight|Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]]. With assistance from [[J0-N0]], Bane, Todo, and HELIOS-3E were able to retrieve the plans from the droids. Todo and HELIOS-3E then returned 3PO and R2 to resume their errands after their memories were wiped.<ref name="Evil Plans" />

[[File:CadBanesGroupComfortRidePassengerAirspeeder-HostageCrisis.png|thumb|right|242px|Bane's group nearing the Senate Building]]
Bane, at the behest of Jabba and the [[Grand Hutt Council|Hutt Council]],<ref name="Evil Plans" /> was then hired to free Jabba's uncle [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] from the [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center]]. To free Ziro, Bane assembled his group, including Sing, [[Robonino]], and [[Shahan Alama]], and went to the Senate building. Bane and his group ruthlessly eliminated several [[Senate Guard]]s at the landing platform before infiltrating the building, killing even more guards. Bane and his group then shut down power to the building and held several Senators hostage. After Chancellor Palpatine and Senator [[Orn Free Taa]] acceded to Bane's demand, [[HELIOS-3D]], another [[IG-86 sentinel droid]], was sent to retrieve Taa and take custody of Ziro. Despite difficulty from Skywalker, Bane and his group were able to leave the building without the [[Coruscant Guard]] stopping them.<ref name="Hostage Crisis" />

====Work for the Confederacy====
In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of "[[Prisoners]]" and "[[Crisis on Naboo]]" to [[20 BBY]]. As "[[Deception]]" takes place between those two episodes according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, the episode must also take place in 20 BBY.</ref> Bane was then hired by [[Count]] [[Dooku]], to free his agent [[Moralo Eval]] from the Detention Center. However, Bane and Eval were then joined by another bounty [[Rako Hardeen]], who was actually [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in disguise.<ref name="Deception" /> At first, Bane and Eval tried to get rid of Hardeen, to no avail. After their [[Showdown on Orondia|encounter]] with Skywalker and his apprentice Tano,<ref name="F&E">{{TCW|Friends and Enemies}}</ref> Bane, Eval, and Hardeen were then able to meet up with Dooku on [[Serenno]].<ref name="The Box">{{TCW|The Box}}</ref>

There, Bane and several other bounty hunters underwent a test in the [[Box]]. Despite several of the bounty hunters losing their lives, Bane was then chosen by Dooku to lead the group of survivors, including [[Rako Hardeen]], secretly [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in disguise, [[Embo]], [[Twazzi]], and [[Derrown]], in [[Plan to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine|kidnapping]] Chancellor Palpatine<ref name="The Box" /> during [[Naboo]]'s [[Festival of Light]] in [[Theed]]. However, the plan was foiled when Kenobi revealed himself and helped detain the group alongside fellow Jedi Skywalker and [[Mace Windu]].<ref name="Crisis on Naboo">{{TCW|Crisis on Naboo}}</ref>
