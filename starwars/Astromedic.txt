'''Astromedics''' were a model of [[medical droid]] that were [[astromech droid]]s reprogrammed to heal injuries with advanced [[bacta]]-based technologies. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Galactic Empire]] made use of black-painted astromedics on the battlefield.<ref name="SWC">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref>
