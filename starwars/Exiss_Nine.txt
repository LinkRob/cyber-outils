'''Exiss Nine''' was a terrestrial astronomical object.<ref name="Echoes of Fear 2" /> By around [[482 BBY]],<ref name="Flash Date">The present-day events of ''[[The High Republic Adventures: Quest of the Jedi]]'' take place between those of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' and ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'', both of which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]]. The flashbacks of ''Quest of the Jedi'' take place around a [[Standard century|century]] earlier and therefore around [[482 BBY]]. As the flashbacks in [[The High Republic Adventures – Echoes of Fear 2|''The High Republic Adventures – Echoes of Fear'' 2]] occur shortly after those in ''Quest of the Jedi'', they must also be set around that same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker|Wayfinder]] [[Tar'ak Borran]] defeated an [[army]] of [[Pirate|marauders]] on Exiss Nine by using a [[Force artifact]] known as an [[Echo Stone]].<ref name="Echoes of Fear 2" /> Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Flash Date" /> when the Jedi [[Barnabas Vim]] was looking for Borran on the [[planet]] [[Petris Major]], he spoke with an [[Unidentified capped individual|individual]] who told Vim that, according to stories they had heard, Borran had defeated the marauders on Exiss Nine without touching a single being. Vim later mentioned hearing the story of the marauders' defeat on Exiss Nine in a [[Hologram|recording]] he made for his [[Padawan]], [[Vix Fonnick]]&mdash;a message that was stored in a [[Barnabas Vim's holocron|holocron]] kept in the [[Jedi Archives]] on<ref name="Echoes of Fear 2" /> the planet<ref name="DB Coruscant">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Echoes of Fear 2" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Present Date">{{THRContent228BBY|EOF2}}</ref> the recording was viewed by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Reath Silas]] and Padawan [[Amadeo Azzazzo]].<ref name="Echoes of Fear 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Exiss Nine was mentioned in a flashback in the [[The High Republic Adventures – Echoes of Fear 2|second issue]] of the [[2024]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Echoes of Fear]]''. The issue was written by [[George Mann]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Echoes of Fear 2" /> on [[October 9]], 2024,<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3014-601/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures--Echoes-of-Fear-2|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures--Echoes of Fear #2}}</ref> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Echoes of Fear 2" />
