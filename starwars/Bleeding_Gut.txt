'''Bleeding Gut''' was a [[fungus]] from the<ref name="Fallen Order" /> [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="GalaxyMap">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Dathomir]]. The fungi consisted of a flat [[Color|red]] base with spiky stalks, with some varieties having extra stalks. Unlike other fungi on the planet, it did not reproduce through the use of spores, but rather their cells divided spontaneously. This gradually formed small pods which burst open when fully matured. [[Cal Kestis]] found a Bleeding Gut [[seed]] during his [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|mission to rebuild the Jedi Order]] and added it to [[Greez Dritus]]' [[terrarium]] aboard the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]''.<ref name="Fallen Order" />
