'''Anya''' was a [[Serennian]] [[Gender|female]], the lady of [[House Serenno]], and the [[Marriage|wife]] of [[Count]] [[Gora]] during the [[Republic Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. As consort to the [[Count of Serenno]], Anya held the title of Countess. She was the [[Parent|mother]] of [[Jenza]] and [[Ramil]], as well as the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Dooku]] who joined the [[Jedi Order]]. She would eventually [[Death|die]] and be buried at the [[Serenno Mausoleum|Serenno family mausoleum]] on the moon [[Mantero]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Biography==
===Motherhood and Marriage===
Sometime prior to [[102 BBY]], Anya was [[Marriage|married]] to [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Gora]] of [[Serenno]] and [[Reproduction|bore]] him a son, [[Ramil]]. In 102 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], she gave birth to a second son named [[Dooku]], however the boy soon showed signs of [[Force-sensitive|Force-sensitivity]] which frightened her husband. On Gora's orders, the infant Dooku was left to the elements on the edge of a forest, where he was found by a [[Jedi seeker]] and taken in by the [[Jedi Order]]. Anya would go on to have a third child, a daughter named [[Jenza]].<ref name=":0">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>

===Celebrations on Serenno===
In [[90 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Serenno played host to a festival held in the planetary capital of [[Carannia]], designed to advertise what [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planets could offer to the [[Galactic Republic]]. Anya attended the event with her husband and children, but Gora proved to be dismissive of the affair, cynically viewing it as a waste of time. Unhappy with her husband's attitude, Anya instructed the family's [[protocol droid]], [[D-4]], to look after Ramil and Jenza as they went off to explore the festival.<ref name=":0" />

However, unbeknownst to her and Gora, their other son, Dooku, was also present with members of the Jedi Order from [[Coruscant]]. Dooku ran into both Jenza and Ramil during the celebrations, striking up a connection with Jenza in particular that would last for much of the rest of his life. However, Anya did not get the chance to see her long-lost son before he was forced to leave the planet by his enraged father.<ref name=":0" />

===Death and Legacy===
Anya died suddenly in around [[82 BBY]]. On her passing, Jenza invited Dooku to her [[funeral]] on Serenno's moon of [[Mantero]]. Despite being forbidden by the [[Jedi Council]], Dooku travelled with [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Lene Kostana]] and his close friend, [[Sifo-Dyas]], to Mantero to attend the burial. His presence enraged Gora who verbally assaulted him, however the funeral was also marred by protests from [[Serennian|Serenno's citizens]] who were unhappy with Gora's rule over them and the inequality in their society. In his attempts to prevent a clash between Gora's [[security droid]]s and the protestors, Dooku's use of [[the Force]] not only scattered the crowds but disturbed Anya's body, which led to Gora physically attacking him. This altercation led to Dooku's relationship with Jenza becoming strained for a time.<ref name=":0" />

Later, when [[Mission to Asusto|experiencing]] the [[Dark side of the Force|Dark Side]] on the planet [[Asusto]], Dooku heard Anya's voice in his head as he struggled to cope with it. Ultimately, Anya's second son would go on to leave the Jedi Order and become [[Count of Serenno]] himself, killing Ramil in the process. Years after that, Dooku would also execute his other sibling after finally falling to the Dark Side and joining the [[Sith]].<ref name=":0" />

==Personality and traits==
Anya was unlike her husband in that she showed genuine care for their children. Nevertheless, she was content to leave practical responsibility for them in the hands of the family protocol droid. She did not share her husband's cynicism towards the celebrations held at Carannia and scolded Gora for displaying his attitude in front of Ramil and Jenza. She seems to have had little affection for Gora, pointedly noting his bad temper and rudeness. On her death, Jenza would remember her as a compassionate woman who worked to better the lives of Serenno's ordinary citizens.<ref name=":0" /> {{Char-stub}}
