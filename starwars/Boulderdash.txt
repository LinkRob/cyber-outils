The '''Boulderdash''' was a building-sized terrarium in which private parties were thrown. The [[Quenk jazz/Legends|Quenk jazz]] artist [[Mrp-Mrp Poo]] once organized one of her [[Standard year/Legends|annual]] [[Vector Day]] gala at the Boulderdash. The terrarium contained a dance floor and a space known as the "[[Monotony Room]]." Some of the Boulderdash's walls were covered with non-reflective mirrors.
