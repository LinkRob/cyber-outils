'''Daniel M. Lavery''' (born [[November 28]], [[1986]]) is the author of the short story "[[An Incident Report]]" from the [[2017]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''. Written from [[Admiral]] [[Conan Antonio Motti]]'s point of view, the story is similar in style to Lavery's articles on [[Wikipedia:The Toast (website)|''The Toast'']], a website which he co-founded.

==Biography==
{{Quote|I first got into the Expanded Universe [now Legends] novels when I was in fourth or fifth grade, and so I'd been waiting for an e-mail like the one Elizabeth sent me for roughly 19 years. My body was ready. I will write for ''Star Wars'' anytime, anywhere. I am all in.|Daniel M. Lavery|<ref name="StarWars.com" />}}
[[File:Admiral_Motti.png|thumb|right|270px|Lavery's story "An Incident Report" centered around Admiral Motti.]]
Daniel M. Lavery,<ref name="FACPOV" /> who was born on [[November 28]], [[1986]],<ref name="Thanksgiving" /> is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] author<ref name="FACPOV" /> who has been a ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan since his childhood, having read several works&mdash;such as ''[[Heir to the Empire]]'' and ''[[Tales from Jabba's Palace]]''&mdash;from the franchise.<ref name="StarWars.com" /> He wrote the short story "[[An Incident Report]]" in ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]'', an anthology of stories set during<ref name="FACPOV" /> the [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. Lavery's involvement in the project was revealed on [[StarWars.com]] on [[April 17]], [[2017]],<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-from-a-certain-point-of-view-anthology-book-celebrates-40-years-of-a-galaxy-far-far-away|text=''Star Wars: From a Certain Point of View'' Anthology Book Celebrates 40 Years of A Galaxy Far, Far Away}}</ref> and it was later published on [[October 3]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin-Hardcover">{{Penguin|url=books/7864/from-a-certain-point-of-view-star-wars-by-renee-ahdieh-meg-cabot-pierce-brown-nnedi-okorafor-sabaa-tahir-and-more/9780345511478|text=From a Certain Point of View (Star Wars)}}</ref>

The editor of the anthology, [[Elizabeth Schaefer]], created a list of her favorite authors with whom she wished to work<ref name="The Making of">{{SW|url=news/making-from-a-certain-point-of-view|text=The Making of ''From a Certain Point of View''}}</ref> and invited Lavery to contribute. In an interview with StarWars.com's [[Kristin Baver]], Lavery speculated that Schaefer was aware of his many tweets and articles on [[Wikipedia:The Toast (website)|''The Toast'']], a website which he co-founded, about the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] character [[Kyp Durron]] from the ''[[Star Wars: The Jedi Academy Trilogy]]''. From a list of side characters in ''A New Hope'', Lavery selected [[Admiral]] [[Conan Antonio Motti]] and wrote about Motti filing a workplace incident report after he was [[Force choke]]d by<ref name="StarWars.com" /> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Lavery said he loves "fussy bureaucrats" and sought to capture his own impulses toward self-pitying and petty grievances taken to [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] proportions by Motti. He further noted that he imagines that Motti [[Death|dies]] shortly after filing the report in the story, taking his dislike of Vader's "workplace proselytization" down with him. Baver and Lavery compared the story's style to his work on ''The Toast''.<ref name="StarWars.com" />
