'''Daniel José Older''' (born [[January 18]], [[1980]]) is the author of "[[Born in the Storm]]," a short story which appears in the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]'', celebrating the 40th anniversary of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']],<ref name="Older tweet">{{Twitter|djolder|status/908360010522152960|Daniel José Older|quote=Speaking of dewbacks (we weren't but you know): LOOK AT THIS GLORY!! I LIIIIVE!!! AIIIIII #FromACertainPOV http://randomhousebooks.com/FromACertainPOV|archivedate=20200813205232}}</ref> about [[Sardis Ramsin]], a [[stormtrooper]] riding a [[dewback]]. Older has loved dewbacks from a young age and expressed his love for the species on multiple occasions.<ref name="Dewback">{{Twitter|djolder|status/886262164561985536|Daniel José Older|quote=I was lowkey obessed with the dewbacks when I was a kid. In retrospect they were an early hint at the masterful worldbuilding at play.|archivedate=20200813205232}}</ref><ref name="NYCC">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2017-new-thrawn-novel-revealed-and-more-lucasfilm-publishing-panel|text=NYCC 2017: New Thrawn Novel Revealed and More from Lucasfilm's Publishing Panel}}</ref>

Older also wrote the ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' tie-in novel ''[[Last Shot]]'', which was released on [[April 17]], [[2018]]. Older also narrated part of [[Last Shot (audiobook)|the novel's audiobook]].

He worked on ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' with [[Cavan Scott]], [[Claudia Gray]], [[Charles Soule]], and [[Justina Ireland]].<ref name="Celebration">{{StarWarsShow|sP6SaKn2WuM|Star Wars Celebration Chicago 2019 Live Stream - Day 4 &#124; The Star Wars Show LIVE!}}</ref>
