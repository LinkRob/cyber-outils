The '''Chagrian courts''' was a court system utilized by the [[Chagrian]] [[Species|people]]. The courts were considered some of the most fair and balanced courts in [[the galaxy]]. All evidence was examined thoroughly and trials were usually brief since most Chagrians put on trial admitted their guilt<ref name="LBE" />
