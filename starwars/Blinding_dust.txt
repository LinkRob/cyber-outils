'''Blinding dust'''<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|groups|kagewarriors|Kage Warriors}}</ref> was a [[crystal]] compound used by the [[Kage Warriors]] of the [[planet]] [[Quarzite]] by the time of the [[Clone Wars]]. The dust blinded its target for a short period, leaving it vulnerable to attack. [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] was stunned by blinding dust during a job for [[Lord]] [[Otua Blank]] on Quarzite.<ref name="Bounty">{{TCW|Bounty (episode)}}</ref>
