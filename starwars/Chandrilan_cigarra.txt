'''Chandrilan cigarras''' were a type of [[cigarra]] that shared their name with<ref name="MA" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]].<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> They could be [[Fire|ignited]] and smoked for recreation by [[human]]s.<ref name="MA" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> while [[Mission to Pijal (Rael Averross)|serving]] as the [[lord]] [[regent]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] planet [[Pijal]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Rael Averross]] enjoyed smoking a [[Chandrilan]] cigarra as he spoke to the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] in the [[Pijali palace]]'s [[Throne room (Pijal)|throne room]].<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chandrilan cigarras appeared in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
