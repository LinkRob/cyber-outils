'''Carlac''' was a peaceful, [[Water|snow]]-covered [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and [[New Territories]]. A [[Ming Po Town|town]] inhabited by members of the [[Ming Po]] culture existed on the planet during the [[Clone Wars]]. However, that village was [[Battle on Carlac|burned]] to the ground by [[Death Watch]], a radical [[Mandalorian]] [[terrorist]] group.

==Description==
{{Quote|That's Carlac. Prepare for landing.|Yord Fandar|<ref name="Lost / Found" />}}
Carlac was a [[Water|snowy]] [[planet]]<ref name="A Friend in Need" /> in the [[Prefsbelt sector]]'s [[Carlac system]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> Though the planet lacked [[moon]]s, a loose [[asteroid]] ring orbited Carlac.<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> Its [[atmosphere]] was breathable to [[human]]s, and the planet had a cold,<ref name="A Friend in Need" /> frozen climate. The surface was covered in [[forest]]s,<ref name="A Friend in Need" /> [[mountain]]<ref name="Databank" /> chains in the north, and cold blue-sand [[desert]]s in the lower latitudes and on the equator. Molasse rocks found in the northern mountains were made from primeval marine deposits, and ancient thrust tectonics caused fold mountain outcroppings to form, with rocky arches formed from expanding frozen water and wind erosion. The mountains contained winding caves. Blue dust particles from the southern latitudes were swept into the northern regions to mix with mountain snow. The [[Piteous Ranges]] were a subpolar mountain chain devoid of even primitive [[lichen]]s.<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" />

==History==
===High Republic Era===
{{Quote|Prisoner mutiny. Transport went down over Carlac.|Vernestra Rwoh|<ref name="Lost / Found" />}}
Carlac was the site of a [[Engagement on Carlac|battle]] of [[Operation: Counterstrike]] during the [[Nihil conflict|conflict]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Nihil]]<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref> in [[231 BBY]].<ref name="Tempest Runner Date">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'' establishes the engagement on [[Carlac]] took place between the [[Valo Atrocity]] and the [[Battle of Galov]], which are both dated to [[231 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> A number of Nihil marauders were captured on Carlac and then questioned, and they revealed to the [[Jedi]] that the Nihil were divided into three [[Tempest]]s and led by [[Tempest Runner]]s, individuals who wielded a great deal of autonomy within the organization. The responses also made the Republic and Jedi more certain of the false belief that [[Lourna Dee]] was the [[Eye of the Nihil]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

[[File:CarlacSystem-LostFound.png|thumb|left|300px|The ''Palwick'' plummets towards Carlac]]
By [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Acolyte Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> Carlac had sparse habitations in its southern latitudes; a [[Yarkora]] [[Yarkora colony|colony]] had settled in the blue equatorial deserts, and [[Ming Po]] [[monk]]s were in the process of raising [[Monastery|monasteries]] to the north.<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> The [[DTE-CSA-17 Calaboose prison ship|Republic prison transport]] [[Palwick|''Palwick'']] crashed in Carlac's<ref name="Lost / Found" /> northern mountains<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> after its occupants deactivated the ship's [[Variable geometry droid|pilot droids]] and stole its [[escape pod]]s. The only remaining prisoner was [[Verosha Aniseya|Osha Aniseya]], a [[meknek]] and former [[Padawan]] accused of [[Indara's murder|murdering]] a [[Indara|Jedi Master]]. While on Carlac, she experienced a [[Force vision|vision]] of her [[twin]] sister, [[Mae-ho Aniseya|Mae]], who confessed that she was responsible for the Jedi's death. Osha shared this information when she was soon after [[Mission to Carlac|confronted]] on a rocky cliff by Jedi investigators.<ref name="Lost / Found" /> Their leader, Master [[Sol]], believed Aniseya's story and allowed her to freely come aboard their [[Polan-717 Jedi transport]].<ref name="Revenge / Justice">{{Acolyte|Revenge / Justice}}</ref>

===The Clone Wars===
[[File:CarlacMingPoVillage-AFIN.png|thumb|right|250px|The [[Ming Po Town]]]]
During the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the secessionist [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Carlac was home to a [[Carlac Death Watch camp|camp]] belonging to the [[Mandalorian]] [[terrorist]] faction [[Death Watch]].<ref name="A Friend in Need" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] traveled to Carlac with her friend [[Lux Bonteri]], who secretly planned to join Death Watch in an attempt to get revenge on [[Count]] [[Dooku]] for the death of his mother. Upon being taken to the Death Watch camp, Bonteri and Tano discovered [[Ming Po]] slaves working for Death Watch. A celebration was interrupted by [[Pieter]], [[Chief]]tain of the nearby [[Ming Po Town]], who demanded Death Watch return the women they had taken as slaves. [[Pre Vizsla]], leader of Death Watch, agreed.<ref name="A Friend in Need" />

[[File:WelcomeToDeathWatch-FIN.png|thumb|left|200px|Death Watch burns down the Ming Po settlement.]]
When Death Watch arrived at the town, Vizsla killed Pieter's granddaughter in front of him and ordered his soldiers to burn the village down. Tano revealed herself to be a Jedi, attempting to defend the village, but she was ultimately captured. Bonteri became aware of the terrorist nature of Death Watch when Tano's execution was ordered for her supposed crimes against [[Mandalore]]. The two were rescued by [[R2-D2]], however, and escaped aboard the [[Phoenix (GX1 short hauler)|''Phoenix'']].<ref name="A Friend in Need" />

===Galactic Civil War===
{{Quote|[Death Watch] then retreated to the ice planet Carlac and forced the Ming Po living there into their service.|Eloc Throno|<ref name="Database">{{GermanFanCite|106|Database}}</ref>}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]] following the rise of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Force spirit]] of the Jedi [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] told his apprentice [[Luke Skywalker]] the fate of Carlac, and many other planets, would rely on him confronting the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref>

Death Watch's occupation of Carlac was mentioned in "[[Mandalorians Through the Ages]]," a publication by the [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]].<ref name="Database" />
