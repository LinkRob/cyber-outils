'''Commander-in-Chief''' was the title held by a person who exercised supreme control over a military force, typically reserved for a [[Head of State]] of a [[Politics|government]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] was the [[Commander]]-in-Chief of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Databank Cody" /> The [[Kaleesh]] cyborg [[warlord]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] held the title of [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Martial Commander]] and, therefore, acted as commander-in-chief of the [[Separatist Droid Army|Separatist Droid Armies]] during the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars.<ref name="Helmet Collection" />

During the [[Imperial Era]] within the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], it was [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] who held that title.<ref name="UVG" /> Recognized as a military leader<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> even before being ultimately elevated to the position of [[supreme commander of the Imperial fleet|Supreme Commander of the Imperial Fleet]],<ref name="rafga">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>the [[Sith Lord|Sith]] warlord [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] acted as the ''de facto'' Commander-in-Chief of the [[Imperial Military|Imperial Armed Forces]]<ref name="Bust Collection" /> due to being empowered by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] Palpatine.<ref name="Dark Lord 12">[[Darth Vader (2017) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 12]]</ref>
