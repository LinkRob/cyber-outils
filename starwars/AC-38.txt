'''AC-38''', or "'''Ace'''," was an [[AC-series pilot droid]] who had its [[Starspeeder 1000|StarSpeeder]] hijacked by a rogue [[protocol droid]] and taken on a dangerous trip across [[the galaxy]]. Because of the incident, all AC-series units were retired from service and replaced by [[RX-series pilot droid|RX-series]] droids, which proved to be only slightly more reliable. Rumors indicated that the tour company for which Ace worked had him demoted to janitorial duties.<ref name="Droidography" />
