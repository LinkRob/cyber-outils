'''A Taste of Takodana''' was a [[Foodstuff|meal]] for [[youngling]]s available at [[Tuggs' Grub]] when the mobile [[restaurant]] was docked at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The dish was suitable for [[human]] [[Diet|consumption]].<ref name="Docking Bay 7" />

[[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] previously worked for [[Maz Kanata]],<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref> who ran a [[Cantina|tavern]] within her [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]], until its [[Battle of Takodana|destruction]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Tuggs' reputation in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] had led to many beings wanting what Tuggs called "a taste of Takodana" in his [[Book|cookbook]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''. He began operating Tuggs' Grub out of a [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|modified transport]] and wrote of it, "It's given me a chance to share not just a taste of Takodana but an entire galaxy of flavors."<ref name="Cookbook" /> "A Taste of Takodana" became the slogan of Tuggs' Grub,<ref name="GEAttr">{{GEAttr|BSO 401.72}}</ref> and the dish of that name was served when he was the visiting chef at Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" />

==Behind the scenes==
A Taste of Takodana is a children's meal served at the [[In-universe|real-world]] Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]].<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> The menu's description reads, "Black Bean Hummus, Edible Soil with Nuts, Chilled Dipping Vegetables, and Multigrain Crisps with a side of 'Ranch' dressing served with choice of Small Lowfat Milk or Small DASANI&reg; Water. Meets [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] Nutrition Guidelines for Complete Meals without substitutions."<ref name="Menu 1">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref> Since its introduction, it has been renamed multiple times to "Takodana Veggie Dippers Youngling Meal" and to "Takodana Veggie Dippers."<ref name="Menu 2">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch Menu}}</ref>
