'''"A New Journey Begins"''' is a TV spot for [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu|''The Mandalorian and Grogu'']] that aired during [[Wikipedia:Super Bowl LX|Super Bowl LX]] on [[February 8]], [[2026]]. Directed by  [[Jon Favreau]], the commercial features the titular characters of the film at the reins of a [[wagon]] pulled by [[tauntaun]]s. Designed to invoke familiar "Big Game" advertisements, the spot received mixed reactions.

==Plot summary==
On [[Hoth]],<ref name="ILM">{{ILM|url=ilm-super-bowl-commercials-mandalorian-grogu-jurassic-park|text=ILM Contributes to 2 Super Bowl Commercials – ‘The Mandalorian and Grogu’ & ‘Jurassic Park’}}</ref> four [[tauntaun]]s pull a [[wagon]] through a valley. The [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] controls the [[Species|creatures]] until his ward, [[Din Grogu]], uses [[the Force]] to take the reins and urge the beasts on. Later, Grogu shares a moment with one of the tauntauns, placing his hand on its snout.

==Development==
"A New Journey Begins" was created in partnership between [[Lucasfilm]] and [[Industrial Light & Magic]] to promote the [[2026]] film [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu|''The Mandalorian and Grogu'']]. [[Jon Favreau]], the director of the film as well as the creator of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', also served as the director of the commercial. According to Lucasfilm's marketing brand lead [[Ryan Stankevich]], the spot was intended to allude to previous "Big Game" ads for various brands.<ref name="Hollywood Reporter" /> Voiceover for the spot was provided by actor [[Sam Elliott]].<ref name="Deadline" />

==Release and reception==
The commercial aired on [[February 8]], 2026, during [[Wikipedia:Super Bowl LX|Super Bowl LX]], the [[Wikipedia:National Football League|National Football League]] championship game between the [[Wikipedia:Seattle Seahawks|Seattle Seahawks]] and the [[Wikipedia:New England Patriots|New England Patriots]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/the-mandalorian-and-grogu-big-game-spot|text=The Mandalorian and Grogu Debuts a Big Game Spot}}</ref> Reviewers pointed out that "A New Journey Begins" featured stylistic similarities to ads produced by companies such as [[Wikipedia:Ford Motor Company|Ford]] and [[Wikipedia:Budweiser|Budweiser]].<ref name="Hollywood Reporter" /><ref name="SlashFilm">{{WebCite|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/2096885/disney-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-super-bowl-trailer-explained|text=Disney's Bizarre Mandalorian And Grogu Super Bowl Trailer, Explained|author=Ryan Scott|date=2026-02-09|work=[[Wikipedia:/Film|/Film]]|archivedate=20260209205435}}</ref> Aimee Hart of [[Wikipedia:Polygon (website)|''Polygon'']] felt that the spot failed to properly promote ''The Mandalorian and Grogu'', stating that the commercial "could easily be mistaken for a beer or car advertisement, and not a particularly good one."<ref name="Polygon">{{WebCite|url=https://www.polygon.com/star-wars-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-superbowl-trailer-disappointment|text=The Mandalorian and Grogu's Super Bowl trailer is one huge disappointment|author=Aimee Hart|date=2026-02-09|work=[[Wikipedia:Polygon (website)|Polygon]]|archivedate=20260209184841}}</ref> [[Wikipedia:/Film|Slashfilm]]'s Ryan Scott referred to the ad as "bizarre" while noting that it was unlikely to dissuade audiences from seeing the film.<ref name="SlashFilm" />
