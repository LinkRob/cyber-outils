'''Custodian of the Paths''' was a title within the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization held by the [[Eye of the Nihil]]. The title referred to the secret [[hyperspace]] [[Path]]s the Nihil had access to, and the Custodian's duties were to safeguard and dole out information on the Paths. [[Asgar Ro]] and his son [[Marchion Ro|Marchion]], who successively held the title of Eye of the Nihil, were also Custodians of the Paths.<ref name="LotJ" />
