{{Quote|Do you know why we are here to kill that Pyke? He ordered the ''bombing'' of one of our fight schools. Students. ''While they slept.'' Is that honor?|translated=1|A Kanji assassin, to Tasu Leech, on Omel Sampak|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" />}}
Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Omel Sampak]]&mdash;a [[gangster]] in the [[Pyke Syndicate]] criminal organization&mdash;ordered the '''bombing''' of a [[Nar Kanji fight school|fight school]] on<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]]<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Nar Kanji]]. The school was used by a rival of the Pykes&mdash;a group of [[assassin]]s<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" /> known as [[Kanjiklub]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27 solicit" />&mdash;and its [[bomb]]ing resulted in the [[death]]s of a number of students as they [[Sleep|slept]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" />

To celebrate his victory, Sampak hired out the [[Accretion Disco]] [[space station]] and knowing that Kanjlikub would seek revenge for his attack, the [[Pyke]] hired the [[T'onga's crew|crew]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] to keep him and his entourage alive. The assassins later [[Havoc at the Accretion Disco|attempted]] to kill the gangster on the station, though their efforts were thwarted by T'onga and her crew. After being informed of the reality of the bombing by [[Unidentified Kanji assassin|one]] of the assassins, [[Tasu Leech]]&mdash;a member of T'onga's crew and a [[Homeworld|native]] of Nar Kanji&mdash;killed Sampak himself.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bombing of a Nar Kanji fight school was mentioned in the [[Bounty Hunters 27|twenty-seventh issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" /> on [[September 14]], [[2021]]. Prior to its release, the bombing was mentioned in a preview of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[September 7]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-bounty-hunters-27-exclusive-preview|text=An Uneasy Alliance in Marvel's ''Star Wars: Bounty Hunters'' #27 &ndash; Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
