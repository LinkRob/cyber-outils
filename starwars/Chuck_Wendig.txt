'''Chuck Wendig''' is a novelist who wrote the three novels of the [[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy|''Aftermath Trilogy'']] ([[2015]]'s [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], [[2016]]'s ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' and [[2017]]'s ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''),<ref name="NYCC">{{SW|url=news/journey-to-the-force-awakens-panel-at-new-york-comic-con-recap|text=Journey to ''The Force Awakens'' Panel at New York Comic Con – Recap}}</ref> a [[canon]] series of books set after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]'' ''<ref name="After Endor" /> as well as the short story "[[We Don't Serve Their Kind Here]]" focusing on the character of Wuher from A New Hope. He also wrote the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Adaptation|comic adaptation]] of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]<ref name="TFA comic">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cbr.com/exclusive-marvel-announces-star-wars-the-force-awakens-adaptation|text=EXCLUSIVE: Marvel Announces "Star Wars: The Force Awakens" Adaptation|author=White, Brett|date=2016-02-19|work=Comic Book Resources|archivedate=20190909122857}}</ref> and has written at least one comic focusing around the character of Vader.

He previously wrote the non-''[[Star Wars]]'' novels ''Blackbird'' and ''The Blue Blazes'', amongst many others.<ref name="Chaos">{{SW|url=news/chaos-throttles-the-capital-in-star-wars-aftermath-special-excerpt|text=Chaos Throttles the Capital in ''Star Wars: Aftermath'' &ndash; Special Excerpt!}}</ref>

==Biography==
Chuck Wendig was hired to write ''Aftermath'' after posting a tweet saying he wanted to write for ''Star Wars''. In the tweet, on [[September 4]], [[2014]], Wendig wrote: "Dear Internet: I wanna write a licensed STAR WARS novel. Please make this happen. *squeezes Internet hard enough to make wishes fall out*"<ref name="dearinternet">{{Twitter|ChuckWendig|status/507677975064887296|Chuck Wendig|quote=Dear Internet: I wanna write a licensed STAR WARS novel. Please make this happen. *squeezes Internet hard enough to make wishes fall out*|archivefile=File:Chuck Wendig Dear Internet Tweet.png}}</ref> The request was successful, and ''Aftermath'' was released exactly one year after the tweet, on [[September 4]], [[2015]].<ref name="Chaos" />

He was originally scheduled to write the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Shadow of Vader]]'' along with an as-yet-unnanounced book,<ref name="Killed Projects">{{Twitter|ChuckWendig|status/1050822080130895878|Chuck Wendig|quote=So, here's a thing that has happened – I just got fired from Marvel. Taken off issues 4 and 5 of SHADOW OF VADER, and taken off an as-yet-unannounced SW book. This might be a long thread, so apologies in advance.|archivedate=20200803152944}}</ref> but the series was canceled following his dismissal from Marvel Comics.<ref name="Ultimate Fate of Shadow?">{{Twitter|ChuckWendig|status/1052526172393926657|Chuck Wendig|quote=Also possible they are looking for a brand new writer on the first three issues OR they just said fuck it, and canceled it quietly. Obviously, ha ha, they're not gonna keep me on the loop on that one.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/ELZdu}}</ref> In [[2023]], Wendig affirmed that he was not allowed to "touch ''Star Wars'' anymore in any direction."<ref name="NotAllowed">{{Bluesky|chuckwendig.bsky.social|post/3kes5zozjxm2v|[[Chuck Wendig]]|date=2023-11-22|quote=Yeah they don't let me touch Star Wars anymore in any direction — and generally, I'm okay with that, if kinda sad. That said, continuing the AFTERMATH characters would be about the only thing that could bring me back because I love them so.|archivefile=File:ChuckWendig-NotAllowed-Bluesky.png}}</ref>
