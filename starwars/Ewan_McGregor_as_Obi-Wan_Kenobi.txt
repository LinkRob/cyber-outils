"'''Ewan McGregor as Obi-Wan Kenobi'''" is a collection of quotes from [[Ewan McGregor]] published in [[Star Wars Insider 112|''Star Wars Insider'' 112]]. The quotes pertain to McGregor's experience filming ''[[Star Wars]]'' and his portrayal of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in terms of his inspiration from [[Alec Guinness]].
