'''Fabarian''' was a [[human]] [[Agent]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Security Bureau]]. The foremost investigator of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]'s [[sector 35]], he believed in his career and disapproved of the corruption within the Empire, instead desiring order. When the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Scourge of Coruscant|released]] the [[Scourge]] intelligence on Coruscant, Fabarian defended the people of sector 35 from the infected [[droid]]s until [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] [[Protocol 23 (Coruscant)|ordered]] the destruction of the sector, [[Death|killing]] numerous people in the process. Fabarian defied orders to investigate the events, but was charged for doing so and was sent to the [[ISB Office of Internal Review]], where [[Sub-Administrator]] [[Sly Moore]] recruited the agent into the [[Schism Imperial]], a faction intending to restore order to the Empire by overthrowing the Emperor. Accepting his demotion to [[Assistant Data Processor]], Fabarian awaited orders from the Schism. The Schism Imperial eventually came to work for Vader himself, although Fabarian disliked having to serve the [[Sith Lord]].

Fabarian used his position to investigate [[Baron]] [[Slandarv]], who the Schism suspected was developing a [[Slandarv's battle droid model|new battle droid]]. Fabarian obtained data to confirm the suspicions, subsequently accompanying forces of the Schism, including Vader, to Slandarv's [[Slandarv's factory|factory]] on [[Slandarvia M-9]] to claim the [[battle droid]]s for the Sith Lord. Slandarv intended to destroy the facility, unfazed by the resultant civilian casualties, but Fabarian shot and killed him, although later regretted the action as having been influenced by his service to Vader. On the planet [[Tython]], Fabarian assisted in acquiring [[kyberite]] and clashed with [[Senior Lieutenant]] [[Enric Pryde]], who served the Schism out of loyalty to Vader. Fabarian then accompanied the Schism members in a kyberite-plated [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class shuttle]] to the world of [[Exegol]], where Vader launched an [[Assault on Exegol|assault]] on the [[Sith Eternal]] forces of the Emperor.

==Biography==
===The Scourge and the Schism===
====No good deed goes unpunished====
{{Quote|I&hellip;I was working sector 35 the day of the ''droid plague.'' There&hellip;there were ''kids.'' I fought off the first onslaught of droids. But then the entire block exploded.''"<br />"''And you believe I was responsible.''"<br />"''N-''no.'' I found out later the destruction was ordered by ''Mas Amedda.'' Protocol 23. I know the ''Emperor'' gave the command.|Fabarian and Darth Vader|<ref name="Vader 44" />}}
The [[human]] Fabarian served as an investigator in the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] during its [[Imperial Era|reign]].<ref name="Vader 44" /> Holding the rank of [[Agent]],<ref name="Vader 43" /> he operated within [[sector 35]] of the<ref name="Vader 44" /> [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. According to [[Baron]] [[Slandarv]], Fabarian was the most prominent ISB investigator of sector 35.<ref name="Vader 44" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Darth Vader (2020) 40|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 40]] are set between the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Fabarian was present in sector 35<ref name="Vader 44" /> when the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Scourge of Coruscant|released]] [[Spider-droid|disk droids]] carrying the [[Scourge]] intelligence onto Coruscant to test his [[Sith Master|master]], [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. As the Scourge infected more [[droid]]s and began massacring the population,<ref name="Vader 40">[[Darth Vader (2020) 40|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 40]]</ref> Fabarian attempted to hold back the onslaught of droids while the people of sector 35, including [[Youngling|children]], fled to safety.<ref name="Vader 44" />

[[File:FabarianDismay-2020DarthVader44.png|thumb|left|270px|Fabarian looks on at the destruction of sector 35.]]
The ISB investigator requested backup, but was informed of the imminent enaction of [[Protocol 23 (Coruscant)|protocol 23]]<ref name="Vader 44" />&mdash;ordered by [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] under the command of the Emperor<ref name="Vader 40" />&mdash;and instructed to evacuate. Before Fabarian could protest, sectors 35<ref name="Vader 44" /> and [[Sector 36|36]] were destroyed to contain the Scourge incursion,<ref name="Vader 40" /> and the investigator was thrown to the ground by the explosion. Distraught upon seeing the destruction and the bodies of [[Death|dead]] civilians, Fabarian called out for help, only to spot Vader in the distance<ref name="Vader 44" /> atop the [[Imperial Palace]].<ref name="Vader 40" />

Fabarian launched an independent investigation into the destruction of the two sectors, despite having been ordered to report to a new assignment on<ref name="Vader 43" /> the planet<ref name="Vader 28">[[Darth Vader (2020) 28|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 28]]</ref> [[Skako Minor]]. He discovered that the official reports of the incident did not mention Vader's presence on the scene,<ref name="Vader 43" /> also learning that protocol 23 had been ordered by Amedda at the Emperor's command. Despite knowing Vader himself was not responsible for the order, Fabarian heard that the [[Sith]] had been infected by the entity controlling the droids.<ref name="Vader 44" /> The [[Imperial officer]] was charged for his actions and brought in [[stuncuffs]] to the [[ISB Office of Internal Review]] to be sentenced.<ref name="Vader 43" />

====Standing against chaos====
{{Quote|Everyone here's talking about ''corruption'' and ''chaos'' within the Empire! And you're just going to ''ignore'' that?|Fabarian, to Sly Moore|<ref name="Vader 43" />}}
At the office, Fabarian and several other charged Imperials&mdash;[[Admiral]] [[Corleque]], [[Governor]] [[Tauntaza]], and Council Member [[Xanto]]&mdash;were delivered by an [[Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (ISB Office of Internal Review)|RA-7 protocol droid]] to speak with [[Sub-Administrator]] [[Sly Moore]]. Moore reviewed their charges, with Fabarian stating his belief of Vader's involvement. Afterwards, Fabarian protested that Moore was ignoring the corruption within the Empire that he and the others had spoken of, and the Sub-Administrator asked whether he felt unjustly treated and wished to appeal to the Emperor. Xanto was the only one to accept the offer of a personal meeting with the Emperor, with Moore [[Telepathy|detecting]] the thoughts of Fabarian and the others&mdash;a lack of faith, believing that there was no appeal and no justice to be had in the Empire.<ref name="Vader 43" />

[[File:WelcomeToTheSchism-2020DarthVader43.png|thumb|right|350px|Fabarian is welcomed to the Schism Imperial alongside Corleque and Tauntaza.]]
However, Moore instead executed Xanto and addressed Fabarian and the others, telling them that they knew the Emperor's nature and saw the Empire for what it truly was. Moore released their cuffs, welcoming Fabarian, Corleque, and Tauntaza to the [[Schism Imperial]], a faction of Imperials who were dissatisfied with the Emperor and wished to overthrow him to restore order. Under Moore's instructions, Fabarian accepted his penalty and demotion<ref name="Vader 43" /> to the rank of [[Assistant Data Processor]],<ref name="Vader 44" /> quietly waiting until the Schism Imperial required his service.<ref name="Vader 43" /> In his new role, Fabarian processed fines and other records under the orders of a [[Unidentified Imperial supervisor|supervisor]]. Eventually, Vader himself aligned with the Schism Imperial, whose members were also made to serve the [[Sith Lord]].<ref name="Vader 44" />

===Barons and battle droids===
====Investigation through irritation====
{{Quote|Fabarian. I read up on you. Once sector 35's greatest ''ISB investigator,'' now just a ''fine processor&hellip;''because you ''poked around'' in the ''Emperor's'' business. In ''Lord Vader's'' business.''"<br />"''I was just doing my job. A lot of people died. I was supposed to find out who was ''responsible&mdash;"<br />"''Did I ask you to speak? You haven't learned a thing, have you? That's right. ''Know'' your ''place.'' Look down and say ''nothing.'' Because you ''are'' nothing, Assistant Data Processor. In fact, you're not even an ''Assistant Data Processor'' any longer.|Slandarv and Fabarian|<ref name="Vader 44" />}}
A [[Standard month|month]] after his induction into the Schism, Fabarian's [[supervisor]] instructed him to delete a traffic fine for<ref name="Vader 43" /> the [[Class A speeder|speeder]] of Baron Slandarv.<ref name="Vader 44" /> Fabarian was hesitant, but his supervisor demanded it to avoid the backlash they would face for fining a baron.<ref name="Vader 43" /> However, Slandarv was a person of interest to [[Administrator]] Moore, Vader, and the Schism, who suspected that he was developing a new and advanced [[Slandarv's battle droid model|model]] of [[battle droid]]. As such, Fabarian deleted four tickets for Slandarv but let one remain approved, which, as planned, irritated the baron. While on break later that [[Standard day|day]], Fabarian visited the under-repair sector 35 and viewed a memorial. He was then contacted by his supervisor, who informed the processor that Slandarv had summoned him to his private offices.<ref name="Vader 44" />

[[File:FabarianDemoted-2020DarthVader44.png|thumb|left|310px|Fabarian was demoted by Slandarv, but succeeded in acquiring the baron's data.]]
Visiting Slandarv's office, Fabarian carried a [[comlink]] in which Moore had fitted an [[autoslicer]] in the hope of discreetly copying the baron's data. Baron Slandarv greeted Fabarian by informing the former ISB agent that he knew of his history and his demotion as a result of prying in the business of the Emperor and Vader. Fabarian attempted to explain, but the baron reprimanded him for speaking and disparaged him as being insignificant. Slandarv ordered that Fabarian be discharged from Imperial service, despite the latter's protest, and two [[RA-7 protocol droid]]s removed his uniform's [[Imperial crest|insignia]] and requested he hand over his [[Datapad|tablet]] and comlink. Fabarian argued that he had [[Galactic Credit Standard|paid]] for the comlink himself, and Slandarv mockingly let him keep it before having him thrown out of the office. Outside, Fabarian's supervisor scolded him and told him that his [[time]] in the sector was done, recommending that he leave the planet.<ref name="Vader 44" />

====Enforcing the fine====
{{Quote|What the hell, Fabarian?! You&hellip;you think you can stay ''pure'' in ''this'' galaxy?''"<br />"''Probably not. But you should have just ''paid'' that ''ticket.''|Slandarv and Fabarian, before the latter kills the former|<ref name="Vader 44" />}}
Fabarian departed Coruscant, taking a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] to meet with a [[starship]] carrying Moore and Vader. Moore sensed Fabarian's distress and ordered him not to hide it from Vader, and the former ISB agent explained his experience during the destruction of sector 35, stating that although he did not blame Vader, he was concerned that the Sith had been infected by the Scourge. Vader, however, declared that he had merely been testing the entity and was not under its influence. Fabarian subsequently recognized the [[M.A.R. Corps]], a group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[cyborg]]s who had been captured and controlled by Vader, and greeted Tauntaza, who questioned how he would increase Vader's power. Although Fabarian expressed confusion over serving both the Schism and Vader, he insisted that he had been useful, revealing the data that the comlink's autoslicer had copied from Slandarv's office.<ref name="Vader 44" />

As the Schism forces arrived on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] astronomical object [[Slandarvia M-9]], where Slandarv's [[Slandarv's factory|factory]] was situated, Fabarian explained that the baron had been working on the advanced battle droid model in secret to maximize bargaining power when selling it. As the [[factory]] was surrounded by worker housing that was home to hundreds of civilians, Fabarian recommended a [[blockade]] and parley, believing Slandarv would agree to a deal; however, Vader refused, instead launching an [[Mission to Slandarvia M-9|attack]] to breach the factory alongside the M.A.R. Corps. Entering behind with the droid [[ZED-6-7]], Fabarian led the way to a shocked Baron Slandarv. Fabarian aimed a [[blaster]] at Slandarv as ZED-6-7 requested that he authorize a reprogram of his droids, and the baron refused until he learned that they were working under Vader. As ZED-6-7 reprogrammed the droids to serve Vader instead of the Emperor, Slandarv, who was loyal to the latter, refused to involve himself. ZED-6-7 declared that the baron was not invited, and Slandarv fled while his guards attempted to hold off Fabarian.<ref name="Vader 44" />

[[File:ShouldvePaidTheTicket-2020DarthVader44.png|thumb|right|370px|Fabarian shoots and kills Baron Slandarv.]]
Shooting the guards dead, Fabarian pursued Slandarv through the facility, ordering him to surrender. However, Slandarv revealed that he could destroy the entire factory save for the chamber he and Fabarian were standing in, killing everyone but them in the process. Slandarv declared that Vader could not beat the Emperor and nothing would be gained even if he did, and Fabarian questioned whether the civilian housing would also be destroyed. When the baron claimed the civilians were a necessary sacrifice, Fabarian shot him in the chest. Injured, Slandarv criticized Fabarian for trying to keep a conscience, but the latter merely told the baron he should have paid his ticket before shooting him again, killing him. Moore entered, commenting on Fabarian's motive for killing Slandarv, but the administrator&mdash;along with Vader, both being [[Force-sensitive]]&mdash;were struck by an episode of disorentiation<ref name="Vader 44" /> as a result of the [[Force Wave]].<ref name="Vader 40" /> After the Schism forces departed and Moore awoke, stating that she had felt the fear and anger of the M.A.R. Corps' trapped consciousnesses while unconscious, Fabarian entered with Tauntaza, blaming the administrator for involving the Schism with Vader.<ref name="Vader 44" />

===Expedition to Exegol===
====The confessions of Tython====
{{Quote|He was going to ''blow up'' the facility and kill all of his ''workers'' and their ''families."<br />"''So you had to stop him.''"<br />"Stop.'' Not ''kill&hellip;''but in the service of ''Lord Vader,'' there was ''nothing'' and ''no one'' telling me not to ''rub'' that smug piece of garbage out on the spot&hellip; What kind of order is that?|Fabarian reflects on his murder of Slandarv to Enric Pryde|<ref name="Vader 45">[[Darth Vader (2020) 45|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 45]]</ref>}}
Fabarian subsequently traveled with Corleque, Tauntaza, and [[Senior Lieutenant]] [[Enric Pryde]] in a ''Lambda''-class shuttle to the [[Deep Core]]<ref name="Vader 45" /> planet<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Tython]], intending to rendezvous with Vader in the [[Meridional Ice Cap]]. However, their [[shuttle]] crashed due to a storm, forcing them to ascend a [[mountain]] to their destination on foot. When Vader flew overhead in his [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|TIE Advanced]], unaffected by the storm, Fabarian expressed his distaste for Pryde's admiration of the Sith Lord. Pryde asserted his loyalty to Vader, leading Fabarian and the others to note their hatred for the one they now served. Fabarian explained his past as an ISB officer, noting that his recent actions, including the raid on the droid factory and his murder of Slandarv&mdash;the latter of which he recognized as having been influenced by his service to Vader&mdash;were not the order that the Schism desired.<ref name="Vader 45" />

Soon afterward, Fabarian and the others met Vader, Moore, and ZED-6-7 outside the [[Martyrium of Frozen Tears]], part of an ancient [[Jedi Temple (Tython)|Jedi temple]] that contained a [[confessional]] made of solid [[kyberite]], which Vader intended for the group to [[Mining|mine]]. After Pryde became fearful when Vader ordered him to lead the group into the Martyrium, Fabarian mocked the former's previous words. As the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] had previously used the site as a base, Pryde scouted the area and destroyed a trap left by the rebels. However, a droid activated the Martyrium, leading the Force-sensitive Moore and Vader to be presented with a [[Force vision|vision]] of the confessions of ancient [[Jedi]]. Fabarian was saddened when Moore described a confession from a Jedi whose students perished in a [[fire]] and was haunted by their faces. Vader, after witnessing a confession from<ref name="Vader 45" /> his [[Skywalker family|son]],<ref name="Vader 46">[[Darth Vader (2020) 46|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 46]]</ref> [[Luke Skywalker]], ordered the Schism forces to acquire as much kyberite as possible before destroying the site.<ref name="Vader 45" />

====Ultimate power====
{{Quote|We're shielded with ''kyberite.'' That should spare us from the ''worst'' of the ''psychic attacks.'' We just need to ''outrun'' them.''"<br />"''No.''"<br />"No?'' That thing's as big as a ''city!'' We can't&mdash;''"<br />"''Do you ''still'' not see what we have ''done?'' What the ''Schism Imperial'' has done? ''[&hellip;] We will never run again.''|Fabarian and Sly Moore|<ref name="Vader 47">[[Darth Vader (2020) 47|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 47]]</ref>}}
After carrying out Vader's demands, the Imperials were ordered to shield a shuttle with the kyberite and rendezvous with the Sith Lord at the edge of<ref name="Vader 45" /> the [[Red Honeycomb Zone]]<ref name="Vader 46" />&mdash;a passage through [[Realspace|space]] that led to [[Exegol]], an ancient Sith world in the [[Unknown Regions]] where the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Religion|cult]]<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and forces of the Emperor resided.<ref name="Vader 46" /> Although Vader did not initially divulge his plans,<ref name="Vader 45" /> he intended to launch an [[Assault on Exegol|assault]] on Exegol in order to loot the world and challenge the Emperor's power.<ref name="Vader 46" /> Once the Sith Lord departed Tython in his [[TIE Advanced x1|TIE Advanced]], Fabarian confronted Pryde as to whether the latter expected any recognition for his service to Vader, while Tauntaza suggested that Pryde flee the conspiracy while he had the chance; Pryde, however, reaffirmed his choice and his loyalty to Vader.<ref name="Vader 45" />

While Corleque met Sith Eternal cultists outside the Red Honeycomb Zone with a shipment of supplies and kyberite for Exegol that secretly held battle droids and Vader himself, the Schism forces shielded a Lambda shuttle with kyberite plating.<ref name="Vader 46" /> Fabarian accompanied his fellow Schism members<ref name="Vader 47" /> aboard the kyberite-shielded Lambda shuttle, carrying a droid decoy of Vader. The shuttle traversed the Red Honeycomb Zone, with the plating partially protecting them from the psychic effects of the [[Unidentified summa-verminoth subspecies|summa-verminoth subspecies]] that lived within the area. After the shuttle landed outside the [[Sith Citadel]] on Exegol, the Sith Eternal [[Sith cultist|cultists]] swiftly defeated Vader's decoy, but the Sith Lord himself emerged, claiming a large [[kyber crystal]] from the [[Kyber Temple]]; however, the cultists summoned<ref name="Vader 46" /> multiple [[summa-verminoth]] to assist them.<ref name="Vader 47" />

As Vader and Pryde returned to the shuttle with the kyber, Fabarian approached with Tauntaza and ZED-6-7, wishing to load the [[crystal]] on board before the remaining cultists attacked. Aboard the shuttle, Tauntaza expressed concern over the summa-verminoth, but Fabarian assured her that they were protected by the shielding and only needed to outrun the creatures. Although Fabarian doubted that they could take on the summa-verminoth, Moore refused to run, citing the Schism Imperial's recent accomplishments that had empowered Vader. As Fabarian and the others watched, the Sith Lord used portions of the kyber to strengthen his [[Zaly Shield|shield]] and [[Darth Vader's electrostaff|electrostaff]]. Pryde piloted the Lambda shuttle toward the summa-verminoth, crying out from the creatures' effect; Fabarian questioned whether he was alright, but Pryde remained determined. Vader, standing atop the shuttle, used his empowered weaponry to defeat the summa-verminoth.<ref name="Vader 47" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I was an ''ISB officer,'' Pryde. And I ''believed'' in my job. Enforcing ''rules.'' Because ''without'' them, people can&hellip;no, people ''will'' do the worst things they can imagine.|Fabarian, to Enric Pryde|<ref name="Vader 45" />}}
[[File:Fabarian-2020DarthVader43.png|thumb|left|270px|Fabarian was opposed to the corruption and chaos within the Galactic Empire.]]
As an officer of the Galactic Empire, Fabarian sought to maintain order and protect the people of his sector, but had difficulty with grayer moralities.<ref name="Vader 44" /> Fabarian nevertheless believed in his career with the ISB, feeling that people would turn to the worst acts imaginable if rules were not enforced.<ref name="Vader 45" /> Though he understood it was challenging to keep a pure conscience,<ref name="Vader 44" /> he was outraged at the corruption and chaos that was present in the Empire, knowing that as a result of the regime's nature, justice and order were not permitted for most. Like the other members of the Schism Imperial, Fabarian had little faith in the Emperor<ref name="Vader 43" /> and believed that order could be restored if he was overthrown. Fabarian felt that he had to determine who had been responsible for the destruction of sector 35 and the deaths of many people, including children.<ref name="Vader 44" /> He was unafraid to defy orders and investigate the matter independently, even when it led to his demotion.<ref name="Vader 43" />

Fabarian hated Darth Vader, regarding the Sith Lord and his actions to be evil.<ref name="Vader 45" /> He was disturbed by serving the Sith Lord and questioned whether Vader's approach would restore order.<ref name="Vader 44" /> Fabarian was not proud of his actions while working for Vader, particularly his murder of Slandarv. Despite disliking Slandarv and knowing he had to be stopped, Fabarian felt that there had been nothing to prevent him from following through with the urge to execute him as a result of his own service to Vader. Fabarian clashed with Enric Pryde during their collaboration; he was irritated by the latter's admiration of Vader, while Pryde saw Fabarian as being soft on the Empire's enemies.<ref name="Vader 45" />

Fabarian was protective of the lives of civilians, not wishing to endanger innocents on Slandarvia M-9 and shooting Slandarv when the baron planned to kill them all to destroy his factory. He had little sympathy for Moore when she was pained by the suffering of the M.A.R. Corps at Vader's hands, as it had been her her who had pulled the Schism into allying with the Sith Lord, but found some familiarity in the cyborgs' fear and anger. As an Assistant Data Processor,<ref name="Vader 44" /> Fabarian was reluctant to erase official records to let<ref name="Vader 43" /> Baron Slandarv<ref name="Vader 44" /> off of a fine. Fabarian had [[Color|gray]] [[hair]], blue [[eye]]s, and light skin.<ref name="Vader 43" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|The ''autoslicer'' Administrator Moore fitted into my ''comlink'' copied the ''data'' we needed, just as planned.|Fabarian|<ref name="Vader 44" />}}
While a member of the Imperial Security Bureau, Fabarian [[Clothing|wore]] an [[Imperial military uniforms|Imperial officer's uniform]], including a [[Imperial kepi|cap]] with an [[officer's disk]].<ref name="Vader 44" /> His uniform featured [[code cylinder]]s<ref name="Vader 43" /> and a [[rank insignia plaque]] that displayed two blue squares and two red. Fabarian also carried a comlink and a blaster. As an Assistant Data Processor, Fabarian wore a gray uniform that featured the Imperial insignia and carried a datapad and a comlink, the latter of which had an autoslicer installed into it by Administrator Moore. After being discharged from service, Fabarian changed into a black Imperial uniform and carried a blaster and a [[holoprojector]].<ref name="Vader 44" /> In the Meridional Ice Cap of Tython, Fabarian briefly wore an outfit consisting of a heavy white jacket, gray pants, gray [[glove]]s, and a white cap that covered his [[ear]]s.<ref name="Vader 45" /> Fabarian [[pilot]]ed a ''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle when traveling from Coruscant to meet with Vader and the Schism.<ref name="Vader 44" />
