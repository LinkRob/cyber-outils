'''By Bosigg's Decree...''' was a memory game located on [[The Island]] of a [[Unidentified planet (The Island)|planet]]. Set up just outside of the [[Hippodrome Simulator]], the game could be activated via the console. Upon starting the game, one of the three tiles, [[color]]ed orange, purple, and green, would light up, requiring the player to step on it. Once stepped on, the next round would start where the first tile would light up again, then a second tile would randomly light up, requiring the player to step on both tiles in the order they lit up. This would keep going on, with each new round adding another randomly selected tile to light up, until the player makes a mistake, causing the game to reset. The game's console would record which round the player was on.<ref name="Castaways" />
