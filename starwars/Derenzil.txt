'''Derenzil''' was a [[swamp]] [[planet]]<ref name="Complete Locations" /> located in the [[Wild Space]] [[Derenzil system]] in grid square S-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Galaxy" /> A short-lived [[Derenzil civil war|civil war]] occurred there in which both sides were [[Arms dealer|supplied]] with highly specialized, [[Water|aquatic]] [[sonic weapon]]s by [[Geonosian]]s from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Geonosis]]. The [[weapon]]s were created in a [[Dooku's hangar|factory]] near the [[Stalgasin hive]] on Geonosis that was equipped with a [[Starfighter|fighter]] [[hangar]] due to the risk of the project; however,<ref name="Complete Locations" /> by [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[22 BBY]].</ref> the [[factory]] was abandoned.<ref name="Complete Locations" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Derenzil was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="Complete Locations" /> from the [[2003]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]'', which was written by [[Simon Beecroft]] and [[Curtis Saxton]] and marked the first mention of the [[Derenzil/Legends|world]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]''</ref>
