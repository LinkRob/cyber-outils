'''Braigh''' was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[pilot]] in the [[Nath Tensent's squadron|squadron]] of [[Nath Tensent]]. During an [[Battle at Trenchenovu|attack]] on the [[Trenchenovu shipyards]], Braigh attempted to sell out her [[squadron]] to the [[Galactic Empire]] in return for her life, but was [[Death|killed]] by Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
