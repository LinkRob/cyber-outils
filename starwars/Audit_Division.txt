The '''Audit Division''' was a department of the [[Galactic Empire]] that was responsible for auditing. While [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] was the [[Baron Administrator]] of [[Cloud City]], [[Bondeeni]] threatened to have his [[Bondeeni's cousin|cousin]], who worked for the agency, involved in the deal he and Lando were having.<ref name="Lando 1" />
