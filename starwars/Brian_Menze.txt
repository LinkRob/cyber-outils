'''Brian Menze''' is an [[Artist/Legends|artist]] and [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developer at [[Obsidian Entertainment]] that is credited as a member of the Art team for ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]''. He is also known for his work on ''Fallout 2'' and ''Fallout: New Vegas''.
