A '''charging array''' was a device that was used for energy discharge augmentation. The high-capacity charging arrays could be used in military-grade shipboard [[turbolaser]]s. The [[freighter]] ''[[Yissira Zyde]]'' was carrying 46 such arrays when it was hijacked by the [[First Order]].<ref name="Before">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref>
