The '''Evroes family''' was a family of [[Chiss]] that was neither a member of the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] nor the Forty [[Great Families]]. [[Evroes'pu'titor]] "Oesputi" was a member of the Evroes family who guided [[Mitth family]] [[Syndic]] [[Mitth'urf'ianico|Thurfian]] during a dispute the Evroes had with the [[Xodlak family]]. Evroes'ky'mormi (Oeskym), the weapons officer aboard Admiral [[Ar'alani]]'<nowiki/>s [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|Vigilant]], was another member of this family.<ref name="Greater Good" />
