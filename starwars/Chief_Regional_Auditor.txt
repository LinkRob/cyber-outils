'''Chief Regional Auditor''' was a title used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Bird|avian]] [[Cygnus Anton]], an operative of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate within the Empire, held the title.<ref name="Vader 18" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Darth Vader (2020) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 18]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, the events of ''Darth Vader'' 18 must take place between these [[Standard year|years]].</ref> Anton visited the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Calior]] to claim its [[southern water works]] for the Empire. However, the avian was unable to do so as they were [[Death|killed]] by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who was hunting down all Crimson Dawn members in the Empire's service.<ref name="Vader 18" />

==Behind the scenes==
The title of Chief Regional Auditor appeared in the [[Darth Vader (2020) 18|eighteenth]] issue of the [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']] comic series,<ref name="Vader 18" /> which was published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 1]], [[2021]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/89724/star_wars_darth_vader_2020_18|text=Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020) #18}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]] and illustrated by [[Leonard Kirk]].<ref name="Vader 18" />
