'''Azzazzo's lightsaber'''<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> was a light [[Color|green]] [[lightsaber]] owned by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Amadeo Azzazzo]]<ref name="TCOC" /> that contained a [[kyber crystal]].<ref name="Kyber" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[The Call of Coruscant]]" takes place concurrently with the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]].</ref> during a destructive [[Groundquake on Mikkia|groundquake]] on the [[planet]] [[Mikkia]], Azzazzo was in pursuit of the [[miner]] [[Lor Botho]], who was trying to take advantage of the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Mikkian]]s in their vulnerable state. While in a [[starship]] with both of them, Botho released [[poison]] smoke, forcing Azzazzo to hold his breath. In order to get the miner to let his guard down, Azzazzo deignited his [[lightsaber]] and pretended to fall to the ground, so when Botho came to finish the job, the Jedi leapt up, deflected the [[blaster]] bolts, and grabbed Botho's [[Breath mask|respirator]]. Later, on the planet [[Coruscant]], Azzazzo showed off his [[weapon]] to his new friends at the behest of the [[human]] [[Sym]].<ref name="TCOC" />
