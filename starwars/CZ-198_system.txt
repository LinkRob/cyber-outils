The '''CZ-198 system''' was located in the [[Unknown Regions]] in grid square H-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The CZ-198 [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> Although "Star Systems of the Galaxy" initially placed the CZ-198 system in grid square G-6,<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[October 14]], 2025<ref name="Oct14">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-14}}</ref> update to the appendix changed its grid square to H-5.<ref name="Appendix" />

The system originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] [[CZ-198]], which was introduced in the [[August 6]], [[2013]] Game Update 2.3 of ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', a [[2011]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]]<ref name="PatchNotes">{{TORweb|url=patchnotes/2.3/titans-industry|text=Game Update 2.3: Titans of Industry Patch Notes}}</ref> developed by [[BioWare]].<ref name="LucasArts Announce">{{LucasArts|url=company/release/news20081021.html|text=LucasArts and BioWare reveal ''Star Wars'': The Old Republic}}</ref> Prior to the release of the update featuring CZ-198, it went live on the game's Public Test Server on [[June 28]] of the same year,<ref name="PTS">{{TORweb|url=info/news/%5Bnews-category%5D/20130628|text=Game Update 2.3 is Now Live on PTS!}}</ref> and screenshots showcasing the moon were also published on the game's [https://www.swtor.com/ official website] on the same day.<ref name="Tour">{{TORweb|url=info/news/blog/20130628|text=Facilities: Take a Virtual Tour}}</ref>
