The '''Battle on Carlac'''<ref name"Fortnite ID">{{FortniteCite|Improvised Battle Staff}}</ref> was a skirmish that occurred on [[Carlac]] between [[R2-D2]], [[Lux Bonteri]], [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Pre Vizsla]]'s [[Death Watch]] warriors. Bonteri, Tano, and R2-D2 had arrived on the [[planet]] [[Carlac]] after the [[Confederate–Republic peace conference]], where Bonteri had interrupted. He had gained the location of [[Count]] [[Dooku]] through a [[holotrace device]]. Bonteri wished to get revenge on Dooku for the death of his mother, [[Mina Bonteri]], but saw that the Death Watch were not allies he had believed they are when the skirmish started. The Death Watch warriors, under Vizsla's command started to torch [[Ming Po Town]]. Tano was captured during her attempt to halt the slaughter, but eventually escaped with R2-D2 and Bonteri.

==Prelude==
===Origins of anger===
The [[Mandalorian]] [[terrorist]] group known as [[Death Watch]] came into existence to oppose [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]]'s [[New Mandalorians]] and their pacifist government. Lead by [[Pre Vizsla]], who was angered by the Duchess and her government not following the ancient [[Soldier|warrior]] ways of [[Mandalore]],<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref> Death Watch allied with [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and his [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. However, their planned takeover failed<ref name="DOM">{{TCW|Duchess of Mandalore}}</ref> and Vizsla confronted the [[Count of Serenno|Count]].<ref name="A Friend in Need" /> According to Death Watch's account, Dooku betrayed Death Watch,<ref name="Eminence" /> and the Confederate-Mandalorian alliance fell out. During their encounter,<ref name="A Friend in Need" /> Dooku scarred Vizsla with his [[Dooku's lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="Death Watch History Gallery 12">{{Databank|url=death-watch-history-gallery|title=Death Watch History Gallery}} {{C|Slide 12}}</ref> Death Watch became nomadic crew of brigands,<ref name="Death Watch Databank">{{Databank|death-watch|Death Watch}}</ref> and the once strictly regulated [[army]]'s<ref name="A Friend in Need episode gallery 10">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/a-friend-in-need-episode-gallery|text="A Friend in Need" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Image 10 caption}}</ref> military precision fell,<ref name="Death Watch History Gallery 11">{{Databank|url=death-watch-history-gallery|title=Death Watch History Gallery}} {{C|Slide 11}}</ref> making them into a group of ruthless outlaws.<ref name="A Friend in Need episode gallery 10" />

[[File:Vizsla-Dooku-argue.png|thumb|left|300px|Pre Vizsla forged an alliance with Count Dooku's Confederacy of Indepedent Systems to reclaim Mandalore, yet their agreement crumbled in time.]]
Death Watch ultimately found safe haven on<ref name="Death Watch History Gallery 11" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Carlac]], creating a [[Carlac Death Watch camp|temporary base]] near [[Ming Po Town]]. The Mandalorians were able to raid this defenseless village,<ref name="Carlac Databank">{{Databank|carlac|Carlac}}</ref> stealing [[Foodstuff|food]] and taking the town's women as servants. Death Watch also possessed a small group of [[droid]]s, some of which were reassembled [[battle droid]]s, to use as target practice. With their alliance with the Separatists over, Death Watch made plans to assassinate Dooku. To accomplish this goal they came into contact with [[Lux Bonteri]], who also wished to kill Dooku as well; the son of Separatist [[Senator]] [[Mina Bonteri]], Bonteri's mother and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], a close friend and Republic senator, attempted to [[Confederate–Republic peace initiative|promote peace]] between their warring governments. During that [[time]], Lux also met and befriended [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]],<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref> eventually developing some romantic feelings for her.<ref name="A Friend in Need" />

However, the peace initiative failed with the [[bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid]],<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> and Dooku ordered for his [[agent]]s to kill Mina.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> While he initially filled his mother's role in the [[Separatist Senate]], Lux learned Dooku was behind his mother's death and swore revenge against him.<ref name="Lux Encyclopedia">{{SWE|characters|luxbonteri|Lux Bonteri}}</ref> Believing Death Watch was a noble group, he allied with them and planned to pinpoint Dooku's current location using a [[holotrace device]]. With Dooku's location, Bonteri hoped that the Mandalorians would be able to kill the Separatist [[Head of State]] to avenge his mother's death.<ref name="A Friend in Need" />

===Interrupting the peace conference===
To get the Count's attention, Bonteri traveled to Mandalore to interrupt for the first-ever [[Confederate–Republic peace conference]]. Upon entering, Bonteri surprised the group, which included Amidala and Tano, and declared that Dooku had killed his mother. The Confederate representatives rejected his claims and had him arrested by two [[BX-series droid commando]]s. Secretly followed by Tano, Bonteri was taken inside a [[C-9969 landing craft]] and brought before Dooku's [[hologram]], but he repeated his accusation and secretly activated his holotrace device to locate the count. Ordering for the droids to kill Bonteri, Dooku's hologram deactivated just before Tano entered the room,<ref name="A Friend in Need" /> as Bonteri had hoped.<ref name="LuxBioGal4">{{Databank|url=lux-bonteri-biography-gallery|title=Lux Bonteri Biography Gallery}} {{C|Image 4 caption}}</ref> The Jedi Padawan was able to quickly defeat both droids and called [[R2-D2]] to prepare their [[starship]] for escape. Running from the [[landing craft]], Tano and Bonteri were chased through the hallways of [[Sundari]] until they approached a Republic [[GX1 short hauler]] designated the ''[[Phoenix (GX1 short hauler)|Phoenix]]'', allowing for the pair to escape while their droid pursers were held up by [[Captain]] [[Taggart (Senate Guard)|Taggart's]] [[Senate Guard]]s.<ref name="A Friend in Need" />

[[File:HowLuxGetsTheGirl-AFIN.png|thumb|right|300px|Lux Bonteri stuns Ahsoka Tano to take control of the ''Phoenix'']]
The Separatist-Republic peace talks effectively collapsed around the same time. Reuniting with R2-D2 aboard the ship, it was Tano's intention to depart for [[Coruscant]], where her master [[Anakin Skywalker]], speaking to her via hologram, confirmed they could discuss giving Bonteri amnesty. Intending to reach Carlac, Bonteri pulled a small [[blaster]] on Tano after Skywalker's hologram vanished, although the Padawan quickly claimed the weapon from the inexperienced fighter. Declaring that he was longer a Separatist either but would not join the Republic, which he blamed for not helping his mother, Bonteri revealed that he had made contact with a like-minded group on Carlac. Ignoring Tano's warning that his crusade would result in his own demise, Bonteri tazed the distracted Tano with a small device he was carrying. When R2 entered, Bonteri claimed she needed [[sleep]] and took control of the ship, jumping into [[hyperspace]] to reach Carlac.<ref name="A Friend in Need" />

===Time with Death Watch===
{{Quote|What have you gotten us into?''"<br />"''It's all under control.''"<br />"''These are the Death Watch. They're Mandalorian terrorists. They'll kill us both.|Ahsoka Tano and Lux Bonteri on Carlac|<ref name="A Friend in Need" />}}
[[File:LR-57CombatDroid-AoN.png|thumb|left|250px|Battle droid 513 fights back against their Mandalorian tormentors alongside several other droids.]]
Bonteri also took Tano's [[lightsaber]]s and hid them, but Tano woke up sometime after landing. She told R2-D2 to find them, but after going to outside to question Bonteri Death Watch forces appeared. Tano recognized the group and Bonteri told [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] he had the information. When questioned, Tano claimed to be Bonteri's betrothed. R2 came off the ship having found Tano's lightsaber, but she silently gestured to the droid to hide them from view. The Bonteri, Tano, and R2 arrived to the camp with the Death Watch [[soldier]]s, and as they entered various Death Watch soldiers blasted at captured droids, which frightened R2. Tano and Bonteri were brought inside to part of the camp.<ref name="A Friend in Need" />
