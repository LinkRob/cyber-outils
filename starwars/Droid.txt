'''Droids''', less commonly known as '''robots''' and '''automatons''', were mechanical beings that possessed [[artificial intelligence]]. They were used in a variety of roles and environments, often those considered too menial or too dangerous for other [[species]], but also in fields that required extensive specialization and knowledge.

==History==
{{Quote|Never underestimate a droid.|Leia Organa, to Rey|<ref name="Episode 9">{{Film|IX}}</ref>}}
The earliest origins of droids were lost to history, and, by the [[time]] of the [[New Republic]], it was unknown exactly when the first droid was created,<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> though some [[historian]]s, only taking into account the more advanced worlds, placed the origin of the first droids to approximately 30,000 [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin|Battle of]] [[Yavin]].<ref name="Bust Collection 10">{{BustCollectionCite|6|Star Wars Universe}}</ref> Nevertheless, for [[Millennium|millennia]], droids served in [[The galaxy|galactic]] society alongside [[Species|organic]] beings.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> According to the [[B1-series battle droid]] author [[R0-GR]], the bond between organic and mechanical lifeforms were tenuous with the former fearing that droids would rise up against their masters. Though several droid revolts took place, Roger claimed that an "unspoken understanding" between [[human]] and droid cultures kept everyone working on the same team throughout the ages.<ref name="Droidography" />

Droids existed as far back as the earliest [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Jedi Order]],<ref name="SWE Huyang">{{SWE|characters|huyang|Huyang}}</ref> which was founded over 25,000 years before the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> One such ancient droid, [[Professor]] [[Huyang]], served the [[Jedi]] for several millennia.<ref name="SWE Huyang" /> As a result of this ancient coexistence with living beings, the [[galaxy]] often had an uneven relationship between organics and mechanicals. Records of uprisings and clashes between droids and their organic overlords became increasingly common as droids slowly became more important in galactic society.<ref name="Propaganda" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] welcomed droids into their ranks.<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref> They also designed [[blaredroid]]s and [[scav droid]]s as weapons.<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>

[[File:MalastareBattleDroidArmy-TCWS02E18.jpg|thumb|270x270px|left|During the Clone Wars, the Confederacy deployed a massive army of various battle-droid models. The extensive use of droids in combat led to an increase in anti-droid sentiment.]]
During the pan-galactic conflict known as the Clone Wars, the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] used a [[Separatist Droid Army|variety]] of [[battle droid]]s in its fight against the [[Galactic Republic]]. This mass use of droids in combat cemented the idea of the "droid as an existential threat to biological life" narrative in the minds of the Republic.<ref name="Propaganda" /> Droids were not utilized by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] by the time of the Clone Wars.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> Ultimately, the Republic won the war and was [[Proclamation of the New Order|replaced]] by an authoritarian [[Galactic Empire]]. Under the new regime, many people in the galaxy were afraid or otherwise distrustful of droids, due to their bad memories of the conflict.<ref name="SWCT">[[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]]</ref>

The Galactic Empire fostered [[anti-droid sentiment]] and treated droids as property. Many sentients continued the Clone Wars policies of [[Memory wipe|wiping droids' memories]], fitting them with restraining bolts, and curtailing their movements. Several establishments also excluded droids.<ref name="Droidography" /> Further, anti-droid sentiment adversely affected the labor fields dedicated to droid design and manufacture, which forced those specialized in those fields&mdash;such as [[Maela's mother]]&mdash;to begin working in Imperial droid factories in order to maintain a job. Otherwise, there were simply not enough openings in the market for those skills.<ref name="EyesEmpire">{{StoryCite|story=Eyes of the Empire|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> After the Clone Wars, the [[Imperial Senate]] also passed a law prohibiting programming that would allow for the harming of organics in the droids used by the Empire.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Thus, with both public opinion<ref name="Droidography" /> and the law against it,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition" /> battle droid production fell sharply.<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> However, this law was bypassed by Arakyd Industries in conjunction with the [[Imperial Military]] by marketing their [[KX-series security droid|KX-series droids]] as "[[security droid]]s".<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition" />

The illegal sport of [[droid-fighting]] was practiced on [[Nal Hutta]]<ref name="OR">[[Star Wars: Outer Rim|''Star Wars'': Outer Rim]]</ref> and in [[the Lodge]], where Clone Wars-survivor [[Ralakili]] ran the fighting pits due to his hate of droids.<ref name="Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], which opposed the Empire, treated droids not as property but as sentient beings.<ref name="TLJ Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Several droids were encouraged by the Rebellion and carried out acts of resistance such as bypassing repressive programming, deactivating restraining bolts, and demanding [[Droid rights|freedom]]. The Rebellion's acceptance of droids as equals was continued by the [[New Republic]] and [[Resistance]].<ref name="Droidography" /> [[Support Droid]]s were units that existed at some stage of galactic history that provided various assistant roles in battle.<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>

==Definition==
===Mechanical "beings"===
{{SeeAlso|Artificial intelligence}}
{{Quote|Droids are not good or bad. They are neutral reflections of those who imprint them.|[[Kuiil]]|audio=KuiilDroids-TMS01CH07.flac|<ref name="Chapter 7: The Reckoning">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 7: The Reckoning}}</ref>}}
[[File:R0GRandotherdroids.png|thumb|right|270px|Various droid models existed for many roles.]]
Droids were mechanical constructs designed to assist organic lifeforms in various tasks. Depending on the nature of the work they performed, they possessed various degrees of artificial [[Sentience|intelligence]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Most droids had hard-coded restrictions against harming organics. Notable exceptions included [[security droid]]s<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> and [[assassin droid]]s.<ref name="SWCT" /> It was the nature of less complex droids to assume that if a human acted as if they belonged somewhere, then that human did belong there.<ref name="Master & Apprentice">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref>

Droids came in all sizes and shapes, and many of them resembled [[humanoid]] organics, possessing an upright posture, two arms, two legs, hands, feet, one thorax, a neck, and a head.<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> The B1 battle droids were designed to resemble their [[insectoid]] [[Geonosian]] creators.<ref name="DB Battle Droid">{{Databank|battle-droid|Battle Droid}}</ref> Likewise, the [[Colicoid]]s designed the three-[[Limb|legged]] [[droideka]]s in their image.<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> Some droids, like the [[LOM-series protocol droid|LOM-series unit]], were made to resemble the organics they served.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

Contrary to organics, who were [[Reproduction|born]] and healed slowly,<ref name="WiaW">[[Star Wars: What is a Wookiee? (2015)|''What is a Wookiee?'']]</ref> droids were easily built en masse, were less fragile, and could be repaired easily.<ref name="DV 18">[[Darth Vader (2015) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 18]]</ref> They also did not have to learn skills (such as calculation abilities), as they were directly programmed with them. Most droids possessed a form of self-awareness, with advanced models even having emotions such as fear for their existence.<ref name="WiaW" /> Droids did not need to [[Atmosphere|breathe]],<ref name="THRA TNT1">[[The High Republic Adventures – The Nameless Terror 1|''The High Republic Adventures – The Nameless Terror'' 1]]</ref> [[Foodstuff|eat]]<ref name="The Gunslinger">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 5: The Gunslinger}}</ref> or [[water|drink]].<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> They were capable of surviving in a vacuum and underwater, and were immune to [[cosmic radiation]],<ref name="Station to Station">{{Resistance|Station to Station}}</ref> all [[poison]]s, toxins<ref name="KoF">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref> and [[disease]]s.<ref name="Station to Station" /> They did not need to [[sleep]], although they could be deactivated as a form of sleep and had occasional maintenance cycles.<ref name="IJ">''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]''</ref>

===Not "alive" in an organic sense===
{{Quote|An actual ''horror'' for them, I imagine. Much ''simpler'' to be a ''droid''. Wipe our memory banks, reprogram us, it's all the same. But imagine being a creature of ''flesh''&hellip; ''mangled'', nearly ''killed'' in battle&hellip; and having the most vital parts of your ''body'' replaced by ''machinery''&hellip; only to find yourself ''the tool'' of your ''enemy''? I suppose we should be grateful, eh?|A [[Unidentified Rebel Alliance protocol droid|protocol droid]], after learning of Darth Vader's efforts to take control over the cybernetic warriors of the [[M.A.R. Corps]]|<ref name="ToolsoftheEmpire">{{StoryCite|story=Tool of the Empire|book=Revelations (2023) 1}}</ref>}}
[[File:TripleZeroDistraction-2015DarthVader5.png|thumb|left|250px|0-0-0 theorized that a special engine powered by human blood could connect a droid to the Force.]]
Droids were broadly considered not to be alive. They could not be detected by scanners designed to detect the presence of life-forms.<ref name="Episode IV2">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Similarly, Droids could not access [[the Force]],<ref name="DV 18" /> an energy field that connected all living things in the galaxy.<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Additionally, not being alive, droids could not be sensed in the Force, though a skilled Jedi might sense their presence through the empty spots they left in the Force.<ref name="Sharing the Same Face">{{StoryCite|story=Sharing the Same Face|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref> However, R0-GR nonetheless referred to droids as mechanical life-forms and artificial lives.<ref name="Droidography" /> The reprogrammed [[Protocol droid|protocol unit]] [[0-0-0|Triple-Zero]], theorized that the Force might flow through a droid equipped with a special engine driven by [[human]] [[blood]].<ref name="DV 18" />

They could not [[Death|die]] in a traditional sense, though they could be destroyed. If enough of the droid survived, they could be repaired, however.<ref name="TCW Voices">''{{TCW|Voices}}''</ref> Droids were usually completely obedient and precise, and normally followed their proprietors' orders.<ref name="Episode IV" /> However, unlike mere machines or primitive [[computer]]s, droids were capable of developing unique personalities by gathering experiences. They could form friendships, and experience emotions such as happiness, pain, and sadness.<ref name="Builders">''[[Star Wars Builders: Droids]]''</ref> To avoid that, most droid owners performed regular [[memory wipe]]s on their properties.<ref name="AbEverything" /> However, memory wipes were not always perfect, occasionally leaving droids with vague recollections.<ref name="Tales">[[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 1|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 1]]</ref><ref name="The Red One">{{StoryCite|story=The Red One|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

Even so, some droids were [[Manumission|emancipated]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> Movements for [[droid rights]] existed throughout the galaxy, promoting equality between droids and other [[Sentience|sentient]] beings,<ref name="Tales2">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]''</ref> with droids such as [[L3-37]] believing strongly in this cause.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|l3-37|L3-37}}</ref>

==Classes of droids==
===Overview===
{{Quote|For easy identification, droids are traditionally divided into five classes based on their specifications. (Frankly, I think that lumping us together like this is a little rude. I assume this list was developed by a human with zero class.)|[[R0-GR]]|<ref name="Droidography" />}}
[[File:L337walking-SWBC59.png|thumb|right|200px|L3-37 was a custom, self-made droid from many different droid parts]]
Droids could be entrusted with a multitude of tasks, ranging from running elementary diagnostics to performing complicated medical procedures, or sending communications to flying [[starship]]s.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Depending on their technology, skills, and purpose, droids were grouped into five "classes,"<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref><ref name="SuperGraphic">''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]''</ref> also called "degrees."<ref name="Propaganda" />

===First class droids===
The [[Class one droid|first class]] was made up of droids skilled in mathematical, physical, and medical sciences. Examples included the [[JN-66 analysis droid]], the [[2-1B surgical droid]], the [[midwife droid]],<ref name="SuperGraphic" /> the [[DD-13 medical assistant droid|DD-13 tripedal medical droid]], and the [[GH-7 series medical assistance droid]].<ref name="VE" />

===Second class droids===
Droids of the [[Class two droid|second class]] were skilled in engineering and technical sciences. Examples included the [[DUM-series pit droid]], the [[PLNK-series power droid]], and the [[WED-15 Septoid Treadwell]].<ref name="VE" />

===Third class droids===
[[File:B1BattleDroid-TCWCEJtB.png|thumb|left|180px|B1-series battle droids were fourth class droids]]
[[Class three droid|Third class]] droids were skilled in social sciences and service functions. Examples included the [[CZ-series secretary/business communications droid|CZ]] [[secretary droid]], the [[LEP-series service droid]], the [[FA-5 valet droid]], and the [[BD-3000 luxury droid]].<ref name="VE" />

===Fourth class droids===
Droids of the [[Class four droid|fourth class]] were skilled in military and security functions. Examples included the [[GU-series Guardian police droid]], the [[KX-series security droid]], and the [[B1-series battle droid]].<ref name="VE" />

===Fifth class droids===
[[Class five droid|Fifth class]] droids were programmed for manual labor and intensive jobs that did not require a high level of intelligence. Examples included the [[GNK-series power droid]], the [[MSE-6 series repair droid]],<ref name="SuperGraphic" /> the [[IW-37 pincer loader droid]], the [[R-H029 sifter droid]], the [[PK-series worker droid]], and the [[UX-53 Autopolisher MK.II Droid]].<ref name="VE" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Well, the droids were there to serve. Obviously droids are servants of man. They do as they are commanded and all that kind of stuff but at the same time I love droids, they're my favorite people. I didn't want them to be cold robots. Even the robots in ''THX'' are very friendly. They're not malevolent. In ''Star Wars'' I really wanted to get into the robots and their problems in life; a little equal time for robots, who have taken a lot of shit over the years and have never really had a chance to prove themselves.|George Lucas, referring to ''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope''|<ref name="Rolling Stone">{{WebCite|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/movies/movie-news/george-lucas-the-wizard-of-star-wars-2-232011|text=George Lucas: The Wizard of 'Star Wars'|author=Scanlon, Paul|date=1977-08-25|work=[https://www.rollingstone.com Rolling Stone]|archivedate=20200623204338}}</ref>}}
The word ''droid'' was stylized as ''{{'}}droid'' in the [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|novelization of ''Star Wars: A New Hope'']] and other early [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] material.

In [[1977]], during an interview regarding the [[original trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], [[George Lucas]] stated that droids were designed to serve [[Sentience|sentients]]. However, droids were Lucas' favorite people, and the filmmaker did not want droids to be cold or malevolent, just like how the robots in [[Wikipedia:THX 1138|''THX 1138'']] were "very friendly." On the other hand, he did not intend droids to be sentient themselves: he stated that, droids, even though some of them like C-3PO and R2-D2 are very human in nature, had no soul, only programmed to act like human beings.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/tv-movies/tv-movie-news/george-lucas-and-the-cult-of-darth-vader-247142|text=George Lucas and the Cult of Darth Vader|author=Gavin Edwards|date=2005-06-02|work=[[Wikipedia:Rolling Stone|''Rolling Stone'']]|archivedate=20230207061219}}</ref><ref name=":0">{{HomeVideoCite|set=Star Wars Trilogy (DVD)|featurette=The Characters of Star Wars|ftext=The Characters of ''Star Wars''}}</ref>

Lucas wanted to focus on droids and their issues in life, giving them equal screentime since they had endured much suffering over the years and had little to no opportunities to prove themselves.<ref name="Rolling Stone" /> As with [[Wikipedia:Akira Kurosawa|Akira Kurosawa's]] film [[Wikipedia:The Hidden Fortress|''The Hidden Fortress'']], which presents two peasants traveling with a princess and a general, ''A New Hope'' follows the points of view of two lowly beings&mdash;the droids R2-D2 and C-3PO.<ref name=":0" />

===Trademark===
George Lucas holds a trademark on the term "droid." The miniature 'mech combat wargame [[Wikipedia:BattleTech|''BattleTech'']] was originally released under the name ''BattleDroids'', but was renamed due to trademark issues with [[Lucasfilm]].

[[Wikipedia:Pixar|Pixar Animation Studios]]' film [[Wikipedia:The Incredibles|''The Incredibles'']] ([[2004]]) contains a reference to Lucasfilm's droid with the large weapon of destruction, the Omnidroid; an apparently unrelated killer droid of the same name also appears in [[Star Wars Galaxies]]''. The credits of the film give a nod to Lucasfilm.

From [http://www.mickeynews.com/News/DisplayPressRelease.asp_Q_id_E_11254Pixar ''Mickey News''] (25 November 2004):

:''[In] the closing credits... a notice that the term "Omnidroid" was used by permission of Lucasfilm Ltd. has prompted some fans to speculate that ''The Incredibles'' offers a sneak peak at a character from the next ''Star Wars'' film.''
:''"No. No, no, no," Bird [Brad Bird, director of ''The Incredibles''] says with a laugh, when asked if the secret weapon has a secret of its own. "I like that they think that, but it's more the term 'droid' is Lucas and we made the term Omnidroid (and then got the OK as a courtesy). So, no, there's no sneak. But God bless those fans. They're crazy."''

The term "Droid" has been licensed by Verizon Wireless for their line of [[Wikipedia:smartphone|smartphones]] based on the [[Wikipedia:Android (operating_system)|Android]] operating system. Motorola's late-2009 Google Android-based cell phone is called the [[Wikipedia:Motorola Droid|Droid]], and this line of phone has been expanded to include other Android-based phones released under Verizon. The term is used under license from Lucasfilm.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.elliotsblog.com/dont-mess-with-verizon-motorola-and-lucasfilm-18391|text=Don't Mess with Verizon, Motorola and Lucasfilm|nobackup=1}}</ref>
