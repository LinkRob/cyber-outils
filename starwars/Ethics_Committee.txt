The '''Ethics Committee''' was a [[Galactic Senate]] [[Politics|Legislative]] committee of the [[Galactic Republic]], and was considered a spiritual partner of the [[Judiciary Committee]]'s [[Internal Activities Committee]]. The Ethics Committee had the power to investigate [[senator]]s and other government officials, and in order to do so could issue subpoenas for witnesses for hearings.<ref name="Rise" />

Although similar to the Internal Activities Committee, the Ethics Committee was restricted from giving censures and could not file recommendations for criminal charges. Despite that, censures by the committee usually created enough public pressure to force a criminal investigation and a resignation or call for a [[Vote of No Confidence]]. The committee was active during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Rise" /> which lasted from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Ethics Committee was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''.<ref name="Rise" /> The [[Ethics Committee/Legends|Ethics Committee]] was originally mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2012]] novel [[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']], written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Darth P">[[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']]</ref>
