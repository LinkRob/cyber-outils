'''''Enhanced Cloud City''''' is a boutique [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game]] set by [[Decipher]] that was used to get rid of excess [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City]] booster packs. It was first released in December [[1999]]. With each individual sealed pack, there were four Cloud City booster packs and three premium cards. There were 12 premium cards in this set. Other "Enhanced" sets produced by Decipher included [[Enhanced Premiere]] and [[Enhanced Jabba's Palace]].
