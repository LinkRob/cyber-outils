The '''Bekkra Star Cluster''' was a [[Star cluster/Legends|star cluster]] located within the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]]. The [[Tragan system]] was located near it.

==Behind the scenes==
The Bekkra Star Cluster was briefly mentioned by [[Jean-Michel Ringuet]] in the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] aid "[[Providence (Avalon)|Providence]]," which appeared in the French magazine ''[[Avalon 1]]''.
