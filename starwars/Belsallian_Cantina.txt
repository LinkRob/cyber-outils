The '''Belsallian Cantina'''<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was a [[cantina]] located in the [[city]] of [[Carannia]] on [[Serenno]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> It shared its name with the [[Belsallian Sea]].<ref name="BMF 70">{{BuildFalconCite|70|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> Patrons of the cantina were advised to arm themselves with weapons due to its disreputable nature. The [[Sith apprentice]] [[Asajj Ventress]] visited the cantina while [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|searching]] for [[Jenza]], the sister of her [[Sith Master|Master]], [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]]. Upon visiting the cantina, Ventress sat on a chair where she was harassed by a [[Khormai]], leading to Ventress hitting them with a tray. After the confrontation, a waitress [[droid]] guided Ventress to the [[Razorclaw Gang]] that [[Glute]] was a part of. After torturing a [[Pendago]] by cutting of three of his tentacles, the [[Gran]] obeyed and told Ventress where Glute lived.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Belsallian Cantina first appeared in the audiobook ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> It shares the name of the [[Belsallian Sea/Legends|Belsallian Sea]], which first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets 1|''Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets'' 1]].<ref name="Agent of the Empire—Hard Targets, Part 1">[[Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets 1|''Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets'' 1]]</ref>
