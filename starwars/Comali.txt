'''Comali''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who held the title of [[Lady]] on [[Shu-Torun]]. Her [[Comali's son|son]] was a guard for the [[planet]]'s [[Monarch of Shu-Torun|monarch]], [[Queen]] [[Trios]], but was [[Death|killed]], with Trios claiming he had died protecting her from [[terrorist]]s. Still grieving over her son's death, Comali attended a party Trios held in the [[Abyssal Rooms]], leaving before the event had finished. However, due to a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Mission to Shu-Torun (Rebel Alliance)|mission]], Comali was unable to leave, as the planet's entire security system had been shut down by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Heroes of Yavin|her team]].<ref name="Star Wars 64" />
