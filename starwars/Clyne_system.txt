The '''Clyne system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Corellian sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]. It was located in grid square M-11 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Clynese]]|system=Clyne [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> the [[Clynese]] [[writing]] [[Language|script]].<ref name="Art Last Jedi">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]''</ref>
