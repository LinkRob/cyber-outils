'''Corellian rescue frigates''' were a class of disc-[[hull]]ed [[frigate]] that saw use by the [[Space Rescue Corps]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] around the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Along with the nimble [[Sprint-class rescue craft|''Sprint''-class rescue craft]], the Corellian rescue frigate was a [[starship]] model commonly deployed by the rescue [[corps]] in order to fulfill larger assignments, while also seeing utilization in locating missing starships and [[Realspace|space]] battle survivors as well as responding to distress calls.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian rescue frigates were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Rescue frigate/Legends|rescue frigate]] was introduced in the [[Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan: The Aurorient Express 1|first issue]] of the miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan: The Aurorient Express]]'', which was authored by [[Mike Kennedy]], illustrated by [[Lucas Marangon]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Aurorient Express">[[Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan: The Aurorient Express 1|''Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan: The Aurorient Express'' 1]]</ref> on [[February 27]], [[2002]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/11-434/Star-Wars-Qui-Gon-and-Obi-Wan---The-Aurorient-Express-1-of-2|text=Star Wars: Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan &mdash; The Aurorient Express 1 of 2}}</ref>
