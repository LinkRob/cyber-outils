'''Bon Tapalo''' was a [[Gender|male]] member of the [[Royal House of Naboo]] who served as [[Monarch of Naboo|king]] of the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] culture on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. The construction of the [[Theed Hangar]] in the Naboo [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Theed]] began during Tapalo's reign,<ref name="BMF24" /> with the [[hangar]] having been completed by the [[time]] of the [[Trade Federation]] [[Invasion of Naboo]] during the reign of [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> At that [[time]], a statue of [[Queen]] [[Consort]] [[Scotia Belford-Tapalo]] stood a top the hangar's domed roof.<ref name="CLNE">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Bon Tapalo was mentioned in "Theed: Naboo's Capital City," an article in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 24|twenty fourth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF24" /> which was released around [[June 17]], [[2015]]<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|24}}</ref> and written by [[Tim Veekhoven]].<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:Credits-TimVeekhoven-Twitter.png|creator=[[Tim Veekhoven]]}}</ref> The [[Bon Tapalo/Legends|character]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where he appeared in the [[2012]] novel [[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']] by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Plagueis">[[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']]</ref>
