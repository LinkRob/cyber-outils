A '''CT-900 freighter''' was a model of [[freighter]], that has a distinctive hammerhead. One known CT-900 freighter was the ''[[StarScavenger]]'', the [[starship]] and transport of the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]], a family of scavengers who lived during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The [[Mon Calamari]] starship builder [[Quarrie]] regarded the CT-900 as an antique ship that had not been seen since the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Zander Freemaker]] managed to keep the ''StarScavenger'' operational by modifying the ship with spare parts.<ref name="TOT" />
