The '''Bacta Cartel'''<ref name="FACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=The Impossible Flight of Ash Angels|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> was in operation by the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]] on<ref name="Incredibuilds">''[[Star Wars: A-wing Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]''</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="GE2">{{EncyclopediaCite|General Equipment (2)}}</ref> [[Thyferra]], whose [[Vratix]] and [[human]] [[species]] inhabitants produced the substance [[bacta]]. Before becoming a [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the human Thyferran<ref name="HC">{{HelmetCollectionCite|75|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> [[Arvel Crynyd]] worked for the cartel as a [[Z-95 Headhunter]] [[starfighter]] [[pilot]].<ref name="Incredibuilds" />
