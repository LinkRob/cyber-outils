The '''"Quicker Draw" Hands-Free Trigger''' was a model of [[automated weapon mounting]] manufactured by [[Mitrinomon Transports]]. It cost 3,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and allowed its user to fire a [[Limb|hand]]-held weapon while keeping both of their hands free.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Quicker Draw" Hands-Free Trigger was first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref>
