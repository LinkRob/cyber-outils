'''A Long Time Ago in a Mixed-Up Galaxy Far, Far Away...''' was a comic printed in the ''[[Star Wars Jedi Quest CommLink Premier Issue]]''. [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] sensed a disturbance in the Force, and [[Yoda]] asked why, just before [[Maul|Darth Maul]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] appeared. Vader was confused about his whereabouts, and Yoda told him to go because he was in the wrong movie. [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] asked Qui-Gon if things could "get any crazier," and [[Jar Jar Binks]] appeared, irritating Obi-Wan and Qui-Gon. Vader allied with Maul, but asked who they were fighting. Jar Jar tripped Yoda, who called Jar Jar a "clumsy Gungan." Jar Jar singlehandedly subdued both Maul and Vader, the latter of whom apparently had no recollection of anyone else present. Yoda warned Jar Jar that he would zap him if the Gungan stepped on him again.
