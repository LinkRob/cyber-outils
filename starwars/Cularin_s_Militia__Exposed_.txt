'''Cularin's Militia: Exposed!''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[January 9]] [[2003]] and took the form of an interview by [[Yara Grugara]] with Commander [[Broof Yurdel]] of the [[Cularin Militia]].
