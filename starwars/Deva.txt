'''Deva''' was a genre of [[pop]] [[music]]. The [[New Republic pilot]] [[Chass na Chadic]] enjoyed the music, specifically one track she felt featured an easygoing beat as well as saccharine and somewhat ironic lyrics about a local holiday that had been banned by the [[Galactic Empire]]. She played the track off of a [[datachip]] in her [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]] during missions. During the [[Battle of Pandem Nai]]<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> she played the song on an open [[Comm unit|comm]] channel for any nearby [[starship]] to hear. Following the [[New Republic]] victory, fellow [[pilot]] [[Wyl Lark]] and members of the [[New Republic ground crew|ground crew]] took one of Chadic's datachips that contained deva pop music and played it during the victory celebration at the makeshift [[cantina]] [[Ranjiy's Krayt Hut]] aboard the [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class battleship]] ''[[Lodestar]]''. When Chadic noticed that her music was playing, she realized that the song helped quell her feelings of desperation and despair, which had remained with her despite their victory.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

==Behind the scenes==
Deva appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], the first book in the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy, written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> [[Deva/Legends|Deva]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, first being mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters]]'', a sourcebook written by [[Mark Rein-Hagen]] and [[Stewart Wieck]] and published for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' by [[West End Games]] in [[1990]].<ref name="GG6">''[[Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters]]''</ref>
