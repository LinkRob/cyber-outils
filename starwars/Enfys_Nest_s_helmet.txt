'''Enfys Nest's helmet'''<ref name="Card Trader">{{SWCT|set=Solo: A Star Wars Story - Gear & Gadgets|cardname=Enfys Nest's Helmet}}</ref> was a [[helmet]] worn by [[Enfys Nest]], leader of the [[Cloud-Riders]].<ref name="Solo Guide" /> It was passed down to her by [[Enfys Nest's mother|her mother]] who had previously led the group.<ref name="Women of the Galaxy" /> The helmet was inscribed with a poem, "Until we reach the last edge, the last opening, the last star, and can go no higher."<ref name="Solo Guide" /> The inscription used an [[Unidentified Aurebesh script (battle helmet)|Aurebesh-like script]].<ref name="Solo" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Early iterations of Enfys Nest's inscribed battle helmet were inspired by a [[Wikipedia:Dolphin|dolphin]] skull which was lacquered in black and red by [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] artists and then decorated in gold powder, while the chrome eyepiece of the helmet was inspired in the mirrored aviator sunglasses worn by Boss "The Man with No Eyes" Godfrey in the [[1960s|1967]] film [[Wikipedia:Cool Hand Luke|''Cool Hand Luke'']]. The helmet went through many prototype designs, with these designs ultimately being used for members of the Cloud-Riders.<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

In [[2019]], when [[Glyn Dillon]], a costume designer of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story|Rogue One]]'' and ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story|Solo]]'', was asked about the alternate font on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], he revealed that it has been developed at the request of director [[Gareth Edwards]], for the film ''Rogue One'', and was re-used for Enfys Nest's helmet in ''Solo'', and said the script had been named "Dishabesh."<ref name="Dillon Tweet">{{Twitter|glyn_dillon|glyn_dillon/status/1110995215219855363|[[Glyn Dillon]]|quote=The fonts were made at Gareth's request for R.O. he didn't want a universe with just one text (and mostly one font). They were designed by the art dept graphics team. I was able to use them in any designs that needed text. Dishabesh just seemed to suit Enfys more than the others.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/z5GaL|archivefile=File:Glyn-dishabesh.png}}</ref>
