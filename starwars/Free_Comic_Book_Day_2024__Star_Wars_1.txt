'''Free Comic Book Day 2024: ''Star Wars'' 1''', is a one-shot [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]]. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]] and [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Ibraim Roberson]], [[Ramon Rosanas]], [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and [[Guru-eFX]], and published as a [[Free Comic Book Day]] title by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 4]], [[2024]].

The ''[[Star Wars (2020)|Star Wars]]'' side of the issue&mdash;a story entitled "[[Return to Echo Base]]"<ref name="Return to Echo Base">{{StoryCite|story=Return to Echo Base|book=Free Comic Book Day 2024: Star Wars 1|bformatted=Free Comic Book Day 2024: ''Star Wars'' 1}}</ref>&mdash;was designed to be welcoming for new readers, ensuring anyone could pick up the story without prior context needed,<ref name="Charles New Readers">{{Twitter|CharlesSoule|status/1787222414574879165|[[Charles Soule]]|quote=Oh yeah. It's designed for new readers. {{C|In response to: "I'm not caught up on your main run (on issue 21) but I got this yesterday and was wondering if I can still read it without major spoilers or missing any important context?"}}|archiveurl=https://archive.is/hDtdD}}</ref> while the ''[[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|Darth Vader]]'' side&mdash;a story entitled "[[The Curse (comic story)|The Curse]]"&mdash;ties heavily into the ongoing story of that comic series.<ref name="The Curse">{{StoryCite|story=The Curse (comic story)|book=Free Comic Book Day 2024: Star Wars 1|bformatted=Free Comic Book Day 2024: ''Star Wars'' 1}}</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''Are there REBEL survivors stranded on HOTH? LUKE SKYWALKER, LEIA ORGANA, LANDO CALRISSIAN and CHEWBACCA mount a dangerous rescue mission to ECHO BASE! Lightsabers! Blasters! Snowtroopers! All-out action amongst the wreckage of AT-AT walkers, Rebel airspeeders and frozen weapons of mass destruction!''

''DARTH VADER employes a surprise character from the past to help him find LUKE SKYWALKER. But SABE, ''[''sic'']'' former handmaiden of PADME AMADALA ''[''sic'']'' is also on a quest for the son of Vader. Having worked for him as an IMPERIAL COMANDER, Sabe ''[''sic'']'' has enough resources to cause chaos for anyone who stands in her path. How will this affect the DARK LORD'S plans to remove PALPATINE from power?''
