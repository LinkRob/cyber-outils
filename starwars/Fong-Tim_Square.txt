'''Fong-Tim Square''' was an area located in a [[Langston's mining district|mining district]] on the [[Langston's largest continent|largest continent]] of the [[planet]] [[Langston]].<ref name="Brotherhood" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] to [[22 BBY]].</ref> during a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Aid mission to Langston|aid mission]] to Langston in the wake of a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Attack on Langston|attack]] there early in the [[Clone Wars]], [[Captain]] [[Raptor]] of the [[302nd Battalion]] set up in Fong-Tim Square. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], one of the leaders of the mission, instructed a [[clone trooper]] to deliver ten crates of supplies to a neighborhood at [[sector N-seven-A-I]] before meeting up with Raptor in the square to divide up the remaining crates for distribution.<ref name="Brotherhood" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fong-Tim Square was mentioned in the [[2022]] novel [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']], written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />
