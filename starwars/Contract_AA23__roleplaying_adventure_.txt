"'''Contract AA23'''" is a roleplaying-game adventure in the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign that was written by [[Russell Foubert]] and [[Sean Yo]]. The title is a reference to [[Detention Block AA-23/Legends|AA-23]], the detention block aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]] in which [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]] is kept in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].

==Plot summary==
The heroes are sent to [[Nar Shaddaa/Legends|Nar Shaddaa]] to escort [[Korlana Toryn]], the daughter of an important [[Senator/Legends|senator]], back to [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. However, an old bounty placed on them by [[Velin Wir]] just before his death 10 years before resurfaces, and they are attacked by [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]].
