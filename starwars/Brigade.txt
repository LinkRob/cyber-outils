A '''brigade''' was a large military infantry formation. The [[Grand Army of the Republic]] made use of brigades in its unit structure as massive formations of [[clone trooper]]s. Two entire brigades, for example,<ref name="SWE Tion Medon">{{SWE|characters|tionmedon|Tion Medon}}</ref> were made up of many individual [[battalion]]s.<ref name="The End Battalions">{{EncyclopediaCite|The End of the Clone Wars}} {{C|Original French text: "Par conséquent, la flotte de Destroyers stellaires et les bataillons de soldats clones qui accompagnèrent le Général Kenobi sur Utapau étaient trois fois plus importants que le contingent qui aurait été déployé en temps normal pour prendre un petit système stellaire."}}</ref> Furthermore, the Republic organized its personnel within administrative brigades, such as its [[special forces]] under the [[Special Operations Brigade]],<ref name="HC 21">{{HelmetCollectionCite|21|Databank A-Z}}</ref> and young [[clone cadet]]s into various [[Clone Youth Brigade]]s.<ref name="Death Trap">{{TCW|Death Trap}}</ref> Standard [[clone trooper]]s organized into brigades were led by a [[Jedi Commander]]. [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], serving as [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], believed that a single brigade of clone troopers could penetrate a [[planet]]'s orbital defenses and conquer the world.<ref name="Rogue One Novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref>

The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] utilized maintenance brigades within the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|CIS Navy]]. These brigades were deployed after the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]'' was critically damaged during the [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula]].<ref name="Ships of R&S">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic and Separatist Ships (1)}}</ref> The [[Battle of Utapau]] at the end of the war saw two full clone brigades,<ref name="SWE Tion Medon" /> including personnel from the [[7th Sky Corps]],<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> deployed against the CIS.<ref name="SWE Tion Medon" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], the rebellious factions against the [[Galactic Empire]] utilized the designation, such as the [[Maya Pei Brigade]].<ref name="Sagrona Teema">{{Andor|Sagrona Teema}}</ref> The Empire itself also included brigades within the [[Imperial Army]], with [[Brenna's brigade|one such formation]] being under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Colonel]] [[Brenna]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> A resistance group allied with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Coruscant civil war]] went by the name of [[Anklebiter Brigade]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> [[Standard year|Years]] later, the Empire's successor state, the [[First Order]], also utilized brigades, with [[Unidentified brigade|one brigade]] being led by a [[Unidentified First Order major|First Order major]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />
