{{Quote|The New Republic offers us little, Princess Leia&mdash;and we require more than your half-chewed scraps, even those veiled in diplomacy. As of this moment, the planets of the ''Fenril Sector'' have united under one banner. Trade with the New Republic is suspended until a new treaty is agreed upon, and any trespassers into our territory will be dealt with&mdash;and met with ''lethal'' force.|Prime Minister Kezrin Almovar to Senator Leia Organa and Agent Bastion|<ref name="SW1" />}}
The '''Fenril crisis''' was a conflict that arose in the early days of the [[New Republic]] and escalated to combat due to the machinations of the [[Anzati]] Sorceress and former [[Acolytes of the Beyond|Acolyte of the Beyond]], [[Reyna Oskure]]. Some time after the [[Battle of Jakku]], just before a pivotal [[Unidentified trade summit|trade summit]], the planets of the [[Fenril sector]] grew dissatisfied of the New Republic's inability to defend supply shipments from [[Raid on New Republic supplies|pirate attacks]]. Seeing the New Republic's actions so far as those of a bully attempting to mask their lack of power, especially following [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s last stand, [[Pirros|Pirrosi]] [[Prime Minister]] [[Kezrin Almovar]] met with [[Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to declare this as an attack began on New Republic trading vessel ''[[Nechrim's Wake]]''.

The Fenril sector then isolated itself as the [[Fenril Consortium]], which was also secretly aligned with [[Gallana Venk]]'s [[Zantarrk Gang]], many of whom were [[Nagai]], who were causing chaos near the Fenril Sector. While the New Republic's forces were investigating the Consortium, its fleet began approaching New Republic space. In order to combat this new enemy in time and help free the Nagai from their oppression, New Republic hero [[Han Solo]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]], pilot [[Rynn Zenat]], and bounty hunter [[Beilert Valance]], led their forces against the Fenril Consortium and joined up with the [[Nagai rebellion]] on [[Kaltrais]], which led to the [[Battle of Nagi]]. While the battle raged and a New Republic fleet arrived, Luke Skywalker defeated [[Reyna Oskure]] just as she activated her many vessels' [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destruct]] sequences, trapping and damaging the New Republic's forces around and on Nagi and raining debris on the surface. The battle continued there, but was stopped by the arrival of Mothma and Organa announcing the New Republic's support of Nagi and the Nagai– at the behest of the people of Pirros. Finally, Zenat used the [[Crown of Verity]] to subdue and weaken Oskure, defeating her and her coalition and freeing the Nagai.

==Prelude==
===Garlev's game===
{{Quote|You sure that intel is worth sticking our necks out for you, ''Garlev''?"''<br/>''"It's too late now, ''Solo''&mdash;we either shoot our way out&mdash;or we embrace a shared darkness."''<br/>''"We're running for our lives and this guy's writing sonnets&hellip;|Han Solo and Garlev on Karimenen, in a blasterfight.|<ref name="SW3" />}}
[[File:GarlevsQuestForData-2025StarWars3.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Garlev getting data on Karimenen]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca]], and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] were allies with fellow smuggler, [[Garlev]]. On the planet [[Karimenen]], the group of four was under fire from local thugs, and decided to make a run back towards a data nest. Solo, Chewbacca, and Calrissian were there to aid Garlev's quest, and covered him as he entered the nest and obtained data pertaining to individuals named [[Mika (Nagai)|Mika]], [[Kariss]], and [[Tuula]], as Solo ran back in to check on him.<ref name="SW3" />

Some time later, over a decade before the [[Battle of Jakku]], Solo, Chewbacca, and Calrissian stood up [[Harkoon]] to meet Garlev once more. They trusted the man, and he said he had a "can't-miss" score– but they had company. In the outskirts of the [[Moraysian sector]], the group was being fired upon by smugglers. Garlev had a load of [[Moraysian]] weapons in the ship's loading bay, which scared Solo because of the strictness of the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] about things of the sort. Calrissian and Solo were concerned to the point where Han demanded to see what Garlev was carrying. Garlev continued to be cagey, until they reached the loading bay and saw a crowd of [[Nagai]], led by [[Marntiv]], instead of weapons. Han Solo reacted incredibly negatively to this, drawing the line at slavery and trafficking, but Garlev explained that he's not selling these people, they're his people, and he's smuggling them to safety.<ref name="SW3" /> Garlev was a Nagai hero, a criminal who fought for justice, freeing his people and leading a [[Nagai rebellion]].<ref name="SW3" /><ref name="SW6" />

===Space station standoff===
{{Quote|I&mdash;I don't know who they are&hellip;all I know is that they're pirates&hellip;hidden in the shadows&hellip;"''<br/>''"Where are they striking next?|The Gran merchant being interrogated by Luke Skywalker|<ref name="FCBDSW25" />}}
[[File:LukeAndPykes-2025FCBD.jpg|thumb|left|210px|Luke surrounded by Pyke goons on the space station over Maldran]]
Years later, shortly after the Battle of Jakku,<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] was investigating the theft of [[New Republic]] hardware following a [[raid on New Republic supplies]] on the [[Rimma Trade Route]]. Dozens of citizens were killed, and their ships were destroyed. On a former Imperial [[space station]] over [[Maldran]], Luke overheard a [[Gran]] [[merchant]] claim to be selling genuine New Republic technology. As Luke approached him, several [[Pyke]] [[Pyke Syndicate|Syndicate]] [[Pyke Syndicate Foot Soldier|goons]] surrounded the Jedi. Luke attempted to use [[The Force]] to get through to them, but resorted to [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]], fighting and demanding them to tell him who their supplier was. As Luke is talking, a Pyke hits him with a large hammer, knocking him to the ground as the goons refuse to cooperate.<ref name="FCBDSW25" />

But suddenly, a [[blaster]] bolt pierced the chest of the Pyke threatening Luke– it's [[New Republic pilot]] [[Rynn Zenat]] to the rescue! Rynn explained that she was held up by Pyke goons as well earlier. After Luke rises and runs after the Gran, he and Zenat cornered the merchant, and Luke used the Force to hold him over a ledge until he confessed. Finally, he admits that he got the generators he's been trying to sell from a secret pirate organization, hiding in the shadows, but he doesn't know when they'll strike next. Luke and Rynn then contacted their base to send a pickup for the stolen goods, as they got in their [[X-wing starfighter|X-Wings]] and prepared to head towards the shipment entering into the [[Fenril sector]], as it might be threatened.<ref name="FCBDSW25" />

===Fenril sector frenzy===
{{Quote|What&mdash;what is an Imperial Star Destroyer doing out here?|Rynn Zenat|<ref name="SW1" />}}
Outside the Fenril sector, Luke Skywalker, Rynn Zenat, [[Blade Squadron]], and [[77 Squadron|Squadron 77]] arrived at a [[gas giant]] to cut off the pirates before they reached the New Republic's supply shipment. A pirate vessel that raided [[Pirros]] was spotted, and Zenat and fellow pirate [[Sandrin]] followed it around a nearby planet. To Rynn's shock, a heavily modified [[Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] emerged to rescue the pirate vessel, and the X-Wings quickly took defensive formation. More pirate vessels suddenly appeared, and [[Skirmish near the edge of the Fenril sector|a skirmish]] began. As the pirates and X-Wings engaged, Sandrin was heavily fired upon. She tried to shake them, but was too late. Backup couldn't come in time, and Sandrin's starfighter was destroyed.<ref name="SW1" />

[[File:XWingOutsideFenrilSector-2025StarWars1.jpg|thumb|right|280px|Nagai pirates engage New Republic forces]]
These pirates were much better equipped than the New Republic's pilots anticipated, so Luke Skywalker then told the squadron to pull back while maintaining hot cannons, but Rynn Zenat had other plans. Wanting vengeance for Sandrin's death, she chased the pirate vessel to the surface of a nearby planet, Skywalker following her as the rest of the squadron departed to inform [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] of the threat. The pirate's ship went down in a wooded area, and Zenat ran to the crash site, while Luke hesitated– feeling the dark side' presence there. Igniting his lightsaber, Luke ran off after Zenat and found her pointing a blaster at a tattooed, [[Unidentified Nagai pirate|gray-skinned man]] wielding a [[vibroblade]]. The pirate charged the two, but Luke quickly used his lightsaber and the Force to knock him down, questioning who he is and why his people are raiding New Republic shipments. Drawing Luke close,<ref name="SW1" /> the pirate warned the Jedi about who he's facing, and told him about [[Ibo Truddispal|a collector]] of [[Jedi artifact]]s on the planet [[Gadrilam]], who has a crown that will help Luke "learn the truth". He then told Luke to brace himself,<ref name="SW2" /> as he detonated himself to cover his tracks, knocking Luke out.<ref name="SW1" />

After Skywalker had awoken, he was in a trance-like state, single-mindedly focused on going to Gadrilam. This left Zenat quite confused, as Skywalker rushed to his X-Wing and quickly departed into [[hyperspace]], telling Rynn that he needed to follow this lead alone after hearing what the Nagai had told him.<ref name="SW2" />

===Guile on Guat'a===
{{Quote|Yeah, these are the creeps I told you about&mdash;should've been on your scanners ''months'' ago.<br/>Didn't bring any of your shiny ''New Republic Friends'' for ''backup'', did you?"''<br/>''"This isn't a New Republic mission, Valance. I'm on my own.|Beilert Valance and Han Solo under cover from blaster fire on Guat'a|<ref name="SW1" />}}
[[File:ZantarrkGang-2025StarWars1.png|thumb|left|200px|Zantarrk Gang stormtroopers]]
Meanwhile in [[Unidentified bar (Alshuey Town)|a bar]] in [[Alshuey Town]] on [[Guat'a]], [[cyborg]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] had located the [[Zantarrk Gang]]. Valance had been tracking the group for months, and had started a fight with them in the bar. Getting knocked onto the ground outside, Valance threatened the gang members, who responded with another threat of death. One member said that their boss wanted Valance alive, but the leader said the boss wasn't here so it didn't matter– before a blaster bolt narrowly missed him. Han Solo had arrived to save his old friend Valance.

A [[Skirmish on Guat'a|blasterfight]] immediately erupted, as Solo and Valance took cover from the Zantarrk Gang. Valance told Solo that this is the group he'd told him about previously, but hoped Han would've brought New Republic reinforcements, to which Han let him know that this was a personal, non-official mission. Still fired upon and pinned down, Solo told Valance to aim for the bar's generator, which Valance did with his [[hand cannon]]s. This caused a huge explosion, stunned the Zantarrk Gang, and allowed the two rogues to run back to the ''Millennium Falcon'', wondering how the Gang had suddenly become so powerful, when just before this they were nobodies.<ref name="SW1" />

===Perilous Pirrosi===
{{Quote|How can you be so sure, Mon? Pirros was once a longtime member of the previous Republic. They have a storied"''<br/>''"I am well aware of the history, my friend&mdash;but those Pirrosi leaders are long dead. And their successors are not as&hellip;eager to negotiate.|Leia Organa and Mon Mothma before meeting with the Pirrosi delegation on Chandrila|<ref name="SW1" />}}
[[File:AlaytiaIntroducesKezrin-2025StarWars1.jpg|thumb|right|240px|Councilor Alaytia introduces the Pirrosi delegation]]
At the same time, [[Pirrosi delegation|delegates]] from Fenril sector world Pirros were on [[Chandrila]] to negotiate about an [[Unidentified trade summit|upcoming trade summit]] the New Republic and themselves would be attending. [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] discussed their concerns about current generation Pirrosi being much less willing to collaborate than older generations, just before they entered the room [[Prime Minister]] [[Kezrin Almovar]] and the [[Prefect]]s were seated in. Councilor [[Kith Alaytia]] introduced the delegation, and Almovar expressed her honor to be there, but regretfully informed Organa that she and her planet did not wish to join the New Republic, whom they saw as a desperate bully.<ref name="SW1" />

Leia responded fiercely with Mothma's support, the two still wanting Pirros and its neighbors to join into their Republic for their mutual benefit. Scared by the New Republic's inability to defend its own supply lines, Almovar refused to join the New Republic and refused to admit them to the important trade summit that was on the horizon. Instead, she introduced the [[Fenril Consortium]], a loose coalition of planets in the Fenril Sector, and expressed that she finds the New Republic far too weak after the Battle of Jakku and [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard|Adelhard]]'s chaotic actions – and mocks Kith Alaytia for her former Imperial allegiance. Leia brushed this all off as gossip, and told Almovar that she should be wary of the enemies she makes, which the Pirrosi Minister took as a threat, which Leia denied.<ref name="SW1" />

[[File:NecrimsWake-2025StarWars1.png|thumb|280px|left|''Nechrim's Wake'' entering into the Fenril sector]]
After receiving news that the New Republic was formally expelled from the trade summit, Leia discussed the situation with [[Agent]] [[Bastion (agent)|Bastion]], who confirmed that Pirros and its allies wanted nothing to do with the Republic. Then, in a hologram, Almovar told them that the New Republic was inessential to [[the galaxy]], that the Fenril sector has now formally united, and that New Republic vessels were not allowed in their space. Should they be found there, Almovar said the response from Consortium ships would be lethal force. As this was declared, [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Nechrim's Wake]]'' was entering the Fenril sector, and was cornered by starships who told it to leave immediately. The ''Wake'''s crew was confused, [[Finch|one member]] expressing that the sector's planets had never been this aggressive before. Right after this, the vessels opened fire on the corvette and other vessels,<ref name="SW2" /> forcing the ''Wake'' to crash land on the planet [[Loradil]].<ref name="SW3" /><ref name="SW1" />

==The crisis==
===Check-in on Chandrila===
{{Quote|What are you suggesting?"''<br/>''"Not suggesting, more of a hunch&mdash;but it feels like our enemies are just ''starting'' to get into position.|Mon Mothma and Leia Organa|<ref name="SW2" />}}
[[File:MonMothmaAndLeia-2025StarWars2.jpg|thumb|left|230px|Mon Mothma and Leia discuss Luke Skywalker's whereabouts]]
On Chandrila, Organa and Mon Mothma were informed by Rynn Zenat via hologram that Luke Skywalker had gone missing, looking shaken and running off immediately after waking up. Rynn expressed her confusion at this, but Leia reassured her that if Luke was leaving, there was a good reason for him to do so. Chancellor Mothma then interrupted this to remind Organa that there were more pressing matters at hand, even though Zenat's story had worried her. Organa was still grappling with the loss of ''Nechrim's Wake'' and other trading vessels without even a moment to respond. Concerning Prime Minister Almovar's move to create the Fenril Consortium, and the chaos in their sector, Leia stated that she thinks their enemies are still in the early phases of whatever. plan this is.<ref name="SW2" />

===Gone to Gadrilam===
{{Quote|That pirate shared something before he died. Something I couldn't ignore.|Luke Skywalker to R2-D2 upon landing on Gadrilam|<ref name="SW2" />}}
[[File:LukeOnGadrilam-2025StarWars2.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Luke Skywalker lands on Gadrilam]]
Arriving on the snowy world of Gadrilam, Luke apologized to [[R2-D2]] about the promptness and strangeness of [[Mission to Gadrilam|his actions]] as he rushed off towards a [[Unidentified village (Gadrilam)|village]], thinking of what the pilot had told him about: a crown that would reveal the truth, held by Ibo Truddispal. Thinking back to his past readings of [[The Journals of Ben Kenobi|Obi-Wan Kenobi's journals]], Luke recalled the [[Crown of Verity]], an artifact that, when worn, would cause the user to only be able to speak the absolute truth– but it was thought to be destroyed.<ref name="SW2" />

In the village, Luke entered into a [[Unidentified bar (Gadrilam)|bar]] filled with patrons, many of whom were Nagai. Luke dramatically entered and removed his hood, which disrupted the bar's atmosphere, and spoke with the [[bartender]], [[Hossalana]]. The gloomy bartender was dismissive of Luke asking about Truddispal, but he was answered by the Nagai woman sitting next to him, [[Jesrit]]– who spotted that Luke was a Jedi. Angry, Hossalana screamed that they should leave the bar, as [[Gallana Venk]] and her gang entered the establshment, letting Luke know that they'd found his ship, and know he's a New Republic pilot on some kind of mission. Jesrit tried to leave as Luke asked her about Truddispal, which made Venk's men grab him, wondering why Luke was looking for him. Responding with the Force, Luke pushed them back, and took out his lightsaber, to everyone's surprise.<ref name="SW2" />

[[File:LukeInTunnel-2025StarWars2.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Luke Skywalker enters the tunnel on Gadrilam]]
Chaos ensued as the hostile Nagai charged at Skywalker, and Jesrit suggested that he should leave, but Luke hadn't come here to run. A battle commenced, and Skywalker quickly dispatched with his enemies, analyzing their body language and using their own tells against them. Eliminating them and incredibly determined, Skywalker scared off Venk and the rest of her men, as Luke pleaded with Jesrit to stay and help him. She had called Truddispal a smuggler, which Luke had never said, meaning she knew him. Jesrit tried to explain herself as just a simple laborer, but Luke, sensing a darkness on Gadrilam, requested her help once more. The two then went into the nearby woods by torchlight, Jesrit guiding Luke to the tunnel where Truddispal was held. Skywalker asked what Jesrit was doing on the barren world, to which she told him that she isn't there by choice, and was brought there with many of her people, the Nagai. Luke was confused, because Venk is also a Nagai, but Jesrit asserted that she is not truly one of them, and is a traitor to the followers of Garlev. Luke then told Jesrit his name, and tells her he wants to help, as it's what Jedi do. As they arrived at the tunnel, Jesrit told Luke that her people are forced laborers on Gadrilam, and that they need help, if he wants to– and, ever the Jedi, of course he does.<ref name="SW2" />

Sent into the tunnel alone by Jesrit, Luke felt different. The pilot knew far too much for this to be a normal situation, not like false artifacts Luke had been fooled by before. Going deeper, Luke overheard Venk and her men speaking about him, and approached, lightsaber lit. The hostile Nagai were surrounding a cowering and injured Ibo Truddispal, and Gallana Venk tossed down the silver object she was holding– the Crown of Verity. Luke rushed forward and caught it, as Venk spoke ill Garlev's followers, frustrated at how prevalent they are. Venk then fired at Truddispal from point-blank range, killing the Jedi relic collector. As her and her men left, she introduced Luke to members of the Zantarrk Gang, who emerged from the other side of the tunnel. Luke cut them down and ran away after one of them revealed that they took out the village, which he found to be true upon emerging from the tunnel's depths and seeing the fiery blaze.<ref name="SW2" />

===Cloud City conundrum===
{{Quote|I need to talk to&hellip;''Lando Calrissian''&hellip; I have to find&hellip;Han&hellip;|Garlev to a group of Bespin Wing Guards on Cloud City, after crashing.|<ref name="SW3" />}}
[[File:GarlevOnCloudCity-2025-StarWars3.jpg|thumb|right|220px|An injured Garlev arrives on Cloud City]]
In the lavender skies of Bespin, a vessel streaked through the sky as it left twin trails of smoke behind it, falling towards [[Cloud City]]. A group of [[Bespin Wing Guard]]s spotted it, and were confused by the fact that the rapidly descending vessel was unidentified, in communications and in model. Nobody recognized what it was, and one guard remarked that the way it was flying didn't make it look like it was in for a safe landing. Alarmed, the guards immediately requested a crew go out to landing platform [[East Two-Eleven]], where the ship unceremoniously crashed and exploded. As the Wing Guard approached the smoldering wreckage, an injured and eyepatched Nagai emerged. This was Garlev, leader of Nagai revolutionaries, and he wanted to speak with [[Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and find Han Solo.<ref name="SW3" />

[[File:HanValanceDate-2025StarWars3.jpg|thumb|left|230px|Beilert Valance and Han Solo argue over drinks on Suboreen]]
After their debacle on Guat'a, Han Solo and Beilert Valance sat together, "off the grid", in a bar on [[Suboreen]]. Solo was now concerned that this situation was more serious than he first suspected, which Valance interpreted as complaining. All of Valance's sources were surprised by the Zantarrk's emergence into the galactic underworld, as they crept in from past the [[Southern Reaches]] with gear and Imperial hardware that indicated both financial resources and connections. As Solo questioned how the Zantarrk got the weapons they have, Valance was jokingly critical of the former smuggler. This struck a nerve with the Corellian, who knocked over the cyborg's drink and aggressively told his ally that he's not ashamed of his old lifestyle, and did what he needed to to survive. Just after the speech, Han received a call– it was Lando Calrissian, telling him that their old friend Garlev had crashed on Cloud City with information that was reportedly important for the New Republic, but it looked like somebody didn't want Garlev to make it there alive.<ref name="SW3" />

[[File:GarlevsFuneral-2025StarWars3.jpg|thumb|right|210px|Garlev's funeral]]
Solo and Valance then quickly traveled to Cloud City, where Calrissian welcomed them– Solo reflecting on his previous experience there. Garlev's condition worsening rapidly, the group departs to see him as Han and Lando remember the last time they saw the man, when he was smuggling his people through the Moraysian sector. To the mens' horror, upon entering the infirmary room, both the nurses, the [[2-1B surgical droid|medical droid]], and Garlev had been freshly shot dead by blaster. Springing into action, Calrissian, Solo, Valance, and a Wing Guard set off chasing an unidentified presence towards the platforms. In the city's pristine hallways, the men encountered a masked, running figure, and unleashed a volley of blaster bolts as the being sprinted away from them. Solo, Wanting to solve the mystery of Garlev's death, ran full speed into the stranger, taking them down to the floor, before getting elbowed and kicked off. The mysterious figure is just barely able to reach a small ship on the platform under constant blasterfire, and escaped into the evening sky.<ref name="SW3" />

In a room on Cloud City, Han Solo, Lando, and Valance presided over a [[Funeral of Garlev|makeshift funeral]] for the legendary Garlev. Solo acknowledged the complicated aspects of the Nagai's character, in that he was a criminal and a killer. But he also acknowledged that the men presiding over that very funeral were the same thing in the past. Solo spoke of Garlev's dedication to his family and his people, and made it clear that he was going to find out what the dying rogue desperately needed to tell them.<ref name="SW3" />

But Han picked up the hologram while he and Valance were sprinting away from a group of Zantarrk stormtroopers blasting them. Stopping to answer the call, Han told Leia that Oskure had captured Rynn and Jesrit, and that he doesn't know where Luke is but he's not particularly worried about that. As Han hinted that Venk had been killed as well, an explosion went off behind the two men– prompting an end to the call as Han asked for some New Republic help and ran off toward Bastion's location. Worried for her husband, for her brother, and for the New Republic, Leia held her son and questioned what Han had done. Some time later, in a hangar near a CR90 corvette, Organa begged Mon Mothma to help her bring them home. Mon told her that her place was there on Chandrila, and that the Republic needed her– governing thousands with a word. Leia still wanted to go save her family, but Mothma was able to convince her to stay. Though she wasn't going to go, Leia placed Alaytia in charge of the Republic's response, walking away with Ben and C-3PO.<ref name="SW9" />

In the barren wastes of Nagi, Luke and Tuula looked into a valley, a valley she knew from her time there as a child. They descended the cliff, and Tuula found a disruption in the soil, allowing her to track their enemies to Reyna Oskure. Oskure, at that moment, was escorting the captured Zenat and Jesrit to a shuttle, guarded by Zantarrk stormtroopers. Jesrit mocked Oskure, calling her a coward, to which Oskure said that it was a strategic retreat, but that she admired the Nagai's defiance. Zenat, growing sicker, was coughing as Jesrit exclaimed that she was dying, which Oskure confirmed. But the Anzati also said that the "meal" would keep her "content" on her journey home. Concurrently, Solo and Valance had met up with Bastion and continued to flee the Zantarrk. Under fire and taking cover, Han asked if Bastion had a trace on Oskure, and just as she got it, the legendary agent was shot in the head, collapsing backwards in Valance's arms, dying. Saying her death was not in vain as he managed to get Oskure's location from a device on Bastion's arm, Valance used a smoke grenade to distract the stormtroopers. The two then escaped into the woods, as the cyborg said that joining the Rebellion, marrying Leia, and having Ben did indeed make Han soft– but it also made him honorable.<ref name="SW9" />

[[File:NewRepublic-Fleet-2025StarWars9.jpg|thumb|right|180px|The New Republic's fleet arrives at Nagi to break the blockade]]
In orbit over Nagi, the Zantarrk gang's fleet, many vessels manned by Nagai, blockaded the rocky world. Onboard one ship, Nagai captain [[Stannix]] received the news that Gallana Venk had died from a crewman, to which he asked what their new orders were. As they had no orders, Stannix said they would hold the blockade until they hear otherwise. But suddenly, the crewman pointed out that a fleet of vessels was coming in from all sides. A New Republic fleet, led by Alaytia, consisting of a [[Defender-class cruiser|''Defender''-class cruiser]], at least two [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate|escort frigates]], several corvettes, and many starfighters arrived over Nagi. After speaking to both [[Preeti]] and [[Jarek Yeager]], Alaytia broadcasted to the Fenril Consortium's blockade, peacefully asking them to stand down. She informed them that Almovar and Venk were dead, and that Nagai and its people were now members of the New Republic and will be defended as such.<ref name="SW9" />

Back on the surface, Oskure's shuttle was almost done being loaded as Zenat mocked the witch, calling her a weak pretender. Angered by this, Oskure choked Zenat, lifting her off the ground and telling her that she would soon bask in her victory. Then, a blaster bolt flew right past Oskure's face to her shock. Skywalker, Solo, Valance, and Tuula, reunited, stood on a balcony looking down at Oskure. A new battle had begun as the stormtroopers unleashed a volley of fire at the New Republic heroes, who had the high ground. Valance, Tuula, and Solo responded with blasts of their own as Oskure drained more of Rynn's life force, but the pilot managed to knee the Anzati, stunning her. Luke noticed this from above, and slashed stormtroopers with his lightsaber as he dove down to face Oskure. She'd been waiting for this, and accepted the young Jedi's challenge. The Anzati witch dove in with her claws out, slicing Luke and seeking to feast on his life force. She knocked his lightsaber out of his hand, and asked him if he'd made peace with his past and future. He then said that he'd made peace with who he was, and that his past is the Jedi's past, and that was also his future. Using the Force, Luke held Oskure back, pushing her to the wall as he held her still and approached her.<ref name="SW9" />

[[File:OskureVsSkywalker-2025StarWars9.jpg|thumb|left|260px|Oskure and Skywalker finally face off]]
Luke then put the Crown of Verity on Reyna's head, causing her to scream as she was now only able to speak the absolute truth. Just before she began to speak, Oskure secretly pressed a button on a remote behind her back, and told Luke that if he wanted the "truth", that she, a creature of lies and shadows, would reveal it. She confessed to forcing the Nagai peasants to haphazardly assemble a fleet made of Imperial scraps, but in a way that would specifically ensure victory for herself. To Luke's horror, Oskure then said that the truth would set her free, and prove to be his final resting place. Then, to the shock of Stannix in orbit, the fleet above Nagi suddenly detonated, causing massive explosions and scaring New Republic pilots. Nagi had now become a death trap.<ref name="SW9" /> New Republic pilots in orbit dodged and fought to eliminate debris from the detonated fleet, some of it managing to fall to the surface, threatening the Nagai. There, the standoff between Skywalker and Oskure ended with the Anzati throwing off the Crown of Verity as wreckage from the fleet rained.<ref name="SW10" />

===Final fight===
{{Quote|You sought truth, all of you&mdash;and now you must bear witness to the destruction of the very people you hoped to save!"''<br/>''"The whole world's coming ''apart''!|Reyna Oskure and Han Solo as the sky rains debris.|<ref name="SW10" />}}
Back on Chandrila, a communication from the Pirrosi reached Organa and Mothma. Consul [[Bardi (pirros)|Bardi]] was calling to ask for help after the death of Prime Minister Almovar at the hands of the treacherous Oskure. They were turning to the New Republic as a people, disgusted with Oskure's actions, using their people and forces to oppress the Nagai and their uprising. Humbly, Bardi asked the New Republic to eliminate the threat that was Oskure, in order to maintain the balance of power in the galaxy. Leia, always willing to make an ally, agreed to help. Mothma believed that this was because of Bastion's death, but Organa explained that it was about more than that– she wanted to fight for peace, protecting what they've built. Mothma reassured Organa, and the two set off to do good.<ref name="SW10" />

[[File:ReynasForces-Nagi-2025StarWars10.jpg|thumb|right|230px|Reyna Oskure commands her allied forces on the surface of Nagi]]
On Nagi, Skywalker and his allies dodged raining fiery debris, but realized that the damage was not going to be as bad as Oskure intended because of their fleet's actions. Close by, Oskure was running away before she was shot in the back, falling down to see the one holding the blaster: Jesrit. Oskure taunted the Nagai follower of Garlev, but Jesrit wouldn't hear it, certain that Garlev's dream would become reality. But as they spoke, Oskure's combined remaining ground forces arrived! Tof soldiers led by [[Draviil]], Zantarrk pirates, and traitorous Nagai stood against Jesrit, and Oskure commanded them to finish the Nagai rebellion off. Draviil  struck Jesrit in the face with his blaster, knocking her down, and taunted her about her previous confident words. Rising back up, Jesrit kicked Draviil's leg backward, breaking it and causing the hulking alien to fall to the ground.<ref name="SW10" />

Skywalker, Valance, Solo, Jesrit, and Tuula fought Oskure's forces fiercely, as Luke helped Rynn Zenat to limp away, her condition worsening even more. Skywalker wanted to help her, but she begged Luke to stop Oskure and end the fighting first. Solo noticed that they were outnumbered, and Tuula said that they desperately needed to stop Oskure to ensure the Nagai would have lasting freedom. Picking a Nagai traitor up by his collar, Jesrit questioned why they would betray their own people, to which the Nagai said they were just following orders for wealth and power promised by Gallana Venk. Dropping the Nagai, Jesrit reminded them that for these promises, they've received nothing in return. Close by, feeling every ache and pain, Zenat crawled on the ground to grab the Crown of Verity while Luke defended her with his lightsaber, as Oskure approached her ship to escape.<ref name="SW10" />

[[File:FreeNagi-2025StarWars10.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Mon Mothma and Leia Organa arrive as Jesrit addresses the Nagai]]
Then, while the battle raged, a New Republic transport accompanied by two X-Wings landed– and Mon Mothma and Leia Organa emerged to announce that the New Republic stood with the Nagai. The fighting ceased upon this declaration, the combatants looking at the arriving dignitaries. Jesrit took this opportunity to address the crowd– saying that they now stood on the precipice of true peace, a free Nagi, and freedom from mainpulators, monsters, and their lackeys. Leia denounced the Fenril Consortium as a shadow government run by Oskure, and stated that the New Republic stands with Nagi as a free, independent world they will help rebuild. Jesrit spoke of unity amongst Nagai, united in Garlev's dream, and the enemy Nagai removed their salvaged Imperial trooper helmets, cheering the phrase "in Garlev's name", raising their fists to the sky.<ref name="SW10" />

===The sorceress succumbs===
{{Quote|Your people&mdash;the ''Anzati''&mdash;drain the life force of their victims. But that's not all.<br/>You leave a piece of yourself in them. A ''sickness'' that you carry. A ''darkness'' that can consume you&hellip;but the ''crown'' has shown me this&hellip;and I can send your own ''darkness'' back and restore myself!|Rynn Zenat to Reyna Oskure|<ref name="SW10" />}}
[[File:Reyna-Oskure-Defeated-2025StarWars10.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Reyna Oskure falls as Rynn Zenat sends her darkness back into the Anzati]]
As the Nagai celebrated, a weak Zenat was reunited with Preeti and the two kissed, just before Zenat departed with Skywalker, Valance, and Solo to finish end Oskure's terror for good. At her ship, Oskure told her soldiers that their sacrifice would not be forgotten, as she intended to leave them behind. That is, until Skywalker and his friends arrived, attacking the vampiric witch. Oskure was able to knock Skywalker down while her guards were killed, but Zenat emerged from behind, placing the Crown of Verity this time on her own head. Rynn's eyes glowed, and she explained that the Crown allows the wearer to not only see the truth, but all that they've left behind. Grabbing Oskure, Zenat told the ancient Anzati that she'd taken something from her, a piece of her that's helped her destroy so many lives in the Fenril sector. Oskure mocked Zenat's [[Spicerunner|spice runner]] background, but the pilot continued, telling Oskure that she'd left something behind inside of her.<ref name="SW10" />

Energy released from the two women as Zenat forced this piece back into Oskure, the sorceress's skin growing more and more wrinkly as she begged to be left alone. Zenat exclaimed that the crown revealed to her that Anzati leave a piece of themselves in their victims, sickening them, and she was now sending it back. This began to restore Zenat as well, who became somewhat mad with power, taunting the dying witch– to Skywalker's dismay. He begged her to stop, to not lash out for revenge, and it was Solo's hand on Zenat's shoulder that finally stopped her, as he reminded her that she had won, and shouldn't drag herself down with Oskure. The crumpled, old body of Reyna Oskure then fell to the ground, barely clinging to life, as Rynn removed the Crown.<ref name="SW10" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|The New Republic is committed to helping rebuild Nagi&mdash;as an independent world, ruled by its own people."''<br/>''"Not as a proxy state&mdash;or as a pawn."''<br/>''"As ''Garlev'' intended. As I will ensure.|Leia Organa, Mon Mothma, and Jesrit looking upon the rebuilding of Nagi.|<ref name="SW10" />}}
[[File:Victory-on-Nagi-2025StarWars10.jpg|thumb|left|170px|Victory and a new dawn on Nagi]]
Some time later, Rynn asked Luke if it was really over, to which the Jedi said that it was, for now– but there was always a potential challenge waiting around the corner. In the city of Siva, the New Republic heroes celebrated and gathered with the Nagai, as they began clearing debris and rebuilding. There, Leia reassured Jesrit that the New Republic was committed to helping the Nagai rebuild their world as a new independent Nagi, which Jesrit said was what Garlev intended. The young Nagai leader thanked Luke and his friends, and Luke clarified that he was just keeping his promise from Gadrilam. Organa told Jesrit she hoped this was the beginning of a strong partnership, and Jesrit told her she hoped for the same, and that she had a lot to learn from Leia in how she unified her people. But Organa told the young woman her own words only nudged along what Jesrit had done. Pulling Organa away from the conversation, Han had a question for his wife: why had they been apart for so long? Zenat saw this and all the rest of it, and reflected on the moment of happiness with Luke, who told her that these moments were incredibly important. The Jedi said these moments were what keep one moving through the dark times, even when the fight feels endless: moments of peace and happiness like this give one life.<ref name="SW10" />
