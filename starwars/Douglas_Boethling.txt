'''Douglas Boethling''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] video game producer who has worked on a number of ''[[Star Wars]]'' and ''Star Wars''&ndash;related projects. Boethling was first hired by [[LucasArts]] in [[August]] [[2012]] as a "Quality Assurance Tester,"<ref name="LinkedIn" /> though he would leave LucasArts less than a year later in [[April]] of [[2013]], not credited with any major involvements during this brief period.<ref name="LinkedIn" /> He would go on to work with [[Wikipedia:Sony Interactive Entertainment|Sony Interactive Entertainment America]] for a little under a year, before being brought onto the [[Lucasfilm Games]] team in [[February]] [[2014]].<ref name="LinkedIn Experience">{{LinkedIn|url=in/dboethling/details/experience/|text=Douglas B. Experience|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/HmMeE}}</ref> During his first few years with the team, Boethling served as the Lead QA Brand Tester on games including ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'',<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref> ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'',<ref name="LEGO TFA">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'', and the ''Star Wars'' portions of ''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]''.<ref name="LinkedIn Experience" />

In [[July]] [[2016]], he was promoted to Associate Producer, going on to work heavily on ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Challenges]]'' and its subsequent expansions, as well as continuing to manage with ''Star Wars: Commander''. He would also go on to produce other titles including ''[[Star Wars: Force Arena]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Puzzle Droids]]'', the ''[[Garmin vívofit jr. 2: Star Wars Themed Activity Tracker]]'' and mobile app, and the ''Star Wars''&ndash;related elements of both ''[[Disney Emoji Blitz]]'' and ''[[LINE: Disney Tsum Tsum]]''. As of [[May]] [[2021]], Boethling has been promoted to full Producer, leading production on ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'',<ref name="LEGO SS">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''</ref> as well as serving as both "Producer I" and "Producer II" on ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' and other ongoing ''Star Wars'' mobile titles.<ref name="LinkedIn" />
