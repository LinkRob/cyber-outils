'''272 BBY''', or '''272<ref name="Lourna Birth" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] of the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]] that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]]. [[Lourna Dee]], a [[Twi'lek]] from the [[Planet|world]] [[Aaloth]] who eventually became a [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]],<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was [[Reproduction|born]] around that year.<ref name="Lourna Birth" />

==Behind the scenes==
