'''Cod''' was a type of [[fish]] with [[Color|white]] [[Foodstuff|meat]]. It was used as an ingredient in [[Crait Crusted Cod]], a dish created by the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] to showcase the [[salt]] from the [[planet]] [[Crait]]. The cod was baked in the salt until the meat browned, after which the salt crust was shattered before the dish was served. Tuggs described the meal as moist and tender if baked to perfection and included a recipe for it in his second cook[[book]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'', which was released at some point after his first [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]<ref name="LDCookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cod was introduced to ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2021]] ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish uses fillets of [[Wikipedia:Cod|cod]].<ref name="LDCookbook" />
