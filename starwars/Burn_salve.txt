'''Burn salve''' was a medicinal ointment for treating burns<ref name="BTC" /> and soothing inflammations.<ref name="Insider 190" /> It could be dispensed as an [[Color|ocher]] gel that came in tubes<ref name="BTC" /> and was commonly found among basic first aid equipment in elementary [[Medpac|medikits]] such as [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] medpacs.<ref name="Insider 190" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> the Imperial [[stormtrooper]] [[Thara Nyende]] brought supplies for some patrons at her [[Thara Nyende's uncle|uncle's]] [[Thara Nyende's uncle's cantina|cantina]] on the [[planet]] [[Sullust]]. The [[cantina]] owner called the diminutive [[Sullustan]] [[Myan]] over to hand him a tube of [[Salve|burn salve]] to aid some injured workers from [[dorm four]].<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Burn salve first appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
