The '''Darnaburny Civil War''' was an event during the [[High Republic Era]] that took place in the vicinity of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Darnaburny system]] by around [[160 BBY]]. The [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] of the [[Jedi Order]] took part in the civil war, which [[Jedi Knight]] [[Indara]] came to learn about. While Indara searched for Rwoh around that [[Standard year|year]], she wondered if the Mirialan would tell her stories about the Darnaburny Civil War.

==The civil war==
By around [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[High Republic Era]], the Darnaburny Civil War<ref name="Wayseeker" /> took place in the vicinity<ref name="Battlenamia" /> of the [[Darnaburny system]], which was located in the [[Kanz sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="Appendix" /> [[Vernestra Rwoh]]&mdash;a [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]]&mdash;participated in the event.<ref name="Wayseeker" />

==Aftermath==
[[File:Wayseeker-Cropped.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Indara (right) wondered if Vernestra Rwoh (left) would tell her stories of when she took part in the Darnaburny Civil War.]]
The Jedi [[Indara]] eventually learned of Rwoh's role in the Darnaburny Civil War<ref name="Wayseeker" /> by around 160 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> and considered it a historical and tragic event.<ref name="Wayseeker" /> Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> as Indara&mdash;by then a [[Jedi Knight]] and [[Jedi archivist]]&mdash;traveled to find the now [[Jedi Master]] Rwoh at the request of the [[Jedi High Council]], she thought about events the Mirialan had been involved in, including the Darnaburny Civil War, and wondered whether she would share stories with her about them.<ref name="Wayseeker" />

The Jedi Knight noted it was one thing to learn about the event from Jedi who had not taken part in it such as Jedi Masters [[Yoda]] and [[Oppo Rancisis]], but as Rwoh had personally been involved in it she would surely speak with more expertise. Indara then considered whether, because Rwoh's [[species]] lived far longer than [[human]]s, she had come to view such events with the quiet detachment of age.<ref name="Wayseeker" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Darnaburny Civil War was mentioned in ''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]'', a [[2025]] novel written by [[Justina Ireland]].<ref name="Wayseeker" />
