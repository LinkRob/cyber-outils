'''"Ember"''' was the nickname of a<ref name="ALR" /> [[human]] who served as a [[First Order Special Forces TIE fighter pilot|pilot]]<ref name="SFM" /> in the [[First Order Special Forces]].<ref name="Special" /> "Ember" [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|piloted]] a [[Color|red]] [[TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor|TIE/ba interceptor]] and wore a matching [[First Order]] [[TIE series|TIE]] [[First Order TIE pilot flight suit|flight suit]].<ref name="SFM" /> At some point, the [[pilot]] flew alongside two [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s near a red [[nebula]].<ref name="X-wing">{{FFGXW2|set=Major Vonreg's TIE Expansion Pack|cardname="Ember"}}</ref> They<ref name="ALR" /> had light skin, brown [[hair]], and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="SFM" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Ember" was first mentioned in "A Legend Reborn," an article on [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' official website detailing a then-upcoming expansion for the ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'' miniatures game on [[January 8]], [[2020]].<ref name="ALR" /> The expansion, named ''[[Major Vonreg's TIE Expansion Pack]]'', was released on [[January 31]] that year<ref name="Major Vonreg Release">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2020/1/31/available-now-january-24-1|text=Available Now &ndash; January 31|date=2020-01-31}}</ref> and included a card representing "Ember."<ref name="X-wing" /> Their first appearance was in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Starfighter Missions]]'',<ref name="SFM" /> which was released on [[November 19]], 2020.<ref name="Release">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gamerbraves.com/star-wars-starfighter-missions-launches-november-for-japan-korea-and-sea|text=''Star Wars: Starfighter Missions'' launches November for Japan, Korea and SEA|author=Kit|date=2020-11-11|archivedate=20210617074707}}</ref> In ''Starfighter Missions'', "Ember's" TIE/ba [[interceptor]] was available in two additional color schemes: black with blue accents, and a blue-and-teal scheme.<ref name="SFM" />
