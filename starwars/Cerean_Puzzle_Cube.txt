The '''Cerean Puzzle Cube''' was a cube-shaped puzzle used for entertainment, easier to solve than the similar [[5x5 Puzzle Cube]]. It was well-known across [[the galaxy]] and even [[youngling]]s were known to solve it.<ref name="5x5" /> It shared its name with the [[Cerean]] [[species]] native to the [[planet]] [[Cerea]],<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|cerean|Cerean}}</ref> located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]].<ref name="Cerea">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Cerean Puzzle Cube was mentioned in a Crew Run in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', published by [[Kabam]],<ref name="SWU">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> prior to the game's shutdown in [[2016]].<ref name="Shutting down">{{Kabam|subdomain=community|url=forums/showthread.php?675751-Star-Wars-Uprising-is-Shutting-Down|text=Discussion Thread: Star Wars Uprising is Shutting Down|date=2016-09-22}}</ref> The [[Cerean Puzzle Cube/Legends|Cerean Puzzle Cube]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as a purchasable item in the [[2002]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' adventure ''[[Shadows in Green]]'', written by [[Lee Pickler]] as part of the larger [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign by [[Wizards of the Coast]].<ref name="SiG">{{LivingForce|Shadows in Green}}</ref>
