'''Aki-Cake''' was a type of [[cake]] associated with the [[Aki-Aki]], a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Pasaana]]. The Aki-Cake was one of the delectable [[Foodstuff|sweets]] sold at the [[Festival of the Ancestors]] held on Pasaana's [[Forbidden Valley]], and was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="LEGO TSS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aki-Cake first appeared in a side quest titled "Speedy Sweets" in the [[2022]] [[Canon|non-canonical]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''.<ref name="LEGO TSS" />
