'''Bozeden Jeems''' was a [[human]] [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Imperial officer|officer]] who served as [[Inspector General]] and oversaw the protection of the [[Scarif vault|data vault]] in the [[Imperial Center of Military Research]] on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]]. His role made him aware of many of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s secrets.

Jeems's ambitions of becoming [[Deputy Director]] were dashed in [[1 BBY]] when a [[Rogue One|group]] of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] managed to [[Battle of Scarif|steal]] the [[Death Star plans|plans]] to the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] from the [[Data vault|vault]], after which the entire facility was destroyed by the [[Death Star]]'s [[Mk I Superlaser|superlaser]].

==Biography==
Bozeden Jeems was a [[human]] [[Imperial officer|officer]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]<ref name="UVG" /> and so was aware of many of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s closely guarded secrets.<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> An up-and-coming [[Inspector General]] based in the [[Imperial Center of Military Research]] on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]], Jeems was responsible for defending the secrets of the [[weapon]]s development carried out by [[Imperial Intelligence]].<ref name="UVG" /> This included overseeing the protection of the [[Scarif vault|data vault]] on Scarif<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> which contained the [[Death Star plans|plans]] to the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]].<ref name="UVG" />

[[File:Citadel_data_vault.png|thumb|left|300px|Jeems failed to protect the Scarif data vault.]]
Jeems hoped to achieve the position of [[Deputy Director]], but these ambitions dissipated when a [[Rogue One|group]] of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] [[Battle of Scarif|infiltrated]] the [[Data vault|vault]] on Scarif<ref name="UVG" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Jeems fiercely scrambled [[Scarif Deployment|forces]] in response,<ref name="Geektionary" /> but the efforts were in vain,<ref name="UVG" /> as the rebels succeeded in stealing the [[Death Star]] plans and transmitting them to the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance fleet]]. The Imperial Center of Military Research was then destroyed by the [[Mk I Superlaser|superlaser]] of the superweapon itself.<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
When deploying [[troop]]s after the breaching of the data vault on Scarif, Jeems was more concerned with salvaging his career than recovering the valuable military data. He had light skin, [[Color|brown]] [[hair]], and blue [[eye]]s.<ref name="UVG" />

==Equipment==
Jeems wore an [[Imperial officer's uniform]] that made him look every bit a [[soldier]] who had been promised a great career in the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Geektionary" /> His white [[Clothing|tunic]] bore a [[code cylinder]] on each [[breast]] and a [[rank insignia plaque]] showing three red pips and then one blue. His black [[Imperial kepi]] had an [[officer's disk]] as did the silver buckle of his black [[belt]], on which two silver storage cases hung above his black pants.<ref name="UVG" />
