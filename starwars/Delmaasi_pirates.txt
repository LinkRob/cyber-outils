The '''Delmaasi pirates'''<ref name="Rise" /> originated from the [[Delmaas system]]<ref name="Rise" /><ref name="Planetnamia" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> The group was active during the latter [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Republic Era]]. After the [[Clone Wars]] [[First Battle of Geonosis|erupted]],<ref name="Rise" /> fought from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] had supported [[Pirate|piracy]] and [[privateer]]ing organizations, although secretly as numerous Separatist [[Planet|worlds]] had suffered from pirate raids for [[Standard century|centuries]]. Offering government-issued letters of marque into their [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval force]], [[Head of State]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] neglected capturing those that opposed the [[Galactic Republic]], including the Delmaasi pirates. He instead contracted them through signed terms to exclusively target the Republic and even agreed to purchase their stolen materiel from the [[democracy]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Delmaasi pirates were introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the [[Delmaasi pirates/Legends|Delmaasi pirates]] were introduced in ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'', a [[2012]] reference book authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Paul R. Urquhart]].<ref name="EGtW">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
