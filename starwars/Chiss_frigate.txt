A class of '''frigate''' was utilized by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. It was the fifth largest class of warship used by the Ascendancy, behind the [[Heavy cruiser]], and the [[Man-of-war]] classifications.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> One such [[frigate]], the ''[[Midsummer]]'', was part of the [[Xodlak fleet|fleet]] used by the [[Xodlak family]], although it was abandoned on [[Celwis's largest moon]].<ref name="Greater Good" />
