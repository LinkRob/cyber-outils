'''Bronze''' was a type of metal alloy. The [[Bronze Cluster]] of the [[Kalidor Crescent]] used bronze.<ref name="Lead">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> The alloy shared its name with "Bronze Jedi" and "Bronze Saber Design," a [[Clothing|costume]] and [[weapon]] respectively, which were used by the [[Grand Arena]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[J-3DI]]<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date2">{{HuntersDate}}</ref>
