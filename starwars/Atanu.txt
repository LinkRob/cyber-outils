{{Quote|Jan Dodonna was always planning for the future&hellip;he had preliminary scans for possible new rebel bases.''"<br />"''Gimme a look at that&hellip;Atanu, Jabone IV, Lonania Prime, Hoth, Mupin XII?|Leia Organa and Han Solo|<ref name="Star Wars 67">[[Star Wars (2015) 67|''Star Wars'' (2015) 67]]</ref>}}
'''Atanu''' was a location<ref name="Star Wars 67" /> in the [[Atanu system]] within the [[Alderath sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was situated in grid square L-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

Prior to his [[death]]<ref name="Star Wars 67" /> in [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] undertook preliminary scans for a new base of operations. Atanu was among the locations scanned, along with [[Hoth]], [[Jabone IV]], [[Lonania Prime]], and [[Mupin XII]].<ref name="Star Wars 67" /> Around [[2 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars (2015) 67|''Star Wars'' (2015) 67]] to around [[2 ABY]].</ref> Rebel Alliance members [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Luke Skywalker]], and [[Han Solo]] were sent on a reconnaissance mission to the potential locations, including Atanu.<ref name="Star Wars 67" />

==Behind the scenes==
Atanu was mentioned in the [[Star Wars (2015) 67|sixty-seventh issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series. The issue was written by [[Kieron Gillen]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 67" /> on [[June 19]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/71193/star_wars_2015_67|text=Star Wars (2015) #67}}</ref>
