'''B-1 series mercenary sentry droids''' were a variant of [[B1-series battle droid]] that existed in [[the galaxy]] by the early [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Era|Era]].<ref name="Official Trailer swcom">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-trailer-key-art-2|text=Pedro Pascal Stars in New Trailer for ''The Mandalorian and Grogu''}}</ref> A number of these B1 units were members of the [[Droid Gotra]]. After the Gotra was hired to provide protection for [[the Twins]], a number of these [[mercenary]] [[droid]]s patrolled [[Hutt Palace|the Twins' palace]] with their gotra comrades. During the [[Battle of Nal Hutta (Din Djarin)|Battle of Nal Hutta]], several ran past a series of [[hangar]] [[blast door]]s as they opened and [[HH-87 Starhopper]] [[starfighter]]s launched from them, and several were destroyed when one of the fighters later crashed and exploded.<ref name="Mandalorian and Grogu" />

The mercenary sentry droids' weathered plating was a mixture of [[color]]s, including blue, orange, yellow, and dark green, but was predominantly bone white.<ref name="Cardboard Cutout" /> Many B-1s wore tattered [[cape]]s.<ref name="Mandalorian and Grogu" />
