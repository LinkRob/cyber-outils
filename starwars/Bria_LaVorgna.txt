'''Bria LaVorgna''' is a writer for [[StarWars.com]]. She wrote essays for issues of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Age of Republic]]'', [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (comic series)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']], and ''[[Star Wars: Age of Resistance]]''.<ref name="ChaosBria" />
