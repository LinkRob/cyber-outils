'''Club 69''' was a pleasure boutique in [[Doom City]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].<ref name="CB">{{CasusBelliCite|HS24|Il faut sauver Miss Pickalho}}</ref>

A sex shop rather than a brothel, its regular customers included members of the Coruscant élite - although they generally did not shop there in person.<ref name="CB" />

==Behind the scenes==
Club 69 appears in ''[[Il faut sauver Miss Pickalho]]'', a French-language scenario for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' which appeared in ''[[Casus Belli]]'' magazine in March 1999. As this material was not approved through the [[Lucas Licensing]] process, it is considered non-canon.
