'''Ammuud''' was a [[Temperature|cold]] [[planet]]<ref name="HC2" /> located in the [[Ammuud system]] in the [[Corporate Sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The planet saw [[Standard decade|decades]] of fighting between its seven resident family clans, who eventually united to govern Ammuud under the [[Code of Ammuud]].<ref name="HC2" />
