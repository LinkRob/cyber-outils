A '''battle''' occurred near the [[planet]] [[Fanha]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was fought between the [[Iska pirates|Iska]], a [[pirate]] group, and the forces of the Empire, commanded by [[Admiral]] [[Terrinald Screed]]. Screed led the Empire to victory, driving the pirates away from Fanha and forcing them to retreat towards [[Tosste]].<ref name="TSA" />
