'''Derla Pidys''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Argiopid]] who considered herself the finest sommelier in the [[Corporate Sector]]. She visited a [[Ubialla Gheal's nightclub|club]] in [[Canto Bight]] owned by [[Ubialla Gheal]] to purchase a bottle of [[wine]].<ref name="Wine in Dreams" /> Her large head had four [[Color|yellow]] [[eye]]s, two upper and two lower eyes.<ref name="TLJVD" />
