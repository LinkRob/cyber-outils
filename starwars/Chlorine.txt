'''Chlorine''' was a substance found in the soil of the [[planet]] [[Lah'mu]]. The [[boron]], chlorine, [[cobalt]], [[iron]], [[nitrogen]], and [[zinc]] content of the soil made Lah'mu's ground[[water]] unpleasant to drink.<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
