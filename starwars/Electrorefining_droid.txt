The '''electrorefining droid''' was a model of [[Class five droid|fifth-degree]] [[mining droid]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> employed by the [[Techno Union]] to pan for melted [[ore]] in [[Magma|lava]] rivers on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] and carry it to central processing facilities.<ref name="Droids" /> They were protected from boiling lava<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> by heat resistant<ref name="Droids" /> [[carbonite]]-alloy [[armor]] and [[Deflector shield|energy shields]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />
