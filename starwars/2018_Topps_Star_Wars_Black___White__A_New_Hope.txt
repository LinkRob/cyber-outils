'''2018 Topps ''Star Wars'' Black & White: ''A New Hope''''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] on [[February 14]], [[2018]]. Part of the company's Black & White series, it featured 140 black and white photos from {{Film|IV}}, along with a variety of black & white behind-the-scenes cards and autographs.
