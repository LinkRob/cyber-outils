In the [[galactic standard calendar]], '''Benduday''' was the fifth and final [[Standard day|day]] of the [[Standard week|week]]. The day was named for the [[Dai Bendu|Bendu monks]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and immediately followed [[Zhellday]].<ref name="Falcon61">{{BuildFalconCite|61|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>

After [[Rotta|Rotta the Hutt]] became a [[gladiator]] in the [[The Pits (arena)|fighting pits]] of [[Shakari]], [[Painting|posters]] were put up outside the arena to attract spectators.<ref name="TMAG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref> According to the [[Aurebesh]] poster, the fighting pits presented no holds barred "fight nights" with Rotta on "every last Benduday of the [[Standard month|month]]."<ref name="TheDirect">{{WebCite|url=https://thedirect.com/article/star-wars-first-poster-jabba-rotta-hutt|text=Star Wars Releases First-Ever Poster for Jabba the Hutt's Son, Rotta|author= Aeron Mer Eclarinal|date=2026-05-22|work=The Direct|archivedate=20260524020617}}</ref>
