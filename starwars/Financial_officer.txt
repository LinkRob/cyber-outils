'''Financial officer''' was a [[Assignment|position]] in the [[Trade Federation]]. In addition to the typical [[Neimoidian dress]] they wore, a financial [[officer]] also wore a [[Financial officer's collar|special collar]].<ref name="CVD" /> In the [[Galactic Senate Chamber]] during the [[Invasion of Naboo]], when [[Senator]] [[Lott Dod]] argued that a commission needed to be sent to [[Naboo]], a financial officer sat behind him.<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref>
