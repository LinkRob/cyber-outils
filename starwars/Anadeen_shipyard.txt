During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[New Republic]], a '''shipyard''' at [[Anadeen]] was under the control of the Empire. The New Republic fought to gain control of it<ref name="Life Debt" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Anadeen shipyard was first mentioned in the [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', the middle installment of [[Chuck Wendig]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Life Debt" />
