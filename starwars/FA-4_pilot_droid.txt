'''FA-4 pilot droids''' were a model of [[pilot droid]] used during the era of the [[Galactic Republic]].

==Details==
{{Quote|Either way, they became highly valued, not only for their skills in the cockpit, but also for their discretion when it came to keeping their masters' travel plans under wraps.|R0-GR|<ref name="Droidography" />}}
The FA-4 was a [[pilot droid]] model with a thin, spindly frame mounted on a wheeled unit. They had ball heads, and at over two [[meter]]s in height were tall enough to [[pilot]] many [[starship]]s,<ref name="Databank" /> including [[Punworcca 116-class interstellar sloop|''Punworcca 116''-class interstellar sloops]].<ref name="SotG" /> FA-4 units had the unordinary ability to be discreet regarding their masters' travels.<ref name="Droidography" />

==History==
[[File:DookuFA4PilotDroid-AOTC.png|thumb|left|200px|Darth Tyranus and his FA-4.]]
FA-4 [[droid]]s were utilized in the last decades of the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="Databank" /> and during the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[Sith Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]], head of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], owned at least two FA-4 pilot droids, which he used onboard his [[Punworcca 116-class interstellar sloop|''Punworcca 116''-class interstellar sloop]] [[solar sailer]].<ref name="SotG" /> One droid piloted the [[Sith]] from the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]] to the planet [[Coruscant]] after the Separatists' defeat during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref> This droid was destroyed when Dooku [[Skirmish at Vanqor|crashed]] onto the planet [[Vanqor]]. The [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] later held the droid's face plate.<ref name="Dooku Captured">{{TCW|Dooku Captured}}</ref> At some point afterwards Dooku would acquire another of this droid, who flew his new apprentice [[Savage Opress]] to his mission on [[Devaron]] but on [[Toydaria]], Opress piloted the Punworcca swoop himself.<ref name="Monster">{{TCW|Monster}}</ref> Dooku's FA-4 droid drove him to [[Zygerria]],<ref name="Escape from Kadavo">{{TCW|Escape from Kadavo}}</ref> [[Scipio]],<ref name="Crisis at the Heart">{{TCW|Crisis at the Heart}}</ref> [[Oba Diah]],<ref name="The Lost One">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref> and to Coruscant upon his master summoning him.<ref name="Sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref>

FA-4 units also worked on Coruscant itself, and around the time of the [[Battle of Coruscant]], these droids bustled around the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]]<ref name="Databank" /> and the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>
