'''Chromomites''' were a type of technology used to maintain or change the color of the item they were applied to.<ref name="TLJICS">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref><ref name="LJ:TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Characteristics==
[[Resistance]] [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo|Holdo]] frequently dyed her hair different colors with chromomites.<ref name="LJ:TVD" /> The [[SolarNova TT-86]] [[Landspeeder]] made use of alloy plates with integrated chromomites.<ref name="TLJICS" />
