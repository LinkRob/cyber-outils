The '''Crest of Alderaan''' was the emblem of the [[Alderaanian monarchy]]. Shaped like an upside-down triangle made of interlacing curves, it notably adorned several elements of [[Viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa's]] wardrobe,<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> and his daughter [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] had a [[hair]] clasp shaped like it.<ref name="A Princess on Lothal">{{Rebels|A Princess on Lothal}}</ref> It also appeared in the letterhead of mails sent by members of the royal family.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
