'''Devil Squadron''' was a military [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]] squadron that was part of the [[Aquilaean starforce]]. It was composed of at least six fighter wings. General [[Luke Skywalker (The Star Wars)|Luke Skywalker]] commanded the squadron from [[Aquilae (The Star Wars)|Aquilae]] while they attacked the [[Space Fortress]]. The squadron suffered casualties, although not without inflicting the Space Fortress with the same.
