The '''Blood Moon Uprising''' was a conflict which occurred by the [[High Republic Era]]. A [[phrik staff]] from the uprising found its way into the [[Jedi Archives]] and was among many artifacts displayed in the [[Starlight Pavilion]] during the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> in [[231 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> as part of the ''[[Secrets of the Jedi]]'' exhibit curated by [[Jedi archivist]] [[OrbaLin]].<ref name="Rising Storm" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Blood Moon Uprising was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Rising Storm" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The [[2022]] [[Imperial Era]]-set short story "[[Blood Moon Uprising (short story)|Blood Moon Uprising]]" by [[Vera Strange]], published in the anthology ''[[Stories of Jedi and Sith]]'', references the uprising in its title, but is otherwise unrelated.<ref name="Blood Moon Uprising">{{StoryCite|story=Blood Moon Uprising (short story)|book=Stories of Jedi and Sith}}</ref>
