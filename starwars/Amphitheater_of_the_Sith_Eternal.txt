The '''Amphitheater of the Sith Eternal'''<ref name="CompleteLocationsNew">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> was the throne room of the [[Immortality|reborn]] [[Galactic Emperor]] and leader of the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Religion|cult]], [[Darth Sidious]], and located within the [[Sith Citadel]] of [[Exegol]]. It housed the ancient [[Throne of the Sith]], which the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] offered to both [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], two promising successors. Opposite to the throne was an amphitheater that could hold hundreds of [[Sith cultist]]s. Between these two structures laid a wide space upon which [[Darth]] Sidious' [[Sovereign Protector]]s stood guard. Suffering from deteriorating flesh, Sidious relied on the [[Ommin harness]] for mobility inside the throne room, where the [[Sith]] was confronted by Rey and the [[Mission to Kef Bir|redeemed]] Ben Solo during the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
