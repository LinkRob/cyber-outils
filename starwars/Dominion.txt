The '''''Dominion''''' was a [[Star Destroyer]] at the time of the [[Liberation of Kashyyyk]]<ref name="LD" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> At this time, it was infiltrated by [[New Republic]] forces, and used to attack two other Star Destroyers over [[Kashyyyk]], the ''[[Neutralizer]]'' and the ''[[Vitiator]]'', the latter of which it destroyed.<ref name="LD" />
