{{Quote|That's an artifact of ''the Ascendant''. They used tech so dark that even the ''Sith'' avoided it.|Chelli Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 15">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 15|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 15]]</ref>}}
The '''Ascendant''' was a cult aligned with the [[dark side of the Force]]<ref name="Aphra 19" /> that sought to recreate the power of [[the Force]] with technology<ref name="Aphra 22" /> and fight against the [[Sith]].<ref name="Aphra 24" /> The cult was obsessed with powerful and dark technology, some of which the later [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] claimed was too dark even for the Sith,<ref name="Aphra 19" /> who decried the Ascendant as heretics. As such, the Sith [[Darkseekers]] made efforts to locate and destroy the cult.<ref name="Aphra 24" /> One artifact of the Ascendant was the [[Thought Dowser|thought dowser]], a device that, according to legend, was made of a special material that amplified thoughts to control others.<ref name="Aphra 19" /> Despite their hate of the Ascendant,<ref name="Aphra 24" /> the Sith came to use the device to teach [[Sith apprentice|apprentices]] how to compel and manipulate people.<ref name="Aphra 19" />

They built their [[Tomb of the Spark Eternal|main temple]] on [[Bar'leth]].<ref name="Aphra 19" /> Eventually, the Sith located the Ascendant's temple on Bar'leth and [[Slaughter of the Ascendant|slaughtered]] its members, who had been working on completing what was poised to become their crowning achievement, the [[Spark Eternal]].<ref name="Aphra 22" /> Ascendant leader<ref name="Aphra 24" /> [[Miril]] gave her life to lock the Spark away, killing herself and the surrounding Sith to ensure it would not fall under their control.<ref name="Aphra 22" />

At some point, the [[University of Bar'leth]] was built on the ruins of their temple.<ref name="Aphra 19" /> [[Standard century|Centuries]] after the cult's destruction.<ref name="Aphra 22" /> one thought dowser fell into the hands of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate, which kept it aboard its [[fortress]]-[[flagship]], [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']]. While attempting to retrieve another item from the gallery where the thought dowser was kept, the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] briefly wielded the device against the Crimson Dawn [[assassin]] [[Deathstick]] and several guards.<ref name="Aphra 15" />
