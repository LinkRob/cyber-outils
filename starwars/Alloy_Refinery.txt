'''Alloy Refineries''' were structures utilized by both the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They were used to refine [[Metal|alloys]] extracted from various worlds that could, in turn, be used to enhance various military bases owned by both factions. These alloys were transferred to [[Alloy Depot]]s immediately after the refining process was complete, where they were stored until use.<ref name="Commander" />
