An '''aurodium mine''' was located in [[Grid 24B-30]] of the [[planet]] [[Elphrona]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Freeno]], one of several [[Er'Kit (species)|Er'kit]] [[miner]]s searching for the [[mineral]] [[aurodium]], discovered a valuable [[kantak crystal]] in the mine and attempted to unearth it with his [[rockspanner]]. His efforts, however, caused the mine to collapse. With the help of [[Jedi]] [[Loden Greatstorm]] and [[Bell Zettifar]], Freeno was able to escape the mine.<ref name="Set For Life" />
