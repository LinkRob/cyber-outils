The '''Bardotta system''', also known as the '''Bardottan system''', was a [[star system]] located in the [[Shasos sector]] of the [[Colonies]]. The system contained the [[planet]] [[Bardotta]], [[homeworld]] of the [[Bardottan]] [[species]].<ref name="TDP1" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 69|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 69]] places the Bardotta system in J-13 as in ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'', however, all subsequent sources place it in K-13 instead.
