'''Chu''' was a [[Farakai]] who bonded with the [[Rodian]] [[Lok (Rodian)|Lok]] to become the [[gangster]] [[Chulok]]. The Farakai met the Rodian while serving [[Queen]] [[Sybil]] in the [[Mining|mines]] of the [[planet]] [[Metagos]], and after becoming close allies, offered him a bond. As Chulok, they led an [[Chulok's organization|organization]] and held significant power in the [[city]] of [[New Xaxxis]].<ref name="The Glass Abyss" />
