'''Chipp''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Lannik (species)|Lannik]] individual who was a member of a group of thieves on the [[planet]] [[Tenoo]] during the [[High Republic Era]] alongside [[Emmett]] and [[Z-3OR]]. Chipp was part of the group's effort to steal [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] from a [[hovertrain]] after stealing a group of [[Dunnel]]s.<ref name="The Search for the Missing Dunnels" /> Later, they were a part of an attempt to take the [[droid]] [[MB-4]] for the locations of mines in the droid's [[memory bank]].<ref name="A Mission to Remember">{{YJA|A Mission to Remember}}</ref>
