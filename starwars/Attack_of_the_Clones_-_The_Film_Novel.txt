'''''Attack of the Clones - The Film Novel''''' ([[Wikipedia:French language|French]]: '''''L'Attaque des Clones - Le roman du film''''') is a junior novel adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. ''Attack of the Clones - The Film Novel'' was written by [[Catherine Kalengula]] and published by [[Hachette Jeunesse]] on [[February 24]], [[2016]].<ref name="Hachette" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Since the blockade of the planet Naboo by the Trade Federation, the Republic, ruled by Chancellor Palpatine, has been in crisis. A group of dissidents, led by the dark Jedi Count Dooku, are expressing their dissatisfaction with the regime's operations. The Senate and the intergalactic population are concerned about this emerging threat. ''
''Some senators are calling for a strong Republic military to prevent further deterioration. Meanwhile, Padmé Amidala, now a senator, is threatened by the Separatists and narrowly escapes an attack. Padawan Anakin Skywalker is charged with her protection. His master, Obi-Wan Kenobi, goes to investigate the attempted murder and discovers the formation of a mysterious army of clones&hellip;''<ref name="Hachette" />
