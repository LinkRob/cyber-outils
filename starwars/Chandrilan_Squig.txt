'''Chandrilan Squigs''' were a [[Chandrilan]] [[Squig (beverage)|squig]] from the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. In [[230 BBY]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Amadeo Azzazzo]] walked past a [[restaurant]] on the Core Worlds planet [[Coruscant]] serving the [[squig]] and the [[squig]] [[eel]]s that went in them. During the [[Imperial Era]], in [[5 BBY]], Chandrilan Squigs were imbibed by [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Perrin Fertha]], and their old school friend [[Tay Kolma]] while in the [[Chandrilan Embassy]] on Coruscant.

==Description==
Chandrilan Squigs<ref name="Chandrilan Squigs">{{SW|url=news/andor-chandrilan-squigs-recipe|text=Raise a Glass to Mon Mothma (and the Rebellion) with this Chandrilan Squigs Recipe}}</ref> were a type of [[Chandrilan]] [[Squig (beverage)|squig]] from<ref name="COC" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Chandrila]].<ref name="COC" /> The [[cocktail]] was clear in [[color]] until the addition of [[squig]]s&mdash;Chandrilan [[eel]]s&mdash;<ref name="Chandrilan Squigs" /> which caused the drink to fizz and take on a yellow hue. The beverage was [[Diet|potable]] to [[human]]s<ref name="Narkina 5" /> and shared their name with a [[fathier]] named [[Chandrilan Squig (fathier)|Chandrilan Squig]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> [[Reproduction|born]] around [[1 ABY]].<ref name="BirthYear">{{OutlawsDate}} Chandrilan Squig is two years old during the events of ''Outlaws'', meaning they must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[1 ABY]].</ref>

==History==
During the [[High Republic Era]], a [[restaurant]] on<ref name="COC" /> the Core Worlds planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> [[Coruscant]] served the cocktails.<ref name="COC" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[The Call of Coruscant]]" takes place concurrently with the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Amadeo Azzazzo]] walked past the restaurant, taking care to avoid the workers outside the establishment offering Chandrilan Squigs to passersby.<ref name="COC" />

[[File:Squigs-Narkina5.png|thumb|left|300px|Chandrilan Squigs were served in the Chandrilan Embassy on Coruscant.]]
By the [[time]] of the [[Imperial Era]], the Chandrilan [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] and her [[Mothma family|daughter]]&mdash;[[Leida Mothma]]&mdash;disliked the drink, while her [[Marriage|husband]]&mdash;[[Perrin Fertha]]&mdash;enjoyed it. At the couple's party in the [[Chandrilan Embassy]] on Coruscant<ref name="Narkina 5" /> in [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date 5 BBY">{{AndorDate|108}}</ref> Chandrilan Squigs were served. Both Mothma, Fertha, and their old school friend [[Tay Kolma]] drank the beverage, although Mothma chose not to have the [[worm]]-like creatures in her drink. When Leida commented to her [[Gender|mother]] about the drinks being disgusting, Kolma responded that that was the point.<ref name="Narkina 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Chandrila has a rich history, full of heritage and judicious customs. Even their food and drinks come from a long tradition, and this fizzy delight is the perfect beverage to toast to any celebratory occasion&mdash;just ask Mon Mothma.|Jenn Fujikawa, about Chandrilan Squigs|<ref name="Chandrilan Squigs" />}}
[[File:Chandrilan-Squigs.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Chandrilan Squigs made from a recipe on StarWars.com]]
Chandrilan Squigs first appeared in "[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]]," the eighth episode of the [[Andor Season 1|first season]] of the [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'',<ref name="Narkina 5" /> released on [[October 26]], [[2022]].<ref name="Release dates">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1557442379975573504|[[Star Wars]]|date=2022-8-10|quote=This is what revolution looks like. Watch #Andor, a Star Wars Original Series, streaming only on @DisneyPlus September 21.|archivedate=20220810191942}}</ref> Referred to only as squigs in the episode,<ref name="Narkina 5" /> the beverage was fully identified in a [[StarWars.com]] article on the drink itself authored by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and published [[October 28|two days]] after the episode. The article provided an out-of-universe recipe for the Chandrilan Squigs that mixed [[Wikipedia:Mango|mango]] juice, [[Wikipedia:Lemon-lime drink|lemon-lime soda]], and [[Wikipedia:Ice|ice]], with the squigs being made using [[Wikipedia:Raspberry|raspberry]] [[Wikipedia:Gelatin|gelatin]] powder, unflavored gelatin, boiling [[Wikipedia:Water|water]], [[Wikipedia:Heavy cream|heavy whipping cream]], and flexible plastic straws.<ref name="Chandrilan Squigs" />
