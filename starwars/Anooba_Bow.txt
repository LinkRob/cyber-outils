The '''Anooba Bow''' was a [[composite bow]] utilized by the [[Zabrak]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Nox]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Wild Bow|standard bow]]. It appeared to consist primarily of the bones of an [[anooba]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Anooba Bow was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Nox's Wild Bow, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Anooba Archer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
