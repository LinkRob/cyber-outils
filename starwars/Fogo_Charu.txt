'''Fogo Charu''' was a [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. He was the author of a work titled ''The Wisdom of Master Fogo Charu, Jedi'', which was entirely dedicated to [[fishing]].<ref name="The Living Force" /> In [[33 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{LivingForceDate}}</ref> Master [[Eeth Koth]] found a gilded [[datapad]] in the [[Lamplighter Boutique]] on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]], which contained Charu's work, and had been imprinted with a symbol indicating it had circulated through the library in the [[Kwenn Jedi outpost]]. Although Koth considered this a lesser work, it was a sign that items stolen from the outpost had been sold to the boutique.<ref name="The Living Force" />
