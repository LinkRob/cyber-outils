"'''Bloodbath'''" was a member of the [[Staved Skulls]] [[pirate]] gang who, along with another member "[[Menace Machine]]," jostled for leadership of the gang after the death of its preceding leader, the self-proclaimed [[Eviscerator (pirate)|Eviscerator]]. His appearance was similar to that of his predecessor.<ref name="The Living Force" />

[[Zilastra]], leader of the [[Riftwalkers]] gang, had [[Death|killed]] the Eviscerator, along with the leaders of the other pirate organizations in [[the Slice]], in order to gain power for herself. Realizing some time later that nobody knew she had been responsible for the attack, she organized a call with the other four gangs in order to talk to whoever had taken over from their dead leaders. Bloodbath attended this call, in which Zilastra proposed uniting all five pirate gangs under herself.<ref name="The Living Force" />

Just before the scheduled bicentennial [[Celebration on Kwenn]], Zilastra gathered the leaders of the five gangs, plus some of their rivals, in a holo summit. Bloodbath and Menace Machine both attended, and expressed concern about the plan to attack the [[Jedi High Council]] while the members were gathered in the [[Gala Key Arena]] on [[Kwenn]]. The two Staved Skulls were worried about what the Jedi might do in retaliation, but Zilastra assured them that the Jedi did not do revenge.<ref name="The Living Force" />

Bloodbath, along with leaders of all the gangs, set up a control room aboard the casino barge ''[[Pelagic]]'' for the planned attack. They all watched holographic feeds of the proceedings from inside the barge. Bloodbath had trouble remembering the exact details of Zilastra's plans, and was impatient with the slow pace of the ceremony. Later, when [[Jedi Master]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] infiltrated the Staved Skulls and the ''Pelagic'', he found that the split in leadership between Bloodbath and Menace Machine meant that the Skulls weren't necessarily familiar with even their own members.<ref name="The Living Force" />
