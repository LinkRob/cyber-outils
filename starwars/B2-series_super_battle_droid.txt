{{Quote|Don't let the nickname fool you; the B2-series battle droid wasn't really all that ''super''. Sure, they had a level of strength and durability that a B1 could only dream about, but they were slow and clumsy, and they barely had enough processing power to aim their weapons in the right direction.|R0-GR, battle droid veteran|<ref name="Droidography" />}}
'''B2-series super battle droids''',<ref name="Build R2 35">{{BuildR2Cite|35|Droid Directory}}</ref> also known as '''B2 super battle droids''',<ref name="super battle droid databank">{{Databank|super-battle-droid|Super Battle Droid}}</ref> were an advanced<ref name="Kraken B2s">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/databank-download-kraken-super-tactical-droid-b2-battle-droids|text=Databank Download: Kraken, Super Tactical Droid, & B2 Battle Droids|date=2023-06-05}}</ref> [[battle droid]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Although one model of the B2-series was only equipped with [[wrist blaster]]s on their right [[Limb|forearm]],<ref name="Episode II" /> another was outfitted with wrist blasters on both forearms.<ref name="Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)" /> Super battle droids were also equipped with [[wrist rocket]]s and a rapid fire function for their wrist blaster.<ref name="Battlefront2">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

Super battle [[droid]]s were much stronger than their [[B1-series battle droid|predecessors]], and like the updated B1s used by the Confederacy, they did not require a [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|command system]] to operate, which gave the droids limited independence. Another improvement was their thick [[armor]] casing, which contained their fragile [[sensor]]s. However, B2s were designed with simple processors, limiting their ability to formulate strategies. As a result, they relied on [[Species|organic]] [[commander]]s or [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|tactical droids]] in order to effectively operate.<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|technology|superbattledroid|super battle droid}}</ref> The B2 was much stronger than the B1s, with enough strength to lift a [[clone trooper]] off the ground.<ref name="Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)" />

==History==
===The Clone Wars===
====Early battles====
{{Quote|Commander, have you pinpointed the Jedi's location?''"<br />"''Affirmative. The clones gave away their position. Our heavy troops are moving to engage them now.|[[Asajj Ventress]] and a B1 battle droid after a group of super battle droids had located Republic forces during the [[Battle of Rugosa|Ambush on Rugosa]]|<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref>}}
[[File:Super_Battle_Droid_AtG_JB_Casacop.png|thumb|left|250px|B2 super battle droids during the Battle of Geonosis]]
Having been developed before the onslaught of the [[Clone Wars]], the first batch of B2 super [[battle droid]]s was put into operation on [[Geonosis]], during the [[First Battle of Geonosis|battle]] that started the conflict.<ref name="SWDF">[[Star Wars: Droid Factory (book)|''Star Wars: Droid Factory'']]</ref> The [[droid]]s could take more hits and could stand up better to their enemies than the [[B1-series battle droid]] could, and it saw greater production after the Battle of Geonosis.<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> However, the super battle droid cost more than the B1, and their higher cost made it so that there were often one hundred B1 battle droids for every B2 on a battlefield.<ref name="Across the Galaxy Super Battle Droid">{{Destiny|set=Across the Galaxy|cardname=Super Battle Droid|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/08020}}</ref>

With the Clone Wars spreading across the galaxy,<ref name="Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)" /> [[Separatist officer]]s worked to ensure B2 units became part of their [[Army|armies]], as they had seen how well the new droids had fought on Geonosis.<ref name="Super Battle Droid History Gallery Image 4">{{Databank|super-battle-droid-biography-gallery|Super Battle Droid Biography Gallery}} {{C|Image 4 caption}}</ref> Early into<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref> the long lasting [[defense of Dantooine]],<ref name="Brain Invaders">{{TCW|Brain Invaders}}</ref> B2s fought against<ref name="BTF 37">{{BuildFalconCite|37|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> a Republic defense.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]] once cut down<ref name="BTF 37" /> an overwhelming wave of B2s<ref name="Brotherhood" /> and fought back against [[seismic tank]]s on [[Dantooine]].<ref name="BTF 37" /> 

In the [[Battle of Christophsis]], B2s reduced their targets to wreckage, which proved to be a common role for the super droids.<ref name="Super Battle Droid History Gallery Image 4" /> Nevertheless, B2s failed to ensure a Separatist victory on [[Christophsis]], ultimately falling with the rest of the [[Whorm Loathsom's army|invasion army]]. Concurrently, B2s were among the battle droids stationed on the [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] [[Teth]], where they ensured [[Rotta]] the [[Hutt]]let remained in custody until a [[Battle of Teth|Republic counterattack]].<ref name="Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)" />

====Return to Geonosis====
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> B2 super battle droids and other droid units were being constructed on Geonosis,<ref name="WFactory">{{TCW|Weapons Factory}}</ref> which the Separatists had taken back from the Republic,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> in the<ref name="WFactory" /> [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|largest droid foundry]] ever constructed.<ref name="Senate Spy">{{TCW|Senate Spy}}</ref> To reconquer the planet and shut down [[Poggle the Lesser]]'s [[Geonosis droid factories|factories]], the Republic launched an [[Second Battle of Geonosis|invasion]], and, during their push to reach [[Point Rain]], [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[501st Legion]] confronted the super droids and B1 battle droids stationed at the [[eastern barrier]]. Once Skywalker and his [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], made their way to the top of the wall, [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] ordered that blaster fire be focused on the droids.<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref>

[[File:Droidekas_and_B2_Super_battle_droids.png|thumb|right|250px|B2 super battle droids were among the droids that again fought on Geonosis in 21 BBY.]]
Ultimately, Skywalker and Tano, who were joined by Rex once he reached the top, successfully took down a pair of [[droideka]]s and the barrier.<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" /> After Republic forces were reinforced by the [[41st Elite Corps]], they made an attack on Poggle's primary droid factory and fought its B1, B2, and [[Geonosian]] defenders. However, the entire attack was a distraction to allow Jedi [[Commander]]s Tano and [[Barriss Offee]] to enter and destroy the factory. Using a Separatist [[Separatist super-tank prototype|super tank]], the two Padawans successfully destroyed the foundry.<ref name="WFactory" />

====Continued service====
[[File:DiplomaticCorps-CotH.png|thumb|left|250px|B2 super battle droids served in the [[invasion of Scipio]].]]
One super battle droid was [[G21]], who once gave a report to a superior that the planet it was stationed on was secured and that the population was under control.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The destroyed remains of B2s were among the wreckage strewn around the [[Ando Sith temple|ancient temple]] used as a base by the [[Death Wind]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 4">[[Obi-Wan 4|''Obi-Wan'' 4]]</ref>

The B2 super battle droids continued to see action until the end of the Clone Wars.<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> Thus, they served in the [[Outer Rim Sieges]], fighting in events like the [[Battle of Anaxes]]<ref name="Unfinished Business">{{TCW|Unfinished Business (episode)}}</ref> and [[Battle of Yerbana]].<ref name="Old Friends">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> A number of units also served aboard [[Grievous]]' [[flagship]] ''[[Invisible Hand]]'' during the [[Battle of Coruscant]]. All B2 units were shut down following the [[Mission to Mustafar|execution]] of the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]], ending the Clone War in a victory for the newly-formed [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="ROTS" />

===Post-Clone Wars===
{{Quote|Ooh, Baktoid Combat Automata fourth degree B2 SBD Super Battle Droids? Those are extremely rare.|[[Neeku Vozo]]|<ref name="The Mutiny">{{Resistance|The Mutiny}}</ref>}}
After the Clone Wars at least one B2 fell into the hands of scrappers on a [[Unidentified planetoid (scrap pile)|planetoid]]. This B2 was saved by [[C1-10P]], and would join a [[Unidentified droid group|droid group]] dedicated to helping other droids.<ref name="EtSP">{{RebelsMagCite|19|Escaping the Scrap Pile}}</ref> [[Sallowpink]] was the name of a modified B2 scheduled to take part in a [[Pit Fight Night]] against [[Rheen]] the Powderizer<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> between [[10 BBY]] and [[3 ABY]].<ref name="10 BBY and 3 ABY">The entry in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' that mentions Rheen takes place after [[Han Solo]] has completed the [[Kessel Run]], but before he encounters a [[bounty hunter]] on [[Ord Mantell]], which is mentioned as having occurred prior to the [[Battle of Hoth]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' establishes that Solo completes the [[Kessel]] Run nine [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]], meaning Solo's Kessel Run must take place in [[10 BBY]]. The ''Atlas'' also dates the Battle of [[Hoth]] to [[3 ABY]]; therefore, the entry in the [[in-universe]] [[book]] ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' must have been written between 10 BBY and 3 ABY.</ref> While on the planet of [[Aloxor]], [[Major]] [[Dayja Collerand]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], working under the alias "Mole," told [[Sisay's gang]] and several undercover operatives&mdash;[[Lieutenant]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], Imperial [[Assistant Director]] [[Brierly Ronan]], and [[death trooper]]s [[Waffle]] and [[Pik]]&mdash;about a narrow and unofficial backdoor to an Imperial docking area. Stating no large droids would be able to fit through, he claimed to have heard that someone had tried to get a super droid through.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref>

On the planet [[Koboh]], B2-HA super battle droids and other former Separatist forces were reprogrammed and used as soldiers by the [[Bedlam Raiders]].<ref name="Survivor" />

[[File:B2SuperBattleDroidsVsDarthVader-2015DarthVader3.png|thumb|right|315px|B2 super battle droids on Quarantine World III.]]
Also after the war, some B2s fell into the hands of [[Quarantine World III]]. These units were reprogrammed and were used as security for the location, and several of these droids confronted the [[Archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] during her [[Theft of the Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix|mission]] to steal the [[Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix]] from the [[planet]]. The droids that encountered Aphra were destroyed later in the mission by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who had come to recruit the archaeologist.<ref name="Vader III">[[Darth Vader (2015) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 3]]</ref> One [[Unidentified droid bounty hunter|droid bounty hunter]] active during the Imperial Era was made from the body of a B2 and the head of a [[BX-series droid commando]], yet the unit was nevertheless destroyed during a [[skirmish on Arbiflux]].<ref name="Aphra 34">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 34|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 34]]</ref> Several B2s were part of the [[Second Revelation]].<ref name="Book of Ajax">{{StoryCite|story=The Book of Ajax|book=Dark Droids: D-Squad 1}}</ref>

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], bounty hunters [[Zuckuss]] and [[4-LOM]] found a cache of super battle droids in [[Spur Orbiting Market]] while tracking [[Beilert Valance]] and [[Cadeliah]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 6">[[Bounty Hunters 6|''Bounty Hunters'' 6]]</ref> Zuckuss found the droids and came up with an idea to use them. Once repaired, the droids were loaded onto the ''[[Mist Hunter]]'' and the tracked the pair to the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Lowik]], where a [[Skirmish on Lowik|skirmish]] occurred with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], with the super battle droids battling the [[Rebel trooper|Rebel soldiers]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7">[[Bounty Hunters 7|''Bounty Hunters'' 7]]</ref>

[[File:Loading_docks.png|thumb|left|315px|B2 super battle droids served Plazir-15's government.]]
During the [[New Republic Era]], a large amount of B2 battle droids were acquired by the [[Plazir-15's government]] and were repurposed for civilian duties, with a large number of them being used as [[loader droid]]s at the [[loading docks]]. One of these droids was [[Unidentified rogue B2-series battle droid|infected]] with spiked Nepenthé, causing it to go rogue. The droid was later destroyed by Kryze and Djarin, who had been hired by the government to investigate reports of droids going haywire.<ref name="Chapter 22">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 22: Guns for Hire}}</ref> A [[Unidentified Mandalorian scout|Mandalorian scout]] who survived in the ruins of [[Mandalore]] after the [[Great Purge of Mandalore|Great Purge]] made use of a B2 faceplate as a [[pauldron]].<ref name="Chapter 23">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 23: The Spies}}</ref>

During the [[Cold War]], a number of B2 units served as security on [[Cato Neimoidia]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 21">[[Poe Dameron 21|''Poe Dameron'' 21]]</ref>

Not too long before the [[Battle of Exegol]]<ref name="TROS dictionary chronology">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that [[Sidon Ithano]] recently made a sale of B2 super [[battle droid]]s to [[Kragan Gorr]], an event depicted in "[[The Mutiny]]." As Ithano was featured in a section of the visual dictionary titled "the final battle," his description must be set before the [[Battle of Exegol]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. Therefore, the events of "The Mutiny" must have been a short [[time]] before the battle.</ref> in [[35 ABY]],<ref name="TROS Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] to one [[Standard year|year]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], the events of ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must occur in [[35 ABY]].</ref> the [[pirate]] [[Sidon Ithano]] made a substantial sale of a [[B1's B2-series super battle droid battalion|vintage cache]] of super battle droids to the pirate [[Kragan Gorr]] and his [[Warbird gang|Warbird pirates]],<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> who bought them in an [[Mutiny on the Colossus|attempt]] to seize control of the [[supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]''. However, as the droids were considerably outdated, Gorr recruited the aid of the ''Colossus'' resident, [[Neeku Vozo]] to aid him in upgrading the droids to a more efficient state. Vozo upgraded the droids, but was wary of Kragen's intentions and reprogrammed a single [[B1 (Colossus)|B1]] battle droid to become the designated commander of the B2s and use him to take control of the B2s.<ref name="The Mutiny" /> Along with B1, all the B2 droids were disabled by three [[Aeosian]] warriors who used their [[Aeosian spear|vibro-blade spears]] after the ''Colossus'' arrived on the planet [[Aeos Prime]].<ref name="The New World">{{Resistance|The New World}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
B2 super battle droids first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="Episode II" /> Originally, as seen in animatics for the film, the super battle droids were to dual wield [[E-5 blaster rifle]]s instead of having a built-in wrist blaster.<ref name="BlurayScene">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]'', Disc 7, Star Wars Archives: Episode II Deleted Scene: Raid on the Droid Control Ship and Extended Arena Fight</ref>

B2 super battle droids were later featured in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''<ref name="Ambush" /> and ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''.<ref name="The Mutiny" /> The droids had a similar stylized appearance in both shows.<ref name="Ambush" /><ref name="The Mutiny" /> In the novel ''[[The Legends of Luke Skywalker]]'', an image depicting the [[rescue of R2-D2]] features a droid that resembles a B2.<ref name="Legends of Luke Skywalker">''[[The Legends of Luke Skywalker]]''</ref> The [[I, Droid|manga adaption of this story]] later confirmed a B2 was indeed present for its events.<ref name="I Droid">{{StoryCite|story=I, Droid|book=The Legends of Luke Skywalker&mdash;The Manga}}</ref>

An [[The Clone Wars Legacy|unfinished Clone Wars episode]] had Rex and R2-D2 encounter a B2 super battle droid who they eventually grow attached to.
