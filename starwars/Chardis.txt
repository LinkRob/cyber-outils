'''Chardis''' was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="New Prospects" /> in the [[Trilon sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> At some point by [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Tales of Enlightenment: New Prospects]]" take place during the events of [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|''The High Republic'' Phase II]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Lee Harro]] lost his [[starship]] in a [[gravity]] sink on Chardis.<ref name="New Prospects" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chardis was first mentioned in "[[Tales of Enlightenment: New Prospects]]," a short story written by [[George Mann]] and published in the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine's [[Star Wars Insider 213|213th issue]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' series<ref name="New Prospects" /> on [[September 13]], [[2022]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=insider/subscriptions|text=Star Wars Insider Subscription Information}}</ref>
