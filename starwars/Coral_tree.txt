'''Coral trees''' were a type of [[tree]] that grew on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Eiram]]. The [[Plant|trees]] shared their name with [[coral]],<ref name="Convergence" /> a type of stationary plant-like organism.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> While in the village [[U'ronoh]] on the Outer Rim planet [[E'ronoh]]<ref name="Convergence" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> [[Iana Percei]], the settlement's [[marshal]], conversed with the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]], telling the latter that she opted to continue residing on only one planet&mdash;rooting herself down like a coral tree&mdash;because she believed war to be inescapable.<ref name="Convergence" /> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> during a [[Skirmish at the wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran|skirmish]] at the [[Wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran|wedding]] of Eiram and E'ronoh's heirs&mdash;[[Prince]] [[Phan-tu Zenn]] and [[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]]&mdash;in the [[Erasmus City Center]], a [[Lurmen]] [[Unidentified Lurmen bounty hunter|bounty hunter]] climbed a coral tree to confront [[Jedi Master]] [[Creighton Sun]] and [[Jedi]] Knight [[Aida Forte]].<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coral trees appeared in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a [[2022]] novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" />
