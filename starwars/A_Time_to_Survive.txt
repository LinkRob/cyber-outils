"'''A Time to Survive'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]] title "'''Überlebenskampf'''") is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Martin Fisher]], drawn by [[Ruairí Coleman]] and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. The story was published in the 27th issue of the [[Wikipedia:Germany|German]] [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]], which was released on [[January 18]], [[2017]].

Fisher placed this comic between "[[Homecoming]]" and "[[The Honorable Ones]]."<ref name="Time to Survive Facebook">{{Facebook|username=starwarsupcomingbooks|url=starwarsupcomingbooks/posts/1135365116578510:0|name=Star Wars Upcoming Books & Comics|quote=timeline-placement, according to Martin Fisher: in-between 2.16 (Homecoming) and 2.17 (The Honorable Ones)|archivefile=File:SWBooks-ATimetoSurvive-November-24-2016.png}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
[[Wullffwarro]], [[Kanan Jarrus]]. [[Ezra Bridger]], [[Sabine Wren]], and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] are out [[Mission to Oliu|searching]] for Wullffwarro's son, [[Kitwarr]], on the planet [[Oliu]] when a storm rolled in. Wullffwarro had suggested his son find a forested planet to see if he could [[Force-sensitive|commune]] with life. With driving rain and frequent lightning, the five began to look for shelter, but a lightning strike nearly took out Bridger. The lightning started a fire, which forced the group to change plans, instead looking for a place of safety. However, they find their way blocked by a large barrier of tree trunks. Wullffwarro and Orrelios climbed the trunks, while Bridger and Jarrus used [[the Force]] to lift Wren to the top of the barrier. Only Bridger was left at the bottom when another lightning bolt struck nearby, flinging Bridger away and starting another fire. The fire blossomed too quickly for the [[Spectres]] to return to look for Bridger, instead retreating to the ''[[Ghost]]'' to use the ship's scanners.

Meanwhile, Bridger awoke, lying on a tree branch jutting out over a deep ravine. He spotted a vine rope nearby and just as he climbed onto the rope, the tree branch broke off and fell away. He started to climb the vine, but that too broke and he fell, only to be caught by Kitwarr, who was swinging through on another vine rope. Kitwarr brought Bridger to safety and explained he was trying to find a connection to the natural world, but the storm was still pounding the area and Bridger and Kitwarr sought cover.

They found safety in a small cave where Bridger discovered his [[comlink]] was broken. Bridger, after igniting his [[Ezra Bridger's first lightsaber|lightsaber]], provided support for Kitwarr by explaining that he too had struggled to find a connection at first as well and that he believed in Kitwarr's ability to do it. They were interrupted by the cave crumbling over top of them. They flung themselves out of the cave as fast as they could. As they begin the search again for cover during the storm, another lightning bolt strikes nearby, sending wood fragments flying. One of them struck Bridger in the head, knocking him unconscious. Kitwarr, strengthened by the need to take care of his friend, pulled three trees down using the Force to create a shelter over the unconscious Bridger.

Some time later, Bridger awoke and congratulated Kitwarr on saving them during the storm. Soon after, the storm cleared and they were quickly discovered by the ''Ghost'' and Wullfwarro's [[Auzituck anti-slaver gunship|''Auzituck'' anti-slaver gunship]].
