'''Domina Tagge''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] member of the prominent [[House of Tagge|Tagge family]] and the head of the [[Tagge Corporation]]. Tagge held the title of [[Lady]] and operated from [[Tagge Space]] aboard the [[starship]] ''[[Acquisitor]]''. She was the aunt of [[Ronen Tagge]], whom she often spoiled, allowing him to have whatever he wanted. Domina was the cousin of [[Adelaide Tagge]] and the sister of [[Ulric Tagge]], [[Orman Tagge]], and [[Silas Tagge]].

When Ronen sought to acquire the supposedly powerful artifacts known as the [[Rings of Vaale]], the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]], who had attempted to acquire the Rings herself, destroyed his [[Ronen Tagge's Private Penthouse|penthouse]], nearly [[Death|killing]] him in the process.<ref name="Aphra 5" /> After the near-death of her nephew, Tagge placed a [[bounty]] on Aphra. The archaeologist was captured by the [[sniper]] [[Just Lucky]] and the [[bounty hunter]] [[Kharrek]] and delivered to the ''Acquisitor'', where Tagge ordered Aphra to undertake a task. Tagge would require Aphra to infiltrate the rival company [[De'Rruyet Industries]], whose leader, [[Beol De'Rruyet]], claimed to possess a [[Nihil]] [[Path engine]] that could provide a revolutionary method of [[lightspeed]] travel and thus financially ruin the Tagge dynasty.<ref name="Aphra 6" />

In [[3 ABY]], Silas hired the [[Ubese]] bounty hunter [[Boushh]] to assassinate other members of the Board of Tagge Corporation so he could take control from Domina. Following a skirmish between Domina armed with a [[Tagge protoblade]] and [[Boushh's crew]], Domina killed Silas aboard the ''Acquisitor''.

==Biography==
===Early life===
Domina Tagge was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] of the powerful [[House of Tagge|Tagge dynasty]] who lived during the [[Imperial Era|age]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. She had at least three older brothers: [[Ulric Tagge]], [[Orman Tagge]], and [[Silas Tagge]]. [[Adelaide Tagge]] was Domina's cousin. As a child, Domina survived at least two assassination attempts and resolved that she would never be a "pawn" thereafter. Instead, she saw herself as a player in a "game" that was hers to win.<ref name="WotBH Boushh" />

Domina had a nephew named [[Ronen Tagge]] whom she spoiled, often giving him anything he wanted.<ref name="Aphra 1">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 1|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 1]]</ref>

Eventually, Domina rose to lead the [[Tagge Corporation]]. She commanded that company's [[flagship]], the ''[[Acquisitor]]'', from [[Tagge Space]];<ref name="WotBH Boushh" /> a region of [[the galaxy]] ruled by her Tagge family.<ref name="Aphra 5" /> Domina claimed to have made TaggeCo stronger and more powerful under her leadership than it had ever been.<ref name="WotBH Boushh" />

===Silas' coup===
[[File:Shunk-WOTBHBoushh1.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Domina kills Silas]]
Domina's brother Silas coveted her position, believing himself an underappreciated genius.<ref name="WotBH Boushh" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Silas hired a group of [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] [[Ubese]] [[bounty hunter]]s led by [[Boushh]] to assassinate the rest of TaggeCo's [[Executive Board]], seeking to seize power in that coup. A [[Skirmish aboard the Acquisitor|skirmish]] ensued between the [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] and members of the Tagge Corporation.<ref name="WotBH Boushh" />

Domina wielded the [[Tagge protoblade]] against many of the attackers, besting several of them. Eventually, one of the bounty hunters targeted Silas, who unwittingly confessed to his treachery by proclaiming that he had hired the mercenaries for the attack. At that moment, Domina arrived, attacking both Silas and the Ubese who had confronted him. Domina felt vindicated at Silas' confession, but did not feel it was required for her to take action against him. Domina's [[Honor Guard (Domina Tagge)|Honor Guard]] eventually arrived, forcing an end to hostilities. Domina had most personnel evacuated from the ''Acquisitor'' before making a deal with Boushh's crew and [[Death|killing]] Silas with the unpowered protoblade.<ref name="WotBH Boushh" />

===Mutually beneficial arrangements===
{{Quote|Some ''ground rules'' before we start&hellip;''"<br />"''Rules? I'm more into ''improvisation''.''"<br />"''I don't leave ''anything'' to chance, Aphra. ''Defined terms. Hard boundaries.'' If we're going to move forward, I need your ''agreement''.''"<br />"''Oh, I ''agree''. ''Danger'' and ''authority'' are two of my favorite things. Maybe we can ''continue agreeing'' over there?''"<br />"''<nowiki></nowiki>''Optimization'' is ''everything''. Although&hellip;''iteration'' is an important part of the process.|Tagge and Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 35">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 35|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 35]]</ref>}}
Domina Tagge summoned Doctor Chelli Aphra to the ''Acquisitor'' regarding an attempted theft on Milvayne. She informed the archeologist that pirates had attempted to steal a prototype Tagge battle droid from a warehouse as to sell the droid on Nar Shaddaa, but the pirates and buyer were killed by the droid. Aphra observed that the droid's power cells were missing, which Tagge confirmed, a situation that should have been outright impossible. Tagge agreed and tasked the doctor with investigating a second warehouse of the droids on Havel Prime. Aphra initially refused given the risks, but quickly accepted when Tagge offered her danger pay.<ref name="Aphra 35" />

Tagge then led the doctor to her personal chambers and began to disrobe. The executive then informed Aphra that she had rules and boundaries, things she needed the archeologist to abide by before they could proceed. Aphra readily agreed, citing authority and danger as two of her favorite things, inquiring if they could continue "agreeing" in bed. Tagge stated that optimization was "everything," throwing Aphra down and into the covers before shedding her dress, conceding that iteration was also important. Aphra and Tagge kissed and proceeded to make love.<ref name="Aphra 35" />

The next day, Tagge attended a meeting where she secured the right to Keptik and its moons. Aphra woke three hours later to Tagge working on her computer, surprised that the other woman was already awake. When the archeologist tried to raise a conversation about the events of the night prior, Tagge reiterated that her terms were extremely clear and that she was not interested in committing to a relationship before dismissing Aphra.<ref name="Aphra 35" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:DominaTaggeProtobladeAttack-WOTBHBoushh1.jpg|thumb|right|x200px|Domina Tagge]]
Domina Tagge held a cynical view of her Tagge family, describing as a "nest of vipers" to her secretary<ref name="WotBH Boushh" /> [[Lapin Tagge]].<ref name="Aphra 28">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 28|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 28]]</ref> She had a Machiavellian sense of morality, evidenced by her lust for power and her remark to Silas that his only crime had been "being caught" as she killed him. Domina was willing to convert former enemies into assets. She hired the bounty hunters of Boushh's crew for her own purposes immediately after they attempted to assassinate her and others of her family at the commission of Silas Tagge.<ref name="WotBH Boushh" />

==Equipment==
[[File:DominaWearingProtoblade-WOTBHBoushh1.jpg|thumb|left|x200px|Domina wielding the Tagge protoblade]]
Domina Tagge wielded the Tagge protoblade with high proficiency in combat against her honor guard during testing, and later against the bounty hunters of [[Boushh's crew]]. She used the weapon to cut through walls and destroy enemies' weapons.<ref name="WotBH Boushh" />

After the protoblade's power supply failed, Domina continued to wield the unpowered weapon, which then functioned as a traditional melee weapon. After evacuating the ''Acquisitor'' and remaining aboard with only Silas, her honor guard, and the bounty hunters Silas had hired; Domina used the broken Tagge protoblade to kill her treacherous brother.<ref name="WotBH Boushh" />
