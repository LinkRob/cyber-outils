'''East-1''' was the [[callsign]] of a [[human]] individual who served in the [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]] [[Corporate Tactical Forces]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], East-1 participated in an [[ambush on Ferrix]] against [[Cassian Jeron Andor]]. East-1 tried to snipe Cassian Andor and [[Luthen Rael]], but lost them in the city. He later rescued [[Deputy Inspector]] [[Syril Karn]] after being detained by Andor and helped to recover the [[North Team]] after Luthen Rael had exploded a [[landspeeder]].<ref name="Episode 3" />
