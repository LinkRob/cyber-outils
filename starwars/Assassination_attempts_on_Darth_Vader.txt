{{Quote|Two attempts have been made on my life. Evidence suggests a conspiracy within the Imperial Officer Corps.|[[Darth]] Vader, to members of the Imperial Officer Corps|<ref name="Dark Lord 12" />}}
During the early stages of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]], the [[Imperial officer]]<ref name="Dark Lord 12" /> [[colonel]]<ref name="Dark Lord 8" /> [[Barokki]] and [[Unidentified Imperial officer (Coruscant)|another]] [[officer]] within the [[Imperial Officer Corps]] instigated two '''assassination attempts''' on the newly-christened [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The first [[Mission to Cabarria|attempt]] occurred on [[Cabarria]]<ref name="Dark Lord 12" /> in the [[Colonies]] region, where Vader, along with the [[Ninth Sister]] of the [[Inquisitorius]], investigated reports of an individual [[Force power|using]] [[the Force]] to escape from a [[Unidentified bar (Cabarria)|bar]] fight. The report was a trap set up by the [[officer]]s who placed a [[bounty]] on Vader and hired a [[Chas|family]] of [[bounty hunter]]s led by [[Bhada Cha]] to [[Death|kill]] Vader. The attempt failed and Vader learned that the bounty had been placed by someone in the [[Imperial Executive Building|Republic Executive Building]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Dark Lord 11" />

After realizing the officers did not know his place in the hierarchy, Vader had [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] hold a meeting of the [[Imperial Officer Corps]] to properly address his role in the [[Imperial Military]]. After Palpatine clarified that any order from Vader was to be taken as one from himself, Vader had five officers, whom he claimed had been chosen randomly, step forward. While Vader was not certain who had ordered his death, he made the gathered officers know he would always survive, while five Imperials chosen at random would not: Vader [[Force choke]]d the five Imperials before him. After this, Vader left the crowd, having used this as a demonstration to state that none of them could survive his power. One of the officers he killed was Barokki.<ref name="Dark Lord 12" />
