'''Dagobah-Bog-Be-Gone''' was a substance sold on [[Dagobah]] in order to make [[X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] [[swamp]]-proof. [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] appeared in advertisements for it, one of which was collected by [[Poe Dameron]] in a [[Unidentified book|vehicle guidebook]].<ref name="Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dagobah-Bog-Be-Gone was mentioned in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''.<ref name="Vehicles" />
