The '''4XB programming droid'''<ref name="Every Droid">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Droid in Star Wars|video=UQg6aX7lqTA|nolive=1}}</ref> was a type of [[programmer droid]]. One 4XB programming [[droid]], [[DA1-4XB]], worked for the [[Pyke Syndicate]] at the [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] of [[Kessel]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> DA1-4XB was freed during a [[Raid on Kessel|uprising]] initiated by the droid [[L3-37]].<ref name="Solo" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The 4XB programming droid first appeared in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> But it was given a name in the [[StarWarsKids.com]] video, ''Every Droid in Star Wars''.<ref name="Every Droid" /> In [[Star Wars Legends]] the 4XB programming droid was called the [[4XB Programmer Droid]]. The [[droid]] appeared in [[Escape from Balis-Baurgh]] in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 1|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 1]].<ref name="EBB">{{JournalCite|1|Escape from Balis-Baurgh}}</ref>
