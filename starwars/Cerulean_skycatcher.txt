'''Cerulean skycatchers''' were a type of [[Color|blue]] [[bird]] with a plucky nature found on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> on a quiet [[Local day|morning]] after [[Water|rain]], a pair of cerulean skycatchers ducked and dived through the [[Atmosphere|air]] above the [[Esmelle and Shirene's house|home]] of the couple [[Esmelle]] and [[Shirene]] in the Akivan [[city]] [[Myrra]] while the [[New Republic]] [[pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]] sat on the building's rooftop. Wexley felt the two birds chasing each other could have been taking part in a territorial dog-fight, a [[Reproduction|mating]] dance, or both given the creatures' nature.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cerulean skycatchers appeared in the [[2015]] novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
