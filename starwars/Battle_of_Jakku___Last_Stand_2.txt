'''''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand'' 2''' is the second issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] limited series ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Last Stand]]''. The issue was written by [[Alex Segura]], illustrated by [[Jethro Morales]] and [[Jim Campbell]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 8]], [[2025]].

==Publisher's summary==
''A BRAZEN ASSASSINATION ATTEMPT LEAVES THE HEROES REELING &ndash; AS A DARKER TRUTH IS UNCOVERED! LUKE, LEIA and HAN are reunited &ndash; but under dire circumstances! What's left of the Empire must react to a new threat &ndash; on the eve of the BATTLE OF JAKKU! A traitor revealed! The heroes are faced with an ominous new threat careening toward them &ndash; with JAKKU as the target!''

==Plot summary==
With [[Solo family|his family]] and friends in danger, [[Han Solo]] speeds in the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' towards [[Chandrila]]. Elsewhere, near the [[Anoat sector]] [[planet]] [[Gallowyss]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[New Republic]] [[pilot]] [[Rynn Zenat]] prepare to jump to [[hyperspace]] to help Luke's sister [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] on Chandrila. She recently had a near-[[death]] experience during a [[terrorist]] attack ordered by rogue [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] and executed by the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[religion|cult]] the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]]. Luke recognizes the urgency of the situation and that Adelhard has become desperate and chaotic; he can only be stopped by Luke working with his friends in a united lockstep. 

On Chandrila, Leia, [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]], and [[Agent]] [[Bastion (agent)|Bastion]] examine the carnage wrought by the recent [[bomb]]ings. There are many casualties and significant rubble, and Leia is further traumatized by Agent [[Timra Ariz]] dying in her arms just after being pulled from deep cover. The women start strategizing potential improvements to their security and to better elucidate Adelhard's coalition of Acolytes and criminal underworld contacts. Leia is at least grateful Adelhard is without his command [[starship|ship]], ''[[Scepter (Imperial-class)|Scepter]]''. Bastion then gives an unfortunately timed update to Leia that Adelhard has regained control of the ''Scepter'' and [[Adelhard coalition|his coalition]] of Acolytes and [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] are endangering [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian's]] forces. With all the New Republic forces positioning to [[Jakku]], backup is unlikely to arrive for Lando.

The women are soon interrupted by the [[Dowutin]] Acolyte [[Saycos Hawn]], armed with a [[TL-50 Heavy Repeater]] and prepared to [[assassin]]ate the New Republic leaders on behalf of Adelhard. Bastion helps the women get to cover, and Leia pulls out her own [[blaster pistol]]. She disarms the Dowutin and strikes him in the contralateral [[limb|shoulder]]. Hawn is not so easily defeated, and when Leia gets close enough, he grabs the [[reproduction|pregnant]] woman by her neck, choking her. Bastion orders him to release Leia, but he barely loosens his grip enough for Leia to bemoan fanaticism. With a decisive shot to the right shoulder, Hawn is taken down by Han Solo. Leia approaches her [[marriage|husband]] and the two kiss. 

Soon after, elsewhere in [[Hanna City]], Luke and Rynn arrive. The group begin to strategize about how to best handle Adelhard from here. With Lando's forces losing and Acolytes continually attempting assassinations, times are dire. Interrogations of Hawn did not go far. Other intelligence shows that perhaps Adelhard has strained his relationships with his allies, but still has tricks up his sleeve.

In the Anoat sector, Lando and [[Preeti Delmin]] evade [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] fire and attacks from the Spice Runners. With their enemies closing in, even evasion is starting to seem unlikely. Within one of the Spice Runners' ships, [[Vigilch]] orders Lando and Preeti's surrender. Before they can reply, [[Gen Tri]] informs Vigilch that [[Tomasso]] has spoken to the assassin [[Kranor]] and that their orders have changed. Vigilch is frustrated, but has the Spice Runners' forces pull back. This gives Lando and his surviving forces an opportunity to flee. 

On board the ''Scepter'', Kranor finishes a [[hologram|holo-call]] with Tomasso. The [[Anzati]] mystic of the Acolytes of the Beyond, [[Reyna Oskure]] then announces herself, calling out Kranor for his betrayal. She ignites her [[double-bladed spinning lightsaber]] and engages the [[Trandoshan]] in a [[lightsaber duel|duel]], fighting against his [[electro sword]]. Kranor eventually gets the upper hand, knocking Oskure to the ground. He tells her they are alike, both trying to play all sides and using alliances only as long as they are advantageous. Oskure sneakily impales Kranor with her a hidden [[dagger]] and reclaims her [[lightsaber]]. Her victory is short-lived, however. She is soon disarmed by New Republic [[spy]] [[Kith Alaytia]]. Kith decides to help Kranor evacuate the ship, and she [[stun]]s Oskure.

On the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|Vigilance]]'' [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] speaks with [[Counselor to the Empire|Counselor]] [[Gallius Rax]] about Adelhard's most recent attacks on both Imperial and New Republic targets and betrayal from [[Moff]] [[Goldsmythe]]. With more deserters, there is potential for failure at Jakku. Rax asks for Sloane to do what is necessary.

Leia visits the [[prison|cell]] of Saycos Hawn. The Dowutin expresses how [[the Force]] failed him in his assassination attempt. Leia scoffs at this, and the two debate about Adelhard. In this interrogation, Hawn reveals that Adelhard can win just by tipping the scales at Jakku. Afterwards, Leia speaks with Han, Luke, and Mon Mothma about an encrypted message they received from Kith Alaytia. Her intelligence changes everything. She informs the Republic that Adelhard has gone mad and is preparing to go to Jakku to annihilate both the New Republic and Empire. He is already outflanking the New Republic forces to do so, and there is no way to stop him.
