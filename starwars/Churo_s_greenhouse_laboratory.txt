The [[Hutt]] [[Churo]] had a '''greenhouse laboratory''' in his [[Devirsivik Clan|family's]] [[Devirsivik Clan's palace|palace]] on the [[planet]] [[Nal Hutta]]. Churo kept [[insect]]s and exotic [[plant]]s from all over [[the galaxy]] in the greenhouse. He used the greenhouse to conduct experiments, such as seeing if [[coolsap shrub]]s and [[snoruuk mushroom]]s could grow there and measuring the progress of [[ant]] growth.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />
