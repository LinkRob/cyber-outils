"'''Below the Belt: Philology'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year two of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the third scenario in the [[Below the Belt trilogy]] and was written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] and [[Christina Nichols]].

==Plot summary==
After his second-in-command, [[Len Markus]], goes missing in the [[Cularin asteroid belt]], the [[Crime lord/Legends|crime lord]] [[Nirama]] sends the heroes to find him. However, Markus has an agenda of his own and steals an ancient [[Sith artifact/Legends|Sith artifact]] known as the [[Darkstaff]]. This causes the [[Almas Sith fortress]] to lash out at it with a huge beam of [[Force lightning/Legends|Force lightning]], which causes the [[Cularin system/Legends|Cularin system]] to disappear for ten years and then reappear during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]], with it seeming to the people there as if no time has passed at all.
