This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. [[Cthallops]] was a member of this species.<ref name="SC5" />

==Biology and appearance==
This massive [[species]] was distinguished by their [[Color|green]] skin and massive tentacle-like appendages, as well as smaller facial tentacles that resembled facial [[hair]], green eyes, and exposed nostrils.<ref name="SC5" />

==Species in the galaxy==
Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> one member of this species, [[Cthallops]], was a patron of the [[Skull Ridge Mountain Hotel & Spa]] on the [[planet]] [[Lanupa]] who encountered the [[Youngling|children]] [[Fern]], [[Neel]], [[Wim]], and [[KB]], as well as the [[pirate]] [[Jod Na Nawood]] and the [[droid]] [[SM-33]], while they all [[Mission to At Attin|searched]] for the [[Captain Rennod's Lair|lair]] of the legendary pirate [[Captain]] [[Tak Rennod]].<ref name="SC5" />
