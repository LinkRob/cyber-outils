'''Conscription trooper pilots'''<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/confined-episode-guide|text="Confined" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref> were conscripted soldiers that served as [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|Imperial Starfighter Pilots]] for the [[Galactic Empire]] during the early [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Confined" /> They acted as a precursor to the TIE fighter pilots that were later used by the Empire.<ref name="Episode Guide" />

==Equipment==
Conscription trooper [[pilot]]s wore [[TK stormtrooper armor]] that was outfitted with [[Unidentified life support pack|life support gear]] and air-supply hoses that connected to the filtration system of their [[helmet]]s.<ref name="Confined" />

==History==
===First deployment===
During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|S3}}</ref> a [[Unidentified conscription trooper pilot|conscription trooper pilot]] flew a [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttle]] designated [[Shuttle 135]] on the planet of [[Wayland]] to deliver a [[squad]] of [[TK stormtrooper]]s to the [[Tantiss Base]] on [[Mount Tantiss]]. While on final approach to the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] installation, the [[shuttle]] was struck by lighting from a nearby storm, causing its systems to become unresponsive. The pilot managed to crash-land the shuttle in the [[jungle]] surrounding Tantiss, and released its ramp to allow his passengers to get out before climbing out of the [[cockpit]]. [[TK-343|One of]] the stormtroopers attempted to call for backup on his [[comlink]], but the Imperials were subsequently attacked by a [[dryax]], resulting in the [[death]]s of the pilot and his stormtrooper passengers.<ref name="Confined" />

Several months later, several conscription trooper pilots were deployed in [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|V-wing starfighters]] and ordered by [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]] to shoot down the rogue [[Cloning|clones]] [[Omega]] and [[Crosshair]], who were escaping Mount Tantiss in a hijacked [[Rho-class transport shuttle|''Rho''-class transport shuttle]]. After being informed that the clone Omega's [[blood]] was vital to a [[Project Necromancer|clandestine Imperial project]], Hemlock ordered for the pilots to break off their pursuit.<ref name="Shadows">{{TBB|Shadows of Tantiss}}</ref>

Two conscription trooper pilots flew a shuttle on patrol in the outskirts of [[Lau]] saw the crashed ''Rho''-class transport shuttle and reported it to Captain [[Mann]].<ref name="A Different Approach">{{TBB|A Different Approach}}</ref>

Several conscription trooper pilots flew [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] gunships on both [[Pabu]] and [[Erebus]], the former to drop troops off during the [[Raid on Pabu|raid]] to recapture Omega, while one of them was shot by [[CX-2]] when [[Hunter]] tried to hijack one of the gunships,<ref name="Point of No Return">{{TBB|Point of No Return (The Bad Batch)}}</ref> and the latter to chase after Clone Force 99, who hijacked the [[HCVw A9 turbo tank]] [[Transport One (Juggernaut)|Transport One]] in order to [[Rescue of Edmon Rampart|extract]] former Vice Admiral [[Edmon Rampart]] to figure out the location of Tantiss Base.<ref name="Juggernaut">{{TBB|Juggernaut (episode)}}</ref> More were later seen in [[Imperial Station 003]] at [[Coruscant]] during the squad's [[Mission to Imperial Station 003|infiltration of the orbital station]] and [[Unidentified TK stormtrooper pilot|one of the pilots]] aboard the [[Imperial research transport]] even informed Lieutenant [[Novitz]] of unscheduled droid activity in the vessel's cargo hold, which was actually [[Echo]] infiltrating the ship to deactivate the [[proximity sensor]]s.<ref name="Into the Breach">{{TBB|Into the Breach}}</ref>

===Final Days on Tantiss===
Conscription trooper pilots once again piloted V-wings and LAAT/i gunships to deal with Clone Force 99 and Edmon Rampart arriving at [[Wayland]], first by engaging in a dogfight with the stolen ''Rho''-class transport shuttle [[Alpha-44]] and then dropping off troops in the Tantiss jungle to search for the rouge clones and former Vice Admiral. One conscription trooper pilot would attempt to strafe [[Crosshair]] in a ''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttle during the squad's skirmish with the [[CX squad]], though he was shot down by his [[773 Firepuncher sniper rifle]] before the clone was subdued by the [[Clone X trooper]]s and had his PTSD-afflicted hand cut off by CX-2 wielding one of the clone [[assassin]]'s [[vibroblade]] [[sword]]s. Despite Clone Force 99 being captured by the CX squad, the base itself would fall, with its clone prisoners and specimens liberated, several TK troopers and [[Imperial weapons technician]]s, [[Scorch]], and Hemlock killed, and [[Nala Se]] ensuring Omega would be forever safe from Project Necromancer by using a [[thermal detonator]] to blow up the data alongside Rampart.<ref name="The Calvary Has Arrived">{{TBB|The Cavalry Has Arrived}}</ref>

===The Janix Hunt===
Around [[17 BBY]], Conscription trooper pilots were deployed to the Mid Rim world of [[Janix]] where they piloted LAAT/i gunships and ''Rho''-class transport shuttles to ferry TK stormtroopers around the city after [[2B0T]] called in the Empire when the [[Tactical Defense Force]] failed to report the renegade Sith Lord [[Maul]] to them.<ref name="Inquisition">{{MSL|Chapter 5: Inquisition}}</ref> A conscription trooper pilot flew a LAAT/i gunship to fight [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]], his [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]], [[Brander Lawson]], and his son [[Rylee Lawson|Rylee]] when the [[Inquisitor]] [[Marrok|First Brother]] tracked the Order 66 survivors to the [[Lawson's apartment home|Lawson apartment]]. During the fight Izara jumped onto the gunship and pulled the pilot out before taking control of the LAAT/i gunship. Two more pilots followed the hijacked gunship, but Izara pulled a maneuver, causing one to crash and Lawson's [[blaster pistol]] manage to kill another pilot before crashing onto a platform.<ref name="Night of the Hunted">{{MSL|Chapter 6: Night of the Hunted}}</ref>
