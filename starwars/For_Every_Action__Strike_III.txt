"'''For Every Action: Strike III'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year five of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the third adventure in the [[For Every Action trilogy]] and was written by [[Rob Edwards]] and edited by [[Ronald A. Heintz|Ron Heintz]] and [[Margaret van Poelgeest-Heintz|Margaret Heintz]].

==Plot summary==
The heroes are confronted on the remote planet of [[Holador]] by [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]] hired by the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] [[Riboga]]. They are able to escape their clutches and, with the help of the [[Cularin Militia]], are able to capture Riboga.
