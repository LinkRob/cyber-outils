'''Albus Marinith''' was an [[Imperial officer]] who served as [[Flag Captain]] of<ref name="Dawn" /> the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> ''[[Chimaera]]'', the [[flagship]] of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]. An old and reliable [[Imperial Navy]] [[officer]], Marinith was handpicked by Thrawn himself, becoming a close colleague. Marinith, along with other officers of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]], described Thrawn as cool and analytical, and appreciated his passion of art and tactics.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Albus Marinith was mentioned in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplement that was released in [[2018]] and published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Dawn" />
