'''Casparar''' was the second [[Planet/Legends|planet]] orbiting the [[Star/Legends|star]] [[Stisste/Legends|Stisste]] in the [[Saleucami system/Legends|Saleucami system]]. Located in the [[Suolriep sector/Legends|Suolriep sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]], it was an [[Water/Legends|aquatic]] world.
