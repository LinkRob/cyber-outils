A [[human]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] used the callsign "'''Dark Four'''" in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Dark Squadron]] between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]]. The [[squadron]] [[Assassination of Tarl Sokoli|attacked]] the Imperial [[light cruiser]] ''[[Forerunner II]]'' to eliminate the alleged [[Crimson Dawn]] mole [[Tarl Sokoli]]. During the battle, Dark Four picked off enemy [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], but ignored their peers' warnings to pay attention to the crossfire, paying the price when they were swiftly destroyed by enemy [[TIE series|TIEs]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|This&hellip;this feels wrong. We're attacking our ''own'' people. We might have gone to the academy with ''some'' of these TIE pilots.''"<br />"''For kriff's sake, this mission came from ''Darth Vader'' himself. Do you want to go ''back'' and tell him you think he's wrong?|Dark Three and Dark Four|<ref name="BH19" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="BH19" /> the [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> led a successful career as an Imperial [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]]. They were assigned to Empire's [[Dark Squadron]] under the callsign "[[Dark Four]]"<ref name="BH19" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> Reporting to the [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]], the squadron flew [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter|TIE defenders]] and were stationed aboard the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]'', the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[flagship]]. Dark Squadron was placed under the [[Squadron commander|command]] of the [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]],<ref name="BH18">[[Bounty Hunters 18|''Bounty Hunters'' 18]]</ref> "[[Dark One]],"<ref name="BH19" /> who had been captured and forced into service by Vader.<ref name="BH18" /> The squadron was then given the mission of [[Death|eliminating]] the Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Tarl Sokoli]], who was alleged to have become a mole for the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate.<ref name="BH19" />

"Dark Four" and the rest of their squadron traveled to Sokoli's [[light cruiser]], the ''[[Forerunner II]]'', outside the [[Eriadu system]], the [[cruiser]] having been redirected under Vader's orders. Valance had the squadron begin its attack immediately as the [[cruiser]] began deploying [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. With "[[Dark Three (pilot)|Dark Three]]" feeling uneasy about fighting other Imperial [[pilot]]s, Dark Four reminded them that the mission had come from Vader, asking if the other pilot wanted to raise the issue with the [[Sith]] [[Lord]] himself. As the battle raged on, Dark Four made picked off TIE fighters, one squadronmate commending their shooting. The pilot also heard [[Lieutenant]] [[Jeela Brebtin]] "[[Dark Two]]" scorn [[Dark Three]] for not calling her by her callsign.<ref name="BH19" />

[[File:DarkFourDestroyed-BountyHunters19.jpg|thumb|left|300px|"Dark Four" is destroyed by Tarl Sokoli's TIE fighters.]]
Dark Three was soon killed by the ''Forerunner II''{{'s}} [[cannon]]fire. "[[Dark Five (pilot)|Dark Five]]" iterated the words "kill" as they shot down [[TIE series|TIEs]], leading Dark Four to question where the Empire had found the other pilot. Brebtin told Dark Four to focus on the battle, the latter complying and having to correct themself when not using "Dark Two." Valance backed the lieutenant, telling Dark Four to focus on the crossfire ahead. Dark Four did not listen, bragging that they were one of the Empire's best Imperial pilots for a long time. However, the a TIE fighter cut off the pilot by destroying them and their own fighter. Despite Dark Squadron's losses, Valance was able to kill Sokoli himself, completing the squadron's mission.<ref name="BH19" />
