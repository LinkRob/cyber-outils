A '''blade''' was owned by and the favorite weapon of [[Commander]] [[Ellian Zahra]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Zahra intended to use the [[Sword|blade]] to [[Death|kill]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], who Zahra held responsible for the [[death]] of her mentor [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], during the [[Battle of Yavin|destruction]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Star Wars 8" />
