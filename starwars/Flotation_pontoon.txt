'''Flotation pontoons''' were gas-filled devices designed to provide buoyancy in [[water]], allowing a [[starship]] to make aquatic landings and remain afloat.

==Description==
A flotation pontoon was a device filled with a [[gas]]<ref name="Manual">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> that provided buoyancy in [[water]].<ref name="Manual" /><ref name="EFV">''[[Join the Resistance: Escape from Vodran]]''</ref> Pontoons could be used to build boats,<ref name="EFV" /> or be fitted to the underside of [[starship]]s that made occasional water landings. Pontoons could either be rigid or inflatable.<ref name="Manual" />

==History==
[[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]'s [[YT-1300 light freighter]] had the option to have flotation pontoons fitted to its underside for water landings. These pontoons could be filled with pressurized gas, or the ship's [[landing jet]]s could be modified to divert gas into the pontoons. Despite this, the YT-1300 was perfectly capable of making emergency water landings without pontoons, so long as it was properly maintained.<ref name="Manual" />

[[File:Millennium-Falcon-pontoons-SWA-10.png|thumb|left|The ''Millennium Falcon'' activated its flotation pontoons]]
The ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was fitted with flotation pontoons. These pontoons helped save [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] after the pair made a splash landing on a [[Unidentified planet (droid uprising)|droid planet]].<ref name="Powered Down 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=10|story=Powered Down, Part 1}}</ref>

[[Resistance]] [[cadet]] [[Dec Hansen]] once rigged together a pontoon boat. He and his brother, [[AG-90]], used the boat on [[Ques]] to see how far downriver they could go. Dec later recounted this adventure to [[Harra]] the [[Hutt]].<ref name="EFV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flotation pontoons were first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 44|forty-fourth issue]] of the magazine ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'', published by [[De Agostini]]<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|44|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref> around [[November 4]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|44}}</ref>
