The '''Atchapat Family''' was a family of [[Taymar]]s that lived in [[the galaxy]]. They were most famous for their [[Atchapat Family Galan-Kalank Orchestra]] which traveled the galaxy bringing [[Galan-Kalank]] music to amphitheaters from [[Chandrila]] to [[Coruscant]]. The family orchestra's lead singer was [[Svi'no Atchapat]], and it also included her [[Svi'no Atchapat's mother|mother]], [[Svi'no Atchapat's father|father]], and six brothers, one of those being [[Barg'no Atchapat|Barg'no]]. Svi'no's [[Svi'no Atchapat's grandmother|grandmother]] taught her songs. The family also possessed shipyards in which they would work between gigs. The Atchapats were said to have changed the galactic music game, putting [[Coronet City]] on the pop-cultural arts map and to have given [[Corellia]] fame that was for once more than about making great [[Starship]]s.<ref name="MH" />
