The '''Anselmi''' were a [[species]] of [[Color|blue]]-skinned [[humanoid]]s. They were one of the two [[Sentience|sentient]] species that called the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Glee Anselm]] their [[Homeworld|home]]. Long before the [[Clone Wars]], the Anselmi were the dominant species on Glee Anselm. They had a vast empire above the ocean, and dams surrounding their land pushed back the ocean. However, their empire eventually fell, and the Anselmi populace became scarce. At some point afterward, the Anselmi lost their capability to breathe [[Water|underwater]]. Particularly among extremists of their species, Anselmi resented their aquatic cousins, the [[Nautolan]]s, whom they referred to as "gill heads." Given the scarcity of land on Glee Anselm, the Anselmi strove to survive.

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Anselmi?''"<br />"''Land dwellers, unable to breathe underwater.|Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" />}}
The Anselmi of [[Glee Anselm]] were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] of [[humanoid]]s with [[Color|blue]] skin. They looked not unlike the [[Nautolan]]s, who evolved on the same [[planet]]. There were, however, marked differences between the two species. The Anselmi were squatter than their cousins, had only four fingers per hand and three toes per foot, and lacked the Nautolans' head tentacles. Moreover, the Anselmi sported thin tails and two [[antenna]]-like growths jutting from the top of their skulls. Most importantly, the Anselmi could not breathe [[Water|underwater]], while the Nautolans were [[Amphibian|amphibious]].<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" />

==Society and culture==
{{Quote|Your kind don't belong here, water-breather.|An Anselmi, to Nautolan [[Nakano Lash]]|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5" />}}
[[File:GleeAnselmAd.jpg|thumb|left|200px|The Anselmi resented their tentacle-headed cousins, the Nautolans.]]
The Anselmi were a greedy and manipulative culture. When their population was still substantial, the Anselmi strived to dominate both ocean and land from their Nautolan neighbors. They built a great empire on a large island outside of the ocean, and had dams surrounding the area.<ref name="Vengeful Waves">{{StoryCite|story=Vengeful Waves|book=Myths & Fables}}</ref>

Since Glee Anselm was an oceanic planet without major landmasses, and by the time of the [[Clone Wars]] the Anselmi could only live above water, they were at a disadvantage compared to the Nautolans. Resentment against their aquatic cousins resulted in Anselmi who referred to Nautolans as "gill heads"<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" /> or "water-breathers,"<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5">[[Bounty Hunters 5|''Bounty Hunters'' 5]]</ref> with such Anselmi hoping to dominate their cousin species.<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" />

The Anselmi were unsophisticated and superstitious. The Nautolan [[Jedi Master]] [[Kit Fisto]] described them as "little more than savages." They hunted with primitive [[bow]]s and [[arrow]]s, as well as bladed [[polearm]]s and [[Blowpipe/Canon|blowpipes]] firing paralyzing thorns. They practiced ancestor worship, hoping the spirits of their forefathers would help them triumph over the "gill heads" in exchange for sacrifices.<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" />

==History==
{{Quote|Legend has it that there used to be a vast continent teeming with life, but it sank to the seabed millennia ago.|Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" />}}
The Anselmi [[Homeworld|hailed]] from Glee Anselm, an oceanic world located in the [[Jalor system]]<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" /> of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] region.<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref>

[[File:VengefulWaves.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Anselmi flee a tidal wave in the legend of the fall of their empire]]
According to legend, the planet used to have a vast continent bursting with life until it sank to the seabed [[Millennium|thousands]] of [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Republic Era|fall]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Both species on Glee Anselm lived in peace for thousands of years, until the Anselmi people became greedy, thinking of themselves with greater importance compared to the Nautolans. The Anselmi built a vast empire on a large island above the ocean, and had dams created to push back the ocean; creating more land on the ocean floor for the Anselmi to use. However, the ocean spirit of Glee Anselm grew angry at the imbalance of power, and tore down the Anselmi empire with tidal waves and storms. The Anselmi empire crumbled and the population of Anselmi became sparse. The Anselmi were still a numerical minority by the end of the Republic, and kept a bellicose attitude toward the Nautolans which had been put in place long ago.<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" /><ref name="Vengeful Waves" />

During the childhood of the Nautolan [[Nakano Lash]], Anselmi extremists who would attack Nautolans grew bolder, resulting in travel advisories among the water-breathing species. When [[Nakano Lash's family]] ventured to an island, Lash stumbled upon a spear-wielding Anselmi who hated her species and wanted her off the island. [[Nakano Lash's mother]], pregnant with twins, attacked the Anselmi to protect her daughter and demanded she escape, with Lash running as her mother and [[Unidentified Nautolan (Nakano Lash)|other parent]] engaged the extremist. In the end, Lash was the only survivor of her family.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5" /> A [[Anselmi–Nautolan war|war]] was waged between the Nautolans and Anselmi during the chancellery of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]]<ref name="Jango Fett 1">[[Jango Fett 1|''Jango Fett'' 1]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|You shall be offered to the spirits of our ancestors. Only then will we be victorious over the gill heads.|An Anselmi hunter, to Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" />}}
[[File:Anakin_and_Anselmi.png|thumb|left|250px|Using the Force, Anakin Skywalkers frightens a band of Anselmi hunters into obedience.]]
The Anselmi were reintroduced into ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure]]'', a [[2019]] adventure book written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Elsa Charretier]]. However, as they only appear in one of the book's optional paths, the full canonicity of their scenes is not known. Within the book, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] traveled to Glee Anselm in search for [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], who had been kidnapped and taken there by mysterious [[battle droid]]s. When the Jedi went to investigate an island covered in dense [[forest]], they fell into a pit trap built by a band of Anselmi [[Hunting|hunters]]. The natives expressed their intention to sacrifice the Jedi to please their ancestors, but Skywalker decided to use their superstition to his advantage. Using [[the Force]], the Padawan rose from the pit to hover above the Anselmi, who dropped to their knees in terror. Although the natives did not immediately obey the Padawan when he ordered them to flee, they ended up scampering away after Skywalker caused a few nearby [[tree]]s to shake.<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" />

The Anselmi design is based on the original design for [[Darsana]], an Anselmi senator who was cut from [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/1182473099406540800|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=That is what the Darsana art looked like. I don't recall it ever being published.|nobackup=1}}</ref>
