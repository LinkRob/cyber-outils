The '''Bauble Battlestation''' was a vehicle built by [[Lax Torios]] for [[planet]]ary use on [[The Island]] of a [[Unidentified planet (The Island)|planet]]. It was a festive, [[Color|green]] and red variation of her [[Dwarf Star]] planned to be used for a since-scrapped [[Life Day]] parade. She sold it at her vehicle shop for three hundred [[probits]].<ref name="Castaways" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bauble Battlestation appeared in the [[2021]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Castaways]]''.<ref name="Castaways" />
