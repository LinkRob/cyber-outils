{{Quote|With the Force as my guide, death would not be the final word in my story. But my fall would signal a necessary end to the Galactic Empire. If the vast and powerful forces I had gathered could not perform their sworn duty and protect their own Emperor, they did not deserve to lay claim to this galaxy in my absence. For their failures, I would see them burn. And the galaxy along with them. ''[&hellip;]'' With the Empire erased from existence, something new could rise in its place. And from beyond the grave, I would control its every move&hellip;|Darth Sidious, ''[[The Secrets of the Sith]]''|<ref name="Secrets of the Sith">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
The '''Contingency''', also known as '''Palpatine's contingency plan''', was a secret plan devised by [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]] to destroy his [[Galactic Empire]] and ensure its rebirth in the [[Unknown Regions]] of [[Realspace|space]] in the event of his [[death]]. The task of putting the Contingency into effect was given to [[Gallius Rax]], a protégé of the [[Emperor]], whose plan was to have the Empire destroyed, culling its weak elements and punishing it for failing to protect him, while also simultaneously asserting that the Empire be [[Neo-Imperialism|rebuilt]], which the [[First Order]] sought to achieve.

Ultimately, the plan was to destroy the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] during a great [[Battle of Jakku|battle]] between the Empire and its adversaries; namely, the [[New Republic]], the galactic government established by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. After the Emperor's death at the [[Battle of Endor]], Rax began the Contingency with [[Operation: Cinder]], scouring both loyal and rebel worlds across [[the galaxy]], such as [[Naboo]] and [[Vardos]], which was ordered via [[Sentinel (Contingency)|Messenger]] [[droid]]s. He also arranged for the deaths of several [[Imperial Military]] [[commander]]s, and allowed the opposing New Republic to win a succession of victories against the Empire.

Rising to become the self-appointed [[Counselor to the Empire]], Rax was able to unite the Empire under his command despite challenges from unruly Imperial warlords. He brought the remnants of the Empire to Jakku, where he intended to blow up the planet during the battle between Imperial and [[New Republic Defense Force|Republic forces]], effectively crippling both sides. However, he was prevented from fulfilling the final stage of the plan by [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]], who fatally shot Rax during the Battle of Jakku. The mortally wounded Rax urged Sloane to flee to the Unknown Regions and regroup with the others, telling her that she now served the Contingency in his place. 

Sloane took charge of Palpatine's Contingency from the dying Rax and departed for the Unknown Regions aboard the [[Imperialis (Jakku)|''Imperialis'']] alongside [[Brendol Hux]], his son [[Armitage Hux]], and their [[Gallius Rax's child soldiers|child soldiers]]. They regrouped with others aboard the [[Eclipse (Executor-class)|''Eclipse'']], where Sloane intended to fulfil the Contingency. Unknown to all, Sidious ensured his return from death via a cloned body; his spirit escaped to his secret followers, the [[Sith Eternal]], who finished constructing a massive fleet he called the [[Final Order]]. This was all to achieve the First Order's hidden purpose: the perpetual reign of a new [[Sith Empire]]. Sidious waited to reveal his forces until they were ready in [[35 ABY]], the true final phase of the Contingency.

==History==
===Origins===
{{Quote|My boy. My precious boy. Are you ready to be the Outcast? Are you prepared to become the Contingency should it come to that? There will be others you must call to your side.|Darth Sidious, to Gallius Rax|<ref name="Empire's End" />}}
[[File:Acme-AMasterofEvil.png|thumb|left|200px|The Contingency was Darth Sidious' plan to destroy the Galactic Empire in the event of his death.]]
The Contingency was a secret plan devised by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], whose true identity was that of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious, to ensure that the [[Galactic Empire]] did not outlive him should he [[Death|die]] prematurely,<ref name="Empire's End" /> while also simultaneously ensuring the Empire's rebirth among the resources and personnel that would manage to survive the Contingency.<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Sidious intended for only the most ruthless of the Empire to survive his purge.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> In secret, Emperor Palpatine had been sending Imperial scouts into the [[Unknown Regions]] of [[the galaxy]] over the course of many [[Standard decade|decades]]. During this time, he had been investing in expensive resources used to seed the Unknown Regions with storehouses, [[shipyard]]s, and [[Laboratory|laboratories]], a fact known only to Emperor Palpatine and an elect assembled by him.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

The first part of the Contingency was the [[Jakku Observatory]], one of many [[Observatory|Observatories]] established by Palpatine across the [[galaxy]]. The Jakku Observatory was an underground storehouse and repository in the [[Plaintive Hand plateau]] which was built over a borehole that led directly to Jakku's core. In [[30 BBY]], while still [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], Palpatine and his [[Emperor's adviser|Adviser]] [[Yupe Tashu]] had initiated the construction of the Observatory and the borehole. Palpatine used an army of [[droid]]s to build the facility. The Contingency involved pouring [[Sith]] [[Sith artifact|relics and artifacts]] down the borehole to detonate Jakku's core, triggering a cataclysm that would destroy the planet and the orbiting and grounded Imperial and enemy forces. Using the analogy of a [[Shah-tezh]] game, Palpatine viewed this as a means of destroying both the Empire and his enemies.<ref name="Empire's End" />

[[File:Jakku-PoeDameronFlightLog.png|right|thumb|200px|Jakku was where Sidious found Galli, an orphan he would groom to execute the Contingency.]]
The second part of the Contingency was [[Gallius Rax]], a [[human]] [[Homeworld|native]] of [[Jakku]] whom Palpatine had taken on as a protégé after Rax stumbled upon the construction site. Palpatine tasked Rax with guarding the site during his absence, during which Palpatine engineered his [[Clone Wars|rise to power]]. After Rax had proven his service, Palpatine rewarded him by inducting him into Imperial service once the Empire was fully formed.<ref name="Empire's End" /> Palpatine arranged for him to become a [[Commander]] in the [[Naval Intelligence Agency]] before rising to the rank of [[Fleet Admiral]].<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> In the event of his own death, Palpatine intended for Rax to carry out the Contingency, referring to it as the latter's destiny. In order to transmit posthumous orders, Palpatine had special [[Sentinel (Contingency)|sentinel]] droids built to communicate with Rax and other "worthy" [[Imperial officer]]s.<ref name="Empire's End" />

The third part of the Contingency involved Rax and Palpatine's elect&mdash;a select number of "worthy" Imperial officers and personnel, which Rax expanded upon&mdash;fleeing into the Unknown Regions, which were notorious for being near impossible to navigate, using secret [[hyperspace]] coordinates. Using information partially gleaned from Grand [[Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], a native of the Unknown Regions, ancient [[computer]]s housed inside the Jakku Observatory plotted routes through the Unknown Regions for [[Standard year|years]], eventually charting a safe passage. Once the safe route had been mapped, Rax arranged for the Emperor's [[Super Star Destroyer]], the [[Eclipse (Executor-class)|''Eclipse'']], to travel in advance to the Unknown Regions. There, Rax planned to form a New Empire.<ref name="Empire's End" />

===Activation===
{{Quote|Operation: Cinder is to begin at once. Resistance. Rebellion. Defiance. These are concepts that cannot be allowed to persist. You are but one of many tools by which these ideas shall be burned away. |A posthumous message from Darth Sidious, to a select group of Imperial officers|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
[[File:Sentinel_BFII.png|thumb|left|200px|It was the Emperor's will that the Empire would not be allowed to outlive him.]]
After Emperor Palpatine perished at the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], the Contingency was activated and began with [[Sentinel (Contingency)|Messenger]] droids delivering the Emperor's final command: [[Operation: Cinder]], which involved the use of [[climate disruption array]]s to scour his homeworld [[Naboo]]<ref name="SE 2" /> and many other [[planet]]s, both those loyal to the Empire,<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> such as [[Vardos]],<ref name="Battlefront II" /> and those that were rebellious,<ref name="SE 4" /><ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> such as [[Mon Cala]].<ref name="Palpatine">{{EncyclopediaCite|Emperor Palpatine}}</ref> Fleet Admiral Rax received a Sentinel droid of his own informing him of the Emperor's death and ordering him to begin the Contingency.<ref name="Empire's End" /> In the time following the events on [[Endor]], Admiral Rax engineered the deaths of numerous Imperial commanders by leaking information about Imperial fleet movements and the [[Akiva Summit|emergency summit]] on [[Akiva]] to the [[New Republic]], the government established by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] after the Battle of Endor.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>

Admiral Rax kept the Contingency a secret from even his own subordinates, including [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]], who disliked his secretive style of leadership, and his [[Shadow Council]], an advisory council assembled by Rax consisting of senior Imperial officials and commanders.<ref name="Life Debt" /> During the activation of the Contingency, [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard|Adelhard]] proved to be a challenge for Rax, pushing for an independent [[Anoat sector]] and against the will of the Shadow Council.<ref name="RUS2" /> In order to eliminate Adelhard, the Shadow Council ordered [[Acolytes of the Beyond|Acolyte of the Beyond]] [[Reyna Oskure]] to kill him, but the assassination attempt failed.<ref name="RUS4">[[Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege 4|''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 4]]</ref> After Sloane was presumed lost or captured during an [[Attack on Chandrila|attack]] on the New Republic [[capital]] of [[Chandrila]], Rax assumed leadership of the united Empire as [[Counselor to the Empire]]. He then ordered the Empire to assemble at Jakku for a [[Battle of Jakku|final showdown]] with the New Republic. Unknown to the Shadow Council, Rax was planning to kill them, with the exception of the former [[Commandant]] [[Brendol Hux]], whom he regarded as essential to the Contingency and the Empire's future.<ref name="Life Debt" />

[[File:TerexCorlacJakku-PoeDameron8.jpg|thumb|right|350px|The Contingency was brought into full motion at the Battle of Jakku.]] 
On Jakku, Counselor Rax put the Contingency into motion by setting the stage for a massive battle with the New Republic. Intending to destroy the "Old Empire," Rax had Commandant Hux train local children into a [[Gallius Rax's child soldiers|force of soldiers]] who would become the foundations of the new Empire; Commandant Hux had previously run a [[Commandant's Cadets|secret society]] of child [[soldier]]s at his [[Arkanis Academy|academy]].<ref name="Empire's End" /> A massive battle began to rage between Imperial and New Republic forces,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> with an [[Arliz Hadrassian's brother|Emperor's Royal Guard]] fighting as part of the Imperial Military before falling in battle.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> Seeking to cause chaos, [[Adelhard's fleet]] arrived over Jakku and attacked both New Republic and Imperial forces with help from the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]]. Despite this new factor, Adelhard was defeated by [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Rynn Zenat]], as the Spice Runners turned on the former Imperial – allowing the attempted Contingency to continue.<ref name="LS3">[[Battle of Jakku — Last Stand 3|''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand'' 3]]</ref><ref name="LS4" />

Meanwhile, Rax and Yupe Tashu poured Sith relics down the planet's core in order to trigger a cataclysm. Rax then killed Tashu as part of the Contingency, pushing him into the borehole. Before Rax could escape offworld with Hux, his son [[Armitage Hux|Armitage]], and their child soldiers aboard a [[Imperialis (Jakku)|replica]] of Palpatine's yacht, the ''[[Imperialis]]'', Rax was killed by a vengeful Grand Admiral Sloane with the help of the New Republic operatives [[Norra Wexley]] and [[Brentin Lore Wexley]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

[[File:GalliusRaxDeath-BattleOfJakkuLastStand4.png|left|thumb|350px|With his final breaths, Gallius Rax passed his service to the Contingency on to Sloane, urging her to rebuild the Empire once again.]]
In his last dying moments, Counselor Rax told Sloane that she now served the Contingency in his place and told her to accompany the others aboard the ''Imperialis'' to the Unknown Regions, where the Emperor's flagship ''Eclipse'' was waiting in uncharted space. Despite Sloane foiling Rax's plot, the attrition afflicted by the Contingency helped bring about the Empire's defeat during the Battle of Jakku. Following the Battle of Jakku, [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] formally [[Imperial Instruments of Surrender|surrendered]] the Empire to the New Republic and signed the [[Galactic Concordance]]<ref name="Empire's End" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> The Galactic Concordance [[Inner systems' Imperial remnant|collapsed ]] the Empire, forcing its remains within defined boundaries in the [[Core Worlds|Core]], [[Colonies]], and [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]],<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> while on [[Coruscant]], the capital world of the Empire, a [[Coruscant's provisional government|provisional government]] overseen by the Republic was established.<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

===Aftermath===
{{Quote|It's time to start over. This is our first order. To begin again. And to get it right, this time.''"<br />"''Yes, of course, Grand Admiral. Anything you need. Glory be to Grand Admiral Sloane.''"<br />"''No. Glory goes only to the Empire.|Rae Sloane and Brendol Hux|<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>}}
[[File:DeckNine-AORFinn1.jpg|thumb|250px|Following the collapse of the Empire, selected Imperials went into hiding in the Unknown Regions, where they created the First Order.]]
Several Imperial remnants rejected the peace treaty signed between the Empire and the Republic and continued to hold out in the [[Standard month|months]] following the end of the [[Galactic Civil War]], although they were few in number as a result of the Emperor's Contingency. Other Imperial forces fled into the Unknown Regions using the secret hyperspace coordinates.<ref name="Empire's End" /> Chafing under New Republic restrictions and war reparations, the "Old Empire" eventually withered away into a remnant of hardliners locked into a [[Cold War]] with the Republic.<ref name="TFA VD" /> Other Imperial sympathizers were part of the Republic itself, some of whom joined with the [[Centrists]] in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]].<ref name="Bloodline" />

Aboard the replica ''Imperialis'', which was piloted by a Sentinel droid, Sloane, Brendol and Armitage Hux, and their child soldiers eventually reached the ''Eclipse'' in the Unknown Regions, where the Emperor's elect awaited Rax. There, Sloane saw an opportunity to rebuild the Empire, deeming it their "first order." This movement birthed the [[First Order]], a offshoot of the Contingency which sought to reclaim the lost Imperial cause and glory. During their [[First Order military|military]] buildup, the First Order conquered and colonized large swaths of the Unknown Regions. The First Order would later be joined by Imperial secessionists from within the New Republic as well as Imperial fleets whom vanished from Imperial Space.<ref name="Bloodline" /><ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> The First Order was eventually taken over by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]],<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> a failed<ref name="Inside Intel" /> strandcast clone<ref name="The Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> of Palpatine<ref name="Inside Intel" /> genetically created by the Sith Lord and his Sith Eternal cult prior to his demise at the Battle of Endor, who was originally discarded as a potential vessel for his creator's spirit,<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> but was instead utilized as the Sith's proxy in power over the Forces over the contingency.<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> Naturally force-sensitive<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> and particularly strong with the dark side,<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> Snoke, regardless of not being a Sith, he possessed the knowledge and power to lead the First Order,<ref name="TROS VD" /> which allowed Sidious to indirectly ensure the First Order was his own to control and gather the Forces which would form the hermit faction into a military capable of opposing the New Republic.<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" />

The dark side adept's rise to power was seen as unexpected by the last of the old Imperial leadership. [[34 ABY|Years later]], as the First Order [[First Order-Resistance War|destroyed]] the New Republic, Snoke reflected on the Contingency, recalling how Emperor Palpatine had arranged for various assets and materials to be sent to the Unknown Regions beforehand.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Ultimately, Snoke was a proxy Palpatine was manipulating to spread his influence across the galaxy through less direct means,<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> much to his underling's and the rest of the First Order's obliviousness.<ref name="The Star Wars Book" />
[[File:HuxAndStormtroopersAtCommandCenter-TFAOfficialTeaser.jpg|thumb|250px|The First Order sought to reclaim the galaxy and establish a galaxy-wide regime once again.|left]]
Though Snoke initially maintained an iron grip on the fascistic military junta, his reign was cut short during the conflict between the First Order and the [[Resistance]] spearheaded by [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. Snoke had, in reality, been genetically engineered by [[Sith cultist|members]] of the [[Sith Eternal]] cult, a zealous collection of dark side loyalists which sought to revive the Sith religion and establish a galaxy-wide [[Fascism|authoritarian regime]] once again. Deep within the reaches of the Unknown Regions existed [[Exegol]], the hidden world of the Sith, which housed the reborn Darth Sidious as he pulled the strings of Snoke in order to seduce the scion of the Skywalker bloodline, the young Jedi Ben Solo.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> Sidious's plans reduced the First Order to a mere pretender to the throne,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> one which he would guide through Snoke,<ref name="Episode IX" /> that would rule until his new [[Sith Empire]] was ready to assert itself.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" />

As Snoke's apprentice, Ben Solo took the name of Kylo Ren, [[Ren|Master]] of the [[Knights of Ren]] and champion of the First Order; a dark warrior who possessed raw strength in [[the Force]]. Ren tolerated years of abuse under Snoke's wing, eventually culminating in the [[Battle of Oetchi|assassination]] of Snoke at the hands of the dark knight and his subsequent usurpation of Snoke's position of Supreme Leader. Believing he had at last freed himself from harsh confines of apprenticeship, Ren had, in actuality, completed the final test crafted by Sidious' Sith Eternal by acting as a true Sith and killing his own master in the name of obtaining greater power.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> It was now clear that Kylo Ren had established himself as worthy of inheriting the mantle of Galactic Emperor, with Sidious eager to harness the power of his new Imperial heir.<ref name="Episode IX" />

{{Quote|The First Order is on the verge of bringing unprecedented peace to the galaxy. An era of harmony once unimaginable amid the corruption and chaos of the New Republic.|A stormtrooper sergeant, 709th Legion|<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 5">[[Galaxy's Edge 5|''Galaxy's Edge'' 5]]</ref>}}
[[File:KyloRen-GalaxysEdge5.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The First Order conquered much of the galaxy under Supreme Leader Kylo Ren.]]
Within a year of Ren's ascension to Supreme Leader,<ref name="TROS VD" /> the galaxy was shaken by a [[Palpatine's broadcast|broadcast]] of the presumed-dead Emperor, threatening revenge against free worlds and heralding the return of the Sith. Unwilling to allow this threat to his power to persist, Supreme Leader Ren immediately shifted his efforts from annihilating the remnants of the Resistance to locating and eliminating the phantom menace on Exegol. Though Ren had discovered the whereabouts of Sidious, he abandoned his original plan to simply kill the Emperor upon being informed of the extent of the Dark Lord's thirty-year-long machinations. Immediately prior to the death of his original physical body, Sidious had [[Immortality|transferred his consciousness]] far into the depths of the Unknown Regions, where an imperfect [[Cloning|clone]] body crafted by the Sith Eternal awaited possession. It was on this hidden world where Sidious and his underlings forged the groundwork of the new Empire: the [[Final Order]], the largest fleet in the galaxy comprised of ''[[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|Xyston-]]''[[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|class Star Destroyers]], each housing an [[axial superlaser]] capable of obliterating entire [[Planet|worlds]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

With the combined might of the Final Order and sizable [[First Order Navy|First Order Naval fleet]], Sidious was confident that his new Empire would subjugate the galaxy at large through sheer intimidation and military might. Ren was emboldened by the prospect of himself as new Emperor, though this position was promised to him on the sole condition that he would eliminate the only threat to Sidious' plan, the Jedi aspirant [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], whom was also revealed to be the granddaughter of the Emperor himself. Ren feigned allegiance to Sidious, although the Supreme Leader had no intention of obeying the Emperor and still intended to go with his original plans of killing the Dark Lord and destroy the Sith. Sidious himself also lied to Ren;<ref name="Episode IX" /> he intended to rule the galaxy alone, using the massive fleet to enforce his will and was only using Ren to get to Rey. Thus, the Final Order had been the secret final stage to the Contingency.<ref name="Inside Intel">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-inside-intel-palpatines-contingency-plan|text=''Star Wars'' Inside Intel: Palpatine's Contingency Plan|int=Star Wars Inside Intel}}</ref>
[[File:DarthPalpatine-TROS.png|thumb|right|250px|The First Order was a culmination of Darth Sidious' vision to rule the galaxy under a everlasting Sith Empire.]]
Through the bond the two [[The Force|Force]] users possessed, Ren deduced that he and Rey formed an exceedingly powerful [[Force dyad|dyad in the Force]], a phenomenon which had not been seen for generations. It was the extent of their bond which gave Ren the confidence to declare that the combined strength of him and Rey would overpower Sidious and allow the dyad to rule the galaxy together as Emperor and Empress. While he nearly maintained his [[Dark side of the Force|darkness]] to the very end, Ren was eventually redeemed by the combined compassion of his family and Rey herself, reclaiming the identity of Ben Solo once more. Not discouraged by this setback, he shifted his attention to his granddaughter, seeking to possess her physical body through an [[Transference|ancient Sith ritual]] which involved Rey striking him down in anger. Rey journeyed to Exegol in an effort to confront the phantom menace in the flesh, though she nearly succumbed to the temptations of Sidious; the Sith Lord claimed she could become empress and save the Resistance if his spirit transferred into her,<ref name="Episode IX" /> but the ritual was instead a way for him to secure a new body, with which he could become an immortal emperor.<ref name="Inside Intel" />

Upon sensing Solo's arrival, Rey rejected Sidious' offer, passing the [[Skywalker lightsaber]] to Solo through their Force bond. Upon Solo's defeat of the [[Knights of Ren]] and Rey's elimination of Sidious' [[Sovereign Protector|Sovereign protectors]], the dyad stood together in defiance of the Sith Lord, although the reunion was interrupted by the Emperor's mastery of the Force, inadvertently discovering the existence of the dyad which was held between the two Jedi. Seeing no need for a host body, Sidious [[Force drain|drained the life force]] from both Ben and Rey, eventually restoring himself to full power and naming himself as the Sith Eternal's one true Emperor. In retaliation for his initial death thirty-one years prior at the hands of his grandfather the [[Chosen One]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], Sidious disposed of Solo into a nearby chasm, declaring the end of the Skywalker bloodline with Solo's death. The Emperor's reign was short-lived, however, as Rey called upon the strength of all the Jedi from the past including Skywalker in order to ultimately defeat the Dark Lord of the Sith.<ref name="Episode IX" />

The immense strain and exertion to completely obliterate the Sith Lord as well as the surrounding amphitheater led to the subsequent death of Rey, whose life was restored by Solo (who had survived the Emperor's attempt on his life), at the cost of his own. With the permanent death of their figurehead, both the Sith Eternal and his planned revived Empire crumbled into nothingness, owing to the arrival of the [[Citizens' Fleet]] which bolstered the [[Exegol air team|Resistance force]] that had [[Battle of Exegol|invaded]] Exegol. The conclusion of the actions on Exegol marked the final end of Sidious' [[Imperial Era|decades-long]] tyrannical influence over the inhabitants of the galaxy, with the decisive victory serving as the catalyst for a [[Uprising against the First Order|series of galaxy-wide uprisings]] which saw countless systems rise against the authoritarian rule of the First Order.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Operation Cinder therefore resembled both the 'Nero Decree' issued by Adolf Hitler aimed at destroying German infrastructure in March 1945 and his belief that 'the population of Berlin should share his suicide.'|Doctor Chris Kempshall, ''The History and Politics of Star Wars''|<ref name="PoliticsofSW">{{BookCite|author=Doctor [[Chris Kempshall]]|title=The History and Politics of Star Wars: Death Stars and Democracy|publisher=Taylor & Francis|date=2022|isbn=9781351382700}}</ref>}}
The Contingency was first featured in the novel ''[[Lost Stars]]''.<ref name="Lost Stars" /> In his book ''The History and Politics of Star Wars'', Doctor [[Chris Kempshall]],<ref name="PoliticsofSW" /> a ''[[Star Wars]]'' author<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> and real world historian, compared Palpatine's contingency plan, particularly Operation: Cinder, to the actions of German Dictator [[Wikipedia:Adolf Hitler|Adolf Hitler]] in the final days of the [[Wikipedia:World War II|Second World War]], in which Hitler believed all of [[Wikipedia:Berlin|Berlin]] "should share in his suicide." In particular, Kempshall compared Operation: Cinder to Hitler's [[Wikipedia:Nero Decree|Nero Decree]] designed to destroy Germany's own infrastructure.<ref name="PoliticsofSW" />
