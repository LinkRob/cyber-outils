In [[5 ABY]], the [[New Republic]] launched an '''attack''' on the [[Scepter (Imperial-class)|''Scepter'']], the [[flagship]] [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] of [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]], following his involvement with an [[Assassination attempt on Luke Skywalker]] on the New Republic [[capital]] [[Chandrila]]. Prior to the New Republic's involvement, however, Adelhard had confronted his [[Advisor]] [[Reyna Oskure]] after discovering her allegiance to the remains of the [[Galactic Empire]], whom [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant|Adelhard's Imperial remnant]] had broken ties with.<ref name="Republic Under Siege 4" />

As New Republic forces began to arrive, Oskure, with the help of the [[Mercenary]] [[Kranor]], gained the upper hand over Adelhard, and ordered Kranor to execute the Grand Moff as she took command of the ''Scepter'' for battle. However, in Oskure's absence, Kranor allowed Adelhard and his right hand man [[Bragh]] to flee while still claiming Oskure he had [[death|killed]] them, allowing Adelhard and his most loyal [[troop]]s escape to [[Gallowyss]] via [[escape pod]]. Shortly after, Oskure requested backup from [[Adelhard's fleet]] and disbanded the [[Iron Blockade]], a [[blockade]] in which Adelhard had effectively quarantined the [[Anoat sector]] following the [[Battle of Endor]]. After receiving a communication from Oskure informing them of such actions and requesting that they leave the Anoat sector alone, the New Republic retreated back to Chandrila at the request of [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]].<ref name="Republic Under Siege 4" />
