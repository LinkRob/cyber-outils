The '''blockade of Serenno''' was an engagement that took place<ref name="BMF 70" /> sometime between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> in [[orbit]] of the [[planet]] [[Serenno]] during the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]] fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the [[blockade]], the confederate government utilized several [[Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel]]s in their protection of the planet, which was the [[homeworld]] of their governmental [[Head of State|leader]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]].<ref name="BMF 70" />

==Behind the scenes==
The blockade of Serenno was mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 70|seventieth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 70" /> in a section written by [[Mark Newbold]],<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> published around [[May 4]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|70}}</ref> The blockade was misspelled in the issue as the "blockade of Serreno."<ref name="BMF 70" />
