'''Ak-rev''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Weequay]] who played as a [[drum]]mer in the [[Max Rebo Band]], whose second drummer was [[Umpass-stay]]. He was a bodyguard to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]].

==Biography==
Ak-rev was a [[Gender|male]] [[Weequay]] who was a [[drum]]mer in the [[Max Rebo Band]], alongside fellow drummer [[Umpass-stay]]. He was also a bodyguard to the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]].

In the [[Standard year|year]] [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Ak-rev and the other members of the [[Max Rebo Band|band]] played at the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] of Jabba the [[Hutt]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The band played "[[Jedi Rocks]]" for the Hutt, who ordered it played a second [[time]] but then dropped the [[Slavery|enslaved]] dancer [[Oola]] into [[Pateesa|his rancor's]] pit during the repeat performance when she refused his advances. Ak-rev and the band stopped playing as Oola was [[Death|eaten]] by the [[rancor]], and then witnessed the arrival of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] disguised as the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boushh]] with the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] as a [[prisoner]].<ref name="Return of the Jedi" />

==Personality and traits==
Ak-rev was a beefy<ref name="Fancy">{{StoryCite|story=Fancy Man|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> Weequay with [[Color|brown]] [[hair]] and brown skin.<ref name="Max Rebo 1" />
