'''Michael Bruce Sinclair Logan'''<ref name="NY Times" /> ([[May 15]], [[1940s#1946|1946]]<ref name="Deadline" /> - [[April 10]], [[2025]]<ref name="THR" />) was a cinematographer,<ref name="Deadline" /> animator,<ref name="THR" /> director,<ref name="LAPPG">{{WebCite|url=https://lappg.com/2020/12/10/meet-director-producer-cinematographer-writer-vfx-supervisor-editor-colorist-bruce-logan-asc|text=Meet Director, Producer, Cinematographer, Writer, VFX Supervisor, Editor, Colorist Bruce Logan, ASC|work=the [https://lappg.com ''Los Angeles Post Production Group'']|archivedate=20250529002154}}</ref> and visual effects artist<ref name="NY Times" /> among many more.<ref name="LAPPG" /> He was known for his work on ''[[Wikipedia:2001: A Space Odyssey|2001: A Space Odyssey]]'', ''[[Wikipedia:Tron|Tron]]'', and ''[[Wikipedia:Airplane!|Airplane!]]''.<ref name="NY Times" /> For ''[[Star Wars]]'', he worked on second unit photography for the miniature and optical effects unit on [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

He died in [[Wikipedia:Los Angeles|Los Angeles]] in the [[Wikipedia:United States|United States]] after a short illness.<ref name="THR" />
