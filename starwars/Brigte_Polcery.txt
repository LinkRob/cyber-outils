'''Brigte Polcery''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] [[colonist]] on the [[planet]] [[Cyphar]]. She was one of the three conspirators hired by [[Nevil Cygni]] to create dispute between the [[Afe clan]] and the human colonists. The real reason was that the Afe territory was full of [[spice]] called [[Scarn (spice)|scarn]]. [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] ambushed and captured the raiders including their leaders.<ref name="Thrawn" />
