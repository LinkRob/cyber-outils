The '''Abandoned Workshop''' was a workshop and research facility inhabited by [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] during his journey and stay on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Bogano]]. It was built with the use of [[DUM-series pit droid|pit droids]] and consisted of two floors. The workshop was built into the geology of the planet, and could be entered from several regions of the planet. Various types of equipment were present in the facility, each with different uses, including a [[weather sensor]] and a water filtration system.

In the workshop, Cordova researched [[Zeffonian]] technology and the local wildlife, and created detailed illustrations of the [[Homeworld|native]] fauna. The workshop had been abandoned by the [[time]] of the [[Imperial Era]], and in [[14 BBY]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] visited the workshop with his newly met [[droid]] companion [[BD-1]].

==Description==
[[File:AbandonedWorkshop-BottomFloor.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The bottom floor of the Abandoned Workshop]]
Located on<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Bogano DB">{{Databank|bogano|Bogano}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Bogano]], the Abandoned Workshop was owned and operated by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]]. It could be entered from several locations: the [[Fractured Plain]]s, [[Bogdo Sinkholes]], [[Great Divide]], and the [[Hermit's Abode]]. From the Great Divide to the workshop, a small [[metal]] bridge was placed. The facility was built into the [[limestone]] geology of the planet.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

The workshop consisted of two levels. The lower level had a large, circular entrance from the Great Divide, and it consisted of a massive pool of [[water]], with only one small limestone island and pillar within. Also inside the pool were lights that were installed by Cordova. One of the islands stood tall and had [[vine]]s on the side which could be used to climb up to the base of the workshop's staircase. Behind the pillar, four lamps hung off the natural walls. The second level was accessible down a long hall leading further up the staircase. A circular room with seven lamps, it contained the tools and items Cordova had used and collected, with Zeffo statues, pottery, and glyphs scattered about. The room contained a [[lightsaber]] workbench, as well as another table on which laid Cordova's repair kit and sketches. At the top of the stairs was a door that led out to the surface.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> The workshop also contained water filtration systems, dehumidifying fans, and mechanical slits to maintain the structure's internal climate.<ref name="Art JFO">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

==History==
===Creation and use===
Jedi Master Eno Cordova traveled to Bogano during his study of the [[Zeffonian]] [[species]], and commissioned [[DUM-series pit droid]]s<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> to assist him<ref name="Art JFO" /> with the workshop's construction. He studied the Zeffo's [[Bogano Vault]] and the [[Homeworld|native]] flora and fauna of the planet. On the land above his workshop, he built a [[weather sensor]] in order to know when to wear a sweater. However, he instead discovered that there was much less [[Electromagnetic wind|electromagnetic radiation]] in the winds as opposed to the Zeffo homeworld of the [[Zeffo|same name]]. This led him to theorize this as the reason for the Bogano Vault having been built on Bogano. Cordova used the scraps of the [[droid]]s that helped him build it in order to form a cairn in their honor.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

===Rebuilding the Jedi Order===
By [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFODate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Imperial Era]], Cordova had left the workshop. His personal droid [[BD-1]] remained on Bogano, with all of Cordova's research, theories, and personal recordings of himself left for a traveler who would encounter the droid.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="JFODate" /> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] visited Bogano as part of his [[secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
While on Bogano, Kestis and BD-1, who had become Kestis' companion, visited the workshop. Entering from the Great Divide, he used the [[Force push]] ability to push down the bridge allowing him to enter the workshop through the bottom level. Through the use of [[psychometry]], Kestis learned more about the workshop and additionally found several chests. In them, the Jedi found the emitter and switch for a [[Valor and Wisdom]] lightsaber, materials for the "Offshore" [[poncho]] style, and [[Laminasteel|lamina steel]] which could be used as a lightsaber component.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

Using Cordova's workbench, Kestis took the [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] of [[Eno Cordova's lightsaber|Cordova's lightsaber]] from the table and integrated it into his [[Jaro Tapal's lightsaber|own lightsaber]] to transform it from a single-bladed lightsaber into a [[double-bladed lightsaber]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:AbandonedWorkshop-Concept.png|thumb|right|250px|Gabe Yeganyan's concept illustration of the Abandoned Workshop]]
The Abandoned Workshop first appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]]. In the game, the player can choose to acquire the double-bladed lightsaber from several locations, one of which is the Abandoned Workshop.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> While developing the workshop, it was imagined that Eno Cordova's studies of the Bogano landscape and fauna might have inspired the the workshop itself. Concept illustration for the workshop was done by artists [[Gabe Yeganyan]] and [[Liam MacDonald]].<ref name="Art JFO" />
