The '''41st Elite Corps''', simply known as the '''Elite Corps''', was a [[Republic Military|military]] [[corps]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. The 41st Elite Corps, which consisted of sub-units such as [[Green Company]], was composed of [[clone trooper]]s under the leadership of [[Jedi General]] [[Luminara Unduli]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Despite being a corps itself, the 41st was organized under the [[9th Assault Corps]] and at times designated as the '''41st Elite Legion''' or '''41st Legion'''.

==Characteristics==
===Organization===
[[File:SWBF2_41st_Elite_updated.jpg|thumb|left|150px|One type of color combination of the 41st Elite Corps.]]
The 41st Elite Corps was a [[corps]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], and as such consisted of 36,864 [[trooper]]s divided into sixteen [[regiment]]s.<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] establishes that a [[corps]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] contains 36,864 [[clone trooper]]s divided into sixteen [[regiment]]s. Since the 41st Elite Corps is a corps within the Grand Army, it must have 36,864 troopers divided into sixteen regiments.</ref> One subunit of the 41st was [[Green Company]], which was led by "[[Green Leader (clone trooper)|Green Leader]]" and a [[Unidentified Clone Captain (Tranquility)|Clone Captain]].<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> Another was the [[41st Scout Battalion]], which participated in the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]].<ref name="Episode III" /> The [[41st Ranger Platoon]] was a [[Special forces|strike team]] within the 41st Elite Corps.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

The 41st Elite Corps was led by [[Jedi General]] [[Luminara Unduli]], whilst [[Clone Commander]] [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]] served as the second-in-command.<ref name="Gree DB" /> [[Clone Captain]] [[Jek]]<ref name="Battles" /> and Unduli's Jedi [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Barriss Offee]], was also part of the corps. The 41st Elite Corps consisted of standard [[clone trooper]]s, as well as [[clone scout trooper]]s, [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|Advanced Recon Force Scout Troopers]],<ref name="Episode III" /> and [[Clone trooper pilot|clone pilots]]. One [[pilot]] in the corps held the rank of Clone [[Captain]].<ref name="Weapons Factory" /> [[Jek]] served in the [[Ranger Corps]] with the rank of captain.<ref name="Battles" /> One battalion in the corps fell under the command of Jedi General [[Depa Billaba]]. For command, she was joined by her Padawan, the Jedi Commander [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]], and two Clone Captains,<ref name="Topps Note" /> [[Grey (clone trooper)|Grey]] and [[Styles]], who both helped them as major unit leaders.<ref name="Kanan 1">[[Kanan 1|''Kanan'' 1]]</ref> Another officer in Billaba's battalion was [[Clone Lieutenant]] [[Remo]].<ref name="Topps Note" />

===Equipment===
The 41st Elite Corps had several varying identifying [[color]] schemes for the unit. Standard clone troopers of the corps had olive green markings<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> with many different patterns, light green markings,<ref name="Weapons Factory" /> gray markings, and green camouflage on their [[armor]].<ref name="Episode III" /> Standard clone troopers made use of the [[phase I clone trooper armor]]<ref name="Weapons Factory" /> and later [[phase II clone trooper armor]]. Soldiers of the 41st Elite Corps were typically equipped with [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]s, [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s,<ref name="Episode III" /> [[RPS-6 rocket launcher]]s,<ref name="Weapons Factory" /> and more.<ref name="Episode III" />

===Vehicles===
[[File:LAATs-WF.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The 41st Elite Corps en route to Skywalker's position on Geonosis to reinforce him.]]
The 41st Elite Corps used a variety of vehicles. The corps' aerial component consisted of [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] and [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/carrier|LAAT carriers]] for transportation. Ground vehicles consisted of [[walker]]s such as [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s,<ref name="Weapons Factory" /> [[All Terrain Attack Pod]]s, and [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s. Other ground vehicles in its arsenal included [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]]s, [[Infantry Support Platform]]s, and [[BARC speeder]]s. At times, members of the 41st Elite Corps were present aboard [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] such as the [[Guarlara (Venator-class)|''Guarlara'']], where they manned its guns,<ref name="Episode III" /> the ''[[Tranquility]]'', and, on at least one occasion, a [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|''Consular''-class cruiser]].<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

==History==
===The Clone Wars===
====Arresting Nute Gunray====
{{Quote|Take this Separatist scum away.|A member of Green Company orders the arrest of Gunray|<ref name="Bombad Jedi">{{TCW|Bombad Jedi}}</ref>}}
[[File:Gunray_surrounded.png|thumb|left|225px|Green Company captures Viceroy Gunray]]
After the [[Clone Wars]] erupted with the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]],<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> the 41st Elite Corps<ref name="Hasbro">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars Rebels (toy line)|text=''Star Wars Rebels'' Saga Legends|pack=#15: Clone Commander Gree|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/SL15-Clone-Commander-Gree-Rebels-Saga-Legends.asp}}</ref> was part of the Grand Army of the Republic and was placed under the command of Jedi General Luminara Unduli,<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> her Jedi Padawan [[Jedi Commander]] Barriss Offee,<ref name="Weapons Factory" /> and Clone Commander Gree.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> Early during the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars, [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]] was captured during the [[Mission to Rodia]] by [[Viceroy of the Trade Federation]] [[Nute Gunray]] and Separatist forces. After her [[droid]], [[C-3PO]], managed to contact a Republic clone trooper, the ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer ''Tranquility'' was dispatched and ferried Green [[Company]] to [[Rodia]]. Green Company, led by [[Commander]] Gree, landed in Rodia's [[Senatorial Palace]] via LAAT/i [[gunship]]s and arrested Viceroy Gunray after linking up with Senator Amidala, [[Junior Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]], C-3PO, and Senator [[Onaconda Farr]]. After the arrest, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] thanked the senators and Binks for their help via a [[hologram]] set up by clone troopers, and dispatched supplies to Rodia immediately.<ref name="Bombad Jedi" />

====Attack on the ''Tranquility''====
{{Quote|Green Company, prepare to repel the enemy.|Commander Gree, to Green Company|<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />}}
Green Company brought Gunray aboard a ''Consular''-class cruiser from the surface of the planet to the ''Tranquility''. General Unduli and Padawan [[Ahsoka Tano]] accompanied Gunray as security, and when the [[cruiser]] docked with the ''Tranquility'', Gunray was brought to the ship's brig with the help of [[Senate Commando]]s so that he could be interrogated en route to [[Coruscant]]. Separatist forces, however, quickly created a strategy to either [[Death|kill]] Gunray or rescue him, so that Separatist secrets could not be revealed. Separatist [[Head of State]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] dispatched his [[assassin]], [[Asajj Ventress]], along with a group of Separatist light starships. After some time of interrogation, three [[Droch-class boarding ship|''Droch''-class boarding ships]] and [[Vulture-class starfighter|Vulture droid starfighters]] [[Rescue on the Tranquility|attacked]] the ''Tranquility''. Commander Gree, who was alongside the Jedi and Senate Commando Captain [[Faro Argyus]] when they felt the attack, contacted a [[Unidentified clone naval captain|clone naval captain]] in the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] requesting a status report. Upon hearing of Separatist engagement, Commander Gree deployed Green Company to repel Separatist boarders. The three boarding ships all successfully breached through the ''Tranquility''{{'s}} [[hull]] and deployed [[B2-series super battle droid]]s to the [[hangar]].<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

[[File:Green_Company.png|thumb|right|275px|Green Company engages Separatist forces onboard the ''Tranquility''.]]
At least twelve members of Green Company, including "Green Leader," initially went to the hangar to engage Separatist forces and attempt to stop them from spreading throughout the ship. During the skirmish in the hangar, Separatist forces prevailed and killed many clone troopers, including Green Leader. Republic forces ultimately failed in preventing Separatist forces from spreading, and the [[battle droid]]s headed to the prison level. In the aftermath of the skirmish in the hangar, Ventress stealthily deployed from a boarding ship and encountered a Green Company survivor, who attempted to alert the rest of Republic forces of Ventress but was beheaded before he could warn them. As Separatist units advanced, the rest of Green Company retreated to the prison level, all while engaging battle droids in the ship's hallways. While 41st Elite Corps forces were engaging Separatist troops, Ventress rigged explosives on the ''Tranquility''{{'s}} [[reactor]]. The Republic [[WED-15 Treadwell droid]] [[327-T]] conducted a perimeter sweep of the reactor room but Ventress managed to stay hidden. 327-T encountered a Clone Captain and a fellow trooper who arrived to check in with 327-T before Commander Gree called on them to head to the prison level. Eventually, Unduli and Green Company managed to destroy all Separatist battle droids, but sensed that their troubles were not over.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

During the attack, Padawan Tano had remained in the ship's brig with Captain Argyus and four Senate [[Commando]]s while Green Company, Unduli, and Commander Gree repelled the boarders. Ventress, however, infiltrates the brig and fights against Tano and the Senate Commandos, killing two and rendering Argyus and the remaining two guards unconscious. Unduli sensed the fight, and along with Gree and two other troopers, headed towards the brig and getting there just as Ventress freed Gunray and imprisoned Tano in a prison cell. Unduli ordered Ventress to surrender, but the explosive charges in the ''Tranquility''{{'s}} reactor room exploded, allowing Ventress to escape. Unduli ordered Tano to guard Gunray along with Agryus and Gree, while Unduli would pursue Ventress and the two clone troopers headed to another part of the ship. Unduli eventually managed to track down Ventress and engage her in a [[lightsaber duel]], but was defeated.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

[[File:TCW_Tranquility_hallway.png|thumb|left|275px|Fallen Green Company troopers in one of the hallways of the ''Tranquility'']]
Tano, who decided to defy Unduli's orders after speaking with Gree and Argyus, managed to rescue Unduli and continue to fight Ventress. Now having both Jedi occupied, Ventress sent Argyus a signal, which revealed Argyus as a traitor as he killed his fellow guards and engaged Gree. After fighting in [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]], Argyus managed to defeat Gree and free Gunray, fleeing to the ''Consular''-class cruiser that brought Gunray onboard initially, while at the same moment, Ventress managed to evade Tano and Unduli and, after killing two troopers, use a [[Republic escape pod]] to rendezvous with the cruiser nearby. After the Republic defeat, Unduli and Ahsoka reported to [[Yoda]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] about the loss as Green Company troopers stand guard on the bridge.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

====Attack on Geonosis====
{{Quote|We'll have to make a bold move if we're to draw them out of the factory and keep their attention on us and not the Padawans.''"<br />"''The best way to that is to march our troops straight up the middle, parade style. They can't resist that.''"<br />"''With no cover?''"<br />"''Well, we have to make it tempting.|Jedi Generals Luminara Unduli and Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Weapons Factory" />}}
[[File:Gree-FistPump.png|thumb|right|250px|Clone troopers celebrate a victory on Geonosis]]
The 41st was later sent to [[Geonosis]] to [[Second Battle of Geonosis|retake]] the planet. There, they assisted Jedi General Anakin Skywalker and the [[501st Legion]] in distracting the [[Geonosian]] leader [[Poggle the Lesser]] at his [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|primary droid foundry]] while Jedi Commanders Ahsoka Tano and Barriss Offee infiltrated the foundry. Clone trooper [[Draa]] and the others encountered heavy resistance and fell back with the 501st as Poggle unleashed the [[Separatist super-tank prototype|super tanks]]. Despite the tanks, the foundry was destroyed.<ref name="Weapons Factory" /> They then took part in cleaning up the remaining forces on Geonosis and searched for Poggle. During the clean up operations, Unduli and clone trooper [[Buzz]] used BARC speeders to search for Poggle, eventually tracking him to the [[Progate Temple]]. There, Buzz was killed and Unduli was captured, although she managed to contact the other Jedi during her captivity. Eventually, Unduli was rescued by Republic forces and the temple was destroyed.<ref name="Legacy of Terror">{{TCW|Legacy of Terror}}</ref>

===Closing stages of the war===
{{Dialogue|Ki-Adi-Mundi|What about the droid attack on the Wookiees?|Mace Windu|It is critical we send an attack group there immediately.|Obi-Wan Kenobi|He's right, it's a system we cannot afford to lose.|Yoda|Go, I will. Good relations with the Wookiees, I have.|Mace Windu|It's settled, then. Yoda will take a battalion of clones to reinforce the Wookiees on Kashyyyk.|attr=Jedi Generals [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], [[Mace Windu]], Obi-Wan Kenobi, and Yoda dispatch the 41st to Kashyyyk|src=<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:41st_assembls_on_Coruscant.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Members of the 41st Elite Corps assemble on Coruscant.]]
During the war, the 41st Ranger Platoon, a subunit of the 41st Elite Corps, conducted missions behind enemy lines to sabotage supply lines and vulnerable installations.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Jedi General Depa Billaba's battalion<ref name="Kanan 9">[[Kanan 9|''Kanan'' 9]]</ref> of the 41st<ref name="Topps Ebay">{{Topps|set=2021 Topps Star Wars: The Bad Batch|cardname=Clone Captain Grey|link=https://www.ebay.com/itm/353464296687}}</ref> took part in the [[skirmish on Kardoa]],<ref name="Kanan 9" /> the [[Third Battle of Mygeeto]],<ref name="Kanan 10">[[Kanan 10|''Kanan'' 10]]</ref> and the [[Battle of Kaller]]. Under the leadership of Billaba, Jedi Commander Caleb Dume, and their<ref name="Kanan 1" /> Clone Captains,<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref><ref name="Kanan 1" /> Grey and Styles, their battalion engaged with General [[Kleeve]]'s occupying droid forces on [[Kaller]] until Kleeve withdrew.<ref name="Kanan 1" />

During the [[Battle of Coruscant]], troopers of the 41st Elite Corps were stationed aboard the ''Guarlara'' when it exchanged broadsides with the Separatist [[flagship]] ''[[Invisible Hand]]''. During the broadside exchange, troopers of the 41st manned the [[cannon]]s and many were killed when their stations were destroyed by Separatist fire, although the ''Guarlara'' was victorious from the broadside. Following the Battle of Coruscant, the 41st Elite Corps assembled at a [[Coruscant shipyard|Coruscant cruiser staging area]] as equipment and vehicles were loaded unto a ''Venator''-class [[Star Destroyer]] and Jedi General Yoda arrived to command a sub-unit of the corps, the 41st Scout Battalion, before they were to be dispatched to [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:Gree-CTRaF.png|thumb|right|220px|Commander Gree led the 41st under General Unduli until his death during the execution of Order 66.]]
The 41st [[Scout]] [[Battalion]] arrived on Kashyyyk and unloaded vehicles and equipment, preparing themselves alongside the [[Kachirho Wookiee Militia]] to defend the [[city]] of [[Kachirho]] from Separatist forces.<ref name="Episode III" /> While on Kashyyyk the 41st Scout Battalion was led by Jedi Generals Yoda and Luminara Unduli.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> Eventually, the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]] erupted and the battalion defended the beachhead against the Separatists, taking positions on the beach itself as well as the large [[tree]]s surrounding the city as well as structures.<ref name="Episode III" />

In the midst of the battle, Supreme Chancellor Palpatine of the Republic, secretly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious, executed [[Order 66]], an initiative that labeled the Jedi as traitors. Commander Gree received the message from Palpatine in the command center, so he<ref name="Episode III" /> and Clone Captain Jek of the Ranger Corps<ref name="Battles" /> moved into position to execute Yoda. Yoda, however, sensed the deaths of his fellow Jedi across [[the galaxy]], allowing him to decapitate Gree and Jek before they could fire their [[blaster]]s.<ref name="Episode III" />

The two [[Wookiee]]s beside Yoda, [[Chewbacca]] and General [[Tarfful]], then helped Yoda leave the city. Once the battle was over and day had turned to night, members of the battalion, which included ARF troopers on AT-RTs, hunted for Yoda but failed to stop him from escaping the planet. On the planet [[Utapau]], at least one member of the 41st also took part in the [[Battle of Utapau]] alongside the [[212th Attack Battalion]] of the [[7th Sky Corps]], running beside the orange-clad clones just as [[Clone Marshal Commander|Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]] received Order 66 from Sidious. Afterward, several members of the 41st Elite Corps were on Utapau alongside the 212th in the [[Occupation of Utapau|post-battle cleanup]] as Republic forces imprisoned their former [[Pau'an Warrior]] allies. Several members of the 41st Elite were near Cody as he requested an update during the search for Jedi General [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:DepaandGreyfightonKaller.png|thumb|left|300px|Depa Billaba's battalion fights on Kaller]]
Meanwhile,<ref name="Aftermath" /> the 41st<ref name="Topps Ebay" /> battalion operating under Billaba found itself defending Kaller when the world was besieged in General Grievous's post-Coruscant counterattack. Billaba, Dume, Grey and her soldiers were aided by [[Clone Force 99]] before the execution of Order 66. While Billaba was executed by Grey and her other troops, she was able to urge Dume to run. While scared of Clone Force 99, he successfully escaped.<ref name="Aftermath" /> Under Grey and Styles's leadership,<ref name="Kanan 4">[[Kanan 4|''Kanan'' 4]]</ref> the clones then carried out a [[hunt for Caleb Dume]]<ref name="Kanan 2">[[Kanan 2|''Kanan'' 2]]</ref> until Grey, regaining his sense of self after Order 66, [[Rescue of Caleb Dume|sacrificed himself]] to protect his former friend<ref name="Kanan 5">[[Kanan 5|''Kanan'' 5]]</ref> in [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
