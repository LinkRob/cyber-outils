'''Darlo''' was a<ref name="BMF20" /> dim [[Color|white]]<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> [[star]] located in the [[Dagobah system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[Sluis sector]]. It was located 50,250 [[light-year]]s from the [[Core Worlds|Core]] in M-19 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. The [[planet]] [[Dagobah]] filled its second orbital position.<ref name="BMF20" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Darlo was mentioned in the [[2015]] magazine [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 20|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 20]].<ref name="BMF20" /> The [[Darlo/Legends|star]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2006]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] guide ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War: Forces of Corruption Expansion: Prima Official Game Guide]]'', which was written by [[Michael Knight]].<ref name="EaWGuide">[[Star Wars: Empire at War: Forces of Corruption Expansion: Prima Official Game Guide|''Star Wars: Empire at War: Forces of Corruption Expansion'': Prima Official Game Guide]]</ref>
