{{Quote|This is a meeting where every voice counts, does it not? Then let me ask you two. How do you vote? Are we an Empire of curs and cuckoo hens? Clucking and whimpering in the dark?|Valco Pandion, to the Imperials at the Akiva summit|<ref name="Aftermath" />}}
'''Cuckoo hens''' were a type of [[chicken]] that made clucking sounds. By the [[time]] of the [[Imperial Era]], being compared to one of the hens could be considered a symbol of cowardice. While attending an [[Akiva Summit|emergency summit]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> [[Grand Moff]] [[Valco Pandion]] berated his fellow [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] for their lack of courage to attack the nascent [[New Republic]], questioning if they were part of an Empire consisting of cuckoo hens.<ref name="Aftermath" />
