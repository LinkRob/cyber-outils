'''Carolyn Lee Hennesy''' is an American actress who voiced [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] in ''[[LEGO Star Wars: All-Stars]]''<ref name="All Stars">{{All-Stars|From Trenches to Wrenches: The Roger Story (episode)}}</ref> and ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''.<ref name="Resistance">{{Resistance|Station Theta Black (episode)}}</ref> She also portrayed [[Domine Lithe]] in the [[2019]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. Hennesy is known for playing Diane Miller on ''[[Wikipedia:General Hospital|General Hospital]]'' and playing recurring roles on TV series such as ''[[Wikipedia:Dawson's Creek|Dawson's Creek]]'', ''[[Wikipedia:Cougar Town|Cougar Town]]'', [[Wikipedia:Revenge (TV series)|''Revenge'']], [[Wikipedia:Jessie (2011 TV series)|''Jessie'']] and [[Wikipedia:The Bay (web series)|''The Bay'']].
