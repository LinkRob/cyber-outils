'''Agustin''' was a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] who served the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] alongside their master, [[Mayanka Catrine]]. Agustin accompanied their master on a mission to the [[Outpost 716]] to capture [[Asajj Ventress]]. After successfully apprehending Ventress, Agustin and Catrine left on a separate mission, creating an opening for Ventress to [[Capture of Outpost 716|overtake]] the outpost.<ref name="HS 5" />
