'''Beyond Creative''' is an [[Wikipedia:Republic of Ireland|Irish]] game development studio operating in ''[[Fortnite]] Creative'' the Unreal Editor for ''Fortnite''. They collaborated with [[Epic Games]] and [[Lucasfilm]] in [[2025]] to develop the ''Star Wars Watch Party'' island, which premiered the first two episodes of ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Underworld]]''.<ref name="Watch Party">{{Twitter|username=FNCreate|url=status/1917280877815439855|name=[[Fortnite|''Fortnite'' Developers]]|date=2025-04-29|quote=Shout out to @BeyondFN for their amazing work on the Star Wars Watch Party Island 👏|archivefile=File:Fortnite-BeyondCreativeStarWarsWatchPartyTOTU-Twitter.png}}</ref>

They collaborated with Epic Games and Lucasfilm again in [[2026]] to develop the game mode ''[[Star Wars: Escape Vader]]'', and worked with [[Jon Favreau]]'s creative studio Fairview Portals to create ''[[The Mandalorian and Grogu Watch Party]]'' island, set on the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]], which released on [[May 19]] and features a 10 minute sneak peek of [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu|the film]].<ref name="Web">{{SW|url=news/fortnite-star-wars-may|text=Step Inside the ''Star Wars'' Galaxy in Fortnite This May}}</ref>
