The '''Eellayin''' were a civilization who resided on [[Polis Massa]] before their extinction. By the end of the [[Clone Wars]], some [[Kallidahin]] [[archaeologist]]s and [[Biologist|exobiologist]]s believed the Eellayin were their [[species]]' ancestor, so they began excavating Polis Massa in an attempt to recover Eellayin tissues and use [[Kaminoan]] [[cloning]] technology to recreate life.<ref name="CE" />
