'''Charth Brethen''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|male]] who served as an [[ambassador]] for the [[Ryloth Defense Authority]], a faction of [[Ryloth]]'s government that wished to remain free from oppression or [[slavery]]. Brethen, who was the son of [[Yendor Brethen|Yendor]], and his sister [[Hahnee Brethen|Hahnee]] welcomed the [[Resistance]] personnel aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' onto Ryloth, and eventually joined the group.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />
