The '''Battle of Galov''',<ref name="The High Republic 9">[[The High Republic (2021) 9|''The High Republic'' (2021) 9]]</ref> also known as the '''Battle of Relay Post''',<ref name="Script">[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner (script)|''The High Republic: Tempest Runner'' script]]</ref> was a conflict between the combined forces of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Jedi Order]] against [[Nihil]] Raiders lead by [[Lourna Dee]], who were attempting to capture [[Relay Post Epsilon One]] at the direction of Nihil leader [[Marchion Ro]]. When it became clear that the Nihil would not be able to capture the station, Dee fired on the station with a [[turbolaser]], setting off a chain reaction that would eventually lead to its destruction. After the battle ended with a Galactic Republic and Jedi Order victory, the Republic captured most of the Nihil forces, including the entire crew of the cruiser [[Lourna Dee (cruiser)|''Lourna Dee'']] and Lourna Dee herself. But the Republic was unaware they had Dee in custody, believing her to have died in the battle.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />
