'''Fori Nagor''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Twi'lek]] who was born by around [[407 BBY]]. Around [[381 BBY]], she visited a [[Unidentified public house|public house]] in the [[Unidentified settlement (Dantooine)|oldest settlement]] on the [[planet]] [[Dantooine]]. There, she noticed the [[Evereni]] [[pirate]] leader [[Marda Ro]] and became startled when she recognized Ro's [[species]]. When the Evereni woman left the public house, Nagor followed her into an alley where Ro was waiting for the Twi'lek. She confronted Nagor and offered her a position in her pirate [[Nihil|group]]. Nagor accepted the offer and worked alongside the [[human]] [[Milan Stor]].

At some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]], Nagor, Stor, and the [[Kessurian]] partners [[Esstrop]] and [[Kortanio]] who also served Ro attacked a refugee camp. Capturing the refugees, they brought them to Ro's [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', where Nagor presented them to her leader. Ro was enraged at the marauders for capturing other beings and ordered Nagor to let them go at the next [[spaceport]] with the exception of a [[Unidentified green-skinned man|green-skinned man]]. She also threatened the Twi'lek to never capture others again and told Nagor that if she wants more raiders, she can simply recruit them.

Nagor would go on to recruit more pirates, and some time later her [[Fori's team|team]] was involved in a firefight with a [[Star system|system]] security, during which two of Nagor's raiders [[Death|perished]]. Later, Ro mentioned the Twi'lek in [[Marda Ro's journal|journal entries]] she recorded for her [[Ro family|descendants]], which her great-great-grandson [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to by [[252 BBY]].

==Biography==
===Meeting Marda Ro===
{{Quote|I've heard stories about you. Your people.''"<br />"''What do you want?''"<br />"''I want to touch you.|Fori Nagor and Marda Ro|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
[[File:MardaRo-PathOfDeceit.png|thumb|right|190px|Fori Nagor followed Marda Ro (pictured) out of a public house.]]
Fori Nagor was a [[Gender|female]] [[Twi'lek]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> who was born by around [[407 BBY]].<ref name="Birth" /> Around [[381 BBY]],<ref name="DantooineDate">"[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" establishes that [[Marda Ro]] visited [[Dantooine]] around a [[Standard year|year]] after taking possession of the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', an event that ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]]. Therefore, Ro's trip to Dantooine must have occurred around [[381 BBY]].</ref> Nagor was present in the [[Unidentified settlement (Dantooine)|oldest settlement]] on the [[planet]] [[Dantooine]] and visited a [[Unidentified public house|public house]] in the settlement. She was in the public house when [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the [[Evereni]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> founder of a group of [[pirate]]s who would eventually become the [[Nihil]]<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref>&mdash;visited it during<ref name="Closed Fist" /> her<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> search for others of her [[species]]. While passing by Ro, Nagor recognized her as an Evereni. She stopped for a moment and her [[eye]]s widened before she continued walking again.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

When Ro left the public house, Nagor followed her into an alley, stopping when she noticed Ro was standing still and facing her direction. Ro drew a [[Staff|stave]] wrapped in [[mynock leather]] against her and confronted Nagor, asking what she wants. Nagor explained that she had heard stories about Evereni and was only interested in touching Ro. Ro then offered her a position in her band of pirates. Nagor accepted the offer in exchange for a kiss and was taken by Ro aboard her ship, the ''[[Gaze Electric]]''.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Nagor became one of Ro's raiders, and worked alongside the [[human]] [[Milan Stor]]. The two used her [[Fori's ship|ship]] to work and looked for opportunities to smuggle or steal things. Sometimes, they would carry out larger raids with the help of the [[Kessurian]] partners [[Esstrop]] and [[Kortanio]].<ref name="Closed Fist" />

===Capture of refugees===
{{Quote|What did they expect me to do with people? Sell them? To the Hutts? I could not trust prisoners or let them loose on my ship. I could not&mdash;I did not&mdash;one thing I would not take was freedom.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
[[File:GazeElectricpaths.jpg|thumb|left|280px|Fori Nagor presented the refugees to Marda Ro aboard the ''Gaze Electric''. (pictured)]]
They conducted one such attack<ref name="Closed Fist" /> at some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]],<ref name="RaidDate">The segment of "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" depicting a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi's]] [[death]] onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' takes place around [[379 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:ClosedFistJediDeathDate|here]]. The raid in which the [[Unidentified green-skinned man|green-skinned man]] and other refugees were captured by [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Pirate|raider]]s occurs after the [[Jedi]]'s death; therefore, it must have taken place no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> raiding a refugee camp. The refugees lacked any valuable posessions, so as a result the marauders decided to take the refugees themselves instead. Once they returned to the ''Gaze Electric'', Nagor took the captured refugees to the ''Gaze's'' [[throne hall]]. She presented the refugees to Ro, who stared at them and was furious at the actions of Nagor and the other raiders.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Ro asked the refugees whether any of them had the ability to use [[the Force]] and while she waited for an answer, her [[Aide|assistant]] [[Alirya]] walked over to kneel next to Ro. Receiving no answer from the refugees, Ro threatened them with leaving their fate up to the raiders who worked for her and a [[Unidentified green-skinned man|green-skinned man]] ended up confessing to being [[Force-sensitive]]. Ro announced to the other refugees that they can join her group if they wish so, otherwise they will be freed at the next [[spaceport]], then walked over to Nagor and ordered her to never capture other beings again, threatening her with a knife against her [[lekku]]. She reluctantly agreed and led the refugees away, leaving only Ro, Alirya, and the man in the throne hall. After Nagor left, Ro then took the man to the [[Great Leveler]], a [[Shrikarai|monster]] that fed on the [[Living Force]].<ref name="Closed Fist" />

===Leading a team and legacy===
{{Quote|Fori lost two of her team in a firefight, escaping from some cheap system security.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
At some point after the incident with the refugees, Nagor found herself leading a [[Fori's team|team]] of raiders for Ro. The team ended up in a fight against a [[Star system|system]] security, and two of the team's members died while escaping the security. As a consequence, the ''Gaze'' was taken away from known systems and Ro's group laid low for a while. The team was still active by the time Ro threw a feast in the throne hall during the Evereni twins [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]]'s stay with the pirates and a [[Unidentified team member|member]] of the team baked [[finger cake]]s for the occasion. Later, Ro made a [[Marda Ro's journal|recording]] for her [[Ro family|descendants]], where she mentioned Nagor.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during his youth, Ro's great-great-grandson [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to her journal, including the entries where Nagor was mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Do not bother me with this sort of thing again. If you want to recruit, recruit. I am not interested in sentient prizes.''"<br />"''Fine.|Marda Ro and Fori Nagor|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
Nagor had pink skin and a scar on her forehead. Her [[Tooth|teeth]] were sharp, but less so than Evereni teeth. During her first meeting with Ro her lips were painted black.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> Nagor was slightly taller than Ro, who measured 1.68 [[meter]]s (5 [[Foot|ft]] 6 in),<ref name="Height" /> but only due to her lekku. She was the first person brought aboard the ''Gaze Electric'' by Ro who had never heard about the [[Path of the Open Hand]]&mdash;a cult Ro used to be a member of. Nagor did not have any qualms regarding the concept of [[slavery]] and had no issues taking away refugees against their will,<ref name="Closed Fist" /> despite her species' history of enslavement.<ref name="Helmet">{{HelmetCollectionCite|58|Databank A-Z}}</ref>

==Equipment==
{{Quote|She was dressed like any trader or smuggler: jumpsuit, plentiful pockets, straps for weapons and tools and bags. Her lekku were wrapped at the base with dark lace.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
Nagor wrapped her lekku with a dark lace at their base. She wore clothing typical for traders and smugglers&mdash;a [[jumpsuit]] with numerous pockets and straps for weapons and tools&mdash;and possessed a blaster. Fori also owned a ship.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fori Nagor appeared in flashbacks in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" />
