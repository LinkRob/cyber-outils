'''Defels''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] whose members had the ability to become almost invisible by bending light around themselves. Shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]], a Defel named [[Arleil Schous]] was a patron of [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] when [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] arrived in search of [[transport]] to [[Alderaan]]. [[Glahst Ombra]] was a Defel [[mercenary]] who worked with [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] until she was [[Death|killed]] and skinned by [[Magna Tolvan]].

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Got that creepy shadow aura trick going on with the fur.|Chelli Aphra, on Glahst Ombra's hiding ability|<ref name="Aphra 15" />}}
Defels were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] with [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]], two red [[eye]]s, sharp [[Tooth|teeth]], and two elongated [[ear]]s that were pointed upwards. They had two [[Gender|sexes]], male<ref name="BH">''[[Bounty Hunt]]''</ref> and female.<ref name="Aphra 15" /> Defels had the ability to bend light around themselves to become practically invisible. Elderly Defel, however, tended to lose that ability.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> Defels had a good sense of smell, being able to scent at least a molecule of [[blood]] from six miles away.<ref name="Aphra 15" /> They could feast on sentient species, but preferred the meat liquified.<ref name="Aphra 16">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 16|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 16]]</ref>

==Defels in the galaxy==
{{Quote|Aren't these guys like a movable buffet for you?''"<br />"''Only if you like your meat served liquified.|[[Sister Six]] and Glahst Ombra|<ref name="Aphra 16" />}}
[[File:GlahstOmera-2016DoctorAphra15.png|thumb|right|150px|The Defel mercenary Glahst Ombra]]
[[Arleil Schous]] was a male Defel fortune hunter who visited [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> when [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] arrived in search of a [[pilot]] who could take them to [[Alderaan]].<ref name="ANH" /> [[Glahst Ombra]] was a female Defel [[mercenary]] who was hired by the [[Son-tuul Pride]] to join [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]]<ref name="Aphra 15" /> on a [[Mission to Skako Minor (Son-tuul Pride)|mission]] to [[Skako Minor]], where she was [[Death|killed]] and skinned by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Lieutenant-Inspector]] [[Magna Tolvan]].<ref name="Aphra 16" />
