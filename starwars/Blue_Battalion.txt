'''Blue Battalion''' was a training [[battalion]] in the [[Imperial Academy]]. Alongside [[Red Battalion]], Blue Battalion was placed in the command of [[Imperial cadet]]s and contained [[stormtrooper]] units that were rotated off active duty for training. The battalion was led by the cadet [[Rav Horan]].<ref name="Secrets" />
