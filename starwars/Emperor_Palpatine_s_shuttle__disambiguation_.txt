'''Emperor Palpatine's shuttle''' may refer to:

==[[Canon]]==
*'''[[Palpatine's H-2 executive shuttle]]'''&mdash; an [[H-2 executive shuttle]] used to escape from the [[Imperial Executive Building|Republic Executive Building]] during the [[Zillo Beast incident]]
*'''[[Darth Sidious' Eta-class shuttle]]'''&mdash; an ''Eta''-class shuttle used by Palpatine under his [[Darth Sidious]] persona to travel to [[Mandalore]] and stop [[Maul]]'s empire.
*[[Theta-class T-2c shuttle|''Theta''-class T-2c shuttle]]&mdash; a ''Theta''-class shuttle utilized by Palpatine as his personal transport early in his reign as [[Galactic Emperor]].
*'''[[Emperor Palpatine's shuttle (Rho-class transport shuttle)|Emperor Palpatine's ''Rho''-class shuttle]]'''&mdash; a [[Rho-class transport shuttle]] utilized by Palpatine during his visit to [[Tantiss Base]].
*'''[[Emperor's personal shuttle]]'''&mdash; a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] used by Palpatine as his personal transport during the [[Imperial Era]].
*''[[Imperialis]]'' and its [[Imperialis (Jakku)|replica]]&mdash; Palpatine's personal pleasure craft.

==[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]]==
*'''[[Palpatine's transport]]'''&mdash; a vessel that brought [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] to [[Nar Shaddaa/Legends|Nar Shaddaa]] in order to retrieve [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] and transport them back to [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]
*'''[[Palpatine's H-2 executive shuttle/Legends|Palpatine's H-2 executive shuttle]]'''&mdash; an [[H-2 executive shuttle/Legends|H-2 executive shuttle]] used to escape from the [[Republic Executive Building/Legends|Republic Executive Building]] during the [[Zillo Beast incident/Legends|Zillo Beast incident]]
*'''[[Darth Sidious' Eta-class shuttle/Legends|Darth Sidious' ''Eta''-class shuttle]]'''&mdash; an [[Eta-class shuttle/Legends|''Eta''-class shuttle]] used by [[Palpatine/Legends|Darth Sidious]] to travel to [[Mandalore/Legends|Mandalore]]
*'''[[Palpatine's shuttle (bombing)|Palpatine's shuttle]]'''&mdash; a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle/Legends|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] destroyed on Coruscant during the [[Anti-Sith conspiracy]]
*'''[[Emperor's personal shuttle/Legends|Emperor's personal shuttle]]'''&mdash; a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle/Legends|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] used by [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] as his executive Imperial transport.
*The ''[[Emperor's Shadow (shuttle)|Emperor's Shadow]]''&mdash; a shuttle piloted by [[Jeng Droga]] which crashed on [[Kaal/Legends|Kaal]]
