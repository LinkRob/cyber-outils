'''Bettyjill''' was an [[Eirami]] [[fisher]] [[Homeworld|hailing]] from the [[planet]] [[Eiram]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], she worked alongside her fellow Eirami [[Nelly]] and [[Gorge]] as part of the [[Queen Xiri's crew|crew]] of the ''[[Queen Xiri]]'' [[fishing]] [[boat]] under its [[captain]], [[Okoro]]. When the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|crashed]] in Eiram's [[ocean]]s<ref name="Tales" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> its impact damaged the world's coastal [[city]] of [[Barraza City|Barraza]] and nearly swamped the ''Queen Xiri''.<ref name="Tales" />

Following the incident, Bettyjill opted to depart the sea trade, leaving her crew understaffed and [[Anti-Jedi sentiment|resentful]]&mdash;like most Eirami&mdash;toward the [[Jedi Order]] for their role in bringing the station to their world. One [[Standard month|month]] afterward, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] met with Okoro and offered to join his crew after learning about how their former crew member departed. Though the captain was skeptical of Zettifar, he allowed the Jedi to join them on their next cruise due to being shorthanded.<ref name="Tales" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bettyjill was mentioned in a flashback in the short story "[[All Jedi Walk Their Own Path]]," written by [[Charles Soule]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Tales" />
