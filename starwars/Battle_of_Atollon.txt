{{Quote|We'll never surrender to you, Thrawn.''"<br />"''You misunderstand, captain. I'm not accepting surrenders at this time. I want you to know failure. Utter defeat. And know that it is I who delivers it crashing down upon you.|Hera Syndulla and Thrawn|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
The '''Battle of Atollon''', also known as the '''Siege of Atollon''' or as the '''attack on Atollon''', broke out in the [[standard year]] [[2 BBY]] between the nascent [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] with the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Force-sensitive]] being known as [[Bendu]] on and above the planet [[Atollon]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. It started when [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] discovered the location of [[Chopper Base]], the headquarters of the [[Phoenix Cell]]. Thrawn's [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|7th Fleet]] blockaded [[Atollon]], trapping both [[Phoenix Squadron]] and [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]]'s [[Massassi Group]]. Thrawn's fleet inflicted heavy casualties on the two rebel forces. However, [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]]'s decision to ram [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]]'s [[Interdictor]] vessel allowed the rebels [[Ezra Bridger]] and [[C1-10P]] to escape and seek help.

In the second phase, while his fleet held a '''blockade of Atollon''', Thrawn launched a ground assault led by himself after an orbital bombardment of the base, while [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] assumed command in space. Meanwhile, on [[Mandalore]], Ezra succeeded in getting the help of [[Sabine Wren]], a former [[Spectres|Spectre]], [[Fenn Rau]], and several members of [[Clan Wren]] in destroying Thrawn's second Interdictor vessel. Despite Thrawn attacking [[Chopper Base]], [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]], and General Dodonna managed to escape with the surviving rebel fleet into space.

The rebels and Imperials were both attacked by an angry Bendu, an enigmatic [[Force wielder]], who used his [[The Force|Force]] powers to destroy Chopper Base before being subdued by Thrawn. The surviving rebel forces led by Hera Syndulla and General Dodonna fled to [[Yavin 4]]. A number of members of the Rebel Alliance, including [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] herself, saw the defeat at Atollon as proof their organization was not yet ready for open war.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Everything leading up to this moment has been rehearsal. The real performance is about to begin.|Grand Admiral Thrawn to Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin about the Rebel Alliance's assault on Lothal|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
In [[3 BBY]], the [[Phoenix Cell]] discovered [[Atollon]] thanks to the rebel [[C1-series astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] and the former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Inventory droid]] [[AP-5]].<ref name="Forgotten Droid">{{Rebels|The Forgotten Droid}}</ref> The rebels built a base called [[Chopper Base]] in the [[Atollon Coral Mesa]] to honor Chopper.<ref name="Mystery">{{Rebels|The Mystery of Chopper Base}}</ref> By [[2 BBY]], the Imperial [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] had turned his attentions to the Phoenix Cell in the [[Lothal sector]].<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>

[[File:Chopper_Base.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Chopper base]]
During the [[Infiltration of the Imperial Armory Complex|undercover mission]] on [[Lothal]], the rebels [[Ezra Bridger]], [[Kanan Jarrus]], and Chopper learnt about Thrawn's plans to build the [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter|TIE Defender]] prototype fighter in mass. They were also aided by the rebel [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] who became a rebel [[spy]] known as [[Fulcrum]].<ref name="An Inside Man">{{Rebels|An Inside Man}}</ref> Kallus had become sympathetic to the rebellion's cause after meeting [[Garazeb Orrelios]] on [[Bahryn]], as Orrelios saved him from dying on the Geonosian moon.<ref name="Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref>

Thrawn learnt that the rebels had a spy within the Imperial [[Rank insignia plaque|ranks]] during the Infiltration of the Imperial Armory Complex. His [[Rescue of Alexsandr Kallus|search]] for Fulcrum coincided with his search for Chopper Base, which involved the use of [[E-XD-series infiltrator droid]]s<ref name="Warhead">{{Rebels|Warhead (episode)}}</ref> and a [[IGV-55 surveillance vessel]].<ref name="DAD">{{Rebels|Double Agent Droid}}</ref> However, the rebels frustrated the Empire's efforts by an [[EXD-9's infiltration of Chopper Base|infiltrator droid's attempt]] at locating the base and [[Mission to recover Imperial codes|an attempt to retrieve the location from a droid]].<ref name="Warhead" /><ref name="DAD" /> Despite Kallus' attempts to stop Thrawn from looking for Fulcrum by framing [[Lieutenant]] [[Yogar Lyste]] as Fulcrum, Thrawn realized Kallus was Fulcrum but allowed him to continue spying so that he would lead him to Chopper Base.<ref name="TIE">{{Rebels|Through Imperial Eyes}}</ref>

Thrawn's spies later discovered that the Phoenix Squadron and the [[Massassi Group]] were planning to launch a coordinated attack against the TIE Defender factory on Lothal. After learning the two fleets were massing somewhere, Thrawn saw this opportunity as a chance to wipe them out with one swift strike, but [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] ordered that he instead take the rebellion's leadership prisoner, planning to make public examples of them. Agent Kallus was listening to the meeting through a [[MSE-6 series repair droid|mouse droid]] and when he tried to warn the Rebels, Thrawn caught him in the act. They fought each other in [[Melee weapon|melee]] combat and Thrawn successfully defeated Kallus without suffering injuries. Thrawn used the trajectory of Dodonna's fleet and Kallus' Fulcrum transmission to find a planet which didn't appear in Imperial [[Star map|charts]], Atollon. However, Thrawn knew about it thanks to his study of art of the [[sector]]. Thrawn later told [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]] to dispatch Thrawn's [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|fleet]] to Atollon.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

==The battle==
===Trapped on Atollon===
{{Quote|General Dodonna, Commander Sato, Captain Syndulla. At last, we meet in this theater of war, however briefly. There is no escape, and your forces are badly outnumbered. This, uh, "rebellion" ends today.|Grand Admiral Thrawn to Jan Dodonna, Jun Sato and Hera Syndulla|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
A section of Thrawn's Seventh Fleet consisting of five [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyers]] and two [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class cruisers]] arrived over Atollon, blockading the Atollon system and trapping both Rebel cells on the planet. Additionally, Thrawn had dispatched two [[Interdictor-class heavy cruiser|''Interdictor''-class heavy cruisers]] to prevent the rebel cells from escaping to [[hyperspace]]. At this time, Dodonna's forces consisted of three [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunships]], three [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]]s, and three [[CR90 corvette]]s; Phoenix Squadron's fleet consisted of four CR90 corvettes, three [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|Hammerhead corvettes]], two [[GR-75 medium transport]]s, and a [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|Quasar Fire-class bulk cruiser]]. Upon arriving, Konstantine jammed all long-range transmissions to prevent any reinforcements to assist the rebels. As soon as the Imperial fleet was in position, 

As soon as the Imperial fleet exited hyperspace, General Dodonna, realizing the rebel fleet was outnumbered and outgunned ordered his fleet to retreat to hyperspace, with Syndulla agreeing that the mission to Lothal had to be scrapped. [[Kassius Konstantine's Interdictor]] activated its [[gravity well projector]]s and pulled the first frigate to flee out of hyperspace, with Admiral Konstantine ordering his vessel's [[Laser Battery|batteries]] to destroy it.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

Shortly after, Thrawn arrived on his [[flagship]], the ''[[Chimaera]]'', which he positioned in the center of the [[Star Destroyer]] formation.  On board the ship was [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] of the Lothal sector and the captive rogue Kalllus. Noticing the Interdictor destroying Dodonna's first frigate, Pryce asked Thrawn that if he remembered Tarkin's orders to take prisoners, to which Thrawn replied that he knew Dodonna had courage and would not be on the first frigate to flee. Thrawn then contacted the Rebel leaders via [[hologram]], telling them that he'd end their rebellion and informing them that he would not be accepting any surrenders.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

Knowing that the rebel fleet was unable to break through Thrawn's blockade, [[Captain]] Syndulla proposed launching a diversionary attack in order to allow one of their ships to flee into hyperspace with news of the siege of Atollon. Bridger and Chopper were picked for the mission and the two were assigned the late [[Maul]]'s [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|Gauntlet starfighter]] [[Nightbrother (Gauntlet fighter)|''Nightbrother'']], which had been renamed ''Gauntlet''. Meanwhile, Jarrus traveled into Atollon's wilderness to seek the enigmatic [[Force wielder]] [[Bendu]]'s help.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

===The space battle===
{{Quote|Phoenix Squadron, keep those fighters busy. Green Squadron, attack the lead destroyer.|Hera Syndulla to Green and Phoenix Squadrons|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
<div style="float: right">[[File:WedgeAntillesRZ1Awing-SWZ83.png|thumb|250px|Wedge Antilles engages Imperial forces in an RZ-1 A-wing.]]</div>
Putting Syndualla's plan into action, Commander Sato deployed the rebel fleet to Atollon's orbit, ordering the rebel ships to assume spearhead formation and to deploy their [[starfighter]]s. The rebel ships would protect Ezra's ''Gauntlet'' as they tried to force an opening through the Imperial formation. However, Thrawn anticipated Sato's move and ordered his fleet to reinforce the center and to deploy [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] into the fray. Despite Konstantine's complaints, Thrawn refused to attack the rebels with overwhelming force since he was familiar with their unconventional and reckless tactics.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

[[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wings]] from [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]] entered the battle and attacked the Imperial fleet head-on, managing to damage the ''[[Dark Omen]]'' with [[proton bomb]]s, but were soon overwhelmed by TIE fighters. The Imperial fleet also deployed [[TIE/sa tactical bomber]]s which swarmed the Rebel starships. The combined Imperial firepower from the capital ships and TIEs led to the destruction of several rebel ships, including three CR90 corvettes and numerous starfighters. Sato desperately ordered the rebel ships to charge the blockade, but as the ships were getting closer to the Imperial fleet, they were directly attacked by the Star Destroyers, resulting in the destruction of a second Nebulon-B frigate and the ''Braha'tok''-class gunship [[Orion (starship)|''Orion'']].<ref name="Zero Hour" />

[[File:Phoenix_Home_rams_Imperial_interdictor.jpg|thumb|left|250px|''Phoenix Nest'' and the interdictor collide]]
With the rebel ships in point-blank range of the blockade, Thrawn had the two Arquitens cruisers operating on the side of his formation flank the rebels from both sides, surrounding the fleet and destroying another CR90 corvette. Realizing that the rebels would be unable to break through the Imperial lines, Commander Sato ordered his crew to abandon the [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]] ''[[Phoenix Nest]]'', staying on the ship with two crew who volunteered to stay behind. He then flew towards the center of the Imperial formation. Seeking to gain glory for himself, Admiral Konstantine defied Thrawn's orders and brought his interdictor cruiser out to intercept ''Phoenix Nest''. Sato then rammed his ship into the interdictor, destroying both ships and all hands aboard. Sato's sacrifice created a gap in the gravity field coverage and allowed Bridger and Chopper to flee on the ''Nightbrother'' into hyperspace.<ref name="Zero Hour" /> Future [[Governor]] and Grand Moff [[Ubrik Adelhard]] witnessed and learned from the space battle from the bridge of the ''Chimaera,'' next to Thrawn.<ref name="TheRising">{{StoryCite|story=The Rising|book=Battle of Jakku &mdash; Insurgency Rising 1}}</ref>

===Seeking help===
{{Quote|You have poor timing Bridger.''"<br />"''We're in the middle of a civil war.|Fenn Rau and Ursa Wren upon Ezra's request for help|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
Having escaped the Imperial blockade and jumped into hyperspace, Bridger tried to petition [[Mon Mothma]] for help. However, the rebel leader was unable to send reinforcements since she did not want to expose the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] true strength to Thrawn. In desperation, Bridger and Chopper traveled to the planet [[Krownest]] to seek the help of their rebel comrade [[Sabine Wren]] and her family [[Clan Wren]]; who were locked in a [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|civil war]] against the Imperial-backed [[Clan Saxon]].<ref name="Zero Hour" />

At the [[Wren Stronghold]], [[Countess]] [[Ursa Wren]], Sabine's mother, were unable to provide assistance since they were bogged down against Clan Saxon's forces. After some discussion, Ursa agreed to allow her daughter Sabine, her son [[Tristan Wren|Tristan]], as well as [[Fenn Rau]] to lead several Mandalorian volunteers and ships to accompany Bridger and Chopper back to Atollon. The ''Gauntlet'' and several [[Fang-class Protectorate fighter|Fang fighters]] traveled back to Atollon. Their plan was to destroy the lone remaining interdictor cruiser, the ''[[Constrainer]]'', so that the remaining rebels could escape offworld.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

Meanwhile, Jarrus had much less success in convincing Bendu to aid the rebels. The enigmatic Force wielder was ambivalent to the wars and problems of the mortal realm and was angered that Jarrus had brought war to Atollon. After Jarrus failed to reason with Bendu, he accused the creature of cowardice. This enraged Bendu and he vowed to destroy the Jedi in accordance with the will of [[the Force]]. Bendu disappeared and Jarrus traveled back to Chopper Base on his [[speeder bike]] just as storm clouds began to gather.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

===The ground assault===
{{Quote|I bring death!|Bendu unleashing his power onto the warring factions|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
As soon as the Interdictor was destroyed, Thrawn ordered his ships to press the attack and force the rebels to ground to prevent any more ships from escaping. The Star Destroyers charged the remaining rebel ships, destroying two more CR90 corvettes. Syndulla reluctantly ordered a retreat, with the remaining rebel forces returning to Chopper Base to regroup and rest. The rebels Orrelios and [[Rex]] managed to activate Sabine's prototype [[deflector shield generator]] to defend from a potential bombardment. Detecting the shield, Thrawn ordered his fleet to bombard it to test its strength. While the shield managed to protect the base and rebel ships, the generator was weakened by the assault. Thrawn then decided to deploy ground forces to finish off the surviving rebels.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

<div style="float: right">[[File:RexAndZebVsATATs-SWRS03E22.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Rex and Zeb attacking the AT-ATs]]</div>
At the advice of General Dodonna, the rebels decided to ambush Thrawn's forces along a narrow canyon leading to the Atollon Coral Mesa. Orrelios and Rex planted [[detonator]]s along the road which they used to blow up several [[All Terrain Defense Pod|AT-DP walkers]]. However, Thrawn had sent the AT-DPs as decoys to draw out the rebel defenders. He then deployed his heavy battalions, which included several [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|AT-AT walkers]]. Thrawn also ordered the walkers' gunners to mark the enemy mines in firing position. [[Stormtrooper]]s accompanied the heavy walkers, as well as [[K79-S80 Imperial Troop Transport|Imperial Troop Transports]].<ref name="Zero Hour" />

Jarrus managed to damage one of the walkers by slicing off two of its legs but failed to slow the Imperial advance. He along with Orrelios and Rex fled into the caves which led back to the coral mesa that housed Chopper Base. Rebel starfighters including [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Derek Klivian|Hobbie's]] A-wings tried to attack the walkers but were driven away by TIE fighters. Thrawn and a contingent of [[death trooper]]s then disembarked from a [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]] and led the stormtrooper assault on Chopper Base. Meanwhile, Governor Pryce commanded the Imperial fleet above.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

With the base breached, General Dodonna and Captain Syndulla ordered the remaining rebel ships to evacuate. Two ships managed to escape but a [[GR-75 medium transport]] was shot down by one of Thrawn's AT-AT walkers. Thrawn then surrounded the Spectres, Rex, AP-5, and General Dodonna and demanded their surrender. However, the Bendu arrived in the form of a storm cloud and attacked the Imperials and rebels with lightning. Syndulla and the remaining rebels fled on the ''[[Ghost]]'' and other ships for space. In his wrath, Bendu struck down one of the fleeing A-wings as well as managing to destroy two AT-AT walkers and numerous other grounded rebel starfighters.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

===Escaping the blockade===
{{Quote|Phoenix Squadron, let's get out of here!"''<br />"''Roger, Phoenix Leader. We're right behind you.|Captain Hera Syndulla and Wedge Antilles|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
[[File:EscapingAtollon.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Ghost and the remaining forces escaping from Atollon after their defeat]]
In space, Bridger, Chopper and their Mandalorian allies launched an attack on the flanks of Thrawn's fleet. While Fenn Rau's Fang fighters harried the TIE fighters, Bridger and Sabine flew the ''Gauntlet'' to the ''Constrainer''. While Chopper stayed aboard, Bridger and the Mandalorians landed on the interdictor's hull and proceeded to attack the gravity well projectors. In response, Governor Pryce dispatched [[jumptrooper]]s but these reinforcements failed to stop the Mandalorians from destroying the cruiser.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

The destruction of the ''Constrainer'' coincided with Syndulla and the remaining rebel ships reaching the space around Atollon. The rebel convoy then fought their way a second time through the Imperial blockade. Without the interdictor, there was nothing to stop them from jumping into hyperspace. Syndulla and the crew of the ''Ghost'' managed to rescue the former ISB agent Kallus, who had escaped in an [[escape pod]]. After breaking through the blockade, the rebel ships jumped into hyperspace.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

==Aftermath==
===Immediate aftermath===
{{Quote|What manner of creature are you?''"<br />"''One beyond your power to destroy.''"<br />"''It would not seem so.''"<br />"''You cannot see... but I can.''"<br />"''What do you see?''"<br />"''I see your defeat, like many arms surrounding you in a cold embrace.|Thrawn and Bendu|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
[[File:Thrawn_Bendu_ZH.png|thumb|right|250px|Thrawn and the Bendu]]
The rebels had fled Chopper Base to orbit with two CR90 corvettes (including the ''Liberator''), two Hammerhead corvettes (''P1'' and ''P3''), a GR-75 medium transport, a ''Braha'tok''-class gunship, and the ''Ghost'', but enemy fire from the blockade left only the ''Liberator'', both Hammerheads and the ''Ghost'', joined by two surviving Y-wings, four surviving A-wings, the ''Gauntlet'', and Mandalorian Fang fighters, able to escape into hyperspace.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

On the surface, after the rebels fled, Bendu demanded that Thrawn leave but the Grand Admiral was not intimidated by the Force entity. He directed his troops and AT-AT walkers to shoot down the "eyes" in the middle of the storm clouds. This tactic worked and the Imperials succeeded in shooting down Bendu despite losing several men and one AT-AT in the process. Thrawn and his men later confronted the fallen but still defiant Bendu. The Bendu prophesied that Thrawn would eventually meet defeat but the scientifically-minded Thrawn refused to take him seriously. Thrawn shot Bendu with his [[blaster]] but the creature warned that he was beyond the Grand Admiral's power to destroy, his body vanishing,<ref name="Zero Hour" /> becoming one with the Force,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> but his voice still heard.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

===Rebel response===
{{Quote|But, instead, when we amassed our forces, we were routed by Thrawn's starfleet and taught a valuable lesson: that we are not ready for open war.''"<br />"''Yeah, well, some people say it's already begun.''"<br />"''Indeed, they do. This is a time of difficult choices. Sometimes impossible ones.|Mon Mothma and Ezra Bridger|<ref name="INR" />}}
The Battle of Atollon was a serious defeat for the fledgling Rebel Alliance<ref name="Zero Hour" /> but was not the disaster it could have been.<ref name="Battles that Changed" /> While Phoenix Squadron and Dodonna's Massassi Group were not the only rebel forces in [[the galaxy]], as the rebels assumed Thrawn thought, Mon Mothma's decision not to send reinforcements did help conceal the true strength of the Alliance. However, Phoenix Squadron and Massassi Group sustained heavy casualties on Atollon. Chopper Base was lost and much of its facilities were damaged by the fighting and Bendu's rampage. Syndulla led the surviving rebel forces to [[Yavin 4]]. To hide their tracks, the rebels jumped into hyperspace three times.<ref name="Zero Hour" /> To Mothma and others, the battle was proof that they were not yet ready for open war.<ref name="INR" /> As such, this era of the conflict between the Alliance and Empire was defined more by surgical strikes<ref name="ROVisual">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> instead of large-scale conflicts.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

[[File:AftermathAtollon.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The remaining rebel forces headed to Yavin 4 to regroup with Rebel Command]]
While the rebels were demoralized by the defeat, some, like Jarrus, were determined to continue the fight to free the galaxy from Imperial rule. For their help in the battle,<ref name="Zero Hour" /> the rebels supported Clan Wren in the ongoing Mandalorian Civil War by allowing Jarrus, Bridger, and C1-10P to join their forces, carrying out the [[mission to Mandalore]] that saw the death of [[Governor]] [[Tiber Saxon]] and resulted in [[Lord|Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] uniting various [[Mandalorian clan]]s into the formalized [[Mandalorian resistance]]<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Heroes of Mandalore]]''</ref> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Meanwhile, the survivors of Atollon made a home for themselves on the Alliance headquarters of Yavin 4, where they were joined by Jarrus, Bridger, C1-10P, and, as she chose to rejoin her rebel family, Sabine Wren after the mission to Mandalore. The revelation that the Alliance was not embarking upon large-scale missions, such as the attack on Lothal set to help his people, upset Bridger, who debated the nature of the conflict with the Empire with Mothma; whereas Mothma said the defeat at Atollon was proof the rebels were not yet ready for open war, Bridger pointed out how others in the galaxy already thought an open conflict had started. All the same, he understood Mothma's reasoning.<ref name="INR" />

===Imperial response===
{{Quote|My war with the rebels is not yet over. I ''will'' defeat them.|Thrawn, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Alliances2-Textless-Rahzzah.png|thumb|right|300px|Darth Vader had a negative opinion of Grand Admiral Thrawn's alleged failure at Atollon.]]
The Battle of Atollon was a victory for the Galactic Empire<ref name="Battles that Changed" /> in a tactical sense; Thrawn had inflicted heavy casualties on the rebel fleet through his cunning strategies. However, Thrawn failed to fulfill Grand Moff Tarkin's orders to capture senior rebel officers, and a number of rebels had, in the end, managed to escape.<ref name="Zero Hour" /> The battle was seen as a failure in the eyes of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] due to the rebel's escape. Palpatine understood that the rebel's escape was beyond Thrawn's control and not his fault, but he still thought of it as a humiliating defeat for the Grand Admiral. Vader, meanwhile, had no patience for failure no matter the reasons. Vader was particularly angered by Thrawn's failure to capture or kill Jarrus. While Thrawn explained a mysterious being&mdash;the Bendu&mdash;had attacked his forces, with his [[death trooper]] guard reporting much the same, Vader displayed a lack of trust in the Grand Admiral's claim.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

Thrawn promised the Emperor that he would deal with the Phoenix cell, and Jarrus in particular,<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> and continued his service to the Empire. Following Thrawn's success in a [[Engagement in Sector 56|battle in Sector 56]],<ref name="TA1" /> Vader went on a [[Mission to Batuu (Galactic Empire)|mission to Batuu]] [[Battle of Mokivj|and Mokivj]] with the Grand Admiral. In Thrawn's absence, the Seventh Fleet's hunt for the Atollon rebels, who Thrawn knew would be racing to join other cells, was taken over by Commander [[Woldar]] and Tarkin. During their mission, Vader reminded Thrawn about his failure constantly.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> Afterward, Thrawn continued his Lothal operations<ref name="Family Reunion – & Farewell">{{Rebels|Family Reunion – and Farewell}}</ref> and also served as an advisor to Tiber Saxon during the Mandalorian Civil War.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />

===In history===
Bendu's prophecy about Thrawn's defeat<ref name="Zero Hour" /> ultimately came true during the [[liberation of Lothal]].<ref name="Family Reunion – & Farewell" /> The Rebel Alliance would continue to pose a serious threat to the Empire, leading up to the [[Battle of Yavin]]. The growing threat of the Alliance led the Empire to deploy its new [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] superweapon in an attempt to crush the rebellion.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Ultimately, Jarrus' dream was realized with the deaths of both the Emperor and Darth Vader during the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> The Alliance's successor, the [[New Republic]], later inflicted a lasting defeat on the Empire during the [[Battle of Jakku]] in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>
