{{Quote|Dates in this book are given using the 'ABY-BBY' dating system, which is centered around the Battle of Yavin, when the first Death Star was destroyed.|Excerpt from ''[[Galactic Atlas]]''|<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>}}
The ''''ABY-BBY' dating system''' was a chronological framework used to differentiate [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]] (designated as '''BBY''') from those following it ('''ABY'''). As one of multiple dating conventions employed throughout [[the galaxy]], it provided a structured means of historical reference within various records and scholarly works.

==Description==
[[File:Exds.png|thumb|250px|The destruction of the Death Star at Yavin became the year zero in the 'ABY-BBY' system.]]
The 'ABY-BBY' dating system<ref name="GA" /> established its "[[Standard year|year]] zero" based on the destruction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first]] [[Death Star]] during the [[Battle of Yavin]], an event recognized by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] as its first major victory against the [[Galactic Empire]]. The system utilized the abbreviations "BBY" (Before the Battle of [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]]) and "ABY" (After the Battle of Yavin) to distinguish historical periods.<ref name="Falcon61">{{BuildFalconCite|61|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> Year numbers could be positioned either before (e.g., "[[6 ABY]]")<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> or after (e.g., "[[5 BBY|BBY 5]]") the respective abbreviation.<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> The designation "0 BBY,"<ref name="GA" /> or simply "0," encompassed events surrounding the rebel triumph at Yavin.<ref name="GANew">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> The year immediately preceding "0 BBY" was denoted as "[[1 BBY]]," while the subsequent year was marked as "[[0 ABY]]."<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Therefore, I have come to favor a dating system that highlights the years before the Battle of Yavin (BBY) and those that came after (ABY). I have no doubts that this will produce the sort of stringent debate from both my colleagues and other readers which often accompanies such decisions.|Beaumont Kin, in ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
The 'ABY-BBY' dating system was utilized during the [[Galactic Civil War]], primarily within the ranks of the Alliance to Restore the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]].<ref name="Falcon61" /> At the time, multiple alternative dating conventions were also in use, including the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar]],<ref name="This week">{{ThisWeek|fjHmSJy3BFM|Star Wars: The High Republic Box Set Reveal, Andor Props, and More!|sw_url=video/star-wars-the-high-republic-box-set-reveal-andor-props-and-more}}</ref> the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> the [[Naboo calendar]],<ref name="SWP">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> and a system based on the [[Domancion Accord]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 6">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 6|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 6]]</ref>

[[File:TROS-SayTheLine.png|thumb|left|250px|Historian Beaumont Kin (pictured) used the ABY-BBY system in ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire'' but knew that could kick off an academic debate about how to date history.]]
In later years, [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] adopted the system in his work ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', arguing that the destruction of the Death Star at Yavin represented a crucial turning point in Imperial history, effectively dividing events into pre- and post-Yavin periods. The selection of this reference point was recognized as a topic of scholarly debate, as alternative epochs—such as the [[Proclamation of the New Order|birth]] of the Empire or the [[Battle of Endor]]—were also considered by historians.<ref name="Rise" />

Additionally, the system was incorporated into ''[[Galactic Atlas]]'', a compilation of maps<ref name="GA" /> created<ref name="TOM">{{GermanFanCite|96|Database}}</ref> by the [[Ithorian]] [[artist]] [[Gammit Chond]]. The chronology presented in this work spanned from the [[Invasion of Naboo]] in [[32 BBY]] to the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]] in [[34 ABY]], with a particular focus on the historical influence and actions of the [[Skywalker family]].<ref name="GA" /> Further maps and notes from Chond were recovered in an updated edition of the ''Galactic Atlas'' centering on events from the Invasion of Naboo to the aftermath of the [[Battle of Exegol]] in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="GANew" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 'ABY-BBY' dating system was first used in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[2014]], in ''[[Star Wars Workbook: 2nd Grade Reading]]''.<ref name="2nd Reading">''[[Star Wars Workbook: 2nd Grade Reading]]''</ref> In [[2016]], the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' by [[Emil Fortune]] made extensive use of this system.<ref name="GA" /> It was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1996]] [[West End Games]] [[roleplaying game]] guide ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded]]'' written by [[Bill Smith]].<ref name="SW:TRG">''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded]]''</ref>

===0 BBY===
Following the release of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' in 2016, it was noted by sources like ''Star Wars: Galactic Atlas'' dates the events of ''Rogue One'' and [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], prior to the Battle of Yavin, to 0 BBY.<ref name="GA" /> However, in [[2022]], according to the Amazon listing for the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''  states that these events took place in 1 BBY.<ref name="Amazon Timelines">{{Amazon|url=dp/0744060877|text=Star Wars Timelines|oldversion=20221111122746|nolive=1}}</ref> [[Emily Shkoukani]], a member of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] stated on Twitter that 0 BBY no longer exists, and that events prior to the Battle of Yavin are now assigned 1 BBY, and The Battle of Yavin itself is "0," and any events in the year after that battle are 0 ABY.<ref name="Shkoukani 0 BBY">{{Twitter|username=shkouks|url=status/1534280911130447872|name=shkouks　(they/she)|date=2022-07-07|quote=Correct! No more 0 BBY, all events that take place in the year leading up to the Battle of Yavin are now assigned 1 BBY. 0 is the Battle of Yavin, and any events in the year after are 0 ABY. :)|archivedate=20220607210819|nolive=1}}</ref> Though the "[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]]" episode guide once again used 0 BBY for ''Rogue One''.<ref name="Narkina 5 EG">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e8-trivia-gallery|text=Andor &#124; Season 1, Episode 8 - Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref>

Eventually, with the release of ''Star Wars: Timelines'' in [[2023]], the events of ''Rogue One'' still take place in 1 BBY; however, the events of ''A New Hope'' take place in "0."<ref name="Timelines" /> The "0 BBY" name appeared in the reference book ''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]'', which was released in the same month as ''Timelines'', as a date for ''A New Hope'' and the last year of "the height of the Empire's reign." However, the book, on another page, uses the date 1 BBY, 0 ABY, 1 ABY for ''A New Hope''.<ref name="100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> In [[2025]], addressing fan questions on Bluesky, [[Pablo Hidalgo]], a member of the [[Lucasfilm]] Story Group, explained that there is no 0 BBY, and the Battle of Yavin is the zero mark.<ref name="Pablo Bluesky">{{Bluesky|username=infinata.bsky.social|url=post/3lr3zdqpdls2r|name=[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2025-06-08|quote=The TIMELINES book went with the year leading up to the Battle of Yavin is 1BBY which counts down to the event at the end of its year. There is no 0 BBY. But then the battle is the zero mark, the start of 0 ABY and 1 ABY doesn't start until a year has passed.|archivedate=20250626153023}}</ref> Later, the 2025 reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events that preceding the Battle of Yavin depicted in ''A New Hope'', including [[destruction of Alderaan]], to 1 BBY and Battle of Yavin is dated to "0."<ref name="GA 2025">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> Since recent reference books do not use "0 BBY," this article assumes that "0 BBY" has been abolished and that the events up to the [[rescue of Princess Leia]] occurred in 1 BBY.
