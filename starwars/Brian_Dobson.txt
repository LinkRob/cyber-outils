'''Brian Dobson''' is an [[Wikipedia:English people|English]]-[[Wikipedia:Canada|Canadian]] voice actor who provided the voice of [[Jek-14/Legends|Jek-14]] in ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Yoda Chronicles]]'' and ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'', as well as [[Jek 15]] in the former. He is the younger brother of Michael and [[Wikipedia:Paul Dobson (actor)|Paul Dobson]].
