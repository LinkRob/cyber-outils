'''Canapés''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] that was served at a [[Mission to Ktath'atn|party]] at the [[Citadel of Ktath'atn]]. During the affair, [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] enjoyed one such canapé and remarked to her companion [[Luke Skywalker]] that they were "amazing" and later that they were "delicious."<ref name="The Screaming Citadel 1" />
