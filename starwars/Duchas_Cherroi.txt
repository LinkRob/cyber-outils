'''Duchas Cherroi'''<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> was a [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in [[Squadron Three (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Three]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> Around [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">{{SW|url=news/shadow-fall-author-interview|text=In ''Shadow Fall'', the Dark Secrets of ''Alphabet Squadron'' Revealed}} establishes that ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' takes place approximately half a [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], while ''[[Before the Awakening]]'' states [[Kes Dameron]] and [[Shara Bey]] moved to [[Yavin 4]] around six [[Standard month|months]] after the Battle of [[Endor]]. Given that ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates their move to [[5 ABY]], the events of ''Shadow Fall'' must also take place around that [[time]].</ref> while the [[fighter wing]] was stationed aboard the [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser carrier]] ''[[Aerie]]'', Cherroi had a competition with the 204th's [[Squadron Five (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Five]], but fell behind. The Imperial [[pilot]]'s efforts were discussed by another pilot in the ''Aerie''{{'s}} mess hall, which was briefly overheard by the fighter wing's [[commanding officer]] [[Major]] [[Soran Keize]] as he headed for a meeting. Cherroi shared an intimate relationship with fellow Squadron Three pilot [[Gargovik]], who were often seen together between missions.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
Duchas Cherroi was first mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> The pilot's first name, Duchas, was identified in ''Shadow Fall''{{'s}} sequel, Freed's [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
