A breakfast favorite on Nar Shaddaa, it features a poached gorg egg, Klatooine paddy frog sausage and a slice of jerba cheese on a pedunkee mufkin.|Part of the sandwich's description on Dex's menu|<ref name="DaD" />}}
'''"Hungry Hutt" breakfast sandwiches''' were a type of breakfast [[Sandwich/Legends|sandwich]] made by the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] [[Species/Legends|species]] that was popular on the Hutt-controlled [[Moon/Legends|moon]] of [[Nar Shaddaa/Legends|Nar Shaddaa]]. The sandwich contained a poached [[Gorg/Legends|gorg]] [[Egg/Legends|egg]], [[Klatooine paddy frog/Legends|Klatooine paddy frog]] [[Klatooine paddy frog sausage|sausage]], and a slice of [[jerba cheese]], all served on a [[pedunkee mufkin]].<ref name="DaD" /> At some point prior to its destruction during the first two [[Standard year/Legends|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] reign,<ref name="DexEra">''[[The Last of the Jedi: Underworld]]'' established that [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] was destroyed by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. ''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' later stated that the novel took place around the [[Standard year/Legends|year]] [[18 BBY/Legends|18 BBY]]. ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' states that the Empire was formed in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]], so Dex's Diner was destroyed either during 19 BBY or 18 BBY.</ref> the [[Restaurant/Legends|eatery]] known as [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] served the sandwiches with a breakfast side dish for 6.5 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]] each. The [[Besalisk/Legends|Besalisk]] [[Chef/Legends|chef]] and owner of the diner, [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster]], considered the [[Foodstuff/Legends|food]] to be delicious and described it as a "punky muffin" sandwich.<ref name="DaD" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Hungry Hutt" breakfast sandwiches were featured in the [[2009]] [[StarWars.com]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] exclusive article "[[Dining at Dex's]]," which was written by [[Gregory Walker]] in the form of an [[in-universe]] menu.<ref name="DaD" />
