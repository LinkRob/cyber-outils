'''Devaronian Soufflé''' was a tangy [[soufflé]] that was considered a classic [[Foodstuff|dish]] but was also a potential explosive during its preparation. [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] witnessed [[Devaronian]] cook [[Sama Macoy]] cause such a mishap at [[Takodana Castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]] and, [[Standard century|centuries]] later in [[34 ABY]], he included the story with his recipe for the dish in his [[Book|cookbook]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''.

==Description==
{{Quote|That's a Devaronian specialty&mdash;a kind of tangy soufflé. I seen it before. But you need to know your whisk times. You slow down too much&hellip;|Strono Tuggs|<ref name="A Recipe for Death" />}}
Devaronian Soufflé was known as a classic baked [[Foodstuff|dish]] with a tangy flavor, but its explosive quality distinguished it from other [[soufflé]]s. While [[chef]]s usually worried that their soufflés would fall,<ref name="Cookbook" /> the [[Devaronian]] specialty required stirring the batter at a specific speed or else it would detonate. Although explosive during preparation, the finished soufflé was [[Diet|edible]] by Devaronians<ref name="A Recipe for Death" /> and [[Artiodac]]s.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==History==
{{Quote|It wasn't my fault! The batter&hellip;it was a bad batch&mdash;''"<br />"''It is a poor chef who blames the ingredients.|Sama Macoy makes excuses for her failed soufflé to competition judge Carly|<ref name="A Recipe for Death" />}}
[[File:Strono_Tuggs_VD.png|thumb|right|250px|alt=A disfigured Artiodac with a brown apron and green shirt stands with a cleaver in his left hand.|Strono Tuggs, cookbook author and witness to the explosion of a Devaronian Soufflé]]
The Artiodac [[Strono Tuggs]] was head chef for [[Maz Kanata]]<ref name="A Recipe for Death" /> at the [[Cantina|tavern]] she operated out of her [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]],<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> in the [[Standard century|centuries]] before its [[Battle of Takodana|destruction]] in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Competition date">"[[A Recipe for Death]]" establishes that [[Strono Tuggs]] has served in [[Takodana Castle]] for more than twenty [[Standard year|years]], and ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]'' establishes that Tuggs served in the castle for [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Battle of Takodana]], an event that ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. As such, "A Recipe for Death" must take place at least 180 years before the battle.</ref> Around [[146 BBY]],<ref name="RecipeDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[A Recipe for Death]]" to around [[146 BBY]].</ref> after Tuggs had worked there for more than twenty [[Standard year|years]], he held a cooking competition to determine who would replace his [[Death|deceased]] [[sous-chef]], [[Robbs Ely]]. Unbeknownst to the contestants, he was also attempting to determine who had killed Ely and stolen his [[Robbs Ely's recipe book|recipe book]]. In one round, as the competitors faced the added challenge of dodging [[stun]] blasts, the Devaronian prep cook [[Sama Macoy]] began preparing her soufflé while working through being stunned, but her failure to maintain constant motion in the volatile batter caused it to explode in her face.<ref name="A Recipe for Death" />

Although all present were uninjured, the batter explosion forced Macoy to start over with another dish. Judges [[Carly]] and [[J'Nell]], a pair of [[BD-3000 luxury droid]]s, were unimpressed when the Devaronian blamed the soufflé's ingredients rather than her own abilities for the explosion. She was knocked out of both the competition and the list of suspects in Ely's murder, which had in fact been committed by the [[droid]]s.<ref name="A Recipe for Death" />

Many [[Standard decade|decades]] later,<ref name="Competition date" /> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Cookbook date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs included the anecdote about Macoy and a recipe for the soufflé in his [[Book|cookbook]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'' that he published after Takodana Castle was destroyed. He expressed his hope that people following his recipe would experience better luck than Macoy, but if they needed a last-[[Standard minute|minute]] replacement after an unexpected detonation, he recommended [[Flash-fried zuchii|fried zuchii]] as an easy alternative.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Devaronian Soufflé first appeared in the short story "[[A Recipe for Death]]," written by [[Landry Q. Walker]] and included in the [[2016]] anthology book ''[[Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I]]''.<ref name="A Recipe for Death" /> The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe was created for the [[2019]] cookbook ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and classifies the [[Wikipedia:Soufflé|soufflé]] as appropriate for [[Wikipedia:Vegetarianism|vegetarian]] and [[Wikipedia:Gluten-free diet|gluten-free]] dietary considerations. The ingredients are [[Wikipedia:Butter|butter]] for greasing, [[Wikipedia:Sucrose|sugar]] for the batter and for dusting, four separated large [[Wikipedia:Egg|eggs]], a pinch of [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], softened [[Wikipedia:Cream cheese|cream cheese]], crumbled [[Wikipedia:Goat cheese|goat cheese]], [[Wikipedia:Lemon|lemon]] [[Wikipedia:Juice|juice]] and [[Wikipedia:Zest (ingredient)|zest]], and [[Wikipedia:Paprika|paprika]].<ref name="Cookbook" />
