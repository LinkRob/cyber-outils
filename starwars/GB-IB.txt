The '''GB-IB''' was a hovering [[Droid/Legends|droid]] used by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] on [[Kessel/Legends|Kessel]] and [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]] in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]].<ref name="FamiMaga">{{BookCite|author=|container=Star Wars|title=Family Computer Magazine|number=1987 #21|publisher=Tokuma Shoten|date=December 4, 1987|issn=|link=https://archive.org/details/famimaga-dec-1987}}</ref> They were shaped roughly like a half-sphere with manipulator arms underneath.<ref name="sw87" />

==Behind the scenes==
The GB-IB appeared in the 1987 [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] [[Star Wars (1987 video game)|''Star Wars'' game]].<ref name="sw87" /> Their name was revealed in the [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese language]] guide of the game serialized in Family Computer Magazine.<ref name="FamiMaga" />
