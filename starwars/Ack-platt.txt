'''Ack-platts''' were creatures who lived in familial groups. [[Unidentified sentient creature|A creature]] once wrote in its diary the wish to trap and eat a family of Ack-platts, regurgitate and play with a baby Ack-platt and then eat him again.<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ack-platts were mentioned in ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a book published in [[1993]].<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]].<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
