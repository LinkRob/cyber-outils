'''Ansel Hsiao''' is an artist who specializes in 3D modeling.
[[File:ImpStarDestroyer-SWI125.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Ansel's artwork as featured in ''Insider'' 125.]]

His [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] model was featured in [[Star Wars Insider 125|''Star Wars Insider'' 125]] and he was one of the artists that provided illustrations for ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''. Hsiao also supplied some information regarding the [[Assertor-class Star Dreadnought/Legends|''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought]] and the [[Bellator-class dreadnought/Legends|''Bellator''-class dreadnought]] in [[Star Wars Insider 134|''Star Wars: Insider'' 134]]. In addition to his [[fanon]] artwork and the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] model, he created 3D representations of [[Star Wars Legends|then-canon]] vessels for ''Warfare'', including the [[Praetor Mark II-class battlecruiser|''Praetor II''-class Star Battlecruiser]], the [[Interdictor-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Interdictor''-class Star Destroyer]], the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought/Legends|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] (which was also his first ''Star Wars''-related 3D artwork), the [[Harrower-class dreadnought|''Harrower''-class dreadnought]], the [[Nebula-class Star Destroyer|''Nebula''-class Star Destroyer]] and the [[Endurance-class fleet carrier|''Endurance''-class fleet carrier]].

Hsiao noted that he had based the appearance of certain background [[Starship/Legends|starships]] seen in ''The Essential Guide to Warfare''{{'s}} illustration of [[Naval Station Validusia/Legends|Naval Station Validusia]] on the depiction of<ref name="Email">{{EmailCite|email=File:FractalSponge_correspondence_part_1.png|creator=Ansel Hsiao|date=2012-06-14}}</ref><ref name="Email2">{{EmailCite|email=File:FractalSponge_correspondence_part_2.png|creator=Ansel Hsiao|date=2012-07-18}}</ref> [[Freighter/Legends|freighters]] in the [[Murk system/Legends|Murk system]] that was included in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>

Besides ''Star Wars''-related modelling projects, he also was tasked with creating the final design for the [[W:c:halo:UNSC Infinity|UNSC ''Infinity'']] for [[W:c:halo:Halo 4|''Halo 4'']].

His website, [http://fractalsponge.net fractalsponge.net], contains a plethora of the models that he has created.

In [[2019]], Ansel Hsiao complained that some of the artworks published on his website were reused without his consent by the illustrator [[Joe Corroney]] in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide]]''.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://http://fractalsponge.net/?p=4224|text=Hey, Disney and Joe Corroney|author=Hsiao, Ansel|date=2019-02-16|archivedate=20190224032232}}</ref><ref>{{ArtStation|username=fractalsponge|url=fractalsponge/blog/wAWe/turns-out-desaturate-in-photoshop-is-great|name=Ansel Hsiao|text=Turns out, desaturate in Photoshop is great.|archivedate=20201111204527}}</ref> In [[2020]], Hsiao noticed similar appropriation of his work in the sourcebook ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''.<ref>{{ArtStation|username=fractalsponge|url=fractalsponge/blog/OKYE/next-up-in-filters|name=Ansel Hsiao|text=Next up, in filters.|archivedate=20210524155302}}</ref>
