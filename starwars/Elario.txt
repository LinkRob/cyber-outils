'''Elario''' was an individual<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> who by [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> had stashed a number items in a container  on the edge of a ledge overlooking a [[water|waterfall]] near the [[City|town]] of [[Jaunta's Hope]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[thief]] named [[Kay Vess]] discovered the stash and stole Elario's stashed possessions.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Elario was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game the contents of Elario's stash are randomized;<ref name="Outlaws" /> as such this article does not assume any specific items to be [[canon]]ical.
