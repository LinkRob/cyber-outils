A [[Gender|male]] '''individual''' was the grandfather of the [[human]] girl [[Fern]] from the isolated [[planet]] [[At Attin]].<ref name="SC 101" />

Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> Fern and<ref name="SC 101" /> her<ref name="Fern">{{Databank|fern|Fern}}</ref> friend [[KB]] argued with the boys [[Wim]] and [[Neel]] about who a [[starship]] buried in the [[Forest|woods]] should belong to. With Wim believing the [[vessel]] to be a [[Jedi temple]], Fern claimed to have heard from her grandfather that the construction was supposed to be a new neighborhood development. Her grandfather had allegedly recounted to her a story involving construction workers. Following a system failure, the workers would have been intoxicated with poison [[gas]]. Although they had survived, she claimed the substance had made them go crazy, leaving no choice but to bury them alive in what in fact was a buried ship.<ref name="SC 101" />
