'''Frobb''' was a [[human]] [[Second Lieutenant]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] who was stationed at the [[Citadel Tower]] on [[Scarif]] in [[1 BBY]]. Responsible for overseeing [[starship]]s launching and landing on [[Landing Platform Nine|pad nine]], Frobb was present for the arrival of the [[Mission to Eadu|stolen]] Imperial [[Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle|cargo shuttle]] [[SW-0608]], which was carrying a [[Rogue One|team]] of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel trooper|soldiers]] aiming to steal the [[Death Star plans]].

Frobb summoned an inspection team to board the [[shuttle]], but the rebels incapacitated the Imperials and stole their [[Imperial military uniforms|uniforms]], allowing them to enter the tower unnoticed. Other rebels then sneaked off the shuttle and detonated a series of explosives, initiating the [[Battle of Scarif]]. The [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] obliterated the tower shortly thereafter once the rebels successfully transmitted the plans to their [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]].

==Biography==
===Stationed at Scarif===
The [[human]] Frobb served the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[Second Lieutenant]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Frobb was stationed at the Empire's [[Citadel Tower]] on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]] as the [[deck officer]] of [[Landing Platform Nine]]. He was responsible for cataloging [[shuttle]] landings and takeoffs, ensuring that [[Starship|vessels]] were stocked with enough [[fuel]], [[life support]], and [[Foodstuff|victuals]], and ordering ship inspections should something seem unusual.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

Frobb,<ref name="Rogue One" /> [[Lieutenant]] [[Tobix Chasser]],<ref name="Visual Guide" /> and a [[stormtrooper]] were present at one of pad nine's control panels when the Imperial [[Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle|cargo shuttle]] [[SW-0608]] was directed to land there. SW-0608 had originally been stationed on the planet [[Eadu]] but, unbeknownst to Frobb and the Empire, had been [[Mission to Eadu|stolen]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and was now carrying a [[Rogue One|group]] of [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldiers]] who planned to [[Thief|steal]] the [[Death Star plans|plans]] to the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] from the Citadel Tower's [[Scarif vault|data vault]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

===Rebel attack===
[[File:Frobb_on_landing_pad.png|thumb|left|250px|Frobb (left, at console) was assigned to Landing Platform Nine at the Citadel Tower on Scarif.]]
Frobb ordered an inspection team to board the shuttle and messaged the crew of SW-0608 to warn them that the team was approaching. The rebels then incapacitated the boarding team and stole their [[Imperial military uniforms|uniforms]], allowing rebel [[agent]]s [[Jyn Erso]] and [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] to disguise themselves and disembark the shuttle with their [[KX-series security droid|droid]], [[K-2SO]]. Chasser opened the [[blast door]] to the landing pad for the disguised rebels, who then mounted a<ref name="Rogue One" /> [[railspeeder]]<ref name="ROjn">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> up to the Citadel Tower.<ref name="Rogue One" />

A large number of rebel [[soldier]]s then sneaked off the shuttle without Frobb or Chasser noticing and moved to other landing pads to set explosives. They detonated these charges as a distraction to draw the [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] away from Erso and Andor, and [[Battle of Scarif|fighting]] soon erupted between rebels and Imperials outside the tower, including on pad nine. Eventually, the rebels successfully transmitted the Death Star plans to the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] above Scarif, shortly after which the Death Star itself fired upon the Citadel Tower, destroying it and annihilating the surrounding area.<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Personality and traits==
Frobb had [[Color|light]] skin; red [[hair]], including a beard; and green [[eye]]s.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

==Equipment==
Frobb wore a black Imperial military [[Clothing|uniform]], including a [[Imperial kepi|hat]] and a [[utility belt]], with both bearing [[officer's disk]]s, during his service at the Citadel Tower on Scarif.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:FrobbDeletedScene.png|thumb|right|300px|Frobb conversed with K-2SO in a scene that was cut from the final film.]]
Frobb was portrayed by actor [[Geoff Bell]] in the [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Anthology'']] film, ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which was released on [[December 16]], [[2016]].<ref name="YbY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> Prior to the release of the film, Frobb was first depicted in a promotional featurette for ''Rogue One'' released on [[November 17]], 2016.<ref name="Featurette">{{SWYouTube|u5sdztM-p98|Rogue One: A Star Wars Story Featurette|sw_url=video/rogue-one-a-star-wars-story-intro-featurette}}</ref> His name and [[Military rank|rank]] were revealed in the film's credits despite never being spoken in the film.<ref name="Rogue One" /> Production footage from the film shows a scene being filmed in which Frobb confronts K-2SO on the landing platform,<ref name="Deleted">{{HomeVideoCite|film=Rogue One|featurette=Jyn: The Rebel}}</ref> although this did not make it into the final release.<ref name="Rogue One" />
