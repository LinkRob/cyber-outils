In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the '''destruction of the Lightning Crash''', also known as the '''attack on the Lightning Crash''', was carried out by the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] and [[Jedi Order]]. The [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] and [[Jedi]] wanted to destroy the [[Lightning Crash]], a [[Nihil]] [[space station]] which housed a [[crystal array]] that powered the [[Stormwall]] barrier in order to take out the Stormwall's power. Once the attack was a success and the station destroyed, the Republic Defense Coalition and Jedi began operations to round up any surviving Nihil as [[prisoner]]s. After [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] learned that the Lightning Crash had been destroyed, he ordered for his [[flagship]], the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' to become the Stormwall's new power source.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
