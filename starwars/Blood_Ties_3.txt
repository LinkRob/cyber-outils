'''''Blood Ties'' 3''' is the third issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Blood Ties]]'' miniseries of comics from [[Dark Horse Comics]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Boba Fett has discovered that his father left a legacy to someone Boba has never heard of. He manages to track down the man, but if he is to learn the reasons behind Jango's posthumous generosity, Boba Fett must first save the man from an army of bounty hunters who want the guy's head!''

==Plot summary==
{{Plot|comic}}

==Continuity==
Boba Fett's story within Blood Ties - that portrays Boba as an adult and already a notorious bounty hunter, well known to other legendary bounty hunters like [[Bossk/Legends|Bossk]] (who makes a brief cameo) - and the timeline of Legends "cannon" in relation to the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]] is not provided. As this is Boba's second story within Legends comics as an adult, the timeline is approximated as being [[2 BBY/Legends|2 BBY]] and for two reasons: first, due to the events within the sequel series, ''[[Star Wars: Blood Ties: Boba Fett is Dead|Blood Ties: Boba Fett is Dead]]'' stipulating within its credits as occurring in [[1 BBY/Legends|1 BBY]]; and second, because of the events in ''[[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|A New Hope]]'' (where Boba has a minor cameo), ''[[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|The Empire Strikes Back]]'' (where Boba plays a major role), and ''[[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|Return of the Jedi]]'' (where Boba is incorrectly assumed to have died within the [[Sarlacc]] on [[Tatooine]]). This spans a timeline of [[1 BBY/Legends|1 BBY]] to [[4 ABY/Legends|4 ABY]], and thus has to be earlier.

However, in ''[[Star Wars: Jango Fett: Open Seasons|Jango Fett: Open Seasons]]'' no. 3 the in-text narrative states [[Dooku/Legends|Count Dooku/Lord Tyranus]] recruited Jango to be the prime clone of the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]] 10-years before the [[First Battle of Geonosis/Legends|Battle of Geonosis]] occurs. This puts Open Seasons contemporary storyline occurring in [[32 BBY/Legends|32 BBY]]. The credits in Open Seasons state these events take place 36-years before the Battle of Geonosis, which is a confusing timeline that [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]] provided, considering timelines are based on before or after the Battle of Yavin, and must be due to the fact Jango dies at Geonosis. Nonetheless, ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' stipulates the flashback events of Open Seasons cover 58-40 BBY in relation to Jango's origins, his fostering by [[Jaster Mereel]], and meeting Dooku when he was still a member of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]]. Relating this all to Boba's story throughout the Legends "cannon" timeline - comics, books, novels, etc. - no birthdate prior to the Battle of Yavin has ever been provided as to when Boba was created as a clone of Jango.

Through researching relevant materials, Boba's age at Geononsis - when Jango is killed by [[Mace Windu/Legends|Mace Windu]] - is at the oldest approximately 9-years old. That is assuming after Jango accepted the commission from Dooku, he immediately travelled to [[Kamino/Legends|Kamino]] to begin the process of creating [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Clone Troopers]]. This obviously also includes immediately having an unaltered [[Cloning|Clone]] of himself created by the [[Kaminoan/Legends|Kaminoan]] scientists. From these events, it would make Boba's approximate age within the timeline of the first Blood Ties series to be around 28 to 30-years old in 2 BBY. 

Unfortunately, the ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' (p. 34-35) printed in 2005, and the ''[[Star Wars: The Comics Companion]]'' (p. 25-27) printed in 2006, do not mention either Blood Ties series due to being printed 5 and 6 years, respectively, afterwards; however, timelines for Jango's events within Open Seasons are provided (on the same pages listed above). Furthermore, although the ''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' was published in 2012, it only includes publications predominantly made by [[StarWars.com]], [[Bantam Spectra]], and [[Del Rey]] on behalf of [[LucasBooks]] - not any of the comics published by Dark Horse in relation to the Legends storyline.
