It was consumable by [[human]]s.<ref name="Kat Saka's Kettle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Crispy Kajaka Wrap was a snack available for purchase in the [[In-universe|real world]] at Kat Saka's Kettle in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]] exclusively for [[Star Wars Nite]]s in [[2022]].<ref name="2022 brochure">[[Star Wars Nite]] [[2022]] brochure, as seen in {{YouTube|video=k1UrJziLWro|text=Disney's FIRST Star Wars Nite! &#124; Disneyland After Dark 2022|channel=@PatrickDougall|name=Patrick Dougall|date=2022-05-04}}</ref> The menu described the dish as "[[Cow|Beef]], [[Color|Yellow]] [[Wikipedia:Chili|Chile]], [[Wikipedia:Mashed potato|Mashed]] [[Potato]]es, [[Wikipedia:Pea|Peas]], [[Wikipedia:Mozzarella|Mozzarella]], [[Wikipedia:Ají (sauce)|Ají]] Green [[Sauce]]."<ref name="DLP Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/kat-sakas-kettle/menus|text=Disneyland: Kat Saka's Kettle Snack Menu}}</ref><ref name="Food at Disneyland">{{WebCite|url=https://foodatdisneyland.com/star-wars-nite-unofficial-foodie-guide|text=Star Wars Nite &ndash; Unofficial Foodie Guide|work=[https://foodatdisneyland.com Food at Disneyland]|archivedate=20220429025655}} Includes menu screenshot.</ref>
