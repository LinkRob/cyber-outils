'''Angilar Bosh''' served as the [[art director]] of a leading [[Unidentified advertising agency|advertising agency]] on [[Contruum]] before taking on lucrative freelance work for the [[Bureau of Ships and Services]]. Following such freelance success, Bosh moved to the [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[capital]] [[Coruscant]] to pursue [[Politics|government]] contracts from the [[Galactic Republic]]. This government work included producing the [[Painting|piece]] titled "[[Strength and Unity]]" based on a concept by [[Vice Chair|Vice Chancellor]] [[Mas Amedda]] for a campaign meant to combat the [[human]]-dominated imagery of most [[Core Worlds]] government messaging and stir up patriotic unity amongst viewers. Due to the inclusion of Amedda's own likeness in the image, the piece was mostly remembered as a work of self-indulgence and ego by the Vice Chancellor, rather than a piece of unifying [[propaganda]].<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
Angilar Bosh was created by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] for the reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', which was released in [[2016]].<ref name="Propaganda" />
