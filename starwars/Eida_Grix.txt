'''Eida Grix''' was a passenger aboard the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> While they were aboard, an announcement was made over the [[Starship|ship's]] [[speaker]] system in [[Huttese]] to inform them that their special order of [[colo claw fish]] had arrived.<ref name="Outlaws" />
