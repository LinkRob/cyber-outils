'''Axxila''' was a sparsely-populated<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="To the Last" /> located in the [[D'Astan sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> An exporter of [[ore]]s, the once prosperous world eventually fell into obscurity after outside forces like the [[Galactic Empire]] engaged in [[Mining|strip mining]] operations.<ref name="To the Last" /> Axxila was the [[homeworld]] of Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Firmus Piett]].<ref name="USW" />

{{Planet-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Axxila was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''.<ref name="USW" /> Before that, it first appeared in the [[1998]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] eight-part story arc ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Rogue Squadron: Masquerade]]'' from the comic book series ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Rogue Squadron]]'' published by [[Dark Horse Comics]].<ref name="Masquerade">''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Rogue Squadron: Masquerade]]''</ref>
