'''''Animal Icons: Star Wars Creatures''''' was a documentary that aired on the ''[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Planet Animal Planet]'' TV channel in [[2005]] in honor of the completion of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga. It was narrated by [[Anthony Daniels]].

{{Media-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''As each new installment of the Star Wars saga unfolds, the creatures seem to become more and more fantastical, yet strangely familiar. Star Wars creator George Lucas and his concept artists often look to nature for their alien inspiration.''
