The '''First Order Light Cruiser''' was a model of [[light cruiser]] used by the [[First Order]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 19" /> It could be docked on the nose of a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]], and was also used as a crew transfer [[shuttle]].<ref name="Cross" /> The ''[[Enshado]]'' was one such [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 19" /> Following the discovery of [[Lor San Tekka]], the First Order dispatched another [[Unidentified First Order Light Cruiser|cruiser]] to [[Rescue of Lor San Tekka|recover him]], but it was destroyed by [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 25" />

==Characteristics==
The cruiser could carry two dozen [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s as well as a [[Xi-class light shuttle|''Xi''-class light shuttle]] in an onboard hangar.<ref name="Poe Dameron 19" />

A building error left a [[power conduit]] too close to the hull, allowing a concerted effort by a team of starfighters that breaches the vessel's shields to deactivate the whole ship.<ref name="Poe Dameron 25" />
