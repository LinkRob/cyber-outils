'''Emilio Laiso''' is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] artist who worked on [[Shattered Empire 2|''Shattered Empire'' 2]], alongside fellow artists [[Marco Checchetto]] and [[Angel Unzueta]].<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" /> He also has drawn [[Star Wars Annual (2015) 2|''Star Wars Annual'' (2015) 2]].<ref name="Star Wars Annual 2" /> In [[2017]], he was the artist responsible for the art in the [[Star Wars: Rogue One Adaptation|comic adaptation]] of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' during the 6-issue mini-series, and began penciling the [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Doctor Aphra'']] series starting with [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 14|issue 14]].
