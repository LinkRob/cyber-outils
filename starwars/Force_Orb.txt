'''Force Orb''' was a [[The Force|Force]] [[Force power|power]] capable of creating a bubble of air. During the [[Battle of Mon Cala]] during the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Kit Fisto]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] created a Force Orb around [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s head after her glass helmet was cracked by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[commander]] [[Riff Tamson]]. Although the Force Orb worked initially, [[Naboo]] [[Junior Representative|Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]]' saliva proved to be a more effective method of sealing the helmet.<ref name="TCW:Prisoners" />
