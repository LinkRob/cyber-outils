'''Burin Kassal''' was an individual who served as a [[pilot]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Kassal was a [[smuggler]] prior to joining the rebellion, and when he contemplated leaving the rebellion [[Calle Tharandon]] convinced him to stay. He was an expert at slipping past [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] patrol crafts.<ref name="Legends" />
