The [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]] commanded<ref name="Honorable" /> a '''squad''' of [[stormtrooper]]s<ref name="Comprehensive" /> that was [[Onderon insurgency|deployed]] to the [[Planet|world]] [[Onderon]]<ref name="Honorable" /> around [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> The mission was Kallus' first assignment following his graduation.<ref name="Comprehensive" /> During a routine patrol, the [[squad]] was [[Attack on an Imperial patrol|attacked]]<ref name="Honorable" /> by a [[Partisans|rebel group]] led by the Onderon revolutionary [[Saw Gerrera]].<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> Kallus was rendered unconscious by the first blast, and after regaining consciousness, found himself paralyzed, unable to move as he witnessed a [[Unidentified Lasat mercenary|Lasat warrior]] calmly walking through the destruction, methodically [[Death|killing]] the rest of his [[Gender|men]]. Despite this, the [[Lasat]] spared the agent's life.<ref name="Honorable" /> Kallus harbored a hatred for Lasat due to the event.<ref name="Dawn" /> He later recounted the demise of his [[Stormtrooper Corps|squad]] to his long-time [[Spectres|rebel]] adversary, the Lasat [[Garazeb Orrelios]], when the two were stranded on the [[Water|icy]] [[moon]] [[Bahryn]]<ref name="Honorable" /> in [[3 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DawnOfRebellionGuide-Final.png|thumb|right|180px|''Dawn of Rebellion'' erroneously dated the squad's mission on Onderon to 7 BBY.]]
The squad was first mentioned in "[[The Honorable Ones]],"<ref name="Honorable" /> the seventeenth episode in the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', which premiered on [[February 24]], [[2016]].<ref name="Honorable Release">{{InsiderCite|169|Star Wars Rebels: Season Two Episode Guide|''Star Wars Rebels'': Season Two Episode Guide}}</ref>

The reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', published on [[April 25]], [[2023]],<ref name="Timelines Release">{{DK|url=us/book/9780744060874-star-wars-timelines|text=Star Wars Timelines}}</ref> states that Kallus lost his squad around 18 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> which is contradicted by ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'', published on [[December 5]], 2023,<ref name="Dawn Release">{{PenguinComics|url=book/?isbn=9780744087345|text=Star Wars Dawn of Rebellion The Visual Guide}}</ref> which instead dated the mission to [[7 BBY]]<ref name="Dawn" /> within a section written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="DawnAuthor">{{WookYouTube|-nqSqVOvfM4|Wookieepedia Interviews - Emily Shkoukani & Jason Fry}}</ref> However, the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'',<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> as well as the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'', both establish the mission to Onderon was his first since his graduation.<ref name="Comprehensive" /> Given that the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story "[[Flight of the Falcon, Part 1: Spy Games]]," written by [[Michael Moreci]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="Spy Games">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=14|story=Flight of the Falcon, Part 1: Spy Games}}</ref> on [[September 26]], [[2018]],<ref name="Solicitation 14">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2017-2020-14/digital-comic/696061|text=Star Wars Adventures #14|nolive=1}}</ref> depicts Kallus on a mission<ref name="Spy Games" /> dated by ''Timelines'' to around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Spy Games Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashback events of "[[Flight of the Falcon, Part 1: Spy Games]]" to around [[10 BBY]].</ref> Kallus' first mission must be prior to around that [[Standard year|year]]. Therefore, this article assumes that ''Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide'' is incorrect and that ''Star Wars: Timelines'' is correct.
