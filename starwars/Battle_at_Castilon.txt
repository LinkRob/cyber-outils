According to rumors in [[cantina]]s, a '''battle''' of the [[Galactic Civil War]] took place on<ref name="Squadrons" /> the [[ocean]]<ref name="Castilon">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Castilon]]<ref name="Squadrons" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> The battle involved the [[New Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], with the latter utilizing at least one [[Star Destroyer]]. During the battle, the New Republic sent the [[starship]] crashing down into the [[Water|ocean]].<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle was mentioned in the [[2020]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]'' in a description for the cosmetic [[flight helmet]] variant for [[New Republic pilot]]s, "Castilon Dawn."<ref name="Squadrons" />
