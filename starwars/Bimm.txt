The '''Bimms''' were a [[species]] of diminutive [[Sentience|sentients]] who resembled short [[human]]s. [[Rycar Ryjerd]], a patron of [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[Mos Eisley]], [[Tatooine]], was a Bimm.<ref name="CL 2016" /> A different species of [[Hair|furred]] individuals with floppy [[ear]]s was also called [[Bimm (furred species)|Bimm]].<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref>
