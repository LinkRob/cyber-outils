During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[61st Mobile Infantry]] fought a '''battle''' against the Empire in the [[mountain]]s of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naator]] in [[3 ABY]]. The battle was part of the 61st's campaign [[Operation Ringbreaker]], which targeted a number of weaker Imperial targets in hopes of forcing [[Imperial Military|military]] redeployments from the Empire's [[Kuat Drive Yards]] to leave its defenses weakens for an attack. The 61st won the battle, sustaining some casualties between that conflict and the following [[Battle of Xagobah|battle]] at the celestial body [[Xagobah]], but the rebel unit ultimately did not complete Operation Ringbreaker.

==Prelude==
[[File:Twilight_Company_patch.png|thumb|left|250px|The 61st Mobile Infantry (patch pictured) targeted Naator as part of Operation Ringbreaker.]]
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] led a campaign named [[Operation Ringbreaker]]. Devised by the ex-Imperial [[Governor]] [[Everi Chalis]], the campaign's end target was the Empire's [[Kuat Drive Yards]], the Empire's primary manufacturer and servicer of its [[Star Destroyer]]s located at the [[planet]] [[Kuat]]. In order to weaken the [[shipyard]]s' defenses, the 61st targeted a number of weaker Imperial installations to draw out [[Imperial Military|military]] redeployments from Kuat.<ref name="Twilight" /> The [[Mid Rim Territories]] planet<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> [[Naator]] was among the targets of the campaign, the world being a contributor to the Empire's war effort. The planet was listed in a report on the Operation Ringbreaker's first phase by [[General]] [[Crix Madine]] when the [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service]] vetted the campaign.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==The battle==
The 61st moved on to Naator after its [[Attack on Nakadia|attack]] on the [[agriworld]] [[Nakadia]]. The rebel unit fought and won against the Empire in the planet's<ref name="Twilight" /> [[Water|icy]]<ref name="Starships and Speeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> [[mountain]]s. Casualties were sustained between that battle and<ref name="Twilight" /> the campaign's next [[Battle of Xagobah|engagement]], which took place at the<ref name="Rebel Files" /> astronomical object<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[Xagobah]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Aftermath==
While the campaign proved successful in forcing redistribution of forces from the Kuat Drive Yards, the 61st ultimately did not make it to Kuat, having given up Operation Ringbreaker after being ambushed by the Empire at the planet [[Sullust]].<ref name="Twilight" /> The Alliance Intelligence reports on the campaign, including the one listing Naator, were eventually [[archive]]d in a compilation of non-electric documents called ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' by [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle of Naator was first mentioned in [[Alexander Freed]]'s [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''.<ref name="Twilight" />
