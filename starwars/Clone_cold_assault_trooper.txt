'''Clone cold assault troopers''',<ref name="charencupdated">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> also known as '''cold assault clone troopers'''<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> or '''Clone Snowtroopers'''<ref name="SWKids">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Type of Trooper in Star Wars|video=IKvho95xFho|nolive=1}}</ref> were a specialized variant of [[clone trooper]]s within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="SWECG" /> They were equipped with [[HT-77 Cold Assault Armor]]<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> and other cold-assault gear to handle combat in frigid environments,<ref name="Armor history">{{Databank|clone-trooper-armor-history-gallery|Clone Trooper Armor History Gallery}}</ref> including high winds, deep [[Water|snow]] and ice cliffs.<ref name="SWECG" /> All clone troopers were trained in the use of cold-weather gear, but the cold assault troopers were routinely stationed on frigid [[Planet|worlds]] with tough conditions. Cold assault troopers were proficient in armed and [[unarmed combat]], as well as military tactics.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Like most clone troopers,<ref name="Clone Troopers">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref> cold assault troopers were 1.83 [[meter]]s or 6 [[Foot|feet]] tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

Cold assault troopers served on planets like [[Caliban]], [[Orto]], [[Rhen Var]], [[Toola]],<ref name="SWECG" /> and [[Orto Plutonia]]. They also flew [[CK-6 swoop bike|freeco bikes]] on Orto Plutonia. [[Unidentified platoon (Galactic Republic)|A platoon]] of clone troopers of the [[501st Legion]]<ref name="Trespass" /> donned cold-assault gear<ref name="Armor history" /> and served as clone cold assault troopers during the [[One-Day War]].<ref name="Trespass" /> The [[Galactic Empire]] later modeled its [[cold weather assault trooper]]s after this type of clone trooper.<ref name="Complete Visual New" />
