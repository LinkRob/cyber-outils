'''Bildor's Canyon''' was located on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Complete Locations" /> The north entrance of the [[canyon]] connected to Tatooine's [[Desert Valley]] region and its south entrance led to the [[North Jundland Wastes]] region.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> 

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] began operating a [[Imperial Checkpoint|checkpoint]] at the north entrance and a small [[Unidentified Imperial outpost (Bildor's Canyon)|outpost]] near its south entrance.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BildorCanyon-CompleteLocations.jpg|thumb|200px|right|Bildor's Canyon on a map of Tatooine]]
Bildor's Canyon was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] published by [[Dorling Kindersley]].<ref name="Complete Locations" /> The canyon later appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

[[Bildor's Canyon/Legends|Bildor's Canyon]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in "[[Drawing the Maps of Peace: The Moisture Farmer's Tale]]," a short story written by [[M. Shayne Bell]] and published in ''[[Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina]]'' anthology by [[Bantam Spectra]] in [[1995]].<ref name="Maps of Peace">"[[Drawing the Maps of Peace: The Moisture Farmer's Tale]]"</ref>
