'''Candied rind''' was a curly garnish.<ref name="FACPOV" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"[[But What Does He Eat?]]" takes place concurrently with {{Film|V}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[Devaronian]] [[Executive Chef]] [[Torro Sbazzle]] garnished [[dumpling]]s with candied rind on top while working in [[Cloud City]] of the [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]].<ref name="FACPOV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Candied rind appeared in "[[But What Does He Eat?]]," a short story authored by [[S.A. Chakraborty]] and published in the [[2020]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="FACPOV" />
