The '''E-5C heavy blaster rifle''',<ref name="Battle Droids Encyclopedia" /> known simply as the '''E-5C''',<ref name="BF">{{EA|url=games/starwars/battlefront/star-wars-battlefront-2/classes/heavy-trooper|text=Heavy Trooper Class - Star Wars™ Battlefront™ II}}</ref> was a model of [[heavy blaster rifle]].<ref name="Battle Droids Encyclopedia" /> The E-5C was identical to the default [[E-5 blaster rifle]] used by the battle droids, save for an added wooden stock and modified barrel.<ref name="BFII" /> The [[bounty hunter]] [[C-21 Highsinger]] used an E-5C during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Bounty" /> [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[Ohnaka Gang|pirates]] also made use of this weapon, particularly [[Gwarm]].<ref name="ANB" /> The [[Heavy Battle Droid]]s employed by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] were notorious for using these blasters to rain fire upon the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="BFII" /> Standard [[B1-series battle droid]]s were also sometimes equipped with the E-5C<ref name="Base Series">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 4 - Rebels|cardname=Battle Droid}}</ref> as "E-5C B1 [[Trooper]]s."<ref name="E-5C card">{{Legion|set=Clone Wars Core Set|cardname=E-5C B1 Trooper}}</ref>

[[Shriv Suurgav]], an [[Alliance officer|officer]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], made use of an E-5C during the [[Battle of Theed]].<ref name="BFII" />

[[Kranor]], a [[Trandoshan]] hunter and member of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]], carried an E-5C as one of his primary weapons.<ref name="Republic Under Siege">[[Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege 2|''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 2]]</ref>
