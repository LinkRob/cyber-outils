The '''Creature Husbandry Team''' was a subdivision of the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] focused on the care and release of various [[Sentience|non-sentient]] creatures from [[the galaxy]] under the watch of the organization. After the [[Battle of Exegol]]<ref name="Bestiary" /> in [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BestiaryVol1Date}}</ref> the [[San Tekka clan]] member [[Ardis San Tekka]] became a part of the team before she eventually became the organization's [[Director of Creature Cartography]]. San Tekka mentioned her time in the Creature Husbandry Team in the introduction of her [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|bestiary]], written at some point during her tenure.<ref name="Bestiary" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Creature Husbandry Team was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[S.T. Bende]].<ref name="Bestiary" />
