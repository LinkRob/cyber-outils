'''Bot-drones''' were a type of remotely [[pilot]]ed [[drone]] craft. Developed with the intention to be used by the [[Galactic Empire]], they were abandoned due to the unsteadiness of the technology involved, and a number of bot-drones fell into the hands of [[Wef]], an [[arms dealer]] who often worked for the [[Hidden Hand]] crime syndicate. [[Rebak]], an operative of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], requested to purchase the bot-drones, but the [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance's crew|crew]] led by [[Beilert Valance]] wished to use them in their [[Hunt for Vader|hunt]] for [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The [[slicer]] [[Chio Fain]] piloted the bot-drones in a skirmish against Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], and merged the drones together into a wedge, colliding them into the [[Conning tower|command tower]] of the [[Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> ''[[Formidable]]''.<ref name="Target Vader 4">[[Target Vader 4|''Target Vader'' 4]]</ref>
