The '''"Sweeper" Emitter Modification Kit''' was a model of [[beam splitter]] manufactured by [[Safidine]]. It caused a [[blaster]]'s bolts to spread, allowing it to potentially hit multiple targets.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Sweeper" Emitter Modification Kit was first mentioned in ''[[Forged in Battle]]'',<ref name="Forged in Battle" /> a [[2016]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Soldier On!">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/8/17/soldier-on-1|text=Soldier On!}}</ref>
