A '''blade-breaker''' was a small [[vibroblade]] that could catch an opponent's melee weapon. Blade-breakers were typically held in the off-hand and used in conjunction with a larger weapon, although it was possible to wield a blade-breaker in each hand. The weapons, which cost 250 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], were usually fitted with an [[ultrasonic generator]] that was able to shatter [[durasteel]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blade-breakers were mentioned ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> They were first introduced in the [[2015]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] book ''[[Fly Casual]]''.<ref name="Casual">''[[Fly Casual]]''</ref>
