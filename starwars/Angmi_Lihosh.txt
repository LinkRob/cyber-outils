'''Angmi Lihosh''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]<ref name="Call to Action">{{Rebels|Call to Action}}</ref> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[4 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Alongside [[Tsoklo]] and [[Lornto Teswam]], she listened to a [[Ezra Bridger]]'s [[Ezra's broadcast|broadcast]] denouncing the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Call to Action" /> She later found herself at the mercy of Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s. However, the [[Spectres]] led by [[Kanan Jarrus]] managed to rescue her, and she went to live in the rebel hideout known as the [[Haven]], along with other defenseless citizens.<ref name="Recon Missions" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Help me, and I will teach you how to grow stronger.|Lihosh offers to teach one of the Spectres in return for aid|<ref name="Recon Missions" />}}
After being brought to the Haven, Lihosh remained frightened but was given hope by a member of the Spectres, whom she thanked.<ref name="Recon Missions" />

==Equipment==
Angmi Lihosh wore a conical [[helmet]] with a [[Color|green]] tunic and trousers.<ref name="Call to Action" />

==Behind the scenes==
Angmi Lihosh first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Call to Action]],"<ref name="Call to Action" /> and was first identified in the mobile game ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Recon Missions]]''.<ref name="Recon Missions" /> Along with extras from the episodes "[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion|Spark of Rebellion]],"<ref name="SOR">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref> "[[The Future of the Force]],"<ref name="TFOTF">{{Rebels|The Future of the Force}}</ref> and "[[Legacy (episode)|Legacy]],"<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref> Lihosh's character model is a recolored version of that of Imperial [[Minister]] [[Maketh Tua]].<ref name="Droids in Distress">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref>
