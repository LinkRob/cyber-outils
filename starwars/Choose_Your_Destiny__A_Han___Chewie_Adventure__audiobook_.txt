'''''Choose Your Destiny: A Han & Chewie Adventure''''' is the audio version of the [[canon]] [[Choose Your Destiny: A Han & Chewie Adventure|book]] of the same name, written by [[Cavan Scott]] and part of the ''[[Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny]]'' series. The audiobook was released exclusively on [[Wikipedia:Amazon|Amazon]] FreeTime Unlimited in [[2019]].<ref name="Website">{{WebCite|url=https://cavanscott.com/2019/12/20/star-wars-choose-your-destiny-named-one-of-alexas-top-ten-skills-of-2019|text=Star Wars Choose Your Destiny named one of Alexa's top ten skills of 2019|archivedate=20201115001018}}</ref> As of June 2024, it is exclusively available as an Alexa Skill (this one story is free without an Amazon Kids+ subscription).<ref name="Skill">{{Amazon|url=/Star-Wars-Choose-Your-Destiny/dp/B07LCZGLD7|text=Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny|archivedate=20240615023033}}</ref> 

There is one canon ending and there are 21 non-canon endings. The path to the canon ending is [BB,AB,AAB],BBABABA,[AA,BAA],
ABBB,[A,BA],[B,AA,ABA],BAA (the brackets indicate a choice of paths). Therefore there are 36 different paths to the canon ending, taking between 41 to 47 minutes to reach the ending. The total duration of all 67 story segments is approximately 91 minutes and there are 242 total paths possible.
