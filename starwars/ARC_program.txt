The '''ARC program''', also known simply as '''ARC training''',<ref name="CEUE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> and alternatively known as the '''Clone Commander training program''',<ref name="GAR">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Grand Army of the Republic}}</ref> was a training program that certain [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] underwent in order to turn them into leaders by developing their individual thinking.<ref name="CEUE" /> Additionally, all [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s received the training program prior to attaining their status.<ref name="Note">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'' establishes that [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s receive ARC program training prior to becoming ARC [[trooper]]s.</ref> The training also served to prepare Advanced Recon Commandos for the special missions they would be tasked with carrying out.<ref name="The Amazing Book">''[[The Amazing Book of Star Wars]]''</ref> [[Neyo]] and [[Rex]] were among the first one hundred graduates of the ARC program.<ref name="GAR" />
