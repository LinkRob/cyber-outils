The '''Alsakan system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Alsaka sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> and [[the Interior]]<ref name="Interior" /> in [[the galaxy]]. The [[Star system|system]] was situated in grid square L-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> It contained the terrestrial<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Alsakan]]<ref name="SWGalaxy" />, one of the [[Galactic Empire]] greatest supporters.<ref name="First Order" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Alsakan system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] it was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' in [[2009]] by its authors [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Atlas">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
