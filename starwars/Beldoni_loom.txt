A '''Beldoni loom''' was a loom used to weave [[clothing]].<ref name="SWTATVG" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Timeline">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> [[Senator]] for the [[Sern sector]] [[Bal-Cobor Sampurn]] wore robes made of [[Ghorman twill]] woven on a 15,000 [[Standard year|year]]-old Beldoni loom. The loom shared its name with the [[Beldoni Valley]] on the [[planet]] [[Ghorman]].<ref name="SWTATVG" />
