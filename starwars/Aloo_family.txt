The '''Aloo family''' was a wealthy and influential [[human]] family from the [[ecumenopolis]] of [[Coruscant]]. They also owned a residence on [[Centax-3]], one of Coruscant's [[moon]]s.<ref name="TMCAFOF" /> Under the [[Imperial Era|rule]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Sim Aloo]] was one of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] closest [[Emperor's adviser|advisers]].<ref name="Sim Aloo DB" /> His daughter [[Alinka Aloo|Alinka]] helped him take care of the family business and retrieve [[Sith]] [[Sith artifact|artifacts]] for the Emperor.<ref name="TMCAFOF" />
