The '''FG 8T8-T''', also known as the '''Twin Block2 Special''', was a unique, technologically advanced, and visually striking model of [[podracer]] produced by the small boutique manufacturer [[Farwan & Glott]]. It prioritised safety over speed and was among the slowest factory customs avaliable.<ref name="Endless Vigil" /> The [[Xamster]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Neva Kee]] flew a modified [[Neva Kee's Custom Podracer|FG 8T8-T]] during the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] in [[32 BBY|7945]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] but vanished during the race.<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>
