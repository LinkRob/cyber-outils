The '''Esstran sector''' was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]]. The [[sector]] contained the [[planet]] [[Moraband]]<ref name="Mustafar and Other Outer Rim Planets" /> and the [[Thurra system]] which contained the planet [[Thule]].<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref> The [[Sith Worlds]] was a subregion of the sector.

==Behind the scenes==
The Esstran sector first [[canon]]ically appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Sacrifice (episode)|Sacrifice]],"<ref name="Sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref> which was released on [[Netflix]] on [[March 7]], [[2014]], as part of the show's [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth season]],<ref name="TCW on Netflix">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-the-lost-missions-now-on-netflix|text=Star Wars: The Clone Wars &ndash; The Lost Missions Now on Netflix|nolive=1}}</ref> of which it is the thirteenth and final episode.<ref name="Explore: Sacrifice">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sacrifice-episode-guide|text="Sacrifice" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> The sector was first identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity, the [[Esstran sector/Legends|Esstran sector]] first appeared in [[The Empire Strikes Back Monthly 149|''The Empire Strikes Back Monthly'' 149]],<ref name="TESB 149">{{StoryCite|story=Death Masque|book=The Empire Strikes Back Monthly 149}}</ref> and was first identified in the [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion]], published on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
