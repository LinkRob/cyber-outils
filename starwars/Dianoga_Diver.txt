The '''Dianoga Diver''' was an outfit owned by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Salvagers|standard costume]]. This garb featured a distinct design for each Jawa, with the upper brother sporting a large [[Color|red]] cloak with a hood resembling the head of a [[Dianoga]], while the cloak's frayed hem resembled thick tentacles with spikes and green [[algae]]-like markings. The upper Jawa also wore a [[stormtrooper]]-style [[Armor|chestplate]] with various tubes and greebles attached, while the lower brother wore brown [[robe]]s with the same algae patterns and what appeared to be a [[goggless]]-like attachment on their head. On the back of the upper Jawa's hood was a large eye like that of a Dianoga, and suspended to their back was an assortment of [[droid]] parts, including a damaged [[MSE-6 series repair droid]] and the head of an [[RA-7 protocol droid]]].<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dianoga Diver was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Utooni, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dianoga Set]].<ref name="Hunters" />
