By around [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Reveal">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[A Coruscant Solstice]]" to around [[232 BBY]].</ref> '''elemental engines''' were used to carefully maintain the [[Coruscant weather control|weather]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and could be used to decide when wind would blow or [[holo-snow]] would fall.<ref name="ACS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Elemental engines were mentioned in "[[A Coruscant Solstice]]," a short story written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in the [[2021]] ''[[Life Day Treasury]]'' anthology.<ref name="ACS" />
