'''Eric Musco''' is the Community Manager for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', a MMORPG developed by [[BioWare]].

==Biography==
Eric Musco started working at [[BioWare]] in [[2011]], as a Social Media Coordinator. He was promoted to Community Coordinator in [[2012]], and Community Manager, in [[2013]].<ref name="LinkedIn" />
