'''FX-6''' was a [[Service droid|waiter droid]] at the [[presidential manor]] on [[Begamor]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. During [[Sean]]'s [[mission to Begamor]], FX-6 detected [[Allergy|allergens]] in the [[Smoked Kaadu Ribs|kaadu ribs]] the [[chef]] [[Bellon]] had prepared. Out of anger, Bellon attacked the [[droid]], but FX-6 was able to [[stun]] him.<ref name="Vow" />
