The '''C1 personal comlink''' was a standard model of [[comlink]] produced by [[SoroSuub Corporation]] and used by the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="DB comlink" /> and the [[First Order]].<ref name="TROS" /> When [[Luke Skywalker]] [[Rescue of Princess Leia|rescued]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] from the cells of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first Death Star]], he kept in contact with the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] via a C1 personal comlink.<ref name="ANH" />
