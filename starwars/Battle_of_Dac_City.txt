The '''Battle of Dac City''' was a conflict that occurred<ref name="Dark Lord 14" /> one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], when [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] undertook a [[Invasion of Mon Cala|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]] to determine if a [[Jedi]] was hiding there and [[Advisor|advising]] the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Mon Calamari monarchy|king]], [[Lee-Char]], in negotiations with the Empire. [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] aboard the ''[[Sovereign]]'' had previously sent down [[ambassador]] [[Telvar]] to make an trade deal with Lee-Char but the [[king]] continued to deny the [[Gender|man]] and he was forced to take leave for the [[time]] being.<ref name="Dark Lord 13" />

At that moment, Vader arrived, along with three [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]] and a squad of elite [[Purge Trooper]]s. They were confronted by the king's [[Chief of Security|chief of security]] [[Gial Ackbar]] who denied the possibility of there being a threat on Mon Cala. However the [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] carrying Telvar exploded, [[Death|killing]] all on board. This prompted Tarkin to deploy his [[Imperial Military|forces]] onto Mon Cala. Lee-Char informed the [[Ferren Barr|Jedi]] of this and the Jedi said he could handle the Inquisitors and that he knew the identify of the [[Color|black]] [[Darth Vader's armor|armored]] individual Ackbar had failed to get the name of.<ref name="Dark Lord 13" />

[[Mon Calamari]] and [[Quarren]] forces under [[King of Mon Cala|King]] [[Lee-Char]] battled an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] battalion consisting of [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT walkers]], [[All Terrain Defense Pod|AT-DP walkers]], and [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. Meanwhile, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]] [[Ninth Sister]], [[Tenth Brother]], and the [[Sixth Brother]], and [[Purge Trooper]]s secured the landing platform after killing many Mon Calamari and Quarren.

The Inquisitors headed towards the palace. The Ninth Sister thought that it was folly to believe that the [[Jedi]] fugitive would be hiding near the King. Vader responded that King Lee-Char knew how to contact the Jedi fugitive. The Tenth Brother thought that Lee-Char probably fled the moment that the Imperial battalion arrived. However, Vader sensed that the king was still there. Per his previous encounter with the king , Darth Vader reasoned that the king would not abandon his city utill the battle was done.

In the command center, King Lee-Char viewed a hologram of the battle. [[Commander]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] informed him that the defenders were holding their own but the Empire had landed substantial forces on all their "above ground" settlements, which the Mon Calamari and Quarren did not excel at fighting. King Lee-Char gave orders for the troops to hold until the evacuation was complete. Ackbar promised that their forces would hold and that they had already moved fifty percent of civilians below the surface.

[[Admiral]] [[Raddus]] was unwilling to surrender Mon Cala's surface cities to the Empire since this was their planet. Lee-Char countered that this was strategic retreat. Once their populations were safe, he said that they would consider their response. He explained that his goal was to convince the Empire to leave without shedding the blood of Mon Cala. He saw two options: war and diplomacy.
