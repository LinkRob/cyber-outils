'''Alaphani''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]. They had plated and grooved [[Color|gray]] skin with six light blue [[eye]]s between plates on their heads. Several grey [[Tooth|teeth]] jutted outward from their [[mouth]] and had holes above them. Their heads were attached to their body by multiple thin connections. Alaphani had two segmented [[Limb|arms]] that ended in a hand with three long-nailed fingers each. They also had two legs and a foot for each. Members of the Alaphani species spoke a [[Alaphani (language)|language of the same name]].<ref name="SW50" />

In [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'' (2015) 50 to [[1 ABY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]]<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Han Solo]] met with an [[Unidentified Alaphani|Alaphani]] on the [[Ring of Kafrene]]<ref name="SW50" /> [[space station]].<ref name="CGSWG" /> While there, Solo made use of the [[translator]] [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] to make a deal with the Alaphani for a set of [[transponder code]]s. According to Solo, no Alaphani worth their name would give ten percent off in exchange for a [[droid]].<ref name="SW50" />
