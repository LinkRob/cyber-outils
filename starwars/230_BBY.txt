The following events took place in or around the year '''230 BBY''', aka '''3047 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]], '''211 BFE''' according to the [[Imperial calendar|Imperial Calendar]], and '''Year 7747''' according to the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.

==Events==
*[[Farzala Tarabal]] and [[Qort]] are promoted to Jedi Knights on the ''[[Star Hopper]]''.<ref name="THRA 13">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 13|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 13]]</ref>
*[[Starlight Beacon]] is [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destroyed]] by the [[Nihil]].<ref name="Fallen Star" />
*[[Reath Silas]] is promoted to [[Jedi Knight]] on [[Corellia]], and his mentor, [[Jedi Master]] [[Cohmac Vitus]], leaves the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
*[[Nan Yujin]], the younger brother of [[Nan Holi]], becomes friends with [[Nan Qiwen]].<ref name="NanQiwen">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. The webnovel states that [[Nan Yujin]], the younger brother of [[Nan Holi]], had been friends with [[Nan Qiwen]] for thirty [[Standard year|years]].</ref>
*[[Nan Zaizhou]] begins to work under Nan Holi as the latter's secretary.<ref name="NanZaizhou">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. The webnovel states that [[Nan Zaizhou]] had served [[Nan Holi]] as his for nearly thirty [[Standard year|years]].</ref>
*The [[Jedi High Council]] implements the [[Guardian Protocols]].<ref name="SOS 1">[[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 1]]</ref>
