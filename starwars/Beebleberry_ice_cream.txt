'''Beebleberry ice cream''' was a type of [[ice cream]]. Prior to the [[Battle of Starkiller Base|assault]] on [[Starkiller Base]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Finn]], [[BB-8]] and [[C-3PO]] had to find some of it for [[Nien Nunb]] so that he would help them find their needed supplies for the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.
