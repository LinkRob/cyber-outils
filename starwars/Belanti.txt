'''Belanti''' was a company that manufactured [[repulsorlift]] [[engine]]s. Its engines did not produce a shimmer between the [[Repulsorcraft|vehicle]] using the engine and the [[planet]]ary surface it was pushing against, as was the case with some short-range repulsorlift engines.<ref name="BMF 38" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Belanti was mentioned in the article "Repulsorlifts," which was published in the "[[Secrets of Spaceflight]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 38|thirty-eighth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine<ref name="BMF 38" /> around [[September 23]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|38}}</ref> The [[Belanti/Legends|company]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'', a [[1987]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Curtis Smith]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="SWS">''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]''</ref>
