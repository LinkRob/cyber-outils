'''Daldom''' was the [[language]] of the [[Elnacon]] [[species]].<ref name="YouTube">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref> The Elnacon [[Dava Cassamam]] spoke in Daldom while playing [[sabacc]] in [[the Lodge]] on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]]<ref name="Solo" /> in [[10 BBY]].<ref name="SoloOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Daldom first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> It was first identified as Daldom in the video "Every Language in ''Star Wars''" which released in [[2019]] on the ''Star Wars'' Kids [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel.<ref name="YouTube" />
