'''David Freiberg''' (born [[August 24]], [[1930s|1938]])<ref name="SWYT 27-08-2020" /> is a musician who was the keyboardist for the rock band [[Jefferson Starship]]. He also portrayed the [[Unidentified Human keyboardist (Blockade of Kashyyyk)|keyboardist]] in the [[Unidentified Human band|Holographic Band]] segment of ''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]''.
