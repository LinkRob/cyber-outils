An '''ancient hunter carving''' made from [[driftwood]] had been created long before the [[Imperial Era]]. The carving depicted a great hunt and represented the thrill of hunting.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> It had been stored in a container at an abandoned camp in the [[Flyer Ridge]] cavern beneath the [[Typhon's Rock]] mound on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> [[pirate]]s had stored items at the same camp in Flyer Ridge and the camp had been protected by a [[Ray shield|energy barrier]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] used a [[power module]] for her [[Kay's blaster|blaster]] to destroy a wall and enter the cavern system. While there she found and stole the ancient carving.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
An ancient hunter carving appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, the carving is a major charm that players can equip to increase the amount of [[adrenaline]] gained from all sources.<ref name="Outlaws" />
