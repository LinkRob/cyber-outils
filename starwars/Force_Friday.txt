'''Force Friday''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' merchandising event.

==2015==
The original Force Friday event was held on [[September 4]], [[2015]]. The event was a celebration of the release of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] and features the release of the film's toy and action-figure line, a number of new novels and reference books that are part of the [[Journey to Star Wars: The Force Awakens|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']] initiative, and other merchandise such as cosmetics.<ref name="arrive">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-force-awakens-products-to-arrive-on-force-friday-september-4|text=''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Products to Arrive on 'Force Friday,' September 4}}</ref>

As part of its marketing initiative, [[The Walt Disney Company]] partnered with nine major brands: Covergirl & Max Factor, Duracell, FCA US, Pottery Barn Kids, General Mills, HP, Subway and Verizon.<ref name="grip">{{WebCite|url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/business/wp/2015/08/17/with-the-force-of-star-wars-disney-tightens-grip-on-toy-universe|text=With the force of 'Star Wars,' Disney tightens grip on toy universe|author=Harwell, Drew|date=2015-08-17|archivedate=20170421202007}}</ref> [[Wikipedia:Toys "R" Us|Toys "R" Us]] also confirmed its participation in Force Friday, being held at all of its locations in the United States with the sole exception of stores located in [[Wikipedia:Paramus, New Jersey|Paramus, New Jersey]]. Visitors received a free ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' poster. As part of its event, Toys "R" Us held a contest with the winner offered an exclusive "Midnight Madness VIP Shopping Spree." The winner was Thomas Burns of Jacksonville, Florida.<ref name="toysrus">{{WebCite|url=http://www.toysrus.com/shop/index.jsp?categoryId=33810026&ab=Tru_homepage_aspot:bottom:2:Destination-Star-Wars-|text=Toys "R" Us: Star Wars 2015|nobackup=1}}</ref> [[Wikipedia:Walmart|Walmart]] also participated in the event, offering an exclusive ''[[Star Wars]]'' collectible that was previewed at Comic Con.<ref name="walmart">{{WebCite|url=http://news.walmart.com/news-archive/2015/07/09/the-force-is-strong-at-walmart-company-to-offer-a-new-generation-of-star-warstm-merchandise-at-12-01-am-on-force-friday|text=The Force is strong at Walmart: Company to offer a new generation of Star WarsTM merchandise at 12:01 a.m. on 'Force Friday'|author=Barron, Andy|date=2015-07-09|archivedate=20150713005757}}</ref>

In addition to in-store events, merchandise was also offered simultaneously online at retailers such the [[Wikipedia:Disney Store|Disney Store]] and [[Wikipedia:Amazon.com|Amazon.com]].<ref name="mercury">{{WebCite|url=https://www.mercurynews.com/2015/08/18/sept-4-force-friday-merchandise-launch-is-meant-to-stoke-fan-fervor-for-next-star-wars-film|text=Sept. 4 'Force Friday' merchandise launch is meant to stoke fan fervor for next 'Star Wars' film|date=2015-08-18|archivedate=20200812131200}}</ref>

Online leaks confirmed the existence of action figures for major new characters in the film, such as [[Finn]] and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], as well as bobbleheads of these characters and others like [[BB-8]] and [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].<ref name="highdef">{{WebCite|url=http://www.highdefdigest.com/blog/star-wars-force-friday-2015|text=Force Friday! A.K.A. Just Take All My Money, Disney!|author=Nutt, Shannon|date=2015-08-17|archivedate=20171018205044}}</ref> On [[August 24]], 2015, [[Hasbro]] released a catalog of its planned offerings for the event. These included character masks, various action figures, a "Battle Action ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''", Micro Machines vehicles and more.<ref name="hasbrocatalog">{{WebCite|url=http://www.cleveland.com/entertainment/index.ssf/2015/08/hasbro_unveils_star_wars_the_f.html|text=Hasbro unveils 'Star Wars: The Force Awakens' toys available 'Force Friday'|author=Smith, Troy|date=2015-08-24|archivedate=20200812131508}}</ref>

On [[September 3]], the [http://www.youtube.com/user/starwars ''Star Wars'' YouTube channel] live streamed a worldwide [[Wikipedia:Unboxing|unboxing]] event by [[Wikipedia:Maker Studios|Maker Studios]]. The event was broadcast from fifteen major cities, leading up to the midnight release of Force Friday products.<ref>{{SW|url=news/the-force-awakens-around-the-world-star-wars-fans-invited-to-join-global-event-on-youtube|text=''The Force Awakens'' Around the World: ''Star Wars'' Fans Invited to Join Global Event on YouTube}}</ref>

==2016==
''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', the ''Star Wars'' [[Star Wars Anthology Series|anthology]] film, received a Force Friday event on [[September 30]], [[2016]].<ref name="movieweb">{{WebCite|url=https://movieweb.com/rogue-one-star-wars-story-force-friday-september-2016|text=Rogue One: A Star Wars Story Is Getting Its Own Force Friday This September|author=Orange, B.|date=2016-02-13|archivedate=20170224175708}}</ref> [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] and [[Lucasfilm]] announced the release of new toys in conjunction with its "Go Rogue" initiative, which encourages fans to create their own stories or skits in the ''Star Wars'' [[The galaxy|galaxy]].<ref name="gorogue">{{SW|url=news/go-rogue-and-tell-your-star-wars-story-in-upcoming-contest|text=Go Rogue Now: UGC Contest Open and ''Rogue One'' Toys Hits Stores}}</ref>

==2017==
A Force Friday II event was held at midnight on [[September 1]], [[2017]] for [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="lastjedifriday">{{WebCite|url=https://whtc.com/news/articles/2017/feb/16/disney-plans-midnight-star-wars-event-to-unveil-last-jedi-toys|text=Disney plans midnight 'Star Wars' event to unveil 'Last Jedi' toys|author=Richwine, Lisa|date=2017-02-16|archivedate=20200812131820}}</ref> Force Friday II was accompanied by an [[Wikipedia:Augmented reality|augmented reality]] [[Find the Force|event]] from September 1-3.<ref>{{SW|url=news/announcing-force-friday-iis-find-the-force-ar-event|text=Announcing Force Friday II's "Find the Force" AR Event}}</ref>

==2019==
Triple Force Friday was held on [[October 4]], [[2019]], and featured merchandise for [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' and the video game ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''.<ref>{{SW|url=news/triple-force-friday-announced|text=Triple Force Friday Announced}}</ref> On [[September 26]], [[Anthony Carboni]] and [[Warwick Davis]] hosted a YouTube livestream from [[Pinewood Studios]], in which cast members from all three projects assisted in unveiling some of the upcoming merchandise.<ref>{{SW|url=news/triple-force-friday-livestream|text=A Galaxy of ''Star Wars'' Stars Count Down to Triple Force Friday}}</ref>
