The '''Bescane system''' was a [[star system]] located in the regions known as the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> Located in grid square K-3 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], Bescane was connected via [[hyperspace route]]s to the astronomical objects [[Bastion]], [[Dubrillion]] and [[Jaemus system|Jaemus]].<ref name="TFABG" />
