The '''C-B3 cortosis battle droid''',<ref name="Official Magazine 118" /> or '''C-B3''' for short,<ref name="rising" /> was a [[cortosis]]-armored model variation of the [[B2-series super battle droid]]s which were produced in secret by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] on the [[planet]] [[Mokivj]] near the end of the [[Clone Wars]], in [[19 BBY]]. They were manufactured in the [[Mokivj droid factory]] by [[Duke]] [[Solha]], his [[Solha's brother|brother]] and his [[Solha's sister|sister]].<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />

When [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] discovered the cortosis super battle droids and the factory that produced them, he considered them "unstoppable" and a big threat to the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Galactic Republic]]. He therefore decided that he should destroy the factory that produced them along with all the already manufactured ones and the mine where the cortosis was being extracted. So that the Separatists couldn't manufacture any more droids on Mokivj. Skywalker set the [[Mining|mine]] to explode, despite the objections of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], and [[Nodlia|LebJau]]. The explosion was expanded by the cortosis which activated [[Volcano|volcanic]] activity on the planet and caused a [[Mission to Mokivj|catastrophe]]. The schematics were also reprogrammed so that any future cortosis battle droids would end up destroying themselves.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" /> While some C-B3s were later produced elsewhere, such as ones deployed to the planet Wrea, they used the sabotaged designs making them ineffective.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />

The cortosis B2 super battle droid had the exact same weaponry as the standard model and the same height. However, the cortosis in their armor made them much heavier and also made them invulnerable to [[lightsaber]]s and [[blaster]] bolts in their whole body but their [[Eye|optical sensors]]; as they weren't covered by cortosis. They were equipped with a [[Wrist blaster|dual wrist blaster]], like the B2-series battle droid did.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />

==Description==
The C-B3 cortosis battle droid<ref name="Official Magazine 118" /> was a variation of the [[B2-series super battle droid]], the cortosis variation had the same firepower, height, color and sensors as the base model. They were created by [[Duke]] [[Solha]] on the [[planet]] [[Mokivj]].<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />

==History==
===Production===
In [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Thrawn Alliances date">The events of ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'' set during the [[Clone Wars]] are dated to [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>, [[Count of Serenno]] [[Dooku]] dispatched Duke Solha, his [[Solha's brother|brother]] and his [[Solha's sister|sister]] to oversee [[mining]] operations, processing of [[cortosis]], and manufacturing of the cortosis variant of the B2 and cortosis [[clone trooper armor]] on Mokivj. Solha and his siblings used a [[fortress]] built prior to the [[Clone Wars]] to manufacture cortosis clone trooper armor and the cortosis battle droids.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />

===Defending the Mokivj droid factory===
Cortosis B2 super battle droids participated in the raid on Solha's factory, defending the factory from the [[Jedi]] and [[Chiss Ascendancy]] attackers, cortosis super battle droids escorted Solha through the factory, protecting him from one of the attackers, [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]. After Skywalker tried to confuse Solha's super battle droid escort, the battle droids sieged the raiders in another section of the factory.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />

During the infiltration, Skywalker's droid [[R2-D2]] gained access to the assembly's control system, and reprogrammed it so the cortosis would also cover the discharge capacitor, meaning every time it fired it would slowly damage its own system and its [[Droid brain|brain]] until it gets completely fried, meaning it would only last five minutes in active combat.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" /> Some of these defective C-B3s would see production, and indeed were inefficient in battle. [[Clone Intelligence]] spotted some during a [[siege on Wrea]]. Later, the C-B3 was mentioned in an intelligence report, which was included in the survival compendium "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]."<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />

===Imperial Era===
In the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Saw Gerrera]] set up a base on [[Wrea]] and would use some of the droid bodies there for training. While staying with Gerrera, the young [[Jyn Erso]] encountered a shiny C-B3 droid that had been modified hastily. Erso considered the C-B3 to be a new model, believing it had only started to see manufacture following the Clone Wars, unaware<ref name="rising" /> that C-B3s were used during the conflict and even the siege on Wrea.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />
