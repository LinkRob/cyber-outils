'''Faro Argyus''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[captain]] who served with the [[Senate Commando|elite commandos]] of the [[Senate Guard]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. He [[Treason|betrayed]] the [[Galactic Republic]] while on an assignment to deliver [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] to [[Coruscant]], believing that his contribution to the [[Rescue on the Tranquility|rescue]] of a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] leader would be rewarded by [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. Argyus was [[Death|killed]], however, by Dooku's personal [[assassin]], [[Asajj Ventress]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
{{Quote|Sometimes being a good soldier means doing what you think is right.|Argyus|audio=ArgyusGoodSoldier.flac|<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />}}
Faro Argyus was [[Reproduction|born]] as a member of the affluent [[Argyus family]], who served the [[Senate Guard]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. By the [[time]] Argyus joined, the family had served the Guard for five generations. Due to his abilities as a leader and as a [[soldier]], Argyus was inducted in the Guard's famed [[Senate Commando]] [[division]].<ref name="HC 30" /> At the time of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> Argyus held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[captain]] in the Senate Commandos.<ref name="Captain Argyus">{{Databank|captain-argyus|Captain Argyus}}</ref><ref name="Senate Guard">{{Databank|senate-guard|Senate Guard}}</ref> In secret, however, Argyus came to regard his life as a Senate Guard as an empty experience where he did nothing but serve others.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

===Incident on the ''Tranquility''===
{{Quote|As successful a rescue one could hope for, Viceroy. I will be a legend for this.|Argyus to Nute Gunray|<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />}}
[[File:Argyus_vs_Gree.jpg|left|thumb|231x231px|Argyus attacks Gree.]]
Following the [[Mission to Rodia|arrest]] of [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Argyus and a team of [[commando]]s were tasked with escorting the [[prisoner]] to [[Coruscant]], where he would stand trial for acts of [[treason]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. In response to the capture of Gunray, the Separatist [[Head of State]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]], reached out to Argyus with a generous payment in return for Gunray's return to the Separatists. Tired of his life as a Senate Guard, Argyus agreed.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

Meanwhile, [[assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] led an [[Rescue on the Tranquility|assault]] on the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tranquility]]'' in order to retrieve or silence the [[Neimoidian]] should he talk. Argyus and the commandos guarded Gunray in the [[Starship|ship's]] [[Prison|brig]] alongside [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]] while [[Jedi General]] [[Luminara Unduli]] and the [[clone trooper]]s of [[Green Company]] defended the vessel against Ventress and her [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

[[File:Argyus_death.png|thumb|right|220x220px|Ventress kills Argyus]]
After Ventress failed in her first attempt to rescue Gunray, Argyus encouraged Tano to aid Unduli in her pursuit of the assassin. With the [[Padawan]] gone, however, Argyus [[Death|killed]] the remaining commandos and fought with [[Commander]] Gree to liberate Gunray. Gree overpowered Argyus by knocking him down, but was then hit by a rifle as Gunray snuck up on him. Despite the [[Clone Commander]]'s efforts, Argyus fled the [[Star Destroyer]] in an [[escape pod]] with both Gunray and Ventress. The former captain believed that his efforts would be rewarded by [[Count]] [[Dooku]], only to be impaled in side of the chest by Ventress while attempting to claim credit for the success of the mission.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

===Legacy===
Ultimately, Argyus's treason brought the loyalty of the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] Guard into question.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] was troubled by the captain's betrayal, seeing that the Republic's enemies were around them all.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> The Guard's authority was weakened, and [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] view of the organization was diminished.<ref name="Rise" /> While Senate Commandos were once seen as a high level of security,<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> the [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Red Guard]] and [[Jedi Order]] became the groups that oversaw the transfer of high-value prisoners.<ref name="Rise" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:CaptainArgyus-Db.png|thumb|180px|right|Argyus was arrogant and greedy.]]
{{Quote|Why'd you do it, Argyus?''"<br />"''A ''clone'' like you could never understand. I wanted a ''life'' of more than empty servitude.|Gree and Argyus|<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />}}
Argyus was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Captain Argyus" /> who stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]]).<ref name="Join the Battle" /> He had [[Color|blond]] [[hair]], amber [[eye]]s, and light skin.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

As a member of the Senate Commandos,<ref name="Senate Guard" /> Captain Argyus<ref name="Captain Argyus" /> was among the best and bravest [[soldier]]s in the ranks of the Senate Guard.<ref name="Senate Guard" /> Despite his service to the Republic, Argyus resented his career as a soldier, which he regarded as a life of meaningless servitude. His disillusionment and desire for wealth caused his loyalties to falter, allowing for his allegiance to be purchased through bribery by Count Dooku. Though motivated by the promise of financial compensation, Argyus justified treason through his conviction that a good soldier acted in accordance with his conscience.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a captain in the Senate Commandos, Argyus was skilled in armed and unarmed combat, military tactics and security.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
Argyus wore senate commando armor, which bore the symbol of the [[Galactic Senate]] on the left shoulder pad of his Senate Commando [[armor]].<ref name="episode guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode009.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Cloak of Darkness}}</ref> His armor was a modified version of [[clone trooper armor]] with over-sized,<ref name="COD commentary">{{SW|url=video/cloak-of-darkness-episode-featurette|text="Cloak of Darkness" Episode Featurette}}</ref> blaster-proof<ref name="COD decoded">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness|d=y}}</ref> shoulder pads. Argyus wore the traditional helmet of the Senate Guard,<ref name="COD commentary" /> which boasted a crest.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />
