'''Asha Fahn''',<ref name="Beware" /> also known as the "'''Red Ghost''',"<ref name="Ewoks 1">[[Ewoks (2024) 1|''Ewoks'' (2024) 1]]</ref> was a [[Gender|female]] [[Ewok]] of the [[Bright Tree tribe]] on [[Endor]]. She was one of [[Chief]] [[Chirpa]]'s daughters<ref name="Ewoks 2" /> and along with [[Teebo]], one of the tribe's best [[Hunting|hunters]]. When Chirpa decided to aid the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] in their fight against the [[Galactic Empire]], Asha and Teebo gave him their support, which allowed him to completely overrule the veto of [[Logray]], the tribe's [[shaman]]. The next morning, Asha, Chirpa and Teebo led the Ewoks who were able to join the fight into the forest, while the young and the elderly stayed at the village. When the Ewoks assaulted the Empire's bunker, Asha led the fiercest warriors of the tribe. She and her men leapt from atop the bunker, and the huntress killed an Imperial officer with her knife, leaving him no time to shout a command to his troopers. After the [[Battle of Endor]] was won both on ground and in [[Realspace|space]], Asha was part of the revelries that took place at Bright Tree Village.<ref name="Beware" />

==Behind the scenes==
Asha Fahn was introduced back to [[canon]] through the novel ''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]'', which was an adaptation of the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] written by [[Tom Angleberger]]<ref name="Beware" /> and published on [[September 22]], [[2015]].<ref name="Disney">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/beware-the-power-of-the-dark-side|text=Beware the Power of the Dark Side!}}</ref> The Ewok was later depicted for the first time in the [[Ewoks (2024) 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Ewoks (2024)|''Star Wars: Ewoks'']],<ref name="Ewoks 1" /> which was written by [[Steve Orlando]], illustrated by [[Laura Braga]] and [[Álvaro López]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 9]], [[2024]].<ref name="Marvel 1">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/112935/star_wars_ewoks_2024_1|text=Star Wars: Ewoks (2024) #1}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity, The Ewok huntress was only known as Asha and first appeared in the [[Asha (episode)|thirteenth episode]] of the [[Ewoks: Season One|first season]] of the [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']] animated TV series, which was directed by [[Raymond Jafelice]], written by [[Paul Dini]],<ref name="Asha">{{Ewoks|Asha (episode)}}</ref> and aired on [[November 30]], [[1985]].<ref name="This Week Gideon">{{ThisWeek|xCDs0meB6OY|Moff Gideon Enters the Battlefield, The Child Gets a Name, and More!|sw_url=video/moff-gideon-enters-the-battlefield-the-child-gets-a-name-and-more}}</ref> Being the episode's writer, Dini created Asha specifically for the animated series.<ref name="A Star Wars CELibration">{{InsiderCite|27|A Star Wars CELibration|A ''Star Wars'' CELibration}}</ref>
