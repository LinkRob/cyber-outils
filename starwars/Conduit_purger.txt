'''Conduit purgers''' were pest control devices designed to eradicate [[conduit worm]]s from the conduits the creatures infested. The device was [[Color|orange]] in color and consisted of a body with two nozzles.<ref name="VE" />

==Behind the scenes==
The conduit purger was mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="VE" /> The device originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2002]] ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]]<ref name="AOTCVD">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and had some props designed for it by concept sculptor [[Robert E. Barnes]] and model make [[Don Bies]].<ref name="Archive">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/ref/f20061031/index.html|text=Building a ''Complete Visual Dictionary''}}</ref>
