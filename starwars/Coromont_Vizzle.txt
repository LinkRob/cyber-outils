'''Coromont Vizzle''' was an [[alien]] criminal and associate of [[Pirate Queen|pirate queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. A famed outlaw, Vizzle's name was echoed throughout [[the galaxy]] in [[Music|songs]], many of which he penned himself. The rotund pirate formed an uneasy relationship with [[Officer]] [[Rado]], a diminutive lawman who rode on Vizzle's back wherever he went; assigned to watch the criminal at all times and monitor for illegal activity, Rado never reported his findings for fear of being implicated himself. Around the [[Standard year|year]] [[382 BBY]], Vizzle stood by Kanata's side during a [[Turf war with the Dank Graks|turf war]] with the [[Dank Graks]], traveling with [[Maz Kanata's pirate crew|her crew]] to uncover their rivals' motives.

==Biography==
Coromont Vizzle was an [[alien]] [[pirate]] who came to prominence during the [[High Republic Era]], renowned both for his acts as well as for the number of times he had been arrested.<ref name="THRA 1" /> After one particular encounter with the law, the court ruled that Vizzle would henceforth be attended to at all times by [[Officer]] [[Rado]], a very small individual who would report on any crimes Vizzle might commit in the future. However, as simply being present for any such acts would incriminate Rado himself, the officer never filed his citations, opting instead to pester Vizzle any time he witnessed illegal actions. Rado kept up with him by residing in a compartment attached to the back of the much larger alien's [[armor]]. Vizzle paid the officer little mind, going about his business as normal and enjoying his life as a pirate. He made a point of promoting his reputation with [[Music|songs]] he wrote himself, ballads that told the tales of his escapades throughout [[the galaxy]].<ref name="THRA 2" />

Vizzle eventually came to work with [[Takodana]]'s [[Pirate Queen|pirate queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] and [[Maz Kanata's pirate crew|her crew]].<ref name="THRA 1" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic Adventures (2022) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2022) 1]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> he was present at [[Takodana Castle]] when a brawl broke out between the [[cyborg]] [[Alak]] and [[Inspector]] [[Raf Thatchburn]], a member of the [[Galactic Antipiracy Command]] and Alak's former lover. Vizzle and fellow pirate [[Thermoculus Krisintvolt Scissorpunch|Therm Scissorpunch]] rushed to aid Alak, but the fight was quickly ended by Kanata, who sent the inspector out. Later, Vizzle was present when Kanata learned that the rival [[Dank Graks]] were planning a [[Turf war with the Dank Graks|turf war]] to take Takodana and agreed to assist her in stopping them.<ref name="THRA 1" /> He embarked with the crew on Kanata's ''[[Venomed Scabbard]]'' and boarded the Graks' [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Grim Devourer]]'', to discover their motives. While exploring the empty ship, Vizzle informed the crew's newest member, [[Savina Besatrix Malagán|Sav Malagán]], of Alak's history with Tatchburn as well as his own with Officer Rado.<ref name="THRA 2" />

Their discussion was cut short when the ''Devourer'' was [[Skirmish above Takodana|attacked]] by the ''Scabbard'', having been commandeered by the Dank Graks. Kanata ordered Vizzle to take Malagán and man the ship's weapons, but he found himself unable to do so, as the ''Devourer'' utilized [[Sorcerers of Tund|Tund]] technology and was therefore only operable by [[Force-sensitive]] beings. Another blast from the Graks briefly rendered Vizzle and Rado unconscious, leaving them unable to witness Malagán, an undercover [[Padawan|Jedi Padawan]], take control of the ship's cannons. The battle came to a stalemate when Inspector Thatchburn arrived and threatened both parties with arrest. Kanata and her crew escaped, planning to catch back up with the Graks to take back their the ''Scabbard'' and rescue its [[pilot]], [[Abadoo]].<ref name="THRA 2" />
