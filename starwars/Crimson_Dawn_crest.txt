A '''Crimson Dawn crest''' was a small object that was granted to members and allies of the criminal [[syndicate]] [[Crimson Dawn]] by its leader, the [[human]] [[crime lord]] [[Qi'ra]]. The crest prominently featured the [[syndicate]]'s symbol.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] was gifted a crest after she gained an excellent reputation with the criminal organization.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A Crimson Dawn crest appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When equipped in the game as a minor charm, the crest increased the player's ability to avoid detection.<ref name="Outlaws" />
