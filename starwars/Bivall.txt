The '''Bivall''' were a [[Amphibian|semi-aquatic]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] of tall [[Humanoid|bipeds]] with lanky bodies, large stalked [[eye]]s, crested heads and long fingers that were [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Protobranch]] in the [[Core Worlds]]. [[Sionver Boll]] and [[Okalin]] were members of this species.

==Biology and appearance==
The Bivall were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] of tall, instantly recognizable [[Humanoid|bipeds]] with their lanky bodies, large [[eye]]s positioned on ocular stalks, crested heads and long fingers, and were native to [[Protobranch]], a [[planet]] located in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Alien Archive" /> Their scaly skin ranged in [[color]] from white and red to green or blue.<ref name="DB Bivall" /> The species' lives revolved around [[water]], but they did not need it to survive, and could settle far from it.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Bivall, like other aquatic beings, were immune to the [[disease]] [[malongo pox]].<ref name="Queen's Bloom">{{StoryCite|story=The Queen's Bloom|book=The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref>

==Society and culture==
The Bivall were renowned for their high intelligence, leading to their spread across the [[galaxy]] in various scientific positions.<ref name="Alien Archive" /> The Bivall [[homeworld]] of Protobranch was an [[agriworld]] and a major producer of [[bacta]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>

==History==
By the time of the [[High Republic Era]], the Bivall had integrated with the rest of the Galaxy.<ref name="Into the Dark">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref> During the late [[Republic Era]], a [[solar storm]] occurred on Protobranch, causing the floating cities of the planet to fall to the surface.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Bivall in the galaxy==
Because of their high levels of intelligence, Bivall could be found in numerous well-to-do positions, such as [[Doctor|physicians]], [[Politics|political]] [[advisor]]s, researchers, and [[scientist]]s.<ref name="DB Bivall" />

During the High Republic Era, [[Ry Harket]] was a Bivall [[Gender|female]] [[Dean]] of Student Life at [[Reena University]].<ref name="Queen's Bloom" /> [[Scover Byne]], another Bivall female, was the owner of the [[Byne Guild]], which subsequently dissolved after she was arrested.<ref name="Into the Dark" /> [[Quin Carree]] was a Bivall [[slicer]] who was a member of the [[Nihil]]<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref> before becoming a [[bounty hunter]] alongside her lover, the former [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]].<ref name="Tempest Breaker">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]''</ref>

[[Sionver Boll]] was a Bivall scientist who worked for the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. She was later tasked, by [[Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], to study the [[Zillo Beast]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="TZB" />

[[Okalin]] was a male Bivall who served the reigning [[king]] of [[Onderon]] as an advisor during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Front Runners">{{TCW|Front Runners}}</ref>
