'''Frell''' was a [[Tholothian]] who served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] as a member of the [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Pathfinders]]. Frell and a number of other Pathfinders were stationed aboard a [[CR90 corvette]] of the [[Fourth Division]] after the [[Battle of Hoth]].<ref name="Star Wars 1" />
