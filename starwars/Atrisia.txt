'''Atrisia''', also known as '''Kitel Phard''', was a [[Core Worlds]] celestial body featuring [[Plant|brush]] on its surface. Atrisian mythology included the [[Deity|demigod]] [[Wapoe]], and at some point, the celestial body was ruled over by the [[Atrisian Emperor]]s. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Atrisia was the site of the [[Royal Academy of Arts]], in which the [[Noble|aristocrat]] [[Resinu Santhe-Caltra]] was at one point enrolled. The [[human]] [[Calum Gram]] worked as a search and rescue [[pilot]] for the [[Wilderness Preserve service]] on Atrisia.

==Description==
Atrisia,<ref name="R1UVG" /> also known as Kitel Phard,<ref name="TLJVD" /><ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-09-15.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-09-15}}</ref> was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="R1UVG" /> located in the [[Kitel Phard system]], a part of the [[Atrisian Commonwealth]] [[sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It lay in grid square J-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[hyperspace route]] known as the [[Giju Run]] linked it to the [[Deymasoll system]].<ref name="GijuRun" /> Atrisia's surface featured dense [[Plant|brush]].<ref name="R1UVG" /> Atrisia shared its names with the [[Atrisian Basic]] [[language]]<ref name="Propaganda" /> as well as the [[Kitel Phard dynasty]], which, during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], was associated with a style of expensive [[Kitel Phard vase|vases]].<ref name="Smuggler">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|An aristocrat married into the powerful Santhe family of Lianna, Santhe-Caltra studied at the Royal Academy of Arts on Atrisia, earning accolades for her work celebrating the Imperial armed forces.|''A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy''|<ref name="Propaganda" />}}
Long before the [[First Order-Resistance War|armed conflict]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> that began in<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Atrisia was [[Politics|governed]] by rulers known as [[Atrisian Emperor]]s.<ref name="TLJVD" /> The name of the [[Praetorian Guard]],<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> a personal [[bodyguard]] unit of mysterious origins<ref name="TLJVD" /> that was originally affiliated with the [[Imperial Remnants]]<ref name="SWE" /> and was active<ref name="Chapter 23">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 23: The Spies}}</ref> by [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoDate|23}}</ref> dated back to the reign of the [[14th Atrisian Emperor]] of Atrisia.<ref name="TLJVD" />

[[File:Calum_Gram.png|thumb|right|300px|The rebel Calum Gram originally worked as a pilot for the Wilderness Preserve service on Atrisia.]]
During the reign of the Galactic Empire,<ref name="Propaganda" /> which lasted from [[19 BBY]] to [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Atrisia was the site of the [[Royal Academy of Arts]]. The [[Noble|aristocrat]] [[Resinu Santhe-Caltra]] was enrolled at the academy at that [[time]], a fact which was eventually mentioned in the publication ''[[A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Propaganda" /> The ancestors of the [[human]] [[Vonreg family]], which was affiliated with the Empire, were rumored to have hailed from Atrisia.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>

The human [[Calum Gram]] worked as a search and rescue [[pilot]] for the [[Wilderness Preserve service]] on Atrisia at some point before joining the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]<ref name="R1UVG" /> and participating in the [[Battle of Scarif]]<ref name="R1">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> On Atrisia, Gram piloted an [[airspeeder]] through the dense vegetation and thus developed precision flying skills.<ref name="R1UVG" /> In 9 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="MandoVGDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> Atrisia fell within the borders of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Inhabitants==
The mythology of Atrisia's inhabitants included the [[Deity|demigod]] of disguise known as [[Wapoe]].<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> An old Atrisian maxim stated that upon the change of generations a party may switch from being someone's ally to being an enemy instead.<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Star_Wars_Propaganda_New_Cover.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Atrisia was first properly reintroduced in the current ''Star Wars'' canon in the book ''Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy''.]]
Kitel Phard's name was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in the [[January 29]], [[2016]] [[StarWars.com]] article "[[Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?|Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?]],"<ref name="WorldsTFA">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?}}</ref> and it was first established as a distinct celestial body in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''.<ref name="TLJVD" /> Meanwhile, Atrisia was first indirectly mentioned in the current canon in the<ref name="CL" /> [[September 27]], 2016<ref name="DK">{{DK|url=us/book/9781465452726-star-wars-complete-locations|text=Star Wars Complete Locations}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|re-issue]]<ref name="CL" /> of the [[2005]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="CL2005">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'', 2005 edition]]</ref> In the book,<ref name="CL" /> in a republished section from [[James Luceno]]'s [[2004]] ''Star Wars'' Legends reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars Trilogy]]'',<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars Trilogy]]''</ref> Atrisia was introduced via a mention of the Atrisian demigod Wapoe.<ref name="CL" /> The canon astronomical object was then first properly identified in ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', a book authored by Hidalgo<ref name="Propaganda" /> and published on [[October 25]], 2016.<ref name="Harper">{{Harper|url=9780062466822/star-wars-propaganda|text=Star Wars Propaganda}}</ref>

In Legends, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> [[Atrisia/Legends|Atrisia]] and the name "Kitel Phard" were both introduced in the ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'', a [[1989]] supplement written by [[Greg Gorden]] for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. However, the book did not definitively establish that "Kitel Phard" also referred to an astronomical object instead of just the [[Kitel Phard Dynasty]].<ref name="ISB">''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]''</ref> That was eventually done by [[Timothy S. O'Brien|Timothy O'Brien]] in his [[August]] [[1997]] article, "[[From the Files of Corellia Antilles]]," which was published in the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]''{{'s}} [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 14|fourteenth issue]].<ref name="Files">{{JournalCite|14|From the Files of Corellia Antilles}}</ref> Finally, the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]] established that "Kitel Phard" was an alternate name for Atrisia.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> No connection between the canon Atrisia and Kitel Phard was established until [[2025]], when Fry confirmed both names referred to the same celestial body.<ref name="Fry" />
