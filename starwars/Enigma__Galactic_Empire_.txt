The '''''Enigma''''' was a [[starship]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. After receiving prime marks at the [[Carida Academy|Imperial Naval Academy]] on<ref name="Final Order" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[Carida]], the [[Ilohian]] [[Tian Karmiya]] gave fine service aboard the ''Enigma''. Following this, the [[Imperial officer]] manned the starship ''[[Victory at Batonn]]'' before serving on the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Ultimatum]]'' with the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[commander]].<ref name="Final Order" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"[[The Final Order]]" takes place during the events of {{Film|V}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> [[Captain]] [[Canonhaus]], Karmiya's [[commanding officer]] aboard the [[Star Destroyer]], reflected on her previous service, including her tenure on the ''Enigma''.<ref name="Final Order" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Enigma'' was mentioned in the short story "[[The Final Order]]," which was written by [[Seth Dickinson]] as part of the [[2020]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="Final Order" />
