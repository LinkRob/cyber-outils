The '''Antilles Intercept''' was a flight maneuver used by [[New Republic pilot|pilots]] of the [[New Republic]] after the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> and was later taught while training [[Resistance pilot|pilots]] of the [[Resistance]] around thirty [[Standard year|years]] later. It was named in honor of the venerated [[Rebel pilot|pilot]]<ref name="TFAVD" /> [[Wedge Antilles]], who flew against both the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first]] and [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Stars]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and survived to serve its successor, the New Republic.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> Four years after the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]], pilots of the [[Temperance battle group|''Temperance'' battle group]] were warned to only perform this maneuver in open space, along with the [[Dragon Kite]] and most of all, the [[Enlightened Bantha]].<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Antilles Intercept was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and published in [[2015]].
