"'''50 Greatest Reasons to Love the ''Star Wars'' Prequels!'''" is an article published in [[Star Wars Insider 147|''Star Wars Insider'' 147]]. Several ''[[Star Wars]]'' actors, crew members, writers, and fans were asked to reveal what they loved most about the [[prequel trilogy]]. It was later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2015|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2015]].
