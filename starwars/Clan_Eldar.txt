{{Quote|A fine clan full of great warriors, willing to sacrifice their lives resisting the terrible Empire. I wear this armor with pride.|Aran Tal|<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>}}
'''Clan Eldar''' was a [[Mandalorian clan]] that was active during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Chronology Note" /> after a [[Mission to Mandalore|mission]] undertaken by [[Clan Wren]], [[Clan Kryze]], and members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] that took place during the [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|Mandalorian Civil War]], Clan Eldar pledged themselves to [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] along with other clans, thus becoming part of the [[Mandalorian resistance]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" /> The members of Clan Eldar were willing to put their lives on the line in the name of fighting against the Empire.<ref name="Hunters" />

Clan Eldar consisted of Mandalorian warriors that donned [[Mandalorian armor]] with [[Color|black]], dark green, and orange color combinations.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" /> Their [[signet]] depicted the face of a leonid creature<ref name="The Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> known as a [[lion]].<ref name="Hunters" /> During the [[New Republic Era]], some members of Clan Eldar were part of the [[Children of the Watch]], and their signet was visible on a flag when [[Ragnar Vizsla]] swore to the [[Mandalorian Creed]].<ref name="Chapter 17" /> [[Aran Tal]] wore a suit of Clan Eldar [[armor]] while working as a [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|hunter]] in the [[Grand Arena]], donning that gear with pride after they had resisted the Empire.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clan Eldar first appeared in the premiere of [[Star Wars Rebels Season Four|the fourth season of ''Star Wars Rebels'']], titled ''[[Heroes of Mandalore|Star Wars Rebels: Heroes of Mandalore]]'', which aired in [[2017]].<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />
