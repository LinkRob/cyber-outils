'''Faksir''' was an individual who served as a [[She'ar]] for the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> while onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', the [[human]] [[senator]] [[Ghirra Starros]] assumed the name for the individual was "Faskir," but was unsure. The She'ar was later [[Death|killed]] by the [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]], who poisoned his drink.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
