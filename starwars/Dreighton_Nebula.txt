{{Quote|In the last weeks alone, the Defense Coalition has engaged on Carlac, Magaveene, and in the Dreighton Nebula.|Jedi Master Avar Kriss|<ref name="Tempest Runner" />}}
The '''Dreighton Nebula''' was a [[nebula]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> and [[New Territories]],<ref name="New Territories" /> situated in the [[Dantus sector]] in square J-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'' establishes that the [[Engagement in the Dreighton Nebula]] took place between the [[Valo Republic Fair]] and [[Battle of Galov]], both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[231 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[Engagement in the Dreighton Nebula|engaged]] the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] in the Dreighton Nebula as part of [[Operation: Counterstrike]], a [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] campaign to defeat the group following the [[Valo Atrocity]]. Later, during a briefing of the Republic Defense Coalition on the Republic [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] shortly prior to [[Battle of Galov]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] used their engagement with the Nihil on Magaveene as an example of the continued success of Operation: Counterstrike.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] continuity, the Dreighton Nebula was first mentioned in<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> the [[2021]]<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> audio drama ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'',<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> which was published as part of [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia initiative,<ref name="Reader's Guide" /> with its canon spelling being confirmed by the release of the drama's [[The High Republic: Tempest Runner (script)|script book]] in [[2022]].<ref name="TR script">[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner (script)|''The High Republic: Tempest Runner'' script]]</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Dreighton Nebula/Legends|Dreighton Nebula]] first appeared in the video game ''[[Star Wars: Rebel Assault II: The Hidden Empire]]'', released in [[1995]].<ref name="History">{{LucasArts|url=20th/history_3.htm|text=20th Anniversary History, Part Three, Broadening Horizons, 1995-1998}}</ref>
