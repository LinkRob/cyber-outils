{{Quote|I had to get a ZX1‐capacitor from the supply depot. We have to update the D‐93 sonic tri‐denax cables.|Kazuda Xiono, to two stormtroopers while on the ''Titan''|audio=ZX1D93-StationToStation.flac|<ref name="STS" />}}
A '''D-93 sonic tri-denax cable''' was a type of cable. While pretending to be a [[First Order technician]] aboard the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Titan]]'' during a [[Infiltration of the Titan|mission]] to [[Thief|steal]] a [[trans-binary deflector]]<ref name="STS" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|2|11}}</ref> the [[Resistance]] member [[Kazuda Xiono]] confidently indicated to [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper 3 (Titan)|two]] [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper 4 (Titan)|stormtroopers]] that he had acquired a [[ZX1-capacitor]] from the [[Supertanker fuel depot|station's]] [[supply depot]] to update some D-93 sonic tri-denax cables. The [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] pair then expressed their disdain for [[technician]]s, successfully convinced that Xiono and his colleague, [[Neeku Vozo]], were working for the [[First Order]].<ref name="STS" />

==Behind the scenes==
D-93 sonic tri-denax cables were mentioned in "[[Station to Station]],"<ref name="STS" /> the eleventh episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[December 15]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney release">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref>
