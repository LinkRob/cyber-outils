'''Florrum''' was a [[sulfur]]ous [[desert]] [[planet]] located in the [[Florrum system]], within the [[Sertar sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], it was the location of a [[pirate]] [[Ohnaka Gang|gang]] led by [[Hondo Ohnaka]]. Florrum was briefly held by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] before the forces of [[General]] [[Grievous]] were repelled by Ohnaka's forces and the [[Jedi]].

==Description==
{{Quote|If you need transport, the nearest planet is Florrum. It's six parsecs away.''"<br />"''Is it civilized?''"<br />"''That depends on your definition of civilized.|Hondo Ohnaka offers a stranded Count Dooku passage off Vanqor|<ref name="Dooku Captured" />}}
Florrum was a [[Temperature|hot]] and dry<ref name="VE" /> [[desert]] [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Dooku Captured" /> in grid square R-5 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Coordinates" /> In addition to the barren rocky deserts and dustbowls<ref name="VE" /> that covered the planet, Florrum also possessed geological features such as canyons and [[sulfur]]ic geysers,<ref name="The Gungan General" /> the later of which could be found in [[acid]] flats.<ref name="VE" /> It was located six [[parsec]]s away from the planet [[Vanqor]].<ref name="Dooku Captured" />

==History==
{{Quote|First, you lose this system, and ''Grievous'' comes in and destroys my entire stronghold, leaving me here to just ''rummage'' through the leftovers of my once-great empire.|Hondo Ohnaka, to Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Revival" />}}
[[File:HondoOutpost-BFR.png|thumb|270px|left|Florrum was home to Hondo Onhaka's pirate gang for a time during the Clone Wars.]]
A [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Ohnaka Gang|gang]] led by [[Hondo Ohnaka]] was [[Hondo's complex|headquartered]] on Florrum. After their capture of [[Count]] [[Dooku]] on [[Vanqor]], they returned to the planet with the [[Sith Lord]]. Later it was there that Ohnaka captured Jedi Knights [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] after they came to check that Dooku was there to pay the ransom for him. They escaped soon after, with the help of [[Jar Jar Binks|Representative Binks]], [[Stone (clone trooper)|Commander Stone]] and several clone troopers. The planet was left in peace by the Republic,<ref name="Dooku Captured" /> but, later in the war, Dooku sent Separatist forces led by [[General]] [[Grievous]] to [[Invasion of Florrum|destroy]] Hondo's complex.<ref name="ANB">{{TCW|A Necessary Bond}}</ref>
