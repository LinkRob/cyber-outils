A [[Gender|male]] '''human''' was born on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] but moved to the planet [[Coruscant]], where he had two children with a [[Anandra Milon's mother|another]] [[human]] [[Alderaanian]]. The family lived in an [[Milon family apartment|apartment]] on [[Level 3204]] of Coruscant, a level inhabited by many other Alderaanian immigrants,<ref name="OTLD" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> The man was away from Coruscant on a trade mission when his [[homeworld]] was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the [[Alderaan vigil rioting|riots]] on Level 3204 following the destruction of Alderaan, the man's partner assured their children that their [[Parent|father]] was safe and would be home soon, but neither child believed her. His eldest child, [[Anandra Milon]], was reminded of her father when her younger brother [[Santigo Milon]] showed bitter determination while fleeing Imperial persecution.<ref name="OTLD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anandra Milon's father was first mentioned, but not named, in "[[One Thousand Levels Down]]," a short story written by [[Alexander Freed]] and published in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' [[Star Wars Insider 151|151]].
