At least two individuals were the '''parents''' of the [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Elzar Mann]]. Because their son was Force-sensitive, they sent him to be [[Jedi training|trained]] as a [[Jedi]] at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> By [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|TOTF}}</ref> Mann had risen to the rank of [[Jedi Master]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> after fellow Jedi Master [[Avar Kriss]] told Mann that she loved him, he sought the advice of Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] of whether it was possible for Jedi to love an individual while being free of attachment. In response, Yoda asked Mann if he thought his [[parent]]s loved him less because they had sent him to the [[Jedi temple|Temple]], to which Mann stated that they did not. When the Grand Master pressed for why, Mann noted it had been his [[destiny]] to be a Jedi due to his Force-sensitivity and that they had wanted what was best for him. After Yoda questioned even if it meant he would not be in their lives and he would grow up without remembering their names, Mann stated his parents had loved him, and that they had let him pass through their lives through their love for him. He then noted that even if they had wanted to hold onto him, they had not.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
