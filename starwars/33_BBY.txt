The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''33 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3244 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''14 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7944''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Ahsoka Tano]] is accepted into the [[Jedi Order]] after [[Plo Koon]] discovers her on [[Shili]].<ref name="Ahsoka date" />
*[[Jedi Master]] [[Sifo-Dyas]] commissions the [[Kaminoan]]s to create a [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone army]] for the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Insider 206 ACPOV">{{InsiderCite|206|A Certain Point of View}}</ref>
*[[Lott Dod]] replaces [[Nute Gunray]] as the [[senator]] of the [[Trade Federation]].<ref name="The Separatists">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Separatists}}</ref>
*[[Mon Mothma]] had her first [[watervine peach]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="SW 46">"[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]]" establishes [[Mon Mothma]] was sixteen when she was elected [[senator]]. [[Star Wars (2020) 46|''Star Wars'' (2020) 46]] states Mothma had her first [[watervine peach]] at fifteen, placing it a [[Standard year|year]] beforehand. ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'' establishes that Mothma was elected senator four years prior to the events of the novel, which is set in [[28 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, Mothma had her first watervine [[peach]] five years before 28 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], or 33 BBY.</ref>
*[[Prop 31-814D]] is passed by the [[Galactic Senate]].<ref name="Insider 200">{{InsiderCite|200|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> {{Circa}}<ref name="Prop Date" />
**In response to these new taxations, the [[Trade Federation]] threatens to begin [[blockade]]s.<ref name="Jango Fett 4" /> {{Circa}}<ref name="33 BBY">{{JangoFettSeriesDate}}</ref>
*An uneasy peace<ref name="Jango Fett 4" /> is established during the [[Anselmi–Nautolan war]], which is set to come to a total end with a [[Anselmi–Nautolan treaty]].<ref name="Jango Fett 1" /> However, as per the machinations of [[Count]] [[Dooku]], efforts to put a stop to the war fail, creating another major blow to [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]].<ref name="Jango Fett 4" /> {{Circa}}<ref name="33 BBY" />

==Births==
*[[Cassian Jeron Andor]]<ref name="Andor birthyear">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' establishes that Cassian Jeron Andor was twenty-seven [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which the book dates the events of to [[5 BBY]].{{Databank|cassian-andor|Cassian Andor}} establishes that Andor was nine years old when he left [[Kenari]], with {{SW|url=series/andor/andor-season-1-episode-3-episode-guide|text="Reckoning" Episode Guide &#124; Andor}} establishing that this occurred before the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] had formed. ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'' establishes that the [[Raxus Address]] and the following informal forming of the CIS occurred two [[Standard year|years]] before the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' and other sources state to take place in [[22 BBY]]. For all this information to be true, Cassian Andor had to have been born in 33 BBY, with his departure taking place after his birthday, but before the Raxus Address in [[24 BBY]], and the events of ''Andor'' taking place before his birthday in [[5 BBY]].</ref>
