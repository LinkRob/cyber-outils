The '''Dandelo system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Tammuz sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]]<ref name="Slice" /> that fell within grid square T-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Dandelo system was mentioned as just "Dandelo" on a map of [[the galaxy]]<ref name="CLNE">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> designed by [[Christopher Ibbitt]]<ref name="Fry email">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}</ref> and written by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="TTS">{{WebCite|url=https://tarkinstopshelf.libsyn.com/381-talking-star-wars-complete-locations-with-emily-shkoukani-and-jason-fry|text=381: Talking Star Wars: Complete Locations with Emily Shkoukani and Jason Fry!|date=2025-05-09|work=[https://tarkinstopshelf.libsyn.com Tarkin's Top Shelf]|archivedate=20250511074859}}</ref> in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]'',<ref name="CLNE" /> which was released on [[March 6]], [[2025]].<ref name="Amazon UK">{{DK|url=uk/book/9780241731673-star-wars-complete-locations-new-edition|text=Star Wars Complete Locations New Edition}}</ref> It was then identified as the Dandelo system in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were also written by Fry.<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The [[Dandelo system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in "[[Coby and the Starhunters]],"<ref name="CatS">{{DroidsCite|Coby and the Starhunters}}</ref> the ninth episode of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO]]'', which aired on [[November 2]], [[1985]].<ref name="Insider27">{{InsiderCite|27|A Star Wars CELibration|A ''Star Wars'' CELibration}}</ref> The [[Star system/Legends|system]] was later identified in the StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Fry.<ref name="Essential Atlas Online Companion">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
