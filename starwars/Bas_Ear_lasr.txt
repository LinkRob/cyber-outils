'''Bas Ear'lasr''' was a [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] who served on the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', the [[flagship]] of [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. They [[Diet|fed]] a three-[[Limb|legged]] [[Bas Ear'lasr's tooka|tooka]] within a [[hangar]] on the ''Gaze'' which eventually came to Ro's attention.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|TOTF}}</ref> when the Eye was at a [[Norisyn crystal farm|crystal farm]] on the [[Norisyn's fifth moon|fifth moon]] of<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Norisyn system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Norisyn]] investigating a [[Blight|plague]], he asked his [[secretary]], [[Thaya Ferr]], to send a Nihil to bring him the [[tooka]] among other items. In response, Ferr had a [[Unidentified human Nihil (tooka)|human Nihil]] take the [[cat]] in a bag to Ro. The Eye then threw the tooka into the [[crystal]]s that had been infected by the plague, and it eventually [[Death|died]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
