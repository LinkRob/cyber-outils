'''Blythe''' was an [[Abyssin]] [[Gender|male]] who was the [[Parent|father]] of the [[human]] [[Forvan]]. The two were members of the [[Colony of Kezarat|Colony of ''Kezarat'']] in [[No-Space]], which Blythe was the [[captain]] of.<ref name="Star Wars 31" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars (2020) 30|''Star Wars'' (2020) 30]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> Blythe was present on a [[Unidentified starship (Colony of Kezarat)|starship]] that rescued<ref name="Star Wars 30" /> an elite [[squad]]<ref name="216 Launchpad">{{InsiderCite|216|Launchpad}}</ref> from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], which had encountered<ref name="Star Wars 30" /> [[Killdroid]]s whilst in No-Space.<ref name="Star Wars 31" /> After the rebels entered the [[Starship|ship]], Blythe greeted the "news" over a speaker and told them they would need their weapons. When Captain [[Amilyn Holdo]] replied that they could not give them away, Blythe understood it as their decision, but then stated it would be their decision to vent the [[Airlock|air lock]] the rebels were in if did not to throw their weapons out of the ship's [[Magnetic field|magnetic shield]]. The rebels then complied with the request, and Holdo informed Blythe they were now defenseless, before asking what would happen next.<ref name="Star Wars 30" />

Blythe welcomed the rebels to the Colony of ''Kezarat'', and they discovered they were headed towards the ''[[Kezarat]]'' [[tanker]]. [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] thanked Blythe saving their lives and stated the rebels would give whatever they could in return, but stated they needed to understand where they were and why they could not leave as they needed to return home. Blythe responded that they were in No-Space where normal rules didn't apply, and then informed them that people sometimes made their way to No-Space and started off as "news" and then eventually became "olds," adding that no one had found a way out in over two [[Standard century|centuries]]. Blythe then finished by telling the rebels that they instead of going home, they should get used to one idea, before opening a door and revealing themselves and telling the rebels No-Space was now their home.<ref name="Star Wars 30" /> He shared his name with the [[Blythe family]].<ref name="LotJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>
