{{Quote|We stand together and our faith in the Light is absolute. The Great Tomb will be completed as planned.|Quote attributed to Elder A'shora in the holobook "The Final Rest"|<ref name="Outlaws" />}} 
'''A'shora''' was a [[Khepi]] [[Elder (title)|Elder]] who was a part of an [[envoy]] to the [[Galactic Republic]] when the news of the impending [[Destruction of Khepi|destruction]] of their [[Khepi (planet)|homeworld]] reached them on<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]]. A quote attributed to Elder A'shora proclaiming the Khepis' absolute dedication to their faith in [[the Light]] as a response to the news was included in the [[holobook]] "[[The Final Rest]]" by [[Lento Ye-Gimu]], which was published in [[59 BBY|7918]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate|APF=1}}</ref> the [[Rokana Raiders]] [[pirate]] gang had at least one copy of "The Final Rest" at their base in [[Khepi Tomb]] while their leader [[Stinger Tash]] searched for a way to open the tomb's burial chamber.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
While in Khepi Tomb<ref name="Outlaws" /> around 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] found a [[datapad]] with page seventy-five of the book which included A'shora's quote.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}
