'''Chibias''' was an astronomical object<ref name="MoF" /> in the [[Chibias system]] in the [[Sombure sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]], in grid square K-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> that was represented by [[Senator]] [[Tontra Doroon]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and, after the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|reformation]] into the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Empire's [[Imperial Senate]].<ref name="MoF" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Chibias was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', which was written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first instalement of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" /> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in "[[Handoff]]," a short story written by [[Timothy Zahn]] and published in [[Star Wars Gamer 10|''Star Wars Gamer'' 10]]<ref name="Gamer10">{{GamerCite|10|Handoff}}</ref> on [[May 7]], [[2002]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SWArchive|url=gaming/other/rpg/news20020416.html|text=Episode II in Gamer #10}}</ref>
