'''Brain crawlers''' were a type of [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[worm]] whose [[homeworld]] was [[Cheelit]]<ref name="RSG" /> in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Cheelit system]]</ref> They could infest the [[brain]]s of members of the domesticated [[happabore]] species, which could be found on [[planet]]s throughout the [[Western Reaches]]. The instruction manual for the slurry formulation of [[Galacti-Stok]]'s [[Happy-Bore Medicated Dewormer]]&mdash;a product containing [[Trestibinexocycline 5-Gamma]], [[thlank oil]], [[greel-root acid]], and forage products&mdash;recommended that the [[dewormer]] not be used to treat brain crawler infestations. The [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] found a copy of the instructions while living on the [[desert]] planet [[Jakku]] and included them in a [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]] that<ref name="RSG" /> she<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> had compiled<ref name="RSG" /> by [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' includes an addendum at the end describing some of the events of {{Film|VII}}, written several [[Standard day|days]] after they take place. The addendum is the final entry in the guide and is included chronologically after the entry mentioning brain crawlers. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the film to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Brain crawlers were mentioned in ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'', a [[2015]] replica journal written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="RSG" />
