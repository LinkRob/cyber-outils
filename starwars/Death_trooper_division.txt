The '''death trooper division''' was a [[division]] of the [[Imperial Army]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] that consisted of the elite [[death trooper]]<ref name="Imperial Troops" /> [[commando]]s.<ref name="HC 80">{{HelmetCollectionCite|80|Helmets}}</ref> The division was originally created to safeguard Imperial operations.<ref name="Imperial Troops" /> The death troopers in the division were founded by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]].
