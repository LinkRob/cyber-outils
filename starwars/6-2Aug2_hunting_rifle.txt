The '''6-2Aug2 hunting rifle''' was a model of [[slugthrower rifle]]<ref name="HC 66" /> present in multiple homesteads on backwater [[Planet|worlds]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] such as [[Saleucami]]<ref name="The Deserter">{{TCW|The Deserter}}</ref> and [[Tatooine]].<ref name="ANH" />

==Design==
This model of [[slugthrower rifle]] utilized a more old-fashioned design that was visually similar to other [[slugthrower]]s such as the [[Tusken]] [[cycler rifle]],<ref name="ANH" /> with an elongated [[barrel]] that allowed for precise shots at long range. Like similar [[rifle]]s, this model was reloaded manually.<ref name="Cut and Run" />

==History==
[[File:HunterandSuu.png|thumb|left|220px|Suu Lawquane used a slugthrower rifle]]
[[Suu Lawquane]] owned a 6-2Aug2 hunting rifle on the planet [[Saleucami]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="The Deserter" /> and shortly afterward.<ref name="Cut and Run" /> [[Gender|She]] used the rifle on multiple occasions, such as a precautionary measure when a group of [[Galactic Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s that included an injured [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] sought out shelter at her [[Cut Lawquane|husband's]] [[Farm]]. Her husband Cut Lawquane also used the slugthrower days afterward to fight off [[Battle droid|droids]] in his home with the help of Captain Rex.<ref name="The Deserter" /> She again used the rifle years later to fend off a [[nexu]] that was attacking a young girl named [[Omega]] soon after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise of]] the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Cut and Run" />

While [[Savage Opress]] was attack by local [[Junker]]s, one of them used a 6-2Aug2 to snipe the unaware [[Zabrak]] from behind but [[Morley]] attacked the Junker by slapping his tail into his hip, which made the Junker miss his shot.<ref name="Brothers" />

[[Luke Skywalker]] also owned this model during his youth on [[Tatooine]]. He took the rifle with him when he went to search for runaway [[droid]] [[R2-D2]] in his [[X-34 landspeeder]]. The rifle was destroyed when Luke was subsequently ambushed in the [[Jundland Wastes]] by the [[Tusken Raider]] [[A'Koba]] prior to Skywalker's rescue by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="ANH" />
