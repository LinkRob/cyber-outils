The '''Crombach Nebula''' was located in the [[Belsmuth sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was situated in grid square O-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> fought between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="GalacticAtlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> the [[nebula]] served as the site of an [[Battle of Crombach Nebula|engagement]] between [[Galactic Republic]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Wom-Nii Gnaden]], who was [[pilot]]ing an [[NTB-630 naval bomber]], and a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Tide of Progress XII|''Munificent''-class star frigate]]. During the battle at the Crombach Nebula, Master Gnaden was [[Death|killed]] following his [[bomber]]'s collision with the [[Munificent-class star frigate|frigate]], leaving a mark on the [[warship]]'s starboard [[hull]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Crombach Nebula was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']].<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Crombach Nebula/Legends|Crombach Nebula]] was introduced in ''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', a [[2005]] reference book authored by [[Curtis Saxton]].<ref name="Incredible Cross-Sections">''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> It was later first depicted in ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', a [[2009]] reference book authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
