'''Derek Connolly''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] screenwriter and film producer credited as co-writing the story for [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="WGA">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/who-wrote-star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker|text=Exclusive: 'Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker' Final Writing Credits Revealed|author=Lawrence, Gregory|date=2019-11-15|work=[[Wikipedia:Collider|Collider]]|archivedate=20200109120439}}</ref> having written the original script with [[Colin Trevorrow]] entitled [[Star Wars: Episode IX Duel of the Fates|''Duel of the Fates'']].<ref name="Trevorrow Writing">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/star-wars-9-rey-parents-colin-trevorrow|text='Star Wars: Episode IX' Director Colin Trevorrow Promises "Satisfying" Answer to Rey Theories|author=Chitwood, Adam|date=2016-01-12|archivedate=20200807083816}}</ref><ref name="Thorne writing">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/heat-vision/star-wars-episode-ix-gets-a-new-writer-1026003|text='Star Wars: Episode IX' Gets a New Writer (Exclusive)|author=Kit, Borys|date=2017-08-01|work=The Hollywood Reporter|archivedate=20200216113051}}</ref><ref>{{WebCite|url=https://makingstarwars.net/2020/01/i-read-colin-trevorrow-and-derek-connollys-final-star-wars-episode-ix-script|text=I read Colin Trevorrow and Derek Connolly's final Star Wars: Episode IX script!|author=Ward, Jason|date=2020-01-15|archivedate=20200312035536}}</ref>

Connolly and Trevorrow also worked on the [[2012]] film [[Wikipedia:Safety Not Guaranteed|''Safety Not Guaranteed'']], the [[2015]] film [[Wikipedia:Jurassic World|''Jurassic World'']] and its [[2018]] sequel [[Wikipedia:Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom|''Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom'']]. Connolly has also written other films, such as the 2018 film [[Wikipedia:Pacific Rim: Uprising|''Pacific Rim: Uprising'']] (starring [[John Boyega]]) and the [[2019]] film [[Wikipedia:Pokémon: Detective Pikachu|''Pokémon Detective Pikachu'']].
