'''B-R72'''<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|Droid Depot|store}}</ref> was a [[heavy loader droid]]<ref name="GEDD" /> manufactured by [[JLD Mechanicals Consortium]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> with [[Color|yellow]] and red plating. The [[droid]] belonged to the [[Droid Depot]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. During the [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of Batuu by the [[First Order]]<ref name="GEDD" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> B-R72 was stood on display outside the depot, placed on a plinth beside several other droids behind a railing.<ref name="GEDD" /> The [[Labor droid|loader droid]] stood 1.75 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Stats" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Hoo-onk&hellip;grumble&hellip;Translation:Worker Droid good to fix machines. Fix machines give rewards. Make Droid happy.|One of B-R72's two possible dialogues in ''Droid Tycoon''|<ref name="Fortnite">''[[Fortnite]]'' &mdash; ''[[Star Wars: Droid Tycoon]]''</ref>}}
B-R72 first appeared as a prop within the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GEDD" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> B-R72 appears in the non-[[canon]] ''[[Star Wars: Droid Tycoon]]'' game mode added to the [[Epic Games]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Fortnite]]<ref name="Fortnite">''[[Fortnite]]'' &mdash; ''[[Star Wars: Droid Tycoon]]''</ref> on [[May 1]], [[2026]].<ref name="FortniteDate">{{SW|url=news/fortnite-star-wars-may|text=Step Inside the ''Star Wars'' Galaxy in Fortnite This May}}</ref>

The heavy loader droid can be found beside the location where players can upgrade their droids and has been upgraded to "diamond" rarity, swapping the yellow on B-R72's plating for an iridescent blue. The default color scheme for heavy loader droids in the game matches B-R72's normal coloration, although they are titled "BU-4D,"<ref name="Fortnite" /> similar to the heavy load lifter droid [[B-U4D]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> When spoken to, B-R72 can respond with one of two pieces of translated dialogue, one advising the player to complete daily missions and the other advising them to use [[Labor droid|worker droids]] to repair broken machinery.<ref name="Fortnite" />
