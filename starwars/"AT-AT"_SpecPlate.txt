The '''"AT-AT" SpecPlate''' is a fine-art print produced by [[Acme Archives Direct]] as part of the [[SpecPlate]] series with art by [[Jeff McGuigan]] and a design by [[Louis Solis]], released on [[August 20]], [[2014]]. It depicts the technical schematics of the [[All Terrain Armored Transport/Legends|All Terrain Armored Transport]] [[Walker/Legends|walker]]. Twenty-five SpecPlates with the AT-AT print were released.

==Contents==
{{Quote|Featuring art by Jeff McGuigan, the SpecPlate includes technical specs, schematics, and general information on AT-ATs, all presented in a visually stunning design.|An excerpt from StarWars.com|<ref name="Exclusive Preview" />}}
The "AT-AT" SpecPlate depicts the technical schematics of the [[All Terrain Armored Transport/Legends|All Terrain Armored Transport]] [[Walker/Legends|walker]]. It also features information regarding its [[in-universe]] designers and [[Engineer/Legends|engineers]] as well as the corporation and faction involved in its development&mdash;specifically, [[Kuat Drive Yards/Legends|Kuat Drive Yards]] and the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]], respectively. The image's dimensions were thirty-six by seventeen inches, and forty-three by twenty-four inches when framed. Like all [[SpecPlate]]s, it came with a [[Wikipedia:Certificate of authenticity|certificate of authenticity]].<ref name="SpecPlateWebsite">{{Acme|url=products/at-at-star-wars-specplate|text=AT-AT SpecPlate}}</ref>

==Development==
The model was colored in gold aluminum that was designed by a group of architects in [[Wikipedia:Seattle|Seattle]], [[Wikipedia:Washington (state)|Washington]] that were affiliated with the [[Wikipedia:United States Navy|United States Navy]]. It was mounted in two-inch polished stainless steel standoffs on a supporting plate in a shadow box frame.<ref name="SpecPlateWebsite" /> The "AT-AT" SpecPlate is inspired by Commemorative Christening Plates affixed to the bulkhead of real-world ships. Art for the piece was created by [[Jeff McGuigan]], while the piece was designed by [[Louis Solis]]. The piece was released on [[August 20]], [[2014]]<ref name="Exclusive Preview" /> with a limited set of twenty-five SpecPlates&mdash;each one denoting to the owner which piece out of the 25 they received.<ref name="SpecPlateWebsite" /> A preview for the print was released on [[StarWars.com]] on the same day to announce its release.<ref name="Exclusive Preview" />

==Continuity==
The All Terrain Armored Transport&mdash;the vehicle depicted by the SpecPlate<ref name="Exclusive Preview" />&mdash;was created for<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of The Empire Strikes Back]]''</ref> the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film<ref name="EpV">{{Film|V}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="MakingOf" /> The names [[D. Beasley]], [[C. Cantwell]], [[S. Gawley]], [[J. Johnston]], [[R. McQuarrie]]&mdash;referring to various in-universe designers and engineers&mdash;on the SpecPlate are references to<ref name="Exclusive Preview" /> [[Lucasfilm]] staff members [[David Beasley]], [[Colin Cantwell]],<ref name="EpIV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[Steve Gawley]], [[Joe Johnston]], and [[Ralph McQuarrie]].<ref name="Exclusive Preview" /> Gawley,<ref name="EpV" /> Johnston, and McQuarrie were themselves involved in the creation of the AT-AT; McQuarrie created concept art depicting the vehicle, Johnston created designs for tank-like precursors of the AT-AT in early drafts of ''The Empire Strikes Back'',<ref name="ROTOR Trivia">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/relics-of-the-old-republic-trivia-gallery|text="Relics of the Old Republic" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> and Gawley was the foreman of the modelshop utilized during the film's production.<ref name="EpV" /> Some details on the print match the AT-AT's schematics in the [[1996]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels]]'', written by [[Bill Smith]].<ref name="EGVV">''[[The Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels]]''</ref>
