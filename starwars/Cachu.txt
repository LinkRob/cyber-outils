'''Cachu''' was a kind of [[fruit]] sold in [[the galaxy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. On the [[planet]] [[Lau]], a [[Unidentified street urchin (Lau)|street urchin]] sold the fruits in [[Unidentified town (Lau)|town]]. Cachu was a mottled red and yellow color with rows of blunt prickles arranged in a vertical fashion.<ref name="Different" />
