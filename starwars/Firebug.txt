'''Firebugs''' were a type of nesting [[insect]].<ref name="Survivor" /> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> [[Jedi]]-in-hiding [[Bode Akuna]] described [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] as having "kicked the firebug nest" after Kestis [[Death|slew]] [[Rayvis]], leader of the [[Bedlam Raiders]], during a [[Second mission to the Shattered Moon|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Koboh]]'s [[Shattered Moon]].<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], firebugs were mentioned during a cutscene in the [[2023]] [[Respawn Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Survivor" /> The [[Firebug/Legends|insects]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were mentioned in "[[Special Ops: Ship Jackers]]," a ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' article written by [[John Beyer]] and [[Kathy Burdette]] and published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 13|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 13]] in [[May]] [[1997]].<ref name="Journal13">{{JournalCite|13|Special Ops: Ship Jackers}}</ref>
