'''Fragmentation missiles''', also known as '''frag missiles''', were a type of [[missile]] designed for use against large formations of lightly armored [[Trooper|troops]]. Upon detonating, fragmentation missiles would spread shrapnel around the area of impact, causing injuries to anyone in the area. Types of fragmentation missiles included the [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.|Merr-Sonn]] [[C-88 Fragmentation Missile|C-88]] and the [[SoroSuub Corporation|SoroSuub]] [[Mk XVIII Anti-Personnel Missile]].<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
