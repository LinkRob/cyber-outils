'''Batiki trees''' were a type of [[tree]] that grew in the [[forest]]s of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Grava]] and had [[Plant|fronds]]. While walking toward the [[Gravan Monastery]]<ref name="LS" /> in [[7 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the section of ''[[Last Shot]]'' that batiki [[tree]]s appear in to [[7 ABY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] traveled down a few crumbling walking paths shrouded by the fronds of batiki [[Plant|trees]].<ref name="LS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Batiki trees appeared in ''[[Last Shot]]'', a [[2018]] novel written by [[Daniel José Older]].<ref name="LS" />
