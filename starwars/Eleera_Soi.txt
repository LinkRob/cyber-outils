'''Eleera Soi''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Twi'lek]] who worked for [[Crimson Dawn]]. She was one of the few survivors in a mission to retrieve a [[Jedi]] artifact. By [[3 ABY]], she served as a Crimson Dawn operative and contract on the planet [[Toshara]].

==Biography==
[[File:StarWars2020-18-YuGho.png|thumb|left|250px|Eleera Soi got to know future Dawn leader Qi'ra (pictured) during a mission to Thorum and ensuing training.]]
Eleera Soi was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="CC" /> born sometime around [[35 BBY]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Soi was originally recruited into the criminal organization [[Crimson Dawn]] after being noticed by the syndicate lieutenants while participating in rigged fights in the competitive fighting circuit. On the [[planet]] [[Thorum]], she joined [[Qi'ra]], [[Myem]] and [[Qorsha]] on a collection job in the city of [[Calda]]. She determined Qi'ra was a formidable warrior and offered to train her in the use of [[vibroblade]]s. Reaching the business, she intimidated the owner and was impressed when Qi'ra did the same. Despite getting the credits, she threatened to burn the business down. The group left the business and Soi revealed that she had been sent along by [[Cerveteri Slane]] to observe and see if Qi'ra would fail the mission. After collecting the money owed to Crimson Dawn, Soi and the rest of the group went to a shop that was delinquent in paying for three months in a row. She gave her [[flamethrower]] to Qi'ra, who used it to set fire to a tree outside the business.<ref name="CC" />

She provided Qi'ra with combat lessons in exchange for knowledge about the [[White Worms]] and how they managed to take over the underworld of the planet [[Corellia]]. After receiving a mission to sabotage and steal ships from a local criminal organization, Soi asked Qi'ra to join her in a trip to the shipyard for some reconnaissance. While there, she expressed discontent that Cerveteri hadn't provided them with all the details about the target. After leaving Thorum, she returned to the ''[[First Light]]'' and trained with the [[Hylobon]] guards while staying in the barracks on the lower levels of the yacht. Soi attended some of Vos's parties where she would work the crowd and gather information.<ref name="CC" />

===Contact on Toshara===
By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Soi served as a Crimson Dawn contact and operative on the planet [[Toshara]]. When the thief Kay Vess accepted a Crimson Dawn contract to steal information from [[Pyke Syndicate]] boss [[Gorak Palas]], Soi called Vess under a disguised voice, giving her directions on infiltrating Palas' base. After Vess successfully completed the contract, she returned to [[Makal's Gambling Parlor]], where she was greeted by the broker [[Danka Orsato]] and Soi. Soi congratulated Vess on the mission, before promising to hide her from the crime syndicate [[Zerek Besh]], which had placed a [[death mark]] on Vess, if she continued to work for the Dawn. She then gave Vess two [[fuel injector]]s, before stating that they would meet again and telling her that [[Bram Shano]], Vess's friend, was healing from his injuries.<ref name="Outlaws" />

After Vess brought the fuel injectors to her ship, the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', in the settlement [[Jaunta's Hope]], Soi approached her for another mission: infiltrate an [[Imperial dockyard]] in Toshara space to delete some data, before pinning the operation on the Pyke Syndicate. When Vess wondered how she could trust the Dawn, Soi replied that she couldn't, but that was the game she had to play. Despite her mechanic [[Waka]]'s reservations, Vess successfully completed the mission, choosing to either frame the Pykes as planned or betraying and framing the Dawn instead.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Personality and traits==
Soi was well-muscled and confident in her ability to defend herself. She looked down on almost everyone and was terse and solitary.<ref name="CC" /> Due to her close relationship with Qi'ra, she was afforded a large degree of trust to carry out her work as she saw fit. She was not interested in leading a syndicate or group, instead she preferred to stay in the shadows to ensure she had the opportunity to slip away if things became complicated. Soi had olive skin and brown eyes.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Equipment==
Soi wore brightly colored [[Body glove|bodysuits]] with knives hidden inside. She carried a small silvery flamethrower.<ref name="CC" /> While on [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Soi wore a red shirt, a red jacket, a Crimson Dawn belt, black fingerless gloves, gray pants and black boots.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eleera Soi was created for<ref name="EK">{{WookYouTube|YIsCBrOcUlU|Wookieepedia Interviews - E. K. Johnston}}</ref> the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. Prior to the game's release,<ref name="EK" /> she debuted in the [[2023]] young-adult novel ''[[Crimson Climb]]'', written by [[E. K. Johnston]].<ref name="CC" /> Later, ''Outlaws'' identified Eleera's last name as Soi and featured her first visual depiction.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Johnston noted that she added Soi to ''Crimson Climb'' at the request of those she was working with, as ''Outlaws'' had been under development at the time. When the author questioned whether the character needed to be doing anything specific in the novel, she was given the freedom to write what she wished, having only been given a vague description and early sketch of Soi to work with. Johnston was unaware which video game Soi would eventually feature in, having only been told that she was a game character.<ref name="EK" />
