'''Cheddatown''' a [[city]] located on the [[planet]] [[Orto]]. It was a bustling city with many businesses and suburban residences. The streets of Cheddatown were lined with streetlamps and were suitable for civilian speeders, double-decker [[landspeeder]] buses, and also pedestrians. The city hosted tourists in large streetcars. The city featured some steep hills, the steepest being where [[Broadway Street]] was located. Cheddatown also had a perpetually crowded [[Cheddatown spaceport|spaceport]].<ref name="FACPOV" />

The [[Chedda Corporation]] maintained a [[Chedda Corporation factory|factory]] in downtown Cheddatown, along one of the city's major thoroughfares. During the [[Imperial Era]], the city was patrolled by both the [[Cheddatown police]] as well as by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="FACPOV" />
