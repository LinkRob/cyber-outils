The '''Bounty Apprentice''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Rieve]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Sith Apprentice (Rieve)|standard costume]]. Its design was intended to resemble that of the infamous [[Theelin]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Latts Razzi]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bounty Apprentice was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Rieve, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 6.0.0 alongside Season 6 on [[April 15]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Bounty Saber Pattern]]. It was made immediately free-to-use at the start of Season 6, unlocked alongside the majority of in-game cosmetics.<ref name="Hunters" />
