'''Emir of Ryloth''' was a title<ref name="Bust Collection 42">{{BustCollectionCite|42|Star Wars Universe}}</ref> established by [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and granted to [[Wat Tambor]],<ref name="Tambor biography image 2" /> the lead [[engineer]] and [[foreman]] of the [[Techno Union]]<ref name="Wat Tambor" /> and a member of the [[Executive Separatist Council]].<ref name="Bust Collection 42" /> Tambor led the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] operation to [[Battle of Ryloth|invade and occupy]]<ref name="Wat Tambor SWE">{{SWE|characters|wattambor|Wat Tambor}}</ref> the [[planet]] of [[Ryloth]] and was empowered by Dooku<ref name="Tambor biography image 2" /> to supervise the occupation<ref name="Wat Tambor" /> as an [[emir]].<ref name="Tambor biography image 2" /> However, Tambor's occupation of Ryloth came to an end when he was captured by the combined might of the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Twi'lek Resistance]].<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" />

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Emir of Ryloth position first appeared through Wat Tambor in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Storm Over Ryloth]]," within which Tambor appeared via flashbacks and [[hologram]].<ref name="StormOver">{{TCW|Storm Over Ryloth}}</ref> He was later identified as an emir through the subsequent episode "[[Liberty on Ryloth]]," within which Tambor appeared in the flesh,<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> and Tambor's entry in the [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|StarWars.com Encyclopedia]] identified "emir of Ryloth" as a specific title.<ref name="Wat Tambor SWE" /> The capitalization of "Emir" in the title was confirmed through [[Star Wars Bust Collection 42|''Star Wars Bust Collection'' 42]].<ref name="Bust Collection 42" />
