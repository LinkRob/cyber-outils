The '''Attahox system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Hocatar sector]] of the [[galaxy]]'s [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was situated along the [[Nanth'ri Trade Route]],<ref name="Nanth'ri Route" /> between [[Daiyu]] and [[Batuv]].<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Attahox system was first created by [[Alan Moore]] for ''[[The Pandora Effect]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] published in [[The Empire Strikes Back Monthly 151|''The Empire Strikes Back Monthly'' 151]] magazine in [[1981]]. It was confirmed to be [[canon]] when shown on a galactic map included in the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] released by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for their roleplaying system in [[2016]].
