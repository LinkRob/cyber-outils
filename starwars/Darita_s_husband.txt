A male individual was the '''husband''' of [[Darita]], a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] and [[captain]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> Darita was [[Death|killed]] during a [[Battle of Troithe|battle]],<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> around [[5 ABY]].<ref name="SFDate">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="VPDate">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> Darita's [[Marriage|husband]] was stationed with Imperial loyalist forces on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. After the 204th also traveled to the world, the dead captain's sister, [[Lieutenant]] [[Darita (lieutenant)|Darita]], took a [[shuttle]] to Jakku's surface to inform her brother-in-law about the death of his wife.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
