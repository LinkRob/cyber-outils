The '''At Attin Mint''' was a structure under a [[Unidentified city (At Attin)|city]] on the [[planet]] [[At Attin]] that produced [[Credit ingot]]s, during the [[New Republic Era]]. One of the ships built by the Mint was the ''[[Onyx Cinder]]''.{{Fact}}

There were 1,139 massive underground vaults underneath the mint, lining a shaft, accessible by a [[turbolift]] for [[Starship|starships]]. Each vault had six stacks, each stack was six blocks high and nine blocks long.<ref name="Real Good Guys">{{SkeletonCrew|The Real Good Guys}}</ref>

[[Pirate]] [[Captain]] [[Tak Rennod]], [[SM-33]] and the crew of the ''[[Onyx Cinder]]'' attempted to find the mint. However, Captain Rennod's crew mutinied against him and killed him. However, Rennod rigged the ''Onyx'' to crash into At Attin.<ref name="Lot to Learn">{{SkeletonCrew|You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates}}</ref>
[[File:OR Mint.png|left|thumb|250px|A vault of the mint]]
In [[9 ABY]], Captain [[Brutus]] and his [[Silvo's pirates|crew]] of pirates captured a group of [[Skeleton Crew|children]] who claimed to be from At Attin.<ref name="Way Out Past the Barrier">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}}</ref> Former Captain [[Jod Na Nawood]] was able to convince the children to let him help them find their way home. After finding [[Captain Rennod's Lair|Rennod's Lair]] under [[Skull Ridge Mountain]]'s [[Skull Ridge Mountain Hotel & Spa]] on [[Lanupa]], Nawood learned from Rennod's [[Datalog]] that At Attin had the last Republic Mint.<ref name="Lot to Learn" /> He was able to convince his crew to spare him in order to lead them to At Attin in order to take the mint.<ref name="Zero Friends Again">{{SkeletonCrew|Zero Friends Again}}</ref> After re-capturing the ''Onyx'' and it's crew, Nawood was able to sneak aboard it and was taken to the mint by posing as a Republic emissary/jedi.<ref name="So Much Trouble" /> The [[Old Republic Mint]] was revealed to be the [[Great Work]], a government work program on At Attin run by [[The Supervisor]], a [[droid]] inhabiting a [[Supervisor's Tower|tall tower]] in the heart of [[Unidentified city (At Attin)|At Attin's main city]]. However, Nawood and his crew were unable to take control of the mint when the [[New Republic Defense Force]] destroyed his frigate and crew.<ref name="Real Good Guys" />
