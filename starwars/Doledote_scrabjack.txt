'''Doledote scrabjacks''' were a type of entity.<ref name="DftOZ 3" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFTOZ3}}</ref> after<ref name="DftOZ 3" /> his<ref name="Escape from Valo">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref> [[Spider Cruiser]] took damage from a [[satellite]] [[mine]] striking into its [[hull]] above the [[moon]] [[Vrant Tarnum]], the [[Umbaran]] [[scientist]] [[Niv Drendow Apruk]] angrily ordered for two [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] onboard the [[cruiser]] to stand up and begin repairing the [[starship]], referring to them as doledote scrabjacks.<ref name="DftOZ 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
Doledote scrabjacks were mentioned in the [[The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone 3|third issue]] of the [[2024]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone]]''. The comic was written by [[Daniel José Older]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="DftOZ 3" /> on [[December 18]], 2024.<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3016-088/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III--Dispatches-from-the-Occlusion-Zone-3|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III--Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone #3}}</ref>
