"'''BonadanBouncer'''" was the [[HoloNet]] handle of an individual who once transported [[puffer pig]]s for the [[Jablogian]] [[crime lord]] [[Azmorigan]]. Because the Jablogian refused to pay for the cleanup, "BonadanBouncer" left a negative review on Azmorigan's profile in [[Cynabar's InfoNet]]'s "Who's Who in the [[Lothal sector|Lothal Sector]]," adding that Azmorigan should be avoided.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>
