'''Crathmore''' was an individual that held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[lieutenant]] while serving within a [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant|rogue faction]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] that operated beneath the command of [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]].<ref name="Last Stand 1" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''Battle of Jakku &mdash; Last Stand'' 1 take place between the [[attack on Chandrila]] and the [[Battle of Jakku]], which are both dated to [[5 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the leadership of the [[Iron Blockade]] was usurped by the [[Anzati]] mystic [[Reyna Oskure]],<ref name="Republic Under Siege 4">[[Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege 4|''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 4]]</ref> but Crathmore remained aware of the continued survival of [[Governor]] Adelhard, who claimed that his former lover had underestimated the hold that he continued to have over his crew. Two nights after the mutiny aboard the [[Scepter (Imperial-class)|''Scepter'']] brought Oskure to power after Adelhard's propertied [[Death|demise]], Crathmore travelled to the [[planet]] [[Gallowyss]] aboard a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] to retrieve the waiting Grand [[Moff]] and his followers, who were notified to his impending arrival by [[Commander]] [[Bragh]]. After landing, Crathmore was praised by his leader, who promised that the lieutenant would be rewarded when the [[time]] came and took comfort from this indication that some among his [[Imperial Remnants|Imperial remnant]] still understood who they served.<ref name="Last Stand 1" />
