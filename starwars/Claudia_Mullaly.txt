'''Claudia Mullaly''' is an artist and modelmaker who worked on various projects for [[Lucasfilm]]. These include working as a scenic artist for ''[[Ewoks: The Battle for Endor]]''<ref name="Battle for Endor" />, a modelmaker for the original [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] attraction<ref name="Taking Off" />, and a concept artist for the [[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition|Special Edition]] of {{Film|VI}}.<ref name="6 Fascinating" />

Mullaly made a brief appearance within the ''Star Tours'' ride alongside visual effects supervisor [[Dennis Muren]] and camera operator [[Selwyn Eddy]] as crew members working in the maintenance area of the [[Tomorrowland Starport|starport]]. [[RX-24/Legends|RX-24]] nearly crashes the ship into their office, sending the trio diving for cover behind a desk.<ref name="Taking Off" />
