'''Bin Essada''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who served as the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Moff]] of the [[Circarpous sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Expansion Region]]. When [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] decreed that all regional [[governor]]s were required to increase production, Essada demanded that all resources be stripped from the [[planet]] [[Mimban]], and sent the [[Imperial Military]] to ensure continued operations and to pacify the world.<ref name="TOG" />

The [[Mimbanese]] resisted, and a [[Mimban Campaign|war]] erupted on Mimban. Essada ran the war from the safety of [[Bin Essada's offices|his offices]] on [[Gyndine]].<ref name="TOG" />
