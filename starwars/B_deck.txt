'''B deck''' was a deck in [[Mirogana Spaceport]] in the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in the [[Cadma sector]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Toshaal system]]</ref> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[coordinator]] at the [[spaceport]] made an announcement over the port's [[speaker]] system requesting that [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] security forces report to B deck.<ref name="Outlaws" /> The same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> another announcement was made over the spaceport's speaker system requesting the presence of the individual [[Dip Narmall]] on B deck.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
B deck was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
