'''Benjamin Hartley''' is an English former helicopter pilot and military aviation advisor<ref name="BXW 24" /> who portrayed [[Harb Binli]] in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="FotF">{{WebCite|url=https://thefutureoftheforce.com/2017/03/08/star-wars-a-pilots-perspective-benjamin-hartley-interview|text=Benjamin Hartley &#124; A Pilot's Perspective (Interview)|author=Roberts, Phil|date=2017-03-08|work=[https://thefutureoftheforce.com Future of the Force]|archivedate=20201202101311}}</ref>

Having realized that action scenes in movies look better when they are acted out by former armed forces personnel, Hartley founded Military Film Services, which provides extras that are ex-military or even still serving. One day, his company received a call from an assistant director who needed actors with [[cockpit]] experience for a "major secret project." Hartley and his employees were amazed to discover that it was for a ''[[Star Wars]]'' movie.<ref name="BXW 24" />
