{{Quote|There's spice, of course, but stick with andris, carsunum, or sansanna and never mess around with ryll or the prime Kessel strains.|Platt Okeefe|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
'''Andris''' was a variant of [[spice]] that was [[Mining|mined]] on [[Seylott]],<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> [[Empress Teta]] and its moon [[Atale]]. It was used as food additive and preservative, but could also be used as a remedy for [[hibernation sickness]].<ref name="NOP" /> The [[smuggler]] [[Platt Okeefe]] recommended it as a profitable cargo for smugglers in the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]]'s ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' and warned against trafficking variants such as [[ryll]] or the strains of spice mined on [[Kessel]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Andris was first mentioned in the [[2018]] [[canon]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, andris was introduced in the [[1998]] novel ''[[Young Jedi Knights: Return to Ord Mantell]]'', written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]] and [[Rebecca Moesta]].<ref name="Return to Ord Mantell">''[[Young Jedi Knights: Return to Ord Mantell]]''</ref>
