'''19 BBY''', or '''19 Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[Republic Era]] and [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Events==
*[[Maul]]'s criminal alliance [[Shadow Collective]] is founded,<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref> initially dissolved,<ref name="Darth Maul 4">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 4|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 4]]</ref> and later reformed.<ref name="Together Again">{{TCW|Together Again}}</ref>
*Despite being found innocent at her [[Trial of Ahsoka Tano|trial]] for the [[bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar]] (the real culprit was [[Barriss Offee]]), [[Ahsoka Tano]] leaves the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="TWJ">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref>
*[[Order 66]] prematurely activates for [[clone trooper]] [[Tup]] during the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]], leading [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]] to discover the [[Behavioral modification biochip|control chips]] and learn how they can force clone troopers to [[Great Jedi Purge|eliminate]] the [[Jedi]] without question. However, the ARC trooper is [[Death|killed]] by members of the [[Coruscant Guard]] before he can properly warn his fellow [[Cloning|clones]] and the Jedi.<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref><ref name="Fugitive">{{TCW|Fugitive}}</ref><ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref>
*After a [[Wikipedia:genocide|genocidal]] [[Atrocity on Mahranee|attack]] on the [[planet]] [[Mahranee]], the [[Jedi High Council]] reluctantly approves a plan to assassinate [[Count]] [[Dooku]], sending [[Jedi Master]] [[Quinlan Vos]] on an undercover operation to team up with Dooku's former assassin [[Asajj Ventress]] for the job.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>
*[[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] is killed by [[General]] [[Grievous]] during the [[Second Battle of Dathomir]].<ref name="Darth Maul 4" />
*Tano befriends [[Level 1313]] resident [[Trace Martez]] and her sister [[Rafa Martez|Rafa]], and the three become [[Escape from Oba Diah|embroiled]] in trouble with the [[Pyke Syndicate]] after Rafa is forced to recruit her sister for a [[spice]]-running job.<ref name="Gone with a Trace">{{TCW|Gone with a Trace}}</ref><ref name="Deal No Deal">{{TCW|Deal No Deal}}</ref>
*ARC trooper [[Echo]] is discovered to be alive and a captive of the [[Techno Union]]. He is rescued by [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Rex]], and [[Clone Force 99]].<ref name="ADE">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref>
*The [[Siege of Mandalore]] begins. Ahsoka Tano, working with [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] and the [[Mandalore resistance]], asks Skywalker and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] for help. When the two [[Jedi General]]s are abruptly called away to the [[Battle of Coruscant]], Skywalker splits the [[501st Legion]] and gives Tano a [[332nd Division|new division]], including the [[332nd Company]]. Since Tano is no longer a member of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], Skywalker promotes Rex to [[Clone Commander|Commander]] to lead the unit, with Tano officially serving as an [[advisor]].<ref name="Old Friends">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref>
*Following a duel, Count Dooku is decapitated by Anakin Skywalker aboard the ''[[Invisible Hand]]''.<ref name="Episode III" />
*Skywalker and Kenobi manage to rescue [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] from the ''Invisible Hand''.<ref name="Episode III" />
*Chancellor Palpatine reveals to Skywalker that he is a [[Dark Lord of the Sith]].<ref name="Episode III" />
*General Grievous is [[Battle of Utapau|slain]] by Kenobi on [[Utapau]].<ref name="Episode III" />
*Anakin Skywalker informs [[Mace Windu]] of his findings about Chancellor Palpatine.<ref name="Episode III" />
*Mace Windu, [[Kit Fisto]], [[Agen Kolar]], and [[Saesee Tiin]] [[Duel in Palpatine's office|attempt]] to arrest Palpatine after learning that the Chancellor is a [[Sith Lord]], but fail and are killed by Sidious with assistance from the manipulated Anakin Skywalker. Skywalker subsequently becomes Darth Vader, Sidious's new [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]].<ref name="Episode III" />
*Clone troopers led by Vader [[Operation: Knightfall|besiege]] the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="Episode III" />
*The [[Great Jedi Purge]] begins with [[Order 66]].<ref name="Episode III" />
*The [[Galactic Empire]] is [[Proclamation of the New Order|founded]] by the self-anointed [[Galactic Emperor]] Palpatine, replacing the [[Galactic Republic]] and putting [[the galaxy]] directly under the control of the [[Sith]].<ref name="Episode III" />
*The [[Galactic Senate]] becomes the [[Imperial Senate]].<ref name="Episode III" />
[[File:SideshowArtPrint-FateoftheChosenOne.png|thumb|right|250px|The temptation and fall of Anakin Skywalker came to its head in 19 BBY.]]
*Darth Vader [[Mission to Mustafar|slaughters]] the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] on [[Mustafar]] and remotely deactivates the [[Separatist Droid Army]], dissolving the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and ending the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode III" />
*Yoda and Sidious [[Duel in the Galactic Senate|duel]] in the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]].<ref name="Episode III" />
*Kenobi and Vader [[Duel on Mustafar|duel]] on Mustafar. After losing his remaining organic limbs in the duel, Vader is sealed into a [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] [[Darth Vader's armor|suit of armor]].<ref name="Episode III" />
*[[Padmé Amidala Naberrie]], the secret wife of Anakin Skywalker, dies on [[Polis Massa]] after giving birth to their children, [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]]. Her [[Padmé Amidala's funeral|funeral]] takes place on [[Naboo]].<ref name="Episode III" />
*Fearing that Anakin's children will be hunted down because they could pose a threat to the Emperor, the Jedi Masters [[Yoda]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], as well as [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], agree to hide the whereabouts of the [[Skywalker family|Skywalker]] [[twin]]s.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref><ref name="Episode III" /> As a result, the [[House of Organa]], the royal family of [[Alderaan]], adopts Leia, while the [[Lars family]] of Tatooine takes in Luke Skywalker.<ref name="Episode III" />
*Yoda goes into a self-imposed exile on [[Dagobah]], while Kenobi goes to live as a [[hermit]] on [[Tatooine]] watching over Luke Skywalker.<ref name="Episode III" />
*After the crash<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> of the ''[[Tribunal]]''<ref name="TCW Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> as a result of [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|onboard fighting]] caused by the issuance of Order 66, as well as the escape of Maul,<ref name="Victory and Death" /> Ahsoka Tano and Rex go into hiding in the Outer Rim to escape the Empire, with Tano faking her death<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> by abandoning her [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|lightsabers]] at the crash site.<ref name="DB:Ahsoka">{{Databank|ahsoka-tano|Ahsoka Tano}}</ref>
*[[Saw Gerrera]] of [[Onderon]] forms his rebel cell, the [[Partisans]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
*With the [[Republic Era|fall of the Republic]], the galaxy enters a period known as the "[[Imperial Era|dark times]]."<ref name="Episode III" /><ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
*[[Tipoca City]] is [[Attack on Kamino|destroyed]] by the Empire<ref name="Return to Kamino" /><ref name="TBB Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of [[The Bad Batch Season 1|''The Bad Batch'' Season 1]] in [[19 BBY]].</ref>
*Tano, under the false identity of Ashla, settles with the [[Fardi clan]] on the Outer Rim world of [[Thabeska]].<ref name="Ahsoka" />
*The [[Hidden Path]] is formed.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
