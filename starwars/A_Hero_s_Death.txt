'''A Hero's Death''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the ''[[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]]'' campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[July 27]], [[2005]] and took the form of a message posted on [[HoloNet/Legends|HoloNet]] by the [[Slicer/Legends|slicer]] [[Baylan]].
