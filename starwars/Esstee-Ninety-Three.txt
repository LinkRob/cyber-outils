'''Esstee-Ninety-Three''' was a [[PJ unit]] [[droid]] who worked at the [[Nodo's shipyard|shipyard]] of the criminal [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Rekias Nodo]] during the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Esstee-Ninety-Three was a hulking bipedal droid with a pair of powerful piston [[Limb|arms]] that featured pincers. The droid had a singular [[Color|red]] [[sensor]] and gray plating with yellow stripes. Esstee-Ninety-Three was a close friend to [[Gorko]], another of Nodo's employees. The employees of the [[shipyard]], located on a [[Unidentified planet (Han Solo)|jungle planet]], dismantled [[Thief|stolen]] [[starship]]s for scrap.<ref name="CYD" />

When the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] crash-landed onto the [[planet]] on which Nodo's shipyard was situated<ref name="CYD" /> at some point after the [[Standard year|year]] [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Solo encountered Esstee-Ninety-Three while [[Thief|stealing]] a [[heat exchanger]] from [[Nodo's yacht]] to repair his own starship, the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. Esstee-Ninety-Three was shot and destroyed by Solo, who was subsequently caught by Nodo. Solo and Chewbacca eventually escaped the planet aboard their starship.<ref name="CYD" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Dialogue|Gorko|He shot Esstee-Ninety-Three.|Nodo|Big mistake. Ninety-Three was Gorko's best friend.|Solo|Then I did you a favor. Seriously, how dumb can a droid get?|attr=Gorko, Rekias Nodo, and Han Solo, in one of the paths featuring Esstee-Ninety-Three|src=<ref name="CYD" />}}
Esstee-Ninety-Three appeared in ''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Han & Chewie Adventure]]'', a [[2018]] novel in the ''[[Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny]]'' series written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Elsa Charretier]].<ref name="CYD" />

The droid is featured in two paths that diverge after Solo attempts to steal the heat exchanger, both of which leave Esstee-Ninety-Three destroyed. In one path, Esstee-Ninety-Three confronts Solo, and Solo throws the exchanger to the droid, ordering it to catch the component. Surprised, the droid does so, and is subsequently shot by Solo. Gorko, angered by his friend's destruction, catches Solo, who remarks on the droid's lack of intelligence after being taken to Nodo. The other path has Esstee-Ninety-Three restrain [[Meecha Odon]], a [[Slavery|slave]] of Nodo's who previously encountered Solo while trying to escape. Odon attempts to inform the droid of the presence of Solo and the ''Millennium Falcon'', but Esstee-Ninety-Three does not believe her claims. Solo sneaks up behind Esstee-Ninety-Three, shooting the droid in the back. As both paths lead to the same point in the story and result in Esstee-Ninety-Three's destruction,<ref name="CYD" /> this article does not assume which set of events is the [[canon]] story, only that the droid is destroyed.
