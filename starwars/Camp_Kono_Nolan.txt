'''Camp Kono Nolan''' was a small camp located in the [[Markaran Plains]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Balmorra/Legends|Balmorra]] that was used by the [[Balmorran resistance]]. The [[Scientist/Legends|scientist]] [[Iannos Tyrek]] set up shop there after defecting to the resistance.
