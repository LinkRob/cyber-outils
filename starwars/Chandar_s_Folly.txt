{{Quote|That sounds like Tarkin. He once taught me something as well, on a world called Chandar's Folly.|[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to [[Ellian Zahra]]|<ref name="SW 8">[[Star Wars (2020) 8|''Star Wars'' (2020) 8]]</ref>}}
'''Chandar's Folly''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Saijo sector/Legends|Saijo sector]]<ref name="SWApp" /> in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Vader 18" /> During or before the [[High Republic Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Republic|High Republic]], the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Te'Ami]] attended a rodeo on Chandar's Folly.<ref name="LotJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Darth Vader requested that [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and nineteen [[Wilhuff Tarkin's hunting team|other]] [[Hunting|hunters]] come to Chandar's Folly try to challenge or even kill the [[Sith Lord]]. After ten days on the planet, Vader had killed all nineteen hunters, and only Tarkin remained.<ref name="Vader 18" />
