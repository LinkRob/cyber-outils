The '''Benelex Marshal Service Headquarters''' was a building on the astronimical object [[Paqualis III]] that served as the headquarters of the [[Benelex Marshal Service]].<ref name="Han Chewie 5" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> [[Marshal]] [[Buck Vancto]] took the criminal [[Corbus Tyra]] to the headquarters after capturing him on [[Iakar]]. Shortly after arriving, however, Tyra was [[Extraction on Paqualis III|extracted]] by the [[Khel Tanna's crew|crew]] of [[Khel Tanna]], the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]], and the [[bounty hunter]] [[Greedo]].<ref name="Han Chewie 5" />
