'''Edge''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[Tango Company]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Biography==
[[Tango Company]]<ref name="Episode Gallery" /> was assigned to escort a [[Pelta-class frigate|medical frigate]] to an [[Ord Cestus]] [[Ord Cestus medical station|medical station]] under the command of [[Jedi]] [[Commander]]s [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Barriss Offee]]. However, Tango Company [[Clone trooper|trooper]] [[Scythe (clone trooper)|Scythe]] was infected with a [[Geonosian]] [[brain worm]] and secretly brought a batch of eggs aboard the [[Starship|ship]]. As the infection spread throughout the ship, Edge became aware that his fellow troopers were acting strangely. However, he was too late, as he too was infected. As Edge was the last uninfected [[Cloning|clone]] on board, they attacked their next targets, Tano and Offee. They eventually infected Offee, but were later saved by Tano.<ref name="BI" />

==Equipment==
Edge wielded a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] as well as a [[DC-15A blaster rifle]].<ref name="BI" /> His helmet sported [[Color|blue]] markings on the arms and the Tango Company logo, which was a [[tiger]]'s face.<ref name="Concept Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/brain-invaders-concept-art-gallery|text="Brain Invaders" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> He had a [[tattoo]] of the [[Aurebesh]] letter [[Senth]] over his left eyebrow.<ref name="BI" />

==Behind the scenes==
Edge first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Brain Invaders]]." Like all clone troopers in the series, he was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="BI" /> Edge re-uses the same head model as clone trooper [[Sketch]], and as such they both share the same tattoo of the Aurebesh letter Senth above their eyebrow.<ref name="Trivia Gallery" />
