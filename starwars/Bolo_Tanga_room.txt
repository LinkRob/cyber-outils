The '''Bolo Tanga room''' was a location in [[Cloud City]], [[Bespin]]. After [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], former [[Baron Administrator]] of the [[city]], regained control from the [[Galactic Empire]], the Imperial [[officer]]s that refused to surrender were trapped in the Bolo Tanga room. Calrissian was guided to the room by his cohort [[Lobot]] and proceeded to secure a surrender from all but one of the Imperials that remained in the room.<ref name="Empire's End" />
