'''Dotharian''' was a [[planet]] located deep within [[Hutt Space]]. It was the home of the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate|Mourner's Wail]] criminal syndicate, led by [[Lord]] [[Khamdek]], who operated from a secure [[Fortress of the Mourner's Wail|fortress]]. The former [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] visited Dotharian to acquire information about the [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Nakano Lash]] from the Mourner's Wail, who Lash had [[Mission to Corellia (Nakano Lash's crew)|betrayed]] [[Standard year|years]] earlier.

==Description==
A terrestrial [[planet]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 13">[[Bounty Hunters 13|''Bounty Hunters'' 13]]</ref> Dotharian was located in the [[Dotharian system]] in [[Hutt Space]], in grid square S-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> along a [[Circumtore–Syvris hyperlane|hyperlane]] that ran from [[Circumtore]] to [[Syvris]].<ref name="Map" /> Dotharian was covered in rocky cliffs, with [[plant]] life present on the surface.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2" />

==History==
{{Quote|I don't care about the bounty. I'm here for ''information''.|T'onga, on Nakano Lash|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2" />}}
At some point by [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Bounty Hunters 1|''Bounty Hunters'' 1]] take place shortly after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], dated to [[3 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, the events of ''Bounty Hunters'' 1, and therefore [[Bounty Hunters 2|''Bounty Hunters'' 2]], must take place around that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the criminal organization known as the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate]] came to reside in a [[Fortress of the Mourner's Wail|fortress]] on Dotharian, led by [[Lord]] [[Khamdek]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2" />

Around 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> former [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] infiltrated the Fortress of the Mourner's Wail on Dotharian in order to find information about the [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Nakano Lash]], who had resurfaced following her disappearance after she [[Death|killed]] Khamdek's son<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2" /> [[Khamus]], the heir to the syndicate, in a [[Mission to Corellia (Nakano Lash's crew)|mission]] [[Standard year|years]] prior.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 1">[[Bounty Hunters 1|''Bounty Hunters'' 1]]</ref> T'onga was caught after she killed several of the Mourner's Wail's guards, but Lord Khamdek allowed her to live so she could speak. She requested information about Lash's reappearance from Lord Khamdek, wishing to hunt down and kill her.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2" />

==Inhabitants==
The members of the Mourner's Wail Syndicate resided on Dotharian, including their leader, the [[human]] Lord Khamdek.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|Most impressive&hellip;you made it deep into the most ''fortified'' stronghold in the sector.|Lord Khamdek, to T'onga|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2" />}}
The Mourner's Wail crime syndicate operated from a vast fortress located on the surface of Dotharian. Lord Khamdek claimed that his fortress was the most fortified in the entire [[sector]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2" />
