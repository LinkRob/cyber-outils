The '''Concordia Tradition''' was a set of [[armor]] worn by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal Warrior|standard costume]].<ref name="Hunters" /> This costume resembled the [[Color|blue]] [[Mandalorian armor|armor]] worn by the Mandalorian warriors [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] to Concordia, some of whom would eventually lead to the creation of the [[Death Watch]].<ref name="The Mandalore Plot">{{TCW|The Mandalore Plot}}</ref>

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Concordia Tradition was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Aran Tal, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Concordia WESTAR-35]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
