"'''Balancing the Books'''" is a short narrative story published in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'' in [[1987]]. Presented as an in-universe memo, [[Calk Fen]] reports on the amount of [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]] that [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] now owes to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba]] the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]]. It was reprinted in the [[The Star Wars Sourcebook (Second Edition)|second edition]] of the book in [[1994]], and later online as part of ''[[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club]]''.

==Plot summary==
[[Calk Fen]] authors a report to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba]] the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] about the debt incurred by [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] after dropping a consignment of [[Spice/Legends|spice]].<ref name="SWS" />
