'''Charlie Akin''' (born April, 1989) is an English actor who played a [[Resistance]] Tech and a [[First Order stormtrooper|Stormtrooper]] and worked as a stand-in for [[Gwendoline Christie]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. He also played a Marine Soldier in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Interviews">{{WebCite|url=https://starwarsinterviews.com/sequel-trilogy/episode-vii-the-force-awakens/charlie-akin-first-order-stormtrooper|text=Charlie Akin (First Order Stormtrooper)|author=[[Dennis Pellegrom]]|date=2016-03|work=[[Star Wars Interviews]]|quote=Because the original Stormtrooper design costumes were used for Rogue One, I didn't fit into it thus couldn't be a Stormtrooper. But I was a marine soldier as part of the resistance.|archivedate=20230205201458|nolive=1}}</ref>
