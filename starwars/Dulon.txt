'''Dulon''' was a [[Jedi training|training]] regimen to help [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] learners in the art of [[lightsaber combat]]. This training involved solo sequences of moves in which they did not fight an actual opponent, but merely envisioned the opponent's presence. This, in combination with other [[lightsaber]] drills known as [[velocities]], helped to prepare them for live combat by using [[the Force]] to take them beyond what would normally be physically possible.<ref name="Complete Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>
