The '''DLS-12 heavy blaster carbine''' was the [[blaster carbine]] version of [[DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle]] that was designed for the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the final [[Standard month|months]] of the [[Clone Wars]] by [[BlasTech Industries]].<ref name="Gadgets" />

While the redesign limited the carbine's maximum effective range, it maintained a prodigious energy output. Still bulky to have been considered a type of carbine, the DLS-12 offered some of the best stopping power in its class. And while the Republic purchased the carbine in mass quantity, the Clone Wars between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] was brought to an end before the [[blaster]] was used on the front lines, and since the [[Imperial Military|forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] never adopted the carbine in significant numbers, the weapons were sold in a surplus of auctions for 1,350 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], finding their way into the hands of [[bounty hunter]]s, [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], and other hired guns.<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
The DLS-12 heavy blaster carbine was mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets" />
