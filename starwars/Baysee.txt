[[Lieutenant]] '''Baysee''' was a [[human]] who flew alongside [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]], [[Captain]] [[Carson Teva]], Lieutenant [[Jensu]], Lieutenant [[Mowaat]], and Lieutenant [[Lander (individual)|Lander]] in an [[Incident on Seatos|unauthorized mission]] to [[Seatos]]. After the group detected the ''[[Eye of Sion]]'', a massive [[hyperspace transport ring]], exiting the [[atmosphere]] of Seatos, the group flew to cross its axis and block its path. The unit failed and suffered casualties when the ''Sion'' entered [[hyperspace]] through the group. Baysee's X-wing was flung around and crashed into Mowaat's own X-wing, killing both of them.<ref name="Part Four: Fallen Jedi" />
