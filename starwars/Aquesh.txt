'''Aquesh''' was a [[Quarren]] [[gambler]] that [[Akivan|resided]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> they played [[sabacc]] with the [[Rodian]] [[Fazlee]] at [[The Alcazar]] [[cantina]] in Akiva's [[capital]] [[city]] [[Myrra]] while wearing a [[Color|blue]] [[clothing|outfit]]. Aquesh had orange skin, black [[eye]]s and [[Language|spoke]] [[Huttese]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] won a game of [[Kessel Sabacc]] against Aquesh and Fazlee.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aquesh appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, they were at one of several sabacc tables that the player could optionally choose to play against in order to earn [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
