"'''Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge'''" is the first episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'' and was released on [[April 6]], [[2026]] alongside "[[Chapter 2: Sinister Schemes]]." Its title was revealed on [[March 17]], 2026 by the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' Twitter account.<ref name="Twitter" />

==Official description==
''In the criminal underworld of Janix, Maul enacts a plan to consolidate his power.''

==Plot summary==
===A bank robbery===
The episode opens with a landscape shot of a sprawling metropolis. A group of armored [[Mandalorian]]s led by [[Rook Kast]] and the [[Dathomirian]] [[Zabrak]] brothers [[Icarus]] and [[Scorn]] abseil down a [[Nico Deemis' bank tower|bank tower]] as a [[bank transport]] approaches a hangar bay below flanked by two smaller [[airspeeder]]s. The [[droid]], [[Spybot]], fires a [[computer spike]] which causes the door to unlock. The Mandalorians then enter the hangar and take out the [[security droid]]s and [[bank droid]]s and are joined by the Zabraks who help them take out the defenders. A bank droid manages to alert members of the [[Tactical Defense Force]] (TDF), who dispatch airspeeders to the scene. 

After the spybot alerts Kast, she deploys several cable traps that cause one of the airspeeders, [[Unit 10]], to crash into the street below. The second airspeeder dodges the cable traps but is hit by the spybot's [[blaster]] bolts. Despite losing sight of the hijacked bank transport, the TDF droid receives reports of an unidentified shuttle seen in [[Sector 8-J]]. The officer [[Pak (Tactical Defense Force)|Pak]] calls for reinforcements to converge on the shuttle's coordinates. The Mandalorians park their stolen bank transport near a [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|''Kom'rk''-class fighter/transport]].

Pak and other TDF officers arrive in their airspeeders and surround the bank robbers, demanding their surrender. Kast pretends to comply just as a hooded figure descends from the shuttle. The TDF officers shoot at the newcomer, who deflects their blaster bolts with his [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|double-bladed lightsaber]]. He reveals himself as [[Maul]].

===Fugitives=== 
Meanwhile, the [[Jedi]] fugitives [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]] watch police speeders heading towards  Sector 8-J to confront the bank robbers. Izara says something is going on. Hungry, she eyes some fruits being sold by a vendor. She suggests stealing a fruit but Master Daki rules out the idea of stealing, saying they will rely on the kindness of strangers. After Daki is rebuffed by a pedestrian, Izara heads towards the vendor's stall where the [[Didynon]] vendor is haggling with an [[Ithorian]] customer. While the vendor is distracted, she steals several fruits, which she hides under her cloak. 

While returning to Daki, she accidentally drops the fruits, alerting the vendor who summons a pair of [[police droid]]s. The police droids arrest Izara for shoplifting. Daki convinces his Padawan to comply with the police and reassures her that they will release her. Before surrendering to the droids, Izara hands her cloak to Daki. The droids lead Izara away while Daki sighs. 

===The crime scene===
The following day, a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] named [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]] arrives in an airspeeder at the bank tower where police droids are conducting a crime scene examination. He tells a human police officer that he wants a full scan of the route. A police droid [[2B0T]] ("Two Boots") tells him that all [[Holocamera|holocams]] and [[sensor]]s were destroyed during the heist. Two Boots laments the destruction of droids and that the bank supervisor droid is missing. Lawson wonders who would be crazy enough to rob [[Nico Deemis]]' bank. Two Boots says that Deemis is involved in underworld activities and suggests that his main rival [[Looti Vario]] was behind the heist. 

Lawson disagrees, saying that Vario knows that this would amount to a declaration of war with Deemis. He notices that the robbers left a lot of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] behind but kidnapped the supervisor droid. He also observes the cable traps left by the robbers and believes that it was too sophisticated to have involved the local crime syndicates. Lawson warns that Deemis will not take the bank robbery lightly. 

Elsewhere, the [[Gran]] crime lord Nico Deemis hurls several objects in his office after learning about the bank robbery. A [[Trandoshan]] lieutenant suggests that his rival Vario was responsible. Deemis orders his staff to arrange a meeting with Vario, seeking to question him about the bank robbery. At the suggestion of a lieutenant, Deemis agrees to suggest a meeting on neutral territory. Unknown to the criminals, they are being monitored by a spybot belonging to Maul and the Mandalorians. 

===The shadow lord===
Meanwhile, Maul speaks with Rook Kast, telling her about his desire to train an apprentice. Kast reports that the local crime syndicates are honoring their truce. Maul suggests motivating Deemis to retaliate. When Kast asks why they are bothering with low-level targets like crime syndicates, Maul explains that the rise of the [[Galactic Empire]] has caused many to suffer including the Jedi. Maul seeks to target those benefiting from the rise of the Empire including the [[Pyke Syndicate]], [[Crimson Dawn]], Vario and Deemis. Maul explains that the bank heist was revenge against Deemis for taking what Maul deemed to be his. 

When Kast suggests she could assemble a team to assassinate Deemis, Maul replies that he prefers to keep a low profile for now. He orders a Zabrak henchman to activate the kidnapped bank supervisor droid, which is a [[3PO-series protocol droid]]. The bank droid is defiant and warns Maul and his henchmen about incurring the wrath of Deemis. At lightsaber point, Maul orders the bank droid to convey a message to her master. 

===Police work===
While Daki waits in the streets, two police droids ask Izara her name. She refuses to cooperate. Izara attracts the attention of Captain Lawson, who tells the droids to go easy on her because she is a child. Lawson agrees to process Izara and tells her that she is charged with attempted robbery, failure to cooperation and lacking identification. Noticing a picture of Lawson's wife and son, Izara asks if that is her son. He warns her to produce identification or she will spend a night in jail. When she refuses, Lawson orders a police droid to process her. 

Two-Boots informs Lawson that he has recovered footage from the memory banks of the police droid accompanying Officer Pak. The footage shows a hooded figure armed with a laser sword. Lawson generates a life-size [[hologram]] image of Maul and asks Two-Boots to run a facial recognition scan. Two-Boots finds that Maul's file has been classified by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] (ISB) and that they have been instructed to report any sightings of the suspect immediately. Lawson is unwilling to involve the [[Galactic Empire]] in local law enforcement matters and tells Two-Boots to keep it quiet. 

===Taking out Nico Deemis===
Later, Deemis meets with the [[Aleena]] crime lord Looti Vario at a dilapidated bar. The two are accompanied by their droids and underlings. Vario denies he was involved in the heist and suggests that Deemis' other enemies were responsible. Vario suggests that it was somebody whom Deemis betrayed. Deemis speculates that this was a misdirection by Vario. Vario responds that he has no interest in starting a war with the rival crime lord. Deemis accuses Vario of seeking to encroach on his territory and undermine him. Just then, a servant droid brings a pot containing the head of Deemis's kidnapped bank droid. 

Vario expresses shock but Deemis does not believe him and orders his underlings to attack. A gun battle breaks out, which is watched by the spybot with satisfaction. Spybot livestreams the gunfight to Maul and Kast. Vario flees and Deemis pursues him. Elsewhere, Lawson and Two-Boots receive news of an incident in the port as police droids rush to respond. Back at the port, Deemis shoots Vario's armored suit from behind. However, Vario escapes the suit and shoots Deemis in the back four times. While Vario savors his victory, he is being watched by Maul, who tells Kast to "finish the job." 

Kast sends her strike team in pursuit of Vario, who flees in his [[landspeeder]]. Vario takes his speeder into a highway and manages to maneuver his smaller vehicle behind the strike team's larger speeder. The two Zabrak henchmen exchange fire with him. Following a struggle, Vario's speeder crashes into a barrier. One of the Zabraks shoots Vario's armored suit, disabling it. Vario climbs out of his suit but is cornered by the Zabrak, whom he recognizes as one of Maul's henchmen. The confrontation is interrupted by Lawson and Two-Boots, who arrive in a police speeder. 

They order Maul's strike team to surrender but the second Zabrak opens fire with a [[repeating blaster]]. Vario expresses relief to see Lawson. As more police reinforcements arrive, Maul's strike team retreat. Watching the operation via the spybot, Kast expresses concern that the local authorities will alert the Empire. Maul vows that Vario will not escape him even in police custody. When the two are alone, Kast advises against an attack against a police station to avoid exposing them. Maul is determined to punish Vario for his transgression and says he senses something else at the police station. He adds that their actions serve a greater purpose. As Spybot returns, Maul tells Kast that events are falling into place. 

===Unfinished business===
At the police precinct, Vario tells Lawson that he killed Deemis in self-defense and claimed he was set up by an old enemy. He asks Lawson if the police headquarters has enough security. When Lawson asks Vario what he is worried about, Vario asks if he has heard about the [[Shadow Collective]], which Lawson is unaware of. Later, Izara watches as Lawson and Two Boots escort Vario into his cell, which is opposite her cell. Vario asks the Twi'lek what she is looking at and complains about his spartan cell. Two Boots replies that the cell is adequate to his needs. 

Two Boots tells Lawson that he has been unable to identify the two Zabraks, whom he says has different markings from the hooded individual captured in the holocam footage from the droid. When Two Boots suggests alerting the Empire, Lawson disagrees and orders Two Boots to keep an eye on Vario. Outside, Daki waits in the streets while rain showers down the city. Under the police precinct, Maul and his Zabrak henchmen travel through a tunnel while Spybot disables the security cameras and sensors. Entering the detention block, the Zabraks take out two prison guards.

At the command center, Lawson and Two Boots receive reports of outages on Level 8. The police discover that their systems have been hacked. As the emergency systems activate, Lawson and Two Boots head to the detention block to protect Vario. On Level 8, Maul's Zabrak henchmen corner a police officer and a unit of police droids, gunning them down with a repeating blaster. While Izara waits anxiously inside her cell, Spybot activates the blast doors, separating Two Boots and Lawson. Two Boots attempts to regain control but Spybot shocks him through a [[network terminal]]. Through the Force, Izara silently communicates with Master Daki, who senses her distress. 

Two Boots uses his [[datapad]] to regain control of the prison's computer systems and unlocks the blast doors. With comms down, Lawson sends Two Boots to summon reinforcements. Lawson comes to the aid of police officers and droids who are being attacked by Maul's Zabrak associates. Lawson comes face to face with Maul, who uses the Force to pull his [[blaster pistol]] out of his hand before knocking him unconscious. Joining his associates, Maul takes out more police officers and droids with his lightsaber while Izara and Vario cower in their cells. 

After taking out the last police droid, Maul and his associates force their way into Vario's cell. When Maul confronts him for his betrayal, Vario begs Maul for mercy, offering ships, weapons, and money. Vario manages to convince Maul not to kill by telling him that he can help him get to [[Marg Krim]], leader of the Pyke Syndicate. Maul orders his Zabrak associates to take Vario into custody. Before leaving, Maul notices Devon Izara. He enters her cell and Izara recognizes him as Maul.
