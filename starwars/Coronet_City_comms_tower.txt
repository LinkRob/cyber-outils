A '''comms tower''' in [[Coronet City]]<ref name="EFV" /> on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Corellia]]<ref name="Location" /> operated during the [[High Republic Era]]. The [[Comm tower|tower]] consisted of a tapered, solid [[metal]] pole topped by an [[antenna]]e cluster. At some point, the aspiring [[pirate]] [[Zyle Keem]] scaled the comms tower and struggled to maintain their grip on its smooth surface. While climbing an [[acthorn tree]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Valo]]<ref name="EFV" /> in [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> Keem reflected that ascending the [[tree]] was easier than their prior experience in Coronet [[City]], thinking they had only stayed on the structure through sheer force of will.<ref name="EFV" />

==Behind the scenes==
