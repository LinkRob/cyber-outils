'''2023 Topps ''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'' 40th Anniversary''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] to celebrate the 40th anniversary of {{Film|VI}}. Two cards were released each day starting on [[May 22]]<ref name="Topps 1" /> and ending on [[June 2]], [[2023]] for a total of 20 cards.<ref name="Topps 20" />
