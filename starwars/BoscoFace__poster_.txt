'''''BoscoFace''''' was a decorative [[Painting|poster]] of the [[Lasat]] of the same name. It showed them against a [[Color|red]] and grey background, with the name [[BoscoFace]] printed in large [[Aurebesh]] characters at the bottom. The poster was used to decorate one of the crates at [[Zeb's Hideout]], a [[cargo container]] at [[Chopper Base]] on [[Atollon]], where fellow Lasat [[Garazeb Orrelios]] relaxed.<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base" />
