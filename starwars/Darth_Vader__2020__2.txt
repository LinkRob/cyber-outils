'''''Darth Vader'' 2''' is the second issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. It was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]], and published on [[March 11]], [[2020]] by [[Marvel Comics]]. The issue features the story "'''Dark Heart of the Sith Part II'''," in which [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] comes face to face with [[Sabé]], a former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] of the late [[Padmé Amidala]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE FACE OF THE QUEEN! In the wake of the shocking revelations of ''The Empire Strikes Back'', DARTH VADER continues his quest for revenge against everyone who hid his son Luke from him. But when his search takes him deep into his past, he uncovers a hauntingly familiar face that will challenge everything he knows.''
