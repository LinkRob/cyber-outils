==Behind the scenes==
Bumi maggot was mentioned in the [[Wikipedia:Written Chinese|Chinese]] web novel ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''<ref name="TVoSD" /> written by "[[His Majesty the King]]," within its fifty-first chapter, which was released on [[January 16]], [[2021]]. The maggot's name was written in [[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|simplified Chinese]] as "布密蛆" (bù mì qū).<ref name="Chapter 51">{{WebCite|url=https://www.qidian.com/chapter/1015950219/630378568|text=第51章 一声谢谢便弥足珍贵 _《星球大战：白银誓约》小说在线阅读|author=[[His Majesty the King]]|date=2021-01-16|language=[[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|Simplified Chinese]]|archivedate=20260611104537}}</ref>
