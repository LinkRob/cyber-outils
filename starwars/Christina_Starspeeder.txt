'''Christina Starspeeder''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|girl]] from [[Obroa-skai/Legends|Obroa-skai]]. She attended the [[Jedi academy]] based at the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] and was one of its top-performing [[Padawan/Legends|students]]. Christina had a younger brother named [[Victor Starspeeder]] who began attending in a later [[Standard year|year]] than her. Though outwardly she seemed embarrassed by her brother, she helped and trained him in private and tried to give him advice to succeed in fitting into the academy and doing well academically. For his part, Victor acted sometimes as if he couldn't stand her, but secretly hero-worshipped her.<ref name="newclass" />

==Biography==
===Early life===
Christina Starspeeder was born on [[Obroa-skai/Legends|Obroa-skai]], a couple of years before her brother, [[Victor Starspeeder]].

===Jedi academy years===
Christina attended the [[Jedi academy]] based at the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] and became one of its top-performing [[Padawan/Legends|students]]. A student named [[Zachary O'Halleran]] had romantic feelings for her and asked her out, but she disapproved of his antagonistic and cunning nature and refused him.<ref name="newclass" />

Eventually, Victor began attending in a later [[Standard year|year]] than her. Though outwardly she seemed embarrassed by her brother, she helped and trained him in private and tried to give him advice to succeed in fitting into the academy and doing well academically. For his part, Victor acted sometimes as if he couldn't stand her, but secretly hero-worshipped her. She was appalled that Zachary was using Victor to get closer to her, but when Victor realized the truth, she saved him from Zachary's wrath by shoving him against a locker.<ref name="newclass" />

The following year, Christina encountered her father [[Edward Starspeeder|Krio Vin]] on Coruscant. She gave evidence against him, assisting the Jedi while pretending to serve him with loyalty and faith. On one occasion, Victor saw her speaking with Vin, but she sensed him coming and Force-pushed him away. She joined Victor when he attacked the academy outright, where [[Mr. Zefyr]] revealed her duplicity to the enemy. She was awarded a medal for her bravery and intellect.<ref name="forceoversleeps">''[[Jedi Academy: The Force Oversleeps]]''</ref>

===Graduation===
Christina graduated and began a new training course at a new academy. A year after Vin's defeat, she attended Victor's graduation.<ref name="revengeofthesis">''[[Jedi Academy: Revenge of the Sis]]''</ref><ref name="principalstrikesback">''[[Jedi Academy: The Principal Strikes Back]]''</ref>
