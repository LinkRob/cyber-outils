"'''Comics Link'''" was a department in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' from [[Star Wars Insider 50|issue 50]] to [[Star Wars Insider 76|issue 76]]. It reviewed the most recent ''Star Wars'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic books]]. The magazine's comics column was previously named ''[[The Horse's Mouth]]'', in reference to publisher [[Dark Horse Comics]].

The section was then renamed "[[Drawn by the Force]]" starting with [[Star Wars Insider 77|''Star Wars Insider'' 77]] as part of [[IDG Entertainment]]'s overhaul of the magazine.
