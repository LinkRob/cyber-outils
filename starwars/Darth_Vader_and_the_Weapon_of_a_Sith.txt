"'''Darth Vader and the Weapon of a Sith'''" is a short story in the anthology ''[[Galactic Adventures Storybook Collection]]'' written by [[Ivan Cohen]] and illustrated by [[TomatoFarm]]. It is an adaptation of the comics [[Darth Vader (2017) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 3]]&ndash;[[Darth Vader (2017) 5|5]].<ref name="Galactic Adventures Storybook Collection" />
