'''Ahakista''' was a location in the [[Ahakista system]] where the [[Daystar Casino]] was built. The [[Dug]] [[Gambling|gambler]] [[Gronson Takkaro]], who at one point worked as operations manager of the [[casino]], fell deeply into debt on Ahakista and fled to the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. The [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] tracked Takkaro to Lothal in order to capture him and bring him back to Ahakista to claim a [[bounty]] placed on the Dug.<ref name="EG" />
