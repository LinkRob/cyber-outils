{{Quote|Sonn-Blas provides weapons to the First Order in defiance of New Republic law, with the F-11D proving the weapon of choice for new ranks of stormtroopers.|The ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''|<ref name="Galactic Explorer's Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref>}}
The '''F-11D blaster rifle''' was a [[blaster rifle]] manufactured by [[Sonn-Blas Corporation]]. This ranged weapon was the successor of the older [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. It served as the standard issue weapon of [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]].<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Design==
[[File:BustCollection-StormtrooperOfficer.png|thumb|left|200px|A First Order stormtrooper using a F-11D blaster rifle with an extended stock.]]
In [[9 ABY]], the [[Imperial Armored Commando]]s of [[Gideon's Imperial remnant]] wielded [[EF-11D blaster rifle]]s, which were a step between the Imperial E-11 and the F-11D.<ref name="MandoVG"/>

Design features included an adjustable [[J19 electroscope]] with eight-power magnification and low-light capability, a removable [[stock]] assembly, magnatomic adhesion grip with integrated power feed instigator and a collapsible foregrip. The power pack on the F-11D was moved from the left-hand side of the [[blaster]] to the right hand side to enable the blaster to be holstered on the right leg of the [[First Order stormtrooper armor|stormtrooper's armor]]. [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] used an [[Phasma's blaster rifle|F-11D]] that featured a [[chromium]] finish, buttstock, and a recurved trigger guard.<ref name="TFAVD" />

[[First Order snowtrooper|First Order Snowtroopers]] had a modified F-11D that had antifogging optics and heat shunting devices. These helped the weapon function in colder climates.<ref name="Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

The [[Pyre's blaster|F-11D]] was also used by [[Commander]] [[Pyre]], featuring a gold finish,<ref name="The Children from Tehar">{{Resistance|The Children from Tehar}}</ref> due to the [[electrum]] crystal, which gives anything it is coated with a reflective gold sheen.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>
