A '''fuse box''' was a component used in [[speeder bike]]s. On<ref name="RTCT" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="RS">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Valo]]<ref name="RTCT" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] used [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|levitate]] several [[speeder]] parts around him&mdash;including a fuse box&mdash;while repairing a [[Unidentified speeder bike|speeder bike]] in a [[Jedi garage|repair garage]]. As he mentally accounted for the parts around him, he reflected that one of the objects was a fuse box, which would fit alongside the [[Speeder bike|bike's]] retrovaporizer and connect to the rest of its machinery.<ref name="RTCT" />

==Behind the scenes==
A fuse box first appeared in [[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower|''Race to Crashpoint Tower'']], a junior novel written by [[Daniel José Older]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="RTCT" /> Phase I that was published on [[June 29]], [[2021]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> Prior to its release, the component's mention was included in a preview for the work printed in the junior novel [[The High Republic: A Test of Courage|''A Test of Courage'']],<ref name="ATOC">''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]''</ref> published on [[January 5]] of the same year.<ref name="Phase I" /> In the real world, fuse boxes are metal boxes that contain fuses, an electrical safety device that shuts off when a fuse is powered beyond it's limit.<ref name="FuseBox">{{WebCite|url=https://www.acelectricohio.com/fuse-box-vs-circuit-breaker|text=Fuse Box vs Circuit Breaker: What's the Difference?|date=2022-07-05|archivedate=20230401232141}}</ref>
