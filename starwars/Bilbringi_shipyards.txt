The '''Bilbringi shipyards''' were a [[shipyard]] located at [[Bilbringi]]. The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] ''[[Lucid Voice]]'' was dismantled sometime after the [[Clone Wars]] between the Separatists and the [[Galactic Republic]] at the shipyards. Four [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Starship|ship]] was dismantled, its array tower was stolen and used to build a [[Berch Teller's warship|warship]] by [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|dissidents]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bilbringi shipyards were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], written by [[James Luceno]] and published by [[Del Rey]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> The shipyards were first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[The Last Command]]'', a [[1993]] novel written by [[Timothy Zahn]] and published by [[Bantam Spectra]].<ref name="Last Command">''[[The Last Command]]''</ref>
