'''Base Gamma One''' was the naval code for a type of [[orbital bombardment]]. One such code was used by the [[Galactic Empire]] to quell an [[Bombardment of Haruun Kal|insurrection]] on [[Haruun Kal]]. This level of bombardment heated Haruun Kal's atmosphere to the point where its surface water boiled off.<ref name="Final Order">{{StoryCite|story=The Final Order|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>
