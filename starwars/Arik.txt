'''Arik''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. At some point long after the end of the war, their acquaintance and last living [[Cloning|clone]], [[Clone Corporal|Corporal]] [[Kix]], suffered a [[Starweird]]-induced [[hallucination]] in which he saw the empty [[Clone trooper armor|armor]] of Arik and the other clones of the [[Galactic Republic]].

==Biography==
[[File:UnhelmetedKix.png|thumb|right|200px|Kix (pictured) saw Arik's armor during a hallucination.]]
Created on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]]<ref name="Clone troopers" /> to serve in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], the [[trooper]] who would take on the name Arik was<ref name="Sinister Secret" /> a [[Cloning|clone]] of the [[human]] [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]].<ref name="Clone troopers" /> They fought against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict which spanned from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]].<ref name="WarDate">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> During their service to the [[Galactic Republic]], Arik became acquainted with the [[clone medic]] [[Clone Corporal|Corporal]] [[Kix]]. At some point after the war,<ref name="Sinister Secret" /> no earlier than around [[31 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "Galactic Tales: The Crimson Corsair and the Sinister Secret of the Binary Star" depict [[Kix]] as part of [[Sidon Ithano]]'s [[pirate]] [[Sidon Ithano's pirate crew|crew]], meaning it must take place after he joined the crew in "[[The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku]]." The events of "The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku" take place around [[31 ABY]] per the reasoning [[Template:SequelEraContentDate|here]].</ref> Kix, now the last living clone trooper, served as a member of [[Sidon Ithano]]'s [[pirate]] [[Sidon Ithano's pirate crew|crew]] and accompanied them on a mission to the [[Oreenda Fayeet system]] to steal supplies from an old, abandoned [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] research [[Starship|ship]] that was trapped in the [[gravity]] between two [[star]]s. While aboard, Kix suffered a [[hallucination]] induced by an infant [[Starweird]], a [[species]] of semi-corporeal [[Ghost (folklore)|spirits]]. In the hallucination, Kix saw the empty, unmarked [[Clone trooper armor|armor]] of his clone siblings, including Arik's [[armor]].<ref name="Sinister Secret" />

==Personality and traits==
Arik was a clone trooper<ref name="Sinister Secret" /> made from the [[Clone template|genetic template]] of Jango Fett,<ref name="Clone troopers" /> and as such, weighed 80 [[kilogram]]s<ref name="Weight" /> and shared Fett's [[Color|tan]] skin and height of 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|ft]]).<ref name="Clone troopers" />

==Equipment==
Like all other clone troopers of the Grand Army of the Republic, Arik was implanted with a [[behavioral modification biochip]] by the [[Kaminoan]] [[Scientist|scientists]] to ensure that they would not resist certain [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|orders]].<ref name="Clone troopers" /> They were also equipped with protective clone trooper armor. During his hallucination, Kix saw Arik's [[bone]] white armor holding a [[blaster]].<ref name="Sinister Secret" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arik was mentioned in the short story "[[Galactic Tales: The Crimson Corsair and the Sinister Secret of the Binary Star]]" where the clone's armor appeared in a hallucination. Part of the "[[Galactic Tales]]" series, the story was written by [[Landry Q. Walker]] and published in the [[Star Wars Insider 234|212th issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine,<ref name="Sinister Secret" /> on [[October 14]], [[2025]].<ref name="Published">{{TitanComics|url=m/187-star-wars-insider-234/|text=Star Wars Insider #234|archivedate=20251014175216}}</ref>
