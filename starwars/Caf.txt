'''Caf''',<ref name="AND" /> also known as '''coffee''',<ref name="Blade Squadron" /><ref name="Good Kiss">{{StoryCite|story=A Good Kiss|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> was a brewed beverage prepared from ground [[caf bean]]s.<ref name="Last Shot" /> It had a bitter taste,<ref name="AEE">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> and stimulating properties because of the [[caffeine]] it contained.<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy" /> [[Cream]] could be added to caf, as could [[sugar]]. Caf without any cream or sugar added was referred to as "[[Color|black]]"; [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was known to prefer black caf.<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref> One form of caf was described as "black and sweet".<ref name="STET!">{{StoryCite|story=STET!|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> Another variant was known as "cold brew" and served iced.<ref name="Docking">{{GEAttr|Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo}}</ref>

[[Ram Jomaram]] did not drink caf.<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> When, late one evening, [[Zare Leonis]] asked [[Auntie Nags]] for some caf, wanting something hot to drink, she refused his request as it would likely keep him awake, suggesting that he have [[hot chocolate]] with [[tang bark]] instead. He agreed, as this was the way he liked his hot chocolate.<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy" /> [[Bix Caleen]] and [[Timm Karlo]] had caf in the morning after spending the night together.<ref name="Andor 2">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}}</ref>

[[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Hera Syndulla]] each enjoyed a cup of [[Spiran caf]] together before Syndulla's mission to [[Fort Anaxes]], reminding Kanan of their time with one another on the [[moon]]s of [[Rion]].<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/out-of-darkness-trivia-gallery|text="Out of Darkness" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> [[Zuckuss]] once ordered a caf with lots of cream, while [[Parazeen Parapa]] ordered a black and sweet variant.<ref name="STET!" />

[[Lieutenant]] [[Braylen Stramm|Stramm]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] used to drink coffee.<ref name="Blade Squadron" /> [[Zaluna Myder]], the shift leader of [[Myder's Mynocks]] at [[Transcept Media Solutions]], considered herself a caf addict.<ref name="AND" /> The [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi]] found standard-issue Imperial caf disgusting and believed [[General]] [[Hurst Romodi]] thought the same upon seeing the [[Imperial officer]] put [[alcohol]] in his coffee.<ref name="Darth Vader (2020) 22">[[Darth Vader (2020) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 22]]</ref>

While at [[Cantham House]] with her father, [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], the day after the opening for the [[Apprentice Legislature]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] drank caf with her breakfast. When Bail pointed out that people said that drinking caf stunts your growth Leia responded by saying that her growth was already stunted and that if she was stuck being short she might as well have the caf causing her father to laugh out loud, making Leia laugh.<ref name="Leia">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> [[Cold Brew Black Caf]] and [[Cold Brew Black Caf with Taro Topper]] were served at [[Black Spire Outpost]].<ref name="Docking" />

Caf was sometimes used to describe a color, such as with the [[Caf-Colored Robes]] worn by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] during the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Caf first [[canon]]ically appeared in the [[2014]] novel [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']], written by [[John Jackson Miller]].<ref name="AND" /> Although coffee and caf had never been designated as the same thing in a narrative source at the time, Miller explained that he decided to use caf as a more exotic ''Star Wars'' equivalent of ''[[Wikipedia:Coffea arabica|Coffea arabica]]''.<ref name="blog">{{Faraway|url=fiction/books/swanewdawn.html|text=''A New Dawn'' production notes}}</ref> Later, the short stories "[[Blade Squadron (short story)|Blade Squadron]]" and "[[A Good Kiss]]" identified the two beverages as the same;<ref name="Blade Squadron" /><ref name="Good Kiss" /> unlike the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where [[Caf/Legends|caf]] and [[coffee]] are two different beverages.<ref name="Hasty Exit">{{WEGCite|story=Hasty Exit|book=Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook}}</ref><ref name="Children of the Jedi">[[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']]</ref>

In that continuity, caf first appeared in the short story "[[Hasty Exit]]" by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]], which was published in [[1996]] in [[West End Games]]' ''[[Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook]]''.<ref name="Hasty Exit" /> Coffee was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars (1977) 59|fifty-ninth issue]] of the [[1977]] [[Star Wars (1977)|Marvel ''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]], written by [[David Michelinie]] and [[Walter Simonson]]<ref name="Bazarre">[[Star Wars (1977) 59|''Star Wars'' (1977) 59]]</ref> and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[February 16]], [[1982]].<ref name="tcr">''[[Amazing Heroes]]'' #8, [[Wikipedia:The_Comic_Reader#On_the_Drawing_Board.2FThe_Comic_Reader|''The Comic Reader'']] #199 and [[Wikipedia:The Comics Journal|''The Comics Journal'']] #70</ref> It first appeared in the novel [[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']], written by [[Barbara Hambly]] and published in [[1995]].<ref name="Children of the Jedi" />

[[In-universe|Out-of-universe]], the breakfast menu for [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] describes Black Caf as "Regular coffee."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/breakfast|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Breakfast Menu}}</ref>

A 2014 non-canonical television special ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'' features the occupation of [[barista]], whose duties would include preparation and serving of the caf drinks.
