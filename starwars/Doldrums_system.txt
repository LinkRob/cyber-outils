The '''Doldrums system''' was a [[star system]] located in the unexplored reaches of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Doldrums system originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity and was included in the [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion]] appendix of systems,<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> but was excluded from the updated [[canon]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix that was released on [[June 24]], [[2025]], due to it being a codename. The appendix's author [[Jason Fry]] included it in a list of "unlisted" systems that are still tracked internally by [[Lucasfilm]] despite their exclusion from the apppendix,<ref name="Substack" /> and has stated that the systems should be treated as existing in the canon continuity.<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-08-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-08-31}}</ref>
