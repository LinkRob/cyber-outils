This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. The individual [[Bon]] was a member of this species.<ref name="What a Festive Evening" />

==Biology and appearance==
This bipedal [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] was distinguished by their [[Color|pale pink]] skin, white [[hair]], and pale [[eye]]s, with craggy lines and dark mottled spots covering most of their body.<ref name="What a Festive Evening" />

==Species in the galaxy==
By [[3 BBY]], a member of this species, [[Bon]], served as a proprietor of the [[Niki-Bon Travel Agency]] along with the [[human]] [[Niki]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="What a Festive Evening" />

==Behind the scenes==
The species made their debut in the [[Harvest|sixth episode]] of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'',<ref name="What a Festive Evening" /> directed by [[Ariel Kleiman]] and aired on [[April 29]], [[2025]]<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref> The species was designed by [[Wikipedia:Artist|artist]] [[Tom Blake]].<ref name="Blake Concept">{{WebCite|url=https://www.tomblakedesign.com/design/andor2|text=STAR WARS ANDOR SEASON 2|work=[[Tom Blake]]'s [https://www.tomblakedesign.com official website]|archivedate=20250623003358}}</ref>
