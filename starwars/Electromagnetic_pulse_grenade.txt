An '''electromagnetic pulse grenade (EMP) grenade''', also called a '''droid popper''',<ref name="Droid Popper">{{Databank|droid-popper|Droid Popper}}</ref> was a type of [[grenade]] that utilized [[Electromagnetic pulse|electromagnetic emissions]]. They could be used to disable a [[battle droid]]'s circuits<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know" /> or to incapacitate [[Human|humans]].<ref name="Common Ground" /><ref name="The Outpost" /> When used against [[stormtrooper]]s,<ref name="War-Mantle">{{TBB|War-Mantle}}</ref> it overloaded their [[armor]] electronics,<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref> though its effectiveness depended on distance and placement relative to the target. They were used by the [[Jedi]] and their [[clone trooper]]s against the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Droid Popper" />

==Behind the scenes==
Electro Magnetic Pulse grenades first appeared in ''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel written by [[Dave Wolverton]]<ref name="The Courtship of Princess Leia">''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]''</ref> that was released on [[April 1]], [[1994]].<ref name="The Courtship of Princess Leia release date">{{Amazon|url=Courtship-Princess-Leia-Star-Wars/dp/0553089285|text=The Courtship of Princess Leia}}</ref> They became [[canon]] when they appeared in "[[Duel of the Droids]]," the seventh episode of the canon animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]],<ref name="Duel of the Droids" /> which originally aired on [[November 14]], [[2008]].<ref name="Duel of the Droids air date">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode007.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Duel of the Droids}}</ref>
