'''Cartao''' was an [[Expansion Region]] astronomical object where the [[Galactic Empire]] kept a [[Cartao Imperial facility|facility]] that became under threat from a potential [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|dissident]] attack in [[14 BBY]]. It was also the site of [[Battle of Cartao|fighting]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], involving the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]].

==Description==
Cartao was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Tarkin" /> located in an [[Cartao system|eponymous star system]] in the [[Prackla sector]] within the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was situated at the coordinates O-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" />

==History==
{{Quote|Our main concerns are Lantillies, from which we have already repositioned many of our resources. Also, the Imperial facility on Cartao, and Ice Station Beta on Anteevy.|Dodd Rancit, to Sheev Palpatine|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Cartao was the site of an [[Cartao Imperial facility| Imperial facility]]. When a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|group]] of dissidents [[Mission to Murkhana|stole]] the Imperial [[corvette]] ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' from [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and used it to [[Attack on Galidraan Station|attack]] [[Galidraan Station]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> in [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Cartao and its Imperial facility, along with [[Lantillies]] and [[Ice Station Beta]] on [[Anteevy]], were listed as theorized targets by [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]] of [[Naval Intelligence Agency|Naval Intelligence]] when he was questioned on the situation by [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] [[Battle of Cartao|fought]] on Cartao.<ref name="TC" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> when the [[company]] was [[Siege of Inyusu Tor|stranded]] on the manufacturing [[planet]] [[Sullust]], the tentative [[Captain]] [[Hazram Namir]] asked [[Vifra]], the head of [[engineer]]ing, whether she had been present for the fighting on Cartao. Vifra replied that she had only joined six [[Standard month|months]] prior, and so Namir told her that he'd tell her about Cartao if she helped keep the 61st alive.<ref name="TC" />
