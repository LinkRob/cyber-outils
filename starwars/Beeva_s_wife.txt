A [[human]]<ref name="One Year Later" /> [[Gender|woman]] was the '''wife'''<ref name="One Year Later AD" /> of the human [[Beeva]], who served as a [[senator]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. In [[4 BBY]], the couple attended the [[Wedding of Stekan Sculdun and Leida Mothma|wedding]] of the [[Chandrilan]]s [[Leida Mothma]] and [[Stekan Sculdun]]. On the first [[Local day|day]] of the three-[[Standard day|day]] event, they arrived with Beeva at the [[Mothma Estate]] on<ref name="One Year Later" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|chandrila|Chandrila}}</ref> [[Chandrila]], carrying a gift for the [[Marriage|wedding]].<ref name="One Year Later" />

As they walked towards the estate, Leida's [[Parent|mother]], Senator [[Mon Mothma]], greeted the senator as she walked past the couple. Beeva's wife returned Mon's greeting with a nod before continuing into the estate. They had dark skin and [[color|black]] [[hair]]. When they arrived at the estate, they were wearing a yellow and green [[clothing|outfit]] with gold-colored [[Piercing|earrings]].<ref name="One Year Later" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beeva's wife appeared in "[[One Year Later]]," the first episode of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the live-action series [[Star Wars: Andor|''Andor'']], which was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="One Year Later" /> on [[April 22]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref> The scene in which Beeva's wife appeared<ref name="One Year Later" /> was filmed as a "[[Wikipedia:Long take|oner]]" and was the final visual effects shot filmed for the season, completed in collaboration between [[Industrial Light & Magic]] and [[Scanline VFX]] on [[August 5]], [[2024]].<ref name="Declassified">{{SW|url=news/andor-trivia-guide-week-4|text=Secrets from the Set of ''Andor'' Season 2: Week 4 Trivia}}</ref>
