'''2005 Topps ''Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith''''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] in [[April]] [[2005]] to coincide with the then-upcoming [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Topps site" />

The former Collecting section of [[StarWars.com]] featured first looks at Artist Sketch Cards by [[Kilian Plunkett]], [[Tom Hodges]], [[Dan Parsons]],<ref name="SW First Look">{{SWArchive|url=collecting/news/topps/news20050111.html|text=First Look: Topps Episode III Artist Sketch Cards}}</ref> [[Joe Corroney]],<ref name="SW Update 1">{{SWArchive|url=collecting/news/topps/news20050125.html|text=Topps ''Revenge of the Sith'' Sketch Card Gallery Update}} {{C|1/9}}</ref> [[Cat Staggs]],<ref name="SW Update 2">{{SWArchive|url=collecting/news/topps/news20050311.html|text=Topps ''Revenge of the Sith'' Sketch Card Gallery Update}} {{C|2/9}}</ref> [[Monte Moore]],<ref name="SW Update 3">{{SWArchive|url=collecting/news/topps/news20050324.html|text=Topps ''Revenge of the Sith'' Sketch Card Gallery Update}} {{C|3/9}}</ref> [[Grant Gould]],<ref name="SW Update 4">{{SWArchive|url=collecting/news/topps/news20050328.html|text=Topps ''Revenge of the Sith'' Sketch Card Gallery Update}} {{C|4/9}}</ref> [[Robin Pronovost]],<ref name="SW Update 5">{{SWArchive|url=collecting/news/topps/news20050404.html|text=Topps ''Revenge of the Sith'' Sketch Card Gallery Update}} {{C|5/9}}</ref> [[Chris Trevas]],<ref name="SW Update 6">{{SWArchive|url=collecting/news/topps/news20050412.html|text=Topps ''Revenge of the Sith'' Sketch Card Gallery Update}} {{C|6/9}}</ref> [[Otis Frampton]],<ref name="SW Update 7">{{SWArchive|url=collecting/news/topps/news20050427.html|text=Topps ''Revenge of the Sith'' Sketch Card Gallery Update}} {{C|7/9}}</ref> [[Justin Chung]], [[David Rabbitte]],<ref name="SW Update 8">{{SWArchive|url=collecting/news/topps/news20050503.html|text=Topps ''Revenge of the Sith'' Sketch Card Gallery Update}} {{C|8/9}}</ref> [[Matt Busch]], [[Sarah Wilkinson]], and [[Jeff Chandler]].<ref name="SW Update 9">{{SWArchive|url=collecting/news/topps/news20050510.html|text=Topps ''Revenge of the Sith'' Sketch Card Gallery Update}} {{C|9/9}}</ref>
