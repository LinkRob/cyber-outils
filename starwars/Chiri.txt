'''Chiri''' was the name given by the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]] to an [[Color|orange]]-[[Hair|furred]] [[handsy]] that lived on the [[jungle]] [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Wevo]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. In [[232 BBY]], while Cantaros was [[Wevo crisis|stranded]] on the moon among a group of others, the handsy jumped onto him and began riding on Cantaros' shoulders as he foraged for [[fruit]]. The Padawan became connected to the handsy and subsequently decided to name him Chiri.

The following [[Local day|day]], when Cantaros and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] investigated a [[disturbance in the Force]], Chiri accompanied them inside the Padawan's [[Clothing|tunic]]. When they discovered the source of the disturbance, Chiri ran into a [[Nihil]] [[Starship|cargo hauler]] before being [[Death|killed]] by the [[pirate]] [[Klinith Da]]. The handsy's death further fueled Cantaros' anger&mdash;as he was already grieving the [[Attack on the Steady Wing|loss]] of his [[Jedi Master]], [[Douglas Sunvale]]&mdash;and he sought revenge for both deaths. He came close to falling to the [[dark side of the Force]] before being pulled back by Rwoh.

==Biography==
===Bonding with Imri Cantaros===
[[File:Imri.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Chiri befriended Imri Cantaros (pictured) while he was stranded on Wevo]]
The [[handsy]] who later became known as Chiri [[Homeworld|lived]] on [[Wevo]], a [[jungle]] [[moon]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> as the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]] was [[Wevo crisis|stranded]] on Wevo along with [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]], the [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]] and her [[nanny droid]] [[J-6]], and the [[Dalnan]] [[Gender|boy]] [[Honesty Weft]], he and the group searched the jungle for [[fruit]]. While doing so, the handsy jumped from a [[tree]] onto Cantaros' [[Limb|head]], and despite his efforts, the Padawan was unable to escape, which caused his companions to all laugh. Deciding that he liked Cantaros better than his [[Plant|tree]], the handsy rode around on the Padawan's shoulders for the rest of the [[Local day|day]]. At one point, the handsy used Cantaros as a jumping off point to get fruit from the tree's highest branches.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

Afterward, as the handsy began to chew on Cantoros' [[Padawan braid]], Weft asked the Padawan if he planned to name the creature, to which Cantaros replied that he planned to call him Chiri. Starros then asked if the name was a result of it rhyming with the Padawan's own name, noting they were a "wild" fruit picking team. When the group returned to their [[cave]] where they were staying while stranded on the moon, Chiri accompanied them and curled up inside Cantaros' [[Clothing|tunic]] while the other handsies went back to their hidey-holes. While [[sleep]]ing, Rwoh noted that Chiri occasionally made a sleepy chirp.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

===Taken too soon===
{{Quote|[Chiri chirps]|Chiri chirps in pain after being shot|audio=ChiriScreams-ATestOfCourage.flac|<ref name="A Test of Courage" />}}
[[File:Klinith_Da.png|thumb|left|250px|Klinith Da (pictured) shot Chiri when he entered the Nihil's ship]]
The following [[Standard day|day]], after Cantaros and Rwoh agreed that they should investigate a [[disturbance in the Force]] they had both felt, the Padawan put his [[Imri's lightsaber|lightsaber]] together and put it in his [[holster]]. In response, Chiri squeaked in annoyance but remained inside of the tunic, with the Jedi Knight noting that the handsy had decided the disturbance was a worthy compensation for a nice place to nap. Cantaros and Rwoh then left the cave to investigate the disturbance and Chiri accompanied them, remaining inside of the Padawan's tunic.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

When the two Jedi discovered the source of the disturbance, a [[Nihil]] [[Starship|cargo hauler]] that contained two [[pirate]]s&mdash;[[Klinith Da]] and [[Gwishi]]&mdash;they listened to their conversation as the Nihil talked about their next steps. After Rwoh reached a hiding spot, she motioned for Cantaros to follow, but all of a sudden Chiri jumped out from inside of the Padawan's tunic and made his way toward the ship. Cantaros stood to run after the handsy but was unable to prevent him from entering the hauler. Before the two Jedi could follow Chiri into the ship, they heard<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> Da shout that the handsy would not eat her [[Foodstuff|food]] and shoot at him.<ref name="A Test of Courage audiobook">[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage (audiobook)|''The High Republic: A Test of Courage'' audiobook]]</ref> Chiri chirped in pain before his body was hurled out of the hauler.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

Cantaros then made his way to the handsy, and whispered Chiri's name, noting that he was not moving. Rwoh, meanwhile, sensed that the [[living Force]] had left the handsy's body and pulled the Padawan away before they were shot themselves. Cantaros suggested they go after the Nihil but the Knight, sensing his anger, insisted that they return to their cave so they could plan their next steps.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|Did they hurt Chiri?''"<br />"''Yes.|Honesty Weft and Imri Cantaros|<ref name="A Test of Courage" />}}
[[File:Vernestra_Rwoh_ToC_Updated.png|thumb|right|250px|Vernestra Rwoh (pictured) prevented Cantaros from falling to the dark side as the Padawan sought revenge for Chiri's passing.]]
When the Jedi returned to the cave, Weft asked them where Chiri was, to which Cantaros responded that he was gone. Noticing the Padawan was downcast, Starros mentally noted that she knew he meant that the handsy was dead rather than having returned to the jungle. After talking about the discovery of the source of the disturbance, the Dalnan asked if the two Nihil had hurt Chiri. In a flat and emotionless voice, Cantaros stated they had hurt him, which left Starros feeling she wanted to hug him.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

The Padawan later mentally reflected on Chiri, noting that it was not fair that Chiri had perished after the two connected. Already grieving the loss of his [[Jedi Master]]&mdash;[[Douglas Sunvale]], who had been killed in an [[Attack on the Steady Wing|attack]] by the same pair of Nihil&mdash;Cantaros decided to take revenge on them, almost falling to the [[dark side of the Force]]. He was prevented from doing so, however, by Rwoh, and the two Nihil were taken [[prisoner]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Mission to Disaster date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> when he befriended the [[tooka]] [[cat]] [[Gemmy]] at the [[Dalna temple|Jedi temple]] on<ref name="Mission to Disaster">''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]''</ref> the Outer Rim<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> [[planet]] [[Dalna]], Cantaros thought of Chiri and the handsy's sad demise. He then pushed the memory to the side and tried to focus on the present.<ref name="Mission to Disaster" />

==Personality and traits==
Chiri had [[Color|orange]] [[Hair|fur]]. Jedi Knight Vernestra Rwoh considered Chiri to be tenacious and cute. Chiri was unbothered by Wevo's [[acid rain]]. After the handsy's death, Cantaros reflected on it and considered it unfair that Chiri was killed after he had trusted the Padawan so easily.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />
