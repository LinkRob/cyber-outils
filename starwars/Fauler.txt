'''Agent Fauler''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] member of the [[Republic Strategic Information Service]] during the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]] between the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Sith Empire]].

==Biography==
In [[3643 BBY]], he was stationed on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] and used [[N0-HX]] to contact a Republic-allied individual for their help. Fauler believed that the [[Czerka Arms/Legends|Czerka Corporation]] had a doomsday device, but they didn't build or test a device on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]]. The individual discovered that instead, they built their facility around an artifact that was soon discovered by the Republic.<ref name="TOR" />
