'''Ann Gella''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] [[Homeworld|from]] the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] who was [[Slavery|enslaved]] with her [[twin]] sister [[Tann Gella]] and sold to the [[Dug]] [[Podracer]] [[Sebulba]] by [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]. The twins accompanied Sebulba to the planet [[Tatooine]] when he competed in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] there in [[32 BBY]], and Ann manicured his toenails before the [[Podracing|race]].

==Biography==
Ann Gella was a [[Gender|female]] [[Rutian]] [[Twi'lek]]<ref name="Force Collection" /> [[Homeworld|from]] the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]].<ref name="Geektionary French" /> She and her [[twin]] sister, [[Tann Gella]], were [[Slavery|enslaved]] and purchased as property by the [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Sebulba]] from the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]].<ref name="Force Collection" /> The [[racer]] used them as [[yobana]]s and they also caught the attention of other podracers, including [[Teemto Pagalies]] and [[Mars Guo]].<ref name="DieWannaWanga">{{SW|url=news/die-wanna-wanga-encounters-of-the-twilek-kind|text=Die Wanna Wanga: Encounters of The Twi'lek Kind|nolive=1}}</ref> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}} to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the twins were with Sebulba on the planet [[Tatooine]] where he planned to take part in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] race out of<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Mos Espa]] [[spaceport]].<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref>

Prior to the start of the race, the twins helped the Dug prepare, with Ann<ref name="TPM" /> manicuring<ref name="Geektionary" /> his toenails and Tann massaging his shoulders while he sat with his [[Sebulba's Podracer|podracer]] in the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]]'s [[hangar]].<ref name="TPM" /> Sebulba enjoyed the ministrations of the two comely masseuses,<ref name="DB">{{Databank|url=sebulba-biography-gallery|title=Sebulba Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> although the massage was only a cover for the Dug to guard his vehicle from race officials and other pilots.<ref name="CL">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> 

==Personality and traits==
Ann Gella had [[Color|blue]] skin and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="Force Collection" /> She was considered beautiful.<ref name="DieWannaWanga" />

==Equipment==
Gella and her sister wore matching yellow net [[Clothing|outfits]] with a gray headpiece and a black collar.<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:SebulbaGellaTwinMassage-TPM.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Sebulba lounges in a chair in the Mos Espa hangar as Ann Gella sits in front of him and works on his toenails and Tann Gella stands behind him and massages his shoulders.|Ann Gella (left) appeared in ''The Phantom Menace''.]]
Ann Gella was portrayed by actress [[Nifa Hindes]] in the film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']],<ref name="Blog">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-a-family-affair|text=''Star Wars'', A Family Affair|nolive=1}}</ref> which was released on [[May 19]], [[1999]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Nifa acted alongside her real life twin sister [[Nishan Hindes]], who played Tann Gella in the same scene,<ref name="Blog" /> which was filmed on B Stage at [[Leavesden Studios]].<ref name="365Days">''[[Creating the Worlds of Star Wars: 365 Days]]''</ref>

In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Ann Gella was first named in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile card game]] ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'',<ref name="Force Collection" /> which initially launched in [[2013]]. Although the launch of ''Force Collection'' predates<ref name="Force Collection release">{{SW|url=news/announcing-star-wars-force-collection|text=Announcing Star Wars: Force Collection|nolive=1}}</ref> the ''Star Wars'' canon reset of [[2014]],<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]] confirmed that the game was kept updated to fit with canon.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|holocronkeeper|status/846807700482666496|[[Leland Chee]]|date=2017-03-28|quote=I'm not gonna go through the trouble of changing them and I don't have any reason to believe currently that anyone would. [&hellip;] On the off-chance that we DID have a current need to change any of these, we would have for Force Collection.|archivedate=20210505080831}} &mdash; [[Leland Chee]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] has confirmed that [[in-universe]] information from ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' conforms with ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]].</ref> The name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in ''[[Star Wars Episode I Who's Who: A Pocket Guide to the Characters of The Phantom Menace]]'', a reference book written by [[Ryder Windham]]<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and released on [[April 21]], 1999.<ref name="Amazon">{{Amazon|url=Star-Wars-Episode-Whos-Who/dp/0762405198|text=Star Wars Episode I Who's Who: A Pocket Guide To The Characters In The Phantom Menace}}</ref>
