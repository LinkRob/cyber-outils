The '''Core Five''', also known as the '''Council of Five''', was the ruling body of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] on the [[planet]] [[Scipio]].<ref name="Clu Lesser" /> The Council was arrested after being charged with corruption, allowing [[Senator]] [[Rush Clovis]] to assume total control of the Banking Clan.<ref name="The Rise of Clovis" /> [[Clu Lesser]] served as a spokesperson for the council during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="An Old Friend" />
