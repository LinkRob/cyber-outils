'''Ardennia''' was an [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Ardennian]] [[species]]. The [[human]] [[Juronus Opto]] and the [[Wayuning]] [[Topi Topi]] were also native to that world, and the inhabitants of Ardennia held repeated events known as [[Mynock roast]]s. The latter fact was known to the Ardennian [[pilot]] and [[thief]] [[Rio Durant]], who hailed from Ardennia as well.

==Description==
Ardennia was a terrestrial<ref name="Solo" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Bestiary">''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]''</ref> located in the [[Ardennia system]] of the [[Fath sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> a part of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="GalaxyMap" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> Situated within grid square K-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was connected<ref name="GalaxyMap" /> via the [[Entralla Route]] [[hyperspace route]]<ref name="Entralla" /> to the [[Carlac system]] as well as the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim's]] [[Orinda system]].<ref name="GalaxyMap" /> Ardennia also shared its name with the [[Ardennian Guild of Technicians]] that was active during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="RisingStorm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:Rio_Durant_SoloTOCE.png|thumb|left|300px|Ardennia was the homeworld of the Ardennians, including Rio Durant (pictured).]]
Ardennia's [[Homeworld|native]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]], the [[Ardennian]]s,<ref name="AlienArchive" /> were already known off-world<ref name="QFPX">''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]''</ref> by [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> [[Rio Durant]], an Ardennian [[pilot]] and [[thief]] who had served with the [[Galactic Republic]]-aligned [[Freedom's Sons]] [[army]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="SoloGuide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> of [[22 BBY]]&ndash;[[19 BBY]],<ref name="SWT">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> hailed from Ardennia.<ref name="SoloGuide" />

In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="SWT" /> during an attempted [[Heist on Vandor|theft]]<ref name="Solo" /> of a shipment of valuable refined [[coaxium]] from a [[Galactic Empire]] [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport]] on the planet [[Vandor]],<ref name="SoloGuide" /> the [[human]] [[Tobias Beckett]], the leader of the [[Beckett's gang|crew]] of criminals that Durant was a part of, excitedly declared that their upcoming haul would render that [[Standard day|day]] the best of the Ardennian's life. In response, Durant quipped that he was not sure about that since Beckett had never participated in a [[Mynock roast]] event on Ardennia.<ref name="Solo" />

Ardennia fell within the borders of the [[New Republic]]<ref name="MandoVG" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="MandoVGDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> It was the homeworld of the human [[Juronus Opto]] and the [[Wayuning]] [[Topi Topi]], which was noted in the postings of [[Bounty|bounties]] issued for them by the [[time]] of the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the planet [[Batuu]]<ref name="Datapad">{{DatapadCite|''[[Star Wars: Batuu Bounty Hunters]]''|BatuuBountyHunters-JuronusOpto.png|BatuuBountyHunters-TopiTopi.png}}</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GEDates">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which includes those of ''[[Star Wars: Batuu Bounty Hunters]]'', to [[34 ABY]].</ref> At some point, Ardennia was also mentioned in an entry of an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]] dedicated to the Ardennians. The entry was included in a section of the [[Book|work]] describing sentient species that originated in [[Water|wet]] habitats. The journal was restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="AlienArchive" /> at some point no earlier than 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref> In addition, the Mynock roasts of Ardennia were noted in the [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]] of the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] [[cartographer]] [[Ardis San Tekka]],<ref name="Bestiary" /> which was published at some point no earlier than [[35 ABY]].<ref name="35 ABY">{{BestiaryVol1Date}}</ref>

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|Mynock roasts are a popular form of entertainment on planets such as Ardennia ''[&hellip;]''|Ardis San Tekka's journal|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
[[File:TopiTopi-SWBH.png|thumb|right|220px|The Wayuning Topi Topi, a native of Ardennia]]
Ardennia was the homeworld of the Ardennian species, members of which had four [[Limb|arms]] as well as prehensile toes on their two feet.<ref name="AlienArchive" /> The natives of the planet also included the human Juronus Opto and the Wayuning Topi Topi.<ref name="Datapad" /> Ardennia's inhabitants repeatedly held events known as Mynock roasts, which the Ardennian Rio Durant once described as "nuts."<ref name="Solo" /> The events involved the [[Foodstuff|cooking]] of [[mynock]]s<ref name="Bestiary" />&mdash;[[Diet|edible]], [[silicon]]-based, ergovorous creatures<ref name="AlienArchive" /> that originated from the planet [[Ord Mynock]]<ref name="SWGE">{{GEAttr|Creature Stall}}</ref> and could survive in deep [[Realspace|space]] as well as in planetary surface conditions.<ref name="AlienArchive" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ardennia was first mentioned in the [[2018]] ''[[Star Wars Anthology Series]]'' film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" />
