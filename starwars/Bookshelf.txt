{{Quote|Beginning with this issue, ''Star Wars Insider'' introduces a new column dedicated to the universe of ''Star Wars'' books, both fiction and non-fiction.|The inaugural edition of "Bookshelf"|<ref name="Bookshelf 44">{{InsiderCite|44|Bookshelf}}</ref>}}
"'''Bookshelf'''", written by [[Jason Fry]], was a department in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine. It showcased both new and classic works of ''Star Wars'' [[List of books|literature]], including novels, young adult novels, and reference books, and it frequently included excerpts from interviews with authors.

The column was first featured in [[Star Wars Insider 44|''Star Wars Insider'' 44]] under the title '''''Star Wars'' Bookshelf''', with "''[[Star Wars]]''" dropped from its title from [[Star Wars Insider 50|Issue 50]] onwards. Initially, the column appeared in every other issue of ''Insider'', rotating with a comics column called "[[The Horse's Mouth|Straight from the Horse's Mouth]]."

"Bookshelf" was renamed "[[Jedi Library (department)|Jedi Library]]" with [[Star Wars Insider 77|Issue 77]] as part of [[IDG Entertainment]]'s overhaul of the magazine.
