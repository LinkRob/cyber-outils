The '''Calrissian family''', also known as the '''Calrissians''',<ref name="Battlefront II" /> was the family of [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]], the [[human]] [[smuggler]] who became a [[general]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] considered the lives of the Calrissian family to be a tragic story during the Galactic Civil War.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

The family consisted of [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]], his [[Lando Calrissian's father|father]] and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian's mother|mother]],<ref name="Solo" /> and his daughter [[Kadara Calrissian]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ign.com/articles/star-wars-shadows-sith-novel-excerpt-luke-skywalker-lando|text='Star Wars: Shadow of the Sith' - Exclusive Excerpt From the Episode IX Prequel Novel|archivedate=20220610211611}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Calrissian family first appeared through [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]] in the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]].<ref name="Episode V" />

The first attempt to expand on Lando Calrissian's family was during development of the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] in the form of Lando's father, who was to be named "Grando Calrissian" and slated to be portrayed by [[Samuel L. Jackson]]. However, the character was scrapped when Jackson instead opted to sign to play [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] for the [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="Star Wars Insider 131">[[Star Wars Insider 131|''Star Wars Insider'' 131]]</ref>

An early version of the script from the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' had [[Korso]] as the older brother of Lando Calrissian.<ref name="Jon's Twitter 2">{{Twitter|JonKasdan|status/1168287139848773639|[[Jon Kasdan]]|quote=KORSO. The muscle in Beckett's crew who died in battle shortly after they crash-landed on Mimban. Korso was in every draft of the script (including an early version where he was Lando's big brother) and every cut of the movie 'til just before lock when he was removed to simplify.|archivedate=20200421172446}}</ref>
