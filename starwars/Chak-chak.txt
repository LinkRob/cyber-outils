The '''chak-chak''' were small, six-[[limb]]ed [[rodent]]s that were popular pets on their [[homeworld]], the [[planet]] [[Chad]]. Their two arms were long and deceptively strong, folding away beneath their long [[Hair|fur]], and they moved at great speed on the four smaller legs. Shortly before the [[Clone Wars]], the Chad-based company [[Rebaxan Columni]] created the [[MSE-4]], [[MSE-5]], and [[MSE-6 series repair droid]]s all inspired by the chak-chak. Although most potential customers were unconvinced by the prospect of owning a mechanical [[rat]], the [[Galactic Empire]] eventually bought the company's entire remaining stock of MSE-6s.

==Biology and appearance==
The chak-chak were a diminutive [[mouse]]-like [[rodent]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Chad]],<ref name="Labor" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Chadra-Fan">{{Databank|chadra-fan|Chadra-Fan}}</ref> homeworld of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Chadra-Fan]] [[species]]. The creatures had six [[limb|limbs]], four smaller legs and two long and deceptively strong [[Limb|arms]] that could fold up to be partially concealed by the chak-chak's long [[Hair|fur]]. Due to their great sense of balance, chak-chak could reach impressive speeds on their four legs, resembling a furry [[torpedo]] with no obvious sense of propulsion.<ref name="Labor" />

==History==
[[File:KismaUttersondHouse-THRTrailOfShadows4.jpg|thumb|right|300px|alt=The brown-furred Chadra-Fan Doctor Kisma Uttersond stands in the foreground with his wife Moonanoo stood in the doorway of their home on Chad in the background.|Chak-chak were native to Chad, homeworld of the Chadra-Fan.]]
When the Chad-based [[droid]] manufacturer [[Rebaxan Columni]] decided to create a general, multi-purpose droid for the [[The galaxy|galactic]] market shortly before the [[Clone Wars]] broke out<ref name="Labor" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> they chose the chak-chak as inspiration. Based on the rodent, they created the experimental [[MSE-4]] and [[MSE-5]] models and then billions of the [[MSE-6 series repair droid]], not even researching the reaction to the first two models due to the Rebaxan executives' confidence in the product. Like a chak-chak, MSE-6s had two long arms that could fold away and the droids moved around rapidly on four wheels.<ref name="Labor" />

Despite chak-chak being popular pets on Chad and the company's 400-[[sector]] marketing campaign, potential customers were unconvinced about owning a mechanical [[rat]] and so Rebaxan Columni nearly went bankrupt. The [[Galactic Empire]] then purchased the remaining stock of the chak-chak-inspired droids for use on the [[Starship|vessels]] of its [[Imperial Navy|Imperial Fleet]].<ref name="Labor" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], chak-chak were mentioned in "[[Labor, Repair and Miscellaneous Droids]]," a booklet in the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series published by [[De Agostini]]<ref name="Labor" /> on [[June 29]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref> The [[Pleeky|creatures]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in the [[The Official Star Wars Fact File 19|nineteenth issue]] of De Agostini's ''[[The Official Star Wars Fact File]]'' magazine, which was written by [[Iain Lowson]] and [[Jim Swallow]]<ref name="FF19">{{FactFile|19|MOU 1-2|MSE-6 'Mouse Droid'}}</ref> and published on [[May 8]], [[2002]].<ref name="Date">{{FFdates|19}}</ref> The chak-chak were then additionally identified as the pleeky in ''[[The New Essential Guide to Droids]]'', a [[2006]] reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="NEGtD">''[[The New Essential Guide to Droids]]''</ref>
