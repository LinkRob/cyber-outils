{{Quote|Move a little further out from the Galactic Core and you'll find the Colonies. Planets in this ring-shaped region may not get the same level of recognition as their counterparts in the Core, but they really aren't all that different when you boil it down. They're still situated on major hyperspace trade routes, givin' the wealthy folks who live on these worlds access to anythin' they could ever want.|[[Strono Tuggs]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]''|<ref name="TUC">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref>}}
The '''Colonies''' was a region of [[the galaxy]] located between the [[Core Worlds]] and the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]. It contained numerous [[planet]]s, including [[Abednedo (planet)|Abednedo]], [[Carida]], and [[Uquine]]. The Colonies sat along major trade routes, and some of them were extremely wealthy.

==Description==
The Colonies was a ring-shaped region of [[the galaxy]] that sat between the [[Core Worlds]] and the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]], meaning it was considered part of the "inner [[planet]]s." Important [[hyperspace route]]s made the worlds of the Colonies hubs for education, fashion, finance, and technology.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==History==
===Galactic Republic===
The Inner Rim Territories were colonized by explorers who hailed from the Colonies and Core Worlds.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The Colonies, along with the Core Worlds, were also overpopulated during the [[High Republic Era]]. Thus, poorer individuals from the Colonies decided to move to the [[Outer Rim Territories]] to make new lives for themselves and their families.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

[[File:CNCityBridges-SS.png|thumb|left|250px|Cato Neimoidia's bridge cities]]
Ten [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Clone Wars]], a group of [[Halcyon usurpers|usurpers]] captured the [[planet]]ary [[Politics|government]] of the Colonies world of [[Halcyon (planet)|Halcyon]] to protest the planet's membership of the [[Galactic Republic]]. After failed negotiations with the usurpers, the Republic [[Mission to Halcyon|dispatched]] the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Judicial Department]] to deal with the usurpers.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> The Colonies planet of [[Castell]]<ref name="Lando 1">[[Lando 1|''Lando'' 1]]</ref> was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Gossam]] [[Shu Mai]], who later became [[Presidente]] of the [[Commerce Guild]] during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

The Colonies were also home to several [[Neimoidian]] [[Purse world|Purse Worlds]],<ref name="BMF 31" /> including [[Cato Neimoidia]]<ref name="Cato Neimoidia">{{Databank|cato-neimoidia|Cato Neimoidia}}</ref> and [[Narq]]. The Neimoidians [[Slavery|enslaved]] the [[Narquois]] [[species]] and kept them in servitude for several [[Standard century|centuries]].<ref name="Aliens">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref>

===Clone Wars===
<div style="float: right">[[File:Temple_of_Eedit.png|thumb|225px|The Jedi Temple of Eedit]]</div>
During the Clone Wars, a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] and [[Trade Federation]] [[fleet]] led by [[Cad Bane]] [[Battle of Devaron|attacked]] the [[New Republic outpost|Republic outpost]] on the Colonies planet of [[Devaron]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> and captured the [[Jedi Master]] [[Bolla Ropal]]. Bane tried to force Ropal to unlock a [[holocron]] he had [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|stolen]] from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. Despite a rescue mission mounted by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], and [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]],<ref name="Cargo of Doom">{{TCW|Cargo of Doom}}</ref> Ropal was [[Death|killed]] and Bane managed to steal the [[Jedi holocron|holocron]] and a [[Kyber memory crystal]].<ref name="Children of the Force">{{TCW|Children of the Force}}</ref>

At the request of the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]], [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] undertook a [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia|mission]] to Cato Neimoidia in [[21 BBY]]<ref name="CN Date 1">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the [[Zillo Beast incident]] and the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]] in [[21 BBY]]. As the episode this event takes place within takes place between the events of the episodes the other events are attached to according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, this event must also take place 21 BBY.</ref> to [[spy]] on Senator [[Rush Clovis]], who was secretly colluding with the Trade Federation and the Separatists. During the mission, Amidala obtained a [[hologram disc]] which proved that Clovis had dealings with the Separatists, who were planning to build a new [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|droid foundry]] on [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Senate Spy">{{TCW|Senate Spy}}</ref>

In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Devaron Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of "[[Monster]]," which include the [[Devaron]] massacre, to [[20 BBY]].</ref> [[Count]] [[Dooku]] [[Devaron massacre|sent]] [[Savage Opress]] to Devaron to take out the [[Jedi]] [[Temple of Eedit]] in order to test his prowess. Savage killed the Jedi Master [[Halsey]], his Padawan [[Knox]], and several [[clone trooper]]s. Satisfied, Dooku took Opress as his new [[Sith apprentice]] in his plot to overthrow [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Monster">{{TCW|Monster}}</ref> Later, the Republic [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]] and his [[D-Squad]] including [[R2-D2]] [[Carida incident|foiled]] a Separatist plot to use a captured [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] to disrupt a [[Republic strategy conference]] being held in the [[Carida system]].<ref name="Point of No Return">{{TCW|Point of No Return (The Clone Wars)}}</ref>

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="CN Date 2">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the [[Battle of Mandalore|Battle of Sundari]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s departure from the [[Jedi Order]] to [[19 BBY]]. As the episode this event takes place within takes place between the events of the episodes the other events are attached to according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, this event must take place in 19 BBY.</ref> Skywalker and Tano took part in the [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|defense]] of Cato Neimoidia against the Separatists before being recalled to Coruscant to investigate a [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bombing]] at the [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref>

Following the disappearance of the [[Dagoyan Order|Dagoyan Masters]] on the Colonies world of [[Bardotta]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> [[Queen]] [[Julia]] [[Diplomatic mission to Bardotta|enlisted]] the help of [[Junior Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] and Master [[Mace Windu]] in rescuing the masters. Binks and Windu discovered the existence of a secret society called the [[Frangawl Cult]] which worshiped the [[demon]] [[Malmourral]].<ref name="Disappeared Part I">{{TCW|The Disappeared, Part I}}</ref> After the Frangawl Cult kidnapped Julia, Binks and Windu [[Mission to Zardossa Stix|pursued]] her kidnappers offworld to [[Zardossa Stix]] where they stopped a plot to revive the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]].<ref name="Disappeared Part II">{{TCW|The Disappeared, Part II}}</ref>

Following the initiation of [[Order 66]], Jedi Master [[Plo Koon]]'s [[Starfighter|fighter]] was shot down after the [[Battle of Cato Neimoidia]]. Shortly later, the Republic was [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] into the [[Galactic Empire]], which [[Great Jedi Purge|exterminated]] the Jedi and defeated the Separatists.<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>

===Imperial Era===
[[File:ZareLeonis-FFp79.png|thumb|left|175px|Zare Leonis was born on Uquine]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Imperial cadet]] and later rebel informant [[Zare Leonis]] was [[Reproduction|born]] on the planet [[Uquine]]. Due to his [[parent]]s' work as agricultural [[scientist]]s, Leonis lived on several offworld locations including the [[space station]] [[Hosk Station]] and the planet [[Lothal]]. By [[5 BBY]],<ref name="RitR Date">''Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks'' takes place concurrently to "[[Breaking Ranks]]," which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[5 BBY]].</ref> the planet Carida was known to host an [[Carida Academy|Imperial Academy]] for senior [[cadet]]s.<ref name="RITR">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref>

Following the [[Battle of Yavin]], the Jedi and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] starfighter [[pilot]] [[Luke Skywalker]] visited the Jedi Temple of Eedit on Devaron. He was subsequently involved in a [[Skirmish in the Temple of Eedit|skirmish]] with [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] and the [[scavenger]] [[Sarco Plank]]. This was the young Jedi's first duel with a [[lightsaber]].<ref name="The Weapon of a Jedi">''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]''</ref>

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Castell was a colony world of the Empire that was ruled by [[Moff]] [[Ssaria]].<ref name="Lando 1" /> At some point following the events above [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]], the [[gambler]] and entrepreneur [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] [[Theft of the Imperialis|stole]] [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] Palpatine's luxury yacht ''[[Imperialis]]'', which was undergoing repairs at the [[Sienar Fleet Systems]] [[Orbital Shipyard CC-24]] above Castell. In response, the Emperor dispatched ships to CC-24 to punish its [[Commander]] [[Pasqual]] for failing to prevent the theft of the ''Imperialis''.<ref name="Lando 2">[[Lando 2|''Lando'' 2]]</ref>

===New Republic===
Prior to the [[Battle of Jakku]], the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] [[Attack on a New Republic outpost|attacked]] a [[New Republic]] outpost on Devaron.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> Following the rise of the New Republic, the Narquois sued the Neimoidians in the New Republic courts and sought compensation for centuries of enslavement.<ref name="Aliens" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Timeline">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] to [[34 ABY]]. Because ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' places [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] one [[Standard year|year]] later, it must take place in [[35 ABY]].</ref> during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], all [[Hyperspace route|hyperlanes]] leading to the Colonies were blocked, though [[Zorii Bliss]] and [[Poe Dameron]] believed that a First Order [[captain]]'s [[First Order transit data-medallion|medallion]] could get someone through them.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
