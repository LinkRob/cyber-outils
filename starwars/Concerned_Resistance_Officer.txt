'''Concerned Resistance Officer''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Human]] aligned with the [[Resistance]]. He admired the late [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] and wanted to get the rookie soldiers out of the traps on [[Endor]], [[Crait]] and [[Jakku]].<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
