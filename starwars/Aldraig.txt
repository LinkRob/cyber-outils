'''Aldraig''' was an astronomical object in the [[Aldraig system]] of the [[Core Worlds]]' [[Alderaan sector]]. In [[1 BBY]], a [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]] shipment from there fell into the hands of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] with the help of Imperial [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], who covered his tracks by having arranging a mistake in the shipment's route from an Aldraig data clerk. The Empire also produced Imperial rations at factories on Aldraig.

==Description==
Aldraig was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in the [[Aldraig system]], located in the [[Alderaan sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]. It was situated in grid square M-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==History==
{{Quote|I have acknowledged that a data clerk on Aldraig mistakenly routed the shipment through Baylagon, but one extra layover is barely an inconvenience for a competent pilot.|Bail Organa, in a letter to Chief Investigator Axvering|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
[[File:Bailrogueone.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Bail Organa allowed a U-wing shipment from Aldraig to fall into Rebel Alliance hands]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="1 BBY">In ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', the files mentioning the acquisition of [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]]s are set between the defection of [[Bodhi Rook]] and the [[Battle of Scarif]], both dated to [[1 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> a shipment of [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]] was scheduled to be taken to the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] from Aldraig. Intent on seizing the [[starship]]s for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] of the [[Imperial Senate]] and the [[Alderaanian]] [[House of Organa]] arranged for a data clerk on Aldraig to mistakenly route the shipment via [[Baylagon system|Baylagon]]. After the shipment did not make it to Coruscant, secretly falling into rebel hands, the Empire's [[Imperial Security Bureau]] launched an enquiry led by [[Chief Investigator]] [[Axvering]]. Organa, trying to look compliant with the investigation, provided confirmation that the shipment had left Aldraig and apologized for the routing error by the clerk in a letter to Axvering, offering to compensate for extra fuel that the error may have expended.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

A copy of Organa's letter to Axvering was sent to the Alliance [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]] and [[General]] [[Airen Cracken]] of the [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|intelligance branch]]. Conscious that the U-wings would eventually be seen fighting against the Empire, Organa suggested to the pair that further assistance covering his tracks would be beneficial. The subsequent file was included in ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', an archive of non-electronic documents compiled by [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Imperial rations were produced by factories on Aldraig. [[Yrica Quell]], a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], remembered the aftertaste of Aldraig factory-stamped rations as astringent.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

''The Rebel Files'' was buried during an Imperial [[Bombardment of Durkteel|bombardment]] on the planet [[Durkteel]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="TRF dates">The reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' establishes that the [[in-universe]] artifact ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' was buried on the same [[Standard day|day]] as the [[Battle of Endor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. Given that the former also states that ''The Rebel Files'' was rediscovered thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the battle, the artifact must have been found in [[34 ABY]].</ref> before being recovered in an [[Excavation on Durkteel|excavation]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TRF dates" /> falling into the hands of [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Resistance]], a military faction with Rebel Alliance roots. The daughter of Bail Organa, Leia annotated her late father's letter, notably remarking that it was easier to steal from a hostile faction that it was to request from a friendly one like the Resistance did with the<ref name="Rebel Files" /> [[New Republic]], the Alliance's successor.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Aldraig was introduced into the ''[[Star Wars]]'' when it was mentioned in [[Daniel Wallace]]'s [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
