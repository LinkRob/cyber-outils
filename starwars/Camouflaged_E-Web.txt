The '''Camouflaged E-Web''' was an [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]] owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Prototype E-Web|standard E-Web]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Camouflaged E-Web was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Sentinel's Prototype E-Web, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 3.0.0 update alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Camouflaged Trooper]]. This costume could be unlocked as one of two grand prizes within the "Disguised Trooper Vault."<ref name="Hunters" />
