An '''Antarean butterfly''' was a type of [[poison]]ous [[butterfly]] long thought to be extinct<ref name="Poison" /> by [[9 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="SWH Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> [[Arena News]] reported that<ref name="Poison" /> the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]'s<ref name="SWH">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> [[Grand Arena]] co-owner [[Balada]] the [[Hutt]] brought<ref name="Poison" /> the [[Zabrak]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Nox]]<ref name="SWH" /> to her [[Balada's office|office]] and showed an Antarean butterfly, noting that it was the Hutt's rarest find.<ref name="Poison" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:AntareanButterfly-SW1977-48.png|250px|thumb|right|The ''Star Wars'' Legends depiction of the butterfly]]
An Antarean butterfly was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "Poison in Private," an article of the [[in-universe]] media coverage of the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', which was released on [[October 31]], [[2024]].<ref name="Poison" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Antarean butterfly/Legends|butterfly]] first appeared, albeit unidentified, in the [[Star Wars (1977) 48|forty-eighth issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']], which was written by [[Larry Hama]], illustrated by [[Carmine Infantino]],<ref name="SW 48">[[Star Wars (1977) 48|''Star Wars'' (1977) 48]]</ref> and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[March 24]], [[1981]].<ref name="Marvel SW 48">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53838/star_wars_1977_48|text=Star Wars (1977) #48}} {{C|First printing}}</ref>
