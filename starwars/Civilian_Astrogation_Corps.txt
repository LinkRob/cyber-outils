The '''Civilian Astrogation Corps''' was a loose group of [[Scout|explorers]] and [[Astrogation|astrogators]] responsible for keeping [[hyperspace]] navigational data current. They were the primary users of the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]'s [[JumpMaster 5000]] [[Patrol ship|patrol boat]].<ref name="S&S">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref>

During the [[Imperial Era]], the task of updating hyperspace data was centrally managed by the [[Imperial Survey Corps]]. During the early days of [[Galactic Empire]]'s rule, the Survey Corps offered many members of the Civilian Astrogation Corps military commissions, but most rejected the offer, which stipulated that all data on the [[Unknown Regions]] was to be considered state secrets. Instead, the free spirits of the Astrogation Corps continued to map the fringes of [[Wild Space]] in their [[Starship|JumpMasters]].<ref name="S&S" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Civilian Astrogation Corps was first mentioned in the [[2020]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement, ''[[Starships and Speeders]]''.<ref name="S&S" /> The [[Civilian Astrogation Corps/Legends|organization]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in Fantasy Flight Games' [[2013]] [[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire Core Rulebook|rulebook]] for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' roleplaying series.<ref name="EotECore">[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire Core Rulebook|''Star Wars: Edge of the Empire'' Core Rulebook]]</ref>
