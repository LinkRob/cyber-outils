'''Brikal shells''' were the of [[Color|blue]] shells of the [[brikal]], an arthropod native to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dalna]]. They were used [[Religion|religiously]] by the [[Path of the Open Hand]] to mark their faces with a pigment they made with the crushed shells, [[chalk]], and a binding agent. The Path considered anything that shared the same blue hue as the shells to be a good omen.<ref name="POD">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref>
