{{Quote|Can you get me more on Lechra? Oh, and also Forrn&ndash;I seem to recall they had a moon with carnium as well.|Leia Organa, to Nioma|<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />}}
A '''moon''' with a source of the energy-rich material [[carnium]] [[orbit]]ed the [[Planet|world]] [[Forrn]],<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> which was located in the [[Forrn system]] within the [[Xan sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Galaxy" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square Q-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Galaxy" />

In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'' to [[4 ABY]].</ref> when [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] discussed with [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Rebel Intelligence]] [[Major]] [[Nioma]] reaching out to worlds with energy-rich materials, the former requested from the latter data on Forrn, as Organa recalled the carnium of the world's moon. The princess eventually reached out to [[Empress]] [[Eliaora]], who [[Monarchy|ruled]] Forrn, about the world siding with the nascent [[New Republic]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />

==Behind the scenes==
Forrn's moon was mentioned in ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'', a [[2022]] novel written by [[Beth Revis]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />
