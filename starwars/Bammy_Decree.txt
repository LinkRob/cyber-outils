{{Quote|I know Bammy Decree can get me off Nar Shaddaa without going through customs as long as I pay upfront. I don't have enough.|Loonoo, in the ''Smuggler's Guide''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
'''Bammy Decree''' was an individual on [[Nar Shaddaa]] who could provide passage off the [[moon]] without going through customs if one paid up front. The [[con artist]] [[Loonoo]], who had come into possession of the famous [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]]'s ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', intended to sell the [[book]] to gain enough [[Currency|money]] to pay Decree's price. However, he was mugged outside of [[Bammy's Spacebarn]] by the criminal [[Skrrll]], who stole the ''Smuggler's Guide'' before Loonoo could follow through on his plan, which he had recorded in the book itself.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bammy Decree was first mentioned in the [[2018]] [[canon]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, Decree first appeared in the [[2008]] novel [[Millennium Falcon (novel)|''Millennium Falcon'']], written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Millennium Falcon">[[Millennium Falcon (novel)|''Millennium Falcon'']]</ref>
