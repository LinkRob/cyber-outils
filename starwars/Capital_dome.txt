A large, metropolitcan '''capital dome''' was located in the [[Pijal's capital city|capital city]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]. It featured an antechamber, a hover platform, and several viewing boxes.<ref name="MA" /> By [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> the dome served as the home of the [[royal orchestras]].<ref name="MA" />

That year,<ref name="Date" /> the [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] extensively promoted a rally in the [[capital]] dome to celebrate the upcoming [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]] of [[Princess]] [[Fanry]].<ref name="MA" />
