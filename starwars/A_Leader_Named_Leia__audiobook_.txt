'''''A Leader Named Leia''''' is the unabridged audio version of the [[canon]] young-readers [[World of Reading]] book [[A Leader Named Leia|of the same name]] that was released as part of [[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'']]. The audiobook was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[September 19]], [[2024]].<ref name="Audible" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Whether a princess or a general, Leia Organa is a force to be reckoned with! Spanning the entire Star Wars saga, this exciting audio story introduces Padawans to the feats, talents, and triumphs of everyone's favorite princess.''<ref name="Audible" />
