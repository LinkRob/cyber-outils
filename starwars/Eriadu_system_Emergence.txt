In [[232 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> during the [[High Republic Era]], an '''Emergence''' of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] occurred in the [[Eriadu system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Kassav Milliko]], who had been given data on upcoming [[Emergences]] by [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]], attempted to use the Emergence to extort [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] from [[Eriadu]]an [[Governor]] [[Mural Veen]], promising to destroy the incoming [[hyperspace]] debris if she paid up. After several [[transport]]s carrying [[lommite]] ore were destroyed by debris, and with the news that the next debris would strike [[Eriadu's inhabited moon]], Veen reluctantly paid Milliko the fifty million credits he had demanded. However, the crew of Milliko's [[flagship]] ''[[New Elite]]'' was unable to reach and destroy the debris in time and it struck the [[moon]], causing many [[death]]s. The Eriaduans had extensively scanned Milliko's [[starship]] before he was forced to retreat, which allowed the [[Galactic Republic]] to identify him. Veen swore vengeance upon Milliko, eventually personally killing the [[pirate]] during the [[Battle of Kur]].<ref name="LotJ" />

The Emergence resulted in a heavy bombing of [[Eriadu City]],<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" /> leading the [[Lava reactor core|reactor]] that powered up the region containing Eriadu City and [[Bri-Phrang City]] to become fractured, leaving the possibility that half of the planet could be wiped out with a disturbance or explosion.<ref name="Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone 1" /> After Eriadu was later engulfed by the Nihil's [[Occlusion Zone]], [[Tragkul Tarkin]], a member of the [[Tarkin family]] with an anti-[[Galactic Republic|Republic]] agenda, recalled the bombing at a meeting of the [[Quintad]], attempting to further his agenda by questioning Republic representatives on where the Republic had been when the [[city]] had been bombed.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" />
