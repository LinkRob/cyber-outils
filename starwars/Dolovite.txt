'''Dolovite''' was a substance [[Mining|mined]] on the [[Magma|lava]] world of [[Mustafar]] and [[Samovar]]. [[Kongo]] the Disemboweler once dwelled in the dolovite mines.<ref name="Star Wars 11" /> It was also present on [[Burnin Konn]], one of the four [[Mining Worlds|primary mining worlds]] of the [[Anoat sector]].<ref name="OS Locations">{{WebCite|url=https://playstarwarsuprising.com/game-guide/introduction/#locations|text=Locations|work=Star Wars: Uprising Official Website|archivedate=20160624013822}}</ref>

It could be refined into a polished crimson colored alloy that was suitable for use in [[lightsaber]] construction.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

[[Marchion Ro]] had goblets made of pure dolovite on the ''[[Gaze Electric]]''.<ref name="Trials">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dolovite first appeared in ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''. Its name is similar to the real world's [[Wikipedia:Dolomite (mineral)|dolomite]] mineral.
