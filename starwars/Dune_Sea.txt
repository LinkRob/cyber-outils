The '''Dune Sea''', known as the '''Great Dune''', was a vast stretch of [[desert]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]]. The [[Great Pit of Carkoon]], which contained a creature known as the [[sarlacc]], was located in the Dune Sea.<ref name="Episode VI" /> The [[Western Dune Sea]] was where the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]] was located.<ref name="AbEverything" /> The [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]<ref name="PalaceDB" /> and the [[Ben Kenobi's home|home]] of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] were located outside the Dune Sea.<ref name="Episode IV" />. A [[Leviathan krayt dragon]] also prowled the dunes near [[Mos Algo]], ambushing any who attempted to explore too far from the abandoned settlement.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

Archaeological evidence suggested that, long before the [[Imperial Era]], the Dune Sea had been a sea that Tatooine's two [[Star|suns]], [[Tatoo I]] and [[Tatoo II]], dried up.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> According to [[Jawa]] folklore, the Dune Sea was once a genuine [[ocean]]. Although many [[colonist]]s found it hard to believe that [[water]] had once flowed abundantly on Tatooine, ancient fossil-bearing rocks and eroded canyons seemed to back up the Jawas' tales.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

Two monitors inside the [[Niki-Bon Travel Agency]] on [[Coruscant]] showed an image of the desert and read "DUNE SEA" in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="Friends Everywhere">{{Andor|I Have Friends Everywhere}}</ref>
