'''Ace Kallisto''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Master Thief|master thief]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. She was once the rival of [[Jedi Master]] [[Zia Zaldor Zanna]]. Around [[233 BBY]], the Master Thief reunited with her former rival once again by stealing her [[shuttle]]. They soon got into an altercation causing them to crash into a [[Unidentified cave (Stalaat)|cave]] guarded by [[stalaat]]s. Through teamwork, Kallisto and the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Kai Brightstar]] made it out of the cave and sought help.

==Biography==
===Early life===
Ace Kallisto was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[thief]] from the [[planet]] [[Rex Strata]]. She was a rival to the [[Jedi]] [[Zia Zaldor Zanna]], though their rivalry seemed to fade as they grew older.<ref name="The Jedi and the Thief" />

===Reunion===
Around [[233 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|107A}}</ref> Kallisto noticed that Zanna had return, the thief quickly stole the [[shuttle]] she arrived in. Unsurprisingly, Zanna caught up to Kallisto and entered the [[cockpit]] of the ship with a [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Kai Brightstar]]. In an attempt to intimidate Kallisto, Brightstar drew his [[Brightstar's second lightsaber|lightsaber]], but he accidentally threw it and slashed the cockpit causing a malfunction. The shuttle soon crashed as they were unable to repair the ship.<ref name="The Jedi and the Thief" />

[[File:Ace_and_Kai-YJAS1E7.png|left|230px|thumb|Kallisto and Kai Brightstar working together to pass the stalaat.]]
Soon, they woke up and Zanna was unable to move due to her ankle being injured by the crash. The three then noticed that they crashed into a [[Unidentified cave (Stalaat)|cave]] home to a [[stalaat]]. The thief and the youngling went out together to call for help. However, another stalaat jumped in as they walk to the exit. While Brightstar distracted the stalaats with his lightsaber, Kallisto took the opportunity to flee and activated the armaments of the shuttle to scare away the creatures. She quickly told him to get out and call for help with a [[comlink]]. As help soon arrived, Kallisto took the moment to talk with Zanna before parting ways. As she walk away, she heard Brightstar asking Zanna if she would become good. Kallisto smiled and left the two Jedi.<ref name="The Jedi and the Thief" />

Looking for the [[Tharnakan Story Stone]], Kallisto and her crew, which included a [[Gungan]] [[Tooba Jinx]] and [[Tharnakan]] [[Ishbul Ekwesh]], repelled down into a cavern from a hole above and retrieved the Story Stone, attempting to retrieve it for Ekwesh. Kallisto and her crew made a campfire during a storm, waiting for it to pass, when she saw [[Kai Brightstar]]. Having recognized Brightstar, Kallisto removed her helmet and Brightstar introduced [[Lys Solay]], [[Nubs]], and [[0G-LC]]. Brightstar accused her of stealing the stone, which prevented 0G-LC from researching the Stone for historical purposes. Kallisto revealed that she was trying to retrieve it for Ekwesh, and when 0G-LC began questioning Ekwesh in awe, Ekwesh started to tell his story. When the storm had begun to pass, Kallisto explained they should get going, but then Jinx betrayed the group, stealing the stone in attempt to sell it. Together, Kallisto, Ekwesh, Brightstar, Solay, 0G-LC, and Nubs chased after Jinx on two speeders, traversing the rocky terrain. When 0G-LC used his grappling hook, with an assist from the [[Jedi]]'s [[force]] abilities, to stop Jinx, Kallisto grabbed the stone from the gungan just moments before he reached his ship. However Jinx overpowered Kallisto, and she was knocked to the ground. Luckily, Ekwesh had cornered Jinx on the boarding ramp, and Kallisto put Jinx in handcuffs. Having retrieved the Story Stone, Kallisto and Ekwesh returned the Jedi and 0G-LC to their ship and Kallisto left with Ekwesh to return the stone to the Tharnakans.<ref name="The Thieves of Tharnaka"/> 

===Battle of Tenoo===
Kallisto participated in the [[Battle of Tenoo]] after hearing Brightstar's distress message.<ref name="The Battle of Tenoo">{{YJA|The Battle of Tenoo}}</ref>
