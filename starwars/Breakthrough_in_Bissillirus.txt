"'''Breakthrough in Bissillirus'''" is the six adventure in ''[[The Bissillirus Campaign]]''.

==Plot summary==
The Rebels on [[Draenell's Point]] infiltrate the under-construction [[Bissillirus Resupply Base]] to spirit away a defector with information on the project.
