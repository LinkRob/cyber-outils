The '''Bromlarch Council''' was the [[planet]]ary council of [[Bromlarch]], located in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] of [[the galaxy]]. During the [[Republic Era|Fall of the Republic]] era, it was led by [[Councillor]] [[Eema]]. After Bromlarch's [[Bromlarch planetary aqueduct system|aqueduct system]] was damaged by seismic activity, the Bromlarch Council submitted a plan for repairs that was shown to the [[Galactic Senate]] by [[Senator]] [[Mina Bonteri]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />
