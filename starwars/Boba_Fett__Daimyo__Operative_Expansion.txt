The '''Boba Fett (Daimyo) Operative Expansion''' is a ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' Operative Expansion set. It was released on [[December 16]], [[2022]]. An updated version, combined with the [[Boba Fett (Daimyo) Operative Expansion]], was released on [[July 25]], [[2025]] as the [[Outer Rim Outlaws Expansion]].

==Publisher's summary==
===Online summary===
''The infamous Boba Fett returns to Star Wars: Legion in this new Operative Expansion! Featuring a new version of this iconic character based on his appearance in The Mandalorian and The Book of Boba Fett, this expansion gives players powerful new Operative that can fight alongside their mercenary and Rebel forces. The beautifully sculpted Boba Fett miniature found in this expansion menaces his foes with his gaderffii stick and can be assembled with our without his Mandalorian helmet. Additionally, players will find three command cards that make Boba Fett even more versatile in combat. Finally, an upgrade card allows players to invite Boba Fett with his Flame Projector for even more firepower.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

===Back blurb summary===
''Boba Fett saw his father killed in battle as a child, survived the Clone Wars, and made his mark as a bounty hunter before a too-close encounter with the Sarlacc temporarily separated the man from his Mandalorian armor. Few things can slow down or stop Boba Fett from getting what he desires. Armed with a variety of weapons, Fett does not hesitate to use brute force when needed in battle.''<ref name="Legion Expansion">{{Legion|set=Boba Fett (Daimyo) Operative Expansion}}</ref>

''In addition to Fett's Mandalorian armor and jetpack, he also has been known to use a gaderffü stick and whipcord launcher in close combat.''<ref name="Legion Expansion" />
