The '''assault on Moff Gideon's cruiser''' was a mission to rescue [[Din Grogu|Grogu]]. During the [[New Republic Era]] in [[9 ABY]], a [[Bo-Katan Kryze's unit|group]] of [[Mandalorian]]s and a [[New Republic]] [[marshal]] along with a [[mercenary]] and former [[bounty hunter]] mounted a rescue of Grogu from [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]'s [[Gideon's light cruiser|light cruiser]]. They were able to pilot their stolen [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]] through the launch tube and crash-land in the cruiser, allowing the team to board it.

After a fight between the team and [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] [[stormtrooper]]s, they were able to successfully captured the light cruiser's bridge. Djarin soon returned with Gideon in [[stuncuffs]], having rescued Grogu from his holding cell, and holding the [[Darksaber]], thereby making him the [[Mandalore (title)|ruler]] of [[Mandalore]].

However the [[Phase-III Dark Trooper|Dark Trooper]] [[Gideon's Dark Trooper platoon|platoon]] that Djarin thought he eliminated flew back to Gideon's cruiser and boarded it, eventually making their way to the bridge; the team sealed the [[blast door]]s to stall them, though the droids began to punch the doors. However a [[Luke Skywalker|mysterious robed figure]] wielding a [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|green-bladed]] [[lightsaber]] had boarded the ship and cut his way through the droids and destroyed them. The [[Jedi Master]] revealed himself to be Luke Skywalker and beckoned Grogu to come with him, and informed Djarin that the child was asking for his permission. Skywalker then took Grogu with him as he departed from the cruiser.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|The Moff has a light cruiser. It could be helpful in your effort to regain Mandalore.''"''<br />''"''[&hellip;] We will help you. In exchange, we will keep that ship to retake Mandalore. [&hellip;] One more thing. Gideon has a weapon that once belonged to me. It is an ancient weapon that can cut through anything. [&hellip;] It cannot cut through pure beskar. I will kill the moff and retake what is rightfully mine. With the Darksaber restored to me, Mandalore will finally be within reach.''"<br />"''Help me rescue the child, and you can have whatever you want. He is my only priority.|Din Djarin and Bo-Katan Kryze|<ref name="Chapter 16" />}}
[[File:GideonGrogu-TMCh14.png|thumb|left|250px|Moff Gideon hunted Grogu, a mysterious child whose blood contained a significant M-count.]]
As part of their experiments involving [[Midi-chlorian|M-count]] [[blood]] transfusions,<ref name="Chapter 12">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 12: The Siege}}</ref> a [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|remnant]] of the [[Galactic Civil War|fallen]] [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]<ref name="Chapter 7">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 7: The Reckoning}}</ref> sought to [[Hunt for Grogu|acquire]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]],<ref name="Chapter 13">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref> a mysterious [[Youngling|child]] [[alien]] of the same species as [[Yoda]]<ref name="Chapter 1" /> with [[The Force|extraordinary]] [[Force power|powers]].<ref name="Chapter 2">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 2: The Child}}</ref> However, the child was being protected by [[Din Djarin]],<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> a [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] who went rogue from the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] in order to [[Incident on Nevarro|rescue]] and protect the child,<ref name="Chapter 3">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 3: The Sin}}</ref> which he raised as a [[foundling]] of his [[Clan Mudhorn|clan]].<ref name="Chapter 8" />

Djarin was quested with reuniting Grogu with his own kind, which a [[The Armorer|Mandalorian armorer]] identified as being the [[Jedi]].<ref name="Chapter 8" /> With the help of fellow Mandalorian [[Bo-Katan Kryze]],<ref name="Chapter 11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref> Djarin eventually located former<ref name="The Wrong Jedi">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref> Jedi [[Ahsoka Tano]], who refused to [[Jedi training|train]] Grogu and instead directed Djarin to a [[Force-henge|ancient temple]] on [[Tython]], where Grogu could reach out through the Force and potentially be sensed by another Jedi. There, however, he was found by Gideon's forces, who captured Grogu, returned him to [[Gideon's light cruiser]], and then jumped into [[hyperspace]] to a location unknown to Djarin. However, Djarin was joined in his quest to rescue Grogu by [[Boba Fett]] and [[Fennec Shand]], who deemed themselves indebted to Djarin after he allowed Fett to keep his [[Boba Fett's armor|armor]] that he had previously captured.<ref name="Chapter 14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref> After [[Mission to Morak|raiding]] a [[Morak Imperial remnants|remnants]] [[Morak Imperial refinery|base]] on [[Morak]] with the help of Djarin's friend [[Carasynthia Dune|Cara Dune]], a [[New Republic]] [[marshal]], and ex-Imperial [[Sniper|sharpshooter]] [[Migs Mayfeld]], Djarin and his allies discovered the coordinates to Gideon's [[light cruiser]].<ref name="Chapter 15" />

Djarin, Dune, Fett, and Shand captured Gideon's [[Shuttle 2743]], which had been transporting [[Doctor]] [[Penn Pershing]], a [[Cloning|clone]] [[engineer]] overseeing the experiments and killed [[Mott (individual)|one]] of the [[Jorad Mainos|two]] pilots.<ref name="Chapter 16" /> They then traveled to a [[Lafete|desert world]] where they enlisted the aid of [[Mandalorian warrior]]s Bo-Katan Kryze and [[Koska Reeves]]; Kryze sought to reclaim the [[Darksaber]] from Moff Gideon, who had acquired the [[Lightsaber|weapon]] following the [[Great Purge of Mandalore]],<ref name="Chapter 8" /> in order to reclaim the Mandalorian throne and unite the Mandalorians to take back their [[homeworld]] of [[Mandalore]].<ref name="Chapter 16" />

==The rescue==
{{Quote|Phase one, ''Lambda'' shuttle issues a distress call. Two, we come in hot and emergency land at the mouth of the fighter launch tube, cutting off any potential interceptors. Koska, Fennec, Dune, and myself disembark with maximum initiative. Once we've neutralized the launch bay, we make our ways through these tandem desks in a penetration maneuver.''"<br />"''And me?''"<br />"''We'll be misdirection. Once we draw a crowd, you slip through the shadows, get the kid.|Bo-Katan Kryze and Din Djarin|<ref name="Chapter 16" />}}
[[File:Dark_trooper_platoon.png|thumb|right|280px|A platoon of dark troopers is activated in response to Din Djarin's team's arrival.]]
Arriving out of [[hyperspace]] near Moff Gideon's light [[cruiser]], Boba Fett, from the ''[[Slave I]]'', feigned fire on the [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]] containing the rest of the team. He drew away two [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s that deployed from Gideon's cruiser while the shuttle crash-landed through the launch tube into the cruiser, allowing the team to board it. Fett blasted the [[starfighter]]s before then jumping to hyperspace himself.<ref name="Chapter 16" />

Inside the cruiser, Dune, Shand, Kryze, and Reeves made their way to the bridge, fighting against [[stormtrooper]]s along the way, while Djarin alone snuck to Grogu's cell. As he made his way to the child's cell, Djarin found the cargo bay storing the [[platoon]] of [[Phase-III Dark Trooper|third-generation dark troopers]]; however, the [[Battle droid|combat droids]] had already been activated. Using Pershing's [[code cylinder]], Djarin closed the doors, though one of the [[droid]]s escaped and caught Djarin. Djarin ultimately destroyed it with his [[beskar spear]] and opened up the room's sealed doors, pulling the rest of the platoon out into [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Chapter 16" />

While the rest of the team seized the bridge, Djarin defeated the [[trooper]]s guarding the child's cell; but inside, he found Gideon with the Darksaber unsheathed and above Grogu. Gideon forced Djarin to kick his [[blaster pistol]] over to him, and tricked Djarin into a false deal: in exchange for the child, Djarin agreed to leave Gideon and never cross paths with him again. When Djarin moved in, Gideon attacked the Mandalorian with the Darksaber. In the hallway outside the cell, Djarin unsheathed his [[beskar spear]] and briefly dueled Gideon, ultimately defeating the moff.<ref name="Chapter 16" />

[[File:DjarinDeliversGideon-TMCh16.png|thumb|left|280px|Djarin escorts a defeated Gideon onto the bridge as Kryze looks at the Darksaber in Djarin's hand.]]
Djarin freed Grogu, took the Darksaber, and delivered a captured Gideon to the bridge before Kryze and the rest of the team. Gideon remarked that Kryze was unable to take the Darksaber, and thus claim the Mandalorian throne, for she had not defeated him in combat, but Djarin had instead. With their [[rocket booster]]s, the dark trooper droid platoon flew back to Gideon's cruiser and boarded it, eventually making their way to the bridge; the team sealed the [[blast door]]s to stall them, though the droids began to punch the doors in an effort to pound through them. However, before the droids could breach the doors, a [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|T-65B]] [[X-wing starfighter]] arrived out of hyperspace and docked inside the cruiser.<ref name="Chapter 16" />

===Aid from a Jedi===
{{Quote|A Jedi.|[[Bo-Katan Kryze]]|<ref name="Chapter 16" />}}
From the bridge, the team and Moff Gideon watched the [[security camera]]s as a mysterious robed figure wielding a [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|green-bladed]] [[lightsaber]] cut his way through the droids; Kryze identified the figure to be a [[Jedi]]. The Jedi eventually ascended in an elevator to the hallway before the bridge and destroyed the remaining dark troopers. Gideon picked up a nearby [[blaster]] [[pistol]] that he had been hiding and repeatedly shot Kryze; however, before he could then fire on Grogu, Djarin leaped in front of the child, shielding him with his beskar [[Din Djarin's armor|armor]]. Gideon then attempted to kill himself with the blaster, but Dune knocked him out before he could.<ref name="Chapter 16" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Hey, go on. That's who you belong with, he's one of your kind. I'll see you again. I promise. It's time to go. Don't be afraid.|Din Djarin asks Grogu to go with Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Chapter 16" />}}
[[File:LukesNewStudent-TMCh16.png|thumb|right|180px|Luke Skywalker took Grogu with him and R2-D2.]]
With the remaining dark troopers destroyed, Din Djarin opened up the blast doors as the Jedi entered through the smoke, turned off his lightsaber and removed his hood, revealing himself to be [[Luke Skywalker]]. Djarin asked if he was a Jedi, to which Skywalker confirmed he was. Skywalker beckoned Grogu to come with him, and informed Djarin that the child was asking for his permission. Djarin gave the child his blessing and wished him farewell, removing his helmet to say goodbye. Alongside his [[astromech droid]], [[R2-D2]], Skywalker then took Grogu with him as he departed from the cruiser.<ref name="Chapter 16" />

Boba Fett, who had left the rescue early on after having fulfilled his debt to Djarin, went with Fennec Shand to [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on [[Tatooine]], which was owned by [[Bib Fortuna]] and [[Attack on Jabba's Palace (Boba Fett)|attacked the palace]] and killed Fortuna where Boba sat on the throne<ref name="Chapter 16" /> Fett then formed his own [[Fett gotra|criminal empire]] from the remnants of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]'s [[Jabba's criminal empire|former empire]] and became the [[Daimyo]], who sought to rule out of respect rather than fear that had existed under Jabba.<ref name="Stranger">{{BOBF|Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref>
