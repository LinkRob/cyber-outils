'''Canon-Man''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[humanoid]] who had a [[Color|gray]] [[cannon]] for a head and black [[eye]]s.<ref name="Launchpad" /> Sometime prior to [[1 BBY]],<ref name="date">[[Ponda Baba]] loses an [[Limb|arm]] in the [[Secret mission to Tatooine]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]]. As "The Light Side" in the [[Launchpad]] section of [[Star Wars Insider 217|''Star Wars Insider'' 217]] depicts Baba with two arms, the events in the comic must have occurred by that [[Standard year]].</ref> a [[Dressellian]] named [[Orrimaarko]] was at a table with [[Ponda Baba]], a member of the [[Aqualish]] [[species]], discussing the potential for the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Youngling|child]] [[Boomka Slay]] to be the [[reproduction|son]] of the [[Yarkora]] "[[Saelt-Marae|Yak Face]]."<ref name="Launchpad" />

Orrimaarko asked if this was a factual statement and subsequently clarified this, using the term "canon" followed by the word "man," spoken in exasperation. Taking the question literally, Ponda Baba pointed to Canon-Man&mdash;who was standing nearby drinking a [[Foodstuff|beverage]] with an umbrella in the cup via a straw&mdash;and responded to Orrimaarko by saying "''No, that's Canon-Man!''". Canon-Man was wearing a [[Color|red]] [[jacket]] with a yellow center panel and a yellow strip down the sleeve. A brown [[belt]] with three pouches sat on their waist above red [[Clothing|pants]], while grey [[glove]]s adorned their [[Limb|hands]].<ref name="Launchpad" />

==Behind the scenes==
Canon-Man was featured in the [[Canon|non-canonical]] comic "The Light Side" with the [[Launchpad]] section of [[Star Wars Insider 217|''Star Wars Insider'' 217]], released in [[2023]]. The comic was written and illustrated by [[Jamie Cosley]] and plays on the dual meanings of the homophones "canon,"<ref name="Launchpad" /> part of an accepted body of work;<ref name="Canon">{{WebCite|url=https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/canon|text=Canon Definition & Meaning|work=[[Wikipedia:Merriam-Webster|Merriam-Webster]]|archivedate=20230316165444}}</ref> and "cannon," a large gun typically mounted on a carriage.<ref name="Cannon">{{WebCite|url=https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/cannon|text=Cannon Definition & Meaning|work=[[Wikipedia:Merriam-Webster|Merriam-Webster]]|archivedate=20220212184646}}</ref>
