'''Desert roses''' were a type of [[flower]] used to make [[desert rose incense]] that could be found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]. At some point during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[E'roni]] [[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]] watched the first of the roses bloom. While [[Commanding officer|commanding]] the [[E'ronoh's fleet]]'s [[Thylefire Squadron]]<ref name="Convergence" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> A'lbaran thought about the many beautiful things she had witnessed on her [[homeworld]], including watching desert roses flower.<ref name="Convergence" /> Later the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> after boarding his [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Eventide]]'', the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[envoy]] [[Axel Greylark]] noticed that the interior of his vessel smelled like desert rose [[incense]].<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
Desert roses were mentioned in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase II<ref name="Convergence" /> that was published on [[November 22]], [[2022]]. Prior to its release, a mention of the roses was included in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the novel on [[October 4]] of that year.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-convergence-excerpt|text=A Mysterious Mission Begins in ''The High Republic: Convergence'' &ndash; Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
