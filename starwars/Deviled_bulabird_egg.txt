{{Quote|This cheese board showcases the flavors of its host planet&ndash;with deviled bulabird eggs and tintolvies plucked fresh from the trees linin' the crystalline cliffs just north of Hanna City&ndash;as well as favorite snacks from a number of worlds across the galaxy (all of which are included in this collection).|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Deviled bulabird eggs''' were a [[Foodstuff|dish]] made with the [[Bulabird egg|eggs]] of the [[bulabird]]. The dish consisted of a solidified [[egg]] [[Color|white]] with yellow filling in the center and a black garnish on top of the filling. Deviled bulabird eggs could be served as one of the components of a [[Chandrilan Cheese Board]], a recipe that showcased the best flavors of the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]],<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> where bulabirds could be found.<ref name="LifeDebt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

Following a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he made<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for deviled bulabird eggs in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> It was included as part of the recipe for a Chandrilan Cheese Board, although he advised readers to change what they served on the board to stop those eating from becoming bored of it.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Deviled bulabird eggs were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish makes use of large [[Wikipedia:Eggs as food|eggs]], [[Wikipedia:Mayonnaise|mayonnaise]], [[Wikipedia:Dijon mustard|Dijon mustard]], chopped, fresh [[Wikipedia:Dill|dill]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic powder|garlic powder]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], [[Wikipedia:Celery#Seeds|celery seeds]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper#White pepper|white pepper]], [[Wikipedia:Fried onion|fried onions]], and [[Wikipedia:Sesame#Culinary use|black sesame seeds]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
