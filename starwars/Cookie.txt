'''Cookies''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. [[Sweet-sand cookie]]s were a type of cookies popular with [[Youngling|children]].<ref name="Servants">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Mando Date">{{MandoDate}}</ref> [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] used [[the Force]] to steal a pack of [[Blue macaron|blue cookies]] on [[Nevarro]].<ref name="Chapter 12">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 12: The Siege}}</ref> [[Wookiee Cookie]]s were flat, round [[Color|purple]] cookies that could be [[Diet|eaten]] by [[Wookiee]]s.<ref name="Wookiee" /> During or after [[34 ABY]],<ref name=" Pals Date">{{GalacticPalsDate}}</ref> the [[Safari droid]] [[M1-RE]],<ref name="Wookiee" /> a specialized member of the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]],<ref name="Learn to care">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galactic-pals|text=Learn to Care for Creatures and Aliens in ''Star Wars Galactic Pals''}}</ref> gave one of these cookies to a [[Unidentified Wookiee youngling|Wookiee youngling]] after they learned to control their temper.<ref name="Wookiee" /> M1-RE later unsuccessfully used a Wookiee cookie and several other treats in an attempt to lure an [[Unidentified Ewok youngling|Ewok youngling]] out of his shyness.<ref name="Ewok">{{GalacticPals|Ewok}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The non-[[canon]]ical [[2016]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]] introduced [[Wookiee Cookie/LEGO|Wookiee Cookies]], a type of cookies favored by the [[Wookiee]] warrior [[Chewbacca]].<ref name="LEGO TFA">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref> Wookiee cookies first appeared in canon in "[[Wookiee]]," the first episode of the ''[[Star Wars Galactic Pals]]'' animated series,<ref name="Wookiee" /> which was released on [[April 12]], [[2022]].<ref name="Learn to care" />
