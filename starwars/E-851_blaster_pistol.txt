The '''E-851 blaster pistol''' was a type of [[blaster pistol]] manufactured by [[Kelvarek Consolidated Arms]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Description==
[[File:ZoriiBlissE851BlasterPistol-TROSSeries2Weapons.png|thumb|250px|left|Zorii Bliss wielding one of her blaster pistols.]]
The E-851 [[blaster pistol]] had a [[bronzium]] [[barrel]] with recoil stabilizing porting notch, a "crystal pan" charge indicator diode, and a retain pin for grip. The weapon also had a power charge port in the grip, a segmented body shell that opened for servicing, and sculpted trigger guards. The E-851 was a quick-draw [[blaster pistol]] that fired a steady shot, making it useful for [[Underworld (disambiguation)|underworld]] criminals such as the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]]. They were made to have minimum recoil, ideal for quick, brutal confrontations.<ref name="TROS VD" /> Despite the appearance of the blaster's weighted grips resemble those of old-time space [[pirate]]s, their firepower was state-of-the-art.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|zorii-bliss-s-blasters|Zorii Bliss's blasters}}</ref>

==History==
[[Zorii Bliss]] notably [[Zorii Bliss's blasters|used two]] on [[Kijimi]].<ref name="TROS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The E-851 blaster pistol was first identified in ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', the reference book for the film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] where the blaster first appeared. The dictionary was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released by [[Dorling Kindersley|DK Publishing]] on [[December 20]], [[2019]], the same day as the film itself.<ref name="TROS VD" />
