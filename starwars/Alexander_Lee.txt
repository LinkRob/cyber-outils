'''Alexander Lee''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan who designed the [[droid]] [[QZ-M2]] for the [[Autodesk Droid Design Contest]]. Lee was the winner of the "[[Padawan]]" student class of applicants in the "Animated" droid category.<ref name="Winners">{{WebCite|url=https://www.research.autodesk.com/blog/autodesks-droid-maker-contest-unleashing-the-force-of-creativity|work=[https://www.research.autodesk.com Autodesk Research]|text=Autodesk's Droid Maker Contest: Unleashing the Force of Creativity|archivedate=20260528135421}}</ref>
