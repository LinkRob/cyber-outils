'''Aeroxite''' was a type of [[Color|chrome-colored]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[steel]].<ref name="Steel" /> It could be cast from [[ore]] [[Mining|mined]] in [[Velser's Ring]],<ref name="100 Objects" /> an [[Asteroid field|asteroid belt]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Bespin system]].<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> By [[35 ABY]],<ref name="35 BBY">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' describes the state of [[the galaxy]] up to and during the events of {{Film|IX}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[35 ABY]].</ref> [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]&mdash;a [[Resistance]] member<ref name="TROS VD" /> and former [[gambler]],<ref name="100 Objects" /> [[smuggler]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[Baron Administrator]] of the [[Cloud City]] [[City|settlement]] of the [[planet]] [[Bespin]],<ref name="USWNE" /> and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Alliance officer|officer]]&mdash;possessed a [[greel ebonwood]] [[Lando's cane|cane]] with an aeroxite head and tip.<ref name="TROS VD" /> The [[cane]]'s head was sculpted to resemble Cloud City and had the [[Aurebesh]] [[Writing|inscription]] "Baron Landonis Calrissian" on it, while its tip could have potentially been used to jab anyone suggesting that the aging Calrissian was old or infirm&mdash;as was eventually noted in an entry on the cane included in<ref name="100 Objects" /> a [[Unidentified historical book|book]]<ref name="InUniverse">{{Bluesky|kristinbaver.bsky.social|post/3lxdw6e32j42a|[[Kristin Baver]]|date=2025-08-26|quote=It is! Like the visual dictionaries, Skywalker: A Family at War, and The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire, it’s part of a long line of books that are based on a construct that I adore: that the events and items in Star Wars are real.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/hodDN}} {{C|In response to: "A question, is 100 objects intended to be an in-universe book?"}}</ref> describing objects relating to the history of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="100 Objects" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aeroxite first appeared as part of Lando Calrissian's cane in ''[[The Galaxy Needs You]]'', a picture book written by [[Caitlin Kennedy]], illustrated by [[Eda Kaban]],<ref name="GalaxyNeedsYou">''[[The Galaxy Needs You]]''</ref> and published on [[December 17]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/the-galaxy-needs-you|text=The Galaxy Needs You}}</ref> The illustration was based on Calrissian's look in<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> the [[December 20]], 2019 [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref>

Chief costume concept artist [[Glyn Dillon]] and prop concept designer [[Matthew Savage]] created several concept art drawings featuring Lando Calrissian's cane. In Dillon's artwork, the cane's tip was white and its head was [[gold]]-colored, while Savage's illustration indicated that the cane's head incorporated "solidified [[tibanna]] ore."<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]''</ref> Prior to the cane's debut in [[in-universe]] stories, it was first pictured on [[September 26]], 2019 in a [[StarWars.com]] [https://web.archive.org/web/20200102160517/https://www.starwars.com/news/triple-force-friday-products article] previewing then-upcoming ''[[Star Wars]]'' merchandise, including that related to the film.<ref name="Triple">{{SW|url=news/triple-force-friday-products|text=Check Out New ''Star Wars'' Products Just Revealed for Triple Force Friday|date=2019-09-26}}</ref> The metal was identified by name in the film's accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
