'''1138''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] serving under the [[Trade Federation]] [[Trade Federation Droid Army|Droid Army]] during the [[Invasion of Naboo|Naboo Crisis]]. 1138 took part on the Battle of Naboo under the command of [[OOM-9]]. The [[droid]]s were able to create a victory for the Federation, but this was short-lived, as every Federation battle droid was shut down when [[Anakin Skywalker]] destroyed the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Droid Control Ship]] above [[Naboo]].<ref name="tpm" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
1138 was created for [[George Lucas]]' 1999 film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], and was featured as both an animatronic model and a computer generated character. The character's identification number is featured on its back, and is one of the many references to Lucas' debut feature film, [[Wikipedia:THX 1138|''THX 1138'']].
