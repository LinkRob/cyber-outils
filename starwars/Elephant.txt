An '''elephant''' was a [[species]] of creature. The [[Ortolan]] [[music]]ian [[Maximilian Rebo|Max Rebo]] had a [[Nose|snout]] resembling the trunk of an elephant.<ref name="AmazingBook">''[[The Amazing Book of Star Wars]]''</ref> Species resembling elephants were referred to as [[elephantine]]s.<ref name="Holocron">{{Instagram|username=holocronkeeper|url=p/C-LNoySvnKy|name=[[Leland Chee]]|date=2024-08-03|quote=Elephantine #starwarsalien|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/G7W9S|archivefile=File:LelandChee-Elephantine-IG.png|archivefile2=File:LelandChee-Elephantine2-IG.png}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Let's try to redress an elephant again, as they did for the banthas in A New Hope. That was literally the framework: Let's have some new updated versions of old creatures.|Christian Alzmann|<ref name="ArtofTFA">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>}}
Elephants were first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Canon]] in an in-universe context in the [[2016]] young reader's reference book ''[[The Amazing Book of Star Wars]]'', written by [[Elizabeth Dowsett]].<ref name="AmazingBook" />

They were first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity in the [[1976]] novel [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope'']], which was an adaptation of the 1977 film of the same name.<ref name="ANHNovel">[[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope'' novelization]]</ref> Their first visual depiction in the same continuity&mdash;albeit a non-canon one&mdash;was in the [[2011]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars III: The Clone Wars]]'', in which they appear as a ridable mount in the level "Liberty on Ryloth."<ref name="LEGOSW3">''[[LEGO Star Wars III: The Clone Wars]]''</ref>

[[File:Creating_a_bantha.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Mardji in-costume on the set of ''A New Hope'']]
The real-life [[Wikipedia:Elephant|counterparts]] to elephants have been utilized in some form multiple times in ''Star Wars'' production history. During the production of the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episove IV ''A New Hope'']], an [[Wikipedia:Asian elephant|Asian elephant]] named [[Mardji]] portrayed the [[bantha]] under added cosmetics.<ref name="Bantha - Databank">{{DB|creature|bantha|bantha}}</ref> The movements of the [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s seen in the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] were based on footage of Mardji walking.<ref name="Empire's Elephant">{{SWArchive|url=episode-v/explore/classic/2001/10/classic20011031.html|text=Episode V Classic Moment - Empire's Elephant}}</ref>

Elephants were also used in the sound design of the films. The sound of a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]'s engines were the result of mixing cars streaking past and the trumpeting cry of an elephant.<ref name="TIE Fighter - Databank">{{DB|starship|tiefighter|TIE fighter}}</ref> For the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], the design characteristics of the [[Multi-Troop Transport]] were based on a massive elephant.<ref name="Trade Federation MTT - Databank">{{DB|vehicle|tfed_mtt|Trade Federation MTT (Multi Troop Transport)}}</ref>

In the [[2015]] art book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'', written by [[Phil Szostak]], an excerpt from<ref name="ArtofTFA" /> illustrator and concept artist<ref name="Alzmann">{{WebCite|url=http://www.christianalzmann.com|text=Christian Alzmann Studios|author=[[Christian Alzmann]]|archivedate=20230210222655}}</ref> [[Christian Alzmann]] states that the production crew of the 2015 film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] could redress elephants to create new creatures for the film, similar to how banthas were created in ''A New Hope''.<ref name="ArtofTFA" />
