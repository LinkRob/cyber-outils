'''Adlerber''' was a skittish [[Arcona]] dealer who worked with the [[Kubaz]] [[Monigallgh]]. The two were invited by [[Zeva Bliss]] of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] to a [[Kijimi summit|summit]] on [[Kijimi]], where Bliss secretly intended to [[Death|kill]] the criminals present. Bliss was stopped by former Spice Runner [[Poe Dameron]], and Adlerber and the other criminals were allowed to escape.<ref name="Free Fall" />
