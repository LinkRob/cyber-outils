'''Bobarian Afol''', better known as '''Jedi Bob''', was a [[Jedi]] who once sought to prevent the [[space-time continuum]] of [[the galaxy]] from being disrupted.<ref name="RebuildTheGalaxy" />

A [[Force Builders|Force Builder]] [[Jedi]], he disobeyed his superiors and tampered with [[The Cornerstone of the Galaxy]], unintentionally causing his galaxy to reset, resulting in a completely new one that he could not revert back to its original state. For years afterward, he protected The Cornerstone in a hidden [[Jedi temple]] on [[Fennesa]], until it was removed from its spot in the temple wall by [[Sig Greebling]], resetting the galaxy yet again.

Afol accompanied Sig on a journey across the galaxy to restore The Cornerstone to its spot in the temple, until Sig found out via his changed brother [[Darth Devastator]] that his galaxy could not be restored, which Afol confirmed he had lied about. After being left for dead by Dev, and Sig giving Darth Devastator The Cornerstone, he returned to help Sig take back The Cornerstone from him. Afol then took Sig and the crew they had amassed along the way back to the temple, and reburied the temple underground to protect The Cornerstone for the foreseeable future.<ref name="RebuildTheGalaxyTwo">{{RebuildTheGalaxy|Part Two}}</ref>

Bobarian Afol appeared in the [[canon]] continuity in the non-canon [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] special ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Rebuild the Galaxy]]'', which was released on [[September 13]], [[2024]].<ref name="RebuildSWcom">{{SW|url=news/lego-star-wars-rebuild-the-galaxy|text=Get Ready for Darth Jar Jar, TIE-Wings, and More in ''LEGO Star Wars: Rebuild the Galaxy''}}</ref> He<ref name="Dark Falcon" /> is voiced by [[Bobby Moynihan]].<ref name="RebuildSWcom" /> Bob originated as a non-canon character in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the LEGO set [[:w:c:brickipedia:7163 Republic Gunship|7163 Republic Gunship]],<ref name="7163Gunship">{{LEGOCite|aotc|7163|Republic Gunship}}</ref> and was first identified as Bob in the [[2009]] LEGO reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Visual Dictionary]]''.<ref name="LEGOVD">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Bob's last name, Afol, is similar to the acronym "AFOL," which means "[[Wikipedia:Lego fandom#Adult fans of Lego|adult fans of Lego]]."<ref name="LEGOAFOL">{{LEGOWebCite|url=/en-us/articles/life/ll5funfactsaboutafolsaug19-57c77740ce1d42cd94cadb3b3d1d6f7b|subdomain=kids|text=5 fun facts about AFOLs}}</ref>
