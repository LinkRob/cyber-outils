'''Askajians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[humanoid]] [[species]] that were [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Askaji]]. On their homeworld, they lived in tribes led by a [[chief]].<ref name="Alien Archive" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Klefan Opus]]<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> and [[Yarna d'al' Gargan]], who was forced to dance for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] as one of his [[Slavery|slaves]], were both Askajians.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />
