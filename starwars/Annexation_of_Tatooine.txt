{{Quote|In the years leading up to the Battle of Yavin, Tatooine retained aspects of independence from the Empire, something the residents were apparently highly defensive of, but over time Imperial patrols in the space around the planet and in some of the major cities began to increase. ''[&hellip;]'' Tatooine, and other worlds along the borderline of the Empire's territory, remained locked in this dual existence of being partially occupied and colonized without being formally conquered, for much of the Empire's existence.|[[Beaumont Kin]], ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''|<ref name="RiseFall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
At some point between [[9 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date note" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] was '''annexed''' by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Battles" /> The Empire largely remained away from Tatooine<ref name="Patrolling the Cantina">{{Topps|set=2022 Topps Star Wars Chrome Galaxy|cardname=Patrolling the Cantina|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/328979/cid/20392575}}</ref> but maintained a [[Tatooine garrison|military presence]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 5">[[Obi-Wan 5|''Obi-Wan'' 5]]</ref> In the [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], Imperial patrols in the [[Realspace|space]] surrounding the planet and in a number of major [[City|cities]] began to increase. Along with other worlds on the edge of the Empire's territory, Tatooine was not formally conquered but instead partially colonized and occupied for a large duration of the [[Imperial Era|reign of the Empire]].<ref name="RiseFall" /> The Empire finally arrived in force<ref name="Patrolling the Cantina" /> in [[Secret mission to Tatooine|pursuit]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[droid]]s [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]]<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>
