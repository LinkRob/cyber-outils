'''Fiona''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who worked at an [[Unidentified underground mining colony|underground mining colony]] on an [[Unidentified airless moon (Outer Rim)|airless moon]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. She was [[Marriage|married]] to [[Ryland]], and they had a daughter named [[Laina]]. When her daughter was six [[Standard month|months]] old, Fiona was framed by a coworker, [[Corbin]], for being a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[spy]], and [[Death|killed]]. After her death, her husband and daughter were spirited to safety by relatives. Two [[Standard year|years]] after Fiona's death, her husband had a [[hologram]] of her that he kept for their daughter.<ref name="Laina" />
