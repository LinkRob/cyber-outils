'''Enviro-engineering''' was a type of science related to the creation and replication of different environments. [[Samera Ra-oon]], the [[coordinator]] of the [[Valo Republic Fair]], described the [[Republic Fair|fair's]] [[World Pavilions|sky-islands]] as a "marvel" of enviro-engineering because they were able to replicate the cold environment of [[Rhinnal]] in [[Valo]]'s [[summer]] heat.<ref name="Rising Storm" />
