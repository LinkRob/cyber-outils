A '''blaster rifle''' was owned by [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Sidon Ithano]]. The [[heavy bore rifle]] was used by the "Crimson Corsair" during the time of the [[Cold War]] in the [[New Republic Era]], after he had stolen the [[weapon]] from a member of the [[Kanjiklub]] gang. During the same time, the Kanjiklubber [[Crokind Shand]] also used a visually identical weapon. Like other weapons used by the gang, both blaster rifles utilized makeshift components, with the [[Stock|shoulder stock]]s were made out of [[bone]] (Shand's using that of a [[Roggwart]]) and hand-made [[bayonet]]s being attached to the [[barrel]]s with [[Color|brown]] cord. Ithano also owned a [[blaster pistol]] with a similar design.<ref name="TFA VD" /> During the [[Battle of Exegol]]<ref name="IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> in [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Ithano instead used<ref name="IX" /> a [[Czerka Arms]] [[Longswat-44 sniper blaster rifle]] in the same crimson color as his [[armor]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
