'''Dennis Pellegrom''' is a fan interviewer who was active between [[2005]]<ref name="About" /> and [[2023]]. During that period he conducted 225 interviews<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|StarWarsINTRVWS|status/1622283193066864641|StarWarsInterviews.com|date=2023-02-05|archivedate=20230206105952}}</ref> of people who worked on ''[[Star Wars]]'', and published them on his website: [[Star Wars Interviews]]. Some of his interviews have been cited in several official books:<ref name="About" /> ''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]'',<ref name="MakingOfRotJ">''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]'', Bibliography - Websites</ref> ''[[Star Wars: Stormtroopers: Beyond the Armor]]'',<ref name="Stormtroopers">''[[Star Wars: Stormtroopers: Beyond the Armor]]'', Bibliography</ref> ''[[Star Wars Icons: Han Solo]]'',<ref name="Icons">''[[Star Wars Icons: Han Solo]]'', Notes</ref> ''[[The Star Wars Archives: Episodes IV–VI, 1977–1983]]''<ref name="Archives 77-83">''[[The Star Wars Archives: Episodes IV–VI, 1977–1983]]'', Bibliography - Websites</ref> and ''[[The Star Wars Archives: Episodes I–III, 1999–2005]]''.<ref name="Archives 99-05">''[[The Star Wars Archives: Episodes I–III, 1999–2005]]'', Bibliography - Websites</ref>
