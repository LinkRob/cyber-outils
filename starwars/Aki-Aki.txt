The '''Aki-Aki''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]]<ref name="Episode IX" /> [[elephantine]]<ref name="Holocron" /> [[species]]<ref name="Episode IX" /> [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Pasaana]]. They were generally peaceful and hospitable, making them welcoming to offworlders when they arrived on their planet.<ref name="TROS VD" /> According to [[Unkar Plutt]], they were very literal.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref> The Aki-Aki maintained a pre-starflight level of civilization, but did adopt a number of advanced tools to aid in their life on Pasaana. Aki-Aki history was not always peaceful, however; their species faced strife between scattered clans, though they eventually worked together to survive Pasaana's harsh climate.<ref name="TROS VD" /> Aki-Aki had bifurcated trunks which grew throughout their lifespan. When they were born, they possessed only a singular short trunk which bifurcated around maturity.

==Behind the scenes==
The Aki-Aki species first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. In the English subtitles to the film, the name of the species is misspelled as "Acky Acky."<ref name="Episode IX" />
