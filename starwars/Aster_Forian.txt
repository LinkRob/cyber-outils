'''Aster Forian''' was a [[human]] [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. In [[230 BBY]], Forian was in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] when the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Deployment of the stormseeds|created]] a [[hyperspace]] barrier known as the [[Stormwall]]. After receiving a transmission from the Nihil [[Eye of the Nihil|leader]] [[Marchion Ro]], the [[Jedi High Council]] dispatched the Padawan to retrieve [[Jedi Master]] [[Soleil Agra]].

Rushing to find Agra, Forian located her in the [[Jedi temple|temple]] talking to Jedi Master [[Elzar Mann]] and attempted to explain what had happened but struggled due to shock. Mann calmed the Padawan down, and Forian informed them about the Stormwall's formation and the destruction of a [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[Republic Defense Coalition Outreach Fleet|fleet]] when it became operational.

Aster Forian was a [[human]] who served as a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 2" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The part of [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 2|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 2]] in which Aster Forian appears takes place immediately after the [[Deployment of the stormseeds|creation]] of the [[Stormwall]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]].</ref> one [[Standard week|week]] after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]], Forian was present at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 2" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] when the Nihil [[Deployment of the stormseeds|sealed off]] a [[Occlusion Zone|large portion]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] and left it stranded behind a [[hyperspace]] barrier known as the [[Stormwall]]. Following this, Forian was sent by the [[Jedi High Council]] to retrieve [[Jedi Master]] [[Soleil Agra]], as a transmission from the Nihil's [[Eye of the Nihil|leader]], [[Marchion Ro]], had come in.<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 2" />

The Padawan found Agra, along with Jedi Master [[Elzar Mann]], talking at one of the [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple's]] terraces and rushed to the Masters. Agra spotted Forian and asked them what the matter was. In response, the Padawan informed<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 2" /> her<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi" /> of their assignment by the [[Jedi Council]], noting that something significant had occurred and that a transmission had been received. When trying to explain what the marauders had done, the Padawan struggled because of shock. In response, Mann told Forian to breathe before<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 2" /> he<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi" /> asked the Padawan what was going on.<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 2" />

Forian then explained to the two Jedi Masters about the Nihil's creation of the Stormwall and informed them they could not travel through it in hyperspace or send communications through it. The Padawan finished by noting that a [[Republic Defense Coalition Outreach Fleet|fleet]] of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] had been destroyed when the Stormwall had been activated.<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 2" />

==Personality and traits==
Aster Forian had [[Color|blue]] [[eye]]s, light skin, and black [[hair]]. When they found Jedi Master Soleil Agra, the Padawan had a bewildered expression and was unable to at first explain about the Nihil's creation of the Stormwall. After Jedi Master Elzar Mann calmed them down, however, Forian was able to inform them of what had happened.<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 2" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a member of the Jedi Order, Aster Forian was [[Force-sensitive]] and [[Jedi training|trained]] to [[Force power|wield]] [[the Force]].<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 2" />

==Equipment==
The Padawan wore white and gold [[Temple robes|Jedi robes]] and had a [[Padawan braid]]. Forian wore a green-colored [[Padawan sash|sash]]<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 2" /> that was the same color as their [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' [[Padawan]]s.</ref>
