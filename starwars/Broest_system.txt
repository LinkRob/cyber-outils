The '''Broest system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]]<ref name="Interior" /> that fell within grid square L-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> [[Ruica]] [[Plant|seeds]] from the location [[Broest]]<ref name="L2">[[Princess Leia 2|''Princess Leia'' 2]]</ref> in the Broest system<ref name="Appendix" /> were transported across [[the galaxy]] to the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] for [[farmer]]s to grow during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="L2" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Broest system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Broest system/Legends|Broest system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[1999]] novel ''[[X-Wing: Solo Command]]'', which was written by [[Aaron Allston]].<ref name="X-Wing">''[[X-Wing: Solo Command]]''</ref>
