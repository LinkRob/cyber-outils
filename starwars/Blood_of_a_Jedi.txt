"'''Blood of a Jedi'''" is the sixth and final episode of the [[Return of the Jedi (radio)|''Return of the Jedi'' radio drama]]. It first aired on [[National Public Radio]] on [[December 10]], [[1996]].<ref name="Kushner" /> The episode covers the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]], the climax of both the radio drama and the film {{Film|VI}}.

==Plot summary==
The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] commandos burst through the backdoor of the [[Endor shield generator bunker]]. [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]], and [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Princess Leia]] disarm the troops inside while [[Bren Derlin/Legends|Major Darlin]] begins planting demolition charges to destroy the generator, which will clear the way for the incoming [[Alliance Fleet/Legends|Alliance Fleet]] to attack the [[Death Star II/Legends|Death Star]]. But outside the bunker, [[C-3PO/Legends|See-Threepio]], [[R2-D2/Legends|Artoo-Detoo]], and [[Wicket Wystri Warrick/Legends|Wicket]] witness a large reinforcement, complete with [[All Terrain Scout Transport/Legends|scout walkers]], approaching. To Threepio's dismay, both Wicket and Artoo disappear, leaving him alone and unsure how to help his friends.

The Alliance Fleet [[Reversion/Legends|emerges from hyperspace]]. In the ''[[Millennium Falcon/Legends|Millennium Falcon]]'', [[General/Legends|General]] [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Nien Nunb/Legends|his copilot]] check their [[Sensor/Legends|sensors]] for the Death Star's [[Deflector shield/Legends|deflector shield]] and realize that they are being jammed&mdash;suggesting that the Empire knew they were coming. Almost immediately ships of the [[Imperial Navy/Legends|imperial starfleet]] appear, closing the trap. The Death Star itself fires on a Rebel cruiser, showing its weapons to be fully operational. [[Gial Ackbar/Legends|Admiral Ackbar]] orders a retreat, but Lando insists that the Rebels stay longer to wait for Han's team to deactivate the shield. 

In the [[Emperor's Throne Room/Legends|Emperor's Throne Room]], [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] taunts [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] as they watch the unfolding battle. He urges Luke to embrace the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|Dark Side of the Force]] because only then will he be able to defeat him. 

Back on the moon's surface, the captive Rebels are escorted out of the bunker. From atop a small hill, Threepio and Artoo call out to the troopers. When a few go up to apprehend them, the [[Ewok/Legends|Ewok]] warriors stage an ambush, allowing the Rebels to evade capture and fight back. Han and Leia set about getting the bunker door back open. They call Threepio and Artoo to help. Out in space, Lando orders his fighters to counterattack the Imperial [[Star Destroyer/Legends|star destroyers]]; he still holds out hope that the commandos will manage to take down the Death Star's shield.

In the throne room, Luke, overwhelmed by Palpatine and Vader's words, ignites [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber/Legends|his lightsaber]]. Vader follows suit, and the two begin to duel. Palpatine cheers on Luke's aggression, prompting him to slow down and adopt an evasive strategy. But Vader's attack is relentless. Father and son fight furiously to the Emperor's perverse delight.

The droids come to the bunker door, but before Artoo can hack into it, he is shot. Han's attempt to hotwire it open fails as well. Han and Leia surrender to an approaching scout walker that turns out to be piloted by Chewbacca. Major Derlin now also approaches with a large force of Ewoks. Han boards the walker and impersonates an imperial officer in a message to the bunker control room, hoping to draw out the remaining Imperials. The commander obligingly opens the door; the Rebels and Ewoks then surround and capture the imperial troops. Derlin again leads the team to place demolition charges.

Vader continues pursuing Luke. He now senses that Luke's thoughts dwell on his sister, so he threatens Leia. This again provokes Luke to attack furiously and cut off his father's hand. Palpatine approaches, demanding that Luke slay his father and thereby embrace the Dark Side. Aboard the Headquarters Frigate, Ackbar sees that the shield is down and orders the fighters to begin their attack on the Death Star's reactor. Lando speeds the ''Falcon'' into the station's interior, followed by all surviving Rebel fighters. 

[[File:VadersMaster-RotjRadio.flac|thumb|Vader renounces and kills his master.]]
Luke sees the battle turning and tells Palpatine that he and his empire are doomed. Palpatine unleashes "[[Force lightning/Legends|the lightning of pure will]]" against Luke, [[Torture/Legends|torturing]] him with the full strength of the Dark Side of the Force. He orders Vader to dispose of Luke's body once Palpatine is finished with him. Luke begs his father instead to save him. Vader suddenly turns on his master, asserting his freedom from Palpatine and from the Dark Side. He throws Palpatine, not Luke, down a nearby core shaft, meanwhile taking the brunt of his lightning attack.

Deep inside the Death Star, Lando and [[Wedge Antilles/Legends|Wedge]] approach the main reactor and destroy it. They have to dash out the way that they came to escape the station before the its systems overload completely. Back in the throne room, a dying Anakin Skywalker asks Luke to remove his mask for him so that they can see one another face to face. He tells Luke that he is proud of him, then breathes his last.

The Rebels on Endor cheer as they see the Death Star explode in the sky. Leia assures Han that Luke escaped it alive. Han offers to step aside when Luke arrives, but Leia tells him that Luke is her brother. Leia tells Han to be honest about his feelings and kiss her. That night, Luke is back on Endor. As Ewoks and Rebels celebrate all around, Luke, accompanied only by See-Threepio, lights a [[Funeral of Anakin Skywalker/Legends|funeral pyre]] to burn Anakin's armor and mourn his father. The Ewok music begins as all the friends reunite. Luke tells Threepio about the vision that he is experiencing: the [[Force ghost/Legends|spirits]] of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan]], [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]], and Anakin, three comrades in arms reunited after death.

==Continuity==
Most ''Star Wars'' radio episodes simplify the films' structure, avoiding rapid cuts and changes of point of view. "Blood of a Jedi," by contrast, rapidly cycles between the action on Endor, inside the Death Star throne room, and in the space battle. A few elements from the film are simplified: most of the Ewok counterattack, including Chewbacca's capture of a walker, happens off-mic and is explained through the dialogue of other characters. So is Luke's escape from the Death Star with Vader's armor. The destruction of the ''[[Executor/Legends|Executor]],'' a dramatic moment in the film, is omitted entirely.<ref name="Film">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

In the film, a Rebel starfighter designated [[Red Three/Legends|Red Three]] is shown being shot down, and just after this, [[Arvel Crynyd/Legends|Green Leader]] says the line, "Three of them coming in, twenty degrees."<ref name="Film" /> In the radio episode, a similar line is given instead to Red Three: "Three eyeballs, twenty degrees high." In [[1997]], ''[[Star Wars: The Annotated Screenplays]]'' also attributed the line to Red Three.<ref name="Annotated Screenplays">''[[Star Wars: The Annotated Screenplays]]''</ref> The two film actors resemble one another. The radio episode also has Red Three fly inside the Death Star with the other Rebel fighters, again contradicting the film.<ref name="Dramatization">[[Return of the Jedi: The National Public Radio Dramatization|''Return of the Jedi'': The National Public Radio Dramatization]]</ref>

Luke mentions escaping in [[Emperor's personal shuttle/Legends|the Emperor's personal shuttle]]; this contradicts other sources which say that he used [[ST 321|Vader's shuttle]] instead.<ref name="The Rise and Fall of Darth Vader">''[[The Rise and Fall of Darth Vader]]''</ref> The episode, produced in 1996, features the song "[[Ewok Celebration]]," which was replaced in [[1997]] by "[[Victory Celebration]]" for the film's [[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition|Special Edition]].<ref name="Film" />
