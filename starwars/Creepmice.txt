'''Creepmice''' were a type of [[Mouse|mice]]. In [[382 BBY]], [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Children of the Open Hand|member]] [[Yana Ro]] encountered the mice on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]]. [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Sifo-Dyas]] and [[Dooku]] sneaked into the [[Archive of Forbidden Artifacts]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] in [[88 BBY]], with the former [[youngling]] making a comment about creepmice.

==Description==
Creepmice were a type of [[Mouse|mice]] that could be found living within the [[Jedi Archives]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]<ref name="D:JL" /> and in alleyways in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]].<ref name="Vengeance" />

==History==
[[File:YanaRo-2022THR9.png|thumb|right|300px|Yana Ro saw creepmice while on Jedha.]]
In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="DateVeng">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[Evereni]] [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Children of the Open Hand|member]] [[Yana Ro]] encountered a family of creepmice scurrying away from a mangy [[felnox]] in an alleyway in Jedha's Holy City.<ref name="Vengeance" /> In [[88 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Sifo-Dyas]] and [[Dooku]] planned to break into the [[Archive of Forbidden Artifacts]], a hidden [[Bogan Collection|vault]] within the Archives. The pair entered the [[library]] late at night to avoid being caught by [[Chief Librarian]] [[Restelly Quist]] or other [[Jedi]]. Dooku was nervous about being spotted, but Sifo-Dyas assured him that only creepmice were present in the Archives at such a late [[time]]. Dooku later recounted the incident to his [[House Serenno|sister]] [[Jenza]] via [[holo-letter]].<ref name="D:JL" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="DJL-JournalsDate">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku gave [[Datacard|data disks]] of his conversations with Jenza to his recently recruited [[Sith assassin]]&mdash;[[Asajj Ventress]]&mdash;for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into Jenza's location, and she listened to the message mentioning creepmice.<ref name="D:JL" />

==Behind the scenes==
Creepmice were first mentioned in a flashback in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="D:JL" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin-Download">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The spelling of creepmice was confirmed by the audio drama's [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]]<ref name="DJL script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin-Download" /> The mice first appeared in the [[2023]] young-adult novel [[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance|'' Path of Vengeance'']], also written by Scott and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Vengeance" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2023}}</ref>
