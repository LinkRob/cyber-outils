During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Bothan Spynet]] undertook an '''intelligence operation''' on behalf of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. During the course of the mission, a massive [[Death|loss]] of life occurred, including many [[Bothan]]s, but the spynet learned that the [[Galactic Empire]] was constructing a [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] at the [[moon]] [[Endor]] in the [[Moddell sector]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> The Bothans also managed to secure footage from Endor of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II]]'s construction, which less than a [[Standard week|week]] after the operation, was shown to [[Alliance High Command]] and the Alliance's top [[admiral]]s and [[general]]s at a meeting on [[Zastiga]].<ref name="Moving target" />
