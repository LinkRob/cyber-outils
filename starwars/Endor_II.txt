'''Endor II''' was one of the two [[star]]s<ref name="AEYNTK" /> located at the center of the [[Endor system]] in the [[Moddell sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="USW" /> The [[Star system|system's]] other star was named [[Endor I]], and the pair was orbited by the [[gas giant]] [[Endor (planet)|Endor]].<ref name="AEYNTK" />

{{Star-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Endor II was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]'', which was released in [[2015]].<ref name="AEYNTK" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the Endor system only had one sun, called [[Ibleam]].<ref name="atlas">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
