'''Dallenor''' was a [[desert]] [[planet]]<ref name="Age of Republic" /> located in the [[Aldino sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="Appendix" /> An [[Dallenor Excavation|excavation]] led by the [[archaeologist]] [[Clatriffe]] discovered a [[Dallenor holocron|Jedi holocron]] on the planet, notifying the [[Jedi Order]]. To inspect the [[holocron]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] [[Mission to Dallenor|traveled]] to Dallenor on behalf of the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]].<ref name="Age of Republic" />
