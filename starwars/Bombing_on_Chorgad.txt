By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''bombing on Chorgad''' was carried out by a crew of the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate]] against their rival, the [[Unbroken Clan]] criminal syndicate. The [[bomb]]ing, which took place on the astronomical object [[Chorgad]] while the two syndicates [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate–Unbroken Clan war|warred]] with each other, resulted in the [[death]]s of a number of Unbroken Clan [[soldier]]s.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Nautolan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Nakano Lash]] was informed of the bombing by a [[Unidentified human (Ruusan)|friend]] of hers when she asked them for news. The friend told Lash that the bombing sounded like a problem of hers, as the Nautolan had started the war between the syndicates years earlier after she killed the [[Khamus|heir]] of the Mourner's Wail during a [[Mission to Corellia (Nakano Lash's crew)|job]] that went awry.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> That same year,<ref name="Date" /> the Unbroken Clan's [[General]] [[Vukorah]] carried out an [[Attack on a spice run|unapproved attack]] on a [[spice]] run at the [[Smugglers' Refueling Station]] in<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Kessel sector]] by the Mourner's Wail to avenge those lost at Chorgad.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bombing on Chorgad was mentioned in the [[Bounty Hunters 3|third issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> on [[June 24]], 2020.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80122/star_wars_bounty_hunters_2020_3|text=Star Wars: Bounty Hunters (2020) #3}}</ref>
