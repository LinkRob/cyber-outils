'''Dolomite''' was a material with a rhombohedral crystalline structure. After the [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]] ran studies on a [[Sith artifact]] known as the [[thornseed]], she determined its hardness, density, and structure were similar to dolomite, though it also differed in some aspects.<ref name="Into the Light">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref>
