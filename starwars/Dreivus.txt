'''Dreivus''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="BTC" /> located in the [[Oeiros sector]]'s [[Dreivus system]], within an intersecting portion of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> Situated at the coordinates P-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it was where the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] once fought<ref name="BTC" /> sometime by the [[Standard year|year]] [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate|the mention of Dreivus}}</ref> The [[company]] celebrated after, and the [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] [[Hazram Namir]] made an impression on some [[fire]] dancers on Dreivus.<ref name="BTC" /> In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> another [[soldier]] named [[Twitch]] brought up the fighting on Dreivus to other members of the company on their [[CR90 corvette]], the ''[[Thunderstrike]]''. Whilst the [[corvette]] was put in for repairs on the [[junker]] [[Planet|world]] [[Heap Nine]] a few [[Standard day|days]] later, Namir also brought up the events on Dreivus with the [[Besalisk]] [[Gadren]], who remembered Namir's [[time]] with the fire dancers.<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dreivus was mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
