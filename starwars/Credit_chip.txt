'''Credit chips''',<ref name="whatwookiee" /> or '''Credit Data Cards''',<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> were a means of carrying [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], standard [[currency]] in the [[Galactic Republic]] and later the [[Galactic Empire]]. They were small enough to be convenient for carrying on one's person and came in a number of common denominations for easy exchange.

Republic-era credit chips were smaller, narrow rectangles that were [[color]]ed either gold or silver depending on the denomination.<ref name="Evil Plans">{{TCW|Evil Plans}}</ref>

Imperial credit chips were larger, wider rectangles with rounded corners, and were all gold-colored.<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref>

Credit chips were not considered acceptable currency at [[Watto's junkshop]]. All advertised prices were in [[coin]]s.<ref name="whatwookiee" />
