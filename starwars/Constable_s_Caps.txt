'''Constable's Caps''' was a [[Foodstuff|dish]] consisting of [[Color|gray]] [[fungus]] stuffed with various fillings. The dish's name was inspired by the [[Kyuzo]] [[Niima Outpost Militia|Outpost Militia]] of [[Niima Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]],<ref name="Cookbook" /> who were led by a [[constable]] and wore [[Kyuzo war shield]] [[helmet]]s<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> that bore a resemblance to the fungus caps used in the dish.<ref name="Cookbook" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for the dish in his [[Book|cookbook]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''.<ref name="Cookbook" />

In the book, Tuggs suggested that the best place to find large fungus to use in the recipe was the fungal [[forest]]s of the<ref name="Cookbook" /> planet<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|skako-minor|Skako Minor}}</ref> [[Skako Minor]], as the mushrooms of Jakku were rarely big enough to stuff. He stated that a wide range of fillings could be used to stuff the fungus, including diced meat, mixed [[vegetable]]s, or [[imitation killcrab]]. Tuggs provided a recipe using his favorite filling, which he claimed tasted spectacular.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Constable's Caps was created as one of the recipes in the [[2019]] cookbook ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe uses [[Wikipedia:Agaricus bisporus|button mushrooms]] cooked with [[Wikipedia:Butter|butter]] and minced [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic]], then stuffed and further baked with a mixture of [[Wikipedia:Bread crumbs#Panko|panko breadcrumbs]], grated [[Wikipedia:Parmigiano-Reggiano|Parmesan cheese]], finely chopped [[Wikipedia:Scallion|green onions]], chopped [[Wikipedia:Parsley|parsley]], [[Wikipedia:Garam masala|garam masala]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], and [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|pepper]].<ref name="Cookbook" />
