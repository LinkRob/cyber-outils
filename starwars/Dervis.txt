'''Dervis''' was an individual who served as a [[major]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The major was part of the Alliance forces stationed in [[Echo Base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]] when it was [[Battle of Hoth|attacked]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[3 ABY]].

At the beginning of the battle, [[Rebel trooper|infantry]] under Dervis's command hurried out of the entrance of the base's [[North Hangar 7]] in order reach the base's defensive trenches. Ultimately the Alliance were defeated by the Empire at Hoth, but managed to evacuate many of the base's personnel.<ref name="CL 2016" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Derivs was first mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], in a section written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="CL 2016" /> The character was originally mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2004]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars Trilogy]]'', which was also written by Luceno.<ref name="OTWorlds">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars Trilogy]]''</ref>
