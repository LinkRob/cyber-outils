The '''blutfish''' was a type of [[fish]].<ref name="SWA 12" /> Between [[1 ABY]] and [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"Squad Goals, Part 2" featured [[Rogue Squadron]] being commanded by [[Luke Skywalker]]. Rogue Squadron formed during [[Star Wars (2015) 52|''Star Wars'' (2015) 52]], which ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' dates to one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]], or [[1 ABY]]. ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' and {{StoryCite|story=Rendezvous Point (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}} establish that [[Wedge Antilles]] replaced Luke as [[commander]] after the [[Battle of Hoth]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]]. Therefore, "Squad Goals, Part 2" takes place some time between 1 [[Galactic standard calendar|ABY]] and 3 ABY.</ref> [[Rebel pilot]] [[Ibti Myrak]] was stationed on the [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80A ''Home One'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]], the ''[[Home One]]'' after her reckless actions during the [[Attack in the Cilpar system|attack]] in the [[Cilpar system]]. But Myrak wanted to return to the action and prepared to leave, ignoring her orders. [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] told her that following orders was more important then going back to fight and ordered her to the command deck. But Myrak was angry and insulted Ackbar, calling him a blutfish before being pulled away by [[chief]] [[technician]] [[Garn Stewer]].<ref name="SWA 12" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blutfish was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' canon in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story, "[[Squad Goals, Part 2]]" in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 12|twelfth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars Adventures (2020)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] anthology comic series. The story was written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="SWA 12" /> and published by [[IDW Publishing]] on [[November 24]], [[2021]].<ref name="Preview">{{WebCite|url=https://comic-watch.com/news/sneak-peek-preview-of-idw-publishings-star-wars-adventures-12-on-sale-11-24|text=SNEAK PEEK: Preview of IDW Publishing's Star Wars Adventures #12 (On Sale 11/24!)|work=[https://comic-watch.com Comic Watch]|archivedate=20211121235637}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity [[Blutfish/Legends|blutfish]] was mentioned in the [[West End Games]] adventure for [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]], ''[[Death in the Undercity]]'' published in [[1990]].<ref name="Undercity">''[[Death in the Undercity]]''</ref>
