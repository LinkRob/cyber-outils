A '''biome cover''' was a piece of equipment that could be installed on the roof of an [[Akiva]]n home to allow individuals to live on the roof. In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[technician]] talked with an [[engineer]] while in [[The Alcazar]], a [[cantina]] in [[Myrra]], about installing a biome cover on their roof after their home flooded.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Biome covers were mentioned an ambient conversation that players can overhead in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
