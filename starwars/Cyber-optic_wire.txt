A '''Cyber-optic wire''' was a type of [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] that gave people sharp [[Eye|vision]].

[[Cycyed Ock]] had a Cyber-optic wire connected directly to his [[brain]] which gave him sharper vision, allowing him to analyze crystals for faults, examine microcircuitry, and throw his [[vibrorang]] with unerring accuracy.<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
