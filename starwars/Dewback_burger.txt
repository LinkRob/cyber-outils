'''Dewback burgers''' were a type of [[burger]] made using the meat of<ref name="FITYMI" /> the [[dewback]], a creature [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="InheritanceDate">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-author-roundtable|text=The High Republic Author Roundtable: "The End of the Beginning"}} establishes the events of "Galactic Tales: Inheritance" take place during the events of {{Film|II}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> following a messy skirmish in [[Dex's Diner]] on the planet [[Coruscant]], the [[Mirialan]] [[Servit]] asked the owner, [[Dexter Jettster]], if he could have a dewback burger instead of the [[brakkenback stew]] he had been offered earlier as it had been strewn all over the floor during the fight.<ref name="Inheritance">{{InsiderCite|211|Galactic Tales: Inheritance}}</ref>

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Fake It Till You Make It]]" takes place concurrently with {{Film|V}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> when the [[Lepi]] criminal [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] visited the [[Baron Administrator]] of the planet [[Bespin]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], he noticed a case full of [[aurodium]] ingots, considering it enough for the business he hoped to set up with enough left over for a lifetime's supply of dewback burgers.<ref name="FITYMI" />

==Behind the scenes==
The dewback burger was first mentioned in "[[Fake It Till You Make It]]," a short story written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in the [[2020]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="FITYMI" />
