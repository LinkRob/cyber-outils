'''Bimmini''' was a [[language]].<ref name="Inquisitor" /> Between [[22 BBY]] and around [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'' establishes that [[Uumay Hawlatha]] and [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris']] [[meditation]] took place between the latter's [[mission to Thule]] and her [[mission to Olgothon 3]]. The novel establishes the former took place a few [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], dated to [[22 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', and states the latter mission occurred approximately two [[Standard year|years]] later. Therefore, the meditation must have taken place between 22 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and around [[20 BBY]].</ref> while [[Meditation|meditating]] together in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] [[garden]]s on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], the [[Bimm]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Uumay Hawlatha]] sang a [[Music|song]] in Bimmini to his mentee, the [[Pkorian]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]], which sounded reminiscent of birdsong to her.<ref name="Inquisitor" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bimmini appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Delilah S. Dawson]]'s novel ''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'', which was published in [[2023]].<ref name="Inquisitor" /> [[Bimmini/Legends|Bimmini]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, first appearing in [[Heir to the Empire|''Heir to the Empire'']], the first novel in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[Star Wars: The Thrawn Trilogy|Thrawn Trilogy]], which was released in [[1991]].<ref name="Heir to the Empire">''[[Heir to the Empire]]''</ref> It was first identified as Bimm in [[Daniel Wallace]]'s reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]'', published in [[1998]],<ref name="TEGTPAM">''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]''</ref> and as Bimmini in [[Steve Miller]] and [[Owen K.C. Stephens|Owen Stephens]]' reference book ''[[Alien Anthology]]'', which was released in [[2001]].<ref name="Alien Anthology">''[[Alien Anthology]]''</ref>
