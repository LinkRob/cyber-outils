The '''Blade Master''' was a [[Krath war droid/Legends|Krath war droid]] with arms containing retractable metal swords who led a group of other [[Krath/Legends|Krath]] war [[Droid/Legends|droids]]. The droids crashed centuries before the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], on a planet inhabited by the technologically-primitive [[Numm]], whom they terrorized until being reprogrammed by [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]].
