'''Darth Tenebrous''' was the [[Sith Master]] of [[Darth Plagueis]]. Tenebrous was suspected to have designed the ''[[Scimitar]]'', the personal [[starship]] of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]] that was officially designed by<ref name="BMF" /> [[engineer]] and starship designer [[Raith Sienar]].<ref name="Epic Battles">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Visual Guide: Epic Battles]]''</ref>

As with a number of other ancient [[Sith Lord]]s, Tenebrous served as the namesake of one of the [[Sith trooper]] [[legion]]s of the [[Sith Eternal]], a [[Sith cult|cult]] led by<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Plagueis' [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], [[Darth Sidious]],<ref name="BioGal1">{{Databank|darth-sidious-biography-gallery|Darth Sidious Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Specifically, the [[Tenebrous Legion|26th Legion]] was dubbed the "Tenebrous Legion," and as [[Sith]] history was kept secret from the rest of [[the galaxy]], only [[Sith cultist|Sith Eternal cultists]] were aware of the name's significance.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DarthTenebrousHS-SWI130.jpg|thumb|right|120px|Darth Tenebrous, as pictured in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]]
Darth Tenebrous was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 44|forty-fourth issue]] of the magazine ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'', published by [[De Agostini]]<ref name="BMF" /> around [[November 4]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|44}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Darth Tenebrous/Legends|Darth Tenebrous]] was originally introduced in "[[The Tenebrous Way]]," a short story in [[Star Wars Insider 130|''Star Wars Insider'' 130]], published by [[Titan Magazines]]<ref name="The Tenebrous Way">{{InsiderCite|130|The Tenebrous Way}}</ref> on [[December 13]], [[2011]].<ref name="Insider Date">{{GameSpot|subdomain=comicvine|url=star-wars-insider-130/4000-445535|text=Star Wars Insider #130}}</ref> The story was written by [[Matthew Stover]] and illustrated by [[Brian Rood]].<ref name="The Tenebrous Way" /> The character was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Bith/Legends|Bith]] and was also identified as Rugess Nome in the novel [[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']], written by [[James Luceno]] and published in [[2012]].<ref name="Darth Plagueis">[[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']]</ref>
