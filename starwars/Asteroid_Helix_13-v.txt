'''Asteroid Helix 13-v''',<ref name="Aphra 38" /> or the '''Comet Helix''',<ref name="Aphra 40">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 40|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 40]]</ref> was an [[asteroid]] located in the [[Asteroid-Helix]]. The [[The Force|Force]] cult known as the [[J'doon]] constructed a shrine<ref name="Aphra 38" /> known as the [[Helix temple]]<ref name="Aphra 39">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 39|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 39]]</ref> on the asteroid. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a cell of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] camped on the asteroid while traveling from their previous base<ref name="Aphra 38" /> to [[Hoth]].<ref name="Aphra 39" /> The [[scholar]] [[Korin Aphra]], attempting to investigate the ruins, encountered the rebel cell, who provided him with rations and made him leave. After capturing Aphra, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] traveled to the shrine with the [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]], the daughter of Korin Aphra. Despite having left Asteroid Helix 13-v,<ref name="Aphra 38" /> the rebels continued to watch over the asteroid base using hidden [[camera]]s, learning that the Empire was searching for information on their new base.<ref name="Aphra 39" /> While in the shrine, Vader experienced a [[Force vision|vision]], and was compelled to leave the asteroid, declaring that the rebels were no longer present. After leaving the asteroid, Vader ordered [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE bombers]] to destroy it.<ref name="Aphra 38" />
