'''Chita''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[smuggler]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], she was hired to bring [[Arluu]], a [[prince]] being pursued by the [[Galactic Empire]], to safety.<ref name="Smuggler" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The main character is a rogue and I wanted to make it an action-packed fun adventure. When we were discussing the project, I thought of Han Solo, and the design became similar.|Masahiko Otsuka|<ref name="First Look" />}}
Chita first appeared in "[[The Smuggler (short film)|The Smuggler]]," a non-[[canon]] short film directed by [[Masahiko Otsuka]] that released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Trigger (animation studio)|Studio Trigger]], released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. The character was first introduced at the ''Star Wars: Visions'' panel at [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]] on [[April 19]], 2025.<ref name="Day 3">{{SWYouTube|83cNchT1gdA|Star Wars Celebration - DAY 3 Replay|livestream=p20GM68wyC4}}</ref> She is voiced by [[Ami Maeshima]] in the original [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] version and by [[Emma Myers]] in the English dub.<ref name="Smuggler" />
