'''Fourth Officer''' was a [[military rank]] in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet|Expansionary Defense Fleet]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The rank of Fourth Officer was mentioned in ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final installment of ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''. However, the novel refers to [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," as the Fourth Officer of the [[Patrol cruiser|Chiss patrol cruiser]] ''[[Parala]]'' during the [[pirate attack at Stivic]],<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> whereas ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of the trilogy, notes him as the [[third officer]] of the [[Starship|vessel]] during that incident, thus making the mention of the former rank erroneous.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref>
