'''Efavan Major Homicides and Secret Investigations Department''' was a fictitious [[law enforcement agency]] created by [[Jedi investigator]] [[Emerick Caphtor]] and<ref name="Trail of Shadows 2" /> [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Trail of Shadows 3">[[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 3|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 3]]</ref> [[private investigator]] [[Sian Holt]] to gain access to a crime scene.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 2" /> Around [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 2|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 2]] take place [[Standard week|weeks]] after the events of [[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 1|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 1]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[231 BBY]].</ref> Capthor and Holt [[Mission to Vorzyd V|traveled]] to<ref name="Trail of Shadows 2" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Region">[[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 2|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 2]] establishes that [[Vorzyd V]] is located in the [[Vorzyd sector]], which ''[[Nexus of Power]]'' places in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Solicit">{{SW|url=news/marvel-november-2021-star-wars-comics-exclusive|text=Luke Continues His Jedi Journey, and More in Marvel's November 2021 ''Star Wars'' Comics - Exclusive}}</ref> [[Vorzyd V]] to meet with an undercover [[Unidentified Galactic Republic agent|Republic agent]] within the [[city]] [[Efavan]]. When they arrived at their rendezvous, a [[Unidentified Cyclorrian|Cyclorrian]] law enforcement [[sergeant]] stopped them and informed the two it was a crime scene and asked what agency they were with.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 2" />

After Holt informed the sergeant they were in the Efavan department, the [[Cyclorrian]] stated they had not heard of it. In response, Caphtor said they would not have due to its name, meaning the agency was doing its job, and threatened to call the sergeant's [[Unidentified bureau chief|bureau chief]] if they did not give the two the incident's rundown. The sergeant complied with his request and they discovered the agent had been [[Death|killed]].<ref name="Trail of Shadows 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
