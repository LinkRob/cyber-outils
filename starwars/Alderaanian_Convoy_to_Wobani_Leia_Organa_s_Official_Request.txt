'''Alderaanian Convoy to Wobani&mdash;Leia Organa's Official Request''' was a file sent by [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[New Republic]]'s [[New Republic Senate|Senate]] to the [[Alderaanian]] [[refugee]]s who had been part of the [[Alderaan Flotilla]], the only people she knew would respond. The request was sent when it became apparent to Organa that emergency aid was needed on the [[planet]] [[Wobani]] while she watched a briefing on its status following its abandonment by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Rise" />

Within [[Standard day|days]], a [[convoy]] of [[Bulk freighter|heavy freighters]] carrying [[bacta]] and [[foodstuff]] was chartered and paid for by the Alderaanians, who also [[pilot]]ed the [[Starship|vessels]] under the leadership of [[Commander]] [[Rutowan Cannter]], although the supplies delivered were not enough for all of Wobani. The request file was stored in the "Relief Efforts" section of the [[New Republic Archives]] and later cited by [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] when<ref name="Rise" /> he<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> mentioned the convoy in his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Alderaanian Convoy to Wobani&mdash;Leia Organa's Official Request was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
