The '''Bestine Cantina''' was a [[cantina]] in the [[city]] [[Bestine (city)|Bestine]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].

==Description==
The cantina was located in and named after the [[city]] of [[Bestine (city)|Bestine]] on [[Tatooine]] which was located on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. It had two entrances to the main street as well as a back entrance with a lift that lowered to the [[spaceport]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==History==
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[contract broker]]s, [[EE'zok]] and [[Sona Plondu]] operated out of the cantina.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
During the same year, the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] visited the cantina while tracking down the missing [[Musician|drummer]] of the [[Skungy Gungus and the Refractors]], [[Remla Moonis]], for their manager [[Aldic Nelleer]]. When asked about Moonis the cantina's [[bartender]] told vess that Moonis had been there and had asked to perform at the cantina. However the bartender rejected his offer but allowed him to add his song "[[Growing Old in Wayfar]]" to the cantina's [[Jukebox]]. Using the song as a clue, Vess was able to track down Moonis to the town of [[Wayfar]]<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Bestine Cantina first appeared in canon in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends]] continuity the cantina was also called [[Cheapside Cantina]] and first appeared in the [[2003]] online video game ''[[Star Wars Galaxies]]'' developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment and published by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="Galaxies">{{GalaxiesNGE}}</ref>
