'''Donny Buc''' was a [[Spacer/Legends|spacer]] that ran a regular route between [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] and [[Sorrus]] over a decade before the [[Battle of Naboo/Legends|Battle of Naboo]]. [[Padawan/Legends|Padawans]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Siri Tachi/Legends|Siri Tachi]] arranged passage to Coruscant with him, after a failed attempt to capture [[Ona Nobis]].
