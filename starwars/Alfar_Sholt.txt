'''Alfar Sholt''' was [[human]] who was the owner of multiple [[casino]]s in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]]. Sholt often bought up the debts of others, subsequently forcing them to work in the casinos. While spectating a [[fathier]] race at the [[Galanx Casino]], Sholt was [[assassin]]ated by the [[sniper]] [[Just Lucky]], who had been sent to eliminate the target by the [[Sixth Kin]] criminal organization.<ref name="Aphra 8" />
