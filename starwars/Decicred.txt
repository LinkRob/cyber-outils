A '''decicred''', also called a '''dime''', was one tenth of a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]].<ref name="EpGuide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode006.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Downfall of a Droid}}</ref> When [[Anakin Skywalker]] was upset about seemingly losing his [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]], fellow [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] referenced decicreds by stating that [[R2-series astromech droid|R2 units]] were "a dime a dozen."<ref name="Downfall" />
