The '''''CCR''-class pinnace''' was a civilian [[starship]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the vessel was used as a [[scout ship]]. One particular vessel was used as a scout in the [[Western Reaches]]. [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]] discovered the vessel. Using clearance codes created by [[Jyn Erso]], the crew boarded the vessel and overpowered the Imperial passengers, stealing valuable data about what the surveyors had discovered.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''CCR''-class pinnace appears in [[Beth Revis]]'s [[2017]] young adult novel ''[[Rebel Rising]]''.
