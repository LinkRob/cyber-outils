'''Fox''', designated '''CC-1010''', was a [[clone trooper officer]] who served as the [[Clone Commander]] of the [[Coruscant Guard]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. While the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] deployed across [[the galaxy]] under the command of [[Jedi General]]s, [[Commander]] Fox and his [[Imperial shock trooper|clone shock troopers]] were stationed on [[Coruscant]], the [[capital]] [[planet]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Like all [[clone trooper]]s under his command, Fox was bound by duty and honor to serve and protect [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] as well as [[Senator|members]] of the [[Galactic Senate]]. Over the course of the war, Fox became one of the most highly decorated [[soldier]]s in the [[Republic Military]].

[[Cloning|Cloned]] from the [[DNA|genetic]] [[Clone template|template]] of the [[human]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], Fox trained on the [[extragalactic]] world of [[Kamino]] as part of an [[army]] of identical soldiers during the last [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Republic Era]]. Following the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], he was transferred to the Republic capital where he assumed command of the Coruscant Guard. As the war progressed, Fox's clone guards supplemented the [[Senate Guard]] as a [[Law enforcement agency|peace-keeping force]] on Coruscant. In addition, his [[troop]]s undertook off-world missions by [[Diplomatic service|providing security]] for [[Jedi]] [[ambassador]]s and senatorial dignitaries.

Fox retained command of the Coruscant Guard in the aftermath of [[Order 66]] and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], and continued his military service as an [[Imperial shock trooper]]. During the early [[Standard day|days]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] Palpatine's [[Imperial Era|reign]], Fox organized a security perimeter encircling the [[Operation: Knightfall|fallen]] [[Imperial Palace|Temple]] of the [[Jedi Order]] while the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] attempted to capture [[Jedi Master]] [[Jocasta Nu]]. Ultimately, Fox was [[Death penalty|executed]] by Vader after the shock troopers tried to [[Death|kill]] him, having mistaken the [[Sith]] for a Jedi.

==Biography==
===Clone Wars===
====Entering service====
The [[clone trooper]] designated CC-1010&mdash;who would, in [[time]], adopt the name of Fox&mdash;was a [[Gender|male]] [[Cloning|clone]]<ref name="Databank:Clone Cmdr.Fox" /> bred from the [[DNA|genetic material]]<ref name="Databank:Clone Troopers" /> of the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]]<ref name="CompEncyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Jango Fett]]. Created on<ref name="Databank:Clone Troopers" /> the [[extragalactic]]<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Kamino]] as part of a program to grow an [[army]] for the [[Galactic Republic]], Fox and his brethren were called to service by the Republic<ref name="Databank:Clone Troopers" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> approximately one [[Standard decade|decade]] after the initial creation of the [[Clone trooper|clone army]]. Thus, the Kamino-produced soldiers were used to form the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]<ref name="Episode II" /> as the backbone of the new [[Republic Military]]<ref name="Databank:Clone Troopers" /> at the [[First Battle of Geonosis|start]] of the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Episode II" />

[[File:Sideshow-FoxInAHallway.png|thumb|right|350px|Clone Commander Fox was assigned to the planet Coruscant, capital of the Republic.]]
While thousands of clone [[trooper]]s served on the front lines under the leadership of the [[Jedi Order]],<ref name="Databank:Clone Troopers" /> Fox was stationed on the Republic [[capital]]<ref name="Databank:Clone Cmdr.Fox" /> planet [[Coruscant]] in the [[Core Worlds]],<ref name="CompEncyclopedia" /> where he served as [[Clone Commander|commander]] of the [[Coruscant Guard]]. Under Fox's command, the Guard served as [[Law enforcement agency|peacekeepers]] for the Republic capital as an elite<ref name="Databank:Clone Cmdr.Fox" /> and famous institution.<ref name="CompEncyclopedia" /> On Coruscant, the guard was responsible for the safety of the [[Galactic Senate]], as well as [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]],<ref name="Databank:Clone Cmdr.Fox" /> in addition to carrying out more general security roles.<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref>

====Protecting Coruscant====
{{Quote|Should we arrest the Hutt, Senator?|Commander Fox speaks with Senator Amidala after apprehending Ziro|audio=ShouldWeArrestTheHutt-TCWf.flac|<ref name="TCW (f)">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>}}
[[File:CoruscantGuard-TCWfilm.png|thumb|left|300px|Commander Fox and the Coruscant Guard raided Ziro's palace to rescue Senator Padmé Amidala.]]
During the [[Battle of Teth|mission]] to rescue [[Rotta]], son of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] made [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] his [[prisoner]] in order to prevent her from exposing his part in Rotta's abduction. She was ultimately liberated by the efforts of [[Commander]] Fox and a contingent of [[Imperial shock trooper|clone shock troopers]] who [[Raid on Ziro's Palace|raided]] Ziro's [[Ziro's Palace|establishment]] in [[Imperial City|Galactic City]], having been informed of the Hutt's illegal actions by Amidala's [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]]. After eliminating the security [[droid]]s, Fox arrested Ziro who, confessed his betrayal to Jabba<ref name="TCW (f)" /> before being taken to [[prison]] at the [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center]].<ref name="Assassin">{{TCW|Assassin (episode)}}</ref><ref name="HC">{{TCW|Hostage Crisis}}</ref>

The capture of Ziro consequently led to a [[Senate hostage crisis|crisis]] on Coruscant when a group of senators were taken hostage by several [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], including the [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]]. In response, a [[squad]] of shock troopers secured the [[Emperor's office|Chancellor's office]] in the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate headquarters]] while Fox and his [[troop]]s surrounded the detention center to prevent Ziro's escape. In spite of his efforts, Fox was forced to allow the [[bounty]] hunters to leave with Ziro in order to save Bane's hostages.<ref name="HC" />

[[File:FoxFooled-HOBS.png|thumb|right|250px|Fox was fooled by a group of Separatist droids that he referred to as "stupid droids."]]
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Heroes Date">{{TCWcanondates|310}}</ref> a group of modified [[Infiltrator demolition droid|demolition droids]] were sent to disrupt the [[Confederate–Republic peace initiative]] on Coruscant. At the [[time]] Fox was tasked with protecting the [[central power distribution grid]] on [[Level 5000]]. The droids deceived him with a security clearance code, which Fox deemed authentic. As a result, the droids completed their mission by destroying power grid, causing a [[Ecumenopolis|city-wide]] blackout on Coruscant. Infuriated by the Confederacy's [[terrorist]] [[Bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid|attack]] on the [[The galaxy|galactic]] capital, the Senate rejected the peace initiative.<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" />

====Hunting fugitives====
{{Quote|Can't say I blame you, Commander Tano, but all the same, you're under arrest.''"<br />"''I&ndash;n&ndash;no. I did not do this.|Commander Fox and Ahsoka Tano, after Letta Turmond was killed while Tano was in her cell|<ref name="TJWKTM" />}}
[[File:RexFoxArgue-TJWKTM.png|thumb|left|250px|Fox and Captain Rex disagreed about Ahsoka Tano as the rogue Padawan fled from the military.]]
Following the [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|terrorist attack]] on the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] of Coruscant in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Letta Turmond]] was imprisoned in the [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|Republic Center for Military Operations]] due to her participation in the attack. [[Ahsoka Tano]], the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] who arrested the terrorist, visited the facility at Turmond's request, but was required to surrender her [[lightsaber]]s to Commander Fox as a security precaution. Although the clone initially praised Tano for bringing Turmond to justice, he was compelled to arrest her after Turmond [[Force choke|was strangled to death by way]] of [[the Force]]. Unbeknownst to Fox,<ref name="TJWKTM" /> Turmond was murdered by the [[Dark side of the Force|fallen]] Jedi [[Barriss Offee]], whose distorted views led her to commit acts of betrayal and terrorism.<ref name="Databank:Barriss">{{Databank|barriss-offee|Barriss Offee}}</ref> Offee further tainted her incarcerated comrade's reputation by killing three clone officers with her lightsaber, leading Fox to believe that Tano was the culprit. In response to Tano's [[Escape from the Republic military base|escape attempt]], Fox deployed the Coruscant Guard alongside [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex|CT-7567]] "Rex" as they pursued the Jedi escapee.<ref name="TJWKTM" />

In the aftermath of the [[Capture of Ahsoka Tano|incident]] with Tano,<ref name="TCJ">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref> whose innocence was proven after Skywalker disrupted her [[Trial of Ahsoka Tano|trial]] to reveal Offee as the real [[Treason|traitor]],<ref name="TWJ">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref> Fox was ordered to hunt another fugitive&mdash;the [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]] of the [[501st Legion]]. Fives fled from the [[Grand Imperial Medical Facility|Grand Republic Medical Facility]] where he had attempted to assassinate Chancellor Palpatine after learning of his involvement in a conspiracy to destroy the Jedi Order. Tracking the renegade clone to [[Level 1325]], Fox and a contingent of clone shock troopers confronted Fives, demanding that he stand down. In his panic and desperation, the ARC trooper raised a [[blaster pistol]] at the approaching guards, but Fox opened fire, killing Fives with a shot through his chest.<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref>

====Order 66====
{{Quote|I was here on Coruscant. Did my part&mdash;all the clones did. Shut those Jedi agitators down cold.|Commander Fox, recalling his involvement in Order 66|<ref name="Dying Light III">[[Darth Vader (2017) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 9]]</ref>}}
During the initial stage of the [[Great Jedi Purge]], Fox and his fellow clones participated in the implementation of [[Order 66]] on Coruscant.<ref name="Dying Light III" /> The Jedi Order had been accused of treason by Chancellor Palpatine; as such, they were marked for death by the clone troopers who once served them.<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> Ultimately, Fox and his troops successfully carried out their orders to purge the capital of its Jedi inhabitants,<ref name="Dying Light III" /> with a number of Coruscant Guard shock troopers engaging [[Jedi Master]] [[Kelleran Beq]] and [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]] on a landing platform.<ref name="Chapter 20">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 20: The Foundling}}</ref>

===Imperial service and death===
{{Quote|We've got two full companies here, plus a big contingent of Coruscant Security Force. The whole place is surrounded. No one's getting past us.''"<br />"''See that they do not.|Commander Fox and Darth Vader|<ref name="Dying Light III" />}}
[[File:ImperialCommanderFox-2017DarthVader9.png|thumb|right|250px|Fox had the Jedi Temple surrounded by the Coruscant Guard, in accordance with Darth Vader's instructions.]]
The Coruscant Guard remained under Commander Fox's leadership after Palpatine [[Proclamation of the New Order|declared]] himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]], reorganizing the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]]. In the early days of the [[New Order]], Fox and his troops encountered Jedi Master [[Jocasta Nu]], [[Chief Librarian]] of the Jedi [[Jedi temple|Temple]], who used the Force to [[Mind trick|influence]] the clones in order to pass them safely.<ref name="Dying Light I" /> After Nu [[Hunt for Jocasta Nu|infiltrated]] the Temple, Fox surrounded the Jedi Order's former home with two full [[Company|companies]] of Imperial clone shock troopers with the [[Coruscant Security Force]] providing auxiliary support. He assured the Emperor's [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Vader, that the perimeter around the Temple was secure.<ref name="Dying Light III" />

Due to his lightsaber, Fox's soldiers mistook the [[Sith]] for a Jedi and opened fire on him during Nu's attempted escape. Fox immediately ordered them to cease their attack when he realized who they were shooting. Although he tried to explain his actions, Fox was [[Death penalty|summarily executed]] for failing to provide the clones with Vader's description. The [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] used [[telekinesis]] to break the clone's neck, ending the life of Commander Fox.<ref name="Dying Light IV">[[Darth Vader (2017) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 10]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|A lot of innocent people died in that blast. Good job capturing her.|CC-1010, expressing his satisfaction with Ahsoka Tano's arrest of Letta Turmond|<ref name="TJWKTM" />}}
[[File:AhsokaUnderArrest-TJWKTM.png|thumb|left|250px|Bound by his sense of duty to the Republic, Fox arrested Ahsoka Tano as a suspect in the murder of Letta Turmond.]]
Commander Fox was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] clone who stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s,<ref name="Databank:Clone Cmdr.Fox" /> a physical trait he inherited from his [[Clone template|template]] Jango Fett.<ref name="Databank:Clone Troopers" /> He was also a fearless [[soldier]] who led the elite Coruscant Guard shock troopers into combat, and was always the first to charge into battle. As with the rest of the Guard, Fox possessed a strong sense of duty and honor which compelled him to loyally serve and protect the Republic's highest governing body&mdash;the Galactic Senate and the Supreme Chancellor. His service was exemplary, leading him to become one of the most highly decorated soldiers in the Grand Army of the Republic.<ref name="Databank:Clone Cmdr.Fox" />

Like the rest of his clone brethren, Fox was fiercely loyal to the Galactic Republic, and had little patience for criminals such as the terrorist [[Letta Turmond]]. When Turmond died in her cell, Fox stated that he did not personally blame Ahsoka Tano, but nonetheless arrested her as a suspect for the murder of Turmond. Later, he unhesitatingly ordered his guards to kill Tano, whom he believed responsible for the deaths of several clones during her escape, but grudgingly complied with Anakin Skywalker's orders to capture the Padawan alive.<ref name="TJWKTM" /> When Fox and his troops confronted Fives, he demanded that the ARC trooper surrender to them. Ultimately, Fox opened fire on Fives after the clone raised a blaster at him.<ref name="Orders" />

Fox was loyal to the Republic's successor, the Galactic Empire, and continued to lead the Coruscant Guard in the name of the self-styled Emperor Palpatine. Like the other clones, he was complicit in the execution of Order 66, which he saw as his duty to carry out. Believing the Jedi Order responsible for instigating a rebellion on the capital, Fox viewed the Jedi as "agitators" and enemies of the state.<ref name="Dying Light III" /> Though he intended no harm to Darth Vader, he failed to warn his guards against mistaking the Sith [[Lord]] for a Jedi. This oversight on Fox's part ultimately cost the commander his life.<ref name="Dying Light IV" />

==Skills and abilities==
As the commanding officer of the Coruscant Guard, Fox was skilled in riot control and as a leader. He also was trained in combat abilities, military tactics and reconnaissance.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" />

==Equipment==
[[File:Clone-commander-fox_detail.png|thumb|right|180px|Fox wore a customized version of the Phase II clone trooper armor.]]
As a clone soldier who served in the early period of the Clone Wars, Commander Fox wore the standard [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] albeit with the [[Color|crimson]] markings and original pattern design of the Coruscant Guard. In addition to his [[armor]], Fox wore a [[kama]] and a customized helmet similar to the one worn by [[Cody]] of the [[212th Attack Battalion]].<ref name="TCW (f)" />

With the introduction of [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], Fox switched to the upgraded version alongside his unit. His new armor was colored mainly in red which in fact is the inverted color scheme of the standard kit of the clone shock troopers.<ref name="TJWKTM" /> His weapons included the standard [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]<ref name="TCW (f)" /> and a pair of [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|DC-17 hand blasters]].<ref name="TJWKTM" />

Fox retained his distinctive armor as the Republic transitioned into the Galactic Empire, though noticeably removed the insignia of the Coruscant Guard from his shoulder pauldrons.<ref name="Darth Vader (2017) 9">[[Darth Vader (2017) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 9]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
CC-1010 first appeared in the [[2008]] [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' animated film]], voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="TCW (f)" /> Concept art of Fox in his Phase I armor was done by [[Tim Brock]].<ref name="Tumblr">{{Tumblr|subdomain=starwars|name=''Star Wars''|url=post/110650161158/artist-of-the-week-tim-brock|text=Artist of the Week - Tim Brock}}</ref>

In the [[2021]] [[Canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars Battles]]'', the lower half of his chest armor and his kneepads are erroneously depicted as white, despite both canonically being red.<ref name="LEGOSWBattles">''[[LEGO Star Wars Battles]]''</ref> The [[2023]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set [[w:c:brickipedia:75354 Coruscant Guard Gunship|75354 Coruscant Guard Gunship]] utilizes this same erroneous design. The front box art of the Coruscant Guard Gunship set features Fox helping to pilot the vehicle over Coruscant's Federal District, whereas the back depicts Fox on the ground with another Coruscant Guard trooper outside the vehicle after it has landed.<ref name="LEGOGunship">{{LEGOCite|num=75354|name=Coruscant Guard Gunship}}</ref>
