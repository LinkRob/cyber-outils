The '''Bosphian sestertius''' was a type of [[Color|brown]] [[metal]]lic [[coin]] used during the [[High Republic Era]] as [[currency]] in [[Point Ueda]], a [[city]] where multiple currencies were accepted on the [[planet]] [[Ueda]] in the informal [[unincorporated Bosphian provinces]] [[sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' places [[Ueda]], and therefore part of the [[unincorporated Bosphian provinces]] where Ueda is located, in the area of [[Realspace|space]] ''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]'' identifies as the [[New Territories]].</ref> The coin was disc-shaped with patterning including an outer circle along the rim and an inner circle with a dot at the center and lines extending out radially from its sides. Bosphian sestertii partially shared their name with<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> the [[planet]] [[Bosph]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

The Bosphian sestertius was created for<ref name="SDCC">{{SW|url=news/sdcc-2024-lucasfilm-costume-display|text=SDCC 2024: The Stranger from ''The Acolyte'' Arrives at the Lucasfilm Pavilion}}</ref> the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', although the prop is not visible in the show,<ref name="Acolyte">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''</ref> and was shown among other props from the series at [[Wikipedia:San Diego Comic-Con|San Diego Comic-Con]] in [[2024]].<ref name="SDCC" /> The prop was then first identified in ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', a [[2025]] reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> Hidalgo later shared an image of various ''[[Star Wars]]'' currency props with the Bosphian sestertius and another coin beside a label reading "High Republic Credit."<ref name="BlueSky">{{Bluesky|infinata.bsky.social|post/3lwd5sa6qdc2i|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2025-08-14|quote="Look, I ain't in this for your revolution, and I'm not in it for you, princess. I expect to be well paid. I'm in it for the money."|archiveurl=https://archive.is/K5rEF|archivefile=File:PabloHidalgo-Currencies-Bluesky.png}}</ref> In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Sestertius|sestertii]] were a type of [[Wikipedia:Ancient Rome|ancient Roman]] [[Wikipedia:Coin|coin]].<ref name="Britannica">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/topic/sesterce|text=Sesterce|author=The Editors of Encyclopædia Britannica|date=1998-07-20|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20250324110153}}</ref>
