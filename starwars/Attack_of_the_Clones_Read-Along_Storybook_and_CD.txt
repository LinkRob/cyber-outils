'''''Attack of the Clones'' Read-Along Storybook and CD''' is a young readers' story from [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]], released on [[February 7]], [[2017]]. It presents an illustrated version of the story of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] with an accompanying CD with narration of the story and character voices.

==Publisher's summary==
''Relive all of the excitement of ''Star Wars'': Episode II'' Attack of the Clones'' in this illustrated storybook-and-CD set.''<ref name="Disney" />
