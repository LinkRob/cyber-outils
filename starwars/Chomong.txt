'''Chomong''' were a [[Ocean|sea]] creature native to [[Velusia]], and were referred to by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] as diaphanous blobs of glowing flesh. Their fossilized [[Death|corpses]] made up an aggregate of [[crystal]]line matter off the coast of [[Kolo-ha]], an island on Velusia. Chomongs were eaten raw by the native [[Velusian]]s.<ref name="ALD" />
