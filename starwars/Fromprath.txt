The '''Fromprath''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]], [[snake]]like, six-[[Limb|armed]] [[species]] that lived in [[the galaxy]]. [[Standard century|Hundreds]] of [[Standard year|years]]<ref name="Last Shot" /> before [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashback events of ''[[Last Shot]]'' that feature [[Han Solo]] to around [[3 BBY]].</ref> the Fromprath lived on an [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Dathomir|planet]]<ref name="CompEncyc">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> but were forced to live in [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] away from the [[planet]] after being forced out<ref name="Last Shot" /> by the [[Nightsisters]], who renamed the world Dathomir.<ref name="CompEncyc">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

During their rule over Dathomir,<ref name="CompEncyc" /> the Fromprath had exploited the world's natural resources. The Nightsisters formed a symbiotic alliance with [[rancor]]s in order to drive out the Fromprath<ref name="TDS">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side]]''</ref> and claim the world for themselves,<ref name="CompEncyc" /> much to the sorrow of the Fromprath. [[Frapsen]] was a [[Gender|male]] Fromprath [[musician]] who played at the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of [[Maz Kanata]]<ref name="Last Shot" /> around 3 BBY.<ref name="Date" />

{{Species-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side]]'' suggested that the Fromprath were colonizers of Dathomir.<ref name="TDS" /> However, the later reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'', building off the lore established about the Nightsisters introduced in ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'', instead established that the Fromprath had been the inhabitants of Dathomir before the Nightsisters had arrived.<ref name="CompEncyc" />
