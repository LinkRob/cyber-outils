A '''battle''' took place on the [[planet]] [[Celes IV]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. After the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] established a [[Separatist fortress|fortress]] that shielded enough to prevent an orbital assault, a group of [[Galactic Republic]] forces attempted to attack the fortress but ended up pinned down. Led by a [[Unidentified Jedi (Altadan Igar)|Jedi]], the force, which included [[Altadan Igar]], were eventually led to a bloody victory.<ref name="FACPOV" />
