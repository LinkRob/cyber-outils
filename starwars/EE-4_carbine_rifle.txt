The '''EE-4 carbine rifle''', also known as the '''EE-4 blaster rifle''', was a powerful medium-ranged [[blaster carbine]] model that was manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Successor to the [[EE-3 carbine rifle]], the EE-4's shorter and stubbier [[barrel]] allowed the [[blaster rifle]] to fire more effectively at close range with spread shots but at the cost of a reduced accuracy at range compared to its predecessor.

The two-[[Limb|handed]] EE-4 carbine [[rifle]] was favored by [[Sana Starros]], a [[bounty hunter]] and [[smuggler]] who later joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Starros wielded the [[blaster]] on multiple occasions in [[0 ABY]], including [[Skirmish on Hradreek|selling]] the [[Darth Atrius' lightsabers|lightsabers]] of [[Darth Atrius]] on [[Hradreek]], [[Escape from the Monsua Nebula|confronting]] fellow smuggler [[Han Solo]] in the [[Monsua Nebula]], and [[Rescue of Luke Skywalker|rescuing]] the [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]] on [[Nar Shaddaa]].

==Description==
{{Listen3|EE-4.flac|EE-4 carbine rifle firing}}
[[File:EE-3.png|thumb|left|255px|The EE-3 carbine rifle (pictured) was the EE-4 carbine rifle's predecessor.]]
Manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]], the EE-4 carbine rifle,<ref name="SG" /> or the EE-4 blaster rifle,<ref name="BF" /> was a powerful type of [[blaster carbine]] that was capable of discharging [[Color|red]]-colored bolts with a medium range.<ref name="BF 2" /> Distinguished for its two-round spread burst fire, the [[blaster]] was the successor to the [[EE-3 carbine rifle]]. While the EE-3 focused on precision and range, the EE-4 was built to be more effective at close range and had a superior rate of fire with its shorter and stubbier [[barrel]],<ref name="BF" /> which was designed as a hybrid between a [[Blaster rifle|rifle]] and a [[Blaster pistol|pistol]].<ref name="BF Bespin">{{EA|subdomain=starwars|url=starwars/battlefront/news/bespin-new-weapons-and-star-cards|text=Bespin &ndash; New Weapons & Star Cards|date=2016-06-20|nolive=1}}</ref> On the other hand, these alterations caused a reduction in the [[weapon]]'s accuracy at range and cooling power in comparison to its predecessor.<ref name="BF" />

The black-colored EE-4 consisted of a short barrel that contained cooling vents, along with two vertical grips attached beneath it, which allowed the wielder to hold the [[rifle]] with two [[Limb|hands]]. One of the grips was located in the middle of the weapon, with the other being at the backside, as the blaster lacked a long [[stock]], unlike its predecessor. In addition to the rifle's main [[scope]], which was visually identical to the EE-3's [[electronic scope]], the EE-4 was equipped with four smaller scopes, two positioned on either side of the blaster's frame. The rifle also contained a guard that surrounded the trigger.<ref name="BF" />

==History==
===Masse Goskey's weapons business===
{{Quote|I've got to move this merch because everything we picked up on Nar Shaddaa is illegal in sixty-six sectors. That means we've gotta sell this stuff to the syndicates: Crymorah, Black Sun, you know.|Masse Goskey|<ref name="SG" />}}
[[File:SanaStarrosEE4-2015StarWars9.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The EE-4 carbine rifle, weapon of choice for Sana Starros]]
Sometime between [[19 BBY]] and [[10 BBY]],<ref name="SG note">According to ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', [[Masse Goskey]] and [[Ephant Mon]]'s [[weapon]]s purchase took place some [[time]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Mon also mentioned Goskey's [[death]] in the [[in-universe]] ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', which later came into possession of [[Dryden Vos]], meaning Goskey died before Vos did. The weapons purchase must have therefore occurred between the Empire's formation and [[Showdown on Savareen|Vos's death]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]] and [[10 BBY]], respectively.</ref> [[Masse Goskey]], the proprietor of [[Masse Goskey's Arms Emporium and Explosives Mart]]<ref name="SG" /> in [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the planet [[Tatooine]],<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> and his associate [[Ephant Mon]] bought some weaponry on the [[moon]] [[Nar Shaddaa]], including an EE-4 carbine rifle that came at a price of 850 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. A complete inventory of the merchandise was recorded in a [[book]] that would eventually be known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''. According to Goskey's note in the book, all the weapons they picked up on the moon were illegal in sixty-six [[sector]]s, compelling them to [[Arms dealer|sell]] the arms to crime syndicates such as the [[Crymorah Syndicate]] and the [[Black Sun]].<ref name="SG" />

===Skirmish on Hradreek===
{{Quote|Come back here!|Sana Starros, while firing upon R2-D2|<ref name="SWA 4" />}}
The EE-4 carbine rifle was used by [[Sana Starros]], a [[human]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="SW 12" /> [[bounty hunter]] and [[smuggler]] active during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="SW 34">[[Star Wars (2015) 34|''Star Wars'' (2015) 34]]</ref> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[0 ABY]],<ref name="SWT">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Starros carried her EE-4 in a [[holster]] when she [[Skirmish on Hradreek|traveled]] to [[Hradreek]] to sell a pair of [[Darth Atrius' lightsabers|ancient lightsabers]] that were once wielded by the [[Sith]] [[Darth Atrius]]. Although she had promised them both to the Empire and an [[Ungrila]] [[Unidentified crime boss (Hradreek)|crime boss]] at the same [[time]], Starros only delivered one of the [[Sith lightsaber|lightsabers]] to each of her buyers.<ref name="SWA 4" />

[[File:StarrosFiresAtR2D2-2015StarWarsAnnual4.jpg|thumb|left|320px|Sana Starros fires upon R2-D2 with her EE-4.]]
When the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] realized the smuggler's deception, he ordered [[stormtrooper]]s to locate her and the other [[lightsaber]]. Soon after, Vader himself found Starros, who fired on the [[Sith Lord]] with her EE-4 carbine rifle. However, Vader [[Force deflection|deflected]] the blaster bolts with his hand by using [[the Force]]. Starros eventually escaped the Sith by activating a [[bomb]] hidden next to him.<ref name="SWA 4" />

Due to the same explosion, the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan|trainee]] [[Luke Skywalker]], who was also coincidentally on Hradreek to retrieve [[Currency|funds]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], dropped his [[Credit chip|cred stick]] and was separated from his [[R2-series astromech droid|astromech droid]], [[R2-D2]]. Shortly after, Starros came across the credit chip lying on the ground and picked it up. The [[Astromech droid|droid]] thereupon attacked Starros, caused her to fall to the ground, and took the credits back. She then fired upon R2-D2 with the EE-4 in anger, but the astromech escaped.<ref name="SWA 4" />

===Hunting Han Solo===
{{Quote|&hellip;why is your ''wife'' pointing a ''gun'' at me?''"<br />"She's not my wife!''<nowiki />|Leia Organa and Han Solo, on Sana Starros|<ref name="SW 8" />}}
[[File:StarrosSoloAndOrgana-2015StarWars8.jpg|thumb|right|320px|Starros wielded her EE-4 when she confronted Han Solo and Leia Organa in the Monsua Nebula.]]
Later in 0 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="SWT" /> Starros began searching for [[Han Solo]], a fellow smuggler who had taken her cut after the two pulled off a [[Thief|robbery]] disguised as their "[[Marriage|wedding]]." Starros's quest came to an end on an [[Unidentified planet|unnamed planet]] near the [[Monsua Nebula]], where Solo was on a mission for the Rebellion along with the [[Alderaanian]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. By using her EE-4, Starros initially drew a bead on both rebels, alerted Imperials to claim the [[bounty]] issued for Organa, and forced Solo to come with her.<ref name="SW 8" />

However, she had a change of heart upon learning that Solo had become a rebel and that the Empire would arrest him as well.<ref name="SW 9">[[Star Wars (2015) 9|''Star Wars'' (2015) 9]]</ref> With Starros's [[starship]], the ''[[Volt Cobra]]'', the trio [[Escape from the Monsua Nebula|escaped]] the [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]].<ref name="SW 10">[[Star Wars (2015) 10|''Star Wars'' (2015) 10]]</ref>

===Rescue of Luke Skywalker===
{{Quote|Shoot it!''"<br />"''Blasters don't work&hellip;|Leia Organa and Sana Starros, on Kongo the Disemboweler|<ref name="SW 12" />}}
[[File:RebelsRescuingLuke-2015StarWars12.jpg|thumb|left|230px|Starros's EE-4 was disabled by Grakkus the Hutt's electromagnetic pulse.]]
After losing the Imperials, Organa made a deal with Starros to transport their group to Nar Shaddaa to [[Rescue of Luke Skywalker|rescue Luke Skywalker]], who had been abducted by the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]].<ref name="SW 10" /> By the time the trio reached [[Palace of Grakkus the Hutt|Grakkus's palace]], Skywalker was forced to fight an [[Grakkus Arena|arena]] match against<ref name="SW 12" /> a [[roggwart]] [[Species|creature]] known as [[Kongo]] the Disemboweler.<ref name="SWT" />

Confronted by the rescue party, Grakkus activated a local [[electromagnetic pulse]] that disabled Kongo's [[shock collar]] as well as all nearby blasters, including Starros's EE-4 carbine rifle, rendering her unable to fire upon the ravenous beast when it charged the rebels. Nonetheless, the rebels successfully rescued Skywalker,<ref name="SW 12" /> and Starros later allied herself with the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="SW 16">[[Star Wars (2015) 16|''Star Wars'' (2015) 16]]</ref>

Starros kept her EE-4 by her side when she traveled to the planet [[Corellia]] during the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]]'s search for an ancient device known as the [[Path engine]]<ref name="Aphra 6" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="SWT" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The EE-4 is the continuation of an idea we had for the Outer Rim expansion, though that we couldn't quite fully realize execute with the blasters at that time. We were quite excited by the idea of a blaster that fires multiple bolts in a spread shot. First seen wielded by Sana Starros in Marvel's comic, ''Star Wars''™ : &ndash; Showdown on the Smuggler's Moon, its stubby design makes it an interesting hybrid between a pistol and a rifle. The weapon fires a two blast burst, and each blast fires 3 bolts, meaning a single pull of the trigger will discharge 6 bolts at a rapid rate. So while it may not be as accurate as the EE-3, it makes up for it with a higher rate of fire and spread to damage multiple enemies.|Official [[Electronic Arts]] website|<ref name="BF Bespin" />}}
[[File:EE-4_statistics.png|thumb|right|265px|The EE-4's weapon statistics in ''Star Wars Battlefront'']]
The EE-4 carbine rifle first appeared, albeit unnamed, in the [[2015]] [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Star Wars (2015) 6|''Star Wars'' (2015) 6]], written by [[Jason Aaron]] with art by [[John Cassaday]].<ref name="SW 6" /> It was initially identified as the EE-4 blaster rifle in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'', as part of the [[Bespin]] expansion pack released in [[2016]],<ref name="BF Bespin" /> while the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]], later introduced the name EE-4 carbine rifle.<ref name="SG" /> In ''Star Wars Battlefront'', the rifle is unlockable via an [[W:c:battlefront:Hutt Contract|in-game challenge]] called "Sana's Legacy,"<ref name="BF" /> referring to Sana Starros, who wields the weapon in Marvel's [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']] comic-book series.<ref name="BF Bespin" />

The EE-4 also appeared in the game's [[2017]] sequel, ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'', as part of the "infiltration" ability for the [[W:c:battlefront:Specialist (class)|specialist trooper class]]&mdash;such as [[OOM-series security droid|security battle droids]], [[clone sharpshooter]]s, Imperial [[scout trooper]]s, [[Rebel Marksman|rebel sharpshooters]], and [[First Order stormtrooper sniper|First Order specialists]]&mdash;rather than a full-fledged blaster option. In both games, the rifle can be used by [[trooper]]s from all factions, including the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]],<ref name="BF 2" /> the Galactic Empire, the Rebel Alliance, the [[New Republic]],<ref name="BF" /> the [[First Order]], and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="BF 2" />
