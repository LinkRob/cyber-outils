'''3-76''' was a [[droid]] owned by [[Elad Zhalto]] in the [[Standard year|year]] [[3 ABY]]. One day Elad Zhalto hired [[Isabalia]] to convince [[Na'Tala]] to return to his employ, in part by saying he would send his 3-76 if she did not agree; Isabalia knew sending 3-76 meant Na'Tala would be killed. She accepted the job and collected Na'Tala.<ref name="Beyond the Clouds">{{StoryCite|story=Beyond the Clouds|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>
