'''Boss''' was the title given to the leader of the [[Gungan]] community. [[Rugor Nass]] was Boss during the [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="Boss Nass">{{Databank|boss-nass|Boss Nass}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], he was succeeded by [[Lyonie]].<ref name="Boss Lyonie" /> The Boss had the power to send the [[Gungan Grand Army]] to war or to banish trouble-making citizens.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

The title existed outside of Gungan society as well, often denoting a leader, particularly in some business-related or criminal capacity. During the waning [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], [[Xev Xrexus]] was known as the '''Boss-Mistress''' of the [[Xrexus Cartel]].<ref name="Darth Maul I" />
