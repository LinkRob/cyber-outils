'''Audj Seedol''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Mikkian]] from the [[planet]] [[Lenahra]]. The sister of [[Casul Seedol]] and daughter of [[Ampher]] and [[Chemi]], Seedol became the leader of her small community after the [[death]]s of all of the adults who remained on Lenahra after abusing [[the Power]] stolen from the planet. In young years she entered a romantic relationship with fellow [[Lenahran]] [[Zae-Brii]].<ref name="Padawan" />

In [[41 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] [[Mission to Lenahra|arrived]] on Lenahra in search for information about the [[High Republic Era]] [[Wayseeker]] [[Orla Jareni]] who had seemingly visited the planet [[Standard century|centuries]] earlier. Audj first distrusted the new arrival and stole his [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's first lightsaber|lightsaber]], but after seeing that he wasn't a threat, she accepted him into their [[Lenahran's home base ship|home]]. Although Audj was sympathetic towards Kenobi, she didn't like his questions about them and their ways and, after Kenobi saw them hurting the planet by stealing it's [[The Force|Force]]-like Power and tried to convince them to leave it in peace, she became antagonistic towards the Padawan. Audj eventually had a change of heart after she saw Kenobi being able to [[Meditation|meditate]] in harmony with the planet's seemingly hostile [[plant]]s and [[Species|animal]]s and instead had the group return their stolen orbs of the power and assisted Kenobi in stopping her newly returned uncle [[Loegrib]] in stealing all of Lenahra's remaining Power.<ref name="Padawan" />

During their fight with [[Loegrib's mining-turned-enforcer droid|his droids]] at the [[great gash]], Loegrib managed to descend into the [[power cave]] and to steal and consume a large amount of the Power. In order for Kebobi, Casul and her to stop him, Audj once again had to steal a orb of the Power. Although Kenobi eventually managed to kill Loegrib, the thefts of the power had enraged Lenahra to much as that anyone could hope to still be able to live in peace with it. As such Audj and her family evacuated with the help of Loegrib's [[Loegrib's crew|former crew]], with the intention to find a place with [[water]] where her [[Nautolan]] family members [[Shush]], [[Trill (Nautolan)|Trill]] and [[Whistle (Nautolan)|Whistle]] could live in comfort and the hope of possibly locating the remaining survivors of the Lenahrans who had fled the planet [[Standard decade|decades]] prior.<ref name="Padawan" />
