'''Basalt''' was a rocky substance found on multiple [[planet]]s, including [[Iego]]<ref name="Cliffhold" /> and [[Pamarthe]]. Many of Pamarthe's islands possessed basalt cliffs, with some featuring [[spaceport]]s carved into them.<ref name="Bloodline" /> Iego's surface was covered with basaltic spires, and many of those spires had [[City|cities]], such as [[Cliffhold]], built within them.<ref name="Cliffhold" /> One type of basalt, known as [[flood basalt]], could be found on the [[Volcano|volcanic]] world of [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Dark Lord 21" /> A [[Basalt Forest|forest]] of basalt pillars was present on the planet [[Koboh]], drawing the fascination of [[High Republic Era]] [[scientist]]s.<ref name="Survivor" />
