A '''battle''' occurred in the [[Standard year|years]] leading up to the [[Battle of Yavin]] between the [[Sammun insurgents]] and the [[96th Task Force]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Commander]] [[Karyn Faro]], acting under [[Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] direction, pacified the insurgents. Her forces destroyed two enemy ships and captured four vessels and a considerable range of small arms ordnance.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Prelude==
During the [[Imperial Era]], the insurgent leader [[Nevil Cygni|Nightswan]] led an [[Nightswan's campaign|insurgency]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Towards the end of his campaign, Nightswan shifted [[Batonn sector insurgency|his operations]] to the [[Batonn sector]] with insurgent groups operating on the planets [[Batonn]], [[Sammun]], and [[Denash]]. The planet Sammun was governed by [[Governor]] [[Estorn]], who answered to the Empire. Following the [[Battle of Scrim Island|seizure]] of [[Scrim Island]] by the [[Batonn insurgents]], [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Jok Donassius]] decided to take action against the sector insurgent groups including the [[Sammun insurgents]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

==The battle==
After [[Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] insisted on studying the situation on Scrim Island, Admiral [[Durril]] was instead assigned to reclaim Scrim Island. Thrawn's [[96th Task Force]] was instead assigned to pacify the Sammun insurgents. Deciding to investigate the Batonn insurgents, Admiral Thrawn and [[Commander]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] assigned [[Commander]] [[Karyn Faro]] to deal with the insurgents on Sammun.<ref name="Thrawn" />

Having studied Sammuni art, Thrawn instructed Commander Faro to enter the Sammun's system at a distance and disperse the rest of the task force. She was to bring the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]'' close to Sammun and demand the surrender of the insurgents. Imperial intelligence indicated that the insurgents were sheltering inside bunkers and were protected by [[deflector shield]]s. However, these defenses could not withstand Star Destroyer [[turbolaser]]s. In short, Thrawn's plan was to threaten at attack with the goal of driving them out of their positions.<ref name="Thrawn" />

Implementing Thrawn's plan, Commander Faro bombarded the Sammun insurgents' stronghold, forcing them to flee in [[starship]]s. This plan worked and the Ninety-Sixth Task Force succeeded in destroying two enemy ships and capturing four others. In addition, the Empire captured a considerable array of small ordnance.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Aftermath==
Following the pacification of the Sammun insurgents, Admiral Thrawn briefed Fleet Admiral Donassius and the other Imperial commanders about the successful operation. While Admiral [[Kinshara]] had [[Battle of Denash|success]] against the [[Denash insurgents]], Admiral Durril's [[One Oh Third Task Force]] was repelled by the Batonn insurgents at Scrim Island with serious damage to the starships. As a result, Thrawn was assigned with ending the siege of Scrim Island. This enabled him to turn his attention to Nightswan and the remaining Batonn insurgents.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Sammun was first mentioned in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[2017]] novel [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']], a rebooted [[Canon]]ical origins story of the titular character.
