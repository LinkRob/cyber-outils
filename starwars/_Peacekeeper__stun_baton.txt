The '''"Peacekeeper" stun baton''', also known simply as the '''"Peacekeeper" baton''', was a model of [[stun baton]] manufactured by [[Czerka Arms]] and sold for 500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. It was a heavy, lengthy [[baton]] made out of [[metal]] and had powerful [[stun]] capabilities that could still be felt after getting hit by it. The [[weapon]]'s shock capabilities led to multiple conscientious groups blacklisting it, as well as discontinuing its use. Some of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[governor]]s mass ordered the weapon during the [[Galactic Civil War]] to keep their populations in check. The weapon was also popular among [[bounty hunter]]s, who used the baton to circumvent laws and their client's request for non-lethal weaponry.<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />
