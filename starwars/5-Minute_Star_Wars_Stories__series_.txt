'''''5-Minute Star Wars Stories''''' is a series of children's storybooks published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]. Each title contains illustrated short stories adapting events from films in the [[Star Wars saga|''Star Wars'' saga]].<ref name="5-Minute 2015" /> The series is an offshoot of a larger umbrella series titled ''5-Minute Stories'' from [[Wikipedia:Disney Publishing Worldwide|Disney Publishing Worldwide]] adapting various intellectual properties owned by [[the Walt Disney Company]].<ref name="Disney">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=series/5-minute-stories|text=5-Minute Stories Books}}</ref>

The [[5-Minute Star Wars Stories|first book]] in the series was published in [[2015]],<ref name="5-Minute 2015" /> with three subsequent titles being released in the years since.<ref name="Hero Stories">''[[5-Minute Star Wars Hero Stories]]''</ref><ref name="Villain Stories">''[[5-Minute Star Wars Villain Stories]]''</ref><ref name="5-Minute Strikes" /> A fourth title, a [[5-Minute Star Wars Stories (second edition)|refresh]] of the original book in the series, was scheduled for release in [[2020]] but never saw publication in English.<ref name="Edelweiss">{{LegacyEdelweiss|url=#sku=1368051871|text=5-Minute Star Wars Stories (Refresh)}}</ref>
