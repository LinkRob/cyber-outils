A '''data solid''' was a device capable of storing data. By the [[time]] of the [[Republic Era]], the [[Pijali palace]]'s [[Palace library|library]] and [[Regent's private office|regent's office]] on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]] had several data solids, which the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] considered old.<ref name="MA" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> the devices were noticed in the palace's library by Jinn and his [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Not long after, when visiting the [[regent]]'s office, the [[Jedi]] pair took note of various data solids, [[holoprojector]]s, and scanners stacked in high piles upon entering the room.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Data solids appeared in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
