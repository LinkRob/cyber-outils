The '''Forome system''' was a [[star system]]<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> located within the [[Kessel sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square T-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> [[Zargo Anaximander]], the [[regent-captain]] of<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> the Outer Rim<ref name="Nightside">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Therezar system|Nightside system]]</ref> [[mining]] [[Space station|station]] [[Nightside]], considered the Forome system to be a long way out from the [[Therezar system]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />

In [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> the [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi]] was present in the Forome system when he was contacted by Anaximander with news that he had a [[Palpatine family|family]] Ochi had placed a [[bounty]] on held [[Prisoner|captive]] aboard the station. Due to being in the system at the [[time]], Ochi informed Anaximander that he would not be able to personally travel to Nightside and collect the family and [[Raid on Nightside|sent]] the [[Droid Crush Pirates of Bestoon]] group instead.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Forome system was first mentioned in ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'', a [[2022]] novel by [[Adam Christopher]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
