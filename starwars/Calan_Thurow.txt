'''Calan Thurow''' was a programmer for ''[[Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided]]''<ref name="Star Wars: Galaxies" />, a sub-lead server programmer for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''<ref name="SW:TOR" />, and the lead server engineer for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Rise of the Hutt Cartel]]''<ref name="SW:TOR:ROTHC" />. He was also the technical director for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Eternal Throne]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Onslaught]]''.<ref name="SW:TOR:KOTET" /><ref name="Star Wars: The Old Republic: Onslaught" />
