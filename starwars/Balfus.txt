'''Balfus''' was a [[Wookiee]] who lived on<ref name="Life Day" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Kashyyyk]],<ref name="Life Day" /> by around [[41 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Life Day (comic story)|Life Day]]," which include Balfus' kidnapping, to around [[41 BBY]].</ref> In the lead-up to [[Life Day]], an important Wookiee festivity, [[Trandoshan]] [[Hunting|hunters]] took advantage of the Wookiees' weakness during the holiday to capture Balfus. [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]&mdash;who was on Kashyyyk with his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], to study the Wookiees&mdash;asked [[Brennonn]], a Wookiee who lived in the same [[City|village]] as Balfus, of the latter's whereabouts. Brennonn replied that he was missing, with Jinn noting it was unusual behavior to miss Life Day celebrations. The Trandoshans later attacked the Wookiee village, capturing other Wookiees and Jinn himself.<ref name="Life Day" />

The Wookiees were eventually [[Rescue of Qui-Gon Jinn|freed]] by Kenobi, who first freed Jinn by [[Telekinesis|opening]] his cage [[Force power|with]] [[the Force]] and throwing<ref name="Life Day 2">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=4|story=Life Day Part 2}}</ref> his<ref name="SWE" /> Master's [[Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber|lightsaber]] to him. As Jinn was freed, other [[Wookiee warrior]]s arrived to support the two Jedi. The hunters then surrendered and released the [[Prisoner|captive]] Wookiees.<ref name="Life Day 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Balfus was mentioned in the comic story "[[Life Day (comic story)|Life Day]]," published in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 3|third issue]] of the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series<ref name="Life Day" /> on [[February 3]], [[2021]].<ref name="Previews World">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2020-3/digital-comic/888167|text=Star Wars Adventures (2020-) #3|nolive=1}}</ref> The comic story was written by [[Michael Moreci]] and illustrated by [[Megan Levens]] and [[Charlie Kirchoff]] and was the first of a two-part story<ref name="Life Day" /> that continued with "[[Life Day Part 2]]" in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 4|fourth issue]]<ref name="Life Day 2" /> on [[March 24]], 2021.<ref name="Previews World2">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2020-4/digital-comic/893337|text=Star Wars Adventures (2020-) #4|nolive=1}}</ref>
