'''Evan-Ott''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] who was apprenticed to [[Jedi Master/Legends|Master]] [[Sora Mobari]]. In [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]], Evan-Ott [[Attack against Ithorian terrorists|tried]] to [[Death/Legends|kill]] his Master in an explosion, but died himself in the attempt. Mobari ultimately succumbed to her wounds as well.

==Biography==
A [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-sensitive]] [[Gender/Legends|male]],<ref name="JA4" /> Evan-Ott<ref name="CSWE">{{CSWECite|I|243|Evan-Ott}}</ref> was a [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] in the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]] and apprenticed to [[Jedi Master/Legends|Master]] [[Sora Mobari]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="JA4" /> In [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]],<ref name="TNEC" /> Evan-Ott and his Master were sent<ref name="JA4" /> to the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Ithor/Legends|Ithor]]<ref name="CSWESM">{{CSWECite|II|321|Mobari, Sora}}</ref> to lead an [[Attack against Ithorian terrorists|attack]] against [[Ithorian/Legends|Ithorian]] [[Terrorist/Legends|terrorists]]. However, Evan-Ott betrayed Mobari and tried to [[Death/Legends|kill]] her<ref name="JA4" /> in an explosion.<ref name="CSWESM" /> While Mobari initially survived the blast, the Padawan was killed. Master Mobari later succumbed to her mortal injuries while at a Republic [[Medcenter/Legends|medcenter]] on the planet [[New Holstice/Legends|New Holstice]].<ref name="JA4" />

==Personality and traits==
The [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] Evan-Ott betrayed his Master and tried to kill her.<ref name="JA4" />
