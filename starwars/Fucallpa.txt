'''Fucallpa''' was the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Lokori]]. According to [[Lyra Erso]], it looked like an "enormous flower bed" due to the riotous [[color]]s and shapes of the city's basketlike buildings and spiked domes. The city was very organic in its architecture, including vines and plants woven into the residential areas and streets. Fucallpa also included horticulutrual farmland, a production plant, a historic center, and a [[spaceport]] with immigration and customs services. [[Helical HyperCom]] had its [[Helical HyperCom's headquarters|headquarters]] and [[Helical HyperCom production facility|production facilities]] in the city. These buildings were in great contrast to the natural elements of the rest of the city, with a cubical and bland style. Many of its employees lived in Fucallpa's western district. South of the city was a hilly area with hiking trails that ended with a ridge, which fell off into a basin.<ref name="Catalyst" />
