'''Apina'''<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was a [[youngling]] who was in [[Tabor Hospital]] on [[Protobranch]] when the [[planet]] was hit by a [[Protobranch solar storm|solar storm]] that disabled its technology, including the [[antigrav platform]]s that held the [[City|cities]] in the air. Apina was rescued by [[Doctor]] [[Pira]], with the help of the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s [[Dooku]] and [[Sifo-Dyas]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
