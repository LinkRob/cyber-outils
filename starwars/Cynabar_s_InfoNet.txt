'''Cynabar{{'s}} InfoNet''', also known as the '''Big CYN''', was a news organization that reported on the growing [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|conflict]] in [[the galaxy]]. They reported on another news report by [[Nal Hutta Kal'tamok]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

Sometime during the reign of [[Galactic Empire]], a news report mentioned that [[Pyke Syndicate]] was tearing down smaller criminal organizations in its bid to become [[the galaxy]]'s sole [[spice]] dealer, with insiders predicting [[Razzi Syndicate]] to be next. Cynabar's analysts were doubtful that her syndicate would survive this.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>
