'''Bay 7''' was a location just outside of the [[Vandor Railyard]], a battlefield of the [[Grand Arena]] utilized by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This area could be accessed via a large set of [[blast door]]s found on the side of the arena furthest from the drop-off, identified by the big bold [[Aurebesh]] text reading "Bay 7" displayed on them. These doors remained closed during ongoing matches between the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]].<ref name="Hunters" />
