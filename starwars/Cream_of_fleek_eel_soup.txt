'''Cream of fleek eel soup''' was a type of soup on the menu at [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. The soup contained eight live [[Fleek eel/Legends|fleek eels]], or four per cup, which were coated in [[pepper oil]]. It was a daily soup available every day and sold for 1.7 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]] a cup and 2.7 credits a bowl.
