'''Duja's brooch''' was created by [[Duja]], a [[human]] [[Gender|woman]] who served as a [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala]]. The [[Color|gold]]-and-red pin was purposefully designed to appear amateurishly-handmade like a [[Youngling|child's]] gift to their [[Parent|mother]], but in reality, it served as a [[beckon call]] for her [[transport]] [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Possibility]]''.

In [[19 BBY]], Duja brought her brooch to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]] while investigating [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] activities. Her fact-finding inquiries resulted in Separatist [[Duke]] [[Solha]] ordering Duja's [[assassin]]ation to prevent her from uncovering more evidence, and the woman's body was left in a coffin outside a [[Oga's Cantina|bar]] with her brooch still on her chest. After arriving on Batuu, Amidala acquired the pin and used it to call the ''Possibility'' to her.

==Description==
Duja's brooch was a wearable brooch crafted by [[Duja]], a [[human]] [[Gender|woman]] who had served as a [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala]]<ref name="THA" /> at some point between [[32 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Service Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the recruitment of [[Padmé Amidala]]'s first [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaidens]] to [[32 BBY]] and her [[Mission to Batuu|mission]] to [[Batuu]] to [[19 BBY]]. As ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'' establishes that [[Duja]] had served as Amidala's handmaiden prior to the novel's events, she must have been in the [[Gender|woman's]] employ at some point between those two [[Standard year|years]].</ref> The jewelry pin was fashioned out of modified [[plastoid]] and designed to aesthetically appear as an amateurishly-made [[Youngling|child's]] gift to their [[Parent|mother]].<ref name="THA" /> The [[Color|gold]]-and-red device was roughly oval in shape and bore an inscription reading "mom" in the [[Aurebesh]] [[language]].<ref name="TA1" /> Duja's brooch also served as a [[beckon call]] for her [[Starship|ship]], the [[Nomad Four]] [[transport]] ''[[Possibility]]'', and could be activated by squeezing hard in its center<ref name="THA" /> to pop open its top.<ref name="TA1" />

==History==
[[File:PadmeAmidala-ThrawnAlliancesBNExclusivePoster.png|thumb|left|210px|Padmé Amidala (pictured) acquired the brooch on Batuu after Duja's death.]]
At some point, Duja created a brooch to use as a beckon call for the ''Possibility''. She and Amidala discussed its sentimental value, including that it was made to symbolize the former's future hopes to settle down and have a child who might make a similarly fashioned gift. For several [[Standard year|years]], Duja wore her brooch often despite multiple individuals telling her it was unfashionable to don.<ref name="THA" />

In 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Padmé Amidala]]'s [[Mission to Batuu|mission]] to [[Batuu]], which is depicted in ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'', to [[19 BBY]].</ref> Duja traveled to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]] in the ''Possibility'' while investigating [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] activities during the [[The galaxy|galaxy]]-wide [[Clone Wars]] between the Confederacy of Independent Systems and the [[Galactic Republic]]. After landing her ship on the world, she brought her brooch with her while she went on a fact-finding excursion in the nearby [[Black Spire Outpost]] settlement. While investigating, Duja uncovered evidence of a Separatist [[Mokivj droid factory|droid factory]] secretly operating on the planet [[Mokivj]]. She returned to the ''Possibility'' and recorded a message for Amidala, asking her former employer to come and investigate on Batuu with her.<ref name="THA" />

[[File:DujasBroochCalling-ThrawnAlliances1.png|thumb|right|260px|Padmé Amidala used Duja's brooch to call the ''Possibility'' to her.]]
Afterward, Duja again left her ship behind in a hiding spot, taking her brooch with her to use as a beckon call for it when she returned. Around this [[time]], the Mokivj [[factory]]'s operator, [[Duke]] [[Solha]], discovered that she had learned of his operations and ordered her [[assassin]]ation. Shortly after, Duja was [[Death|killed]] on his orders, and her corpse, with her brooch still pinned to her chest, was left at a local [[Oga's Cantina|bar]]. [[Standard day|Days]] later, Amidala arrived on Batuu and visited the [[Cantina|bar]], seeing the woman's body lying in a coffin out front. Eventually, after singing a [[Funeral|funerary]] [[Music|song]] for her former handmaiden, Amidala took Duja's brooch from her corpse. Afterward, the senator used the pin as a beckon call, and the ''Possibility'' [[autopilot]]ed itself to her location.<ref name="THA" /> Inside the vessel, Amidala watched a [[Hologram|holographic]] message recorded by her late handmaiden in which Duja was wearing the brooch.<ref name="TA1" />

The issue was written by [[Timothy Zahn]] and [[Jody Houser]], illustrated by [[Pat Olliffe]] and [[Andrea Di Vito]], and released by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="TA1" /> on [[January 24]], 2024.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80019/star_wars_thrawn_alliances_2024_1|text=Star Wars: Thrawn Alliances (2024) #1}}</ref>
