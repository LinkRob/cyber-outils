'''Bay 4''' was a [[hangar]] bay in [[Mirogana Spaceport]] in the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in the [[Cadma sector]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Toshaal system]]</ref> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[coordinator]] at the [[spaceport]] made an announcement over the port's [[speaker]] system calling for an all-hands clean up in Bay 4.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bay 4 was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
