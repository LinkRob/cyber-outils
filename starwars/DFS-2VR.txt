'''DFS-2VR''' was a [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class starfighter]] that served in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] during the [[Clone Wars]], and at one point fought alongside [[R0-GR/LEGO|R0-GR]], a [[B1-series battle droid]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] were hired to repair and deliver DFS-2VR to the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Secret Base 79]].<ref name="Delivery" />
