'''Chaos''' may refer to the following items:

==[[Canon]]==
*'''[[Chaos]]''', a mythological place associated with disorder that was also known as '''Hell'''.
*The '''Chaos''', the [[Chiss]] term for the [[Unknown Regions]].
*The '''[[Crystal Chaos]]''' of [[Cardovyte]], a structure the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' weaved through whilst [[hyperspace skipping]] in [[35 ABY]].
*[[Axel Greylark]], a [[human]] who was called "'''Chaos'''" by [[Elecia Zeveron|The Mother]] of the [[Path of the Open Hand]].

==[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]]==
*[[Chaos/Legends|'''Chaos''']], a region in the [[Netherworld of the Force/Legends|Netherworld of the Force]] that was also known as Hell or The Void.
*The '''[[CX-133 Chaos fighter]]''', a [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]] used by the [[Krath/Legends|Krath]] in the [[Great Sith War/Legends|Great Sith War]].
*The '''[[Chaos Triangle]]''', a location the ''[[Admonitor]]'' was sent to by [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]], leader of the [[Empire of the Hand]].
*'''[[Chaos trooper]]s''', [[Death/Legends|suicide]] warriors of the Krath.
*'''[[Chaosphere]]s''', weapons developed by [[Darth Mekhis]] around the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Great Galactic War]].
*'''[[Embraced Chaos of Exar Kun]]''', an unwilling follower of [[Exar Kun/Legends|Exar Kun]] who became an agent of the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side of the Force]].
