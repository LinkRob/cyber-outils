An '''Alderaanian fleet trooper helmet''' also referred to as a '''service helmet''' or an '''iron rebel mask''', was an elongated helmet used by the [[Alderaanian consular security]], members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[New Republic army]].

==Description==
[[File:AlderaanianFleetTrooperHelmet.png|thumb|250px|left|A member of Alderaanian consular security wearing the helmet]]
The [[helmet]] was referred to as an Alderaanian fleet trooper helmet,<ref name="Helmet Collection 20" /> a service helmet,<ref name="Rogue One Ultimate Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> and an iron rebel mask<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> It was a [[color|white]] elongated helmet with a rear portion that extended down behind an individual to act as a neck guard. To keep the helmet securely fastened in place, a strap extended from each side of the helmet; meeting in the middle with a buckle integrate in a chin rest. The helmets commonly included an adjustable polarized visor which could be lowered to protect the wearer's [[eye]]s. Many helmets also included an integrated [[comlink]]; built into the left side of the helmet and an [[antenna]] extended upwards.<ref name="Helmet Collection 20" />

==History==
[[File:AlderaanGuard-ROShadowPortraits2.png|thumb|250px|right|An Alderaanian Guard wearing a fleet trooper helmet]]
The helmets were used by as part of the uniform of [[Alderaanian consular security]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. As part of the [[House of Organa|Royal House of Alderaan]]'s secret support for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], it allowed members of the Alliance to "steal" its [[starship]]s, including the some which contained shipments of fleet trooper helmets.<ref name="Helmet Collection 20" /> 

By [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Messenger Date">{{AndorDate|207}}</ref> members of the Rebel Alliance had began using the helmets as part of an [[Alliance military uniform|military uniform]] while at [[Great Temple|their base]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]].<ref name="Messenger">{{Andor|Messenger}}</ref> During the same time, at least one [[Unidentified rebel flagman|flagman]] at the base used a variation of the helmet without its polarizing visor.<ref name="Welcome to the Rebellion">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref> The rebels continued to use the helmet and, by [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines ANH">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> [[technician]]s and other ground personal at the base used variation of the helmet both without visors and some without comlinks.<ref name="Helmet Collection 20" /> Several fleet troopers aboard the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' also utilized the visor-less version of the helmet while defending the [[corvette]] during a boarding action by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

It continued to be used by rebel members through out the [[Galactic Civil War]] including members of [[Typhoon Squadron]] while they were stationed on the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During that [[Standard year|year]], a [[human]] [[thief]] named [[Kay Vess]] stole a grey version of the helmet from Typhoon Squadron's [[Kivo-Base]] in the [[Myrra Valley]] on Akiva.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

After the Alliance to Restore the Republic founded the [[New Republic]] by in [[4 ABY]], soldiers began using the helmet as part of their new [[New Republic soldier's uniform|uniform]].<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Behind the scenes==
The helmet first appearead though unidentified in the [[1977]] film {{Film|IV}}.<ref name="ANH" /> It was first identified as a "Alderaanian fleet trooper helmet" in the [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 20|twentieth issue]] of ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'' published by [[De Agostini]] on [[May 20]], [[2015]].<ref name="Helmet Collection 20" /> It was later identified as a "service helmet" in the [[2016]] reference book, ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Rogue One Ultimate Visual Guide" /> The [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] included the "iron rebel mask", a collectible [[speeder]] trophy which players could use to custimize the appearance of their [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
