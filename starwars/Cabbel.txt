'''Cabbel''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[lieutenant]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] and a [[controller]] aboard the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tyrant]]'', a part of the [[Death Squadron]] [[fleet]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the fleet was [[Project Swarm|tasked]] with finding the secret headquarters of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and ultimately located the [[Echo Base|base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]] in [[3 ABY]].

While the ''Tyrant'' and the rest of Death Squadron formed a [[blockade]] over the world as [[Blizzard Force|Imperial ground forces]] [[Battle of Hoth|assaulted]] the base, the [[Alliance Military|rebel forces]] below began their evacuation. The first rebel [[GR-75 medium transport|transport]] launched was the ''[[Quantum Storm]]'', which the ''Tyrant'' moved to intercept. On the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer's]] [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], Cabbel informed the ''Tyrant''{{'s}} [[captain]], [[Lennox]], of the approaching rebel [[Starship|vessel]]. A [[tractor beam]] was used in an attempt to capture the [[transport]]; however, a shot from the Alliance's Hoth-based [[V-150 Planet Defender|ion cannon]] disabled the ''Tyrant'', allowing the ''Quantum Storm'' to escape.

==Biography==
[[File:ExecutorEscort-BTMF27.png|thumb|left|250px|Cabbel was a part of Death Squadron.]]
Cabbel<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=Star Wars Force Attax: The Force Awakens|cardname=Lieutenant Cabbel|link=https://www.tradingcarddb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/122192/cid/8287769/2016-Topps-Star-Wars-Force-Attax-The-Force-Awakens-41-Lieutenant-Cabbel}}</ref> was a [[human]] member of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Officer Corps]], serving in the [[Imperial Navy]]'s [[Imperial Navy's officer corps|faction]].<ref name="ESB" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|V}} to [[3 ABY]].</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="ESB" /> Cabbel held the [[military rank]] of [[lieutenant]]<ref name="Topps" /> and was stationed as a [[controller]]<ref name="Novel" /> on the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the<ref name="ESB" /> [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="ShipsAndVehicles">{{ShipsandVehiclesCite|4|History of the Ship}}</ref> ''[[Tyrant]]''.<ref name="Tyrant">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Toryn Farr - Communications Officer}}</ref> The ''Tyrant'' was [[Commanding officer|commanded]]<ref name="ESB" /> by [[Captain]] [[Lennox]]<ref name="Lennox">{{Topps|set=Star Wars Force Attax: The Force Awakens|cardname=Captain Lennox|link=https://www.tradingcarddb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/122192/cid/8287767/2016-Topps-Star-Wars-Force-Attax-The-Force-Awakens-39-Captain-Lennox}}</ref> and served as one of the five [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] in [[Death Squadron]], the personal [[fleet]] of [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="OTFL">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref>

Arriving at the [[planet]] [[Hoth]], the location of the [[Echo Base|secret headquarters]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], Death [[Squadron]], including ''Tyrant'', formed a [[blockade]] around the planet while [[Blizzard Force|ground forces]] were [[Battle of Hoth|deployed]] to destroy a [[Deflector shield|shield]] protecting the rebel base from [[orbital bombardment]]. The Alliance began evacuating all personnel from its base, with the first escape [[Starship|vessel]] launched being the [[GR-75 medium transport]]<ref name="ESB" /> ''[[Quantum Storm]]'',<ref name="Tyrant" /> escorted by two [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s. As the trio of starships left Hoth,<ref name="ESB" /> the ''Tyrant'' moved to block their path,<ref name="Want to be a Jedi">''[[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?]]''</ref> and Cabbel approached Lennox on the [[Star Destroyer]]'s bridge to inform him that the rebel vessels were approaching their sector.<ref name="ESB" />

[[File:Lennox_Cabbel.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Cabbel served aboard the ''Tyrant''.]]
In order to capture the enemy ships with a [[tractor beam]], the ''Tyrant'' lowered its shields, unknowingly giving an opening to a [[v-150 Planet Defender]] [[ion cannon]] that the Alliance had built in its base below. The [[cannon]] fired several shots at the ''Tyrant'', disabling the vessel and sending it careening away into [[Realspace|space]] as the rebel vessels escaped<ref name="Want to be a Jedi" /> the [[star system]]. Ultimately the Empire claimed victory at Hoth, although many other rebel vessels also succeeded in escaping.<ref name="ESB" />

==Personality and traits==
Cabbel had fair skin and [[Color|brown]] [[hair]].<ref name="ESB" />

==Equipment==
Cabbel wore a standard olive-gray [[Navy|naval]] [[Imperial officer|officer's]] [[Imperial officer's uniform|uniform]] including an [[Imperial kepi]]. On his left [[breast]],<ref name="ESB" /> he<ref name="Novel" /> bore a single [[code cylinder]] and a [[rank insignia plaque]] with two red bars over two blue.<ref name="ESB" />

Cabbel was portrayed by [[Oliver Maguire]]<ref name="Maguire autograph">{{WebCite|url=http://www.starwarsautographcollecting.com/Autographs/StarWarsTrilogy/ESB/OliverMaguire.htm|text=Trilogy Autographs|work=[http://www.starwarsautographcollecting.com Dahoov's Autograph Collecting]|quote=Oliver Maguire was Lt. Cabbel in ''Star Wars: Empire Strikes Back''.|archivedate=20040929002343}}</ref> in the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. Maguire was credited only as one of several "Other Officers,"<ref name="ESB" /> with the film's script describing the character as a controller.<ref name="BTM">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref> The character first received his name in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a card in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars Force Attax: The Force Awakens|''2015 Topps Star Wars Force Attax The Force Awakens'']] set of trading cards released by [[Topps|The Topps Company, Inc.]]<ref name="Topps" /> The name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared on a card for the [[1998]] ''[[Hoth Limited]]'' expansion set of [[Decipher]]'s [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]].<ref name="HothLimited">{{CCG|set=Hoth Limited|cardname=Lieutenant Cabbel}}</ref>
