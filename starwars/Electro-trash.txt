'''Electro-trash''' was a genre of [[music]] listened to by [[Reena University]] student [[Kozmo Sundrel IV]].<ref name="QueensBloom" /> Around [[396 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' establishes that [[Axel Greylark]] was eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old thirteen years prior to its events. As ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''Convergence'' to [[382 BBY]], he must have been [[Reproduction|born]] in [[413 BBY]]. Greylark is seventeen years old in "[[The Queen's Bloom]]," dating its events to around [[396 BBY]].</ref> on the [[Standard day|day]] of the [[Queen's Bloom Festival]], Sundrel blared electro-trash through his [[speeder]] while he and fellow students [[Leyli Romero]] and [[Axel Greylark]] snuck out of Reena [[University]]'s campus to flout their volunteering duties at the festival.<ref name="QueensBloom" />

==Behind the scenes==
Electro-trash appeared in the short story "[[The Queen's Bloom]]," written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="QueensBloom" />
