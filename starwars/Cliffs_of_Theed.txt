The '''Cliffs of Theed''' were a line of towering cliffs located on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Naboo]]. The city of [[Theed]], the [[capital]] of Naboo's [[human]] [[Naboo (people)|inhabitants]], stood on a plateau at the top of one of those cliffs, with the [[Theed Royal Palace|Royal Palace]] sitting directly on its edge. Below lay Theed's busy [[Theed Spaceport|spaceport]].<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" />
