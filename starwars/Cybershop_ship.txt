'''Cybershop ships''' were [[starship]]s that acted as mobile [[cybernetics]] showrooms, installation, and maintenance facilities. Cybershop ships with legal [[Cyberneticist|cybernetics masters]] offered services from basic cybernetic prosthetics to advanced post-injury reconstruction and enhancement and were renowned in those areas. Some unscrupulous crafters offered illegal or illicit modifications, cutting-edge cybernetics with blatantly criminal utility, such as concealed weapons or lock-picking tools, that legal ships avoided installing—or at least advertising.<ref name="Special Modifications" />

Originating from hospital or emergency response ships, maintenance support starships, or ships that specialized in neither, the conversion from hospital or emergency response ships was relatively easy, although sometimes lacked the defenses required by outlaw cyberneticists. Maintenance support ships were converted by adding medical bays and surgical suites, an operation favored by providers desiring a wide array of devices and technical resources for building cybernetics and cybernetic experimentation. Cybershop ships that originated from neither of the first two fields had a much more costly and labor intensive conversion but could be selected for the starship's performance attributes.<ref name="Special Modifications" />

Cybershop ships operated by poorly run or indebted organizations provided cheap services, but such workshops were a dirty and unwelcoming house of horrors. One cybershop ship was a modified [[CR90 corvette]] produced by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] and cost 1,300,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. The ship was equipped with long-range sensors, a navicomputer, and its primary Class 2 [[hyperdrive]] and a Class 15 hyperdrive. The cybershop, staffed by 135 enlisted [[officers]] and crew, including [[Doctor|medical staff]], transported up to 100 passengers, eighty patients, and forty [[medical droid]]s and had one [[Standard year|year]] of consumables. The CR90 was armed with one dorsal turret-mounted twin medium [[turbolaser]] battery and one ventral turret-mounted twin medium turbolaser battery.<ref name="Special Modifications" />
