'''Cippa Tarko''' was an [[Arkanian]] [[Gender|female]] [[Jedi Initiate]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. Because she was unusually gifted, she was assigned to [[Jedi Master]] [[Yaddle]].<ref name="Cataclysm" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Biography==
Cippa Tarko hailed from [[Arkania]].<ref name="Cataclysm" /> She was discovered by Jedi Master Yaddle,<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> who became Tarko's mentor.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

While traveling to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]], Yaddle contacted [[Yoda]] via [[hologram]], and Tarko asked her whether Yoda was her brother. Due to a poor connection, Yaddle misunderstood Yoda's instructions and went to Dalna with Tarko. On the way, they met with Jedi [[Creighton Sun]] and [[Aida Forte]] as well as two of the Chancellor's guards, [[Lu Sweet]] and [[Priv Ittik]]. Landing on Dalna, Yaddle had the guards look after Tarko while the other Jedi investigated the [[Path compound]]. Tarko soon escaped and rejoined Yaddle, with Sweet and Ittik following behind her. Yaddle gave all of them stolen robes to disguise themselves as members of the [[Path of the Open Hand]]. On Tarko's suggestion, the group split up, with Yaddle and Tarko investigating the Path's huts, which they discovered were occupied only by the elderly and infirm. They found missile launchers hidden in the compound and returned to their ship to warn the others. However, Path members had found the ship, and they stunned Tarko.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

As fighting broke out, Yaddle hid Tarko in a vegetable storage structure and told her to stay there until Yaddle came back. However, Tarko disobeyed her and joined the [[Night of Sorrow|battle]].<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Personality and traits==
Cippa Tarko had medium olive skin, white eyes, and white hair, which hung in four braids over her shoulders.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> She was impatient and confident.<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" />

==Powers and abilities==
Cippa Tarko was considered by Yaddle to be unusually gifted in the Force. She could use [[telekinesis]] to split apart a [[blue ice apple]] and throw people into the air.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cippa Tarko first appeared in the [[2023]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' by [[Lydia Kang]].<ref name="Cataclysm" />
