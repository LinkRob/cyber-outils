The '''Chardaan Orbital Yards''' were [[shipyard]]s<ref name="TLJ ICS" /> located in the [[Chardaan system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. The [[freighter]] [[pilot]] [[Lantra Tan]] hid in the shipyards due to a [[bounty]] placed on her head.<ref name="NoDisint" /> The [[Guild d'Lanseaux]], an artisan shipwright, operated there, where they built the [[yacht]] ''[[Libertine]]'' in [[66 BBY]].<ref name="TLJ ICS" />
