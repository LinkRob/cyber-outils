"'''Callsigns: Names of Kashyyyk'''" is a ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' article published in [[Star Wars Gamer 10|''Star Wars Gamer'' 10]]. It was written by [[Owen K.C. Stephens]]. It details the nature and meaning of [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] names in [[Shyriiwook/Legends|Shyriiwook]].
