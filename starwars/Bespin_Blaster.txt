The '''Bespin Blaster''' was a modified [[heavy blaster pistol]] utilized by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Zaina]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Veteran Blaster|standard blaster]]. This [[blaster]] [[pistol]] featured a [[Color|golden]] body, barrel, and scope, a small silver plate and connector, and a blue grip with white markings and an almost cloudy white overlayer.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bespin Blaster was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Zaina's Veteran Blaster, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cloud Officer (Zaina)|Cloud Officer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
