The '''Diagu''' were a group of [[human]]s, located on [[Targyon]]. They were sympathetic to the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Great Jedi Purge]].<ref name="Inquisitors 1" /> Between [[14 BBY]] and [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[The Grand Inquisitor]] visited the Diagu based on intelligence the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Tensu Run]] was building a refuge on Targyon. The Diagu refused to reveal the [[Jedi]]'s location the [[Galactic Empire]], with The Grand Inquisitor [[Death|killing]] the [[Unidentified Diagu elder|elder]]. [[Gender|He]] then ordered the remaining humans be executed with their childen taken to the Imperial [[capital]], [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Inquisitors 1" />
