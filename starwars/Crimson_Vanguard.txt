The '''Crimson Vanguard''' was a set of armor worn by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Loyalist Trooper|standard costume]]. This [[armor]] sported an added [[antenna]] on the side of the [[helmet]], and seemed to have some connection to the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] and [[Fortress Vader]] according to Sentinel.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Crimson Vanguard was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Sentinel, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 4.0.0 update alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Crimson E-Web]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing it within the Hunters in-game shop,<ref name="Hunters" /> or in the Crimson Vanguard Pack on the official Nintendo website.<ref name="Nintendo Store">{{WebCite|url=https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/star-wars-hunters-crimson-vanguard-pack-switch|text=STAR WARS: Hunters™ - Crimson Vanguard Pack|work=[https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/ My Nintendo Store]|archivedate=20250322014616}}</ref>
