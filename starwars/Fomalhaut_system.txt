The '''Fomalhaut system''' was a [[Star system/Legends|star system]] the primary [[Star/Legends|star]] of which was [[Fomalhaut]]. The system regularly underwent dangerous seasons during which the star unleashed powerful [[Solar flare/Legends|stellar flare]], thus filling the system with dangerous radiations.

==Behind the scenes==
The Fomalhaut system has only been mentioned in ''[[L'Arche d'Alliance]]'' ("The Ark of Covenant"), a scenario published in the French RPG magazine ''[[Dragon Radieux 17|''Dragon Radieux'' #17]]''.
