The '''Anadeen system''' was located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]]<ref name="Slice" /> in grid square O-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> It contained [[Anadeen]], the site of a [[Galactic Empire]] [[Anadeen shipyard|shipyard]] the control over which was contested by the [[New Republic]]<ref name="Life Debt" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="5 ABY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="9 ABY">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> the Anadeen system was within New Republic territory.<ref name="MVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Anadeen [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix released alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]].<ref name="Systems" /> Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
