'''Brentaal IV''', also referred to simply as '''Brentaal''', was a terrestrial [[planet]] located in the [[the galaxy]]'s [[Core Worlds]], on both the [[Hydian Way]] and the [[Perlemian Trade Route]].

==Description==
A [[planet]]<ref name="Vanguard Squadron" /> in the [[Core Worlds]] region of [[the galaxy]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> Brentaal IV was located at the confluence of the [[Hydian Way]] and the [[Perlemian Trade Route]], positioned at the coordinates L-9<ref name="The Force Awakens Beginner Game" /> on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Rebel Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref>

Brentaal IV had a rocky surface and an [[atmosphere]] that was breathable to [[human]]s. At least one [[moon]] could be seen in its sky.<ref name="Star Wars 61" /> The planet shared its name with the [[Brentaali]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|You never heard of the butcher of Brentaal IV?|[[Stel]]|<ref name="Adventures in Wild Space: The Dark">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Dark]]''</ref>}}
The [[pirate]] [[Q'anah (pirate)|Q'anah]] and her [[Q'anah's father|father]] were natives of Brentaal IV, and Q'anah's [[Parent|father]] served [[House Cormond]] as a [[bodyguard]] until he was given a lucrative offer by [[House Elegin]] of the planet [[Asmeru]] to work for them instead.<ref name="Tarkin" /> The planet was the site of the [[Republic Futures Program]] training facility, where [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]] met and befriended [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]]<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> in [[36 BBY]].<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> Youths and academics on Brentaal IV, as well as those on the Republic [[capital]] planet [[Coruscant]], took notice of the plight of the [[Alvadorjian]] people and took part in an activism campaign designed to raise attention to their troubles, culminating in the ultimately doomed-[[Alvadorjian Senate bill]].<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

[[File:JulHandsOutSupplies-2020DarthVader29.png|thumb|left|300px|[[Jul Tambor]] helps the [[Skakoan]]s on exile on Brentaal IV]]
The [[Nuxan]] [[Information broker|informant]] [[Bodo Linx]] had a contact,<ref name="Star Wars 56" /> [[Qensog]],<ref name="Star Wars 61" /> on Brentaal IV, who he believed could help reconnect the fragmented [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] after the [[Battle of Mako-Ta]] giving [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] the contact's details.<ref name="Star Wars 56" /> The rebels later met with Qensog, and he gave them the locations of both [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar's]] ''[[Home One]]'' and [[Hera Syndulla]]'s [[Geist (cruiser)|''Geist'']].<ref name="Star Wars 61" />

Brentaal IV was a key foothold for the [[New Republic]] during the final days of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. It and other planets in the Bormea sector were defended by the New Republic's [[Vanguard Squadron]].<ref name="Vanguard Squadron" />

==Inhabitants==
Brentaal IV was the [[homeworld]] of [[Platt Okeefe]],<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> a famous [[smuggler]], freedom fighter, and entrepreneur.<ref name="BMF66">{{BuildFalconCite|66|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:MrAndMrsVaderDepart-2020DarthVader32.png|thumb|right|300px|Imperial forces on the rocky ground of Brentaal IV.]]
Brentaal IV was first mentioned in [[canon]] in the novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], written by [[James Luceno]] and released in [[2014]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> It made its first appearance in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Star Wars (2015) 61|''Star Wars'' (2015) 61]], written by [[Kieron Gillen]], illustrated by [[Andrea Broccardo]], and published on [[February 6]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/71187/star_wars_2015_61|text=Star Wars (2015) #61}}</ref>

Its first mention was in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, in the "[[Galaxywide NewsNets]]" segment of [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 3|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 3]], published in [[1994]].<ref name="Galaxy">{{JournalCite|3|Galaxywide NewsNets}}</ref>
