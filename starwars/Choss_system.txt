The '''Choss system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained the [[planet]] [[Choss]].<ref name="Repair" /> It was situated at the coordinates J-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Choss system was mentioned in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
