'''Darrell Rodriguez''' was President of [[LucasArts]] from April [[2008]] to May [[2010]]. Under his leadership, the company saw various changes aimed at maximizing its profitability, including budget reduction, outsourcing increase, and staff layoff.<ref name="LinkedIn" /><ref name="Eurogamer" />

Former to his position at LucasArts, Darrell Rodriguez was notably the [[Wikipedia:Chief operating officer|chief operating officer]] at [[Wikipedia:Danger Close Games|EA Los Angeles]].<ref name="LinkedIn" />
