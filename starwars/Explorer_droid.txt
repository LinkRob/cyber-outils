An '''explorer droid'''<ref name="The Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> was a type of [[droid]] that assisted individuals in remote or dangerous locations. One such model was the [[BD explorer droid|BD unit]], which was designed to be an assistant to [[Scout|explorers]] or researchers who operated alone in the field. The BD unit [[BD-1]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|bd-1|BD-1}}</ref> accompanied the [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]], and later the former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]].<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

These droid types were designed to store and gather information. BD-1 was able to scan objects and present information about the item to Kestis. BD-1 could also [[Holoprojector|project]] [[hologram]]s of his old master that were stored within him.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Chapter 5 Date">{{BoBFDate|5}}</ref> the unit [[BD-72]]<ref name="Mandalorian Experience">{{YouTube|video=BKDLvemhY_k|text=The Mandalorian Experience, Full Tour at Star Wars Celebration 2022|channel=attractionsmagazine|name=Attractions Magazine}}</ref> projected holographic building instructions for an [[Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter|N-1 Starfighter]] to the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]].<ref name="Chapter 5">{{BOBF|Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian}}</ref>
