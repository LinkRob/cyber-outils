'''Crispy Kaadu Cutlet Sandwich''' was a [[sandwich]] served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]], on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Docking" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> It was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="Docking" />

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Crispy Kaadu Cutlet Sandwich first appeared as a provision available to guests at the Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo restaurant in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disneyland]] Park, exclusively for [[Star Wars Nite]]s in [[2022]].<ref name="2022 brochure">[[Star Wars Nite]] [[2022]] brochure, as seen in {{YouTube|video=k1UrJziLWro|text=Disney's FIRST Star Wars Nite! &#124; Disneyland After Dark 2022|channel=@PatrickDougall|name=Patrick Dougall|date=2022-05-04}}</ref> The menu described it as "[[Wikipedia:Breadcrumbs|Panko]]-crusted [[Wikipedia:Tonkatsu|Pork Cutlet]], [[Wikipedia:Pineapple|Pineapple]] [[Wikipedia:Gremolata|Gremolata]], [[Wikipedia:Guava|Guava]] [[Wikipedia:Barbecue sauce|Barbecue Sauce]], Spiced [[Wikipedia:Pork rind|Pork Rind]], [[Wikipedia:Portuguese sweet bread|Hawaiian Bread]] Bun, [[Color|Purple]] [[Wikipedia:Coleslaw|Slaw]]."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref>
