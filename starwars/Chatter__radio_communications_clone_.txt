'''Chatter''' was a rookie [[Radio Communications Clone|radio communications]] [[clone trooper]] who participated in the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="The Complete Season Two" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Chatterconcept.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Concept art of Chatter]]
Chatter was slated to appear in "[[Landing at Point Rain]]," the fifth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''. However, he was cut from the finished episode.<ref name="The Complete Season Two" />

Concept art and several animatics featuring Chatter were later made available in the Jedi Archives section on the first disk of [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Two|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Two]].<ref name="The Complete Season Two" />
