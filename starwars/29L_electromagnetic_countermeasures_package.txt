The '''29L electromagnetic countermeasures package''' was a device produced by [[Carbanti]]. [[Smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] linked a 29L electromagnetic countermeasures package with his ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'s '' [[jammer]], which expanded the jammer's broadcast. Unfortunately for Solo, the first time he used the 29l in unison with his jammer he wound up jamming his own ship's internal communications.<ref name="CFOWM" />
