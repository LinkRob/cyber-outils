'''Bevin Twelve''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Abyss" /> in the [[Bevin system]] in the [[Rolion sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was ruled by a [[satrap]]. The [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] was present on Bevin Twelve when the satrap was overthrown, which occurred sometime prior to the [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="Abyss" />
