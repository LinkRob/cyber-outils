The '''DV supervisor droid''' was a model of [[supervisor droid]].<ref name="Every Droid" /> One DV supervisor,<ref name="Solo" /> [[DV-4D4]],<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2018 Topps Solo: A Star Wars Story|cardname=#27 - DV-4D4|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/162389/cid/11326516/2018-Topps-Solo:-A-Star-Wars-Story-27-DV-4D4|nobackup=1}}</ref> worked in the control room of the [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] of [[Kessel]]<ref name="Solo" /> in [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on [[Kessel]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to nine [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]]; therefore, the events on Kessel in ''Solo'' must take place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> DV-4D4, along with the other [[droid]]s in the control room, were freed in an [[Raid on Kessel|uprising]] by [[L3-37]].<ref name="Solo" />
