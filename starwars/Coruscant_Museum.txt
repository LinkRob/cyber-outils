The '''Coruscant Museum''' was located in [[Imperial City]] on<ref name="Aphra 16" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Aphra 16" /> By between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-marvel|text=SWCE 2023: Marvel Reveals ''Star Wars: Dark Droids'' Horror Event and More}} establishes that the events of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']], including [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 16|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 16]], take place between the events of {{Film|V}} and {{Film|VI}}, which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Gender|male]] individual [[Tase Somer]] was the [[museum]]'s [[Head Curator]]. After they were instructed to acquire an artifact of the [[Ascendant]] [[Religion|cult]] by [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]] of the [[Tagge Corporation]], the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] traveled to Somer's [[Tase Somer's residence|residence]] on Coruscant. Once they arrived, Starros asked Aphra if<ref name="Aphra 16" /> she<ref name="USW" /> was sure her acquaintance would have information on Ascendant artifacts. In response, the archaeologist claimed to the smuggler that Somer used the [[black market]] to fill the museum's collections and that he was most likely to have an artifact they sought or know someone who did.<ref name="Aphra 16" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Coruscant Museum was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 16|sixteenth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 16" /> on [[November 17]], [[2021]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/96839/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_16|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #16}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Coruscant Museum/Legends|Coruscant Museum]] was mentioned in ''[[Cracken's Threat Dossier]]'', a supplement for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' that was published in [[1997]].<ref name="Cracken's Threat Dossier">''[[Cracken's Threat Dossier]]''</ref>
