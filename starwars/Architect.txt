[[File:Orson_Krennic_RO.png|thumb|right|200px|Orson Krennic was an Imperial architect]]
An '''architect''' was an individual who designed buildings or structures. The construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]] required the permanent on-site presence of habitat architects, in addition to [[scientist]]s and weapons specialists.<ref name="tarkinnovel">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[Tiaan Jerjerrod]], a scion of wealthy family from [[Tinnel IV]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> worked as an architect before he became an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Moff]].<ref name="beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref>

[[Orson Callan Krennic]] was largely responsible for the remodeling and expansion of [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|Republic Center for Military Operations]].<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> There also existed [[Architect droid|models]] of [[droid]]s that fulfilled that function.<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|characters|huyang|Huyang}}</ref> The architects of [[Jedi Order|Jedi]] temples designed the buildings to reflect their personalities, and [[Kanan Jarrus]] speculated that the architect of the [[Lothal Jedi Temple]] had a fondness for mystery.<ref name="Ezra">''[[Ezra's Duel with Danger]]''</ref>
