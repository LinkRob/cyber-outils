'''Enthenium''' was a [[jungle]] [[planet]]<ref name="Yoda 7" /> located in the [[Enthenium system]] in the [[Jjannex sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was situated in grid square K-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> along the [[Lipsec Run]]<ref name="LipsecRun" /> in the [[Western Reaches]]. A [[Separatist base (Enthenium)|Separatist base]] was located on the planet.<ref name="Yoda 7" />
