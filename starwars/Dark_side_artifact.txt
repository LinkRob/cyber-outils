{{Quote|Throughout the galaxy, there have been legends of objects imbued with the dark side. Of amulets and crystals and even glaciers that contained malevolence as much as any living creature. Some say that the most powerful Sith where able to to that&mdash;to infuse their own darkness to objects around them.|Cohmac Vitus|<ref name="ItD">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref>}}
A '''dark side artifact''' was an [[Force artifact]] infused with the [[dark side of the Force]]. The [[Sith]] created [[Sith artifact|their own]] dark side artifacts,<ref name="ItD" /> such as [[Sith mask]]s,<ref name="SotS">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref> [[Sith scroll]]s, and [[Sith holocron]]s.<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> According to the [[Jedi Master]] [[Cohmac Vitus]], at least one dark side artifact was known to him in around<ref name="ItD" /> [[230 BBY]].<ref name="ItDdate">''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' dates the launch of the [[Starlight Beacon]] to 232 [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to [[232 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. As ''The High Republic: Into the Dark'' depicts the [[dedication of the Starlight Beacon]], its events must occur around that year.</ref> There where multiple different types of dark side artifacts, including amulets, crystals, and even glaciers.<ref name="ItD" /> The [[Archive of Forbidden Artifacts]] contained many dark side artifacts of the Sith, [[Yacombe]], and [[Sorcerers of Tund]], including scrolls, the [[Saber of Darth Krall]],<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> the [[Mask of Lord Momin|mask]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Momin]], diverse [[lightsaber]]s,<ref name="SWIIBC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-inside-intel-the-bogan-collection|text=''Star Wars'' Inside Intel: The Bogan Collection|int=Star Wars Inside Intel}}</ref> and a [[parang]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
