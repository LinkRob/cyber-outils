The '''"Blinder" 4B2 jamming array''' was a type of [[sensor jammer]] manufactured by [[Irilliad]] and was commonly installed on the [[RZ-2 A-wing interceptor]].<ref name="IncrediBuilds" /> It worked in conjunction with an advanced [[CloakEye sensor jammer]]<ref name="RSOWM">''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> that broadcast data bursts to disrupt enemy sensors and impede detection.<ref name="IncrediBuilds" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Blinder" 4B2 jamming array was first mentioned in the [[2017]] activity book ''[[Star Wars: A-wing Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]'', written by [[Michael Kogge]].<ref name="IncrediBuilds" />
