The '''''Environmental Action Gazette''''' was a [[HoloNet]] publication that dealt with environmental action reporting. The gazette managed to put a stop to a number of business practices that the demolitions expert [[Skelly]] considered bad<ref name="AND" /> by [[11 BBY]].<ref name="AND Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']] to [[11 BBY]].</ref> Skelly came across the gazette several times while researching how the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[mining]] involvements on the [[moon]] [[Cynda]] were damaging its integrity, but did not consider himself part of the gazette's audience.<ref name="AND" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="AND Date" /> the [[Twi'lek]] [[pilot]] [[Hera Syndulla]] convinced Skelly she was a [[Journalist|reporter]] for the ''Environmental Action Gazette'' in an attempt to learn about Skelly's research. Although Skelly believed that the gazette had been shut down, Syndulla lied by claiming it was simply retooling and offered him the chance to be part of their relaunch. Syndulla succeeded in learning about Cynda's perilous position, and although Skelly soon realized she was not truly affiliated with the gazette, they later worked together to [[Gorse Conflict|stop]] the Empire from destroying the moon.<ref name="AND" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Environmental Action Gazette'' was mentioned in the novel [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']], written by [[John Jackson Miller]] and published in [[2014]].<ref name="AND" />
