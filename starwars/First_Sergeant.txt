'''First Sergeant''' was a [[Military rank|rank]] for [[soldier]]s. During the height of the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] [[Hazram Namir]] was a First [[Sergeant]] in [[61st Mobile Infantry|Twilight Company]], acting as a link between the [[officer]]s and enlisted [[troop]]s.<ref name="B:TC" /> [[Jimmon Arbmab]] was a First Sergeant in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Hammers Elite Armor Unit|Imperial Hammers]]<ref name="SWROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Vanguard armor regiment|regiment]].<ref name="Forged in Battle">''[[Forged in Battle]]''</ref>
