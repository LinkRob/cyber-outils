'''Fergriss Pharmaceuticals''' was one of the massive corporations that controlled the polluted [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Balosar]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The company and its corporate rivals on the world, including [[BioTech Industries]], continually engaged in corporate espionage and sabotage against one another while ensuring that any illegal activity was ignored by the Empire through substantial kickbacks to corrupt officials.<ref name="CandM" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Fergriss Pharmaceuticals was mentioned in ''[[Cyphers and Masks]]'', a [[2018]] supplement for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying]] game published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="CandM" /> The [[Fergriss Pharmaceuticals/Legends|company]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 11: Criminal Organizations]]'', a [[1994]] supplement written by [[Rick D. Stuart]] for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="GG11">''[[Galaxy Guide 11: Criminal Organizations]]''</ref>
