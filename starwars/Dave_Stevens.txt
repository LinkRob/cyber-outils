'''Dave Stevens''' was an American illustrator best known for his [[Wikipedia:good girl|good girl]] [[Wikipedia:pin-up|pin-up]] artwork, and as the creator of [[Wikipedia:The Rocketeer|The Rocketeer]].

Stevens assisted [[Russ Manning]] on the [[Wikipedia:Tarzan|Tarzan]] newspaper strips in the 1970s, and was one of the artists who helped Manning complete the run of the ''[[The Frozen World of Ota]]'' story arc when Manning fell ill during the run of that strip.

On [[March 10]], [[2008]], Dave Stevens passed away after a prolonged bout with Leukemia.
