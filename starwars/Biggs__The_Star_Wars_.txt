'''Biggs''' was a [[Prince/Legends|prince]], as well as the son of [[King/Legends|King]] [[Kayos]] and [[Queen/Legends|Queen]] [[Breha (The Star Wars)|Breha]] of [[Aquilae (The Star Wars)|Aquilae]]. He had a brother, [[Windy (Aquilae)|Windy]], and a sister, [[Leia (The Star Wars)|Leia]].

Biggs and Windy discovered that their sister Leia was preparing to leave the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] and informed their father about Leia's departure, urging him to say goodbye to her.
