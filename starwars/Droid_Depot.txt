'''Droid Depot''' was a shop found in the [[Land Port]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. Owned by [[Mubo]], a sixth generation droid builder on Batuu, the shop repaired and sold [[droid]]s, tools, parts, and manuals.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> A sign outside of the shop advertised "SALES," "SERVICE," and "TRADES" in stylized [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="D23 First">{{D23|url=first-look-stunning-star-wars-galaxys-edge-photos-reveal-intergalactic-new-details|text=Stunning ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge Photos Reveal Intergalactic New Details}}</ref> The shop had an attached repair station and [[oil bath]], although it was unknown how long the oil had been in there; customers were advised to use the bath at their own risk. The Depot was well stocked with [[C1-series astromech droid|C1-series]], [[R-series]], and [[BB-series astromech droid|BB-series]] droids that could be modified to the customer's specifications.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Droid_Depot_SWGE.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of the shop]]
The Droid Depot is one of the gift shops for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], specializing in customizable remote-control droid toys. These droids have interactive functionality within the themed land. This includes reacting differently to areas such as the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance Supply|encampment]] or [[First Order]] area near [[Docking Bay 9]], depending on what particular personality chip is installed in the droid. The droids can also interact with each other, if multiple are left idle but still switched on while in close proximity to each other.
