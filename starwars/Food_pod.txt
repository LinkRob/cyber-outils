A '''food pod'''<ref name="Roy Choi">{{SWYouTube|fwqguHvfG2Y|Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu &#124; Cooking with Roy Choi &#124; In Theaters May 22}}</ref> was a type of small [[Restaurant|eatery]]. The food pod [[Hugo's]], ran by the [[Ardennian]] [[fry cook]] [[Hugo Durant]], served freshly made and grilled [[Flat Meat Fry|Flat Meat Fries]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. The food pod was open at [[Local day|night]] and could be shuttered by its four-armed vendor from within.<ref name="Official Trailer">{{SWYouTube|IHWlvwu8t1w|The Mandalorian and Grogu &#124; Official Trailer &#124; In Theaters May 22}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The food pod would appear in the May [[2026]] [[Jon Favreau]] film ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]'', having been first shown in its trailer that was released on [[February 17]], 2026.<ref name="Official Trailer" /> As the director and also a writer of the film, Favreau added the food pod scene and also called it a "[[Wikipedia:Food truck|food truck]]," which exist in the real world as mobile food vendors. Favreau then contacted the chef [[Wikipedia:Roy Choi|Roy Choi]] for help with the food to be shown, eventually leading to the creation of a [[Flat Meat Fry]] sandwich that the digitally animated character Hugo would cook with his four arms, complete with voice-over by filmmaker [[Martin Scorsese]].<ref name="Roy Choi" />
