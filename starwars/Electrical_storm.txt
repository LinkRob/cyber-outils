An '''electrical storm''' was a weather phenomenon which could occur on the [[water]] [[planet]] [[Kamino]]. Near the top of [[Tipoca City]]'s main dome, several [[static discharge tower]]s were placed which helped secure the [[city]] during electrical storms.<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
