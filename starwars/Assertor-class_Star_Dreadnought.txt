The '''''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought''', also known as the '''''Assertor''-class Command Dreadnought''', was a class of [[Star Dreadnought]] manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]] that served in the [[Galactic Empire]]. One of the largest [[Starship|vessels]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] in terms of size and power, it was characterized by its long and thin arrow-shaped [[hull]]. The [[dreadnought]] boasted a large array of armaments and utilities to fulfill its roles as a [[command ship]], [[sector]] patrol vessel, and [[fleet]] operations craft. Introduced between [[19 BBY]] and [[3 BBY]], when the Empire experimented on larger [[battlecruiser]] models, few of the large [[warship]]s were built.

The ''Assertor''-class dreadnought saw use in the [[Imperial Era]] during the [[time]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], with the lead ship of the class, the ''[[Assertor]]'', and its sister ship, the [[Wrath (Assertor-class)|''Wrath'']], being laid down prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], both ships and their [[battle group]]s conducted anti-Alliance operations along the border between the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid]] and [[Outer Rim Territories]].

==Characteristics==
===Design===
Manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]] and priced at 1,100,000,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]],<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> the ''Assertor''-class [[Star Dreadnought]],<ref name="BMF 34" /> also known as the ''Assertor''-class Command Dreadnought,<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> was a class of Star Dreadnought<ref name="BMF 34" /> characterized by its long, thin [[arrow]]-shaped [[hull]]. The hull featured a broad ridge that ran along roughly three-quarters of the dorsal face of the [[Starship|ship]], ending just forward of the tapering fan-tail, which overhung the primary [[ion engine]]s.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

[[File:Eclipse_Executor-class_SWA.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Assertor''-class was second only to the 19,000-meter-long ''Executor''-class (pictured).]]
In terms of size and power, the ''Assertor''-class was second only to the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]],<ref name="Lead by Example" /> which measured 19,000 [[meter]]s and was armed with 5,000 [[turbolaser]]s, among other [[weapon]]s.<ref name="Starfighters">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> As such, it was the largest vessel in the family of [[Imperial Navy]] [[dreadnought]]s that included the [[Bellator-class dreadnought|''Bellator''-class]] and [[Mandator-class Dreadnaught|''Mandator''-class]].<ref name="Lead by Example" /> [[Naval Station Validusia]] was large enough to have the capability of docking an ''Assertor''-class along with a dozen other [[capital ship]]s.<ref name="BMF 34" />

===Equipment and complement===
To fulfill its roles, the ''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought was equipped with numerous sophisticated [[Communications suite|communications]] and long-range [[Sensor array|sensor suites]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> The ship featured command and control systems, allowing its [[Commanding officer|commanders]] to have a near-unparalleled view of the battlefield.<ref name="Lead by Example" /> It was also equipped with a [[navigation computer]], a class 2 primary [[hyperdrive]] with a class 12 acting as secondary, and enough consumables to last three [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

In order to operate the vessel, the Assertor was manned by 125,000 [[officer]]s, [[pilot]]s, and enlisted crew, while also carrying 20,000 [[troop]]s and support staff. It maintained a complement of 120 [[starfighter]]s and numerous [[shuttle]]s, [[landing barge]]s, [[assault ship]]s, [[gunboat]]s, [[Ground warfare|ground assault]] vehicles, and [[AT-series]] [[walker]]s.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

===Armament===
Overall, the Assertor was armed with twenty port and twenty starboard [[Quad turbolaser cannon|quad heavy turbolaser batteries]], fifteen port and fifteen starboard [[quad battleship ion cannon]] batteries, and ten dorsal twin heavy turbolasers. Additionally, there were ten forward, ten port, and ten starboard quad light turbolaser batteries. Twenty ventral twin [[battleship]] [[ion cannon]] batteries were also placed, along with ten port and ten starboard dorsal medium ion [[cannon]]s and twenty heavy [[tractor beam projector]]s.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

There were also twenty forward, twenty port, and twenty starboard [[turret]]-mounted [[quad laser cannon]]s, in company with thirty forward, thirty port, and thirty starboard [[concussion missile launcher]]s.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> These weapon emplacements crowded the vessel's dorsal and ventral hull, giving each ''Assertor''-class vessel the firepower to nearly destroy an entire [[planet]].<ref name="Lead by Example" />

==Role==
The ''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought was tasked with fulfilling several roles, including [[fleet]] and [[sector]] patrol duties and fleet operations. The Assertor was additionally optimized as a sector [[command ship]] and served as a powerful mobile weapons platform.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> The dreadnoughts were typically the [[flagship]]s of their own [[battle group]]s as they supported combat operations.<ref name="Lead by Example" />

==History==
[[File:GalaxyMap-GETravelersGuide.jpg|thumb|right|280px|The battle groups of the ''Assertor'' and ''Wrath'' operated in the borders of the Mid and Outer Rim Territories.]]
In [[19 BBY]], Kuat Drive Yards was contracted as the exclusive supplier of [[Star Destroyer]]s for the Imperial [[Navy]], leading to a fleet buildup during the [[Imperial Era]]. In the [[time]] between 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[3 BBY]], the [[Galactic Empire]] experimented with larger [[battlecruiser]] models of the Star Destroyer design family, creating the ''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought and the ''Bellator''-class dreadnought. However, few of the large [[warship]]s were built.<ref name="Timelines" />

The class's lead ship, the ''[[Assertor]]'', and its sister ship, the [[Wrath (Assertor-class)|''Wrath'']], were laid down at [[Kuat shipyards|Kuat Drive Yards]] at some point prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Lead by Example" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the ''Assertor'' was deployed with a [[Assertor battle group|battle group]], while the ''Wrath'' served in a different [[Wrath battle group|fleet]]. Both battle groups operated concurrently on the border between the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid]] and [[Outer Rim Territories]] to provide support for anti&ndash;[[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] operations.<ref name="Lead by Example" />

Although the exact composition of the battle groups was not known, it was assumed that both vessels operated with dozens of other ships, including smaller dreadnoughts, [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class]] and [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]], numerous [[cruiser]]-sized vessels, and numerous support ships. The fleet of an ''Assertor''-class vessel at one point consisted of at least two ''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers and large scores of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE/ln fighters]], and was located near a light [[Color|blue]] celestial body that was in proximity to a smaller, darker-colored terrestrial body.<ref name="Lead by Example" /> At one point during the Empire's reign, an agent of the Rebel Alliance was held [[Prisoner|captive]] aboard an Assertor, awaiting interrogation. In order for the Alliance to preserve its secrets, the agent had to be eliminated or rescued.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:AptlyNamedWrath-EGW.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The ''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought originated in ''Star Wars'' Legends storytelling.]]
The ''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 34|thirty-fourth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s magazine series ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'',<ref name="BMF 34" /> published around [[August 26]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF 34 Date">{{BMFdates|34}}</ref> The Assertor was later pictured in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for use with the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Lead by Example" /> The book's depiction of the ship was drawn by [[BalanceSheet|Steve Hamilton]].<ref name="Artist">{{DeviantArt|username=balance-sheet|url=balance-sheet/art/Assertor-Class-Dreadnought-591340371|name=Steve Hamilton|text=Assertor Class Dreadnought}} &mdash; According to {{WebCite|url=https://balancesheetart.com/resume|text=balancesheetart.com/resume|work=https://balancesheetart.com/|archivedate=20200615215902}}, "Balance-Sheet" is [[BalanceSheet|Steve Hamilton]].</ref> The ''Assertor''-class was originally a fan creation designed by [[Ansel Hsiao]] in [[2010]].<ref name="StarDestroyer.net">{{WebCite|url=http://bbs.stardestroyer.net/viewtopic.php?p=3242676#p3242676|text=Something big|author=fractalsponge1|date=2010-10-02|work=[https://bbs.stardestroyer.net/index.php StarDestroyer.Net BBS]|archivedate=20200615214309}} &mdash; According to {{WebCite|url=http://fractalsponge.net|text=fractalsponge.net|work=https://fractalsponge.net/ Fractalsponge]|archivedate=20200412235819}}, [[Ansel Hsiao]] is "fractalsponge1."</ref> Both Hsiao's design and the name [[Assertor-class Star Dreadnought/Legends|''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought]] were later introduced to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in [[Jason Fry]] and [[Paul R. Urquhart]]'s [[2012]] reference book, ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''.<ref name="Essential Guide">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>

Due to the heavy armament of the ''Assertor''-class, ''Lead by Example'' suggests that player characters should not be forced to directly fight an Assertor. Instead, the sourcebook provides an alternative way of using the vessel in roleplaying game adventures&mdash;as a backdrop or the location of an objective that the characters must attain. An example given in the book describes a game-master player using an Assertor's weapons systems to target the player characters' starships on each turn of the game while a space battle is being fought across the dreadnought's surface. A game mechanism is also suggested that allows for missed shots by the Assertor's weapons to damage the ships of the characters' enemies instead.<ref name="Lead by Example" />
