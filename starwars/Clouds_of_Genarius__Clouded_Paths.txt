"'''Clouds of Genarius: Clouded Paths'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the second episode of the [[Clouds of Genarius trilogy]] and was written by [[Jason Nichols]] and [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Fesvk Wefos is again having employee problems. This time, he has lost one in Depatar, also known as the City of Masks. Are the heroes up for another rescue mission, and a chance to explore one of the largest black markets in existence? An adventure for Living Force heroes levels 1-6. It is strongly recommended that this be played after Head in the Clouds and before Into the Storm Clouds.''
