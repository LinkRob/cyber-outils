A '''bombardier''' was an individual who attended to the bombs release trigger of a [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17]]. [[Nix Jerd]] served as the bombardier of the [[Resistance]] [[bomber]] ''[[Cobalt Hammer]]''<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" /> whilst [[Edon Kappehl]] served as the bombardier aboard ''[[Crimson Hailstorm]]''.<ref name="ICC" />

The [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter]] was armed with [[ArmaTek]] [[VL-61/79 proton bomb]]s, deployed from a [[proton bomb]] chute attached to the [[starfighter]]'s [[armor]]ed [[hull]], and was optionally manned by a bombardier/[[gunner]].<ref name="UVG" />
