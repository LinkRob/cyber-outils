'''Bokk Naarg''' was a [[Sullustan]] who fought alongside [[Saw Gerrera]] before the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and later as a member of Gerrera's [[partisans]]. In [[0 ABY]], shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], Naarg attempted to defect from the Partisans after seeing the futility of their cause, claiming to have information on [[Lux Bonteri's stepdaughter|where]] the [[Dreamers|remaining Partisans]] were getting information from. [[Inferno Squad]] was sent to [[Mission to the Tellik Four Station|recover and extract]] Naarg from [[Tellik Four Station]], but failed to do so because three other Partisans carried out a [[Death|suicide]] bombing, which [[Death|killed]] Naarg. Despite this, [[Del Meeko]]'s [[ID10 seeker droid|ID10 seeker]] [[Dio|droid]] managed to recover Naarg's intel on a [[datachip]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
