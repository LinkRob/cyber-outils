The '''Clarr family fleet''' was a [[fleet]] used by the [[Clarr family]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] as a defense force. As a member of the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]], the Clarr had the right to a fleet of genuine [[warship]]s. Some of the fleet's warships were located at the [[Clarr fleet depot]], including the ''[[Orisson]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

The [[Grysk]] agent "[[Jixtus]]" suborned the Clarr [[Patriarch]] [[Clarr'ivl'exow]] "Rivlex," convincing him to unwittingly lend the Clarr family fleet to the [[Grysk Hegemony]] as part of the [[Jixtus's plan|plot to destroy the Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
