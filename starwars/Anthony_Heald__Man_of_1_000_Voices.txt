"'''Anthony Heald: Man of 1,000 Voices'''" is an article by [[Rich Handley]] in [[Star Wars Insider 60|''Star Wars Insider'' 60]], released on [[June 4]], [[2002]]. The article recounts an interview with ''[[Star Wars]]'' audiobook narrator [[Anthony Heald]], and it discusses Heald's acting journey and the challenge of narrating the audiobooks.<ref name="Article" />
