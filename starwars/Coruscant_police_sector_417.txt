'''Coruscant police sector 417''' was a [[sector]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="PoP" />

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Coruscant police sector 417 first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Pursuit of Peace]]." Police Sector 417 was written in [[Aurebesh]] on the side of the [[Panther police interceptor|police speeders]] that appeared in that episode.<ref name="PoP" />
