The '''Cron Supernova''' was a [[supernova]] that took place in the [[Cron Cluster]]. According to the [[Qel-Droma Epics]], the supernova was triggered by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Naga Sadow]], and the [[Jedi]]-controlled [[planet]] [[Ossus]] was devastated by the event.<ref name="NOP" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cron Supernova was first [[canon]]ically mentioned in the sourcebook ''[[Nexus of Power]]''.<ref name="NOP" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Cron Supernova/Legends|Cron Supernova]] first appeared in the comic book [[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 4|''Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War'' 4]], and it was triggered by [[Krath/Legends|Krath]] sorceress [[Aleema Keto]].<ref name="TOTJ-TSW-4">[[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 4|''Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War'' 4]]</ref>
