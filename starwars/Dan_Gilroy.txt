'''Daniel Christopher Gilroy''' is an screenwriter who wrote three episodes for ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.

==Biography==
Dan Gilroy was one of the writers for the ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series.<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> In [[November]] [[2019]] in [[Wikipedia:New York|New York]],<ref name="Vision to life">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/andor-beau-willimon-sanne-wohlenberg-interview|text='Andor's EP Sanne Wohlenberg and Writer Beau Willimon on Bringing Tony Gilroy's Vision to Life|author=Lovitt, Maggie|date=2022-11-10|work=[[Wikipedia:Collider (website)|''Collider'']]|archivedate=20221110211930}}</ref> the first writers' room session with Gilroy and fellow writers [[Tony Gilroy]] and [[Beau Willimon]]<ref name="Breaks down">{{SW|url=news/tony-gilroy-andor-season-1-interview|text=Tony Gilroy Breaks Down ''Andor'' Season 1}}</ref> lasted five or six days. Dan and Tony had a similar skill set, and they got to work on the [[Aldhani heist|heist]] arc, with which they were familiar due to their previous work.<ref name="Easter eggs">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/andor-tony-gilroy-talks-luthen-rael-monologue-easter-eggs-1235258961|text=‘Andor' Creator Tony Gilroy Talks Luthen Rael's Future and Being Surprised by Certain Easter Eggs|author=Davids, Brian|date=2022-11-09|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20221109220924}}</ref> The writers knew they were doing the [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex|prison]]<ref name="Breaks down" /> on [[Narkina 5]],<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> though it took the group a couple days to figure out how to make it unique from other prisons.<ref name="Breaks down" /> To make the prison the opposite of dark and gritty, they made it brightly lit and antiseptic. The writers decided the prisoners would build things on a factory floor<ref name="Vision to life" /> and the floor would be electrified. They realized the guards would need to wear [[Tunqstoid protective boot|rubber boots]]. In an early iteration of the story, the prisoners would steal the boots to [[Narkina 5 prison escape|escape]].<ref name="Breaks down" />

Dan Gilroy wrote the episodes "[[Aldhani (episode)|Aldhani]],"<ref name="Aldhani" /> "[[The Axe Forgets]],"<ref name="The Axe Forgets">{{Andor|The Axe Forgets}}</ref> and "[[The Eye]]."<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref> Dan, Tony, and Willimon brought in [[Tom Bissell]] to work as a writer for [[Andor Season 2|Season 2]]. The writers spent about seven days in the writers' room working on the season.<ref name="Easter eggs" />
