'''Cortex bombs''' were specialized [[bomb]]s used as [[Death|kill]] switches for [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Species|beings]]. They used a series of tiny [[explosive charge]]s wired to an implanted individual's [[brain]], that would detonate when a specific trigger code was seen, heard, or spoken. Cortex bombs didn't deal any damage to the surrounding area, and dealt very little discernible damage to the implanted individual. They cost 1,200 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and were used as last-resort measures to keep [[agent]]s from breaking under [[Torture|interrogation]].<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cortex bombs were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Cyphers and Masks]]'',<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Accept Your Mission">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/8/16/accept-your-mission|text=Accept Your Mission}}</ref> They originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', a [[2011]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] that was produced and released by [[BioWare]].<ref name="SWTOR">''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''</ref>
