An '''attack cruiser''' was a classification of [[cruiser]] that was used by [[Navy|navies]]. In the [[Republic Navy]], the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] was considered to be an attack cruiser.<ref name="Republic Attack Cruiser">{{Databank|republic-attack-cruiser|Republic Attack Cruiser}}</ref> In the [[Imperial Navy]], attack cruisers were considered a downgrade from [[Star Destroyer]]s. Due to attritional losses of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s Star Destroyers after the [[Battle of Jakku]], the remnants of the Imperial Navy were forced to rely on attack cruisers such as the [[Garrote (attack cruiser)|''Garrote'']].<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
