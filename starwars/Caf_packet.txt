'''Caf packets''' were a [[caf]] product that was included on a supplies list written by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Technician|mechanic]] and [[artist]] [[Sornah Bocc]]<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> around [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> The list was [[Writing|written]] in [[aurebesh]] of a [[datapad]] that Bocc left in her [[Sornah's Garage|garage]] on the [[planet]] [[Malastare]], where the [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Volo Bolus]] also lived.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />
