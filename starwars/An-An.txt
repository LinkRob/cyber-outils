'''An-An''' was the [[language]] of the [[Elia-An]] of the [[planet]] [[Oanne]]. Its vocalizations were similar to [[Shyriiwook]], the language of the [[Wookiee]]s. The Wookiee [[Jedi Knight]] [[Burryaga Agaburry]] was able to speak An-An, softening his Shyriiwook to reflect the vocalizations of the Elia-An's language.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
