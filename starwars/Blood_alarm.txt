'''Blood alarms''' were a type of [[alarm system]] that could send a [[Comm unit|comm]] notification to individuals when a [[blood-sensitive sensor]], a [[sensor]] that detected the spillage of [[blood]], was triggered. In [[32 BBY]], [[Captain]] [[Quarsh Panaka]] installed a blood alarm and its sensor in [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[Padmé Amidala's apartment (Theed Royal Palace)|apartments]] within the [[Theed Royal Palace]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]].

That [[Standard year|year]], the blood-sensitive sensor was triggered when Amidala's [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] [[Saché]] started her [[period]], and it sent a blood alarm notification to the captain, leading him and his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Mariek Panaka]], to investigate. Upon entering the rooms and discovering what had happened, Saché was deeply embarrassed. Afterward, Mariek helped the [[handmaiden]] clean up and admonished Quarsh for placing a blood alarm in an apartment inhabited by teenage [[Gender|girls]].

==Description==
{{Quote|It's never gone off before.|Quarsh Panaka, about the blood-sensitive sensor's blood alarm in Padmé Amidala's apartment|<ref name="QP" />}}
A blood alarm was a type of [[alarm system]] that worked in tandem with a [[blood-sensitive sensor]], a type of [[sensor]] that detected when [[blood]] had been spilled. When the sensor was triggered, a blood alarm could send a [[Comm unit|comm]] notification to individuals as an alert.<ref name="QP" />

==History==
{{Quote|I'm assuming she's not injured, at least?''"<br />"''She's fine. And you're an idiot for putting a blood-sensitive sensor in the bedroom of a teenage girl.|Quarsh and Mariek Panaka after a blood alarm was triggered in Padmé Amidala's apartment|<ref name="QP" />}}
[[File:Theed_Palace_full.jpg|thumb|left|230px|A alarm was installed in Padmé Amidala's apartment within the Theed Royal Palace (pictured).]]
In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="QP Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' to [[32 BBY]].</ref> [[Captain]] [[Quarsh Panaka]] of the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]] acquired a blood alarm connected to a blood-sensitive sensor and installed both in [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[Padmé Amidala's apartment (Theed Royal Palace)|apartments]] in the [[Theed Royal Palace]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]] as part of his security preparations. At some point, Amidala's [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] [[Eirtaé]] accidentally triggered the sensor after pricking her [[Limb|finger]] with a needle while sewing, which activated the alarm.<ref name="QP" />

Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="QP Date" /> the [[handmaiden]] [[Saché]]'s [[period]] started and triggered the blood-sensitive sensor in Amidala's rooms when blood stained the [[queen]]'s bedsheets. As a result, the blood alarm Panaka had installed in the apartment was triggered and sent an alert to his comm. He and his [[Marriage|wife]], [[officer]] [[Mariek Panaka]], hurried to Amidala's apartments to determine the cause of the incident and ensure Amidala's safety. Upon loudly entering the rooms, they startled Saché in her [[sleep]] and turned on the lights. After realizing the handmaiden's period had started, Mariek sent Quarsh away and helped a mortified Saché clean up the bedroom's sheets and acquire new [[Clothing|sleeping attire]].<ref name="QP" />

Afterward, Mariek assured Quarsh that Amidala was safe and admonished him for putting a blood alarm and its sensor in a room lived in by young teenage [[Gender|girls]], some of whom were not yet using [[suppressant shot]]s to manage their period cycles. The captain was embarrassed, promising to apologize later.<ref name="QP" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="QH Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Hope]]'' to [[22 BBY]].</ref> one of Amidala's former handmaidens, [[Sabé]], reminisced about the incident and Quarsh's horror upon realizing why his blood-sensitive sensor's alarm had been triggered.<ref name="QH">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A blood alarm first appeared in ''[[Queen's Peril]]'', a [[2020]] novel written by [[E. K. Johnston]] as the second installment of ''[[Star Wars: Queen's Series]]''.<ref name="QP" /> During the development of ''Queen's Peril'', Johnston indicated that several members of [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] felt that the scene that a blood alarm featured in was incredibly awkward and left comments on the draft after reading it. The author felt strongly about the scene's inclusion in the work.<ref name="RebelReads Interview">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@RebelLegionOfficial|link=Rebel Legion|video=PLsER9FqeCM|text=Rebel Reads EK Johnston Interview|date=2021-03-21}}</ref>
