{{Quote|Is this about the shipping barons from Kuat? Because let me tell you, they were the ones who insisted on ordering the cuttle-tick. My people warned them that the carapace was venomous and just part of the presentation.|Toro Sbazzle, to Lando Calrissian|<ref name="FACPOVESB" />}}
The '''cuttle-tick''' was an [[Foodstuff|edible]] creature that had a venomous carapace.<ref name="FACPOVESB" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"[[But What Does He Eat?]]" takes place concurrently with {{Film|V}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[Devaronian]] [[Executive Chef]] [[Torro Sbazzle]] served cuttle-tick to a group of shipping [[baron]]s from<ref name="FACPOVESB" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="BTC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> [[Kuat]], her staff warning them that the carapace was for show and not to be eaten. The [[Gender|barons]] had insisted on ordering the dish and while Sbazzle made it for them, she was worried that they might ingest the venom by mistake. Sbazzle later recounted the incident to her employer, the [[Baron Administrator]] of [[Cloud City]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]].<ref name="FACPOVESB" />

==Behind the scenes==
