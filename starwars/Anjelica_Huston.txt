'''Anjelica Huston''' (born [[July 8]], [[1950s#1951|1951]]) is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] actress regarded as an iconic figure for her various roles, including [[Wikipedia:Morticia Addams|Morticia Addams]] in [[Wikipedia:The Addams Family|''The Addams Family'']]. In [[2023]], she voiced the [[Sith Mother]] in the short film "[[Screecher's Reach (short film)|Screecher's Reach]]," a part of the anthology series ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|Volume 2]]. The episode's director, [[Paul Young]], was delighted to have Huston involved. In the [[2024|following year]], she voiced an additional character, [[Isa Durand]], in the [[The Bad Batch Season 3|third season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''.

==Biography==
{{Quote|We have the wonderful Anjelica Huston who plays the Sith Mother, and she plays it quite nicely. It's like she's a lovely person but she's hiding something behind that voice, that facade.|Paul Young|<ref name="Filmmaker Focus">{{VisionsFocus|Screecher's Reach}}</ref>}}
Anjelica Huston was born on [[July 8]], [[1950s#1951|1951]] in [[Wikipedia:Los Angeles|Los Angeles]], [[Wikipedia:California|California]], [[Wikipedia:United States of America|United States of America]]. She<ref name="Vanity" /> is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]]<ref name="Britannica" /> actress who spent her early years in [[Wikipedia:Ireland|Ireland]].<ref name="Vanity" /> Huston is regarded as a screen icon,<ref name="Visions announcement">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-visions|text=SWCE 2023: ''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Trailer, Key Art, and Cast Revealed}}</ref> and she is known for numerous roles, including her [[Wikipedia:Academy Award|Academy Award]]-winning performance in the [[1985]] film [[Wikipedia:Prizzi's Honor|''Prizzi's Honor'']] and for her role as [[Wikipedia:Morticia Addams|Morticia Addams]] in [[Wikipedia:The Addams Family|''The Addams Family'']] [[1991]]&mdash;[[1993]] film series.<ref name="Britannica" /> Illustrator [[Elsa Charretier]] based the design of the [[Nautolan]] character [[Clariah]] on Huston's appearance.<ref name="Clariah">{{Twitter|e_charretier|status/1005125821588295681|[[Elsa Charretier]]|date=2018-06-09|quote=Clariah's design, one of the characters from our STAR WARS ADVENTURES #11 Lando story was based on Anjelica Huston 😬 !|archivedate=20230523123136}}</ref> That character first appeared in the short comic story "[[Tales from Wild Space: Family Affair, Part 1]]," published in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 10|tenth issue]] of [[IDW Publishing|IDW's]] [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'' series]]<ref name="Family Affair, Part 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=10|story=Tales from Wild Space: Family Affair, Part 1}}</ref> on [[May 9]], [[2018]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2017-2020-10/digital-comic/652799|text=Star Wars Adventures #10|nolive=1}}</ref>

[[File:SithMother.png|thumb|right|250px|Anjelica Huston's first ''Star Wars'' character: The Sith Mother]]
In [[2023]], Huston joined the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise as the voice of the [[Sith Mother]] in "[[Screecher's Reach (short film)|Screecher's Reach]]," the second episode of [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|Volume 2]] of the anthology television series ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]''.<ref name="Screechers">{{Visions|Screecher's Reach (short film)}}</ref> The episode was produced by [[Cartoon Saloon]], an [[Wikipedia:Irish people|Irish]] animation studio, and directed by [[Paul Young]], who was proud of the creation of a ''Star Wars'' short film that featured a predominantly Irish cast. Given Anjelica's previous time living in Ireland and her father [[Wikipedia:John Huston|John Huston's]] support of the [[Wikipedia:Cinema of Ireland|Irish film industry]], Young was glad to have her involved. A team from [[Lucasfilm]] facilitated Huston's participation, and Young enjoyed the actress's ability to switch her performance from a stern<ref name="Space" /> yet lovely<ref name="Filmmaker Focus" /> [[Parent|motherly]] figure to a more sinister character<ref name="Space" /> hiding something beneath her façade.<ref name="Filmmaker Focus" /> Huston's casting was first announced at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]] on [[April 10]], 2023, prior to the release of Volume 2 on [[Disney+]] on [[May 4]] of that year. Her involvement in the series was described as a "surprise" by the announcement post on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Visions announcement">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-visions|text=SWCE 2023: ''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Trailer, Key Art, and Cast Revealed}}</ref>

Huston voiced a different ''Star Wars'' character, the [[Devaronian]] [[Isa Durand]], in the Disney+ series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''. The character first appeared in "[[Paths Unknown]]," the second episode of the series' [[The Bad Batch Season 3|third season]],<ref name="Paths Unknown">{{TBB|Paths Unknown}}</ref> which premiered on [[February 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Watch">{{SW|url=news/the-bad-batch-season-3-trailer|text=Watch the ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 3 Trailer}}</ref>
