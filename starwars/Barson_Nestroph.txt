'''Barson Nestroph''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[colonel]] in the [[Imperial Army|army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. They were the [[Parent|father]] of [[Heater (pilot)|Heater]], a [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] in the [[New Republic]]'s [[Riot Squadron]].<ref name="Alphabet" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{AlphabetSquadronDate}}</ref> Heater and a number of other [[pilot]]s aboard the [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] ''[[Hellion's Dare]]'' played ''[[Who? What? Where?]]'', a game where each participant set out who they wanted to be informed first of their [[death]], their cause of death, and where it would happen. Heater chose Nestroph to be the first to hear of his death, expressing his desire for his father to know he made something out of himself.<ref name="Alphabet" />

==Behind the scenes==
Barson Nestroph was mentioned in the [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], the initial volume of the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Alphabet" />
