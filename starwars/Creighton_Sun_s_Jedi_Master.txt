A [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]] was the '''Jedi Master''' of eventual [[Jedi Master]] [[Creighton Sun]]. During the [[Night of Sorrow]]<ref name="Cataclysm" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Cippa Tarko]] told Jedi Master [[Yaddle]] that she wanted to help their allies [[Priv Ittik]] and [[Lu Sweet]]. Yaddle refused the [[youngling]]'s request and reminded Tarko that she had promised to stay by her side. Sun then also told Tarko to stay close and to listen to Yaddle as she knew more than them all. Following this, Sun thought back to when he was a youngling about how he had wanted to do more than he was allowed and how he had known more than his Master.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
