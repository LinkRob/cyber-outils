A '''Confederacy of Independent Systems hex icon''',<ref name="Low Red Moon">''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]''</ref> also known as the '''Separatist hex icon''' was the symbol used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="SWP">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> It was a hexagon with dark [[Color|blue]] outlines resting on one of its bases, dissected into six trapezoids, and containing a smaller hexagon at its heart.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The ''[[Taxation Without Federation]]'' [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] featured the symbol patterned in a web. The final version of ''[[Report Sedition]]'', a work of propaganda made by [[Chrigeld Tinnine]] for the [[Galactic Empire]], depicted the arrestation of a man who had just defaced an [[Imperial crest]] on a wall and painted a Separatist hex icon below it.<ref name="SWP" />
