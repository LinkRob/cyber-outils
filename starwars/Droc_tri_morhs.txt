'''Droc'tri'morhs''', [[core name]] '''Octrimo''',<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> was a [[Chiss]] [[Gender|male]] of the [[Droc family]] who served as a [[Mid Commander]] and [[pilot]] aboard the [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']], a [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[Nightdragon man-of-war]] commanded by [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Biography==
At the Lioaoin heartworld, Octrimo specifically makes a comment about the aptitude and ability of Chiss sky-walkers. During the Vigilant's recent journeys, Chiss ambassador [[Boadil'par'gasoi]] (Ilparg) repeatedly made derogatory comments about the Vigilant's current sky-walker, [[Che'ri]], directly within her earshot. At one point, these comments almost brought her to the point of sleeplessness due to the anxiety she felt. The Vigilant's whole bridge crew, in fact, had quietly seethed at Ilparg's treatment of the girl.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Droc'tri'morhs first appeared in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first book of [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''The Ascendancy Trilogy'']] by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> He was first identified by his full name in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>
