'''''Doctor Aphra'' 11''' is the eleventh issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The comic was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 30]], [[2021]].

==Publisher's summary==
''MYSTERIES ABOUND ABOARD A DURGE-ANGED SHIP! DOCTOR APHRA and SANA STARROS discover an eerie abandoned ship while on a new mission from DOMINA TAGGE. But on board they will come face-to-face with a nightmarish horror not seen in the galaxy for untold eons. And then there's&mdash;.THE FEARSOME BOUNTY HUNTER DURGE!''
