A '''breaching torpedo''' was a type of [[missile]] that could be used to break through walls. When extracting [[Tunga Arpagion]] from the [[Dex Acquisitions]] [[Dex Acquisitions Depot|depot]] on [[Meor Ain]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] had [[Han Solo]] use a breaching torpedo to get into the building.<ref name="Star Wars 45" />
