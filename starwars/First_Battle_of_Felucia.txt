The '''First Battle of Felucia''' was a battle that took place for control of the [[planet]] [[Felucia]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The Republic was forced to retreat when Separatist forces threatened to overrun them.

==The battle==
{{Quote|Come on, grunts! We are leaving!|Captain [[Rex]] ordering his men onto the gunships|<ref name="Holocron Heist" />}}
The [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Grand Army of the Republic|forces]] on [[Felucia]] under [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Jedi Commander|Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] were surrounded by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[battle droid]]s that were overrunning their positions. During the battle, Skywalker sent Tano on patrol into the [[jungle]].<ref name="Holocron Heist" /> The Separatists also set up a [[Felucia blockade|blockade]]<ref name="Collapse" /> around the [[planet]]. Thus, Kenobi's and Skywalker's [[troop]]s knew they had to retreat.<ref name="Holocron Heist" />

[[File:PloKoonDelta7B-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|Plo Koon and his forces broke the blockade of Felucia.]]
[[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Plo Koon]] and his [[104th Battalion|forces]] then arrived. Koon's [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] were able to blow a hole in the Separatist's blockade. Plo Koon, in [[Blade of Dorin|his]] [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]], along with several [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s, accompanied the [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|gunships]] to evacuate Kenobi's and Skywalker's forces. Koon then took on the [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] while [[Clone trooper pilot|clone pilots]], including [[Warthog]], protected the [[gunship]]s. The gunships were able to land and started to evacuate Kenobi's and Skywalker's forces.<ref name="Holocron Heist" />

However, Padawan Commander Tano refused to be evacuated, now that her patrol had the battle [[droid]]s on the run. She continued pushing the droids back, but Skywalker convinced her to retreat when he said the droids were regrouping and were about to overrun her position. Thus, Tano and her troops abandoned their vehicles and boarded the gunships,<ref name="Holocron Heist" /> with her vehicles ultimately being destroyed by a [[battalion]] of [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="Holocron Heist AD" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|The setback at Felucia has affected our efforts in the whole sector. It'll take weeks before we can…|Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Holocron Heist" />}}
Due to her actions on the battlefield, Tano was assigned by the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] to guard duty in the [[Jedi Archives]]. The retreat from Felucia affected the Republic's effort in the entire [[Thanium sector|sector]].<ref name="Holocron Heist" />
