A '''desert plum''' was a type of [[plum]] found in [[desert]]s. The [[Twi'lek]] [[Majordomo]] [[Bib Fortuna]] referred to the [[musician]] [[Jess]] as his "little desert plum."<ref name="FACPOV" />
