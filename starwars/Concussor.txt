'''''Concussor''''' was a [[Burst Fire-class Deep-Space Patrol Vessel|''Burst Fire''-class Deep-Space Patrol Vessel]]. Originally in service of the [[Imperial Remnant]], it was seized by [[Wraith Squadron]] in [[44 ABY]]. ''Concussor'' was self-destructed by the Wraiths after a confrontation with the [[Galactic Federation of Free Alliances|Galactic Alliance]] vessel ''[[Starhook]]''.
