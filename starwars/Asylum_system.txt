The '''Asylum system''' was a [[star system]] located in the unexplored reaches of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Asylum system originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as the [[Asylum]] [[space station]] in the video game ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Fallen Empire]]''<ref name="KOTFE">''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Fallen Empire]]''</ref>  but was excluded from the updated [[canon]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix of star systems that was released on [[June 24]], [[2025]], due to it lacking defined coordinates or being a codename. The appendix's author [[Jason Fry]] included it in a list of "unlisted" systems that are still tracked internally by [[Lucasfilm]] despite their exclusion from the apppendix,<ref name="Substack" /> and has stated that the systems should be treated as existing in the canon continuity.<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-08-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-08-31}}</ref>
