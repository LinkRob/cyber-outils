The '''Fleet Trooper''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Zaina]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Rebel Veteran|standard costume]].<ref name="Hunters" /> It strongly resembled the [[Alderaanian]] [[Alderaanian consular security|consular security]] [[Clothing|uniforms]] sometimes donned by the [[Rebel trooper|fleet troopers]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fleet Trooper was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Zaina, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Fleet Blaster]].<ref name="Hunters" />
