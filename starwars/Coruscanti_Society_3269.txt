'''''Coruscanti Society'' #3269''' was an issue of the periodical ''[[Coruscanti Society]]'' that included an entire section titled "Palpatine&mdash;Supreme Chancellor at War," which covered [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The section feted Palpatine for how the long [[Standard hour|hours]] he worked were, the many [[Galactic Senate]] committees he attended, and the energy and vigor he put into maintaining the [[Galactic Republic]]'s efforts in the war. The [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] cited the section of ''Coruscanti Society'' #3269 on Palpatine in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
''Coruscanti Society'' #3269 was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
