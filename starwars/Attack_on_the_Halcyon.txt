In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[pirate]]s led by [[Crimson Jack]] launched an '''attack''' on the<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 1" /> [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]]<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> ''[[Halcyon]]'' after they intercepted a transmission from a [[Resistance]] [[spy]] who was traveling aboard the [[starship]]. Jack knew the information would be of value to the [[First Order]], who would pay him a lot for it and demanded the ship's [[captain]], [[Riyola Keevan]], turn over the spy, with the threat of destroying the ship if his demands were not met. Keevan, however, insisted to her crew that they should remain calm as panicking would not help their passengers. As Jack began to grow tired of waiting, he had his [[Crimson Jack's starship|ship]] fire on the ''Halcyon'' as of way of showing that he was not bluffing.<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 1" />
