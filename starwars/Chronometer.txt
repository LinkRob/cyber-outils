A '''chronometer''', '''chrono''',<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> or '''clock''',<ref name="ANH Illustrated">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref> was an electronic device for the keeping of [[time]].

When trying to erase all of evidence of her use of a [[snooper]] program, [[Merei Spanjaf]] was foiled by a [[computer]] terminal that had a bad chronometer. Spanjaf also kept a personal chronometer of her own on her person. She once slept so soundly that when she checked it after waking up, she found it was two hours after dawn.<ref name="secret">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref>

The lack of a clock on the wall of [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] cell on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], along with the austere surroundings and lack of company, left her unmoored in time.<ref name="ANH Illustrated" />

Mechanical clocks with 12 [[Standard hour|hours]] were used in [[the galaxy]] in ancient times.<ref name="Bomber Command">''[[The Last Jedi: Bomber Command]]''</ref>
