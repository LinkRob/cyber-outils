The '''DF-90 Mortar''' was a portable [[mortar]] artillery used by [[coastal defender stormtrooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The launcher had two projectile tubes, and was anchored down by two leg braces attached to either side of the launcher near the barrel openings.<ref name="Legion" />

==Behind the scenes==
The DF90 Mortar was first mentioned and pictured in the ''[[Imperial Shoretroopers Unit Expansion]]'' for ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'', an asymmetrical two-player, infantry-based war game published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Legion" />
