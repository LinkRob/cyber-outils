'''Fleet logistics liaison''' was an [[assignment]] aboard certain [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[warship]]s, including [[Star Destroyer]]s. They liaised between the [[Starship|ship]] and the [[Imperial Navy]]'s [[logistics corps]], ensuring that the crew had the necessary supplies to stay [[Foodstuff|fed]], [[Clothing|clothed]], and in fighting shape. Although a desk job, it involved a lot of datawork that made it very stressful.<ref name="The Sith of Datawork">{{StoryCite|story=The Sith of Datawork|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> Around [[1 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> [[Arvira]] held that position aboard the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]''.<ref name="The Sith of Datawork" />
