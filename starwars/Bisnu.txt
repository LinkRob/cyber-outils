'''Bisnus''' were a type of small, quadrupedal, [[Reproduction|egg]]-laying creature that could be found in the junkyards on the<ref name="ScrappingSong" /> [[planet]]<ref name="BraccaDatabank">{{Databank|bracca|Bracca}}</ref> [[Bracca]]. They had [[Color|purple]] skin, green [[eye]]s, and gray [[Hair|fur]].<ref name="ScrappingSong" /> Sometime between [[232 BBY]] and [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|304A}}</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Initiate|Padawan]] [[Lys Solay]] and<ref name="ScrappingSong" /> her<ref name="THR Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[astromech droid]] [[Gigi (droid)|Gigi]] went searching for an instrument on Bracca for [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Nash Durango]]'s [[Gender|mom]]. When the two found a [[guitar]]-like instrument, they discovered that a bisnu was trying to use the soundhole as a nest for her eggs. Realizing the instrument provided too little space for the creature, Solay offered her a bigger crate to be used as a nest instead. The bisnu accepted it, and Solay took the instrument, eventually giving it to Nash.<ref name="ScrappingSong" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bisnus appeared in the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Three|third season]] of the [[Disney+]] animated show ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'' in the episode "[[Scrapping for a Song]],"<ref name="ScrappingSong" /> which premiered on [[December 8]], [[2025]].<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/young-jedi-adventures-season-3|text=Watch the ''Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures'' Season 3 Trailer with Your Younglings}}</ref>
