'''Assistant Data Processor''' was a role in the [[Galactic Empire]] that was tasked with the processing of fines.<ref name="Vader 44" /> After being charged for launching an investigation into the destruction of [[Coruscant]]'s [[Sector 35|sectors 35]] and [[Sector 36|36]]<ref name="Vader 43">[[Darth Vader (2020) 43|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 43]]</ref> during the [[Scourge]] [[Scourge of Coruscant|assault]],<ref name="Vader 40">[[Darth Vader (2020) 40|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 40]]</ref> [[Agent]] [[Fabarian]]<ref name="Vader 43" /> of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] was demoted to the role of Assistant Data Processor. Fabarian, having been discreetly recruited into [[Administrator]] [[Sly Moore]]'s [[Schism Imperial]], a faction dissatisfied with the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]], used his position to approve a fine for [[Baron]] [[Slandarv]], who the Schism suspected was developing a new [[Slandarv's battle droid model|model]] of [[battle droid]]. Slandarv called the fine processor to his office and removed his rank, but Fabarian secretly [[Slicer|sliced]] into the baron's data.<ref name="Vader 44" />
