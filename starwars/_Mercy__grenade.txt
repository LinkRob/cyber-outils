The '''"Mercy" grenade''' was a model of [[stun grenade]] produced by [[Goseia Heavy Industries Concern]]. The grenades cost 175 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] per unit and used a [[gas]] to incapacitate enemies. Originally designed as a riot-control device, the grenade was eventually shelved after it began to cause psychological symptoms in the people that it was used against.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
