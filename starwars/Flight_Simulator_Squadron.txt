'''''Flight Simulator Squadron''''' was a [[flight simulator]] [[hologame]] that could be played by two individuals. Players would race their [[starship]]s by moving their bodies to control their ship's movements. There were three different versions of the game, and the [[Ace Squadron|Ace]] [[pilot]] [[Torra Doza]] owned all three, which she played in her [[Torra's Quarters|room]] in [[Doza Tower]] on the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|refueling station]] ''[[Colossus]]''. In [[34 ABY]], Doza played ''Flight Simulator Squadron'' with her friend [[Kazuda Xiono]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Wow, you've got all the latest flight simulators.|Kazuda Xiono is impressed by Torra Doza's collection of flight simulators, including ''Flight Simulator Squadron''|audio=FlightSimulatorSquadron2-SecretsAndHolograms.flac|<ref name="Secrets and Holograms" />}}
[[File:FlightSimulatorSquadronScreen-SecretsAndHolograms.png|thumb|right|270px|''Flight Simulator Squadron'' gameplay]]
''Flight Simulator Squadron'' was a [[flight simulator]] [[hologame]] in which a player raced against another in a [[starship]] while avoiding [[asteroid]]s and an occasional [[exogorth]] that could pop up from the top of the screen. The game utilized gesture controls from the player in order to pilot<ref name="Secrets and Holograms" /> the computer-generated<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|flight-simulator-squadron|Flight Simulator Squadron}}</ref> starship, requiring players to move their bodies to play. ''Flight Simulator Squadron'' also included [[music]] that would play during the game. In total, there were three versions of the game.<ref name="Secrets and Holograms" /> There were other games similar to ''Flight Simulator Squadron'', such as the [[arcade game]] ''[[Space Slug Slam 6]]'',<ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref> which featured similar graphics and challenges but involved a single player interacting with a 2D monitor and piloting the [[starfighter]] with two shiftable sticks.<ref name="Live Fire">{{Resistance|Live Fire}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|I'm gonna crush you at that Flight Sim.''"<br />"''I'd like to see you try.|Kazuda Xiono and Torra Doza prepare to face off in ''Flight Simulator Squadron''|audio=FlightSimulatorSquadron3-SecretsAndHolograms.flac|<ref name="Secrets and Holograms" />}}
[[File:Kaz-and-Torra-SecretsandHolograms.png|thumb|left|270px|Kazuda Xiono was excited to play ''Flight Simulator Squadron''.]]
The [[human]] [[Ace Squadron|Ace]] [[Torra Doza]] owned the three versions of the game in [[Torra's Quarters|her quarters]] on the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|refueling station]] ''[[Colossus]]''.<ref name="Secrets and Holograms" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|1|10}}</ref> Doza's [[Doza family|father]] [[Imanuel Doza|Imanuel]] restricted her from flying due to the presence of the [[First Order]] on the [[Supertanker fuel depot|station]], so Doza was left to play ''Flight Simulator Squadron'' instead of flying her ship,<ref name="Secrets and Holograms" /> the ''[[Blue Ace]]''.<ref name="Blue Ace">{{Databank|blue-ace|Blue Ace}}</ref> Later, when speaking with her friend, the [[Technician|mechanic]] and [[pilot]] [[Kazuda Xiono]], and complaining about her flight restrictions, she sullenly said that the only flying she could do was play ''Flight Simulator Squadron''. Xiono was excited to learn that she owned the game, and Doza happily invited him to her quarters in [[Doza Tower]] to play. The two proceeded to sneak into the tower, as Doza was not allowed to have guests, but eventually made it to her room. The two played one of the versions of the game until the [[security droid]] [[4D-M1N]] approached, causing them to abort the game so Xiono could hide.<ref name="Secrets and Holograms" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:FlightSimulatorSquadron-ConceptArt.png|thumb|right|275px|''Flight Simulator Squadron'' concept art created by Colas Guathier.]]
''Flight Simulator Squadron'' first appeared in "[[Secrets and Holograms]],"<ref name="Secrets and Holograms" /> the tenth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which was released on [[December 2]], [[2018]].<ref name="Air date">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> The game was originally pictured on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[September 14]] of the same year<ref name="YouTube">{{SWYouTube|MDtY2vVlfaQ|The Aces &#124; Star Wars Resistance|sw_url=video/disneyxd-meet-the-aces-star-wars-resistance}}</ref> before being identified in the episode.<ref name="Secrets and Holograms" /> ''Flight Simulator Squadron'' was created as a cross between the real-world [[Wikipedia:Video game|video games]] ''[[Wikipedia:Asteroids|Asteroids]]'' and ''[[Wikipedia:Dance Dance Revolution|Dance Dance Revolution]]''. In early drafts of the episode, the game was not a flight simulator and instead involved players navigating "an [[Ewok]] through [[laser]]s fired by [[stormtrooper]]s."<ref name="Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-secrets-and-holograms-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 9 Fun Facts from "Secrets and Holograms" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref> Concept art of ''Flight Simulator Squadron'' was done by [[Lucasfilm Animation]] artist [[Colas Gauthier]]. Multiple assets were created as part of the design, with one work by Guathier being dated to [[May 12]], [[2017]].<ref name="Concept">{{SW|url=star-wars-resistance-secrets-and-holograms-concept-art-gallery|text="Secrets and Holograms" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref>
