The '''Advanced Science Division''' was a clandestine research and science [[division]] of the [[Imperial Science Bureau]] that was headquartered inside a highly classified [[Tantiss Base|complex]] within [[Mount Tantiss]] on the [[planet]] [[Wayland]].<ref name="Tipping Point" /> One of the Advanced Science Division's projects was [[Project Necromancer]], a secret [[Cloning|clone]] research project considered to be of great importance to the longevity of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" />

==Description==
===Organization===
The Advanced Science Division was part<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" /> of the [[Imperial Science Bureau]]<ref name="Endless">''[[Endless Vigil]]''</ref> under the [[scientific minister]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" /> The division was led by a [[Chief Scientist]]<ref name="Tipping Point" /> and maintained top secret research facilities in various [[planet]]s.<ref name="Unknown">{{TBB|Paths Unknown}}</ref> Projects and operations within the Advanced Science Division were overseen by other [[Chief Scientist|chief scientist]]s<ref name="Identity Crisis">{{TBB|Identity Crisis}}</ref> and a [[Head Medical Officer]].<ref name="Scalder DB">{{Databank|doctor-scalder|Doctor Scalder}}</ref> [[Clone Commander]] [[Scorch]], a [[commando]], oversaw the division's military forces.<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" />

===Research divisions===
{{Main|Imperial cloning program}}
[[File:ProjectNecromancer.png|thumb|270x270px|Project Necromancer was the most critical area of the Division's research]]
Central to the Advanced Science Division's efforts was the conduction of advanced [[Biologist|biological]] research. This included the production of exotic substances to be used as new [[fuel]] sources for Imperial machines,<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" /> the creation of [[bioweapon]]s,<ref name="Unknown" /> the conditioning of clone troopers into assassins, and the introduction of [[The Force|Force]]-inhibiting factors such as the rare [[spice]] [[karrak]].<ref name="Endless" />

Another area of major research<ref name="Metamorphosis">{{TBB|Metamorphosis (episode)}}</ref> which was kept secret even among Science Corps staff<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" /> was the development of the [[Imperial cloning program]]<ref name="Metamorphosis" /> under the command of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" /> [[gender|himself]].<ref name="DBPalpatine">{{Databank|emperor-palpatine-darth-sidious|Emperor Palpatine/Darth Sidious}}</ref> This included the creation of [[Zillo Beast]] clones that were grown from larvae to full-size adults in a matter of [[standard day|days]] using proprietary [[Kaminoan]] technology as well as [[Nala Se]]'s personal research<ref name="Metamorphosis" /> and [[Hutt clone trooper]]s used as guards for [[Tarkin Initiative]] facilities.<ref name="Aphra 18">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 18|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 18]]</ref> The program's ultimate goal was to produce a [[Force-sensitive]] [[cloning|clone]] of Palpatine as a successor and fail-safe in case he was [[death|killed]], and was developed under [[Project Necromancer]] in the [[Tantiss vault]].<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" />

===Military divisions===
{{Quote|There is a division of clones trained as specialized operatives and initiated into a secret deep cover program run by Hemlock. Their identities are erased. They undergo conditioning. The few that make it through come out different.|Crosshair|<ref name="Infiltration" />}}
[[File:CloneX-TCC.png|left|thumb|320x320px|A clone assassin of Hemlock's deep cover program]]
The Advanced Science Division experimented on a number of [[clone trooper]]s decommissioned from the [[Imperial Military]] and imprisoned in its facilities some time after the end of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Confined">{{TBB|Confined}}</ref> A number of [[clone commando]]s formed the [[Imperial royal guard]] and were tasked with protecting key personnel<ref name="Tipping Point" /> and areas of the division such as the Tantiss Vault.<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" /> 

A few clones were trained under a [[Royce Hemlock's deep undercover program|deep undercover program]], which turned them into a [[Clone X trooper division|unit]] of [[Clone X trooper]]s, who performed [[assassin]]ations and other covert operations for the Empire.<ref name="Infiltration">{{TBB|Infiltration (episode)}}</ref> Security for Tantiss Base was provided by two army divisions, with these divisions initially being composed of additional clone commandos supplementing the Imperial Royal Guard, but after [[Project War-Mantle]] went into full swing, these commandos were in turn supplemented by non-clone TK stormtroopers.<ref name="TCHA">{{TBB|The Cavalry Has Arrived}}</ref>

==History==
===Disaster on Setron===
The Advanced Science Division was formed shortly after the formation of the Galactic Empire in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of [[The Bad Batch Season 1|''The Bad Batch'' Season 1]] in [[19 BBY]].</ref> During this time, the position of Chief Scientist was held by [[Royce Hemlock]],<ref name="Tipping Point">{{TBB|Tipping Point}}</ref> and the Division was headquartered in an [[Imperial medical laboratory]] on [[Setron]]. There, Hemlock began experimenting on clone troopers assigned to the facility, including [[clone cadet]]s, by collecting [[blood]] samples for them in search of an individual with a sufficiently high [[midi-chlorian]] count to be used as a test subject. In addition to Hemlock's research scientists of the facility also created [[slither vine]]s, an experimental bioweapon which broke its containment and overran the facility, forcing the staff to evacuate and perform a [[Base Delta Zero]] [[orbital bombardment]] to destroy it.<ref name="Unknown" />

===Relocation and clone experiments===
[[File:Blood_collection_room_weyland.png|thumb|240x240px|Nala Se performed experiments on the clones of Tantiss Base]]
Soon after the disaster on Setron, the Division moved its headquarters to [[Tantiss Base]] on the planet [[Wayland]]. Following the Empire's [[attack on Kamino]] which destroyed the [[Kaminoan]] facilities, essential personnel including [[Chief Medical Scientist]] Nala Se were moved to Tantiss by Imperial royal guards and forced to work on developing the cloning program under Hemlock.<ref name="Lost">{{TBB|Kamino Lost}}</ref> Nala Se's personal advancements were essential in the development of the Zillo Beast clones,<ref name="Metamorphosis" /> and at some point, she was further tasked with performing blood tests on clones held in the base<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" /> to continue the Empire's search for midi-chlorian-rich blood donors.<ref name="Harbinger">{{TBB|The Harbinger}}</ref>

While Nala Se and fellow scientist [[Emerie Karr]] searched for an m-count among the clones,<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" /> Hemlock assigned select class one [[bounty hunter]]s<ref name="Bad">{{TBB|Bad Territory}}</ref> with capturing Force-sensitive subjects from around [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Harbinger" /> These were delivered to Tantiss base and kept in [[Prison|stasis]] inside the Vault to be preserved for Hemlock's experiments. Other research performed by the Division at this time included the development of exotic fuels<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" /> and brainwashing clones for the clone assassin program.<ref name="Infiltration" />

===Hunting Omega===
{{Main|Hunt for Omega}}
[[File:Scaleback-Hemlock-Plan99.png|left|thumb|270x270px|Imperial royal guards trapping Clone Force 99 after Ciddarin's tip]]
Despite receiving both encouragement and threats from Hemlock, Nala Se had many misgivings for her work under the Division and frequently refused to cooperate, stalling the projects' progress. Hemlock consulted the Kaminoan [[Prime Minister]] [[Lama Su]] for a way to force her to work, learning about the scientist's close connection with a unaltered clone named [[Omega]], who used to be Se's research assistant in Kamino.<ref name="Metamorphosis" /> Omega was freed by the [[Clone Force 99]] soon after the [[Proclamation of the New Order]] when they went rogue, and their whereabouts were unknown.<ref name="Run">{{TBB|Cut and Run}}</ref> Hemlock tasked ex Force 99 [[sniper]] [[Crosshair]] with tracking the group down, but when he eventually located them on Kamino he allowed them to walk away.<ref name="Lost" /> Hemlock placed Crosshair back to the clone test subject pool and began looking for possible witnesses around the galaxy, eventually coming in contact with [[information broker]] [[Ciddarin Scaleback]] who was hosting the Force in her [[Cid's Parlor|parlor]] on [[Ord Mantell City]]. [[Clone Commander]] [[Scorch]] traveled to [[Ord Mantell]] with a clone commando detachment and captured Omega alive, bringing her to Tantiss and successfully using her as leverage to force Se to continue her experiments.<ref name="Plan 99">{{TBB|Plan 99 (episode)}}</ref>

During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="303 Date">{{TBBDate|303}}</ref> when Emperor Palpatine visited Tantiss to tour the facility and monitor the Division's progress, Emerie Karr accidentally discovered that despite seemingly cooperating, Nala Se had been disposing of Omega's blood samples instead of testing them. Karr's test yielded a positive result for a high midi-chlorian count, but when attempting to locate the clone Hemlock realized that she was in the process of escaping alongside Crosshair. Hemlock ordered the V-wings pursuing them to stand down, as Omega had become too valuable to kill,<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss" /> and notified Imperial forces around the galaxy to hunt her down. Hemlock and Scorch personally oversaw the attempts to locate her, including on the planets [[Lau]]<ref name="Different">{{TBB|A Different Approach}}</ref> and [[Teth]], but were repeatedly unsuccessful.<ref name="Extraction">{{TBB|Extraction}}</ref>
