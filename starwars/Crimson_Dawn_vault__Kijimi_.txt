The '''Crimson Dawn vault''' was a room in the [[Crimson Dawn District (Kijimi)|Crimson Dawn district]] of [[Kijimi City]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]], where the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal [[syndicate]] kept valuables<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The vault could be opened by using three [[keycard]]s assigned to members of the Dawn which included [[Kuroma]], [[Dart Subterra]], and [[EE'zok]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==History==
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" />  the vault contained [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], a [[Target Audit]] [[Shift Token]], and the materials necessary to apply a "Crimson Reign" paint scheme to [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
The [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] gained access to the room outside the vault<ref name="Outlaws" />  in 3 ABY,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> at which time she accessed the data terminal and learned the location of three keycards required to unlock the vault. After recovering the keycards, Vess returned to unlock the vault and stole the credits, the shift token and the paint materials.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

In 35 ABY, the vault was destroyed along with the planet during the [[Destruction of Kijimi]] by the [[Sith Eternal]] with the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Derriphan]]'' of the [[Final Order]] [[fleet]].<ref name="Destruction" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Crimson Dawn vault appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The vault was one of several in the game that the player could access after finding three corresponding keycards.<ref name="Outlaws" />
