'''Fire paste''' was a versatile chemical putty that could be used to ignite and sustain emergency fires on nearly any flammable object. Manufactured by the [[Corellian Chemical Corporation]], the paste was packaged with an igniter in small cubes, similar to tiny plastic explosive charges. After the cube was spread across a [[fuel]] source, it could be ignited by pulling a tab on the igniter. Even without a fuel source, the paste was capable of burning in any [[atmosphere]] with the intensity of a small campfire for roughly an [[Standard hour|hour]], although the paste did not function in a [[vacuum]] or [[Water|underwater]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the paste cost five hundred [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and was simple to find for purchase.<ref name="G&G" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], fire paste was mentioned in the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="G&G" /> The [[Fire paste/Legends|paste]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[The Unknown Regions]]'', a [[2010]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' supplement published by [[Wizards of the Coast]].<ref name="TUR">''[[The Unknown Regions]]''</ref>
