A '''brooch''',<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> or '''broach''' was a piece of jewellery.<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Halcyon Legacy 5|''Halcyon Legacy'' 5]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> a [[human]] passenger of the [[Star Cruiser|star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' had a broach. After the [[Crimson Jack's crew|crew]] of the [[pirate]] [[Crimson Jack]] [[Attack on the Halcyon|took over]] the ''Halcyon'' in an attempt to claim a [[bounty]] on a [[Resistance]] [[Shorr Komrrin|spy]], one of his pirates took the broach from the human. The human protested, and informed the pirate it was a family heirloom, to which the pirate responded that it still would be a family heirloom, just in their family.<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5" />
