'''Derosha Six''' was a [[space station]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Security Bureau]]. It was situated in the [[Derosha system]], [[orbit]]ing a [[Planetary ring|ringed]] [[Unidentified planet (Derosha system)|planet]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> ISB [[Director]] [[Sliro Barsha]], leader of [[Zerek Besh]], met with Imperial enforcer [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on the station. During the meeting, the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] and the [[slicer]] [[Riko Vess]] infiltrated Derosha Six aboard a stolen [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class shuttle]] to steal Barsha's security codes for an imminent heist on his [[Canto Bight]] [[Westhill Palace|mansion]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
