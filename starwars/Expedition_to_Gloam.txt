An '''expedition to Gloam''' was undertaken by [[Republic Pathfinder]] [[Outer Rim Expedition Two-Five-Two]]<ref name="QFTHC" /> in [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> The team had been sent to [[Gloam]]'s [[Double planet|twin planet]], [[Aubadas]], in response to a request by the [[planet]]'s native [[Katikoot]] for assistance in finding a sustainable energy source, as [[diplomat]]ic teams were momentarily unavailable due to the ongoing [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]]. As the Katikoot were on the verge of an energy crisis, [[Jedi Master]] [[Rok Buran]], the [[Outer Rim Exploration Team|Pathfinder team's]] leader, decided to investigate Gloam's [[Gloam mines|mines]] to see if they could be temporarily restarted to tide the Katikoot over before a [[Galactic Republic]] [[engineer]]ing team could arrive. The mines had been abandoned due to rumors of legendary [[Gloam monster|monsters]] which inhabited Gloam, and Buran wanted to see whether or not the monsters were real. A Katikoot guide, [[Rillik]], accompanied the team. However, Rillik, sick with the [[miner's curse]] and driven by a desire for wealth, betrayed the Pathfinders, having released the monsters in the first place. He tricked them into entering an [[ancient city]] where the monsters roamed loose, before backtracking, destroying the Pathfinders' [[starship]] [[Foundling (starship)|''Foundling'']], and returning to Aubadas in a prepared [[Mining ship/Canon|mining ship]] with a tale of an attack. Buran, initially left for [[Death|dead]], was the only member of his team to survive.<ref name="QFTHC" />
