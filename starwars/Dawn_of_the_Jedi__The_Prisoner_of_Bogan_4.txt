'''''Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan'' 4''' is the fourth issue in the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan|''Prisoner of Bogan'']] story arc of the ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series and the ninth issue of the series in total. It is set in the earliest days of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]], in a [[Pre-Republic era|time before]] the polarization of the [[Light side of the Force/Legends|light]] and [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark sides]] of [[The Force/Legends|the Force]]. This issue was released on [[March 27]] [[2013]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Even as the Je'daii close in on the mysterious Xesh and his mad ally Daegen Lok, a third party has entered the chase—a powerful woman from Xesh's past.''

''Meanwhile, Lok has sought the help of old enemies to help him attain a common goal—the downfall of the Je'daii!''
