'''EB-3''' was an [[SP-4 analysis droid|SP-4 unit]] in [[Taborr's gang]].<ref name="YJA" />

==Biography==
===Raid on Hap's Sap Tap===
EB-3 was a member of a [[pirate]] gang led by [[Cyrus Vuundir|Taborr Val Dorn]]. Alongside Val Dorn and the [[Gamorrean]] [[Pord]], the droid participated in several raids on [[Hap's Sap Tap]], a [[Cafe|café]] on [[Tenoo]]. During [[Raid on Hap's Sap Tap|one]] of these raids, the pirates were interrupted by [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]] and [[Nubs]], who told them to return their loot. Val Dorn ordered EB-3 and Pord to take the cart with their stolen goods to their [[Iron Talon|starship]], but they were quickly overpowered by the Jedi. Val Dorn managed to distract the Jedi, allowing the pirates to escape, but the Jedi managed to retrieve the loot with the help of [[Nash Durango]] and several other locals, forcing the gang to leave empty-handed.<ref name="The Young Jedi">{{YJA|The Young Jedi}}</ref>
