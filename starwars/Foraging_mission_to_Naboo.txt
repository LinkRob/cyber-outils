{{Quote|Master Ansho told me his first mission as a Jedi Knight was to take a group of Padawans foraging on Naboo, and even then, there was a master along, just in case.|Tualon Yaluna, to Iskat Akaris|<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />}}
A '''foraging mission''' to<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Naboo]] was conducted by a group of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s led by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Bavoc Ansho]] and accompanied by a [[Unidentified Jedi Master (Naboo)|Jedi Master]]. This mission took place<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> by [[22 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> and served as Ansho's first as a Jed Knight; he recounted it to his Padawan [[Tualon Yaluna]] at some point afterward.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> In 22 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the latter briefly mentioned the story to his friend [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] during their first [[Mission to Thule|mission]] as Jedi Knights, contrasting the simplicity of Ansho's mission with their own being in wartime. Yaluna also noted how Ansho even had a [[Jedi Master]] along for support if necessary, a lack he and Akaris both felt in their own situation.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />

==Behind the Scenes==
The foraging mission to Naboo was mentioned in [[Delilah S. Dawson]]'s [[2023]] novel [[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade|''Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade'']].<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />
