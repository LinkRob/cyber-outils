'''Bumblethunk''' was an [[Esoomian]] brawler designated as '''Prowling Thug 36''' in the criminal [[Razzi Syndicate]] under [[crime lord]] [[Latts Razzi]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The entry mentioning Bumblethunk in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' takes place chronologically prior to an entry written by [[Tobias Beckett]], who ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' establishes [[Death|died]] in [[10 BBY]].</ref> Bumblethunk had encountered the [[Gambling|gambler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], who considered the Esoomian a goon and took a swing at the brawler in order to impress a [[Trianii]] [[Unidentified Trianii debutante|debutante]]. Bumblethunk beat up Calrissian, who then had the [[book]] known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' lifted from his person by the Razzi Syndicate [[thief]] "[[Sticky Fingers]]."<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

The book ended up in the possession of Razzi herself, who was informed of Bumblethunk's part in its acquisition by her [[submister]] [[Twirm]]. The book later fell back into the hands of Calrissian, who annotated his previous entries with a note stating that he regretted attacking Bumblethunk and losing the book.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bumblethunk was mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
