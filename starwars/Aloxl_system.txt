The '''Aloxl system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Deep Core]]. It was located in grid square L-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the [[planet]] [[Aloxl]].<ref name="Origin" />
