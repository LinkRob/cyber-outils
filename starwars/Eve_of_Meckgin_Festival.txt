The '''Eve of Meckgin Festival''' was a celebration on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]] that occurred directly before the holiday of [[Meckgin]], which celebrated [[Geonosian]] virtues of industry. During the [[Clone Wars]], the Geonosian [[Archduke]] [[Poggle the Lesser]] returned to Geonosis following his [[Prison|imprisonment]], arriving on the Eve of Meckgin.

==Description==
{{Quote|Let blood be spilled and drones prove themselves worthy of escalation.|translated=1|Poggle announces that the gladitorial games will continue for another three days after the Eve of Meckgin|<ref name="Catalyst" />}}
[[File:Geonosis_arena.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Eve of Meckgin Festival took place in the Petranaki Arena.]]
The Eve of Meckgin Festival was a [[Geonosian]] celebration that occurred the [[Local day|day]] before [[Meckgin]], a holiday that celebrated Geonosian virtues of industry. The holiday occurred during the planet's [[Water|rainy]] [[season]]. Prior to the day of the festival, there were three days of [[gladiator]]ial games held in the [[Petranaki Arena]], where thousands of Geonosian drones were [[Death|killed]]. The festival itself also involved combat inside the arena. Geonosian aristocrats spent fortunes to sit in the arena and be splattered by the gore from the gladiatorial affairs, while a hundred thousand other attendees filled the rest of the stadium.<ref name="Catalyst" />

==History==
{{Quote|How blessed I am by the Creator in being able to join you on an occasion of such spectacle and bravery.|translated=1|Poggle the Lesser addresses the Geonosian people on the Eve of Meckgin|<ref name="Catalyst" />}}
[[File:Poggle_Geo.jpg|thumb|left|170px|During the Clone Wars, the festival was marked by Poggle's return to Geonosis.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the [[Galactic Republic]], [[Archduke]] [[Poggle the Lesser]] returned to Geonosis following his release from Republic [[Prison|custody]], on the account that the Geonosians assist with a Republic [[Project Stardust|project]]. Poggle returned to his [[homeworld]] on the Eve of Meckgin, arriving in time for the festivities. Poggle spoke in front of the Geonosian masses in the Petranaki Arena. Several Republic emissaries, namely [[Orson Callan Krennic]], [[Sate Pestage]], [[Janus Greejatus]], [[Ars Dangor]], and [[Sly Moore]], were also on hand to witness the celebrations. In front of the massive amount of Geonosians, Poggle announced that they would be working alongside the Galactic Republic on assembling a [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|moon-sized battle station]].<ref name="Catalyst" />
