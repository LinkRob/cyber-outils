'''B-3PO''' was a [[3PO-series protocol droid|3PO]] aboard a [[starship]] that had been attacked by [[pirate]]s, who [[Death|killed]] all [[Sentience|sentient]] beings, except for [[Pax Maripher]], who had managed to hide [[Gender|himself]]. Two of the [[protocol droid]]s aboard the ship, [[Z-3PO]] and [[G-3PO]], discovered Maripher, prompting B-3PO to investigate. The [[droid]]s raised Maripher, who was eventually rescued after fifteen [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="MA" />
