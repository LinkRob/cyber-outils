"'''Destroy the Death Star!'''" is a short story initially published in ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]''. It adapts the film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="5-Minute Star Wars Stories" />

The story shares illustrations with the [[2016]] book [[Death Star Battle (book)|''Death Star Battle'']],<ref name="DSB">[[Death Star Battle (book)|''Death Star Battle'']]</ref> with different text written by [[Trey King]].<ref name="5-Minute Star Wars Stories" />
