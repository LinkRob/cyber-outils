'''BD-15959''' was an [[OOM-series security droid]]<ref name="Note" /> in the [[Separatist Droid Army]] stationed on a [[Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[droid]] was destroyed by [[Anakin Skywalker]] when [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] escaped from his [[prison]] cell.<ref name="The Big Bang" />
