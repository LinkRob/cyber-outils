The '''Alisandre Hotel''' was a [[hotel]] located in the [[Federal District]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Thrawn" /> In business since at least the [[High Republic Era]],<ref name="ITD">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref> it continued operating into the [[Imperial Era]]. The hotel's ballroom was thought to be one of the biggest in the Federal District. It also had a concentration of smaller rooms surrounding it where, during large gatherings, people would meet more privately. During the early [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], an [[Ascension Week]] celebration took place in the hotel.<ref name="Thrawn" />
