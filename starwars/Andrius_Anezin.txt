'''Andrius Anezin''' is a professional illustrator who was commissioned to provide artwork for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' miniatures game [[Star Wars: Armada|''Star Wars'': Armada]]. He illustrated the card "''[[Hand of Justice]]''" in the ''[[Imperial Light Cruiser Expansion Pack]]''.<ref name="ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=anezin|url=projects/YvAob|name=Andrius Anezin|text=Star Wars: Armada|archivedate=20200215215221}}</ref><ref name="ILC">''[[Imperial Light Cruiser Expansion Pack]]''</ref>{{Mediacat|imagecat=images by Andrius Anezin}}
