The '''Director of COMPNOR''' was the title of the leader of the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]], a position held by [[Chairman]] [[Armand Isard]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. As director, Isard sat on COMPNOR's [[Select Committee]].<ref name="Rise" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] and [[Resistance]] paramilitary member [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned the title in several instances in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />
