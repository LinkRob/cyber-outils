'''Blue Squadron''' was a [[Squadron|unit]] of ten [[clone trooper pilot]]s who served in the [[Republic Navy]] and [[Republic Starfighter Corps]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. At the [[Battle of Ryloth]], they flew [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s under the command of [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. As Tano led the squadron into battle against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Ryloth blockade|blockade]], four more [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class frigates]] jumped out of [[hyperspace]] and joined the blockade. Although she received orders to abandon the assault, Tano recklessly pressed forward. Her disobedience cost her squadron, as only two of her [[pilot]]s survived the battle.<ref name="Storm Over Ryloth" /> At least one pilot from the squadron saw action during the [[Battle of Coruscant]], during which he faced several [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droid starfighters]].<ref name="GotR">{{FFGXW2|set=Guardians of the Republic Squadron Pack|cardname=Blue Squadron Protector}}</ref>
