The '''Chandrilan temple''' was a [[Jedi temple]] of the [[Jedi Order]] that was located on<ref name="Rogue Element" /> the Core Worlds [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide to Batuu">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Chandrila]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Rogue Element" /> By [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Chandrilan temple date">"[[Rogue Element]]" establishes that [[Conithr]] became the [[Jedi Master]] of [[Ruu]] on [[Corellia]] prior to the [[Jedi Order]]'s recall to [[Coruscant]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]]. Therefore, she must have been stationed at the [[Chandrila]]n [[Jedi temple|temple]] as a [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Conithr]] was stationed at the Chandrilan temple and often snuck out of the temple at [[Local day|night]] to buy [[jaquira fruit]] from a [[Unidentified stall|stall]] located near the temple.<ref name="Rogue Element" />

At some point before her [[death]]<ref name="Rogue Element" /> between 230 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Rogue Element]]" take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]]. The story establishes Conithr [[Death|died]] between the destruction of [[Starlight Beacon]] and the story's events, therefore meaning she passed away between those two [[Standard year|years]].</ref> Conithr&mdash;by then a [[Jedi Master]] at the [[Coronet City temple|Jedi temple]] on the [[Core Worlds]] planet Corellia&mdash;tried to get her [[Padawan]], the [[Lucem]] [[Gender|boy]] [[Ruu]], to [[Diet|eat]] sour [[Foodstuff|foods]] including a jaquira [[fruit]]. When Ruu groaned, Conithr told him about how as a [[youngling]] she had snuck out of the temple to buy the fruit.<ref name="Rogue Element" /> In 229 BBY,<ref name="Date" /> sometime after Conithr's passing, Ruu recalled the conversation where she had mentioned the Chandrilan temple after seeing the [[Chadra-Fan]] female [[Hamatan]] roll out a blob of jaquira-fruit [[sugar]] to make [[Twist-candy|candy]].<ref name="Rogue Element" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chandrilan temple was mentioned in flashbacks in "[[Rogue Element]]," a short story written by [[Alyssa Wong]]<ref name="Rogue Element" /> that was published in the Barnes & Noble exclusive edition<ref name="Celebration Day 2">{{SWYouTube|Qi-b2c0RY7I|Star Wars Celebration LIVE! 2023 &ndash; DAY 2|nolive=1}}</ref> of the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Rogue Element" />
