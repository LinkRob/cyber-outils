'''Armored computer cores''' were specialized [[memory bank]]s designed to protect information from [[starship]] crashes. They used [[laminanium]]-plating, redundant backups, and sometimes ejection fail-safes to protect the information stored within them. They were practically indistinguishable from debris, and were used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] to protect [[astrogation]] data, ship activity records, and mission logs. They typically cost 1,100 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and notable models included the [[Astralor Corp.]] [["Black Hole Box" T-11 Armored Databank]] and the [[Loronar Corporation]] [["Sole Survivor" Backup Computer Core]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
