The '''cargo skiff''' was a common model of [[skiff]] that was manufactured by [[Ubrikkian Industries]]. The [[Hauler air skiff]] was a version of the skiff that was designed for higher altitudes.<ref name="Unlimited Power" />

==Behind the scenes==
The cargo skiff was mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' supplement ''[[Unlimited Power]]'',<ref name="Unlimited Power" /> released on [[May 31]], [[2018]].<ref name="Unleash Your Power">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/5/31/unleash-your-power|text=Unleash Your Power}}</ref>
