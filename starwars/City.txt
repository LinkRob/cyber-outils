[[File:LessuEmpire-DD.png|thumb|right|275px|The city of Lessu was the planetary capital of the Twi'lek homeworld of Ryloth.]]
A '''city''' was a large and permanent settlement inhabited by [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Species|beings]]. A very large city was known as a '''megalopolis''',<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> while a [[planet]]-covering city was known as an '''[[ecumenopolis]]'''.<ref name="HttJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref>

==Description==
{{Quote|Coruscant&hellip; the entire planet is one big city.|[[Ric Olié]]|<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>}}
[[File:Coruscant_landscape_RotS.png|thumb|left|250px|Galactic City was built over the entire planet of Coruscant.]]
In [[the galaxy]], some cities could cover an entire [[planet]]'s surface, the most well known example being [[Coruscant]]'s [[Imperial City|Galactic City]].<ref name="TPM" /> Other cities such as [[Mos Eisley]] on [[Tatooine]] could be simply large settlements with little to no infrastructure.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Similarly, the [[Wookiee]] [[homeworld]] of [[Kashyyyk]] boasted [[tree]]-cities, communities of artificial structures surrounding and built in harmony with multiple [[wroshyr tree]]s.<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> While most cities were built on a planet's surface, some cities were "floating" above the planet, such as [[Cloud City]] on [[Bespin]],<ref name="Cloud City">{{Databank|cloud-city|Cloud City}}</ref><ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> or on [[water]] such as [[Tipoca City]] on the aquatic world of [[Kamino]].<ref name="AotC">{{Film|II}}</ref> Cities could also be built below ground, such as on [[Utapau]], where the cities were housed within large sinkholes that covered the planet's surface.<ref name="RotS">{{Film|III}}</ref> During construction, builders would also sometimes make efforts to blend the city with its surrounding natural habitat. A prime example was the city of [[Theed]] on [[Naboo]], a planet which was known for its beautiful river cities of [[Naboo classical architecture|classical architecture]] and greenery.<ref name="Naboo">{{Databank|naboo|Naboo}}</ref>

Large cities could hold planetary [[spaceport]]s, company headquarters, offices, and all services.<ref name="Coruscant">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref> To offer these services, cities were sometimes divided into different districts,<ref name="DexDiner">{{Databank|dexs-diner|Dex's Diner}}</ref> public buildings, residential and industrial.<ref name="Coruscant" />

[[Capital]] cities were the [[Politics|political]] center of a planet, sometimes also the cultural center, and they held different buildings for those purposes.<ref name="Coruscant" /> [[Lessu City|Lessu]] was the capital city of [[Ryloth]], the homeworld of the [[Twi'lek]] [[species]].<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref> As the cultural center of the galaxy and the capital of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Coruscant" /> Coruscant was filled with government and business buildings, such as the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]],<ref name="AotC" /> as well as homes, theaters,<ref name="RotS" /> clubs, and other entertainment centers for the enjoyment of the city's population.<ref name="AotC" />
