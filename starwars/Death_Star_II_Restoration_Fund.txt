The '''Death Star II Restoration Fund''' was a foundation formed during or after [[4 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> to restore the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]]. The fund asked people to contribute, so they would be able to revive the [[superweapon]], and it could also be utilized as a conference center, events space, and community hub. ''[[Raise the Death Star II!]]'', a poster made for the fund, was collected by [[Poe Dameron]] in a [[Unidentified book|vehicle guidebook]].<ref name="Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Death Star II Restoration Fund was mentioned in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''.<ref name="Vehicles" />
