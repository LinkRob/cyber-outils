The term '''battlecruiser''' or '''battle cruiser''' designated any [[warship]]s that were around 2,000-5,000 meters in length in the [[Anaxes War College System]].<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> Known classes of battlecruiser were the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]],<ref name="TFA Secret Inside">{{SW|url=news/the-force-awakens-secret-inside-the-secret-academy|text=The ''Force Awakens'' Secret Inside ''The Secret Academy''}}</ref> the [[Bulwark-class battle cruiser|''Bulwark''-class battle cruiser]],<ref name="RotS">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> the [[Praetor-class Star Battlecruiser|''Praetor''-class Star Battlecruiser]],<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> and the [[Kilji war cruiser]] or Kilji battlecruiser.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>
