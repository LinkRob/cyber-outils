'''Courier droids''', also called '''messenger droids''', were [[droid]]s used to physically transport messages between locations. Several distinct models of messenger droids saw manufacturing, including the [[ASN courier droid]], and the [[KUP24 messenger droid|KUP24 messenger]]. [[R4 courier droid|Modified]] [[R4 astromech droid|R4-series astromech droids]] saw use as courier droids by the [[Galactic Empire]].

==Models==
[[File:GOUD-4.png|thumb|left|108x108px|[[GOUD-4]], a messenger droid enslaved on Kessel]]
Several distinct models of messenger [[droid]]s existed in [[the galaxy]], all with the purpose of transporting information. The [[ASN courier droid]] was a small [[Repulsorlift|flying]] courier droid<ref name="SWTVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> 0.3 [[meter]]s in height,<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> which contained a small tube in which a message could be placed for delivery. However, the droid could also be modified to operate as an [[assassin]], dispensing deadly items from its message canister.<ref name="AOTC" />

The [[KUP24 messenger droid|KUP24 messenger]] was a small, flattened model that maneuvered close to the ground which saw use under [[Slavery|enslavement]] by the [[Pyke Syndicate]] in the [[Spice mines of Kessel|Spice Mines]] of [[Kessel]].<ref name="Every Droid">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Droid in Star Wars|video=UQg6aX7lqTA|nolive=1}}</ref>

[[MSE-6 series repair droid]]s, commonly known as mouse droids, were small, wheeled, black-plate general multipurpose labor droids that, among other functions, delivered messages across military instillations.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>

[[File:R4_astromech.png|thumb|right|234x234px|An Imperial R4 courier droid]]
The [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Press Corps]] employed modified [[R4 astromech droid|R4-series astromech droids]] as [[R4 courier droid|courier droids]] with the purpose of carrying sensitive information between distanced locations while avoiding networks.<ref name="Courier Droid">{{Databank|courier-droid|Courier Droid}}</ref> R4 courier droids were specifically programmed by the Empire to be devoid of personality,<ref name="Battle to the End">''[[Battle to the End]]''</ref> and sported specialized markings denoting their courier status.<ref name="Courier Droid" />
