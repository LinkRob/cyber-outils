'''Burantia''',<ref name="JK7">[[Jedi Knights 7|''Jedi Knights'' 7]]</ref> also known as '''Planet WG4587''', was a terrestrial [[planet]] with [[mountain]]ous [[forest]]s where [[Homeworld|indiginous]] [[kaijura]] lived. It remained uncharted until the [[CR70 corvette]] of [[Galactic Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] crashed there<ref name="JK3" /> between [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knights]]'' take place during the time [[Finis Valorum]] is [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. As ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'' establishes that the Supreme Chancellor served four-[[Standard year|year]] terms, and the flashback section of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] which mentions of Valorum's re-election is dated to [[36 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', Valorum must have served as Chancellor beginning in [[40 BBY]]. ''Timelines'' also dates the [[Supreme Chancellor election, 32 BBY|end]] of Valorum's time as Chancellor to [[32 BBY]], therefore meaning ''Jedi Knights'' must take place between those two years.</ref> The [[Jedi]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] were [[Mission to Planet WG4587|dispatched]] in a [[T-6 shuttle]] to the world to find Organa and his staff, but their [[Starship|ship]] was destroyed by a kaijura before they could reach the [[corvette]].<ref name="JK3" />

The Jedi then escaped the kaijura and found the corvette and its crew, only to learn a pack of kaijura was rapidly approaching. Using [[the Force]], Jinn helped the damaged corvette take off and had its [[captain]],<ref name="JK3" /> [[Jeremoch Colton]],<ref name="DM">{{DMCite|dm=File:JeremochColton-MarcGuggenheim-Twitter.png|creator=[[Marc Guggenheim]]|date=2025-05-14}}</ref> launch the vessel's [[escape pod]]s into a kaijura that grabbed them in mid-air. Once the creature let go of the corvette, it was able to depart from Planet WG4587 and return safely home. Kenobi later wrote an after-action report on the mission.<ref name="JK3" />
