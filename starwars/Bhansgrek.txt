'''Bhansgrek''' was a [[bush]]y [[plant]] found on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] that produced a fragile [[flower]] once every hundred [[Standard year|years]]. One such plant was kept onboard [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[J-type diplomatic barge]] in a [[stasis-field horticapsule]] that prolonged its [[time]] of blooming.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" />
