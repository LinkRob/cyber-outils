'''Felicity Rose Hadley Jones''' (born [[October 17]], [[1983]])<ref name="This Week Posters" /> is an actress. She portrayed [[Jyn Erso]] in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', a ''[[Star Wars]]'' standalone film released on [[December 16]], [[2016]]. She was nominated for an Academy Award for her role as [[Wikipedia:Jane Wilde Hawking|Jane Wilde Hawking]] in [[Wikipedia:The Theory of Everything (2014 film)|''The Theory of Everything'']].<ref name="Announcement" />

In the "Jyn: The Rebel" featurette in the Blu-ray for ''Rogue One'', Felicity said that she often used [[Wookieepedia]] to conduct research for her role.<ref name="FANDOM article">{{WebCite|url=http://fandom.wikia.com/articles/felicity-jones-wookieepedia-rogue-one|text=Felicity Jones Used Wookieepedia to Prep for 'Star Wars' Role|author=Brandon Rhea|archivedate=20170331013757|suppress=1|nolive=1}}</ref>
