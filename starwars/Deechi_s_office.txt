[[Senator]] [[Mee Deechi]] utilized an '''office''' which was located in the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]] on [[Coruscant]] during his time in the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Galactic Senate|Senate]].

==History==
After the [[Funeral of Onaconda Farr|funeral]] of [[Onaconda Farr]], [[Mee Deechi]] and [[Halle Burtoni]] were drinking in Deechi's office when [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] asked them for information about Farr's [[death]]. Later on, Amidala, Organa, and [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tan Divo]] discovered Deechi's body in his office where he had been stabbed by [[Lolo Purs]].<ref name="Senate Murders" />

==Behind the scenes==
Deechi's office first appeared, albeit unidentified, in "[[Senate Murders]]," the fifteenth episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]].<ref name="Senate Murders" /> It was first identified as Deechi's office in the episode's accompanying [[The Clone Wars Episode Guides|guide]] on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/senate-murders-episode-guide|text="Senate Murders" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>
