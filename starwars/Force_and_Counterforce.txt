"'''Force and Counterforce'''" is the thirteenth and final episode of the [[Star Wars (radio)|''Star Wars'' radio drama]]. It first aired on [[National Public Radio]] on Monday, [[May 25]], [[1981]].<ref name="Highlights" /> The episode covers the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], the climax both of the audio drama and the film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].

==Plot summary==
A voice aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]] announces that the Rebel base will be in firing range in thirty minutes, and [[Conan Antonio Motti/Legends|Motti]] tells [[Wilhuff Tarkin/Legends|Governor Tarkin]] and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] that the [[Superlaser/Legends|prime weapon]] is prepared and all personnel ready for battle. Vader goes to inspect other parts of the station, leaving Tarkin and Motti alone. Motti again flatters Tarkin, emphasizing the power that he commands, now at least equal to [[Palpatine/Legends|the Emperor]] himself. He believes that Tarkin can now demand a position of authority in the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]]. Tarkin points to Vader as an obstacle to any such scheme; Motti says that "Vader can be dealt with." Tarkin says that he will consider Motti's suggestion after the Rebels are put down.
[[File:KissForLuck-SWRadio.flac|thumb|Leia says goodbye to Luke.]]
As the Rebels prepare to go into battle, [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke]] tries once more, unsuccessfully, to convince [[Han Solo/Legends|Han]] to stay and help. [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia]] also finds Luke before he mans his fighter, giving him another kiss for luck. [[Biggs Darklighter/Legends|Biggs]] sees this and tells Luke, "I guess you found your dream girl." [[C-3PO/Legends|See-Threepio]] comes to tell Luke that because of a shortage of droids, [[R2-D2/Legends|Artoo-Detoo]] has been selected to accompany Luke in his fighter. As the Rebel [[Snubfighter/Legends|snubfighters]] take off to attack the Death Star, Luke hears the voice of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Ben Kenobi]].

In the base's [[Strategy Center|operations center]], [[Jan Dodonna/Legends|General Dodonna]] explains the attack plan to Leia and Threepio: the [[BTL Y-wing starfighter/Legends|Y-wing fighters]] of [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)/Legends|Gold Flight]] are the first to make [[Proton torpedo/Legends|torpedo]] runs on the Death Star's [[Thermal exhaust port/Legends|exhaust shaft]]. The [[T-65 X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] of [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)/Legends|Red Flight]] will cover the Y-wings and draw enemy fire. They will make an attack run if Gold Flight fails. A direct hit on the shaft will trigger a chain reaction that will stop the space station.

At first the fighters are able to evade the heavy Imperial guns, but very soon the Death Star launches [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter/Legends|TIE fighters]] against them. The Rebels begin to suffer losses. All the ships of Gold Flight are eliminated before they can reach the target. With only five minutes until the Death Star is in range to fire on the Rebel base, the X-wings of Red Flight begin their attack.

Back aboard the Death Star, an aide informs Tarkin that while the Y-wings are all destroyed, a group of X-wings has evaded the imperial fighters and is attempting an attack of their own. Vader is pursuing them now, but Tarkin orders that his ship be prepared in case it should seem prudent to evacuate the Death Star. Before this can be done, Motti advises Tarkin not to leave: relinquishing his command could jeopardize his ability to hold supreme power. When an analyst approaches Tarkin and says that the Rebel attack poses a danger, Tarkin now laughs at the suggestion.

The first X-wings of Red Flight begin their own attack run. Red Leader orders Luke to remain above the trench with Biggs and [[Wedge Antilles/Legends|Wedge]]. Red Leader manages to fire a torpedo, but his [[Targeting computer/Legends|targeting computer]] fails to score a direct hit. Vader, flying a [[TIE Advanced x1/Legends|prototype fighter]], shoots him down with both of his wingmen. Luke, Biggs, and Wedge then enter the trench themselves. Wedge's ship is damaged and cannot maintain speed, while Biggs is killed.

[[File:LukeHan-StubbornWookiee.flac|thumb|Han tries to explain why he came back to help the Rebels.]]
Luke again hears the spectral voice of Ben Kenobi. He tells Luke to trust [[The Force/Legends|the Force]], prompting him to turn off his own computer. Vader's fighter damages Artoo, but before he can fire again, the ''[[Millennium Falcon/Legends|Millennium Falcon]]'' appears and shoots the Imperial fighters, leaving Luke clear to fire a torpedo using the Force. It hits the target precisely and destroys the station.

Some time later, Luke, Han and [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] get ready for a ceremony to celebrate the victory. Han reflects on his decision to fight for the Rebels. He also says that he does not think that he eliminated the pilot in the prototype fighter. The ceremony begins, and Leia presents [[Medal of Bravery|medals]] to Luke and Han.

==Continuity==
Motti's would-be conspiracy with Tarkin continues a subplot established in the episode "[[Death Star's Transit]]." This characterization of Motti and Tarkin was not in the film but was brought into the [[Canon|''Star Wars'' canon]] in the [[2017]] short story "[[Verge of Greatness]]," written from Tarkin's point of view. The story reuses some of the exact dialogue from the radio drama, integrating it with events from ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref>"[[Verge of Greatness]], in ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''</ref>

This scheme also reframes Tarkin's decision not to prepare an evacuation shuttle during the battle. In the radio version, Tarkin has already ordered that an escape ship be prepared until Motti talks him out of it. Motti argues that Tarkin will not be able to exert his authority if he leaves the Death Star. So when Tarkin then scoffs at the suggestion that he evacuate "in our moment of triumph," he is concealing his true motives. Tarkin's death is therefore a result of his ambition and scheming rather than pure hubris.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

Like the previous episode "[[The Case for Rebellion]]," this episode uses terminology that is different from other ''Star Wars'' media, identifying Red and Gold Squadrons as Red Flight and Gold Flight, and calling their weapons "photon" instead of "proton" torpedoes.
