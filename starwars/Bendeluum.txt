'''Bendeluum''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Flock">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: "One of the Flock"</ref> in the [[Bendeluum system]] in the [[Anoat sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> along the [[Corellian Trade Spine]].<ref name="BMF 18" /> The independent [[Nothoiin cartel]] criminal organization was based there.<ref name="Flock" /> At the [[time]] of the [[Iron Blockade]], gang wars broke out across Bendeluum, which disrupted business and endangered citizens. In an attempt to protect their shops, some merchants offered [[crystal]]s to individuals who would quell the violence.<ref name="Gang">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: "Gang Wars"</ref>
