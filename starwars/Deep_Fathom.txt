'''Deep Fathom''', formerly known as '''Deep Current''', was a resistance group primarily focused on intelligence gathering. They were active during the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]], as well as during the rise of the [[First Order]].

==History==
{{Quote|Is this Deep Fathom?''"<br />"''Confirmed. One of the Resistance's best resources for intel out of the Unknown Regions.|Gial Ackbar, and Caluan Ematt|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
When known as Deep Current during the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]], they were led by [[Saltbite]], operated on [[Shipwrights' Trace]] and [[Tynna]], were able to leverage [[Tynnan]] skills for bureaucracy in order to gain enormous amounts of information in regards to deployments and [[shipyard]] cargo traffic of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] hoped that if they supplied data, the Rebel Alliance could offer protection, and Deep Current and Saltbite were featured in a report to [[Mon Mothma]]. The report would later be included in ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

By the [[time]] the [[Galactic Empire]] fell and the [[New Republic]] and [[Resistance]] grew, Deep Current became Deep Fathom, and Saltbite was still in command of the group. Deep Fathom became one of the best resources for intelligence in the [[Unknown Regions]] for the [[Resistance]]. When ''The Rebel Files'' were uncovered and handed down to [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], leader of the Resistance, [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] and [[Major]] [[Caluan Ematt]] exchanged notes on Saltbite and Deep Fathom.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
Deep Fathom was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', a boxed set and reference book first released in [[2017]] and authored by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
