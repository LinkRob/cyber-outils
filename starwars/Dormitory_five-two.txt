A '''dormitory''' was a standard 50-cell dormitory in the cell ring on [[Level 5]] of an [[Unidentified Imperial factory facility|Imperial factory facility]] within the [[Narkina 5]] [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex|Prison Complex]]. It housed [[Room five-two|work room five-two's]] [[Unit Five-Two-D|day]] and night shift workers.<ref name="Narkina 5" /> [[Table Five]] prisoners [[Cassian Jeron Andor]], [[Xaul]], [[Jemboc]], [[Ham]], [[Taga]], and [[Ruescott Melshi]] as well as [[floor manager]] [[Kino Loy]] planned the [[Narkina 5 prison escape]] in this dormitory following [[Ulaf]]'s [[death]].<ref name="One Way Out">{{Andor|One Way Out}}</ref>
