A '''customs droids''' was a type of [[droid]] used to monitor the cargo of [[starship]]s to prevent the [[Smuggler|smuggling]] of contraband,<ref name="Customs Droids">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: "Customs Droids"</ref> and were also utilized by the [[Galactic Republic Customs of Coruscant]] to process immigrants at entry turnstiles on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Lethal Trackdown" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
During the [[Iron Blockade]], which was established by the [[Galactic Empire]] in an attempt to prevent news of their crushing defeat by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] at the [[Battle of Endor]] from reaching the [[Anoat sector]]'s populace,<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> [[the Smuggler]] sent four of their crew members to quietly reprogram [[Ione]]'s customs droids at the request of [[Leeka Pali]], making the illicit cargo aboard [[Trade Spine League]] [[Starship|ships]] less likely to be noticed.<ref name="Customs Droids" />
{{EndGame}}
