{{Quote|That's an old ''Dathomirian poison''. Without the antidote, you'll be dead in ten minutes.|Deathstick, to Ariole Yu and Just Lucky|<ref name="Doctor Aphra 13" />}}
A '''Dathomirian poison''' was used by the [[Crimson Dawn]] [[assassin]] [[Deathstick]],<ref name="Doctor Aphra 13" /> the daughter of a [[Dathomirian]] [[Gender|female]] named [[Shelish]],<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. According to Deathstick, an individual would [[Death|die]] within ten [[Standard minute|minutes]] of being affected by the [[poison]] if they did not have an [[antidote]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 13" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 13|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 13]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Deathstick used weapons that were coated in the poison to subdue the assassin [[Just Lucky]] as he fought his former mentor [[Gallin Crae]] aboard the [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']], the [[Fortress]]-[[Flagship]] of Crimson Dawn.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 13" /> Not long later, Deathstick used [[Knife|knives]] coated in the poison to pin the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] to a wall on the ''Vermillion''.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 15">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 15|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 15]]</ref>
