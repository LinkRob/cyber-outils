The '''COO-series cook droid''',<ref name="R2 39" /> also known simply as a '''COO Cook Droid''',<ref name="BMF 50">{{BuildFalconCite|50|Starship Fact File}}</ref> was a type of [[class three droid]].<ref name="SWTVE" /> The 6.5 or 7 [[Foot|feet]] [[Cooking droid]],<ref name="New Droid Cooki" /> manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]], was designed to demands of places where large numbers of people all needed to be fed efficiently such as [[mining]] operations, military bases, civilian [[space station]]s, and scientific research complex. The [[droid]] had extendable [[Limb|arm]]s for handing to multiple customers and [[magnet]]ized feet that could lock onto deck plates. COO-series droids also had a [[photoreceptor]] that could see into the [[infrared]] spectrum in order to analyze [[Temperature|heat]] distribution while cooking, and an [[olfactory sensor]] to determine if food was spoiled. Located inside the torso is a recipe storage unit. To upgrade this, the arms of the droid had to have been removed, which was a time-consuming and tedious process, so most didn't bother, leaving the COO droids simply to reheat pre-packed food. As a result of these, they became known for being rather grumpy.<ref name="R2 39" />

==History==
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> a [[COO-2180|cook droid]] served aboard the [[AA-9 Freighter-Liner]] ''[[Jendirian Valley]]'' that [[transport]]ed the [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and her guard, the [[Jedi]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], to Amidala's [[Homeworld|home]] [[planet]] of [[Naboo]].<ref name="AOTC" />

One COO-series cook droid served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] aboard the [[starship]] ''[[Silver Arrow]]''. Like the rest of the ship's droid crew, the cook droid was [[Scourge crisis|infected]] by the [[Scourge]]. The Scourge used the droids to slaughter all [[Species|organic]] crew aboard. Later, the Scourge used the COO-series droid and its other infected droids aboard the ''Silver Arrow'' to ambush [[Magna Tolvan's crew]].<ref name="Aphra 37" /> 

In [[4 ABY]], one unit worked in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]].<ref name="Satisfaction">{{StoryCite|story=Satisfaction|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BoBFDate}}</ref> the palace still had a unit stationed there. When the former [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] and [[Master Assassin]] [[Fennec Shand]] infiltrated the palace through a grate in the kitchen, the COO-series cook droid was preparing [[Foodstuff|food]] alongside a [[Unidentified EV-series sous-chef droid|EV-series sous-chef droid]]. The two [[droid]]s heard the sound of the grate, which the [[EV-series supervisor droid|EV-series]] guessed was a [[rat]]. The [[Cooking droid|cook droid]] sent the [[sous-chef]] droid to find the rat, contacting the [[Unidentified LEP-series service droid|LEP-series rat catcher]], but the EV droid was instead knocked down by Fett. Stepping out from behind its counter to confront Fett, the chef droid began to spin the [[Knife|knives]] on its six [[Limb|arms]]. However, before the droid could attack, Shand crept up behind it, slicing off its head.<ref name="Storm" />

A COO-series droid was acquired by the [[Plazir-15's government]] and were repurposed for civilian duties, with one of these droids was spiked by [[Nepenthé]] which had been laced with [[nano-droid]]s and attacked patrons with it's knives.<ref name="Chapter 22" />
