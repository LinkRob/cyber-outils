The '''CSS-1 Corellian Star Shuttle''', sometimes referred to simply as the '''CSS-1 shuttle''' or as the '''Republic shuttle''', was a [[transport]] used by the [[Galactic Republic]] during at least a [[Standard decade|decade]] before the start of the [[Clone Wars]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:MesonMartinet-TheMutiny.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Meson Martinet'' was a highly modified CSS-1.]]
The CSS-1 Corellian Star Shuttle was a [[transport]] manufactured by [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] for the [[Galactic Republic]]. It was equipped with an impressive meeting room and comfortable quarters for VIP passengers, which it could carry 200 of. Its consumables lasted three [[Standard year|years]] and was equipped with an unusually strong [[deflector shield generator]]. It was 80 [[meter]]s in length and could reach speeds of 800 [[kilometer per hour]]. It had a class 1.5 [[hyperdrive]] and carried no weapons, yet had a heavily armored [[hull]].<ref name="Starfighters" />

==History==
[[File:Meson_Martinet_TFA.png|thumb|right|200px|Ithano loading cargo in his ship, the ''Meson Martinet''.]]
The CSS-1 Corellian Star Shuttle ''[[Perpetuus]]'' was used by members of the [[Jedi Order]] and the recently elected [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], to travel to [[Naboo]] following the [[Battle of Naboo]].<ref name="Episode I" /> A successor to the CSS-1, the [[CCS-8]], was being developed until it was indefinitely put on hold due to the eruption of the pan-[[Galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Starfighters" /> During the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars, the CSS-1 was used in [[Republic Military]] operations for bulk [[transport]]ation.<ref name="Rise" /> During the [[Imperial Era|rule]] of the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|successor]], the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] used CSS-1s to transport their forces.<ref name="SWC" /> The [[New Republic]] had a similar vessel that featured two-engines instead of three. This transport was used by [[Ambassador]] [[Nadura]] who used it to travel to [[Calodan]] before it was destroyed by the [[Scout guard]]s.<ref name="The Path of Hate">{{TOTE|The Path of Hate}}</ref> After the fall of the Empire and the establishment of the [[New Republic]], [[Sidon Ithano]] and his [[Sidon Ithano's pirate crew|crew]] used a modified CSS-1, the ''[[Meson Martinet]]'', for their [[pirate]] activities.<ref name="Starfighters" />
