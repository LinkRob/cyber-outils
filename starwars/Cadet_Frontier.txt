{{Quote|The Imperial Broadcast has announced Cadet Frontier &ndash; a new entertainment program that lets aspiring future cadets go through six weeks of simulated combat against rebels. Applications are now open.|Dorna Parros, HoloNet News|audio=CadetFrontier-Outlaws.flac|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
'''''Cadet Frontier''''' was an entertainment program announced by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Broadcast]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The program was planned to feature six [[Standard week|weeks]] of simulated combat against [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] for a group of aspiring future [[Imperial cadet|cadets]]. The announcement was covered on the [[HoloNet News]] by [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Dorna Parros]], who advertised that applications for the program were now open.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Cadet Frontier'' was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
