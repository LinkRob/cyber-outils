The '''12th Engineering Division''' was a [[Division/Legends|division]] that participated in the [[Battle of Malachor V]], under the command of [[Jedi General/Legends|Jedi General]] [[Meetra Surik]].

==Behind the scenes==
In a cut content in ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]'', [[Bao-Dur]] reveals that he and the [[Sullustan/Legends|Sullustan]] mechanic [[Tien Tubb]] were part of this division.
