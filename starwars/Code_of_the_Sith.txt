The '''Code of the Sith''', also known as '''Qotsisajak''',<ref name="Guardians">[[Guardians of the Whills (novel)|''Guardians of the Whills'']]</ref> was a mantra that expressed the core beliefs of the [[Sith]]. It was an inversion of the [[Jedi Code]], a set of rules for [[Jedi|members]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. It notably taught its followers that it is for the strong to destroy the weak.<ref name="Backstories">''[[Darth Vader: Sith Lord]]''</ref>

{{Stub}}

==The Code==
<poem>
''Peace is a lie. There is only Passion.
''Through Passion, I gain Strength.
''Through Strength, I gain Power.
''Through Power, I gain Victory.
''Through Victory my chains are Broken.
''The Force shall free me.<ref name="Guardians" />
</poem>

In some versions of the Code, the initial line "Peace is a lie" was repeated after "The Force shall free me."<ref name="Backstories" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Code of the Sith first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Altar of Mortis (episode)|Altar of Mortis]]."<ref name="TCW-AoM">{{TCW|Altar of Mortis (episode)}}</ref> The [[Code of the Sith/Legends|Code of the Sith]] was originally created by [[David Gaider]], who wrote the [[Korriban/Legends|Korriban]] segment of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''.<ref name="SW:KotOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref> Initially, Gaider had asked [[Lucasfilm]] what the Sith's philosophy was or if they had a code like the Jedi did. When he was asked to write them himself, Gaider reversed the existing Jedi Code, with the accompanying dialogue about the Sith on Korriban being partly inspired by [[Wikipedia:Mein Kampf|''Mein Kampf'']], the [[1920s#1925|1925]] [[Wikipedia:Nazism|Nazi]] manifesto.<ref name="Gaider">{{Tumblr|subdomain=the-gaider-archives|url=post/108932517363/on-knights-of-the-old-republic|name=DGaider Tumblr Archives|text=On Knights of the Old Republic}}</ref>
