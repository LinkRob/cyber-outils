'''Before the Republic''' is a publishing era used by [[Lucas Licensing]] to denote material that is set between the [[Standard year/Legends|years]] 37,000 BBY and [[25,000 BBY/Legends|25,000 BBY]] in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' universe.<ref name="Volume 1">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi Book One—Force Storm]]''</ref> Before the Republic was the seventh in-universe publishing era to be established, and was created to classify the [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]''. The era includes the three arcs of ''Dawn of the Jedi'', the tie-in novel ''[[Dawn of the Jedi: Into the Void]]'', and two accompanying short stories.

Originally, issues of ''Dawn of the Jedi'' stated that Before the Republic spanned 36,000&ndash;25,000 BBY, but the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi Book One—Force Storm|first trade paperback]] of the series established the boundary at 37,000 to accommodate the start of the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm|first issue]], which is set in [[36,453 BBY]]. The [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan|next]] [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force War|two arcs]] reflected the correct date.
