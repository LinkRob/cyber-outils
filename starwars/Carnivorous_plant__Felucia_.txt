A [[species]] of large [[Color|blue]] '''carnivorous plant''' with a bulbous mouth could be found on the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Felucia]]. During the [[Quest for the Kyber Saber Crystals|search]] for the [[Kyber Saber]], after [[youngling]] [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]], [[Jedi]]-in-training [[Luke Skywalker]], and [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|R0-GR]] "Roger" crashed their [[starship]] on Felucia, Roger briefly found himself eaten by one of these [[carnivorous plant]]s before being forcefully spat out, all much to his disdain.<ref name="Crossing Paths" />
