The '''destruction of the Jedi Temple''' by [[Ben Solo]] occurred on [[Ossus]] in [[28 ABY]] during the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]]. The [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker]] had been established by [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] to [[Jedi training|train]] Solo, who was his [[Skywalker family|sister's son]], and other [[Padawan]]s in a [[Jedi Order|new generation]] of [[Jedi]]. Solo's [[Parent|mother]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], had hoped that her brother could train her [[Force-sensitive]] son in the ways of [[the Force]] and help him resist the [[Dark side of the Force|dark]] presence she could feel attempting to influence him. [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] of the [[First Order]] had been seducing Solo from afar since the boy's childhood as an unknowing tool of revenge for [[Darth Sidious]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]].

Solo had kept secret both his contact with Snoke and his fascination with the dark side, but Skywalker glimpsed enough to grow concerned and decided to confront his nephew. One night, Skywalker entered [[Ben Solo's hut|Solo's room]] and looked into the sleeping Solo's mind. Seeing and fearing the strength of the darkness there, Skywalker instinctively ignited his [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|lightsaber]] before quickly realizing his error, but it was too late: Solo awakened and believed his uncle was about to [[Death|murder]] him. Solo lashed out with the Force to [[Telekinesis|telekinetically]] collapse his hut and trapped Skywalker under the debris. Standing outside the [[Jedi temple|temple]] full of fear and anger, Solo gave in to the dark side's power and created a [[Force power|lightning storm]] that razed the structure, killing all of the students who were present. Solo fled and was pursued by the three surviving Padawans&mdash;[[Tai]], [[Voe]], and [[Hennix]]&mdash;who all died attempting to bring Solo to justice. He completed his turn to the dark side and became Kylo Ren, master of the [[Knights of Ren]], and joined the First Order under the tutelage of Snoke.

Skywalker abandoned his hope of rebuilding the Jedi Order and went into [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] on [[Ahch-To]], the [[Homeworld|birthplace]] of the Jedi Order, until [[34 ABY]] when the Force prodigy [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] sought his aid in the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the First Order. When Skywalker joined the conflict, he distracted his nephew with a confrontation to allow Organa and her [[Resistance]] movement to escape. The effort ended his life, but his sacrifice allowed the Resistance to continue the fight and ultimately [[Battle of Exegol|defeat]] the First Order and the [[Sith]].

==Prelude==
===Restoring the Jedi Order===
{{Quote|The Force runs strong in your family. Pass on what you have learned.|Yoda, to Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>}}
After the [[Galactic Concordance|end]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] was the last [[Jedi]] left in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> His [[Jedi Master]], [[Yoda]], had [[Death|died]] after securing his promise to pass on what he had learned.<ref name="Episode VI" /> His [[Twin]] sister, [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[New Republic]], was his initial [[Padawan|student]] until a [[Force vision|vision]] led her to choose another path.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> With help from allies such as [[Lieutenant]] [[Shara Bey]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]<ref name="Shattered Empire 4">[[Shattered Empire 4|''Shattered Empire'' 4]]</ref> and the explorer [[Lor San Tekka]], Skywalker sought out ancient relics and [[Sacred Jedi texts|texts]] of the [[Jedi Order]] to better understand their [[Timeline of galactic history|history]] and teachings.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> Skywalker had great hope for the future of the Jedi, believing that if a single Jedi could help bring back balance to [[the Force]], then a restored Jedi Order could maintain that peace and prevent the [[dark side of the Force]] from regaining its hold on the galaxy.<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>

[[File:Luke_Skywalker_Jedi_Master_VII.jpg|thumb|left|250px|After the Galactic Civil War, Jedi Master Luke Skywalker began training a new generation of Jedi.]]
In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Birth date">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' establishes that Ben Solo was [[Reproduction|born]] on [[Chandrila]] the [[Standard day|day]] the [[Galactic Concordance]] was signed. ''Aftermath: Empire's End'' also states that the [[The galaxy|Galactic]] Concordance was signed during the [[Battle of Jakku]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[5 ABY]]. Therefore, Solo was born on Chandrila in 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> Organa and her [[Marriage|husband]], [[General]] [[Han Solo]], welcomed their [[Solo family|son]] and named him [[Ben Solo]]. Organa sensed the capacity for both [[Light side of the Force|light]] and darkness within Ben.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> After several [[Standard year|years]], she could feel the influence of [[Snoke]], the [[Supreme Leader]] of the shadowy [[First Order]] that had arisen from the ashes of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> In truth, the secretly resurrected [[Darth Sidious]] was manipulating and engineering Luke Skywalker's eventual failures;<ref name="DB:Order">{{Databank|jedi-order|Jedi Order}}</ref> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] had created Snoke as a tool for his revenge. Sidious's [[Sith apprentice]] had been Organa and Skywalker's [[Skywalker family|father]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], who had ultimately rejected his dark side persona as [[Darth]] Vader by killing Sidious, saving his son's life at the cost of his own.<ref name="The Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> Hoping to protect Ben Solo from Snoke, Organa asked her brother to [[Jedi training|train]] the boy as a Jedi. Solo welcomed the training with his uncle, but he was unaware that his family was concealing the truth about his grandfather.<ref name="Skywalker: A Family at War">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]''</ref>

With his nephew and a handful of other disciples, Skywalker established a [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|Jedi temple]] to train his students in the ways of the Jedi. Throughout Solo's training, Skywalker sensed the growing darkness within his nephew, but he believed that he could contain it and keep Solo on the path of the light.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Unbeknownst to Skywalker, mysterious voices were grooming Solo towards the dark side.<ref name="Fascinating Facts">''[[Star Wars: Fascinating Facts]]''</ref> He hated the pressure of living up to his namesake, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Obi-Wan "Ben" Kenobi]], along with his family's legacy,<ref name="Kylo 2">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 2|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 2]]</ref> and he grew fascinated by the dark side as Snoke spoke into his mind.<ref name="Skywalker: A Family at War" /> As his own power increased, Solo believed that Skywalker sensed and feared it,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and the boy grew into a man who resented his uncle despite respecting the Jedi Master's power.<ref name="Kylo 2" />

===A terrible misunderstanding===
{{Quote|I saw darkness. I'd sensed it building in him. I'd seen it in moments during his training. But then I looked inside, and it was beyond what I ever imagined. Snoke had already turned his heart. He would bring destruction, and pain, and death, and the end of everything I love because of what he would become. And for the briefest moment of pure instinct, I thought I could stop it. It passed like a fleeting shadow. And I was left with shame&hellip; and with consequence. And the last thing I saw were the eyes of a frightened boy whose master had failed him.|Luke Skywalker, to Rey|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Wakey_wakey_Ben.png|thumb|right|250px|Ben Solo believed that his master, Luke Skywalker, had betrayed him.]]
One night,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> in [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Skywalker went to Solo's [[Ben Solo's hut|hut]] to confront his nephew's growing darkness. Skywalker found Solo asleep and used the Force to look into Solo's mind; he saw even greater darkness than he had feared, and that Snoke's machinations had already turned his nephew's heart.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> In a vision, he saw that Solo would wield a [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|crackling red lightsaber]] as he brought death and destruction to everything Skywalker loved.<ref name="TLJ junior">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> Reacting to what he saw, Skywalker instinctively ignited his [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|lightsaber]] with the momentary thought of preventing the future by killing Solo, and the thought passed quickly into shame. However, Solo awakened to his uncle standing over him with an [[Lightsaber|ignited blade]]; knowing what Skywalker had seen in his mind, he believed his uncle had come into his room to murder him in his sleep. Solo ignited his own lightsaber and attempted to kill Skywalker first. As they locked blades, Solo used the Force to bring the roof of his room collapsing down upon Skywalker.<ref name="TLJ novel">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

==Destruction==
===Night of fire===
{{Quote|Why? Why? Why did you do it?|Ben Solo, regarding Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Kylo 1" />}}
[[File:BenSoloNeedsVisine-TheRiseOfKyloRen1.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Red eyes and red clouds&mdash;Ben Solo creates a lightning storm to destroy Luke Skywalker's temple as his fear, anger, and hate consume him.]]
To Solo, his uncle had betrayed him and proved his worst fears about his family were true; Skywalker had reacted to his nephew's darkness with a momentary thought of killing him.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> With Skywalker trapped under the rubble and believed dead, Solo exited the ruins of his room and faced Skywalker's temple. The sky was cloudless as he began raging in confusion over why his uncle had seemingly attacked him. He pointed his ignited lightsaber towards the temple and screamed for answers.<ref name="Kylo 1" /> As he lashed out against Skywalker and his uncle's temple,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> he was led by his fear and anger.<ref name="Age of Resistance: The Official Collector's Edition">[[Star Wars: Age of Resistance – The Official Collector's Edition|''Star Wars: Age of Resistance'' – The Official Collector's Edition]]</ref> He surrendered to the power of the dark side and [[Force power|created a storm]];<ref name="Encyclopedia: Kylo" /> his pupils constricted in his [[Color|red]]-clouded [[eye]]s, then red storm clouds formed above the temple. He questioned their sudden appearance just before the clouds erupted with lightning.<ref name="Kylo 1" /> The storm he had created set fire to the dwellings and razed the temple, killing most of his fellow students.<ref name="Encyclopedia: Kylo" />

The temple's initial explosion knocked Solo off his feet and he rose to see the scattered bodies of dead students. Although he protested and attempted to run into the burning temple, he was again knocked down by another explosion. He watched the temple burn and said he had not wanted it. The disembodied voice that often communicated with Solo claimed that Skywalker and the Jedi had chosen it, not Solo himself.<ref name="Kylo 1" />

===Discovering the destruction===
{{Quote|Ben, please. The story you're telling us... it can't possibly be true. There must be more to what happened. The temple, Master Luke, the other students... just talk to us. It can't be as bad as what you're saying.''"<br />"''I already told you what happened here. This place was a lie. Now it's over. I'm leaving. You all should too.|Tai and Ben Solo|<ref name="Kylo 1" />}}
[[File:TaiConfrontsBenSolo-TheRiseOfKyloRen1.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Tai, Voe, and Hennix confronted Ben Solo in the aftermath of the temple's destruction.]]
Jedi students [[Hennix]], [[Tai]], and [[Voe]] initially survived the temple's destruction because they were away for an off-[[Planet|world]] mission. They returned to discover the temple had collapsed in flames, all their fellow students besides Solo were dead, and they could not sense any other presence except for Solo, who was watching the temple burn. Solo told them that Master Skywalker had tried to murder him in his sleep and now was dead. When Voe expressed skepticism, Solo replied that he was stronger in the Force than the other three Jedi combined and stronger than Skywalker. He called the temple a lie that was now over. Tai was unable to defuse the situation, and Hennix and Voe drew their lightsabers to stop Solo from leaving. As the three Jedi failed to take down Solo, he used the Force to [[Telekinesis|strike]] them with debris, and stopped after he impaled Hennix. While Tai and Voe attended to Hennix's wound, Solo fled aboard his personal [[starship]] [[Grimtaash (starship)|''Grimtaash'']]. The other Jedi pursued Solo in their starship ''[[Verity]]'', but Solo fired a precise shot that disabled their ship's main engines. The three decided to continue tracking Solo to get answers about the destruction. Hennix was bewildered, Tai did not believe Solo could have betrayed or killed anyone, and Voe wanted to bring Solo to justice for the murders of Master Skywalker and the other Jedi.<ref name="Kylo 1" />

===Awakening in the ruins===
{{Quote|He was training a new generation of Jedi. One boy, an apprentice turned against him, destroyed it all. Luke felt responsible&hellip; He just walked away from everything.|Han Solo, about Luke Skywalker and Ben Solo|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:LukesTempleBurning-TLJ.jpg|thumb|left|220px|After regaining consciousness, Skywalker and R2-D2 stared at the destroyed temple in shock and shame.]]
After Solo and his pursuers had departed from the site,<ref name="Kylo 1" /> Skywalker regained consciousness to see the temple burning and most of his students' corpses littered the grounds.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> His intention of intervening in Solo's growing darkness had instead resulted in Solo destroying his temple, killing his students, and turning to the dark side.<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> With some of his students missing from the ruins, Skywalker mistakenly believed that Solo had slaughtered those who would not turn to the dark side as Solo had, and that the missing students had agreed to join Solo.<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi" /> Skywalker, overcome with despair and shame with only his faithful [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] by his side, watched as his Jedi temple and the future of the Jedi Order collapsed into flames.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Ben Solo's journey to the dark side===
{{Quote|Wait... aren't ''you'' one of Skywalker's students? At that temple he built?''"<br />"''Not anymore. And the temple's gone. Burned. I destroyed it. That's why the other students came after me. To kill me.|"Ren" and Ben Solo|<ref name="Kylo 3" />}}
After the destruction of the temple and deaths of the other students, Solo felt he was misunderstood and being pushed away.<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> He fled to a [[Amaxine Space Station|space station]] to meet Snoke, who embraced him. Snoke doubted Skywalker was dead and asked Solo what he planned to do next now that he was no longer under Skywalker's influence. Solo decided to seek out the [[Knights of Ren]], a shadowy group of dark siders led by the enigmatic "[[Ren (human)|Ren]]" who subscribed to an ideology based on his lightsaber, the [[Ren (lightsaber)|Ren]].<ref name="Kylo 1" /> Tai, Voe, and Hennix tracked Solo to [[Elphrona]] where he had [[Mission to Elphrona|first met]] and been invited to learn from the Knights as a child. In their [[Battle of Elphrona|duel]], Solo attempted to kill Voe and inadvertently killed Hennix. Afterward, Tai asked what had happened to Hennix, but Solo said it did not matter. He collapsed the outpost's entrance to stop Voe and Tai from following him, then he left to meet the Knights of Ren.<ref name="Kylo 3" />

[[File:BenKillsRen-TheRiseOfKyloRen4.jpg|thumb|right|220px|With lightning and Tai's lightsaber, Ben Solo kills Ren.]]
Solo told the Knights of Ren that he wanted to join them. In describing the events leading to Hennix's death, he said he had burned and destroyed Skywalker's temple. Although they were unimpressed with the story, Ren said Solo was strong in the "shadow" and Snoke had vouched for him.<ref name="Kylo 3" />

Solo still failed to impress on the [[Minemoon]] of [[Mimban]], where Voe and Tai caught up to him again. Tai entreated Solo to change his dark path, but before Solo could make a decision, Ren killed Tai. Solo gave in to the dark side of the Force to duel Ren; his fall was sensed by Snoke, Darth Sidious, his mother, and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]. Lightning from above surrounded Solo and struck Ren as Solo impaled him with Tai's lightsaber, killing Ren and throwing back the Knights and Voe. Solo then killed Voe with the Ren lightsaber. The Knights accepted Solo as the new [[Ren|Master of the Knights of Ren]]. With Voe's murder and Solo's turn, the destruction of Skywalker's Jedi Order seemed complete. On the ''[[Night Buzzard]]'', the ship used by the Knights of Ren, the former Jedi [[Bleeding|bled]] his [[kyber crystal]] and took on the name Kylo Ren.<ref name="Kylo 4" />

==Aftermath==
===Rising within the First Order===
{{Quote|Forgive me. I feel it again. The pull to the light. The Supreme Leader senses it. Show me again the power of the darkness, and I'll let nothing stand in our way. Show me, Grandfather, and I will finish what you started.|Kylo Ren, to the remnants of Darth Vader's helmet|<ref name="Episode VII" />|audio=ThePullToTheLight.flac}}
[[File:Decisive_Blow_SoR.png|thumb|left|250px|Ben Solo embraced the persona of Kylo Ren after his fall to the dark side.]]
Kylo Ren joined the First Order under the tutelage of Supreme Leader Snoke.<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Ren's betrayal and destruction of Skywalker's Jedi students earned him the moniker of "Jedi Killer" amongst the First Order's ranks,<ref name="Complete VD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> although it was many years until the wider galaxy learned about what happened to the temple.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> Solo had wiped out the fledgling Jedi Order,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> leaving only Skywalker,<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> but Snoke believed that Skywalker also had to be destroyed to prevent the rise of new Jedi.<ref name="Episode VII" />

Ren idolized Darth Vader, the dark side incarnation of his grandfather.<ref name="TFA novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref> He felt tormented by the continued lure of the light that could lead him away from his grandfather's legacy.<ref name="Episode VII" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Han Solo came to [[Starkiller Base]] and asked his son to come home, but Ren murdered his father in an attempt to end his inner conflict.<ref name="Episode VII" /> Soon afterward, he killed Snoke and usurped the position of Supreme Leader of the First Order.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Skywalker's despair===
{{Quote|Leia could see the darkness taking hold of her son and entrusted me with helping Ben find balance. Yet as the darkness continued to grow in the boy, I wasn't strong enough to keep it from taking hold. My mistakes pushed Ben further from the light&mdash;and he dragged the galaxy into the darkness with him.|Luke Skywalker, ''The Secrets of the Jedi''|<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi" />}}
Skywalker felt that he had failed to restore the Jedi Order and protect his students, especially his own nephew, from the dark legacy of their family,<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi" /> and imagining what his other students had experienced was too painful for him.<ref name="Encyclopedia: Luke Skywalker">{{EncyclopediaCite|Luke Skywalker}}</ref> He blamed himself instead of Snoke for Solo's fall to the dark side,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> along with the resulting breakdown of his sister's family,<ref name="TLJ novel" /> and believed himself ultimately responsible for Kylo Ren turning the galaxy towards darkness.<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi" /> The Jedi Master went into exile on [[Ahch-To]], the birthplace of the Jedi Order and the home of the [[first Jedi Temple]], where he intended to one day die. He intended to end the Jedi religion, which he believed had only a legacy of [[Separatist Crisis|hubris]] and [[Clone Wars|destruction]] in the galaxy&mdash;in his mind, it was the Jedi that allowed Darth Sidious to create the Galactic Empire and seduce his father, Anakin Skywalker, to the dark side.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Rebirth of the rebellion===
{{Quote|That pathetic dream was shattered when Skywalker's prize pupil&mdash;his nephew, Ben Solo&mdash;turned against the Order's teachings and cut down his fellow students. Skywalker wallowed in self-pity, fleeing into seclusion and abandoning the ways of the Force. [...] How fitting, then, that Skywalker would ultimately give his own life in battle against the child whose soul he failed to save&mdash;Ben Solo, the boy who had become Kylo Ren.|Darth Sidious, ''The Secrets of the Sith''|<ref name="Secrets of the Sith">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Luke_Skywalker_on_Crait.png|thumb|right|300px|Skywalker projected his appearance into the Battle of Crait, sacrificing his own life to confront Kylo Ren.]]
There were many rumors and stories about where Skywalker had fled to, but those closest to him believed that he went looking for the first Jedi temple. The First Order and the [[Resistance]], led by General Organa, both searched for Skywalker and sought out San Tekka, who had part of a [[Map to Skywalker|map]] to Ahch-To. The map was pieced together by the Resistance and given to Rey, a powerful Force prodigy, who used it to find Skywalker on Ahch-To.<ref name="Episode VII" /> She tried for some time to convince Skywalker that the return of the Jedi was critical if the Resistance was to prevail in its [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the First Order, but he was too guilt-stricken. Meanwhile, the [[Force dyad|connection]] between Kylo Ren and Rey strengthened.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

When Skywalker first told Rey about Solo's fall, he omitted key details. Ren asked if Skywalker had told her why he had destroyed his uncle's temple, then provided his own point of view; Rey subsequently confronted Skywalker to learn the truth. When she left the planet, she believed she could turn Ren back to the light, and he thought she would embrace the dark side. The [[Force spirit|spectral]] intervention of Yoda shook Skywalker from his guilt and he put his faith in Rey as the future of the Jedi. During the [[Battle of Crait]], he [[Force projection|projected]] his appearance into the conflict to distract Ren with a personal confrontation, allowing the Resistance to escape from certain destruction. The effort Skywalker expended proved fatal and he passed away into the Force on Ahch-To; however, his actions helped the legend of Skywalker and the hope of the Jedi and the Resistance to spread across the galaxy.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

During that time, Darth Sidious had continued his secret machinations on the Sith planet [[Exegol]]. At some point, he recorded ''[[The Secrets of the Sith]]'', a [[book]] that compiled his knowledge of the Sith and his own history. He described using Snoke as his proxy to turn Ben Solo against his family, the Jedi Order, and the light. According to Sidious, despite Solo cutting down the other Jedi students and contributing to Skywalker's sacrifice, the destruction of the Jedi would not be complete until he killed Rey.<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> Solo ultimately returned to the light, and he helped Rey defeat Sidious before he joined the Force by giving his life to restore hers.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Origin and development===
{{Quote|Ultimately, the one who lies is Luke, in the very first flashback, where he omits the fact that he had a lightsaber in his hand. Kylo is basically telling the truth about his perception of that moment.|Rian Johnson|<ref name="Art of TLJ">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]''</ref>}}
The destruction of Luke Skywalker's Jedi Temple was first referenced in the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]]. Although the event took place in the film's backstory, its aftermath was briefly seen in Rey's vision.<ref name="Episode VII" /> The event's development and its aftermath were featured in its [[2017]] sequel [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], during Luke Skywalker's and Kylo Ren's flashback sequences.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> The destruction itself was first depicted in [[The Rise of Kylo Ren 1|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1]].<ref name="Kylo 1" />

[[File:LukesMistake-TLJ.png|thumb|left|250px|Luke Skywalker's expression in the third flashback]]
Though included in the finished version of the film, ''The Last Jedi'' director [[Rian Johnson]] made a late decision to include the three flashbacks which showed the prelude and aftermath of the event, as they were not present in the film's script before shooting began. Taking inspiration from the [[1950s#1950|1950]] film [[Wikipedia:Rashomon|''Rashōmon'']], Johnson decided to divide the sequence in three parts from different points of view, instead of just doing a long flashback as he wanted something harder to influence Rey's journey in the story.<ref name="Art of TLJ" />

Prior to crafting ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Kylo Ren|The Rise of Kylo Ren]]'', Lucasfilm developed a framework through a conversation with [[J.J. Abrams]]. Writer [[Charles Soule]] and artist [[Will Sliney]] used this framework to develop the story of Solo's turn to the dark side. Soule and Sliney knew the destruction of the Jedi temple was a fixed point in the timeline prior to Ren's introduction on [[Jakku]] in ''The Force Awakens''. Soule chose to omit dialogue in some panels to put the focus on Sliney's art and Solo's internal struggle.<ref name="Ben Solo's Turn">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-the-rise-of-kylo-ren-interview|text=The Creators of Marvel's ''Star Wars: The Rise of Kylo Ren'' on Ben Solo's Turn}}</ref>

{{SW|url=films/star-wars-the-last-jedi-story-gallery|text=''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Story Gallery}}</ref> Some of those sources also specified that Solo burned the temple;<ref name="TLJ VD" /><ref name="Secrets of the Jedi" /><ref name="Royal Rebel" /> the aftermath of the temple in flames appeared in ''The Force Awakens'' and ''The Last Jedi'', along with related adaptations of the films.<ref name="Episode VIII" /><ref name="Episode VII" /> Released on [[December 18]], [[2019]],<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/82376/star_wars_the_rise_of_kylo_ren_2019_1|text=Star Wars: The Rise of Kylo Ren (2019) #1}}</ref> ''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1 depicted a lightning strike from red storm clouds sparking the fire.<ref name="Kylo 1" /> The [[2021]] book ''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]'' by [[Kristin Baver]], a biography of the Skywalker family, does not describe the actual moment of destruction, instead presenting the prelude and the aftermath without directly identifying Solo as the cause. The book introduces an alternative narrative [[in-universe]] that [[Wikipedia:Meteorology|meteorological]] reports afterward gave support to an interpretation of events attributing the fire to a "freak [[Wikipedia:Dry thunderstorm|dry-lightning storm]]."<ref name="Skywalker: A Family at War" /> Other materials published after ''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1 state that Solo was responsible for the destruction without mentioning the storm itself,<ref name="Kylo 3" /><ref name="The Star Wars Book" /><ref name="Fascinating Facts" /><ref name="TROS VD" /><ref name="Age of Resistance: The Official Collector's Edition" /><ref name="Encyclopedia: Luke Skywalker" /><ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /><ref name="Kylo 2 crawl">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 2|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 2]], mentioned in the opening crawl</ref><ref name="Kylo 4 crawl">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 4|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 4]], mentioned in the opening crawl</ref> while the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] booklet "[[Kylo Ren and Other Followers of the Dark Side]]" states, "In fury, the boy unleashed his full powers, creating a storm that set the surrounding structures on fire and brought down the Jedi temple, killing most of his fellow students."<ref name="Encyclopedia: Kylo" />

===Contradictions===
Prior to ''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'', sources did not mention that any of Skywalker's students survived the destruction. ''[[Princess Leia: Royal Rebel]]'' stated Leia's son "killed all the apprentices" before taking the name Kylo Ren.<ref name="Royal Rebel" /> Skywalker said in ''The Last Jedi'' that a "handful of [his] students" left with Ren after he "slaughtered the rest."<ref name="Episode VIII" /> ''[[The Secrets of the Jedi]]'' added that Ren had "slaughtered any students who refused to follow him to the dark side."<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi" /> ''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1 instead showed that Skywalker was mistaken, as three students who weren't present for the destruction pursued Solo in the aftermath, and all died by the end; none turned to the dark side with him.<ref name="Kylo 4" />

''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]'' stated that the Knights of Ren were involved in the temple's destruction,<ref name="Absolutely Everything" /> and ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'' said Ren "led its destruction,"<ref name="TLJ VD" /> implying that at least one other individual worked with him to destroy it. The "Dark Side Servants" feature in [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 51|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 51]] also said that he led the Knights of Ren to do so, and included a screenshot from Rey's vision in ''The Force Awakens'' of Ren and his Knights in the rain.<ref name="Helmet Collection 51" /> However, ''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1 did not explicitly feature the Knights of Ren during the destruction or show that any other individual was involved before Solo fled from Tai, Hennix, and Voe.<ref name="Kylo 1" />

Two sources stated that Ren's lightsaber skills prevented the Jedi Order's return<ref name="TFA VD" /> and he annihilated Skywalker's students with his "talent in combat."<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> ''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1 depicted Solo wielding his lightsaber as he raged at the temple, but he did not use it in combat.<ref name="Kylo 1" /> Despite this, ''[[The Secrets of the Sith]]'' likewise stated that Ben Solo "cut down his fellow students."<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" />

===Authorial intent===
[[Matt Martin]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] has responded several times to questions on Twitter about this event. According to Martin, statements in ''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary'' that Ben Solo set fire to and destroyed Skywalker's temple did not contradict ''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1 as some readers believed. Martin noted that the series had not yet concluded at the time, but he did not know where people's misinterpretation of the comic came from.<ref name="Twitter MM TROKR misinterpretation">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1209528928097853442|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=Rise of Kylo doesn't say no. I'm not sure where the misinterpretation comes from, it seems clear to me, but I'm not going to explain it in depth now before the whole series is even out. Suffice to say, it's not a contradiction and both essentially say the same thing.|archivefile=File:Matt_Martin_Rise_of_Kylo_Ren_misinterpretation.png|archivedate=20191224174634}}</ref> He compared Solo's reaction afterward to Anakin Skywalker's reaction after the death of [[Mace Windu]] in the [[2005]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Twitter MM TROKR panel">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1209542184619298821|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=That panel reminds me very much of this moment.|archivefile=File:Matt_Martin_Rise_of_Kylo_Ren_panel.jpg|archivedate=20200129214819}}</ref>

Martin's personal take is that Rey's awakening to the Force occurred in response to "Ben's destruction of the temple and Jedi." He said if there was any ambiguity about the event, he could not change that through a tweet, but that "the movie" said Solo destroyed the temple.<ref name="Twitter MM destruction">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1239959897300553728|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=My personal take is that the the [sic] Force awakened within [Rey] to combat the shift of balance caused by Ben's destruction of the temple and Jedi (has little, if anything, to do with her lineage). [...] The movie says he did it but it could still be ambiguous, sure. And if it's ambiguous I'm not going to change that with a tweet. I'm just using that terminology as shorthand for my answer to a separate question.|archivefile=File:Matt_Martin_destruction.png|archivedate=20200317173807}}</ref> Martin disagreed with a claim that the "comics [made] it clear that Ben did not destroy the temple," and responded to the user's theory about Snoke or Palpatine as "one way to read it."<ref name="Twitter MM TROKR clear">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1239977907813896192|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=I don't think it makes that clear but it does leave it a little ambiguous. That said, it's a much easier shorthand to use in my answer above. Especially when limited to 280 characters. [...] That's one way to read it.|archivefile=File:Matt_Martin_TROKR_clear.png|archivedate=20200317183353}} {{C|In response to: "The Rise of Kylo Ren comics make it clear that Ben did not destroy the temple. [...] I don't think it's ambiguous. Ben wasn't even in the temple when it was blasted with somebody's force [sic] lightning. He said plainly that he didn't want that, but left in a panic because Voe blamed him. No intent, no motive, plus a solid alibi [equals] not guilty. Snoke or Palps did it."}}</ref> Regarding whether someone else destroyed the temple or Solo unconsciously did so in his rage, Martin replied, "The latter is more my read on it."<ref name="Twitter MM TROKR rage">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1311505257353023490|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=The latter is more my read on it.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/wtisD}} {{C|In response to: "Hey Matt, I have a question about the destruction of Luke's Jedi Temple. Is The Rise of Kylo Ren imply [sic] that this was destroyed by someone else? Like Snoke or Sidious? Or is it Ben Solo who unconsciously destroyed the Temple in his rage?"}}</ref>

Martin has said that he would "really rather not completely explain things like this" and suggested asking the writer.<ref name="Twitter MM TROKR accident">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1307874788572950530|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=I'd really rather not completely explain things like this. It sort of ruins the magic. You can try asking the writer if he feels like getting into it.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/Ej3WH}} {{C|In response to: "Sorry to bother you, but I was curious now that Rise of Kylo Ren has been out for a bit if you could clarify the destruction of the temple because there's still some confusion amongst my friends and I. Was the lightning something Ben caused on accident? Everyone I knew thought it was Snoke/Palpatine since Ben looks confused just before the lightning hits and he pops in to Ben's head right after, but back after issue 1 came out you also said that the comic didn't contradict the visual dictionary saying that Ben destroyed it."}}</ref> When Soule was asked if there was "any chance" he would answer a question about the lightning strike shown in ''The Rise of Kylo Ren'', he provided a literal response that there was a chance.<ref name="Twitter CS TROKR">{{Twitter|CharlesSoule|status/1329148579596935170|[[Charles Soule]]|quote=There's a chance|archiveurl=https://archive.is/mqzub}} {{C|In response to: "Okay, so to be honest I can't tell if this it [sic] a joking "Sure there's a *chance*" and it'll just be left at that or if you're waiting on a follow-up. But if it's the latter, I'm mostly curious about the lightning strike and whether it was something accidentally created by Ben's anger, or if it was Palpatine's work as most people including me thought after first reading it."}}</ref>
