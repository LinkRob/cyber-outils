'''Amy Richau''' is the author of the [[2021]] book ''[[Star Wars: I Love You. I Know.]]'', published by [[Dorling Kindersley]].<ref name="Love">''[[Star Wars: I Love You. I Know.]]''</ref> She is also a contributor to [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="SW.com" />, ''[[Star Wars Insider]],''<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|199|A Seat on the Council}}</ref> and since 2022 is the Managing Editor for the official [[Star Wars Celebration (article)|Star Wars Celebration]] Commemorative Guide<ref>{{LinkedIn|url=posts/amyrichau-freelance-writer-editor-research-entertainment-reference-denver-boulder_swce-2023-commemorative-program-guide-activity-7079560027501457408-OZ-q/|text=I'm really proud of my work as Managing Editor for the last two Star Wars Celebration Commemorative Guides.}}</ref>. She created a ''[[Star Wars]]'' fansite dedicated to the women of ''Star Wars'': "365 Star Wars".<ref name="SW.com" />
