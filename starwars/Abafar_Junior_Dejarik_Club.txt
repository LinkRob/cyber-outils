The '''Abafar Junior Dejarik Club''' was an organization found in the [[city]] of [[Pons Ora]] on the [[planet]] [[Abafar]]. Marketed towards [[Youngling|youth]], the club focused on the [[Hologram|holographic]] game of [[Dejarik]]. At the time of the [[Clone Wars]], the club had advertisements placed throughout the settlement which read "ABAFAR JUNIOR DEJARIK CLUB, NO [[Wookiee|WOOKIEES]]!" in [[Aurebesh]]. The club had at least one ad inside [[Power Sliders]], a local [[restaurant]].<ref name="Missing in Action" />
