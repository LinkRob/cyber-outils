The '''Fortress Dustbowl''' was a modified [[blaster rifle]] utilized by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Firestorm Rifle|standard rifle]]. This [[blaster]] [[rifle]] featured a metallic [[Color|gray]] and brown frame, with white and yellow paint, and a glowing yellow element on the scope.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fortress Dustbowl was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Imara Vex's Firestorm Rifle, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cityworld Prototype]]. Unlike most Weapon Wraps in the game, Fortress Dustbowl did not share the same rarity as its cosmetic set counterpart, with Cityworld Prototype instead being a "Legendary" rarity "Costume." This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing the Bounty Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
