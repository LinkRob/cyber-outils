The '''EasyRide passenger airspeeder''',<ref name="DI3.0">''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]''</ref> was a model of open-air [[repulsorcraft]] [[airspeeder]] manufactured by the [[Hyrotii Corporation]]. The EasyRide model was comparable to Hyrotii's [[ComfortRide passenger airspeeder]],<ref name="2018Vehicles" /> with the main difference being that the EasyRide was shorter in length.<ref name="HC">{{TCW|Hostage Crisis}}</ref> Both the EasyRide<ref name="2020Vehicles" /> and the ComfortRide<ref name="2018Vehicles" /> were commonly used as [[air taxi]]s on the [[ecumenopolis]] [[Coruscant]] which gave both the names '''Coruscant air taxis'''<ref name="DB">{{Databank|coruscant-air-taxi|Coruscant Air Taxi}}</ref><ref name="2018Vehicles" /> or '''Coruscant taxi''' and were one of the planet's most characteristic sights. They were piloted by trained taxi drivers to ferry their passengers from one destination to another and were allowed unrestricted "free travel" that allowed them to leave the official autonavigated skylanes that confined most other vehicles on long journeys along defined corridors to prevent unmanageable traffic chaos<ref name="2020Vehicles" /> and also utilized [[airbus]] lanes.<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the films, the EasyRide passenger airspeeder was realized as both a [[Wikipedia:Computer-generated imagery|computer-generated]] model and a full-sized [[Wikipedia:Mockup|mockup]].<ref name="OldDB">{{DB|vehicle|coruscantairtaxi|Coruscant air taxi}}</ref>

The "Coruscant Air Taxi" entry in the new [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] uses an image of a ComfortRide passenger airspeeder, but lists<ref name="DB" /> the dimensions of the smaller EasyRide passenger airspeeder,<ref name="2020Vehicles" /> with the text having been copied from the site's previous [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|Encyclopedia]] which had used a correct image of an EasyRide.<ref name="SWcomE">{{SWE|technology|coruscantairtaxi|Coruscant air taxi}}</ref>
