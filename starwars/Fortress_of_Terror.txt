"'''Fortress of Terror'''" is an adventure seed contained in the ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]''. A dark tower for priests aligned with the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side of the Force]] must be destroyed. Possible alternate storylines that could be adapted by a gamemaster were also included. It was written by [[Bill Olmesdahl]].
