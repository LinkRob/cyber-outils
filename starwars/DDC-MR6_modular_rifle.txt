The '''DDC-MR6 modular rifle''' was a model of modular [[blaster rifle]] that was built with an electromagnetic rail system that allowed users to quickly reconfigure the rifle for different situations. The rail system consisted of electromagnetic strips along the body of the weapon, which mean attachments could be swapped quickly even during battle. The rifle was also equipped with a [[stun]] setting.<ref name="SM" />

The DDC-MR6 was produced by [[Drearian Defense Conglomerate]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Due to the rifle's high cost, the Separatists only ordered a single experimental manufacturing run before ultimately deciding to use cheaper alternatives. By the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the rifles were fairly rare and cost 1000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. The few that were still in circulation were mostly held by collectors or [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] with a specific need for a versatile weapon that could cover a variety of their different needs.<ref name="SM" />

==Behind the scenes==
The DDC-MR6 modular rifle was first mentioned in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Special Modifications]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="SM" />
