The '''Flynn-Fletcher family''' of [[human]]s lived on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Phineas" />

==History==
===Missing family===
The Flynn family lived on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Around this time, [[Linda Flynn-Fletcher]] gave birth to a newborn son [[Phineas Flynn]]. Unfortunately, [[Candace]] left the family and joined the [[Galactic Empire]] shortly after, and was never heard of again by the rest of the family. For a while, Phineas grew up without a sister.<ref name="Phineas" />

===A new family===
Sometime after Candace's disappearance, Linda remarried to a new husband [[Lawrence Fletcher]]. Lawrence had a son of his own prior to the marriage [[Ferb Fletcher]].<ref name="Phineas" />
