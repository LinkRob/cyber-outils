The '''''Daisy Mae''''' was a unique customized [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] owned by [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]].<ref name="Exit from Endor" />

==History==
===The Abduction of R2-D2===
Shortly after the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] was preparing to leave with the droids [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]] to [[Mandalore]] to liberate some [[battle droid]]s. When [[Luke Skywalker]] and the others wanted to hear 3PO's stories of his time with the [[Galactic Republic]], Ackbar was polishing the ship and encouraged 3PO to tell them. After retelling the story about his history with [[Anakin Skywalker]] and the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the [[Clone Wars]], a [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|mysterious figure]] abducted R2 and stole the ''Daisy Mae''. C-3PO and Ackbar were forced to use another ship to go after them.<ref name="Exit from Endor" /> They would later find the ship on [[Coruscant]], hidden under a pile of [[paper]], but it was taken away by a tow truck and the two [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]] would part ways.<ref name="Crisis on Coruscant">{{DroidTales|Crisis on Coruscant}}</ref> Ackbar would later arrive on [[Geonosis]] with the ship and use it to rescue the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' from [[Maximilian Veers' giant B1 Battle droid|a giant B1 battle droid]] controlled by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]].<ref name="Gambit on Geonosis">{{DroidTales|Gambit on Geonosis}}</ref>

===Service under the Resistance===
During the [[Rescue of Admiral Ackbar]], the ''Daisy Mae'' would be used by its owner and C-3PO to escape the [[First Order]] after freeing Ackbar from the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Subjugator (Resurgent-class)|''Subjugator'']].<ref name="Poe to the Rescue" /> The ship would later be present at the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance base|base]] on [[D'Qar]] around [[34 ABY]].<ref name="LEGO Star Wars: TFA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The design of the ''Daisy Mae'' is derived from the [[2015]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set [[:w:c:brickipedia:75087 Anakin's Custom Jedi Starfighter|75087 Anakin's Custom Jedi Starfighter]].<ref name="75087AnakinStarfighter">{{LEGOCite|eu|75087|Anakin's Custom Jedi Starfighter}}</ref> The set itself is a depiction of the ''[[Azure Angel]]'', a modified [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor/Legends|Delta-7 ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] piloted by [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="CW">''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]''</ref>
