'''Codo Keburr''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Thisspiasian]] with a white beard. Sometime during the [[Imperial Era]], he, [[Killaen Chamat]], and [[Omi Vass]] searched a [[temple]] on [[Malian]] for a [[holocron]]. Keburr was wounded by a [[HK Guardian Droid/Canon|guardian droid]].<ref name="Unlimited Power" />
