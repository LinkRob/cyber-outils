'''Chilla Zin''' was a four-armed [[Voridsec]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Gender|male]] who wielded four blades and was a member of the [[Assassin's Guild]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Alongside the [[droid]] [[G-90|Gee-Ninety]] and the [[sniper]] [[Loriach]],<ref name="Vader 18" /> he<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> was hired by the [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]] to [[Assassination on Vincorba|assassinate]] a [[Crimson Dawn]]&ndash;allied [[Unidentified pontiff|pontiff]] on [[Vincorba]]. Unknown to Zin and his associates, the job was a test from Ochi's master, the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who [[Vader's crew|recruited]] them in his quest to destroy Crimson Dawn.<ref name="Vader 18" />
