The '''Astenban system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Seswenna sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square M-18 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the moon [[Astenban]].<ref name="Edge of Balance 3" />
