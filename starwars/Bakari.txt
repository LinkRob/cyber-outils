'''Bakari''' was a [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]] stationed at the [[Bromlarch outpost|Jedi outpost]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Bromlarch]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Shortly after the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Valo Atrocity|attacked]] the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Valo]]'s [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] in [[231 BBY]], they responded to [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]]'s [[Comm unit|comm]] for assistance. Not long afterward, Bakari [[Pilot|flew]] a [[Jedi Vector]] during a [[Second Battle of Cyclor|battle]] against the Nihil at the [[Cyclor Shipyards]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|All wings, report in.|Stellan Gios, to Bakari and other Jedi at the Cyclor Shipyards|<ref name="TRS" />}}
[[File:StellanGios-SWTLC.png|thumb|right|245px|Bakari responded to Stellan Gios's (pictured) call for help on Valo and later flew under his command at the Cyclor Shipyards.]]
As a [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]], the [[Force-sensitive]] individual Bakari was stationed at the [[Bromlarch outpost|Jedi outpost]] on<ref name="TRS" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="QS">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref> [[Bromlarch]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. When the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization [[Valo Atrocity|attacked]] the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Valo]]'s [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]]<ref name="TRS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> communications to and from the [[Republic Fair|fair]] were inoperable until Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] repaired the world's [[Crashpoint Tower|comm tower]]. Afterward, [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] called on his fellow Jedi over [[Comm unit|comms]] from the ground on Valo to coordinate reinforcements. Bakari promptly responded to the summons alongside their colleagues, indicating where they were stationed on Bromlarch at the [[time]].<ref name="TRS" />

Not long after, Bakari joined their fellow Jedi at the [[Cyclor Shipyards]] [[orbit]]ing the Mid Rim planet [[Cyclor]] to [[Second Battle of Cyclor|engage]] the Nihil under Gios's [[Commanding officer|command]], having duped the Nihil [[Tempest Runner]] [[Pan Eyta]] into believing the location would be unguarded. Before their enemy arrived, the Jedi responded to a roll call while in their [[Jedi Vector]] [[Starfighter|fighters]]. Having been well-prepared for the attack, the fighting between the Nihil and the combined forces of the Jedi, the [[Republic Defense Coalition]], and their other allies was short, resulting in a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] victory over [[Pan Eyta's Tempest]].<ref name="TRS" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a member of the Jedi Order, Bakari could wield [[the Force]]. They were capable of [[pilot]]ing during [[Starfighter combat|combat]] situations, and responded to Gios's call for reinforcements during a deadly attack on Valo.<ref name="TRS" />

==Equipment==
During the battle at the Cyclor [[Shipyard]]s, Bakari flew a Jedi Vector, a swift and nimble type of [[Starship|vessel]] designed specifically for Jedi use.<ref name="TRS" />
