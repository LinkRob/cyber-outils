'''Drophun III''' was an astronomical object<ref name="TROS VD" /> located in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]]'s<ref name="Interior" /> [[Drophun system]]. It was situated in grid square L-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[human]] [[Clemitt Polus]] was [[Reproduction|born]] at Drophun III, serving as a local defense [[pilot]] before joining the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drophun III was mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
