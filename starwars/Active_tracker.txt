{{Quote|Hyperspace tracking is new tech, but the principle must be the same as any active tracker.|[[Rose Tico]]|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
An '''active tracker''' was a form of technology used to track [[starship]]s. By the [[First Order-Resistance War]] the [[First Order]] had created the [[Hyperspace tracker]]. This allowed them to track enemy vessels through [[hyperspace]], which had been impossible before. All active trackers were [[A-class process]]es, so they had their own dedicated outbreakers and were controlled by a starship's [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />
