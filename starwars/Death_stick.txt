A '''death stick''' was a highly addictive illicit substance readily found in the underworld of [[Coruscant]] and other locales across [[the galaxy]]. Delivered inside luminescent sticks, they were a favorite among desperate addicts and thrill seekers.<ref name="Death-Sticks-Databank">{{Databank|death-sticks|Death Sticks}}</ref> Death sticks were created from distilled and refined [[ixetal cilona]], made from the [[balo mushroom]]s found on the [[planet]] [[Balosar]].<ref name="Cyphers">''[[Cyphers and Masks]]''</ref>

==History==
Death sticks were used as early<ref name="Light Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> as [[232 BBY]]<ref name="Light Jedi date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]]. In that year, death sticks and other intoxicants were available to the assembled ranks of the [[Nihil]] within their [[Great Hall of the Nihil|Great Hall]] as part of a celebratory feast after the [[attack on the Garello convoy]].<ref name="Light Jedi" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref>''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> [[Ovarto Bitolo-Bash]] smoked a death stick, even though he wasn't supposed to smoke on the job.<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> [[Rael Averross]], despite being a [[Jedi]], sometimes used death sticks.<ref name="Master & Apprentice">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref>

[[File:Sleazebaggano_mind_tricked.png|thumb|left|200px|Elan Sel'Sabagno trying to sell death sticks to Obi-Wan Kenobi]]
While on the [[planet]] Coruscant ten years after the [[Invasion of Naboo]], and attempting to track down [[Zam Wesell]], an [[assassin]] that had targeted the [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] entered the [[Outlander Club]] in Coruscant's [[Uscru Entertainment District]]. Inside, he was approached by [[Elan Sel'Sabagno]], who attempted to sell him death sticks. Kenobi, however, used a [[Mind trick|Jedi mind trick]] to convince him both that he did not want to sell him death sticks and that he wanted to go home and rethink his life.<ref name="AOC" /> The human [[Enric Pryde]] used death sticks until he joined the [[Galactic Empire]], for which he gave up his use of the drug in order to fit his idea of what an [[Imperial officer]] should be.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Though the death sticks are illicit substances, the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry of Elan Sleazebaggano lists death sticks as a weapon.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|elan-sleazebaggano|Elan Sleazebaggano}}</ref>
