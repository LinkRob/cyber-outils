'''Evergrass tea''' was a type of [[tea]] [[Diet|potable]] by [[Evereni]] and [[human]]s.<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the Evereni [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] [[Children of the Open Hand|member]] [[Yana Ro]] visited<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> [[Elecia Zeveron]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref>&mdash;a human who was the Path's leader and was also known as the [[Mother (title)|Mother]]&mdash;in Zeveron's [[The Mother's listening room|listening room]] in the [[Path compound]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dalna]].<ref name="Path of Deceit" />

While the two conversed, a Path [[Elder (Path of the Open Hand)|Elder]] brought them a tray with evergrass tea and [[sweet roll]]s, which Ro initially turned down. After the Mother poured them both a cup of the [[Foodstuff|tea]], Ro took an overlarge sip of the beverage, while Zeveron took a delicate sip of her own.<ref name="Path of Deceit" />

==Behind the scenes==
Evergrass tea appeared in the [[2022]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and [[Tessa Gratton]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase II">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> Within the novel, the tea appeared in a chapter written by Ireland.<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref>
