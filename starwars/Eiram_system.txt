The '''Eiram system''',<ref name="THR 12">[[The High Republic (2021) 12|''The High Republic'' (2021) 12]]</ref> also known as the '''Eiram-E'ronoh system''',<ref name="Convergence" /> was an [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[star system]] on the [[Galactic Frontier]] containing the [[Double planet|twin]] [[planet]]s of [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]]. The system held a gateway that was a waypoint through [[hyperspace]], causing the inhabitants of the two worlds to compete over control of the region,<ref name="Into the Dark" /> occasionally [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|going to war]] against each other. [[Eirie]],<ref name="Convergence" /> a [[moon]] that had once belonged to either Eiram or E'ronoh, eventually drifted free of its [[orbit]], coming to rest in the dead [[Realspace|space]] between the planets. During the [[High Republic Era]], a [[Eiram–E'ronoh crisis|crisis]] broke out after rulers from Eiram and E'ronoh were kidnapped by the [[Directorate]]. Twenty-five [[Standard year|years]] later, the Eiram system hosted the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]].<ref name="Into the Dark" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Eiram system first appeared in the short story "[[Starlight: Go Together|Starlight: Part One: Go Together]]" by [[Charles Soule]], published in [[Star Wars Insider 199|''Star Wars Insider'' 199]].<ref name="Starlight">{{InsiderCite|issue1=199|issue2=200|Starlight: Go Together}}</ref> In the excerpt of the [[The High Republic (2021) 1|first issue]] of [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Volume 1]] published in ''[[The High Republic Free Digital Sampler]]'', the Beacon was located in an area called the [[Starless Expanse]],<ref name="Sampler">''[[The High Republic Free Digital Sampler]]''</ref> but the term was removed in the final comic.<ref name="THR 1">[[The High Republic (2021) 1|''The High Republic'' (2021) 1]]</ref>
