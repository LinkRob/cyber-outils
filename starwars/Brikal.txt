The '''brikal''' was a [[Color|blue]]-shelled [[arthropod]] found on the [[planet]] [[Dalna]], which was used by the [[Path of the Open Hand]] to make the blue paint that members wore on their faces. The paint was created by crushing the shells and mixing them with [[chalk]] and a sweet-smelling binding agent. [[Diet|Eating]] the larvae of the arthropod could turn other animals, such as [[honey glider]]s, the same bright blue [[color]].<ref name="Path" />
