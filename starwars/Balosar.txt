{{Quote|I promised you the stars when we first met, but I couldn't give you that on Balosar, where the pollution blots out the night sky.|[[Stolak Vandello]], to [[R'Tess]]|<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5">[[Halcyon Legacy 5|''Halcyon Legacy'' 5]]</ref>}}
'''Balosar''' was a ruined [[planet]]<ref name="CandM" /> in the [[Balosar system]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Balosar (species)|Balosar]] [[species]].<ref name="Balosar">{{Databank|balosar|Balosar}}</ref> Balosar was known for its endemic [[balo mushroom]]s, which were refined by companies such as [[BioTech Industries]] and [[Fergriss Pharmaceuticals]] into [[ixetal cilona]], a powerful narcotic used in [[death stick]]s and interrogation drugs.<ref name="CandM" />

The planet was dominated by [[Factory|factories]] owned by the aforementioned companies, who often engaged in espionage and sabotage against one another, often with the native Balosar involved. Many Balosar on the world lived in poverty due to the companies' stranglehold on the planet, and as such many turned to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] to survive and fight back against the [[Galactic Empire]], who turned a blind eye to what was occurring on the world.<ref name="CandM" /> The planet was polluted, blotting out stars in the night sky.<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5" />
