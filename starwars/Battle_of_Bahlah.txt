A '''battle''' took place at the [[Stronghold of the Morubas crime family]], on [[Bahlah]] between [[Maul]]'s [[Shadow Collective]] and the [[Morubas Crime Family]]. The crime family, led by [[Jjan]], had taken Maul hostage and the Shadow Collective, led by Maul's brother [[Savage Opress]] arrived to rescue. But Maul had intentionally allowed for [[Gender|himself]] to be captured in order to easily get into the stronghold. Maul was able to free himself and fought off the guards. Opress and his fighters managed to infiltrate the stronghold and take out the remaining guards, leaving Jjan to pick up the remains of his once profitable criminal family.

==Prelude==
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> the former [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Maul]] had formed the [[Shadow Collective]] to disrupt the plans of his former [[Sith Master]], [[Darth Sidious]] and to compete with Sidious for galactic domination. During this time Maul and his brother, [[Savage Opress]] rallied the forces of the [[Death Watch]], the [[Black Sun]], the [[Pyke Syndicate]], the [[Hutt Clan]], and the [[Nightbrothers]].<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref> He then moved onto the [[Morubas Crime Family]].<ref name="The Hostage" />

While at a [[cantina]], Maul told his brother that he will gain the favor of the leader of the Morubas crime family, Jjan by presenting him with an offer too good to refuse. After they had earned his favor they would [[Death|kill]] him and take the families [[soldier]]s and [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] for themselves. But a patron at the bar snitched on Maul to Jjan who then prepared to [[poison]] him when he arrived.<ref name="The Hostage" />

Shortly after at the [[Stronghold of the Morubas crime family]], Jjan held a [[Toast (ritual)|toast]] with Maul. When Maul took a sip of his beverage he got poisoned by it. Jjan told him that it was a sedative and ordered his soldiers to subdue and disarm him.<ref name="The Hostage" />

==The battle==
[[File:Maul_fights_soldiers.png|thumb|left|150px|Maul fought off the soldiers sent after him]]
Outside of the stronghold, Opress lead the Collective fighters in breaking down the large door. The fighters fired on the door as Opress ordered for the fighters to use more firepower to bring down the defenses.<ref name="The Hostage" />

As the attack increased, Jjan boasted to Maul about his capture and all his family's achievements. But Maul believed that Opress and his fighters will breach his defenses that Jjan believed to be impenetrable. But Maul noted that he had allowed him in. He then broke his resistraints and used [[The Force]] to reach for [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]]. He then proceeded to take out all the soldiers sent after him and revealed that he had allowed for his capture in order to gain access into the stronghold.<ref name="The Hostage" />

After all the soldiers were killed, Opress appeared and reported to Maul that they had breached the defenses and were spreading their forces throughout the stronghold. The fighters were taking out small pockets of resistance but would gain full control of the place within the [[Standard hour|hour]]. Maul then proclaimed that he had taken the Morubas crime family's power.<ref name="The Hostage" />

==Aftermath==
After the battle,<ref name="The Hostage" /> the forces of the Shadow Collective banded together to [[Takeover of Mandalore|takeover]] the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]].<ref name="Shades of Reason">{{TCW|Shades of Reason}}</ref> But his rule of the planet would come to an end when his former master, Darth Sidious arrived and [[Battle of Mandalore|dueled]] Maul and his brother. Opress would be killed and Maul was captured.<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref>
