'''Delsh Oquan''' was [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] operative during the [[Clone Wars]]. Delsh Oquan went on a [[Mission on Leyoye Prime|mission]] on [[Leyoye Prime]] in a unit comprised [[Poan of Riek]], [[Baby Yezz]], [[Seebee One]], [[Bargaz Barnok]], and [[Alvadorjian]] [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]]. Delsh Oquan perished during the mission.<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Delsh Oquan appeared in a memory in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
