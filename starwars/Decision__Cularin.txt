"'''Decision: Cularin'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year four of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the third adventure in the [[Decisions trilogy]] and was written by [[Ronald A. Heintz|Ron Heintz]].

==Plot summary==
Although they have been ordered to leave the [[Cularin system/Legends|Cularin system]], the [[Thaereian military]] have refused and the heroes must help the [[Cularin Militia]] drive them out.
