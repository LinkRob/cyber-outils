'''Brisksaber''' is a game for mobile devices by Brisk and Star Wars. Brisksaber allows battles as [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]], [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]] and many other characters from the new 3D version of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. Brisksaber has a blend of both 2D and 3D elements and has the option for players to choose the [[Light side of the Force/Legends|Light Side]] or the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|Dark Side]]. Unlocking new characters or objects require codes found under the caps of specially marked packs of Brisk 1-Liter Iced Tea.
