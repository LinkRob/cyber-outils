The '''Defense Council building''' was the headquarters of the [[New Republic]] [[Senate Defense Council]] on the [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. It was located in a [[skyscraper]] surrounded by [[repulsor platform]]s and a number of pedestrian [[skybridge]]s connected to multiple levels of the building. It prominently featured the [[Great Galactic Seal]] on its top and a large New Republic flag above an entrance. The skyscraper was part of a complex that included<ref name="Part 7">{{Ahsoka|Part Seven: Dreams and Madness}}</ref> the [[New Republic Coruscant skyscraper|headquarters]] of the [[New Republic Amnesty Program]],<ref name="Chapter 19">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 19: The Convert}}</ref> as the two were built side by side and directly connected with each other.<ref name="Part 7" />
