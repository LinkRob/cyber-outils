'''Darth Shaa''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[humanoid]] [[Sith Lord|Sith Lady]] with the title of [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]. After hearing of a [[Force-sensitive]] [[sculptor]] named [[Darth Momin|Momin]] who had been imprisoned by his people, [[Lady]] Shaa went to free him, and made him her apprentice. Eventually, she was [[Death|slain]] by her apprentice. She carried two red [[Darth Shaa's lightsabers|Sith lightsabers]].

==Biography==
===Training Momin===
{{Quote|From the beginning, Lady Shaa called herself my master and considered me her apprentice. I never understood this.|Darth Momin, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />}}
[[File:ShaasDeath-2017DarthVader22.png|thumb|left|200px|Shaa's death at the hands of her apprentice, Momin]]
Darth Shaa<ref name="SW Book" /> was a [[Gender|female]] [[humanoid]]<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> [[Sith]] who lived prior to the Sith [[Rule of Two]] doctrine,<ref name="SW Book" /> which was created by [[Darth Bane]] at the end of the [[Jedi-Sith War]] and the reformation of the [[Galactic Republic]]. She eventually became a [[Sith Lord|Lady]] of the Sith<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> and acquired the title of [[Dark Lord of the Sith]],<ref name="Pocket Expert">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side Pocket Expert]]''</ref> as well as two [[Color|red]] [[Sith lightsaber]]s. During her [[time]], she heard tales of [[Darth Momin|Momin]], a [[Force-sensitive]] individual who was [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] for his creations. Shaa freed Momin, [[Death|killing]] the guards and cutting through to his cell, taking him as her [[Sith apprentice]]. Over time, she trained Momin, who was able to learn much from her<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> and also gained the title of [[Darth]],<ref name="SW Book" /> constructing [[Darth Momin's lightsabers|his own]] lightsabers. During Darth Momin's apprenticeship, he created the [[Mask of Lord Momin]] and mutilated his own face.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

Although under Darth Shaa's tutelage, Darth Momin was never able to understand why Lady Shaa called herself Momin's [[Sith Master|master]] while he was her apprentice. Momin knew that he would never be bold enough to call himself a master, but believed that being called an apprentice was reprehensible. Shaa disagreed, and during a [[lightsaber duel]], Momin proved to Shaa that she was wrong, impaling her mid-air in the abdomen with one of his lightsabers and killing her<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> around between 1,100 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[1000 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|I never took an apprentice of my own. I had no time to teach. There was too much to learn.|Darth Momin, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />}}
After Shaa's death, Momin studied the lore of the dark side of the Force by visiting its many great sites. Through his studies, Momin realized that his audience was not the people of [[the galaxy]], but rather the Force itself. Although Momin swore never to take an apprentice, he maintained a [[Darth Momin's acolytes|group of acolytes]] who helped him in his goals. Eventually, using resources he had inherited from Shaa, Momin created a [[Darth Momin's superweapon|superweapon]] to [[Darth Momin's destruction of a city|destroy]] a [[Unidentified city (Momin)|city]], but was stopped by the [[Jedi Order]]. Momin's physical body was destroyed during the events, but his spirit remained in the mask.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

At some point, the [[Kho Phon Farrus' cape|raiment]] once owned by Shaa was acquired by [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]], leader of the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate. [[The Archivist]] offered it to [[Kho Phon Farrus]] as incentive for continuing to attempt to release an [[Unidentified Sith Lord (Fermata Cage)|ancient Sith Lord]] within the [[Fermata Cage]]. Farrus accepted the trade even knowing the risks it entailed toward their life.<ref name="Hidden Empire 2">[[Hidden Empire 2|''Hidden Empire'' 2]]</ref>

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="SotS date">The flashback in which Shaa is mentioned in ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' takes place concurrently with [[Darth Vader (2020) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 11]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Sith cultists belonging to the [[Sith Eternal]] repeated Shaa's name in something similar to a chant, along with the names of other Sith while searching the catacombs beneath the [[Sith Citadel]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]].<ref name="SotS">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Momin is second to none. Lady Shaa disagreed, and I saw only one way to prove it to her.|Darth Momin, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />}}
[[File:MominAndShaa-2017DarthVader22.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Shaa took Momin as her apprentice]]
Darth Shaa<ref name="SW Book" /> was a female humanoid who eventually rose to become a lady. She had brown [[hair]], black [[eye]]s, and grey skin. After hearing of Momin's abilities, she decided to seek him out and believed she could take him as her apprentice. But Shaa's faith in Momin ultimately proved to be her undoing: finding the role of apprentice distasteful, Momin killed Shaa by impaling her with one of his lightsabers.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|The Lady Shaa freed me and told me I was now her apprentice. I learned much from her. About the Force, and its dark side.|Darth Momin, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />}}
Darth Shaa<ref name="SW Book" /> was able to effectively use a pair of lightsabers in combat, using a reverse grip on one [[Limb|hand]] while using a standard grip on the other at times. Shaa was able to duel with Momin for a time, but was not able to foresee that he would take the opportunity to murder her during the duel. Shaa had a great knowledge of the Force and its dark side, and was able to pass on that knowledge to Momin.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

==Equipment==
Darth Shaa utilized a pair of red Sith lightsabers and a [[helmet]] that concealed her face. She also used an outfit that donned spikes and a [[cape]].<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Shaa debuted in the [[2018]] [[canon]] comic [[Darth Vader (2017) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 22]] in a flashback. The comic was written by [[Charles Soule]] and illustrated by [[Giuseppe Camuncoli]].<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> She was identified as Darth Shaa in the [[2020]] reference book ''[[The Star Wars Book]]''.<ref name="SW Book" />
