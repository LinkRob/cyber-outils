The '''Fellio Asteroid Belt''' was an [[Asteroid field|asteroid belt]] located<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> in the [[Fellio system]] within the [[Cadavine sector]] of<ref name="SWSystems" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> and [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing Sectors" /> It was situated in grid square P-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWSystems" />

At some point, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] formed a [[Rebel base (Fellio Asteroid Belt)|base]] in the [[asteroid]] belt, from which the rebels, which included a number of [[human]]s, communicated with their undercover operative<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> [[Gita]], who was disguised as the [[Tusken Raider]] "Urrr'k,"<ref name="Target Vader 5">[[Target Vader 5|''Target Vader'' 5]]</ref> and was eventually recruited by the [[Hidden Hand]] criminal syndicate.<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Target Vader 1|''Target Vader'' 1]] to [[1 BBY]].</ref> she contacted the rebels at the base and informed them that the [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] of [[bounty hunter]]s she had joined was beginning its [[Hunt for Vader|hunt]] for the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The rebel who received the message at the base then passed it on to Gita's superior, [[Aeliar]], who also resided at the base at the [[time]].<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fellio Asteroid Belt appeared in the [[Target Vader 1|first issue]] of the [[2019]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]''. The issue was written by [[Robbie Thompson]], illustrated by [[Marc Laming]] and [[Cris Bolson]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> on [[July 3]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/77664/star_wars_target_vader_2019_1|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #1}}</ref>
