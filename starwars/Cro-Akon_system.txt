The '''Cro-Akon system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Thuldin's Grant sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was situated in grid square J-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained [[Cro-Akon]], an astronomical object with an [[Water|icy]] tundra.<ref name="TLoLS" /> Between [[0 ABY]] and [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date 5">"I, Droid" depicts [[Luke Skywalker]] using the [[Skywalker lightsaber]], which he received in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] and lost in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places these stories in [[0 ABY]] and [[3 ABY]] respectively, meaning that "I, Droid" takes place between these two [[Standard year|years]].</ref> a construction crew worked on building a new settlement in the tundra, but were attacked by [[Slavery|enslavers]] led by the [[Tectozin]] [[U'rum]] who captured the [[construction droid]] [[Zeta]].<ref name="TLoLS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cro Akon system first appeared in a flashback in the [[2017]] novel ''[[The Legends of Luke Skywalker]]'', which was written by [[Ken Liu]].<ref name="TLoLS" /> It was then depicted for the first time in the "[[I, Droid]]" portion of the [[2020]] [[The Legends of Luke Skywalker—The Manga|manga adaptation]] of the novel, which was written and illustrated by [[Haruichi]].<ref name="Manga">{{StoryCite|story=I, Droid|book=The Legends of Luke Skywalker&mdash;The Manga}}</ref> The system was first identified in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
