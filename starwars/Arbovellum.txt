'''Arbovellum''' was a type of stationery [[paper]] used on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]].<ref name="QP" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the [[Gender|girl]] [[Sabé|Tsabin]] received three acceptance letters [[Writing|written]] on arbovellum after leaving the [[Theed Conservatory]] [[music]] school, inviting her to sit on the back row of an orchestra. However, she felt that the letters were pretentiously written and threw them away, choosing instead to become a [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] of [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of Naboo.<ref name="QP" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arbovellum appeared in ''[[Queen's Peril]]'', a [[2020]] young-adult novel by [[E. K. Johnston]] as the second installment of ''[[Star Wars: Queen's Series]]''.<ref name="QP" />
