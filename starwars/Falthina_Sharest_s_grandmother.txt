A [[Gender|female]] individual was the '''grandmother''' of the [[Corellian]]<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> [[human]]<ref name="Solo Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> female [[Falthina Sharest]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> By [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the section of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]'' in which [[Falthina Sharest]]'s [[Gender|grandmother]] is mentioned to [[13 BBY]].</ref> Sharest worked as a [[Lead Transport Security Officer]] in [[Corellian Security Forces|CorSec]]&mdash;a [[Law enforcement agency|security force]] on the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Solo Official Guide" /> [[planet]] [[Corellia]]&mdash;and her grandmother often told her that the job was as dull as an old [[milk]] [[knife]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while on duty in [[Coronet City Spaceport]], Sharest [[Brain|mentally]] recalled her grandmother's feelings on her job.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />
