The '''Coruscant Eastern Spaceport''', also known as '''Eastport''' or the '''Coruscant Spaceport''', was one of the two major [[spaceport]]s on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and was located in the world's [[Port District (Coruscant)|Port District]]. Most newcomers to Coruscant passed through the immense industrialized area, which was intimidating to the average visitor but equipped to handle a diverse range of physiologies.

The spaceport's terminals were designed to be practical but with elegant flowing curves. Display boards with information for travelers were featured prominently, and movement throughout the spaceport was controlled by a strict code of conduct. The Coruscant Eastern Spaceport had a transit car terminal with many floors of narrow walkways. The port saw its busiest period during the [[Separatist Crisis]], which began in [[24 BBY]], when migrant traffic increased 6,000 percent and inter-[[species]] tensions in the port flared, leading to a massive [[Weequay-Houk brawl|brawl]] between [[Houk]] and [[Weequay]] immigrants.

In [[21 BBY|7956]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]], a shipment of tainted [[Ardees Beverage|Moogan tea]] was seized by the [[Coruscant Security Force]] from the [[Eastport Docks]], one of the many docking facilities in Eastport. In [[5 BBY]], former [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]] employee [[Syril Karn]] returned to Coruscant through the Coruscant Eastern Spaceport from the planet [[Morlana One]]. That same [[Standard year|year]], [[Axis network|rebel agent]] [[Kleya Marki]] arrived at the spaceport via transit car while on her way to meet fellow rebel [[Vel Sartha]].

==Description==
===Gateway to the galaxy===
{{Quote|Telgordo Travel service to Hosnian Prime, Plexis, and Eufornis Major will now be departing from platforms 7-12 and 7-13.|PA System|<ref name="Aldhani" />}}
[[File:SyrilSuitcases-Aldhani.png|thumb|right|300px|Coruscant Eastern Spaceport's terminal design was practical and elegant.]] 
The Coruscant Eastern Spaceport,<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> sometimes shortened to just Eastport<ref name="BTMF50" /> or the Coruscant Spaceport,<ref name="Insider219">{{InsiderCite|219|Galactic Britain II: Cassian's Travels}}</ref> was a large [[spaceport]] district<ref name="BTMF50" /> that was one of the two major ports of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]], the [[ecumenopolis]] covering the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The other major port, [[Western spaceport|Westport]], acted as the planet's main departure point, while most newcomers passed through Eastport,<ref name="Coruscant" /> although it was also used as part of some travelers' [[Standard day|daily]] commute or as the start point for long journeys across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="CGttSWG" />

Both were located in the [[Port District (Coruscant)|Port District]] and served as more than just spaceports, consisting of immense industrialized areas with many port facilities of all kinds.<ref name="Coruscant" /> The [[Eastport Docks]] were one such facility,<ref name="Scum & Villainy" /> as were [[Platform 7-12|platforms 7-12]] and [[Platform 7-13|7-13]].<ref name="Aldhani" /> The Coruscant Eastern Spaceport was an enormous and intimidating space for the average visitor but was prepared to handle a diverse range of physiologies amongst those that passed through, with port authorities being as tolerant of cultural and social differences as was possible.<ref name="Coruscant" /> It accommodated the tens of thousands of passengers that passed through on a [[Local day|daily]] basis with many gates, wide, winding hallways, and large, airy atria. To keep the masses appraised of their journey's status or designated platform, the port featured arrivals and departures signs prominently.<ref name="CGttSWG" /> Multiple [[AA-9 Freighter-Liner]]s departed from the port to new destinations on an almost daily basis.<ref name="BTMF50" />

===Terminals and transit cars===
The Coruscant Eastern Spaceport's terminals were designed to combine practical functionality with elegant sweeping lines, with a strict code of conduct about where passengers should be walking and their direction of travel. This kept the crowds moving in an efficient manner.<ref name="CGttSWG" /> One such terminal complex consisted of a long, curved walkway beside the landing platforms with a series of floor-to-ceiling [[Viewport|windows]] facing out toward the incoming ships. A [[water]] feature followed the curve of the walkway on the exterior of the window's base, with an overhanging lip above the windows. Both the exterior and interior of the terminal were [[Color|white]] in color, with gray flooring. At least two large information displays were positioned above the walkway, with a narrow winding platform that hung above the walkway connected to one of the displays. Similar displays also lined the walls of a windowless portion of the walkway.<ref name="Aldhani" />

[[File:EastportTransitCarTerminal-Announcement.png|thumb|left|300px|Coruscant Eastern Spaceport had a transit car terminal.]]
The Coruscant Eastern Spaceport also included<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> a transit car<ref name="AnnouncementAD">{{Andor|Announcement}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> terminal where passengers could exit from the car onto one of many floors of narrow walkways. Directly in front of the arrival area was a circular desk manned by two [[protocol droid]]s at small terminals. At the desk's center was a [[Hologram|holographic]] transit car map and above the arrival area was a sign in [[Aurebesh]] warning travelers to mind the gap. The transit car terminal was connected to a windowless corridor with gray flooring, white walls, and a white ceiling. The corridor had strip lighting down its center, a row of small circular lights on either side, and gray grating that ran along the center of the corridor's walls.<ref name="Announcement" />

==History==
===Republic Era===
[[File:MooganTea-ScumAndVillainy.png|thumb|right|300px|A shipment of tainted Moogan tea was seized by the Coruscant Security Force in Eastport.]]
Coruscant's eastern and western spaceports both experienced the busiest time in their history during the [[Separatist Crisis]], in which many planets split from the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Coruscant" /> beginning in [[24 BBY]].<ref name="Dates">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'' states that the [[Separatist Crisis]] started two [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the start of the war in [[22 BBY]], the crisis must have begun in [[24 BBY]].</ref> This resulted in many citizens of Coruscant leaving to return to their newly independent [[homeworld]]s, but also caused an influx of [[refugee]]s from those same worlds. Over a period of two [[Standard year|years]], the ports saw an increase of 6,000 percent in migrant traffic. During such a tense period, the mixing of different [[species]] in the port inevitably created issues, with it not being unusual for port authorities to have to paralyze a temperamental [[Wookiee]] and an aggressive [[Trandoshan]],<ref name="Coruscant" /> species with a history of conflict,<ref name="Wookiees">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Wookiees]]''</ref> in order to separate them.<ref name="Coruscant" />

In one instance of inter-species tension, a massive and uncontrolled [[Weequay-Houk brawl|brawl]] broke out in Eastport between immigrant members of the [[Houk]] and [[Weequay]] species, leaving fifteen [[Death|dead]] and forty-five with serious injuries.<ref name="Coruscant" /> In [[21 BBY|7956]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] during the preceding [[Clone Wars]], [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[Inspector]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tanivos Divo]] confiscated a shipment containing several pallets of tainted [[Ardees Beverage|Moogan tea]] from the [[Ardees Beverage Company]] at Eastport Docks after receiving a tip from [[Mandalorian Guard|Sundari Police Authority]] [[Captain]] [[Patrok Ru-Saxon]]. The shipment had come from a [[Moogan]] [[Mooga smuggling ring|smuggling ring]] linked to a [[poison]]ing outbreak on the planet [[Mandalore]] and was destined for the [[Coruscant Underworld]] before being intercepted, preventing any harm.<ref name="Scum & Villainy" />

===Imperial Era and beyond===
In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="AldhaniDate">{{AndorDate|104}}</ref> [[Telgordo Travel]] operated in Eastport, offering [[transport]] to locations such as [[Hosnian Prime]], [[Plexis]], [[Eufornis Major]], [[Balfron]], [[Jonsior system|Jonsior]], [[Lanz Carpo]], [[Foerost]], [[Tepasi]], [[Zeltros]], [[Ord Mantell]], [[Cantonica]], [[Mawan]], [[Cato Neimoidia]], [[Obroa-skai]], and [[Sluis Van]]. After being fired from the [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation]] on<ref name="Aldhani" /> the planet<ref name="CGttSWG" /> [[Morlana One]], the [[human]] [[Syril Karn]] returned to Coruscant via the eastern spaceport, carrying two suitcases through one of its terminals. As he passed an [[astromech droid]] on the curved walkway, it was announced that Telgordo Travel's service to Hosnian Prime, Plexis, and Eufornis Major was departing from platforms 7-12 and 7-13.<ref name="Aldhani" /> 

[[File:EastportImperials-Announcement.png|thumb|left|300px|Kleya Marki passed stormtroopers in Eastport.]]
Later that year,<ref name="AnnouncementDate">{{AndorDate|102}}</ref> the [[Axis network|rebel agent]] [[Kleya Marki]] arrived in the Coruscant Eastern Spaceport via a transit car, exiting into the station alongside a crowd of human and [[alien]] passengers. Joining a stream of other travelers, she passed several [[Clothing|uniformed]] staff members and then entered the windowless corridor, carrying on as<ref name="Announcement" /> a guard detail<ref name="AnnouncementAD" /> of four [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s and an [[Imperial officer]] watched both Marki's orderly line and another line heading in the opposite direction. Marki then passed a holographic advert for [[peezo]]s and a reminder to carry [[chain code]]s from the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] before exiting the spaceport to meet with fellow rebel [[Vel Sartha]].<ref name="Announcement" />

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="GhormanDate">{{AndorDate|205}}</ref> Marki's ally [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] also passed through the spaceport while traveling from Coruscant to [[Ghorman]]<ref name="Friends">{{Andor|I Have Friends Everywhere}}</ref> a planet<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> in the [[Colonies]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Ghorman system]]</ref> While traversing the spaceport's curved stairs and raised walkways, Andor used an earpiece to listen to details of a new cover identity he was to use on Ghorman.<ref name="Friends" /> Divo's [[Divo family|grandson]], [[Exantor Divo]], included information on the [[tea]] shipment seizure in the Coruscant Eastern Spaceport in his [[book]], ''[[Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'',<ref name="Scum & Villainy" /> which was published in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{ScumAndVillainyDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|What we did, then, was increase the height of the building. We were putting it many, many stories up from the ground, because a spaceship had to land right outside. And then I was thinking, ‘It'd be a real shame to paint all these caustic reflections out because they're so beautiful.' So, we actually designed a little moat that ran along the edge of the building, and just used the water from the plate and justified the caustics that way.|Scott Pritchard discussing the Coruscant spaceport|<ref name="Befores and Afters">{{WebCite|url=https://beforesandafters.com/2023/01/31/heres-how-industrial-light-magic-orchestrated-that-luthens-lightsaber-ship-in-andor|text=Here's how Industrial Light & Magic orchestrated Luthen's ‘lightsaber' ship in ‘Andor'|author=Failes, Ian|date=2023-01-31|work=[https://beforesandafters.com Befores and afters]|archivedate=20230201102819}}</ref>}}
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Coruscant Eastern Spaceport was first mentioned as Eastport in "AA-9 Coruscant Starfreighter," an article<ref name="BTMF50" /> written by [[Tim Veekhoven]]<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:Credits-TimVeekhoven-Twitter.png|creator=[[Tim Veekhoven]]}}</ref> in the "[[Starship Fact File]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 50|fiftieth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine published by [[De Agostini]]<ref name="BTMF50" /> around [[December 16]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|50}}</ref> The [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' then retroactively established that a spaceport<ref name="CGttSWG" /> that had first appeared in "[[Aldhani (episode)|Aldhani]]"&mdash;the fourth episode of the [[Andor Season 1|first season]] of the [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''<ref name="Aldhani" />&mdash;on [[September 28]], [[2022]],<ref name="Release dates">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1557442379975573504|[[Star Wars]]|date=2022-08-10|quote=This is what revolution looks like. Watch #Andor, a Star Wars Original Series, streaming only on @DisneyPlus September 21.|archivedate=20220810191942}}</ref> was the Coruscant Eastern Spaceport.<ref name="CGttSWG" />

[[File:McClarenSpaceport.png|thumb|right|300px|Coruscant Eastern Spaceport was filmed at the McLaren Technology Centre.]]
The spaceport had only previously been identified as the Coruscant spaceport by the [[StarWars.com]] Episode Guide for "Aldhani,"<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e4-trivia-gallery|text="Aldhani" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 7}}</ref> and then as Coruscant Spaceport by the article "[[Galactic Britain II: Cassian's Travels]]," which was written by [[Mark Newbold]] and [[Sander de Lange]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 219|''Star Wars Insider'' 219]]<ref name="Insider219" /> on [[June 13]], [[2023]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/131-star-wars-insider-219|text=Star Wars Insider #219}}</ref> The name [[Eastport/Legends|Eastport]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[1996]] novel ''[[Before the Storm]]'', which was written by [[Michael P. Kube-McDowell]].<ref name="BtS">''[[Before the Storm]]''</ref> It was depicted for the first time in the [[2010]] [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Kinect Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Kinect">''[[Kinect Star Wars]]''</ref> The name [[Coruscant Spaceport]] also separately originated in ''Star Wars'' Legends, appearing in [[BioWare]]'s [[2011]] massively multiplayer online roleplaying game ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.<ref name="SWTOR">{{TORcite|type=location|[[Coruscant Spaceport]]|[[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]}}</ref>

For ''Andor'', the spaceport was filmed at the [[Wikipedia:McLaren Technology Centre|McLaren Technology Centre]] in [[Wikipedia:Woking|Woking]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]]. [[Industrial Light & Magic]] visual effects supervisor [[Scott Pritchard]]'s team elevated the landing pad several stories above the ground because they figured a starship would have to land outside. Pritchard thought it would be a shame to paint out the beautiful caustic reflections, so the team designed a little moat that ran along the edge of the building. The team used the water from the plate and justified the caustics that way.<ref name="Befores and Afters" />
