A '''flight pack''' was a chest-mounted device worn by the [[Imperial ground crew|ground crews]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Color|Black]] in color, the device was equipped with [[sensor]]s that helped the ground crew members in their work and complemented their dark [[Clothing|jumpsuits]]. Ground crew member [[Kent Deezling]] wore a flight pack while working on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]], where it was [[Thief|stolen]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Jyn Erso]]<ref name="HC56" /> in [[1 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> when she took Deezling's entire [[Imperial military uniforms|uniform]] to use as a disguise.<ref name="HC56" />
