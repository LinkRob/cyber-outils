The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''22 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3255 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7955''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[Antar 4 schism]] occurs over whether to support the [[Galactic Republic]] or the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="GA" />
*A [[Border dispute on Ansion|border dispute]] occurs on [[Ansion]].<ref name="Episode II" />
*[[Zam Wesell]] and [[Jango Fett]] [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|attempt]] to [[assassin]]ate [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Episode II" />
*The [[Separatist Crisis]] ends and the [[Clone Wars]] begin with the [[First Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="Episode II" />
*[[Anakin Skywalker]] and Padmé Amidala are [[Marriage|married]] in a [[Wedding of Anakin Skywalker and Padmé Amidala|secret ceremony]] on [[Naboo]].<ref name="Episode II" />
*Anakin Skywalker is promoted to [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Knight|Knight]].<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref>
*Concurrent to the early period of the Clone Wars, [[RA-7 protocol droid]]s are created with plating from the [[3PO-series protocol droid]]. One of the [[droid]]s made during this [[time]] is [[4-A7]],<ref name="4-A7">{{BuildR2Cite|11|Droid Directory}} establishes that [[RA-7 protocol droid]]s that shared much of their plating with the [[3PO-series protocol droid]] were created early in the [[Clone Wars]]. Because the issue also says [[4-A7]] had a full body of 3PO plating, he must have been created early in the war. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates [[First Battle of Geonosis|the start]] of the Clone Wars to the [[Standard year|year]] 22 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. The [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]] shows that 4-A7's [[death]] was part of the [[Battle of Teth]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the [[Battle of Christophsis]] and the [[Mission to Rodia]] to 22 BBY. Since the Battle of [[Teth]] takes place between the Battle of [[Christophsis]] and the Mission to [[Rodia]], the events of the battle, including 4-A7's death, must have also taken place in the same year. Given that 4-A7 was created after the start of the war and died in 22 BBY, he must have been created in that year as well.</ref> who is actually assembled by Separatist allies within [[Arakyd Industries]].<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11">{{BuildR2Cite|11|Droid Directory}}</ref>
*The [[Battle of Muunilinst]] occurs; the Republic defeats the Separatist stronghold. However the victory comes at great cost: [[Muun]] [[City|cities]] were heavily damaged in the battle, and the engagement will lead the galaxy to face a financial crisis.<ref name="Build Falcon 45">{{BuildFalconCite|45|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>
*Jedi General [[Tholme]] leads his forces in a [[Battle of unidentified planet (Tholme and Ventress)|major battle]] against [[Asajj Ventress]] and her [[battle droid]]s. Ventress wins the day and murders Tholme.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref><ref name="Timeline">The [[Battle of unidentified planet (Tholme and Ventress)|battle]] between [[Tholme]] and [[Asajj Ventress]] seen in ''[[Dark Disciple]]'' occurs during the [[Clone Wars]], meaning the battle takes place some time after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] seen in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones]], which the [[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]] dates to [[22 BBY]]. Furthermore, {{EncyclopediaCite|Boba Fett and Other Characters of the Underworld}} establishes that the engagement was fought before the [[Battle of Christophsis]], which is also dated to 22 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] by ''Galactic Atlas''. Therefore, the battle between Tholme and Ventress, including Tholme's [[death]], must take place in 22 BBY.</ref>
*[[Mace Windu]] and his Jedi [[Mace Windu's squad|squad]] [[Operation on Hissrich|battles]] with the Separatists and the hired [[droid]] [[mercenary]] [[AD-W4]] over the planet [[Hissrich]].<ref name="Mace Windu 1">[[Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu 1|''Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu'' 1]]</ref>
*Anakin Skywalker destroys [[Trench|Admiral Trench's]] [[Invincible|flagship]] above the [[Battle of Christophsis]] and delivers needed supplies to senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] on the ground.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />
*[[Ahsoka Tano]] is assigned to Anakin Skywalker as his [[Padawan]] learner.<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>
*Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano rescue [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] son [[Rotta]] from Asajj Ventress.<ref name="TCW film" />
*[[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] the Hutt is imprisoned after Padmé Amidala exposes his role in the plot to kidnap Rotta.<ref name="TCW film" />
*[[General]] [[Grievous]] takes the [[warship]] ''[[Malevolence]]'' as his flagship and launches a [[Malevolence Campaign|campaign]] in which he brought about the destruction of multiple Republic fleets. The ship is later destroyed by Republic and Jedi forces.<ref name="Destroy">{{TCW|Destroy Malevolence|Destroy ''Malevolence''}}</ref>
*The [[Twi'lek]] homeworld of [[Ryloth]] is [[Invasion of Ryloth|invaded]] by Separatist forces led by [[Techno Union]] [[Foreman]] [[Wat Tambor]]. The Republic's [[Outer Rim garrison]] is destroyed in the ensuing invasion and the planet is occupied by Separatist forces.<ref name="Supply Lines">{{TCW|Supply Lines}}</ref>
*The planet [[Rodia]] rejoins the Republic after a successful [[Mission to Rodia|mission]] by Senator Padmé Amidala, Representative [[Jar Jar Binks]], and [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]].<ref name="Bombad">{{TCW|Bombad Jedi}}</ref>
