'''Che-Yuan Wang''' is credited for Level Editor and Gameplay Programming on ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]''. He did also do some work on ''[[Star Wars: DroidWorks]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Mysteries of the Sith]]''. Che-Yuan participated in other [[LucasArts]] titles such as ''[[Wikipedia:Grim Fandango]]'' and ''[[Wikipedia:Indiana Jones and the Infernal Machine]]''.
