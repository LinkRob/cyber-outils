'''Abran Balfour''' was a [[moff]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] whose [[Star Destroyer]]s often patrolled the [[Llanic Spice Run]] [[Hyperspace route|trade route]] in the [[Llanic system]]. Balfour represented the Empire the nearest to where the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] was stationed&mdash;in the [[Sujimis sector]]<ref name="Heir" />&mdash;in [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Heir to the Jedi]]'' to [[0 ABY]].</ref>

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] cautioned fellow Alliance member [[Luke Skywalker]], who was scheduled to travel to the Llanic [[Star system|system]], to avoid making Balfour wonder if the Rebel [[fleet]] was situated in the [[sector]] governed by the Moff. As Skywalker [[Skirmish over Llanic|arrived]] in the system, he immediately came across one of Balfour's Star Destroyers, but it was too far away to engage.<ref name="Heir" />

==Behind the scenes==
Abran Balfour was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Heir to the Jedi]]'', written by [[Kevin Hearne]].<ref name="Heir" /> [[Abran Balfour/Legends|He]] was introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1993]] supplement to ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', ''[[Galaxy Guide 9: Fragments from the Rim]]'', written by [[Simon Smith]] and [[Eric S. Trautmann|Eric Trautmann]] and illustrated by [[Mike Vilardi]].<ref name="Legends">''[[Galaxy Guide 9: Fragments from the Rim]]''</ref>
