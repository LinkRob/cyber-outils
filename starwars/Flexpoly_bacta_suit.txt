'''Flexpoly bacta suits''' were a type of emergency medical suit filled with artificial [[bacta]] manufactured by [[Zaltin Corp]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE" /> They consisted of transparent bacta circulation and filtration tubing and a retaining collar lined by status [[sensor]]s with wireless telemetry. 

Before [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[Unidentified Quarren (bacta suit)|Quarren]] sold fake bacta suits while on [[Corellia]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

When the [[Resistance]] had to [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuate]] their [[Resistance base|base]] on the [[planet]] [[D'Qar]]<ref name="TLJ" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. As [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] carries on immediately from the events of ''The Force Awakens'', it can be assumed to take place in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the injured [[First Order]] deserter [[Finn]] was put into an expired flexpoly bacta suit while in a medical coma and transferred to a storage room-turned temporary medical suite onboard the [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']].<ref name="TLJVD" /> He remained unconscious in the suit until after the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] had successfully exited [[hyperspace]] after jumping to [[lightspeed]], but then awoke and left his bed in search of the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]. As he moved through the corridors of the ''Raddus'' the expired bacta suit began leaking as it had become fragile. Eventually, the [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] spotted the confused and mostly naked Finn and found him a change of [[Clothing|clothes]].<ref name="TLJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
A flexpoly bacta suit first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ" /> which was released on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-viii-to-open-december-15-2017|text=''Star Wars: Episode VIII'' to Open December 15, 2017}}</ref> The costume was not named in the film,<ref name="TLJ" /> but received identification in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref>
