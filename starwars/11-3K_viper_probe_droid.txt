'''11-3K viper probe droids''', or '''11-3K Viper Droids''' were a variant of [[viper probe droid]]<ref name="TOG" /> produced by [[Aratech Repulsor Company]]<ref name="DB" /> and used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Like most other Imperial [[droid]]s, they were programmed with unflagging loyalty and received frequent [[memory wipe]]s. They were commonly used for investigation, exploration, and reconnaissance. They included a rotating<ref name="TOG" /> [[blaster cannon]] collar assembly able to rotate a 360 degree<ref name="DB" /> and an articulated manipulator with sampling claws.<ref name="TOG" /> It was more heavily armored than the standard viper probe droid, and as such was used on remote planets<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> such as guarding the [[Crispin Imperial Depository]] at the end of the [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport|conveyex]] [[train]] line on [[Vandor]],<ref name="Solo" /> or [[Koboh]] and [[Jedha]].<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> During the [[heist on Vandor]], the criminal [[Val]] fought several 11-3K viper probe droids near the depository which had been dispatched to the scene.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:11-3K viper probe droid concept.png|thumb|left|Early concept art "Probe Droid Version 1B" by Jack Dudman]]
The 11-3K viper probe droids first appeared in the [[2018]] anthology film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Solo" /> and were first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TOG" /> They were originally conceived for the [[Battle of Mimban]] sequence and were designed by [[Jack Dudman]]. One of the early pieces titled "Probe Droid Version 1B" depicts the droid with an umbrella shaped body, while "Viper Droids Versions 2A" and "Version 17" are close to the final design in the film.<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

They later re-appeared in the [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]].<ref name="Survivor" /> Concept art for their appearance in the game was done by [[Tim Wallace]] and [[Chelsea Velazquez]]. Producer [[Kasumi Shishido]] explained that they wanted to introduce a new type of flying droid for the game,<ref name="Art of JS">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> after the regular viper probe droids had appeared in it's predecessor ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'',<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> and looked at the 11-3K droids from ''Solo''. The 11-3K's slightly larger scale allowed them to include new types of encounters for Imperial areas in the game.<ref name="Art of JS" />
