'''Ferize''' was a [[Kessarine]] [[Gender|female]] member of the [[Path of the Open Hand]]. She had two male partners, [[Er Dal]] and [[Fel Ix]].<ref name="POD" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Ferize and her partners hatched a clutch of five children, including [[Fe Isle]], [[Fe Tale]], and [[Fe Remine]].<ref name="POD" />
