'''East Narcene''' was an area of the of the [[Ecumenopolis|city-world]] of [[Coruscant]].<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="GhormanDate">"[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]" dates the events of "[[I Have Friends Everywhere]]" to [[3 BBY]].</ref> Varian Skye, an alias used by the rebel [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], supposedly lived and worked in East Narcene on [[Level 4888]].<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" />

==Behind the scenes==
East Narcene was first mentioned in "[[I Have Friends Everywhere]]," an episode of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" /> on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref>
