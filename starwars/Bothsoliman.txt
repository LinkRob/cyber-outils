'''Bothsoliman''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> located in the [[Bothsoliman system]] within the [[Hollan sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was situated in grid square M-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

According to the [[human]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], good [[tailor]]s could be found on Bothsoliman.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> By [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> Calrissian spent time on Bothsoliman and was given a bottle opener for [[Azuran steam spirit]] by a [[Unidentified sommelier|sommelier]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> after Calrissian was [[Skirmish on Torrenoteer Minor|captured]] by [[bounty hunter]]s on<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Torrenoteer Minor">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Torrenoteer system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Torrenoteer Minor]], he used the bottle opener provided by the [[sommelier]] to escape from a [[CAP-2]] [[walker]]. After showing his companion&mdash;[[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]&mdash;the bottle opener, Calrissian informed Skywalker of where he acquired it, before noting to the [[Jedi]] of the good tailors on Bothsoliman and that he could recommend one.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
