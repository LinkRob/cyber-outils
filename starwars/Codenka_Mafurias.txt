'''Codenka Mafurias''' was the moniker used by the creator of the [[podracing]] [[Painting|poster]] "[[The Sport's Greatest Rivalry]]," which depicted the rivalry between [[Pilot|podracers]] [[Ben Quadinaros]] and [[Sebulba]] in the final [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]]. Mafurias was originally widely believed to be a talented [[Nikto]] [[Gender|female]] [[Artist|illustrator]] from the [[planet]] [[Malastare]] who was on the payroll of the [[Grand Hutt Council]]; however, it later became assumed that it was actually an alias used by an art workshop financially backed by [[Marlo]] the [[Hutt]].<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
Codenka Mafurias was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on [[October 25]], [[2016]].
