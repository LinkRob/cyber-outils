'''Aloxians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] from the [[planet]] [[Zav Alox]] on the edge of [[the galaxy]]. Most young Aloxians wore [[Vonduun skull helmet|helmets]] made from the [[Bone|skulls]] of [[vonduun crab]]s. Born with immense strength, Aloxians were prized as child [[soldier]]s. [[Qort]], a [[Gender|male]] Aloxian, was a [[Jedi Knight]] during the [[High Republic Era]].

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Qort-THRTrailOfShadows5.jpg|thumb|left|Qort without his mask on]]
Aloxians<ref name="Qort Card">{{Twitter|IDWPublishing|status/1424031627601133569|[[IDW Publishing]]|quote=Card #3: Qort was on our TikTok https://vm.tiktok.com/ZMdK2eudR|archivedate=20210808021010}}</ref> were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] from the [[planet]] [[Zav Alox]].<ref name="HRA 9" /> Diminutive with a [[humanoid]] build, they had [[Color|blue]] skin, brown [[eye]]s,<ref name="HRA 1" /> and white [[hair]],<ref name="HRA 9" /> and although they could understand [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]], spoke their own [[language]].<ref name="HRA 1" /> Aloxians were born with immense physical strength and a naturally violent nature, which the [[Patrolian]] [[Farks]] claimed was due to the Aloxians' [[homeworld]] being infested with [[vonduun crab]]s.<ref name="HRA 9" /> Aloxians could be [[Force-sensitive]].<ref name="HRA 1" />

==Society and culture==
[[File:Aloxians-2021THRA9.jpg|thumb|right|Several Aloxians]]
As an initiation rite, young Aloxians were sent out to [[Hunting|hunt]] a vonduun crab, wearing the [[Bone|skull]] of the [[crab]] if they were successful. Aloxians believed the crab skulls fused with their wearer's head and balanced out their naturally aggressive nature, which Farks claimed was the reason the species wasn't in a state of constant civil war. As an Aloxian grew into maturity, their expanding head would shatter their [[Vonduun skull helmet|helmet]], which was seen as a sign of Aloxians fully becoming themselves.<ref name="HRA 9" />

==Aloxians in the galaxy==
Aloxian [[Youngling|children]] were prized as [[soldier]]s due to their strength and natural aggressiveness, often being captured by [[Pirate|raiders]] to be sold as child soldiers. Some captured Aloxian children were separated from their skull [[helmet]]s to enhance their murderous capacity.<ref name="HRA 9" />

During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Qort]], a [[Force-sensitive]] Aloxian [[Gender|male]], was found by [[Maz Kanata]],<ref name="HRA 9" /> and eventually trained as a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] in the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="HRA 1" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 12|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 12]] to [[230 BBY]].</ref> an Aloxian [[Jedi Initiate]] named [[Zint]]<ref name="HRA 13">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 13|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 13]]</ref> was recruited into the Order by [[Jedi Master]] [[Cherff Maota]] and was present aboard the ''[[Star Hopper]]'' during the [[battle at Dol'har Hyde]].<ref name="HRA 12">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 12|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 12]]</ref> Sometime later, Zint was present on [[Starlight Beacon]] when it was [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|attacked]] by the [[Nihil]]. Zint, along with the other younglings, as well as Qort, were taken off the station by [[Jedi Master]] [[Emerick Caphtor]] and [[private investigator]] [[Sian Holt]].<ref name="Trail of Shadows 5">[[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 5|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 5]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Aloxians first appeared in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']] comic book.<ref name="HRA 1" /> The species was first identified in a digital trading card posted on [[IDW Publishing]]'s social media in [[August]] [[2021]].<ref name="Qort Card" /> Author [[Daniel José Older]] confirmed that Aloxians age quicker than humans, but still live longer, calling them "complicated beings."<ref name="Daniel Tweet 1">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1719757461165383824|[[Daniel José Older]]|date=2023-11-01|quote=Aloxians still live longer than humans. Maturing faster doesn't mean dying sooner! They're complicated beings folks I don't know what to tell you|archivedate=20231101174315}}</ref>
