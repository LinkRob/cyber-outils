'''Beedee-Beedee''' was an [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]] in service to the [[Resistance]] during its [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] with the [[First Order]]. Beedee-Beedee flew in the [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] of [[Karé Kun]] during [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron's]] [[Mission to the Outer Rim|mission]] to gather allies and bring new [[Star system|systems]] into the Resistance.
