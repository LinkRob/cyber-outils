'''Darius Pax''' was an individual who ran the [[Galactic League (racing)|Galactic League]], an unsanctioned [[podracing|racing]] circuit that operated in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="trailer 2 article" /> during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="swcom page">{{SW|url=games-apps/star-wars-galactic-racer|text=Star Wars: Galactic Racer}}</ref> Pax had a boisterous voice, and recruited [[Shade]], a mysterious [[racer]] with a grudge against the [[Bool family]], to help take down the ruthless [[Kestar Bool]], who dominated the League<ref name="trailer 2 article" /> and had his own power-hungry plans for it.<ref name="swcom page" />
