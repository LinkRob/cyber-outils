'''Crondre''' was an astronomical object that was part of the [[Dufilvian sector]]'s [[Crondre system]],<ref name="Appendix" /> which fell within square R-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] as part of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="MandoVG" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[human]] [[stormtrooper]] designated [[TK-7919]],<ref name="MandoVG" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Dates">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'' states that TK-7919 was thirty-one [[Standard year|years]] old at the [[time]] of their [[death]] during the [[incident on Nevarro]], also placing Crondre within the borders of the [[New Republic]] at that [[time]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the incident to [[9 ABY]], TK-7919 must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[22 BBY]].</ref> TK-7919 served the [[Galactic Empire]] and then went on to also serve under the [[Imperial Remnant]], eventually [[Death|dying]] on the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]]<ref name="MandoVG" /> in [[9 ABY]]. During that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Dates" /> Crondre fell within the borders of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Crondre was introduced in the [[2026]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="MandoVG" /> [[Crondre/Legends|Crondre]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[1993]] [[Timothy Zahn]] novel ''[[The Last Command]]'', the third book in ''[[Star Wars: The Thrawn Trilogy]]''.<ref name="TLC">''[[The Last Command]]''</ref> It then appeared for the first time in the [[1998]] [[Coolhand Interactive]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']], although the game misspelled its name as "Chrondre."<ref name="Chondre">[[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']]</ref>
