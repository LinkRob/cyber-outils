'''Bola carbine''' was an individually handcrafted, unique energy weapon that ensnared and tripped up targets. [[Bounty hunter]]s often fielded a bola carbine as their primary weapon to both take out hostiles and capture targets.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

{{Stub}}

==Description==
The bola carbine featured [[wood]]en furniture, often adorned in custom etchings, making it an excellent addition to any enthusiast's collection. Instead of using standard blaster energy, the bola carbine mixed in a series of hard energy particles that produce a small [[Stun net|energy net]] as a by-product of a weaker blaster bolt. While the energy net usually faded after a few seconds, specific atmospheric conditions have been known to extend its duration. The specific blaster gas and hard particles often gave the energy a purple hue.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bola carbine was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
