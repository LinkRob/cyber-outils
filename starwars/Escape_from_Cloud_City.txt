{{Quote|Attention! This is Lando Calrissian. Attention! The Empire is taking control of the city. I advise everyone to leave before more Imperial troops arrive.|[[Baron Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
The '''Escape from Cloud City''', also remembered as a '''skirmish on Cloud City''', took place during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. As part of a plot to capture [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] led the [[Imperial Military]] to [[Cloud City]], a [[tibanna]] gas [[Mining|mine]] in the atmosphere of [[Bespin]]. The [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] laid a trap for Skywalker and [[Duel on Cloud City|dueled]] the fledgling [[Jedi]] in an attempt to capture him. At the same time, members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] fought to rescue [[Han Solo]] from the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]], who was working for the [[Hutt Clan]], and escape from Cloud City. They were unable to rescue Solo, who had been frozen in [[carbonite]] as part of Vader's plot, but they were able to fight their way past [[stormtrooper]]s to escape from the city aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. The Rebels, along with the city's [[Baron Administrator]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], briefly returned in order to rescue Skywalker, who was injured in his duel with the Dark Lord, before evading the [[Imperial Navy]] and making the jump to [[hyperspace]] to rejoin the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]].<ref name="Episode V" />

==Prelude==
===Capture===
{{Quote|I had no choice. They arrived right before you did. I'm sorry.|Baron Administrator Lando Calrissian, to Han Solo and the other rebels|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
Hoping to turn [[Luke Skywalker]] to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] sent part of the [[Imperial Navy]] to [[Cloud City]], after it was discovered by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] to be their destination, in order to capture Luke's friends [[Han Solo]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Chewbacca]] and use them as bait to lure Skywalker out of hiding and turn him. Having landed just before the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', Vader made a deal with the city's [[Baron Administrator]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], to capture Skywalker's friends in exchange for his city's autonomy from the Empire.<ref name="Episode V" />

When the ''Millennium Falcon'' eventually arrived at Cloud City, its crew was warmly welcomed by Calrissian, who gave them rooms to stay in and arranged for their ship to be repaired. Unbeknownst to him, however, the ''Falcon''{{'s}} [[hyperdrive]] was secretly deactivated by a team of Imperial technicians in the event of their escape. On the way to their apartment, [[C-3PO]] was separated from the group after being distracted by another [[protocol droid]], finding and being shot by a [[stormtrooper]], dismembering him. C-3PO's parts were later sent to the garbage incinerator of Cloud City, but they were found by Chewbacca before they were destroyed. On their last day, Calrissian invited them to dinner, but it turned out to be a trap set by Darth Vader. Solo, upon seeing Vader, tried to shoot the Sith Lord, only for Vader to block the shots with one gauntleted hand, and [[Telekinesis|pull]] the [[smuggler]]'s [[blaster]] away from him with the other. Stormtroopers surrounded the rebels, ensuring that they could not escape.<ref name="Episode V" />

While Vader decided to [[torture]] Solo in order to draw Skywalker's attention, Calrissian began to feel uneasy as Vader altered the agreement. When he protested the idea of keeping Organa and Chewbacca in the city as prisoners, and giving Solo to Fett, Vader reminded him that disobedience would result in the end of his colony's freedom. Now seen as a traitor to his friends, Calrissian was forced to comply with Vader. Vader's trap did, as planned, eventually reach Luke as a [[Force vision|vision]], and he rushed off to save his friends, despite objection from both [[Yoda]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Ben Kenobi]].<ref name="Episode V" />

===Freezing Solo===
{{Quote|I am altering the deal. Pray I don't alter it any further.|Darth Vader, to Lando Calrissian|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
Vader had planned to put Skywalker in [[carbonite]], in order to be delivered to [[Darth Sidious|the Emperor]] personally. Hoping to avoid his son's death, Vader chose Solo as a test subject for the modified [[Carbon-freezing chamber|freezing chamber]]. Solo, Organa, Chewbacca, and C-3PO (partially reassembled by Chewbacca in prison) were brought to the [[carbon-freezing]] facility, where Solo learned of his fate. Despite Chewbacca's attempt to set him free, Solo insisted that there would be [[Rescue of Han Solo|another time]] to rescue him. Before he was frozen, Organa told Solo how she felt about him, to which he responded "I know." Solo was then frozen, and found to have survived. Afterwards, Vader was informed that Skywalker's [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] had just landed. Vader then ordered Calrissian to bring Organa and Chewbacca to [[Executor|his ship]]. When he protested, Vader simply stated that he was altering the deal.<ref name="Episode V" />

==The escape==
{{Quote|Well done. Hold them in the security tower. And keep it quiet.|Lando Calrissian, upon [[Lobot]]'s execution of Code Force Seven|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
[[File:StarWars2020-12-Sprouse-textless.png|thumb|left|250px|Leia Organa exchanges fire with Imperial pursuers during the escape from Cloud City]]
Realizing that the "deal" had gone too far,<ref name="Episode V" /> Calrissian issued a [[Code Force Seven]]<ref name="SWCE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> and arranged for the [[Bespin Wing Guard]] to ambush the Imperial team escorting himself, Organa, Chewbacca, and C-3PO&mdash;still strapped to the [[Wookiee]]'s back&mdash;to Vader's ship. Chewbacca and Organa, however, did not trust him due to his prior betrayal, with Chewbacca nearly strangling him until he revealed that there was still a chance to save Solo from Fett. Deciding it was worth the risk, the foursome rushed to intercept Fett, reaching his landing platform just in time to see the ''[[Slave I]]'' take off.<ref name="Episode V" />

Realizing that&mdash;for now, at least&mdash;Solo was gone, they redirected their efforts into escaping. After reaching the ''Falcon''{{'s}} hangar door, they discovered that the access codes had been changed, preventing Calrissian from unlocking the doors. C-3PO then realized that R2-D2 could override the security systems, but accidentally directed the [[Astromech droid|astromech]] into plugging into a power socket. Before leading the group to another door with a computer terminal, Calrissian informed the citizens of Cloud City of the Imperial occupation, and advised them to leave as soon as possible. The ensuing chaos worked to their advantage, helping them get to another door mostly unhindered by Imperial forces, though they encountered several stormtroopers upon reaching another door.<ref name="Episode V" /> After R2-D2 plugged in, he was informed by the computer that she had been given a message by the ''Falcon''{{'s}} [[Maintenance droid|repair droid]], named [[Treadwell]], to warn them that the ''Falcon''{{'s}} hyperdrive had been deactivated.<ref name="Faith in an Old Friend">{{StoryCite|story=Faith in an Old Friend|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>

[[File:HanSolosMillenniumFalcon-SaS.png|thumb|right|250px|The ''Millennium Falcon'' is pursued by TIE fighters.]]
However, when he tried to inform the group about this, C-3PO interrupted him, saying that the hyperdrive had been fixed, and telling him to open the door. After Artoo did so, and covered their retreat with a smokescreen, the group boarded the ''Falcon'' and took off, barely escaping the Imperial troops. Skywalker, having just been defeated by Vader in a [[lightsaber duel]], was clinging for his life at a vane on the bottom of Cloud City. First calling to Ben Kenobi for help and receiving no response, he called to someone else: Leia. Organa, on board the ''Millennium Falcon'' on her way out of the city, heard Skywalker's plea and ordered the ship around. Finding Skywalker dangling from the vane, they pulled up beneath it, and Calrissian pulled the young Jedi to safety. As the ''Falcon''{{'s}} hatch closed, three [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] showed up and pursued the rebels into space while firing at the freighter.<ref name="Episode V" />

Having left the planet's gravity well, the ''Falcon'', upon sighting the ''Executor'', attempted to jump to hyperspace, only to find the "repaired" hyperdrive nonfunctional. As the crew desperately attempted to fix the hyperdrive while R2-D2 was reassembling C-3PO, the astromech realized what the problem was, having been told earlier by Cloud City's central computer, and managed to reactivate the drive, allowing the rebels to escape into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Episode V" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Similarly, Darth Vader authorized the full annexation of Cloud City&mdash;a tibanna gas mining facility in the atmosphere above Bespin&mdash;shortly after the Battle of Hoth. This was at least partially motivated by the capture of leading Rebel Alliance figures there, but General Lando Calrissian, who formerly served as Cloud City's administrator, believes that Vader intended to take over the facility regardless.|[[Beaumont Kin]], ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''|<ref name="Rise Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
[[File:LeiaCloudCityDissidents-2020StarWars4.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Leia Organa stands with members of the Bespin Guard during the second escape from Cloud City.]]
After the ''Falcon'' jumped to hyperspace, the team of Imperial technicians that had deactivated its hyperdrive expected to be executed. To their surprise, Vader did nothing, simply striding out of the bridge of his flagship.<ref name="LS">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> The ''Falcon''{{'s}} crew, meanwhile, rendezvoused with the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] during the [[battle at Rendezvous Point Delta-Three]]. At [[Backup Rendezvous Point Gamma-Nine]],<ref name="Star Wars 1">[[Star Wars (2020) 1|''Star Wars'' (2020) 1]]</ref> Skywalker received a [[Cybernetics|prosthetic]] [[Mechno-arm|arm]] to replace the one he had lost, with it being completed as Calrissian and Chewbacca set off on a mission to [[Rescue of Han Solo|rescue]] Solo from [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]],<ref name="Episode V" /> only to return to the Rebel fleet upon discovering Fett had yet to drop Solo off at [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]];<ref name="Star Wars 2">[[Star Wars (2020) 2|''Star Wars'' (2020) 2]]</ref> the bounty hunter had been distracted by the hunt for his old ally, [[Nakano Lash]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 1">[[Bounty Hunters 1|''Bounty Hunters'' 1]]</ref>

Meanwhile, Cloud City was taken into Imperial control,<ref name="Episode V" /> with several trades, such as cybernetic implants, being suppressed,<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> and a [[Imperial Garrison (Cloud City)|skeleton garrison]] stationed there.<ref name="SWU">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> Shortly after the escape, Calrissian, Organa, Skywalker, and R2-D2 [[Mission to Cloud City|returned]] to Cloud City the aim of rescuing Lobot and sabotaging the mining operations, with the small team also wanting to study carbon freezing due to Solo's capture and retrieve Skywalker's lightsaber. Rescuing Lobot and a number of other residents, the mission was largely a success, and, even though Skywalker was unable to find his blade, he did recover his X-wing.<ref name="Star Wars 4">[[Star Wars (2020) 4|''Star Wars'' (2020) 4]]</ref> In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Endor]], however, the garrison was relocated to a more strategic location, setting the stage for the city's eventual [[Liberation of Cloud City|liberation]].<ref name="SWU" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Escape from Cloud City first appeared in the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]].<ref name="Episode V" /> The novel ''[[Lost Stars]]'' also featured the escape from the viewpoint of [[Ciena Ree]], who was one of the Imperial technicians tasked with deactivating the ''Falcon''{{'s}} hyperdrive.<ref name="LS" />
