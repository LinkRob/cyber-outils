'''Automated banking structures''' were unattended machines from which people could get [[Currency|money]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], such structures could notably be found on [[Darlyn Boda]], where they were designed to stop constant [[Thief|robbing]] of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] financial stronghold.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>
