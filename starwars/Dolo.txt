'''Dolo''' was a [[bantha]] who lived on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], a former [[Jedi Master]], lived among the bantha herd in which Dolo belonged, and the [[human]] considered the banthas to be his family. One [[Standard day|day]], after a violent encounter with the [[bounty hunter]] [[Krrsantan]], Kenobi returned to the herd and noted that Dolo was looking sad, while the bantha [[Nara (bantha)|Nara]] was looking well.<ref name="SW20" />
