'''Cinderhawks''' were a type of [[Hawk (animal)|hawk]] found on<ref name="TRS" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Elphrona]]. After being severely injured by a [[harpoon]] during a [[First Battle of Cyclor|battle]] against the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization<ref name="TRS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] imagined himself hearing the voice of a [[Jedi Master]] as he struggled to stay conscious. The voice told him about the [[Living Force|energy]] in all living things, including cinderhawks and [[charhound]]s. The Padawan briefly considered the words and heard the sound of the hawks calling, but in reality, his charhound [[Ember]] was barking at him to keep him lucid.<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cinderhawks were mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
