A '''contingency sphere''' was a [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] technological artifact that could be used as a source of power.

==Properties==
Contingency spheres were [[Color/Legends|black]], one [[Meter/Legends|meter]] diameter spheres built to contain compacted energy. One single sphere could provide enough power to supply several [[Star Destroyer/Legends|Star Destroyers]]. They were also equipped with an auxiliary [[Navigation computer/Legends|navigation computer]].<ref name="CB99" />

==History==
At some point during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], one such device was used by a psychopathic [[Changeling/Legends|changeling]] known as [[Hydra (shapeshifter)|Hydra]] in order to locate and power up the ''[[Nocturnô]]'', a forgotten [[Death Star/Legends|Death Star]] prototype. This specific sphere was recovered from the wreck of an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="CB99" />
