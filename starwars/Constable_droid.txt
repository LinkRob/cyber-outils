'''Constable droid''' was a type of [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] droid. The small constable droids of [[Plazir-15]] were unarmed, befitting the [[planet]]'s pacifistic society. Often working in groups, they were tasked with keeping the peace and moving people out of harm's way, such as when [[Din Djarin]] and [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] tangled with a droid rebel.<ref name="CGttSWG" />
