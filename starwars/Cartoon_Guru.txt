"'''Cartoon Guru'''" is an article for [[Star Wars Insider 68|''Star Wars Insider'' 68]] written by [[Eric Moro]]. It features an interview with ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' director [[Genndy Tartakovsky]] about his work on not just ''Clone Wars'', but ''[[Wikipedia:The Powerpuff Girls|The Powerpuff Girls]]'', ''[[Wikipedia:Dexter's Laboratory|Dexter's Laboratory]]'', and ''[[Wikipedia:Samurai Jack|Samurai Jack]]''.<ref name="Insider" />
