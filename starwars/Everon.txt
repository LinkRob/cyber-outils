'''Everon''' was a tempest [[planet]] and the [[homeworld]] of the [[Evereni]] [[species]].<ref name="Eye 1" />

==History==
Before the [[High Republic Era]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Evereni]] put their trust in their stormy [[homeworld]] of Everon in an attempt to live in harmony with nature; however, the [[Great Storm]] came, in the form of a vortex storm that rose from the [[planet]]'s core bringing strong winds and lightning, destroying the native [[Evereni]]'s civilization.<ref name="EotS1">[[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]]</ref>

A series of [[Standard century|centuries]]-long [[Evereni conflicts|conflicts]] in the aftermath were fought among the Evereni for the remaining resources following the Great Storm. This brought the Evereni to near-extinction, forcing those who survived to [[Everon exodus|flee]] the planet around [[500 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> and become [[the Wanderers]]. With his people long ago having fled their homeworld,<ref name="EotS1" /> the later Evereni [[Marchion Ro]] felt that the closest thing had to a home was his [[flagship]], the ''[[Gaze Electric]]''.<ref name="LotJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The planet Everon and its Evereni people were created by [[Charles Soule]],<ref name="Soule Homeworld">{{Twitter|CharlesSoule|status/1826268406804562244|[[Charles Soule]]|quote=That was my intention when I wrote that line and created Everon and the Evereni. {{C|In response to: "So the "Ro family's homeworld" mentioned in Light of the Jedi is actually supposed to be a reference to the Evereni homeworld, not some planet picked out by the Ros to live on right?"}}|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/wbPjq}}</ref> a ''[[Star Wars]]'' author who works on the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' initiative. Everon was first indirectly mentioned in Soule's [[2021]] ''High Republic'' novel ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' as a homeworld Marchion Ro reflected upon.<ref name="LotJ" /> It was later identified as Everon in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], where it appeared for the first time via flashbacks.<ref name="EotS1" /> Soule later confirmed that the homeworld Ro reflected on in ''Light of the Jedi'' was meant to be his species's homeworld of Everon, not a separate planet the [[Ro family]] had claimed as a homeworld at some point.<ref name="Soule Homeworld" />
