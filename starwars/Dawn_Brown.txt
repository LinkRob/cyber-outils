'''Dawn Brown''' is a set designer who worked for [[Lucasfilm]] on [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew|Skeleton Crew]]. She was nominated for an [[Wikipedia:Emmy Awards|Emmy]] for her work on ''Skeleton Crew''.<ref name="Emmy">{{WebCite|url=https://theemmys.tv/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/4th-Annual-Childrens-Family-Emmy%C2%AE-Awards-Nominees-FINAL.pdf|text=4th Annual Children's & Family Emmy® Awards &ndash; FINAL NOMINEE LISTING|date=2026-02-27|work=[[Wikipedia:Emmy Awards|Emmy Awards]]|format=[[Wikipedia:PDF|PDF]]|pages=p. 52|archivedate=20260309213510}}</ref>
