The '''Battle of Cawa City''' was fought during the [[Galactic Civil War]] when the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] fought to liberate [[Sterdic IV]] from the shattered [[Galactic Empire]]. It took place seventeen [[Standard day|days]] after the [[Battle of Endor]] and resulted in a Rebel victory.

==The battle==
{{Quote|Green Group, we need an approach corridor for the Y-wings, now!|A Rebel commander at Cawa City|<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" />}}
[[File:FeralGroupAndGreenSquadron-ShatteredEmpire2.png|thumb|left|250px|Green Group's A-wings on an attack run]]
Cawa City, on Sterdic IV, was occupied by the Empire, with ground forces consisting an [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] and air support from [[TIE series|TIEs]]. The Alliance sent [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Group]] and [[Feral Group]] to liberate the city from the Imperial forces. A-wings from Green Group, which included the squadron's new acting commander, [[Lieutenant]] [[L'ulo L'ampar]], screened the air of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] to clear a path for Feral Group's [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|Y-wing]] bombers. Just as Green Group were clearing the last of the TIEs, [[Shara Bey's A-wing]] was hit by a pursuing TIE fighter, although not fatal, her ship started leaking coolant. L'ampar managed to destroy the TIE fighter before it could do any further damage.

[[File:YWingsCawaCity-ShatteredEmpire2.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Feral Group drop their bombs]]
Once the approach was clear, Feral Group made their bombing run. Their Y-wings dropped [[magnetising cluster bomb]]s, which magnetized to the All Terrain Armored Transport and destroyed it instantly. Both sides suffered heavy losses, however it was a Rebel victory.<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" />
