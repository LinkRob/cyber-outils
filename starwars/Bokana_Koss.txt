'''Bokana Koss''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Ovissian]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="PoV" /> disciple of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]].<ref name="Battle of Jedha" /> While he appeared to have a minor degree of Force-sensitivity prior without knowing it,<ref name="PoV" /> his actual [[Force power]]s only seemed to come into being as a result<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> of a [[Mission to Planet X (Path of the Open Hand)|mission]] to the mysterious [[Sophros|Planet X]], on which his powers suddenly came into being.<ref name="PoV" />

==Biography==
Bokana Koss worked as a [[mercenary]]. In time, he decided to fight on the side of the [[E'roni]] during their [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|war]] with the [[Eirami]] as a mercenary, but he later deserted the war when ordered to kill civilians.<ref name="PoV" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> he joined with the [[Path of the Open Hand]]. During the [[Battle of Jedha]], he was attacked by three rioters during the [[Battle of Jedha]]. The rioters attacked him because they blamed the Path of the Open Hand for starting the rioting and the battle. Fellow Path member [[Marda Ro]] found him being attacked and ignited the late [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kevmo Zink]]'s [[Kevmo's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and told the rioters to step away from Koss. They merely taunted her and disparaged [[the Force]] and a [[Kyuzo]] used a slur against Ro's [[species]], the [[Evereni]], Ro cut off the Kyuzo's hand and stabbed and killed him to defend Koss, but dropped the lightsaber in shock afterward.<ref name="PoV" />

The other rioters started to fire at them, but they were saved by [[Jedi Master]] [[Silandra Sho]], who threw [[Silandra Sho's shield|her shield]] at one of the rioters and thus scared them off. When Sho asked about the dead Kyuzo with the lightsaber hole in his chest, Koss lied for Ro and said that they had found the man like that and Ro had tried to revive him to no avail, and that was when the rioters attacked them. However, Koss suffered severe injuries and nearly collapsed while speaking to Sho and the [[Elecia Zeveron|Mother]], the leader of the Path that Sho had been escorting, about what had happened. Ro caught him, and while she held him close, he whispered that he had spotted the lightsaber roll underneath a piece of rubble to her right when she had dropped it. The Mother came towards him and acknowledged his injuries, but he said he believed that he would recover by the will of The Force. After Sho received a message to join the other Jedi, Ro and the Mother convinced her to leave them alone.<ref name="PoV" />

After Sho left, Ro retrieved the lightsaber and the two women recognized Koss's heroism, but then he and Ro noticed that the Mother was severely injured. He introduced himself and then they began their journey to where Path member [[Radicaz Dobbs]]'s ship the ''[[Scupper]]'' was parked. However, on the way the Mother started to collapse and so Koss decided to carry her despite his injuries. He navigated them through the city and thought he knew where he was going, but he ended up leading them to a dead-end alleyway. He apologized to the Mother and Ro as a creature cornered them and Ro ignited the lightsaber.<ref name="PoV" />
