'''Eleodie Maracavanya''' was a [[Gender|non-binary]] [[human]] [[pirate]] ruler after the fall of the [[Galactic Empire]] who led zher pirate crew to take control of the [[Liberty's Misrule|''Annihilator'']], a [[Super Star Destroyer]] that once belonged to the Imperial [[Grand General]] [[Cassio Tagge]], which was renamed to ''Liberty's Misrule''. With this [[Starship|vessel]] and a fleet under zher control, Maracavanya came to be known as the "pirate ruler of [[Wild Space]]" and a ship-based pirate nation, the [[Sovereign Latitudes of Maracavanya]].

==Biography==
===Background===
{{Quote|Thank you for the ship and your supplies. I have been Eleodie Maracavanya. It has been ''your'' pleasure.|Eleodie Maracavanya|<ref name="Life Debt" />}}
[[File:Annihilator_Executor-class_SWA.png|thumb|right|250px|The ''Annihilator'', before it was captured by Eleodie Maracavanya]]
Eleodie Maracavanya was a<ref name="Life Debt" /> [[Gender|non-binary]]<ref name="Wendig Tweet NB" /> [[human]]<ref name="Wendig Tweet pronouns" /> who grew up on the [[moon]] [[Nar Shaddaa]]. Zhe was once [[Slavery|enslaved]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] but later became a [[pirate]]. Following the [[Battle of Endor]], Maracavanya became the leader of pirate gang based in [[Wild Space]]. Zher underlings included the [[Omwati]] [[Shi Shu]], [[Vinthar]] the [[Sarkan]] of [[Egg-Brood Xazin'nizar]], and several [[Weequay]] pirates. Maracavanya and zher gang took over the [[Super Star Destroyer]] [[Liberty's Misrule|''Annihilator'']],<ref name="Life Debt" /> the former [[flagship]] of the Imperial [[Grand General]] [[Cassio Tagge]].<ref name="Vader 2">[[Darth Vader (2015) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 2]]</ref> Maracavanya established a pirate [[Politics|empire]] based aboard a [[flotilla]] of [[Starship|ships]] that included the former Imperial Super [[Star Destroyer]].<ref name="Life Debt" />

===Seizing ''Starfall''===
{{Quote|I am a precious realist, and what you get in this lifetime is purely the result of what you take. Come with me. Come to my nation. Become part of my fleet. Join my sovereign space. Enjoy the freedom that comes with taking whatever you want, whatever you can get, whenever the chance.|Eleodie Maracavanya, inviting passengers aboard the ''Starfall'' to join zher pirate crew|<ref name="Life Debt" />}}
In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> Maracavanya's ''Annihilator'' boarded a [[New Republic]] [[CR90 corvette]] called the ''[[Starfall]]''. The ship was carrying a diplomatic mission led by [[Senator]] [[Tia'dor Emshwa]], which was traveling on a [[Mission to Ithor|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Ithor]] to recruit the world to the nascent [[The galaxy|galactic]] government. ''Starfall'' was carrying reclaimed [[Ithorian]] artifacts, [[Foodstuff|food]], medicine, and a bounty of technology.<ref name="Life Debt" />

[[File:Flash_Grenade_DICE.png|thumb|left|200px|Flash grenades were used to pacify the passengers and crew aboard the ''Starfall''.]]
Maracavanya supervised the boarding with zher second-in-command Shi Shu, who informed zher about the ship. While zhe was reluctant to pick a fight with the New Republic, zhe accepted Shi Shu's explanation that plundering the ''Starfall'' would replenish their flotilla's supplies. After disabling the ship's comm array and sealing the breaching airlock, Maracavanya and Vinthar commanded the boarding of the ''Starfall''. After the pirates pacified the crew and passengers with [[flash grenade]]s and [[Flash detonator/Legends|flash detonators]], Vinthar told Maracavanya to board the ship and meet the captives.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Maracavanya waited as Vinthar addressed the captives and introduced his captain with a short speech that zhe had written zherself. After displaying zher [[electro-scythe]], Maracavanya used a [[Vocabulator|vocoder]] to speak in a decipherable way to the ''Starfall''{{'s}} crew and passengers. Zhe identified zherself as the captain of the ''Annihilator'' but vowed to rename the ship. Zhe promised to spare the lives of the captives if they did not resist but expropriated their ship and its cargo. Since zhe was no murderer and slaver, zhe gave them the chance to flee into the ''Starfall''{{'s}} [[escape pod]]s.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Though Maracavanya did not impress crew into zher services, zhe still sought to recruit the ''Starfall''{{'s}} crew and passengers into zher services voluntarily. Zhe promised that they would enjoy a life of spoils and riches as pirates and mocked the New Republic. To zher surprise, a young [[Chandrilan]] woman named [[Kartessa]] took up the offer. The youth wanted a life of adventure and did not want to be cloistered on [[Chandrila]] anymore. The [[Kartessa's mother|woman's mother]] objected, but Maracavanya told her to respect her daughter's decision. The woman reluctantly agreed to follow her daughter and join Maracavanya's pirate empire. Zhe then allowed the remaining crew and passengers to leave aboard escape pods.<ref name="Life Debt" />

===''Liberty's Misrule''===
{{Quote|I am enacting my divine right. ''Attack."<br />"''Do you know what you're doing?''"<br />"''No, girl. That's what makes this so interesting. The present is a pair of dice always about to leave my hand, and I never know if it'll come up zero or one, win or lose.|Eleodie Maracavanya discussing with Kartessa zher decision to destroy Star Destroyers instead of capturing them|<ref name="Empire's End" />}}
In the [[Standard month|months]] leading up to the [[Battle of Jakku]], Eleodie Maracavanya renamed the ''Annihilator'' the ''Liberty's Misrule''. Zhe also became the leader of the self-proclaimed [[Sovereign Latitudes of Maracavanya]], a nation of pirates and criminals that numbered ten thousand beings. The Super Star [[Destroyer]] became the center of Eleodie Maracavanya's new nation, which had no planet and was instead spread across ''Liberty's Misrule'' and two dozen starships, along with a contingent of starfighters that flew the nation's [[color]]s: red, yellow, and black. Maracavanya's Sovereign Latitudes was replenished by stolen ships and pirates and refugees who had fled into Wild Space. These pirates and refugees built ramshackle homes and markets in the Super Star Destroyer's [[hangar]] bays. Maracavanya also found a new confidante in the form of Kartessa, the rebellious teenage girl zhe had encountered on the ''Starfall''.<ref name="Empire's End" />

When Kartessa revealed that she had abandoned her [[Parent|mother]] on the engineering sublevel for not leaving her alone, Maracavanya chastised the girl for disrespecting her mother. After a few minutes of scuffing her heels, Kartessa asked Maracavanya how zher pirate nation would work out. Zhe explained that it was better for the pirates to band together than to fight and kill each other for scraps. Kartessa pointed out that zhe had not answered her question and that pirates were naturally selfish. Maracavanya countered that pirates had a mutual benefit for working together. Zhe reassured her that they would kill less because of the threat of their numbers. Zhe also vowed to share the spoils equally.<ref name="Empire's End" />

[[File:ISDIIESB.png|thumb|right|250px|An ''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]
The two were interrupted by Shi Shu, who informed them that they had visitors. Maracavanya quickly deduced from the hyperspeed vectors and recent battle damage on the three [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]] that they were fleeing the Battle of [[Jakku]]. The [[Ssori]] [[gunner]] [[Carklin Ryoon]] and Shi Shu suggested tricking the Imperials and [[Destruction of Imperial Star Destroyers|stealing]] their ships. However, Maracavanya ordered zher crew to destroy them and their escape pods and send their pieces to the New Republic in order to curry the new government's favor. Zhe proposed charging the New Republic for their clean-up.<ref name="Empire's End" />

When Kartessa countered that the New Republic was not their friend, Maracavanya responded that destroying the Star Destroyers could give them some leverage in their dealings with the Republic. When Kartessa added that the ships had considerable firepower, Maracavanya promised to repay the other fleet captains for any damage. After Shi Shu ordered the rest of the bridge crew to activate the targeting computers, weapons systems, and tractor beam, Kartessa asked Maracavanya if zhe knew what zhe was doing. Zhe admitted zhe was taking a leap into the unknown and likened life to a pair of dice. The ''Liberty's Misrule'' wiped out the weakened Star Destroyers.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Girl, it's like this: The sea is changing, and the tides are shifting. It's about to get real nasty for us nasty types. Either we're gonna be running from the new sheriff in town, or we're gonna be trying to kill each other in the farthest-flung dung-heap systems, stabbing each other over a few scraps of what was once ours by right. I'm proposing we get together and we stay together. Scoundrels like us, we always worked together&mdash;it just wasn't official. So I'm making it official.|Eleodie Maracavanya's vision of the Sovereign Latitudes of Maracavanya, a pirate nation|<ref name="Empire's End" />}}
Eleodie Maracavanya's personal pronouns were primarily "zhe," "zher," and "zherself," while occasionally alternating those with "his" or "her." Zhe had golden [[eye]]s and zher natural voice was loud and alive, vibrating with deep intensity. Maracavanya believed that pirates should work together for their mutual benefit and sought to create a new pirate state based on freedom, greed, and self-interest.<ref name="Life Debt" /> Zhe also believed in sharing the spoils of zher catch equally among zher underlings. Zher vision led to creating the pirate nation known as the Sovereign Latitudes of Maracavanya.<ref name="Empire's End" />

A self-described realist, Maracavanya was an opportunistic<ref name="Life Debt" /> and politically shrewd pirate leader<ref name="Empire's End" /> who took advantage of the slow death of the Empire following the Battle of [[Endor]]. Although zhe was skeptical of the New Republic and believed that the foundling government had little chance of saving the galaxy,<ref name="Life Debt" /> zhe sought to gain leverage with them by destroying Imperial Star Destroyers.<ref name="Empire's End" />

Zhe admired a rebellious young Chandrilan girl named Kartessa who joined zher crew out of a desire for adventure and to spite her mother.<ref name="Life Debt" /> However, when Kartessa abandoned her mother in the engineering deck, Maracavanya chastised her for the disrespect. Zhe also had philosophical discussions with the young woman and kept her apprised of developments.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Equipment==
Maracavanya wore a shimmering,<ref name="Life Debt" /> chromatophoral [[cape]]<ref name="Empire's End" /> made of chromatic scales. When addressing a captive audience, zhe spoke with a vocoder that was worn around zher throat and modified zher voice to make it velvety, rich, and vibrating. Zhe also carried an electro-scythe that appeared to be a long, baroque staff.<ref name="Life Debt" />
