The '''''Cataclysm''''' was an [[Onager-class Star Destroyer|''Onager''-class Star Destroyer]] in service to the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. At one point, the [[Star Destroyer]] was stationed near two [[gravity well]]s, and was approached by a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] flanked by a pair of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. The ''Cataclysm'' was equipped with [[engine]]s, a pair of [[superweapon]]s at its bow,<ref name="Armada" /> and two [[deflector shield generator dome]]s.<ref name="Card Trader" /> As an Onager, the ''Cataclysm'' was 1,288 [[meter]]s in length.<ref name="Ships" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Cataclysm'' was first mentioned in the [[2020]] ''[[Onager-class Star Destroyer Expansion Pack]]'', an expansion for the miniatures game [[Star Wars: Armada|''Star Wars'': Armada]] by [[Fantasy Flight Games]], on a card of the same name.<ref name="Armada" />
