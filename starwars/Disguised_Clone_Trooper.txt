'''Disguised Clone Troopers'''<ref name="Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> were members of the [[501st Legion]] who donned [[Jedi apparel]] during<ref name="Kit Reveal">{{EA|url=games/starwars/galaxy-of-heroes/news/kit-reveal-disguised-clone-trooper|text=Kit Reveal: Disguised Clone Trooper}}</ref> and after the [[Operation: Knightfall|siege of the Jedi Temple]] in order to ambush any surviving [[Jedi]] who ventured to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="French Novel" /> Those [[trooper]]s who adopted the garb were highly skilled in ambush tactics and successfully took down a number of approaching Jedi, who never reached close enough to see through their disguises.<ref name="GalaxyNEws">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'' newsletter</ref> Proving to be more hurtful to him than the damage done to the building itself, the sight was distressing to [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who [[Infiltration of the Jedi Temple|infiltrated]] the Temple alongside [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]].<ref name="French Novel" /> After fighting through the 501st troopers outside the building,<ref name="Rots">{{Film|III}}</ref> the Jedi made their way into the building and evaded the internal patrols, only to be greeted with the carnage of the 501st's attack, a sight that hurt Kenobi even more than the damage and Disguised Clone Troopers had.<ref name="French Novel" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|You clones make terrible Jedi.|Obi-Wan Kenobi, in a deleted scene|<ref name="Complete Saga">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Clone_in_Jedi_disguise.jpg|thumb|left|180px|A behind the scenes photo of Temuera Morrison as a Disguised Clone Trooper]]
Disguised Clone Troopers first appeared in [[canon]] through the French novelization of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], ''[[Revenge of the Sith - The Film Novel]]''.<ref name="French Novel" /> In their first pictured appearance, they later appeared as a playable character in the video game ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'' and were first identified there as "Disguised Clone Troopers."<ref name="Galaxy" /> According to the Kit Reveal for their appearance in ''Galaxy of Heroes'', they were added into the game to provide more clones who were visually distinct yet still present for the events of Operation: Knightfall.<ref name="Kit Reveal" />

The concept of the Disguised Clone Troopers was considered for ''Revenge of the Sith'' and were featured in a deleted scene, in which Kenobi and Yoda approach the Jedi Temple and are greeted by several clones pretending to be Jedi who have recaptured the Temple. Quickly seeing through the act, Kenobi and Yoda fight through the detachment. An early animation of this scene was included in the [[2011]] ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]'' Blu-ray collection.<ref name="animation test">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''</ref> A production photo of a blaster-wielding [[Temuera Morrison]], the actor for clone troopers, in the Jedi disguise garb was also taken and featured within the ''Revenge of the Sith'' DVD shows.<ref name="III movie extras">{{Film|III}}, DVD extras.</ref> Despite being cut from the film, the Disguised Clone Troopers appeared within the film's novelization through the members of [[Decoy Squad Five]]<ref name="IIINovel">[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (novelization)|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'' novelization]]</ref> and were mentioned within [[Star Wars: Episode III — Revenge of the Sith 4|issue four]] of its [[Star Wars: Episode III — Revenge of the Sith (comic series)|comic book adaption]].<ref name="III comics">[[Star Wars: Episode III — Revenge of the Sith 4|''Star Wars: Episode III — Revenge of the Sith'' 4]]</ref> The Kit Reveal for ''Galaxy of Heroes'' makes reference to Decoy Squad Five and its role in the novelization.<ref name="Kit Reveal" />
