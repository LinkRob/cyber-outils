'''Cloud-tree root tea''' was a type of [[tea]] made from the [[Plant|roots]] of the [[cloud-tree]], which was found on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[swamp]] [[planet]] [[Dagobah]]. The [[Foodstuff|beverage]], served in mugs while [[Temperature|hot]], was made by the [[Yoda's species|alien]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] during his [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] on the planet<ref name="Atlas" /> from [[19 BBY]] to [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Yoda eventually included a menu listing the tea&mdash;as well as the [[Mud and Bug Soup]] and [[Swamp Stew]] meals&mdash;in a [[Yoda's Guide to the Galaxy|travel guide]] he [[author]]ed.<ref name="Atlas" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cloud-tree root tea was mentioned in the [[2021]] [[Canon|non-canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Yoda's Galaxy Atlas]]''.<ref name="Atlas" />
