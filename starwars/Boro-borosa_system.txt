The '''Boro-borosa system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Senex sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> [[Pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] planned to hold an auction there to sell a forgery of [[Smuggler's Guide|Maz Kanata's Journal]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Boro-borosa system was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and released in [[2018]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
