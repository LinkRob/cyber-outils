The '''Firing Range''' was a location on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] advertised on a sign within the [[Coruscant Underworld (battlefield)|Coruscant Underworld]] battlefield within the [[Grand Arena]]. The sign consisted of a vertical rectangle with various [[neon]] [[Color|blue]], red, and yellow designs, as well as black components, giving it an almost 3D effect. A large target-like circle was showcased on one side, with various small depictions of [[blaster]] bolt holes dotted the background. In front was an illustration of both an [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] and a [[DH-17 blaster pistol]], both mid-firing, and both reversed and mirrored on either side of the design. At the forefront were the words "Firing Range" stacked vertically in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="Hunters" />
