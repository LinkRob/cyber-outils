The '''Arakhyn''' were a [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]] of ruthless [[Hunting/Legends|hunters]].

==Biology and appearance==
The Arakhyns were [[Humanoid/Legends|humanoids]], with four [[Limb/Legends|limbs]]. Tall and muscular, all [[Gender/Legends|male]] Arakhyns are instinctive and relentless.

==Society and culture==
At a very young age, all male Arakhyns were abandoned on hostile [[Planet/Legends|planets]] to pass rituals to adulthood. Those rituals consist primarily of surviving, killing hostile beings, and escaping from the planet. The Arakhyn spoke with growls, and sometimes used [[Galactic Basic Standard/Legends|basic]] translators.

Proud to the extreme, the Arakhyn didn't always measure the danger they faced and were often seen racing straight to the biggest threat, ignoring the lesser, seemingly weakest creatures.

==Arakhyns in the galaxy==
Arakhyns were mythical, lone hunters, chasing the biggest prey throughout the entire [[The galaxy/Legends|galaxy]]. They tolerated valorous humanoids at their side if it can lead them to greater exploits.

==Behind the scenes==
The Arakhyn, along with [[Schizode]], [[Slugh]] and [[Sylphe]] species, were created by [[Patrick Bousquet]] and [[Christophe Debien]] for an ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] game help which appeared in the French magazine [[Casus Belli 95|''Casus Belli'' 95]]. The species was illustrated by [[Thierry Ségur]]. The character blank card mistakenly mentioned "Slugh" for the species.
