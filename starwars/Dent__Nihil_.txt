'''Dent''', formerly known as '''Ultident Margrona''', was a [[human]] who held the rank of [[Cloud (rank)|Cloud]] in [[Lourna Dee]]'s [[Lourna Dee's Tempest|Tempest]] of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization. Dent led an [[Raid on Elphrona|operation]] to kidnap the [[Blythe family]] from [[Elphrona]] in order to ransom them to wealthy relatives on [[Alderaan]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> in [[232 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> However, the [[Jedi]] stationed at an [[Elphrona Outpost|outpost]] on the [[planet]] found out about the kidnapping and pursued, ultimately resulting in the pirate's death at the hands of Jedi [[Indeera Stokes]] while she was rescuing [[Ronn Blythe]] from Dent's [[starship]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
