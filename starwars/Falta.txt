'''Falta''' was a [[Dathomirian]] [[witch]] who lived in the final years of the [[Republic Era]]. Belonging to no clan, she was renowned for her skills at making magical objects, and lived in a [[Falta's home|hut]] on the far side of the [[swamp]] near the [[Nightsister lair|Nightsister fortress]]. She had owned a few [[rancor]]s. Falta eventually used her skills to perform an unprecedented act of [[magick]], creating for herself a daughter whom she named [[Yenna]]. [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]], the leader of the [[Nightsisters]], was intrigued by Yenna, and eventually persuaded the girl to join her clan, despite Falta's worries.<ref name="Bug" />

Around [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates {{StoryCite|story=Bug (short story)|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}} to circa [[20 BBY]].</ref> after the [[Battle of Dathomir]] in which the Nightsisters were almost completely wiped out, Falta traveled to the Nightsister fortress in search of her daughter, and was told by the dying witch [[Daka]] that Talzin had had Yenna evacuated off of [[Dathomir]] during the battle. Falta's search eventually brought her and her pet [[tooka]], [[Ichor]], to the [[planetoid]] [[Sídi]], where she met a young [[human]] girl named [[Bug (Sídi)|Bug]] and found a transmission indicating Yenna had been sent in a pod to somewhere in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. When she left Sídi, Falta took Bug with her as her new apprentice.<ref name="Bug" />
