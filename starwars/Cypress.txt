'''Cypress''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who served as a [[general]] in the [[Resistance Army|ground forces]] of the [[Resistance]], a paramilitary group founded by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to monitor and [[Cold War|oppose]] the actions of the [[First Order]]. Thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], the First Order used [[Starkiller Base]], an [[Ilum|ice planet]] turned into a [[Superweapon|megaweapon]], to [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroy]] the entire [[Hosnian system]], dealing a major blow to the [[New Republic]] by eliminating its current [[Politics|government]]. In response, the Resistance set off to destroy the megaweapon. During the ensuing [[Battle of Starkiller Base|assault]] on Starkiller Base, Cypress was among the [[officer]]s who monitored the [[starfighter]]s from the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]].<ref name="TFA" />

==Behind the scenes==
In [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], General Cypress was played by [[Gerald W. Abrams|Gerry Abrams]], the father of the movie's director [[J.J. Abrams]]. He can be seen to the left of the screen when [[C-3PO]] declares the Resistance pilots "need a miracle." Although Cypress was identified as a captain in the movie credits, the rank badge he wears<ref name="TFA" /> is that of an army [[general]].<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Abrams later reprised this role for [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
