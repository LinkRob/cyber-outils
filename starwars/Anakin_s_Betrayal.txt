"'''Anakin's Betrayal'''" is a piece written by [[John Williams]] for the [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith (soundtrack)|Revenge of the Sith soundtrack]]. It represents the tragic turn of [[Anakin Skywalker]] to the [[dark side of the Force]]. It was performed by the [[London Symphony Orchestra]] and the [[London Voices]]. The piece won Williams a Grammy nomination for Best Film Score. A [[Descent motif|motif]] from [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Attack of the Clones'']] forms the central structure of the piece. It first appears when [[Darth Sidious]] issues [[Order 66]] to the [[Clone trooper|clone troopers]], and plays through the deaths of multiple [[Jedi]] across the galaxy. A slightly altered version appears on Mustafar as Padmé talks to Anakin, where it begins as she realizes he's no longer the same person she loved, and plays through Anakin's conversation with Obi-Wan, where it concludes the moment they ignite their lightsabers.

In the [[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker (soundtrack)|''The Rise of Skywalker'' soundtrack]] for the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], "Anakin's Betrayal" appears in the track "The Force Is with You," where it morphes into "[[Rey's Theme]]," which in turn transitions to "[[The Emperor's Theme]]."
