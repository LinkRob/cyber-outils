'''Cylo's whale fleet''' was a massive [[fleet]] made out of organic [[whale-ship]]s that was led by [[Doctor]] [[Cylo]] himself and held [[Cylo's base]]. Cylo planned to [[Cylo Hijacking|hijack]] the ''[[Executor]]'', which was under construction over [[Kuat]], but his plans ultimately failed and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] used [[the Force]] and convince the whale-ships to fly into a [[star]] in the [[Kuat system]], thus destroying the entire whale-ship fleet.<ref name="Darth Vader 25" />
