During the [[Operations on Tatooine]], a massive force of the local [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Tatooine garrison|garrison]] '''attacked''' the [[Saponza's homestead|main base of operations]] for the [[Tatooine rebel cell]] in response to a string of attacks against Imperial facilities across [[Tatooine]], including a recent [[Attack on Fort Sunfire|attack]] resulting in the destruction of [[Fort Sunfire]]. The attack was led by Imperial [[Captain]] [[Kosh]] of the [[68th Legion]], who assaulted the base several Imperial armored vehicles including an [[Elite AT-AT]], nine [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s, and two [[2-M Saber-class repulsor tank|2-M Saber-class]] Hover Tanks. Despite causing heavy damage to the rebel base, the 68th Legion was repelled and all of their mechanized forces were destroyed, resulting in a [[Second attack on the Tatooine rebel cell's headquarters (68th Legion)|second attack]] led by the 68th Legion shortly after.<ref name="Commander" />
