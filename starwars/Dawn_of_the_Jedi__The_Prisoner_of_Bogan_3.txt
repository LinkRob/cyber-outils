'''''Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan'' 3''' is the third issue in the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan|''Prisoner of Bogan'']] story arc of the ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series and the eighth issue of the series in total. It is set in the earliest days of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]], in a [[Pre-Republic era|time before]] the polarization of the [[Light side of the Force/Legends|light]] and [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark sides]] of [[The Force/Legends|the Force]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Xesh and Daegen Lok are on a mission to build Forcesabers—a weapon that Lok knows will take him closer to his vision of being leader of the Je'daii''.

''But the Je'daii Rangers are on the pair's trail, and this first encounter between the Je'daii and the prisoners of Bogan will carry heavy consequences!''
