'''Blast shields''' were a type of shield.<ref name="BBH" /> The term blast shield could refer to a large, handheld shield, such as the [[M3 Bulwark Blast Shield]] used by the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]],<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref> or the blast shield on a [[helmet]] that covered the wearer's face.<ref name="SW:ANH" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], clone troopers used M3 Bulwark Blast Shields at the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]]<ref name="The Unknown" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GEDates">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], including ''[[Star Wars: Batuu Bounty Hunters]]'', to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[EGL power droid]] [[B0-LT]] had illegally modified itself with a blast shield which, along with the [[droid]]'s reprogramming and evasion of arrest, resulted in a 525 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] [[bounty]] being placed on its head while it hid on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="BBH" />
