'''Bihun''' was a [[gender]]less [[Wrosidian]] "related" to the fellow Wrosidian [[Basko]]&mdash;who originated from the same [[Plant|stalk]]&mdash;although the two were not siblings.<ref name="TVG" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> Bihun and Basko were seated at a table in<ref name="LF">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> the [[Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop]] on the [[planet]] [[Ueda]],<ref name="TVG" /> [[Diet|eating]] [[noodle]]s with<ref name="LF" /> the [[Reshtini]] [[Kesmer Faloodi]].<ref name="TVG" /> The [[human]] [[assassin]]<ref name="LF" /> [[Mae-ho Aniseya]]<ref name="TVG" /> entered the shop during Bihun's stay there and passed by their table on her way to [[Indara's murder|murder]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]]. Bihun and Basko abandoned their table during the assassin's subsequent fight with the [[Jedi]] and her companions.<ref name="LF" />

Bihun had [[eye]]s with [[Color|red]] sclera and brown irises and their skin was green, shifting to brown on their [[nose]] and having a purple tint on some parts of their face, such as around the eyes. During their noodle shop visit, the Wrosidian wore dark gray [[robe]]s, a large [[belt]], and a [[Clothing|collar]] that covered their shoulders.<ref name="TVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I was given key creatures to design and I also had a new Junior designer (Nico Ferrard) helping me at the start of the production on the twin noodle characters which was great fun.|Tim Berry|<ref name="Tim Berry">{{WookCite|url=p/4400000000003961302|text=Interview with Tim Berry|forum=1}}</ref>}}
Bihun first appeared, albeit unidentified, in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="LF" /> the first episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] series [[Star Wars: The Acolyte|''The Acolyte'']], which aired on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="EPG">{{SW|url=series/the-acolyte/lost-found-episode-guide|text="Lost / Found" Episode Guide &#124; The Acolyte}}</ref> Prior to their debut in the episode, the Wrosidian was first depicted in a trailer shown exclusively at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{CelebrationTrailer|''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''|Celebration Europe (2023)}}</ref> on [[April 7]], [[2023]], during the [[Lucasfilm]] Studio Showcase.<ref name="Studio Showcase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-lucasfilm-studio-showcase|text=SWCE 2023: 11 Highlights from Lucasfilm's Studio Showcase|date=2023-04-08}}</ref> The individual was later identified as Bihun in the ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]], published in [[2025]].<ref name="TVG" /> Bihun was brought to life by a droid and creature FX team that was led by the creative supervisor [[Neal Scanlan]],<ref name="Design">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-creature-shop-bazil-reveal|text=Inside ''The Acolyte'' Creature Shop: Meet Bazil, the Tynnan Tracker - First Details}}</ref> and the noodle shop scenes<ref name="Fantha Part I">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fanthatracks.com/news/the-acolyte-a-journey-to-the-filming-locations-part-1-madeira|text=The Acolyte: A journey to the filming locations Part 1: Madeira|work=[https://www.fanthatracks.com/ Fantha Tracks]|archivedate=20241204220147}}</ref> that featured them<ref name="LF" /> were filmed at [[Shinfield Studios]],<ref name="Fantha Part I" /> [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="Julian Foddy">{{Lucasfilm|url=news/julian-foddy-acolyte|text=''The Acolyte'' Visual Effects Supervisor Julian Foddy}}</ref>

The Wrosidian was designed<ref name="Tim Berry" /> and built<ref name="Tim insta">{{Instagram|username=timatronics|url=p/C8KyF_CKYZq|name=[[Tim Berry]]|date=2024-06-13|quote=The two noodle creatures - Animatronic design and build by myself and the then animatronic padawan Nicolas Ferrard @obviouslycookies Performed by Hannah Hornsby and Caitlin Nicholas. Puppeteered by Wim Openhiemer and Patrick Comberford.|archivedate=https://archive.ph/PIvlN}}</ref> by senior designer [[Tim Berry]] with help from junior designer [[Nicolas Ferrard]] at the start of production.<ref name="Tim Berry" /> Bihun was performed by [[Hannah Hornsby]] and [[Caitlin Nicholas]], and puppeteered by [[Wim Oppenheimer]] and [[Patrick Comberford]].<ref name="Tim insta" /> Concept art of Bihun was created by artist [[Andre Gilbert]] and included in ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', a<ref name="Art of Acolyte">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''</ref> [[2026]]<ref name="Abrams">{{Abrams|url=product/art-of-star-wars-the-acolyte_9781419777349|text=The Art of Star Wars: The Acolyte}}</ref> reference book written by [[Kristin Baver]].<ref name="Art of Acolyte" /> In real life, [[Wikipedia:Bihun goreng|bihun]] is an [[Wikipedia:Indonesia|Indonesian]] noodle dish<ref name="Bihun">{{WebCite|url=https://takestwoeggs.com/bihun-goreng/#h-what-is-bihun-goreng|text=Bihun Goreng (Vermicelli Stir Fry)|work=[https://takestwoeggs.com/ Takes Two Eggs]|archivedate=20250326114738}}</ref> and [[Wikipedia:Bakso|bakso]] is an Indonesian type of [[Wikipedia:Meatball soup|meatball soup]].<ref name="Bakso">{{WebCite|url=https://www.tasteatlas.com/bakso|text=Bakso|work=[https://www.tasteatlas.com/ Taste Atlas]|archivedate=20250326113050}}</ref> The two dishes are sometimes served together.<ref name="Bihun Bakso">{{WordPress|subdomain=myaromakitchen|url=2019/02/24/stir-fried-vermicelli-with-meat-ball-bihun-goreng-bakso|text=Stir fried Vermicelli with Meat ball (Bihun goreng bakso)|name=MyAromaKitchen|archivedate=20250326121336}}</ref>
