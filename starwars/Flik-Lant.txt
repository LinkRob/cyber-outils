'''Flik-Lant''' was a [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was stationed at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Core Worlds]] planet<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]], where he worked as the [[Jedi temple|Temple's]] [[metalsmith]]. At one point, he created a [[Avar Kriss' first diademdiadem|diadem]] for [[Jedi]] [[Stellan Gios]], which he then gave to his friend and fellow Jedi [[Avar Kriss]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> Jedi Master [[Elzar Mann]], another of Kriss' friends, decided to not visit Flik-Lant when he wanted to have a new [[Avar Kriss' second diadem|diadem]] made for Kriss after she had sold her first while trapped in the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Occlusion Zone]]. Mann instead visited the metalsmith [[Limal Pakt]], who operated a [[Limal Pakt's shop|shop]] in Coruscant's [[Federal District]]. Once she was finished, Pakt teasingly stated to Mann that she would like to have a drink with Flik-Lant to trade secrets about their craft. Mann, however, [[Brain|mentally]] noted that he would not tell Flik-Lant that as he knew that Flik-Lant was particular about his work.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
