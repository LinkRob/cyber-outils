An '''engineering officer''' was an [[officer]] that worked in [[Engineer|engineering fields]]. The the [[Galactic Empire]]'s engineering officers wore [[Imperial officer's uniform]]s with [[Rank insignia plaque]]s that denoted the engineering officer's [[military rank]],<ref name="OYL" /> and the [[New Republic]]'s engineering officers were stationed on [[starship]]s.<ref name="Victory's Price" /> The [[Imperial officer]] [[Soden Petigar]] was an engineering officer for the Galactic Empire,<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> as was an [[Unidentified engineer officer|attendee]] of the [[Maltheen Divide conference]].<ref name="OYL" />
