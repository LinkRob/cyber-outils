'''Dankayo''' was a location<ref name="Star Wars: Scum and Villainy" /> in the [[Dankayo system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Colonies]] that was a suspected base of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] operations. Dankayo and several other locations were identified in a [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Year Ref">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' establishes that this report was generated in the [[Standard year|year]] 7980 C.R.C., the same year that [[Han Solo]] was frozen in [[carbonite]]. Given that Solo was frozen in carbonite during the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' established took place in [[3 ABY]], it can be concluded that 7980 C.R.C. is equivalent to 3 ABY.</ref> [[Imperial Security Bureau]] report that generated targets for [[Viper probe droid]] surveys by cross-referencing areas with a high degree of suspicious activity with the unsettled parts of the galaxy favored by the rebels.<ref name="Star Wars: Scum and Villainy" />
