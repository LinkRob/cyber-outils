A '''Forward Command Center''' was a mobile command station used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s established a command center on the front lines of the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]] during the [[First Battle of Geonosis|first battle]] of the war. As the [[Jedi]] adopted the role of ''de facto'' [[general]]s of the [[Cloning|clone]] [[army]], leading thousands of [[soldier]]s onto the battlefield against the [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the overall battle was overseen from the Forward Command Center by [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] of the [[Jedi Order]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:BailOrganaOnChristophsis-OtFL.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Bail Organa used a forward command center to command his relief effort on Christophsis.]]
A Forward Command Center<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> was a small command center used by the [[Republic Military]]. The center had a circular design and contained several [[computer]] screens, which allowed [[clone trooper]]s to give orders to other locations, including the [[Cloning|clone]] crews commanding [[artillery]] like [[Self-Propelled Heavy Artillery]] Turbolasers. The centers could be staffed by a [[commanding officer]], who could be a [[Clone Commander]], a [[Jedi General]],<ref name="AOTC" /> or even a [[senator]].<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> During the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], a [[Clone Captain]] was also stationed at the center.<ref name="AOTC" /> [[Military rank|Higher ranking]] clones and officials were not always at command of the stations, however. During the [[Battle of Christophsis]]<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> and [[Second Battle of Geonosis]],<ref name="Legacy of Terror">{{TCW|Legacy of Terror}}</ref> Forward Command Centers were staffed only by several regular clone troopers.<ref name="TCW film" /><ref name="Legacy of Terror" />

From a Forward Command Center, a Clone Commander and their [[Jedi]] [[officer]] could command forces of the [[Galactic Republic]] against their enemies,<ref name="AOTC" /> or it could be used as the command center for a [[Galactic Senate]] relief mission.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> The command centers also possessed small ramps that could be raised off the ground to reach [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic Gunships]] for easier disembarkment.<ref name="AOTC" />

==History==
===Battle of Geonosis===
{{Quote|Master Yoda, all forward positions are advancing.''"<br />"''Very good&hellip; ''Very'' good.|A Clone Commander and Yoda|<ref name="AOTC" />}}
[[File:Forward_Command_Center_AotC.png|thumb|right|250px|A Forward Command Center was established on Geonosis during the push against the droid army.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the newly-formed [[Grand Army of the Republic]] deployed thousands of clone troopers to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[planet]] of [[Geonosis]].<ref name="AOTC" /> Led by a cadre of ''de facto'' Jedi [[general]]s,<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> the Galactic Republic's [[Republic Military|forces]] issued orders and coordinated strategy from a Forward Command Center staffed by a [[Unidentified Clone Commander (Geonosis)|Clone Commander]], a Clone Captain, and several clones during the Battle of Geonosis. After delivering the survivors of [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]]'s [[Jedi assault team|assault team]] to the front line of the battlefield, a Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry [[gunship]] [[transport]]ed [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], leader of the [[Jedi Order]], to the Republic mobile command station.<ref name="AOTC" />

As the Republic [[troop]]s advanced on the enemy,<ref name="AOTC" /> establishing air and ground supremacy under the skillful leadership of the [[Jedi High Council]],<ref name="CL" /> Yoda ordered the Republic Self-Propelled Heavy Artillery Turbolasers to concentrate all of their firepower on the nearest [[Trade Federation]] [[core ship]]. After the [[commander]] informed Yoda that the [[Separatist Droid Army]] had begun a full withdrawal from the battle, the Grand Master departed from the command center to pursue the Separatist [[Head of State]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]].<ref name="AOTC" />

===Battle of Christophsis===
====Organa's mission====
{{Quote|They're heading for Bail Organa and the command center! They're going to bomb it!|Wullf Yularen|<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />}}
[[File:BailOrganaForwardCommandCenter-CatAndMouse.png|thumb|left|250px|Bail Organa oversaw a relief mission on Christophsis from a Forward Command Center.]]
Shortly after the Battle of Geonosis, the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] world of [[Christophsis]] was [[Battle of Christophsis|invaded]] by the Separatists. The planet was [[Christophsis blockade|blockaded]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederacy navy]], effectively cutting Christophsis off from the greater [[The galaxy|galaxy]]. In an effort to aid the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Human|Christophsians]], the Galactic Senate sanctioned a relief mission to Christophsis led by [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], the [[Galaxy|galactic]] senator of [[Alderaan]].<ref name="FL">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> Organa oversaw the Republic's relief efforts from a Forward Command Center,<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> but had to contend with the [[battle droid]]s of General [[Whorm Loathsom]].<ref name="FL" />

As the Republic's supplies on Christophsis dwindled, Organa beseeched [[Coruscant]] to send aid but the Separatist [[fleet]], under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Trench]], was able to prevent the [[Republic Navy]] from breaking through the [[blockade]]. Therefore, the Republic deployed an experimental [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette|stealth ship]] to Christophsis under the command of Jedi General [[Anakin Skywalker]] and Admiral [[Wullf Yularen]]. Trench dispatched a [[squadron]] of [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Bombers]] to obliterate Organa's command center in an effort to draw out the cloaked [[Starship|vessel]], causing Skywalker to go on the offensive. Organa contacted Jedi General [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and informed him of the imminent attack on his command station, knowing there was little chance of survival from a bombing run. However, Skywalker was able to outmaneuver the admiral, resulting in the destruction of his [[flagship]] ''[[Invincible]]''. With the loss of the Separatist [[command ship]], Skywalker and Yularen completed their mission by delivering supplies to Organa and the Republic forces on Christophsis.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />

====Kenobi and Skywalker on the ground====
[[File:Forward_command_center_on_Christophsis.png|thumb|right|250px|The Forward Fommand Center used by the Jedi's forces.]]
The destruction of Trench's flagship<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> allowed the Republic fleet to break through the Separatist blockade and finally let the Republic to land its troops on the ground. Sometime after Kenobi and Skywalker landed their soldiers to engage the enemy, however, the CIS were able to reinforce their blockade once more, trapping the Jedi on the ground with dwindling supplies. In addition to this, Skywalker, Kenobi, and their clone troopers had to deal with the approaching [[Whorm Loathsom's army|droid army]] commanded by Loathsom. During this time, the Jedi used a Forward Command Center to help command their troops.<ref name="TCW film" />

Later in the battle, Skywalker was assigned [[Ahsoka Tano]] as his Jedi [[Padawan]], and Skywalker, Kenobi, Tano, and Clone Captain [[Rex]] discussed their plan to combat the Confederacy's [[deflector shield]] nearby this command center. The group created a plan where Kenobi, Rex, and the rest of the clones would draw the droids into the [[city]], while Skywalker and Tano went behind enemy lines to take out the [[deflector shield generator]]. Through this plan the Republic was able to take out the shield, and their [[AV-7 Anti-vehicle Artillery Cannon]]s were then able to destroy the enemy battle droids and [[armored cavalry]].<ref name="TCW film" />

===The Second Battle of Geonosis===
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="GA" /> due to vital intelligence about a [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|droid factory]] on Geonosis gained by Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] during the [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia]],<ref name="Senate Spy">{{TCW|Senate Spy}}</ref> the Republic launched a [[Second Battle of Geonosis|second invasion]] of Geonosis. The Republic were able to break through the defenses<ref name="Landing">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> and destroy the primary foundry.<ref name="WF">{{TCW|Weapons Factory}}</ref> After that victory, the Republic set up a Forward Command Center nearby the destroyed factory and staffed it with by several clone troopers. The command center was surrounded by LAAT gunships and [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|''Consular''-class cruisers]]. Nearby were several [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class transgalactic military assault ships]], and one of the Acclamators were also used as a command center by the Jedi.<ref name="Legacy of Terror" />

[[File:Republic_gunship_flying.png|thumb|left|300px|A Forward Command Center surrounded by Republic vehicles]]
After the Command Center and surrounding vehicles had been set up, Jedi Master [[Luminara Unduli]] and clone trooper [[Buzz]] set out on [[BARC speeder]]s to hunt [[Poggle the Lesser]], leader of the [[Geonosian]]s. This lead to an encounter with [[Karina the Great]], the current [[queen]] of the Geonosians, but Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin Skywalker, and a group of clones from the [[212th Attack Battalion]] arrived to reinforce Luminara. This group rescued her, captured Poggle, and defeated Karina the Great.<ref name="Legacy of Terror" /> A command center was set up by the wreckage of the [[Progate Temple]] and nearby the [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigate]] [[TB-73]]. This Forward Command Center was staffed by a group of clone troopers.<ref name="Brain Invaders">{{TCW|Brain Invaders}}</ref>
