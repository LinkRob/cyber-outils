'''Durite''' was a shiny [[Color|black]] metal used in the housing of [[IT-O interrogation droid|IT-O Interrogation Units]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[14 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' takes place five [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Great Jedi Purge]], which is dated to [[19 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'' is set in [[14 BBY]].</ref> the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] acquired enough durite in an [[Abandoned Village]] on the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]] to fashion a [[lightsaber]] hilt.<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>
{{EndGame}}
