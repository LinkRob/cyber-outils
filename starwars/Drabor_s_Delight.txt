'''Drabor's Delight''' was an [[alcohol]]ic specialty [[cocktail]] served in the [[Sublight Lounge]] on board the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''<ref name="Cruiser" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The drink could be consumed by [[human]]s and was [[Color|red]] with a slice of [[fruit]]. The beverage shared its name with the ship's designer, the [[Anzellan]] [[Shug Drabor]].<ref name="Halcyon Legacy">''[[Halcyon Legacy 1]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Drabor's Delight was an alcoholic beverage available in the [[in-universe|real world]] at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> It was last served during the [[September 28]][[September 29|&ndash;]][[September 30|30]] voyage of [[2023]], the final voyage before the closure of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser.<ref name="Official website">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref> The out-of-universe beverage used [[Wikipedia:Gin|gin]], [[Wikipedia:Vermouth|vermouth]], and [[Wikipedia:Campari|bitter and sweet]], and was topped with an [[Wikipedia:Orange (fruit)|orange]] slice.<ref name="Cruiser" /> It has been compared to the real-world cocktail [[Wikipedia:Negroni|negroni]]<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2022/03/review-out-of-this-world-cocktails-from-the-sublight-lounge-on-the-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/|text=REVIEW: Out of This World Cocktails from the Sublight Lounge on the Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|author=Francis, Katie|date=2022-03-04|work=[https://www.wdwnt.com WDW News Today]|archivedate=20230925035514}}</ref>
