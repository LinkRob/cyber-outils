A '''battle''' took place<ref name="Wild Space" /> during the [[22 BBY/Legends|first year]]<ref name="ERC" /> of the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Korriban/Legends|Korriban]]. [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Eeth Koth/Legends|Eeth Koth]] led a detachment of [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]] to defend the besieged people of Korriban, but his [[Troop/Legends|troops]] suffered numerous [[Death/Legends|casualties]] against [[Unidentified anti-Republic force|their enemy]]. His fellow [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] had a brief [[Force vision/Legends|vision]] of the battle while on a [[Mission to Zigoola|mission]] to the planet [[Zigoola]].<ref name="Wild Space" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle was briefly mentioned in ''[[The Clone Wars: Wild Space]]'', a [[2008]] novel written by [[Karen Miller]].<ref name="Wild Space" />
