During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] carried out an '''assault''' on a [[Separatist weapons depot|weapons depot]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The mission was assigned by [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]] to a [[Heater's squad|squad]] of [[clone trooper]] deserters led by [[Heater (clone trooper)|Heater]]. The [[squad]] managed to infiltrate the depot and plant [[detonator]]s, but were confronted by the [[Unidentified super tactical droid (Melagawni)|super tactical droid]] in charge of the facility. The [[tactical droid]] was destroyed by [[Racetrack (clone trooper)|Racetrack]], a trooper who had considered deserting again during the mission, allowing the squad to destroy the facility. Following the mission, Heater and the other clones chose to return to Republic service.<ref name="Battle Tales 4" />
