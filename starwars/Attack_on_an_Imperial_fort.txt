An '''attack on an Imperial fort'''<ref name="BTC" /> took place in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> on the [[planet]] [[Coyerti]] when the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] assaulted a strategic [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] fortification in the battle for the planet. Consisting of at least a dozen [[squad]]s, the plan was devised by one of the squad leaders, [[Carver]]. Following a lengthy effort to take out an Imperial [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]], [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] and [[Namir's squad|his squad]] watched the flashes of the battle erupt from afar and predicted the fort would be defeated soon enough. The battle subsequently ended in a victory for the Sixty-First.<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on an Imperial fort appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
