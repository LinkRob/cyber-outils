'''Central Processing'''<ref name="Star Wars 4">[[Star Wars (2020) 4|''Star Wars'' (2020) 4]]</ref> was the central command mainframe of [[Cloud City]], located on [[Level 109]]. [[Lobot]] was connected to the mainframe in Central Processing after the [[Galactic Empire]] took control of the [[city]],<ref name="Star Wars 3" /> but was [[Mission to Cloud City|rescued]] by former [[Baron Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]].<ref name="Star Wars 4" />
