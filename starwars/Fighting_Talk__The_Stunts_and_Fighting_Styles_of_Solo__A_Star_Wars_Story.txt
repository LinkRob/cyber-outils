The Stunts and Fighting Styles of ''Solo: A Star Wars Story'''''" is an article with words from 2nd unit director [[Brad Allen]] and stunt coordinator [[Guillermo Grispo]] about the stunt work in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.
