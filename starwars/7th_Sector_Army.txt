The '''7th Sector Army''' was one of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s twenty [[Sector Army|Sector Armies]] active during the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[army]] served in the northern quadrant of [[the galaxy]] along with the subsequent Sector Armies up to the [[12th Sector Army]]. At the start of the war, the Grand Army was organized into ten [[Systems Army|Systems Armies]], with each being split into two Sector Armies and drawn from a specific area of the [[galaxy]]. The Sector Armies were responsible for the defense of their specific areas. As a result, the 7th Sector Army belonged to the same Systems Army as another Sector Army.<ref name="BMF 18" />

Similar to the [[Galactic Republic]]'s other Sector Armies, the 7th Sector Army had one of twenty [[Haven-class medical station|''Haven''-class medical stations]] towed to them.<ref name="Note" /> The unit contained 147,456 [[clone trooper]]s apportioned into four [[corps]],<ref name="CL 2016" /> and comparable to other Sector Armies, it was attached to one of the twenty numbered [[fleet]]s of the [[Republic Navy]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The 7th Sector Army was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 18|eighteenth issue]] of the [[De Agostini]] magazine ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'', published in [[2015]].<ref name="BMF 18" /> The [[Seventh Army]] was introduced in the [[2012]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Paul R. Urquhart]], as the [[Seventh Army]].<ref name="ESG">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
