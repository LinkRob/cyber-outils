The '''chinar tree''' was a [[species]] of [[Star|sun]]-sensitive [[tree]] from [[Alderaan]] that had a distinctive scent of [[spice]] and [[fire]]. Although Alderaan was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Rules of the Game" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> the chinar trees outlived their [[homeworld]] thanks to seed banks. The [[Canto Bight]] [[Canto Bight Piazza|piazza]], located on the planet of [[Cantonica]], was lined with such trees.<ref name="Rules of the Game" /> Chinar trees could also be found in the rooftop [[garden]]s of [[Coruscant]] many [[Standard year|years]] prior to Alderaan's destruction.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />
