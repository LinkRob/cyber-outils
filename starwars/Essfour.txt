'''Essfour''',<ref name="Star Wars 26" /> also spelled as '''Essfor''', was an [[R5 series astromech droid]]<ref name="Star Wars 29" /> that served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He was assigned to [[Luke Skywalker]], following [[R2-D2]]'s mission to rescue a captured [[C-3PO]].<ref name="Star Wars 26" />
