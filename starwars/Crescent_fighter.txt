'''Crescent fighters''' were [[starfighter]]s used by [[Eiram's military]] during the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]]. After [[Plana Van]] discovered a [[Unidentified transport (Path of the Open Hand)|transport]] on the [[moon]] [[Eirie]], [[Commander]] [[Ailee]] ordered her to search the transport, and sent four crescent fighters as backup.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
