The '''Delacrix system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> Located at grid square T-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was connected to<ref name="TFABG" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Klatooine]] via [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="TFABG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Delacrix was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] boxed set for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="TFABG" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Delacrix/Legends|Delacrix]] was introduced in ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'', a [[2012]] reference book authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Paul R. Urquhart]].<ref name="TEGTW">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
