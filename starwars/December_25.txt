'''December 25''' is the 359th day (360th in leap years) of the year. There are 6 days remaining.</noinclude>

[[In-universe]], this day is represented by [[Life Day]], a [[Wookiee]] celebration of joy and harmony.

*[[1975]] &ndash; [[Daxiong]] is born.
*[[1977]] &ndash; ''[[Star Wars]]'' released as ''La Guerra de las Galaxias'' in [[Wikipedia:Mexico|Mexico]].
*[[1979]] &ndash; [[Star Wars (1977) 33|''Star Wars'' (1977) 33]] published.
*[[1987]] &ndash; [[Joe Kaye]] dies.
*[[2002]] &ndash; ''[[The Official Star Wars Fact File 52]]'' published.
*[[2015]] &ndash; [[Jason Wingreen]] dies.
*[[2020]] &ndash; ''[[Disney Gallery: The Mandalorian: Making of Season Two]]'' premieres on [[Disney+]].
*2020 &ndash; [[Han Solo (booklet)|''Han Solo'']] published in France.
*[[2023]] &ndash; [[Richard Franklin]] dies.
*[[2024]] &ndash; [[Battle of Jakku &mdash; Last Stand 1|''Battle of Jakku &mdash; Last Stand'' 1]] published.
