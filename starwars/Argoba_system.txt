The '''Argoba system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Trilon sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square H-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Argoban]] raiders.<ref name="HRA 9" />
