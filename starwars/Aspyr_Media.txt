'''Aspyr Media''' has released ports of a number of ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]]s to devices released by [[Wikipedia:Apple,Inc.|Apple Inc]]. Aspyr is a company that trades in doing this with a number of games. Their library of ''Star Wars'' ports to Apple's Macintosh systems includes ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Video Game]]'', ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed: Ultimate Sith Edition]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Battlefront]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight II: Jedi Outcast]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Jedi Academy]]''.<ref name="SW Games">{{WebCite|url=https://www.aspyr.com/games/tags?tag=Star+Wars|text=Aspyr &ndash; Games, amplified. (Star Wars tag)|work=[https://aspyr.com aspyr.com]|archivedate=20190618001200}}</ref> They have released other ports of ''Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic'', for the [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] on [[May 30]], [[2013]]<ref name="KOTOR iPad app">{{AppStore|url=id611436052?mt=8|text=Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic}}</ref> and for [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] on [[April 29]], [[2015]].<ref name="KOTOR android app">{{GooglePlay|url=apps/details?id=com.aspyr.swkotor&hl=en_GB|text=Star Wars: KOTOR}}</ref>
