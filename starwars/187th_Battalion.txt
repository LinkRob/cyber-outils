The '''187th Battalion''' was a [[Grand Army of the Republic]] [[infantry]] [[battalion]] of [[clone trooper]]s that served under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]] during [[the galaxy]]-spanning [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Windu led the [[trooper]]s of the 187th during the [[Battle of Anaxes]], an engagement of the [[Outer Rim Sieges]] in [[19 BBY]], during which the [[Republic Military|Republic's forces]] sought to defend the vital [[Anaxes shipyards]] from the Confederacy or otherwise risk compromising their entire [[Unidentified reserve fleet|reserve fleet]].

The battalion defended the [[planet]] [[Anaxes]] alongside members of the [[212th Attack Battalion]], the [[501st Legion]], and [[Clone Force 99]]. The unit was equipped with standard [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] with [[Color|maroon]] markings. Ultimately, the Republic emerged victorious in the defense of Anaxes. The battalion shared its numerical designation with another unit led by Windu: the elite [[187th Legion]].

==Description==
[[File:Windu_unit_Anaxes.png|thumb|250px|left|Troopers of the 187th Battalion.]]
The 187th Battalion was a [[clone trooper]] [[infantry]] [[battalion]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. [[Trooper]]s affiliated with the unit were equipped with [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] with reddish-brown markings. A vertical stripe with the hue ran down the center of the [[helmet]], with the markings also painted on the sides of the air filtration systems. Their primarily white chest [[armor]] was additionally painted with the reddish-brown hue, which diverged into two vertical stripes down the center. Shoulder, upper arm, and lower arm armor also featured the unit's colors, as did the set's upper legs, upper thighs, and knee guards.<ref name="Hasbro" />

187th troopers were armed with [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s. As a battalion of the Grand Army of the Republic,<ref name="Unfinished Business" /> it was composed of four [[Company|companies]] of 144 troopers each for a total of 576 troops.<ref name="Note" /> The 187th Battalion<ref name="Hasbro" /> shared the same unit designation as the elite [[187th Legion]],<ref name="Disney Helmet">{{DisneyToyCite|set=The Walt Disney Company|text=Disney Store|pack=187th Legion Clone Trooper Voice-Changing Helmet|link=187th-legion-clone-trooper-voice-changing-helmet-for-adults-star-wars-418142450797.html}}</ref> another unit led by Windu.<ref name="Hasbro reveal">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@hasbropulse|name=Hasbro Pulse|video=bQDGvr0oGqs|text=Hasbro Pulse Premium 1027 Event &#124; 2023|date=2023-10-27}}</ref>

==History==
===Initial onslaught===
The 187th Battalion participated in the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Unfinished Business" /> and was under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]].<ref name="Episode Guide" /> The 187th Battalion took part<ref name="Unfinished Business" /> in the [[Outer Rim Sieges]]<ref name="A Distant Echo">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref> through the [[Battle of Anaxes|defense]] of the [[jungle]] [[planet]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|fort-anaxes|Fort Anaxes}}</ref> [[Anaxes]], located on the [[Core Worlds|Coreward]] edge of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]],<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> One of the Republic's strongholds in their efforts against the Separatist Alliance,<ref name="Battles" /> it was the location of the vital [[Anaxes shipyards]],<ref name="The Bad Batch" /> one of the largest shipyards in the war effort.<ref name="The First Order">{{EncyclopediaCite|The First Order}}</ref> The collapse of Anaxes could have compromised the entire [[Republic Navy]]'s [[Unidentified reserve fleet|reserve fleet]].<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref>

[[File:187th-Anaxes.png|thumb|right|300px|The 187th battle on Anaxes against overwhelming Separatist forces.]]
As the Separatist [[Trench's fleet|fleet]] and [[Trench's army|army]] under [[Admiral]] [[Trench]] launched a two-pronged campaign against [[Republic Military|Republic forces]], Mace Windu's 187th Battalion fought alongside their Jedi General on the ground. In one such engagement, Windu and 187th troopers confronted [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]] consisting of many [[battle droid]]s and [[Multi-Troop Transport]]s, with Separatist [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]] and [[transport]]s hovering above the invasion forces. The 187th was supported by [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s but suffered several casualties.<ref name="The Bad Batch" />

After multiple [[Standard week|weeks]] of the Separatist onslaught, the Republic's control of Anaxes became precarious. After an intelligence mission behind Separatist lines to the [[Cyber Center]] on Anaxes<ref name="The Bad Batch" /> and a subsequent [[Mission to Skako Minor (Clone Wars)|mission]] to<ref name="Unfinished Business" /> the planet<ref name="Doctor Aphra 15">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 15|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 15]]</ref> [[Skako Minor]] to retrieve trooper [[Echo]], who was unwillingly used by the Separatists to deduce a [[Rex's battle strategy algorithm|battle strategy algorithm]] developed by Echo and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], the Republic mounted a final attack to end the Battle of Anaxes.<ref name="Unfinished Business" />

===Last attempt===
[[File:Mace-Attacks-Anaxes-Shipyards.png|thumb|left|300px|Windu and his 187th Battalion engage Separatist forces in the shipyards.]]
Republic forces consisting of a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], a [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|''Consular''-class cruiser]], and waves of [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]]s, [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170 starfighters]], and [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] carrying troopers from the 187th and [[212th Attack Battalion]] moved to attack the [[shipyard]]s. After the [[bomber]]s laid waste to Separatist anti-air [[J-1 proton cannon]]s and punched a hole in the topside infrastructure of the shipyards, Jedi Generals Mace Windu and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] jumped from their gunships and were followed by 212th troopers with [[jetpack]]s, with scores of 187th and 212th troops rappelling from their transports. The troopers fired their weapons as they descended on the Separatist forces below, landing on top of and around containers on the facility's ground level. In the firefight, 187th and 212th troopers fought side by side in clearing Separatist droids before another wave of droid reinforcements was dispatched.<ref name="Unfinished Business" />

All droid forces were sent to neutralize the Republic's detachments, allowing Echo, who had infiltrated the Separatist [[flagship]] ''[[Invulnerable]]'' with a Republic team, to disable the droids altogether with a signal. As the Republic forces defeated a droid wave, several 187th Battalion troopers formed a circle around Windu and Kenobi along with personnel from the 212th and [[501st Legion]]. Further droid reinforcements arrived and surrounded the group before they were shut down by Echo. With his army disabled, Admiral Trench resorted to destroying Anaxes with a large [[bomb]] planted in the shipyard's [[Fusion generator|fusion reactor]].<ref name="Unfinished Business" />

[[File:Jedi_surrounded_Anaxes.png|thumb|right|300px|Republic forces are surrounded on Anaxes.]]
Windu ordered Kenobi to evacuate Republic forces while he attempted to disarm the bomb, and Kenobi commanded the surrounding troops from the 187th, 212th, and 501st to organize their [[platoon]]s and retreat. With Republic troops evacuating aboard their [[gunship]]s, Windu managed to neutralize the bomb after receiving the deactivation codes from Echo, and then from Admiral Trench, who was forced to relinquish the final code. With the bomb disarmed and hostile forces defeated, the Republic emerged victorious from the battle.<ref name="Unfinished Business" />

===The other one===
{{Quote|The decision not to use the purple color scheme in Clone Wars goes back to CW Season 1, though I can't speak to why. [[File:Ponds-SWE.png|thumb|right|210px|Clone Commander Ponds, who appeared in ''The Clone Wars'' Season One, had the same colored markings as the 187th Battalion clone troopers.]]
According to [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]], the decision to not use the purple color scheme of Mace Windu's [[187th Legion/Legends|187th Legion]] from [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] was made in the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the series,<ref name="Leland" /> first airing in [[2008]].<ref name="Ambush Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode001.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Ambush}}</ref> In "[[Liberty on Ryloth]],"<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref> the penultimate episode of the first season that aired on [[March 13]], [[2009]],<ref name="TCWEG">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> [[Clone Commander]] [[Ponds]] serves under Mace Windu and sports similar colored markings to<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> the later-identified 187th Battalion.<ref name="HasbroPulse Livestream" /> Sources including [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 76|seventy-sixth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'' magazine&ndash;<ref name="Helmet Collection 76">{{HelmetCollectionCite|76|Helmets}}</ref> written by [[Iain Lowson]]<ref name="HC-Credits">{{EmailCite|email=File:HCSV-Email-2025-07-26.png|author=[[Iain Lowson]]|date=2025-07-26}}</ref> and originally released on [[January 31]], [[2017]]<ref name="HC France">{{HelmetDatesFR|76}}</ref>&ndash;and the [[July 18]], 2017,<ref name="Amazon">{{Amazon|url=Star-Wars-Front-Daniel-Wallace/dp/1785652141|text=Star Wars &ndash; On the Front Lines}}</ref> reference book, ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'', authored by [[Daniel Wallace]],<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> clarify that Ponds served as the leader of [[Lightning Squadron]]<ref name="Helmet Collection 76" /> of the [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" />

Chee further elaborated that it was a conscious decision to try to keep the 187th Legion and Windu's unit in ''The Clone Wars'' series as different units rather than the same unit that uses two different color schemes.<ref name="Leland" /> The 187th Legion were identified in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] "Republic Fighter Tank" set that was revealed in 2022.<ref name="LEGO">{{LEGOWeb|url=en-us/product/republic-fighter-tank-75342|text=Republic Fighter Tank}}</ref>
