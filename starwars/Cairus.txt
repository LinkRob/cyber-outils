'''Cairus''' was a jungle astronomical object located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Pathfinders 1" /> Around [[362 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''The High Republic Adventures – Pathfinders'' 1 take place around twenty [[Standard year|years]] after the events of [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|''The High Republic'' Phase II]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> a team of [[Republic Pathfinder]]s led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Temesh]] were stopped on Cairus when they were contacted and directed to the [[Jagoth system]] in order to investigate the murder of a [[Jedi]].<ref name="Pathfinders 1" />
