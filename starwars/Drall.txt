The '''Drall''' were a [[species]] with [[eye]]s that were especially vulnerable to light.<ref name="G&G" /> They inhabited the [[Corellian system|Corellia system]] alongside [[human]]s and [[Selonian]]s.<ref name="The Rise and Fall of the Galatic Empire">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> The non-lethal weapon known as the [[flashstick]] was particularly effective against Drall and was used almost exclusively by the Drall [[Law enforcement agency|constabulary]], with most users wearing polarized [[goggles]] to prevent the weapon blinding them as well as the target.<ref name="G&G" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] the Drall were first mentioned in the [[2019]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] roleplaying supplement ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''.<ref name="G&G" /> The [[Drall/Legends|species]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[1980]] novel ''[[Han Solo and the Lost Legacy]]'', which was written by [[Brian Daley]].<ref name="Lost Legacy">''[[Han Solo and the Lost Legacy]]''</ref>
