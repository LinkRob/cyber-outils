An '''Ewok Civil War'''<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-jaxxon|text=Holy Hutch! ''Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories Annual&mdash;Jaxxon 2023'' Revealed}}</ref> took place on the [[moon]] [[Endor]] at some point<ref name="Jaxxon" /> no earlier than [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> between the supporters of [[Graak (lawbreaker)|Graak]] and the supporters of [[princess]] [[Kneesaa a Jari Kintaka]], the daughter of [[chief]] [[Chirpa]] who had mysteriously disappeared during a [[Dulok]] raid, over who had a right lead the [[Bright Tree Village]]. In fact Chirpa had been kidnapped by the [[Children of Vulgarr]], a Dulok faction aided by Graak and his brother [[Gruekk]], and Graak was only alleging Chirpa had abandoned his people in order to take control of the village. After Kneesaa and chief [[shaman]] [[Logray]] requested help, a [[New Republic]] [[Diplomatic mission to Endor|diplomatic mission]] consisting of [[captain]] [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] and the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] were sent by [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to resolve the conflict. Jaxxon was eventually able to discover the truth, free Chripa, and end the usurpers' plot.<ref name="Jaxxon" />
