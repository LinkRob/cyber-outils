'''Cinsar''' was a [[Chiss]] [[Gender|male]] who held the rank of [[Senior Commander]] and served as the Fifth Officer aboard the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] [[starship]] [[Steadfast (Chiss heavy cruiser)|''Steadfast'']]. He was assigned to guide [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]], a former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Lieutenant Commander]] sent as an [[emissary]] to the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] by [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]. Despite not entirely enjoying his task, Cinsar respected Vanto, but nonetheless found his initial use of the [[Cheunh]] [[language]] amusing.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
