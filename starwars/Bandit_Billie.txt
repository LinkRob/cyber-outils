'''Bandit Billie''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[thief]] who at one point owned the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''. Stealing the ''Guide'' from [[Tobias Beckett]] in [[Zarra]] on [[Cato Neimoidia]] after switching packs with him, Billie recounted several of her heists in the logbook, including one where she hired several [[Taurill]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
