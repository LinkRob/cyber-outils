Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] fought a '''battle with the droid uprising'''<ref name="Vader 37" /> that consisted of a [[Droid uprising|large force]] of [[droid]]s assembled by the [[Droid Crush Pirates of Bestoon]] that attempted to ambush the [[Sith Lord]] on<ref name="Vader 36" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Ringo Vinda">{{Databank|ringo-vinda|Ringo Vinda}}</ref> [[Ringo Vinda]]<ref name="Vader 35" /> and [[Death|kill]] him in revenge for the [[time]]s that he had killed the assembled droids during his career. Unbeknownst, to the droids, one of them, the [[forensics droid]] [[ZED-6-7]], was secretly still working in the service of Vader and led the droids into a confrontation with Vader where they were completely wiped out, except for those who, such as [[Arex]] who was turned against the force of droids, and the [[assassin droid]]s [[0-0-0]] and [[BT-1]] who decided to pick what they saw as the winning side and began to attack their fellow droids before they later escaped. After the battle, Vader had the droids rebuilt and they began to follow him.<ref name="Vader 36" />
