'''Belk''' was a [[Gender|male]] criminal who was a member of a [[Telsla's crew|crew]] led by the [[Abednedo]] [[Telsla]]. He frequently squabbled with fellow crewmember [[Fleecs]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], Telsla's crew overthrew [[Tomarian's Annual Festival of Light Gala]] held at the [[Tomarian's theater|his theater]] of the [[Naboo]] resident [[Tomarian]], who owed them a debt. After entering Tomarian's theater, the crew used [[gas]] concocted by Belk in order to knock out the celebration's guests, including Tomarian's [[Jedi Knight]] protector, [[Anakin Skywalker]]. Belk later searched within the theater to determine if anyone else remained in the building&mdash;though Fleecs had already looked inside, he was not satisfied with the extent of her surveillance. [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of Naboo, who had managed to escape the gas attack by being inside the theater, ambushed Belk as he searched the room, incapacitating the criminal and taking his [[blaster]]. After detonating an explosive on the theater's stage, Telsla noticed that Belk had not returned; Fleecs suggested that he had been backstage and had gotten caught in the explosion, and Telsla was unconcerned by the thought.<ref name="Adventures 5" />
