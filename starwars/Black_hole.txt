A '''black hole''' was a [[Realspace|celestial]] phenomenon which was the result of a large [[star]] experiencing a [[supernova]] following the end of its fusion [[reactor|reactions]] and their collapse into themselves.<ref name="BMF 34" /> A [[Color|black]] expanse,<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> a black hole contained a singularity and possessed immense [[gravity]], devouring all available matter<ref name="Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> including light itself<ref name="BMF 34" /> or accumulating it in a region known as its accretion disk.<ref name="Catalyst" />

A super-massive black hole that stars rotated around was located in the [[Deep Core|center]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> It was called the [[Galactic Centre]].<ref name="BMF 36">{{BuildFalconCite|36|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>

By [[40 BBY]], it was possible to take pictures of a black hole from a point just shy of its event horizon. Obi-Wan's friend [[Jape]] sent Obi-Wan such images.<ref name="M&A">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref>

The [[Akkadese Maelstrom]] sub-region around the [[Kessel sector]] contained a [[Maw Cluster|number of black holes]], the most dangerous being The Maw. A cell of the criminal organization [[Crimson Dawn]] known as [[Beckett's gang]], along with his partner [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and his female droid [[L3-37]], fell in that black hole and were about to be sucked in, until they used the [[coaxium]] as a power source for the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to escape the hole's gravity.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

Shortly after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]], accompanied by [[Mas Amedda]] and several others, traveled to a binary black hole formation known as the [[Hero Twins]], where they tested a prototype version of the [[superlaser]] that would eventually be installed on the Empire's [[planet]]-[[Superweapon|destroying]] [[battle station]], the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], by firing it at the deceased stars.<ref name="Catalyst" />

The [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Arbitrator]]'', while attempting to escape a [[New Republic Defense Fleet|New Republic fleet]], accidentally input erroneous [[hyperspace]] [[Galactic coordinates|coordinates]] into its [[Navigation computer|navicomputer]], which caused it to exit hyperspace near a black hole, pulling the ship in and destroying it.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
