A '''Circuit Breaker Module''' was a component used in [[computer]] technology.<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>

{{Tech-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Circuit Breaker Modules first appeared in the current [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'', developed by [[EA Capital Games]]. In the game, Circuit Breaker Modules, designated "Mk 2", they are used to upgrade "mods",<ref name="GoH" /> which were added on [[July 12]], [[2016]],<ref name="Mods">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/53649/game-update-7-12-2016|text=Game Update - 7/12/2016|archivedate=20240718113752|nolive=1}}</ref> and are used to improve characters.<ref name="GoH" /> In the [[Legends]] continuity, the [[circuit-breaker module]] originated in the [[1978]] ''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]''.<ref name="The Star Wars Holiday Special">''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]''</ref>
