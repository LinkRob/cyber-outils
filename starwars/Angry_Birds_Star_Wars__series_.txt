The ''Angry Birds Star Wars'' series is a series of video games and books set in the ''Star Wars'' galaxy. The games consist of ''[[Angry Birds Star Wars]]'' and ''[[Angry Birds Star Wars II]]'', which were developed by [[Rovio Entertainment]]. The books, such as [[Ultimate Factivity Collection: Angry Birds Star Wars]] were also licensed by the game development company.
