{{Quote|I guess you'll have figured out already that ''Star Wars'' turned out to ''be'' my destiny! And they say there's no such thing as luck. As editor of ''Insider'', I find myself even more deeply engaged with events in the galaxy far, far away than I was when I was simply a fan. I've borne witness to the love, passion, and care that so many creative people put into every new Disney+ series, novel, videogame, or collectible that's out there, and that's reflected in the features we have for you this issue.|Christopher Cooper, in "[[A Message from the Editor]]" of [[Star Wars Insider 231|''Star Wars'' Insider 231]]|<ref name="Insider 231">{{InsiderCite|231|A Message from the Editor}}</ref>}}
'''Christopher Cooper''', also credited as '''Chris Cooper''', was a writer for ''[[Star Wars Comic UK]]'' and is the current editor of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine under publisher [[Titan Magazines]]. His tenure began with [[Star Wars Insider 179|issue 179]], having succeeded former editor [[Will Salmon]]. Cooper's favorite ''[[Star Wars]]'' character is [[Wedge Antilles]].<ref name="Insider 229">{{InsiderCite|229|A Message from the Editor}}</ref>
