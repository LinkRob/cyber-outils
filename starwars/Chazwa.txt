'''Chazwa''' was an astronomical object located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]], along the [[Perlemian Trade Route]].<ref name="TFABG" /> It was located in the [[Chazwa system]] in the [[Orus sector]].<ref name="Appendix" /> After the [[Battle of Endor]], remnants of the [[Galactic Empire]] had a presence on Chazwa, and the [[New Republic]]'s [[Meteor Squadron]] were sent on a scouting mission to Chazwa.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
