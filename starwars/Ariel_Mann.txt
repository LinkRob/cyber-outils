'''Ariel Mann''' is an experienced composer, producer, and professional guitarist<ref name="Website" /> employed at [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] Consumer Products and Interactive, specializing in mobile, virtual reality (VR), and augmented reality (AR) titles. Mann joined Disney in [[January]] of [[2015]] as an audio producer, being promoted to senior composer in [[September]] [[2016]].<ref name="LinkedIn">{{LinkedIn|url=in/mannmusica/|text=Ariel Mann}}</ref> Since then, he has worked on over 30 titles, including trailers and soundtracks for ''[[Star Wars]]''&ndash;related games such as ''[[Star Wars: Force Arena]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Puzzle Droids]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Challenges]]'', ''[[The Mandalorian AR Experience]]'', and ''[[Minecraft Star Wars]]'', as well as games featuring some degree of ''Star Wars'' content like [[Disney Infinity 3.0|''Disney Infinity'']], ''[[Disney Magic Kingdoms]]'', and ''[[Disney Emoji Blitz]]''.<ref name="Website" />
