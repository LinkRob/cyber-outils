'''Dol'har Hyde''' was a [[forest]] [[planet]] situated in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Aphra 8" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], a [[Krix Kamerat's cell|cell]] of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization that was led by [[Krix Kamerat]] was based on Dol'har Hyde.<ref name="THRA 8">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 8|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 8]]</ref> The site of a [[Battle at Dol'har Hyde|battle]]<ref name="Aphra 7">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 7|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 7]]</ref> [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Imperial Era]] that involved the Nihil, the planet became strewn with the wreckage of [[starship]]s. Despite the damage that had been done to the planet, its forests grew back around the wreckage.<ref name="Aphra 8" />

The [[smuggler]]<ref name="Aphra 7" /> [[Remy (smuggler)|Remy]] acquired a Nihil [[Path engine]] from the remains of a marauder vessel, selling it to [[Beol De'Rruyet]] on [[Corellia]]. While investigating the Path engine and its history, the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] traveled to Dol'har Hyde with the smuggler [[Sana Starros]], where they climbed the large [[tree]] that had swallowed the wreckage.<ref name="Aphra 8" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dol'har Hyde was first depicted in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 7|seventh issue]] of the [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]], written by [[Alyssa Wong]], penciled by [[Minkyu Jung]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 7" /> on [[January 20]], [[2021]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/82210/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_7|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #7}}</ref> After seeing the planet and its history of Nihil activity, [[Daniel José Older]] later texted Wong to ask if he could use the setting,<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1439422208569393155|[[Daniel José Older]]|quote=Aphra was the first mention! When I saw it I texted @crashwong like sooo I noticed you have a planet with some waybackwhen Nihil activity might i borrow it??|archivedate=20210921012147}}</ref> which he included in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 8|eighth issue]] of [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']], illustrated by [[Toni Bruno]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="THRA 8" /> on [[September 1]], 2021.<ref name="Comixology">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-8/digital-comic/951946|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures #8}}</ref>
