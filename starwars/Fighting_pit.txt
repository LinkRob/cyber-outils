[[Sergeant]] [[Kreel]]'s [[Kreel's father|father]] and [[Kreel's mother|mother]] both [[Death|died]] in a fighting pit on [[Chagar IX]].<ref name="SW21" /> The [[Xonti Brothers]] were a group<ref name="Docter Aphra 10">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 10|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 10]]</ref> who taught [[Krrsantan]] to be a [[gladiator]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 5">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 5|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 5]]</ref> The [[Kanji Criminal Enterprise|Kanji]] and the Hutts sponsored several fighters at [[Nar Shaddaa]]'s [[Garkata Fighting Arena]].
