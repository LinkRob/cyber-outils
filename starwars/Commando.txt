[[File:Clone-Commandos-Kamino-BF2.png|thumb|300px|Clone Commandos were the elite special operations soldiers of the Galactic Republic.]]
A '''commando''' was an elite [[soldier]] who specialized in environments or situations that were beyond the capacity of normal [[infantry]] men.<ref name="Episode 2" /> Examples included the [[Galactic Senate]]'s [[Senate Commando]]s,<ref name="Cloak of Darkness">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness}}</ref> the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[clone commando]]s,<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars: Battlefront II]]''</ref> and [[Maul]]'s [[Mandalorian super commando]]s.<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref> The [[Separatist Droid Army]] fielded [[BX-series droid commando]]s to serve as commando-level [[battle droid]]s,<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II" /> with the [[Techno Union]]'s [[Commando (Techno Union)|Skakoan commandos]] also serving the Separatist cause.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> During the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]], [[death trooper]]s served as special-assignment commandos.<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Uingali foar Marocsaa]] and his [[Paccian Governance]] had a unit of [[Paccian commando]]s that he used to board the [[Hammer (Kilji picket cruiser)|''Hammer'']] and [[Death|kill]] [[Colonel]] [[Tildnis]].<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> [[Commando (Crimson Dawn)|Commandos]] who served the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate wore red robes and were active during [[Lord|Lady]] [[Qi'ra]]'s time as syndicate leader.<ref name="OuwlawsArt">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]''</ref> The criminal organization [[Haxion Brood]] [[Haxion Brood commando|also employed commandos]] as muscle.<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>
