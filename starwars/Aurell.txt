'''Aurell''' was, along with [[Ryern]], one of two [[star]]s located in the [[Auratera system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Vorzyd sector]]. The star, which lay along the [[Salin Corridor]] [[hyperspace route]], was [[orbit]]ed by three [[planet]]s, one of which was known as [[Auratera]].<ref name="NoP" /> It was situated in grid square R-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> As Aurell was the larger of the [[Star system|system's]] stars, the three planets orbited it exclusively, although Ryern still illuminated Auratera's sky. As a result, nights occurred on the planet only when Aurell completely [[eclipse]]d Ryern.<ref name="NoP" />

Ryern and Aurell's propinquity varied over [[time]], which affected Auratera's [[temperature]]. At times, the two stars appeared to be conjoined, which caused particularly bright and hot [[Standard day|days]], while at others the stars were not present in the sky at the same time. Regardless of their proximity, Aurell was always brighter than Ryern, with the latter star never being more than a third of the former's brightness.<ref name="NoP" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aurell was introduced via mention in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Nexus of Power]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for use with the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="NoP" />
