'''Chinook Station''' was a fuel depot affiliated with the [[Galactic Empire]], outfitting their forces with valuable [[tibanna]] gas. It was located on the [[planet]] [[Bespin]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

Some [[time]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], a number of [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] were stationed here to guard the fuel depots. A [[Mission to Chinook Station|mission]] by members of [[Inferno Squad (Resistance)|Inferno Squad]] of the [[New Republic]] saw the destruction of these depots, as well as three [[Star Destroyer]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
