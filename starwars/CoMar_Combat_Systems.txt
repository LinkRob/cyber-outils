'''CoMar Combat Systems''' was a company which produced the [[SLD-26 planetary shield generator]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], the [[Galactic Empire]] installed an SLD-26 on the [[moon]] [[Endor]] to provide a shield for the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]] [[space station]] being constructed in orbit.<ref name="USD" />

==Behind the scenes==
CoMar Combat Systems was first mentioned in the ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'' by [[Greg Gorden]] which was a supplementary book released in [[1989]] for the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' by [[West End Games]]. The company was confirmed to be [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which was released in [[2015]].
