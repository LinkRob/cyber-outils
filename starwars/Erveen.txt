'''Erveen''' served as a [[Senior Senator]] in the [[Imperial Senate]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] where she represented [[Hodi]]. In [[2 BBY]], she assisted Senior Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] in invoking [[Article 17-252]] in order to allow Senior Senator [[Mon Mothma]] to give a [[Escape from the Senate|speech]] condemning the [[Ghorman Massacre]].

==Biography==
Erveen was a [[Politics|politician]] who by [[2 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{Andor|Messenger}} dates the events of {{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}} to [[2 BBY]].</ref>  had become a [[Senior Senator]] representing [[Hodi]] in the in the [[Imperial Senate]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Following the [[Ghorman Massacre]]<ref name="Welcome" /> in 2 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the senate held a session to discuss the events. Erveen was one of several senators who spoke within the [[Galactic Senate Chamber|Grand Convocation Chamber]] as part of the [[Standard day|day's]] agenda. After the Senate's [[Unidentified Oathkeeper|Oathkeeper]], serving as [[Chair of the Senate]], recognized [[Senator]] Erveen, Erveen yielded a moment of her time to [[Bail Prestor Organa]], Senior Senator of [[Alderaan]]. Organa subsequently invoked [[Article 17-252]], and further yielded his time to Senior Senator [[Mon Mothma]] of [[Chandrila]], who used the time to denounce [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] as responsible for the Ghorman Massacre. Mothma subsequently made an [[escape from the Senate]]<ref name="Welcome" /> and stated the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance]] above [[Dantooine]]'s orbit, officially creating the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Erveen was a human with light skin and [[color|gray]] [[hair]]<ref name="Welcome" />. She was a confident public speaker and a trusted ally of [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]].<ref name="Databank" />

==Equipment==
During Mon Mothma's speech, Erveen wore an elegant maroon [[clothing|dress]] with bands of gold colored crecents running down the front and a gold colored [[belt]].<ref name="Welcome " />

==Behind the scenes==
