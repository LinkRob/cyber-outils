'''Fitz''' was the [[captain]] aboard the [[freighter]] ''[[Dromedar]]''. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Nevil Cygni]] suggested it was Fitz or [[Toom]] who locked the [[hyperdrive]] aboard the [[Starship|vessel]] to prevent [[pirate]]s accessing it and stealing the ship. She and her crew were taken prisoners by the pirates, who were planning to sell them in [[Cartherston]] on [[Keitum]], but were later rescued by [[Lieutenant]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], [[Ensign]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] and their companions.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Captain Fitz first appeared in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[2017]] novel [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']].<ref name="Thrawn" /> She later appeared in the comic adaptation of the novel, where she is dressed in the [[uniform]] of an Imperial captain<ref name="Thrawn II">[[Thrawn 2|''Thrawn'' 2]]</ref> but in the novel she has no Imperial rank, as both Vanto and Thrawn give Fitz orders without anyone seeing it as a breach in chain of command.<ref name="Thrawn" />
