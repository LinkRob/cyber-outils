The '''''Deathless''''' was a [[Nikardun Battle Dreadnought]] that served as the [[flagship]] of [[General]] [[Yiv]], and the mobile headquarters of the [[Nikardun Destiny]]. Yiv oversaw the [[Nikardun conquests|expansion]] of the Destiny from the ship's throne room.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

Like other [[Nikardun]] [[warship]]s, the ''Deathless'' was distinguished with a very large [[viewport]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
