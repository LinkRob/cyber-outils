'''Brandon Badeaux''' is a comic book artist and author. He penciled and inked three issues of ''[[Star Wars: Republic]]'' and the four-part story ''[[Nomad]]'' for ''[[Star Wars Tales]]'', before launching the new series [[Star Wars: Rebellion (comic series)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']] with author [[Rob Williams]]. Badeaux drew most of the first story arc, [[Star Wars: Rebellion: My Brother, My Enemy|''My Brother, My Enemy'']], and he and Williams together wrote the story for the next arc, [[Star Wars: Rebellion: The Ahakista Gambit|''The Ahakista Gambit'']].
