'''Burner''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Coruscant Guard]] after [[Operation: Knightfall]]. He was one of at least nine [[Cloning|clones]] ordered to redecorate the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] after [[Operation: Knightfall]]. While redecorating, he tripped onto some scaffolding after tripping over a [[holoprojector]], making [[Scatter (Coruscant Guard)|Scatter]] believe he turned into a [[Ghost (folklore)|ghost]].<ref name="LEGO" />
