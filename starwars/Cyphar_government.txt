By the time I fount out they were ''really'' smuggling I was already on the ground and couldn't bow out without risking a blaster shot to the head.|Nevil Cygni, to Thrawn|<ref name="Thrawn" />}}
The '''Cyphar government''' was a government on the [[planet]] [[Cyphar]]. During the [[Imperial Era|rule]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], a group of [[human]]s contacted [[Nevil Cygni]] for aide after claiming that they were being [[Cyphar Incident|pressured]] between the [[Afe clan]] and the Cyphar government. When Cygni discovered that the humans were actually [[spice]] [[smuggler]]s, Cygni continued to work for them under false pretenses in hopes that [[Commander]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] of the [[Imperial Navy]] would notice and end the incident.<ref name="Thrawn" />
