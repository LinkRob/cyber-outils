'''Clone X troopers''', also known as '''CX troopers''', '''Clone X series''', '''clone assassins''', or '''Shadows''' by [[Rex]], were specialized [[clone commando]]s conditioned by the [[Advanced Science Division]] of the [[Imperial Science Bureau]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Conditioned to be fanatically loyal to the Empire, during the process, their identities were erased and were monitored with enhanced tracking to ensure they completed their missions without fail. Acting as specialized [[assassin]]s active under a [[Royce Hemlock's deep undercover program|deep undercover program]] of the Advanced Science Division, they wore unique [[armor]] and were equipped with a variety of [[weapon]]s. In the event of possibly being captured, they were provided with a [[electro capsule]].

[[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]] was given [[Clone X]] to silence [[clone trooper]]s who knew of his involvement in the [[Attack on Kamino]] and planned to expose him to the [[Imperial Senate]]. Months later, the Empire sent [[CX-1]] to eliminate [[Senator]] [[Avi Singh]], and, when CX-1 was captured by the [[Clone Underground]] movement, sent [[CX-2]] to silence CX-1 on [[Teth]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Their devotion to the Empire was unwavering, and they targeted anyone who their masters saw as a threat—including senators and even fellow clones. They called themselves "believers" and were willing to take their own lives to protect the truth about their missions. I called them “shadows” since they were such stealthy devils.|Rex|<ref name="SecretsCloneTroopers">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Clone Troopers]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Sniper_x_taking_aim.png|thumb|left|300px|The clone assassin Clone X acts as a sniper on a secret mission for Vice Admiral Rampart.]]
Clone X troopers, designated as CX troopers<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|clone-x-trooper|Clone X trooper}}</ref> and also known as clone assassins, were specialized [[clone trooper]]s<ref name="Truth Slide 2">{{SW|url=truth-and-consequences-story-gallery|text="Truth and Consequences" Story Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> that were activated<ref name="Echo">{{Databank|echo|Echo}}</ref> to serve as [[assassin]]s<ref name="Truth Slide 2">{{SW|url=truth-and-consequences-story-gallery|text="Truth and Consequences" Story Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> and [[clone commando]]s.<ref name="Extraction subtitles">{{TBB|Extraction}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> The clones were products of [[Chief Scientist]] [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock's deep undercover program]], where clones were conditioned into new warriors without a trace of their old identities. The conditioned soldiers served as special operatives organized under<ref name="Infiltration" /> the classified<ref name="Extraction">{{TBB|Extraction}}</ref> [[Advanced Science Division]].<ref name="Infiltration" /> In order to avoid detection, only one clone assassin is activated at a time.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|clone-x-trooper|Clone X trooper}}</ref> If a clone assassin fails his mission or is captured, another will be activated and tasked with [[Death|killing]] his predecessor.<ref name="Infiltration" />

[[File:CXDaggerConcept-TBB-Infiltration.png|thumb|right|300px|Clone X troopers flew in special starships, the CX Dagger vessel.]]
During operations, Clone X troopers were equipped with [[Color|black]] military [[armor]] that contained [[Comlink|communications]] devices on the [[helmet]]s, and could have a [[rangefinder]] attached. Their helmet was pressurized and possessed a mechanized visor that would retract and protract as needed.<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" /> Additionally, a control panel brace could be added for direct control to their [[CX Dagger vessel]]s. Among the Clone X troopers were a variety of roles, with [[Clone X]], [[CX-1]], and [[CX-2]] being trained as [[Sniper|sharpshooters]] and as such were provided with a [[773 Firepuncher sniper rifle|773 Firepuncher rifle]], an [[RK-3 blaster]], and a [[backpack]] equipped with gear like [[thermal detonator]]s, attached to their armor.<ref name="Infiltration" /> Four Clone X troopers deployed by Hemlock during a [[Skirmish on Wayland|raid on Mount Tantiss]] were equipped with different helmets and were sometimes resistant to stun blasts and blaster fire, with it taking several well-paced shots to kill each of the assassins. Furthermore, these troopers were trained to wield weapons such as [[electrospear]]s, superheated [[vibroblade]]s, [[electrified bola]]s, and double-barreled [[blaster pistol]]s.<ref name="Cavalry">{{TBB|The Cavalry Has Arrived}}</ref> In order to maintain their clandestine nature in the event of being compromised, Clone X troopers had all records of their previous identities (including [[Identifying code|identifying numbers]]) erased from all databanks<ref name="TaC" /> and were implanted with [[electro capsule]]s in one of their teeth to be used in the event of capture and imprisonment<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" /> as well as an internal [[Homing beacon|homing device]] that was undetectable to scanners and remained active while the soldier remained alive.<ref name="Infiltration" />

==History==
===Coruscant Conspiracy===
{{Quote|You're fighting the wrong battle, brother. You're limited.''"<br />"''So what does that make you?''"<br />"''A believer.|Clone X and Rex|<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" />}}
Over a [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the Empire, these specialized [[clone trooper]]s<ref name="Truth Slide 2">{{SW|url=truth-and-consequences-story-gallery|text="Truth and Consequences" Story Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> were active when one such [[commando]],<ref name="Clone X">{{Databank|clone-x|Clone X}}</ref> [[Clone X]], who was deployed to serve [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]], was ordered to assassinate an Imperial clone trooper known as [[Cade]], who was planning to expose Rampart for his involvement in the [[Attack on Kamino|destruction]] of [[Tipoca City]] to the [[Imperial Senate]]. Set up upon a rooftop near [[79's]], a [[cantina]] on [[Coruscant]] that catered to clone troopers, Clone X shot and killed Cade as he exited the cantina alongside clone stormtrooper [[Slip (clone trooper)|Slip]]. Clone X attempted to terminate Slip as well, but the panicked clone managed to commandeer a [[speeder bike]] and escape.<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" />

[[File:CloneXRampart-TCC.png|thumb|left|300px|A clone assassin was assigned to Rampart.]]
Soon after, Clone X managed to track down Slip and successfully assassinate him in the streets of Coruscant after spotting the trooper speaking with [[Pantoran]] [[Senator]] [[Riyo Chuchi]]. In an attempt to tie up any loose ends, Clone X proceeded to attack the senator and her [[bodyguard]]s, and pursued them through a nearby warehouse. After killing all of Chuchi's men, Clone X prepared to kill the Pantoran senator but was stunned by the rogue [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], who showed up to extract Slip before his demise. Clone X later awoke to find that Rex and Chuchi brought him to [[Martez sisters' repair shop|Trace Martez's repair shop]], on [[Level 1313]] of the [[Coruscant Underworld]]. After refusing to answer Rex's questions, Clone X revealed his knowledge of Rex's name and told him that he was fighting the wrong battle, calling him "limited". The assassin then proclaimed himself as a "believer" before biting down on an electro capsule, killing himself instantly.<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" />

The next day, Clone X's corpse, now on a [[hoversled]], was shown to the rogue commandos of [[Clone Force 99]], with Rex mentioning that the assassin's identifying number had been wiped.<ref name="TaC">{{TBB|Truth and Consequences}}</ref> Three months later,<ref name="Date">[[Omega]] tracks the days (164) that have passed since her capture in "[[Plan 99]]," which takes place sometime after "[[Truth and Consequences]]". Therefore, the events of "[[Shadows of Tantiss]]" must take place approximately six months after the death of [[Clone X]].</ref> clone assassin [[CX-1]] was present at [[Tantiss Base]], briefly watching a group of clones being escorted by commandos before entering a turbolift.<ref name="SoT">{{TBB|Shadows of Tantiss}}</ref> The [[genetically defective clone]] commando [[Crosshair]] was admitted into the undercover program, but their conditioning attempts were unsuccessful, likely due to his defective nature.<ref name="Infiltration" />

===Exposing the Clone Underground===
{{Quote|They are coming. For all of you.|CX-1|<ref name="Infiltration" />}}
[[File:InfiltrationTBB-Thumbnail.png|thumb|right|300px|Scorch activated another assassin to track the previous one and neutralize him.]]
A couple days later, CX-1 was sent to assassinate [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] Senator [[Avi Singh]], who went into hiding with his droid [[GS-8]] after the [[Occupation of Raxus Secundus|occupation]] of his [[Raxus Secundus|homeplanet]] by the Empire after the end of the [[Clone Wars]] after getting saved by Clone Force 99. He was meeting with Senator Chuchi on her homeworld [[Pantora]], to discuss forming a galaxy wide [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebellion]] against [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], and were guarded by Captain Rex and several members of his [[Clone Underground]] rebel cell. CX-1 infiltrated the [[Pantora tea parlor|tea parlor]] where the meeting took place and prepared to assassinate Singh, but was compromised at the last moment by Rex. CX-1 tossed a [[thermal detonator]] into the room to cover his escape, but was pursed to the top of a maintenance tower, where he was stunned by Captain [[Howzer]]. Not wanting a repeat when he questioned Clone X, Rex had Howzer remove CX-1's electro capsule and he recovered a highly encrypted [[Holopuck|data puck]] from his gear which was a target register. CX-1's capture and inability to kill himself resulted in [[Clone Commander]] [[Scorch]] activating another assassin, [[CX-2]], to kill him. CX-2 traveled on his [[CX Dagger vessel]] to the Clone Underground's hideout on the planet [[Teth]] and infiltrated it. After killing several guards, CX-2 eliminated CX-1 and continued to engage the Clone Underground, killing multiple members of Rex’s rebel cell and causing the base to collapse from detonators he planted earlier. Concurrently, Commander Scorch dispatched a [[Recovery Strike Team]] to reinforce CX-2, led by Commander [[Wolffe]], to recover [[Omega]], who was Doctor Hemlock’s prime target and discovered with Clone Force 99 and the Clone Underground.<ref name="Infiltration" />

Upon the arrival of reinforcements, CX-2 disregarded Wolffe's order to use stun blasts and continued to operate separately from the recovery strike team, and kept his blaster set to kill. Despite the animosity between the two clones, CX-2 claimed that his efforts had prevented the target from escaping. CX-2 later engaged in hand-to-hand combat with rogue sharpshooter Crosshair, angered at his inability to become a clone assassin due to his defective nature. Crosshair ultimately defeated CX-2, though he survived the fight.<ref name="Extraction" />

===Hemlock's final weapon===
[[File:New-CX-Troopers.png|thumb|left|300px|Hemlock activates the other Clone X troopers]]
Eventually, after a successful raid on Pabu conducted by CX-2, Omega was recaptured and brought back to Tantiss Base on [[Wayland]] and placed in Imperial confinement. Desperate to recover their older sister, Clone Force 99 raided Tantiss Base. When a [[Zillo Beast]] containment breach sent Imperial forces stationed at the base in disarray, Hemlock decided to activate the remainder of his clone assassins whom he was getting ready in the training room, [[Sniper CloneX3]], [[Sniper CloneX4]], [[Sniper CloneX5]], and [[Unidentified Clone X trooper|another trooper]]. Arriving in the the training room, Hemlock opened the doors of the assassins' stasis chambers, releasing the troopers, who were subsequently dispatched to Tantiss base' hangar to deal with Clone Force 99. After neutralizing the rogue clones in a brief brutal firefight, the assassins brought the Clone Force 99 back into the training room, where Hemlock began torturing them in an attempt to recondition them into more operatives. Concurrently, Omega and Corporal Echo freed the clone prisoners and led them to the training room to rescue Clone Force 99. As CX-2 and one assassin remained in the reconditioning room to guard Clone Force 99, the other assassins entered the training room just below to neutralize the freed prisoners. Soon after, Omega snuck into the reconditioning room and was caught, but not before managing to free one of Wrecker's restraints. Meanwhile, the other assassins continued to battle Echo and the freed clone prisoners, inflicting heavy casualties.<ref name="Cavalry" />

As Wrecker awoke, he freed himself and knocked out Scorch, CX-2, a clone assassin, and Hemlock. The clone assassin attempted to neutralize him, but Wrecker managed to overpower him and knocked him into a recovering CX-2. As Scorch and Hemlock escaped with Omega, Wrecker pushed the assassin through the window down into the training room. Hunter grabbed the electrospear and tried to find CX-2, but his sight was blurred due to previous torture. CX-2 attempted to sneak up behind Hunter and tried to shoot him. As he approached Crosshair's restraining chamber, he pulled the trigger but missed as Crosshair kicked him at the side. This caused Hunter to able to locate CX-2 using his heightened senses and threw the [[spear]] at CX-2, impaling his chest and killing him.<ref name="Cavalry" />

[[File:VikBlaster-TBB-TheCavalryHasArrived.png|thumb|right|300px|Vik killed the last CX Trooper, narrowly saving Echo's life.]]
Meanwhile, as Wrecker and one assassin fell into the training room, Echo knocked out one of the assassins by grabbing the other assassin's hand with the blaster and shot him. The assassin got up but sustained more shots from Wrecker, therefore killing him. Echo returned the favor, killing the assassin behind Wrecker with a flurry of lasers. As the two fought the two remaining clone assassins, Wrecker was shot in the leg. He managed to get up and kill an assassin who had managed to sneak up behind Echo by slamming his head into a wall. As the final assassin attempted to attack Echo, [[Vik (clone)|Vik]] killed the assassin.<ref name="Cavalry" />
