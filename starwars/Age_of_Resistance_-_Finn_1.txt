'''''Age of Resistance - Finn'' 1''' is the first one-shot in the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] anthology series ''[[Star Wars: Age of Resistance]]''. It was written by [[Tom Taylor]], illustrated by [[Ramon Rosanas]], and published on [[July 3]], [[2019]] by [[Marvel Comics]]. The issue features a story set before the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], and it also includes an essay written by [[Bryan Young]].

==Publisher's summary==
''A LITTLE REBELLION! Before he was a rebel hero, FINN had another calling. He was&hellip;a janitor. But how could this lead FN-2187 to question everything? How much action can someone see mopping floors and dealing with a pest problem? It turns out, a whole lot.''
