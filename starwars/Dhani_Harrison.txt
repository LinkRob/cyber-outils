'''Dhani Harrison''' (born [[August 1]], [[1978]]<ref name="Emmy news" />) is a [[Wikipedia:British people|British]] musician, composer and songwriter who portrays [[FN-0878]] in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. He is the son of [[Wikipedia:The Beatles|the Beatles]] member [[Wikipedia:George Harrison|George Harrison]]. In addition to being a member of the band [[Wikipedia:thenewno2|thenewno2]], he is known for his work as a composer; alongside his writing partner [[Wikipedia:Paul Hicks (musician)|Paul Hicks]], he scored the 2013 fantasy film ''[[Wikipedia:Beautiful Creatures (2013 film)|Beautiful Creatures]]'' and the 2014 comedy ''[[Wikipedia:Learning to Drive (film)|Learning to Drive]]'', as well as television series such as ''[[Wikipedia:The Divide (TV series)|The Divide]]'', ''[[Wikipedia:Good Girls Revolt|Good Girls Revolt]]'' and the WGN TV series ''[[Wikipedia:Outsiders (American TV series)|Outsiders]]''.
