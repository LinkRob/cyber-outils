The '''family''' of the half-[[Theelin]] [[pilot]] [[Friielan]] was present on<ref name="TOTF" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="TRS">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> [[Valo]]<ref name="TOTF" /> during the world's [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] in [[231 BBY]]. When [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Valo Atrocity|attacked]] the [[Republic Fair|fair]], the strike resulted in the [[death]]s of numerous [[Jedi]] and civilians,<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> including Friielan's immediate family members. Following their relatives' passing, the pilot adopted a sharply focused demeanor, though still maintained a companionable attitude.<ref name="TOTF" />

By [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''The High Republic: Temptation of the Force''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> Friielan&mdash;as part of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]]&mdash;began working with [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] on dangerous [[Missions across the Stormwall|missions]] across the Nihil's [[Stormwall]] [[hyperspace]] barrier, which took the pair into the marauders' [[Occlusion Zone]] to rescue individuals and bring them back to [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="TOTF" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Kriss reflected on how the loss of their family had resulted in Friielan's change in countenance.<ref name="TOTF" />

==Behind the scenes==
