'''Flitchee''' was an [[Ewok]] [[warrior]]<ref name="Card Trader">{{SWCT|set=Moments of Triumph|cardname=Flitchee's Victory Cry}}</ref> who was a member of the [[Bright Tree tribe]] that lived in [[Bright Tree Village]] on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]].<ref name="RotJ" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Flitchee was among those Ewoks who aided the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Battle of Endor]]. At some point during the fighting, the Ewok scavenged<ref name="RotJ" /> a discarded<ref name="Card Trader" /> [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] and after an [[Tempest Scout 2|All Terrain Scout Transport]] captured by Alliance and Ewok forces managed to destroy another [[All Terrain Scout Transport|walker]] still under Imperial control,<ref name="RotJ" /> Flitchee waved the stoeln [[blaster]] in the air and pumped his [[Limb|arms]] as he<ref name="RotJ AD" /> let out a victory cry. Ultimately the Ewoks and Rebels claimed victory over the Empire.<ref name="RotJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flitchee first appeared in the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="RotJ" /> In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character was first identified in the ''[[Star Wars: Card Trader]]'' app with the [[2019]] card "Flitchee's Victory Cry."<ref name="Card Trader" /> The name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in a [[Hasbro]] action figure set released in [[2012]].<ref name="Hasbro">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: Movie Heroes|pack=Ewok Pack|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/Ewok_Pack_2012_Star_Wars_Movie_Heroes_Hasbro.asp}}</ref>
