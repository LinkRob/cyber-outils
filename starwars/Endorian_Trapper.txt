The '''Endorian Trapper''' was a costume owned by the [[Trandoshan]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Charr]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Cunning Trapper|standard costume]]. This outfit included a large [[Hair|fur]] collar, a large [[Bone|skull]] shoulder plate, and jewelry adorned with either [[Tooth|teeth]] or [[claw]]s.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Endorian Trapper was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Charr, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Endorian Scatter Gun]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing the Endorian Charr Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
