'''Engle''' was a [[clone trooper pilot]] who served under [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s command during the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[19 BBY]], Kenobi's [[fleet]] was [[Attack on Obi-Wan Kenobi's fleet|attacked]] by a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Separatist armada]] led by [[General]] [[Grievous]]. During the battle, Engle performed bombing runs on the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[warship]]s in their [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|Y-wing starfighter]] while another [[Cloning|clone]] [[pilot]], "[[Killer]]," provided cover [[fire]]. Engle's gunner also shot down [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] during the battle. However, after Killer was [[Death|killed]], vulture [[droid]]s attacked and destroyed Engle's Y-wing. Eventually, Engle crash-landed in the [[hangar]] bay of the ''[[Negotiator]]''. Engle survived the crash that killed their gunner and Kenobi helped the wounded [[pilot]] to safety from the wreckage.<ref name="Rescue" />

Engle had the standard [[Phase II clone trooper armor|Phase II armor]] worn by clone pilots with [[Color|green]] markings on his [[helmet]] and his shoulders.<ref name="Rescue" />
