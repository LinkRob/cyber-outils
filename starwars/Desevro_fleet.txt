A '''fleet''' of the [[Imperial Navy]] was active on [[Desevro]] and was led by [[Captain]] [[Amos]] from the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tormentor]]'' until he was [[Death|killed]] when the ''Tormentor'' became his only ship and was destroyed. The fleet consisted of several [[Star Destroyer]]s that were all eventually destroyed.

==Description==
The [[Imperial Navy]] [[fleet]] at [[Desevro]] consisted of several Star Destroyers including the ''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer ''[[Tormentor]],'' the [[flagship]]. Additionally, the fleet was composed of [[TIE series|TIE fighter series]] [[starfighter]]s, which aided in the defense of Desevro.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==History==
Following the [[Battle of Endor]], the fleet at Desevro was under the command of [[Captain]] [[Amos]] aboard the ''Tormentor'', and participated in several [[fleet battles over Desevro]] against the [[New Republic]]. The actions, considered pointless and reckless by the captain's colleagues, destroyed all of the fleet's vessels except for the ''Tormentor'', which was damaged by the [[time]] the ISD [[Overseer (Imperial-class)|''Overseer'']] arrived in the region after contacting local Imperial officials that it was undertaking a delicate mission to locate Republic [[Project Starhawk]]. When the ''Overseer'' arrived, wreckage from the fleet's destroyed ships littered the [[Realspace|space]] around Desevro, and [[Titan Squadron]] began tracking a [[Unidentified New Republic convoy|New Republic convoy]] of [[engineer]]s en route to Project Starhawk.<ref name="Squadrons" />

At that time, Captain Amos and the ''Tormentor'' emerged from [[hyperspace]] and attacked the [[convoy]], forcing the ''Overseer'' to defend the Republic convoy until its teams could board a [[GR-75 medium transport]] and secure its navigational data as the mission was endangered. By that time during the [[battle above Desevro]], the ''Tormentor'' had sustained heavy fire, and was destroyed by a chain reaction of explosions that decimated the ''Tormentor'' after a Republic [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] exploded near the ship.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Commanders and crew==
The fleet at Desevro was under the command of Captain Amos, who was considered by other Imperials as trigger-happy and reckless.<ref name="Squadrons" />
