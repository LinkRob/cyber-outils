'''Ben Bailey Smith''', also known by his rapper alter ego '''Doc Brown''', is a British actor, screenwriter and rapper<ref name="Pais" /> who played [[Blevin]] in the [[2022]] ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series.<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> He is the brother of the famous novelist [[Wikipedia:Zadie Smith|Zadie Smith]].<ref name="Pais" /> As an actor, he is best known for playing [[W:c:lawandorder:Joe Hawkins|Joe Hawkins]] in the British police procedural series ''[[Wikipedia: Law & Order: UK|Law & Order: UK]]'' and [[W:c:4oclockclub:Nathan Carter|Nathan Carter]] in the comedy drama series ''[[Wikipedia:4 O'Clock Club|4 O'Clock Club]]''.

Smith originally auditioned for the role of young [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] for the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', a part which ultimately went to [[Donald Glover]]. In [[2020]], he did a casting for what he was lead to believe was a military drama called ''Pilgrims'', and was told that his role was "a sergeant major chewing out some soldiers." A few days later, he was told that he had gotten the job and that the project was actually ''Star Wars''.<ref name="Pais" />

Due to studio secrecy, Smith was only given the script for his own scenes, with the context being explained to him on the shooting day. The scripts were titled ''Pilgrims'' and false names were put up on the dressing rooms. The daily production sheets were also fake. To keep drones from photographing them, the actors had to wear large cloaks when walking from their dressing room to the set. Smith struggled with the lines, which were full of sci-fi language, names of fictional [[planet]]s, and fictional laws, and didn't know what the character was actually talking about. According to the actor, "One day I was delivering a line and the director came over. He's like, can we do it again with a bit more urgency? Because this planet is about to explode. And I was like, is it? I didn't know. Now I know what's going on."<ref name="Pais" />
