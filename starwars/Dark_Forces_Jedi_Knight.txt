'''''Dark Forces/Jedi Knight''''' is a multimedia project centered on a series of [[Star Wars video games|video game]]s following the adventures of [[Kyle Katarn]], [[Mara Jade Skywalker|Mara Jade]] and [[Jaden Korr]]. The ''Dark Forces'' games have multimedia tie-ins, while the ''Jedi Knight'' games aren't related to any other products.

==Development==
''Dark Forces'' debuted with the [[Star Wars video games|first-person shooter]] ''[[Star Wars: Dark Forces]]'' in [[1995]], by [[LucasArts]], and introduced the character [[Kyle Katarn]]. It was followed by a sequel, ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]'', in [[1997]], which saw the ''Dark Forces'' title slide to a subtitle as the series adopted the new ''Jedi Knight'' main title, mirroring the evolution of Katarn from a spy operative in the original game to a [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] in the sequel, which featured [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsabers]] and [[Force power/Legends|Force powers]]. It was followed in [[1998]] by an expansion, ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Mysteries of the Sith]]'', with [[Mara Jade Skywalker|Mara Jade]] as the main character.

Between 1997 and 1998, the first two games were adapted by [[William C. Dietz]] into [[Star Wars: Dark Forces (novellas)|''Star Wars: Dark Forces'' novellas]]: ''[[Dark Forces: Soldier for the Empire]]'', ''[[Dark Forces: Rebel Agent]]'' and ''[[Dark Forces: Jedi Knight]]''. The novellas followed the events of the game as well as providing an extended view of the background of the series.

With a second sequel, ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight II: Jedi Outcast]]'', the game series abandoned the ''Dark Forces'' title. Released in [[2002]], it was the first time the series departed from internal development at [[LucasArts]] and instead was developed by independent developer [[Raven Software]]. In [[2003]], Raven also released a fourth entry to the series, ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Jedi Academy]]'', this time focusing on the new character of [[Jaden Korr]]. The LucasArts website posted a series of [[in-universe]] stories, the ''[[N.R.I. Reports]]'', which provided some background to the then upcoming game.

In [[2005]], [[roleplaying game]] publisher [[Wizards of the Coast]] released two series of web supplements focusing on the ''Dark Forces'' storyline, written by [[Abel G. Peña]] and [[Jason Fry]]. ''[[The Dark Forces Saga]]'' provided players with rules and background information for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'', while ''[[Kyle Katarn's Tale]]'' did the same for [[Star Wars Miniatures|''Star Wars'' Miniatures]].

Two rejected concepts for future games in the series include ''Jedi Knight III: Brink of Darkness'' and ''Jedi Master''.<ref name="Rogue Leaders">''[[Rogue Leaders: The Story of LucasArts]]''</ref> ''Jedi Master'' was one of the many video-game concepts LucasArts considered for what would become ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]''. It was to be a survival horror game&mdash;a much darker, more violent, and more mature continuation of the ''Jedi Knight'' series set during the [[Great Jedi Purge/Legends|Great Jedi Purge]] immediately following [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']]. The concept was originally put out to potential consumers to test the demand for both another ''Jedi Knight'' game and a more mature ''Star Wars'' game, but both the consumers and the developers felt the concept was too gratuitous and derivative, so it was thrown out.<ref name="Making of TFU">''[[The Art and Making of Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]''</ref>

On [[August 23]], [[2023]] [[Nightdive Studios]] announced ''[[Star Wars: Dark Forces Remaster]]'', a remastered version of the original ''Star Wars: Dark Forces'' video game with enhanced graphics, redone cutscenes, behind-the-scenes content and the demo previously playable at Consumer Electronics Show in 1994. The remastered game was released on [[February 28]], [[2024]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/dark-forces-remaster|text=''Dark Forces'' ''Remaster'' Comes to Consoles and PC|date=2024-02-28}}</ref>

==Continuity==
[[File:DFlogo.jpg|thumb|250px|''Dark Forces'' logo]]
These works were the first video games (years before ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'') that had new elements referenced by subsequent [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] sources and [[Retroactive continuity|retcon]]s, transitioned afterwards to the wider Expanded Universe corpus, thus creating several important and integral concepts in the ''Star Wars'' continuity and the [[Timeline of galactic history/Legends|galactic history]]. Examples include some [[Force power/Legends|Force powers]], the [[Dark Trooper/Legends|dark troopers]], the [[Seventh Battle of Ruusan|Battle of Ruusan]], [[Prophets of the Dark Side]], [[Dromund Kaas/Legends|Dromund Kaas]], and the [[Reborn]].
