The '''C1-series astromech droid'''<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> was a model of [[C-series]] [[astromech droid]]<ref name="SWTVE" /> manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> They were used during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Homecoming">{{Rebels|Homecoming}}</ref> and the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]], though by then they were considered antiques<ref name="ROTR">''[[Rise of the Rebels]]''</ref> and had passed their manufacturer's expiry date a few [[Standard decade|decades]] prior.<ref name="DID">[[Droids in Distress (book)|''Droids in Distress'' chapter book]]</ref> They had three mechanical arms to manipulate objects with,<ref name="VG" /> and featured an electrical [[arc welder]].<ref name="The Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref> [[CH-33P]], nicknamed "Cheep," served onboard the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tribunal]]'' during [[Order 66]] and assisted [[Ahsoka Tano]] in capturing [[Rex|Commander Rex]] as well as helping both escape the ship before being executed by clones.<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> [[C1-10P]], a [[droid]] nicknamed "Chopper" who served on the ''[[Ghost]]'', was a C1 model, although he was mismatched after years of repairs and patch jobs.<ref name="VG" />
