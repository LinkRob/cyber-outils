'''Codia I''' was a [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet]]. It was [[orbit]]ed by two [[moon]]s, the [[Codian Moon]] and [[Codia's second moon|another]] one that was tidally locked to the planet's [[Codian dwarf star|star]]. [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] at one point mentioned Codia I in his [[The Journals of Ben Kenobi|journals]], and it was also noted on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[map]] included in the [[book]] ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''.

==Description==
{{Quote|Codia I had ''two moons''&mdash;one moss-covered, ''forested'' under starlight. ''[&hellip;]'' The second moon was a "''phoenix world''," possessing a ''vast ocean'' on its darker side, but with a rockier surface facing the Codian star.|The journals of Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" />}}
Codia I,<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" /> also referred to simply as Codia,<ref name="Traveler">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> was a [[planet]] located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Codia system]]<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" /> within grid square H-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="TFABG" /> It occupied the first [[orbit]]al position around the [[Codian dwarf star]]<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" /> and shared its name with the location known as [[New Codia]].<ref name="Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> The planet itself was circled by two [[moon]]s, the [[forest]]- and [[moss]]-covered<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" /> [[Codian Moon]]<ref name="USWNE" /> and [[Codia's second moon|another moon]], which was tidally locked to Codia I's [[star]]. The darker side of that natural satellite was nevertheless illuminated by planetshine.<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" />

==History==
When [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], visited the Codia [[Star system|system]] while on a [[Mission to Codia's second moon|mission]] to Codia I's second moon<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" /> at some point between [[41 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="DateRange">In the flashbacks seen in [[Obi-Wan 2|''Obi-Wan'' 2]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] go on a [[Mission to Codia's second moon|mission]]. [[Padawan (novel)|''Padawan'']] depicts Kenobi's first [[Mission to Lenahra|mission]], and since ''Obi-Wan'' 2 depicts a different mission, it must take place afterward. As Jinn is still alive in the flashback, it must take place before his death, and since ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the novel to [[41 BBY]] and Jinn's death to [[32 BBY]], the flashback must take place between those two [[Standard year|years]].</ref> Kenobi pointed out to Jinn how Codia's second moon was illuminated by the planet.<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" /> Around [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The frame story from [[Obi-Wan 2|''Obi-Wan'' 2]] occurs around [[1 BBY]] per the [[:Template:Obi-WanFrameDate|reasoning here]].</ref> Kenobi also briefly mentioned Codia I in his [[The Journals of Ben Kenobi|journals]],<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" /> and at some point, the [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] also noted the location of the planet on a [[map]] of [[the galaxy]] included in his [[book]] ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''.<ref name="Traveler" />

==Behind the scenes==
Codia was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[Galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' boxed set.<ref name="TFABG" /> It was first established as a planet in [[Cole Horton]]'s [[2020]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''.<ref name="Traveler" /> The [[Obi-Wan 2|second issue]] of the [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan (comic series)|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series, written by [[Christopher Cantwell]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" /> on [[June 29]], [[2022]],<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/101188/star_wars_obi-wan_2022_2|text=Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi (2022) #2}}</ref> first identified the planet as Codia I.<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" />
