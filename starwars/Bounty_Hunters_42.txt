'''''Bounty Hunters'' 42''' is the forty-second and final issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Jethro Morales]], [[Paolo Villanelli]], and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 17]], [[2024]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE EXPLOSIVE END OF THE SAGA! VALANCE THE BOUNTY HUNTER has one final mission to complete&hellip;and it may indeed be his LAST! Can he and the crew fight their way past BOBA FETT&hellip;and JABBA's SECRET WEAPON? The GRAND FINALE of the series that's four years in the making!''

==Development==
===Teases and later clarifications===
While developing the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]], writer [[Ethan Sacks]] asked [[Marvel Comics]] and [[Lucasfilm]] for specific things.<ref name="3 things">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1713663464231633102|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=Yes, it's true. STAR WARS: BOUNTY HUNTERS ends with Issue 42 in January. If you weren't at the panel, I can tell you I asked @Marvel and Lucasfilm for three things to end it with a bang (no, that's not a blowing up Valance reference). 1/|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/KiSOe}}</ref> The first was for the issue to be longer, meaning it would need two artists&mdash;the first being [[Jethro Morales]]<ref name="Artist 1">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1713663465955467403|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=NO. 1: It's going to be extra-sized to send Valance with off an explosive finale. (Sorry.) It's so big we need TWO artists including the great Jethro Morales&hellip; 2/|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/flw0S}}</ref>&mdash;and the second being the returning [[Paolo Villanelli]], whose return was also his second request.<ref name="Artist 2">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1713663467251511467|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=NO. 2: I asked for was to bring back @paolovillanelli for the ending. YES! If you weren't at the panel, you missed Paolo jumping up from the audience, yelling "You promised I was done, I can't take this anymore," and running out of the room. We're proud of that bit. 3/|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/vjWIJ}}</ref> Villanelli was happy to return to the series to aid in bringing a story he helped start to the finish line.<ref name="Villanelli tweet">{{Twitter|paolovillanelli|status/1747592818460328439|[[Paolo Villanelli]]|quote=Issue 42 of #starwars #bountyhunters is out today. Is the end of nearly 3 years of crazy storytelling with fellows @ethanjsacks and arifprianto super grateful to all the @marvel editorial team and @lucasfilm for giving me a chance to come back to finish what we started /1|archiveurl=https://archive.is/62vxH}}</ref> Part of what Villanelli drew was the comic's ending. Sacks also had a third request:<ref name="Third request">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1713663468509823395|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=Don't worry he's under contract. He had to draw the ending. And it's perfect. Then there was request NO. 3. 4/|archivedate=20231016040613}}</ref> he wanted a chance to write a comic about the bounty hunter [[Jango Fett]]<ref name="Jango is third">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1753033058645664151|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=Yup {{C|In response to: "what was request three? was it the jango miniseries?"}}|archiveurl=https://archive.is/DSexx}}</ref> and said as such when asked what he wanted his next project to be. The request was quickly green-lit, leading to the 2024<ref name="Jango Ethan Tweet">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1713663476323828162|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=They asked me what I wanted to do next. I said Jango. They said yes. They said your artist will be Luke Ross. I spontaneously combusted. Coming in March 2024.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/B07ES}}</ref> comic miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Jango Fett (comic series)|Star Wars: Jango Fett]]''<ref name="Publishing Panel">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2023-lucasfilm-publishing-panel|text=NYCC 2023: 13 Highlights from the Lucasfilm Publishing Panel}}</ref> by Sacks with art by [[Luke Ross]].<ref name="Jango Ethan Tweet" /> According to Sacks, it is the loyalty of ''Bounty Hunters'' readers that got the series to forty-two issues.<ref name="Sacks Thanksgiving">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1727831947425067456|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=This Thanksgiving, I'm grateful for the readers who have supported A HAUNTED GIRL, as well those poor tortured souls in #ValanceNation who have gotten us to 42 issues, a rarity in comics these days. Thank you.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/7mkeL}}</ref>

At [[Wikipedia:New York Comic Con|New York Comic Con]] 2023, it was revealed that issue 42 would be the final of the series,<ref name="3 things" /> with Sacks claiming it would feature [[Beilert Valance]]'s "last adventure."<ref name="Last adventure" /> Stating it was a "true story," Sacks claimed in a Tweet on [[October 20]], 2023, that he had happy endings prepared for the issue but abandoned them when Villanelli returned, instead adopting what he called "Plan B."<ref name="Happy endings">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1715525887599227212|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=True story: I had plans for a series of happy endings for all the major characters at the end of Issue 42. But then @paolovillanelli agreed to come back. So we went with Plan B|archivedate=20231024191223}}</ref> However, after the comic's release and the endings for most characters indeed being positive, Sacks revealed the Villanelli part of the Tweet had been a joke: the happy endings half of the Tweet had been the "true story" all along.<ref name="Got us good">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1748165032909681043|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=The true story part was the first part. The Paolo part was a joke.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/UZTij}}</ref> Indeed, Valance's ending had been planned by Sacks from the start, with the only question being who he would reach out to.<ref name="Valance ending">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1748113672726106572|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=That last page of Bounty Hunters #42 was always how I wanted to end the series. I just didn't know who would be on the other end of the grasp yet.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/AiUla}}</ref> At first, all Sacks was certain of was that it would not be his former love [[Yuralla Vega]] because she had moved on from their relationship. According to Sacks, the ending sees Valance having finally found worth in his own being<ref name="Valance ending 2">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1748115035967180885|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=I just knew ...SPOILER ALERT...>>>>>>>...that it couldn't be Yura. She had to be strong enough to have moved on after he left her for years, a he struggled with the feelings that he was horribly damaged and not worthy. And he had to grow to find the worth in himself.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/8ZHjj}}</ref> and having become the person [[Nakano Lash]] saw in him.<ref name="Lash saw in him">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1748124971468001541|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=He finally grew into the person she saw in him. {{C|In response to: "It somewhat reminded me of this, which is interesting!" and an attached image of Lash telling Valance "Because I was once that kid... and no one was there to save me."}}|archiveurl=https://archive.is/zIR7H}}</ref> 

Via his Twitter account on [[June 28]], [[2023]], in what would be the first public reference to issue 42, Sacks teased the comic could address the fates of the characters [[Dengar]] and [[Manaroo]], thereby explaining why Dengar was still working as a bounty hunter into the [[New Republic Era]] despite his plans to quit and also explaining Manaroo's absence in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy|The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Sacks issue 42">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1685054610740854784|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=May be addressed in Bounty Hunters 42 {{C|In response to: "Do you guys ever think about Manaroo and Dengar planning to leave bounty hunting behind and retire but then Dengar is still doing bounty hunting during the time of Aftermath and Manaroo is nowhere in sight?}}|archivedate=20230731162214}}</ref> In the final issue, Manaroo is shown to have grown annoyed with Dengar's attitude and actions.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 42">[[Bounty Hunters 42|''Bounty Hunters'' 42]]</ref> When asked by a fan if the issue was teasing Manaroo soon breaking off her relationship with Dengar, Sacks responded "a lot of seeds [had been] planted for some bitter fruit for the lug!"<ref name="Bitter fruit">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1748124839590670629|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=There have been a lot of seeds planted for some bitter fruit for the lug!|archiveurl=https://archive.is/YtMM5}}</ref> Issue 42 also sees a mention of [[Droopy McCool]] as a gift for Tommy Stella,<ref name="Droopy">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1748138144996708580|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=Verified. That was for Tommy. {{C|In response to "I have acquired the big grand Bounty Hunter finale and found the Droopy McCool reference! Ethan told me earlier today that he put the reference in just for me (not a bit) and it's the nicest thing anyone has ever done for me. I would die for u @ethanjsacks"}}|archiveurl=https://archive.is/rBme4}}</ref> Sacks's, in Stella's words, "Droopy muse."<ref name="Droopy Muse">{{Twitter|tommy_stella|status/1748138468574658911|Tommy Stella|quote=It's an honor to be your Droopy muse. Or as the kids say, your droose. Hayden said that earlier. Hayden is kids.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/SpPsK}}</ref>
