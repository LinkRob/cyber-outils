'''Dog biscuits''' were a type of [[biscuit]] intended for [[Diet|consumption]] by [[dog]]s. A [[fish]]y type was fed to [[Corellian hound|hounds]] on [[Corellia]], but they could also be eaten by most bipedal [[species]], such as [[human]]s. Starving beings such as [[Han Solo]] and [[Qi'ra]] ate them for their nutrients.<ref name="Most Wanted" /> As a youngster, Qi'ra attempted to pickpocket one from [[Moloch]] who took her to [[Proxima]] and forced her to join the [[White Worms]].<ref name="CC">''[[Crimson Climb]]''</ref>
