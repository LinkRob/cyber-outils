'''Endor''', designated '''IX3244-A''', also known as the '''Forest Moon of Endor''' or the '''Sanctuary Moon''', was a small [[forest]]ed [[moon]] that was the ninth moon that [[orbit]]ed the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of the [[Endor (planet)|same name]]. It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Dulok]], [[Ewok]], and [[Yuzzum]] [[species]], as well as the [[Wistie]] and semi-sentient [[Gorax]] races.

==Description==
[[File:Endor-SWCT.png|thumb|left|200px|View of Endor from space]]
A remote [[moon]]<ref name="Stewards of the Forest Moon">{{InsiderCite|212|Stewards of the Forest Moon}}</ref> in the [[Endor system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], Endor was covered in dense woodlands, tall [[mountain]]s, and [[savanna]]s, and home to the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Ewok]], [[Dulok]], and [[Yuzzum]] [[species]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /><ref name="AbEverything" /> The semi-sentient [[Gorax]]<ref name="Sentience">The Gorax appearing in "[[Traps and Tribulations]]" wears a sash and uses a [[tree]] as a crude tool. This shows at least [[Sentience|semi-sentience]].</ref> also dwelled on the moon as well as the [[Wistie]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Some of Endor's [[tree]]s reached more than 1,000 [[meter]]s in height.<ref name="SWCL" /> It contained a breathable [[atmosphere]] to [[human]]s and 8% surface [[water]]. It also had two [[Star|suns]]: [[Endor I]] and [[Endor II]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /><ref name="AbEverything" />

==History==
Throughout galactic history, offworld raiders occasionally came to Endor to [[Hunting|hunt]] the [[Homeworld|native]] Ewok population to make [[Ewok Jerky]], which was a popular snack across the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="AbEverything" />

During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Empire, deeming Endor to be perfect for its purposes due to how remote the moon was,<ref name="Stewards of the Forest Moon" /> established an outpost on the moon that included a [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] [[Death Star's shield generator|complex]] to protect the incomplete [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] in [[orbit]] of the moon.<ref name="Beware" /> The Empire [[Occupation of Endor|occupied]] Endor.<ref name="Yub Nub 217">{{InsiderCite|217|Yub Nub! 40 Years of the Ewoks}}</ref> In order to make way for the complex, an Ewok village was razed by Imperial [[troop]]s, leading to strained Imperial&mdash;Ewok relations.<ref name="Beware" /> As a result of the [[repulsorlift]] field emitted by the [[SLD-26 planetary shield generator]] to keep the Death Star in a stationary orbit, Endor was prone to [[Groundquake|earthquakes]], tidal imbalances, and other geological disturbances<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> that slowly but surely began to break up the small moon.<ref name="Masters">{{StoryCite|story=Masters|book=Stories of Jedi and Sith}}</ref> The [[Tarkin Initiative]] also established [[Research Station 9]] on the moon.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

<div style="float:right">[[File:ISDIISurroundedByTIEsAtEndor-SWROTJVA.png|thumb|left|250px|The Galactic Empire commenced a naval presence at Endor.]]</div>
The moon was later the site of the monumental [[Battle of Endor]] that saw combat between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and its Ewok allies against [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] who were attempting to protect the shield generator and prevent the Alliance from destroying its [[superweapon]] in orbit. After fierce combat on the [[forest]] moon, the Rebel Alliance managed not only to destroy the shield generator complex, but also the second Death Star. With the destruction of the Empire's dreaded [[battle station]] and the [[death]] of [[Darth Sidious|Emperor Palpatine]] and his enforcer, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the rebels and Ewoks held a victory celebration on the moon, one of many across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="RotJ" />

[[File:EndorParty-ROTJ.png|thumb|left|250px|Ewoks and rebels celebrating victory on Endor.]]
The Rebels set up shields and [[tractor beam]]s to protect the moon from debris from the destroyed [[space station]].<ref name="Endor Debris">{{Twitter|starwars|status/790904500768866304|Star Wars|quote=The Rebel Alliance fleet deployed shields and tractor beams to prevent Death Star 2's meteroric debris from devastating Endor's forest moon.|archivefile=File:SWTweetEndorDebris.png}}</ref> A field of detritus settled into orbit around the moon in the [[Standard month|months]] following the battle.<ref name="Aftermath" /> Additionally, the many [[Hyperspace anomaly|hyperspace anomalies]] present in the [[Moddell sector]] absorbed the worst of the Death Star debris fallout.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Despite that, some debris still rained down on the moon for a [[Standard decade|decade]] after the battle, but Endor's ecosystem proved resilient.<ref name="TROSEE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

At some point, the [[Ewok Civil War]] brought strife to Endor. The [[New Republic]] intervened by sending C-3PO and [[Captain]] [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] on a [[diplomatic mission to Endor]].<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-jaxxon|text=Holy Hutch! ''Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories Annual&mdash;Jaxxon 2023'' Revealed}}</ref> During the [[New Republic Era]], Endor remained remote and rarely-visited.<ref name="Explorer's Guide" /> In [[35 ABY]], following the [[Battle of Exegol|Battle]] of [[Exegol]], a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] stationed above the forest moon was [[Uprising on Endor|destroyed]] through use of the [[Holdo maneuver]]. The event was watched by [[Wicket Wystri Warrick|Wicket W. Warrick]] and his son, [[Pommet Warrick]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
Endor made its first appearance in the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. The scenes of the forest moon were filmed in the giant redwood forests of northern California, in particular Tall Trees Redwood Grove and other forests near the remote town of Smith River,<ref name="AbEverything" /> and at soundstages in England, where the Ewok village scenes were shot.<ref name="Yub Nub 217" /> During the production of the film, Endor was referred to as the "Green Moon of Endor."<ref name="MakingOf1983">''[[The Making of Star Wars: Return of the Jedi]]''</ref>

Some location scenes for Endor were filmed in Stout Grove, part of Jedediah Smith Redwoods State Park, 5 miles east of Crescent City, California, near the Tolowa village Hiouchi. Scenes requiring explosions were shot on private logging company land in the vicinity because the trees were scheduled to be logged and the State Park would not allow damage to their trees.<ref name="Making of">''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]''</ref>
