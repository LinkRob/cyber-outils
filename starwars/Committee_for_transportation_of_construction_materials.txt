The '''committee for transportation of construction materials''' was a committee in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Galactic Senate]], led by [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] of [[Alderaan]], that dealt with the [[transport]]ation of construction materials to [[planet]]s in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. Other members of the committee included Senators [[Mon Mothma]] of [[Chandrila]] and [[Onaconda Farr]] of [[Rodia]]. Several [[Standard month|months]] into her tenure as senator of [[Naboo]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] joined the committee.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />
