The '''''Beta''-class shuttle''' was a small [[shuttle]] used on [[Coruscant]] in [[382 BBY]].<ref name="date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Night of Sorrow]] to 382 BBY. [[Kyong Greylark]] used a ''Beta''-class shuttle in this battle.</ref>

==Description==
The ship was trapezoidal in shape and was equipped with armor and weapons.<ref name="cataclysm" />

==History==
[[Kyong Greylark]] borrowed a ''Beta''-class shuttle from the armory on Coruscant to travel to [[Dalna]] in an attempt to save her son [[Axel Greylark]].<ref name="cataclysm" />
