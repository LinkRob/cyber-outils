'''Duoday''' was a [[Standard day/Legends|day]] of the [[Standard week/Legends|week]] used during [[Roan Novachez]]'s time at the [[Jedi academy]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. It was used alongside [[Monoday]], [[Triday]], [[Quadday]], [[Pentaday]], [[Hexaday]], and [[Heptaday]].<ref name="Jedi Academy">[[Jedi Academy (novel)|''Jedi Academy'']]</ref>
