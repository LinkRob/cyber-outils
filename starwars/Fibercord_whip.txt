[[File:MandoAhsokaWhipcord.png|thumb|right|300px|Din Djarin using his fibercord whip to tie up Ahsoka Tano]]
A '''fibercord whip''' was a projected length of [[fibercord]], which was shot out of a [[whipcord launcher]] and used to ensnare an individual or item. A [[grappling hook]] was usually attached to the end.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[Din Djarin]] used a fibercord whip to ensnare the leg of an escaping [[Quarren]] [[Quarren Trawler|trawler]] during a battle on a fight in a [[Boatman's Reach|bar]] on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Pagodon]] in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Mandalorian 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> As well as using the whip to temporarily restrain [[Ahsoka Tano]] during their short fight on [[Corvus]]. [[Din Djarin]] also used the whip to climb alongside the side of a moving [[Unidentified sandcrawler|sandcrawler]] during a [[Mission to Arvala-7|skirmish]] with a [[Jawa]] [[Chettkap's tribe|tribe]] on [[Arvala-7]].<ref name="Mandalorian 2">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 2: The Child}}</ref>
