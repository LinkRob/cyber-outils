The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''205 BBY''',<ref name="Mando gatekeeper">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that the [[Unidentified Mandalorian gatekeeper|Mandalorian gatekeeper]] at [[Presidential manor|Jamie Brasson's estate]] had been employed for five [[Standard year|years]], the [[Mandalorian]] must have been employed around [[205 BBY]].</ref> approximately '''3072 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''186 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7772''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*A [[Unidentified Mandalorian gatekeeper|Mandalorian]] is employed as a gatekeeper at [[Jamie Brasson]]'s [[Presidential manor]].<ref name="Mando gatekeeper" />
**[[Huang]] begins paying off his debts to [[Li Yu]] by working as a member of Li's [[Silver Knights]].<ref name="Wong debts">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that [[Huang]] had been working as a member of [[Li Yu]]'s [[Silver Knights]] to pay off his debts for five [[Standard year|years]], Huang must have joined the Knights around 205 BBY.</ref>
**Zhang Jincheng ascends to the position of the Fire station's manager.<ref name="Fire ascension">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that [[Zhang Jincheng]] ascended to the position of the [[Fire (city)|Fire]] [[Space station|station's]] manager five [[Standard year|year]] ago, the promotion must have occurred around [[205 BBY]].</ref>
