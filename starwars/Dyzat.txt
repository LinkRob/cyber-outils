'''Dyzats''' were unpleasant, verminous animals of [[Ansion/Legends|Ansion]].

In [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]], during the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Border dispute on Ansion/Legends|mission to Ansion]], the [[Qulun]] [[Qulun camp|camp]] pursuers of the small [[Gwurran]] [[Tooqui]] cursed the fleeing runt by invoking the dyzat's name.
