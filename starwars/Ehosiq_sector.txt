The '''Ehosiq sector''' was a [[sector]] of [[the galaxy]]. The [[Force-sensitive]] [[Nogratu]] [[Fourth Sister|Lyn Rakish]], later known as the Fourth Sister, was a [[Homeworld|native]] of the sector and went on to serve in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Jedi Order]] and then the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Inquisitorius]].<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Ehosiq_sector.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The Ehosiq sector in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Ehosiq sector was identified in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''.<ref name="CGttSWG" /> The [[Ehosiq sector/Legends|sector]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 10: Bounty Hunters]]'', a [[1994]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Rick D. Stuart]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="GG10">''[[Galaxy Guide 10: Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>

An appearance of the [[Kiros system/Legends|Kiros system]] in [[The Clone Wars 1|''The Clone Wars'' 1]]&mdash;a [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] written by [[Henry Gilroy]], illustrated by [[Scott Hepburn]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="TCW1"> [[The Clone Wars 1|''The Clone Wars'' 1]]</ref> on [[September 10]], [[2008]]<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/15-679/Star-Wars-The-Clone-Wars-1|text=Star Wars: The Clone Wars #1}}</ref>&mdash;was retroactively established to be the first appearance of the [[Sector/Legends|sector]] when the [[Star system|system]] was established to be a part of the Ehosiq sector by [[Jason Fry]]'s [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion]] in [[2009]].<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
