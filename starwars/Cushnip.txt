'''Cushnip''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] which could be served baked with [[fral]]. 

==Description==
Cushnip was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] which could be served baked with [[fral]].<ref name="TFAVD" />

==History==
The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] cooked baked cushnip with fral at the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]] around thirty [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="TFAVD" />

Cushnip was available on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> In was listed as costing five [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on both the menu at the [[Hot Pot]] [[Restaurant|eatery]] in the [[Market District (Mirogana)|market]] of the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] and at the [[Daruda Diner]] in the town of [[Jaunta's Hope]], both located on the moon.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cushnip first appeared as a prop in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], a film released in [[2015]]. It was not identified in the film, but was named in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released the day after the film.
