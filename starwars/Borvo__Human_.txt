'''Borvo''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[sniper]] and [[soldier]] who served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Biography==
Borvo was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] as a [[soldier]] and [[Sniper|marksman]].<ref name="Boots" /> By the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date Note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]]. Given that the events of ''[[Rogue One: Boots on the Ground]]'' take place around and during the events of ''Rogue One'', it can be determined that the game takes place in the same year.</ref> the Alliance was involved in the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. During this time, Borvo may have served the Alliance on various missions based on the [[planet]] [[Jedha]] and may have also participated in the [[Battle of Scarif|battle on the]] [[Scarif|planet Scarif]].<ref name="Boots" />

==Personality and traits==
Borvo was a light-skinned human with a [[Color|graying]] [[Hair|beard]] and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="Boots" />

==Equipment==
While participating in missions, Borvo would wear the garb of a [[rebel trooper]]. In addition to carrying a [[blaster rifle]], Borvo utilized a [[sniper rifle]] that could be used to pick off targets at long range.<ref name="Boots" />

==Behind the scenes==
Borvo was created as a playable character for the online browser [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Rogue One: Boots on the Ground|Star Wars: Boots on the Ground]]'', and was one of four characters&mdash;alongside [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], [[K-2SO]], and [[Shara (rebel)|Shara]]&mdash;that the player was given to use at the start of the game. Borvo could be selected as a member of the player's three-man rebel squad that would undergo missions together.<ref name="Boots" />

Given the freedom that the player has in creating their squad, there is no certainty as to what missions and battles in the game, if any, Borvo actually participated in.<ref name="Boots" />
