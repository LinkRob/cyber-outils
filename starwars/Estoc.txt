'''Estoc''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] [[Imperial officer]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], serving as a [[lieutenant]] and later as a [[commander]].

==Biography==
===Stationed on Kethmandi===
[[File:Estoc_on_Kethmandi.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Estoc aboard a 20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport moving prisoners.]]
At some point, [[Corporal]] Estoc was stationed on the [[planet]] [[Kethmandi]]. During her [[time]] there, she was transporting [[prisoner]]s on a [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport]] to a [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[prison]] on Kethmandi. Two prisoners, [[Chewbacca]] and [[Lena Freemaker]], tried to escape but only Lena managed to escape and Chewbacca did not. Lena later made her way to the [[prison]] to find a way off the planet.<ref name="The Chase with Han/Escape with Chewbacca">{{All-Stars|The Chase with Han / Escape with Chewbacca}}</ref>

During her escape, Lena ran right into [[Pace Freemaker/LEGO|Pace]] who was trying to hide from [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]] the [[Hutt]] because he owed a [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] to him. Then a [[stormtrooper]] and [[Dengar]] spotted the two and Lena and Pace took off on a [[speeder]] and later escaped them. The two eventually reached an Imperial transport which was heading to the planet [[Vandor]].<ref name="The Chase with Han/Escape with Chewbacca" />

===Tracking the fugitives===
[[File:Estoc_aboard_her_Y-45_armored_transport_hauler.jpg|thumb|248x248px|right|Estoc aboard her Y-45 armored transport hauler.]]
Pace and Lena landed on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]]. Later they entered a bar with several individuals; one of them, [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], made a deal that included the capture of [[corusca gem]]s from a corusca gem [[comet]]. During their discussion about this task, a [[Gender|male]] [[Narquois]] named [[Pru Sweevant]], who worked for Graballa, reported Pace Lena to the [[Hutt]]. When Graballa found out that Estoc was searching for Lena, he contacted Estoc about the fugitive.<ref name="Dealing with Lando/Han and Chewie Strike Back">{{All-Stars|Dealing with Lando / Han and Chewie Strike Back}}''</ref>

When Pace, Lena, Calrissian and [[L3-37]] reached the [[comet]], they started to obtain the gems, but Graballa and Estoc arrived. They caught Calrissian's [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', into a [[tractor beam]]. Pace and Lena hid behind the comet with the chance to escape with the gems but stayed. They released the gems onto Graballa's ship and Estoc's [[Y-45 armored transport hauler]], disabling both vessels and allowing Pace, Lena, Calrissian and L3-37 to escape.<ref name="Dealing with Lando/Han and Chewie Strike Back" />

===Tracking Leia and Luke===
[[File:Estoc_investigating_Freemaker_garage.jpg|thumb|251x251px|left|Estoc investigating the Freemaker Salvage and Repair shop.]]
After the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] leaders [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Luke Skywalker]] fled aboard [[The Wheel]], Estoc gave a [[hologram]] report warning the residents about the presence of the rebels. Estoc then led a squad of [[stormtrooper]]s to search [[Freemaker Salvage and Repair]] in the [[Middle Ring/LEGO|Middle Ring]] for any signs of the rebel fugitives.<ref name="Crossing Paths" />

[[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi]] and [[Zander Freemaker]] hid Princess Leia inside a trash box before Lieutenant Estoc and her [[trooper]]s could enter the garage. Kordi and Zander claimed not to have seen the rebels but allowed the Imperials to search their business. When Estoc noticed the absence of the starship ''[[StarScavenger]]'', Kordi claimed that the ship had departed on a salvage mission. In reality, Luke, [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]], and the [[battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|R0-GR]] had taken the ship on an accidental trip. Kordi and Zander then tried to push out the trash box but Estoc followed and pointed out that they had missed the [[Garbage compactor|trash incinerator]]. Kordi denied hiding rebels inside the box but dumped Leia and the contents inside the trash incinerator to escape Estoc's inquisitiveness. Satisfied, Estoc and her troopers left while Kordi and Zander rescued Leia.<ref name="Crossing Paths" />

Following a pursuit throughout the Wheel, Lieutenant Estoc and her stormtroopers cornered Kordi and Leia at a diner in the Middle Ring. While searching the diner, her troopers found Kordi hiding inside a pot. Unable to find Leia, Estoc ordered her troopers to send Kordi to a dreary prison planet. At that moment, Leia resurfaced from hiding inside an [[alien]]'s [[hair]] and used a plate to knock out the stormtroopers. Leia kicked Estoc out of the diner and the Imperial officer was sucked up by a passing garbage truck.<ref name="Crossing Paths" />

After reuniting with Zander, the Freemakers and Leia returned to the Freemaker Garage where they sent out the rebels' [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing]] into space. The [[starfighter]] was promptly destroyed by two Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. Shortly later, Estoc and her stormtroopers entered the Freemaker Garage to confront Kordi and Zander. When the siblings claimed that Leia had forced them to help at gunpoint, Estoc realised they were lying but left them alone because she believed that Luke and Leia were dead. Believing this would boost her career, she and the stormtroopers went to celebrate. In her absence, the Freemakers helped Leia and her brother Luke, who had just returned from an adventure to [[Felucia]], to escape by selling them a [[Ugly|Z-wing]] at a much reduced price.<ref name="Crossing Paths" />

===Attack of the ''Arrowhead''===
After the Freemakers [[Skirmish on Kashyyyk (Freemakers)|attacked]] Imperial forces on [[Kashyyyk]], Commander Estoc was assigned with picking up the surviving [[Unidentified AT-AT commander (Kashyyyk)|AT-AT commander]] and his men. Estoc then traveled with the AT-AT commander aboard the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] [[Desolator (Imperial-class)|''Desolator'']] to an Imperial repair facility near [[Alzoc III]], which was orbited by several Star Destroyers. Estoc complained that the Imperial bureaucracy had overbooked the repair yards.<ref name="APR">{{FreemakerAdventures|A Perilous Rescue}}</ref>

Shortly later, a squadron of [[X-wing starfighter]]s known as [[Blue Squadron]] exited hyperspace. Believing that the fighters did not pose a threat, Estoc ordered the Imperials to switch off the proximity alarms. Before Imperial forces could shoot down the fighters, the Freemakers' starship [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']] exited hyperspace. The AT-AT commander panicked, recognizing the ship that had devastated his forces on Kashyyyk.<ref name="APR" />

Zander used the ''Arrowhead''{{'s}} [[embersteel]] blade to slice off the bridge of a Star Destroyer before devastating the docked Star Destroyers. The AT-AT commander tried to convince Estoc to retreat but she ordered the Star Destroyers to open fire. The ''Arrowhead'' sliced through a second Star Destroyer before flying through several more warships, causing them to explode.<ref name="APR" />

Seeking to curry the Empire's favor, Estoc tried to trap the ''Arrowhead'' with her Star Destroyer's [[tractor beam projector]]. However, Zander got Blue Squadron's leader [[Lieutenant]] [[Colvett Valeria|Valeria]] to disable the projector, allowing the ''Arrowhead'' to slice the ''Desolator'' in half. While the AT-AT commander confirmed that the ''Arrowhead'' was indeed the ship his men had encountered, Estoc frowned in humiliation.<ref name="APR" />

==Personality and traits==
Lieutenant Estoc was a stern, no-nonsense Imperial officer. She was known for her inquisitive nature and could discern when someone was lying. Estoc eagerly led a manhunt for Princess Leia and Luke Skywalker. Despite her eagerness, she was bested by Leia in physical combat. Estoc also fell for a ruse when the Freemakers sent out the rebels' Y-wing as a decoy and mistakenly believed Luke and Leia had perished. An ambitious woman, she viewed the purported deaths of Luke and Leia as a chance to advance her career.<ref name="Crossing Paths" />

Estoc's pride and confidence in Imperial technological superiority and firepower led her to underestimate the threat posed by the ''Arrowhead'', a new powerful starship built by the Freemakers. Ultimately, this caused Estoc her Star Destroyer and pride.<ref name="APR" />
