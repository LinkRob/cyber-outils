An '''attack''' on the [[planet]] [[Atraken]] took place during the [[Clone Wars]] sometime between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], as both sides wanted control over the planet's rich natural mineral resources. Although the battle was ultimately a Republic victory, millions were [[Death|killed]] in the fighting, and the whole world was left uninhabitable.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the attack was mentioned in the [[Obi-Wan 4|fourth issue]] of the [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan (comic series)|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] mini-series, which was written by [[Christopher Cantwell]], pencilled by [[Madibek Musabekov]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[August 31]], [[2022]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />

The event originated in the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' continuity, where it first appeared in "[[Republic HoloNet News Core Edition 14:4:14]]," an article written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and illustrated by [[Joe Corroney]], published in the [[Star Wars Insider 67|sixty-seventh issue]] of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' on [[April 15]], [[2003]].<ref name="Insider 67 HN">{{InsiderCite|67|Republic HoloNet News Core Edition 14:4:14}}</ref> It was later first identified as the Battle of Atraken in the [[1992]] [[Roleplaying game]] book [[Planets of the Galaxy, Volume Two]].<ref name="PotG">''[[Planets of the Galaxy, Volume Two]]''</ref>
