'''Defender sporting blaster pistols''',<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> also known simply as a '''Defender Pistol''',<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> were a type of [[blaster pistol]] favored and used as a sidearm by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. She first learned to shoot the Defender as a pre-teen, and displayed a rare talent for shooting. She stunned a mutinous [[Poe Dameron]] with one aboard the ''[[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|Raddus]]''.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> [[Amilyn Holdo]], whose command Dameron had overthrown, carried a Defender pistol.<ref name="SWGOH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>

The Defender was used for small game hunting and self defense. The blasters were popular with nobility and aristocrats due to its lightweight design, ease of disassembly and reassembly, and its ability to be concealed. When broken down into its three components, the blasters could pass through most security scans undetected.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Models of Defender sporting blaster pistols included the [[Satine's Lament]] and the [[Defender-5 sporting blaster]], which were both made by the [[Drearian Defense Conglomerate]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /><ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
