The '''442nd Siege Battalion''' was a [[clone trooper]] [[battalion]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], that served during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Troopers of the battalion were equipped with [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] that featured green markings, and utilized vehicles such as the [[Infantry Support Platform]] and [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]. Adept at besieging enemy strongholds, they served in the [[Battle of Abrion Bridge]] around [[22 BBY]] and, during the final [[Standard hour|hours]] of the war, were [[Battle of Cato Neimoidia|deployed]] to the [[Trade Federation]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Cato Neimoidia]] in [[19 BBY]].

==Description==
The 442nd Siege Battalion was an [[Tank|armor]] [[battalion]] of [[clone trooper]]s in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]],<ref name="Hasbro" /> composed of four [[Company|companies]]<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'' establishes that a [[battalion]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] is made up of four [[Company|companies]].</ref> with 144 [[trooper]]s in each.<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] establishes that a [[company]] in the Grand [[Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] is composed of 144 [[clone trooper]]s.</ref> [[Clone trooper officer]]s served in the unit. Members of the 442nd sported [[phase I clone trooper armor]],<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> and later [[phase II clone trooper armor]], decorated with bright [[Color|green]] markings. They were typically equipped with [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s. Proficient in besieging enemy fortifications, they were equipped with vehicles such as [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s and [[Infantry Support Platform]]s that also featured green-colored markings.<ref name="Hasbro" />

==History==
The 442nd Siege Battalion was created to serve the Grand Army of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], against the secessionist [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Hasbro" /> The unit fought alongside the [[212th Attack Battalion]] and [[Mekedrix's company]] during the [[Battle of Abrion Bridge]]<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> around [[22 BBY]].<ref name="22 BBY Date">The [[Obi-Wan 3|''Obi-Wan'' 3]] flashbacks take place shortly after the [[Defense of Rishi Station]], which appears in "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]]." {{TCWcanondates|105}} Therefore ''Obi-Wan'' 3, including the [[Battle of Abrion Bridge]], also takes place around 22 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[19 BBY]], they were deployed to the [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]] in [[Battle of Cato Neimoidia|battle]] against the Separatist forces there.<ref name="EE web">{{WebCite|url=https://www.entertainmentearth.com/prodinfo.asp?number=HSC0495|text=Star Wars Black Series Clone Troopers of Order 66|date=2016-10-06|archivedate=20161006030615}}</ref> Fighting alongside [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]], who was dispatched to coordinate the 442nd's siege,<ref name="TCW encyclopedia" /> and his [[wingmate]] [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Jag]],<ref name="AE">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> the battalion secured a victory for the Republic. Afterward,<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> the battalion took part in the [[Great Jedi Purge|execution]] of [[Order 66]].<ref name="Hasbro" />

==Behind the scenes==
In [[canon]], the 442nd Siege Battalion was first pictured through the workbook ''[[Star Wars Workbook: Kindergarten Math Skills]]''<ref name="Kingdergarten">''[[Star Wars Workbook: Kindergarten Math Skills]]''</ref> and then identified by [[Hasbro]] via a 6-inch figure of a 442nd clone trooper was released in late [[2016]] with three other troopers: one from the [[212th Attack Battalion]], one from the [[501st Legion]], and a [[Imperial shock trooper|clone shock trooper]]. The set was originally released via Entertainment Earth. The 442nd [[clone trooper armor]] coloring is an exact recolor of the 212th Attack Battalion's markings.<ref name="EE web" /> Hasbro previously released a version of the trooper in the ''[[Star Wars: The Saga Collection]]'' line; however, this falls under the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Saga Collection">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Saga Collection|pack=#057: Clone Trooper (442nd Siege Battalion)|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/tsc057clone442.asp}}</ref>
