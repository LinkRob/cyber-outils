'''Club Lao Che''' was a [[cantina]] in [[the galaxy]]. [[Clone Force 99]] joined the [[pirate]] [[Phee Genoa]] on a mission to the club to meet with the criminal [[Lanse Crowder]].

==Description==
Club Lao Che was a [[cantina]] that was commonly frequented by a variety of patrons. The club was dark inside with many bright lighting elements, including three signs in different [[color]]s spelled out the establishment's name in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="Pabu" />

==History==
During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|213}}</ref> [[Omega]] and [[Phee Genoa]] met up with the criminal [[Lanse Crowder]] and his associates at the [[cantina]] to purchase a [[Unidentified artifact (Phee Genoa)|tree-shaped artifact]]. When Crowder insisted that they [[Galactic Credit Standard|pay]] first, Genoa countered by insisting on checking that it was not a "knock-off." When Crowder noticed Omega and asked if she works alone, Genoa explained that she was teaching Omega the "art of negotiation." Crowder offered drinks but Genoa refused, dismissing it as [[poison]]. Genoa admitted that he was impressed but secretly released a [[kouhun]] under the table.<ref name="Pabu" />

[[File:Club_Lao_Che_interior.png|thumb|left|280px|The interior of the club]]
Meanwhile, Omega's fellow [[Clone Force 99]] member [[Hunter]] sensed movement. Omega responded that Genoa was not a [[pirate]] but a liberator of ancient wonders. Genoa agreed while Hunter played a game of [[sabacc]] with a [[Bith]], and proceeded to grab the Bith's hands before they could reach for a card. Before the kouhun could pierce Genoa's neck with its stinger, however, Hunter [[Death|impaled]] the creature against the wall with his [[knife]]. Crowder's helmeted henchmen raised their [[blaster]]s at Hunter. Crowder believed that he has the upper hand but Genoa revealed that Hunter was not her sole "muscle."<ref name="Pabu" />

At that, [[Wrecker]], another member of Clone Force 99, made his presence known and took out several of Crowder's henchmen, triggering a gunfight. Omega jointed the gunfight and fired her [[Omega's Zygerrian energy bow|energy bow]] at Crowder and his men, before grabbing the artifact and shooting down the hanging ceiling lamp over Chowder. Hunter requested pick-up from fellow team member [[Tech]], who readied the [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Marauder'']] away from the club. Genoa and the clones made a getaway by using [[Smoke grenade|smoke bombs]] to disorientate Crowder and his men. They escaped the cantina and fled aboard the parked ''Marauder''.<ref name="Pabu" />

==Behind the scenes==
The club is named after [[w:c:indianajones:Lao Che|Lao Che]], the opening antagonist from the [[Lucasfilm]] production ''[[Wikipedia:Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom|Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom]]''. The name pays back a ''Star Wars'' reference in the movie, where the opening scene takes place in an establishment called [[w:c:indianajones:Club Obi Wan|Club Obi Wan]],<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=pabu-trivia-gallery|text="Pabu" Trivia Gallery &#124; The Bad Batch|quote=The opening scene where Phee tries to make a deal with Crowder for a precious artifact is heavily inspired by the opening sequence of Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom. The Aurebesh signs outside the club reads "Club Lao Che," which pays back the Star Wars reference in Temple of Doom, where the club was called "Club Obi Wan."}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> named after [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] from the [[Star Wars saga|''Star Wars'' saga]].<ref>{{D23|url=things-you-didnt-know-about-indiana-jones-and-the-temple-of-doom|text=7 Things You Didn't Know About ''Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom''|author=Seastrom, Lucas|date=2019-05-23}} {{C|"Club Obi Wan" is a tribute to the Jedi Master.''' No, you aren't seeing things. That famous Shanghai nightclub at the beginning of ''Temple of Doom'' really is named "Club Obi Wan" after the Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi from the ''Star Wars'' saga.}}</ref> The scene in the [[cantina]] is heavily similar to the opening of the film,<ref name="Trivia" /> in which the [[w:c:indianajones:Indiana Jones|titular character]] and Lao Che negotiate over an artifact, the negotiations turn violent, the titular character has a [[poison]]ed [[Alcohol|drink]] placed in front of him and reveals that he secretly brought back-up; much like what happens between [[Lanse Crowder]] and [[Phee Genoa]] in this episode.<ref name="SlashFilm">{{WebCite|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/1228346/star-wars-the-bad-batch-season-2-episode-13-review-a-major-confrontation|text=Star Wars: The Bad Batch Brings More Indiana Jones References And Sets Up A Major Confrontation/|author=Young, Brian|date=2023-03-15|quote=This episode opens up with an exchange patterned after the one at the beginning of "Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom" inside the Club Obi-Wan.
