'''Chay Lelando''', also known as "'''Millennium Falcon Man'''," was a<ref name="Blog" /> [[Human/Legends|Human]]<ref name="Video" /> [[Gender/Legends|male]].<ref name="Blog" /> At some point, Lelando was present on<ref name="Video" /> the [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] and inspected a [[YT-1300 light freighter/Legends|YT-1300 light freighter]] using a [[Datapad/Legends|datapad]]. They congratulated an individual on their ownership of the [[Light freighter/Legends|light freighter]] and remarked that the [[Freighter/Legends|ship]] was made famous by<ref name="Video" /> the [[Smuggler/Legends|smuggler]]<ref name="IV" /> [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo's]] [[Millennium Falcon/Legends|''Millennium Falcon'']].<ref name="Video" />

Lelando had [[Color/Legends|brown]] [[Hair/Legends|hair]], brown [[Eye/Legends|eye]]s, and light skin. While on Tatooine, Lelando wore a brown [[Clothing/Legends|vest]] over a tan-colored long sleeved [[shirt]] with [[Goggles/Legends|goggles]] around their neck. They also wore tan-colored pants with a brown [[Belt/Legends|belt]] and brown [[Boot/Legends|boots]].<ref name="Video" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I had a great time making it|Keeper of the Holocron Leland Chee on portraying Chay Lelando in the trailer|<ref name="Blog" />}}
[[File:Holocron-screenshot.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Chay Lelando's entry in the Holocron continuity database as of 2012]]
Chay Lelando was portrayed by Keeper of the Holocron [[Leland Chee]] in a trailer released on [[October 23]], [[2010]] promoting the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Millennium Falcon: A 3-D Owner's Guide]]'',<ref name="Blog" /> which was authored by [[Ryder Windham]] and released on [[September 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Amazon">{{Amazon|url=Star-Wars-Millennium-Falcon-Owners/dp/0545210380|text=Star Wars: Millennium Falcon- A 3-D Owner's Guide}}</ref> While unidentified within the trailer, the character was subsequently identified via a post on Chee's blog on [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 24]] where he expressed having a great time making the video.<ref name="Blog" /> In a StarWars.com article about Leland Chee's [[Holocron continuity database]], the entry for Lelando was utilized to display the database's appearance, mentioning Lelando's named in [[Aurebesh/Legends|Aurebesh]] [[Writing/Legends|text]].<ref name="Holocron">{{SW|url=news/what-is-the-holocron|text=What is the Holocron?}}</ref>
