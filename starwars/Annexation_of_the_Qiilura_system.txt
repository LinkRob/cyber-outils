The '''annexation of the Qiilura system''' was an operation launched by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="MM" /> in [[20 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> to take over the [[Qiilura system]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="MM" /> The location of [[Qiilura]] thus became a battleground.<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep405|text="Mercy Mission" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}} {{C|Relationships section}}</ref> To try to block the annexation attempt, the [[Galactic Republic]] deployed an [[Wolffe's Acclamator-class assault ship|''Acclamator''-class transgalactic military assault ship]], which carried forces from the [[104th Battalion]] under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]]<ref name="MM" /> to the battle on Qiilura.<ref name="Episode Guide" />

However, because a series of devastating [[groundquake]]s rocked the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Aleen]], the [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|Acclamator]], joined by a trio of [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigates]], and Wolffe's [[clone trooper]]s&mdash;joined by [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] and [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]]&mdash;were redirected to perform a desperately needed [[Mission to Aleen|relief mission]] on Aleen. The [[battalion]] was under orders to unite with<ref name="MM" /> its [[Jedi General]],<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> [[Plo Koon]], once the mission was complete and after rendezvousing with<ref name="MM" /> [[Jedi]] [[General]]<ref name="Nomad Droids">{{TCW|Nomad Droids}}</ref> [[Adi Gallia]],<ref name="MM" /> who was also involved in the mercy mission,<ref name="Build X-Wing 47">{{BuildXWingCite|47|Starfighter Aces}}</ref> aboard her [[Adi Gallia's flagship|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="Nomad Droids">{{TCW|Nomad Droids}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The annexation of the Qiilura system was mentioned within the opening newsreel of "[[Mercy Mission (episode)|Mercy Mission]],"<ref name="MM" /> the fifth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which first aired on [[October 7]], [[2011]].<ref name="The Clone Wars Episode Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref>
