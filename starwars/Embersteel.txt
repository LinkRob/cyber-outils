'''Embersteel''' was a strong [[metal]] that could be forged into [[starship]] components. The mystical starship known as the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']] had embersteel blades.<ref name="TTOAN">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Tower of Alistan Nor}}</ref> The wings of the [[H-2 executive shuttle|''Theta''-class H-2 executive star shuttle]] were also made from embersteel.<ref name="TEB">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Embersteel Blade}}</ref>
