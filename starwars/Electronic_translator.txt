'''Electronic translators''' were devices that automatically [[Translator|translated]] between different [[language]]s, such as between [[Alien|non-human]] [[language]]s and [[Galactic Basic Standard]].

Electronic translators included the [[translating vocoder]]s that converted alien languages into speech, such as [[vocoder mask]]s<ref name="r1vg">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> or [[translator collar]]s.<ref name="Battle Scars EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/battle-scars-episode-guide|text="Battle Scars" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref> Other devices, such as [[Data visor|translation visors]], rendered the translation as [[Aurebesh]] text.<ref name="ADE">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref>

Most members of such species as [[Kyuzo]],<ref name="AotG">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Anomid]]s, [[Gigoran]]s,<ref name="r1vg" /> and [[Ithorian]]s<ref name="F&E">{{TCW|Friends and Enemies}}</ref> relied on such translation devices or [[Translator|interpreter]] [[droid]]s when talking with outsiders.
