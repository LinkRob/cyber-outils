'''Ambrosia''' was an entity rumored to be found on [[Sophros|Planet X]], a mysterious [[planet]] thought by many to have been a myth.<ref name="Quest" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while on the planet [[Batuu]], the [[pilot]] [[Sky Graf]] told [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] rumors they had heard about Planet X, including that it had fields of incredible ambrosia. In response, the [[hyperspace]] [[prospector]] [[Dass Leffbruk]]&mdash;who had previously been to Planet X&mdash;refuted the rumors.<ref name="Quest" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ambrosia was mentioned in the [[2023]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] as part of the [[Star Wars: The High Republic]] multimedia project's<ref name="Quest" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2023}}</ref> In [[Wikipedia:Greek mythology|Greek]] and [[Wikipedia:Roman mythology|Roman mythology]], [[Wikipedia:Ambrosia|ambrosia]] was the food of the gods.<ref name="Ambrosia">{{WebCite|url=https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/ambrosia|text=ambrosia|work=[https://dictionary.cambridge.org/ Cambridge Dictionary]|archivedate=20231221104019}}</ref>
