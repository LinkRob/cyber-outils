A '''battle''' was fought on [[Blacktar Cyst]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] by [[3 ABY]]. Both the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] and the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] took part in the fighting, where the 61st [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] [[Pol Andrissus]] received scarring injuries that disfigured his face and gave him a stutter. [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] started a pair of arguments with [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]] and the [[soldier]] [[Brand]] respectively on Blacktar Cyst.

During his visit to the Alliance [[Echo Base|base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]] in [[3 ABY]], Evon and his associates were told by [[General]] [[Philap Bygar]] that the 61st did not deserve to be deployed to Blacktar Cyst. The [[New Republic Intelligence]] [[agent]] [[Caern Adan]] regarded the 204th's actions at Blacktar Cyst as among the main reasons he believed the [[fighter wing]] had to be stopped. On the [[planet]] [[Troithe]], a member of the 61st recounted the fighting on Blacktar Cyst in front of [[Yrica Quell]], an ex-member of the 204th. Members of the 204th later recalled Blacktar Cyst over a [[fire]] at Troithe's [[Taa Complex]].

==The battle==
{{Quote|Howl's a genius. You won that argument on Blacktar Cyst.|Hazram Namir, to Brand|<ref name="TC" />}}
[[File:204thLegion-TIEFighter1.png|thumb|left|250px|The 204th Imperial Fighter Wing (unit pictured) was deployed to Blacktar Cyst.]]
By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY" /> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="TC" /> forces from each side fought a battle on [[Blacktar Cyst]], where both the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] and the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] were deployed.<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> During the battle, the [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldier]] [[Pol Andrissus]] was injured by shrapnel, that scarred and disfigured his face. Some of the shrapnel reached his brain, giving the man a stutter, and many of his comrades considered his "good looks" to have been taken away.<ref name="TC" />

While on Blacktar Cyst, [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] of the 61st lost an argument with fellow [[soldier]] [[Brand]] on whether their [[commanding officer]], [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]], nicknamed Howl, was a genius or not. Namir also begun an ongoing argument with Howl on whether the first sergeant underestimated his men and their motivation to fight.<ref name="TC" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|If we don't stop them there, there will be another Nacronis. Or another Blacktar Cyst.|Caern Adan, to [[IT-O]], on the 204th|<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>}}
[[File:YricaQuell-TIEFighter1.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Yrica Quell (pictured) fought at Blacktar Cyst while she was part of the 204th and later reflected on it during her time as part of Alphabet Squadron.]]
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY" /> Howl and the former Imperial [[Governor]] [[Everi Chalis]] were invited to the [[Alliance High Command]]'s [[Echo Base|secret base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]], bringing Namir and two other soldiers as escort. The Alliance [[General]] [[Philap Bygar]] greeted the party and voiced sentiment that Twilight Company did not deserve to have been sent to Blacktar Cyst or [[Praktin]].<ref name="TC" />

The Alliance eventually became the [[New Republic]] following the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> and [[Yrica Quell]], a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the 204th<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> who had fought at Blacktar Cyst,<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> defected to the New Republic. The [[New Republic Intelligence]] [[agent]] [[Caern Adan]] soon recruited Quell to [[Hunt for Shadow Wing|hunt]] the 204th, citing Blacktar Cyst among the [[fighter wing]]'s deployments prior to Endor. Adan viewed the 204th's actions as disastrous for the other side, listing it alongside the fighter wing's [[Destruction of Nacronis|genocide]] of the [[planet]] [[Nacronis]]'s civilization as primary reasons why the New Republic desperately needed to stop 204th.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

Around [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> Quell and her [[squadron]] in the New Republic, [[Alphabet Squadron]], traded stories with members of the 61st on the planet [[Troithe]]. When one [[New Republic soldier]] recounted her side of Blacktar Cyst, Quell noticed the details were different to what she remembered, but chose not to admit she had taken part with the 204th. Some time later, [[Soran Keize]], the 204th commanding [[Imperial officer|officer]], joined some 204th pilots in telling stories, including the battle of Blacktar Cyst, around a [[fire]] at Troithe's [[Taa Complex]] greenhouse.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle of Blacktar Cyst was first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="TC" />
