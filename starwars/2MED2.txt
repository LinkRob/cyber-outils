'''2MED2''', spelled '''Toomedtoo''', was a [[medical droid]] who served the [[Resistance]] during the [[Cold War]].

==Biography==
In [[31 ABY]] or [[32 ABY]],<ref name="Year" /> [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] of the Resistance was captured by the [[First Order]]. Afraid that Ackbar would be executed, the Resistance made a team of fighters led by [[Captain]] [[Hoff (Resistance captain)|Hoff]] who were to figure out where Ackbar was being held. The team, at some point captured a First Order droid named [[0-MR1|O-MR1]], who they believed knew the location of Ackbar. On the way back to the Resistance base, the team's ship crashed on [[Taul]] and the only survivors of the crash were 2MED2, O-MR1, the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]], the [[construction droid]] [[CO-34]], and the two [[security droid]]s [[PZ-99]] and [[VL-44]].<ref name="The Phantom Limb" />

The group of droids decided to work together to get home. A small amount of time later, the droids picked up a signal from a First Order vessel. On their way to the vessel, the droids were attacked by [[spice spider]]s. PZ-99 sacrificed himself to give the others time to escape, by drawing the spiders to him. Not much later, the remaining droids were attacked by [[Thidaxx]] and [[can-cell]]. 2MED2 was destroyed in the attack, along with CO-34 and VL-44.<ref name="The Phantom Limb" />
