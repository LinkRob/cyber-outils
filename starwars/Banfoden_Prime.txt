'''Banfoden Prime''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="shield" /> in the [[Banfoden Prime system]], which was located in the [[Alchenaut sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was situated in grid square L-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Between around [[389 BBY]] and around [[379 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Shield of the Jedi]]" establishes that [[Rooper Nitani]] is around eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old during its events. In ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of to [[382 BBY]], Nitani is fifteen years old. Therefore, "Shield of the Jedi" must take place around [[379 BBY]]. The uprising on Banfoden Prime took place before the events of "Shield of the Jedi", and after Nitani became a [[Padawan]], which "Shield of the Jedi" establishes as having been ten years before its events.</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] settled an [[Uprising on Banfoden Prime|uprising]] that occurred there. Shortly before her [[Rooper Nitani's Jedi Trials|Jedi Trials]]<ref name="shield" /> around 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Nitani remembered settling the uprising on Banfoden Prime while thinking about the challenges she had faced and the lessons she had learned in the past few [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="shield" />

==Behind the scenes==
Banfoden Prime was first mentioned in the short story "[[Shield of the Jedi]]," written by [[George Mann]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="shield" />
