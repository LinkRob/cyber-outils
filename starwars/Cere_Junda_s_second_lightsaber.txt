Sometime during the five [[Standard year|years]] that [[Cere Junda]] was separated from the [[Stinger Mantis crew|''Stinger Mantis'' crew]], she constructed a new [[Color|blue]]-bladed '''lightsaber''' to replace her [[Cere Junda's lightsaber|previous]] one<ref name="Survivor" /> from which she'd sold the [[kyber crystal]] to help pay off [[Greez Dritus]]' debts with the [[Haxion Brood]] and given the hilt to [[Cal Kestis]] for him to create a [[Cal Kestis' second lightsaber|new lightsaber]] with its parts.<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

Junda used this lightsaber to fight the Fifth Brother on [[Murkhana]].<ref name="Battle Scars" />

She carried this [[lightsaber]] while helping the secret network known as the [[Hidden Path]], which helped to smuggle [[Jedi]], other [[Force-sensitive]]s, and those who helped them, to safety from the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Survivor" />

Junda would use this saber in a [[Lightsaber combat|duel]] with the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on the [[desert]] [[moon]] [[Jedha]], while [[Raid on the Archive|attempting]] to defend her [[The Archive|Archive]]. This battle would result in Junda's [[death]]. The lightsaber would be recovered by Cal however, along with Junda's body, and would be laid to rest alongside her on the hidden world [[Tanalorr]].<ref name="Survivor" />
