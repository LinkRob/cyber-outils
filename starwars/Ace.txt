An '''ace''' was a [[starfighter]] [[pilot]] that had been credited with a certain number of [[Death|kills]] (five) in [[Starfighter combat|flight combat]]. Pilots who exceeded the minimum number of kills required for ace status could also become double aces (such as [[Dex Tiree]], with thirteen kills), triple aces (such as [[Biggs Darklighter]], with sixteen kills), or even quadruple aces (such as [[Jon Vander]], [[Garven Dreis]], and [[Antoc Merrick]] with upwards of twenty-two kills).<ref name="Grounded">{{StoryCite|story=Grounded|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> The [[Jedi Knight]] [[Taradon]] was an ace.<ref name="Nexus">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref> The [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] was a mega-ace.<ref name="Card Trader">{{Topps|set=2020 Topps Women of Star Wars|cardname=#86: Tallie Lintra|link=https://www.tcdb.com//ViewCard.cfm/sid/216003/cid/14374579/2020-Topps-Women-of-Star-Wars-86-Tallie-Lintra|nobackup=1}}</ref>
