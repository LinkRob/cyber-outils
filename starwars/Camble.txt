'''Camble''' was an individual who left a stash including [[sodium]]-laden [[fuel]] in a locked container under a set of scaffolding in the corner of a [[hangar]] on [[Renpalli Station]], a [[space station]] [[orbit]]ing the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] discovered the stash and took the fuel.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}
