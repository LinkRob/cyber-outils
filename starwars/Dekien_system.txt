The '''Dekien system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]]. It contained the [[planet]] [[Dekien]], which was occupied by the [[Empire (Rei'izu)|Empire]] and visited by the [[Sith]] known as [[The Ronin]] in the aftermath of his organization's [[Sith rebellion|rebellion]].<ref name="Ronin" />
