The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''46 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3231 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''27 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7931''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[Greater Seswenna raids]] end. {{Circa}}<ref name="Raids Date">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] establishes that [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] was eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old when [[Q'anah (pirate)|Q'anah]] [[Death|died]] and fifty years old during its present date events, which occur in [[14 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, [[Greater Seswenna raids]], including Q'anah's death, must take place around 46 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>

*[[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Naberrie]] on [[Naboo]].<ref name="Timelines" />
*[[Sabé|Tsabin]] on Naboo. {{Circa}}<ref name="Age">''[[Queen's Shadow]]'' establishes that [[Sabé]] and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] are the same age. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the Padmé's [[Reproduction|birth]] to 46 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. Therefore, Sabé must have been born around 46 BBY.</ref>
*[[Davits Draven]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Davits">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' establishes Davits Draven as fourty-six [[standard year]]s old during the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]]. Therefore, Draven must have been born around  46 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>
