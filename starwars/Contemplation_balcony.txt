The '''contemplation balcony''' was an exterior platform situated halfway up the [[Palace spire|Temple Spire]], the main spire<ref name="D:JL script" /> of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The location was used by [[Jedi|members]] of the [[Jedi Order]] for solitary [[meditation]] and reflection.<ref name="D:JL" /> In [[88 BBY]],<ref name="Dates">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Dooku]] and [[Sifo-Dyas]] sneaking into the [[Archive of Forbidden Artifacts|Bogan Collection]] to [[88 BBY]]. As the flashback in which [[Lene Kostana]] and Dooku meet on the contemplation balcony takes place the same [[Standard year|year]], their meeting must have occurred in 88 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] as well.</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Lene Kostana]] awaited the return of her [[convor]], [[Ferana]], upon the balcony. Shortly thereafter, Kostana and [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Dooku]] had an altercation on the balcony as Dooku suspected Kostana was secretly a [[Sith]]. Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] broke up the [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] after Dooku became too aggressive and helped the Initiate see that he was incorrect in his accusations toward Kostana.<ref name="D:JL" />

Dooku later told his [[House Serenno|sister]], [[Jenza]], about the events in a [[holo-letter]].<ref name="D:JL" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="DJL-JournalsDate">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku gave several [[Datacard|data disks]] containing his messages to Jenza to his recently recruited [[Sith assassin]]&mdash;[[Asajj Ventress]]&mdash;for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into his [[Gender|sister's]] location, and she listened to the holo-disk that mentioned the contemplation balcony.<ref name="D:JL" />

==Behind the scenes==
The contemplation balcony appeared in a flashback sequence in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="D:JL" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin-Download">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The capitalization of the contemplation balcony was confirmed by the audio drama's [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]]<ref name="D:JL script" /> on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin-Download" />
