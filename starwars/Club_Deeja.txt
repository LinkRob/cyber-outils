'''Club Deeja''' was an entertainment establishment in the [[city]] of [[Keren]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the club was owned by [[Mul Sanaka]] and protected by a number of [[Besalisk]] guards, including [[Snes]]. [[Melodic Order]], a cloister of [[Alderaanian]]s, performed via [[hologram]] at the club. [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] traveled to Club Deeja after the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]] in order to rescue the Alderaanians there from the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Leia 2" />
