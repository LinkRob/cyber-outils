'''Bacta bulbs''' were a type of medical device which made use of [[bacta]]. The [[Resistance]] [[Doctor]] [[Harter Kalonia]] carried bacta bulbs in her medical field kit while station in the [[Resistance base]] on the [[planet]] [[D'Qar]] around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]. As such she always had them and other equipment useful for treating [[blaster]] burns within easy reach.<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bacta bulbs were first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in [[2015]].
