'''1211''' was a level of<ref name="MOE" /> [[Imperial City]],<ref name="Rebellion">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref> the [[ecumenopolis]] that covered the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[TC-series protocol droid]] [[TC-99]] considered 1211 to be a den of iniquity so bad that even the electronic thought of it made his [[heuristic processor]] overheat, meaning the less said about the level the better. While visiting [[Level 1978]] of Imperial City<ref name="MOE" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Master of Evil]]'' begin during the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 5]] and then spans to before the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 6]], which corresponds to [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> TC-99 thought about how [[Level 1313]] and 1211 were worse levels then Level 1978, although the [[droid]] still held a low opinion of it.<ref name="MOE" />

==Behind the scenes==
1211 was mentioned during a flashback in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Master of Evil]]'', which was written by [[Adam Christopher]].<ref name="MOE" />
