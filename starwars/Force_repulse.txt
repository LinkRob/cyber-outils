'''Force repulse''',<ref name="TCW Complete Season Four">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Four|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Four]]</ref> also known as '''Force pulse''',<ref name="Witches of the Mist Episode Guide" /> was a [[Telekinesis|telekinetic]] [[Force power|ability]] of [[the Force]] used in an offensive or defensive manner.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

==Description==
An advanced version of the [[Force push]], a user could fire Force energy from their center like a wave, essentially creating a pulse of telekinetically-derived force that could stagger, shove or knock over anyone within the radius of the attack. The resulting wave could be so powerful that it could manifest visually unlike a normal Force push, creating a ring of displaced air resembling the shockwave of a sonic boom. The technique could be used to push targets away from the user,<ref name="Witches of the Mist">{{TCW|Witches of the Mist}}</ref> or to pull targets towards the user.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> It could be used by practitioners of both the [[Light side of the Force|light]]<ref name="Shadow Warrior" /> and [[Dark side of the Force|dark]] sides of the Force.<ref name="Witches of the Mist" />

==Users==
In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="WotM canon date">{{TCWcanondates|314}}</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]], [[Savage Opress]] betrayed his [[Sith Master]] [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]] aboard the [[Dooku's Providence-class carrier/destroyer|latter's]] [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]]. Seeking an escape from the [[dreadnought]], Opress headed to a [[hangar]] where his [[Punworcca 116-class interstellar sloop|solar sailer]] was waiting. However, he was intercepted by numerous [[battle droid]]s, who surrounded the [[Sith assassin]] and began firing on him. The droids quickly overwhelmed Opress, striking him with blaster fire multiple times. Calling on the Force, Savage used a pulse of Force energy to knock the gathered battle droids off their feet, allowing him to escape.<ref name="Witches of the Mist" />

Sometime later, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] [[Skirmish on Naboo|confronted]] [[Count]] Dooku in [[Rish Loo's laboratory]] on [[Naboo]]. The [[Jedi]] engaged in a [[Lightsaber combat|duel]] with the [[Sith Lord]]. During the fight, Tyranus had his four [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s attack Skywalker. The four droids shocked Skywalker simultaneously using their [[electrostaff]]s, forcing the Jedi Knight to the ground. Determined to defeat Dooku, Anakin used a Force repulse to throw the MagnaGuards of him, but was subsequently defeated by the [[Count of Serenno]].<ref name="Shadow Warrior" /> Skywalker could also create an inward pulse of Force energy, dragging opponents towards himself rather than away.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

[[Darth]] Vader used the ability to blast dozens of [[Lylek]]s on [[Ryloth]].<ref name="LotS" />

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFO Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] used the technique during his [[secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order]]. He displayed the ability to both push and pull targets with his Force repulses. During a [[Liberation on Kashyyyk|mission to Kashyyyk]], Kestis dueled the [[Ninth Sister]] of the [[Inquisitorius]] atop the [[Origin Tree]] of [[Kashyyyk]]. The Ninth Sister used Force pulses to upturn the surface of the tree, hoping to damage Kestis in the process. Later, during the [[Mission to Nur]], Kestis fought the [[Second Sister]] within the [[Fortress Inquisitorius]]. Similarly to the Ninth Sister, the Second Sister used Force pulses in an attempt to knock Kestis off his feet.<ref name="Fallen Order" /> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="JS Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> fallen Jedi Knight [[Dagan Gera]] used force repulse to great effect during Cal Kestis' [[Battle at the Koboh Observatory|third encounter]] with him while projecting a [[Force vision|force hallucination]].<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

[[Luke Skywalker]] was also capable of creating Force repulses.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
