In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref> [[crime lord]], and [[Capo]] of [[Crimson Dawn]], [[Dryden Vos]] had an<ref name="Official Guide" /> '''ancient navi-computer dataplaque''',<ref name="Card Trader">{{SWCT|set=Solo: A Star Wars Story - Gear & Gadgets|cardname=Ancient Navi-Computer Dataplaque}}</ref> that contained glyphs that allegedly pinpointed the location of the long lost ''[[Queen of Ranroon]]'', onboard his [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]''.<ref name="Official Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The legend of the ''Queen of Ranroon'' first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity novels [[Han Solo's Revenge]] & [[Han Solo and the Lost Legacy]] by [[Brian Daley]]. In the novels, the vessel was a treasure ship of the powerful [[Pre-Republic era|pre-Republic]] [[Warlord/Legends|warlord]], [[Xim/Legends|Xim the Despot]]. It was said to have vanished while loaded with a vast fortune, inspiring searches by treasure hunters for millennia.
