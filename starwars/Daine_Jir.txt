'''Daine Jir'''<ref name="Scum">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served in the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] as an [[Imperial officer]]. Jir served under the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] aboard the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]'' during the [[Secret mission to Tatooine|hunt]] for the [[Battle of Scarif|stolen]] [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[Death Star plans|plans]]<ref name="Episode IV" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Episode IV Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref>

After the ''Devastator'' managed to track down the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' above [[Tatooine]]. [[Darth]] Vader believed that [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] had the plans for the [[Death Star]]. After a [[Space warfare|space battle]], the ''Tantive IV'' was boarded by the Empire's [[501st Legion]] where they [[Death|killed]] and captured the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] crew. After Organa was captured, Jir spoke with Vader suggesting that holding her was dangerous and could create sympathy for the rebellion in the [[Imperial Senate]], though Vader rebutted that Jir should not be concerned.<ref name="Episode IV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Daine Jir first appeared in the [[1977]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], where he was played uncredited by [[Al Lampert]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> Jir was first identified in the [[2018]] [[canon]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''.<ref name="Scum" /> The character's name is likely a play on the word "danger," inspired by Jir's line: "''Holding her is dangerous''."<ref name="TEF:WWitIM">{{InsiderCite|96|The Empire's Finest: Who's Who in the Imperial Military}}</ref>

The [[2020]] [[Disney Die-Cut Classics]] [[Star Wars: A New Hope (Disney Die-Cut Classics)|adaptation]] of ''A New Hope'' by [[Ryder Windham]] erroneously describes Jir and Vader as being the ones who discover Organa on the ''Tantive IV''<ref name="Die-Cut">[[Star Wars: A New Hope (Disney Die-Cut Classics)|''Star Wars: A New Hope'' die-cut children's book]]</ref> rather than the stormtroopers as depicted in the film.<ref name="Episode IV" />
