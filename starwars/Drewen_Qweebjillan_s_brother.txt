A [[Segredo]] [[Gender|male]] was the '''brother''' of<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 2" /> [[Drewen Qweebjillan]].<ref name="Saber for Hire 1">[[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 1|''The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire'' 1]]</ref> When [[Pirate|marauders]] attacked their village, Drewen and his brother were among those strong enough to work who captured and taken aboard the raiders' [[starship]]. During their [[time]] aboard the ship, Drewen's brother would tell him stories about the [[Jedi]]. When the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] caused starships to be thrown out of [[hyperspace]], those captured aboard the vessel were able to fight back. Drewen's brother got him into an [[escape pod]], saving his life, but did not make it out alive himself.<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 2" />
