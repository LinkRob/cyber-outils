'''Ellé''', originally named '''Elleen''', was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as an [[aide]] to [[Governor of Naboo]] [[Sio Bibble]]. During the early stages of the [[Clone Wars]], she became one of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaidens]], upon which she changed her name.<ref name="Queen's Hope" />

She was interviewed by Amidala's other [[handmaiden]] [[Dormé]], along with [[Moteé]], who also became Amidala's handmaiden. The two were hired to replace [[Cordé]] and [[Versé]],<ref name="Queen's Hope" /> handmaidens who had been killed during an [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|assassination attempt]] on the senator's life.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>

Ellé helped when [[Sabé]], Amidala's decoy, filled in for Amidala, shortly after the outbreak of the war. However Sabé noted that Amidala seemed to be becoming more isolated and spending less time with her staff and friends. Ellé and Moteé took on a more traditional role of a handmaiden, serving Amidala but not working as friends and confidants, due to Amidala's [[Wedding of Anakin Skywalker and Padmé Amidala|secret marriage]] to [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]. Dormé recommend telling Ellé and Moteé about the [[marriage]].<ref name="Queen's Hope" />
