'''Danassic''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual who<ref name="Empire's End" /> by [[5 ABY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> was [[Marriage|married]] to [[Grand Moff]] [[Randd]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. As her husband was not prone to humor, Danassic told him that she believed he only laughed once or twice a [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Behind the scenes==
Danassic was mentioned in the [[2017]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the third installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Empire's End" />
