{{Quote|My old friend Freerago used to run a satellite diner that orbited Hosnian Prime. To be honest, the frangella pie that the place served was only just passable, but somehow his kitchen droids managed to cook up the best plate of fritzle fries anywhere in the galaxy.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="cookbook" />}}
'''Frangella pie''' was served at [[Freerago's Satellite Diner and Motel]], a [[satellite]] [[Restaurant|diner]] that [[orbit]]ed<ref name="Last Shot" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="planet">{{Databank|hosnian-prime|Hosnian Prime}}</ref> [[Hosnian Prime]]. The dish was [[Diet|edible]] by both [[human]]s<ref name="Last Shot" /> and [[Artiodac]]s.<ref name="cookbook" /> In [[7 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Last Shot]]'' to [[7 ABY]].</ref> [[Seven-Seven Dirgeos|Ruas Fastent]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[admiral]], ordered frangella [[pie]] with [[cabbage]], noting that there was a reason for requesting the two [[Foodstuff|foods]] together. After the pie failed to arrive, he complained about the tardiness to a [[Unidentified waitress|waitress]] at the diner, who explained that she had already checked with the [[Cooking droid|kitchen droids]] several times, but to no avail.<ref name="Last Shot" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date2">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] voiced his opinion on the diner's frangella pie in his ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''; he felt that the pie was merely passable, which made the establishment's [[Fritzle Fries]] being of high quality to be a surprise.<ref name="cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Frangella pie was first mentioned in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Last Shot]]'', written by [[Daniel José Older]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
