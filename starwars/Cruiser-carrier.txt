A '''cruiser-carrier''' was a type of [[cruiser]] that primarily served as a [[carrier]]. The [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class bulk cruiser]] was used by the [[Galactic Empire]] as a type of cruiser-carrier,<ref name="DB">{{Databank|quasar-fire-class-cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-Class Cruiser-Carrier}}</ref> and was considered a [[light carrier]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref>
