'''Airships''' were a type of flying machine made up primarily of two components: the [[airbag]] for buoyancy, and the [[gondola]], for [[transport]]ation. Airships were used by the rival factions [[Open]] and [[Closed]] during the [[Open-Closed War|Open-Closed war]] on the [[planet]] [[Carnelion IV]]. [[Airgun]]s were a notable type of airship.<ref name="Anakin 2">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 2|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 2]]</ref>
