'''For Light and Life''' was an age-old mantra used by the [[Jedi Order]],<ref name="Eye of Darkness">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref> referring to the [[light side of the Force]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> It was also used as a rallying cry, inspiring hope in all who heard it.<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> In the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Bell Zettifar]] reflected on the longstanding mantra, a tenet he held as a veritable truth for every living entity and the attainment of peace.<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> 

The motto was also used by Jedi such as [[Avar Kriss]],<ref name="High Republic 2021 1" /> [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]],<ref name="The Pirate and the Prince">{{YJA|The Prince and the Pirate}}</ref> the [[bond-twins]] [[Ceret]] and [[Terec]],<ref name="High Republic 2021 5">[[The High Republic (2021) 5|''The High Republic'' (2021) 5]]</ref> [[Jora Malli]], [[Stellan Gios]], [[Lina Soh]], [[Indeera Stokes]],<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]], [[Farzala Tarabal]],<ref name="High Republic Adventures 2021 1">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 1]]</ref> [[Obratuk Glii]], [[Qort]],<ref name="High Republic Adventures 2021 13">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 13|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 13]]</ref> [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]], [[Kantam Sy]], [[Cohmac Vitus]], [[Svi'no Atchapat]], [[Smeemarm]], [[Fezzonk]], [[Alys Ongwa]],<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> [[Pra-Tre Veter]], [[Elzar Mann]], [[Porter Engle]],<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> [[Cibaba]] and [[Keeve Trennis]].<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 1">[[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 1|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 1]]</ref>

The phrase was also used by some non-Jedi: [[Elarec Yovet]], the [[regasa]] of [[Shili]], used the motto, having learned it from fellow [[Togruta]] Jora Malli,<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> and monster hunter [[Drewe Henley]] quoted the mantra after [[Ty Yorrick]] took him on as her apprentice.<ref name="Saber for Hire 4">[[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 4|''The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire'' 4]]</ref>

In the aftermath of the [[Imperial Era|Galactic Empire's rise]], travelers navigating the [[Hidden Path]] recorded enigmatic phrases as markings in the [[Mapuzo safehouse]], one of which echoed the sentiment of the Jedi Order in its prime: "For Light and Life."<ref name="100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The mantra "for Light and life" first appeared in the [[2018]] book ''[[William Shakespeare's Jedi the Last: Star Wars Part the Eighth]]'', a [[Canon|non-canon]] junior novelization of the [[2017]] film {{Film|VIII}}, written in the style of [[Wikipedia:William Shakespeare|William Shakespeare]]. Following the [[Battle of Crait]],  [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] [[Force sense|senses]] the presence of [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and says to herself, "We are for light and life, and so adieu: Ne'er shall Rey look on thee in hope again."<ref name="Jedi the Last">''[[William Shakespeare's Jedi the Last: Star Wars Part the Eighth]]''</ref> The phrase makes its first [[canon]] appearance in the comic issue [[The High Republic (2021) 1|''The High Republic'' (2021) 1]], in which the [[Jedi Order|Jedi]] quote the mantra in unison during [[Avar Kriss|Avar Kriss']] dedication speech on [[Starlight Beacon]].<ref name="High Republic 2021 1" />

The Trivia Gallery for the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' episode "[[Part III]]" on [[StarWars.com]] reveals a list of additional [[Aurebesh]] [[Writing|inscriptions]] that were carved into the walls in the [[Mapuzo safehouse]], including "For Life and Light,"<ref name="Part III Trivia">{{SW|url=obi-wan-kenobi-part-iii-trivia-gallery|text=Part III Trivia Gallery &#124; Obi-Wan Kenobi|quote=There are also a variety of Jedi and Force phrase, axioms, and mantras on the walls, including the High Republic call of "For Life and Light" from the series of books set during the prime of the Jedi Order}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> a paraphrasing of<ref name="High Republic 2021 1" /> the rallying cry from ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]''.<ref name="Part III Trivia" /> However, this inscription cannot be read in the final episode.<ref name="Part III">{{Kenobi|Part III}}</ref>
