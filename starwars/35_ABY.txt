The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''35 ABY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3312 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''8012''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] discovers that [[Darth Sidious]] is alive on [[Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
*The [[Festival of the Ancestors]] is held on [[Pasaana]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
*The [[Knights of Ren]] wipe out most of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]], leading to the dissolution of the group.<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
*[[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] discovers her true [[Palpatine family|heritage]] as the paternal granddaughter of Sheev Palpatine.<ref name="Episode IX" />
*Leia Organa passes away on [[Ajan Kloss]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
*Kylo Ren is redeemed and becomes Ben Solo once again.<ref name="Episode IX" />
*First Order [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]] takes command of the [[Sith Eternal]]'s fleet, the [[Final Order]], after swearing allegiance to [[Darth]] Sidious.<ref name="Episode IX" />
*[[Kijimi]] is [[Destruction of Kijimi|destroyed]] by the Sith Eternal.<ref name="Episode IX" />
*[[Poe Dameron]] is promoted to acting [[General]] and is made head of the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
*The [[Battle of Exegol]] occurs leading to the permanent death of Darth Sidious, the destruction of the Sith Eternal and its [[Sith Eternal military|forces]]<ref name="Episode IX" /> and the end of the [[First Order-Resistance War]].<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>
*The inhabitants of the galaxy launch a [[Uprising against the First Order|series of attacks]] against the remaining First Order forces.<ref name="Episode IX" /> The First Order soon collapses.<ref name="Timelines" />
*Rey dies defeating Darth Sidious.<ref name="Episode IX" />
*Ben Solo sacrifices himself to revive Rey and vanishes into the Force.<ref name="Episode IX" />
*To honor the [[Skywalker family|Skywalker legacy]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] takes on the surname "Skywalker" and buries [[Skywalker lightsaber|Anakin]] and [[Leia's lightsaber]]s on [[Tatooine]] near the [[Lars homestead|Lars moisture farm]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
*''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' is published by [[Beaumont Kin]].<ref name="Rise and Fall Date">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>
