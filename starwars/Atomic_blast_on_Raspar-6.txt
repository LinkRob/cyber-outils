The '''atomic blast on Raspar-6''' was a large-scale nuclear explosion on the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] [[Raspar-6]],<ref name="MAGL" /> located in the [[Tragan Cluster/Legends|Tragan Cluster]], within the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Raspar system/Legends|Raspar system]]</ref> The blast impacted the [[Atmosphere/Legends|atmosphere]] of a [[Unidentified planet (West Fissure)|nearby planet]] during the month of [[Stolivea]] in that [[Unidentified calendar (Raspar system)|world's calendar]]. Its effects on the planet were expected to be the spreading of a phenomenon known as [[talking fog]]s into hollows and low valleys, as well as generating [[irrational echo]]es, heavy rain and eruption of snow in lower elevations. Forecasts advised composting and for the [[dust farmer]]s on the planet affected to take up [[Hydroponics/Legends|hydroponics]] in [[fribulation tank]]s during this period, which could reportedly last until mid-[[Aint]] or early [[Nnters]].<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behind the scenes==
The atomic blast on Raspar-6 was mentioned in a one-page weather forecast titled "Region 29: The West Fissure," written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that combines creature designs and photographs from various [[George Lucas]] projects with original text by Carrau.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]] with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
