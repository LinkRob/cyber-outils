'''Bobo''' was a [[Sanyassan]] who led the [[pirate]] gang known as the [[Vile]]. Shortly after ascending to become the leader of the Vile, Bobo became aware of a deal between the Vile and the other gangs in the region. [[Zilastra]], the leader of the [[Riftwalkers]] gang, revealed the details of the agreement and promised each of the leaders that they would then receive a payment in the form of a locked [[Regal Voyager case|case]]. Communicating via [[hologram]], Zilastra's [[droid]] [[courier]]s uttered the code phrases that unlocked the cases. However, instead of receiving the valuables, Bobo and the other leaders were [[Death|killed]] by the cases, which were secret explosives that Zilastra had sent.

==Biography==
Bobo was a [[Sanyassan]] [[pirate]] who was a member of a gang known as the [[Vile]].<ref name="The Living Force" /> By [[33 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> he had replaced the gang's former leader, [[Jupas]]. As the leader of the gang, Bobo had the privilege of sitting upon an iron throne. Shortly after his ascension to leader of the Vile, Bobo was instructed by a [[droid]] [[courier]] to call in to a [[hologram]] communication with leaders of the other gangs in [[the Slice]] region of [[the galaxy]]. Bobo and the leaders of the other gangs listened as [[Zilastra]], the leader of the [[Riftwalker sabacc|Riftwalkers]], recounted the deal the gangs had struck, explaining the details to Bobo, who had not been in a leadership position when the Vile had agreed to Zilastra's plan. There, Bobo learned the reason that each of the gangs had agreed to not attack any [[Starship|ships]] belonging to the conglomerate [[Regal Voyager]], understanding that a [[Regal Voyager case|case]] of valuables had been delivered to them as part of the deal. Bobo pointed out that he had not been able to open the case or even scan what was inside, and Zilastra told him that the droids were there to utter the code phrases to open them. However, the cases had been a ploy by Zilastra, and were filled with explosive [[baradium-357]]. As the cases were opened, Bobo, along with the other members of the call, were [[Death|killed]] by the explosions, and Zilastra's plan swiftly rendered her rivals leaderless.<ref name="The Living Force" />

==Personality and traits==
Bobo was a frightening-looking individual with a malicious voice and [[Color|golden]] [[eye]]s. As a Sanyassan, he had scaly skin and many [[Tooth|teeth]]. During the meeting with Zilastra, he had smeared a black, oily substance on his face. Bobo had [[hair]] and also wore furs.<ref name="The Living Force" />
