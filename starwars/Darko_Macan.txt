'''Darko Macan''' is an author and illustrator who has created and collaborated on [[Star Wars comic books|comic books]]. He wrote [[Star Wars: Chewbacca (Dark Horse)|''Star Wars: Chewbacca'']], ''[[Star Wars: Jedi vs. Sith]]'', and ''[[Vader's Quest|Star Wars: Vader's Quest]]'' for [[Dark Horse Comics]], as well as "[[Lady Luck (comic story)|Lady Luck]]" and ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Rogue Squadron: The Phantom Affair]]''.
