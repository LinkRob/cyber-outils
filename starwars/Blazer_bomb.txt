'''Blazer bombs''' were devastating<ref name="hyena bombers">{{SWE|technology|hyenabombers|hyena bombers}}</ref> [[Fire|incendiary]] [[Bomb|explosives]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] is set between the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] and the [[Battle of Christophsis]], both of which are dated to [[22 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> the [[Cadesura district]] of [[Zarra]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]], was [[Cadesura district|destroyed]] in a [[bomb]]ing which used both Separatist and [[Galactic Republic]] munitions, including scaled-down blazer bombs. When [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was sent to [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Kenobi and Skywalker)|investigate]] the disaster, he learned from [[Neimoidian Royal Guard]] [[Ruug Quarnom]] that a contact of hers had discovered evidence that blazer bombs had been used. In conjunction with the evidence of Republic involvement, Kenobi and Quarnom determined that the bombing must have been carried out by a third party attempting to play both sides of the Clone Wars.<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref>

During the Separatist [[Battle of Ryloth|occupation]] of the planet [[Ryloth]], blazer bombs deployed via [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Bombers]] were used to firebomb the villages of the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Twi'lek]]s.<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blazer bombs first appeared in "[[Liberty on Ryloth]]," the twenty-first epsiode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]],<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> which originally aired on [[March 13]], [[2009]].<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth air date">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/liberty-on-ryloth-episode-guide|text="Liberty on Ryloth" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> They were first identified in the hyena bomber's entry in [[StarWars.com]]'s now-discontinued [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|Encyclopedia]].<ref name="hyena bombers" />
