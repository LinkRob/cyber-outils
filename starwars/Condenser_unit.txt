The '''condenser unit''' was a device manufactured by [[SurvivalGear]]. It could generate [[water]] from moisture in the [[atmosphere]] and cook [[Foodstuff|foods]] without creating smoke. The unit was composed of a two-[[liter]] receptacle with a flared metal shade. A series of coils were attached to a small power unit and condenser in the middle. The metal shade could be inverted to generate heat capable of smokeless cooking and the condenser could generate enough water to sustain one person. The condenser unit's [[battery]] could last up to six [[Standard month|months]].<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The condenser unit was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Forged in Battle]]'',<ref name="Forged in Battle" /> a [[2016]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Soldier On!">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/8/17/soldier-on-1|text=Soldier On!}}</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[1994]] reference book ''[[A Guide to the Star Wars Universe]]'', compiled by [[Raymond L. Velasco]] and published by [[Ballantine Books]].<ref name="Guide">''[[A Guide to the Star Wars Universe]]''</ref>
