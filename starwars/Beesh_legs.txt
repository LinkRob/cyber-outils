'''Beesh legs''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] item that were rich in flavor and could be found on<ref name="The Secrets of Long Snoot">{{StoryCite|story=The Secrets of Long Snoot|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="Force Collection">{{ForceCollection|Garindan|star=2}}</ref> [[Kubindi]].<ref name="The Secrets of Long Snoot" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas 1 BBY">The events of "[[The Secrets of Long Snoot]]" take place concurrent to the [[secret mission to Tatooine]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the [[Kubaz]] [[Garindan ezz Zavor]], who had consumed beesh legs at one point in his life, reminisced about tasting the legs and [[leevil pâté]] when he inhaled a multitude of scents in [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[desert]] planet [[Tatooine]], realizing that it had been a considerable amount of [[time]] since he last tasted them.<ref name="The Secrets of Long Snoot" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beesh legs were mentioned in "[[The Secrets of Long Snoot]]," a short story written by [[Delilah S. Dawson]] and published in the [[2017]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''.<ref name="The Secrets of Long Snoot" />
