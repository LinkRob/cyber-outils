'''Bond-twins''' were a type of [[twin]]. With an even closer bond than regular twins, they were siblings that effectively shared a gestalt mind. [[Kotabi]] bond-twins [[Terec]] and [[Ceret]], members of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]], frequently finished each others' sentences even when not in the same room.<ref name="THR 2">[[The High Republic (2021) 2|''The High Republic'' (2021) 2]]</ref>
