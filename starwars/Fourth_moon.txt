{{Quote|We scanned the fourth moon before our arrival. Tensu Run was not there.|The Grand Inquisitor to a Diagu|<ref name="Inquisitors 1" />}}
The '''fouth moon''' was one of at least four that orbited the astronomical object [[Targyon]].<ref name="Inquisitors 1" /> Between [[14 BBY]] and [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Star Wars: Inquisitors]]'', take place between the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' and ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Fallen Order'' to [[14 BBY]] and ''Jedi: Survivor'' takes place in [[9 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:JediSurvivorDate|here]].</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] scanned the [[moon]] in order to locate [[Jedi Knight]] [[Tensu Run]]. The Empire was unable to locate the [[Jedi]] on the moon so they interrogated the [[Diagu]] on Targyon. One of the Diagu stated he heard Run say he was going to the fourth moon. [[The Grand Inquisitor]] knew this was a lie and had the Diagu [[Death|executed]] for not complying.<ref name="Inquisitors 1" />
