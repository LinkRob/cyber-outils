'''Bellis''' was a fictional character in the [[holodrama]] ''[[Love on Deck 5]]''. The series released at least three parts<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> They had horns and were self described as the famous [[Musician|singer]] in the [[Core Worlds|Core]]. During the beginning of the drama Bellis was traveling on the [[starship]] however they kept finding themselves eating alone. While talking about this with the [[Captain (Love on Deck 5)|Captain]], the Captain invites them to dinner. Bellis accepted hoping to make the [[chief officer (Love on Deck 5)|chief officer]] jealous. Despite their efforts, things did not work out between them and the chief officer. Bellis however booked another ticket to travel on the ship the following day and informed the Captain that they were going to bring the Captain on stage to play the [[valachord]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bellis's voice first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
