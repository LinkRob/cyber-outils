The [[Force power]] of '''dark illusion''' gave its [[Force-sensitive|user]] the ability to manipulate [[Force vision]]s to directly attack a selected target. Dark illusion was summoned through an ancient [[Sith]] ritual using both the connection of a [[Force-bond]], one coming from someone with a link to the target, and reciting a Sith incantation in the old [[Balc]] [[language]]. Compounded with the [[blood]] of the bonded and [[Force lightning]], as well as the [[dark side of the Force]], dark illusion could reach its intended victim from across [[the galaxy]].

During the [[Galaxy|galactic]] conflict known as the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]] used dark illusion to attack [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] of the [[Jedi Order]], exploiting his [[Sith apprentice]] [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus']] bond to Yoda for the ritual. Also using the power of the long-[[Death|deceased]] [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Bane]]'s [[Tomb of Darth Bane|tomb]], Sidious was able to, while on [[Coruscant]], reach Yoda on [[Moraband]], the ancient [[homeworld]] of the Sith.

==Description==
{{Quote|Kintik hadzuska sutta chwituskak! Kintik hadzuska sutta chwituskak! Jiaasjen Jidai, jiaasjen Jidai!|Darth Sidious, reciting the Sith incantation in the Balc tongue|audio=BalcSpeech01-SAC.flac|<ref name="Sacrifice" />}}
The [[Force power]] known as dark illusion<ref name="Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sacrifice-episode-gallery|text="Sacrifice" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Excerpt: ''He and Dooku together zap the water with their Force lightning, while Sidious recites an ancient Sith incantation in the Balc tongue. Sidious dips his face toward the basin. He now wields the power of dark illusion, and will lead Yoda into a trap.''}}</ref> was one born out of the [[dark side of the Force]].<ref name="Moraband" /> This ability allowed the wielder to manipulate [[Force vision]]s to affect a selected target.<ref name="Sacrifice" /> To harness dark illusion, an ancient [[Sith]] ritual was required.<ref name="Sith" /> This involved the [[blood]] of someone who was [[Force-sensitive]] and had a [[Force-bond]] with the intended victim. The bonded one then needed to focus on their bond with the target, and once the blood was drawn, it had to be compounded with an array of [[Force lightning]] in a sacrificial altar basin. An old Sith incantation was spoken in the [[Balc]] [[language]] to complete the ritual, at which point dipping one's face in the powered basin would allow one to access dark illusion. The resulting [[The Force|Force]] visions could be used to impersonate specific individuals, resembling them in both physical appearance and voice. The illusion could also be amplified using a source teeming with dark side energy, and could make the affected target believe that they were elsewhere during these visions.<ref name="Sacrifice" />

==Applications==
{{Quote|The Sith of your time will find you there, and you will find them. Be careful, for what they may offer will certainly ''feel'' like an answer.|The Force Priestesses to Yoda, with a warning of the Sith's visions|<ref name="Sacrifice" />}}
[[File:Darth_sidious_balc.png|thumb|left|300px|Darth Sidious drew upon the dark side to summon the power of dark illusion.]]
The art of dark illusion was ancient, known only to a few Sith.<ref name="Sith" /> When the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Darth Sidious]], sensed that [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] was on [[Moraband]], the ancient Sith [[homeworld]], he sought answers to why the [[Jedi]] had [[Mission to Moraband|ventured there]] and also hoped to break the [[Grand Master]]'s will. For this reason, he conjured the power of dark illusion, using his own sacrificial altar basin from a lair on [[Coruscant]]. As Yoda traveled into the [[Tomb of Darth Bane|tomb]] of [[Darth Bane]], an archaic [[Sith Lord]] from a thousand [[Standard year|years]] prior, Sidious further amplified the dark illusion's power with the power of the tomb, which had been used as a Jedi sacrificial chamber in [[Time|centuries]] past. This power allowed Sidious to tempt Yoda using familiar scenarios in the visions that followed. Ultimately, Sidious sought to subjugate [[the galaxy]], but once Yoda refused the Sith [[Lord]]'s temptations and visions, he knew he needed more time before the Jedi could be defeated and the Sith could return to power.<ref name="Sacrifice" />

==History==
{{Quote|Only the presence of someone significant to you would allow you to sense this ripple through time and space.''"<br />"''My old master&hellip; Yoda.''"<br />"Yes. ''Master Yoda has come to Moraband, although why, I do not know.|Darth Sidious and Darth Tyranus, speaking to Dooku's Force-bond with Yoda|<ref name="Sacrifice" />}}
During the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict that tore apart the [[galaxy]] and divided the [[Galactic Republic]], the Sith Lord [[Darth]] Sidious and his [[Sith apprentice]], [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]], used the ritual to summon the power of dark illusion.<ref name="Sacrifice" /> Sidious closely guarded his Sith identity, acting as the [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the Republic Palpatine, as did Tyranus, who was publicly known as [[Count]] Dooku, the leader of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]].<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', pgs. 72&ndash;73, 76&ndash;77</ref> When they both felt the presence of Master Yoda, Dooku's former teacher, on the [[planet]] Moraband, Tyranus was called to Coruscant to confer with his [[Sith Master]].<ref name="Sacrifice" />

In an abandoned industrial sector of Coruscant known as [[The Works]], Sidious informed his apprentice that they would be exploiting Dooku's Force-bond with Yoda, which the two had established during their time together in the [[Jedi Order]], to find out why Yoda had ventured to Moraband and to break his will;<ref name="Sacrifice" /> prior to Yoda's arrival on Moraband, only the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] member [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] suspected that the Sith were capable of exploiting Yoda's bond to Dooku.<ref name="Voices">{{TCW|Voices}}</ref> Over a sacrificial altar basin,<ref name="Episode Gallery" /> Sidious cut Tyranus with a knife to draw his blood, dripping it into the [[water]]-filled basin, and recited the Sith incantation in Balc. Together the Sith Lords then jolted the basin with Force lightning, and once Tyranus focused on his bond with Yoda, Sidious harnessed the power of dark illusion. When Tyranus expressed concern over Yoda sensing their presence, Sidious said he would come to Yoda in the face of someone familiar as he dipped his face into the sacrificial altar.<ref name="Sacrifice" />

[[File:Sifo-Dyas_1920_illusion.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Darth Sidious came to Yoda with a vision of Sifo-Dyas, a familiar face, using dark illusion.]]
Sidious pretended to be the long-[[Death|dead]] Jedi Master [[Sifo-Dyas]], imprisoned in the tomb and sacrificial chamber of the ancient Sith Lord Darth Bane through his vision to Yoda. Sidious tempted the old Jedi Master with an opportunity to&mdash;at last&mdash;learn Darth Sidious's true identity, since the Jedi were aware only of his existence. When Yoda refused, Sidious entranced Yoda further by using the power of the tomb to enhance his illusion and make the Jedi Master believe himself to be back on Coruscant. Yoda found himself in the presence of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and a [[squad]] of [[clone trooper]]s from the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[501st Legion]], in an ongoing pursuit of both Dooku and Sidious in the Works. When the Jedi cornered the two Sith Lords in the illusion, the 501st troopers were quickly incapacitated and, in turn, Skywalker beheaded Dooku as Yoda pursued the hooded Sidious. The two briefly [[Lightsaber combat|dueled]] before the vision of Sidious threatened to kill Skywalker while simultaneously attacking Yoda with Force lightning. The Jedi Master was then presented with a choice: either learn the identity of the Sith Lord once and for all or save Skywalker, whom Sidious had thrown off a catwalk. Yoda chose the latter and sacrificed himself, falling into the depths of Coruscant with the vision of Sidious as the dark illusion fell apart.<ref name="Sacrifice" />

After the illusion ceased, Sidious arose from the altar basin and informed Tyranus that they had failed to break Yoda's will, and that they would need more time to defeat the Jedi.<ref name="Sacrifice" /> Unbeknownst to either of the Sith, Yoda had been put on trial by the ethereal [[Force Priestesses]] to learn the [[Force spirit|secret to immortality]], and had passed when he defeated the dark-siders he encountered, including Sidious's own dark illusion.<ref name="Ultimate" /> In addition, despite Tyranus' presence throughout the ritual, only Sidious was in control of the visions presented to Yoda, leaving Tyranus unaware of the foreshadowing of his own death or of Skywalker's eventual fall to the dark side.<ref name="Sacrifice" /> Near the end of the Clone Wars, Skywalker [[Battle of Coruscant|killed]] Dooku in the same manner he had slain the count in Sidious's dark illusion. Dooku's death became one of Skywalker's first steps into becoming Darth Vader, Sidious's new Sith apprentice, prior to the beginning of the [[Great Jedi Purge]], which fulfilled Sidious's plot for the revenge of the Sith.<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The dark illusion Force power debuted in "[[Sacrifice (episode)|Sacrifice]]," the thirteenth episode of [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|The Lost Missions]], which was released as the sixth and final season of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series.<ref name="Sacrifice" /> Dark illusion was first identified by name in the "Sacrifice" episode gallery on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Episode Gallery" /> In the episode's script, the Sith incantation spoken by Sidious was noted as "Balc speech" and was written out as "''Kintik hadzuska sutta chwituskak! Kintik hadzuska sutta chwituskak! Jiaasjen Jidai, jiassjen Jidai!''".<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sacrifice-trivia-gallery|text="Sacrifice" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Excerpt: ''In the script, Sidious' incantation is said to be "Balc speech," and is written as follows: "Kintik hadzuska sutta chwituskak! Kintik hadzuska sutta chwituskak! Jiaasjen Jidai, jiassjen Jidai!"''}}</ref> Darth Sidious, who recited the Sith incantation in Balc to summon the power of dark illusion, was voiced by [[Tim Curry]].<ref name="Sacrifice" />
