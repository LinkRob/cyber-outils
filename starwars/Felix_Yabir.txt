'''Felix Yabir''' was a [[Sullustan]] [[Jedi Initiate]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Brotherhood" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] is set between the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Battle of Christophsis]], as the novel's plot involves [[Anakin Skywalker]] becoming a [[Jedi Knight]], a rank he attained between those events. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates both events to [[22 BBY]], ''Brotherhood'' must be set in that year.</ref> he and his [[Mill Alibeth's Initiate Clan|Initiate Clan]] were scheduled to travel to the [[planet]] [[Ilum]] to undergo [[Gathering|the Gathering]] and earn their [[kyber crystal]]s. Despite his shorter [[Limb|legs]], Yabir outran [[Zabrak]] Initiate [[Mill Alibeth]] to get aboard their transport, the ''[[Crucible]]''. Yabir was a native speaker of [[Sullustese]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />
