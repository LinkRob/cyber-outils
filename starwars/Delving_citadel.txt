{{Quote|That delving citadel&hellip; it was built centuries ago. It's the core of production in this barony. It is ''irreplaceable''. It&hellip; It'll never be productive again.|Trios laments the destruction of a delving citadel|<ref name="V16" />}}
'''Delving citadels''', also known as '''delving-citadels''', were a type of vehicle used by [[ore-duke]]s to mine on the [[planet]] [[Shu-Torun]]. During the [[Shu-Torun War|War on Shu-Torun]], the [[Galactic Empire]] destroyed one of the citadels belonging to the dukes, also horrifying their ally [[Queen]] [[Trios]], who knew that the vehicles would be needed after the war to meet Imperial quotas. She asked the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to stop destroying the citadels, but was refused.<ref name="V16" /> She later requested that the [[Rubix's delving citadel|citadel]] of [[Baron]] [[Rubix]] be the second to be targeted, as he had attempted to have her [[Death|killed]].<ref name="V17">[[Darth Vader (2015) 17|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 17]]</ref>
