'''Darth''' was a title that preceded the moniker of a [[Sith Lord]]. Predating the history of the [[Rule of Two]], it was used by ancient [[Sith]] such as [[Darth Atrius]], [[Darth Caldoth]], and [[Darth Noctyss]]. The Darth title was preserved through [[Darth Bane]]'s reforms, spawning a [[Millennium|thousand-year]] lineage that included [[Darth Zannah]], [[Darth Tenebrous]], and [[Darth Plagueis]]. [[Darth Sidious]] was the culmination of generations of Sith leading to the [[Imperial Era|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. As a [[Sith Master]], Sidious bestowed the title of Darth on his three successive [[Sith apprentice]]s: [[Maul|Darth Maul]], [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]], and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].

==Overview==
{{Quote|This is the way of the Sith. I named you ''{{'}}Vader{{'}}'' after you pledged yourself to the Sith. You proved yourself worthy of it then. I am certain you will prove yourself worthy now.|Darth Sidious, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Darth Vader 6">[[Darth Vader (2015) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 6]]</ref>}}
[[File:SithTeachings-Convergence.png|thumb|left|250px|The name of a Sith Lord consisted of the Darth title and a personal moniker.]]
Darth was a [[Sith]] title given as both a<ref name="Episode III SG31">{{SW|url=films/star-wars-episode-3-revenge-of-the-sith-story-gallery|text=''Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith'' Story Gallery}} {{C|Image 31 of 53}}</ref> name<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> and title<ref name="Episode III SG31" /> to the [[Sith Lord]]s, an order of [[Force-sensitive]]s who channeled their [[Force power|power]] through the [[dark side of the Force]].<ref name="SOTS">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref> The title, which roughly translated to "[[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]],"<ref name="SWEasy">''[[Star Wars Made Easy]]''</ref> was part of a Sith [[Lord]]'s mantle,<ref name="Anakin Skywalker BG18">{{Databank|anakin-skywalker-biography-gallery|Anakin Skywalker Biography Gallery}} {{C|Image 18 of 22}}</ref> preceding a moniker that replaced the Sith Lord's original name.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> The Darth title and accompanying moniker symbolized one's identity as a Sith.<ref name="Episode III SG31" /> The Sith names of [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], [[Dooku]], and [[Anakin Skywalker]] were Darth Sidious, Darth Tyranus, and Darth Vader, respectively.<ref name="Episode III" /> In at least two cases, the title and the accompanying moniker were bestowed on the [[Sith apprentice]] by the [[Sith Master]].<ref name="Sidious DB">{{Databank|emperor-palpatine-darth-sidious|Emperor Palpatine/Darth Sidious}}</ref> Under the [[Rule of Two]], the Master and apprentice both bore the Darth title at the same [[time]].<ref name="Episode III" />

==History==
===Ancient past===
{{Quote|Do you know who I am?''"<br />"''Yes. Darth Bane, the ancient Sith Lord you are.|Darth Bane's specter and Yoda|<ref name="Sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:MominAndShaa-2017DarthVader22.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Darth Shaa and Darth Momin were among the Sith who predated the Galactic Empire.]]
The Sith were a [[Religion|sect]] of [[Force-sensitive|Force adepts]] who [[Hundred-Year Darkness|splintered]] off from the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Sith SWE">{{SWE|groups|sith|Sith}}</ref> There were many Sith who affixed the title "Darth" to their name, including legends such as [[Darth Caldoth]],<ref name="Gaze of Stone">{{StoryCite|story=Gaze of Stone|book=Myths & Fables}}</ref> [[Darth Noctyss]], [[Darth Sanguis]]<ref name="A Life Immortal">{{StoryCite|story=A Life Immortal|book=Dark Legends}}</ref>, and [[Darth Wrend]].<ref name="Master & Apprentice">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> [[Darth Atrius]] bore the title when the Sith were considered plentiful and powerful.<ref name="Annual 4">[[Star Wars Annual (2015) 4|''Star Wars Annual'' (2015) 4]]</ref> Other title bearers included [[Darth Momin]], [[Darth Ravi]], [[Darth Shaa]], and [[Darth Tanis]].<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>

[[Darth Krall]] was a powerful Sith Lord who was also once a [[Jedi]], known as [[Jedi Master]] Radaki, until he turned to the dark side of [[the Force]]. The [[Jedi Code]] conflicted with Radaki's belief that the Jedi should retain their family ties and wealth, and he eventually became one of the [[Lost Twenty]].<ref name="Dooku JL">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> [[Darth Quellus]] was said to have used the dark side to make [[candle]]s burn black during the [[Sith Empire]]'s [[occupation of Aaloth]].<ref name="Kindling">{{StoryCite|story=The Kindling|book=Life Day Treasury}}</ref>

The Sith were in danger of extinction due to a combination of infighting<ref name="Sacrifice" /> and their conflict with the Jedi.<ref name="Bane SWE">{{SWE|characters|darth-bane|Darth Bane}}</ref> [[Darth Bane]], the sole survivor of the ancient Sith, developed the Rule of Two&mdash;limiting their ranks to a master and an apprentice&mdash;by training<ref name="Sacrifice" /> [[Darth Zannah]]<ref name="HC 2">{{HelmetCollectionCite|2|Databank A-Z}}</ref> as his only disciple.<ref name="Sacrifice" />

===Modern era===
====Darth Sidious====
{{Quote|As Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine, I would stand for a united Republic. As Sith Lord Darth Sidious, I would conspire to rip that Republic asunder.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="SOTS" />}}
[[File:Rise-of-Palpatine.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Sidious rose to power in the Galactic Republic as Sheev Palpatine from Naboo.]]
Bane's reformation was a resilient legacy<ref name="Sacrifice" /> that lasted for a [[millennium]], creating a lineage of Sith Lords that culminated with Darth Sidious who came to power as Sheev Palpatine during the [[Republic Era|late era]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="SOTS" /> In time, the Sith tradition led to [[Darth Tenebrous]], who trained the Sith Lord [[Darth Plagueis]].<ref name="BMF 44">{{BuildFalconCite|44|Starship Fact File}}</ref> Carrying on the Rule of Two, Plagueis trained Darth Sidious; after learning the secrets of Plagueis,<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Sidious [[Death|killed]] his master and trained his own apprentice, the [[Dathomirian]] warrior [[Maul|Darth Maul]].<ref name="SidiousBG1">{{Databank|darth-sidious-biography-gallery|Darth Sidious Biography Gallery}} {{C|Image 1 of 30}}</ref>

The [[Jedi High Council]] became aware of the Sith Order's return during the [[Invasion of Naboo]], as a result of [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]'s first encounter with Maul on [[Tatooine]]. The High [[Jedi Council|Council]] was skeptical about Jinn's findings, the Jedi having not encountered a living Sith Lord in over a thousand [[Standard year|years]]. Jinn death in a [[lightsaber duel]] with Maul, during the [[Battle of Naboo]], removed any doubt that the High Council had about the "mysterious warrior's" identity as a Sith. The High Council also suspected the existence of a second Sith Lord due to their awareness of the Rule of Two. However, they could not be certain whether the Sith who killed Jinn had been the master or the apprentice.<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref>

The name Darth Sidious was first revealed to the Jedi by a former member of their Order, [[Count]] Dooku, who informed [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] about Sidious' influence over the [[Galactic Senate]]. Neither Kenobi nor [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] believed Dooku, however, as in their view the Count's credibility was undercut by his conversion to the dark side and betrayal of the Jedi Order. Nevertheless, [[Mace Windu]] advised the High Council to exercise greater caution in their future dealings with the Senate.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>

[[File:EmperorAmused-Orientation.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Sidious discarded the persona of Sheev Palpatine after becoming Emperor of the Galactic Empire.]]
Sidious successfully maintained a double life throughout his rise to power, the two aspects of his persona&mdash;Darth Sidious and Sheev Palpatine&mdash;working in tandem toward the realization of the [[Grand Plan]]<ref name="SOTS" /> and the unlimited power that he coveted. He was [[Proclamation of the New Order|declared]] the [[Galactic Emperor]] by the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the [[Clone Wars]], which also witnessed the [[Great Jedi Purge|extermination]] of the Jedi throughout [[the galaxy]] under [[Order 66]].<ref name="Episode III" /> As [[Emperor]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Sidious saw no use in preserving his alter ego as Palpatine. Although the name "Palpatine" was included in his public title as Emperor, in his own mind he was only "Darth Sidious" for the remainder of his life.<ref name="PalpatineBG29">{{Databank|palpatine-biography-gallery|Palpatine Biography Gallery}} {{C|Image 29 of 29}}</ref>

Lady [[Qi'ra]], the leader of [[Crimson Dawn]], learned about the Sith through her connection to Maul, including the history of Darth Sidious. Opposed to the [[New Order]], Qi'ra conspired to bring an end to the Sith's reign over the galaxy with the help of the [[Knights of Ren]] and other allied groups. During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], Qi'ra revealed Palpatine's Sith name to her allies while denouncing his overall reign as Emperor.<ref name="CR1" />

====Darth Maul====
{{Quote|So, the rumors are true, Darth Maul lives.''"<br />"''Formerly Darth, now just Maul.|Seventh Sister and Maul|<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref>}}
[[File:Darth_Maul_1_Albuquerque.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Darth Maul was trained from a young age as the Sith apprentice of Darth Sidious.]]
The [[Zabrak]] [[Maul's family|son]] of [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]], the child who would one day become Darth Maul was taken by Sidious at a young age, and was trained in the ways of the Sith. At some point, he was given the Sith moniker “Darth Maul” by his master, and forgot his original birth name. As a Sith apprentice, Darth Maul was trained in secrecy until Sidious decided to reveal their Order's survival to the Jedi. Maul longed to avenge his predecessors' defeat in the [[Jedi-Sith War]] of [[Standard century|centuries]] past, but was defeated and nearly killed by Obi-Wan Kenobi.<ref name="SOTS" /> At the time, his name was unknown to the Jedi who referred to him simply as the "mysterious warrior."<ref name="Episode I" /> However, by the time of the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars, the Order had discovered his name. Kenobi initially mistook [[Savage Opress]] for Maul, who was his brother, because of their resemblance.<ref name="Witches of the Mist">{{TCW|Witches of the Mist}}</ref>

Maul endeavored to lead his own secret empire, the [[Shadow Collective]], after recovering from his injuries.<ref name="SOTS" /> He still considered himself as a Sith Lord,<ref name="Revival">{{TCW|Revival (episode)}}</ref> despite no longer being Sidious' apprentice,<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> and assumed the role of master for his brother Opress.<ref name="Revival" /> During the [[Battle of Mandalore]], Sidious killed Opress and defeated Maul, citing that only two Sith Lords could exist at any given time.<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref>

Maul survived the Clone Wars and longed for revenge against the Jedi and Sith alike.<ref name="SOTS" /> While he still maintained his connection to the dark side of the Force, Maul no longer considered himself as a Sith. Nonetheless, the [[Seventh Sister]] acknowledged him as "Darth Maul," to which the former Sith revealed that he had discarded the title of Darth by the time of the [[Inquisitor]]s' [[Mission to Malachor|arrival]] on [[Malachor]].<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice" />

====Darth Tyranus====
{{Quote|Vowing to help me eradicate the corruption from the institutions he had once served, Dooku was reborn as Darth Tyranus.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="SOTS" />}}
[[File:Dooku-TLC.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Tyranus was publicly known as Count Dooku, leader of the Confederacy of Independent Systems.]]
After the events of the Invasion of [[Naboo]], former Jedi Master Count Dooku replaced Maul as Sidious' Sith apprentice and was consequently renamed Darth Tyranus.<ref name="SOTS" /> Dooku had already used the name Tyranus in his dealings with the [[Ruling Council (Kamino)|Kaminoan government]]<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref> and the [[Pyke Syndicate]]. As his Sith name, he would come to use it in conversations with his Sith Master.<ref name="The Lost One">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref> However, he retained the use of his original name in his public identity as the [[Count of Serenno]] and [[Head of State]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Tyranus and Sidious kept their Sith identities secret by using their public names, respectively Dooku and Sheev Palpatine, throughout the [[Separatist Crisis]]<ref name="Episode II" /> and the Clone Wars.<ref name="Episode III" />

The Jedi Order gradually learned of the plot to an end the era of their guardianship over the galaxy, beginning when Kenobi learned that an individual named "Tyranus" recruited the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] to serve as the [[clone template]] for [[Sifo-Dyas]]' [[army]] of [[clone trooper]]s. The Jedi discovered that the mysterious Tyranus was in fact Dooku when the [[Pyke]]s on [[Oba Diah]] addressed the Count by his Sith moniker in the presence of Kenobi and Skywalker. Their subsequent report to the Jedi High Council confirmed that the Sith were indeed responsible for the creation of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. By then, however, the Jedi had come to trust the clones who served under their [[Jedi General|command]]. The High Council was also aware of the impact the information would have on the Republic's morale; therefore, [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] instructed his colleagues not to reveal Dooku's role in the clone army's origins to anyone, including Chancellor Palpatine.<ref name="The Lost One" />

====Darth Vader====
=====A new name=====
{{Quote|Henceforth, you shall be known as Darth&hellip; Vader.|Darth Sidious, to the former Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:VaderAnakin-DV5cover.png|thumb|left|250px|The Chosen One Anakin Skywalker was rechristened Darth Vader after he pledged his loyalty to Darth Sidious.]]
Although Sidious perceived his [[Palpatine family|lineage]] as the strongest in the Force, the raw power of the [[Skywalker family|Skywalker bloodline]] was undeniable to him, and as such planned to eventually replace Tyranus with Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="SOTS" /> Hailed as the [[Chosen One]] destined to bring balance to the Force, Skywalker was taken into the ranks of the Jedi Order and [[Jedi training|trained]] by Obi-Wan Kenobi.<ref name="Episode I" /> Through his elevated position as "Chancellor Palpatine," leader of the Senate, Sidious cultivated a friendship with the young Skywalker<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin 1">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 1|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 1]]</ref> in order to ensure their future partnership as Master and apprentice.<ref name="SOTS" />

Under the influence of Sidious, Skywalker was gradually seduced by the dark side of the Force.<ref name="SOTS" /> During the [[Battle of Coruscant]], he executed Tyranus on the orders of Chancellor Palpatine, who subsequently revealed his secret identity as a Sith Lord to the Jedi. Skywalker ultimately turned to the dark side after thwarting the High Council's efforts to [[Duel in Palpatine's office|depose]] Sidious.<ref name="Episode III" /> By pledging his loyalty to the Sith, Skywalker was deemed worthy of the name Darth Vader by Sidious,<ref name="Darth Vader 6" /> who took the former [[Jedi Knight]] as his new Sith apprentice. The newly christened Vader led the [[501st Legion]]'s [[Operation: Knightfall|attack]] on the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] where he personally killed multiple Jedi, including [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] who recognized him as Skywalker.<ref name="Episode III" />

Vader's actions in the [[Jedi temple|Temple]] were discovered by Kenobi and Yoda upon their [[Infiltration of the Jedi Temple|return]] to [[Coruscant]]. Kenobi's reluctance to confront his former Padawan was replaced with resolve after Yoda counseled him to separate Skywalker from the persona of Vader. According to Yoda, Skywalker had effectively ceased to exist when he turned to the dark side. Vader was [[Duel on Mustafar|defeated]] and left to die on [[Mustafar]] by Kenobi, but survived through the intervention of Sidious who rebuilt his apprentice into an [[armor]]ed [[cyborg]].<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:BattlesthatChangedGalaxy-Vader-boarding.png|thumb|right|300px|Abandoning his past, Darth Vader waged a war of rage against all who had wronged him and those who stood in the Empire's way.]]
After years of [[Hunt for Obi-Wan|hunting]] his former master, Vader [[Fight on a barren moon|confronted]] Kenobi on a [[Unidentified barren moon|barren moon]]<ref name="Part VI">{{Kenobi|Part VI}}</ref> in [[9 BBY]].<ref name="9 BBY">{{KenobiDate|VI|the fight on the barren moon}}</ref> During the [[Lightsaber duel|duel]], [[Darth Vader's armor|Vader's mask]] was damaged by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber|Kenobi's lightsaber]]. Kenobi apologized to his one-time friend upon seeing the face of Anakin Skywalker, but as Darth Vader, the Dark Lord declared that he was responsible for the destruction of his former persona. Convinced that Skywalker was truly lost to the dark side, Kenobi bid Vader farewell, referring to him as "Darth" instead of "Anakin."<ref name="Part VI" />

During their [[Rescue of Princess Leia|final duel]]<ref name="Episode IV" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> Kenobi found himself unable to say his old friend's real name<ref name="Time of Death">{{StoryCite|story=Time of Death|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> and instead called him "Darth."<ref name="Episode IV" /> In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Yavin]], Sidious warned his apprentice to not underestimate how much he disappointed his master by losing to Kenobi on Mustafar. Sidious had given the title of "Darth Vader" to Anakin Skywalker because at the time he believed that the fallen Jedi was worthy of bearing a Sith name. Having grown more doubtful of his apprentice, Sidious required Vader to prove that he was still worthy of his title by overcoming the [[Cylo Directive|obstacles]] in his path to Sith ascendancy.<ref name="Darth Vader 6" />

=====A certain point of view=====
{{Quote|Your father was seduced by the dark side of the Force. He ceased to be Anakin Skywalker and became Darth Vader. When that happened, the good man who was your father was destroyed. So what I told you was true&hellip; from a certain point of view.|Obi-Wan Kenobi, to Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>}}
[[File:VadersRevelation-TESB.png|thumb|left|250px|Luke Skywalker learned that Darth Vader was who Anakin Skywalker became when he turned to the dark side of the Force.]]
The former identity of Darth Vader was kept secret from his son, [[Luke Skywalker]], by Jedi Masters Obi-Wan Kenobi and Yoda.<ref name="Episode VI" /> Although Skywalker eventually learned that his father had served alongside Kenobi as a Jedi Knight in the Clone Wars,<ref name="Episode IV" /> Kenobi sought to shield Skywalker from the full truth of Anakin's fate.<ref name="Obi-Wan Kenobi DB">{{Databank|obi-wan-kenobi|Obi-Wan Kenobi}}</ref> He told Skywalker that his father was killed in the systematic purge of the Jedi Order by a fallen Jedi, Darth Vader. Skywalker endeavored to learn the ways of the Force as a Jedi, as his father did,<ref name="Episode IV" /> all the while believing Anakin to be dead until he [[Duel on Cloud City|confronted]] his father's "killer," Vader, in [[Cloud City]] on [[Bespin]] where the Sith Lord revealed that he was the young Jedi's father.<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref>

As a [[Force spirit]], Kenobi explained that his motive for hiding the truth was to protect Luke Skywalker.<ref name="Obi-Wan Kenobi DB" /> According to Kenobi, he had told the truth&mdash;from his point of view&mdash;about Vader's role in the "death" of Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="Episode VI" /> Yoda had also referred to Vader and Anakin as different personas shortly after discovering his turn to the dark side.<ref name="Episode III" /> Vader and his Master, Sidious, alluded to the idea that Anakin was a separate person from Vader as well.<ref name="Episode V" />

After Yoda and Kenobi confirmed Vader's identity, Luke Skywalker became convinced that he could turn his father back to the [[light side of the Force]]. Initially, Vader rejected his son's entreaties to renounce the dark side, and denied having any remaining connection to the name of Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="Episode VI" /> However, during the [[Battle of Endor]] Skywalker's compassion<ref name="Obi-Wan Kenobi DB" /> towards Vader caused him to protect his son from the Emperor in an act of self-sacrifice,<ref name="Episode VI" /> resulting in his death as Anakin Skywalker rather than Darth Vader.<ref name="Obi-Wan Kenobi DB" />

===Return of Sidious===
{{Quote|But darkness is eternal. Unlike the light, it cannot be extinguished. It will always find a way to rise again. And I, Darth Sidious, have risen with it.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="SOTS" />}}
[[File:DarthPalpatine-TROS.png|thumb|right|250px|The Sith legacy endured through Darth Sidious, whose resurrection was made possible through the art of cloning and the power of the dark side.]]
The prophesied destruction of the Sith<ref name="Episode III" /> came to pass when Darth Sidious and Darth Vader destroyed each other, ending the thousand-year lineage that began with Darth Bane.<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Unbeknownst to the galaxy, however, Sidious was resurrected after his first demise at [[Endor]]. Utilizing the knowledge that he acquired from his late Master, Darth Plagueis, Sidious succeeded in moving his consciousness to a clone of his original body, through a dark-side technique known as [[Transference]].<ref name="SOTS" /> In his new host form, Sidious endeavored to achieve the restoration of Sith rule across the galaxy.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

After Luke Skywalker's death, Sidious revealed his survival to the public through a galaxy-wide [[Exegol Broadcast|broadcast]], leading to the search for Exegol by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and the Jedi apprentice Rey&mdash;the respective descendants of Darth Vader and Darth Sidious. Although the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]] referred to the Emperor by his family name, Palpatine,<ref name="Episode IX" /> this time Sidious made no effort to conceal his Sith identity,<ref name="TSWB" /> declaring that the [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[fleet]], the [[Final Order]], would usher in the rise of a new [[Sith Empire]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|At the height of their powers, they allowed Darth Sidious to rise, create the Empire, and wipe them out. It was a Jedi Master who was responsible for the training and creation of Darth Vader.|Luke Skywalker, to Rey|<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>}}
The [[Darth Malted Milkshake]] was a drink sold in the galaxy.<ref name="3rdMath">''[[Star Wars Workbook: 3rd Grade Math]]''</ref> [[Darth Krist-Ov]] was a character in a tragic play performed during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Intermission">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=12|story=Intermission, Part 1}}</ref> Darth Vader's name was remembered and hated for years after his death. During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], Jedi Master Luke Skywalker mentioned his father and Sidious by their Sith names while recounting their history to the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Behind the scenes==
The title "Darth" first appeared in [[George Lucas]]' [[1977]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], as part of the name Darth Vader. According to ''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]'', Lucas intended the name "Darth Vader" to suggest "dark father."<ref name="BtM">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref> The Sith character [[Exim Panshard]] does not have "Darth" in his name, which author [[Adam Christopher]] took as a sign that the Sith Order, at one time, was not formally established with such titles.<ref name="Predate Organized">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/1843879823804117321|[[Adam Christopher]]|quote=I figured he predates organised Sith. {{C|In response to: "Do you know why Exim Panshard doesn't have "Darth" in his name?"}}|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/9Ulu8}}</ref>
