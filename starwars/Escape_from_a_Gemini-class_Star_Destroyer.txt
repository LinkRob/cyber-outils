In the [[time]] following the [[Battle of Exegol]],<ref name="TwinsDate" /> the [[dark side adept]] [[Karre]] and his [[astromech droid]], [[R-DUO]], sought to '''escape''' a [[Gemini-class Star Destroyer|''Gemini''-class Star Destroyer]] with the [[kyber crystal]] that powered it. Karre had foreseen that his sister, [[Am]], would [[Death|die]] if the [[crystal]] was used, but she rejected his advice and attempted to stop him.<ref name="Twins" />
