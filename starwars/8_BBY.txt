'''8 BBY''', or '''8 Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]. The year was a part of the [[Imperial Era]], a [[time]] period in which [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]'s [[Galactic Empire]] governed [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Timelines" />

==Events==
*[[Lieutenant]] [[Gorn]] witnesses the [[Eye of Aldhani]].<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref>
*The Empire begins planning a refueling station for the [[Death Star]] on [[Dizon Fray]].<ref>''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

==Births==
*[[Un'hee]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Un'hee">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Thrawn: Treason]]'' to [[1 BBY]]. As ''Thrawn: Treason'' establishes that [[Un'hee]] is seven [[Standard year|years]] old, she must have been born around 8 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Han & Chewie Adventure]]'' around 8 BBY. However, it also dates "[[Powered Down, Part 1]]" and [[Powered Down, Part 2|2]] to [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> As the Powered Down comics take place after ''A Han & Chewie Adventure'', this placement is impossible.
