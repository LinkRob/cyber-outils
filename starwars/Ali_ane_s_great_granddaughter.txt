The [[Lupr'or]] [[Ali'ane]] had a '''great granddaughter''' who lived during the [[First Order-Resistance War]]. She possessed a necklace that was salvaged from an abandoned Lupr'or starship. [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] enlisted the girl's help in locating the Lupr'or starship in order to retrieve technology needed to attack the [[Tsw'ells]] guarding the [[R'ora]] [[R'ora stronghold|stronghold]]. Phasma wanted to eliminate [[Lieutenant]] [[Sol Rivas]] since he was the only who knew that she had lowered [[Starkiller Base]]'s [[Deflector shield|shields]], causing the [[superweapon]]'s destruction.

==Biography==
This [[Lupr'or]] [[Gender|girl]] was the great granddaughter of [[Ali'ane]], the oldest living Lupr'or who was born in the [[star]]s. The girl inherited a necklace from her grandmother, which was salvaged from an abandoned Lupr'or starship. After the [[First Order]] visitor [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] studied the [[R'ora]] [[R'ora stronghold|stronghold]], she grabbed Ali'ane and asked her where she got the necklace. The child led Phasma, the [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] [[TN-3465]], and the Lupr'or guide [[Dar'en]] to the home of Ali'ane.<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" />

The girl introduced Ali'ane to Captain Phasma, who asked about the origins of the necklace. Ali'ane replied that she had obtained the necklace from the [[Lupr'or homeworld|"old world"]]. When Phasma asked where she could find more, Ali'ane replied that it was forbidden. At Phasma's prompting, the girl tried to convince her relative that Phasma had come to save them from the R'ora and the [[Tsw'ells]]. She added that Jair'i had said that the gods had sent Phasma to help them. Regarding technology as evil, Ali'ane refused to reveal the location of the ship.<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" />

Later, the girl told Phasma that she was better at climbing rocks than her companion TN-3465. Phasma replied that she had seen a [[Parnassos|world]] similar to [[Luprora]] and that it was a hard life. The girl agreed to take Phasma to the ship if she did not inform Ali'ane. For several hours, the Lupr'or girl led Phasma, TN-3465, and Da'ren to the last Lupr'or ship, which rested on a cliff that had not been claimed by the sea.<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" />

Using the technology salvaged from the ship, Phasma led an attack on the R'ora stronghold. The Lupr'or electrocuted the Tsw'ells and rowed across the lake to the R'ora stronghold.<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" /> While the Lupr'or battled the R'ora, Phasma took the opportunity to [[Hunt for Rivas|hunt down]] and kill [[Lieutenant]] [[Sol Rivas]], concealing her role in the [[Battle of Starkiller Base|destruction]] of [[Starkiller Base]]. Having outlived their usefulness, Phasma then abandoned the Lupr'or to the R'ora and the rising sea levels.<ref name="Captain Phasma 4">[[Captain Phasma 4|''Captain Phasma'' 4]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Ali'ane's great granddaughter was a prepubescent Lupr'or [[Gender|girl]] with green skin, black hair, and gray eyes. She admired Captain Phasma, believing that she was a hero sent by the gods to deliver them from the R'ora and the Tsw'ells. Her trust and respect for Phasma led her to show her the location of the last Lupr'or ship. The girl wore a blue tunic, white trousers, and white [[scarf]].<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" />
