'''Brace Marko''' was a [[pilot]] who served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. A member of [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]], Marko flew as [[Gold Six]]. The [[Rebel pilot]] participated in an [[Battle of Scarif|attack]] on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]] to help steal the [[Death Star plans|plans]] for a [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|superweapon]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Visual Guide" />
