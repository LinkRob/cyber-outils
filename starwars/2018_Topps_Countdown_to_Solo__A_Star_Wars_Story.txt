'''2018 Topps Countdown to ''Solo: A Star Wars Story''''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] in [[2018]] to coincide with the release of the film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. As indicated by the name, a card is released during the days counting down for the movie, with 25 cards being planned for release, one each weekday.
