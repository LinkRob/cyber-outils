"'''Chapter 10: The Dark Lord'''" is the tenth and final episode of the [[Maul - Shadow Lord Season 1|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'' and was released on [[May 4]], [[2026]] alongside "[[Chapter 9: Strange Allies]]".<ref name="Twitter" />

==Official description==
''One last test, one last chance to escape and survive.''

==Plot summary==
===The wrath of Vader===
Facing [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], [[Maul]] charges with [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]] at the [[Sith Lord]], who fends off his attack with [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|his own lightsaber]]. Just as [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]] join the fight with [[Devon Izara's lightsaber|their]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki's lightsaber|lightsabers]], Vader strikes Maul's [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] [[Limb|leg]] while [[Brander Lawson]] and his son [[Rylee Lawson]] shoot at the armored Sith Lord before fleeing into the forest. Lawson convinces them to flee towards their rendezvous point through a ruined structure. Vader follows them and uses the Force to rain down debris in an attempt to slow them down. While the non-[[Force sensitive|Force users]] flee, the Jedi and Maul make a stand against Vader.

Maul deduces that Vader is not an Inquisitor and asks what he is. The three take turns fighting Vader but are unable to match the relentless Sith Lord. Vader hurls Daki aside with [[the Force]] and continues fighting Maul and Izara. Vader gains the upper hand over Maul and attempts to strike him down but is blocked by [[Devon Izara's lightsaber]]. Together, Maul and Izara push Vader back before retreating through the ruins with Daki. Vader uses the Force to [[telekinesis|levitate]] debris and stony structures to clear a path.

===Continuing the journey===
Elsewhere, [[Looti Vario]] leads the Lawsons and [[2B0T]] ("Two Boots") through the forest towards their rendezvous point. Vario is contacted by [[Dryden Vos]] via [[comlink]]. Vos tells him that he and his companions are traveling through [[hyperspace]] towards [[Janix]]. Vario warns Vos that they ran into complications with the [[Galactic Empire]]. Vos warns Vario that he does not want any trouble with the Empire and tells him to ensure that Maul is at the extraction point. The pilot tells Vos they have to silence communications as they approach Janix. After Vos cuts off contact with Vario, the pilot warns that Maul's extraction could be risky. Vos responds that Maul will be a great asset in helping him take over the [[Crimson Dawn]]. He promises to share the profit. 

While Vario and his companions head towards the rendezvous point, Master Daki tells Izara that he believes their new adversary was a [[Sith Lord]]. When Izara asks how they will defeat this new foe, Maul tells her to let go of her restraint and to tap into her rage. He tells her to let her rage serve her. Daki counsels his Padawan against giving into such emotions and tells her to remain calm in the face of fear. He reminds her that true power lies in their patience and cunning. Maul counters that that their enemy knows how the Jedi fight and that they must adapt to the Sith. 

While Daki navigates through the ruined structure, Maul sits down on a staircase. He tells Izara that her Master would be wise to heed his advice. He says that he knows how to defeat their new enemy because he was trained by the most powerful Sith Lord, [[Darth Sidious]]. He urges Izara to listen to him, explaining that his master perpetrated the [[Great Jedi Purge]] and installed himself as [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]]. He counsels her to use her rage and to use her potential. Daki tells Izara to go ahead and find the Lawsons while he travels with Maul. 

===Fight and flight===
Once they are alone, Daki speaks with Maul. Maul says that the Sith Lord is powerful. Daki responds that they will be stronger if they defend one another. Maul disagrees with the Jedi philosophy, arguing that aggression is the only way. Daki continues his journey but the two are ambushed by Lord Vader, forcing them to resume the duel. 

Meanwhile, Izara reaches a ruined stone bridge with holes around it. She finds her path blocked by [[Marrok]] and the [[Eleventh Brother]]. Izara discards her robe and fights off the advancing Inquisitors, who pursue her across the bridge. Daki and Maul retreat while fending off Vader. They cross paths with Izara and the Inquisitors at the bridge. Master Daki passes [[Eeko-Dio Daki's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] to Izara, who uses it to fend off the Inquisitors' lightsaber attacks. The three soon find themselves surrounded by the Inquisitors and Vader. Resuming the fight, Maul takes on Vader while the Jedi take on the Inquisitors. 

Elsewhere, the Lawsons, Two Boots and Vario encounter more [[TK stormtrooper]]s and a parked [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class shuttle]] in the fog. They engage the stormtroopers, who deploy an [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]]. As the fugitives retreat back into the forest, the [[Stormtrooper Commander]] orders the shuttle crew to take off. While the Jedi and Maul fight the Inquisitors and Vader, the other fugitives duck in the forest while stormtroopers search for them. The Imperial shuttle assists in the manhunt by strafing the forest below.

===Fighting back===
While Izara is distracted by the sight of the ''Lambda''-class shuttle flying over the forest, Marrok hurls [[Marrok's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] at her but Master Daki intercepts the blade. Meanwhile, Two Boots convinces the others to use the Imperial blaster cannon to take down the shuttle. He and Brander convince a reluctant Vario to serve as a decoy while they take over the Imperial blaster cannon. Back at the ruined structure, Daki manages to trick Marrok into falling off the cliff. Izara fights with the Eleventh Brother and dodges a blow by Vader. 

Brander tells Rylee to hide in the bushes while Vario distracts the Imperials manning the blaster cannon. Two Boots takes over the cannon while Brander shoots the stormtroopers nearby. Rylee manages to shoot a stormtrooper and Brander joins his son in the gunfight. The shuttle begins strafing the Lawsons but Two Boots manages to use the blaster cannon to hit one of the shuttle's rear engines, causing it to crash into the ground and explode. 

With Vader disconcerted by the loss of his transport, Daki and Maul press on their counter-attack. More Imperial reinforcements arrive in two [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] (LAAT/i) gunships, which pursue Vario. Two Boots manages to shoot down one of the gunships before the second gunship destroys the blaster cannon. Brander stays behind and tells Two Boots to stay with Rylee and Vario. Daki and Maul continue fighting Vader but are no match for the Sith Lord's mastery of the Force. Vader hurls them down. Daki resumes his battle with Vader while Maul suffers from his cybernetic leg injury. Desperate Maul Force pushes Daki closer to Lord Vader and retreats across the bridge. 

Daki fights the Sith Lord alone, using his lightsaber skills and Force abilities to keep the Sith Lord at bay. Izara manages to escape the Eleventh Brother and watches as Lord Vader stabs Master Daki through the chest. The Jedi Master breathes his last and close his eyes. Enraged by the [[death]] of her master, Izara attacks the Eleventh Brother with her lightsaber but he hurls her aside with the Force, separating her from her lightsaber. As the Inquisitor faces Izara, Maul throws her one of his lightsabers. She fights back and manages to drive the Inquisitor back. Maul comes to her aid and the two fight the Inquisitor.

===The rescue and turning points===
Shortly later, Dryden Vos arrives in his starship and shoots down the remaining LAAT/i gunship. His ship lands near the forest. While Vos and one his crew exchange fire with stormtroopers, Vario runs up the gangplank and tells Vos that Maul is behind him. Via [[comlink]], Rylee tells his father that he is pinned down. Brander tells Rylee that he loves him and instructs Two Boots to get his son to [[Drea Lawson|his mother]]. Making a last stand, Brander attacks the stormtroopers with a [[repeating blaster]] while Rylee and Two Boots flee aboard Vos's ship. Rylee screams in shock and despair while Two Boots leads him aboard the ship. 

Meanwhile in the structure, Izara and Maul continue their fight against the Eleventh Brother and Lord Vader. Before Vader can finish off Izara, Maul uses the Force to bring down the temple. While Vader uses the Force to shield himself from the debris, Maul tells Izara that they need to go. Izara wants vengeance against the Inquisitor and the Sith Lord but Maul warns that she is not yet strong enough. He promises to train her and vows they will have their revenge. They flee before the structure collapses into ruins. 

Running through the forest, they evade the stormtroopers and board Vos's ship. While Vader watches, Vos's starship flees into space and jumps into [[hyperspace]], eluding a pair of LAAT/i gunships. A sobbing Izara bangs on a door while Two Boots comforts the grieving Rylee. Inside the cockpit, Vos tells Maul that he has upheld his side of the bargain. Maul promises to do the same and exits the cockpit, while a wary Vario watches him. Back in the passenger compartment, Izara eyes a lightsaber intently. Maul sits beside her. Making eye contact, Izara tells Maul that she is ready to become his apprentice. 

==Development== 
The episode was originally announced on [[March 17]], 2026 by the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' Twitter account as simply "'''Chapter 10: Finale'''."<ref name="Twitter" /> In an interview with [[Wikipedia:ComicBook.com|''ComicBook.com'']], executive producer [[Matt Michnovetz]] hinted that Finale was not the actual title,<ref name="ComicBook.com">{{OfficialYouTube|video=n54CgjMRY-o|text=The STRONGEST Darth Maul You've EVER Seen! Sam Witwer & The Maul: Shadow Lord Cast Interview!|channel=@Comicbook|name=[[Wikipedia:ComicBook.com|''ComicBook.com'']]|archivedate=20260504212111}}</ref> and the official title The Dark Lord was later revealed in the episode itself.<ref name="Episode" /> On [[Disney+]], the episode list originally called it simply "'''Episode 10'''" before being updated on [[May 6]].<ref name="Disney+">{{DisneyPlus|url=browse/entity-9ba0f41b-6173-4ff1-8967-71cad0620244|text=Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord|archivedate=20260504175736}}</ref>

The different title was done so as to not spoil the appearance of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Episode" />
