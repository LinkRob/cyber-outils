'''Austin Walker''' is an author who contributed a short story to the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''. He features on a ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' podcast called ''[https://amorecivilizedage.net A More Civilized Age]'' with Ali Acampora, Natalie Watson, and, host, Rob Zacny.
