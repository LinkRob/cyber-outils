"'''Backdraft'''" was the nickname of a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the [[First Order]].<ref name="Starfighter Missions" /> Backdraft served as a [[First Order Special Forces TIE fighter pilot|Special Forces pilot]] who flew one of the elite [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s.<ref name="Note" /> As a [[pilot]] of the [[First Order starfighter corps]], Backdraft was equipped with the fighter pilot uniform,<ref name="Starfighter Missions" /> and was described as a fiery fanatic. At one point, Backdraft flew near a [[star]] in his fighter.<ref name="X-wing">{{FFGXW2|set=First Order Conversion Kit|cardname="Backdraft"}}</ref> He had light skin and [[Color|black]] [[hair]].<ref name="Starfighter Missions" />
