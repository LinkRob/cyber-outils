'''Angel''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[pirate]] aboard the [[freighter]] ''[[Dromedar]]''. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Angel and other pirates, under the command of [[Nevil Cygni]], deceived [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] and other crew members aboard the ''[[Blood Crow]]'' into boarding the ''Dromedar''. This enabled the pirates to take the boarding crew hostage.<ref name="Thrawn" />
