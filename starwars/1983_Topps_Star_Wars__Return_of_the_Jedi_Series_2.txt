'''1983 Topps ''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'' Series 2''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] in [[1983]].<ref name="Volume Three" />

The set was based on [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], and featured stills from the film and promotional photos, all within a blue and yellow border. The backs of the cards featured "Star Quiz" trivia questions from the original trilogy of movies. A sub-set of sticker cards were included, the backs of which included a portion of a larger image that could be pieced together with other cards like a puzzle.<ref name="Volume Three" />

Card numbering in the set continued from the [[1983 Topps Star Wars: Return of the Jedi Series 1|previous set]].<ref name="Volume Three" />

==Continuity==
Card 150 features an image from a deleted scene in which [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Commander/Legends|Commander]] [[Tiaan Jerjerrod/Legends|Jerjerrod]] is ordered to destroy the [[Endor/Legends|moon of Endor]].<ref name="Blu-Ray">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''</ref> An image from another deleted scene featuring Darth Vader walking through a Death Star II corridor with officers and stormtroopers is featured on card 206.
