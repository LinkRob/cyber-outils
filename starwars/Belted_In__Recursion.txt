"'''Belted In: Recursion'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year three of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the third adventure in the [[Belted In trilogy]] and was written by [[Christy Mullins]] and [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] and edited by [[Jae Walker]].
