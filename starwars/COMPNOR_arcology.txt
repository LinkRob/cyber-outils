The '''COMPNOR arcology''' was a labyrinthine building on [[Coruscant|Imperial Center]], the [[capital]] [[Planet|world]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> It was located in the planet's [[Federal District]], on [[Level 5127]],<ref name="PressPin" /> and was considered one of the points of the district's "supreme triangle" alongside the [[Imperial Palace]] and [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|Naval Intelligence headquarters]]. It housed several Imperial organizations, including the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]] and the [[Ubiqtorate]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> Five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Minister of Information]] of the Galactic Empire, [[Pollux Hax]], maintained an office there.<ref name="PressPin" />

After the end of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[New Republic]] continued to use the former ISB headquarters and used it to house the [[Municipal Records]]. Former [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service]] operatives also data-stripped the entire building to uncover all of the ISB's secrets.<ref name="GT:AFitC" />
