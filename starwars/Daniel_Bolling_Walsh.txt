'''Daniel Bolling Walsh''' (born in [[Wikipedia:Mona Vale, New South Wales|Mona Vale, New South Wales]], [[Wikipedia:Australia|Australia]]) is an [[Wikipedia:Australians|Australian]] concept artist and illustrator.<ref name="Facebook" /> For the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, he created the cover art for [[Claudia Gray]]'s [[2017]] young-adult novel ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''<ref name="Leia">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> and [[Leia, Princess of Alderaan (audiobook)|its audiobook adaptation]].<ref name="Audiobook">[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan (audiobook)|''Leia, Princess of Alderaan'' audiobook]]</ref> Bolling Walsh considered working on the cover art to be an honor and described the initial versions of the cover to be "illustrative" before the project morphed into its final version.<ref name="Instagram" />
