'''Almu Redondo''' served as the art director of the animated short "[[Screecher's Reach (short film)|Screecher's Reach]]," the second episode of [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|Volume 2]] of the anthology series ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]''. It was produced by [[Cartoon Saloon]], directed by [[Paul Young]], and premiered on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="Screecher">{{Visions|Screecher's Reach (short film)}}</ref>
