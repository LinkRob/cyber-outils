The '''Clan of the Toribota''' was a nomadic enclave native to the [[planet]] [[Isde Naha]]. After astronomical conjecture pointed the enclave to the [[desert]] [[moon]] of [[Jedha]], they believed the [[star]] in the system to be the [[First Light (star)|First Light]], the first star glimpsed by their early ancestors. [[Omisha Joyo]], a member of the Clan of the Toribota,<ref name="UVG" /> was present in [[Jedha City]] shortly before it was [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Rogue One" />
