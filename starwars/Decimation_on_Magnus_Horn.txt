The '''decimation on Magnus Horn''' took place<ref name="TC" /> sometime prior to [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> where the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] suffered heavy casualties on the terrestrial astronomical object [[Magnus Horn]]. After the decimation, the Alliance tried to reassign the surviving [[trooper]]s to other units, but the 61st's [[captain]], [[Micha Evon]], fought to keep his [[soldier]]s together and to save the [[company]] from obliteration.<ref name="TC" />

In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> after Evon and some of his [[Alliance officer|officers]] were [[Death|killed]], the [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldier]] [[Hazram Namir]] compared the losses to the decimation on Magnus Horn and the [[Massacre at Asyrphus|massacre]] at the terrestrial world [[Asyrphus]], both of which he viewed as a blow to the company. The decimation on Magnus Horn and Evon's actions in the wake of it were also brought up by [[Pol Andrissus]], another trooper of the 61st, in a conversation during the captain's [[funeral]].<ref name="TC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The decimation on Magnus Horn was mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="TC" />
