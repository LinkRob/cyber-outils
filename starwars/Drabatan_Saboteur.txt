'''Drabatan Saboteurs''' were [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[Drabatan]] [[soldier]]s for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. They fought during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], and were known as fierce [[commando]]s that charged into combat yelling full-throated war cries at their enemies. These [[saboteur]]s utilized [[boiler rifle]]s and [[sonic grenade]]s to create micro-fractures in enemy defenses.<ref name="SWC" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Commander}}
In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'' to [[0 ABY]].</ref> Drabatan Saboteurs were trained by a [[Tatooine rebel cell|rebel cell]] originating on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] led by [[Colonel]] [[Jennica Pierce]] against [[Captain]] [[Kosh]]'s [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]]. They were stationed across multiple worlds, including Tatooine, [[Er'Kit]], [[Dandoran]], [[Yavin 4]], [[Sullust]], [[Hoth]], and [[Takodana]].<ref name="SWC" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Drabatan Saboteurs first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'', a [[2014]] mobile strategy [[Star Wars video games|game]] by [[Disney Interactive]].<ref name="SWC" /> The unit was introduced in the second wave of content released on [[December 1]], [[2016]] based on the [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' that released on [[December 16]] of the same year.<ref name="ROExpansion">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@disneygames|name=Disney Games|video=aQgKNcQaZCU|text=Star Wars: Commander Rogue One Expansion #2}}</ref> In the game, Drabatan Saboteurs were bruiser units that could be trained through the [[cantina]] by players affiliated with the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="SWC" /> Concept art for the Drabatan Saboteur was created by [[Matthew Elser]] and was based on the Drabatan commando [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek|Paodok'Draba'Takat]]<ref name="ElserArtstation">{{ArtStation|username=mattelser|url=artwork/39n06g|name=[[Matthew Elser]]|text=Star Wars: Commander - Rogue One Vehicle & Character Concepts|date=2023-12-21}}</ref> from ''Rogue One''.<ref name="ROmovie">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
