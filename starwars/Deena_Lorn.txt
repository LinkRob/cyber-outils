==Behind the scenes==
Deena Lorn is a character who appeared in the short story "[[The Witness]]," written by [[Adam Christopher]] and published in the collection ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]'' in [[2020]].<ref name="FACPOV" /> Christopher created Lorn's stormtrooper designation as a reference to the writer [[Bria LaVorgna]],<ref name="Twitter 1">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/1313517111898234883|[[Adam Christopher]]|quote=YOU CANON NOW. {{C|In response to "THAT'S MY TKID"}}|archivedate=20201006194559}}</ref> who identifies as TK-27342 in the [[501st Legion (fan organization)|501st Legion]] fan organization.<ref name="Twitter 2">{{Twitter|chaosbria|status/798982959566983168|[[Bria LaVorgna]]|quote=I'm now ID-27342. I will give a gold star to whoever figures out why I picked those numbers. (Don't say if I already told you.)|archivedate=20201006194931}}</ref>
