The '''FidoNet Star Wars Echo''' was a [[Wikipedia:FidoNet|FidoNet]] "echomail" message forum distributed on [[Wikipedia:Bulletin board system|bulletin board systems]] in the 1990s. Discussing [[George Lucas]]'s ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga and pre-dating the modern [[Wikipedia:Internet|Internet]], it was one of the earliest influential forms of Star Wars on-line [[Wikipedia:Fandom|fandom]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://theforce.net/jedicouncil/editorials/050901.shtml|text=In The Beginning&hellip; Star Wars Comes To A Wired World|author=Chris Knight|date=2001-05-09|work=[[TheForce.net]]|archivedate=20200112185233}}</ref>

==History==
Founded on [[February 25]], [[1991]] by Eli "Skip" Shayotovich, the Star Wars Echo quickly became one of the most popular forums within FidoNet. Launched a few months before [[Star Wars: The Thrawn Trilogy|''The Thrawn Trilogy'']] novels, the Echo was uniquely placed to become a focal point of online ''Star Wars'' fandom just as the [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] was coming into focus, and leading up to the announcement of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|Episode I]] and discussion of the [[prequel trilogy]] of films.<ref name="OutOfTheMaw1">{{WebCite|url=http://www.theforce.net/jedicouncil/editorials/OutOfTheMaw1.txt|text=''Out of the Maw'' (Vol. 1, No. 1)|author=Mike Schwab (Ed.)|date=1994-11-01|work=[[TheForce.net]]|archivedate=20200112185149}}</ref>

Overall usage statistics are unknown, but it is known that the Echo received an average of 60 to 70 message posts per day between November 1994 and July 1995.<ref name="OutOfTheMaw2">{{WebCite|url=http://www.theforce.net/jedicouncil/editorials/OutOfTheMaw2.txt|text=''Out of the Maw'' (Vol. 1, No. 2)|author=Mike Schwab (Ed.)|date=1995-12-01|work=[[TheForce.net]]|archivedate=20200112185141}}</ref>

Echo moderator Skip Shayotovich later served double-duty as president of the America Online Star Wars Fan Club on [[Wikipedia:AOL|AOL]], but was unsuccessful in having his personal BBS, "Star Wars Central", officially sanctioned by Lucasfilm.<ref name="OutOfTheMaw1" />

==Contributors==
Noteworthy participants in the Echo message forum included ''Star Wars'' authors [[Kevin J. Anderson]] (who included [[Foreign Intruder Defense Organism|FIDO]] as a reference to FidoNet in ''[[Champions of the Force]]''<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.echostation.com/echo2/2-kja.htm|text="The Pen is Mightier than the Saber"|author=Dave Phillips (Ed.)|date=1995|work=Echo Station|archivedate=20160305015342}}</ref> ), [[Roger MacBride Allen]], [[Brian Daley]], [[Barbara Hambly]], [[Lora Johnson]], [[Vonda N. McIntyre]], [[L. Neil Smith]], [[Kathy Tyers]], [[Tom Veitch]], [[Daniel Wallace]], [[Timothy Zahn]], and occasional [[Lucasfilm]] representatives.<ref name="OutOfTheMaw2" />

Other participants included people who worked for [[West End Games]] (creators of [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]), such as [[Sterling Hershey]], and for [[Dark Horse Comics]], such as [[Chris Gossett]].<ref>{{Blog|silverforce|14|Tangents and the Fidonet Star Wars Echo}}</ref>

==Achievements==
In November [[1994]], the Echo launched a monthly newsletter/[[Wikipedia:fanzine|fanzine]] entitled ''Out of the Maw'', which was also distributed on America Online and Prodigy. Echo member Rich Mason also compiled his own fanzine, entitled ''Starfighter Command''.<ref name="OutOfTheMaw1" />

The Echo pioneered a parallel message forum devoted to playing [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]] on-line.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.theforce.net/jedicouncil/editorials/OutOfTheMaw3.txt|text=''Out of the Maw'' (Vol. 1, No. 3)|author=Mike Schwab (Ed.)|date=1995-03-01|work=[[TheForce.net]]|archivedate=20200112185152}}</ref><ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.echostation.com/roleplay/echorpg.htm|text=The Phantom RPG Menace|author=David O. Little|date=1999-03-06|work=Echo Station|archivedate=20160305005955}}</ref>

Due to the participation of early Star Wars authors, as well as people who worked for Lucasfilm, West End Games and Dark Horse Comics, Echo members have been credited with helping to flesh out some of the early [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] material.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.geocities.com/jjehrnwa/internet/ootm4.txt|text=''Out of the Maw'' (Vol. 1, No. 4)|author=Mike Schwab (Ed.)|date=1995-07-01|work=Star Wars Internet Archive|archivedate=20030415151822}}</ref>

In late 1994, some members of the Echo attempted to compile a [[Wikipedia:CD-ROM|CD-ROM]] containing all of the ''Star Wars'' digital media&mdash;images, sound files, text documents, etc.&mdash;that were available online at that time (very little by modern standards). It was a non-profit endeavor, asking members to pay only the cost of the blank CD-ROM. However, when members approached Lucasfilm's licensing department for permission to create the CDs, that permission was denied, and the CD-ROM was never distributed.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.geocities.com/jjehrnwa/internet/ootm5.txt|text=''Out of the Maw'' (Vol. 1, No. 5)|author=Mike Farnham (Ed.)|date=1995-09-01|work=Star Wars Internet Archive|archivedate=20041225125429}}</ref> This attempt by the fans did encourage [[LucasArts]] to create some early commercial ''Star Wars'' media compilations&mdash;such as [[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]&mdash;and it was also the initial impetus for Lucasfilm to begin thinking seriously about how to interact with networked online fans, and to develop a strategy to deal with the protection of their [[Wikipedia:intellectual property|intellectual property]] online.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://theforce.net/jedicouncil/editorials/050901.shtml|text=In The Beginning&hellip; Star Wars Comes To A Wired World|author=Chris Knight|date=2001-05-09|work=[[TheForce.net]]|archivedate=20200112185233}}</ref>

A letter from Shayotovich was printed in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Dark Empire 1|''Dark Empire'' 1]]. A letter from co-moderator Mike Farnham about the FidoNet Star Wars Echo was printed in [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 2]].
