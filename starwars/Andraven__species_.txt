'''Andravens'''<ref name="Elephantine" /> were a [[Sentience|sentient]]<ref name="Snowy" /> [[elephantine]]<ref name="Elephantine" /> [[species]] who lived on the frigid [[planet]] [[Andraven]]. They had [[Color|gray]] or light blue colored skin. [[Gumar Adraki|Gumar]], [[Jam (individual)|Jam]], [[Varna (Andraven)|Varna]],<ref name="Snowy" /> and [[Onderrs]]<ref name="Circuit">{{YJA|The Andraven Circuit}}</ref> were members of this species.<ref name="Snowy" />

==History==
At some point, generations before [[232 BBY]], a group of Andravens settled on the hitherto uninhabited planet Andraven, where they cooperated to survive in its harsh environment. Their successes included utilization of [[heat stone]]s, which could be found near the roots of bramble-covered [[tree]]s, consumption of [[snowfig]]s, and the use of [[kao reed]]s to make [[Kao reed flute|flutes]]. The flutes allowed the early settlers of the planet to tame and befriend [[Zaba wolf|zaba wolves]], which were otherwise a hostile predator.<ref name="Circuit" />

By 232 BBY, Andraven children competed in the [[Andraven Ancestors Circuit]], a trial in which each child would have a single attempt to collect three items important to the ancestors who settled the planet. They had to collect heat stones, snowfigs, and kao reeds, the latter of which the child had to construct into a flute. By tradition, they were able to use the help of friends to complete the circuit.<ref name="Circuit" />
