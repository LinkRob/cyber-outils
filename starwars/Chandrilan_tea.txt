'''Chandrilan tea''' was a type of [[tea]] that shared its name with the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. The tea was [[Diet|consumable]] by [[human]]s and [[Umbaran]]s and could be kept stored aboard [[Starship|ships]]. In [[232 BBY]], the [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker]] [[Orla Jareni]] prepared herself some of the tea while traveling on the ''[[Vessel]]'', a [[transport]] ship. While traveling aboard the ''Vessel'' [[229 BBY|three years]] later, the [[pilot]] [[Affie Hollow]] drank some of the tea to help soothe her to [[sleep]]. In [[40 BBY]], Chandrilan tea was kept in the mess stores of the [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] [[Meryx (starship)|''Meryx'']] by the [[gemstone]] [[thief]] [[Rahara Wick]]. The tea was also served on the worlds [[Alderaan]] and [[Coruscant]] in [[3 BBY]].

==Description==
Chandrilan tea was a type of [[tea]] that was consumable by [[human]]s<ref name="Leia" /> and [[Umbaran]]s.<ref name="ITD" /> The beverage could be served [[Temperature|hot]] in mugs<ref name="MA">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> or cups and was also storable aboard [[Starship|ships]]. Chandrilan tea shared its name with the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]] and could be refreshed with heated [[water]].<ref name="Leia" />

==History==
[[File:OrlaJareni-ConceptArt.png|thumb|left|210px|Orla Jareni kept Chandrilan tea aboard the ''Vessel'' while traveling.]]
In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="ITD Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> Chandrilan tea was stored in the mess of the [[Byne Guild]] [[transport]] ship ''[[Vessel]]''. The [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker]] [[Orla Jareni]] prepared herself some of the beverage during her voyage aboard the ''Vessel'' from the Core Worlds planet [[Coruscant]] to the [[Galactic Frontier]].<ref name="ITD" /> While traveling aboard the ''Vessel''<ref name="ATF">{{StoryCite|story=After the Fall|book=The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref> in [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates [[Starlight Beacon]]'s [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|fall]] and the [[Guardian Protocols]]' implementation to [[230 BBY]] and [[229 BBY]], respectively, while [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|''Shadows of Starlight'' 1]] establishes that the Guardian Protocols' implementation happened one [[Standard week|week]] after the [[space station]]'s destruction. The section of "[[After the Fall]]" including the harbormaster's mention takes place one [[Standard month|month]] after Starlight Beacon's fall, equating to 229 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] per ''Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia''.</ref> one [[Standard month|month]] after [[Starlight Beacon]]'s [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|fall]] the [[pilot]] [[Affie Hollow]] kept some Chandrilan tea in the ship's mess. While speaking to her co-pilot [[Leox Gyasi]], Hollow reflected that he had likely witnessed her drinking the tea late at night in the ''Vessel''{{'s}} mess, hoping it would soothe her to [[sleep]].<ref name="ATF" />

[[Standard year|Years]] later, in [[40 BBY]],<ref name="MA Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> Chandrilan tea was kept aboard the [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] [[Meryx (starship)|''Meryx'']] for its pilot, [[gemstone]] [[thief]] and former [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] [[Slavery|enslaved]] worker [[Rahara Wick]]. The owner of the ''Meryx'', her colleague [[Pax Maripher]], made some of the tea for his partner while they assisted [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in locating the [[Opposition]] performance troupe during their [[Mission to Pijal|mission]] to the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] planet [[Pijal]]. Maripher's displeasure with the pair's presence aboard the [[cruiser]] led him to purposefully avoid offering the Jedi any of the beverage. [[Standard day|Days]] later, Wick poured herself a mug of the tea as she and Maripher waited for the pair of Jedi to return. On their next [[scout]]ing operation with Jinn and Kenobi, Wick was recaptured by Czerka at the company's [[Czerka facility|facility]] on [[Pijal's moon]]. Maripher mourned the loss of her presence aboard the ''Meryx'' with what she had left behind, including the Chandrilan tea in the mess store.<ref name="MA" />

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="LPOA Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] had a cup of steaming Chandrilan tea resting atop her desk while meeting with [[Alderaanian]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] in her offices on Coruscant. Days later, on the world of [[Alderaan]], [[Viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] discussed the aftermath of the [[Arrests on Christophsis|arrests]] of Imperial traitors made on the planet Christophsis with his [[House of Organa|family]] while a [[droid]] refreshed his Chandrilan tea with hot water.<ref name="Leia" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chandrilan tea first appeared in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="Leia" />
