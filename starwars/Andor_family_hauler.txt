The '''[[Andor family]]''' owned a [[FR-193D cargo transport|FR-193D]] '''hauler''' from at least [[24 BBY]]. By [[5 BBY]], it had become disused and was nonfunctional, but was kept by [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] as a hideaway.

==History==
===Salvage runs===
By [[24 BBY]],<ref name="24BBY" /> [[Marriage|spouses]] [[Clem Andor|Clem]] and [[Maarva Carassi Andor]] used the hauler to transport themselves around [[the galaxy]] on salvage runs.<ref name="Maarva DB">{{Databank|maarva-andor|Maarva Andor}}</ref> On one such trip, they traveled to the [[planet]] [[Kenari]], where they encountered the nine-year-old<ref name="Cassian DB">{{Databank|cassian-andor|Cassian Andor}}</ref> [[Kenari (people)|Kenari]] [[Gender|boy]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Kassa]] whom they later adopted and raised on their homeworld of [[Ferrix]].<ref name="Reckoning" />

===Retired on Ferrix===
[[File:Andor-hauler-wreck-TWBM.png|thumb|left|290x290px|The hauler in 5 BBY among piles of scrap.]]
By [[5 BBY]], the hauler was a wreck on Ferrix. Their adopted son Cassian would sleep in the wreck,<ref name="Kassa" /> on the same sleeping berth he once sat on when leaving Kenari.<ref name="Reckoning" /> He concealed a stolen [[Imperial N-S9 Starpath Unit]] in a hidden compartment before attempting to sell it to [[Luthen Rael]].<ref name="TWBM">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The transport first appeared as a wreck in "[[Kassa (episode)|Kassa]]," the first episode of [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="Kassa" /> It first appeared functionally in a flashback in "[[Reckoning (episode)|Reckoning]]," the third episode of the series.<ref name="Reckoning" />
