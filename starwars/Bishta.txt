'''Bishta''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]], who, along with his partner [[Argin]], planned to [[Thief|steal]] from the [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|antiquities store]] of the [[collector]] [[Dok-Ondar]]. When Bishta heard of the destruction of Dok-Ondar's [[security droid]] [[BK-86]], he contacted Argin, suggesting they carry out the job, but Argin informed him that the [[droid]] had been destroyed in a skirmish with [[Kase Finto]], who similarly attempted to steal from Dok-Ondar.<ref name="Datapad" />
