{{Quote|The Esseveya system is renowned for the pillars of light on display, produced by a combination of magnetic waves reacting to the elements in this particular part of the galaxy. It's a rare phenomenon and quite a treat.|Riyola Keevan|<ref name="Princess">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>}}
The '''Esseveya system''' was a [[star system]]<ref name="Princess" /> in the [[Maldrood sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that frequently was visited by [[meteor]] storms. The ''[[Halcyon]]'' passenger liner sometimes passed through the system to allow its passengers to view the light shows generated by magnetic fields. On trips in both [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], [[Captain]] [[Oswin Dicto]] took the ''Halcyon'' through the Esseveya system.<ref name="Princess" />
