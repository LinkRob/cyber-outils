The '''Chancellor's accommodation suite''' was the personal quarters reserved for the [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] in his [[Emperor's office|office suite]] in the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]]. It included a private [[refresher]] with a sink and [[shower]], a wardrobe with formal and ceremonial wear ready for use as well as an [[Alderaanian]] [[relaxa-bed]] that offered a full night's [[sleep|rest]] within a few [[standard hour|hours]]. The suite was accessible by an antechamber that also led to the [[Chancellor's Archive]] and a working office where a [[Red Guard]] monitored the office suite's [[security camera]]s.<ref name="CL2016" />
