A '''double jocimer'''<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> was a kind of musical instrument, often played by [[Bith]]. [[Musician]]s played it by blowing on its [[Jocimer reed|reed]]. [[Ickabel G'ont]] of the [[band]] [[Figrin D'an and the Modal Nodes]] played the double jocimer.<ref name="Not for Nothing" />
