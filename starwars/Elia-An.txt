The '''Elia-An''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Oanne]]. They had a [[Symbiosis|symbiotic]] [[Reproduction|gestation]] with the planet's [[nativity tree]]s; those who wished to be [[parent]]s connected with a [[Grandfather tree]] in order to find their [[seed-heart]] and pair with a younger [[tree]]. A [[Unidentified Ho'Din scientist|Ho'Din scientist]] who had studied the Elia-An described their relationship with the trees as [[seed]]s to be cross-pollinated, with communities drifting to new trees. The Elia-An possessed an extra set of [[Limb|arms]] with seven-fingered forehands that they used to hook into their nativity trees. During the [[High Republic Era]], the Elia-An made a trade deal with the [[Galactic Republic]] in return for study into their symbiotic gestation in the hope of determining an arrangement to allow them to travel offworld.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
