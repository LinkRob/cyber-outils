==History==
Enlavers prized the [[Wookiee]]s of [[Kashyyyk]] due to the [[species]]' long lifespan, strength, and keen intellect.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> When [[Asajj Ventress]] was an infant, she was taken by a [[Hal'Sted|enslaver]].<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref>

During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] restored the [[Zygerrian Slave Empire]] as a puppet state.<ref name="Escape from Kadavo">{{TCW|Escape from Kadavo}}</ref> With the backing of the [[Separatist Droid Army]], the [[Zygerrian]]s captured and [[Slavery|enslaved]] the [[Togruta]] people of the [[Kiros]] colony. A [[Mission to Zygerria|rescue mission]] was launched, consisting of [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]].<ref name="Slaves of the Republic">{{TCW|Slaves of the Republic}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the drafting for the young-adult novel ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'',<ref name="DragonCon" /> released on [[March 5]], [[2019]],<ref name="QHRelease">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/queens-shadow|text=Queen's Shadow}}</ref> author [[E. K. Johnston]] worked with an editor to ensure the novel's inclusion of the concept of enslavement was written to use active wording rather than object words, specifically in regard to referring to someone as an "enslaver" rather than "a slaver." The novel ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' by [[Claudia Gray]],<ref name="DragonCon" /> released on [[April 16]] of the same year,<ref name="MA Release">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/574531/master-and-apprentice-star-wars-by-claudia-gray/9780525619376|text=Master & Apprentice (Star Wars)}}</ref> similarly included slavery as a central theme in the work. Gray indicated that she also preferred using the terms "enslavement" and "slavers" unless there was sufficient context to justify other usages to remain sensititve to the issue. In the usages of "slave" and "slaver" that she did keep in ''Master & Apprentice'', Gray noted that she included them to not word the concept too softly, indicating that evil individuals would not care about objectifying individuals and thus would choose to refer to individuals working for them derogatorily.<ref name="DragonCon" />
