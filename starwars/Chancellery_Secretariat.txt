The '''Chancellery Secretariat''' was a suite of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Office of the Chancellor]] located beneath the [[Galactic Senate Chamber]] within the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]] of the [[Federal District]], on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. It housed the [[Chancellor's working office|Chancellor's "working office"]], which itself held the [[Chancellor's Podium]]. Supposedly the [[Supreme Chancellor]]'s place of work, the final Chancellor of the Republic, [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]],<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> operated instead from his [[Emperor's office|formal office suite]] at the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]].<ref name="CL2016" />
