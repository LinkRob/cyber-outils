The '''Chorgad system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Fei Hu sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square Q-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Chorgad]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bounty Hunters 3|''Bounty Hunters'' 3]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> a [[Bombing on Chorgad|bombing]] was carried out on Chorgad by a crew of the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate]], which resulted in the [[death]]s of [[soldier]]s of their rival, the [[Unbroken Clan]] criminal [[syndicate]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chorgad system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
