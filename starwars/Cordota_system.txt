The '''Cordota system''' was located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]],<ref name="TheSlice" /> within the [[Lantillian sector]]. It was situated in grid square P-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and connected via a [[hyperspace route]] to the [[Bracca system|Bracca]] and [[Lantillies system]]s.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> The [[star system]] contained the terrestrial celestial body [[Cordota]].<ref name="TRS" /> At the start of [[journalist]] [[Rhil Dairo]]'s career, in the [[High Republic Era]], she worked as a cub reporter covering [[micro-g basket]] weaving on Cordota at the behest of her [[Rhil Dairo's producer|producer]], among other stories.<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cordota system was first mentioned in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' on a [[The galaxy|galaxy]] map authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Christopher Ibbitt]],<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> which was released on [[June 17]], [[2025]].<ref name="Edelweiss">{{DK|url=us/book/9780593958605-star-wars-the-acolyte-visual-guide|text=Star Wars The Acolyte Visual Guide}}</ref> The system was identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]] of the same year&mdash;with both works being authored by Fry.<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
