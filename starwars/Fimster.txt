{{Quote|He sold me out. We got into trouble on Fimster. Left me for dead. But I don't need him. I'm going to get myself an army. Whatever it takes.|Andrik Keller, on the Frizznoth Nihil|<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />}}
'''Fimster''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> located in the [[Fimster system]] within the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] that was situated in grid square L-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It shared its name with the location known as the [[Fimster Quadrant]].<ref name="Lando's Luck">''[[Lando's Luck]]''</ref>

In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'' establishes that [[Andrik Keller]] and the [[Unidentified Frizznoth Nihil|Frizznoth Nihil]] got into trouble on Fimster between the [[Battle of Galov]] and the present-day events of the audio drama, both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[231 BBY]].</ref> as he sought to rescue his former [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] from [[Galactic Republic]] custody after the [[Battle of Galov]], the [[Er'Kit]] [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] [[Andrik Keller]] caught up with a [[Unidentified Frizznoth Nihil|Frizznoth Nihil]] who had also served in [[Lourna Dee's Tempest|Dee's Tempest]] on<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Cerea]]. Eventually, the two made their way to Fimster where they got into trouble, with the [[Frizznoth]] leaving Keller for [[Death|dead]]. The Er'Kit survived and left Fimster.<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Keller traveled to<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Sran">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Sran system]]</ref> [[Ash Worlds]] planet [[Sran]] and told the former Tempest Runner [[Pan Eyta]], who was also looking for Dee, about his [[time]] on Fimster.<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fimster was first mentioned in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', a [[2021]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The spelling of the celestial body's name was later confirmed with the release of the audio drama's [[The High Republic: Tempest Runner (script)|script]] in [[2022]].<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />
