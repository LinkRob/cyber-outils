'''Chikua City''' was a [[city]] on the [[Core Worlds|Core]] [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]]. The [[Stinger Mantis crew|''Stinger Mantis'' crew]] traveled to Chikua City to meet [[Qeris Lar]], a wealthy man using his [[Currency|money]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Latero]] [[Greez Dritus]] didn't like the city because of its lack of [[plant]]s.<ref name="Battle Scars" />
