'''Artists''' were individuals who created original works of art. In the [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Colonial Academy]] offered art classes.<ref name="Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> Following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Mandalorian]] artist and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Sabine Wren]] became notorious for [[painting]] rebellious [[graffiti]] as a form of [[propaganda]].<ref name="Sabine Sketchbook">''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]''</ref> [[Chef]]s sufficiently skilled at cooking considered their work to be art.<ref name="recipe">{{StoryCite|story=A Recipe for Death|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref>
