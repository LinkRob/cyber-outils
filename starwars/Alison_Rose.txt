'''Alison Rose''' is a British actress, playwright and screenwriter<ref name="Mandy" /> who portrays [[Lieutenant]] [[Draper]] in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="Credits">{{Film|IX}} credits</ref> She has also appeared in TV series such as ''[[Wikipedia:The Bill|The Bill]]'', ''[[Wikipedia:Mistresses (American TV series)|Mistresses]]'' and ''[[Wikipedia:Midsomer Murders|Midsomer Murders]]''.
