The '''First Order flametrooper''' was a variant of the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] utilized by the [[First Order]]. Utilizing [[D-93 Incinerator flamethrower|D-93 Incinerators]] to root out entrenched enemy positions and engulf any resistance, flametroopers were equipped with flame-proof armor and were feared for their ability to turn any battlefield into a blazing inferno.

==Description==
[[File:FlametrooperBack-SWBC44.png|thumb|left|200px|A flametrooper viewed from the back]]
Commonly known as "toasters," "hotheads," "roasters", or "burnouts" by [[Resistance]] fighters, First Order flametroopers were best used to flush out enemy entrenchments, with standard infantry firing on those fleeing the flames. One of the three main types of weapons specialists assigned to stormtrooper squads of ten, flametroopers and stormtroopers worked in tandem, as the faster stormtroopers could protect the slower and more methodical flametroopers while out in the field. Additionally, the flame created by the flametrooper required them to move at a slower pace to avoid entering their own blazes. When strategically deployed, they could deny cover to enemy forces along with supplying cover for allies with walls of flame that stormtrooper armor could withstand. In dry and flammable environments, flametrooper deployment could lead to a quick and decisive victory. Additionally, the flame itself was nearly inescapable when directed at an opponent.<ref name="Visual" />

A standard flametrooper was equipped with a [[D-93 Incinerator flamethrower|D-93 Incinerator]] that was a combination of a single, double-barreled [[D-93w flame projector gun]] and backpack that housed twin outer [[conflagrine-14]] fuel tanks and a central pressurized propellant tank. The suit included a reinforced [[cyramech]] helmet with minimized openings, a narrow slit visor to reduce weapon glare, and supplemental breathing tank linkages. Other suit features included a temperature control [[body glove]], integrated knee covers, articulated greaves for greater foot movement, and positive grip boots.<ref name="Visual" /> Flametrooper officers were equipped with a [[pauldron]] that was situated on the right side of the [[armor]].<ref name="Age of Resistance" />

==History==
Based on the millennia-old infantry mainstay of [[Flamethrower|incendiary weaponry]], modernized by [[Mandalorian]] [[Mandalorian super commando|super commandos]] and [[clone trooper]]s during the [[Clone Wars]], the flametroopers of the First Order would continue the practice of utilizing flame-based weaponry to crush opposition with some of the latest technology available to the First Order's martial forces.<ref name="Visual" />

==="Infestation" on Starkiller Base===
[[File:BrayFlamthrower-AORFinn1.png|thumb|right|300px|Captain Bray uses his flamethrower against the creatures.]]
On [[Starkiller Base]], flametroopers and [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] led by [[Captain]] [[Bray (stormtrooper)|Bray]] eliminated a so-called "infestation," which turned out to be a habitat of native [[species]].<ref name="Age of Resistance" />

===Mission to Ovanis===
Flametroopers were deployed on a [[mission to Ovanis]] under the command of [[Agent]] [[Terex]]. There, they torched an egg sacred to [[Crèche]] in an attempt to extract information on [[Operation: Sabre Strike]] from [[Poe Dameron]], but the intense heat made the egg hatch instead of hurting the [[Unidentified Crèche creature|creature]] within.<ref name="PD 2" /><ref name="PD 3">[[Poe Dameron 3|''Poe Dameron'' 3]]</ref>

===Attack on Tuanul===
[[File:FlametrooperTuanul-Databank.png|thumb|300px|Flametroopers devastate the village of Tuanul on Jakku.|left]]
Flametroopers were used in the [[Attack on Tuanul|subjugation]] of the [[Sacred Villages|spiritual village]] [[Tuanul]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. They set fire to huts that housed members of the [[Church of the Force]], including that of [[Lor San Tekka]].<ref name="TFA" />

===Attack on the Resistance fleet===
Captain [[Phasma]] led a squad of stormtroopers and officers to the location of hacking, where [[Finn]], [[Rose Tico]] and [[DJ]] were about to sabotage. Phasma held both Finn and Rose in the [[hangar]] bay of the ship, revealing to them that their accomplice DJ had traded information with the First Order that allowed the escaping [[shuttle]]s from the ''[[Raddus]]'' to be targeted and [[Battle of Oetchi|destroyed]]. Phasma brought out [[First Order stormtrooper executioner|stormtrooper executioners]] believing that death by [[blaster]] was too painless. Among the stormtroopers present in the hanger were the flametroopers. Phasma was about to order their execution when the hangar bay suddenly exploded in light and fire, killing most of the personnel stationed in the hangar.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Cake?! Let me light the candles!|A Flametrooper, during the Battle of Takodana|<ref name="LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>}}
First Order Flametroopers appear in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. Flametroopers can be seen in [[Starkiller Base]] among standard First Order troopers. For the costumes used for the film, the troopers' armor and helmet were made of molded [[Wikipedia:Polyurethane|polyurethane]], with their leather and cotton gloves also included polyurethane elements. Their collars were built from neoprene and rubber, with gussets made from both cotton lycra and molded rubber. Other elements include rubber belts, cotton lycra undergarments, leather boots, and a backpack and gun, which were 3D-printed and feature aluminum metal work.<ref name="SW7N">{{WebCite|url=https://www.starwarsnewsnet.com/2015/04/closer-look-at-the-force-awakens-flametrooper-snowtrooper-and-more.html|text=Closer look at ''The Force Awakens'' flametrooper, snowtrooper and more!|work=[https://www.starwarsnewsnet.com Star Wars News Network]|archivedate=20170607072928}}</ref>
