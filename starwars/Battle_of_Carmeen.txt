The '''Battle of Carmeen''' was a battle that occurred on [[Carmeen]] before the [[Imperial Era]] that resulted in the death of the majority of the [[Carmeenian]]s involved. [[Director of Advanced Weapons Research|Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] studied the battle during his time at the [[ISB Academy and Offices|ISB Academy]]. [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] had been taught that the battle was in fact a systematic execution.<ref name="What a Festive Evening" />

By [[3 BBY]], [[Davo Sculdun]], a [[Chandrilan]] businessman had several items pertaining to Carmeenian culture as well as the Battle of Carmeen in particular within [[Davo Sculdun's gallery|his private gallery]] on [[Coruscant]]. One piece, the [[Lost Tairn]], was left behind during the battle. During a [[Senate Investiture]] [[Davo Sculdun's party|party]] hosed by Sculdun, Mothma and Krennic verbally sparred about the battle.<ref name="What a Festive Evening" />
