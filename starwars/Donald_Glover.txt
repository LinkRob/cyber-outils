'''Donald Glover''' (born [[September 25]], [[1983]]),<ref name="This Week Lightsaber" /> also known by the stage name of '''Childish Gambino''',<ref name="StageName">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vulture.com/2019/07/how-donald-glover-became-a-quintuple-threat.html|text=Tracing the Career Arc of Donald Glover, a Bonafide Quintuple Threat|author=Jones, Nate|date=2019-07-11|work=[[Wikipedia:New York (magazine)#Digital expansion and destination sites|''Vulture'']]|archivedate=20220119080806}}</ref> is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] actor, comedian, singer, writer, producer, director, rapper, and DJ who played [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Lando">{{WebCite|url=http://www.thewrap.com/han-solo-film-casting-young-lando-calrissian-exclusive|text='Han Solo' Film Is Casting Young Lando Calrissian (Exclusive)|author=Gonzalez, Umberto|date=2016-09-11|work=[[Wikipedia:TheWrap|''TheWrap'']]|archivedate=20210924015202}}</ref> Prior to the release of the film, he also played the character in a ''[[Saturday Night Live]]'' skit.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://variety.com/2018/tv/news/saturday-night-live-snl-donald-glover-1202752455|text='Saturday Night Live' Books Donald Glover as Host and Musical Guest|author=Turchiano, Danielle|date=2018-04-14|work=[[Wikipedia:Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|archivedate=20180625105902}}</ref> Glover will return to the role of Lando Calrissian in the upcoming film [[Lando (film)|''Lando'']], which he will also co-write alongside [[Stephen Glover]].<ref name="ATL">{{WebCite|url=https://abovetheline.com/2023/07/27/donald-glover-lando-tv-show-stephen-glover-justin-simien-disney-plus|text=Exclusive: Donald Glover and Stephen Glover to Write Lucasfilm's Lando Series as Justin Simien Exits|author=Jeff Sneider|date=2023-07-27|work=''Above The Line''|archivedate=20230727231158}}</ref>
