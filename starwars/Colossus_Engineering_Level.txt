The '''Engineering Level''' was located on the [[supertanker fuel depot]] known as the ''[[Colossus]]'', and was the area of the [[Space station|station]] in which the [[Chelidae]] resided. After being rescued from the [[First Order]] by [[Kazuda Xiono]], [[refugee]]s from [[Tehar]] known as [[Kel]] and [[Eila]] also resided in the Engineering Level.<ref name="The Children from Tehar" />
