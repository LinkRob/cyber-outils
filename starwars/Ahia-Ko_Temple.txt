'''Ahia-Ko Temple''' was a temple on the slopes of [[Mount Selasa]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]] built by the [[Ahia-Ko]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Sometime before or during [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the materials to add a [[Color|carmine]] coating to a [[blaster pistol]] were left in a container in the temple.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During 3 ABY,<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] visited the temple and retrieved the materials.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ahia-Ko Temple first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The location contained one of the paint schemes that players could use to customize the appearance of their blaster pistol.<ref name="Outlaws" />
