'''Erub II''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="FACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=Tooth and Claw|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> in the [[Erub system]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where, by [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Tooth and Claw]]" take place in parallel to the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] had found the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] trawling the sewage seas in search of spare parts. Bossk nearly succeeded in capturing the pair in order to claim the [[bounty]] on their heads, but ultimately failed.<ref name="FACPOV" />
