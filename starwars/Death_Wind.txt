The '''Death Wind''' were an execution [[squad]] which attacked the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] alike during the [[Clone Wars]]. They shared their name with the [[Death Wind Corridor|Death Wind]] [[spice]] trade corridor. They were founded by the [[Roonian]] [[soldier]] [[Mekedrix]], who went rogue from the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] due to trauma he suffered during the war.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />

==History==
After participating in the disastrous [[Battle of Abrion Bridge]], [[Commander]] Mekedrix went on to distinguish himself during the [[Battle of Ryloth]] and the [[Battle of Saleucami]]. However, he was deeply traumatized by the horrors of war. Following the loss of the [[Temple of Eedit]] on [[Devaron]], he deserted from the Galactic Republic. Believing that war is merely pretense and the true purpose of existence is to kill and to die, he founded the Death Wind, a cult dedicated to the worship of death, naming it after a trade corridor that passed by Roon.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />

The Death Wind established an outpost in the ruins of an ancient [[Sith]] [[Ando Sith temple|Temple]] from the [[Jedi-Sith War]] in the swamplands of Ando. They attacked both sides of the war indiscriminately, ambushing detachments of [[clone trooper]]s, killing high-ranking members of various separatist councils, and slaughtering the residents of a [[Commerce Guild]] hub on [[Minntooine]] despite it not being a military target.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />

Three months after the attack on Minntooine, the Republic discovered the Death Wind's location and secretly sent [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] to eliminate Mekedrix. Near the ruins of the Sith Temple, they were confronted by concealed members of the Death Wind, who confiscated their [[lightsaber]]s. Inside the Temple, the two confronted Mekedrix, who now wielded a sword made from the bones of a clone trooper.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />

Obi-Wan asked Mekedrix to surrender and come with them, but Mekedrix did not believe he committed any crimes. He explained that he showed the people he killed "the truth", explaining that death was the ultimate goal of existence and that the clones, the Confederacy, and the Jedi ultimately all served death. However, Obi-Wan countered that it is life that gives the universe meaning and reminded Mekedrix of his friends and the beauty of sunrise on his homeplanet. Apparently angered, Mekedrix attacked Obi-Wan, who closed his eyes and accepted his fate. However, Anakin retrieved [[Skywalker lightsaber|his lightsaber]] using the [[The Force|Force]] and impaled Mekedrix. With his last breath, Mekedrix apologized, saying that it seemed like the quickest way back to the sunrise.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />
