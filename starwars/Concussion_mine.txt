A '''concussion mine''' was a type of [[space mine]]. It was typically deployed as an anti-[[starfighter]] measure, and used potent explosives to create shockwaves capable of breaking open [[starship]] [[hull]]s. Concussion [[mine]]s were sold for 500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

==Behind the scenes==
Concussion mines were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']],<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> a [[2017]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="A Fistful of Credits">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/2/2/a-fistful-of-credits|text=A Fistful of Credits}}</ref> They [[Concussion mine/Legends|originated]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, and were first mentioned in ''[[The Free-Trader's Guide to Sevarcos]]'',<ref name="Guide to Sevarcos">{{JournalCite|2|The Free-Trader's Guide to Sevarcos}}</ref> a [[1994]] roleplaying game article written by [[Tony Russo|Anthony P. Russo]] for the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 2|second issue]] of the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal]], published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="cargo bay">{{CargoBay|url=item-detail/7125|text=Adventure Journal No. 2}}</ref>
