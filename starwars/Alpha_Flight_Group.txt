'''Alpha Flight Group''', also known as '''Alpha Group''', was a [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Starfighter|fighter]] [[flight group]] in the service of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Description==
{{Quote|Alpha Flight Group, come in. Please provide TIE interceptor support.|An Imperial, to Alpha Flight Group|<ref name="Battlefront" />}}
Alpha Flight Group, also known simply as Alpha Group, was a [[flight group]] within the [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] that utilized [[TIE series|TIE fighter series]] [[starfighter]]s including [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s and [[TIE/IN interceptor]]s. It consisted of at least ten members who used numbered [[callsign]]s, including [[Alpha Two]], [[Alpha Three (Galactic Empire)|Alpha Three]], [[Alpha Five]], [[Alpha Seven]], [[Alpha Eleven]], [[Alpha Twelve]], [[Alpha Thirteen]], [[Alpha Fourteen]], [[Alpha Fifteen]], [[Alpha Sixteen]].<ref name="Battlefront" />

==History==
{{Quote|Alpha Group, please respond. Air support requested.|Alpha Flight Group is asked for air support|<ref name="Battlefront" />}}
Alpha Flight Group participated in the widespread [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
