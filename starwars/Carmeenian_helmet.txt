A '''Carmeenian helmet''' was a type of [[helmet]] used by the [[Carmeenian]]s.<ref name="What a Festive Evening" />

By [[3 BBY]], [[Davo Sculdun]], a [[Chandrilan]] businessman had acquired a Carmeenian helmet and displayed it in [[Davo Sculdun's gallery|his personal gallery]] on [[Coruscant]]. The helmet was dated to a few [[Standard century|centuries]] after the [[Grand Conflict]].<ref name="What a Festive Evening" />
