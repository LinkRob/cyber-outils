The '''Anooba Archer''' was a costume owned by the [[Iridonian]] [[Zabrak]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Nox]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Wild Archer|standard costume]]. This full body outfit included a spike-covered hood, [[helmet|mask]], and armor plating seemingly made from [[anooba]] [[bone]]s, including a [[pauldron]] resembling an anooba's upper skull.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Anooba Archer was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Nox, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Anooba Bow]].<ref name="Hunters" />
