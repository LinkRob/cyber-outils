The '''Battle of Tansarii Point Station''' was a battle involving [[M3-A Scyk interceptor]]s at [[Tansarii Point Station]] which saw the defeat of [[Talonbane Cobra]], one of [[Black Sun]]'s [[ace]] [[pilot]]s. With Cobra's defeat, the [[Car'das]] [[smuggler]]s present managed to gain the upper hand and turn the tide of the battle. In its aftermath, the battle's survivors were respected throughout the [[sector]] Tansarii Point Station inhabited.<ref name="X-Wing" />
