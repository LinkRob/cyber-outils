The '''Clan Assembly''' governed the [[Twi'lek]] homeworld of [[Ryloth]] and its colony worlds such as [[Aaloth]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Based in the [[Assembly building]] in Ryloth's [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Lessu City|Lessu]], the Clan Assembly signed the [[articles of membership]] making Ryloth and other Twi'lek [[planet]]s members of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" />
