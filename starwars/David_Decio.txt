'''David Decio''' is an English actor who has been involved in two ''[[Star Wars]]'' productions. David worked on the [[Disney+]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' as the [[Wikipedia:Body double (disambiguation)|double]] and [[Wikipedia:Stand-in|stand-in]] for [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine/Darth Sidious]] played by [[Ian McDiarmid]].<ref name="Tweet 2">{{Twitter|DavidDecio|status/1558034117857533953|[[David Decio]]|quote=Happy Birthday Ian. It was an honour to be your Double/stand-in for Emperor Palpatine on @StarWars @obiwankenobi. I enjoyed our time working together.|date=2022-08-12|archivedate=20220924213653}}</ref> In 2014, he helped create licensed consumer products through a company under contract with [[Lucasfilm]], on {{Film|VII}}. As part of his work, he would visit the production of ''The Force Awakens'' at [[Pinewood Studios]] in England.<ref name="Facebook">{{Facebook|username=DavidDecio|url=story.php?story_fbid=pfbid0gnttrersHZUqDUyV1fjP6TkzGzDsNXt1CJGTD9bzDBXwq7erhDBJ94irgCRUBM7Ql&id=100057823580183|name=David Decio|archivefile=File:DecioPinewood.png}}</ref>
