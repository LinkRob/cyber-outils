'''2004 Topps ''Star Wars'' Heritage''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] celebrating the first six films in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise. It was released on [[September 20]], [[2004]] to coincide with the DVD release of the original trilogy.
