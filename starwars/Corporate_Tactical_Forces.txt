'''Corporate Tactical Forces''' were the private [[Law enforcement agency|security forces]] that were employed by conglomerates aligned with the [[Galactic Empire]] and were distinct from the [[Imperial Military]].

==History==
During the [[Imperial Era]], employees of such conglomerates viewed themselves as the [[Galactic Empire]]'s first line of defense. The [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces|Pre-Mor Enforcement]] of the [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]] formed part of the Corporate Tactical Forces.<ref name="That Would Be Me" />
