'''''Chewie and the Courageous Kid''''' is a young-readers storybook by [[Nate Millici]] that adapts issues 1&ndash;3 of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Chewbacca (2015)|''Star Wars: Chewbacca'' (2015)]]. The book was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[April 17]], [[2018]]. It was later republished as part of the young-reader story collection ''[[Galactic Adventures Storybook Collection]]'' on [[October 2]], 2018, where it was titled "Chewbacca and the Courageous Kid."

==Publisher's summary==
''An 8×8 with stickers featuring Chewbacca''.

''A Wookiee is a girl's best friend! When Chewbacca meets young Zarro on and ''[sic]'' Outer Rim planet, he has no choice but to set aside his own mission to help her rescue her father from a dangerous mine''.<ref name="Amazon" />
