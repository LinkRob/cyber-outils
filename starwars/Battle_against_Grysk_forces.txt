In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Mokivj|battle]] over the [[planet]] of [[Mokivj]], the [[Galactic Empire]] '''pursued''' the [[Grysk Hegemony]] over [[Realspace|space]]. Their goal was to rescue the kidnapped [[Force-sensitive]] [[Chiss]] [[Gender|girls]] who were being held inside [[hibernation chamber]]s, as well as to locate the [[Grysk homeworld]]. The Empire successfully achieved the former, but discovered the Grysks were nomadic. Late in the battle, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] observed a [[Chiss shuttle]] leaving one of the Grysk warships and escaping into [[hyperspace]], this lead Vader to correctly surmise that a group of Chiss under the control or at least working with the [[Grysk Hegemony]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />
