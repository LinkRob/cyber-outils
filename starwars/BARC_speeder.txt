The '''Biker Advanced Recon Commando (BARC) speeder''' was a [[speeder bike]] manufactured by [[Aratech Repulsor Company]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Based on [[Mobquet Swoops and Speeders]]' design of the [[Ripper (speeder bike)|Ripper]] speeder bike,<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> the BARC speeder was used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], after being championed by [[Clone Commander]]s [[Bacara]] and [[Neyo]].<ref name="HC 33">{{HelmetCollectionCite|33|Helmets}}</ref> The BARC speeder was the predecessor to the [[74-Z speeder bike]],<ref name="HC 5">{{HelmetCollectionCite|5|Databank A-Z}}</ref> which also was designed for the Republic and saw use since the [[First Battle of Geonosis|start of the war]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref>

[[File:BARCSpeeder-2025RepUnit.png|thumb|left|250px|A BARC speeder with a combat sidecar attached.]]
The BARC speeder gave the ordinary [[clone trooper]] fighting on foot increased speed, mobility, and firepower. Used primarily as a scouting and escort vehicle, it was readily adaptable for various environments and missions. With the addition of optional support struts, a sidecar or stretcher could be attached to its side.<ref name="SWE" /> A BARC speeder variant known as the [[RapidResponse police speeder]]<ref name="Land, Sea and Air Speeders">{{EncyclopediaCite|Speeders on Land, Sea, and Air (1)}}</ref> was also available for use by civilian [[Law enforcement agency|police]], specifically the [[Coruscant Security Force]], to patrol the [[Galactic Republic]] [[capital]] [[planet]] of [[Coruscant]].<ref name="LL" />

Near the end of the Clone Wars, Neyo and a clone trooper of the [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]] rode BARC Speeders during the [[Siege of Saleucami]]. [[Order 66]] was issued, and Neyo and his wingman shot [[Stass Allie]]'s [[74-Z speeder bike|74-Z speeder]], which crashed, killing Allie.<ref name="Episode III" />
