'''Chief gunnery officer''', or '''Gunnery Chief''', was an [[assignment]] aboard certain [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[warship]]s, including [[Star Destroyer]]s. [[Gender|They]] acted as the [[gunnery captain]]'s direct subordinate.<ref name="TSOD" /> Around [[1 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> [[Hija]] held that position aboard the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]''.<ref name="TSOD" /> [[Varnillian]] also served as a Gunnery Chief at some point.<ref name="Onager">{{Armada|set=Onager-class Star Destroyer Expansion Pack|cardname=Gunnery Chief Varnillian}}</ref>

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GA" /> [[Peera Maso]] also held the position aboard the [[First Order]] [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]''.<ref name="TLJ VD" />
