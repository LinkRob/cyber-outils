'''D4-FI''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] with old programming that served in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] on a [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Trade Federation battleship]]. During a raid on the [[battleship]], [[Captain]] [[Nuno Tuuk]] built a trap to trick the [[Starship|ship's]] attackers. However, D4-FI was able to solve the trap in sixty [[second]]s despite his old programming.<ref name="TSS" />
