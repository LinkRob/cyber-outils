'''''Coffee With Kenobi''''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]''&ndash;centric podcast hosted by author [[Dan Zehr]] that first debuted in [[2013]]. The podcast strives to be a spoiler-free space for community conversation, often discussing the mythology of the franchise and interviewing ''Star Wars'' creators. As of [[2024]], ''Coffee With Kenobi'' has released over 800 episodes.

As of [[2023]]'s [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]], ''Coffee With Kenobi'' holds the record for the highest number of ''Star Wars'' Celebration panel appearances, having also hosted at [[2015]]'s [[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim]], [[2017]]'s [[Celebration Orlando]], [[2019]]'s [[Celebration Chicago]], and [[2022]]'s [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim]].

==History==
===Founding, scope, and content===
{{Quote|We wanted our show to have a coffee shop atmosphere that features myriad opinions and perspectives through a critical lens. ''[&hellip;]'' Our sub heading is Star Wars Discussion, Analysis, and Rhetoric, and this drives our programming on each show. We stay away from rumors, spoilers, and speculation, and try to focus on confirmed news, as well as the plethora of Star Wars themes, concepts, and ideas that exist in the saga.|Dan Zehr, in a StarWars.com interview on the podcast's behalf|<ref name="SWcom">{{SW|url=news/fully-operational-fandom-podcasting-across-the-galaxy-part-2|text=Fully Operational Fandom: Podcasting Across the Galaxy, Part 2}}</ref>}}
The podcast ''Coffee with Kenobi''{{'s}} first episode debuted on [[May 12]], [[2013]].<ref name="Email" /> Its original co-founders and hosts were Cory Clubb<ref name="Email" /><ref name="Hall of Fame" /> and [[Dan Zehr]]; the latter of whom is the co-author of several ''[[Star Wars]]'' titles<ref name="About" /> such as ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' ([[2020]]),<ref name="Publisher">{{Penguin|url=books/636357/the-star-wars-book-by-cole-horton|text=The Star Wars Book}}</ref> ''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]'' ([[2021]]),<ref name="About" /> ''[[Star Wars: I Am Your Father]]'' ([[2022]]),<ref name="Penguin">{{Penguin|url=books/703661/star-wars-i-am-your-father-by-dk/9780744055207|text=Star Wars I Am Your Father}}</ref> and ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' ([[2024]]), as well as a frequent contributor to [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="About" /> Other former contributors to the podcast include Tom Gross, Rob Wainfur, and Melinda Wolf.<ref name="Hall of Fame" /> As of 2024, ''Coffee With Kenobi'' has released over 800 episodes<ref name="Episode Count" /> and has Zehr as its brand manager and sole host.<ref name="About" />

[[File:DanZehrCWK.jpg|thumb|right|340px|Dan Zehr, the co-founder and host of Coffee With Kenobi]]
In the time since its inception, ''Coffee With Kenobi'' has strived to be a space fostering discussion about ''Star Wars'' in a spoiler-free environment with an intelligent and humorous tone. The podcast primarily focuses on officially released content, with an increased focus on discussion of fan passion for the franchise.<ref name="SWcom" /> ''Coffee With Kenobi'' discusses ''Star Wars'' mythology while featuring content in a variety of formats&mdash;including reviews, event coverage, and interviews.<ref name="About" /> Several notable ''Star Wars'' creators interviewed by the podcast include [[Ewan McGregor]], [[Hayden Christensen]], [[Dave Filoni]], [[Jon Favreau]], [[Pedro Pascal]], [[Anthony Daniels]], [[James Arnold Taylor]], [[Ashley Eckstein]], [[Stephen J. Sansweet]], [[John Morton]], [[Jim Cummings]], [[Carl Weathers]], [[Joonas Suotamo]], [[Vanessa Marshall]], [[Taylor Gray]], [[Dan Madsen]], [[Charles Soule]], [[Cavan Scott]], [[Claudia Gray]], [[George Mann]],<ref name="Email" /> [[Tricia Barr]],<ref name="SWcom" /> [[Catherine Taber]],<ref name="CWK Celebration Chicago">{{WebCite|url=https://www.spreaker.com/episode/cwk-show-272-celebration-chicago-podcast-stage-featuring-james-arnold-taylor-catherine-taber--17643897|date=2019-04-14|format=[[Wikipedia:Podcast|Podcast]]|archivedate=20241220060559|title=CWK Show #272: Celebration Chicago Podcast Stage, featuring James Arnold Taylor & Catherine Taber}}</ref> [[Adam Christopher]],<ref name="CWK Europe">{{WebCite|url=https://www.spreaker.com/episode/cwk-show-638-star-wars-celebration-europe-podcast-stage-2023--53503346|date=2023-04-11|format=[[Wikipedia:Podcast|Podcast]]|archivedate=20241220060708|title=CWK Show #638: Star Wars Celebration Europe Podcast Stage (2023)}}</ref> [[Justin Ridge]], and [[Bobby Moynihan]].<ref name="Vanity Fair">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2019/10/star-wars-first-openly-gay-couple-star-wars-resistance|text=Star Wars Producers Confirm the Franchise’s First Openly Gay Onscreen Couple|author=[https://www.vanityfair.com/contributor/laura-bradley Laura Bradley]|date=2019-10-01|work=[[Wikipedia:Vanity Fair|''Vanity Fair'']]|archivedate=20241124171601}}</ref> In these, Zehr felt the podcast sought to tailor the topics of the interviews to match the guests they are interacting with.<ref name="SWcom" />

Over time, ''Coffee With Kenobi'' has been covered by several mainstream news outlets such as [[Wikipedia:HuffPost|''HuffPost'']], [[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']], [[Wikipedia:Chicago Tribune|''Chicago Tribune'']], [[Wikipedia:Journal Star (Peoria)|''Journal Star'']], [[Wikipedia:Good Morning America|''Good Morning America'']], [[Wikipedia:Vanity Fair|''Vanity Fair'']], and [[Wikipedia:Entertainment Weekly|''Entertainment Weekly'']],<ref name="About" /> as well as StarWars.com. On the podcast's behalf, Zehr was interviewed for the StarWars.com article "Fully Operational Fandom: Podcasting Across the Galaxy, Part 2," by [[Amy Ratcliffe]], published on [[June 19]], [[2014]]. Within, he discussed why they chose podcasting as their medium of choice, the subjects they cover, and their best fan moments.<ref name="SWcom" />

===''Star Wars'' Celebration===
{{Quote|This year we have some records being made. Coffee With Kenobi makes its 5th appearance on the stage (only having missed Celebration Europe 3 back in 2016) which puts it ahead of Full of Sith, Star Wars Minute, RADIO 1138, Skywalking Through Neverland and Fangirls Going Rogue who all tie with 4 appearances out of a possible 6 on the podcast stage since Anaheim in 2015.|[[Mark Newbold]], not long before 2023's Celebration Europe|<ref name="Count">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fanthatracks.com/news/conventions-events/star-wars-celebration-europe-holonet-news-stage-panels-revealed|text=Star Wars Celebration Europe: HoloNet News Stage panels revealed|author=[[Mark Newbold]]|date=2023-03-18|archivedate=20230329221307}}</ref>}}
[[File:DanZehrCWK.png|thumb|left|180px|Dan Zehr, ''Coffee With Kenobi''{{'s}} host, with Harrison Ford]]
''Coffee With Kenobi'' was the first podcast to record a live podcast session on a ''Star Wars'' [[Celebration]] stage at [[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim]],<ref name="Email" /> held from [[April 16]] to [[April 19]] in [[2015]].<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/dave-filoni-and-more-confirmed-for-star-wars-celebration-2015|text=Dave Filoni and More Confirmed for ''Star Wars'' Celebration 2015}}</ref> They were joined by John "Dak" Morton for the session.<ref name="CWK Anaheim 2015">{{WebCite|url=https://www.spreaker.com/episode/cwk-show-86-celebration-anaheim-podcast-stage-live-featuring-john-dak-morton--16542621|format=[[Wikipedia:Podcast|Podcast]]|archivedate=20241220060226|title=CWK Show #86: Celebration Anaheim Podcast Stage LIVE, featuring John "Dak" Morton}}</ref> The podcast also participated in the same capacity at [[Celebration Orlando]],<ref name="Email" /> held from [[April 13]] to [[April]] 16 [[2017|two years later]],<ref name="Celebration 2017 Set for Orlando">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2017-set-for-orlando|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration 2017 Set for Orlando}}</ref> marking co-host Cory Clubb's last episode.<ref name="CWK Orlando" />

''Coffee With Kenobi'' returned for its third time on the Podcast Stage at [[Celebration Chicago]],<ref name="Email" /> which ran from [[April 11]] to [[April 15|15]] in [[2019]],<ref name="Chicago 2019">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-is-heading-to-chicago-and-the-star-wars-show-has-the-details|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration is Heading to Chicago and ''The Star Wars Show'' has the Details}}</ref> where they were joined by James Arnold Taylor and Catherine Taber.<ref name="CWK Celebration Chicago" /> The podcast was also present for [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim]]<ref name="Email" />&mdash;held between [[May 26]] and [[May 29]], 2022,<ref name="Preponement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-anaheim-2022-dates-changing|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim 2022 Dates Changing}}</ref> where they were joined by Dan Madsen.<ref name="CWK Anaheim 2022">{{WebCite|url=https://www.spreaker.com/episode/cwk-show-531-star-wars-celebration-anaheim-podcast-stage-2022--50012788|date=2015-04-17|format=[[Wikipedia:Podcast|Podcast]]|archivedate=20241220060543|title=CWK Show #531: Star Wars Celebration Anaheim Podcast Stage (2022)}}</ref> ''Coffee With Kenobi'' returned again for [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]],<ref name="Email" /> hosted from [[April 7]] to [[April 10]], [[2023]].<ref name="CelebrationSchedule">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-panel-schedule|text=Plan Ahead with the ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Panel Schedule}}</ref> For the panel, they were joined by Adam Christopher on the HoloNet News Stage.<ref name="CWK Europe" /> As of 2023's Celebration Europe, ''Coffee With Kenobi'' has the most Celebration appearances of any podcast on the Podcast Stage, later renamed the HoloNet News Stage, having only missed [[2016]]'s [[Celebration Europe (2016)|Celebration Europe]].<ref name="Count" />
