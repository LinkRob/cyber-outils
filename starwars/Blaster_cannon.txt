{{Quote|Takes a pretty confident person to think he can beat modern turbolasers with blaster cannons, though.|[[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]], to [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]|<ref name="Thrawn (novel)">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>}}
A '''blaster cannon''' was a weapon that utilized gas to produce a visible short-range beam of intense energy.<ref name="SW EOSAOV (SE)">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)]]''</ref> They were less powerful than [[laser cannon]]s.<ref name="SW EOSAOV (SE)">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)]]''</ref> While blaster cannons were commonly fitted to [[starship]]s<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> or ground vehicles like the [[Flash speeder]],<ref name="Databank Flash speeder">{{Databank|flash-speeder|Flash Speeder}}</ref> there also existed handheld models like the [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon]].<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref>

Models of blaster cannons included the [[Vulk TAU-6-23 "Blastmill" rotary blaster cannon]],<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> the [[Mark II EWHB-10]],<ref name="Power">{{EA|subdomain=starwars|url=starwars/battlefront/features/power-ups|text=Power-Ups &ndash; ''Star Wars: Battlefront''}},</ref> and the [[F-11ABA heavy blaster cannon]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
