The '''Firewolf man-of-war''' was a type of [[man-of-war]] [[warship]] used by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] that was named for the [[firewolf]], a creature. Firewolf man-of-wars were equipped with [[engine]]s and a [[hyperdrive]]. Their size was comparable to that of a [[Shatter-class WarMaster|''Shatter''-class WarMaster]] [[Starship|warship]] of the [[Grysk]] warlike [[species]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> [[Chiss Defense Force]] [[Admiral]] [[Ers'ikaro]] [[Commanding officer|commanded]] the [[Bellicose (Firewolf man-of-war)|''Bellicose'']], a Firewolf man-of-war.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Firewolf man-of-war was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the third volume in his [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''Ascendancy Trilogy'']].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
