'''Burowgas''' were [[farm]] animals that loved to dig and burrow. They were playful and curious creatures by nature. [[Farmer]]s utilized the animal's love for digging to help get soil ready for planting [[seed]]s.<ref name="Big Brother's Bounty">{{YJA|Big Brother's Bounty}}</ref>

==Burowgas in the galaxy==
The [[Rodian]] [[farmer]] [[Weebo]] used burowgas on her [[Weebo's farm|farm]] on the planet [[Tenoo]]. Due to the animal's curious nature, one of her burowgas went missing so she hired [[bounty hunter]] [[Ansen Strung]] to locate the animal.<ref name="Big Brother's Bounty" />

==Behind the scenes==
The burowgas first appeared in "[[Big Brother's Bounty]]," the second part of the [[Tree Troubles / Big Brother's Bounty|thirteenth episode]] of the [[Disney+]] and [[Disney Junior]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''{{'}} [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]],<ref name="Big Brother's Bounty" /> which aired on [[August 2]], [[2023]].<ref name="More YJA Episodes">{{ThisWeek|aYGTZacheog|A Look at Ahsoka, More Young Jedi Adventures Episodes, and More!|sw_url=video/a-look-at-ahsoka-more-young-jedi-adventures-episodes-and-more}}</ref> The subtitles provided on Disney+ for "Big Brother's Bounty," consistently capitalized the word burowga.<ref name="Big Brother's Bounty S">{{YJA|Big Brother's Bounty}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> However, the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' decapitalizes the creatures' name in one instance.<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> This article assumes the decapitalization form to be correct as the subtitles had shown inconsistencies in the past and that the reference book is a more recent source to have come out.
