'''''Finn and Poe Team Up!''''' is a young-readers book in the [[World of Reading]] series that adapts scenes from [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. The book, a level one title, was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[June 14]], [[2016]]. The book comprises 32 pages, and is intended for children ages six through eight.<ref name="Hachette" />

==Publisher's summary==
''This level 1 early reader retells a favorite scene from ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''. Featuring beautiful full-color illustrations, this World of Reading book is perfect for little ''Star Wars'' fans who are ready to read on their own.''<ref name="Hachette" />
