A '''rebel cell''' of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] was active during the [[Imperial Era]] on the [[Galactic Empire]] capital [[planet]] of [[Coruscant]]. Its members included [[Quartermaster]] and [[squad leader]] [[Ko-Tun Feralo]], [[Jarrod Kelvin]] and his [[droid]] [[J4X-7]], as well as the [[Wookiee]] [[Drokkatta]].<ref name="Heart of the Empire" /> On at least one occasion, they were assisted by [[Jyn Odan]], a [[smuggler]] who worked for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Cover">The ''[[Heart of the Empire]]'' box cover depicts Jyn Odan beside Ko-Tun Feralo on Coruscant.</ref>
