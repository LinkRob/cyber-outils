'''327-T''', also known by the nickname '''327''', was a spunky, simpleminded [[WED-15 Treadwell droid|WED-15]] [[WED Treadwell repair droid|Treadwell]] [[Maintenance droid|repair droid]] who served aboard the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class]] [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tranquility]]'' during the [[Clone Wars]], maintaining many of the ship's systems on board. He survived a series of explosions detonated by [[Asajj Ventress]] in the ship's [[reactor]] room, but was trapped beneath rubble until recovered by the [[Jedi]] aboard, [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Luminara Unduli]].

==Biography==
[[File:327-T_FAIL.png|thumb|left|253px|327-T fails to notice Ventress]]
327-T was stationed aboard the [[Jedi]] [[Cruiser]] ''Tranquility'' during the Clone Wars, where he maintained a number of its systems. During a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Rescue on the Tranquility|boarding of the vessel]] to free [[Trade Federation]] [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] from Republic capture, 327-T was assigned to patrol duty in the reactor room by two members of [[Green Company]], who mistakenly referred to the [[droid]] by his nickname, 327, much to his annoyance.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

Soon after, [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] infiltrated ''Tranquility''{{'s}} reactor room and planted numerous time-delayed [[thermal detonator]]s while stealthily evading 327-T's notice, after which she successfully escaped the room through its ventilation ducts. A short while later, Ventress remotely detonated the bombs, prompting 327-T to attempt to avoid the explosion in a panic. The droid avoided destruction, but became trapped by falling debris.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

Soon after the explosion, [[Jedi Master]] [[Luminara Unduli]] and [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] dueled Ventress in the critically damaged reactor room, and, while tracking her there, sensed the movement of 327-T struggling under the debris by which he was trapped. Initially presuming him to be Ventress, Luminara freed the droid and immediately held him at bay with [[Luminara's lightsaber|her lightsaber]] before realizing her mistake. She then righted 327-T on the ground before continuing pursuit of Ventress. He quickly hurried off to assess the damage to the reactors.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

==Characteristics==
As a WED-15 Treadwell repair droid, 327-T was manufactured by [[Cybot Galactica]], stood 1.24 [[meter]]s tall, and was equipped with rugged all-terrain treads, fine-focus binocular vision, a telescoping neck, interchangeable tool sockets, dual head-mounted flood lamps, and a database containing millions of mechanical items and repair instructions.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

[[File:327-t.png|thumb|236x236px|327-T was equipped with illuminating floodlamps built into his binocular [[photoreceptor]]s.]]
While not a highly intelligent droid, 327-T showed dedication to his duty guarding ''Tranquility''{{'s}} reactor room, despite his ultimate failure to do so. 327-T was also somewhat skittish, and detested being called by his nickname, despite the fact that many droids across [[the galaxy]] were frequently called by their shorthand name.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

==Behind the scenes==
327-T first appeared in "[[Cloak of Darkness]]," the ninth episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'s}} [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]].<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

The droid's name is another conspicuous appearance of the digits "327," a recurring number in the productions of [[George Lucas]].<ref name="Episode Guide" />
