'''Aajjay Mahajan''' is a concept artist who, alongside [[TG Satheesh]], was responsible for designing most of the original elements of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|Volume 2]] episode "[[The Bandits of Golak]]," produced by [[88 Pictures]],<ref name="Concept Gallery" /> where he also serves as the Art Director.<ref name="LinkedIn" /> He also designed the film's official poster.<ref name="ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=aajjay|url=artwork/blB2ng|name=[[Aajjay Mahajan]]|text=Poster Design for The Bandits of Golak|quote=This poster was created for The Bandits of Golak (StarWars Vision 2)|archivedate=20250311223538}}</ref>

Prior to joining 88 Pictures in [[October]] of [[2020]], Mahajan worked as an Art Director for the company ArtGuru,<ref name="LinkedIn" /> though he was the Founder and Creative Director at Pencil to Pixels Studios even earlier than that, starting the company sometime prior to [[2015]].<ref name="Vimeo" /> Some of Mahajan's work on "The Bandits of Golak" was displayed at the [[Wikipedia:Autodesk|Autodesk Convergence 2023]] event during 88 Pictures' founder and CEO [[Milind D. Shinde]]'s presentation, entitled "Bringing Breathtaking Stories & Characters to Life from India to the World."<ref name="Autodesk">{{LinkedIn|url=posts/88-pictures-limited_lucasfilm-autodeskconvergence2023-storytelling-ugcPost-7104852029956591616-i4dz/|text=88 Pictures|date=2023-09-05|quote=🌟 Honored and Thrilled to be part of Autodesk Convergence 2023! 🌟 Our CEO and Founder, Milind D. Shinde, presenting at the prestigious Autodesk Convergence 2023 event.🎙️ Milind shared his insights on the fascinating topic of "Bringing Breathtaking Stories & Characters to Life from India to the World." 🌎📽️ This was a great opportunity to one of the most captivating audiences to showcase how India's rich cultural tapestry contributes to crafting captivating narratives and unforgettable characters that resonate with audiences worldwide and the process of how we collaborated with #LucasFilm and the Star Wars Universe to create an indigenous film from script to the screen.🌐 We are deeply grateful to Autodesk for extending this invitation. 🤝 #AutodeskConvergence2023 #Storytelling #IndiaToTheWorld #Innovation #Inspiration #88pictures #indigenous #scripttoscreen|archivedate=20250311195203}}</ref>
