'''Autumn''', also known as '''fall''', was one of four [[season]]s that occurred on certain [[planet]]s, including [[Lothal]]. It marked the transition from [[summer]]<ref name="ranks">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref> into [[winter]].<ref name="justice">''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]''</ref> With the arrival of autumn, the [[temperature]] started to cool gradually, and many [[plant]]s turned from bright [[Color|green]] to pale green or brown. On Lothal, autumn was also the season during which the [[Jogan fruit|jogan]] blossoms were at their most fragrant.<ref name="ranks" />
