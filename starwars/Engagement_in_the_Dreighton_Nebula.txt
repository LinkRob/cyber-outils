In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Galactic Republic]] '''engaged''' the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s in the [[Dreighton Nebula]] during [[Operation: Counterstrike|Operation Counterstrike]]&mdash;the Republic and [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Nihil conflict|response]] to the [[Valo Atrocity|devastation]] inflicted by the Nihil on<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> [[Valo]] during the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]]. The battle resulted in a victory for the [[Republic Defense Coalition]]. While on the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] shortly before the [[Battle of Galov]]<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> later in the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Avar Kriss]]&mdash;the [[Jedi]] [[marshal]] of the station&mdash;reported the engagement against the Nihil in the Dreighton [[Nebula]] during a briefing to update the Jedi and members of the defense coalition on the status of Operation Counterstrike.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

==Behind the scenes==
The engagement in the Dreighton Nebula was mentioned in flashbacks in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', a [[2021]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
