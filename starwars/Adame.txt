'''Adame''' was a [[human]] [[smuggler]] who was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Along with [[Selentia]], Adame approached another smuggler, [[Han Solo]], requesting to borrow his [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to travel to the [[Gomar sector]] as part of a mission on behalf of [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].

==Biography==
Adame and Selentia followed Han Solo from the last planet he visited to the one he was currently at. After Adame saw that Selentia was made, Adame pointed their [[blaster]] at Solo and asked to have a word with him. When they arrived at the "Millennium Falcon", Adame happily greeted [[Chewbacca]] as an old acquaintance and friend. Adame later explained that they were sent by Leia Organa to pilot the ship to the [[Gomar sector]]. Solo outright denied permission to use his ship for any mission concerning the Rebellion especially if he was not going to pilot his own ship.<ref name="HS1" />
