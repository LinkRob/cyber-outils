'''Benis''' was a individual who served as a [[Rebel technician|technician]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] as a member of the [[Massassi Group]] around the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Benis was a diligent and organized worker, keeping an inventory of [[flight suit]]s and [[helmet]]s that were accompanied by an icon guide to ensure the [[pilot]]'s helmets were never mixed up. She was capable with a [[hydrospanner]], which she would use when working on [[starship]]s. [[Nera Kase]] regarded her as a very skilled tech.<ref name="Grounded" />
