The '''''Amarills''-class freighter''' was a type of [[freighter]]. The [[Starship|ships]] included multiple rooms, an [[airlock]], and a kitchen. The [[enslaver]] known just as [[Captain (individual)|Captain]] owned one such [[Unidentified Amarills-class freightter|vessel]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]]. The freedom fighter-turned escapee [[Jyn Erso]] was sent their to alter documents after being captured by [[Allehander Pso]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
