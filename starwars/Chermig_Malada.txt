In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> a [[human]] [[Unidentified cleaner (Morenia)|cleaner]] working on the ''Morenia'' mentioned Malada being a showman and him traveling on the [[cruiser]] to the [[gambler]] [[Kay Vess]] while cleaning her room aboard the cruiser.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Chermig Malada was first mentioned in the "Wild Card" story expansion for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref> Malada was mentioned by a human cleaner that players could listen to after calling them to clean Vess's room aboard the ''Morenia''. The interaction with the cleaner is part of one path players could take to acquire a universal [[keycard]] to the cruiser. However players who choose an alternate path to acquire the keycard or who walk away from the cleaner early can miss this dialog.<ref name="Outlaws" />
