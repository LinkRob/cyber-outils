{{Quote|The Archaic Arsenal of Ordo and Scabbard Monument of Krownest each claimed losses of ancient Mandalorian weapons within weeks of each other&mdash;a rally master lance and a munit'kad halberd.|Exantor Divo, ''Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious''|<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />}}
The '''Archaic Arsenal of Ordo''' was located on the [[Mandalorian]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Ordo]]. It housed a [[rally master lance]], an ancient Mandalorian weapon, although it was [[Thief|stolen]] in [[11 BBY|7966 C.R.C.]]. Within [[Standard week|weeks]] of the [[lance]]'s theft from Ordo, an ancient [[munit'kad halberd]] was stolen from the [[Scabbard Monument of Krownest|Scabbard Monument]] on the Mandalorian world of [[Krownest]], causing both worlds to suspect each other for the loss of their own weapons. Following the thefts,<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> the [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]] displayed the weapons aboard his [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]'',<ref name="Solo Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> by the [[Standard year|year]] [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' aboard the ''[[First Light]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref>

During the [[New Republic Era]], a file on stolen artifacts thought to have possibly been in Vos's possession was included in ''[[Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', a collection of case files compiled by the head of the planet [[Hosnian Prime]]'s [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]], [[Commissioner]] [[Exantor Divo]]. Documented in the file was the rally master lance's theft from the Archaic Arsenal of Ordo.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Archaic Arsenal of Ordo was mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />
