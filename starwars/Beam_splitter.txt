Similar to a [[blaster rifle]]'s spread [[barrel]] modification, '''beam splitters''' functioned by modifying the [[galven-pattern]] and carefully mounting three or more emitters into a honeycomb configuration inside a stock [[blaster]] barrel. Shots fired from a weapon equipped with a beam splitter diffused into a weaker spread shot rather than forming the blaster bolt, subsequently giving a single shot the potential to connect with multiple targets. The cost of a beam splitter was 1,250 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets" />

[[Trooper]]s were known to use beam splitters in order to transform weaponry into effective standoff weapons used to pin down enemy [[troop]]s and hold chokepoints in narrow [[starship]] corridors.<ref name="Gadgets" /> Beam splitters were also used on [[starfighter]]s such as the [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7 ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]], where they were connected to the starfighter's resonator shaft.<ref name="Complete Vehicles">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref>
