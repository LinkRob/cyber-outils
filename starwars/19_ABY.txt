The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''19 ABY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3296 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7996''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*During his attempt to rebuild the [[Jedi Order]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] [[Mission to Elphrona|visits]] the [[Elphrona Outpost]] with his [[Padawan]], [[Ben Solo]], and the explorer [[Lor San Tekka]], but they encounter the [[Knights of Ren]], who have also arrived to claim the [[Jedi artifact|artifacts]] stored inside the outpost.<ref name="Date" />
*[[Exantor Divo]] graduates from the [[Hosnian Prime Academy of Law Enforcement]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>
*[[Phasma]] orchestrates the [[death]] of her [[Phasma's clan|family]] so she and [[Keldo]] can join the [[Scyre]] and live in the [[Nautilus]].<ref name="Phasma" /><ref name="Phasma date" />
*[[Heist on Guat'a]]<ref name="Free Fall" /> {{Circa}}<ref name="Heist on Guata date">''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]'' establishes that the [[heist on Guat'a]] was only around a [[Standard year|year]] after [[Poe Dameron]] joined the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' establishes that Dameron joined the Spice Runners in [[18 ABY]]. Therefore, the heist must have occurred sometime around [[19 ABY]].</ref>
*[[Poe Dameron]] leaves the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] during the [[Kijimi summit]].<ref name="Free Fall" /><ref name="Kijimi summit date" />
*[[Captain]] [[Archex|Cardinal]] begins training a new generation of [[First Order stormtrooper]]s on the [[Absolution]] under [[Brendol Hux]].<ref name="Phasma" /><ref name="Phasma date" />
