'''Betu''' was a continent on the [[planet]] [[Rodia]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin|destruction]] of the first [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], [[Luke Skywalker]] took a luxury [[yacht]], named the ''[[Desert Jewel]]'', to Betu for a [[Mission to Rodia (Luke Skywalker)|secret mission]].<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" />
