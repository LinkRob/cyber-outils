The '''Devshi prison complex''' was a [[prison]] located on the [[ocean]] floor of the [[moon]] [[Devshi]]. The complex was run by [[warden]] [[Mattis Coto]]. As a matter of security, the prison was almost entirely staffed with [[droid]]s.<ref name="Showdown" /> By [[48 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/marvel-mace-windu-1-preview|text=Marvel's ''Mace Windu'' #1 Showcases the Prequel Hero in an Early Adventure – Exclusive Preview}} reveals ''Star Wars: Mace Windu'', of which "Showdown at Ocean's Deep" is a part, take place prior to [[Mace Windu]]'s entry into the [[Jedi High Council]]. ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' establishes that Windu is part of the Jedi Council at the [[time]] of [[Nim Pianna]]'s [[Mutiny on the Advent|death]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[48 BBY]]. Therefore, the events of "Mace Windu" must take place by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> Coto had been selling [[skeleton key]]s to [[Prisoner|inmates]] as a way to buy himself out of his job. After one of these keys was used by an [[Unidentified convict|escapee]] in a [[Prison break on Kevan 8|prion break]] on [[Kevan 8]], the [[Jedi]] [[Mace Windu]] [[Mission to Devshi|infiltrated Devshi]]. After confronting Coto and battling some [[security droid]]s, Windu left the prison through the emergency aqualock to the surface to follow multiple [[astromech droid]]s who had received the master skeleton key from Coto, while he left him behind for the approaching authorities.<ref name="Showdown" />
