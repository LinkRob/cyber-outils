The '''Borsk Fey'lya Army Base''' was a [[Galactic Federation of Free Alliances|Galactic Alliance]] military installation on [[Vandor-3]] named after former [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] [[Chief of State (New Republic)/Legends|Chief of State]], [[Borsk Fey'lya]]. It was the base of operations for all [[Galactic Alliance Army]] activities in the [[Coruscant sector/Legends|Coruscant sector]]. It was also the headquarters for the [[Pop-Dogs]] army unit.
