'''Blaster power packs'''<ref name="TC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> or '''energy packs'''<ref name="BH">{{StoryCite|story=Beyond Hope|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> were energy storage devices which supplied power to a [[blaster]]. During their [[funeral]]s, fallen members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] had power packs drained in their honor.<ref name="TC" /> During the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="BH" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Private]] [[Emon Kref]] used an [[A295 blaster rifle]] with a blaster power pack which had a tendency to fall out, a problem Kref attempted to solve with tape.<ref name="BH" />
