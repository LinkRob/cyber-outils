'''Berclan''' was an individual who flew as a [[pilot]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Battle of Endor]] in the [[Endor system]].<ref name="GTS" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> as the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second]] [[Death Star]] was about to explode, Berclan and several pilots attempted to escape the inevitable shockwave the [[space station]] would create. As they flew, Berclan rejoiced in triumph on the [[Comm unit|comms]] of their [[Starship|ship]] only for their ship to be destroyed moments later with the comm channel turning to static. [[Ca Isiora]], one of the pilots flying with Berclan, compressed her sadness on their [[death]] and remembered to celebrate after the battle was over. The Death Star was subsequently destroyed with Isiora making it to safety.<ref name="GTS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Berclan's voice appeared in "[[Galactic Tales: Shockwave]]," the fifth installment of the ''[[Galactic Tales]]'' short story series. The story was written by [[Alexander Freed]] and published in the [[Star Wars Insider 220|220th]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="GTS" /> on [[August 15]], [[2023]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/135-star-wars-insider-220|text=Star Wars Insider #220}}</ref>
