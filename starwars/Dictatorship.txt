'''Dictatorship''' was a [[Politics|form of government]] in which the ruler possessed absolute power and absolute authority, in contrast with [[democracy]].<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|People ''want'' this. Our trouble isn't that Palpatine is a dictator or that he has total control of the military. Our trouble is that, by and large&mdash;all exceptions aside&mdash;the citizens of our nation are willing to believe his lies, ignore his purges, and accept his rule in return for stability. They may not want ''him'', but they don't want war. They want anything ''but'' war, and Palpatine has boxed us in so that war appears to be the only way out.''"<br />"''If people understood&mdash;''"<br />"''They understand. They understand enough. If they hadn't been willing to trade the Jedi for stability, they wouldn't have accepted the Emperor's lies so easily.|[[Senator]]s [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Bail Prestor Organa]] during the early Imperial Era|<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>}}
[[File:HiddenEmpire2-PatchZircher-textless.png|thumb|right|250px|The Galactic Empire was a dictatorship ruled by Darth Sidious, a Sith Lord whom the galaxy knew as Emperor Sheev Palpatine.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> while [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] were staying on [[Naboo]] temporarily to avoid a potential [[assassin]]ation attempt on the former, they briefly discussed the corruption that had befallen the [[Galactic Republic]] and were wondering how to alleviate it. Skywalker responded that they probably would need to be made to resolve their differences by "someone wise," causing Amidala to note that what Skywalker was proposing sounded similar to a dictatorship. Skywalker did not deny it and implied he thought it may work.<ref name="Episode II" />

Under the leadership of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], the [[Galactic Senate|parliamentary]] [[The galaxy|Galactic]] Republic slowly turned into a dictatorship as the Chancellor used the [[Clone Wars]] as a pretext to amend the [[Galactic Constitution|constitution]] and accrue more executive power.<ref name="SWP">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> At the end of the conflict, the Republic was officially [[Proclamation of the New Order|replaced]] by a formal dictatorship, the [[Galactic Empire|First Galactic Empire]].<ref name="VE" /> Other dictatorial regimes included [[Hay-Zu]]'s leadership over his fellow [[Patitite]]s,<ref name="Patitite">{{Databank|patitite|Patitite}}</ref> and the rule of the [[Albee Dewaw]]&mdash;actually a group of [[DUM-series pit droid]]s&mdash;over the [[Balnab (group)|Balnab]]. Both would ultimately be stopped by [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]].<ref name="ND">{{TCW|Nomad Droids}}</ref>
