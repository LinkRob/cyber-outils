'''Denic''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] [[pilot]] and [[Technician|mechanic]] who worked in a [[Denic's garage|personally-owned garage]] at [[Jedha City]] a few [[Standard month|months]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. It was rumored she used to be a [[smuggler]] in the [[Corporate Sector]]. Denic had arrived in the city shortly before the [[Galactic Empire]] and during her [[time]] on [[Jedha]], she gave intelligence to the [[Guardians of the Whills]] [[Chirrut Îmwe]] and [[Baze Malbus]] about Imperial supply runs in exchange for a cut of the supplies. Later, the two Guardians were able to convince her to pilot a [[Thief|stolen]] [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]] to [[Escape of Jedha City's orphans|evacuate]] the [[Gimm sisters' orphanage]] from the [[moon]].<ref name="GotW" />

==Personality and traits==
Denic had tan skin and dark [[eye]]s. She wore white<ref name="Manga" /> [[Torjeka]] scanner [[goggles]] with macro and micro visual settings and thermal, light enhancing and electromagnetic views. Because of the goggles advanced age, Denic blackened the skin around her eyes to block out light coming in through the weakened seals. She wore a thick [[spacer]]'s [[jacket]], pants and [[boot]]s<ref name="GotW" /> and a cap over her hair<ref name="Manga" /> and carried a [[blaster]] in a [[holster]] strapped to her thigh.<ref name="GotW" />
