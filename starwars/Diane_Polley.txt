'''Diane Polley ''' (died on [[January 10]], [[1990]]) was the voice actress who provided the voice of [[Dobah]] on [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']]; she performed in a total of thirteen episodes.

Polley passed away on January 10, 1990, at the age of 53.<ref name="Sarah Polley" />
