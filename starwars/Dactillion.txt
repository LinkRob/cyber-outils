'''Dactillion''', also known as '''dactyls'''<ref name="Bust Collection">{{BustCollectionCite|68|Star Wars Universe}}</ref> or '''dactilloids''',<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|40|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> were large, winged, [[Diet|carnivorous]] [[reptavian]]s<ref name="EV" /> native to the [[planet]] [[Utapau]].<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Dartdactilli.jpg|thumb|left|300px|A Dactillion]]
The creatures used their wings to catch thermal updrafts from within the massive sinkholes they called home, allowing them to fly toward the surface. They could also use their [[claw]]s to scales the walls of the sinkholes. They sported two pronged [[Horn/Legends|horns]] on the end of their beaked mouth that they used in both defense and combat.<ref name="Alien Archive" />

The native [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Utai]] managed to tame to dactillion by feeding them meat, though they themselves used to be part of the creatures' diet.<ref name="Bust Collection" /> Dactillion were also present on the world of [[Shantipole]].<ref name="Wings of the Master">{{Rebels|Wings of the Master}}</ref>

==History==
The dactillion's ability to traverse great distances quickly made it an important means of travel for locals. They were also used as mounts in battle by the [[Utapaun Security Forces]],<ref name="Bust Collection" /> grabbing enemies with their strong claws and using their horns.<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dactillions were first envisioned as a [[Jedi]] mount for the sequences on Utapau during the story development of the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] and were envisioned that they would be ridden by [[Jedi Master]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]]. A small concept model of the dactillion was created by [[Michael Murnane]] within two or three days to get it's proportions.<ref name="TAoSWEIII:RotS">''[[The Art of Star Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref> They were later meant to reappear in an arc of the sixth season of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''. Although the show was canceled before the episodes aired, they were released as unfinished story reels on [[StarWars.com]] in [[2014]] as part of [[The Clone Wars Legacy|''The Clone Wars'' Legacy]] and are still considered [[canon]].<ref name="YouTube">{{SWYouTube|vnH1JKr4o_8|Star Wars: The Clone Wars Legacy|sw_url=video/the-clone-wars-legacy}}</ref> The creature's model for the episodes was created by [[Amy Beth Christenson]] and finished on [[March 26]], [[2012]].<ref name="Concept Gallery">{{SW|url=tv-shows/crystal-crisis-on-utapau-concept-art-gallery|text=Crystal Crisis on Utapau Concept Art Gallery|nolive=1}}</ref>
