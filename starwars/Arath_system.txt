The '''Arath system''' was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Tunka sector]] and was home to a [[Unidentified sentient race|sentient race]]. The [[Jedi]] [[Sturm Umbrik]] led an [[Outer Rim expedition oh-four-seven|expedition]] to a [[moon]] in the [[Star system|system]]&mdash;[[Shara'Tam]]&mdash;that discovered treasure and confirmed the race's past presence. However, the expedition's members were [[Death|killed]] by [[pirate]]s, leaving the location of Shara'Tam unknown for [[Standard century|centuries]]. In [[22 BBY]], it was rediscovered when the [[Mirialan]] [[Servit]] used an [[Sturm Umbrik's EX droid|EX droid]] that had been used in the expedition to listen to a [[Holorecorder|holorecording]] of Umbrik, revealing the moon's coordinates.

==Description==
The Arath system was a [[star system]] located in the [[Tunka sector]]<ref name="Galaxy" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Inheritance" /> and the [[Western Reaches]],<ref name="WesternReaches" /> situated in grid square J-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Galaxy" /> It contained the [[moon]] [[Shara'Tam]].<ref name="Inheritance" />

==History==
{{Quote|Prospectors have been talking about that fateful expedition for centuries. The story goes that Sturm's team uncovered a great treasure on an arid moon somewhere out on the frontier. A moon called Shara'Tam.|Dexter Jettster|<ref name="Inheritance" />}}
[[File:Dex-Inheritance.png|thumb|right|240px|Prospectors such as Dexter Jettster (pictured) knew the tale of the Arath system as a legend.]]
At one point, the Arath system was [[Homeworld|home]] to a [[Unidentified sentient race|sentient race]], as evidenced by a [[Unidentified statue|statue]] located on Shara'Tam. During the [[High Republic Era]], the Arath system fell within the [[Galactic Frontier]],<ref name="Inheritance" /> a section of the Outer Rim containing locations considered removed far enough from [[the galaxy]]'s center for the [[Galactic Republic]] to send explorers known as [[Republic Pathfinder]]s there.<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Millennium|Millennia]] after the statue's creation, it was discovered by the [[Jedi]] [[Sturm Umbrik]]'s team of Republic Pathfinders, confirming Umbrik's theory of the presence of a [[Sentience|sentient]] race in the system.<ref name="Inheritance" />

However, the Pathfinders were subsequently [[Death|killed]] in a [[pirate]] [[Raid on Shara'Tam|raid]], leaving Shara'Tam's whereabouts unknown for [[Standard century|centuries]] until a message from Umbrik regarding the moon was discovered on an old [[EX communication droid|EX droid]] by the [[Mirialan]] [[Servit]], a descendant of a [[Servit's ancestor|member]] of the Pathfinder team,<ref name="Inheritance" /> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="InheritanceDate">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-author-roundtable|text=The High Republic Author Roundtable: "The End of the Beginning"}} establishes the events of "[[Galactic Tales: Inheritance]]" take place during the events of {{Film|II}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> Following a skirmish with the [[Weequay]] [[Jarl]]'s [[Jarl's crew|crew]] in [[Dex's Diner]] on<ref name="Inheritance" /> the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]],<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Servit; [[Dexter Jettster]], the diner's owner and a [[prospector]] familiar with the legend of Shara'Tam; and Jarl all watched Umbrik's [[Hologram|holographic]] message, which was recorded on Shara'Tam. Using the coordinates that Umbrik provided, Servit intended to go to Shara'Tam to find the statue for himself.<ref name="Inheritance" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Arath system first appeared in a hologram in "[[Galactic Tales: Inheritance]]," an installment of the ''[[Galactic Tales]]'' series of short stories. The story was written by [[George Mann]] and was published in the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine's [[Star Wars Insider 211|211th issue]]<ref name="Inheritance" /> on [[June 7]], [[2022]].<ref name="Date">{{TitanComics|url=m/102-star-wars-insider-211|text=Star Wars Insider #211}}</ref>
