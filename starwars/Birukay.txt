'''Birukay''' was a barely-populated [[planet]] located at the farthest edge of [[the galaxy]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] would stash the [[assassin droid]]s [[BT-1]] and [[0-0-0]] on Birukay, where they remained until they were discovered by [[Sana Starros]], Aphra's companion and ex-lover, and her [[Sana Starros' crew|crew]].

==Description==
Birukay was a [[planet]] located in the far edges of [[the galaxy]]'s<ref name="Aphra 23" /> [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Aphra 25" /> The planet had few residents. It had [[Color|green-ish]] [[ocean]]s, with purple [[beach]]es that came right up against its brightly-colorful [[jungle]]s.<ref name="Aphra 23" />

==History==
At one point, Birukay was home to an [[Unidentified avian species|avian species]] that later went extinct.<ref name="Aphra 23" />

After [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] betrayed [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]<ref name="Aphra 23" /> and helped keep the [[Hoth|location]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Echo Base|base]] safe from the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Aphra 40">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 40|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 40]]</ref> she stashed her two [[assassin droid]]s, [[BT-1]] and [[0-0-0]], on Birukay.<ref name="Aphra 23" />

Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 23|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 23]] take place between the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> the criminal syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]] learned of the planet's existence and traveled there, though the forces sent were massacred by the two [[assassin droid]]s. Soon after, a [[Sana Starros' crew|team]] assembled by [[Sana Starros]] that consisted of [[Korin Aphra]], [[Kho Phon Farrus]], [[Magna Tolvan]], and [[Detta Yao]], also traveled to Birukay in search of Aphra, having deduced that the world was significant to Aphra due to a variety of factors. Finding numerous [[Death|dead]] bodies, the team were then attacked by more Crimson Dawn personnel, who they killed. Afterwards, the team realized that Aphra left something on the world as a trap to those who sought to follow in her steps, then encountering 0-0-0 and BT-1.<ref name="Aphra 23" /> After Starros convinced the two [[droid]]s to join them, the group left Birukay to continue their mission to find Aphra.<ref name="Aphra 24">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 24|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 24]]</ref>
