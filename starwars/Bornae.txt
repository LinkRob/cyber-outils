'''Bornae''' was a young, [[Color|light]]-skinned, [[human]] from the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. They lived in the [[Alderaan Enclave]] on the planet [[Sullust]] following their [[homeworld]]'s [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="L3" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of Alderaan visited the conclave as part of an [[Mission to rescue the survivors of Alderaan|effort]] to [[Purge of Alderaanians|rescue]] surviving [[Alderaanian]]s from the Empire. Not long after her arrival, Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s attacked the enclave and Bornae was [[Death|killed]] during the fighting. After the Alderaanians managed to defeat the Imperials, [[Preserver]] [[Jora Astane]], the head of the enclave, found Bornae's body and mourned their death, which Organa offered her condolences over.<ref name="L3" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bornae appeared as a corpse in the [[Princess Leia 3|third issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Princess Leia]]''. The issue was written by [[Mark Waid]], illustrated by [[Terry Dodson]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="L3" /> on [[April 29]], 2015.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52371/princess_leia_2015_2|text=Star Wars: Princess Leia (2015) #2}}</ref>
