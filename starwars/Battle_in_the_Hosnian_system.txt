A '''battle''' occurred at the border of the [[Hosnian system]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] at the same [[time]] as the [[Battle of Endor]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. The Rebel Alliance ships, consisting of at least two [[MC75 Star Cruiser]]s, the ''[[Accordance]]'' and the ''[[Temperance]]'', repulsed a surprise attack on the [[Core Worlds]] by an [[Unidentified Imperial armada|Imperial armada]]. The ''Accordance'' was lost, although the ''Temperance'' rescued some survivors.<ref name="Databank" />

==Prelude==
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Note" /> during the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[MC75 Star Cruiser]]s the ''[[Accordance]]'' and the ''[[Temperance]]'' were in the [[Core Worlds]] defending the [[Hosnian system]] when they were met by an [[Unidentified Imperial armada|Imperial armada]].<ref name="Databank" />

==The battle==
The Imperial armada launched a surprise attack on the Core Worlds at the same time as the [[Battle of Endor]] and battled the two MC75 Star Cruisers. During the battle the ''Accordance'' was destroyed but the ''Temperance'' rescued the survivors and went on to win the battle against the armada.<ref name="Databank" />

==Aftermath==
The ''Temperance'' became the [[flagship]] of [[Vanguard Squadron]] and was temporarily placed under the command of [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]].<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> The ''Temperance'' also served in the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]].<ref name="Databank" />
