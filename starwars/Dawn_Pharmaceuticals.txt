'''Dawn Pharmaceuticals''' was a large company active on the [[planet]] [[Begamor]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. During the [[mission to Begamor]], [[president]] [[Jamie Brasson]] gave the order to [[Death|kill]] everyone in the [[Silver Sails Business Group]], another large company on Begamor, and blame it on Dawn Pharmaceuticals.<ref name="TVoSD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dawn Pharmaceuticals was mentioned in the [[Wikipedia:Written Chinese|Chinese]] web novel ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''<ref name="TVoSD" /> written by "[[His Majesty the King]]," within its ninth chapter, which was released on [[December 21]], [[2020]]. The company's name was written in [[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|simplified Chinese]] as "曦光制药" (xī guāng zhì yào).<ref name="Chapter 9">{{WebCite|url=https://www.qidian.com/chapter/1015950219/623613939|text=第9章 混淆是非 _《星球大战：白银誓约》小说在线阅读|author=[[His Majesty the King]]|date=2020-12-21|language=[[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|Simplified Chinese]]|archivedate=20260607160211}}</ref>
