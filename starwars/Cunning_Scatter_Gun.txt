The '''Cunning Scatter Gun''' was a [[scatter gun]] utilized by the [[Trandoshan]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Charr]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cunning Scatter Gun was a "Base" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Charr's scatter gun, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.18.0 during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cunning Trapper]]. This wrap was unlocked by default when Charr became playable.<ref name="Hunters" />
