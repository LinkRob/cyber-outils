During a [[Gungan tribal war|war]] between the [[Gungan]] tribes of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], the ancient [[fortress]] [[city]] [[Spearhead]] was '''destroyed''' by a Gungan [[Gallo's army|army]] led by [[Boss]] [[Gallo]]. [[Time|Sometime]] after the arrival of [[human]]s on Naboo in approximately [[4,000 BBY|4000 BBY]] and [[Millennium|millennia]] before the [[Imperial Era]], Gallo had united the Gungan tribes under one banner and raised a massive [[army]] to attack Spearhead, home to the rogue [[warlord]] Boss [[Rogoe]] and the remaining holdouts against Gallo's united Gungans. Once Gallo's army had destroyed the city, Gallo laid the [[Ancient Quarter|foundations]] for a new Gungan [[capital]] called [[Otoh Gunga]], which was expanded in subsequent generations as Gungans sought to escape the humans and Naboo's predators.<ref name="Nexus" />

Kent]],<ref name="Prima">[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds: Prima's Official Strategy Guide|''Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds'': Prima's Official Strategy Guide]]</ref> released on [[November 7]], [[2001]],<ref name="PrimaDate">{{Amazon|url=Star-Wars-Galactic-Battlegrounds-Official/dp/0761537503|text=Star Wars Galactic Battlegrounds: Prima's Official Strategy Guide}}</ref> before appearing in the [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds]]'',<ref name="GB">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds]]''</ref> released on [[November 11]] in the same year.<ref name="GBDate">{{GameSpot|url=games/star-wars-galactic-battlegrounds|text=Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds}}</ref>
