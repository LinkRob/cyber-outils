'''FN-2000''', nicknamed "'''Zeroes'''," was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] in the [[First Order military|military forces]] of the [[First Order]]. Assigned to the [[FN Trooper Corps]], Zeroes trained alongside [[trooper]]s [[FN-2003]], [[Finn|FN-2187]], and [[FN-2199]] in the [[Standard decade|decades]] following the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He had [[Color|dark brown]] skin and a healed, light-colored scar on his right cheek.

==Biography==
FN-2000, also known as "Zeroes,"<ref name="BTA" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> [[Gender|male]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who served in the [[First Order]] in the [[Standard decade|decades]] after the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He was trained under [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] in the [[FN Trooper Corps]] alongside [[FN-2199]] "Nines," [[FN-2003]] "Slip," and [[Finn|FN-2187]] "Eight-Seven." The four [[trooper]]s practiced in simulated combat for most of their lives. During their first deployment, the FN [[Corps]] was brought to the [[Pressy's Tumble refinery complex|mining facility]] in [[Pressy's Tumble]], an [[asteroid field]], and ordered to [[Death|kill]] the [[miner]]s that had gone on [[Strike at Pressy's Tumble|strike]]. Zeroes and Slip opened fire on the workers with FN-2187 refusing to shoot a [[Abednedo]] negotiator and Nines being at first reluctant to shoot but eventually followed orders.<ref name="BTA" />

==Behind the scenes==
FN-2000 was first introduced in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Before the Awakening]]'', written by [[Greg Rucka]].<ref name="BTA" /> In the free-roam mode of ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'', FN-2000 appears on Takodana and is seen mourning FN-2199's passing during the battle. FN-2000 provides the player with a side mission, in which he asks the player to retrieve FN-2199's stun baton, which has been stolen by mercenaries residing on Takodana. Completing that mission awards the player with a gold brick.<ref name="Lego Star Wars">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>
