'''Airspeeders''', also called '''air speeders''' or simply '''speeders''', were [[repulsorlift]] vehicles common throughout [[the galaxy]]. On extremely urban [[planet]]s such as [[Coruscant]], airspeeders were controlled by [[skylane]]s to allow them to maneuver between the dense streams of traffic and [[skyscraper]]s.<ref name="Coruscant">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref><ref name="airtaxi">{{Databank|coruscant-air-taxi|Coruscant Air Taxi}}</ref>

There were many forms and variations of airspeeders, from civilian transports like the [[ComfortRide passenger airspeeder|ComfortRide Passenger Airspeeder]],<ref name="HC">{{TCW|Hostage Crisis}}</ref> [[Koro-2 all-environment Exodrive airspeeder]], [[XJ-6 airspeeder]],<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref> and [[Praxis Mk. I turbo speeder]]<ref name="StarfightersEncyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> to combat vehicles like the [[T-47 airspeeder]]<ref name="ESB">{{Film|IV}}</ref> and [[V-4X-D ski speeder]].<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|resistance-ski-speeder|Resistance ski speeder}}</ref>
