'''Clayton Sandell''' is one of the authors of the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''<ref name="Timelines" /> and behind-the-scenes books ''[[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi: A Visual Archive]]'',<ref name="RotJ Visual Archive">{{Insight|url=products/star-wars-return-of-the-jedi-a-visual-archive|text=Star Wars: Return of the Jedi: A Visual Archive}}</ref> and the upcoming ''[[Star Wars: The Phantom Menace: A Visual Archive]]''.<ref name="TPM Visual Archive">{{Insight|url=collections/star-wars/products/star-wars-the-phantom-menace-a-visual-archive|text=Star Wars: The Phantom Menace: A Visual Archive}}</ref>

Sandell is also a contributing feature writer for the websites of [[Industrial Light & Magic]]<ref name="ILM 1">{{ILM|url=star-wars-skeleton-crew-vfx-ilm-part-1|text=‘Star Wars: Skeleton Crew’: ILM’s Visual Effects Treasure Chest, From At Attin to Starport Borgo}}</ref> and [[Skywalker Sound]]<ref name="Skywalker Sound">{{WebCite|url=https://medium.com/@SkywalkerSound/sounds-of-sith-mpse-celebrates-20th-anniversary-of-episode-iii-with-special-screening-5dca19ecc722|text=Sounds of ‘Sith’: MPSE Celebrates 20th Anniversary of Episode III with Special Screening|author=Clayton Sandell|date=2025-05-19|work=[[Wikipedia:Medium (website)|Medium]]|archivedate=20250617190636}}</ref> and hosts behind-the-scenes deep dive videos on his social media accounts.<ref>{{Instagram|username=claytonsandell|name=Clayton Sandell}}</ref> He has also written for [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="SW Contributor>{{SW|url=news/contributor/csandell|text=Clayton Sandell}}</ref>. 

==Biography==
At [[ABC Television Network|''ABC News'']], in [[2018]], Sandell co-produced [[The Force of Sound (documentary)|''The Force of Sound'']], a documentary on the sound design of [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] that featured sound designers [[Matthew Wood]], [[Ren Klyce]], and director [[Rian Johnson]].<ref name="ABC" />

Sandell has produced a series of TV news features on visual effects and sound design in relation to ''[[Star Wars]]''.<ref name="VFX Sound Design">{{WebCite|url=https://claytonsandell.com/vfx-&-sound-design|text=VFX & SOUND DESIGN|work=Clayton Sandell's [https://web.archive.org/web/20241105120258/https://claytonsandell.com/home official website]|archivedate=20250710032911}}</ref> In [[2023]], he reported on the closure of [[32Ten Studios]], the San Rafael, California special effects company that worked on Star Wars projects and was based in the former Kerner Blvd. home of [[Industrial Light & Magic|ILM]].<ref name="32Ten">{{WebCite|url=https://www.scrippsnews.com/entertainment/movies/legendary-movie-effects-studio-shutting-down-amid-strikes|text=Legendary movie effects studio shutting down amid strikes|author=Clayton Sandell|date=2023-10-13|work=[[Wikipedia:E. W. Scripps Company|''Scripps'']]|archivedate=20250612122847}}</ref> He has also chronicled the mental health challenges faced by [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] actor [[Jake Lloyd]]<ref name="Jake Lloyd">{{Substack|subdomain=claytonsandell|url=p/jake-lloyds-personal-saga-new-focus|text=Jake Lloyd's Personal Saga: New Focus Is Helping Determine His Current Reality|name=Clayton Sandell|username=claytonsandell|date=2025-01-01|archivedate=20250725013858}}</ref>.

As a [[Celebration|Star Wars Celebration]] stage host, Sandell moderated the "Bringing Worlds to Life: The Concept Artists of ILM" panel with guests [[Doug Chiang]] in [[2023]],<ref name="SWCE 2023">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-panel-schedule|text=Plan Ahead with the Star Wars Celebration Europe 2023 Panel Schedule}}</ref> and the [[Lucasfilm]] Publishing "Manga Mania!" in [[2025]].<ref name="SWC 2025">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-lucasfilm-publishing-panel|text=Phase III of ''The High Republic'' And All The Lucasfilm Publishing News from ''Star Wars'' Celebration Japan 2025 – UPDATE}}</ref>

He is also an honorary member of the [[501st Legion (fan organization)|501st Legion]] and [[Rebel Legion]] costuming groups.<ref name="Honorary">{{Instagram|username=claytonsandell|url=p/DDSka_CyDO1|name=Clayton Sandell|date=2024-12-07|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/1OKwy}}</ref>
