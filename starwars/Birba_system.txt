The '''Birba system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Deep Core]]. It was located in grid square M-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Birban globe-lantern]]|system=Birba [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> the [[Birban globe-lantern]].<ref name="From a Certain Point of View">{{StoryCite|story=From a Certain Point of View (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi|audiobook=1}}</ref>
