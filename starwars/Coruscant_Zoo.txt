The '''Coruscant Zoo''' was an [[zoo]]logical institution located on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Following an [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|attempt]] by [[bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]] to [[assassin]]ate [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] in [[22 BBY|7955]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]], the bisected remains of the two [[arthropod]]s that had been used by Wesell in the attempt were sent to the zoo, where they were identified by [[myriapodologist]]s as [[Kouhun|''Indoumodo kouhuns'']]. By [[34 ABY|8011]] C.R.C., the zoo no longer held the menagerie it once did, with the private menagerie of [[King]] [[Grevoth Prana IX]] said to rival the diversity that institution once held, according to [[Hosnian Prime]] [[Law enforcement agency|police]] [[Commissioner]] [[Exantor Divo]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coruscant Zoo was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />
