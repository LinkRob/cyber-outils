The '''Bontormia system''' was located in the [[Bortele sector]], a part of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square R-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Star system|system]] was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=the [[Bontormian klesplong]]|system=the Bontormia [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> the [[Bontormian klesplong]] [[music]]al instrument.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Bontormia system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Bontormia system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention in the StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
