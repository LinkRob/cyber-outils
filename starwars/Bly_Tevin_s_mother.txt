The '''mother''' of the [[pilot]]s [[Bly Tevin]] and [[Lina]] was an [[E'roni]] [[Gender|woman]] who [[Homeworld|hailed]] from the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]. She lived on the world with her [[Bly Tevin's family|family]] and sometimes cooked [[pilafa stew]] to [[Diet|feed]] her relatives, which her son enjoyed. During the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]], Tevin and his [[Parent|mother]] watched together as [[Lina's starfighter]] crashed, [[Death|killing]] her. Afterward, a grieved Tevin enlisted in [[E'ronoh's fleet]] to follow in his sister's footsteps and eventually joined the [[fleet]]'s [[Thylefire Squadron]]. However, in [[382 BBY]], he perished in a supply [[Skirmish in the Eiram system|mission]] when his [[Saboteur|sabotaged]] [[Bly Tevin's devilfighter|devilfighter]] locked him out of the controls, thinking of his mother in his final moments.

==Biography==
[[File:Eronoh.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Bly Tevin's mother hailed from E'ronoh and lived on the world with her family.]]
An [[E'roni]] [[Gender|woman]] was the [[Parent|mother]] of the [[pilot]]s [[Bly Tevin]] and [[Lina]]. She lived on her [[homeworld]], the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]], in an [[Bly Tevin's apartment|apartment]] with her [[Bly Tevin's family|family]]. While raising her [[Youngling|children]], she often watched them play in the dusty streets near the world's [[E'ronoh palace|royal palace]]. At some point, the woman began making thin [[pilafa stew]] out of [[Ration pack|rations]] to [[Diet|feed]] her family, which Tevin enjoyed.<ref name="Convergence" /> Between [[387 BBY]] and [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]]. As [[Lina]]'s [[death]] took place during the war, it must have occurred between the two years.</ref> Lina enlisted in [[E'ronoh's fleet]] to fight in the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]] against E'ronoh's enemy, the Outer Rim planet [[Eiram]]. However, Tevin and his mother watched [[Lina's starfighter]] malfunction and crash shortly after departing on a [[Mission to Eiram's western isles|recon mission]] to Eiram's [[Eiram's western isles|western isles]], resulting in the pilot's [[death]].<ref name="Convergence" />

Grieved, Tevin enlisted in E'ronoh's [[fleet]] to fight for his family, with his mother promising to cook him fresh pilafa [[stew]] when he returned home on leave. As he struggled through basic training, the pilot was grateful his mother was unable to witness his struggle.<ref name="Convergence" /> In 382 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Tevin was assigned to the fleet's [[Thylefire Squadron]] on a supply escort [[Skirmish in the Eiram system|mission]] and told his colleagues he missed his mother's stew. While on the assignment, his [[Saboteur|sabotaged]] [[Bly Tevin's devilfighter|devilfighter]] malfunctioned and locked him out of the controls, causing him to panic. After the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]] helped him reset his [[Devilfighter|vessel's]] systems, the pilot reminisced about his childhood growing up under his mother's care. However, when the systems rebooted, he saw they were set to [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destruct]]. In the moments before his death, Tevin thought of his [[time]] with his mother, including watching her cook and when they both witnessed Lina's death.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Personality and traits==
As a citizen of E'ronoh, the woman often visited a [[E'ronoh watchtower|watchtower]] on the planet to wait for the safe return of the enlisted [[soldier]]s, including her son Bly Tevin. Although she was loyal to E'ronoh, she also wished to glimpse the [[Color|turquoise]] [[Ocean|seas]] of Eiram at least once. The woman cared deeply for both of her children, happy to see them enjoying their play even as she struggled to feed their family with meager rations. She was also [[Religion|religious]] and prayed to E'ronoh's [[Deity|gods]]. Tevin thought highly of his mother, considering her the primary reason he enlisted to fight against Eiram&mdash;to protect his family. By the time of the war between E'ronoh and Eiram, she had a thin appearance.<ref name="Convergence" />
