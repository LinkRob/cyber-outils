'''Duracrete grout''' was a substance that could be used to repair damage to [[starship]]s.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> At some point between [[0 ABY]] and [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The entry in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' that mentions duracrete grout details events after [[Han Solo]] has begun working with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], as depicted during the [[Battle of Yavin]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], but before he encounters a [[bounty hunter]] on [[Ord Mantell]], which is mentioned as having occurred prior to the [[Battle of Hoth]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' establishes that the [[Standard year|year]] [[0 ABY]] begins at the end of the Battle of [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]] and dates the Battle of [[Hoth]] to [[3 ABY]]; therefore, the entry in the [[in-universe]] [[book]] ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' must have been written between these two dates.</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] used [[duracrete]] grout to patch up [[stone mite]] bite trails within his [[YT-1300 light freighter]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', after his co-[[pilot]] [[Chewbacca]] removed an infestation of the mites. After several [[Standard hour|hours]] of making repairs, Solo had duracrete grout all over his [[Clothing|shirt]], which he wrote complaints about in the logbook that would eventually become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Duracrete grout was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
