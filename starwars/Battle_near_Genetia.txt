In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] took part in a '''battle near Genetia''' against the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s, during the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Nihil conflict|offensive]] against the Nihil after the [[Deployment of the stormseeds|formation]] of the [[Stormwall]]<ref name="The Force Provides" /> barrier that prevented [[hyperspace]] travel into the [[Occlusion Zone]], a region of Nihil controlled [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The battle, which took place near<ref name="The Force Provides" /> the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Genetia">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Genetia system]]</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Out of the Shadows">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref> [[Genetia]], convinced Rwoh that the Order was lost in the fight against the Nihil and that the [[Galactic Republic]] would have to combat the pirates until the Order found its way again.<ref name="The Force Provides" />

After the battle and dropping Jedi Knight [[Lyssa Votz]] off on Genetia, Rwoh, already dealing with trauma after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]], decided she had had enough and that she would follow the [[Cosmic Force|will]] of [[the Force]].<ref name="The Force Provides" /> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the Mirialan [[Brain|mentally]] reflected on the battle, noting it had been "particularly awful," and the effects it had on her.<ref name="The Force Provides" /> The [[228 BBY|following year]],<ref name="Defy the Storm date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFS}}</ref> Rwoh returned to the Order.<ref name="Defy the Storm">''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The battle near Genetia was first mentioned in the short story "[[The Force Provides]]," written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="The Force Provides" /> An article documenting Verenestra Rwoh's story published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[May 29]], [[2024]], stated that the battle took place on Genetia.<ref name="Who Is Vernestra Rwow">{{SW|url=news/who-is-vernestra-rwoh|text=Who Is Vernestra Rwoh?}}</ref> This articles follows the placing of the battle's location from "The Force Provides."
