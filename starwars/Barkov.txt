'''Barkov''' was a [[Lasat]] member of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[religion|cult]] and its elite subgroup, the [[Children of the Open Hand]]. In [[382 BBY]], during the [[Battle of Jedha]], he and the rest of the Children, under the leadership of [[Werth Plouth]], the [[Herald (title)|Herald]] of the Open Hand, raided many locations in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] in search of the [[Rod of Daybreak]], a [[Force artifact]] that could control the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures. They eventually located it in a [[Unidentified vault|vault]] underneath the fallen [[Final Protector]] [[Jedi]] statue but were discovered by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vildar Mac]] and the [[thief]] [[Tey Sirrek]]. Plouth ordered Barkov to stop the attackers alongside fellow member of the Children [[Shea Ganandra]], but Sirrek defeated the two using the [[Hand of Siberus]], a [[Sith]] [[gauntlet]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Hello, gorgeous. Where have you ''been'' all my life?|Tey Sirrek, while using the Hand of Siberus against Barkov|<ref name="THR 9" />}}
[[File:TeyUsingHOS-2022THR9.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Tey Sirrek (center bottom) uses the Hand of Siberus against Barkov (top left) and Shea Ganandra (top right).]]
Barkov was a [[Lasat]] member of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[religion|cult]]. Because he had shown promise in the past,<ref name="PoV" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Children Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'', part of Phase II of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'', take place in [[382 BBY]] per '[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. Because Barkov was chosen for the [[Children of the Open Hand|Children]] between the [[ambush on Thelj]] as depicted in ''Path of Deceit'' and the [[Battle of Jedha]], which ''The High Republic Character Encyclopedia'' dates to 382 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], he must have joined the Children in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> he was handpicked by one of the cult's leaders, the [[Nautolan]] [[Gender|man]] [[Werth Plouth]] who served as the Path's [[Herald (title)|Herald]], to be a member of the Path's elite subgroup, the [[Children of the Open Hand]], which performed missions and stole [[Force artifact]]s for the Path's [[Mother (title)|Mother]], [[Elecia Zeveron]].<ref name="PoV" /> That year,<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> during the [[Battle of Jedha]], Barkov and the rest of the Children, under the leadership of Plouth, searched and raided many locations in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] of the sacred [[moon]] [[Jedha]], including the [[Temple of the Kyber]] and the [[Dragigan Annals]], in search of the [[Rod of Daybreak]], a Force artifact that could be used to control creatures known as the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]].<ref name="PoV" />

Eventually, Barkov, Plouth, and the rest of the Children confronted [[Jedi Master]] [[Leebon]] and<ref name="THR 7">[[The High Republic (2022) 7|''The High Republic'' (2022) 7]]</ref> [[Jedi Knight]]<ref name="PoV" /> [[Oliviah Zeveron]] in the [[Dunes of Contemplation]], near the [[Final Protector]] [[Jedi]] statue,<ref name="THR 7" /> which had fallen during the battle.<ref name="PoV" /> After Plouth stabbed Zeveron<ref name="THR 7" /> and incapacitated Leebon, he found the [[Nameless control rods|Rod]] of Daybreak in a [[Unidentified vault|vault]] underneath the statue, but he was quickly discovered by<ref name="THR 8">[[The High Republic (2022) 8|''The High Republic'' (2022) 8]]</ref> the [[Kiffar]]<ref name="PoV" /> Jedi Knight [[Vildar Mac]] and the [[Sephi]] [[thief]] [[Tey Sirrek]].<ref name="THR 8" /> The Herald ordered Barkov to stop the two alongside [[Shea Ganandra]],<ref name="THR 9" /> another member of the Children.<ref name="PoV" /> Using a [[staff]], the Lasat fought Sirrek and successfully knocked him down. When Barkov and Ganandra approached Sirrek, the Sephi picked up and put on the [[Hand of Siberus]], a [[Sith]] [[gauntlet]] that had been kept in the [[Bogan Collection|vault]]. Using the gauntlet, Sirrek sent a blast of energy toward the pair, sending them both flying backward.<ref name="THR 9" />

==Personality and traits==
Barkov was a Lasat<ref name="PoV" /> with [[Color|black]] [[hair]]<ref name="THR 9" /> who was considered physically imposing by [[Yana Ro]], a fellow member of the Children of the Open Hand.<ref name="PoV" />

==Skills and abilities==
[[File:TeyGetsHit-2022THR9.jpg|thumb|right|150px|Barkov knocks Tey Sirrek down with a staff.]]
{{Quote|Aah! Will everyone stop hitting me?!|Sirrek, while fighting Barkov and Ganandra|<ref name="THR 9" />}}
Yana Ro did not consider Barkov to be particularly skilled and believed he had been chosen for the Children of the Open Hand for his size, not his deftness.<ref name="PoV" /> However, Barkov was a capable fighter and was able to quickly knock down Tey Sirrek while fighting him using a staff.<ref name="THR 9" />

==Equipment==
During the Battle of Jedha, Barkov fought with a staff and wore full body [[armor]], including a [[Breath mask|respirator mask]] and a large cylindrical object on his back.<ref name="THR 9" />

==Behind the scenes==
Barkov first appeared in the [[The High Republic (2022) 7|seventh issue]] of the [[2022]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2022)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']]. The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Andrea Broccardo]] and [[Marika Cresta]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="THR 7" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]]<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> on [[March 29]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/100742/star_wars_the_high_republic_2022_7|text=Star Wars: The High Republic (2022) #7}}</ref> [[March 8|Three weeks before]] the release of the issue, he was pictured in a preview of the comic in the twelfth episode of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Show]]''.<ref name="High Republic Show">{{HighRepublicShow|f0l-OuG3BVg|High Republic Phase II Deep Dive, Star Wars Celebration, and More!|sw_url=video/high-republic-phase-ii-deep-dive-star-wars-celebration-and-more}}</ref> The character was first identified in the young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'', also written by Scott,<ref name="PoV" /> and published as part of Phase II on [[May 2]], 2023.<ref name="Reader's Guide" />
