The '''DN-25 treadable''' was a model of [[treadable]] developed by [[Pasaana Kitha-Garra-du]] on the [[planet]] [[Pasaana]].

[[Kalo'ne]] drove a DN-25 treadable across Pasaana's [[desert]] terrain during the [[Festival of the Ancestors]] in [[35 ABY]]. A disguised [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] used Kalo'ne's treadable to hide [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], [[Chewbacca]], [[Poe Dameron|Poe]], [[C-3PO]], [[Finn]], and [[BB-8]] from the [[First Order]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
