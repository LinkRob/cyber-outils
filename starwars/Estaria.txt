'''Estaria''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> in the [[Estaria system]] in the [[Indrexu sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that featured a number of [[orbit]]al [[Spaceport|ports]] and was known for its dense civilian traffic. During the [[Battle of Jakku]], the final large-scale engagement of the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[New Republic]],<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[New Republic pilot]] [[Nath Tensent]] noted that he had never seen so many [[starship]]s in one place, considering it more dense than at the Estaria orbital ports and at [[Planet|worlds]] of the [[Commerce Guild]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> conglomerate.<ref name="Build R2 36">{{BuildR2Cite|36|Droid Directory}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Estaria was mentioned in the new [[Canon|''Star Wars'' canon]] in ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the third novel in the [[Alexander Freed]] ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy which was published in [[2021]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Estaria/Legends|Estaria]] was first mentioned in ''[[Hideouts & Strongholds]]''&mdash;a sourcebook for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' which was written by [[Sterling Hershey]] and published in [[1998]]&mdash;with the introduction of "[[Estaria Central Starport]]."<ref name="Hideouts">''[[Hideouts & Strongholds]]''</ref> Estaria was first individually named by "[[Xim Week: The History of Xim and the Tion Cluster]]," an article written by [[Jason Fry]] for [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] in [[2009]].<ref name="Xim Week">{{SW|url=news/essential-atlas-extra-the-history-of-xim-and-the-tion-cluster|text=Essential Atlas Extra: The History of Xim and the Tion Cluster|int=Xim Week: The History of Xim and the Tion Cluster}} {{C|[https://web.archive.org/web/20100202081315/http://starwars.com/fans/hyperspace/source/ximweek01/index.html backup link] to the original [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] article on [[StarWars.com]]}}</ref>
