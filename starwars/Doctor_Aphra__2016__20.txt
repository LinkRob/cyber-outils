'''''Doctor Aphra'' 20''' is the twentieth issue of the [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The comic was written by [[Simon Spurrier]] with art by [[Kev Walker]] and was released on [[May 23]], [[2018]]. The issue contains the story "'''The Catastrophe Con Part I'''," in which [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] is a [[prisoner]] aboard the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Accresker Jail]].

==Publisher's summary==
''BROKEN, INSIDE BEGINS NOW! Aphra is behind bars. Again. But this time, she finds herself in Imperial custody, strapped to an explosive transmitter synced to a single hub-droid. Take one step too far? KABLOOEY! Normally, this wouldn't be an issue to sit still for Aphra &ndash; but what happens when her Imperial captors send her hub-droid to the middle of a war zone? And, uh, what's this rumor about the prison being haunted?''<ref name="Marvel" />

==Plot summary==
===Prisoner of the Empire===
A [[Standard week|week]] after her capture<ref name="Aphra 20">''Doctor Aphra 20''</ref> during a [[Galactic Empire]] [[Second attack on the Son-tuul Pride|attack]] on the [[Son-tuul Pride]] criminal syndicate,<ref name="Aphra 19">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 19|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 19]]</ref> the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] is a [[prisoner]] aboard the mobile Imperial [[wreckage-prison]] [[Accresker Jail]], which consists of a mass of [[starship]] wreckage pulled by a [[cruiser]]. As a prisoner, Aphra is forced to serve in the [[Accresker Penal Legion]], an expendable force deployed into battle by the Empire. While being interrogated by an Imperial [[Unidentified Imperial interrogation officer|officer]], Aphra recounts the events of the previous battle, an [[Attack on an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate|attack]] on a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[frigate]].<ref name="Aphra 20" />

During the battle, Aphra sabotages [[Unidentified hubdroid|the]] [[hubdroid]] supervising [[Chelli Lona Aphra's squad|her squad]], causing it to explode. The explosion [[Death|kills]] all the prisoners in the squad, aside from the fearful [[electrician]] [[Cornelius Evazan|Lopset Yas]]. With the destruction of their hubdroid, the [[proximity bomb]]s implanted in Aphra and Yas' necks threaten to detonate, but the two prisoners are rescued by another hubdroid<ref name="Aphra 20" />&mdash;[[Dek-Nil]], a [[droideka]] modified by Aphra<ref name="Aphra 15">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 15|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 15]]</ref> who had also been collected by the Empire.<ref name="Aphra 19" />

===The homicidal droids===
Meanwhile, on the [[planet]] [[Son-tuul]], the [[assassin droid]] [[0-0-0]], whose status at the front of the Son-tuul Pride was lost in the recent attack, stands within the wreckage of the [[Son-tuul Spaceport]] with fellow assassin droid [[BT-1]]. The droids intend to hunt down Aphra,<ref name="Aphra 20" /> who had previously placed an encryption on the memories of the [[Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix]], which Triple-Zero had forced the archaeologist to retrieve for him.<ref name="Aphra 19" />

===The ghostly figure===
Aboard Accresker Jail, Aphra leads Dek-Nil and Yas away from the battle in an attempt to escape the [[prison]]. They encounter an intact [[escape pod]], trapped at the edge of Accresker Jail's [[Airdome|airshield]]. Before the prisoners can escape, however, an unseen [[The Force|force]] pushes Dek-Nil's [[restraining bolt]] through the [[beskar]] casing that protects his control circuit, allowing the Imperial programming to overtake him. Dek-Nil destroys the escape pod, foiling Aphra's escape plan. As the escape pod explodes, Aphra notices a [[Force spirit|ghostly figure]] nearby, who speaks of justice. The battle comes to an end, and the prisoners are ordered to return to Accresker Jail. Aphra loses sight of the figure, and is forced to give up, retreating back to the prison.<ref name="Aphra 20" />

===A friend in low places===
The officer terminates the interrogation, and his [[Unidentified lieutenant (Accresker Jail)|lieutenant]] concludes that Aphra is hiding something from the [[Interrogation droid|interrogator droid]]. Aphra, with no plan and no hope of escape, is escorted back to the prison by Dek-Nil, who is still under the control of the Imperial restraining bolt. When Aphra returns to her quarters, she meets Yas, who offers her a [[transmitter]] that he built from scrap. With the Empire able to track and jam any unwanted signals, Yas gives Aphra one chance to contact anyone in [[the galaxy]]. At the same [[time]], the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] receives a communication via a [[holocomm]].<ref name="Aphra 20" />
