'''Felucia birds''' were a type of [[bird]] that lived on<ref name="YouTube" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Felucia Databank">{{Databank|felucia|Felucia}}</ref> [[Felucia]].<ref name="YouTube" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> several flew by forces of the [[327th Star Corps]] during the [[Battle of Felucia (Outer Rim Sieges)|Battle of Felucia]], and one of these birds flew near [[Clone Commander]] [[Bly|CC-5052]] and other [[clone trooper]]s as they [[Death|fired on]] [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]].<ref name="rots">{{Film|III}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Felucia birds first appeared in the [[2005]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="rots" /> They were first identified in the current [[canon]] in the video "Every Creature in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' Movies", which was released by the ''Star Wars'' Kids [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel in [[2019]].<ref name="YouTube" />
