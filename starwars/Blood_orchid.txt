'''Blood orchids''' were a type of [[orchid]] that possessed decorative qualities, which made them good gifts. During the [[Rebellion on Akiva|rebellion]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[gangster]] [[Toomata Wree]] searched for a gift to give his [[Gender|girlfriend]], [[Looda]], and considered presenting her with some blood orchids placed inside the shiny head of a [[protocol droid]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blood orchids were mentioned in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
