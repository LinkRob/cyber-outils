'''Flyer Ridge''' was a ridge on [[Typhon's Rock]] in the [[Lost Steppe]] region on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|canon}}
During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] found a [[Tooka]] paw charm in a container on the ridge.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Flyer Ridge appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The Tooka paw charm that the player finds will greatly decrease fall damage when equipped.<ref name="Outlaws" />
