'''Clan Vizsla''' was a [[Mandalorian clan]] that was active during the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], and the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Mandalorian]] [[Jedi]] [[Tarre Vizsla]] served as the [[Mandalore (title)|Mand'alor]] of the Mandalorians sometime prior to the fall of the Old Republic. During the [[Clone Wars]], his descendant [[Pre Vizsla]] was the leader of [[Death Watch]], a Mandalorian splinter faction that opposed the [[planet]]'s [[New Mandalorians|pacifist government]]. He succeeded in [[Takeover of Mandalore|taking over]] [[Mandalore]] and became Mand'alor and [[Prime Minister]], but was [[Death|killed]] shortly thereafter by the renegade [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]].

During the [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|Mandalorian Civil War]] against the Galactic Empire and [[Clan Saxon]], Clan Vizsla rallied behind [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] of [[Clan Kryze]], along with [[Clan Eldar]], [[Clan Rook]], [[Clan Wren]], the [[Journeyman Protector]]s, and others, making Bo-Katan the new Mand'alor.

==Description==
{{Quote|Your mark?''"<br />"''The sign of Clan Vizsla.|Maul and Pre Vizsla on the stylized jai'galaar emblem|<ref name="Eminence" />}}
Clan Vizsla was a [[Mandalorian clan]]<ref name="Eminence" /> that was the leading clan of [[House Vizsla]], one of the [[Politics|political factions]] of [[Mandalorian]] society and government.<ref name="Protector Recon" /> Even during the days of the pacifist [[New Mandalorians|New Mandalorian government]],<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" /> Clan Vizsla was composed entirely of [[Mandalorian warrior]]s.<ref name="Transmission">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/scene-to-stats-mandalorian-super-commandos-2|text=Scene to Stats: Mandalorian Super Commandos}}</ref>

The ancient emblem of the clan was a stylized [[shriek-hawk]], or ''jai'galaar'' in [[Mando'a]]. Eventually, the Vizsla shriek-hawk was adopted by the entire Death Watch faction.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> By the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]], Vizsla warriors adorned their armor with a different emblem, representing a [[flower]]ed branch.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" /> Clan Vizsla had a rivalry with [[Clan Tal]].<ref name="Star Wars: Hunters">{{HuntersCite|character=Aran Tal|costume=Clan Vizsla Warrior}}</ref>

==History==
===During the Old Republic===
[[File:Tarre_Vizsla_statue.png|thumb|left|150px|The statue of Tarre Vizsla, the ancient Mand'alor and Jedi]]
{{Quote|I didn't know Mandalorians developed a type of lightsaber.''"<br />"''We didn't. This was one of a kind. Legend tells that it was created over a thousand years ago by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian ever inducted into the Jedi Order. After his passing, the Jedi kept the saber in their temple. That was until members of House Vizsla snuck in and liberated it. They used the saber to unify the people and strike down those who would oppose them. One time, they ruled all of Mandalore wielding this blade.|[[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Fenn Rau]], on the darksaber|<ref name="Trials of the Darksaber" />}}
Clan Vizsla was formed prior to the foundation of the modern [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Note" /> During this time, [[Tarre Vizsla]] was inducted into the [[Jedi Order]] as the first Mandalorian to do so.<ref name="Complete Season Three">[[Star Wars Rebels: Complete Season Three|''Star Wars Rebels'': Complete Season Three]], "Trials of the Darksaber" DVD commentary</ref> As a [[Jedi]], Tarre created the [[Darksaber]], a unique [[Color|black]]-colored [[lightsaber]] that became the symbol of leadership for [[House Vizsla]].<ref name="Trials of the Darksaber" /> Eventually, Tarre became [[Mandalore (title)|Mand'alor]] and led his fellow Mandalorians.<ref name="Art History" />
After Tarre's [[death]], the [[Sword|saber]] was kept in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] until it was stolen by members of House Vizsla who snuck in and liberated it<ref name="Trials of the Darksaber" /> during the [[fall of the Old Republic]].<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" /> The members of House Vizsla used the Darksaber to unify the people and defeat those who opposed them, allowing them to rule all of Mandalore at one moment.<ref name="Trials of the Darksaber" /> During that time, the Darksaber was used to kill many Jedi.<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" />

===During the modern Galactic Republic===
====Early coup attempt====
{{Quote|This lightsaber was stolen from your Jedi Temple by my ancestors during the fall of the Old Republic. Since then, many Jedi have died upon its blade. Prepare yourself to join them.|Pre Vizsla, to Obi-Wan Kenobi|audio=Darksaber-TMP.flac|<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" />}}
Many [[Standard year|years]] later following the end of the [[Great Clan Wars|Mandalorian Civil War]], the Mandalorian warriors were exiled to the [[moon]] of [[Concordia]], where most of them were believed to have died out. The survivors, however, regrouped and secretly rallied behind Tarre Vizsla's descendant, [[Pre Vizsla]], [[governor]] of Concordia, and became known as the [[Death Watch]]. The Death Watch opposed the pacifist New Mandalorian government and its leader, [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" /> The Death Watch believed that Duchess Satine presented a weak-minded rule and degraded Mandalorian traditions and their reputation as fierce warriors.<ref name="Eminence" />

Clan Vizsla remained a warrior faction<ref name="Transmission" /> despite Kyrze's pacifist policies. While Vizsla attempted to reignite the Mandalorian warrior tradition through Death Watch and gathered a sizeable following,<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" /> Clan Viszla, by and large, was a disenfranchised clan during this time.<ref name="Transmission" /> Early during the Clone Wars, Pre Vizsla and the Death Watch allied themselves with the [[Sith Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]], leader of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] that fought against the Galactic Republic. After a [[Attack on the Memorial Shrine|bombing]] of the [[Memorial Shrine]] in [[Sundari]], Mandalore's capital, and subsequent [[fight on Concordia]], Pre Vizsla and his organization were exposed and forced to retreat.<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" /> After an [[Skirmish aboard the Coronet|failed attempt]] to capture Duchess Satine on the ''[[Coronet]]'' by Mandalorian [[Prince]] and [[Senator]] [[Tal Merrik]] on behalf of Death Watch, Duchess Satine was able to present her case to the [[Galactic Senate]] to maintain her planet's neutrality in the Clone Wars.<ref name="Voyage of Temptation">{{TCW|Voyage of Temptation}}</ref>

Working with the Confederacy, Death Watch and Pre Vizsla attempted to orchestrate a Republic occupation of Mandalore, so that Pre Vizsla and Death Watch could move in to liberate the people, and thus usurp Duchess Satine from power. The coup ultimately fell apart, when Duchess Satine managed to persuade the Senate on Mandalore's position.<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore">{{TCW|Duchess of Mandalore}}</ref>

====Exiled and in retreat====
Following the failed coup, Pre Vizsla and with Dooku had a confrontation which resulted in Death Watch and the Confederacy severing ties. Eventually, Death Watch was located on [[Carlac]], where they subjugated the local [[Ming Po]] population and entered in league with [[Lux Bonteri]] in an attempt to [[assassin]]ate Dooku. Unknowingly, Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], who was with Bonteri, was revealed as a Jedi and following a [[skirmish on Carlac]], Tano and Bonteri escaped Death Watch and Pre Vizsla.<ref name="Friend in Need" />

====Rise to power====
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Pre Vizsla and Death Watch came across an [[escape pod]] containing the rogue Sith [[Lord]] [[Maul]] and his brother and [[Sith apprentice]], [[Savage Opress]]. Curious about their situation, Pre decided to rescue them and bring them to [[Zanbar]]. After the two recovered, they agreed to an alliance to bring down Duchess Satine and seek revenge against [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Pre Vizsla and his Death Watch forces, alongside Maul and Opress traveled to [[Mustafar]] and recruited the [[Black Sun]] after killing their [[Black Sun ruling council|ruling council]]. The [[Pyke Syndicate]] also joined them after hearing of their move to build an [[army]]. Eventually, after a [[mission to Nal Hutta]] and [[attack on Jabba's Palace]], the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] joined Pre Vizsla and the others, forming the [[Shadow Collective]].<ref name="Eminence" />

Now with an army and the resources required to sustain a war without the scrutiny of the Galactic Republic, Maul revealed to Pre Vizsla that after Mandalore was under Vizsla's control, Maul would then operate a new criminal underworld. Frustrated, Pre Vizsla suggested that Mandalore was the leader of the [[Council of Neutral Systems]], which contained 1,500 worlds, and thus allowed for more opportunities. Satisfied, Maul then ordered Vizsla to send [[scout]]s to Mandalore in order to identify targets vital to Mandalore's security. [[Bo-Katan Kryze]], top [[lieutenant]] to Pre Vizsla, expressed her concerns that Maul and Opress could not be trusted, but Vizsla assured her that Maul and Savage would be dead alongside Duchess Satine.<ref name="Shades">{{TCW|Shades of Reason}}</ref>

====Taking Mandalore====
[[File:VizslaByeBye.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Pre Vizsla is beheaded by Maul]]
{{Quote|I am Pre Vizsla of Clan Vizsla. Death Watch is here to save you from these intergalactic gangsters that threaten our great city. This is a war, and we will win. Join me, and let us defend Mandalore against the criminals. We need action, not pacifism!|Pre Vizsla, to his fellow Mandalorians|<ref name="Shades" />}}
Vizsla's advanced guard acquired their targets, which allowed Maul and the Shadow Collective to devise their strategy. The crime syndicates attacked key targets, and, all according to plan, members of Death Watch arrested them, winning the hearts of the people. Duchess Satine was subsequently captured and removed from power by Vizsla and his forces. He declared himself [[Prime Minister]] and Mand'alor.<ref name="Shades" />

Then, he betrayed and imprisoned Maul and Opress. However, they managed to liberate themselves and bring former Prime Minister [[Almec]] under their fold. Subsequently, Maul challenged Vizsla for his place as leader of Mandalore and Death Watch, ultimately triumphing over the Mandalorian and beheading him.<ref name="Shades" /> Maul's arrival onto the scene finally revitalized Clan Vizsla and various other Mandalorian warrior clans, whose soldiers rallied behind him in the name of restoring Mandalorian tradition.<ref name="Transmission" />

However, Bo-Katan and other Death Watch members refused to accept Maul and fled to create the [[Mandalore resistance]], igniting another [[Mandalorian Civil War (19 BBY)|Mandalorian civil war]].<ref name="Shades" /> Eventually, the Galactic Republic invaded Mandalore and Maul was removed from power. Bo-Katan was installed as [[Regent]] of the planet as the Republic [[Proclamation of the New Order|transitioned]] into the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref>

===During the Galactic Empire===
[[File:Clan_Vizsla_Imperial_Era.png|thumb|left|190px|Clan Vizsla declares for Bo-Katan Kryze]]
{{Quote|Clan Vizsla is with you.|A member of Clan Vizsla declares for Bo-Katan Kryze|<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />}}
Lady Regent Bo-Katan Kryze was removed from power by the Empire during its [[occupation of Mandalore]]. Nearly two [[Standard decade|decades]] after the end of the Clone Wars, another [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|civil war]] ignited when Imperial [[Viceroy]] [[Gar Saxon]] of [[Clan Saxon]] was killed during a [[skirmish on Krownest]] against [[Clan Wren]]. After a [[mission to Mandalore]] by members of Clan Wren and [[Clan Kryze]], Governor [[Tiber Saxon]] was killed. Bo-Katan became the new Mand'alor, gaining the support of Clan Vizsla, [[Clan Rook]], [[Clan Eldar]], the [[Journeyman Protector]]s, Clan Wren, and others.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />

===During the New Republic===
{{Quote|Fate has brought this blade back to my clan, and now fate will end yours!|Paz Vizsla, to Din Djarin|<ref name="Return" />}}
[[File:Ragnar-YearInReview2023.png|thumb|right|250px|Ragnar Vizsla, son of Paz Vizsla and member of the Tribe.]]
[[Paz Vizsla]],<ref name="Return" /> and his son, [[Ragnar Vizsla|Ragnar]],<ref name="Foundling">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 20: The Foundling}}</ref> were members of the Mandalorian sect known as the [[Children of the Watch]], were members of Clan Vizsla and descendants of Tarre Vizsla. When he learned that [[Din Djarin]] had come into possession of the Darksaber, he attempted to claim it by [[Fight for the Darksaber|dueling]] him on [[Glavis Ringworld]]. Although Vizsla managed to take the saber and briefly wield it, Djarin ultimately won the duel by placing him in a lock and putting a [[Vibroknife|vibro-knife]] to his throat.<ref name="Return" />

Paz Vizsla was eventually reunited with his son on a [[Armorer's planet|planet]]. There, Ragnar undertook the [[Mandalorian initiation ritual]] and was given a helmet. However, the ritual was interrupted by a [[lake monster]].<ref name="Apostate">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}}</ref> Later, while under Imperial attack on Mandalore, Vizsla sacrificed himself to allow his fellow Mandalorians to escape.<ref name="The Spies">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 23: The Spies}}</ref>
