'''Ca Isiora''' was a [[rebel pilot]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Isiora flew with [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]] during the [[Battle of Endor]] alongside her husband [[Telem]].<ref name="Galactic Tales: Shockwave" />

==Biography==
Isiora was a worker at the [[Green Ring of Nenyezh]], the same place where [[Ca Isiora's mother|her mother]] would [[Death|die]] in a house [[fire]] incited by the [[Galactic Empire|empire]]; it was there she met Telem, the man who would eventually become her husband, while he was working as a performer. They dated, broke up and started dating again after evacuating their homes due to the empire burning their houses and taking control of Nenyezh. At some point they were separated during the [[scouring of the Hamnibus Glade]].<ref name="Galactic Tales: Shockwave" />

===Stranded on Chadawa===
During a visit to the [[planet]] [[Chadawa]], Isiora and Telem became stranded at an unpopulated section of the planet. After days of arguing and hopelessness, Isiora would eventually find a still operational [[X-wing starfighter|X-Wing]], lacking only a way to fly it. She and Telem would construct an [[astromech droid]] out of crashed [[satellite]] parts, deciding to call it [[M3-LOD]] "Melody".<ref name="Galactic Tales: Shockwave" />

===Serving the Rebellion===
Both Ca and Telem would end up becoming [[starfighter]] pilots for the rebellion, serving under Red Squadron, swapping which one Melody would accompany with each mission. They would serve until at least the Battle of Endor where Isiora would witness the destruction of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Second Death Star]], becoming temporarily blinded as a result.<ref name="Galactic Tales: Shockwave" />

==Personality and traits==
Isiora is seen to be an experienced pilot, being able to maneuver and land an X-Wing while being mostly blinded.<ref name="Galactic Tales: Shockwave" />

She also expresses discontent at her current relationship, citing it as stale and predictable. She holds more affection to her droid, Melody, stating that it is the only reason she and Telem are still together.<ref name="Galactic Tales: Shockwave" />
