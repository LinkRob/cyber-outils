'''Aisha Freitas''' was a senior analyst within [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Alliance Intelligence]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Freitas was in an argument with [[Colonel]] [[Haxen Delto]] on an unverified intelligence report about [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|another rumored Death Star]]. Eventually [[General]] [[Davits Draven]] was informed, who later sent a report to [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
