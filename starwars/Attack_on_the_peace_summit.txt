The '''attack on the peace summit'''<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> was a bombing which occurred at the [[Second Spire]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] during a [[Summit on Jedha|summit]] that was intended to see the signing of a [[Eiram–E'ronoh peace treaty|treaty]] between the [[planet]]s [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]] to end their [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|war]].<ref name="Battle of Jedha" />
