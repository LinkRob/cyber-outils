==Behind the scenes==
The bantha tick was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Bounty Hunters 19|nineteenth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Ramón F. Bachs|Ramon Bachs]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="BH19" /> on [[December 29]], [[2021]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/96835/star_wars_bounty_hunters_2020_19|text=Star Wars: Bounty Hunters (2020) #19}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, a [[bantha tick]] was first mentioned in "[[You're in the Army Now!]]", an adventure for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published in [[May]] [[1994]].<ref name="cargo bay">{{JournalCite|2|You're in the Army Now!}}</ref>
