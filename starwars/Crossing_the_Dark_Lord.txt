"'''Crossing the Dark Lord'''" is a short story written by [[Grant S. Boucher]], originally published in ''[[Galaxy Guide 1: A New Hope]]''. It was also included in ''[[The Movie Trilogy Sourcebook]]''.

{{Shortstory-stub}}

==Plot summary==
In a story based on the accounts of a number of [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] officers that defected to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]], as told to [[Voren Na'al/Legends|Voren Na'al]], a [[Star Destroyer/Legends|Star Destroyer]] [[Captain/Legends|captain]] uses security cameras to watch [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] while he is meditating. After he hears Vader vowing to track down the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]] infiltrators, the captain mocks him out loud. Vader then looks into the camera, reveals that he heard the captain's joke, and then [[Telekinesis/Legends|strangles]] him through [[The Force/Legends|the Force]].
