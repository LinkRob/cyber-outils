'''Brackis''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who was a member of a criminal [[Sisay's gang|gang]] on [[Aloxor]] led by [[Sisay]]. When Sisay's gang ran into the undercover [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] and [[Brierly Ronan]], who were on Aloxor as part of a [[Mission to Aloxor|mission]] for [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], they were captured by Sisay and her gang, who were suspicious of them. [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[agent]] [[Dayja Collerand]], who was also undercover on the [[planet]], found Sisay's gang and incapacitated Brackis and the other members, saving the Imperial personnel.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
