The '''bo-rifle''' was a type of weapon that uniquely combined the functions of an [[electrostaff]] and a [[blaster rifle]], intended for exclusive use by the [[Lasan High Honor Guard|Honor Guard]] of [[Lasan]]. It was considered a great insult if it was used by anyone else. The only known exception to this was if the individual in question had defeated a guardsmen and been presented with their weapon, in accordance with the [[Boosahn Keeraw]], the warrior way of the [[Lasat]] [[species]].

==Description==
It consisted of a [[staff]] that could be collapsed to a length roughly equivalent to a standard blaster rifle. When extended, the weapon's tip(s) were electrified, up to 11,000 volts, either purple or yellow in color<ref name="Star Wars Rebels: Visual Guide: Epic Battles">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Visual Guide: Epic Battles]]''</ref> and were the primary contact points of the weapon during melee combat. The number of tips on the weapon depended on the model (AB-75 and J-19 had two, the CJ-9 had one). The weapon functioned as a [[quarterstaff]] when used in melee combat. When retracted or folded, the weapon functioned as a blaster rifle.<ref name="Rebels: The Visual Guide" /> The bo-rifle's tips emitted an [[electromagnetic pulse]] that stunned opponents and neutralized [[ray shield]]s. The bo-staff configuration can activate by twisting the top handle downward, pressing both handles inward, and then activating both EMP tips.<ref name="Comprehensive Guide">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:DV45-Stephen_Segovia-Kallustextless.png|thumb|left|250px|Agent Kallus of the ISB wields a J-19 bo-rifle.]]
The bo-rifle was the exclusive weapon of the [[Lasan High Honor Guard]]. It was an insult to [[Lasat]] culture for anyone else to wield the weapon<ref name="Droids in Distress" /> unless it had been won in combat via the [[Boosahn Keeraw]], the Lasat warrior way.<ref name="The Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref> Following the [[Siege of Lasan|devastation]] of Lasan, bo-rifles were rarely seen in [[the galaxy]],<ref name="Rebels: The Visual Guide" /> although some [[CJ-9 electrified bo-rifle|CJ-9 bo-rifles]] would later fall into the hands of both the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] by the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Battlefront" />

[[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]], a member of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Security Bureau]] who took part in the siege of Lasan, acquired his [[J-19 bo-rifle]] via the Boosahn Keeraw. During the battle, he defeated a Honor Guard, who gifted Kallus his rifle as a sign of the agent's superior ability in combat. Kallus was surprised by the action but accepted.<ref name="The Honorable Ones" /> A Lasat survivor named [[Garazeb Orrelios]] continued to use a [[AB-75 bo-rifle]] and went onto duel Kallus during the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Droids in Distress" /> Orrelios originally believed that Kallus had taken his J-19 as a trophy, only to learn that his enemy had indeed earned it via the Boosahn Keeraw during a period of time where both were trapped on [[Geonosian]] [[water|ice]] [[moon]] of [[Bahryn]].<ref name="The Honorable Ones" /> The incident would prove to be life changing for Kallus, who defected to the rebellion.<ref name="Inside Man">{{Rebels|An Inside Man}}</ref>
