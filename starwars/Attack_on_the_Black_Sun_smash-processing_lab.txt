Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> the [[Orphans]] attacked a [[Black Sun smash-processing lab]] on [[Dantooine]], during the [[Syndicate War]]. The [[Laboratory|lab]] was developing a formula to make the drug [[smash]] more potent to more [[species]]. The Orphans were hired by [[Crimson Dawn]] leader, [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]], to destroy the base. The group successfully destroyed the lab without the [[Black Sun]] learning who was responsible causing the criminal syndicate to believe another syndicate was responsible. News of the attack on the facility made its way onto the [[Shadowfeed]]s, including footage of the facility burning with the [[Aurebesh]] text "Black Sun is in eclipse" displayed over it.<ref name="CR1" />
