The '''Ferrix Rising''' was a costume owned by the [[Ewok]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Pilbush]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Explosive Ewok|standard outfit]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ferrix Rising was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Pilbush, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Ferrix AT-ET]].<ref name="Hunters" />
