'''Cliffhold''' was a [[spaceport]] [[city]] that was built directly into the face of a [[basalt]] cliff on the [[planet]] [[Iego]]. By the time of the [[Clone Wars]], Cliffhold was much less busy than in previous times due to the [[spice]] convoys no longer running through the area.<ref name="Mystery of a Thousand Moons" />
