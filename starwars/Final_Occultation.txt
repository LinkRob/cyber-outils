The '''Final Occultation''' was a [[Religion|cult]] which operated during the [[Republic Era]] and sought to bring chaos to [[the galaxy]] through the use of the chaotic and anarchic aspects of [[the Force]] which flowed from the [[Netherworld of the Force|Netherworld]]. Led by the three [[priest]]s [[Heldi Cerebron]], [[Vasik Aldritch]], and an "[[Unidentified Final Occultation leader|emissary of chaos]]," most of the members of the cult were detained by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] for attempting to expose him. Placed on a [[prison ship]] en route to [[prison]] [[planet]], the [[Starship|ship]]'s [[officer]]s were quickly overrun by the cultists aboard, who began a ritual to open a rift to madness and echo screams across the galaxy. Sent by his master Sidious, the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]] [[Mission to a prison ship|travelled]] to the ship and killed at least two of the leaders, before crashing the ship into an [[Unidentified ice planet|ice planet]] below.

==Description==
{{Quote|The prisoners belong to an order known as the Final Occultation. They are aided by chaotic forces that flow from the Netherworld.|Darth Maul|<ref name="Darth Maul 1" />}}
The Final Occultation was a [[The Force|Force]]-based [[Religion|cult]]<ref name="Darth Maul 1" /> which sought to bring chaos,<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/110338/star_wars_darth_maul_-_black_white_red_2024_1|text=Star Wars: Darth Maul &ndash; Black, White & Red (2024) #1}}</ref> anarchy, and pain to [[the galaxy]]. In this task they were aided by forces originating from the [[Netherworld of the Force]],<ref name="Darth Maul 1" /> an afterlife<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> where the [[soul]]s of every being were believed to go after the [[death]] of their physical bodies in [[The Force|Force]]-based societies.<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> The cult rejected the binary approach of the [[Light side of the Force|light]] and [[dark side of the Force|dark sides of the Force]] as promoted by the [[Sith]] Order<ref name="Darth Maul 1" /> and the [[Jedi Order]],<ref name="ROTS" /> instead believing both sides to be the same thing. They viewed the light side and dark side as ways of order, which they sought to replace with chaos as a superior system of [[Universe|universal]] rule,<ref name="Darth Maul 1" /> though they still drew the power from dark side channels.<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/marvel-darth-maul-black-white-and-red-reveal|text=Marvel's ''Darth Maul – Black, White & Red'' Coming April 2024 - Exclusive}}</ref>

The cult was led by a trinity of [[priest]]s including [[Heldi Cerebron]], [[Vasik Aldritch]], and a [[Unidentified Final Occultation leader|third leader]]. Cereberon had the ability to release spores from her exposed [[brain]], capable of inflicting visions on others' minds. Aldritch was a fighter who centered his disorderly combat style around his strong physical attributes amd the third leader considered themselves to be a "emissary of chaos".<ref name="Darth Maul 1" />

==History==
===Opposition to the Sith and imprisonment===
{{Quote|A transport has gone dark on its way to a prion planet. It contains our enemies.|Darth Sidious to Darth Maul|<ref name="Darth Maul 1" />}}
During the [[Republic Era]] and height of the [[Jedi]] Order, the [[Sith Lord]]s [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Maul|Darth Maul]] operated in hiding from the Jedi and hoped to eventually inflict revenge upon the Order,<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> following the Sith's [[Defeat of the Sith|defeat]] and apparent destruction [[Standard year|years]] prior during the [[Jedi-Sith War]].<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> During this time frame, Sidious operated in secret as [[Senator]] Palpatine of his [[homeworld]] [[Naboo]].<ref name="TPM" /> Between [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the Final Occultation discovered the existence of the two Sith Lords and sought to expose them. The cult coveted the Sith's power and hoped that the Sith would be extinguished before they could take over the galaxy. Before they were able to expose Sidious and Maul, the Sidious' efforts resulted in the detainment of all three leaders as well as dozens of members. The cult members were placed aboard a [[Unidentified prison ship|prison ship]] en route to a [[prison]] [[planet]].<ref name="Darth Maul 1" />

===Prison ship overrun===
{{Quote|Do you know what it is we worship? Chaos and pain!|Vasik Aldritch to Darth Maul|<ref name="Darth Maul 1" />}}
[[File:HeldiCerebronVision-DarthMaulBlackWhiteAndRed1.png|thumb|right|250px|Maul's spore-inflicted vision featured his master Darth Sidious (right) and attacker Heldi Cerebron (left).]]
While traveling over an [[Unidentified ice planet|ice planet]], the Final Occultation members aboard the ship took overran and took over the [[Starship|ship]], causing it to exit [[hyperspace]]. At this point, the third Final Occultation member congregated with many of the cult's followers to use the ship's [[hypermatter]] in order to open a rift to madness. After noticing that the ship had gone dark, Sidious attempted to establish contact, but he merely received a transmission of a [[humanoid]] creature. Sidious described to Maul that the audio sounded like thousands crying out in pain, and described the imagery as that of disorder. He continued to state that such was the stuff of [[Dream|nightmares]] and unstable [[Magic|dark magic]], and instructed Maul to [[Mission to a prison ship|venture]] to the prison [[transport]] to arrest and detain the cult once more.<ref name="Darth Maul 1" />

Upon arrival, Maul entered a hallway which had become overrun with Cerebron's spores. The Sith Lord encountered an [[Unidentified human (Heldi Cerebron)|afflicted officer]] who informed him that the spores were Heldi's doing. Behind Maul, Heldi appeared and the spores began to affect Maul as well. Having terrifying visions featuring Sidious, Cerebron, and a large beast, Maul called upon his training and fought through the chaos in his mind. He used his [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|lightsaber]] to slice the cultist in half. Continuing to the detention block, where corpses of stationed [[officer]]s were left scattered along the floor. Maul then encountered Aldritch, who began to shoot at him with his [[blaster cannon]]. Maul attempted to utilize precision to counteract Aldritch's bigger weight and slowness, but he was overwhelmed by the cultist's disorderly approach to combat. Eventually, as Aldritch grabbed Maul, the latter was able to use the Force to pull his [[lightsaber]] from afar and cut the former's [[Limb|forearms]]. Before plunging his weapon into Aldritch's chest, Maul inquired the location of the final priest, to which Aldritch informed him to follow the screams.<ref name="Darth Maul 1" />

===Boiling red planet===
{{Quote|The hypermatter particles won't take this ship to hyperspace. They will open a rift of madness. Screams will echo across the galaxy. All will live on a red boiling planet.|The third priest, to Darth Maul|<ref name="Darth Maul 1" />}}
[[File:FinalOccultationLeader-DarthMaulBlackWhiteAndRed1.png|thumb|left|300px|The "emissary of chaos" led the Final Occultation summoning ritual to open a rift of madness.]]
Following the screams Maul arrived in a massive open area, filled with cultists led by the third and final priest. The priest explained to Maul of the cult's beliefs, explaining that after they had finished opening the rift to madness, screams would echo across the galaxy and that all would live on a boiling [[Color|red]] planet. Maul was offered a position as a [[High Priest|high priest]] by the leader given he had decided to stop his assault of the cultists, but Maul instead decided to throw his lightsaber into the rift's origin point, destroying the ceremony. After the Final Occultation leader sent the remaining followers after Maul, the Sith Lord decided to run for the ship's control room, using the controls to manuever the ship into the [[Water|ice]] planet below. Despite knowing that his master requested to maintain the cult as prisoners, Maul deliberately decided to crash land the ship to kill all the cultists inside, believing that it was not worth the interrogation or enlistment of the Final Occultation. Maul himself cut a whole in the ship so that he could escape, leaving the ship to crash land into the snow. Establishing [[Holocomm|communications]] with his master, he questioned why his master did not wipe them out immediately instead of detain them. Sidious stated that he sensed much confusion in Maul and believed that the Final Occultation had broke Maul, with Maul replying that they nearly broke the galaxy.<ref name="Darth Maul 1" />
