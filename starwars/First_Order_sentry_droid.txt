{{Quote|Easily recognized by their distinctive white chassis, First Order sentry droids patrol the regime's starships and military installations alert for intruders and any hint of dissent in the ranks.|The ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''|<ref name="Galactic Explorer's Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref>}}
The '''First Order sentry droid''' was a [[Color|white]] plated model of [[sentry droid]] station on the [[First Order]]'s [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]] around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]. These [[droid]]s possessed a square or circular head with several antennae protruding from the top, which sat on a body with four short [[Limb|legs]] ending in rolling casters that allowed the sentries to move easily through the base interiors. Sentry droids also patrolled the corridors of the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]''.<ref name="TFAVD" /> They also had a series of armaments equipped inside of them, including four tasers and two [[blaster]]s. These armaments folded out of the droid's chassis as its head extended upwards.<ref name="SWFB2" /> [[Unidentified First Order sentry droid|One sentry droid]] used these armaments to [[Mission to Station Theta Black|attack]] [[Resistance]] [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] and [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] on [[Station Theta Black]], though it was ultimately destroyed by Dameron's [[Glie-44 blaster pistol]].<ref name="STB">{{Resistance|Station Theta Black (episode)}}</ref> Three sentry droids patrol in the [[supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Titan]]''.<ref name="Station to Station>{{Resistance|Station to Station}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The First Order's sentry droids first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which was released in [[2015]].<ref name="VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> They were not named in the film, but were identified as sentry droids in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="TFAVD" />

The fold-out armaments of the droid first were first depicted in the [[2017]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'', where it can be deployed as an ability of [[Captain]] [[Phasma]].<ref name="SWFB2" /> However, they were originally designed for "[[Station Theta Black (episode)|Station Theta Black]]"<ref name="BucketsListSTB">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-station-theta-black-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 11 Fun Facts from "Station Theta Black" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref>&mdash;the eleventh episode of ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'s'' [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]]&mdash;which would not premiere until one year later on [[December 9]], [[2018]].<ref name="STBAirdate">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref>

The droid's entry in the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] erroneously states that the droids' only appearance is in ''Resistance'',<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|first-order-sentry-droid|First Order Sentry Droid}}</ref> despite its appearances in the [[Sequel trilogy]]<ref name="VII" /> and ''Battlefront II''.<ref name="SWFB2" />
