'''Corals''' were non-moving [[plant]]-like organisms that secreted a hard external skeleton and grew either above ground or [[Water|underwater]] depending on the [[species]]. Coral forests were hot and dry environments where [[reptile]]s and [[insect]]s thrived.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The oceans of the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]] were filled with lush coral reefs,<ref name="Mon Cala">{{Databank|mon-cala|Mon Cala}}</ref> amid which the native [[Mon Calamari]] built beautiful, organic-looking [[City|cities]].<ref name="SWE Mon Calamari">{{SWE|groups|moncalamari|Mon Calamari}}</ref> [[Rugosa]], a [[moon]] located in the vicinity of [[Toydaria]], had "[[forest]]s" of above-ground corals.<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> Similarly, a dry [[Atollon Coral Mesa|mesa]] covered in above-ground corals existed on the planet [[Atollon]].<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base">{{Rebels|The Mystery of Chopper Base}}</ref> A massive coral reef covered the entire oceanic planet of [[Pillio]].<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|pillio|Pillio}}</ref> The organism shared its name with a color featured in the names of various costumes and [[weapon]] wraps used by [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]]s of the [[Grand Arena]] on [[Vespaara]] including [[Aran Tal]], [[Imara Vex]], and [[J-3DI]]<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date2">{{HuntersDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'', corals are classified as plants.<ref name="VE" /> However, in real life, they are in fact sessile animals that secrete a calcareous skeleton.<ref name="Corals IRL">{{WebCite|url=https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/coral.html|text=Are corals animals or plants?|work=National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) website|archivedate=20200728210238}}</ref>
