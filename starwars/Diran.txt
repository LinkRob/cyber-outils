'''Diran''' was an individual who had planned to flee the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]] in order to escape the [[Galactic Empire]] by [[3 ABY]]. Diran's plan included convincing [[Nih]], a [[Tosharan]] [[farmer]], to come with them. 

==Biography==
[[File:Toshara-Outlaws.png|thumb|right|300px|Toshara, the moon that Diran planned to leave]]
Diran was an individual who resided on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Diran had planned to leave Toshara with a group of people in order to escape the Empire.<ref name="Outlaws" />

The individual's plans included taking a [[Tosharan]] [[farmer]] named [[Nih]] with the group. They wrote a data message three [[Standard day|days]] before their planned departure, detailing their plans and noting that they were aware Nih would need convincing, but that they were sure she would come around to the need to leave.<ref name="Outlaws" />

Diran's message went on to mention that they had stashed a getaway bag in a [[cave]] south of [[Gushiro Canyon]] on Toshara in case the group decided to leave via the canyon route toward the [[Pyke poaching station]]. A [[datapad]] containing Diran's data message had been left at a [[Unidentified windfarm (Lost Steppe)|windfarm]] in the [[Lost Steppe]] region of Toshara<ref name="Outlaws" /> by 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate" />

{{GameMechanics}}
That [[Standard year|year]], the [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] found the datapad and read Diran's message. She later traveled to the cave described in the message, discovering a [[Miyuki Trade League]] [[Smuggler Cache 22|smuggler cache]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Diran was mentioned on the "It is done" datapad entry in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. Reading the datapad starts the "Getaway Stash" intel quest, which directs the player to the cave with Smuggler Cache 22.<ref name="Outlaws" />
