'''Crukibolt Nart Adeen''', known as '''Crufeela''', was a [[Corellian]] [[Shani]] [[Gender|female]] [[Musician|singer]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. Notorious for her penchant for [[Diet|eating]] creatures from endangered [[species]], Crufeela was wanted in multiple [[star system]]s for violating environmental laws. On one occasion, after a scandal involving endangered [[papdoo]]s, Crufeela attempted to make a comeback by performing the [[Music|song]] "[[Corellia for Corellians]]" on a [[Unidentified galactic talent show|galactic talent show]], but it was quickly discovered that she was lying about having written the song and had been lip-synching to a voice recording of a [[Unidentified Naboo teenager|Naboo teenager]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> Crufeela was hired to perform at the [[Finance Ball]] after [[Svi'no Atchapat]] had to pull out. Atchapat's friend [[Alys Ongwa]], the head of [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection]], was working with the [[Jedi]] to uncover [[Nihil]] corruption in the [[Coronet City]] government, and set up a sting operation with [[Padawan]]s [[Reath Silas]] and [[Ram Jomaram]] to entrap Crufeela with a meal from an endangered species, leading to her arrest by the [[Coronet City Chapter of the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
