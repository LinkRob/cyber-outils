'''Clouzon-36''' was a type of [[Color|blue-green]] [[gas]] that was used as a [[hyperdrive]] [[fuel]] for [[starship]]s. It was also inhaled by [[vacuum]]-based lifeforms such as [[gralloc]]s and [[purrgil]], the latter of which used it to power their natural ability to jump into [[hyperspace]].

==Description==
Clouzon-36 was a rare<ref name="Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref> but powerful [[fuel]]; according to [[Sabine Wren]], one decent-sized shipment of Clouzon-36 was enough to keep the entire fleet of the [[Phoenix Cell]] fully fueled for a whole cycle.<ref name="The Call" /> Standard starship [[sensor]]s had trouble identifying the gas at a distance, making it difficult to locate spills or deposits.<ref name="Treason" />

Clouzon-36 conduits on [[starship]]s were generally well armored to prevent leakage,<ref name="Treason" /> though they were sometimes exposed as a result of starship combat.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> Containers of the gas were identifiable by markings of [[Color|red]]-yellow-red-white-red.<ref name="Treason" />

Although species such as [[human]]s and [[Lasat]]s could not breathe in Clouzon-36, [[Rodian]]s and [[Moyn]] could.<ref name="The Call Trivia">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/the-call-episode-gallery|text="The Call" Episode Gallery - Star Wars Rebels}}</ref>

==History==
During the [[Imperial Era]], hijacked Clouzon-36 was on the permanent watchlist of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]].<ref name="Treason" /> In [[3 BBY]], the [[Spectres|crew]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'' [[Heist at the Asteroid Belt Gas Refinery|raided]] the [[Mining Guild]]'s [[Asteroid Belt Gas Refinery]], hoping to steal Clouzon-36 to replenish the Phoenix Cell's fuel supplies.<ref name="The Call" />

In his corrupt scheme to steal components of the [[Project Stardust]], [[Grand Admiral]] [[Balanhai Savit]] employed Clouzon-36 canisters to attract [[gralloc]]s to his [[freighter]]s, allowing their crews to feign [[hyperdrive]] malfunctions and escape the system. The discovery of trace amounts of the gas in a dissected gralloc's stomach contributed to the [[Skirmish against the Third Fleet|exposure]] of the scheme by Grand Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]].<ref name="Treason" />

During her [[Hunt for Shadow Wing|hunt]] for the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Yrica Quell]] discovered trace amounts of Clouzon-36 in the atmosphere of [[Jiruus]]; the presence of the hyperdrive fuel indicated a [[Skirmish at Jiruus|significant battle]] there.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
