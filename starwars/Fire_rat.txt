{{Quote|And so they searched in the shadows, fear gnawing in their bellies as fire rats gnaw on lava crystals&hellip;|Vaneé, describing Spikewheel's crew|<ref name="Return 1" />}}
'''Fire rats''' were a type of [[Species|creature]] native to [[Mustafar]]. They were known to gnaw on the [[lava crystal]]s that could be found on the [[Volcano|volcanic]] world. While recounting a tale to the prisoner [[Thom Hudd]] about the exploits of the [[droid]] [[captain]] [[Spikewheel]] and her crew on [[Lotho Minor]], the [[Sith]] [[Aide|attendant]] [[Vaneé]] likened the fear gnawing at the group to the [[Tooth|teeth]] of [[fire]] [[rat]]s gnawing at [[crystal]]s.<ref name="Return 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fire rats were first mentioned in [[Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle 1|''Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle'' 1]], a [[Star Wars comic books|comic issue]] written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="Return 1" /> on [[October 2]], [[2019]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-Return-to-Vader-s-Castle-1-of-5/digital-comic/796191|text=Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle #1|nolive=1}}</ref>
