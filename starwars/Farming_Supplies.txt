'''Farming Supplies''' was a business operating in [[Saleucami town]], that sold [[farm]]ing equipment and was advertised by a glowing sign written in an [[Unidentified Aurebesh script (battle helmet)|alternate Aurebesh script]].<ref name="CaREG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/cut-and-run-episode-guide|text="Cut and Run" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Saleucami_Billboards.jpg|thumb|right|Concept art of the billboards seen in "Cut and Run"]]
The name of the business was developed for "[[Cut and Run]]," the second episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' as [[Aurebesh]] letters written on a billboard.<ref name="CaREG" /> Although the billboard isn't visible in the episode itself,<ref name="CaR">{{TBB|Cut and Run}}</ref> it was shown in a concept art image included in the episode guide for "Cut and Run" on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="CaREG" />
