The '''All Terrain Construction Transport''' ('''AT-CT''') was a [[walker]] and an unusual construction variant of the [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]. Its production was limited and its armaments were replaced by a [[tractor beam projector]]. The AT-CT was used to transport heavy materials during the construction of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] installations.<ref name="Walkers and Ground Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:AT-CT.jpg|thumb|right|150px|The AT-CT, as it appeared in ''Star Wars'' Legends]]
The All Terrain Construction Transport first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] video game created by [[LucasArts]] and released on [[September 16]], [[2008]].<ref name="The Force Will Be Unleashed on September 16">{{SWArchive|url=gaming/videogames/news/news20080403.html|text=The Force Will Be Unleashed on September 16}}</ref> The AT-CT was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Walkers and Ground Vehicles of the Empire and First Order]], a booklet in a series of canon reference booklets titled the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']], published on [[May 11]], [[2021]].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref>
