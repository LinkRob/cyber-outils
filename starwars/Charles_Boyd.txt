'''Charles Boyd''' is a former writer and a game director at [[BioWare]] Austin for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' and its extensions. He also co-authored the reference guide ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic Encyclopedia]]''.

[[File:CharlesBoyd.png|thumb|left|150px|Charles Boyd in 2009]]
Joining Bioware in 2006, Charles Boyd joined the team of writers under the leadership of [[Daniel Erickson]] working on ''Star Wars: The Old Republic'', where he created the story for one of the eight classes of the game: the [[Meteor (Havoc Squad)|Trooper]]. After the departure of Erickson in late 2012, Boyd was promoted lead writer alongside [[Hall Hood]], working on the first extension of the game, [[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Rise of the Hutt Cartel|''Rise of the Hutt Cartel'']] ([[2013]]). Then, he became the sole lead writer for the second narrative extension, [[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Shadow of Revan|''Shadow of Revan'']] ([[2014]]).

Charles Boyd also held the position of lead writer for [[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Fallen Empire|''Knights of the Fallen Empire'']] ([[2015]]). He was soon promoted to lead creative designer after the release of this extension, and from there ascended to creative director in mid-[[2016]] to work on [[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Eternal Throne|''Knights of the Eternal Throne'']] and future content for ''The Old Republic''. He wrote the short story "[[Chasing Copero]]" on the official website of ''The Old Republic''.

Charles Boyd left Bioware on August 05, 2022 after 16 years with the company.<ref name="Gamespot">{{GameSpot|url=articles/star-wars-the-old-republic-creative-director-is-leaving-bioware/1100-6505574|text=Star Wars: The Old Republic Creative Director Is Leaving BioWare}}</ref>

Charles Boyd is a costuming enthusiast, being part of the [[501st Legion (fan organization)|501st Legion]], the [[Rebel Legion]], and the [[Mandalorian Mercs]].
