The '''Canto Bight Police Department''' ('''CBPD'''), also known simply as '''Canto police''',<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> was the [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] of the [[casino]] [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Deal me in.|CBPD officer returning from patrol.|<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>}}
The CBPD was broken into the [[Uniformed Branch]] and the [[Canto Bight Mounted Police]] (CBMP). The Uniformed Branch had the most public visibility, and served as a visible deterrent to petty crime and thievery. The CBMP operated lightweight [[Zephyr GB-134 speeder|GB-134 pursuit craft]], also known as jet-sticks, to navigate the narrow roadways of the Old Town.<ref name="TLJ VD" />

[[File:Canto_Bight_Security_SaV.png|thumb|left|250px|The CBPD investigate a crime scene.]]
The CBPD was notoriously corrupt. Every law and regulation in the [[city]] was negotiable, depending on the wealth of the suspect or the accuser. As such, the CBPD was both a byproduct and aide in the city's tiered society, with the wealthiest having the greatest sense of security and immediate access to justice&mdash;a flexible concept in [[Canto Bight]]. As long as transgressions did not disturb the resort city's relaxing atmosphere, or cross any unspoken rules, much was ignored.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> Not all officers were corrupt, however, as with [[Detective]]s [[Pol Ipol]] and [[Oosha Choi]], who were actually honest, leading them to earn the reputation of "suckers".<ref name="DJ:MW" />

While the CBPD was corrupt, it was not ineffective. Public safety was taken very seriously, and the veneer of the coastal city was carefully and expensively maintained. Property crime was not tolerated, and offenses such as vandalism and littering were punished with greater severity on [[Cantonica]] than on most other planets.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> Trials were held for offenders at the convince of the magistrate.<ref name="TLJ ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>

Due to the ultra-wealthy environment CBPD officers operated in, hospitality training was included as part of CBPD academy curriculum, as keeping guests content was key to keeping them docile. Police were trained to subdue and arrest without resorting to lethal force, all in an effort to maintain the confidence and comfort of the public. The slightest injury suffered by a bystander or suspect could have costly legal and public-relations repercussions.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> CBPD officers were well paid for their work.<ref name="TLJ ICS" />

==History==
{{Quote|Let the herd go, stick with the pups.|RCBD Officer|<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>}}
[[File:GB-134.png|thumb|right|250px|A Canto Bight policeman on a Zephyr GB-134 speeder.]]
The Canto Bight Police Department served the city of Canto Bight, Cantonica, [[Corporate Sector]].<ref name="TLJ VD" /> Their jurisdiction included not only Cantonica, but also the world's moons.<ref name="DJ:MW" />

In [[34 ABY]], the CBPD briefly arrested and detained [[Finn]] and [[Rose Tico]] during their [[Mission to Canto Bight|mission]] for the [[Resistance]] against the [[First Order]] shortly after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]. They later dealt with a rampage of [[Fathier]]s through the streets of the city after they were released from their pens at the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Bight racetrack]].<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

==Equipment==
[[File:EdgyPolicePromo.jpg|thumb|right|350px|A Canto Bight policeman by the sea.]]
Police uniforms featured a [[betaplast]] armored collar for protection and a fineweave cape . Rank was displayed on a pin attached to the collar of duty uniforms. Helmets featured a high-impact betaplast neck guard. Holographic department letters written in [[Aurebesh]] were attached to the front of officer helmets, and could reverse for rearview mirror legibility.<ref name="TLJ VD" />

Officers were well-equipped<ref name="TLJ ICS" /> with non-lethal weaponry and equipment. Weapons included the [[Relby K-25 blaster]] in its rifle configuration, the [[Relby]] K-25 blaster in its default configuration to serve as a [[blaster pistol]], and a Relby K-25 blaster in its heavy configuration, which featured a long-range microscope and a noise suppression and range-extending barrel attachment. Such blaster weaponry was set to stun by default. Since weapons were prohibited in public areas, firefights in Canto Bight were rare.<ref name="TLJ VD" />

In addition to blasters, police carried [[electro-shock prod]]s to disable targets with ease. They also had access to glow rods, which could double as a crude baton, and featured a multi-spectrum illuminator array for crime scene investigation. Officers carried a single short-range wrist [[comlink]] and scrambled long-range comlink on their belts.<ref name="TLJ VD" />
