"'''Chapter 5: Inquisition'''" is the fifth episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'' and was released on [[April 20]], [[2026]] alongside "[[Chapter 6: Night of the Hunted]]". Its title was revealed on [[March 17]], 2026 by the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' Twitter account.<ref name="Twitter" />

==Plot summary==
===The Empire arrives===
The episode opens with an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] flying over [[Janix city]]. The Star Destroyer deploys several [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] and a [[Rho-class transport shuttle|''Rho-class'' shuttle]] carrying several [[TK stormtrooper]]s. Several civilians including [[Rylee Lawson]] watch in shock as Imperial forces march onto the streets of Janix city. An [[Imperial officer]] announces that all offworld travel from [[Janix]] has been suspended. [[Rook Kast]] and several [[Shadow Collective]] members watch the arrival of the [[Galactic Empire]] from a balcony. 

Elsewhere, [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] tells his [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]] that they need to flee Janix city before the Empire grounds transports. When Izara asks about [[Maul]], Daki says that the Empire will deal with him and they need to focus on their own survival. Nearby, a squad of stromtroopers questions two locals including a [[Bith]]. 

At the [[Janix Civil Defense HQ]], [[Chief]] [[Klyce]] tells [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]] that his droid [[2B0T]] ("Two Boots") has created a mess for them. Lawson says that Two Boots was just following his protocol. Klyce warns that the Imperial intervention will not go down well and that the Empire will demand answers. When Lawson offers to take the blame, Klyce says that is her responsibility and tells Lawson that he has done enough. Klyce says that at least they were not harboring Jedi. Lawson gives a nervous expression. 

A squad of stormtroopers led by [[Lieutenant]] [[Blake]] enters the command center and demands an audience with Chief Klyce. Blake is accompanied by a [[IMG-099 Imperial Mark IV patrol droid]], who displays a [[hologram]] of Maul. He says he has become aware that a dangerous fugitive has been terrorizing Janix city and accuses Klyce of failing to report the individual to the Empire. Klyce tells him not to make "baseless accusations." Blake announces that he will be taking over the investigation and warns that the Empire will be investigating those who withheld knowledge of his whereabouts. Blake orders that Klyce submit to an interrogation. She passes her blaster to a pair of stormtroopers while telling Lawson not to interfere. Before leaving to interrogate Klyce, Blake warns Lawson not to go away.

===Sticking to Maul's plan===
In the streets of Janix city, Imperial officers stop commuters and check pedestrians for their identification. [[Spybot]] monitors the Imperial troops before returning to [[Maul's safe house]] in the depths of Janix city. He relays his report to Kast, who informs Maul that the Empire is preparing to impose a full lockdown on Janix. When she suggests that the relocate to another system, Maul says that they are not ready and insists on continuing with his plan to groom and train Izara. Kast regards Izara as a threat but Maul insists that she stick to the plan. Kast submits to Maul's orders. 

[[Looti Vario]] asks Maul what is his plan since his syndicate's smuggling operations have been disrupted by the Imperial occupation. When Maul replies that sacrifices have to be made, Vario says that Maul has achieved his goals of eliminating [[Marg Krim]] and bringing the [[Pyke Syndicate]] back into his pocket. When Vario demands to know what he gets in return for holding up his end of the deal, Maul replies that his rewards is being allowed to live. When Vario asks if he is living as his prisoner, Maul tells the [[Aleena]] crime lord that he is free to leave and take his chances with the Empire. Fearing prosecution by the Empire for his criminal activities, Vario decides to stay with Maul in the under levels of Janix city. 

Kast is confronted by two disgruntled Mandalorians who are unwilling to get entangled with the Empire and demand to be paid for their services. She orders them to return to their posts, which they comply. 

===Enter the Inquisition===
Lawson waits nervously in his office while Imperials analyze the [[Tactical Defense Force]]'s information about Maul. Two Boots senses that his master is distress and serves him two mugs of hot drinks as a consolation. He tells Lawson that he has forgiven him for shutting him down. When Lawson asks Two Boots if he comprehends the gravity of his actions, Two Boots explains that he requested Imperial reinforcements to deal with an adversary they were ill equipped to deal with. Lawson warns that the Empire will not leave their planet after dealing with Maul. Two Boots replies that he does not have sufficient information to make an assessment on the matter. Lawson says that the droid is smart but doesn't fully comprehend the implications of the Empire extending their stay. 

Their conversation is interrupted by two stormtroopers, who detain Lawson for questioning. Blake tells Lawson that Klyce has been transferred permanently from her job and warns Lawson to be more forthcoming. Two stormtroopers frog march Lawson out of the command center. Lawson is led to an interrogation room where he sees a [[hologram]] displaying an image of Maul. Lawson notices that there are scratch marks on the table, indicating foul play. Lawson is confronted by the [[Inquisitor]] [[Marrok]], who sneaks up on him and demands everything he knows about Maul. 

===Imperial law and order===
While walking through the streets of Janix city, Daki tells Izara that their plan is to head to the forest. When Izara questions why they should waste their abilities and powers by fleeing, Daki warns her not to let Maul's words poison her mind. Izara says she is confused. Even together, the two were unable to defeat him. She asks about Maul's offer for them to fight as allies. Daki replies that Maul only seeks to help himself. Izara says she dislikes Maul but says that he may be right about having to work together to fight the Empire. Daki agrees that they share a common enemy in the form of the Empire but says that Maul will never be their ally. The two walk away as Imperial stormtroopers approach the walkway and begin questioning other pedestrians. 

A crowd of frantic and confused civilians gather outside the Janix Civil Defense HQ. The [[Tactical Defense Droid|police droids]] explain that they are unable to process their cases due to the Imperial takeover. Rylee wades through the crowd. After a police droid says his dad is unavailable, Rylee sneaks through an access door and heads to the command center. He reunites with Two Boots, who is relieved to see him. Rylee says that his dad is not picking up his [[comlink]]. Two Boots reveals that Brander is under investigation and begins to usher Rylee out, citing protocol. The two run into Lieutenant Blake, who objects to the presence of a civilian. When Two Boots explains that Rylee is Lawson's son, Blake orders the droid to escort Rylee out. 

In the interrogation room, Lawson tells Marrok that he encountered Maul during an ambush that killed most of his comrades. When Marrok questions his account, Lawson replies that he was lucky to survive that encounter. Meanwhile as the two Jedi approach a train station, Daki tells Izara that their plan is to use a train to escape the city and then travel on a foot to a ruin in the forest. He plans to find another way offworld for them. Daki reassures Izara that he has experienced many challenges throughout his life and adds that tyranny is always defeated. He encourages Izara to remain hopeful.

Shortly later, a train arrives and several stormtroopers disembark from the passenger carriages. They begin screening the commuters for their identification. Daki uses a [[mind trick]] to convince a stormtrooper that he does not need to see their identification. Before they can slip away, another stormtrooper confronts the stormtrooper for letting them through, prompting the former to snap out of the mind trick. The two Jedi use their Force powers to fight off the stormtroopers and jump over another passing train. They hide above the ceiling lights until the stormtroopers leave the train station. As Daki sighs from his exertion, Izara checks on her master's well being. He reassures her that he is alright before they continue their journey. 

===Desperate times===
While Marrok and a detachment of stormtroopers and LAAT/i gunships travel to the abandoned factory where Maul ambushed Lawson's police forces, Rylee waits anxiously at home. The [[holoprojector]] rings and he is contacted by his mother [[Drea Lawson]]. Rylee tells his mother that the Empire has detained his father Brander for an undisclosed reason. Drea reassures her son that the stormtroopers are on Janix to keep the people safe. She advises her son not to interfere with the Imperials and to cooperate with them. When Rylee asks about his father, Drea tells Rylee to tell Brander to cooperate. She reaffirms her love for Rylee before heading back to work. 

At the abandoned factory, Marrok discovers the charred remains of Lawson's [[probe droid]] and also finds evidence of a lightsaber battle. Elsewhere, Daki decides that they should stay low due to the Imperial patrols and checkpoints around the city. He tells Izara that he knows a contact. Meanwhile, Spybot obtains footage of the two Jedi's encounter with Imperial stormtroopers and relays the information to Kast. She visits Maul in his chambers while his leg is being repaired by [[Icarus]] and [[Scorn]]. Kast tells Maul that the two Jedi have been unable to leave the city due to the Imperial patrols around the city, which delights Maul who still intends to recruit Izara as his apprentice.

Meanwhile, Lieutenant Blake informs Captain Lawson he is free to go and thanks him for his cooperation. A weary Lawson drives home to his flat. Upon arriving, he is greeted by Rylee, who is relieved to see his father. Lawson says he had a challenging day at work. He sighs when he sees Izara and Daki sitting around the dining table.
