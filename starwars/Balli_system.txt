The '''Balli system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Abrion sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square S-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the [[Homeworld|home]]<ref name="Planetnamia" /> of the [[Ballian]] [[species]].<ref name="Origin" />
