At least two individuals were the '''parents''' of the [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Cair San Tekka]],<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] between [[257 BBY]] and [[253 BBY]]<ref name="Date">In ''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]'', [[Cair San Tekka]] is described as a few [[Standard year|years]] older than [[Jordanna Sparkburn]]. As Sparkburn is twenty years old in ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of to [[231 BBY]], she must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[251 BBY]], meaning San Tekka must have been born by [[253 BBY]]. In ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'', which is set in [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:THRContent228BBY|here]], San Tekka is stated to be in his twenties, meaning he cannot have been born before [[257 BBY]].</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], and were members of the [[San Tekka clan]]. In Cair's youth, his [[parent]]s, alongside his uncles [[Marlowe San Tekka|Marlowe]] and [[Vellis San Tekka]], encouraged him to nurture his talent for making trouble and [[Spy|espionage]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> while on the planet [[Rilias II]] broadcasting an illicit message from [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] as part of their resistance against the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] group, Cair reflected that he had not regretted following his parents' and uncles' guidance until he met and [[Marriage|married]] the rival [[Graf family]]'s scion [[Xylan Graf]], whom Cair had been spent to spy on. Cair believed he had spent too much time serving his family's interests, and chose instead to defy the Nihil.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
