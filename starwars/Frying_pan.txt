A '''frying pan''' was a cooking device used across [[the galaxy]]. Its main purpose was cooking; however, sometimes it could be used as a [[melee weapon]].<ref name="Brothers" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|In the script, the cook Loubo is holding a blaster to which the spaceport police droid says, "Lou! Are you crazy, pointing that thing at a Jedi?" Changing the prop into a frying pan turned this moment into a joke.|StarWars.com Trivia Gallery for "Brothers"|<ref name="Trivia" />}}
The scene where Loubo tried to pull the pan on [[Anakin Skywalker]] was originally intended for Lou to pull a [[blaster]] on him instead.<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/brothers-trivia-gallery|text="Brothers" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref>
