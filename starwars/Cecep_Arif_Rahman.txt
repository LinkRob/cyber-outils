'''Cecep Arif Rahman''' (born [[August 18]], [[1972]])<ref name="SWYT 08-20-2020" /> is an [[Wikipedia:Indonesia|Indonesian]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] who portrayed [[Kanjiklub]] member [[Crokind Shand]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. Rahman, aswell as his fellow Kanjiklub members [[Yayan Ruhian]] and [[Iko Uwais]] also appeared in the Indonesain action thrillers [[Wikipedia:The Raid (2011 film)|''The Raid'']] and [[Wikipedia:The Raid 2|''The Raid 2'']].<ref name="BBC" />
