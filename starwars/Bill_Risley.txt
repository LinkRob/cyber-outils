'''Bill Risley''' was an actor who portrayed a [[Rebel pilot/Legends|Rebel pilot]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. According to [[Tom Spina]] and [[Pablo Hidalgo]], he also portrayed a [[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]] local. Bill Risley was also an extra on numerous other films.
