The '''Fountains of Plenty''' were fountains located in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] of<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[moon]] [[Jedha]].<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic (2022) 1|''The High Republic'' (2022) 1]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> a [[Sarkan]] [[Unidentified Sarkan pilgrim|pilgrim]] intended to visit the Fountains of Plenty during their stay within the Holy [[City]], which they told<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> the [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Vildar Mac]] as they sat next to each other on a [[starship]] headed for the city.<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> In [[1 BBY]], the Holy City was [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]]<ref name="Destruction" /> by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Fountains of Plenty were mentioned in the [[The High Republic (2022) 1|first issue]] of the [[2022]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2022)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']],<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]]. The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 12]], 2022. Prior to the release of the comic, the fountains were mentioned in a preview article of the comic published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[September 15]] the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-the-high-republic-1-exclusive-reveal|text=Meet Vildar Mac in Marvel's ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' #1 &ndash; Exclusive Reveal}}</ref>
