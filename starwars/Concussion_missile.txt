A '''concussion missile''' was a type of [[missile]] in which energy warheads would release clouds of high-velocity proton particles upon impact.<ref name="Cross" /> They were commonly mounted on [[starfighter]]s such as the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]] and the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bomber]],<ref name="USW" /> and fired through [[Concussion missile launcher|special launchers]] to provide [[hull]]-piercing capability.<ref name="SW EOSAOV (SE)" />

[[Boba Fett]] charged extra for jobs that required him to use concussion missiles.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==History==
During the [[Battle of Endor]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] fired concussion missiles at the main reactor of [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], triggering the fatal blow, after wingman [[Wedge Antilles]] used [[Proton torpedo]]es with his [[Wedge Antilles' X-wing|X-Wing]] to take out the battle station's power regulator.
