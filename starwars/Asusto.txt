'''Asusto'''<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was a small [[forest]]ed [[planet]] located within the [[Tamsis Nebula]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], strong with the [[dark side of the Force]] and often visited by [[smuggler]]s. The last of the [[Presagers of Hakotei|Presagers]] of [[Hakotei]] hid on the planet. The Presagers [[Mission to Asusto|led]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Lene Kostana]] and her [[Padawan]] [[Sifo-Dyas]] into a trap on Asusto by tricking them into following a supposed trail for [[Sith artifact]]s from [[Karazak]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
{{Planet-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Asusto first appeared in the audiobook ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> The spelling of Asusto's name was given in the [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script]] of the audiobook.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" />
