'''Ema's parents''' were at least two individuals who were [[parent]]s to the [[Corellian]]<ref name="CC" /> [[humanoid]]<ref name="Species">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' states that the [[scrumrat]]s recruited by the [[White Worms]] were all [[humanoid]]s.</ref> [[Ema]], who was [[Reproduction|born]]<ref name="CC" /> around [[27 BBY]].<ref name="Date1" /> They raised her on<ref name="CC" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="TravelersGuide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Corellia]], keeping the [[Gender|girl]] well cared for and fed. However, when they [[Death|died]]<ref name="CC" /> around [[15 BBY]],<ref name="Date1" /> Ema was left to fend for herself on the streets of Corellia and was recruited by the [[White Worms]] criminal organization as a [[scrumrat]].<ref name="CC" /> By [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">The chapter of ''[[Crimson Climb]]'' in which [[Ema]]'s [[parent]]s are mentioned takes place the same [[Standard day|day]] [[Han Solo|Han]] escaped [[Corellia]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[13 BBY]].</ref> the scrumrat [[Qi'ra]] had learned of the parents' deaths and later reflected on that part of Ema's life when the other girl was promoted to [[Head]] of the White Worms.<ref name="CC" />
