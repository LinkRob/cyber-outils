'''Acacia''' was a [[Color|black]] [[fathier plushy]] owned by the [[human]] [[Jaylen Vrax|Jaylen Barsha]] while growing up on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. The plushy was given to Jaylen by his mother, [[Roisem Barsha]] after she returned from a trip to the [[casino]] planet [[Cantonica]].

==Description==
Acacia was a [[Color|brown]] [[fathier plushy]].<ref name="Low Red Moon" />

==History==
[[File:JaylenVrax-OutlawsVCG.png|thumb|right|300px|Acacia was owned by Jaylen Barsha (pictured).]]
The [[human]] [[Jaylen Vrax|Jaylen Barsha]] owned Acacia while growing up on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. Around [[33 BBY]],<ref name="Low Red Moon Date">{{LRMDate|Prologue}}</ref> Jaylen's [[gender|half-brother]], [[Sliro Barsha]], asked [[Roisem Barsha|Jaylen's mother]] if she could bring him back a fathier plushy from her trip to the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[casino]] planet [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Low Red Moon" /> 

While at the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]], Roisem Barsha purchased two of the plushies; however, when she returned home she secretly gave both to Jaylen. When Jaylen asked her why she did not get one for Sliro, she told him there were only two left and that it would not be fair to give him and Sliro the same number since Sliro was just "half a Barsha." She went on to tell Jaylen not to show the plushies to Sliro. Jaylen agreed and named one Acacia and the other [[Riven]].<ref name="Low Red Moon" />

Two [[Standard day|days]] later, Jaylen heard Sliro crying in his room and entered it to check on him. While the pair were talking in Sliro's room, Sliro revealed that he had asked for a fathier plushy. Jaylen considered giving one of the plushies to Sliro in order to cheer him up but he did not want to get in trouble and since he had already named both fathiers he decided not to mention them to his brother.<ref name="Low Red Moon" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Low Red Moon Date" /> preparing to become the [[Chief Executive Officer]] of his [[Barsha family|family's]] [[Barsha Corporation|company]], Jaylen thought about his time as a child sleeping with the fathiers.<ref name="Low Red Moon" />
