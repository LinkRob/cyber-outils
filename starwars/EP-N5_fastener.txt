{{Quote|Across its prison system, the Empire put inmates to work. Cassian Andor reported, having escaped from the facility on Narkina 5, that he had spent weeks&mdash;and other inmates years&mdash;building some form of mechanical joint system for the Empire, though it is unclear what these devices were ever used for.|[[Beaumont Kin]], ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''|<ref name="RiseFall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
'''EP-N5 fasteners''' were a type of six-pronged panel joint assembled by the [[Prisoner|inmates]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex]] on the [[moon]] [[Narkina 5]].<ref name="CompEncyc" /> Complex in design<ref name="Ham">{{Databank|ham|Ham}}</ref> and also identified as "widgets,"<ref name="Taga">{{Databank|taga|Taga}}</ref> the fasteners were used in the construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]'s [[Mk I Superlaser]] dish,<ref name="CompEncyc" /> to keep receptor cells in alignment within the focusing lens.<ref name="DoRVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[The galaxy|The wider galaxy]] never learned of the joint system's true purpose, with escapee [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] only knowing it was a type of joint.<ref name="RiseFall" />
