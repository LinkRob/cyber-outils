The '''Albarrio sector''' was a [[sector]]<ref name="USW" /> of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Note" /> It contained the [[Mygeeto system|Mygeeto]],<ref name="BMF 45" /> [[Scipio system|Scipio]] and [[Albarrio system|Albarrio]] [[star system]]s. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Scipio]]<ref name="USW" /> was the headquarters of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]]<ref name="AOF">{{TCW|An Old Friend}}</ref> which led to a [[Invasion of Scipio|battle]] taking place there between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="CatH" />

[[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] served as a [[Fulcrum]] recruitment [[agent]] in the Albarrio sector.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Albarrio sector was first mentioned in [[Riders of the Maelstrom (adventure)|''Riders of the Maelstrom'']], a ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' supplement written by [[Ray Winninger]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' by [[West End Games]]. In the ''Legends'' reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' the sector was placed in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]; however, when the Albarrio sector was confirmed to be [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'' it was accidentally placed within the Core Worlds region instead. That was the result of an editing mistake, however, rather than a change in the sector's location.<ref name="Jason Fry Cite">{{JCFCite|url=posts/53103573|board=Literature|thread=Wookieepedia, the Star Wars wiki|user=jasonfry|date=2016-01-08|time=2:05 PM|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160220103244/https://boards.theforce.net/threads/wookieepedia-the-star-wars-wiki.20673016/page-111|quote=To better fit the storytelling. Scipio was placed in the Core when nothing was known about it other than that it was the homeworld of Rush Clovis. A Core placement seemed to fit based on that, though it wasn't much to go on. When Clone Wars gave us stories actually set on Scipio, we saw that it was a critical IGBC world with a significant Muun presence, so it was moved to a location in the ORT near Muunilinst. Which makes *far*more sense than the Core. This change was actually made for Ultimate Star Wars -- note it says Scipio is in Albarrio sector, which is in the ORT. The region wasn't changed because of an editing mistake.|archivefile=File:Jason Fry on Scipios location.png}}</ref>
