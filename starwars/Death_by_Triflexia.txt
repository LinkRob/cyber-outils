"'''Death by Triflexia'''" is an [[adventure]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. It was written by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]] and published in the magazine ''[[Challenge 76]]''. The story involves a group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] agents on [[Reginard]] trying to solve a case of [[triflexia]] [[Poison/Legends|poisoning]].
