'''Dinzo''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="TWC" /> located in the [[Dinzo system]] in the [[Msst sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> 

== Description ==
Dinzo was a rocky [[planet]] with many hills and a breathable atmosphere.<ref name="TWC" /> [[Cortosis]], a rare mineral, could be found on the planet.<ref name="TWC" />

== History ==
Around [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{Twitter|Jeremy_Barlow|status/838612089543376901|[[Jeremy Barlow]]|nolive=1|nobackup=1}} places the events of "[[The Wrong Crowd]]" shortly after the events of "[[Homecoming]]," which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[3 BBY]].</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] operated a [[Cortosis mine (Dinzo)|cortosis mine]] there. The mine was discovered by [[Tonk's crew]] who planned to blow it up as a demonstration of their skills to the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebels]] that they had heard existed. [[Tonk]] sent out a message claiming that there were refugees that needed rebel assistance on the planet in an attempt to lure the rebels to Dinzo.<ref name="TWC" />

In an effort to distract the Imperial forces from the mine, Tonk's crew blew up their own ship and a little over half the crew engaged Imperial [[stormtroopers]] and [[All Terrain Armored Transport|All Terrain Armored Transports]] while two other members infiltrated the base. The rebels [[Hera Syndulla]] and [[Sabine Wren]] arrived aboard the [[shuttle]] ''[[Phantom]]'' and eliminated the Imperial threat that Tonk's crew were facing. After landing, Syndulla and Wren sought out the refugees, but Tonk revealed his ruse and instead showed them the Imperial mine, divulging he intended to blow it up. Syndulla instead wanted to capture it and in the following argument, Tonk's crew blew up the mine after mishearing Tonk.<ref name="TWC" />

== Behind the scenes ==
Dinzo made its first appearance in the "[[The Wrong Crowd]]", a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] found in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 30|thirtieth issue]] of the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]] released on [[April 12]], [[2017]].<ref name="TWC" />
