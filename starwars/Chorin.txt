'''Chorin''' was a [[Stone|rocky]] [[mining]] [[planet]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]' [[Chorin system]]. Its [[human]] population, including the [[Valance family]], was [[Slavery|enslaved]] as [[miner]]s for generations before being [[Liberation of Chorin|liberated]] by an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Military|force]] led by [[Captain]] [[Tarl Sokoli]], with the Empire also maintaining a presence on the planet. After the mines were depleted, the Empire abandoned Chorin, leaving its population defenseless [[Attack on Valance's village|against]] the [[Pirate|raiders]] that would eventually form the criminal syndicate known as the [[Hidden Hand]].

In [[1 BBY]], during a visit to Chorin, [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] offered the [[cyborg]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]], a native of the planet whom he had captured, a position in the Empire. Shortly after the visit, the residents of Valance's [[Unidentified village (Chorin)|village]] on Chorin left their [[Homeworld|home]] and migrated to a [[Rebel outpost (Lowik)|rebel base]] on the world [[Lowik]] following a deal between Valance and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] [[Gita]] and [[Aeliar]]. Vader later sent a detachment of [[stormtrooper]]s to the settlement to [[Death|slaughter]] its people, but upon arriving, the [[trooper]]s discovered that the [[City|village]] had been abandoned.

==Description==
An [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Galaxy" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> in the [[Chorin system]],<ref name="Galaxy" /> Chorin had a breathable Type I [[atmosphere]].<ref name="Atmosphere" /> It was situated in grid square N-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Galaxy" /> Chorin's surface was [[Stone|rocky]], with hills and [[mountain]]s forming the landscape. When viewed from [[Realspace|space]], Chorin appeared [[Color|blue]] in color.<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> The [[Itani Nebula]] was visible from the planet's surface.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 6">[[Bounty Hunters 6|''Bounty Hunters'' 6]]</ref>

==History==
===Slavery and liberation===
{{Quote|Our people mined these hills for generations&hellip;as ''slaves''. But now we mine for ''profit''. As free men and women. Thanks to our liberators. Thanks to&hellip;''the Empire''.|Beilert Valance's father, to his son|<ref name="Target Vader 2">[[Target Vader 2|''Target Vader'' 2]]</ref>}}
For generations, the [[human]]s of Chorin, which included the [[Valance family]], were [[Slavery|enslaved]]<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> by [[pirate]]s controlling the [[Mining|mines]].<ref name="Bounter Hunters 18" /> They [[Miner|mined]] the hills of the planet until they were [[Liberation of Chorin|liberated]]<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> by a [[Galactic Empire]] [[Imperial Military|force]] led by [[Captain]] [[Tarl Sokoli]] during the Empire's [[Imperial Era|reign]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 19">[[Bounty Hunters 19|''Bounty Hunters'' 19]]</ref> In that [[time]], he also led the [[Liberation of Chorin City|liberation]] of [[Chorin City]], [[Death|killing]] seventeen pirates in the battle.<ref name="Bounter Hunters 18" /> The regime maintained its presence on the planet with multiple [[starship]]s, including [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]], [[class four container transport]]s, and [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighters]]. As a free population, the people began mining the planet for profit. The [[Beilert Valance's father|father]] of [[Beilert Valance]], a human [[Youngling|child]] miner, explained Chorin's history when the [[Gender|boy]] inquired if he himself would remain a miner his whole life like his [[Beilert Valance's grandfather|grandfather]] before him.<ref name="Target Vader 2" />

[[File:EmpireChorin-TargetVader2.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The Galactic Empire maintained a presence on Chorin.]]
The Empire later held a [[parade]] in one of the planet's [[Unidentified city (Chorin)|cities]], in which several [[stormtrooper]]s and an [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] [[walker]] [[pilot]]ed by Sokoli walked among a crowd. Valance ran through the crowd in order to see the walker himself but was seized by a stormtrooper. Dismissing the [[trooper]], the captain approached the boy and gave him a [[ration bar]], explaining how he himself had once experienced cruelty.<ref name="Bounter Hunters 18" /> At some point, Valance owned a miniature model of a V-wing [[starfighter]] and had his [[Beilert Valance's mother|mother]] repair it when it was damaged. He wished to fly a V-wing himself, with his mother telling him not to forget them when he was "living a glamorous life in space," despite his [[Parent|father]] expressing disapproval and stating the boy's destiny was to work in the mines like the rest of Chorin's populace.<ref name="Bounter Hunters 34">[[Bounty Hunters 34|''Bounty Hunters'' 34]]</ref>

At one point, Valance and his lover, [[Yuralla Vega]], sat atop a cliffside while they discussed the former's desire to leave the planet and join the Empire,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 6" /> having been inspired by Sokoli's actions,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 19" /> with Vega expressing her sadness at the fact. Vega later handed him a [[fire ruby]] belonging to her family for him to remember her by.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 6" /> After reaching adulthood, Valance departed his [[Unidentified village (Chorin)|village]]<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> via an Imperial [[transport]] ship<ref name="Bounty Hunters 6" /> in order to become a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] in the [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]]<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> around [[13 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The Empire later abandoned the planet after the mines were depleted, leaving Chorin's population vulnerable to raiders, who [[Attack on Valance's village|attacked]] the village on at least one occasion. Without the Empire's protection, many of Chorin's inhabitants were killed despite attempts to defend themselves.<ref name="Target Vader 5" />

===Abandoned by the Empire===
{{Quote|What remains of your people is still down there. Safe. For now.|Darth Vader, to Beilert Valance|<ref name="Target Vader 5" />}}
In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> after being discharged from the Imperial Military following the [[Mimban Campaign]], in which Valance was gravely injured and received extensive [[cybernetics]], Valance returned to Chorin only to discover that his village was left in ruins. He found the people of his village by a [[Fire|bonfire]] and learned about the cause of his [[Homeworld|home's]] destruction from Vega. Determined to hunt down the raiders, Valance left the village in pursuit of them and managed to locate the mountain where the raiders resided. However, he found that they had already left the area&mdash;the raiders had gone on to form the [[Hidden Hand]] criminal syndicate after departing the planet. Following his failure to locate the raiders, Valance left Chorin and started a career in [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunting]].<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> Around 10 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> and shortly prior to embarking on a [[Mission to Corellia (Nakano Lash's crew)|job]], Valance contacted a [[droid]] stationed at [[Receiver Station 356-39]] on Chorin and attempted to speak with Vega while she and a group of miners were hauling supplies from the mines. However, he closed the transmission line just before communication was established between the two.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" />

[[File:ValanceChorinReturn-TargetVader5.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Beilert Valance and Darth Vader on Chorin]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="TV Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Valance and [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] traveled to Chorin after the bounty hunter was captured<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> in a [[Hunt for Vader|skirmish]] on the world [[Heva]].<ref name="Target Vader 4">[[Target Vader 4|''Target Vader'' 4]]</ref> Upon arriving on Chorin's surface, Vader invited Valance back to the Empire after showing him that his people at the village were temporarily safe from harm.<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> Sometime afterward, the population of the village on Chorin migrated to a [[Rebel outpost (Lowik)|rebel base]] located on the world [[Lowik]] as part of a deal between Valance and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] members [[Aeliar]] and [[Gita]], with the [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] promising a supply of [[weapon]]s in return. When Valance abandoned Vader and his forces at the Hidden Hand's [[Hidden Hand asteroid base|main outpost]] after dealing with the syndicate's leader, [[Gwi]], the [[Sith Lord]] sent a detachment of stormtroopers to massacre the population at Valance's home. Upon arriving, however, the troopers discovered that the village had been completely abandoned.<ref name="Target Vader 6">[[Target Vader 6|''Target Vader'' 6]]</ref>

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|The Valance family has dug, scratched and clawed through these hills for ''centuries''.|Beilert Valance's father, to his son|<ref name="Target Vader 2" />}}
The mining planet Chorin was inhabited by humans, including the Valance family, who were enslaved as miners for many generations until their liberation by the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> Chorin had the stench of mine dust and sweat.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35">[[Bounty Hunters 35|''Bounty Hunters'' 35]]</ref> Following a deal made between Valance and a group of rebels, the population of Valance's village departed their home.<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> Individuals of various [[species]], including a [[Rodian]], once attended a parade on Chorin.<ref name="Bounter Hunters 18" />

==Locations==
The planet's hills housed mines<ref name="Target Vader 2" />&mdash;controlled by pirates before the Empire liberated the planet<ref name="Bounter Hunters 18" />&mdash;that were excavated for resources.<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> At least one mine had miners wear a [[breath mask]] and a pair of [[goggles]] during work.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> A village was located near the hills, but it was ravaged during an attack by raiders<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> and later abandoned when the population migrated to Lowik.<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> A makeshift [[Burial|graveyard]] for the victims of the attack was also created near the village, with each miner's [[helmet]] laid over the graves.<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> Receiver Station 356-39, a communication station located near the mines, could be used to communicate with individuals off-world.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> The planet also contained at least two [[City|cities]], Chorin City and another at which an Imperial parade took place.<ref name="Bounter Hunters 18" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chorin first appeared in a flashback scene in the [[Target Vader 2|second issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] mini-series ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]'', written by [[Robbie Thompson]], illustrated by [[Stefano Landini]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> on [[August 14]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel-TV2">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/78430/star_wars_target_vader_2019_2|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #2}}</ref> The world received its name in the [[Target Vader 5|fifth issue]], which was also written by Thompson<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> and published on [[November 13]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel-TV5">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/82616/star_wars_target_vader_2019_5|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #5}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Homeworld/Legends|homeworld]] of [[Beilert Valance/Legends|Beilert Valance]] was the [[Mining/Legends|mining]] [[Planet/Legends|world]] [[Shinbone]], which was first featured in "[[The Hunt Within: Valance's Tale: Part 1]]," a scenario authored by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[December 2]], [[2004]] on [[Wizards.com]] for use with [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s [[Star Wars Miniatures|''Star Wars'' Miniatures]] tactical miniatures wargame.<ref name="Wizards">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=swminis/article/huntwithin1|text=The Hunt Within: Valance's Tale: Part 1|int=The Hunt Within: Valance's Tale: Part 1}}</ref>
