"'''A Bitter Harvest'''" is a short story that was published in the [[Wikipedia:Target Corporation|Target]]-exclusive edition of ''[[Dark Legends]]''. The story was authored by [[George Mann]], illustrated by [[Grant Griffin]], and published on [[August 30]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23" />

==Plot summary==
[[Menir]] came to Batuu to live a quiet life. He eventually married a local woman named [[Elise]] and was enjoying a good life. But things soured when Elise invited [[Iren]], a stranger visiting the village, into their house to stay the night. Menir grew furious and hurled the traveler out of the house. Iren's sole possession, a pouch with a few seeds, was left behind in the struggle. Menir, after mocking what little the traveler had, tossed the seeds outside. The seeds eventually grew into monstrous creatures known as [[Drengir]], which destroyed both Menir's home, killing Elise.
