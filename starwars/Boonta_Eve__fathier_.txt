'''Boonta Eve''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[fathier]] with [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] and black [[eye]]s<ref name="Outlaws" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Birth year" /> They shared their name with<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[Boonta Eve]] holiday held on<ref name="Boonta Eve">{{Databank|boonta-eve-classic-podracer-pilots|Boonta Eve Classic Podracer Pilots}}</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Boonta Eve" /> Boonta Eve took part in the [[Canto Cup]] [[Fathier racing|fathier race]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> that took place in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> and was held in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the planet [[Cantonica]]. By the time of the race, they stood 283 [[centimeter]]s tall and weighed 967 [[kilogram]]s. While Boonta Eve was moving to the starting line a [[Unidentified Canto Cup announcer (Basic)|announcer]] for the Canto Cup stated that the fathier was hoping to get payback after the previous racing season.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Boonta Eve appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' as one of the fathiers that can be randomly assigned in the optional Canto Fathier Racing activity. For every race in the game, six [[jockey]]s are assigned a random fathier and a random color uniform and symbol. As such, this article does not assume one pairing or uniform color to be [[canon]]ical.<ref name="Outlaws" />
