"'''A New Home'''" is the first episode of the [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season Two|second season]] of ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''. It was released on [[Disney XD]] on [[June 17]], [[2017]]. The episode is a collection of the five short films "[[Home One (episode)|Home One]]," "[[Thrown Into Battle]]," "[[Rowan's Secret Adventure]]," "[[Zander Freemaker, Superstar Pilot Guy!]]," and "[[Beware, the Gamorrean Flu!]]."<ref name="LEGO A New Home" />

==Official description==
''New to Home One, the Freemakers embark on a series of misadventures to assist and maybe even impress the rebel fleet''.<ref name="Disney ABC Press" />

==Plot summary==
==="[[Home One (episode)|Home One]]"===
To secure a job with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] travel throughout [[the galaxy]] to obtain a [[Type A vector coil]]. [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] uses her bartering skills to obtain the vector coil for [[Lieutenant]] [[Colvett Valeria|Valeria's]] [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wing starfighter]].

==="[[Thrown Into Battle]]"===
Following a botched attempt to befriend the local [[droid]]s aboard the [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80A ''Home One'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]] ''[[Home One]]'', [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]] builds a [[buffer]]. He becomes an unlikely hero when he accidentally knocks out the [[deflector shield generator]] of an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]].

==="[[Rowan's Secret Adventure]]"===
After accidentally damaging [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron's]] [[starfighter]]s, [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] and Roger hastily rebuild the ships. They become unintentional passengers aboard Lieutenant Valeria's [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing]] during the [[Assault on Strike Station Aurek|assault]] on [[Strike Station Aurek]].

==="[[Zander Freemaker, Superstar Pilot Guy!]]"===
[[Zander Freemaker]] attempts to join Valeria's Blue Squadron in his own [[starfighter]], the [[Blazemaker Mark II|Blazemaker]]. He and his siblings stack the starfighter with too many [[laser cannon]]s, causing it to explode at the end of the dogfight.

==="[[Beware, the Gamorrean Flu!]]"===
The Freemakers undertake a [[Mission to rescue Lando Calrissian and Chewbacca|mission]] to rescue the rebel agents [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Chewbacca]], whose ship the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' is missing its [[fuel intermixer]]. They also encounter some old enemies in the form of the [[Imperial officer]]s [[Durpin]] and [[Plumestriker]].
