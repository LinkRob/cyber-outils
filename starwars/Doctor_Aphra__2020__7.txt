'''''Doctor Aphra'' 7''' is the seventh issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. It was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]], and first published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 20]], [[2021]]. It features the story "'''The Engine Job Part 2: The Offer'''," in which [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] and [[Sana Starros]] pay a visit to [[Lady]] [[Proxima]] on [[Corellia]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE OFFER! Hired by DOMINA TAGGE, APHRA sets her sights on a mysterious piece of tech that could shift the balance of the galactic civil war. Her hunt takes her to CORELLIA&hellip; and to SANA STARROS' doorstep! But Aphra's not the only one on Corellia with an eye on the tech&hellip;!''

==Plot summary==
====An unwelcome visitor====
On [[Corellia]], [[Sana Starros]] returns home from a long day to find her door ajar. Understanding it was tampered with, she charges in with her blaster ready, only to find [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Doctor Aphra]].

When asked how she found her, Aphra admits to slicing databanks and stealing shipping records. She explains she is tracking an artifact—a [[path engine]]—that she believes passed through Corellia on a smuggler's ship. Aphra wants Starros to introduce her to [[Proxima|Lady Proxima]]. Starros refuses and pushes Aphra outside.

====A contract ends====
Meanwhile, on the ''[[Acquisitor]]'' in [[Tagge Space]], [[Just Lucky]] learns from [[Lapin Tagge|Lapin]] that his contract is over, as [[Ronen Tagge]] is no longer alive.

====A brother's debt====
Lucky travels to the [[Crescent Royale Casino]] on [[Canto Bight]], where he finds his brother, [[Pak]], whose face is bruised. Pak explains that [[Wen Delphis]] is getting impatient and wants her money back. Lucky promises to take care of it.

Soon after, Lucky visits Wen Delphis, Leader of the [[Sixth Kin]], in the [[Worker's District]]. Wen confirms Pak's debt is substantial. Lucky agrees to take a bounty job to clear his brother's debt.

====The Den of the White Worms====
Back on Corellia, Aphra waits outside Starros's home until morning. Starros finally appears, having agreed to the introduction just to get rid of Aphra.

They visit the [[Den of the White Worms]], where Lady Proxima is outraged that Aphra would show her face. To barter for information, Aphra presents an ancient artifact: a [[Lengilese]] Life-Fasting Bracelet. Aphra shows Proxima a hologram of the path engine she is seeking.

Proxima reveals that a smuggler named [[Remy (smuggler)|Remy]] brought the artifact and sold it to a man named [[Beol De'Rruyet|De'rruyet]]. She also warns them that the [[Unbroken Clan]] is asking about its whereabouts.

====The Unbroken Clan investigates====
Elsewhere, [[Vukorah]] of the Unbroken Clan is violently interrogating Remy. She learns that Remy was seen the previous day at the fish market with Sana Starros.

====Escape from Corellia====
Starros and Aphra, who are nearby, learn that Vukorah has Remy's [[datapad]]. Aphra creates a distraction by throwing a ring in their direction, which promptly explodes. Starros realizes the "priceless artifact" Aphra offered Proxima was actually a [[detonite ring]].

Aphra and Starros flee to their ship at the [[Volt Cobra]]. Starros emphasizes they need to get off-planet, explaining that the Unbroken Clan is one of the most dangerous syndicates on Corellia. As they escape, Aphra attempts to slice Remy's datapad. She announces she has found something, telling Starros their next stop is a battlefield.
