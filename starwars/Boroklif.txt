'''Boroklif''' was a [[captain]] who worked for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] in [[19 BBY]]. Serving aboard the [[freighter]] ''[[Larkrer]]'', Boroklif and his crew shipped supplies to a secret [[Mokivj droid factory|Separatist factory]] on the [[planet]] [[Mokivj]] from a [[Oga's Cantina|transfer point]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] world [[Batuu]].

[[Mission to Batuu|Traveling]] to Batuu in search of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] defeated Boroklif's comrades with the help of the [[Chiss]] [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]. The pair then took the ''Larkrer'' to the Separatist [[factory]], relying on Boroklif having preset a course to Mokivj in his freighter, and explained to the factory's [[Solha|leader]] that the captain and his crew had been compromised to avoid any suspicion.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Captain Boroklif isn't here. There was trouble at Black Spire. Boroklif and his crew weren't in any condition to fly.|Anakin Skywalker, to Solha|audio=Boroklif2.flac|<ref name="Alliances" />}}
Boroklif was an individual who served as [[captain]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[freighter]] ''[[Larkrer]]''.<ref name="Alliances" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'' set during the [[Clone Wars]] to [[19 BBY]].</ref> he and his crew helped ship supplies on the ''Larkrer'' from a [[Oga's Cantina|transfer point]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]] to a secret [[Mokivj droid factory|Separatist factory]] on the planet [[Mokivj]]. One [[Standard day|day]], the [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|Naboo Royal Handmaiden]] [[Duja]] was caught investigating the Separatists that Boroklif worked with and was [[Death|killed]] by them shortly thereafter. After Duja's superior, [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], disappeared while [[Mission to Batuu|searching]] for her handmaiden, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] traveled to Batuu in search of them both.<ref name="Alliances" />

Accompanied by the [[Chiss]]<ref name="Alliances" /> [[Senior Captain]]<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], Skywalker defeated Boroklif and his comrades and took the ''Larkrer'' to the Separatist [[factory]] on Mokivj. When the freighter reached Mokivj, Boroklif's superior, [[Duke]] [[Solha]], asked to speak to the captain. To avoid Solha's suspicion, Skywalker then explained that Boroklif and his men had been compromised. The Jedi and Thrawn further told Solha that Boroklif had preset the course to Mokivj in the ''Larkrer'', explaining how the pair had reached the Separatist factory.<ref name="Alliances" />

==Behind the scenes==
Boroklif was mentioned in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'', the second installment of the [[Star Wars: The Imperial Trilogy|''Star Wars: Thrawn'']] trilogy written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Alliances" />
