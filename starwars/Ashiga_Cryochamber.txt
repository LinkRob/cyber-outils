The '''Ashiga Cryochamber''' was a section of the [[Dai Bendu Monastery|Ashiga Clan Stronghold]] where the [[Ashiga Clan]] stored the [[origin strand]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==History==
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Crimson Dawn]] supported [[Krisk Ashiga]] in a coup attempt to replace [[Queen]] [[Ashiga]], ruler of the [[Ashiga Clan]]. Crimson Dawn however, used the distraction of the coup to try and take control of the origin strand, the generic coding of the clan, which would give them control over the Ashiga Clan. However a [[thief]] named [[Kay Vess]], who was hired to sneak in and take down the stronghold's defense, learned of the plans on the origin strand and intercepted the Crimson Dawn agents at the cryochamber where she fought off the Dawn and protected the origin strand. She was later instructed to bring the strand to the throne room and decide the outcome of the coup.<ref name="Outlaws" />

In 35 ABY, the cryochamber was destroyed along with the planet during the [[Destruction of Kijimi]] by the [[Sith Eternal]] with the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Derriphan]]'' of the [[Final Order]] [[fleet]].<ref name="Destruction" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ashiga Cryochamber first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It is only accessible during the "Hive" main quest.<ref name="Outlaws" />
