'''CD-5502'''<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] in the [[First Order]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> CD-5502 was deployed to [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] as part of a [[Wulfgar Kath's squad|squad]] based off the [[Star Destroyer]] [[Penumbra (Star Destroyer)|''Penumbra'']] and led by [[Lieutenant]] [[Wulfgar Kath]] to [[Battle of Batuu|track down]] the [[Resistance]] [[Spy|operative]] known as [[Vi Moradi|Starling]]. Wearing standard [[First Order stormtrooper armor]], CD-5502 and the other [[trooper]]s were patrolling the [[Black Spire Outpost Market|market streets]] of the outpost when a local [[merchant]] named [[Gol]] approached them, wishing to report intelligence on Resistance movement. Gol proceeded to describe that he suspected his [[Kriki|former employee]] of joining the Resistance. Although Lieutenant Kath did not think much of the information, he ordered CD-5502 to record Gol's description of the employee and search the outpost for her.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />
