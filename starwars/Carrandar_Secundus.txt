'''Carrandar Secundus''' was a terrestrial astronomical object located in the [[Carrandar Secundus system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Portmoak sector]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Galactic Empire]] destroyed a [[Carrandar Secundus nest|nest]] in the system around five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. News about the nest's destruction was published and [[Merei Spanjaf]], a young [[Human]] living on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]], read about the destruction on her [[datapad]]'s newsfeed.<ref name="RitR" />

==Behind the scenes==
