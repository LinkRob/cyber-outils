'''Able Nereno''' was a [[Rodian]] [[Gender|male]] who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], with the rank of [[private]]. He worked as a [[nurse]] aboard the [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] ''[[Redemption]]'' during the [[Battle of Endor]]. While he was treating a patient, one of the ship's [[oxygen]] converters exploded near his post. Nereno's patient lived, but the Rodian nurse did not survive the explosion.

Twenty [[Standard day|days]] after the battle, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], a leading member of the Alliance, took time to personally notify [[Mrs. Nereno|Nereno's wife]].<ref name="SE2" />
