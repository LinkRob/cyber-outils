"'''Dream Labyrinth'''" was a short story written by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]], and it appeared in the "Command Crew" section of ''[[The DarkStryder Campaign]]''{{'s}} Campaign Book. It depicted an incident when [[Jessa Dajus]] awoke from a nightmare about being lost in a labyrinth and encountering a dark [[Spider/Legends|spider]].

==Plot summary==
In a dream, [[Jessa Dajus]] finds herself in a shifting labyrinth. A distorted voice speaking in a [[Language/Legends|language]] she does not understand calls out to her. Spying a large, dark [[Spider/Legends|spider]], it speaks to her and she follows it through the maze.

Stopping when she realizes that the spider is sucking on something, Dajus hears the sounds of [[Tongue/Legends|tongues]] lapping at liquid, [[Tooth/Legends|teeth]] tearing flesh, and [[Bone/Legends|bones]] breaking. As she creeps forward to see the mangled body, Dajus wakes up in a sweat. Reaching for her [[Blaster/Legends|blaster]], Dajus finds [[Kentor Sarne]]'s warm hand as he sits on the bed next to her and asks if she was having bad dreams.
