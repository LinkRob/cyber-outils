The '''arrawtha-dyr''' was a creature native to the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]], notable for its many-pronged [[antler]]s.<ref name="Life Debt" />

In the [[Standard year|year]] [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> [[Lozen Tolruck]], [[Grand Moff]] of Kashyyyk, had a chair made of dark [[wood]] and framed by arrawtha-dyr antlers, and a [[robe]] made from the dyr's pelt.<ref name="Life Debt" />
