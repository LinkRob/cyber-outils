'''Beppi''' was a [[human]] from [[Mokivj]] who worked maintenance at the [[Mokivj droid factory|factory]] the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] took over. During the [[Clone Wars]], Beppi got an electric [[bracelet]] from [[Nodlia|LebJau]] to go anywhere in the [[factory]], but a [[droid]] guard of the Confederacy realized that the bracelet was a [[Death|dead]] employee's own, and executed Beppi.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />
