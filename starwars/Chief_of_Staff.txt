'''Chief of Staff''' was a title held by the [[Umbaran]] [[Sly Moore]], a chief [[aide]] to [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. Moore's duties as Chief of Staff included setting the Chancellor's schedule, and she had control over what [[advisor]]s, [[bureaucrat]]s, lobbyists, or [[Senator]]s gained access to the Chancellor.<ref name="RotS" />
