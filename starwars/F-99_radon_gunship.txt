The '''F-99 radon gunship''' was a class of [[gunship]]. In [[18 BBY]], [[Cli Pastayra]] of the [[Wandering Star]] syndicate deployed a pair of F-99s in a confrontation with [[Fyzen Gor]] and his organic-augmented [[droid]]s on [[Utapau]]. By [[7 ABY]], those models were still in use in order to protect the [[Phylanx Redux Transmitter]] while in the [[Mesulan Remnants Belt]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
