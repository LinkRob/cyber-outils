'''300 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''2977 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''281 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7677''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> was a [[Standard year|year]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="HRE">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the start of the [[High Republic Era]] to around [[500 BBY]] and its end to [[100 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The year 300 BBY was indirectly mentioned in the [[2020]] reference book ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]], [[Cole Horton]], and [[Dan Zehr]]. Although it was described as the beginning of the [[High Republic Era]],<ref name="Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> the beginning of the High Republic Era was later placed at [[500 BBY]].<ref name="HRE" /> This article assumes the more recent sources are correct.
