'''''Crimson Reign'' 2''' is the second issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Crimson Reign]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Steven Cummings]], [[Victor Olazaba]], and [[Guru-eFX]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[February 2]], [[2022]]. The issue contains the story '''Part 2: "The Assassins"'''.

==Publisher's summary==
''THE ASSASSINS! QI'RA sends two killers to do what they do best to continue with her plan to plunge the galaxy into chaos. The relentless, Force-blinded OCHI OF BESTOON and the mysterious, unstoppable DEATHSTICK each have a target, and nothing will get in their way!''

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|Everyone kills, under the right circumstances. Everyone. It’s just about how you dress it up. The story you tell yourself.|Qi'ra|<ref name="Crimson Reign 2">[[Crimson Reign 2|''Crimson Reign'' 2]]</ref>}}
The [[The Archivist|Archivist]] continues her record from the [[holocron]], detailing Lady [[Qi'ra]]'s "Grand Shadow War" against the [[Sith]]. She explains that while deception was Qi'ra's primary tool, she occasionally required the "truth of death" to communicate her intent. To achieve this, she employed two unique assassins: [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]] and [[Deathstick]].

Aboard the [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']] Qira meets with Deathstick to assign a sensitive target. Despite the ongoing [[syndicate]] war, Qi'ra identifies a unique variable: [[Cadeliah]], a young girl who is the heir to two rival syndicates ([[Mourner's Wail Syndicate|Mourner's Wail]] and the [[Unbroken Clan]]). Cadeliah is currently hidden among a cell of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] fighters. Qi'ra tasks Deathstick with "taking her off the board."

Privately, Qi'ra explains to her advisors, [[Margo]] and [[Trinia]], the three motivations for killing: Faith (ideologues), Hope (those seeking a better life or revenge), and Joy (those who enjoy the act). She reveals that her assassins are motivated by deeper psychological wounds:

*Ochi of Bestoon kills out of pervasive fear. Having seen "monsters," he serves the greatest monster - the [[Emperor]] - to feel safe, yet he remains terrified of being discarded.
*Deathstick kills out of profound loneliness. As one of the few survivors of the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] massacre during the [[Clone Wars]], she seeks to share her pain with others.

The story follows the two assassins as they execute their missions. On [[Coruscant]], Ochi acquires a specialized array of timed toxins from an underworld dealer. Using an expensive ASN-909 droid, Ochi infiltrates the Works District to poison the Emperor’s personal [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guard]] with [[Ziroxin-12]]. Despite a timing error caused by an Imperial officer skipping breakfast, Ochi successfully delivers a precise dose of "[[Yurfloo]]" toxin to his targets.

Simultaneously, Deathstick locates the Rebel base on [[Panisia]]. Rather than attacking directly, she alerts the Imperial anti-terrorism watchline to the base's coordinates. As a [[Star Destroyer]] and [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] arrive to lay waste to the rebels, Cadeliah is forced into hiding. Deathstick corners the girl in a shipping container, easily disarming her.

The issue concludes at the [[Imperial Palace]] on Coruscant. As Emperor [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] discuss the syndicate war, the Emperor's Royal Guards suddenly collapse, dead from Ochi's timed poisons - a theatrical demonstration that the Sith are not untouchable. Meanwhile, back on the ''Vermillion'', Deathstick delivers Cadeliah to Qi'ra. Qi'ra removes the girl's binders and welcomes her, beginning a new phase of her grand design.
