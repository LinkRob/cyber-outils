'''Brandon Auman''' was the head writer and executive producer on ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''. He is also known for his work on [[W:c:turtlepedia:Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2012 TV series)|''Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles'']].

{{Bio-stub}}

==Biography==
Work on the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' began in [[2016]], during which, [[Athena Yvette Portillo]] enjoyed working with Brandon Auman.<ref name="Athena">{{StarWarsShow|video=nOMREPOkBmc|text=Solo's Aliens Come to Life and Athena Portillo Joins the Resistance|date=2018-09-1}}</ref> On [[April 26]], [[2018]], [[StarWars.com]] announced ''Star Wars Resistance'' and that it would be executive produced by Brandon Auman, as well as Portillo and [[Justin Ridge]]. Auman had previously worked on [[Wikipedia:Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2012 TV series)|''Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles'']].<ref name="Announcement" /> Later in the year, he was revealed to be head writer in a video posted to the ''[[Star Wars]]'' YouTube channel on [[August 29]], where he helped introduce the audience to [[Team Fireball|Team ''Fireball'']].<ref name="TeamFireball">{{SWYouTube|dlucTaqVVcs|Meet Team Fireball &#124; Star Wars Resistance|date=2018-08-29}}</ref> In another video posted to the YouTube channel&mdash;on [[September 14]], 2018&mdash;Auman helped introduce the [[Ace Squadron]] of the ''[[Colossus]]'' [[Supertanker fuel depot|refueling platform]].<ref name="Ace">{{SWYouTube|MDtY2vVlfaQ|The Aces &#124; Star Wars Resistance|date=2018-09-14}}</ref> Earlier in the week of [[October 3]], 2018, Auman attended an exclusive screening of the debut for ''Star Wars Resistance''.<ref name="Early">{{StarWarsShow|video=NJfBB0VLVPs|text=Behind the Mask of Vader Immortal and a Sneak Peek at Star Wars Resistance|date=2018-10-03}}</ref>

Each episode of ''Star Wars Resistance''{{'s}} [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]]&mdash;which aired from [[October 7]], 2018 to [[March 17]], [[2019]]<ref name="AirDates">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref>&mdash;was executive produced by Auman.<ref name="Announcement" /> Auman also wrote five of the season one episodes: "[[The Recruit]],"<ref name="Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref> "[[Signal from Sector Six]],"<ref name="Signal">{{Resistance|Signal from Sector Six}}</ref> "[[Station Theta Black (episode)|Station Theta Black]],"<ref name="STB">{{Resistance|Station Theta Black (episode)}}</ref> "[[No Escape: Part 1]],"<ref name="NE1">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 1}}</ref> and "[[No Escape: Part 2]]."<ref name="NE2">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 2}}</ref> Ahead of the final two episodes&mdash;"No Escape: Part 1" and "No Escape: Part 2"&mdash;StarWars.com announced Auman, along with others, would be attending [[Celebration Chicago]] that year for a panel on [[April 15]] to discuss the season finale and preview the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|next season]].<ref name="PanelAnnouncement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-chicago-panel-schedule|text=Check Out the Full ''Star Wars'' Celebration Chicago Panel Schedule}}</ref>
