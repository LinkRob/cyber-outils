The '''father''' of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] [[Emon Kref]] was a [[Gender|male]] [[general]] in the [[Koshagan People's Movement]], a group of freedom fighters who led an [[Koshagan uprising|uprising]] against the ruling class of the [[planet]] [[Koshaga]] for many [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Beyond Hope" /> Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Beyond Hope]]" establishes that the [[Galactic Empire]] intervened in the [[Koshagan uprising]] a few [[Standard week|weeks]] prior to the [[Battle of Hoth]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]]. The Empire's intervention must have therefore taken place around that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> however, the [[Galactic Empire]] conducted a an operation which quickly defeated the People's Movement, taking leaders like Kref's father prisoner.<ref name="Beyond Hope" />

==Behind the scenes==
Emon Kref's father was mentioned in the short story "[[Beyond Hope]]," written by [[Michael Moreci]] as a part of the [[2020]] short story collection ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="Beyond Hope" />
