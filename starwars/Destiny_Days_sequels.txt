A year after taking part in [[Unidentified seminar|a seminar]], [[Skreen]], the technical director of the [[Manifold Mollusk Matrix and Matrimony Association]], planned to pen '''three sequels''' to his work ''[[Destiny Days]]'', a fictional account based on true events that transpired<ref name="MAGL" /> on the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim]]<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Lusaanda]] after the author himself had [[Death/Legends|died]].<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behind the scenes==
These sequels were mentioned in an entry for Skreen in a segment named "The Seminar Participants," written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that collects concept designs and photographs from various [[George Lucas]] projects.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]].<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref> The entry on Skreen specifically stated that the author wrote about events that happened after his death.<ref name="MAGL" />
