The '''admiralty''' was a command organization of the [[Republic Navy|naval military branch]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. On the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the Republic [[Navy]] had a void of small craft, and as such pressed diplomatic and civilian [[Starship|ships]] such as the [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class space cruiser]] into service. The admiralty recognized that this was a temporary solution, and as such, it initiated a campaign to provide reliable war equipment to the navy.<ref name="Rise" />
