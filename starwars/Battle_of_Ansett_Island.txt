The '''Battle of Ansett Island''' was a mission undertaken by [[Company 77]] of the [[First Order]] lead by [[Captain]] [[Shikra]] on [[Ansett Island]] on the planet [[Epo]] sometime before the [[mission to Kef Bir]]. The mission aimed to recover shipments of supposed First Order weapons which turned out to be orphan children from the [[Merchant Uprising]]. When ordered to fire upon the captured and disarmed smugglers, the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] designated as [[Jannah|TZ-1719]] and the rest of the company laid down their weapons. After capturing Captain Shikra and his shock troopers, the company freed the captives and took over the [[light cruiser]] ''[[Culverin]]'' which had stayed in orbit with only a skeleton crew. To avoid being hunted as deserters, the company transmitted an official record claiming that the ''Culverin'' and the entire company had been destroyed during a battle on Ansett Island. Afterwards, TZ-1719 took on the name Jannah and the company settled on the [[ocean]] [[moon]] [[Kef Bir]].<ref name="Episode IX" /><ref name="TLO" />
