'''Bravo Squad''' is the name given to several units.

==Canon==
*'''[[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]]'''&mdash; a [[clone cadet]] [[squad]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]
*'''[[Bravo Squadron (Galactic Republic)|Bravo Squadron]]'''&mdash; a [[Republic Military]] ground [[squadron]] that served under [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] during the [[Battle of Christophsis]].
*'''[[Bravo Squadron]]'''&mdash; a [[squadron]] of the [[Naboo Royal Space Fighter Corps]], containing [[Bravo Flight A]] and [[Bravo Flight B]].
