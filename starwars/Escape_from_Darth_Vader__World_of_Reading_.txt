'''''Escape from Darth Vader''''' is a young-readers story from [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] that was released on [[September 16]], [[2014]]. Written by [[Michael Siglain]] with illustrations by [[Stéphane Roux]], it tells the story of the opening events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[CR90 corvette|vessel]] ''[[Tantive IV]]'' being [[Secret mission to Tatooine|pursued]] by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]'' to the [[droid]]s [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]] escaping and landing on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], the former carrying [[Death Star plans|secret plans]] to the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]].

==Publisher's summary==
''C-3PO and R2D2'' [sic] ''must keep Darth Vader from discovering the Rebels' secret plans! This fully-illustrated level 1 reader retells the classic scenes from ''A New Hope'' where the droids crash land on the desert planet of Tatooine and meet a young farmer named Luke''.<ref name="Disney" />

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|Princess Leia had to keep the secret plans hidden from Darth Vader. She gave them to R2-D2 to keep them safe.|Description from ''Escape from Darth Vader''|<ref name="escape" />}}
A [[Tantive IV|rebel vessel]] carrying [[Prince]]ss [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] is [[Secret mission to Tatooine|under attack]] by a [[Devastator|larger spaceship]]. Organa is in possession of secret plans for an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|battle station]]. R2-D2, a [[R2-series astromech droid|specialized droid]], desperately seeks the princess, who moves swiftly to escape a boarding party sent by the Empire. Meanwhile, a droid of [[3PO-series protocol droid|another type]], C-3PO, worries that everyone aboard the [[CR90 corvette|vessel]] is doomed.<ref name="escape" />

Organa evades pursuing [[Stormtrooper|troopers]] and provides R2-D2 with the secret plans to keep them from falling into the hands of the feared [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Vader. [[Gender|She]] is soon captured by the pursuing [[troop]]s and taken to Vader, but she does not reveal the plans' whereabouts. C-3PO finds R2-D2, and together the two board an [[escape pod]], which lands on a sandy planet. Having escaped Vader with the secret plans, they begin a new journey that will bring them into contact with<ref name="escape" /> farm boy<ref name="ANH:PSFB">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref> Luke Skywalker,<ref name="escape" /> [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="ANH:PSFB" /> [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]],<ref name="escape" /> [[smuggler]]<ref name="ANH:PSFB" /> [[Han Solo]],<ref name="escape" /> and the [[Wookiee]]<ref name="ANH:PSFB" /> [[Chewbacca]].<ref name="escape" />

==Development==
[[File:Mean_and_Scary_Darth_Vader.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Situations and concepts are presented in simple language.]]
On [[April 25]], [[2014]], [[Lucasfilm]] announced a new storytelling structure for the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, wherein past titles considered part of the Expanded Universe would be rebranded as [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], while new releases would form a new continuity of [[canon]].<ref name="End of EU">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> ''Escape from Darth Vader'', a thirty-two-page fully illustrated book written by [[Michael Siglain]] and illustrated by [[Stéphane Roux]],<ref name="escape" /> was published on [[September 16]], 2014,<ref name="Disney" /> making it part of the new canon.<ref name="End of EU" />

The title, designed for readers ages 6&ndash;8,<ref name="Disney" /> retells situations and events previously depicted in full in the [[original trilogy]] film ''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope''.<ref name="Amazonescape">{{Amazon|url=World-Reading-Escape-Darth-Vader/dp/1484705009|text=World of Reading Star Wars Escape From Darth Vader: Level 1 (Star Wars Classic Reader): Disney Book Group}}</ref> [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] released an electronic edition on [[October 7]], 2014,<ref name="Amazon-Digital" /> and [[Egmont]] issued a U.K. edition on [[June 4]], [[2015]].<ref name="escapeegmont" /> [[Scholastic]] also published an Australian edition.<ref name="ScholasticAU">
{{Scholastic|subdomain=shop|domain=com.au|url=Product/8400586/Star-Wars-Rebels-Galactic-Reading-Adventure-Escape-from-Darth-Vader-Level-1|text=Star Wars Rebels &#91;''sic''&#93; Galactic Reading Adventure: Escape from Darth Vader Level 1}}</ref>

==Continuity==
''Escape from Darth Vader'' is part of a series of ''Star Wars'' titles under the [[World of Reading]] banner that retell events from the franchise in a format suitable for the youngest audiences. Other titles in the series include ''[[Use the Force!]]'',<ref name="Amazonforce" /> [[Rescue from Jabba's Palace (World of Reading)|''Rescue from Jabba's Palace'']],<ref name="Amazonjabba">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/world-of-reading-rescue-from-jabbas-palace|text=World of Reading: Rescue from Jabba's Palace}}</ref> and [[AT-AT Attack! (book)|''AT-AT Attack!'']],<ref name="Amazonatat" /> all of which were published in 2015.<ref name="Amazonatat" /><ref name="Amazonforce" /><ref name="Amazonjabba" />

Two short stories from other authors share the same title as ''Escape from Darth Vader''. A [[Escape from Darth Vader (The Original Trilogy Stories)|short story]] by [[Rebecca L. Schmidt]] was published in the anthology ''[[The Original Trilogy Stories]]''<ref name="OTStories">''[[The Original Trilogy Stories]]''</ref> on [[September 1]], 2015,<ref name="DisneyOTDate">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-the-original-trilogy-stories|text=Star Wars: The Original Trilogy Stories}}</ref> while a [[Escape from Darth Vader (5-Minute Star Wars Stories)|second story]], written by [[Brooke Vitale|Brooke Dworkin]], was published as one of eleven stories within ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]'',<ref name="fiveminute">''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]''</ref> a [[December]] 2015<ref name="Disney5Min">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-5-minute-star-wars-stories|text=Star Wars: 5-Minute Star Wars Stories}}</ref> compilation from Disney&ndash;Lucasfilm Press. It retains the same basic narrative and illustrations by Stéphane Roux but features completely rewritten text by Dworkin as well as direct quotations from the film not featured in Siglain's version.<ref name="fiveminute" />

''Escape from Darth Vader'' was one of several titles included in ''[[World of Reading Star Wars Boxed Set: Level 1]]'', released on [[October 25]], [[2016]].<ref name="Boxed">{{PenguinComics|url=book/?isbn=9781484780220|text=World of Reading Star Wars Boxed Set (Level 1)}}</ref> It was also included in the compendium book ''[[3 Out-of-This-World Tales!]]'', published on [[August 29]], [[2017]]. The title also includes the stories ''AT-AT Attack!'' and ''[[Ewoks Join the Fight]]'', along with an accompanying CD narration.<ref name="amazon3in1">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-3-1-listen-along-reader|text=Star Wars 3-in-1 Listen-Along Reader|nolive=1}}</ref>
