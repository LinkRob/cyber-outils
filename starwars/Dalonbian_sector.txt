The '''Dalonbian sector''' was a [[sector]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It contained the [[forest]] [[planet]] [[Arbiflux]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

{{Sec-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Dalonbian sector had its first appearance in [[canon]] in a flasback in [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 32|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 32]], the thirty second issue of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']].<ref name="Aphra">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 32|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 32]]</ref>  However it was first identified as the Dalonbian sector in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]].''<ref name="Encyclopedia" />
