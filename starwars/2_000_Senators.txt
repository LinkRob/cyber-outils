'''''2,000 Senators''''' was a [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] created by the [[Gender|female]] [[Ukian]] [[artist]] [[Kas unloDos]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Separatist Crisis]]. It evoked the sentiment common on [[Ukio]] that the [[Galactic Republic]] was not worth the membership because of its dispassionate and distant [[Galactic Senate|Senate]]. The poster depicted the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]] at the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], and had text in both [[High Galactic]] and [[Aurebesh]]. The Aurebesh stated, "The winds of liberty are blowing this way, can you feel them?", while the High Galactic at the top stated, "There are 2000 senators inside this building," and slightly below that, "None of them care about you." [[Writing|Written]] at the very bottom of the poster, in High Galactic was "Confederacy of Independent Systems."<ref name="Propaganda" />
