The '''Coruscant elevated maglev train''', also known as the '''speeder train''', was a maglev train used on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Maglev train were similar to the [[luxury train]].

==Description==
The maglev train utilized [[repulsorlift]] technology and ran along a set of elevated rails throughout [[Imperial City|Galactic City]].<ref name="LighsaberLost" /> One such rail called the [[Black Line]] connected the [[Federal District]] to the [[Verity District]].<ref name="LifeDebt" />

==History==
During the search for her missing [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|lightsaber]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] chased her [[lightsaber]]'s [[thief]], [[Cassilyda Cryar|Cassie Cryar]], onto the elevated maglev [[train]] as it left the [[Train station|station]]. Cryar smashed her way into the train's passenger compartment and took two people hostage, just before the train arrived at the station. As she attempted to leave the train, she was surprised by [[Jedi Master]] [[Tera Sinube]] who disarmed and overwhelmed her. She was then taken into custody by [[Coruscant Security Force|Coruscant Police]] [[GU-series Guardian police droid]]s.<ref name="LighsaberLost" />
