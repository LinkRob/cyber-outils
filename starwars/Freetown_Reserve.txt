'''Freetown Reserve''' was a [[cocktail]] served in [[the galaxy]]. Said to be made "with [[Bantha]] hides mashed with fermented [[grain]]s," it originated on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]],<ref name="AllEars">{{WebCite|url=https://allears.net/2022/07/02/review-come-to-disneys-brand-new-star-wars-themed-lounge-with-us/|text=Come to Star Wars: Hyperspace Lounge on the Disney Wish With Us!|work=AllEars.net|archivedate=20220809074512}}</ref> the home of a settlement also named "[[Freetown]]."<ref name="Chapter 6">{{BOBF|Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger}}</ref> It was a smoky drink created using an infusion tower, and included a [[Color|blue]] [[Water|ice]] cube featuring a Hyperspace logo.<ref name="Checked Out" />

==Behind the scenes==
Freetown Reserve is a [[In-universe|real-world]] [[alcohol]]ic beverage featured in the [[Star Wars: Hyperspace Lounge|''Star Wars'': Hyperspace Lounge]] aboard the [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney Cruise Line]] cruise ship ''Disney Wish''.<ref name="Checked Out" /> Its ingredients include [[Wikipedia:Woodford Reserve|Woodford Reserve]] Double Oaked [[Wikipedia:Bourbon whiskey|bourbon]].<ref name="AllEars" />
