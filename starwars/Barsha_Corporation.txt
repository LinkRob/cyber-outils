'''Barsha Corporation''', or '''Barsha Corp''',<ref name="Low Red Moon">''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]''</ref> was a [[Corellian]] [[starship]] manufacturing company owned by the [[Barsha family]] until the end of the [[Clone Wars]] when [[Sliro Barsha]] sold out the family to the [[Galactic Empire]] in order to earn a job as a [[director]] in the [[Imperial Security Bureau]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> The company produced engine turbines for the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]s used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]],<ref name="Low Red Moon" /> as well as the [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
