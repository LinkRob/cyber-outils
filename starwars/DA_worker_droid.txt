The '''DA worker droid''' was a model of bipedal [[labor droid]]<ref name="Kids" /> with a flat disc-shaped head and long [[Limb|arms]] ending in large hands. They could have [[Color|white]] plating and sensors of both red and yellow. One such [[droid]] was present in [[Coronet City Spaceport|Coronet Spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]<ref name="Solo" /> in [[13 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' that take place on [[Corellia]] to six [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Empire's formation to [[19 BBY]]; therefore, the events on Corellia in ''Solo'' must take place in [[13 BBY]].</ref> The droid carried a large cylinder on its back and was stood next to an elevated duty station manned by [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] when the criminals [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]] entered the [[spaceport]]. After Qi'ra was captured by members of the [[White Worms]] gang, the droid was stood by one of the queues in the spaceport and turned and watched as she was dragged away.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
A DA worker droid first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. The droid was not named in the film,<ref name="Solo" /> but was identified in the video "Every Droid in Star Wars", which was posted on ''[[StarWarsKids.com]]'' in [[2019]].<ref name="Kids" />
