'''Bbbl''' was a [[Northern Mustafarian]] who was part of a [[Kkkt's clan|clan]] led by [[Father (title)|Father]] [[Kkkt]]. In [[14 BBY]], Bbbl was part of a scouting group sent to investigate the crash site of a [[Fire|fiery]] [[Naboo Royal Starship|object]] on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]]. While the group initially found nothing upon their arrival, Bbbl spotted the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who [[Telekinesis|hurled]] a boulder into the [[Mustafarian]] and sent them falling into [[Magma|lava]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Well, ''something'' definitely came down from the sky around here&mdash;we all saw it&mdash;but&hellip; no debris.''"<br />"''Mm. It probably hit one of the lava flows and dissolved. Just more minerals to harvest when the time comes.|Bbbl and Sssp|<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />}}
The [[Northern Mustafarian]] Bbbl was a member of a [[Mustafarian]] [[Kkkt's clan|clan]] on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] led by [[Father (title)|Father]] [[Kkkt]] during the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the entire clan witnessed<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> a [[Fire|fiery]] [[Naboo Royal Starship|object]] crash onto the planet.<ref name="Dark Lord 21">[[Darth Vader (2017) 21|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 21]]</ref> Concerned, Kkkt sent a scouting party&mdash;consisting of Bbbl, fellow Northern Mustafarian [[Sssp]], and two [[Southern Mustafarian]]s<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> including [[Rrrn]]<ref name="Dark Lord 24">[[Darth Vader (2017) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 24]]</ref>&mdash;to investigate the site.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

[[File:BbblsDeath-2017DarthVader22.png|thumb|left|300px|Darth Vader launched Bbbl into a molten death.]]
Upon reaching the supposed crash site on [[lava flea]]s, the group found no debris. Sssp concluded the object must have landed in one of the planet's [[Magma|lava]] flows and disintegrated prior to their arrival, advising the rest of them to report back to Kkkt. That was when Bbbl spotted a [[Color|dark]] figure through their [[binoculars]], the figure being the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Vader immediately [[Telekinesis|launched]] a boulder at Bbbl, knocking the Mustafarian off their lava flea and into Mustafar's lava. In the resulting [[Skirmish on Mustafar|skirmish]] between Vader and the rest of the scout party, only Rrrn was able to escape<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> and told Kkkt about their attacker.<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />
