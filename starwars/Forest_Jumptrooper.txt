'''Forest Jumptroopers''' were [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Jumptrooper]]s that had modifications to their standard [[jetpack]]s which produced more efficient power routing, allowing them to move more quickly in densely [[forest]]ed environments. They wore [[armor]] similar to that of [[Coastal defender stormtrooper|shoretroopers]].<ref name="Commander" />
