'''Eemee''' was a [[Dulok]] [[Gender|female]] who was close with the [[Ewok]] [[Falloo]]. The [[Youngling|young]] pair lived on the [[forest]] [[moon]] [[Endor]], where they met in a decorated [[cave]] to spend [[time]] together. Eemee and Falloo took part in various activities together, including picnics, diving, [[fungus]] collecting, and [[flower]] garland making.

When the pair's two sets of [[parent]]s arrived at the cave, they pulled their children apart and began to argue. Eemee and Falloo then held [[Limb|hands]] and forced them to make peace, after which the two families shared a picnic in the cave. The [[shaman]] [[Logray]] later told a tale about the pair in [[Bright Tree Village]].

==Biography==
Eemee was a [[Dulok]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Twitter" /> who lived on the [[forest]] [[moon]] [[Endor]] and was close with the [[Ewok]]<ref name="Wokling" /> [[Falloo]]<ref name="Twitter" /> of the [[Bright Tree tribe]]. The [[Youngling|young]] pair used a decorated [[cave]] away from each of their homes to see each other, on one occasion holding a picnic there. As Falloo and the Ewok's [[bordok]] companion arrived to the picnic, Eemee presented them with garlands of [[flower]]s to wear, placing the bordok's garland on the creature's head. As they ate and drank, the pair then discussed Eemee's [[parent]]s, who had been angry when the young Dulok left. Eemee and Falloo later dived together into a pool of [[water]] and then collected [[fungus]] in the forest, with Eemee becoming concerned when Falloo nearly ate one of the [[mushroom]]s.<ref name="Wokling" />

[[File:FamilyPicnic-TheWoklingsTale.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Emee and Falloo site on a rug in a cave with their respective parents and their bordok having a picnic.|Eemee and Falloo convinced their parents to share a picnic.]]
At later meetings, Falloo gifted Eemee with an Ewok [[Clothing|hood]] and the pair bashed a [[tree]] with [[club]]s, causing [[Plant|flowers]] to fall from it. They then used the flowers to make more garlands back at the cave. However, as they played with the flowers, both Eemee and Falloo's parents arrived at the cave.<ref name="Wokling" />

Each dragging their daughter behind them, the two couples argued until their children clasped [[Limb|hands]] and irritability pointed to a sign in the cave that ruled against anger. The two sets of parents then made peace, and after the adults looked around the decorations in the cave, the two families and the bordok all donned garlands and shared a picnic. The Ewok [[shaman]] [[Logray]] of the Bright Tree tribe later told a story about Eemee and Falloo to gathered Ewoks in [[Bright Tree Village]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Wokling" />

==Personality and traits==
Eemee was close with Falloo despite Eemee's parents' anger and Falloo's parents' fear of Duloks. The [[Color|green]]-[[Hair|furred]] Dulok blushed when the Ewok gifted Eemee with a hood and held hands with Falloo when the pair stood defiant together against their angry parents in the cave.<ref name="Wokling" />

==Equipment==
Eemee wore a band of red beads as a hair band and a brown [[bandolier]]. While spending time with Falloo, the Dulok also wore a garland of purple flowers on several occasions, a yellow hood gifted by the Ewok, and a satchel. Eemee used a woven basket while mushroom collecting and a [[wood]]en club while collecting flowers.<ref name="Wokling" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eemee appeared in "[[The Woklings' Tale]]," a story written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and illustrated by [[Paulina Ganucheau]] which was published in the one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] anthology [[Return of the Jedi – Ewoks 1|''Return of the Jedi – Ewoks'' 1]]<ref name="Wokling" /> on [[April 12]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103245/star_wars_return_of_the_jedi_-_ewoks_2023_1|text=Star Wars: Return Of The Jedi &ndash; Ewoks (2023) #1}}</ref> As the story has no text, the character was not identified in the comic,<ref name="Wokling" /> but Wong provided the Dulok's name and gender on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] following its release.<ref name="Twitter" />
