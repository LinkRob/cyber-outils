The '''Dorvalla system''' was located in the [[Videnda sector]] within an intersecting portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Inbrief" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western Reaches" /> Situated at the coordinates M-18 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it contained the celestial body [[Dorvalla]], where the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] took on some recruits, including the [[Mirialan]] [[Lylee Anaraku]]<ref name="Inbrief" /> at some point between [[2 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]].<ref name="0 BBY">{{InbriefDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Dorvalla [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published on [[StarWars.com]] alongside the article [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]."<ref name="Systems" /> Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]] and released on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
