'''Cesilia Gutierrez''' is an [[Wikipedia:Illustrator|illustrator]] who worked as a designer for the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''.<ref name="Squadron">{{YJA|Squadron / Forest Defenders}}</ref> Gutierrez was responsible for color design seen in the series, including the sets on [[Vuundalla]], as well as various [[planet]] exteriors.<ref name="YJA Design" />
