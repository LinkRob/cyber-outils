A '''Crustacean''' was a class of animal life with hard heavy shells. One known crustacean creature was [[the Sleeper]], which dwelled on the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Quinlan Vos]] [[Death|killed]] the Sleeper while under the tutelage of former [[Sith]] [[assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]].<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> The [[opee sea killer]] of [[Naboo]] was another type of crustacean.<ref name="usw">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>
