'''A'Shar Farless''' was a [[human]] [[captain]] in the [[Imperial Military|military]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. While Farless was training at the [[Arkanis Academy]] he learned that the older [[cadet]]s planned to turn in the younger cadets for trashing a [[port]] even though the younger cadets were ordered to. So the captain gathered some of his fellow students and told them go to the cliffs to distract the villagers long enough so the young cadets could run away. He inspired his classmates to jump off a cliff and to the rescue.<ref name="Goodbye" />

Farless was the stationed [[Imperial Center of Military Research|Imperial security complex]] on the the [[planet]] [[Scarif]] along with his [[DT-series sentry droid]], [[DT-JZ]] that his [[Farless family|family]] sent to protect him. One [[Standard day|day]], [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] arrived at the complex. After giving a speech, he invited Farless to walk with him. Farless told him about his time at the Arkanis Academy. Tarkin revealed that he personally requested that Farless be stationed on Scarif because of his ability to inspire action. He told Farless that the [[Citadel Tower]] was housing very important [[Death Star plans|plans]] in the [[Scarif vault]] and was worried about [[Spy|spies]] infiltrating the complex. He wanted Farless to tighten up the act of the Imperial personnel at the complex and assigned him his own [[A'Shar Farless' squad|squad]] to command. Tarkin would not allow for DT-JZ to stay with Farless so the captain went ahead and said a last goodbye to the [[droid]]. Farless then met his new squad and gave them a speech about fighting for the Empire.<ref name="Goodbye" />
