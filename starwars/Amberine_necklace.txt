An '''Amberine necklace''' was a type of [[necklace]] commonly handmade by [[Tosharan]] locals on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] .<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an amberine necklace could be sold for forty eight [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Amberine necklaces appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game, the necklace could only be sold for credits, and did not have another function.<ref name="Outlaws" />
