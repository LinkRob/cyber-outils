'''C4-ST''' was a [[protocol droid]] who operated as a [[merchant]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Anoat sector]] during its [[Iron Blockade|blockade]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]]. The [[droid]] joined the crew of [[the Smuggler]], a young [[thief]] active in the [[sector]] during the Iron Blockade, who dispatched C4-ST on a variety of missions throughout the Anoat sector.

==Biography==
The [[protocol droid]] C4-ST was in service during the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="C4-ST" /> between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> It operated as a [[merchant]]<ref name="C4-ST" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Anoat sector]] while it was locked down under the [[Iron Blockade]],<ref name="Uprising" /> instigated by [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]],<ref name="IR 1">[[Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising 1|''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 1]]</ref> as part of the Empire's attempts to prevent the [[sector]]'s populace from learning of the crushing Imperial defeat at the hands of the Rebel Alliance at the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Uprising" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
Several [[Standard month|months]] into the [[blockade]], C4-ST joined the crew of [[the Smuggler]], a young [[thief]] who [[Smuggler|smuggled]] goods in the Anoat sector, and was dispatched on a variety of missions throughout the sector.<ref name="Uprising" />
{{EndGame}}

==Characteristics==
C4-ST was a protocol droid with [[Color|red]] plating and yellow [[photoreceptor]]s. With a background in [[Diplomat|diplomacy]] and a preference for stealth, it was watchful and observant, and could read [[Sentience|sentients]] as well as it could understand their [[language]]s. These abilities allowed the droid to skillfully negotiate any type of contract, agreement, or arrangement.<ref name="C4-ST" />

==Behind the scenes==
C4-ST was created for the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was developed by [[Kabam]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms, and in [[2015]]<ref name="Uprising" /> prior to its shutdown in [[2016]].<ref name="ENDSWU">{{Kabam|subdomain=community|url=forums/showthread.php?675751-Star-Wars-Uprising-is-Shutting-Down|text=Discussion Thread: Star Wars Uprising is Shutting Down|date=2016-09-22}}</ref> The droid serves as a unique five-star crew member<ref name="C4-ST" /> available through the Premium Crane, an in-game shop where the player, as the Smuggler, can spend [[chromium]], an in-game currency, in exchange for randomized loot ranging from three stars or better in quality.<ref name="Uprising" />

As such, the player does not need to acquire C4-ST as a crew member. However, this article assumes 100 percent game completion of ''Star Wars: Uprising'', which means the Smuggler acquired C4-ST as a member of the crew and dispatched the droid on Crew Runs.<ref name="Uprising" /> While on Crew Runs, C4-ST provides a bonus to all Diplomatic and Stealth Crew on the same Run and is strong on Credit Finder Runs.<ref name="C4-ST" />
