'''Brian Cummings''' is an American voice actor who has done extensive work in animation and commercials. He is known for working on a many projects for [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]].<ref name="Official site" /> He provided the narration for the video screen travel promotions in the queue of the original [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] attraction for Disney Parks.<ref name="Tales">{{WebCite|url=https://talesfromthecollection.com/star-tours/|text=Meet the Star Tours Creators Through Interviews, Autographs|work=Tales from the Collection|archiveurl=https://archive.is/q5cvG}}</ref>
