The '''dumblefish''' was a type of [[fish]].<ref name="Terror" /> Between [[1 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{TerrorOnMustafarDate}}</ref> the [[mouth]] of [[Vardos Star Scouts]] member [[Drik Maylon]] bobbed open and shut like that of a dumblefish after his fellow [[scout]] [[Deeta Kardon]] informed him that a visitor that they were waiting for on the [[planet]] [[Vardos]] was coming from the planet [[Coruscant]], introducing the possibility that it was [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Terror" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dumblefishes were mentioned in ''[[Terror on Mustafar]]'', an audiobook written by [[Cavan Scott]] and released in [[2026]]. As the story is only available in audio format, no official spelling for the fishes' name is available.<ref name="Terror" />
