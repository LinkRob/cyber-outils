The '''Cantonican Planetary Control''' was the organization that oversaw planetary security on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. It ran the customs that all arriving travellers had to pass through. They particularly looked out for potential [[Spy|spies]] from the [[First Order]] or [[Resistance]].<ref name="Rules of the Game" />
