'''Adam Schuman''' is a concept artist who served as principle character artist for [[2017]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Challenges]]'' augmented/virtual reality experience. Schuman worked on ''Jedi Challenges'' while employed by [[Blind Squirrel Games]], starting with the game development company in [[June]] of [[2015]] but left in [[August]] [[2019]], going on to work for [[Wikipedia:inXile Entertainment|inXile Entertainment]] and eventually [[Wikipedia:Gunfire Games|Gunfire Games]], where he is employed to this day.<ref name="LinkedIn" />
