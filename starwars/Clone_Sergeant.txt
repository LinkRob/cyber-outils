'''Clone Sergeant''',<ref name="CL" /> also known as '''clone trooper sergeant''',<ref name="Black Series">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|text=''Star Wars: The Black Series'' (2014)|pack=#07: Clone Trooper Sergeant|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/07-Clone-Trooper-Sergeant-The-Black-Series-Blue-6-Inch.asp}}</ref> was a [[Military rank|rank]] held by [[clone trooper]]s in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] who commanded [[squad]]s of nine [[trooper]]s. The four [[sergeant]]s of a [[platoon]] were in turn led by their respective [[Clone Lieutenant]]. Standard<ref name="CL" /> [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> sergeants wore [[armor]] featuring four [[Color|green]] circles on the left breastplate as well as a stripe of pesto down the length of each [[Limb|arm]], vertically up the middle of the [[helmet]] and laterally around the visor.<ref name="CL" />

At least one [[Regulation manuals|clone trooper regulation manual]] contained information on what clones should do if their sergeant was unable to lead them.<ref name="Rookies" /> Bearers of the rank included [[Slick]] of a [[Slick's squad|squad]],<ref name="The Hidden Enemy">{{TCW|The Hidden Enemy}}</ref> [[O'Niner]],<ref name="Rookies" /> [[Stew (clone gunner)|Stew]], [[Crasher]] of the [[Clone Youth Brigade]],<ref name="Death Trap">{{TCW|Death Trap}}</ref> [[Appo]] of the [[501st Legion]],<ref name="General">{{TCW|The General (episode)}}</ref> [[Sinker]] of the [[104th Battalion]],<ref name="Rising Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> and [[Hunter]] of [[Clone Force 99]].<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref>
