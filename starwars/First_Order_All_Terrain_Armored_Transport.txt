The '''First Order All Terrain Armored Transport (AT-AT)''' was a model of [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] [[walker]] manufactured by [[Kuat-Entralla Drive Yards]] and fielded by the [[First Order]]. The design was reminiscent of the [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Galactic Civil War)|AT-AT]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Incredible Cross-Sections" /> Two First Order AT-ATs were deployed alongside the larger [[All Terrain MegaCaliber Six]] during the [[Battle of Crait]]. The walker featured increased armor and a boarding ramp under its chin in place of the heavy laser cannons; instead, its primary cannons were mounted on the sides of its head.<ref name="The Last Jedi">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

==History==
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> after the [[death]] of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] and the ascension of [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] as leader of the [[First Order]], a pair of these [[walker]]s led the [[Battle of Crait|charge]] in a [[Veers Formation]] against the [[Resistance]] [[Crait outpost|base]] on the [[planet]] [[Crait]], along with a 11 [[All Terrain MegaCaliber Six|AT-M6s]] and [[Kylo Ren's command shuttle|Ren's]] [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|''Upsilon''-class command shuttle]], with numerous [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s and [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s providing air support.<ref name="The Last Jedi" />
