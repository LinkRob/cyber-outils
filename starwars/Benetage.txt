'''Benetage''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was abandoned almost 30,000 [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was believed that the [[Crown of Verity]] was used as a symbol of the planet's [[magistrate]]s before its abandoning, and the crown eventually came into the possession of the [[Jedi Order]]. That crown was later taken to [[Auratera]] by [[Jedi Master]] [[Tobar Ka-Teen]].<ref name="Nexus" />
