'''Clone Captain''',<ref name="CL" /> also known as '''clone trooper captain''',<ref name="Black Series">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|text=''Star Wars: The Black Series'' (2014)|pack=#13: Clone Trooper Captain|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/13-Clone-Trooper-Captain-6-inch-The-Black-Series.asp}}</ref> was a [[Military rank|rank]] held by [[clone trooper officer]]s.

==Description==
In the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]], Clone Captains commanded [[Company|companies]] of 144 [[trooper]]s,<ref name="CL" /> although this limitation did not apply to every captain.<ref name="501 databank">{{Databank|501st-legion|501st Legion}}</ref> The sixteen [[captain]]s of a [[regiment]] were in turn led by their respective [[Clone Commander]],<ref name="CL" /> and, if they were part of such units, a [[Jedi General]]<ref name="Rex DB">{{Databank|clone-captain-rex|Clone Captain Rex}}</ref> and [[Jedi Commander]].<ref name="TCWfilm">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> Standard<ref name="CL" /> [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> captains wore [[armor]] featuring four [[Color|maroon]] circles on the left chest plate as well as a stripe of maroon down the length of each [[Limb|arm]], vertically up the middle of the helmet and laterally around the visor.<ref name="CL" /> Clone Captains also could be assigned forces in the [[Republic Navy|Galactic Republic Navy]].<ref name="R2 Come Home" /> The Clone Captain rank persisted into the early [[Imperial Era]] as part of the [[Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="Devil's Deal" />

==History==
[[File:Sideshow-RexPhaseI.png|thumb|left|200px|The respected Clone Captain Rex]]
[[Rex]] was a respected<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref> and notable Clone Captain<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> who served as the leader of the [[501st Legion]]<ref name="501 databank" /> and the [[legion]]'s [[Torrent Company]]. He often took orders directly from [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]],<ref name="Rex DB" /> and served as Skywalker's first in [[Commanding officer|command]].<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> Rex also had authority over [[Execute Battalion]] and [[Carnivore Battalion]] of the 501st Legion.<ref name="Cargo">{{TCW|Cargo of Doom}}</ref>

In the final moments of the [[Clone Wars]], Clone Captains,<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> along with the rest of the Grand Army, were ordered by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] to [[Order 66|betray and kill]] their Jedi Generals.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> One of these captains included Clone [[Grey (clone trooper)|Captain Grey]], who executed [[Jedi General]] [[Depa Billaba]] on [[Kaller]].<ref name="Aftermath episode">[[Aftermath (episode)]] | Wookieepedia | Fandom</ref>

The rank of Clone Captain continued to exist in the [[Imperial Military]] during the early [[Standard year|years]] of Darth Sidious' reign as [[Galactic Emperor]] as the clones continued their military service as the first generation of [[stormtrooper]]s. [[Howzer]] and [[Wilco]] were two notable troopers that held the rank of Clone Captain in the Galactic Empire, though the former would eventually be arrested for insubordination,<ref name="Rescue on Ryloth (episode)">{{TBB|Rescue on Ryloth (episode)}}</ref> and the latter would be executed by the corrupt [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]] for refusing to falsify an official military report.<ref name="Ruins of War">{{TBB|Ruins of War}}</ref>
