"'''A Galaxy On Your Doorstep'''" is an article written by [[James Floyd]] that was published in [[Star Wars Insider 203|''Star Wars Insider'' 203]]<ref name="Article" /> on [[June 8]], [[2021]]<ref name="Titan Comics" />

The article is about filming locations in [[California]] and information on how to visit the publicly accessible sites. The primary focus is on the specific sites in [[Death Valley]] ([[Tatooine]] in ''[[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|
A New Hope]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|Return of the Jedi]]'', [[Arvala-7]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'') but also focuses on the [[Redwood National Park|redwood forests]] and other parts of northern California ([[Endor]] in ''Return of the Jedi''), the Colorado desert near [[Yuma]] ([[Khetanna|Jabba's Sail Barge]] and [[Great Pit of Carkoon]] in ''Return of the Jedi''), Rocky Peak Park ([[Tython]] in ''The Mandalorian''), and minor sites across the state.<ref name="Article" />
