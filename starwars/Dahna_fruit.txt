'''Dahna fruit''' was a name given by the [[Dreamers]] to a bright-[[Color|yellow]] [[fruit]] found on the [[planet]] [[Jeosyn]]. The fruit was tangy-sweet and collected by the Dreamers on supply runs.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />

The Dreamers gave the fruit the nickname after their [[Twi'lek]] member [[Dahna]], who particularly loved the fruit.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
