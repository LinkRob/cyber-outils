'''First Order fleet gunners''', also known as '''fleet engineers''', served the [[First Order]], notably aboard the ''[[Supremacy]]'', [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnoughts]]<ref name="Last Jedi Visual Dictionary" /> and aboard [[Star Destroyer]]s like the ''[[Finalizer]]''.

==History==
[[File:FirstOrderFleetGunner-CGSWG.png|thumb|250px|left|A First Order fleet engineer mans a gunnery station]]
Fleet gunners served aboard First Order Star Destroyers during the [[First Order-Resistance War]]. In [[34 ABY]] they manned the guns on the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''Finalizer''<ref name="TFA" /> and the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''Supremacy''. During the [[Battle of D'Qar]] and subsequent [[Battle of Oetchi]], fleet gunners onboard the ''Supremacy'' fired at the retreating [[Resistance]] [[Resistance navy|fleet]]. [[Brun Obatsun]] was a fleet engineer during this time.<ref name="Last Jedi Visual Dictionary" /> The [[Sith Eternal]] had [[Fleet technician|fleet technicians]], which served the same role on the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]].

==Uniform==
First Order fleet gunners wore black uniforms, work gloves, and boots. The [[First Order insignia]] was displayed on the uniform's shoulders. They also wore black, swooping helmets with a face visor displaying targeting data.<ref name="Last Jedi Visual Dictionary" />
