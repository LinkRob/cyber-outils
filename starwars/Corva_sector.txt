{{Quote|We'll go here&mdash;the Corva sector. It's about as far away from Endor and Sullust as you can get without falling off the galactic disk.|[[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]|<ref name="Moving Target" />}}
The '''Corva sector''' was a region of space in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] had a limited presence in the sector during the [[Galactic Civil War]], so the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] used it as part of a mission to distract Imperial forces from detecting the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] that gathered at [[Sullust]].<ref name="Moving Target" />
