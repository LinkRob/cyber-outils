The '''design regiment''' was a sub-unit of the [[Republic Corps of Engineers]]. [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]], a member of the [[corps]] that was secretly transferred to the [[Republic Special Weapons Group]], was offered a position within the design regiment. Republic [[Vice Chair]] [[Mas Amedda]] conversed with Krennic on his career, to which Krennic mentioned the design regiment.<ref name="Catalyst" />
