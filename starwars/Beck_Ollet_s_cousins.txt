'''Cousins''' of [[Beck Ollet]], a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] living on [[Lothal]], existed during the same time that Ollet himself was a teenager. At one point, when [[Zare Leonis]] was looking for Ollet, his parents told him that he was spending a [[Standard month|month]] riding [[Speeder bike|jumpspeeders]] around Lothal with these cousins. This was actually cover for the fact that Ollet had joined an underground resistance against the [[Galactic Empire]]'s activities on Lothal.<ref name="edge">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref>{{Stub}}
