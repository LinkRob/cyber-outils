'''''Dark Droids'' 4''' is the fourth issue of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] limited series ''[[Star Wars: Dark Droids]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Luke Ross]] and [[Alex Sinclair]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 15]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE SCOURGE IS ON THE VERGE OF HIS ULTIMATE TRIUMPH! The EMPIRE decrees: BEWARE THE DROIDS! Horrible laboratories across the galaxy are focused on transporting his many minds from DROIDS&hellip;into ORGANICS! But unbeknown to the Scourge, a FINAL CHALLENGE to its total victory is building, from an enemy it believed it had defeated once and for all!''
