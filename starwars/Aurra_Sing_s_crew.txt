'''Aurra Sing's crew''' was a crew of [[bounty hunter]]s led by the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Palliduvan]] [[Aurra Sing]]. [[Boba Fett]] spent the early [[Standard day|days]] of his [[bounty]] hunting career as a member of the crew.<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters" /> [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] and [[Castas]] were also members of Aurra Sing's crew helping Boba track down [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]],<ref name="Death Trap" /> though Castas was killed by Sing at a bar on [[Florrum]].<ref name="R2 Come Home">{{TCW|R2 Come Home}}</ref>
