'''Control''' was one of the many types of games played in the [[Grand Arena]]. In Control, the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]] had to capture a designated point and hold it for a certain amount of [[time]].<ref name="Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
Control first appeared in the [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' as one of the playable game modes, which was developed by [[NaturalMotion]]'s [[BossAlien]] and published by [[Zynga]]<ref name="SWH" /> on [[November 16]], [[2021]], as a soft launch to select countries.<ref name="Soft Release">{{Twitter|ZyngaStarWars|status/1460656552734605317|[[Zynga]] Star Wars|date=2021-11-16|quote=We are excited to announce Star Wars: Hunters is now entering soft launch and is available on Android for players in India, Philippines, Malaysia & Indonesia.|archivedate=20220625084625|archivefile=File:ZyngaStarWars-SoftLaunchDate-Twitter.png}}</ref> The game mode followed the standard video game concept of [[Wikipedia:King of the hill (game)#In video gaming|king of the hill]],<ref name="KOTH">{{WebCite|url=https://www.larksuite.com/en_us/topics/gaming-glossary/king-of-the-hill-koth|text=King of the Hill (KOTH)|work=[https://www.larksuite.com larksuite.com]|date=2024-06-26|archivedate=20250302122238}}</ref> in which two teams had the same objective of taking control of a specific point and defend it until they reach a certain threshold to win.<ref name="SWH" /> Following the introduction of game modes [[Dynamic Control]]<ref name="DC">{{Hunters|url=news/0-19-patch-notes|text=Game Update: January 9th, 2024}}</ref> and [[Power Control]] to the game,<ref name="PC">{{Hunters|url=news/game-update-march-28th-2024|text=Game Update: March 28, 2024}}</ref> Control was removed from the game's rotation and has yet to make a return to ''Star Wars: Hunters'' since soft launch.<ref name="SWH" />
