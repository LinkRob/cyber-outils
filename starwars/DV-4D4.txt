'''DV-4D4''', or '''DVD-4D4''', was a [[DV supervisor droid|DV supervisor]] who worked in the control room in the [[spice mines of Kessel]] in [[10 BBY]]. After [[L3-37]] freed him and other [[droid]]s, DV-4D4 freed every droid and [[Slavery|slave]] in the mine.

==Biography==
DV-4D4,<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2018 Topps Solo: A Star Wars Story|cardname=#27 - DV-4D4|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/162389/cid/11326516/2018-Topps-Solo:-A-Star-Wars-Story-27-DV-4D4|nobackup=1}}</ref> also known as DVD-4D4,<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> was a [[DV supervisor droid|DV supervisor]] [[droid]]<ref name="Kids" /> that was [[Slavery|forced to work]] in the criminal [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]]. The [[supervisor droid]] worked in the [[Mining|mines']] control room and was controlled by a [[restraining bolt]].<ref name="Solo" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events on [[Kessel]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref> a group of disguised criminals working for the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate [[Raid on Kessel|arrived]] at the mine with the aim of stealing [[coaxium]] from the [[Pyke]]s. Believing that the criminals were there to trade in enslaved workers, mine [[director]] [[Quay Tolsite]] guided a group of them into the control room as DV-4D4 was walking past the door. The droid moved over to an open case by the room's wall as Tolsite began to negotiate with the criminals, but the Crimson Dawn affiliates soon attacked Tolsite and his guards, killing all of the Pyke personnel in the room.<ref name="Solo" />

The criminal droid [[L3-37]] then removed the restraining bolt from the enslaved droid [[DD-BD]] and told him to free the other droids. After the<ref name="Solo" /> assistant [[adminmech droid]] [[GB-BD]]<ref name="SoloOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> was freed, it in turn removed the restraining bolt from DV-4D4's chest.<ref name="Solo" /> As the freed droids became self-aware, they began to view L3-37 as something akin to a [[Deity|goddess]] and DD-BD her [[prophet]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

Attempting to emulate L3-37,<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> who was focused on guiding her ally [[Han Solo]] remotely through the mine tunnels and opening doors for him as he went,<ref name="Solo" /> DV-4D4 found a large red button on a control room console<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> and flipped a switch next to it before pressing the button and exclaiming "freedom." The button turned green and released the shackles of all the enslaved organic workers throughout the mine, who quickly rose up alongside the droids and began attacking the remaining Pykes. DV-4D4 then began ripping cabling from one of the central control room consoles as other droids tore wiring from the walls. The supervisor droid was still at one of the consoles when the criminals gave a rallying cry and then rushed from the control room to fight their way off world.<ref name="Solo" />

==Characteristics==
DV-4D4 felt ambition rising within himself for the first [[time]] when he pressed the button to release the mine workers.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> The droid had [[Color|brown]] platting and three white [[sensor]]s.<ref name="Solo" />
