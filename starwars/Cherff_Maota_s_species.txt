This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TFA" /> [[Cherff Maota]] was a member of this species.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Biology and appearance==
This [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] was distinguished by their elongated faces, with small [[nose]]s, pointed [[ear]]s, and tall philtrums.<ref name="TFA" />

==Species in the galaxy==
During the [[High Republic Era]], a member of this species, [[Cherff Maota]], served as a [[Jedi Master]] within the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TFA Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Maota was immortalized via decorative busts, one of which ended up in the possess of [[Maz Kanata]] at her [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]].<ref name="TFA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The species made their debut via a bust in the [[2015]] film {{Film|VII}}, directed by [[J.J. Abrams]].<ref name="TFA" /> In the movie's [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|novelization]], the private collection of Kanata notably includes what is described as "a bust of some unknown bearded [[human]]."<ref name="Novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref> Maota's bust, however, clearly has [[Alien|non-human]] features.<ref name="TFAVD" />
