{{Quote|The long-necked, dark-striped etobi bears a passing resemblance to its similarly named cousin, the eopie. However, etobi have four eyes&mdash;exceeding the eopie's by two&mdash;and bear a more marked pattern of camouflage.|From the journal of Ardis San Tekka|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
The '''etobi''' were a type of [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[Limb|quadrupedal]] creature [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Pasaana]], where they were used as mounts. The [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Aki-Aki]] [[species]] there created toys and decorations in the creatures' likeness, which included four [[eye]]s, a pair of [[Mouth|mandible]] pincers, and a long neck. The creatures were also used as mounts and pack animals on the desert planet [[Tatooine]], where the [[Jedi]] [[Rey Skywalker]] encountered an [[Unidentified Tatooine elder|elder]] with a saddled etobi near the [[Lars homestead]] in [[35 ABY]].

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Their darkened topcoats complement the desert sand, and their light underbellies help them blend with the sky, which is very advantageous if a subterranean predator is looking up. Etobi grasp food and defend themselves with mandible pincers.|From the journal of Ardis San Tekka|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
[[File:EtobiHead-Beastiary.png|thumb|right|200px|Etobi had mouth pincers.]]
The etobi was a type of [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[Limb|quadruped]] creature with hooved feet and a long neck, bearing resemblance to both its similarly-named cousin, the [[eopie]],<ref name="Bestiary" /> and the [[giraffe]].<ref name="TROSAD">{{Film|IX}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> Etobi were covered in [[Hair|fur]], with a darker topcoat of [[Color|orange]] with dark black stripes that complemented the sands of their [[desert]] habitat and provided a marked pattern of camouflage.<ref name="Bestiary" />

The fur on the creatures' underside was a lighter white color that helped to blend with the sky, giving them an advantage against subterranean predators looking up at them from underneath. An etobi's head consisted of four black [[eye]]s surronded by white circles, two furred [[ear]]s, and a pair of black [[Mouth|mandible]] pincers that the creatures used to grasp [[Foodstuff|food]] and defend themselves.<ref name="Bestiary" />

==History==
{{Quote|The creatures are used as mounts on Tatooine and their native Pasaana, where the Aki-Aki copy their likeness to create toys and decorations for younglings.|From the journal of Ardis San Tekka|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
[[File:Elder_with_etobi.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Rey Skywalker encountered an elder with an etobi in the deserts of Tatooine.]]
Etobi were native to the desert [[planet]] [[Pasaana]] where they were used as mounts. The [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Aki-Aki]] native to Pasaana also created toys and decorations in the likeness of the etobi for their [[youngling]]s,<ref name="Bestiary" /> with the hand-painted wood carvings showing the species' belief that the children were their future. Both busts and full statues of etobi,<ref name="GeneralEquipment">{{EncyclopediaCite|General Equipment (1)}}</ref> including a [[sculpted etobi head]] and a [[wooden etobi rocker]], were available during the Aki-Aki [[Festival of the Ancestors]],<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> held in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref>

Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Timelines" /> a [[human]] [[Unidentified Tatooine elder|elder]] on<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> the desert planet [[Tatooine]], where etobi were also used as mounts<ref name="Bestiary" /> and pack beasts,<ref name="SWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> passed by the ruined [[Lars homestead]] with a saddled etobi. The elder stopped with the creature upon seeing the [[Jedi]] [[Rey Skywalker]] at the homestead and asked her name.<ref name="TROS" /> Skywalker, who assumed the woman and the gangly etobi were heading to a trading post, identified herself, after which the elder left with the etobi.<ref name="TROSEE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] [[Director of Creature Cartography]] [[Ardis San Tekka]] later included an illustrated entry on etobi in her [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]].<ref name="Bestiary" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I was playing with the idea that local fauna might have some evolutionary relationship to the Aki-Aki. J.J. liked these animals enough that we made three for the shoot, although he didn't want them to have trunks and the design changed from this look.|Jake Lunt Davies on "Elephantine Alien 17"|<ref name="ArtOf">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]''</ref>}}
[[File:FenStilts1b-TROSArt.png|thumb|right|300px|"Fen Stilts 1B"]]
An etobi first appeared in the [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']],<ref name="TROS" /> which was released on [[December 20]], [[2019]].<ref name="Jedi Redeemed">{{InsiderCite|196|The Jedi Redeemed: Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker|The Jedi Redeemed: ''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker''}}</ref> Several pieces of concept art of the creature was created for the film by [[Jake Lunt Davies]], including "Fen Stilts 1B," which showed the creatures with curled trunks and longer ears in a marsh environment that was to be the Aki-Aki's home early in development. Davies' idea was that the creatures' long legs, height, and trunks would allow them to wade deeper into the swampy environment.<ref name="ArtOf" />

This design also appeared alongside Aki-Aki with similar trunks in a desert environment in Davies' "Elephantine Alien 17" design, with Davies exploring the idea that the creatures shared an evolutionary relationship with the Aki-Aki. Director [[J.J. Abrams]] requested that the trunks be changed, and in Davies' head study of the creatures, titled "Giraffes 09," the trunks were replaced with mandible pincers, although the creatures still had the longer ears. These were later shortened due to concerns that the creatures might be confused with the long-eared [[fathier]]s that had appeared in the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="ArtOf" />

[[File:Giraffes09-TROSArt.png|thumb|left|300px|"Giraffes 09"]]
A sculpt of the creature with these shorter ears was created and then painted over by Davies for the concept art piece "Giraffes 12 Smaller Ears," although Davies considered the ear change unfortunate. Abrams liked the creatures enough that three were made for the film shoot,<ref name="ArtOf" /> although only one appears in the final cut. The creature was not identified in the film itself,<ref name="TROS" /> but the "etobi" identification first appeared on labels for the Aki-Aki carvings in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TROSVD" /> and released on the same day as the film.<ref name="DKhard">{{DK|url=us/book/9780241357699-star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker-the-visual-dictionary|text=Star Wars The Rise of Skywalker The Visual Dictionary}}</ref>
