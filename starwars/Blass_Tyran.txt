'''Blass Tyran''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who was present in the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of [[Maz Kanata]] on [[Takodana]] when the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Takodana|attacked]] the [[planet]].<ref name="VII" />

==Behind the scenes==
Tyran appeared in the [[2015]] film ''[[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens]]''.<ref name="VII" /> He was only named in ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'', which adapts the events of the movie in LEGO form.<ref name="LEGO">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref> Although the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] games are non-canon,<ref name="Leland">{{Twitter|holocronkeeper|status/697867043870093312|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=@InevitablyBill @_drewski In the Holocron, I call it LEGO canon.|archivefile=File:HolocronKeeper-LEGOcanon.png}}</ref> [[Pablo Hidalgo]] has confirmed that the names assigned to movie background characters are [[canon]]ical.<ref name="Pablo">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/749616406245699584|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2016-07-03|quote=@LelalMekha Yeah, same with Infinity.|archivefile=File:PabloHidalgo-LegoName-Twitter.png|nolive=1}} {{C|In response to: "@pablohidalgo <nowiki>youtube.com/watch?v=JwxAeHPLpPg</nowiki> The Lego game identifies a few BG characters from TFA, but are those names canonical?"}}</ref>
