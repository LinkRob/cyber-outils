'''Corbus Tyra''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] criminal.<ref name="Han Chewie 5" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> Tyra was contacted by the [[Kajain'sa'Nikto]] [[Vizam]] about a job with a reward of a million [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]: to recover an [[Unidentified urn|urn]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] that contained the ashes of [[Krestrel D'Naran]], one of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]'s most hated rivals. Tyra then noted that for the amount of credits on offer, they would not be the only one after the urn, to which Vizam replied that a crew, that consisted of [[Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca]], and [[Greedo]], had already been sent by Jabba. Tyra then traveled to Corellia where, posing as Solo's father [[Ovan]],<ref name="Han Chewie 1" /> he encountered the trio, though they were unable to acquire the urn as it had been sold to [[The Archivist|Madelin Sun]] on [[Antillion]].<ref name="Han Chewie 3">[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 3|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 3]]</ref>

After they made their way to Antillion and found the urn, Tyra was shot in the back by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Krrsantan]]<ref name="Han Chewie 3" /> who left with Solo and traveled to [[Mollo Tanka]]. Chewbacca then rescued Tyra and took him back to Solo's [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', so they could rescue Solo. After they made their way to Mollo Tanka, Tyra stayed aboard the ''Falcon'' whilst Chewbacca [[Mission to Mollo Tanka|rescued]] Solo, though when the two arrived back at where the ''Falcon'' had been parked, they discovered it was gone.<ref name="Han Chewie 4">[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 4|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 4]]</ref> Tyra, in possession of the urn, made his way to [[Iakar]], and whilst waiting to speak with an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[supervisor]], was arrested by [[Marshal]] [[Buck Vancto]] for various prior crimes. The marshal took Tyra to the [[Benelex Marshal Service Headquarters]] on [[Paqualis III]], though the forces of Solo, Chewbacca, Greedo, and the [[Khel Tanna's crew|crew]] of [[Khel Tanna]] arrived to [[Extraction on Paqualis III|extract]] Tyra. They successfully extracted Tyra, though Chewbacca was captured.<ref name="Han Chewie 5" />
