{{Quote|But it's deep in the heart of ''Hutt Space''&hellip;if you show up in an ''Imperial shuttle'', they'll scatter like ''worrts'' on a ''hot pan''. But if&hellip; we took my ''barge''&hellip;|Bokku|<ref name="Vader 13" />}}
A '''barge''' was owned by the [[Hutt]] [[Bokku]] and operated by the [[nav droid]] [[Gak-Sixtoo]]. Bokku used the barge to transport the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to [[Zee-Nine City Seven]], deep within [[Hutt Space]], as part of Vader's search for the [[carbonite]]-frozen [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]]. After Vader [[Plot to assassinate Darth Vader|defeated]] the [[assassin droid]] [[IG-88B|IG-88]] within the [[city]], Bokku had Gak-Sixtoo [[Aerial bombardment|aerially bombard]] the [[Sith Lord]], but Vader survived, deflecting the fire with his [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. Bokku, having realized that Vader could not be defeated, landed the barge on the ground despite the betrayal, allowing Vader to board.<ref name="Vader 13" />
