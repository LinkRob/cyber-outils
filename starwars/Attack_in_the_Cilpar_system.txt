An '''attack ''' occurred during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Draedan pirates]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] sometime<ref name="SWA 11" /> between [[1 ABY]] and [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> in the [[Cilpar system]] when the pirates attacked and damaged a Rebel [[CR90 corvette]]. [[Rogue Squadron]] arrived to attack the [[Unidentified pirate starfighter (Draedan pirates)|pirate ships]]. The rookie pilot [[Ibti Myrak]] pursued a fighter running off, but were ambushed by more. She was saved in turn by the rest of the squadron. Afterward, Myrak was grounded.<ref name="SWA 11" />
