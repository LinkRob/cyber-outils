'''AD-W4''', also referred to as '''Ay-Dee''', was a [[mercenary]] [[droid]] that [[General]] [[Grievous]] hired to oversee the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] operations on the [[planet]] [[Hissrich]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. AD-W4 was ultimately [[Death|destroyed]] after a [[Operation on Hissrich|duel]] with [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] on Hissrich.

==Biography==
Shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the [[Clone Wars]], AD-W4 was approached by the [[cyborg]] [[General]] [[Grievous]], the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Martial Commander]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Armies]]. Grievous hired Ay-Dee to oversee the Separatist operations on the [[planet]] [[Hissrich]], a job the [[droid]] [[mercenary]] only accepted after seeing the [[Currency|pay]] he would get from the job.<ref name="Mace Windu 3" />

[[File:MaceWinduVsADW4-JOTRMaceWindu5.jpg|thumb|left|250px|AD-W4 found himself in another duel against Mace Windu before the end of the operation on Hissrich.]]
While in Hissrich, AD-W4 first heard of some [[Jedi]]'s arrival when they ambushed one of the droid patrols. AD-W4 learned of this when a [[Unidentified B1-series battle droid 1 (Hissrich)|B1 battle droid]] reported it to him.<ref name="Mace Windu 1" /> Later on, [[Rissa Mano]] accidentally activated a [[B1-series battle droid]] [[scout]], which gave AD-W4 the chance to set up an ambush for the [[Mace Windu's squad|four Jedi]]. During the ambush on the [[Operation on Hissrich|Hissrich operation]], AD-W4 faced the [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] in single combat. The robot mercenary eventually bested him for a few seconds and escaped.<ref name="Mace Windu 2" /> He attempted to increase his [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] payment by lying to General Grievous about besting and killing Windu. However, when Grievous asked for [[Mace Windu's lightsaber|Windu's lightsaber]] as proof, AD-W4 was forced to take further action against the Jedi.<ref name="Mace Windu 3" /> Although Ay-Dee bested Windu the first [[time]], Windu managed to critically wound Ay-Dee with [[Mace Windu's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] during the second duel. Realizing that he had lost, AD-W4 detached his head from his body in an attempt to escape, but Windu managed to [[Death|blow up]] AD-W4's head using [[the Force]].<ref name="Mace Windu 5">[[Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu 5|''Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu'' 5]]</ref>

==Characteristics==
===Personality and traits===
{{Quote|When I went to Ravi to offer to relieve him of this new rival, he told me this bounty, copious as it was, was a drop in the bucket of his wealth. If I killed Osmo, I would have well-paying jobs in Ravi's employ for life. Ravi cried--oh how he cried--when I ''gutted'' him like a frella fish. And then I painted the interior of his ''garish'' palace with the red blood of his guards before taking his entire sum for myself.|AD-W4|<ref name="Mace Windu 3" />}}
[[File:ADW4AndB1BattleDroid-JOTRMaceWindu2.png|right|thumb|250x230px|AD-W4 hated the battle droids he needed to command.]]
AD-W4 was a tall, fearsome droid of an unknown model, with [[Color|red]] [[photoreceptor]]s and purple plating with red markings. He regarded credits as the one true ruler of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Mace Windu 1" /> He was also sadistic and bloodthirsty, and was, according to General [[Grievous]], known for having a "flare ''[sic]'' for the dramatic." According to one B1 battle droid that began to complain to Grievous, AD-W4 was even meaner than the general himself.<ref name="Mace Windu 3" />

He was disdainful of the [[Jedi Order]], regarding its members as arrogant and their beliefs&mdash;particularly their commitment to peace&mdash; as asinine.<ref name="Mace Windu 2" /> He also disliked the Separatist [[battle droid]]s he was assigned to command, viewing them as stupid and totally expendable, an attitude not unlike that of his final client, General Grievous. He was generally annoyed by their behavior and disliked how they addressed him by multiple different ranks. AD-W4 didn't care about officer ranks, which had no meaning to him, when Grievous reprimanded the mercenary droid for not addressing him with the title of general.<ref name="Mace Windu 3" />

===Skills and abilities===
{{Quote|Do not confuse me with one of your mass-produced, fall-in-line, 37-percent kill-rate-at-best, military drones. That's not what I am. That's not why you're here.''"<br />''[&hellip;]''<br />"''Of course not. Your mastery of ''killing'' is only matched by your apparent flare for the ''dramatic''.|AD-W4 and General Grievous|<ref name="Mace Windu 3" />}}
AD-W4 carried a [[DL-44 heavy blaster pistol]] and also had some skill with an [[electrostaff]], as he was able to hold his own against and on occasion overpower [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]]. He could also pilot his own [[Single Trooper Aerial Platform]]. The droid mercenary had jets built into his feet&mdash;allowing him to fly&mdash;and an energy blaster built into his wrist. His body was built so as to have a high degree of physical strength, able to pick up and hurl a ball of rubble much larger than himself.<ref name="Mace Windu 2" /> He was also able to remain active even after being stabbed through the chest with a lightsaber. As a last resort, he could detach his head from his body and fly away at high speed.<ref name="Mace Windu 5" />
