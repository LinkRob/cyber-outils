'''Cocktails''' were a type of [[alcohol]]ic beverage that mixed one or more alcohols with additional ingredients. They were often served in [[cantina]]s.<ref name="Cookbook" /> During the [[Republic Era]], cocktails were served in the [[restaurant]] [[Dex's Diner]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Wookiee" /> On the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]], [[Photon Fizzle]] cocktails were served in the traveling [[Restaurant|diner]] [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|Tuggs' Grub]].<ref name="Cookbook" />
