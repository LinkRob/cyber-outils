The '''droid transport carrier''' was a [[Troop transport/Legends|troop transport]] utilized by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems']] [[Separatist Droid Army/Legends|Droid Army]]<ref name="7654DroidBattlePack" /> in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]].<ref name="TNEC">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' dates the events of {{Film|III}} to [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]]. As the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set [[w:c:brickipedia:7654 Droids Battle Pack|7654 Droids Battle Pack]] is intended to depict events from the film, it must be depicting events from 19 [[Galactic Standard Calendar|BBY]] as well.</ref> The [[Troop carrier/Legends|troop carrier]] was [[Pilot/Legends|piloted]] by a single [[B1-series battle droid/Legends|B1-series battle droid]], and could carry up to five [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]]&mdash;either B1-Series battle [[Droid/Legends|droids]] or [[B2-series super battle droid/Legends|B2-series super battle droids]]&mdash;as passengers. The carrier also had a place to stow two [[E-5 blaster rifle/Legends|E-5 blaster rifles]], and had an armament of two [[Cannon/Legends|cannons]]&mdash;one on each side of its front end. One droid [[Transport/Legends|transport]] carrier participated in the [[Battle of Kashyyyk/Legends|Battle of Kashyyyk]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]], alongside a [[Single Trooper Aerial Platform/Legends|Single Trooper Aerial Platform]].<ref name="7654DroidBattlePack" />
