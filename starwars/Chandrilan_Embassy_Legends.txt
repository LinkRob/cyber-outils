The '''Chandrilan Embassy''' was the official representation of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Chandrila/Legends|Chandrila]] located on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] at the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. It housed 58 staff members, including acting [[Ambassador/Legends|ambassador]] [[Irem Gehldon]], [[Major/Legends|major]] [[Jonas Davan]] and [[Jennie Pickalho]], [[Secretary/Legends|secretary]] to the [[Visa Office]] of the embassy.
