'''Carvian''' referred to a period of [[time]] that followed the [[third millennium]] as well as the [[Lost Empire]] period of time. During an art exhibition on<ref name="FestiveEvening" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] hosted by [[banker]] [[Davo Sculdun]]<ref name="FestiveEvening" /> in [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]" establishes that the events of the episode take place during [[3 BBY]]. The events of the two following episodes of [[Star Wars: Andor|''Andor's'']] [[Andor Season 2|second season]], including "[[What a Festive Evening]]," take place shortly after "Ever Been to Ghorman?" and therefore must take place in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] as well.</ref> artifact peddler [[Luthen Rael]] showed a [[Luthen's ring|ring]] he wore to fellow partygoer [[Strang]], who guessed that the [[ring]] predated the Carvian period. Strang further guessed that the [[stone]] in the ring was Carvian in origin, but that the ring itself was older; Luthen affirmed this by stating he believed the ring came from the Lost Empire period.<ref name="FestiveEvening" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Carvian period was mentioned in "[[What a Festive Evening]]," the sixth episode of the [[Disney+]] series [[Star Wars: Andor|''Andor's'']] [[Andor Season 2|second season]],<ref name="FestiveEvening" /> which premiered on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref>
