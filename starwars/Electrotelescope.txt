'''Electrotelescopes''' were devices that could be used to observe celestial bodies remotely.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> During the early [[New Republic Era]], a long range electrotelescope array was located on the [[planet]] [[Adelphi]].<ref name="NYCC 2025">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2025-lucasfilm-publishing-reveals|text=NYCC 2025: All the News and Reveals from the Lucasfilm Publishing Panel}}</ref> Decades later ,before establishing a [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|base]] on the [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]], the [[Resistance]] waited several [[Standard month|months]], observing it with electrotelescopes to ensure it had not been discovered.<ref name="TROS VD" />
