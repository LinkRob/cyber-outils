'''Dez Rydan''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi Knight]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He had [[Color|golden]] skin and black hair.

==Biography==
Before becoming a [[Jedi Knight]], Dez Rydan was the [[Padawan]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Jora Malli]]. Rydan was one of several [[Jedi]] who traveled aboard the [[starship]] ''[[Vessel]]'' to the [[Starlight Beacon]]. Craving adventure, Rydan had resigned from the [[Zeitooine mission]] to request the assignment. However, the ''Vessel'' was waylaid by the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] and stranded at an [[Amaxine Space Station|ancient]] [[space station]].<ref name="Into the Dark" />

While exploring the station's lower levels, Rydan was trapped behind a door and seemingly disintegrated by a set of [[helix ring]]s, leading the other Jedi&ndash;[[Reath Silas]], [[Cohmac Vitus]], and [[Orla Jareni]]&ndash;to assume him [[Death|dead]]. In reality, Rydan, who had sustained a head injury, had been transported via [[hyperspace]] [[Amaxine relay|relay]] to a [[Mulita|planet]] inhabited by the [[Drengir]], a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]] [[carnivorous plant]]s inherently attuned to the [[dark side of the Force]]. Rydan was tortured by the Drengir for [[Standard day|days]] on how to operate the relay, but he was insensible due to the head injury. When the three other Jedi [[Mission on the Amaxine station|returned]] to the station, Silas discovered the relay and was transported to the Drengir planet, where the creatures attempted to force Rydan to kill him. The [[Padawan]] was able to rescue Rydan and bring him back to the station. Subsequently, recovering from his injuries, Rydan feared that his connection to [[the Force]] had been severely damaged by his treatment at the hands of the Drengir, leading him to decide to swear the [[Barash Vow]].<ref name="Into the Dark" />

Four years later,<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]'' take place in [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:IntotheLightDate|here]].</ref> Rydan returned to the [[Jedi Order]]. He participated in the [[Evacuation of Inad Komesh]], which had fallen victim to the [[blight]], and afterwards mused that the process for welcoming back those who had taken the Barash Vow was insufficient in wartime. During their return to Coruscant, Rydan reminisced with Silas on Jora Malli and discussed the changes in the Order. Rydan was unsettled by the increase in threats by the [[Nihil]] and the blight since his departure. Despite his years under the Vow, Rydan remained marked by the [[Dark side of the Force|darkness]] he was subjected to, and he remained sensitive to sources of darkness such as the former Jedi [[Azlin Rell]] and the [[Thornseed]]. <ref name="Into the Light" />

==Personality and traits==
Dez Rydan was an energetic and outgoing Jedi. Rydan was highly regarded by younger members of the Jedi Order. He notably had a close relationship with Padawan Reath Silas due to the fact that Silas was the new Padawan of Rydan's former master, Jora Malli. The two would practice dueling with each other on occasion, a practice that Silas noted made other Padawans jealous of him. Rydan sought adventure during his service to the Order, which he admitted was a problem that caused him to take the Barash Vow, saying that he could not balance his own desire for adventure and a Jedi's need to constantly be in service to others.<ref name="Into the Dark" />

After his return from the Barash Vow, Rydan became more cautious and thoughtful. Though he retained much of his good nature, he was more conscious of his faults, and he was particularly conscious of the influence of darkness. During the [[Mission to Kashyyyk (blight)|mission to Kashyyyk]], he struggled with the proximity of the Thornseed, and several times doubted whether he had successfully healed through the Barash Vow; however, he retained his composure, and Silas, observing him in combat against the Drengir, thought that his hesitation had been fully conquered. <ref name="Into the Light" />
