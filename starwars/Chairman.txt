A '''chairman''',<ref name="Trespass" /> also known as a '''chairperson''',<ref name="Battle of Jedha">''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]''</ref> was an individual who presided over an assembly.<ref name="Trespass" /> [[San Hill]] was the chairman of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="USW" /> The [[Pantoran Assembly]], the council of [[Politics|elected]] officials that ruled over [[Pantora]], was headed by a [[Supreme Chairman]]. In the last [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], the role of Supreme Chairman of the [[Pantoran Assembly]] was filled by [[Chi Cho]], until he [[Death|died]]<ref name="Trespass" /> and was replaced by [[Baron]] [[Notluwiski Papanoida]].<ref name="Sphere">{{TCW|Sphere of Influence}}</ref>

In the closing stages of the Clone Wars, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] informed [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] that [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[Head of State|leader]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] was on the [[planet]] [[Utapau]], citing a partial message in a diplomatic package from the Chairman of Utapau, which Skywalker relayed to the [[Jedi High Council]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> [[Pitina Mar-Mas Voor|Pitina Voor]] served as Chair of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Coalition for Progress]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Aphra 35" />
