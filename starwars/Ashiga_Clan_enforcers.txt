'''Ashiga Clan enforcers''' were soldiers serving under the [[Ashiga Clan]], along with [[Queen]] [[Ashiga]], the leader of the clan. The enforcers consists mainly of the [[Melitto]] species, armed with the [[ABR-2 Zato blaster rifle|ABR-2 Zato]] [[blaster rifle]] and sometimes with [[vibroblade]]s. Based at the [[Dai Bendu Monastery|Ashiga Clan stronghold]] on the frozen world of [[Kijimi]], they served as the primary soldiers of the Melitto hive, guarding Ashiga facilities and enforcing Ashiga rule over the Kijimi population. 

==History==
===Early history===
The Ashiga Clan enforcers originated from a clan of [[Melitto]] known as the Ashiga Clan, a group which settled on [[Kijimi City]] during the [[High Republic Era]] to eventually establish a strong influence over the frigid [[planet]] of [[Kijimi]]. Serving under the [[queen]] of the clan, [[Ashiga]], many Melitto of the hive would become the Ashiga Clan enforcers as guards for Ashiga facilities, or as soldiers implementing clan activities such as extortion.<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Imperial Era===
[[File:AshigaClanEnforcers-Outlaws.png|thumb|300px|left|Ashiga Clan enforcers patrol Kijimi City]]
Ashiga Clan enforcers were prevalent within Kijimi City by the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Outlaw Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> However, as the Ashiga Clan's power began to weaken, Queen Ashiga established a relationship with the [[Galactic Empire]], manufacturing explosives in return for resources to fund their expansion across the galaxy. Due to the said expansion, Ashiga Clan enforcers also had a small presence on the desert world of [[Tatooine]] to guard the Ashiga Clan operative [[Zyssyk]]. At some point, the clan would capture the explosive expert [[Ank Parako]] to build explosives for the Empire. However, the decisions of Queen Ashiga caused dissent amidst the hive, with the daughter of the queen, [[Krisk Ashiga]], allying with the [[Crimson Dawn]] leader [[Qi'ra]] for a plot to the hive. Some enforcers would defect to Crimson Dawn as a result.<ref name="Outlaws" />   

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaw Date" /> the outlaw [[Kay Vess]] and her [[Kay Vess' crew|crew]] would come to Kijimi in search of Ank Parako, hoping to [[Jaylen Vrax's campaign|recruit]] her as the safe cracker for a [[Heist on Canto Bight|heist]], where they encountered Ashiga Clan enforcers when landing at the [[Kijimi Spaceport|Kijimi spaceport]]. After failing to convince the queen to release Parako, Vess allied with Crimson Dawn as part of their revolution that can free Parako along the way. Several Ashiga Clan enforcers were present when Kay Vess [[Rescue of Ank Parako|rescued]] Ank Parako from the [[Ashiga weapons factory]]. Many Ashiga Clan enforcers also guarded the Ashiga Clan stronghold when Crimson Dawn launched an [[Mission to the Ashiga Clan stronghold|attack]] against the clan.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Equipment==
[[File:KriskAshigaExecution.png|thumb|250px|right|An Ashiga Clan enforcer executing Krisk Ashiga with a vibroblade]]
Ashiga Clan enforcers were equipped with the ABR-2 Zato blaster rifle as their weapon-of-choice. On rare occasions, the enforcers also utilized vibroblades, such as guards that were present in the [[Throne room (Ashiga Clan Stronghold)|throne room]] of the Ashiga Clan stronghold, often for executions.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ashiga Clan enforcers were created for ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]].''<ref name="OutlawsSetting">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/exclusive-interview/2023/08/15/star-wars-outlaws-setting-is-a-dream-playground-according-to|text=Star Wars Outlaws' Setting Is A Dream Playground, According To Narrative Director|work=Game Informer|author=Kyle Hillard|date=2023-08-15|access-date=2023-08-30|archivedate=20230827125620}}</ref> The enforcers were heavily influenced by Japanese culture, with their use of [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katana katana-like] swords<ref name="Outlaws" /> and outfits inspired by Japanese [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimono kimonos].<ref name="OutlawsNavigatingTheUnderworld">{{UbisoftYouTube|BxyqrPk51KM|Star Wars Outlaws: Behind The Scenes &ndash; Navigating the Underworld}}</ref>
