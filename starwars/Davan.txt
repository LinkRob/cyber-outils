'''Davan''' was a [[human]] individual who served as a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Pathfinders]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Davan was present among a group of individuals who [[Sergeant]] [[Kes Dameron]] assembled to accompany him on an unsanctioned mission to rescue his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Shara Bey]] from the Imperial [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tarkin's Will]]''. Davan was shot while the others tried to plant detonators on the Star Destroyer.<ref name="Star Wars 21" />
