'''Barnahof''' was an astronomical object in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], patrolled by [[stormtrooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]]. While transporting the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] members [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Han Solo]], [[Sana Starros]] stopped on Barnahof to meet with the [[Nuxan]] [[Information broker|informant]] [[Bodo Linx]].

==History==
Shortly after the [[Battle of Mako-Ta|assault]] on the [[Mako-Ta Space Docks]],<ref name="Star Wars 55">[[Star Wars (2015) 55|''Star Wars'' (2015) 55]]</ref> [[Sana Starros]] took [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], her older twin brother [[Jedi]] [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]], and [[Captain|Capatin]] [[Han Solo]] to a [[cantina]] on Barnahof so that they could meet with the [[Nuxan]] [[Information broker|informant]] [[Bodo Linx]]. Several [[stormtrooper]]s entered the establishment, and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] hid beneath a table. The stormtroopers briefly interrogated Starros and Linx before leaving, allowing the rebel fugitives to continue making their deal. Linx gave Organa a [[datacard]] containing the details of his [[Qensog|contact]] on [[Brentaal IV]], and Starros left Barnahof, transporting the rebels in her ship the ''[[Volt Cobra]]''.<ref name="Star Wars 56" />
