The '''Drabor Configuration''' was the arrangement of the [[engine]]s on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]]. It was named after [[Shug Drabor]], the designer of the MPO-1400.<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> It had thirteen sublight engines, making it the first starship ever to have that many, in part because Drabor considered 13 to be his lucky number.<ref name="Halcyon">{{SW|url=news/ships-of-the-galaxy-the-halcyon-starcruiser|text=Ships of the Galaxy: The ''Halcyon'' Starcruiser|int=Ships of the Galaxy}}</ref>
