The '''Creekpath Mining and Refining complex''' was a [[Mining|mine]] near [[Paeragosto City]] on the [[planet]] [[Batonn]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Around the [[time]] a vein of [[doonium]] was discovered in the [[Pryce Mine]] on [[Lothal]], the Batonn mine was looking to hire an assistant manager and an experienced [[foreman]]. After losing [[Pryce Mining]], [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]]'s [[Parent|father]] [[Talmoor Pryce]] worked as a foreman at the mine. Following the [[Battle of Scrim Island]], [[Nevil Cygni]]'s [[Batonn insurgents]] relocated to the Creekpath mining complex since it was protector by a [[deflector shield generator]]. [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Military|forces]] and their [[Batonn Defense Force]] allies subsequently laid [[Siege of the Creekpath Mining and Refining complex|siege]] to the facility. [[Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] tried to negotiate a peaceful end to the siege but was rebuffed by Nevil. The [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Gudry]] also planted explosives in the mine's explosive cache and deflector shield. Pryce later remotely blew up the mining complex in order to hide her role in Gudry's murder. The explosion wiped out the Batonn insurgents and numerous civilians.<ref name="Thrawn" />
