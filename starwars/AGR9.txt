'''AGR9'''<ref name="Harvest Subtitles">{{Andor|Harvest}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> was an agricultural [[droid]] on the<ref name="Harvest" /> [[planet]] [[Mina-Rau]]<ref name="General Store">{{Databank|mina-rau-general-store|Mina-Rau General Store}}</ref> in [[4 BBY]]. During that [[Standard year|year]], they attended at least one communal gathering between local [[farmer]]s and [[Technician|mechanics]] on Mina-Rau. They observed [[B2EMO]] playing with children at the gathering, at one point playfully warning B2EMO that a [[Unidentified AT-ST kid|kid]] was behind him.<ref name="Harvest" />

==Behind the scenes==
AGR9 appeared in the episode "[[Harvest]]" of the [[Disney+]] live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' which was released on [[April 22]], [[2025]]. They are only identified by the show's subtitles,<ref name="Harvest" /> and as several droids are shown in the scene, this article does not assume which droid is AGR9.
