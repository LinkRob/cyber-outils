'''Cosa Simo''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Umbaran]] [[Marriage|married]] to the Umbaran [[archivist]] [[Asala Suro]]. While tracing the lineage of her ancestors with the help of Suro, Simo discovered a [[Unidentified familial line|familial line]] dating back more than three [[Millennium|millennia]] before<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> [[132 BBY]]. In that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date"> [[Star Wars: The Acolyte|''The Acolyte'']]&mdash;and therefore "[[Revenge / Justice]]"&mdash;takes place in [[132 BBY]].</ref> Simo&mdash;accompanied by Suro&mdash;visited [[Olega City]] on [[Olega]], the [[planet]] to which she traced the line, in hopes of being allowed access to ancient records stored in the local [[Olega Jedi temple|temple]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> Soon after [[Jedi Master]] [[Torbin]] from the local temple was [[Skirmish on Olega|murdered]], Simo and Suro were in one of the [[city]]'s streets standing near the wall of a building.<ref name="RJ">{{Acolyte|Revenge / Justice}}</ref> Her [[eye]]s were [[Color|brown]] and&mdash;during her visit of Olega City&mdash;Simo wore a travel [[Shadowcloak (clothing)|shadowcloak]] and bundled [[clothing]] to protect her light-sensitive white skin.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cosa Simo first appeared, albeit unidentified, in "[[Revenge / Justice]],"<ref name="RJ" /> the second episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] series [[Star Wars: The Acolyte|''The Acolyte'']], which aired on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> The individual was later identified as Cosa Simo in ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and published in [[2025]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> The Olega scenes<ref name="Location">{{Lucasfilm|url=news/lucasfilm-on-location-the-acolyte|text=Lucasfilm on Location: ''The Acolyte''}}</ref> featuring Simo<ref name="RJ" /> were shot at [[Shinfield Studios]],<ref name="Location" /> [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="Julian Foddy">{{Lucasfilm|url=news/julian-foddy-acolyte|text=''The Acolyte'' Visual Effects Supervisor Julian Foddy}}</ref>
