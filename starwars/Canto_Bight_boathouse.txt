The '''Canto Bight boathouse''' was a structure in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the edge of the [[Sea of Cantonica]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Canto Bight" /> In around [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date_note">{{SW|url=news/4-reasons-canto-bight-is-an-essential-read|text=4 Reasons ''Canto Bight'' Is an Essential Read}} states that the [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']] anthology takes place [[Standard day|days]] before [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], which corresponds to the timeline of events for the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], as ''The Last Jedi'' begins immediately in the latter's aftermath. Given that ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], the events of ''Canto Bight'' must also take place in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the ''[[Undisputed Victor]]''&mdash;a [[Yacht (aquatic)|yacht]] owned by [[Baron]] [[Yasto Attsmun]]&mdash;moored at the boathouse to pick up guests attending a party onboard.<ref name="Canto Bight" />
