'''Docking Bay 42''' was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] on the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]] [[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|''Vigilance'']]. In [[4 ABY]], when [[New Republic pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]]' [[HH-87 Starhopper]]&mdash;the ''[[Rover]]''&mdash;was [[Tractor beam|towed]] into the bay, he opted to rig it to [[Mission to Akiva|explode]] and flee the scene. In the ensuing [[Fire|fiery]] aftermath, [[Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] instructed [[Lieutenant]] [[Nils Tothwin]] to search for Antilles. The ''Vigilance''&mdash;Docking Bay 42 included&mdash;was destroyed not long after in a [[Rebellion on Akiva|battle]] over the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].

==Description==
Docking Bay 42 was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] on the [[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|''Vigilance'']], a [[Star Destroyer]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The bay featured a [[glass]]-encased balcony and was large enough to fit a [[battalion]] of [[stormtrooper]]s and several [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> 

==History==
{{Quote|Could be a suicide attack. Maximize the damage&mdash;''"<br />"''No. He's here. And he can't be far. Find him.''"<br />"''Yes, Admiral. Right away.|Nils Tothwin and Rae Sloane, after Wedge Antilles' ship exploded in the bay|<ref name="Aftermath" /> }}
[[File:WoTG-RaeSloane-Stormtroopers.png|thumb|right|230px|Rae Sloane (pictured) supervised the troops in the docking bay alongside Nils Tothwin.]]
The ''Vigilance'' was in the Galactic Empire's service by [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Haven">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[battle at Fostar Haven]] to [[0 ABY]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]'' establishes that the [[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|''Vigilance'']] participated in the conflict, the [[Star Destroyer]] must have been in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s service by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Imperial [[admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] ordered a battalion of stormtroopers and several [[TIE series|TIE fighters]] to report to the bay when the ''Vigilance''{{'s}} [[tractor beam]] was [[Mission to Akiva|bringing]] the ''[[Rover]]'', a [[HH-87 Starhopper]] flown by the [[New Republic pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]], onto the [[Starship|Star Destroyer]]. She observed her [[Imperial Military|forces]] from Docking Bay 42's balcony with [[Lieutenant]] [[Nils Tothwin]], musing over the subpar quality of those under her [[Commanding officer|command]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

However, as the ''Rover'' drifted into Docking Bay 42, Antilles rigged his [[Starfighter|ship]] to explode while he fled the scene. As Sloane watched, two [[Color|black]] [[astromech droid]]s used foam to extinguish the [[fire]] caused by the incident, navigating around several stormtroopers' bodies. Furious at the resulting damage, the admiral ordered Tothwin to have her forces search the premises for the [[pilot]]. Sloane dismissed the latter's suggestion that Antilles may have been trying to avoid capture by [[Death|ending his own life]], leaving several stormtroopers' bodies in the bay as a reminder to others. Following the event, Sloane studied a [[hologram]] of the docking bay and eventually concluded that Antilles must have escaped through the area's ducts. Not long afterward, the ''Vigilance''&mdash;including Docking Bay 42&mdash;was destroyed in a [[Rebellion on Akiva|battle]] over the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Docking Bay 42 appeared in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
