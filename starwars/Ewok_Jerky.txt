'''Ewok Jerky''' was a dish made using the meat of [[Ewok]]s, a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Tree|forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]] who were [[Hunting|hunted]] by offworld raiders in order to make the dish. A popular snack on [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] worlds,<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know" /> it was served by the [[chef]] [[Borkus]] at [[Power Sliders]], a [[Restaurant|diner]] built in the settlement of [[Pons Ora]] on the [[planet]] [[Abafar]].<ref name="Missing in Action" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ewok Jerky was first mentioned in [[Aurebesh]] on a menu in "[[Missing in Action]]," the twelfth episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]].<ref name="Missing in Action" />
