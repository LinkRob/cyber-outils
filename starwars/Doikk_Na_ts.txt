'''Doikk Na'ts''' was a [[Bith]] [[musician]] who played the [[Dorenian Beshniquel]] as a regular member of the [[band]] [[Figrin D'an and the Modal Nodes]]. Na'ts was present when the band played at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="USW" />
