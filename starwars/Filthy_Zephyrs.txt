The '''Filthy Zephyrs''' was a gang of [[pirate]] [[Quarren]] who operated on the [[ocean]] [[planet]] of [[Manaan]]. At one point, [[Skoova Stev]], [[Wan'yek Whett]], and the rest of the [[Unidentified fishing crew|crew]] of the ''[[Grapnel]]'' encountered the gang after crashing on Manaan.

==History==
The Filthy Zephyrs had a hideout on the ocean floor of Manaan. After the ''[[Grapnel]]''{{'s}} crash-landing and subsequent sinking on the ocean planet, several of the Filthy Zephyrs surrounded the [[Unidentified fishing crew|crew]] and attempted to raid them, with their list of demands including the ''Grapnel''{{'s}} entire fish cargo, which contained the carcass of a [[gooberfish]]. The crew briefly escaped before being cornered in a dead end by four of the pirate Quarren, armed with [[blaster]]s. The Filthy Zephyrs failed to apprehend the crew however, as first mate [[Skoova Stev]] successfully intimidated the pirates with his deranged expression when attempting to imitate the mating call of a [[glumbrig eel]]. The pirates dropped their weapons and in the ensuing confusion, Captain [[Wan'yek Whett]], Stev, and the rest of the crew managed to evade the Filthy Zephyrs and steal their ship, fleeing Manaan.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Equipment==
At least four of the Filthy Zephyrs carried blasters. They also owned at least one starship, which was stolen by Wan'yek Whett and his fishing crew after crashing their previous ship.<ref name="Survivor" />
