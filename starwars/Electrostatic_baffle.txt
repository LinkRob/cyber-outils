'''Electrostatic baffles''' were a type of [[baffle]] that could be used in [[starship]] construction. The [[TUG-b13]] [[spacetug]] manufactured by [[Sublights Products Corporation]] had an electrostatic baffle in the intake of each of its four large [[engine]]s in order to keep dust out.<ref name="TFAICC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Electrostatic baffles first appeared in the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> They were not identified in the film, but received a name in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]] and released alongside the film.<ref name="TFAICC" />
