'''Chris Tietz''' is a [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney Imagineering|Walt Disney Imagineering]] employee who helped design the original [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] attraction. He came up with the idea of taking [[Wikipedia:Audio-Animatronics|Audio-Animatronic]] [[Wikipedia:Goose|geese]] from [[Disneyland]]'s now-defunct [[Wikipedia:America Sings|America Sings]] attraction to create the [[G2 repair droid/Legends|G2 repair droid]] for ''Star Tours''.<ref name="SWI 104">{{InsiderCite|104|Star Tours: Taking Off on a Star Tour}}</ref>
