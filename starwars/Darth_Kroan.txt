'''Darth Kroan''' was a [[Humanoid/Legends|humanoid]] [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]]<ref name="Game Informer" /> who was active during the reign of [[Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire]], between [[130 ABY]] and [[138 ABY]].<ref name="Dating note">{{KraytDates|bots=1}}</ref> Kroan had [[Color/Legends|yellow]] [[Eye/Legends|eyes]], gray skin, and a bald head.<ref name="Game Informer" />

Kroan wore a [[Gold/Legends|golden]] [[Breath mask|breathing apparatus]] over their [[Nose/Legends|nose]]. They were [[Clothing/Legends|clothed]] in a long black [[Cloak/Legends|cloak]] with golden accents and adornments lining the shoulders of it. The cloak had a high collar that stretched just above Kroan's head. The outfit was paired with multiple golden [[Necklace/Legends|necklaces]] that were worn around Kroan's neck.<ref name="Game Informer" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Kroan is a character created for [[Red Fly Studio]]'s [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Battle of the Sith Lords]]''. Kroan's design was created by concept artist [[Thomas A. Szakolczay]] at some point prior to the game's cancellation in [[2011]].<ref name="Game Informer" />
