"'''Falling for an Outlander'''" was a [[music]] track by the [[band]] [[Snoopio's Ghost]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it could be heard on various [[jukebox]]es on the [[moon]], [[Toshara]], including at [[Makal's Gambling Parlor]] in [[Mirogana]] and at [[Daruda Diner]] in [[Jaunta's Hope]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Falling for an Outlander" was created as an [[in-universe]] music track by [[Cody Matthew Johnson]] for the [[2024]] [[Ubisoft]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" /> It was released in the game's soundtrack ''[[Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)]]''.<ref name="Songs from the Underworld">{{OST|Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)}}</ref>
