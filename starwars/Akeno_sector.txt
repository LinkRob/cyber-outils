The '''Akeno sector''' was a [[sector]] of [[the galaxy]]. During a [[Catastrophe in the Seikara Caverns|mission]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dekien]], the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] disguised himself as an [[Musician|artiste]] while onboard a [[Eternity Enterprises barge|pleasure barge]]. The Ronin's companion, [[the Traveler]], claimed to a [[Unidentified woman wearing silk|woman]] on the [[Sail barge|barge]] that the Ronin was actually the Akeno sector's most sought after theatrical leading [[Gender|man]].<ref name="Ronin" />
