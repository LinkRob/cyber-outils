'''Field lasers''' were [[laser]]s that could dispel [[plasma]] [[fire]]. In [[40 BBY]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], used the lasers to assist a [[Unidentified soulcraft|soulcraft]] that had been [[Strike on a soulcraft|attacked]] by a [[plasma bomb]] above the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]. Though they began cutting through the plasma fire, a [[Meryx (starship)|starship]]&mdash;which they later learned was the ''Meryx''&mdash;fired an [[antiplasma charge]] and doused the flames.

==Description==
A type of [[Limb|handheld]] [[laser]], field lasers could dispel [[plasma]] [[fire]] caused by [[plasma bomb]]s. They emitted a thin, [[Color|white]] beam when activated.<ref name="MA" />

==History==
{{Quote|We're approaching the soulcraft, Master.''"<br />"''All right. Seal up.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Qui-Gon Jinn, preparing to use field lasers to dispel plasma fire|<ref name="MA" />}}
During the [[Republic Era]], field lasers were kept aboard a [[Unidentified Consular-class cruiser (Pijal)|''Consular''-class cruiser]] operated by the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="MA" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> the [[Consular-class light cruiser|cruiser]] was sent to [[transport]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], to the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]] to [[Mission to Pijal|mediate]] the signing of the upcoming [[Governance Treaty]] initiative.<ref name="MA" />

[[File:PijalCover.jpg|thumb|left|225px|Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi used field lasers to aid a soulcraft attacked by blackguards.]]
As they entered the [[Pijal system]] and approached Pijal, the cruiser encountered a struggling Pijali [[Unidentified soulcraft|soulcraft]] vessel that had been [[Strike on a soulcraft|attacked]] with a plasma [[bomb]] by [[Pijali]] [[blackguard]] [[soldier]]s, who were secretly operating under the orders of Pijal's [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] to derail the treaty's signing ahead of her [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]]. Jinn ordered the ship's [[Unidentified navigator|navigator]] to stop so they could assist, donning a [[Environment suit|space-suit]] alongside his Padawan and obtaining the cruiser's field lasers. Once floating in [[Realspace|space]], the [[Jedi]] pair cut through the sickly green haze of glowing plasma fire with their field lasers, sending small blobs of it floating away from them as the [[soulcraft]] started an emergency descent. As Jinn worked, he noticed a second plasma fire on the other side of the [[Starship|soulcraft]] and mentally bemoaned how useless his field laser would be to combat it.<ref name="MA" />

However, before the Jedi Master could move toward the second fire with his field laser, a [[Meryx (starship)|starship]] darted forward and fired an [[antiplasma charge]], dispelling the flames. Afterward, the struggling soulcraft was brought safely aboard the [[Branch-class vehicle|Czerka Corporation cruiser]] ''[[Leverage]]'' and delivered to Pijal. In the [[Pijali palace]], the Jedi were informed that the [[Opposition]]&mdash;a performance troupe known for [[Opposition protests|protesting]] the Governance Treaty&mdash;were seemingly responsible for the attack; in reality, the troupe was being used as a scapegoat for the [[Blackguard campaign|blackguards' attacks]] throughout the Pijal [[Star system|system]]. Later, Jinn and Kenobi learned that the [[Gozanti-class cruiser|starship]] that had fired the antiplasma charge&mdash;thus ending their work with the field lasers&mdash;had been the ''Meryx'', operated by [[Gemstone|jewel]] [[Thief|thieves]] [[Rahara Wick]] and [[Pax Maripher]].<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Field lasers appeared in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
