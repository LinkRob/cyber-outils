This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that was found within [[the galaxy]]. These bipedal [[humanoid]] sentients were distinguished by their [[Color|cream]]-colored skin, quadruple [[Limb|arms]], yellow [[hair]], and large, pointed [[ear]]s with pink interiors.<ref name="Music Mayhem" /> By sometime between [[232 BBY]] and [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|302A}}</ref> a member of this species named [[Anora]] was a [[Musician|drummer]] who played for the [[band]] the [[Star Strummers]], performing alongside fellow band the [[Ku-Bops]] on the [[planet]] [[Andraven]].<ref name="Music Mayhem" />
