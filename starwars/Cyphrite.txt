'''Cyphrite''' was a type of [[ore]] that could be found on the [[moon]] [[Okala Five]]. It could be used to created a powerful toxin when it was mixed with [[tolium]] [[gas]]. The [[Galactic Republic]] banned the use of cyphrite during the [[Clone Wars]], which resulted in the destruction of star maps leading to the ore's deposits, except for the one on Okala Five. It was said that the moon contained 2.8 trillion [[Metric ton|tons]] of pure cyphrite ore. In [[3 ABY]], a [[Kessel Sabacc]] tournament was held aboard the [[casino]] [[cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]'' with the grand prize being Okala Five's coordinates. [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Governor]] [[Thorden]] plotted to acquire the coordinates in order to use the toxin as weapon to maintain his own empire.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
