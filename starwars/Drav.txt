'''Drav'''<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> [[Gender|male]] [[stormtrooper]] who held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[sergeant]] in the [[1st Legion]], the personal [[legion]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Serving [[Kimmund's unit|closely]] alongside [[Commander]] [[Kimmund]]'s [[troop]]s, Sergeant Drav fought against the [[Grysk]]s at [[Batuu]] during the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />
