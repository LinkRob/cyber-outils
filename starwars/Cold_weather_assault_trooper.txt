The '''cold weather assault troopers''', or '''snowtroopers''' and referred to as "'''snowies'''" by the [[trooper]]s themselves, were a specialized [[stormtrooper]] variant used by the [[Galactic Empire]] equipped to operate in frigid environments. Snowtroopers who refused to [[Galactic Concordance|surrender]] to the [[New Republic]] and continued their service after the [[Galactic Civil War]] were further identified as '''Remnant Snowtroopers''', owing to their allegiance to [[Imperial Remnants]].

==History==
[[File:Snowtroopers-SWL2024update.png|thumb|left|300px|Snowtroopers were trained to use a variety of weapons.]]
The [[Galactic Empire]]'s cold weather assault-class of [[stormtrooper]] was a descendant of the [[clone cold assault trooper]]s, whom had fought in the [[Clone Wars]] in frigid environments such as [[Orto Plutonia]], [[Rhen Var]], and [[Toola]].<ref name="Ultimate SW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

Snowtroopers accompanied normal stormtroopers to investigate the wreckage of the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tribunal]]'',<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> in [[17 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> where their [[commanding officer]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], found the [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|lightsaber]] of his former [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Victory and Death" /> Later in [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> snowtroopers of the [[Imperial Garrison (Ilum)|Imperial garrison]] on [[Ilum]] [[Mission to Ilum|encountered]] the [[Jedi]] [[Cal Kestis]].<ref name="JFO" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Aphra2016Date">{{Aphra2016Date|5}}</ref> Snowtroopers serving under [[Captain]] [[Magna Tolvan]] pursued the rogue archeologist [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] at the [[Citadel of Rur]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 5">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 5|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 5]]</ref> At a later date, snowtroopers serving under [[Commodore]] [[Kurtruk]] [[Mission to Banas|fought]] alongside [[Range trooper]]s against [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] on [[Banas]].<ref>''[[Hyperspace Stories 2]]''</ref>

When the Empire located the Alliance to Restore the Republic's [[Echo Base|base]] on [[Hoth]] [[3 ABY|three years after]] the [[Battle of Yavin]], the elite [[Blizzard Force]], led by [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]], were involved in the [[Battle of Hoth]]. The battle became a major Imperial victory and a major blow to the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> Shortly later, at least one snowtrooper took part in the [[invasion of Ison]].<ref name="Legends Alliance">''[[Legends of the Alliance]]''</ref>

After the Empire fell, snowtroopers who refused to surrender and fled from [[New Republic]] authority to continue their service under any of the galaxy's various [[Imperial Remnants]] were identified as Remnant Snowtroopers.<ref name="RemnantSnowtrooper">{{HotToysCite|set=Movie Masterpiece Series|packtype=Sixth Scale Figure|pack=#838: Imperial Remnant Snowtrooper|link=collectibles/star-wars-imperial-remnant-snowtrooper-hot-toys-915297}}</ref> Many served under an [[Barro's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] led by [[Commander]] [[Barro]] on [[Unidentified ice planet (Barro)|an ice planet]].<ref name="The Mandalorian and Grogu" /> Most of these troopers were killed by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] during his [[Attack on Barro's Imperial remnant|attack]].<ref name="The Mandalorian and Grogu" />

==Equipment==
[[File:Snowtrooper-helmet-SWCT.png|thumb|right|200px|A snowtrooper helmet]]
[[Snowtrooper armor]] was well insulated to protect against extreme cold, with a suit and heated breather mask powered by a battery pack that could last up to two [[standard week]]s. For greater survivability, it also included ice boots, an insulated belt [[kama]],<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> polarized snow goggles, grappling hooks, ion flares, and a homing beacon. Snowtroopers were often equipped with [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]]s and [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]]s.<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> Some could be trained to handle either [[flamethrower]]s or [[T-7 ion disruptor rifle]]s.<ref name="Snowtroopers Unit Expansion">{{Legion|set=Snowtroopers Unit Expansion}}</ref> [[Rank insignia plaque]]s were placed within snowtrooper armor to designate their commanding [[officer]]s, as was the case for<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> Captain [[TS-4068]].<ref name="Duty">{{StoryCite|story=The Truest Duty|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> Snowtrooper [[commander]]s and [[lieutenant]]s were known to not wear helmets despite operating in cold environments.<ref name="Star Wars: Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>

Specialized types of snowtroopers included the [[Heavy Snowtrooper]] and the [[Arctic Jumptrooper]]s, both variations of the [[Heavy Weapons Stormtrooper]]s and [[Jumptrooper]]s,<ref name="Commander" /> and the [[Snowtrooper Ops]] or Scouts, which specialized on stealth and reconnaissance and [[Scout|scouting]] missions.<ref name="GoH" /> A similar [[Hoth Scout Trooper|cold weather variant]] of [[scout trooper]]s also existed.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref>

Many of the snowtroopers serving Barro's Imperial remnant wore helmets with the white cloth hoods removed, though some did have them, and wielded E-11 [[blaster rifle]]s and [[DLT-20A blaster rifle]]s with a [[glowrod]] attachment.<ref name="The Mandalorian and Grogu" />
