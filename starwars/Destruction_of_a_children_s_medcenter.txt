By [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a [[Unidentified children's medcenter|children's medcenter]] on the astronomical object [[Novor]] was '''destroyed''' by the [[Hutt Clan]] [[Enforcer (occupation)|enforcer]] [[Ola Hest]] after the [[Sisters of the Ninth Plane]] who ran the [[medcenter]] refused to pay Hest protection [[Currency|money]] she was sent to collect. After the Sisters told Hest to leave, she aimed her [[Ola Hest's frigate|frigate's]] [[Engine|thrusters]] at the building, bringing it down and [[Death|killing]] all inside, later claiming it had been an accident. Hest was eventually [[Capture of Ola Hest|captured]]<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> by [[Jedi Master]] [[Porter Engle]] on<ref name="Characters of">{{SWYouTube|6_701eyzskI|Jedi Master Porter Engle: Characters of Star Wars the High Republic|sw_url=video/jedi-master-porter-engle-characters-of-star-wars-the-high-republic}}</ref> the [[Hutt Space]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Nal Hutta]]<ref name="Characters of" /> and [[Prison|imprisoned]] on the [[Galactic Republic]] [[prison ship]] [[Lourna Dee (prison ship)|''Restitution'']].<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />

In 231 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> ''Restitution'' [[prisoner]] [[Sestin Blin]] told [[Lourna Dee]], a [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] also imprisoned there, about Hest's destruction of the [[Youngling|children's]] medcenter. Blin believed it had not been an accident, stating Hest knew what she was doing and wanted people to know. After Blin suggested not antagonizing Hest, Dee questioned if it was because she would drop a building on her, noting people had "tried worse."<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />
