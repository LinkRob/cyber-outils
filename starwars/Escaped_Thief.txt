The '''Escaped Thief''' was a set of [[clothing]] and [[armor]] owned by the [[Kubaz]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Glikkin]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Master Thief (Glikkin)|standard costume]].<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Escaped Thief was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Glikkin, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 5.0.0 alongside Season 5 on [[January 27]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Lockpick Blades]].<ref name="Hunters" />
