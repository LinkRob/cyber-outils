'''BNZ-83''' was an [[RA-7 protocol droid]]. During the [[Cold War]], [[Han Solo]] conducted a test on the [[planet]] [[Azen]] to determine who would join [[Han Solo's shipping company|his crew]] before a [[Mission to Twon Ketee|mission]] to capture [[Rathtar]]s for king [[Grevoth Prana IX]], which involved recovering a [[hyperdrive]] from the top of a mountain. BNZ-83 tripped and fell into rocks, failing the test.<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
BNZ-83 appeared in "[[The Solo Test]]," a comic written by [[Martin Fisher]] and featured in the magazine [[LEGO Star Wars 32|''LEGO Star Wars'' 32]].<ref name="LEGO" />
