The '''''Broken Horn''''' was a modified [[C-ROC Gozanti-class light cruiser|C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class light cruiser]] and the personal [[transport]] of [[Devaronian]] criminal [[Cikatro Vizago]], leader of the [[Broken Horn Syndicate]]. 

== Description ==
The ''Broken Horn'' was a modified [[C-ROC Gozanti-class light cruiser|C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class light cruiser]]. It measured 73.9 [[meter]]s in length with vast cargo holds for [[Smuggler|smuggling]] goods. Although its weapon systems were poor, Vizago relied on its speed and [[Deflector shield|deflector shields]] to make a clean escape.<ref name="Databank" /> It was piloted by Vizago's [[IG-RM bodyguard and enforcer droid|IG-RM bodyguard and enforcer droids]].<ref name=":0">{{InsiderCite|158|Rebel Bluff}}</ref>

== History ==
In [[4 BBY]], Vizago and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] used the ship to leave [[Lothal]] after the [[Skirmish in Tarkintown]] between the townsfolk and the [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]].<ref name=":0" />
