'''G1-G1''', also known as '''Gee-One-Gee-One''', was a [[garden droid]] [[astromech droid|astromech]] who served the [[Barsha family]] at their [[Barsha compound|compound]] on the [[moon]] [[Gus Treta]] under the supervision of the droid [[A1-A1]]. At the early [[Imperial Era]], G1-G1 was many decades old and considered an older model. It and had arms sticking out of it's dome and carried various gardening tools.<ref name="LRM" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> G1-G1 was temporarily repurposed to serve as a [[service droid|server]] for guests at [[Gus Treta incident|a party]] held by [[Roisem Barsha]] at the [[Barsha compound]] on [[Gus Treta]] after the [[Barsha family]]'s pardon. At some point during the party, it was serving guests refreshments on a tray in the surrounding gardens, when it noticed the body of the guest [[Zeev Oversam]] which he approached to check if he wanted a refill of his drink. When the astromech noticed that Oversam had been killed, it tried notify security at a nearby terminal, but noticed Oversam's murderer, the [[BX-series droid commando]] [[ND-5]], who had been sent to kill everyone present. Nine seconds after it approached him, the commando droid punched a deep hole through G1-G1's chest and twisted out one of it's legs, leading to it being immobilized and only speaking random distorted sounds. Thanks to it's [[maintenance beacon]], Oversam and G1-G1 were soon after found by [[Jaylen Vrax|Jaylen Barsha]] and A1-A1 who investigated the deaths. A1-A1 used an interface cable to access the droid's data. Although G1-G1 was unable to identify ND-5's model and had no functioning audio or visual log it could describe the commando droid's height and jacket, which was enough for Barsha to recognize him.<ref name="LRM" />
