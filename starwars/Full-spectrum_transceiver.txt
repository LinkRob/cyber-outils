A '''Full-Spectrum Transceiver''' ('''FST''') was a type of [[sensor]] device which used a variety of scanners to detect all types of objects, energies and fields, giving them then name "universal sensors." However their accuracy at detecting objects, particularly at long range, was proportional to the size of the FST's receptor dish.<ref name="YT1300" /> FSTs were, among others, equipped in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[IGV-55 surveillance vessel]]s as part of their main sensor package.<ref name="DRVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> The [[YT-1300 light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was illegally equipped with an military-grade universal sensor suite fitting for a much larger ship.<ref name="SWMF54">{{FalconCite|54|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref>
