The '''Belted In trilogy''' was a trilogy of adventures released by the [[RPGA]] on behalf of [[Wizards of the Coast]] in [[2003]], for the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign. The trilogy was set in the [[Cularin asteroid belt]] and centered on the [[Blockade of the Cularin system]] by the [[Thaereian military]] and the discovery that the [[Oblee]] species was actually still in existence as "shadow lurkers."
