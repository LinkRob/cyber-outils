An '''emitter matrix''' was a device that could be added to a [[lightsaber]]. It improved use against [[droid]]s.<ref name="ROTS" /> Inverting the emitter matrix would cause the lightsaber to backfire and explode, potentially killing anybody in the vicinity.<ref name="A Test of Strength">{{TCW|A Test of Strength}}</ref>
In [[20 BBY]]<ref name="Date note">{{TCWcanondates|506}}</ref> [[Mark IV architect droid|Mark IV]] [[architect droid|architect]] [[droid]] [[Professor]] [[Huyang]] informed [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Initiate|youngling]] [[Petro]] that the only person he would [[Death|kill]] with [[Petro's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] was himself as he rushed through the construction of his lightsaber without following the diagram and inverted the emitter matrix. As a result, Huyang instructed the youngling to restart the building process of his lightsaber.<ref name="A Test of Strength" />
