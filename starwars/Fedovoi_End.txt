'''Fedovoi End''', or '''Fedovoi''', was a [[planet]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]] and [[Trailing Sectors]] occupied by the [[Yomo Council]], a breakaway faction from the [[Galactic Empire]] following its defeat at the [[Battle of Endor]]. While primarily a military outpost, Fedovoi End was home to half a million [[troop]]s and their families. As the Yomo Council committed actions that the Imperial loyalists saw as [[treason]]ous, including refusing to follow the orders of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]], the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] was sent to [[Destruction of Fedovoi End|destroy]] Fedovoi End in a renewed [[Operation: Cinder]] campaign.

After [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]] announced the unit's intentions to destroy Fedovoi End and eliminate the leaders of the Yomo Council, inviting any other former Imperials to abandon the planet and join them, the 204th shattered areas of Fedovoi End's polar [[Water|ice]] caps, opening up [[gas]] pockets and slowly [[poison]]ing the planet's [[atmosphere]]. After the [[New Republic]] became aware of Fedovoi End's destruction, [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] intended to reroute [[starship]]s she had left in the [[Nythlide Array]] to look for survivors on the planet.

==Description==
{{Quote|Send the order to move in. The escorts will take position near the moons. Commander Broosh will lead the TIEs into the atmosphere.|Soran Keize, to the bridge crew of the ''Yadeez''|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
A terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in the [[Fedovoi End system]] of the [[Croynar sector]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Galaxy" /> and [[Trailing Sectors]],<ref name="Trailing Sectors" /> Fedovoi End,<ref name="Victory's Price" /> or simply Fedovoi, was situated at the coordinates M-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Galaxy" /> It appeared [[Color|brown]] and green from [[Realspace|space]] and was [[orbit]]ed by three rose-colored [[moon]]s. The planet was situated in the same region as the planets [[Dybbron III]] and [[Kortatka]], and together the three worlds formed a near-straight line of travel. Fedovoi End possessed an [[atmosphere]] and polar [[Water|ice]] caps that held pockets of [[Poison|toxic]] [[gas]]es beneath the ice sheets.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==History==
===Remnant of the Empire===
{{Quote|This is Colonel Soran Keize of the 204th Imperial Fighter Wing and the carrier ''Yadeez''. In response to the Yomo Council's treasonous actions&mdash;its defiance of Grand Admiral Sloane's order to direct assets to the D'Aelgoth sector, its refusal to acknowledge the Empire's rightful regent on Coruscant, and its alliance with the Shiortuun Syndicate, among others&mdash;we have been sent to bring retribution to your world.|Soran Keize|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
[[File:Shattered_Empire_1_textless_cover.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Battle of Endor (pictured) was such a loss to the Galactic Empire the regime began to fracture, leading to rebellious factions like the Yomo Council of Fedovoi End.]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], Fedovoi End was under the control of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Following the Empire's defeat at the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> a number of factions broke away from the Imperial forces. One such faction, the [[Yomo Council]], whose leaders included [[Governor]] [[Brashan]] and [[General]] [[Tuluh]], took control of Fedovoi End. The Yomo Council refused to cooperate with the Imperial loyalists, defying the orders of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> as a result of the Yomo Council's actions, seen as [[treason]] by the loyalists, Fedovoi End became a target in a renewed [[Operation: Cinder]]&mdash;a campaign to destroy numerous worlds first undertaken by the Empire after the Battle of Endor&mdash;with the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], led by [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]], sent to [[Destruction of Fedovoi End|destroy]] the planet and eliminate the Yomo Council. The 204th's [[bulk freighter]] ''[[Yadeez]]'' and its escorts positioned above Fedovoi End, defeating the heavy firepower that the Yomo Council sent toward them. Keize informed the Yomo Council of the planet's imminent destruction and urged any inhabitants not loyal to their superiors to abandon Fedovoi End. The Yomo Council did not reply to Keize's message, instead powering their defenses' [[ion cannon]]s.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

===Death of a world===
{{Quote|We haven't received word on the status of Fedovoi End, but we can only assume Shadow Wing has come and gone. At last count, Fedovoi End housed half a million troops and their families. It was primarily a military outpost, it's true&mdash;but we haven't seen slaughter of this sort since Operation Cinder.''"<br />"''The Empire is eating its own.''"<br />"''Yes. The loyalists have gone to war with the breakaway factions&mdash;civilians caught in the cross fire be damned.|Hera Syndulla and [[New Republic pilot]] [[Chass na Chadic]]|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
[[File:Disintegrating-Imperial-Crest-RISEANDFALL.png|thumb|right|300px|The fractured Galactic Empire was at war with itself in 5 ABY, with the Yomo Council and loyalist Imperials clashing at Fedovoi End.]]
The 204th's [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], led by [[Commander]] [[Teso Broosh]], moved into Fedovoi End's atmosphere. They were originally directed toward Fedovoi End's [[Fedovoi End capital|capital]] to eliminate the planetary defenses, but under a suggestion from [[Lieutenant]] [[Yrica Quell]]&mdash;a former 204th [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] who had returned from [[New Republic]] service with the intent of assisting in the destruction of the [[fighter wing]]&mdash;Keize instead ordered the [[starfighter]]s to take the riskier approach of heading directly for the planet's polar regions, where they bombed the ice caps, releasing the gases that had been held within. As the [[TIE series|TIEs]] continued to bombard the ice caps, the atmosphere of Fedovoi End was gradually poisoned and its population eventually [[Death|succumbed]] to the toxic gases.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

A number of the planet's inhabitants, including [[Major]] [[Njock]], escaped its destruction by accepting Keize's offer and joining the Imperial loyalists. Three [[Standard day|days]] after Fedovoi End's devastation, the [[New Republic]] discovered a recording of Keize's message, learning of the planet's fate. New Republic [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] intended to reroute [[starship]]s that she had left in the [[Nythlide Array]] under Major [[Jaun]] to look for survivors at Fedovoi End.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|To the rest of the inhabitants of this world, I offer a choice. Once, you were Imperials&mdash;not in name alone, as now, but in heart and hierarchy. You can be so again, but only if you accept the demise of your disloyal superiors and the planet you now occupy.|Soran Keize|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
Though Fedovoi End was primarily an Imperial military outpost, it was home to over half a million [[troop]]s and their families. Prior to destroying the planet, Keize believed that some of the former Imperials on Fedovoi End would be willing to abandon the planet, but that others would be incapable of doing so or refuse to leave Fedovoi End or join the Imperial loyalists for various reasons. Ultimately, some of the inhabitants did abandon the Yomo Council, but the remainder of the population fell victim to the planet's destruction.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|The TIEs are going straight to the capital?''"<br />"''Simplest to eliminate the planetary defenses before turning to the real work.|Yrica Quell and Soran Keize|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
Fedovoi End had a [[capital]] that was armed with planetary defenses, as well as military bases and other population centers. However, the planet's population was small enough that its military bases were few in number.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
