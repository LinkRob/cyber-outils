{{Quote|These fancy meringue drops were designed to resemble the clouds of Tibanna gas that drift gently through the planet Bespin's atmosphere, and you ain't gonna believe the storm front of flavor in each whimsical wisp.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
'''Bespin Cloud Drops''' were an opulent, [[Color|pink]]-and-blue [[Foodstuff|meringue]] that was designed to look like the [[Tibanna]] [[gas]] clouds that could be found in the [[atmosphere]] of the [[planet]] [[Bespin]]. When eaten, they melted in the consumer's [[mouth]]. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for Bespin Cloud Drops in his first [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'',<ref name="Cookbook" /> published in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs described the meringues as extremely flavorsome and claimed that he could eat the dainty treats by the [[Limb|handful]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bespin Cloud Drops were created as a recipe for the [[2019]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the [[Wikipedia:Meringue|meringues]] calls for [[Wikipedia:Egg white|egg whites]], [[Wikipedia:Potassium bitartrate|cream of tartar]], [[Wikipedia:Sucrose|sugar]], dessert pear [[Wikipedia:Syrup|syrup]] or [[Wikipedia:Grenadine|grenadine]], and light-blue [[Wikipedia:Food coloring|food coloring]].<ref name="Cookbook" />
