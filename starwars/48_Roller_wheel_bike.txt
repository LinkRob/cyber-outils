The '''48 Roller wheel bike''' was a model of personal wheel bike manufactured by [[GallisTech]]. The bike was consisting of a single, large wheel piloted from within. [[GallisTech]] decided to power its wheel bike with a pair of jet engines. This gave it an impressive speed but maked it even harder to control.

Skilled operators can steer it by simply leaning it the direction they want to turn. Unskilled drivers, on the other hand, quickly found themselves smeared across the landscape. The 48 Roller Wheel Bike was often found on frontier planets on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], where, it was most popular among speed-obsessed youth.<ref name="Starships and Speeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref>
