The '''Cliffside Fortress''' was a [[fortress]] on the [[Strangled Cliffs]] on the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]]. It was used by the [[Nightbrothers]] and was situated close to their [[Unidentified Nightbrother village|village]]. It was located near the [[Swamp of Sacrifice]] and utilized as a protective outpost.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
