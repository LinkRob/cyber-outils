A pair of '''crossguard lightsabers''' were once owned by [[Darth Atrius]] during a [[time]] when the [[Sith]] were plentiful and powerful. Sometime after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Lightsaber|sabers]] were recovered by the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] and she sold one of them to a [[Unidentified crime boss (Hradreek)|crime lord]] on [[Hradreek]], and the other to the [[Galactic Empire]]. Both were later destroyed by [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in [[0 ABY]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==History==
===Serving Atrius===
{{Quote|The lightsaber of Darth Atrius. A powerful weapon. So much rage was channeled through this blade, they say it still resonates with the anger Atrius wielded when he cut down his enemies.|A crime lord, speaking about one of the lightsabers|<ref name="Star Wars Annual 4" />}}
[[Millennium|Millennia]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], during the [[time]] the [[Sith]] were plentiful and powerful, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] [[Darth Atrius|Atrius]] constructed a pair of [[crossguard lightsaber]]s. Atrius used the [[Lightsaber|sabers]] to strike down many opponents and claim many victories. Over time Atrius' rage infected the saber, causing anyone who wielded it to fall into a frenzy. Upon Atrius' [[death]] the sabers were lost.<ref name="Star Wars Annual 4" />

===Rediscovery and destruction===
{{Quote|N-no. No&hellip;I'm not going to let you&hellip;overwhelm me.|Luke Skywalker, shortly before he destroys one of the sabers|<ref name="Star Wars Annual 4" />}}
[[File:DarthAtriusLightsabers-2015StarWarsAnnual4.png|thumb|left|250px|Though both crossguard in design, the sabers differed in appearance.]]
A [[0 ABY|short]] time after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Galactic Empire]] hired [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] to retrieve the lightsabers. [[Gender|She]] tracked down the sabers and retrieved them, however she had also been been hired by a [[Unidentified crime boss (Hradreek)|crime boss]] on [[Hradreek]] to fulfill the same task. Attempting to deceive the groups she gave one saber to each employer, hoping they wouldn't take notice of the missing second saber. First she gave one to the Empire and they were fooled by her trick, she then gave the second saber to the [[Crime lord|crime boss]] but was called out on her deceit.<ref name="Star Wars Annual 4" />

The crime boss ignited the blade and began questioning the smuggler on the location of the other saber, he fell into a frenzy and killed all of his men. At the same time an [[Imperial officer]] presented the saber to [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who planned to hand over the sabers to his [[Sith Master|Master]], [[Darth Sidious]]. When Vader learnt of the missing saber he [[Force choke|executed]] the [[officer]] and set off to recover the other. Meanwhile the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[pilot]] [[Luke Skywalker]] entered the room in which the crime boss had slaughtered his crew and found the lightsaber on the floor, he used the weapon to slaughter a squad of Imperial [[Stormtrooper]]s who discovered him with the corpses.<ref name="Star Wars Annual 4" />

[[File:LukeSlaughtersStormtroopers-2015StarWarsAnnual4.png|thumb|right|300px|Luke using one of Darth Atrius' lightsabers]]
After using it, Luke was horrified on the amount of rage he showed and deactivated the lightsaber. To avoid more Imperial forces, Skywalker stole a nearby podracer and threatened a podracer with it. Although Luke crashed it shortly after evading the Empire, Skywalker decided that the lightsaber was too dangerous to be kept in one piece, and used [[Skywalker lightsaber|his lightsaber]] to destroy Atrius' blade. Meanwhile, Vader ran into a group of thugs who Starros had hired to keep the Imperials off her back, he utilized the weapon [[Jar'Kai|along]] with [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|his own lightsaber]] to kill all of the thugs. He proceeded to track Starros to a warehouse where she had set an ambush for him, Starros detonated the roof of the warehouse, crushing Vader and allowing her to escape. When Vader escaped the rubble he observed Skywalker leaving. Deciding the saber was too unpredictable Vader crushed the weapon.<ref name="Star Wars Annual 4" />
