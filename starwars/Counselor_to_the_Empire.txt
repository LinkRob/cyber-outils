{{Quote|As I am the adviser to this group and to the Empire at large, I shall take for myself the title of Counselor to the Empire, serving as an interstitial leader only until Grand Admiral Sloane returns to us.|Gallius Rax, to the Shadow Council|<ref name="Life Debt" />}}
The '''Counselor to the Empire''', also known simply as the '''Counselor''', was an [[advisor]]y title held by [[Gallius Rax]] during the last [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Officially, the Counselor to the Empire was only a purportedly temporary role that advised the [[Shadow Council]] but, in effect, ruled as [[Galactic Emperor]] in all but name. Having ousted [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] as ''de facto'' leader of the [[Imperial Navy]] and reduced [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] to a figurehead, [[Fleet Admiral]] Rax effectively seized control of the Galactic Empire.

By his will, Rax rallied the remaining [[Imperial Military|military]] under his banner and brought them to his [[homeworld]] of [[Jakku]] as the [[Galactic Civil War]] drew to an end. Unknown to the [[soldier]]s under Rax's command, he conspired to destroy the Empire in accordance with the [[Contingency]]&mdash;Emperor [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] final order condemning the Empire, which he held responsible for his [[death]]. After the Counselor was killed in the [[Battle of Jakku]], Amedda regained the [[Politics|political]] authority that he needed in order to [[Imperial Instruments of Surrender|surrender]] the Empire to the [[New Republic]] and [[Galactic Concordance|end the war]].

==Powers==
{{Quote|You are little more than a shadow&mdash;and yet you lead the Empire.|Rae Sloane, to Gallius Rax|<ref name="Life Debt" />}}
[[File:ShadowCouncil-BattleOfJakkuRepublicUnderSiege2.png|thumb|left|250px|The Counselor to the Empire was the ''de facto'' leader of Imperial forces during the last days of the Galactic Empire.]]
The loyalist portions<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> of the [[Galactic Empire]] were effectively ruled by the Counselor to the Empire during the final [[Standard month|months]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. As the Empire's ''de facto'' leader, the Counselor superseded the [[Grand Vizier]]&mdash;the second-highest [[Politics|political]] office in the Imperial state&mdash;in power if not in rank. Though the Counselor was officially the equivalent of an [[advisor]], in reality it filled the vacuum of power that ensued after [[Darth Sidious]]' [[death]], making the Counselor a [[Galactic Emperor]] in all but name.<ref name="Life Debt" /> The Counselor was, therefore, the leader of the [[Shadow Council]], rather than its advisor, due to [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]]'s personal authority and influence as the founder of that organization.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==History==
{{Quote|It's come to this, then? Death on a dead world. You've driven us all to the edge of the galaxy. To the edge of everything.''"<br />"''As I say: There is a purpose.|Rae Sloane and Gallius Rax|<ref name="Empire's End" />}}
[[File:GalliusRax-BattleOfJakkuInsurgencyRising2.png|thumb|right|250px|In the aftermath of Emperor Palpatine's death, the shadowy Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax seized power as the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Empire.]]
In the wake of [[Darth]] Sidious' demise at the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Grand [[Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] was positioned to succeed the late Emperor as the next sovereign of [[the galaxy]]. However, beyond his influence over the [[Core Worlds|Core World]] [[Coruscant]], Amedda lacked the ability to unite the [[Galaxy|Galactic]] Empire under his rule. With the [[Galactic Civil War]] shifting in the [[New Republic]]'s favor, [[Imperial officer]]s such as [[Gallius Rax]] took matters into their own hands, seizing political and [[Imperial Military|military]] power at Amedda's expense. Wielding a degree of authority that exceeded his [[Military rank|rank]] in the [[Imperial Navy]], [[Fleet]] [[Admiral]] Rax founded the Shadow Council as part of his vision of a reformed Empire that was, in his view, worthy of ruling the galaxy.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Though he also promoted [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] as a figurehead, initially planning to rule through her as the power behind the [[throne]], Rax ultimately sidelined Sloane and created the position of Counselor to the Empire for himself. It was an unprecedented title that did not command the full confidence of the Council; [[General]] [[Hodnar Borrum]] even protested its creation, believing that the title of Counselor was too vague and essentially rendered the Empire leaderless. Although Rax claimed it was only a temporary solution to the Empire's dilemma,<ref name="Life Debt" /> his new role enabled him to wield absolute power not unlike what the Emperor possessed throughout his [[Imperial Era|reign]]. With Amedda a virtual [[prisoner]] in the [[Imperial Palace]], [[torture]]d and reduced to a figurehead by [[soldier]]s loyal to Rax, the Counselor had become the Emperor in effect. To signify his new status in the Empire, Rax began wearing a [[Color|white]] [[Navy|naval]] [[Imperial military uniforms|uniform]] with a red [[cape]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

[[File:GalliusRaxDeath-BattleOfJakkuLastStand4.png|thumb|left|250px|Counselor Rax was killed during the Battle of Jakku by Grand Admiral Rae Sloane, who subsequently joined the Imperial exodus to the Unknown Regions.]]
By [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Imperial forces rallied to the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Jakku]], Counselor Rax's [[homeworld]]. Though desperate and hardened by Jakku's harsh conditions, the Imperial [[troop]]s were unaware that their leader secretly followed the Emperor's [[Contingency|posthumous orders]] instructing him to destroy the Empire as a failed state. As a result, Rax brought his [[army]] to Jakku not to turn defeat into a triumph for the Empire, but rather to ensure that both the Empire and Republic perished in the final battle of the war. At the same [[time]], the Counselor sought to forge a new Empire, ruled by himself in the [[Unknown Regions]] of the galaxy.<ref name="Empire's End" />

During the [[Battle of Jakku]], Rax's strategy went according to plan until he was confronted by his rival Sloane, who killed him in single combat. Before dying, he implored the Grand Admiral to take his place and carry out the Emperor's [[Contingency|final wish]] by creating a [[First Order|stronger Empire]] in the Unknown Regions. With the Counselor's death and the destruction of his [[flagship]], the Republic [[New Republic Defense Force|military forces]] claimed victory on Jakku. As a result, the Imperial government officially [[Galactic Concordance|capitulated]] the war under the direction of Amedda, who returned to power for the sole purpose of [[Imperial Instruments of Surrender|surrendering the entire Empire]] to the Republic.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Residence==
{{Quote|Little Rae Sloane, manning an entire Super Star Destroyer all by herself. Our last, isn't it?|[[Valco Pandion]], to Rae Sloane|<ref name="Aftermath" />}}
[[File:Ravager_Executor-class_SWA.png|thumb|right|250px|Counselor Rax's flagship, the ''Ravager'', was one of the last Super Star Destroyers in the Imperial Navy.]]
Unlike his predecessors Darth Sidious and Mas Amedda, both of whom maintained their government's seat of power on the galactic [[capital]] world Coruscant,<ref name="Aftermath" /> Gallius Rax oversaw the Empire from a [[Starship|mobile]] command station. As a Fleet Admiral, he served as the [[commanding officer]] of the ''[[Ravager]]'',<ref name="Empire's End" /> a massive and rare [[warship]] of the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] line. As its highest-ranking [[officer]], Rax had a private upper garden installed within the ''Ravager'' for recreational purposes.<ref name="Life Debt" /> Rax retained control of the [[dreadnought]]&mdash;also known as a [[Super Star Destroyer]]&mdash;during his reign as Counselor to the Empire, using it as his [[command ship]] in a fleet of [[Star Destroyer]]s orbiting Jakku. During the final battle, the ''Ravager'' crashed into the surface of Jakku.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Behind the scenes==
The title of Counselor to the Empire first appeared in the novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''<ref name="Life Debt" /> and returned in its sequel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''.<ref name="Empire's End" /> In these novels, Counselor to the Empire is a title created in the wake of Rae Sloane's separation from the Galactic Empire and, according to General Hodnar Borrum, marks the first use of "Counselor" as an Imperial title,<ref name="Life Debt" /> with Sloane only learning of Gallius Rax's use of the title "Counselor" during the events of ''Empire's End''.<ref name="Empire's End" />

The comic [[Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege 2|''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 2]] incorrectly features Rax using the title of counselor in Sloane's presence, with Sloane herself calling him "Counselor Rax."<ref name="Under Siege 2">[[Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege 2|''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 2]]</ref>
