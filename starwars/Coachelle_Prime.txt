'''Coachelle Prime''',<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> also known simply as '''Coachelle''', was a<ref name="Galaxy Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="PoV">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> located in the [[Terr'skiar sector]]'s [[Coachelle system]] within the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was represented by the [[Lepi]] [[racer]] [[Thrar]] in the [[Forty-eighth annual Dragonfire Air Rally|forty-eighth]] annual [[Dragonfire Air Rally]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> The Lepi [[smuggler]] [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] was also from Coachelle Prime, and titled himself as the "Haulage King of Coachelle Prime" when attempting to start his own business, the [[Tumperakki Haulage Company]].<ref name="FACPOV ESB">{{StoryCite|story=Fake It Till You Make It|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>
