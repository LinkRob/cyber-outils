"'''Coda'''" is a mini-comic that serves as an epilogue to [[Darth Vader (2015) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 25]], and was written by [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2015)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']] writer [[Kieron Gillen]] with artist [[Max Fiumara]]. The events of ''Coda'' overlap and continue off of [[Darth Vader (2015) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 1]].

==Plot summary==
During [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] visit to [[Tatooine]] following the [[Assault on Cymoon]], the [[Sith Lord]] slaughters an entire village of [[Tusken Raider]]s. A survivor manages to hide in the dunes until nightfall. He builds a fire out of fallen branches.

Later that night, he is discovered by a different clan of Tusken Raiders who ride [[bantha]]s. He shows the clan his village and later recounts his story to the Tusken clan's [[shaman]]. Later, the shaman recounts how the other Tusken village was massacred by a [[lightsaber]] wielding warrior who drew his powers from one of Tatooine's [[Star|suns]]. He describes the attacked as a black armored warrior wielding a [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|red lightsaber]].

His story frightens the other Tusken clan, who bury the remains of the fallen villages. They then gather wood and debris to build a giant "[[Wikipedia:Wicker man|wicker man]]" in the likeness of Darth Vader. The unfortunate survivor is tied to the "wicker man" which is set alight while the other Tuskens worship the Darth Vader "wicker man."
