For me that's what Star Wars is all about, and to be in that environment knowing that there are a certain few, if not everyone, who get it, who still get those goose bumps from time to time has to be the best feeling in the world.|Dee Tails, on ''[[Star Wars]]''|<ref name="Star Wars Interviews" />}}
'''Dee Tails''' is a [[Wikipedia:United Kingdom|British]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] who worked as a creature and droid performer for [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'' Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], and portrayed [[Cratinus]] in the film. He also portrayed [[K-OHN]] in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', and [[Slowen Lo]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] alongside his puppeteer work. In [[2018]], he portrayed [[Quay Tolsite]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.

==Biography==
{{Quote|I love Cratinus and K-OHN it's hard to say which I love more, but if it wasn't for my performance as Cratinus I doubt if I would've even have been seen for the L-1 droid let alone have Neal vouch for me. I have to say I love them both, these two Star Wars films have been beyond my dreams coming true and I have to take them as the individual moments they are and truly be grateful.|Dee Tails|<ref name="Star Wars Interviews" />}}
[[File:Cratinus_CT.png|thumb|right|200px|Dee Tails as Cratinus]]
Dee Tails was born on [[December 6]], [[1976]] in [[Wikipedia:London|London]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="AllMusic" /> Having had a successful career in music,<ref name="DigitalSpy">{{WebCite|url=http://www.digitalspy.com/music/news/a152343/whatever-happened-to-mn8|text=Whatever happened to&hellip; MN8?|author=Balls, David|date=2009-04-13|archivedate=20170203104351}}</ref> Tails eventually began to pursue acting roles.<ref name="The Force Awakens">{{Twitter|deetails|status/715160702206480384|Dee Tails|quote=Twin Ubdurian Brothers Prashee & Cratinus aka Tom Bell & Dee Tails|archivefile=File:Dee tails twin tweet.png}}</ref>

In [[2015]], Tails portrayed [[Cratinus]] in ''[[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens]]''.<ref name="The Force Awakens" /> He was originally set to play one of the [[Hassk triplets]], but those roles went to other actors. [[Neal Scanlan]], the creature effects supervisor for the film, reassigned Tails to be [[Prashee]]'s brother Cratinus. During filming, Tails and [[Tom Bell]], who was playing Prashee, stayed at their table in order to appear in the background throughout the recording shoots.<ref name="Star Wars Interviews" />

Based on his work as Cratinus, Neal Scanlan vouched for Tails' talent, and in [[2016]], got him a role as the [[droid]] [[K-OHN]]<ref name="Star Wars Interviews" /> in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. Though his costume was changed several times, he learned to adapt to his new masks by improvising with other actors on set.<ref name="Star Wars Interviews" /> In [[2017]], Tails physically portrayed the character [[Slowen Lo]] in ''[[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi]]'',<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|deetails|status/944211209519292416|Dee Tails|quote=I can now confirm, that I do indeed play this fantastic character called SLOWEN LO in The Last Jedi.|archivefile=File:Dee_Tails_Slowen_Lo.png}}</ref> with [[Joseph Gordon-Levitt]] doing the voice work.<ref name="Ossus">{{WebCite|url=http://www.ossus.pl/biblioteka/Slowen_Lo|text=Slowen Lo Bibliography|language=Polish|archivedate=20180614021327}}</ref> In [[2018]], Tails was cast as [[Quay Tolsite]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
