The [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] used a modified [[Eta-class shuttle|''Eta''-class shuttle]] as his personal '''shuttle''' in the later stages of the [[Clone Wars]]. He used the ship to travel to the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]] to thwart his former [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] [[Maul]]'s rise to power.<ref name="The Lawless" />

Later after Sidious used the shuttle to depart from the [[Spire (Stygeon Prime)|Spire]], a [[fortress]] controlled by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] on [[Stygeon Prime]].<ref name="Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 1">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 1|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 1]]</ref>
