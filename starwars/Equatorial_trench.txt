The '''Equatorial trench''' was the wide trench than ran around the equator of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first]] and [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second]] [[Death Star]]s, separating their northern and southern hemispheres.<ref name="ROUVG" /><ref name="Complete Locations" /> It contained the [[battle station]]s' [[Ion engine|ion drive]] arrays and [[Hangar|docking bays]], and the other trenches ran parallel to it.<ref name="SWCT">[[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]]</ref>

==Legacy==
{{Main|Ilum#First Order superweapon}}
The [[planet]] [[Ilum]] under the [[Imperial Era|rule]] of the Empire,<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> and later the [[First Order]],<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> also had a similarly surrounding equatorial trench, yet one of a larger size.<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref>
