'''CC-1137''' was the designation of a [[clone trooper]] made on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] from the [[Clone template|genetic template]] of [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] to serve in an [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]] created for the [[Jedi]]. As a [[human]] [[Cloning|clone]] of Fett,<ref name="100Scenes" /> CC-1137 stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|ft]]) tall and had [[Color|tan]] skin.<ref name="Clones" /> They also weighed 80 [[kilogram]]s<ref name="Weight" /> and was implanted with a [[behavioral modification biochip]] by the [[Kaminoan]] [[Scientist|scientists]] to ensure that the [[trooper]] would not resist certain [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|orders]].<ref name="Clones" /> CC-1137 wore white [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] and, at a facility on Kamino,<ref name="100Scenes" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[LEGO Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]'' establishes the scene with CC-1137 takes place during the events of {{Film|II}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> carried a [[blaster rifle]] over their shoulder while in formation with fellow clone troopers as [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] oversaw the unit. The clone army fought for the [[Galactic Republic]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] soon afterward.<ref name="100Scenes" />

==Behind the scenes==
CC-1137 was mentioned in the [[Canon|non-canon]] [[2015]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]''.<ref name="100Scenes" />
