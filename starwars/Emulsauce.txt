{{Quote|Emulsauce is like the astromech of condiments. See, astromech droids were designed to repair starships, but they can also act as copilots, slicers, and even adorable travelin' companions. That adaptability is pretty hard to find. '''Emulsauce''' was a type of highly versatile savory [[Foodstuff|condiment]] that could be used as a dip, a dressing, a [[sandwich]] topping, or a spread. It was smooth in texture and yellow in [[color]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Cookbook date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for the [[sauce]] in his first cookbook, ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', and he also used it as an ingredient in the recipe for [[Deviled Pikobi Egg]]s included in the [[book]]. Tuggs compared the versatility of the condiment to that of an [[astromech droid]] and claimed to eat emulsauce by the spoonful when no one was looking. He recommended it as a dip for [[Fritzle Fries]] or as a sauce for his version of [[Ronto Wrap]]s to make them taste more like the cook [[Bakkar]]'s original.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Emulsauce was introduced into the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', written by [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the sauce blends [[Wikipedia:Egg|egg yolks]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], [[Wikipedia:Water|cold water]], [[Wikipedia:Rice vinegar|rice vinegar]], [[Wikipedia:Olive oil|cold olive oil]], and [[Wikipedia:Miso|white miso paste]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> The [[Emulsauce/Legends|sauce]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was created by [[Gregory Walker]] for "[[Dining at Dex's]]," a [[2009]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]]-exclusive article.<ref name="DaD">{{Hyperspace|url=fans/hyperspace/source/dexdiner/index.html|text=Dining at Dex's|int=Dining at Dex's}}</ref> Walker considered emulsauce to be "''Star Wars''-style [[Wikipedia:Mayonnaise|mayonnaise]]."<ref name="Blog">{{Blog|narbflick|11|Dining at Dex's: The Story Behind the Story}}</ref>
