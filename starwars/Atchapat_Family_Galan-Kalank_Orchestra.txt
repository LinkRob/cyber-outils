The '''Atchapat Family Galan-Kalank Orchestra''' was a [[Corellian]] [[music]]al group consisting of members of the [[Atchapat Family]], active during the [[High Republic Era]]. Performing in the [[Galan-Kalank]] genre, the orchestra's lead singer was [[Svi'no Atchapat]], who rose to fame by the time she was a teenager. It also included her mother, father and six brothers, one of those being [[Barg'no Atchapat|Barg'no]]. The family was responsible for transforming Galan-Kalank into a galactic sensation, touring amphitheaters and music halls from [[Chandrila]] to [[Coruscant]]. They put [[Coronet City]] on the pop-cultural arts map.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
