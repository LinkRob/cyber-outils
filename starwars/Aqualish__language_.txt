{{Quote|And there you go, honking away. Don't you get it, Parr? No one can understand you, and even if they could, they wouldn't care what you have to say.|[[Lourna Dee]], to [[Parr]]|<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref>}}
'''Aqualish''' was the [[language]] spoken by the [[species]] of the [[Aqualish|same name]]. From a [[human]] perspective, it sounded like [[nerf]] bleats.<ref name="DiD-book" /> According to the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]],<ref name="Droids in Distress">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref> who claimed to be fluent in over six million forms of communication,<ref name="ANH" /> an amateur translator of Aqualish could easily confuse the words for "seven" and "seventeen." [[Sabine Wren]], a [[Mandalorian]] weapons expert who rebelled against the [[Galactic Empire]], could speak Aqualish. During a [[Mission to steal Imperial weapons|mission]] to steal a cargo of [[T-7 ion disruptor rifle]]s from the Empire, Wren used that knowledge of hers to deliberately mistranslate an exchange between [[Maketh Tua]] and the Aqualish arms dealer [[Amda Wabo]], leading Tua to the wrong [[Hangar|docking bay]].<ref name="Droids in Distress" />

It shared certain structural similarities with [[Huttese]] and [[Rodian (language)|Rodian]], enough that if you learned one of these languages it made the others easier to learn.<ref name="Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook">''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]''</ref>
