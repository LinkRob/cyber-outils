'''482 BBY''', or '''482<ref name="Date" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> that was part of the [[High Republic Era]]. Around that year,<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Barnabas Vim]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Vix Fonnick]], went on a [[Mission to Angcord|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Angcord]]. Following the mission, Vim set out to find the [[Sophros|source]] of the [[Echo Stone]] so that he could return its fragments there.<ref name="Quest">''[[The High Republic Adventures: Quest of the Jedi]]''</ref>
