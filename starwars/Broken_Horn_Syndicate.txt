The '''Broken Horn Syndicate''' was a criminal organization that ruled the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]'s underworld. The [[Devaronian]] [[Cikatro Vizago]] was the syndicate's leader.<ref name="Visual Guide" /> In truth, the so-called "syndicate" was made up only of Vizago and his [[IG-RM bodyguard and enforcer droid]]s, which he only owned a handful of.<ref name="Crim and Punishment">{{InsiderCite|209|Crime and Punishment}}</ref> At times, a [[Spectres|group of rebels]] engaged in operations against the [[Galactic Empire]] was forced to run missions for this group in order to maintain funding.<ref name="Visual Guide" />
