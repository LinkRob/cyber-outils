The '''diplomatic service'''<ref name="Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/ambush-concept-art-gallery|text="Ambush" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> was a unit of the [[Coruscant Guard]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. During the [[Battle of Rugosa|Ambush on Rugosa]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] was accompanied by [[Clone Lieutenant]] [[Thire]], [[Jek]], and [[Rys]],<ref name="Ambush" /> members of diplomatic service.<ref name="Sharing the Same Face" /> [[Clone Commander]] [[Thorn]] led a [[garrison]] of the diplomatic service, who landed in twelve specialized [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic gunships]] and one [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|''Consular''-class Armed Cruiser]], to escort [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] to [[Scipio]],<ref name="Crisis EP" /> although the entire detachment was later massacred by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] garrison during the [[Invasion of Scipio|Battle of Scipio]].<ref name="Crisis" />
