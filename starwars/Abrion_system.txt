The '''Abrion system''' was a [[star system]] that was the location of [[the Wheel]], a [[space station]] that was home to the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] scavenging family. [[Freemaker Salvage and Repair]] was based in the [[Middle Ring/LEGO|Middle Ring]] of the Wheel.<ref name="Freemaker Salvage and Repair" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Abrion system first appeared in the non-[[canon]] [[2016]] [[Disney XD]] animated television series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''. It was first identified as the location of the Wheel space station in the one-minute [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] video "[[Freemaker Salvage and Repair (video)|Freemaker Salvage and Repair]]" on [[August 29]], [[2016]].<ref name="Freemaker Salvage and Repair" /> While non-canon, the ''Freemaker Adventures'' was produced in conjunction with the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]].<ref name="SWCE 2016">{{SW|url=news/swce-2016-lego-star-wars-the-freemaker-adventures-panel-liveblog|text=SWCE 2016: ''LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures'' Panel Liveblog}}</ref> 

[[The Wheel/Legends|The Wheel]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, and was located in the [[Besh Gorgon system/Legends|Besh Gorgon system]],<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> rather than the Abrion system invented for the show.<ref name="Freemaker Salvage and Repair" /> The [[canon]] [[The Wheel|version of the station]] kept the same placement of the [[Besh Gorgon system]] when it was introduced in the book ''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure]]''.<ref name="Obi-Wan and Anakin">''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure]]''</ref>
