'''Blood-sensitive sensors''' were a type of [[sensor]] that could detect when [[blood]] had been spilled and worked alongside a [[blood alarm]] to notify individuals via [[Comm unit|comm]] when it was triggered. In [[32 BBY]], [[Captain]] [[Quarsh Panaka]] installed a blood-sensitive sensor in [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[Padmé Amidala's apartment (Theed Royal Palace)|apartments]] within the [[Theed Royal Palace]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]].

That [[Standard year|year]], the sensor was set off when Amidala's [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] [[Saché]] started her [[period]], so it sent a blood [[Alarm system|alarm]] notification to the captain, leading him and his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Mariek Panaka]], to investigate. Upon entering the rooms and discovering what had happened, Saché was mortified. Afterward, Mariek helped the [[handmaiden]] acquire new [[Clothing|sleeping attire]] and admonished Quarsh for placing a blood-sensitive sensor in rooms housing teenage [[Gender|girls]].

==Description==
{{Quote|It's never gone off before.|Quarsh Panaka, about the blood-sensitive sensor in Padmé Amidala's apartment|<ref name="QP" />}}
A blood-sensitive sensor was a type of [[sensor]] that detected the spilling of [[blood]]. It worked alongside a [[blood alarm]] that could notify individuals via [[Comm unit|comm]] when the sensor was set off. The sensor was not able to discern the cause of the blood being spilled and would respond with a full alert to any such spillage, whether major or minor.<ref name="QP" />

==History==
{{Quote|It was the blood alarm, and I don't think it was a sewing accident this time.''"<br />"''We keep absorption pads in the bathroom. The instructions are on the package. I can figure it out.|Mariek Panaka and Saché|<ref name="QP" />}}
[[File:NabooRoyalHandmaidens.png|thumb|left|200px|A blood-sensitive sensor was installed in an apartment used by Padmé Amidala and several Naboo Royal Handmaidens (pictured).]]
As part of his security preparations in the [[Theed Royal Palace]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]], [[Captain]] [[Quarsh Panaka]] of the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]] acquired a blood sensor and installed it in [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[Padmé Amidala's apartment (Theed Royal Palace)|apartments]]<ref name="QP" /> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="QP Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' to [[32 BBY]].</ref> Afterward, the sensor was accidentally set off when Amidala's [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] [[Eirtaé]] pricked her [[Limb|finger]] with a needle while sewing.<ref name="QP" />

Later the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="QP Date" /> the [[handmaiden]] [[Saché]] [[Sleep|slept]] in Amidala's bed while her [[queen]] and fellow handmaidens attended a concert for the [[band]] [[Neurotransmitter Affection]]. Her [[period]] started during the [[Local day|night]], resulting in blood staining the bed's sheets. The sensor Panaka had installed in the apartment was triggered shortly afterward, which sent a blood [[Alarm system|alarm]] alert to his comm. He and his [[Marriage|wife]], [[officer]] [[Mariek Panaka]], rushed to Amidala's apartments to investigate the cause of the disturbance and ensure the [[Monarchy|queen's]] safety. Upon loudly entering the rooms, they startled Saché in her sleep and turned on the lights.<ref name="QP" />

After realizing the handmaiden's period had started, Mariek sent Quarsh away and claimed no emergency had occurred. Saché was mortified at what had happened and excused herself from the room to obtain absorption pads from the [[Refresher|bathroom]] while the older officer helped acquire new sheets and clean [[Clothing|sleeping attire]]. Afterward, Mariek assured Quarsh that Amidala was safe, admonishing him for putting a blood-sensitive sensor in a room occupied by young teenage [[Gender|girls]], some of whom were not yet using [[suppressant shot]]s to manage their period cycles. The captain was embarrassed and promised to apologize later.<ref name="QP" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="QH Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Hope]]'' to [[22 BBY]].</ref> one of Amidala's former handmaidens, [[Sabé]], fondly remembered the incident and Quarsh's horror upon realizing why his blood-sensitive sensor had been triggered.<ref name="QH">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A blood-sensitive sensor first appeared in ''[[Queen's Peril]]'', a [[2020]] novel written by [[E. K. Johnston]] as the second installment of ''[[Star Wars: Queen's Series]]''.<ref name="QP" /> During the development of ''Queen's Peril'', Johnston indicated that several members of [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] felt that the scene that a blood-sensitive sensor featured in was incredibly awkward and left comments on the draft after reading it. The author felt strongly about the scene's inclusion in the work.<ref name="RebelReads Interview">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@RebelLegionOfficial|link=Rebel Legion|video=PLsER9FqeCM|text=Rebel Reads EK Johnston Interview|date=2021-03-21}}</ref>
