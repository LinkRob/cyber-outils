A '''demon squid''' was a type of [[squid]] with poisonous barbs, acid-green skin, and long tentacles. It would expand when agitated but could deflate once calmed down. In [[41 BBY]], a baby demon squid was planted into [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s soup by [[Bolla Ropal]]. Its tentacles caused burn-like welts on Kenobi's skin before [[Prie]] stroked its spines, calming it down.<ref name="Padawan" />
