The '''Drovan Freighter''' was a type of [[freighter]]. At least one Drovan freighter flew with the [[Citizens' Fleet]] during the [[Battle of Exegol]] against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]].<ref name="Kids" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Drovan.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Drovan Freighter concept art]]
A Drovan Freighter appeared in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> The vehicle was first identified in the [[2020]] video "Every Ship in the Star Wars Sequel Trilogy" which was released as part of the ''[[Star Wars By the Numbers]]'' series on the [[StarWarsKids.com]] [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel.<ref name="Kids" />

The Drovan freighter also appeared in the Gizmodo article "See How Star Wars' Most Impressive Fleet Was Created in This Exclusive Rise of Skywalker Clip", where it was presented alongside many other starships that were kitbashed together specifically for the Citizen's Fleet.
