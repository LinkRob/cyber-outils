'''Csaus''' was an astronomical object located in the territory of the [[Unknown Regions]] government named the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> The [[Chiss Space]] celestial body was situated in the [[Csaus system]], within grid square F-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> during a [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|crisis]] in the Ascendancy, a [[Battle at Csaus|battle]] was fought at Csaus between the [[Tahmie]] and [[Droc family|Droc]] [[Chiss family|families]], wherein [[Chiss Defense Force]] [[Admiral]] [[Ers'ikaro]]'s [[Firewolf man-of-war]] [[Bellicose (Firewolf man-of-war)|''Bellicose'']] stopped the conflict before it could further escalate. Another [[Skirmish at Csaus|conflict]] took place at Csaus late during the crisis.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Csaus was first mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final volume of the ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Csaus/Legends|Csaus]] was introduced in [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]]'s [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
