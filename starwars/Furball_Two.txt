'''Furball Two''' was a [[Loth-cat]] owned by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Vukorah]] around [[3 ABY]] after she left the [[Unbroken Clan]] criminal syndicate. The [[tooka]] accompanied Vukorah to [[Kirkeide Station]], where the [[Hunting|hunter]] hoped to acquire new work from the [[hybroid]] [[Syphacc]]. After arriving at the station, Vukorah told Furball Two to remain aboard her [[Vukorah's ship|ship]].

Furball Two, along with Vukorah's other tookas, later assisted Vukorah when she traveled to the [[planet]] [[Epikonia]] with the [[bounty]] hunter [[Losha Tarkon]] to [[Rescue of Beilert Valance|rescue]] the latter's [[T'onga|wife]] from the entity named the [[Scourge]]. Sometime later, after Vukorah captured a [[Unidentified bounty|bounty]], she informed the tookas they would have extra [[blue milk]] to celebrate.

Vukorah traveled to [[Kirkeide Station]] aboard her [[Vukorah's ship|ship]] in order to register with [[Syphacc's Bountiful Bounties]], operated by the [[hybroid]] [[Syphacc]], as she was in need of work. The hunter told the cats to wish her luck, adding that the [[blue milk]] they [[Diet|drank]] was not [[Currency|cheap]]. When Furball Two attempted to follow Vukorah, she indicated they would need to remain on the [[Starship|ship]], as she did not want to kill one of her new colleagues if they ate the tooka.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39" />

===A good kitty===
{{Quote|Mama bagged another sleemo. Once we get him to ''Nevarro'' to collect&mdash;we'll have some extra ''blue milk'' to celebrate, Furball Two!|Vukorah, to her tookas|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 42" />}}
While at Kirkeide Station, Vukorah and Tarkon defeated several [[droid]]s that had been [[Scourge crisis|infected]] by the entity known as the [[Scourge]]. Afterward, the two and Vukorah's tookas left for<ref name="Bounty Hunters 41" /> the [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Epikonia]] to [[Rescue of Beilert Valance|rescue]] Tarkon's [[Marriage|wife]], T'onga, and her friends. Once they arrived at Epikonia, Furball Two and the three other tookas carried [[Class-A thermal detonator|thermal detonators]] in their [[mouth]]s to where T'onga was being held [[prisoner]], and they dropped the [[Thermal detonator|grenades]], causing explosions. The cats then began attacking the Scourged [[cyborg]]s and<ref name="Bounty Hunters 41" /> [[spider-droid]]s,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 38">[[Bounty Hunters 38|''Bounty Hunters'' 38]]</ref> with Vukorah calling them "good kitties" and saying she would give them treats.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 41" />

[[File:FurballTwo-BountyHunters42.png|thumb|right|300px|Vukorah and her tookas, including Furball Two, after she caught a bounty]]
Once T'onga and her friends were successfully rescued, Vukorah, Tarkon, and the four tookas fled the facility, eventually leaving Epikonia.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 41" /> Sometime later, Vukorah was hired to collect a [[Unidentified bounty|bounty]]. Once she captured the bounty, she planned to take him to<ref name="Bounty Hunters 42">[[Bounty Hunters 42|''Bounty Hunters'' 42]]</ref> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Nevarro]] to collect her payment and told her tookas&mdash;including Furball Two&mdash;that they would have extra blue [[milk]] to celebrate.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 42" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Such ''good'' kitties! Mama's gonna give you all the treats!|Vukorah, to her tookas as they attack the Scourged cyborgs and spider-droids|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 41" />}}
Furball had [[Color|silver]] skin,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39" /> black [[eye]]s,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 41" /> and lilac fur with dusty gold-colored accents and a paler underbelly. They liked to drink blue milk.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39" /> When Furball Two and the other tookas attacked the Scourged cyborgs and spider-droids on Epikonia, Vukorah called them good cats and said she would reward them with treats.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 41" />

==Behind the scenes==
Furball Two first appeared in the [[Bounty Hunters 39|thirty-ninth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Davide Tinto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39" /> on [[October 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103059/star_wars_bounty_hunters_2020_39|text=Star Wars: Bounty Hunters (2020) #39}}</ref>
