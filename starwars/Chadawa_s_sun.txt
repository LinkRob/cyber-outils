'''Chadawa's sun''' was located in the<ref name="Victory's Price" /> [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim's]] [[Chadawa system]], which was situated in the [[Croynar sector]] at the coordinates N-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> The [[Star|sun]] was [[orbit]]ed by [[Chadawa]], an inhabitable [[Waterworld|ocean planet]] that had been constructed by a [[Unidentified species (Chadawa)|species]] eons before the [[Galactic Civil War]], along with its desolate [[Chadawan moon|moon]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chadawa's sun appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the final installment of [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
