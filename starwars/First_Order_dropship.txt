The [[First Order]] used '''dropships''' to carry vehicles into battle. Found aboard the mobile [[capital]] ''[[Supremacy]]'', at least three delivered [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport]]s and the [[superlaser siege cannon]] to the [[Battle of Crait]] in [[34 ABY]]. These vessels could carry six [[walker]]s each.<ref name="The Last Jedi">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The dropships first appeared in the [[2017]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].
