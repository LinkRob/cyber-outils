The '''''Consular''-class light cruiser''', also known as the '''''Consular''-class space cruiser''', and commonly referred to as the '''Republic Cruiser''', was a [[starship]] used by the [[Galactic Republic]] during the events leading up to the [[Invasion of Naboo]] and during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Design==
{{Quote|Captain, look!''"<br />"''Shields up!|The crew of the ''Radiant VII'' moments before its destruction.|audio=WilliamsAndMadakor.flac|<ref name="TPM" />}}
Manufactured in the orbital shipyards of the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]], the ''Consular''-class was a space [[cruiser]] propelled by three powerful [[Dyne 577 radial atomizer engine]]s situated at the aft of the hundred-and-fifteen-[[meter]] vessel. It was also equipped with a [[Longe Voltrans]] tri-arc [[CD-3.2 hyperdrive]], and protected by sturdy [[deflector shield]]s generated from a cluster of radar dishes forward of its three engines. Aside from its shielding, the ''Consular''-class was typically unarmed. A [[docking ring]] was located on the side of the vessel, and the bridge was situated at the fore,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> above a diplomatic [[salon pod]].<ref name="Starfighters" />

===Color signal===
The striking red [[color]] of the Republican ship sent a clear message.
The scarlet indicated his diplomatic status and warns of possible attacks.
Red has been the color of diplomatic relations and neutrality on Galactic Republic starships for generations.
The unusual all-red paint job meant it comes directly from the mighty main world of Coruscant.
During the chancellorship of [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] of [[Naboo]], the color red becomes even more important, as he prefers to use it on any Republic ships.<ref name="Complete Vehicles New" />

===Communication in a colorful galaxy===
[[File:Republic_Cruiser_Cockpit.png|thumb|left|250px|The control console of the Radiant VII was designed in such a way that the captain and co-pilot can share control, but one can fly the ship alone if necessary.]]
In order to communicate with any culture on the journey, the Republican Corvette had many bowls and other antennas.
Two com-officers were specifically concerned with the operation of the communication computers, the translation of [[language]]s and the decoding of unorthodox [[transmissions]] of foreign worlds.<ref name="Complete Vehicles New" />

===Strict safety measures===
The Radiant VII was a modified consular-class ship.
Civilian models were used for simple transportation purposes, but the red versions of the Republic were equipped for the special demands of the Galactic government service.
On missions, it was not uncommon for their reputation as absolutely safe ships to be decisive for diplomatic conferences and crisis meetings.
For security reasons, the crew was reduced to a minimum, with many ship functions performed by [[auxiliary droids]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles New" />

===The salon pod of the corvette===
Republican cruisers like this corvette were often serve as neutral ground for conferences of officials and leaders of rival groups.
In order to provide space for physiologically different foreign worlds, [[Coruscant]]'s hangars had suitable parlor pods with which the ship could be equipped. In case of emergency, the entire salon pod was replaced. It had its own sensors and an independent [[life support]] system for the delegation on board. However, when the Radiant VII was attacked, the crew was unable to escape.<ref name="Complete Vehicles New" />

==History==
{{Quote|What&hellip; what are they?''"<br />"''Sk&hellip; Skygift! They're Skygift!|Fighters in the Open-Closed war sighting a fleet of ''Consular''-class cruisers|<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin, Part V">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 5|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 5]]</ref>}}
The ''Consular''-class cruiser became recognizable throughout [[the galaxy]] during the last decades of the [[Galactic Republic]], due to its distinctive shape and hue.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|republic-cruiser|Republic Cruiser}}</ref> Painted with a striking red [[color]] scheme, the cruiser's hue declared the ship's diplomatic immunity, as red marked a [[starship]] as neutral in the Republic.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> It also indicated that the vessel was conducting a mission on behalf of the [[Galactic Senate]], the [[Supreme Chancellor]], or the [[Jedi Order]].

[[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]] [[Escape from the Saak'ak|dispatched]] the ''Consular''-class cruiser<ref name="Databank" /> ''[[Radiant VII]]'',<ref name="SWJ1" /> carrying [[Jedi]] ambassadors [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], to negotiate the [[Invasion of Naboo|Blockade of]] [[Naboo]] by the [[Trade Federation]]. Docking within the landing bay<ref name="TPM" /> of the ''[[Saak'ak]]'',<ref name="SWJ1" /> the ''Radiant VII'' was fired upon by a concealed [[turbolaser]] [[turret]] on the orders of [[Darth Sidious]]. The ship's crew were taken by surprise, and the vessel was destroyed.<ref name="TPM" /><ref name="Databank" />

A fleet of Republic cruisers arrived on [[Carnelion IV]] at the culmination of the [[Open-Closed War|Open-Closed war]], after a summons by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] who claimed the planet's [[atmosphere]] had a high concentration of [[tibanna]] [[gas]]. The cruisers arrived to secure the planet and survey for signs of the gas, and their presence was enough to bring the battle between the [[Open]] and the [[Closed]] to a halt.<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin, Part V" />

With the onset of the [[Clone Wars]], numerous ''Consular''-class cruisers were converted into [[frigate]]s adapted for combat as part of [[Project Supercharger]].<ref name="Databank Republic Frigate">{{Databank|republic-frigate|Republic Frigate}}</ref><ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> These [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|Republic frigates]] were equipped with new [[armor]]ed [[hull]]s and [[laser]] turrets.<ref name="Databank Republic Frigate" />
