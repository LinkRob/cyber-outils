'''Bodo Linx''' was a [[Nuxan]] [[Gender|male]] who worked as an [[Information broker|informant]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] [[Death|killed]] his family, setting him against them. He met with [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Han Solo]] and [[Sana Starros]] on [[Barnahof]], giving Organa the details for a [[Qensog|contact]] of his on [[Brentaal IV]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|You've been unable to make contact with&hellip;your now-elusive organization. I'm sure my friend on Brentaal IV can help.|Bodo Linx, to Leia Organa|<ref name="Star Wars 56" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era]], Linx's entire family were killed by the [[Galactic Empire]], leading him to oppose the regime. He found work as an [[Information broker|informant]], giving information to others in exchange for [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Star Wars 56" />

[[File:BodoDatacard-2015StarWars56.jpg|thumb|left|280px|Bodo meeting with the rebels]]
In a [[cantina]] on [[Barnahof]], Linx met with [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] heroes [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Luke Skywalker]], and [[Han Solo]],<ref name="Star Wars 56" /> who were being secretly transported by [[Sana Starros]] after the Empire [[Battle of Mako-Ta|drove]] them from their base at the [[Mako-Ta Space Docks]].<ref name="Star Wars 55">[[Star Wars (2015) 55|''Star Wars'' (2015) 55]]</ref> Linx possessed a [[datacard]] that contained details of<ref name="Star Wars 56" /> [[Qensog]],<ref name="Star Wars 61">[[Star Wars (2015) 61|''Star Wars'' (2015) 61]]</ref> a contact on [[Brentaal IV]] who he believed could help reunite the fragmented Rebellion. While they were discussing the deal, [[stormtrooper]]s entered the cantina, and the rebel fugitives hid underneath the table.<ref name="Star Wars 56" />

The stormtroopers demanded to see both Starros' and Linx's [[Identichip|identification]], which they provided. After the stormtroopers showed them [[hologram]]s of the rebel leaders that were currently hiding beneath them, Starros denied seeing any of them. When the stormtroopers inquired about the table's large size for two people to sit at, Starros claimed that it was because of the Nuxan's natural smell, which Linx agreed with. The stormtroopers turned to leave, and the rebels emerged from under the table. Linx handed the datacard containing his contact's details to Organa, who thanked him. Starros and the rebels left, and Linx wished them luck, telling them to be careful.<ref name="Star Wars 56" /> The rebels eventually met Qensog on Brentaal, and the [[Herglic]] provided them with coordinates that led them to the ''[[Home One]]'' in the [[Kaliida Nebula]].<ref name="Star Wars 61" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You're lucky the Imperials have a way of turning even amoral monsters like me against them.|Bodo Linx|<ref name="Star Wars 56" />}}
Linx claimed that he had no morals, calling himself a "monster." Despite this, the deaths of his family at the hands of the Empire made him sympathetic to the Rebellion's cause, and he was willing to assist them. As a [[Reptile|reptilian]] Nuxan, he had [[Color|green]] [[eye]]s and green skin with spots of brown.<ref name="Star Wars 56" />
