'''Finnilus''' was a [[lieutenant]] who served in the [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|intelligence branch]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Finnilus was tasked with analyzing ballistics testing and reports after the [[Primtara incident]], an attack on the [[Primtara outpost]] by an alleged [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Special Commando Advanced Recon Trooper|SCAR unit]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
