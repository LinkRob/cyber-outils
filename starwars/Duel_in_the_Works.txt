A '''duel''' in [[the Works]] erupted between [[Count]] [[Dooku]]<ref name="The Sith Lord" />&mdash;a former [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> who had been secretly working with the [[Sith Master]] [[Darth Sidious]]&mdash;and [[Jedi]] Master [[Yaddle]]. After Yaddle followed Dooku,<ref name="The Sith Lord" /> who had been hiding his Sith dealings by use of the code name "Tyranus,"<ref name="Lost One">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref> to the [[Inquisitorius Headquarters|LiMerge Power building]], the two engaged in a [[lightsaber duel]], which resulted in her death. In the aftermath of the duel, Dooku fully succumbed to the [[dark side of the Force]] and became a [[Sith Lord]]<ref name="The Sith Lord" /> under the name [[Darth]] Tyranus.<ref name="Episode II" />
