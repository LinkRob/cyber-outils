'''Advanced Recon Force troopers''', also referred to as '''ARF troopers''' or as '''AT-RT Drivers''', were [[clone trooper]]s<ref name="CT">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=AT-RT Driver - Galactic Republic}}</ref> who wore lighter [[Clone trooper armor|armor]] for stealth and speed and often rode [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s into battle,<ref name="CT Armor" /> wielding [[RPC-2 Rocket Launcher]]s and [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]s.<ref name="RotS" /> ARF troopers also served as the reconnaissance specialists of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]],<ref name="Store link">{{Asmodee|subdomain=store|url=products/star-wars-legion-arf-troopers|text=Star Wars: Legion &ndash; ARF Troopers}}</ref> with those who were engaged in [[scout]] duty identified as '''Advanced Recon Force Scout Troopers'''.<ref name="CT" />

ARF troopers wore [[Phase I ARF armor]]<ref name="BFII Info">{{EA|subdomain=battlefront-forums|url=discussion/137644/community-transmission-where-are-those-droidekas|text=Community Transmission &ndash; Where Are Those Droidekas}}</ref> and, eventually, another model of armor. [[Phase II ARF armor]] also would come to exist and was worn by [[clone scout trooper]]s.<ref name="RotS" />

==Description==
[[File:Stak_helmet.png|thumb|left|250px|ARF trooper Stak in Phase I gear]]
Like most clone troopers,<ref name="clonetroopers">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref> ARF troopers were 1.83 [[meter]]s or 6 [[Foot|feet]] tall. They were the cavalry of the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic army]] riding AT-RTs on scouting missions and into battle. Clones with exceptional agility are given ARF training and they regard themselves a separate breed of trooper. They saw piloting an AT-RT as the equivalent of riding a living mount, rather than driving a vehicle. ARF troopers were skilled in reconnaissance, cavalry operations, armed and unarmed combat, and military tactics.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|All these Wookiees are dead. Move to the east.|An Advanced Recon Force AT-RT driver|<ref name="RotS" />}}
In the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and following the kidnapping of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]'s infant, [[Rotta]], two ARF [[trooper]]s followed several of Jabba's [[bounty hunter]]s to a [[B'omarr Order]] [[B'omarr Order Monastery|Monastery]] on [[Teth]]. The [[Unidentified Advanced Recon Force commander|ARF trooper commander]] informed [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]] of this. They awaited the arrival of [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and informed him that at least two [[battalion]]s of [[battle droid]]s were guarding the [[monastery]] against an attack.<ref name="TCW film" /> Several ARF troopers were stationed at [[Tipoca City]]'s [[Tipoca City Military Complex|Military Complex]].<ref name="CC">{{TCW|Clone Cadets}}</ref>

[[File:ARFTroopersExpansion-Ministravaganza2024.png|thumb|right|300px|ARF troopers in Phase I armor battle Confederacy crab and aqua droids.]]
During the [[Ryloth]] [[Battle of Ryloth|campaign]], several ARF troopers served under [[General]] [[Mace Windu]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Ponds]] in [[Lightning Squadron]]. The ARF troopers and their [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport|AT-RTs]] were able to take out several detachments of [[Emir]] [[Wat Tambor]]'s occupation forces en route to [[Lessu City]]. Windu then took two ARF troopers, [[Stak]] and [[Razor]] to find the [[Twi'lek Resistance|Resistance fighters]] under General [[Cham Syndulla]]. Windu and the two ARF troopers were able find them and were taken to their [[Twi'lek freedom fighters hideout|hideout]]. Windu then had Commander Ponds and General Syndulla prepare their men for their assault on Lessu. After Windu, Stak, and Razor were able to take control of the [[plasma bridge]], Commander Ponds' ARF troopers took their AT-RTs into Lessu alongside Syndulla's [[blurrg]] riders.<ref name="LoR" />

Several ARF troopers of the [[212th Recon Division]] fought under General [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] during the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="LaPR" /> During Separatist General [[Grievous]]' [[Battle of Kamino|attack]] on [[Tipoca City]], [[Clone Commander|Clone ARC Commander]] [[Blitz]] was accompanied by three ARF troopers, and ordered one to use a [[RPS-6 rocket launcher|rocket launcher]] to destroy the last of the [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident''-assault ships]].<ref name="ARC Troopers" /> Many of these troopers belonged to [[Rancor Battalion]], and had distinctive red markings on their [[clone trooper armor]], while ARF troopers who served in the [[501st Legion]] had [[Color|blue]] markings.

[[File:ATRT-SWL71.png|thumb|left|300px|An ARF trooper on an AT-RT.]]
Several ARF troopers under Commander [[Trauma]], General [[Halsey]], and his [[Padawan]] [[Knox]], were stationed at the [[Temple of Eedit]] on [[Devaron]]. The temple then came under [[Devaron massacre|attack]] by battle [[droid]]s. Trauma and his ARF troopers were able to hold off the droids, but when they came under attack by [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s new [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], [[Savage Opress]], their forces were wiped out.<ref name="Monster" /> At the [[Battle of Umbara]], many ARF troopers rode [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s as part of the forces led by Jedi General [[Pong Krell]].<ref name="DoU" /> Several were also present on the planet [[Kashyyyk]] at the war's end, where they helped enforce the execution of [[Order 66]].<ref name="RotS" /> ARF troopers later served the [[Galactic Empire]], the successor state of the Republic.<ref name="HC 37" />

=== Legacy ===
Years later, the [[Imperial Remnants]] of the Empire would utilize their own version of [[Imperial Remnant AT-RT Driver|AT-RT Drivers]].<ref name="AT-RT Driver">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|pack=Imperial Remnant AT-RT Driver|altlink=https://toysrus.com.au/products/star-wars-black-series-6-figure-imperial-remnant-at-rt-driver|archivedate=20260519075753}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] established that ARF troopers and AT-RT Drivers are the same class of clone trooper by referring to AT-RT Drivers in [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] as ARF troopers.<ref name="CT Armor" /> ''[[Star Wars: Card Trader]]'' treated AT-RT Drivers and ARF troopers as separate classes of clones, claiming that the former were more intelligent and, at least in terms of survival skills, better trained. However, ''Card Trader'' also referred to AT-RT Drivers with the "Advanced Recon Force" designation.<ref name="CT" />
