A '''communicator monitor''' was a type of monitor that could be used for communication. The [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|''Sheathipede''-class transport shuttle]] was equipped with a communicator monitor in the front of the [[shuttle]].<ref name="Ships">{{EncyclopediaCite|Miscellaneous Ships and Vehicles (2)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The communicator monitor was first identified in "[[Miscellaneous Ships and Vehicles (2)]],"<ref name="Ships" /> a booklet in a series of reference booklets titled the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']], published by [[De Agostini]] on [[July 29]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref>
