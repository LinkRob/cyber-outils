'''Evord the Incinerator''' was an individual who [[Writing|wrote]] letters in [[Low Core Basic]]. As a teenager, the [[Chandrilan]] [[Mon Mothma]] learned to read Low Core Basic in order to interpret Evord's letters.<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> 

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate}}</ref> Mothma, by then a [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Senate]], spoke with a [[Unidentified Gossam woman|Gossam woman]] at a gala on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. When the [[Gossam]] [[Gender|woman]] asked her if she remembered the [[Courtsilius Wax Revolution]], Mothma thought of her [[Mon Mothma's teenage bedroom|teenage bedroom]] and her library of foreign histories, remembering Evord's letters and her purchase of a manuscript of ''[[Porayne's Guide]]'' before recalling the revolution. After telling the Gossam she remembered it, the Gossam told Mothma to speak with the former [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Tychon Nulvolio]] to gain the support of the ex-Separatist voting bloc.<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Evord the Incinerator was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
