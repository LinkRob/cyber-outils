'''Archaeo-Prime''' was a structure located on the [[Cosmatanic Steppes]], a [[Planetoid|mantle]] of land suspended over a [[Cosmatanic Steppes gas giant|gas giant]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Archaeo-Prime was maintained by the [[Archaeological Association]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" />

==History==
After [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] [[Death|killed]] and stole an artifact from [[Ulbik Tan]], she flew the ''[[Ark Angel II]]'' to Archaeo-Prime on the [[Cosmatanic Steppes]]. There, she attempted to sell the artifact, but could not due to her doctorate being under review due to her shady "finds" on [[Boothi XII]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" />

Angry and confused, Doctor Aphra found her [[Parent|father]], [[Korin Aphra]], who revealed that he leaked the info, so she could help him find the [[Ordu Aspectu]]. They then departed on the ''Ark Angel II'' to find it.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 2" />
