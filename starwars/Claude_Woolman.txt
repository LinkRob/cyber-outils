'''Claude Woolman''' was an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] actor who played an [[Unidentified Imperial officer (wallscreen)|Imperial officer]] on a [[wallscreen]] in [[1978]]'s ''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]''. Throughout his acting career, he also appeared in such TV programs as ''[[Wikipedia:Mission: Impossible (1966 TV series)|Mission: Impossible]]'', ''[[Wikipedia:The Man from U.N.C.L.E.|The Man from U.N.C.L.E.]]'', and ''[[Wikipedia:Cagney & Lacey|Cagney & Lacey]]''.<ref name="Aveleyman">{{WebCite|url=https://filmdope.com/Statics/A00034000/ActorCredit34383.html|text=Claude Woolman|work=FilmDope|archivedate=20260125092212}}</ref>
