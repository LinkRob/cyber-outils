The '''Drunk Droid''' was a [[Cantina/Legends|cantina]] chain which operated locations throughout [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]], including several on [[Mek-Sha]] and [[Ord Mantell/Legends|Ord Mantell]], around [[3626 BBY]]. Locations could be recognized by their distinctive logo of a [[Droid/Legends|droid]] drinking through a straw. <ref name="Onslaught" >''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Onslaught]]''</ref><ref name="Legacy" />
