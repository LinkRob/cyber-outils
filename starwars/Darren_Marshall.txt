'''Darren Marshall''' is a sculptor and design artist who designed the characters for the [[Wikipedia:Cartoon Network|Cartoon Network]] animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''. Prior to joining the show, Marshall had worked for [[Industrial Light & Magic]], and was approached by [[Dave Filoni]], supervising producer of ''The Clone Wars'', who had seen some of Marshall's maquettes and asked him to come work on the show. In that job, Marshall makes clay sculptures of the central characters in the show, as well as characters who had previously appeared in other ''[[Star Wars]]'' mediums, so they can see how they will look in 3-D during the show.<ref name="Insider124">{{InsiderCite|124|Making Maquettes}}</ref>

Marshall has a background in [[Wikipedia:Stop motion|stop motion animation]], and previously worked for years at Mackinnon & Saunders in [[Wikipedia:Manchester|Manchester]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]], which made puppets for films and television commercials.<ref name="Insider124" /> He also worked at [[Wikipedia:Jim Henson's Creature Shop|Jim Henson's Creature Shop]], a company founded by puppeteer [[Wikipedia:Jim Henson|Jim Henson]], creator of [[Wikipedia:The Muppets|The Muppets]].<ref name="Designing">{{OfficialBlog|url=2008/07/25/designing-the-clone-wars|text=Designing the ''Clone Wars''}}</ref>

Marshall was involved in the production of the ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth-season]] episode [[Sabotage (episode)|''Sabotage'']], making a clay model of Jedi Master [[Cin Drallig/Legends|Cin Drallig's]] head.<ref name="Sabotage guide">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep517|text="Sabotage" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide}}</ref>
