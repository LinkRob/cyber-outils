During the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the [[planet]] [[Skako]], [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Skakoan]] [[species]], indirectly provided support to the Separatists. While the [[Techno Union]], a commerce guild over which Skakoans had majority control, had officially declared itself neutral in the conflict, [[Foreman]] [[Wat Tambor]], the organization's leader, directly supported the Separatists. Tambor's expansionist leanings turned the Republic against<ref name="Fully Operational">[[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']]</ref> his homeworld of Skako,<ref name="Skako Note" /> which their [[Grand Army of the Republic|forces]] '''captured'''.<ref name="Complete Locations" /> When the Separatists [[Mission to Mustafar|fell]] at the end of the Clone Wars, Skako was quickly sanctioned by the Republic and placed under an emigration restriction by its [[Proclamation of the New Order|succesor]], the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />
