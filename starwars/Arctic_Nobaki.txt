'''Arctic Nobaki''' was a [[band]] in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the band had recorded the [[Music|song]]: "[[Wake Up the Enrako]]," which could be selected from the [[jukebox]] at [[the Domak Refectory]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]]. The band shared its name with the [[Nobaki rat]], a [[rodent]] that could be found throughout [[Kijimi City]] on Kijimi during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arctic Nobaki was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]], published by [[Ubisoft]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> and released on [[August 30]], [[2024]].<ref name="Story trailer">{{SWYouTube|DUyk6D5rgcE|Star Wars Outlaws: Official Story Trailer}}</ref> In the game, the band's name is shown to players when they are selecting songs from the jukebox in The Domak Refectory.<ref name="Outlaws" /> "Wake Up the Enrako" was composed for the game by [[Cody Matthew Johnson]]<ref name="Songs from the Underworld">{{OST|Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)}}</ref> and released the same day as the game<ref name="qobuz">{{WebCite|url=https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/album/songs-from-the-underworld-cody-matthew-johnson/okblkfn26uyuc|text=Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)|work=[https://www.qobuz.com Qobuz]|archivedate=20240908212835}}</ref> as part of the soundtrack: ''[[Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)]]''; which listed Arctic Nobaki as a "Featured Artist."<ref name="Songs from the Underworld">{{OST|Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)}}</ref>
