'''''Choose Your Destiny: A Scavenger Mission''''' is a [[canon]] audiobook written by [[Cavan Scott]] and part of the ''[[Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny]]'' series. The audiobook was released exclusively on [[Wikipedia:Amazon|Amazon]] FreeTime Unlimited in [[2019]].<ref name="Website" />

There is one canon ending and there are 13 non-canon endings. The path to the canon ending is BA,[A,B,C],[A,B],A,[A,BB],BBB,[B,AA,ABA,ABB],[B,ABB,ABAB],AB (the brackets indicate a choice of paths). Therefore there are 720 different paths to the canon ending, taking between 21 to 24 minutes to reach the ending. The total duration of all story segments is approximately 41 minutes and there are 2225 total paths possible.
