'''Arezi Mar''' was a [[Jedi]] who was the Master of [[Gella Nattai]]. Prior to becoming the Master of Nattai, Mar was told a [[Unidentified Jedi fable|fable]] by her own [[Arezi Mar's Jedi Master|Master]] about a [[Unidentified Jedi Knight (fable)|Jedi Knight]] who wanted to see the entire [[The galaxy|galaxy]] in his lifetime. At some point before<ref name="Convergence" /> [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Mar told the fable to Nattai, who later told it to [[Axel Greylark]].<ref name="Convergence" />
