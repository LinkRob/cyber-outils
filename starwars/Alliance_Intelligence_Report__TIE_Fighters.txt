"'''Alliance Intelligence Report: TIE Fighters'''" is an article appearing in the [[May]] [[1996]] [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 10|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 10]], written by [[Timothy S. O'Brien]] as a supplement for the [[West End Games]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. Diagram illustrations were provided by [[Stephen Crane]].

The article is written from an [[in-universe]] perspective by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] [[Lieutenant Commander/Legends|Lieutenant Commander]] [[Bakki Sourthol]], serving as a tie-in to the West End Games sourcebook ''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]''.
