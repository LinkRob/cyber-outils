'''Fytoun''' was an [[Expansion Region]] astronomical object located in the [[Brak sector]]. It was home to the [[collector]] [[Sinvatt Bovic]]. By [[22 BBY]], the [[bounty hunter]]s [[Zam Wesell]] and [[Jango Fett]] traveled to Fytoun to capture Bovic on account of the debt he owed the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]], getting past his [[Security guard|guards]] to detain him and split the [[bounty]].

==Description==
Fytoun was a terrestrial astronomical object that had at least one [[moon]].<ref name="Wesell Run" /> It was located in the [[Brak sector]]'s [[Fytoun system]], within the [[Expansion Region]]. Fytoun was situated in grid square O-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==History==
{{Quote|I can take him here.|Zam Wesell prepares to leave Fytoun with Sinvatt Bovic|<ref name="Wesell Run" />}}
[[File:ZamWesell-TWR.png|thumb|right|280px|Zam Wesell (pictured) traveled to Fytoun to capture Sinvatt Bovic.]]
By [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{InsiderCite|209|Galactic Tales: The Wesell Run}} takes place before [[Zam Wesell]]'s [[death]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> the rich [[collector]] [[Sinvatt Bovic]] was in heavy debt to the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]], who sent the [[bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]] to capture him alive. Wesell traveled to Fytoun and spent a [[Standard day|day]] scouting Bovic's [[Sinvatt Bovic's residence|house]], which she learned had too many [[Security guard|guards]] for her to handle&mdash;[[security droid]]s, [[human]]s, and three [[Mabari]] warriors. Recognizing her disadvantage, she called in a favor from fellow [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], offering to split Bovic's bounty. Fett landed his [[Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft|starship]], ''[[Slave I]]'', in the valley and accompanied Wesell to take down the non-Mabari guards and [[Bomb|detonate]] an outer wall to get inside.<ref name="Wesell Run" />

The explosion alerted the Mabari, who gave the bounty hunters trouble: it was only after Wesell used her [[Clawdite]] [[Changeling|morphing]] abilities that she and Fett were able to defeat the warriors and move toward Bovic's study. They found an individual who bore Bovic's appearance, but after Fett knocked out the third Mabari guard, the Bovic lookalike turned out to be another Clawdite who had been assuming the collector's appearance. Fett [[Death|killed]] the imposter but not before the changeling hit him with a [[bluebarb wasp]] [[poison dart]] that nearly killed the hunter until Wesell located an [[Poison|antivenin]]. With their opposition gone, the pair moved forward, blasting another wall to get to a [[hangar]] where the real Bovic was trying to escape. Wesell [[Sniper|sniped]] the collector's [[speeder]] and [[stun]]ned him, allowing her to easily detain Bovic and for her and Fett to leave Fytoun with their bounty.<ref name="Wesell Run" />

==Inhabitants==
The human Sinvatt Bovic lived on Fytoun along with his human guards and Mabari warriors.<ref name="Wesell Run" />

==Locations==
Bovic lived on the celestial body in his residence, which was located near [[Plant|foliage]] as well as a valley that was lit by moonlight during the [[Local day|night]].<ref name="Wesell Run" />

==Behind the scenes==

Bende]] and was published in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]''{{'s}} [[Star Wars Insider 209|209th issue]]<ref name="Wesell Run" /> on [[March 15]], [[2022]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/96-star-wars-insider-209|text=Star Wars Insider #209}}</ref>
