'''A'lbaran''' was a [[human]]<ref name="THRCE" /> [[Gender|male]] [[Monarch of E'ronoh|Monarch]] of the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was the father of [[Niko A'lbaran|Niko]] and [[Xiri A'lbaran]]. After [[Niko A'lbaran|Niko]] died on a diplomatic mission to [[Eiram]] he doubled down on the war between the two planets again.<ref name="Convergence" />
