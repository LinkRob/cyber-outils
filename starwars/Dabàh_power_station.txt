'''Dabàh power station''' was a group of buildings in the [[Mirogana Valley]] on the [[planet]] [[Toshara]] that in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> included a [[speeder]] [[fuel]] station.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 ABY,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] talked to a [[Rodian]] windfisher outside one of the buildings, who told her about a wind tunnel on Toshara rumored to contain old artifacts. Inside the building she listened in on a conversation between two smuggler about a stash that one of them hid behind a waterfall near [[Jaunta's Hope]], which Vess would later steal for herself. Also inside Vess found and stole a copy of episode 58 of the [[Baradium Dynasty]] [[holodrama]]. On a balcony of one of the building, Vess found a stash that contained the materials to be used to apply a "Commander" paint job to Vess's [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dabàh power station first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The location contained one of the paint schemes that players could use to customize their speeder bike, as well as the start of two intel quests, "Waterfall Stash" and "Wind Tunnels". Whether Vess applied this paint job to her speeder bike has not be determine to be [[canon]]ical.<ref name="Outlaws" />
