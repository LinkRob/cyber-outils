'''At Achrann''' was a [[planet]] within [[the galaxy]] that was one of the nine [[Jewels of the Old Republic]]. During the [[New Republic Era]], At Achrann was a war-torn world that greatly resembled [[At Attin]], with two tribes&mdash;the [[Troik]] and [[Hattan]]&mdash;fighting each other. The [[Myykian]] [[Neel]] encountered the [[human]] [[Hayna]] on At Achrann.<ref name="Getty">{{Getty|url=thewaltdisneystudios/allaccess/#!asset/5v5xch2rffs43bqb63mwm83m|text=GR-029412 R|date=2024-11-08}}</ref>
