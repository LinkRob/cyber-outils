'''Ashas Rine''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="THR 9" /> located in the [[Ashas Rine system]] within the [[Mortex sector]] of<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It was situated in grid square R-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

In the aftermath of the [[Valo Atrocity]]<ref name="THR 9" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> the [[Talpini]] [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Zeetar]] was spotted by [[Togruta intelligence]] on Ashas Rine. The [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Galactic Republic]] had identified<ref name="THR 9" /> him<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> as a potential candidate for becoming [[Eye of the Nihil]] in the wake of the presumed [[death]] of [[Lourna Dee]],<ref name="THR 9" /> another [[Tempest]] Runner they had mistaken as the Nihil's leader,<ref name="Rising Storm" /> at the [[Battle of Galov]]. [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] reported on the sighting of Zeetar at a briefing at the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]], displaying a [[hologram]] image of the Tempest Runner on Ashas Rine.<ref name="THR 9" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ashas Rine first appeared in a hologram, in flashbacks, in the [[The High Republic (2021) 9|ninth issue]] of the [[2021]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']], released as part of<ref name="THR 9" /> the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Ario Anindito]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="THR 9" /> on [[September 1]], 2021.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/94642/star_wars_the_high_republic_2021_9|text=Star Wars: The High Republic (2021) #9}}</ref>
