An '''ancient Sith ritual altar''' came into the possession of [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] by the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the [[Clone Wars]].

==Description==
A [[crystal]]line structure atop a sturdy base of a [[Stone|rock]] or metallic makeup, the ancient [[Sith]] ritual altar was of a [[Color|pink]] and dark hue.<ref name="Complete Locations" />

==History==
Found on [[Ziost]], the [[Sith]] ritual altar was in the possession of [[Darth Sidious]] during the [[Clone Wars]], who stored it in a desolate structure in [[The Works]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] before relocating it, along with the bulk of [[Darth Sidious' collection|his personal collection]], to the secret [[Sith retreat]] within the [[Grand Republic Medical Facility]] after the renegade [[clone trooper]] [[Fives|CT-5555's]] [[Escape from the Grand Republic Medical Facility|escape from the facility]].<ref name="Complete Locations" />
