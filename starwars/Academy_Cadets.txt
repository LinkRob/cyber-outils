"'''Academy Cadets'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Martin Fisher]], drawn by [[Eva Widermann]] and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. The story was published in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 18|eighteenth issue]] of the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]] series by [[Egmont]] UK Ltd, which was released on [[May 18]], [[2016]].

According to Fisher, the story takes place between the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episodes "[[Legends of the Lasat]]" and "[[The Call]]."<ref name="Twit">{{Twitter|tronprogram2|status/730464194366148608|[[Martin Fisher]]|quote=@TMBGeeks its between Legacy of Lasan and The Call.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/C8vZi}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
A group of Imperial cadets, including a male cadet named [[Jeken]] and a female cadet named [[Reann Tomvig]], are stranded after their ship is damaged by an explosion. They send out a distress signal, and the crew of the ''Ghost'' (the rebels) comes to their rescue. Jeken sees this as an opportunity to capture the rebels and hand them over to the Empire to earn a commendation. However, Reann Tomvig has a change of heart after meeting the rebels and refuses to harm those who came to their aid. When Jeken orders her to stun the rebels with a blaster, she turns on him instead and neutralizes him. The rebels allow the other cadets to go free. The Imperial cadets eventually find their way to Admiral Kassius Konstantine's Star Destroyer, the ''[[Relentless (Star Destroyer)|Relentless]]''. Jeken reports the incident to the Admiral, detailing his plan and Tomvig's betrayal, which earns him praise from [[Kassius Konstantine|Konstantine]].
