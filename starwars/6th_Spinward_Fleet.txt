The '''6th Spinward Fleet''' was a numbered [[fleet]] of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] that was active during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the final stages of the war, Alliance [[shock trooper]] [[Carasynthia Dune]] trained under veteran fighters of the fleet and saw most of her action after the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], clearing up Imperial [[warlord]] strongholds.<ref name="Mando VG" />
