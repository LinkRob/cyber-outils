'''Dave Young''' was the special effects supervisor for [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars:'' Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. He designed and built the highly adaptable rigging used for the models of the [[XJ-6 airspeeder|XJ-6]] and [[Koro-2 all-environment exodrive airspeeder|''Koro''-2 all-environment exodrive]] [[Airspeeder/Legends|airspeeders]] for filming the chase through [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].<ref name="Mythmaking">''[[Mythmaking: Behind the Scenes of Attack of the Clones]]''</ref>
