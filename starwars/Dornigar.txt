'''Dornigar''' is a fictional lead character in the [[holodrama]] ''[[The Adventures of Flux Magnetar]]''. The series released at least twenty nine episodes<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> In the series, after [[Flux Magnetar]] led a failed uprising against the [[King]] on [[Bolaxia]]. Dornigar was ordained a judge and jury over Flux. And sentenced Flux to trial by combat in the burning pits of [[Mount Vardosh]]. However Flux survive the ordeal and defeated Dornigar, seeing them fall to their apparent doom. However Dornigar survived. When Flux captured the King's fortress, Dornigar took [[Pema]], Flux's love interest, hostage.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dornigar's voice first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
