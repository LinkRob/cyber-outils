'''Cordylleum''' was an explosive substance that could be stored in kegs. During the [[Rebellion on Akiva|rebellion]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Special Forces|Special Forces]] [[Sergeant major]] [[Jom Barell]] mentally compared the rising anti-[[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] sentiment and tension amongst the population of the world's [[capital]] [[city]], [[Myrra]], to a keg of cordylleum that was about explode.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cordylleum was mentioned in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
