'''''Amazing Heroes'' 13''' was the thirteenth issue of the comic book fan magazine, ''[[Amazing Heroes]]'', released in [[July]] [[1982]].

The issue featured an article about the history of Star Wars in the comics and comic strips, with excerpts from interviews of comic strip writer [[Archie Goodwin]] and then-current Marvel series writer, [[David Michelinie]]. Michelinie discusses the challenge of writing between movies without knowing what would happen in the upcoming [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|"Revenge" of the Jedi]].

The issue also included the first installment of a reprint of ''[[The Serpent Masters]]'' comic strip story, a ''Star Wars'' cover by [[Kevin Nowlan]], and other non-''Star Wars'' content.
