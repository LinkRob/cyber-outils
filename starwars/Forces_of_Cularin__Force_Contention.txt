"'''Forces of Cularin: Force Contention'''" is a roleplaying-game adventure in year three of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It is the second adventure of the [[Forces of Cularin trilogy]] and was written by [[Matthew Domville]] and edited by [[Jae Walker]].

==Opening crawl==
''In the Cularin system and everywhere else, the Jedi Knights have always been regarded as the guardians of peace and order in the galaxy. Now, however, dark rumors are spreading – tales of an order run mad with its own power and arrogance. It is incumbent upon the Jedi – and those who consider themselves their friends – to prove otherwise.''

==Plot summary==
A group of heroes are contacted by [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Kirlocca]] and asked to investigate the activities of Jedi [[Nek Lawsirk]] who was recorded killing civilians in a cantina on [[Cularin/Legends|Cularin]]. In actuality, it was a [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]] impersonating him. The heroes investigate initially by checking out the [[Jaraana irstat]] where Lawsirk had been negotiating for the [[t'salak]], his apartment in [[Hedrett]] and other clues. These eventually lead the heroes to [[Tilnes]], where Lawsirk has been help captive by the Dark Jedi and taken over by the t'salak. The heroes best Lawsirk, but the Dark Jedi escapes.
