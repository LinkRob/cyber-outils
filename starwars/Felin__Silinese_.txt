'''Felin''' was a [[Silinese]] warrior who,<ref name="TCC" /> in [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> when he was between the ages of fifteen and twenty-five, participated in [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|that year's]] [[Crowning Convocation]]. During the [[great hunt]] portion of the competition, Felin helped [[Sitia Arthyst]] fight off a [[lylek]], a dangerous creature let loose into the arena by [[Orisent's mercenaries|criminals]]. During the battle with the creature, Felin was inoculated by the lylek's [[poison]] after being struck by one of its [[limb|tentacles]]. Following the fight, Felin was incapacitated by the poison, diaphoretic and seething in pain.<ref name="TCC" />
