'''Dennis Schmidt-Foss''' is a voice actor who provided additional voices for the audio dramas [[Dark Lord (Audio drama)|''Dark Lord'']] and [[Erben des Imperiums|''Heir to the Empire'']].<ref name="Dark Lord" /> Schmidt-Foss would later voice [[Kanan Jarrus]] in the [[German Rebels audio dramas]].<ref name="German Rebels">[[German Rebels audio dramas]]</ref>
