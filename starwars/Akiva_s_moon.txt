A [[stone|rocky]] '''moon''' of the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]],<ref name="Outlaws" />  which was located in the [[Akiva system]] within the [[Meerian sector]] of<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] and [[New Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was situated in grid square O-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="NewTerritories" />

==Behind the scenes==
The [[moon]] first appeared in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />  Prior to the game's release on [[August 30]], [[2024]],<ref name="Official Story Trailer">{{SWYouTube|DUyk6D5rgcE|Star Wars Outlaws: Official Story Trailer}}</ref> the moon was first pictured in the game's "Star Wars Outlaws: Official Gameplay Showcase" trailer that was released on [[June 10]], 2024.<ref name="Building An Open World">{{SWYouTube|R_O1eqWWbe4|Star Wars Outlaws: Official Gameplay Showcase &#124; Ubisoft Forward}}</ref>
