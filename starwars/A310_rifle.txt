The '''A310 rifle''' was a model of [[blaster rifle]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]], as part of the [[A-series]]. It had a perforated, air-cooling [[barrel-shroud]] that was [[Color|white]] in color while the rest of the rifle was black. The weapon also included a [[scope]], and a strap that attached to the end of the barrel. In the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the [[Britarro]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Nik Hepho]] carried a A310 with him while he worked in [[Jedha City]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]].<ref name="ROUVG" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The A310 rifle first appeared as a prop in the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which was released in North America on [[December 16]], [[2016]]. It was not identified in the film, but received a name in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on the same day.
