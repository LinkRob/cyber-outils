'''Arvik Cornade''' was a businessman during the [[Imperial Era]]. He was the founder and chief executive of [[Cornade Mining and Assembly]], a highly-profitable company<ref name="MoF" /> by [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|10}}</ref> He had spent a considerable amount of time with [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], having known him since he was Senator of [[Naboo]].<ref name="MoF" />

In 19 BBY,<ref name="Date" /> Cornade met with [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], along with [[Baron]] [[Shkan-Shkan Yew]] and [[Hasalia Prederiko]] in the [[Coruscant]] moon [[Hesperidium]] to discuss Mothma's [[Imperial Rebirth Act]].<ref name="MoF" />
