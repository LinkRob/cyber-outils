The '''FRC-40''' was a model of [[Forward ReCon fighter]]. The FRC-40 was an older and smaller version of the current FRC that was being produced in [[382 BBY]].

==Description==
The ship had 3 wings, with the middle set being larger and equipped with forward-facing guns. It had a two-seat cockpit and an [[astromech droid]] [[Astromech socket|socket]] that was accessible from the bottom of the hull. The ship was also equipped with a dorsal tail cannon.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==History==
[[Xiri A'lbaran]], [[Phan-tu Zenn]], and [[4VO-TG]] found a FRC-40, designated [[G-2]], in a barn on [[Dalna]]. After some hasty field repairs, the group managed to get the ship working and used it during the [[Night of Sorrow]] battle.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
