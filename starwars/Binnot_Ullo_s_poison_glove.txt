'''Binnot Ullo's poison glove''' was a [[glove]] worn by [[Binnot Ullo]] that contained multiple types of [[poison]].<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> it contained an [[unidentified droid poison]] as well as a poison that could kill [[humanoid]] species in less than a [[Standard minute|minute]]. It was a [[Color|dark]] glove that reached to the elbow of the wearer. During the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]], it was intended to carry [[Eirami poison|Erami poison]], which was substituted after [[Axel Greylark]] [[Skirmish at the wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran|stole the poison]]. The glove was embedded with tiny [[needles]] that would imbue the poison stored in vials in its sleeve. The needles extended when the owner's hand squeezed inside the glove, and retracted when the fingers were extended.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
