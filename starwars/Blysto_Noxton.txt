'''Blysto Noxton''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] avant garde [[holovid]] [[artist]]<ref name="SoloOG" /> who in [[10 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on [[Vandor]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to nine years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]], therefore the party aboard ''[[First Light]]'' attended by Blyston Noxton while it was moored on [[Vandor]] must take place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> attended a party aboard ''[[First Light]]'', the star [[yacht]] of [[Crimson Dawn]]'s leader [[Dryden Vos]] while the [[Starship|ship]] was moored on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]].<ref name="Solo" /> He considered his partygoing habits research for the masterpiece holovid he planned to create.<ref name="SoloOG" />

Noxton viewed himself as a deep thinker, and he had a high opinion of his own work, expecting his future holovid to be a masterpiece. He wore a black magnetic amulet which he incorrectly believed helped focus his thoughts, along with white trousers paired with an open-chested black and white [[robe]] made out of [[fineweave sherculién-cloth]].<ref name="SoloOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blyston Noxton first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Solo" /> and was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" />
