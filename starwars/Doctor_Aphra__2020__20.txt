'''''Doctor Aphra'' 20''' is the twentieth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 18]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''FROM HERE TO ETERNITY! DOCTOR APHRA and SANA STARROS confront KHO PHON FARRUS at the heart of an ARCHAEOLOGICAL RUIN! But they're all about to learn the horrifying truth behind the SPARK ETERNAL&hellip;and ONLY ONE will escape unscathed!''

As Kho Phon Farrus makes their way to Bar'leth University in search of the fabled Ascendant tech called the Spark Eternal, their school
