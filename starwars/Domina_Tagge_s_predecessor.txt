{{Quote|It's certainly an improvement over my predecessor's attempt to replicate a ''Jedi's weapon''. This iteration is sleek. Elegant. Compact. Terribly ''volatile'', and just as ''expensive''. The Empire will never pay for it.|Domina Tagge, on the Tagge protoblade|<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" />}}
An individual served as '''head''' of the [[Tagge Corporation]] operated by the [[House of Tagge]]. During their [[time]] as head of the company, the individual created a replication of a [[Jedi]] [[lightsaber]].<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]] served as the [[Executive officer|leader]] of Tagge Corporation.<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Domina tested the [[Tagge protoblade]]&mdash;a [[weapon]] which had been created by the company&mdash;against her [[Honor Guard (Domina Tagge)|Honor Guard]]. After the test, Domina noted that while the protoblade was an improvement over her predecessor's attempts to replicate a lightsaber, it was volatile and expensive.<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" />
