'''Aidan Cook''' is a puppeteer and actor who worked as a creature and droid performer for [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], and ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''. His characters include [[Bobbajo]], [[Strono Tuggs]], [[Edrio]], [[Benthic]] in all his appearances, [[Caitken]], [[Boolio]] and [[Quadpaw|Doctor Quadpaw]] .
