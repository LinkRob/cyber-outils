The '''Arena News''' was a media coverage that published various news, interviews, and events of the [[Grand Arena]].<ref name="Rieve Wrath" />

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Arena News is an [[in-universe]] news coverage being hosted on the ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' official website.<ref name="Rieve Wrath" /> It was first mentioned in an update blogpost on [[March 28]], [[2024]].<ref name="Arena news Announced">{{Hunters|url=news/game-update-march-28th-2024|text=Game Update: March 28, 2024}}</ref> Arena News later posted their first article on [[April 16]], 2024.<ref name="First Edition" />

Two articles, "Opportunity Nox" and "Survival At All Costs" were later posted to the ''Star Wars: Hunters'' website, with the incorrect dates listed.<ref name="Opportunity Nox">{{Hunters|url=news/arena-news/opportunity-nox|text=Opportunity Nox}}</ref><ref name="Survival At All Costs">{{Hunters|url=news/arena-news/survival-at-all-costs|text=Survival At All Costs}}</ref> One Arena News article, "Star Wars: Hunters - Important Message," is not an in-universe page<ref name="Important Message">{{ArenaNews|url=3e377700457a4d79a1c5fe269ff04b7c-en-gb|text=Star Wars: Hunters - Important Message}}</ref> and is instead a repost of the ''Star Wars: Hunters'' article "Sunset on Vespaara."<ref name="Sunset on Vespaara">{{Hunters|url=news/sunset-on-vespaara|text=Sunset on Vespaara}}</ref>

Several articles are tied to in-game Clash events, however alternate versions were written for the other potential winner. These alternate versions were not accessible in-game.<ref name="Grozz the Great">{{ArenaNews|url=9e2dc219c99f47e48b5cce0ec5f78c61-en-gb|text=Grozz the Great}}</ref><ref name="Exceptional Ewok">{{ArenaNews|url=ee5a648b850945f2a8e4ba4ea8e0db7d-en-gb|text=Exceptional Ewok}}</ref><ref name="Victory for Rieve">{{ArenaNews|url=f018382938ea42fd82932b10fb9b2cbc-en-gb|text=Victory for Rieve}}</ref><ref name="Bullseye for Nox">{{ArenaNews|url=f4cce0397ffe4723b30362090a29b31d-en-gb|text=Bullseye for Nox}}</ref><ref name="Kudos to the Kubaz">{{ArenaNews|url=5e972d861a7342ceb6dcc992bc4ae716-en-gb|text=Kudos to the Kubaz}}</ref><ref name="Jubilant Jawas">{{ArenaNews|url=27e3d4f350754628a44c89593e3e6e89-en-gb|text=Jubilant Jawas}}</ref>

===Contradictions===
Both ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''<ref name="SWH">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> and Arena News articles referred to Tal as the newest Hunter when Rieve was already an established Hunter;<ref name="Tal Me About It">{{ArenaNews|url=56adfce346974e1b80272941d65a00f0-en-gb|text=Tal Me About It}}</ref> however, the [[canon]] tie-in novel ''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]'' revealed that [[Aran Tal]] was an established Hunter by the time [[Rieve]] joined the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] roster.<ref name="BftA">''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]''</ref> As the tie-in novel provided a better cohesive [[in-universe]] narrative, this article assumes it to be the correct version of events.

In ''Hunters: Battle for the Arena'', it was established that Frontline was already in operation as [[Rieve]]&mdash;a new [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] at the time&mdash;was shown battling in the battlefield.<ref name="BftA" /> However, an Arena News article titled "A New Battlefield" stated that Hoth Frontline was still in the process of being built.<ref name="A New Battlefield">{{ArenaNews|url=0602a28bcf744fd19be6216d638e2b25-en-gb|text=A New Battlefield}}</ref> As the novel provides a more cohesive [[in-universe]] narrative, this article assumes ''Hunters: Battle for the Arena'' to be the correct version of events.
