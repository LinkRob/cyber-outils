'''Charra crop''' was a a type of [[plant]] grown on the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. The crop doesn't seed on Akiva until after the planet's [[monsoon season]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a pair of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s harassed and threaten a [[Mon Calamari]] Charra [[farmer]] in [[Myrra]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of Akiva, for being late on their taxes.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Charra crop was mentioned in an ambient conversation the player can overhear at the [[Myrra Spaceport]] in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
