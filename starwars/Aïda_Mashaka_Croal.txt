'''Aïda Mashaka Croal''' is a television writer who, along with [[Matt Michnovetz]], wrote four un-produced episodes of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''. Following the cancellation of ''The Clone Wars'', the episodes&mdash;"The Enemy of My Enemy," "A Tale of Two Apprentices," "Proxy War," and "Showdown on Dathomir"&mdash;were adapted into the four-part [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Darth Maul&mdash;Son of Dathomir]]'' in [[2014]].<ref name="SoD TPB">[[Star Wars: Darth Maul &mdash; Son of Dathomir (TPB)|''Star Wars: Darth Maul&mdash;Son of Dathomir'' trade paperback]]</ref>
