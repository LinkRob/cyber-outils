The '''AT-ET Dasher''' was an external [[All Terrain Ewok Transport]] [[walker]] configuration utilized by the [[Ewok]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Pilbush]] that she could operate for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Original AT-ET|standard walker]].<ref name="SWH" />

{{Veh-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
AT-ET Dasher was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Pilbush's Original AT-ET, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dashing Ewok]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="SWH" />
