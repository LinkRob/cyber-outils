{{Quote|Worry not. Throughout my long life, I have seen the galaxy endure many changes. Tyranny is always defeated. We must remain hopeful.|Eeko-Dio Daki, to Devon Izara|<ref name="Inquisition">{{MSL|Chapter 5: Inquisition}}</ref>}}
'''Eeko-Dio Daki''' was a [[gender|male]] [[Mosyk]] [[Jedi Master]] who lived in [[the galaxy]] before and during the [[Imperial Era]]. The [[Twi'lek]] [[Devon Izara]] studied as Daki's [[Padawan]] during the final days of the [[Galactic Republic]], and they went on the run together in the wake of [[Order 66]], to escape the [[Proclamation of the New Order|newly formed]] [[Galactic Empire|Galactic Empire's]] [[Great Jedi Purge]].  Hiding on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet|world]] of [[Janix]] together by [[17 BBY]], Izara was arrested when she attempted to steal food for her virtuous Jedi Master, who insisted that the kindness of strangers could always be relied on instead. She was then kidnapped from the [[Janix Civil Defense HQ|Police HQ]] of [[Janix Civil Defense]] by [[Maul]], a former [[Sith]] turned renegade [[crime lord]] out for revenge and seeking a new apprentice. Daki attempted to rescue his Padawan, but Maul forced him to choose to save civilians instead by collapsing a pedestrian walkway. Izara later escaped [[Maul's safe house]] by herself, and informed Daki the [[Zabrak]] wanted to fight their common enemy, the Empire. Ever principled, Daki counseled that Maul could never be the Jedi's ally. Ultimately, Daki would be killed by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] after being betrayed by Maul.

==Biography==
===Fugitive===
{{Quote|Uh, pardon, would you be so kind?"''<br/>''"E Chu ta|Eeko-Dio Daki unsuccessfully begs a Janix resident.|<ref name="MSL S1E1"/>}}
Eeko-Dio Daki was a [[Gender|male]] [[Mosyk]]<ref name="Databank"/> [[Jedi Master]] who lived in [[the galaxy]] before and during the [[Imperial Era]]. The [[Twi'lek]] teenager<ref name="Interview">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-cast|text=Meet the Cast of ''Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord''}}</ref> [[Devon Izara]] [[Jedi training|studied]] as Daki's [[Padawan]] during the final days of the [[Galactic Republic]], and was well trained by Daki.<ref name="Izara DB"/> The pair went on the run together in the wake of [[Order 66]], to escape the ascendant [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Great Jedi Purge|Jedi Purge]].<ref name="Databank" /> By [[17 BBY]],<ref name="ShadowLordDate">{{ShadowLordDate}}</ref> the pair were in hiding on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Shadow of Maul 1">[[Shadow of Maul 1|''Shadow of Maul'' 1]]</ref> of [[Janix]].

[[File:MSL-Chapter5-Thumbnail.png|thumb|right|300px|Izara's desire to take action clashed with Daki, want to hide.]]
One night, the renegade former [[Sith]] turned [[crime lord]] [[Maul]] orchestrated a [[raid on a bank tower]]. Daki and Izara watched as [[Tactical Defense Force]] [[airspeeder]]s sped by on their way to respond, and the Jedi Master told his Padawan it was not their concern, then looked longingly at [[fruit]] in a stall operated by a [[Didynon]]. Noticing this, Izara offered to steal some, but Daki rejected the prospect and insisted that they would always be able to rely on strangers' kindness instead. When he asked a passerby for aid, however, he was rudely rebuffed. Izara then went ahead with shoplifting but was promptly caught by a passing [[police droid]], who charged her with theft and arrested her. Daki tried to explain it was a simple mistake, but the [[droid]] refused to budge. With avoiding a disturbance imperative for the fugitives, Daki convinced his Padawan to comply and reassured her that the police would soon see reason and release her. Before surrendering herself, Izara handed her cloak to Daki. As the droids led her away, Daki sighed.<ref name="MSL S1E1">{{MSL|Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge}}</ref>

The next day, Daki waited patiently on the streets below the Janix Civil Defense Police HQ as Izara was processed and [[Prisoner|imprisoned]]. That night, he sat cross-legged through a storm as rain pelted down and lightning illuminated him. At the same time, Maul and his criminal associates [[Raid on the Janix Civil Defense HQ|infiltrated]] the police building, [[death|slaying]] numerous personnel and police droids in order to reach the cell of a local [[syndicate]] leader they intended to kill, [[Looti Vario]]. Through the Force, Daki sensed Izara's distress at the ongoing carnage and looked up towards the tower. Maul successfully made his way to Vario, taking the [[Aleena]] alive, before entering Izara's cell across the hall, sensing an opportunity to gain a new apprentice.<ref name="MSL S1E1" />

===Fight and flight===
{{Quote|Hey buddy. You alright?|Brander Lawson to Eeko-Dio Daki as he strains to hold up a collapsing walkway|<ref name="MSL S1E2">{{MSL|Chapter 2: Sinister Schemes}}</ref>}}
With their raid successful, Maul and his [[Nightbrothers|Nightbrother]] and [[Mandalorian]] associates fled with their kidnapped prisoners in a stolen [[bank transport]] and a smaller airspeeder, attracting police attention. Eeko-Dio Daki watched the fleeing vehicles from an overhead bridge, then [[Force jump|Force jumped]] onto the highway, causing the smaller vehicle carrying the Zabraks and Vario to flip and crash and the bank transport to collide with the smaller vehicle. Maul emerged to face off with Master Daki as police forces led by [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]], who had been investigating Maul's criminal activities, converged on the crash site. However, Maul distracted his pursuers by using [[the Force]] to split a nearby pedestrian bridge. Looking away from Izara, Daki used his Force powers to suspend the bridge structure long enough for Lawson and his officers to evacuate the civilians, grunting and straining as he did so, and allowing Maul to get away. When all the civilians were safe, Lawson looked back to where Daki had been on the highway just moments earlier, only for the Jedi to be gone.<ref name="MSL S1E2"/>

Later, after meeting with [[Rheena Sul]] to find out more about Maul's former syndicate, the [[Shadow Collective]], Lawson walked down an alleyway and sensed he was being followed. Indeed, Master Daki was following him, but when Lawson turned around with his blaster drawn, there was again nobody there. Getting into his airspeeder, Lawson flew off, leaving Daki looking up after him.<ref name="MSL S1E2"/>

Sometime after this, Daki found Lawson once again at his son [[Rylee Lawson|Rylee's]] [[shockball]] match. Lawson asked why Daki was following him, to which Daki assured Lawson that he meant him no harm. Daki hoped to discuss Izara, who he claimed was her niece, asserting that she was an innocent bystander targeted by the dangerous Maul and telling Lawson that Maul was not like anything he had dealt with before. Daki also revealed that Maul's intentions with Izara were mysterious to him as well. As Daki moved to leave, he suggested that the Empire might make Lawson's job easier; Lawson deflected, voicing his trepidation of the Empire's involvement, and guessed that Daki also was no fan of the Empire either. After Lawson received a call about an event at the [[Janix Spaceport|Janix spaceport]], he turned to find that Daki had disappeared once more.<ref name="MSL C3">{{MSL|Chapter 3: Whispers in the Unknown}}</ref>

In the meantime, Daki searched the city for his Padawan.<ref name="MSL C4">{{MSL|Chapter 4: Pride and Vengeance}}</ref> Eventually, Izara escaped Maul's safe house.<ref name="MSL S1E2" /> That night, as Daki wandered the streets, he sensed Izara's presence and reunited with her with a hug. After checking Izara's wounded arm, the two sought refuge in the sewers where Daki had kept Izara's belongings - including her lightsaber - safe. The two discussed Maul, with Daki believing that they should leave Janix immediately to avoid the Empire. Izara, however, believed that they should stay to bring Maul to justice, invoking their duty as Jedi, though Daki recognized that the galaxy's people no longer saw the Jedi as authorities on justice. Izara suggested that they could work with Janix's local authorities, including Lawson, to overcome Maul's mercenary allies. Already familiar with Lawson, Daki sent a tip containing Izara's information on Maul's whereabouts to the Tactical Defense Force under the alias "Uncle Daki." The next day, Lawson immediately acted on the information, gathering a strike team to confront Maul.<ref name="MSL C4" />

===Confronting Maul===
Anticipating that Lawson would take action, Daki and Izara made their way to Maul's safehouse the next night. Izara was unsure that the Tactical Defense Force would come; Daki admitted that nothing in life was certain, but that he was confident that Lawson would act. At that moment, Lawson and his strike team arrived. The two Jedi observed Maul make an offer to Lawson via hologram and moved about the safehouse in an attempt to locate Maul. As negotiations between Maul's forces and the Tactical Defense Force broke down and fighting began, Daki and Izara found Maul. After Maul greeted them, Daki introduced himself and gave Maul an offer just as Lawson had&mdash;if Maul were to surrender to the Jedi, they would not harm him. Maul mocked Daki's passive ideology, pointing out the Jedi's downfall, but Daki was determined that Maul would face justice even during such dark times. Maul and Daki debated on what it meant to survive and live, with Maul believing that the Jedi's fall from grace at the hands of inferior beings was not befitting of their power; Daki held fast to his belief that the Jedi existed only to serve others. After Daki called Maul delusional for claiming the Jedi's restraint was a weakness, all three ignited their lightsabers, with Daki advancing first.<ref name="MSL C4" />

[[File:Devon duels Maul.png|thumb|left|300px|Daki was injured by Maul during their fight.]]
As the three dueled, Maul focused on Izara, using powerful blows to eject Daki from the fighting. As the duel advanced, Daki kicked Maul in his cybernetic right leg, which began to falter much to Maul's frustration. Angered, Maul pushed Daki backwards onto a staircase, kicking Daki and causing him to fall, where the Jedi sustained a painful injury to his midsection. Maul pounced on Daki, but Izara jumped to block Maul's killing blow. Maul voiced that Izara's master was fortunate to have her by his side. As Maul's forces moved to evacuate and evade Lawson's reinforcements, Maul lingered for a moment, holding his lightsaber blade to Izara's face before leaving Daki and Izara alive. Daki held Izara back from pursuing Maul.<ref name="MSL C4" />

As the dust settled, Lawson ran to Daki and Izara's position, hoping to find Maul. Holding her injured master upright, Izara ignited her lightsaber as Lawson discovered them. At Lawson's asking, Izara informed Lawson that Maul had fled and retracted her saber. Lawson then asked why Daki had not told him that he and Izara were Jedi, to which Daki bluntly cited obvious reasons. Lawson handed Daki his own dropped lightsaber and urged the two to go as Tactical Defense Force reinforcements arrived. By morning, Daki and Izara recovered to the safety and obscurity of Janix's alleyways. When the two stopped, Izara asked Daki if he was alright, to which Daki apologized to his Padawan, believing he had failed her.<ref name="MSL C4" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You're hungry, you need to eat. They won't miss it.''"<br />"''I told you already. We do not steal.''"<br />"''I'm tired of begging.''"<br />"''We could always rely on the kindness of strangers.|Devon Izara argues with her Jedi Master, Eeko-Dio Daki.|<ref name="MSL S1E1" />}}
Eeko-Dio Daki was a loyal follower to the tenets of the Jedi Order. Long-lived and rather traditional, he was still a fierce warrior and adept duelist when the need arose.<ref name="Databank"/> His desire to remain hidden whilst in hiding on Janix sometimes conflicted with the desire of his young Padawan, Devon Izara, to take action.<ref name="Izara DB">{{Databank|devon-izara|Devon Izara}}</ref>

Master Daki refused to compromise his values and steal food, even when starving, and remained strong in his belief in the kindness of others. When his Padawan attempted to steal food for him despite his protestations, and was promptly discovered and arrested, he maintained his faith in others' better nature, assuring her that the police would soon see reason. Daki was also patient, [[Meditation|meditating]] in the rain whilst waiting for Izara's release.<ref name="MSL S1E1" /> When he confronted Maul in an attempt to rescue his recently kidnapped Padawan, and the [[Dark side of the Force|darksider]] destroyed a populated pedestrian bridge with the Force as a distraction, Daki choose to use his own Force powers to hold the collapsing structure up long enough for the many imperiled civilians to be rescued, rather than rescue his own apprentice.<ref name="MSL S1E2"/>

Daki had mottled green and gray skin, and gray [[eye]]s, with white [[hair|whiskers]] on his chin. As a Moysk, his two [[Limb|hands]] had three clawed fingers each, and his [[head]] had an elongated crest at the back.<ref name="MSL S1E1"/> Daki stood 1.51 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Databank" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a Jedi Master, Eeko-Dio Daki was a skilled Force user. He was able to use the energy field that bound the galaxy together<ref name="Episode IV2">{{Film|IV}}</ref> to jump great heights, flip a speeding vehicle, and, with some difficulty, telekinetically hold up a collapsing pedestrian bridge.<ref name="MSL S1E2"/> He was also a fierce warrior and duelist capable of using multiple lightsaber forms, and carried both a makeshift cane and a [[Eeko-Dio Daki's lightsaber|hidden blue]] lightsaber.<ref name="Databank"/>

==Equipment==
Daki wore a [[flight suit]] beneath a hooded [[cloak]] and carried a length of pipe as a makeshift [[cane]], as well as a concealed [[Color|blue]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki's lightsaber|lightsaber]] that he was adept with.<ref name="Databank"/>

Master Daki carried a makeshift cane constructed from a length of pipe, as well as a concealed, blue-bladed lightsaber. He wore a green and white flight suit beneath a dark hooded cloak.<ref name="Databank"/>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Eeko-Dio Daki Concept Art.jpg|thumb|right|350 px|Concept art of Eeko-Dio Daki by [[Chris Madden]].]]
Eeko-Dio Daki appeared in the [[Disney+]] television show ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]''. The character was voiced by [[Dennis Haysbert]].<ref name="teaser sw.com">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-first-trailer-poster-art|text=Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord Teaser Trailer and First Poster Arrive}}</ref> Daki was first featured in the teaser trailer for the show released on [[January 22]], [[2026]],<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|oonACDq89BY|Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord &#124; Official Teaser Trailer &#124; Streaming April 6 on Disney+}}</ref> and was first identified in the article that accompanied the teaser's release, on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="teaser sw.com" /> However, the character's name and likeness were not linked until the release of an article on StarWars.com alongside the official trailer for the show on [[March 16]], which featured a poster and a description of Daki's location on it.<ref name="Trailer sw.com">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-trailer|text=Watch the Official Trailer for ''Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord''}}</ref> A trivia article on StarWars.com revealed that Daki was lovingly referred to as "Dino Jedi" before being given his official name during the show's development.<ref name="MSL making">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-trivia|text=Inside the Making of ''Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord''}}</ref>
