{{Quote|Since when does the ''First Order'' work with a half-rate slicer like Finwej? It looks like we have a ''fraud'' on our&mdash;|Mek Nu'Tiv|<ref name="PDA2" />}}
'''Finwej''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[slicer]].<ref name="PDA2" /> Sometime between [[32 ABY]] and [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">[[Poe Dameron Annual 2|''Poe Dameron Annual'' 2]] is set after the formation of [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] in [[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]], but prior to the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' places the formation of Black [[Squadron]] to two [[Standard year|years]] before the events of ''The Force Awakens'', which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the events of ''Poe Dameron Annual'' 2, including the mention of Finwej, must take place between [[32 ABY]] and 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] acquired [[First Order]] codes from Finwej in order to [[Heist of the Alderaanian data archive|steal]] a [[Archive of the Great Library of Alderaan|data archive]] from the [[Merchant|trader]] [[Mek Nu'Tiv]], as he was the only slicer they could afford to pay. The smugglers met with Nu'Tiv, disguised as [[First Order officer|officers]] of the First Order, and the trader recognized Finwej's codes. Nu'Tiv, who believed that Finwej was an inadequate slicer and doubted that the First Order would work with him, denounced Solo as a fraud.<ref name="PDA2" />
