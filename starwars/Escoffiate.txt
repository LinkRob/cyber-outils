An '''escoffiate''' [[Hat|headpiece]] was worn by [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]] with her [[Senate gown]] for a speech in the [[Galactic Senate]]. Symbolic of the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] culture, her escoffiate incorporated the [[Royal Sovereign of Naboo medal]], a [[Hair|wig]] styled to resemble [[guarlara]] horns with [[gold]]en hairbands and hairtip ornaments, and a cap with [[orichalc]] finework [[suspensa]]s.

==Description==
[[File:AmidalaSenateGown.png|thumb|left|200px|Queen Amidala wearing an escoffiate]]
[[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] owned an escoffiate [[Hat|headpiece]].<ref name="TCVD NE" /> Her escoffiate incorporated the [[Royal Sovereign of Naboo medal]] in the center of a [[Color|dark brown]] [[Hair|wig]] that was divided and styled to resemble the horns of a [[guarlara]] creature, with a smaller amount of hair gathered behind her head.<ref name="SWTVE" /> Red and blue [[gemstone]]s<ref name="Episode I" /> studded the [[gold]]en hairbands that encircled the gathered hair. The ends of the two "horns" were accented with grand finial hairtip ornaments, cylindrical in shape and colored red with gold filigree. Her close-fitting gold cap had the [[Symbol of the Royal House of Naboo]] centered on her forehead and [[orichalc]] finework [[suspensa]]s attached above her temples; the gold-colored rope chains dangled to the same length as the hairtip ornaments to frame her face.<ref name="TCVD NE" />

==History==
In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Queen]] Amidala wore an escoffiate to address the [[Galactic Senate]] on the [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[capital]] [[Coruscant]] after her [[homeworld]] was [[Invasion of Naboo|invaded]] by the [[Trade Federation]].<ref name="Episode I" /> Worn in conjunction with her [[Senate gown]], her [[Clothing|attire]] expressed the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] culture through the regal trappings of the [[Royal House of Naboo]], but it also helped her to maintain her stoic composure.<ref name="TCVD NE" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|We felt this headdress was worth the effort, weight and expense of having real gold.|Trisha Biggar, costume designer|<ref name="Costume Design">{{SWArchive|url=episode-i/feature/19990501/indexp10.html|text=''Star Wars'' Episode I: Production Notes: Costume Design}}</ref>}}
Padmé Amidala's escoffiate first appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Episode I" /> In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the escoffiate was first identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''', which was written by [[Adam Bray]], [[Cole Horton]], and [[Tricia Barr]].<ref name="SWTVE" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the name was first used in the 1999 reference book [[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary|''Star Wars: Episode I The Visual Dictionary'']], written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="TPMVD">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

Artist [[Iain McCaig]] conceptualized the design and the finished headdress was realized by costume designer [[Trisha Biggar]] and the costume department.<ref name="The Art of Episode I">''[[The Art of Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace]]''</ref> The design was inspired by [[Wikipedia:Mongolian|Mongolian]] headdresses. It was first made in [[Wikipedia:Copper|copper]] using [[Wikipedia:Electroforming|electroforming]], then [[Wikipedia:Gold plating|gold-plated]]<ref name="Dressing a Galaxy">''[[Dressing a Galaxy: The Costumes of Star Wars]]''</ref> with real [[Wikipedia:Gold|gold]] to achieve the right quality of color.<ref name="Costume Design" /> The film's marketing featured the Senate costume and headdress, such as on the sides of buses.<ref name="The One Abroad">{{SWArchive|url=episode-i/feature/20000601/index.html|text=The One Abroad}}</ref>
