'''Autopilot''' was a [[computer]] system on a [[starship]] that allowed it to be navigated without input from a [[droid]] or [[Sentience|sentient]] [[pilot]]. During the [[Battle of Naboo]], the [[N-1 starfighter]] in which [[Anakin Skywalker]] was hiding along with [[R2-D2]] engaged its autopilot to join the fight over the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. Skywalker had R2-D2 disengage the autopilot so that he could manually control the [[starfighter]].<ref name="TPM" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[brain worm]] inflected [[clone trooper]]s activated the [[TB-73|''TB-73'']]'s autopilot to prevent [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Barriss Offee]] from reaching the bridge.<ref name="BI">{{TCW|Brain Invaders}}</ref>

The [[Kaminoan flight pod]]s, also had an autopilot setting, which [[AZI-345211896246498721347|AZI-3]] and [[Advanced Recon Commando|Arc trooper]] [[Fives]] to distract the [[Kamino security]] forces while the two returned to the clone laboratories to continue their investigation into the "[[Behavioral modification biochip|tumors]]."<ref name="Fugitive">{{TCW|Fugitive}}</ref>

While chasing a giant [[kyber crystal]], Skywalker set a [[Unidentified freighter (Utapau)|freighter]]'s autopilot to crash into a [[Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel|supply ship]]'s [[hyperdrive]] to keep the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] from escaping.<ref name="The Big Bang">{{TCW|The Big Bang}}</ref>

[[Clone Force 99]], set the autopilot on their stolen [[Rho-class transport shuttle|''Rho''-class transport shuttle]], [[Alpha-44]] before it was shot down by [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|V-wing starfighters]], but not before the group manage to disembark into the forest surrounding [[Tantiss Base]].<ref name="Flash Strike">{{TBB|Flash Strike}}</ref>
