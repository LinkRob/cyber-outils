The '''Coruscanti government''' was the [[planet]]ary [[Politics|government]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[capital]] [[planet]], [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], the planet was represented by a [[Unidentified Coruscant senator|senator]] in the [[Imperial Senate]].<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> By the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Jakku]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]], Imperial [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] was the leader of the government.<ref name="Victory's Price" /> The [[Coruscanti]] government was replaced by a [[New Republic]] [[Coruscant's provisional government|provisional government]] following the war.<ref name="Empire's End" />
