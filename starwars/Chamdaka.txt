'''Chamdaka''' was a [[Gigoran]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Vader 49" /> [[cyborg]] who was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] and part of the [[M.A.R. Corps]]. Together with [[Tandorvo]], [[Blades]] and [[Masakint]], they fought the [[Galactic Empire]] at [[Zee-Nine City Seven]]. During this [[Skirmish at Zee-Nine City Seven|skirmish]], they all were corrupted by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] when he had [[droid brain]]s inserted into their [[cybernetics]].<ref name="Vader 42" />
