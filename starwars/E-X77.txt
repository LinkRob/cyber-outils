'''E-X77''',<ref name="TPB">[[Star Wars: Ewoks (trade paperback)|''Star Wars: Ewoks'' trade paperback]]</ref> also known as '''Ee-Exsevenseven''' or '''Exsev''', was an [[RA-7 protocol droid]] head that was carried around by the [[Dowutin]] [[scavenger]] [[Koll Orvenk]] and was hired along by [[Tern Koyatta]].<ref name="Ewoks 1" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
E-X77 first appeared in the [[Ewoks (2024) 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Ewoks (2024)|''Star Wars: Ewoks'']],<ref name="Ewoks 1" /> which was written by [[Steve Orlando]], illustrated by [[Laura Braga]] and [[Álvaro López]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 9]], [[2024]].<ref name="Marvel 1">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/112935/star_wars_ewoks_2024_1|text=Star Wars: Ewoks (2024) #1}}</ref> Braga created character design concepts of the droid. In the comics, the character is only refered to as Ee-Exsevenseven or Exsev, but the [[2025]] [[Star Wars: Ewoks (trade paperback)|trade paperback]] release of the miniseries introduced the spelling of E-X77.<ref name="TPB" />
