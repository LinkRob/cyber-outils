By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[hueche]]s '''attacked''' a [[Colicoid]] nest which included its [[Unidentified Colicoid queen|queen]], [[Jinnix]], and his [[Jinnix's drone-mate|drone-mate]]. During the attack, the drone-mate failed to protect the [[Queen (biology)|queen]] and afterward listed to Jinnix the reasons for why they had failed. Seeing the reasons as excuses for their failure, Jinnix ripped off the drone-mate's head and ate their ganglia. Sometime later, when Jinnix was a [[Storm (rank)|Storm]] in the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization, he informed his [[Strike]] [[Towl]] of the attack on his nest by hueches and his response to his drone-mates attempts to explain their failure to protect the [[queen]] when Towl made an excuse for not following an order.<ref name="Tempest Runner Script" />
