The '''Bronzium Paintwork''' was a bodily configuration owned by the [[droideka]] [[Team Slingshot droideka|member]] of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]] that she could swap for her standard [[Beloved Paintwork|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This configuration featured a scuffed appearance consisting of mostly [[Color|bronze]]-colored plating resembling [[bronzium]], as well as tan stripes, and exposed metallic gray components as well. The other member of team Slingshot, the [[Ugnaught]] [[Dizzy (Ugnaught)|Dizzy]], referred to this appearance as the droideka's "natural essence," claiming that just because she wasn't gussied up with the fanciest parts didn't mean she still wasn't beautiful to him.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bronzium Paintwork was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Bronzium Racer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
