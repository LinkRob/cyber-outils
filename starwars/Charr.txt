'''Charr''' was a [[Trandoshan]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Behind the Scenes" /> [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] at the [[Grand Arena]] on [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Battle for the Arena" /> As a [[big game hunter]], Charr sought out prey on many worlds, including [[Pagodon]], where he hunted [[ravinak]];<ref name="Ravinak Diver">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' {{C|Charr &mdash; Ravinak Diver Costume}}</ref> [[Felucia]], where he battled a [[sarlacc]];<ref name="Ranked Mastery Part 2">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' {{C|Ranked Mastery - Part 2}}</ref> and [[Hoth]], where he killed both [[tauntaun]]s and [[wampa]]s.<ref name="Frozen Trapper">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' {{C|Charr &mdash; Frozen Trapper Costume}}</ref><ref name="Icy Scatter Gun">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' {{C|Charr &mdash; Icy Scatter Gun}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Quote|Now, the contest begins&hellip;|Charr|<ref name="SWH" />|audio=CharrContest-SWH.wav}}
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> the [[Trandoshan]] Charr partook as a [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] in the sporting competition [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]]<ref name="SWH">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> being held on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="BftA" /> As a newcomer, Charr challenged the [[Wookiee]] Hunter [[Grozz]] for a fight, though the Trandoshan was left unanswered. While Grozz's decision left him confused, Charr became more eager for the challenge. The [[human]] Hunter [[Zaina]] took notice of Charr's bothersome behavior against Grozz, pushing her to challenge him instead. Charr then accepted the surprising challenge thrown by Zaina.<ref name="SWH" />

Charr later went on victorious against Zaina, but the Trandoshan had a newfound respect for his opponent. Despite his win, however, the confusion of Grozz's decision to ignore his challenge still lingered. Later, Grozz finally issued a statement that he would not accept challenges from upstarts who had yet to prove themselves, leaving Charr in anger and vowing revenge against the Wookiee.<ref name="SWH" /> At some point, the human Hunter [[Rieve]] accidentally barged in on Charr, who was dressing up in a [[prep pod]].<ref name="BftA">''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Some privacy, please!|Charr, to Rieve|<ref name="BftA" />}}
Charr was a [[Trandoshan]] with [[Color|green]] skin and orange [[eye]]s with a white streak on his right eye. He wore an orange sleeveless vest with yellow [[Clothing|tunic]] underneath. He decorated himself with a [[Hair|hide]] and [[Bone|skull]] of a [[nexu]] around his torso.<ref name="SWH" />

Being a big game hunter, Charr had a passion for the wilderness, a trait which he and sharpshooter [[Diago Velaar]] bonded over.<ref name="SWH" /> He also had great respect for the [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk]], a fellow Trandoshan whom Charr believed was worthy of becoming an Arena Hunter.<ref name="Krayt's Scatter Gun">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' {{C|Charr &mdash; Krayt's Scatter Gun Weapon Wrap}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Charr first appeared in the novel ''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]'',<ref name="Battle for the Arena" /> which was written by [[Mark Oshiro]] and published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[March 7]], [[2023]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-hunters-battle-for-the-arena|text=Star Wars Hunters: Battle for the Arena}}</ref> Charr was pictured for the first time in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', during update 0.18.0 as a new playable character, which released on [[September 13]] of the same year.<ref name="Update 18">{{Hunters|url=news/game-update-september-13th-2023|text=Game Update: September 13th, 2023}}</ref> He was voiced by [[Adam James]] in the game.<ref name="Behind the Scenes" />

Upon global release of ''Hunters'' on [[June 4]], [[2024]], Charr was available to unlock through the Hunter's Path progression system, where he was the eighth unlockable character. Charr is considered a level-three difficulty Tank-class Hunter. He has three active abilities. His Snare Trap keeps enemies in place when caught in its vicinity. His [[Laser Tether]] attaches Charr to an enemy to keep them near. His Regen Booster heals him and converts all incoming damage into additional healing. Charr also has a talent known as Dig In, in which his [[claw]]s reduce the effectiveness of knock-back attacks. Charr's Ultimate is Ferocious Hunt, in which his movement speed increases as he throws down his scatter gun, swiping furiously with his claws. Attacks pin enemies down, and eliminations increase its duration.<ref name="SWH" />
