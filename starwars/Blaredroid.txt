'''Blaredroids''' were a model of [[droid]] invented by the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Zeetar]]. Designed to intimidate and disorient enemies, the droids were outfitted with [[speaker]]s which played a mixture of white noise and [[wreckpunk]] [[music]]. The droids saw their debut in the Nihil's [[Valo Atrocity|attack]] on [[Valo]].<ref name="Rising Storm" />
