The '''Amentor ''Midna''-class shuttle''' was a class of [[shuttle]]. One such shuttle was owned by the criminal syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="LRM" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{LRMDate|2|[[Crimson Dawn]]'s Amentor ''Midna''-class shuttle}}</ref> the shuttle was parked at a [[Unidentified spaceport|spaceport]] on [[Sern Prime]] to pick up Crimson Dawn's accountant [[Madel Nureth]] and transport her to [[Botor]]. The criminal [[Jaylen Vrax]] and his [[BX-series droid commando|BX commando droid]] [[ND-5]] were following her through the spaceport and arrived to see the shuttle, but the two were incapacitated by Crimson Dawn and ND-5 was taken on board the shuttle before they took off.<ref name="LRM" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Amentor ''Midna''-class shuttle first appeared in the [[2026]] novel ''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]'', written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="LRM" />
