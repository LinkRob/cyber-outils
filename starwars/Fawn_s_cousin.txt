The '''cousin''' of the [[human]] [[Fawn]] was a [[musician]].<ref name="ACOF" /> Around [[31 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]]. The novel establishes that [[Fawn]]'s cousin visited [[Batuu]] three [[Standard year|years]] prior to its events, thus placing her visit to around [[31 ABY]].</ref> she came from<ref name="ACOF" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] to visit the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]], and [[farmer]] [[Julen Rakab]] developed feelings for her. His friends, musicians Fawn and [[Neelo]], later teased him for his bad romantic decisions. Rakab recalled liking the cousin's [[music]], but cut off further conversation about his love life.<ref name="ACOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fawn's cousin was mentioned in ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', a [[2019]] young-adult novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="ACOF" />
