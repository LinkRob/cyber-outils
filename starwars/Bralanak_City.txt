'''Bralanak City''' was a [[city]] situated on [[Trymant IV]], surrounded by an [[ocean]]. It was the most populated area on the [[planet]]. Several [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] and subsequent [[Emergences]], a commune of the [[Elders of the Path]] settled in Bralanak City, where they owned a [[meeting house]]. The city came under threat from [[hyperspace]] debris from an [[Trymant IV disaster|Emergence]], leading the [[Jedi]] [[cruiser]] ''[[Star Hopper]]'', the only Jedi or [[Galactic Republic]] [[starship]] within range, to lend assistance.<ref name="HRA 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bralanak City debuted in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 1|first issue]] of the [[2021]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']], released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]], and published by [[IDW Publishing]] on [[February 3]], 2021.<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume I]]''</ref> On [[November 17]] of that year, Bralanak City was first identified by name in a [[StarWars.com]] article announcing ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'', which also marked its first visual appearance in the form of uncolored pages from the first issue.<ref name="Announcement Post">{{SW|url=news/idw-star-wars-the-high-republic-adventures-announce|text=Yoda Returns with Another Lesson in IDW's ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures''}}</ref> The [[2022]] reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume I]]'' by [[Kristin Baver]] included several of Tolibao's sketches and uncolored linework for the city's appearances in ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures''.<ref name="Art" />
