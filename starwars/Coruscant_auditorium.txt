This '''auditorium''' was located on [[Coruscant]] during the early [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Rule of Five II" />

==Description==
The auditorium was a high ceilinged room. One end contained a stage that could be accessed from a staircase on each side. Two banners featuring the [[Imperial crest]] were located on this end; a smaller one hung from the railings of the stage and a larger one displayed on the wall behind it.<ref name="Rule of Five II" />

==History==
[[File:ChockedOfficers-2017DarthVader12.png|thumb|left|250px|Imperial officers getting choked through the Force by Darth Vader as punishment for assassination attempts against him]]
In [[19 BBY|19]]&mdash;[[18 BBY]], several [[Assassination attempts on Darth Vader|assassination attempts]] were made on [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] right hand man, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], by [[officer]]s of the [[Imperial Officer Corps]]. As a result, many officers were summoned to this auditorium by Palpatine, where he explained that an order from Vader was to be treated as an order from himself. After Palpatine finished speaking, Vader called five officers at random; [[Barokki]], [[Corin Ferro]], [[Zorta Bingan]], [[Joon Strephi]], and [[Tomas Azoras]]; to the front of the room. He elaborated on the assassination attempts, claiming that while he did not know exactly who ordered them, he would always survive. Vader began [[Force choke|choking]] the five officers with [[the Force]], who died as their fellow officers watched in horror. Of the officers killed, only Barokki was part of the conspiracy. In the audience, the sole surviving [[Unidentified Imperial officer (Coruscant)|conspirator]] watched.<ref name="Rule of Five II" />

==Behind the scenes==
