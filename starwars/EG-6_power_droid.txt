The '''EG-6 power droid''',<ref name="CL" /> also known as the '''EG-6 series droid''',<ref name="Star Wars 47">[[Star Wars (2020) 47|''Star Wars'' (2020) 47]]</ref> was a model of [[EG-series power droid|EG]] [[power droid]] used during the [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="CL" /> The [[droid]] model was still active on [[planet]]s such as [[Jakku]] in the [[Standard year|years]] following the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="High Noon">{{StoryCite|story=High Noon on Jakku|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref> They greatly resembled the similar [[GNK-series power droid]], also called the Gonk droid, to the point that EG-6 droids were also called gonk droids.
