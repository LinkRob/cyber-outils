The '''B8G labor droid''' was a model of bipedal [[labor droid]]<ref name="Kids" /> with a large [[gas]]-burning exhaust vent in its torso. The model had a head with two black [[sensor]]s, one larger than the other, and an [[antenna]] that protruded from its back. The [[droid]]s could have [[Color|tan]] plating. [[B8G-H0R]] was a B8G labor droid who worked as a [[Slavery|slave]] in the criminal [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s [[spice]] [[Spice mines of Kessel|mines]] of the [[planet]] [[Kessel]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on [[Kessel]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to nine [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]]; therefore, the events on Kessel in ''Solo'' must take place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> it took part in a [[Raid on Kessel|slave uprising]] in the [[Mining|mines]] led by the droid [[L3-37]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A B8G labor droid first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. The droid was not named in the film,<ref name="Solo" /> but was identified in the video "Every Droid in Star Wars", which was posted on ''[[StarWarsKids.com]]'' in [[2019]].<ref name="Kids" />
