'''Copalroot''' was a resilient [[plant]] that survived in the strong winds of the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the survivalist [[Chrissanta Palus]] had a [[Copalroot bead|bead]] made from copalroot that could be worn for vitality and was available for purchase for one thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on Toshara.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Copalroot appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
