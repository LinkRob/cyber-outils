'''Bartender droids''' were a type of [[droid]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], one such model of bartender droid, the [[C5 bartender droid]], was known to serve in the suites of the [[Alisandre Hotel]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Thrawn" /> Bartender droids could also be repurposed individuals of different models, such as [[BU-11]] who worked on [[Ziro's Palace]],<ref name="BuildR2">{{BuildR2Cite|5|Droid Directory}}</ref> [[Unidentified bartender droid (Lau)|another droid]] who worked as the [[bartender]] of a [[Unidentified cantina (Lau)|cantina]] on [[Lau]],<ref name="Different">{{TBB|A Different Approach}}</ref> a [[Unidentified modified DUM-series pit droid|former]] [[DUM-series pit droid]] who worked as a bartender in the [[Suria space station]]<ref name="DB">{{Databank|tanda-s-cantina|Tanda's Cantina}}</ref> and [[M0]] who worked the [[droid cantina]] ''[[The Resistor]]'' on [[Plazir-15]].<ref name="For Hire">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 22: Guns for Hire}}</ref>
