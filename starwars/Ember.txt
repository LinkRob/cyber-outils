'''Ember''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[charhound]] owned by the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] during the [[High Republic Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Republic|High Republic]]. Ember accompanied Zettifar while he was stationed at the [[Elphrona Outpost|Jedi outpost]] on the [[ore]] [[planet]] of [[Elphrona]],<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> often wandering the world's [[iron]] hills.<ref name="Meet the Padawans">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-the-high-republic-meet-the-padawans|text=Inside ''Star Wars: The High Republic'': Meet the Padawans}}</ref> She was friendly with all of the Jedi stationed at the outpost, namely [[Indeera Stokes]] and [[Porter Engle]]. When the Jedi chased a group of [[Nihil]] who had [[Raid on Elphrona|captured]] the [[Blythe family]] from their [[Blythe homestead|home]], Ember accompanied them and killed one of the marauders at an opportune moment, saving Engle's life.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

==Behinds the scenes==
{{Quote|I want a spin-off series: ''The Adventures of Ember''. I'd write it for free. Don't tell them that&hellip; but I would.|Cavan Scott|<ref name="Growing World">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-young-jedi-adventures-new-episodes-interview|text=''Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures'' Creators on the Growing World of ''The High Republic''}}</ref>}}
Ember was created for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project. She was revealed in an article published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 21]], [[2020]], which introduced several Padawans of the High Republic Era&mdash;including Ember's owner Bell Zettifar.<ref name="Meet the Padawans" /> Ember later made her first appearance in [[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi|''Light of the Jedi'']], a [[2021]] novel written by [[Charles Soule]] as part of<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> the project's Phase I.<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>

On [[November 8]], [[2023]],<ref name="Growing World" /> Ember appeared in "[[Charhound Chase]]," the fourteenth episode of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''.<ref name="{{YJA|Charhound Chase}}">{{YJA|Charhound Chase}}</ref> On the same day, episode writer [[Cavan Scott]] mentioned in an article posted on [[StarWars.com]] that he wanted and would write a spin-off series of the show for Ember, coining it ''The Adventures of Ember''.<ref name="Growing World" />
