The '''AJ^6 cyborg construct''', also known as '''Lobot-Tech headgear''', was a [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] device attached onto the backside of a user's head, designed to increase the user's productivity. However, it would often erase their personality and occasionally cause their behavior to become erratic.<ref name="Databank Lobot-Tech Headgear" /> 

==History==
[[File:AJ6CyborgConstruct-CGSWG.png|thumb|250px|left|Lobot was known for wearing the AJ^6 construct.]]
The [[Galactic Republic]] used the construct. It was worn by the former [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Echo]], after he was turned into a [[cyborg]] by the [[Techno Union]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> 

The [[Galactic Empire]] also used the construct. It was worn by the [[Rodian]] [[Tseebo]] of the [[Imperial Information Office]], who utilized the technology to steal [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] data,<ref name="Empire Day" /> and worn by battlefield analyst [[Lobot]]. However, Lobot eventually abandoned the Empire and became the business partner of [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]].<ref name="Lando 1">[[Lando 1|''Lando'' 1]]</ref>

The Imperial [[controller]] designated [[Boon|LT-319]] also wore the headset.<ref name="DAD">{{Rebels|Double Agent Droid}}</ref> [[Vice Chairman]] [[Anolo]] had his [[Unidentified Givin codebreaker|Givin codebreaker]] implanted with the technology.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2">[[Galaxy's Edge 2|''Galaxy's Edge'' 2]]</ref> The technology was used on [[Cloud City]] by Lobot, who served as the aide of [[Baron Administrator]] Calrissian.<ref name="Episode V" />

The [[Pirate]] [[Gunter]] wore the headset,<ref name="A Real Adventure">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref> and [[Astronomer]] [[Kh'ymm]].<ref name="SC 3">{{SkeletonCrew|Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem}}</ref>

The [[First Order]] used a cyborg construct on [[Terex]] to ensure his loyalty to their cause.<ref name="PD9" />
