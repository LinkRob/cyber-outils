'''Blacktar Cyst''' was a [[Mid Rim Territories]] terrestrial astronomical object where both the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]], also known as Twilight Company, and the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] were deployed sometime by [[3 ABY]]. During the subsequent [[Battle of Blacktar Cyst|battle]], the [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] [[Pol Andrissus]] received scarring injuries that caused him to develop a stutter. In the wake of the fighting, rebel [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] started an ongoing quarrel with [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]] and lost another argument with the fellow [[soldier]] [[Brand]].

In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], [[General]] [[Philap Bygar]] cited Twilight Company's troubles on Blacktar Cyst when commending their past service to Evon, Namir, and three other [[company]] members at the Alliance's [[Echo Base|base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]]. On the planet [[Troithe]] around [[5 ABY]], stories of the battle on Blacktar Cyst were told on separate occasions by an [[infantry]]woman of Twilight Company at a [[Troithe's main spaceport|spaceport]] and, some [[Standard day|days]] later, among members of the 204th at the [[Taa Complex]] greenhouse.

==Description==
Blacktar Cyst was an astronomical object located in an [[Blacktar Cyst system|eponymous star system]] in the [[Oktos sector]]<ref name="Systems" /> within [[the Slice]] portion<ref name="Slice" /> of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was situated at the coordinates R-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> 

==History==
{{Quote|Howl's a genius. You won that argument on Blacktar Cyst.|Hazram Namir, to Brand|<ref name="BTC" />}}
Sometime by [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY">{{BTCdate}}</ref> [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]], better known as Twilight Company, was deployed to the terrestrial astronomical object Blacktar Cyst, leading to a [[Battle of Blacktar Cyst|battle]]<ref name="BTC" /> in which the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] also took part.<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> The Twilight Company [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] [[Pol Andrissus]] was wounded by shrapnel, which heavily scarred and disfigured his face, leaving the [[soldier]] with a permanent stutter due to shrapnel that had reached his [[brain]]. On Blacktar Cyst, the rebel [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] lost an argument with another rebel soldier, the ex&ndash;[[bounty hunter]] [[Brand]], about whether the [[company]]'s [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]], nicknamed "Howl," was a genius or not.<ref name="BTC" />

[[File:YricaQuell-TIEFighter1.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Yrica Quell fought with the 204th Imperial Fighter Wing at Blacktar Cyst and chose not to reveal such when she spoke to members of Twilight Company.]]
In the wake of Twilight Company's deployment to Blacktar Cyst, Evon had a long-lasting debate with Namir about whether the latter underestimated his men and their motivation to fight. The Alliance [[General]] [[Philap Bygar]] of [[Alliance High Command]] believed the company never should have been sent to Blacktar Cyst and voiced that opinion when commending Twilight Company's past service to Evon, Namir, and three other members of the company during their visit to the Alliance's [[Echo Base|secret base]] on the planet [[Hoth]]<ref name="BTC" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="3 ABY" />

In [[4 ABY]], following the [[Battle of Endor]], the Alliance [[Declaration of a New Republic|became]] the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">'[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> Twilight Company, as part of a New Republic [[Barma Battle Group|battle group]], [[Troithe operation|fought]] on the [[planet]] [[Troithe]] during a [[Cerberon system campaign|campaign]] in the [[Cerberon system]]. During a social gathering between several of the company's soldiers and the New Republic's [[Alphabet Squadron]] at [[Troithe's main spaceport]], one Twilight Company [[infantry]]woman relayed her point of view of the fighting on Blacktar Cyst. Alphabet Squadron's [[Squadron commander|commander]], [[Yrica Quell]], who was a former member of the 204th, realized that her recollection of the battle was different than that of the soldier's but decided not to admit to having served on the enemy's side. Sometime later, at the [[Taa Complex]] greenhouse on Troithe, [[Soran Keize]], the field-promoted [[colonel]] in charge of the 204th, joined a group of 204th [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilots]] in telling stories, including ones about the battle of Blacktar Cyst, around a [[fire]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blacktar Cyst was first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
