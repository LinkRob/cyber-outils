"'''Czerka 431'''" was a scenario for [[Knights of the Old Republic (Star Wars Miniatures)|''Star Wars Miniatures: Knights of the Old Republic'']], that was written by [[Sterling Hershey]] and added to [[Wizards.com]] on [[October 1]], [[2008]].

On [[December 2]], [[2008]], the story of this scenario continued in a second scenario, ''[[Permanent Demotion]]''.

==Plot summary==
During the [[Jedi Civil War]], [[Czerka Arms/Legends|Czerka Corp]] sold to both sides. [[Joni Ree]], a scientest at [[Czerka 431]] has gotten tired of the arrangement and has leaked its location to the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] for a rescue.
