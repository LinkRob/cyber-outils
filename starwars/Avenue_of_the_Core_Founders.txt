The '''Avenue of the Core Founders''' was a large concourse which served as the pathway to the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building's]] entrance on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> It was adorned with [[humanoid]] statues representing the original [[Core Founder|founders]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], all of whom hailed from the [[Core Worlds]]. By the [[time]] of the [[Invasion of Naboo]], those statues were criticized for no longer being representative of the multi-[[species]] Republic.<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
