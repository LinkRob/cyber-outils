The '''Ashas Ree Temple of the Force'''<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|ashas-ree-temple-of-the-force|Ashas Ree Temple of the Force}}</ref> was a [[Jedi temple]] located on the [[planet]] [[Ashas Ree]]. The temple had been built atop a [[Ashas Ree Sith temple|Sith Temple]] in an attempt to contain and suppress the [[Dark side of the Force|dark power]] located inside it. By the [[time]] of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], the [[Jedi]] temple had fallen into some disrepair, and an [[Ashas Ree outpost|outpost]] had sprung up next to it.<ref name="The Relic Raiders" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> an [[archaeologist]] named [[Mika Grey]] got trapped inside the temple. She was soon discovered by [[Castilon]] resident [[Kazuda Xiono]]. Xiono accidentally fell in where Mika was after being tempted to touch a relic that was held inside the temple. Mika claimed that she was inside the temple for almost two days. After Mika arrived on Ashas Ree, she stated that the villagers of the planet fled after Mika excavated the temple.<ref name="The Relic Raiders" />
