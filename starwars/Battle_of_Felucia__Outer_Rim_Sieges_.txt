The '''Battle of Felucia''' was the third major engagement for control over the [[planet]] [[Felucia]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. As one of the last battles of the conflict, [[Jedi Master]] [[Aayla Secura]] commanded the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic's troops]] as they moved into position in readiness to engage the Separatists; however [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] gave word that [[Order 66]] be carried out, leading to the [[Clone trooper|clones]] turning on Secura and [[Death|killing]] her.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|The setback at Felucia has affected our efforts in the whole sector.|Obi-Wan Kenobi, after the First Battle of Felucia|<ref name="Holocron Heist">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref>}}
[[File:Rescue_on_Felucia.jpg|left|thumb|200x200px|Republic forces during the First Battle of Felucia]]
The site of a major [[hyperspace route]],<ref name="Padawan Lost">{{TCW|Padawan Lost}}</ref> a hub for [[Nysillin]] production,<ref name="Battles" /> and the headquarters of the [[Commerce Guild]],<ref name="Rise of the Separatists">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Felucia]] was a heavily contested planet in the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Felucia Encyclopedia">{{SWE|locations|felucia|Felucia}}</ref> as it was sought by both the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Padawan Lost">{{TCW|Padawan Lost}}</ref> Early in the war, the Confederacy led an [[First Battle of Felucia|assault]] on Felucia and secured the planet, overcoming [[Grand Army of the Republic|the forces]] of [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and establishing an [[Felucia blockade|orbital blockade]]. Breaking through the enemy [[starship]]s, [[General]] [[Plo Koon]] led the successful evacuation of the [[Jedi]] and their [[clone trooper]]s, though the planet had still fallen into the hands of the Separatists. Such a loss set back the Republic's efforts in the entire [[Thanium sector]].<ref name="Holocron Heist" />

Later, the Republic had managed to establish [[Felucia Medical Station HCTFF2|Medical Station HCTFF2]] over Felucia, but the [[Haven-class medical station|''Haven'' medical station]] was attacked by a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Separatist fleet]] and [[Destruction of the Felucia medical station|destroyed]]. When Skywalker, Tano, and Kenobi were sent to investigate, they were met with a waiting [[automated vulture droid deployment station]], which deployed several [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] that took down their [[T-6 shuttle]]. Upon crashing on Felucia, the Jedi became embroiled in a [[Skirmish on Felucia|conflict]] between the [[Ohnaka Gang]] and a group of [[Felucian]] [[farmer]]s.<ref name="BH">{{TCW|Bounty Hunters (episode)}}</ref> Later, Skywalker, Koon, Tano, and their clones were again deployed to Felucia, having been tasked with taking over Separatist-controlled portions of the world. Learning that the Republic sought to take out [[TZ-33]]'s [[Separatist outpost (Felucia)|outpost]], General [[Grievous]] attempted to secure his control over the [[Felucia system]] by dispatching reinforcements, but the [[Second Battle of Felucia]] still ended in a Republic victory by taking out the [[CIS mobile command center|Confederate outpost]].<ref name="Padawan Lost" />

During the final days of the war, Felucia was home to a Separatist stronghold<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> despite the Republic's earlier victory.<ref name="Padawan Lost" /> The [[Commerce Guild]] [[poison]]ed Felucia's [[water]]s in an attempt to prevent the planet from being of value to the Republic.<ref name="BTM" />

==The battle==
[[File:FeluciaLandscape-SWBC53.png|thumb|right|300px|Separatist AATs and homing spider droids during the battle]]
During the [[Outer Rim Sieges]], Jedi General [[Aayla Secura]], [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Bly]], and their [[trooper]]s in the [[327th Star Corps]] were deployed to the planet<ref name="ROTS" /> to capture [[Shu Mai]], [[Presidente]] of the Commerce Guild and member of the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]],<ref name="Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition]]''</ref> rescue [[Ormo Hyn]],<ref name="Battles" /> and stop the poison from spreading.<ref name="BTM" /> The ensuing conflict became the war's third major battle for Felucia. Early in the battle,<ref name="Battles" /> Secura flew [[Aayla Secura's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|her Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]] and pursued a [[HMP droid gunship]].<ref name="Aayla Actis" /> At another point, Secura and her [[troop]]s stood together before engaging a target.<ref name="Old Friends" /> Additionally, at some point two clones standing near each other were fighting in the battle.<ref name="Legion Phase Two Clones">{{Legion|set=Phase II Clone Troopers Unit Expansion|cardname=Phase II Clone Troopers}}</ref>

Felucia was one of the planets which the Republic dubbed a key target that needed to be be taken from the CIS.<ref name="The End of the Clone Wars">{{EncyclopediaCite|The End of the Clone Wars}}</ref> In the [[Standard day|days]] that came after the [[Battle of Coruscant]], Jedi General [[Stass Allie]] was ordered to lead reinforcements to Felucia. Before she could make it to Felucia but after leaving the [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]], she was redirected to<ref name="YaOUotF">{{EncyclopediaCite|Yoda and Other Users of the Force}}</ref> the planet<ref name="Battles" /> [[Saleucami]], which had recently [[Siege of Saleucami|fallen]] to Republic troops under Jedi Generals [[Quinlan Vos]]<ref name="ROTS" /> and [[Oppo Rancisis]].<ref name="Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition" />

[[File:ATOTATTEFelucia-BTCG.png|thumb|left|300px|An AT-OT and AT-TE on Felucia.]]
Later, Secura and the 327th marched<ref name="ROTS" /> through the wild underbrush in search of Confederate forces.<ref name="Felucia Encyclopedia" /> By that [[time]], Mai was not on Felucia and was instead [[Klegger Corp Mining Facility|with]] other members of the Separatist Council on [[Mustafar]], having been transferred from [[Unidentified core ship|their base]] on [[Utapau]]. Eventually, Secura and her troopers located enemy forces gathered in a clearing directly in front of them. However,<ref name="ROTS" /> as the [[OG-9 homing spider droid]]s and [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tanks]] advanced,<ref name="Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> Bly received [[Order 66]] from [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="ROTS" /> Forced to obey the [[Chancellor]]'s command by their [[Behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chips]],<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> Bly, [[Clone Lieutenant]] [[Galle]], and the other clones followed its directive, shooting Secura in the back before she could react. Secura was finished off by fire from the clones surrounding her before full-scale combat operations could begin.<ref name="ROTS" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Felucia, then&mdash;in reprisal for the way the Republic left it.''"<br />"''Of no significance.|Wilhuff Tarkin and Darth Vader|<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>}}
In the end, the Battle of Felucia ended with a Republic victory,<ref name="Battles" /> while the war itself ended with the entire droid army being shut down from Mustafar, securing a victory for the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|newly established]] successor state, the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="ROTS" /> The Empire established an [[Felucia Imperial garrison|Imperial garrison]] on Felucia following the fighting, but the planet was left in a ruined state. As such, [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] considered it a possible target for [[Berch Teller's rebel cell]], but the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Vader disagreed.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|This was all pre-script only. This is the Padawan younglings being forced to fight. And we thought it might be cool, since they can't fight all alone. If they fought in formation in order to face off against the droids&mdash;but they'd still be slain.|Derek Thompson on an early idea for the Battle of Felucia|<ref name="TAoSWEIII:RotS">''[[The Art of Star Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
The Battle of Felucia first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="ROTS" /> While developing the film's story before a script was written, it was initially proposed by concept artist [[Derek Thompson]] to have several [[Padawan]]s accompanying the clone troopers to the battle and aiding them against the droids, but the idea was dropped as the Padawans eventually had to die anyway.<ref name="TAoSWEIII:RotS" />
