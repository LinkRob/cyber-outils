The [[Standard year|year]] '''350 BBY''' was a year of the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]] that fell within the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> In other calendars, it was known as '''2927 LY''', according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''331 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7627''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> Between around 350 BBY and around [[330 BBY]], [[Mari San Tekka]] was kidnapped.<ref name="Timelines" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 52|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 52]] stated that the [[Trade Federation]] was formed by the [[Neimoidian]]s in 350 BBY.<ref name="BMF 52">{{BuildFalconCite|52|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> However, the novel ''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]'' later pushed its forming to around [[232 BBY]].<ref name="FedFormation">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' establishes that the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] occurred in [[232 BBY]]. As ''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]'' establishes that the [[Trade Federation]] was formed in the wake of that event, it must have been formed around that [[Standard year|year]].</ref>
