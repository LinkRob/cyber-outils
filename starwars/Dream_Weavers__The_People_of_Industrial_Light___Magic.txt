"'''Dream Weavers: The People of Industrial Light & Magic'''" is an article in [[Star Wars Insider 64|''Star Wars Insider'' 64]]. It profiles the major players in [[Industrial Light & Magic]] who worked on [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].

The article also lists 10 Easter Eggs.
