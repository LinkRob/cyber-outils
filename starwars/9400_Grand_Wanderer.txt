'''9400 Grand Wanderers''' were [[landspeeder]]s that were used primarily as mobile homes. The landspeeder grew in popularity amongst the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] and [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Unlimited Power" />

{{Veh-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
9400 Grand Wanderers were first mentioned in ''[[Unlimited Power]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Force and Destiny|''Force and Destiny'']] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' module that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2018]].<ref name="Unlimited Power" />
