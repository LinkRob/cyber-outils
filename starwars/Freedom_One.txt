The '''Freedom One''' was a popular [[blaster]] manufactured by the [[SoroSuub Corporation]]. It was a technological clone of [[BlasTech Industries]]'s [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]], the standard-issue [[blaster rifle]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]]. The Freedom One was the most successful blaster out of many attempts by various different corporations to copy the success of the E-11. It was formerly known as the '''Stormtrooper One''', but the corporation renamed it to the Freedom One after changing their allegiance to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Freedom One was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition|second edition]] of the [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]], a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] roleplaying sourcebook released in [[October]] [[1992]].<ref name="Second Edition">[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition|''Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game'', Second Edition]]</ref> The Freedom One was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Blasters and Ranged Weapons]]," a reference book by [[De Agostini|Altaya]] released exclusively in France on [[February 23]], [[2021]]<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />
