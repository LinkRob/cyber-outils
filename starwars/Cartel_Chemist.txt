The '''Cartel Chemist''' was a costume owned by the [[Rodian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Skora]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Underworld Chemist|standard costume]]. This outfit consisted of [[Color|gray]], brown, tan, and cream components, with light [[armor]], including shoulder pads, with a [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] insignia printed on the right one. She also wore [[goggles]], as well as an [[Bandolier|ammo pouch]] on her leg and a chemical-containment device attached to her back.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cartel Chemist was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Skora, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cartel Applicator]]. This wrap could be unlocked by progressing through the "Skora Master II Event" Quest.<ref name="Hunters" />
