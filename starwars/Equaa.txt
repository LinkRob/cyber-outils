'''Equaa''' was a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Night of Sorrow]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Equaa participated in an [[Night of Sorrow|attack]] on the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[The Force|Force]] [[religion]] on the [[planet]] [[Dalna]], running alongisde fellow Jedi [[Toorin]], [[Woo]], [[Riponjonang]], [[Enya Keen]], and [[Char-Ryl-Roy]] at some point by [[Time|nightfall]]. All six had their [[lightsaber]]s ignited as [[Security droid|enforcer droids]] fired on them.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
