The '''Cobalt Laborers' Reformation Front''', or just '''Cobalt Front''', was an anti-[[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] workers' association on the [[planet]] of [[Sullust]], which fought for better safety and factory standards. The organization became increasingly anti-Imperial but by the time the [[Sullustan resistance]] arrived to help, their most militant members were imprisoned and the others were too scared to fight. When the [[61st Mobile Infantry]] were stranded on Sullust, their [[Captain]], [[Hazram Namir]], contacted the Sullustan resistance and devised a plan for retaking control from the Empire. Their leader [[Nien Nunb]] rallied the Sullustan resistance and willing members of the Cobalt Front to take the capital of [[Pinyumb]] whilst the imperial forces clashed with the 61st at the [[Siege of Inyusu Tor]].

==History==
===Premature revolution===
{{Quote|I thought there was a resistance movement.''"<br />"''The Cobalt Front hasn't ever been much of a resistance. Their heart's in the right place, but&hellip;|Hazram Namir and Corjentain Malaqua|<ref name="BTC" />}}
The Cobalt Laborers' Reformation Front, shortened to the Cobalt Front, was a workers' association that operated on the [[Volcano|volcanic]]<ref name="BTC" /> [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Scarif and others">{{EncyclopediaCite|Scarif and Other Planets in the Outer Rim}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Sullust]] by the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="BTC" /> Anti-Imperial, it was led by the [[Sullustan]] [[Biyel Broan]] until he was forced out due to his associations with criminals.<ref name="Uprising" /> The Cobalt Front campaigned for workers rights, speaking out about safety procedures and factory working conditions.<ref name="BTC" />

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> the Cobalt Front put up a protest against the Empire, and some of its increasingly militant members armed themselves with [[pipe bomb]]s and attacked [[Factory|factories]]. In response, the Empire decreed the workers group to be terrorists and began cracking down, [[Prison|imprisoning]] an estimated eighty percent of their membership. Meanwhile, a small Alliance cell called the [[Sullustan resistance]] came to the planet to formally ally itself with the Cobalt Front. The group's leader [[Nien Nunb]] condemned the Empire to workers at Sullust's<ref name="BTC" /> [[capital]] [[city]]<ref name="Scarif and others" /> of [[Pinyumb]], but what remained of the Cobalt Front were reluctant to take up arms. The rebel cell thus turned to running an operation that smuggled relief supplies into Pinyumb.<ref name="BTC" />

===Revolution lamented===
{{Quote|After the accident with the magma release, he said maybe the Cobalt Front was right. Maybe we do need to stand up for ourselves.''"<br />"''The Cobalt Laborers' Reformation Front, is a band of terrorists. They probably caused the accident in the first place.|Two Sullustan workers|<ref name="BTC" />}}
After a steam pipe accident scalded some workers living in Pinyumb, one resident started considering the Cobalt Front's call for workers to start standing up for themselves, giving his [[Parent|father]] a leaflet and telling them about it. The father, a worker from the [[Inyusu Tor mineral processing facility]] near Pinyumb, spoke about the conversation to a colleague at a Pinyumb [[Thara Nyende's uncle's cantina|cantina]]. The colleague called the Cobalt Front terrorists, speculating that the association had caused the steam pipe accident in the first place.<ref name="BTC" />

Meanwhile, indications reached the Empire that the Cobalt Front may be trying to make ties with the Alliance. In the [[Imperial Garrison (Sullust)|Imperial Garrison]] at Pinyumb, a [[Unidentified Imperial lieutenant (Sullust)|Imperial lieutenant]] briefed some [[stormtrooper]]s from the local [[97th Legion]] on the situation showing the faces of rebels with ties to Sullust, including Nunb, [[Corjentain Malaqua]], and [[Sian Tevv]].<ref name="BTC" />

Continued activity from the Sullustan resistance on Sullust and from other Alliance operations elsewhere increased the Empire's crackdowns in Pinyumb and its workload drives to make up for production losses elsewhere. At the same Pinyumb cantina, a worker lamented that if the Cobalt Front had not decided to more militant, they might have been there for still speak up for the workers. Another worker argued that the association never cared and was a front for the Alliance from the start.<ref name="BTC" />

===The actual revolution===
{{Quote|What about you? What's the plan here?''"<br />"''We'll rally anyone left from the Cobalt Front. Try to fight back one last time, protect Pinyumb from what's coming. Can't let them round up all our neighbors. Shouldn't take too long.''"<br />"''No. But if we're stuck until dawn, you may as well show my what you've got. I can look at your plans, give you a fresh perspective. Maybe drag out your war a little longer.|Hazram Namir and Corjentain Malaqua|<ref name="BTC" />}}
The situation boiled even further when the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] [[Siege of Inyusu Tor|captured]] the Inyusu Tor facility, prompting a full lockdown of Pinyumb. The 61st lost its [[Thunderstrike|troop transport]] and sent a team led by its acting [[captain]], [[Hazram Namir]], to Pinyumb to search for rebel activity that they had caught wind of. Namir eventually met with the four members of the Sullustan resistance, who explained their story, including the suppression of the Cobalt Front.<ref name="BTC" />

The Sullustan resistance members planned on rounding up remaining members of the Cobalt Front and making a final stand against the increasing Imperial crackdowns in Pinyumb, but Namir stepped in and formed an altered plan with them that involved the 61st defending the Inyusu Tor facility and distracting Imperial forces. It also involved a coordinated effort of the Inyusu Tor's magma extractors and mining equipment in the possession of both the Alliance groups to try and flood the Imperial Garrison at Pinyumb with lava. The plan was successful, and the [[garrison]] and half a dozen guard posts were flooded with lava. An uprising came thereafter, liberating Pinyumb from the Empire.<ref name="BTC" />
