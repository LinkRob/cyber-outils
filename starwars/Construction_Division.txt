The '''Construction Division''' was a division of the [[House of Tagge]]'s [[Tagge Corporation]].<ref name="Aphra 6" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 6]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[Gortus 4 colony|colony]] on the [[moon]] [[Gortus 4]] signed over the rights to the moon to [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]]&mdash;the [[Executive officer|leader]] of Tagge Corporation. Domina was informed of the news by her<ref name="Aphra 6" /> [[secretary]]<ref name="Boushh">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]]</ref> [[Lapin Tagge]],<ref name="Aphra 28">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 28|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 28]]</ref> after which she instructed<ref name="Aphra 6" /> them<ref name="Boushh" /> to tell the Construction Division to set up an [[ore]] [[factory]] on the moon within a [[Standard week|week]].<ref name="Aphra 6" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Construction Division was mentioned in flashbacks in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|sixth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 6" /> on [[November 25]], 2020. Prior to the release of the comic, the division was mentioned in a [https://www.starwars.com/news/marvel-doctor-aphra-6-exclusive-preview preview article] of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[November 18]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-doctor-aphra-6-exclusive-preview|text=There's a New Bounty in Marvel's ''Doctor Aphra'' #6 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
