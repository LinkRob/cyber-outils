'''Ahsoka's Delta-7B Jedi Starfighter''' was a [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] piloted by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] during the [[Clone Wars]], periodically flying with [[R7-A7]] as her [[astromech droid]] co-pilot. [[Gender|She]] piloted the ship during the [[Battle of Ryloth]]<ref name="SoR" /> and the [[Battle of Umbara]].<ref name="DoU">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref>

==History==
===Battle of Ryloth===
[[File:Blue_Leader_at_Battle_of_Ryloth.png|thumb|right|270px|Tano leading her squadron in her starfighter over Ryloth.]]
[[Ahsoka Tano]] was made [[Blue Leader]] by [[Anakin Skywalker]] and flew the starfighter, along with [[R7-A7]], during the [[Battle of Ryloth]] in [[21 BBY]]. Her goal was to lead [[Blue Squadron (Galactic Republic)|Blue Squadron]] to break through the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[blockade]] consisting of a [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class battleship]] and two [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]], allowing the [[Ryloth invasion fleet|Republic invasion fleet]] led by Masters [[Mace Windu]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] to commence their ground attack to liberate the occupied planet. She was initially successful at destroying the [[Droid starfighter|droid fighters]] and pressing their defences, until four more frigates joined the blockade, catching Skywalker and Tano by surprise. Tano disobeyed Skywalker's order for her squadron to retreat back to the ''[[Resolute]]'' and continued the battle, resulting in over half her squadron being shot down. Tano made it back to the ''Resolute'' just before Skywalker had his [[Skywalker's fleet|fleet]] jump to [[hyperspace]]. They would later break the blockade, allowing the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic forces]] to get through.<ref name="SoR" />

Tano later flew her Delta-7B alongside Skywalker in a campaign to destroy [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class droid fighter/bombers]] which were targeting populated villages. Skywalker and Tano destroyed the last of them before they bombed the [[capital]] city of [[Lessu City|Lessu]].<ref name="Liberty">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref>

===Battle of Umbara===
[[File:AhsokaFighter-DoU.png|left|thumb|Tano flying her starfighter in the space battle above Umbara.|270x270px]]
In [[20 BBY]], Tano flew her Delta-7B in the [[Battle of Umbara]]. Alongside her friend [[Barriss Offee]] who flew in [[Barriss Offee's Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|her own Delta-7B]], Tano commanded a fighter squadron consisting of [[Clone Z-95 starfighter|Z-95 Headhunters]] and [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber|BTL-B Y-wing fighter/bombers]]. Their efforts allowed the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] to break through the Separatist blockade defending the planet.<ref name="DoU" />

===Later use===
Tano and Skywalker flew their Delta-7Bs to [[Plop Dribble's]] at the [[Stobar Spaceport|spaceport]] on [[Stobar]]. They had intended to rendezvous with their [[cruiser]], but Skywalker was hungry and had grown tired of government rations. Nevertheless, they witnessed the aftermath of events after [[Savage Opress]] assaulted one of the waitresses working at the diner.<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref>

By the time of the [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|Battle of Cato Neimoidia]], Tano ceased to use her Delta-7B and switched to an [[Ahsoka Tano's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]].<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref>

==Crew==
The starfighter was piloted by [[Ahsoka Tano]], who also functioned as its captain. Tano's [[astromech droid]] [[R7-A7]] functioned as the ship's navigator and repairer should it be damaged in combat.<ref name="SoR" />
