The '''Cliffside Hideout''' was [[smuggler]] hideout that was built into a cliff in the western area of [[Great Chott salt flat]] region of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it contained a [[LC3-skifter]] and had a single entrance that was protected by three [[turret]]s that were powered by three separate external generators<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] used her [[blaster]] to destroy a rock near the base of the cliff which gave her access to climb up to the hideout. Once at the hideout the turrets activated however Vess was able to destroy each turrets generator thus shutting them down. Afterward she gained access to the hideout and acquired the LC3-Skifter inside, which she used to allow her to magnetically influence [[Kessel Sabacc]] dice.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The hideout appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The location contained a key part, the LC3-Skifter, which allowed the player to unlock the Magetic Dice cheat for the optional activity Kessel Sabbac from the [[Teeka]] expert tree<ref name="Outlaws" />
