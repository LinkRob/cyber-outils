'''Drop pods''' were simple crafts that resembled [[escape pod]]s and were used to bring vehicles to the battlefield. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] modified such craft to deploy troops and supplies directly into battle.<ref name="FA">''[[Star Wars: Force Arena]]''</ref><ref name="SWB">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref>
