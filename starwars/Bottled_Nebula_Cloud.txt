'''Bottled Nebula Cloud''' was a container that thrumed with energy and contained a sample of swirling [[ionize]]d [[gas]] allegedly from the [[Sayma Nebula]] in [[Wild Space]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate|APF=1}}</ref> the [[pirate]] [[Qorvino]] had buried the container on an island in [[Lake Amari]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. Alongside the cloud, Qorvino also buried a [[Qorvino's Starmap|starmap]] that led to the [[Khepi Tomb|Great Tomb]] of the [[Khepi]] hidden in the Sayma Nebula.<ref name="Outlaws" />

Around that same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] followed data messages Qorvino left for their child [[Hella]] to recover both the Bottled Nebula Cloud and the [[Star map|starmap]]. She later used the coordinates contained in the star map to travel to the Khepi's Tomb.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bottled Nebula Cloud appeared in the ''A Pirate's Fortune'' story expansion to the [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]], published by [[Ubisoft]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> released on  [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref>. In the game, the cloud could be equipped to the player to allow them to consume [[adrenaline]] to slow time when shooting from the hip using quickdraw, while sliding, or when aiming the blaster quickly, after rolling or vaulting over an object.<ref name="Outlaws" />
