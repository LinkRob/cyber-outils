'''CloneKL-555''' was the designation of a [[human]] [[clone trooper]] from [[Kamino]] who served the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Clone" /> At some point, CloneKL-555's [[doctor]] recommended that he walk 10,000 steps per day. In order to accomplish this, he got an [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]] and left a four star review of the vehicle, which was eventually published in a [[Unidentified book|book]] written by [[Poe Dameron]].<ref name="Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
CloneKL-555 was mentioned in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''.<ref name="Vehicles" />
