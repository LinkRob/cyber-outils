'''Chiu Mo Threads''' was an outdoor [[clothing]] market stall at the [[Daiyu marketplace]] in [[Daiyu City]] on the [[planet]] [[Daiyu]]. A [[Color|red]] [[Aurebesh]] stated the name of the business. After rescuing a young [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] from kidnappers, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] lead her through the marketplace, buying her a green [[cape]] and a pair of [[glove]]s at the stall and telling her to pretend that they were a farming family from [[Tawl]].<ref name="Kenobi" />
