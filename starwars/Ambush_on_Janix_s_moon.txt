The '''ambush on Janix's moon''' was a raid that was launched by the former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] and his [[lieutenant]], the [[Mandalorian]] [[Rook Kast]], on the [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s shipment sent to the [[Janix's star system]]. After Kast questioned the [[Aleena]] [[crime lord]] [[Looti Vario]] on the location of the [[Pyke]]'s shipment, [[Spybot]] smuggled himself into the [[Pyke cruiser]], sabotaging the [[hyperdrive]] and forcing it to land on [[Janix]]'s [[Janix moon 1|nearby moon]]. Upon landing on the [[moon]], Maul infiltrated the [[starship]], entering through the cargo lift. He [[Death|killed]] the [[Unidentified Pyke captain|Pyke captain]] and all of<ref name="MSL2" /> his<ref name="CH2 MSL AD">{{MSL|Chapter 2: Sinister Schemes}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> forces, while Kast and her [[Mandalorian super commando]] [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] stole all the credits.<ref name="MSL2" />
