'''Camie Marstrap''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who lived in [[Anchorhead]] on [[Tatooine]]. Her family grew [[hydroponics]] gardens, buying the [[water]] from [[Owen Lars]]. Marstrap frequented the [[Tosche Station]] along with her boyfriend, [[Laze Loneozner|Laze "Fixer" Loneozner]] and their friends [[Deak]], [[Windy]], and [[Luke Skywalker]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
[[File:Camie-SWCT.png|thumb|left|220px|Marstrap in her youth]]
Camie Marstrap [[Homeworld|hailed]] from [[Tatooine]],<ref name="SWCT" /> a [[Star|sun]]-scorched [[desert]] [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]]. [[Gender|Her]] [[parent]]s, a couple of [[Hydroponics|hydroponic]] gardeners, frequently bought [[water]] from the [[moisture farmer]]s [[Owen Lars|Owen]] and [[Beru Whitesun Lars|Beru Lars]], who [[Lars homestead|lived]] in the [[Jundland Wastes]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> As a result, Marstrap became acquainted with [[Luke Skywalker]], the Larses' nephew and adopted son.<ref name="SWCT" /> In her late teens, Marstrap lived in [[Anchorhead]]<ref name="PSFB">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref> and spent her free [[time]] at [[Tosche Station]] with Skywalker, [[Biggs Darklighter]],<ref name="SWCT" /> [[Deak]], [[Windy]],<ref name="PSFB" /> and her boyfriend [[Laze Loneozner|Laze "Fixer" Loneozner]].<ref name="SWCT" /> Like the other members of that small circle, Marstrap nicknamed Skywalker "Wormie," which sometimes made him doubt the sincerity of their friendship.<ref name="PSFB" />

===A mysterious stranger===
{{Quote|Stop it! Leave him alone!|Camie Marstrap, to the Niktos attacking Laze Loneozner|<ref name="BoBF Ch.2">{{BOBF|Chapter 2: The Tribes of Tatooine}}</ref>}}
[[File:CamieLazeDateNight-BoBFCh2.png|thumb|right|300px|Loneozner and Marstrap witness Boba Fett taking out the Nikto bikers.]]
Sometime after Skywalker's [[Rescue of Han Solo|rescue]] of [[Han Solo]], Marstrap and Loneozner encountered several [[Nikto]] bikers at [[Tosche Station]]. They had several of their things and drinks taken from them. Loneozner verbally protested their actions, something Marstrap attempted to dissuade him from doing but failed to do so. The Niktos then began to rough him up. Marstrap attempted to intervene, shouting for them to stop, but she was restrained by one of the Niktos and was forced to watch the Niktos beat Loneozner with a [[shock stick]]. As she and Loneozner continued to struggle, the Niktos suddenly stopped their attack when [[Boba Fett]] entered the room. The Niktos then attacked Fett who proceeded to dispatch the Niktos one by one while Loneozner and Marstrap huddled in a corner and watched.<ref name="BoBF Ch.2" />

===Skywalker's dream===
In [[34 ABY]], [[Jedi Master]] Luke Skywalker experienced a [[dream]] whilst living in [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] on [[Ahch-To]]. In his dream, he [[Marriage|married]] Camie Marstrap during the early stages of the [[Galactic Civil War]], and remained in the [[moisture farm]]ing business with his uncle and aunt. While dreaming of a different life, Skywalker stood outside of his homestead, gazing at the sunset with his wife as they reminisced of the [[time]] they shared together during the war between the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Marstrap became unsettled by her husband, suspecting that he wanted to be far away from her, but Skywalker assured her that it was not the case.<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
A [[human]] female, Camie Marstrap had light skin and [[Color|blue]] [[eye]]s. She had long, wavy brown [[hair]], which she wore down.<ref name="SWCT" />

==Behind the scenes==
During the original production of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] (then known simply as ''Star Wars'') in [[1976]], there were several minutes of film involving Luke Skywalker spending time at Tosche power station with his friends. The only girl in the group was Camie, portrayed by the [[Wikipedia:United States|American]] actress [[Koo Stark]]. Although those scenes were not used in the final film release, they still appeared in the novelization of the film as well as the [[Star Wars (1977) 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars (1977)|''Marvel Star Wars'']] comics adaptation of ''A New Hope''.<ref name="MC1">[[Star Wars (1977) 1|''Star Wars'' (1977) 1]]</ref> They were also retained in the [[1981]] [[Star Wars (radio)|''Star Wars Radio Drama'']] broadcast by [[National Public Radio]]. In this dramatization, Camie was voiced by [[Stephanie Steele]].<ref name="SWR">[[Star Wars (radio)|''Star Wars'' radio drama]]</ref> Camie made a few more appearances in various stories and sourcebooks. These sources are part of [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], previously known as the Expanded Universe, and are therefore not official [[canon]].<ref name="EUNewPage">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> However, Camie and her backstory were partially canonized by [[Topps]]'s app [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]], which was released in [[2015]].<ref name="SWCT" />

In [[Chapter 2: The Tribes of Tatooine]], the second chapter of the series ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'', she was portrayed by [[Mandy Kowalski]].<ref name="BoBF Ch.2" />
