'''''Angry Birds Star Wars II: Darth Swindle's Secrets''''' is a children's book by [[Dorling Kindersley]]. It was released on [[November 18]], [[2013]].

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Angry Birds Star Wars II'' will be coming to an app store near you on September 19, 2013!

Wicked Darth Swindle is a Pig Lord with many secrets. Learn all about them in this all-new Level 1 Reader from DK. Delve into the wacky world of ''Angry Birds Star Wars'' and get to know the pesky pigs on the Pork Side.
