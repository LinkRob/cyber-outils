{{Quote|Ma'am, it's wonderful. Many thousands of Alderaanian survivors got word of your mission to unite us all. They've ''joined'' us, ma'am!|A crewmember of the ''[[Lord Junn]]'' reports the fleet's formation to Organa|<ref name="PL5" />}}
The '''Alderaan Flotilla''',<ref name="LD" /> that was also known as the '''Survivor Fleet''',<ref name="Star Wars 48" /> was a collection of [[starship]]s which carried the surviving [[Alderaanian|population]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] following that world's [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]. The Survivor Fleet was formed by the [[Princess]] of Alderaan, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who, following the loss of her [[homeworld]] and the [[Battle of Yavin]], began [[Mission to rescue the survivors of Alderaan|seeking out]] other Alderaanian survivors and rescuing them from getting killed by the Empire. Hearing of her mission, many other Alderaanians made their way to join her above the planet [[Espirion]], where the combined Alderaanian forces destroyed an Imperial [[Dreed's Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] that was pursuing them. Organa left the [[fleet]] under the watchful eyes of her friend [[Lieutenant]] [[Evaan Verlaine]] when they returned to their individual duties in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="PL5" /> Following the [[Battle of Endor]], the Survivor Fleet consisted of seven ships and was positioned in the [[Alderaanian Graveyard|remains of Alderaan]] in the [[Alderaan system]]. Ruled in Organa's absence by [[Regent Administrator]] [[Eglyn Valmor]], the flotilla was gifted the remains of the first [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] so that they could build a [[space station]] in the Alderaan [[Star system|system]] to house them.<ref name="LD" />
