'''Blutopians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> from the [[water]]y [[planet]]<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref> of [[Blutopia]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> [[Oolin Musters]] was a [[Gender|female]] Blutopian who was imprisoned by the [[Galactic Empire]] on [[Wobani]].<ref name="RO" /> [[Oga Garra]] was another female Blutopian who owned [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blutopians first appeared in the [[2016]] film, ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="RO" /> The name Blutopian came from the production name of the species called "Blue Top."<ref name="Production Name">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1697597980856221741|Pablo Hidalgo|date=2023-09-01|quote=The production name for this creature design was Blue Top, which is why its species name is Blutopian.|archivedate=20230901133624}}</ref>
