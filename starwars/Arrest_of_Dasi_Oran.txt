In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="2BBY" /> the [[Galactic Empire]] carried out the '''arrest''' of [[Senator]] [[Dasi Oran]], the representative of the [[planet]] [[Ghorman]] in the [[Imperial Senate]].<ref name="Welcome" /> Ghorman had long been of interest to the Empire thanks to its supply of [[Kalkite]], resulting in the drawn out [[suppression of Ghorman]]<ref name="Mess?">{{Andor|Messenger}}</ref> that culminated in the [[Ghorman Massacre]].<ref name="WAY?">{{Andor|Who Are You?}}</ref> Once Imperial victory was secured on Ghorman, [[stormtrooper]]s entered the [[Imperial Senate Building]]&mdash;something that was a rare sight&mdash;and arrested Oran without charges. Oran begged his fellow senators to take notice as he was taken way, declaring that his people were being slaughtered and the same would happen to their own worlds.<ref name="Welcome" />
