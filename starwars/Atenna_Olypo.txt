'''Atenna Olypo''' was a [[Rutian]] [[Twi'lek]] who negotiated [[Smuggler|smuggling]] routes near the neutral [[Planet|world]] [[Arthuria]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. However,<ref name="FoD" /> in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Forces of Destiny—Ahsoka & Padmé|''Star Wars: Forces of Destiny—Ahsoka & Padmé'']] to [[21 BBY]].</ref> the [[Arthurian]]s considered an alliance with the Republic, jeopardizing Olypo's business ventures. As such, Republic [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] feared that the Twi'lek might try to disturb her scheduled meeting with [[Arthurian delegates]], and as a precaution, Amidala asked [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] to protect her and her guests during the meeting. To prepare Tano for the task, Amidala showed her a [[datapad]] displaying a picture of Olypo and alerted the Jedi of the potential threat.<ref name="FoD" />

Atenna Olypo had [[Color|red]] [[eye]]s and blue skin, and wore a gray collar and a gray and green [[Clothing|dress]]. On her head Olypo wore a gold-black headdress with a red [[Gemstone|gem]], and she had black straps around her [[lekku]].<ref name="FoD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Atenna Olypo was depicted in the one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] [[Forces of Destiny—Ahsoka & Padmé|''Star Wars: Forces of Destiny—Ahsoka & Padmé'']], published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="FoD" /> on [[January 24]], [[2018]].<ref name="Comixology">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-Forces-of-Destiny-Ahsoka-Padme/digital-comic/595811|text=Star Wars Adventures: Forces of Destiny&mdash;Ahsoka & Padme}}</ref> The comic was written by [[Beth Revis]] and illustrated by [[Valentina Pinto]].<ref name="FoD" />
