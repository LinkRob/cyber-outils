'''Chandrila Star Line (CSL),<ref name="Star Wars GC Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)|''Star Wars'': Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)]]</ref>'''<ref name="Batuu Guide" /> or '''Chandrila Star Lines''', and formerly known as the '''Chandrila Charter Company'''<ref name="Princess and the Scoundrel">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref> was a company that operated luxury cruises in the [[the galaxy]] since the early years of hyperspace exploration, with their [[Purrgil-class starcruiser|''Purrgil''-class starcruisers]] being among the first commercial vessels to offer hyperdrive travel.<ref name="SWGC Overview">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref> Promising both comfort and adventure, it brought travelers to the fringes of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] from other regions.<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> Chandrila Star Line took passengers to the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] aboard a [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Corellian MPO-1400 star cruiser]] named the ''[[Halcyon]]''.<ref name="Batuu Guide" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], the company owned the [[Chandrila Charter Company shipyards]] that the [[Nihil]] [[Defense of the Halcyon|stole]] the codes for.<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 1">[[Halcyon Legacy 1|''Halcyon Legacy'' 1]]</ref>
