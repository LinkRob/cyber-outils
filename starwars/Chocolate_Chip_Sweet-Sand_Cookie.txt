The '''Chocolate Chip Sweet-Sand Cookie''' was a type of [[sweet-sand cookie]] found at the [[Milk Stand]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. The [[chocolate]] [[cookie]] was edible by [[human]]s<ref name="SWGE" /> and sold in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Chocolate Chip Sweet-Sand Cookies were available for purchase in the [[In-universe|real world]] at the Milk Stand in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]] for the [[2022]] [[Black Spire Day]] festivities, which coincided with [[May 4|Star Wars Day]] celebrations, and was available for a limited time.<ref name="Disney Parks Blog">{{DPB|url=2022/05/foodie-guide-may-the-4th-and-beyond-explore-galactic-goodies-at-disney-parks|text=Foodie Guide May the 4th and Beyond: Explore Galactic Goodies at Disney Parks|nolive=1}}</ref> The menu at the Milk Stand described the cookie as a "[[Wikipedia:Chocolate chip|Chocolate Chip]] and [[Wikipedia:Toffee|Toffee]] Cookie topped with [[Wikipedia:Pistachio|Pistachios]] and Sea [[Salt]]."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/milk-stand|text=Disneyland: Milk Stand}}</ref>
