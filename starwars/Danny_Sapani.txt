'''Danny Sapani''' (born [[November 15]], [[1970]]) is an English actor who portrayed an [[Unidentified Anodyne pilot|''Anodyne'' pilot]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.theroot.com/in-star-wars-the-last-jedi-john-boyega-people-of-col-1821323399|text=In Star Wars: The Last Jedi, John Boyega, People of Color Get to Shine in a Galaxy Far, Far Away|author=Jason, Johnson|date=2018-12-15|work=The Root|archivedate=20191218182521}}</ref><ref>{{Film|VIII}} credits</ref>

Sapani is best known for playing [[W:c:penny-dreadful:Sembene|Sembene]] in the horror drama series [[Wikipedia:Penny Dreadful (TV series)|''Penny Dreadful'']], and Berber the Moor in the historical drama series [[Wikipedia:The Bastard Executioner|''The Bastard Executioner'']].<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://talknerdywithus.com/2015/11/exclusive-interview-with-the-bastard-executioners-danny-sapani|text=Exclusive Interview with The Bastard Executioner's Danny Sapani|author=Villery, Michele|date=2015-11-17|work=Talk Nerdy With Us|archivedate=20200822095117}}</ref> Other roles of note include gang member Nate in [[Wikipedia:Danny Boyle|Danny Boyle's]] psychological thriller film [[Wikipedia:Trance (2013 film)|''Trance'']], probation officer [[W:c:misfits:Tony|Tony Morecombe]] in the first episode of the science fiction series [[Wikipedia:Misfits (TV series)|''Misfits'']], and [[W:c:tardis:Manton|Colonel Manton]] in the [[Wikipedia:Doctor Who|''Doctor Who'']] episode "[[Wikipedia:A Good Man Goes to War|A Good Man Goes to War]]."
