'''Brooke Vitale''' (born '''Dworkin''') is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] children's book author and editor. For eight years, Vitale was employed as Senior Editor and author at [[Wikipedia:Disney Publishing Worldwide|Disney Publishing Worldwide]]. During her tenure, Vitale contributed to a plethora of ''[[Star Wars]]'' titles published under the [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] imprint.

After a transition to freelancing, Vitale has continued to author ''Star Wars'' young readers titles. Most notably, Vitale has written three short stories which were collected in the [[2015]] children's storybook ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]'' and subsequent limited-release publications. Additionally, she has contributed to publishing programs tying into the [[Disney XD]] series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' and the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.

==Biography==
===''Star Wars'' beginnings===
Brooke Vitale, born Dworkin,<ref name="Linkedin">{{LinkedIn|url=in/brooke-dworkin-vitale-b51ab81|text=Brooke Dworkin Vitale|archiveurl=https://archive.is/Vt2Sf}}</ref> is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] children's book author and editor.<ref name="Reedsy" /> She is credited with work on many ''[[Star Wars]]'' titles published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]].<ref name="Storybook Library">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Storybook Library]]''</ref>

In [[July]] [[2010]], Vitale joined [[Wikipedia:Disney Publishing Worldwide|Disney Publishing Worldwide]] as an Editor and was promoted to Senior Editor three years later.<ref name="Linkedin" /> After the acquisition of [[Lucasfilm]] by [[The Walt Disney Company]] in [[2012]],<ref name="Acquisition">{{WebCite|url=http://insidemovies.ew.com/2012/12/21/walt-disney-completes-lucasfilm-acquisition|text=Mickey meets 'Star Wars': Walt Disney Co. completes acquisition of Lucasfilm|author=[https://ew.com/author/solvej-schou Schou, Solvej]|date=2012-12-21|work=[[Entertainment Weekly Books|''Entertainment Weekly'']]|archivedate=20121222151818|nolive=1}}</ref> ''Star Wars'' books began to be published by Disney Publishing Worldwide under the Disney&ndash;Lucasfilm Press banner.<ref name="Disney-Lucasfilm Press Announce>{{SW|url=news/announcing-an-exciting-new-array-of-star-wars-childrens-books|text=Announcing An Exciting New Array of ''Star Wars'' Children's Books|nolive=1}}</ref> Beginning in [[2014]], Vitale oversaw the development of a variety of ''Star Wars'' young readers titles and wrote several of them herself.<ref name="Linkedin" />

===''Star Wars Rebels: Storybook Library''===
Vitale's first ''Star Wars'' titles were published as part of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Storybook Library]]'' box set<ref name="Storybook Library" /> on [[December 22]], 2014.<ref name="Hachette Rebels Storybook">{{HachetteBookGroup|url=titles/disney-book-group/star-wars-rebels-storybook-library-btms-custom-pub/9781484725337|text=Star Wars Rebels: Storybook Library (BTMS Custom Pub)}}</ref> For the collection, she authored [[Art Attack (book)|''Art Attack'']], [[Entanglement (book)|''Entanglement'']], and [[Property of Ezra Bridger (book)|''Property of Ezra Bridger'']]. Each book retells the events of the shorts from the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of the [[Disney XD]] television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. Additionally, Vitale authored ''[[Who Are the Rebels?]]'', which provides general information about each of the main characters from the series.<ref name="Storybook Library" />

''[[TIE Fighter Trouble]]'' was adapted by Vitale from the first season episode "[[Fighter Flight]]," written by [[Kevin Hopps]].<ref name="Fighter Flight">{{Rebels|Fighter Flight}}</ref> The book was originally planned to be published independently from the box set on [[March 31]], [[2015]], but it was canceled and released exclusively in the ''Storybook Library'' in the United States.<ref name="Edelweiss TIE Fighter Trouble">{{LegacyEdelweiss|url=#sku=1484704924|text=Star Wars Rebels: TIE Fighter Trouble}}</ref> In India, ''TIE Fighter Trouble'' was still published separately by [[Scholastic]] in [[November]] 2015.<ref name="TIE Fighter Trouble back cover">[[:File:TIE Fighter Trouble Back Cover.jpg|Back cover]] of ''[[TIE Fighter Trouble]]''</ref>

===Other storybooks===
[[File:Brooke_Vitale_published_books.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Several of the ''Star Wars'' titles written by Vitale]]
Between July 2015<ref name="Galactic Adventures 2015 Release Date">{{HachetteBookGroup|url=titles/disney-book-group/star-wars-classic-story-time-tin-btms-custom-pub/9781484729090|text=Star Wars Classic: Story Time Tin (BTMS Custom Pub)|nobackup=1}}</ref> and [[December]] 2015,<ref name="5-Minute Release Date">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-5-minute-star-wars-stories|text=Star Wars: 5-Minute Star Wars Stories}}</ref> three short stories by Vitale were published in ''Star Wars''&ndash;themed illustrated storybooks. The titles included "[[Escape from Darth Vader (5-Minute Star Wars Stories)|Escape from Darth Vader]]," "[[Rescue from Jabba's Palace (Star Wars Galactic Adventures)|Rescue from Jabba's Palace]]," and "[[The Ewoks Join the Fight (short story)|The Ewoks Join the Fight]]."<ref name="fiveminute">''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]''</ref> Each story shares a title with and recycles art from standalone [[World of Reading]] books by author [[Michael Siglain]],<ref name="Siglain">''[[Ewoks Join the Fight]]''</ref> but the writing in each of Vitale's tales is wholly original.<ref name="fiveminute" />

Vitale's three short stories saw wide release with the publication of ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]''<ref name="fiveminute" /> on [[December 18]], 2015.<ref name="5-Minute Release Date" /> Additionally, the stories were published in limited-release storybooks between 2015 and [[2017]] including [[Star Wars Galactic Adventures (2015)|''Star Wars Galactic Adventures'']]<ref name="Galactic Adventures 2015">[[Star Wars Galactic Adventures (2015)|''Star Wars Galactic Adventures'' (2015)]]</ref> and [[Star Wars Galactic Adventures (2016)|its 2016 reissue]],<ref name="Galactic Adventures 2016">[[Star Wars Galactic Adventures (2016)|''Star Wars Galactic Adventures'' (2016)]]</ref> ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Hero Stories]]''<ref name="Hero Stories">''[[5-Minute Star Wars Hero Stories]]''</ref> and [[5-Minute Star Wars Villain Stories|''Villain Stories'']],<ref name="Villain Stories">''[[5-Minute Star Wars Villain Stories]]''</ref> as well as ''[[Star Wars Galactic Stories: 7 Stories from a Galaxy Far, Far Away....]]''.<ref name="Galactic Stories">''[[Star Wars Galactic Stories: 7 Stories from a Galaxy Far, Far Away....]]''</ref> On [[January 17]], [[2022]], Vitale's short stories from ''5-Minute Star Wars Stories'' were released in a [[Wikipedia:German language|German]] [[5-Minute Star Wars Stories (audiobook)|unabridged audiobook of the same name]].<ref name="Amazon 5-Minute Audiobook">According to {{Amazon|domain=de|url=exec/obidos/ASIN/3844544054|text=Star Wars 5-Minuten-Geschichten}}, the [[5-Minute Star Wars Stories (audiobook)|''5-Minute Star Wars Stories'' audiobook]] is an unabridged adaptation. By definition, this means that every story included in the original printed release will be adapted into the audiobook format. As Vitale is credited as having written several titles in the short story collection, her stories must be adapted into the audiobook.</ref>

As an editor, Vitale contributed to ''[[3 Out-of-This-World Tales!]]'', a collection of three World of Reading books with an accompanying audio CD.<ref name="Vitale">{{WebCite|url=https://brookevitale.com|text=Brooke Vitale|author=Vitale, Brooke|work=brookevitale.com|nobackup=1}} {{C|[[:File:BrookeVitaleEditingPortfolio.png|Screenshot]]}}</ref> The title was published on [[August 29]], 2017<ref name="3 Out-of-This-World Amazon">{{Amazon|url=World-Reading-3-Listen-Along-Adventure/dp/1368002161|text=World of Reading: Star Wars Star Wars 3-in-1 Listen-Along Reader (World of Reading Level 1): 3 Tales of Adventure with CD!}}</ref> and is the last ''Star Wars'' title Vitale worked on as Senior Editor at Disney Publishing.<ref name="Vitale" />

===Freelance work===
In [[January]] [[2018]], after the birth of her sons, Vitale left her position at Disney Publishing and transitioned to freelance editing and writing.<ref name="Webinar">{{YouTube|video=2Tn8LzE9C8Q|text=4 Things You Need To Know Before You Write a Picture Book|channel=UCpo0MkTAyEl73PmIVs8CQvQ|name=Reedsy}}</ref> Though she no longer holds a position at Disney, Vitale has still authored several titles for Disney&ndash;Lucasfilm Press as a freelancer.<ref name="Mandalorian Publishing Program">{{SW|url=news/the-mandalorian-publishing-program-revealed-exclusive|text=''The Mandalorian'' Publishing Program Revealed - Exclusive}}</ref>

In [[2019]], Vitale wrote [[Star Wars Epic Adventures: Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']], a 96-page hardcover illustrated storybook adaptation of the [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|eponymous film]]. The story was written from the perspective<ref name="Bende ROTS">{{WebCite|url=https://www.stbende.com/kids-books/star-wars-books/star-wars-epic-adventures/star-wars-epic-adventures-revenge-of-the-sith|text=Star Wars Epic Adventures: Revenge of the Sith|author=[[S.T. Bende|Bende, S.T.]]|work=[https://www.stbende.com Author S.T. Bende]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/bgdIm}}</ref> of the [[protocol droid]]<ref name="IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[C-3PO]] and was scheduled to be published in a joint venture between Disney&ndash;Lucasfilm Press and [[Sandvik Publishing]] as part of the latter's ''[[Star Wars Epic Adventures]]'' monthly subscription series. While the book was completed and several copies were printed,<ref name="Bende ROTS" /> it never reached publication as the ''Epic Adventures'' series was canceled before the book's scheduled release.<ref name="ROTS Canceled Explain">{{Amazon|url=Star-Wars-Epic-Adventures-Subscription/dp/B07SYR7V9W|text=Star Wars Epic Adventures Subscription}} explains that the ''[[Star Wars Epic Adventures]]'' subscription series was canceled after its second monthly drop, featuring [[Star Wars Epic Adventures: Attack of the Clones|''Attack of the Clones'']]. Since the series overall was canceled, it can be inferred that [[Star Wars Epic Adventures: Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']] was canceled as well.</ref>

Two short stories by Vitale<ref name="5-Min 2020">[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories (second edition)|''5-Minute Star Wars Stories'', second edition]]</ref> were scheduled to be published in the United States by Disney&ndash;Lucasfilm Press in [[5-Minute Star Wars Stories (second edition)|''5-Minute Star Wars Stories'']], a refresh of the 2015 title of the same time, though the book never saw release.<ref name="Edelweiss">{{LegacyEdelweiss|url=#sku=1368051871|text=5-Minute Star Wars Stories (Refresh)}}</ref> The short stories eventually saw publication internationally, starting with an Italian publication on [[January 20]], [[2021]].<ref name="Italy">{{Amazon|domain=it|url=Racconti-avventure-altri-mondi-colori/dp/8833470555|text=Racconti per avventure da altri mondi. Star Wars. Ediz. a colori}}</ref> Vitale's short stories, "[[An Unlikely Ally]]" and "[[A Spark of Hope]]" adapt sequences from<ref name="5-Min 2020" /> the 2015 film<ref name="TFA Date">{{Film|VII}}</ref> {{Film|VII}} and the 2017 film<ref name="TLJ Date">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> {{Film|VIII}} respectively.<ref name="5-Min 2020" />

===''The Mandalorian''===
On [[June 30]], [[2020]], Vitale was announced as a contributor to [[The Mandalorian publishing program|''The Mandalorian'' publishing program]], a multi-publisher initiative releasing books related to the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.<ref name="Mandalorian Publishing Program" /> Her first two books as part of the program, ''[[The Mandalorian: Allies & Enemies]]''<ref name="Hachette Allies">{{HachetteBookGroup|url=titles/brooke-vitale/star-wars-the-mandalorian-allies-enemies-level-2-reader/9781368070812|text=Star Wars: The Mandalorian: Allies & Enemies Level 2 Reader}}</ref> and ''[[The Mandalorian: A Clan of Two]]''<ref name="Hachette Clan">{{HachetteBookGroup|url=titles/brooke-vitale/star-wars-the-mandalorian-a-clan-of-two/9781368070720|text=Star Wars: The Mandalorian: A Clan Of Two}}</ref> adapted storylines from [[The Mandalorian Season One|''The Mandalorian'' Season One]] and were published on [[December 1]], 2020.<ref name="Hachette Allies" /><ref name="Hachette Clan" /> On [[May 4]], 2021,<ref name="Amazon Die-Cut">{{Amazon|url=dp/0794447694|text=Star Wars: The Mandalorian Die-Cut Classic (Disney Die-Cut Classics)}}</ref> the storybook ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian: Volume 1]]'', Vitale's full adaptation of the series' first season, was published as part of the ''[[Disney Die-Cut Classics]]'' series from publisher [[Studio Fun International]].<ref name="Die-Cut">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian: Volume 1]]''</ref>

Vitale also authored ''[[The Mandalorian: The Mandalorian's Quest]]''<ref name="Edelweiss Mandalorian's Quest">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-the-mandalorian-the-mandalorians-quest|text=Star Wars: The Mandalorian: The Mandalorian's Quest}}</ref> and ''[[The Mandalorian: The Path of the Force]]''<ref name="Edelweiss Path of the Force">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-the-mandalorian-the-mandalorians-quest|text=Star Wars: The Mandalorian: The Mandalorian's Quest}}</ref> which adapt events from [[The Mandalorian Season Two|''The Mandalorian'' Season Two]].<ref name="Edelweiss Path of the Force" /> The titles were published by Disney&ndash;Lucasfilm Press on [[January 11]], 2022.<ref name="Edelweiss Mandalorian's Quest" /><ref name="Edelweiss Path of the Force" />
