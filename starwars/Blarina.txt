The '''Blarina''' were a short [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that were [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Rina Major]] who were notably fond of words and were famously accomplished liars. Many other species found it difficult to tell different Blarina apart, which some Blarina used to their advantage when dealing with others. They had a broad face with a short, narrow snout; small, beady [[eye]]s; and a [[mouth]] full of sharp [[Tooth|teeth]].

Around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], a Blarina named [[Wollivan]] was present in the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of [[Maz Kanata]] shortly before [[Battle of Takodana|its destruction]] by the [[First Order]]. Around the same time, the Blarina [[Naka Iit]] and [[Ohn Gos]] worked as a [[scavenger]] and a [[merchant]] respectively on the [[desert]] planet [[Jakku]], and aided the [[Resistance]] [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] in getting off the planet.

==Biology and appearance==
The Blarina were a short, pudgy<ref name="AotG" /> bipedal [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] who were not especially strong, but made up for it by being incredibly quick,<ref name="TFAn" /> rugged and resourceful. With tough skin, they could survive in both high and low [[temperature]] extremes and through decompression, and were resistant to [[poison]] and disease.<ref name="AotG" /> Most other sentient species had trouble telling Blarina apart based on their appearance, with members having broad faces with short, wide snouts and a small pair of [[eye]]s with slitted pupils. Below their snout, the Blarina had a [[mouth]] with more sharp [[Tooth|teeth]] than a [[human]] mouth contained, including incisors. The species' skin was scaly<ref name="TFAn" /> and could be [[Color|pink]]<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> in color, while their eyes could have a golden hue. They had five-fingered hands with claws at the end of each digit.<ref name="TFAn" />

Blarina were susceptible to the [[Anoat pox]], a disease that left colored marks on the skin amongst other symptoms.<ref name="SWU">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Daxxus"</ref>

==Society and culture==
{{Quote|It's said that the Blarina are an exceptionally hospitable people.''"<br />"''You've heard that? That must be referring to some other Blarina. It certainly doesn't sound like me.|Pilot Poe Dameron and Naka Iit|<ref name="TFAn" />}}
Amongst the most notable traits<ref name="TFAn" /> of the highly sociable<ref name="AotG" /> Blarina were their fondness for words and speech, which made some members of the species quick with words. They could speak [[Galactic Basic Standard]], and convey sarcasm, despair, delight and amusement through their tones when using the language. The species was also famously known for being accomplished liars, with at least one member of the species often lying about his identity in order to avoid trouble and take advantage of the problems other species had in identifying individual Blarina. They often hissed during speech and made a cackling hiss when delighted. The species were not especially known for their hospitality, although at least one member of the species was sympathetic to others, and another offered a stranger lost in the desert transport to civilization.<ref name="TFAn" /> Blarina would stick together when faced with species of larger size, often forming family-based guilds or mercantile associations.<ref name="AotG" />

==Blarina in the galaxy==
{{Quote|I'm Naka Iit. A scavenger of sorts. I might just scavenge you.|Naka Iit to Poe Dameron|<ref name="TFAn" />}}
[[Daxxus]] was a Blarina in the [[Anoat sector]] who avoided an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] inspection of their cargo by describing the symptoms of the Anoat pox and having berry marks on their face.<ref name="SWU" /> By [[5 BBY]],<ref name="time">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Daxxus" describes the deterring of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] inspection in the past tense. The [[Star Wars video games|game]] is set during [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s [[Iron Blockade]], which was defeated just before the [[Battle of Jakku]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Battle of Jakku to [[5 ABY]], the events of ''Uprising'' must take place by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> this incident was described to [[the Smuggler]], leader of the [[Uprising]] who had recruited Daxxus to their cause.<ref name="SWU" />

The Blarina [[Naka Iit]] began working as a [[scavenger]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] around ten [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], and made the acquaintance of [[Ohn Gos]], another Blarina living on Jakku who worked as a [[merchant]] in [[Blowback Town]]. Twenty years into Iit's career he encountered the [[Resistance]] [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] lost in the [[desert]] and agreed to take him to Blowback Town to try and arrange transport off world with Gos, who was generally more sympathetic to others. En route to the town, Iit and Dameron were attacked by the [[Strus clan]], but the pilot shook off the pursuers, earning Iit's gratitude. The Blarina agreed to intercede with Gos in thanks, and the two Blarina arranged Dameron transport off world, allowing him to return to the [[Resistance base]] on the planet [[D'Qar]].<ref name="TFAn" />

The [[hyperspace]] [[scout]] [[Wollivan]] was a Blarina with a large family, and due to their similar appearance, often claimed to be different members of his family to avoid trouble.<ref name="TFAVD" /> Shortly after Iit and Gos helped Dameron,<ref name="TFAn" /> Wollivan was in the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]] when the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] arrived looking for help getting the [[astromech droid]] [[BB-8]] to the Resistance. After Solo's arrival, the occupants of the castle were drawn outside to witness the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[Hosnian system]] by the [[First Order]]'s [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]]. The castle was then [[Battle of Takodana|attacked]] and destroyed by the First Order.<ref name="TFA" /> Wollivan wore a [[Blarina vac-suit]] while at the castle.<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
A member of the Blarina species first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], but the species' appearance was first revealed in an exclusive [[Wikipedia:Vanity Fair|''Vanity Fair'']] [http://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2015/05/star-wars-force-awakens-photos article] published seven months before the film's release in [[2015]].<ref name="Vanity Fair">{{WebCite|url=http://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2015/05/star-wars-force-awakens-photos|text=Adam Driver and Lupita Nyong'o's Characters Revealed in Exclusive Star Wars: The Force Awakens Photos|author=Leibovitz, Annie|date=2015-05-04|archivedate=20180620101746}}</ref> The species was not named in the article or film. but received a name in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in conjunction with the film.
