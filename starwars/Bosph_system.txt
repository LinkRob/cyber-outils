The '''Bosph system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Bosph sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It fell within grid square Q-3 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained [[Bosph]], a [[planet]] that the [[Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] repeatedly [[Bombardment of Bosph|bombarded]] in [[3 BBY]] in an attempt to commit genocide on a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Unidentified Bosph species|species]] that [[Homeworld|dwelt]] there. Those members of the [[species]] who survived chose to [[Dis-remembering|dis-remember]] the Empire.<ref name="Rise" /> 

During the [[High Republic Era]], the Bosph sector was known as the unincorporated Bosphian provinces, and the [[Bosphian sestertius]] was a common currency throughout the then-informal sector.<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Bosph system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Bosph system/Legends|Bosph system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Alien Encounters]]'', a [[1998]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="AE">''[[Alien Encounters]]''</ref>
