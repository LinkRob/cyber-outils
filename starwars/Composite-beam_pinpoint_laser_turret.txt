The '''composite-beam pinpoint laser turret''' was a model of composite-beam [[laser turret]] used on [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> and emitted a [[Color|green]] [[laser]] beam.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> Two [[turret]]s were optional bow side-mounted manned turrets, while two others were remote controlled and located on each wing.<ref name="CVD" />

The [[Prototype B6|prototype Blade-wing]] designed by [[Mon Calamari]] [[Engineer]] [[Quarrie]] utilised one of these as its primary heavy weapon against [[Capital ship]]s, and it was an optional configuration for the production model [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and later [[New Republic]].
