'''Droid targeting systems''' were specialized [[droid brain]]s that acted as [[targeting computer]]s for ranged weapons. They utilized [[sensor]]s and miniature gyromotors to improve their user's ranged accuracy. Most [[droid]] targeting systems developed bossy and impatient personalities, and were looked down upon by many [[soldier]]s. Some users also found the vibrations caused by the droid adjusting their aim to be frustrating. Droid targeting systems cost 3,200 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and could be equipped to any ranged weapon. Notable models included the [[Baktoid Industries]] [[T-80 Droid Targeter]] and the [[GoCorp]] [["Surefire" Enhancement System]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
Droid targeting systems were first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref>
