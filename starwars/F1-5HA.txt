'''F1-5HA''' was an [[astromech droid]] who served the [[Resistance]] on the [[planet]] [[Ajan Kloss]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War]]. The [[droid]] was one of five astromechs who worked under [[R5-2JE]] and who were all absent from their jobs at the landing pad one day. F1-5HA was busy trying to fish a king-sized catch down by the river and refused to leave until the droid caught a large catch.<ref name="LEGO saga" />

==Behind the scenes==
F1-5HA first appeared in the [[2022]] non-canon video game ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', published by [[TT Games]]. The droid name's spelling resembles the slang word "Fisha," which represents the droids character.<ref name="LEGO saga" />
