An individual was the '''Jedi Master''' of the eventual [[Kadas'sa'Nikto]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Aida Forte]]. At one point, they taught Forte that it was easier to let [[water]] run downhill than push rain back into [[cloud]]s. When Forte and [[Creighton Sun]] visited<ref name="Cataclysm" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Dalna]] to [[Mission to Dalna (Sun and Forte)|investigate]] the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]], Forte quoted her master.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
