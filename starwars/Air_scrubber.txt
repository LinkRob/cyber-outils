An '''air scrubber''' was a device that cleaned and purified air.<ref name="TK">{{InsiderCite|166|TK-462 (short story)}}</ref> [[Sentinel Base]], commanded by [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], employed a number of scrubbers to clear out the staleness from its air and filter [[ozone]], but they were overworked.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> The [[parent]]s of the man who became the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] [[TK-462]] operated a scrubber farm on [[Eriadu]].<ref name="TK" />
