A '''council''' controlled the [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] on the [[planet]] [[Andelm IV]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], the [[gangster]] [[Jaum]] paid the council to turn a blind [[eye]] to his [[mining]] operations in the [[Andelm beetle caverns]], where he forced [[Slavery|enslaved]] debtors to harvest [[dedlanite]] for him. When [[Zarro]], one of Jaum's workers, escaped the [[cave]]s and attempted to report his crime, she was told by the local law enforcement they could not help due to Jaum's bribing of the council.<ref name="Chewbacca 1" />
