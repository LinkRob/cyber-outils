'''Force healing''' was a rare [[Force power|Force ability]] to heal oneself or others through the use of [[the Force]]. It was mostly used to mend physical wounds, often at a deep cost to the user, however, it could also be used to repair a shattered mind or mend a bleed kyber crystal.

==Description==
===Aiding another===
{{Quote|I just transferred a bit of life, Force energy from me to him.|Rey after healing a [[vexis]]|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:BabyYoda_Force_Heal.png|thumb|left|250px|Grogu using the Force to heal Greef Karga's arm wound.]]
Force healing was a rare [[Force power]] wielded by only some [[Force-sensitive]]s.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> The user of [[The Force|Force]] healing would place their hand over the wound,<ref name="TROS French" /> or wave a hand towards the wounded person,<ref name="SWBII" /> and pour their own life energy into it, healing the target, although at the expenditure of the user's own vitality, which could be later recovered by resting or meditating.<ref name="TROS French">[[The Rise of Skywalker (French junior novelization)|''The Rise of Skywalker'' French junior novelization]]</ref> The Force could also allow a being to overcome exhaustion.<ref name="UP" /> The ability was also capable of curing the user's target of [[poison]]ing. Sometimes there was no visible display of this power beyond the mending of flesh.<ref name="Chapter 7">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 7: The Reckoning}}</ref> Other times, there was a blue-white pulse,<ref name="SWBII" /> or a [[Color|golden glow]] of healing.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony" />

At least in the rare case of a [[Force dyad]], the [[Ben Solo|user]] was able to transfer enough of their own Force energy to bring their [[Rey Skywalker|target]]&mdash;the other half of their [[Force-bond|bond]][[Resurrection|—back to life]], though at the cost of their own.<ref name="Episode IX" /> By going into a trance, a Force user trained in the art could heal more quickly.<ref name="UP">''[[Unlimited Power]]''</ref> While the Force was a mighty tool for healing, sometimes technological means of mending wounds were faster and more efficient.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony">''[[Disciples of Harmony]]''</ref>

===Alternate Applications===
[[File:ReyHealingKyber-TheRiseOfSkywalker1.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Rey healing a damaged kyber crystal.]]
An alternate usage of Force healing was described in the [[sacred Jedi texts]], specifically the ''[[Chronicles of Brus-bu]]''. The method in question detailed the ability to heal a damaged [[kyber crystal]] through the use of Force healing. [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] used the technique to heal the [[crystal]] of [[Skywalker lightsaber|Anakin Skywalker's lightsaber]], which had been cracked during the [[Battle of Crait]]<ref name="EpIXVD" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date Note">{{FilmDate|VIII}}</ref> There also existed talismans, such as necklaces, that were imbued with the Force, that would improve the healing of those who possessed them.<ref name="UP" />

Many alchemists who studied the healing arts fell to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]].<ref name="UP" /> The art of healing, as well as harming with the Force, was of particular note to the [[Sith Lord]]s.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony" /> In practice, as seen when [[Darth Sidious]] forcefully drew from the powers of a Force dyad to heal himself, at least one [[Sith]]-aligned power, Force Drain, that acted like Force healing would see the Sith individual forcefully take the energies of another in the name of healing themself. Doing this restored his eyes, withered body, twisted hands, and allowed him to stand without cybernetics for the first time in decades. Meanwhile, [[Jedi]]-aligned Force healing saw the Jedi willingly offer some of their own life to aid those in need.<ref name="Episode IX" /> The dark side&ndash;aligned [[Nightsisters]] used [[Magick|Force magick]] to heal.<ref name="UP" /><ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> The Nightsister style could be used to cure mental ailments as well.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> They could also wield the dark side to craft potions to cure the wounded and [[Water of Life|revive the recently deceased]].<ref name="UP" />

==History==
===Republic Era===
[[Jedi Master]] [[Char-Ryl-Roy]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Enya Keen]], of the [[High Republic Era]] did not have the power of Force healing. When they encountered [[Goi Ganok]], someone who was secretly a member of the radical anti-Jedi [[Path of the Open Hand]] and thus believed the Force was not to be used by any living being, Ganok recoiled at the suggestion that the Jedi would heal him, only for Keen to reveal she nor her master had access to Force healing and that they would instead offer medicine. Ganok's reaction helped key the Jedi into his true allegiances.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> During the [[Republic Era|Fall of the Republic]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Stass Allie]] was a notable [[Jedi healer|practitioner]] of Force healing. [[Gender|She]] was a member of the [[Circle of Jedi Healers]] and served as the [[overseer]] of the [[Medical Corps]]. Even during her deployment as a [[Jedi General]] during the [[Clone Wars]], Allie continued to hone her healing abilities.<ref name="ForceCollection">{{ForceCollection|Stass Allie|star=3}}</ref>

[[File:TransferForce-AOM.png|thumb|left|300px|With the help of the Daughter, Anakin was able to bring back his apprentice Ahsoka Tano]]
[[Rig Nema]] was learned in Jedi healing arts, especially a risky meditation intended to open the mind.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> [[Padawan]] [[Barriss Offee]] could also use the ability<ref name="SWCEUE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> and was credited with the quick recovery of all Jedi injured in the [[First Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="Queen's Hope">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref> [[Jedi Knight|Knight]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] was capable of limited self-healing.<ref name="Inquisitor">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker]] used a variation of this ability on [[Mortis]], with help from the mortally wounded Force-wielder known as the [[Daughter]], and [[Force drain|drained]] her Force energy to revive [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Altar of Mortis">{{TCW|Altar of Mortis (episode)}}</ref> [[Grand Master|Grandmaster]] [[Yoda]] was so skilled with this power that he could heal himself<ref name="Allies and Adversaries">''[[Allies and Adversaries]]''</ref> or all in his immediate vicinity with just a wave of his hand.<ref name="SWBII">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

===Imperial Era===
[[File:ForceHealer-Messenger.jpg|thumb|right|200px|A Force healer on Yavin IV]]
A [[Unidentified Force healer|Force healer]] was among the ranks of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="JKE">{{Andor|Jedha, Kyber, Erso}}</ref> having joined the rebellion and been welcomed to the [[Great Temple|Yavin headquarters]] sometime before<ref name="Messenger">{{Andor|Messenger}}</ref> the Alliance's official foundation<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref> in [[2 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> At least by the end of his life, Obi-Wan Kenobi also had the skill of Force healing.<ref name="Allies and Adversaries" />

===New Republic Era===
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="The Mandalorian Date">{{MandoDate|7}}</ref> the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] and his allies traveled to [[Nevarro]] to put an end to the bounty placed on the Force-sensitive infant [[Din Grogu|Grogu]]. In preparation for a [[Clash on Nevarro]], Djarin and his allies camped out in an uninhabited region of the planet. During the night, several [[Nevarro reptavian|native reptavians]] attacked the party. Although the creatures were fought off, [[Greef Karga]] sustained a venomous wound to his right arm. In response, Grogu healed Karga's wound using the Force, although the effort greatly strained the infant.<ref name="Chapter 7" /> Sometime around [[11 ABY]], Din Djarin would be captured by the [[The Twins|Hutt Twins]] and sent to face a [[Dragonsnake]]. Although Djarin survived, he would get bit by the beast and [[Poison|poisoned]] as a result. When Grogu, now Djarin's adopted son and apprentice, came to rescue Djarin alongside the [[Anzellan Droidsmiths]], Djarin told Grogu to leave [[Nal Hutta|the planet]]. Despite this, Grogu would stay, finding Djarin in a forest and attempting to Force heal his wound. Though this wouldn't work and Djarin would ultimately be saved thanks to an [[Dragonsnake venom antidote|antidote]] made by Gatori.<ref name="Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref> [[Luke Skywalker]] studied the techniques of Force healing, among other powers.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref><ref name="EpIXVD" /> Rey sought to use the Force to recover from being slashed by a guard while she and Kylo Ren fought for their lives against Snoke's protectors, keen on avenging their lord.<ref name="Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>  

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Episode IX Date">{{FilmDate|IX}}</ref> the Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and her comrades in the [[Resistance]] encountered a [[vexis]] on [[Pasaana]] after sinking into an underground tunnel system through the [[Shifting Mires]]. Rey's companions [[C-3PO]] and [[Poe Dameron]] feared the creature, believing that there was no way past the vexis and that it was hostile. However, Rey realized the vexis was injured and approached the creature. Using the Force, Rey healed the vexis' wound and soothed its mind. The creature subsequently slivered off, revealing a passageway back to the surface that it had initially been blocking.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:Rey_heals_Kylo_Ren.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Rey healing Kylo Ren's wound on Kef Bir after she impaled him.]]
Later, during a [[Mission to Kef Bir]], Rey dueled [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] on the [[Death Star ruins|ruins]] of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. Although Ren was dominating most of the duel, he was caught off guard when he sensed the death of his mother [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], allowing Rey to impale him with his [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|own weapon]]. Realizing she had attempted to slay Ren in an act of rage, Rey immediately regretted her actions. Rey then proceeded to heal Kylo Ren's wound using the Force, before fleeing the planet in [[Kylo Ren's TIE whisper|Ren's TIE whisper]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

Shortly after the Mission to Kef Bir, Rey lost her life in battle against the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]] (who had himself healed his body by stealing health from the Jedi) during the [[Battle of Exegol]]. Ben Solo, who had since discarded his Kylo Ren identity and opposed Sidious alongside Rey, was a dyad in the Force with the aforementioned Jedi. As such, he was able to use Force healing to restore Rey's life. However, the amount of vitality required for her resurrection completely drained Solo, who subsequently vanished into the Force. Similarly, as shown by Rey's [[Parent|grandfather]], the power [[Force drain]] could be used to mend flesh, eyes, and more by stealing health from others.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
Force healing first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Altar of Mortis (episode)|Altar of Mortis]]," the sixteenth episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Altar of Mortis" /> which aired on [[February 4]], [[2011]].<ref name="Altar of Mortis release date">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode316.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Altar of Mortis}}</ref> The power was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the novel [[Splinter of the Mind's Eye]]. The role-playing game rulebook [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game (first edition)|''Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game'']] gave game rules for Force Healing and [[Retroactive continuity|retroactively established]] it to have been used in the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].

Similar to the method prescribed by the ''Chronicles of Brus-bu'', it is possible to restore a [[Bleeding|corrupted, red-colored]] kyber crystal. [[Ahsoka Tano]] purified two such crystals she recovered from the [[Death|fallen]] [[Sixth Brother]]'s [[lightsaber]] following their [[Raada Uprising|duel]] on [[Raada]]. With the presence of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] removed, the healed crystals turned [[Color|white]], following which the former [[Jedi]] incorporated them into [[Ahsoka Tano's white lightsabers|her new lightsabers]].<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> The idea of purifying dark side crystals was first introduced into Star Wars in the Legends novel ''Riptide''.<ref name="Riptide (novel)">[[Riptide (novel)|''Riptide'']]</ref>

How Rey learned Force healing was originally set to appear in a cancelled episode of ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'',<ref name="Wook">{{WookYouTube|ML1nYpSU0d0|Wookieepedia Interviews - Brandon Auman}}</ref> "[[The Lost Book]],"<ref name="MelchingWook">{{WookYouTube|_aRy5PWaiPY|Wookieepedia Interviews - Steven Melching (Part 1)}}</ref> which was scripted for the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] but cut due to Disney's budgetary issues. The episode would have included [[Maz Kanata]] recruiting both [[Mika Grey]]'s and [[Kazuda Xiono]]'s help to [[Mission to acquire a Jedi text|retrieve]] an ancient [[Jedi]] [[Unidentified Jedi text|text]] for Rey.<ref name="Wook" />
