'''2015 Topps ''Star Wars'' Illustrated: ''The Empire Strikes Back''''' is a [[Topps]] card set that illustrates the original [[1983]] [[The Empire Strikes Back (radio)|radio adaptation]] of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. The set was released on [[April 29]], [[2015]] as the follow up to [[2013 Topps Star Wars Illustrated: A New Hope|2013 Topps ''Star Wars'' Illustrated: ''A New Hope'']].

2015 Topps ''Star Wars'' Illustrated: ''The Empire Strikes Back'' comprises 100 base cards and three insert subsets: One Year Earlier, Movie Poster Reinterpretations, and The Mission: Capture Skywalker. The 100 base cards also come in purple, bronze, or gold parallels. Additional hits include ''The Empire Strikes Back'' Film Cel Relics, Sketch Cards, Panorama Sketch Cards, Artist Autographs, Sketchagraphs, and Printing Plates.

Artists [[Matt Busch]] and [[Randy Martinez]] illustrated the base set, [[Grant Gould]] illustrated the One Year Earlier subset, while [[Jerry Vanderstelt]], [[Tony Harris]], [[Mike Mayhew]], [[Chad McGowan]], [[Michael Allred]], [[Tom Hodges]], [[Jason Crosby]], [[Chris Trevas]], [[Doug Cowan]], and [[Kevin Graham]] contributed the Movie Poster Reinterpretations.

==Development==
Topps previewed the 2015 Topps ''Star Wars'' Illustrated: ''The Empire Strikes Back'' card set at [[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim]] from [[April 16]]&ndash;[[April 19|19]], [[2015]]. Fans attending the four-day convention were given the opportunity to purchase a Celebration-exclusive 2015 Topps ''Star Wars'' Illustrated: ''The Empire Strikes Back'' ten-card mini-set as well as collect autographs from the set's contributing artists on show-exclusive cards.<ref name="Facebook 4/16">{{Facebook|username=OfficialToppsStarWars|url=OfficialToppsStarWars/photos/a.305898082871093.1073741825.274996949294540/695074750620089|name=[[Topps]]|date=April 16, 2015|quote=Check out our booth at Star Wars Celebration! Come visit Topps Booth #1105 for exclusive merchandise, free giveaways, signings from sketch artists, and more!|archivefile=File:ToppsCelebration4-16.png}}</ref><ref name="Facebook 4/17">{{Facebook|username=OfficialToppsStarWars|url=OfficialToppsStarWars/photos/a.305898082871093.1073741825.274996949294540/695754800552084|name=[[Topps]]|date=April 17, 2015|quote=Come to Topps Booth #1105 right now at Star Wars Celebration to meet artist Matt Busch signing copies of his Topps 'Star Wars Illustrated: The Empire Strikes Back' show-exclusive cards.|archivefile=File:ToppsCelebration4-17.png}}</ref><ref name="Facebook 4/19">{{Facebook|username=OfficialToppsStarWars|url=OfficialToppsStarWars/photos/a.305898082871093.1073741825.274996949294540/696649923795905|name=[[Topps]]|date=April 19, 2015|quote=It's the final day of Star Wars Celebration! Swing by Topps Booth #1105 to pick up our exclusive Star Wars Celebration Illustrated card set featuring 10 character cards all together. Here's Jedi Master Yoda. See you there!|archivefile=File:ToppsCelebration4-19.png}}</ref>

The full factory set went on sale beginning [[April 29]], 2015.<ref name="Facebook 4/29" /> The 2015 Topps ''Star Wars'' Illustrated: ''The Empire Strikes Back'' official checklist on the Topps website includes noticeable numerical gaps in listing the set's Film Cel Relics cards. However, rather than an incomplete checklist, these gaps represent cards that were not produced, whereas other cards could not have their numbering changed.<ref name="Facebook 4/30">{{Facebook|username=OfficialToppsStarWars|url=OfficialToppsStarWars/photos/a.305898082871093.1073741825.274996949294540/702374326556798|name=[[Topps]]|date=April 30, 2015|quote=Some cards didn't get produced but then we couldn't change the numbering so there are gaps. Apologies about that. But the checklist is comprehensive|archivefile=File:Topps4-30.png}}</ref>
