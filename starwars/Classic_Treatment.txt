The '''Classic Treatment''' was a bodily configuration owned by the [[droideka]] [[Team Slingshot droideka|member]] of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]] that she could swap for her standard [[Beloved Paintwork|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This configuration featured a primarily [[Color|black]] and dark gray frame, with distinctly-shaped [[Limb|arm]] [[cannon]]s featuring what appeared to be a grill and headlights, with glowing yellow components, including the interior of the [[barrel]], as well as red-tipped [[Missile|rockets]] attached to the side.<ref name="Hunters" />

The droideka featured extra [[armor]] plating on the shoulders and head, with red and yellow lines running up and over the top of the head, while the letter "S" was displayed in [[Aurebesh]] on white patches on the side. Glowing yellow [[sensor]]s also protruded from the front of the droideka's face.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Classic Treatment was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.16.0 on [[March 13]], [[2023]] during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Classic Racer]]. Unlike most Weapon Wraps in the game, Classic Treatment did not share the same rarity as its cosmetic set counterpart, with Classic Racer instead being an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap."<ref name="Hunters" />
