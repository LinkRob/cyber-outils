Shortly after the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] scattered in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Hoth]], [[Commander]] [[Ellian Zahra]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] initiated a '''battle''' with the rebel [[Eighth Division]], situated near [[Malastare]]. Having discovered the locations of both the Eighth and [[Fourth Division]]s using a custom signal analysis protocol, Zahra led her own [[Battle at Rendezvous Point Delta-Three|attack]] on the Fourth Division. The rebel [[fleet]] near Malastare was trapped by an [[interdictor]] vessel, and the Eighth Division was completely destroyed.<ref name="Star Wars 1" />
