"'''Droids, Aliens & Creatures'''" is a feature of ''[[Star Wars: The Official Starships & Vehicles Collection]]'' magazine. It provides backstory, a profile, and a behind-the-scenes glimpse on a figure closely associated with the main character featured on the issue's cover.

==Articles by issue==
*[[Star Wars: The Official Starships & Vehicles Collection 6|''Star Wars: The Official Starships & Vehicles Collection'' 6]]: '''[[Bib Fortuna/Legends|Bib Fortuna]]''': Obsequious [[Majordomo/Legends|Major-Domo]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba]] the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]]
**For many years, any who sought to do business with Jabba first had to do business with Fortuna. Outwardly fawningly loyal to his master, Fortuna in fact hated the Hutt, and was plotting to usurp him.
