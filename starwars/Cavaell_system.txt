{{Quote|This one old 434 unit had the most spectacular recipe for Cavaellin creams&mdash;snappy little cookies from the Cavaell system filled with exotic fruit.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref>}}
The '''Cavaell system''' was located in the [[Emmo sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="The Interior" /> It was situated in grid square M-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The Cavell [[Star system|system]] was the origin point of the [[cookie]]s known as [[Cavaellin Spice Creams|Cavaellin creams]]. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] learned a recipe for the cookies from an old [[434 unit]] when he worked at [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on [[planet]] [[Takodana]]. Tuggs mentioned their origin point in the recipe for Cavaellin Spice Creams that he published in one of his [[Book|cookbooks]], ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which he wrote sometime after the [[Battle of Takodana]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Cavaell system was introduced to the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', authored by [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and [[Marc Sumerak]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Cavaell system/Legends|Cavaell system]] was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
