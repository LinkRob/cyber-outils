The '''Council of Neutral Systems''' was a [[Politics|political]] organization in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] that consisted of over 1,500 [[star system]]s that wished to remain neutral during the [[Clone Wars]], the conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Formed in [[22 BBY]], the council was led by [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] of the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]].

==Description==
{{Quote|This newly formed Council of Neutral Systems will stand on principles, not violence. For once we commit to violence, we have already lost. Extremism is rising across the galaxy. It must be extinguished before more lives are lost. There is only one solution for all of us, on all sides: de-escalation. And there is only one path to de-escalation: refusing to contribute to this war.|Satine Kryze|<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref>}}
The Council of Neutral Systems was a group of the [[Galactic Senate]]<ref name="Rise" /> that consisted of 1,500 [[star system]]s that were led by [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] of the [[New Mandalorians]] and included [[Senator]] [[Prince]] [[Tal Merrik]] of [[Kalevala]], Senator [[Onaconda Farr]] of [[Rodia]], Senator [[Zinn Paulness]], Senator [[Kin Robb]] of [[Taris]], and Senator [[Dantum Roohd]].<ref name="The Mandalore Plot">{{TCW|The Mandalore Plot}}</ref> The Council of Neutral Systems was an outlier wing of the [[Pacifist faction]] in the [[Galactic Republic]] Senate, and as such, abstained from most votes related to the war effort and voted against endeavors to censure or sanction neutral systems for [[treason]]ous trade or other economic activities.<ref name="Rise" />

Most of the economies of members of the Council of Neutral Systems needed free trade with both the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in order to have sustainability, and wanted what was best for their [[planet]]s, although some members attempted to seek war profits. Traditionally, the Council of Neutral Systems introduced delegates from outside the Republic and offered them senate pods and time on the floor to address the Galactic Senate.<ref name="Rise" /> As the designated regent of the organization, Duchess Kryze was the primary representative of the group in negotiations with the Galactic Republic.<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> [[Senator]] [[Orn Free Taa]] enjoyed ties with the Council of Neutral Systems despite his position as a [[Loyalist]].<ref name="BioGall 6">{{SW|url=orn-free-taa-biography-gallery|text=Orn Free Taa Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref>

==History==
In [[22 BBY]], the Galactic Republic entered [[Clone Wars|war]] with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] after years of increasing tensions between the two governments.<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Despite being a nominal member of the Republic,<ref name="Duchess">{{TCW|Duchess of Mandalore}}</ref> Duchess Satine Kryze of [[Mandalore]] declared her planet neutral, wanting to avoid letting her people be drawn into war after being ravaged during the [[Great Clan Wars|Mandalorian Civil War]].<ref name="Collapse" /> Duchess Satine's choice drew the ire of the Republic and Confederacy, but inspired many systems across the galaxy who felt similarly to choose neutrality as well.<ref name="Voyage" /> The ruler of Mandalore opened negotiations with these like-minded systems<ref name="Collapse" /> and the Pacifist faction of the Galactic Senate,<ref name="Rise" /> and these talks resulted in the establishment of the Council of Neutral Systems<ref name="Collapse" /> in 22 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].<ref name="22 BBY Date" />
