'''Butter sauce''' was a type of [[sauce]] made with [[butter]]. For those who did not wish to [[Diet|eat]] [[Klatooine paddy frog]]s alive and screaming in the [[Hutt]] fashion, the [[frog]]s could be roasted in butter sauce and served with a side of [[hubba gourd]]s.<ref name="Ooze" />

==Behind the scenes==
Butter sauce was mentioned in the [[2021]] in the children's reference book ''[[The Star Wars Book of Monsters, Ooze and Slime]]'', which was written by [[Katie Cook]].<ref name="Ooze" />
