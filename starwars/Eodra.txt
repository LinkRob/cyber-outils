'''Eodra''' was a [[Zabrak]] [[gambler]] who resided on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>  During which they played [[Kessel Sabacc]] with a [[human]] named [[Kelel]] and a [[Sullustan]] named [[Rellra]] at the [[Dizzy Droid]] [[Sabacc parlor]] in the [[Wonto District]] of [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on Tatooine. They had tan skin and [[Color|brown]] eyes. While playing [[Sabacc]] at the Dizzy Droid, Eodra wore a grey [[clothing|shirt]], dark pants and a black waist [[sash]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eodra appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
