The '''Corporate Sector Authority Security Police''',<ref name="Allies and Adversaries" /> also known as the '''Corporate Sector Authority police''',<ref name="Scum">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> or as the '''CSA security police''', or '''Espos''',<ref name="SotS" /> was a [[law enforcement agency]] that was part of the [[Corporate Sector Authority]].<ref name="Allies and Adversaries" /> During the [[New Republic Era]], the CSA tried to transform its police force into a true [[army]], creating the new position of "[[CSA trooper]]" out of the Espo ranks.<ref name="SotS" />

==History==
[[File:Agent_of_the_Emperor_-_LotDS_FFG.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A woman hiding from police]]
At some point, a [[Color|red-haired]] [[human]] [[Gender|female]] was seen hiding behind the wall from two members of the<ref name="GaG">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref> Corporate Sector Authority Security Police<ref name="Allies and Adversaries">''[[Allies and Adversaries]]''</ref> on an unknown [[planet]].<ref name="GaG" />

By around [[33 ABY]],<ref name="33 ABY">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' dates [[Sturg Ganna]] becoming untouchable to the [[Canto Bight Police Department]] and the [[Corporate Sector Authority]] Security Police by 8010.321.2 [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]]. ''Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious'' also dates the [[carbon-freezing]] of [[Han Solo]], dated to [[3 ABY]] of the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', to 7980.421.2 in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar]]. Therefore, the event must have happened by around [[33 ABY]].</ref> [[Councillor]] [[Sturg Ganna]] of the [[Canto Bight legislative body]] was untouchable by the [[Canto Bight Police Department]] and the Corporate Sector Authority police due to the fact that his business acumen was profitable for the [[Canto Bight]] [[city]].<ref name="Scum" />
