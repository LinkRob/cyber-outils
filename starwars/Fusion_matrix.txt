A '''fusion matrix''' was a scientific instrument used to create synthetic [[crystal]]s which could focus and channel power. During the [[High Republic Era]], the devices were rare, although one was kept on [[Coruscant]],<ref name="MtD" /> the [[capital]] [[planet]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> Aspiring [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]] repeatedly asked her mentor, [[Professor]] [[Glenna Kip]], if they could go see the fusion matrix, but Kip responded by joking that she was banned from Coruscant, and the matter was put to rest. Starros was later [[Attack on Port Haileap|kidnapped]] by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s and taken to the planet [[Dalna]], where Nihil-affiliated scientist [[Doctor|Dr.]] [[Zadina Mkampa]] had a secret laboratory. Upon being assigned to be Mkampa's assistant, Starros saw the fusion matrix in the lab, suspecting it to have been stolen and wondering who had owned it.<ref name="MtD" />
