'''Cub''' was a [[clone trooper pilot]] who flew members of a [[Cardiff Baye's battalion|battalion]] led by [[Clone Commander]] [[Maximus (Clone Commander)|Maximus]] and [[Jedi General]] [[Cardiff Baye]] to the [[planet]] [[Katou]] aboard an [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i]] [[gunship]].<ref name="HS:Grievous" />

==Biography==
Created on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]],<ref name="Clones" /> the [[clone trooper]] who would take on the name Cub<ref name="HS:Grievous" /> was developed from the [[Clone template|genetic template]] of the [[human]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]]<ref name="Clones" /> to serve in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="HS:Grievous" /> At some point between [[22 BBY]] and [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Hyperspace Stories: Grievous]]'', including the [[death]] of Cardiff Baye, are set during the [[Clone Wars]] but before [[Rex]] adopts [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], something Rex takes on before the events of "[[Darkness on Umbara]]." ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' establishes the war [[First Battle of Geonosis|begins]] in [[22 BBY]] and that the events of "Darkness on Umbara" occur in [[20 BBY]], thereby putting the events of ''Hyperspace Stories: Grievous'' between those two [[Standard year|years]].</ref> a [[Cardiff Baye's battalion|battalion]] led by [[Jedi General]] [[Cardiff Baye]] was taking part in a [[Battle on Covenanon|battle]] on the astronomical object [[Covenanon]] when they were informed by Jedi General [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] of a [[Distress signal|message]] the [[Galactic Republic]] received from the [[Wild Space]] planet [[Katou]]. Kenobi then informed Baye that they had a new mission to go investigate the [[Invasion of Katou|disturbance]] the signal spoke of. [[Pilot|Piloting]] an [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i]] [[gunship]], Cub transported [[General]] Baye, [[Clone Commander]] [[Maximus (Clone Commander)|Maximus]], and five other clone troopers to the planet's surface. Landing in a valley where the source of the distress signal originated, Cub stayed behind on the [[Starship|ship]] while the rest of the unit left to investigate. During the group's leave, and unbeknownst to Cub, General [[Grievous]] of the [[Separatist Droid Army]] damaged the LAAT/i during the night, sabotaging their means of escape. The evening the day after, the clones returned to the Cub and the gunship to return back to Covenanon and flee from Grievous when they realized the sabotage. Cub quickly repaired the gunship and took off with his fellow clone troopers while Baye stayed behind and dueled Grievous.<ref name="HS:Grievous" />
