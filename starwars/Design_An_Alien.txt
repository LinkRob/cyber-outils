'''''Design An Alien''''' was a contest held by ''[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine]]'' challenging readers to design an original ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Alien/Legends|alien]] or creature. The contest was announced in [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 3|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 3]], with winners revealed in [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 7|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 7]]. Additional honorable mentions were included in issues [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 8|8]]&ndash;[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 10|10]] within the magazine's "[[Galactic Bazaar]]" department.

[[Leland Chee]] stated that the aliens published for this contest were canon&mdash;since rebranded [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]]&mdash;though in the event of any discrepancies with another source, that source would likely supersede the contest entry.<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=Star Wars Galaxy Magazine #7 published the winners of the "Design an Alien" contest, where fans were encouraged to submit artwork and backstories of new SW aliens. Are these aliens canon? [...] Yes, though any text discrepencies with other sources would likely to superceded by the other source.}}</ref>
