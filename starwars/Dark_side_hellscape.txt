A '''dark side hellscape''' was located in [[the galaxy]]. Its location was known to the former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] and [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]], the [[Crime lord|leader]] of [[Crimson Dawn]],<ref name="Crimson Reign 5" /> but was unknown to the [[Sith]] [[Lord]]s [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Crimson Reign 4">[[Crimson Reign 4|''Crimson Reign'' 4]]</ref> Prior to his [[Darth Momin's destruction of a city|defeat]] at the hands of the [[Jedi Order]], the Sith Lord [[Darth Momin]] visited the hellscape.<ref name="Darth Vader 22">[[Darth Vader (2017) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 22]]</ref> Momin's [[Fermata Cage]]<ref name="HE 2">[[Hidden Empire 2|''Hidden Empire'' 2]]</ref> was kept stored in the hellscape. Thousands of [[Standard year|years]] later,<ref name="Crimson Reign 5" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Crimson Reign 5|''Crimson Reign'' 5]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> the [[Knights of Ren]] and [[the Archivist]] visited the hellscape to recover the Fermata Cage at the request of Qi'ra.<ref name="Crimson Reign 5" />
