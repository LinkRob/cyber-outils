'''Arthon''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Flight Log" /> located in the [[Arthon system]] within the [[Ottega sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was situated in grid square L-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was inhabited by members of<ref name="Flight Log" /> the [[Sentience|sentient]]<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Ottegan]] [[species]].<ref name="Flight Log" />

By [[32 ABY]],<ref name="Date">[[Poe Dameron 6|''Poe Dameron'' 6]] states that [[Lor San Tekka]] visited Arthon after meeting with [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus the Hutt]] on [[Megalox Beta]], which [[Poe Dameron 4|''Poe Dameron'' 4]] establishes took place prior to [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron's]] [[mission to Ovanis]] as depicted in [[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]]. As ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that Dameron began leading missions with Black [[Squadron]]&mdash;with the mission to [[Ovanis]] being their first mission&mdash;two [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]], Tekka's visit to Arthon must have taken place by [[32 ABY]].</ref> the [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Scout|explorer]] [[Lor San Tekka]] visited the Ottegans on Arthon but later disappeared into hiding.<ref name="Flight Log" /> Sometime later, information [[Mission to Megalox Beta|provided]] to the [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] by the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] included a record of San Tekka's visits to both Arthon and<ref name="Poe Dameron 6" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> [[Hosra]]. Dameron passed on the information to the [[Resistance]]'s [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who believed it to be true.<ref name="Poe Dameron 6" /> Dameron later [[Writing|wrote]] about the discovery of San Tekka's visit to Arthon in his [[Poe Dameron's flight log|flight log]] compiled after the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]]<ref name="Flight Log" /> of [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

===''LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens''===
{{Quote|Stable orbit directly above the Ottegan enclave confirmed. Fighters and transports dispatched and en route.''"<br />"''Good. The map will be in our possession soon.|First Order General [[Armitage Hux]] and Kylo Ren|audio=Arthon-LEGOTFA.flac|<ref name="LEGO" />}}
[[File:Arthon_LEGO.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Arthon, as it appears in ''LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']]
In ''LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens'', Arthon is featured in the level "Ottegan Assault," separate to the main levels that adapt the events of ''The Force Awakens''. It is depicted as a terrestrial planet with a cloudy [[atmosphere]], covered in landmasses and [[ocean]]s. The planet is home to an elevated [[Ottegan enclave]], located on the pinnacle of a large [[plant]] structure and inhabited by members of the [[Sacred Order of Ramulus]].<ref name="LEGO" />

In the game, the Ottegans on Arthon assist the Resistance by mapping the location of the lost [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and giving the [[Map to Skywalker|map]] to Lor San Tekka. The [[First Order]], [[Hunt for Lor San Tekka|seeking]] to retrieve the map for itself, [[Ottegan Assault|attacks]] the enclave while Resistance forces led by [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] attempt to evacuate the Ottegans. In search of San Tekka, the First Order warrior [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] pursue and shoot down a [[Resistance transport]] holding the [[Ottegan Leader|leader]] of the enclave. Leading a ground assault to locate and interrogate the leader, Ren fights through [[Resistance trooper]]s and local Ottegans. He successfully captures and [[Mind probe (Force power)|interrogates]] the leader, discovering that San Tekka is on the planet [[Jakku]]. As the forces of the First Order depart, the remnants of the enclave break apart behind them.<ref name="LEGO" />
