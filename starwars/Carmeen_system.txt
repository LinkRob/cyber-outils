The '''Carmeen system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Altier sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was located in grid square O-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Carmeen]].<ref name="Origin" />
