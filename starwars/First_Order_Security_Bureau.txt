The '''First Order Security Bureau''' ('''FOSB''') was an enforcement and espionage agency of the [[First Order]] lead by [[Major]] [[Chetachi Gohl]]. [[Agent]] [[Terex]] was a [[Military rank|ranking]] [[First Order officer|officer]] of that agency, as was [[Lieutenant]] [[Weel]]. [[Tierny|Agent Tierny]] hunted down [[Resistance]] operative, [[Kazuda Xiono]] and his accomplices. The agency acted as the successor of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]. During the early stages of the [[Cold War]], the Security Bureau saw action against the Resistance [[starfighter]] [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] and his [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] along with [[Hunt for the Colossus|tracking and hunting]] for the ''[[Colossus]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Tierny-BravuraScumAndVillainy.png|left|thumb|right|270px|Agent Tierny was a member of the First Order Security Bureau.]]
The First Order Security Bureau was the [[First Order]]'s intelligence agency<ref name="PD5" /> alongside [[First Order Intelligence]].<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Members included [[agent]]s like [[Terex]], [[Tierny]],<ref name="Descent">{{Resistance|Descent (episode)}}</ref> and [[Lieutenant]] [[Weel]]. Such operatives were tasked with guarding the First Order's secrets, hunting resistance members and recovering information that had been stolen by the First Order's enemies.<ref name="PD2" /><ref name="PD7">[[Poe Dameron 7|''Poe Dameron'' 7]]</ref>

Terex operated a [[spy]] network consisting of various individuals he had coerced into spying for him including the [[Resistance]] [[technician]] [[Oddy Muva]].<ref name="PD11">[[Poe Dameron 11|''Poe Dameron'' 11]]</ref> Terex maintained Muva's cooperation by keeping his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Sowa Chuan]] among his [[Slavery|slaves]], whose relatives had also been coerced into doing his bidding, aboard his personal ship, the ''[[Carrion Spike]]''.<ref name="PD12">[[Poe Dameron 12|''Poe Dameron'' 12]]</ref> Under [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]'s directive, First Order agents and other [[First Order military]] forces were prohibited from seeking open hostility against forces aligned with the [[New Republic]]&mdash;including the Resistance,<ref name="PD11" /> although skirmishes would occur anyway<ref name="Poe Dameron 27">[[Poe Dameron 27|''Poe Dameron'' 27]]</ref>&mdash;until the outbreak of open war.<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

==History==
===Mission to Ovanis===
{{Quote|You're with the First Order now, Terex?''"<br />"''Yes, and it's ''Agent'' Terex. I'm an officer in their intelligence-gathering arm.|Kan Be and Terex|<ref name="PD5" />}}
[[File:Terex-PoeDameron2.png|thumb|right|150px|Agent Terex, a Security Bureau agent]]
During the early stages of the [[Cold War]], First Order Security Bureau agents were deployed on intelligence-gathering missions against Resistance forces such as [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]]'s [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]].<ref name="PD2" /> After the former [[stormtrooper]]&ndash;turned&ndash;[[crime lord]] Terex intercepted a shipment of [[First Order stormtrooper armor|stormtrooper armor]] bound for the First Order, Terex decided to join the First Order.<ref name="PD10">[[Poe Dameron 10|''Poe Dameron'' 10]]</ref> He subsequently became an agent of the First Order Security Bureau.<ref name="PD3">[[Poe Dameron 3|''Poe Dameron'' 3]]</ref> Following [[Operation: Sabre Strike]], Terex was dispatched by [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] to recover information that Dameron had stolen from New Republic [[Senator]] [[Erudo Ro-Kiintor]], an undercover First Order asset.<ref name="PD2" />

Terex was given substantial resources including a [[Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser|''Maxima-A''-class heavy cruiser]], [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]], [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s and a [[First Order landing platform]]. During the [[Mission to Ovanis|mission]] to [[Ovanis]], Terex terrorized the [[Crèche]] cult in an attempt to force them to surrender Dameron.<ref name="PD2" /> Terex and his forces were subsequently involved in a conflict with Dameron's [[Color|Black]] [[Squadron]], the Crèche, and two [[Unidentified Crèche creature|winged creatures]]. Dameron and [[BB-8]] defeated Terex in combat and forced the agent to order his forces to stand down. To avoid further hostilities between the New Republic and the First Order, Black Squadron allowed Terex and his men to leave peacefully.<ref name="PD3" />

===Competing for Grakkus===
[[File:Weel-PoeDameron7.png|thumb|left|200px|Lieutenant Weel, another Security Bureau agent]]
With the help of his unwilling spy Muva,<ref name="PD11" /> Terex learned that Dameron and Black Squadron were [[Mission to Megalox Beta|traveling]] to [[Megalox]] [[prison]] on the high [[gravity]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Megalox Beta]] to question [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] the [[Hutt]] about the location of the explorer [[Lor San Tekka]],<ref name="PD4">[[Poe Dameron 4|''Poe Dameron'' 4]]</ref> who [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] believed could help them find her lost brother, [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="PD1">[[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]]</ref> Terex bribed [[Warden]] [[Luta]] and the guards into abandoning Black Squadron inside the prison. However, Black Squadron managed to fight their way into Grakkus' [[Fortress of Grakkus the Hutt|fortress]]. Due to the competition, Grakkus decided to disclose his information to whoever freed him from Megalox prison first.<ref name="PD4" />

Angered that Grakkus had broken their deal, Terex met with the rival crime [[lord]]s [[Toren]], [[Kan Be]], and [[Isin]]. Identifying himself as a First Order intelligence agent, Grakkus hired the imprisoned crime lords to use their gangs to attack Grakkus' palace, and [[Death|kill]] Black Squadron and Grakkus' [[Grakks|guards]]. Terex planned to kill Grakkus after extracting the information from him. In return, he offered to give the crime lords transportation out of Megalox. The crime lords agreed and attacked Grakkus' compound.<ref name="PD5" /> However, Dameron outmaneuvered Terex by having his squadron's [[astromech droid]]s sabotage the prison's gravity shield, exposing Terex and the [[gangster]]s to Megalox Beta's high gravity.<ref name="PD6">[[Poe Dameron 6|''Poe Dameron'' 6]]</ref>

Dameron forced Luta to let Black Squadron leave with Grakkus in return for restoring the prison's gravity shield. Terex was rescued by the crew of his [[starship]] ''[[Carrion Spike]]''. Angered by his failure, Terex used the ''Carrion Spike'' to attack the [[orbit]]ing [[Megalox space station]] and the rescue [[shuttle]]s. However, he was forced to retreat into [[hyperspace]] by Black Squadron, who inflicted damage on the ''Carrion Spike''. Rather than report back to First Order Space to face a debriefing for his failure, Terex decided to seek revenge against Dameron.<ref name="PD6" />

===Skirmish on Pheryon===
[[File:OddyMuva-PoeDameron1.png|thumb|right|175px|Oddy Muva, an unwilling First Order spy]]
Later, Lieutenant Weel captured Dameron and the ''[[Galaxy Beacon]]'' [[journalist]] [[Suralinda Javos]] during a [[Skirmish on Pheryon|skirmish]] on [[Pheryon]]. He interrogated Javos at a warehouse. The [[Squamatan]] Javos, however, escaped by ejecting a corrosive spray from her [[mouth]], a unique physiological feature of her [[species]]. She and Dameron then escaped back to the Resistance with information about the First Order's illegal rearmament program in violation of the [[Galactic Concordance]].<ref name="PD7" />

===Terex's rogue mission===
Continuing his vendetta against Dameron, Terex traveled back to the [[Ranc Gang]]'s stronghold on [[Kaddak]].<ref name="PD8">[[Poe Dameron 8|''Poe Dameron'' 8]]</ref> After retaking control of the Ranc Gang, Terex embarked on a rogue mission to destroy the Resistance in order to improve his standing within the First Order.<ref name="PD9">[[Poe Dameron 9|''Poe Dameron'' 9]]</ref> Terex's plan involved feeding the Resistance [[Resistance spy droid network|spy droid]] [[N1-ZX]] with disinformation in order to track Dameron to the [[Resistance base]]. Despite Phasma's objections that his plan violated Snoke's directive to avoid conflict with the Resistance, Terex believed that the Ranc Gang could succeed. However, Dameron realized his plan and led Terex and the Ranc Gang to a remote [[Unidentified desert planet|desert planet]].<ref name="PD11" />

Terex's Ranc Gang forces were involved in a [[Battle over a desert planet|skirmish]] with Dameron, Black Squadron, and Muva. Muva managed to free his wife and the other slaves from Terex's captivity.<ref name="PD12" /> Following a battle in space and on land, Terex and his forces were defeated by Black Squadron and First Order forces led by Commander [[Malarus]]. After destroying the ''Carrion Spike'' and the Ranc Gang's "[[Ugly|Uglies]]," Malarus arrested Terex with the consent of Dameron.<ref name="PD13">[[Poe Dameron 13|''Poe Dameron'' 13]]</ref> Aboard a First Order Star Destroyer, Terex demanded to speak with Phasma about vital information that would lead to the Resistance's destruction. Terex told her how the Resistance used an ancient protocol droid to run their spy network and told her that he was able to retrieve its memory core. However, Phasma told him that it was worthless<ref name="PD14">[[Poe Dameron 14|''Poe Dameron'' 14]]</ref> due to a electrical surge created by BB-8.<ref name="PD13" /> Despite this, Terex claimed he was the First Order's most valuable agents and still had many secrets to share with the First Order. Phasma agreed to let Terex out of his cell, but decided to give him a brain/cyborg construct in order to control him.<ref name="PD14" />

[[File:Ansiv_Garmuth.png|thumb|left|175px|Colonel Garmuth was stationed aboard Snoke's flagship ''Supremacy'']]
During the First Order's [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupation]] of the ''Colossus'' on [[Castilon]], Agent Tierny was sent to assist Commander Pyre and his [[Colossus garrison|garrison]] in capturing known Resistance agents. Following her arrival, Tierny questioned mechanic [[Tamara Ryvora]] about her employer [[Jarek Yeager]], fellow mechanic [[Kazuda Xiono]], and their involvement with the Resistance. Ryvora said that she was not with the Resistance, and knew nothing about them except that Yeager was friends with Commander Poe Dameron. Tierny believed her and told her the truth about Xiono: his upbringing on [[Hosnian Prime]] from a wealthy family, his time in the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]], and subsequent recruitment into the Resistance as spy.<ref name="Descent">{{Resistance|Descent (episode)}}</ref>

===First Order–Resistance war===
[[File:TiernysUniform-IntoTheUnknown.png|thumb|right|250px|Agent Tierny of the First Order Security Bureau]]
[[First Order-Resistance War|Open warfare]] would finally begin between the First Order and its enemies, chief among them the New Republic and the Resistance, with the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]], which decapitated the New Republic.<ref name="Episode VII" /> During the First Order's [[Battle of Oetchi|subsequent attack]] on the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]], [[Colonel]] [[Ansiv Garmuth]] was stationed aboard the ''[[Supremacy]]''. Garmuth was tipped off by [[BB-9E]] that [[Finn|FN-2187]] and Resistance fighter [[Rose Tico]] had infiltrated the ship. He gathered stormtroopers and informed Phasma of this.<ref name="TLJ:TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref><ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

During the [[Mission to Garel (Resistance)|Mission to Garel]], a director named [[Orlok (First Order)|Orlok]] of the First Order Security Bureau, nicknamed the "Gamemaster", came to [[Garel]] to find the Resistance.<ref name="SW Adventures 28">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=28|story=Secret Agent Droids, Part 2}}</ref> All the while, Security Bureau Agent Tierny of oversaw the [[Hunt for the Colossus|hunt]] for the ''Colossus'' [[supertanker fuel depot]] alongside Commander Pyre of the Stormtrooper Corps. In the end, however, she would be executed by Supreme Leader [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] for her failures. Her efforts to tempt the [[pilot]] [[Tamara Ryvora]] into joining the First Order also failed, ending with Ryvora rejoining her friends on the ''Colossus''.<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref>
