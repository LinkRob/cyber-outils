{{Quote|Fall Guys is inherently slapstick and radiates a sense of fun, so it was always very important to the team that the world felt physical and that the characters were vibrant and colourful, so this was in our heads from the very start.|Senior designer Anthony Pepper|<ref name="Pepper Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://waytoomany.games/2020/08/18/interview-with-anthony-pepper-senior-designer-behind-fall-guys|text=Interview with Anthony Pepper, Senior Designer behind Fall Guys|author=Leo Faria|date=2020-08-18|work=[https://waytoomany.games/ WayTooManyGames]|archivedate=20240509104507}}</ref>}}
'''''Fall Guys''''', also formerly known as '''''Fall Guys: Ultimate Knockout''''', is an online [[Wikipedia:Free-to-play|free-to-play]] [[Wikipedia:Platformer|platformer]] [[Wikipedia:Battle royale game|battle royale]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Mediatonic]] and published by [[Epic Games]], which initially released on [[August 4]], [[2020]], before a wider release on [[June 21]], [[2022]].

On [[May 4]], [[2024]], it was announced that ''Fall Guys'' and ''[[Star Wars]]'' would be collaborating to introduce ''Star Wars'' content to the game from [[May 7]] to [[May 20]]. This new cosmetic content was inspired by the films [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] and featured new costumes of the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] riding atop the [[starship]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', the smuggler [[Chewbacca]], the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]], and a classic [[stormtrooper]]. It also included a special celebration featuring the [[Sarlacc]] [[Great Pit of Carkoon|pit]] and a [[carbon-freezing chamber]].

A second ''Star Wars'' collaboration began on [[July 30]] and ran through [[August 12]], 2024. This series of cosmetic content featured characters from the film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. It included new costume sets of [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]], [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], and the [[droid]] [[R2-D2]]. Other cosmetic items were also added with similar themes, such as profile banners featuring Maul and R2-D2; as well as an R2-D2 emote and a celebration based on Maul and his [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|double-bladed lightsaber]].

==Gameplay==
{{Quote|Falling over is basically ''[the characters']'' superpower. It's what they do best. We had to make sure falling over was always slapstick funny. We were looking at stuff like Takeshi's Castle and Exceed and Wipeout. You're there for the falls. Making sure that ragdolling was working, could work at scale, was a big challenge.|Creative director Jeff Tanton|<ref name="Tanton Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://venturebeat.com/games/fall-guys-ultimate-knockout-how-mediatonic-crafted-a-cute-battle-royale|text=Fall Guys: Ultimate Knockout &mdash; How Mediatonic crafted a cute battle royale|author=[[Wikipedia:Dean Takahashi|Dean Takahashi]]|date=2020-07-29|work=[[Wikipedia:VentureBeat|VentureBeat]]|archivedate=20240319193354}}</ref>}}
[[File:OriginalKeyArt-FallGuys.png|275px|thumb|left|Beans are the default playable characters of ''Fall Guys''.]]
''Fall Guys'' is an online [[Wikipedia:Free-to-play|free-to-play]] [[Wikipedia:Platformer|platformer]] [[Wikipedia:Battle royale game|battle royale]].<ref name="Free" /> In ''Fall Guys'', players take control of a [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Fall Guy|Fall Guy]],<ref name="What">{{YouTube|video=46qwfF6F_fQ|text=Fall Guys - What is Fall Guys?|channel=@FallGuys|name=Fall Guys|date=2022-09-16}}</ref> also known as a Bean,<ref name="SW Announcement">{{SW|url=news/fall-guys-star-wars|text=The Beans Strike Back as ''Star Wars'' Comes to Fall Guys}}</ref> to compete against other players through multiple rounds of the Blunderdome. The common types of obstacle courses range from [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Course|races]], [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Survival|survivals]], [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Hunt|hunts]], [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Logic|logic puzzles]], [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Team|team challenges]], and [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Final|finals]] at the end of a show. There are two permanent types of shows players can choose to participate in, [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Solos|Solo]] and [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Squads|Squad Shows]]. More modes, playlists, and challenges are also available; however, they are time-limited and may change every week.<ref name="What" />

Aside from officially made rounds, ''Fall Guys'' allows for the community to create and publish their own courses through [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Fall Guys Creative|Creative]] mode, using the game's massive catalog of assets. Community courses can be included in a featured community playlist based on in-game analytics, such as the amount of plays and playtime; as well as the originality and creativity of the course.<ref name="Creative">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fallguys.com/en-US/creative|text=Fall Guys Creative Mode &#124; Build & Play Community Courses|work=''Fall Guys''{{'}} [https://www.fallguys.com official website]|archivedate=20240511013822}}</ref> While creators are given freedom in designing their courses, moderations and rules are still set to avoid inappropriate behavior.<ref name="Creative Rules">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fallguys.com/en-US/news/content-rules|text=Fall Guys Creative &ndash; Creator Rules|work=''Fall Guys''{{'}} [https://www.fallguys.com official website]|archivedate=20240511015003}}</ref> While most modes in ''Fall Guys'' are quick and competitive, the [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Explore|Explore]] mode takes a more relaxed approach to the courses. It allows players to play through an endless run of courses, which they can choose to quit whenever they want. Each round's time limit is extended up to half an hour, giving players the option to finish at a much slower pace. With the mode being endless, Explore implements courses made by the community on Creative mode to help keep players' experience new and fresh.<ref name="Explore">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fallguys.com/en-US/news/fall-forever-update|text=The Fall Forever Update|date=2024-04-29|work=''Fall Guys''{{'}} [https://www.fallguys.com official website]|archivedate=20240509173652}}</ref>

''Fall Guys'' supports two spendable currencies, [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Kudos|Kudos]] and [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Show-Bucks|Show-Bucks]]. Kudos is a free currency that players can earn by progressing through the seasonal Fame Pass and other in-game events,<ref name="Kudos">{{WebCite|url=https://www.eurogamer.net/fall-guys-kudos-how-to-get-9027|text=Fall Guys Kudos and how to get Kudos explained|author=Lottie Lynn|date=2022-06-21|work=[[Wikipedia:EuroGamer|EuroGamer]]|archivedate=20231204190124}}</ref> while Show-Buck is the game's premium currency obtainable via [[Wikipedia:Microtransaction|microtransaction]]; however, a limited amount of Show-Bucks can still be earned freely in the Fame Pass.<ref name="Show-Bucks">{{WebCite|url=https://www.trueachievements.com/n50129/fall-guys-showbucks-guide|text=Fall Guys: What are Show-Bucks, and how do you earn them?|author=Luke Albigés|date=2022-06-21|work=[https://www.trueachievements.com/ TrueAchievements]|archivedate=20240509145351}}</ref> Both currencies can be used in the in-game shop to purchase the cosmetic items in daily rotation, though the latter currency offers a more premium selection of items.<ref name="Currency Change">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fallguys.com/en-US/news/upcoming-fall-guys-currency-changes|text=Upcoming Fall Guys Currency Changes|date=2022-06-15|work=''Fall Guys''{{'}} [https://www.fallguys.com official website]|archivedate=20240118204934}}</ref> Another type of currency is the [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Crowns|Crown]], which can be earned by winning shows or completing events. However, Crowns are not a spendable currency; rather, it can be used on the [[:w:c:fallguysultimateknockout:Crown Rank|Crown Rank]] and unlock exclusive cosmetic items as players progress.<ref name="Currency Change" />

==''Star Wars'' content==
{{Quote|Basically nobody knows about ''[Star Wars: Jedi Temple Challenge]'', but it's the closest anybody's got to a Star Wars obstacle course thing. I think if I could do anything in the world, I think it'd probably be trying to make Fall Guys do stupid Jedi balancing obstacle courses.|Design lead Joe Walsh, on the potential of a ''Star Wars'' crossover|<ref name="Walsh Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gamebyte.com/fall-guys-developer-interview-egx-2021|text=One Year On, Mediatonic Discusses The Immense Success Of Fall Guys &#124; Developer Interview|author=Joshua Boyles|date=2021-10-12|work=[https://www.gamebyte.com/ GameByte]|archivedate=20240221162033}}</ref>}}
[[File:BobaTrooper-FallGuys.png|260px|thumb|right|The ''Star Wars'' cosmetic items were based on the original trilogy.]]
During ''Fall Guys''{{'}} first ''[[Star Wars]]'' collaboration, multiple cosmetic items based on the [[original trilogy]], specifically<ref name="SW Announcement" /> the [[1980]] film<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] and<ref name="SW Announcement" /> the [[1983]] film<ref name="Year by Year" /> [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], were added to the in-game shop. Characters such as<ref name="SW Announcement" /> the [[smuggler]]s<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]], with the former riding atop the [[starship]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'',<ref name="SW Announcement" /> the [[bounty hunter]]<ref name="SWE" /> [[Boba Fett]], and a [[stormtrooper]] were available as costume sets. Along with the costumes, two victory celebration sequences based on Solo's [[carbon-freezing]] process from Episode V and Fett's fall to the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]] from Episode VI were added to the in-game shop as well.<ref name="SW Announcement" />

For the second ''Star Wars'' collaboration, it featured cosmetic items based on the<ref name="Yoda">{{YouTube|video=Z-_tfogwUsU|text=Yoda, R2-D2, Padme Amidala and Darth Maul land in Fall Guys!|channel=@FallGuys|name=Fall Guys|date=2024-7-26}}</ref> [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Year by Year" /> Characters such as<ref name="Yoda" /> the [[Sith apprentice]]<ref name="SWE" /> [[Maul|Darth Maul]],<ref name="Yoda" /> the [[Monarch of Naboo|queen]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]<ref name="SWE" /> [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]],<ref name="Yoda" /> [[Grand Master]]<ref name="SWE" /> [[Yoda]], and the [[droid]] [[R2-D2]] were added to the in-game shop as costume sets for a limited time. Alongside the costumes, two profile banners featuring Maul and R2-D2, respectively, were added; as well as an emote mimicking R2-D2 and a celebration sequence based on Maul with his [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|double-bladed lightsaber]].<ref name="Yoda" />

==Development==
{{Quote|When we released Fall Guys, we suddenly realised that we had Season 2 to release just two or three months afterwards. And we really didn't have any idea what Season 3 was going to be, let alone Season 4.|Joe Walsh, on ''Fall Guys''{{'}} initial unplanned roadmap|<ref name="Walsh Interview" />}}
In [[January]] [[2018]], the conception of what became ''Fall Guys: Ultimate Knockout'' first started at [[Mediatonic]]. The idea was a 100-player battle royale inspired by the live [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] game show [[Wikipedia:Takeshi's Castle|''Takeshi's Castle'']]. The game was initially considered to be named ''Fool{{'s}} Gauntlet'' and ''Stumble Chums'', before landing on ''Fall Guys'' later on. During the [[Wikipedia:Game Developers Conference|Game Developers Conference]] of that same year, Mediatonic pitched the game to ten publishers until [[Wikipedia:Devolver Digital|Devolver Digital]] agreed to fund it. The development of ''Fall Guys'' then started six months after the initial publishing agreements,<ref name="Concept" /> using [[Wikipedia:Unity (game engine)|Unity]] as its game engine.<ref name="Engine Verge" />

[[File:MultipleRounds-FallGuys.png|thumb|300px|left|''Fall Guys'' offers a variety of courses to play through.]]
During the gaming exhibition<ref name="">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cbsnews.com/news/e3-2019-video-game-industry-readies-for-major-annual-showcase|text=E3 2019: Video game industry readies for major annual showcase|author=Steve Watts|date=2019-06-11|work=[https://www.cbsnews.com/ CBS News]|archivedate=20241218012657}}</ref> [[Wikipedia:E3 2019|E3 2019]], ''Fall Guys: Ultimate Knockout'' was first announced in Devolver Digital's annual pre-recorded game showcase "Devolver Direct."<ref name="Direct 2019" /> On [[August 4]], [[2020]], ''Fall Guys: Ultimate Knockout'' was released on the [[Wikipedia:PlayStation 4|PlayStation 4]] and [[Wikipedia:Steam (service)|Steam]] for [[Wikipedia:Microsoft Windows|Microsoft Windows]] and [[Wikipedia:Mac OS|Mac OS]].<ref name="Direct 2020" /> It was rated "Everyone" by the [[Wikipedia:Entertainment Software Rating Board|ESRB]].<ref name="ESRB" /> Following [[Epic Games]]' purchase of Tonic Games Group&mdash;which included Mediatonic&mdash;on [[March 2]], [[2021]], ''Fall Guys: Ultimate Knockout'' was no longer published and distributed by Devolver Digital.<ref name="Tonic Epic" /> On [[June 21]], [[2022]], massive changes and adjustments were made to the game,<ref name="Free" /> including currencies, costumes, and seasonal passes.<ref name="Currency Change" /> The game became free-to-play for all pre-existing platforms<ref name="Free" /> and rebranded itself to ''Fall Guys'', removing its subtitle.<ref name="Name 1">{{WebCite|url=https://store.steampowered.com/app/1097150/Fall_Guys_Ultimate_Knockout|text=Fall Guys: Ultimate Knockout|work=[[Wikipedia:Steam (service)|Steam]]|archivedate=20220528202640}}</ref><ref name="Name 2">{{WebCite|url=https://store.steampowered.com/app/1097150/Fall_Guys|text=Fall Guys|work=[[Wikipedia:Steam (service)|Steam]]|archivedate=20220623094104}}</ref> On the publishing front, the game was delisted on Steam, switching exclusively to the [[Wikipedia:Epic Games Store|Epic Games Store]] instead; however, ''Fall Guys'' remained available to players who bought the game on Steam prior. Along with the change, the game was also released on the gaming platforms [[Wikipedia:Xbox One|Xbox One]], [[Wikipedia:Xbox Series X and Series S|Xbox Series X/S]], [[Wikipedia:Nintendo Switch|Nintendo Switch]], and [[Wikipedia:PlayStation 5|PlayStation 5]].<ref name="Free" />

[[File:SpaceSmugglers-FallGuys.png|275px|thumb|right|''Star Wars'' cosmetic items were available for a limited time in ''Fall Guys''.]]
On [[May 4]], [[2024]], it was announced that a collaboration between ''Fall Guys'' and ''Star Wars'' titled "Return of the Bean" would be introduced to the game starting on [[May 7]] until [[May 20]] of the same year.<ref name="SW Announcement" /> However, the in-game news feed and shop countdown stated that the campaign would only last until [[May 14]], 2024,<ref name="News">''Fall Guys'' news feed states that the ''[[Star Wars]]'' campaign would last until [[May 14]], [[2024]]. {{C|[[:File:StarWarsNewsFeed-FallGuys.png|screenshot]]}}</ref> ending much earlier than initially announced.<ref name="May 14">{{WebCite|url=https://fallguysstore.com/14-may-2024-fall-guys-store|text=14 May 2024 &mdash; Fall Guys Store|work=[https://fallguysstore.com FallGuysStore.com]|archivedate=20240523104124}}</ref><ref name="May 15">{{WebCite|url=https://fallguysstore.com/15-may-2024-fall-guys-store|text=15 May 2024 &mdash; Fall Guys Store|work=[https://fallguysstore.com FallGuysStore.com]|archivedate=20240523104131}}</ref> Later, on [[July 26]] of the same year, ''Fall Guys'' announced its second wave of collaboration with ''Star Wars'', titled "The Phantom Menace." As announced, the ''Star Wars'' collaboration was officially released on [[July 30]],<ref name="Yoda" /> lasting until [[August 12]], 2024.<ref name="July 30">{{WebCite|url=https://fallguysstore.com/30-july-2024-fall-guys-store|text=30 July 2024 &mdash; Fall Guys Store|work=[https://fallguysstore.com FallGuysStore.com]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/wip/oxDfV}}</ref>

==Continuity==
{{Quote|Chase down rebels for the Empire as part of the Galaxy's clone army|''Fall Guys'' erroneously described stormtoopers as clones|<ref name="Stormtrooper Error">''Fall Guys'' erroneously describes the [[stormtrooper]] costume set as "the [[Galaxy]]'s [[Cloning|clone]] army." {{C|[[:File:StormtrooperDesc-FallGuys.png|screenshot]]}}</ref>}}
The description for the stormtrooper costume reads, "Chase down rebels for the Empire as part of the Galaxy's clone army."<ref name="Stormtrooper Error" /> Given that the costume depicts the classic [[stormtrooper armor]], this erroneously considers the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Stormtrooper Corps]] at the time this armor was used to be a [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone army]]. By the time of that iteration of stormtrooper armor becoming typical, most stormtroopers were non-clones.<ref name="Armor">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>
