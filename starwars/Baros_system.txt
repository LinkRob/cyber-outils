The '''Baros system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]]'s<ref name="The Slice" /> [[Lothal sector]]. It was positioned in grid square U-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Baros system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] boxed set for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> The system was later identified as the "Baros system" in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]],"<ref name="Appendix" /> an appendix accompanying the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]], an article on [[StarWars.com]] featuring a map of the galaxy.<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]] and posted on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Fry">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map...|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Baros system/Legends|Baros system]] was introduced in "[[Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star, How I Wonder Where We Are]]," a [[1990]] roleplaying source article published in [[Voyages SF 13|''Voyages SF'' 13]]. Since that article was released outside of the [[Lucas Licensing]] process,<ref name="Twinkle">{{VoyagesCite|13|Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star, How I Wonder Where We Are}}</ref> it is considered non-canon with respect to the Legends continuity.<ref name="Unlicensed">{{UnlicensedStatus}}</ref> The Baros system was later mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry, which marked its first unambiguously canonical debut.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> The system later made its first appearance in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', a [[2011]] [[BioWare]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]].<ref name="SWTOR">{{TORcite|type=space|Baros Ambush}}</ref>
