The '''67e fusion core''' was a type of [[fusion generator]] manufactured by [[Klyd-Marro]]. These were used in the [[K-88 "Event Horizon" sublight engine]]s commonly installed on [[RZ-2 A-wing interceptor]]s. Cryogenic power tubes from the K-88 "Event Horizon" engines fueled the enormous 67e fusion cores that provided massive thrust to achieve high speeds for the RZ-2 A-wing.<ref name="IncrediBuildsAwing" />
