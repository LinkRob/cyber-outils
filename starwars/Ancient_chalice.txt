An '''ancient chalice''' supposedly once belonged to the [[Kingdom of Elweys]]. It was lost over a [[millennium]] before<ref name="Entombed" /> [[18 BBY]]. Between [[19 BBY]] and 18 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="date">{{TBBDate|205}}</ref> the [[pirate]] [[Phee Genoa]] gained a lead on the chalice, supposedly located on [[Vadnay]]. After her [[Discovery on Skara Nal|discovery]] of the ancient [[walker]] [[Skara Nal]] in the [[Kaldar Trinary system]] with the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]], Genoa decided that their next trip would be to search for the chalice.<ref name="Entombed" />
