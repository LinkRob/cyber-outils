"'''Celebration Thru the Lens'''" is an eight-page magazine article which was published in [[Star Wars Insider 174|''Star Wars Insider'' 174]] by [[Titan Magazines]]<ref name="SWI 174" /> on [[July 11]], [[2017]].<ref name="Titan 174" /> The article delves into the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Celebration Orlando]] convention and includes various pictures taken by photographers [[Joe McDonald]], [[Kim Stace]], and [[Leon Hurley]] during the event.<ref name="SWI 174" />
