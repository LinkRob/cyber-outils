'''Aq Vetina'''<ref name="The Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> was a [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Din Djarin and Other Characters of the Underworld}}</ref> inhabited by a number of [[human]]s. During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Battle on Aq Vetina|attacked]] a small [[Unidentified village (Aq Vetina)|settlement]] on the planet, [[Death|killing]] the population. The [[Mandalorian]]s of [[Death Watch]] intervened in the attack, fighting back against the Separatist [[Separatist Droid Army|droid forces]].<ref name="Chapter 8" /> Aq Vetina was the [[homeworld]] of<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> the young human [[Din Djarin]], who was rescued from the attack by a [[Unidentified Mandalorian warrior|Mandalorian warrior]] and raised as a [[foundling]] in the [[Mandalorian Creed]].<ref name="Chapter 8" />
