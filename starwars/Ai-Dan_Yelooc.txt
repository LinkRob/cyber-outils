'''Ai-Dan Yelooc''' was a [[human]]<ref name="The High Republic 2">[[The High Republic (2023) 2|''The High Republic'' (2023) 2]]</ref> [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Aidan Cooley post" /> [[Jedi]] who operated during the [[High Republic Era]].

==Biography==
A [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], Ai-Dan Yelooc served on the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] ''[[Gios]]'' and accompanied fellow Jedi [[Keeve Trennis]], [[Ceret]], [[Terec]], and [[Santar]] to [[Ballum]] to intercept a meeting between [[Skarabda]], chief diplomatic envoy of the [[Hutt Clan]], and the [[Nihil]].<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> When the encounter there became a [[Battle at Ballum|battle]], Yelooc [[Pilot|flew]] a [[Jedi Vector]] [[starfighter]] to fight back against the Hutt forces, saving Ceret's life by taking down a Hutt [[skiff fighter]]<ref name="The High Republic 2" /> and proving Yelooc's skill behind the controls of a Vector.<ref name="Aidan Cooley post" /> When Nihil [[Tempest Runner|leader]] turned Hutt enforcer [[Lourna Dee]] ejected crates full of people while attempting to flee Ballum in the stolen Jedi [[cruiser]] ''[[Ataraxia]]'', Yelooc [[Telekinesis|used]] [[the Force]] to manuver the people safely into the ''Gios''.<ref name="The High Republic 3">[[The High Republic (2023) 3|''The High Republic'' (2023) 3]]</ref>

==Powers and abilities==
As a Jedi Ai-Dan was [[Force-sensitive]] and was capable of moving large objects through [[Telekinesis]].<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> Ai-Dan could pilot [[Jedi Vector]]s.<ref name="The High Republic 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ai-Dan Yelooc first appeared in the [[The High Republic (2023) 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']],<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> written by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Ario Anindito]] and [[Jim Towe]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 8]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/110346/star_wars_the_high_republic_phase_iii_2023_1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic &lsqb;Phase III&rsqb; (2023) #1}}</ref> The character was created as a [[tuckerization]] and homage to Aidan Cooley, who connected with Scott through the [[Wikipedia:Make-A-Wish Foundation|Make-A-Wish Foundation]] with the dream of being transformed into a Jedi.<ref name="Aidan Cooley post" />
