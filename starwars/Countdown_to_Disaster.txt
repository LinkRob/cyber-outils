"'''Countdown to Disaster'''" is an instant [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|RPG]] adventure. It was published as part of the ''[[A Taste of Adventure]]'' article in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 7|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 7]]. Unlike the other adventures in the same article, ''Countdown to Disaster'' is set in the [[New Republic era]].
