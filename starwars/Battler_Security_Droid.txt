The '''Battler Security Droid''' was a body configuration owned by the [[KX-series security droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[K-A0S]] that she could swap for her standard [[KX-Series Security Droid (K-A0S)|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" /> This frame resembled the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] known as [[Kalani]].<ref name="Kalani">{{TCW|Front Runners}}</ref>

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Battler Security Droid was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter K-A0S, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 3.0.0 update alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Battler Grafted Shields]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
