'''Exis Station''' was a location that, according to the quasi-historical poems known as the [[Qel-Droma Epics]], was situated near the [[Jedi Order]] [[planet]] of [[Ossus]], located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Auril sector]]. The epics claimed that before Ossus was devastated by the [[Cron Supernova]], the [[Jedi]] managed to [[Evacuation of Ossus|save]] a fraction of the artifacts housed on the planet, which made it to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the planet [[Coruscant]] via Exis Station.<ref name="Nexus of Power">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>
