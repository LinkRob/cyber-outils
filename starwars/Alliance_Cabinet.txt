The '''Alliance Cabinet''', also referred to as '''the Cabinet''', and sometimes informally called the '''Political High Command''',<ref name="Rebel Files" /> was a branch of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Alliance Civil Government|Civil Government]]. Arranged beneath the Alliance's titular [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|chief of state]], the cabinet was made up of six allied [[minister]]s with specific portfolios, including [[Minister of Finance (Alliance)|finance]], [[Minister of Industry|industry]], [[Minister of Education|education]],<ref name="UVG" /> [[Minister of State (Alliance)|state]], [[Minister of Supply|supply]], and [[Minister of War (Alliance)|war]], and were led by the [[Secretary of the Cabinet]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
