'''Felucian Fizz''' was a non-[[alcohol]]ic beverage that shared its name with the [[planet]] [[Felucia]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> the [[Oga's Cantina]] establishment in the [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]] offered the drink to its customers, especially on [[Temperature|hot]] [[Local day|days]] when it would be most refreshing.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="TGDate" /> the [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] wrote about Felucian Fizz's traits in his Batuu guidebook, ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', mentioning that Oga's Cantina patrons could [[Water|rehydrate]] themselves by imbibing it.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Felucian Fizz was mentioned in the [[2020]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', which was authored by [[Cole Horton]] as part of the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge (media project)|''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge'' multimedia project]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />
