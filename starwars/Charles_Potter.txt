'''Charles Potter''' is an [[Wikipedia:Film Director|audiobook director]] who directed the two ''[[Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina]]'' audio dramas as well as writing the dramatization of [[We Don't Do Weddings: The Band's Tale (audio drama)|''We Don't Do Weddings: The Band's Tale'']].<ref name="Band's Tale" /> Potter went on to direct abridged audiobooks for [[Bantam Doubleday Dell Audio Publishing]] including the [[Rebel Dawn (abridged audiobook)|''Rebel Dawn'' abridged audiobook]].<ref name="Rebel Dawn">[[Rebel Dawn (abridged audiobook)|''Rebel Dawn'' abridged audiobook]]</ref>
