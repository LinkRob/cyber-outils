'''Faz''' was an extremely cold terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Storm" /> in the [[Faz system]] in the [[Farlax sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s could be stationed to. The [[Stormtrooper Corps|stormtrooper]] deserter [[Sardis Ramsin]] mentioned Faz while complaining about the heat of the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] on an Imperial incident report form he filled out.<ref name="Storm" />
