The '''Endor Grafted Shields''' were a pair of [[Shield (physical armor)|shields]] owned by the [[KX-series security droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[K-A0S]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her standard [[Grafted Twin Shields]]. A depiction of the [[Battle of Endor]] was crudely scribbled across the shields, and a number of leaves, twigs, and arrows protruded from its surface.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Grafted Shields was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter K-A0S' Grafted Twin Shields, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 5.0.0 alongside Season 5 on [[January 27]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Endor Security Droid]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
