During the [[High Republic Era]] the [[Kara Xoo's Tempest]] launched an '''attack''' on the people of [[Hon-Tallos]]. The [[Nihil]] member [[Deva Lompop]] took part in the attack and wanted to have fun with the attack before the [[Jedi]] arrived to stop them. The Nihil used [[flamethrower]]s and a [[Spider Cruiser]] to attack the [[city]].

==Prelude==
During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|maraduers]] waged a [[Nihil conflict|conflict]] with the [[Galactic Republic]] starting with the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]].<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> On the astronomical object [[Hon-Tallos]], the [[Kara Xoo's Tempest]] prepared to launch an attack before the [[Jedi]] arrived to stop them. [[Deva Lompop]] and other Nihil were already on Hon-Tallos and were waiting to strike. During this [[time]], Lompop helped the [[Nautolan]] [[Youngling|girl]] [[Vroma]], stop her bullies. Lompop told her to leave so the attack could begin.<ref name="SWA14" />

==Attack==
The attack soon began after Lompop told Vroma to leave. Lompop and the other Nihil [[priate]]s took out [[flamethrower]]s and began to light up the buildings around them. This caused the civilians to run off from the attack and seek shelter. But Vroma casually walked away, believing Lompop to be a nice [[Gender|lady]]. During the attack, there was also a large Nihil [[Spider Cruiser]], towering over the city and laying waste to the buildings around it. The Spider Cruiser used its [[laser cannon]]s to create explosions all throughout the city. Because of the flamethrowers, fires erupted around the city, adding to the chaos from the fleeing civilians.<ref name="SWA14" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on Hon-Tallos appeared in "[[Tales of Villainy: A Very Nihil Interlude]]" the backstory of the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 14|fourteenth]] and final issue of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars Adventures (2020)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] anthology [[Star Wars comic books|''Star Wars'' comic book]] series. The comic story was written by [[Justina Ireland]], illustrated by [[Nick Brokenshire]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="SWA14" /> on [[February 9]], [[2022]]<ref name="Amazon">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2020-14/digital-comic/973690|text=Star Wars Adventures (2020-) #14|nolive=1}}</ref>
