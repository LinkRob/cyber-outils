The '''Colonial Academy''' was an academic institution which began operating prior to the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="TC" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date">The section of ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'' mentioning the Colonial Academy is set 22 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Clone Wars]], which [[Mission to Mustafar|ended]] with the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] in [[19 BBY]], according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. The events mentioning the academy must have therefore occurred in [[3 ABY]].</ref> The [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Everi Chalis]] enrolled into the academy<ref name="WOTG">''[[Star Wars: Women of the Galaxy]]''</ref> in order to get off of her [[Unidentified planet (Everi Chalis)|homeworld]],<ref name="TC" /> leaving the [[planet]] behind to study art.<ref name="WOTG" /> Sometime after the Empire's rise,<ref name="TC" /> Chalis's studies at the academy drew the attention of the Imperial [[Count]]<ref name="WOTG" /> [[Denetrius Vidian]], who offered her an apprenticeship. Chalis later recounted her [[time]] at the academy to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]]<ref name="TC" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Colonial Academy was first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="TC" />
