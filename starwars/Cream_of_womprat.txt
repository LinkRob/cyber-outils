'''Cream of womprat''' was a [[Foodstuff|soup]] advertised and sold in the [[Grand Arena]] during the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] sporting event. This potage was priced at four [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the menu of the [[The Restraining Bolt]], an [[Restaurant|eatery]] found on the bottom level of the [[Mos Espa Waystation]], one of the Grand Arena's battlefields located on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" /> [[Womp rat]]s,<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> a [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[rodent]] [[species]] found across the [[desert]] planet [[Tatooine]],<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> served as the soup's namesake.<ref name="Hunters" />
