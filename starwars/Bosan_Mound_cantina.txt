'''Bosan Mound cantina''' also known as '''abandoned cantina''' was a [[cantina]] built next to [[Bosan Mound]] in [[Mirogana Valley]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the cantina had been abandoned.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|canon}}
During 3 ABY,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] used her [[ion blaster]] to gain access to the cantina. Inside her [[merqaal]] companion [[Nix]] was able to turn on the cantina's power which gave them access to the backroom. In the backroom, Vess found a [[Scout IV]] trinket, a piece from the [[Canto Pass]] game.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Bosan Mound cantina was created for the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The Scout IV trinket that the player finds in the cantina can be used when customizing the appearance of the [[Trailblazer]] or [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
