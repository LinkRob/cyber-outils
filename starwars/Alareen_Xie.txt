'''Alareen Xie''' was a [[Jedi]] who was active during the [[High Republic Era]]. At some point she ended up in [[No-Space]], where the [[Nihil]] had their [[Great Hall of the Nihil|main base]]. She eventually joined the [[Colony of Kezarat|Colony of ''Kezarat'']], and helped defend it from the Nihil. The [[Abyssin]] [[Blythe]] later stated that without Xie's help, the colony would not have survived.<ref name="Star Wars 31" /> By [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars (2020) 31|''Star Wars'' (2020) 31]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. As Alareen Xie is stated to have [[Death|died]] before the events of ''Star Wars'' (2020) 31, she must have died by 4 ABY.</ref> Xie had [[Death|died]] and was remembered by the colony with her being depicted on a mural. After her death, a belief rose up through the colony that another Jedi would one [[Standard day|day]] arrive and help them leave No-Space. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Blythe and his son [[Forvan]] showed the mural and recounted Xie's story to members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], including the [[Padawan|Jedi]]-in-[[Jedi training|training]] [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Star Wars 31" />
