The '''Dur-24 wrist laser''' was a wrist-mounted [[laser]] weapon manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]] that combined the full firepower of a standard [[blaster rifle]] with a range of up to 50 [[meter]]s. [[Boba Fett]] incorporated one into his left [[Boba Fett's armor|wrist gauntlet]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> During a [[Rescue of Han Solo|skirmish]] at the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]], Fett attempted to shoot [[Luke Skywalker]] with his wrist laser after the [[Jedi]] destroyed his [[EE-3 carbine rifle]], but was knocked into the [[sarlacc]] after firing a few blasts that missed the target.<ref name="ROTJ" /> Some time later, after escaping the sarlacc and retrieving his [[Mandalorian armor|armor]], Fett made further use of the weapon against a platoon of [[stormtrooper]]s belonging to [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]'s [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] whilst [[Battle of Tython  (9 ABY)|aiding]] [[Din Djarin]] on [[Tython]].<ref name="Chapter 14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref>
