The '''destruction of the Taris shipyards''' took place during the [[Galactic Civil War]] when the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] launched an attack on the [[Taris shipyards|shipyards]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] that was servicing an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]]. [[Blade Squadron]] deployed [[bomb]]s while [[Rogue Squadron]] fended off Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. The [[shipyard]] and the [[Star Destroyer]] were destroyed.<ref name="SWA 11" />
