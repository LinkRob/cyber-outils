According to a story told by [[Acolytes of the Beyond|Acolyte of the Beyond]] [[Vaneé]], '''Cranwell''' was a [[human]] and the assistant to [[scientist]] [[Restin]] of the [[Tarkin Initiative]]. Because of the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of his [[homeworld]] of [[Alderaan]], Restin [[Treason|betrayed]] the [[Galactic Empire]] and plotted to defeat the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Learning of this, Cranwell warned Vader's [[Star Destroyer]] and turned against his superior. However, Restin captured him.<ref name="Shadow of Vader's Castle" />

When Vader, pretending not to know Restin was a traitor, arrived at the scientist's base, Restin injected Cranwell with his weapon: a [[Gamorrean Serum|genetic serum]] that would turn its victims into [[Gamorrean]]s. Though the infected Cranwell spread the infection to Vader's escort of two [[stormtrooper]]s, the [[Sith]] [[Lord]] used [[the Force]] to hold them back. After forcing Restin to confess, he used the Force to make the scientist touch Cranwell and the other two Gamorreans, turning the scientist into one of them. Vader then departed as the three Gamorreans attacked their infected creator.<ref name="Shadow of Vader's Castle" />
