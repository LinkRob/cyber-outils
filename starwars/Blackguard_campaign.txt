A violent '''campaign''' led by a division of [[Pijali]] [[blackguard]]s on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]] took place<ref name="MA" /> in [[40 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> Operating secretly under the orders of the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]], the [[soldier]]s carried out several attacks across Pijal and its [[Pijal's moon|moon]] under the guidance of the monarch's [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] [[captain]] [[Deren]].<ref name="MA" />

As the attacks became increasingly bolder, Fanry assisted in blaming the incidents on the [[Opposition]] performance troupe, who had been conducting a [[Opposition protests|series of peaceful protests]] against the signing of the [[Governance Treaty]] initiative. Many of the locations sabotaged were owned by the [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] and managed by its local [[Sector Supervisor]], [[Meritt Col]]. Believing the Opposition to be the result of the blackguards' actions, Pijal's [[lord]] [[regent]] [[Rael Averross]] attempted to hunt the troupe down.<ref name="MA" />
