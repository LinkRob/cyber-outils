The '''A-wing Starfighter''', also referred to as '''A-wing''', was a type of [[starfighter]]. During the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]] and the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[New Republic]] used the [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]. Approximately thirty [[Standard year|years]] later, the type's successor, the [[RZ-2 A-wing interceptor]], was used by the [[Resistance]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War]].<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

==History==
===Early rebellion===
[[File:A-wing_BF.png|thumb|left|300px|An A-wing]]
At least a few A-wings were engaged in combat operations as early as [[18 BBY]]. [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa's]] [[Bail Organa's resistance movement|forces]] operated at least eight of these fighters, and deployed them to assist an [[Raada Uprising|uprising]] on the [[planet]] [[Raada]]. The entire population of Raada was evacuated as a result of the uprising. Further, [[Ahsoka Tano]] joined the rebellion as a [[Fulcrum]] agent in its aftermath.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

===Phoenix Squadron===
====Lothal====
In [[4 BBY]], [[Darth]] Vader launched an [[Attack on Phoenix Squadron (4 BBY)|attack on Phoenix Squadron]], decimating [[Phoenix Squadron]]'s contingent of A-wings. Out of eight fighters deployed, six fell victim to the [[Sith Lord]]'s guns. Vader also destroyed the [[Phoenix Cell]]'s [[flagship]], ''[[Phoenix Home]]'', forcing the rebel fleet to retreat into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Siege">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref>

====Ibaar====
At least five A-wings saw action in a rebel attempt to break the [[Blockade of Ibaar]]. The rebels successfully broke the blockade and delivered relief supplies to the [[Ibaar rebel network]]. A single A-wing was destroyed during the battle.<ref name="Wings of the Master">{{Rebels|Wings of the Master}}</ref>

====Deep space====
In [[3 BBY]], Imperial ships engaged in a [[battle with Phoenix Squadron]] in deep [[Realspace|space]]. The rebels emerged victorious, but the A-wing piloted by [[Phoenix Three]] was destroyed by TIE fighters.<ref name="A Day's Duty">{{RebelsMagCite|15|A Day's Duty}}</ref>

====Garel====
Five of Phoenix Squadron's A-wings saw action in the [[Battle of Garel]], during which the [[Imperial Military]] forced the cell to abandon its hideouts on the planet. Only a single [[Taylander shuttle]] was destroyed, and no A-wings were lost.<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref>

====Concord Dawn====
Five of Phoenix Squadron's A-wings engaged [[Skull Squadron]], a [[Mandalorian]] unit of [[Fang-class Protectorate fighter|Fang fighters]], in a [[dogfight over Concord Dawn]]. Two A-wings were destroyed, and the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors']] victory forced the A-wings to retreat back to the fleet.<ref name="The Protector of Concord Dawn">{{Rebels|The Protector of Concord Dawn}}</ref>

====Ryloth and return to deep space====
[[File:A-wings_chase_Heras_TIE_bomber.png||thumb|right|250px|A-wings chase a TIE bomber operated by the Spectres]]
Two of Phoenix Squadron's A-wings were deployed in a [[Mission to Ryloth (Phoenix Squadron)|mission to Ryloth]], in which they were tasked with firing on a [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bomber]]. The [[bomber]] was actually operated by the rebels, and they planned to use it to board and hijack an Imperial [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser carrier]]. The Imperial crew took the bait and allowed the shuttle into the [[carrier]]'s hangar, and the A-wings jumped into hyperspace when the Imperials returned fire. The mission ended with the hijacking of the carrier and the destruction of an [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]].<ref name="Homecoming">{{Rebels|Homecoming}}</ref>

Later, two A-wings were scrambled to defend against an [[Attack on Phoenix Squadron (3 BBY)|attack on Phoenix Squadron]]. The attack was a tactical victory for the Imperials, but a strategic victory for the rebellion, as the Phoenix Cell successfully relocated to [[Chopper Base]] shortly afterwards. No A-wings were confirmed lost in the attack.<ref name="The Forgotten Droid">{{Rebels|The Forgotten Droid}}</ref>

====Yarma====
In [[2 BBY]], four of Phoenix Squadron's A-wings battled Imperial forces after the [[Spectres]] successfully completed a [[mission to Reklam Station]]. The rebels captured five [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|Y-wing starfighters]] and destroyed [[Reklam Station]], and no A-wings were lost.<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>

====Teralov====
The six A-wings of [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]] were all destroyed in a [[massacre near Teralov]]. This particularly brutal and one-sided battle finally convinced Imperial cadets [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Derek Klivian]] to join the rebellion.<ref name="The Antilles Extraction">{{Rebels|The Antilles Extraction}}</ref>

====Mykapo====
[[File:Iron_Squadron_26.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A-wings participate in the rescue Iron Squadron over Mykapo]]
Two A-wings of Phoenix Squadron saw action in a [[skirmish over Mykapo]]. As a result of this action, [[Iron Squadron]] joined the rebellion after successfully evacuating the system.<ref name="Iron Squadron">{{Rebels|Iron Squadron (episode)}}</ref>

====Tatooine====
[[Ezra Bridger]] and [[C1-10P]] "Chopper" used an [[RZ-1T trainer]] A-wing to undertake an unsanctioned [[Mission to Tatooine (Imperial Era)|mission to Tatooine]], with the objective of recruiting former [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. The mission was unsuccessful, as Kenobi was obligated to stay on [[Tatooine]] to protect [[Luke Skywalker]], but it resulted in the [[death]] of the former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]]. The A-wing was destroyed by a tribe of [[Tusken Raider]]s, and the wayward Rebels returned to Chopper Base in Maul's old [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|''Kom'rk''-class fighter/transport]] [[Nightbrother (Gauntlet fighter)|''Nightbrother'']]<ref name="Twin Suns">{{Rebels|Twin Suns (episode)}}</ref>

====Atollon====
[[File:Battle_of_Atollon.png|thumb|right|250px|Alliance ships prepare to engage Imperial forces over Atollon]]
Many of Phoenix Squadron's A-wings saw action at the Battle of [[Atollon]], a decisive Imperial tactical victory. Chopper Base was destroyed, the rebels' planned [[attack on Lothal]] had to be postponed, and the remains of Phoenix Cell were forced to relocate to [[Yavin 4]]. All but four of the rebels' A-wings were destroyed in the battle.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

===Galactic Civil War===
====Post-Scarif====
After the small-scale rebellion proved inconclusive at toppling the Empire, the Alliance to Restore the Republic escalated the conflict into open warfare in the [[Battle of Scarif]]. This battle in [[1 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> marked the beginning of the 5-year [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Most A-wings in Rebel service at this time were stripped of their shields and heavy weapons launchers in favor of increased speed and agility in combat.<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Rebel A-wings assisted [[Tatooine rebel cell|Saponza's Gang]] in the [[Battle for Tatooine]], which liberated the planet from Imperial control.<ref name="Commander" />

A-wing fighters, including [[Shara Bey's A-wing]], took part in the [[battle at Rendezvous Point Delta-Three]] after the [[Battle of Hoth]].<ref name="Star Wars 2020 1">[[Star Wars (2020) 1|''Star Wars'' (2020) 1]]</ref> Bey and [[L'ulo L'ampar]], who piloted an [[L'ulo L'ampar's A-wing|A-wing]] of his own, later joined [[Starlight Squadron]] during [[Operation Starlight]].<ref name="Star Wars 2020 10">[[Star Wars (2020) 10|''Star Wars'' (2020) 10]]</ref> Seven A-wings joined in the [[Unity Day attack]].<ref name="Star Wars 2020 26">[[Star Wars (2020) 26|''Star Wars'' (2020) 26]]</ref> Three A-wings joined the ''Millennium Falcon'' in the [[Assault on Coruscant|aerial assault on Coruscant]], but all three fighters were lost. However, the pilot of the last, [[Nien Nunb]], managed to abandon his doomed ship in time and join the crew of the ''Falcon''.<ref name="Star Wars 2020 36">[[Star Wars (2020) 36|''Star Wars'' (2020) 36]]</ref>

====Endor and beyond====
[[File:Endorshaft.jpg|thumb|left|250px|An A-wing escorts the ''Millennium Falcon'' down the ''Death Star II''{{'s}} reactor shaft]]
In [[4 ABY]], A-wings participated in the [[Battle of Endor]] with numerous units in [[Blade Squadron|Blade]],<ref name="BS">{{InsiderCite|issue1=149|issue2=150|Blade Squadron (short story)}}</ref> [[Riot Squadron|Riot]],<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> Green and [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red squadrons]]. The battle struck a major blow to the Empire, with the death of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]], the destruction of the ''[[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]]''.<ref name="Episode VI" />

17 days after the Battle of Endor, Green Squadron's A-wings participated in the [[Battle of Cawa City]] on the planet [[Sterdic IV]]. The battle ended in a Rebel victory and resulted in the liberation of the city.<ref name="Shattered Empire 2">[[Shattered Empire 2|''Shattered Empire'' 2]]</ref> A-wings were deployed in the [[Attack on Fondor]], a crushing Rebel defeat that saw the loss of many starfighters. The battle resulted in Imperial forces securing [[climate disruption array]]s necessary for [[Operation: Cinder]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> A few months after the Battle of Endor, [[New Republic]] A-wings were deployed to assist a local [[rebellion on Akiva]]. The rebellion resulted in the capture of high-level Imperial personnel, the rescue of [[Captain]] Wedge Antilles, and the liberation of [[Akiva]]. Shortly thereafter, Akiva became the first world in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] to be admitted to the New Republic.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>

====5 ABY battles====
[[File:Battle_of_Jakku.jpg|thumb|250px|A-wings slug it out with Imperial forces at the Battle of Jakku]]
In [[5 ABY]], at least 1 A-wing participated in the [[Battle of Fondor Shipyards]], serving in [[Shepherd Squadron]]. The battle ended with a New Republic victory.<ref name="The Ghost Ship">{{StoryCite|story=The Ghost Ship|book=Star Wars Adventures: Destroyer Down}}</ref> A-wings served with [[Phoenix Squadron (Jakku)|Phoenix]] and Shepherd Squadrons in the [[Battle of Jakku]], the final battle of the Galactic Civil War.<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref><ref name="The Ghost Ship" />

===New Republic Era===
====Ovanis====
In 34 ABY, [[Black Squadron]] embarked on a [[mission to Ovanis]] with the aim of [[Hunt for Lor San Tekka|locating Lor San Tekka]] and enlisting his assistance to find the missing Jedi Master Luke Skywalker. A single A-wing participated in the mission,<ref name="PD1" /> which eventually devolved into a dogfight between [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] and First Order forces.<ref name="Poe Dameron 2">[[Poe Dameron 2|''Poe Dameron'' 2]]</ref> The engagement ended in a draw, with a few TIEs being destroyed but no Black Squadron casualties.<ref name="Poe Dameron 3">[[Poe Dameron 3|''Poe Dameron'' 3]]</ref>

====Death of L'ulo L'ampar====
In a [[battle over a desert planet]], A-wing pilot [[L'ulo L'ampar]]'s fighter was destroyed in a 3-sided battle with the [[Ranc Gang]] and the First Order.<ref name="Poe Dameron 13">[[Poe Dameron 13|''Poe Dameron'' 13]]</ref>

====Mamkoda and Sheh Soahi====
Resistance [[RZ-2 A-wing interceptor]]s saw action at the [[Battle of Mamkoda]]. The battle ended in a Resistance victory, with a pirate spaceport destroyed by a group of [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17]] bombers. Later, a similar [[Battle of Sheh Soahi|battle]] took place at [[Sheh Soahi]].<ref name="Bomber Command">''[[The Last Jedi: Bomber Command]]''</ref>

====D'Qar====
[[File:The_Last_Jedi_Space_Battle.png|thumb|left|250px|Resistance starfighters escort bombers in the Evacuation of D'Qar]]
RZ-2 A-wings carried out escort duty at the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation of D'Qar]], providing fighter cover for Resistance bombers tasked with destroying the [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought]] ''[[Fulminatrix]]''. The Dreadnought was destroyed, at the expense of the entire bombing fleet<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and a single A-wing piloted by [[Zizi Tlo]].<ref name="TLJ Junior Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>

====Oetchi system====
All of the Resistance's starfighters were destroyed by the First Order in an [[Battle of Oetchi]], just after the fleet exited hyperspace in the [[Oetchi system]]<ref name="TLJ Junior Novel" />

==Behind the scenes==
The A-wing series of starfighters first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], during the climatic Battle of Endor.
