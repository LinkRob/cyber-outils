'''Carbon''' was an element. Some carbon-based lifeforms were capable of eating the various [[silicon]]-based [[species]] of [[mynock]].<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> [[Carbonite]] was a substance made from gaseous carbon.<ref name="SYWTBAJ">''[[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?]]''</ref>
