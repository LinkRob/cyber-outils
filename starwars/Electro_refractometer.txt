An '''electro refractometer''' was a device that measured the angle of light within gem types. The [[Bravaisian]] podmates [[Sonsigo]] and [[Munduri]], who bought gems and precious metals, owned one such tool.<ref name="VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
