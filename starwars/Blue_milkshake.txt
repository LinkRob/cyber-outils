A '''blue milkshake''' was a beverage made using [[blue milk]]. Two [[Ithorian]]s were drinking a blue milkshake at [[Freerago's Satellite Diner and Motel]] shortly before a [[Skirmish on Freerago's Satellite Diner and Motel|skirmish]] broke out between [[Han Solo]] and [[Fyzen Gor]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
