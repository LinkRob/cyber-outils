'''Baython''' was a [[human]] who went to [[Screecher's Reach]] with [[Daal]], [[Quinn]], and [[Keena]].<ref name="Screecher" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
===Development===
Baython appeared in "[[Screecher's Reach (short film)|Screecher's Reach]]," the second episode of ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]{{'}}'' [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second volume]], which premiered on [[Disney+]] on [[May 4]], [[2023]]. He was voiced by [[Alex Connolly]].<ref name="Screecher" /> In concept art created by [[Federico Pirovano]], his name was spelled as "Bayton."<ref name="PirovanoWebsite">{{WebCite|url=https://federicopirovano.com/star-wars-visions-season-2|text=Star Wars Visions Season 2 &mdash; Federico Pirovano|archiveurl=https://archive.is/3AoTY}}</ref> [[Leo Weiss]] created concept art for Baython's speeder and Pirovano illustrated Baython piloting it.<ref name="WeissWebsite">{{WebCite|url=https://leo-weiss.com/screechers-reach|text=Screecher's Reach &mdash; Leo Weiss|archiveurl=https://archive.is/g1WsJ}}</ref>
