A [[human]] [[Gender|female]] was the '''mother''' of [[Crane (princess)|Crane]], the [[princess]] of the [[Kingdom of Biwaji]]. When the kingdom was [[Destruction of the Kingdom of Biwaji|attacked]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], Crane's [[Parent|mother]] sang a [[Unidentified song (Crane)|song]] to Crane while watching a building burn with her. She later urged Crane to escape without her to survive and asked the [[astromech droid]] [[R9-TR2]] to take care of her. Between [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]], as a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], Crane remembered her mother while performing [[Reconnaissance mission on Joetz|reconnaissance]] on the [[planet]] [[Joetz]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|I need you to run now, Crane.''"<br />"''I can't. I don't want to leave you, Mom.|Crane's mother and Crane|<ref name="Four Wings" />}}
[[File:BiwajiBurns-TheSongOfFourWings.png|thumb|right|280px|Crane and her mother watch as the Kingdom of Biwaji is destroyed.]]
A [[human]] [[Gender|female]] was the [[Parent|mother]] of [[Crane (princess)|Crane]], the [[princess]] of<ref name="Four Wings" /> the [[Kingdom of Biwaji]].<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|username=@starwars|url=status/1985452327793533225|name=[[Star Wars]]|date=2025-11-03|quote=building the blueprints. Catch the AT-AT in action in Star Wars: Visions Volume 3 now streaming on @DisneyPlus.|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/RHuHv}}</ref> When the kingdom was [[Destruction of the Kingdom of Biwaji|attacked]] by the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Four Wings" /> she<ref name="Four Wings AD" /> sang a [[Unidentified song (Crane)|song]] while standing with her [[Limb|hand]] on Crane's shoulder as they watched a building burn.<ref name="Four Wings" /> Later, Crane and her mother knelt behind the ruins of a wall as [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s walked nearby. As Crane sobbed, her mother placed her hand on her head and told her to run. Crane refused, not wanting to leave her mother. Her mother told her they were the only ones left and urged her again to leave so she could live and become who she was meant to be. She then told the royal [[astromech droid]] [[R9-TR2]] to take care of Crane.<ref name="Four Wings" />

Crane eventually became a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]],<ref name="Four Wings" /> and between [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|302}}</ref> while performing [[Reconnaissance mission on Joetz|reconnaissance]] on the [[planet]] [[Joetz]], she came across a [[Unidentified village (Joetz)|village]] destroyed by the Empire, causing her to remember watching the kingdom burn with her mother. While later being pursued by<ref name="Four Wings" /> [[AT-ATQ]] walkers,<ref name="Twitter" /> she remembered when her mother told her to leave. After escaping the Empire on Joetz, Crane hummed the song her mother had sang.<ref name="Four Wings" />

==Personality and traits==
Crane's mother had [[Color|orange]] [[hair]] and light skin. She cared for Crane, wanting her to escape and survive when their kingdom was attacked, even though she would be left behind.<ref name="Four Wings" />

==Equipment==
Crane's mother wore white [[clothing]] with red stripes around the wrists and shoulders and blue stripes around the neck. She wore a golden colored [[bracelet]] on her wrist.<ref name="Four Wings" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crane's mother appeared in flashbacks in "[[The Song of Four Wings]]," an animated short film produced by [[Project Studio Q]] as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the non-[[canon]] ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series, released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Four Wings" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="First Look">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-star-wars-visions-volume-3-spinoff-announce|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 3 First Look and Spinoff Announced – ''Star Wars'' Celebration Japan 2025}}</ref> She was voiced by [[Manaka Iwami]] in the original [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] and by [[Jennie Kwan]] in the English dub.<ref name="Four Wings" />
