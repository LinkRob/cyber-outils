'''Byblos Drive Yards''' was a company that produced the [[G-1A starfighter]]. The company designed the [[starfighter]] as an all-purpose system patrol [[Starship|craft]] and left ample room for upgrades and additions to the vessel's stock systems. Byblos envisioned producing several aftermarket weapon and [[sensor]] packages for the G-1A, but only a few were ever built, as the vehicle never gained the popularity or sales numbers that were expected, causing the company to drastically scaled back production.<ref name="S&S" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Byblos Drive Yards was first mentioned in the [[2017]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']].<ref name="ND" /> The [[Byblos Drive Yards/Legends|company]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was created by [[Bill Smith]] for the [[1993]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels]]''.<ref name="EGtVaV">''[[The Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels]]''</ref>
