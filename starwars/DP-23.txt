The '''DP-23''' was a type of [[blaster]] that could pierce through enemy defenses. It was used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[clone trooper]]s during<ref name="New Models" /> the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="AotC">{{Film|II}}</ref> The DP-23 fired blue [[Laser|blaster bolts]], and had a ridged [[barrel]] with a pointed muzzle, a black [[stock]], and a small foregrip. At one point during the war, a [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]] wearing [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] fired the DP-23 on<ref name="Legion DP23" /> the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Felucia]].<ref name="FeluciaDB">{{Databank|felucia|Felucia}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The DP-23 was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in an article titled "[https://web.archive.org/web/20210122063653/https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/news/2019/10/30/new-models/ New Models]," published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] on their [https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/ website] in [[2019]].<ref name="New Models" /> The blaster was created for an upgrade card in the ''[[Phase I Clone Troopers Upgrade Expansion]]'',<ref name="Legion DP23" /> a [[2020]] expansion pack for the ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' miniatures game, also published by Fantasy Flight Games.<ref name="Available Now">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2020/1/31/available-now-january-24-1|text=Available Now &ndash; January 31}}</ref>

The DP-23 originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as the [[DP-23 Rifle]], and first appeared in the [[Star Wars Galaxies: Trials of Obi-Wan|''Trials of Obi-Wan'']] expansion<ref name="Trials of Obi-Wan">''[[Star Wars Galaxies: Trials of Obi-Wan]]''</ref> for [[Sony]] Online Entertainment's [[Star Wars Galaxies]]'' [[Star Wars video games|MMORPG]], published by [[LucasArts]] in [[2005]].<ref name="SWGMB">[[Star Wars Galaxies Memory Book|''Star Wars Galaxies'' Memory Book]]</ref>
