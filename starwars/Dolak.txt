'''Dolak''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Imperial Office of Customs/Legends|Imperial Customs]] officer and also the [[Commander/Legends|commander]] of an [[Imperial Customs Frigate]] stationed in the [[Rama system]].

==Personality and traits==
Dolak was brutal, vicious and corrupted. He let his suspects escape for a few credits.<ref name="CB62" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dolak was created by [[Jean Balczesak]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] scenario "Hold-up galactique" which appeared in the French magazine ''[[Casus Belli 62]]''.<ref name="CB62" />
