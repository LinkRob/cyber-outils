'''Contrail''', designated '''CT-4981''', was a [[clone trooper pilot]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. He flew under the [[callsign]] "'''Shadow Eleven'''" as part of [[Shadow Squadron]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Pilot]]ing a [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|Y-wing starfighter]], he participated in the [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula]], helping to cripple [[General]] [[Grievous]]'s [[flagship]], the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]''. He piloted an [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighter]] at the [[Battle of Coruscant]], during which he defended the [[Federal District]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
A [[Cloning|clone]] of the infamous [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]],<ref name="Clone Troopers" /> CT-4981&mdash;who took on the name Contrail<ref name="SWZ80">{{FFGXW2|set=Nimbus-class V-Wing Expansion Pack|cardname="Contrail"}}</ref>&mdash; was [[Reproduction|bred]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] to serve in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]<ref name="Clone Troopers" /> as a [[clone trooper pilot]]. During the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" /> he<ref name="Siege of Coruscant" /> was assigned to [[Shadow Squadron]], a [[squadron]] renowned for their effectiveness in combat and low casualties, and adopted the [[callsign]] Shadow Eleven.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" />

===Hunting the ''Malevolence''===
Following the disastrous [[Battle of Abregado]],<ref name="Rising Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> Shadow Squadron was assembled on the flight deck of the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Resolute]]'', where Contrail and the rest of his squadron were briefed by [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] on their attack on the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]''.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" /> The [[flagship]] of [[General]] [[Grievous]],<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|leader]] of the [[Separatist Droid Army]],<ref name="General Grievous">{{Databank|general-grievous|General Grievous}}</ref> the [[cruiser]] was responsible for the destruction of dozens of [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] task forces, including [[Jedi Master]] [[Plo Koon]]'s [[fleet]] in the [[Abregado system]].<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> The [[Jedi Knight]] explained that, although the [[Republic Navy]]'s [[capital ship]]s were vulnerable to the ''Malevolence''{{'s}} weapons systems, he believed that a squadron of [[bomber]]s could outmaneuver the ship's [[ion cannon]]s and destroy the [[heavy cruiser]]'s [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], hoping to [[Death|kill]] Grievous and bring down the ship. Skywalker then ordered Shadow Squadron to prepare their [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|Y-wing starfighters]] for the attack.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" />

As Shadow Squadron was preparing to depart the ''Resolute'', [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] received reports that the ''Malevolence'' had [[Battle of the Ryndellia system|attacked]] a convoy of [[Pelta-class frigate|medical transports]] in the [[Ryndellia system]], which was home to the Republic's secret [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] medical [[Space station|station]], [[Kaliida Shoals Medical Center]]. Skywalker predicted that the space station would be Grievous's next target.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" />

===Battle of Coruscant===
[[File:V-wing_SWDConv.png|thumb|right|250px|Contrail piloting a V-wing during the Battle of Coruscant.]]
Contrail was still a pilot in Shadow Squadron during the [[Battle of Coruscant]]<ref name="SWZ80" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> though by that point the squadron had been upgraded to piloting [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighters]]. During the battle, he defended the [[Federal District]].<ref name="SWZ80" />

==Personality and traits==
A clone of the [[human]] male Jango Fett,<ref name="Jango Fett">{{Databank|jango-fett|Jango Fett}}</ref> Shadow Eleven was 1.83 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Clone Troopers" /> He had tan skin, brown [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]]. Shadow Eleven was a skilled [[pilot]], and was able to successfully navigate through the Kaliida Nebula while only losing his astromech droid. During the Battle of the Kaliida Nebula, he was able to evade the EMP blast that disabled half of his squadron, and survived the bombing run that resulted in the destruction of the ''Malevolence''{{'s}} ion cannons and crippling of the heavy cruiser.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" />

==Equipment==
Shadow Eleven wore [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] with a [[flight helmet]] that was covered in gray markings. During the Battle of the Kaliida Nebula, he [[pilot]]ed a Y-wing starfighter.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" /> Near the end of the war, he piloted an Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighter during the Battle of Coruscant.<ref name="SWZ80" />

==Behind the scenes==
Shadow Eleven first appeared in [[Shadow of Malevolence|Shadow of ''Malevolence'']], the third episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''' [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]]. Like all clone troopers in the show, Shadow Eleven was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" />
