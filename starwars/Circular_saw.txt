A '''circular saw''' or '''buzz saw''' was a cutting tool that consisted of a rotating circular piece of metal with sharp blades protruding from its circumference.<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> Many different types of [[droid]]s were produced with buzz saws, including [[astromech droid]]s<ref name="RotJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> and [[toy droid]]s.<ref name="Kenobi I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref>

In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="MH date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'' to [[230 BBY]]. ''The High Republic: Midnight Horizon'' takes place shortly before and concurrently to ''The Fallen Star''. Therefore, its events must take place in approximately 230 BBY.</ref> the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization used a large modified buzz saw to attack [[Jedi Master]]s [[Yoda]] and [[Kantam Sy]] during the [[Battle of the Corellian Shipyards]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Pistoeka sabotage droid]]s were armed with circular saws, enabling them to cut through the [[hull]]s of [[starship]]s during combat.<ref name="Buzz droid">{{SWE|technology|buzz-droid|buzz droids}}</ref> In [[9 BBY]], [[L0-LA59]] used her saw to cut through the [[rope]]s tying [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] when she was [[Ambush on Alderaan|kidnapped]].<ref name="Kenobi I" /> Similarly in [[4 ABY]], [[R2-D2]] used his circular saw to free himself and his friends from an [[Ewok]] trap during their [[Battle of Endor|mission]] to [[Endor]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>
