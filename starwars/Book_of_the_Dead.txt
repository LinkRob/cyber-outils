The '''Book of the Dead''' was a rumored [[Book|text]] said to be owned by the [[Arakein Monks]]. During the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] joined a game of ''[[Who? What? Where?]]''<ref name="Victory's Price" />&mdash;in which the participants listed who they wanted to be the first to learn of their [[death]], what they thought would cause the death, and where they thought it would take place<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>&mdash;over a [[Comm unit|comm]] channel with the enemy [[New Republic pilot]] [[Vitale]]. Choosing the Arakein Monks for who first learned of their death, the Imperial [[pilot]] claimed that the [[monk]]s wrote deceased individuals' names in the Book of the Dead and that they would remember the individual forever.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Book of the Dead was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the third installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
