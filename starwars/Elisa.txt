'''Elisa''' was an [[Alderaanian]] [[Gender|female]] who served as the third [[aide]] of [[Galactic Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. According to Soh, the woman's [[Elisa's family|family]] had served as [[senator]]s and aides for generations.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> Elisa activated a [[holopad]] within the [[Emperor's office|Chancellor's Officer]] on<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Core Worlds]] planet<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] that allowed Soh to speak with the [[Nihil]] [[Minister of Information (Nihil)|Minister of Information]] [[Ghirra Starros]], who was at the [[time]] aboard her [[cruiser]], the ''[[Pearl of Faretta]]'', waiting for permission to dock. After she had turned on the device, the aide moved quickly out of the Chancellor's space. After Soh was finished with her conversation with Starros, she gestured at the aide, who cut the call off.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
