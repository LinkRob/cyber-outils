The '''Candorian Scourge''',<ref name="UP" /> also known as the '''Candorian Plague''',<ref name="Altaya">{{EncyclopediaCite|Naboo}}</ref> was an outbreak of the [[Blue Shadow Virus]] which occurred several [[Standard decade|decades]] prior to the [[Clone Wars]]. It claimed millions of lives in only forty-eight [[Standard hour|hours]].<ref name="UP" />

==Behind the scenes==
The [[Candorian plague/Legends|Candorian plague]] was created in the [[1995]] magazine [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 5|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 5]] as the disease that was used by the Galactic Empire to decimate the planet [[Dentaal]],<ref name="GNN5">{{JournalCite|5|Galaxywide NewsNets}}</ref> an event later mentioned in several [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] books over the years. The [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' [[Retroactive continuity|retconned]] the Blue Shadow Virus from [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars'' TV series]] as the Candorian plague virus.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> This retcon was conserved after the [[2014]] [[canon]] reboot as the [[Roleplaying game|RPG]] sourcebook ''[[Unlimited Power]]'' clearly associated the Blue Shadow Virus and the Candorian Scourge.<ref name="UP" />
