'''Frequency Zero''' was a frequency that was audible to the [[purrgil]], [[Realspace|space]]-faring creatures capable of [[hyperspace]] travel. By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the frequency was no longer in use. During the [[liberation of Lothal]], [[Ezra Bridger]] tasked [[Mart Mattin]], [[Wolffe]], and [[Cikatro Vizago]] with transmitting a signal on Frequency Zero. This drew a large pod of purrgil which wiped out the [[blockade]] by [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]] over Lothal and carried Bridger, Thrawn, and the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]'' into the depths of space.<ref name="FRAF">{{Rebels|Family Reunion &ndash; and Farewell}}</ref>
