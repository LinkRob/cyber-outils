The '''Critonia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square I-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Tarinna]] [[species]], also known as Critonian story vampies.<ref name="Adventures 32" />
