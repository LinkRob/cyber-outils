==Events==
*The [[Chiss Syndicure|Syndicure]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] removes the [[Coduyo family]] from the [[Ruling Families]], demoting them to a [[Great Families|Great Family]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Greater Good" /><ref name="Coduyo demotion note">''Greater Good'' takes place around 18 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. [[Senior Aide]] [[Xodlak'ji'iprip]] dated the Coduyo [[family emergency]] at [[Massoss]] to 32 years prior, and [[Coduyo'po'nekri]] said the Coduyo had not been a Ruling Family for 100 years before that; dating their removal to around 150 BBY.</ref>
