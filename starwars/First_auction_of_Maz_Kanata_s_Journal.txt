The '''first auction of ''Maz Kanata's Journal''''' was held by [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] in [[panopticon garret]] on the [[Zirtran's Anchor]] for the fake ''[[Smuggler's Guide|Maz Kanata's Journal]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Prelude==
{{Quote|What is an item worth? Whatever the buyer thinks it is worth.|Hondo Ohnaka quoting his [[Hondo Ohnaka's mother|mother]] in the ''Smuggler's Guide''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
After acquiring the [[book]] known as ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' in the [[lost-items office]] of [[Thikkiiana City]] on [[Kashyyyk]], [[Hondo Ohnaka]] wondered if it was fake, which inspired a new business plan. He visited the [[planet]] [[Florrum]] to find the master [[Forger (profession)|forger]], [[Bar the Rotter]], in the [[Cellar of Penitence]] at the [[Church of Infinite Perception]]. Hondo left Florrum, having persuaded Bar with a drink of [[Whyren's Reserve]], and set the course to [[Balmorra]] to find the [[Marquess]] next, who could manipulate [[Undervine]] network. Hondo planned for Bar to complete three logbook forgeries and for Marquess to reach out for buyers on the anonymous Undervine to arrange three auctions, while bidders would be unaware of competing auctions.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

Hondo organized the first auction in [[panopticon garret]] above the [[Casino floor (Zirtran's Anchor)|casino floor]] on the [[Zirtran's Anchor]] [[space station]], renting the room for 5,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==The auction==
{{Quote|This chamber is surely the most closely surveilled room in the entirety of Prosphura Belt. Who could pull a double cross in such a place?|Hondo Ohnaka about panopticon garret in the ''Smuggler's Guide''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
The first auction of ''Maz Kanata's Journal'' attracted agents from the [[Rang Clan]], the [[Xrexus Cartel]], and [[Novabarrel Distillery]].
[[Bar the Rotter]] played the part of rare document expert and the [[Marquess]] a convincing security guard. Bidding would have started at 75,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], until Rang Clan used a document authenticator to verify the fake ''[[Smuggler's Guide|Maz Kanata's Journal]]'' which ended the auction and started a [[blaster]] fight, but Hondo was prepared for such a catastrophe.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Be loyal to your partners, until better partners come along.|From the [[Book of Hondo]]|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
[[File:Droideka-USC.png|thumb|right|200px|Hondo thought it was unfortunate that the Xrexus Cartel had brought a droideka.]]
At the first sign of a [[blaster]], the [[Marquess]] jerked her neck to rattle the gears inside her headpiece, which made every screen on the walls flash white at the same time, blinding anyone who hadn't closed their eyes. But [[Xrexus Cartel]]'s [[droideka]] remained and followed Hondo, Marquess and Bar to the [[turbolift]]. Hondo closed the turbolift doors just before the droideka assumed attack stance, leaving behind Marquess and Bar, claiming that the turbolift wasn't big enough to fit three people. The word spread on [[Undervine]] about the forgery of ''Smuggler's Guide'', derailing Hondo's plans for the auction in the [[Boro-borosa system]] and the third auction.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The first auction of ''Maz Kanata's Journal'' was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and released in [[2018]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
