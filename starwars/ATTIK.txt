'''ATTIK''' is an advertising and branding agency that has created several pieces of work for [[LucasArts]]<ref name="official site">[http://www.attik.com/ attik.com], accessed August 7, 2010</ref> and the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] franchise, including the cover art of ''[[The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance]]''.<ref name="Fatal Alliance">''[[The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance]]''</ref> The design company, founded in [[1986]] by Simon Needham and James Sommerville in [[Wikipedia:Huddersfield|Huddersfield, England]], is now a division of the [[Wikipedia:Dentsu|Dentsu]] agency brand. ATTIK has offices in [[Wikipedia:San Francisco|San Francisco]], [[Wikipedia:California|California]] and [[Wikipedia:London|London]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]],<ref name="official site" /> and the company employs seventy people.<ref name="LinkedIn">{{LinkedIn|url=companies/18416|text=ATTIK|archivedate=20140630085607}}</ref> Aside from LucasArts, other clients of ATTIK include the [[Wikipedia:National Football League|National Football League]], [[Wikipedia:Adidas|Adidas]], [[Wikipedia:Coca-Cola|Coca-Cola]], [[Wikipedia:Sony Online Entertainment|Sony Online Entertainment]], [[Wikipedia:Toyota|Toyota]], and more.<ref name="official site" />
