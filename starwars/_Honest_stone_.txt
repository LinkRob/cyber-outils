'''"Honest stones"''' were irregular polyhedron&ndash;shaped items that changed their [[color]] when in the presence of electronic cheating devices that were used in [[sabacc]] games. A trio of "honest stones" was present on a sabacc table during a game of the [[Corellian Spike]] variant at [[the Lodge]] [[Cantina|saloon]] of the [[planet]] [[Vandor]] in [[10 BBY]].

==Description==
"Honest stones" were a type of item used to detect cheating during [[sabacc]] games. They had the shape of irregular polyhedrons and were made out of a [[Color|dark purple]] or light gray material with a [[marble]]-like surface. When in the presence of numerous varieties of electronic cheating technology, "honest [[stone]]s" would change their color.<ref name="SoloGuide" />

==History==
[[File:CorellianSpikeGame-Solo.png|thumb|left|300px|Three "honest stones" were once present on a sabacc table of the Lodge, where they were placed in front of Saucer Head (left from center).]]
In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' featuring the appearance of "honest stones" to [[10 BBY]].</ref> during a game of<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> the [[Corellian Spike]] variant of sabacc at the [[Cantina|saloon]] known as [[the Lodge]] on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]], three "honest stones," one light and two dark,<ref name="SoloGuide" /> were present on the saloon's gaming table.<ref name="Solo" /> They were strung on a solid wire attached to a [[wood]]en block, atop which also rested a holder for a deck of sabacc cards as well as a pair of [[Spike die|spike dice]] used in the game.<ref name="SoloGuide" /> The block was placed in front of the game's dealer,<ref name="Solo" /> [[Saucer Head]].<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2018 Topps Solo: A Star Wars Story|cardname=Saucer Head|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/176424/cid/11326611}}</ref> That [[Local day|night's]] game was ultimately won by the [[gambler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], a victory that was achieved through his usage of<ref name="Solo" /> a [[grabber]]&mdash;a mechanical, spring-powered device<ref name="Smuggler">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> that let Calrissian surreptitiously slip a hidden sabacc card out of his [[Clothing|shirt's]] sleeve into his [[Limb|hand]].<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Solo_A_Star_Wars_Story_Theatrical_Poster.png|thumb|right|190px|"Honest stones" were created for ''Solo: A Star Wars Story''.]]
"Honest stones" appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Anthology Series]]'' film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo" /> which released on [[May 25]], [[2018]].<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> They were named by the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloGuide" />

Before the film's release, the prop representing the set of "honest stones" seen in the film was also featured in a tie-in advertisement for the restaurant [[Wikipedia:Denny's|Denny's]]. In the advertisement, which was released on [[April 4]], 2018 and re-enacts a different version of the corresponding scene from the film, the items are also present on the sabacc table. Saucer Head is absent from the scene, and the "honest stones" are situated between the advertisement's representations of Lando Calrissian and<ref name="Denny's">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@dennys|name=Denny's|video=s8q-y2NkIK0|text=Solo: A Star Wars Story exclusive trading cards, available only at Denny's!|date=2018-04-02|nolive=1}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Han Solo]].<ref name="Solo" /> The prop from ''Solo'' was also present at a real-life sabacc game played as part of the [[September 26]], 2018 installment of ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] video series by the host [[Andi Gutierrez]] and the film's supervising stand-by propman [[Sonny Merchant]].<ref name="StarWarsShow">{{StarWarsShow|-fUc4a5CS-c|Vader Immortal First Look and Meet the Cast of Star Wars Resistance|sw_url=video/disneyxd-vader-immortal-first-look-and-meet-the-cast-of-star-wars-resistance}}</ref>
