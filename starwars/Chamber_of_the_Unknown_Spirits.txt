The '''Chamber of the Unknown Spirits''' was a location that, during the [[High Republic Era]], was considered holy ground by a number of individuals. It also housed the [[Reliquary]], a [[Religion|religious]] object that was considered holy by the same individuals, including a [[Unidentified steward|steward]].<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> By around [[247 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'' establishes that [[Kassav Milliko]] had [[Bombing of the Chamber of the Unknown Spirits|destroyed]] the [[Reliquary]] in the Chamber of the Unknown Spirits by the time of the failed [[Death|assassination]] attempt on [[Asgar Ro]] that resulted in [[Lourna Dee]] being promoted to [[Tempest Runner]], an event dated to around [[247 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Weequay]] [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Kassav Milliko]] was in the chamber, and threatened to destroy the Reliquary, despite those who considered the object holy being on their knees and begging him to not as the chamber was holy ground. Milliko, however, ignored them and [[Bombing of the Chamber of the Unknown Spirits|bombed]] the chamber with a [[proton bomb]], destroying the Reliquary.<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />

Around 247 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Milliko recounted when he had destroyed the Reliquary in the Chamber of the Unknown Spirits to [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Asgar Ro]]. After noting the object was left with a lot of holes in it, the two laughed.<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chamber of the Unknown Spirits was mentioned in flashbacks in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', a [[2021]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The capitalization of the chamber's name was later confirmed with the release of the audio drama's [[The High Republic: Tempest Runner (script)|script]] in [[2022]].<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />
