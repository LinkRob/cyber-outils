'''Cularin at Night''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] adventure ''[[Night Eyes: Night's Friend]]''. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[October 2]], [[2003]] and took the form of a recorded voice-only conversation posted to the [[Cularin/Legends|Cularin]] holonet by anonymous sources. It discussed the activities of the Thaereian Navy in rounding up citizens at night.
