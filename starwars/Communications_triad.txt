'''Communications triads''' were structures used as long-range communication relays within the [[Unknown Regions]]. Communication triads were thus named because they consisted of three poles or other structures used as transmitters or receivers. Such triads were used by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]],<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> the [[Grysk Hegemony]], and other civilizations in and bordering the Unknown Regions.<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref>

Communications triads could be constructed of land-based structures, vehicles, [[starship]]s, or even [[space station]]s.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], a communications triad was present on [[Batuu]], used by the [[Darshi]] to communicate with their [[Grysk]] overlords.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />
