'''Andronian''' was an entity that could produce ink utilized in [[painting]]s. En route to<ref name="Double or Nothing 1" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="BXW">{{BuildXWingCite|63|Starfighter Aces}}</ref> [[Batuv]]<ref name="Double or Nothing 1" /> in [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Lando - Double or Nothing 1|''Lando - Double or Nothing'' 1]] to [[10 BBY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]] considered adding a large Andronian-inked portrait of himself in the foreground of the area surrounding the [[hologame table]],<ref name="Double or Nothing 1" /> within the main hold<ref name="OWM">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> of his [[YT-1300 light freighter]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. He believed that the portrait would nicely accent new [[crystal]] hologame tables.<ref name="Double or Nothing 1" />
