'''Futhark''' was an ancient [[Writing|script]] of the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]], the [[human]] inhabitants of the [[planet]] of the [[Naboo|same name]]. By the time of the [[Invasion of Naboo]], it was still used by [[Royal House of Naboo|royalty]], the [[Noble|nobility]], and the government, as well as by the military to identify [[Starship|spaceships]]. The common folk, however, used the more recent [[futhork]] script for informal day-to-day communication.<ref name="VE" />

Futhork character were painted on the side of the [[N-1 starfighter]] piloted by [[Anakin Skywalker]] during the [[Battle of Naboo]].<ref name="Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace">{{Film|I}}</ref> Decades later, Futhork writing on the side of [[Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter]] said "Bheylic ZF".<ref name="Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger">{{BOBF|Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger}}</ref>
