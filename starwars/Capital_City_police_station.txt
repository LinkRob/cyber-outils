A '''police station''' was located in [[Capital City]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]]
of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Elainye Pryce]], the [[Parent|mother]] of [[Arihnda Pryce]], was held in custody at the station after being arrested by the [[Lothal police]] on the charge of embezzlement following complaints from the office of [[Governor]] [[Ryder Azadi]]. Arindha and her father [[Talmoor Pryce|Talmoor]] attempted to visit Elainye but were told by the [[Police officer|police]] at the station that she had been denied bail and they had also been ordered to not allow her visitors.<ref name="Thrawn" />
