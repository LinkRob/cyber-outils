An '''insurgency''' was fought in the [[Batonn sector]] between a coalition of insurgent groups on the planets [[Batonn]], [[Denash]], and [[Sammun]] against the rule of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The insurgent coalition was led by [[Nevil Cygni|Nightswan]], a shadowy insurgent leader who wanted to overthrow the Empire. The Batonn sector insurgency came to a violent end at the [[Siege of the Creekpath Mining and Refining complex|siege]] of the [[Creekpath Mining and Refining complex]] on [[Batonn]]. Following Nightswan's [[death]] there, the surviving insurgents fled into space. Due to his role in eliminating the rebel presence in the Batonn sector, the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]] promoted [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] to [[Grand Admiral]] and placed him in command of the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]].

==Prelude==
During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[pirate]] and insurgent leader [[Nevil Cygni]] (or "Nightswan") led an [[Nightswan's campaign|insurgent campaign]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]; which he regarded as a corrupt tyranny. Nightswan engaged in a range of activities including smuggling [[doonium]], [[Iridium (element)|iridium]], and [[tibanna]] to bankroll his campaign against the Empire. Nightswan's rebellious activities took the form of operating a [[Imperial espionage operation|spy ring]] on [[Coruscant]] and liberating [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Wookiee]]s. Nightswan's activities drew the attention of the [[Chiss]] [[Imperial Navy]] officer [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], his colleague [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]], and [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Colonel]] [[Wullf Yularen]], and the ambitious [[Lothalite]] [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

==History==
Following the [[Battle for Botajef|attempted secession]] of the [[Botajef]], the [[Batonn insurgents]] under [[Nevil Cygni]] captured and took hostage the [[Scrim Island Imperial garrison|Imperial garrison]] on [[Scrim Island]]. In response, [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Jok Donassius]] dispatched [[Admiral]] [[Durril]] to retake the island. Durril's [[One Oh Third Task Force]] was repelled by the insurgent forces on Scrim Island. Meanwhile, other elements of the [[Imperial Navy]] led by [[Admiral]] [[Kinshara]] and [[Commander]] [[Karyn Faro]] neutralized the insurgent forces on [[Denash insurgents|Denash]] and [[Sammun insurgents|Sammun]]. However, most of the Denash insurgents managed to evacuate to Batonn's [[Creekpath Mining and Refining complex]] where they joined forces with the Batonn insurgents under Nightswan.<ref name="Thrawn" />

Thrawn's [[96th Task Force]] later managed to use cunning tactics to neutralize Scrim Island's defenses and force the surrender of the insurgents there. Thrawn's attempts to end the [[Siege of the Creekpath Mining and Refining complex|siege]] of the Creekpath complex peacefully were undermined by an [[Imperial Security Bureau]] operation to plant explosives in the Creekpath and Governor Pryce's ignition of the explosives to hide her murder of the ISB [[Agent]] [[Gudry]]. The resulting explosion wiped out the Batonn insurgents and killed many civilians.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Aftermath==
Despite the high death toll, the pacification of the Batonn sector was considered an Imperial victory because the rebel presence in the Batonn sector had been eliminated. As a reward for his services, the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]] promoted Thrawn to [[Grand Admiral]] and placed him in command of the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]]. Due to his stellar reputation, Governor Pryce's enlisted Thrawn's help in combating the [[Phoenix Cell]] and [[Spectres]] rebel cell in the [[Lothal sector]].<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref> Following a prolonged campaign, Thrawn succeeded in identifying the Phoenix Squadron's [[Chopper Base|base]] on [[Atollon]] and inflicted heavy casualties on the rebels during the [[Battle of Atollon]].<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Batonn sector insurgency was first indirectly referenced in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' [[Star Wars Rebels Season Three|Season Three]] television movie ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]'', which premiered at [[Celebration Europe (2016)|Celebration Europe]] on [[July 16]], [[2016]].<ref name="Steps Into Shadow" /> It later appeared in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[2017]] novel [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']].<ref name="Thrawn" />
