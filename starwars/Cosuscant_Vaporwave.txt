{{Music-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cosuscant Vaporwave was designed for the [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'',<ref name="Art of Survivor" /> however it does not appear in the game itself.<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> It was first mentioned in ''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'', in which it was named in [[Aurebesh]] text on the album art. The name Cosuscant appears to be a misspelling of [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Art of Survivor" /> The artwork is similar to the final art used for "[[Ebon Flow]]," "[[Meet Me at the Sabacc Table]]," "[[Who Mourns for Taris?]],"<ref name="Survivor" /> and the real-world [[Sounds from the Galactic Skylanes (Original Music from Star Wars Jedi: Survivor)|''Sounds from the Galactic Skylanes'']].<ref name="Soundtrack">[[Sounds from the Galactic Skylanes (Original Music from Star Wars Jedi: Survivor)|''Sounds from the Galactic Skylanes'' (Original Music from ''Star Wars Jedi: Survivor'')]]</ref>
