'''Auk-Waimanu''' was a [[Lanai]] [[Gender|male]] who served as the [[captain]] of the [[Visitors]], a group consisting of the male Lanai population of the [[Temple Island]] on the [[planet]] [[Ahch-To]]. The group spent all of their time fishing and [[hunting]] while traveling across the [[ocean]] to other islands. Only returning to their home once per [[Standard month|month]], Auk-Waimanu would deliver their haul of [[Foodstuff|food]] to the [[Caretakers|female Lanai]] and then celebrate with them in a multi-[[Standard day|day]] [[Festival of Return|festival]]. He wore a [[Color|blue]] [[Clothing|robe]] under a [[flotation vest]] made from [[vacullacle]] shells along with a woven [[hat]]. Auk-Waimanu's skin was a mixture of gray and white in color and he had green [[eye]]s.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Auk-Waimanu was created to be a part of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']];<ref name="TLJVD" /> however, the scene he appeared in was ultimately cut before the final release.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> Despite this, the character still appeared in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> and released alongside the film on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref>
