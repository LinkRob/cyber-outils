The '''''Alif''-class Longbeam''',<ref name="Convergence" /> also known as the '''''Delta''-class starship''',<ref name="Art of THR">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref> was a class of [[starship]] used by the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> a party of Longbeams arrived in the [[Eiram system]] and was witnessed by [[E'ronoh]]'s [[Thylefire Squadron]] while they were rendezvousing with an [[ice hauler]].<ref name="Convergence" /> The ship was a precursor to the [[Longbeam-class corvette|''Longbeam''-class corvette]].<ref name="Art of THR" />
