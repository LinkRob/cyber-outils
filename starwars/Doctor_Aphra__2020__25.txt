'''''Doctor Aphra'' 25''' is the twenty-fifth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 26]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''DESOLATION! The SPARK UNDYING lives in DOCTOR APHRA'S body, and as its power grows, so does its hunger! But Aphra's plotting her own escape&hellip;! As everyone flocks to the ''VERMILLION'', their showdown will change everything!''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=DESOLATION
|text=As a Spark-possessed Doctor Aphra sought Ascendant tech aboard the ''Vermillion'', flagship of Crimson Dawn, the true Aphra found herself
trapped within her own memories. In this mental prison, Aphra encountered Miril, the creator of the Spark Eternal, who revealed the origins
of Ascendant technology.

Searching for their friend, Sana Starros and her team of Aphra associates arrived on Birukay, where they found the two murderous droids
BT-1 and 0-0-0 in need of some entertainment.

Ordered to steal the Spark, Just Lucky and Ariole Yu landed on the ''Vermillion'', where the Spark welcomed them with a Sith-killing blade&hellip;.}}
