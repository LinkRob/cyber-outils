'''Ateles''' were type of hooting [[bird]] that could be found in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]'s [[Great Basin|Canyon of Akar]]. While visiting the planet [[Kashyyyk]]<ref name="ALD" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> the Akivan [[Homeworld|native]] and [[pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]] considered the relative silence of the world, comparing it to the noises made by Akiva's fauna, including ateles in the Canyon of Akar.<ref name="ALD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ateles were mentioned in ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', a [[2016]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the second installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="ALD" />
