The '''Brentaal system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Bormea sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained at least one [[planet]], the [[gas giant]] [[Cavas]].<ref name="Squadrons" /> It lay at the junction of the [[Hydian Way]] and the [[Perlemian Trade Route]], two of the galaxy's most vital [[hyperspace route]]s, as well as the [[Commenor Run]].<ref name="Map" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Galactic Empire]] carried out an incursion into the Bormea sector, using jamming ships to cause communications blackouts for the [[New Republic]]. [[Commander]] [[Lindon Javes]] sent [[Vanguard Squadron]] to investigate these blackouts on the edge of the Brentaal System and re-establish communications.<ref name="Squadrons" />
