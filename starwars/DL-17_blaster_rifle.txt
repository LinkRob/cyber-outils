The '''DL-17 blaster rifle''' was a [[blaster rifle]] model built by [[BlasTech Industries]]. The [[bounty hunter]] [[Tam Posla]] carried one that was modified with an extended magazine when he was on the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[moon]] of [[Jedha]].<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide" /> The [[Mandalorian]] [[Pen Varad]] also carried a captured version of the rifle.<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref>
