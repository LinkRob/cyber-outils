'''Arsha Lome''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who worked as a [[lawyer]] representing [[Samovar]]'s [[mining]] conglomerate. During his [[time]] as a [[Adjutant General]], [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] knew Lome and claimed she had as sharp a legal mind as he'd ever encountered. Shortly after an operation carried out by a group of [[smuggler]]s at Samovar, Lome contacted Tarkin via [[hologram]] claiming her clients had no involvement in the arm shipment that the [[Galactic Empire]] discovered, Tarkin however dismissed this and claimed that the truth would eventually come out and that Lome's clients would have a day in court.<ref name="Catalyst" />
