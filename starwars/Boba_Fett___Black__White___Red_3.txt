'''''Boba Fett — Black, White & Red'' 3''' is the third issue of [[canon]] [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Boba Fett — Black, White & Red]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Juan José Ryp]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 26]], 2025.

==Publisher's summary==
''FETT IS OUT FOR REVENGE AGAINST THE ONE BOUNTY THAT'S ELUDED HIM! A dangerous figure from the BOUNTY HUNTER's past is back. And what is the connection to the mysterious B'OMARR MONKS? Featuring a no-holds-barred battle against the deadly DJAS PUHR!''

==Plot summary==
===Breach on Nar Shaddaa===
In the [[F'cote District]] of [[Nar Shaddaa]], the information broker [[Sheraxx Quorsha]] attempts to hide behind one-meter-thick reinforced durasteel doors. Despite the protection of the [[Sakiyan]] mercenary [[Djas Puhr]] and several hired killers, [[Boba Fett]] breaches the room using his jetpack and flamethrower. Fett subdues the security team, telling Puhr to "go back to sleep" after a physical altercation. He then threatens Quorsha, demanding information on a "dormant" bounty from his past.

===Failure on Ord Radama===
Years prior, a younger Boba Fett pursued [[Corm Vargon]] at the [[Blood Oath]] Spice Den on [[Ord Radama]]. The bounty was small, placed by poor fishmongers whose son Vargon murdered over a game of chance. Vargon, protected by a large bodyguard, mocked Fett’s skills and his father [[Jango Fett|Jango's]] legacy. After his bodyguard incapacitated Fett, Vargon left the hunter alive, taunting him that he would soon "ascend" beyond mortal reach.

===The B'omarr connection===
In the present, Fett consults with [[Korin Aphra]] to identify a symbol tied to a breakaway sect of [[B'omarr Order|B'omarr]] Monks. Aphra explains that this particular group consists of murderers and thieves who remove their brains and place them in nutrient-rich jars to escape judgment for their mortal sins.

===Final judgment on Javeek===
{{Quote|Oh, the bounty expired long ago. Nobody was looking for you anymore... except me. And I never let a bounty get away.|Boba Fett|<ref name="BWR3">[[Boba Fett — Black, White & Red 3|''Boba Fett — Black, White & Red'' 3]]</ref>}}
Fett tracks the sect to Javeek, where he finds Vargon's brain housed within a spider-droid chassis. Vargon claims that because the fishmongers' bounty expired long ago, he is no longer a target. Fett rejects this logic, declaring that while the bounty is dead to the rest of the galaxy, he never lets a target get away. He destroys Vargon’s droid form, finally completing the decades-old contract.
