'''Clan Kowak''' was a [[Clan (Jedi)|Jedi clan]] that existed during the [[High Republic Era]]. The clan contained [[Force-sensitive]] [[Conjeni]] [[youngling]]s. On one particularly hot [[Standard day|day]], the clan was chaperoned by [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Reina Bilass]] to a lesson at a swimming pool near the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. On the return journey to the temple, some of the rambunctious group wore their [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]] while others remained in [[swimsuit]]s.<ref name="What a Jedi Makes" />

Upon reaching [[Processional Way]] in front of the [[Jedi temple|temple]], Bilass silenced the younglings with a snap of her fingers, causing them to fall into a straight line as they approached the steps to the temple. [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] then emerged from the structure to greet the group, and when Yoda asked how their swimming lesson had gone, the clan members shouted back gleefully in response. Kriss then guided the group into the temple to lead them in [[meditation]].<ref name="What a Jedi Makes" />
