That's about it.|archivefile=File:Avga Rosene tweets Pablo Hidalgo.png}}</ref> was a [[Zuzabol]]<ref name="DI3.0" /> member of the [[Resistance]] who worked as part of a [[fuel]]ing crew<ref name="Actors tweet">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/878842136464052224|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=They were given names as part of a costume exhibit. Following the [[Battle of Takodana]], Rosene was at the landing zone of the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' shortly after it landed on D'Qar but headed over to the [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] ''[[Black One]]'' as its [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] dismounted. ==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Hey! Our mutual friend tells me you can help me blow up the garbage on this landing pad so my ship can land.|Avga Rosene greets the player in ''Disney Infinity 3.0''|<ref name="DI3.0" />}}
Avga Rosene first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']],<ref name="TFA" /> which was released on [[December 18]], [[2015]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> The character was one of a pair labeled as a fueling crew on the Resistance base who were played by actors [[Kiran Shah]] and [[Arti Shah]].<ref name="Actors tweet" /> The character did not receive a name in the film,<ref name="TFA" /> but was given one by [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Pablo Hidalgo]],<ref name="Naming tweet" /> who created Avga Rosene as a shorting of the term "[[Wikipedia:Aviation gas|aviation gas]]" and part of the word [[Wikipedia:Kerosene|kerosene]].<ref name="Name origin tweet">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/697526913271783424|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=I can't recall if the name for the 2nd one got out. It looks like I coined the one. Avga = aviation gas Rosene = kerosene|archivefile=File:Avga Rosene tweets Pablo Hidalgo.png}}</ref> The name was then revealed as part of a costume exhibition.<ref name="Actors tweet" />

Rosene also appeared in the non-[[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]'', which was released in conjunction with the film. In the game, Avga is owed a favor by the [[smuggler]] [[Athgar Heece]], who sends the player to help Avga destroy junk that is preventing the Resistance member's [[transport]] from landing on the planet [[Takodana]]. Once the [[starship]] lands, Avga has the player disguise a set of contraband as an [[astromech droid]] so it can be smuggled off world.<ref name="DI3.0" />
