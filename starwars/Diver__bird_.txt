The '''diver''' was a type of [[bird]] that could be found on [[Birren]],<ref name="Survivor" /> a [[planet]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]].<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> While living on Birren with her [[Akuna family|family]], the [[human]] [[Kata Akuna]] would go bird-watching and saw divers and other types of bird. She eventually left the planet and, after [[Death|losing]] both her [[parent]]s, she joined the [[Stinger Mantis crew|crew]] of the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'', a [[luxury yacht]] owned by the [[pilot]] [[Greez Dritus]],<ref name="Survivor" /> in [[9 BBY]]. In that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> Akuna listed the diver as part of an effort to remember all the birds from Birren while she was on the [[yacht]]. Dritus asked what she was doing after hearing the list and, after Akuna explained, the pilot asked for more information on the birds since he had never been to Birren. Akuna, however, put off the discussion till later.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
The diver was mentioned in an ambient conversation in the [[2023]] [[Respawn Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Survivor" />
