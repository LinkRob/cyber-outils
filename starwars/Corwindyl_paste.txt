'''Corwindyl paste''' was a bright [[Color|blue]] paste made from crushing [[Corwindyl]] [[Plant|herbs]] together with a little oil. It had a tangy-sweet aroma as well as warming and mild pain relieving affects. The [[masseur]], [[Lexo Sooger]], used it to treat his hands by having his daughter [[Lula Sooger]] rub it along the knuckles and tendons in his [[Limb|hands]].<ref name="Hear Nothing">{{StoryCite|story=Hear Nothing, See Nothing, Say Nothing|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref>
