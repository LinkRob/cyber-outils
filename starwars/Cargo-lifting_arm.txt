A '''cargo-lifting arm''',<ref name="SC 103 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> alternatively known as a '''cargo lift''',<ref name="SC 103" /> a '''cargo-loading claw arm''',<ref name="SC 105 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> or a '''lift claw''',<ref name="SC 107 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|We're Gonna Be In So Much Trouble}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> was a claw-shaped mechanical device designed for heavy-duty lifting and grasping. A robust structure equipped with multiple joints and flexible grippers, the arm allowed for a range of motion and could easily adapt to various shapes and sizes.<ref name="SC 103" />

A cargo-lifting arm was installed in the ''[[Onyx Cinder]]'', a [[starship]] that once belonged to the renown [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Tak Rennod]].<ref name="SC 105" /> Abandoned on the isolated [[planet]] of [[At Attin]], the ''Onyx Cinder'' was found by four [[youngling]]s who accidentally started the [[vessel]] and embarked on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] journey.<ref name="SC 101">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref> Known the [[Skeleton Crew]],<ref name="T19XifsgFS0">{{SWYouTube|T19XifsgFS0|Skeleton Crew &#124; Blast from the Past &#124; Streaming Dec 2 on Disney+}}</ref> the children allied themselves with former Captain [[Jod Na Nawood]] and together with him travelled to the [[Observatory Moon]] to seek guidance from a friend of his, [[Kh'ymm]]. As the crew walked out of the ''Onyx Cinder'', the youngling [[Wim]] mounted the cargo-lifting arm as it descended down the entry ramp, babbling to<ref name="SC 103" /> himself.<ref name="SC 103 AD" /> After Jod found<ref name="SC 103" /> the [[Gender|boy]]<ref name="SC 101" /> playing, he reprimanded him for using the arm for unintended purposes.<ref name="SC 103" />

The Skeleton Crew younglings used the cargo-lifting arm one more [[time]] on [[Lanupa]]. Dressed as the "most wise elders", they mounted the lift claw and held on to it as it descended<ref name="SC 105" /> for a more spectacular entry.<ref name="SC 105 AD" /> After the children parted ways with Jod, thier ship ''Onyx Cinder'' was intercepted by a [[Silvo's pirates|pirate crew]] that had been keeping Jod prisoner. As pirate Captain [[Brutus]] walked up the entry ramp, the minors grasped him in the cargo lift, promising to release him only if their demands were satisfied. With Brutus trapped in the claw, Jod approached him and shot him [[Death|dead]], assuming the leadership of the pirate crew.<ref name="SC 107" />

==Behind the scenes==
A cargo-lifting arm appeared in the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'', with several names used for the mechanism. In "[[Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem]]," the audio-description voice-over artist refers to it as a ''cargo-lifting arm'',<ref name="SC 103 AD" /> only for [[Jod Na Nawood|Jod]] to call it a ''cargo lift'' [[Time|moments]] later.<ref name="SC 103" /> In addition, the voice-over artist uses the names ''cargo-loading claw arm'' in "[[You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates]]"<ref name="SC 105 AD" /> and ''lift claw'' in "[[We're Gonna Be In So Much Trouble]]".<ref name="SC 107 AD" />
