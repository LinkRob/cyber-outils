'''Further Conversations with Lanius''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[September 16]], [[2004]] and included an interview of [[Lanius Qel-Bertuk]] by [[Yara Grugara]] on the state of affairs at the [[Almas Academy]], oversight by the [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]], and changes in Academy staff.
