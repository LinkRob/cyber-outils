The '''Corellian Lawman''' was a costume owned by the [[Miraluka]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Diago Velaar]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Frontier Sharpshooter|standard costume]]. This outfit included a semi-formal uniform over top.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian Lawman was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Diago, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.17.0 update on [[July 18]], [[2023]], during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Lawman's Rifle]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing the Corellian Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
