'''Allyuen''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Contact" /> located in [[Allyuen system]] in the [[Anoat sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]], in grid square K-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was home to the [[Allyuen spaceport]],<ref name="Contact" /> which was [[garrison]]ed by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Assault" /> Allyuen and several other locations were identified in a [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Year Ref">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' establishes that this report was generated in the [[Standard year|year]] 7980 C.R.C., the same year that [[Han Solo]] was frozen in [[carbonite]]. Given that Solo was frozen in carbonite during the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' established took place in [[3 ABY]], it can be concluded that 7980 C.R.C. is equivalent to 3 ABY.</ref> [[Imperial Security Bureau]] report that generated targets for [[Viper probe droid]] surveys by cross-referencing areas with a high degree of suspicious activity with the unsettled parts of the galaxy favored by the rebels.<ref name="Star Wars: Scum and Villainy" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
During the [[Iron Blockade]], which was established by the Empire in an attempt to prevent news of their crushing defeat by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] at the [[Battle of Endor]] from reaching the Anoat [[sector]]'s populace,<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> an [[Unidentified individual (Allyuen)|unidentified individual]] on Allyuen sent out a call for help on a less used communication frequency, requesting medical aid and extraction. In response, [[the Smuggler]] sent a crew member to Allyuen to render assistance.<ref name="Help">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: "A Call for Help"</ref>
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Allyuen was first mentioned in the [[The Empire Strikes Back (radio)|radio adaptation]] of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="The Empire Strikes Back">[[The Empire Strikes Back (radio)|''The Empire Strikes Back'' radio drama]]</ref> It became [[canon]] when it appeared in the [[Wikipedia:Action role-playing game|action role-playing game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was developed by [[Kabam]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms<ref name="Uprising" /> and released on [[September 10]], [[2015]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-uprising-available-now|text=''Star Wars: Uprising'' Available Now}}</ref> The [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' misspelled Allyuen as Alluyen on a map of suspected Rebel bases.
