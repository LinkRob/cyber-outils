A '''light cruiser''' belonged to the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation's [[Coduyo family]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> In [[50 BBY]],<ref name="Coduyo emergency note">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' takes place around 18 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. The novel establishes that the [[Unidentified alien sect|alien]] threat to [[Coduyo family]] holdings on Massoss took place thirty-two years prior to the novel's events, in [[50 BBY]].</ref> a breakaway [[alien]] [[Unidentified alien sect|sect]] threatened Coduyo family holdings on the [[planet]] [[Massoss]]. As the [[Chiss Defense Force]] was occupied elsewhere, the [[Chiss family|family]] [[Family emergency|recalled]] [[Chiss officer|officers]] and [[Chiss warrior|warriors]] from Defense Force [[warship]]s to man the light cruiser. They were able to gather three-hundred Coduyo family personnel, and were able to drive off the aliens from Massoss.<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Behind the scenes==
The light cruiser was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', the second installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Greater Good" />
