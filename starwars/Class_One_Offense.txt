A '''Class One Offense''', also known as a '''Class One infraction''', was a category of crime outlined in the [[Imperial Penal Code|Imperial Legal Code]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Following the implementation of the [[Public Order Resentencing Directive]] in [[5 BBY]], all crimes with even indirect effect to the [[Galactic Empire]]'s functions were reclassified as Class One offenses, carrying longer and more severe sentences. When [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] was arrested on [[Niamos]] shortly after the PORD came into effect, the crimes he was accused of&mdash;among others, fleeing the scene of anti-Imperial activity and anti-Imperial speech&mdash;resulted in a [[prison]] sentence of six [[standard year|years]]. Years later, when [[Jyn Erso]] was arrested by Imperial forces on [[Corulag]], she was similarly charged with two Class One offenses and sentenced to more than five years of prison time.

==Description==
Under the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] justice system as it was codified in the [[Imperial Penal Code|Imperial Legal Code]],<ref name="Build R2 8">{{BuildR2Cite|8|Droid Directory}}</ref> crimes were grouped into five different classes<ref name="Scum">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> including Class One and Class Two,<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> with Class One offenses being the most severe. Most Class One offenses carried a [[prison]] sentence<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> often served in supermax security facilities, but the Imperial response could also escalate to the direct involvement of [[Imperial Security Bureau|Imperial Security services]] or the [[Imperial Military|military]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> Crimes classified as Class One included:

*Damaging Imperial property<ref name="Announcement" />
*Disruption<ref name="Announcement" />
*Fleeing the scene of anti-Imperial activity<ref name="Announcement" />
*Anti-Imperial speech<ref name="Announcement" />
*Aggravated assault against Imperial military personnel<ref name="ROUVG" />
*Escape from custody<ref name="ROUVG" />
*Conspiracy against the Galactic Empire<ref name="Scum" />
*Unsanctioned and unsupervised<ref name="Rise" /> [[Pirate|piracy]]<ref name="Scum" />
*Fomenting rebellion<ref name="Rise" />
*Active sedition<ref name="Rise" />
*Assassination attempt against a senior Imperial official<ref name="Rise" />

==History==
In [[5 BBY]]<ref name="Date">"[[Announcement]]" takes place shortly after "[[Kassa (episode)|Kassa]]," which dates itself to [[5 BBY]].</ref> following the shocking [[Aldhani heist]] the Galactic Empire passed two new laws -the [[Public Order Resentencing Directive]] and the [[Imperial Emergency Act]]&mdash;to strengthen its security and better respond to anti-imperial activities. The former directly affected the Imperial justice system, reclassifying all crimes with even indirect effects to the Empire as Class One offenses and stipulating stricter sentences for them.<ref name="Announcement" />

During that same year, [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], one of the perpetrators of the heist, was arrested on the [[planet]] [[Niamos]] after being caught in the same area as some suspected [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]]. For declaring his innocence and trying to reason with the [[coastal defender stormtrooper]]s performing the arrest, Andor was accused of disruption, anti-Imperial speech, fleeing the scene of an anti-Imperial activity, and damaging Imperial property, which usually carried a six-[[Standard month|month]] prison sentence. As the four crimes were now classified as Class One offenses, the sentence was increased to six years<ref name="Announcement" /> and Andor was transferred to the [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex]].<ref name="N5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref>

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> former [[Partisans|Partisan]] [[Jyn Erso]] was arrested on [[Corulag]] after being caught carrying unsanctioned [[weapon]]s and resisting the Imperial forces attempting to arrest her. Erso broke out of Imperial custody after assaulting several officers,<ref name="Scum" /> and was charged with two Class One offenses: aggravated assault and escape from custody,<ref name="ROUVG" /> being sentenced to more than five years of hard work<ref name="R1 novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> on the [[Imperial Detention Center & Labor Camp LEG-817]].<ref name="R1">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
