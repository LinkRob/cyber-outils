'''Coburn Sear''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Kage]] [[Kage Warriors|Warrior]] and [[Colonel]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]] and was also the brother of fellow CIS officer, Captain [[Rackham Sear]].

==Biography==
Sear was part of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederate]] [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid forces]] on the [[planet]] [[Kardoa]]. There, alongside [[General]]s [[Grievous]] and [[Kleeve]], they saw surveillance from a [[Ringneck recon droid|Recon droid]] confirming the [[Galactic Republic|Republic's]] presence on Kardoa. Kleeve and Grievous then left Sear to deal with [[Jedi]] [[Jedi General|General]] [[Depa Billaba]]'s [[Clone trooper|clone]] battalion. Sear then led the [[Skirmish on Kardoa|attack]], separating Billaba from her battalion and [[Padawan|apprentice]], [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]].<ref name="Kanan 9" />

However, Sear was forced to retreat when Billaba and a couple of troopers attacked his [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|transport shuttles]].<ref name="Kanan 9" /> Sear then went to the planet [[Mygeeto]] and joined Grievous in the [[Third Battle of Mygeeto|third battle]] for that world. Sear, Grievous, and their [[Confederacy military|forces]] then attacked Billaba's base. There he killed [[Cloning|clone]] trooper [[Stance]] in front of Dume. The colonel then confronted Dume as Grievous confronted Billaba.<ref name="Kanan 10">[[Kanan 10|''Kanan'' 10]]</ref>

Dume then fought Sear to avenge Stance. They fought until Dume slashed at Sear's [[flamethrower]]. Sear was then offered a chance to surrender, but refused. Unfortunately, Sear inadvertently lit themself on fire and attempted to take Dume out as well but [[Death|died]] when Caleb cut him down with his [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Kanan 11" />

==Equipment==
