'''Fruit punch''' was an [[Color|orange-colored]]<ref name="Workbook" /> [[fruit]] beverage that was [[Diet|consumable]] by [[human]]s<ref name="MA" /> and [[Ithorian]]s. The drink could be served in [[glass]]es with a slice of magenta-colored fruit as a garnish.<ref name="Workbook" />

In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> the human [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] attended a private [[yacht]] party sponsored by the company [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]], which was put on to celebrate her upcoming [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]]. She sipped from a pink glass of fruit punch while speaking with the company's [[Sector Supervisor]] [[Meritt Col]].<ref name="MA" /> At some point, the Ithorian [[Momaw Nadon]] drank fruit punch while seated in<ref name="Workbook" /> [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in the [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Tatooine]].<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fruit punch was first mentioned in [[Claire Piddock]] and [[Megan Butler]]'s ''[[Star Wars Workbook: 3rd Grade Math]]'', published in [[2017]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars Workbooks]]'' series. The beverage was featured as part of a hypothetical math problem in which<ref name="Workbook" /> the [[Pirate King|smuggler queen]]<ref name="Datbank-MazKanata">{{Databank|maz-kanata|Maz Kanata}}</ref> [[Maz Kanata]] divided thirty-two [[liter]]s of fruit punch into eight glasses.<ref name="Workbook" /> Fruit punch made its first appearance in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" /> In the real world, fruit punch is a beverage made from [[Wikipedia:Fruit|fruit]] [[Wikipedia:juice|juices]] mixed with [[Wikipedia:Water|water]] or [[Wikipedia:Carbonated water|soda water]] that is often served [[Wikipedia:Ice|iced]].<ref name="Vocabulary">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vocabulary.com/dictionary/fruit%20punch|text=Fruit punch - Definition, Meaning & Synonyms|work=[https://www.vocabulary.com vocabulary.com]|archivedate=20231127023938}}</ref>
