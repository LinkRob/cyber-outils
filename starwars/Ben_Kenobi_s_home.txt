{{Quote|Well, I don't know anyone named Obi-Wan, but old Ben lives out beyond the Dune Sea.|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode IV" />}}
'''Ben Kenobi's home''', also known as '''Obi-Wan's hut'''<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and '''Obi-Wan's House''',<ref name="Obi-Wan's House">{{Databank|obi-wan-s-house|Obi-Wan's House}}</ref> was the home of [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who used the alias Ben Kenobi, on [[Tatooine]]. Abandoned by its [[Unidentified moisture prospector|previous owner]], the hut was located in the remote [[Jundland Wastes]], situated on a bluff surrounded by the [[Western Dune Sea]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

==Description==
It was located 136 [[kilometer]]s from the [[Lars homestead]].<ref name="SW:AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> Created out of [[Synstone/Canon|synstone]], the house was a simple dwelling with one large room divided into a bathroom, a living room and a raised kitchen.<ref name="Jedi Master 4">[[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 4|''Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine'' 4]]</ref> It was built on top of a sheltered [[cave]]. Kenobi used the cave as a small workshop and a cellar to store [[Foodstuff|food]], and spent most of his [[time]] in the house above. A [[moisture vaporator]] was next to the house.<ref name="Episode IV" /> Kenobi spent most of his time walking around the canyons near his house and meditating.<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

==History==
[[File:ValiantDeed-CM.png|thumb|left|250px|Luke Skywalker defends himself against Boba Fett while visiting Kenobi's hut.]]
Built high on a cliff, the hut<ref name="Time of Death">{{StoryCite|story=Time of Death|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> was built by mining prospectors from the [[Core Worlds]] who thought that Tatooine was rich in mineral wealth. A moisture prospector built it due to its higher-than-average water condensation.<ref name="Complete Locations" /> The building was eventually abandoned and left empty.<ref name="Time of Death" />

After the [[Order 66|fall]] of the [[Jedi Order]], when he brought the infant [[Luke Skywalker]] to live with the [[Lars family]], Kenobi initially lived in a [[Ben Kenobi's cave|cave]] near the Lars homestead for ten years.<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref> Finding it on its windswept bluff,<ref name="Time of Death" /> Kenobi eventually moved from the cave into the small house. He lived there for nine [[Standard year|years]] until the [[Rescue of Princess Leia|mission]] to rescue [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]], a mission in which Kenobi was [[Death|killed]] by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> After Kenobi's [[death]], Skywalker returned to the home in order to learn more about the ways of the [[Jedi]].<ref name="SW5">[[Star Wars (2015) 5|''Star Wars'' (2015) 5]]</ref> While searching the hut, he was found by [[Boba Fett]], whom Darth Vader had hired to find the [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] who had [[Battle of Yavin|destroyed]] the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. They [[Duel in Ben Kenobi's home|fought]], but Skywalker escaped.<ref name="Star Wars 6">[[Star Wars (2015) 6|''Star Wars'' (2015) 6]]</ref> Sometime later, Vader, accompanied by [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]], came to the hut in his search for his son. When they left, Aphra used a [[Molecular purge bomb]] to conceal the fact that she and Vader had visited the hut.<ref name="Darth Vader 7">[[Darth Vader (2015) 7|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 7]]</ref>

Shortly before the [[rescue of Han Solo|rescue of]] [[Han Solo]] from [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]], Luke returned to the home and found some essential parts for [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|his new green lightsaber]].<ref name="beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|Star Wars: Complete Locations]] states that [[Bestine]] is the nearest settlement. This information is contradicted by the same book. The map of Tatooine reveals multiple cities like [[Mos Espa]] who are closer to Kenobi's home.<ref name="Complete Locations" />

In the original script of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], Ben was described as living in a cave. By the time production started on Episode IV, the location was transplanted into a hut, but there are still blueprint drawings referring to his home as a "cave." The series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' introduced a cave that was drawn from the original script. Placing Kenobi in a cave and not his hut during the series met several story needs. As established in [[Dorling Kindersley|DK Publishing's]] ''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]'' map, Kenobi's hut was quite far from the Lars homestead, and the series needed him closer to keep an eye on the farm.<ref name="Part VI guide">{{SW|url=series/obi-wan-kenobi/part-vi-guide|text=Obi-Wan Kenobi: Part VI Episode Guide}}</ref>
