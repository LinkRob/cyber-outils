'''Charlie''' was a [[Jawa/Legends|Jawa]] in the galactic classic book ''[[Charlie and the Droid Factory]]''. In the tale, Charlie won the chance to tour a [[Droid factory (disambiguation)|droid factory]], as well as operating it later on. The story also featured Charlie coming into possession of an object known as [[The Golden Widget]].<ref name="bully" />
