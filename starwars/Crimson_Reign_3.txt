'''''Crimson Reign'' 3''' is the third issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Crimson Reign]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Steven Cummings]], [[Victor Olazaba]], and [[Guru-eFX]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[March 9]], [[2022]]. The issue contains the story '''Part 3: "The Archivist"'''.

==Publisher's summary==
''ODYSSEY OF THE ARCHIVIST! LADY QI'RA'S ultimate plan to reshape the galaxy continues as we focus on one of her most key operatives &ndash; the mysterious SAVA MADELIN SUN, former expert in the DARK SIDE OF THE FORCE, now known only as THE ARCHIVIST. Qi'ra has given this woman a crucial task and put all the resources of CRIMSON DAWN at her disposal. The Archivist's search will find answers to mysteries at the very heart of the EMPIRE'S formation&hellip;and the ultimate fall of THE JEDI ORDER.''

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|It's not that the Jedi are forgotten. It's that the very idea of them is a death sentence. Those who remember, stay silent. In time, they will die. And the Order will truly be lost.|The Archivist|<ref name="Crimson Reign 3">[[Crimson Reign 3|''Crimson Reign'' 3]]</ref>}}

===The Scholar and the Syndicate===
The [[The Archivist|Archivist]] provides a holographic meditation on her identity, revealing her former life as '''Madelin Sun''', a "Sava" and prominent scholar at the [[University of Bar'leth|University of Bar’leth]]. Her academic career and personal stability were dismantled following [[Order 66]], which reclassified her research into [[The Force|the Force]] - specifically the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] - as a treasonous act. After years spent living as a destitute junk dealer, Sun was confronted by Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s who discovered a forbidden Force artifact in her possession. [[Qi'ra|Lady Qi'ra]] intervened, executing the troopers and recruiting Sun into [[Crimson Dawn]].

Qi'ra solidified Sun’s loyalty by revealing that Emperor Palpatine was the Sith Lord Darth Sidious. This revelation transformed Sun’s despair into rage, as she realized her career had been intentionally suppressed by a Sith agenda rather than mere historical circumstance. Sun accepted the title of the Archivist and was granted access to a repository of Sith relics and [[Ascendant]] technology, including the [[Thought Dowser]].

===The Search for Yoda===
The Archivist's inaugural assignment for Crimson Dawn was to determine the fate of [[Yoda|Grandmaster Yoda]]. While the Empire claimed the [[Jedi]] were extinct, Qi'ra suspected Yoda’s survival due to the lack of an official death confirmation. Utilizing her expertise in research and database [[slicing]], the Archivist tracked Yoda's post-purge movements to the [[Tantive III]] and [[Bail Organa]].

She eventually located [[Jeremoch Colton]], a former pilot for the [[House of Organa]], in an Imperial detention facility. Using the [[Thought Dowser]], she extracted repressed memories from Colton, tracing Yoda’s path through  [[Polis Massa]]  to the [[Sluis sector]]. Her subsequent academic analysis identified [[Dagobah]] as a likely destination due to its immense strength in the Force.

===Transformation on Dagobah===
Upon arriving at Dagobah, the Archivist narrowly survived a [[Swamp Beast|swamp beast]] attack before being drawn to a powerful Force nexus beneath a massive tree. Inside the cave, she underwent a spiritual trial that resulted in a physical transformation, leaving her with greyed skin and a clouded white eye. In the aftermath of this encounter, she gained the clarity to realize that Yoda’s location was a secret of immense value that could buy her freedom from either Qi'ra or the Empire.

However, moved by the fact that Yoda permitted her to live and learn rather than destroying her, the Archivist chose to protect the Jedi's sanctuary. Returning to the ''[[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|Vermillion]]'', she provided a false report to Qi'ra, claiming that Yoda was deceased. Qi'ra accepted the findings, and the Archivist found a sense of peace by prioritizing loyalty to her new cause over personal advancement.
