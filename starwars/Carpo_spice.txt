'''Carpo spice''' was a type of [[spice]] distributed on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Lanz Carpo]] by [[Boss]] [[Carpo]] and his [[Carpo Crime Syndicate|crime syndicate]]. When [[Han Solo]] was sent with [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] on a [[Mission to Lanz Carpo|mission]] to Lanz Carpo for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], he considered trying some Carpo spice.<ref name="Star Wars 68" /> Solo was eventually captured by Boss Carpo, and when the [[crime lord]] let him go free in exchange for the rebels eliminating the regional [[Unidentified Imperial governor (Lanz Carpo)|Imperial governor]], who Carpo believed was out to attack him, Solo was given a crate of Carpo spice.<ref name="Star Wars 73">[[Star Wars (2015) 73|''Star Wars'' (2015) 73]]</ref>
