==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TechnicianTemplate.jpg|250px|thumb|Randomly generated depictions of Alben Kultosi]]
Dialogue pertaining to Alben Kultosi appeared in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']]. However, he never actually appeared on the [[Star Wars video games|video game]]'s live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]].<ref name="AED" />
