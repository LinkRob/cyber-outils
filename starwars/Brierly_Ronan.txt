'''Brierly Ronan''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who served as a [[colonel]] and the [[Assistant Director|assistant director]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] under the [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]]. After working with the Imperial [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], he agreed to join the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], under the pretense of serving as an ally, though he also intended to become an enemy if he found Thrawn's true interests did not align with those of the Empire.

==Biography==
[[File:Ben_Mendelsohn_Rogue_One.png|thumb|left|250px|Birerly Ronan was a subordinate of Director Orson Krennic (pictured).]]
[[Colonel]] Brierly Ronan was the [[Assistant Director]] on the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Project Stardust]] and was extremely loyal to [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]]. He was assigned to work with [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] when [[gralloc]]s began attacking shipments in the [[Kurost sector]] vital to the construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. Ronan, throughout the journey, hated Thrawn and did his best to slow the [[officer]] down, to get the funds necessary for the Stardust project, though he found himself coming to respect the officer at least a slight bit more during their time together. During his time with Thrawn, Ronan witnessed the threat that the [[Grysk Hegemony]] posed to [[Galactic Empire|the Empire]] and encountered members of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. He believed that [[Vah'nya]], a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Chiss]] [[navigator]], was a [[Jedi]] after seeing her react to an Imperial falling forward before he actually fell. He also worked with [[Lieutenant]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]], a former [[commander]] of the [[Imperial Navy]] and current member of the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]], in the [[mission to Aloxor]], where they sought to learn what was truly happening with the [[starship]]s attacked by grallocs.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

Thrawn had agreed to destroy the grallocs within a [[Standard week|week]], or else funding for his [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]] project would be given to Stardust. Ronan witnessed Grand Admiral Thrawn solve the true conspiracy behind the creatures' attacks as the work of Grand Admiral [[Balanhai Savit]], who believing Stardust would not succeed, had caused the gralloc attacks to let his operatives [[Hyperspace|jump]] away with project components he could steal. Thrawn had defeated Savit in the [[skirmish against the Third Fleet]], but, because of Ronan's belief in Stardust and that the [[Chiss]] officer had not technically destroyed the grallocs, Ronan told Krennic and [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] that Thrawn had failed. After this, however, Thrawn suggested that [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] be assigned to watch over Stardust as security until the first test since he was the only Imperial who could deal with the threats to the project. [[Governor]] Tarkin agreed with the Grand Admiral's idea.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

[[File:Vader-Alliances.png|thumb|right|225px|Thrawn threatened Brierly Ronan by bringing Darth Vader into Project Stardust.]]
After the discussion with Krennic and Tarkin ended, Thrawn told Ronan that he would be unable to keep his hidden contempt for [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] a secret from Vader like he did from Krennic. Thus the Grand Admiral gave him three choices: return to the project and be [[Death|killed]] by the [[Sith Lord]] for harboring hatred towards the Emperor, resign and try to travel far away, or to travel with Admiral [[Ar'alani]] to join the Chiss Ascendancy. Due to his position in the project, Ronan knew he would be questioned if he took the second option, thus leading to death at Vader's hands for abandonment as well as his negative thoughts about Palpatine. Despite this, he was originally resistant to Thrawn's third choice. Trying to convince Ronan to join, Thrawn cited that he had seen the threat of the [[Grysk]]s, that he had worked with many [[alien]] [[species]], that his insights into how others thought and acted could be useful in the coming Grysk war, and that he eventually would be killed if he stayed within the Empire. Thrawn also promised that Ar'alani could return him to the Empire if he was not useful. In making his decision, Ronan also remembered Vah'nya and her Force-sensitivity.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

Falsely believing that Thrawn and Palpatine had a secret plan to destroy the supposed Jedi among the Chiss, Ronan thought Thrawn may be sending him to be a hidden Imperial asset within the Ascendancy. He then realized Thrawn had not been facing him when he saw Vah'nya use [[the Force]] and believed the Grand Admiral could not know he had realized she was Force-sensitive. Still, Ronan thought he could become a secret ally, believing such a role could sometimes be better. Thus, he agreed to leave with Ar'alani, though he claimed he would only go for the time being, and only if she could prove the Grysks were a threat to the Empire. Thrawn offered Ronan the chance to change his mind, but the Assistant Director decided he would not, or at least not yet. Additionally, Ronan thought that, if Thrawn was actually working against the [[Emperor]], he would not be a pawn or secret ally. Instead, he believed he could be a dangerous enemy. Ar'alani herself thought Ronan's recruitment was a terrible mistake, though Thrawn felt the man's unwillingness was part of "the framework."<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|His name is assistant director Ronan. He is one of the assistant directors on Stardust, extremely loyal to Director Krennic, not really liking Thrawn much at all.|Timothy Zahn on Ronan|<ref name="Slashfilm">{{WebCite|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/565710/timothy-zahn-is-planning-more-star-wars-books-teases-thrawn-tetralogy-at-star-wars-celebration/|text=Timothy Zahn Is Planning More Star Wars Books, Teases Thrawn Tetralogy At Star Wars Celebration|author=Solzman, Danielle|date=2019-04-14|work=[[Wikipedia:/Film|''SlashFilm'']]|archivedate=20250806000525}}</ref>}}
Brierly Ronan first appeared in ''[[Thrawn: Treason]]'', a novel by [[Timothy Zahn]] which was released in [[July]] [[2019]] as the third and final installment in the [[Star Wars: The Imperial Trilogy|''Star Wars: Thrawn'']] trilogy.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> At [[Celebration Chicago]] in [[2019]], Zahn&mdash;while not able to reveal much ahead of release&mdash;introduced the character as someone incredibly loyal to Krennic and who was not very fond of Thrawn. He also confirmed that Ronan wore a cape but, owing to his station as an assistant director, it was only thigh-length, instead of matching the full-length cape donned by Krennic.<ref name="Slashfilm" />
