It was created by [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] following the [[Death|execution]] of the [[Treason|traitorous]] [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]], head of the [[Naval Intelligence Agency]],<ref name="Palpatine" /> in [[14 BBY]].<ref name="Date" />
