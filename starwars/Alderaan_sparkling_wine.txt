'''Alderaan sparkling wine''' was a type of sparkling [[wine]]<ref name="Cataclysm" /> that shared its name with the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Creighton Sun]] and the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Aida Forte]] were familiar with the beverage, with the former opening a bottle of it on a [[Temperature|hot]] [[Standard day|day]] on at least one occasion.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the two [[Jedi]] [[Night of Sorrow|fought]] together against the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|religious]] group on the planet [[Dalna]], with Forte spotting several Path members carrying explosive devices on their [[belt]]s. Forte conversed with Sun about his experience opening a bottle of Alderaan sparkling wine, indicating that they were about to jostle their opponents similarly to shaking an unopened container of the wine.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
