The '''Derelict Hangar''',<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> also known as the '''Landing Pad'''<ref name="Art JFO">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> was a [[hangar]] located on the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]], used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was close to the [[Ice Caves]] and the [[Abandoned Village]]. During a [[Mission to Zeffo|mission]] to Zeffo as part of their [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|mission]] to rebuild the [[Jedi Order]], [[Cere Junda]] and [[Cal Kestis]] arrived at the hangar from the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'', [[pilot]]ed by [[Greez Dritus]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{Struct-stub}}

==History==
By 14 BBY, the Empire arrived on the planet Zeffo and initiated Project Auger. Because of this, the Derelict Hangar became off-limits to anyone other than the Empire through Imperial Code 94364. The code also forbade trade or selling at the Derelict Hangar.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Derelict Hangar was created for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]] and published by [[Electronic Arts]]<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> on [[November 15]], [[2019]].<ref name="YBY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> The location was first revealed in the [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1wWkQXUMQYI Xbox E3 Briefing] of the game on [[June 10]], 2019.<ref name="JFO Xbox E3 Briefing 2019">{{EASWYouTube|1wWkQXUMQYI|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order Official Trailer &ndash; Xbox E3 Briefing 2019|sw_url=video/star-wars-jedi-fallen-order-e3-trailer}}</ref> Concept art of the hangar was created by artists [[Bruno Werneck]] and [[Jean-Francois Rey]].<ref name="Art JFO" /> Like with the rest of the game, the location is heavily influenced by [[Wikipedia:Metroidvania|3D Metroidvania type design]]&mdash;an [[Wikipedia:Action-adventure game|action-adventure]] subgenre defined by large, interconnected maps containing paths that could be unlocked as the player progresses through the game.<ref name="GameSpot">{{GameSpot|url=articles/e3-2019-star-wars-jedi-fallen-order-didnt-begin-as/1100-6467511|text=E3 2019: Star Wars: Jedi Fallen Order Didn't Begin As A Star Wars Game}}</ref> The hangar was the very first area that was developed for the game. In ''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' reference book, the Derelict Hangar was referred to as the "Landing Pad."<ref name="Art JFO" />
