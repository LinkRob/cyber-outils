During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] ordered the '''destruction''' of a [[Jedi Temple (Ledeve)|Jedi Temple]] on [[Ledeve]]. After previously [[Death|killing]] a [[Unidentified Mikkian Jedi|Jedi]] and their [[Unidentified Jedi Padawan (Grievous)|Padawan]], Grievous came across the [[Jedi temple|temple]] and attempted to claim the temple's secrets for himself. Following his experiences in the temple, where he was mocked by voices for what he had become and being small, Grievous ordered a [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] to destroy the temple. In the aftermath of the temple's destruction, Grievous claimed that for all of the entities' talk of power, they burned just as easily. However, a glowing item arose from the wreckage of the temple as the General left.<ref name="Age of Republic" />

==Behind the scenes==
The reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' established the destruction of the Ledeve Jedi Temple occurs in [[20 BBY]], laying out its timeline placement as between the [[Battle of Umbara]]<ref name="Timelines" /> and [[conquest of Kiros]].<ref name="Timelines note" /> However, the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' later alleged that the mission occurred before the [[skirmish on Naboo]],<ref name="SWECG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> which would not match the placement given for that skirmish as a pre-Umbara battle in ''Timelines''. As ''Timelines'' is specifically designed to lay out the chronology of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' universe,<ref name="Timelines" /> this article assumes ''Star Wars Encyclopedia'' is incorrect on this matter.
