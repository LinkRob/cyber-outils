The '''DL-2 line''' was a type of [[B1-series battle droid]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[Rodian]] [[Unidentified Rodian droidsmith (DL-2)|droidsmith]] in [[Myrra]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Akiva]], sold [[battle droid]]s including at least one of the DL-2 line. They priced the DL-2 line at 500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] each.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> [[Kay Vess]], while acting as a middle person for a [[Unidentified human government official|government official]] purchased a DL-2 line from a [[droidsmith]] near the [[Hutt Cartel District (Myrra)|Hutt Cartel district]] of Myrra. When she later met with the official for payment she learned that the official was working with [[Crimson Dawn]] and was having surveillance equipment installed in the droid.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The DL-2 line appeared as part of the "Government Business" intel quest in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. If the player has an excellent reputation with the Hutt Cartel when purchasing the droid they will be able to get the droid for free instead of paying the 500 credits.<ref name="Outlaws" />
