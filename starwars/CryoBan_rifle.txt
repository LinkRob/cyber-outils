The '''CryoBan rifle''', manufactured by [[Loronar Corporation]], was a model of [[rifle]] made in the tradition of [[Ulban Arms]] and several other manufacturing companies who have weaponized [[CryoBan]] technology. Capable of flash freezing targets, the CryoBan rifle made inorganic substances particularly brittle. [[Mercenary|Mercenaries]] engaging [[battle droid]]s often brought at least one such rifle to make the [[droid]]s vulnerable to allied attacks. The rifle was also popular among [[bounty hunter]]s, firefighters, and scientists.<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
The CryoBan rifle was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Gadgets" /> It was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'',<ref name="TOR">''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''</ref> a [[2011]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] developed by [[BioWare]] Austin and released by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="LucasArts Announce">{{LucasArts|url=company/release/news20081021.html|text=LucasArts and BioWare reveal ''Star Wars'': The Old Republic}}</ref>
