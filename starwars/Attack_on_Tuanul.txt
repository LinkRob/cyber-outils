The '''attack on Tuanul''', also known as the '''Massacre at Tuanul''', the '''Battle of Tuanul''', the '''Assault on Jakku''', or as the '''secret mission to Jakku''', was a raid carried out against [[Tuanul]], a [[Sacred Villages|spiritual village]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]], by the [[First Order]] during the [[Cold War]]. The assault was led by the [[Dark side of the Force|dark]] enforcer [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], who had been seeking a [[Map to Skywalker|map]] that would lead to the [[Ahch-To|location]] of the missing [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]. As four First Order [[squadron]]s from Kylo Ren's forces descended on Tuanul with a force of eighty [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]], the [[Resistance pilot]] [[commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] received the map from the [[Resistance]] ally [[Lor San Tekka]]. Dameron attempted to flee prior to the assault but his [[Poe Dameron's blue X-wing|T-70 X-wing starfighter]] was disabled by the attacking stormtroopers, despite his [[droid]] [[BB-8]]'s warning just before the assault began. Dameron had BB-8 escape the village with the map. Subsequently, Tekka was killed by Kylo Ren, Dameron was captured, and the villagers were killed.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|I've traveled too far and seen too much to ignore the despair in the galaxy. Without the Jedi, there can be no balance in the Force.''"<br />"''Well, because of you, now we have a chance.|Lor San Tekka and Poe Dameron|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
By [[34 ABY]], the [[First Order]] knew [[Lor San Tekka]] was hiding on [[Jakku]] with a piece of the [[Map to Skywalker|map]] containing the location of [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]], whom they wanted to find and destroy. [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] wanted the map, and so he, [[Captain]] [[Phasma]], and a First Order unit of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] and [[First Order flametrooper|flametroopers]] traveled in [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport]]s to the village where Tekka lived, [[Tuanul]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

==The attack==
===Arrival of the First Order===
{{Quote|You have to hide.''"<br />"''You have to leave.|Poe Dameron and Lor San Tekka|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:Stormtroopers-raid-Jakku.png|thumb|left|250px|The First Order raided the village of Tuanul during the hunt for Luke Skywalker.]]
As they disembarked from their shuttles, the stormtroopers quickly overcame initial resistance of the villagers with few casualties. Meanwhile, at [[Resistance]]<ref name="Episode VII" /> [[commander]]<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Poe Dameron]]'s urging, [[BB-8]] successfully fled from Tuanul with the map in tow as the pilot joined in the battle. Dameron shot and killed several stormtroopers, including [[FN-2003]], whose [[death]] was witnessed by fellow [[trooper]] [[Finn|FN-2187]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> FN-2003's death led to an awakening of his conscience and opened the trooper's eyes to the brutality of the First Order, which would eventually lead him to desert the First Order.<ref name="FinnDB">{{Databank|finn|Finn}}</ref> Just as FN-2187 began to see this brutality, [[Kylo Ren's command shuttle]] landed.<ref name="Episode VII" />

===The massacre===
{{Quote|Sir, the villagers?''"<br />"''Kill them all.|Phasma and Kylo Ren|<ref name="Episode VII" />|audio=PhasmaRen-KillThemAll.flac}}
[[File:KyloRenFacesLorSanTekka-SWBC44.png|thumb|right|250px|The villagers were executed on the orders of Kylo Ren, who personally killed Lor San Tekka.]]
The defeated villagers of Tuanul were herded into a large group before San Tekka was brought to Ren himself, who requested him to turn over information on the map. San Tekka claimed that Ren could not deny the truth that was his own [[Skywalker family|family]], causing Ren to slay the old man with [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]] after sarcastically telling the latter that he was correct in his claim. In retaliation, Dameron fired on Ren, but the dark warrior [[Force Stasis|caught]] the blast and subsequently captured the [[pilot]], and deduced that San Tekka had given the map to him. Dameron was searched and taken aboard Ren's [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]'' for interrogation. Meanwhile, Captain Phasma and her stormtroopers&mdash;except for FN-2187&mdash;executed the remaining villagers on Ren's orders.<ref name="Episode VII" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|The First Order. They're horrible. Rumor has it an attack squadron of theirs destroyed a sacred village right close to here, over near Kelvin Ravine.|Rey|<ref name="TFA novel" />}}
[[File:The_Massacre_at_Tuanul.jpg|thumb|left|200px|The attack on Tuanul was remembered in anti-First Order propaganda.]]
Disillusioned by the brutality of the [[First Order military|First Order forces]] during the attack on Tuanul, FN-2187 decided to defect from the First Order. He freed Dameron from his [[Prison|cell]] and convinced him to [[Escape from the Finalizer|escape]] together on a stolen [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]. Their TIE was shot down by the ''Finalizer''{{'s}} weaponry and crash-landed in Jakku's [[Goazon Badlands]]. After being separated from Dameron, Finn encountered BB-8 and the local [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> Rey likewise had heard rumors about the attack, and also had distaste for the First Order from their more brutal methods.<ref name="TFA novel" /> They were pursued by First Order forces but managed to [[Escape from Jakku|escape]] on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' into [[Realspace|space]]. After encountering [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]], they convinced Solo to help them get BB-8, who was carrying the map, back to the Resistance.<ref name="Episode VII" />

In large part because of the brutal nature of the attack, it inspired a [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] showcasing flametroopers that participated in the attack, called "[[The Massacre at Tuanul]]."<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|My first thing I had to do was kill Max von Sydow. I have this long saber, and I can't see where it's going. So I didn't want to, on my first day, just whack Max von Sydow. That would be the end of the movie, for me. I missed him — but it was total luck, because I was kind of swinging blind|Adam Driver's concerns while filming the scene|<ref name="Kylo's Fear" />}}
The attack on Tuanul appears in the [[2015]] film {{Film|VII}}, the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

In the early iterations of the film, Kylo Ren mentioned his parentage to Lor San Tekka in the film's first scene instead of San Tekka implying it in the aftermath of the attack. This idea was later dropped by director [[J.J. Abrams]], as he didn't wanted to reveal the mystery of Ren's parents to the audience too soon.<ref name="Commentary">{{Film|VII}} Director [[J.J. Abrams]] commentary</ref> On the other hand, the aftermath of the attack was [[Adam Driver]]'s first scene on the film and he feared to be fired if he accidentally struck [[Max von Sydow]] with Ren's lightsaber as he couldn't see clearly with his mask. He ultimately avoided whacking von Sydow by good luck.<ref name="Kylo's Fear">{{WebCite|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/movies/movie-features/adam-driver-star-wars-dec-2019-cover-912404|text=How Adam Driver's angst and brooding intensity made the world fall in love with a 'Star Wars' supervillain|author=Hiatt, Brian|date=2019-12-02|work=''[[Wikipedia:Rolling Stone|Rolling Stone]]''|archiveurl=https://archive.is/ZM1Q2}}</ref>

The scene with Poe Dameron and BB-8 along his X-wing were filmed in the studio backlot using the full-sized vehicle. Abrams and the filming crew used the death of FN-2003 to show that stormtroopers were humans and not robots.<ref name="Commentary" />
