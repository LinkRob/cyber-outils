This '''landing field''' was owned by the [[Corporate Alliance]], and was part of a [[spaceport]] located on the [[planet]] [[Murkhana]]. It consisted of a hexagonal spaceport, and a quartet of ten-[[kilometer]] bridges that linked it up to the [[Murkhana City]]. After the [[Clone Wars]], it was slagged and tipped on the piers that supported it, while the bridges had collapsed into the [[water]] below it. Because of its state, [[starship]]s arriving at Murkhana were redirected to the city's original spaceport.<ref name="Tarkin" />
