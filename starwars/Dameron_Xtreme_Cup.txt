The '''Dameron Xtreme Cup''' was a race hosted on [[Castilon]] in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> The race was made up a single round against [[Poe Dameron]], and the [[pilot]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] would eventually win the race. Upon winning, Kazuda received an [[T-70 X-wing starfighter|X-wing]].<ref name="Racer" />

{{Event-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Dameron Xtreme Cup first appeared in the non-[[canon]]ical [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Resistance Racer]]''. The game has the option to race as [[Kazuda Xiono]] or [[Tamara Ryvora]]. This article assumes Kazuda, who is the default choice, is correct.<ref name="Racer" />
