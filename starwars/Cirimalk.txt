'''Cirimalk''' was a [[City|town]] on<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion VG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Adelphi]] that contained a card hall. On one occasion before his [[Calrissian family|daughter]] [[Kadara Calrissian]] was kidnapped<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> in [[15 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the former [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[general]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] spent a few [[Standard day|days]] in Cirimalk with Kadara and<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> the [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|woman]]<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref> [[Kaasha Bateen]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> In [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> when he visited the [[New Republic staging outpost]] at Adelphi with [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]], Calrissian [[Brain|mentally]] recalled the [[time]] he had visited Cirimalk.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
