"'''Build the X-Wing'''" is a department in ''[[Star Wars: Build Your Own X-Wing]]'', a reference magazine published by [[De Agostini]]. It contains instructions for building the [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] using the parts supplied in the issue.<ref name="Issue 1">{{BuildXWingCite|1|Build the X-Wing}}</ref>
