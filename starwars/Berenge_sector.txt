The '''Berenge sector''',<ref name="OOTS" /> also capitalized as the '''Berenge Sector''',<ref name="CotJ">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> was a largely empty [[sector]]<ref name="OOTS" /> that was a subsector of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Rayter sector]].<ref name="Appendix" /> A largely administrative division, the sector was once controlled by the [[San Tekka clan]] on a lease from the [[Galactic Republic]] for [[hyperspace]] experimentation. After the lease expired, a group of academics led by [[Professor]] [[Thaddeus Wolk]] petitioned for the sector to be set aside for academic purposes. However [[Xylan Graf]] wanted to secure a lease on the sector for his family, the [[Graf family]], in order to conduct weapon testing in it. While the sector's use was being disputed, the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] and the [[scientist]] [[Chancey Yarrow]] hid in the Berenge sector on the [[Gravity's Heart]] [[space station]] in order to test an experimental [[gravity well projector]]. The Republic did not know about the existence of the Nihil space station, however [[Thaddeus Wolk]] had the theory that a [[Nihil]] hyperspace weapon was operating in that part of space which subsequently resulted in an investigation being launched by [[Ghirra Starros]] to verify or disprove his theory. The investigation led to the discorvery and subsequent destruction of the space station, additonally it uncovered ties of the [[Graf family]] with the Nihil.<ref name="OOTS" />
