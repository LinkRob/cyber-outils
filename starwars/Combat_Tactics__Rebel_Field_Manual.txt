'''Combat Tactics: Rebel Field Manual''' was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] document authored by [[Sergeant]] [[Ruescott Melshi]] of the [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces]] during the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]]. The manual discussed guerrilla warfare tactics and included in-depth descriptions of various [[stormtrooper]] variants of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The document was later included in ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', a collection of Alliance documents that was [[Excavation on Durkteel|discovered]] in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

The first version of field guide also contained mentions of the [[Partisans]] although a new edition was created shortly after [[Mon Mothma]]'s censure of Saw Gerrera and the Partisans, resulting in [[Pitt Onoran]] revising and expunging all mentions of the group at the behest of Mothma.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
