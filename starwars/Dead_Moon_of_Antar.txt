The '''Dead Moon of Antar'''<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> was a [[moon]]<ref name="Destroy Malevolence" /> that [[orbit]]ed the [[planet]] [[Antar]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> located in the [[Prindaar system]]<ref name="Location" /> of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="Beginner Game" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> Situated along the [[Nanth'ri Trade Route]]<ref name="Nanth'ri Route" /> between the celestial bodies [[Lonera]] and [[Gyndine]], it was mapped in grid square N-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Beginner Game" />

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula|boarded]] the<ref name="Destroy Malevolence" /> [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> ''[[Malevolence]]'' and sabotaged its [[navigation computer]], locking it into a collision course with the [[Death|dead]] moon. Unaware of Skywalker's modifications, the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[starship]]'s crew of [[B1-series battle droid]]s activated its [[hyperdrive]], destroying the ''Malevolence'' and its [[droid]] crew when it [[Ramming|smashed]] into the Dead Moon of Antar at [[lightspeed]].<ref name="Destroy Malevolence" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The idea being that we haven't seen a ship smash into a planet at the speed of light or in hyperspace, so this was the opportunity to show it, and what better ship than the ''Malevolence''?|[[Henry Gilroy]], on the Dead Moon of Antar's role in the ''Malevolence''{{'s}} destruction|<ref name="Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season One">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season One|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season One]]</ref>}}
The Dead Moon of Antar first appeared, albeit unidentified, in "[[Destroy Malevolence|Destroy ''Malevolence'']],"<ref name="Destroy Malevolence" /> the fourth episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]], which originally aired on [[October 17]], [[2008]].<ref name="Episode Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> It was first mentioned, albeit indirectly, in ''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]'', a [[1989]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] supplement written by [[Troy Denning]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races">''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]''</ref> The dead moon was first identified as a "dead moon of Antar" in ''[[The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia]]'', a reference book<ref name="CSWE">{{CSWECite|II|280|''Malevolence'' (I)}}</ref> released on [[December 9]], 2008.<ref name="The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia release date">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/160596/the-complete-star-wars-encyclopedia-by-stephen-j-sansweet-pablo-hidalgo-bob-vitas-daniel-wallace/9780345477637|text=The Complete Star Wars® Encyclopedia by Stephen J. Sansweet, Pablo Hidalgo, Bob Vitas, Daniel Wallace}}</ref> This name, albeit capitalized, became canon when it was used in ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', a [[2015]] reference book written by [[Tricia Barr|Patricia Barr]], [[Adam Bray]], [[Daniel Wallace]], and [[Ryder Windham]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />
