'''CT-Thirty-Eight''' was a [[droid]] that served onboard the ''[[Ataraxia]]'' while it was owned by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Lourna Dee]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. When [[Jedi Master]] [[Keeve Trennis]] boarded the ''Ataraxia'' by cutting through its [[hull]] with her [[lightsaber]], CT-Thirty-Eight contacted the [[starship|ship]]'s flight deck to report the breach and activated [[force field|energy field]]s to seal it. After she informed the flight deck that the breach had been sealed, she was destroyed by Trennis. Lourna Dee then attempted to contact CT-Thirty-Eight, but Trennis responded instead and told Dee of the droid's destruction.<ref name="THR 2023 3" />
