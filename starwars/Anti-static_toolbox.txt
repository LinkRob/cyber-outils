An '''anti-static toolbox'''<ref name="SWCL 2016" /> was a type of [[Color|white]]-colored box for storing tools. One side of the toolbox had a large square indent topped by a curved lip. The [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] kept an anti-static toolbox on a shelf next to the workbench in his bedroom, which was located in his [[Skywalker home|home]] in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The toolbox was on the shelf<ref name="TPM" /> in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}} to [[32 BBY]].</ref> when Skywalker showed [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] the [[protocol droid]] he had been working on, [[C-3PO]]. Skywalker did not take the toolbox with him when he later left Tatooine to join the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
An anti-static toolbox first appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="TPM" /> The box was first named in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="SWCL 2016" /> The name originated in the [[2000]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]].<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
