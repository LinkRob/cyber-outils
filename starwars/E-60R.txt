The ''' E-60R''' was a model of [[missile launcher]] utilized by [[B1-series battle droid]]s of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. They could be used to pierce [[armor]], even when fired at long-range.<ref name="New Models" /> [[Rebel Vanguard]]s made use of the E-60R during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="SWC" />
