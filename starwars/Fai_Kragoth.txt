'''Fai Kragoth''' was a [[human]] [[merchant]] who operated a stall in the [[South Stretch Worker's District]] of the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Kragoth had light skin, [[Color|brown]] [[hair]] and brown [[eye]]s. During the [[Canto Cup]] [[fathier]] race<ref name="Outlaws" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the shop's inventory included [[bacta vial]]s and wore a white [[Clothing|shirt]], brown [[belt]], green [[hat]], brown pants.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fai Kragoth appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
