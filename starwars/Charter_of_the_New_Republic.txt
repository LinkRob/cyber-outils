The '''Charter of the New Republic'''<ref name="Chapter 7">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 7: The Reckoning}}</ref> was a document outlining the principles of the [[New Republic]]'s government, as agreed upon by the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]]. The document contained multiple passages, including ones detailing the authority of the [[Chancellor (New Republic)|chancellor]], later the proposed [[First Senator]] if it came to fruition, and one declaring that no person should have to answer for the crimes of a parent. Several [[Senator|members]] of the Senate, including [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], believed the constitution to be flawed.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> It also allowed scavengers to declare ownership of a [[droid]]'s wreckage ("flotsam") that they had discovered. [[Kuiil]], an [[Ugnaught]], recovered [[IG-11]]'s body and claimed it as his own, in accordance with the charter.<ref name="Chapter 7" />
