A '''dental mop''' was a cooking utensil recommended to be used in the cooking of the creature [[Smapp]]. The recipe called for six quarts of viscous [[Butter/Legends|butter]] to be sculpted and then mixed with twelve tubes of [[Herbs de Lunar Eclipse]], all of which was then spread over the creature's body via the dental mop prior to putting it in the oven.<ref name="MAGL">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dental mop was mentioned in a one-page recipe titled "Cooking a Smapp," written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that collects concept designs and photographs from various [[George Lucas]] projects.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]]<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon?
