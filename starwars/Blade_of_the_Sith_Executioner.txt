The '''Blade of the Sith Executioner''' was a [[Sith sword]] wielded by the [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Koval Renge]] during his time as the [[Sith Executioner]], the personal enforcer of the [[Tenebrae|Emperor]] of the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|revitalized Sith Empire]]. Forged thousands of [[Standard year/Legends|years]] before the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]] between the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and the Sith Empire, in a [[Time/Legends|time]] before [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsabers]], the Blade was used by Renge to [[Death/Legends|kill]] hundreds of the Emperor's enemies. However, when Renge attempted to betray the Emperor<ref name="Codex" /> sometime well before [[3950 BBY]],<ref name="Revan" /> the Blade was turned upon its owner. Part of the sword broke off in Renge's chest, and though his wounds were healed before he was cast into the depths of the [[Dark Temple]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Dromund Kaas/Legends|Dromund Kaas]], the fragment remained buried in Renge's body.<ref name="Codex" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Blade of the Sith Executioner was first mentioned in its own Codex entry in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', a [[2011]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] released by [[BioWare]] in which the Blade was originally slated to appear before being cut.<ref name="Codex" />
