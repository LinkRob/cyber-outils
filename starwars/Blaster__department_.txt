'''Blaster''' is a department in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine, first appearing in [[Star Wars Insider 101|''Star Wars Insider'' 101]]. It involves all the latest merchandise including, books, comics, collecting and toys. It also features sub-departments like ''[[Ask Lobot]]'', [[Bounty Hunters (department)|''Bounty Hunters'']] (Pictures of fans with the ''Star Wars'' stars) and [[Bantha Tracks (department)|''Bantha Tracks'']]. It replaced the magazine's earlier ''[[Jawa's Corner]]'' department.

From [[Star Wars Insider 179|''Star Wars Insider'' 179]] onwards, ''Blaster'' has been edited by [[Christopher Cooper]].
