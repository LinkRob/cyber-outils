The '''''Conquest''''' was a [[Secutor-class Star Destroyer|''Secutor''-class Star Destroyer]] in service of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> in [[14 BBY]].<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> It was in use by [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]] as a [[command ship]] while partaking in the [[blockade]] of the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' above [[Carida]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> The ''Conquest'' was 2,200 [[meter]]s in length.<ref name="Ships" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Conquest'' was created for [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], a novel written by [[James Luceno]] and released in [[2014]].<ref name="Tarkin" />
