The '''All Terrain Ewok Transport''' ('''AT-ET''')<ref name="Rebel Ewok">{{ArenaNews|url=fb34948329d642718c47b6d0b4ba68cf-en-gb|text=The Rebel Ewok}}</ref> was a custom-built [[walker]] utilized by the [[Ewok]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Pilbush]] during the competitive sporting show [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] within the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="SWH">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> The walker was built by the [[Mon Calamari]] Hunter [[Sprocket]] as a scaled down replica of the [[All Terrain Scout Transport]].<ref name="Ewok Editorial" /> It came equipped with a side mounted [[grenade launcher]] also capable of launching [[Cluster bomb (ground)|cluster bombs]], and could also gain an additional temporary speed boost while also healing its driver.<ref name="Announcement" />
