The '''BTL-A4 LP''', or "'''Longprobe'''," was a model of [[BTL Y-wing starfighter]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was designed for long-distance reconnaissance missions, and provided more space for the crew and equipment. It also had more efficient [[sensor]]s, and several were provided with ejectable emergency modules which could be used as vehicles with limited [[Repulsorlift|repulsors]].<ref name="Starfighters" />
