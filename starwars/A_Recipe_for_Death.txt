"'''A Recipe for Death'''" is a short story by [[Landry Q. Walker]] that was published inside the anthology ''[[Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I]]'' on [[April 5]], [[2016]]. The story recounts the disappearance of a [[Chef|sous chef]] working at [[Takodana Castle]].

==Publisher's summary==
''When his sous chef turns up dead, the cook at Takodana Castle holds an unusual competition to find the culprit in "A Recipe for Death."''<ref name="Edelweiss" />
