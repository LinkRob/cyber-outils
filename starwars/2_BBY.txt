==Events==
*After [[Batonn insurgency|defeating]] [[Batonn insurgents|insurgents]] led by [[Nevil Cygni|Nightswan]] on [[Batonn]], [[Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] is promoted to [[Grand Admiral]] by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>
*Grand Admiral Thrawn is deployed to [[Lothal campaign|confront]] the growing rebellion.<ref name="Steps" /><ref name="SWR S3 date" />
*[[Phoenix Cell]] [[Mission to Reklam Station|acquires]] five [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]s from the [[Reklam Station]] [[salvage yard]], which are given to [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]] and [[Massassi Group|his cell]].<ref name="Steps" /><ref name="SWR S3 date" />
*With the help of [[Sabine Wren]], [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Derek Klivian]] [[Escape from Skystrike Academy|escape]] from [[Skystrike Academy]] and join the Phoenix Cell.<ref name="TAE" /><ref name="HC57">{{HelmetCollectionCite|57|Highlights of the Saga}}</ref>
*The [[Syndulla residence]] on [[Ryloth]] is destroyed by [[Hera Syndulla]].<ref name="HH" /><ref name="SWR S3 date" />
*The [[Journeyman Protector|Protectors]] of [[Concord Dawn]] are wiped out by [[Viceroy]] [[Gar Saxon]] and his [[Imperial Super Commando]]s.<ref name="ISC">{{Rebels|Imperial Supercommandos}}</ref><ref name="SWR S3 date" />
*The [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|Mandalorian Civil War]] begins.<ref name="Legacy of Mandalore" /><ref name="SWR S3 date" />
*The [[Ghorman Massacre]] occurs when Imperial [[troop]]s respond to peaceful protestors on the planet [[Ghorman]] with violence.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
*[[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] is branded a traitor after speaking out against the Empire's actions at the Ghorman Massacre and [[Escape from the Senate|flees]] [[Coruscant]]<ref name="UVG" /> and the [[Imperial Senate]].<ref name="Fully">{{StoryCite|story=Fully Operational (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref><ref name="Escape Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' establishes that the [[escape from the Senate]] occurred between the [[Ghorman Massacre]] and before [[Operation Handoff]], both of which occur in [[2 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>
*The rebellious cells [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|organized]] into the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] above [[Dantooine]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" />
*The rogue [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] has [[Mission to Tatooine (Imperial Era)|one final duel]] with [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Although the [[Jedi]] [[Death|defeats]] him, Maul dies in Kenobi's [[Limb|arms]], stating that [[Luke Skywalker|the one he believes to be]] the [[Chosen One]] would avenge them both.<ref name="Death year">According to ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]'', [[Maul]]'s [[death]] in his final confrontation with [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] happened thirty years after Kenobi first saw him on [[Tatooine]]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates that event to [[32 BBY]], Maul died in 2 BBY.</ref><ref name="Twin Suns">{{Rebels|Twin Suns (episode)}}</ref>
*[[Phoenix Cell]] is decimated by Thrawn during the [[Battle of Atollon]].<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref><ref name="SWR S3 date" />
*[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and Thrawn investigate a disturbance in the Force and come face-to-face with the [[Grysk Hegemony]], which threatens to bring the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] into civil war. Due to his duty to the Empire, Thrawn is unable to return home to help his people.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />
*Darth Vader and [[Governor]] [[Ahr]] lead a [[Pacification of Ahr's sector|pacification effort]] in a [[Esuain sector|Mid Rim sector]], where Vader is made to obey Ahr's commands by the Emperor as a lesson. After Ahr repeatedly toys with Vader, the Sith Lord manipulates his wording to destroy the biggest threat he can find as a way to kill Ahr, much to the Emperor's joy.<ref name="Darth Vader" /> {{Circa}}<ref name="Age of Vader date" />
