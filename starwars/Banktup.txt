'''Banktup''' was a [[Banchiian]].<ref name="Premonition" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|EOBP}}</ref> they told [[Jedi Knight]] [[Lily Tora-Asi]] about a [[Unidentified Banchiian village|Banchiian village]] where the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Nima Allets]] and [[Viv'Nia Nia'Viv]] could be protected. Tora-Asi relayed the information to [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], who safely delivered the [[youngling]]s to the [[City|village]].<ref name="Premonition" />

==Behind the scenes==
Banktup first appeared in the [[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 3|third volume]] of the [[Wikipedia:Manga|manga]] series ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Edge of Balance]]''. The volume was written by [[Shima Shinya]] and [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Mizuki Sakakibara]], and published by [[Viz Media]]<ref name="Edge of Balance 3">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 3|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 3]]</ref> on [[August 13]], [[2024]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> They were identified in ''[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance: Premonition]]'', another volume of the series. The volume's story was written by Older, its art was created by [[Haining]], and it was published by Viz<ref name="Premonition" /> as part of the ''The High Republic''{{'s}} Phase III on [[April 8]], [[2025]].<ref name="Phase" />
