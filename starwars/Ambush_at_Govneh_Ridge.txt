During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Military|forces]] '''ambushed''' [[New Republic soldier|soldiers]] of the [[New Republic]] at [[Govneh Ridge]] on the [[planet]] [[Nag Ubdur]]. Despite losing a number of [[Gender|men]] and women, the New Republic was able to capture Govneh Ridge.

==Prelude==
During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], the [[planet]] [[Nag Ubdur]] was strip-mined by the [[Galactic Empire]] for [[zersium]], an [[ore]] used in the manufacture of [[durasteel]]. Nag Ubdur was home to the [[Ubdurian]] [[species]] along with many transplanted [[Keldar]] and [[Artiodac]] [[refugee]]s. Following the [[Battle of Endor]], the Empire and the [[New Republic]] [[Battle of Nag Ubdur|fought]] for control of the planet. The Empire was unwilling to yield Nag Ubdur due to its vast zersium deposits.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==The ambush==
The [[New Republic Thirty-First]], a ground unit of the [[New Republic army]], took part in combat operations on Nag Ubdur. One such skirmish took place at [[Govneh Ridge]], a plate shift where the ground bulged regularly. The Ridge was lined will tall [[crystal]]s. [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] used the terrain to ambush the Thirty-First. During the fight, [[Squad leader]] [[Hachinka]] was shot in the neck. [[Gender|Her]] comrades managed to evacuate her for medical treatment. [[New Republic Defense Force|New Republic forces]] eventually managed to secure the ridge but lost many men and women.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Aftermath==
One of the survivors was the [[Kupohan]] New Republic [[Private]] [[Rorith Khadur]], who related his account of the battle to the [[Queen of the Core Network]] [[journalist]] [[Tracene Kane]] and the [[Trandoshan]] camera-operator [[Lug]]. The New Republic Thirty-First later occupied the Ubdurian [[city]] of [[Binjai-Tin]] only to discover that the Empire had [[Massacre at Binjai-Tin|massacred]] the civilian population. Later, Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] launched a [[Death|suicide]] wave attack on New Republic forces occupying the city.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ambush at Govneh Ridge was first mentioned in [[Chuck Wendig]]'s [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', the second installment in [[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy|The Aftermath Trilogy]].
