'''Ashbo''' was the name given to the [[star]] in the [[Atollon system]] by the [[Lasat]], a [[Lasat (language)|Lasat]] word that roughly translated to "steady" or "balanced." The Lasat databases did include the coordinates of the star and few details on [[Atollon]]. The [[Boosahn Keeraw]] attached an emphasized significance to stellar constellations, and included stellar information into their weapons. Because of this, the Lasat who followed traditional methods of navigation by [[bo-rifle]] used Ashbo's constant position.<ref name="Dawn" />
