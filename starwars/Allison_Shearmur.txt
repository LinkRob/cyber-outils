'''Allison Shearmur''' (deceased on [[January 19]], [[2018]]) was an executive producer of the stand-alone ''[[Star Wars]]'' films ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' and ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.

In addition to the ''[[Star Wars Anthology Series]]'', she also produced many other film series, like [[Wikipedia:American Pie (film series)|''American Pie'']], [[Wikipedia:Bourne (film series)|''Bourne'']] and [[Wikipedia:The Hunger Games (film series)|''The Hunger Games'']].
