'''Anselmi throat intonations''' was a type of [[music]] produced by the [[Anselmi]],<ref name="Survivor" /> an aquatic [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Glee Anselm]].<ref name="CYOD">''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure]]''</ref> It could only be performed [[Water|underwater]] and used the low-frequency band as a carrier for [[Ocean|deep-sea]] telemetry.<ref name="Survivor" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Survivor}}
In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> having read about the intonations, the academic [[Toa]] requested that the music producer [[Ashe Javi]] have the [[DJ]] [[droid]] [[DD-EC]] play Anselmi throat intonations at the [[cantina]] [[Pyloon's Saloon]] on the planet [[Koboh]], suggesting that they could decode the subaqueous music in [[atmosphere]]. Javi was familiar with the music genre and considered it a deep cut, but refused the request, telling Toa that the other patrons would not appreciate the intonations.<ref name="Survivor" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Anselmi throat intonations were mentioned in an optional conversation in the [[2023]] [[Respawn Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Survivor" />
