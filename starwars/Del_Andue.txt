The '''Del Andue''' were a strange-looking [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]]. In [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]], the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] spent some [[Time/Legends|time]] living in a hovel on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] with two Del Andues while he made preparations to rescue his friend [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] from the clutches of the [[Crime lord/Legends|crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba the Hutt]].

==Behind the scenes==
The Del Andues featured in rough draft of the script of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], the final installment of the [[Original trilogy]], and were set to appear in the opening scenes of the film. In the revised rough draft version of the script, the Del Andues were replaced by the [[Jawa/Legends|Jawas]] [[Tweetu]] and [[Atetu]].
