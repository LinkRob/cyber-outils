The [[planet]] [[Malastare]], [[homeworld]] of the [[Dug]] [[species]], was '''colonized''' by the [[Gran]] [[Standard century|hundreds of years]] prior to the [[Imperial Era]] when the [[Galactic Republic]] was considered as the "Old Republic." The Gran colonized the planet as they [[Gran expansion|expanded]] across [[the galaxy]], with the Dugs violently resisting the colonization attempt. Despite the superiority of Gran technology, the Dugs were resourceful and relentless, catching the Gran colonists unprepared. The war's escalation resulted in an intervention by the Republic, and due to the Gran's influence in the Galactic Republic government, were effectively awarded the planet. The Republic enabled the [[Gran Protectorate Act]], which enabled the colonists to take over the eastern continent of Malastare and relocated most of the Dugs to the western continent. As a result of that injustice, many Dugs grew extremely [[Xenophobia|xenophobic]].<ref name="Special Mods" />

By the time shortly prior to the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Gran had established the [[Gran Protectorate]]<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> and the [[Congress of Malastare]].<ref name="Episode II" />
