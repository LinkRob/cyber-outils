'''Fifth Fleet''' can refer to:

==Canon==
*'''[[Fifth Fleet]]'''&mdash; a numbered [[Galactic Republic Navy]] [[fleet]] that participated in the [[Clone Wars]] as part of the [[Open Circle Fleet|Open Circle Armada]].

*'''[[Imperial Fifth Fleet]]'''&mdash; a fleet in the [[Imperial Navy (Sith Empire)|Imperial Navy]] of the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|reconstituted Sith Empire]] commanded by [[Grand Moff/Legends|Grand Moff]] [[Rycus Kilran]].
*'''[[5th Fleet Security]]'''&mdash; also known as the '''Fifth Fleet Security''', a [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] [[Law enforcement agency/Legends|security force]].
*'''[[Fifth Fleet (Ai-Ari Kuldi)|Fifth Fleet]]'''&mdash; a fleet led by [[Ai-Ari Kuldi]].
*'''[[Fifth Fleet of the Open Circle Fleet]]'''&mdash; a major fleet of the [[Open Circle Fleet/Legends|Open Circle Armada]] that participated in the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].
*'''[[New Republic Fifth Fleet]]'''&mdash; a [[Combined Defense Forces of the New Republic]] fleet.
*'''[[Galactic Alliance Fifth Fleet]]'''&mdash; a fleet of the [[Galactic Federation of Free Alliances]].
