'''''Forces of Destiny: Daring Adventures: Volumes 1 & 2''''' is the unabridged audio version of the [[canon]] young-reader books ''[[Forces of Destiny: Daring Adventures: Volume 1]]'' and ''[[Forces of Destiny: Daring Adventures: Volume 2]]'', written by [[Emma Carlson Berne]]. The audiobook was performed by [[January LaVoy]] and was released by [[Blackstone Audio|Blackstone Publishing]] on [[December 15]], [[2017]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Based on the all-new animated micro-series spotlighting some of Star Wars’ greatest heroes, these six exciting adventures feature the heroic Rey, Sabine Wren of Star Wars Rebels, Senator Padmé Amidala, rebel leader Princess Leia, Rogue One’s Jyn Erso, and Jedi apprentice Ahsoka Tano! Includes introductions by Maz Kanata.''<ref name="Blackstone Audio" />
