A '''beastmaster''' was an individual who tamed and trained [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[species]]. The [[human]] [[Malakili]] worked as a beastmaster in [[Hutt]] [[circus]]es and in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]].<ref name="LD" /> A [[rancor keeper]] was a type of beastmaster.<ref name="Rancor Keeper">{{Databank|rancor-keeper|Rancor Keeper}}</ref>
