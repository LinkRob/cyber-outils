An '''antigrav bracket''' was a piece of [[antigrav]] technology that could be used to support shelving. When a group led by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] visited the [[junk dealer]] [[Watto]]'s [[Watto's junkshop|junkstore]] in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="CL" /> in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> Watto had an antigrav bracket holding up his trophy shelf of [[podracing]] memorabilia.<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], an antigrav bracket was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="CL" /> from the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]], illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]], and marked the first mention of the [[Antigrav bracket/Legends|bracket]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
