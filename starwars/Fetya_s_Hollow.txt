'''Fetya's Hollow''' was a town located on [[Arvina]], where the [[Besalisk]] [[arms dealer]] [[Fetya]] resided during the [[Imperial Era]]. The [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]] and his [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] visited the town during their [[Hunt for Vader|hunt]] for the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], seeking to purchase weapons from Fetya. When Fetya and her associates turned on Valance's crew, the latter escaped the world when the [[Galactic Empire]] arrived and began attacking the town.

==Description==
Located on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] world of [[Arvina]], Fetya's Hollow was a desolate town with at least one firearms store run by the [[Besalisk]] [[arms dealer]] [[Fetya]].<ref name="Target Vader 2" />

==History==
During the [[Imperial Era]], Fetya resided in Fetya's Hollow, selling arms from the area. During their [[Hunt for Vader|hunt]] for the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]] and his [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] of [[bounty hunter]]s travelled to Fetya's Follow and visited Fetya, looking to purchase [[sonic cannon]]s from her. The deal went sour when she turned on Valance and his team and intended to kill them as the cyborg had previously failed to complete a job with the Besalisk in the past.<ref name="Target Vader 2" />

[[File:ValanceEscapeArvina-TargetVader2.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Valance and his team escape Fetya's Hollow in the ensuing chaos.]]
However, Valance had allowed the [[Galactic Empire]] to track them down, and Vader immediately arrived in an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]], which razed the town completely. Valance and his crew then fought their way through Fetya's associates in the ensuing chaos and escaped the world aboard the cyborg's [[starship]], the ''[[Broken Wing]]''. Following the skirmish, Vader interrogated Fetya for information on the Hidden Hand and subsequently [[Death|killed]] her.<ref name="Target Vader 2" />
