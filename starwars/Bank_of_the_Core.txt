The '''Bank of the Core''' was a major banking company which existed in [[the galaxy]]. The [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Treasury|treasury]] had worked long in conjunction with the Bank of the Core and the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] to ensure [[currency]] stability.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Bank of the Core was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> The Bank of the Core was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] in ''[[Han Solo and the Corporate Sector Sourcebook]]'', a [[1993]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] guide published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Han Solo">''[[Han Solo and the Corporate Sector Sourcebook]]''</ref>
