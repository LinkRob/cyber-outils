A '''Coruscanti necklace''' was a type of [[necklace]] from<ref name="FACPOV" /> the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Each of the piece of jewellery's beads or [[Gemstone|gems]] was individually knotted in place so that any section could break without it ruining the whole necklace. When designing the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]], [[Moff]] [[Tiaan Jerjerrod]] built in failsafes and choke-points that gave the [[battle station]] the elegance of a [[Coruscanti]] necklace and ensured that it would not suffer a catastrophic chain reaction similar to the one that had [[Battle of Yavin|destroyed]] the original [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="FACPOV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coruscanti necklaces were mentioned in "[[Any Work Worth Doing]]," a short story written by [[Amal El-Mohtar]] and published in the [[2023]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="FACPOV" />
