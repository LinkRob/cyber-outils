'''Directive 081-Omega''' was a directive of the [[Galactic Empire]], which allowed individuals of the highest authority to access and alter Imperial records. [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] held the authority to utilize Directive 081-Omega, and did so to erase information on the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] from the Imperial [[Inspectorate]] [[databank]]s. When attempting to find information on Aphra, [[Lieutenant-Inspector]] [[Magna Tolvan]] instructed an [[Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Inspectorate)|RA-7 protocol droid]] to search the Inspectorate databanks, but discovered that the file's contents had been deleted using Directive 081-Omega.<ref name="Aphra 15" />
