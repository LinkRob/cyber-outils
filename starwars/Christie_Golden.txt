'''Christie Golden''' is an award-winning author of many science fiction and fantasy novels, and is one of the authors for the [[Star Wars: Fate of the Jedi|''Fate of the Jedi'']] series.<ref name="DelRey">{{SWArchive|url=vault/books/news20080912.html|text=Del Rey ''Star Wars'' books continue through 2013}}</ref> She also wrote the [[canon]] novels ''[[Dark Disciple]]'' and ''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''.

Outside of ''Star Wars,'' she has written books for ''World of Warcraft, StarCraft, Assassin's Creed, Star Trek: Voyager,'' ''HEX: Shards of Fate,'' and the ''Ravenloft'' and ''Forgotten Realms'' campaign settings of ''Dungeons & Dragons.'' She also wrote the novelizations for the film adaptations of ''Warcraft, Assassin's Creed,'' and ''Valerian & The City of A Thousand Planets.'' She also wrote her own book series ''Invasion America'' and standalone novels including ''Instruments of Fate, King's Man and Thief,'' and ''A.D. 999.''<ref name="Website bibliography">{{WebCite|url=http://www.christiegolden.com/bibliography.html|text=Christie Golden bibliography|archivedate=20150923203048}}</ref>

==Biography==
Christie Golden was born on [[November 21]], [[1960s|1963]] in Atlanta, Georgia.<ref name="Contemporary Authors" /> At thirteen years of age, Golden would spend entire afternoons watching [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="anewhope" />

Golden worked as one of the three authors in the nine-book [[Star Wars: Fate of the Jedi|''Fate of the Jedi'']] series. Her first entry into both the series and into the [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] in general, ''[[Fate of the Jedi: Omen]]'', reached #4 on the New York Times bestseller list and #3 on the Publisher's Weekly bestseller list.<ref name="stillhere">{{WordPress|subdomain=christiegolden|url=2009/07/11/im-still-here|name=Musings and Meanderings|text=I'm still here!|author=Christie Golden|date=2009-07-11|archivedate=20170202121148}}</ref> Golden also wrote a short story prequel to ''[[Omen]]'' that focuses on [[Vestara Khai]]. The short story was exclusive to [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] members, and is titled ''[[Imprint]]''.

Golden was to write a ''Star Wars'' trilogy focusing on [[Jaina Solo Fel]] and her husband [[Jagged Fel]] called [[Sword of the Jedi (trilogy)|''Sword of the Jedi'']].<ref name="SWCVI">{{SWArchive|subdomain=starwarsblog|url=index.php/2012/08/24/swcvidel-rey%E2%80%99s-star-wars-expanded-universe|text=SWCVI: Del Rey's Star Wars Expanded Universe}}</ref> The series was canceled indefinitely due to the announcement from Disney Publishing Worldwide and Random House about the relaunch of the ''Star Wars'' adult novel continuity.<ref>{{SWArchive|url=news/disney-publishing-worldwide-and-random-house-announce-relaunch-of-star-wars-adult-fiction-line.html|text=Disney Publishing Worldwide and Random House Announce Relaunch of Star Wars Adult Fiction Line}}</ref><ref>{{SWArchive|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page.html|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> Following the reorganization of the [[canon]] structure under Disney, Golden returned to write the novel ''[[Dark Disciple]]'', a story focused on [[Quinlan Vos]] and [[Asajj Ventress]] towards the end of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="AmazonDD">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/7868/dark-disciple-star-wars-by-christie-golden|text=Dark Disciple: Star Wars}}</ref>
