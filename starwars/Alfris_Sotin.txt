'''Alfris Sotin''' was a [[Fiumarian]] [[Gender|male]] [[smuggler]] and [[Slavery|slaver]]. Sotin offered a deal to the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] on [[Ankot Station]], but instead [[Skirmish at Ankot Station|betrayed]] them to the [[Guavian Death Gang]].<ref name="Free Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alfris Sotin first appeared in the [[canon]] young-adult novel ''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]'', which was authored by [[Alex Segura]]<ref name="Free Fall" /> and published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[August 4]], [[2020]].<ref name="Free Fall Amazon">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/poe-dameron-free-fall|text=Poe Dameron: Free Fall}}</ref> The Fiumarian was later depicted in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] one-shot [[Return of the Jedi – The Rebellion 1|''Return of the Jedi – The Rebellion'' 1]], which was written by Segura, illustrated by [[Matt Horak]], [[Brent Peeples]], and [[Rafael Pimentel]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[July 19]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103246/star_wars_return_of_the_jedi_-_the_rebellion_1_2023_1|text=Star Wars: Return Of The Jedi &ndash; The Rebellion (2023) #1}}</ref>
