{{Quote|I knew keeping them in the open would draw you out!|Fife to Ahsoka Tano, upon capturing her|<ref name="Together Again" />}}
'''Fife''' was a [[Pyke]] [[Gender|male]] who served as the [[majordomo]] of [[Marg Krim]] and was a member of the [[Pyke Syndicate]]<ref name="DarkDisciple" /> and [[Shadow Collective]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Two" />

By the [[Imperial Era]], Fife still served the Pykes and made dealings with the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] but it ended badly.<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters" />

==History==
===Aiding Krim===
{{Quote|Fife. He's Marg Krim's majordomo. I've worked with him before. Usually he thinks he's more than enough to handle a situation.|[[Asajj Ventress]] tells Quinlan Vos about Fife|<ref name="DarkDisciple" />}}
[[File:Darkdisciple.png|thumb|left|230px|Fife meet with Ventress and Vos who hired them in a desperate gambit to rescue his family.]]
After [[Lom Pyke]]'s death,<ref name="TLO">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref> Marg Krim was chosen to be his successor,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> with Krim choosing Fife as his majordomo. He was acquainted with [[Asajj Ventress]] during her bounty hunting career.<ref name="DarkDisciple" /> After Maul's capture on [[Mandalore]] by his former [[Sith Master]], [[Darth Sidious]],<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref> [[Ziton Moj]], leader of the [[Black Sun]], approached Krim with an offer to merge their criminal organizations. Krim rejected the proposal, unwilling to lose control of the [[Pyke Syndicate]] and determined to protect his operations. In response, Moj kidnapped Krim's wife, [[Tezzka Krim|Tezzka]], and their children, [[Vram (Pyke)|Vram]] and [[Laalee]], imprisoning them within the [[Black Sun fortress]] on [[Mustafar]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Distrustful of his own subordinates, whom he believed viewed him as weak, Krim hired the bounty hunters Asajj Ventress and the undercover [[Jedi Master]] [[Quinlan Vos]] to rescue his family. After learning of Krim's involvement, Moj ordered his forces to pursue the bounty hunters back to the Pyke stronghold on [[Oba Diah]], dispatching fleets of [[starfighter]]s and [[Interceptor IV-class frigate|''Interceptor''-class frigates]] to punish Krim.<ref name="DarkDisciple" /> 

===Shadow Collective dealings===
{{Quote|<nowiki></nowiki>''You're'' the warrior. We're mercenaries&mdash;and there's no more profit in this fight.|Fife, to [[Rook Kast]]|<ref name="Son 4">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 4|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 4]]</ref>}}
[[File:Darth maul speaks with fife and ziton moj.png|thumb|right|230px|Fife meet with the rest of the Shadow Collective]]
After Maul was freed by his commandos, Krim sent Fife to [[Ord Mantell]] to meet with the other leaders of the [[Shadow Collective]]. Fife subsequently participated in the [[Battle on Ord Mantell]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Two" />

[[File:KastMojFifeHolo-SoD-4.png|thumb|left|230px|Facing heavy losses, both Fife and Ziton agreed to withdraw from the Shadow Collective.]]
Following heavy casualties within the Shadow Collective, Fife and Ziton Moj contacted Maul after the [[Assault on Vizsla Keep 09]], expressing concern over the mounting losses suffered in Maul's war against the Sith. Maul reassured them that their organizations would be compensated and promised that the galaxy would belong to them once Darth Sidious was dead. During the [[Battle of Dathomir]], Fife and Moj contacted Maul's [[Mandalorian]] [[lieutenant]], [[Rook Kast]], to report that the Separatists were launching coordinated attacks against Shadow Collective strongholds. They demanded to speak directly with Maul, but Kast refused and ordered them to continue resisting the Separatists. After suffering severe losses, both Fife and Moj withdrew their respective syndicates from the Shadow Collective, leaving Maul and his [[Mandalorian super commando]]s to continue the conflict alone.<ref name="Son 4" />

===Delayed Spice===
{{Quote|This is another deception."''<br />"''I know, but I don't have much of a choice. I must regain that spice.My life depends on it as much as theirs."''<br />"''You think he will find out about this?"''<br />"''He knows. He always knows.|Fife and Krim|<ref name="Son 4" />}}
[[File:PykeCheckingSpiceDND.png|thumb|right|230px|Suspisious of Krim's behavior, Fife checked the cargo and captured the smugglers.]]
At some point the Pyke Syndicate rejoined the Shadow Collective and made a deal with King [[Yaruba]] of the [[Yaruba Family of Kessel]] with his majordomo [[Kinash Lock]], negotiated a deal with [[Rafa Martez]] to deliver spice to the Pyke Syndicate on Oba Diah. Rafa, along with [[Ahsoka Tano]] and her sister [[Trace Martez]] met by Marg Krim, Fife, and a squad of guards. Unbeknownst to Krim, the trio had ejected their cargo and planned to swindle the former using Tano's Jedi [[mind trick]], taking the payment from Fife. However, Fife grew suspicious of Krim's odd behavior, finding it highly irregular, and with Krim's permission he had the cargo checked before the girls left. Upon seeing the cargo crates empty, he ordered several [[Sarisa-class gunship|Pyke patrol ships]] through his [[wrist comm]] to catch the trio's ship ''[[Silver Angel]]'' in a [[tractor beam]] before it could escape.<ref name="Deal No Deal">{{TCW|Deal No Deal}}</ref>

[[File:Ahsoka-rescues-the-Martez-sisters.png|thumb|left|230px|Knowing the third girl was still at large, Fife set a trap to capture all three of them.]]
After being captured by the Pykes, the trio were briefly imprisoned, but Tano discretely used [[the Force]] to deactivate the cell's security and help the sisters escape. The three of them managed to take out a few more Pyke guards before Trace and Rafa were finally found by Fife and a few other guards. Knowing Tano was still at large, he had the girls in the opening to lure Tano out. Upon capturing them, Fife opened a transmission to Krim, who asked them if they thought they would get away with fooling a Pyke. Rafa attempted to bargain for her and her sister's lives by reminding Krim of the spice's location. But Krim responded by saying that he would torture their friend for the information. Fife suggested to kill the girls and be done with it to move onto the third one, which Krim then ordered Fife to execute the sisters before ending the transmission, which Tano showed up in a hijacked [[MSP80 Pteropter hover pod]]. Despite the chaos, Fife manage to get back up and pass the gates to get more reinforcements, which their [[Flash speeder]]'s [[laser cannon]] destroyed the stolen pod.<ref name="Together Again" />

Back in captivity, Krim gave Rafa an ultimatum; pay her debt or watch her friends die slowly. However, Tano then cut in and offered to make a deal with Krim, feigning ambivalence towards Trace and Rafa's fates. Krim asked her once more for the location of the spice and Ahsoka responded that she didn't know where it was; only that the sisters had hidden it off-world. She then told Krim to give them one rotation to return the spice. If they didn't, she would inform Krim of their family's location. Krim asked Ahsoka what she would get out of this, to which Ahsoka said that she simply wanted her percentage.<ref name="Together Again">{{TCW|Together Again}}</ref>

After Krim agreed to the terms with the sisters left and Tano was imprisoned, Fife told Krim that the girls were playing another deception, which Krim revealed that he's aware of it, but that he also didn't have much of a choice, since his life depended on regaining that spice as much as theirs. Fife further questioned Krim if he thought their master would find out about the delay, to which Krim responded that he knows since he always knows.<ref name="Together Again" />

[[File:MaulThreatensKrim-TA.png|thumb|right|300px|Fife was present when Maul warning Krim of his failures.]]
Afterwards, Krim was contacted by Maul, whose Shadow Collective the Pyke Syndicate still served. Krim insisted that the current delay would not change things and that their operations would proceed as planned. Maul then reminded Krim that he was but one small piece in the mechanism he designed and when he faltered, it jeopardized everything, which Krim tells Maul that the Republic is cracking down on his operations since a week ago they raided one of his shipments coming to [[Coruscant]] and none of his men made it out, which Maul assumes the Jedi were involved. Sensing Tano's presence, Maul didn't warn Krim of her presence but stated that he had more important things to attend to other than the Pyke's incompetence. He then told Krim to make sure there were no more delays. Though if there were, then he was certain [[Crimson Dawn]] would love the opportunity to take control of his operation.<ref name="Together Again" />

Once the transmission ended, Fife told Krim that Maul played members of the Collective against one another, to which Krim conceded that this was an efficient way of dealing with their kind of business. Fife expressed certainty that Maul knew of the Pykes' unwillingness to jump to his commands. Though Krim then asked Fife if he would like to make that clear to him to which the latter shook his head in fear. A guard then showed up and informed both Fife and Krim that Tano has escaped.<ref name="Together Again" />

Fife, Krim, and three guard found Tano at the control room where she found out that through their transmission log, Maul was on Mandalore. As Tano fought the guards off, revealing she was a Jedi. As more Pyke guards come in to restrain her with Krim ordering one guards to [[stun]] her.<ref name="Together Again" />

At the throne room, Krim was pleased of Tano's timing since it will help restore the Pykes' reputation, with Trace and Rafa returning with the spice, they recently stole from the Pykes. Before executing the girls, Tano activated a series of [[magnetic thermal detonator]]s she had previously planted throughout the Pyke Palace from their armory. While Krim and his guards, including Fife, were stunned by the explosions, the trio managed to escape from the palace. Krim ordered his guards to capture them or they would all suffer the consequences. Though unfortunately for Krim and Fife, Tano and the sisters would evade capture one last time before finally leaving Oba Diah, placing the Pykes in Maul's wrath.<ref name="Together Again" />

Shortly after the [[Siege of Mandalore]], Krim attempted to betray Maul, when he approached his old ally for safe haven, but Krim failed to kill Maul. Needless two years after the Empire formed, Maul came to Oba Diah and killed Krim for his betrayal, appointing his [[Captain of the Guard]] [[Kalt]] as the new public figure of the Pyke Syndicate.<ref name="Chapter 4">{{MSL|Chapter 4: Pride and Vengeance}}</ref>

===Imperial Era===
At some point during the [[Imperial Era]], Fife was still active and continued serving the Pykes Syndicate. He hired the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] and his shipping company, [[Ohnaka Transport Solutions]], to transport raw spice to Coruscant. However, they loaded [[coaxium]] instead of spice, and by delivering the wrong cargo, making their contract voided.<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|This is highly irregular. I am going to make sure everything sorts out to our liking.|Fife to Krim|<ref name="Deal No Deal" />}}
[[File:TogetherMargKrimFifeDebate.png|thumb|left|300px|Loyal to Krim, Fife often voiced his concerns to his leader.]]
Fife was a [[Gender|male]]<ref name="DarkDisciple" /> [[Pyke]]<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Two" /> who was 1.91 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]], 3 inches) tall,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> with [[Color|magenta]] [[eye]]s<ref name="DarkDisciple" /> and gray skin.<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Two" /> Unlike many of his fellow syndicate members, Fife was not weak-willed and thus unable to be properly [[mind trick]]ed.<ref name="Deal No Deal Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=deal-no-deal-episode-gallery|text="Deal No Deal" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 11}}</ref> He was very loyal to Marg Krim<ref name="DarkDisciple" /> but also voiced his concerns with Krim.<ref name="Together Again" />

==Skills and abilities==
As Marg Krim's majordomo, Fife was a highly capable warrior, who was very effective at combat, military tactics, and security.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> He skills as a warrior enabled him to fight with the forces of the Shadow Collective on Ord Mantell<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Two" /> and later enabled him to outmaneuver [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Trace Martez]], and [[Rafa Martez]] as the trio attempted to flee [[Oba Diah]], keeping two of the girls in the opening so Tano can come back and save them. Even when Tano hijacked a MSP80 Pteropter hover pod, Fife manage to shoot the pod down with a Flash speeder.<ref name="Dangerous Debt">{{TCW|Dangerous Debt}}</ref> Fife was also skilled in organization and bureaucracy.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
[[File:Discretion-SWQ04-RomainKurdi.png|thumb|270px|right|Pyke foot soldiers in battle, led by Fife wielding a P13-M Disruptor.]]
Like many Pykes, Fife was armed with a [[CC-420 pistol]].<ref name="Deal No Deal">{{TCW|Deal No Deal}}</ref> He had an [[imagecaster]].<ref name="Dangerous Debt" /> He also was capable with the [[P13-M Disruptor|P13-M Disruptor rifle]].<ref name="SWQ04">{{NewLegion|set=Mercenary Card Pack|cardname=Discretion}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fife first appeared in ''[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 2]]'', the second issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir]]''.<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Two" /> He later made his on screen appearance during the ''[[The Clone Wars: Season Seven]]'' episode "[[Deal No Deal]]," where he was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="Deal No Deal" />
