The '''Coruscant League''' was a [[grav-ball]] league on the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Coruscant DB">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref> [[Coruscant]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Galactic Empire]] used grav-ball in its [[propaganda]] and broadcasts of tournaments were often accompanied by recruitment messages featuring popular players. [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] sometimes attended games on Coruscant. The file ''[[Grav-ball's Greatest Games: Coruscant League]]'' had information about the Coruscant League, which the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] referenced when<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> he<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> mentioned the league in his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> in [[35 ABY]]<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> In the book, Kin noted that while Tarkin had attended the matches, it was unclear if he actually had any interest in grav-ball.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Coruscant League was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise and Fall" />
