The '''CloakShape fighter''' was a type of [[starfighter]] in use during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Nakari Kelen]] were attacked by [[pirate]]s in CloakShapes while flying the ''[[Desert Jewel]]'' though [[Hutt Space]]. The pirates, flying three CloakShapes and a number of [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]]s, attacked after the ''Jewel'' dropped out of [[hyperspace]] in the [[Nanth'ri system]].<ref name="HTTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the CloakShape fighter first appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Heir to the Jedi]]'', by author [[Kevin Hearne]].<ref name="HTTJ" />

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the CloakShape first appeared in the [[1992]] comic issue [[Dark Empire 3|''Dark Empire'' 3]], the third issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Empire]]''series of comics.<ref name="Dark Empire 3">[[Dark Empire 3|''Dark Empire'' 3]]</ref>{{Ship-stub}}
