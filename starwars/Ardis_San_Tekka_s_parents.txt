{{Quote|My love of wildlife developed at a young age. As a child, I traveled with my family across the galaxy, continuing the San Tekka clan's legacy of locating and mapping out hyperspace lanes. While my parents worked, I'd sneak off to explore whatever planet we'd landed on, searching for new-to-me creatures.|Ardis San Tekka, in her journal|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
At least two '''individuals''' were members of the [[San Tekka clan]] and at one point had a [[Youngling|child]]&mdash;[[Ardis San Tekka]]. While Ardis was a child, she and her [[parent]]s traveled across [[the galaxy]] so they could locate and [[Cartographer|map]] [[hyperspace route]]s, continuing the family's legacy. While her parents worked after they landed on [[planet]]s, Ardis snuck off to explore and search for new creatures. Sometime later, Ardis discovered her first [[hyperspace]] lane and, in keeping with San Tekka tradition, her parents gave her a [[gundark]] [[belt]].<ref name="Bestiary" />

While Ardis served as [[Director of Creature Cartography]] in the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]]<ref name="Bestiary" /> no earlier than [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BestiaryVol1Date}}</ref> she and her [[safari droid]] [[B8-T5]] "Bait" compiled a [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]] about various [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[species]] they had encountered together. In the [[Book|journal's]] introduction, Ardis mentioned her travels with her parents, noting it had resulted in her love of wildlife. Within an entry about gundarks later in the journal, Bait recalled Ardis' being given the belt by her parents.<ref name="Bestiary" />

==Behind the scenes==
