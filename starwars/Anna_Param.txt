'''Anna Param''' was a [[human]] [[technician]] who served on board the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]<ref name="Double" /> in [[4 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> during its construction over the [[forest]] [[moon]] [[Endor]]. Param heard rumors from an individual named [[Aklin]] that when [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] arrived to the Death Star II, his collection of [[Sith]] relics brought aboard included a [[unidentified ring|powerful ring]] that could turn [[death|corpses]] into worker drones. She told this rumor to fellow technicians [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Tril Jindrax]], who managed to [[thief|steal]] the ring from Palpatine's [[Sith acolyte]]s in order to meet the demanding timelines imposed by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] high command.<ref name="Double" />

Param was against Jindrax's irresponsible plan, and told the lieutenant commander multiple times to stop. Eventually, her frustration mounted to a point in which she threatened to expose Jindrax's ploy to [[Moff]] [[Tiaan Jerjerrod]] or [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. In response, Jindrax gleefully called on his aggressive army of the dead to slaughter Param, then reanimated her to join them.<ref name="Double" />

When Jindrax lost control over the actions of the undead, Param's reanimated form focused on attacking the technician.<ref name="Double" />
