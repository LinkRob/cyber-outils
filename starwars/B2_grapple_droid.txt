The '''B2 grapple droid''' was a variant of the [[B2-series super battle droid]] model which was designed for close-range combat.<ref name="Dathomir 2" /> They served as a successor to the [[B1 grapple droid]].<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> The B2 grapple droid was one of the droids that participated in the [[Battle on Ord Mantell|Battle of Ord Mantell]]. One unit discovered [[Gar Saxon]], a member of [[Black Sun]], and a [[Pyke]] hiding behind cover during the battle and punched through to their position, grabbing the Pyke and pulling him through the wall.<ref name="Dathomir 2" />
