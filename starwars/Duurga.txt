'''Duurga''' was a [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] of the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] [[Durga Besadii Tai]], created by [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]] between [[11 ABY/Legends|11 ABY]] and [[25 ABY/Legends|25 ABY]]. Thrawn enjoyed arranging arm-wrestling competitions between Duurga and the clone [[Chewbaacca]], which ended after Chewbaacca tore out one of Duurga's [[Limb/Legends|arms]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Why Thrawn keeps Duurga around I don't know. Still, there's a marsh behind the king's palace, and Hutts do tend to keep down the frog population.|Luuke Skywalker, speculating on Thrawn's purpose for Duurga|<ref name="Apology" />}}
[[File:Durga-NEGTC.png|thumb|right|250px|Duurga was a clone of the Hutt Durga Besadii Tai (pictured).]]
Duurga was a [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] created from genetic samples of the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]]<ref name="Apology" /> [[Crime lord/Legends|crime lord]]<ref name="Darksaber">[[Darksaber (novel)|''Darksaber'']]</ref> [[Durga Besadii Tai]] that were collected by the clone [[Luuke Skywalker]]<ref name="Apology" /> between [[11 ABY/Legends|11 ABY]] and [[25 ABY/Legends|25 ABY]].<ref name="NEC" /> Following Skywalker's inability to retrieve a biological sample of the ghost [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Exar Kun/Legends|Exar Kun]] for his master, [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]], he successfully tried to appease Thrawn by giving him a sample of Durga, which Thrawn used to create Duurga. Skywalker did not understand Thrawn's purpose in keeping Duurga but suspected that the Hutt clone was useful for controlling the [[Frog/Legends|frog]] population in the marsh behind Thrawn's [[Roberts' palace|palace]].<ref name="Apology" />

During or after 25 [[Galactic Standard Calendar|ABY]],<ref name="NEC" /> Thrawn created a clone of the [[Destruction of Sernpidal|deceased]] [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]], named [[Chewbaacca]], whom he enjoyed pitting in arm-wrestling contests with Duurga. Eventually, Chewbaacca pulled one of Duurga's [[Limb/Legends|arms]] out, which led to Thrawn ending the competitions. The Grand Admiral also created another clone, [[Duuurga]], near whom he refused to let Chewbaacca go.<ref name="Apology" />

==Personality and traits==
As a clone of the original Durga, Duurga measured 3.7 [[Meter/Legends|meters]] long, had orange [[Eye/Legends|eyes]], and yellow skin.<ref name="NEGTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Duurga was mentioned in "[[An Apology]]," a non-[[canon]] short story written by [[Timothy Zahn]] that was released online on [[Unbound Worlds|Suvudu]] on [[April 1]], [[2012]] as an [[Wikipedia:April Fools' Day|April Fools' Day]] joke.<ref name="Apology" />
