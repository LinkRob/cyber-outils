The '''Bantha Set''' was the name given to a set of two weapons, an [[Arc Caster]] and a [[Pulse Blaster]], utilized by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Set|standard set]]. These sandy weathered [[weapon]]s featured a [[Color|black]] and brown color scheme, with a yellow tube protruding from an Arc Caster, and both [[weapon]]s were wrapped in ornate elements including vibrant cords and cloth, dark [[Hair|fur]], and tan string.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bantha Set was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Utooni's Suspicious Set, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.18.0 during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Bantha Brothers]].<ref name="Hunters" />
