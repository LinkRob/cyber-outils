==Behind the scenes==
The chase through Mos Espa first appeared in "[[Chapter 3: The Streets of Mos Espa]]," the third episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'' television series.<ref name="Chapter 3" /> During the chase, some workers were carrying a [[painting]] which is actually concept art of [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba the Hutt's Palace]] that was created for the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'' Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] by artist [[Ralph McQuarrie]].<ref name="Easter eggs">{{D23|url=did-you-spot-these-details-and-easter-eggs-in-the-book-of-boba-fett|text=Did you Spot These Details and Easter Eggs in ''The Book of Boba Fett''?}}</ref> Also, the wooden infrastructure of the Mos Espa set can be seen as Skad rounds a corner while on his [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Chapter 3" />
