'''Center One''', also known as '''Center 1''', was a reeducation facility constructed by [[Galactic Empire]]. Located on a [[Unidentified planet (Center One)|planet]], Center One was meant to detain [[Politics|political]] [[prisoner]]s and realign their beliefs. The size of a [[city]], the gargantuan facility was under construction&mdash;one of five total facilities being built<ref name="MOF" />&mdash;by around [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|61}}</ref> laid out in a way that was formulaic, designed for uniformity and coercion. [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] showed [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] the facility after Mothma's [[Imperial Rebirth Act]], which attempted to limit the Empire's power, passed in the [[Imperial Senate]]. A short time later, [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]], namely the rebels [[Karama]] and [[Nankry]], visited the planet and planted [[bomb]]s at the construction site, [[Attack on Center One|destroying]] Center One. In response, the Empire framed the attack as being on a refueling station, using [[propaganda]] to further the narrative that [[Onderonian]] terrorists were an ongoing threat to [[the galaxy]].<ref name="MOF" />
