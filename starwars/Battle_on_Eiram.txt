{{Quote|There was a battle, on Eiram. I was sent in as part of a contingent assisting the crown forces. We were to storm a munitions plant, or that's what we where briefed. The first wave blew the walls and we went in.|Bokana Koss, to [[Marda Ro]].|<ref name="PoV" />}}
Between [[387 BBY]] and [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]], a '''battle''' took place on the [[planet]] [[Eiram]]. A [[Unidentified E'roni squad|squad]] of E'roni soldiers were briefed that their assignment was to take a munitions plant, but in reality, the plant was a [[Unidentified refugee shelter|refugee shelter]] filled with [[refugee]]s. Confronted with the reality of the assault, [[Bokana Koss]] turned and fled, while the rest of the squad kept firing. When Koss reached an [[Unidentified E'roni commander (Bokana Koss)|E'roni commander]], the commander told Koss to keep on fighting, calling him a deserter. When the commander pulled his [[blaster]] on Koss, Koss shot first and fled Eiram to join the [[Path of the Open Hand]].<ref name="PoV" />
