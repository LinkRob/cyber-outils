'''Death Bar Two''' was a [[Cantina|bar]] in the southern hemisphere of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II]] [[battle station]].<ref name="Twenty and Out" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Imperial weapons technician]] [[Pog Degor]] revealed to fellow [[technician]] [[Corr Lerrann]] that the latter's surprise retirement party would be held at Death Bar Two in two [[Standard day|days]], Degor having already told Lerrann about the surprise a [[Standard week|week]] earlier.<ref name="Twenty and Out" /> However, the [[space station]] and its facilities were destroyed at the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="RotJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> later that day.<ref name="Twenty and Out" />

==Behind the scenes==
Death Bar Two was mentioned in the [[2023]] short story "[[Twenty and Out]]," written by [[Lamar Giles]] and published in the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="Twenty and Out" />
