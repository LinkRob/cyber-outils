The '''Baklek system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square N-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained [[Baklek Base]], a military base of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The system was located near the [[Lansend system]], which was the site of the [[space station]] [[Lansend Twenty-Six]].<ref name="Origin" />
