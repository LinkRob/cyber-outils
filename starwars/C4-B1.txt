'''C4-B1''' was a [[DUM-series pit droid]] with [[Gender|masculine programming]] that worked in a [[galaxy]]-wide [[taxi]] service. On [[Kef Bir]], C4-B1 facilitated the use of a [[sea skiff]] rather than a taxi.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
C4-B1 was created for the [[2022]] non-canon videogame ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''. In the game, the taxi service for which he works serves as the primary method of traveling between different locations on the same [[planet]] for players.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
