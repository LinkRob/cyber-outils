The '''''Dreadstar''''' was a [[Battleship (boat)|battleship]] that [[Rae Sloane]] served aboard. At one point, Sloane [[Fuel|refueled]] the [[Starship|ship]] at a [[Unidentified floating depot|floating depot]] on the [[ninth moon of Tilth]]'s [[Sea of Carawak]]. While at the depot, an oncoming storm made the [[Ocean|sea]] look monstrous.<ref name="LifeDebt" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> the anger of Sloane's superior, [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]], reminded her of the stormy Sea of Carawak she witnessed during her time on the ''Dreadstar''.<ref name="LifeDebt" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Dreadstar'' was mentioned in the [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the second entry in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]].<ref name="LifeDebt" />
