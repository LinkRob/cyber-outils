'''Catapults''' were [[artillery]] weapons used to launch projectiles against enemy [[troop]]s over greater distances.<ref name="Ency">{{SWE|groups|gungans|Gungans}}</ref> In [[32 BBY]], the [[Gungan catapult]] was used against [[Trade Federation]] [[droid]]s during the [[Battle of Naboo]], throwing large [[Booma]]s to deactivate the droids.<ref name="Ep1">{{Film|I}}</ref> Later, catapults were used by the [[Ewok]]s to defend against [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Military|forces]] such as [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-STs]] in the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Ep6">{{Film|VI}}</ref>
