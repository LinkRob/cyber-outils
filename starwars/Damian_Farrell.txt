'''Damian Farrell''' is an Irish puppeteer who worked as a creature and droid performer in the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'' Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. He also played [[Munduri]] in the film. He also played the role of "[[Mimban]] Guard" in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', which was released in [[2018]].<ref name="Credits">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
