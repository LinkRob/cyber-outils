{{Quote|Meanwhile, amid the slightly outdated tendencies of some instructors at the Imperial Academy on Alsakan, Major Urkhaste's lessons on defensive tactics and deployments were apparently legendary.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
The '''Alsakan Academy''' was an [[Imperial Academy]] located on<ref name="Altaya" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Alsakan system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Alsakan]].<ref name="Altaya" /> [[Enric Pryde]], who would go on to become [[Allegiant General]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s successor state, the [[First Order]], attended the Academy at age sixteen<ref name="TROS VD" /> in [[11 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' establishes that [[Enric Pryde]] attended the Alsakan Academy forty-five [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates that battle to [[34 ABY]], Pryde must have attended the academy in [[11 BBY]].</ref> Pryde had put in work to gain acceptance to the prestigious institution, getting in as a [[cadet]]<ref name="FACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=The Steadfast Soldier|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> and receiving advanced placement there.<ref name="TROS VD" /> Attending the Alsakan Academy was part of Pryde's ultimate life plan of controlling his destiny.<ref name="FACPOV" /> Pryde was then assigned to the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Imperial-class)|''Steadfast'']], where he received proper training from an [[Imperial officer]].<ref name="Altaya" />

Lectures from the Alsakan Academy were included in the [[Imperial Archives]]. [[Historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] also covered the academy in<ref name="Fall" /> his<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'',<ref name="Fall" /> published in [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Rise date">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> where he described some faculty's as having been slightly outdated with the exception of [[Major]] [[Urkhaste]], who was well known for his defensive tactics and employments.<ref name="Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Alsakan Academy was first mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
