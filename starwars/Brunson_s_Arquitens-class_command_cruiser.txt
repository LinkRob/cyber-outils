Around [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> an '''[[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]]''' was captained by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[captain]] [[Brunson]]. It was patrolling [[Geonosis]] when it encountered [[Phoenix Cell]], who were in search of [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[Partisans|unit]]. In the following [[Mission to investigate Geonosis|skirmish]], the [[cruiser]] was destroyed by the ''[[Ghost]]''.<ref name="Ghosts" />
