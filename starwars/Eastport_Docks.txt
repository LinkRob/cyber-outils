The '''Eastport Docks''' were a docking area in [[Coruscant Eastern Spaceport|Eastport]],<ref name="Scum & Villainy" /> a [[spaceport]] in the [[Port District (Coruscant)|Port District]] of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]], the [[ecumenopolis]] covering the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Coruscant">{{EncyclopediaCite|Coruscant}}</ref> In [[21 BBY|7956]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]], the [[Coruscant Security Force]] received a tip off from [[Sundari Police Authority]] [[Captain]] [[Patrok Ru-Saxon]] that a [[transport]] belonging to a [[Moogan]] [[Mooga smuggling ring|smuggling ring]] linked to a [[poison]]ing outbreak on the planet [[Mandalore]] had stopped on Coruscant.<ref name="Scum & Villainy" />

This led Coruscant Security Force [[Inspector]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tanivos Divo]] to confiscate a shipment containing several pallets of tainted [[Ardees Beverage|Moogan tea]] from the [[Ardees Beverage Company]] at Eastport Docks. The goods had been destined for the planet's [[Coruscant Underworld|underlevels]] but were intercepted before they could do any harm. Divo's [[Divo family|grandson]], [[Exantor Divo]], later mentioned the docks in his [[book]], ''[[Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'',<ref name="Scum & Villainy" /> which was published in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{ScumAndVillainyDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Eastport Docks were mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Scum & Villainy" />
