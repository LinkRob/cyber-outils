'''Feenix''' was located in the [[D'Aelgoth sector]]'s [[Feenix system]] within an intersecting portion of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western Reaches" /> Situated at the coordinates K-17 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was represented by the [[Homeworld|native]] [[human]] [[Senator]] [[Tanner Cadaman]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], Cadaman was outspokenly critical about [[Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] wartime leadership and joined around two thousand legislators known as the [[Delegation of 2,000]] in signing the [[Petition of 2,000]] to protest the chancellor's [[Emergency Powers Act|emergency powers]].<ref name="Guide">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> One [[Standard day|day]] after the Republic [[Proclamation of the New Order|transitioned]] into the [[Galactic Empire]], Cadaman and sixty-two other senators involved with the Delegation of 2,000 were [[Senatorial arrests|arrested]] under accusations that they had colluded with an alleged [[Jedi]] insurgency.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
Feenix was introduced into the current [[canon]] when it was mentioned in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Originating in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, it was first mentioned in "[[Republic HoloNet News Special Inaugural Edition 16:5:241]]," a segment of the [[in-universe]] [[HoloNet News (Insider)|''HoloNet News'']] feature written by Wallace and [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and published in the [[Star Wars Insider 84|eighty-forth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine series<ref name="SWI81">{{InsiderCite|84|Republic HoloNet News Special Inaugural Edition 16:5:241}}</ref> on [[September 26]], [[2005]].<ref name="SWA">{{SWArchive|url=community/fanclub/mag/news20050913.html|text=The Ultimate Clone Guide in ''Star Wars Insider'' #84}}</ref>
