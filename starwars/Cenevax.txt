'''Cenevax''' was a [[Jenet]] [[Gender|female]] [[crime lord]] who ran a [[Cenevax's syndicate|syndicate]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and had [[Spy|spies]] in many places. After she learned that the [[Jedi]] [[Arath Tarrex]], indebted to the Jenet, was secretly the child of [[Jedi Master]] [[Yula Braylon]], Cenevax blackmailed Braylon into ignoring her crimes.

Around [[58 BBY]], during the [[forty-eighth annual Dragonfire Air Rally]], Cenevax made a deal with the [[Serennian]] [[racer]] [[Ramil]] in which he would purposefully lose so Cenevax's [[gambling]] ring could earn money from those who bet on his victory. When Ramil broke the deal, Cenevax sabotaged his [[T-22|airspeeder]]. The corrupt [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[inspector]] [[Sartori]] attempted to cover up the sabotage for Cenevax, but the Jedi Master [[Dooku]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], followed the inspector to the Jenet's [[Cenevax's den|den]]. After capturing the crime lord, the Jedi uncovered the truth about Tarrex's [[parent]]age, and Cenevax was sent to the [[Citadel]] [[prison]].

==Biography==
===The schemes of Cenevax===
{{Quote|It's true. All of it. I have&hellip;looked the other way for years, at Madame Cenevax's behest.''"<br />"''But why? You have no need for money, dirty or otherwise.''"<br />"''She didn't pay me. She didn't have to.|Yula Braylon and Jedi Master [[Jor Aerith]]|<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />}}
[[File:Coruscant_SWCT.png|thumb|right|270px|Cenevax operated on the planet Coruscant.]]
The [[Jenet]] [[Gender|female]] Cenevax<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> was a [[crime lord]] who lived during the [[Republic Era]]. She led a [[Cenevax's syndicate|syndicate]]&mdash;an unlicensed [[gambling]] ring&mdash;from her [[Cenevax's den|den]], located in the [[Hythan Quarter]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], collecting many treasures that she kept in her hoard. Cenevax had many [[Spy|spies]] and contacts, including officials of the [[Galactic Republic]], and also commanded a number of [[Trandoshan]]s.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Sometime after [[68 BBY]],<ref name="Date 68">[[Arath Tarrex]] discovers he is the child of [[Yula Braylon]] following the [[summit on Station Zeta]]. The summit takes place after [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] becomes the [[Padawan]] of [[Dooku]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[68 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Arath Tarrex]] became indebted to Cenevax from the game [[batana]]. Although Cenevax threatened Tarrex, she learned through his attempts to talk his way out of it that he was the child of [[Jedi Master]] [[Yula Braylon]], a member of the [[Jedi High Council]]. Cenevax used that information to blackmail Braylon, who looked the other way on behalf of the crime lord for [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

In around [[58 BBY]],<ref name="Date 58">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' establishes that the [[forty-eighth annual Dragonfire Air Rally]] took place shortly before [[Dooku]] accepted a seat on the [[Jedi High Council]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to around [[58 BBY]].</ref> Cenevax orchestrated a scheme involving the [[forty-eighth annual Dragonfire Air Rally]], an [[airspeeder]] race hosted on Coruscant. The Jenet paid the [[Serennian]] [[racer]] [[Ramil]], expected by many to win the race, to lose, thus profiting from the bets placed on him. As insurance, however, Cenevax placed a [[detonator]] in his [[T-22|speeder's]] [[fuel]] tank. In order to cover up her crimes, she paid the [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[inspector]] [[Sartori]] and dispatched a gang of her Trandoshans.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

===Cenevax discovered===
{{Quote|Cenevax promised you, eh? Promises, promises. So easy to break. You promised her, Sartori. Cenevax pays you, you keep her enemies away. Remember that promise, do ya?''"<br />"''And I have. It isn't easy, you know. Especially when you blow up a speeder with the entire planet watching.|Cenevax and Sartori|<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />}}
During the [[Dragonfire Air Rally]], Ramil initially allowed himself to be overtaken, but eventually went back on his deal with Cenevax, intending to win the race. Cenevax's detonator went off, causing Ramil's [[speeder]] to [[Attack at the Dragonfire Air Rally|crash]], and Sartori allowed Cenevax's Trandoshans to retrieve the speeder from his [[Jaffkee House|precinct house]]. The Jedi Master [[Dooku]]&mdash;Ramil's [[House Serenno|brother]]&mdash;and his [[Padawan]], [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], having witnessed the incident, investigated, despite being ordered not to by Braylon. The two Jedi learned of Cenevax's plan, following Sartori to the Jenet's lair after Dooku intimidated the inspector.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

[[File:Justice.jpg|thumb|left|310px|Dooku (left) and Qui-Gon Jinn (right) confronted Cenevax in her lair.]]
After Sartori contacted Cenevax's subordinate [[Bagara]] and demanded to see her, the Jenet met the inspector in her den. However, Cenevax was unconcerned with Sartori's worries regarding the Jedi, reminding him of his promise to keep trouble away from her. When Sartori demanded that Cenevax cleaned up her mess, she picked up a trinket box from her hoard and beckoned the confused inspector over to see it. Cenevax proceeded to strike Sartori with the box and then chastised the inspector for getting [[blood]] on it, subsequently hitting him several more times. The Jenet&mdash;having realized the Jedi had followed Sartori to her lair&mdash;scolded the inspector for leading the Jedi to her as her Trandoshans surrounded Dooku and Jinn.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

Cenevax called out to the Jedi, telling them that hiding was useless. Jinn accused Cenevax of [[Death|murdering]] Sartori, though the Jenet revealed that the inspector still lived<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> by kicking him.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> She seized the Jedi's [[lightsaber]]s, considering to add the [[Kyber crystal|crystals]] within to her collection. Cenevax was undeterred by Dooku's confidence in her defeat, claiming she would make him disappear and revealing that she knew of his relation to Ramil. When Cenevax disclosed that she was aware that Dooku had been ordered not to intervene, the Jedi Master deduced she had a contact on the [[Jedi Council|Council]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

===Cenevax and the Council===
{{Dialogue|Dooku|Who is it, Cenevax? You wouldn't tell me in your lair&mdash;|Cenevax|Only 'cos you didn't have the guts to hurt Cenevax.|Dooku|As you quite rightly pointed out, it is not the Jedi way, but neither is accepting bribes.|Cenevax|Cenevax is saying nothing!|Jinn|Fortunately, you don't have to.|attr=Dooku and Jinn interrogate Cenevax before the Jedi Council|src=<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />}}
Dooku called down the [[convor]] [[Calleen (convor)|Calleen]], startling Cenevax. The Jedi [[Telekinesis|summoned]] their weapons and cut down the Trandoshans while Cenevax was distracted, although the Jenet bit into and killed the [[bird]]. Cenevax attempted to escape, but Dooku [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to drag her back. As Cenevax clawed at her treasure, Dooku demanded to know the identity of her Jedi contact. To intimidate the Jenet, Dooku threatened to suffocate her in her own hoard, but Cenevax claimed that it would not be in his nature as a Jedi. Although Dooku began to press her into her treasure, he ultimately relented, instead apprehending Cenevax and taking her to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

[[File:TheCitadel-TC.png|thumb|right|280px|The Jedi imprisoned Cenevax in the Citadel.]]
After Dooku confronted the Council, he had [[Jedi Temple Guard|Temple Guards]] bring Cenevax into the [[Jedi Council Chamber|chamber]]. Dooku stated his accusations against Cenevax, which the Jenet claimed he could not prove. Although Cenevax refused to disclose who her contact was, Dooku was able to [[Force sense|sense]] Braylon's guilt, determining she was the traitor. When confronted, Braylon admitted to overlooking Cenevax's activities and confessed to being Tarrex's [[Parent|mother]]. Braylon and Tarrex were both punished, while Cenevax was sent to the [[Citadel]] [[prison]] on [[Lola Sayu]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

After learning of Cenevax's intended fate from [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], Dooku<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> bitterly<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> realized that the Jedi were not going to the authorities over the matter. As a result of his injuries sustained due to Cenevax's sabotage, Ramil had to wear an [[exosuit]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> In [[42 BBY]],<ref name="Date 42">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[incident on Serenno]] to [[42 BBY]].</ref> when Dooku was taken captive by his brother, who had since become [[Count of Serenno|Count]] of the planet [[Serenno]] and orchestrated its [[Incident on Serenno|invasion]] by [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], the Jedi Master remarked on the condition that Cenevax had left Ramil in.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Typical Jedi. But you're not in yer Temple now, oh-no-no-no. You'll disappear. How sad. And you won't be missed. Oh no. Not you. Too much like yer brother&hellip;Yeah, Cenevax knows who you are, Dooku.|Cenevax taunts Dooku|<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />}}
Though the [[rat]]like Cenevax was skittish, she was also deadly. While in her lair, Cenevax scurried around her hoard with quick movements, picking up and then discarding various trinkets.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> She was unbothered by Sartori's concerns over the Jedi, quickly becoming irate and reminding the inspector of his task. Cenevax struck Sartori with one of her own trinkets in anger, claiming that he had ruined her. Despite being threatened by Dooku, Cenevax was confident he would not harm her, believing the Jedi were weak in nature. A [[Unidentified Trandoshan heavy|Trandoshan]] who worked for Cenevax claimed that she would kill him if he revealed the identity of his master.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
