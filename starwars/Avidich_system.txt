The '''Avidich system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Unknown Regions]] that was part of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. [[Haplif]] and his group of [[Agbui]], along with their passengers [[Coduyo'po'nekri|Yoponek]] and [[Yomie]], passed through the system on their way to the [[Grand Migration]] on [[Shihon]].<ref name="Greater Good" />
