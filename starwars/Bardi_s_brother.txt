A '''Brother''' of the [[human]] rebel [[Bardi]]. Both were members of the [[Maya Pei Brigade]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. In [[4 BBY]], the brigade was [[Ambush of the Maya Pei Brigade|attacked]] and Bardi and his brother were apart of a group of at least sixteen members of the brigade that escaped aboard a [[starship]] that landed on [[Yavin 4]]. However Bardi's brother subsequently departed in the ship leaving the rest of the group stranded on the [[moon]]. Bardi later claimed that his brother had left look for more survivors however [[Gerdis]] doubted the claim, stated that no one believes that.<ref name="One Year Later" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bardi's brother was mentioned in the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', appearing in the series's first episode, "[[One Year Later]]" which was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="One Year Later" /> on [[April 22]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref>
