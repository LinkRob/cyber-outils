'''Annjin''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual worker of [[Allehander Pso]] on the [[Five Points]] [[space station]] in the [[Five Points system]]. After her duty expired, Pso sold her and his other workers as [[Slavery|slaves]] to the [[Galactic Empire]]. However, [[Jyn Erso]] captured the crew of the spaceship and rescued them.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
