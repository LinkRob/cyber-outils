The '''13th Battalion''', also known as the '''"Iron" Battalion''', was a [[battalion]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] that participated in the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]. It was commanded by [[Jedi General]] [[Jaro Tapal]], the [[Jedi Commander]] [[Cal Kestis]], and a [[Unidentified Clone Commander (13th Battalion)|Clone Commander]]. It fought in the [[Bracca Invasion]], and later participated in the [[Skirmish aboard the Albedo Brave|implementation]] of [[Order 66]] against the [[Jedi]], which resulted in the [[death]] of Tapal and the entire unit, as well as the destruction of the ''[[Albedo Brave]]''.

==Characteristics==
[[File:13thBattalionCloneCommander-JFO.png|thumb|left|150px|The 13th Battalion's Clone Commander]]
The 13th [[Battalion]], also known as the "Iron" Battalion due to the resilience they showed in conflict, was an [[infantry]] battalion of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] that consisted of [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="13th Battalion Figure">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|packtype=Gaming Greats Figure|pack=#12: 13th Battalion Clone Trooper|altlink=https://galacticfigures.com/figureDetails.aspx?id=29266}}</ref> The [[trooper]]s were equipped with [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] with light [[Color|yellow]] markings and [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s. The battalion was led by [[Jedi General]] [[Jaro Tapal]] and a [[Unidentified Clone Commander (13th Battalion)|Clone Commander]] who was outfitted with the same gear as the standard clone troopers of the battalion. Tapal's [[Padawan]], [[Cal Kestis]], also served in the battalion as a Jedi Commander. The 13th Battalion was headquartered on the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Albedo Brave]]''. Several members of a [[Chiata's unit|separate military unit]] led by [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Chiata]] had the same color scheme of the 13th Battalion. As a battalion of the Grand Army,<ref name="JFO" /> it was composed of four [[Company|companies]] of 144 [[trooper]]s each.<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'' establishes that a [[battalion]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] is made up of four [[Company|companies]]. [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] furthermore establishes that a company in the Grand [[Army]] of the Republic is composed of 144 [[clone trooper]]s.</ref>

==History==
===Bracca campaign and training===
{{Quote|You're in a rush.''"<br />"''Master Tapal's called me for training.''"<br />"''You got this, kid.|A clone trooper and Cal Kestis|<ref name="JFO" />}}
[[File:JaroTapalClones.png|thumb|right|250px|The 13th Battalion was stationed on the ''Albedo Brave'' during the Bracca campaign.]]
The 13th Battalion was a battalion<ref name="Art of JFO" /> in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], and fell under the command of Jedi General Jaro Tapal, the Jedi Commander Cal Kestis and a [[Clone Commander]], who served as the unit's leading [[clone trooper officer]]. The 13th Battalion participated in the [[Bracca Invasion]], and by the [[time]] after the [[Battle of Coruscant]], managed to secure the [[planet]]. The 13th Battalion was then located on their headquarters, the ''Venator''-class [[Star Destroyer]] ''Albedo Brave''.<ref name="JFO" />

After [[Bracca]] was secured, rumors spread amongst the members of the 13th that they would be getting a new deployment and they were true because the battalion was ordered to assist Jedi Master [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] and his forces gain victory in the [[Fourth Battle of Mygeeto]]. Around the same time, Jedi Padawan Cal Kestis was summoned by his Master, Tapal, to the ''Albedo Brave''{{'s}} Jedi training site. On the way, Kestis spoke with several [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s on topics such as having rematches, rumors of their next deployment, and whether Kestis was on his way to [[Jedi training|training]]. At least one member of the battalion believed that Bracca was a "dump" and another was friendly enough with Kestis to give him a high-five and wish him luck.<ref name="JFO" />

Kestis then entered the training room, where Tapal was speaking with the Clone [[Commander]] in the control center. Tapal instructed Kestis to reach their position, and Kestis undertook a training course. During the training, the Clone Commander fired upon Kestis as part of the training course, although Kestis was able to deflect the blasts. Kestis then reached the control center, to which Tapal then informed Kestis that the battalion secured Bracca and would move to [[Mygeeto]] shortly.<ref name="JFO" />

===Order 66===
====Initial attack====
{{Quote|Execute Order 66.|Darth Sidious, to the Clone Commander|<ref name="JFO" />}}
[[File:Tapal13thBattalionOrder66-FallenOrder.png|thumb|left|250px|The 13th Battalion turned against Jedi General Jaro Tapal as a result of Order 66.]]
At that time, the Clone Commander received [[Order 66]] from [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], secretly [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious, just as Tapal felt a [[disturbance in the Force]] of the Jedi being attacked across [[the galaxy]]. The Clone Commander raised his [[blaster]] ready to shoot Tapal, when Tapal activated [[Jaro Tapal's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] and cut the blaster and then slashed across the clone's stomach, [[Death|killing]] him. Although Kestis was confused, Tapal told him that the clones had betrayed them. Tapal then instructed Kestis to get to the ''Albedo Brave''{{'s}} [[escape pod]]s to [[Skirmish aboard the Albedo Brave|escape the ship]], but to use the maintenance halls and that they would rendezvous there along with further instructions. Other clone troopers then began blasting their way into the control room while the two Jedi parted ways. Kestis made his way to the maintenance halls while the ship was put on high alert and clone troopers were deployed, some of which spotted Kestis and fired on him.<ref name="JFO" />

====Skirmish on the ''Albedo Brave''====
{{Quote|Reporting casualties! Tapal has taken out multiple squads. Shoot on sight.|A clone trooper, to his comrades|<ref name="JFO" />}}
[[File:Skirmish_above_Bracca.png|thumb|right|250px|Tapal was mortally wounded while protecting his apprentice, Padawan Cal Kestis, from the clone troopers.]]
After breaching a panel and entering the the maintenance hall, Kestis walked above several members of the battalion, who were updated on the Jedi's status. They reported that the Jedi were on the move and that there was no sign of Kestis and that they needed to search everywhere, but were then alerted to Kestis's movements above them. A clone then instructed his fellow [[soldier]]s to hold the room while he searched the area. After Kestis reached another area of the ship, the clones below him reported to others that there were casualties and that Tapal had destroyed several [[squad]]s.<ref name="JFO" />

While Kestis continued, the clones discovered that there was someone in the maintenance halls and explosions erupted throughout the vessel. Sometime during the skirmish, Tapal overloaded the ship's main [[reactor]]. Kestis then reached an area with [[turbolift]]s, which were damaged after several explosions. After moving in the area, a clone trooper spotted Kestis and reported to his comrades and began firing. Kestis then encountered two more troopers which began firing on him, and in the fight Kestis lost his [[lightsaber]], although Tapal arrived and killed the clones by pushing them off the doorway and into the turbolift shaft. Tapal then instructed Kestis to continue onward.<ref name="JFO" />

====Escape from the ''Albedo Brave''====
{{Quote|I overloaded the ship's reactors. The explosion will mask our escape. This&hellip; war is not over, my Padawan.|Jaro Tapal, to Cal Kestis|<ref name="JFO" />}}
[[File:Albedo-Brave-destroyed.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Albedo Brave'' was destroyed in an explosion, killing the clones aboard shortly after Kestis' escape.]]
Leaving the turbolift shaft, Kestis moved through another maintenance hall as his master slaughtered a contingent of clone troopers below. Tapal then informed Kestis that there was an override nearby and that it must be activated in order for them to escape the vessel. Once Kestis unlocked the override, Tapal moved from one hallway to another, killing several other clone troopers. Kestis eventually arrived at the [[escape pod]] bay, when he confronted two troopers. Tapal arrived simultaneously and dispatched the two troopers, although a large group of Republic forces soon converged on their location from a central hallway. Tapal defended Kestis as he opened the door, but was eventually wounded by the barrage of [[blaster]] fire. After a final attempt to protect Kestis, Tapal used [[the Force]] against the clones but was hit by another barrage of laser fire, and fell backwards into the pod. Kestis used [[Force Stasis]] to freeze the clones and allow time to escape. Once the escape pod launched, Kestis listened to Tapal's final words as the ''Albedo Brave'' succumbed to an explosion that claimed the vessel. The explosion killed all members of the 13th Battalion.<ref name="JFO" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|Master. I will never forget. The loss has become a part of me.|Cal Kestis, to an apparition of Jaro Tapal|<ref name="JFO" />}}
Kestis travelled to [[Zeffo]] a [[Second mission to Zeffo|second time]] and explored [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Zeffo)|the ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] that had crashed there. Kestis encountered several [[Psychometry|Force Echos]] of the [[skirmish on Zeffo]] that occurred when the [[Chiata's unit|forces]] of Jedi General [[Chiata]] turned against her and her padawan [[Marseph]], resulting in Kestis reminiscing on his experience during Order 66's execution. During Kestis' [[Mission to Dathomir (Cal Kestis)|mission to Dathomir]], he had a vision of his experience against the 13th Battalion during the initial implementation of Order 66 while attempting to enter the [[Tomb of Kujet]].<ref name="JFO" />
