The '''''Cuervo''-class cruiser''' was a model of [[cruiser]] in use during the [[High Republic Era]]. [[Baron]] [[Boolan]], [[scientist]] of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]], owned a souped-up ''Cuervo''-class cruiser that radiated a [[Color|purple]] glow. Nihil [[commander]] [[Vark Tarpalin]] spotted the cruiser in the [[fleet]] arriving above [[Lonisa City]] on the [[planet]] [[Valo]] for his [[Dark Fair]] celebration<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> in [[229 BBY]].<ref name="EFV Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'', including the attendance of a ''Cuervo''-class [[cruiser]] at the [[Dark Fair]], take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> Tarpalin had heard that Baron Boolan may have also brought [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] with him. The Dark Fair was interrupted, however, by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] acting as a vigilante known as the "[[Scarlet Skull]]." Several of the attending vessels were destroyed, while the rest, including one with a purple glow, departed.<ref name="Escape from Valo" />
