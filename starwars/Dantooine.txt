'''Dantooine''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Dantooine system]], in the [[Raioballo sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' northern [[New Territories]] region. Dantooine was affiliated with both the [[Galactic Republic]] and later, the [[Galactic Empire]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Dantooine is too remote to make an effective demonstration. But don't worry&mdash;we will deal with your Rebel friends soon enough.|[[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]|<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>}}
[[File:Blba1.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Blba trees dotted the surface of Dantooine.|alt=Blba tree]]
A terrestrial [[planet]] within the [[Raioballo sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], near the [[Sinsang system|Sinsang]] and [[Angor system]]s, Dantooine was the fourth planet in-[[orbit]] around its [[star]], [[Dina]]. Dantooine, itself, was orbited by two [[moon]]s, the larger of which shone a light [[Color|purple]] luminescence, while the lesser of the two gave a greenish radiance. Two [[Hyperspace route|hyperspace lanes]] ran near-to Dantooine: the long, winding [[Veragi Trade Route]], and the smaller [[Myto's Arrow]], a rather backwater route from Dantooine to [[Jaemus system|Jaemus]]&mdash;though which provided travelers with a faster trip. Nevertheless, despite having a close proximity to both lanes, Dantooine was considered somewhat inaccessible.<ref name="BMF 37" />

Most of the planetary surface topography and geography of Dantooine consisted of grassy plains and low, rolling hillocks and mounds dotted with [[blba tree]]s, as well as forested hills containing creeks, rivers, and large freshwater lakes.<ref name="USWNE" /> Much of Dantooine's weather away from the colder poles was rather uneventful, remaining steadily temperate, mild, and semi-dry throughout much of its 378&ndash;[[local day]] [[Standard year|year]]. In springtime, the rainy season in [[spring]], which brought torrential downpours that turned the fertile soil into mud and tinged the brown [[grass]] with a shade of lavender.<ref name="BMF 37" /> Dantooine also had damp, dense [[forest]]s with thorny undergrowth and an endless canopy, where [[sap]] [[farm]]s were grown.<ref name="The Trigger" />

Dantooine was rich in wildlife. The foliage from the plains blba trees sustained large herbivores, such as the [[piket]]. Native predators included the [[stalker lizard]], the [[graul]],<ref name="BMF 37" /> and the [[kath hound]],<ref name="Savage Spirits">''[[Savage Spirits]]''</ref> though the latter had become rare in the wild after many were captured as guard animals and pets. [[Fabool]], a balloon-like creature, were sometimes punctured by blba tree thorns and eaten by [[carnivorous snail]]s.<ref name="BMF 37" />

==History==
===Early history and High Republic Era===
{{Quote|It started in a public house on the planet Dantooine. The settlement is their oldest but remains small. A port for trade, with little local product of their own except for the expeditions into the vast jungle. ''[&hellip;]'' Travelers come and go there, people of all kinds, all work, above and below and alongside the law.|Marda Ro, in her [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]]|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
Several [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|onset]] of the [[Clone Wars]], records mentioned involvement from the [[Jedi Order]] on Dantooine. At one point, a [[Jedi Temple (Dantooine)|temple]] was maintained on the planet. A few [[Jedi]] found [[Dantari crystal|kyber crystals]] within the eggs of them cave-dwelling [[kinrath]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gears" /> Following the Jedi's withdrawal from the planet, Dantooine was resettled as part of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s campaign to populate the [[New Territories]].<ref name="BMF 37" /> The oldest [[Unidentified settlement (Dantooine)|settlement]] on the planet contained a [[Unidentified public house|public house]] and acted as a trading port, but the town itself was still small<ref name="Closed Fist" /> as of circa [[381 BBY]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="381 Date">"[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" establishes that [[Marda Ro]] visited Dantooine around a [[Standard year|year]] after taking possession of the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', an event that ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]]. Therefore, Ro's trip to Dantooine must have occurred around [[381 BBY]].</ref> the [[Evereni]] [[Marda Ro]] arrived in the public house in [[Marda Ro's hunt|search]] of other members of her kind, meeting a [[Unidentified human (Marda Ro)|human]] inside who pointed her towards two Evereni on a [[Unidentified space station (Pantora)|space station]] close to<ref name="Closed Fist" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="USWNE" /> [[Pantora]].<ref name="Closed Fist" />

In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Galactic Republic [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] prepared to open. Among the areas of its [[biosphere zone]] was a recreation of Dantooine [[jungle]], which was toured by Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] while he attended a [[Starlight Beacon conclave|conclave]] there. The [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], an incident in which the [[freighter]] ''[[Legacy Run]]'' was destroyed in [[hyperspace]], occurred soon afterwards. In the aftermath, [[Emergences]] of the [[starship]]'s debris into [[realspace]] began occurring at random throughout the [[Outer Rim Territories]], leading [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] to institute an extensive hyperspace [[blockade]] to prevent further disasters. The [[eighteenth Emergence]] occurred near Dantooine, prompting the blockade to be extended another fifty [[parsec]]s. Another one of the first twenty-one Emergences destroyed an [[orbital shipping facility]] near the planet which had been coordinating a large aid shipment to Outer Rim worlds affected by the blockade.<ref name="LotJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

===Clone Wars===
{{Quote|I've just received a distress signal from our forces on Dantooine. Master Windu's defenses held, but at a great cost to his men. They need medical supplies immediately.|Jedi Master [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]]|<ref name="Brain Invaders" />}}
During the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], Dantooine was populated by peaceful [[farmer]]s who came to the planet to grow crops and raise livestock on homesteads primarily powered by wind energy. During the war, the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] arrived on Dantooine, supposedly in search of ancient artifacts, and the Republic responding by dispatching [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] to [[Defense of Dantooine|deal]] with the situation. During the battle, many of Windu's troopers perished, but the Jedi was able to defeat the Confederacy's [[B2-series super battle droid]]s and [[seismic tank]]s that caused dangerous shock waves to the planet's surface.<ref name="BMF 37" />

While on Dantooine, the Republic troops were able to send out a distress signal which was later picked up by the Republic,<ref name="BMF 37" /> who sent supplies and medical aid to the planet immediately aboard the [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigate]] ''[[TB-73]]'' with the help of Jedi Padawans [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Barriss Offee]]. However, the two Jedi were unable to complete their delivery mission as a result of an [[Skirmish aboard TB-73|infestation]] of [[Geonosian]] [[brain worm]]s aboard the [[frigate]], and was later undertaken by Jedi Master [[Kit Fisto]].<ref name="Brain Invaders" />

===Galactic Civil War===
{{Quote|Our scout ships have reached Dantooine. They have found the remains of a Rebel base, but they estimate that it has been deserted for some time. They are now conducting an extensive search of the surrounding systems.|An Imperial officer to [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Grand Moff Tarkin]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]|<ref name="ANH" />|audio=Cass-ANH.flac}}
[[File:DantooineRebels.png|thumb|right|210px|Dantooine as viewed from space]]
By the early [[Galactic Civil War]], Dantooine was an [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance]] regional operations sector headquarters and meeting hub for executive-level discussions. Dantooine was included in a map of the [[galaxy]] that listed [[safe world]]s, [[starfighter]] hubs at level five or higher, regional headquarters and operations sectors, and deep space caches, which was later incorporated in ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> The Rebellion used the planet as the location of their first [[Dantooine base|base]].<ref name="Databank" />

In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> during the early rebellion, the former Imperial [[Senator]] and rebel leader [[Mon Mothma]] made arrangements for the rebel fleet to rendezvous above Dantooine. She wanted to [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|unify]] the disparate rebel cells and factions and make official the Rebel Alliance. With the help of [[Phoenix Leader]] [[Hera Syndulla]] and the [[Spectres]], Mothma managed to travel through the dangerous [[Archeon Pass|Archeon pass]] to Dantooine. Above Dantooine, Mothma gave an impassioned [[HoloNet]] speech urging [[the galaxy]] to rise up in rebellion against the Empire. Shortly later, the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|rebel fleet]] converged above Dantooine.<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref>

[[File:DantooineBaseUnderground.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Rebel Alliance established their first base on Dantooine.]]
At some point before the [[Battle of Scarif]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] tracking device was discovered hidden in a shipment of supplies by [[Ryle Torsyn]]. Although the device was destroyed and the Empire remained unaware of Rebel activity on the planet, the Alliance took no risks and evacuated.<ref name="BMF 37" /> They established their new [[Great Temple|headquarters]] on the jungle [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]], a location that was already in use by [[Jan Dodonna]]'s [[Massassi Group|rebel cell]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

When [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was held [[Prisoner|captive]] on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] threatened to destroy her [[Homeworld|home planet]] of [[Alderaan]] if she did not provide him with the location of the Rebellion's secret base. Organa then lied to him, stating the base was still on Dantooine. Tarkin, impatient to try out the Death Star's [[superlaser]] at full capacity, deemed Dantooine "too remote to make an effective demonstration" and ordered the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of Alderaan anyway.<ref name="ANH" /> Nonetheless, Imperial forces, led by [[General]] [[Cassio Tagge]],<ref name="The Trigger" /> reconnoitered Dantooine, only to find the remains of the abandoned Rebel base. [[Thane Kyrell]] was one of those who were sent to explore Dantooine, and he was surprised by the level of organization and sophistication he saw in the abandoned base.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

[[Chelli Lona Aphra]] was on Dantooine at the time of Tagge's expedition, scavenging at the abandoned base for [[3.23 colicoidic pulse field modulator]]s with which to repair and upgrade [[Droideka]]s. She surrendered to the Imperial forces to prevent them from discovering the [[Ark Angel II|''Ark Angel'']], and learned about the destruction of Alderaan from Tagge. Although the general didn't think she was a rebel, he ordered a [[Unidentified stormtrooper (Dantooine)|stormtrooper]] to execute her anyway. Dr. Aphra escaped by jamming the stormtrooper's blaster.<ref name="The Trigger" />

Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="date_note">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' dates the Imperial map to 7980 [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]], corresponding to around [[3 ABY]].</ref> [[Imperial Intelligence]] and the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] reverse-mapped suspicious activity reports against population studies in order to determine likely locations of Rebel bases. Dantooine was suspected as a possible system and appeared on a map along with other sparsely-populated worlds.<ref name="Scum">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>

===Rise of the First Order===
{{Quote|This is Dantooine. The First Order has taken control of the planet, creating a blockade and cutting off all long-range communications.|Venisa Doza|<ref name="Rebuilding" />}}
[[File:DantooineSurface.png|thumb|right|250px|Dantooine's surface during the war between the First Order and the Resistance]]
Following the outbreak of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]], the [[First Order]], a successor to the Galactic Empire, occupied and [[Blockade of Dantooine|blockaded]] Dantooine. The Resistance [[Commander]] [[Venisa Doza]] led a mission to evacuate several Resistance sympathizers. Doza's [[Jade Squadron]] and the [[Ace Squadron]] [[pilot]]s [[Torra Doza]], [[Kazuda Xiono]], and [[Hype Fazon]] managed to eliminate a squadron of [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] blockading Dantooine.<ref name="Rebuilding" />

They managed to rendezvous with three [[Resistance shuttle]]s but were attacked by [[Commander]] [[Pyre]]'s [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Thunderer]]''. Though Jade Squadron and the Aces managed to destroy several TIEs and evacuate two shuttles, one shuttle was shot down by [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|First Order TIE Fighter Pilot]] [[Jace Rucklin]].<ref name="Rebuilding" />

==Inhabitants==
Dantooine was poorly developed and quiet, with a low population made up mostly of [[farmer]]s. Indoor lighting was considered a novelty<ref name="The Trigger" /> around [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> [[Historian]]s debated the reason civilization never took hold on the fertile planet, but most agreed that it was due to the lack of significant resources and its remote position in the Galaxy.<ref name="BMF 37" />

Dantooine was the homeworld of [[Davish Krail]], a Rebel Alliance pilot who served in [[Gold Squadron]] and was killed at the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="USWNE" />

==Locations==
One settlement on Dantooine was [[Dantoo Town]].<ref name="The Trigger" /> Another [[Unidentified outpost (Dantooine)|outpost]] was located on the planet's plains, and had spaceport facilities for at least three [[shuttle]]s.<ref name="Rebuilding" />

The Rebellion built their [[Dantooine base|base]], disguised as a sap farm in a dense forest, on the opposite side of the planet from Dantoo Town.<ref name="The Trigger" />

At some point after the start of the Imperial occupation, the Empire established a mining outpost on its surface.<ref name="BMF 37" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Y-wings_Dantooine_Chiang.jpg|thumb|right|250px|''Rogue One'' concept art of Y-wings on Dantooine]]
Dantooine was first mentioned in the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]].<ref name="ANH" /> Stories from [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], particularly the [[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic (comic series)|''Knights of the Old Republic'']] and ''[[Star Wars: The New Jedi Order]]'', provided extensive details both about the planet's composition and history, within the Legends context.

Early versions of the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] had the [[First Order]] converting Dantooine into their [[Starkiller Base|superweapon]] and retrofitting the abandoned Rebel base for their own purposes, having been previously retrofitted by the Galactic Empire. The superlaser was set inside an old [[volcano]], stealing energy from the suns of the system, with X-wing fighters trying to dive-bomb into it. Literally and metaphorically bringing more darkness to the galaxy, the base's name was to be the "Doom Star."<ref name="TFA Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>

Initially, the evacuation of the Dantooine Rebel base was to be featured in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', but did not make the cut due to budget constraints.<ref name="THR FB">{{Facebook|username=HollywoodReporter|url=HollywoodReporter/videos/10154143289392750|name=The Hollywood Reporter|date=07-02-2017|quote=Meet your Oscar nominee John Knoll, Visual Effects Supervisor on 'Star Wars: Rogue One.' Ask him questions in the comments below.|archivedate=20200819121723}}</ref>
