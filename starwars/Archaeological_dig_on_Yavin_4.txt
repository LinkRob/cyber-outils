Sometime before the [[Battle of Coruscant]], an '''archaeological dig'''<ref name="sw100">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> took place on the [[moon]] of [[Yavin 4]].<ref name="Coruscant" /> The dig uncovered the [[Great Sith Wars Bas-Relief]]<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|226|Art Wars}}</ref> in one of the enormous stone temples built by the [[Massassi]] on the moon some five thousands [[Standard year|years]] before the rise of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Helmet Collection" />

Later, [[Darth Sidious]], a [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] of the [[Sith]] whose public persona was [[Supreme Chancellor]] Sheev Palpatine of the [[Galactic Republic]], retrieved the Great Sith Wars Bas-Relief at the archaeological site on Yavin 4.<ref name="Coruscant" />
