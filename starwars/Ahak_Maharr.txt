The '''Ahak Maharr''' were a tusked [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]. They spoke a guttural, deep-pitched [[Ahak Maharr (language)|language]]. They had a traditional [[Toast (ritual)|toast]] that, once translated into [[Galactic Basic Standard]], meant "May we rend the flesh of our enemies, and drink their [[blood]] as we do this liquid before us."<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
