The '''Baran Do Sages''' were an ancient [[The Force|Force]]-based [[Kel Dor]] organization. They protected their [[homeworld]] of [[Dorin]] and were famous for their well-honed [[precognition]] skills, which enabled them to launch early evacuations in cases of deadly storms and advise government officials. For [[Standard century|centuries]], the [[Jedi Order]] recruited Dorin's strongest [[Force-sensitive]]s, which ultimately caused the Baran Do order to fall into obscurity sometime before the Empire arose.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony" /> Still, they had a working relationship with the Jedi, who they were allied to. The sages found being unable to foresee the future regarding the Jedi who was liaison to them concerning.<ref name="Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

The teachings of the Baran Do were organized into three branches inspired by natural elements: the [[Telekinesis|Guided Wind]], the [[Force lightning|Guided Lightning]], and the [[Force sense|Guided Stream]]. The Sages were entirely subservient to the will of the Force, and sought to only act at precisely the right place and time to avoid undesired consequences. If they did not meditate before making major decisions or acted rashly, this could take a toll on their mental health, yet conversely they were strong in the virtue of discipline and understood how to use lightning without needing to draw on the dark side. Even though they focused on patience and [[meditation]]&mdash;to the point that other Kel Dors found them apathetic to suffering&mdash;some of them trained in [[quarterstaff]] combat and mastered [[Force power|powers]] of the elements.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony" />
