'''Carida Squadron''' was a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] made up of [[Imperial cadet|cadets]] from the [[Carida Academy|Imperial Academy]] on [[Carida]]. The squadron fought in the [[mission on Qhulosk]] on [[Qhulosk]] after the [[cadet]]s had finished their training in the [[Imperial Academy|academy]].<ref name="Imperial Cadet 4" />
