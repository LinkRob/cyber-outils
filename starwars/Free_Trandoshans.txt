The '''Free Trandoshans''' were a group lead by [[Trandoshan/Legends|Trandoshan]] [[Ronko Bist]] which operated throughout the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the war, Bist was arrested by [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and jailed in the [[Prison/Legends|prison]] [[The Prism]].<ref name="Ghost Prison 4" />
