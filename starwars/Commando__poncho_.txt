'''Commando''' was a [[Color|green]] [[poncho]] with smeared brown spots and a white [[bandolier]] around the neck.<ref name="JFO" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFO Date5">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] found the Commando poncho in a chest inside the cave of an [[Albino Wyyyschokk]] near the [[Imperial Refinery]] on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="JFO" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Commando was created for the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' as an optional collectable cosmetic for players to find.<ref name="JFO" />
