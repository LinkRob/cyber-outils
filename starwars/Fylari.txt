The '''Fylari''' were the inhabitants of the [[Fylar region]] of the [[planet]] [[Ontotho]]. When the other regions of Ontotho united to form a [[Ontothon government|planetary government]] in order to enter into the [[Galactic Republic]], the Fylari refused to agree to join.<ref name="Dark Temple 1" /> The Fylari protected a [[Temple on Ontotho|temple]] in the forests of Fylar containing [[Zeffonian]] ruins.<ref name="Dark Temple 2">[[Jedi Fallen Order - Dark Temple 2|''Jedi Fallen Order - Dark Temple'' 2]]</ref>
