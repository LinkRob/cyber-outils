'''Blast shields''' were a type of physical shielding employed on the [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]], a model of [[Low Altitude Assault Transport|LAAT]] [[gunship]] used by the [[Republic Military]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The blast shielding was not always employed, being able to close over the gunship if ordered. During the [[Battle of Teth]]<ref name="TCWfilm" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] ordered [[Clone Lieutenant]] [[Hawk]], a pilot of his LAAT/i, to close the blast shielding in response to heavy Separatist fire from the [[battle droid]]s stationed at the [[B'omarr Order Monastery|Teth Monastery]]. The gunship was able to reach the ground intact, allowing Skywalker and the others on the LAAT/i to participate in the rest of the battle.<ref name="TCWfilm" />
