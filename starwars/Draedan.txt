{{Quote|Let me tell you something about Draedans, Princess. I'm one of eighteen brothers and twenty-two sisters&mdash;which makes mine a shamefully small family. I have more cousins than I can count in every town within five hundred kilometers of here.|Aurelant, to Leia Organa|<ref name="MT" />}}
'''Draedans''' were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]] [[humanoid]]s native to the [[Water|aquatic]] [[planet]] of [[Sesid]]. They had [[Color|green]] skin, red [[eye]]s, and sharp [[Tooth|teeth]], and were known for having large families. During [[Operation Yellow Moon]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and her [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] team made contact with the Draedan [[Aurelant]], [[captain]] of the ''[[Daggadol]]''&mdash;a [[Submarine|subaquatic vessel]] crewed by Draedan [[pirate]]s who opposed the [[Galactic Empire]]. Aurelant and his crew aided the Rebels in [[Chase on Sesid|avoiding]] Imperial capture on Sesid, escaping off-world.<ref name="MT" />

==Behind the scenes==
Draedans first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] book ''[[Galaxy Guide 12: Aliens — Enemies and Allies]]'', a supplement to ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published in [[1995]].<ref name="GG">''[[Galaxy Guide 12: Aliens — Enemies and Allies]]''</ref> The species was later [[canon]]ized in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]'' of the [[Journey to Star Wars: The Force Awakens|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']] multimedia series.<ref name="MT" />
