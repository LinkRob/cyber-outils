'''Chuck Kallenbach''' was a playtester who later became a game designer for [[Decipher]], the company that created the [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]] between [[1995]] and [[2001]]. He worked for [[Sony]] on the ''[[Star Wars Galaxies Trading Card Game]]''<ref>{{SonyForumsCite|url=strategygames/posts/list.m?topic_id=24090|text=Q+A Answers for 12/4 - 12/17/2009}}</ref> and [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures (video game)|''Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures'']].<ref name="CWA">[[Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures (video game)|''Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures'']]</ref>
