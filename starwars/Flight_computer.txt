A '''flight computer''' was a type of [[computer]] used aboard [[starship]]s, whose primary purpose was translation of the movement of the controls into adjustments to the direction of the ship's thrust.<ref name="TFA VD" /> Flight computers could also store information, such as ship schematics and language information,<ref name="CL" /> or run programs off [[datachip]]s, such as [[Flight simulator|flight simulations]].<ref name="BtA" /> The company [[Torplex]] manufactured sophisticated flight computers which were used aboard the [[First Order]] [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE/fo]]<ref name="TFA VD" /> and [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter|TIE/sfs]],<ref name="Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> as well as an [[Experimental flight computer (TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor)|experimental one]] for [[Elrik Vonreg's TIE Interceptor|Elrik Vonreg's TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor]].<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> When [[Tamara Ryvora|Tam Ryvora]] was fixing a [[Fireball|racing starfighter]], her [[Aurebesh]] list of parts included "wiring for the flight computer".<ref name="Dangerous Business">{{Resistance|Dangerous Business}}</ref> When [[Kazuda Xiono]] was impersonating a [[First Order]] [[technician]] aboard the ''[[Titan]]'', he carried a [[datapad]] listing open work orders. The seventh point on the list, which was written in Aurebesh, read "install flight computer".<ref name="Station to Station">{{Resistance|Station to Station}}</ref>
