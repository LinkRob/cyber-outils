'''''Fortnite'' X ''Star Wars''''' was a limited event within [[Epic Games]]' ''[[Fortnite Battle Royale]]'', to promote [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. It began on [[December 14]], [[2019]], and ended on [[January 7]], [[2020]], coinciding with ''Fortnite''{{'s}} Winterfest event. The crossover event was officially announced on [[December 8]] on ''Fortnite''{{'s}} Twitter and in-game,<ref name="Announcement">{{Twitter|fortnite|status/1203392481532039170|Fortnite|quote=Live from Risky Reels ''Fortnite'' Presents: #StarWarsTheRiseOfSkywalker exclusive scene premiere. Watch it live on December 14. Doors open at 1:30 PM ET, show begins at 2 PM ET.|archivedate=20191217052211}}</ref> and cosmetics for ''Rise of Skywalker'' characters [[Finn]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and a [[Sith trooper]] were made available on [[December 12]].<ref name="Cosmetics">{{Twitter|fortnite|status/1205276331296079874|Fortnite|quote=Get the Rey, Finn and Sith Trooper Outfits in the Item Shop now!|archivedate=20191216004117}}</ref> Later that day at [[Wikipedia:The Game Awards 2019|The Game Awards 2019]], Donald Mustard announced that a [[TIE/wi interceptor|TIE Whisper]] Glider would be granted to all players for free during the event.<ref name="Glider">{{Twitter|fortnite|status/1205532839946661890|Fortnite|quote=Tune in tomorrow, December 14 at 2 PM ET to watch an exclusive clip of #StarWarsTheRiseOfSkywalker and earn the TIE Whisper Glider for free!|archivedate=20191216114647}}</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''[[File:Fortnite_event.jpg|thumb|right|300px|A screenshot during the event, using the Rey outfit]]''
''The live event took place at Risky Reels, a location on the ''Fortnite'' map. It started with the ''Millennium Falcon''{{'s}} arrival and landing attempt, before a First Order fleet arrived, sending three TIE fighters after the ''Falcon'', which was able to destroy all of them. Afterwards, the ''Falcon'' landed at Risky, and Geoff Keighley exited as a hologram, welcoming everyone, before bringing out Ben Schwartz, in character as a stormtrooper named FN0143. Shortly after, the pair welcomed director J.J. Abrams out in front of the crowd, who discussed ''The Rise of Skywalker'', and provided a vote for what the players thought the scene would be, between Darth Jar Jar, a Jedi mind trick, the Knights of Ren and a Rey and Kylo power ballad; ultimately, the mind trick had the most votes which turned out to be the correct scene.<ref name="Event">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cnet.com/news/fortnites-star-wars-event-added-lightsabers-to-the-game-heres-how-to-get-one|text=''Fortnite''{{'s}} ''Star Wars'' event added lightsabers to the game. Here's how to get one|author=Oscar Gonzalez|date=2019-12-18|archivedate=20200617032810}}</ref>''

''The live event showed a small clip from ''The Rise of Skywalker'', in which Rey, Finn and Poe board the ''Steadfast'', ending with Rey using a mind trick to make the stormtroopers allow their entry. Following it, a new vote took place allowing players to select their favorite lightsaber color, but the fleet of Star Destroyers returned, causing the trio to quickly rush back onto the ''Falcon''. As they left, Sheev Palpatine's voice stated, as the players entered slow motion, that it was the "day of the Sith." Immediately after, players were granted access to lightsabers&mdash;of the color they chose in the previous vote. The rest of the game was a team deathmatch using lightsabers only.<ref name="Clip">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theverge.com/2019/12/14/21020296/star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker-fortnite-jj-abrams-lightsabers|text=J.J. Abrams shows off ''Star Wars'' clip inside ''Fortnite'', and now lightsabers are in the game|author=Webster, Andrew|date=2019-12-14|work=The Verge|archivedate=20200926134043}}</ref>''

''Following the event, ''Fortnite'' X ''Star Wars'' properly took form, with TIE fighter crash sites scattered around the map, and lightsabers were added to the game for a limited time, along with First Order Blaster Rifles which could be found at the crash sites.<ref name="Event" />''

==Gameplay==
[[File:Fortnite_lightsabers.jpg|thumb|left|200px|A lightsaber and blaster rifle in game]]
''Fortnite'' X ''Star Wars'' brought lightsabers into the world of ''Fortnite'', allowing players to choose between Kylo Ren's, Rey's, [[Luke Skywalker]]'s and [[Mace Windu]]'s. For the duration of the event, players were able to obtain these weapons in the Battle Royale mode from special chests.<ref name="Gameplay">{{FortniteYouTube|F3LtBdw__fI|''Fortnite'' X ''Star Wars'' - Gameplay Trailer}}</ref>

During ''Fortnite''{{'s}} Winterfest event, players were able to open one in-game gift per day. Two of these gifts were ''[[Star Wars]]'' themed, one being a ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' Glider, and the other a Backbling depicting the Sith logo.<ref name="Winterfest">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theverge.com/2019/12/18/21027970/fortnite-winterfest-challenges-presents-star-wars-skins-emotes-gliders|text=Fortnite's winter event kicks off today with two weeks of in-game presents|author=Webster, Andrew|date=2019-12-18|work=The Verge|archivedate=20200917120431}}</ref>

On [[December 21]], new cosmetic outfits were added, bringing Kylo Ren and [[Zorii Bliss]] into the game.<ref name="Item Shop">{{EpicGames|url=fortnite/en-US/news/the-force-is-strong-with-fortnite|text=The Force Is Strong With Fortnite}}</ref>
