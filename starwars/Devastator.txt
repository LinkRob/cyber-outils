The '''''Devastator''''' was an [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] of the [[Imperial Navy]], famous for its tenure as the [[flagship]] of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Under Vader's command, the ''Devastator'' participated in the [[Battle of Scarif]] and the capture of the ''[[Tantive IV]].'' After the ''[[Executor]]'' became Vader's new flagship, the ''Devastator'' participated in the [[Battle of Hoth|Imperial victory]] at [[Hoth]] and was destroyed during the [[Battle of Endor|decisive battle]] fought at [[Endor]] while under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Jhared Montferrat]].

==Description==
===Overview===
[[File:TantiveIVcapture-hd.png|thumb|left|200px|''Tantive IV'' is pulled into ''Devastator''{{'s}} docking bay.]]
The ''Devastator'' was an impressive [[starship]] that served as the personal [[flagship]] of the equally impressive [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="POV 220">{{InsiderCite|220|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> The [[Star Destroyer]] was 1,600 [[meter]]s long, the standard length for an [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]]. As such, it was armed with [[turbolaser]] batteries, [[ion cannon]]s, and several [[tractor beam]] [[Tractor beam projector|projectors]].<ref name="sweISD">{{SWE|technology|imperialstardestroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer}}</ref>

===Upgrades===
Several dozens of [[time]]s in its service life, the ''Devastator'' was structurally altered from its original design to include the latest weapons and systems, which kept it more than competitive with the [[battleship]]s that were commissioned later.<ref name="BS" /> Unlike some other ''Imperial I''-class [[Star Destroyer]]s, such as ''[[Lawbringer]]'' and ''[[Relentless]]'',<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref> the [[hangar]]s within ''Devastator''{{'s}} ventral docking bay were split into two smaller sections, and the aft side featured a cover, so that the bays behind were not visible.<ref name="ANH" /> Additionally, its [[tractor beam targeting array]] with diagonal support beams<ref name="DiD-book">[[:File:ISD blueprints.png|ISD blueprint]] shown in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' [[Star Wars Rebels chapter books|chapter book]] [[Droids in Distress (book)|''Droids in Distress'']]</ref> (located between its two [[deflector shield]] [[Deflector shield generator|generator]] domes)<ref name="ANH" /> was replaced<ref name="BS" /> with a [[communications tower]] with vertical support beams.<ref name="SWCT" />

==History==
===Early years===
{{Quote|We leave for Jabiim at once.|Darth Vader|<ref name="Part V" />}}
The ''Devastator'' was the last of the ''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyers to lumber out of [[Kuat Drive Yards]] before the facility began producing the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class]].<ref name="SWROUVG" /> Despite this, it was still considered a state-of-the-art [[warship]], and had undergone numerous retrofits to keep it comparable with newer [[Starship|ships]] of the line.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

By [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Kenobi Date">{{KenobiDate|IV|the [[attack on Jabiim]]}}</ref> the ''Devastator'' belonged to the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who, together with a [[Unidentified Devastator captain|captain]], used the vessel in an attempt to capture [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and destroy the [[Hidden Path]], an underground resistance network smuggling individuals wanted by the Empire, including [[Force-sensitive]]s. Having tracked the fugitive Jedi to [[Jabiim]], the impressed [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] summoned the Third Sister aboard his flagship to reward her for her insight by elevating her to the rank of [[Grand Inquisitor]]. Not wanting to waste time in a long siege, Vader had the ''Devastator'' launch two [[V-82 dropship]]s along with the [[Inquisitor]] transport [[Scythe (Phi-class)|''Scythe'']], ferrying Reva, to [[Attack on Jabiim|storm]] the Path's hideout.<ref name="Part V" />

[[File:Devastator_pursues_Obi-Wan.png|thumb|left|200px|The ''Devastator'' pursues Obi-Wan Kenobi.]]
After the Path escaped from Jabiim, The ''Devastator'' was used to pursue [[Kawlan Roken]]'s [[Roken's freight transport|freight transport]]. Obi-Wan used a drop ship to draw the Imperials attention away from his allies. [[The Grand Inquisitor]] counselled [[Lord]] Vader to continue their pursuit of the insurgents' ship, but his master countered that that was no ordinary Jedi before ordering the ''Devastator'' to pursue Kenobi's ship. While the Jedi Master headed to the [[Unidentified barren moon|barren moon]] nearby, the Dark Lord ordered his crew to prepare his [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]], vowing to face his old mentor alone.

In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> fourteen [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the ''Devastator'' was the [[flagship]] of [[Lord]] [[Tion (Lord)|Tion]].<ref name="TRB" />

===Again under the Emperor's Fist===
====Scarif====
{{Quote|Sir, shall I begin targeting their fleet?''"<br />"''Lord Vader will handle the fleet.|Hurst Romodi and Wilhuff Tarkin, during the Battle of Scarif|<ref name="Rogue One" />}}
<div style="float: right">[[File:Devastator-GR75Destruction.png|thumb|200px|The ''Devastator'' arrives over Scarif.]]</div>
By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="ROdate">{{FilmDate|RO}}</ref> the ''Devastator'' was again the flagship of Vader, although now captained by [[Shaef Corssin|Shaef Corsin]], and was employed in his struggle against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. As the [[Battle of Scarif]] unfurled, the [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] had [[General]] [[Hurst Romodi]] call in Lord Vader and the ''Devastator'' to deal with the rebel forces. Appearing over the [[planet]] [[Scarif]] near the conclusion of the confrontation, the arrival of the Dark Lord's flagship proceeded to cut off the escape of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]], immediately destroying a [[GR-75 medium transport]] by colliding with it, and disabling several ships, including the Rebel flagship ''[[Profundity]]'' which had received the [[Death Star plans|plans]] for the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. After boarding the enemy craft, the Dark Lord proceeded to cut through [[Rebel trooper]]s who were delivering the schematics. Vader failed, and the plans were delivered to [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], onboard the ''[[Tantive IV]]'', a [[CR90 corvette]] that was docked onboard the ''Profundity''. Fleeing at the last [[second]], the ''Tantive IV'' escaped from the [[Star system|system]] and the Dark Lord pursued the starship in the ''Devastator''.<ref name="Rogue One" />

====Tatooine====
{{Quote|My ship was captured by the ''Devastator''. That was Darth Vader's flagship at the time.|Leia Organa recalls being captured years later to [[Ransolm Casterfo]]|<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>}}
[[File:DevastatorTantiveIV.png|thumb|left|300px|The ''Devastator'' pursues the ''Tantive IV'' over Tatooine.]]
Arriving over [[Tatooine]], a brief [[Secret mission to Tatooine|skirmish]] ensued over the planet, resulting in the [[Star]] Destroyer successfully capturing the ''Tantive IV''. [[Stormtrooper|Imperial troops]] boarded the enemy corvette, and the ''Devastator'' also targeted and destroyed any [[escape pod]]s that were launched in order to ensure none of the Rebels escaped. The sole exception was [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]]'s pod&mdash;which even then was only because Imperial [[Gunner]]s did not detect any life signs in the pod, and dismissed it as a malfunction.<ref name="ANH" />

====The Cylo incident====
Lord Vader used the ''Devastator'' to track [[Doctor]] [[Cylo]]'s [[Cylo's whale fleet|fleet]] of [[Whale-ship]]s in the [[Crushank Nebulae]], courtesy of information from the [[scientist]]'s colleague [[Thlu-Ry]]. As the Star Destroyer approached the [[nebula]]e, many of the abominations fled. This caused the Dark Lord to lead the chase in his [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|TIE Advanced]], supported by many [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE/ln starfighters]]. However, Cylo's flagship broke off from the main group, and attacked the ''Devastator'' with an ion pulse. With the ship disabled, Vader flew his TIE into [[Tulon Voidgazer]]'s ship, just before the [[fleet]] could jump to [[hyperspace]].<ref name="DV 21">[[Darth Vader (2015) 21|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 21]]</ref> They emerged over [[Kuat]], the construction site of the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]'', which was to be under the command of the [[Supreme Commander (Galactic Empire)|Supreme Commander]], [[Grand General]] [[Cassio Tagge]].<ref name="DV 23">[[Darth Vader (2015) 23|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 23]]</ref>

The rogue [[scientist]] took over the [[Super Star Destroyer]],<ref name="DV 23" /> but he was soon [[Death|killed]] by the Dark Lord, along with all of his abominations and staff. Due to [[General]] Tagge's misplaced trust in Doctor Cylo, he was demoted by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], then summarily choked to [[death]] by Lord Vader's invisible [[Force choke|grip]]. The ''Executor'' was given to Vader for defeating the traitorous scientist.<ref name="Darth Vader (2015) 25" />

===New command===
====The Pride of Death Squadron====
<div style="float:right">[[File:SW_Executor_01.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Death Squadron]]</div>
With the transfer of Lord Vader's flag to the ''[[Executor]]'', [[Admiral]] [[Jhared Montferrat]] took command of the ''Devastator''. However, the legendary vessel remained a part of Death [[Squadron]], the personal [[flotilla]] of the [[Emperor]]'s [[Emperor's Fist|Fist]].<ref name="BS" />

[[Task Force 99]], an [[Special Commando Advanced Recon Trooper|elite]] stormtrooper [[squad]], used the ''Devastator'' to interrogate C-3PO after he was captured during the [[siege on Tureen VII]]. Although Lord Vader wanted the [[protocol droid]] destroyed, [[Sergeant]] [[Kreel]] felt it would be in the group's best interests to use the droid as bait to lure the Rebellion to [[Mission to rescue C-3PO|rescue]] him.<ref name="SW 26">[[Star Wars (2015) 26|''Star Wars'' (2015) 26]]</ref>

====Victory on Hoth and Fall at Endor====
[[File:GinaMoonsong-SWZ99.png|thumb|300px|left|The ''Devastator'' was destroyed at Endor after an attack run by Blade Squadron B-wings.]]
Under Admiral Montferrat's command, the ''Devastator'' notably participated at the [[Battle of Hoth]],<ref name="BS" /> a clear Imperial victory,<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> where it destroyed a [[Adon Fox's wife's transport|transport]].<ref name="BS" /> In the aftermath of the battle, Vader sent Death Squadron into the [[Hoth asteroid field]] to look for the ''Millennium Falcon''.<ref name="On The Fron Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> During the search, all ships in Death Squadron were damaged, including the ''Devastator''.<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>

The ''Devastator'', the pride of Death Squadron, met its fate at [[Endor]], during a [[Battle of Endor|battle]] which the Emperor had meant to be the end of the Rebellion. The ship, still commanded by Admiral Montferrat, stood between [[Blade Squadron]], a group of Rebel [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing]] [[Rebel pilot|pilots]] and the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]]. Ultimately, the ship was destroyed when two of those [[pilot]]s, [[Gina Moonsong]] and [[Braylen Stramm]], caused a chain reaction of explosions that led to the systematic demolition of the Star Destroyer and the demise of Admiral Montferrat.<ref name="BS" />

==Commanders and crew==
Since prior to the Jabiim incident and up until the [[destruction of Alderaan]]'s brief aftermath, the ''Devastator'' was the property of [[Darth]] Vader. As of the Battle of [[Hoth]], it was helmed by Jhared Monteferrat, who held the title of admiral by the time the vessel was destroyed during the fight over Endor.<ref name="BS" /> The senior [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[officer]] onboard was [[Captain]] [[Ronnadam]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />
