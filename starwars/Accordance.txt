The '''''Accordance''''' was an [[MC75 Star Cruiser]] in service to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] alongside its sister [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Temperance]]''.<ref name="Temperance" /> Manufactured by [[Mon Calamari Shipyards]]<ref name="HC 39" /> and active during the [[Galactic Civil War]], it was part of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Navy]] and defended the borders of the [[Hosnian system]]. At the same [[time]] as the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Temperance" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Note" /> it fought a [[Battle in the Hosnian system|battle]] alongside the ''Temperance'' against an [[Unidentified Imperial armada|Imperial armada]] that had launched a surprise attack against the [[Core Worlds]]. The ''Accordance'' was destroyed by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces during the battle, although its surviving crew was rescued by the ''Temperance'', which then defeated the Imperials.<ref name="Temperance" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Accordance'' was mentioned in the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry of the ''Temperance'' on [[StarWars.com]]. The entry was published by [[June 19]], [[2020]]<ref name="Temperance" /> in the lead up to the release of the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]'' by [[Electronic Arts]] that heavily featured the ''Temperance''.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>
