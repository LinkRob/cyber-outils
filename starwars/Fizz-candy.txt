'''Fizz-candy''' was a type of [[candy]] that featured frothy bubbles. At the onset of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]<ref name="LOTJ" /> in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> [[Hetzal system]] [[scantech]] [[Merven Getter]] thought to himself that, at the speed the pieces of [[hyperspace]] debris entering the [[Star system|system]] were moving, anything they hit would be destroyed regardless of the items in their path being [[asteroid]]s, [[starship]]s, or fizz-candy bubbles.<ref name="LOTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fizz-candy was mentioned in [[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi|''Light of the Jedi'']], a [[2021]] novel written by [[Charles Soule]]<ref name="LOTJ" /> and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> Prior to its release, the candy's mention was included in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the novel on [[November 12]], [[2020]].<ref name="Excerpt">{{SW|url=news/light-of-the-jedi-exclusive-excerpt|text=Explore ''Light of the Jedi'' in ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' - Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
