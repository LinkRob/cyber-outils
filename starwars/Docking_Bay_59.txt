'''Docking Bay 59''' was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] located in [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[water]] supply drop was scheduled to take place in the docking bay. A client hired the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] to steal the shipment.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Docking Bay 59 appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. Though unlabeled on the game's map, it is identified in one of the game's contract quests.<ref name="Outlaws" />
