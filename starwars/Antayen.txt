'''Antayen''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Human/Legends|Human]] employee of [[Czerka Arms/Legends|Czerka Arms]] during the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]]. He was the [[Director/Legends|director]] of the team that worked on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] excavating [[The Imprisoned One's mind prison]] and also included [[Doctor/Legends|Doctors]] [[Di-go]], [[Lerek]], [[Soprin]] and [[Grommik Kurthson]]. Eventually their tampering with the ancient device proved disastrous, as despite all their safety precautions [[The Imprisoned One]] broke free and killed or enslaved all personnel present at Czerka base at the time. Antayen was among those cybernetically transformed to serve the ancient [[Rakata/Legends|Rakata's]] will and remained inside the old Czerka base for decades to come. Eventually, the base was rediscovered in [[3643 BBY]] and an explorer of the base killed Antayen.
