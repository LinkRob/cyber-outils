By some point between the [[Standard year|years]] [[402 BBY]] and [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''war''' was fought between a [[Unidentified Dimok baron|baron]] from the location [[Dimok]] and the location [[Ripoblus]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. During the war, the [[baron]] acquired two orphaned [[Force-sensitive]] [[Youngling|children]] as part of a plan to cultivate [[army]] [[general]]s to fight the war. The [[Jedi Master]] [[Char-Ryl-Roy]] interrogated the baron regarding the location of the abducted children, forcing him to confess within an [[Standard hour|hour]] by [[Force power|using]] [[the Force]] to close the [[Viewport|windows]] and raise the [[temperature]] in the room. The children were recovered and sent to the [[moon]] [[Jedha]]. Master Char-Ryl-Roy's [[Padawan]], [[Enya Keen]], witnessed the interrogation and,<ref name="Convergence" /> in 382 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> recalled it after she used the same technique in an attempt to force a confession while interrogating [[Viceroy]] [[Ferrol]] for his connections to the [[Children of E'ronoh]] [[terrorist]] group on the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]].<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dimok-Ripoblus war was mentioned the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a [[2022]] novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" /> The conflict originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as the [[Sepan Civil War]] created for [[Totally Games]]' [[1994]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], [[Star Wars: TIE Fighter (video game)|''Star Wars: TIE Fighter'']].<ref name="TIE fighter">[[Star Wars: TIE Fighter (video game)|''Star Wars: TIE Fighter'']]</ref>
