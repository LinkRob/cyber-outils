'''Ewokese''' was the lively [[language]] spoken by the furry, diminutive [[Ewok]]s who lived on the forest [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. It was mutually intelligible with the [[Yuzzum (language)|language]] of the [[Yuzzum]], and contained more than three dozen words for [[conifer]]s that they inhabited. The most common of those terms was "[[life tree]]s."<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

When the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|strike team]] came to Endor to destroy the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star's]] [[SLD-26 planetary shield generator|shield generator]], the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] communicated with the Ewoks using Yuzzum,<ref name="CL 2016" /> and managed to translate the Ewokese into [[Galactic Basic Standard]] so the Ewok named [[Wicket Wystri Warrick|Wicket]] was able to guide the Rebels to the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Death Star's shield generator|bunker]].<ref name="ROTJ" />

Ewokese had a word to describe those who were not Ewoks, which literally translated to "nakeds" in [[Galactic Basic Standard]].<ref name="Last Shot" />

The [[human]] [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] learned the language from a salvaged [[Starship|ship]] [[computer]] on [[Jakku]], but had not found an opportunity to use it.<ref name="AB Update" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ewokese language first appeared in the [[1983]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], the last installment of the [[original trilogy]] of ''[[Star Wars]]''.<ref name="ROTJ" /> It was first identified as "Ewokese" in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (novelization)|novelization]] of the film.<ref name="Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (novelization)">[[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (novelization)|''Star Wars Episode VI: Return of the Jedi'' novelization]]</ref> 

It was developed by [[Ben Burtt]], sound designer at [[Industrial Light & Magic|ILM]], and was a mix of words imitating [[Wikipedia:Tibetan language|Tibetan]], [[Wikipedia:Kalmyk Oirat|Kalmyk Oirat]] and [[Wikipedia:Lakota language|Lakota]].<ref name="guide">[[Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide (real-life book)|''Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide'']]</ref> [[Wikipedia:Languages of Africa|African]] and [[Wikipedia:Oceanic languages|South-Pacific]] languages also influenced Ewokese.<ref name="BTM">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref> In addition, Burtt and actor [[Anthony Daniels]] later used and even invented their own terms in the language when doing the scene where C-3PO was reciting the story of the protagonists's against the Empire in [[Yuzzum (language)|Yuzzum]].<ref name="comment" />

Burtt firstly decided to make Ewokese based on Tibetan and Kalmuck after hearing it in a documentary. After interviewing two Tibetan speakers, a father and son,<ref name="guide" /> he got [[Kosi Unkov]], an 80-year-old Mongol woman to speak tales of her people through her native Kalmyk.<ref name="comment" /><ref name="guide" /><ref name="SW-Jedi at 40 &#124; Ben Burtt and Randy Thom on Crafting Ewokese, Jabba's Voice, and the Rancor's Roar">{{SW|url=news/jedi-at-40-ben-burtt-randy-thom|text=''Jedi'' at 40 &#124; Ben Burtt and Randy Thom on Crafting Ewokese, Jabba's Voice, and the Rancor's Roar|author=Brandon Wainderdi}}</ref> He then recorded the sounds, with the various actors imitating various portions of the recording.<ref name="comment">Ben Burtt in the {{Film|VI}} commentary</ref> Burtt credits Kosi Unkov, Lama Kunga, Sr. and Jr., M.K. Nepali, Khendup and Ditry Daza as the principal contributors of the Ewokese sounds, with [[Adeal Crooms]] as the voice of [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]].<ref name="guide" />

Ewokese babbling, chanting and singing, including the "[[Ewok Celebration]]," were performed by a subset of the Oakland Inspirational Choir.<ref name="guide" />
