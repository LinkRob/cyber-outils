'''GO-series dealer droids''' were a model of [[dealer droid]] used in [[the galaxy]]. In [[34 ABY]]<ref name="TFA VD Date">The [[droid]]s are being carried during the events of the [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of the movie to 30 [[Standard year|years]] after [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], which happen in [[4 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref>, [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] carried several deactivate GO-series dealer droids aboard their [[bulk freighter]], the ''[[Eravana]]''.<ref name="Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
