The '''"Surefire" Safety Sensor''' was a model of [[Marked-Target Firing Lock]], a type of ranged [[weapon]] attachment that mitigated collateral damage with the use of [[sensor]]s, manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]]. It cost 900 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to purchase.<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Surefire" Safety Sensor was first mentioned in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']],<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> a [[2017]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="A Fistful of Credits">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/2/2/a-fistful-of-credits|text=A Fistful of Credits}}</ref>
