'''''Back the Imperial Forces''''' was a [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] for the [[Imperial Military]]. It featured an [[Imperial officer]] to the left, a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in full uniform to the right, and a [[Stormtrooper]] behind them and standing in the center prominently, with the words "Back the Imperial Forces" in red [[High Galactic]] font.<ref name="Propaganda" />

{{Imp-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
This poster was first released on [[StarWars.com]] as part of an online promotional campaign for the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' animated series in [[February]] [[2014]].<ref name="SW-Star Wars Rebels Imperial Propaganda Posters">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-rebels-imperial-propaganda-posters|text=Star Wars Rebels Imperial Propaganda Posters}}</ref>
