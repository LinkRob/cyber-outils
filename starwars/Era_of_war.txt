The '''era of war''' was a [[time]] period in [[the galaxy]] where the light of the [[Jedi Order]] had long since passed. During this time, the ruler of [[Hy Izlan]], [[Margrave]] [[Juro]], began a quest to restore the [[Jedi]] by seeking out [[Padawan|students]] and also reforging [[lightsaber]]s. However, the [[Sith|Sith Order]]<ref name="The Ninth Jedi" />&mdash;the ancient enemy of the Jedi<ref name="The Siege of Lothal">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref>&mdash;was also active and plotted to conquer the [[galaxy]].<ref name="The Ninth Jedi" />
