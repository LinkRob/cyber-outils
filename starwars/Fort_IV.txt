The '''Fort IV'''  ([[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]: フォートⅣ) was a wall-mounted [[Autoturret/Legends|autoturret]] used by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] to protect the corridors and walkways in their [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|first]] [[Death Star/Legends|Death Star]]. They were capable of detecting and blasting intruders.<ref name="FamiMaga">{{BookCite|author=|container=Star Wars|title=Family Computer Magazine|number=1987 #22|publisher=Tokuma Shoten|date=December 4, 1987|issn=|link=https://archive.org/details/zines_famimaga_1987_22}}</ref>

[[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], just prior to [[Rescue of Princess Leia/Legends|rescuing]] Princess [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]] in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]], encountered these droids while exploring the Death Star.<ref name="sw87" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fort IV appeared in the 1987 [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] [[Star Wars (1987 video game)|''Star Wars'' game]].<ref name="sw87" /> Their name was revealed in the [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese language]] guide of the game serialized in Family Computer Magazine, in which it was rendered as フォートⅣ (''Fōto IV'') in [[Wikipedia:Katakana|katakana]] syllabary.<ref name="FamiMaga" />
