'''Abel Drongite''' was the proprietor of the [[Pyloon's Saloon|saloon]] in [[Rambler's Reach Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]] at some point prior<ref name="Survivor" /> to [[9 BBY]].<ref name="JS-Date" /> Local prospectors [[Gulu]] and [[Gido]] recalled to [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] that Drongite would let customers go hundreds of cycles without settling their tabs and allow patrons to [[sleep]] on the floor if necessary. He ran a [[Smuggler|smuggling]] ring on the planet and had a deal with the [[Bedlam Raiders]] that they would receive a cut of any profits he received. However, on one occasion, Drongite shorted the Raiders their fee and was [[Death|killed]] when one of the gang members shot him with their [[blaster]]. After his death, ownership of the saloon was picked up by the [[bartender droid]] [[M-6NK]] and the [[Latero]] [[Greez Dritus]], who named it after his great-grandmother [[Pyloon]].<ref name="Survivor" />
