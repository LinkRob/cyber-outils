'''''From Star Wars to Indiana Jones: The Best of the Lucasfilm Archives''''' is a predominantly photo-based reference book that chronologically surveys the [[Lucasfilm Archives]]'s highlights: props, models, storyboards, backdrops, and sketches, spanning the [[Original trilogy|''Star Wars'' Trilogy]] as well as the ''[[Indiana Jones]]'' Trilogy. This book is very similar to [[Star Wars Chronicles|''Star Wars: Chronicles'']], also published by the Chronicle Books, but is less indepth.

In the United Kingdom, the book was published by [[Virgin Books]] as '''''The Star Wars Archives'''''.
