The '''Erso family''' was a family of [[human]]s active from before the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Catalyst" /> and the [[Battle of Scarif]]<ref name="Rogue One" /> who lived on [[planet]]s such as [[Grange]], [[Coruscant]] and [[Lah'mu]]. The earliest members of the family were a human [[Galen Walton Erso's father|man]] and a [[Galen Walton Erso's mother|woman]] who had one son: [[Galen Walton Erso]]. With his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Lyra Erso|Lyra]], Galen had a daughter named [[Jyn Erso|Jyn]].<ref name="Catalyst" />

When Galen was [[Capture of Galen Erso|taken]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] to work on the [[Project Stardust|Death Star project]] by the [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]] [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]], Lyra was [[Death|killed]] and Jyn went into hiding with the [[Partisans]]' leader [[Saw Gerrera]]. Ultimately, Jyn sacrificed her life at [[Scarif]] when she stole the [[Death Star plans]] to give them to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] before being killed by the [[Death Star]]'s [[superweapon]]. As Jyn never had descendants, the family line ended with her.<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Behind the scenes==
In early versions of the ''Rogue One'' story, the matriarch of the family was a surviving Jedi, and there was additionally a younger brother for Jyn to look after.<ref name="AoRO">''[[The Art of Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
