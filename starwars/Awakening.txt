The '''Awakening''' was the name used by the [[Unidentified Cianap species|people]] of [[Cianap]] to refer to the waking of the [[Ender]]. The Awakening followed the [[Slumber]], the [[time]] period while the Ender was asleep. [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] caused one Awakening to happen when crash-landing his [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|TIE Advanced]] onto the Ender, but successfully [[Death|killed]] it.<ref name="Dark Visions 1">[[Vader - Dark Visions 1|''Vader - Dark Visions'' 1]]</ref>
