'''Devon Izara's lightsaber''' was the personal [[Color|blue]]-bladed [[lightsaber]] of the [[Twi'lek]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]]. The saber's [[hilt]] was made of black and silver colored [[metal]], and covered by a [[leather]] grip.

==Description==
Producing a [[Color|blue]] blade,<ref name="MSL S1C4"/> Devon Izara's lightsaber was the personal [[lightsaber]] of the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]],<ref name="Legacy saber" /> a [[Twi'lek]] who made use of the lightsaber over the course of the [[Republic Era]] and subsequent [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="MSL S1C3"/> An adept fighter, Izara was capable of using the weapon to defend herself<ref name="Devon DB">{{Databank|devon-izara|Devon Izara}}</ref> when needed.<ref name="meet the cast">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-cast|text=Meet the Cast of ''Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord''}}</ref> The lightsaber [[hilt]] was made of a black and silver colored [[metal]], which was then covered by a [[leather]] grip.<ref name="Legacy saber" /> 

==History==
[[File:Devon_lightsaber_unignited.png|thumb|left|200px|Devon Izara holds her unignited lightsaber on Janix.]]
During the early [[Imperial Era]], Izara wielded her lightsaber on the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet]] of [[Janix]]<ref name="Shadow of Maul 1">[[Shadow of Maul 1|''Shadow of Maul'' 1]]</ref> whilst on the run from the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Legacy saber" /> She was forced to keep her lightsaber hidden, much like her nature as a Jedi.<ref name="MSL S1C3" />

When Izara was arrested for shoplifting and imprisoned by Janix's [[Tactical Defense Force]], and subsequently [[raid on the Janix Civil Defense HQ|kidnapped]] by the renegade former [[Sith]] [[crime lord]] [[Maul]], her [[Jedi Master]], [[Eeko-Dio Daki]], kept her saber safe for her.<ref name="MSL S1C4">{{MSL|Chapter 4: Pride and Vengeance}}</ref> As she attempted to escape [[Maul's safe house]], Izara encountered the [[Zabrak]] himself, who tried to engage her in a [[Cassius tea|tea]] ceremony, only for the teenage Twi'lek to snatch up his [[Maul's lightsaber|lightsaber]] with [[the Force]]. Izara's wielding of the red-bladed weapon prompted Maul to question how it felt, commenting that she must have kept her own saber hidden for some time.<ref name="MSL S1C3"/>

Izara managed to make it past Maul and escape, and made her way back to her Master and their hideout in the streets of [[Janix city]]. There, Daki handed her back her lightsaber, and she took it, thanking him for keeping it safe. Izara then put it down, and discussed her recent experience, convincing Daki it was their responsibility to help stop Maul. Tipping off Tactical Defence Force [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]] as to Maul's location, the two Jedi also converged on his safe house.<ref name="MSL S1C4"/>

[[File:Devon duels Maul.png|thumb|right|250px|Devon Izara uses her blade to block Maul from striking down Master Eeko-Dio Daki]]
There, as Lawson and his [[Tactical Defense Droid]] backup fought [[Shadow Collective|Maul's mercenaries]] after negotiations for a peaceful solution failed, Daki and Izara did the same against the [[Dark side of the Force|darksider]] himself. The pair clashed their blue single-bladed lightsabers against Maul's red [[double-bladed lightsaber]], dueling him, but the powerful Zabrak had the upper hand. Just as it was about to stike down a wounded Daki, Maul's blade was blocked by Izara's. Pressing his blade against hers, Maul complimented Izara on her devotion to her master, and then fled due to approaching police reinforcements. Moments later, Brander Lawson arrived, and Izara wearily reignited her weapon, but the lawman allowed the Jedi to leave before his backup arrived. She kept it on her [[belt]] as she helped her injured Master through the streets of Janix.<ref name="MSL S1C4"/>

Later, she used the blue-bladed weapon against [[TK stormtrooper]]s firing from a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]] [[Gunship]] to defend Lawson and his son [[Rylee Lawson|Rylee]],<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|oonACDq89BY|Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord &#124; Official Teaser Trailer &#124; Streaming April 6 on Disney+}}</ref> and to duel the [[Inquisitor]]s [[Marrok|First Brother]] and the [[Eleventh Brother]] aboard a [[train]] alongside Maul.<ref name="Official">{{SWYouTube|DkVepshZhGc|Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord &#124; Official Trailer &#124; Streaming April 6 on Disney+}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Devon's lightsaber hilt design is incredible, and we know fans will be thrilled to wield her lightsaber for themselves.|Cody Hampton, Senior Manager of Merchandise Strategy and product developer for the Legacy Lightsaber line|<ref name="Legacy saber" />}}
[[File:DevonIzaraLightsaber-lava.png|thumb|right|250px|Product image of Devon Izara’s Legacy Lightsaber]]
Devon Izara's lightsaber first appeared in the teaser trailer for ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'', released on [[January 22]], [[2026]].<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|oonACDq89BY|Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord &#124; Official Teaser Trailer &#124; Streaming April 6 on Disney+}}</ref> It was first identified on [[March 26]] in an article on [[StarWars.com]]. The article revealed Izara's lightsaber was the latest lightsaber of the "Limited Edition Legacy Lightsaber" series, with a limited global run of 3000 units. The replica weapon has a silver and black hilt, covered with a "distinctive" weathered brown faux leather grip. The hilt's switch activates "realistic" lightsaber noises and illuminates the separately-sold attachable blade with a "brilliant" blue light.<ref name="Legacy saber" />
