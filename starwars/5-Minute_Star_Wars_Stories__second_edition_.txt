'''''5-Minute Star Wars Stories''''' is a hardcover anthology collecting twelve ''[[Star Wars]]'' children's stories. The title serves as a refresh of the [[2015]] [[5-Minute Star Wars Stories|collection of the same name]] with new art and some new stories. Although it was scheduled for release in the United States on [[November 3]], [[2020]], it never came out and has not been officially confirmed as cancelled by publisher [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]. Internationally, the book was fully translated and released in a number of countries. The English translation for some of the stories were released in ''[[Star Wars: Advent Calendar: A Storybook Library]]'' and later fully in ''[[Star Wars 5-Minute Stories Audio Collection: Volume 1]]'' and ''[[Star Wars 5-Minute Stories Audio Collection: Volume 2|Volume 2]]''. 

As part of the 50th anniversary of ''[[Star Wars]]'' in 2027, the title will be released in English hardcover format on [[January 5]], though it will only include nine of the twelve stories.

===Original release===
''A refresh of 2015's'' 5-Minute Star Wars Stories'', this collection features twelve all-new tales that retell iconic scenes from Episodes I-IX.''<ref name="Edelweiss" />

===2027 reprint===
''A collection of nine stories from across the Star Wars galaxy that can each be read in 5 minutes or less.''

''Celebrate the 50th anniversary of'' Star Wars ''with this exciting 5-Minute Stories collection that follows the adventures of Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia, and the rest of the rebel forces as they fight to protect the galaxy. Each of the nine tales in this sturdy hardcover book features vibrant illustrations and is ideal for reading aloud in just five minutes—a great length for new readers and perfect for bedtime, story time, or anytime!''

==Development==
[[File:5-Minute_Star_Wars_Stories_2020_poster.jpg|thumb|left|150px|A promotional poster for ''5-Minute Star Wars Stories'' released for ''Star Wars'' Reads]]
''5-Minute Star Wars Stories'' was first revealed in an official book listing published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on the publishing industry repository [[Wikipedia:Above_the_Treeline#Edelweiss+|Edelweiss+]]. In the listing, the publisher revealed that the title would follow the format of the [[2015]] [[5-Minute Star Wars Stories|collection of the same name]], but would feature all-new stories from the [[Star Wars saga|''Star Wars'' saga]]. The book was listed as due for release on [[November 3]], [[2020]].<ref name="Edelweiss" />

On [[September 10]], [[2020]], [[StarWars.com]] revealed the upcoming slate for that year's [[Star Wars Reads|''Star Wars'' Reads]], an annual event fostering reading in children and ''[[Star Wars]]'' fans of all ages. In the announcement, StarWars.com released a poster and cover image for ''5-Minute Star Wars Stories'', revealing that the book would mimic the art style of the ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]'' animated micro series.<ref name="SW Reads">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-reads|text=Celebrate ''Star Wars'' Reads This October! (2020)|oldversion=https://archive.vn/hlSVt}}</ref>

Despite the official listing and announcement by StarWars.com, ''5-Minute Star Wars Stories'' was never published in the United States and missed its designated November 3 release date. Internationally, the book saw release in a number of languages. ''5-Minute Star Wars Stories'' would later receive two audiobook releases in English, ''[[Star Wars 5-Minute Stories Audio Collection: Volume 1]]'' and ''[[Star Wars 5-Minute Stories Audio Collection: Volume 2|Volume 2]]''.<ref name="Audio Collection 1">''[[Star Wars 5-Minute Stories Audio Collection: Volume 1]]''</ref><ref name="Audio Collection 2">''[[Star Wars 5-Minute Stories Audio Collection: Volume 2]]''</ref>
