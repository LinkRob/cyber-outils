'''First Order Captain's Medallions''', also known as '''First Order Officer's transit data-medallion''' or '''First Order code chip''', were transit [[data-medallion]]s<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> manufactured by [[MerenData]]<ref name="2024 Encyclopedia" /> that granted access to anywhere in [[First Order]] territory. These medallions emitted an encrypted subspace code which authorized interdiction passage though First Order blockades.<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[Zorii Bliss]] had one and wanted to use it to escape to the [[Colonies]], but decided to give it to [[Poe Dameron]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] to help him save [[Chewbacca]], who had been captured by the First Order.<ref name="Episode IX" />
