'''C1-MNST4''' was a [[C1-series astromech droid]] that was constructed using spare parts of other droids on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The droid was colored in [[Color|green]], purple, brown and black.<ref name="Toy" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Once we landed on a monster theme, we knew the C-series droid was a great fit due to the arms looking just like bolts.|Cody Hampton|<ref name="Alive" />}}
C1-MNST4 is an action figure from the ''[[Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|Star Wars: Droid Factory]]'' line made available exclusively from [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] Parks and [[Wikipedia:Disney Store|shopDisney]] for the [[2023]] [[Wikipedia:Halloween|Halloween]] season, first made available in July 2023.<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2023/07/star-wars-halloween-droid-figures-grogu-plush|text=Star Wars Halloween Droid Action Figure and Grogu Pumpkin Plush Available Now at Disney’s Hollywood Studios|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20230807152055}}</ref> Its colors and details are inspired by Frankenstein's monster.<ref name="Alive" />
