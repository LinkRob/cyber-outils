'''Espresso Martinis''' were an [[alcohol]]ic [[cocktail]] that were [[Color|brown]] in color and consumable by [[human]]s. The drink was available to guests on board the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''<ref name="Cruiser" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> On board the [[starship]], it was served as a "morning concoction"<ref name="Cruiser" /> in the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]] during breakfast and lunch,<ref name="Crown" /> and in the [[Sublight Lounge]].<ref name="Cruiser" />

==Behind the scenes==
Espresso Martinis were available at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] [[Wikipedia:Espresso martini|beverage]] is made with [[Wikipedia:Vodka|vodka]], [[Wikipedia:Cream liqueur|cream liqueur]], [[Wikipedia:Vanilla|vanilla]], and [[Wikipedia:Espresso|espresso]].<ref name="Cruiser" /> Espresso Martinis were last served at the hotel during the [[September 28]]&ndash;[[September 30|30]] voyage of [[2023]], the final voyage before the closure of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser.<ref name="Official website">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref>
