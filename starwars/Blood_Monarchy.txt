The '''Blood Monarchy''', also referred to as the '''Blood Court''', was a constitutional [[monarchy]] that governed the [[planet]] [[Thisspias]] and its native [[Thisspiasian]]s. It was led by a ruler known as the [[Blood Monarch]] and was loyal to the [[Galactic Republic]], having joined in its very early founding. The Blood Court's hereditary line dated back 20,000 [[Standard year|years]] prior to when it joined the Republic.<ref name="Unlimited Power" />
