'''Corin Ferro''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as an [[officer]] in the [[Imperial Officer Corps]] in the early [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Following a conspiracy against [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], Ferro, along with four other officers, were [[Death|killed]], with Vader stating that although he had suspicions on the conspirators, he would always survive.

==Biography==
Corin Ferro was an [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial officer|officer]] in the [[Imperial Officer Corps]]. Following an [[Assassination attempts on Darth Vader|attempted]] [[assassin]]ation on [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on [[Cabarria]] by [[bounty hunter]]s, Vader ordered [[Darth Vader's droid|his droid]] to conduct an analysis of the origins of the kill contract as they were arriving on [[Coruscant]]. The [[droid]] stated that the contract came from the highest levels of the [[Imperial Executive Building]], including the offices of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. The droid continued, and stated that there was a 97% probability that the signal originated from there, and that there was no evidence of [[Slicer|slicing]].<ref name="Rule of Five II" />

Vader then requested a list of individuals with access codes of Imperial systems that were above [[Level 1A1]]. On the list provided, Ferro's name was indexed there, along with the names of [[Joon Strephi]], [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], [[Cassio Tagge]], [[Jer Croteau]], [[Tomas Azoras]], and [[Zorta Bingan]]. Following a second assassination attempt in Coruscant's orbit that resulted in Vader and the [[Ninth Sister]] crashing [[Vader's starship|their starship]], Vader met with the Emperor and [[Meditation|meditated]] and discovered that [[Barokki]] was also involved, an officer he had [[force choke]]d some [[time]] before, and he later approached the Emperor with a solution.<ref name="Rule of Five II" />

Ferro, along with many other officers, were called into an [[Coruscant auditorium|auditorium]] on Coruscant. Vader believed that the conspiracy against him was because the officers did not understand why Vader was [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] second in command and were likely jealous. Following a brief speech by Emperor Palpatine that elaborated that Vader was his direct subordinate and spoke and command for him, Vader "randomly" selected Ferro, Strephi, Azoras, Barokki, and Bingan and [[Death|killed]] them through [[the Force]], and stated that although he had suspicions on the conspirators, he would always survive. Of those killed, Barokki was one of two officers that were involved in the conspiracy, the other, who was an individual with two missing [[Limb|fingers]] was not selected and thus spared.<ref name="Rule of Five II" />
