The '''"Outreach" Target Sensor''' was a model of [[rocket guidance system]], a custom [[weapon]] attachment and guidance system for [[Wrist rocket|micro-rockets]], manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] It was sold for 50 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Outreach" Target Sensor was first mentioned in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']],<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> a [[2017]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="A Fistful of Credits">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/2/2/a-fistful-of-credits|text=A Fistful of Credits}}</ref>
