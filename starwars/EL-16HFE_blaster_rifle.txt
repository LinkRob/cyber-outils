The '''EL-16HFE blaster rifle''',<ref name="Visual" /> also known as the '''EL-16 heavy field blaster''',<ref name="Destiny">{{Destiny|set=Spark of Hope|cardname=EL-16 Heavy Field Blaster|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/11090}}</ref> was a [[blaster rifle]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]].<ref name="Visual" />

==Characteristics==
[[File:Resistance_trooper-SWFC.png|thumb|left|200px|A Resistance trooper wields an EL-16HFE]]
Used by the [[New Republic]], the EL-16HFE was the Heavy Field Edition of the smaller [[EL-16 blaster rifle]], though it was outdated surplus by the [[time]] of the [[Resistance]]'s fight [[Cold War|against]] the [[First Order]] thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Visual" /> It was larger than the standard version and included a [[stock]] and a longer [[barrel]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> The rifle featured a stock with gas reservoir, a [[macroscope]] adjustment control, and a removable galven-circuitry barrel tip.<ref name="Visual" />

==History==
After its usage by the New Republic, the EL-16 HFE would go onto be used by the [[Resistance Army]]<ref name="Visual" /> during the [[Cold War]] against the [[First Order]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> although by that time the weapon was considered outdated surplus.<ref name="Visual" /> [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] of the [[Resistance Starfighter Corps]] [[Poe's blaster rifle|used]] a EL-16HFE during the [[Attack on Tuanul|attack]] on the [[Sacred Villages|village]] [[Tuanul]] on [[Jakku]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> It, alongside the smaller version, were later used during the [[Battle of Crait]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />
