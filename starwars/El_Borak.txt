'''El Borak''' was the friend of the [[mutant]] [[Wolf/Legends|Wolf]], [[Wilk (wolf)|Wilk]]. The two roamed [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] together, until they came in contact with [[Doctor/Legends|Doctor]] [[Ames]]. The two friends allowed themselves to be recruited by the doctor into helping him complete an insane quest.
