{{Quote|When I was growing up, before we moved to the Undercity, my dad used to plant bebatek seeds in soil boxes in the little alley behind our house. I would always try to water them&mdash;you know, I wanted to help! But he said, ''Sian, you have to let the galaxy do its work''. And sure enough, it rained for the next three days straight. And we had a tiny harvest of bebatek peppers a month later.|Sian Holt, to Emerick Caphtor|<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />}}
'''Bebatek peppers''' were a type of [[pepper]] grown from bebatek [[seed]]s. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Sian Holt's father|father]] of the [[human]] [[Youngling|child]] [[Sian Holt]] planted some [[Vegetable|pepper]] seeds in soil boxes in the alley behind their [[Sian Holt's family|family's]] [[Sian Holt's house|house]]. Though Holt often wished to [[water]] the [[plant]]s, at one point,<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> her<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Parent|father]] told her to let [[the galaxy]]'s natural way of things take its turn. For the next three [[Standard day|days]] after the conversation, rain watered the pepper seeds, and Holt's family was able to harvest a small batch of bebatek peppers a [[Standard month|month]] later.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="IssuesDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 4|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 4]] to [[230 BBY]].</ref> Holt&mdash;now an adult working as a [[detective]]&mdash;recounted her father's lesson about the bebatek peppers to the [[Jedi Master]] [[Emerick Caphtor]]. Caphtor attempted to relate the story to their ongoing [[spy]] work against the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]], wondering if the operatives they had sent out were the seeds, soil, or peppers.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bebatek peppers were mentioned in the [[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 4|fourth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Trail of Shadows]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], published by [[Marvel Comics]],<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> and released on [[January 26]], [[2022]]<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-the-high-republic-trail-of-shadows-4-exclusive-preview|text=The Jedi Rush to Face the Nihil in Marvel's ''Star Wars: The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' #4 &ndash; Exclusive Preview}}</ref> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia series' [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> Prior to the issue's release, a page mentioning the peppers was included in a [[StarWars.com]] [https://web.archive.org/web/20220119140728/https://www.starwars.com/news/marvel-star-wars-the-high-republic-trail-of-shadows-4-exclusive-preview preview] for the issue on [[January 19]] of that year.<ref name="Preview" />
