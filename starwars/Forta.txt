The '''Forta'''<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> were a group who were at odds with the [[Lerall]] during the [[Republic Era]]. [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] of the [[Jedi Order]] negotiated a treaty between the two groups.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Forta were first mentioned in the audiobook ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> The spelling of their name was given in the [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script]] of the audiobook.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" />
