'''Atari''' is a company founded in [[1972]] as '''Atari, Inc.'''. In [[1984]], Atari Inc. was split in '''Atari Corporation''' and '''Atari Games.''' It again changed, becoming '''Atari Interactive''' (a division of Hasbro Interactive) in [[1998]].

Atari produced several [[Arcade game/Legends|arcade games]] for the Star Wars universe, including ''[[Star Wars: The Arcade Game]]'' ([[1983]]), [[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (video game)|Star Wars: Return of the Jedi]] ([[1984]]), and [[The Empire Strikes Back (arcade game)|''The Empire Strikes Back'']] ([[1985]]).

''[[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi: Death Star Battle]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Arena]]'', ''[[Star Wars: The Arcade Game]]'', and [[Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back (video game)|''Star Wars'': ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] were also published by [[Parker Brothers]] for Atari's home consoles.
