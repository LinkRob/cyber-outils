The '''Cioral system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], which was situated in the [[Unknown Regions]] and was in the [[Standard Galactic Grid|grid square]] F-8.<ref name="GMA" /> [[Cioral]] was an orbiting terrestrial object<ref name="Greater Good" /> in the star system and was the stronghold of the [[Chiss]] [[Xodlak family]].<ref name="GMA" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The Cioral system was first mentioned in [[Canon]] in the "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix posted on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="GMA" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Jason">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map...|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the Cioral system was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
