The '''Dai Bendu temple guardians''' were a group that guarded the [[Dai Bendu Monastery]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet]] of [[Kijimi]]. During the rise of criminality in the [[city]], the thieves once outrun the temple guardians, hiding in the shadows of zigzagging alleyways of the [[Thieves' Quarter]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
