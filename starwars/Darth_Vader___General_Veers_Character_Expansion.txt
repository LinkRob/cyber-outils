The '''Darth Vader & General Veers Character Expansion''' ('''SWQ103''') is an expansion to the tabletop game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. It contains miniatures of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]], and was released on [[October 3]], [[2025]] as part of Atomic Mass Games' overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
''Darth Vader is the living embodiment of the Empire's might. The mere presence of the Dark Lord of the Sith inspires terror in his foes and unquestioning submission in his allies. Wielding this fear as skillfully as his lightsaber, nothing can stand against Vader as he carries out the Emperor's bidding.''

''General Veers brings ironclad Imperial discipline and a tactical cunning to the Empire’s forces. Veers's skill at mechanized combat tactics makes him an ideal choice to lead aggressive frontal assaults on enemy prepared defenses.

''This Character Expansion provides two character miniatures to Star Wars: Legion.''

==Contents==
*2 miniatures:
**[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]
**[[General]] [[Maximilian Veers|Veers]]
*2 Unit Cards
**Darth Vader &mdash; The Emperor's Apprentice
**General Veers &mdash; Master Tactician
*6 Command Cards
**Implacable (Darth Vader)
**New Ways to Motivate Them (Darth Vader)
**Master of Evil (Darth Vader)
**Maximum Firepower (General Veers)
**Evasive Maneuvers (General Veers)
**Imperial Discipline (General Veers)
*2 Upgrade Cards
