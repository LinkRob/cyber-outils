The '''''Freedom's Run''''' was an [[MC80 Star Cruiser]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] that served in the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Although other [[Star Cruiser]]s such as ''[[Home One]]'', ''[[Nautilian]]'', ''[[Liberty]]'', and ''[[Amalthea]]'' were confirmed for the [[Battle of Endor|assault]] on [[Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], the ''Freedom's Run''{{'s}} status was tentative, as was the [[cruiser]] ''[[Restoration]]''{{'s}}. [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] listed the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Navy]] [[starship]]s and their condition in a report to [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]], and was included in ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.

==History==
[[File:Alliance-Fleet-scrambled-Endor.png|thumb|left|300px|Prior to the Battle of Endor (pictured), the ''Freedom's Run''{{'s}} availability was unconfirmed for the assault.]]
The ''Freedom's Run'' was an [[MC80 Star Cruiser]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] that was active during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and manufactured by [[Mon Calamari Shipyards]].<ref name="HC 39" /> Shortly prior to the [[Battle of Endor]], a [[moon]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Moddell sector]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Alliance [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] sent a report to [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]] in regards to [[fleet]] composition and strength. Ackbar had drawn in critical fleet elements in preparation for the [[Rebel Alliance Navy]]'s assemblage above the<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Outer Rim [[planet]]<ref name="Princess Leia 3">[[Princess Leia 3|''Princess Leia'' 3]]</ref> [[Sullust]] for the Endor assault.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

Admiral Ackbar confirmed that the [[Star Cruiser]]s available for the attack were ''[[Home One]]'', ''[[Nautilian]]'', ''[[Liberty]]'', and ''[[Amalthea]]'' pending its [[hyperdrive]] repair. ''Freedom's Run''{{'s}} status was listed as tentative, along with the [[cruiser]] ''[[Restoration]]''. Ackbar's report was incorporated into ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', a physical data archive that contained a non-electronic copy of sensitive information. The physical archive was buried on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] world of [[Durkteel]] during an Imperial [[Bombardment of Durkteel|bombardment]]. The depository was [[Excavation on Durkteel|excavated]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="TRF Date">The reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' establishes that the [[in-universe]] artifact ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' was buried on the same [[Standard day|day]] as the [[Battle of Endor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. Given that the former also states that ''The Rebel Files'' was rediscovered thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the battle, the artifact must have been found in [[34 ABY]].</ref> and soon reached General [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Resistance]], a private military with roots to the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Freedom's Run'' was mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', a [[2017]] box set and reference book authored by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
