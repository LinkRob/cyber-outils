'''Encrust II''' was a location<ref name="Star Wars 46" /> in the [[Jubilar sector]]'s [[Encrust system]], which was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was situated in grid square T-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> According to [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the [[Clawdite]] [[Changeling|shapeshifter]] [[Tunga Arpagion]] was personal friends with all the members of the [[Meor nobility]] that were within 1.3 [[parsec]]s of Encrust II<ref name="Star Wars 46" /> by [[1 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars (2015) 46|''Star Wars'' (2015) 46]] to [[1 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Encrust II was mentioned in the [[Star Wars (2015) 46|forty-sixth issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series, which was written by [[Kieron Gillen]], illustrated by [[Salvador Larroca]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 46" /> on [[April 4]], [[2018]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/66699/star_wars_2015_46|text=Star Wars (2015) #46}}</ref>
