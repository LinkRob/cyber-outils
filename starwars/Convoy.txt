A '''convoy''' was a group of [[starship]]s or vehicles that traveled together for mutual support.

During the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]'s [[Malevolence Campaign|''Malevolence'' campaign]], the ''[[Malevolence]]'' destroyed a [[Republic Navy]] '''convoy''' during the [[Battle of the Ryndellia system]] using its [[mega-ion cannon]]s.<ref name="Shadow">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/shadow-of-malevolence-episode-guide|text="Shadow of Malevolence" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>
