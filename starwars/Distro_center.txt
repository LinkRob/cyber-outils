'''Distro centers''' were a type of commercial location where [[holobook]]s could be purchased.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the best-selling, tell-all autobiography of former [[Senator]] [[Phenesta Wesh]], ''[[Turmoil]]'', could be purchased at distro centers across [[the galaxy]], which was advertised by [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Dorna Parros]] during coverage of the [[book]] on the [[HoloNet News]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Distro centers were mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
