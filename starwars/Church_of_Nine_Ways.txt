The '''Church of Nine Ways''' was a [[religion]] in [[the galaxy]]. The [[Theelin]] [[Chass na Chadic]] lied about being part of the religion to three [[Chevin]] brothers that were sent against her and her [[Yrica Quell|partner]] on [[Spirana]]. The confrontation ended in all five of them confronting the salesman that had sent the Chevin brothers.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Church of Nine Ways was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the final installment of [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
