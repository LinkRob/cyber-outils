{{Quote|Have you read the testimonies of Cervil the Uncanny? She states that the whip was sometimes used to defend against the Sith Lords who used the Forbidden Forms.|Vernestra Rwoh, to Imri Cantaros|<ref name="A Test of Courage" />}}
The '''Forbidden Forms''' were forms of [[lightsaber combat]] used by the [[Sith]] during the [[Sith Wars]]. According to the testimonies of the [[Jedi]] [[Cervil the Uncanny]], [[lightwhip]]s&mdash;a [[lightsaber]] variant with a flexible blade&mdash;were used to defend against [[Sith Lord]]s who utilized the forms.<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]], while stranded on the [[jungle]] [[moon]] [[Wevo]], brought up Cervil the Uncanny's testimonies about the Forbidden Forms to [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]] in response to his concerns about the modifications she had made to her [[Vernestra Rwoh's purple-bladed lightsaber|lightsaber]] to give it a lightwhip mode.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Forbidden Forms were mentioned in the [[2021]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]]<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
