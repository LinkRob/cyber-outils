"'''Connections'''" is the eighth episode of ''[[Disney Gallery: The Mandalorian]]'', a docuseries on the production of the [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''. The episode premiered on [[June 19]], [[2020]] on Disney+.<ref name="D23" />

==Official description==
''Jon and Dave look back at some of the Star Wars characters and props that made their way into'' The Mandalorian. ''We also take a moment to showcase the 501st legion.''<ref name="Informer">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneyplusinformer.com/full-list-everything-coming-to-disney-in-june-us|text=FULL LIST: EVERYTHING COMING TO DISNEY+ IN JUNE (US)|date=2020-05-15|work=Disney Plus Informer|archivedate=20200517022955}}</ref>
