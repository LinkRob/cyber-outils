'''Dolin''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[farmer]] who lived on the outskirts of [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]] with his grandmother, [[Grana]]. After saving the life of [[Vi Moradi]], Dolin left Grana to join Moradi in the [[Resistance]]. He owned a pet [[truffleboar]] named [[Waba (truffleboar)|Waba]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />
