The '''early death dog''', also known as '''urcaninus lic morte''' or simply '''urcaninus''', was a foul-tempered, strong, and voluminous [[Diet/Legends|predator]]. It was a nocturnal hunter that inhabited [[Swamp/Legends|swamps]], [[veldt]]s, and plains and fed on [[Color/Legends|red]] and blue meat, including [[Poison/Legends|poisonous]] [[Snake/Legends|snakes]], though it was also known for eating [[aquatic broccoli]]. [[Gender/Legends|Female]] early [[Death/Legends|death]] [[Dog/Legends|dogs]] typically hid their offspring from the males to avoid [[cannibalism]] during the first [[Standard year/Legends|years]] of life. The creatures could be found from the [[Middle Plains]] to 30,000 [[league]]s south of the [[Troon]], depending on the season. They were also kept as pets by certain [[Species/Legends|species]].

==Biology and appearance==
The early [[Death/Legends|death]] [[Dog/Legends|dog]], also known by the name of urcaninus lic morte, or simply urcaninus, was a large-sized creature, with an average height of twenty-seven to thirty-six [[Mortar (unit)|mortars]], light [[Color/Legends|brownish]] skin, a beige pelt, small black [[Eye/Legends|eyes]] at the top of its head with periorbital dark pigmentation, a red-colored depilous face, and a large [[Mouth/Legends|mouth]] in comparison with the rest of the body. The urcaninus had strong shoulders and [[Limb/Legends|arms]]; a lumbarless spine that, at one point, was in the process of de-evolving; four limbs, two clawed and two pawed; and an overdeveloped torso with an ancillary pelvic structure. It possessed a retractable tail, rose-colored gums, self-cleaning carnivorous [[Tooth/Legends|fangs]], no [[Hair/Legends|hair]], and a spiked, pink [[Tongue/Legends|tongue]]. It was also able to walk on four limbs.<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behavior and intelligence==
The urcaninus was a nocturnal species, possessing frontal optical lobes that allowed the creature to hunt [[Poison/Legends|poisonous]] [[Snake/Legends|snakes]] and feed on red and blue meat, though the creature was known to eat [[aquatic broccoli]] as well. Its nighttime predisposition made it particularly irritable during daylight [[Standard hour/Legends|hours]], even though it was naturally seen as aimless, erratic, and frustrated most of the [[Time/Legends|time]], often suffering from a psychological condition known as ''[[melancholia agressiva]]''. Its main habitats were [[Swamp/Legends|swamps]] and [[veldt]]s, ranging from the [[Middle Plains]] to 30,000 [[league]]s south of [[Troon]], depending on the season. Domestic urcaninus were known to enjoy scratching [[Tree/Legends|trees]].<ref name="MAGL" />

The creatures did not maintain long-term relationships between [[Gender/Legends|females]] and males, with the former hiding offspring from the males during their first [[Standard year/Legends|years]] to avoid filial [[cannibalism]]. Some urcaninus wore accessories that resembled [[Bracelet/Legends|bracelets]] around their arms.<ref name="MAGL" />

==Early death dogs in the galaxy==
Early death dogs could be kept as pets, following proper security recommendations. Caretakers were advised to keep them in high-voltage enclosures, walk them with wide-range [[Laser/Legends|laser]] "leashlets," and possess trees for the urcaninus to scratch. The creature could be bought at pet shops and, at one point, cost 970.X*.000/45, although that price depended on availability and the exchange rate.<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behind the scenes==
The early death dog was featured in a one-page entry written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that combines creature designs and photographs from various projects of [[George Lucas]], creator of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, with original text by Carrau.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref> Although the entry for the urcaninus specifically stated that the predator did not possess hair, the image for it depicted a furred creature and the entry specifically states the creature had a beige pelt.<ref name="MAGL" />
