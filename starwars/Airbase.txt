An '''airbase''', also spelled '''air base''' or called an '''airfield''', was a type of military installation.<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the Separatist-aligned [[Umbaran Militia]] maintained an [[Umbaran airbase|airbase]] on the [[planet]] [[Umbara]] that they used to resupply the [[Umbaran capital city]]. [[501st Legion]] forces commanded by [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] and [[Jedi General]] [[Pong Krell]] would take the base during the [[Battle of Umbara]].<ref name="The General">{{TCW|The General (episode)}}</ref> By [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|104}}</ref> the [[Imperial Navy]] maintained an airbase known as the [[Steergard Naval Yard]] in [[Steergard]].<ref name="Aldhani" /> The airbase was attacked by [[Anto Kreegyr's group]], which resulted in the attackers' overwhelming defeat, as the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] was made aware of their plans beforehand.<ref name="Daughter">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> The [[Aldhani Garrison]] maintained [[Alkenzi Air Base|another airbase]] hosting the [[Alkenzi Air Command]]<ref name="Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Aldhani]].<ref name="Aldhani" /> The Empire also had an [[Imperial airbase|airbase]]<ref name="Star Wars 26">[[Star Wars (2020) 26|''Star Wars'' (2020) 26]]</ref> on the [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Bar'leth]].<ref name="Star Wars 26" />
