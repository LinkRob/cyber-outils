"'''Blinking Eyes: Holes'''" is an adventure from the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign that was written by [[Lee Pickler]]. It was released in [[January]] [[2002]] and was the third adventure in the [[Blinking Eyes trilogy]].
