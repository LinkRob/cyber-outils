A '''Caregiver''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Chiss]] assigned to take care of [[Sky-walker|ozyly-esehembos]], or ''sky-walkers'' in [[Cheunh]]. Originally, all caregivers were former ozyly-esehembos, though that practice eventually faded away. [[Mitth'ali'astov]] served as the Caregiver to [[Che'ri]] while aboard the ''[[Springhawk]]'', and was the first former sky-walker in at least a hundred years to become a caregiver<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref>. Che'ri called her caregiver a "'''momish'''."<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> 

==History==
Originally, all caregivers were former sky-walkers. This practice stopped at least a hundred years before 19 BBY<ref name="Chaos Rising" />. Caregiver [[Mitth'ali'astov|Thalias]] assumed the reason no former sky-walkers went on to become caregivers was because they leave the [[sky-walker corps]] exhausted. However, after meeting with [[Cohbo'rik'ardok|Borika]], a sky-walker researcher and former sky-walker, Thalias realized the real reason that former sky-walkers were discouraged from becoming caregivers was to prevent caregivers and sky-walkers from comparing notes and realize they both have the same memory loss as the result of their childhood memories being wiped. The process, known as ''fading'', was introduced to prevent young sky-walkers from being distracted from their training by homesickness<ref name=":0">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>.

==Duties and roles==
Caregivers were responsible for giving classes to the sky-walkers under their care, as well as [[Foodstuff|feeding]] and [[bath]]ing them.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Caregivers are also responsible for procuring recreational supplies and clothing for their sky-walkers, for which they have a fairly unlimited budget<ref name=":0" />.

Caregivers accompanied ozyly-esehembos on their journeys, often with the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]], though both were considered civilians. While outside of the military chain of command, caregivers have the authority to give orders even to the captain in situations involving the welfare of the sky-walker<ref name="Greater Good" />. The pairing between sky-walkers and caregivers was not permanent, with Che'ri having had eight different caregivers before Thlias was assigned to her. The assignment of new caregivers often caused additional emotional stress on sky-walkers<ref name="Chaos Rising" />.

Most caregivers do not stay on the bridge while their sky-walker navigates the [[hyperspace]], with Thalias being the exception<ref name="Chaos Rising" />. While the ship was in combat, the caregiver and her sky-walker would be escorted back to the safety of their [[Sky-walker suite|suite]]<ref name=":0" />. If a quick withdrawal into hyperspace was needed, they could be placed in the ship's [[secondary command]] instead<ref name="Greater Good" />.

Thalias believes that many caregivers do not truly understand the mental and physical burden of the sky-walkers, as they have never experienced life as a sky-walkers themselves, and many caregivers were just "going through the motions", giving little to no emotional support to their assigned sky-walkers<ref name="Greater Good" />. Che'ri was thankful for Thalias as she understood the overloading spell caused by extended use of [[Precognition|third sight]], something none of her previous caregivers understood, with one even gotten angry with her<ref name="Chaos Rising" />.
