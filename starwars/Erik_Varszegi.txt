'''Erik Varszegi''' is [[the LEGO Group]]'s Lead Model Designer. He was responsible for designing the scale model set for the [[Freemaker Salvage and Repair|Freemaker Garage]] in the [[2016]] animated television series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''.<ref name="Designing the Freemaker Garage">{{LEGOWebCite|url=en-us/starwars/videos/freemaker-garage-designer-video-ce691c98a6e4459abada5932ca68c4e1|text=Desinging the Freemaker Garage from LEGO Star Wars Freemaker Adventures|nolive=1}}</ref> He is also an avid [[Star Wars]] fan and builder of LEGO Star Wars vehicles and vessels.<ref name="FBTB">{{WebCite|url=http://www.fbtb.net/features/interview-erik-varszegi|text=Interview: Erik Varszegi|date=2005-08-10|work=From Bricks to Bothans|archivedate=20091208035255}}</ref>
