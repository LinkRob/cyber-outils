'''Aquint Garva''' was an [[Information broker|informant]] who resided in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TMoD FCBD" /> Between [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Tensu Run]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] attempted to locate Garva on the [[moon]] of [[K'shar]], who had information in regards to [[Corlis Rath]], an individual who attempted to [[Death|kill]] Jinn. While venturing in the streets of the moon, they eventually found the corpse of Garva who had been killed by Rath.<ref name="TMoD FCBD" />
