The '''A140 blaster pistol''' was a model of [[blaster pistol]] produced by [[BlasTech Industries]]. The blaster pistol featured a [[scope]] with a white [[Barrel-shroud|barrel shroud]].<ref name="Dawn" /> 

==History==
While fighting alongside the [[Onderon rebels|rebels]] on the [[planet]] [[Onderon]] during the [[Onderonian Civil War]], resistance fighter [[Lux Bonteri]] used an A140 while defending the [[Saw Gerrera's camp|Onderon insurgent camp]] from [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] attack<ref name="Fronts" /> and when rescuing [[King]] [[Ramsis Dendup]] in the [[city]] of [[Iziz]]. The leader [[Steela Gerrera]] also carried one as a backup weapon, but primary used her [[sniper rifle]] to take out a pair of [[BX-series droid commando]]s. <ref name="Soft">{{TCW|The Soft War}}</ref> The [[bounty hunter]] [[Castas]] also wielded an A140.<ref name="R2 Come Home">{{TCW|R2 Come Home}}</ref> The racer [[Jet Venim]] also owned an A140 blaster, which he attempted to use to kill [[Tech]] for beating him in [[Riot Racing]], before his boss [[Grini Millegi]] stopped him.<ref name="Faster">{{TBB|Faster}}</ref>
