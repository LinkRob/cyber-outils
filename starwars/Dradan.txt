'''Dradans''' were a type of [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. After witnessing the [[Death|deceased]] body of the [[Whiphid]] criminal [[J'Rea]], [[Jonram Dremel]] stated her body was dead like a Dradan.<ref name="Creature" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Dradan was mentioned in "[[The Creature from the Trash Compactor]]," the second story in the [[Star Wars comic books|graphic novel]] ''[[Tales from the Death Star]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Creature" /> on [[October 17]], [[2023]].<ref name="Date">{{PenguinComics|url=book/?isbn=9781506738291|text=Star Wars: Tales from the Death Star}}</ref> The [[Dradan/Legends|Dradan]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' book ''[[Domain of Evil]]''.<ref name="Domain of Evil">''[[Domain of Evil]]''</ref>
