'''E'roni tea''' was a type of bitter [[tea]] that could be found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]. The drink could be made with the severed heads of [[desert nettle]]s and [[Diet|consumed]] by [[human]]s.<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[E'roni]] [[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]] drank cups of E'roni tea while [[Aid mission on E'ronoh|traveling]] to the [[U'ronoh]] settlement aboard the ''[[Amaryliss]]'', a [[sail barge]] loaded with relief supplies for E'ronoh's citizens.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
E'roni tea appeared in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a [[2022]] novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" />
