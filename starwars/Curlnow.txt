'''Curlnow''' was a location on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]]. The [[Starros family homestead|manor]] of the [[Starros Clan]] was located on the outskirts of Curlnow near a [[Unidentified private spacedock|private spacedock]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> In [[34 ABY]], Hosnian Prime was [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroyed]] by the [[First Order]]'s [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
