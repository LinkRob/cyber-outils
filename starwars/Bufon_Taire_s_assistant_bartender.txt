A light-skinned [[humanoid]] worked as an '''assistant bartender''' to the [[bartender]] [[Bufon Taire]] at the [[Outlander Club]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] after having been fired from most of the respectable bars on the world.<ref name="CLNE" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|disarmed]] the [[bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]] by the club's bar while Taire was behind it.<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref> Taire then left the bar while the assistant bartender and two other employees manned it as the Jedi interogated Wesell outside. The assistant bartender wore a [[Color|red]] [[Clothing|shirt]] and tan pants while at the bar.<ref name="CLNE" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the assistant bartender was first mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> The [[Bufon Taire's assistant bartender/Legends|character]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in the the [[2003]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]'', authored by [[Simon Beecroft]] and [[Curtis Saxton]], and illustrated by [[Hans Jenssen]] and [[Richard Chasemore]].<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]''</ref>
