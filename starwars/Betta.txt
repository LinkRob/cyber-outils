'''Betta''' was a [[mercenary]] who worked as part of a [[Orisent's mercenaries|group]] led by the [[human]] [[Gender|woman]] [[Orisent]] on the [[planet]] [[Siline]]<ref name="TCC" /> in [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> where they illegally [[Hunting|hunted]] [[Ericadi]] during the [[great hunt]] of that [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|in that year's]] [[Crowning Convocation]]. [[Capture of Lionine Graf and Jecki Lon|During]] their poaching they were caught by a group of participants and instead captured [[Lionine Graf|Lio Graf]] and the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] and took them to them to their hiding place in the [[Heart of Siline]]. Lon ultimately managed to [[Rescue of Jecki Lon|flee]] and Betta and the other mercenaries tried to catch her, but were apprehended by the arriving Silinese officials and Lon's Jedi companions.<ref name="TCC" />
