The '''''Caisson''-class engineering troop carrier''' was a class of [[troop transport]] that was a derivative of the [[CR20 troop carrier|CR-20 troop carrier]] and was a survivor of the [[Clone Wars]]. It was deployed by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />
