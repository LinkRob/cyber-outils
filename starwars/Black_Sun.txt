'''Black Sun''' was a criminal syndicate that came into prominence during the [[Clone Wars]] as the [[Jedi Order]] focused more on military affairs and less on law enforcement. Black Sun was allied with the [[Shadow Collective]] and its leader, the renegade Sith Lord [[Maul]]. The syndicate maintained bases on [[Mustafar]] and [[Ord Mantell]] and had many of the [[Falleen]] species as members.

==History==
===Late Republic Era===
Black Sun had been around for thousands of [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Species From A to Z">{{GermanFanCite|115|Database}}</ref> [[Nodrus Cay]] was a ranking [[lieutenant]] in Black Sun<ref name="Obi-Wan 1">[[Obi-Wan 1|''Obi-Wan'' 1]]</ref> around [[49 BBY]].<ref name="49 BBY Date">The flashbacks seen in [[Obi-Wan 1|''Obi-Wan'' 1]] occurred when [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was eight [[Standard year|years]] old. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates Obi-Wan's [[Reproduction|birth]] year to [[57 BBY]], the flashbacks must have taken place around [[49 BBY]].</ref> The [[Zabrak]] lieutenant attempted to kidnap the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Gehren Rand]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], but both escaped her grasp.<ref name="Obi-Wan 1" />

===The Clone Wars===
{{Quote|They're a crime syndicate, hardly a force to rally with.|[[Pre Vizsla]]|<ref name="Eminence" />}}
[[File:Aggression-SWQ04.png|thumb|250px|left|Black Sun took part in the Takeover of Mandalore as part of the Shadow Collective.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], the syndicate was very influential throughout [[the galaxy]] and run by a group of [[Falleen]] [[noble]]men from a [[Black Sun fortress|fortress]] on [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Black Sun">{{Databank|black-sun|Black Sun}}</ref> One of its members was [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] the [[Hutt]].<ref name="The Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> During the war, Black Sun took advantage of less surveillance from the [[Galactic Republic]] and expanded their sphere of influence.<ref name="Species From A to Z" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="19 BBY">{{TCWcanondates|514}}</ref> Black Sun was [[Mission to Mustafar (Death Watch)|coerced]] by the rogue [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] into joining his [[Shadow Collective]]<ref name="Eminence" /> after his brother [[Savage Opress]] [[Death|decapitated]] their leader, [[Xomit Grunseit]], leaving only [[Ziton Moj]] alive. Moj joined Maul's Shadow Collective and took part in the [[Takeover of Mandalore]]. Black Sun openly maintained a base on [[Ord Mantell]].<ref name="SoD2">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 2|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 2]]</ref>

Under direction of the Shadow Collective, Black Sun and the [[Pyke Syndicate]] participated in the large [[Battle on Ord Mantell|Battle of Ord Mantell]], defending against [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] attackers led by [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and [[Grievous]].<ref name="SoD2" />

Towards the end of the Clone Wars, Black Sun's leader [[Ziton Moj]] proposed merging with the Pyke Syndicate, which specialized in distributing illegal narcotics. When the Pyke leader [[Marg Krim]] rejected his demands, Moj retaliated by kidnapping his [[Krim|family]] in order to force Krim to change his mind. Krim then hired two independent [[bounty hunter]]s&mdash;the former [[Sith apprentice]] [[Asajj Ventress]] and the undercover [[Jedi Master]] [[Quinlan Vos]]&mdash;to rescue his family. The two bounty hunters raided the Black Sun [[fortress]] on Mustafar and succeeded in [[Rescue of Marg Krim's family|rescuing]] Krim's family. During the rescue mission, Moj and his Falleen guards were assaulted by Ventress and Vos. Black Sun retaliated by sending a [[fleet]] to attack the Pyke stronghold of [[Oba Diah]].<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>

[[File:KastMojFifeHolo-SoD-4.png|thumb|right|200px|Fife and Ziton Moj withdraw their syndicates from the Shadow Collective.]]
Soon afterward the Black Sun came under [[Attack on the Black Sun|attack]]  by a Separatist armada and lost a significant number of ships. The Pykes also suffered a similar [[Attack on the Pyke Syndicate|attack]] from the Separatists and abandoned Maul because of his lack of assistance. The Black Sun and soon the Pyke Syndicate followed and both Fife and Ziton Moj informed Maul's [[Lieutenant (criminal)|Lieutenant]] [[Rook Kast]] their partnership was finished as they no longer saw any financial incentive. Because of this, the Shadow Collective fell apart, and Maul withdrew with his remaining forces following their [[Second Battle of Dathomir|loss]] on [[Dathomir]].<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Four">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 4|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 4]]</ref> However, the Pyke Syndicate and the Black Sun would later rejoin Maul and the Collective.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref>

At the time of the [[Siege of Mandalore]], shortly before the enactment of [[Order 66]], Maul communicated via hologram with the syndicate leaders members of the Shadow Collective, including the Black Sun, which was represented by Ziton Moj, to go into hiding.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" />

===Age of the Empire===
====Finding new footing====
{{Quote|Pilot Ashla. Congratulations. You have come to the attention of Black Sun.|A Black Sun agent, to Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref>}}
[[File:Black_Sun_Blaster_Pistol_AtG.png|thumb|right|250px|A Black Sun Guard with their signature double-barrel blasters.]]
After [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] the Republic into his own [[Galactic Empire]] at the end of the Clone Wars, Black Sun formed a durable relationship with the new autocratic government<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> and kept watch over the Outer Rim,<ref name="Ahsoka" /> becoming the dominant power in the Outer Rim's criminal underworld.<ref name="Species From A to Z" /> Early into the [[Imperial Era]], a [[Unidentified Black Sun racketeer|Black Sun racketeer]] worked on the planet [[Murkhana]] until Darth Vader, in one of his first actions as Palpatine's servant, carried out a [[Mission to Murkhana (Darth Vader)|mission to Murkhana]], during which he executed the racketeer. Thanks to Vader, the Black Sun abandoned Murkhana, which allowed the [[Sugi]] [[Faazah]] to rise into power on that world.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> After the rise of the Empire, Maul was betrayed by the Black Sun and his other Shadow Collective allies, who then prospered from his absence.<ref name="MSL Ch1">{{MSL|Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge}}</ref> [[Rheena Sul]] told her friend [[Brander Lawson]] that the Shadow Collective allies, including [[Nico Deemis]] and [[Looti Vario]], disbanded the syndicate after Maul vanished after the Clone Wars, probably happy Maul was gone.<ref name="MSL Ch2">{{MSL|Chapter 2: Sinister Schemes}}</ref>

A [[Standard year|year]] after the establishment of the Empire, Black Sun took notice of [[pilot]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]&mdash;although they only knew her by her alias of "Ashla" and did not know she was a former [[Padawan]]&mdash;after she intruded in their [[Sentience|sentient]] trafficking, as the secret [[Force-sensitive]] had taken to helping the people of the Outer Rim. Learning that "Ashla" had been hired by the [[Fardi clan]], Black Sun knew they could pay her more than the smaller group, though they also wanted her to work to make back the thousands of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] she had cost them. Thus, Black Sun ordered their agents to either hire or kill the pilot. When [[Unidentified Black Sun agent|one Black Sun agent]] found her on an [[Unidentified planet (Thabeska system)|Outer Rim planet]], Tano rejected their job offer, escaping aboard her [[Unidentified light freighter (Thabeska)|light freighter]] as the agent fired on her.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

Using their [[Unidentified Black Sun starship|sleek starship]], the agent pursued Tano, even as she concurrently realized her encounter with the agent had helped clear her mind of doubts. Tano and the agent's dogfight was interrupted by [[Chardri Tage]]'s [[Chardri Tage's starship|starship]], having been sent by [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]] to locate Tano, and the agent elected to escape instead of attack the larger craft. After boarding Tage's ship and reuniting with [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]], Tano rested aboard her ship when she arranged for it to float above a nameless [[moon]] that orbited the planet she had fought the agent on; even though she figured the agent would be repairing their ship, she was certain they were not hiding where she was.<ref name="Ahsoka" /> During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], Black Sun owned a [[Pantolomin cruiser]].<ref name="Solo - Imperial Cadet 3">[[Han Solo - Imperial Cadet 3|''Han Solo - Imperial Cadet'' 3]]</ref> The [[mercenary]] [[Saponza]] was hired by Black Sun for a job that he then abandoned, so the syndicate sent a [[Mandalorian]] [[assassin]] after him.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>

The former [[Imperial cadet]]s [[Sabine Wren]] and [[Ketsu Onyo]] decided to join Black Sun as bounty hunters. However, Onyo got "greedy," left her former classmate for dead, and joined Black Sun by herself.<ref name="Blood Sisters" /> Wren, operating on her own, caught her other friend [[Ojo]] giving confidential Black Sun info to the syndicate's enemies and apprehended him. He was imprisoned in the [[Black Sun base|base]] on [[Smarab]]. Between [[3 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref>''[[Star Wars Rebels Volume 3: Rebellion at the Edge of the Galaxy]]'' places "The Second Chance" after "[[The Gangsters of Galzez]]," which {{Twitter|tronprogram2|status/751843992254423040|[[Martin Fisher]]|quote=@LelalMekha the most recent story happens between Shroud of Darkness and The Forgotten Droid|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/uVHHn}} places between "[[Shroud of Darkness]]" and "[[The Forgotten Droid]]," which corresponds to [[3 BBY]] per ''Star Wars: Timelines''. "The Second Chance" also includes [[Sabine Wren]] with [[Color|blue-green]] [[hair]], and Sabine's hair is light-purple in ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[2 BBY]]. Therefore, "The Second Chance" takes place between 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 2 BBY.</ref> Wren and the astromech droid [[C1-10P]] liberated Ojo, significantly damaging the base and dispatching several of the Black Sun guards.<ref>{{RebelsMagCite|24|The Second Chance}}</ref>

At one point, [[Duke]] [[Blazor]] of the Black Sun expected money from the [[Gatto Gang]], leading to him hiring bounty hunter [[IG-88B]].<ref name="IG-88 vs Gatto Gang">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=9|story=Tales from Wild Space: IG-88 vs. the Gatto Gang}}</ref> [[Gyuti]], a [[Boss]] in Black Sun, was involved in an [[Fiasco on Ryloth|embarrassing incident]] on [[Ryloth]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 12|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 12]]</ref> Black Sun abandoned Mustafar during the Imperial Era, after the Galactic Empire established a [[Fortress Vader Imperial garrison|garrison]] on the planet, in favor of a lower profile world.<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>  Years later, Onyo accepted a Black Sun job to collect a [[GNK-series power droid]] called [[EG-86]], which was carrying information vital to the rebellion. This mission brought her into conflict with Wren, who despised Black Sun for engaging in [[Slavery|slave trafficking]]. However, the two former classmates reconciled and joined forces to [[Skirmish in the Garel City Spaceport|escape]] the Empire and to deliver the [[droid]] to the rebellion.<ref name="Blood Sisters">{{Rebels|Blood Sisters}}</ref>

====Rivalries renewed====
In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the crime syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]] returned after a long absence and sent out a message to various criminal factions in the underworld. In the message, the syndicate's current leader [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]] invited the other syndicates to join in an auction for the [[carbonite]] encased [[Han Solo]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 11|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 11]]</ref> Black Sun answered the call and arrived at the planet [[Jekara]], where one of their ships was hit by the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. In response, Black Sun sent out their fighters to destroy the ship, though it managed to evade them and crashed onto the planet below.<ref name="Star Wars 14" />

Gyuti then entered the [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']] along with [[Xet]] and a few other Falleen.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12" /> Black Sun bid for Solo during the [[Auction for Han Solo|auction]], but they were quickly stifled by a [[mind trick]] done unto them by Imperial<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 2">[[War of the Bounty Hunters 2|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 2]]</ref> [[Sub-Administrator]]<ref name="Darth Vader 14">[[Darth Vader (2020) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 14]]</ref> [[Sly Moore]]. The auction was won by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] of the [[Grand Hutt Council]], but his victory was short-lived as it was interrupted by the arrival of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Guarded by two [[death trooper]]s, the [[Sith Lord]] declared that Solo belonged to him.<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 2" /> In the ensuing chaos, Vader attacked several of the present syndicates, including Black Sun, forcing them to retreat from Jekara.<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 3">[[War of the Bounty Hunters 3|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 3]]</ref> In the chaos that ensued, the Hutt Clan attacked several Imperial ships present.<ref name="Crimson Reign 1">[[Crimson Reign 1|''Crimson Reign'' 1]]</ref>

Following the auction, Gyuti would have an audience with Qi'ra aboard the Black Sun frigate ''[[Darkpath]]''. The Black Sun official listened as Qi'ra offered the Dawn's services and also suggested that Black Sun assert themselves to gain the partnership with the Empire previously had by the Hutt Clan before their entanglement with the Empire. The tip piqued the interest of Gyuti.<ref name="Crimson Reign 1" />

Soon after the meeting with Q'ira, Crimson Dawn's devious leader began to direct her [[Orphans|operatives]] to engage in attacks on various other criminal syndicates, including Black Sun. The [[Black Sun smash-processing lab]] on the planet [[Dantooine]] was soon [[Attack on the Black Sun smash-processing lab|assaulted]] and burned to the ground. Gyuti viewed footage of the aftermath of the attack on the [[Shadowfeed]]s, although he was unable to deduce who could have been responsible.<ref name="Crimson Reign 1" />

===New Republic===
{{Quote|Black Sun is currently headquartered on the lava planet Mustafar and is mainly controlled by Vigos from several noble Falleen families.|"Index of Organizations" describes Black Sun during the New Republic Era|<ref name="Species From A to Z" />}}
[[File:Pantoran_Prisoner_SaV.png|thumb|left|220px|A [[Pantoran]] Black Sun member's mugshot]]
At the time of the [[New Republic]]'s founding, Black Sun was one of several major crime syndicates operating in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> In [[5 ABY]], the Black Sun Boss Gyuti placed a bounty on the head of the [[Zabrak]] bounty hunter [[Jas Emari]] for debts incurred by her aunt [[Sugi (bounty hunter)|Sugi]]. [[Vigo|Underboss]] [[Rynscar]] and her henchmen ambushed Jas and Sugi while they were visiting [[Nar Shaddaa]] for information on Han Solo's whereabouts. Jas managed to convince Rynscar not to turn her in by offering to repay them double the amount she owed. Rysncar also provided Jas with information about Han Solo's whereabouts.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

Shortly before the [[Battle of Jakku]], the bounty hunter [[Mercurial Swift]] decided to collect Gyuti's bounty on Emari's head in retaliation for her role in [[Capture of Mercurial Swift|ambushing]] him on the [[planet]] [[Taris]]. Later, Black Sun conspired with the upstart [[Red Key Raiders]] to prolong the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They bribed and blackmailed five New Republic [[Senator]]s [[Ashmin Ek]], [[Dor Wieedo]], [[Rethalow]], [[Grelka Sorka]], and [[Nim Tar]] into opposing [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]]'s resolution to send [[New Republic Defense Force]] forces to the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] world of [[Jakku]], where the Galactic Empire had retreated to. Their plot was [[Mission to Nakadia|foiled]] by a team led by [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] and Han Solo, who managed to rescue [[Nim Tar's child]] and convince the senators to change their votes in return for pardons and [[Politics|political]] favors.<ref name="Empire's End" /> Following the fall of the Galactic Empire, Black Sun still clung to significant territory in the Outer Rim. During this time, Maul's manipulations over Black Sun became public knowledge.<ref name="Species From A to Z" />

By around [[9 ABY]], during the [[New Republic Era|reign]] of the [[New Republic]], the Black Sun put a bounty on [[Stolak Vandello]], who was in debt to the syndicate. A crew of bounty hunters consisted of [[T'onga]], [[Zuckuss]], [[4-LOM]], [[Wooro]], [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], and [[Losha Tarkon]] undertook a [[Capture of Stolak Vandello|job]] to capture or kill Vandello. After Vandello surrendered himself to the crew and they took him back to Black Sun, who executed Vandello.<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5">[[Halcyon Legacy 5|''Halcyon Legacy'' 5]]</ref>

During the [[New Republic Era]], Black Sun had re-established its presence on Mustafar and was run by Falleen from noble families. Black Sun also developed a rivalry with the resurgent Crimson Dawn, and the two syndicates engaged in occasional skirmishes. Information on Black Sun was included in "[[Index of Organizations]]," a survival compendium sent out by [[Carson Teva]] to New Republic recruits.<ref name="Species From A to Z" />

==Organization==
A major criminal power, Black Sun dealt in illegal ventures like sentient-trafficking. While that activity was considered awful by others, members of Black Sun simply called it a "business venture."<ref name="Ahsoka" /> [[Black Sun Guard]]s<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> were Falleen who served as on the ground<ref name="SoD2" /> foot [[soldier]]s<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/eminence-trivia-gallery|text="Eminence" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> and guardians for the syndicate.<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> They carried [[BH-4 Double Blaster]]s.

==Behind the scenes==
Black Sun first appeared in the now-[[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] multimedia project ''[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire]]'', released in 1996 as a novel, comic book, video game, and more. During the development of "[[Eminence (episode)|Eminence]]," the fourteenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', it was decided that Maul would be given a base of power within the criminal underworld. [[Dave Filoni]], the show's supervising director, had remembered Black Sun from ''Shadows of the Empire'', and knew that the Expanded Universe showed Maul as having fought against Black Sun prior to the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. With that, Filoni and his team opted to bring Black Sun into the show.<ref name="Eminence Featurette">{{SW|url=video/shadows-of-the-sith|series=Clone Wars Download|text=Shadows of the Sith}}</ref>
