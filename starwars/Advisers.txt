{{Quote|Mm. I promise you, Smuggler―I am no Jedi. My order, too, was nearly extinguished by the Empire&hellip; but we are a heartier breed. Shall we continue?|Mentor Xiath, to the Smuggler|<ref name="Star Wars: Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>}}
The '''advisers''' were a [[The Force|Force]]-using organization based in the [[Anoat sector]]. Their existence was tied to that of the [[Noble Court]], the assembled ruling families of the [[sector]]. The advisers' role was to ensure the survival of the Court, which involved the teaching of Force techniques to some nobles.<ref name="Light Side Missions intro" /> With the rise of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Anoat sector was [[Subjugation of the Anoat sector|subjected]]. However, despite the Empire's efforts to stomp the noble families, they managed to survive in secret<ref name="Last of the Zenowai">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Story Missions: "Last of the Zenowai"</ref> with the help of the advisers. By the time of the Imperial [[Iron Blockade]] of the sector, the nobles and the advisers were still working to return to their former power and prestige. [[Xiath]], a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who held the title of [[Mentor (title)|Mentor]] among the advisers, hid from the Imperials with his [[Nothoiin (species)|Nothoiin]] apprentice [[Amrosio]] in the [[Ancient Palace]] on [[Hosra]], a [[planet]] that was of no interest to the Empire due to its lack of valuable resources. In spite of all the secrecy, the survival of the advisers and the Court was known to the [[Data Seekers]], an underground scholarly organization. [[Standard month|Months]] into the blockade, a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] from [[Burnin Konn]] acquired a pack of holodiscs claiming to outline secrets behind the Noble Court's survival against the Empire.<ref name="Light Side Missions intro" /> After traveling to Hosra, the Smuggler met Mentor Xiath, who tested the visitor before agreeing to impart some of his knowledge on them.<ref name="Planting the Seed">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Light Side Missions: "Planting the Seed"</ref>
