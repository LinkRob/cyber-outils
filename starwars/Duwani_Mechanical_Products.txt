'''Duwani Mechanical Products''' was a [[droid]] manufacturing company. The company's products included [[protocol droid]]s<ref name="SWE" /> by [[382 BBY]] during the [[High Republic Era]], including [[ME-8D9]],<ref name="HRDate">As [[ME-8D9]], who was manufactured by Duwani Mechanical Products per ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'', appears in [[The High Republic Adventures (2022) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2022) 1]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]] and places during the [[High Republic Era]].</ref> a [[droid]] rumored to have served the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Old Republic-era]]. Duwani Mechanical Products also produced [[astromech droid]]s that were considered an ancient twist on an older but efficient [[droid]] line<ref name="SWE" /> by [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|1}}</ref> The [[Ace Squadron]] member and [[racer]] [[Freya Fenris]] owned the astromech droid [[T3-K10]], whom Duwani Mechanical Products manufactured.<ref name="SWE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Duwani Mechanical Products was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''.<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the company was first mentioned in ''[[Tales of the Jedi Companion]]'', a [[1996]] [[roleplaying game]] reference book published by [[West End Games]] as a tie-in to the [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (comic series)|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series.<ref name="Tales of the Jedi">''[[Tales of the Jedi Companion]]''</ref>
