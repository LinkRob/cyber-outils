A '''blue-winged mink''' was a type of creature with [[Color|blue]] [[Limb|wings]].<ref name="Crown" /> In [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> while [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] was [[Jedi training|training]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Crown" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]], she scrambled through a [[rope-and-bar course]] and mentally compared her speed and grace to that of a blue-winged mink's.<ref name="Crown" />

==Behind the scenes==
