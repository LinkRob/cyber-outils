The '''Aria Prime system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square O-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the [[planet]] [[Aria Prime]].<ref name="Origin" />
