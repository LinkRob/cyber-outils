'''Big Muhh''' was a [[pirate]] who operated on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The pirate was included in an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] report of piracy on Akiva that had been stored at Imperial [[Research Station 57]] on Akiva<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate|APF=1}}</ref> In the report, they were listed as being notorious and still at large along with fellow pirates [[Silver-fin Bollas]] and the [[Dread Triplets]]. The report blamed the local [[Satrapy of Myrra|satrapy government]] for failing to prevent privacy in the [[Akiva system]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Around 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] accessed the report at Research Station 57 while looking for information about the whereabouts of a different pirate named [[Qorvino]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Big Muhh was mentioned on a [[Computer|data]] entry in the "A Pirate's Fortune" story expansion of the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> The data entry that mentions Big Muhh is a required step of the expansions first main quest, "A Pirate's Plight." Starting the story expansion quest requires players to have completed the game's base story. As such, this article assumes completion of the base game story and completion of "A Pirate's Plight," the first quest of the story expansion.<ref name="Outlaws" />
