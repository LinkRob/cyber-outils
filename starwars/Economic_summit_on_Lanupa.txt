The '''economic summit on Lanupa''' was an event held by the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] at the [[Skull Ridge Mountain Hotel & Spa]] on the [[planet]] [[Lanupa]]<ref name="Learn About Pirates" /> around [[9 ABY]]<ref name="SC Date" /> during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Learn About Pirates" />

The [[Skeleton Crew|crew]] of the ''[[Onyx Cinder]]'' visited the hotel and spa during the summit to search for the coordinates of the [[planet]] [[At Attin]] in the ancient [[pirate]] lair of [[Tak Rennod]] in the caverns below the spa. The presence of the group, including [[Jod Na Nawood]], resulted in a [[Silvo's pirates|pirate group]] attacking the spa during the summit.<ref name="Learn About Pirates" />

==Behind the scenes==
The summit appeared in "[[You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates]]," the fifth episode of the [[Disney+]] live&ndash;action series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'' which was released on [[December 24]], [[2024]].<ref name="Learn About Pirates" />
