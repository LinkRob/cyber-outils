{{Quote|They savaged the outpost at Furies Gate, then took the Giants&mdash;Obri and Mawr and their moons&mdash;before we blunted their attack on Tython itself.|[[Je'daii Master]] [[Daegen Lok]]'s log|<ref name="DOTJ 11" />}}
During the [[Invasion of the Tython system|invasion]] of the [[Tython system/Legends|Tython system]] by the [[Rakata/Legends|Rakata]] [[Species/Legends|species]], the Rakatan [[Infinite Empire/Legends|Infinite Empire]] '''captured''' the gas giant [[Obri]], one of the [[Settled Worlds]] of the [[Star system/Legends|system]], and its [[Moon/Legends|moons]]. The battle followed an [[Attack on Fury Station|attack]] on the [[Space station/Legends|space station]] [[Fury Station]], and the Empire also [[Capture of Mawr|captured]] the gas giant [[Mawr]] around the same time. From there, the Rakata [[First battle of Tython|struck]] at the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tython/Legends|Tython]].<ref name="DOTJ 11" />

==Behind the scenes==
The capture of Obri was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Dawn of the Jedi: Force War 1|''Dawn of the Jedi: Force War'' 1]], a [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] written by [[John Ostrander]] and released on [[November 20]], [[2013]].<ref name="DOTJ 11" />
