'''Celestial Watchtowers''' were forts built by the early [[Pijali|inhabitants]] of [[Pijal]] to watch over their [[soulcraft]]. When travel in [[Realspace|space]] became less dangerous, the Watchtowers were no longer needed, falling into ruin. [[Blackguard base|One]] was renovated by the [[blackguard]]s, [[Treason|traitorous]] members of the [[Royal guard (Pijal)|Pijal royal guard]], into a military outpost.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Celestial Watchtowers appeared in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
