The '''Frost MT-97''' was a pair of [[MT-97]] [[blaster pistol]]s owned by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal MT-97|standard blaster pistols]]. These [[Color|white]] and light gray [[pistol]]s featured a red [[Rebel Alliance starbird]] displayed on the sides of each.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Frost MT-97 was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Aran Tal's Clan Tal MT-97, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Frost Warrior]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing the Frosty Aran Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
