"'''Clone Trooper Troubles'''" is a [[Canon|non-canon]] animated [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short released on [[October 5]], [[2025]]<ref name="Clone Trooper Troubles" /> as part of ''[[LEGO STAR WARS: Celebrate the Season]]''.<ref name="DisneyNOW" />

==Official description==
''In this action packed celebration of The Clone Wars series, two Clone Troopers try to one-up each other's battlefield skills as they work to rescue Jedi Knights Ki-Adi-Mundi and Aayla Secura.''<ref name="Clone Trooper Troubles" />
