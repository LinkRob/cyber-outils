The '''Aldorus system''' contained seven planets which orbited [[Aldorus]], an old blue star.<ref name="CasusBelli" />

The system was at two days of the [[Abelard system]] and five of the [[Kubilaï system]] with a Class 2 [[Hyperdrive/Legends|hyperdrive]].<ref name="CasusBelli" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Aldorus system was created by [[Jean Balczesak]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] scenario ''[[L'écume de l'espace]]'' which appeared in the French magazine [[Casus Belli 44|''Casus Belli'' 44]].<ref name="CasusBelli" />
