'''Anaakin Solo''' was a [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] of the [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Solo]]. He was cloned by [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]]. He replaced Anakin with Anaakin at some point.<ref name="Best Thing" />

==Behind the scenes==
'''Anaakin Solo''' was mentioned in "[[An Apology]]," a short story by [[Timothy Zahn]] that was released on [[April 1]], [[2012]] as an April Fool's Day joke.
