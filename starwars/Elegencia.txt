The '''''Elegencia''''' was a [[corvette]]-sized [[starship]] owned by [[Pan Eyta]], a [[Tempest Runner]] of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization. It had a beautiful interior, with surfaces covered in soft [[leather]] and lighting that accented Eyta's design choices.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
