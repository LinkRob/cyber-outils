'''Flare Two''' was the [[callsign]] assigned to the second [[pilot]] of the [[New Republic]]'s [[Flare Squadron]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], an [[X-wing starfighter]] [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] was designated this callsign during the [[Battle of Jakku|Battle]] of [[Jakku]], where the squadron's X-wings were tasked with covering the bombers during their bombing runs on the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]'s flagship, ''[[Yadeez]]''.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
