The '''Ascendant personal stealth flyer''' was an experimental [[airspeeder]] developed by [[Sienar Design Systems]]. It was the predecessor to a similarly [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette|experimental corvette]] designed to break through [[blockade]]s during the [[Clone Wars]], but the project was scrapped when the [[corvette]] was destroyed. The Ascendant model was equipped with an advanced [[cloaking device]] that made it appear almost entirely invisible.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony" />
