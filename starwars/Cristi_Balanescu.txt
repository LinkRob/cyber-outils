'''Cristi Balanescu''' is an artist who has illustrated a number of card illustrations for ''[[Star Wars: The Card Game]]'' by [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. He has also provided illustrations for all three of the FFG's roleplaying game branches: [[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire|''Edge of the Empire'']], [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion|''Age of Rebellion'']] and [[Star Wars: Force and Destiny|''Force and Destiny'']].
