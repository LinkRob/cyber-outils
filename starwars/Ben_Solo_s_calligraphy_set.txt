'''Ben Solo's calligraphy set''' was a calligraphy set owned by [[Padawan]] [[Ben Solo]] during his [[Jedi training|training]] in [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]'s [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|Jedi temple]]. It had once belonged to Jedi Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], otherwise known as Ben Kenobi, who used it to write a set of [[The Journals of Ben Kenobi|journals]]<ref name="100 Objects" /> and whom Ben Solo was named after<ref name="TROKR1">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 1|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1]]</ref>. It included a set of pens, a box with perforations in the lid which served as pen sockets, and parchment scrolls with [[Jedi]] insights.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> The calligraphy set was sitting on Solo's nightstand, next to his [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|Jedi lightsaber]], the night he was [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|confronted]] by his [[Skywalker family|uncle]], Skywalker.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>
