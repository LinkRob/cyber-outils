'''Cid's mine''' was a mostly exhausted [[ipsium]] [[Mining|mine]] on the northern territories of [[Ipsidon]].<ref name="Crossing" /><ref name="Retrieval">{{TBB|Retrieval (episode)}}</ref> The [[Trandoshan]] [[Ciddarin Scaleback]] was tricked into purchasing it on the belief there would be a large quantity of the mineral present, although in actuality the mine had been almost completely picked through. As part of their employment by her, [[Clone Force 99]] were forced to work on the mine and extract what little ipsium was left for her.<ref name="Crossing" />
