'''Erta''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who was the girlfriend of [[Moona]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. In [[382 BBY]], shortly before the [[Battle of Jedha]], Moona promised [[Piralli]] to introduce him to Erta.

==Biography==
===Erta and Moona===
During the [[High Republic Era]], Erta and the [[Twi'lek]] [[Moona]] met and they eventually became girlfriends.<ref name="No Such Thing" /> Moona's friend [[Piralli]] noticed that Moona visited the [[Cantina|tapbar]] [[Enlightenment]] less than she did before she had known Erta.<ref name="Last Orders">{{InsiderCite|219|Tales of Enlightenment: Last Orders}}</ref>

===Meeting in Enlightenment===
{{Quote|I'm going to introduce you to her tonight. So, you'd better be on your best behavior.|Moona, to Piralli, about Era|<ref name="No Such Thing" />}}
In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Tales of Enlightenment: No Such Thing as a Bad Customer]]" take place concurrent with the [[Battle of Jedha]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Erta and Moona planned to meet in the tapbar Enlightenment<ref name="No Such Thing" /> on the [[moon]]<ref name="Battle of Jedha">''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]''</ref> [[Jedha]]. Shortly before the [[Battle of Jedha]], the [[Sullustan]] [[Unidentified docks|dock]] worker Piralli talked about his and Moona's friend [[Keth Cerepath]] and that Cerepath spent too much time with [[Silandra Sho]]. When Piralli called San Tekka Cerepath's new friend, Moona told Piralli that she also had a friend and she wanted Piralli to meet her.<ref name="No Such Thing" />

==Personality and traits==
Erta was a small human [[Gender|woman]] with [[Color|brown]] skin and wide [[eye]]s. She was a helpful person and tried to assist the [[Jedi]] when the [[healing house|almshouse]] of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] collapsed.<ref name="No Such Thing" />

==Behind the scenes==
Erta was first mentioned in the first part of the short story "[[Tales of Enlightenment: No Such Thing as a Bad Customer]]" and first appeared in the second part. The story was written by [[George Mann]] and published in the [[Star Wars Insider 217|217th]] and [[Star Wars Insider 217|218th]] issue of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]''<ref name="No Such Thing" /> on [[March 14]] and [[May 2]], [[2023]]<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=insider/subscriptions|text=Star Wars Insider Subscription Information}}</ref> as part of [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]] of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]''.<ref name="Companion">{{InsiderCite|221|Star Wars: The High Republic: Phase II Companion|''Star Wars: The High Republic'': Phase II Companion}}</ref>
