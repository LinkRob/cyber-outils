The '''Allied Armada''' was a [[Navy|naval]] [[squadron]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] that participated in the [[Battle of Xorrn]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. It arrived on [[Xorrn]] several [[Standard day|days]] after the undercover rebel agent [[Nath Alico]] warned the Rebel Alliance about the Empire's plan to attack Xorrn.<ref name="Friends" />

When the force arrived in the [[Xorrn system]], it reinforced the anti-Imperial allied group on the surface and attacked the [[Ralchio Nervi's task force|Imperial task force]] of [[Captain]] [[Ralchio Nervi]] that consisted of the [[Gladiator-class Star Destroyer|''Gladiator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Blood Ambition]]'' and the two [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvettes]]; ''[[Sargantuan]]'' and ''[[Warhawk]]''. The Allied Armada consisted of two [[MC80 Liberty Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80 ''Liberty'' Type Heavy Star Cruisers]], at least one [[MC30c frigate|MC30C Frigate]], one [[GR-75 medium transport]], and five other [[starship]]s. Additionally, the naval squadron contained six [[fighter wing]]s.<ref name="Friends" />
