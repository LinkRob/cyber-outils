A '''commwall''', also known as a '''display wall''', was a type of wall dedicated for use by [[Communications droid|comms droids]] to project images onto. They were [[Color|silver]] and flat as to improve the image of the projection. There was at least one such wall aboard the [[Emissary-class Republic Cruiser|''Emissary''-class Republic Cruiser]] ''[[Third Horizon]]''.<ref name="LotJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>
