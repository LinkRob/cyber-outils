'''Death Watch Warriors''' were [[Mandalorian warrior|warriors]] that served the [[Mandalorian]] terrorist group [[Death Watch]].<ref name="Collapse" /> Led by [[Pre Vizsla]], the warriors of Death Watch were exiled for their intent to restore the ways of [[Mandalore]]'s warrior past, opposing the [[New Mandalorians]] and their pacifist ways. They wore [[Color|blue]] [[Mandalorian armor]] and wielded several types of [[blaster]]s, including the [[WESTAR-35 blaster pistol]], the [[GALAAR-15 blaster carbine]],<ref name="TMP" /> and the [[Relby V-4d blaster rifle]].<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref>
