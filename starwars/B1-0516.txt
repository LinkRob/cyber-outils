'''B1-0516''', nicknamed '''Bats''', was a [[B1-series battle droid]] in the [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], B1-0516 fought in a [[Battle of Horain|battle]] against the [[Galactic Republic]] on [[Horain]], where his [[processor]] was short-circuited by a stray [[blaster]] bolt. The damage caused B1-0516 to fight with the Republic [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]], and assisted [[Jedi General|General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Rex]], who gave the [[battle droid]] the nickname "Bats" for easier pronunciation.<ref name="Roger Roger" />

==Biography==
{{Quote|The blast must have short-circuted its processor.''"<br />"''A battle droid with a ''conscience''. Now, I've seen everything.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Clone Captain Rex, regarding "Bats"|<ref name="Roger Roger" />}}
In [[20 BBY]]<ref name="Star Wars Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>, during the [[Clone Wars]], B1-0516 served in a [[Horain garrison|unit]] of the [[Separatist Droid Army]] on the [[planet]] [[Horain]] in the [[Crantori system]]. While in the midst of a [[Battle of Horain|battle]] with the [[Galactic Republic]], B1-0516 encountered [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] alongside a unit of droids. The Separatists engaged the pair, in the midst of which B1-0516's [[processor]] was damaged by Kenobi's deflection of a [[blaster]] bolt. As the droid came to terms with itself, it began to question why so many [[battle droid]]s were attacking them. Stating that the skirmish seemed unfair, as there were so many droids in contrast to the two of them. This logic convinced the [[droid]] to aid the pair in fighting off the advancing Separatist forces.<ref name="Roger Roger" />

Saluting his new superiors, B1-0516 offered his service to the Republic, gaining the nickname "Bats." As a new wave of Separatist reinforcements began to advance on their position, Bats gained the attention of the [[OOM command battle droid|droid commander]], appearing from behind a downed [[All Terrain Attack Pod]] with Kenobi and Rex. Claiming that the two were his prisoners, the commander complimented Bats on his efforts, stating that the pair must be taken to [[Count]] [[Dooku]] immediately. Showcasing his new affliction, Bats quickly turned on the Separatists, wielding a pair of [[E-5 blaster rifle]]s and destroying several droids while his superiors escaped. Despite putting up a sizable resistance, Bats was overwhelmed and captured, being taken to a nearby Separatist garrison for analysis.<ref name="Roger Roger" />

[[File:Bats_Horain_evacuation_zone.png|left|thumb|255x255px|Bats joined the Republic on Horain.]]
There, the [[Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Horain)|commanding]] [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|tactical droid]] suggested that Bats' original programming be restored with a memory wipe, stating that there were still several [[clone trooper]]s on Horain that required blasting. Resisting this attempt, Bats fought off the nearby guards utilizing the detached arm of another battle droid. The tactical droid attempted to reason with Bats, ordering him to submit to reprocessing immediately. Continuing to resist, Bats prepared to fight off his oppressors until an unregistered [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tank]] breached the garrison. Believing the infiltration the result of another malfunctioning battle droid, the tactical droid ordered tank to be taken down, only for Kenobi to emerge from the tank, quickly dispatching a OOM command droid and rescuing Bats, who had taken to opportunity to assault the T-1 with the dispatched arm. Entering the hijacked tank, Bats and his companions quickly left the garrison and returned to the [[Republic Evacuation Point]]. Reuniting with [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his forces, Kenobi and Rex introduced Skywalker to Bats and stated that they could find an undercover battle droid useful for future missions. Bats then proceeded to board a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic gunship]] with his companions, pleading to receive his own [[lightsaber]] from Skywalker.<ref name="Roger Roger" />
