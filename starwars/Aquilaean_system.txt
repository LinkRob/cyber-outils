The '''Aquilaean system''', or '''Aquilaean systems''', was a [[Star system/Legends|star system]] that contained the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Aquilae (The Star Wars)|Aquilae]]. It was targeted by the [[Galactic Empire (The Star Wars)|New Galactic Empire]], as one of the last [[Independent Systems]], for annexation. [[Crispin Hoedaack]] was appointed its [[Governor/Legends|Governor]] by [[Emperor (The Star Wars)|Emperor]] [[Cos Dashit]]. The planet [[Yavin 4/legends|Yavin 4]] was apparently part of this system, though it was also placed in the [[Forbidden Systems]]. An [[Asteroid field/Legends|asteroid field]] separated Aquilae from Yavin.

==Behind the scenes==
In the [[The Star Wars rough draft|rough draft]] of the script for [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']], the astrography of the Aquilaean system(s) is somewhat confused. The destruction of the Imperial [[Space Fortress]] is visible from Yavin.
