"'''Freedom's Winter'''" is the first episode of [[The Empire Strikes Back (radio)|''The Empire Strikes Back'' radio drama]]. It first aired on [[National Public Radio]] on [[February 14]], [[1983]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> The episode's action begins not long before the events of {{Film|V}}. Most of the episode takes place on the planet [[Hoth/Legends|Hoth]] and depicts the Rebels' struggle to establish [[Echo Base/Legends|Echo Base]]; other scenes take place in the [[Derra system/Legends|Derra system]] and aboard an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer/Legends|Imperial Star Destroyer]].

==Plot summary==
[[File:Narra-ESBRadio.flac|thumb|"Boss" Narra is shot down by TIE fighters over Derra IV.]]
As the episode opens, [[Arhul Narra/Legends|Commander "Boss" Narra]], the leader of [[Renegade Flight]], is giving orders to his pilots as they escort their convoy near the planet [[Derra IV/Legends|Derra IV]]. He tells them to be on alert for Imperial ships. Sure enough, a huge number of TIE fighters approach from all directions and begin an all-out attack. The fighters and transports scramble, but none are able to make the jump to [[Lightspeed/Legends|lightspeed]] in time. The entire fleet, including the fighters, is destroyed.

Elsewhere, a lieutenant brings news of the battle to the imperial [[Lorth Needa/Legends|Captain Needa]]. Far from being pleased, Needa is frustrated that his [[Viper probe droid/Legends|probe droids]] have brought back no useful activity; the victory at Derra IV makes him worry that the war will be over before he sees any success. Before launching the next round of droids, Needa tells the lieutenant that [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] has sent new instructions that must be added to their programming: priority is to be given to finding [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and the ''[[Millennium Falcon/Legends|Millennium Falcon]]''.

On the planet Hoth, Rebel officers meet to discuss the progress of establishing Echo Base, including [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Lieutenant Commander Skywalker's]] work to place sensors to create a regional warning network. Cold-weather supplies are low. [[Carlist Rieekan/Legends|General Rieekan]] says that the situation will improve once the convoy arrives. As the meeting breaks up, Luke approaches the [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Princess Leia]] to talk, but no sooner does he get her attention than [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] interrupt to complain about the slow pace of repairs to the ''[[Millennium Falcon/Legends|Millennium Falcon]].'' This annoys Leia and Luke, who question Han's commitment to the Rebel cause.

Rieekan returns and tells Luke and Leia that he has just received a message detailing the disaster at Derra IV. This will deprive the base of urgently-needed supplies and reinforcements. Rieekan immediately promotes Luke to the rank of [[Commander/Legends|Commander]]; his [[Rogue Squadron/Legends|Rogue Flight]] will now have to defend the base almost single-handedly. Luke tries to ask for some spare techs for the ''Falcon'' on Han's behalf, but Rieekan does not promise anything. As Luke mourns Boss, Leia quietly tells him, "We need you more than ever now."

Luke and Han ride out on tauntauns to place more warning sensors. Luke's mount is nervous. Han mentions seeing a carcass that looked like a killing by a [[Wampa/Legends|wampa]] ice creature. As Han heads back to the base, Luke lingers to investigate a meteor strike. He is attacked as the scene ends.

Back at Echo Base, Leia asks [[C-3PO/Legends|See-Threepio]] if Luke and Han have returned; he and [[R2-D2/Legends|Artoo-Detoo]] tell her no. They next encounter [[K-3PO/Legends|the base's coordinator droid]], who informs them that they have been reassigned - Threepio to the communications center and Artoo to digging duty in the ice caverns. Threepio indignantly tells the coordinator that both he and Artoo are personal aides to Commander Skywalker and Princess Leia, at which point he rescinds the reassignment. Threepio goes on to complain that temperature in Leia's room had been freezing until they turned up the heating system - at which point the coordinator warns them that this could cause the walls to melt. Threepio and Artoo hurry away to correct their mistake.

Luke regains consciousness hanging by his feet in the wampa's cave. His [[Comlink/Legends|comlink]] no longer works. Luke [[Telekinesis/Legends|uses the Force]] to call [[Anakin Skywalker's first lightsaber|his lightsaber]] to him, and with it free himself and kill the wampa. Stranded with no survival gear and no tauntaun, he tries to walk back to base. 

[[File:Luke-Ben-ESBRadio.flac|thumb|Luke struggles to make sense of the appearance of Ben Kenobi's ghost.]]
Han arrives at Echo Base. He checks on Chewbacca, who is slowly working - alone - on repairs to the ''Falcon''. Back outside, an exhausted and dazed Luke sees the [[Force ghost/Legends|spirit]] of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Ben Kenobi]]. Luke at first doubts what he is seeing, but Ben tells him to go to [[Dagobah/Legends|Dagobah]] to seek [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]]. The figure then vanishes as night begins to fall.

==Continuity==
[[File:Derra-181st.jpg|thumb|TIE fighters attack the Rebel convoy at Derra IV in ''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' 25.]]
"Freedom's Winter", like the opening episode "[[A Wind to Shake the Stars]]" in the first [[Star Wars (radio)|''Star Wars'' radio drama]], opens with several scenes not in the film. The [[Battle of Derra IV]] makes its first appearance, ending with the destruction of a Rebel supply convoy and of [[Renegade Flight]]. The battle was later illustrated in the [[1997]] comic [[X-Wing Rogue Squadron 25|''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' 25]] in a flashback scene that incorporated some dialogue from the radio episode.<ref name="X-Wing Rogue Squadron 25">[[X-Wing Rogue Squadron 25|''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' 25]]</ref> 

In another added scene, [[Lorth Needa/Legends|Captain Needa's]] [[Star Destroyer/Legends|Star Destroyer]] ''[[Avenger/Legends|Avenger]]'' is shown to be the ship that launched the Imperial [[Viper probe droid/Legends|probe droid]] to the Hoth system. Later [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] sources would inconsistently identify both the ''Avenger'' and another Star Destroyer, the ''[[Stalker/Legends|Stalker]]'', as the ship launching the droid.<ref name="Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back">''[[Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back]]''</ref><ref name="Inside the Worlds of Star Wars Trilogy">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars Trilogy]]''</ref><ref name="The Essential Atlas">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>

The first scenes in Echo Base are also original with Daley's script. Leia and Rieekan discuss the base's ongoing construction and their urgent need for more supplies&mdash;creating dramatic irony since the audience already knows that the convoy was destroyed. Han argues with Rieekan over help repairing the ''Falcon'', showing that he has not completely shed his selfish side. The episode also depicts Leia and Rieekan getting news of the battle, Luke's promotion to commander, and See-Threepio's confrontation with a coordinator droid. The coordinator droid was identified in [[1996]] as [[K-3PO/Legends|K-3PO]].<ref name="Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back Second Edition">[[Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back (Second Edition)|''Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back'', Second Edition]]</ref> His conversation with Threepio adds some detail to how the droids inadvertently caused the walls of the princess's quarters to melt, an event alluded to in the film. After this, the episode catches up to the events of the film's opening scenes.

Luke at first is shocked to see Ben Kenobi and doubts that the apparition can really be him; he even says, "It can't be... I saw you die," despite having heard the dead Jedi's voice in ''A New Hope,'' both [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|the film]] and [[Star Wars (radio)|the radio adaptation]].<ref name="Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Kenobi's spirit also made contact with Luke during the time before these events in multiple other [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] materials.<ref name="Allegiance novel">[[Allegiance (novel)|''Allegiance'']]</ref><ref name="Splinter of the Mind's Eye">''[[Splinter of the Mind's Eye]]''</ref>
