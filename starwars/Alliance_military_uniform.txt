The [[Alliance Military|military]] forces of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] wore a wide array of '''uniforms''' in the build up to and during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. These uniforms differed based on the role of an individual within the military and the environment they were stationed in.

==Description==
===Hoth===
On the icy [[planet]] [[Hoth]], the Alliance personnel had to survive in sub-zero temperatures and as such they wore specialized snow gear to help them survive.<ref name="CEUE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> The highest-ranking personnel stationed in the Alliance's [[Echo Base]], such as [[General]]s [[Carlist Rieekan]] and [[Pharl McQuarrie]] and [[Major]] [[Bren Derlin]] wore an insulated [[Color|brown]] [[cold-weather issue command parka]] bearing their [[rank insignia plaque]],<ref name="Bust">{{GentleGiantCite|set=2015 Convention Exclusive|pack=General McQuarrie Collectible Mini Bust|link=General-McQuarrie-Mini-Bust-Gentle-Giant-Celebration-Anaheim.asp}}</ref> a brown [[utility belt]], a pair of white [[Command gauntlet]]s,<ref name="CEUE" /> white pants, gray [[boot]]s and an [[insulated watch cap]], which had a [[Comlink|com set]], a face wrap, two cap warmers and a set of [[Anti-glare goggles]] all equipped.<ref name="Bust" />

Lower ranking [[Alliance officer|officers]] in the base such as [[Lieutenant]]s [[Cal Alder]] and [[Tigran Jamiro]] wore the same headgear, pants, boots and [[gauntlet]]s as the higher ranking officers, but used a sleeveless [[jacket]] over a white [[Clothing|shirt]] instead of the command parka. The base's communication center staff wore a variation of the lower ranking officer's outfit with a communication headset rather than the watch cap and with a much higher collar on their sleeveless jackets, matching those of the command parka. The base's [[technician]]s wore another variant of the lower ranking officer's uniform which used brown gloves and a simpler [[Hat|cap]].<ref name="ESB" />

The [[Cold-weather Soldier|soldiers]] of the bases like [[Colonel]] [[Ledick Firest]] again wore the same headgear and boots, but wore a shorter pair of white [[glove]]s, gray or brown pants, and made use of a [[Thermal flak jacket/Canon|thermal flak jacket]]<ref name="CEUE" /> and bulky [[backpack]].<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The [[pilot]]s stationed at the base wore a variant of the usual [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps]] uniform, with a high-collared orange jacket over the normal flightsuit and white command gauntlets rather than black gloves.<ref name="ESB" />

[[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] wore an outfit distinct from other personnel that was symbolically white due to her role as princess of the [[Destruction of Alderaan|lost]] world [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Complete Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> The outfit consisted of a rebel-issue, white [[all weather vest]]<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> that was heated, over a white [[insulated jumpsuit]] with a pair of gray [[military snow boot]]s with bindings. [[Smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] also wore a visually distinct uniform, wearing a dark brown [[heavy weather parka]] with a fur-lined hood along with a pair of gray pants and boots.<ref name="Complete Dictionary" />
