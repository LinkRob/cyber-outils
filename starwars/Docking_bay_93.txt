'''Docking bay 93''' was one of the numerous [[Hangar|docking bays]] located in the [[spaceport]] [[City|town]] [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. It was situated adjacent to [[Docking Bay 94]].<ref name="100 Scenes" />

==Behind the scenes==
Docking bay 93 was mentioned in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]'', written by [[Daniel Lipkowitz]].<ref name="100 Scenes" />
