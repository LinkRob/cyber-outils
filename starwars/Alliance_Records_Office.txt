The '''Alliance Records Office''' was operated by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] on the astronomical object [[Fusai]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. According to the rebel [[3PO-series protocol droid|protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]], [[Translator|translating]] and datawork was among the work carried out at the records office.<ref name="Star Wars 19" />

By [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars (2015) 19|''Star Wars'' (2015) 19]] to [[0 ABY]].</ref> C-3PO had heard from a rebel [[Unidentified R5 series astromech droid|R5 astromech droid unit]] that the office on Fusai was looking for [[protocol droid]] staff. After an [[Prison siege at Sunspot|attack]] on the Alliance's<ref name="Star Wars 19" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Sunspot Prison]]<ref name="Star Wars 19" /> that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> C-3PO told the [[R2-series astromech droid|astromech]] [[R2-D2]] that<ref name="Star Wars 19" /> he<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> planned to request a transfer to the Fusai records office, believing the work there to be safer and more suitable for him. R2-D2 then asked if<ref name="Star Wars 19" /> he<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> could accompany C-3PO, to which the protocol droid stated he could not as there were no openings for [[Astromech droid|astromechs]] at the office.<ref name="Star Wars 19" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Alliance Records Office was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars (2015) 19|nineteenth issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']]. The issue was written by [[Jason Aaron]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 19" /> on [[May 25]], [[2016]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/55768/star_wars_2015_19|text=Star Wars (2015) #19}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, a [[Fusai library|library]] on<ref name="The New Essential Guide to Droids">''[[The New Essential Guide to Droids]]''</ref> the [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="Complete Encyclopedia">{{CSWECite|I|299|Fusai}}</ref> [[Fusai/Legends|Fusai]] that housed the [[BRT supercomputer/Legends|BRT supercomputer]]<ref name="The New Essential Guide to Droids" /> [[Mistress Mnemos]] was operated as a stronghold by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] and first appeared in the comic story "[[Gambler's World]]." The story was written and illustrated by [[Russ Manning]]<ref name="Gambler's World">"[[Gambler's World]]"</ref> and published between [[March 12]] and [[September 8]], [[1979]]<ref name="Classic Newspaper Comics">''[[Star Wars: The Classic Newspaper Comics Vol. 1]]''</ref> as part of the daily [[Star Wars (comic strip)|''Star Wars'' comic strip]] distributed by the [[Los Angeles Times Syndicate]] to major American and Canadian newspapers from 1979 to [[1984]].<ref name="Insider2012">{{InsiderCite|Special Edition 2012|The Empire Strips Back}}</ref>
