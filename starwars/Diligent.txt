{{Quote|With each day we see new ships join us. Just today the veterans of Ryloth joined us in the Star Destroyer ''Diligent''.|[[Grand Moff]] [[Randd]], to Counselor to the Empire Gallius Rax|<ref name="Empire's End" />}}
The '''''Diligent''''' was a [[Star Destroyer]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] that,<ref name="Empire's End" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[Battle of Jakku]] to [[5 ABY]].</ref> traveled from the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] to join the Imperial forces on the planet [[Jakku]]. This was in order to fight against the [[New Republic]] in the [[Battle of Jakku]], under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Counselor to the Empire]] [[Gallius Rax]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Diligent'' was first mentioned in the [[2017]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', written by [[Chuck Wendig]].<ref name="Empire's End" />
