The '''Enhanced Privacy Invasion Bill''' was an infamous law<ref name="Rise" /> due to be debated by the [[Galactic Senate]] during the [[Clone Wars]] when the [[Senate hostage crisis]] took place. A group of [[senator]]s including [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] and [[Padmé Amidala]] agreed to oppose the bill when [[Cad Bane]]'s gang of [[bounty hunter]]s took control of the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]], demanding [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] the [[Hutt]]'s release from [[prison]].<ref name="Hostage Crisis" />

The bill would allow the [[Supreme Chancellor]] the unprecedented ability<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> and broad powers<ref name="Rise" /> to spy on [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] citizens who were suspected as being Separatists.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> However, the bill's defeat was ultimately spearheaded by the political resistance group it galvanized. Still, the process had revealed the Galactic Republic's contradictory hopes: a want for both peace and [[Republic Military|military]] victories.<ref name="Rise" />
