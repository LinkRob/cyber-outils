'''Dxun''' was one of the four [[moon]]s of the [[planet]] [[Onderon]], located in the [[Japrael system]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim's]] [[Japrael sector]].<ref name="BMF 57" /> The moon was situated at grid square O-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Grid" /> It shared its name with [[Dxunwood]].<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dxun was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 57|fifty-seventh issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 57" /> published around [[February 3]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdates">{{BMFdates|57}}</ref> The [[Dxun/Legends|moon]] first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[Tales of the Jedi 1]]'', the first issue of the [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (miniseries)|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'']] miniseries authored by [[Tom Veitch]]<ref name="Tales 1">[[Tales of the Jedi 1|''Tales of the Jedi'' 1]]</ref> and published on [[October 19]], [[1993]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54566/star_wars_tales_of_the_jedi_1993_1|text=Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (1993) #1}}</ref>
