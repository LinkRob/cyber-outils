'''Camba-fruit''' was a type of [[fruit]] that could be easily sliced. While [[Jedi training|training]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="CYD" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]]<ref name="CYD" /> in [[26 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure]]'' to [[26 BBY]].</ref> [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] used the [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's second lightsaber|lightsaber]] of his Master, the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], to cut through a [[Marksman-H training remote|training drone]] with ease, similar to slicing a camba-fruit.<ref name="CYD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Camba-fruit was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure]]'', a [[2019]] novel in the ''[[Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny]]'' series written by [[Cavan Scott]].<ref name="CYD" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Camba-Fruit]] was introduced in [[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']], a [[1995]] novel authored by [[Barbara Hambly]].<ref name="COTJ">[[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']]</ref>
