'''FE-1211'''<ref name="Phasma" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> [[Gender|female]] who was training to be a [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] aboard the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Absolution]]''. She was taught by [[Captain]] [[Archex|Cardinal]], always scoring the highest scores in his [[intelligence test]]ing and had a mean [[eye]] with a [[blaster]]. On the [[Standard day|day]] Cardinal was to attend a meeting on the ''Absolution'' with individuals such as [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] and Captain [[Phasma]], FE-1211 along with [[FB-0007]] and [[FM-0676]] briefly spoke with Cardinal whilst he sat in the [[Starship|vessel's]] cafeteria. Before returning to his quarters, Cardinal told the group he would be checking their scores after his meeting. After being excluded from the meeting, Cardinal returned to the cafeteria where he learned that FB-0007 had done well when FE-1211 was out of the picture, Cardinal then made a note to himself to shuffle around the groups to ensure that the conniving FE-1211 wouldn't get all the glory.<ref name="Phasma" />
