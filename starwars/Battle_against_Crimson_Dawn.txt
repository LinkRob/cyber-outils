Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''battle''' was fought between the [[Crimson Dawn]]<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" /> [[Fortress]]-[[Flagship]]<ref name="Aphra 23">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 23|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 23]]</ref> [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']] and the [[Galactic Empire]]. After becoming aware that the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]] was hunting her, Crimson Dawn [[Crime lord|leader]] [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]] activated her [[Sleeper Agent|spies]] within the various major powers of [[the galaxy]] to create chaos in order to buy her [[time]] to follow through with her plans. Qi'ra hid with the [[Orphans]] on an astronomical object aboard their [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Siroto]]'', where they were eventually discovered by the [[Imperial Navy]]. The Empire deployed an [[Interdictor-class Star Destroyer|''Interdictor''-class]] to prevent Qi'ra and her flagship from escaping.<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" />

As Qi'ra spoke personally with Sidious and outlined her plans to destroy him, Qi'ra sent the ''Vermillion'' to attack the [[interdictor]]. The ''Vermillion'' was attacked by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and [[TIE/IN interceptor]]s, but they were unable to prevent the ship from crashing directly into the interdictor, destroying both. [[Escape pod]]s were deployed from the ''Vermillion'', and Sidious ordered they all be destroyed, allowing for no one to escape.<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" />

The destruction of the ''Vermillion'' allowed for Qi'ra and the Oprhans to escape from the Imperials aboard the ''Siroto'' and as they left, [[Chanath Cha]] asked Qi'ra if they should pick up any potential survivors from the escape pods, Qi'ra thought for a moment before declining, adding that they could honor their sacrifice by seeing her plans through to the end.<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" />

The fact that Qi'ra survived was later inadvertently revealed to Vader by Cha. When Cha [[Battle against the Orphans|confronted]] Vader, he informed her that she would not avenge Qi'ra's death, which surprised the hunter. Vader sensed this and realized that Qi'ra had actually survived, adding that his Master would be fascinated to learn of this.<ref name="Hidden Empire 3" />
