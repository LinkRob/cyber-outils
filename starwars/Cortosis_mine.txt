During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|pro-separatist]] [[Duke]] [[Solha]] built a '''cortosis mine''' following the orders of the Separatist movement's [[Head of State]], [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] orders, the [[Mining|mine]] was built to extract [[cortosis]] from the [[planet]] [[Mokivj]], the mine was located near a [[river]] and the [[Mokivj droid factory]], where the cortosis was refined and used to manufacture [[C-B3 cortosis battle droid|cortosis B2 super battle droids]]. During the [[Mission to Mokivj|attack]] on the factory, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] or [[Core name|Thrawn]] destroyed the mine and a section of the factory in an effort to cancel any possibilities of the Separatists constructing more cortosis battle droids or Clone armor. The destruction of the factory and the mine rendered Mokivj barren, and activated [[Volcano|volcanic]] activity that destroyed the grasslands and best [[Farm|croplands]] of the planet.

==History==
When [[Count]] [[Dooku]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] received a tip from a [[Grysk|mysterious]] [[species]] that [[cortosis]] could resist [[lightsaber]]s, and was located on [[Mokivj]], Dooku dispatched [[Duke]] [[Solha]] as well as his [[Solha's brother|brother]] and [[Solha's sister|sister]]. They took over the [[planet]] and established a [[Mining|mine]] on the planet, and soon, a [[Mokivj droid factory|droid factory]].<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />

[[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] and [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] raided the droid factory. During the raid, the humans and Chiss discovered that the Separatists were manufacturing a [[cortosis]]-armored [[C-B3 cortosis battle droid|model]] of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s on the planet, using cortosis sourced to the nearby cortosis mine. Padmé and Anakin resolved to prevent the Separatists from continuing to manufacture such fortified droids. Thrawn procured a [[deflector shield generator]], which he termed a "Republic energy shield," for the Ascendancy.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />

The mission concluded with a catastrophe. In an effort to collapse the Separatists' cortosis mine on Mokivj, Anakin Skywalker accidentally sent [[Magma|lava]], ash, and smoke into the planet's best cropland and [[water]]. Skywalker did not account for the cortosis in the [[Mining|mine]] redirecting the heat of the explosions. As a result, the explosive force did more than merely collapse the mine's tunnels. It was sent down the planet's crust, triggering [[Volcano|volcanic activity]] and leading to widespread devastation, deforestation, and desertification on Mokivj.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />

Later, the cortosis mine was mentioned in a [[Clone Intelligence]] report describing the C-B3 cortosis battle droid, which was included in the survival compendium "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]."<ref name="Official Magazine 118">{{GermanFanCite|118|Database}}</ref>
