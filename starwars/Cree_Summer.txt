'''Cree Summer''' (born [[July 7]], [[1960s#1969|1969]]) provided the voice of [[Princess/Legends|Princess]] [[Kneesaa a Jari Kintaka/Legends|Kneesaa a Jari Kintaka]] for the first season of [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']] and [[Luminara Unduli/Legends|Luminara Unduli]] for the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' cartoon. She also portrayed [[Hylo Visz]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Fallen Empire]]'' and [[Marlaa Jinara]] in ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''.

Her father, [[Don Francks]], performed multiple voices in the [[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO|Droids]] and [[Ewoks (television series)|Ewoks]] cartoon series.
