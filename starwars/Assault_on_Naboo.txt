In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> an '''assault on Naboo''' was carried out by the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Republic Defense Coalition]] in order to liberate<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Naboo]] which was under [[Occupation of Naboo|occupation]] from [[Nihil]] [[Ministry of Protection|forces]] led by [[General]]<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Abediah Viess]].<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4" /> While the [[Jedi]] and [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] forces led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] were able to successfully free Naboo from the Nihil, they were unable to prevent Viess from escaping.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
