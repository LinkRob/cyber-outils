'''Amnesty Housing''' was a housing complex on [[Coruscant]] that provided accommodation to members of the [[New Republic Amnesty Program]]. [[Doctor]] [[Penn Pershing]], [[Elia Kane]],<ref name="Chapter 19" /> and their fellow parolees lived in modest apartments<ref name="World Building">{{InsiderCite|227|World Building (Star Wars Insider 227)}}</ref> at the complex.<ref name="Chapter 19" />
