In [[40 BBY]], the [[Hall of Assembly]] building on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]] was '''destroyed''' with [[Nanotechnology|nanotech]] by [[Pijali]] [[blackguard]]s secretly working for the Pijali [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] and the [[royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] [[captain]] [[Deren]], as the [[Gender|princess]] hoped to prevent the [[Governance Treaty]] initiative's signing by sowing [[Blackguard campaign|unrest]] in the [[Pijal system]]. Afterward, the structure's [[security camera]]s showed how its [[mirror]]s had been dissolved during the strike. Deren kept a copy of the [[Hologram|holographic]] recording of the incident, which he later provided to [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], for their [[Mission to Pijal|investigation]] into the unrest within the [[Pijal system]]. Though the [[Opposition]] performance troupe was initially blamed for the incident, the group's name was cleared after Fanry's violent [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|crowning]]&mdash;in which she attempted to seize power&mdash;failed.

==Prelude==
As the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]]'s [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]] neared<ref name="MA" /> in [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] and the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]'s [[Mission to Pijal (Rael Averross)|serving]] [[lord]] [[regent]] [[Rael Averross]] used the results of a [[Pijal census|census]] of the [[Pijal system]]'s population to draft the [[Governance Treaty]] initiative, which would reorganize the reigning [[Pijali monarchy]] into a relatively powerless constitutional [[monarchy]] and cede power to a [[Democracy|representative]] [[Assembly (Pijal)|Assembly]]. However, Fanry felt that Czerka had been exploiting her world's citizens by unfairly [[Slavery|enslaving]] them and did not wish to sign the treaty upon her [[crown]]ing, hoping to retain her power as future [[queen]]. Averross, meanwhile, felt that the Governance Treaty would benefit Pijal by allowing the Assembly to open the [[Pijal Hyperspace Corridor]] [[hyperspace route]] in conjunction with the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="MA" />

==Dissolving the mirrors==
Fanry and her [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] [[captain]] [[Deren]] began secretly organizing a contingent of Pijali [[blackguard]] [[soldier]]s to carry out a [[Blackguard campaign|campaign]] throughout the Pijal [[Star system|system]], hoping to sow enough unrest in the system to prevent the treaty's signing. As one of those attacks, the blackguards used [[Nanotechnology|nanotech]] to dissolve the [[mirror]]s forming the framework of the [[Hall of Assembly]]&mdash;a building intended to serve as the Assembly's new headquarters&mdash;with each panel turning cloudy, then gray, then crumbling to dust.<ref name="MA" />

==Aftermath==
[[File:JediMasterAndPadawan-TPM.png|thumb|right|300px|Qui-Gon Jinn (right) and Obi-Wan Kenobi (left) investigated the upheavals in the Pijal system, including the Hall of Assembly's destruction.]]
In the aftermath of the event, many Pijali were confused about what had happened to the building, which had been intended to be a symbol of incoming democracy. The inquiry into the incident afterward eventually showed investigators [[Hologram|holographic]] [[security camera]] footage displaying how nanotech had eroded the building from within.<ref name="MA" />

The Hall of Assembly's destruction was blamed on the [[Opposition]]&mdash;a [[Politics|political]] performance troupe known for advocating against the Governance Treaty&mdash;and Deren stored a recording of the event. Some [[Standard day|days]] later, Deren gave the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], the recording of the Hall of Assembly's destruction while they were [[Mission to Pijal|investigating]] several recent upheavals on Pijal. Eventually, following Fanry's unsuccessful attempt to crown herself queen during her violent coronation, the Opposition was cleared of blame for several of the blackguards' strikes, including the destruction of the Hall of Assembly.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack appeared in a hologram in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
