A [[human]] [[Imperial officer|officer]] served as the '''subordinate''' of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Governor]] [[Ahr]]. After [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] disobeyed Ahr and was ordered by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] to follow Ahr's commands, the governor attempted to cause the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord's]] [[death]]. During an attempt to get Vader killed by a [[Unidentified creature (Kankalo Belt Containment Zone)|creature]] that resided in the [[Kankalo Belt Containment Zone]], the [[Sith Lord]] led the creature back to Ahr's [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], where it broke through into the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], instead killing Ahr and the other crew.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
