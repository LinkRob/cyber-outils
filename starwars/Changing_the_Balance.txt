"'''Changing the Balance'''" is the first adventure in ''[[The Bissillirus Campaign]]''.

==Plot summary==
A group of Alliance operatives on [[Draenell's Point]] learn that the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] is planning to expand their influence on the planet. Traveling to the [[Sector/Legends|sector]] [[Capital/Legends|capital]], [[Deysum III]], the group infiltrates the [[Imperial Resources Base]] and discovers that the Empire is planning to construct the [[Bissillirus Resupply Base]] in the system.
