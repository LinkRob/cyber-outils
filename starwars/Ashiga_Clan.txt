The '''Ashiga Clan''' was a [[Melitto hive]] and criminal syndicate that ruled the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. Coming to power during the [[High Republic Era]], they continued to dominate the Kijimi underworld during the [[Imperial Era]].

==Organization==
The Ashiga Clan was a powerful dynasty<ref name="Overview">{{Twitter|username=StarWarsOutlaws|url=status/1824838649650749743|name=''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''|date=2024-08-17|quote=Order and hierarchy are the tenets of the Ashiga Clan - will you stay in their good books or be just another sacrifice for the cause?|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/Jb6Up}}</ref> with a strict code of honor and based around a hierarchical [[Melitto]] hive. With their entire worldview being about the survival of the collective,<ref name="OutlawsSetting">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/exclusive-interview/2023/08/15/star-wars-outlaws-setting-is-a-dream-playground-according-to|text=Star Wars Outlaws' Setting Is A Dream Playground, According To Narrative Director|work=Game Informer|author=Kyle Hillard|date=2023-08-15|access-date=2023-08-30|archivedate=20230827125620}}</ref> individual clan members were willing to give their lives in the name of the whole, which, in the chaotic underworld, gave the Ashiga an advantage.<ref name="Overview" /> Everything and everyone was judged on their usefulness to the whole.<ref name="OutlawsSetting" /> Major tenets within the Ashiga way of life were hierarchy and order.<ref name="Overview" />

==History==
====Early History====
{{Quote|The Ashiga Clan's been running the underworld here for decades, [...]|ND-5, to Kay Vess, regarding the Ashiga Clan's control over Kijimi|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
The Ashiga Clan was formed during the [[High Republic Era]]. The Clan was based on the world of [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> The Ashiga worked their way into every aspect of Kijimi's underworld, from smuggling to extortion or sabotage and even weapons manufacturing.<ref name="OutlawsSetting" /> As such, the Ashiga Clan ruled over both the planet and its underworld for generations.<ref name="Overview" />

====Imperial Era====
{{Quote|We don't need the Outer Rim. Our place is here!|Krisk Ashiga, to Queen Ashiga|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
The [[Melitto hive]] remained active during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaw Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> however, their control over Kijimi was starting to weaken.<ref name="Overview" /> The Ashiga Clan started to provide the [[Galactic Empire]] access to the planet and came into a partnership. for its valuable [[rhydonium]] mines in exchange for weapons contracts. The arrangement with the Empire was something that made [[Krisk Ashiga]], the heiress of the Ashiga Clan hive and crime syndicate, question the leadership of her mother, [[Ashiga|Queen Ashiga]]. Krisk made a deal with [[Qi'ra]], the leader of the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime syndicate, to dethrone her mother and assume leadership of the Ashiga Clan as the new queen. Crimson Dawn helped Krisk, as the change in leadership could help Crimson Dawn establish its control over Kijimi. In the power struggle between the Ashiga Clan and the Crimson Dawn, the Crimson Dawn stole a relic important to the hive. However, the relic would be recovered and delivered to Queen Ashiga by the thief [[Kay Vess]], who did this to try to free [[Ank Parako]], who was being held by the Ashiga Clan because of a debt of millions of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Clan's]] weapons were stolen by Zerek Besh, but were intercepted by the Kay Vess, who was hired by the Ashiga Clan to steal the weapons.<ref name="Outlaws" />
