The '''dual excavator drill-equipped mining crawler''' was a massive mobile [[mining]] vehicle used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. The crawler rolled on treads and was equipped with an enormous drill apparatus on each flank. Imperial forces brought one to [[Joetz]] to exploit [[coaxium]] veins under the [[planet]]'s pole. It was [[Reconnaissance mission on Joetz|destroyed]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Princess]] [[Crane (princess)|Crane]] and her companions, the [[astromech droid]] [[R9-TR2]] and the young [[Gigoran]], [[Woopas]].<ref name="Four Wings" />

==Behind the scenes==
The mining transport first appeared in "[[The Song of Four Wings]]", a [[Canon|non-canon]] short film that was released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume ]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Project Studio Q]], was released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Four Wings" /> Its name is identified in [[aurebesh]] text that appears above a schematic projected by the droid [[R9-TR2]].<ref name="Four Wings" /> The transport has the overall shape of a [[Jawa]] [[sandcrawler]]. Director [[Hiroyasu Kobayashi]] chose this design because, like the episode's AT-AT walkers, the sandcrawler is a classic giant mech from the [[Original trilogy|original ''Star Wars'' trilogy]] and he felt the two designs would complement one another.<ref name=CinemaToday>{{WebCite|url=https://www.cinematoday.jp/news/N0151763|text=『スター・ウォーズ』プリンセスがドロイドアーマー装着！メカ愛とロマンあふれるアニメ誕生の裏側|date=2025-11-08|work=CINEMATODAY|language=Japanese|archiveurl=https://archive.is/YrQhV}}</ref>
