'''Explosive micro-rockets''' were a class of [[Wrist rocket|micro-rocket]] that were designed to destroy groups of [[infantry]]. The rockets typically sold for 150 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and were the most popular class of micro-rocket on the market. Explosive micro-rockets used a [[chepatite impact explosive]] to create a blast of energy. The rockets were not powerful enough to break through full body [[armor]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
