"'''Bounties to Die For'''" is an article appearing in [[Star Wars Gamer 6|''Star Wars Gamer'' 6]]. Written by [[S. Mitch Ritchie]], with illustrations by [[Ramón Pérez]], it takes the form of a database of 10 bounties from the [[Imperial Enforcement DataCore/Legends|Imperial Enforcement DataCore]] that characters in the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' can track down.
