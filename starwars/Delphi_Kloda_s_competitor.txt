The retired [[pirate]] [[Delphi Kloda]] sent his fourteen [[Standard year|year]] old protege [[Bazine Netal]] to steal from one of his [[Gender|male]] '''competitors''' as her first job for him. She attempted to use her looks to seduce the mark, but he realized her plan almost immediately and attacked her with a [[flamethrower]]. Suffering burns to the left side of her scalp and losing much of her [[hair]], Netal still managed to draw a knife from each of her [[boot]]s and [[Death|kill]] the competitor, going on to successfully steal what Kloda had sent her for. She later told the spy-in-training [[Orri Tenro]] about her encounter with the competitor in order to teach him the importance of remaining unseen while on a job.<ref name="TPW" />

==Behind the scenes==
The competitor was first mentioned, but not named, in the short story "[[The Perfect Weapon]]," which was written by [[Delilah S. Dawson]] and released as an [[eBook]] in [[2015]].
