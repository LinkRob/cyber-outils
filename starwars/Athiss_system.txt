The '''Athiss system''' was located in the [[Esstran sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in [[the galaxy]]. The [[Star system|system]] was located in grid square R-4 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Athiss system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the article "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> The [[Loro Babis system|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1996]] sourcebook ''[[Tales of the Jedi Companion]]'' for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="TotJ">''[[Tales of the Jedi Companion]]''</ref>
