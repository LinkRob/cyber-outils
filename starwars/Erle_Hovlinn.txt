'''Erle Hovlinn''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who served as a [[Lieutenant Colonel]] in the [[Imperial Army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. [[Lieutenant]] [[Colonel]] Hovlinn was stationed on the ''[[Blood Ambition]]'', the [[flagship]] of a [[Ralchio Nervi's task force|task force]] under the command of [[Captain]] [[Ralchio Nervi]], and led its [[Erle Hovlinn's invasion force|complement]] of ground forces. Hovlinn and his forces participated in the [[Battle of Xorrn]].<ref name="Friends" />
