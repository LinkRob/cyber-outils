'''Ankarres sapphires''' were a type of [[gemstone]] known for their [[Color|blue]] hue.<ref name="Rising Storm" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> while being shown around the [[Carnival District|grounds]] of the [[Valo Republic Fair]] by [[Coordinator]] [[Samera Ra-oon]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]] compared the shade of the sky of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]] on a [[summer]] [[Standard day|day]] to an Ankarres [[sapphire]].<ref name="Rising Storm" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ankarres sapphires were mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Rising Storm" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase II">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Ankarres Sapphire]] was an individual [[Gemstone/Legends|gemstone]] which first appeared in the short story "[[Of Possible Futures: The Tale of Zuckuss and 4-LOM]]" by [[M. Shayne Bell]], published in the [[1996]] anthology ''[[Tales of the Bounty Hunters]]''.<ref name="Of Possible Futures">{{StoryCite|story=Of Possible Futures: The Tale of Zuckuss and 4-LOM|book=Tales of the Bounty Hunters}}</ref> The [[Sapphire/Legends|sapphire]] was first depicted in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']].<ref name="SWG">{{SWGcite|exp=AED|type=item|Rare Ankarres Sapphire}}</ref>
