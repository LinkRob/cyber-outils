'''Cadomai Prime''',<ref name="Alien Archive" /> also known as simply '''Cadomai''',<ref name="Beginner Game" /> was a [[planet]]<ref name="Alien Archive" /> located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]], situated along the [[Hydian Way]]<ref name="Beginner Game" /> that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Snivvian]] [[species]]. The [[winter]]s of Cadomai Prime were very harsh for the Snivvians to survive, during which they spent the [[time]] in underground [[cave]]rns in [[artist]]ic pursuits. Some did escape the [[Water|icy]] tundras to become [[bounty hunter]]s offworld.<ref name="Alien Archive" />
