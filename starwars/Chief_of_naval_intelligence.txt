The '''chief of naval intelligence''' was a position within the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. An [[Unidentified chief of naval intelligence|individual]] held the title during the war and was in a meeting to review progress on [[Project Stardust]] at the [[Strategic Planning Amphitheater]] in the [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|Republic Center for Military Operations]].<ref name="Catalyst" />
