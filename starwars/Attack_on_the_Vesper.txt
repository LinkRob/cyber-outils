The '''attack on the ''Vesper'''''<ref name="Part Three">{{Ahsoka|Part Three: Time to Fly}}</ref> was a event that occurred in [[9 ABY]], when the former [[Jedi Knight]] [[Baylan Skoll]] and his apprentice [[Shin Hati]] boarded and attacked the [[New Republic]] [[Defender-class cruiser|''Defender''-class cruiser]] ''[[Vesper]]'' to rescue [[Morgan Elsbeth]]. The [[cruiser]] was scuttled and destroyed.<ref name="Part One" />

==Prelude==
During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Morgan Elsbeth]], a [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] and [[witch]], joined the [[Galactic Empire]]'s war effort, charging several of her [[factory|factories]] and [[shipyard]]s with building heavy machinery for the [[Imperial Navy]]'s fleet.<ref name="Part Two">{{Ahsoka|Part Two: Toil and Trouble}}</ref> Following the collapse of the Empire and the formation of the [[New Republic]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], Elsbeth, a close ally of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] hid on the [[planet]] [[Corvus]] where she ruled as a [[Magistrate]] of the [[city|village]] [[Calodan]].<ref name="Chapter 13">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref> 

Several [[standard year|years]] later, former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] began searching for Elsbeth, believing her to hold the key for Thrawn's location and fearing the former Grand Admiral's re-emergence. Discovering her whereabouts in Corvus, Tano repeatedly attempted to gain access to Calodan, but she was repelled by the village's [[scout guard]]s. Eventually, in [[9 ABY]], [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] arrived to the planet searching for Tano after learning of her location by her former ally [[Bo-Katan Kryze]]. After finding the Jedi, Djarin asked for her help in training his [[foundling]], [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], who was also a Padawan like Tano. In exchange, she asked for his help in retaking the city and capturing Elsbeth, which Djarin did. After a fierce duel, Tano successfully captured the Magistrate<ref name="Chapter 13" /> and delivered her to the New Republic for questioning.<ref name="Part One" />

==Attack of the "Jedi"==
Elsbeth was detained by the [[New Republic Defense Force]] and boarded the ''[[Vesper]]'' to be carried to ''[[Home One]]'' for questioning by New Republic [[officer]]s. After dropping out of [[hyperspace]] close to ''Home One''{{'s}} position in [[Sector G-38]], the [[Unidentified New Republic nav droid|navigation droid]] detected an [[Baylan's shuttle|''Eta''-class shuttle]] approaching their ship and requested their clearance codes. The [[Eta-class shuttle|shuttle]] transmitted an old Jedi code, much to the crew's surprise, requesting to land on board the ship and question the prisoners. [[Captain]] [[Hayle]], suspecting the actions of Imperial collaborators, ordered the crew to allow the ship to land on the cruiser to apprehend them alongside Elsbeth.<ref name="Part One" />

Hayle personally greeted the shuttle's occupants, [[Baylan Skoll]] and [[Shin Hati]] on board alongside eight [[New Republic soldier|Defense Force soldiers]], and asked them to peacefully surrender. In response, Hati lunged at the soldiers, killing two of them before they had time to react, and Skoll informed Hayle that they were indeed not Jedi before killing him as well. Watching the massacre from the ship's [[security camera]]s, [[Executive officer]] [[Jensen Corbyt]] placed the ship on high alert and sealed off the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] in an attempt to buy time until they reached the main fleet; however Skoll neutralized all defenses, slaughtering the soldiers, and Hati reached the bridge before it was secured, killing Corbyt and the crew stationed there. After joining his apprentice on the bridge, Skoll took her down to the detention cells and liberated Elsbeth, all three escaping on board their shuttle.<ref name="Part One" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|They took out an entire security detail and scuttled one of our new starships.|Hera Syndulla|<ref name="Part One" />}}
The ship was discovered by the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]], adrift in space and heavily damaged. Defense command was forced to have it scuttled. Several survivors were observed being treated in a hanger on ''Home One''.<ref name="Part One" />

After Elsbeth was freed, she went on to oversee the final stages of the ''[[Eye of Sion]]''{{'s}} preparations above [[Seatos]].<ref name="Part Two" />
