During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] fought fallen Jedi Knight [[Barriss Offee]] in a '''[[Lightsaber combat|lightsaber duel]]''' in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. The duel occurred after Skywalker learned that Offee framed his Padawan, [[Ahsoka Tano]], for the [[bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar]] that occurred several days earlier. After speaking with [[Asajj Ventress]], and learning about the ambush that occurred during the attempted capture, Skywalker felt that Offee was the only other person capable of bombing the Jedi Temple. This proved to be true and she engaged Skywalker with Ventress' [[Sith]] [[lightsaber]]s. Offee was ultimately defeated and brought to Tano's trial, where she confessed to bombing the Jedi Temple, believing that the Jedi had become an army fighting for the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] and it was only a matter of time before the Republic failed.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Funny! Those belong to Ventress. You should have gotten rid of them!''"<br />"''"I think they suit me.|Anakin Skywalker and Barriss Offee at the beginning of their duel|<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" />}}
[[File:AnakinVentress-TWJ.png|thumb|left|300px|Skywalker confronts Ventress about her part in the bombing.]]
After [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] was both framed for and charged with the [[bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar]] and the [[Death|murder]] of [[Letta Turmond]], Tano decided to hide in the [[Coruscant Underworld|underworld]] and prove her innocence on her own, but was eventually [[Capture of Ahsoka Tano|recaptured]] by [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Plo Koon]]. Tano was taken back to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]], where she was expelled by the [[Jedi High Council|Council]] and turned over to the [[Republic Military]]. During her [[Trial of Ahsoka Tano|military tribunal]] before the [[Galactic Senate]], Skywalker went into the underworld and found [[Asajj Ventress]], assuming she was the mastermind behind the recent events. Ventress pleaded innocence and revealed that someone else had attacked her and stole [[Asajj Ventress's lightsabers|her lightsabers]], after she parted ways with Tano at the [[Abandoned munitions warehouse|warehouse]] which contained the [[NM-K reconstitutor|nano-droids]] used in the bombing.

The only other person to have known that she and Tano went to the warehouse was [[Barriss Offee]], the one who sent them there. Skywalker returned to the [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple]] and found the [[Jedi]] in her quarters. When he questioned her, Offee admitted to have been in contact with Tano, but claimed that she was only trying to help her. To prove his suspicions, Skywalker attempted to strike at Offee with her own [[lightsaber]]. Offee summoned Ventress' lightsabers, which she had kept hidden in a pot instead of disposing, to defend herself, proving that she was behind the bombing and framed Tano for her crimes. Skywalker then engaged Offee in a duel.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" />

==The Duel==
{{Quote|Cease hostilities!|Jedi Temple Guards preventing Barriss from escaping|<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" />}}
[[File:TempleDuelTrainingGround-TWJ.png|thumb|right|300px|Skywalker engages Offee on the temple training grounds.]]
After engaging Offee in [[lightsaber combat]], Skywalker kicked her out of her room and Offee escaped into the Temple hallways. Skywalker quickly followed her and the [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] continued. When she broke off in an attempt to escape, four [[Jedi Temple Guard]]s arrived, boxing Offee from the two ends of the corridor. Seeking an alternative escape route, Offee broke the Temple's windows and jumped off from the Temple. After Offee knocked Skywalker off a balcony, the duel spilled onto the [[Jedi Temple training ground|training grounds]] where [[Tera Sinube]] was instructing a group of [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]]. Sinube ignited [[Tera Sinube's lightsaber|his]] [[sabercane]] to defend the [[youngling]]s and assist Skywalker. As the Temple Guards closed in, Skywalker was able to disarm Offee of one lightsaber by squeezing her right wrist. After another round of clashing blades, Skywalker used [[telekinesis]] to slam and pin her to a [[tree]]. Offee was then taken before the court of Tano's trial and was forced to confess her crimes. As she confessed, Offee denounced the Jedi, claiming they had lost their way and were the real villains of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" />

==Aftermath==
With Offee's confession, the charges against Tano were dropped and she was released from [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] custody. Plo Koon apologized on behalf of the council and asked Tano to rejoin the Order, but to everyone's shock, she refused and left the Jedi Order.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" />
