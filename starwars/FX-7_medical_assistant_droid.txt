The '''FX-7 medical assistant droid''' was a model of [[medical droid]] used in the [[galaxy]]. [[FX-7]] monitored [[Luke Skywalker]] during his [[bacta]] treatment on the ice [[planet]] [[Hoth]] shortly before the [[Battle of Hoth|battle]].<ref name="UltimateSW" /><ref name="ESB" /> An FX-7 droid was station aboard the ''[[Tribunal]]'' when [[Ahsoka Tano]] used one of the arms to scan for an [[Behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chip]] within [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]]'s [[head]] but was unable to find it.<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref>
