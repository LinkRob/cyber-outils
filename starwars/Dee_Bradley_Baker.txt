'''Dee Bradley Baker''' is an American voice actor, who worked on the ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|film]] and [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|TV series]], voicing all [[clone trooper]]s and many other characters, as well as voicing [[Ilco Munica]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="awakensvoices">{{SW|url=news/from-blast-that-x-wing-to-traitor-the-voices-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=From "Blast that X-Wing!" to "Traitor!": The voices of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''}}</ref> He reprises the roles of the clone troopers in ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (television series)|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'']], and ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', where he also voiced [[Kassius Konstantine]]. He also voices the members of [[Clone Force 99]] and various other characters in ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' and [[Nubs]] and various other characters in ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''.<ref name="Baker Nubs interview">{{SW|url=news/dee-bradley-baker-nubs-interview|text=Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures’ Dee Bradley Baker on Creating a Voice for Nubs}}</ref>
