In [[230 BBY]], during the [[Nihil conflict]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Nihil]] '''attacked''' [[Japeal]]. The location was chosen because it was far away from [[Starlight Beacon]], and the [[Jedi]]. During the attack, the Nihil attacked civilians just to cause chaos. It was one of four attacks at the same time, with simultaneous attacks being launched on [[Attack on Tais Brabbo|Tais Brabbo]], [[Attack on Aleen|Aleen]], and [[Attack on Yeksom|Yeksom]].<ref name="TFS" />
