{{Quote|You need a blaster with enough punch.|Padmé Amidala, on her ELG-3A|<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>}}
The '''ELG-3A blaster pistol''', also known as the '''Naboo royal pistol'''<ref name="USW" /> or the '''royal blaster pistol''',<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|characters|padmeamidala|Padmé Amidala}}</ref> was a model of diplomatic<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" /> [[hold-out blaster]] pistol manufactured by the [[SoroSuub Corporation]],<ref name="CGSWG" /> and also attributed to [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]]<ref name="USW" /> The [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] of [[Naboo]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], used an ELG-3A [[blaster pistol]] during the [[Battle of Naboo]], as did several of her [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|Handmaidens]]. The compact weapon was designed for easy concealment and came in both a short and extended barrel version.<ref name="CGSWG" /> Two of the blasters with the shortened [[barrel]]s were kept in a secret compartment of the [[Queen]]'s [[throne]] in the [[Theed Royal Palace]], which came in useful as last resort weapons.

==Description==
[[File:ELG-3A_blaster_pistol_-_SW_Card_Trader.png|thumb|left|250px|The extended barrel variant of ELG-3A.]]
The ELG-3A was a lightweight, elegant, and functional [[Hold-out blaster|hold-out]] [[blaster]] [[Blaster pistol|pistol]] designed for practical use and easy concealment.<ref name="USW" /> It was a small, sleek weapon with a polished chrome finish and a wooden grip,<ref name="TPM" /> and fired [[Color|green]]<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> or blue [[Laser|bolts]], as well as a [[stun]] setting.<ref name="Assassin">{{TCW|Assassin (episode)}}</ref> The slim blaster was fired using a snap trigger, which required a firm squeeze to prevent misfires.<ref name="USW" /> Despite being a small blaster, it still packed a punch,<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> and it was able to destroy an [[IG-86 sentinel droid]] with a shot to the chest<ref name="TCW Film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> or a [[BX-series droid commando]] with a headshot, despite the [[droid]]'s [[titanium]]-reinforced [[steel]] heavy [[armor]].

==History==
[[File:Resourceful-Senator.png|thumb|right|200px|Padmé Amidala utilized a ELG-3A]]
The ELG-3A became practically a standard accessory for [[diplomat]]s and [[noble]]s in need of a personal sidearm. [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and her [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens]] carried the extended-barrel version,<ref name="USW" /> and utilized the weapons during the [[Battle of Naboo]]. <ref name="TPM" /> 

As [[Senator]] of [[Naboo]], Amidala mainly carried the long-barrel version of the blaster during the period leading up to and during the [[Clone Wars]]. Amidala wielded an ELG-3A during missions to Naboo,<ref name="Blue Shadow Virus">{{TCW|Blue Shadow Virus (episode)}}</ref> the [[Coruscant Underworld]],<ref name="Senate Murders">{{TCW|Senate Murders}}</ref> and when she was under threat of [[assassin]]ation on [[Alderaan]], getting the jump on [[bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing]] and stunning her with a single shot.<ref name="Assassin" /> However, when she was attacked by the [[bounty hunter]]s [[Chata Hyoki]] and [[Robonino]], Amidala used the short-barrel model to defend herself.<ref name="Pursuit of Peace">{{TCW|Pursuit of Peace}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'' cites the weapon's manufacturer as [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]], although also credits it as a [[SoroSuub Corporation|SoroSuub]] ELG-3A. In [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] material, the blaster was a SoroSuub spin-off of the Merr-Sonn product, the [[Q2 hold-out blaster]]. ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'', released by [[Dorling Kindersley]] on [[November 19]], [[2024]] confirms SoroSuub as the manufacturer. It also confirms that the blaster comes in both a short and extended barrel variation. This rendered the short-barrel model being identified as the Q2 hold-out blaster in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'' and [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 62|''Build the Millennium Falcon'' 62]] as obsolete.
