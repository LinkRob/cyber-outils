The '''Easthills''' was an area near [[Lothal]]'s [[Capital City]]. During the time of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Academy for Young Imperials]] used it to host numerous training exercises and morning drills. [[Zare Leonis]]' older sister [[Dhara Leonis]] disappeared during a training exercise in the Easthills. Later, Zare and his fellow cadets took part in several field exercises and obstacle courses in the Easthills.<ref name="EG">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref> Later, [[BlasTech Industries]] built a research laboratory in the Easthills.<ref name="IJ">''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]''</ref>
