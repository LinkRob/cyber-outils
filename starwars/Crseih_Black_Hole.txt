The '''Crseih Black Hole''' was a [[black hole]] [[orbit]]ed by the [[space station]] [[Accretion Disco]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date note">The events of [[Bounty Hunters 27|''Bounty Hunters'' 27]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> the [[Pyke Syndicate]] [[gangster]] [[Omel Sampak]] hired out the Accretion Disco to celebrate a [[Bombing of a Nar Kanji fight school|victory]] over a rival. The [[gangster]] also hired the [[T'onga's crew|crew]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] to keep the [[Pyke]] and his entourage alive as they celebrated. As the crew neared the Accertion Disco, the [[Trandoshan]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] felt the effects of the Crseih Black Hole on his [[stomach]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" /> After [[Tasu Leech]] [[Death|killed]] Sampak, the Pykes [[Havoc at the Accretion Disco|attempted]] to kill the crew, and after realizing they were outnumbered and would need an alternative to fighting their way off the station, the [[LOM-series protocol droid]] [[4-LOM]] destroyed the console which controlled the Accretion Disco's [[Engine|thrusters]], sending the station into the event horizon of the Crseih Black Hole.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 28">[[Bounty Hunters 28|''Bounty Hunters'' 28]]</ref>

McIntyre]].<ref name="Crystal Star">''[[The Crystal Star]]''</ref>
