It was only when he resurfaced as part of a rebel cell on Sullust that it became clear he had defected right under the nose of the Empire, and rebel propaganda quickly adapted to make use of him.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
'''Edi Myrtaan''' was an individual who gained a significant fan following playing as [[Keeper (grav-ball)|keeper]] for the [[Coruscant Emeralds]] [[grav-ball]] team. He was recruited by the [[Galactic Empire]] and appeared in numerous Imperial recruitment materials before<ref name="Rise" /> realizing he was being used by the Empire.<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|232|The Force of&hellip; Sports}}</ref> Disappearing from the public eye,<ref name="Rise" /> he [[Desertion|defected]] to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Insider" /> His defection only became apparent when he surfaced as part of a [[Unidentified rebel cell (Sullust)|rebel cell]] on<ref name="Rise" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Travellers">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Sullust]], after which the Alliance quickly adapted its [[propaganda]] to make use of him. A personnel file for Myrtaan existed within the [[Rebel Alliance Archives]] and was cited by the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] when<ref name="Rise" /> he<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> mentioned Myrtaan in his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>
