{{Quote|Let me explain the Cosmatanic Steppes. It is a layer of mantle suspended above a gas giant. It's been a center of archaeological digs and scavenging for the last hundred years of the historical art rush.|Chelli Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 1" />}}
A '''gas giant''' was located<ref name="Aphra 1" /> in the [[Cosmatanic Steppes system]] within the [[Grohl sector]] of<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Aphra 1" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was situated in grid square R-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Timelines" /> The [[Super-hyperroute|hyperspace route]] known as the [[Triellus Trade Route]] connected it to the celestial bodies [[Molavar]] and [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Triellus" /> The [[gas giant]] [[planet]] lay in a [[Star system|system]] with at least three [[star]]s,<ref name="Vader21" /> and the [[Cosmatanic Steppes]], a habitable layer of mantle, was suspended above the world.<ref name="Aphra 1" />

The nature of the Cosmatanic Steppes made it the center of many [[Archaeology|archaeological]] digs as part of the [[historical art rush]], with [[archaeologist]]s attempting to determine the formation's nature and origin. While on the Cosmatanic Steppes<ref name="Aphra 1" /> in [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{Aphra2016Date|1}}</ref> at least one hundred [[Standard year|years]] into the historical art rush, the rogue archaeologist [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] explained the Steppes, its gas giant, and the archaeological effort, to the [[assassin droid]] [[0-0-0]].<ref name="Aphra 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The gas giant was first mentioned in [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 1|first issue]] of the [[2016]] [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series. The issue was written by [[Kieron Gillen]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 1" /> on [[December 7]], 2016.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/62073/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_1|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #1}}</ref>
