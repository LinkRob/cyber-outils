The '''ebon hawk''' was a type of [[Hawk (animal)|hawk]]. They were known to [[Hunting|prey]] on the [[skittermouse]],<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> a creature found on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]],<ref name="Aftermath EE">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> which the [[bird]]s would catch in their [[Claw|talons]]. During her [[Battle of Batuu|mission]] to the planet [[Batuu]]<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] compared the [[Batuuan]] residents believing that they were safe from the [[First Order]] to an unaware skittermouse that was being preyed on by an ebon hawk when speaking to the [[farmer]]s [[Grana]] and [[Dolin]], likening the presence of a First Order [[Penumbra (Star Destroyer)|Star Destroyer]] to a hawk overhead.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ebon hawk was mentioned in the [[2019]] novel ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'', written by [[Delilah S. Dawson]]. The bird shares its name with<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> the [[Starship/Legends|starship]] ''[[Ebon Hawk]]'' in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, introduced in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''.<ref name="Knights of the Old Republic">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref> Dawson stated that the ''Ebon Hawk'' contributed to her obsession with birds in ''[[Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Interview">{{SW|url=news/delilah-dawson-star-wars-galaxys-edge-black-spire-interview|text="It's All in ''Black Spire''": Delilah S. Dawson on Her New ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge Novel}}</ref>
