The '''Aldhani dam''', also known as '''Aldhani's outpost''', was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] base established on the [[planet]] [[Aldhani]] in around [[18 BBY]] that housed the [[Aldhani Garrison|planetary garrison]] as well as the vault where the entire [[Cademimu sector|sector's]] quarterly [[Galactic Credit Standard|payroll]] was stored. It was located on the valley of [[Akti Amaugh]], damming up the [[Nasma Klain]] [[Water|river]], and comprised of several facilities including the dam, a communications bunker, [[barracks]], and [[hangar]] facilities.

The dam was the site of a major [[Aldhani heist|attack]] by a [[Axis network|group]] of rebel [[saboteur]]s in [[5 BBY]]; the attackers managed to infiltrate the facility on the [[Standard day|day]] of the [[Dhani|local]] festival for the celestial event of the [[Eye of Aldhani]] and [[Thief|stole]] eighty million credits, the majority of the [[sector]]'s payroll. The severity of the attack led to the implementation of Empire-wide measures in response to the growing insurgency movement, including the civilian-focused [[Public Order Resentencing Directive]] and the military [[Imperial Emergency Act]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Garrison, observation tower, sentry defenses, all to protect this runway opening here.|Vel Sartha briefs Cassian Andor on the dam's defenses|<ref name="Aldhani" />}}
[[File:Aldhanidam-comms-center.png|thumb|left|290px|The dam's communications center was manned by Corporal Kimzi.]]
The Aldhani dam was a military facility which served as the headquarters of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|military]] presence on the [[planet]] [[Aldhani]]. The site was built on top of a hill in the valley of [[Akti Amaugh]], next to the remains of the [[Nasma Brani]] temple as a [[dam]], holding back the [[water]]s of the [[Nasma Klain]] river. Both the river and the surrounding valley had a great spiritual significance for the planet's [[Homeworld|natives]], the [[Dhani]]s, as the Brani temple was the site of the important festival of the ''Mak-ani bray Dhani'', a celebration of the celestial phenomenon known as the [[Eye of Aldhani]]. The [[Alkenzi Air Base]] was 52 [[kilometer]]s away, as was the exclusion zone of the [[Stone Valley]]; all three sites functioned as a complete system of protection, the dam functioning as a depot for supplies, [[weapon]]s and the Imperial [[Cademimu sector|sector]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|payroll]] and the air base providing air support in the event of an attack, with its [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE/ln starfighters]] being able to reach the dam in just nine [[Standard minute|minutes]].<ref name="Aldhani" />

The dam complex was comprised of an elevated three-legged command and observation tower, a [[Aldhani Garrison|garrison]] building<ref name="Aldhani" /> and [[barracks]] on the eastern shore of the river,<ref name="The Axe Forgets">{{Andor|The Axe Forgets}}</ref> the dam wall itself,<ref name="Aldhani" /> and a bunker<ref name="The Axe Forgets AD">{{Andor|The Axe Forgets}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> housing the site's independent communications facility on top of it.<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref> The dam also included a small underground [[hangar]] bay where a [[Max-7 Rono box freighter|Max-7 Rono freighter]] was stored,<ref name="Aldhani" /> mounted on an overhead rail<ref name="The Axe Forgets" /> allowing it to be launched via a tunnel leading to the base of the dam. [[Turret]] emplacements and sentry defenses were positioned next to the dam to protect the vital runway opening,<ref name="Aldhani" /> while roadblocks<ref name="The Eye" /> and armed [[pillbox]]es<ref name="The Eye AD">{{Andor|The Eye}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> guarded by [[Imperial Army trooper]]s were located along the treks leading to the complex.<ref name="The Eye" /> At least one [[rifle]] was also mounted on the dam's parapet railing near the communications bunker to defend the valley below.<ref name="The Axe Forgets AD" /> The facility was guarded by a mere forty [[trooper]]s<ref name="Aldhani" /> and a communications [[technician]].<ref name="The Axe Forgets" />

[[File:Beehaz-quarters.png|thumb|right|290px|Commandant Jayhold Beehaz lived in his personal quarters with his wife and son.]]
Apart from the military facilities, the base also housed the vault where the [[sector]]'s quarterly payroll was stored and living quarters&mdash;including a living room and personal suites&mdash;for the [[commandant]] and their family. The vault was located underground, right behind the hangar<ref name="Aldhani" /> and was protected by a [[steel]] gate,<ref name="The Axe Forgets AD" /> which could be opened with the commandant's handprint. Up to eighty million credits were stored in large decagonal cylinder crates secured to the walls with remotely controlled fasteners.<ref name="The Eye" />

==History==
{{Quote|Akti Amaugh, the Valley of Caves. The sacred valley. The Empire came in 13 years ago and liberated the Air Base Alkenzi 50 klicks west of here. They quickly discovered the unique storage possibilities of the caverns, claimed the land for the Emperor and dammed up Nasma Klain, the sacred river.|Vel Sartha narrates Akti Amaugh's history to Cassian Andor|<ref name="Aldhani" />}}
[[File:AldhaniGarrison.jpg|thumb|left|290px|The dam was located on a hill in the Akti Amaugh valley.]]
The Aldhani base was established<ref name="DB">{{Databank|aldhani|Aldhani}}</ref> sometime around [[18 BBY]]<ref name="Established" /> following the Imperial occupation of the planet. Imperial units liberating the Alkenzi airbase quickly realized the unique storage possibilities of the [[cave]]rns under Nasma Klain and dammed up the river to begin exploiting them,<ref name="Aldhani" /> forming a massive reservoir in the process.<ref name="The Axe Forgets AD" /> The local Dhanis were driven away from the sacred valley by Imperial forces and the temple located there was demolished, leaving behind only a small tri-arch structure<ref name="The Axe Forgets" /> which was littered with debris<ref name="The Axe Forgets AD" /> and used for target practice by Imperial troopers.<ref name="The Axe Forgets" />

In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|106}}</ref> a [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|team]] of [[saboteur]]s led by [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]]'s [[Mothma family|cousin]] [[Vel Sartha]], working under spymaster [[Luthen Rael]] and with assistance from disgruntled Imperial [[Lieutenant]] [[Gorn]], performed a [[Aldhani heist|heist]] on the complex in order to [[Thief|steal]] the sector quarterly payroll stored in its vault. The team, consisting of former [[stormtrooper]] [[Taramyn Barcona]] and fellow rebels [[Arvel Skeen]], [[Cinta Kaz]], and [[Karis Nemik]], assisted by inside information provided by Gorn set up a temporary [[Vel Sartha's camp|camp]] in a location with similar properties to the dam site and began training on infiltration and sabotage. Gorn also helped Nemik, who was tasked with observing the dam from a nearby hill, match his notes on the [[fortress]]' exterior with the interior floor plans and constructing a scaled model of the entire facility, which allowed the rebels to formulate their final plan. After five [[Standard month|months]] of preparations, the team was joined by [[mercenary]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] three [[Standard day|days]] before the attack, on Rael's insistence; Andor was able to solve the final problems that persisted on the infiltration plan, including how to start the getaway [[freighter]] and was ultimately accepted into the group.

[[File:The_Eye_of_Aldhani.png|thumb|right|250px|The Eye of Aldhani over the Aldhani dam]]
The team's plan kicked into motion as they successfully entered the complex's premises under the guise of an escort [[squad]] for Commandant [[Jayhold Beehaz]], who would be present in the Eye celebrations. As the commandant left, the team followed him, proceeding to capture [[Jayhold Beehaz's family|his family]] as hostages and [[Death|killing]] visiting [[engineer]] [[Colonel]] [[Soden Petigar|Petigar]] in the process. Sartha and Kaz joined the main force after jamming the dam's communications and occupied the command tower, leaving their hostages inside. The team then proceeded to the hangar and after forcing Commandant Beehaz to open the vault had the Imperial troopers stationed there load eighty million credits to the Rono. Despite being discovered by troopers led by [[Corporal]] [[Kimzi]] and an ensuing firefight which cost the lives of Barcona and Gorn, the team was eventually able to board the freighter and escape via the tunnel, leaving the vault half empty and the base in disarray.<ref name="The Eye" />

The heist's success proved to be disastrous for the Empire's public image, being the first time a military installation was directly targeted in an insurgency strike. As a direct result, all of the dam's surviving personnel were interrogated by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], while 106 Dhanis participating in the festival were detained as part of the [[Imperial Emergency Act]]. The incident was widely reported on the [[HoloNet]] around the Empire, where reactions varied from shock to enthusiasm as civilians from both sides saw it as the beginning of a more active rebellion.<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Aldhani dam was first pictured in the [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j5UX1Adanis teaser trailer] for the [[2022]] [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' [[Andor Season 1|Season One]], released on [[May 26]], 2022.<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|j5UX1Adanis|Andor &#124; Teaser Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/andor-teaser-trailer}}</ref> It was first mentioned in "[[Aldhani (episode)|Aldhani]],"<ref name="Aldhani" /> the fourth episode of series, released on [[September 28]], 2022.<ref name="Companion">{{InsiderCite|217|Star Wars: Andor Season One Companion|''Star Wars: Andor'' Season One Companion}}</ref> and first appeared in the following episode, "[[The Axe Forgets]]"<ref name="The Axe Forgets" /> released on [[October 5]] of the same year.<ref name="Companion" /> Exterior scenes for the Aldhani dam were filmed at [[Wikipedia:Scotland|Scotland's]] [[Wikipedia:Cruachan Power Station|Cruachan pumped storage hydro plant]].<ref name="Instagram-dam">{{Instagram|username=bencruachaninn|url=p/CiAmNIvK47K|name=Ben Cruachan Inn|date=2022-09-02|quote=Lots of excitement with the release of #starwars series #Andor|archivedate=20221013000727}}</ref>

[[File:AldhaniDamConceptArt-Andor.jpg|thumb|left|290px|Concept art of the Aldhani dam was created by Chester Carr.]]
Concept art of the dam was created by concept artist [[Chester Carr]] during the late production stage<ref name="Instagram">{{Instagram|username=chestbearman|url=p/CliqBBbI82v|name=[[Chester Carr]]|date=2022-11-29|quote=@andorofficial released some more concepts - here's one I worked on for the Al Dhani heist episodes.|archivedate=20230308213214}}</ref> based on the appearance of the Cruachan facility, which had been discovered earlier during the scouting phase by production designer [[Luke Hull]]. According to Hull, he was attracted to the structure due to its similarity with [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[Darth Vader's armor|mask]]. Director [[Tony Gilroy]] described the dam as "massive."<ref name="Vader Mask">{{Twitter|NatGeoTravel|status/1575274368460111874|[[Wikipedia:National Geographic Partners|Nat Geo Travel]]|quote=From post-war Scotland to 1920s Amsterdam, explore the locations that inspired the architecture of #Andor, an original series from Star Wars, now streaming only on @DisneyPlus.|archivedate=20230315113736}}</ref>
