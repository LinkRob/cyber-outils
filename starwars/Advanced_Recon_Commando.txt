'''Advanced Recon Commandos''', also known as '''ARC troopers''', were an elite variant of the [[clone trooper]]s that served the [[Galactic Republic]]. Chosen from the ranks of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], the status of ARC [[trooper]] was bestowed on [[Cloning|clones]] who served with distinction on the battlefield, such as [[Echo]] and [[Fives]]. The training of [[clone cadet]]s also served as a means for identifying particular [[cadet]]s who demonstrated the potential to become future ARC troopers.

ARC troopers such as [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Rex]] were trained to serve in positions of command in the Grand [[Army]], and could also be sent on challenging assignments less suitable for standard clones. They were given their own [[ARC trooper armor|unique armor set]], which differed greatly from the [[Phase I clone trooper armor|standard issue kit]] given to the rank-and-file clone [[soldier]]s, being equipped with more components. During the early [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Clone Wars]], ARC trooper [[armor]] served as an experimental version of the [[Phase II clone trooper armor]].

==Description==
{{Quote|ARC troopers were some of the finest fighters to ever pick up a blaster rifle. […] Being an Advanced Recon Commando had its perks, including better armor and equipment. But it was the respect that came along with the title that really made all the extra training worthwhile.|Rex|<ref name="SecretsCloneTroopers">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Clone Troopers]]''</ref>}}
Like most clone troopers,<ref name="clonetroopers">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref> ARC troopers were 1.83 [[meter]]s or 6 [[Foot|feet]] tall. They had ammunition cartridges on top of their chest plates. They were elite clone troopers who often led soldiers into battle as officers. Some clones were trained for ARC responsibilities while they were cadets on [[Kamino]], while others were chosen from the ranks of regular clone units after proving their bravery and ingenuity on the battlefield. ARC troopers were known for their skills in reconnaissance. They were also skilled as leaders, as well as armed and unarmed combat, military tactics, survival and infiltration.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Gentlemen, who wants to be an ARC trooper?''"<br />"''I do, sir!|[[Bounty hunter]] [[Bric]] and a group of clone cadets|<ref name="Clone Cadets">{{TCW|Clone Cadets}}</ref>}}
[[File:RancorBattalion-CTRaF.png|thumb|right|300px|ARC troopers served as the Galactic Republic's frontline fighting force, often complementing standard clone troopers in battle.]]
An elite variant of the standard [[clone trooper]], Advanced Recon [[Commando]]s<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>&mdash;also known as ARC [[trooper]]s&mdash;were widely regarded as the best [[soldier]]s in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="CC Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/clone-cadets-episode-gallery|text="Clone Cadets" Episode Gallery}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], the ARC troopers trained the first wave of [[Clone Commander]]s, such as [[Bly]],<ref name="Character Encyclopedia Updated">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> and [[Rex]].<ref name="HC 17">{{HelmetCollectionCite|76|Helmets}}</ref> The [[Rancor Battalion]] had several ARC troopers, including [[Clone Commander|Clone ARC Commanders]] [[Colt]], [[Havoc (ARC trooper)|Havoc]], [[Blitz]], and [[Hammer (ARC trooper)|Hammer]]. These ARC troopers were stationed at [[Tipoca City]] on [[Kamino]]. There they looked for clone cadets that could become ARC troopers, such as the members of [[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

[[File:Sideshow-Echoruns.png|thumb|left|250px|ARC trooper Echo in his Phase I ARC gear]]
ARC troopers served in the [[Battle of Muunilinst]]<ref name="Battle and War Droids Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Battle Droids and Other Droids of War}}</ref> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Build Falcon 45">{{BuildFalconCite|45|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> ARC trooper [[Muzzle]] abandoned the GAR at some point, but, as leader of the [[Aurodium Sword]] [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], often continued to support the Republic by working as security for its corporate personnel.<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> Colt and the other ARC troopers supported the [[Battle of Kamino|defense]] of Tipoca [[City]] against a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] invasion led by [[General]] [[Grievous]] and the [[Force-sensitive]] [[assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]]. However, Colt was [[Death|killed]] by Ventress while Havoc died fighting a group of [[AQ-series battle droid]]s. Blitz ordered two [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|ARF troopers]] to destroy a [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident''-class assault ship]]. In the aftermath of the battle, [[Echo]] and [[Fives]] of the [[501st Legion]] were promoted to the status of ARC trooper in recognition of their contribution to the [[Galactic Republic]]'s victory on Kamino.<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

[[File:Fives-SWCTRankFile.png|thumb|180px|ARC trooper [[Fives]] in Phase II ARC trooper armor.]]
Echo and Fives were later sent on a [[Battle of Lola Sayu|mission]] to rescue [[Jedi Master]] [[Even Piell]] from the [[Citadel]] on [[Lola Sayu]].<ref name="TC">{{TCW|The Citadel (episode)}}</ref> The two fought their way through the Citadel. However, Echo was presumed to have died in an explosion while attempting to secure a [[Sheathipede-class Type B shuttle|shuttle]].<ref name="Counterattack">{{TCW|Counterattack}}</ref> He survived the blast, but was captured and subjected to experimentation on by the Separatist-aligned [[Techno Union]],<ref name="ADE">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref> and became a former ARC trooper.<ref name="Echo-oldDB">{{Databank|echo|Echo|oldversion=20211120030642}}</ref> Fives, on the other hand, escaped with the surviving members of his team.<ref name="Citadel Rescue">{{TCW|Citadel Rescue}}</ref> The [[Ossus conspiracy theory]] concerned a supposed moment in the war where ARC troopers traveled to the planet [[Ossus]], where they recovered artifacts before ordering an airstrike. The rumor was supposedly told first by a dying [[Unidentified ARC trooper|ARC trooper]].<ref name="NoP">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>

Fives continued serving in the 501st, from [[Umbara]]<ref name="Darkness on Umbara">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> to [[Ringo Vinda]],<ref name="Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref> until his death on [[Coruscant]], where he tried to reveal [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] role in the [[Behavioral modification biochip|bio-chip]] [[Order 66|conspiracy]] to [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and Clone Captain [[Rex]].<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref> After the death of Fives, [[Jesse]]&mdash;also of the 501st&mdash;was promoted to ARC trooper.<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref> He continued serving as a [[Clone Lieutenant]]<ref name="Victory & Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> in the 501st during the [[Battle of Anaxes|Battle]] of [[Anaxes]]<ref name="The Bad Batch" /> and through the [[Siege of Mandalore|Siege]] of [[Mandalore]] as part of the [[332nd Division]].<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> Following his brief capture by [[Maul]],<ref name="Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref> Jesse took part in guarding the former [[Sith]] while en route to Coruscant. When the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious issued Order 66,<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> Jesse took command of the 332nd and other troopers from [[Commander]] Rex on the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Victory & Death" /> ''[[Tribunal]]''.<ref name="TCW Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars The Clone Wars Character Encyclopedia: Join the battle!]]''</ref> However, the Star Destroyer crashed on a [[Unidentified moon|moon]], and Jesse was killed along with the rest of the company.<ref name="Victory & Death" />

==Equipment==
[[File:JT12Jetpacks-SWL70.png|thumb|left|250px|ARC troopers made use of JT-12 jetpacks]]
ARC troopers were equipped with [[ARC trooper armor]], a variant of the [[clone trooper armor]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> ARC trooper armor included [[Phase I ARC Trooper Armor]]<ref name="Knights of Fate">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref> and [[Phase II ARC Trooper Armor]].<ref name="Collapse" /> The [[armor]] could be outfitted with a double-sided [[pauldron]],<ref name="ARC Troopers" /> a [[kama]],<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> and [[breathmask survival gear]].<ref name="Unknown" /> ARC troopers utilized a wide array of [[blaster]] weapons including dual [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|DC-17 hand blasters]],<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]s, [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s,<ref name="ARC Troopers" /> [[DC-15x sniper rifle]]s, [[ascension cable]]s,<ref name="Legion">{{Legion|set=ARC Troopers Unit Expansion}}</ref> [[reciprocating quad blaster cannon]]s,<ref name="Forged in Battle">''[[Forged in Battle]]''</ref> and [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon]]s.<ref name="FA">''[[Star Wars: Force Arena]]''</ref> They also made use of [[jetpack]]s including the [[JT-12 jetpack]].<ref name="SWL70">{{AtomicMassGames|url=character/arc-troopers|text=SWL70 ARC Troopers|int=Atomic Mass Games}}</ref>

The [[All Terrain Force Reconnaissance Walker|AT-FRC Force Reconnaissance Walker]] was created by [[Kuat Drive Yards]] towards the end of the war for ARC troopers.<ref name="LbE">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:SWFA_-_arc-trooper.png|thumb|right|250px|An ARC trooper in [[Star Wars: Force Arena]]'']]
According to [[Genndy Tartakovsky]] on the DVD commentary for [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume Two|''Star Wars: Clone Wars'' Volume Two]], the design of the ARC troopers came from [[Hasbro]].<ref name="Volume Two">[[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume Two|''Star Wars: Clone Wars'' Volume Two]]</ref> They first appeared in the Legends story [[Republic 50|''Republic'' 50]], a comic from the ''[[Star Wars: Republic]]'' series by [[Dark Horse Comics]].<ref name="R50">[[Republic 50|''Republic'' 50]]</ref> In the current canon, they appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, first seen in "[[Clone Cadets]]."<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

Two concept art pieces&mdash;each dated to [[April 22]], [[2020]]&mdash;by [[S. Carey]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' episode "[[War-Mantle]]" refer to clone commandos as a variant of ARC trooper.<ref name="Mantle Concept 1">{{SW|url=war-mantle-concept-art-gallery|text="War-Mantle" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref><ref name="Mantle Concept 2">{{SW|url=war-mantle-concept-art-gallery|text="War-Mantle" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> Furthermore, a promotional image asset uploaded to [[The Walt Disney Company]]'s official Getty Images page identified the clone commandos as "Clone ARC Commando Troopers."<ref name="Getty">{{Getty|url=thewaltdisneystudios/allaccess/#!asset/mjh5wpw9bxpvcss79s8fsnfz|text=ABB-FF-004834|date=2024-02-21}}</ref> However, according to ''[[Star Wars: Battlefront II]]'', ARC troopers and clone commandos are separate classes, as the game separates both clone types into separate Reinforcement units.<ref name="BF II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> ''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Clone Troopers]]'' later reinforced this by confirming ARC Troopers and clone commandos were separate identifications.<ref name="SecretsCloneTroopers" />
