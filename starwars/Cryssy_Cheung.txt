'''Cryssy Cheung''' ([[Wikipedia:Chinese language|Chinese]]: 張宝云)<ref name="Instagram">{{Instagram|username=cryssyface|name=Cryssy Cheung|archivedate=20191026023633}}</ref> is an illustrator who contributed to ''[[Star Wars: Women of the Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Women of the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Women of the Galaxy]]''</ref> She was also commissioned to create a ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' art piece that was displayed on [[May 4|Star Wars Day]], on [[Disney+]].<ref name="SW Day" /> She has also participated in the Star Wars Celebration art show in [[Celebration Orlando]],<ref name="Orlando">{{WebCite|url=https://jediinsider.com/8-18369|text=Star Wars Celebration 2017 Art Print: "Rogue One: Heroes" By Cryssy Cheung|date=2017-04-05|work=[https://jediinsider.com jediinsider.com]|archivedate=20200807121252}}</ref> as well as [[Celebration Chicago]]<ref name="Chicago">{{SW|url=news/official-star-wars-celebration-chicago-art-revealed|text=Official Star Wars Celebration Chicago Art Revealed}}</ref> and [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]].<ref name="Europe">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-art-show|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Art Show Revealed}}</ref>
