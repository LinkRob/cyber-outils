'''Bendu's valley''' was a valley on the planet [[Atollon]] at the center of a mesa near the [[Atollon Coral Mesa|Coral Mesa]]. The valley had a ring of nearly a dozen steps into an open flat space, and the [[krykna]] and [[dokma]] were more prevalent in the area of the valley, though they were unusually at peace. A krykna hive was located in the valley, and [[convor]]ees were common. The [[Force-sensitive]] being, [[Bendu]], dwelled in the valley.<ref name="Dawn" />
