'''Behpour''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> in the [[Naboo system]]<ref name="Fry" /> within the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim's]] [[Chommell sector]]. It possessed a relatively small population. Behpour was represented by [[Olan Carrus]] at a summit held by [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]].<ref name="Queen's Peril" />
