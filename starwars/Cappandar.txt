'''Cappandar''' was an [[alien]] who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]]. By [[3 ABY]], he was one of the longest-serving members of the [[company]]. Rebel [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] approved Cappandar for a [[Raid on an Imperial heavy freighter|mission]] to seize supplies aboard an [[Unidentified Imperial heavy freighter|Imperial heavy freighter]] in the [[Redhurne system]]. Cappandar [[Death|perished]] during the mission after being shot half a dozen times and was subsequently given a [[funeral]], with a one-line eulogy from another member of the company.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Cappandar took half a dozen shots before he hit the ground.''"<br />"''One more to drink to when we get back.|Fektrin and Hazram Namir|audio=Cappandar2.flac|<ref name="Twilight Company" />}}
Cappandar was a [[soldier]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]], better known as "Twilight Company," during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Twilight Company" /> By the [[Standard year|year]] [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> he was one of the longest-serving members of the [[company]]. As a result, [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] approved him&mdash;along with a number of others with experience in zero-[[gravity]] combat, EVA procedure, and [[Environment suit|space suit]] and [[Breath mask|oxygen mask]] handling&mdash;for a [[Raid on an Imperial heavy freighter|mission]] to board an [[Unidentified Imperial heavy freighter|Imperial heavy freighter]] in the [[Redhurne system]] to gather supplies that were needed to repair the [[hyperdrive]] in Twilight Company's [[CR90 corvette]], the ''[[Thunderstrike]]''.<ref name="Twilight Company" />

As a part of a [[Fektrin's squad|squad]] led by the [[alien]] [[Sergeant]] [[Fektrin]], Cappandar was tasked with escorting a trio of [[engineer]]s assigned to scavenge the necessary supplies while aboard the [[Imperial heavy freighter|Imperial freighter]]. Together with at least five other [[squad]]s&mdash;intended to distract the security on the [[starship]]&mdash;Cappandar's squad boarded the [[freighter]] using [[boarding pod]]s equipped with [[laserdrill]]s. While escorting the team of engineers to the freighter's command center, Cappandar was [[Death|killed]] guarding the group after being shot half a dozen times. Meeting Fektrin and the remainder of his group at the command center afterward, Namir noticed that the sergeant was missing one of his soldiers. Fektrin then described Cappandar's death, with Namir replying that Cappandar was one more to drink to after the raid.<ref name="Twilight Company" />

Although his body was not returned to the ''Thunderstrike'', Cappandar received a [[funeral]] alongside the seven others who had also died aboard the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] freighter. A [[quartermaster]] named [[Hober]] read the names of all eight deceased in front of the gathering in the ''Thunderstrike''{{'s}} vehicle bay and over the broadcast that was put out throughout the [[corvette]]. As a part of Twilight Company's tradition, Cappandar also received a one-line eulogy from another member of the unit. After the ceremony, friends of Cappandar and the other deceased gathered in the ''Thunderstrike''{{'s}} clubhouse to share stories of their fallen comrades.<ref name="Twilight Company" />

==Personality and traits==
The alien Cappandar was unable to [[Language|speak]] [[Galactic Basic Standard]] due to the way his [[lung]]s functioned.<ref name="Twilight Company" />

==Skills and abilities==
Cappandar was experienced in zero-gravity combat, EVA procedures, and the use of [[Realspace|space]] suits and [[oxygen]] masks.<ref name="Twilight Company" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cappandar was mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Twilight Company" />
