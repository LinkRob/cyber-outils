"'''Eerin Siinaa'''" was a [[Music|song]] by the [[band]] [[The Agasar]].<ref name="Survivor" /> By [[9 BBY]],<ref name="SurvivorDate">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> the ex-[[smuggler]] [[Moran (Koboh)|Moran]] had acquired a copy of the song and stored it in his room in [[Pyloon's Saloon]] on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]].

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Survivor}}
In 9 BBY,<ref name="SurvivorDate" /> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] was given permission to access Moran's room and some of his belongings, including the smuggler's copy of "Eerin Siinaa." The song was later given to music producer [[Ashe Javi]] and DJ [[droid]] [[DD-EC]], who added it to their setlist in Pyloon's Saloon.<ref name="Survivor" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
"Eerin Siinaa" was created by [[Wikipedia:Mongols|Mongolian]] band [[The Hu]] for the [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''. The song can be acquired after speaking to Moran a certain number of times in Pyloon's Saloon, at which point players will be granted access to his room in the Saloon and a chest containing the song. If players have recruited Ashe Javi and DD-EC, they can then play the song in Pyloon's Saloon.<ref name="Survivor" />
