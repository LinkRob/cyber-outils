During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization '''attacked''' [[Agricultural Hub 42]], a [[factory]] on the [[Rseik sector]] astronomical object [[Chortose]]. The marauders, whose forces were led by a [[Unidentified Nihil Storm|Storm]], wished to steal the factory's [[sprayer droid]]s for their own purposes. Two [[Chortose (species)|Chortose]] workers were present in the factory at the time; while one, [[Pango]], attempted to send a [[distress signal]] to the [[Jedi Order]], his brother, [[Gru (Chortose)|Gru]], resolved to defend the factory, believing the [[Jedi]] would not arrive. After the Nihil entered Agricultural Hub 42, the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Keeve Trennis]], having been dispatched as a result of the signal, arrived to defend the Chortose. Though she struck one of the Nihil with a deflected [[blaster]] bolt, the [[Storm (rank)|Storm]] knocked her down. At that moment, a [[Force vision]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Krall]] taking the Storm's place prevented her from fighting back; however, the Jedi [[Wayseeker]] [[Orla Jareni]], who had been led by [[the Force]] to assist Trennis, incapacitated the Storm. Jareni placed the remaining Nihil in [[Stuncuffs|binders]], intending to signal the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] to collect them.<ref name="THR 7" />
