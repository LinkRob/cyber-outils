'''Fern's home''' was the home of the [[human]] [[youngling]] [[Fern]] and her mother [[Fara]] on the [[planet]] [[At Attin]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. 

==Description==
[[File:FernsHomeMural-SC-RealAdventure.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Mural in Fern's home]]
The house was a multi&ndash;story building in a nice neighborhood on At Attin. The entry was a room with a spiral staircase which led upstairs and a large wall mural which depicted humans, [[Ithorian]]s and [[Myykian]]s.<ref name="Real Adventure" /> 

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fern's home first appeared in "[[This Could Be a Real Adventure]]"<ref name="Real Adventure" /> the first episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'', which aired on [[December 2]], [[2024]].<ref name="Early">{{SW|url=news/skeleton-crew-premiere-date|text=''Star Wars: Skeleton Crew'' Will Debut Early|date=2024-11-26}}</ref> A mural of the house seen in that episode featured artwork<ref name="Real Adventure" /> that shares similarities with [[Michael Sutfin]]'s [[:File:ThrackanSalSolo-NEGTC.png|illustration]] of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity character [[Thrackan Sal-Solo]] included in the [[2002]] reference book ''[[The New Essential Guide to Characters]]''.<ref name="The New Essential Guide to Characters">''[[The New Essential Guide to Characters]]''</ref>
