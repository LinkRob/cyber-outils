'''Francesca Longrigg''' (born [[1960s#1961|1961]]) is an [[Wikipedia:English people|English]] actor and musician<ref name="Ham & High" /> who played a Bar Patron in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref> [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] credits</ref> She is best known for her small but distinct roles in television and film, such as Mrs. Domandy, [[Wikipedia:Kenneth Branagh|Kenneth Branagh's]] nervous wife in the 2009 British comedy film [[Wikipedia:The Boat That Rocked|''The Boat That Rocked'']],<ref name="Ham & High" /> retitled ''Pirate Radio'' in North America.
