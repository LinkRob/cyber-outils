'''Chouch''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who served as a [[colonel]] within the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Following the [[Battle of Hoth]], Chouch was in command of the [[Ground Defense Command]] protecting the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] [[Eleventh Division]] that was grounded on [[Ab Dalis]]. After dispatching a message for reinforcements, a corporal informed her that [[Starlight Squadron]] had come to their rescue.<ref name="SW 15" />
