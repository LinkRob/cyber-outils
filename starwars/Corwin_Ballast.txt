'''Corwin Ballast''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Homeworld|native]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] [[Mordal]] who lived on the world with his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Lynnta]], and his [[Corwin Ballast's daughter|daughter]]. In [[4 ABY]], Ballast and his [[Ballast family|family]] became trapped in a [[Battle on Mordal|firefight]] between [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] and [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]]. He and Lynnta attempted to rescue their [[Youngling|daughter]], who had become immobilized amidst the fighting. However, by the end of the ensuing fighting, Ballast's wife and daughter had been [[Death|killed]].

Wanting to bring the memories of his family somewhere war would never reach, Ballast traveled to the planet [[Jakku]] and settled there. He became a [[bartender]] at [[Ergel's Bar]], a local establishment. In [[5 ABY]], Ballast met the travelers [[Rae Sloane]] and [[Brentin Lore Wexley|Brentin Wexley]] while working in the bar, unenthused by the former's request for information. [[Super Star Destroyer]]s arrived in Jakku's [[atmosphere]] during their meeting, deeply saddening Ballast.

==Biography==
===Grief and wandering===
{{Quote|War. I'm tired of war.|Corwin Ballast|<ref name="Aftermath" />}}
[[File:Tuanul.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Ballast traveled to Jakku's Tuanul settlement after his family's deaths.]]
The [[Gender|male]] individual Corwin Ballast lived on his [[homeworld]],<ref name="Aftermath" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Mordal system]]</ref> [[Planet|world]] [[Mordal]], during the [[Imperial Era]]. Ballast; his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Lynnta]]; and their [[Corwin Ballast's daughter|daughter]] settled in his hometown, the [[city]] [[Maborn]], during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. While in Maborn<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> he and his [[Ballast family|family]] were caught in a [[Battle on Mordal|battle]] between [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] and sheltered behind crates. Ballast attempted to assist Lynnta in rescuing their immobilized [[Youngling|daughter]] from the middle of a street, running out from behind their cover. The man saw his wife get hit with [[blaster]]fire and then was struck himself. While unconscious, he was transported to a crawler and treated with healing [[bacta]]. Several [[Standard week|weeks]] after the battle, an injured Ballast woke up in the crawler and mourned the loss of his daughter and wife after learning they had [[Death|died]] and were [[Burial|buried]]. Grieved, he kept a picture of his family in his [[boot]] to remind him of their memory.<ref name="Aftermath" />

Ballast left Mordal to travel to<ref name="Aftermath" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] planet<ref name="Jakku">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?}}</ref> [[Jakku]], a location he considered desolate enough for war never to reach. On his way to the world, Ballast acquired a [[HyCor laser repeater]] to defend himself. After arriving at the [[Tuanul]] settlement on Jakku, he bought a dilapidated [[speeder]] from a few local [[anchorite]]s, [[Currency|paying]] them more than he owed as a gesture of apathetic charity. Ballast disembarked his speeder and surveyed the terrain around him, deciding that he liked the world's bleak surroundings. A [[Unidentified rag-man|rag-man]] then attempted to intimidate the newly arrived Ballast, but the Mordal native quickly brandished his [[laser]] repeater and waved off the rag-man's threat.<ref name="Aftermath" />

===Ergel's Bar===
{{Quote|Again, I sell drinks here, so if that's not what you want&hellip;''"<br />"''This is a bar. Bars are usually excellent places to get information.''"<br />"''Oh. You want information? Here's some: The planet you are on is called Jakku. Nothing is here. Everyone on this world is a ghost. If you're here, you might be a ghost, too.|Corwin Ballast and Rae Sloane|<ref name="Life Debt" />}}
[[File:Spectral-TheGhostShip.png|thumb|right|290px|Ballast was horrified to see Imperial ships entering Jakku's atmosphere, knowing it meant war had come to the world.]]
Ballast then visited the nearby [[Cantina|drinking establishment]] [[Ergel's Bar]], greeting the [[Uthuthma]] [[Gazwin]] as he took a seat at the bar. The business's [[bartender]] [[Ergel]] served the Mordal native an [[oil]] can filled with [[Knockback Nectar]], an [[alcohol]]ic beverage he downed swiftly despite his initial wariness of the drink's murky appearance. Ergel attempted to recruit the newcomer to work as a bartender at his establishment and inquired how the man ended up on Jakku. After briefly thinking about the deaths of his family, Ballast replied that he had come to the world to escape war and to bring the memories of his loved ones somewhere peaceful. Ergel assured Ballast that military conflict would never come to Jakku, reminding him that the world was desolate.<ref name="Aftermath" /> Afterward, Ballast accepted Ergel's offer of employment at his establishment.<ref name="Life Debt" />

While working as a bartender at Ergel's Bar<ref name="Life Debt" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> Ballast was approached by the [[human]] travelers [[Rae Sloane]] and [[Brentin Lore Wexley|Brentin Wexley]], dismissing them when both established they were not at the establishment to order a drink. When Sloane repeated her inquiry for information about her former Imperial colleague [[Gallius Rax]], the bartender informed her he did not know him and that the only people present on Jakku were ghosts. Moments after, a [[fleet]] of Imperial [[Super Star Destroyer]]s arrived in Jakku's [[atmosphere]], casting large shadows over the planet. Ballast was horrified by the [[Star Destroyer|ships']] arrival, solemnly noting that war had come to the world.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I got Knockback Nectar if you want some. That'll be ten credits apiece or one quarter-portion from the Orkoon Hub.''"<br />"''I don't want a drink.''"<br />"''Then we don't have anything to talk about.|Corwin Ballast and Rae Sloane|<ref name="Life Debt" />}}
[[File:Jakku_view.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Jakku's bleak surface reminded Ballast of his outlook on life.]]
Corwin Ballast loved his family and was devastated when his wife and daughter died. In his grief, he decided to leave his homeworld and settle on a world that matched his bleak, apathetic outlook on life. After arriving at one of Jakku's barren settlements, he felt that the desolate world perfectly suited the emptiness he felt after his family's passing. Though still willing to engage in brief conversation with friendly individuals he met, Ballast adopted a more ruthless mentality toward those who threatened him and readily brandished his laser repeater at a rag-man to ward him away.<ref name="Aftermath" />

Despite his melancholy demeanor, Ballast tolerated the company of Gazwin, one of the patrons at Ergel's Bar. He grew annoyed when Sloane revealed that she had visited the bar to gather information, only interested in serving customers drinks. Ballast's desire to avoid war persisted, and he was dismayed when Super Star Destroyers arrived on Jakku, as he had hoped the world would remain uneventful. While working at the bar, Ballast maintained an unshaven appearance.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Equipment==
During his travels, the Mordal native owned a HyCor laser repeater and carried some [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] with him. While on Jakku, he bought a junker speeder and kept a photo of his family with him. Ballast also donned a [[Clothing|jacket]] and a pair of boots.<ref name="Aftermath" /> As a bartender at Ergel's establishment, he had access to [[glass]]es to fill with Knockback Nectar for patrons.<ref name="Life Debt" />
