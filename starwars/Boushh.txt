'''Boushh''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Ubese]] from [[Uba IV]] who worked as a [[bounty hunter]]. Following his little-known [[Death|demise]] at the hands of the [[Black Sun]] crime syndicate, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] impersonated him in order to infiltrate [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] [[Boba Fett's Palace|Palace]] and [[Rescue of Han Solo|free]] [[Han Solo]].

==Biography==
===Bounty hunting career===
{{Quote|Calm yourself. We're all friends here. ''{{'}}Await the dawn{{'}}."<br />"''That ''code phrase''. You're with ''Crimson Dawn'' too?''"<br />"''Nah''." [vzew!]|quoteend=0|Boushh to Crimson Dawn members before shooting them both|<ref name="Doctor Aphra 28">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 28|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 28]]</ref>}}
[[Reproduction|Born]] on the [[planet]] [[Uba IV]], Boushh left his [[homeworld]] as soon as he could.<ref name="Bust Collection 11" /> The [[Ubese]] [[Gender|male]] eventually secured a reputation for himself as a formidable and dangerous [[bounty hunter]] despite his small stature<ref name="Card Trader" /> and worked for many paymasters over his career.<ref name="ECUC" />

At some point, Boushh lost his family; he came upon a settlement on [[fire]], and despite how distraught he was, other beings asked what he had done. He was [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] from Uba IV and his public records were erased. In time, Boushh began to lead a [[Boushh's crew|crew]] of fellow exiled Ubese [[bounty]] [[hunter]]s: [[Brene]], [[Hareck]], [[Ilini]] and [[Tans]].<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" />

Between [[19 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date1">Tales from Wild Space: "The Blue Brothers" takes place during the [[Imperial Era]] but prior to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] death. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the beginning of the Imperial Era to [[19 BBY]] and Jabba's death to [[4 ABY]] meaning that Tales from Wild Space: "The Blue Brothers" takes place between these two years.</ref> Boushh assisted in the chase of [[Maximilian Rebo|Max Rebo]]'s brother [[Azool Phantelle]] by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]]. Boushh attempted to stop Phantelle by firing a [[missile launcher]] at the escaping [[Ortolan]]s but instead caused more chaos on the streets of [[Mos Eisley]].<ref name="Blue Brothers">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=13|story=Tales from Wild Space: The Blue Brothers}}</ref>

===Ord Mantell===
{{Quote|So what's the plan?''"<br />"''Patience. The solution will present itself.''"<br />[Boushh fires at the group]<br />"''We've got a problem!''"<br />"''No, that's the solution!|Leia Organa and Maz Kanata|<ref name="Bounty Hunted">{{FOD|Bounty Hunted}}</ref>}}
[[File:FoDBoushh.png|thumb|right|200px|Boushh on Ord Mantell]]
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Bounty Hunted Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that [[Maz Kanata]] helped [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] acquire the [[Ubese]] [[armor]] for her Boushh disguise on [[Ord Mantell]] thirty [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore the events on Ord Mantell must take place in [[4 ABY]].</ref> Boushh was located on [[Ord Mantell]], where he encountered [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]], and the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] members who traveled to the world in the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. The wanted fugitives sought help from [[Maz Kanata]] regarding the recently captured [[Han Solo]],<ref name="Bounty Hunted" /> held in [[Carbon-freezing|carbonite]] by Jabba the Hutt.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

Leading the rebels down an alleyway, Kanata assured an anxious Leia Organa that her solution would present itself with patience. Before Organa could respond, Boushh attacked the group, yet after a brief exchange of [[blaster]] fire and [[Melee weapon|melee combat]], Boushh was disarmed by a calm and collected Leia Organa, before being downed by R2-D2 with a nearby machinery and [[stun]]ned unconscious by Maz Kanata. Boushh's [[armor]] was then taken by Organa as a disguise, per Kanata's suggestion, as a solution to her plans to infiltrate<ref name="Bounty Hunted" /> [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] and rescue Han Solo.<ref name="ROTJ" />

===Demise and legacy===
{{Quote|I have come for the bounty on this Wookiee.|translated=1|Leia Organa, impersonating Boushh|<ref name="ROTJ" />}}
[[File:ChVI-Explosives-and-Ordnance-GaG.png|thumb|left|250px|Leia Organa, disguised as the late Boushh, before Jabba the Hutt]]
Boushh's career ended that year when he tried to blackmail the [[Black Sun]] crime syndicate,<ref name="ECUC" /> which resulted in his [[death]]. Taking advantage of the fact that Boushh's demise wasn't widely known, Leia Organa used his armor and [[helmet]] for infiltration missions.<ref name="Card Trader" /> According to rumors, it was [[Lord]] [[Gyuti]] who brought about the death of Boushh after a deal between the Black Sun lord and the bounty hunter collapsed.<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>

On one such occasion, Organa utilized the armor on [[Arkanis]] in order to rescue [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] from bounty hunters, including the infamous [[Trandoshan]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]]. Cementing Boushh's reputation with Bossk, Leia Organa's preparation for [[Rescue of Han Solo|rescuing]] Han Solo neared completion.<ref name="Age of Rebellion">[[Age of Rebellion - Princess Leia 1|''Age of Rebellion - Princess Leia'' 1]]</ref>

Once again employing her ruse at Jabba's Palace, Organa secured Boushh's stay at the [[Hutt]]'s criminal headquarters.<ref name="ROTJ" /> However, [[Boba Fett]] had informed Jabba in advance that it would not actually be Boushh under the helmet; Jabba presumed that it was a [[smuggler]] using Boushh's armor and helmet.<ref name="BtPotDS!">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> She no longer wore Boushh's attire after her identity was revealed in the midst of attempting to extract Han Solo.<ref name="ROTJ" /> In ''[[The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]'', the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] pondered that, because Boushh's identity under his armor remained a secret, the bounty hunter could secretly still be alive.<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters" />

==Equipment==
[[File:BoushhsHelmet-SW100Objects.png|thumb|right|220px|Boushh's helmet boasted many functions.]]
{{Quote|The mighty Jabba asks ''why'' he must pay 50,000.''"<br />"''Ay yoto!''"<br />"''Because he's holding a thermal detonator!|C-3PO and Leia Organa, disguised as Boushh|<ref name="ROTJ" />}}
Heavily-clad in a distinctive bounty-hunter gear, he wore an all-[[leather]] outfit, [[glove]]s, and a head-concealing [[helmet]] with a metal [[speech scrambler]] and head bracket equipped with a [[Vision-plus scanner/Canon|vision-plus scanner]] and built-in [[targeting laser]]. Boushh wielded a long [[lance]] tipped with a [[shock blade]].<ref name="RotJ novel">[[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (paperback novel)|''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'' junior novelization]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Boushh was created for the [[1983]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. In the film, Leia Organa impersonates Boushh in an attempt to negotiate with Jabba the Hutt about the price of Chewbacca. Boushh's vocal sounds while communicating with Jabba were provided by [[Pat Welsh]].<ref name="Galactic Phrase">[[Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide (real-life book)|''Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide'']]</ref> In ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]'', Boushh was voiced by [[Matt Lanter]].<ref name="Volume 3">''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny: Volume 3]]''</ref>
