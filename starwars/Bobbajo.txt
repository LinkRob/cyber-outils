'''Bobbajo''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Nu-Cosian]]<ref name="ACGS" /> with a [[Color|white]] beard who lived on [[Jakku]] approximately thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Set to Roll">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-vii-set-to-roll-cameras-may-2014|text=Star Wars: Episode VII Set to Roll Cameras May 2014}}</ref> He carried a group of animals around in cages, including [[frog]]-like [[gwerp]]s, [[chicken]]-like [[Sneep|pishnes]], a [[lonlan]], two [[zhhee]] and an ill-tempered [[worrt]] named [[J'Rrosch]].<ref name="ACGS" /><ref name="YouTube" /> His calm demeanor helped settle down the animals while he [[transport]]ed them.<ref name="VD" /> He roamed between the settlements of the [[planet]] telling stories to local villagers and entertaining children with the talents of his menagerie. He claimed his creatures were responsible for the [[Battle of Yavin|destruction]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first Death Star]], but few believe him. This earned him the names of the "'''Storyteller'''" and the "'''Crittermonger'''."<ref name="ACGS" /> Bobbajo was the only other vendor known to be allowed to sell [[Foodstuff|food]] in [[Niima Outpost]] by [[Unkar Plutt]]. His customers, however, were restricted solely to spacers.<ref name="Survival Guide">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Bobbajo was first shown in "Star Wars: Force for Change - A Message from J.J. Abrams," a video released on YouTube in [[2014]] in which [[J.J. Abrams]] introduced the charity campaign Star Wars: A Force for Change. The video was filmed on the set of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] and showed the creature walking behind Abrams while he talked, at one point drawing the director's attention. He was one of the first new characters from Episode VII to have been shown. He was portrayed by [[Aidan Cook]] in the film, and Cook was credited as "creature and droid puppeteer."<ref name="Cook">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dacardworld.com/entertainment/2016-star-wars-the-force-awakens-series-two-aidan-cook-as-bobbajo-foil-auto-18-25|text=2016 Star Wars The Force Awakens Series Two Aidan Cook as Bobbajo Foil Auto #18/25|archivedate=20200220152237}}</ref>
