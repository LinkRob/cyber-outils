'''Afolonkin''' was a [[prince]] who visited the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Ah Re Won]] to [[Hunting|hunt]] the [[Diet|carnivorous]] creatures, [[karafankata]],<ref name="DM-BW&R 4" /> between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> Prince Afolonkin was [[gender|son]] to the [[Pirate King of Sector Nine]]. The prince enjoyed revelry, including [[Alcohol|libations]], and disrespected the ruins of Ah Re Won's [[Ah Re Won Sith temple|Sith temple]], an act observed by the lurking [[Sith apprentice]] [[Darth]] [[Maul]]. When Afolonkin and [[Prince Afolonkin's party|his hunting party]] attempted to stalk their quarry, they found one fiercely protecting its young. The prince's [[blaster rifle]] refused to work, and the hunters were alerted by a [[Color|red]] [[lightsaber]] blade. The prince and his companions were [[Death|slaughtered]] by Maul and subsequently eaten by the family of karafankata.<ref name="DM-BW&R 4" />

Following his death, Afolonkin's [[parent|father]], the [[Pirate King]] of Sector Nine, came to Ah Re Won in search of their son. The man was infuriated to find out their son was allowed to be eaten and attempted to exact revenge on the townsfolk. The attack disturbed Maul's [[meditation]]s, leading the [[Sith]] to take out the [[pirate]]s as well.<ref name="DM-BW&R 4" />
