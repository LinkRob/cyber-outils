The '''clone impound vessel''',<ref name="Cut and Run AD">{{TBB|Cut and Run}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> also known as the '''tow truck''',<ref name="Cut and Run Concept">{{SW|url=cut-and-run-concept-art-gallery|text="Cut and Run" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> was a type of [[starship]] utilized by the [[clone trooper]]s of the newly-formed [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Starship|vessel]] featured two frontward-facing arms that could be used to seize starships. The clone impound vessel was utilized on the [[planet]] [[Saleucami]], where starships&mdash;including civilian vessels&mdash;were seized and taken to an [[Saleucami impound dock|impound dock]] at the [[spaceport]].<ref name="Cut and Run" />
