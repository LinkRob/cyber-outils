An '''Elder'''<ref name="PoD" /> also known as '''Path Elders''',<ref name="QfPX">''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]''</ref> was a title given to older members of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[religion|cult]] that helped lead the group in a [[Council of Elders (Path of the Open Hand)|Council of Elders]] under a [[Herald (title)|Herald]] and the [[Elecia Zeveron|Mother]].<ref name="PoD" /> Later, the Mother added a new leadership position in the form of the [[Guide of the Closed Fist|Guide of the Open Hand]], which was granted to [[Marda Ro]]. When Ro reformed the Path into the [[Path of the Closed Fist]], the Elders were among its ranks<ref name="PoV" /> until they decided to abandon Ro's Path in the name of the "Open Hand" they believed in.<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref>
