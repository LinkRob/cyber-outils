'''''Ewoks'' 15''' was an announced but later canceled issue of the [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] and [[Star Comics]] [[Star Wars: Ewoks (1985)|''Ewoks'']] comic series. Marvel solicited the comic in [[May]] [[1987]], listing a shipping date of [[May 12]]th and an on-sale date of [[June 2]]nd. It was to be written by [[David Manak]], penciled by [[Warren Kremer]] and inked by [[Jon D'Agostino]].<ref name="Marvel Age">''[[Marvel Age]]'' 53''</ref> According to a private conversation [[Joseph Bongiorno|Joe Bongiorno]] had with Manak on [[March 2]]nd, [[2021]], the writer does not recall writing the issue, so it can be considered lost even when it was certainly outlined at least. It would have contained the story "'''The Hidden City'''."

==Publisher's summary==
''Fleeing from the evil Duloks the Ewoks Wicket and Teebo discover the lost city of Endor-la—a place of peace and good will which leads them into more trouble than they imagined! "The Hidden City" is written by Dave Manak, penciled by Warren Kremer, and inked by Jon D'Agostino. A Star comic. $1.00.''
