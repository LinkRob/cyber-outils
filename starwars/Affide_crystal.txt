'''Affide crystals''' were powerful [[neurotoxin]]s which appeared to be [[crystal]]s, used by [[Spy|spies]] in the event of capture or [[torture]]. The crystal was a [[Death|suicide]] [[Suicide pill|pill]], often hidden within a false [[tooth]] in the spy's mouth. In the event of capture or torture, the spy would bite down hard on the false tooth and the affide crystals would dissolve in their mouth, ceasing respiratory functions and [[Death|killing]] the individual within [[second]]s, depending on their [[species]] and constitution.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />
