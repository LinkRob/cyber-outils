'''Barabo''' was a [[Jedi Master]] who lived during or before the [[High Republic Era]]. When [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Lys Solay]], [[Kai Brightstar]], and [[Nubs]] discovered a wall sculpture depicting the [[Jedi]] Solay noticed that they had been a [[Pooba]], just like Nubs.<ref name="Nubs Mistake" />

==Biography==
{{Quote|The Pooba's also in these pictures.|Lys Solay|<ref name="Nubs Mistake" />}}
[[File:PoobaJediWall-NubsDiscovery.png|thumb|left|300px|The Pooba's heroic act depicted on a wall]]
During or before the [[High Republic Era]], Barabo arrived in a town that had been threatened by a ferocious winged creature, a [[chirodactyl]]. The [[Jedi]] drove the beast away and saved the town. Later, a [[Unidentified wall sculpture|wall sculpture]] was built to honor the Pooba and several other Jedi. An image of Barabo holding [[Barabo's lightsaber|their lightsaber]] was carved into wall above three smaller images depicting how the Jedi had saved the town. The Jedi's [[lightsaber]] was placed in a hidden room behind the wall sculpture.<ref name="Nubs Mistake" />

In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|204A}}</ref> three [[Jedi Initiate]]s, the [[human]] [[Kai Brightstar]], the [[Pantoran]] [[Lys Solay]] and the Pooba [[Nubs]], discovered the wall sculptures. Solay noted that the Jedi had been a Pooba, like Nubs. While Solay told the story that was depicted, Nubs leaned against the wall and a hidden door opened, leading the Younglings into a small room. Inside the room was an altar displaying Barabo's lightsaber. Nubs took the lightsaber and Brightstar suggested asking [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] about it.<ref name="Nubs Mistake" />
