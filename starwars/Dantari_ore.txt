'''Dantari ore''' was a substance believed by some to only be found on the<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="BMF 37">{{BuildFalconCite|37|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> [[Dantooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the ore had been found in the [[Canyon of Akar]] on the [[jungle]] planet [[Akiva]] which was also located in the Outer Rim. According to at least one [[scout|explorer]] the ore deposit was the result of a giant [[asteroid]] made up of Dantari crashing into the planet thousands of [[Standard year|years]] prior.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Dantari ore was first mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was mentioned in an ambient conversation that players can overhear while in [[Myrra]] near [[The Alcazar]] [[cantina]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
