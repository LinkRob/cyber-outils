The '''Ankura''' were one of the two major [[Subspecies|races]] of the [[Gungan]] [[species]], denoted by their shorter bill and [[haillu]], hooded [[eye]]s, and stockier build. Despite their close cousin [[Gungan]]s, the thin, lanky [[Otolla]] more well known across the [[galaxy]], Ankurans were nonetheless an older race. The stocky [[Boss]] [[Rugor Nass]] was a typical Ankura Gungan, perhaps one of the most well known.<ref name="USW" />
