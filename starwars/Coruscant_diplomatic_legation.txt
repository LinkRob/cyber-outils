An [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] '''diplomatic legation''' for the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] was led by the [[Chassellon Stevis' mother|mother]] of the [[Apprentice Legislator]] [[Chassellon Stevis]]<ref name="LPOA" /> by [[3 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> While on a [[pathfinding]] trip to the Core Worlds planet [[Alderaan]]<ref name="LPOA" /> that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Stevis mentioned his [[Parent|mother's]] occupation to the [[Alderaanian]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], reflecting that they were both following in their parents' [[Limb|footsteps]] by heading into [[politics]].<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
