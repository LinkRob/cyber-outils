'''Calar''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Setaran]] tracker who was a member of the [[Sacred Circle]] before converting to the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[religion|cult]]<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> in [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> That year, he was assigned to join several other Path members led by [[prospector]] [[Radicaz Dobbs]] on a mission to the fabled [[Sophros|Planet X]] to retrieve eggs of the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] [[species]] and bring them back to the [[Path compound]] on the [[planet]] [[Dalna]] so that they could be used against the [[Jedi]]. Dobbs and [[Elecia Zeveron|The Mother]], the leader of the Path, hoped that Calar could use the Force to locate other eggs on the planet, using a shard of the egg that the [[Great Leveler|Leveler]], a Nameless under the Path's control, had hatched out of. The presence of a Force-sensitive on the mission disturbed [[Marda Ro]], a true believer in the Path's mission of "freeing" the Force from use.<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />

However, as he and the other Path members traveled through [[The Veil (Sophros)|The Veil]] around Planet X in Dobbs's [[starship]] the ''[[Scupper]]'', Calar began to use his telepathy to convince the others to turn around go back and that they shouldn't be there. He warned that they all would die as glob like pieces of the Veil started to attack the ship before warning that the Force will be chained, consumed, and would die and that all would be consumed. Calar began to use the Force to draw the globules to them in the hopes that it would kill them so the galaxy would live. Having a vision, Calar warned that the ''Shrii Ka Rai'', which he also called the 'Nameless Horror', would come for them all and that the [[Nihil|storm]] shall rise and [[Starlight Beacon|stars]] shall [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|fall]] because they crossed the Veil, warning them that because of them the Force would die. Dobbs warned Calar to stop what he was doing as the Veil closed in, but when he refused to, Dobbs shot him, Calar chanting 'The Force will be chained' just before he died.<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />
