'''Eldra Kaitis''', a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Rutian]] [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]], was a [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Republic Era|twilight]] [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].

==Biography==
Prior to the [[Invasion of Naboo]], she became a [[prisoner]] of the [[Xrexus Cartel]]. [[Xev Xrexus]], the [[Boss|leader]] of the Cartel, intended to [[Auction of Eldra Kaitis|sell]] the Padawan to the highest bidder. [[Maul|Darth Maul]], the [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], endeavored to find Kaitis in order to test his abilities against a [[Jedi]].<ref name="Maul 2" />

During the auction, [[Jee Kra]] placed the winning bid, but Maul and his hired [[Cad Bane's group|contingent]] of [[bounty hunter]]s ambushed them in their [[Shekelesh-class freight gunship|''Shekelesh''-class freight gunship]]. Xrexus had secretly sabotaged the [[Starship|ship]], forcing them to crash on [[moon of Drazkel]].<ref name="Maul 3">[[Darth Maul (2017) 3|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 3]]</ref>

They survived the crash, and Maul freed Kaitis to help [[Skirmish on the moon of Drazkel|fight]] the hunters who were chasing them.<ref name="Maul 4">[[Darth Maul (2017) 4|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 4]]</ref> After this was done, Maul and Kaitis [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] each other. Despite facing a Sith Lord and being only a Padawan learner, Kaitis engaged Maul without fear and was able to fight him on an even footing before trapping Maul under a pile of rocks with a [[Telekinesis|throw]] of her [[Eldra Kaitis's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. Maul survived this attack, however, and [[Death|killed]] her with a single strike of his own red blade as she prepared to finish him. After the battle her blue-bladed lightsaber broke and laid with her body.<ref name="Maul 5" />

Maul reflected that Kaitis would have made a good Sith, but killed her to sate his anger and prevent the knowledge of his betrayal of [[Darth]] Sidious from spreading.<ref name="Maul 5" /> Maul later [[Kaitis Cartel|used]] her name in the [[Coruscant Underworld]] to hire the [[Force-sensitive]] [[thief]] [[Zek Peiro]] before killing him.<ref name="Age of Republic">[[Age of Republic - Darth Maul 1|''Age of Republic - Darth Maul'' 1]]</ref>

{{Char-stub}}

==Powers and abilities==
Eldra Kaitis was trained in the ways of the Force by the Jedi Order. She was able to easily deflect blaster bolts back at her attackers and could easily kill multiple bounty hunters in combat. Maul believed that she would have become a Jedi Knight soon.<ref name="Maul 4" /> During her fight with Maul she actually managed to push the Sith Lord on the defensive and wound him. She also displayed great skill in telekinesis by hurling multiple rocks at the Sith and by [[Force push|pushing]] him away with the Force before throwing her lightsaber at the rocks over his head causing them to collapse on him.<ref name="Maul 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eldra Kaitis was created for [[Marvel Comics]]' [[Star Wars: Darth Maul (2017)|''Star Wars: Darth Maul'']] comic series.
