{{Quote|Would you like a bantha patty? They're good, and you can feed the klinnet with them.|A food peddler, to Rinetta Gan|<ref name="Lando's Luck" />}}
'''Bantha patties''' were a [[foodstuff]] made from [[bantha]]s,<ref name="Lando's Luck" /> a [[species]] of large [[Hair|furry]] [[mammal]]s.<ref name="CharDB">{{Databank|bantha|Bantha}}</ref> Found on the [[Neral's moon|moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Neral]] in the [[Corellian sector]], the patties could be fed to the [[klinnet]], a species of [[Water|aquatic]] animal that inhabited the [[moon]]'s canals.<ref name="Lando's Luck" />

In [[11 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Lando's Luck]]'' to [[11 BBY]].</ref> when [[Princess]] [[Rinetta Gan]] of the planet [[Hynestia Prime|Hynestia]] visited Neral's moon, a [[Unidentified food peddler|food peddler]] offered her a bantha patty. Although the food peddler told her they were good and could be given to the klinnet, Gan declined the offer.<ref name="Lando's Luck" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], bantha patties were mentioned in the [[2018]] junior novel ''[[Lando's Luck]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]].<ref name="Lando's Luck" /> [[Bantha patty/Legends|Bantha patties]] were introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity with an appearance in the [[2009]] novel ''[[Death Troopers]]'' by [[Joe Schreiber]].<ref name="Death Troopers">''[[Death Troopers]]''</ref>
