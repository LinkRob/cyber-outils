'''Emo Aucellis''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] individual who worked as an explorer and a historian. During the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], the entertainment park [[Aucellis Park]] was established on the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] [[Keriba VI]], which chronicled Aucellis' life and work.
