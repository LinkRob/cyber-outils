'''Bennie''' was a [[medical droid]] that worked at the [[Moonbender Colony medcenter|medcenter]] of the [[Moonbender Colony]], assisting [[Doctor]] [[Korvik]], who felt that the [[droid]] needed some reprogramming.<ref name="DM-BW&R 2" /> Between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> while the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]] [[Battle for Moonbender|attacked]] the colony to [[Death|kill]] the [[Remainders]] who ran Moonbender, Bennie assisted Korvik in tending to the wounded. After Maul defeated most of the Remainders, he entered the [[medcenter]] and fatally wounded Korvik. The doctor, however, had reprogrammed Bennie and ordered the medical droid to attack Maul, who destroyed him.<ref name="DM-BW&R 2" />
