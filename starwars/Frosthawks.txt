The '''Frosthawks''' were a rebel group active within the [[Partisans]], until the leader [[Saw Gerrera]] purged them from the Partisans sometime prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]] in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /><ref name="Date" />

They were mentioned in a report to [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|leader]] [[Mon Mothma]], later archived in ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
