A '''dead blow hammer''' was a type of [[Color|gray]] [[durasteel]] [[Hammer (tool)|hammer]] with one face larger than the other. The [[starship]] [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Viano Thag]] used a dead blow hammer<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> while working on devices just inside the [[city]] gate<ref name="LostFound">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> of [[Point Ueda]] on the [[planet]] [[Ueda]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> in [[132 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
A dead blow hammer first appeared in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="LostFound" /> the first episode of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which aired on [[Disney+]] on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> It was then identified in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
