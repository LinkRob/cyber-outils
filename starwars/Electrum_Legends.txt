'''Electrum''' was a rare metal once used in [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] construction as a sign of [[The Force/Legends|Force]] mastery. This lightsaber modification was called an '''Electrum Detail''' or '''Electrum Detailing'''.<ref name="JATM">''[[Jedi Academy Training Manual]]''</ref> A purely cosmetic accessory, this feature was only allowed to high-ranking Jedi, an honor bestowed on [[Jedi Master/Legends|Masters]] at their promotions, after having demonstrated their skill. It was a rarity among [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-users]], both [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] and [[Sith/Legends|Sith]].

During the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]], between [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Sith Empire]], electrum was used to build [[Armor/Legends|heavy armor]], including [[Mandalorian armor/Legends|Mandalorian armor]].<ref name="TOR">{{TORcite|Electrum Onslaught Armor}}</ref>

[[Mace Windu/Legends|Mace Windu]] was known to have used an Electrum hilt for his lightsaber, as was [[Palpatine/Legends|Darth Sidious]].<ref name="JATM" /> This Electrum detail gives the lightsaber hilt a distinctive gold color.<ref name="JATM" /> Electrum was also used to make jewelry. Electrum was mined on several [[Planet/Legends|planets]], including [[Abregado-rae/Legends|Abregado-rae]] and [[Duros/Legends|Duros]].
