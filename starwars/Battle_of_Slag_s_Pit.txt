The '''Battle of Slag's Pit''' took place at [[Slag's Pit]] during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="A New Dawn" /> around [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> The [[Unidentified Galactic Republic general|general]] in charge of the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Republic Military|troops]] hoped to use demolitions to bury a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] fortification. When the [[soldier]] and explosives expert [[Skelly]] pointed out that the ground was not firm enough and that the available explosives were the wrong kind, the [[general]] did not listen and ordered a charge. Skelly decided to enter the breach himself, hoping that he could save his fellow soldiers thanks to his innate talents. However, Skelly fell into a [[coma]], and all of his companions [[Death|died]] in the charge.<ref name="A New Dawn" />

Waking from his coma after the Clone Wars had [[Proclamation of the New Order|ended]], Skelly lost his right [[Limb|arm]] in the failed attack. The [[medical droid]]s had run out of [[human]]-compatible [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] parts, so Skelly received a prosthetic intended for [[Klatooinian]]s which did not work properly with his physiology. Due to the botched surgery, Skelly was stuck with the faulty prosthetic and unable to replace it.<ref name="A New Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Slag's Pit was mentioned in the [[2014]] novel [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']], written by [[John Jackson Miller]].<ref name="A New Dawn" /> Miller has stated that the Battle of Slag's Pit is the Clone Wars equivalent to the [[Wikipedia:American Civil War|American Civil War's]] [[Wikipedia:Battle of the Crater|Battle of the Crater]].<ref name="blog">{{Faraway|url=fiction/books/swanewdawn.html|text=''Star Wars: A New Dawn''}}</ref>
