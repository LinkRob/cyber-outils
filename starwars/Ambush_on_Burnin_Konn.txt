In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] was '''ambushed''' on<ref name="Aphra 6" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Art of Uprising">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> [[Burnin Konn]]. After she had been [[Ambush on Taris|ambushed]] on<ref name="Aphra 6" /> the Outer Rim<ref name="Taris">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Taris system]]</ref> world<ref name="Tynnra Pamlo">{{Databank|senator-tynnra-pamlo|Senator Tynnra Pamlo}}</ref> [[Taris]], Aphra was pursued by [[bounty hunter]]s hired by [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]]&mdash;[[Executive officer|head]] of the [[Tagge Corporation]]&mdash;since the archaeologist had blown up her [[House of Tagge|nephew]] [[Ronen Tagge]]. Aphra evaded capture on Burnin Konn and eventually made her way to [[Arveesh Station]], where she was again [[Skirmish at Arveesh Station|attacked]] by [[bounty]] hunters. The archaeologist recalled being ambushed on Burnin Konn and questioned why she was being targeted.<ref name="Aphra 6" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ambush on Burnin Konn was mentioned in flashbacks in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|sixth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 6" /> on [[November 25]], 2020. Prior to the release of the comic, the ambush was mentioned in a preview article of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[November 18]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-doctor-aphra-6-exclusive-preview|text=There's a New Bounty in Marvel's ''Doctor Aphra'' #6 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
