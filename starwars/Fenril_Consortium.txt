The '''Fenril Consortium''', also known as the '''Fenril Planets''', was a loose assembly of planets in the [[Fenril sector]], including [[Pirros]] and [[Kaltrais]]. They were founded in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Jakku]] by Pirrosi [[Prime Minister|prime minister]] [[Kezrin Almovar]] and by the [[Dark side of the Force|Dark Side]] [[Sith cultist|cultist]] [[Reyna Oskure]], operating under the alias "[[Preator (title)|Preator]] Koyon", with the objective of securing independence for Pirros and the rest of the Fenril sector from the [[New Republic]]. 

While formally led by Prime Minister Almovar, the Fenril Consortium was effectively run by Oskure as her own shadow government. 

After assembling considerable military power through the enslavement of the [[Nagai]] [[species]] and an alliance with the [[Zantarrk Gang]] [[Pirate|pirates]], Oskure prepared to move war against the New Republic, despite Almovar's opposition. After murdering Almovar and briefly usurping her power over the Fenril Planets, Oskure was defeated and taken into custody by the New Republic at the end of the [[Battle of Nagi]].

== Organization ==
The Fenril Consortium was a loose assembly of independent planets located in the [[Fenril sector]].<ref name=":0">[[Star Wars (2025) 1|''Star Wars'' (2025) 1]]</ref> Planets within the assembly maintained their own governments,<ref name=":1">[[Star Wars (2025) 4|''Star Wars'' (2025) 4]]</ref> but looked to the planet [[Pirros]] and its government for leadership.<ref name=":0" /> As such, [[Prime Minister]] [[Kezrin Almovar]] of Pirros was the head of the Fenril Consortium.<ref name=":2">[[Star Wars (2025) 5|''Star Wars'' (2025) 5]]</ref> The individual heading the Fenril Consortium bore the title of "Leader."<ref name=":1" /><ref name=":3">[[Star Wars (2025) 7|''Star Wars'' (2025) 7]]</ref>

However, the [[Anzati]] [[Acolytes of the Beyond|Acolyte of the Beyond]] [[Reyna Oskure]] was the real power behind Almovar,<ref name=":1" /> and turned the Fenril Consortium,<ref name=":4">[[Star Wars (2025) 10|''Star Wars'' (2025) 10]]</ref> which she had helped to create,<ref name=":1" />  ntoher own shadow government.<ref name=":4" /> She operated within the  government uder the alias "Koyon" and the title of [[Praetor (title)|praetor]],<ref name=":1" /> claiming to be an advisor to Almovar.<ref name=":4" /> She later murdered her and usurped her position.<ref name=":3" />

== History ==

=== Founding ===
Shortly after the [[Battle of Jakku]], Prime Minister<ref name=":5">[[Battle of Jakku — Last Stand 4|''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand'' 4]]</ref> Kezrin Almovar<ref name=":0" />  of Pirros met in a [[cantina]] located in the Fenril sector with the Acolyte of the Beyond Reyna Oskure, at that time operating under the alias of Preator Koyon. Oskure promised Almovar arms, ships, soldiers, and influence to secure Pirros's independence from the nascent New Republic.<ref name=":5" /> 

The pair started to build the Fenril Consortium, and approached [[President]] [[Tusvein Shoirin]] of [[Kaltrais]] as friends,<ref name=":1" /> with Oskure posing as Almovar's advisor.<ref name=":4" /> The [[Zantarrk Gang]] [[Pirate|pirates]], who had partnered with Oskure, forcibly removed the [[Nagai]] [[species]] from<ref name=":6">[[Star Wars (2025) 6|''Star Wars'' (2025) 6]]</ref> their homeworld [[Nagi]],<ref>[[Star Wars (2025) 2|''Star Wars'' (2025) 2]]</ref> and put them to work in labour camps in service of the Fenril Consortium. The enslaved Nagai worked to repair their ships and served in their secret armies. This actions garnered them the hostilities of the [[Nagai rebellion]], a revolutionary group that aimed to liberate the Nagai from their long line of oppressors.<ref name=":6" />

=== The Fenril Crisis ===

==== Talks with the New Republic ====
[[File:AlaytiaIntroducesKezrin-2025StarWars1.jpg|thumb|275x275px|The Pirrosi delegation to the New Republic]]
Around 6 ABY,<ref name="Date">{{StarWars2025Date|7}}</ref> a [[Pirrosi delegation]] led by Prime Minister Almovar visited the New Republic's temporary capital [[Chandrila]] to discuss the New Republic's entrities for Pirros and its neighbouring planets to join their new government. The delegation met with [[Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Senator]] [[Leia Organa]], and restated Pirros's commitment to the Fenril Consortium over the New Republic. Almovar cited the recent disruption of supply lines under New Republic protection, which had resulted in the destruction of a goodwill present by the New Republic for the Fenril sector. In reality, Oskure's forces were behind the attack.<ref name=":0" />

Additionally, Almovar later communicated through hologram her refusal to admit the New Republic at a trade summit that was to be held soon afterwards, claiming that the New Republic's presence was not needed. She announced that the Fenril Consortium would cease all commerce with the New Republic until a new treaty between the two could be discussed, and that all trespassers within Fenril territory would be dealt with and met with lethal force.<ref name=":0" />

==== War looming ====
Senator Organa later went ona diplomatic [[mission to Kaltrais]] at the request of President Shoirin to plead the New Republic's case, but she and her escort [[Kith Alaytia]] were arrested by the Zantarrk. Shoirin was also taken into custody by the pirates, and was later killed in an escape attempt, although the two New Republic envoys managed to flee. With its leader dead, Oskure announced to Organa that Kaltrais had fully aligned with the Fenril Consortium.<ref name=":1" />

Subsequently, the Fenril Consortium assembled a [[Fenril Fleet|fleet of warships]] and deployed it toward New Republic space.<ref name=":2" /> However, Prime Minister Almovar confronted Oskure on the decision on Kaltrais, fearing war with the New Republic would not bring the Fenril Consortium the power and wealth they sought. Indeed, deploying the fleet left Kaltrais open to attack, which was exploited by the Nagai rebellion who [[Insurrection on Kaltrais|attacked]] the prime minister's location.<ref name=":6" />

As battle raged on, Almovar was confronted by a New Republic delegation that included Agent [[Bastion (agent)|Bastion]], pilot [[Rynn Zenat]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]], as well as the Nagai revolutionary [[Jesrit]]. They promised the prime minister the New Republic was ready to resume peace talks if the Fenril Consortium disavowed the Zantarrk's enslavement of the Nagai and Oskure's war, which Almovar agreed to. She pulled back the Fenril Forces and confronted Oskure, formally ending the Fenril Consortium's partnership with her. In retaliation, the Anzati cultist murdered her and usurped the title of leader, claiming she had transferred control over Pirros and the Fenril Consortium's governments to her.<ref name=":3" />

Oskure then redirected the fleet toward Nagi to crush the Nagai rebellion's attempt to liberate it. Oskure set herself up in the former [[Nagai Royal Palace]] in [[Savi]], Nagi's capital, while the [[Battle of Nagi]] raged on.<ref>[[Star Wars (2025) 8|''Star Wars'' (2025) 8]]</ref> In an attempt to ensure victory for herself, Oskure detonated the Zantarrk fleet blockading Nagi, in order to bombard the surface below with flaming wrecks.<ref name="SW9">[[Star Wars (2025) 9|''Star Wars'' (2025) 9]]</ref>

Simultaneously, the Pirrosi government led by [[Consul]] [[Bardi]] requested the New Republic's help in defeating Oskure and her forces. Chancellor Mothma and Senator Organa departed for Nagi, where their declaration to help rebuild the planet as an independent state ruled by the Nagai convinced the Nagai enslaved by the Zantarrk to stand down. While Oskure tried to flee the liberated Nagi, she was successfully captured by the New Republic.<ref name="SW10">[[Star Wars (2025) 10|''Star Wars'' (2025) 10]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Fenril Consortium&mdash;albeit unidentified&mdash;debut in the [[Star Wars (2025) 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars (2025)|2025 iteration]] of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] flagship series, which was written by [[Alex Segura]], illustrated by [[Phil Noto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 7]], [[2025]].<ref name="SW1 Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/119550/star_wars_2025_1|text=Star Wars (2025) #1}}</ref> The governmental body was later identified as the Fenril Consortium in the [[Star Wars (2025) 4|fourth issue]],<ref name="SW4">[[Star Wars (2025) 4|''Star Wars'' (2025) 4]]</ref> though a proper capitalization was then given in the [[Star Wars (2025) 7|seventh issue]] of the series.<ref name=":3" />

It was first identified as the Fenril Planets in the [[Star Wars (2025) 6|sixth issue]]<ref name=":6" /> though a proper capitalization has not been provided.
