'''DV''' was a [[droid]] who served the [[Twi'lek]] scrapper [[Hudi]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. The droid was present when the [[pirate]] [[Clave Rollek]] and [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]] and [[Bren]] visited [[Hudi's Depot]] seeking to obtain a [[stabilizer coil]] for Brightstar's borrowed starship ''[[Crimson Firehawk]]''. After Rollek stole the stabilizer coil, DV accompanied Brightstar in a [[landspeeder]] to recover the coil from Rollek. In return for recovering the coil from Rollek, Hudi allowed Brightstar and Bren to keep it for their [[starship]].<ref name="A Jedi or a Pirate" />
