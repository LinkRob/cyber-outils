The '''attack on Coruscant's Verity District''' was a mission undertaken by [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. Keize had learned of an extensive [[Hall of Imperial Register|databank]] situated beneath the upper level [[Verity District]] of the [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]; the [[databank]], maintained by the [[death|late]] [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and cataloguing every criminal act and atrocity that Imperial personnel committed, had been used by the [[Sentinel (Contingency)|Messenger droids]] to provide orders for [[Operation: Cinder]] in the aftermath of the Emperor's demise at the [[Battle of Endor]]. Believing that the nascent [[New Republic]] would use the data bank's records to prosecute former Imperials in the event that it won the [[Galactic Civil War]], Keize intended to destroy it by destroying its supports and dropping it onto the district's lower levels. [[Yrica Quell]], former [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] of the 204th, deduced Keize's plan, and the New Republic dispatched her and the [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wing]] [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] [[Kairos]] to stop him.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

The attack, which occurred simultaneous to the Empire's last stand at the [[Battle of Jakku]], saw Keize abandon his unit on his mission to destroy the databank. After breaking his way through the [[Coruscant blockade|Imperial blockade]] in the [[Coruscant system]], Keize descended toward the planet, and was later followed by Quell and Kairos. Though Imperial forces attempted to defend Coruscant, they were destroyed by both Keize and Quell, who proceeded to duel one another. With [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] in pursuit, Kairos ejected from her U-wing shortly before it exploded, destroying many of her pursuers and damaging Keize's [[starfighter]]. Keize crashed, sustaining severe injuries, but intended to destroy the databank using [[proton warhead]]s. Quell landed and confronted the dying Keize, preventing him from completing his mission. At the same time, the New Republic defeated the Empire at [[Jakku]], subsequently sending [[New Republic Intelligence|Intelligence]] to study the databank.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
