'''Duke''' was a title of [[Noble|nobility]]. [[Misaani]] of the [[Anoat sector]]'s [[Noble Court]] was a duke.<ref name="Uprising" /> The [[Serennian]] noble, [[Solha]] was a duke.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" /> [[Rubix]] was a duke on the planet [[Shu-Torun]].<ref name="Darth Vader Annual 1">[[Darth Vader Annual 1|''Darth Vader Annual'' 1]]</ref> [[Blazor]] was a duke within the [[Black Sun]].<ref name="TFWS">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=9|story=Tales from Wild Space: IG-88 vs. the Gatto Gang}}</ref> [[Kayo Organa]] was a duke of [[House of Organa]] of the [[Elder Houses]].<ref name="Part I" /> The title was also used among the [[Mandalorian]]s, such as in the case of Duke [[Adonai Kryze]].<ref name="BMF32">{{BuildFalconCite|32|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>
