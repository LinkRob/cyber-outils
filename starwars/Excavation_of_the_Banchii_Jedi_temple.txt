Between [[230 BBY]] and [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> members of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s led by [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] worked with the [[Harch]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Vol Garat]] to '''excavate''' the ruins of the [[Jedi temple (Banchii)|Jedi temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Banchii]]. Ro hoped to find the [[Azlin's journal|journal]] of former [[Jedi]] [[Azlin Rell]], which had been buried within the temple. After an argument with Garat, Ro allowed him to take charge of the excavation and departed. After, as the excavation continued, they found the modified [[ZZ-model droid]] [[ZZ-10]], who had been buried with the journal to protect it. ZZ-10 fought back against the Nihil, and was [[Skirmish on Banchii|rescued]] by [[Jedi Master]] [[Arkoff]], who had received a communication from the droid.<ref name="Precedent" />
