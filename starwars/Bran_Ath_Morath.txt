'''Bran Ath'Morath''' was a legendary [[Jedi Knight]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. At some point between around [[5000 BBY]] and [[Standard year|years]] before [[1032 BBY]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Caldoth]] [[Attack on Bathoris|attacked]] the [[Jedi temple (Bathoris)|Jedi temple]] on the astronomical object [[Bathoris]], [[Death|killing]] many [[Jedi]]. Ath'Morath [[Lightsaber combat|engaged]] Caldoth in a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]], allowing other Jedi to rally and force Caldoth to retreat. Years later, under the influence of the [[Nightsisters]] of the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]], Caldoth attacked the [[city]] [[Pundith]] on the world [[Kizan]] to lure Ath'Morath away from Bathoris and defeat him. After a long [[Battle on Kizan|duel]] on Kizan, Caldoth deliberately impaled himself on Ath'Morath's [[lightsaber]] and took advantage of his shocked reaction to behead him. 

==Biography==
===Duels with Darth Caldoth===
According to legend, Bran Ath'Morath was a [[Jedi Knight]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="The Gilded Cage" /> At some point, Ath'Morath was the owner of an ancient [[Silandra Sho's shield|plasma shield]] that had been passed between [[Jedi]] for [[Standard century|centuries]].<ref name="Shield">{{StoryCite|story=Shield of the Jedi|book=The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref> At some point between around [[5000 BBY]] and [[Standard year|years]] before [[1032 BBY]],<ref name="Cage Date" /> the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Caldoth]] became aware of a rare fragment of a fresco that related to the ancients and their [[Force power|use]] of [[the Force]] that had been uncovered and transferred to the [[Jedi temple (Bathoris)|Jedi temple]] on the astronomical object [[Bathoris]], where Ath'Morath was stationed. In order to acquire the artifact, Caldoth launched an [[Attack on Bathoris|attack]] on the [[Jedi temple|temple]]. Caldoth [[Death|killed]] many [[Jedi Temple Guard]]s and several Jedi Knights, but Ath'Morath was able to hold his ground and repel Caldoth from the temple's inner sanctum. He then kept the [[Sith]] locked in a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] until more Jedi were able to rally and force him to retreat.<ref name="The Gilded Cage" />

[[File:Caldoth.png|thumb|right|230px|Ath'Morath dueled the Sith Lord Darth Caldoth (pictured).]]
Following the battle, the [[Nightsisters]], [[witch]]es from<ref name="The Gilded Cage" /> the planet<ref name="Dathomir">{{Databank|dathomir|Dathomir}}</ref> [[Dathomir]] who were enemies of Caldoth, enacted a ritual to allow the Nightsister [[Zeldin]] to influence the thoughts of Caldoth.<ref name="The Gilded Cage" /> At some point by 1032 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Cage Date" /> after multiple failed attempts to cause Caldoth's death through manipulating his thoughts, the Nightsisters performed another ritual that granted them a [[Force vision|vision]] of Ath'Morath's [[lightsaber]] being run through Caldoth, and so Zeldin inspired in him a desire to destroy Ath'Morath. Caldoth soon created a plan to attack the world [[Kizan]] to lure Ath'Morath away from Bathoris.<ref name="The Gilded Cage" />

After Ath'Morath received word that Caldoth had destroyed the [[city]] [[Pundith]] on Kizan, he and three other Jedi traveled there to [[Battle on Kizan|face]] him. Caldoth quickly dealt with the other three Jedi, but he was unable to defeat Ath'Morath. They [[Lightsaber combat|fought]] throughout the city, dueling on rooftops, in alleys, and in the ruins of buildings. By [[Local day|dusk]], both Ath'Morath and Caldoth had begun to tire. Realizing that the only way he could defeat Ath'Morath was to surprise him, Caldoth lowered his [[Darth Caldoth's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and thrust himself forward, deliberately piercing himself through the shoulder with Ath'Morath's lightsaber. Ath'Morath was shocked by what Caldoth had done, which allowed the Sith, who had anticipated Ath'Morath's reaction, to behead him. Zeldin, who was watching the duel from Dathomir, screamed in frustration upon seeing Ath'Morath's death and then attempted to fully [[Possession|seize control]] of Caldoth's mind, but Caldoth, who had been aware of her influence, instead trapped her inside his mind.<ref name="The Gilded Cage" />

===Legacy===
By around [[379 BBY]],<ref name="Shield Date">"[[Shield of the Jedi]]" establishes that [[Rooper Nitani]] is around eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old during its events. During the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]], Nitani is fifteen years old. Therefore, "Shield of the Jedi" must take place around [[379 BBY]].</ref> Jedi Master [[Silandra Sho]], who had become the owner of the [[plasma shield]] once owned by Ath'Morath, told her [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] stories of legendary Jedi who had previously possessed the [[Shield (physical armor)|shield]], including Ath'Morath.<ref name="Shield" /> Around that year,<ref name="Shield Date" /> Nitani recalled the story she had heard of Ath'Morath fighting Caldoth while she was searching for the shield as part of her [[Rooper Nitani's Jedi Trials|Jedi Trials]].<ref name="Shield" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a Jedi, Bran Ath'Morath was [[Force-sensitive]]. He was skilled in combat and was more powerful than [[Darth]] Caldoth, who was only able to defeat Ath'Morath by surprising him.<ref name="The Gilded Cage" />

==Equipment==
Ath'Morath wielded a [[Color|yellow]]-bladed lightsaber<ref name="The Gilded Cage" /> and at some point owned an ancient [[plasma]] shield.<ref name="Shield" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bran Ath'Morath first appeared in flashbacks in the short story "[[The Gilded Cage]]," which was published in the [[2020]] short story collection ''[[Dark Legends]]'', written by [[George Mann]].<ref name="The Gilded Cage" />
