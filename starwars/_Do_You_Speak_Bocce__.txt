"'''Do You Speak Bocce?'''" is an article about languages in ''Star Wars'', written by [[Melissa T. Miller]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 206|''Star Wars Insider'' 206]]<ref name="Article" /> on [[November 2]], [[2021]].<ref name="Titan Comics" />

In addition to talking to expert linguists, Miller speaks to [[Brian Herring]] about [[BB-8]]'s beeps.<ref name="Article" />
