The [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[212th Attack Battalion]] carried out the '''attack on Nabat''' during the [[Battle of Ryloth]] in [[21 BBY]]. The skirmish saw the [[city]] of [[Nabat]] liberated from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].

==The liberation==
After [[Skywalker's fleet]] [[Fall of the Ryloth blockade|broke through]] the [[Ryloth blockade]]<ref name="Storm">{{TCW|Storm Over Ryloth}}</ref> and continued to engage its remaining [[starship]]s, the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Ryloth invasion fleet|invasion fleet]] made its way into the atmosphere of the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]], which [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]] under [[Emir of Ryloth|Emir]] [[Wat Tambor]] had [[Battle of Ryloth|occupied]]. In need of a landing zone, [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and members of his [[212th Attack Battalion]] made their way to the occupied [[city]] of [[Nabat]] aboard [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]]. Newly created [[J-1 proton cannon]]s, however, were stationed in the city and offered heavy surface fire that took out most of the LAAT/is and one of the invasion fleet's [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class transgalactic military assault ships]].<ref name="Innocents" />

Kenobi and his remaining forces, among their number the [[clone trooper]]s of [[Ghost Company]], stormed Nabat's outer defenses and infiltrated the deserted city, the inhabitants of which had been taken as living shields by the occupying [[battle droid]]s. In the city, two of Kenobi's [[troops]], [[Waxer]] and [[Boil]], discovered [[Numa]], a young [[Twi'lek]] who had escaped capture. After fighting off a swarm of [[gutkurr]]s, Kenobi and his troops attacked the Separatist command center, drawing most of the droids away to free the [[prisoner]]s and take out the [[cannon]]s. When droid [[commander]] [[TX-20]] cornered Kenobi and Numa aboard an [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I]], the freed Twi'leks attacked the [[tank]] and climbed up to the confused droid, who had failed to calculate such a revolt and was torn apart by the people.<ref name="Innocents" />

==Aftermath==
The invasion fleet was able to set down outside of Nabat after the city's liberation and land the greater GAR forces<ref name="Innocents" /> to be used in the rest of the Ryloth campaign.<ref name="Liberty" />
