The '''Final Protector''',<ref name="The High Republic 5" /> also known simply as the '''Protector''',<ref name="Battle of Jedha" /> or as the '''Sentinel''',<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> was a statue made of [[sandstone]]<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi" /> that stood in the [[Dunes of Contemplation]]<ref name="The High Republic 5" /> on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] outside the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] which it stood in watch over. It was a preferred meditation spot of [[Jedi]] [[Oliviah Zeveron]] who escaped there when she was tired of the [[Convocation of the Force|Convocation]].<ref name="Peace and Unity">{{StoryCite|story=Peace and Unity|book=The High Republic (2022) 1}}</ref> During the [[Battle of Jedha]], a crowd of protestors congregated to tear the statue down, despite the arrival of Zeveron and Master [[Leebon]].<ref name="The High Republic 5" />
