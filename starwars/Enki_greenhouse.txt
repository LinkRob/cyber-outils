'''Enki greenhouse''' was a [[garden]] facility located in a former factory district on the [[planet]] [[Devalok]]. The [[Zohra's mother|mother]] of [[Zohra]] and [[Dumuz]] worked as the [[head gardener]] at Enki prior to the greenhouse's destruction during the [[Siege of Devalok|siege]] at Devalok during the [[Clone Wars]]. During the fighting, Zohra's [[Parent|mother]] and [[Zohra's father|father]] were trapped in the ruins of the greenhouse for five [[Standard day|days]] before [[Jedi General]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] found and freed them after being led there by their [[Youngling|children]].<ref name="Eye of the Beholder" />
