'''C1-D1''',<ref name="Part Two" /> or '''See-One''' for short,<ref name="Ahsoka 2">[[Ahsoka 2|''Ahsoka'' 2]]</ref> was a [[protocol droid]] working at the control centre for the [[Santhe Shipyards]] in [[Coronet City]], on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] during the dismantling of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Navy|fleet]] for the [[New Republic]]. Her function was catalogue inventory on arriving transports. <ref name="Part Two" />

See-One encountered an [[HK-87 assassin droid|HK-class]] [[assassin droid]] that prevented her from completing her task, but was prevented from filing a report as the assassin droid carried a high-level security clearance. It was not until five rotations later that she was able to report this encounter, when [[Ahsoka Tano]] inquired about the use of HKs at the facility, exposing the illegal operation by [[Morgan Elsbeth]] to appropriate reconditioned [[Super Star Destroyer|SSD]] [[hyperdrive]] cores for the [[Eye of Sion]].<ref name="Part Two" /><ref name="Part Three">{{Ahsoka|Part Three: Time to Fly}}</ref>
