{{Quote|I fully believe that this attack is the last demonstration we'll need to launch against the Paataatus. Their diplomats seem fully committed to withdrawing to their borders and leaving the Ascendancy strictly alone.|Admiral Ar'alani, to a tribunal of Syndics|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
The '''attack on the Paataatus''' was a retaliatory strike conducted by the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] on [[Nettehi]], the [[homeworld]] of the [[Paataatus]] [[species]], shortly after an [[Attack on Csilla|attack]] on the [[Chiss]] homeworld of [[Csilla]] around [[19 BBY]]. At the [[time]], the [[Chiss Syndicure|Syndicure]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] did not have definitive proof that the [[Paataatus Hiveborn|Paataatus government]] was behind the attack on Csilla, but justified the attack as a means to punish the Paataatus for proven encroachments into Ascendancy territory.

The [[Paataatus military]] was far more prepared to defend their homeworld than most [[Chiss officer|officers]] of the Chiss [[Picket Force Six]] anticipated. Only [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn" expected their level of readiness. Thrawn disobeyed the orders of [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] to break off, feign weakness, and draw most Paataatus forces away from the bulk of the Chiss force. In doing so, Thrawn bought the other Chiss [[warship]]s time to regroup and eliminate most of the [[Paataatus Hiveborn|Paataatus fighters]], securing a Chiss victory.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|No one sends warships against the Ascendancy on a lark and then goes home. Someone out there is plotting against us. That someone needs to be found and taught a serious lesson.|A [[Unidentified Obbic speaker|Speaker]] for the [[Obbic family]], after Csilla was attacked|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
The [[Paataatus]] were an [[Unknown Regions]] [[species]] whose territory was near the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], against whom they sometimes clashed.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> The [[Grysk Hegemony]], an [[Grysk conquests|expansionist]] empire, directed their client empire the [[Nikardun Destiny]] to [[Jixtus's plan|encircle]] the Ascendancy. At the [[Grysk]]s' behest, the Destiny, led by [[General]] [[Yiv]], [[Nikardun conquests|conquered]] the [[Paataatus Hiveborn]], an act the Paataatus resented.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date note" /> the [[Nikardun]] [[Attack on the edge of the Dioya system|massacred]] a group of [[Paccosh]] [[refugee]]s in the Ascendancy's peripheral [[Dioya system]], and ordered the Paataatus to [[Attack on Csilla|attack]] the Ascendancy's [[capital]] [[planet]] of [[Csilla]] to distract the [[Chiss]] from the massacre. The attack infuriated the Ascendancy's ruling [[Chiss Syndicure|Syndicure]], as Csilla had not been attacked for [[Standard decade|decades]]. The ruling [[Syndic]]s demanded the [[Chiss Defense Force]] promptly ascertain their attackers' identities and retaliate. One Syndic suggested the Paataatus were the attackers, and as such the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet|Expansionary Defense Fleet]] should strike back.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

[[Supreme General]] [[Ba'kif]] considered the question of why Csilla had been attacked more interesting than who had attacked it, but kept such thoughts to [[Gender|himself]] and [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[Core name|known as]] "Thrawn." Neither Ba'kif nor Thrawn were convinced of the Paataatus' responsibility for the attack on Csilla. Nonetheless, the Paataatus had recently conducted raids in Chiss territory. Ba'kif agreed the Paataatus must be punished lest other factions come to believe they could attack the Ascendancy with impunity.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

Thrawn had once commanded the [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'', but the [[Defense Hierarchy Council]] revoked his command in the aftermath of Thrawn's unauthorized combat in the [[Vagaari pirate operations]]. Command had been given to [[Mid Captain]] [[Ufsa'mak'ro]], but Ba'kif restored Thrawn's command, making Samakro Thrawn's [[Executive officer|first officer]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==The attack==
{{Quote|So we play the wounded animal and draw the enemy to us, giving the rest of the force time to regroup and counterattack.''"<br />"''Yes. The Paataatus have a swarm mentality. That thought pattern predisposes them to concentrate their attention on wounded opponents.|Mid Captain Samakro and Senior Captain Thrawn|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
[[File:Steadfast-Thrawn6.png|thumb|left|250px|Six Chiss heavy cruisers (pictured) participated in the attack on the Paataatus.]]
[[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] led an attack on the Paataatus' [[homeworld]],<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> [[Nettehi]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> Ar'alani's [[Picket Force Six]] performed the attack, [[Flagship|led]] by her [[Nightdragon man-of-war|''Nightdragon'' man-of-war]], [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']], and joined Thrawn's ''[[Springhawk]]'' and five other Chiss heavy [[cruiser]]s.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

To the confusion of the Picket Force's [[Chiss officer|officers]], the [[Paataatus military]] was highly prepared for a Chiss attack, mounting a strong defense that included at least fifty [[Paataatus Hiveborn|Paataatus fighters]]. The Paataatus had a swarm mentality, and their [[starfighter]] tactics involved spreading over a wide area and picking off weakest enemies first. Senior Captain Thrawn understood Paataatus tactics and knew the Chiss were less effective at targeting spread-out enemies than tightly-clustered foes. Admiral Ar'alani ordered the Picket Force to remain in formation, but Thrawn disobeyed her order; breaking off, feigning severe damage to the ''Springhawk'', and drawing the Paataatus fighters into a tight cluster targeting his ship. Thrawn's feint gave the rest of the Picket Force [[time]] to regroup and counterattack, tipping the battle's scales and leading to a Chiss victory. Picket Force Six destroyed most defending Paataatus fighters.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

The Chiss later discovered that the Paataatus had known the Ascendancy's optimal attack vector. After a prior conflict, a Chiss [[Unidentified Chiss emissary|emissary]] had pointed towards Chiss [[Realspace|space]] and warned Paataatus officials about the next time they see Chiss forces coming "from those [[star]]s." Among all the [[commanding officer]]s of the Chiss [[warship]]s, only Thrawn was aware of that critical information.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Aftermath==
===Tribunal on Csilla===
{{Quote|We blasted the Paataatus into small bits of metal and got as groveling a peace settlement from them as I've ever seen. And the Aristocra still aren't happy?''"<br />"''No. But we're not the ones they're unhappy at. We just happen to be the most convenient targets right now for their annoyance.|Senior Captain Wutroow and Admiral Ar'alani, as they approach an Aristocra tribunal following the attack|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
[[File:Aralani-full.png|thumb|right|200px|Admiral Ar'alani (pictured) was called to testify at a tribunal on Csilla following Thrawn's disobedience of an order during the attack.]]
Six [[Standard day|days]] after the attack, the [[Aristocra (organization)|Aristocra]] called a tribunal over Thrawn's disobedience of Admiral Ar'alani's order. Ar'alani believed the Aristocra would have pressed charges against Thrawn for insubordination if his actions had not led to victory. Even so, and despite the [[Defense Hierarchy Council]] vindicating him, Thrawn's [[Politics|political]] enemies among the Aristocra saw the incident as a chance to attack him.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

Thrawn was absent from the tribunal as Ba'kif sent him on a [[Mission to the Rapacc system|mission]]. Ar'alani and her second-in-command, Senior Captain [[Kiwu'tro'owmis]] "Wutroow," were questioned by Syndics on Csilla regarding Thrawn's disobedience. Wutroow testified that Thrawn's actions were necessary and proper. She recounted the [[diplomat]] who had warned the Paataatus of the Chiss' optimal angle-of-attack and testified that only Thrawn had realized and saved the day by adapting.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

===Thrawn versus the Nikardun===
{{Quote|The Paataatus were quite unhappy at ''[Yiv's]'' attempt to conquer them. That anger was matched with an equal degree of gratitude for my assistance in breaking the last Nikardun stranglehold on them.|translated=1|Senior Captain Thrawn describes the Paataatus' gratitude for his assistance liberating Nettehi to [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]] of the [[Kilji Illumine]] and "[[Jixtus]]" of the Grysk Hegemony|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
Between the [[Urch incident]] and the [[Primea diplomatic reception]], Thrawn showed [[Caregiver]] [[Mitth'ali'astov|Thalias]] a map of the Unknown Regions. Thrawn highlighted nations on the Ascendancy's borders whose behavior toward the Chiss had markedly changed recently. Thrawn surmised that the Nikardun Destiny sought to encircle the Ascendancy, conquering neighboring civilizations so that the Chiss [[Non-intervention policy|could not engage]] in hostilities.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Indeed, the Grysks had directed Yiv to encircle the Ascendancy to facilitate an invasion.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Thalias suggested the Defense Force could deal with Yiv as they had the Paataatus. Thrawn replied that Paataatus culture indicated the Paataatus would not attack any Chiss for a generation. However, reports from [[Naporar]]&mdash;the planet where most Expansionary Defense Fleet operations were based&mdash;indicated the Paataatus were already rearming for more attacks. Consequently, Thrawn concluded that Yiv had conquered the Paataatus.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

The next year,<ref name="Greater Good year">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' establishes that the attack on the [[Paataatus]], depicted in ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', took place "last [[Standard year|year]]," thus placing the events of ''Greater Good'' the year after ''Chaos Rising''.</ref> Thrawn's forces returned to the space above Nettehi searching for [[pirate]]s and [[Skirmish above Nettehi|liberated]] the planet from Nikardun remnants. The Chiss and Paataatus were thus no longer adversaries, but allies. According to [[Syndic Prime]] [[Mitth'urf'ianico]], the Ascendancy's [[Diplomatic corps (Chiss Ascendancy)|diplomatic corps]] did not know whether to be furious or "salivate" over the breakthrough in Chiss-Paataatus relations.<ref name="Greater Good" /> Grateful for Chiss assistance liberating Nettehi, and the [[Kilji data rectangle|data]] on the [[Kilji]] battle fleet, the [[Illumine Kilhorde]], the Hiveborn's military [[Attack on the Illumine Kilhorde|eliminated]] the Kilhorde after it moved across the space between Ascendancy and Paataatus territory.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on the Paataatus first appeared in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''. The attack is misspelled as "attack on the Paataasus" in the novel.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
