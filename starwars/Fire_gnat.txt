{{Quote|You're in no shape to swat a fire gnat.|Zilastra, to Depa Billaba|<ref name="Force" />}}
A '''fire gnat''' was a type of [[gnat]].<ref name="Force" /> In [[33 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{LivingForceDate}}</ref> when [[Jedi Master]] [[Depa Billaba]] threatened the [[pirate]] [[Zilastra]] after having been [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] by her in brutal conditions, Zilastra was unconvinced, assessing that Billaba could not even swat a fire gnat in her state.<ref name="Force" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], fire gnats were mentioned in ''[[The Living Force]]'', a [[2024]] novel by [[John Jackson Miller]].<ref name="Force" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Fire gnat/Legends|fire gnats]] debuted in the [[1998]] novel [[Young Jedi Knights: Trouble on Cloud City|''Trouble on Cloud City'']], written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]] and [[Rebecca Moesta]] as the thirteenth installment in the ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Knights]]'' series.<ref name="Trouble">''[[Young Jedi Knights: Trouble on Cloud City]]''</ref>
