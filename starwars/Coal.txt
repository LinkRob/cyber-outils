'''Coal''' was a type of [[Color|black]] object that was used in braziers. The phrase "not the warmest coal in the brazier" described someone who is cold and distant.<ref name="PoV">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[thief]] [[Tey Sirrek]] used this phrase to describe the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Oliviah Zeveron]].<ref name="PoV" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Coal-LEGO.jpg|thumb|right|200px|[[Fire|Burning]] pieces of coal in ''LEGO Star Wars: Build Your Own Adventure'']]
Coal was first mentioned in the non-[[canon]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] activity book ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Build Your Own Adventure]]'', released in [[2016]].<ref name="BYOA">''[[LEGO Star Wars: Build Your Own Adventure]]''</ref> It was later introduced into the [[Star Wars]] canon by the novel [[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance|''Path of Vengeance'']], written by [[Cavan Scott]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia series in [[2023]].<ref name="PoV" /> In ''LEGO Star Wars: Build Your Own Adventure'', the [[Ewok]]s used coals to make campfires on their [[homeworld]] [[Endor]]. While visiting Endor, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] [[Zin Evalon]] came across an Ewok who was roasting [[Marshmallow]]s over burning coals. Evalon joined the Ewok and they roasted and [[Foodstuff|ate]] Marshmallows together.<ref name="BYOA">''[[LEGO Star Wars: Build Your Own Adventure]]''</ref>
