'''Caamas''' was an astronomical body located in the [[Cirius system]]<ref name="Adventures 12" /> within [[the galaxy]]'s [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="TFABG" /> It shared its name with [[Caamasi light sculpture]],<ref name="FACPOV ESB">{{StoryCite|story=Fake It Till You Make It|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> and [[Caamasi cheese pie]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 22">[[Bounty Hunters 22|''Bounty Hunters'' 22]]</ref> During the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]], Caamas [[Caamas conflict|suffered]] from horrors inflicted upon it that were comparable to the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]].<ref name="FiB">''[[Forged in Battle]]''</ref>
