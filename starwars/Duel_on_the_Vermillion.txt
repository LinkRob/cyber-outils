{{Quote|A foolish woman. She challenged me to a duel&mdash;to prevent me from taking Solo. We fought. Her skill was&mdash;while not a true threat&mdash;''notable''.|Darth Vader, on Qi'ra|<ref name="Crimson Reign 5">[[Crimson Reign 5|''Crimson Reign'' 5]]</ref>}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> a '''duel''' was fought on the [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']], the [[Fortress]]-[[Flagship]] of the criminal syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]], on the [[planet]] [[Jekara]] between Crimson Dawn [[Crime lord|leader]] [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]] and the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] for possession of the [[carbonite]]-frozen body of the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]].<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 3" />

The [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] began after an [[Auction for Han Solo|auction]], which was won by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] and the [[Hutt Clan]], was held by Crimson Dawn for Solo aboard the ''Vermillion''. Vader arrived after the end of the bidding and laid claim to Solo for [[Gender|himself]], and Jabba ultimately surrendered his possession of Solo to the [[Sith Lord]]. Qi'ra then insisted that whilst Vader and the [[Galactic Empire]] were free to take Solo, they would have to pay the one million [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] that Jabba had bid for Solo during the auction, which Vader refused. Qi'ra then questioned Vader if the Empire was too poor or too cheap to pay the credits, which caused Vader to draw his [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. Qi'ra drew two [[Qi'ra's electro swords|swords]] and the two engaged in a duel.<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 3" />

While not a real threat to the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], Qi'ra's skills and the fact that she knew [[Teräs Käsi]] both were interesting to Vader.<ref name="Crimson Reign 5" /> Vader was able to overpower Qi'ra. As he prepared to deliver a [[Death|killing]] blow to her, however, he sensed the arrival of his son, [[Luke Skywalker]], to Jekara, which allowed Qi'ra to make her escape.<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 3" /> Vader then had the carbonite containing Solo taken to his flagship, the ''[[Executor]]'' whilst he attempted to kill Skywalker, though he failed and Hutt forces led by [[Bokku]], who wanted to reclaim what he felt was the property of the Hutt Clan, launched an [[Attack on the Executor|Attack on the ''Executor'']]. The attack resulted in the destruction of the [[Grand Hutt Council]], and the re-capture of Solo by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]], who delivered Solo to Jabba on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 5">[[War of the Bounty Hunters 5|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 5]]</ref>
