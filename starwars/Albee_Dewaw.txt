'''Albee Dewaw''' was the persona of an enormous [[hologram]] created to rule over the [[Balnab (group)|civilization]] on the [[planet]] [[Balnab]]. However, the figure was in fact being controlled by a group of [[DUM-series pit droid]]s working from the wreckage of a crashed [[starship]]. Dewaw was capable of throwing lightning, which intimidated the members of the civilization.<ref name="ND" />

The holographic Dewaw was exposed when members of the group found the stranded [[droid]]s [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]] and brought them back to the settlement. The two droids easily recognized Dewaw as a hologram, and R2-D2 found and entered the hidden room from which the pit droids controlled Dewaw, revealing the truth to the people the droids had controlled. Subsequently, when the residents rebelled against the pit droids, the settlement was destroyed in an explosion, although R2-D2 and C-3PO escaped.<ref name="ND" />

==Behind the scenes==
Albee Dewaw first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|season four]] episode "[[Nomad Droids]]."<ref name="ND" />
