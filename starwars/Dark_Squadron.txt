'''Dark Squadron''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] of [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]]s that was active during the [[Imperial Era]] and reported to [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]]. After capturing the [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] rebuilt the [[cyborg]] and placed him in command of the squadron.<ref name="Bounter Hunters 18" />
