'''FN-1824'''<ref name="LEGO">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref> was a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> [[Gender|male]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who served in the [[First Order military|forces]] of the [[First Order]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. Following the [[Battle of Takodana]]<ref name="Episode VII" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[FN Trooper Corps|FN]]-1824 was assigned to guard [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], a [[Force-sensitive]] [[scavenger]] held [[Prisoner|captive]] by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] aboard [[Starkiller Base]]. Using her latent [[The Force|Force]] [[Force power|abilities]], Rey used a [[Jedi]] [[mind trick]] on FN-1824 to force him into releasing her from captivity. Her first two efforts failed, but she finally succeeded at using the Jedi ability. FN-1824 unlocked Rey's restraints and, on her command, left his [[F-11D blaster rifle]] behind before leaving her.<ref name="Episode VII" /> The [[trooper]] was eventually located in his quarters and questioned. He was confused and had no memory of what had happened.<ref name="awakensnovel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
FN-1824 appeared in the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], in which he was portrayed by [[Daniel Craig]].<ref name="Craig Role">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/article/2015/12/17/daniel-craig-makes-cameo-star-wars-force-awakens|text=Daniel Craig's Star Wars: The Force Awakens cameo revealed|author=Lee, Chris|date=2015-12-17|work=[[Entertainment Weekly Books|Entertainment Weekly]]|archivedate=20200810000308}}</ref> In both the film's adult and junior novelizations, when Rey's first attempt at a mind trick fails, the guard simply stares at her. Rey succeeds on her second attempt. Additionally, in the novelizations, Rey instructs the guard to return to his quarters and he also promises to speak of the encounter to no one.<ref name="awakensnovel" /><ref name="awakensjunior">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>

Among the filming crew, the character was dubbed FN-007. The cameo came about as Craig was at Pinewood Studios preparing for his next James Bond film ''[[wikipedia:Spectre (2015 film)|Spectre]]'', and asked Ben Dixon, who worked as an assistant director on both ''The Force Awakens'' and ''Spectre'', if he could be involved. One day later, he was brought on the set and given a Stormtrooper armor. In his words, “I had to wear the thing all day and I couldn’t feel my hands by the end of the day."<ref name="Commentary">Director [[J.J. Abrams]] [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] commentary</ref><ref name=podcast>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y_mx8Jf42aM Daniel Craig on GLASS ONION, James Bond, Star Wars, Marvel! Happy Sad Confused w/Josh Horowitz]</ref> Craig expected his role to merely be in the background a la [[Wanten|the stormtrooper who bumps his head in ''A New Hope'']], and was surprised to learn he would be in an entire scene. He was even more surprised when, instead of having someone else voice the character, Abrams had him come in for a 3 hour ADR session.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.express.co.uk/entertainment/films/1497490/James-Bond-Daniel-Craig-Star-Wars-stormtrooper-cameo-Rey-No-Time-To-Die|text=James Bond: Daniel Craig's Star Wars cameo got out of control 'I didn't sign up for this'|author=Kriazis, Stefan|date=2021-09-27|archivedate=20210928005045}}</ref>  

This stormtrooper was nicknamed "JB-007" by fans, referring to Craig's role as James Bond, agent 007.<ref name="Daily Mail">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dailymail.co.uk/tvshowbiz/article-3382960/Daniel-Craig-s-role-stormtrooper-Star-Wars-confirmed-revealed-four-line-dialogue-Force-Awakens-movie.html|text=Daniel Craig's role as a stormtrooper in Star Wars is confirmed as it is revealed he had a four-line dialogue in The Force Awakens movie|author=Bamigboye, Baz; Farmer, Ciara|date=2016-01-03|archivedate=20181001105551}}</ref><ref name="TheSun">{{WebCite|url=https://www.thesun.co.uk/archives/news/938105/confirmed-daniel-craigs-cameo-in-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Confirmed: Daniel Craig's cameo in Star Wars: The Force Awakens|author=Edwards, Natalie; Brankin, Emma|date=2016-01-03|archivedate=20180322193215}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'', he is identified as FN-1824. Despite the game being non-[[canon]], story group member [[Pablo Hidalgo]] confirmed on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that the names provided by the game for previously unidentified characters, including FN-1824, were considered canon.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/749616406245699584|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2016-07-03|quote=@LelalMekha Yeah, same with Infinity.|archivefile=File:PabloHidalgo-LegoName-Twitter.png|nolive=1}} {{C|In response to: "@pablohidalgo <nowiki>youtube.com/watch?v=JwxAeHPLpPg</nowiki> The Lego game identifies a few BG characters from TFA, but are those names canonical?"}}</ref>
