'''Clabron''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Age of Republic" /> in the [[Clabron system]] in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Clabronian]] [[species]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], many Clabronians wished to stay out of the conflict, but [[Grand Minister]] [[Stin]] planned to side with the [[Galactic Republic]]. The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], who had [[Spy|spies]] on the world, sent the [[assassin]] [[Lis Mohles]] to [[Death|kill]] Stin, along with [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], who had went to [[Mission to Clabron|visit]] Clabron and meet the Grand Minister. While Stin's successor, [[Second Minister]] [[Tarmin]], was more reluctant to openly fight in the war, Amidala managed to convince him to consider providing other aid to the Republic.<ref name="Age of Republic" />
