'''Fissol''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Ithorian]] [[bounty hunter]] who worked with the [[Gamorrean]] hunter [[Gowski]]. During the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]], the pair learned of a [[Jedi]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] from a [[Duros]] [[Unidentified Duros bounty hunter|bounty hunter]] and traveled there in order to claim the [[bounty]] on the Jedi. Fissol and Gowski managed to locate and capture the [[Force-sensitive]] local boy [[Ezra Bridger]]; however, they were then defeated by his [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Kanan Jarrus]]. Jarrus [[mind trick]]ed the pair to make them believe they had been misled and sent them to settle the score with the Duros, who they now believed had tricked them.

==Biography==
{{Quote|No wonder the Jedi were wiped out. Bunch of wimps.|Fissol comments on how easily he and Gowski captured Ezra Bridger|<ref name="EotP" />}}
The [[Gender|male]] [[Ithorian]] Fissol worked as a [[bounty hunter]] during the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Around five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], he and a [[Gamorrean]] [[bounty]] hunter named [[Gowski]] scared a [[Duros]] [[Unidentified Duros bounty hunter|bounty hunter]] into revealing that he knew of a [[Jedi]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] of [[Lothal]]. The Duros had hoped to claim the bounty himself, as the price on Jedis' heads was incredibly high, but claimed he would let the two other hunters take it.<ref name="EotP" />

The pair agreed to go after the Jedi and traveled to Lothal, where they met with a [[Fissol's local contact|local contact]] who worked with Fissol and learned the location of the [[Force-sensitive]] street urchin [[Ezra Bridger]]. Gowski then knocked out the contact and the two bounty hunters traveled to a cave where Bridger was trying to train himself to better use [[the Force]]. They at first observed him from a nearby ridge before confronting Bridger in the cave while the target was practicing jumping with the Force. Using Gowski's [[jetpack]] they then pursued the boy when he tried to escape, with Fissol following him through a crack in the wall that Gowski could not fit through. Fissol became stuck trying to fit his weapon through, but the Gamorrean circled round and caught Bridger by surprise, knocking him out with an electric shock.<ref name="EotP" />

Having subdued their quarry, the pair made to leave with Gowski carrying Bridger. Before they could exit the cave however, they were confronted by Bridger's [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] who beat both of them using his fists. Jarrus then placed the pair in [[stuncuffs]] and used a Jedi [[mind trick]] to make them both believed they have been tricked by the Duros and that they should go and settle the score. The pair then left Lothal in a [[YV-666 light freighter]] and found the Duros so they could took vengeance.<ref name="EotP" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You really don't know anything about Jedi, do you? They don't age the same as we do. They might even age backwards.''"<br />"''Everyone knows that. That kid's probably an old man. Means we'd better stay sharp.|Gowski and Fissol discuss Ezra Bridger's age|<ref name="EotP" />}}
Fissol had [[Color|brown]] skin, green [[eye]]s and no [[hair]]. He and Gowski believed they could capture a Jedi, despite believing a number of incorrect facts about them including that Jedi aged backwards, had extremely dense bones, and were capable of flight. The Ithorian was weak minded enough to be mind tricked by Jarrus.<ref name="EotP" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Kid's heavier than he looks.''"<br />"''It's those dense Jedi bones. Let's hope they pay us by the pound.|Gowski and Fissol replies to Gowski in basic using his translator|<ref name="EotP" />}}
Fissol wore a [[translator collar]] which allowed him to speak [[Galactic Basic Standard]]. He used a [[blaster rifle]] in combat and wore green and orange [[armor]].<ref name="EotP" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fissol first appeared in "[[Eyes on the Prize]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] published in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 6|sixth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels Magazine]]'', which was released in [[2015]]. The comic was written by [[Jeremy Barlow]] and illustrated by [[Bob Molesworth]].
