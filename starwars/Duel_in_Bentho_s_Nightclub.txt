A very brief '''duel''' occurred in [[21 BBY/Legends|21 BBY]] between [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Dark Acolyte/Legends|Dark Acolyte]] [[Trenox]].

==The duel==
[[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] tracked [[Trenox]] to a seedy [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] bar known as [[Bentho's Nightclub]]. After confirming his target was inside by [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster]], Skywalker confronted Trenox and ordered him to stand down. Trenox enraged, told Skywalker not to give him orders, and attacked, pushing Skywalker back with his aggressive [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] style. After a brief and undetermined struggle, Trenox retreated, to attack the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]], leaving Skywalker to make his way through the [[Hangar/Legends|docking bays]].
