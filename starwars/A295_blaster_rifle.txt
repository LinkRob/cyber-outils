The '''A295 blaster rifle''' was a [[blaster rifle]] used by [[Emon Kref]] while he was stationed at the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Echo Base]]. Kref used the rifle in the [[Battle of Hoth]], though its [[Power cell|energy pack]] was held in place by only a cord and a piece of tape.<ref name="Beyond Hope" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The A295 props used in the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] were made from resin painted black and measured twenty-eight inches by nine inches by three inches without the barrel. They were based on a Second World War German rifle with distinct attatchments including the barrel, grip, decorative scope, and stock. The carrying strap was leather with metal rings at each end.<ref name="Auction">{{WebCite|url=https://entertainment.ha.com/itm/movie-tv-memorabilia/props/star-wars-episode-v-the-empire-strikes-back-tcf-1980-hoth-rebel-trooper-a295-blaster-rifle/a/7456-11046.s|text=''Star Wars: Episode V - The Empire Strikes Back'' (TCF, 1980), "Hoth Rebel Trooper" A295 Blaster Rifle|work=[[Wikipedia:Heritage Auctions|Heritage Auctions]]|archivedate=20260426203005}}</ref>
