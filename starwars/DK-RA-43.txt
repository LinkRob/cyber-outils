'''DK-RA-43''' was an [[RA-7 protocol droid]]. Originally built for the [[Galactic Empire]] in the [[droid]] factories of [[Affa]], he ended up with a new master, the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]], under unclear circumstances&mdash;the droid was either won in a [[sabacc]] game or exchanged for a purebreed [[Loth-cat]] and a barrel of [[Savareen brandy]]. After a [[memory wipe]], DK-RA-43 was outfitted with databanks extracted from several [[tourism droid]]s, and ordered to prepare dossiers for a book titled the ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''.<ref name="GEG" />
