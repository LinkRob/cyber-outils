'''CryoBan''' was a chemical compound used in [[carbon-freezing]]. In its processed form, it created intense cold that could damage [[Species|living organisms]] by absorbing all heat in an area. The chemical compound was also used in the [[WW-41 CryoBan grenade]], a model of [[grenade]] manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> and in the [[CryoBan rifle]], a [[rifle]] manufactured by the [[Loronar Corporation]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
CryoBan was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'', a [[2003]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[BioWare]] and published by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="Kotor">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref>
