'''Barcel''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy|navy]] as a [[communications officer]] on the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of [[Prelate]] [[Verge]]'s personal [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Herald]]''. During the hunt for former Imperial [[Governor]] [[Everi Chalis]] in [[3 ABY]], Barcel informed [[Captain]] [[Tabor Seitaron]] when word came that the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]], which Chalis was traveling with, had [[Siege of Inyusu Tor|captured]] a [[Inyusu Tor mineral processing facility|facility]] on the [[planet]] [[Sullust]]. The communications [[Imperial officer|officer]] also reported to Seitaron when Chalis attempted to escape Sullust aboard a [[Thief|stolen]] [[shuttle]].

==Biography==

Barcel was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served in the [[Imperial Navy|navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="BTC" /> By the [[Standard year|year]] [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}} Furthermore, Barcel's appearance in the novel must have also taken place in that year.</ref> he was the [[communications officer]] on the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the<ref name="BTC" /> [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> ''[[Herald]]'', the personal [[Star Destroyer]] of the [[Imperial Ruling Council]] member [[Prelate]] [[Verge]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Hoth]], [[Captain]] [[Tabor Seitaron]], Verge's guest aboard the ''Herald'', prepared the numerous [[Planet|worlds]] along the [[Rimma Trade Route]], including [[Sullust]], [[Malastare]], and [[Tshindral]], for an anticipated attack by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]].<ref name="BTC" />

When the 61st [[Siege of Inyusu Tor|captured]] the [[Inyusu Tor mineral processing facility]] on Sullust, Barcel was alerted to the event and was quick to inform Seitaron. The duty [[Imperial officer|officers]] on the ''Herald''{{'s}} bridge then applauded the captain, who asked for Verge and a channel to the [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] of [[Vixus Squadron]]. The [[squadron]] subsequently traveled to Sullust and the ''Herald'' followed suit to assist the [[Imperial Garrison (Sullust)|local Imperial forces]] in engaging the 61st and apprehending the former [[Governor]] [[Everi Chalis]], whom Verge and Seitaron were tracking down.<ref name="BTC" />

Late into the [[Siege of Inyusu Tor|battle]] against the 61st on Sullust, Barcel approached Verge and Seitaron at the bridge, informing them that a [[shuttle]] was leaving one of the [[transport]] stations below. Seitaron asked if it was broadcasting clearance codes, and Barcel told him they were codes the captain had warned to watch out for. Deducing that the shuttle was being [[pilot]]ed by Chalis, Seitaron ordered for the [[Thief|stolen]] [[Starship|vessel]] to be pulled aboard the ''Herald'' with a [[tractor beam]] and departed the bridge with Verge to confront the ex-governor. Ultimately, Chalis set off twenty [[ion bomb]]s inside her shuttle, causing a power surge in the ''Herald'' and forcing its withdrawal from Sullust.<ref name="BTC" />
