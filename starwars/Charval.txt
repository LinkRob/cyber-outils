'''Charval''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] affiliated with the [[Bail Organa's resistance movement|resistance movement]] led by [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], a member of the [[Imperial Senate]]. Organa had him and two other agents, [[Beska]] and [[Selko]], ensure the safe extraction of Senator [[Mon Mothma]] off the [[Imperial Senate Building]] and [[Coruscant]], unaware that Beska was an operative for the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]. After Charval overheard Beska communicating with Captain [[Lagret]] of the ISB, she pretended she was just contacting "work." Charval became suspicious about Beska and requested her to hand him the communication device. She feigned a handoff, then [[Death|killed]] Charval.<ref name="Welcome Rebellion" />
