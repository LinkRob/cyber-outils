"'''Finale''',"<ref name="ESBs">[[Star Wars Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back (soundtrack)|''Star Wars Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back'' soundtrack]]</ref> also titled "'''May the Force Be with You'''," is a [[Musical score|music piece]] composed by [[John Williams]] for the [[Star Wars Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back (soundtrack)|soundtrack]] to the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. Released in both piano<ref name="Piano" /> and orchestra arrangements, the latter as part of a medley, the piece features a rendition of "[[The Force Theme]],"<ref name="Alfred">{{WebCite|url=https://www.alfred.com/the-empire-strikes-back-medley/p/00-FO9718|text=The Empire Strikes Back Medley: Full Orchestra Conductor Score &amp; Parts: John Williams|archivedate=20201021155649}}</ref> the [[Wikipedia:Leitmotif|leitmotif]] for [[the Force]]. The melody of the piece is the same as that in the track "The Rebel Fleet / End Title,"<ref name="ESBs" /> which is played during the final scene at [[Backup Rendezvous Point Gamma-Nine]] in ''The Empire Strikes Back'', as [[Luke Skywalker]] receives his [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] [[Limb|hand]] from [[2-1B]].<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>
