'''Star Wars Celebration Europe''' of [[2023]] is the fifteenth [[Celebration]] convention. The event was held from [[April 7]] to [[April 10|10]] at the ExCel Centre in London, England, UK.<ref name="Twitter Announcement" />

The official Celebration livestream of April 7 was hosted by [[Anthony Carboni]] and [[Krystina Arielle]].<ref name="Day1">{{SWYouTube|VyIHhK2VKvQ|Star Wars Celebration LIVE! 2023 - DAY 1}}</ref>
