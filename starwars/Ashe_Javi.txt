'''Ashe Javi''' was a [[human]] [[musician]] born on the [[planet]] of [[Riosa]]. [[Cal Kestis]] recruited them on the planet [[Koboh]] together with their [[droid]] [[DD-EC]] to play [[music]] at [[Pyloon's Saloon]].<ref name="Survivor" />

==Biography==
Ashe Javi was a [[human]] individual from the [[planet]] [[Riosa]]. Early in their life, they would hear the sounds of [[Factory|factories]] and started making [[music]] out of it in their head.<ref name="Survivor" />

===Time on Coruscant===
Javi started performing and caught the attention of an important individual who worked with famous [[musician]] [[Maximilian Rebo|Max Rebo]]. Javi originally thought they were going to start performing on [[Chandrila]], but was caught off guard when the individual stated they were going to perform on [[Coruscant]] instead. Initially, they enjoyed playing on Coruscant but eventually found the process tiresome as people simply wanted to see the next big thing over anything else. Despite the intention to advertise Javi on billboards around the planet and across the [[Core Worlds|Core]], they decided to leave their penthouse, record label, and fame behind and proceeded to take a transport to [[Kuat]] to further their musical ambitions.<ref name="Survivor" />

===Looking for samples===
Javi soon found that the [[Kuat shipyards]] were home to solid samples to use in their music, later describing the planet's soundscapes as "filthy." It was also in these shipyards that they found DD-EC jamming on some recordings at a monitoring station. After they had become a team, they decided that they needed a change from the sounds of the [[City|cities]], factories, and nightclubs that they were used to so they travelled around to lots of worlds intending to find more organic sounds but despite the number of worlds they had visited, they never stayed anywhere long.<ref name="Survivor" />

===Time on Koboh===
Sometime during their travels they visited Koboh and found something different about the air and the people that caused Javi to stay on the planet along with DD-EC<ref name="Survivor" /> in [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> Soon later, they were recruited by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] to play music at [[Pyloon's Saloon]], a [[cantina]] run by the [[Latero]] [[Greez Dritus]]. Javi appreciated how much of a dive Pyloon's was and commented with excitement on the establishment's wonky dynamics, weirder customers, and barely functioning [[refresher]]s.<ref name="Survivor" />

They spent their time on the planet sampling its ambient sounds for their music. At one point, Javi sampled the sound of [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] from the mountains, an excursion they said nearly led them to die ten times. The captured sounds were later layered over lofi [[Trandoshan funk]], a sound that Javi said you had to hear to believe.<ref name="Survivor" />

After spending time on the planet, they believed that Koboh's faint heartbeat was special and that was what they had been looking for. When an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] arrived over the planet, Javi intended to sample its sounds and turn the aggression into creativity.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ashe Javi first appeared in the [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'', which was developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]]. They were voiced by [[Andi Christensen]].<ref name="Survivor" />
