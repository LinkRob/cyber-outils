'''Anyel''' was a marine biologist who worked at the [[underwater fishing base]] on [[Madurs]]. She conducted a tour of the base for a group of tourists who were on Madurs as part of a cruise on the ''[[Halcyon]]''. She showed some concern at the [[ice quake]]s caused by the [[black tower]], but when [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] offered to connect her to [[University|universities]] such as the [[University of Jenrand]], she refused as they were being monitored by [[Alecia Beck]].<ref name="TPATS" />
