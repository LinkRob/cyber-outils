'''Christophsian sugar''' was a caramelized-[[sugar]] mix that was inspired by the [[crystal]]line [[forest]]s of<ref name="Cookbook" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Christophsis">{{Databank|christophsis|Christophsis}}</ref> [[Christophsis]]. The multipurpose sweetener consisted of [[Color|light brown]] sugar crystals that could add a hint of citrus flavor and warm cooking [[spice]] to many [[foodstuff]]s.<ref name="Cookbook" />

The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] claimed that it was addictive; he wrote that he personally could not resist adding the sugar to popped [[grain]]s, freshly-toasted [[mealbread]], or a cup of [[caf]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Cookbook date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> he published his [[Book|cookbook]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'' that included a recipe for Christophian sugar in which he compared its versatility to his [[Nerfsteak Seasoning]]. The book also contained recipes for [[Moogan Tea]] and [[sweet-sand cookie]]s that both used the sugar as a topping.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Christophsian sugar was included as a recipe in the [[2019]] cookbook ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe uses the [[Wikipedia:Caramelization|caramelization]] process and is made with the ingredients [[Wikipedia:White sugar|sugar]], water, [[Wikipedia:Orange (fruit)|orange]] extract, and [[Wikipedia:Vanilla extract|vanilla extract]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

[[Christophsian sugar/Legends|Christophsian sugar]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was introduced in the [[2009]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] exclusive article "[[Dining at Dex's]]," which was written by [[Gregory Walker]].<ref name="DaD">{{Hyperspace|url=fans/hyperspace/source/dexdiner/index.html|text=Dining at Dex's|int=Dining at Dex's}}</ref>
