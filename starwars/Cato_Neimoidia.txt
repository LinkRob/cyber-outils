'''Cato Neimoidia''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Cato Neimoidia system]], within the [[Quellor sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Colonies]]. It was a [[purse world]] of the [[Neimoidian]]s and [[capital]] of its own [[Cato Neimoidian government|planetary government]]. Cato Neimoidia was also the headquarters of the [[Trade Federation]], a wealthy [[megacorporation]] during the [[Republic Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Much of the world was covered in a persistent fog, while the [[Bridge city|bridge cities]] of Cato Neimoidia were suspended above the planet's [[acid]]ic [[ocean]] surface on bridges anchored on massive giant rock arches, upon which thrived [[forest]]s and [[grass]]lands.

==Description==
[[File:CNCityBridges-SS.png|thumb|left|250px|Cato Neimoidia's bridge cities]]
Cato Neimoidia was located in the [[Cato Neimoidia system]], within the [[Quellor sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Colonies]] region.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Emerging from the depth of its stormy, geyser-spouting<ref name="Scorched">{{InsiderCite|165|Scorched}}</ref> [[acid]]ic [[ocean]]s<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> were massive rock arches,<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" /> cliffs,<ref name="Episode Gallery" /> and canyons<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> where the fog-covered [[planet]]'s [[forest]]s and [[grass]]lands thrived.<ref name="Cato Neimoidia" />

The planet's [[University of Cato Neimoidia]] was a location where [[engineer]]s could study.<ref name="Mask of Fear" />

==History==
===Republic Era===
Cato Neimoidia joined the Galactic Republic approximately [[25,000 BBY|25 millennia before]] the Battle of Yavin.<ref name="BMF 31" /> By the [[High Republic Era]], Cato Neimoidia was known for its [[opera]]; in the aftermath of the [[First Emergence]], [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Pan Eyta]] supported taking [[time]] away from raids, suggesting he would use the break to take a vacation to see Cato Neimoidia's opera.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

===Clone Wars===
{{Quote|That business on Cato Neimoidia doesn't... doesn't count.|Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" />}}
During the [[Clone Wars]] in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="22 BBY Date">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] is set between the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Battle of Christophsis]], as the novel's plot involves [[Anakin Skywalker]] becoming a [[Jedi Knight]], a rank he attained between those events according to the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates both events to [[22 BBY]], ''Brotherhood'' must be set in that year.</ref> the planet tried to remain neutral.<ref name="Brotherhood" /> Two [[Jedi]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], were involved in "[[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Kenobi and Skywalker)|messy business]]" on the planet which required Kenobi to be saved by Skywalker. Kenobi, however, felt that this particular rescue did not count towards Skywalker's running tally of the number of times that he had saved him.<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" />

[[File:Battle-over-bridge-city.png|300x330px|thumb|right|Clone troopers and battle droids clash over a bridge city.]]
During the Clone Wars, the Trade Federation signed over holdings, including Cato Neimoidia, to the Intergalactic Banking Clan in exchange for a loan to purchase equity in a [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|new droid factory]].<ref name="Senate Spy" /> Nevertheless, the Neimoidians would manage to stay in control, and the Trade Federation remained headquartered on the planet.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Senator]] [[Rush Clovis]] of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia|visited]] Cato Neimoidia to negotiate with Senator [[Lott Dod]] of the [[Trade Federation]] at his [[Great Vaults of Cato Neimoidia|palace]] while accompanied by Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]], who was secretly investigating rumors of the Banking Clan backing the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Senate Spy" /> After the Separatists gained control over the planet,<ref name="Build 44">{{BuildR2Cite|44|Droid Directory}}</ref> the Republic [[Conquest of Cato Neimoidia|conquered]] it.<ref name="HC 9">{{HelmetCollectionCite|9|Databank A-Z}}</ref>

Later in the war, Skywalker and [[Ahsoka Tano]] were sent to assist in [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|defending]] Cato Neimoidia from the Separatists, but shortly after their dramatic arrival on the planet they were summoned back to [[Coruscant]] to investigate the [[bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar]].<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> With the [[501st Legion]] pulled away to deal with the bombing, Cato Neimoidia fell under Separatist control<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> once more.<ref name="Build 44" /> Despite its initial neutrality,<ref name="Brotherhood" /> Cato Neimoidia had become a hotbed for Separatist sentiment by the war's end,<ref name="Mask of Fear" /> by which point the Trade Federation's mask<ref name="Prop">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> of neutrality<ref name="Mask of Fear" /> had been exposed as a lie.<ref name="Prop" />

The Republic [[Assault on Cato Neimoidia|attacked]] the planet in an attempt to capture [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] at his [[Nute Gunray's stronghold|stronghold]]. Gunray, however, escaped into the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="BMF 31">{{BuildFalconCite|31|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> Aiming to take back the planet during the final days of the Clone Wars,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> Jedi Master [[Plo Koon]] led the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] in an [[Battle of Cato Neimoidia|attack]] against the Separatist Alliance on the planet, resulting in a Republic victory. Following the battle,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] ordered the [[Order 66|execution of Clone Protocol 66]], forcing the [[Jedi General]]'s own wingmates to open fire on his [[starfighter]], killing him along with the thousands of other [[Jedi]] across the galaxy affected by this swift command.<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" />

===Reign of the Galactic Empire===
[[File:Conveyex_SWSG.png|thumb|left|250px|A 20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport on Cato Neimoidia during the reign of the Galactic Empire.]]
[[Matese]], who served as a [[Republic Intelligence]] [[direct action operative]] during the war, lost his right [[eye]] on Cato Neimoidia, forcing him to wear an [[eye patch]] while he waited for [[Veteran Services]] to cover a [[Cybernetics|replacement's]] cost.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> Several sectors of Cato Neimoidia were left in a ruined state and needed medical supplies and water as the Clone War ended and the [[Imperial Era]] began.<ref name="Mask of Fear" /> Following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], a [[Cato Neimoidia garrison|garrison]] was established on Cato Neimoidia during the Imperial Era as a protectorate, with the Trade Federation reduced to puppet status<ref name="BMF 31">{{BuildFalconCite|31|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> and taken out of galactic business.<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref> For all the Separatist sentiment<ref name="Mask of Fear" /> that had grown<ref name="Brotherhood" /> on Cato Neimoidia, no kind of full-scale rebellion was launched against the Empire on Cato Neimoidia.<ref name="Mask of Fear" />

The new Empire was established by Palpatine, who named himself its [[Galactic Emperor]]. Less than one month into the Imperial Era, a political rival of Palpatine's made fun of the new Empire, which resulted in relief supplies for Cato Neimoidia being denied, officially held up. [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] pondered if the denial of relief for Cato Neimoidia was worth the stability offered by the war's end but knew the tally of sacrifices would only end up getting longer.<ref name="Mask of Fear" />

The Galactic Empire maintained at least one [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport]] being used for transportation.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> Around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date ref">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on [[Vandor]] and in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to nine years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]]; therefore, the events on Vandor and in ''Solo'' must take place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> the [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]] owned a storehouse on Cato Neimoidia which contained items he had [[Collector|collected]].<ref name="Solo Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> The wealthy Neimoidian [[arms dealer]] [[Luk Hutsok]] lived within a [[Luk Hutsok's mansion|mansion]] on Cato Neimoidia with his [[Luk Hutsok's daughter|daughter]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] met with Hutsok in his mansion to discuss selling weapons to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Legends">''[[Legends of the Alliance]]''</ref>

===New Republic Era===
Nearly 28 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]], a mysterious and extremely dangerous annual race known as [[The Gauntlet]] took place on Cato Neimoidia.<ref name="Scorched" /> During the [[Cold War]], the [[Resistance]] undertook a [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Resistance)|mission to Cato Neimoidia]] to rescue [[Lor San Tekka]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 20">[[Poe Dameron 20|''Poe Dameron'' 20]]</ref>

==Inhabitants==
[[File:LottDodPalace-SS.png|thumb|right|250px|Lott Dod's palace on Cato Neimoidia]]
Cato Neimoidia was one of the most prominent [[Purse world|Purse Worlds]] colonized by the [[Neimoidian]]s,<ref name="Brotherhood" /> a wealthy [[species]]<ref name="Cato Neimoidia" /> originally from the planet [[Neimoidia]]<ref name="UFC">''[[Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars]]''</ref> who operated the Trade Federation,<ref name="Neimoidian">{{Databank|neimoidian|Neimoidian}}</ref> which controlled most of the interstellar shipping passing through the Rim territories. One of the wealthiest corporate organizations in the galaxy,<ref name="Trade Federation">{{Databank|trade-federation|Trade Federation}}</ref> Cato Neimoidia was the Trade Federation's headquarters,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and the Neimoidians constructed opulent [[Bridge city|bridge cities]]<ref name="Cato Neimoidia" /> that spanned the world's rock arches<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" /> and cliffs<ref name="Episode Gallery" /> and jeweled palaces owned by Trade Federation [[baron]]s, who sought constantly to one-up each other and dazzle guests with displays of ostentatious hospitality and luxury living.<ref name="Cato Neimoidia" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cato Neimoidia was originally developed as one of a montage of planets, when the initial story of the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] opened with a series of Clone Wars battles across the galaxy. Cato Neimoidia (often spelled Niemoidia at this stage) was very different in its early incarnations. It was a [[gas giant]] surrounded by an artificial [[Ringo Vinda space station|ring space station]], which was meant to echo the donut-ship design of the Neimoidian [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|Trade Federation battleships]]<ref name="Databank">{{DB|location|catoneimoidia|Cato Neimoidia}}</ref> and was eventually reused for the planet [[Ringo Vinda]].<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-unknown-trivia-gallery|text="The Unknown" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> Always envisioned as an affluent world, its interiors were marked by [[Color|golden]] structures and gaudy riches in early paintings. At the same time, the Art Department was developing "bridge world," a planet then named [[Saleucami]]. This planet of curving arc-like structures with city skylines suspended on the bottom edge of the bridges was to be home of the [[Techno Union]].<ref name="Databank" />

Both designs were dropped from the film in the finished shooting script, though Cato Neimoidia was mentioned twice as a place where Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin Skywalker, and [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Cody|CC-2224]] had some rocky history. The shooting script also described Order 66 and Plo Koon's death, but in this version, Koon is killed over [[Mygeeto]].<ref name="Databank" />

During postproduction, the mix of planets changed, and Plo Koon was moved off Mygeeto. The bridge world was resurrected and modified&mdash;the city skyline now rested atop the bridges, and it was no longer a Techno Union world. It was identified as Cato Neimoidia, and the name Saleucami was reused elsewhere for a reworked concept.<ref name="Databank" /> The concept of city skylines suspended on the bottom of bridges eventually appeared in "[[Sabotage (episode)|Sabotage]]," the seventeenth episode of the [[canon]] animated series [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'']]{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]],<ref name="Sabotage" /> which originally aired on [[February 9]], [[2013]].<ref name="Sabotage air date">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep517|text="Sabotage" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}}</ref>
