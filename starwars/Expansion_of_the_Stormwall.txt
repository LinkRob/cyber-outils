The '''expansion of the Stormwall''' was the expansion of the territory within the [[Nihil]]-controlled area of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] known as the [[Occlusion Zone]]<ref name="EyeofDarkness" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="ExpandDate" /> achieved by moving and increasing the size of the [[Stormwall]]. Several [[star system]]s, two whole [[sector]]s, and half of the [[Seswenna sector]] were swallowed by the zone's new borders. The expansion was carried out under the orders of [[Marchion Ro]], who acted in feigned outrage toward the [[Galactic Republic]]'s refusal to provide the Nihil with representation in the [[Galactic Senate]], as well as the [[Republic Defense Coalition]]'s recent [[assault on the Stormwall]].<ref name="EyeofDarkness" /> The shift in the Stormwall's position also trapped RDC operatives [[Pikka Adren]] and [[Joss Adren]],<ref name="Friend or Foe" /> who had been on the Stormwall's prior border [[Mission to recover an abandoned Nihil ship|investigating an apparently abandoned Nihil ship]],<ref name="No Big Deal" /> behind enemy lines and trapped within Nihil space.<ref name="Friend or Foe" />

==Behind the scenes==
The expansion of the Stormwall was is featured as a plot point in several works<ref name="EyeofDarkness" /> from [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]] of [[Star Wars: The High Republic|''The High Republic'']] multimedia project.<ref name="EveryTHR">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref> The event was first depicted but not described in the ''[[The High Republic: Tales from the Occlusion Zone]]'' short story "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: No Big Deal|No Big Deal]]," where it the cliffhanger<ref name="No Big Deal" /> of the Stormwall's expansion<ref name="Friend or Foe" /> is only left implied.<ref name="No Big Deal" /> The expansion would be properly seen and described in ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'', a [[2023]] novel written by [[George Mann]]<ref name="EyeofDarkness" /> The subsequent ''Tales from the Occlusion Zone'' story "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Friend or Foe?]]" then confirmed the cliffhanger from "No Big Deal" was the change in the Stormwall's position.<ref name="Friend or Foe" />

The initial hardcover release of ''The Eye of Darkness'' lists the expansion of the Stormwall taking as taking planets like [[Pantora]], which the zone already contained, [[Sullust]], [[Utapau]], [[Ryloth]], [[Malastare]], and [[Sluis Van]] before its new border nearby [[Eriadu]]. However, the placement of the new border did not align with the placement of Malastare and Sullust on the galactic map. In the digital edition, the list is edited so Malastare, Pantora, and Sullust are not included. The new list of taken planets instead mentions [[Triton]], [[Abtin]], and [[Elrood]].<ref name="Post-release edit">{{WordPress|subdomain=numidianprime|url=2023/11/16/the-occlusion-zone-of-the-nihil-2|name=Numidian Prime|text=The Occlusion Zone of the Nihil|archivedate=20231201060644}}</ref>
