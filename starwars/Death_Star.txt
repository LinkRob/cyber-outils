{{Quote|We call it the Death Star. There is no better name, and the day is coming soon when it will be unleashed.|Scientist Galen Erso|<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>}}
A '''Death Star''' was a gargantuan [[space station]] armed with a [[planet]]-destroying [[superlaser]] powered by [[kyber crystal]]s.

==Death Stars==
===DS-1 Battle Station===
{{Main|DS-1 Orbital Battle Station}}
{{Quote|That's no moon. It's a space station.|Obi-Wan Kenobi|audio=ThatsNoMoon-ANH.flac|<ref name="Episode IV" />}}
[[File:ConstructDeathStar-CM.png|thumb|left|250px|Constructions modules were used during the construction of the Death Star.]]
The DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station,<ref name="Insider 156" /> also known as the DS-1 Orbital Battle Station,<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> was a [[superweapon]] that was originally designed by the [[Geonosian]]s during the waning [[Standard year|year]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]]. The [[Project Stardust|project]] was later taken over by the [[Galactic Empire]];<ref name="Catalyst" /> however, the [[Death Star plans|plans]] for the battle station were [[Battle of Scarif|stolen]] from [[Scarif]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]],<ref name="Rogue One" /> which led to the weapon's destruction at the [[Battle of Yavin]], but not before it unleashed its planet-destroying cannon on the [[Imperial Center of Military Research|Imperial security complex]] on Scarif,<ref name="Rogue One" /> [[Jedha City]] on [[Jedha]],<ref name="Catalyst" /> and [[Destruction of Alderaan|annihilated]] the entire planet of [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> 

The name "Death Star" was coined by the [[scientist]]s who found themselves working on the project, such as [[Galen Walton Erso]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> The name was adopted by the Empire at large, with [[Major]] [[Lio Partagaz]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] considering it "a prescient turn of phrase."<ref name="JKE">{{Andor|Jedha, Kyber, Erso}}</ref>

===DS-2 Battle Station===
{{Main|DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station}}
{{Quote|The data brought to us by the Bothan spies pinpoints the exact location of the Emperor's new battle station.|Mon Mothma|<ref name="RotJ" />}}
[[File:DeathStar2.jpg|thumb|right|200px|The Second Death Star orbiting Endor.]]
After the destruction of the first Death Star, the DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station was the second and final Death Star constructed by the Galactic Empire and was designed to be more powerful than the original. During the [[Battle of Endor]], the Death Star II's energy shield was destroyed and the Rebels used the unfinished superstructure of the battle station against it, destroying the weapon as they had its predecessor.<ref name="RotJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Inspiration of other franchises===
The idea of the Death Stars and empowering players to be able to tell stories with similar engines of death and levels of sci-fi gravitas and drama was an inspiration for the now-massive novel and wargame franchise Warhammer 40,000 as noted at the start of the first rule book for the first edition.<ref>''[https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Warhammer_40,000_Rogue_Trader Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader]''</ref>

===Mimas===
[[File:Mimas.jpg|thumb|right|150px|That's no space station; it's a moon.]]
'''[[Wikipedia:Mimas (moon)|Mimas]]''', the smallest and the innermost of seven major moons orbiting the planet [[Wikipedia:Saturn|Saturn]], is notable for a single enormous impact crater called [[Wikipedia:Herschel (crater on Mimas)|Herschel]]. The appearance of Mimas and Herschel in pictures taken during the space probe [[Wikipedia:Voyager I|Voyager I's]] [[1980]] flyby of the Saturn system showed a similarity to the first Death Star, with Herschel appearing as the superlaser disc, as remarked in the scientific press.<ref>{{BookCite|url=Hunt, G. E.|container=`Voyager' to Jupiter and Saturn: The Journey of a Lifetime - II.|title=[[Wikipedia:Journal of the British Astronomical Association|Journal of the British Astronomical Association]]|number=vol.93, no.3}}</ref>

During the [[Wikipedia:Cassini-Huygens|''Cassini'' orbiter's]] visit to the Saturn system in [[2004]], the resemblance was noted again in the official press releases<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.nasa.gov/mission_pages/cassini/media/cassini-080505.html|text=Cassini Flies by Saturn's Tortured Moon Mimas|date=2005-08-05|work=[[Wikipedia:NASA|NASA]]'s [https://www.nasa.gov official website]|archivedate=20051001113847|nolive=1}}</ref> and by the scientific press.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.newscientist.com/article/dn6999-saturns-moon-is-death-stars-twin|text=Saturn's moon is Death Star's twin|author=Kelly Young|date=2005-02-11|work=[[Wikipedia:New Scientist|''New Scientist'']]|archivedate=20251116143511}}</ref>

===White House petition===
In late 2012, a petition was submitted to the White House website urging that the United States government build a real Death Star as an economic stimulus and a means to ensure people got back to work again, and wanted its completion by 2016 at the earliest. The petition gained more then 25,000 signatures, ensuring that an official response be supplied. In early 2013, the proposal was rejected (in a tongue-and-cheek manner), citing that the overall cost of the Death Star's construction would amount to a cost exceeding $852 quadrillion, and would take 833,000 years before it could even be ready for construction due to the rate of steel production. Another reason for the rejection was also because the Government "did not support blowing up planets."<ref name="Petition">{{WebCite|url=https://petitions.whitehouse.gov/response/isnt-petition-response-youre-looking|text=This Isn't the Petition Response You're Looking For|work=White House|archivedate=20130112150536}}</ref> As such, the [[Star Wars Blog]], in response to it, created a light-hearted, in universe reply from the [[Galactic Empire Public Relations|Galactic Empire's Public Relations department]] were the Empire, with official statements from Admiral Conan Antonio Motti and Governor Wilhuff Tarkin, shrugged off the criticisms by the planet Earth's leaders regarding the design of the battlestation. In addition, in the same source, it also denounced the planet for grossly overestimating the cost of designing the Death Star, as well as commenting the overall lack of production methods of the planet in comparison of the Galactic Empire, even implying that the planet's leaders were cowardly in their decision.<ref name="blog">{{SW|url=news/planet-earth-abandons-death-star-project-in-face-of-superior-galactic-imperial-power|text=Planet Earth Abandons Death Star Project In Face Of Superior Galactic Imperial Power}}</ref>
