{{Quote|My goal in developing Sith was to create a plausible, linguistically sound language. Plausible in that it would sound and feel real&mdash;not like a cartoonish language for the bad guys to speak.|Ben Grossblatt|<ref name="Speak Like a Sith">{{InsiderCite|134|Speak Like a Sith}}</ref>}}
'''Ben Grossblatt''' is a senior editor at [[becker&mayer!]], the bookmakers who produced and developed [[The Jedi Path: A Manual for Students of the Force (real-life book)|''The Jedi Path: A Manual for Students of the Force'']] and ''[[Book of Sith: Secrets from the Dark Side]]''. Grossblatt has a Master's degree in linguistics. In [[November]] [[2010]], he was assigned the creation of a plausible [[Sith (species)/Legends|Sith]] [[Sith (language)|language]] for ''Book of Sith''. In the [[Star Wars Insider 134|134th]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'', Ben Grossblatt wrote ''[[Speak Like a Sith]]'', an article explaining the basics of his Sith conlang.
