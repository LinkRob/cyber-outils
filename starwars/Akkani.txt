{{Quote|The way my eopie, Akkani, lies down facing the walls of the hut, eyes closed.|Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Obi-Wan 1" />}}
'''Akkani''' was an [[eopie]] owned by [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] during his [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. On one occasion,<ref name="Obi-Wan 1" /> during Kenobi's final [[Standard day|days]] on Tatooine,<ref name="Solicit">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-may-2022-exclusive-preview|text=A Tale from Obi-Wan's Youngling Days and More from Marvel's May 2022 ''Star Wars'' Comics – Exclusive Preview}}</ref> Akkani lay down outside [[Ben Kenobi's home|Kenobi's hut]] and faced its walls with its [[eye]]s closed, which, along with other factors, Kenobi took to mean a [[sandstorm]] was approaching.<ref name="Obi-Wan 1" />
