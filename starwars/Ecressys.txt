'''Ecressys''' was the [[Gender|male]] [[governor]] of the [[Velcar sector]] under the [[Galactic Empire]]. He went rogue after the [[Battle of Endor]] as a [[warlord]].

==Biography==
A cynical man disguising himself as an unending patriot, Ecressys routinely complained of the lack of support for his [[sector]] from the ruling government in the [[Core Worlds]], and because of this, was suspected by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] of disloyalty to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Propaganda" />

However, after Palpatine's [[death]] during the [[Battle of Endor]], Ecressys spread the news of his [[emperor]]'s survival in a bid to prop up his own power, closely controlling all communication within the Velcar sector and breaking away from the Empire to create his own [[Velcar Imperial remnant|remnant faction]], becoming a [[warlord]].<ref name="Propaganda" /> Towards that end, he created the propaganda poster, ''[[Endor is A Lie]]''.<ref name="SWTR&FotGE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> Subsequently, he took his newfound reign for granted, plundering the treasuries of the [[planet]]s he ruled. By the time the [[New Republic]] arrived to the [[sector]]'s [[Velcar capital world|capital]] to announce the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]], Ecressys had abandoned the governorship and fled the New Republic into the [[Western Reaches]] with the wealth of hundreds of worlds.<ref name="Propaganda" />
