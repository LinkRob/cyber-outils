The '''''Crucible''''' was a [[Paladin-class corvette|''Paladin''-class corvette]] operated by the [[Jedi Order]]. The [[Starship|ship]], which had been in service from the time of its construction during the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] through to the [[Clone Wars]], was used to ferry [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] from [[Coruscant]] to [[Ilum]]. Once on Ilum, the younglings would undergo a ritual called [[Gathering|The Gathering]], in which they would find the [[kyber crystal]]s for their first [[lightsaber]]s.

==Description==
[[File:Crucible-TCWs5BR1.png|thumb|250px|left|The ''Crucible'' with landing gear down and boarding ramp extended]]
The ''Crucible'' was a [[Paladin-class corvette|''Paladin''-class corvette]]<ref name="Starfighters" /> that had a work area with many drawer storage compartments, in which [[Professor]] [[Huyang]] kept all the parts necessary to construct a complete [[lightsaber]], with the exception of the unique [[kyber crystal]]s that formed the heart of the weapon. By stepping up onto crates, Huyang could reach the higher compartments to search for parts as needed.<ref name="A Test of Strength">{{TCW|A Test of Strength}}</ref>

At the time of the [[Clone Wars]], its inner doors still featured the previous emblem that was in use by the [[Jedi Order]] when the vessel was constructed.<ref name="A Test of Strength" />

The rest of the ship included a hold, a training room,<ref name="A Test of Strength" /> a filtering system,<ref name="Bound for Rescue">{{TCW|Bound for Rescue}}</ref> and an extensive ventilation underneath the hallways. The ship also possessed an emergency power fail-safe.<ref name="A Test of Strength" />

==History==
The ''Crucible'' dated back to the [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]],<ref name="Databank" /> used to bring [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] to the sacred [[Planet|world]] of [[Ilum]], where they would discover their [[kyber crystal]]s through a ritual known as [[Gathering|The Gathering]].<ref name="The Gathering" /> After retrieving their crystals, the students would learn from [[Huyang]], an ancient [[droid]] who would assist in the creation of their lightsabers.<ref name="A Test of Strength" />

[[File:Crucible-DoH.png|thumb|250px|right|The ''Crucible'' arrives on Ilum]]
During the Clone Wars, six [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]], [[Petro]], [[Ganodi]], [[Byph]], [[Katooni]], [[Zatt]], and [[Gungi]], were selected to travel in the ''Crucible'' to Ilum to take part in The Gathering.<ref name="The Gathering" /> When they returned to the ship, Huyang helped the younglings determine the styling of their individual lightsabers. Then, the professor found appropriate hilt casings and any other stylized parts, then provide the inner workings for assembly. A [[Hologram|holographic]] diagram of a lightsaber's exploded view was continuously on display as a guiding blueprint, while the younglings' Jedi escort [[Ahsoka Tano]] and Huyang supervised the build process. Huyang would also perform the final inspections of the new weapons, and inform the younglings whether or not there was more work to be done.<ref name="A Test of Strength" />

When the ship was attacked by [[Ohnaka Gang|pirates]], they took Tano hostage, leaving the younglings, Huyang, and [[R2-D2]] left onboard. They would follow the pirates to the [[planet]] [[Florrum]] to rescue Tano.<ref name="Bound for Rescue">{{TCW|Bound for Rescue}}</ref> However, when they tried to escape, the ''Crucible'' was destroyed by one of the pirates' [[WLO-5 speeder tank|WLO-5 battle tanks]]. Following the [[invasion of Florrum]], [[Hondo Ohnaka]] and the surviving pirates would salvage the wreckage of the ship.<ref name="ANB">{{TCW|A Necessary Bond}}</ref>

Chong.|archivefile=File:Le_Merrer_New_Horizon_art.png}}</ref><ref name="Chong art art">{{Facebook|noprofile=1|url=photo.php?fbid=258801344289651&set=pb.100004793614905.-2207520000.1499796139.&|name=Russel G.
