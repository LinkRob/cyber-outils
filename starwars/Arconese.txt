'''Arconese''' was the [[language]] of the [[Arcona]] [[species]].<ref name="Language">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref> The Arcona [[Hem Dazon]] spoke Arconese while he was in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="ANH" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Arconese first appeared in the [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH" /> It was first identified as Arconese in the video "Every Language in ''Star Wars''" which released in [[2019]] on the ''Star Wars'' Kids [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel.<ref name="Language" /> The name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity where it was first used in ''[[Alien Anthology]]'', a [[2001]] supplement to the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="AA">''[[Alien Anthology]]''</ref>
