'''Dorm four''' was a dormitory located in [[Pinyumb]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Sullust]].<ref name="bftc" /> At some point by [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}} The incident involving dorm four residents is mentioned during the main events and so must have occurred by that year.</ref> the workers who lived there suffered from burns when the steam pipes in some [[magma]] extractors burst. The workers were subsequently forced to take leave off work and although a number of them had returned to their jobs, the rest risked eviction as they were still unable to go back.<ref name="bftc" /> In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[stormtrooper]] [[Thara Nyende]] brought supplies to her [[Thara Nyende's uncle|uncle's]] [[Thara Nyende's uncle's cantina|cantina]]. Her [[Gender|uncle]] gave a tube of [[burn salve]] from the supplies to the [[Sullustan]] [[Myan]] for the workers in dorm four.<ref name="bftc" />

==Behind the scenes==
