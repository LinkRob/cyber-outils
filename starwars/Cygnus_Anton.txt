'''Cygnus Anton''' was an [[Bird|avian]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[Chief Regional Auditor]] and was associated with the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate. Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], Anton visited the [[planet]] [[Calior]]. A [[hologram]] of the avian that appeared to be Anton accompanied their forces&mdash;Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s and Crimson Dawn guards&mdash;on the ground while the Chief Regional Auditor stayed aboard one of their [[All Terrain Scout Transport]] "AT-STs." The avian demanded that the people of Calior give the [[southern water works]] to the Empire and, if Calior's people desired continued access to the facility, a [[Standard month|monthly]] tax of five thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].

As Anton finished speaking, a group of [[Soldier|warriors]] known as the [[Revengers]], which sought to eliminate Crimson Dawn, [[Skirmish on Calior|attacked]] the avian's forces. The Chief Regional Auditor [[Commanding officer|commanded]] their forces to charge; however, [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]&mdash;who was on a quest to [[Death|kill]] all Crimson Dawn members in the Empire's service&mdash;destroyed Anton's AT-ST with his [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|TIE Advanced]], killing the avian.

==Biography==
===Two demands===
{{Quote|First, you will surrender your claim to the southern water works. I know the arguments. You created that lake! No. Your ''parents'' created that lake. While under the employment of the ''regional government''. It is now ''Imperial colonial property'' and has been formally reclaimed. You'll have continued ''access'', of course. Pending your acquiescence to my ''second'' demand&hellip;of ''five thousand credits'' in monthly tribute. Do we have an agreement?|Chief Regional Auditor Cygnus Anton|<ref name="Vader 18" />}}
[[File:Calior-2020DarthVader18.jpg|thumb|left|350px|Cygnus Anton wished to claim Calior's southern water works for the Empire.]]
[[Chief Regional Auditor]] Cygnus Anton was an [[Bird|avian]] [[bureaucrat]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] and came to be affiliated with the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime syndicate, which opposed the Empire.<ref name="Vader 18" /> Sometime between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Anton traveled to the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[Planet|world]] [[Calior]]. A group of Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s and Crimson Dawn guards landed on the planet aboard a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] while accompanying a [[hologram]] of the avian that looked like the real Anton. The Chief Regional Auditor deemed touching Calior's ground foolish and visited the planet aboard one of their two [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s. The [[human]] [[Carrax]], one of Calior's inhabitants, questioned the Chief Regional [[Auditor]] on their identity. As Anton introduced themself, Carrax noticed the avian's Crimson Dawn guards, expressing that the Chief Regional Auditor was supposed to protect Calior from Crimson Dawn.<ref name="Vader 18" />

One of the guards then [[Death|shot]] Carrax, prompting a villager to complain that Anton had executed him before the avian stated what they wanted. As Calior's inhabitants were in a "listening mood" after Carrax's death, the Chief Regional Auditor demanded that they surrender their hold of Calior's [[southern water works]] to the Empire, as the [[water]] works were Imperial [[Colonist|colonial]] property. Dismissing the locals' claim to the water works, Anton remarked that if the installation's users wished to continue using it, they had to pay five thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] every [[Standard month|month]].<ref name="Vader 18" />

===Sith and flames===
{{Quote|It's those ''Revengers''. I ''told'' you they'd come if you brought these Crimson Dawn guards out in the ''open'' like this.''"<br />"''And that's exactly why I ''did'' it. Now you heard her&hellip;''charge!''<nowiki />|A stormtrooper and Chief Regional Auditor Cygnus Anton|<ref name="Vader 18" />}}
[[File:CygnusExplains-2020DarthVader18.jpg|thumb|right|350px|Cygnus Anton's final moments]]
Before the avian could continue speaking, however, three [[Soldier|warriors]]&mdash;[[Ankala Sahm]], [[S'ira]], and [[Tanka]]&mdash;that formed the group known as the [[Revengers]] [[Skirmish on Calior|attacked]] the Chief Regional Auditor's forces. A [[Unidentified stormtrooper (Cygnus Anton)|stormtrooper]] reminded Anton that they had previously warned them that bringing the Crimson Dawn troops out in the open would result in the Revengers' appearance. The avian explained that that was the reason they brought the guards with them, [[Commanding officer|ordering]] their forces to attack the warriors.<ref name="Vader 18" />

After Sahm [[Fire|burnt]] Anton's hologram with a [[flamethrower]] and told them that they now knew how "it felt," Anton revealed their position, with the avian's AT-ST firing at the Revengers. As the group retreated, a [[Vader's crew|crew]] of [[assassin]]s under the command of [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]&mdash;who was on a quest to eliminate all members of Crimson Dawn in the Empire's service&mdash;struck Anton's forces. Vader fired at the avian's AT-ST with his [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|TIE Advanced]], killing Anton, who claimed they could explain in their final moments.<ref name="Vader 18" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Now you know what it feels like, Crimson scum.''"<br />"''Ha. Silly duck. You think I'd be foolish enough to touch the ground of this forsaken planet ''in person''?|Ankala Sahm and Chief Regional Auditor Cygnus Anton|<ref name="Vader 18" />}}
The Chief Regional Auditor considered touching the ground of Calior, which Anton considered a forsaken world, foolish. Anton was an avian with [[Color|red]] feathers, a blue beak, and green [[eye]]s. Yellow feathers covered their neck.<ref name="Vader 18" />

==Equipment==
The Chief Regional Auditor wore a blue [[Clothing|uniform]], which was adorned with two gray epaulets, and a black [[cape]]. A gray [[belt]] with three holes, one of which was red, surrounded his uniform.<ref name="Vader 18" />
