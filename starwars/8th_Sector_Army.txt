The '''8th Sector Army''' was one of the twenty [[Sector Army|Sector Armies]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] that were active during the [[Clone Wars]]. The Sector Army consisted of 147,456 [[clone trooper]]s that were divided into four [[corps]] and was attached to one of the [[Republic Navy]]'s twenty numbered [[fleet]]s. Located in the northern quadrant of [[the galaxy]], the [[army]] was headquartered on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Ord Mantell]] during the war.

==Description==
[[File:Lyndon-Willoughby-TheCloneArmy.png|thumb|left|250px|The 8th Sector Army was composed of clone troopers (pictured).]]
As a [[Sector Army]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], the 8th Sector Army<ref name="BMF 48" /> was responsible for the defense of their specific area. At the start of the war, the Grand Army was organized into ten [[Systems Army|Systems Armies]]. They in turn were split into two Sector Armies and were drawn from a specific area of [[the galaxy]]. As a result, the 8th Sector Army belonged to the same Systems Army as another. The 8th Sector Army served in the northern quadrant of the [[galaxy]] alongside the [[7th Sector Army|7th]]&ndash;[[12th Sector Army|12th]] Sector Armies.<ref name="BMF 18" />

Like other Sector Armies, the 8th Sector Army had one of twenty [[Galactic Republic]] [[Haven-class medical station|''Haven''-class medical stations]] towed to them,<ref name="Note" /> and contained 147,456 [[clone trooper]]s who were apportioned into four [[corps]].<ref name="CL 2016" /> Comparable to other Sector Armies, the [[army]] was attached to one of the twenty numbered [[fleet]]s of the [[Republic Navy]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

==History==
By the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Kashyyyk and Other Planets in the Mid Rim}}</ref> which began in [[22 BBY]] with the [[First Battle of Geonosis]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Black Sun]] criminal syndicate was based in a [[Black Sun stronghold|fortress]] within [[Ord Mantell City]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[shadowport]] [[planet]] [[Ord Mantell]].<ref name="BMF 48" /> The fortress, a symbol of the syndicate's power, was challenged at the start of the Clone Wars after the Republic chose Ord Mantell as one of its main storage worlds, supporting its [[Republic Navy|fleet]] and as a site for several barracks for the 8th Sector Army.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> In the [[Standard year|years]] that followed the start of the conflict, Ord Mantell became the headquarters of the 8th Sector Army and eventually hosted several [[Republic Military]] installations, including a [[Ord Mantell storage facility|storage facility]], [[Ord Mantell barracks|barracks]], and a [[Ord Mantell fleet support base|fleet support base]].<ref name="BMF 48" /> As a result, the Black Sun attempted to regain its power on Ord Mantell by joining the [[Shadow Collective]], a criminal alliance<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> and secretly a rival [[Sith]] order.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the 8th Sector Army was first indirectly mentioned as one of twenty Sector Armies in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 18|eighteenth issue]] of the [[De Agostini]] magazine ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'',<ref name="BMF 18" /> published around [[May 6]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF 18 Date">{{BuildFalconDates|18}}</ref> It was later identified separately in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 48|forty-eighth issue]],<ref name="BMF 48" /> released around [[December 2]] of the same year.<ref name="BMF 48 Date">{{BuildFalconDates|48}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Eighth Army]] debuted in the [[2012]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Paul R. Urquhart]].<ref name="ESG">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
