A '''scrap pile''' was run by the [[Xot]] [[Bidvel]] on a [[Unidentified planetoid (scrap pile)|remote planetoid]]. Bidvel collected discarded [[Droid|droids]], inventoried them, and then sold the useful parts. [[Davil]] worked for him at the site, along with one other employee.<ref name="Scrap Pile" />

== Description ==
The scrap pile was located on a [[Unidentified planetoid (scrap pile)|remote planetoid]] and consisted of a mountainous stack of scrap and half-dismantled [[Droid|droids]] protected by a [[containment shield]]. The scrap pile also had one facility with a room where the droids were inventoried and scrapped. The site also had a crane that rose above a molten pit that could be closed via a hatch.<ref name="Scrap Pile" /> 

== History ==
The scrap pile was owned by the [[Xot]] [[Bidvel]] who had as least two employees, [[Davil]] and an unidentified aide. In [[3 BBY]], the [[C1-series astromech droid]] [[C1-10P]] ("Chopper") was brought to the site and was inventoried. However, during the process, he escaped and stole the codes to activate the other half-dismantled droids. Chopper was recaptured, but not before awakening the droids, who overthrew Bidvel and established their own [[Unidentified droid group|group]] to run the facility.
