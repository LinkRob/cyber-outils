'''Cruiser''' was a designation given to [[starship]]s, usually [[capital ship]]s, of varying sizes and capabilities. While usually considered to be reserved for ships around 400-600 [[meter]]s in size,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> the term sometimes included [[corvette]]s and [[frigate]]s alongside actual cruisers.<ref name="Rise of the Separatists">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> Examples included the [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class light cruiser]],<ref name="DBRepublic">{{Databank|republic-cruiser|Republic Cruiser}}</ref> the [[CR90 corvette]],<ref name="DBalderaan">{{Databank|alderaan-cruiser|Alderaan Cruiser}}</ref> the [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]],<ref name="DBImplight">{{Databank|imperial-light-cruiser|Imperial Light Cruiser}}</ref> the [[MC80 Star Cruiser]],<ref name="DBMonCal">{{Databank|mon-calamari-star-cruiser|Mon Calamari star cruiser}}</ref> and the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], often referred to as a "[[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[attack cruiser]]."<ref name="DBRepAttack">{{Databank|republic-attack-cruiser|Republic Attack Cruiser}}</ref> The [[Kilji]] species' government of the [[Kilji Illumine]] used [[Kilji war cruiser]]s and [[Kilji picket cruiser]]s.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> One such cruiser was part of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[Pommrio family]]'s [[Pommrio fleet|fleet]].<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> This [[Chiss Ascendancy]] made use of diplomatic cruisers.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref>

By the [[time]] of the [[Separatist Crisis]], the [[Jedi Order]] typically used the Republic's cruisers for its diplomatic missions across [[the galaxy]]. However, some tasks required less conspicuous and smaller [[starship]]s such as the [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7 ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref>

==Characteristics==
Cruisers, similar to [[corvette]]s and [[frigate]]s, were depended on for a variety of roles, both in civilian and military applications. In military [[Navy|navies]], cruisers served the vital role of filling in the space between small [[starfighter]]s and large [[capital ship]]s.<ref name="Starships and Speeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> According to the [[Anaxes War College System]], [[warship]]s were divided into seven main classifications. Cruisers were classified to be between 400-600 [[meter]]s, heavy cruisers at 600 to 1,200 meters, and battlecruisers at 2,000 to 5,000 meters. These classifications were flexible, however, and some ships could be moved to a higher or lower class depending on their total firepower and function.<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>

===Sub-classifications===
There were multiple sub-classifications of cruisers, including [[diplomatic cruiser]]s<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> and merchant cruisers. [[Arrestor cruiser]]s were specialized cruisers that used powerful [[tractor beam projector]]s to steer traffic and patrol.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> An [[attack cruiser]], such as the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], was a cruiser honed in for ship-to-ship combat.<ref name="DB Venator">{{Databank|republic-attack-cruiser|Republic Attack Cruiser}}</ref> In the [[Galactic Empire]], attack cruisers were considered a step down from the standard [[Star Destroyer]]s.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> A [[battlecruiser]], was a significantly larger type of cruiser.<ref name="Battles" /> One example of a battlecruiser was the [[Maelstrom-class battle cruiser|''Maelstrom''-class]], or the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class]], which was 2,915 meters long.<ref name="TFA Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> For the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], a battlecruiser was more of a commodious label for midsized [[alien]] starships until their capabilities could be better defined.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>

A [[dreadcruiser]] was a type of cruiser used by the [[Hutt]]s.<ref name="THR 8">[[The High Republic (2021) 8|''The High Republic'' (2021) 8]]</ref> Meanwhile, a heavy cruiser was a type of cruiser that was larger than the standard, but were classified below destroyers in the Anaxes War College System.<ref name="Battles" /> Despite this, at least one heavy cruiser, the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class]], was large and formidable enough to also be considered a [[battleship]],<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> a [[battlecruiser]],<ref name="Battles" /> and [[Star Dreadnought]].<ref name="Collapse" /> The ''Subjugator''-class was 4,845 meters in length,<ref name="Starfighters">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> far longer than the 600 to 1,200 meters that would classify a heavy cruiser in the Anaxes War College System.<ref name="Battles" /> The [[MC85 Star Cruiser]], another model of heavy cruiser, was 3,438 meters long.<ref name="TLJ Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> Other heavy cruisers included the [[Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser|''Dreadnought''-class heavy cruiser]],<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> the [[Chiss heavy cruiser]],<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> the [[Emissary-class Republic Cruiser|''Emissary''-class Republic Cruiser]],<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> the [[Vindicator-class heavy cruiser|''Vindicator''-class heavy cruiser]],<ref name="Forged in Battle">''[[Forged in Battle]]''</ref> and the [[Interdictor-class heavy cruiser|''Interdictor''-class heavy cruiser]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

[[Light cruiser]]s were smaller than the standard cruiser, with models including the [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class light cruiser]] at 115 meters,<ref name="SWJ1">''[[Star Wars Journeys: The Phantom Menace]]''</ref> the [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] at 325 meters,<ref name="BMF 60">{{BuildFalconCite|60|Starship Fact File}}</ref> the [[Carrack-class light cruiser|''Carrack''-class light cruiser]] at 350 meters in length,<ref name="BMF 52">{{BuildFalconCite|52|Starship Fact File}}</ref> and the [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|Imperial ''Gozanti''-class cruiser]]<ref name="HC 20">{{HelmetCollectionCite|20|Databank A-Z}}</ref> at 63 meters.<ref name="Rebels Guide">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

Other cruiser sub-classifications included [[bulk cruiser]]s, [[patrol cruiser]]s, [[picket cruiser]]s, [[pocket cruiser]]s, [[Star Cruiser]]s, and [[war cruiser]]s.
