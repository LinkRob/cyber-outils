The '''Delta Squad, Form Up Squad Pack''' ('''SWP62''') is an expansion for [[Atomic Mass Games]]' miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: Shatterpoint]]''. It was released on [[August 22]], [[2025]], and features the four members of [[Delta Squad]]: [[Boss (clone commando)|Boss]], [[Fixer (clone commando)|Fixer]], [[Sev]], and [[Scorch]].

==Publisher's summary==
===Online summary===
''Deployed on the front lines of the Clone Wars, Delta Squad puts the elite training of the clone commandos to the test. Boss, Fixer, Sev, and Scorch, unwavering in their loyalty to the Republic, execute each mission with deadly precision and firepower.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

''The Delta Squad, Form Up Squad Pack gives players 4 fan-favorite Republic commandos to deploy in their games of Star Wars&trade;: Shatterpoint. Scorch and Sev provide unique area control abilities that allow them to target multiple units and control the battlefield, while Boss and Fixer provide tactics to the team and can manipulate objectives.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

===Physical package summary===
{{Quote|Sorry, Deltas. You heard him. It's back to the action for you.|Clone Advisor|<ref name="Asmodee" />}}
''Deployed on the front lines of the Clone Wars, Delta Squad puts the elite skills of the clone commandos to the test. Boss, Fixer, Sev, and Scorch, unwavering in their loyalty to the Republic, execute each mission with deadly precision and overwhelming firepower.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

''Never ones to sit back and wait, Delta Squad takes control of the battlefield removing every obstacle on their way to securing their objectives. Armed with the customizable DC-17m ICWS blaster and their unparalleled combat training, Delta Squad can complete any mission no matter the enemy.''<ref name="Asmodee" />
