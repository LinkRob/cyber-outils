As the [[Galactic Republic]] continued to [[Fall of the Old Republic|collapse]], the [[Sith]] were able to seize control of much of the known [[The galaxy|galaxy]], ushering in a '''period''' where the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lords]] reigned supreme across the [[star]]s.

==History==
===Sith domination===
In the aftermath of the [[Hundred-Year Darkness]], the [[Sith]] and the [[Jedi]] wage a [[Sith Wars|series of wars]] throughout their history so ruinous that at points each is brought to the brink of destruction.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The Sith ultimately became the dominant power in [[the galaxy]] for a period of [[time]].<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:Jedi_Temple_spires_ROTS.png|thumb|left|300px|The Jedi Temple was built atop the Shrine in the Depths.]]
During one of these [[Sith war (Old Republic)|wars]] the Sith had [[Battle of Coruscant (Sith war)|captured]] the [[planet]] of [[Coruscant]], the [[capital]] of the Republic. They constructed an immense black [[Shrine in the Depths|shrine]] over a light side [[vergence]] on a [[Sacred spire|mountain]] at the [[heart]] of the [[planet]]<ref name="NoP">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref> no later than close to five [[Millennium|millennia]] before [[14 BBY]].<ref name="ShrineDate">According to the novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], the Shrine in the Depths on [[Coruscant]] had been abandoned "close to five thousand years" as of the story's present day in [[14 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> During this period, trade in [[gemstone]]s was much less regulated, although this caused a flooding of the market and the value of gemstones dropped until bans and limits were put in place.<ref name="Master & Apprentice">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref>

===The Ascendant threat===
[[File:DeadSith-2020DoctorAphra22.png|thumb|right|300px|Sith are cut down by their own lightsabers thanks to the Ascendant's Spark Eternal]]
During the Sith's reign, a [[Religion|cult]] known as the [[Ascendant]]<ref name="Aphra 20" /> worked under the [[Chadra-Fan]] [[Miril]]<ref name="Aphra 24">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 24|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 24]]</ref> and attempted to replicate the effects of [[the Force]] through technology. Although the group flourished during the era,<ref name="Aphra 20" /> the Ascendant was in fact opposed to the Sith<ref name="Aphra 22 /> and hoped to use their technology against the dark side users. However, the Sith declared their work to be blasphemous and dispatched the [[Darkseekers]] to destroy the cult.<ref name="Aphra 24" /> In time, the Sith found the Ascendant's [[Tomb of the Spark Eternal|main temple]] and [[Slaughter of the Ascendant|cut down]] its members, but Miril gave her life to ensure they would not take the [[Spark Eternal]] for themselves.<ref name="Aphra 22" />

The Sith's rule across the galaxy would ultimately decrease over time. Although the Republic still continued to fall, the Jedi and remnants of the Old Republic would have [[Jedi-Sith War|one final war]] against the Sith, bringing an end to their despotic reign once and for all, although the Order survived in [[Grand Plan|secret]] through the [[Rule of Two]].<ref name="SWP" /><ref name="Sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref>

===Legacy===
{{Quote|Once more the Sith will rule the galaxy, and we shall have peace.|[[Darth Sidious]], to [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
One thousand years later, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] of the Galactic Republic, who was secretly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious, [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] his [[Politics|government]] into the [[Galactic Empire]], declaring himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] in the process.<ref name="Episode III" /> He was an admirer of the [[Sith Empire]] and the [[Sith Lord]]s who ruled it and wished to usher in a renewed period of its rule. He also possessed various artifacts dating back to the [[Standard day|days]] of the dark empire.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A period of a Sith-controlled galaxy is first mentioned by Sheev Palpatine in a scene from the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Episode III" /> This time was never specified, although the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity had various stories depicting multiple Sith empires throughout the [[Old Republic era]]. This was further elaborated on after ''[[Book of Sith: Secrets from the Dark Side]]'' was published in [[2012]], when, in a segment "written by" Darth Sidious, it was stated that he admired the dark lords [[Darth Malgus|Malgus]] and [[Tenebrae|Vitiate]] of the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Second Sith Empire]] and modeled his vision of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] after the civilization, which was at its height during the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]].<ref name="Book of Sith">''[[Book of Sith: Secrets from the Dark Side]]''</ref><ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
