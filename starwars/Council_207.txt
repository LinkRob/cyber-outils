'''Council 207''' was a [[farm]]ing [[city|settlement]] on the agricultural world [[Mina-Rau]].<ref name="Sagrona Teema" /> In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Sagrona Teema Date">{{AndorDate|202}}</ref> when [[Bix Caleen]] was asked by [[Lieutenant]] [[Krole]], who was part of an [[Imperial Supply Census]] on Mina-Rau, what her council was she responded by stating it was 207.<ref name="Sagrona Teema" />
