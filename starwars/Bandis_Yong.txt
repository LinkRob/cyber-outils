'''Bandis Yong''' was an individual who once attended the [[Vocational School for Institutional Security]] and a friend of [[Jix Hekyl]]. Yong had some talent as a [[slicer]], but also an over-inflated opinion of himself. When [[Merei Spanjaf]] was searching for a way to get a [[snooper]], Hekyl gave his name to her as a contact. Spanjaf met with him and he was able to give her another contact that could get her what she needed. He was, however, lecherous individual with personal hygiene issues. Spanjaf had to fend off three requests to date her and tell him six times that she already had a [[Zare Leonis|boyfriend]] before he would leave her alone. When she later used his name with the contact he had given her, the old [[Human|man]] suggested that she should have better taste in friends.<ref name="ranks" />
