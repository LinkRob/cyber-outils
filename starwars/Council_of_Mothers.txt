The '''Council of Mothers''' was the ruling body of<ref name="LPOA" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Gatalenta system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Gatalenta]] by the time of the [[Imperial Era]]. On Gatalenta, the practice of [[slavery]] was forbidden by the Council of Mothers. The ruling body further dictated that any enslaved individuals brought to the world would be freed if their master was caught.<ref name="LPOA" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> [[Apprentice Legislator]] [[Amilyn Holdo]]&mdash;who [[Homeworld|hailed]] from Gatalenta&mdash;told her friend, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the planet [[Alderaan]], about the Council of Mothers' law concerning slavery.<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Council of Mothers was mentioned in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="LPOA" />
