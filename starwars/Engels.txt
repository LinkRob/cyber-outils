'''Engels''' was a [[human]] who served as an [[Imperial officer|officer]] in the [[Imperial Military|military]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. At the time of the early rebellion against the Empire, he had the rank of [[captain]], and was in charge of a metal factory hovering in the [[atmosphere]] of [[Dooma 4]], supervising the production of the entire facility.

==Biography==
The [[human]] Engels served as [[Imperial officer|officer]] in the [[Imperial Military|military]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] as and was in [[Commanding officer|command]] over a [[metal]] factory in the [[atmosphere]] of [[Dooma 4]]. At one point, the factory was assaulted by the notorious rebel [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[Partisans]], who were hoping to retrieve classified information. Engels allowed a subordinate to lead the [[stormtrooper]]s and [[Death trooper]]s against the rebels, watching the battle via security cameras. As the struggle raged outside, the captain's forces caught Gerrera's [[lieutenant]], [[Megyn Soone]], who had just placed explosive charges inside the factory. Engels, who had read many files about Gerrera and his troubled past&mdash;including the tragic [[death]] of his sister [[Steela Gerrera|Steela]]&mdash;and hoped to use the capture of Soone to his advantage.<ref name="CTL" />

[[File:Partisans-Dooma4-RebelsMagazine4.png|thumb|right|300px|Gererra's forces destroying Engels' factory]]
Engels contacted Gerrera directly via [[comlink]], informing him that Soone lay in their clutches, and that they would let her go if the Rebel told him where the charges were placed. In response, Gerrera offered Engels a seemingly better deal: he would not only deliver the charges' detonator in person, but also surrender and trade places with Soone. At the meeting place, a triumphant Engels came to proceed with the exchange, but instead of surrendering, Gerrera activated the detonator. The charges then exploded, destroying the stabilizers that maintained the factory in the air.<ref name="CTL" />

As the station started crashing with everyone on board, Engels called Gerrera crazy, thinking he had effectively committed suicide. However, the rebel had a plan, for an allied U-wing blasted a hole in the station's wall, creating an escape route for him and Soone. The Imperial captain attempted to escape, but Gerrera caught them by the [[Limb|leg]], and the two engaged in a brief struggle during which the Rebel managed to steal Engels' [[Imperial code cylinder|code cylinder]]. At the eleventh hour, Gerrera and Soone hopped into the hovering U-wing, leaving the crumbling station and abandoning Engels to his fate.<ref name="CTL" />

==Personality and traits==
Engels was a broad-shouldered individual with [[Color|light]] skin, blue [[eye]]s, a prominent nose, and a square jaw. Although completely bald or shaven, the officer sported black eyebrows and a reddish-brown mustache. Even when talking to an enemy, Engels maintained an air of affability and calm, which they only lost when the station started crashing.<ref name="CTL" />
