The '''Alkenzi Air Command''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] command unit stationed at the [[Alkenzi Air Base]] on [[Aldhani]]. The command administered the [[Aldhani Garrison]]'s [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s, including "[[Alkenzi Wing Seven]]." Three TIE fighters participated in the pursuit of a stolen [[box freighter]] during a rebel [[Aldhani heist|mission on Aldhani]], with all three being destroyed by the [[Eye of Aldhani]].<ref name="The Eye" />
