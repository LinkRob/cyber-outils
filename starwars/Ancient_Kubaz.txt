'''Ancient Kubaz''' was a [[writing]] system used by the [[Kubaz]] [[species]] that had a distinct carving style.<ref name="ITD" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> when on the [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine station]], a group of [[Jedi]] including the [[Jedi Master]] [[Cohmac Vitus]] discovered a set of [[binding statues]] with ancient inscriptions in an unfamiliar script. When asked by the [[Wayseeker]] [[Orla Jareni]] if he recognized the script, Vitus shook his head and indicated the inscriptions were not dissimilar to ancient Kubaz. The Jedi Master then added that because the system where the Kubaz resided<ref name="ITD" />&mdash;the [[Ku'Bakai system]]<ref name="CM">''[[Cyphers and Masks]]'' establishes that [[Kubindi]] was located in the [[Ku'Bakai system]]. As {{Databank|kubaz|Kubaz}} specifies the [[Kubaz]] [[species]] is [[Homeworld|native]] to Kubindi, they must reside in that [[Star system|system]].</ref>&mdash;was so distant, and because he was not aware of the species having an [[insect]] [[Deity|god]], he considered the similarity a coincidence.<ref name="ITD" />
