'''Cory Allemeier''' was credited as an artist, alongside Molly Denmark, for the canceled [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic 3]]''. They were revealed as contributers to the project alongside [[John Stafford]] in the [[2008]] reference book ''[[Rogue Leaders: The Story of LucasArts]]''. He was also revealed to have drawn concept art for "[[Naresha]]," a [[Gender/Legends|woman]] of unknown importance to the story.
