The '''''Executor'' project''' was a military shipbuilding project designed for the construction of the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] and established by the [[Galactic Empire]] during its reign. Beginning prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]] in order to reinforce the capabilities of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]] and quell the growing [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] under instructions from [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]], who ordered the construction of four ''Executor''-class [[Starship|vessels]], the project was conducted in secret from the observant [[Imperial Senate]]. In an additional effort to conceal the project's true costs and scale, Emperor Palpatine provided the committees of the Imperial Senate with false information regarding the ''Executor''-class's specifications.<ref name="BMF 27" />

Resulting in the creation of the ''[[Executor]]'' at the [[Kuat shipyards|shipyards]] of the [[planet]] [[Kuat]], and the ''[[Lusankya]]'' at the [[Fondor Shipyards|shipyards]] of the world of [[Fondor]], the project obfuscated the ''Executor''-class's size to between 8,000 and 13,000 [[meter]]s, while its true size would measure to 19,000 meters in length. The project also served as an additional statement of intent from the Emperor, who was determined to locate and eradicate the Rebel Alliance once and for all. However, following the destruction of the [[Death Star]] at the [[moon]] of [[Yavin 4]], the project's tactical importance was increased and construction plans were dramatically stepped up.<ref name="BMF 27" />
