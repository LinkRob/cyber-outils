In [[19 BBY]], during the [[Clone Wars]], a '''battle''' was fought between a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Jedi General]] [[Chiata]], and a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[dreadnought]]. Although the battle saw the destruction of the dreadnought, Chiata's [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Zeffo)|Star Destroyer]] had sustained heavy damage in the fighting, which caused it to [[Crash Site|crash]] on the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]] after dropping out of [[hyperspace]].

However, the crew of the [[Star Destroyer]] managed to survive the crash, but the Jedi General was separated from her [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Marseph]], which forced her [[clone trooper]]s to search for him. Amid their search, the [[Unidentified Clone Commander (Zeffo)|Clone Commander]] and his [[trooper]]s received [[Order 66]], which resulted in an attack on Chiata and her Padawan. All the clone troopers were [[Death|killed]] by the [[Jedi]], both of whom eventually succumbed to their own wounds.

==The battle==
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], [[Jedi General]] [[Chiata]] and her [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Marseph]], were stationed aboard a [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Zeffo)|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] alongside a [[Chiata's unit|unit]] of [[clone trooper]]s led by a [[Unidentified Clone Commander (Zeffo)|Clone Commander]]. While stationed aboard the [[Starship|vessel]], the [[Star Destroyer]] had engaged with a [[Dreadnought|Separatist dreadnought]], resulting in heavy damage to the Venator and the destruction of the dreadnought.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

==Aftermath==
Chiata's Star Destroyer suffered extensive damage during the battle with the dreadnought and afterward crash landed on the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] upon exiting [[hyperspace]]. In the [[Crash Site|crash]], Marseph became separated from his master, which forced Chiata's [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s, including the [[Clone Commander]], to search for him.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

However, amid their search, the entire Republic unit had received a transmission from [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], who ordered the Clone Commander to execute [[Order 66]]. As a result, Chiata's unit began to attack the Jedi General and the Padawan, forcing both [[Jedi]] to fight off their attackers, resulting in the [[death]]s of many clone troopers. During the fighting, both Jedi were injured, with Chiata dying and Marseph following as he succumbed to his wounds after burying his master.<ref name="Fallen Order" />
