'''Dellen Aphra''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] individual who was the [[Reproduction|daughter]] of<ref name="Chaos Agent 5" /> the human male scholar<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Korin Aphra]]. She was therefore also the [[Aphra family|half-sister]] of Korin's daughter [[Chelli Lona Aphra]],<ref name="Chaos Agent 5" /> a rogue [[Archaeology|archeologist]] who had been born to Korin and his wife [[Lona Aphra|Lona]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> During the [[New Republic Era]], Dellen aligned herself with the [[New Republic]], operating under the code name '''Aeturna'''. After their sister was recruited into the New Republic's [[Department of Artifacts and Archival Goods Retrieval]], Dellen had Chelli's [[electro-tattoo]]s deactivated, preventing Chelli from using them.<ref name="Chaos Agent 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dellen Aphra was first pictured on a variant cover for the [[Star Wars (2025) 1|first issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (2025)|''Star Wars'']]. The cover was illustrated by [[Annie Wu]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 1">[[Star Wars (2025) 1|''Star Wars'' (2025) 1]]</ref> on [[May 7]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/119550/star_wars_2025_1|text=Star Wars (2025) #1}}</ref> She was identified as Dellen in the of the [[Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent 4|fourth issue]] of the 2025 comic series ''[[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent]]''. The issue was written by [[Cherish Chen]] and published by Marvel<ref name="Chaos Agent 4">[[Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent 4|''Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent'' 4]]</ref> on [[September 24]] of the same year.<ref name="Marvel 2">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/122235/star_wars_doctor_aphra_-_chaos_agent_2025_4|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra - Chaos Agent (2025) #4}}</ref> Dellen later appeared, and was identified with her surname Aphra in the series' [[Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent 5|fifth issue]]. The issue was written by Chen, illustrated by [[Steven Cummings]], and published by Marvel<ref name="Chaos Agent 5" /> on [[October 29]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel 3">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/122236/star_wars_doctor_aphra_-_chaos_agent_2025_5|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra - Chaos Agent (2025) #5}}</ref>
