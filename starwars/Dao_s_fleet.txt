{{Quote|With regret, I report that my fleet will no longer be able to provide protection for the troops on Ryloth.|Admiral Dao, reporting to the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]]|<ref name="Supply" />}}
During the [[Battle of Ryloth|battle]] for the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]], [[Admiral]] [[Dao]] commanded a '''fleet''' of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Open Circle Fleet|Open Circle Armada]].<ref name="Supply" /> The grouping of [[starship]]s served as a relief supply [[fleet]] for the [[Twi'lek|people]] of Ryloth<ref name="SL Gallery 2">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/supply-lines-episode-gallery|text="Supply Lines" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> and attempted to protect<ref name="SL Gallery 3">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/supply-lines-episode-gallery|text="Supply Lines" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> the planet, which had fallen under a [[Ryloth blockade|blockade]] and [[Invasion of Ryloth|ground invasion]] launched by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The Separatist [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] fought and destroyed Dao's fleet above Ryloth, taking Dao with it.<ref name="Supply" />

==Complement==
{{Quote|Admiral Dao, this is General Di. We're in trouble down here. We need those supplies immediately.''"<br />"''I understand, General, but I am in no position to help. We are critically low on fuel and ammunition.|Jedi General Ima-Gun-Di speaks to Admiral Dao|<ref name="Supply" />}}
Dao's fleet consisted of at least two [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]], which were destroyed after sustained fire from the Separatist [[Starship|ships]].<ref name="Supply" />
