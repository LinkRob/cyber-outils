'''Chav tea''' was a type of [[tea]].<ref name="ROUVG" /> On one occasion when [[Chewbacca]], [[Han Solo]], and [[Hondo Ohnaka]] were staging a fight on the [[moon]] [[Dhandu 3]], Ohnaka shoved Solo too hard into a table and sent a plate of [[glowblue noodles]] and chav flying into a group of [[Pakiphantos]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> The tea was served at [[Gesh's Tapcafe]] in [[Jedha City]] on the moon [[Jedha]].<ref name="ROUVG" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Decraniated]] servants working at the [[tapcafe]] served freshly brewed chav tea and other libations to boisterous off-duty [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] stationed in the [[city]] to keep them entertained.<ref name="ROUVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chav tea first appeared in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] as a prop in the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which was released in North America on [[December 16]], [[2016]]. The tea was not identified in the film,<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> but received a name in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released the same day as the film.<ref name="ROUVG" />
