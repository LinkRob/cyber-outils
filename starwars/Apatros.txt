'''Apatros''' was a [[planet|world]]<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]'' {{C|French version only}}</ref> in the [[Apatros system]], a [[star system]] in the [[Savareen sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Apatros was introduced in the French version of the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''.<ref name="Geektionary" /> [[Apatros/Legends|Apatros]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[2006]] novel ''[[Darth Bane: Path of Destruction]]'', written by [[Drew Karpyshyn]].<ref name="PoD">''[[Darth Bane: Path of Destruction]]''</ref>

Although ''Geektionary'' states that [[Darth Bane]] was from Apatros,<ref name="Geektionary" /> ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side Pocket Expert]]'' state that he was from [[Moraband]].<ref name="JTB CW">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref><ref name="TDSPE">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side Pocket Expert]]''</ref> This article assumes the later sources are correct.
