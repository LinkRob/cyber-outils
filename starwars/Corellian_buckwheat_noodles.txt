'''Corellian buckwheat noodles''' were a type of [[buckwheat]] [[noodle]] from the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. Following the [[Battle of Yavin]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Nakari Kelen]] were sent to meet with the [[Kupohan]] intelligence agent [[Sakhet]] on the [[planet]] [[Denon]]. Skywalker and Kelen successfully reached [[Sakhet's Noodles]], the noodle bar that the Kudophan used as a front for her intelligence services, and Skywalker made their order: Corellian buckwheat noodles with [[nerf nugget]]s for Kelen, and the same noodle with [[rancor sauce]] for him. The pair were given a receipt telling them to return for a meeting the following [[Time|morning]] and then received their orders, although Skywalker was given the same order as Kelen, for which he was truly grateful.<ref name="HTTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian buckwheat noodles appeared in ''[[Heir to the Jedi]]'', a novel written by [[Kevin Hearne]] and released in [[2015]].<ref name="HTTJ" />
