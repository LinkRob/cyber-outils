The '''defense of Cato Neimoidia''', also known as the '''invasion of Cato Neimoidia''' or the '''Battle of Cato Neimoidia''', was a battle fought during the later stages of the [[Clone Wars]] when the [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]], which housed the headquarters of the [[Trade Federation]], was invaded by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. That prompted the [[Galactic Republic]] to launch a defense of the planet, which it had [[Conquest of Cato Neimoidia|assumed]] control of previously. [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]] took part in this battle, but they were recalled to [[Coruscant]] following the [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bombing]] of a [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] [[hangar]].

==The battle==
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] attacked the [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]], which they had previously [[Conquest of Cato Neimoidia|lost]] to the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Build 44">{{BuildR2Cite|44|Droid Directory}}</ref> The Republic dispatched [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], and the elite [[501st Legion]] to defend the planet.<ref name="Sabotage" /> [[Droid tri-fighter]]s played an important role in the fighting<ref name="Build 44" /> after a group of them was deployed from a [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]].<ref name="SWE Tanks and Vehicles" /> After punching through Separatist [[warship]]s in [[orbit]], Skywalker and Tano led a [[squadron]] of [[Z-95 Headhunter]]s to the surface, followed closely<ref name="Sabotage" /> by another squadron<ref name="SWE Tanks and Vehicles" /> of Republic [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] ferrying [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex|CT-7567]] and [[clone jetpack trooper]]s, as smaller Republic [[cruiser]]s engaged Separatist [[Starship|vessels]] around them. After a dogfight in the [[atmosphere]] resulted in [[Anakin Skywalker's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|Skywalker's starfighter]] getting hit by [[Pistoeka sabotage droid]]s, his fighter was disabled and he lost consciousness.<ref name="Sabotage" />

After Tano was alerted to Skywalker's status by [[R2-D2]], also onboard Skywalker's fighter, she mounted a daring rescue by first telling R2 to crash land in a nearby [[mountain]] plain, and then jumped from [[Ahsoka Tano's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|her starfighter]] to his and was able to rescue him, just as his [[starfighter]] went over the cliff and was destroyed, but not before R2 also escaped.<ref name="Sabotage" />

As Skywalker regained consciousness and became aware of what transpired, he received a transmission from [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] who informed Skywalker and Tano that the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] had been [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bombed]], and that they were being recalled to aid in the investigation. Afterwards Skywalker contacted [[Captain]] Rex for a pickup, as the battle continued.<ref name="Sabotage" />

==Aftermath==
Even though the battle had begun to swing in the Republic's favor before Skywalker and Tano were called away,<ref name="TCW encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Clone Wars}}</ref> the Separatists ultimately won the battle at Cato Neimoidia,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> and their tri-fighters had again proven their worth in combat.<ref name="Build 44" /> Additionally, Cato Neimoidia would become embroiled in the war again, being the site of [[Battle of Cato Neimoidia|another battle]] during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]].<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 44|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 44]] stated that the defense of Cato Neimoidia ended in a Republic victory,<ref name="Build 44" /> but ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'' later established it was a Separatist victory.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" />
