The '''Bothrium Beast''' was a [[Sentience/Legends|non-sentient]] creature created by the [[Dread Masters|Dread Master]] [[Brontes]], found in the [[Rooted Depths]] of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Oricon]]. During the [[Dread War]] in [[3638 BBY]] this creature was responsible for spreading corruption across Oricon in the form of [[Tendril]]s. A spacer recovered several devices from [[Dread Host|Dread Guard]] members in the vicinity of the Rooted Depths and used them to locate the Bothrium Beast's lair. They then confronted and [[Death/Legends|killed]] the creature.<ref name="TOR" />
