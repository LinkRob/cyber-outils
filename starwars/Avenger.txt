The '''''Avenger''''' was an [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] in the [[Imperial Navy]]'s [[Death Squadron]], under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Captain]] [[Lorth Needa]]. As a part of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Project Swarm]], the ''Avenger'' was one of the destroyers responsible for deploying thousands of [[Viper probe droid]]s across [[the galaxy]] in [[hyperdrive pod]]s, in an attempt to discover the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] secret [[Echo Base|base]].

==Description==
[[File:ImpStarDestroyer-TSS.png|thumb|left|270px|The ''Avenger'' in orbit of a planet.]]
An [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]],<ref name="SotEaR" /> the ''Avenger'' was equipped with a [[hangar]] bay,<ref name="ESB" /> a [[communications tower]], a [[solar ionization reactor]], and two [[deflector shield generator dome]]s.<ref name="Card Trader ESB" /> Measuring 1,600 [[meter]]s long,<ref name="Length" /> it was propelled by three [[destroyer-I ion engine]]s and four emergency use [[gemon-4 ion engine]]s.<ref name="Card Trader Stories" /> The [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] carried with it numerous<ref name="ESB" /> [[hyperdrive pod]]s containing [[Viper probe droid]]s.<ref name="CE note">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]'' establishes [[hyperdrive pod]]s, which were seen launching from the ''Avenger'' in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], carry [[Viper probe droid]]s and are equipped with [[hyperdrive]]s.</ref>

==History==
===Project Swarm===
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], to [[3 ABY]] and the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the ''Avenger'' was owned by the [[Imperial Navy]], and [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Captain]] [[Lorth Needa]]. It was involved in<ref name="ESB" /> [[Project Swarm]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] operation<ref name="Aphra">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 37|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 37]] identifies the [[Galactic Empire]]'s effort to find [[Echo Base]] with [[Viper probe droid]]s, which [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] shows the ''Avenger'' was a part of, as [[Project Swarm]].</ref> to dispatch Viper [[probe droid]]s across [[the galaxy]] in order to locate the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] secret [[Echo Base|base]], from which they waged [[Galactic Civil War|war]] on the Empire.<ref name="ESB" />

===Pursuing the ''Millennium Falcon''===
[[File:AvengerChaseFalcon-ST.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The ''Avenger'' and other Imperial forces pursuing the ''Millennium Falcon'' out of the Hoth asteroid belt]]
Following the [[Battle of Hoth]], the ''Avenger'' and the rest of Death Squadron pursued the rebel [[Starship|vessel]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', although its captain, Han Solo, evaded the Star Destroyer.<ref name="ESB" /> The ''Falcon'' flew further into the [[Hoth asteroid field|asteroid field]] evading TIE fighters. To answer a call from Emperor Palpatine, the ''Executor'' left the asteroid field. After the ''Millennium Falcon'' underwent repairs in an asteroid cave, it was sighted by the ''Avenger''<nowiki/>'s Captain Lorth Needa. He ordered the ship to fire and landed a shot on the ''Falcon''<nowiki/>'s stern. Solo turned his ship around and charged the Star Destroyer, heading towards the bridge. Needa ordered for the shields to be raised but lost the ''Falcon''. The rebel ship had hidden itself on the back of the ''Avenger''{{'s}} conning tower in a sensor blind spot. Needa decided to take full responsibility for losing the ''Millennium Falcon'' and left to apologize to Vader. Aboard the ''Executor'', Vader ordered to disperse the fleet after killing Captain Needa. Following standard Imperial procedure, the ''Avenger'' dumped its garbage and flew into hyperspace while the ''Millennium Falcon'' detached and "drifted away with the garbage".

===Delivering a Dark Lord===
[[File:VadersArrivalEndor-RotJAVA.png|thumb|250px|left|Darth Vader's shuttle departs from the ''Avenger''{{'}} docking bay.]]
In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="GA" /> shortly before the [[Battle of Endor]], while being escorted by two other [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]], the ''Avenger'' delivered Darth Vader to the [[Endor system]] to oversee the construction of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] orbiting the [[Endor|forest moon of Endor]]. Vader was [[shuttle|ferried]] from the ''Avenger'' to the [[space station]] aboard [[ST 321]], his personal [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]].<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|technology|imperialshuttle|Imperial shuttle}} {{C|Relationships section under Darth Vader}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Avenger'' first appeared in the opening scene of the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="ESB" /> Although ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> and [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 9|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 9]] both identify the Star Destroyer that deployed the probe droid to Hoth as the ''Avenger'',<ref name="HC9">{{HelmetCollectionCite|9|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] conversely calls the vessel the ''[[Stalker]]''.<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> The ''Stalker'' was later confirmed to exist in canon by the [[2021]] book ''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]'', an adaptation of the [[original trilogy]] by [[Jack Mitchell]].<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> This article assumes that ''Complete Locations'' supplies the correct name, being the more recent source.
