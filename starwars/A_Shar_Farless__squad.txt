A '''squad''' served in the [[Imperial Military|military]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and served under [[Captain]] [[A'Shar Farless]] at the [[Imperial Center of Military Research|Imperial security complex]] on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]]. The squad consisted of [[stormtrooper]]s, [[coastal defender stormtrooper]]s, [[Imperial officer]]s, and [[Imperial ground crew]]men. [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] assigned the squad to Farless after Tarkin was impressed in his ability to inspire actions in his troops. When Captain Farless met his new squad he gave them an inspiring speech about fighting together.<ref name="Goodbye" />
