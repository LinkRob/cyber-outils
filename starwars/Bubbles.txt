'''Bubbles''' was a [[rathtar]] that [[Valon|lived]] in the [[Lonisa City Zoo]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. By [[230 BBY]], Bubbles became injured and broke out of the [[zoo]], encountering the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kildo]], [[Gavi Takitaken]], and [[Tep Tep]]. After Tep Tep [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to [[Taming beasts|calm]] Bubbles down as the [[Jedi]] healed its injuries, she brought the rathtar back with her to the [[Valo Temple|Valo Jedi temple]].

While Bubbles was in the [[Jedi temple|temple]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Eicho]] saw the rathtar and passed out, and it attempted to [[Diet|eat]] him. After the incident, Eicho spoke harshly to the [[youngling]] about bringing Bubbles to the temple and told her that she could not keep it. In [[229 BBY]], Kildo and Tep Tep told Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] about Bubbles.

==Biography==

[[File:TepTep_HRConcept.png|thumb|right|250px|Tep Tep (pictured) calmed Bubbles down with the Force and brought it back to the Valo Jedi temple with her.]]
The [[rathtar]] Bubbles [[Valon|lived]] in the [[Lonisa City Zoo]] within [[Lonisa City]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> At one point by [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Escape from Valo date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. The novel establishes that the [[attack on Valo]]&mdash;in which all of the [[Valo Temple]]'s [[Jedi Master]]s, save for [[Kunpar Vasivola]], [[Death|died]] on [[Valo]]&mdash;took place one year before, meaning [[Eicho]] must have passed away in [[230 BBY]]. Therefore, the incident with Bubbles must have taken place by that year.</ref> Bubbles became injured and broke out of the [[zoo]]. After breaking out of the zoo, the rathtar encountered three [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]]: the [[Alcedian]]s [[Kildo]] and [[Tep Tep]], and the [[human]]<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> [[Gavi Takitaken]].<ref name="Alyssa Wong">{{DarkHorse|url=Blog/4284/alyssa-wong-returns-to-star-wars-the-high-republic|text=ALYSSA WONG RETURNS TO "STAR WARS: THE HIGH REPUBLIC ADVENTURES" FOR THE BATTLE OF A LIFETIME!}}</ref> As the [[youngling]]s healed its injuries, Tep Tep<ref name="Escape from Valo" />&mdash;who had a special [[Taming beasts|Force connection]] to animals<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref>&mdash;[[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to calm Bubbles down. Once the rathtar was healed, Tep Tep brought Bubbles back with her to the [[Valo Temple|Valo Jedi temple]], though the rathtar was barely able to fit through the [[Jedi temple|temple's]] door as a result of its size.<ref name="Escape from Valo" />

While in the Jedi temple, Bubbles was seen by [[Jedi Master]] [[Eicho]] who went into shock and passed out. The rathtar tried to [[Diet|eat]] the [[Jedi]], but ultimately did not eat Eicho. After the incident, the Master spoke harshly to Tep Tep about bringing Bubbles to the temple and refused to let her keep the rathtar. As Eicho recovered from his shock, he canceled his [[meditation]] [[Jedi training|lessons]] with the three younglings for two [[Standard week|weeks]]. In retaliation to how the Master had spoken to his friend about the incident with Bubbles, Kildo put a [[spotted spider]] in Eicho's bag when he returned to the temple after his recovery, causing the Master to have to leave the temple to recover again.<ref name="Escape from Valo" />

In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Escape from Valo date" /> when the three younglings introduced themselves to Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]], Tep Tep and Kildo recalled the incident with Bubbles and Eicho, which Kildo felt had been hilarious. Tep Tep stated that she would not have let the rathtar eat the Jedi Master, to which Kildo noted that Eicho had not know that.<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Beware the Nameless date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}} Since ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'' takes place one year and six [[Standard month|months]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon, its events must be set in 228 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] as well.</ref> when [[Living Force|Force]]-consuming [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures were set loose on the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Elite-class vessel|''Elite''-class vessel]] ''[[Innovator]]'', Tep Tep suggested that she could try talking to them. In response, Jomaram noted the creatures were not a rathtar, reminding her<ref name="Beware the Nameless">''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''</ref> of Bubbles,<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> and that the Nameless only destroyed.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|This is Tep Tep. She's great with creatures of all kinds, especially the big carnivorous ones.''"<br />"''They're just so cute.|Kildo and Tep Tep, to Ram Jomaram|<ref name="Escape from Valo" />}}
Bubbles stood six [[meter]]s tall, barely being able to fit through the Valo Jedi temple's door due to its size. When the rathtar came across Jedi Master Eicho, Bubbles tried to eat him. While a member of a carnivorous<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> [[species]],<ref name="Bestiary">''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]''</ref> Tep Tep considered Bubbles cute.<ref name="Escape from Valo" />
