'''Cargin Eimalgan''' was one of the earliest ace [[Rebel pilot|pilots]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps|Starfighter Corps]]. By the time of the foundation of the [[New Republic]], Eimalgan had [[Death|died]]. As a tribute to the late ace, the flight maneuver called "roll-off-the-top" was also known as the "[[Eimalgan Turn]]."<ref name="LD" />
