The '''Almania system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was located in grid square S-5 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], situated in the [[Almanian sector]], which was a subsector of the [[Mortex sector]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Almania system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] boxed set for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> The system was later identified as the "Almania system" in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]],"<ref name="Appendix" /> an appendix accompanying the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]], an article on [[StarWars.com]] featuring a map of the galaxy.<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]] and posted on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Fry">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map...|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Almanian system]] was introduced in ''[[The New Rebellion]]'', a [[1996]] novel authored by [[Kristine Kathryn Rusch]].<ref name="New Rebellion">''[[The New Rebellion]]''</ref> It was later identified as the Almania system in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia]]'', written by [[Stephen J. Sansweet]]<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia]]''</ref> and published on [[June 30]], [[1998]].<ref name="San Diego Comic-Con Exclusives">{{ThisWeek|-BZX-WxjhFE|San Diego Comic-Con Exclusives, Brand New LEGO Art, and More!|sw_url=video/san-diego-comic-con-exclusives-brand-new-lego-art-and-more|date=2020-07-02}}</ref> The Almanian system was later given its first visual depiction in ''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]'', a reference book authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and illustrated by [[Brandon McKinney]] and [[Scott Kolins]]<ref name="Planets">''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]''</ref> that was released in [[July]] of the same year.<ref name="Amazon">{{RandomHouseOld|url=delrey/starwars/catalog/display.pperl?isbn=9780345420688|text=Star Wars: The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons}}</ref>
