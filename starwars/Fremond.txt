The '''''Fremond''''' was a [[spacetug]] used by the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> The [[starship]] shared its name with the [[Fremond system]], located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the ''Fremond'' was part of the [[Sentinel Base's flotilla|flotilla]] belonging to the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] facility known as [[Sentinel Base]], which was [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and oversaw the construction of the Imperial [[battle station]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> later known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Rogue One guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

At that [[time]], when a fake transmission claimed that the nearby [[Rampart Station]] was under [[Attack on Sentinel Base|attack]] from a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]], both the ''Fremond'' and the [[frigate]] [[Salliche (frigate)|''Salliche'']] were vacant and parked in stationary [[orbit]]s over the [[Sentinel moon|moon]] Sentinel Base was situated on. When read a list of the base's starships by a [[Unidentified comm specialist|comm specialist]], Tarkin chose Sentinel's other available vessel, the [[Space warfare|combat]]-ready [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Electrum (Venator-class)|''Electrum'']], to react to the attack.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Fremond'' was mentioned in the novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], which was released in [[2014]] and written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Tarkin" />
