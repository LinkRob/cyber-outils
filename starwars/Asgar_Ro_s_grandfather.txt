An [[Evereni]] [[Gender|male]] was the '''grandfather''' of the Evereni [[Asgar Ro]] and lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. They were a member of the [[Nihil]]<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" /> [[pirate]]s.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The part of [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]] which mentions [[Asgar Ro]]'s [[Parent|grandfather]] takes place twenty [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[232 BBY]].</ref> to ensure that [[the galaxy]] continued to fear the Evereni [[Species|people]], Asgar's [[Ro family|grandfather]] left the family and Nihil.<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Asgar was present aboard the Nihil<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" /> [[flagship]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' and argued with his [[Parent|mother]], [[Shalla Ro]], about their differing plans for the Nihil. Shalla suggested that her son leave like his [[Asgar Ro's father|father]] and grandfather to help ensure the [[galaxy]] feared their people. After she told Asgar to make some [[Death|kills]], he replied that it was a good idea and killed her by pushing her over a railing.<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Asgar Ro's grandfather was mentioned in flashbacks in the [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|first issue]] of the [[2022]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Eye of the Storm]]'' released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]]. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 12]], 2022.<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
