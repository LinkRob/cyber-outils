The '''Daalang system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] at the [[Standard Galactic Grid|coordinates]] Q-12,<ref name="Appendix" /> containing the astronomical object [[Daalang]].<ref name="Where" /> It was located in the [[Daalang sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> at the nexus of the [[Gamor Run]],<ref name="Gamor" /> the [[Trax Tube]],<ref name="Trax" /> and a [[Bothawui–Daalang hyperlane|hyperlane]] extending south to [[Bothawui]].<ref name="Map" />

Following the [[Battle of Yavin]], an [[Imperial Navy]] [[Immobilizer 418 cruiser]] [[Skirmish in the Daalang system|trapped]] the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] ship ''[[Desert Jewel]]'' in that system. Rebel operatives [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Nakari Kelen]], and [[R2-D2]] were traveling aboard the ''Desert Jewel'' with the rescued [[Givin]] cryptologist [[Drusil Bephorin]]. Following a brief dogfight, Skywalker destroyed the Immobilizer 418 cruiser with a [[Utheel Rockcrusher Compact Seismic Charge]].<ref name="Heir to the Jedi">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref>
