'''FN-9330''' was a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> [[Gender|male]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who served in the [[First Order]] during their [[Cold War|conflict]] with the [[Resistance]]. Shortly before [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], [[FN Trooper Corps|FN]]-9330 was stationed at the [[Starkiller Base|base]]. As the [[trooper]] was boarding the [[transport]] compartment, the doors opened, revealing [[Han Solo]], [[Finn]], and [[Chewbacca]] in front of him. He had just enough time to exclaim his surprise before being shot point-blank with the [[Wookiee]]'s [[bowcaster]], blasting the trooper several feet backwards and [[Death|killing]] him. Chewbacca then moved his body out of sight to avoid detection at the base.<ref name="TFA" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|And now we meet this stormtrooper, played by my friend Nigel Godrich. He's er, the producer of Radiohead, and he was an excellent stormtrooper for point four seconds.|Director [[J.J. FN-9330 was played by [[Nigel Godrich]] in the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="TFA" /> Godrich, a well-known record producer, landed the role with the help of British filmmaker [[Wikipedia:Edgar Wright|Edgar Wright]] (friend and frequent collaborator of [[Simon Pegg]]).<ref name="Radiohead">{{WebCite|url=http://www.nme.com/news/music/radiohead-84-1201641|text=Radiohead producer Nigel Godrich plays a Stormtrooper in 'Star Wars: The Force Awakens'|archivedate=20171213204714}}</ref> The specific stormtrooper he played was discovered when [[Matthew Wood]] posted the credits for every small voiceover line in the film.<ref name="SW-From Blast That X-wing! to Traitor!: The Voices of Star Wars: The Force Awakens">{{SW|url=news/from-blast-that-x-wing-to-traitor-the-voices-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=From "Blast That X-wing!" to "Traitor!": The Voices of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''}}</ref>
