The '''Emperor's personal shuttle''',<ref name="S&V6">{{ShipsandVehiclesCite|6|Starships and Vehicles}}</ref> or simply the '''Emperor's shuttle''',<ref name="The Emperor's Shuttle">{{FFGTCG|set=Technological Terror|cardname=The Emperor's Shuttle|link=opposition-cycle/technological-terror/the-emperor%e2%80%99s-shuttle-r1924}}</ref> was a shuttle used by [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. Heavily modified, the [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] featured a [[cloaking device]] that rendered it invisible to all known forms of energy.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> Staffed with [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guards]] and heavily armed with five sets of [[laser cannon]]s,<ref name="AB Update" /> the [[transport]] was easily identified by a [[Color|grey]] stripe that ran down the front of its beak-like [[cockpit]].<ref name="Encyclopedia Starfighters" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[Emperor]] used his shuttle to reach the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] in [[orbit]] over [[Endor]].<ref name="Episode VI" />
