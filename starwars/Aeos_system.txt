The '''Aeos system''' was a [[star system]]<ref name="No Place Safe">{{Resistance|No Place Safe}}</ref> located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It contained the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Aeos Prime]], [[homeworld]] of the [[Aeosian]] [[species]].<ref name="The New World" /> During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the ''[[Colossus]]'' traveled to Aeos Prime with the intent of settling there, but came into conflict with the Aeosians. The Aeosians agreed to let them stay, but the [[First Order]] discovered their presence and [[Battle of Aeos Prime|chased]] the ''Colossus'' away,<ref name="No Place Safe" /> later returning to [[Orbital bombardment|orbitally bombard]] the Aeosian population that had harbored the [[Resistance]] cell.<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref>
