'''''Chalice and Altar''''' was the best-known work of the [[opera]] composer [[Igern]]. It dealt with the morality of self-sacrifice and the repression of desire. Study of that opera was included in the [[Core Worlds Classical Culture]] class of the [[Royal Imperial Academy]] on [[Coruscant]]. At one point, [[cadet]] [[Ciena Ree]] resolved to listen to opera every night leading up to the Culture exam.<ref name="Lost Stars" />
