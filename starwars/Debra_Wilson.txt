'''Debra Wilson''' (born [[April 26]], [[1960s#1962|1962]])<ref name="New Cassian Andor Series" /> is an actress who portrayed [[Cere Junda]] in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order panel">{{SWYouTube|5H6LQkxwVxc|The Galaxy-Wide Premiere of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order Live Panel}}</ref> and ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="SWJS">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

In [[2019]], she voiced [[Nambi Ghima]] in {{Film|IX}}.<ref name="Episode 9">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
