{{Quote|Some travelers are surprised to find a cloud of smoke rolling out of the Carbon Freeze, which is far more enjoyable than actually being frozen in carbonite and comes with none of the carbon sickness afterward.|Eloc Throno, ''Traveler's Guide to Batuu''|<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>}}
'''Carbon Freeze''' was a [[fruit]]y, [[Color|yellow]], and non-[[alcohol]]ic [[Foodstuff|beverage]] that contained [[worrt]] [[egg]]s and shared its name with the process of [[carbon-freezing]]. [[Oga's Cantina]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]] served it in [[34 ABY]]. During that [[Standard year|year]], the [[mercenary]] [[Bazine Netal]] declined the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s recommendation of the drink while the two were having a discussion. On another occasion, the [[Batuuan]] [[human]] [[Julen Rakab]] drank a Carbon Freeze during a conversation with the [[smuggler]] [[Izal Garsea]] about leaving the planet.

==Description==
{{Quote|I usually try to keep my distance to avoid gettin' sucked into any unnecessary drama. But with potent drinks like the Carbon Freeze, the Fuzzy Tauntaun, and the Bloody Rancor on her menu, drama usually manages to find me on its own.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />}}
The Carbon Freeze was a [[fruit]]y, [[Color|yellow]], non-[[alcohol]]ic [[Foodstuff|beverage]] that could be found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. The drink released clouds of [[Water|vapor]], agitating the green spherical<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> [[egg]]s<ref name="GEStaff" /> within it that were laid by a [[Tatooine|Tatooinian]] [[worrt]] living in a tank above the [[cantina]]'s bar.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==History==
{{Quote|Are you sure you won't have that drink? The Fuzzy Tauntaun? Or maybe a Carbon Freeze? No? Well, then, settle back and prepare to be wowed as the great Hondo Ohnaka tells you a story.|Hondo Ohnaka suggests Bazine Netal drink a Carbon Freeze|<ref name="Pirate's Price" />}}
[[File:StronoCookieTuggs.png|thumb|left|200px|Strono Tuggs commented on the Carbon Freeze in ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''.]]
The beverage was served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on Batuu<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events of [[Oga's Cantina]] within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> In 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="PP date">The framing story of ''[[Pirate's Price]]'' occurs between the [[Battle of Takodana]] and the [[Battle of Batuu]], both of which occurred in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[human]] [[mercenary]] [[Bazine Netal]] visited Oga's Cantina to question the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] during her search for the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', a [[YT-1300 light freighter]]. Before beginning his first story about the [[Starship|ship]], Ohnaka suggested that Netal order a Carbon Freeze or a [[Fuzzy Tauntaun]], but she refused.<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> In the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="ACOF date">The epilogue of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' is set during the [[First Order]] occupation of [[Batuu]] and the [[Battle of Batuu]], both of which occurred in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Batuuan]] human [[Julen Rakab]] drank a Carbon Freeze at Oga's Cantina while he and [[Izal Garsea]] discussed Rakab's readiness to leave Batuu.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

In his<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> 34 ABY<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> [[book]] ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', [[historian]] and [[author]] [[Eloc Throno]] described the Carbon Freeze as more enjoyable than being [[Carbon-freezing|frozen]] in [[carbonite]], a process with which it shared its name, and not causing [[Hibernation sickness|carbon sickness]],<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> a resultant condition of being frozen in carbonite.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> [[Strono Tuggs]], [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] and author of ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'',<ref name="Cookbook" /> which was released the same year,<ref name="Cookbook Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> regarded the Carbon Freeze as a potent drink.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:SWPiratesPriceFinal.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Carbon Freeze was introduced via mention in ''Pirate's Price''.]]
Carbon Freeze was first mentioned in the junior novel ''[[Pirate's Price]]'', which was written by [[Lou Anders]]<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> and published on [[January 8]], [[2019]].<ref name="Pirate's Price date">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-pirates-price|text=Star Wars: Pirate's Price}}</ref> On [[February 27]] of that year, [[Wikipedia:D23 (Disney)|D23: The Official Disney Fan Club]]<ref name="D23 Everything You Need to Know About SWGE">{{D23|url=galaxys-edge-announcements|text=Everything You Need to Know About ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> and [[StarWars.com]] announced that the beverage, which was first pictured on StarWars.com, would be sold at the bar Oga's Cantina in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="From Blue Milk to Ronto Wraps">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-food-beverages|text=From Blue Milk to Ronto Wraps: The Food and Beverages of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> a themed land that opened at [[Disneyland]] Park on [[May 31]].<ref name="Foodie Guide">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The menu's description reads, "[[Wikipedia:Powerade|Powerade&reg;]] [[Wikipedia:Lemon|Lemon]] [[Wikipedia:Lime (fruit)|Lime]] and [[Wikipedia:Fragaria|Wild Strawberry]] with [[Wikipedia:Blueberry|Blueberry]] and [[Wikipedia:Apple|Green Apple]] [[Wikipedia:Tapioca balls|Popping Pearls]],"<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/lunch|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Lunch Menu}}</ref> with the popping pearls playing the part of worrt eggs.<ref name="Must-Try Drinks">{{DPB|url=2020/01/must-try-drinks-at-ogas-cantina-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Must-Try Drinks at Oga's Cantina in ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref>

The [[2020]] [[Canon|non-canon]] video game expansion ''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]'' includes Carbon Freezes as drinks sold to the player's [[Wikipedia:The Sims|Sims]] at the Oga's Cantina vendor in the Black Spire Outpost neighborhood. Upon consuming the drink for the first time, the Sim automatically learns the recipe and can prepare it at mixology bars in their home neighborhood.<ref name="Sims">''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]''</ref>
