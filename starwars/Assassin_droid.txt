'''Assassin droids''',<ref name="SWCT">[[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]]</ref> also known as '''war droids''',<ref name="Archive IG-88">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|subset=Archive|pack=IG-88|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/IG-88-The-Black-Series-Archive-Star-Wars-Hasbro.asp}}</ref> or as a '''murder droid''',<ref name="Insider 234">{{InsiderCite|234|Galactic Tales: The Crimson Corsair and the Sinister Secret of the Binary Star}}</ref> were a type of [[droid]] built specifically for the purpose of [[assassin]]ation. They were independently programmed, answering to no masters,<ref name="SWCT" /> but could be programmed to serve one or more masters.<ref name="Vader3">[[Darth Vader (2015) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 3]]</ref> Assassin droids like the [[IG-RM bodyguard and enforcer droid]]s and the [[HK-87 assassin droid]]s also served as [[bodyguard]]s and [[Assassin|enforcers]] for their masters.<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref><ref name="The Path of Anger">{{TOTE|The Path of Anger}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:HK87-TMCh13.png|thumb|left|200px|A HK-87 assassin droid]]
The [[SD-K4 assassin droid]],<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> was a model of assassin droid manufactured by [[Baktoid Combat Automata]]<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]] and used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Separatist Assassin Droid">{{Databank|separatist-assassin-droid|Separatist Assassin Droid}}</ref> They were later used by both the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Commander" /> The [[Czerka Arms|Czerka corporation]] produced the [[HK-87 assassin droid]]<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> based on an antiquated design.<ref name="HK-87 Black Series">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|packtype=''Star Wars: The Mandalorian'' Figure|pack=#29: HK-87|altlink=https://galacticfigures.com/figureDetails.aspx?id=29298}}</ref>

The company [[Holowan Laboratories]] produced a model of assassin droid known as the [[IG-86 sentinel droid]], which were used as enforcers by criminal elements.<ref name="RVG">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> Other assassin droids included the [[BT-1 assassin droid]]s and [[0-0-0]] produced by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Tarkin Initiative]],<ref name="Vader3" /> and the droid [[bounty hunter]]s [[IG-88B|IG-88]],<ref name="IG88DB">{{Databank|ig-88|IG-88}}</ref> [[IG-90]],<ref name="Darth Vader 7">[[Darth Vader (2015) 7|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 7]]</ref> and [[IG-11]].<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> The [[Imperial Security Bureau]] and [[Imperial Army Intelligence]] utilized the [[E522 assassin droid]] for use of black op missions. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Intelligence]] also later used it.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks">''[[Cyphers and Masks]]''</ref>
