The '''Duel on the ''Executor''''' was a [[Lightsaber duel/Legends|lightsaber duel]] on [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader's]] [[Flagship/Legends|flagship]], [[Executor/Legends|''Executor'']]. The conflict took place after Vader brought the [[Droid/Legends|droids]] [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] aboard the ship. The Dark Lord attempted to turn R2 to the dark side, but the droid refused, drawing a [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]]. A short duel ensued with R2 using [[Rocket booster/Legends|rocket boosters]] to maneuver around the Sith Lord. The astromech gained the upper hand, knocking the lightsaber out of Darth Vader's hand and then cutting the control box off of his [[Darth Vader's armor/Legends|armor]]. Badly crippled and unable to breathe, Vader was caught off guard when C-3PO backed up into him and knocked him into a [[Carbon-freezing chamber/Legends|carbon-freezing chamber]]. The Dark Lord of the Sith was frozen in [[Carbonite/Legends|carbonite]] to the excitement of the two droids.
