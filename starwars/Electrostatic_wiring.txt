'''Electrostatic wiring''' was a type of [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] implant that acted as artificial synapses, and could replace the entire nervous system by interfacing with cybernetic ports. [[Beilert Valance]], a [[cyborg]] [[bounty hunter]], had his entire nervous system replaced with electrostatic wiring.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3">[[Bounty Hunters 3|''Bounty Hunters'' 3]]</ref>
