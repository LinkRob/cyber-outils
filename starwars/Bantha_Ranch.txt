'''Bantha Ranch''' was a [[farm|ranch]] located just south west on the [[Mos Eisley Outskirts]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Hutt Clan]] controlled the ranch.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bantha Ranch appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
