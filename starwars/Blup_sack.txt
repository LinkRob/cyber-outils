A '''blup sack''' was part of an [[ecorb]]s' anatomy. It was red-[[Color/Legends|colored]] and pulsated rhythmically when the owner was happy.<ref name="MAGL">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Blup sacks appeared in a story in ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a book published in [[1993]].<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]].<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>

Blup sacks appear similar to real-world [[Wikipedia:Gular skin|gular sacs]] featured in animals such as the [[Wikipedia:Great Frigatebird|Great Frigatebird]].
