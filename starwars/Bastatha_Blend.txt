'''Bastatha Blend''' was a [[Temperature|chilled]] [[vegetable]] [[soup]] [[Diet|edible]] by [[Artiodac]]s that the locals of the [[planet]] [[Bastatha]] made, cooling it in their deepest [[cave]]rns. During a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="Cookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited the planet and tried the soup, which he found refreshing compared to the scorching temperatures even underneath the surface of the world. He then included a recipe for the soup and praised it in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="Cookbook" /> published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Date" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bastatha Blend was introduced in the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Jenn Fujikawa]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] ingredients for the recipe are [[Wikipedia:Tomato|tomatoes]], a [[Wikipedia:Shallot|shallot]], a [[Wikipedia:Cucumber|cucumber]], a red [[Wikipedia:Bell pepper|bell pepper]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], [[Wikipedia:Basil|basil]], [[Wikipedia:Oregano|oregano]], [[Wikipedia:Cumin|cumin]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], [[Wikipedia:Balsamic vinegar|balsamic vinegar]], and [[Wikipedia:Coriander|cilantro]].<ref name="Cookbook" />
