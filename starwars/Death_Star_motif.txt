The '''Death Star motif''' is a piece written by [[John Williams]] for the [[Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope (soundtrack)|A New Hope soundtrack]]. It represents the [[DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station|Death Star]]. It was performed by the [[London Symphony Orchestra]] in [[1977]]. The motif is also played in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Return of the Jedi'']] in representing the threat of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]].

In the [[1995]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars: Dark Forces]]'', the motif is played when [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] first appears. It is also played when [[Phase II dark trooper]]s are deployed to [[Talay/Legends|Talay]].

In the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series episode "[[Cat and Mouse]]," a rendition of the motif is played as [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] reveals the [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette]] to [[Anakin Skywalker]].

The motif reappeared in ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'', where it plays over a [[hologram]] of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. The same motif is also played in "[[Zander's Joyride]]," when [[Zander Freemaker]] encounters and is captured by an Imperial Star Destroyer, as well as when Freemaker is approached by an [[IT-O Interrogation Unit]].

The motif is played in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', as [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] and [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Krennic]] observe the completion of the Death Star's [[superlaser]]. The theme also appears in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' encounters an Imperial [[Star Destroyer]] while fleeing from [[Kessel]] ("Reminiscence Therapy"). As an iron is visually portayed as a large [[starship]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''The Last Jedi'']], the Death Star motif is played. When it is shown that the "ship" is simply an iron, a light rendition of the [[Star Wars Main Title|main theme]] is played following the motif.
