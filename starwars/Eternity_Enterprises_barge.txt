The '''Eternity Enterprises barge''' was a [[Sail barge|pleasure barge]] operated by the tourism company [[Eternity Enterprises]]. When the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] needed to retrieve a shard of the [[Kyber mirror of Shinsui Temple]] from the [[Seikara Caverns]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dekien]], he accessed the [[cave]]rns by taking a tour on the Eternity Enterprises barge.<ref name="Ronin" />
