'''Clone scout troopers''', also known as '''clone scouts''', were specialized [[clone trooper]]s that served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Trained for reconnaissance and warfare over difficult terrain, a [[battalion]] of [[scout]] [[trooper]]s deployed to the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] during the final [[Standard day|days]] of the conflict, and fought alongside the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Wookiee]]s against the [[Separatist Droid Army|droid armies]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].

==History==
===Clone Wars===
[[File:InfantrySupportPlatform-2025RepUnit.png|thumb|left|270px|Clone scout troopers piloting an Infantry Support Platform.]]
[[Cloning|Clone]] [[scout]] [[trooper]]s were a variant of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[clone trooper]], trained in reconnaissance and warfare over difficult or harsh terrain.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Before larger [[troop]] actions, small groups of the scouts were deployed to survey territory, and, because of their survival skills, they were suited for fighting in wilderness conditions.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> They were also trained to pilot vehicles such as the [[Infantry Support Platform]].<ref name="Legion">{{Asmodee|subdomain=cdn.svc|domain=net|url=production-amgcom/uploads/2025/04/DOC13_GalacticRepublic_Units.pdf|text=''Star Wars: Legion'' Galactic Republic Unit Cards (PDF)}}</ref>

During the final [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[41st Scout Battalion]]&mdash;led by [[Jedi General]]s [[Yoda]] and [[Luminara Unduli]], as well as [[Clone Commander]] [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]]&mdash;was deployed to the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] to aid the [[Wookiee]]s in their fight against the [[Separatist Droid Army]]. As [[Confederacy military|Confederate forces]] stormed the [[beach]], scout troopers were tasked with defending [[Kachirho]], the [[capital]] of Kashyyyk. Using barricades and trenches as cover, the scout troopers and Wookiees engaged the [[battle droid]]s in a brutal confrontation. A number of scout troopers acted as sharpshooters along [[Tree Kachirho]] whilst many more lined the top of the seawall.<ref name="ROTS" />

During the battle, [[Commander]] Gree received [[Order 66]] from [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and proceeded to carry out the order. He approached [[Grand Master]] Yoda and the Wookiee [[general]]s, atop a high command post overlooking the battle, and prepared to [[fire]] on his [[commanding officer]]. However, the [[Jedi]] felt a great [[disturbance in the Force]], as Order 66 was carried out across the [[the galaxy]], and beheaded Gree and [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Jek]] before they could [[Death|execute]] him.<ref name="ROTS" />

===Legacy===
[[File:Scout-and-Magna.png|thumb|right|270px|A clone scout trooper is attacked by an IG-100 MagnaGuard Droid on Kashyyyk.]]
After the [[Galactic Empire]] was [[Proclamation of the New Order|established]], the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] transitioned into the [[Imperial Army]] and [[Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="Stormtrooper DB">{{Databank|stormtroopers|Stormtroopers}}</ref> Like the Republic, the Empire made use of its own [[scout trooper]]s, who saw action throughout the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

==Equipment==
Clone scout troopers were equipped with camouflaged<ref name="CVD" /> [[Phase II ARF armor]]<ref name="BFII Info">{{EA|subdomain=battlefront-forums|url=discussion/137644/community-transmission-where-are-those-droidekas|text=Community Transmission &ndash; Where Are Those Droidekas}}</ref> and were trained for reconnaissance.<ref name="databank clone armor">{{Databank|clone-trooper-armor-history-gallery|Clone Trooper Armor History Gallery}}</ref> Scout troopers also possessed survival gear and had [[macrobinoculars]] built into their [[helmet]].<ref name="CVD" />
