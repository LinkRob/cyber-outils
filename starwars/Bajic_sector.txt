{{Quote|We've also determined that the warship was assembled at shipyards in the Bajic sector, jointly owned and operated by the Tenloss Syndicate and lower-level members of the Crymorah Syndicate.|Tarkin briefs Darth Vader and Emperor Palpatine on a dissident warship|<ref name="TRK" />}}
The '''Bajic sector''' was a [[sector]] of [[the galaxy]]. During the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]], a shipyard within the sector was co-operated by the criminal [[Tenloss Syndicate|Tenloss]] and [[Crymorah Syndicate]]s and there a ship was constructed for use by dissident forces against the Empire. The ship was constructed from the stolen components of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] warships destroyed during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. After the ship had been destroyed in an [[Battle in the Nouane system|attack]] on a Imperial convoy, the Empire discovered its origin within the Bajic sector, and [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] informed [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] of the information.<ref name="TRK" />
