"'''Be the Jedi in Three New LucasArts Games'''" is an article by [[Jack Lyon]] published in [[Star Wars Insider 36|''Star Wars Insider'' 36]]. It includes previews and developer interviews for three ''Star Wars'' video games: ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]'', [[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']], and ''[[Star Wars: Masters of Teräs Käsi]]''.
