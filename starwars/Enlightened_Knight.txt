The '''Enlightened Knight''' was a bodily configuration equipped by the [[droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[J-3DI]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Noble Jedi|standard configuration]]. It featured simulated [[blaster]] scorch marks on the faux [[robe]]s that made of this costume's outer shell.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Enlightened Knight was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter J-3DI, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.18.0 during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Enlightened Saber Design]].<ref name="Hunters" />
