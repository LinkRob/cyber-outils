'''Condensed nerf milk''' was a condensed form of the [[Color|white]] [[Nerf-milk|milk]] of the [[nerf]],<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> a domesticated creature [[Homeworld|from]] the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Core Worlds, Colonies, the Inner Rim and Expansion Region}}</ref> Residents of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Etai]] used the condensed milk as a topping for [[jybbuk slush]], completing the sweet beverage with a traditional [[fruit]] [[Foodstuff|garnish]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

To create the beverage known as [[Spiced Jybbuk Juice]], the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] swapped out the fruit garnish in the Etaan recipe for [[candied hot pepper]]s that balanced out the sweetness with heat.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs included the recipe for Spiced Jybbuk Juice in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', following a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Condensed nerf milk was created for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for Spiced Jybbuk Juice uses [[Wikipedia:Condensed milk|condensed cow's milk]] to represent condensed nerf milk.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
