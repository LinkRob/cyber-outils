'''Deborah Colvin Borgo''' is an artist who illustrated several ''[[Star Wars]]'' books for young readers including ''[[Fuzzy as an Ewok]]'', ''[[The Red Ghost: An Ewok Adventure]]'', ''[[The Shadow Stone: An Ewok Adventure]]'', and ''[[Wicket and the Dandelion Warriors: An Ewok Adventure]]''.
