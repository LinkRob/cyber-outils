The '''''Broken Wing''''' was a [[starship]] [[pilot]]ed by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Valance utilized the ''Broken Wing'' when he was hired by the [[Hidden Hand]] criminal syndicate to lead a [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] on a [[Hunt for Vader|hunt]] for the Imperial enforcer [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The vessel was shot down during a skirmish with Vader, but was retrieved and repaired by the Empire, who ordered Valance to work for them. After leading Imperial forces to the [[Hidden Hand asteroid base|base]] of the Hidden Hand, Valance escaped in the ''Broken Wing'', returning to work as a bounty hunter.

At the request of his old associate and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] member [[Han Solo]], Valance used the ''Broken Wing'' to [[Mission to Home One|transport]] the [[engineer]] [[Vharn]], whose intent to betray the Rebellion led Valance into an Imperial [[blockade]]. Valance [[Death|killed]] Vharn, delivering the schematics the engineer had devised to the rebel rendezvous point. At some point, Valance was joined aboard the ''Broken Wing'' by the [[droid]] [[Nine-Four-El]], who assisted him in his missions. Valance used the ''Broken Wing'' to travel to various destinations while searching for his former mentor [[Nakano Lash]], who became a target of numerous hunters after she emerged from hiding.

After Valance found Lash, she tasked him with the protection of the young [[Cadeliah]], the daughter of two heirs of rival criminal syndicates. Aboard the ''Broken Wing'', Valance and Cadeliah were pursued by other hunters sent after them by the [[Unbroken Clan]]. Valance left his ward in the care of his [[Yuralla Vega|former lover]], travelling alone on the ''Broken Wing'' to draw away their pursuers. In a skirmish with [[pirate]]s of the [[Ohnaka Gang]] while attempting to rescue the rebel [[transport]] ''[[Spirit of Jedha]]'' at the [[Terminus]] [[Hyperspace route|hyperlane]] intersection, the ''Broken Wing'' came under heavy fire, and Valance was forced to eject its central module in order to escape.

==Description==
{{Quote|Damn it, that droid should've been back with the ''Broken Wing'' already. Could use that ship's ''firepower'' right about now.|Beilert Valance|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3">[[Bounty Hunters 3|''Bounty Hunters'' 3]]</ref>}}
A modified [[YV-580 light freighter]]<ref name="CGttSWG" /> that was equipped with a [[hyperdrive]],<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> the ''Broken Wing'' had a design centered around a circular body with two rectangular arms extending to the front. The [[cockpit]] was situated on the front edge of the circular body, between the two arms. The rear of the [[starship]] housed its two [[engine]]s, while two wings protruded from either side.<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> One [[laser cannon]] was mounted on each of its wings,<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> and a pair of laser cannons were located on the front of the ship. The ''Broken Wing'' also had separate cannons on each side, which could be controlled manually by a singular [[gunner]]. The ''Broken Wing''{{'s}} central body had a port on either side<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> that could be used to board other vessels.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4">[[Bounty Hunters 4|''Bounty Hunters'' 4]]</ref>

[[File:BrokenWingHeva-TargetVader3.jpg|thumb|left|320px|The ''Broken Wing'' during Valance's hunt for Darth Vader]]
Able to be flown by a single [[pilot]], the ''Broken Wing''{{'s}} cockpit initially only held one seat,<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> though a second was later added.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" /> When the ''Broken Wing'' landed, four landing gear legs and a boarding ramp could be extended from its base. The vessel was equipped with a device that the [[slicer]] [[Chio Fain]] assumed was a customized [[Jammer|communications jammer]] made by [[Grenyt]], though it could also be used to amplify communications.<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> The cockpit of the ''Broken Wing'' contained a [[comm unit]] that provided communication via [[hologram]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 8">[[Bounty Hunters 8|''Bounty Hunters'' 8]]</ref>

The starship underwent several modifications over [[time]]; a third engine unit was added between the two existing engines, and an [[Color|orange]] stripe was painted at the rear of the ship,<ref name="Empire Ascendant 1" /> though was later no longer present.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> The raised central module of the ''Broken Wing'', including the cockpit and middle engine, could be ejected from the ship like an [[escape pod]], leaving the remainder of the cruiser split in two.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 9">[[Bounty Hunters 9|''Bounty Hunters'' 9]]</ref>

==History==
===Starship of a hunter===
{{Quote|I think we lost them.''"<br />"''For now, thanks to the ''Broken Wing''. We ''both'' know how the Empire thinks, Valance. They won't just give up looking.|Beilert Valance and Cavic|<ref name="Target Vader 6">[[Target Vader 6|''Target Vader'' 6]]</ref>}}
[[File:BrokenWingCavic-TargetVader6.jpg|thumb|right|310px|Cavic pilots the ''Broken Wing'' through the Outer Rim.]]
[[Beilert Valance]] acquired the ''Broken Wing''<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> sometime after being discharged from the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Target Vader 5">[[Target Vader 5|''Target Vader'' 5]]</ref> and becoming a [[bounty hunter]].<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> From the ''Broken Wing'', Valance tracked and [[Hunting|hunted]] his [[Bounty|quarries]].<ref name="ValanceDatabank">{{Databank|beilert-valance|Beilert Valance}}</ref>

Sometime during the Empire's [[Imperial Era|reign]], Valance and fellow bounty hunter [[Cavic]] escaped Imperial pursuit in the ''Broken Wing'', with Cavic piloting the starship. The two hunters managed to lose their pursuers in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], and Valance believed that they were safe, but Cavic insisted that the Empire would continue to search for them. Wishing to hide somewhere that the Empire could not reach them, Cavic piloted the ''Broken Wing'' through a nearby [[asteroid field]], ignoring Valance's concerns regarding the danger of doing so. Cavic landed the ship inside an [[Hidden Hand asteroid base|asteroid outpost]] which he had set up as a refuge from the Empire some time earlier.<ref name="Target Vader 6" />

===Hunt for Darth Vader===
====Skirmishes with the Empire====
{{Quote|Valance&hellip;those were Imperial ships! What are you doing?! Are you insane?!|Dengar, after Beilert Valance destroyed Imperial TIE fighters|<ref name="Target Vader 2" />}}
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Target Vader 1|''Target Vader'' 1]] to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Valance landed on [[Coruscant]], where he left the ''Broken Wing'' in [[Docking Bay 21]] while visiting [[BG-RT's Tavern and Spirits]] on [[Level 1313]]. After [[Death|killing]] a number of bounty hunters that he had allowed to track him to the [[cantina]], Valance returned to the ''Broken Wing'', but was followed by [[Gwi]],<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> the leader of the [[Hidden Hand]] crime syndicate,<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> who claimed he was a messenger. Gwi recruited Valance into a [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] that was dispatched to [[Hunt for Vader|hunt down]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the [[Sith apprentice]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. The crew, which included the bounty hunter [[Dengar]], the slicer Chio Fain, the [[Gamorrean]] tracker [[Honnah]], and a [[sniper]] by the name of Urrr'k,<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> who was truly the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] agent<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> Gita in disguise as a [[Tusken Raider]],<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> boarded the ''Broken Wing'' and began their hunt.<ref name="Target Vader 2" />

[[File:BrokenWingVSTIEs-TargetVader2.jpg|thumb|left|320px|The ''Broken Wing'' destroys several TIE fighters.]]
In [[Realspace|space]], Valance attacked and destroyed two Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], amplifying their communications signal so that Vader would be able to track them to [[Arvina]], where the crew visited an [[Fetya's Hollow|outpost]] of the Hidden Hand. Vader, arriving aboard a [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]], pursued the escaping ''Broken Wing'' in [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|his]] [[TIE Advanced x1]], but Valance managed to jump into [[hyperspace]] before Vader could catch him.<ref name="Target Vader 2" />

====Shot down over Heva====
{{Quote|The old ''Broken Wing'' is about to get a lot more ''broken''.|Dengar, watching the ''Broken Wing'' crash|<ref name="Target Vader 3" />}}
Having sliced into the data banks of the outpost, Valance's team traveled to<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> a Hidden Hand [[Heva Hidden Hand outpost|outpost]] on [[Heva]].<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> Informed of the crew's destination by<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> Dengar, who was secretly an Imperial informant on the team,<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> Vader followed the crew aboard the [[Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> ''[[Formidable]]''.<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> Valance attacked the Star Destroyer in the ''Broken Wing'', accompanied by [[bot-drone]]s piloted by Fain. The ''Formidable'' sent [[TIE series|TIEs]] to attack the ''Broken Wing'', but Valance managed to avoid their fire, destroying several [[Starfighter|fighters]] with the help of "Urrr'k," who was acting as the gunner of the ship's manual cannon.<ref name="Target Vader 3" />

[[File:BrokenWingCrash-TargetVader3.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The ''Broken Wing'' after crashing onto Heva]]
Vader attacked the ''Broken Wing'', damaging its cannon. On Valance's signal, Fain used the bot-drones to destroy the ''Formidable''{{'s}} [[Conning tower|command tower]] and cause it to crash. Vader continued to shoot the ''Broken Wing'', damaging its engines. The ''Broken Wing'' crashed onto Heva, and Vader boarded the cruiser, but was unable to catch Valance, who had escaped the wreckage.<ref name="Target Vader 3" />

====Recovered by the Empire====
{{Quote|My ship&hellip;? What the&mdash;|Beilert Valance, encountering the ''Broken Wing'' aboard the ''Vengeance''|<ref name="Target Vader 5" />}}
Valance was ultimately unsuccessful in killing Vader on Heva&mdash;Dengar revealed himself as an Imperial informant, and Valance was captured<ref name="Target Vader 4">[[Target Vader 4|''Target Vader'' 4]]</ref> and [[Prison|imprisoned]] aboard the Star Destroyer ''[[Vengeance]]''. The Empire retrieved the ''Broken Wing'', taking the cruiser aboard the ''Vengeance'' and repairing it. After escaping his prison cell disguised as a [[stormtrooper]], Valance encountered the ''Broken Wing'', and was confronted by Vader. Under orders from Vader, Valance flew the ''Broken Wing'' to his [[homeworld]] of [[Chorin]], where the Hidden Hand had originally began. Vader threatened the safety of Chorin's inhabitants, forcing Valance back into the Empire. After leaving Chorin, Valance visited a [[Rebel outpost (Lowik)|rebel outpost]] on<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> the [[jungle]] [[planet]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7">[[Bounty Hunters 7|''Bounty Hunters'' 7]]</ref> [[Lowik]], landing the ''Broken Wing'' in a nearby [[forest]].<ref name="Target Vader 5" />

[[File:BrokenWingEscape-TargetVader6.jpg|thumb|left|320px|Valance escapes the Hidden Hand base in the ''Broken Wing''.]]
After negotiating a deal with rebel operative [[Aeliar]], Valance flew the ''Broken Wing'' to the same [[asteroid]] outpost that Cavic had constructed [[Standard year|years]] prior, which had been converted into the main base of the Hidden Hand. Valance landed the starship on the surface of the asteroid, but was caught by members of the crime syndicate and taken to Gwi. However, Valance had led Vader to the outpost, and the Empire attacked the forces of the Hidden Hand. With Vader distracted, Valance returned to the ''Broken Wing'' and escaped the asteroid. Valance landed at the rebel outpost on Lowik, where the rebels had saved the inhabitants of Chorin, including<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> his former lover [[Yuralla Vega]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> from the Empire, leaving shortly afterward.<ref name="Target Vader 6" />

===Transporting a rebel===
{{Quote|There she is&hellip;the ''Broken Wing''! Get on board and punch in the hyperdrive coordinates for the rendezvous point while I fire her up.|Beilert Valance, to Vharn|<ref name="Empire Ascendant 1" />}}
Following the failed hunt for Vader,<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> Valance returned to taking jobs as a bounty hunter. Sometime after the hunt, Valance was contacted by [[Han Solo]], member of the Rebel Alliance<ref name="Empire Ascendant 1" /> who formerly served as an [[Imperial cadet]] alongside Valance.<ref name="Imperial Cadet 5">[[Han Solo - Imperial Cadet 5|''Han Solo - Imperial Cadet'' 5]]</ref> Valance landed the ''Broken Wing'' in [[Docking Bay 13]] on the [[moon]] [[Nar Shaddaa]], meeting with Solo, who requested that Valance discreetly [[Mission to Home One|transport]] the [[engineer]] [[Vharn]], designer of schematics for a [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]], to a rebel rendezvous. While taking Vharn back to his ship, Valance was attacked by Imperial forces, but managed to defeat them, boarding the ''Broken Wing'' with the rebel engineer.<ref name="Empire Ascendant 1" />

[[File:BrokenWingBlockade-EmpireAscendant1.jpg|thumb|right|310px|The ''Broken Wing'' exits hyperspace before an Imperial blockade.]]
Vharn gave Valance the coordinates of the rebel rendezvous point, but the ''Broken Wing'' emerged from hyperspace in front of an Imperial [[blockade]]&mdash;Vharn intended to betray the Rebellion in an attempt to ransom his family's freedom. A number of TIE fighters attacked the ''Broken Wing'', and Valance, who had been given the true coordinates by Solo, destroyed a number of the fighters, avoiding destruction long enough to make the hyperspace jump to the correct rendezvous point. Valance killed Vharn to prevent the traitor from revealing any information to the Empire, and landed the ''Broken Wing'' on the rebel [[flagship]] ''[[Home One]]'', where he delivered the schematics to the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Empire Ascendant 1" />

===Search for Nakano Lash===
====A new target====
{{Quote|Time to leave ''this'' hellhole&hellip;for another.|Valance, departing Eriadu for Galmerah|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2">[[Bounty Hunters 2|''Bounty Hunters'' 2]]</ref>}}
Valance eventually acquired a [[droid]] companion,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 1">[[Bounty Hunters 1|''Bounty Hunters'' 1]]</ref> [[Nine-Four-El]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2" /> who assisted him in taking bounties. Shortly after the Battle of [[Hoth]], Valance visited [[Black Spire Outpost]] on<ref name="Bounty Hunters 1" /> the planet<ref name="Batuu">{{Databank|black-spire-outpost|Black Spire Outpost}}</ref> [[Batuu]] in order to take out a bounty on a [[Unidentified Devaronian clan|Devaronian clan]]. While on Batuu, Valance learned about the reappearance of his former mentor [[Nakano Lash]], who disappeared after she caused the failure of a [[Mission to Corellia (Nakano Lash's crew)|mission]] with Valance and several other hunters when she killed their client, [[Khamus]], the heir to the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 1" /> Attempting to track Lash down, Valance took the ''Broken Wing'' to the outskirts of [[Phelar Spaceport|Phelar Port]] on<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2" /> the planet<ref name="Eriadu">{{Databank|grand-moff-tarkin|Grand Moff Tarkin}}</ref> [[Eriadu]]. After finding a clue to Lash's location, Valance and Nine-Four-El departed in the ''Broken Wing'', heading for the [[Burial|graveyard]] planet [[Galmerah]], where the grave of Lash's [[parent]]s was located.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2" />

[[File:BrokenWingGalmerah-BountyHunters3.jpg|thumb|left|290px|The ''Broken Wing'' on the graveyard planet of Galmerah]]
On Galmerah, Valance was attacked by the bounty hunter [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], sending Nine-Four-El to bring the ''Broken Wing'' to assist in their skirmish. However, bounty hunter [[Ooris Bynar]] intercepted the droid before he reached the vessel. During Valance and Bossk's fight, the ''Broken Wing'' was boarded by [[T'onga]], a bounty hunter who formerly worked with Valance and Lash. After incapacitating Bossk, Valance returned to find Nine-Four-El missing his head and T'onga waiting for him in the ''Broken Wing''{{'s}} cockpit.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" />

====Locating Lash====
{{Quote|Took you long enough. That ''other'' hunter already cleared out. Is&hellip;is&hellip;Auntie Nakano&hellip;?''"<br />"''I'm sorry.''"<br />"''Then let's get ''out'' of here.''"<br />"''You&hellip;can pilot a ship?|Cadeliah and Beilert Valance|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5">[[Bounty Hunters 5|''Bounty Hunters'' 5]]</ref>}}
[[File:WreckageOverRuusan-BountyHunters4.jpg|thumb|right|280px|T'onga pilots the ''Broken Wing'' through wreckage above Ruusan.]]
Holding Valance at gunpoint, T'onga forced the bounty hunter to work with her in hunting Lash down. The two took the ''Broken Wing'' to Lash's last known location,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" /> the planet<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Ruusan]], discovering that Lash was aboard a starship within a wreckage field in the [[Ruusan system|system]]. T'onga piloted the ''Broken Wing'' into the wreckage, docking to Lash's [[720 light freighter]] and entering through the [[airlock]]. Once aboard, Valance prevented T'onga from killing Lash, who explained her actions in the disastrous mission years before; intervening in Khamus' attempt to kill [[Krynthia]], the heir of the rival [[Unbroken Clan]] syndicate, with whom he had a child.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" />

However, Boba Fett, one of the hunters Lash had betrayed, was still intent on her execution, and Valance attempted to protect his mentor. Lash had [[Cadeliah]], the young daughter of the two syndicate heirs, escape to the ''Broken Wing'' as she and Valance fended off Fett. With the ''Broken Wing'' still docked to Lash's ship, [[General]] [[Vukorah]] of the Unbroken Clan, who Lash had attempted to request assistance from, fired upon the [[light freighter]]. As the [[freighter]] broke apart, Fett escaped in his [[Slave I|own vessel]], satisfied in the chance of Lash's death. After speaking with Lash, who was injured and trapped on her ship, Valance returned to the ''Broken Wing'', finding Cadeliah in the pilot's seat. After learning that Lash was unable to escape, Cadeliah piloted the ''Broken Wing'' away from Lash's ship, which exploded behind them.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5" />

===On the run===
====Returning to Lowik====
{{Quote|I entered the coordinates. ''Now'' where are we going?''"<br />"''To see the ''one'' person I trust&hellip;''"<br />"''Well, we better hurry. You're in ''bad'' shape.|Cadeliah and Beilert Valance|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 6">[[Bounty Hunters 6|''Bounty Hunters'' 6]]</ref>}}
Having been tasked by Lash with the protection of Cadeliah, Valance stopped at the [[Spur Orbiting Market]] [[space station]] in order to receive repairs to his [[cybernetics]] and provide Cadeliah with [[Foodstuff|food]]. After defeating an [[Unidentified Arcona bounty hunter|Arcona bounty hunter]] who sought the bounty that Vukorah had placed on him, Valance had his cybernetics fixed at [[Slade's Repairs]], where he was attacked by the bounty hunters [[Zuckuss]] and [[4-LOM]]. Valance incapacitated his two assailants and escaped with Cadeliah to the ''Broken Wing'', departing the space station.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 6" />

[[File:BrokenWingDepartsLowik-BountyHunters7.jpg|thumb|left|320px|Valance leaves Lowik in the ''Broken Wing''.]]
With Valance severely injured, he and Cadeliah traveled to the secret outpost on Lowik, where Vega resided<ref name="Bounty Hunters 6" /> after she and the other inhabitants of Chorin were rescued by the rebels.<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> Valance requested help from Vega, while Zuckuss and 4-LOM, who had placed a [[Homing beacon|tracker]] on Valance, followed him to Lowik.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 6" /> After Valance received medical assistance, he [[Skirmish on Lowik|attempted]] to hold back the other bounty hunters as Vega and Cadeliah fled. While Cadeliah was captured, Valance made a deal with Zuckuss and 4-LOM, who allowed him and Cadeliah to remain free for three [[Standard day|standard rotations]], after which they would resume their hunt. Intending to lure his pursuers away, Valance left Cadeliah in the care of Vega and her husband, [[Lieutenant]] [[Kondra]], who was forced to evacuate the rebel forces on Lowik as a result of Valance's actions. Valance departed Lowik aboard the ''Broken Wing'', agonizing over parting with Vega.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7" />

====Termination at Terminus====
{{Quote|Come in, ''Spirit of Jedha''. This is the ''Broken Wing''. I'm here to help. Do you read me, ''Spirit of Jedha''? ''[&hellip;]'' I'm having trouble receiving you&hellip;''"<br />"''&mdash;for Force's sake, ''get out of there''!|Beilert Valance reaches out to the ''Spirit of Jedha''|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 8" />}}
While seeking to remove the bounty on his head, Valance surfaced in various spaceports in order to draw attention from Vega and Cadeliah. Having been informed of a lucrative job by the [[bail bondsman]] [[Syphacc]], Valance traveled to [[Nar Kaaga]], leaving the ''Broken Wing'' in [[Docking Bay 27-A]] while he met Syphacc at the [[H'unn Cabaret Pit]]. Syphacc's promised job turned out to be a trap for Valance, who was attacked by the [[assassin]] [[Tasu Leech]]. Valance incapacitated Leech, stole [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] from Syphacc as payback, and returned to the ''Broken Wing''.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 8" />

[[File:BrokenWingPirateAttack-BountyHunters8.jpg|thumb|right|330px|The ''Broken Wing'' under fire from the Ohnaka Gang.]]
Aboard the ''Broken Wing'', Valance was contacted by Kondra, who requested a favor from the bounty hunter in return for the events on Lowik. Kondra asked that Valance rescue the [[transport]] ''[[Spirit of Jedha]]'', which had suffered a malfunction after evacuating Lowik and dropped out of hyperspace at [[Terminus]], the intersection between the [[Corellian Trade Spine]] and [[Hydian Way]] [[hyperspace route]]s. After receiving the coordinates of the transport's location, Valance left Nar Kaaga and entered hyperspace. The ''Broken Wing'' arrived at Terminus with wreckage from a rebel [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing starfighter]] directly ahead, and Valance maneuvered to avoid it, clipping one of the wrecked starfighter's wings.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 8" />

Valance approached the drifting ''Spirit of Jedha'' and tried to make contact, but a rebel aboard the transport instead warned him to escape&mdash;the ''Spirit of Jedha'' had been attacked by the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]]s of the [[Ohnaka Gang]], led by [[Captain]] [[Skragg]]. Weequay [[Flarestar-class attack shuttle|attack shuttles]] pursued the ''Broken Wing'', seeking to shoot down the cruiser. Valance attempted to avoid the lasers, though one of the ''Broken Wing''{{'s}} engines was hit. The Weequay ships continued to fire upon the ''Broken Wing'', hitting the cruiser with a barrage of shots. With the ''Broken Wing'' taking heavy fire, the ''Spirit of Jedha'' attempted to contact Valance, receiving no response.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 8" />

[[File:BrokenBrokenWing-BountyHunters9.jpg|thumb|left|320px|Valance ejects the central module of the ''Broken Wing''.]]
Having sustained damage to the ''Broken Wing'', Valance contacted the ''Spirit of Jedha'', demanding that the [[hangar]] door be opened for him. The rebels were hesitant to do so, unsure if Valance himself was a pirate and noticing that the ''Broken Wing'' was too large to enter the hangar door. Valance released the central portion of the ''Broken Wing'' from the wings of the cruiser, piloting the small pod into the ''Spirit of Jedha''{{'s}} hangar and abandoning the damaged body of the vessel. Upon landing, Valance remarked that he wished for Kondra to pay for the ''Broken Wing'' to be salvaged and repaired.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 9" />

==Commanders and crew==
{{Quote|Well, since silent but deadly up in the cockpit ain't talking, I'll go ahead and answer your stupid questions, Dengar.|Chio Fain, to Dengar|<ref name="Target Vader 2" />}}
The ''Broken Wing'' was owned and piloted by the bounty hunter Beilert Valance.<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> Early in Valance's bounty hunting career, the ''Broken Wing'' was piloted by his associate Cavic, a fellow bounty hunter.<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> During Valance's skirmish against Darth Vader on Heva, the ship's top cannon was manned by<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> [[Gita]],<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> a Rebel Alliance operative who was undercover<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> on the team as a [[Tusken Raider]] bounty hunter named "Urrr'k."<ref name="Target Vader 3" />

Following the Battle of Hoth, Valance was accompanied by the droid<ref name="Bounty Hunters 1" /> Nine-Four-El,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 2" /> who reported to Valance from the ''Broken Wing'' during bounty hunting jobs.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 1" /> The bounty hunter T'onga piloted the ''Broken Wing'' while she and Valance worked together to hunt down Nakano Lash.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" /> After Valance found Lash, she tasked him with the protection of the young Cadeliah. Possessing the ability to fly starships, Cadeliah piloted the ''Broken Wing'' from the Ruusan system<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5" /> and accompanied Valance<ref name="Bounty Hunters 6" /> until he left her with Vega for her own safety.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Broken Wing'' first appeared in [[Target Vader 1|''Target Vader'' 1]], a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] written by [[Robbie Thompson]], illustrated by [[Marc Laming]] and [[Cris Bolson]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> on [[July 3]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/77664/star_wars_target_vader_2019_1|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #1}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Beilert Valance/Legends|Valance]] owned a ship known as the ''[[Kill Switch]]'',<ref name="Valance's Tale">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=swminis/article/huntwithin1|text=The Hunt Within: Valance's Tale: Part 1|int=The Hunt Within: Valance's Tale: Part 1}}</ref> which first appeared in the [[1978]] comic [[Star Wars (1977) 16|''Star Wars'' (1977) 16]], written by [[Archie Goodwin]] and penciled by [[Walter Simonson|Walt Simonson]].<ref name="SW 16">[[Star Wars (1977) 16|''Star Wars'' (1977) 16]]</ref>

The ''Broken Wing'' shares a strong resemblance to<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> concept art made for the vessel that eventually became the [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wing]] for the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film, ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. The concept art, entitled "Jyn's Ship Version 6" and created by [[David Hobbins]], featured rear wings that could rotate forward when in combat.<ref name="ArtOfRO">''[[The Art of Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
