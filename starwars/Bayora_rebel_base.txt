A '''base''' used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] was located at the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Bayora system]]</ref> astronomical object [[Bayora]]<ref name="Squadrons" /> in the [[Extorin sector]].<ref name="Appendix" /> After the [[Declaration of a New Republic|formation]] of the [[New Republic]]<ref name="Squadrons" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[mineral]]s harvested near the old base were used for modifications of [[flight helmet]]s worn by [[New Republic pilot]]s.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Behind the scenes==
The rebel base was mentioned in the [[2020]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]'' in a description for the Bayora Bronze cosmetic flight [[helmet]] for New Republic [[pilot]]s.<ref name="Squadrons" />
