'''Demas''' was a [[Gender/Legends|Male]] [[Miraluka/Legends|Miraluka]] [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] stationed on [[Corellia/Legends|Corellia]] during the [[Galactic War]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|reconstituted Sith Empire]]. While on Corellia he taught Republic individuals the skill of Diplomacy.<ref name="TOR" />
