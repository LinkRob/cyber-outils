The '''A-A5 Speeder Truck''' was a type of [[speeder truck]] that was created for civilian use but was retrofitted by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]<ref name="Armored Advance" /> for use in the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Though not ordinarily intended to be used for heavy combat, the vehicle was used in battle and to deploy [[Wookiee warrior]] reinforcements. It was equipped with a [[laser cannon]] turret as well as heavy [[armor]].<ref name="Commander" /> Popular<ref name="Savage Spirits">''[[Savage Spirits]]''</ref> and highly successful, the A-A5 was the basis for the development of the [[A-A5m Emergency Response Vehicle]]<ref name="Disciples of Harmony">''[[Disciples of Harmony]]''</ref> and had a relatively spacious crew compartment, a feature it shared with the [[A-A8 BackCountry Speeder Truck]].<ref name="Savage Spirits" />

==Behind the scenes==
The A-A5 Speeder Truck first appeared in an [[August]] [[2018]] update for the [[canon]] mobile game, ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'', which was originally released in August [[2014]].<ref name="Commander" /> The A-A5 had previously appeared in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] in works such as ''[[The Last Command]]'',<ref name="TLC">''[[The Last Command]]''</ref> ''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]'',<ref name="RAS">''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]''</ref> and more.<ref name="Arms & Equipment">''[[Arms & Equipment Guide]]''</ref>
