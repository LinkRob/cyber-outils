'''Ahch-To's second sun'''<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> was a [[binary star]] of the [[Ahch-To system]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition" /> It was [[orbit]]ed by the [[planet]] [[Ahch-To]]<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> and its [[Moon of Ahch-To|moon]].<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ahch-To's second sun first appeared in the [[2017]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> It was first identified in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]'', the [[2018]] novelization of the film written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" />
