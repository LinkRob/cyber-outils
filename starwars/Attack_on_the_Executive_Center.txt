Around [[19 BBY]], shortly before [[Separatist Reintegration Day]], the former [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] operative [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]] attempted an '''attack''' on the [[Imperial Executive Building]] on the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]], hoping to [[Death|kill]] [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. However, Vak-Nhalis's efforts were thwarted by [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], who had attempted to convince him to testify before the [[Imperial Senate]] but had instead been taken captive. Mothma was in possession of the trigger words for the explosive built into Vak-Nhalis's body and, in an effort to prevent the chaos that would follow from Vak-Nhalis's attack, used them to detonate the former Separatist while he was still approaching the [[Federal District]] in an [[airspeeder]].

==Events leading to the attack==
In a desperate attempt to [[Investigation into the framing of the Jedi|clear the name]] of the [[Jedi]], [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]] allied himself with [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]], A [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[assassin]] who held proof that [[Jedi Archives|Jedi Archive]] seals could be forged, just before the destruction of [[Eyo-Dajuritz]]. The two escaped [[Eyo-Dajuritz]] together and headed to [[Coruscant]].  Upon approach, Organa's ship was approached by two Imperial [[airspeeder]]s attempting to arrest them. Realizing their plan to exonerate the Jedi would no longer work, Vak-Nhalis pretended Organa was his hostage both to clear Organa's name and use him as a human shield so he could disappear into a crowd of protesters and regroup. In an attempt to regain contact with Vak-Nhalis, Senator [[Mon Mothma]] had [[Tychon Nulvolio]], who had recruited Soujen to join the Separatists, to set up a meeting in an attempt to get Bail's plan back on track. Nulvolio told Mothma how to activate Vak-Nhalis's [[self-destruct mechanism]], using the word "oubliette" to prime the explosive, then "sublime" to initiate it 5 minutes later.<ref name="MOF" />

==A failed assassination attempt==
When Vak-Nhalis met with Mothma, he came to the conclusion that clearing the Jedi's name wouldn't be enough to destabilize the [[Galactic Empire]] and instead he was going to kill [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and as many senators as he can in a final desperate act to keep the Separatist cause alive and get revenge for his [[Alvadorjian|people]]. Mothma pretended to go along with the plan so she could stay with him long enough to activate his self destruct. They then headed to the [[Imperial Executive Building]] in an airspeeder Vak-Nhalis stole to get to their meeting point. During the drive, Mothma used the priming word "oubliette" into the conversation. Using Mothma's credentials, they passed through the security perimeter and stopped outside a data-processing center affiliated with the [[Department of Agriculture and Non-sapient Creatures]]. Vak-Nhalis then gave Mothma a [[comlink]] to use to guide him through the building and began to cross the pedestrian bridge towards the building. When the bridge was nearly empty, she said "sublime", activating the self destruct, destroying the bridge, and nearly causing her own death. The explosion caused a momentary standoff between the [[Imperial Army]] and former Separatist systems as well as a loss in confidence in the [[Imperial Rebirth Act]] until Organa did damage control and used it to bolster the bill, himself, Mothma, and Nulvolio's reputation with stories of their heroics.<ref name="MOF" />
