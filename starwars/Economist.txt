An '''economist''' was a specialist in the field of [[economics]].<ref name="UP">''[[Unlimited Power]]''</ref> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="10 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the main events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', and therefore those of its [[Solo: A Star Wars Story: A Junior Novel|junior novelization]], to [[10 BBY]].</ref> the [[human]] [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] and his partner [[Chewbacca]] were preparing to board<ref name="SoloJunior">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> the ''[[First Light]]'',<ref name="SoloGuide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> the [[Nau'ur-class yacht|yacht]] of the [[Crimson Dawn]] [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]], while it was docked in front of an old [[Bis Refinery|fuel refinery]] on<ref name="SoloJunior" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="SoloGuide" /> [[planet]] [[Savareen]]. Before walking aboard the [[yacht]], Solo mentally compared the difference between the [[starship]]'s luxurious exterior and the decrepit appearance of the refinery to a [[The galaxy|galactic]] economist's study in contrasts.<ref name="SoloJunior" />

==Behind the scenes==
The occupation of economist was introduced in ''[[Unlimited Power]]'', a [[2018]] sourcebook published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for use with the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' line of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="UP" /> An economist subsequently appeared in [[Beth Revis]]'s [[2022]] novel ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''.<ref name="Princess">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref> In the real world, an [[Wikipedia:Economist|economist]] is a specialist in [[Wikipedia:Economics|economics]],<ref name="Economist">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dictionary.com/browse/economist|text=ECONOMIST Definition & Meaning|work=[[Wikipedia:Dictionary.com|Dictionary.com, LLC]]|archivedate=20230401095429}}</ref> which is a [[Wikipedia:Social science|science]] that studies the general concepts of [[Wikipedia:Production (economics)|production]], [[Wikipedia:Distribution (economics)|distribution]], and [[Wikipedia:Consumption (economics)|consumption]] of [[Wikipedia:Goods and services|goods and services]].<ref name="Economics">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dictionary.com/browse/economics|text=ECONOMICS Definition & Meaning|work=[[Wikipedia:Dictionary.com|Dictionary.com, LLC]]|archivedate=20241204011848}}</ref>

[[Economist/Legends|Economists]] were originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention of [[system economist]]s in ''[[Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters]]'', a [[1990]] sourcebook authored by [[Mark Rein-Hagen]] and [[Stewart Wieck]] and published by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="GG1">''[[Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters]]''</ref> The [[1993]] ''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]'' by [[Michael Allen Horne]] then identified the general occupation as economist.<ref name="DES">''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]''</ref>
