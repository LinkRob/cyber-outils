'''Edwig''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who served in the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], a [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] and intelligence agency of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Towards the end of the [[Galactic Civil War]], she served as [[Flag Lieutenant|flag lieutenant]] to [[Admiral]] [[Firmus Piett]] of [[Imperial Navy High Command|Fleet Command]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

On Piett's behalf, she issued a transmission to all [[fleet]] and [[garrison]] [[commander]]s, warning them that [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|enemy]] [[Spy|espionage]] was probable, and that all information security categories were to be increased by one full level. The transmission itself was later intercepted by the Rebellion.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
