'''Athus Klee''' was a world in the [[Athus Klee system]] in the [[Kalamith sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The [[Besalisk]] [[Dexter Jettster]] traveled to the world during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and discovered an [[Crimson Dawn ore hub|ore hub]] operated by the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate where workers had been [[Cybernetics|cybernetically]] altered by [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]]. The Besalisk managed to rescue one [[Rodian]] [[Unidentified Rodian cyborg|worker]] from the hub and reported it to the [[Imperial Senate]]'s [[Council of Labor Abuses]].

==Description==
[[File:Athus_Klee.png|thumb|left|250px|Athus Klee was located in the Tingel Arm.]]
{{Quote|Four days into the jungle now. Plenty glad I brought that vibro-machete.|Dexter Jettster comments on Athus Klee's dense jungle in the ''Smuggler's Guide''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
Athus Klee was a world<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />  in the [[Athus Klee system]] in the [[Kalamith sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> in the stellar skirt of the [[Tingel Arm]] region, a part of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] portion of [[the galaxy]]. At least one part of the world was covered in thick [[jungle]] that was home to [[Snake|vipers]] and [[gnat]]s. Its [[atmosphere]] was breathable to members of the [[Besalisk]] [[species]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==History==
===Evazan's playground===
{{Quote|The star-streamer made port on Athus Klee this morning. The passengers poured out and melted into the Spacers' District, but me, I don't use as fast as I use to. By the time I made it down the ramp, every single inn had no rooms.|Dexter Jettster describes his arrival on Athus Klee in the ''Smuggler's Guide''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
[[File:Athus_Klee_workers.png|thumb|right|250px|Crimson Dawn workers were cybernetically altered on Athus Klee.]]
The former [[pirate]] [[Queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] marked Athus Klee as a point of interest on a map she included in a [[book]] she began compiling that would later become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate operated an [[Crimson Dawn ore hub|ore hub]] in a valley on the world and offered lucrative one-[[Standard year|year]] contracts for labors to work there. The syndicate employed [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]] to [[Cybernetics|cybernetically]] alter the workers in a gene-mod chop shop housed on site in a cluster of hexagonal silos.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

After hearing rumors of illegal [[Cybernetics|cyborg mods]] and bodies grown to fit buyer specifications in the stellar skirt of the Tingel Arm, the Besalisk [[Dexter Jettster]] arrived on Athus Klee in a [[star-streamer]] to investigate. After the [[starship]] made port in the morning, most passengers poured out quickly and headed directly into the [[Spacers' District]], and by the [[time]] the older Jettster had made it off the vessel every inn had been fully booked out. Jettster instead headed out of the district into the [[Plant|underbrush]] and found a relatively dry rise then lay down under the [[star]]s.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

===The benevolent Besalisk===
{{Quote|Six days, two viper bites, and more gnat nips than I can count, but sure as you're born, that's a Crimson Dawn ore hub down in that valley. Rock haulers heading out and passenger shuttles heading in with more workers to feed the machine.|Dexter Jettster writes about Crimson Dawn's ore hub on Althus Klee|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
[[File:UnidentifiedMiner.png|thumb|left|150px|Jettster rescued a Rodian worker from the ore hub.]]
After six [[Standard day|days]] of hacking through the [[jungle]] with a [[vibro-machete]], Jettster had received bites from two vipers and countless gnats, but managed to find the ore hub. Sneaking around the hub's processing plant, the Besalisk spotted the chop shop and took a number of scans of the workers with his [[macrobinoculars]]. He then rescued a single [[Rodian]] [[Unidentified Rodian cyborg|worker]] from the hub and the pair headed back toward the [[spaceport]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

En route, the pair spent [[Standard hour|hours]] one night starring up at the stars through the jungle canopy. Upon reaching the port, Jettster contacted a [[Unidentified politician (Dexter Jettster)|politician]] on the galactic [[capital]], [[Coruscant]], who he was friends with and informed him of the hub. The politician sent a ship to pick up the Rodian and Jettster's surveillance scans so that they could be taken before the [[Imperial Senate]] [[Council of Labor Abuses]]. Jettster wrote about his experiences on Athus Klee throughout his journey, with Evazan and the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] annotating the pages with their own comments when the book later fell into their possession. While still on Athus Klee, Jettster then visited the [[Rasher's Rest]] [[cantina]], where he met the [[Big game hunter|hunter]] [[Tyro Viveca]]. Vivcea stole the ''Smuggler's Guide'' from Jettster while the Besalisk was in the [[Refresher|latrine]] and the left Athus Klee for the planet [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Inhabitants==
Athus Klee had at least one spaceport and a Spacers' District containing inns where off-world visitors could stay. Crimson Dawn's ore hub was staffed by laborers that had been cybernetically enhanced to better aid them in [[mining]]. The alterations to the workers included swapping out [[Limb|hands]] for shovels, [[mouth]]s and [[nose]]s for [[biomesh]] to filter [[Poison|toxins]], and the enlargement of [[eye]]s to collect what little light made it down into the mines. Many of the workers did not survive to see the end of their contracts, with passenger [[shuttle]]s constantly bringing in new laborers to replace the lost.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
