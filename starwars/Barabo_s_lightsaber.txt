This '''lightsaber''' was a [[Color|blue]]-bladed [[crossguard lightsaber]] utilized by [[Barabo]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Nubs’ Discovery" />

{{Weapon-stub}}
When [[Pooba]] [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Nubs]] visited an [[Unidentified Jedi Temple|ancient Jedi Temple]], he noticed a carving of the [[Jedi Master]] [[Barabo]], and hoping to get a better look, pushed on the carving, revealing a secret passageway into where Barabo's lightsaber was being stored. [[Kai Brightstar]] suggested they ask [[Yoda]] about the lightsaber, who explained that it belonged to the Pooba Barabo, and while the saber was not currently working, it was still precious. Yoda said that the saber must go to the [[Jedi Archives]], and offered to take Nubs with him to deliver it. In the '''[[Crimson Firehawk]]''', Nubs played with the saber as [[Nash Durango]] navigated through ice in their path. Nubs held the lightsaber in his mouth as he operated the ship's weapons system, however he dropped the saber which caused him to be unable to shoot the ice in the ship's path. Because of the ice in the path, one of the engines was hit and the ship crashed. As Durango, Yoda, and Nubs crossed the planet, Nubs held onto the lightsaber, but was unfocused due to his worry of making another mistake. During the crossing of a river, Nubs slipped and the saber fell into the mud. Yoda reassured Nubs that a lightsaber could be cleaned, and they continued onwards. When a plant tried to eat Nubs, he lost the lightsaber while attempting to escape, before it was eaten by the plant. Once Nubs had a pep talk from Yoda, he regained his focus and was able to open the plant with help from Durango and retrieve the lightsaber. Nubs asked Yoda to carry the lightsaber, but Yoda reassured Nubs that the lightsaber was already in good hands. Once the three arrived back on [[Coruscant]], Yoda introduced Nubs to [[Huyang]], who examined the lightsaber and put it on his desk for safekeeping. Huyang noticed the [[Kyber crystal]] inside had been dislodged and the [[Focusing Ring]] was damaged, before fixing it. Once fixed, Huyang handed the lightsaber to Nubs who activated it and held it into the air.<ref name="Nubs’ Discovery" />
