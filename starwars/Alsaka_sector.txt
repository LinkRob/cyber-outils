The '''Alsaka sector''' was a [[sector]] in the [[Core Worlds]] of [[the galaxy]]. It contained the [[Alsakan system|Alsakan]] and [[Opatajji system|Opatajji]] [[Star system|systems]] which were situated in grid square L-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

{{Sec-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Alsaka sector was first mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] it was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion]]'', a series of articles on [[StarWars.com]] published in the wake of the release of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' in [[2009]] by its authors [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Atlas">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
