The '''concussion grenade''' was a [[grenade]] that was used in [[the galaxy]],<ref name="Ezra's Gamble" /> which was used by frontline fighting troops to clear trenches, bunkers, and other military emplacements. The grenade, when detonated, created a lethal blast that would shatter structures and kill targets nearly instantaneously at the blast point.<ref name="KoF" /> Following a botched operation to apprehend the [[Dug]] criminal [[Gronson Takkaro]], the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] hurled a concussion grenade into [[Ake's Tavern]] in an effort to escape [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Ezra's Gamble" /> Models of concussion grenades included the [[Mier-Lang]] [[V-59 concussion grenade|V-59 Concussion Grenade]] and the [[SoroSuub Corporation|SoroSuub]] [[LXR-6 concussion grenade|LXR-6 Concussion Grenade]].<ref name="KoF" />
