The '''Dytrew system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Haserian sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square Q-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the location [[Dytrew]].<ref name="Origin" />
