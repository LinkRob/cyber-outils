'''Bordoks''' were a [[species]] of sure-footed [[equine]] [[Diet|herbivores]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Tree|forest]] [[moon]] [[Endor]]. Since the bordoks could carry large loads relative to their small size, the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Ewok]]s used them as both pack animals and mounts.

==Biology and appearance==
An [[equine]] [[species]],<ref name="BMF 38" /> the bordoks were related to other riding beasts from across [[the galaxy]], including [[pulga]]s, [[fathier]]s, [[orbak]]s, and [[tris]].<ref name="RoS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> They were sure-footed creatures capable of carrying large loads relative to their small size.<ref name="BMF 38" />

==Behavior==
Bordoks were noted for having a friendly behavior, and were domesticable.<ref name="USW" /> They had a [[Diet|herbivorous diet]],<ref name="BMF 38" /> feeding on [[plant]]s.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:Bordok-TheWoklingsTale.png|thumb|Right|180px|A bordok]]
The bordoks [[Homeworld|evolved]] on [[Endor]], a [[Tree|forested]] [[moon]]<ref name="USW" /> that [[orbit]]ted a [[planet]] of the [[Endor (planet)|same name]], in the [[Moddell sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="RoS VD" /> The [[Ewok]]s, a race of diminutive [[Sentience|sentients]] that also originated on Endor, domesticated the bordoks, using them as both pack animals and mounts. Although bordoks were strong for their size, the Ewoks sometimes favored the much larger and stronger [[blurrg]]s, a species of bipedal reptiles, for heavy work.<ref name="BMF 38" />

==Behind the scenes==
A bordok first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] storybook ''[[Wicket Finds a Way: An Ewok Adventure]]'', which was written by [[Melinda Luke]] and released in [[1984]]. The species was confirmed to be [[canon]] when it received mention in the sourcebook ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', released in [[2015]].
