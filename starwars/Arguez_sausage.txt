'''Arguez sausage''' was a type of spicy [[sausage]] that could be found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]]. [[Temmin Wexley]] began delivering [[Foodstuff|food]] packages to his aunts three [[Standard year|years]] prior to the novel, equating to [[1 ABY]].</ref> [[Temmin Wexley]] began to deliver [[Foodstuff|food]] baskets to his aunt [[Esmelle]] and her [[Marriage|wife]], [[Shirene]], each [[Standard week|week]] to help care for them. The baskets usually contained [[bread]] and [[fruit]]s, and occasionally arguez sausage or [[wyrg-jerky]]. The [[pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]] returned to Akiva [[4 ABY|three years]] after she had left her son, Temmin, on the world so that she could fight with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Shirene informed Norra of the self-sustainability Temmin had gained while she was away, citing the food packages as evidence.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arguez sausage was mentioned in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
