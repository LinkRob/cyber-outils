'''Freighter G-55-A''' was a [[freighter]] that was docked in [[Mirogana Spaceport]] in the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> A [[coordinator]] announced over the [[speaker]] system at the [[spaceport]] that the freighter would be departing shortly for [[Mirial]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> a [[planet]]<ref name="LifeDay">''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]''</ref> also located in the Outer Rim.<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref>
