A '''bar-fish gelatin''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] [[Diet|edible]] by [[Pantoran]]s and [[Soikan]]s. It was served at the [[Lady Jara's boarding house|boarding house]] of the [[Theelin]] [[Lady]] [[Jara]] in the [[City|town]] [[Ferdan]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dalna]].<ref name="Path of Deceit" />

In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while undertaking a [[Mission to Dalna (Macri and Zink)|mission]] on Dalna, the Soikan [[Jedi Master]] [[Zallah Macri]] and her [[Padawan]]&mdash;the [[Pantoran]] [[Kevmo Zink]]&mdash;ate a bar-fish gelatin at the boarding house with thin [[Color|white]] [[gravy]]. During their meal, the [[Evereni]] [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] member [[Marda Ro]] arrived to invite Zink to the [[Path compound]], with Ro mentally noting that the two [[Jedi]]'s bar-fish gelatin was gleaming.<ref name="Path of Deceit" />

==Behind the scenes==
A bar-fish gelatin appeared in the [[2022]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and [[Tessa Gratton]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase II">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> Within the novel, bar-fish gelatin appeared in a chapter written by Gratton.<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref>
