The '''Bunka system''' was an inhabited [[star system]] centered around at least [[Bunka system's star|one star]]<ref name="Dark Temple 1" /> in the [[Illisurevimurasi sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Some [[time]] before [[41 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the mission to [[Ontotho]] depicted in the comic to [[41 BBY]].</ref> the star in the system collapsed, resulting in mass evacuations. The evacuations caused a [[division]] of [[Galactic Republic]] [[troop]]s heading for the [[planet]] [[Ontotho]] to be waylaid.<ref name="Dark Temple 1" />
