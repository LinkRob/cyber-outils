'''Bay 4729''' was a [[Hangar|landing bay]] at the [[Imperial Impound Facility]] on<ref name="Han Chewie 6" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Iakar]].<ref name="Han Chewie 6" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> as part of their plan to [[Recovery of the Millennium Falcon|recover]] the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' from the impound facility, [[Khel Tanna]] allowed her [[Enigma Catalyst|starship]] to be captured by the [[Galactic Empire]] and taken to the facility, where it was held at bay 4729 alongside a [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]] and another [[starfighter]] until [[Akko]], who had hid inside a [[smuggling compartment]] alongside Tanna and [[Ooris Bynar]] when the [[Starship|ship]] was captured and remained on the ship as an insurance policy in the event their recovery went bad, took control of the ship and used it to blast [[stormtrooper]]s at [[Impound Paddock XM139]], where the ''Falcon'' was held.<ref name="Han Chewie 6" />
