The '''Cvetaen system''' was a [[star system]] located within the [[Expansion Region]] of [[the galaxy]]. The star system designated as [[LCC-4-4-7]] was located equidistantly from the Cvetaen system and the [[Sumitra system]], another Expansion Region star system. A non-commissioned communications officer informed [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] of this when stolen [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Corvette]] ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' was present in LCC-4-4-7.<ref name="TRK" />
