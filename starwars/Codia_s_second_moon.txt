{{Quote|The second moon was a "''Phoenix World''," possessing a vast ocean on its darker side, but with a rockier surface facing the Codian star. It was pockmarked with massive diamond deposits.|Kenobi writes about the moon in his journals|<ref name="OW2" />}}
'''Codia's second moon''' was a tidally locked [[moon]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Codia I]]. Its darker side was covered by a vast [[ocean]] and its brighter side was rockier with massive [[diamond]] deposits. Between [[44 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]], [[Thief|burglars]] destroyed the [[power core]] of a [[Unidentified mining outpost (Codia's second moon)|mining outpost]] on the brighter side of the [[moon]], causing unusual [[Radiation|radioactivity]] that suppressed the entire light spectrum at the outpost. The [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] were sent to investigate and discovered that the radiation had driven the [[Defel]] [[miner]] [[Rosack Denahst]] into a frenzy. Ultimately, they managed to repair the power core and restore the light and Denahst's sanity.

Codia's second moon was a tidally locked terrestrial [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Codia I]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]'s [[Codia system]]. A "[[Phoenix World]]," the darker side of the moon facing away from the [[Codian star]] was covered with a vast [[ocean]], but received [[planetshine]] from Codia I. The side facing toward the sun was bathed in light, with a rockier surface pockmarked with massive deposits of [[diamond]]s.<ref name="OW2" />

==History==
{{Quote|The Defel are ''sensitive to light''. Not just sensitive&hellip; they can manipulate the light around them, almost ''fade away'' to the naked eye. Obi-Wan, those ''fluctuations''&hellip;''"<br />"''Yes, the ''radiation''. Rosack could be particularly ''sensitive'' to its intensity, the rapid changes. [[File:2ndCodianMoonLandingPad-ObiWan2.png|thumb|right|300px|Part of Codia's second moon's brighter side was darkened by unusual radiation after the destruction of the outpost's power core.]]
Between [[41 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="DateRange">In the flashbacks seen in [[Obi-Wan 2|''Obi-Wan'' 2]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] go on a [[Mission to Codia's second moon|mission]]. [[Padawan (novel)|''Padawan'']] depicts Kenobi's first [[Mission to Lenahra|mission]], and since ''Obi-Wan'' 2 depicts a different mission, it must take place after. As Jinn is still alive in the flashback, it must take place before his death. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the novel to [[41 BBY]] and Jinn's death to [[32 BBY]], the flashback must take place between those two [[Standard year|years]].</ref> a [[Unidentified mining corporation|mining corporation]] managed an [[Unidentified mining outpost (Codia's second moon)|outpost]] for [[mining]] diamonds on the brighter side of the moon. During this period, a group of [[Thief|burglars]] destroyed the [[fuel]] rod of the outpost's [[power core]] with a [[non-incendiary photonic charge]] while attempting to steal a shipment of diamonds. The photonic burst interacted with the [[accelerite]] that primarily made up the core and cause dthe release of unusual [[Radiation|radioactivity]] that suppressed the entire light spectrum in and around the outpost. [[Rosack Denahst]], a [[Defel]] [[miner]] at the outpost, was especially sensitive to the radiation, which drove him mad. Most other miners were moved safely to the outpost's barracks, but six were murdered by the burglars before Denahst slaughtered five of the thieves.<ref name="OW2" />

A sixth [[Unidentified wounded burglar|burglar]] escaped with fatal injuries and was encountered by the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], who had been sent to the outpost to investigate reports of the darkness. Jinn and Kenobi ultimately realized what had happened to the power core with aid from the [[Unidentified mine supervisor|mine supervisor]], who had remained in the darkness. They then incapacitated Denahst and repaired the power core, although Kenobi was temporarily blinded in the process. With the radiation resolved, Denahst recovered his sanity and visited the recovering Kenobi in the outpost's medical center. Many [[Standard year|years]] later<ref name="OW2" /> around [[1 BBY]],<ref name="1 BBY Date">{{Obi-WanFrameDate}}</ref> Kenobi reflected on the incident and write about it in his [[The Journals of Ben Kenobi|journals]].<ref name="OW2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Codia's second moon was created for the [[Obi-Wan 2|second issue]] of the [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan (comic series)|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'']] comic series which was written by [[Christopher Cantwell]] and illustrated by [[Luke Ross]], with cover art of Denahst by [[Phil Noto]].<ref name="OW2" /> Prior to the comic's publication on [[June 29]], [[2022]],<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/101188/star_wars_obi-wan_2022_2|text=Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi (2022) #2}}</ref> the moon was first mentioned in the solicitations blog post for the comic made on [[StarWars.com]] on [[March 15]] of that year.<ref name="SWBlog">{{SW|url=news/marvel-june-2022-star-wars-comics-exclusive|text=Krrsantan Attacks in ''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' and More from Marvel's June 2022 Star Wars Comics – Exclusive}}</ref>
