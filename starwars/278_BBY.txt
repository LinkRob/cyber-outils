'''278 BBY''', or '''278<ref name="Date" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The [[human]] [[Orlis]]<ref name="SoS">''[[The High Republic: Seeds of Starlight]]''</ref> was [[Reproduction|born]] around that year. By [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> he became a [[Petty Officer]] within the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="SoS" />
