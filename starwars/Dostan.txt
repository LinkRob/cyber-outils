'''Dostans''' were a bipedal tentacled [[species]]. Some members of this species could be found dwelling on [[Kundu Minor]].<ref name="Little Lost Droid" />

==Biology and appearance==
Dostans were bipedal species with two legs, as well as two arms with three wide fingers each. They sported five tentacles from their faces, between their nose and their mouth, with two longer ones on the side commonly being wrapped around the sides of their heads and secured at their nape, while the other three were much smaller, dangling over their lips. They had [[Color|orange]] skin with either red or green patterns on their long tentacles that bled down onto their chin and torso.<ref name="Little Lost Droid" />

==History==
[[File:Dostans-Closeup.png|right|250px|thumb|Two Dostans observing a droid they rescued during a sandstorm.]]
At some point during the [[High Republic Era]], at least two Dostans took up residence on the arid [[planet]] [[Kundu Minor]], bearing clothes and equipment that protected them from the local sandstorms, and charting the dangerous cave systems near where they dwelt. These Dostans came into conflict with the [[Besalisk]] [[Braygh]] when she mistook them protecting one of her [[droid]]s from a sandstorm for them stealing it. Eventually, with help from a few [[Jedi]], they were able to sort this misunderstanding out and became amicable neighbors.<ref name="Little Lost Droid" />
