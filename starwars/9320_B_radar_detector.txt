The '''9320/B radar detector''' was a [[Portable scanner|portable]] short range scanner manufactured by [[Neuro-Saav Corp.|Neuro-Saav]]. It had the capability to intercept all types of signals within a perimeter of three hundred [[meter]]s, including life forms, their movement, density, mass, and communication signals. The detector was utilized by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref>
