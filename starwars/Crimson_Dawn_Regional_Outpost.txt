A '''Crimson Dawn Regional Outpost''' was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], in the same [[sector]] as [[Oro Sankeria]]. It was operated by a [[Unidentified Crimson Dawn cell|cell]] of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate.<ref name="Vader 22" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Darth Vader (2020) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 22]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] traveled to the outpost with the Crimson Dawn member [[Sabé]] to take command of the cell in order to lure the full forces of Crimson Dawn within the local [[sector]] out so they could be [[Skirmish on Oro Sankeria|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Vader 22" />
