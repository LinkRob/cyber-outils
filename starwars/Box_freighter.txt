'''Box freighters''' were a large and somewhat unwieldy model of cargo [[freighter]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. One type of box freighter was the [[Max-7 Rono box freighter]]. [[Vel Sartha]] and her [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|team]] planned to steal a Max-7 Rono and escape from the [[Aldhani Garrison|Imperial garrison]] on [[Aldhani]] with the entire Imperial payroll for the sector.<ref name="Aldhani" />
