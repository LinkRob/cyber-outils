A '''burst injector''' was an enhancement to a [[bacta injector]] that used high-pressure jets to increase the efficiency and speed of injections.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Jawa]] [[merchant]] [[Pinota Pakinota]] came into possession of an injector.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] traded nine units of [[durasteel]] and three [[4XB Calculation Unit]]s with Pakinota for the module.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Burst injectors appeared as an upgrade item in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The player must obtain the injector, fifteen units of [[transparisteel]] and six [[helicyclic gear]]s to unlock the "Enhanced Bacta Injector" expert ability which results in the player recovering more health instantly when using [[bacta]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
