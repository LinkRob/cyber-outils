'''Cal Alder''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[lieutenant]] in the [[Alliance High Command|High Command]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. After the [[evacuation of Yavin 4]], he participated in talks to find a location for a new rebel headquarters, and was then stationed in [[Echo Base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]], where he undertook patrols to ensure the base's security.

After rebels [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Han Solo]] went missing, Alder reported to [[Major]] [[Bren Derlin]] that there was still no word from them after the final patrols had returned. Derlin spoke to [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], then had Alder shut the shield doors to Echo Base for the [[Standard day|night]]. Alder later coordinated the defense of Echo Base's perimeter during the [[Battle of Hoth]], which he survived.

==Biography==
===Missing rebels===
Cal Alder was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="SWFC" /> from<ref name="Geektionary" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="TFARPG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Kal'Shebbol]], who served in the [[Alliance High Command|High Command]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Geektionary" /> His role was to find ways to adapt the native ecology of new worlds to the needs of the Alliance, due to its severely limited resources.<ref name="SWFC" /> As a ground mission specialist, he participated in talks to determine what world should be selected to house the Alliance's new headquarters after [[Evacuation of Yavin 4|evacuating]] its [[Great Temple|previous base]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]]. These discussions eventually led to the establishment of [[Echo Base]] on the [[Water|icy]] planet [[Hoth]], where Alder was stationed<ref name="Geektionary" /> as a Deck [[Lieutenant]].<ref name="ESB" /> Alder had a brilliant service record, which convinced his [[commanding officer]], [[Major]] [[Bren Derlin]], to give him the responsibility of undertaking various patrols around the base to ensure its security.<ref name="Geektionary" />

[[File:Alder_reports.png|thumb|left|300px|Cal Alder and Bren Derlin served together on Hoth.]]
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> rebel [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]] went missing while out setting up [[sensor]]s near the base. His friend [[Han Solo]] set out to find the commander; however, he also failed to return by [[Standard day|nightfall]]. After the last rebel patrol returned to base for the day, Alder approached Derlin in one of Echo Base's [[hangar]]s and reported as such. He then began to state that there was still no word from Skywalker or Solo, but the major raised a [[Limb|hand]] to caution him, gesturing to the nearby [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who was a close friend of the missing duo and also listening to Alder's report.<ref name="ESB" />

===Closing the gate===
{{Quote|Your Highness, there's nothing more we can do tonight. The shield doors must be closed."''<br />[Leia nods]<br />"''Close the doors.''"<br />"''Yes, sir.|Major Bren Derlin speaks with Leia Organa and Cal Alder|audio=DerlinAndAlder.flac|<ref name="ESB" />}}
[[File:Leia_Alder_and_Derlin.png|thumb|right|300px|Cal Alder and Bren Derlin gain Leia Organa's permission to close Echo Base's shield doors.]]
Gulping, then choosing his words carefully,<ref name="Novel">[[Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back (paperback novel)|''Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back'' junior novelization]]</ref> Alder finished his report in hushed tones. He and Derlin then approached Organa and the major strongly recommended that the base's shield doors be closed, as there was nothing more that could be done to find her friends during the night. Organa agreed and Derlin ordered Alder to have the doors shut, which he did. Solo and Skywalker were discovered alive the following morning.<ref name="ESB" />

After an Imperial [[XJ9-CS14|Viper probe droid]] was discovered and destroyed near Echo Base, the Alliance began to prepare for evacuation. Before they managed to escape, an [[Death Squadron|Imperial fleet]] arrived above Hoth and deployed [[Blizzard Force|ground forces]] to attack the base.<ref name="ESB" /> Alder participated in the ensuing [[Battle of Hoth]], coordinating the defense of the base's perimeter, and ultimately survived the battle, which ended with the Alliance successfully escaping despite suffering a massive defeat.<ref name="Geektionary" />

==Personality and traits==
Alder had light skin and [[Color|bluish]] [[eye]]s.<ref name="ESB" /> He was an expert [[scout]] and often found himself among the first to become acquainted with the [[alien]] environs of frontier worlds.<ref name="SWFC" /> He took on the responsibility of closing the gates to Echo Base as he reasoned that the intense [[Temperature|cold]] of the night, plus the possibility of [[wampa]] attacks, posed a security risk to the entire base that he was not willing to take, even for [[Alliance officer|officers]] as highly [[Military rank|ranked]] as Solo and Skywalker.<ref name="Geektionary" />

==Equipment==
While in Echo Base, Alder wore rebel snow [[Clothing|gear]] including a brown sleeveless [[jacket]] over a white [[Alliance military uniform|uniform]] as well as white [[command gauntlet]]s and a white [[insulated watch cap]] with a [[Comlink|com set]], a brown face wrap, white [[Hat|cap]] warmers and [[anti-glare goggles]]. When reporting to Derlin, he was carrying a [[datapad]].<ref name="ESB" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Filming===
{{Quote|When [Irvin Kershner] was satisfied I'd got it just right he said, 'Right! Let's shoot it!' Can you imagine? Suddenly I was thrust in front of the whole production team and crew, to deliver my 'immortal' lines. Quite a daunting task I can tell you.|Jack McKenzie|<ref name="Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://www.starwarsinterviews.com/original-trilogy/episode-v-the-empire-strikes-back/jack-mckenzie-cal-alder|text=Jack McKenzie (Cal Alder)|author=Pellegrom, Denis|archivedate=20200117215535|nolive=1}}</ref>}}
[[File:Cal_alder.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Jack McKenzie portrayed Cal Alder.]]
Cal Alder was portrayed by [[Jack McKenzie]] in the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="ESB" /> Alder's scene was shot on the ''Star Wars'' Stage at [[Elstree Studios]] between [[May 22]] and [[May 29]], [[1979]].<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of The Empire Strikes Back]]''</ref> Immediately prior to filming,<ref name="Interview" /> director [[Irvin Kershner]] gave McKenzie his line and asked him to do an American accent rather than his natural English one, as "all the English guys are the bad guys and all the Americans are the good guys."<ref name="YouTube">{{YouTube|video=nKg0Z-SVvXU|text=Entrevista Jack McKenzie &#124;Cal Alder&#124; Star Wars|channel=channel/UCg3vRr393AD4sht571H7_rA|name=Cinema Club}}</ref>

McKenzie repeated the line several times for Kershner, who had the actor soften the emphasis on the second sentence to a conspiratorial tone in order to convey that Leia Organa was being spared the pain of hearing her friends were still missing. It took several takes to get the scene, and during one, McKenzie thought he had heard cut after making his exit and walked back into shot whistling. Kershner joked that the embarrassed McKenzie would never work again, but forgave him.<ref name="Interview" />

===Naming===
In the film, the character was credited only as "Deck Lieutenant,"<ref name="ESB" /> and in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character was first identified in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile card game]] ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'',<ref name="SWFC" /> which initially launched in [[2013]]. Although the launch of ''Force Collection'' predates<ref name="Force Collection release">{{SW|url=news/announcing-star-wars-force-collection|text=Announcing Star Wars: Force Collection|nolive=1}}</ref> the ''Star Wars'' canon reset of [[2014]],<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]] confirmed that the game was kept updated to fit with canon.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|HolocronKeeper|status/846807700482666496|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=I'm not gonna go through the trouble of changing them and I don't have any reason to believe currently that anyone would. [&hellip;] On the off-chance that we DID have a current need to change any of these, we would have for Force Collection.|archivefile=File:Leland_Chee_Force_Collection.png|archivedate=20190727180426}} &mdash; [[Leland Chee]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] has confirmed that [[in-universe]] information from ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' conforms with ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]].</ref> The [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]'' incorrectly states that Alder ordered Derlin to close the shield doors, rather than the other way round.<ref name="Geektionary" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, Alder appeared just prior to the release of ''The Empire Strikes Back'' in the [[Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back (novelization)|novelization]] of the film written by [[Donald F. Glut]].<ref name="Novelization">[[Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back'' novelization]]</ref> The name Cal Alder originates on a card released in the [[1996]] ''[[Hoth Limited]]'' expansion set of the [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]].<ref name="Hoth Limited">{{CCG|set=Hoth Limited|cardname=Cal Alder}}</ref>
