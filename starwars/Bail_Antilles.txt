'''Bail Antilles''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[Politics|politician]] who served in the [[Galactic Senate]] as the [[senator]] for [[Alderaan]] during the tumultuous final [[Standard year|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Antilles was nominated to succeed [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]] after the [[Galactic Senate]] chose to [[Vote of No Confidence|remove]] Valorum from the chancellery.

==Biography==
A [[Gender|male]] [[human]], [[Senator]] Bail Antilles represented the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] in the last [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="HC 34" /> and was related to fellow senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]]'s [[Marriage|wife]], [[Breha Organa]].<ref name="Card Trader">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Bail Organa - Galactic Senate}}</ref> As a representative, Antilles distinguished himself in the fight against corruption and the interests of large corporations, which earned him as many enemies as advocates.<ref name="Encyclopedia Naboo Crisis">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Crisis on Naboo}}</ref>

At the time of the [[Invasion of Naboo]], he was assisted by [[Liana Merian]]<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> and [[Agrippa Aldrete]].<ref name="HC 34" /> When [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]] failed to convince the Senate to stop the [[Trade Federation]] occupation of her [[homeworld]], she called for a [[Vote of No Confidence]] in [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]], whose chancellery had been hampered by the bureaucracies of the Senate. Along with [[Ainlee Teem]] of [[Malastare]] and [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] of Naboo, Antilles was nominated to succeed Valorum as Supreme Chancellor. Palpatine ultimately won the [[Supreme Chancellor election, 32 BBY|election]]<ref name="Episode I" />&mdash;an outcome that led to the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> and the [[Imperial Era|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> At some point following the [[Politics|political]] defeat to Palpatine, Antilles was succeeded by Bail Organa.<ref name="Card Trader" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Alderaan seconds the motion for a vote of no confidence in Chancellor Valorum.|Adrian Dunbar as "Bail Organa," in a deleted scene from ''Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace''|audio=AdrianDunbarOrgana-TPM.flac|<ref name="Screenplay">[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace Illustrated Screenplay|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace Illustrated Screenplay'']]</ref>}}
The name Bail Antilles dates back to the original drafts of the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. The [[May]] [[1974]] [[The Star Wars rough draft|rough draft]] of the film depicted [[Bail Antilles (The Star Wars)|Bail Antilles]] as a rugged-looking galactic trader and confederate of [[Aquillian Ranger/Legends|Aquillian Ranger]] [[Clieg Whitsun]].<ref name="Rough Draft">{{WebCite|url=http://www.starwarz.com/starkiller/2010/03/the-star-wars-rough-draft|text=The Star Wars&ndash;Rough Draft|work=Starkiller&ndash;The Jedi Bendu Script Site|archivedate=20200220123019}}</ref> The [[Adventures of the Starkiller, Episode I: The Star Wars|second draft]] in [[1975]] changed the character to an Aquillian Ranger himself and co-[[pilot]] of Luke Starkiller, the character who would ultimately become [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Starkiller Adventures">{{WebCite|url=http://www.starwarz.com/starkiller/2010/03/adventures-of-the-starkiller-second-draft|text=Adventures of the Starkiller (Second Draft)|work=Starkiller&ndash;The Jedi Bendu Script Site|archivedate=20200220123118}}</ref> In the revised fourth draft, Bail Antilles is the father of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of Alderaan.<ref name="ANHRFD">{{WebCite|url=https://www.starwarz.com/starkiller/star-wars-the-adventures-of-luke-starkiller-revised-fourth-draft|text=A New Hope Revised Fourth Draft|archivedate=20240518134536}}</ref> After [[Ogana Major]] was renamed Alderaan, the slightly-tweaked name of Organa was given to Leia and her royal family.<ref name="Alderaan Old Databank">{{DB|location|alderaan|Alderaan}}</ref> The rough draft version of Bail Antilles appears in the [[2013]] comic book series ''[[The Star Wars]]'', an adaptation of George Lucas' draft.<ref name="TSW1">[[The Star Wars 1|''The Star Wars'' 1]]</ref> Meanwhile, the concept of an Antilles who would pilot alongside Luke was repurposed into [[Wedge Antilles]].

[[File:Alderaan_Delegation.png|thumb|right|300px|Adrian Dunbar as "Bail Organa" (right) in a deleted scene from ''The Phantom Menace'']]
[[Canon]]ically, Bail Antilles was first mentioned in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Episode I" /> Actor [[Adrian Dunbar]] filmed scenes for the role, which at that point was referred to as Bail Organa. In the deleted scene, "Organa" seconded Queen Amidala's call for a Vote of No Confidence in Chancellor Valorum.<ref name="dbOrg">{{DB|character|bailorgana|Organa, Bail}}</ref> During production, the character's name was changed to Bail Antilles,<ref name="MisID">{{Blog|holocron|29|Mistaken Identities}}</ref> and [[Jimmy Smits]] was ultimately cast for the part of Bail Organa in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="Episode II" /> Dunbar was disappointed after hearing from director [[George Lucas]] that the scene had been cut, as the actor took the part because Lucas originally intended to include him in the rest of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="TFN">{{WebCite|url=http://www.theforce.net/episode1/story/Adrian_Dunbar_talks_about_being_cut_from_Episode_I_75524.asp|text=Adrian Dunbar talks about being cut from Episode I|work=TheForce.Net|archivedate=20200220122415}}</ref>

Despite only being mentioned in the film, Antilles made a few appearances in books and other material in the Expanded Universe, which has since been rebranded as the non-canon [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] line. He appeared in two [[James Luceno]] novels, [[2001]]'s ''[[Cloak of Deception]]''<ref name="Cloak of Deception">''[[Cloak of Deception]]''</ref> and [[2012]]'s ''[[Darth Plagueis]]''.<ref name="Darth Plagueis">''[[Darth Plagueis]]''</ref> He was also referred in Luceno's [[2005]] novel ''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''.<ref name="Labyrinth of Evil">''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''</ref> Several reference books also made mention of the character to provide information about him, such as ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''.<ref name="TNEG">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''</ref>
