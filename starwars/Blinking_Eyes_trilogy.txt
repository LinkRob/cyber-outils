The '''Blinking Eyes trilogy''' was a trilogy of scenarios for the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign, a supplement to the [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]. It was released in [[2002]], and its plot focused on the players combating the efforts of a criminal organization known as [[the Syndicate]].
