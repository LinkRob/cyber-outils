The '''"Precision-X" Marksman Rifle''', known simply as the '''"Precision-X" blaster''', was a type of specialized, long-ranged [[blaster rifle]] manufactured by [[Cadregg Armory]]. The [[blaster]] utilized a series of [[cycling chamber]]s to excite the [[blaster gas]] used in the [[rifle]], in order to produce extraordinarily intense beams of energy. These beams were only slightly more powerful than those from similar [[weapon]]s, but maintained coherence over long distances. This allowed the rifle to maintain its stopping power far past a typical blaster's range limitations. The cycling chamber's effects also extended to the weapon's [[stun]] setting, allowing targets to be stunned at medium ranges. The blaster could be equipped with a single [[hard point]].<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

Costing 1,500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], the "Precision-X" blaster was one of the first weapons manufactured by Cadregg Armory. The demand for the weapon's special capabilities was in such high demand by sport shooters, [[assassin]]s, and [[bounty hunter]]s, that its manufacturer could barely keep a stock of the weapon.<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />
