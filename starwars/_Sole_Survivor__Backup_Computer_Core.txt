The '''"Sole Survivor" Backup Computer Core''' was a model of [[armored computer core]] manufactured by the [[Loronar Corporation]]. It used [[laminanium]] plating and redundant data backups to protect information from a [[starship]]'s full destruction. It cost 1,100 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Sole Survivor" Backup Computer Core was first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref>
