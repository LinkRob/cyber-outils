'''Daniel Colon, Jr.''', sometimes credited as '''Dan Colon''', is a artist and animator who worked on various video games for [[LucasArts]], including the [[X-Wing (game series)|''X-Wing'']] and [[Star Wars: Rebel Assault|''Rebel Assault'']] series.
