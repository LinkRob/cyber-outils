The '''Courtsilius Wax Revolution''' was an event that resulted in the [[death]]s of ten percent of the population of the [[planet]] [[Courtsilius]].<ref name="MOF" />  Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|14}}</ref> [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] spoke with a [[Unidentified Gossam woman|Gossam woman]] at a gala on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The [[Gossam]] [[Gender|woman]] asked her if she remembered the Courtsilius Wax Revolution, which Mothma did, although she had not heard the phrase in [[Standard decade|decades]]. After telling the Gossam she remembered it, the Gossam told Mothma to speak with the former [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Tychon Nulvolio]] to gain the support of the ex-Separatist voting bloc.<ref name="MOF" />
