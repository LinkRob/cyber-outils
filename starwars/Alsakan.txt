'''Alsakan''' was a [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] situated along the [[Perlemian Trade Route]]. It was the [[homeworld]] of both [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]] and the career military [[Imperial officer|officer]] [[Enric Pryde]].

==Description==
Alsakan was a terrestrial<ref name="Dawn" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Boba and others">{{EncyclopediaCite|Boba Fett and Other Characters of the Underworld}}</ref> located within an intersecting portion of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> Situated at the coordinates L-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it lay along the [[Perlemian Trade Route]]<ref name="TFABG" /> [[super-hyperroute]]<ref name="BMF 48">{{BuildFalconCite|48|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> between the<ref name="TFABG" /> planets<ref name="Dawn" /><ref name="Fort Anaxes DB">{{Databank|fort-anaxes|Fort Anaxes}}</ref> [[Coruscant]] and [[Anaxes]].<ref name="TFABG" /> The world shared its name with the [[Alsakan Conflicts]] which marked the beginning of “dreadnaught era” which saw deployment of capital ships which matched the scale of those used much later in Galactic history.<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Meanwhile, amid the slightly outdated tendencies of some instructors at the Imperial Academy on Alsakan, Major Urkhaste's lessons on defensive tactics and deployments were apparently legendary.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
[[File:YoungPryde-2020DarthVader42cover.png|thumb|left|220px|Enric Pryde was born and studied on Alsakan]]
The [[human]] [[Enric Pryde]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> was born on Alsakan in [[27 BBY]]. After the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|rose to power]] in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the world became one of its greatest supporters.<ref name="First Order" /> The [[Alsakan Academy]] was established by this [[Imperial Era|time]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> Instructors at the [[Imperial Academy]] were known to have tendencies that were outdated by common practices at the [[time]]. Despite that, the lessons taught there by [[Major]] [[Urkhaste]] were said to be legendary.<ref name="Rise" /> Pryde worked his way into an advanced placement in the Alsakan Academy<ref name="TROS VD" /> by [[11 BBY]]<ref name="11 BBY">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates [[Enric Pryde]] being on an advanced placement at the [[Alsakan Academy]] to forty-five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], which corresponds to [[11 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> and continued into a career as an [[Imperial officer|officer]] in the [[Imperial Military]] and eventually in the [[First Order]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

By [[9 BBY]], the Imperial [[Inquisitor]] [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]] hunted [[Force-sensitive]]s on Alsakan.<ref name="Dawn" /> The [[Imperial Archives]] had a section on the "Alsakan Imperial Academy" that included the file "Lecture Series&mdash;Principles and Precepts of Defensive Fortifications," which gave coverage on Urkhaste's lessons. Having read the file, [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] briefly wrote on the Alsakan Academy in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[35 ABY]]<ref name="35 ABY">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

==Inhabitants==
[[File:DoctorEvazan-ROShadowPortraits2.png|thumb|right|220px|Alsakan was the homeworld of Doctor Evazan.]]
Alsakan was the [[homeworld]] of the human [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]], who went on to become known for his obscene surgical experiments.<ref name="Boba and others" /> Alsakan culture included military riding traditions, which made use of [[Swagger stick|swaggersticks]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alsakan was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', a [[2015]] reference book written by [[Tricia Barr|Patricia Barr]], [[Adam Bray]], [[Daniel Wallace]], and [[Ryder Windham]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> It was originally created for the ''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] sourcebook written by [[Michael Allen Horne]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' and published in [[1993]].<ref name="Dark Empire Sourcebook">''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]''</ref>
