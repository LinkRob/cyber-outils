A '''bow''' was a primitive weapon<ref name="Ewok Databank" /> consisting of a flexible arc bent by a string at each end and used to launch pointed projectiles<ref name="Episode VI" /> called [[arrow]]s.<ref name="Endor Databank">{{Databank|endor|Endor}}</ref> The [[Ewok]]s, a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] from the [[Endor|Forest Moon]] [[orbit]]ing the [[planet]] of [[Endor (planet)|Endor]], had bows among their arsenal.<ref name="Ewok Databank" /> During the [[Battle of Endor]], Ewoks notably used bows and arrows to assist the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in the fight against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Episode VI" />

The Jedi Master [[Yoda]] used a bow and arrow to hunt a [[Unidentified quadruped (Dagobah)|quadruped]] for its meat while he was in exile on [[Dagobah]], and he successfully managed to kill one of the beasts by shooting it in the neck with an arrow.<ref name="Age of Rebellion">{{StoryCite|story=The Trial of Dagobah|book=Age of Rebellion Special 1}}</ref> On [[Dathomir]], the [[Hunter (Nightsister)|Hunters]] of the [[Nightsisters]] clan wielded [[Nightsister energy bow|energy bows]] whose strings and arrows were made of [[plasma]].<ref name="Ultimate" /> The unaltered [[Cloning|clone]] of [[Jango Fett]], [[Omega]] utilized an [[Zygerrian energy bow|energy bow]] as her primary [[Omega's Zygerrian energy bow|weapon]] that she took from a group of [[Zygerrian]] [[enslaver]]s on [[Ord Mantell]].<ref name="Rampage">{{TBB|Rampage (episode)}}</ref> The [[Zabrak]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Nox]] used a [[composite bow]] known as the [[Wild Bow]] as her primary weapon during the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition within the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>
