The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''24 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3253 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7953''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Exploratory mission to investigate a downed craft]] {{Circa}}<ref name="FlashbackDate">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' establishes that [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] was twenty-seven [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of [[Andor Season 1|''Andor'' Season 1]], the events of which are dated to [[5 BBY]] by that same book. {{Databank|cassian-andor|Cassian Andor}} establishes that Andor was nine years old when he left [[Kenari]], with {{SW|url=series/andor/andor-season-1-episode-3-episode-guide|text="Reckoning" Episode Guide &#124; Andor}} establishing that this occurred before the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] had formed. ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'' and ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' establish that the [[Raxus Address|formation the CIS]] occurred two [[Standard year|years]] before the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' and other sources date to take place in [[22 BBY]]. For all this information to be true, Cassian Andor had to have been born in [[33 BBY]], with his departure taking place after his birthday, but before the Raxus Address in [[24 BBY]], and the events of ''Andor'' taking place before his birthday in [[5 BBY]].</ref>
*[[Count]] [[Dooku]] gives the [[Raxus Address]] and founds the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>
**The [[Separatist Crisis]] begins when thousands of [[star system]]s secede from the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="UVG" />
*[[Han Solo|Han]] joins the [[White Worms]] as a [[Scrumrat]].<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
