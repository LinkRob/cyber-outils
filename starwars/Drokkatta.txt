'''Drokkatta''' was a [[Wookiee]] [[Wookiee warrior|Warrior]] within the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] that was stationed on [[Coruscant]]. The Wookiee worked with a [[Cerean]] [[quartermaster]] named [[Ko-Tun Feralo]] and the [[Human]] [[droid]] [[engineer]], [[Jarrod Kelvin]]. Drokkatta was a demolitions expert whose favored weapon was a modified [[MGL-9]] [[micro-grenade launcher]] called "Boomer."<ref name="Heart of the Empire" />

==Biography==
Drokkatta, along with many other Wookiees, was enslaved by the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] and forced to work at a [[Geonosis]] mining facility.<ref name="Heart of the Empire" /> Drokkatta managed to escape enslavement with the help of former Imperial [[Deggen Sont]]. Drokkatta swore a [[life debt]] to the Imperial defector for freeing him.<ref name="Legends">''[[Legends of the Alliance]]''</ref> Drokkatta left many friends and family behind, but promised to return.<ref name="Heart of the Empire" />

Drokkatta joined the Rebel Alliance as a member of a [[Coruscant rebel cell|Rebel cell]] on the planet [[Coruscant]]. The Wookiee worked as a demolitions expert alongside a Cerean quartermaster named [[Ko-Tun Feralo]] and a Human droid mechanic named [[Jarrod Kelvin]].<ref name="Heart of the Empire" />
