'''Chris Hockabout''' worked on the production team for ''[[Star Wars: Dark Forces]]'', [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (video game)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'']], ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Mysteries of the Sith]]'', [[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]], ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunter]]'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]]s.
==Biography==
Hockabout graduated from college in [[1992]]. As a postgraduate trip that summer, he visited [[Wikipedia:Czechoslovakia|Czechoslovakia]], where he took a boat tour in the labyrinthine Gothic-like sewers of [[Wikipedia:Prague|Prague]], lit only by torches and spotlights.<ref name="secrets">''[[The Secrets of Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire]]'', Part 4: Battle Zones, IV. The Final Battle</ref>

He worked as texture artist for [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (video game)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'']] where he worked on the [[Sewers of Coruscant]] and [[Xizor's Palace]] among others.<ref name="secrets" />
