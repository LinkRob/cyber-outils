The '''3B''' was a model of [[shuttle]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Terror" /> Between [[1 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{TerrorOnMustafarDate}}</ref> [[Vardos Star Scouts]] member [[Deeta Kardon]] purposefully misidentified an approaching [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] as a 3B in order to goad her friend [[Zan Videx]] into responding while they and fellow [[scout]] [[Drik Maylon]] waited for the [[shuttle]] to land in [[Kestro City]] on the [[planet]] [[Vardos]]. Vrix nearly started to list the differences between the two models before realising he was being baited.<ref name="Terror" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 3B was mentioned in ''[[Terror on Mustafar]]'', an audiobook written by [[Cavan Scott]] and released in [[2026]]. As the story is only avaliable in audio format, no official spelling for the shuttle's name is avaliable.<ref name="Terror" />
