'''Bargaz Barnok''' was [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] operative during the [[Clone Wars]]. Bargaz Barnok went on a [[Mission on Leyoye Prime|mission]] on [[Leyoye Prime]] in a unit comprised [[Poan of Riek]], [[Delsh Oquan]], [[Baby Yezz]], [[Seebee One]], and [[Alvadorjian]] [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]]. Bargaz Barnok perished during the mission.<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Bargaz Barnok appeared in a memory in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
