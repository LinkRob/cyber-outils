'''''BB-8 and the Snow Monster''''' was a planned young readers book by [[Caitlin Kennedy]] with illustrations by [[Brian Kesinger]]. Slated to be the third entry in the ''[[Droid Tales]]'' series, the book was canceled by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] after several release date delays. The title would have featured the [[droid]]s [[BB-8]], [[C-3PO]], and [[R2-D2]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]].

==Publisher's summary==
''C-3PO is none to ''[''sic'']'' happy when the Resistance must return to the old Rebel base on Hoth, but R2-D2 is nostalgic from their old adventures there and BB-8 is just excited to play in the snow. However, when the little round droid encounters an angry wampa, he'll have to use all of his programming to trick his way out from under the snow beast!''<ref name="Edelweiss 2022" />

==Plot summary==
During or after [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date explain" /> the [[Resistance]] returns to [[Echo Base]] on<ref name="Edelweiss 2022" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="AAYNTK" /> Hoth.<ref name="Edelweiss 2022" /> The [[protocol droid]]<ref name="AAYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> [[C-3PO]] is unhappy over the move, while<ref name="Edelweiss 2022" /> the [[astromech droid]]<ref name="AAYNTK" /> [[R2-D2]] feels nostalgic.<ref name="Edelweiss 2022" /> The astromech droid<ref name="AAYNTK" /> [[BB-8]] plays in the [[Water|snow]] before encountering a [[wampa]].<ref name="Edelweiss 2022" />

==Development==
''BB-8 and the Snow Monster'' was first revealed in official online listings by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] in [[2020]]. Confirmed to be the third book in the ''[[Droid Tales]]'' series, the title would have been written by author [[Caitlin Kennedy]] with illustrations by artist [[Brian Kesinger]]. The book was originally slated with a release date of [[January 4]], [[2022]]<ref name="Edelweiss 2022" /> before subsequently being rescheduled for release on [[January 3]], [[2023]]<ref name="Edelweiss 2023">{{LegacyEdelweiss|url=#sku=136805188X|text=Star Wars BB-8 and the Snow Monster|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/N9LXA|oldversion=1}} {{C|[[2023]] release date}}</ref> and [[February 6]], [[2024]] before being officially canceled in [[January]] 2023.<ref name="Edelweiss 2024">{{LegacyEdelweiss|url=#sku=136805188X|text=Star Wars BB-8 and the Snow Monster|live=1}} {{C|[[2024]] release date}}</ref> Had it been published, ''BB-8 and the Snow Monster'' would have been printed in a 64-page hardcover paper-over-board format with an initial release of 35,000 copies.<ref name="Edelweiss 2022" />
