A '''Blaze''' was a unit used by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s, smaller than a [[Tempest]] but larger than a [[Strike]]. In the final stages of the [[Battle of Kur]], [[Kassav Milliko]] noted that his remaining forces were less than that required to make up a Blaze.<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>
