'''Crux''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="SW" /> who was trapped in a pit. When Crux escaped, the human was tossed back into the pit by [[Stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Pit" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Crux appears in "[[The Pit (short film)|The Pit]]," an episode of ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]''.<ref name="Pit" /> His name comes from [[Wikipedia:Crux (climbing)|crux]], a term used to describe the hardest section of a climbing route.<ref name="Name">{{SW|url=video/directors-leandre-thomas-and-justin-ridge-on-the-pit-star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=Directors LeAndre Thomas And Justin Ridge On "The Pit" &#124; Star Wars: Visions Volume 2}}</ref>
