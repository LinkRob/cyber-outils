'''Falleen''' was a [[writing]] system used by [[Black Sun]], a criminal syndicate<ref name="Aphra 13" /> controlled by the [[Falleen]] [[species]].<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|falleen|Falleen}}</ref> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 13|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 13]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the Falleen [[Gender|female]] [[Xet]] made use of a [[Xet's droid|droid]] that contained Black Sun correspondence, though its codes were not in Falleen. After [[Sana Starros]] incapacitated Xet aboard the [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']], [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] [[Slicer|sliced]] into Xet's [[droid]] and discovered the encrypted codes, and determined that Xet was a [[spy]] for [[Crimson Dawn]].<ref name="Aphra 13" />
