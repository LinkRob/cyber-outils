"'''Nightfall'''" was the callsign of a [[Human]] [[First Order]] [[TIE/wi modified interceptor]] [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|pilot]] and veteran of the [[709th Legion]]. "Nightfall" once pursued an [[RZ-2 A-wing interceptor]] through rocky terrain.<ref name="NightfallCard" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Nightfall" was created for the miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'' in the card of the same name released in the [[2021]] [[Fury of the First Order Squadron Pack]].<ref name="NightfallCard" />
