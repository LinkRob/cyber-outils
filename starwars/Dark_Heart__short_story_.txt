"'''Dark Heart'''" was an online short story published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] for the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign. It was written by August and Cynthia Hahn and released on [[June 16]], [[2005]] .

==Plot summary==
During the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]], [[Darrus Jeht/Legends|Darrus Jeht]] was on the run from [[Asajj Ventress]]. He managed to bury her in the planet's muck, but she escaped to continue the chase. Jeht Force-pushed her away while he ran toward the rendezvous point with his [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]]. She manages to stab him with her lightsaber, and he responds by drawing on the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side]]. His troops arrive to rescue him, and after he is gone, Ventress contacts her master to report the success.
