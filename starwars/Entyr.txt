'''Entyr''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Kiffar]] who was a [[Padawan]] in the [[Jedi Order]] during the late [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="TCC" /> In [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> Entyr trained with fellow Padawan, [[Jecki Lon]], in a moving obstacle course in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. The two were interrupted when fellow Padawan [[Yord Fandar]] and [[Fristia]] entered the room.<ref name="TCC" />

==Personality and traits==
Entyr was a Kiffar with brown skin and [[tattoo]]s on his cheeks. He often had his hair in a braid. He was often nervous and a bit insecure.<ref name="TCC" />
