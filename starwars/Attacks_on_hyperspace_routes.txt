In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="21 BBY" /> following the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], raiders launched a series of '''attacks'''<ref name="Clone Wars English" /> against [[hyperspace]]<ref name="Clone Wars French" /> [[hyperspace route|shipping lanes]] within<ref name="Clone Wars English" /> and near the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Clone Wars French" /> In response, [[Jedi General]] [[Eeth Koth]] led his [[Eeth Koth's fleet|fleet]] to track down the perpetrators.<ref name="Clone Wars" /> However, while traveling through the [[Saleucami system]]<ref name="Lightsabers">''[[Star Wars Lightsabers: A Guide to Weapons of the Force]]''</ref> in the Outer Rim,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> the [[fleet]] was caught in a [[Capture of Eeth Koth|surprise attack]] launched by Separatist [[General]] [[Grievous]],<ref name="Clone Wars" /> who boarded Koth's [[flagship]], the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Venator-class)|''Steadfast'']], with a force of [[battle droid]]s and captured him.<ref name="Grievous">{{TCW|Grievous Intrigue}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The attacks on hyperspace routes were mentioned in "[[The Clone Wars Begin]]," a [[2021]] issue of [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] line of reference magazines.<ref name="Clone Wars" />
