The '''Core Nine mining megafacility''', or simply '''Core Nine''', was a large, dome-topped, cylinder-shaped deep core drilling site used during the heavy excavating of the [[Scar of Troithe]], a continent on the [[planet]] [[Troithe]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> Around [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">{{SW|url=news/shadow-fall-author-interview|text=In ''Shadow Fall'', the Dark Secrets of ''Alphabet Squadron'' Revealed}} establishes that ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' takes place approximately half a [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], while ''[[Before the Awakening]]'' states [[Kes Dameron]] and [[Shara Bey]] moved to [[Yavin 4]] around six [[Standard month|months]] after the Battle of [[Endor]]. Given that ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates their move to [[5 ABY]], the events of ''Shadow Fall'' must also take place around that [[time]].</ref> Core Nine was the only [[mining]] facility remaining. A remnant of the [[New Republic]]'s [[61st Mobile Infantry]] sought to use the facility as a base of operations to make hit and run attacks against the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], which had scattered their [[company]]. The [[fighter wing]]'s [[commanding officer]] [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]] learned of Core Nine when studying the remnant's possible destinations and had his forces take control of it. Subsequently, the 61st remnant launched an [[Battle of Troithe|attack]] on the 204th at the facility. The fighter wing ultimately fled aboard Core Nine's sole [[freighter]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
