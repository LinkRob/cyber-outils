'''Black Team''' was a team of [[stormtrooper]]s in the [[Galactic Empire]] that served under the command of [[General]] [[Hurst Romodi]].<ref name="Vader 22" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the team was sent alongside [[Blue Team]], [[Green Team]], and other Imperial forces to [[Skirmish on Oro Sankeria|eliminate]] presumed [[Crimson Dawn]] personnel associated with the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], but were actually caught up in a skirmish with a [[Unidentified Crimson Dawn cell|cell]] of Crimson Dawn. The team were [[Death|killed]] when the Crimson Dawn forces set off a [[pulse bomb]].<ref name="Vader 22" />
