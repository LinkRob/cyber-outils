'''Duralumin''' was a metal used to make [[speeder bike]] chassis.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>  During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] stole a [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder]] bike with a duralumin chassis from the [[Westhill Palace]] on [[Canto Bight]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Vess later paid for the services of a speeder [[Technician|mechanic]] replace the duralumin chassis of her speeder bike with a more durable [[durasteel]] chassis.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Duralumin first appeared in the [[canon]] continuity as the base speeder chassis in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

[[In universe|Out of universe]], duralumin is a lightweight alloy of aluminum.<ref name="britannica">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/technology/duralumin|text=duralumin alloy|archivedate=20241001095733}}</ref>
