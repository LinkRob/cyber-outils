The '''Aguarl system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Mbandamonte sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing" /> It was located in grid square N-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Aguarl system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The [[Aguarl system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[River of Chaos 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: River of Chaos]]'', which was written by [[Louise Simonson]], illustrated by [[June Brigman]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="RoC">[[River of Chaos 1|''River of Chaos'' 1]]</ref> on [[June 6]], [[1995]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54538/star_wars_river_of_chaos_1995_1|text=Star Wars: River of Chaos (1995) #1}}</ref> It was then first identified in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', which was written by Fry and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
