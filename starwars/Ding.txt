'''Ding''' was a [[Clone trooper|clone]] [[stormtrooper]] in service to the [[Galactic Empire]] after the [[Clone Wars]]. As part of a [[Brighthome garrison|garrison]], Ding and fellow [[Cloning|clone]] "[[Kicker]]" was assigned to clearing out [[Jedi artifact]]s from the [[Jedi]] outpost ''[[Brighthome]]'' in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. When [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] attacked the outpost, Ding tried to kill the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] by throwing a [[grenade]] at him. However, Darth Vader blocked the grenade and then [[Force power|Force-choked]] the clone to [[death]].<ref name="Dark Lord 2" />

==Biography==
During the early [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Ding and a [[Brighthome garrison|small contingent]] of [[clone trooper]]s were assigned to the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[Jedi]] [[space station]] ''[[Brighthome]]'' and tasked with taking inventory of the [[Jedi artifact]]s being held there. Bored with the assignment, Ding at one point wielded a green-bladed [[lightsaber]] and jokingly pretended to be a Jedi, but was reprimanded by a comrade, [[Kicker]]. When the space station was attacked by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], Ding and the clone stormtroopers rushed to defend the site from the [[Sith Lord]], but were ultimately overwhelmed. Ding attempted to kill Vader with a grenade, but the [[Sith]] blocked it and Force-choked the trooper to death.<ref name="Dark Lord 2" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:DingWielding-2017DarthVader2.png|thumb|left|230px|Ding jokingly pretended to be a Jedi.]]
Like all other [[clone trooper]]s, Ding, a [[Cloning|clone]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], measured at 1.83 [[meter]]s tall and was a [[human]].<ref name="Databank" /> Ding considered the assignment on board the ''[[Brighthome]]'' as dull and thought that after one hundred missions, the assignment aboard the ''Brighthome'' would be their death. To lighten up the experience, Ding took a green-bladed [[lightsaber]] and jokingly pretended to be a [[Jedi]].<ref name="Dark Lord 2" />

==Equipment==
Ding wore [[Color|white]] [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] and had bandages on both of [[Limb|arms]]. While archiving on the ''Brighthome'', Ding played around with a green-bladed lightsaber, and later made use of a [[blaster rifle]].<ref name="Dark Lord 2" />
