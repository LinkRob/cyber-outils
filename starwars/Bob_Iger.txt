'''Bob Iger''' was a [[Human]] who possessed a reserved parking zone for their [[starship]] on the [[Disney Parking Planet]]. However, [[Mickey]] took down their sign and parked their own car there. After Iger's sign fell, a sign appeared indicating that Mickey could park in that space as an exception. They had [[Color|yellow]] skin, gray [[hair]], and black [[eye]]s. Iger wore a dark [[jacket]] and a white [[Clothing|shirt]].<ref name="May12th" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bob Iger appeared in the [[Canon|non-canon]] short film ''[[The Simpsons: May the 12th Be With You]]'',<ref name="May12th" /> a [[Disney+]] special released on [[May 10]], [[2024]], in celebration of [[Wikipedia:Mother's Day|Mother's Day]].<ref name="Insta">{{Instagram|username=disneyplus|url=p/C6jkthKCMoe|name=[[Disney+]]|date=2024-05-04|quote="May the 12th Be With You", an all-new short from @TheSimpsons, arrives May 10 only on #DisneyPlus!|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/RRXsI}}</ref> In the real world, [[Bob Iger (chief executive officer)|Bob Iger]] is the [[Wikipedia:Chief executive officer|CEO]] of [[The Walt Disney Company]]. In [[2012]], Iger signed the deal with [[George Lucas]], the creator of ''[[Star Wars]]'', that transferred ownership of [[Lucasfilm]] and the ''Star Wars'' franchise to The Walt Disney Company.<ref name="New Movies Announced">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-movies-announced-as-disney-enters-agreement-to-acquire-lucasfilm-ltd|text=New Star Wars Movies Announced as Disney Enters Agreement to Acquire Lucasfilm Ltd.}}</ref>
