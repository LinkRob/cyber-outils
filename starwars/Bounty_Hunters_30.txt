'''''Bounty Hunters'' 30''' is the thirtieth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 18]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''CHAOS ON BESTINE VI! ''[''sic'']'' T'onga and her team of bounty hunters find themselves in the crosshairs of an old ally! But how will Valance react to learning the dark secret that has been kept from him? Can any of them survive the full might of a vindictive Empire?''

==Plot summary==

=== A Found Family ===
The narrative opens with a flashback to the [[Vargo Spaceport|Vargo spaceport]] on [[Varkana]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], where [[T'onga]]'s [[T'onga's crew|crew]] is unwinding. T'onga appears distracted, leading to playful banter with [[Tasu Leech]] and [[Losha Tarkon|Losha]] regarding drinks. The group discusses the bonds they've formed through their work together. [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], reflecting on his difficult past, expresses how he never expected to find another family after losing his own. T'onga reassures him, declaring, "You are not alone. You're with family now." This shared sentiment highlights their deep camaraderie before their next perilous mission.

=== Confrontation and Revelation ===
The story then shifts to the present on [[Bestine IV|Bestine]], an [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] world, where [[Beilert Valance|Valance]] confronts T'onga. T'onga, believing Valance to be dead, is shocked by his appearance and his allegiance to the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]]. Valance explains that his service to the Empire was a desperate measure to protect [[Cadeliah]] and [[Yuralla Vega|Yura]], whom he had hidden on a secret [[Hidden Rebel Base|Rebel base]]. However, the situation quickly deteriorates as Valance reveals the Empire's capture of Cadeliah, followed by the devastating news of Yura's death and the destruction of the entire Rebel base by Imperial bombardment. Valance, distraught, confesses that the Empire likely tracked Cadeliah's location through memories extracted from him, making him feel responsible for the tragedy.

=== Betrayal on Bestine ===
As Valance processes his grief and guilt, T'onga and her crew are captured by Imperial [[stormtroopers]] on the supply transport. An Imperial officer orders the execution of the "Rebel sympathizers." Enraged by the mention of Yura and the officer's casual dismissal of her death, Valance lashes out, attacking the stormtroopers. Displaying immense strength from his cybernetic enhancements, he declares, "I am--what the Empire made me!" T'onga and her crew quickly discern that the "medical supplies" Valance was tasked with delivering are, in fact, explosives intended for the destruction of a nearby village, a plot Valance was unknowingly part of.

=== A Calculated Sacrifice ===
As the situation escalates, Imperial [[TIE series|TIE]] fighters close in. Valance, utilizing his past experience as an [[Imperial cadet]], heroically engages the TIE fighters, exploiting their known flaws to destroy one with his augmented hand. [[4-LOM]] also manages to take down another. Meanwhile, on the [[Righteous Fist]] [[Star Destroyer]], Lieutenant [[Jyala Haydenn|Haydenn]] loses contact with Valance and the TIE escorts. Valance then transmits a message, appearing to confirm his safety to Haydenn, who attempts to placate him about Yura's death and encourage his continued Imperial service. However, Valance's true intentions are revealed as he diverts the explosive-laden supply transport, sending it on a collision course with the Imperial base on Bestine at full speed. He declares he will "complete the mission" by delivering the [[Thermal detonator|thermal detonators]] "sort of."

The transport crashes into the base in a catastrophic explosion, obliterating the Imperial installation. Valance, T'onga, and her crew survive the blast. T'onga questions Valance's ultimate goal in destroying the base, to which he simply replies, "Bait."

== Vader's Judgment ==
The issue concludes with the arrival of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on Bestine, who directly confronts Valance. Vader coldly states, "Beilert Valance...you have proven to be a disappointment. A betrayal that will not be tolerated."
