'''Adarians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide" /> from the [[Adari system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="Planetnamia" /> that possessed a cranial aperture that enhanced their focus and amplified sound waves. One Adarian called [[Twirm]] served as submister in [[Latts Razzi]]'s criminal [[Razzi Syndicate|syndicate]] and recorded events in the [[Smuggler's Guide]].<ref name="Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide" />
