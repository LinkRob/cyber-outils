A '''royal family''' ruled over the celestial body [[Durga (celestial body)|Durga]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Wayseeker" /> Between around [[170 BBY]] and around [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]'' take place around [[160 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:WayseekerDate|here]]. ''Wayseeker'' states that [[Vernestra Rwoh]] visited [[Durga (celestial body)|Durga]] during her travels as a [[Wayseeker]] which began a [[Standard decade|decade]] earlier, therefore meaning she traveled to Durga between around [[170 BBY]] and around 160 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> during the [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]]'s travels as a [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker]], she visited Durga and met the [[Monarchy|royal family]].<ref name="Wayseeker" /> Around 160 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Rwoh returned to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Wayseeker" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] and recounted her travels as a Wayseeker to other members of the [[Jedi Order]], including when she met the royal family on Durga.<ref name="Wayseeker" />
