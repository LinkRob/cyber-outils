'''EG-58'''<ref name="Datapad" /> was a [[Color|white]]-plated [[GNK-series power droid]]<ref name="RS" /> manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]].<ref name="Stats" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> during the [[First Order]]'s [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Batuu]], EG-58 was part of the [[Resistance]] forces stationed in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the world. The [[power droid]] worked at [[Resistance Supply]],<ref name="RS" /> where it stood with fellow [[droid]] [[EG-57]] storing power for the Resistance next to a short-range [[antenna]] near the stall.<ref name="Datapad" />

==Behind the scenes==
EG-58 first appeared as a prop within the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="RS" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The prop is capable of emitting various sound effects.<ref name="RS" /> The droid simultaneously appeared in the [[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]] application launched alongside the themed land, where it was first identified as EG-58,<ref name="Datapad" /> although ''Galaxy's Edge'' staff were originally told to refer to it as MB-58.<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|Resistance Supply|store}}</ref> The Hollywood Studios datapad menu confirms that EG-57 is the green droid out of the pair, meaning EG-58 must be the white droid.<ref name="Datapad" />
