"'''A Line in the Sand...'''" is a ''[[Star Wars Miniatures Battles]]'' scenario included in [[West End Games]]' April [[1997]] boxed set ''[[Mos Eisley Adventure Set]]''. It includes a map and punch-out paper miniatures. It takes place immediately after [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba's]] death in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] as [[Prefect/Legends|Prefect]] [[Eugene Talmont]] tries to escape [[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]].

==Plot summary==
[[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]] is in chaos after the death of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba the Hutt]]. After it is revealed that [[Prefect/Legends|Prefect]] [[Eugene Talmont]] worked with [[Swoop gang/Legends|swoop gangs]] in order to apply martial law, he tries to escape with the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]] before the [[Tatooine militia]] can arrest him.

==Continuity==
The scenario is built for three players, one for each of the factions. It can end with Prefect Talmont's escape, capture, or death.
