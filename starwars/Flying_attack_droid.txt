'''Flying attack droids''' were a type of floating, spherical [[droid]] that the [[merchant]] [[Tuut Orial]] sold from a stall on the [[asteroid]] [[Borgo Prime|Port Borgo]] around [[9 ABY]]. During a demonstration of the droids, one poked an [[Aqualish]] observer in the [[eye]] and another chased the [[Youngling|child]] [[KB]]. The droid [[SM-33]] later punched a flying attack droid out of the air while defending KB and her [[Skeleton Crew|friends]].

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|Fascinating weaponry. Absolutely novel toxicants.|Tuut Orial|audio=TuutOrial-FascinatingWeaponry-SC2.flac|<ref name="SC2" />}}
Flying attack droids were a type of small,<ref name="SC2AD">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> floating, spherical [[droid]] with two [[limb]]s, one ending in a [[circular saw]] and the other in a trio of blades. The limbs were attached to a [[metal]]lic [[Color|gray]] band that ran around the circumference of the droids' white bodies, and several red [[sensor]]s were also located on the band.<ref name="SC2" />

==History==
{{Quote|Our prices are lightyears from exorbitant and satisfaction is ensured! Buy now! Buy now! Before your enemy samples the famous bargains of Borgo.|Tuut Orial|audio=TuutOrial-BarginsOfBorgo-SC2.flac|<ref name="SC2" />}}
[[File:FlyingAttackDroidKB-SC2.png|thumb|right|300px|A flying attack droid chased KB on Port Borgo.]]
Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> the [[merchant]] [[Tuut Orial]] sold flying attack droids from a cart full of them on the [[pirate]]-infested [[asteroid]] [[Borgo Prime|Port Borgo]], demonstrating the droids' abilities to those that passed by. The merchant called the droids a bargain and advertised them as [[Death|lethal]] [[assassin]]s with fascinating weaponry and novel [[Poison|toxins]]. During one demonstration, Orial threw a piece of [[fruit]] up into the air for a droid to slice in half.<ref name="SC2" />

An [[Aqualish]] observer then poked one of the demonstration droids, which responded by poking the Aqualish in the [[eye]]. One of the droids then began pursuing the watching [[Youngling|child]] [[KB]], who backed away from it into<ref name="SC2" /> her<ref name="SC2AD" /> friend [[Fern]], after which the droid stopped following her. When a group of pirates later surrounded KB, Fern, and their [[Skeleton Crew|friends]] [[Wim]] and [[Neel]] with the intent to [[Thief|rob]] them, the droid [[SM-33]] came to their defense. SM-33 punched the pirate [[Vane]] into a stand by Orial's attack droid stall, attracting the attention of a trio of the flying attack droids, one of whom flew at SM-33 with its saw active. SM-33 then punched the attack droid<ref name="SC2" /> out of the air.<ref name="SC2AD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flying attack droids first appeared in "[[Way, Way Out Past the Barrier]],"<ref name="SC2" /> the second episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'', which aired on [[December 2]], [[2024]].<ref name="Early">{{SW|url=news/skeleton-crew-premiere-date|text=''Star Wars: Skeleton Crew'' Will Debut Early|date=2024-11-26}}</ref> Their name was not spoken in the episode<ref name="SC2" /> but was given in the audio description.<ref name="SC2AD" />
