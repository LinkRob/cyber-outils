'''Befa''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[commander]] in the [[Imperial Military|Military]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Following the Empire's [[Battle of Yavin|loss]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] at [[Yavin 4]], Befa was sent to [[Jedha]] to harvest the remaining [[kyber crystal]]s on the [[moon]]. He failed to harvest enough [[crystal]]s, and was [[Death|killed]] by Commander [[Kanchar]] for his failure.<ref name="Star Wars 38" />
