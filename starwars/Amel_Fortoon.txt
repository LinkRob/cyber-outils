{{Quote|After several years of painstaking restoration, it is my great privilege, and that of the Graf Archive, to present this never-seen trove of art to the galaxy.|Fortoon|<ref name="Galactic Atlas" />}}
'''Amel Fortoon''' was an individual who, at some point, served as the [[Director]] of the [[Graf Archive]] on the [[moon]] [[Orchis 2]]. After a trainee [[archivist]] recovered a set of [[planet]]ary maps hand-drawn by the great [[Ithorian]] [[artist]] [[Gammit Chond]] from the Archive's [[Shadow Stacks]], Fortoon spent several [[Standard year|years]] restoring them. Upon completing his work, the director published the restored maps for all [[the galaxy]] to see.<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> It was under Fortoon's tenure as director that [[Eloc Throno]] came to study cartography at the Archive.<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>
