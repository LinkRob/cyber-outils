The '''Bann Pu'dira''' was an artifact of the [[Ashiga Clan]] that dated back to the early days of the [[Melitto hive|hive]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Crimson Dawn]] [[syndicate]] stole the Bann Pu'dira from the clan on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]]. After the theft, the Dawn stored the artifact at a private club called [[The Wellspring]], which served as their headquarters on the planet. The Ashiga launched an investigation into the theft and eventually confirmed that it was in fact the Dawn that stole the artifact. As a result [[Queen]] [[Ashiga]] instituted [[decree 53795-2]] which put the [[Kijimi City]] [[spaceport]]s and as well as the city's [[Thieves' Quarter]] under surveillance.<ref name="Outlaws" />

Later the same year,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]], traveled to Kijimi looking for a [[Chadra-Fan]] explosive expert named [[Ank Parako]] in order to recruit her for a job. After finding out that Parako had been captured by the Ashiga Clan and later learning of the thief of the Bann Pu'dira, Vess decided to steal back the artifact for the clan, hoping to gain favor with the clan and get them to release Parako. Vess proceeded to sneak into The Wellspring through a vent system. Inside she disabled the security barrier around the Bann Pu'dira and retrieved the artifact. After escaping The Wellspring Vess made her way to the [[Dai Bendu Monastery|Ashiga Clan Stronghold]] where she was able to preset the artifact to the Queen in person which did increase her standing with the clan however not enough to convince the clan to release Parako.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bann Pu'dira appeared as a quest item "The Safecracker" quest of the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
