The '''AT-MP Mark III''' was a design that contained specialized modifications to the base [[All Terrain Missile Platform|AT-MP]] model. It was designed to eliminate shields with devastating volleys of missiles. However, the AT-MP Mark III had a similar grade of lighter [[armor]] that its predecessor used, which made it vulnerable to heavier attacks.<ref name="Star Wars: Commander" />
