'''Doom''' was a [[Clone trooper|Clone]] [[Clone Commander|Commander]] who served [[Jedi]] [[Jedi General|Generals]] [[Tiplee]] and her sister [[Tiplar]] in the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Aided by [[Anakin Skywalker]] and the [[501st Legion]], Doom and his [[Doom's unit|unit]] engaged droid forces led by Separatist [[Admiral]] [[Trench]] aboard the space station orbiting Ringo Vinda. During the battle, 501st clone trooper [[Tup]] was seized by a premature activation of [[Order 66|Clone Protocol 66]] caused by a malfunction in his clone inhibitor chip, and murdered Doom's Jedi General Tiplar. Droid reinforcements capitalized on the disarray caused by Tup's betrayal, forcing Doom and his men to retreat under fire and investigate their general's murder.

==Biography==
===Early Life===
Born on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] and bred from the genes of the [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]],<ref name="DB-CT" /> the [[clone trooper]] [[Clone Commander|commander]] that would eventually receive the name of Doom<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-unknown-episode-guide|text="The Unknown" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> was specifically [[Cloning|cloned]] to fight and [[Death|die]] for the [[Galactic Republic]] as a part of a massive [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone army]]. The army was first pressed into service when Republic [[Jedi]] [[First Battle of Geonosis|engaged]] the [[Separatist Droid Army|droid forces]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] on the planet [[Geonosis]], which sparked the start of the [[The galaxy|intergalactic]] [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="DB-CT" /> Doom was eventually placed under the command of the [[Jedi]] [[Jedi General|Generals]] [[Tiplar]] and her sister [[Tiplee]],<ref name="Doom Databank">{{Databank|clone-commander-doom|Clone Commander Doom}}</ref> and the troopers placed under his command adopted a [[Color|green]] color motif for their [[armor]], along with a yellow triangle to represent their unit.<ref name="The Unknown" />

===Battle of Ringo Vinda===
{{Quote|Follow me.|Tiplar, preparing Doom and the other clones for combat|<ref name="The Unknown" />}}
[[File:Tiplar_and_Doom_marines.png|thumb|left|250px|Doom and Tiplar prepare for an engagement over Ringo Vinda]]
During the war, Doom, Tiplar, and Tiplee participated in a large-scale [[Battle of Ringo Vinda|campaign]] to capture a massive [[space station]] held by [[Admiral]] [[Trench]] above the planet [[Ringo Vinda]], which was currently under the command of Trench and his [[droid]] forces. Doom's unit was joined by the [[501st Legion]], led by Jedi General [[Anakin Skywalker]], for the battle, which at some point reached a stalemate. During the intense fighting that took place aboard the [[Ringo Vinda space station|station]], much of Doom's forces were exhausted, but after several more encounters with Trench's droids, the Republic troops captured<ref name="The Unknown" /> a communications center.<ref name="Episode Guide" /> Skywalker began to pen the next stage of the battle and ordered Tiplar and Doom to take the remainder of their company down one of two passageways that led to Trench's command post. Doom noted that his men were few, which prompted Skywalker to assign [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]] and clone trooper [[Tup]], along with ten other 501st troopers, to accompany Doom and Tiplar. Tiplee would follow Skywalker and the rest of his troopers down another passageway.<ref name="The Unknown" />

The Republic resumed their assault and began closing in on Trench's command center. Tiplar and Doom fought side-by-side as they advanced, and faced minimal casualties during their trip down the passageway. Eventually, they reached Trench's command center and regrouped with Skywalker and Tiplee. However, as the fighting commenced, Tup, having entered a trance-like state, unexpectedly shot Tiplar in the back of the head. Doom witnessed the murder but was forced to continue fighting with the arrival of [[Droideka|destroyer droids]]. Meanwhile, Tup was apprehended by Fives while Tiplar's body was retrieved by Tiplee. The entire attack lost its momentum in the wake of Tiplar's death and Skywalker ordered a retreat. The defeated Republic forces regrouped in the command center they had previously captured, and Doom and his men gathered around Tiplar's body to mourn her death. Tup had killed Tiplar due to a [[Sith]]-implanted [[behavioral modification biochip]] in his head, a device which was actually installed into every clone trooper's head. Tup's chip had malfunctioned, prematurely invoking [[Order 66|Clone Protocol 66]],<ref name="The Unknown" /> which was a command that forced clone troopers to execute all Jedi for acts of [[treason]].<ref name="DB-CT" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Shields up!"<br />"Yes sir!|Tiplar's clones, including Commander Doom, acknowledge an order|<ref name="The Unknown" />}}
A skilled combatant, Doom was bred to be utterly loyal to the Galactic Republic and to obey orders unflinchingly. He knew the stakes that his job presented, and was wise enough to call for reinforcements when needed. He could also tell when the time to retreat was. Doom worked well with Master Tiplar, covering each other during the fighting. He, along with the other troopers of his company, mourned Tiplar's death, and expressed confusion at Tup's seemingly inexplicable murder.<ref name="The Unknown" />

[[File:CommanderDoom-TCW.png|thumb|right|175px|Doom in his Phase II clone trooper armor]]
Doom, like all clones, was a genetic clone of bounty hunter Jango Fett, and stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s.<ref name="DB-CT" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Destroyers!|Doom, before engaging destroyer droids with his hand blasters|<ref name="The Unknown" />}}
Doom utilized [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], along with a kama around his waist and a visor, flashlight, and an antennae and his helmet. By the time of the Ringo Vinda battle, Doom's armor was heavily battle-scarred. His armor was predominantly green, including the upper parts of his leggings, his chest plate, and the majority of his helmet. His vambrace and greave plates, as well as his boots, were gray. His kama was green with yellow stripes, and both his helmet and chest plate bore the yellow triangle emblem that the rest of his company displayed on their armor. His left shoulder pad was also yellow in color.<ref name="The Unknown" />

When it came to battlefield armament, Doom preferred using his two [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|identical]] [[blaster pistol]]s to cut down enemy forces. He used his handguns to great effect during the Ringo Vinda campaign.<ref name="The Unknown" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clone Commander Doom first appeared in previews for the [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth season]] of the TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/a-new-direction-for-lucasfilm-animation|text=A New Direction for Lucasfilm Animation}}</ref> The episode he appeared in was ''[[The Unknown]]'', which first aired on [[Netflix]] on [[February 15]], [[2014]] for German audiences,<ref name="German TV Schedule">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fernsehserien.de/star-wars-the-clone-wars/episodenguide/staffel-6/9660|text=Star Wars: The Clone Wars Staffel 6 Episodenguide|archivedate=20190419012747}}</ref> and was later released in the United States and Canada on [[March 7]], 2014. In the episode, Doom was not identified by name. He received his name in the episode's accompanying guide.<ref name="Episode Guide" /> Like all clone troopers in the series, Doom's voice was provided by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="The Unknown" /> Doom's overall design is based on the [[Marvel Comics]] villain [[Wikipedia:Doctor Doom|Doctor Doom]]<ref name="Episode Guide" /> who series supervising director [[Dave Filoni]] is a fan of<ref name="Celeb">{{SWYouTube|HrulKkPjFms|Bad Batch Star Wars: The Clone Wars Panel &#124; Star Wars Celebration Anaheim|sw_url=video/bad-batch-star-wars-the-clone-wars-panel-star-wars-celebration-anaheim}}</ref> and had at one point a statue of on his desk at [[Skywalker Ranch]].<ref name="The Clone Wars: Declassified">{{HomeVideoCite|set=Star Wars: The Clone Wars – The Lost Missions|featurette=The Clone Wars Declassified|ftext=''The Clone Wars Declassified''}}</ref>

During the 2014 San Diego Comic Con, [[Hasbro]] unveiled a new Clone Commander Doom action figure to be released as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The Black Series]]'' toy line. In the preview, Doom's accessories included the two pistols he was seen using in the TV show.<ref name="Hasbro">{{SW|url=news/sdcc-2014-hasbros-star-wars-panel-liveblog|text=SDCC 2014: Hasbro's ''Star Wars'' Panel - Liveblog}}</ref> The figure was later released during [[2015]].<ref name="Hasbro Release">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|pack=Clone Commander Doom Figure|altlink=https://www.hasbro.com/en-us/product/star-wars-the-black-series-clone-commander-doom-figure:E28F45EB-5056-9047-F528-2DDB0A920011}}</ref>
