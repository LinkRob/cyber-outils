'''Ayadra Ogo''' was a passenger aboard the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> While Ogo was aboard the [[cruiser]], an announcement was made over its [[speaker]] system informing Ogo that they had won an all&ndash;inclusive trip to the golden [[beach]]es of [[Spira]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ayadra Ogo was mentioned in the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]], published by [[Ubisoft]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> and released on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref>
