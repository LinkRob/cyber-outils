'''Ash Moon 1''' was a [[moon]] located in the [[Kartovian Formation]]. A [[Central Isopter]] [[deathshrine]] was located on the moon.<ref name="Aphra 37" /> [[Korin Aphra]] was present at an [[archaeological site]] on Ash Moon 1 when he was contacted by [[Boba Fett]]. The bounty hunter brought him a dismembered hand with a tattoo and asked him if he could provide any information about it. Aphra identified it as a rune related to a breakaway sect of [[B'omarr Order|B'omarr monks]] and told Fett that they were located on [[Javeek]].<ref name="BF:BWR3">[[Boba Fett — Black, White & Red 3|''Boba Fett — Black, White & Red'' 3]]</ref>

When the Central Isopter pilgrims shot down one of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Project Swarm]] [[probe droid]]s, [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]] investigated the moon for [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] activity. When the team arrived, they discovered it had been the pilgrims who shot down the probe, and Veers had the [[stormtrooper]] [[platoon]] use them as target practice.<ref name="Aphra 37" />
