The '''Education Regent''',<ref name="TCVDNE">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> also known as the '''Regent of Education''', was a temporary position that had the responsibility of representing all [[teacher]]s of [[Naboo]]. Once its holder's term was complete, they were expected to return to their previous career.<ref name="Altaya" />
