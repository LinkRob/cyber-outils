The '''fell swoop formation''' was a flight maneuver used by [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilots]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="TIE Fighter 1" /> the elite pilots of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] used the formation when [[Squadron Five (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Five]] were [[Mission to the Kudo system|ambushed]] by the thought-to-be-disabled [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] ''[[Celerity]]'' in the [[Kudo system]].<ref name="TIE Fighter 2" />

Sometime during the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Titan Squadron]] notably used this formation during an engagement against the [[New Republic]]'s [[Vanguard Squadron]].<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The fell swoop formation was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[TIE Fighter 2|''TIE Fighter'' 2]],<ref name="TIE Fighter 2" /> published on [[May 15]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/75573/star_wars_tie_fighter_2019_2|text=Star Wars: Tie Fighter (2019) #2}}</ref> The name made another appearance by a player controlled character's computer-generated voice in the multiplayer portion of the [[2020]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''.<ref name="Squadrons" />
