'''Faris Karan''' was a [[Theelin]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. On the astronomical object [[Blarrum]] in [[231 BBY]], the Theelin and her [[Jedi Master|Master]], [[Carana-Vey Trana]], searched for the wanted beast-[[Merchant|trader]] [[Triv Blago]] in [[Kul Kenada Spaceport]]. The Theelin and the [[Aki-Aki]] confronted Blago while the trader was having a disagreement with the [[monster hunter]] [[Ty Yorrick]], who fled and left them to the Jedi.

Between 231 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[230 BBY]], Karan and Trana attempted to stop [[Nihil]] led by the [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] from [[Raid on Sulon|raiding]] a [[SoroSuub Research Facility|facility]] on the [[moon]] [[Sulon]]. Trana was quickly knocked unconscious, prompting Karan to hold her [[lightsaber]] to the neck of the Nihil [[Quin Carree]] to compel Dee to call off [[scav droid]]s attacking the facility. Though Dee complied, Karan's behavior became increasingly erratic due to the effects of the nearby [[Living Force|Force]]-[[Diet|eating]] [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature&mdash;the [[Great Leveler|Leveler]]&mdash;until Carree became frustrated and knocked the Padawan out. When [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] encountered the group, he let the Leveler eat Trana and then shot Karan, [[Death|killing]] her.

==Biography==
===Blarrum===
{{Quote|I should've known something was wrong when you hired me rather than going to the authorities. No wonder the Jays are looking for you. Looks to me like you ''deserve'' each other.|Ty Yorrick leaves Triv Blago to the Jedi|<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />}}
Faris Karan<ref name="Tempest Breaker" /> was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Theelin]] who served as a [[Jedi]]<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" /> [[Padawan]]<ref name="Tempest Breaker" /> of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak 1|''The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak'' 1]] to [[231 BBY]].</ref> Karan and<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" /> her [[Aki-Aki]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Carana-Vey Trana]]<ref name="Tempest Breaker" /> sought to apprehend the [[Faust]] beast-[[Merchant|trader]] [[Triv Blago]], who was wanted for the illegal capture and [[transport]]ation of dangerous animals. The two Jedi searched for Blago in [[Kul Kenada Spaceport]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] astronomical object [[Blarrum]].<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />

[[File:JediBlagoYorrick-THRATemplePeak1.png|thumb|left|350px|The Jedi confront Triv Blago and their guards on Blarrum.]]
While the Theelin presented a [[hologram]] of Blago to a [[human]], the former Jedi&ndash;turned&ndash;[[monster hunter]] [[Ty Yorrick]], who was returning to the trader after [[Death|killing]] an escaped prize [[gundark]] they had sent her to capture, attempted to sneak past without the two Jedi [[Force sense|sensing]] her, although the Aki-Aki glanced in the direction she had traveled.<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />

Shortly afterward, the two Jedi confronted Blago as the trader was about to have their guards shoot Yorrick for her failure. The Theelin ordered Blago to surrender, and the two Jedi [[Lightsaber combat|deflected]] the [[blaster]] fire of the trader's subordinates. As the Theelin and the Aki-Aki fought the guards, Yorrick escaped on a [[speeder]] and abandoned Blago, and the two Jedi apprehended the beast-trader.<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />

===Sulon===
{{Quote|I'll give you a challenge!''"<br />"''Hmm? Well, well, well. What have we here?''"<br />"''Step away from him.''"<br />"''Let me guess. You're his Padawan. A brave one, too, considering you have your lightsaber at my friend's throat.|Faris Karan and Lourna Dee, on Sulon|<ref name="Tempest Breaker" />}}
Between 231 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Death date" /> Karan and Trana were present on<ref name="Tempest Breaker" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="Scarif">{{EncyclopediaCite|Scarif and Other Planets in the Outer Rim}}</ref> [[Sulon]]. They encountered the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] and her [[Lourna Dee's Tempest|companion]], the Nihil [[Pirate|marauder]] [[Quin Carree]], who were [[Raid on Sulon|raiding]] a [[SoroSuub Research Facility|facility]] operated by the [[SoroSuub Corporation]] for [[weapon]]s and other valuables. After a brief skirmish, Dee knocked Trana unconscious using a [[plasma shield]]. As Dee taunted the motionless Jedi, Karan crept up behind Carree and held her [[lightsaber]] against the Nihil's neck. Karan issued a challenge to Dee, attempting to appear calm, though Dee noticed her worry for Trana and general unsteady demeanor. The Padawan continued to insist that Dee let Trana go, though the Nihil disagreed, aiming a blaster at the unconscious Jedi instead.<ref name="Tempest Breaker" />

[[File:QuinCarree-2021THR13.png|thumb|right|250px|Faris Karan held Quin Carree (pictured) hostage with her lightsaber in an attempt to save Carana-Vey Trana.]]
Eventually, Dee agreed to call off the [[scav droid]]s attacking the facility and contacted her subordinate, [[Muglan Tarantyne]], with new orders. However, the [[Comm unit|comm]] signal was blocked, with Dee trying to boost the signal as Karan became more irate. Karan's temperament quickly became more erratic due to the effects of the [[Living Force|Force]]-[[Diet|eating]] [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature&mdash;the [[Great Leveler|Leveler]]&mdash;nearby. Dee attempted to diffuse the situation, calmly explaining to the Padawan that she had done as requested by throwing away her blaster and calling off the attacking [[droid]]s, which meant the Padawan could lower her lightsaber. However, Karan began to ramble about [[the Force]] abandoning her and blamed the situation on Dee, who insisted it was not her fault. Carree, annoyed, elbowed Karan in the [[stomach]] and then punched her, causing the Padawan to drop to the floor. Carree and Dee discussed the situation afterward, confused why the Padawan's behavior had turned erratic so quickly.<ref name="Tempest Breaker" />

[[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] soon met Dee and Carree in the facility, and the three conversed near the two Jedi, still unconscious. Ro berated Dee for being sentimental by choosing to save Carree from Karan rather than pursue victory. The Nihil then fed Trana to the Leveler and then shot Karan with a blaster, after Carree indicated the Padawan was not yet dead. The Nameless bayed in disappointment at the Padawan's death, having wanted to consume her as well.<ref name="Tempest Breaker" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">Per the reasoning [[:Template:THRContent228BBY|here]], the main events of ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]'' take place in [[228 BBY]].</ref> Dee recounted the events on Sulon&mdash;including her encounter with Karan and Trana&mdash;to the [[journalist]] [[Rhil Dairo]] as part of an interview, though she felt the story was humiliating to reminisce on.<ref name="Tempest Breaker" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|It's a trick. You're testing me. Everyone's always testing me. Master Trana. The Force.|Faris Karan, becoming increasingly erratic under the effects of a nearby Nameless|<ref name="Tempest Breaker" />}}
Faris Karan cared about Trana's well-being and was willing to confront Dee and Carree to save her [[teacher]]'s life. Upon first impression, Dee felt that the Padawan was not much of a threat, surmising that the Jedi's threat to her colleague Carree was low even though the latter was being held in place by Karan's lightsaber. Karan's behavior became increasingly erratic when affected by the Nameless on Sulon, believing she was being tested and that Dee was tricking her.<ref name="Tempest Breaker" /> Karan had [[Color|orange]] [[hair]] that she tied back in a ponytail, yellow [[eye]]s, and purple skin.<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />

==Powers and abilities==
{{Quote|How are you doing this? The Force&hellip;the Force is gone&hellip;It's abandoned me&hellip; How are you doing this?|Faris Karan, when a Nameless affected her ability to use the Force|<ref name="Tempest Breaker" />}}
As a member of the Jedi Order, Karan was a Force-sensitive [[Jedi training|trained]] to [[Force power|wield]] the Force. She was able to utilize a lightsaber.<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" /> Karan was capable of stealth endeavors, successfully sneaking up behind Carree to hold her hostage with a lightsaber. Like all Jedi, her ability to use the Force could be hampered by the presence of a Nameless creature in the near vicinity.<ref name="Tempest Breaker" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|And that's how you get a Jedi to drop their saber. An elbow to the stomach&hellip;|Quin Carree, after incapacitating Faris Karan so the Padawan would drop her lightsaber|<ref name="Tempest Breaker" />}}
The Theelin Jedi wielded a green-bladed lightsaber. She [[Clothing|wore]] [[Jedi apparel]] consisting of a brown [[cloak]] over a light brown tunic and dark brown pants, as well as a brown [[belt]] and black [[boot]]s. The Jedi carried a [[holoprojector]] to display an image of Blago while searching for the beast-trader.<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />

[[File:HighRepublicMonsterOfTemplePeak_1.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Faris Karan debuted in ''The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak'' 1.]]
Faris Karan first appeared, albeit unidentified, in the [[The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak 1|first issue]] of the [[2021]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak]]''. The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Rachael Stott]], and published by [[IDW Publishing]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]] on [[August 11]], 2021.<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>

The Padawan later appeared in flashbacks<ref name="Tempest Breaker A">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]''</ref> in the [[December 3]], [[2024]], [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="Phase" /> audio drama ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]'', also written by Scott, where she was identified as Faris Karan.<ref name="Tempest Breaker" /> Prior to the audio drama's release, Scott confirmed the then-upcoming appearance of Karan in the media in his newsletter, along with the spelling of the character's name. Karan was voiced by Cavan's daughter, [[Chloe Scott]].<ref name="Cavan">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cavletter.com/why-the-high-republic-was-always-planned-to-end-and-other-questions|text=Why the High Republic was always planned to end and other questions|author=[[Cavan Scott]]|date=2024-11-14|work=[https://www.cavletter.com cavletter.com]|archivedate=20251008071425}}</ref> After the audio drama's release, Scott commented on social media that introducing characters such as Faris Karan and Carana-Vey Trana in one part of the ''The High Republic'' initiative to then have them return in a later addition to the multimedia project was one of the joys of working on the project. When he had initially written the character into ''The Monster of Temple Peak'', he had not been aware his daughter would eventually go on to voice Karan in ''Tempest Breaker'',<ref name="Instagram" /> but was proud she had been able to make her ''[[Star Wars]]'' debut for the audio drama.<ref name="Cavan" />
