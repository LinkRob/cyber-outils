'''''Assure Our Victory!''''' was a [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] created by [[Simeon Densend]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. At the top, it said "[[Narglatch AirTech]] reminds you to:", and then in big font requested that the reader "Slow Your Speed and Share Your Speeder" with it showing a speeding [[XJ-6 airspeeder]] with two passengers inside. "Assure Our Victory!" was stated in large [[Color|yellow]] font with the "V" being stylized to include a yellow streak. The very bottom had the words "Commute", "Conserve", and "Contribute."<ref name="Propaganda" />
