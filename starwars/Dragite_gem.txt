'''Dragite gems''' were small, dull, and brittle [[kyber crystal]]s found in the [[D'olop mountain range|D'olop]] [[mountain]] range on the [[Expansion Region]] [[agriworld]] [[M'haeli]]. They were one of the most brittle kyber [[crystal]]s in existence, and were hard to work into a [[lightsaber]]. However, when they were in a lightsaber, they emitted a loud hum, and also produced a shockwave when striking a solid target. These shockwaves had the capability to disorient or harm their target. Dragite [[gem]]s were sold for 14,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="GaG" />
