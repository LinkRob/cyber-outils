A '''battle''' was fought on [[Khar Shian]]<ref name="Legacy Reborn" /> around [[3622 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> between the [[Hidden Chain]] and the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]], the [[Eternal Alliance]], and the [[Mandalorian Trat'ade]].<ref name="Legacy Reborn" />

==Prelude==
===Jadus and the Hidden Chain===
[[File:Khar Shian Fleet.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Hidden Chain fleet orbiting Khar Shian]]
Following the [[Enigma (research vessel)|''Enigma'']] [[Mission to the Enigma|incident]], [[Heta Kol]] and [[Darth Jadus]] arrived on Khar Shian with the full strength of their forces to activate [[Darth Nul]]'s machine, which had been constructed deep within [[Naga Sadow's fortress]]. The [[Fortress/Legends|fortress]] was the only structure capable of housing the machine and surviving the strain of its activation.<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

[[File:Heta and Jadus.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Darth Jadus, Heta Kol, and their forces assembled]]
As they looked on, [[Darth Rivix]] carefully placed [[Darth Nul's holocron]] into its designated position within the machine. Growing impatient, Heta Kol angrily urged Jadus to activate the machine before anything could interrupt their plans. Jadus, however, sharply rebuked her, stating that the machine was far too sophisticated to be treated like one of the Hidden Chain's improvised creations. He insisted that it be tested first before any full activation attempt. To carry out the test, Jadus ordered that [[Sa'har Kateen|the Padawan]] be brought forward from among their four prisoners, who also included a [[Sith/Legends|Sith]], a [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]], and [[Tau Idair]]. In response, Heta signaled [[Ri'kan Kateen]] to bring the Padawan before them to which Tau attempts to resist but Rivix activates her shock collar and demanded no more outbursts as she should be relieved that she isn't dead after her attempted rescue effort.<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

[[File:Sahar pleads Rikan.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Sa'har tries to reason with her brother]]
Ri'kan hesitated at first, visibly reluctant to comply. However, after a sharp demand from Heta, he moved forward and lifted the shackled Sa'har. Desperate, Sa'har pleaded with her brother not to allow them to activate the machine. Her protests were ignored. At Rivix's insistence, Jadus' acolytes used the Force to pull her from Ri'kan's grasp and secure her upon the machine's throne. As Sa'har was restrained in place, Jadus addressed her, telling her that she should be proud. He declared that, just as she had on [[Elom (planet)/Legends|Elom]], she would soon help free countless others from what he described as the Jedi's destructive arrogance. With those words spoken, Rivix proceeded to activate the machine.<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

[[File:Nul Machine Activated.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Darth Nul's machine is activated for testing]]
As Nul's machine activated, a small energy field formed around its central structure. Moments later, two massive rings emerged from the ground, bathed in blue light, and began rotating vertically around the machine. Their movement flooded the surrounding chamber with an intense blue glow, casting shifting shadows across the fortress. Observing the process, Darth Jadus ordered that more power be directed into the machine. The increase immediately took its toll on Sa'har, causing her visible pain as the machine drew upon her connection to the Force. Unsatisfied with the results, Jadus demanded an even greater surge of power. The machine responded with a violent discharge of energy, enveloping Sa'har in crackling arcs of lightning. She cried out in agony as the torrent coursed through her body, inflicting immense suffering. The sight visibly shook Ri'kan, who could no longer remain silent. Disturbed by what he was witnessing, he turned to Heta and protested, insisting that they had gone too far and that this was too much.<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

[[File:Rikan tries to free Sahar.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Ri'kan tries to free Sa'har]]
Ri'kan berated his Field Marshal, questioning why she was following a Sith after previously denouncing them as slavers, dishonorable, and untrustworthy. Heta responded by striking Ri'kan and ordering him to be silent and step back, prompting a Hidden Chain soldier to draw a weapon on him. Unable to endure Sa'har's screams of pain any longer, Ri'kan, renounced Heta and her cause, attacked the soldier, seized his weapon, and attempted to free his sister while fighting his way past Jadus' acolytes. When confronted by four of Jadus' Sith, Ri'kan unleashed a rage-fueled Force attack that rendered them unconscious and damaged the machine to the point that it ceased functioning, freeing Sa'har from its grasp. Recovering quickly, Heta Kol attacked Ri'kan, but he initially gained the upper hand and knocked her aside before reaching Sa'har. 

[[File:Tau fights back.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Tau breaks free and retaliates]]
Tau also managed to free herself, seize a nearby Sith's lightsaber, and hold off the remaining acolytes in the chamber. As Ri'kan attempted to reach his sister, Rivix struck him with [[Force lightning/Legends|Force lightning]], though he was soon distracted when Tau damaged the machine's console. Sa'har, wanting to help her friend, was told by Tau to escape while she held off the Sith pursuers which a grief-stricken complied and left with Ri'kan who were then further pursued by a Hidden Chain squad led by [[Shauz Farr]].<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

===Shae and Malgus===
[[File:Malgus abandons Shae.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Malgus abandons Shae and her cause]]
Meanwhile, prior to the machine's activation, [[Darth Malgus]], [[Mandalore the Avenger|Shae Vizla]], and [[Mandalore the Avenger's crew|their team]] arrived on [[Khar Shian]] aboard a [[Scorpion-class Intercept Shuttle|''Scorpion''-class Intercept Shuttle]] that had successfully passed through the shield surrounding the fortress. As Malgus approached Shae, he remarked that they would finally bring an end to the conflict. However, after hearing strange voices, he abruptly made his way toward the shuttle's exit. When Shae questioned his actions, Malgus stated that there were enemies far more dangerous than Heta Kol and declared that the Field Marshal was no longer his concern, but Shae's. Abandoning his former ally in mid-mission, Malgus departed on his own. Angered by his decision, Shae declared that she and her team were on their own.<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

===The Alliance===
The [[Alliance Commander]] and [[Arn Peralun]] returned to [[Odessen]] to find a Republic fleet where [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] [[Galena Rans]], [[Admiral/Legends|Admiral]] [[Barus Rava]], [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Sal-Deron]], and [[General/Legends|General]] [[Ardinondu Dipolus Daeruun]] waiting for the duo. Upon landing on the flagship, Arn and the Commander found themselves surrounded by Republic soldiers, with [[Lana Beniko]] and [[Theron Shan]] also in custody, to which the Commander wanted to know the reason for this ordeal and Rava says that they will be asking the questions and that they better receive answers or the Commander will be dragged to [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] in chains when Rans supports this decision and blames it on the Commander's insubordination and worst of all meeting with [[Darth Jadus]] which makes them look complicit to which the Commander tried to set the record straight with Arn backing up the Commander. Lana intejects by saying that although this would impact the partnership between the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and the [[Eternal Alliance]] but for the moment they need to deal with the critical situation at hand first and sort out the details later. Rava rejects it and Rans backs him while ordering a trooper to arrest the Commander while the other troopers aim their weapons at the ready. Sal-Deron demands [[Darth Nul's holocron]] but seeing the Commander's expression she summaries that it is no longer in their possession asking the Commander of their actions. The trooper arrives with the cuffs, Lana protests, and the Commander tells the Republic to not do this especially not now to which Arn protests to Rans that is the solution to punish someone who took action and recalls how the Republic honored heroes who protected the Republic, which he credits the Commander for doing, and that now the Republic acts differently where only one decided to stand against Jadus and his machinations and the only person who can stand against Jadus to which the Commander agrees on but get interrupted by an alarm.<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

The fleet of the [[Mandalorian Trat'ade]] arrives, commanded by [[Arbiter (Mandalorians)|Arbiter]] [[Jekiah Ordo]] on the flagship the ''[[Spirit of Vengeance II]]'', surprising the Republic contingent to which the Commander adds that they will need the help of the Mandalorians. Then Jekiah, [[Rass Ordo]], [[Lane Vizla]], and four [[Mandalorian/Legends|Mandalorians]] jetpack into the flagship hangar ready to back up the Commander as Jekiah mentions they mobilized as soon as Lana alerted them. Rans tells Jekiah that the Commander is in serious trouble and that they are on their own and Jekiah replies that Mandalorians dont turn their backs on friends and gives an ultimatum: the Republic can accept the help of the other two powers against the Hidden Chain and Darth Jadus or they can stop the Commander and add more enemies to their growing list to which the Commander emphasizes their importance in the coming conflict and what would happen if they weren't there to which Arn pleads with Rans to listen to reason. General Daerun joins Arn and the Commander saying that they haven't steered the Republic cause in the wrong direction yet. Rava disapproves, Sal-Deron says that they need to stop Nul's machine from activating to which Rans relents and says that they would now need a plan and heads to the briefing room.<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

In the meeting, the Commander retells his encounter with Jadus as well as [[Tau Idair]] and that they encountered Nul's personality matrix that told them Khar Shian was the next suitable planet, after Elom, for the machine to be placed for maximum effect to which Jekiah backs up the claim from the intel he received from the Hidden Chain defector which stated that the Hidden Chain were moving supplies and equipment through Sith space at the scale of building something big. Sal-Deron interjects to say that Khar Shian is not a feasible place to build the machine and Arn adds that the only structure capable of holding the machine is [[Naga Sadow's fortress]]. Daerun expresses their lack of preparation for the coming battle especially since the majority of their forces are occupied with the [[Attack on Vinsoth|Imperial attack]] on [[Vinsoth/Legends|Vinsoth]] and that they wont be getting reinforcements. Jekiah adds that they have scouts patrolling the sector that can find the fortress' exact location and once they had it then the Mandalorians will launch a ground assault against the Hidden Chain as a divergence while a smaller team can enter the fortress to search for Jadus. The Commander will lead the team with Lana and Theron volunteering and Daerun says that the fleets will cover the team's approach. Rava protests Daerun as a general giving orders to his fleet but then Rans overrules his authority and gives the Commander a homing beacon to place on the machine so it can be targeted for destruction. With the plan solidified, the commanders are notified that the fleet is approaching Khar Shian while Jekiah receives a confirmation on the fortress' exact location with a lot of Hidden Chain around it.<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

==Battle==
The Republic and Mandalorian fleet exit hyperspace and immediately face-off against the Hidden Chain fleet on the planet, starting with the Republic opening fire followed by the Mandalorians thus beginning the battle. The strike team on a captured Hidden Chain [[Scorpion-class Intercept Shuttle|''Scorpion''-class Intercept Shuttle]] followed by three Mandalorian shuttles make their way to the fortress. But the fortress' energy shields destroys the two shuttles that attempted to pass through but luckily Hidden Chain shuttles are authorized to enter and cleared for landing.<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Khar Shian first appeared in Game Update 7.9: ''Legacy Reborn'', released on [[June 2]], [[2026]], as part of ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Legacy of the Sith]]'',<ref name="Release">{{TORweb|url=info/news/%5Bnews-category%5D/20260602-0|text=Game Update 7.9 "Legacy Reborn" is live!}}</ref> the eighth digital expansion to the [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.<ref name="Announcement">{{TORweb|url=info/news/article/20210701|text=Announcing Legacy of the Sith, the next expansion for Star Wars: The Old Republic}}</ref>

All saboteur players, upon meeting with the faction leadership, will have their double agent role exposed by their original faction after receiving credible evidence off-screen.<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

If [[Theron Shan]] was left to die at the end of the Fractured Alliances storyline, then [[T7-O1]] will take his place throughout the story.<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

For Imperial players, the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Sith Empire]] partakes in the battle with [[Darth Acina|Acina]], or [[Darth Vowrawn|Vowrawn]] depending on the player's choice in ''War for Iokath'' storyline, [[Darth Krovos]], [[Darth Xarion]], [[Anri|Major Anri]], and [[Maiya Vix]] leading the Empire's forces consisting of seven [[Harrower-class dreadnought|''Harrower''-class dreadnoughts]] and eight [[Terminus-class destroyer|''Terminus''-class destroyers]].<ref name="Into the Shadows" />

Later Krovos and Anri, aboard the ''[[Stygian Talon]]'', a [[Fury-class Imperial interceptor|''Fury''-class Imperial interceptor]], and lead the attack against the [[Hidden Chain]] fleet and prevent their ground forces from following the [[Eternal Alliance]] and the [[Mandalorian Trat'ade]].<ref name="Into the Shadows" /><ref>{{TORcite|exp=lots|type=general|The First Hurdle|Khar Shian}}</ref>
