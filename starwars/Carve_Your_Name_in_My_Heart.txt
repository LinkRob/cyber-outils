"'''Carve Your Name in My Heart'''" was a hit [[Music|song]] by [[Luleo Primoc]], a [[Gallusian]] who was a [[Musician|singing]] superstar prior to the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="USWN" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Carve Your Name in My Heart" was created by author [[Adam Bray]]<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|authoradambray|1316951811341897728|[[Adam Bray]]|quote=In Solo, Luleo Primoc sings the song "Chicken in the pot." Expanding on the idea he looks like a cooked bird, I added to his playlist in Ultimate Star Wars with the songs "Carve Your Name in My Heart" and "Your Love is Gravy." But "Stuffed With Your Love" didn't make the cut.|archivedate=20210428153704}}</ref> for the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''.<ref name="USWN" /> As the song preformed by Primoc in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', "[[Chicken in the Pot]]," already made reference to the visual similarities between the Gallusian and a cooked chicken, Bray expanded on the theme with the new song names in the book.<ref name="Twitter" />
