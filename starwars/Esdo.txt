'''Esdo''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Rodian]] who served as a [[senator]]'s aide on [[Coruscant]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Later, he was imprisoned at [[Ashmead's Lock]] [[prison]] on the planet [[Kashyyyk]]. There, he was forcibly implanted with an [[Behavioral modification biochip|inorganic biochip]] implant that turned him into an unwilling drone of [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]].<ref name="Life Debt" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> Esdo was liberated by a rebel team led by [[Norra Wexley]] and [[Han Solo]]. Later, Esdo took part in the [[attack on Chandrila]] and shot [[Commodore]] [[Kyrsta Agate]] in the face before being subdued by Norra.<ref name="Life Debt" />
