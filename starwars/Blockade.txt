A '''blockade''' was when a military or another entity stationed a [[fleet]] in [[orbit]] around a [[planet]] in order to prevent the coming and going of [[starship]]s.

==History==
===Reign of the Republic===
During the [[Emergences]], the [[Galactic Republic]] established a blockade of [[hyperspace route]]s to affected areas of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], growing the area the blockade covered whenever a new Emergence rained upon a [[star system]]. By blockading the [[hyperspace]] entry points, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] hoped to save [[starship]]s that would otherwise be traveling through hyperspace from destruction, but she did support sending [[supply ship]]s into the hyperlanes if a possible crisis began. Nevertheless, the blockade hurt the economies of various Outer Rim systems.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

The [[Corporate Alliance]] would blockade the [[planet]] [[Malastare]], leading the Republic in dispatching a [[Jedi]]-led task force that would [[Battle of Malastare Narrows|resolve the matter]]. It was presumed its [[commander]], [[Trench]], was [[Death|killed]] as well.<ref name="Cat & Mouse" /> [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|Ships]] of the [[Trade Federation fleet]] would blockade the peaceful world of [[Naboo]] in order to pressure its leader, [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], to sign a petition treaty. This would escalate into the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="The Phantom Menace" />

[[File:BlockadeOfRyloth-SL.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Confederacy-enforced Ryloth blockade]]
[[Standard year|Years]] later, during the [[Clone Wars]], [[Admiral]] Trench, the same commander from the blockade of Malastare, would lead the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance's]] [[Christophsis blockade|blockade]] of [[Christophsis]]. The [[Christophsis blockade|fleet]] would be [[Battle of Christophsis|annihilated]] after the destruction of his [[flagship]], the [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] ''[[Invincible]]'', by an experimental [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette|stealth ship]], allowing a [[Republic Navy|Republic naval]] fleet to destroy the blockade.<ref name="Cat & Mouse" /> The Separatist were able to send reinforcements and force the Republic fleet to retreat. However, Republic reinforcements under [[Grand Master|Master]] [[Yoda]] were able to force the blockade's remaining ships to retreat.<ref name="The Clone Wars (film)" />

A fleet led by [[Captain]] [[Mar Tuuk]] would [[Ryloth blockade|blockade]] [[Ryloth]] during the Separatist invasion of the planet. Tuuk's forces would be [[Battle of Ryloth|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]], who went on to liberate Ryloth.<ref name="Storm Over Ryloth">{{TCW|Storm Over Ryloth}}</ref>

The Trade Federation, under [[Sib Canay]], [[Blockade of Pantora|blockaded]] [[Pantora]] to force the Pantorans to join the Confederacy. However, the blockade was lifted when Canay was revealed to be behind the [[Kidnapping of the Papanoida daughters|kidnapping]] of Chairman [[Notluwiski Papanoida]]'s daughters, [[Chi Eekway Papanoida|Chi Eekway]] and [[Che Amanwe Papanoida|Che Amanwe]].<ref name="Sphere of Influence" />

After the [[Umbaran government]] joined the Separatist Alliance, a fleet [[Umbara blockade|blockaded]] [[Umbara]] to prevent a Republic invasion. The Republic however managed to break through it at the beginning of the [[Battle of Umbara]].<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> The blockade was later used to protect [[commander]] [[TM-171]]s [[Unidentified Separatist supply ship|supply ship]].<ref name="Plan of Dissent">{{TCW|Plan of Dissent}}</ref>

===Age of the Empire===
A [[transit chip]] could be used to bypass the [[Galactic Empire]]'s blockades.<ref name="Rabbit Hunt">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|annual=2020|story=Rabbit Hunt}}</ref> The Empire established a blockade around the planet of [[Mon Cala]] during the [[Invasion of Mon Cala|occupation]]. However, three ships of the [[Mon Cala Mercantile Fleet|Mon Cala's Mercantile Fleet]] under Admiral [[Raddus]] were able to break through the blockade and escape.<ref name="Burning Seas 5">[[Darth Vader (2017) 17|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 17]]</ref>

[[File:Ghost_approaches_blockade_of_Ibaar.png|thumb|241x241px|The ''Ghost'' at the Imperial blockade of Ibaar.]]
The Empire later established a [[Imperial blockade of Lothal|blockade]] under Admiral [[Kassius Konstantine]] around the planet of [[Lothal]]<ref name="Gathering Forces">{{Rebels|Gathering Forces}}</ref> due to heavy [[Spectres|rebel]] activity there.<ref name="secretacademy">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref> The blockade prevented entrepreneurs such as [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] from bringing equipment to Lothal.<ref name="Idiot's Array">{{Rebels|Idiot's Array (episode)}}</ref> Following the [[Action at Mustafar|destruction]] of Governor [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s flagship, the ''[[Sovereign]]'', Lord Vader tightened the blockade in order to punish Lothal and prevent the [[Spectres]] from escaping.<ref name="Siege of Lothal">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref>

Around the same time as the Lothal activity,<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> the Empire [[Cutoff of Ghorman shipping lanes|cutoff shipping lanes]] to the planet [[Ghorman]]<ref name="One Year Later">{{Andor|One Year Later}}</ref> via a blockade. While [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] was able to rally the votes needed to overturn the blockade, the planet continued to be the center of [[Suppression of Ghorman|Imperial interest]]<ref name="Ever Been">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}}</ref> thanks to its supply of [[Kalkite]].<ref name="One Year Later" /> The Empire later sent [[Imperial Security Bureau]] Agent [[Alexsandr Kallus]] and several ships to Ibaar prevent any supplies and aid from being delivered to the Ibaarian underground network. Kallus was able to stop [[Phoenix Squadron]] during their first attack, but the blockade was broken by Captain [[Hera Syndulla]] and a [[Prototype B6|prototype]] [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing starfighter]].<ref name="Wings of the Master" />

During the [[Lothal campaign]], the blockade at Lothal was strengthened by the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]] under [[Grand Admiral|Grand]] [[Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]].<ref name="Occupation">{{Rebels|The Occupation}}</ref> The Seventh Fleet [[Battle of Atollon|blockaded]] [[Atollon]] to prevent Phoenix Squadron and the [[Massassi Group]] from escaping. Commander [[Jun Sato]] attempted to use [[Danaan tactics]] to break through the blockade, but sacrificed himself and ''[[Phoenix Nest]]'' to help [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Ezra Bridger]] to get help. Fortunately, the survivng ships of both rebel cells were able to escape to [[Yavin 4]] after Bridger and [[Clan Wren]] destroyed the last [[Interdictor-class heavy cruiser|''Interdictor''-class heavy cruiser]].<ref name="0 hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref> The blockade under Thrawn's command was able to prevent Syndulla's forces from [[Attack on Lothal|destroying]] Lothal's factories.<ref name="Rebel Assault">{{Rebels|Rebel Assault}}</ref> The blockade was destroyed when the ''[[Ghost]]'' summoned the [[Purrgil]] to assist them.<ref name="Family Reunion – & Farewell">{{Rebels|Family Reunion – and Farewell}}</ref>

[[File:BrokenWingBlockade-EmpireAscendant1.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The ''[[Broken Wing]]'' combats an Imperial blockade.]]
The Empire [[Blockade of Kubindi|blockaded]] the world of [[Kubindi]] and [[Slavery|enslaved]] its population, but [[Garindan ezz Zavor]] plotted to liberate his [[homeworld]], with the [[Kubaz]] [[spy]] even buying the codes necessary to get through the blockade.<ref name="The Secrets of Long Snoot">{{StoryCite|story=The Secrets of Long Snoot|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> After [[Destruction of Alderaan|The Disaster]], the Galactic Empire blockaded the [[Alderaanian Graveyard]].<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> At some point prior to the [[Battle of Hoth]],<ref name="Date">[[Empire Ascendant 1|''Empire Ascendant'' 1]] takes place before the [[Battle of Hoth]], which is dated to [[3 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, the events of "[[Two Sides to Every Sortie]]" must take place sometime prior.</ref> [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] encountered and maneuvered around an Imperial blockade.<ref name="Empire Ascendant 1">{{StoryCite|story=Two Sides to Every Sortie|book=Empire Ascendant 1}}</ref> After the [[Battle of Endor]], Imperial [[governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] initiated the [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]].<ref name="Uprising" />

===Reign of the First Order===
{{Quote|This is Dantooine. The First Order has taken control of the planet, creating a blockade and cutting off all range communications. Three shuttles carrying a handful of Resistance recruits are trying to escape to join us. Our mission to extract the shuttles and escort them back to the ''Colossus'' without the First Order catching on. With luck, we will have minimal engagement, but there are no guarantees.|Resistance [[Commander]] [[Venisa Doza]]|<ref name="Rebuilding" />}}
During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the [[First Order]] [[Blockade of Kashyyyk|block]] [[Blockade of Dantooine|aded]] the planets of [[Kashyyyk]]<ref name="SW Adventures 27" /> and [[Dantooine]].<ref name="Rebuilding" /> Above Kashyyyk, the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' and several [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s broke through the blockade and reached the planet's surface.<ref name="SW Adventures 27" /> On Dantooine, the [[Jade Squadron]] and members of [[Ace Squadron]] arrived at the planet to get [[Resistance shuttle]]s to safety. Although [[Jace Rucklin]], who flying a [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|First Order TIE fighter]] managed to take down one of the [[shuttle]]s, the Resistance and their allies managed to successfully escort the other two craft.<ref name="Rebuilding" />
