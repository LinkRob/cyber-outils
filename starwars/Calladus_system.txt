The '''Calladus system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Ojoster sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square N-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Calladus Festival]]<ref name="Lando">[[Lando - Double or Nothing 3|''Lando - Double or Nothing'' 3]]</ref> took place in the vicinity of the Calladus [[Star system|system]].<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Battlenamia|event=[[Calladus Festival]]|system=Calladus [[Star system|system]]}}</ref>
