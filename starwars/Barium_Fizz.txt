A '''Barium Fizz''' was something that [[Barva-Zul]], a member of the [[Remainders]] of [[Moonbender Colony]], claimed fellow Remainder [[Jeq]] owed after proving him wrong. While fighting the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]], who [[Battle for Moonbender|attacked]] the colony<ref name="DM-BW&R 2" /> between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Darth Maul – Black, White & Red 2|''Darth Maul – Black, White & Red'' 2]] take place during [[Maul|Darth Maul's]] [[time]] as the [[Sith apprentice]] of [[Darth Sidious]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]].</ref> Jeq had noted that Barva-Zul had not landed a shot with their [[blaster cannon]] before Maul escaped. After the pair split up to search and Barva-Zul spotted the body of Jeq&mdash;who Maul had killed and [[Clothing|dressed]] in his [[cloak]]&mdash;the Remainder fell for the trick, believing they had hit a shot and were owed a Barium Fizz.<ref name="DM-BW&R 2" />
