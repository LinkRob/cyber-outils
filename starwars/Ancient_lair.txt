The '''Ancient lair''', or '''Ancient Creature's Lair''' was a [[cave]] used by a [[Krayt dragon]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the dragon had abandoned it leaving behind a large cave system. Which had been taken over by a bandit gang.<ref name="Outlaws" /> During 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] raided the cave to retrieve a [[krayt dragon pearl]] that was stolen by the bandits from a clan of [[Tusken]]s. Vess returned the pearl to the clan in exchange for them releasing the [[Sullustan]] gunslinger [[Hoss]], who Vess wished to recruit for a job.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ancient lair appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was featured in the main quest "The Heavy".<ref name="Outlaws" />
