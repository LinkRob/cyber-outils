'''Electro-sifters''' were devices similar to [[moisture vaporator]]s, used on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Pasaana]] by the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Aki-Aki]]. An electro-sifter would catch [[seed]]s blown by the planet's winds, allowing the Aki-Aki to use [[dustgrain farming]] to grow them. Electro-sifters also contained moisture vaporator technology, and a grid of the devices could irrigate arid desert landscape.<ref name="TROS VD" />
