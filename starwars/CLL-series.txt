The '''CLL-series''' were a line of [[binary loadlifter]]s that were so limited in intelligence that they barely qualified as [[droid]]s. The series generally handled the most mundane loading jobs, with more complex tasks requiring far more intelligent models of [[labor droid]].<ref name="BMF20" /> [[Cybot Galactica]]'s [[CLL-M2 binary loadlifter]] was one of the models in the series, and although it was only introduced shortly before the [[Separatist Crisis]], it was little changed from others of its ilk stretching back to the droid equivalent of prehistoric [[time]]s.<ref name="BR2-54">{{BuildR2Cite|54|Droid Directory}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The CLL-series first appeared in the [[2005]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] the series was identified in the [[2015]] magazine [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 20|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 20]].<ref name="BMF20" />
