'''Dunnak''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="TRS" /> located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and the [[New Territories]]'<ref name="NewTerritories" /> [[Dunnak system]],<ref name="Appendix" /> situated in grid square N-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> The [[Vaathkree Trade Corridor]]<ref name="Fry" /> [[hyperspace route]] connected it to the celestial bodies [[Corsin]] and [[Ubduria]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> Dunnak was home to the [[Docha Institute]], an institute featuring [[Docha Institute's medical library|one]] of [[the galaxy]]'s best medical [[Library|libraries]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="TRS" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] mentally considered the institute on Dunnak, comparing its medical library to the similarly advanced one aboard the [[Elite-class vessel|''Elite''-class]] research [[Starship|vessel]] ''[[Innovator]]'', a ship he was helping escort.<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dunnak was first mentioned in the novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase I and published on [[June 29]], [[2021]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> Prior to its release, Dunnak's mention was included in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the novel on [[May 21]] of that year.<ref name="Excerpt">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-high-republic-the-rising-storm-exclusive-excerpt-3|text=Bell Zettifar is at War with His Emotions in ''Star Wars: The High Republic: The Rising Storm'' - Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
