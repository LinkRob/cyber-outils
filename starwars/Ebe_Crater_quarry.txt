The '''Ebe Crater quarry''' was a quarry located in the [[Ebe Crater Valley]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="CL" /> By [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> [[stone]] [[Mining|mined]] locally from the quarry had been used in the infill of the thick adobe walls of the [[Skywalker home]] in [[Slave Quarters Row]] in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on Tatooine.<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Ebe Crater quarry was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="CL" /> from the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]] and marked the first mention of the [[Ebe Crater quarry/Legends|quarry]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
