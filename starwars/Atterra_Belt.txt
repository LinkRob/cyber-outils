The '''Atterra Belt''' was an [[asteroid field]]<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" /> located in the [[Atterra system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Kanz sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> along the [[Veragi Trade Route]].<ref name="Veragi" /> During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17]] ''[[Cobalt Hammer]]'' traveled through the Atterra Belt and engaged several [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]]. The larger asteroids in the Belt were visible from the surface of [[Atterra Bravo]], and appeared brighter than [[star]]s.<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" />
