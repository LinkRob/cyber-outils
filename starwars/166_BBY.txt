'''166 BBY''', or '''166<ref name="Cabuck" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Cabuck]], a [[Drezar]] [[Homeworld|from]] [[Drez]],<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> was [[Reproduction|born]] around that year. By [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Cabuck" /> he became manager and [[bartender]] of the [[Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop]] in [[Point Ueda]] on the [[Planet|world]] [[Ueda]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
166 BBY was indirectly mentioned in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
