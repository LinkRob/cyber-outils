'''Delphidian''' was a [[language]] spoken by members of the [[Delphidian]] [[species]].<ref name="Language" /> The species used the language when trading within their native [[Delphidian Cluster]], but could also be spoken in many dialects.<ref name="AotG">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[pirate]] [[Sidon Ithano]] conversed with his [[First Mate|first mate]] [[Quiggold]] in Delphidian<ref name="Language" /> while at [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on [[Takodana]].<ref name="The Force Awakens" />
