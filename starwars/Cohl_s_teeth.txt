"'''Cohl's teeth'''" was an expression of surprise used within [[the galaxy]]. While attempting to steal the [[brain]] of the former [[thief]] [[Zaril]] from a [[B'omarr catacombs|secret grotto]] beneath [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]'s [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]], the [[Mirialan]] [[beastmaster]] [[Dagra Van]] used the phrase after taking in the vast amount of brains stored by the [[B'omarr Order]].<ref name="Return 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Cohl's teeth" was mentioned in [[Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle 4|''Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle'' 4]], a [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="Return 4" /> on [[October 23]], [[2019]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-Clone-Wars-4-of-5/digital-comic/838781|text=Star Wars Adventures: Clone Wars #4 (of 5)|nolive=1}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]], the similarly-named [[Arwen Cohl]] was a [[Mirialan/Legends|Mirialan]] freedom fighter and well-renowned figure among [[Gender/Legends|his]] people.<ref name="Cloak of Deception">''[[Cloak of Deception]]''</ref>
