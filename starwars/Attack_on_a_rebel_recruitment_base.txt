In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Squadron Five (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Five]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] '''attacked''' a [[GR-75 medium transport]] which served as a [[Rebel recruitment base|mobile recruitment base]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Deployed by a [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]] along with a complement of [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bombers]], Squadron Five swiftly disabled the recruitment base's [[hyperdrive]] before engaging an escort of [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s as they moved to engage the [[bomber]]s. Although at least two [[TIE series|TIE]] bombers were lost, the squadron was able to pick off the [[X-wing starfighter]]s before assisting the bombers in destroying the terrorist recruitment base.<ref name="TIE Fighter 5" />
