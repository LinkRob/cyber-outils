The '''fight for the Darksaber'''<ref name="Junior Novel">[[The Book of Boba Fett Junior Novel|''The Book of Boba Fett'' Junior Novel]]</ref> was a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] between [[Paz Vizsla]] and [[Din Djarin]] that took place on the [[Mandalorian covert]] on [[Glavis Ringworld]],<ref name="Return" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> Vizsla, who was a member of [[House Vizsla]], challenged Djarin for a duel so he could regain the [[Darksaber]], a weapon whose wielder could lead the [[Mandalorian]]s, back to his [[Clan Vizsla|Clan]]. Eventually [[the Armorer]] stopped the duel when Djarin held a [[knife]] near Vizsla's throat, and asked them if they removed their helmets. When Djarin admitted that he had, the Armorer replied that he was no longer a Mandalorian, and Vizsla told him to leave. Djarin left the covert with the Darksaber.<ref name="Return" />
