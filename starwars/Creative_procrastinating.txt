'''Creative procrastinating''' was a topic of research at the [[University of Desultori]] on the<ref name="Monsters">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref> [[Mid Rim/Legends|Mid Rim]]<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Yde system/Legends|Yde system]]</ref> celestial body [[Yde]]. The [[University/Legends|university]] had four subcommittees on the subject, all of which were chaired by the local [[Alien/Legends|alien]] inventor and [[Scholar/Legends|scholar]] [[Aanxi Raanxi Laanxi]], [[Author/Legends|author]] of more than 250 treatises on the appearance and disappearance of arrivals and departures.<ref name="Monsters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Creative procrastinating was mentioned in the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that combines creature designs and photographs from various projects of [[George Lucas]], creator of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, with original text by [[Bob Carrau]].<ref name="Monsters" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in ''Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas'' is [[Canon|non-canonical]]<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref> with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref>
