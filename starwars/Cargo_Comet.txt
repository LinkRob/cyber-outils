The '''''Cargo Comet''''' was a [[Wayfarer-class medium freighter|''Wayfarer''-class medium freighter]] that was part of [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa's]] secret resistance movement. During the [[blockade of Ibaar]], Organa ordered the ''Cargo Comet'' and the ''[[Cargo Sky]]'' to be loaded with supplies and equipment and to deliver it past the [[blockade]] to the [[Ibaar rebel network|rebel network]] operating there.<ref name="Dawn" />

While Bail Organa's adoptive daughter, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], oversaw the ''Cargo Comet'' and the ''Cargo Sky'' being loaded with the cargo in [[Sanctuary Coast Starport]] on [[Alderaan]], six [[agent]]s from the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] and [[Imperial Office of Customs]] arrived at the landing bay to inspect the shipment, and requested all permits and manifests, but left empty-[[Limb|handed]].<ref name="Dawn" />
