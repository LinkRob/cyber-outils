The '''Benwabula system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Shadola sector]]<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> within [[the Slice]]<ref name="The Slice" /> portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The [[Star system|system]] was situated in grid square U-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> The system contained a location named [[Benwabula]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[flight GH-78]], was scheduled to depart the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] and stopped at both Benwabula and the [[Marleyvane shipyards|Marleyvane yards]]. However it was cancelled after the moon's [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Governor]] [[Thorden]] closed down a section of the spaceport.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Benwabula system was first mentioned the [[canon]] continuity in the article "[https://www.starwars.com/star-wars-galaxy-map The ''Star Wars'' Galaxy]" published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> Previously the location called Benwabula was mentioned in [[Aurebesh]] on a sign that players can read in the "All In" quest as part of the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref> 

In the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity the [[Benwabula system/Legends|Benwabula system]] was introduced in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
