An '''airbus''',<ref name="Thrawn" /> or '''air bus''',<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> was a type of air [[transport]] vessel found on [[planet]]s such as [[Coruscant]] during the reigns of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="AotC">{{Film|II}}</ref> and its successor states, the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="RotJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> and the [[New Republic]]. They could serve as [[public transit]].<ref name="GT:AitC">{{InsiderCite|237|Galactic Tales: Another Face in the Crowd}}</ref>

Shortly before the [[Clone Wars]], [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] traveled incognito in an airbus to board an [[AA-9 Freighter-Liner]] bound for [[Naboo]].<ref name="AotC" /> The largest of Coruscant's air buses could carry up to a thousand passengers.<ref name="CL 2016" />
