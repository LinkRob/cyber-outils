'''Avalu Porviran''' was a [[human]] [[senator]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="TA" /> in [[132 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref>

Senator Porvivan was a senator from the [[Bormea sector]] who approached the debates with an open mind.<ref name="VD">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

In 132 BBY, Proviran served on a tribunal convened at the request of [[Worus Rayencourt]] to determine whether the Senate should exercise oversight of the [[Jedi Order]]. Rayencourt convinced Supreme Chancellor [[Veeth Drellik]] to convene a tribunal, though [[Vernestra Rwoh]] ultimately convinced the tribunal that Master [[Sol]] was solely responsible for the murders. This allowed Vernestra to conceal much from the Senate and pin the blame on Sol. Since he was deceased, no one could verify this with him.<ref name="TA" />
