"'''A Taste of Adventure'''" is an article written by [[Tony Russo]] which appeared in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 7|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 7]].

The article consists of four "instant adventures", which may either stand alone or fit into a campaign:

*"[[Rebel Escape]]" (set in the [[Rebellion era]])
*"[[The Battle for Gap Nine]]" (set in the Rebellion era)
*"[[Silent Fury]]" (set in the Rebellion era)
*"[[Countdown to Disaster]]" (set in the [[New Republic era]])
