The '''Bimmiel system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> Situated at the coordinates N-4 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], Bimmiel was connected through a [[hyperspace route]] to the astronomical objects [[Birgis system|Birgis]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and [[Chanceuxi]].<ref name="Galaxy Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Bimmiel was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] boxed set for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="TFABG" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Bimmiel/Legends|Bimmiel]] was introduced in ''[[The New Jedi Order: Dark Tide I: Onslaught]]'', a [[2000]] novel authored by [[Michael A. Stackpole]] as the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars: The New Jedi Order]]'' series.<ref name="Onslaught">''[[The New Jedi Order: Dark Tide I: Onslaught]]''</ref> The planet was later first depicted in "[[Making the Map]]," an article authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] for the [[Star Wars Insider 65|sixty-fifth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine,<ref name="Making">{{InsiderCite|65|Making the Map}}</ref> which was published on [[January 21]], [[2003]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SWArchive|url=community/fanclub/mag/news20021219.html|text=''Insider'' #65 & ''Bantha Tracks'' #2}}</ref>
