'''''A New Hope - The Film Novel''''' ([[Wikipedia:French language|French]]: '''''Un nouvel espoir - Le roman du film''''') is a junior novel adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. ''A New Hope - The Film Novel'' was written by [[Lucile Galliot]] and [[Thierry Arson]], and published by [[Hachette Jeunesse]] on [[September 9]], [[2015]].<ref name="Hachette" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Civil War rages across the galaxy and Rebel forces fight against the Empire. With the help of Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi and elite pilot Han Solo, young Luke Skywalker must face his fate and learn to master the Force in order to rescue Princess Leia and destroy the absolute weapon of the Empire: the Death Star.''<ref name="Hachette" />
