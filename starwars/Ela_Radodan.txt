'''Ela Radodan''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Imperial officer|officer]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] who held the [[military rank]] of [[lieutenant]] and was part of the [[Imperial Garrison (Cloud City)|Imperial forces]] left on [[Cloud City]] after the [[Duel on Cloud City]]. Her lover, [[Tal Veridian]], was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] sympathizer. Radodan secretly informed her of Imperial activities as they conspired together to free a group of [[Tibanna]] [[gas]] [[miner]]s captured by the Empire.<ref name="The Backup Backup Plan" />
