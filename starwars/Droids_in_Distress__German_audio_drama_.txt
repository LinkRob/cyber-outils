"'''Droids in Distress'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]]: "'''Gefährliche Ware'''") is an episode of the [[German Rebels audio dramas]] adapting the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Droids in Distress]]". It was released on [[May 8]], [[2015]] by [[Universal Music Group]] as part of the album ''[[Star Wars Rebels Episode 1 - Droids in Distress / Fighter Flight]]''. The episode was written by [[Gabriele Bingenheimer]], directed by [[Marian Szymczyk]] and includes narration by [[Hanns Jörg Krumpholz]].<ref name="Kiddinx" />
