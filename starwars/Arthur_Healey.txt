'''Arthur Healey''' was a veteran industry sculptor who was mentor to [[Brian Muir]]. He worked briefly on [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] before moving onto another project and made the chest of [[droid]] [[CZ-1]].<ref name="Costumes">''[[Star Wars Costumes: The Original Trilogy]]''</ref>
