An '''electrospanner''' was a type of tool used in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> defective electrospanners were considered junk.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Electrospanners appeared in the [[canon]] continuity as an inventory item in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game, only defective electrospanners were shown and were depicted with a pile of scraps the same image the game uses for all junk. They were considered junk and could only be sold for one [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]], and did not have another function.<ref name="Outlaws" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity electrospanners were first mentioned in [[2004]] novel ''[[MedStar II: Jedi Healer]]'' by [[Michael Reaves]].<ref name="MedStar II">''[[MedStar II: Jedi Healer]]''</ref>
