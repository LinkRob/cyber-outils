'''Corwi Selgrothe''' was a [[holojournalist]] who turned to creating [[propaganda]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Formerly a reporter for the [[Imperial Press Corps]]<ref name="FACPOV" /> of [[HoloNet News]],<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> Selgrothe was recruited by the [[Galactic Empire]] with a number of other [[journalist]]s after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|fall]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="FACPOV" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Initially naive about her employer's intent, she came to realize the Imperial Press Corps simply served to create propaganda for the Empire. Selgrothe quietly left her job and went into hiding,<ref name="FACPOV" /> eventually defecting to the Rebellion following the [[Ghorman Massacre]]<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> in [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> aided by [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="FACPOV" />

Selgrothe gained fame as a rebel journalist;<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> among her achievements was capturing a [[holovid]] of [[Jyn Erso]]'s inspiring speech at [[Yavin 4]]. She was later present at the Rebel Alliance's [[Echo Base]] on [[Hoth]] to capture more footage for future propaganda. She desired to speak with the three most well-known heroes of the Rebellion, [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and [[Han Solo]], but was not able to meet with any of them&mdash;save for Solo, who was quickly dismissive&mdash;before the Empire discovered the base and [[Battle of Hoth|attacked]], forcing the rebels to evacuate. Selgrothe, who due to her recent arrival was unfamiliar with their [[Code K-one-zero|evacuation plan]], was assisted in evacuating by a rebel soldier named [[L'cayo Llem]]. Inspired by heroic actions from an average soldier, Selgrothe began to talk to her fellow evacuees after they successfully passed the Imperial fleet.<ref name="FACPOV" /> While on Hoth, Selgrothe also captured an image of the evacuation's last stages.<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> Selgrothe was also friends with the propaganda [[artist]] [[Janray Tessime]].<ref name="FACPOV" />
