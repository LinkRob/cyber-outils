The '''dark science laboratory'''<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition]]''</ref> was located in the cavernous [[Sith Citadel]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]]. The [[Sith Eternal]], a [[Sith cult]] loyal to the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], used the laboratory for [[DNA|genetic]] experimentation.<ref name="Episode IX" /> Stricken with an obsession for obtaining [[immortality]], Sidious had long considered [[bioengineering]] and [[cloning]] technology as potential routes along the path towards eternal life. To that end, the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] tasked his underlings with the harrowing mission of creating and sustaining an organic being powerful in [[the Force]] which would act as a sufficient vessel to house Sidious' dark essence.<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

In the process, the Sith Eternal coupled technology and the occult to bring forth unnatural embodiments of the dark side. Numerous [[Strand-Cast|strandcasts]] were spawned within the unholy machinery of the laboratory,<ref name="TROS EE" /> yet many resulted in disfigured abominations, unsuitable to carry out Sidious' plans. Much trial and error eventually had yielded a semi-successful case&mdash;the [[Force-sensitive]] entity known as [[Snoke]], whom Sidious later installed as the [[Supreme Leader|puppet ruler]] of the [[First Order]] in his stead. Snoke served his purpose as Sidious' proxy, training the young [[Ben Solo]] in the ways of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] of [[the Force]]. Several [[Clone template|cloned copies]] of Snoke were kept within the lab, housed in tubes of nutrient-rich fluid tended to by [[Sith cultist]]s.<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Sith Citadel lab first appeared in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the third installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]].

Later on, in [[2022]], the location appeared in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', where you can play a short minigame titled "Build-a-Snoke" in which you align three different body parts to fit Snoke's [[Clothing|attire]], and once it's completed you unlock Snoke as a playable character. In a cutscene, the infamous [[Jar Jar Binks]] can be seen inside of a [[Vat|clone vat]], waving to Kylo Ren.
