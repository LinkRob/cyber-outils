'''Chibbier''' was a [[forest]] [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Chibbier system]]. The [[bounty hunter]] [[Raslin Grace]] claimed to have visited a seedy [[Unidentified dive|dive]] on the world and accepted a [[bounty]] from the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] while there and later recounted their supposed [[time]] on the world while telling a story. By the time of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], the [[human]] [[Delta Jeet]] and her cousins worked on Chibbier cutting [[tree]]s but were laid off when the world's forests were depleted. In [[34 ABY]], Jeet&mdash;now working as a [[pilot]] on the Outer Rim planet [[Batuu]], told the [[smuggler]] [[Izal Garsea]] about her time on the forest planet and why she had left.

==Description==
{{Quote|Chibbier. A forest planet.|Delta Jeet, to Izal Garsea|<ref name="ACOF" />}}
The [[forest]] [[planet]] Chibbier was a terrestrial world<ref name="ACOF" /> located in the [[Stensen sector]]'s [[Chibbier system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> and the [[Western Reaches]],<ref name="WesternReaches" /> situated in grid square I-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Coordinates" />

==History==
{{Quote|Everyone was laid off two months ago.''"<br />"''Why?''"<br />"''No trees left means nothing to cut down.|Delta Jeet and Izal Garsea, about Chibbier|<ref name="ACOF" />}}
[[File:RaslinGraceChibbier-TallTales.png|thumb|right|225px|Raslin Grace (bottom left) claimed to have visited a Chibbier dive.]]
The [[bounty hunter]] [[Raslin Grace]] told a story to several individuals&mdash;including other [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunters]]&mdash;while in the [[Bounty Hunters Guild Social Club]] on<ref name="Tall Tales" /> the planet<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35">[[Bounty Hunters 35|''Bounty Hunters'' 35]]</ref> [[D'Assem]]<ref name="Tall Tales" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The present-[[Standard day|day]] events of "[[Tall Tales]]" take place after the [[Skirmish aboard the Acquisitor|attempted assassination of Domina Tagge]] and before [[Boushh's armor]] is taken by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] in "[[Bounty Hunted]]," events that ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively.</ref> claiming<ref name="Tall Tales" /> she<ref name="Boba Fett 4">[[Boba Fett — Black, White & Red 4|''Boba Fett — Black, White & Red'' 4]]</ref> visited a seedy [[Unidentified dive|dive]] on Chibbier at some point. According to Grace's account, while she was seated at the world's establishment, the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] had sent out a bounty on a pair of [[Unidentified Cathar twin 1|Cathar]] [[Unidentified Cathar twin 2|twins]]. Grace had viewed the bounty's details via a [[hologram]] and chosen to depart Chibbier and pursue the [[twin]]s after learning that they hailed from a [[Unidentified respected family|rich family]] and had swindled the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]]. When Grace claimed that she successfully completed the job, several of the individuals listening did not believe her.<ref name="Tall Tales" />

By the [[time]] of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], a number of workers were employed to cut down [[tree]]s on Chibbier, including the [[human]] [[Delta Jeet]] and her cousins. Eventually, the forests ran out, and all the workers were laid off. Within a [[Standard month|month]], Jeet moved to the Outer Rim planet [[Batuu]], where she worked as a [[pilot]] for the shipping company [[Ohnaka Transport Solutions]].<ref name="ACOF" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> two months after leaving Chibbier, Jeet related her time working on Chibbier to the [[smuggler]] [[Izal Garsea]], whom she had been manipulated into taking [[prisoner]] by Garsea's former associate, the smuggler [[Ana Tolla]].<ref name="ACOF" /> The same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] and [[author]] [[Eloc Throno]] included the forest world on a [[map]] in his Batuu [[Book|guidebook]], ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chibbier was first mentioned in the [[2019]] young-adult novel ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="ACOF" /> The planet first appeared in a flashback in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story "[[Tall Tales]]," written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and illustrated by [[David Baldeón]]. It was published by [[Marvel Comics]] in the one-shot comic [[Revelations (2023) 1|''Revelations'' 1]]<ref name="Tall Tales" /> on [[December 20]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/112390/star_wars_revelations_2023_1|text=STAR WARS: REVELATIONS (2023) #1}}</ref>
