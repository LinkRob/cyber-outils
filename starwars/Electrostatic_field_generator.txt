'''Electrostatic field generators''' were devices that could be used to disable [[droid]]s. Sometime prior to the [[Battle of Naboo]], a [[Unidentified pirate faction|faction]] of [[pirate]]s used makeshift electrostatic field generators to disable the [[Trade Federation Droid Army|droid defense forces]] of [[Trade Federation]] [[Kellux mining facilities|mining facilities]] in the [[Kellux system]]. The [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] then sent his [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]] to [[Death|kill]] the pirates.<ref name="Maul 1" />
