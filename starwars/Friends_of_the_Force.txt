After becoming best friends at Star Wars Celebration Chicago, hosts Brad and Sarah now invite listeners to their weekly in-depth discussions covering film, television, publishing, and other niche topics that you can only find in a galaxy far, far away.|Friends of the Force's website description|<ref name="Email" />}}
'''Friends of the Force''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]''&ndash;centric channel hosted by Brad and Sarah that first debuted on [[May 27]], [[2019]]. The podcast seeks to host deep-dive book discussions, particularly centered around ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' storytelling initiative. They also conduct interviews with the authors, cast, and crew behind ''Star Wars'', some of which are part of their #RememberingResistance online event.<ref name="Email" /> As of [[2025]], Friends of the Force has released over 280 episodes.<ref name="Episode Count" />

Several notable ''Star Wars'' creators interviewed by the channel include [[Nicholas Britell]], [[Beau Willimon]], [[Sanne Wohlenberg]], [[Emily Shkoukani]], [[Emily Swallow]], [[Paul Sun-Hyung Lee]], [[Dee Bradley Baker]], [[David Shayne]], [[Ken Cunningham]], [[Brandon Auman]], [[Christopher Sean]], [[Josh Brener]], [[Nazneen Contractor]], [[Myrna Velasco]], [[Suzie McGrath]], [[Cavan Scott]], [[Claudia Gray]], [[Daniel José Older]], [[Justina Ireland]], [[Zoraida Córdova]], [[Lydia Kang]], [[Tessa Gratton]], [[George Mann]], [[Alyssa Wong]], [[Jason Fry]], [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]], [[Emma Mieko Candon]], [[Ali Hazelwood]], [[John Jackson Miller]], [[Chris Kempshall]], [[Alexander Freed]], and [[Rebecca Roanhorse]].<ref name="Email" />

The podcast attended [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim]]<ref name="Email" />&mdash;held between [[May 26]]th and [[May 29]]th, 2022,<ref name="Preponement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-anaheim-2022-dates-changing|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim 2022 Dates Changing}}</ref> and hosted on the Podcast Stage. They hosted another panel during [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]],<ref name="Email" /> held from [[April 18]]&mdash;[[April 20|20]], 2025.<ref name="Twitter Announcement">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1645449155752480768|[[Star Wars]]|quote=see you in Japan #StarWarsCelebration|archivedate=20230410154801}}</ref>
