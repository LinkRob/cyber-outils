The '''First Order Archives''' were the archival records of the [[First Order]]. The First Order Archives contained an assortment of reference material used by the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] in his publication ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

==Description==
The First Order Archives was the name of a collection of information held by the [[First Order]]. The collection was segmented into different sections pertaining to specific subjects, and each section contained various files.<ref name="Rise" />
