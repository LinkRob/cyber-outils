The '''Dreivus system''' was located in the [[Oeiros sector]] within the [[Expansion Region]] and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> Situated in grid square P-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Dreivus]], where the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] fought and celebrated afterward<ref name="BTC" /> sometime by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate|the mention of [[Dreivus]]}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Dreivus [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published on [[StarWars.com]] alongside the article [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]."<ref name="Systems" /> Both works were written by [[Jason Fry]] and released on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
