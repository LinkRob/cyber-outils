'''G. Willow Wilson''' is a [[Wikipedia:Hugo Award|Hugo]] and [[Wikipedia:World Fantasy Award|World Fantasy Award]]&ndash;winning author best known for writing the [[2012]] novel [[Wikipedia:Alif the Unseen|''Alif the Unseen'']] and co-creating the superhero [[Wikipedia:Kamala Khan|Kamala Khan]] for [[Marvel Comics]]' [[Wikipedia:Ms. Marvel|''Ms. Marvel'']].<ref name="New Yorker" /> Wilson wrote the ''[[Star Wars]]'' comic story "[[The Bridge]]," which was published in [[Age of Resistance Special 1|''Age of Resistance Special'' 1]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Age of Resistance Special">[[Age of Resistance Special 1|''Age of Resistance Special'' 1]]</ref>
