{{Quote|What? No! I won't go into an escape pod. Not after the last time! Nothing good happens when I go into one of those awful things!|C-3PO|<ref name="Star Wars (2015) 56">[[Star Wars (2015) 56|''Star Wars'' (2015) 56]]</ref>}}
'''Escape pods''', also known as '''life pods''', were small escape crafts intended for use in emergencies. They were standard aboard all [[Realspace|deep-space]] [[starship]]s and detach from their host vessels to flee from danger.<ref name="SWEOSAOV (SE)" /> Some luxury models were fitted with [[hyperdrive]]s.<ref name="BtMF 52">{{BuildFalconCite|52|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref>

Some escape pods were designed to fly into the upper atmosphere and wait to be picked up by a ship, which is how [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] departed [[Kashyyyk]] when [[Order 66]] was declared. Some [[planet]]s had a network of escape pods that could launch into the upper atmosphere away from danger.<ref name="Databank Escape pod">{{Databank|escape-pod|Escape Pod}}</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Cal Kestis]] despised escape pods,<ref name="Star Wars Jedi: Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> which was likely caused by his experience during Order 66, in which Kestis' master, [[Jaro Tapal]], died in front of his [[Padawan]] in an escape pod after being mortally wounded by [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> Smaller ships, such as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' and other [[light freighter]]s, carried such crafts that could only accommodate one typically-sized [[humanoid]] in a fixed, immobile, and straightened position.<ref name="The Last Jedi">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

During the [[Battle of Atollon]], [[Alexsandr Kallus]] used an Imperial escape pod to escape the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]'' after incapacitating his [[stormtrooper]] escort.<ref name="ZH">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

[[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]] used an escape pod to smuggle the [[Death Star plans]] off the ''[[Tantive IV]]''. The ''[[Devastator]]''{{'s}} gunnery officers noticed the launch but chose not to fire upon the pod, presuming that it had malfunctioned as they detected no life forms aboard.<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

In order to comply with [[Imperial Senate|Senate]] investigations of possible war crimes, the Devastator would have had to provide justification in accordance with Imperial Naval Regulation 132.CAT.ch(22) to fire upon an escape pod.<ref name="Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<nowiki/>e''</ref>

At the advice of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Han Solo]] jettisoned the escape pods of the ''Millennium Falcon'' when the ship was caught in the [[tractor beam]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. He lamented their loss, as they were expensive, but recognized the value of the move as a ruse and recorded a [[captain]]'s log stating that he had abandoned the ship.<ref name="ANH Illustrated">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref>

The [[Kilji]] [[species]]' escape pods were small, metallic, and cylindrical. An individual aboard the Kilji pods could live for several [[Standard hour|hours]] before running out of [[oxygen]]. The [[Kilji war cruiser]]s were equipped with the escape pods. After being launched, they broadcast their location.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>
