'''Blizzard Force unit two-two-eight-seven''' was a unit that served in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Blizzard Force]] on<ref name="B:TC" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[Hoth]]<ref name="B:TC" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> The unit's numerical designation was acquired by the defecting Imperial [[Governor]] [[Everi Chalis]], who used the unit's identity, along with the ruse of having taken a captured [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[shuttle]], to escape the Imperial [[blockade]] on the planet shortly after the [[Battle of Hoth]] concluded.<ref name="B:TC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blizzard Force unit two-two-eight-seven was first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="B:TC" />
