'''Bastaks''' were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|non-sentient]], [[Diet|carnivorous]] [[crustacean]]s that inhabited the lower levels of the [[prison]] [[moon]] [[Substation Grimdock]]. They had a hypnotic call, which only took effect with [[eye]] contact. The call would put those who heard it into a mesmerized trance, unable to move. Their [[chitin]]ous outer layer prevented [[blaster]]s from harming them, but [[thermal detonator]]s would disorient them.<ref name="Last Shot" />
