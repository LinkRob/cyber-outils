'''Engine Roasted Tip-Yip''' was a dish made from the carcass of a [[tip-yip]] [[bird]] roasted using a [[starship]] [[engine]]. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for the dish in the main courses section of his second [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'', and claimed it was perfect for those celebrating the [[Life Day]] festival away from home.

==Description==
{{Quote|All it takes is a functionin' engine and a tip-yip&mdash;a common Endorian fowl that can be caught or bought on just about any world these days. See, tip-yip is absolutely mouthwaterin' when roasted at high temps&hellip; and there ain't no temp higher than the flames from a ship's thruster.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Life Day Cookbook''|<ref name="LDCookbook" />}}
Engine Roasted Tip-Yip was a [[Foodstuff|dish]] made by roasting the whole carcass of a [[tip-yip]], a [[Bird|fowl]] native to the [[moon]] [[Endor]] but commonly available on most worlds, until it was [[Color|brown]] using the [[Fire|flames]] of a [[starship]] [[engine]]. The bird was de-[[feather]]ed and had its [[Limb|legs]] tied together before cooking.<ref name="LDCookbook" />

==History==
{{Quote|The skin gets crispy, the meat stays juicy, and your crew will be singin' your praises (between verses of "Joh Blastoh"). You could also prepare this one in an oven, I guess&hellip; but where's the fun in that?|Strono Tuggs, ''The Life Day Cookbook''|<ref name="LDCookbook" />}}
[[File:StronoCookieTuggs.png|thumb|left|200px|alt=Strono Tuggs, wearing a green shirt and a brown apron, stands in a kitchen over a stove stirring a frying pan. In the foreground two green fish and a set of red tentacles are visible on trays.|Strono Tuggs (pictured) included Engine Roasted Tip-Yip in his cookbook.]]
The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for the dish in the main courses section of his second [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'', which was released at some point after he published his first [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]<ref name="LDCookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref>

Tuggs presented the dish as something that could easily be made to celebrate the mid-[[winter]] [[Life Day]] festival by those unable to return home for the holidays, and promised anyone who cooked it would find their whole crew singing their praises. He claimed tip-yip tasted best when roasted at high [[temperature]]s like those produced by ship thrusters, for the skin would become crispy while the meat stayed juicy. Tuggs also admitted that the dish could simply be prepared in an oven but believed that it would be less fun.<ref name="LDCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|What are you doing?''"<br />"''Engine thruster roasting. Cooks super fast.|Rose Tico and Poe Dameron|<ref name="LEGO">''[[The LEGO Star Wars Holiday Special]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Dameron-EngineRoasting.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=A LEGO version of Poe Dameron wearing a green Life Day jumper stands on top of the ''Millennium Falcon's'' engine with a tip-yip on a spit, roasting it in the engine's blue flame.|Poe Dameron roasts a tip-yip using the engines of the ''Millennium Falcon'']]
Engine Roasted Tip-Yip first appeared in ''[[The LEGO Star Wars Holiday Special]]'', a [[2020]] non-[[canon]] short film. In the film, [[Resistance]] [[General]] [[Poe Dameron]], having never cooked a tip-yip before, uses the [[YT-1300 light freighter|starship]] [[SRB42 sublight engine|engines]] of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to try and roast one quickly on a spit as part of preparations for a Life Day party on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="LEGO" />

After the [[3PO-series protocol droid|protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] activates the thrusters, Dameron's allies [[Rose Tico]] and [[Finn]] question what he is doing just as the tip-yip carcass blackens and catches fire. The [[astromech droid]] [[D-O]] then extinguishes the flaming bird before a falling [[tree]] knocks the bird from the spit. After the party guest [[Zorii Bliss]] delivers replacement tip-yips, Dameron later serves a less blackened roast tip-yip during the party.<ref name="LEGO" />

Engine Roasted Tip-Yip was then named and made canon when it appeared as a recipe in ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'', a [[2021]] canon recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe calls for a whole [[Wikipedia:Chicken as food|chicken]], softened and unsalted [[Wikipedia:Butter|butter]], minced [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic cloves]], dried [[Wikipedia:Rosemary|rosemary]], [[Wikipedia:Olive oil|extra virgin olive oil]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], half a medium [[Wikipedia:Lemon|lemon]], and a medium [[Wikipedia:Sweet onion|sweet onion]].<ref name="LDCookbook" />
