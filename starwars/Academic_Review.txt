'''Academic Review''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] adventure ''[[Decision: Almas]]''. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[October 7]], [[2004]] and took the form of a conversation started by [[mstrslcr]] who posted on the [[Cularin holonet node]] a transmission between [[Almas]] and [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] that had been sliced. Other Cularin citizens then discussed the ramifications and possible sources of the transmission, from the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]], the [[Thaereian military|Navy of Thaere]] and the [[Metatheran Cartel]].
