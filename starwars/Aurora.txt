'''Aurora''' (plural form: '''aurorae'''<ref name="DA 38" /> or '''auroras''') was a natural phenomenon known to occur on the [[planet]] [[Tiikae]]<ref name="OOTS">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref> [[Piu]],<ref name="DA 38" /> and [[Kavith III]].<ref name="Wolf Trap">{{StoryCite|story=Wolf Trap|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> Taking the appearance of [[color]]ful rainbows of radiation, they were visible in the [[Atmosphere|sky]] at night. During the [[Standard week|weeks]] she spent on Tiikae,<ref name="OOTS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> [[Sylvestri Yarrow]] regularly watched the aurora with [[Jordanna Sparkburn]].<ref name="OOTS" /> The location Piu had an annual event called the [[Giganism Sacrifice]] during which [[prayer-oolite]]s would form aurorae. On one occasion during the [[Imperial Era]], the [[scholar]] [[Korin Aphra]] witnessed the Giganism Sacrifice, seeing aurorae formed by the oolites when the sacrifice hit the troposphere. He later recounted the event to his daughter [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli]], wishing she had been there.<ref name="DA 38" />
