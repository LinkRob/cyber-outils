[[Atha Prime]] owned a '''starship''' that he used to escape [[Yoda]] and [[Vetna Mooncrest]] on [[Veeros]].<ref name="JK2" />

==History==
{{Quote|Destroy them both.|Atha Prime to his tactical droids.|<ref name="JK2" />}}
[[File:PrimeFleesVeeros-JediKnights2.png|thumb|left|250px|Atha Prime was covered by tactical droids when he fled Veeros]]
The [[Bioengineering|bio-engineer]] Atha Prime owned a starship. When Yoda and Vetna Mooncrest began investigating Veeros they were greeted by Prime's [[droid]] [[majordomo]], [[Blue-Tark]] who introduced them to Prime's bio-fields. Mooncrest was bewildered as that was not what they were told was there. Deciding to do further investigation without Atha Prime or Blue-Tark's knowledge, Yoda and Mooncrest pretended to leave Veeros, however, in actuality they stayed behind and while searching Atha Prime's lab they discovered that he was bio-enginnering [[Xerexi]], mindless, obedient and deadly creatures.<ref name="JK2" />

[[File:PrimesStarshipInterior-JediKnights2.png|thumb|right|250px|Atha Prime kept vats of Xerexi aboard his starship]]
The creatures swarmed Yoda and Vetna and were deadly enough to give both of them trouble. When Yoda noticed a balcony he left Vetna to fight off the creatures, while Yoda had a short-lived duel against Atha Prime. Prime swiftly defeated Yoda, however, Yoda and Vetna chased him to his starship where he fled Veeros, covered by [[Tactical droid|Tactical droids]]. Further Jedi were sent to Veeros to eliminate all the Xerexi, however, unbeknownst to them, Atha Prime had many Xerexi stored on his starship.<ref name="JK2" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Apex_Invader_concept_BW.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of the ''Apex Invader'', the personal starship of Atha Prime that was created for ''the Epic Continues'' toyline alongside Prime]]
Atha Prime's starship first appeared in [[Jedi Knights 2|''Jedi Knights'' 2]].<ref name="JK2" /> It had previously existed in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as the ''[[Skyriver Invader]]'', the personal starship of the genetic terrorist, [[Zeta Magnus]].<ref name="SkyeWalkers">''[[SkyeWalkers: A Clone Wars Story]]''</ref> The Skyriver Invader and Atha Prime's starship are based on the ''Apex Invader'', the personal starship of Atha Prime in the cancelled Kenner toyline ''[[the Epic Continues]]''.<ref name="TEC">''[[The Epic Continues]]''</ref>
