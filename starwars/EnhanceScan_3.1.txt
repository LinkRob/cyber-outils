The '''EnhanceScan 3.1''' was a type of [[EnhanceScan general purpose scanner]] manufactured by [[CryonCorp]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Ahsoka Date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> it was used by [[Mandalorian]] [[Sabine Wren]] as she searched the grasslands of the [[planet]] [[Peridea]] for [[Ezra Bridger]].<ref name="Part Six">{{Ahsoka|Part Six: Far, Far Away}}</ref> The only things she had with her were basic supplies and [[Sabine Wren's howler|her howler]]. Wren used this simple but effective long-range scanner to search the area and locate signs of life.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> However, the [[Bandits of Peridea]] destroyed the scanner.<ref name="Part Six" />
