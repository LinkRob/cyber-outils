'''Drol''', also known as the '''Ghost of Drol''', was the fictional [[Deity|ruler]] of [[Iego]]. The [[planet]]'s inhabitants believed that Drol was responsible for preventing them from leaving. However, the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] discovered it was actually [[laser]] [[Laser web|web]] left in place by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], and destroyed it.<ref name="Mystery of a Thousand Moons" />

{{Deity-stub}}

==History==
{{Quote|The spirit of the thousand moons, our protector and destroyer.|Amit Noloff describes Drol to the Jedi|<ref name="Mystery of a Thousand Moons" />}}
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] [[Mission to Iego|travelled]] to the [[planet]] [[Iego]] in search of a [[Reeksa]] root. Upon arrival they were greeted by [[Jaybo Hood]] and his [[army]] of [[B1-series battle droid|B1]] [[Battle droid|battle]] [[droid]]s. He informed them that the [[Iego system|system]] was "haunted" or "cursed" by the phantom ruler of Iego, Drol. Superstitiously believed to be a [[deity]] by Iegoans and referred to semantically as a ghost, Drol was in fact the [[LaserWeb Defense Station]], left behind by the [[Separatist Droid Army]] after abandoning the planet. It was surmised by the inhabitants that "He destroys anything that leaves the planet" after having fifty star [[pilot]]s, believed to be the best in [[The galaxy|The]] [[Galaxy]], [[Death|perish]] attempting to escape. Kenobi and Skywalker later discovered the true nature of the ghost by attempting to leave the planet through the [[moon]] [[Moons of Iego|belt]] aboard the ''[[Twilight]]'' and witnessing a web of [[laser]]s block their path. However, upon relaying this information back to the [[Iego council]], they were met with skepticism from the particularly zealous [[Amit Noloff]]. After the Jedi had destroyed the [[weapon]] formerly revered as a god, this same [[Quarren]] did not appear to celebrate as emphatically as his fellow citizens.<ref name="Mystery of a Thousand Moons" />
