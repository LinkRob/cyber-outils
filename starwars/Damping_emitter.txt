A '''damping emitter''' was a [[lightsaber]] emitter that had been modified to release a limited amount of energy, making the lightsaber's blade non-lethal. A damping emitter cost 2,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Damping emitters were mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
