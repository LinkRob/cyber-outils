The '''Boadil family''' was one of the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. It was strongly rivaled to the [[Great Families|Great]] [[Droc family]], and was allied with its fellow Ruling [[Irizi family|Irizi]] and [[Ufsa family|Ufsa]] families.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

The Boadil family owned a luxury passenger liner that became trapped in the upper [[atmosphere]] of a [[gas giant]] planet in Ascendancy space. The liner was endangered by its Captain, a member of the Ufsa family, and rescued by [[Junior Commander]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Mitth'raw'nuru]] and his patrol boat the ''[[Boco]]''. Thrawn saved the lives of the 8,000 Chiss aboard by shooting off the liner's luxury wings, lightening the vessel's mass enough to secure it with ''Boco'' and ''[[Parala]]''{{'s}} [[tractor beam projector]]s. The Boadil family was angry at the loss of their property, but [[Chiss family#Family politics|family politics]] prevented them from seeking justice from the Ufsa or scapegoating the [[Irizi family]] of Thrawn's commanding officer [[Mid Captain]] [[Ar'alani|Irizi'ar'alani]]. They therefore sought censure and demotion of Junior Commander Thrawn, who subsequently lost command of the ''Boco''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

The [[Diplomatic corps (Chiss Ascendancy)|diplomat]] [[Boadil'par'gasoi]] was a member of the Boadil family.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

According to [[Haplif]] of the [[Agbui]], the Boadil family seemed to switch alliances and allegiances with other [[Chiss family|Chiss families]] as the need, mood, or "possibly just the current solar cycle" required.<ref name="Chaos Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Boadil family first appeared in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first book of [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''The Ascendancy Trilogy'']] by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
