A '''flash charge''' was a device that, when activated, created a brief but intense blinding light, making it useful against enemies using weapon scopes. [[Din Djarin]] and [[Toro Calican]] used flash charges to help capture [[Fennec Shand]] on [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Chapter 5" /> in [[9 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|5}}</ref> to throw off her aim from her thermal [[scope]] of her [[785MK Firepuncher-X sniper rifle|modified MK]] [[sniper rifle]].<ref name="Chapter 5" /> One flash charge was later used by the Mandalorian to distract Calican after he betrayed him and held [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] hostage.<ref name="Chapter 5" /> It also led [[Boba Fett]] to [[Fennec Shand]].<ref name="Gathering Storm">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref>
