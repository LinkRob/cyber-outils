'''Carbon-cotton''' was a durable material that could be used to make [[Scarf|scarves]] or belts. [[Saw Gerrera]] gave [[Jyn Erso]] a dark [[Color|greenish-brown]] carbon-cotton scarf to cover up her [[Jyn's kyber pendant|kyber pendant]].<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref> The [[Con artist|grifter]] [[Haja Estree]] wore a carbon-cotton belt lined with magnets as part of his Jedi disguise when running his con on the [[planet]] [[Daiyu]].<ref name="SWE CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>
