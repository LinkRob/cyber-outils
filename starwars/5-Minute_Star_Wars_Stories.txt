'''''5-Minute Star Wars Stories''''' is a collection of eleven ''[[Star Wars]]'' children's stories. It was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[December 18]], [[2015]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Blast off into hyperspace with these 11 action-packed Star Wars tales! Jedi Master Yoda has a lightsaber showdown with the dreaded Count Dooku; Luke Skywalker and the Rebels race against time to destroy the Death Star; and the brave but lonely ''Rey ''makes a new friend when she meets the astromech droid BB-8. Each of these stories is the ideal length for reading aloud in five minutes &mdash; perfect for galactic adventures at lightspeed.''<ref name="New Books">{{SW|url=news/a-galaxy-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens-books-coming-december-18-first-look|text=A Galaxy of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Books Coming December 18 - UPDATED!}}</ref>
