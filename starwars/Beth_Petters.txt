'''Beth Petters''' was a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[pilot]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="LOTJ" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Petters participated in cleanup efforts for the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] and its subsequent [[hyperspace]] debris [[Emergences]]. During the [[40th Emergence]], Petters was on board a [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] with fellow pilot [[Marcus Augur]] as they attempted to retrieve the [[Black box|flight recorder]] of the ''[[Legacy Run]]'' [[freighter]]. The group was attacked by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders']] [[cruiser]], the [[Lourna Dee (cruiser)|''Lourna Dee'']], as the pirate [[Starship|vessel]] tried to destroy the flight recorder with a [[missile]]. However, Petter and Augur's Longbeam and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Te'Ami]]'s [[Jedi Vector]] intercepted the missile shot, [[Death|killing]] the trio. Afterward, the [[technician]] [[Keven Tarr]] and the [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] discussed the lives lost in the 40th Emergence attack.<ref name="LOTJ" />
