The '''Chopani sector''' was a [[sector]] of [[the galaxy]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It contained the [[Void of Chopani]], and was situated to the north-west of the [[Raioballo sector]], with the [[planet]] [[Lah'mu]] found on the edge of the sector.<ref name="RO Worlds" /> The Chopani sector also contained the planet [[Pabu]].<ref name="2024 Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> The sector was also close to [[Dantooine]], the [[Myto's Arrow]] [[Hyperspace route|hyperlane]], and other sectors such as [[Veragi sector|Veragi]], [[Clacis sector|Clacis]], [[Myto sector|Myto]], and [[Relgim sector|Relgim]] sectors.<ref name="RO Worlds" />
