'''Broonsie''' was a [[humanoid]] individual who served the [[Haxion Brood]] crime syndicate as a [[bounty hunter]] commando during the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]]. They wore a yellow [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] right arm, a gray shirt, a blue chest plate, a [[kama]], and a gray and blue helmet. In combat, they used an [[A280C blaster rifle]], [[flash grenade]]s, [[Repulsorlift|repulsors]] in their gauntlets, and a retractable [[Shield (physical armor)|shield]] which they often used to charge at their enemies. Broonsie was one of the bounty hunters sent to locate and capture [[Cal Kestis]] in [[14 BBY]] after the latter's escape form the Haxion Brood base on Ordo Eris.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
