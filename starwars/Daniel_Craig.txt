'''Daniel Wroughton Craig''' is an English actor best known for portraying [[Wikipedia:James Bond|James Bond]] in the films [[Wikipedia:Casino Royale (2006 film)|''Casino Royale'']] (2006), [[Wikipedia:Quantum of Solace|''Quantum of Solace'']] (2008), [[Wikipedia:Skyfall|''Skyfall'']] (2012), [[Wikipedia:Spectre (2015 film)|''Spectre'']] (2015), and [[Wikipedia:No Time to Die|''No Time to Die'']] (2021). He has also appeared in such films as [[Wikipedia:Elizabeth (film)|''Elizabeth'']] (1998), [[Wikipedia:Lara Croft: Tomb Raider|''Lara Croft: Tomb Raider'']] (2001), [[Wikipedia:Road to Perdition|''Road to Perdition'']] (2002), [[Wikipedia:Sylvia (2003 film)|''Sylvia'']] (2003), [[Wikipedia:Layer Cake (film)|''Layer Cake'']] (2004), [[Wikipedia:Munich (film)|''Munich'']] (2005), [[Wikipedia:The Golden Compass (film)|''The Golden Compass'']] (2007), [[Wikipedia:Defiance (2008 film)|''Defiance'']] (2008), [[Wikipedia:Cowboys & Aliens|''Cowboys & Aliens'']] (2011), and [[Wikipedia:The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo (2011 film)|''The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo'']] (2011).

Craig appears (uncredited) in ''[[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens]]'' (2015) as [[FN-1824|the stormtrooper]] who releases [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] from captivity on [[Starkiller Base]] after she uses a [[Jedi]] [[mind trick]] against him.<ref name="This Week! Ep6" /> Craig first appeared in a [[Lucasfilm]] production in the 1990s, when he played [[W:c:indianajones:Schiller|a German military officer]] in an episode of [[Wikipedia:The Young Indiana Jones Chronicles|''The Young Indiana Jones Chronicles'']].
