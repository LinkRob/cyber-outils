'''Crimson Jack''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[pirate]] who, during the [[First Order-Resistance War]], attempted to claim a [[bounty]] from the [[First Order]] after discovering that [[Shorr Komrrin|someone]] onboard<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5" /> the [[Purrgil-class starcruiser|''Purrgil''-class starcruiser]]<ref name="Mission to Disaster">''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]''</ref> ''[[Halcyon]]'' had sent a transmission to the [[Resistance]]. While threatening [[Captain]] [[Riyola Keevan]] over a [[hologram]] transmission, Jack stated that the First Order would pay him well to turn over the individual responsible.<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 1" /> The name "Crimson Jack" was also an alias<ref name="EG">{{SW|url=series/skeleton-crew/season-1-episode-3-episode-guide|text="Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars: Skeleton Crew}}</ref> used by [[Jod Na Nawood]], another pirate almost twenty-five years earlier.<ref name="Very Interesting">{{SkeletonCrew|Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Crimson Jack made his first [[canon]] appearance in the [[Halcyon Legacy 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Halcyon Legacy]]'', which was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Will Sliney]],<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 1" /> and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[February 2]], [[2022]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/96844/star_wars_the_halcyon_legacy_2022_1|text=Star Wars: The Halcyon Legacy (2022) #1}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Crimson Jack/Legends|Crimson Jack]] debuted in the [[Star Wars (1977) 7|seventh issue]] of the [[Star Wars (1977)|original Marvel ''Star Wars'']] comic series, written by [[Roy Thomas]] and [[Howard Chaykin]], illustrated by Chaykin, and published by Marvel<ref name="Star Wars (77) 7">[[Star Wars (1977) 7|''Star Wars'' (1977) 7]]</ref> on [[October 11]], [[1977]].<ref name="Star Wars (77) 7 date">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53797/star_wars_1977_7|text=Star Wars (1977) #7}} {{C|On-sale date is noted in backup link.}}</ref>
