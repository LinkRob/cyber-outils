The '''Colicoids''' were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]], [[insectoid]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Colla IV]]. Known for their completely emotionless and [[Death|murderous]] ways, they created the design of the three-[[Limb|legged]] [[droideka]]s in their own image.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> They also led the [[Colicoid Creation Nest]], a company that notably manufactured the [[Pistoeka sabotage droid]] and the [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident''-class assault ship]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> The [[Trade Federation]] bought shipments of droidekas from the Colicoids in exchange for exotic meats.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />

==Biology and appearance==
Colicoids were two meter tall insects wrapped in a dense chitinous shell. These terrifying creatures were very warlike, hostile, even among themselves, and extremely violent towards strangers. The Old Republic soon stopped sending representatives, not any Jedi Knights, to silence the protections of others for the excesses of the Colicoids.<ref name="Battle Droids Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Battle Droids and Other Droids of War}}</ref>

==History==
After much debate, especially about costs, the proposal was approved. The [[Neimoidian]]s tasked with creating the army droids searched for a weapons manufacturer. Finally, the contract was awarded to the Colicoids of Colla IV, far from the nucleus of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]].<ref name="Battle Droids Encyclopedia" />
