'''Dianoga omelette''' was a type of [[omelette]] edible by [[human]]s that was served at a canteen on the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|First Death Star]] [[battle station]]. The [[Foodstuff|food]] shared its name with the [[Dianoga]], a [[Limb|tentacled]] [[Species|creature]] with one [[eye]].<ref name="100Scenes" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[LEGO Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]'' establishes that the scene where the dianoga omelette is mentioned takes place during the events of {{Film|IV}}. As the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|First Death Star]] is shown to not be destroyed, the scene must take place prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]], equating to [[1 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''.</ref> aboard the [[Death Star]], a [[stormtrooper]] and a [[Death Star Trooper]] stood outside of a [[prison]] cell while the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] attempted to interrogate [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to reveal the location of the secret [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Great Temple|base]]. The stormtrooper told the other [[trooper]] that they heard the canteen was serving [[Hoth dog]]s, to which the Death Star Trooper replied that anything would be better than the dianoga omelette being served again.<ref name="100Scenes" />
