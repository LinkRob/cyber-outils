'''Arlen-Dempler Luxury Speeders''' was a boutique maker of luxury [[landspeeder]]s based on [[Iceni]]. They created the [[C-B7 Interceptor]],  a high-end and versatile product that was made with off-the-shelf parts from other manufacturers like [[SoroSuub Corporation|SoroSuub]] and [[Incom Corporation|Incom]]. This made the vehicle highly modifiable and very useful to intelligence officers. It was often used by agents operating in classier parts of the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="CaM" />
