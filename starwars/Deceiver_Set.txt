The '''Deceiver Set''' was the name given to a set of two weapons, an [[Arc Caster]] and a [[Pulse Blaster]], utilized by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Set|standard set]]. These [[weapon]]s featured a [[Color|black]], gray, and gold color scheme, wrapped in brown, white, and black straps and rope with dangling bits that had a [[bone]]-like appearance.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Deceiver Set was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Utooni's Suspicious Set, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. Unlike most Weapon Wraps in the game, they seemingly possessed no direct Costume counterpart, thus not being part of an in-game cosmetic set, though its possible they served as a secondary matching cosmetic with the "Legendary" rarity [[Dewback Deceivers]] Costume, which was paired with the [[Dewback Set]] wrap by default.<ref name="Hunters" />
