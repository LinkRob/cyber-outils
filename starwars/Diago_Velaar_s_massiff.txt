A '''massiff''' was depicted as belonging to the [[Miraluka]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Diago Velaar]], described as his "best friend," as well as a "guard." It was distinguished by the [[Color|pink]] circles around its [[eye]]s.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
This [[massiff]] was first depicted within the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. Given the dubious canonicity of the "sticker" cosmetics featured within the game, it is unclear if this massiff actually exists [[Canon|in-universe]], or if it should be treated as a fictitious character created by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] as a part of Diago's [[Massiff Sharpshooter]] [[Clothing|costume]] and persona.<ref name="Hunters" />
