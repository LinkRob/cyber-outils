The '''Cloud City Grand Prix''' was a significant<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> regional<ref name="KoF">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref> race that shared its name with [[Cloud City]] at the [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]]. The [[pilot]] [[Bo Keevil]] once flew his [[Racing starfighter|racer]], the ''[[Yellow Ace]]'', in an edition of the Cloud City Grand Prix.<ref name="SWE" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cloud City Grand Prix was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Knights of Fate]]''.<ref name="KoF" /> a [[2018]]<ref name="Way of the Warrior">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/6/21/way-of-the-warrior|text=Way of the Warrior|date=2018-06-21}}</ref> sourcebook for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]].<ref name="KoF" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the [[Cloud City Grand Prix/Legends|race]] was first mentioned in the article "[https://web.archive.org/web/20170327231516/https://www.starwars.com/news/this-is-podracing-sports-in-the-star-wars-galaxy-part-1 This Is Podracing: Sports in the ''Star Wars'' Galaxy, Part 1]," which was authored by [[Tim Veekhoven]] and [[Sander de Lange]] and published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[March 26]], [[2014]].<ref name="Article">{{SW|url=news/this-is-podracing-sports-in-the-star-wars-galaxy-part-1|series=Sports in the Star Wars Galaxy|text=Part 1: This Is Podracing|author=[[Tim Veekhoven]]; [[Sander de Lange]]|date=2014-03-26|nolive=1}}</ref> It first appeared in ''[[The Jewel of Yavin]]'', an adventure module for Fantasy Flight Games's ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]]<ref name="TJoY">''[[The Jewel of Yavin]]''</ref> that was released on [[March 31]], 2014.<ref name="Heist">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2014/3/31/the-heist-is-on|text=The Heist is On!|date=2014-03-31}}</ref>
