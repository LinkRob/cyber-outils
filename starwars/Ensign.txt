'''Ensign''' was a junior [[officer]] [[Military rank|rank]] within the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> and a low rank in its [[Imperial Navy's officer corps|naval officer corps]].<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> The rank was also used by the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

Ensigns served aboard various [[starship]]s, including [[Star Destroyer]]s.<ref name="A New Dawn" /> Five [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] of the Empire, Ensign [[Baz]] was served on the Imperial construction project [[Sentinel Base]] under [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], and was selected to serve as a midshipman aboard the Governor's personal vessel, the ''[[Carrion Spike]]''.<ref name="Tarkin" /> Three years later, Ensign [[Cauley]] served as a terminal operator on board the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Ultimatum]]'' under [[Captain]] [[Rae Sloane]] during the [[Gorse Conflict|conflict]] on the [[planet]] [[Gorse]].<ref name="A New Dawn" />
