{{Quote|If Dalna joins the Republic we'll get security and safety in our sector of the galaxy.|[[Weft]]|<ref name="AToC" />}}
The '''Dalnan sector''',<ref name="AToC" /> also known as the '''Dalna sector''',<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref> or as the '''Eiram&ndash;E'ronoh sector''',<ref name="Battle of Jedha">[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha (script)|''The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha'' script]]</ref> was a [[Sector|subsector]] of the [[Tunka sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It contained the [[planet]] [[Dalna]], home of the [[Dalnan]] people. The [[Haileap system]], containing the outpost [[Port Haileap]], was situated at its edge.<ref name="AToC" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="382 date note">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi Order]] increased its activity in the sector, much to the annoyance of "[[Elecia Zeveron|the Mother]]" as it disrupted her plans for the [[Path of the Open Hand]].<ref name="Convergence" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[bounty hunter]] [[Deva Lompop]] was [[Deva's asteroid|based]] in the Dalnan sector.<ref name="Jabba the Hutt">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Jabba the Hutt 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Jabba the Hutt'' 1]]</ref>
