'''Aklyrr Bend''' was a town on the [[forest]] [[planet]] [[Tenoo]].<ref name="The Ganguls" /> The town was named for the [[blue aklyrr]], a creature that could be found nearby.<ref name="The Wild Aklyrr">{{YJA|The Wild Aklyrr}}</ref> By around [[233 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|111A}}</ref> the town was taken over by the biker gang [[Ganguls]].<ref name="The Ganguls" />
