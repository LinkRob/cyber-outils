The '''Citizens' Fleet''', also known as the '''citizens' navy''', was an expansive [[fleet]] that was comprised of 14,000 volunteer civilian and military [[Starship|ships]] from throughout the [[Core Worlds]] and [[the galaxy]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. It was assembled by [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] to aid the Resistance in the [[Battle of Exegol]], where they faced the might of the [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[Sith Eternal military|forces]], including the [[Final Order]], led by the [[Cloning|reborn]] [[Darth Sidious]].

==History==
{{Quote|But there are more of us, Poe. There are more of us.|Lando Calrissian, to Poe Dameron|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
At first, many [[Planet|worlds]] were hesitant to aid the [[Resistance]] at the onset of its [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]] due to the recent [[Hosnian Cataclysm|collapse]] of the [[New Republic]]<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> and the advance of the First Order's conquest of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The collapse of the New Republic had seen the destruction of the [[New Republic Home Fleet]], and, although some [[task force]]s survived, they were dissolved by [[Senator]]s to protect their [[homeworld]]s.<ref name="Last Jedi Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> However, [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]'s final stand during the [[Battle of Crait]] sent a message of hope throughout the galaxy<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> that the First Order was unable to suppress.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

Following the [[Palpatine's broadcast|announcement]] of [[Darth Sidious]]' return and the [[destruction of Kijimi]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] veterans [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Chewbacca]] were dispatched to the [[Core Worlds|Core Systems]] on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' at the request of Resistance Generals [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Finn]] to send out a call for help to anyone throughout the [[galaxy]] who would be willing to aid the Resistance in their upcoming battle.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:BattleofExegolcontinues-RiseIDW.png|thumb|left|300px|The citizen's fleet rises to confront the Sith Eternal's armada.]]
After hastily amassing a combined force of 14,000 civilian and military [[starship]]s<ref name="Bucket List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-escape-part-2-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 5 Fun Facts from "The Escape - Part 2" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref> of differing classes and size that were manned by ordinary galactic citizens and Resistance friends,<ref name="Episode IX" /> the Citizens' Fleet<ref name="BTCTG">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> arrived at [[Exegol]], where the remains of the Resistance were engaged in a [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] against the [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[fleet]] of [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]], the [[Final Order]]. This was to reinforce the Resistance in their effort to prevent the massive [[Sith]] armada from deploying off of Exegol and attacking the remaining free worlds. There, they helped turn the tide of the battle and achieve victory, and, as a result, a galactic [[uprising against the First Order]] by the inhabitants of the galaxy began.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Organization==
A number of units served at Exegol including the [[Mon Calamari fleet]], [[Inferno Squad (Resistance)|Inferno Squad]], and [[Phantom Squadron]].<ref name="TROS EE" />

==Complement==
{{Quote|Look at this! Look at this.|Poe Dameron, upon seeing the fleet|<ref name="Date" />}}
[[File:Galaxy_fleet_rear.png|thumb|right|250px|The fleet was composed of many starships of different classes.]]
The Citizens' Fleet was composed of a plethora of starships of various classes that ultimately culminated in over 14,000 starships.<ref name="Bucket List" /> One of those ships was the ''[[Anodyne Two]]''.<ref name="TROS EE" />

===Capital ships===
The gargantuan civilian fleet contained a large number of [[capital ship]]s, prominently those of the [[MC-series star cruiser]] line<ref name="Episode IX" /> developed by the [[Mon Calamari]].<ref name="HC 39">{{HelmetCollectionCite|39|Databank A-Z}}</ref> These [[Star Cruiser]]s included at least twenty-five [[MC75 Star Cruiser]]s, as well as many [[MC75B Star Cruiser]]s, many [[MC75C Star Cruiser]]s, and many of other similar models with the MC75 style of [[hull]].<ref name="Ships">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Ship in the Star Wars Sequel Trilogy|video=bb3vhWXClTY|nolive=1}}</ref> [[MC80 Star Cruiser]]s also participated in the battle,<ref name="Ships FOR">{{EncyclopediaCite|Starships of the First Order and the Resistance}}</ref> as well as at least five [[MC85 Star Cruiser]]s, along with many other models that shared the MC85 hull. Additionally, there were multiple [[MC95 Star Cruiser]]s, along with [[MC95A Star Cruiser]]s, [[MC95B]] Star Cruisers, [[MC95C Star Cruiser]]s, [[MC95D Star Cruiser]]s, and [[MC95E Star Cruiser]]s. In addition to the Mon Calamari Star Cruisers, there were large scores of [[Hynestian Star Cruiser]]s that were present at Exegol as part of the fleet.<ref name="Ships" /> The large [[supertanker fuel depot]] [[space station]] known as the ''[[Colossus]]'' was also part of the fleet,<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> as was at least one restored [[Cronian battlebird]].<ref name="TROS JN" />

===Sub-capital ships===
Ships of smaller classifications, [[sub-capital ship]]s, were also a large part of the fleet's composition. Many of these ships were [[frigate]]s. At Exegol, there were at least seven [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]]s, at least fifteen [[Nebulon-C escort frigate]]s, at least twelve [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigates]], and at least fourteen [[Vakbeor-class cargo frigate|''Vakbeor''-class cargo frigates]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

Additionally, there were many [[corvette]]s, including [[CR90 corvette]]s, [[Lothalian Corvette]]s, [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunships]], [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvettes]], and at least twenty-four [[Free Virgillia-class Bunkerbuster|''Free Virgillia''-class Bunkerbusters]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> [[Transport]]s included [[GR-75 medium transport]]s and [[Gozanti Armed Transport]]s.<ref name="Ships" />

===Light craft===
[[File:Galaxy_fleet_arrives_CA.png|thumb|left|250px|The Citizens' Fleet at Exegol]]
[[Warship]]s were not the only type of vessel in the fleet, however. The fleet also boasted [[freighter]]s, [[transport]]s, [[shuttle]]s, [[gunship]]s and many other light craft. Specific freighter classes included many [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] starships from the [[VCX series]], [[YT-series]] and the [[YV-series]]. These, respectively, included at least three [[VCX-100 light freighter]]s including the ''[[Ghost]]'', a [[YT-1300 light freighter]] which was the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' and lead vessel of the fleet, as well as [[YT-2400 light freighter]]s, [[YT-2400BT freighter]]s, and [[YV-865 Aurore-class freighter|YV-865 ''Aurore''-class freighters]]. There were also at least twenty-five [[Baleen-class heavy freighter|''Baleen''-class heavy freighters]] that were manufactured from CEC. Other freighter classes included [[Darius G-class freighter|''Darius G''-class freighters]] as well as its variant the [[Mining Guild Freighter]], at least one [[Drovan Freighter]], at least one [[Ralltiiri Light Freighter]], at least two [[Sullustan Cargo Freighter]]s, at least one [[720 light freighter]], at least one [[Coruscant Freighter]], and at least one [[ZH-40 Tribune-class light freighter|ZH-40 ''Tribune''-class light freighter]]. More so, the fleet also consisted of [[TUG-b13]]s.<ref name="Ships" /><ref name="Episode IX" />

Further, the fleet also consisted of [[GX1 short hauler]]s, [[T-6 shuttle]]s,<ref name="Ships" /> [[Starspeeder 1000]]s,<ref name="AFA">{{HomeVideoCite|set=Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker (Target Exclusive)|featurette=A Final Alliance}}</ref> alongside at least one [[H-type Nubian yacht]].<ref name="TROS EE" /> Light attack craft included at least one [[Razor Assault Ship]]<ref name="Ships" /> at least one [[Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft|''Firespray-31''-class patrol and attack craft]], at least one [[Pursuer-class enforcer|''Pursuer''-class enforcer]],<ref name="TROS JN">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> [[Avandor-class fighter/transport|''Avandor''-class fighter/transports]], [[Lancer-class pursuit craft|''Lancer''-class pursuit craft]]<ref name="Ships" /> including the ''[[Nebular Kelpie]]'',<ref name="Episode IX" /> [[Auzituck anti-slaver gunship|''Auzituck'' anti-slaver gunships]], [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]], and [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber]]s.<ref name="Ships" />

===Bombers and starfighters===
In addition to the capital ships, sub-capital ships, and light craft, the Citizens' Fleet also had a large force of [[bomber]]s and [[starfighter]]s. Bombers included [[BTA-NR2 Y-wing starfighter]]s, while starfighters such as [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s, [[racing starfighter]]s including<ref name="Ships" /> the ''[[Yellow Ace]]'', ''[[Red Ace]]'', ''[[Green Ace]]'',<ref name="TROS Graphic Novel">[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker Graphic Novel Adaptation|''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker'' Graphic Novel Adaptation]]</ref> the ''[[Fireball]]'', ''[[Blue Ace]]'', and [[Jarek Yeager's racer|Yeager's ship]], [[Durosian fighter]]s, [[Z-95 Headhunter]]s, [[Fang-class Protectorate fighter|''Fang''-class fighters]], [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|''Kom'rk''-class fighter/transports]], [[N-1 starfighter]]s, [[rebel speeder]]s, [[Victor-wing Fighter]]s, [[D-wing fighter]]s, and many other vessels.<ref name="Ships" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Well, one thing we do know is that among the 14,000 ships that arrive with Lando Calrissian over Exegol in ''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker'' are the ''Fireball'', Jarek Yeager's racer, and Torra Doza's ship.|Pablo Hidalgo|<ref name="Bucket List" />}}
[[File:TRXCaseStudyv2-19.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Many of the ships were kitbashed by ILM to create variations of the same model, using color to stylize the various models as well as livery and hull plating.]]
The Citizens' Fleet first appeared in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the third and final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> Not all of the shots that ILM rendered for the movie made it into the final cut, leading to discrepancies between promotional pieces<ref name="Bucket List" /><ref name="ILM FX Reel" /> and the final cut of the movie.<ref name="Ships" /> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Pablo Hidalgo]] stated in a ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' ''[[Bucket's List]]'' article on [[StarWars.com]] that the fleet was made up of 14,000 starships.<ref name="Bucket List" /> In an [[Industrial Light & Magic]] video showcasing the visual effects of the film, it was stated that the shot when the fleet appeared at Exegol was made up of over 16,000 ships.<ref name="ILM FX Reel">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2020/02/rise-of-skywalker-star-wars-visual-effects-vfx|text=Watch the Mesmerizing VFX Reel for The Rise of Skywalker — EXCLUSIVE|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2020-02-04|archivedate=20200221133807}}</ref> This article assumes that the statement from Hidalgo is the canon number for the amount of ships in the fleet.
