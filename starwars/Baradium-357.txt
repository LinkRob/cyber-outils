'''Baradium-357''', also known by [[miner]]s as "Naughty Baby," was a chemical isotope compound of the explosive [[baradium]]. It was invented at [[Calcoraan Depot]]. Significantly more powerful than [[baradium bisulfate]], it was kept in canisters with an outer shell of an unknown toxic [[coolant]] which would produce violent irrational psychotic impulses in anyone exposed to it. For this reason, people who transported such an explosive were required to wear [[hazmat suit]]s.<ref name="A New Dawn" />

[[Count]] [[Denetrius Vidian]] had a massive storage of 357 in Calcoraan Depot, which he intended to use to destroy [[Cynda]] with a falsified plan to harvest the moon's [[thorilide]]-rich core. However, his plan was thwarted by [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Hera Syndulla]], as well as [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Captain]] [[Rae Sloane]].<ref name="A New Dawn" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a canister of Baradium-357 was valued at one hundred [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
