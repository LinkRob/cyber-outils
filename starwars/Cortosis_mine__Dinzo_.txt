The [[Galactic Empire]] maintained a secret '''cortosis mine''' on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Dinzo]]. 

== History ==
At some point during the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Galactic Empire]] established a secret mining operation on the [[planet]] [[Dinzo]] to extract [[cortosis]]. Around [[3 BBY]],<ref>{{Twitter|Jeremy_Barlow|status/838612089543376901|[[Jeremy Barlow]]|nolive=1|nobackup=1}} places "The Wrong Crowd" shortly after the events of "[[Homecoming]]," which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[3 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against the Empire, the guerrilla fighter [[Tonk]] and his [[Tonk's crew|crew]] blew the mine up, against the advice of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] agent [[Hera Syndulla]], who thought the raw cortosis could be useful to the rebel cause.<ref name="TWC" />

== Behind the scenes ==
The cortosis mine on Dinzo made its first appearance in the "[[The Wrong Crowd]]", a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] found in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 30|thirtieth issue]] of the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]] released on [[April 12]], [[2017]].<ref name="TWC" />
