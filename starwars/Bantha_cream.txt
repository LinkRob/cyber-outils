'''Bantha cream''' was a type of [[Cream/Legends|cream]] made from the [[Milk/Legends|milk]] of [[Bantha/Legends|Banthas]], normally known as [[Blue milk/Legends|blue milk]]. It was added to [[Caf/Legends|caf]] to improve taste, in [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].
