'''Adelphi''' was a terrestrial [[Beach|coastal]] [[planet]] in the [[Adelphi system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. In the early [[New Republic Era]], Adelphi hosted an [[Adelphi Base|outpost]] of the [[New Republic]] where the [[New Republic Defense Fleet Starfighter Corps|starfighter squadron]] [[Adelphi Rangers]], including [[New Republic pilot]] [[Captain]] [[Carson Teva]], were stationed.<ref name="MandoVG" /> 

During the [[High Republic Era]], a [[Unidentified town (Adelphi)|town]] with a [[Unidentified theater|theater]] existed on the planet.<ref name="Snackfrog of Doom" />

In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate" /> the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] declined to reveal the identity of his [[starship]], the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', to Teva and his wingmate [[Trapper Wolf]], who were searching the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] for holdouts of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Teva requested that Djarin follow them to the outpost at Adelphi. However, after Djarin transmitted the ''Razor Crest''{{'s}} signal, the [[pilot]]s determined the vessel had been utilized in a [[Bothan-Five incident|prisoner breakout]] aboard the [[CR-225T|New Republic Correctional Transport]] ''[[Bothan-5]]'', and Djarin attempted to escape their pursuit.<ref name="Chapter 10" /> Sometime later that year,<ref name="MandoDate" /> Teva visited a [[The Pilots' Rest|bar]] in the New Republic base on the planet, where [[Trapper Wolf]], [[Jib Dodger]] and [[Sash Ketter]] were also present. Teva received a message from [[Greef Karga]], who needed the New Republic's help with defeating [[pirate]]s. Teva was then approached by [[Garazeb Orrelios]] who doubted the New Republic's ability to assist.<ref name="Chapter 21" /> Around that year, the planet had a population of only 450&mdash;all New Republic personnel.<ref name="MandoVG" />

By [[15 ABY]],<ref name="CirimalkDate">[[Cirimalk]] existed on Adelphi before the kidnapping of [[Kadara Calrissian]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[15 ABY]].</ref> a city called [[Cirimalk]] existed located on Adelphi and the former [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[general]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] spent a few [[Standard day|days]] together with [[Calrissian family|his family]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />

==Behind the scenes==
Adelphi was first mentioned "[[Chapter 10: The Passenger]]" of [[Jon Favreau]]'s live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', directed by [[Peyton Reed]]<ref name="Chapter 10" /> and released on [[November 6]] [[2020]] on [[Disney+]].<ref name="Chapter 10 date">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-november-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in November 2020}}</ref> It first appeared in the [[2022]] novel ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> and was first pictured in the [[2023]] episode "[[Chapter 21: The Pirate]]."<ref name="Chapter 21" />
