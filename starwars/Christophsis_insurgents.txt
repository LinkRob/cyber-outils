The '''Christophsis insurgents''' were a rebel group based on the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]], affiliated with the rebellion. In [[3 BBY]], they were involved in the theft of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Hospital ship|medical frigate]], with the assistance of the frigate's captain. They had the secret backing of many members of the Christophsian government, including the [[Unidentified prime minister|prime minister]] and the planet's [[senator]]s, and were primarily based in the prime minister's home city of [[Tophen]]. When the Empire found out that the prime minister, senators and many other high government officials had supported the insurgents, it conducted a [[Arrests on Christophsis|crackdown]], arresting those government officials, executing the prime minister and "pacifying" Tophen.<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The insurgents were mentioned in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="LPOA" />
