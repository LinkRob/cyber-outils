Some [[species]] employed '''eggs''' as a means of [[Reproduction|birthing]] their [[Youngling|young]], as opposed to live birth. Additionally, eggs served as a [[Foodstuff|food]] source.

===Description===
Eggs were known to be fragile; when the [[First Order]] attempted to break through the massive door which protected the [[Resistance]] [[Crait outpost|outpost]] during the [[Battle of Crait]], [[Poe Dameron]] remarked that the enemy's [[superlaser siege cannon]] would crack the door open like an egg.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

===Reproduction===
Some [[species]] of organism gave [[Reproduction|birth]] to their offspring in eggs instead of through live birth. The egg would protect the young until they eventually hatched.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> 

The [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] hatched from eggs that resembled large jewels.<ref name="The Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref>

The species [[Querm Rybet]] to which "[[Frog Lady]]" belonged [[Reproduction|reproduced]] via external fertilization, in which one member of the species laid eggs which needed to be fertilized by another.<ref name="Chapter 10: The Passenger">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 10: The Passenger}}</ref> After hatching, tadpoles would emerge from the egg.<ref name="Chapter 11: The Heiress">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref>

The [[knobby white ice spider]]s laid eggs arranged in a wide area in caves.<ref name="Chapter 10: The Passenger"/>

===Food===
The [[Jawa]]s from [[Arvala-7]] loved to eat [[mudhorn egg]]s<ref name="Chapter 2">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 2: The Child}}</ref> and [[Hugo Durant]] had an opened [[mudhorn]] egg on the counter of his [[Hugo's|food truck]] on [[Shakari]].<ref name="Mando Trivia">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-trivia|text=''Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu'' Behind-the-Scenes Facts and Trivia - Update|date=2026-05-22|quote=Sooga! Hugo Durant keeps a mudhorn egg in his stall. Fans may remember another mudhorn egg that featured prominently in Season 1 of The Mandalorian, in Chapter 2 “The Child.”}}</ref> Eggs could also be cracked into a bowl and used as an ingredient in cooking.<ref name="Bake-Off">''[[The High Republic Adventures: Galactic Bake-Off Spectacular]]''</ref>

[[Din Grogu|Grogu]] ate unfertilized eggs belonging to that of Frog Lady.<ref name="Chapter 10: The Passenger" />
