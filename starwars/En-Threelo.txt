'''En-Threelo''' was a [[droid]] who served the [[Fylari]] of [[Ontotho]]. After the Fylari rescued [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cere Junda]], [[Neralli]] had En-Threelo watch over her. When Junda went to [[Ontotho City]] in an attempt to make contact with the [[Galactic Republic]], En-Threelo accompanied the Padawan to keep watch over her. Junda was confronted by the [[Daa Security Forces|security forces]] of the [[Daa Corporation]], and she sent En-Threelo back to warn Neralli.<ref name="Dark Temple 2" />
