'''Agorn Mountain''' was a [[mountain]] on the [[planet]] [[Ossus]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]]. A part of the western [[Eocho Mountains|Eocho Mountain range]], the [[Knossa Spaceport]] was established around the mountain to support the [[Jedi]] [[pilgrim]]s.<ref name="NoP" />
