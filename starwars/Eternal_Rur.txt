'''Eternal Rur''',<ref name="Doctor Aphra 5" /> also know as the '''Immortal Rur''',<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> was a disembodied [[Sentience|conscious]] [[artificial intelligence]] based on the [[Death|deceased]] [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[Rur]] that was stored in the [[Rur Crystal|Rur crystal]], the former [[computer]] core of the [[Ordu Aspectu]]'s [[Citadel of Rur]]. Eternal Rur was discovered by the [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] and [[Korin Aphra|her father]] during the [[twilight of the Ordu Aspectu]] in [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 5" />

Chelli Aphra doubted the sincerity of Eternal Rur's claims regarding their identity. The intelligence's claim that they were the true Rur, while Rur's organic body was inhabited by an "Evil Ghost,"<ref name="Doctor Aphra 6" /> was indeed incorrect.<ref name="Kieron Gillen Rur clarification">{{Twitter|kierongillen|status/893002945603698690|[[Kieron Gillen]]|quote=I would say that Aphra's cynicism towards the story (Riiiiiighhhhht) may be the tell. {{C|In response to: "@kierongillen Can you help us solve this debate? Between the AI and "Fake Rur," which is the "real" Rur?"}}|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/RJsef}}</ref> Rur's experiments to copy his memories resulted in an intelligence that<ref name="Doctor Aphra 6" /> mistakenly<ref name="Kieron Gillen Rur clarification" /> believed it was the original Rur.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 6" />
