'''Combat Visor''' were visors sold at [[Mubo]]'s [[Droid Depot]] on [[Batuu]]<ref name="LastCall">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge – Last Call]]''</ref> by [[34 ABY]]. They were used to target enemies during combat.<ref name="date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The game [[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge – Last Call|Tales from the Galaxy's Edge]] describes them by saying "This visor upgrade is designed specifically for combat and spotting enemies at distance. Much like the shield remotes, the onboard HUD will mark enemies with a target, making them more visible from behind obstacles."
