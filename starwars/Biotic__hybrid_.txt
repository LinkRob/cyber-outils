'''Biotics''' were a [[Hybrid/Legends|hybrid]] between a host [[Species/Legends|species]] and a species of [[Unidentified parasitic organism|parasitic organism]] found on the [[Starship/Legends|starship]], ''[[Gorgorror]]''. After the parasitic organism latched onto its host, it would slowly develop, and eventually alter its host to the point where they become the monstrous hybrids referred to as "Biotics" by the [[Mécatek]]s, the people captured by the ''Gorgorror''. Biotics were aggressive toward others and had the strength to tear people apart. They would wander the passageways of the ship in search of [[Foodstuff/Legends|food]], sometimes in groups lead by a dominant [[Gender/Legends|male]]. They would eventually split into about ten polymorphic clusters where new parasites would be reborn. There was [[Unidentified Sullustan hermit|Sullustan hermit]] who managed to stabilize the evolution of hybrids by using a [[Fungus/Legends|fungal]] serum. The Sullustan led a community of non-aggressive Biotics, pacified thanks to the fungal serum. The other, aggressive Biotics hated this community and constantly attacked it.<ref name="Cristaux" />
