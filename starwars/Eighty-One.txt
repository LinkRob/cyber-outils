'''Eighty-One''' was a [[BX-series droid commando]] hunter who was hired by [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to [[Hunt on Chandar's Folly|hunt]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], on [[Chandar's Folly]]. Eighty-One stole [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|Vader's lightsaber]] after it proved to be deadly against Tarkin's [[Wilhuff Tarkin's hunting team|team]]. However, when Vader later led the group into a cavern, he ambushed the hunters and pounced on Eighty-One from above, destroying the droid.

==Biography==
{{Quote|My hunters are good at what they do. I hired well.|Wilhuff Tarkin|<ref name="Dark Lord 18" />}}
[[File:EightyOneDestroyed-2017DarthVader18.png|thumb|left|160px|Vader destroys Eighty-One]]
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date " /> Eighty-One and eighteen other [[hunter]]s were hired by [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to assist him in his [[Hunt on Chandar's Folly|hunt]] for the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on the [[planet]] [[Chandar's Folly]]. At least two [[Standard day|days]] into the hunt, it became clear to Tarkin that they needed to take [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|Vader's lightsaber]], which was allowing the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] to easily [[Death|kill]] his hunters.<ref name="Dark Lord 18" />

On the fourth day of the hunt, Tarkin led an operation to steal the [[lightsaber]]. The hunters tracked Vader near a cliff, where he attacked them. After the [[Sith]] [[Lord]] impaled a fellow hunter, Eighty-One used it's cable to snare Vader's lightsaber and pull it from his hand, shooting at the Sith Lord at the same time. Eighty-One and the other hunters then fired on Vader together, but the Sith used his [[Force power]]s to break the neck of one, forcing the rest to retreat. Vader was able to [[Force choke]] another before the rest escaped.<ref name="Dark Lord 18" />

As Tarkin awaited the dawn of their ninth day of the hunt, Vader was spotted wearing the hide of a [[Valath]]. Tarkin told his hunters it was time to move, ordering [[Gil]] and Eighty-One to take point. The group went in search of Vader and entered a [[crystal]] ravine. Eventually the [[Chadra-Fan]] [[Hardhear]] could hear Vader's breathing. However, he could not tell where because the crystal walls made it echo. not track Vader with their hearing because of the echoing of his breathing. Tarkin ordered the hunters to run and Eighty-One watched the rear as they began moving. Vader then attacked, throwing Gil into the wall and Force-choking Hardhear. After, he jumped from the top of the ravine and into Eighty-One, destroying the droid immediately.<ref name="Dark Lord 18" />
