The '''Chemvau system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained [[Chemvau]].<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> It was situated at the coordinates M-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />
