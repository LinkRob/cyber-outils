The '''Fractured Plain''' was an area of plains on the [[planet]] [[Bogano]], broken up by chasms and cliffsides. When [[Mission to Bogano|visiting]] Bogano as part of their [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|quest]] to rebuild the [[Jedi Order]], [[Cere Junda]] and [[Cal Kestis]] landed on the Fractured Plain in the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]''.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
