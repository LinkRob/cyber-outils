'''Capri Otti''' was a [[Gender/Legends|female]] individual who worked as a [[Courier/Legends|courier]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] during the reign of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. In [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]], Otti [[Pilot/Legends|piloted]] a loaded [[messenger vehicle]] through the [[City/Legends|city]] [[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]] and she encountered [[Luke Skywalker's best friend|a Human farmhand]] who had became stranded in the city after his friend [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] had left Tatooine with the [[Smuggler/Legends|smuggler]] [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]], an acquaintance of Otti, aboard the [[Freighter/Legends|freighter]] [[Millennium Falcon/Legends|''Millennium Falcon'']]. While she didn't have room in her vehicle to take the boy as a passenger, she agreed to pass a message onto his parents&mdash;who operated a [[Moisture farm/Legends|moisture farm]]&mdash;and to bring back any money that they decided to send to their lost son. Otti spoke with the boy's parents and she returned to Mos Eisley several days later, to pass on a note from them to the farmhand in which they gave their son consent to leave Tatooine.

{{RPG|''[[Choose Your Own Star Wars Adventure: A New Hope]]''}}
After reading the note the Human determined to depart Tatooine, to seek out his friend Skywalker, and Otti helped the boy to find a freighter [[Captain/Legends|captain]] who would be willing to transport him to the planet [[Alderaan/Legends|Alderaan]]. While he waited for the [[Starship/Legends|starship]] to be prepared for him, Otti bought the Human a [[Soda/Legends|soda]] in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina/Legends|Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]], then left to get back to her job.
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Capri Otti was created by [[Christopher Golden]] and she appeared in the [[1998]] young readers book ''[[Choose Your Own Star Wars Adventure: A New Hope]]''. Her name is similar to that of Diana Capriotti, a writer who was acknowledged by Golden in the books preface. The novel is told from the perspective of the farmhand that Otti encounters and has multiple endings, with the choices of the reader determining some of the actions of the farmhand. When Otti presents the boy the note from his parents, the reader has a choice as to whether the Human decides to leave Tatooine or to return home. If the reader chooses for him to return home, Otti helps the boy to find someone who can give him a lift back to his parents' farm.
