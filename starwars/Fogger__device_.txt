A '''fogger''' was a device used to produce fogs with a glowing light source inside. A Fogger was used to produce a green glowing fog for a fake [[Shadow monster]] with a speaker system and a puppet as part of [[Cyrus Vuundir|Taborr]]'s plan to steal the [[Ootooan Opal]]s from the [[Ootoo]] on [[Ootoo Prime]].<ref name="Opal Cave">{{YJA|Mystery of the Opal Cave}}</ref> Another fogger was used from [[Jedi Master]] [[Carver Drow]] to train [[Kai Brightstar]] better in pink mist, helping him focus his senses on the [[training droid]]s.<ref name="The Caves of Batuu">{{YJA|The Caves of Batuu}}</ref>
