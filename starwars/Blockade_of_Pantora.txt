The '''blockade of Pantora''', also known as the '''Pantoran blockade''', was established during the [[Clone Wars]] by the [[Trade Federation]] above the [[moon]] [[Pantora]], in the [[Pantora system]]. Orchestrated by [[Sib Canay]], [[Nute Gunray]], and [[Lott Dod]], the move was done in an attempt to force Pantora to join the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]. However, publicly, the blockade was claimed to be over outstanding debts Pantora owed the Federation. To increase pressure, the daughters of the Pantoran [[Supreme Chairman]] [[Notluwiski Papanoida]] were [[Kidnapping of the Papanoida daughters|kidnapped]] as well. With the assistance of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], Pantoran [[Senator]] [[Riyo Chuchi]] and Chairman Papanoida were able to rescue both daughters and uncover the conspiracy, forcing the Trade Federation to lift the blockade.<ref name="Sphere" />

Because he disowned the blockade as a rogue action, Federation [[Senator]] Dod was able to keep his position in the [[Galactic Senate]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>
