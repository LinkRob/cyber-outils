'''Deep-fried gorg''',<ref name="VE" /> or '''deep fried gorg''',<ref name="Hunters" /> were a fried [[Foodstuff|food]] of [[gorg]] that could be found on the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]], and notably on the [[Mos Espa Market]].<ref name="VE" /> They were also advertised and sold in the [[Grand Arena]] during the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] sporting event. This meat was priced at three [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the menu of the [[The Restraining Bolt]], an [[Restaurant|eatery]] found on the bottom level of the [[Mos Espa Waystation]], one of the Grand Arena's battlefields located on the planet [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" />

In the non-[[canon]] [[2022]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', deep-fried gorg is mentioned in an announcement that can be heard on any of the [[capital ship]]s that have been captured by the player, with the announcer stating that the [[Starship|ship's]] cafeteria will be serving the dish as a special in honor of the vessel's new captain.<ref name="LEGO" />
