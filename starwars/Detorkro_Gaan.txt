'''Detorkro Gaan''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Zabrak]] who lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Over the [[Standard year|years]], Gaan made a living through various combat-related occupations: he had been a pit fighter, [[gladiator]], professional [[Fencing/Canon|fencer]], and a champion of the [[Hutt]] arenas. By the [[time]] of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star's]] [[Battle of Endor|destruction]], Gaan was living in the [[Anoat sector]], where he found himself trapped behind the Imperial [[governor]]'s [[Iron Blockade]]. The Zabrak veteran eventually found work with the crew of a talented [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]], carrying out a number of jobs on behalf of his new employer.<ref name="Uprising" />

Trained in warfare, Detorkro Gaan was a skilled fighter who preferred to take the offensive in combat, and favored the use of a [[sword]]. A muscular Zabrak, Gaan possessed [[Color|red]] [[hair]] and yellow skin, upon which he [[tattoo]]ed distinctive black markings around his head and face.<ref name="Uprising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Detorkro Gaan first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the multiplayer [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', created by the [[Kabam]] company and released on [[September 10]], [[2015]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms. Gaan acts as a potential crewmate for the player character, able to be tasked with carrying out timed quests to recover valuable loot.
