At least two individuals were the '''parents''' of the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Celantes]] [[Chippin Jat]], who [[Reproduction|hatched]]<ref name="Visual Guide" /> in [[136 BBY]]. Between 136 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'' take place in [[132 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:TheAcolyteDate|here]]. ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' establishes [[Chippin Jat]] [[Reproduction|hatched]] four [[Standard year|years]] before the events of ''The Acolyte'', meaning he must have hatched in [[136 BBY]].</ref> Jat's [[parent]]s proudly delivered him to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Visual Guide" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] to be tested and [[Jedi training|trained]] as a [[Jedi]].<ref name="Visual Guide" />
