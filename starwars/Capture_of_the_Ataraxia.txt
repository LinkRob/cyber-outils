In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Star Cruiser]] ''[[Ataraxia]]'' was  '''captured''' from [[Lourna Dee]] by [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Keeve Trennis]] with support from [[Republic Defense Coalition]] and [[Hutt Clan]] forces. Following a [[Battle at Ballum|battle]] on [[Ballum]], in which [[Skarabda]] the [[Hutt]] learned that Dee killed her sister [[Myarga Anjiliac Atirue|Myarga]], Skarabda ordered Dee eliminated and entered an alliance with the Republic and Jedi. In the [[realspace|space]] above Ballum, Dee attempted to flee with the ''Ataraxia'', which she named the ''Lourna Dee'', as the combined forces pursued. Trennis managed to breach the ''Ataraxia''{{'s}} hull, where she had to fight various [[droid]]s sent by Dee. Meanwhile, RDC [[Commander]] [[Velko Jahen]] used the [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] ''[[Gios]]''{{'s}} [[tractor beam]] to hold the ''Ataraxia'' in place. Dee countered this by straining the [[starship|ship's]] engines and dumping crates filled with civilians into the path of the tractor beam, forcing Jahen to turn it off. Jedi [[Ai-Dan Yelooc]] used [[the Force]] to direct the loose crates of people into the ''Gios'' for medical care. Following a confrontation in the ''Ataraxia''{{'s}} [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], Trennis [[stun]]ned Dee, captured her, and secured control of the ''Ataraxia''. She was soon joined by [[Kotabi]] [[bond-twins]] [[Terec]] and [[Ceret]] on the ship to lead an unsanctioned rescue mission into the [[Nihil]] [[Occlusion Zone]].<ref name="The High Republic 3" />
