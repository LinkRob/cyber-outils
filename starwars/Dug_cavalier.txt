'''Dug cavaliers''',<ref name="Zillo Beast Old EG">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode218.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: The Zillo Beast}}</ref> also known as '''Dug soldiers''',<ref name="Zillo Beast New EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-zillo-beast-episode-guide|text="The Zillo Beast" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> were a type of [[soldier]] of the [[Dug]]s that served the [[Dug Council]] during the [[Clone Wars]] on the [[planet]] [[Malastare]]. They rode [[insectomorph]]s into battle and used a type of [[electrostaff]]. Additionally, Dug soldiers used [[Dug debris loader]]s that served as [[artillery]] pieces. Dug cavaliers fought with the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Battle of Malastare]].<ref name="The Zillo Beast" />
