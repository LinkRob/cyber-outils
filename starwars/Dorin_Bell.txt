'''Dorin Bell''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[scientist]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> Around [[6 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Bell was the first target for [[Jyn Erso]] to [[assassin]]ate on the planet [[Horuz]]. However, he was [[Mission to Horuz|killed]] not by Erso's weapon, but by a [[grenade]] from a trap set up by the [[Partisans]] in case of Erso's failure.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
