A '''fisher bird''' was a type of [[bird]]. Two [[Standard decade|decades]] after the [[Sith rebellion]], one of the four [[skiff]]s used by [[Jedi lord (Empire)|Jedi lord]] [[Hanrai]]'s entourage on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dekien]] was a [[Color|white]] and sleek vessel sculpted to evoke a fisher bird.<ref name="Ronin" />

==Behind the scenes==
A fisher bird was mentioned in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] novel ''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]'', written by [[Emma Mieko Candon]].<ref name="Ronin" />
