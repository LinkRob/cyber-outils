'''Apapaba''' was an individual wanted for crimes against the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Yahenna Laxo]] of the [[Gray Syndicate]], a minor organization with pretensions of being a serious player in crime on the<ref name="justice" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="datalothal">{{Databank|lothal|Lothal}}</ref> [[planet]] of [[Lothal]], sheltered several people, including Apapaba, from the Empire's attentions. Laxo was willing to hide such individuals only so long as they had enough [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to pay him.<ref name="justice" />

As part of the crackdown on suspicious activities on Lothal<ref name="justice" /> in [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Chronology Note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]'' to [[4 BBY]].</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] hit one of Laxo's warehouses and two of his [[gambling]] clubs. Laxo then asked [[Merei Spanjaf]], a teenage [[Gender|girl]] unwillingly involved with the activities of his Syndicate, to visit Apapaba along with [[Durchine]], [[Holshef]], [[Kinlo]], and [[Marhas]]. She was to inform them that [[Rosey]], another member of his Syndicate, would be moving them to new hiding places that [[Standard day|night]] and that they needed to be packed and ready.<ref name="justice" />
