A local '''government''' was in power on the [[planet]] [[At Attin]] during the [[New Republic Era|New Republic era]]. The standard [[currency]] was [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Real Adventure" /> It was headed by a droid called [[The Supervisor]] and its law enforcement and security was mostly carried out by [[Security droid]]s. However, humans, like [[Fara]] also had important roles in the government. The inner workings of the government were somewhat opaque to the citizens of At Attin as the Supervisor rarely granted audiences and his voice had not been heard in a long time by 9 ABY. In the past Republic Emissaries visited on regular occasions to collect the Old Republic Credits that were minted on At Attin, but by 9 ABY this had not occurred in many years.{{Fact}}
