The '''ANs-5d "lock track" full-spectrum transceiver''' was a device produced by [[Fabritech]]. It was used along with a [[Melihat]] [["Multi Imager" dedicated energy receptor]] and a [[Tana Ire]] [[electro-photo receptor]] to comprise a [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s [[sensor]] system. Alternatively, the ANs-5d could be combined with two additional Fabritech components, a [[PA-9r long-range phased tachyon detection array]] and a [[PG-7u short-range primary threat analysis grid]] to produce the sensor system.<ref name="SWCT X-wing">{{SWCT|set=Blueprints|cardname=X-wing Starfighter}}</ref> This alternate configuration was a [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]s main sensor system.<ref name="SWCT Y-wing" />
