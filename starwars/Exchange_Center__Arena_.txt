The '''Exchange Center''' was a [[currency]] exchange business operating within the [[Grand Arena]], a location utilized by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. Advertised across arenas like the [[Dune Sea Outpost]] and [[Mos Espa Waystation]], the center would allow patrons to exchange various types of currency, including [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], [[druggat]]s, and [[wupiupi]]. These advertisements consisted of simple posters featuring a [[Color|gold]], gray, and white geometric background, with the text "Druggat & Wupiupi" in [[Aurebesh]] on top, the [[Huttese (alternative writing system)|Nal-Huttese]] currency and [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] credit symbols in the middle, and the words "Exchange Center" at the bottom.<ref name="Hunters" />
