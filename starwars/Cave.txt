A '''cave''',<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> also known as a '''grotto''',<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> was a void in a section of rock or dirt. Some caves, often called '''caverns''', were large enough for [[Sentience|sentient]] life to build [[City|cities]] in them.<ref name="Bloodline" /> The surface of the [[planet]] [[Bilzen]] was covered in frozen caverns.<ref name="Ice Breaking">{{RebelsMagCite|16|Ice Breaking}}</ref>

At a test firing of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]], [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] stated violence was part of every [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]], noting that it was painted in caves.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>
