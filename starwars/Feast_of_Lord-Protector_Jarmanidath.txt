The '''Feast of Lord-Protector Jarmanidath''' was a holiday from the [[Colonies]] [[region]] which was approved by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Culture Ministry]]. The [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilots]] of the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] celebrated the Feast officially when throwing a party for their [[commanding officer]] [[Shakara Nuress]]' [[birthday]].

==Biography==
The Feast of [[Lord-Protector]] [[Jarmanidath]] was an obscure holiday which originated from a [[Planet|world]] in the [[Colonies]] [[region]]. The [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Culture Ministry]] approved the holiday for celebration, although the reason eluded [[Yrica Quell]], a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] of the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. Quell's comrade, [[Xion]], once speculated that the approval had been an error which the Culture Ministry had found too embarrassing to correct. The [[pilot]]s of the 204th officially celebrated the Feast when marking the [[birthday]] of their [[commanding officer]], [[Colonel]] [[Shakara Nuress]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

Over the [[Standard year|years]], the birthday party acquired more and more traditions over the true feast. One of the pilots, [[Captain]] [[Samran Phesh]], had been a firm opponent to the gathering and forbade his own [[squadron]], [[Squadron Six]], from attending until<ref name="Victory's Price" /> by [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> when Nuress had since [[Death|perished]] and had been replaced by [[Major]]-then-Colonel [[Soran Keize]]. Despite Nuress's loss, the 204th pilots continued to celebrate her birthday with the Feast of Lord-Protector Jarmanidath that year, and Phesh was proclaimed the Lord-Protector's jester by his squadron.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Feast of Lord-Protector Jarmanidath appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the final installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy which was written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
