'''''Dark Droids'' 3''' is the third issue of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] limited series ''[[Star Wars: Dark Droids]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Luke Ross]] and [[Alex Sinclair]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 11]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE SCOURGE DESCENDS ON MUSTAFAR! A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! For the evil intelligence taking over droids across the galaxy, there is no greater prize than THE FORCE. And who better to steal it from than a being who is more machine than man&hellip;DARTH VADER!''

Sith's connection to the Force. She

plans emerged an evil entity.

The entity is a scourge to droids in
both the Rebellion and Empire and
continued to spread its consciousness
to any droid that it could, including
the trusted protocol droid C-3PO.

After a brief conversation with Luke
Skywalker, it dawned on the scourge
that cyborgs are the bridge to the
humans that it needs to further
spread its control.

And there is no cyborg more powerful
than Darth Vader&hellip;.}}
