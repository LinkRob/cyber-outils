'''Company 326''',<ref name="Yoda 8" /> also designated as '''Squad 326''', was a [[Grand Army of the Republic]] [[clone trooper]] [[company]] under the command of [[Clone Captain]] [[Cesar]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The company fell under the leadership of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] during the [[Hijacking of a Munificent-class star frigate|hijacking of a ''Munificent''-class star frigate]], and was later joined by [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] for a [[mission to Enthenium]], where they discovered a new [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] weapon; the [[megadroid]].<ref name="Yoda 7" /> The two [[Jedi]] and the company subsequently undertook a [[mission to Golotha]] to destroy the weapons, but the Republic force was ambushed and the entire company was annihilated.<ref name="Yoda 8" />
