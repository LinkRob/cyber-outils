The '''Abecederian line''' was a series of executive [[shuttle]]s manufactured by [[Cygnus Spaceworks]] and later [[Sienar Fleet Systems]]. Initial designs for the [[Galactic Republic]] were appropriated by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="ROVG" />

==History==
[[File:KrennicsShuttle-SWROVehicleSeriesRedux.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Delta''-class T-3c shuttle was part of the line]]
The Abecederian line was designed by [[Cygnus Spaceworks]] during the late [[Republic Era]]. Its design team was coaxed to create the line at the behest of [[Republic Sienar Systems]]. The line's output was headed by Cygnus design lead [[Lamilla Tion]] from Cygnus's [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] design studios.<ref name="ROVG" />

==Characteristics==
The Abecederian line shared many hallmark features&mdash;elegant lines, tri-foil symmetry, and articulated wings that folded during landing. The Cygnus design lead, Lamilla Tion, was a sculptor fascinated with [[paper-folding]]. She incorporated the variable geometry landing system into each of her designs at great expense. This served to compress each [[Starship|ship's]] docked footprint and serve as an artistic acknowledgment of her spiritual beliefs.<ref name="ROVG" />
