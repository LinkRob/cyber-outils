'''Butterflying''' was a cooking technique. To prepare a [[Smapp]] dish, one needed to first butterfly the creature in order to begin the cooking process.<ref name="MAGL">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Butterflying was only mentioned in a segment called "Cooking a Smapp," an entry done in the style of an [[in-universe]] guide to purchasing and cooking Smapp. It was written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that combines creature designs and photographs from various projects of [[George Lucas]], creator of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, with original text by Carrau.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]] with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
