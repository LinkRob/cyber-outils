'''Alugomes''' was an [[Iakaru]] [[spice dealer]] active during the [[New Republic Era|time]] of the [[New Republic]]. The dealer was invited by [[Zeva Bliss]] of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] to a [[Kijimi summit|summit]] on [[Kijimi]]. Bliss intended to [[Death|execute]] the criminals she had gathered, but the Spice Runner [[Poe Dameron]] prevented her plans, allowing the criminal guests to escape.<ref name="Free Fall" />
