'''''Darth Vader'' 32''' is the thirty-second issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Ibraim Roberson]] and [[Federico Blee]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[March 22]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''HANDMAIDEN NO MORE? Few bonds in the galaxy are as strong as the sisterhood of the former handmaidens of Padmé Amidala. But all that was threatened when Sabé entered the service of Darth Vader, the man the handmaidens always believed was responsible for Padmé's death. Now Dormé, Saché, Rabé and Eirtaé are determined to save Sabé &ndash; or stop her, no matter what. But the influence of the Dark Lord is strong and dangerous &ndash; and not just to Sabé. What path will the handmaidens choose? And how does all this play into Darth Vader's true plans?''
