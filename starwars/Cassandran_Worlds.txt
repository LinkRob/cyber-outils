The '''Cassandran Worlds''' was a historical name for the region of [[the galaxy]] that, under the [[Galactic Empire]], was divided into the [[Cassander sector]] and its two sub[[sector]]s, the [[Tadrin sector]] and [[Tendrannan sector]].<ref name="Fry" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Sifo-Dyas]],<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> a [[human]] [[Gender|male]], hailed from [[Minashee]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> which was located in the region.<ref name="Ultimate" /> The [[hyperspace route]] [[Relgim Run]] was located there, and during the [[Imperial Era]], the [[pirate]] [[Gunda Mabin|Gunda the Terrible]] sought to plunder the Cassandran Worlds, at least according to the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cassandran Worlds were originally introduced for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] brand in [[2004]], first seen in the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' sourcebook ''[[Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds]]'' as a loose-alliance of multiple planets.<ref name="SWRPG">''[[Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds]]''</ref> In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Cassandran Worlds were first mentioned in the [[2015]] sourcebook, ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Ultimate" />
