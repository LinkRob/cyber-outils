An '''Aquillian Ranger''' was a type of individual who was active during the [[Imperial Era]]. While [[starship]] [[Shipspotter|spotting]] on [[Alderaan]] in [[9 BBY]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] spotted a [[Tri-Wing S-91x Pegasus Starfighter]], and hypothesized that it was an Aquillian Ranger [[scout]]ing for the [[Merson pirates]].<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:MCQ-AquillianRanger.jpg|thumb|right|200px|[[Ralph McQuarrie]]'s concept art of Deak Starkiller, Aquillian Ranger.]]
The Aquillian Rangers originated in [[George Lucas]]' [[Adventures of the Starkiller, Episode I: The Star Wars|second draft]] of the [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|original ''Star Wars'' film]], apparently in reference to the planet [[Aquilae (The Star Wars)|Aquilae]] that had appeared in the previous [[The Star Wars rough draft|rough draft]] and the [[The Star Wars first draft|first draft]]. [[Deacon|Deak Starkiller]] was conceptualized as a [[Jedi]] and an Aquillian Ranger; elements of his character would later be given to Princess Leia. In the [[The Star Wars: From The Adventures of Luke Starkiller|third draft]], the Aquillian Rangers are present with the Rebels on [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]].<ref name="Making of Star Wars">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref>

The backstory of the Aquillian Rangers as allies of the Jedi would be partially incorporated into that of the [[Antarian Rangers/Legends|Antarian Rangers]], in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Making of Star Wars" /> However, the name itself would not be introduced to canon until Leia's mention in [[Part I]] of ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''.<ref name="Part I" />
