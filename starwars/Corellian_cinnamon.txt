'''Corellian cinnamon''' was a type of [[cinnamon]]. [[Standard week|Weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> which occurred in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the members of the [[New Republic]] [[Riot Squadron]] entered a marketplace on [[Jiruus]] that smelt of Corellian cinnamon. The additive shared its name with the [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian cinnamon was first mentioned in the [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], which was written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
