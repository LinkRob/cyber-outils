'''Fayet Kelen''' was the [[Parent|father]] of [[Nakari Kelen]] and founder of the [[Kelen Biolabs]]. [[Gender|His]] business was the largest industry on the planet [[Pasher]].<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" />

==Personality and traits==
Fayet was described as portly, with an abundance of jowls. He shaved his head, which was already losing most of its [[hair]]. When he addressed his employees, he would use a deep booming voice, calling them his minions, and then soften his voice and words right at the end of his sentence. After his [[Nakari Kelen's mother|wife]] was sent to the [[Spice mines of Kessel|Spice Mines of Kessel]] for speaking out against the [[Galactic Empire]], his daughter became the most important person in his life.<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" />
