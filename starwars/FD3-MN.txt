'''FD3-MN''' was a modified<ref name="Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> [[FD3-MN fighting droid]]<ref name="Kids" /> made by [[Ralakili]] to be a [[gladiator droid]] to fight in his [[droid]] [[fighting pit]]s inside [[Fort Ypso]] on [[Vandor]]. The droid's mechanical [[limb]]s could puncture metal with devastating might powered by electricity produced in her interior [[power cell]]s.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|wg-22|WG-22}}</ref> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Official Guide" /> her fight<ref name="Solo" /> with [[WG-22]]<ref name="Official Guide" /> was interrupted by the protests of [[L3-37]] for [[droid rights]], which cost FD3-MN dearly.<ref name="Databank" />
