'''Carrots''',<ref name="FITYMI" /> also known as '''space carrots''',<ref name="Rabbit Hunt">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|annual=2020|story=Rabbit Hunt}}</ref> were a [[Foodstuff|food]] that were used to make [[carrot juice]]. While visiting [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] on [[Bespin]], the criminal [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] was offered carrot juice by Calrissian's assistant, [[Lobot]], which Tumperakki found offensive, and prompted him to want to tell Lobot "where he could put his carrots."<ref name="FITYMI" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Carrot-LEGO.png|thumb|right|150px|A carrot]]
Carrots were first mentioned in the [[2016]] non-[[canon]] activity book ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Choose Your Side: Doodle Activity Book]]''.<ref name="Doodle">''[[LEGO Star Wars: Choose Your Side: Doodle Activity Book]]''</ref> They were subsequently introduced into the ''[[Star Wars]]'' canon as part of the dish [[Ithorian Garden Patty Bun]], which was sold in the [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] restaurant in [[Disneyland]]'s [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]].<ref name="DB7">{{GEAttr|Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo}}</ref>

===Non-canon history===
In ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Choose Your Side: Doodle Activity Book]]'', it is stated that [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] prefers eating carrots over wielding a [[lightsaber]].
