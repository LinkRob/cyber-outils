'''Abel Gustavo Lopez Peña IV''' is an author who has written reference books and articles for ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'', the [[StarWars.com|''Official Star Wars Website'']], the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'', ''[[Star Wars Gamer]]'', ''[[The Official Star Wars Fact File]]'' and ''[[Vader: The Ultimate Guide]]''. He is also a translator of rare Spanish and Italian ''Star Wars'' comics. He is known for referencing obscure and out-of-print ''Star Wars'' material in his work. Peña posted on both [[Star Wars: Blogs|''Star Wars'': Blogs]] and the [[Jedi Council Forums]] under the names "'''Halagad'''" and "'''Halagad_Ventor'''" respectively.

This interest in the obscure [[West End Games]] character [[Halagad Ventor]] led Peña to his second role in ''Star Wars'', that of a model. In [[2005]], artist [[Joe Corroney]] used Peña as the basis of his rendering of Ventor for [[Star Wars Insider 84|''Insider 84'']]{{'s}} [[HoloNet News/Legends|HoloNet News]].

He is a member of the Star Wars Fanboy Association.<ref name="SWFA Member List">{{WebCite|url=http://www.myuselessknowledge.com/swfa/abel-interview.html|text=Members List|work=[https://www.myuselessknowledge.com/swfa Star Wars Fanboy Association]|archivedate=20220710004321}}</ref>
