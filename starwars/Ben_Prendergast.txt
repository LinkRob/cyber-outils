'''Ben Prendergast''' (born [[January 8]]<ref name="Birthday" /> in [[Wikipedia:Melbourne|Melbourne]], [[Wikipedia:Australia|Australia]])<ref name="Supanova" /> is an [[Wikipedia:Australians|Australian]] actor known for his role as [[W:c:godofwar:Týr|Týr]] in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Wikipedia:God of War Ragnarök|''God of War Ragnarök'']]. He<ref name="OfficialWebsite" /> voiced the [[First Order]] [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] in the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' in "[[Station to Station]],"<ref name="Station to Station">{{Resistance|Station to Station}}</ref> which premiered on [[December 15]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref>

Prendergast reprised his role as Hux's voice actor in ''[[The LEGO Star Wars Holiday Special]]'', a [[2020]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short film. He also voiced the [[Zabrak]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]] in the film.<ref name="HolidaySpecial">''[[The LEGO Star Wars Holiday Special]]''</ref> Prendergast's favorite voiced scene in ''The LEGO Star Wars Holiday Special'' was one in which Hux crushed over<ref name="FavScene">{{Instagram|username=benjipea|url=p/CN0pSQ-nIzF|name=Ben Prendergast|date=2021-04-18|quote=Throwback to my favourite ever voiced scene, me as Hux crushing on Kylo #kylux ❤️|archiveurl=https://archive.is/SR0F4}}</ref> First Order [[Supreme Leader]]<ref name="HolidaySpecial" /> [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] when he was shirtless.<ref name="FavScene" />
