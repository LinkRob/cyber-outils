[[Nihil]] [[General]] [[Abediah Viess]] planned, in [[228 BBY]], to author an '''autobiography''' detailing her successes in battle and warfare&mdash;in particular, how she had managed to turn a profit from it. Viess pondered multiple working titles for the [[book]], including '''''Viess: A Warrior's True Truth''''', '''''Viess: Bloody Money''''', and '''''Viess: The Dumbest Woman Alive, Who Will Soon Be Dead'''''. The latter-most came about during the arrival of [[Republic Defense Coalition]] reinforcements during the [[Battle of Eriadu]] as a result of the [[Mission to Planet X|deactivation]] of the [[Stormwall]]. By the time of the Battle of Eriadu, Viess had already written the book's opening paragraph. She considered including the phrase "Life's too short when life is this long" somewhere in the book.<ref name="TrialsOfTheJedi" />

==Behind the scenes==
Abediah Viess' autobiography was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]'', written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Random House Worlds]].<ref name="TrialsOfTheJedi" />
