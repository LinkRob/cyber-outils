'''Draboon''' was a terrestrial [[Astronomer|astronomical]] object<ref name="VoT" /> located in the [[Mandalore sector]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[Great Clan Wars|Mandalorian Civil War]]<ref name="VoT" /> circa [[42 BBY]],<ref name="Date Note">According to {{FactFile2014|98|MAN 1-4|Mandalore}}, the [[Mandalorian]] Civil War took place around 20 [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Clone Wars]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the beginning of the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars to the year [[22 BBY]], it can be determined that the Mandalorian Civil War took place circa [[42 BBY]].</ref> [[Satine Kryze]] of the Mandalorian [[House Kryze]] came under the protection of [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Duchess Satine became a target for bounty hunters, and the two Jedi removed her from [[Mandalore]] for her own safety. At some point during their nearly [[Standard year|year]]-long time on the run, they found themselves on Draboon. While on Draboon, Kryze and Kenobi encountered a swarm of [[venom-mite]]s and Kenobi carried Kryze to safety, though his chivalrous act was tarnished when he dropped her unceremoniously, leaving her with a permanent scar from the fall.<ref name="VoT" />

After the end of the Mandalorian Civil War<ref name="VoT" /> and the establishment of the pacifist [[New Mandalorians|New Mandalorian]] government, a custom-made luxury yacht was created for the Duchess. The interiors of the starship, named the ''[[Coronet]]'', were furnished with many natural resources found in the Mandalore sector, including Draboon [[lapis]] on the ship's promenade.<ref name="BMF 76" />
