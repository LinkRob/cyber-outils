"'''Beeps'''" is the fifth episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Sounds]]'', an inside look at the sound design of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' films and series.<ref name="D+" /> It premiered on [[September 29]], [[2021]] on [[Disney+]].<ref name="Announcement" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Official description==
''Witness the many different droids beeping about in a galaxy far, far away.''
