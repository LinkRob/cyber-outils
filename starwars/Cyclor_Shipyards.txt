The '''Cyclor Shipyards''' was a set of vast [[shipyard]]s that [[orbit]]ed the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Cyclor]], [[homeworld]] of the [[Cyclorrian]] [[species]]. The Shipyards were a licensed manufacturer of [[Incom Corporation]] products, including the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Z-29 Skyhawk]]. The starship ''[[Innovator]]'', designed by the [[engineer]] [[Vam Targes]], was also constructed at the shipyards during the [[High Republic Era]]. A [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] force [[First Battle of Cyclor|attacked]] the Cyclor Shipyards in an attempt to destroy the ''Innovator'' prior to the [[Republic Fair]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
