{{Quote|&hellip;and loaded up these E-11s you've given us, which require one to aim as far as possible away from what one's shooting at in order to have half a chance of hitting it.|Deserting stormtrooper [[Sardis Ramsin|TD-7556]]|<ref name="Born in the Storm">{{StoryCite|story=Born in the Storm|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>}}
The '''E-11 medium blaster rifle''', more commonly known as the '''E-11 blaster rifle''' was a light but powerful [[blaster rifle]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]].<ref name="SW:AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> Although a [[Nihil]] of [[Krix Kamerat's cell]] used a similarly-designed blaster during the [[Battle of Takodana (Nihil conflict)|Battle of Takodana]] of the [[High Republic Era]],<ref name="HRA 10">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 10|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 10]]</ref> the E-11 was only created during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="SW:AEYNTK" /> The [[DAS-430 Neural Inhibitor]] was also of a similar design.<ref name="Galactic Defense">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref> The [[SoroSuub Corporation]]-produced [[Freedom One]] blaster was a technological clone of the E-11.<ref name="Blasters and Firearms">{{EncyclopediaCite|Blasters and Ranged Weapons}}</ref>

The E-11 served as the standard issue weapon of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s,<ref name="SW:AEYNTK" /> including remaining [[clone trooper]]s still active under the Empire.<ref name="Thrawn 1">[[Thrawn 1|''Thrawn'' 1]]</ref> Some [[Imperial Army trooper]]s, including some members of the normal [[infantry]],<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref> [[Imperial swamp trooper]]s,<ref name="Target Vader 5">[[Target Vader 5|''Target Vader'' 5]]</ref> and [[Imperial security trooper]]s, were also equipped with it.<ref name="Ferrix Officer">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|packtype=''Star Wars: Andor'' Figure|pack=#04: Imperial Officer (Ferrix)|altlink=https://www.target.com/p/star-wars-the-black-series-imperial-officer-ferrix-action-figure-target-exclusive/-/A-84832661}}</ref> The E-11 was the successor to BlasTech's [[E-10 blaster rifle]]<ref name="E10-DB">{{Databank|e-10-blaster-rifle|E-10 Blaster Rifle}}</ref> and was based on the [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] used by [[clone trooper]]s during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="SW:AEYNTK" />

==Description==
===Features===
{{Quote|I'm sending the next rendezvous point to your helmet navigation system. It's ten klicks to the southeast. Arrive with your E-11 assembled and await further orders.|[[Chiron]]|<ref name="RebelRanks">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Stormtrooper-fires.png|thumb|left|300px|Stormtroopers armed with E-11 blaster rifles.]]
The E-11 blaster rifle combined lethal firepower with impressive range and a versatile design. Featuring three power settings; lethal, [[stun]], and sting, the rifle proved useful for a variety of combat situations. An advanced cooling system resulted in the blaster's superior performance,<ref name="UltimateSW" /> especially useful for its fully automatic fire setting.<ref name="Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger">''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]''</ref> The E-11 also included a telescopic range-finding sight and a folding three-position stock, which could convert the blaster into a full-length rifle<ref name="UltimateSW" /> and could be modified to house a [[glowrod]].<ref name="Gathering Forces">{{Rebels|Gathering Forces}}</ref> Its standard issue [[power cell]]s carried energy for 100 shots however the rifle could also be loaded with plasma cartridges that could last for more than 500 shots.<ref name="UltimateSW" /> The E-11 could be disassembled for travel and reassembled by its carrier when needed.<ref name="RebelRanks" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I'd always loved the Sterling submachine gun&mdash;to me, it looked like a science-fiction weapon&mdash;so I thought I could turn that into a blaster for the stormtroopers.|Roger Christian|<ref name="Insider230">{{InsiderCite|230|What a Piece of Junk!}}</ref>}}
[[File:E11blaster-SW.jpg|thumb|right|350px|An E-11 blaster medium rifle]]
The E-11 medium blaster rifle was based on the British [[Wikipedia:Sterling submachine gun|Sterling Mk.4 submachine gun]]<ref name="BeyondArmor">''[[Star Wars: Stormtroopers: Beyond the Armor]]''</ref> and designed around it by set decorator [[Roger Christian]] for [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']],<ref name="Insider230" /> where the blaster first appeared.<ref name="ANH" />

Due to the limited budget for the film, Christian designed the E-11 and other props around real-world items, such as the submachine gun, which he already loved the look of and thought emulated a science fiction weapon. He sourced the submachine gun he made the original prop around from Bapty and Co, the only location willing to rent weaponry for film productions. To complete the look, he superglued rubber T-strips to the barrel and made other altercations. Director [[George Lucas]], whose approval was a sign to Christian that he was on the right track, loved his work in creating the blaster, particularly with how it could actually be fired. The appearance and weight of the weapon also pleased Lucas. Interested in seeing Christian's process, Lucas than stayed with him for the rest of the day and worked with Christian to assemble [[Satine's Lament|Leia Organa's pistol]] from a starting pistol.<ref name="Insider230" />
