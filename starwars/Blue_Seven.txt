'''Blue Seven''' was the [[callsign]] of the seventh member of [[Blue Squadron]].

==History==
[[Robich Duggsin]] was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] who used this callsign around the time of the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Visual Guide" />

During the [[Battle of Jakku]], a member of the New Republic's [[77 Squadron]]&mdash;which flew as Blue Squadron in that battle&mdash;flew under the callsign.<ref name="Hyperspace Mountain" />
