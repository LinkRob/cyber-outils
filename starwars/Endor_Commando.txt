The '''Endor Commando''' was a set of [[armor]] worn by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal Warrior|standard costume]]. This [[Mandalorian armor|armor]] consisted of a camouflaged design<ref name="Hunters" /> resembling those worn by members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Endor strike team]] prior to and during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Commando was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Aran Tal, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 1.0.0 alongside Season 1 and the game's global launch on [[June 4]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Endor MT-62]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing the Neon Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
