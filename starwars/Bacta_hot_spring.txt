A '''bacta hot spring''' was a type of spring. The former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] was brought to a [[bacta]] hot spring to recuperate following a [[Duel on Olcatz|duel]] with [[the Grand Master]].<ref name="Payback" /> 

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
A bacta hot spring appeared in "[[The Duel: Payback]]", a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kamikaze Douga]] and [[ANIMA (company)|ANIMA]], was released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. It was first mentioned in a ''[[Star Wars: Visions Filmmaker Focus]]'' video uploaded to the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[October 27]] of that year.<ref name="Focus">{{SWYouTube|UV3kHdgpzZY|VISIONS FILMMAKER FOCUS: THE DUEL: PAYBACK}}</ref>

The bacta spring was inspired by the [[Wikipedia:Onsen|onsen]] hot springs of [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japan]]. The crew of Kamikaze Douga went on location scouts to northern Japan, including [[Wikipedia:Yamagata Prefecture|Yamagata Prefecture]], to find references for the area around the spring.<ref name="Focus" />
