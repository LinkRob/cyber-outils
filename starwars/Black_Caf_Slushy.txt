The '''Black Caf Slushy''' was a [[caf]]-based cold drink served at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="KSK" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> The sweet drink was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="KSK" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Black Caf Slushy is available in the [[In-universe|real world]] at Kat Saka's Kettle in [[Disneyland]]'s [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]]. It first debuted in [[May]], [[2025]].<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/kat-sakas-kettle/menus|text=Disneyland: Kat Saka's Kettle Snack Menu}}</ref><ref name="Review">{{Instagram|username=foodatdisneyland|url=p/DJrqG-nPwPW|name=foodatdisneyland|date=2025-05-15|quote=The New Black Caf Slush from Kat Saka's Kettle in Disneyland $7.99 Magic Key Discounts Available. This new cold brew slushy is topped with chocolate cookie sweet cream and Oreo cookie crumbles. This one actually bears little resemblance to the other Cold Brew Black Caf they serve in Galaxy's Edge, but it is still a pretty good option for people who love sweet dessert style cold brews. This will be a good option come summertime! 8/10 rating|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/6Duyf}}</ref> The menu describes the slushy as a "[[Wikipedia:Cold brew coffee|Cold Brew]] [[Wikipedia:Slushy|Slushy]], [[Chocolate]] [[Cookie]] [[Wikipedia:Cream|Sweet Cream]] and Crème-filled Chocolate Cookie Crumbles."<ref name="Menu" /> The Crème-filled Chocolate Cookie Crumbles were likened to [[Wikipedia:Oreo|Oreo cookies]].<ref name="Review" />
