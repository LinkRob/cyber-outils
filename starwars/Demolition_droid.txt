'''Demolition droids''' were a type of [[droid]] designated with the task of destruction.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> The fraudulent [[Type II loadlifter droid]] series were demolition droids repurposed as [[binary loadlifter]]s, and as such were prone to violent tendencies if not held back via a [[restraining bolt]].<ref name="Gone with a Trace">{{TCW|Gone with a Trace}}</ref> The [[infiltrator demolition droid]] utilized by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] was designed to infiltrate target facilities and act as a [[bomb]].<ref name="R2 77">{{BuildR2Cite|77|Droid Directory}}</ref>
