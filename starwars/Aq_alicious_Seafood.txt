'''Aq'alicious Seafood''' was the name of a seafood [[restaurant]] in the [[central marketplace]] of [[Ro Station]], a [[city]] on [[Pantora]]. The name of the establishment was written in [[Aurebesh]] on big round signs, in the traditional, left to right, way; and by a second set of characters surrounding the others in a circle.<ref name="Cornered" />
