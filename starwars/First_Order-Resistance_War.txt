{{Quote|What our mothers and fathers fought for, we will not let die. Today we make our last stand. For the galaxy. For Leia. For everyone we've lost.''"<br />"''They've taken enough of us. Now we take the war to them.|Poe Dameron and Finn|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
The '''First Order-Resistance War''' ([[34 ABY]]&ndash;[[35 ABY]]) was a yearlong '''new galactic civil war''' fought between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. While the First Order sought to conquer the [[star system]]s and [[Neo-Imperialism|rule]] over [[the galaxy]], the Resistance fought to preserve the [[democracy]] [[Galactic Concordance|brought about]] by the [[New Republic]] after their [[Galactic Civil War|struggle]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].

The war officially began in the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]], three [[Standard decade|decades]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], when the First Order [[Hosnian Cataclysm|fired]] its [[planet]]-converted [[superweapon]], [[Starkiller Base]], on the Republic [[capital]] of [[Hosnian Prime]], destroying the [[Hosnian system]], and with it the [[New Republic Senate]] and [[New Republic Defense Fleet|Defense Fleet]]. Shortly afterwards, the Resistance launched an [[Battle of Starkiller Base|assault]] on the First Order stronghold, ending the [[Starkiller crisis]] with the destruction of the superweapon. Nonetheless, the New Republic collapsed as the First Order moved in to seize [[First Order military|military]] control over the galaxy, escalating the [[Cold War]] into full scale open warfare.

With the New Republic divided, the Resistance had to survive on its own. [[Battle of D'Qar|Forced to depart]] from [[D'Qar]], the Resistance was pursued across [[Realspace|space]] by the First Order, though both sides incurred heavy losses. During the [[Battle of Oetchi]], [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] was [[Death|killed]] by his apprentice, [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], who appointed himself the new Supreme Leader. However, [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] was convinced to return to the fight for galactic freedom. Skywalker sacrificed himself to hold off Ren at the [[Battle of Crait]] while his last [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] fled with the remnants of the Resistance.

In the following [[35 ABY|year]], Supreme Leader Ren discovered the former [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]] alive on [[Exegol]], attended by the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cult|cult]]. From their hidden world, the Sith Eternal had constructed a massive [[fleet]] of planet-destroying [[Star Destroyer]]s, the [[Final Order]], hoping to bolster the [[First Order military|forces of the First Order]] and transform it into a new [[Sith Empire]]. Ren ultimately abandoned the First Order, but [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]] continued the alliance with the [[Sith]]. At the [[Battle of Exegol]], the Resistance rallied a [[Citizens' Fleet]], and together crushed the Final Order. During the battle, Rey sacrificed herself to destroy Sidious and his [[Sith cultist|followers]], though was resuscitated by Ben Solo. Their victory sparked a people's [[uprising against the First Order]] across the galaxy.

==Prelude==
===The Galactic Civil War===
{{Main|Galactic Civil War}}
{{Quote|When we look back upon the war against the Empire—upon the billions of lives lost—sometimes it seems as though nothing could ever have been worth the terrible price we paid. But when we think of those people who perished in the conflict, let us remember that they died for justice. For liberty. For the extraordinary peace we now enjoy. This is what we fought for.|[[Tai-Lin Garr]]|<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>}}
[[File:Declaration.png|thumb|left|250px|Prior to the Galactic Civil War, Darth Sidious consolidated his reign over the galaxy as Emperor of the Galactic Empire.]]
At the end of the [[Clone Wars]] in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], who was secretly in fact the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious, declared himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] and [[Proclamation of the New Order|reorganized]] the [[Democracy|democratic]] [[Galactic Republic]], which had governed the galaxy for over a [[millennium]],<ref name="Republic DB">{{Databank|galactic-republic|Galactic Republic}}</ref> into the first [[Galactic Empire]], replacing the [[Galactic Senate]] with the [[Imperial Senate]]. The Emperor had his new [[Sith apprentice]], the fallen [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] turned [[Sith Lord]] Darth Vader, oversee the [[Mission to Mustafar|deaths]] of the remaining [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Executive Separatist Council|leaders]] and the [[Great Jedi Purge|purging]] of the remaining [[Jedi]] that had survived [[Order 66]] and the [[Operation: Knightfall|march]] on the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]], leaving the [[Jedi Order]] destroyed.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

Despite the promises of security and order that the Old Republic had failed to provide, the Empire eventually faced [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] that came to consolidate into the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref> The Rebel Alliance and the Empire soon came into conflict in the form of the [[Galactic Civil War]], with the former securing a major victory at the [[Battle of Yavin]] where the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] was destroyed.<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Four years later, in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the Emperor used himself and the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] as bait to lure out the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] and [[Luke Skywalker]], a new age Jedi and son of Darth Vader whom the Emperor sought to replace. However, at the [[Battle of Endor]], Vader turned on the Emperor and [[Death|killed]] him, saving his son and redeeming himself, fulfilling the prophecy of the [[Chosen One]]<ref name="Luke Bio Gallery">{{Databank|luke-skywalker-biography-gallery|Luke Skywalker Biography Gallery}}</ref> and leaving the [[Sith]] extinct. Anakin died in his son's arms moments later, though Luke was able to escape as the second Death Star exploded.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

As the Rebel Alliance [[Declaration of a New Republic|transformed]] into the [[New Republic]] and restored the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]],<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> Luke set out on his path to restore the Jedi way to [[the galaxy]].<ref name="SE 4">[[Shattered Empire 4|''Shattered Empire'' 4]]</ref>

[[File:BattleofJakku-SWM32.png|thumb|right|250px|The Empire capitulated to the New Republic following the Battle of Jakku, officially ending the Galactic Civil War.]]
The war between the New Republic and the Empire culminated at the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> one year later in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Though the Republic was victorious in battle, many select [[Imperial officer]]s, [[noble]]s, and [[soldier]]s were able to retreat into the [[Unknown Regions]] as part of a secret [[Contingency]] plan devised by the late Emperor to rebuild the Empire, though the plot to destroy [[Jakku]] and thus cripple both the Old Empire and the New Republic had failed. Imperial remnants that continued to persist in the known galaxy agreed to the [[Imperial Instruments of Surrender]] and signed the [[Galactic Concordance]] with the New Republic.<ref name="Aftermath EE">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

===Cold War===
{{Main|Cold War}}
{{Quote|We're on the brink of war again&mdash;one we may not survive.|Leia Organa|<ref name="Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref>}}
In the aftermath of the war, the New Republic made an earnest effort to rebuild what was lost over the course of the five-year conflict, also taking steps to ensure that nothing on that scale would ever happen again. The Galactic Senate passed the [[Military Disarmament Act]], heavily demilitarizing the [[New Republic Defense Force]] and instead pressuring individual [[planet]]s and [[star system]]s to rely on their local [[planetary defense forces]]. Despite the downsized military, the New Republic was still faced with threats sizable in proportion.<ref name="Episode VII VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

[[File:JakkuLitterbugs-TFAT2.png|thumb|left|250px|As a result of its defeat, the Empire fell and its military was dismantled by the Galactic Concordance.]]
Choosing to ignore the stipulations of the Galactic Concordance, some united remnants of the Empire refused to yield, culminating in a [[Cold War]] between the New Republic and the Imperial remnant. Another [[Inner systems' Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant state]] was confined to predetermined borders in the [[Core Worlds|Core]] and [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="Episode VII VD" /> while on the former [[capital]] world of [[Coruscant]], the central Imperial government was replaced by a [[Coruscant's provisional government|provisional government]] led by [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] that was monitored by Republic observers.<ref name="Aftermath EE" /> The Empire was reduced to a mere rump state hemmed in by strict disarmament treaties and punishing reparations<ref name="First Order DB">{{Databank|the-first-order|The First Order}}</ref> and was totally dissolved.<ref name="Aftermath EE" /> By [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Bloodline Date">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/652893780236988420|[[Del Rey]]|quote=So excited to have @claudiagray writing a #StarWars novel with us. SW: New Republic: Bloodline coming 2016. Set 6 years before TFA.|archivefile=File:New Republic Bloodline Twitter Post.jpg}} establishes that the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] take place six [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|VII}}. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], so ''Bloodline'' must take place in [[28 ABY]].</ref> Coruscant had become a full member of the Republic.<ref name="Bloodline" />

[[File:HuxAndStormtroopersAtCommandCenter-TFAOfficialTeaser.jpg|thumb|right|250px|After the end of the Empire, the First Order emerged in the Unknown Regions.]]
Over the years, Imperial loyalists plotted their return to power, where they established the [[First Order]], hidden away in the Unknown Regions, the First Order colonized planets and rapidly expanded its [[First Order military|military]] power, rebuilding Imperial [[First Order Navy|fleets]] and mobilizing [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] forces within its [[First Order Army|armies]], with the hopes of restoring Imperial glory. Tensions between Imperial sympathizers, many of whom were [[Centrists]], and Republic loyalists, many of whom were [[Populists]], eventually caused many former Imperials to secede from the Republic and join a growing First Order. While the notion was supported by many, others feared that, without Republic oversight, former Imperials would return to the draconian ways of the fallen Empire.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref>

[[File:Cool_Hand_XWM.png|thumb|left|250px|The Resistance was formed by Leia Organa during the Cold War between the New Republic and the First Order.]]
[[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], the twin sister of Skywalker, petitioned the Galactic Senate and [[New Republic High Command|New Republic Command]] to take action, but found herself sidelined by the political realm of the New Republic, especially after the revelation that Darth Vader was her and Luke's father in a political scandal between the Centrists and the Populists. With the Republic seeking to avoid another galactic war at all costs and not viewing the First Order as a credible threat to galactic peace and freedom, [[Princess]] Leia stepped down from her political office in [[28 ABY]]<ref name="Bloodline Date" /> and formed the breakaway [[Resistance]],<ref name="Bloodline" /> where she held the rank of [[General]]. Though not officially supported by the Republic, the Resistance was tolerated, and secretly, some within the Galactic Senate assisted in funding the private military force.<ref name="BTA">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref>

During this time, the First Order enlisted Captain [[Kragan Gorr]] and his [[Warbird gang]] of pirates to raid the ''[[Colossus]]'' on the ocean planet of [[Castilon]]. Commander [[Pyre]] and Major [[Elrik Vonreg|Vonreg]] hoped Gorr's attacks would force the ''Colossus''{{'s}} captain, [[Imanuel Doza]], to accept their aid.<ref name="Triple Dark">{{Resistance|The Triple Dark}}</ref> At first, Doza refused Vonreg's offer to protect the platform.<ref name="High Tower">{{Resistance|The High Tower}}</ref> However, after another [[Raid on the Colossus|raid]] and the [[Torra Doza's abduction|kidnapping]] of Doza's daughter, [[Torra Doza|Torra]], Doza agreed to Pyre's offer of First Order "protection."<ref name="The Doza Dilemma">{{Resistance|The Doza Dilemma}}</ref> Unbeknownst to Doza and the First Order, Commander Dameron and General Organa had sent [[Kazuda Xiono]] to the platform to find out about the First Order's plans.<ref name="Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref>

[[File:FirstOrderMap-TheNewTrooper.png|thumb|right|250px|Resistance spy Kazuda Xiono discovered evidence of the First Order's plans for war.]]
Xiono learned about the First Order's plans for the ''Colossus''<ref name="High Tower" /> and Ren's attack on [[Tehar]].<ref name="The Children from Tehar">{{Resistance|The Children from Tehar}}</ref> He also discovered that the First Order was preparing for war with mining operations<ref name="Station Theta black">{{Resistance|Station Theta Black (episode)}}</ref> and other operations in the [[Dassal system]].<ref name="Core Problem">{{Resistance|The Core Problem}}</ref>

After discovering the [[Corvus (Raider II-class)|''Corvus'']] abandoned on [[Pillio]], [[Iden Versio]] and [[Zay Versio]] began searching for [[Del Meeko]]. This led to them to [[Athulla]] where they went into [[Mission to Athulla|combat]] with a [[Jinata Security]] fleet led by [[Leema Kai]]. During the Battle, Leemai Kai was killed and the Jinata Security Fleet surrendered.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

===The search for Skywalker===
{{Quote|Lor San Tekka. An explorer. He's been all over the galaxy&mdash;there's not a sacred site or Jedi ruin he hasn't seen. He's supposed to be dead, but this holovid you intercepted shows him alive, and older than the last time I saw him. He could still be out there, and if he is, he's our best hope for finding Luke.|Leia Organa|<ref name="Poe Dameron 1">[[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]]</ref>}}
[[File:Tekka_and_Dameron_TFA.png|thumb|left|250px|Lor San Tekka aided the Resistance in their search for Jedi Master Luke Skywalker.]]
Shortly before the war between First Order and the Resistance formally began, Resistance pilot Poe Dameron was sent on a mission to find [[Lor San Tekka]], ally of General Organa, in hopes that he could lead them to the whereabouts of Skywalker, the last Jedi. Skywalker had resided in self-exile since the [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destruction]] of his generation Jedi by [[Ben Solo]], his nephew and the [[Solo family|son]] of Organa and [[Han Solo]]. Solo became the First Order warlord known as Kylo Ren, the [[Ren|master]] of the [[Knights of Ren]], apprentice to [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]. The [[Hunt for Lor San Tekka|search]] for San Tekka eventually led Dameron to [[Tuanul]] village on Jakku, where he found the old ally and retrieved a portion of a map that would lead the Resistance to Skywalker. However, before Dameron could escape, the First Order arrived also in search of San Tekka and the map.<ref name="Episode VII" />

With no other choice, Dameron elected to leave the map inside his droid BB-8 and sent him away to safety. With the map now out of the village, Kylo Ren arrived on the scene, killing San Tekka and capturing Dameron, and the First Order Stormtroopers led by [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] executed all of the villagers. Dameron, after being tortured by Kylo Ren, gave up the location of the map, and the First Order began a hunt to find BB-8. During the attack on Tuanul a stormtrooper, [[Finn|FN-2187]], became disillusioned and proceeded to help Dameron escape captivity aboard the ''[[Finalizer]]''. Their escape led them back to Jakku, where BB-8 had already befriended the [[Force-sensitive]] [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and would soon begin his journey back to the Resistance.<ref name="Episode VII" />

==The war==
===Early engagements===
====A declaration of war====
{{Quote|Today is the end of the Republic. The end of a regime that acquiesces to disorder. At this very moment in a system far from here, the New Republic lies to the galaxy while secretly supporting the treachery of the loathsome Resistance. This fierce machine which you have built, upon which we stand, will bring an end to the Senate, to their cherished fleet. [[File:DestructionoftheHosniansystem.png|thumb|right|250px|The New Republic capital was destroyed by Starkiller Base, leading to open war between the First Order and the Resistance.]]
With conflict rising between the First Order and the Resistance, the government of the New Republic under [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Lanever Villecham]] chose to focus more on trade relations with neutral regions of the galaxy such as the [[Trans-Hydian Borderlands]], unaware of the actual danger that they were in.<ref name="Episode VII VD" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the war began with<ref name="Episode VII" /> what became known as the [[Starkiller crisis]];<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> the First Order, long hidden away in the shadows of the Unknown Regions, used its [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]] to target and destroy the [[Hosnian system]], where the Republic [[capital]] of [[Hosnian Prime]] was located, resulting in the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]. The Galactic Senate and [[New Republic Home Fleet|Home Fleet]] of the [[New Republic Defense Fleet|Defense Fleet]] were completely obliterated,<ref name="Episode VII" /> and the Republic subsequently collapsed with the loss of its leadership and the majority of its Defense Fleet.<ref name="TLJ EE">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

====Battle of Vardos====
Meanwhile, on Vardos, [[Shriv Suurgav]] and Iden Versio confronted Commander [[Gideon Hask]], who killed [[Protectorate]] [[Gleb]]. The two were interrogated and were going to be executed, when fighting broke out between Jinata Security and the First Order. Soon most of the city was in combat with the First Order deploying armored and air units and Jinata Security doing likewise. Hask's Star Destroyer, the ''[[Retribution]]'', shot down the ''Corvus''. Suurgav and Versio were able to make it to one of the escape pods and found Zay in a sniper nest. They fought their way through the city and stole two fighters and made it way into the exhaust port by the engines.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

====Mission Aboard ''Retribution''====
The trio fought their way to the engineering room, which contained the schematics for the [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought]]. Unable to escape off the ship while in hyperspace, the trio sabotaged the reactor to throw the ship out of [[hyperspace]]. Iden had [[Dio]] reroute reinforcements to deal with less troopers. Just as Iden was arming the last explosive, Hask snuck up behind them and held Zay at gunpoint. However, Zay was able to break free and knock Hask down. Zay was thrown off the platform, but was caught by Iden at the last moment. Zay tossed the blaster to Iden who shot and killed Hask. However, Iden died from her wounds shortly after. Shriv and Zay detonated the explosives, ripping the ship out of hyperspace as Starkiller Base was destroyed. Shriv and Zay escaped on a [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]] and contacted Organa who sent them on a [[mission to the Outer Rim]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

====Response to the Hosnian Cataclysm====
{{Main|Battle of Starkiller Base}}
{{Quote|General, the Republic command&mdash;the entire Hosnian system&mdash;it's all&mdash;gone.|[[Taslin Brance]], to Leia Organa|<ref name="Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>}}
[[File:BattleOfStarkillerBase-CaptainPhasma1.png|thumb|left|250px|Resistance X-wing fighters engage the defenses of Starkiller Base as open warfare begins.]]
In the immediate aftermath of the attack on the Hosnian system and the collapse of the New Republic, a [[Battle of Takodana|battle]] broke out between the First Order and the Resistance at Pirate Queen [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on [[Takodana]]. The First Order attacked the castle to capture BB-8 while the Resistance sought to retrieve him.<ref name="Episode VII" /> Dameron later reflected on the importance of the battle as the first true contest between the Resistance and First Order in view of the public: the people of Kanata's castle were able to watch in awe as the Resistance swooped in to protect the innocent from a First Order assault.<ref name="Poe Dameron 27">[[Poe Dameron 27|''Poe Dameron'' 27]]</ref> The short battle resulted in a Resistance victory with BB-8 being reclaimed, but Rey, who, with the help of ex-stormtrooper Finn, had saved BB-8 on Jakku, was captured by Kylo Ren and Kanata's castle was destroyed. Once back at the Resistance base on D'Qar, the Resistance formulated a plan to attack the First Order's Starkiller Base before it could be used again.<ref name="Episode VII" />

With Finn, Solo, and Chewbacca threatening<ref name="Episode VII" /> the always self-interested<ref name="Captain Phasma 4">[[Captain Phasma 4|''Captain Phasma'' 4]]</ref> Captain Phasma into shutting down the base's shields, the Resistance launched a successful [[Battle of Starkiller Base|assault on Starkiller Base]], destroying the planetary superweapon and dealing a significant blow to the new war machine engineered by the First Order. The Resistance, however, lost Solo when he was killed by Ren,<ref name="Episode VII" /> whose guilt at the murder of his own father helped Rey<ref name="Episode VIII" /> in a subsequent duel with the dark side warrior.<ref name="Episode VII" /> Open warfare had begun,<ref name="SDCC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-last-jedi-props-and-costumes-on-display-at-sdcc|text=''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Props and Costumes on Display at SDCC}}</ref> but there was hope for the Resistance: using archival data inside the droid R2-D2 and the portion of the map the droid BB-8 had, they were able to complete the map to Skywalker. With this information, General Organa sent Rey to [[Ahch-To]] to find Skywalker.<ref name="Episode VII" />

[[File:StarkillerGoesBoom-BTCG.png|thumb|right|250px|The Battle of Starkiller Base ended in the destruction of the First Order superweapon.]]
Despite the loss of Starkiller Base,<ref name="Episode VII" /> the First Order stood ready to begin its campaign of conquest of the known galaxy from the Unknown Regions<ref name="Episode VIII VD" /> because the peaceful Republic had been decapitated, its political and military leadership wiped out,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and the galaxy at large was unaware of the destruction of Starkiller Base.<ref name="Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref> Rey would tell Luke Skywalker after finding the Jedi that all the major systems would fall within weeks to the First Order.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Furthermore, the First Order had achieved enough victories that Supreme Leader Snoke, long hidden away aboard his flagship, the [[Supremacy (song)|''Supremacy'']], in the Unknown Regions, decided to risk coming out of the shadows to personally lead the First Order to potential dominance over the Resistance.<ref name="Sphero">''[[Star Wars Droids App by Sphero]]''</ref> Phasma covered up her role in shutting down the shields of Starkiller by [[Hunt for Rivas|framing and executing]] the only witness to her crime, [[Sol Rivas]].<ref name="Captain Phasma 4" />

====Escape of the ''Colossus''====
During the First Order's [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupation]] of the ''Colossus'', the First Order arrested several outspoken dissidents including the [[Ace Squadron]] racer [[Hype Fazon]] and [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza|Aunt Z]] for objecting to the First Order presence and increasing restrictions. The dissidents escaped with the help of Xiono and Captain Doza's daughter Torra.<ref name="The Disappeared">{{Resistance|The Disappeared}}</ref>

After discovering Xiono's involvement in Dameron’s [[Mission to the Dassal system|mission]] to the [[Dassal system]], Stormtrooper Commander Pyre led a raid on former Rebel pilot Commander [[Jarek Yeager]] and his [[Team Fireball]]'s [[Jarek Yeager's Repair Station|garage]]. The entire team except [[Tamara Ryvora]], who was unaware of Yeager and Xiono's connections to the Resistance, managed to escape. To send a signal to the Resistance, Team Fireball submerged the ''Colossus''. However, General Organa was unable to provide reinforcements since her numbers were few and divided due to the search for BB-8 at the time; forcing Yeager's cell to fend for themselves.<ref name="Descent">{{Resistance|Descent (episode)}}</ref>

Following Hosnian Prime's destruction,<ref name="Episode VII" /> Xiono, Yeager, Team Fireball, Captain Doza, and Torra were able to end the First Order's occupation of the ''[[Colossus]]'' on [[Castilon]]. With the help of Ace Squadron and [[Kragan Gorr]]'s [[Warbird gang|Warbird]] pirate gang, the ''Colossus'' was able to escape from Commander Pyre's Star Destroyer ''[[Thunderer]]''.<ref name="No Escape 2" /> However, Ryvora along with the racer [[Jace Rucklin]] and mechanic [[Lin Gaava]] defected to the First Order.<ref name="No Escape 2" /><ref name="No Escape 1">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 1}}</ref> Having already harbored degrees of sympathy for the Order as the granddaughter of an [[Tamara Ryvora's grandfather|Imperial factory employee]],<ref name="The New Trooper">{{Resistance|The New Trooper}}</ref> Ryvora was angry that Yeager and Xiono did not disclosed their ties with the Resistance with her and joined the First Order to fulfill her aspirations of becoming a pilot.<ref name="No Escape 2" />

====Collapse of the New Republic====
{{Quote|The New Republic's home fleet is destroyed, and its surviving senators have dissolved the remaining task forces to protect their homeworlds. Their division makes them defenseless. No power in the galaxy can stand against us, Supreme Leader.|Armitage Hux, to Snoke|<ref name="TLJ EE" />}}
Despite the destruction of Starkiller Base, the First Order dispatched its legions in a blitzkrieg offensive against the greater galaxy. With the destruction of the Republic,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> entire planets<ref name="TLJ Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and star systems surrendered<ref name="TLJCollector">[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi – The Official Collector's Edition|''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' – The Official Collector's Edition]]</ref> under the belief that Starkiller Base still existed<ref name="Black Spire" /> and to avoid conflict with the First Order,<ref name="TLJ Visual Dictionary" /> whose army and navy stood unmatched<ref name="ResistanceAge">[[Star Wars: Age of Resistance – The Official Collector's Edition|''Star Wars: Age of Resistance'' – The Official Collector's Edition]]</ref> after the Republic's destruction.<ref name="TLJ EE" /> While some planets had already been leaning towards the First Order on the hope that it would provide the peace it promised, others surrendered entirely out of fear. Indeed, the destruction of the Hosnian system revealed to the galaxy that the First Order was willing to commit mass slaughter to meet its end goals.<ref name="TLJ Visual Dictionary" />

While Hux had claimed to the First Order that the unveiling of Starkiller Base would force the entire galaxy to heel, and his words would have great truth of them<ref name="TLJCollector" /> with the ensuing mass surrender,<ref name="TLJ Visual Dictionary" /> the First Order would need to deal with cases of planetary and system rebellion during its reign.<ref name="ResistanceAge" /> As the First Order Military advanced, the New Republic's surviving senators dissolved the remaining task forces in a effort to protect their own homeworlds, making what was left of the Republic defenseless against the First Order.<ref name="TLJ EE" /> The galaxy as a whole stood divided. Some planetary leaders, such as King [[Siroc]], acknowledged the overwhelming might of the First Order and refused to stand up.<ref name="Poe29">[[Poe Dameron 29|''Poe Dameron'' 29]]</ref> On other planets, such as [[Ikkrukk]], [[Ikkrukk First Order sympathizers|sympathizers]] and resisters [[Battle of Ikkrukk|fought]] amongst themselves.<ref name="Poe Dameron 30" />

===Pursuit===
====Trapping the Resistance====
{{Main|Battle of D'Qar}}
{{Quote|I have my orders from Supreme Leader Snoke himself. This is where we snuff out the Resistance once and for all.|Armitage Hux|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
As Supreme Leader Snoke deployed his legions to seize military control of the galaxy, General Armitage Hux led a [[fleet]] of the First Order Navy to D'Qar, where the Resistance was [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuating]] its base. General Hux's fleet intercepted the fleeing [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] above the planet. His [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyers]] and [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] were accompanied by a [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|Siege Dreadnought]]<ref name="Episode VIII" /> commanded by Captain [[Moden Canady]], a veteran of the Empire.<ref name="Episode VIII VD" /> The Siege Dreadnought used its two [[orbital autocannon]]s to obliterate the Resistance base on the planet surface, though all of its personnel had evacuated. Meanwhile, Commander Dameron flew his [[T-70 X-wing starfighter|T-70 X-wing]] ''[[Black One]]'' to the First Order Dreadnought with his upgraded engine, kicking it into high speed. He took out the dreadnought's defensive [[turbolaser]] emplacements, all the while evading TIE fighters, while his [[astromech droid]] BB-8 repaired his [[starfighter]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:The_Last_Jedi_Space_Battle.png|thumb|left|300px|The Resistance suffered heavy losses during their evacuation from D'Qar when the First Order attacked.]]
With the Resistance forces now joined with their fleet, General Organa ordered Dameron to disengage, but he disobeyed, seeing an opportunity to take out a First Order Dreadnought. Dameron's actions created an opening for [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17]] [[bomber]]s to unleash payloads on the First Order Dreadnought, accompanied by [[Blue Leader]] [[Tallissan Lintra]] and her escort starfighters. However, all of the Resistance bombers were destroyed, save for ''[[Cobalt Hammer]]'' where [[Paige Tico]] served as [[gunner]]. With the rest of the crew dead and herself injured, Paige unleashed the payload on the Dreadnought, creating a chain reaction of explosions that ultimately consumed her and her ship. Nonetheless, her sacrifice allowed the [[Resistance pilot]]s to return to their fleet and jump into [[hyperspace]]. However, because of his rash and dangerous actions, and because that stunt killed many much needed Resistance pilots, Organa demoted Dameron to Captain.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

====Attacking the fleet====
{{Main|Battle of Oetchi}}
{{Quote|They've tracked us through light speed.''"<br />"''That's impossible.''"<br />"''Yes, and they've done it.|Leia Organa and Finn|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
General Hux was reprimanded by Supreme Leader Snoke for his repeated failure, after he made contact with Snoke aboard the ''Supremacy'', the flagship of the shadowy Supreme Leader. Hux revealed his plan to track the Resistance fleet through hyperspace using new technology, which was done through the ''Supremacy''.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> When the Resistance fleet exited hyperspace in the [[Oetchi system]],<ref name="TLJ Junior Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> Hux's fleet arrived, reinforced by the ''Supremacy'' itself, which opened a barrage of fire on the Resistance [[cruiser]]s. The [[Battle of Oetchi]] by the First Order ultimately cornered the fleeing Resistance, as it was unable to jump into hyperspace again without burning all of the fleet's remaining fuel, which would be meaningless since the First Order would track them anyway, wherever they would go.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:Amilyn_Holdo_Ellen.png|thumb|right|300px|With General Organa incapacitated, command of the Resistance fleet defaulted to Vice Admiral Amilyn Holdo.]]
Kylo Ren and three wingmen engaged in strafing runs on the Resistance flagship [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']], destroying its hangar and burning away most of the [[Resistance Starfighter Corps]]. However, Ren was unable to destroy the bridge of the ''Raddus'', as his mother was still on board. His wingmen took shots instead, destroying the bridge and crippling the [[Resistance High Command|Resistance command]], among them [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]]. General Organa was able to use [[the Force]] to survive, but was in critical condition when the Resistance recovered her. The chain of command passed down to [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]], an old friend and student of Organa. Dameron came into conflict with the Vice Admiral; as the new leader of the Resistance, Holdo was disinclined to reveal her strategy to the demoted pilot.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

====Mission to Canto Bight====
{{Quote|So, the First Order is only tracking us from one Destroyer, the lead one. [&hellip;] But if we sneak on board the lead Destroyer, without them realizing, then we can&hellip;''"''<br />''"''They won't realize it's off for one system cycle. About six minutes.''"''<br />''"''Sneak on board. Disable the tracker. Our fleet escapes before they realize.|Finn and Rose Tico, to Poe Dameron|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
While attempting to desert the ''Raddus'', stormtrooper [[defector]] Finn, who, along with Dameron, Rey, Solo, and [[Chewbacca]], had been instrumental in destroying Starkiller Base, was caught by [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Rose Tico]]. Rose initially intended to hand him over to custody for attempting desertion, but when Finn revealed to her that they were being tracked through hyperspace, a new and foreign technology, she devised a plan to destroy its source. Finn and Rose proposed this to Poe, who informally approved of it. They reached out to [[Maz Kanata]], who directed them to find a [[Master Codebreaker]] on the [[casino]] [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:Rose_Finn_Canto_Bight_Empire_Magazine.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Resistance operatives Finn and Rose Tico traveled to Canto Bight seeking a codebreaker.]]
Finn, Rose, and BB-8 thus undertook a secret [[mission to Canto Bight]], but were arrested by local [[Canto Bight Police Department|police officers]] for illegal parking on a public beach. In their cell, they met the mysterious [[slicer]] [[DJ]], who opened the cell's doors through his own skill in code breaking. They evaded Canto Bight police as they rode [[fathier]]s through the city. When they found themselves trapped on a cliff, DJ and BB-8 arrived in a stolen [[Libertine|ship]], whose [[arms dealer]] had sold both to the First Order and the Resistance, as did most of the greedy, ultra-rich residents of and travelers to Canto Bight.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Seeking to buy time for Finn and Rose's mission, Poe went on to attempt a mutiny against Holdo when he discovered that she aimed to abandon the ''Raddus'', relieving her of her command and seizing the bridge. However, he was ultimately stunned by a reawakened Leia. He and the rest of the mutineers, including [[Lieutenant]] [[Kaydel Ko Connix]], were arrested and loaded onto the [[U-55 orbital loadlifter]] [[transport]]s. Admiral Holdo decided to remain behind in order to pilot the ''Raddus''.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

====Fighting on the ''Supremacy''====
{{Quote|It's time to let old things die. Snoke, Skywalker. The Sith, the Jedi, the rebels. Let it all die. Rey. I want you to join me. We can rule together and bring a new order to the galaxy.|Kylo Ren, to Rey|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:UndercoverFO.png|thumb|right|250px|Finn, Tico, and DJ infiltrated the ''Supremacy'' to disable its hyperspace tracker.]]
Finn, Tico, DJ, and BB-8 jumped to hyperspace and arrived in the [[Crait system]], where DJ snuck them aboard the ''Supremacy'', disguised in [[First Order officer]] uniforms. BB-8, who was covered by a box, was identified by the First Order droid [[BB-9E]], who proceeded to alert the First Order of their presence. DJ managed to slice into the room where the [[hyperspace tracker]] was stored, but the three were caught by [[First Order Security Bureau]] [[officer]] [[Ansiv Garmuth]] and a group of stormtroopers. The stormtroopers took Finn and Tico before General Hux and Phasma, who previously had been humiliated and left to die on Starkiller Base by Finn. With Hux's authorization, Phasma gave full payment to DJ, who revealed that he had betrayed the Resistance by revealing that their transport ships were discreetly fleeing to the [[mineral]] [[salt]] world of [[Crait]]. General Hux ordered for the ''Supremacy'' to open fire on the Resistance transports, while Captain Phasma brought forward two [[First Order stormtrooper executioner|executioner troopers]] to execute Finn and Tico by beheading for the trouble they caused.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Meanwhile, Rey, who had been training with Skywalker, the last Jedi and twin brother of General Organa, arrived aboard the ''Supremacy'', where she was greeted by Kylo Ren, whom she had been sharing a [[Force-bond|bond]] in the Force with. Kylo had two stormtroopers place Rey in handcuffs, and then brought her before his dark side master, Supreme Leader Snoke, who was seated upon his throne and flanked by his eight [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guard]] in his [[Snoke's throne room|throne room]]. Snoke falsely claimed that he bridged Rey and Kylo's minds in order to draw her out, and proceeded to mentally torture her for the location of Skywalker. Once he found Luke's location and revealed his intention to obliterate the entire [[Temple Island]], Rey reacted by attempting to grab [[Skywalker lightsaber|her lightsaber]], though Snoke held on to it. He showed her to an oculus viewing scope, where she was made to watch as the ''Supremacy'' destroyed the Resistance transports one by one. She used the Force to grab [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber]] and charged Snoke, but he threw her once more across the room to Kylo and sentenced her to death at Kylo's hand for having the spirit of a true Jedi. As Kylo reached for his [[lightsaber]] and picked it up, Snoke taunted Rey for believing she could turn his apprentice. The overconfident Snoke watched Kylo's mind as he readied the lightsaber to strike down his true enemy, unaware that he himself was, in fact, Kylo's true enemy. Kylo used the Force to subtly turn Skywalker's lightsaber and sliced through Snoke, cutting the Supreme Leader in half and releasing Rey from his grip.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:ReyRenVsPraetorianGuard-TLJTUG.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Kylo Ren killed his master, Supreme Leader Snoke, and defeated the Elite Praetorian Guards with Rey's help.]]
Rey stood, taking Skywalker's lightsaber while Kylo activated his as they were surrounded by the eight Elite Praetorian Guard, ready to avenge the Supreme Leader. The two warriors engaged in a brutal melee with Snoke's guards, and ultimately overcame them, working together to kill them one by one until the last fell when Rey threw Skywalker's lightsaber to Kylo, allowing him to stab the guardsman through his eye. Rey, still calling him Ben, urged Kylo to order the First Order to stop firing on the Resistance fleet, but Kylo instead had his eyes on Snoke's corpse. He turned to Rey and urged her to let go of the past: Snoke, Skywalker, the Sith, the Jedi, the rebels, all of it. He sought to build a new order for the galaxy with Rey by his side, but a teary eyed Rey instead reached for Skywalker's lightsaber. The two used the Force to fight for it, ultimately causing it to snap in half and explode, the resulting shockwave pushing them back.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

With the ''Supremacy'' opening fire on the transports, Admiral Holdo, still on board the ''Raddus'', acted quickly. Taking control of the [[heavy cruiser]], she re-positioned the [[starship]] towards the ''Supremacy'' and its escort [[Star Destroyer]]s and jumped into hyperspace, slicing through the Mega-Destroyer in half and at least twenty Resurgent Star Destroyers, sacrificing herself.<ref name="Poe Dameron 31">[[Poe Dameron 31|''Poe Dameron'' 31]]</ref> The distraction and resulting chaos saved Finn and Tico from execution and allowed Rey to escape aboard Snoke's escape craft and rendezvous with Chewbacca and [[R2-D2]] onboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. BB-8 hijacked a First Order [[First Order All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] [[walker]] and began open firing on stormtroopers while Finn engaged in a melee with Captain Phasma. Though Phasma knocked him down, Finn gained the upper hand when he landed on an elevating platform, knocking back the surprised Phasma as she was taking shots at Tico. Phasma cursed Finn one last time as the floor around her collapsed, and she fell into the remains of the ship to her fiery death. Finn and Tico escaped on BB-8's hijacked AT-ST and reached a transport shuttle, taking flight to Crait.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

When Hux entered the Supreme Leader's throne room, he found the corpses of Snoke and his Elite Praetorian Guard, as well as Ren lying on the ground, unconscious. Hux reached for his [[blaster pistol]] to kill his rival, but Ren regained consciousness before Hux could do so. Ren told Hux that it was Rey who had killed Snoke, and ordered him to ready their forces, which were relatively unscathed, to march on Crait. An angered Hux questioned Ren's authority to command him and was still reeling from shock that their Supreme Leader was dead, but Ren [[Force choke]]d the general into obedience, allowing Ren to usurp the title of Supreme Leader for himself.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===The Resistance's stand at Crait===
{{Main|Battle of Crait}}
{{Quote|The Rebellion is reborn today. The war is just beginning. And I will not be the last Jedi.|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Resistanceheroes-BattleofCrait.png|thumb|right|330px|The First Order's forces almost destroyed the Resistance during the Battle of Crait.]]
Resistance forces regrouped in an [[Crait outpost|abandoned base]] from the days of the Rebellion on the salt mineral world of Crait, protected by several outposts and a massive door, and dug in trenches where [[Resistance trooper]]s held their ground. Supreme Leader Kylo Ren took personal command of the [[Battle of Crait]] himself from his [[Kylo Ren's command shuttle|command shuttle]] alongside General Hux. The First Order deployed TIE fighters, [[All Terrain MegaCaliber Six|AT-M6s]], and [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-ATs]] to escort their [[superlaser siege cannon]], hauled across the salty dunes by [[All Terrain Heavy Hauler]]s. In response, Resistance troopers opened fire while surviving Resistance pilots and other fighters piloted rebel [[V-4X-D ski speeder]]s salvaged from the abandoned base, among them Poe, Finn, and Rose. First Order TIEs were able to take out several ski speeders but were drawn away when the ''Millennium Falcon'' arrived, piloted by Chewbacca and gunned by Rey. Nevertheless, the ski speeders were unable to reach the superlaser siege cannon on time and were ordered by Poe to fall back. Finn attempted to fly straight into the siege cannon before it could fire to stop it, but Rose stopped him from sacrificing himself by crashing into him, later justifying that they would win the war not by fighting the things they hate, but by saving the things that they love. Finn soon returned to the base dragging along an unconscious Rose.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

The surviving Resistance troops fell back to the base, which had a new opening as the superlaser siege cannon fired on it. With their allies in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] not responding, the few remaining Resistance forces were demoralized, among them General Organa, who declared that the galaxy had lost all hope. However, in that moment, Master Skywalker, older twin brother of General Organa, suddenly arrived. Skywalker reunited with Organa and told her that he came to face her son, Kylo Ren. Organa confessed she knew her son was gone, though Skywalker replied that none were truly ever gone before handing her [[Han Solo's dice|husband's dice]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Skywalker walked out alone onto the battlefield to face the First Order. Ren ordered all available guns to open fire on Skywalker. He allowed his emotions to overcome him as the First Order walkers fired on the Jedi, demanding for heavier firepower, though General Hux eventually called it off. They were stunned, however, to find Skywalker had survived without a scratch. Ren went down to face his uncle alone, ordering his forces to stay behind, buying time for the few surviving Resistance fighters to escape. Led by Poe, they followed native [[Vulptex|vulptices]] to the back of the base where they were fleeing, but they found themselves trapped from the outside by a mountain of rocks.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:LukeKyloCrait.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Skywalker confronted Ren, his fallen nephew and former apprentice, in order to save the Resistance.]]
Meanwhile, Ren and Skywalker ignited their lightsabers. Ren took the first strike at Skywalker but missed. He attempted to strike Skywalker a second time, but he was able to dodge his [[plasma]] blade and evade Ren. Skywalker apologized to his nephew for failing him, but Ren didn't accept it, telling Skywalker that the Resistance was dead and the war had ended, and that when he will slay his uncle, he will slay the last Jedi. Skywalker responded, however, that the rebellion was reborn, the war was just beginning, and that he would not be the last Jedi. Meanwhile, Chewbacca landed near the rubble of rocks, and Rey used the Force to lift them away, allowing the Resistance to flee and escape onto the ''Millennium Falcon''. When Skywalker reminded Ren of his father, Ren launched himself towards his uncle again, this time not missing, yet inexplicably Skywalker had lived. Ren walked up to his uncle and stuck his lightsaber through his chest, revealing that Skywalker was in fact using the Force to [[Force projection|project]] himself. Skywalker told his nephew he would see him again before disappearing.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Kylo Ren, General Hux, and their [[First Order snowtrooper|snowtroopers]] marched onto the base only to find it abandoned once more. The few remaining survivors of the Resistance had escaped aboard the ''Millennium Falcon'', saved by the Jedi. Finn comforted an unconscious Rose while Leia and Rey, the latter holding the broken Skywalker lightsaber, discussed feeling Skywalker passing away, having become one with the Force after his projection. Rey worried that they would not be able to rebuild the Resistance with so few left, however Leia told her that they had everything that they needed in order to do so.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> The aftermath of the Battle of Crait spread across the galaxy, with the Resistance's escape and the role of the Skywalker twins, especially Luke, inspiring rumors across several worlds.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 5">[[Legacy of Vader 5|''Legacy of Vader'' 5]]</ref> Knowing that these rumors could encourage resistance against the First Order, Kylo Ren sought to suppress them<ref name="Legacy of Vader 2">[[Legacy of Vader 2|''Legacy of Vader'' 2]]</ref> and make them appear as exaggerated Resistance propaganda.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 5" />

===The wider war===
====Defense of Grail City====
{{Main|Battle of Ikkrukk}}
At the same time as the Battle of Crait,<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Black Squadron came to the aid of [[Ikkrukk's government]] upon learning [[Grail City]] was under siege from the First Order and its [[Ikkrukk First Order sympathizers|local sympathizers]]. Learning about the trouble his squadron was in thanks to a transmission the ''Falcon'', fresh from the Battle of Crait, received from [[Jessika Pava]],<ref name="Poe Dameron 30" /> Dameron&mdash;promoted back to Commander by Organa&mdash;rushed to his squadron's aid with Organa's blessing and arrived aboard a [[Unidentified starfighter|starfighter]] he loaned from Grakkus the Hutt. The reinvigorated Black Squadron was able to rally and overwhelm the First Order attackers, who had not been aware of Snoke's death until Dameron told them. While [[Prime Minister]] [[Grist]] of the Ikkrukk government initially promised to support the Resistance,<ref name="Poe Dameron 31">[[Poe Dameron 31|''Poe Dameron'' 31]]</ref> she quickly went back on her word, leaving the Resistance without her aid.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

====Resistance reborn====
{{Quote|I will not suffer the existence of any Resistance cell. No matter how small.|Kylo Ren|<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:KyloRen-TheEscape.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The survival of the Resistance was intolerable to Kylo Ren, the new Supreme Leader of the First Order.]]
Despite their escape from Crait, the Resistance was still reduced to a handful of personnel and equipment. With the ''Millennium Falcon'' running low on fuel, Organa decided to land and refuel at [[Ryloth]], one of the few places she felt was safe from the First Order and where she hoped to recruit some allies. The ''Falcon'' arrived at Ryloth, where they were quickly intercepted and guided to a secret location by an unknown militia. The militia revealed itself to be the [[Ryloth Defense Authority]], an autonomous army operating with full permission from the Ryloth government. From there Organa met with the leader of the Authority, [[Yendor Brethen|Yendor]], who explained that after Ryloth's history of subjugation the Authority was formed in response.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

Yendor told Organa that the First Order had been placing pressure on Ryloth and their trade, and, while it was unlikely that the capitol would betray them, the Resistance's time on Ryloth was limited to a few days. Organa then ordered the Resistance members spread out across the galaxy to convene on Ryloth, and to bring with them any allies and resources they were able to acquire. [[Jessika Pava]] and [[Suralinda Javos]] were able to bring [[Teza Nasz]], a former Imperial turned warlord, while Major [[Temmin Wexley]] and his wife [[Karé Kun]] brought with them a handful of pilots led by his mentor and stepfather [[Wedge Antilles]] and his mother [[Norra Wexley]]. Finally, [[Inferno Squad (Resistance)|Inferno Squad]] arrived with General [[Carlist Rieekan]] and a handful of ex-Imperials who were friends of Iden and did not join the First Order. Dameron, while unable to recruit either Maz Kanata or [[Grist]], informed Organa that the First Order was rounding up Resistance sympathizers and they had a list of more.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

[[File:BraccaPlanet-JFO.jpg|thumb|left|200px|The Resistance infiltrated Bracca, acquiring starships in order to rebuild its forces.]]
While Organa wanted to pursue the list, she also knew that without more ships the Resistance would not be able to continue its war effort. That was when Kanata contacted the Resistance, informing them that an organization known as the [[Collective]] was selling the Resistance list to the highest bidder on [[Corellia]]. Dameron decided to split their resources between the two objectives, with Inferno Squad going to [[Bracca]] in order to acquire ships while he and Wedge led a team to Corellia for the list and more ships.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

The [[mission to Bracca]] was ultimately successful, with the Resistance suffering minimal casualties and stealing a handful of [[X-wing starfighter]] models. The [[mission to Corellia]] got more complicated when the First Order raided the auction that the list was being sold at, though Finn and Dameron were able to save their Collective contact and the list she had. At the Corellian shipyard, Antilles and Wexley managed to rescue several political prisoners and steal a [[CR90 corvette]]. The two teams met and fled Corellia, buying the list from the Collective.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

Meanwhile, on Ryloth, the Resistance was attacked by the First Order after the capitol caved in and gave up their location. The Resistance, along with Yendor and the Authority, [[Attack on Ryloth|fled]] the planet and rendezvoused with the Corellian and Bracca teams at a Collective safe house. With the list in their hands and more ships at their disposal, Organa sent the Resistance back out into the galaxy in order to find, protect and recruit the people on the list, ensuring that the Resistance had a future to rebuild.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

====Pursuing the Resistance====
[[File:MandatorIV-Allegiance1.png|thumb|right|250px|The First Order conquered large swathes of the galaxy under Supreme Leader Kylo Ren.]]
In the time following Crait, the First Order began attacking planets that had even the remotest of contact with the Resistance. [[Destruction of Tah'Nuhna|Destroying]] the neutral planet of Tah'Nuhna for briefly hosting the Resistance<ref name="Allegiance 1">[[Allegiance 1|''Allegiance'' 1]]</ref> and taking over the [[Fondor Shipyards]] for communicating with the Resistance, despite the latter rejecting it.<ref name="Allegiance 2">[[Allegiance 2|''Allegiance'' 2]]</ref> Unable to stand the tragedy the First Order was causing across the galaxy, Organa decided to plead the Resistance's cause to [[Mon Cala]]. With [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], [[Rose Tico|Rose]], and [[C-3PO]] went to Mon Cala, where they were hostility greeted.<ref name="Allegiance 1" /> They were unable to convince King [[Ech-Char]] and [[Nossor Ri]] of their cause, but after being attacked by Quarren extremists and being informed that the First Order was coming the King and Nossor reluctantly joined the Resistance. With Nossor Ri sacrificing himself, the Resistance fled with a small fleet of ships and Gial’s son Colonel [[Aftab Ackbar]]. The First Order descended upon Mon Cala for their support of the Resistance, with Hux ominously stating that they would show the galaxy what happens to those who supported the Resistance.<ref name="Allegiance 4">[[Allegiance 4|''Allegiance'' 4]]</ref>

At the same time. the First Order was offering bounties in the millions for known Resistance heroes like Finn and Poe. On their [[Mission to the moon of Avedot|mission]] to acquire weapons for the Resistance they ran into the [[Kendoh Gang]]. The Kendoh Gang, however, were unable to capture Finn and Dameron, who escaped with the weapons.<ref name="Allegiance 1" /><ref name="Allegiance 2" /><ref name="Allegiance 3">[[Allegiance 3|''Allegiance'' 3]]</ref><ref name="Allegiance 4" />

====At the galaxy's edge====
[[File:GalaxysEdge-BlackSpire-CoverArt.png|thumb|left|150px|Resistance Spy Vi Moradi, on Batuu]]
Resistance spy [[Vi Moradi]] was tasked by Leia to build a Resistance base that was well hidden from the First Order, Vi chose the planet [[Batuu]] as it was out of the way and isolated from the rest of the galaxy. Vi, accompanied by former First Order captain [[Archex]], after some bumps acquired some land and equipment to build the base. However, things were complicated by the arrival of Lieutenant [[Wulfgar Kath]] and his First Order squad. Vi was able to turn the tables on the First Order, with Archex sacrificing himself to kill Kath, tricking them into thinking that she died and that the Resistance had no interest in [[Batuu]]. With the First Order gone, the Resistance base flourished for four months. With recruitment and training being highly successful, Vi considered expanding the base to include landing pads for Resistance ships in need of fuel. Before she could go through with those plans, Vi was informed by [[Green Leader (Resistance)|Green Leader]] that the First Order was returning to Batuu with the [[709th Legion]] and Supreme Leader [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].<ref name="Black Spire" />

On Batuu, [[Weequay]] pirate [[Hondo Ohnaka]] hired a team of freelance pilots to [[Heist on a First Order train|steal]] a shipment of [[coaxium]] from the First Order, which was then supplied to the Resistance.

====Hunt for the ''Colossus''====
{{Main|Hunt for the Colossus}}
Knowing that the ''Colossus'' was a valuable resource to General Organa, Captain Phasma ordered Commander Pyre and [[First Order Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Tierny]] to [[Hunt for the Colossus|hunt]] down the ''Colossus'' and since they not secure the station, she ordered them to destroy it. After the ''Colossus'' fled into hyperspace, Xiono, his droid [[CB-23]] and Torra thwarted an attempt by the First Order BB-series astromech droid [[MB-13A]] to transmit the station's location to the First Order.<ref name="Into the Unknown">{{Resistance|Into the Unknown}}</ref>

Following the Battle of D'Qar, the ''Colossus'' arrived and found debris left from both the Resistance and First Order forces during the battle. The [[Team Colossus|Resistance operatives]] and the Warbird gang found the ''Fulminatrix''{{'s}} coaxium and escaped the ''Thunderer'' arrived.<ref name="A Quick Salvage Run" /> To defend their home from the First Order, Yeager and Xiono retrained Ace Squadron as starfighter pilots.<ref name="Live Fire">{{Resistance|Live Fire}}</ref>

Later, Tierny dispatched the [[Nikto]] spy [[Nenavakasa Nalor]] to sabotage the ''Colossus''. Posing as a refugee, Nena won over the station's Nikto mechanic [[Neeku Vozo]] and the two developed romantic feelings for each other. Though Nena managed to sow a wedge between the ''Colossus'' residents and Warbird pirates, her plot was foiled by Xiono and the pirate [[Synara San]], who led efforts to reverse her damage to the station's systems. Despite escaping the ''Thunderer'', Team Colossus were unable to capture Nena, who managed to escape but since she failed to disable the Colossus, Agent Tierny marked her for death and Commander Pyre was more than happy to kill her if they cross paths with the Nikto engineer again.<ref name="The Engineer">{{Resistance|The Engineer}}</ref>

While gathering supplies on the planet [[Ashas Ree]], a landing party led by Xiono encountered a squad of [[First Order Raider]]s, who were gathering [[Sith]] relics for Supreme Leader Ren. However, Team Colossus were able to escape with the help of the [[archaeologist]] [[Mika Grey]], who used a [[Unidentified Sith relic|Sith relic]] to destroy their pursuers. Mika subsequently joined the ''Colossus'' community.<ref name="Relic Raiders">{{Resistance|The Relic Raiders}}</ref>

On Torra's birthday, Captain Doza activated a [[signal beacon]] in order to rendezvous with his wife Commander [[Venisa Doza]] for a family reunion because it was always their tradition ever since she joined the Resistance. However, this attracted the attention of the First Order, resulting in a [[Skirmish by a signal beacon|brief skirmish]] with the ''Thunderer''{{'s}} TIE fighters. Though the ''Colossus'' managed to escape, Venisa was captured by the ''Thunderer''. During her brief captivity, Venisa befriended Tamara and convinced her to question her loyalty and motives for joining the First Order. Venisa and her droid [[Torch]] subsequently [[Escape of Venisa Doza|escaped]] the ''Thunderer'' with Tamara's help and the young girl kept her interaction with Torra's mother a secret from Tierny to protect her.<ref name="Rendezvous Point">{{Resistance|Rendezvous Point (episode)}}</ref>

Xiono, Vozo, and CB-23 later [[Infiltration of the Titan|infiltratred]] to the First Order-occupied supertanker ''[[Titan]]'' and stole a [[trans-binary deflector]] in order to protect the ''Colossus'' from [[Cosmic radiation|cosmic rays]]. Ryvora's conflicting loyalties to her former friends led her to help them escape. The successful heist angered General Hux, who was having a meeting with Pyre to get an update on the First Order's stock of refueling stations and hunt for the Resistance cells for Leader Ren, causing him to reiterate the late Captain Phasma's orders for Commander Pyre to destroy the ''Colossus'' since it was the only supertanker not in the First Order's hands and could not risk it falling into the hands of the Resistance.<ref name="Station to Station">{{Resistance|Station to Station}}</ref>

The ''Colossus'' later traveled to [[Varkana]] during a [[Rescue of Norath Kev|mission]] by Xiono, Yeager, San, and CB-23 to rescue the Resistance spy [[Norath Kev]].<ref name="The Missing Agent">{{Resistance|The Missing Agent}}</ref> When the ''Thunderer'' entered the system to investigate the presence of Resistance agents, Captain Doza hid the station inside a nearby red gas giant. After bounty hunter [[Ax Tagrin]] managed to locate the station in return for his overdue payment, the ''Colossus'' retreated into hyperspace. After Xiono's team managed to rescue Kev, they were evacuated by [[Ace Squadron]] and the ''Colossus''.<ref name="Breakout">{{Resistance|Breakout}}</ref>

The ''Colossus'' later settled down on the ocean world of [[Aeos Prime]], where they were welcomed by the indigenous [[Aeosian]]s; who had previously been [[Attack on Aeos Prime|attacked]] by the First Order because their world housed one of the Rebellion's outpost.<ref name="The New World">{{Resistance|The New World}}</ref> However, a First Order [[Spider probe droid]] alerted the ''Thunderer'' to the presence of the ''Colossus''. The ''Thunderer'' deployed TIE fighters and [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|First Order bombers]] to destroy the ''Colossus'' but they were opposed by Ace Squadron and their new Aeosian allies, who rode [[Krakavora]] steeds. As a result, the ''Colossus'' was able to escape into hyperspace.<ref name="No Place Safe">{{Resistance|No Place Safe}}</ref>

Later, Captain Doza rendezvoused with his wife Venisa and her [[Jade Squadron]], whose pilots included Xiono's fellow [[New Republic]] wingmate [[Hugh Sion]] and Kev. Doza agreed to lend three pilots from Ace Squadron to assist with the evacuation of Resistance recruits from [[Dantooine]], which was being [[Blockade of Dantooine|blockaded]] by the First Order and give the recruits refuge until Venisa found a new location for them since the First Order cracked the Resistance's rendezvous coordinates. During the mission, Ace and Jade Squadrons managed to evacuate two ships carrying Resistance recruits. However, one ship was shot down during the dogfight. Ryvora was promoted to [[Lieutenant]] and Squadron Leader of her own TIE squadron following the death of her commanding officer and [[flight instructor]] Lieutenant [[Galek]]. Following the events on Dantooine, Captain Doza decided to support the Resistance and allowed Jade Squadron and the recruits to remain on the ''Colossus''.<ref name="Rebuilding the Resistance">{{Resistance|Rebuilding the Resistance}}</ref>

In retaliation for the Aeosians' support for the ''Colossus'', a First Order fleet [[Suppression of the Aeos system|orbitally bombarded]] Aeos Prime. This brutal action made Tam decide to defect since she realized Xiono and Yeager were right of how the Empire and the First Order were brutal and genocidal organizations so she contacted the Resistance spy and made arrangements to return to Castilon. However, Agent Tierny and Commander Pyre realized Tam's intentions and tracked her, Xiono, Yeager, and CB-23 down. By that stage, Supreme Leader Ren grew impatient with Tierny and Pyre for failing follow through with the late Phasma’s order for them to destroy the ''Colossus'' and threatened them with death if they failed one more time.<ref name="The Escape" />

Though their shuttle was captured by the ''Thunderer''{{'s}} tractor beam, they managed to elude their First Order pursuers and flee into the ship's engine rooms. Since Rucklin alerted the First Order to the ''Colossus’'' presence in the [[Barabesh system]], Xiono and Tam broadcast a warning message to the ''Colossus'' in the ''Thunderer''{{'s}} engine room. Following a town meeting convened by Captain Doza, the residents of the ''Colossus'' at the urging of Aunt Z and Synara San decided to help Xiono and Tam by standing their ground. Xiono, Yeager and Tam were subsequently recaptured by Commander Pyre but CB-23 managed to escape.<ref name="The Escape" />

Upon entering the Barabesh system, the ''Thunderer'' was attacked by Ace and Jade squadrons. Though the First Order dispatched boarding parties aboard the ''Colossus'', they were repelled by the local residents. During the battle, Xiono and his friends took the opportunity to escape their First Order captors. CB-23 also managed to lower the Star Destroyer's deflector shields, allowing Ace and Jade Squadron to take out the ship's engines. Xiono defeated Commander Pyre in combat while Supreme Leader Ren Force choked Agent Tierny for her failure to follow through with Pyre’s and her order to destroy the ''Colossus''. Following the battle, Tam was welcomed by her friends in Team Fireball, Ace Squadron, and the [[Doza family]].<ref name="The Escape" />

====Discovering the Final Order====
{{Quote|We've decoded the intel from the First Order spy and it confirms the worst. Somehow Palpatine returned. [&hellip;] He's been planning his revenge. His followers have been building something for years. The largest fleet the galaxy has ever known. He calls it the Final Order. In sixteen hours, attacks on all free worlds begin. Sometime in [[35 ABY]], a [[Palpatine's broadcast|broadcast]] was put out to the galaxy using the voice of the thought-to-be dead Galactic Emperor Sheev Palpatine, the secret identity of Darth Sidious, which promised revenge. Supreme Leader Ren undertook a [[Mission to Mustafar (First Order)|mission]] to [[Mustafar]], where he obtained a [[Sith wayfinder]] device that once belonged to his grandfather and it led him to the hidden world of the Sith, [[Exegol]], in the Unknown Regions. There, he found Darth Sidious, who [[Transference|came]] [[Cloning|back]] to life following his death, and discovered his [[Sith Eternal|followers]] who spent [[Standard year|years]] building a massive fleet of [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]], the [[Final Order]]. Sidious planned to unleash his [[Sith Eternal military|forces]] and create a new [[Sith Empire]]. He then offered Ren the fleet if he killed Rey, the last Jedi.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Resistance agents Commander Dameron, Finn, and Chewbacca obtained intelligence from [[Boolio]], an [[Ovissian]] middleman for a mole in the First Order, at their [[Mission to Sinta Glacier Colony|mission]] to the [[Sinta Glacier Colony]]. They escaped the First Order and returned to their [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|new base]] on [[Ajan Kloss]], where they decoded the intel and learnt Darth Sidious returned with a massive fleet planning to attack the free worlds from Exegol to complete the First Order's conquering of the galaxy and finally restore the Empire. Rey recognized the name from the journals of Master Skywalker, who was searching for the world in an effort to end the Jedi Order’s ancient war with the Sith. Leia sent her, Finn, Poe, Chewbacca, C-3PO, and BB-8 on a [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|mission]] to [[Pasaana]], where she believed an old ally was located who could point them on a path to a wayfinder.<ref name="Episode IX" />

The First Order killed Boolio and Kylo Ren presented his head before the [[First Order High Command|Supreme Council]] on his new flagship, the [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']]. General [[Domaric Quinn]] expressed his distrust of the Sith Eternal, but [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]] believed allying with the Sith fleet would be beneficial as it would increase their forces ten thousandfold in preparation towards their ultimate push for galactic conquest and correct Hux’s error of Starkiller Base. Ren silenced Quinn and ordered his councilors to subjugate any worlds that defied them before announcing that he was going hunting for Rey with the [[Knights of Ren]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

====Hunting the scavenger====
{{Main|Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)}}
{{Quote|This fleet, what is it, a gift? What is [Palpatine] asking for in return?''"''<br />''"''Prepare to crush any worlds that defy us. My Knights and I are going hunting for the scavenger.|Domaric Quinn and Kylo Ren, before the Supreme Council|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:RiseCollectorEdition-mission-on-Pasaana.png|thumb|left|270px|The Resistance embarked on their quest to find Exegol by first traveling to Pasaana.]]
The Resistance team arrived on Pasaana amidst the [[Festival of the Ancestors]] that occurred once every 42 years, and were found by General [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. He explained that he and Skywalker were tracking down a [[Jedi hunter|Jedi Hunter]] named [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]], and he abandoned his ship in the desert. The team left Calrissian, who said his days of fighting were over, and sped into the desert on commandeered speeders, but Kylo Ren tracked Rey there after opening their Force-bond. The team were pursued by [[First Order jet trooper]]s, who destroyed their speeders over a sinkhole near Ochi's ship, the ''[[Bestoon Legacy]]'', causing the team to sink beneath the sand. There, Rey used the Force to [[Force healing|heal]] an injured worm and found Ochi's remains along with a [[Blade of Ochi of Bestoon|dagger]]. With the ''Millennium Falcon'' confiscated by the First Order, they instead took the ''Bestoon Legacy'', but Chewbacca was captured by the Knights of Ren. Rey confronted Ren in the desert; the two fought over a First Order transport with the Force, and Rey accidentally destroyed it with [[Force lightning]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

She escaped with Finn, Poe, C-3PO, and BB-8, who believed Chewbacca was dead, but he was actually on another transport. While on Ochi's ship, BB-8 recharged the droid [[D-O]], which Ochi had mistreated. C-3PO's programming forbid him from translating the Sith inscription on Ochi's dagger, which revealed the location of another Sith wayfinder device leading to Exegol, so the team traveled to [[Kijimi]], where Poe knew a [[droidsmith]]. They were caught by [[Zorii Bliss]] and the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]], which Poe used to be a member of, but Bliss agreed to help them after a brief scuffle with Rey. The droidsmith [[Babu Frik]] bypassed C-3PO's forbidden databank, and they learned that the Emperor's wayfinder device in his [[Emperor's Throne Room|throne room]] on the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] in the [[Endor system]]. Meanwhile, the Knights of Ren tracked down the Resistance to Kijimi, and Kylo Ren with his Star Destroyer and launched a massive manhunt for the Resistance in the [[Kijimi City|city]]. Rey sensed Chewbacca through the Force on the ''Steadfast'', so the team decided to mount a rescue.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Using a captain's medallion provided by Bliss, Poe, Rey, and Finn infiltrated the ''Steadfast''. Poe and Finn rescued Chewbacca while Rey recovered Ochi's dagger and Chewbacca's belongings in Ren's quarters, during which time Ren initiated their bond. The two briefly dueled, during which time Ren told Rey that Darth Sidious had her parents killed by Ochi when they wouldn't reveal where she was since they knew he was looking for her. Poe, Finn, and Chewbacca were captured by stormtroopers and brought before General Hux to be killed, but Hux saved their lives, revealing himself to be the spy. Hux brought them to the ''Millennium Falcon'' while Ren confronted Rey in another hangar, where he further revealed to her that she was the Emperor's granddaughter since her father was his [[Dathan|son]]. He also revealed that together they were a [[Force dyad]], two that are one in the Force, and offered his hand again as he wanted her to join him to kill Sidious and take the Throne of the Sith together. Rey hesitated but then escaped aboard the ''Falcon'' with Poe, Finn, Chewbacca, and the droids. Hux attempted to fabricate a story to Pryde, but the Allegiant General saw through the young general's lies and summarily executed him for attempting to make Leader Ren lose the war. Sidious communed with Supreme Leader Ren through the Force, threatening to turn his fleet against the Supreme Leader if Ren betrayed him, but Ren responded that he knew where Rey was going.<ref name="Episode IX" />

====Finding the Emperor's wayfinder====
{{Main|Mission to Kef Bir}}
{{Quote|The only way you're getting to Exegol is with me.|Kylo Ren, to Rey|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
Rey, Finn, Poe Dameron, Chewbacca, and the droids C-3PO, BB-8, and D-O crash landed on [[Kef Bir]], the [[ocean]] [[moon]] of [[Endor (planet)|Endor]]. Upon finding the ruins of the Death Star in the moon's seas and using Ochi's dagger to pinpoint the precise location of the Emperor's Sith wayfinder, the Resistance members were surrounded by [[Jannah]] and her [[Company 77|tribe]] of [[Orbak]] riders, but after learning they were with the Resistance, Jannah agreed to aid them in their mission. She warned that they would need to wait until the next day for the tides to calm, and the team went to repair the ''Falcon'', during which time Finn learned that Jannah and her tribe were actually a company of First Order stormtroopers who refused to fire on civilians and promptly deserted like he did.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:KyloVsRey.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Ren fought against Rey amidst the ruins of the second Death Star.]]
Rey impatiently took a [[skiff]] and piloted to the Death Star ruins anyway. In a side room located within the Emperor's throne room, Rey found the Sith wayfinder, but also confronted a [[Force vision|vision]] of her dark side self. In the throne room, Kylo Ren awaited her; as she stumbled back into the throne room, Ren took the wayfinder and destroyed it, leaving Rey with no other option to Exegol but Ren because he didn't want her to confront her grandfather and destroy the Sith once and for all without him. Enraged, Rey fought Ren in a [[Mission to Kef Bir|duel]] among the Death Star ruins; Finn and Jannah tried to intervene, but Rey pushed them away. Ren nearly bested Rey until he felt his mother, Organa, reach out to him as she passed away. Rey stabbed the distraught Ren with his lightsaber before sensing her mentor's death. Regretting her actions, she healed Ren with the Force, telling him that she wanted to take the hand of Ben Solo, not Kylo Ren, before leaving in his [[TIE/wi modified interceptor|TIE whisper]] and unknowingly taking his Sith wayfinder.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Finn, Dameron, Chewbacca, the droids, and Jannah and her tribe returned to the Resistance base on Ajan Kloss, where they learned of Organa's passing away and that Poe was named acting General in her stead. During this time, Darth Sidious contacted Allegiant General Pryde, who, as a veteran of the Old Empire, joined forces with the Emperor to collect the Sith fleet and unite it with the First Order while putting them both under his control. Sidious sent one of his Star Destroyers, the ''[[Derriphan]]'', to General Pryde and ordered him to have a planet with Resistance ties destroyed as a warning and example; Pryde had Kijimi destroyed with the Sith Star Destroyer, revealing to the galaxy that the Star Destroyers of the Sith fleet were capable of destroying planets like the old Death Stars and Starkiller Base.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:Resistance_Episode_IX.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Resistance devised a plan of attack to stop the Sith Eternal from deploying the Sith fleet.|alt=]]
General Calrissian traveled to the Resistance base and consoled Dameron, telling the young General that he, Solo, Skywalker, and Organa were able to defeat the Empire years earlier because they had each other. Dameron named Finn General with him, and through D-O, the Resistance gathered enough intel on Exegol to develop a plan of attack while Calrissian and Chewbacca were sent to the [[Core Worlds]] on the ''Falcon'' to call out for allies. Rey took a brief detour to the first Jedi Temple on Ahch-To, but was encouraged by the spirit of Master Skywalker, to confront her fear and destroy the Sith once and for all to win the War for the Resistance. After Skywalker lifted his T-65B X-wing, ''[[Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)|Red Five]]'', from beneath Ahch-To's sea, Rey departed in it; she plotted her course to Exegol and transmitted it to the Resistance, allowing them to navigate through hyperspace in the Unknown Regions towards Exegol.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Still on the Death Star ruins, Kylo Ren experienced a vision of the memory of his father, Solo. Ren was encouraged by the memory to cut ties with the dark side and return to the light. After witnessing Rey's kindness and confession, and receiving the love of his mother and father, an emotional Ren threw his lightsaber far towards the sea, renouncing the dark side and becoming Ben Solo once more.<ref name="Episode IX" />

===Stopping the Final Order===
{{Main|Battle of Exegol}}
====Opening moves====
{{Quote|She will take her revenge, and with a stroke of her saber, the Sith will be reborn! The Jedi are ''dead''!|Darth Sidious, to Rey and the Sith Eternal cultists|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:Battle_of_Exegol.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Resistance arrived on Exegol in order to stop Sith armada.]]
Rey landed on Exegol and ventured to the [[Sith Citadel]], where the [[Throne of the Sith]] was located before a massive amphitheater filled with the Emperor's followers: the cultists of the Sith Eternal. Darth Sidious explained to his granddaughter that all the Sith spirits lived in him, and demanded that she sacrifice him so that the spirits, his among them, could transfer into her, making her a Sith and fully restoring the Sith and told her that the only reason Luke managed to beat him was because he had his father to help but she only has him. Meanwhile, the Resistance arrived, beginning the [[Battle of Exegol]]. The Resistance air team targeted a navigation tower containing a signal that the Sith fleet needed to deploy from Exegol, but Allegiant General Pryde had the source of the navigation signal switched to his ship to guide the Sith fleet out themselves. Finn realized that the communications signal was transferred to the ''Steadfast''; Dameron and the air team covered the ground team as they made landing on the Resurgent Star Destroyer to knock out the tower.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Opening the amphitheater, Darth Sidious showed Rey the unfolding battle above, and told her that the only way she could save her friends was by killing him, taking the Sith throne, reigning over the new Sith Empire, and taking command of the Sith fleet. Rey drew the [[Skywalker lightsaber]] and prepared to strike her grandfather down, but through their bond, she instead slipped it to Solo, who had arrived on Exegol using a [[TIE scout]] from the Death Star. Solo used his family lightsaber to defeat the Knights of Ren while Rey used Organa's lightsaber to defeat Sith guards closing around her. Solo and Rey soon united, raising their lightsabers as they confronted the Sith Lord. Angry, Sidious grabbed the two using the Force and inadvertently drew some of their life energy; realizing that they were a dyad in the Force, a fact that Solo had realized but hid from the Emperor, Sidious proceeded to siphon more of the life energy of the dyad, healing and rejuvenating himself while weakening them. The Dark Lord was fully healed as his followers chanted on in support, and he took his revenge on the Skywalkers by tossing Solo down a nearby ravine.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:FalconLeadsTheCharge-TROS.png|thumb|left|250px|A citizens' fleet was gathered by Lando Calrissian and Chewbacca and reinforced the Resistance at Exegol.]]
The ground team boarded the hull of the ''Steadfast'' with the [[Orbak Tribe]] covering the [[Resistance trooper]]s as they exchanged fire with [[Sith trooper]]s and [[Sith Jetpack Trooper]]s sent from the Star Destroyers of the Sith fleet. Finn and Jannah shut down the beacon by tossing [[thermal detonator]]s down a reinforced hatch, but the setback was only temporary as Pryde's crew reset the navigation signal. Realizing that they had to destroy the entire command ship, Finn and Jannah stayed on board the vessel's hull while Commander Tico, Captain [[Beaumont Kin]], Lieutenant [[Kaydel Ko Connix]], and the rest of the ground team retreated. Meanwhile, the Resistance's air team was being cut down by [[TIE/dg starfighter]]s, with Major [[Temmin Wexley]] being among those shot down. Gravely outnumbered, Dameron apologized for leading the Resistance to what seemed to be their doom, but General Calrissian spoke over the comms and informed them that they outnumbered the Sith Eternal forces: his and Chewbacca's mission to the Core systems was successful, and the combined forces of the [[Citizens' Fleet]] moved in to reinforce the Resistance.<ref name="Episode IX" />

====Final actions====
{{Quote|You are nothing! A scavenger girl who is no match for the power in me. I am all the Sith!''"<br />"'' And I&hellip; am all the Jedi.|Darth Sidious and Rey|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:RiseSkywalkeradaptation-Sidious-vs-Rey.png|thumb|right|250px|Restored to power, Darth Sidious moved to crush all opposition to his rule, including his granddaughter Rey.]]
Darth Sidious sat on the throne, from where he unleashed [[Force lightning]] into Exegol's atmosphere, electrifying the Resistance forces and the fleet and causing their systems to fry and ships to free fall. Rey, however, reached out to the voices of the past Jedi, who strengthened her. Rising, Rey grabbed Leia's lightsaber in defiance of the Dark Lord. Sidious targeted his granddaughter with his Force lightning; Rey defended herself with her master's lightsaber as the Sith Lord mocked her, telling her that her power was no match for the power of the Sith that all lived in him. Rey responded by telling him that the Jedi all lived in her, and she used the Force to grab the Skywalker lightsaber and ignited it, causing the Emperor's lightning to deflect back and disintegrate him. Darth Sidious was killed, preventing the rebirth of the Sith and sending out an explosion that caused the Sith Citadel to crumble while his followers were crushed to death by the Citadel's rubble.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:ComeuppanceAtExegol-TROS.png|thumb|left|250px|The Resistance targeted the planet-destroying ''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers of the Sith Eternal's fleet, the Final Order.]]
With the Emperor's lightning attacks ended thanks to Rey, the Resistance and the galaxy fleet continued their attack, targeting the planet-destroying cannons equipped on each of the Sith Star Destroyers. Meanwhile, Finn and Jannah took control over the [[turbolaser]] cannons on the command ship and fired them at the bridge of the ''Steadfast'', killing Pryde and destroying the tower. The ''Steadfast'' began to explode and descended towards the ground of Exegol with Finn and Jannah still on board, but they were rescued by Calrissian and Chewbacca onboard the ''Falcon''.<ref name="Episode IX" />

The shockwave resulting from Sidious' destruction caused Rey to collapse to the ground, dead, her death being felt by Finn. However, Solo climbed out of the abyss and found Rey's lifeless body. Cradling her corpse, Solo decided to sacrifice himself to revive her by pouring his life force into her. Rey was resurrected and the two kissed, but as a consequence of the effort, Solo vanished in her arms. With the Sith fleet unable to leave Exegol, the Resistance and its allies fled. Finn identified ''Red Five'' in the air and realized that Rey was alive. The ''Falcon'' regrouped with the rest of the fleet as they jumped ship from Exegol, having won the battle.<ref name="Episode IX" />

===The spark is lit===
{{Main|Uprising against the First Order}}
{{Quote|People are rising up all over the galaxy. Poe, we did it.''"<br />"''We did it.|Finn and Poe Dameron|<ref name="Episode IX" />|audio=PeopleAreRising-TheRiseOfSkywalker.flac}}
[[File:Uprising_on_Cloud_City.png|thumb|right|250px|The Resistance ignited the spark to light the fire to burn down the First Order.]]
The Resistance and citizens' fleet returned to the Resistance base on Ajan Kloss, where they celebrated their triumph. During their retreat from Exegol, the Resistance learned that across the galaxy, [[Uprising against the First Order|uprisings]] were erupting against the First Order, which had begun to collapse without Darth Sidious to reinforce and lead them; among these worlds were [[Bespin]],<ref name="Episode IX" /> [[Corellia]], [[Coruscant]],<ref name="TROS JN">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> [[Endor]], [[Jakku]],<ref name="Episode IX" /> [[Lothal]], [[Naboo]], and [[Thyferra]].<ref name="TROS JN" /> In doing so, the Resistance achieved their ultimate goal of being the spark to light the fire to burn the First Order down.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Because regardless of what the Centrists, or the Imperial remnant, or the First Order had believed in-the galaxy did not long for a return to Empire.|Beaumont Kin, in ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
With the people of the galaxy uniting against the First Order,<ref name="Episode IX" /> the First Order crumbled and was defeated.<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> With the war over, the galaxy's population began the process of rebuilding the galaxy, with historian and Resistance operative [[Beaumont Kin]] understanding that, even months after the Battle of Exegol, that process would take a long time and be difficult. At the same time, Kin was overseeing the process of excavating the Sith Eternal temple on Exegol and composed a book, ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

All the while, Rey had journeyed to Luke's former homeworld of Tatooine in the wake of the Battle of Exegol to lay his and Leia Organa's lightsabers to rest, ultimately taking on the surname "Skywalker" during her stay on the planet.<ref name="Episode IX" /> She would become a Jedi Master and work at restoring peace.<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> By [[50 ABY]],<ref name="50 ABY date">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@IGN|wplink=IGN|video=qz6s7kvuwMc|text=Kathleen Kennedy on the Future of Star Wars &verbar; Star Wars Celebration 2023|date=2023-04-07|quote=We're fifteen years out from Rise of Skywalker.}} As the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] occur in [[35 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', the events of the [[Untitled Star Wars film (Sharmeen Obaid-Chinoy)|New Jedi Order film]] must occur in [[50 ABY]].</ref> she was at work restoring the Jedi<ref name="New movies">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-new-star-wars-films|text=SWCE 2023: Three New ''Star Wars'' Movies Announced}}</ref> as the "New Jedi Order,"<ref name="Eras">{{SW|url=eras|text=Star Wars Eras}}</ref> building up the organization<ref name="New movies" /> in a time period dubbed as the "[[New Jedi Order Era]]."<ref name="Eras" /> However, arising powers challenged Skywalker's efforts under the hope that they could wipe out the Jedi<ref name="219 Launchpad">{{InsiderCite|219|Launchpad}}</ref> before they were restored.<ref name="New movies" />
