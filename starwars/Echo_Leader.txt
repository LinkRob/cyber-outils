'''Echo Leader''' was the [[callsign]] assigned to the leader of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Echo Squadron]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a [[Echo Leader (Fostar Haven)|pilot]] using this callsign participated in the [[battle at Fostar Haven]] against [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces.<ref name="Squadrons" />
