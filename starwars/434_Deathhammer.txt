The '''434 Deathhammer''' [[blaster]] was model of [[blaster pistol]] that was popular with [[bounty hunter]]s. It has a slim grip designed for easy concealment. The blaster had a vented shroud around the [[barrel]] to prevent overheating in hot climates. It had a large dial on the side that adjusted its power settings.<ref name="SWVG" />
