The '''Bright Star''' was a crescent-shaped [[crystal]] and the most sacred artifact of the [[Mustafarian]]s. Long before the rise of the [[Galactic Empire]], it was stolen by [[Lady]] [[Corvax]], who wanted to harness its energy to power her [[Aeon Engine]] and restore her [[Death|dead]] [[Dorwin Corvax|husband]] to life.<ref name="Vader VR 2" /> Instead, the Bright Star released powerful energies that turned the green and bountiful Mustafar into a hellish wasteland. Thereafter, Corvax kept the artifact hidden in her [[Corvax Fortress|sanctum]]. At some point during the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] retrieved the only key to the Bright Star, but could not wield it himself, because it could only be used by someone from Corvax's bloodline. After several unsuccessful attempts, Vader managed to capture a descendant of Lady Corvax in the person of a [[Corvax descendant|smuggler]] who operated the [[starship]] ''[[Windfall]]''.<ref name="Vader VR 1">{{VaderImmortal|I}}</ref>

This descendant was [[Force-sensitive]], and they used their powers to obtain the Bright Star after Vader trained them. However, they betrayed Vader and tried to give the crystal to the Mustafarians, so they could use it to heal their world. Vader intercepted them and stole the Bright Star, intent on using it to bring back [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], his deceased wife.<ref name="Vader VR 2" />

Vader hooked the Bright Star up to the Aeon Engine once again, and activated it. The crystal emitted a beam of energy into Mustafar's skies, and the shimmering outline of Padmé began to appear. However, before the Star could finish its work (and probably ruin Mustafar even more, or worse, in the process), the descendant of Corvax returned and dueled Vader with Lord Corvax's [[Lord Corvax's Lightsaber|lightsaber]]. The descendant's relationship with Lady Corvax appeared to affect the Bright Star, stopping the revival process. They then plunged the saber into the Bright Star, destroying it and the Aeon Engine.<ref name="Vader VR 3" />

The Bright Star's destruction started a chain reaction that would begin the change of Mustafar from the wasteland it was to its original, flourishing form.<ref name="Vader VR 3" />
