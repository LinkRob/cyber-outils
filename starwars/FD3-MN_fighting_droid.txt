The '''FD3-MN fighting droid''', also called '''FD3 fighting droid''', was a model<ref name="Kids">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Droid in Star Wars|video=UQg6aX7lqTA|nolive=1}}</ref> of [[gladiator droid]] with a [[Color|white]] cylindrical body. They possessed two [[Limb|arms]] which could be equipped with a circular saw.<ref name="Solo" /> [[FD3-MN]]<ref name="Card Trader">{{Topps|set=2018 Topps Solo: A Star Wars Story|cardname=#30 - FD3-MN|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/162389/cid/11326519/2018-Topps-Solo:-A-Star-Wars-Story-30-FD3-MN}}</ref> was a modified<ref name="DB">{{Databank|wg-22|WG-22}}</ref> FD3 fighting [[droid]] that was made to fight in the droid arena of [[the Lodge]] on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]]. In [[10 BBY]], FD3-MN fought against the [[power droid]] [[WG-22]] and [[Death|destroyed]] it before preparing to face a [[FD4 fighting droid]]. The match was interrupted by the droid [[L3-37]] who tried to convince the FD4 not to fight while the arena manager [[Ralakili]] shouted at her.<ref name="Solo" /> [[FD3-LT]] was also a droid of this model.<ref name="“Droid”">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A FD3-MN fighting droid first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. The droid was not named in the film,<ref name="Solo" /> but was identified as an FD3 fighting droid in the video "Every Droid in Star Wars", which was posted on ''[[StarWarsKids.com]]'' in [[2019]].<ref name="Kids" />
