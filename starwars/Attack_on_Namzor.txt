An '''attack''' was carried out on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] world of [[Namzor]] by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] led by [[Moff]] [[Ahr]] and the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The aim was to secure [[coaxium]] supplies and to destroy the [[Namzor insurgents|insurgents]] that were near the area. [[Governor]] Ahr sent out [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|V-wing starfighters]] to annihilate them resulting in most of the insurgents dying. As Ahr saw the coaxium supplies, he recalled all of his [[Starfighter|fighters]] though [[Lord]] Vader in his [[Vader's starship|starship]] attacked, causing the destruction of the coaxium that the Moff wanted.<ref name="Darth Vader" />
