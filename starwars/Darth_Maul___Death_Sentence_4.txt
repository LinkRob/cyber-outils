'''''Darth Maul — Death Sentence'' 4''' is the fourth issue of four in the ''[[Star Wars: Darth Maul — Death Sentence]]'' comic mini-series written by [[Tom Taylor]].

==Publisher's summary==
''As more Jedi&mdash;Obi-Wan included!&mdash;and clone trooper reinforcements arrive to take Savage Opress and the crooked mine owner into custody, Darth Maul convinces the locals that the Jedi are in league with the mine owner who exploited them.''

''If the Jedi fight the natives, they'll be killing innocents. If they don't, they'll be killed themselves! It's the perfect distraction for Maul to find and release his brother!''
