==Events==
*[[Lanever Villecham]] is elected [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Databank Villecham">{{Databank|chancellor-villecham|Chancellor Villecham}}</ref>
*[[Poe Dameron]] forms [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] and begins [[Hunt for Lor San Tekka|searching]] for [[Lor San Tekka]].<ref name="Black Squadron date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that [[Poe Dameron]] began leading [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] on missions two [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. The mission to [[Ovanis]] was the first mission that [[Color|Black]] [[Squadron]] undertook as stated in [[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]]. Therefore, the mission must have occurred in [[32 ABY]].</ref>
