The '''Church of the Contained Crescent''' was one of the many religious organizations active on [[Jedha]]. At one point, the Church occupied the [[Catacombs of Cadera]], in which they notably added a latticed [[Viewport|window]]. [[Standard year|Years]] before [[Saw Gerrera]]'s settlement on Jedha, the Church abandoned the catacombs.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" />
