'''A Hero Falls''' was a music video released in 2005 to further promote [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].

Initially released on StarWars.com, the video was later included as part of ''[[Star Wars: A Musical Journey]]''.
