'''Dryden Vos's shuttle''' was a [[shuttle]] used by [[Dryden Vos]] during the early [[Imperial Era]] to [[Operations on Janix|rendezvous]] with [[Maul]] [[Devon Izara|and]] [[2B0T|his]] [[Rylee Lawson|surviving]] [[Looti Vario|allies]] on [[Janix]] after they escaped [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the [[Marrok|First Brother]], and the [[Eleventh Brother]].<ref name="The Dark Lord" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Vos shuttle MSL concept art.png|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of Vos's shuttle by Ben Bryant, juxtaposed with the ''First Light'']]
Dyden Vos arrives on Janix in a newly designed Crimson Dawn shuttle rather than his [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]'' seen in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. This smaller shuttle allows Dryden to get to the [[Janix]] [[jungle]] rendezvous point much easier than the larger, more conspicuous ''First Light''. Concept art of the shuttle was done by [[Ben Bryant]].<ref name="MakingShadowLord">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-trivia|text=Inside the Making of ''Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord''}}</ref>
