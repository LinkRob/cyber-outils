A '''Maxillipede shuttle''' was used by [[D-Squad]] to carry out a mission to steal an [[encryption module]] from a [[Aut-O's flagship|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] commanded by the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|super tactical droid]] [[Aut-O]]. After the module had been stolen and the squad had departed,<ref name="Secret Weapons" /> the [[Maxillipede shuttle]] was damaged in a [[comet]] [[Comet storm|storm]] above [[Abafar]], and crashed on the [[planet]]. D-Squad abandoned the destroyed ship, and headed in search of a new [[starship]].<ref name="A Sunny Day in the Void">{{TCW|A Sunny Day in the Void}}</ref>
