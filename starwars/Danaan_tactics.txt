'''Danaan tactics'''<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/zero-hour-trivia-gallery|text="Zero Hour" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> were a type of defensive tactic employed during [[space warfare]]. One type of Danaan tactic was the [[spearhead formation]]. At the [[Battle of Atollon]]<ref name="Zero Hour" /> in [[2 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], Alliance [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] made use of a spearhead formation in his [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Phoenix Cell]]'s [[fleet]] against the Empire's [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]]. Imperial [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] recognized Sato's use of the Danaan tactic, and while calling it bold, he admitted to expecting such a method from his opponent. Thrawn responded to the tactic by ordering his own fleet's center be reinforced and deploying [[starfighter]]s.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

==Behind the scenes==
Danaan tactics appeared in the first part of "[[Zero Hour]],"<ref name="Zero Hour" /> the two-part finale of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Three|third season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', which premiered on [[March 25]], [[2017]].<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|174|Star Wars Rebels Season Three Episode Guide|''Star Wars Rebels'' Season Three Episode Guide}}</ref> The name Danaan tactics was provided in the accompanying episode guide, and is in reference to the [[Wikipedia:Achaeans (Homer)|Danaans]], a name for the ancient Greeks in both the [[Wikipedia:Iliad|''Iliad'']] and the [[Wikipedia:Odyssey|''Odyssey'']].<ref name="Trivia" />
