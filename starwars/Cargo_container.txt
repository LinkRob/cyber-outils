'''Cargo containers''' were storage units designed to hold cargo during transit. They were often labelled with the word "CARGO" in [[Aurebesh]] writing.<ref name="Senate Murders">{{TCW|Senate Murders}}</ref>

During the [[Battle of Christophsis]], cargo containers were used to store vital supplies for [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa's]] relief effort on [[Christophsis]]. These supplies were originally intended to be delivered to the surface by the [[Galactic Republic|Republic's]] prototype [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette|stealth ship]], thus bypassing the [[blockade]] around the [[planet]]. Instead, the stealth [[Starship|ship]] attacked the [[Trench|enemy's]] [[Invincible|command ship]] first, and was able to destroy it after several skirmishes. With the blockade broken, the ship delivered the cargo containers and their contents to the surface.<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref>
