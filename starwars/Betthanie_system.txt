The '''Betthanie system''' was located in the [[Wornal sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing Sectors" /> It was situated in grid square K-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

The [[Star system|system]] was known to<ref name="So You Want to Be a Jedi?" /> the [[Alderaanian]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] as where imitations of luxury furniture could be found.<ref name="So You Want to Be a Jedi?" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?]]'' is a retelling of the events of {{Film|V}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> while Organa was staying in an apartment in [[Cloud City]]&mdash;a [[mining]] colony on<ref name="So You Want to Be a Jedi?" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[gas giant]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Bespin]]&mdash;she mentally noted that its furniture had been made to look expensive, but in reality had likely been mass-produced on some [[moon]] in the Betthanie system.<ref name="So You Want to Be a Jedi?" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Betthanie system was first mentioned in ''[[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?]]'', a [[2015]] young-readers book written by [[Adam Gidwitz]] that retells the events of<ref name="So You Want to Be a Jedi?" /> the [[1980]] film<ref name="Year By Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="So You Want to Be a Jedi?" />
