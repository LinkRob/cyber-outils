{{Quote|You should visit 'Canto Bites'! Amazing food&hellip; but terrible service. Like, REALLY bad service&hellip;|A Duros outside Canto Bites|<ref name="LEGOTSS" />}}
'''Canto Bites''' was a [[restaurant]] in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]]<ref name="LEGOTSS" /> on the [[planet]]<ref name="Canto Bight DB">{{Databank|canto-bight|Canto Bight}}</ref> [[Cantonica]]. Its interior was various shades of brown, and there was a wide service window with a conveyor belt connecting the kitchen and the dining room, which had a [[Protocol Droid Terminal]] on one side and a locked safe on the other. The variety of food and drink it serviced included [[Hoth Ice Tea]], [[Canto Club Sandwich]], [[cake]] and drumsticks. The kitchen was staffed by [[Color|red]] protocol droids, and its waitstaff included a [[Duros]] and a [[Mon Calamari]]. The waitstaff were terrible at their jobs, according to an individual standing outside the door of the establishment.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
{{GameMechanics|LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga}}
At some point during or after [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of {{Film|VII}} in [[34 ABY]]. {{Film|VIII}} takes place immediately after the end of ''The Force Awakens''. As The player only gets access to [[Canto Bight]] during the story mode of ''The Last Jedi'', the mission must take place during or after 34 ABY.</ref> a [[Devaronian]] customer ordered a Hoth Ice Tea, a [[human]] customer ordered a Canto Club Sandwich and another human ordered a cake. They both asked an individual to deliver them their orders, as the waitstaff had failed to do so. In order to access the kitchen, the individual used a protocol droid to interface with the terminal, allowing them to gain control of the chef protocol droids and place the orders on the conveyor belt, which enabled them to serve the food and drink. After the individual served the customers, the safe unlocked to reveal a [[kyber brick]].<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Canto Bites appeared in the non-[[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', which was released in [[2022]]. It featured in the side quest "Canto Bites", where the player character had to serve several customers their orders.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
