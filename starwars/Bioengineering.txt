'''Bioengineering''', also referred to as '''genetic engineering''',<ref name="AotG">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref> '''gene splicing''',<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> or '''biotech engineering''',<ref name="Tuya Origin">{{ArenaNews|url=b09174cb67474bf5bd8615295221536d-en-gb|text=Tuya Has Arrived!}}</ref> was the science of altering and adapting, or outright creating, the [[biology]] of a subject or subjects to produce a desired result. The [[Nu-Cosian]] race was created through bioengineering, using genetic material from the [[Cosian]] species. Some [[geneticist]]s surmised that the group of related species collectively known as the [[Delphidian]]s were bioengineered as [[Slavery|slaves]] by some long-gone [[Enslaver|master]].<ref name="AotG" /> Agricultural [[scientist]]s [[Leo Leonis]] and his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Tepha Leonis|Tepha]] worked on creating genetically-modified crops to help feed the people of [[Chrona]].<ref name="EotG">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref> Bioengineering prehensile tails on [[Sentience|beings]] was considered vogue on [[Coruscant]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. They were said to never last more than a few [[Standard month|months]].<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> Seasonal chewfly swarms used to plague the Fruited Moon of the Hetzal system until they had been genetically modified out of existence.<ref name="LotJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

Following the [[death]] and reincarnation of [[Darth Sidious]], [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cultist|cultists]] turned to gene splicing in their efforts to create a suitable vessel for Sidious' essence. Their experimentation on his [[Clone template|template]] produced various [[Strand-Cast|strandcasts]], including a [[Cloning|cloned]] "[[Dathan|son]]" of the Dark [[Lord]], all of which were deemed failed specimens.<ref name="TROS EE" />
