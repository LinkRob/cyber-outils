[[Doctor]] '''Aira''' was an [[Iakaru]] refugee who lived on the [[planet]] [[Gabredor III]]. He had been conscripted to help the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Governor]] [[Tauntaza]], who had developed a new technology which can drain power from a [[planet]]. Later [[Sabé]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] rescued him and the others.<ref name="Vader 24" />
