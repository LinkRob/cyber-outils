'''Battle droid 513''' was one of many [[Monster droid|monster]] [[battle droid]]s assembled by the [[Mandalorian]]s of [[Death Watch]] for target practice during their stay on the [[planet]] [[Carlac]] in the midst of the [[Clone Wars]]. Death Watch cobbled their [[training droid]]s together from [[droid]] parts of various models, including many originating from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]]. Battle droid 513 specifically was composed of the body of an [[LR-57 combat/retail droid]], the right [[Limb|arm]] of a [[B1-series battle droid]] and a leg from a [[LM-432 crab droid]].

Death Watch would continually reuse 513 alongside numerous other droids for target practice until the arrival of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]] and her [[R2-series astromech droid]], [[R2-D2]], the latter of which would convince 513 and its fellow droids to rebel against their masters, resulting in a [[Battle on Carlac|firefight]] during which 513 was destroyed by [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] while [[Gender|its]] compatriots fled off planet. 

==Biography==
{{Quote|Please reassemble us. Please reassemble us.''"<br />"''We are battle droids no longer. Now, we are slaves.|513 and another [[Death Watch]] [[droid]] to [[R2-D2]]|<ref name="A Friend in Need" />}}
Having been reassembled from the remains of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederate]] [[battle droid]]s, 513 was solely designed by the radical [[Mandalorian]] [[terrorist]] organization, [[Death Watch]] to act as a form of target practice for its members during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="A Friend in Need" />

While stationed at a [[Carlac Death Watch camp|Death Watch camp]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Carlac]], one of Death Watch's [[Airborne Trooper|airborne troopers]] used 513 for target practice. As 513 walked by him, this airborne trooper fired his [[Wrist blaster|wrist rocket]] at the [[droid]], blasting 513 in its side and inflicting critical damage to the droid. In response, 513 would collapse to the ground while crying out in agony as the trooper that shot him laughed at the droid's awareness. Later that night, while the leader of Death Watch, [[Pre Vizsla]], was meeting with the former Separatist [[Lux Bonteri]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], 513 would encounter his eventual savior, an [[R2-series astromech droid]] identified as [[R2-D2]], who had been ordered by a pair of airborne troopers to repair their droids so they could be used for target practice again. Acknowledging the droid's orders, 513 and the other droids began to surround R2, begging to be reassembled by the astromech.<ref name="A Friend in Need" />

[[File:LR-57CombatDroid-AoN.png|thumb|left|250px|Battle droid 513 fights back against its tormentors.]] Proceeding to repair each one of the droids, 513 expressed his gratitude to the astromech, asking if there was anything the droids could do for R2 in return. By this time, Ahsoka and Bonteri had witnessed the Death Watch launch an unprovoked attack on the [[Ming Po|native people]] of Carlac, protesting the Mandalorian's slaughter and as a result were taken hostage and sentenced to be [[Death penalty|executed]]. Witnessing Death Watch return with his companions in captivity, R2 asked for 513's aid in rescuing his friends which 513 questioned how such an attempt could be made. R2 explained his plan and in response 513 rallied the other droids to their cause. By the time R2's rescue attempt was put into action, 513 had gathered the Death Watch's droids to aid their newfound companion, sparking a brief [[Battle on Carlac|battle against Death Watch]] and creating a sufficient diversion to allow R2 and his companions to escape the Death Watch aboard a [[landspeeder]]. As the battle continued, 513 was mounted by Pre Vizsla's [[lieutenant]], [[Bo-Katan Kryze]], who destroyed the droid with several direct shots to its head before pursuing the escaping prisoners.<ref name="A Friend in Need" />

Later on in the Clone Wars, a sales bill in [[Pons Ora]], a [[City|settlement]] located on the Outer Rim planet [[Abafar]], advertised the sale of 513.<ref name="Missing in Action">{{TCW|Missing in Action}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Why? Why?|513, after being shot by a [[Mandalorian]] [[Airborne Trooper]] before going offline|<ref name="A Friend in Need" />}}
[[File:Bo-KatanDestroys513.png|thumb|513's continued use by Death Watch for target practice influenced it to attack its masters, even at the cost of its own destruction.]]
Originally a standard [[LR-57 combat/retail droid]], 513 had lost both its right [[Limb|arm]] and right leg, which his [[Mandalorian]] masters had replaced with that of a [[B1-series battle droid]] and [[LM-432 crab droid]], respectively. 513 also had a deep-toned [[vocabulator]], in which it could speak in [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]], and was capable of shooting [[Color|blue]] [[Laser|ion blasts]] from his arm-mounted double [[laser cannon]]. 513's service under [[Death Watch]] soon gave the droid a developed personality, able to understand the horrors of being destroyed and rebuilt for target practice, presumably indicating that 513 had been destroyed and rebuilt several times while serving Death Watch, causing it to plead to be reassembled after encountering [[R2-D2]]. This unique personality was later showcased further as 513 was quickly convinced by R2 into attacking Death Watch to save [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Lux Bonteri]] after R2 successfully reassembled it, causing a [[Battle on Carlac|diversion]] long enough to allow R2 to escape [[Carlac]] with his companions, even at the cost of 513's own destruction.<ref name="A Friend in Need" />

513 was 3.39 [[meter]]s (11 [[Foot|feet]], 1 inches) tall,<ref name="TCWE" /> with [[Color|red]] [[sensor]]s, and brown, orange, and tan colored plating.<ref name="A Friend in Need" />

==Skills and abilities==
513 was proficient in armed<ref name="A Friend in Need" /> and [[unarmed combat]], as well as military tactics. After R2-D2 repaired it, the droid led the droid uprising against Death Watch.<ref name="TCWE" />
