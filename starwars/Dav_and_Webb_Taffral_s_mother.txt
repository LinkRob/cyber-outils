The '''mother''' of [[Dav Taffral|Dav]] and [[Webb Taffral]] was a [[Gender|female]] who had two children with the [[farmer]] [[Glen Taffral]], who lived on the [[planet]] [[Saleucami]]. Prior to the two meeting, Glen thought he would remain a bachelor and a simple farmer. She [[Death|died]] prior to the [[rebellion on Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> Her son Dav joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], while Webb attended the [[Imperial Academy]] and remained loyal to the [[Galactic Empire]]. During a [[Taffral family|family]] dinner at the [[Taffral house]] some [[time]] after the [[Parent|mother's]] death, Glen entreated the two brothers to stop quarreling over their differing loyalties. He fondly brought up the memory of their mother and mourned her.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
