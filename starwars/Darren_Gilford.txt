'''Darren Gilford''' is a [[Wikipedia:Production designer|production designer]] and [[Wikipedia:Art director|art director]] who has worked on such films as [[Wikipedia:Oblivion (2013 film)|''Oblivion'']] ([[2013]]) and [[Wikipedia:Ocean's Thirteen|''Ocean's Thirteen'']] ([[2007]]). Gilford, along with [[Rick Carter]], served as production designer for the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].
