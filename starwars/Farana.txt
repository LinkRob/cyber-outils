'''Farana''' was a [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] where at one point the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] acquired the [[Corona-class armed frigate|frigate]] ''[[Acushnet]]'' and several [[Flarestar-class attack shuttle|''Flarestar''-class attack shuttles]] from a [[Unidentified collector|collector]] of antiquities. The planet was [[Homeworld|home]] to a type of aggressive [[Unidentified Farana creature|creature]], which the [[human]] [[Mosey Cimmaron]] and their [[Mosey Cimmaron's sister|sister]] [[Hunting|hunted]] at some point by [[9 BBY]].

==Description==
Farana was a terrestrial [[Wild Space]] [[planet]]<ref name="BMF 72" /> located in the [[Farana system]], a part of the [[Farana (region)|Farana]] region. It was situated in the grid square S-3 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Farana was [[Homeworld|home]] to a type of fierce [[Unidentified Farana creature|creature]] similar to the [[gorocco]] of<ref name="Survivor" />  the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="AppendixKoboh">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Koboh system]]</ref> planet [[Koboh]].<ref name="Survivor" /> It also shared its name with the [[fathier]] creature named [[Farana's Folly]].<ref name="RoseTico">''[[The Last Jedi: Rose Tico: Resistance Fighter]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:Weequay_mothership.png|thumb|right|300px|Hondo Ohnaka acquired the ''Acushnet'' (pictured) and other starships on Farana.]]
A [[Unidentified collector|collector]] of antiquities once maintained<ref name="BMF 72" /> huge holdings on Farana.<ref name="Ships" /> There, they kept an arsenal of [[starship]]s that included six [[Flarestar-class attack shuttle|''Flarestar''-class attack shuttles]] and a [[Corona-class armed frigate|''Corona''-class armed frigate]]&mdash;one of a limited run that its manufacturer, [[Haor Chall Engineering Corporation]], had sold off to various interested third parties.<ref name="BMF 72" /> At some point by [[21 BBY]],<ref name="21 BBY">{{TCWcanondates|111}} Since the ''[[Acushnet]]'' is in the possession of [[Hondo Ohnaka]] in "Dooku Captured," Ohnaka must have acquired the [[Corona-class armed frigate|frigate]] at some point by that date, as well.</ref> the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] accidentally discovered the [[collector]]'s starships on Farana.<ref name="BMF 72" /> In order to steal the ''Corona''-class [[frigate]] and the ''Flarestar''-class attack [[shuttle]]s, Ohnaka carried out a subtle raid on the collector's holdings. Subsequently, the pirate's crew wrought chaos around the planet, including the blowing up of some of the collector's other vessels on display.<ref name="Ships" />

Finally, the collector agreed to let Ohnaka just leave Farana with both the frigate&mdash;which Ohnaka eventually named the ''[[Acushnet]]''&mdash;and the shuttles in his possession and promised to not seek retribution against the Weequay. Ohnaka then accepted an additional bribe from the former owner of the vessels before departing the planet.<ref name="BMF 72" /> At one point by [[9 BBY]],<ref name="9 BBY">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> the [[human]] [[Mosey Cimmaron]] and their [[Mosey Cimmaron's sister|sister]] [[Hunting|hunted]] a native creature of Farana, an encounter that Cimmaron later related to the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] on Koboh.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
Farana was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 72|seventy-second]] issue of ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 72" /> published around [[May 18]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMF72date">{{BMFdates|72}}</ref> [[Farana/Legends|Farana]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
