The '''Adega system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Auril sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] which contained the [[planet]] [[Ossus]],<ref name="NOP" /> and was situated in grid square S-6 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Location note" /> The [[Adegan crystal]] family was found there. One kind was the [[mephite crystal]], which was a rare, lustrous [[kyber crystal]] found in a handful of places in and around the system.<ref name="GaG" />
{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Adega system first [[canon]]ically appeared in a [[Force vision]] in the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']],<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> and was first identified in the [[Fantasy Flight Games]] roleplaying manual ''[[Nexus of Power]]''.<ref name="NOP" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the system first appeared in the [[Tales of the Jedi – The Freedon Nadd Uprising 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi – The Freedon Nadd Uprising]]'',<ref name="TOTJ">[[Tales of the Jedi – The Freedon Nadd Uprising 1|''Tales of the Jedi – The Freedon Nadd Uprising'' 1]]</ref> written by [[Tom Veitch]], illustrated by [[Tony Akins]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[August 2]], [[1994]].<ref name="Publication">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54587/star_wars_tales_of_the_jedi_-_the_freedon_nadd_uprising_1994_1|text=Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi - The Freedon Nadd Uprising #1 (1994)}}</ref> Although that comic issue was the system's first actual appearance, it was first mentioned in ''[[Dark Empire endnotes|For a Thousand Generations]]'', an endnote written by Tom Veitch and printed in the back of [[Dark Empire 4|''Dark Empire'' 4]].<ref name="endnotes">[[Dark Empire endnotes|''Dark Empire'' endnotes]]</ref>
