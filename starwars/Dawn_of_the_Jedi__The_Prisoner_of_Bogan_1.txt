'''''Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan'' 1''' is the first issue in the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan|''Prisoner of Bogan'']] story arc of the ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series and the sixth issue of the series in total. It is set in the earliest days of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]], in a [[Pre-Republic era|time before]] the polarization of the [[Light side of the Force/Legends|light]] and [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark sides]] of [[The Force/Legends|the Force]]. This issue was released on [[November 28]] [[2012]].

An excerpt of this issue is included in the novel ''[[Dawn of the Jedi: Into the Void]]''.

==Publisher's summary==
''Banished to the dark moon Bogan, the Force Hound Xesh has been living in contemplation; the memory of who he was before his ship crashed on Tython still escapes him. A rival from his past is on his trail, and the Je'daii are doing all they can to discover his origin. And now, Xesh is about to meet someone who will change his life forever, a former great Je'daii . . . the prisoner of Bogan!''

==Plot summary==
''Skal’nas is enraged that Tul’kar has found the force sensitive world of Tython first and is taking it out on his force hound, Trill. Trill convinces Skal’nas that she can still find the planet by tracking Xesh since Xesh and her share a “brood link”. Trill is given a scout ship and let out to hunt for Xesh and Tython itself.

On Bogan Xesh flashes back to his adolescence growing up in a brood of force hounds under the Rakata. He was taken from the brood and subjected to torture which drove fear and the force out of him. This method powered the Rakata ships. Tul’kar recognized his potential and took Xesh for himself to train as a force hound. Xesh recounts discovering Tython, his ship crashing, killing his master, and showing weakness when in the presence of Shae Koda.

Daegen Lok drops in on Xesh by surprise. Xesh isn’t happy about this but entertains a conversation when Daegen reveals he has had a vision of Xesh. Daegen removes the tracking cufflinks from Xesh’s wrist so he may no longer be tracked by the Jed’aii.

Meanwhile Shae Koda, Sek’nos Rath, and Tasha Ryo discuss their displeasure with Master Tave at Stav Kesh on Tython. They do not understand why the masters insisted on sending Xesh to Bogan and seemed to dismiss the journeymen’s opinions on the matter. Master Tave assures them that the masters have considered their shared vision at great length. 

Masters Ketu and Rajivari meet at Akar Kesh. Ketu seeks Rajivari’s advice which suggests that Ketu should heed Daegen Lok and the Journeymen’s visions. All visions include a “blade of fire” which gives them some validity.

Daegen Lok shows Xesh a downed ship from the Despot War. He tells Xesh about his time after the war, when he decides to descend in to the Chasm. Despite his partner Hawk Ryo’s attempts to block, Daegen descends too deep into the Chasm and has a terrible vision of Xesh with his force saber drawn. Daegen needs Xesh to create another force saber so he can use it to prove his visions validity. They must gather supplies for the job on Krev Coeur and Xesh uses the force to power up the downed ship.''
