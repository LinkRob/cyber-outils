The '''Battle Scarred''' was a set of [[armor]] worn by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal Warrior|standard costume]]. This costume consisted of Tal's regular [[Mandalorian armor|armor]] in a damaged state, with much of it dirtied, chipped, or fully replaced by [[Color|tan]] [[Clothing|cloth]] wrappings.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Battle Scarred was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Aran Tal, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Scarred MT-97]].<ref name="Hunters" />
