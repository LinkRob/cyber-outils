The '''Battle on Malenko''' was battle during the [[Clone Wars]] fought between the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]].<ref name="GotM" />

After the [[Hassarian]]s joined Count [[Dooku]]'s [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], General [[Kazameer]] and his droid forces established a secretbase on [[Malenko]]. Kazmeer established his headquarters in the [[Andon]]'s [[Andon Citadel|Citadel]] despite the stories about a curse on the land and the Andon's abilities as necromancers. When the Republic's Grand Army under Jedi Generals Windu and Billaba attacked. As Windu and Billaba moved towards hims, Kazameer ordered his tactical droid to send in more droids. Kazameer then took a speeder back to the citadel as the tactical droid was destroyed with the rest of his forces. Unfortunately, Kazameer and his [[LEP-series service droid|LEP droid]] [[LEP-999]] came under attack by the [[Ghost (folklore)|ghosts]] of the droids he sacrificed.<ref name="GotM" />
