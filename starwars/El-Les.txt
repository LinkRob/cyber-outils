'''El-Les''' was an [[Arcona]] [[Gender|male]] [[bounty hunter]] whom the [[Galactic Republic]] employed as a drill instructor for the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] [[clone trooper]]s<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> after [[Jango Fett]] [[Death|died]].<ref name="Bric card">{{Topps|set=2021 Topps Star Wars Bounty Hunters|cardname=Bric|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/263568/cid/18708464}}</ref> During his [[time]] in [[Tipoca City]], [[capital]] of the [[planet]] [[Kamino]], El-Les worked with [[Bric]]&mdash;a fellow [[mercenary]]&mdash;to train [[Squad|groups]] of [[clone cadet]]s such as [[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]] and [[Domino Squad]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Biography==
El-Les was a [[bounty hunter]] during the [[Clone Wars]], who was contracted along with the [[Siniteen]] bounty hunter [[Bric]] by the [[Galactic Republic]] to train [[clone cadet]]s on the aquatic [[Planet|world]] of [[Kamino]]. Bric and El-Les often worked together, yet their attitudes on the cadets often clashed.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Personality and traits==
El-Les was an [[Arcona]] [[Gender|male]] bounty hunter<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> and [[mercenary]]. He was 1.83 [[meter]]s or 6 [[Foot|feet]] tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and had [[Color|purple]] [[eye]]s<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> While Bric was a tough taskmaster with little patience for trainees who did not meet his standards, El-Les took a gentler approach to his teaching methods than his partner and his kindness was in contrast to Bric's bullying.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> He tried to help the cadets of [[Domino Squad]] while they struggled to master their military training in [[Tipoca City]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Skills and abilities==
As El-Les was a bounty hunter training clone cadets on Kamino, he was known for his exceptional military tactics. He was also skilled in leadership, tracking, and reconnaissance.<ref name="Join the Battle" />
