'''Dunnels''' were a [[species]] of furry quadrupedal steeds with armored horns, backs, and tails used by [[nerf herder]]s<ref name="The Rustler Roundup">{{YJA|The Rustler Roundup}}</ref> and by [[Lys Solay|Lys]] and Myra.<ref name="A New Discovery" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DunnelPredesign-ChrisBlackstock.jpg|250px|thumb|right|Dunnel predesign by Chris Blackstock]]
Dunnels first appeared in "[[The Rustler Roundup]]," the first part of the [[The Rustler Roundup / A New Discovery|second episode]],<ref name="Rustler Roundup" /> as well as multiple other stories within the first batch of episodes, including "[[A New Discovery]],"<ref name="A New Discovery">{{YJA|A New Discovery}}</ref> "[[The Lost Treasure of Tenoo]],"<ref name="The Lost Treasure of Tenoo">{{YJA|The Lost Treasure of Tenoo}}</ref> and "[[The Wild Aklyrr]]," of the [[Disney+]] and [[Disney Junior]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''{{'}} [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|second season]].<ref name="The Wild Aklyrr">{{YJA|The Wild Aklyrr}}</ref> The batch of episodes first aired on [[August 14]], [[2024]].<ref name="S2 Announcement">{{SW|url=news/young-jedi-adventures-season-2-and-nubs-shorts|text=''Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures'' Returns for Season 2|date=2025-03-19}}</ref> The species was designed by [[Wikipedia:Artist|artist]] [[Chris Blackstock]].<ref name="Blackstock">{{WebCite|url=https://blackstockart.com/young-jedi-adventures|text=Young Jedi Adventures|work=[[Chris Blackstock]]'s [https://blackstockart.com official website]|archivedate=20250531045658}}</ref>
