A '''datacore''' was a device capable of storing information.

[[Serennian]] [[Romar Adell]] owned a small [[Model Two datacore]] which he used to store pieces of Serennian culture including [[music]], art and history; as the core was malfunctioning, [[genetically defective clone]] [[Tech]] assisted him with its repair when he was stranded in [[Romar Adell's domicile|his house]] and recovered all of the data from it.<ref name="Ruins">{{TBB|Ruins of War}}</ref> The [[Tarkin Initiative]] deep-storage facility [[Hivebase-1]] included a massive datacore built as a vertical pit accessible only by a floating [[Repulsorlift|repulsor-platform]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 18">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 18|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 18]]</ref> The [[scavenger]] [[Miggs McKane]] sliced into the datacore of a crashed [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] and discovered a complete Imperial order of battle, which he then sold in order to retire.<ref name="RSG">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''</ref>
