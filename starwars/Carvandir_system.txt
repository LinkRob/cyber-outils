The '''Carvandir system''' was located in the [[Carvandir sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]] in grid square J-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Carvandir [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Carvandir system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention in the [[2012]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'', authored by Fry and [[Paul R. Urquhart]].<ref name="EGtW">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
