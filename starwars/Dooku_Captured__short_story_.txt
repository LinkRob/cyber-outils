"'''Dooku Captured'''" is a short story that was published in the anthology ''[[The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark]]'', adapting the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episodes "[[Dooku Captured]]" and "[[The Gungan General]]," where he was kidnaped by [[Hondo Ohnaka|Hondo]]. It was written by [[Lou Anders]], and published on [[August 25]], [[2020]] by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]. The story was narrated by [[Corey Burton]] in the [[The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark (audiobook)|audiobook]]. It canonizes the events of the Legends webcomic ''[[The Clone Wars: Bait]]''.
