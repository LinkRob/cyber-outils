The '''Expansion Region''' was a region of [[the galaxy]] located between the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] and the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> In addition to the [[Ghost Nebula sector]], which contained the [[Umbara system]],<ref name="Darkness on Umbara">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> the Expansion Region included the [[Cvetaen system|Cvetaen]] and [[Sumitra system|Sumitra]] [[star system]]s, as well as the [[planet]]s [[Aquaris]], [[Kerkoidia]], [[Thustra]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> and [[Sibensko]].<ref name="Bloodline" />

==History==
===High Republic Era===
[[Togruta space]] was an area of the galaxy in the Expansion Region<ref name="Leia">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Shili]].</ref> controlled by the [[Togruta]] [[species]] and their [[Togruta government|local government]],<ref name="TRS" /> with its borders including the [[Shili system]]<ref name="Leia" /> and its world of [[Shili]], the Togruta homeworld.<ref name="TRS" /> By [[382 BBY]]<ref name="382Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic (2022) 1|''The High Republic'' (2022) 1]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]],<ref name="THR 1">[[The High Republic (2022) 1|''The High Republic'' (2022) 1]]</ref> the Togrutas were in [[Republic-Togruta conflict|conflict]] with<ref name="TRS" /> the expanding<ref name="THR 1" /> [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="TRS" /> with a frontline developing that the [[Jedi Order]] was called to fight within.<ref name="THR 1" /> While the conflict would eventually come to its end, tensions would remain between the Togrutas and the Republic for over a [[Standard century|century]], although the Republic and Togruta's joint response to the [[Nihil]]<ref name="TRS" /> in [[231 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> opened the possibility of an alliance between both parties.<ref name="TRS" />

In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Acolyte Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> [[senator]]s within the [[Galactic Senate|congress]] of the Galactic Republic that hailed from the Expansion Region intended to give their support to the external review of the [[Jedi Order]] that Worus Rayencourt of the [[Thusa sector]] had proposed. The [[Abednedo]] Senator [[Isedwa Chuwant]] later discussed the significance of this decision with [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]], noting the growing support that the review was accumulating inside the Senate.<ref name="TC">{{Acolyte|Teach / Corrupt}}</ref>

===Clone Wars===
During the [[Clone Wars]], the worlds of the Expansion Region served as battlegrounds due to the region's location between the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] and the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="SW:VE" /> The [[Mud Jumpers]] of the [[224th Imperial Armored Division|224th Division]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] fought [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] forces on the Expansion Region [[Planet|world]] of [[Mimban]].<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref> They also armed and trained the indigenous [[Mimbanese Liberation Army]], promising them freedom when the [[Separatist Droid Army]] was [[Battle of Mimban|repelled]].<ref name="Solo Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>

[[File:UmbaranCrawlerTank-BattleOfUmbara.png|thumb|left|300px|Umbara saw heavy fighting during the Clone Wars]]
In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Umbara Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the [[Battle of Mon Cala]] and the [[Plan to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine|skirmish in Theed]] to [[20 BBY]]. As the episode this event takes place within takes place between the events of the episodes the other events are attached to according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, this event must also take place 20 BBY.</ref> the Expansion Region world of [[Umbara]] was the site of a [[Battle of Umbara|prolonged battle]] between the Grand Army of the Republic and the native [[Umbaran]]s, who had aligned themselves with the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Numerous [[clone trooper]]s and Umbaran [[soldier]]s were [[Death|killed]] during the fighting. The [[Besalisk]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Pong Krell]] commanded the [[501st Legion]] during the battle and proved unpopular with his men.<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> After Krell's [[treason]] was revealed, the [[trooper]] [[Dogma]] executed the fallen [[Jedi]] for betraying the [[Galactic Republic]]. In the aftermath of the battle, the Republic occupied Umbara.<ref name="Carnage of Krell">{{TCW|Carnage of Krell}}</ref>

===Imperial Era===
<div style="float: right">[[File:Kafrene-City.jpg|thumb|250px|The Ring of Kafrene]]</div>
During the [[Imperial Era]], much of the Expansion Region fell under the jurisdiction of the [[Galactic Empire]], a [[New Order|new regime]] that [[Proclamation of the New Order|replaced]] the Republic. Following the [[Mission to Murkhana|theft]] of [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s personal [[starship]] ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', an [[Unidentified Imperial communications officer (Carrion Spike)|Imperial comms officer]] calculated that the Expansion Region planets of [[Aquaris]] and [[Thustra]] were the closest sites to the location of the stolen ship while it was at the location [[sector]]-designated as [[LCC-4-4-7]]. The same [[officer]] also estimated that LCC-4-4-7 was located equidistant to the [[Sumitra system|Sumitra]] and [[Cvetaen system]]s. [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]] also theorized that the [[Cartao Imperial facility|Imperial facility]] on the Expansion Region world of [[Cartao]] would be the next target of [[Berch Teller]]'s [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

In [[10 BBY]], [[Diles Anevi]] served as the [[governor]] of the Expansion Region while [[Moff]] [[Bin Essada]] governed the [[Circarpous sector]] from the administrative world of [[Gyndine]]. Essada ordered Imperial forces to strip Mimban of all its resources. That same year, the Mimbanese Liberation Army resisted Imperial forces, who wanted to exploit Mimban for its [[energy mining]] resources.<ref name="Solo Official Guide" /> [[Han Solo]] served as a [[Imperial swamp trooper]] of the [[Imperial Army]]'s 224th Imperial Armored Division before deserting with the liberated [[Wookiee]] [[Slavery|slave]] [[Chewbacca]] to join [[Tobias Beckett]]'s [[Beckett's gang|gang]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Rogue One Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[intelligence officer]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] [[Mission to the Ring of Kafrene|visited]] the Expansion Region trading post known as the [[Ring of Kafrene]]<ref name="RO Worlds">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-rogue-one|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Rogue One''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Rogue One?}}</ref> to investigate why Imperial shipments of [[kyber crystal]]s were being shipped across [[the galaxy]]. There, Andor encountered the rebel [[Information broker|informant]] [[Tivik]], who informed him that an Imperial [[pilot]] [[Bodhi Rook]] had defected with information about a [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|planet-killing]] [[superweapon]]. Andor was forced to kill Tivik after the latter was wounded during a skirmish with [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

Following the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]] and the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Imperial officer]] [[Ciena Ree]] counseled the disillusioned [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] [[Thane Kyrell]] to find work somewhere in the Expansion Region and [[Outer Rim Territories]] after failing to convince him to return to the Empire. Thane later joined the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>

===New Republic===
In [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Bloodline Date">''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' establishes that events of the novel ''Bloodline'' occur 28 [[Standard year|years]] after those of the film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to the year [[0 ABY]]; therefore, ''Bloodline'' occurs in [[28 ABY]].</ref> the [[water]]y planet [[Sibensko]] was reputed throughout the [[New Republic]] as a crime haven. It had an [[Sibensko complex|underwater city]] that served as the headquarters of the [[Amaxine warriors (New Republic Era)|Amaxine warriors]], a militia group funded by the [[First Order]], a remnant of the Old Empire. The [[city]] was later destroyed during a [[Mission to Sibensko|skirmish]] which wiped out most of the Amaxine warriors.<ref name="Bloodline" />

===First Order&ndash;Resistance War===
In [[35 ABY]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], [[Finn]], [[Poe Dameron]], [[Chewbacca]], [[C-3PO]], and [[BB-8]] traveled to the [[Forbidden Valley]] on the planet [[Pasaana]] to seek out a [[Sith wayfinder]] that could lead them to the planet [[Exegol]]. There, they encountered Rebellion hero [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], who helped to locate the [[Sith]] assassin [[Ochi]]'s [[Blade of Ochi of Bestoon|dagger]], which contained information about the Wayfinder, in the [[Shifting Mires]]. However, the Resistance team were pursued by the [[Knights of Ren]] and [[First Order]] forces led by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], who captured Chewbacca, Ochi's dagger, and the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

==Astrography==
The Expansion Region was subdivided into multiple sectors. Sectors located in the Expansion Region included the [[Circarpous sector]],<ref name="Solo Official Guide" /> the [[Ghost Nebula sector]],<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> [[LCC-4-4-7]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> the [[Narvath sector]],<ref name="Nexus of Power" /> the [[Ombakond sector]],<ref name="ROS VOD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and the [[Thand sector]].<ref name="RO Worlds" />
