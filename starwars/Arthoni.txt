'''Arthoni''' was a fictional [[princess]]-warrior who featured in the play ''[[Amon Daak]]'', written by [[Moobian Tax]]. While impersonating [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Moff]] [[Tan Hubi]], the [[Clawdite]] [[Tunga Arpagion]] performed the play with [[C-3PO]], taking the role of Arthoni.<ref name="Star Wars 48" />
