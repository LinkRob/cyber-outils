'''Custodian of the Nexus''' was a leadership title held by [[Orkle]] on the [[Nexus]] by the [[time]] of the [[mission to Quarmendy]]<ref name="Battle Tales 2 " /> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Date">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 2|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 2]] places the mission to [[Quarmendy]] during the [[Clone Wars]] and prior to the [[siege of Hisseen]], which [[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 1|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 1]] establishes is prior to the [[Battle of Christophsis]]. Since the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the Clone Wars and the Battle of [[Christophsis]] are dated to [[22 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', the mission to Quarmendy must take place in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> Orkle managed to evacuate her people from the Nexus after the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] took over the trading outpost, but she was taken [[prisoner]]. She was eventually rescued by the [[Wolfpack]].<ref name="Battle Tales 2" />
