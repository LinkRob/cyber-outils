'''Cellblock 25''' was a [[Prison|detention area]] located on [[level six]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Imperial Base on Daro|military base]] on the [[planet]] [[Daro]]. After he attempted to [[Desertion|desert]], the [[clone commando]] [[Captain]] [[Gregor]] was imprisoned in the facility before being [[Extraction on Daro|rescued]] by [[Clone Force 99]]. The cellblock was protected by the newly-introduced [[TK stormtrooper|TK troopers]].<ref name="War-Mantle" />
