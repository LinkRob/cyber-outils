'''Blackberry Ice''' was a [[Diet|plant-based]] [[Water|frozen]] [[Foodstuff|dessert]] flavored like a [[fruit]].<ref name="Cruiser" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the dessert was one of several dessert offerings that could be found in the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]] on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''. The sweet treat was safe for [[human]]s to consume.<ref name="Cruiser" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blackberry Ice was a dessert available in the [[In-universe|real world]] at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> The dessert was last served at the hotel during the [[September 28]][[September 29|&ndash;]][[September 30|30]] voyage of [[2023]], the final voyage before the closure of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser.<ref name="Official website">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref> The vegan treat was served during evening dessert on the trip.<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2022/03/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-sweets-treats|text=REVIEW: Sweets & Treats to End the 2nd Night on the Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Brings Little Surprises But Still Worth a Stop|author=Spencer Lloyd|date=2022-03-05|work=[https://www.wdwnt.com WDW News Today]|archivedate=20220814125212}}</ref>
