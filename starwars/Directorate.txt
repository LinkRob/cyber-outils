The '''Directorate''' was an organization active during the [[High Republic Era]]. [[Lord]] [[Isamer]] was one of its leaders. Working for the [[Hutt]]s, the Directorate caused a [[Eiram–E'ronoh crisis|crisis]] between the [[planet]]s [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]] after kidnapping [[Noble|royals]] from both worlds. Secretly, the Hutts' purpose in hiring the Directorate was to get the organization destroyed by the [[Jedi]] and [[Galactic Republic]], which would allow them to move their operations into the Directorate's former area of influence at their leisure.<ref name="Into the Dark" />
