'''Corellian wine''' was a type of [[alcohol]]ic beverage from [[Corellia]].<ref name="Star Wars 6" /> It had a dark red [[color]] and came in different vintages.<ref name="Maul" /> It was favored by [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]]. When hiding on the [[planet]] of [[Monsua Nebula]] from [[Imperial Navy|Imperial]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], Solo offered a drink to [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]; however, the [[princess]] refused by pouring the wine in his face.<ref name="Star Wars 6" /> [[Xev Xrexus]], leader of the [[Xrexus Cartel]], was also known to enjoy it.<ref name="Maul" />
