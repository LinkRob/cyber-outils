'''Faddera Rabar'''<ref name="Card Trader" /> was a [[Dyplotid]] [[pirate]] who was a member of the [[Cloud-Riders]], a pirate gang led by [[Enfys Nest]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. As a member, Rabar participated in a [[Showdown on Savareen|skirmish]] on the [[planet]] [[Savareen]].

==Biography==
A member of [[Enfys Nest]]'s [[Cloud-Riders]], Rabar was native to a world which had been devastated by one of the [[Five Crime Syndicates]].<ref name="Solo" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date Note">The timeline provided in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' places the events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' nine [[Standard year|years]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of that film in [[19 BBY]], we can deduce that the events of the film took place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> a group of Cloud-Riders attempted to [[Heist on Vandor|steal]] a shipment of [[coaxium]] [[fuel]] from a [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport]] on the<ref name="Solo" /> [[Temperature|cold]] [[mountain]]ous [[planet]]<ref name="Vandor">{{Databank|vandor|Vandor}}</ref> of [[Vandor]]. However, the efforts of the gang did not prevail and the shipment was instead stolen by a [[Beckett's gang|crew]] of [[Thief|thieves]] led by [[Tobias Beckett]], and associated with [[Crimson Dawn]], one of the Five Crime Syndicates. Nest commanded her Cloud-Riders to follow the crew. The Riders eventually tracked the crew to the<ref name="Solo" /> planet<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|savareen|Savareen}}</ref> [[Savareen]] where Rabar stood alongside Nest and eleven others as they confronted the crew, before bringing them inside a [[cantina]] for a drink. Nest and Beckett's crew eventually teamed up, and Rabar would participate in a [[Showdown on Savareen|skirmish]] against Crimson Dawn's [[Hylobon Enforcers]], shooting at them with their blaster from a ledge. The Cloud-Riders would achieve victory and were given the coaxium by Beckett's crew, before the two groups parted ways.<ref name="Solo" />

==Personality and traits==
Rabar had [[Color|brown]] skin and black [[eye]]s.<ref name="Solo" />

==Equipment==
Rabar wore a [[Color|white]] [[helmet]], a brown [[Clothing|tunic]], grey pants and armguards, and brown [[boot]]s. They also wielded a [[blaster rifle]].<ref name="Solo" />
