'''''A New Hope''''' is a young readers adaptation of the [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|film of the same name]], written and illustrated by [[Ken Steacy]]. The book was released by [[Fun Works Play To Learn|Fun Works]] on [[January 12]], [[1997]] and features 24 fully-colored pages.

==Publisher's summary==
''Strikingly colorful foil brings Luke Skywalker and the characters from the ''Star Wars'' epic to life on the pages of this amazing storybook.''<ref name="ANH" />

==Plot summary==
After stealing the [[Death Star plans/Legends|Death Star plans]], [[Princess/Legends|Princess]] [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia]] and her [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] friends are fleeing from [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]], a servant of the evil [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]]. Just before being captured by Vader, Leia gives the plans to the [[Droid/Legends|droid]] [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]], who along with his friend [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]], manages to escape to [[Tatooine/Legends|the planet below]].<ref name="ANH" />

On the [[Planet/Legends|planet]], the droids are quickly captured by [[Jawa/Legends|Jawas]] who sell them to [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]]. While cleaning R2-D2, Luke stumbles across the first part of a message from Princess Leia, but R2-D2 would only play the rest of the message for someone named [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. That night, R2-D2 sets across the [[Dune Sea/Legends|Dune Sea]] to deliver the message himself.<ref name="ANH" />

The next morning Luke and C-3PO take off after R2-D2 in Luke's [[X-34 landspeeder/Legends|landspeeder]]. They catch up to R2-D2, but are suddenly attacked by [[Tusken Raider/Legends|Sand People]]. Fortunately, Ben Kenobi, also known as Obi-Wan, appears and frightens the Sand People away.<ref name="ANH" />

Back at [[Ben Kenobi's home/Legends|Ben's dwelling]], R2-D2 plays Leia's desperate message. Ben explains to Luke that he and Luke's father had once been [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knights]], warriors who had guarded [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] for centuries. Their strength came from [[The Force/Legends|the Force]], an energy field that unites all living beings in the galaxy. He gives Luke [[Anakin Skywalker's second lightsaber/Legends|his father's lightsaber]] and encourages him to join the fight against the Empire.<ref name="ANH" />

At a [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina/Legends|cantina]] in the [[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]] spaceport, Luke and Ben arrange a trip to Leia's home planet, [[Alderaan/Legends|Alderaan]], aboard the [[Millennium Falcon/Legends|''Millennium Falcon'']]. The [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]] was a rogue named [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]], and the copilot a large, hairy [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] named [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]]. When Luke accidentally annoys one of the strange creatures at the bar, the alien wants to fight. In less than a heartbeat, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber/Legends|Ben's lightsaber]] flashes in the darkness, putting an end to the confrontation.<ref name="ANH" />

Meanwhile, the princess had been taken to the Death Star, where she refused to tell Darth Vader and [[Governor/Legends|Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin/Legends|Tarkin]] the location of the [[Great Temple/Legends|Rebel base]]. Deciding that there is only one way to get her to talk, Tarkin commands the Death Star to fire upon Alderaan. Leia begs for mercy, but is too late, and Alderaan is [[Destruction of Alderaan/Legends|destroyed]].<ref name="ANH" />

When the ''Millennium Falcon'' pops out of [[Hyperspace/Legends|hyperspace]], Luke and his friends discover that Alderaan is gone. However, before they can change course, they are captured by a [[Tractor beam/Legends|tractor beam]] and dragged onto the Death Star. Luke, Han, and Chewbacca manage to outwit the Imperial [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] and [[Rescue of Princess Leia/Legends|rescue]] Leia after a fierce [[Blaster/Legends|blaster]] battle. Meanwhile, Ben frees the ''Millennium Falcon'' from the tractor beam and goes to rejoin his friends, but he is stopped by Darth Vader.<ref name="ANH" />

The old foes attack each other with grim determination, and Ben [[Death/Legends|sacrifices]] himself to ensure the safe getaway of his friends. Quickly, Luke and his friends make their way to the secret Rebel base. There Luke and Leia prepare for the final [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|battle]], knowing that if they fail, darkness would descend over the entire galaxy.<ref name="ANH" />

Every [[Rebel pilot/Legends|Rebel pilot]] except for Han and Chewbacca takes part in the attack on the Death Star. When the Death Star is only seconds away from firing on the Rebel base, Luke lines up his shot and he hears Ben's voice urging him to use the Force. At that moment, Darth Vader moves [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced/Legends|his fighter]] into position directly behind Luke. At the last possible second, Han returns in the ''Millennium Falcon'' and blasts Darth Vader off course. Luke fires at the Death Star, blowing it to pieces.<ref name="ANH" />

A great ceremony is held back at the Rebel base. The newly polished C-3PO and R2-D2 gleam as brightly as the [[Medal of Bravery/Legends|medals]] Leia awards to Luke and Han. Luke and his friends had saved the Rebel Alliance and won a major battle in the fight to free the galaxy from the evil Empire.<ref name="ANH">'''''A New Hope'''''</ref>

==Continuity==
''A New Hope'' is a standalone, direct adaptation of the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]]. Released twenty years after the film, the book is targeted towards young readers, and it uses simplistic language and pictures to suitably convey the story.
