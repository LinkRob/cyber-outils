'''Bravara''' was a [[human]] [[captain]] who served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Lego FN" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bravara first appeared in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Fortnite Odyssey]]''<ref name="Lego FN" /> and added as part of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' collaboration in update v29.40 on [[May 3]], [[2024]].<ref name="Rebel Adventure">{{FortniteWeb|url=news/lego-fortnite-star-wars-begins-in-v29-40|text=LEGO® Fortnite &#124; Star Wars Begins in V29.40!|date=2024-05-01}}</ref>

===Continuity===
In ''LEGO Fortnite'', Bravara is shown to be wearing the rank of a [[general]]. However, in-game dialogue and its databank entry refer to Bravara as a captain.<ref name="Lego FN" />
