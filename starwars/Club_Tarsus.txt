'''Club Tarsus''' was a [[nightclub]] operated by [[Wen Delphis]] and her [[Sixth Kin]] criminal gang in the [[South Stretch Worker's District]] of the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. One of the district's seedier venues, the club headlined celebrated musicians, and was also a place where Sixth Kin enforcers and associates conducted business. The scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] stole a forged [[Identichip|identicard]] from Delphis's office in Club Tarsus.<ref name="Outlaws" />
