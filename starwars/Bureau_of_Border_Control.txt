The '''Bureau of Border Control''' was a [[law enforcement agency]] on the [[planet]] [[Tsaokallus]]. However, while the official nature of the Bureau had been a planetary security force, this was a well-known cover for its involvement with espionage. Before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Spy|spies]] for the Bureau of Border Control monitored various other planets that within the [[sector]], regardless of their connections to the [[Galactic Republic]]. The resultant civil war that erupted between the Republic and the nascent [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] allowed the agency to confirm the effectiveness of their techniques on a larger scale. After the end of the struggle and the result [[Proclamation of the New Order|proclamation]] of the [[New Order]], the [[Heptooinian]] [[Haki Zeophrine]] &mdash; who had been recruited into the [[Imperial Intelligence|intelligence branch]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] &mdash; reflected that she and other members of the Bureau of Border Control had been spies.<ref name="Mask" />
