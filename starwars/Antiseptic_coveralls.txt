'''Antiseptic coveralls''' were a type of protective clothing that could be worn in [[laboratory]] settings. The loose-fitting garment was white with an aqua yoke and black stripes outlining the top of each shoulder seam, as well as two external right breast pockets for holding [[code cylinder]]s. The antiseptic coveralls prevented lab contamination, and they were worn with white boots that could ground potentially harmful static charges. The aqua yoke would change color in the presence of harmful levels of radiation.<ref name="ROVD" />

The [[Kyber crystal research team]] wore antiseptic coveralls during their work at the [[Eadu]] [[Eadu Energy Conversion Laboratory|Energy Conversion Laboratory]]. Their coveralls had the insignia of the [[Tarkin Initiative]] in black on the left breast<ref name="ROVD" /> and the back of the right shoulder,<ref name="RogueOne">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> as well as the [[Imperial crest]] on the left shoulder. next to the insignia on the left breast were the [[aurebesh]] initials [[Krill|K]][[Cresh|C]].<ref name="ROVD" /> Scientists within [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]'s [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial Remnant]] working on his [[Dark Trooper project]] wore a version of the coveralls with the emblem of the [[Imperial Department of Military Research]] on the right breast and on the back, alongside the Imperial crest on the shoulder.<ref name="Chapter 12" />

A similar set of coveralls was worn by at least one [[Unidentified Imperial nurse|Imperial nurse]] on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. It bore an unidentified symbol on the breast and no insignia on the shoulders. Beneath her uniform, the nurse wore a blue shirt.<ref name="Dark Visions 3" />

During the time of the [[New Republic]], a similar [[New Republic medical assistant's uniform|uniform]] was worn by medical [[aide|assistants]] within the [[Re-Integration Institute]]. It had a darker blue yoke and bore the [[red sigil]] on the left shoulder.<ref name="Chapter 19">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 19: The Convert}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
This uniform first appeared in the [[2016]] film<ref name="Director Announced">{{SW|url=news/gareth-edwards-and-gary-whitta-onboard-for-star-wars-stand-alone-film|text=Gareth Edwards and Gary Whitta Onboard for ''Star Wars'' Stand-Alone Film}}</ref> ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="RogueOne" /> and was subsequently used in ''[[Chapter 12: The Siege]]''<ref name="Chapter 12" /> of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', which aired [[November 20]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-november-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in November 2020}}</ref>

The symbol that appears on the coveralls of Gideon's scientists is identical to the insignia for the [[Imperial Department of Military Research/Legends|Imperial Department of Military Research]] used in [[Star Wars Legends]];<ref name="NEG">''[[The New Essential Guide to Weapons and Technology]]''</ref> it has therefore been assumed that the symbol represents the same organisation in [[Canon]].
