The '''Bastion''' was a building constructed by the [[Stybla family]] to protect an [[alien]] [[superweapon]] known as the [[Starflash]]<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> around [[5000 BBY]]<ref name="Starflash date" /> in the early [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], the nation of the [[Chiss]] [[species]], in an attempt to never use the [[weapon]]. However, [[Standard century|centuries]] later, during a [[Unidentified war (Chiss Ascendancy)|war]] against an [[Unidentified enemy of the Chiss|enemy]] of the Ascendancy, the Chiss were forced to use the Starflash to protect their remaining people from the enemy's [[fleet]]. The Bastion was soon after replaced by the [[Universal Analysis Group]] (UAG) on the Ascendancy [[planet]] [[Sposia]] as the guardian of the alien artifacts the Chiss had collected.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bastion was mentioned in the flashbacks of the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final volume of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
