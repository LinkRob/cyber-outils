'''EG-86''' was an [[EG-series power droid]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> that served the rebellion as a [[Courier droid|courier]] of vital information. Rebel [[Hera Syndulla]] dispatched fellow rebels [[Sabine Wren]], [[Ezra Bridger]], and the [[droid]] [[C1-10P]] to find EG-86 and take it to the [[Havoc Outpost]], but their mission was nearly compromised by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Ketsu Onyo]], who tried to gather the droid for the criminal organization called [[Black Sun]]. Onyo had a change of heart, though, and helped the rebels deliver EG-86 to the Havoc Outpost.<ref name="Blood Sisters" />
