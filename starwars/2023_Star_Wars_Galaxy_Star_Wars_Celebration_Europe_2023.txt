'''2023 ''Star Wars'' Galaxy ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023''' was a trading card set issued by [[Topps]]. Containing 40 cards, the set was only available for purchase at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe 2023]],<ref name="Topps SWCE 2023" /> which took place from [[April 7]] through [[April 10|10]], [[2023]].<ref name="Twitter Announcement" />
