{{Quote|A data rod? Great, that will have the information I need.|[[Kazuda Xiono]], to [[MB-13A]]|<ref name="The New Trooper">{{Resistance|The New Trooper}}</ref>}}
'''Data rods''',<ref name="The New Trooper" /> also known as '''datarods''',<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|datarod|Datarod}}</ref>  '''datastick'''<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref>, or '''memory rods''',<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" /> were devices designed for scanning and storing vast quantities of data. They were handheld, cylinder-shaped, and had [[Color|red]] and gold accents.<ref name="Decommissioned">{{TBB|Decommissioned}}</ref> Although some were small [[datachip]]s.<ref name="The New Trooper" />

[[Clone Force 99]] utilized one to activate the head of a [[tactical droid]]. While [[Tech]] hacked the droid to activate the [[battle droid]]s, he also downloaded a copy of the information, which [[Hunter]] gave the data rod to the [[Martez family|Martez]] sisters [[Trace Martez|Trace]] and [[Rafa Martez|Rafa]].<ref name="Decommissioned" />

Prior to the [[Cold War]], [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] stole a datarod from [[MB-13A]]. Looking at the data rod, they saw a [[star map]] of [[the galaxy]]. [[Jarek Yeager]] speculated that the red dots on the map were [[First Order]] fleets.<ref name="The New Trooper" />

==Behind the scenes==
Memory rods first appeared in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" /> They were first identified as datasticks in ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'', a [[2022]] novel by [[Adam Christopher]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> The technology were then coined as data rods in "[[The New Trooper]]."<ref name="The New Trooper" />
