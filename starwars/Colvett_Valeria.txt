'''Colvett Valeria''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] and former lover of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] who served as a [[Lieutenant]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and later the [[New Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Biography==
===Meeting the Freemakers===
Colvett<ref name="Summer Vacation" /> Valeria was a [[starfighter]] [[pilot]], who briefly romanced [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] while residing on [[Tatooine]],<ref name="Summer Vacation" /> before later joining the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]], holding the rank of [[lieutenant]] in the period between the [[Battle of Hoth]] and the [[Battle of Endor]]. At the time that [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] made contact with the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]], Valeria was flying a [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wing starfighter]]. Since her starfighter lacked a Type A vector coil, Valeria was unable to steer her ship and crashed the U-wing into the [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80A ''Home One'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]] ''[[Home One]]''{{'s}} [[hangar]]. When Valeria chastised the ground crew team member, he replied that the rebels had no more vector coils to spare. As a result, the Freemakers led by [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] traveled across the galaxy and [[Mission to obtain a Type A vector coil|managed to obtain a Type A vector coil]] after bartering with several people.<ref name="Home One">{{FreemakerAdventures|Home One (episode)}}</ref>

After an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] discovered the ''Home One'', Valeria led [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron's]] assault [[Skirmish with an Imperial Star Destroyer|against]] the Star Destroyer but were unable to pierce through its [[deflector shield]]s. Blue Squadron began to take casualties until the Freemakers' [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]] accidentally catapulted himself into space and knocked out the [[deflector shield generator]]. This enabled the ''Home One'' and Blue Squadron to destroy the Star Destroyer and its [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter escorts]].<ref name="Thrown Into Battle">{{FreemakerAdventures|Thrown Into Battle}}</ref>

===Assault on Strike Station Aurek===
Later, Lieutenant Valeria chaired a meeting to plan Blue Squadron's [[Assault on Strike Station Aurek|assault]] on the Imperial [[Strike Station Aurek]]. Her plan involved dropping a [[proton torpedo]] into a [[Thermal exhaust port]], a feat which had been first deployed by [[Luke Skywalker]] during the [[Battle of Yavin]]. [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] and Roger accidentally damaged Blue Squadron's starfighters. While Kordi and [[Zander Freemaker]] distracted Valeria, Rowan and Roger repaired the starfighters with Rowan using his [[The Force|Force]] powers. However, Roger accidentally built himself into Valeria's [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing starfighter's]] [[astromech socket]].<ref name="Rowan's Secret Adventure">{{FreemakerAdventures|Rowan's Secret Adventure}}</ref>

By the time that Valeria had managed to blast through an [[S-foils|S-foil]] which Kordi and Zander had used to obstruct her and her squadron's path, Rowan and Roger had finished rebuilding the starfighters. Valeria entered the cockpit of her Y-wing while Rowan was struggling to free Roger. Without noticing the two, Valeria took her starfighter into space. Valeria led the assault on Strike Station Aurek while Rowan and Roger did their best to guard her flank from TIE fighters. As Valeria approached the thermal exhaust port, Rowan used the Force to deploy the proton torpedo. Strike Station Aurek was destroyed and Valeria complimented her gunner and [[astromech droid]], not realizing they were Rowan and Roger. After returning to the ''Home One'', Rowan and Roger cut themselves out of the Y-wing. Valeria then praised her co-pilot and astromech droid, who expressed bemusement at being left behind.<ref name="Rowan's Secret Adventure" />

===Working with Zander===
Later, Zander Freemaker attempted to convince Valeria to let him join her squadron. However, Valeria turned down his offer because his starship [[Blazemaker Mark II]] lacked [[blaster]]s. Shortly later, Valeria and Blue Squadron were deployed to meet an Imperial [[Rebel-Imperial dogfight|counter-attack]]. Blue Squadron soon found itself outnumbered and outgunned by numerous TIE fighters and [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE interceptors]]. Fortunately for them, Zander came to the rescue in the Blazemaker, which was heavily laden with several [[laser cannon]]s and a giant [[LR1K sonic cannon|magma cannon]]. Zander destroyed several Imperial fighters, turning the tide of the dogfight. Valeria remarked that his ship was messy but effective and complimented Zander for creating an opening. Before Zander could destroy the remaining three stragglers, the Blazemaker Mark II exploded since it was heavily loaded with weapons. Zander survived and was flung against the ''Home One''{{'s}} command bridge.<ref name="ZFSPG">{{FreemakerAdventures|Zander Freemaker, Superstar Pilot Guy!}}</ref>

Sometime later, as the Freemakers were repairing the Alliance fleet's starfighters, they were attacked by two Imperial Star Destroyers. Valeria led Blue Squadron into battle to clear a path for the fleet to escape into [[hyperspace]]. Despite her successful strategy, Valeria's A-wing and the other rebel starfighters sustained damage. While Zander was annoyed due to a shortage of spareparts, Valeria retorted that he could fix it because he was "Zander Freemaker, Superstar Pilot Guy." The Freemakers and the [[Mon Calamari]] starship builder [[Quarrie]] subsequently embarked on a [[Mission to Tibalt|salvage run]] on the planet [[Tibalt/LEGO|Tibalt]].<ref name="Trouble">{{FreemakerAdventures|Trouble on Tibalt}}</ref>

Later, Valeria was present while Zander repaired [[Grayson]]'s X-wing starfighter. While Grayson thought the ship belonged on the scrap heap, Valeria advised patience. Zander managed to extract a cracked coolant compensator and reassured Grayson that the ship would fly. Valeria then praised Zander, who was distracted by his little brother Rowan. After Rowan had left, Zander asked Valeria if he could join her squadron but she replied that he was better off as a mechanic.<ref name="TTOAN">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Tower of Alistan Nor}}</ref>

===Dawn of the ''Arrowhead''===
When the Imperial Navy tracked down the Alliance fleet, Valeria took part in the [[Mission to save the Rebel Fleet|starfighter battle]]. Due to her skills as a pilot, Quarrie enlisted her services in flying the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']] to the desert planet of [[Jakku]] for a test flight. After the ''Arrowhead'' blew up upon landing and its energy matrix activator fell into the hands of the [[Imperial officer]]s [[Durpin]] and [[Plumestriker]], Valeria helped the Freemakers recover the energy matrix activator by knocking out the two Imperial officers.<ref name="FOTA">{{FreemakerAdventures|Flight of the Arrowhead}}</ref>

When the Imperials pursued them in an [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST walker]], Valeria exchanged fire with them. The AT-ST walker fired a blast which knocked Valeria to the ground. Zander then came to her rescue by flying the [[Mini Scavenger]] into the walker. After the ''Arrowhead'' was assembled, Valeria allowed Zander to fly the ship since she was exhausted. Valeria accompanied the Freemakers back to the Alliance fleet and watched as they used the ''Arrowhead'' to destroy a Star Destroyer, forcing the Empire to retreat.<ref name="FOTA" />

===Combat operations===
Following the launch of the ''Arrowhead'', Lieutenant Valeria led rebel forces and their Wookiee allies including [[Chief]] [[Attiburra]] and [[Tantarra]] in [[Skirmish on Kashyyyk (Freemakers)|combat]] against Imperial forces on [[Kashyyyk]]. Outgunned by an [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST walker]], the rebels and their Wookiee allies retreated to their [[Auzituck anti-slaver gunship]] along the beach. Valeria radioed the Freemakers for help.<ref name="APR">{{FreemakerAdventures|A Perilous Rescue}}</ref>

The Freemakers arrived on the ''Arrowhead'' and destroyed the AT-ST walker, forcing the [[scout trooper]]s to retreat. Valeria and her rebel and Wookiee forces attempted to pursue the Imperials but were stopped by the advance of three [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT walkers]]. The Freemakers used the ''Arrowhead'' to damage the three walkers, forcing the surviving Imperials to retreat. Valeria complemented the Freemakers for their help as the ''Arrowhead'' departed into space.<ref name="APR" />

After Kordi convinced the [[Alliance High Command]] to [[Assault near Alzoc III|attack]] an Imperial fleet docking for repairs near [[Alzoc III/Legends|Alzoc 3]], Admiral Ackbar tasked Valeria and her Blue Squadron with attacking the repair station in coordination with Zander, who was flying the ''Arrowhead''. The ''Arrowhead'' destroyed several Star Destroyers. Though the starship was caught in the [[tractor beam]] of [[Commander]] [[Estoc]]'s Star Destroyer [[Desolator (Imperial-class)|''Desolator'']], Valeria managed to free the ''Arrowhead'' by taking out the [[tractor beam projector]]. This allowed the ''Arrowhead'' to destroy the ''Desolator''.<ref name="APR" />

===Helping the Freemakers===
Valeria encountered Zander, Kordi, and Roger when they attempted to steal the ''Arrowhead'' in order to rescue Rowan Freemaker from the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. At Zander's signal, Roger tried to sneak up on Valeria but she dodged him and seized his arm. Realizing that the Freemakers were wanting to launch the ''Arrowhead'' in order to rescue Rowan, she took pity on them and told them that she would have done the same. After returning Roger's arm, she told them to say she was never there before letting them proceed on [[Rescue of Rowan Freemaker|their mission]].<ref name="EFC">{{FreemakerAdventures|Escape from Coruscant}}</ref>

===Battle of Endor===
Prior to the [[Battle of Endor]], Lieutenant Valeria was contacted by the Freemakers, who had traveled to [[Sullust]] to rendezvous with the rebel fleet. Though, Valeria was reluctant to speak with them since she could face a court martial, she informed them that the rebels were heading to the [[Endor system]] to attack the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. The Freemakers tried to warn Valeria that the station was fully operational but their message was lost in the static of space.<ref name="ROTROTJ">{{FreemakerAdventures|Return of the Return of the Jedi}}</ref>

After the Freemakers managed to board the second Death Star and escape their prison cell, Kordi contacted Valeria to inform them off their location. Valeria informed then that Admiral Ackbar had ordered them to enter the Death Star and told the Freemakers to flee.<ref name="ROTROTJ" />

Following the Battle of Endor, Valeria joined the Freemakers and Quarrie at the rebel celebrations in [[Bright Tree Village]]. Quarrie suggsted that the X-wing's design could do with some tweaking.<ref name="ROTROTJ" />

==Personality and traits==
Valeria was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] Rebel Alliance starfighter pilot with dark skin and black hair. Years prior to joining tbe Rebellion, she vacationed to [[Tatooine]], where she had a short-lived romance with [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="Summer Vacation" /> She was able to pilot several different starfighters including U-wings,<ref name="Home One" /> Y-wings,<ref name="Rowan's Secret Adventure" /> and [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wings]].<ref name="ZFSPG" /> She was a good leader who coordinated strikes and dogfights against the Empire.<ref name="Rowan's Secret Adventure" /> Valeria appreciated the Freemakers for their efforts to obtain a Type A vector coil for her U-wing.<ref name="Home One" /> Valeria used her knowledge of rebel tactics and the history of the [[Battle of Yavin]] to destroy the Imperial Strike Station Aurek. However, she was too enthusiastic to notice that Rowan and Roger were aboard her Y-wing<ref name="Rowan's Secret Adventure" /> Valeria appreciated Zander's enthusiasm to join Blue Squadron but turned him down because his starfighter Blazemaker II lacked [[blaster]]s. Valeria relented after Zander refitted his starfighter with laser cannons and turned the tide of a dogfight.<ref name="ZFSPG" />

After expressing doubt about the state of the repairs that the Freemakers did to her A-wing, she said that if she exploded, she would at least take Imperial TIEs down with her.<ref name="Trouble" /> Valeria had faith in Zander's mechanical abilities and praised him for savlaging Grayson's X-wings.<ref name="TTOAN" />

Valeria had a good friendship with the Freemakers, working with them in several adventures on Jakku.<ref name="FOTA" /> Kashyyyk, and at the Imperial repair station near Alzoc III.<ref name="APR" /> Valeria was sympathetic to the Freemakers after learning that Rowan Freemaker had been captured by the Empire. She allowed them to "steal" the ''Arrowhead'' so that they could travel to Coruscant and rescue their brother.<ref name="EFC" />

==Skills and abilities==
Valeria was a skilled starfighter pilot, with Quarrie opining that she had chromium nerves and nexu reflexes. In addition, Valeria was skilled at unarmed combat and a competent sniper.<ref name="FOTA" /> Due to her combat skills and reflexes, Valeria was able to protect herself when Roger tried to sneak up on her and knock her out.<ref name="EFC" />
