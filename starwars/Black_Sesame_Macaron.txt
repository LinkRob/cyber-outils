'''Black Sesame Macarons''' were a type of [[cookie]] served at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Kat Saka's Kettle" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> It was consumable by [[human]]s.<ref name="Kat Saka's Kettle" />

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Black Sesame Macaron was a treat exclusively available at Kat Saka's Kettle in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]] for a limited time beginning [[November 12]], [[2021]]. The snack was a seasonal offering for the park's celebration of [[Life Day]], which corresponded with the [[In-universe|real world]] holiday season.<ref name="DLP Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/kat-sakas-kettle/menus|text=Disneyland: Kat Saka's Kettle Snack Menu}}</ref><ref name="BlogMickey">{{WebCite|url=https://blogmickey.com/2021/11/life-day-specialty-menu-items-coming-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge-at-disneyland|text="Life Day" Specialty Menu Items Coming to Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge at Disneyland|author=Mike|date=2021-11-09|work=[https://blogmickey.com BlogMickey]|archivedate=20211109225753}} Includes menu image.</ref> The menu described the dish as "Toasted [[Wikipedia:Macaron|Macaron]], [[Wikipedia:Passion fruit (fruit)|Passion Fruit]] [[Wikipedia:Buttercream|Buttercream]], and [[Wikipedia:Sesame|Sesame]] [[Wikipedia:Praline (nut confection)|Praline]]."<ref name="DLP Menu" />
