A '''flare''' was a pyrotechnic device commonly used as a signal in the absence of communications. They were often [[color]]-coded so their meaning could be understood quickly.

==History==
During the [[Battle of Christophsis]], [[Jedi General|General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] fired four flares from a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette]], which acted as decoys for the incoming [[tracking torpedo]]es. After launching the flares, Skywalker cloaked the stealth [[Starship|ship]], and the torpedoes veered away from the stealth ship and into one of the flares.<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref>

[[Domino Squad]] member [[Hevy]] used a flare to warn Captain [[Rex]] and Commander [[Cody]] about the [[BX-series droid commando|droid commandos]] at the [[Rishi Moon]]'s [[Rishi Station|outpost]].<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref>
