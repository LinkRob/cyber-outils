'''Corax Oreli''' was a [[Theelin]] [[Gender|female]] member of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]. When the Nihil allowed [[Xylan Graf]], who was serving them as a hostage on the [[Lightning Crash]] [[space station]], to venture outside of the [[Occlusion Zone]] and attend a gathering on [[Hosnian Prime]], Oreli was sent to watch over him as a [[bodyguard]]. Oreli did not wish to enter the venue herself, placing a [[Homing beacon|tracker]] on Graf. After Graf allowed himself to be kidnapped by [[Jordanna Sparkburn]] and [[Sylvestri Yarrow]] unknowingly on behalf of the rogue Nihil [[Deva Lompop]], he planted the tracker within their [[starship]], the ''[[Vengeful Goddess]]''. Oreli followed them to their meeting point in the [[Seswenna system]], but Lompop had arrived first, and destroyed Oreli's vessel.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />
