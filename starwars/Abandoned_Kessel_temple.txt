An '''abandoned temple''' stood on the [[Asteroid/Legends|asteroid]]-shaped [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Kessel/Legends|Kessel]] at the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]; Located in a [[Mountain/Legends|mountainous]] region, the temple contained several rooms ornated with statues, sarcophagi and masks. The building's facade was made of [[Color/Legends|blue]] [[Stone/Legends|stones]] and covered with mysterious glyphs. Shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]] in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]], the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] had placed [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] in the vicinity of the temple, but that place was also the lair of a [[Changeling/Legends|shapeshifter]] known as [[Gyaos Vader]]. The same [[Standard year/Legends|year]], the would-be [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] raided the abandoned temple to save his [[Jedi Master/Legends|mentor]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who had been kidnapped by Gyaos Vader.<ref name="SW87" />

==Behind the scenes==
That abandoned temple appeared in the [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] [[Star Wars (1987 video game)|''Star Wars'' 1987 video game]]. The building's architecture and interior decoration were inspired by the art of [[Wikipedia:Ancient Egypt|Ancient Egypt]]. In the lair of Gyaos Vader hung a [[Gold/Legends|golden]] mask<ref name="SW87" /> similar to that of the [[Wikipedia:Egypt|Egyptian]] [[Wikipedia:Pharaoh|pharaoh]] [[Wikipedia:Tutankhamun|Tutankhamun]].<ref name="Akhet">{{WebCite|url=http://www.akhet.co.uk/tuttreas.htm|text=Tutankhamun's Treasure|work=Akhet&mdash;The Horizon to Ancient Egypt|archivedate=20190715072156}}</ref> A statue of the goddess [[Wikipedia:Taweret|Taweret]], portrayed as a bipedal hippopotamus wearing a wig,<ref name="TGEM">{{WebCite|url=http://globalegyptianmuseum.org/glossary.aspx?id=368|text=Taweret|archivedate=20170213200029}}</ref> could also be seen in the vicinity of the temple.<ref name="SW87" /> The capitals of the temple's columns were also ornamented with portraits resembling the face of the goddess [[Wikipedia:Hathor|Hathor]] found on the pillars of [[Wikipedia:Dendera Temple complex|Dendera temple]].<ref name="Hathor">{{WebCite|url=https://books.google.be/books?id=XIns9M_9DcgC&pg=PA103&lpg=PA103&dq=%22hathor+column%22+dendera&source=bl&ots=LyjBLEvXle&sig=Bz47wjMMmqoYa276ig0wEwaXTBo&hl=fr&sa=X&ei=RkH0UPG0L822hAeZooHIDg%23v%3Donepage&q=%22hathor+column%22+dendera&f=false|text=The Encyclopedia of Ancient Egyptian Architecture|archivedate=20200229021631}}</ref>
