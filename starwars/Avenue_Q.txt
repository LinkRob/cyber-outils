'''''Avenue Q''''' was a musical playing at the [[Galaxies Opera House/Legends|Galaxies Opera House]] in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]]. A sign advertising the show was displayed outside of the [[Opera/Legends|opera]] house during a performance of [[Squid Lake/Legends|''Squid Lake'']] attended by [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]]. The sign depicted the show's title, ''Avenue Q'', written in [[Aurebesh/Legends|Aurebesh]].<ref name="Revenge of the Clone Menace">''[[Tag & Bink: Revenge of the Clone Menace]]''</ref>
