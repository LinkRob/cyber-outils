The '''Declavia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]]. It was located in grid square M-11 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Declavian cognac]]|system=Declavia [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> [[Declavian cognac]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref>
