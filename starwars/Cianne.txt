'''Cianne''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] as the [[aide]] of [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]]. In preparation for the [[Battle of Yavin]], Cianne joined Mothma and a [[pilot]] in a [[shuttle]] that left [[Alliance High Command]] and [[Yavin 4|Yavin IV]] behind as a contingency in the event that the attack on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] failed and resulted in the destruction of the Rebellion.<ref name="Contingency" />

Cianne had a [[biochemical regulatory implant]].<ref name="Contingency" />
