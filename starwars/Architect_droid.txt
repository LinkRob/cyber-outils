'''Architect droid''' was a model of [[droid]] designed to help build things<ref name="Extraordinary Droids">''[[Star Wars: Extraordinary Droids]]''</ref> that dated back to the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] and the nascent [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. One such droid was the [[Mark IV architect droid|Mark IV]] [[Professor]] [[Huyang]], who specialized in the construction of [[lightsaber]]s.<ref name="SWE" />
