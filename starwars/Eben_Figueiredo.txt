'''Eben Figueiredo''' (born c. [[1996]])<ref name="Birth" /> is a [[Wikipedia:United Kingdom|British]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]].<ref name="Artist's Partnership" /> He is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan and was thrilled to have the opportunity to work on a project for the franchise, describing how it was something of a childhood dream come true.<ref name="DWRav4" /> As a child, he grew a [[Padawan braid]], having wanted to be like a [[Padawan|young Jedi]] in [[Jedi training|training]].<ref name="TheGuardian">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2021/may/05/eben-figueiredo-spotlight-on-stage-actor-star-wars|text=Eben Figueiredo: electric actor, soul singer and Star Wars mudtrooper|author=[https://www.theguardian.com/profile/arifa-akbar Arifa Akbar]|date=2021-05-05|work=[[Wikipedia:The Guardian|''The Guardian'']]|archivedate=20210507012151}}</ref> In [[2018]], the actor portrayed an [[Imperial swamp trooper|Imperial mudtrooper]] in the [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Credits">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Figueiredo joined the cast of ''Solo'' after auditioning for and coming close to a variety of roles with<ref name="DWRav4" /> UK casting director<ref name="Credits" /> [[Nina Gold]],<ref name="DWRav4" /> having been called by her to try out.<ref name="TheGuardian" /> He was ultimately cast as an Imperial mudtrooper along with two other actors,<ref name="DWRav4" /> [[Shaquille Ali-Yebuah]] and [[Aaron Heffernan]].<ref name="Credits" /> The three mudtrooper actors spent about a month filming their scenes in ''Solo: A Star Wars Story''.<ref name="DWRav4" />
