The '''Battle of Amaltanna''' was a battle between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] on the planet [[Amaltanna]]. In the end, the Republic triumphed over the Separatists, but the extent of the damage was so severe that its inhabitants were relocated as refugees to worlds such as [[Naboo]] and [[Alderaan]].<ref name="FotL" />

[[File:Impregnable.jpg|thumb|250px|The Legends version of the Battle of Amaltanna appeared in the comic story "Impregnable" and was used by author Adam Lance Garcia to tease its canonization.]]
The Battle of Amaltanna appeared in "[[Galactic Tales: The Failing of the Light]]," a short story by [[Adam Lance Garcia]] that was published in [[Star Wars Insider 233|issue 233]] of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="FotL" /> on [[August 19]], [[2025]].<ref name="Lunar">{{WebCite|url=https://www.lunardistribution.com/home/search?term=Star+Wars+Insider|text=Star Wars Insider|work=[https://www.lunardistribution.com/ Lunar Distribution]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/GLG8o}}</ref> The [[Battle of Amaltanna/Legends|battle]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity and appeared in the comic story "[[Impregnable (comic story)|Impregnable]],"<ref name="imp">{{CWACite|7|Impregnable (comic story)}}</ref> which was released on [[January 31]], [[2007]],<ref name="DH-Link">{{DarkHorse|url=Books/14-333/Star-Wars-Clone-Wars-Adventures-Volume-7|text=Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures Volume 7}}</ref> as part of ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures Volume 7]]''. In the story, the battle was a bloody affair that occurred six [[Standard month/Legends|months]] into the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] and saw conflict between the [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]]s of [[Jedi General/Legends|Jedi General]] [[Bultar Swan/Legends|Bultar Swan]] and the [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]] of<ref name="imp" /> [[Baron/Legends|Baron]] [[Edi Wedd]],<ref name="cswe">{{CSWECite|III|323|Wedd, Baron Edi}}</ref> who commanded the battle from a [[Impregnable (fortress)|seemingly impregnable fortress]].<ref name="imp" />

Garcia intended for the canon battle to have started early in the Clone War, yet he decided against outright confirming as such in "The Failing of the Light" to give other [[Lucasfilm]] creatives a chance to determine its exact timeframe.<ref name="Early" /> Ahead of release on [[July 24]], 2025, Garcia teased the role of the Battle of Amaltanna in his story by posting an image of the battle's Legends equivalent to his Bluesky page.<ref name="Legends">{{Bluesky|adamlancegarcia.bsky.social|post/3lupvvu4wbc2e|[[Adam Lance Garcia]]|date=2025-07-24|quote=[A picture of the Legends Battle of Amaltanna]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/j6vOZ}}</ref> On [[August 2]], Garcia teased that the story would feature "several events from both Legends and Canon" from a specific perspective, all without spoiling what those events were.<ref name="LegendsCanon">{{Bluesky|adamlancegarcia.bsky.social|post/3lvhbzbggt22y|[[Adam Lance Garcia]]|date=2025-08-02|quote=Without giving too much away, you will see several events from both Legends and Canon through a particular set of eyes. How those events shape our protagonist will sit at the core of this story.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/NGH4c}}</ref> After release, Garcia expressed joy to see story elements from "The Failing of the Light" receive their own pages in the [[Wookieepedia]] fan encyclopedia,<ref name="WookMentioned">{{Bluesky|adamlancegarcia.bsky.social|post/3lwqva54b6c2f|[[Adam Lance Garcia]]|date=2025-08-19|quote=Quietly freaking out at seeing canon @wookofficial.bsky.social articles based on "The Failing of the Light" beginning to pop up.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/icQZz}}</ref> including the canonized Battle of Amaltanna.<ref name="TheArticleYoureReadingRN">{{Bluesky|adamlancegarcia.bsky.social|post/3lwvxrv3vs22f|[[Adam Lance Garcia]]|date=2025-08-21|quote=Battle of Amaltanna (was Legends, now Canon)|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/ZR9E4}}</ref>
