The '''Battle of Pandem Nai''' or '''attack on Pandem Nai''' was an attack during the [[Galactic Civil War]] led by the [[New Republic]]'s [[Alphabet Squadron]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]'s base on [[Pandem Nai]], home to the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. The mission was part of the wider [[New Republic Intelligence]] led [[Hunt for Shadow Wing]] campaign.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
