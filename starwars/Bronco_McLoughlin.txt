'''Bronco McLoughlin''' (real name '''Anthony Gerard McLoughlin'''; [[August 10]], [[1930s#1938|1938]] &ndash; [[March 26]], [[2019]])<ref name="Guardian" /> was an Irish stuntman. He played a [[stormtrooper]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']],<ref name="Independent" /> but was not listed in the credits.

McLoughlin was a prolific stunt actor from for decades. His first role was in [[1960s#1967|1967's]] [[Wikipedia:The Viking Queen|''The Viking Queen'']], while his last was in a [[2014]] episode of [[Wikipedia:Vikings (2013 TV series)|''Vikings'']].<ref name="RTE">{{WebCite|url=https://www.rte.ie/entertainment/2019/0326/1038757-irish-stuntman-anthony-bronco-mcloughlin-has-died|text=Irish stuntman Anthony 'Bronco' McLoughlin has died|author=Alan Corr|date=2019-03-27|work=[[Wikipedia:Raidió Teilifís Éireann|Raidió Teilifís Éireann]]|archivedate=20210119161007}}</ref> He was also famous for his appearance in the [[1986]] film [[Wikipedia:The Mission (1986 film)|''The Mission'']], where he was bound to a wooden cross floating on rapids.<ref name="Guardian" />

McLoughlin doubled for [[Wikipedia:Edward Woodward|Edward Woodward]] in the iconic opening scene of [[1973]]'s [[Wikipedia:The Wicker Man|''The Wicker Man'']] and worked extensively with [[Harrison Ford]] on the set of [[Wikipedia:Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade|''Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade'']] ([[1989]]), training Ford on how to utilize his character's famous bullwhip. Other appearances include two [[Wikipedia:James Bond|James Bond]] films, [[Wikipedia:Superman (1978 film)|''Superman'']] ([[1978]]), [[Wikipedia:Rambo III|''Rambo III'']] ([[1988]]) and [[Wikipedia:Troy (film)|''Troy'']] ([[2004]]).<ref name="Guardian" />
