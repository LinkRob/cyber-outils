The '''FN Trooper Corps''',<ref name="TrooperCorps">{{SWU|set=Secrets of Power|cardname=FN Trooper Corps|link=7248761207|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/2o2It}}</ref> also known as the '''FN Corps''' for short, was a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[corps]] in the [[First Order military]]. Prior to their first deployment, members of the FN Corps were trained by [[Captain]] [[Phasma]], who believed their class to be the greatest in the [[First Order]]. [[Finn|FN-2187]] commanded a [[fire team]] within the corps.<ref name="Before the Awakening" />
