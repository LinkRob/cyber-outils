'''Fight day''' was a [[Local day|day]] on the [[planet]] [[Ruathi]] in which the planet's [[gambling]] halls hosted fights. At one point, it had been a daily thing, but by<ref name="Wayseeker">''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]''</ref> around [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{WayseekerDate}}</ref> it had been changed by the [[Ruathi government]] to a one-night [[Standard week|weekly]] event because it had been detrimental to the economy on Ruathi.<ref name="Wayseeker" />

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
