"'''A Droid's Mission'''" is the third short of [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|Season 2]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'', and the ninth short overall. It was released on [[August 2]], [[2024]].<ref name="Announcement" />

==Official description==
''Droids RJ-83 and R0-M1 team up to find a rare part.''<ref name="DisneyPlus" />

==Plot summary==
The episode opens with the [[youngling]]s [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Nash Durango]], [[Nubs]], [[Lys Solay]] and [[Padawan]] [[Wes Vinik]] boarding the ''[[Crimson Firehawk]]'' for a delivery run. The droids [[R0-M1]] and [[RJ-83]] follow their masters. RJ-83 bumps into R0-M1, prompting the larger [[astromech droid]] to tell RJ off in [[Binary]]. 

Once everyone is aboard, Durango prepares the ''Firehawk'' for takeoff but the ship's [[forward thruster plug]] breaks down. Vinik offers the services of his droid R0-M1 and tells him to bring a reluctant RJ-83 along. The two droids protest but Vinik reassures R0 that RJ-83 knows his way around [[Kublop Springs]]. A grumbling R0 and RJ roll away to [[Marlaa Jinara's junkyard|Marlaa's junkyard]]. 

At the junkyard, R0-M1 sorts through a tool box, throwing a wrench and cover at RJ-83. RJ squeals in protest and then spots the thruster plug at the top of a stack of crates. R0 fires up his thrusters and reaches the top. However, RJ-83 is not willing to let the rival droid claim victory and purposely topples the stack. An automated loader picks up the thruster and shovels it into its storage compartment. The loader takes the scrap metal including the forward thruster into an automated [[train]] to be processed for scrap. 

As the train rolls away on the tracks, the two droids decide to put aside their differences. R0-M1 lets RJ-83 piggy back on him as he rockets after the train. RJ-81 attempts to grab the edge of the train car but loses his grip. R0-M1 catches RJ-83 in mid-air and the two continue their pursuit. The two droids hit a metal railway structure but continue their pursuit. RJ-83 convinces R0-M1 to throw him forward onto the train car. RJ manages to retrieve the thruster plug and attempts to jump towards R0-M1 but underestimates his jump and descends into the forest below. RO-M1 manages to rescue RJ-83 using his jets.

The two droids return to the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple]] with the forward thruster plug. Durango thanks R0-M1 and the younglings scurry aboard the ''Crimson Firehawk''. RJ-83 is upset at being unrecognized but R0-M1 extends one of his appendages in a gesture of friendship. He lets RJ-83 ride on top of his dome. Vinik commends them for learning the value of teamwork.
