The '''Celestial Chalice''' was a round temple located within the [[Pijali palace]] complex on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]. As an amphitheater dedicated to the ancient [[Deity|gods]] of Pijal, it was only entered for [[Pijali religion|ceremonial rituals]] of the [[Pijali monarchy]] a few times during a [[Monarchy|monarch's]] reign. Unlike most of the palace, the Celestial Chalice was opulently decorated. In [[40 BBY]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] saw a [[Force vision]] of the Celestial Chalice while on a [[Mission to Pijal|mission]] to protect the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] prior to her [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]]. Although the [[Jedi]] attempted to warn [[Lord]] [[Regent]] [[Rael Averross]] and others of the violence he had foreseen, his warnings were dismissed.

On the [[Standard day|day]] of the coronation, Jinn's [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] accompanied Fanry to the altar of the Celestial Chalice. After being [[crown]]ed [[queen]], Fanry activated a [[Kohlen crystal shield|kohlen crystal–powered shield]] around the center of the temple and violently seized absolute power, ordering the [[death]]s of several individuals who had previously disagreed with her. When Kenobi prevented [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] [[captain]] [[Deren]] from acting on his ruler's execution orders by stabbing him, Fanry was prompted to escape from the Celestial Chalice alongside her [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Aide|attendant]] [[Cady]].

==Description==
{{Quote|In the dream, Qui-Gon envisioned the entire Celestial Chalice&mdash;the chamber for the coronation and the treaty signing&mdash;in great detail. When he saw the Chalice for himself, it was identical to his vision in every sense.|Padawan Obi-Wan Kenobi reporting to the Jedi High Council|<ref name="MA" />}}
The Celestial Chalice was a large, round temple located in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]'s [[Pijali palace]]. The tall, [[glass]]-domed amphitheater was the only part of the palace complex that could be seen from the [[Atmosphere|sky]] due to the richly [[forest]]ed area surrounding it. Within the palace's [[royal reception hall]], two well-[[oil]]ed [[wood]]en doors led to the Celestial Chalice's entrance tunnel. The temple's intricate inner entryway was crafted from smooth [[Color|white]] [[stone]] originally taken from the cliffside of an [[Pijal's ocean|ocean]] on Pijal. In contrast to the majority of the royal palace's decor, the Celestial Chalice was opulently decorated with ceiling gilding and a blue tiled floor.<ref name="MA" />

During [[Pijali religion|coronations]], the temple's octagonal altar was used as a dais by Pijal's future [[Pijali monarchy|monarch]] to address the planet's [[Pijali|citizens]] before they were [[crown]]ed. The Celestial Chalice had a large number of seats surrounding the dais from which [[music]] could be easily heard due to the chamber's echoing acoustics.<ref name="MA" /> At some point after inheriting Pijal's [[throne]] in [[48 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]]. Modifications to the Celestial Chalice were done at some point after [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] inherited [[Pijal]]'s [[throne]] eight [[Standard year|years]] prior to the start of the novel, which equates to [[48 BBY]].</ref> [[Kohlen crystal shield|kohlen crystal–powered shields]] capable of deflecting [[lightsaber]] blades were installed in the temple to surround the Celestial Chalice's altar. Additionally, the glass panes forming the domed roof of the amphitheater were altered to become retractable at will.<ref name="MA" />

==History==
===Ceremonial usage===
{{Quote|Unclear. You want to sabotage the coronation and the hyperspace corridor for something that's 'unclear.'''"<br />"''What's not unclear is the screaming I hear in the visions. Nor fighting with a lightsaber. Nor blood on the floor of the Celestial Chalice. And if any of you could feel the fear and despair that came with that vision&mdash;that is as much a part of it as any image or sound&mdash;you wouldn't doubt this any more than I do.|Rael Averross and Qui-Gon Jinn|<ref name="MA" />}}
[[File:Qui-Gon-Jinn-Databank.jpg|thumb|left|275px|Qui-Gon Jinn saw a Force vision of Fanry's coronation end in violence.]]
Built [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Republic Era]] to honor Pijal's [[Deity|gods]] and their traditions, the Celestial Chalice was a central part of the world's royal palace. For many [[Standard year|years]], the temple served as a venue for the Pijali [[monarchy]]'s sacred ceremonies, which took place two or three times during a monarch's reign. At some point after the Pijali [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] inherited the right to Pijal's throne<ref name="MA" /> in 48 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> she made plans to seize power as an absolute monarch at her [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]] by refusing to sign the monarchy's [[Governance Treaty]] initiative supported by her [[lord]] [[regent]], [[Rael Averross]]. In preparation for her crowning, she ordered modifications to be made to the Celestial Chalice, including the installation of [[Deflector shield|shields]] and a retractable roof. After this, the [[Pijali honor guard]] trained under the [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] [[captain]] [[Deren]] in evacuating a monarch from the temple through its roof using [[dropship]]s.<ref name="MA" />

While on a [[Mission to Pijal|mission]] to protect Fanry<ref name="MA" /> in [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] experienced a [[Force vision]] in which he saw the princess' coronation in the Celestial Chalice end in violence. Jinn visited the amphitheater, and after finding that his vision's details matched the physical traits of the chamber, he assumed that what he had experienced would come true in the near future. The [[Jedi]] Master briefly met the Pijali [[Minister]] [[Orth]] in the temple, finding her claim of checking the Celestial Chalice's security procedures suspicious due to how late it was at [[Local day|night]]. In return, she questioned his motives for visiting the temple when tours were given during the day. Jinn's warnings about his vision were ultimately dismissed by those he mentioned it to, including Averross and Orth. In response, Jinn refused to ratify the Governance Treaty at Fanry's coronation on the [[Galactic Republic]]'s behalf, citing his vision of the temple.<ref name="MA" />

===Princess Fanry's coronation===
{{Quote|Go find loyal guards, if you can. Get them to close the airspace around the Celestial Chalice!|Qui-Gon Jinn to Rael Averross|<ref name="MA" />}}
When [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] explained Jinn's refusal to the [[Jedi High Council]], they appointed him as the Republic's official representative in his Master's stead. Upset, Jinn continued to worry about his vision of violence in Celestial Chalice at Fanry's coronation. The [[Standard day|day]] prior to the crowning, the temple's entrances were sealed off under Deren's orders. When Kenobi visited the amphitheater, he was refused entry by royal guards, who cited security protocols. During the coronation, several guests were escorted into the Celestial Chalice and to their seats. As regal music played, Fanry entered the temple and made her way to the room's center dais.<ref name="MA" />

[[File:ObiWanPadawanLenahra.jpg|thumb|right|280px|Obi-Wan Kenobi accompanied Fanry to her dais at her Celestial Chalice coronation.]]
After swearing her loyalty to Pijal, she received a [[Pijali ceremonial sword|ceremonial sword]] from the [[Unidentified Skykeeper|Skykeeper]]&mdash;Pijal's religious leader&mdash;and was crowned [[queen]]. Fanry proceeded to stab the [[Skykeeper]] with the [[weapon]] as she ordered Deren to activate [[kohlen crystal]]&ndash;powered shields that surrounded her dais. After publicly denouncing [[Democracy|democratic]] ideals and the Governance Treaty, she told her captain to eliminate several individuals who did not support her, including Kenobi and [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] [[Sector Supervisor]] [[Meritt Col]]. Using his sabotaged kohlen crystal&ndash;powered [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's first lightsaber|lightsaber]], Kenobi breached the shield protecting Deren and injured him. Horrified by the sight of her injured companion, Fanry escaped from the Celestial Chalice through its roof by using [[Ascension cable|ascending cables]] to climb into a royal [[Trooper|troop]] dropship with her [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Aide|attendant]] [[Cady]].<ref name="MA" />

After the princess's escape, Kenobi surmised that the [[kyber crystal]] in his lightsaber had been replaced with a kohlen crystal in an attempt to sabotage it prior to his entry to the Celestial Chalice. Jinn instructed Averross to gather loyal royal guards and have them close the airspace surrounding the amphitheater. Not long after their escape from the Celestial Chalice, Cady betrayed Fanry due to the ruler's lack of sympathy for freeing enslaved workers of the Czerka Corporation. Due to the violent outcome of the coronation, the Republic later ruled that Fanry's coronation was invalid. Before Jinn and Kenobi departed Pijal, Averross felt guilt for failing to listen to the Jedi Master's warnings about his Celestial Chalice vision.<ref name="MA" /> The Celestial Chalice was indirectly mentioned in the second chapter of<ref name="SFAW">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]''</ref> [[Skywalker: A Family at War (in-universe)|''Skywalker: A Family at War'']],<ref name="SFAW SW.com">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-reads-recommendations|text=Our ''Star Wars'' Reads Picks For Every Type of Reader in Your Life}}</ref> an exhaustive biography of the [[Skywalker family]] written by the [[journalist]] [[Kitrin Braves]]<ref name="RAF">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> and published at some point after the [[35 ABY]] release<ref name="SFAW Date">{{SkywalkerFamilyAtWarDate}}</ref> of the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]]'s [[book]], ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="RAF" /> The work discussed Jinn's violent visions of Fanry's coronation<ref name="SFAW" /> in the Celestial Chalice<ref name="MA" /> and how they eventually came to fruition.<ref name="SFAW" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Celestial Chalice first appeared in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]]. At separate points, the novel indicates that the tiles decorating the Celestial Chalice are "bright-blue," then "midnight-blue." This article documents that the tiles are blue but leaves out the shade inconsistency.<ref name="MA" />
