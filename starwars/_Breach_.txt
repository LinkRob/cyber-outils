"'''Breach'''" was the callsign of a [[First Order]] [[TIE/se Bomber]] [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|pilot]] and ordnance expert. "Breach" once executed an attack run against an [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17]].<ref name="Breach Card" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Breach" was created for the miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'' in the card of the same name released in the [[2021]] [[Fury of the First Order Squadron Pack]].<ref name="Breach Card" />
