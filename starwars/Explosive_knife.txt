'''Explosive knives'''<ref name="Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/prisoners-concept-art-gallery|text="Prisoners" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> were a type of [[Knife|knives]] used by [[Riff Tamson]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Prisoners" />

==History==
During the final conflict of the [[Battle of Mon Cala]], [[Riff Tamson]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] used four explosive knives on two [[Mon Cala]] soldiers, one [[Quarren]] soldier, and one [[Gungan]] soldier, [[Death|killing]] them all in the process.<ref name="Prisoners">{{TCW|Prisoners}}</ref>

Tamson later engaged [[Prince]] [[Lee-Char]], with Lee-Char stealing one explosive knife and activating its blade before stabbing Tamson with it by throwing it at him like a spear. Unable to remove the knife, Tamson decided to charge instead, giving Lee-Char the opportunity to shoot the detonator and kill Tamson, avenging the death of his [[Yos Kolina|father]].<ref name="Prisoners" />
