An '''electrohammer''' was a heavy [[melee weapon]] wielded by [[Purge Trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Inquisitorius]] after the events of [[Order 66]]. The weapon was able to block [[lightsaber]] attacks.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order" />

In [[14 BBY]], several Purge Troopers wielded electrohammers were stationed on [[Kashyyyk]] and [[Nur]], where [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] fought and [[Death|killed]] them. Later in [[9 BBY]], electrohammers were used by some Imperial [[DT-series sentry droid|DT-series sentry droids]] on [[Jedha]], [[Koboh]] and the [[Imperial Security Bureau|Imperial Security Bureau's]] [[II-0810 Satellite Station|II-0810 Satellite Station.]]<ref name="Survivor" /> Concurrently, the members of the [[Bedlam Raiders]] known as Bedlam Smashers, such as [[Urgost]], Fist of [[Rayvis]], used a different model of electrohammer to the Imperial version on Koboh and its [[Shattered Moon]]. Many of these foes were also defeated by Kestis during his [[Quest for Tanalorr|quest]] for the lost [[planet]] [[Tanalorr]].<ref name="Survivor" /> The electrohammer had two abilities, one being to charge up the hammer's power and slam it onto the ground. This created a shockwave in a short radius, knocking anyone in range off their feet. The second ability was to charge up the hammer's power and slam it onto the floor at an angle, which created a shockwave ripple towards the user's opponent.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order" />
