The '''Chamber of Reason''' was one of the [[Standard century|centuries]]-old [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Chamber|Meditation Chambers]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. The chamber was located in the [[Basalt Forest]] region of Koboh, near the [[Forest Array]]. The chamber had a puzzling nature, for which at least one [[Padawan|Jedi Padawan]] became frustrated in reaching a dead end. Before [[Jedi Knight]] [[Dagan Gera]] made the treacherous journey through the [[Koboh Abyss]] to reach the hidden planet [[Tanalorr]], several Jedi within the chamber discussed the progress of [[Jedi Master]] [[Santari Khri]]'s research on Koboh and its Abyss. At some point, a being scratched a ciphered message into a wall within the chamber, requesting the recipient to meet them at their usual place at a later point.<ref name="Survivor" /> 

The multi-leveled Chamber of Reason contained a series of modified [[orb amplifier]]s that, when activated, created bridges of [[Koboh Matter|Koboh matter]], allowing individuals to cross the wide gaps between the chamber's platforms. The chamber could be accessed using a [[High Republic Era]] [[Republic tuner device|tuner device]], which provided access to a [[Turbolift|lift]] that lowered into the depths below the Basalt Forest.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chamber of Reason was first revealed in a video published by [[Wikipedia:IGN|IGN]] as a side quest in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Survivor" /> The chamber served as an optional experience after players unlocked the High Republic Era tuner device early in the game. Within the chamber, players could collect 5 Databank entries, a [[Datacard|datadisc]], the Diligence [[Blade emitter|emitter]], and the Dexterity perk, which increased the damage to enemies hit with a [[lightsaber]] throw attack.
