'''Control Room 327''' was a [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] control room.<ref name="Bump" />

It was located above [[Docking Bay 327|docking bay 327]] with direct view of the bay it had a large Bank of consoles with direct access to the death star's [[Imperial Computer Network|computer network]] and database there was a door leading to the corridor and another right next to it leading to a maintenance closet.
