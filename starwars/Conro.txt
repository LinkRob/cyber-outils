'''Conro''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[captain]] in the [[Imperial Navy|Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and commanded an [[Conro's Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]]. Under the command of [[Commodore]] [[Idel]], Conro and Captain [[Shan]] traveled to the [[Castell system]] to try and capture the [[yacht]] ''[[Imperialis]]'' after it was [[Theft of the Imperialis|stolen]] by a [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian's crew|group]] of [[Thief|thieves]] led by the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. The trio of Imperials failed in their mission, after Calrissian tricked Conro and Shan into making [[Shan's Star Destroyer|their]] [[Star Destroyer]]s collide and escaped the [[Star system|system]].<ref name="Lando 2" />
