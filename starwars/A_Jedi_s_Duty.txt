"'''A Jedi's Duty'''" is a short story published in the ''[[Stories of Jedi and Sith]]'' short-story collection. The story was written by [[Karen Strong]], illustrated by [[Jake Bartok]], and published on [[June 7]], [[2022]]. The story features [[Barriss Offee]].

{{Plot|story}}

==Continuity==
Offee's memory of the [[Great Hall (Jedi Temple)|Great Hall]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] and her time at the [[Great Tree]] in the [[Jedi Temple training ground]] are similar to the plot points in [[Michael Reaves]] and [[Steve Perry]]'s [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel ''[[MedStar I: Battle Surgeons]]'', specifically the [[Barriss Offee/Legends#Early life|first time]] she used [[Telekinesis/Legends|telekinesis]] before [[Grand Master/Legends|Grand Master]] [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]], and the [[Barriss Offee/Legends#Dark side in the morning|time]] Offee and her [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Luminara Unduli/Legends|Luminara Unduli]] visited the [[blackneedle tree]] at the [[Oa Park]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].<ref name="MBS">''[[MedStar I: Battle Surgeons]]''</ref>
