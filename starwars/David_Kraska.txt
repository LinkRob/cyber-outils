'''David Kraska''' is a sound editor who worked on the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]''. To create the sounds of the [[Unidentified telescopic array|observatory]] in the episode "[[Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem]]," Kraska used the sounds of real-life observatories mixed with other mechanical sounds.<ref name="Strange and Familiar" />
