"'''Darth Vader vs. the Rebel Fleet - Fearsome Fighter Pilot'''" is the twenty-first episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com]] and YouTube channel on [[March 29]], [[2019]].<ref name="YouTube" /><ref name="SWKid">{{Disney|url=star-wars-kids|subdomain=lol|text=Take your first step into a larger world. Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures arrives here at Star Wars Kids on November 30!|date=2020-02-25}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] stalks a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] convoy in his [[TIE Advanced x1]] [[starfighter]]. One of the [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] [[pilot]]s informs the commander that they are picking up a ship on their [[sensor]]s. The narrator explains that in a "[[The galaxy|galaxy]] of adventures," Darth Vader is a fearsome fighter pilot.

Darth Vader runs circles around the X-wings, shooting them down one by one. He then takes on the [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wings]] of [[Phoenix Squadron]], decimating the fighters one by one. The commander wonders how one fighter can best their entire squadron. Lord Vader then fires several [[proton torpedo]]es at the [[CR90 corvette]], destroying it and leaving flaming debris in his wake.
