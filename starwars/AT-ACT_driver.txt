'''AT-ACT drivers''' were [[Galactic Empire]] [[Imperial Army|army]] or [[Imperial Navy|navy]] units who [[pilot]]ed [[All Terrain Armored Cargo Transport]]s.<ref name="HasbroRapidFire" /> They wore [[Color|white]] [[Imperial combat assault tank pilot|Imperial tank pilot]] [[armor]],<ref name="BlackSeriesATACT">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|pack=Imperial AT-ACT Driver|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/Imperial-AT-ACT-Driver-C1982-The-Black-Series.asp}}</ref> with some<ref name="Rogue One" /> drivers' breastplates featuring markings matching those found on [[Imperial combat assault tank commander]] armor,<ref name="HasbroRapidFire" /> along with a white [[coastal defender stormtrooper]] helmet that was adorned with grey and red markings, including a red [[Imperial crest]] located on the pilot's helmet. The drivers were issued either [[E-11 medium blaster rifle|E-11]]<ref name="BlackSeriesATACT" /> or [[E-22 reciprocating double-barreled blaster rifle]]s to carry as personal weapons. Despite their existence,<ref name="HasbroRapidFire" /> specialized AT-ACT driver divisions were not used due to the fact that the AT-ACT was not considered a battlefield assault vehicle.<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], AT-ACT drivers were stationed at the [[Imperial Center of Military Research|Imperial security complex]] on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date Note">This event takes place during ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''Rogue One'' in [[1 BBY]].</ref> the Rebel [[squad]] "[[Rogue One]]" infiltrated the Imperial facility to steal the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[Death Star plans|plans]] and kick-started the [[Battle of Scarif]]. During the battle, AT-ACT drivers used their [[walker]]s to engage the Rebel ground forces, initially proving effective and driving them back. However, the AT-ACTs were quickly destroyed when the Rebels received support from the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]], with [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron's]] [[T-65 X-wing starfighter]]s and [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]] making short work of the walkers. The security complex itself was later destroyed by the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] under orders from [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:AT-ACT_driver.png|thumb|left|200px|An AT-ACT driver]]
AT-ACT drivers were created and developed for the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|film]] ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. However, the unit first appeared as a 3-3/4" figure within [[Hasbro]]'s [[Star Wars: Rogue One (toy line)|''Rogue One'' toy line]], being included with the Rapid Fire Imperial AT-ACT toy (alongside figures of [[Jyn Erso]] and [[C2-B5]]) released in November 2016.<ref name="RebelscumRFATACT">{{Rebelscum|url=Rapid-Fire-Imperial-AT-ACT-Rogue-One-Hasbro.asp|text=Rapid Fire Imperial AT-ACT 3 3/4-Inch Vehicle}}</ref> The artwork on the Rapid Fire Imperial AT-ACT's packaging contains a discrepancy, depicting the AT-ACT driver's helmet with the red symbol found on the shoretrooper helmet, instead of the red Imperial crest found on the actual included figure's helmet and in the film.<ref name="HasbroRapidFire" />

The reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on the same day as the film, states that no specialized corps of pilots existed for the AT-ACT.<ref name="Visual Guide" />
