'''Concealed escape kits''' were specialized escape kits that could be concealed inside of the sole of bulky footwear. They typically contained [[slicewire]], a small pair of wirecutters, a miniature [[mem-stik]] containing a map of the area, a [[durasteel]] file, a thin, flexible [[monoknife]], and a tiny, single-use [[laser cutter]]. Concealed escape kits typically cost 450 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and were used by intelligence [[agent]]s to escape short-term confinements.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />

==Behind the scenes==
Concealed escape kits were first mentioned in ''[[Cyphers and Masks]]'',<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Accept Your Mission">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/8/16/accept-your-mission|text=Accept Your Mission}}</ref>
