'''Cresh Luck armor''', also called '''Cresh "Luck" armor''', manufactured by [[Creshaldyne Industries]], was a type of riot [[armor]] favored among [[Law enforcement agency|police]] and paramilitary units throughout [[the galaxy]]. It includes an armored chest piece and a [[helmet]] with a flip-down face shield. Enhanced with a short-range [[infrared]] [[sensor]] system, this armor could detect nearby threats within thirty [[meter]]s, providing the wearer an extra split [[second]] to react. This feature gave Cresh "Luck" armor its nickname. When activated, the infrared sensor array detected any mass over ten [[kilogram]]s moving faster than one meter per second, along with various other hazards defined by its programming.<ref name="Gadgets" />
