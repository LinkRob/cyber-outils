'''Amigast's Clan''' was a [[clan]] of [[Ugnaught]]s that lived in [[Cloud City]] on the [[planet]] [[Bespin]]. The group was led by [[Amigast]] and was able to escape the [[city]] when it was occupied by the [[Galactic Empire]] much earlier than the [[Lonaste's clan|clan]] led by fellow Ugnaught [[Lonaste]].<ref name="Bespin Escape" />
