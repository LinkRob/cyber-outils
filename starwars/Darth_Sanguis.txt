'''Darth Sanguis''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Sith Lord]] who, according to legend, traveled to [[Exegol]] seeking to learn the secrets of [[immortality]]. After achieving his goal, [[Darth]] Sanguis transformed into a creature over time. Eventually, Sanguis became a servant to [[Darth Noctyss]] when she arrived on Exegol, also seeking immortality. Noctyss sacrificed Sanguis in an immortality ritual, freeing him from a twisted life and dooming herself to a similar fate.

==Biography==
[[Darth]] Sanguis went off to the [[Unknown Regions]] in search of [[Immortality|eternal life]] and was not to be seen again for years, as no one knew his location.<ref name="A Life Immortal" />

[[Darth Noctyss]], also in search of immortality, made her way to [[Exegol]] where she found Sanguis, though decrepit and unrecognizable, and did not realize it was him. Sanguis became her servant<ref name="A Life Immortal" /> and [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]]<ref name="TDSPE" /> for a short time. The ritual found by Noctyss on Exegol required her to sacrifice a living being, and Sanguis was the only living thing the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] could find. She killed Sanguis and did receive eternal life, but in a very different way than expected. Noctyss became a creature very similar to Sanguis, and then realized Sanguis' fate. Noctyss lived the rest of her days as a deformed monster, alone and immortal, on Exegol.<ref name="A Life Immortal" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|As this story was one of the last to be added, and due to approaching printing deadlines, I had to proceed with the illustration without knowing whom or what the main characters were. What I did know was that this story took place on Exegol and was going to have a strong Dr. Frankenstein and Ygor vibe. At the time, I only had the trailer visuals to go off of. Inspired by a screenshot, I built up the scene and shrouded our Sith in a hood to hide their identity. With a bit of Force lightning to showcase the villain's power, I was able to increase the drama in the composition. The curved saber was added later, and if I am not mistaken, is specific to a particular Sith.|Grant Griffin|<ref name="Dark Legends Art">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-dark-legends-art|text=Get a First Look at New Art from ''Star Wars: Dark Legends'' - Exclusive}}</ref>}}
Darth Sanguis appeared in [[2020]] in "[[A Life Immortal]]," a story within the book ''[[Dark Legends]]'', written and illustrated by [[George Mann]] and [[Grant Griffin]], respectively.<ref name="A Life Immortal" />
