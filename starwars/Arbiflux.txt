{{Quote|You can't control ''anything'' in this galaxy&mdash;she ''knew'' that&mdash;so she buried us in a place so ''insignificant'' nothing would ever, ''ever'' change. I guess she was wrong about ''that'' too.|Chelli Aphra, on her mother Lona|<ref name="Aphra 34" />}}
'''Arbiflux''' was a [[forest]] [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] along the [[Veragi Trade Route]]. The future [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] and her mother [[Lona Aphra|Lona]] moved to the world after leaving her [[Korin Aphra|father]]. During that [[time]], the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Coalition for Progress]] manufactured the threat of [[Pirate|raider]] attacks, pushing the inhabitants to turn to Imperial support. Lona Aphra was [[Death|killed]] in one such attack, calling Galactic Empire forces in to rescue her daughter. With the ruse of attacks successful, the Empire occupied Arbiflux, industrializing the planet, ruining its environment. As an adult, Aphra returned to her home on Arbiflux, hiding from the Empire, but she had been tracked to the planet, and was forced to escape.

==Description==
{{Quote|You promised me a ''boring planet'' with a ''foul smell'' and that's exactly what I got. Except&hellip;now it's got bonus ''bad guys''.|Vulaada Klam, to Chelli Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 34" />}}
[[File:Arbiflux-Oru-SWT.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Arbiflux was found near the fringes of the Outer Rim]]
Abiflux was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Aphra 34" /> in the outer reaches of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Aphra 32" /> in a portion that intersected with the [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> Situated at the coordinates L-3 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> it lay along the [[Veragi Trade Route]] between the<ref name="Veragi" /> planet<ref name="Adventures 32">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=32|story=The Battle for Horizon Base}}</ref> [[Oru]] and the astronomical object [[Sernpidal]].<ref name="Galaxy Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> Arbiflux was covered in [[forest]]s, plains of dirt and [[grass]], as well as [[mountain]] ranges.<ref name="Aphra 32" /> The planet was orbited by at least four [[moon]]s,<ref name="Aphra 33" /> and its Type I [[atmosphere]]<ref name="Atmosphere" /> was breathable to [[human]]s,<ref name="Aphra 32" /> though it had an unpleasant smell. Rain and storms could also occur on Arbiflux.<ref name="Aphra 34" /> The planet was home to [[Species|creatures]] such as the [[nuna]] [[bird]], as well as the [[chatterplant]], a [[plant]] able to make sound that could be utilized as a warning alarm.<ref name="Aphra 32" />

==History==
===Lawless backwater===
{{Quote|You know, you should be thanking us&hellip;''you'' get to go back to civilization&mdash;and ''away'' from this wretched world. Things are going to get ''ugly'' around here. Your farm wasn't the ''first'' to get hit, you know.|An Imperial officer, to Chelli Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 35">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 35|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 35]]</ref>}}
[[File:Arbiflux-2016DoctorAphra32.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Arbiflux during the early Imperial Era.]]
In the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]], Arbiflux was an obscure world that was mostly uninhabited. [[Lona Aphra]], the [[Marriage|wife]] of the [[professor]] [[Korin Aphra]], feeling that her husband's work got in the way of their [[Aphra family|family]], took her daughter [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli]] from their [[Aphra family home|home]] on the [[Second Moon of Thrinittik]] and lived on Arbiflux,<ref name="Aphra 32" /> constructing a [[Lona Aphra's house|house]] and a [[farm]].<ref name="Aphra 34" /> Over the two years that they lived on Arbiflux, the younger Aphra came to dislike living on the planet, which she saw as simple and boring.<ref name="Aphra 32" /> The Imperial [[Coalition for Progress]], led by [[Minister]] [[Pitina Mar-Mas Voor|Pitina Voor]], wishing to force the [[Homeworld|natives]] of Arbiflux into requesting Imperial protection, created the threat of [[Pirate|raiders]], staging false attacks on various settlements.<ref name="Aphra 36">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 36|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 36]]</ref> One of the attacks took place at the Aphra farm, fatally wounding Lona Aphra.<ref name="Aphra 32" />

Chelli Aphra fought off the raiders using her mother's [[blaster]], but could not prevent her [[death]]. Before dying, Lona Aphra contacted the Galactic Empire, who sent a [[garrison]] to survey the area. The [[Stormtrooper|troopers]] discovered the young Aphra, who they rescued and sent home to her father.<ref name="Aphra 34" /> According to the [[Imperial officer]] who took care of Aphra, the planet's natives had formally asked the Empire to intervene.<ref name="Aphra 35" /> With the scheme devised by the Coalition for Progress successful, the Empire occupied the planet,<ref name="Aphra 36" /> claiming to have dealt with the raiders.<ref name="Aphra 34" />

===Under the Galactic Empire===
{{Quote|If this recording reaches you, Aphra&mdash;get ''out''. Get out now. We intercepted Imperial ''comms''. They tracked you to ''Arbiflux''.|[[Chief of Intelligence]] [[Airen Cracken]]|<ref name="Aphra 34" />}}
[[File:Arbiflux-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|250px|Arbiflux while under Imperial occupation.]]
Twenty years after originally moving to Arbiflux, [[Doctor]] Chelli Aphra returned, hiding out in her old home, accompanied by the orphan [[Vulaada Klam]], who Aphra had unofficially taken in as her ward. By that [[time]], Arbiflux's environment had been partially ruined by the occupation of the Empire, who had built [[City|cities]] and [[Star Destroyer]] construction facilities, filling the air with smog. However, the Empire knew Aphra was on Arbiflux, having tracked her<ref name="Aphra 34" /> after she stole the [[Farkiller]], an ancient [[Jedi]] weapon.<ref name="Aphra 32" />

The Empire sent several [[bounty hunter]]s to capture her, but one of the hunters, the [[Wookiee]] [[Krrsantan]], recognized Aphra, incapacitating the other hunters as she owed him a large amount of [[Galactic Credit Standard|money]]. Evading a [[platoon]] of stormtroopers, Aphra, along with Klam and Krrsantan, returned to her [[starship]], the [[Ark Angel II|''Ark Angel'']], leaving the planet. They were pursued by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], but managed to escape into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Aphra 34" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|As ''hideouts'' go, it's very ''you''.|Vulaada Klam, to Chelli Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 34" />}}
A number of farms and settlements existed on Arbiflux.<ref name="Aphra 35" /> The homestead constructed by Lona Aphra was located on the edge of one of the planet's forests, near several mountains,<ref name="Aphra 32" /> in between which the Empire would later construct a sprawling city.<ref name="Aphra 34" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|The ''natives'' have formally invited us to ''intervene''. There'll be some losses of ''personal freedom'', of course, but&hellip;well. One has to let go of the things that don't fit the big picture.|An Imperial officer, as the Empire begins to occupy Arbiflux|<ref name="Aphra 35" />}}
During the early Imperial Era, Arbiflux had a native population, as well as a number of settlers,<ref name="Aphra 35" /> at least two of whom were humans, Lona Aphra and her daughter.<ref name="Aphra 32" /> Despite this, the younger Aphra never saw any other [[Sentience|sentient]] beings on Arbiflux for the two years that they lived there. The Galactic Empire's eventual occupation brought more inhabitants to the planet.<ref name="Aphra 34" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arbiflux was first mentioned, albeit indirectly, in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Darth Vader (2015) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 10]], written by [[Kieron Gillen]]<ref name="Vader 10">[[Darth Vader (2015) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 10]]</ref> and published on [[October 7]], [[2015]].<ref name="Marvel Vader 10">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/51678/darth_vader_2015_10|text=Darth Vader (2015) #10}}</ref> It was not given a name until [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 32|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 32]], written by [[Simon Spurrier]]<ref name="Aphra 32" /> and published on [[May 8]], [[2019]],<ref name="Marvel Aphra 32">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/71451/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_32|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #32}}</ref> in which it appeared in a flashback sequence penciled by [[Caspar Wijngaard]].<ref name="Aphra 32" />
