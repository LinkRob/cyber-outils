{{Quote|You have a better option?"<br />"Jump into the pit and get it over with?|Kanan Jarrus and Garazeb Orrelios discuss the dangers of a 22 pickup|<ref name="Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref>}}
'''22 pickup''' was a term used to describe a maneuver in which a individual would hide within a cargo container, which would then be magnetically picked up by a [[starship]]. The [[Spectres|crew]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'' ran a 22 pickup during their [[Mission to free Wookiee prisoners|mission]] to rescue [[Wookiee]] [[prisoner]]s in the [[Spice mines of Kessel|Spice Mines]] of [[Kessel]]. While [[Kanan Jarrus]] held off [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] troops with a [[Kanan Jarrus' lightsaber|lightsaber]], [[Garazeb Orrelios]] and [[Sabine Wren]] led the Wookiee prisoners into a cargo container. That container was then picked up by the ''Ghost'', [[pilot]]ed by [[Hera Syndulla]], on which the crew and the prisoners successfully escaped Kessel.<ref name="Spark" />
