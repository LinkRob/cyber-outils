'''Etro Edthatt''' was a [[Duros/Legends|Duros]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] who lived during the era of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]].<ref name="MAGL" /> Known primarily from an account published in the periodical [[Galactic Gossip|''The Galactic Gossip'']], Etro and his wife, [[Droza Edthatt]], gained notoriety after reportedly being abducted by [[Human/Legends|Humans]] and taken to the planet they identified as "[[Earth/Legends|Urthha]]." The couple recounted their experiences adapting to human customs, such as social gatherings and local food, and their eventual escape using a device referred to as a "[[matter catalyst]]."<ref name="MAGL" />

==Biography==
{{Quote|They were quite happy to have us, actually. They took us to something called a 'party.' This is where a group of Humans dress in different uniforms and gather around various liquid canisters.|Etro Edthatt|<ref name="MAGL" />}}
===Honeymoon and abduction===
Etro Edthatt was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Duros/Legends|Duros]],<ref name="MAGL" /> a [[Species/Legends|species]] of [[Color/Legends|blue-green]] skinned, [[Nose/Legends|noseless]] [[Humanoid/Legends|humanoids]].<ref name="UAA">''[[Ultimate Alien Anthology]]''</ref> After he [[Marriage/Legends|married]] fellow Duros [[Droza Edthatt]], the couple went on a honeymoon. Upon their return, the newlyweds, while still mid-digestion, were battered with metal sticks by [[Unidentified Human abductors|human]] [[Human/Legends|beings]], forced out of their [[Transport/Legends|transport]] and loaded into [[Unidentified transport|another transport]] that Etro Edthatt thought resembled an "upside down" [[re-humidifier]]. The humans then blinded the Duros and levitated them to their [[Homeworld/Legends|homeworld]], which the couple believed was a planet called "[[Earth/Legends|Urthha]]." Etro and Droza were stranded there for over four [[Star/Legends|solar]] [[Time/Legends|periods]].<ref name="MAGL" />

[[File:Blender.png|thumb|100px|The Edthatts escaped Urthha using a matter catalyst, known locally as a "blender."]]
Arriving on the planet, the Duros couple adjusted their portable simultaneous translation devices known as [[aural synchronizer]]s, since neither of them spoke the local language, in order to readily comprehend human speech. Confused, they were then taken to a dinner party, where they were pinched and looked at as the guests laughed. [[Unidentified Human (Urthha)|One]] human insisted in showing Etro his collection of images of naked females, which surprised the [[Alien/Legends|alien]].<ref name="MAGL" />

===Life on Urthha===
The Edthatts were then led to a table, until then an unknown object to them, where they were forced to sit and consume [[Foodstuff/Legends|food]]. To the Edthatts, human cuisine resembled stationary products from the [[Vretha system/Legends|Vretha system]]<ref name="MAGL" /><ref name="Vrethan">{{Planetnamia|Vrethan stationary products}}</ref>&mdash;a [[The galaxy/Legends|galactic]] location within the [[Mid Rim/Legends|Mid Rim's]] [[Hollan sector/Legends|Hollan sector]].<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> Out of habit, Edthatt started to stamp the food, angering the [[Unidentified female Human (Urthha)|hostess]] in the process and making her leave in what they believed to be a "hufff."<!-- sic --> When trying to eat a [[Potato/Legends|potato]], Etro Edthatt experienced an extreme physiological reaction, breaking out in [[collard lesions]], a condition that affected Duros. The sudden reaction scared away the humans, including the home owners, who ran off in fear and did not return to their house for the rest of the period the aliens stayed there. With the domicile empty, the Duros couple used the privacy to go to the freezer, seeing as it was their honeymoon.<ref name="MAGL" />

The Edthatts spent their remaining solar periods on Urrtha enjoying themselves, practicing activities such as bouncing on antennas, reading [[Vegetable/Legends|vegetables]] and choking [[Book/Legends|books]] and [[Painting/Legends|paintings]], which, unlike the ones from their homeworld, put up little resistance. They bemoaned, however, the lack of suitable objects to scratch. When one day [[Unidentified Human (Urthha)|a young Human]] tried to throw folded piles of [[Paper/Legends|paper]] at the exterior of the residence, he saw the couple and helped them leave the planet by means of a [[matter catalyst]], a device large enough to fit two grown Duros and known locally as a "blender." After the child beat them in what they believed was a "professional-level" game of [[Twister (game)|Twister]], the kid put them in the device and sent them on their way home.<ref name="MAGL" />

===Return and recollection===
The married couple later recalled the experience with joy in a thorough article published in an edition of the news periodical [[Galactic Gossip|''The Galactic Gossip'']], as part of an interview conducted by staff writer [[Trebor Uarrac]]. The article included a photo of the couple, as well as an image of the technological device that ensured their safe return, a seven-speed vortex-equipped matter catalyst.<ref name="MAGL" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|One Human kept showing me his collection of female naked imagery. They were very interesting. Not at all what you would expect.|Etro Edthatt|<ref name="MAGL" />}}
Although the Duros were famed for being a species with a strong interest on [[Realspace/Legends|space]] travel and for being one of the first civilizations to develop the technology to do so,<ref name="Galaxy Guide 4">''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]''</ref> Etro Edthatt and his wife, until the abduction, were not completely sure life developed elsewhere and had never before seen humans,<ref name="MAGL" /> the most populous species in the galaxy.<ref name="NEGAS">''[[The New Essential Guide to Alien Species]]''</ref> Additionally, he could not speak Urthha's language, a high-pitched form of verbal communication that, at first, reminded him of [[Mocking Trees]], and which required Etro and his wife to wear portable aural synchronizers to understand and communicate with other individuals. He struggled to grasp many cultural concepts and objects common to humans such as "party," "table," "potato" and pieces of technology like matter catalysts.<ref name="MAGL" />

Initially frightened with the kidnapping and the profound cultural shock, Edthatt over time developed a positive view of the whole affair, enjoying his time on Urthha. He displayed curiosity and surprise towards human anatomy, particularly females, constantly maintaining an inquisitive stance, and often trying out new experiences, although this exploratory behavior was the cause for his collard lesion breakout on one occasion. Edthatt only lamented the fact that he and Droza could not find suitable objects to scratch. Overall, the events that transpired during his honeymoon served to confirm his hypothesis that life, especially [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] life, indeed existed beyond his homeworld, although it was not necessarily very intelligent.<ref name="MAGL" />

As a Duros, Edthatt possessed two [[Eye/Legends|eyes]], no [[Hair/Legends|hair]], and a long, thin and noseless head. He displayed an usual Duros trait upon having enjoyed telling the story about his trip, making use of his photographic memory.<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Duros.png|thumb|The original image from the set of ''Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope'']]
Etro Edthatt was featured in a two-page story titled "Aliens Kidnapped by Humans!," a [[Wikipedia:Gossip magazine|gossip magazine]] parody attributed to Trebor Uarrac of ''The Galactic Gossip''. The story was written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that combines creature designs and photographs from various projects of [[George Lucas]], creator of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, with a variety of original short fiction pieces written by Carrau.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is non-[[canon]]ical with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>

In the story, the Duros newlyweds are "[[Wikipedia:Alien abduction|abducted]] by human beings,"<ref name="MAGL" /> a common fiction trope that usually goes contrariwise.<ref name="tv tropes">{{WebCite|url=https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/AlienAbduction|text=Alien Abduction|work=[http://tvtropes.org TV Tropes]|archivedate=20200602152418}}</ref> A couple of references to [[Wikipedia:Earth|Earth]] are also made, such as when they mishear the name of the human homeworld as "Urthha" and when the couple is said to leave the planet after playing a game of [[Wikipedia:Twister (game)|Twister]] and by means of a "matter catalyst," pictured and referred to as the real-world home appliance known as [[Wikipedia:Blender|blender]].<ref name="MAGL" />

A picture of the Edthatts, originally a production photo from [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']],<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> is included in the segment, although the newlyweds are not individually identified in the image.<ref name="MAGL" /> The Duros in the original picture were canonically revealed to be [[Chachi De Maal/Legends|Chachi De Maal]] in the 1993 sourcebook ''[[Galaxy Guide 7: Mos Eisley]]''<ref name="GG7">''[[Galaxy Guide 7: Mos Eisley]]''</ref> and [[Ohwun De Maal/Legends|Ohwun De Maal]] in the [[2000]] article ''[[The University of Sanbra Guide to Intelligent Life: The Duros]]''.<ref name="sanbra">{{GamerCite|2|The University of Sanbra Guide to Intelligent Life: The Duros}}</ref>
