'''Bruce Boa''' ([[July 10]], [[1930s#1930|1930]]<ref name="SWYT 2020-07-09" /> &mdash; [[2004]]<ref name="Express" />) was a Canadian actor known for his performances in British films and television series, usually playing military or other authority figures. He portrayed [[General]] [[Carlist Rieekan]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. He passed away in [[2004]].<ref name="Express" />
