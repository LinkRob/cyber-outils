The '''Clone Commander Cody Character Expansion''' ('''SWQ72''') is an expansion to the asymmetrical two-player infantry-base wargame ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. An updated version of the earlier [[Clone Commander Cody Commander Expansion]], it was released on [[May 23]], [[2025]] as part of Atomic Mass Games' overhaul of the game, and includes miniatures of [[Cody]], [[Waxer]], and [[Boil]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Commander Cody joins the fight! SWQ72 brings Obi-Wan Kenobi’s trusted commander to the tabletop in Star Wars: Legion. Fighting all through the Clone Wars, Commander Cody and his clones proved themselves time and time again, becoming some of the finest troops for the Galactic Republic.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

''Joined by Waxer and Boil, this Character Expansion for the Galactic Republic provides tactics and abilities which improve vehicles and troops in combined attacks. Waxer and Boil enhance the capabilities of Clone Trooper units and their tactics. For Galactic Republic players, this will be a useful pack to have at their disposal.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

==Contents==
*3 Miniatures: [[Cody]],[[Waxer]], and [[Boil]]
