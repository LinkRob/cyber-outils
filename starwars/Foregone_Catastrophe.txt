The '''''Foregone Catastrophe''''' was a [[starship]] owned by [[General]] [[Abediah Viess]], [[Minister of Protection]] in the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization. Viess arrived in the vessel after [[Jedi Master]]s [[Avar Kriss]] and [[Porter Engle]] [[Skirmish above Seswenna|boarded]] the ''[[Cacophony]]'', the starship of [[Melis Shryke]], in order to prevent the [[Jedi]] from using Shryke's ship to escape the [[Occlusion Zone]]. Engle, however, used a [[drill ship]] to tear through the ''Foregone Catastrophe''. As the ship fell apart, Viess fought Engle and left him for [[Death|dead]]. By the time that more Nihil arrived to search the wreckage of the ''Foregone Catastrophe'', both Viess and Engle had disappeared.<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />
