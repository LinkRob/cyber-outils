'''FO-1103''' was a [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> who served as a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] for the [[First Order]] during the late [[New Republic Era]]. FO-1103 was present on the [[Finalizer|''Finalizer'']]'s stormtrooper training complex for an obstacle course and endurance test, competing for specializations and privileges alongside many other troopers. FN-1103 started the course alongside [[Finn|FN-2187]] and [[FO-7155]], running competitively. During the challenge, FO-1103 was knocked back by an [[Niila|instructor]]'s [[resonator mace]]. The race injured FO-1103's arm, and later she was supposed to fight FO-7155 for the right to become a squad leader– though she never appeared, causing FN-2187, her second for the fight, to think FO-7155 did something to her.<ref name="TLO" />
