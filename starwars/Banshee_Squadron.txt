'''Banshee Squadron''' was a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] [[squadron]] serving in the [[Imperial Navy|starfleet]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. In the immediate aftermath of the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]], the Banshees were attached to the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Solar Storm]]''. A that time, the squadron flew in the [[Battle of Phindar]], during which they lost two [[pilot]]s.<ref name="LCATZA" />

Immediately after debriefing, [[Bruiser (individual)|Bruiser]], [[Heiwei Akrone]], and two other Banshees attended the [[Zero Angle]], the [[cantina]] of the [[Bright Jewel Oversector Flight Base]]. They were still in the cantina, discussing with the members of [[Nashtah Squadron]], when [[Wing Commander]] [[Weller (commander)|Weller]] announced that the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]] had been [[Battle of Yavin|destroyed]], and that all current Army and Navy affiliations were rescinded.<ref name="LCATZA" />
