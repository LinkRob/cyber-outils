The '''Battle of Maraken''' took place<ref name="The Snare" /> in the vicinity of<ref name="Battlenamia" /> the [[Maraken system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> during the early stages of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]].<ref name="Twit" /> The [[Imperial officer]] [[Visler Aqorian Korda III]] lost his lower jaw to a [[Battle droid|combat droid]] during the battle, but was able to destroy the droid in turn. Consequently, he was fitted with a [[Cybernetics|robotic]] jaw lined with sharp metal [[Tooth|teeth]].<ref name="The Snare" />

{{Battle-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Maraken was mentioned on [[Cavan Scott]]'s young adult book ''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Snare]]''.<ref name="The Snare" />

According to the author, it did not take place during the [[Clone Wars]], but rather the early Imperial period.<ref name="Twit" />
