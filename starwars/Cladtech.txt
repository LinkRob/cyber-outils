'''Cladtech''' was an armor factory on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Gilvaanen]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The factory specialized in assembling the interior parts of [[stormtrooper armor]] and worked with [[Quelton Fabrication]], another armory company. Many of the company's [[Ithorian]] workers were opposed to the [[Galactic Empire]], and delayed the production of stormtrooper armor. The workers were supported by the factory's owner [[Mawdo Larrth]], who treated his employees gingerly. After the Imperial efficiency expert [[Count]] [[Denetrius Vidian]] executed Larrth for colluding with [[Thetis Quelton]] against the Empire, the Empire merged Cladtech and Quelton and brought them under the control of the [[Imperial Department of Military Research]].<ref name="Bottleneck" />
