'''Chata Hyoki''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Selkath]] from the [[Water|aquatic]] [[planet]] of [[Manaan]].<ref name="Databank" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], Hyoki, along with fellow [[bounty hunter]] [[Robonino]], was hired by [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to intimidate various [[senator]]s into changing their votes on a [[Republic military enhancement bill|Senate bill]]. Dooku later instructed them to [[assassin]]ate [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] but they were unsuccessful. Hyoki was arrested along with Robonino when they attacked [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] at the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]].<ref name="Pursuit of Peace" />
