'''Aut-O''' was an [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] who served in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] with the [[military rank]] of [[commander]]. In [[20 BBY]], he commanded a Separatist [[Aut-O's fleet|fleet]] from a [[Aut-O's flagship|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] that was used to protect an [[encryption module]] that was key to a major Separatist operation. As a result, Aut-O's [[fleet]] and [[flagship]] were [[Mission to steal a Separatist encryption module|infiltrated]] by a [[D-Squad|squad]] of [[Galactic Republic]] [[droid]]s who retrieved the module. Aut-O personally led some of his forces to intercept them, but he and his droids were all destroyed.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Sir, there was a temporary power loss on "C" deck.''"<br />"''Has the power been reestablished?''"<br />"''Yes. It was only a temporary fluctuation.''"<br />"''Hmm, curious.|A B1-series battle droid and Aut-O|<ref name="SW" />}}
[[File:Push-SWQ38.png|thumb|250px|left|Aut-O oversees a battle from high on a cliff.]]
Prior to, or early in [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">Aut-O perished during the events of the episode ''[[Secret Weapons]]'', which is confirmed to have taken place in [[20 BBY]]. Therefore this battle must have taking place prior to or earlier in 20 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> Aut-O oversaw a battle on an unknown arid world in which his forces, which included [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tanks]], [[B2-series super battle droid]]s and [[B1-series battle droid]]s, faced a group of [[clone trooper]]s and an [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]. While watching the battle from an elevated cliff, a pair of clones managed to reach Aut-O's position but were pushed back by two [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s.<ref name="SWQ38">{{NewLegion|set=Super Tactical Droid Commanders Unit Expansion|cardname=Push}}</ref>

[[File:Aut-0_Command_Bridge.jpg|thumb|right|275px|Aut-O on the bridge of his flagship]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Commander]] Aut-O<ref name="War Droids" /> commanded a [[Aut-O's fleet|fleet]] consisting of mainly [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyers]] and one [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]]. His [[Aut-O's flagship|flagship]] carried an [[encryption module]] that was needed to decipher a coded transmission [[General]] [[Grievous]] sent to his allies. Meanwhile, the [[D-Squad]] infiltrated Aut-O's command ship with a stolen [[D-Squad's Maxillipede shuttle|Confederate shuttle]], intending to steal the module. The [[D-Squad|droid squad]], led by [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]], got past the [[battle droid]] security, and got to [[C-Deck]], where the module was being held. After multiple energy fluctuations, Aut-O decided to investigate the phenomena. Taking several [[B1-series battle droid]]s and [[B2-series super battle droid]]s with him, Aut-O made his way to C-Deck, where he discovered D-Squad attempting to steal the encryption module.<ref name="SW" />

He first held [[R2-D2]] at gunpoint, but the plucky [[Astromech droid|astromech]] turned off the gravity, causing all the droids to float helplessly around. Aut-O's escort was completely destroyed by the Republic forces, while Aut-0 tried to stop R2 and to eliminate Colonel Gascon. In the process, Aut-O held tight onto R2's legs, trying to stop the [[droid]], but R2 burned and melted the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|super tactical droid's]] [[Limb|hands]] with his [[rocket booster]]s. Suddenly, Gascon jumped onto Aut-O's head, kicking the droid's [[Photoreceptor|optical sensors]]. Aut-O only defeated Gascon by shaking his head, but the pesky [[Zilkin]] regained strength and jumped back at Aut-O, who started firing on him. Aut-O was hit out of nowhere by [[WAC-47]] holding a droid leg. Aut-O struck back in retaliation and knocked down WAC, who was distracted by Gascon. With WAC out of the way, Aut-O continued to pull down R2, who had gotten to the module. Aut-O tried at the same [[time]] to pull down the [[Astromech droid|astromech]] and swat Gascon who was nearby.<ref name="SW" />

[[File:Aut-O_head.png|thumb|left|275px|Aut-O's decapitated head]]
In the end, WAC-47 reactivated the gravity. Aut-O fell, then crushed Gascon. Witnessing this, R2 purposely dropped onto Aut-O, beheading him. As a result, D-Squad took possession of the encryption module. Yet after Aut-O's [[Death|destruction]], he sent a distress signal that triggered an alarm. However, battle droids on patrol mistook D-Squad for crew members, allowing the squad to flee Aut-O's flagship.<ref name="SW" />

==Characteristics==
A ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid, Aut-O and stood 1.94 [[meter]]s tall<ref name="Super Tactical Droid Databank" /> and he<ref name="War Droids" /> had [[Color|yellow]] [[sensor]]s. Aut-O found it curious that his battle droids reported to him that there was a temporary power loss on C-Deck. After another energy disturbance on C-Deck, he decided to investigate the situation personally.<ref name="SW" /> 

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|Intruder alert. Intruder alert. Intruder alert.|Aut-O after being decapitated.|<ref name="SW" />}}
Aut-O had a built-in intruder alert. When R2-D2 landed on top of him, he decapitated Aut-O, which sent out a distress signal that triggered an intruder alert.<ref name="SW" /> Aut-O possessed armor, advanced strategic programming and was an effective leader. He was also programmed to resist interrogation by an enemy.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>

==Equipment==
Aut-O carried an [[E-5 blaster rifle]] as he led his forces though C-Deck to apprehend D-Squad.<ref name="SW" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aut-O first appeared in "[[Secret Weapons]]," the tenth episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|season 5]], and was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]], who also voiced all the [[clone trooper]]s in show.<ref name="SW" />
