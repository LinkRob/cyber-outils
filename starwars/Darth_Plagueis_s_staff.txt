The [[Muun/Legends|Muun]] [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith]] [[Lord/Legends|Lord]] [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Darth Plagueis]] owned a [[Staff/Legends|staff]].<ref name="T&B" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Plagueis.jpg|thumb|left| Darth Plagueis standing with his staff in ''Tag & Bink: Revenge of the Clone Menace'']]The staff originated from the comic ''[[Tag & Bink: Revenge of the Clone Menace]]'', a non-canon comic from [[2006]]. This comic also was the first piece of [[Star Wars]] media to feature Darth Plagueis.<ref name="T&B" /> 

Despite never canonically using a staff, numerous pieces of merchandise continue to portray Darth Plagueis with one. The staff was carried over into Plagueis's [[Star Wars Miniatures|miniatures]] figure and card from [[2009]], which displayed the staff and first introduced Plagueis's then as of yet unnamed [[Transpirator/Legends|transpirator]].<ref name="Miniatures Plagueis">''{{SWMiniCite|set=Jedi Academy|pack=Darth Plagueis}}''</ref> While the respirator was carried over into the [[Darth Plagueis (novel)|novel]] named after the Muun in [[2012]] and was identified as a transpirator, the staff was not.<ref name="DP">''[[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']]''</ref> In [[2013]] a 6 inch [[Star Wars: The Black Series|Black Series]] figure of Plagueis was released. The release featured [[Darth Plagueis's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] and a staff. The lightsaber could be attached to the top of the staff and be used as a [[Lightsaber pike/Legends|lightsaber pike]]. Again though, no canonical stories in the lore reflected these decisions.<ref name="Black Series Plagueis">''{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|text=''Star Wars: The Black Series'' (2013)|pack=#18: Darth Plagueis|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/darth-plagueis-18-black-series-hasbro.asp}}''</ref>
