'''Crystalline vertex''' was a type of valuable [[Color|dark green]] [[gemstone]] that could be used as [[currency]] when cut and placed in the center of [[gold]] [[coin]]s. They could be cut into prism shapes, and were partially transparent.<ref name="Skills" /> The [[Ubrikkian Industries]] [[Oduran Model 25]] luxury [[landspeeder]] had crystalline vertex windscreens.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony">''[[Disciples of Harmony]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], crystalline vertex was first introduced in ''[[Disciples of Harmony]]'', a sourcebook for [[Star Wars Roleplaying]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2017]].<ref name="Disciples of Harmony" /> Within the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, crystalline vertex first appeared in the [[1979]] novel ''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]'' written by [[Brian Daley]].<ref name="Stars' End">''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]''</ref>
