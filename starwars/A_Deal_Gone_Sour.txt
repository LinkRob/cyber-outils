"'''A Deal Gone Sour'''" was an RPG source article written by [[Chuck Sperati]] in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 4]]. It was an adventure in which the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] sends characters to [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] to find the [[R2-series astromech droid/Legends|R2-series astromech droid]] [[R2-Z1]].
