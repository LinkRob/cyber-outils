In the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Rise" /> between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Quellor sector]]'s [[purse world]]s were '''blockaded''' by the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[2nd Fleet]]. Suffering from the [[blockade]], the [[Trade Federation]]'s enormous [[Quellor sector fleet|home defense fleet]] was deployed by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The [[fleet]] countered the 2nd Fleet, and with their [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class battleships]] and other [[droid]] control ships, hindered the Republic's operations in the [[sector]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The blockade of the Quellor sector was mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" />
