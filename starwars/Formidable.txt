The '''''Formidable''''' was an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] used by the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. In [[1 BBY]], during [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]]'s [[Hunt for Vader|hunt]] for [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the ''Formidable'' was under the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord's]] [[Commanding officer|command]]. Under Vader's control, the [[Star Destroyer]] arrived at the world [[Heva]] to engage Valance and his [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] of [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunters]], but it was ultimately destroyed in the skirmish by [[bot-drone]]s controlled by the [[slicer]] [[Chio Fain]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Scramble all fighters.|Darth Vader, to an Imperial general|<ref name="Target Vader 3" />}}
An [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]],<ref name="Head to Head" /> the ''Formidable''<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> measured 1,600 [[meter]]s in length<ref name="Length" /> and was equipped with [[engine]]s,<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> a [[solar ionization reactor]], a [[communications tower]],<ref name="Card Trader" /> two [[ISD-72x shield generator dome]]s,<ref name="ISD-72x" /> and a [[hangar]]. The [[Star Destroyer]] carried with it numerous [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], as well as [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|Darth Vader's personal TIE Advanced x1]].<ref name="Target Vader 3" />

==History==
{{Quote|We're all gonna die.|Wef, upon seeing the ''Formidable''|<ref name="Target Vader 3" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era]], the ''Formidable'' served in the [[Imperial Navy]], under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]] led a team of [[bounty hunter]]s on a [[hunt for Vader]],<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> although team member [[Dengar]] was secretly an informant to the [[Sith Lord]].<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> After Dengar alerted Vader that Valance and his [[Beilert Valance's crew|crew]] were on [[Heva]],<ref name="Target Vader 2">[[Target Vader 2|''Target Vader'' 2]]</ref> the [[Sith]] arrived at the world aboard the ''Formidable''.<ref name="Target Vader 3" />

[[File:FormidableDestroyed-TargetVader3.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The ''Formidable'' is destroyed over Heva.]]
Upon spotting the Star Destroyer in the [[Atmosphere|air]], Valance and members of his team engaged the [[Starship|vessel]] aboard their [[cruiser]], the ''[[Broken Wing]]''. Meanwhile, on the ''Formidable'', Vader ordered a [[Unidentified Imperial general (Formidable)|general]] to deploy [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Starfighter|fighters]] to engage the enemy ship, before departing himself to join the skirmish in his personal [[TIE Advanced x1]]. During the fight, Valance's [[slicer]], [[Chio Fain]], took control of a group of [[bot-drone]]s&mdash;which the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] had originally intended to use but abandoned due to their unstable technology&mdash;that the bounty hunters had acquired from the [[arms dealer]] [[Wef]]. Fain had the bot-drones ram into the ''Formidable''{{'s}} [[conning tower]], severing it, [[Death|killing]] the Imperial [[general]], and sending the vessel crashing into the ground below.<ref name="Target Vader 3" />

Following the skirmish, another Star Destroyer, under the command of General [[Gollin]], arrived at Heva. An [[Unidentified Imperial captain (Gollin)|Imperial captain]] aboard the vessel reported the ''Formidable''{{'s}} destruction to the general, although she was already aware of the event.<ref name="Target Vader 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Formidable'' first appeared in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Target Vader 3|third issue]] of the [[2019]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]'', which was written by [[Robbie Thompson]], illustrated by [[Stefano Landini]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> on [[September 25]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel-TV3">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/78926/star_wars_target_vader_2019_3|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #3}}</ref> It was identified by name in the [[Target Vader 4|fourth issue]], which was also written by Thompson<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> and published on [[October 9]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel-TV4">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/82434/star_wars_target_vader_2019_4|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #4}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Formidable/Legends|''Formidable'']] was first mentioned in [[Daniel Wallace]]'s [[2014]] reference book, ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide]]''.<ref name="Handbook">''[[Star Wars: Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide]]''</ref>
