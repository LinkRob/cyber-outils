'''Frank Rinella''' is a [[Wikipedia:Foley (film production)|foley]] artist that worked on multiple ''Star Wars'' projects including ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' and ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. Rinella attended the University of Rhode Island and earned a Bachelor of Science in Secondary Education and Literature from Boston University.<ref name="FrankLinked" />
