'''B'etoni''' was an individual who was [[Padawan|apprenticed]] to the [[Twi'lek]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Skia Ro]] at some point by<ref name="Revenge" /> approximately [[180 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{JediAcademyDates|7}}</ref> Eventually, she decided to abandon her [[Jedi training]], and, when Ro was telling her new apprentice, [[Christina Starspeeder]], about her previous apprentices, she noted that B'etoni was pursuing the career of a [[senator]]<ref name="Revenge" /> of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
B'etoni was mentioned in [[Jedi Academy: Revenge of the Sis|''Revenge of the Sis'']], the [[2019]] seventh entry in the ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Academy]]'' [[Canon|non-canon]] young-reader book series. The book was written by [[Jarrett J. Krosoczka]] and [[Amy Ignatow]].<ref name="Revenge" />
