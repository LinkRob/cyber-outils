'''Darth Vader's TIE Advanced''' was a modified [[TIE Advanced x1]] prototype [[starfighter]] personally employed by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].

==Description==
[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[TIE Advanced x1]] [[starfighter]] was a prototype of the [[TIE series|TIE fighter series]] Advanced series manufactured by [[Sienar Fleet Systems]].<ref name="Ultimate" /> It was used by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Vader during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] in many battles throughout the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="A New Hope" />

The fighter was equipped with a [[deflector shield generator]], a [[life support]] system, and a [[hyperdrive]]; unlike [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE/LN fighters]] and other members of the TIE Line.<ref name="Ships Galaxy" /> Its armament was limited to a pair of twin fixed-mount [[laser cannon]]s, and it was capable of firing [[cluster missile]]s. The interior of the TIE was modified to Vader's specifications, and was customized to work with [[Darth Vader's armor|Vader's armor]].<ref name="Ultimate" />

==History==
{{Quote|I knew my clock was ticking the second you stepped off your TIE fighter.|Chelli Lona Aphra submits to Vader to end her life if he so chooses|<ref name="Darth Vader 4">[[Darth Vader (2015) 4|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 4]]</ref>}}
At one point the starfighter had incurred damage. Efforts were made to repair the starfighter onboard [[Darth Vader's Venator-class Star Destroyer|Darth Vader's flagship Star Destroyer]], but unfortunately, the repairs proved to be unsuccessful. The [[Unidentified Imperial officer (Darth Vader's Venator-class Star Destroyer)|Imperial officer]] responsible for reporting the problem to Vader met an unfortunate demise due their perceived lack of determination in the repair attempts.<ref name="No Good Deed">{{StoryCite|story=No Good Deed...|book=Darth Vader (2017) 1}}</ref>

During the [[Siege of Lothal]], Vader utilized his starfighter during his [[Attack on Phoenix Squadron (4 BBY)|attack]] on [[Phoenix Cell|Phoenix Squadron]] in [[4 BBY]], after tracking a stolen [[Sentinel-class landing craft|shuttle]] back to the fleet. In the aftermath of his solo attack, Vader managed to destroy much of the fleet, including six [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wing starfighters]] and the [[Phoenix Home|command ship]].<ref name="Siege Lothal" />

[[File:Death_star_trench.png|thumb|left|220x220px|Vader pursuing Rebel fighters in the Death Star trench.]]
The ship also saw action during the [[Battle of Yavin]]. During the battle, it was escorted by two handpicked Imperial [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilots]] flying standard TIE/ln space superiority starfighters. After destroying many of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] starfighters, it spun out of control when one of Vader's [[Black Squadron (Galactic Empire)|Black Squadron]] wingmen collided with it in attempting to avoid cannon fire from the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. The [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] was destroyed by [[Luke Skywalker]] soon afterward.<ref name="A New Hope" />

<div style="float:right">[[File:ImperialPirateSkirmish-2015DarthVader2.jpg|thumb|270px|Vader, in his TIE Advanced, dogfights the pirate corvette]]</div>
After Vader's defeat at [[Yavin]], and his [[Assault on Cymoon|subsequent failure]] on [[Cymoon 1]], Vader was demoted by his [[Sith Master]], and placed under the [[Commander|command]] of [[Grand General]] [[Cassio Tagge]].<ref name="Darth Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2015) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 1]]</ref> Vader submitted to his punishment, and soon, after a layover on [[Tatooine]], Vader met Tagge aboard his [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|Super Star Destroyer]] [[Liberty's Misrule|''Annihilator'']]. Soon thereafter, Vader boarded the fighter and went to the aid of an [[CZ-246|Imperial shuttle]] who was [[Skirmish over an unidentified green planet|under attack]] from [[Crymorah-backed pirates|pirates]]. He arrived in the fray just as the [[Unidentified shuttle pilot|pilot of the shuttle]] [[Death|blew up]] the [[shuttle]] to prevent the pirates from stealing their cargo. Vader fired on the [[pirate]] ship, a [[Droid|robotic]] [[CR90 corvette]], destroying a [[Proton torpedo|torpedo]]. He then ordered his two wingman, [[Black Two|Black 2]] and [[Black Three|3]], to follow his lead in destroying the now fleeing corvette. Vader was able to evade the pirate ship's fire long enough to disable the corvette's engines using the twin laser cannons. Vader then returned to the ''Annihilator'', tracked the pirates back to their [[Unidentified pirate space station|base of origin]] and [[Attack on an unidentified pirate base|destroyed it]].<ref name="Darth Vader 2" />

Vader soon left Tagge's company, and went in search of a rogue [[archaeologist]], [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]]. Vader used the TIE Advanced to travel to several different worlds to track the doctor down, before discovering that Aphra was on [[Quarantine World III]] in [[Kallidahin Space]]. Vader and the TIE then traveled to the quarantined world and touched-down in one of its [[hangar]]s. However, [[Utani Xane]], Quarantine World III's [[Curator]], was alerted to Vader's presence by one of her [[B2-series super battle droid]]s who had noticed the Dark Lord's arrival. Vader quickly dispatched Xane and her [[battle droid]]s, and requested Aphra join him.<ref name="Darth Vader 3">[[Darth Vader (2015) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 3]]</ref>

[[File:ArkAngel-2015DarthVader3.jpg|thumb|left|200px|The TIE Advanced docked with the ''Ark Angel'']]
She agreed, and Vader took the fighter, and docked with the doctor's ship: the [[Ark Angel II|''Ark Angel'']]. The two discussed Vader's plans, and eventually made way to the planet [[Geonosis]] in the [[Arkanis sector]] to [[Secret mission to Geonosis|steal a]] droid [[factory]] to build an army of unquestionably loyal battle droids for the Dark Lord's personal use.<ref name="Darth Vader 4" />

The ''Ark Angel'', with the TIE Advanced still attached, traveled through hyperspace to Geonosis, where Vader, Aphra, and new recruits [[BT-1]] and [[0-0-0]]&mdash;a pair of specialized [[assassin droid]]s&mdash;were dropped down to the [[desert]] surface of Geonosis. Not long after, the ''Ark Angel'', on Aphra's orders barraged the location of a [[homing beacon]] she had placed, blowing a massive hole in the ceiling of a [[Karina's lair|lair]] where the factory was located so [[Naboo Royal Starship|Vader's J-type 327 Nubian]] could extract the factory. Later, the team returned to the ''Ark Angel'', with the TIE Advanced still docked, and created two [[platoon]]s of [[BX-series droid commando]]s to [[Skirmish in Cylo's research base|storm]] Doctor [[Cylo]]'s [[Cylo's base|research base]]; to eliminate Vader's rivals for the Emperor's favor.<ref name="Darth Vader 5">[[Darth Vader (2015) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 5]]</ref>

During the [[Battle of Vrogas Vas|battle]], Vader engaged the [[X-wing starfighter]]s, and managed to take out both [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]] and [[Yellow Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Yellow Squadron]], as he lost contact with Aphra. Vader was then confronted by Luke, who was flying as [[Red Five]], and flew his X-wing into Vader's starfighter, hoping to kill him. During the collision, their [[stabilizer]]s were damaged prompting their craft to crash on the planet's surface.<ref name="VD1">[[Vader Down 1|''Vader Down'' 1]]</ref>

During [[Crimson Dawn]]'s [[war against the Sith]], Vader flew his Advanced in a [[skirmish on Calior]] against rogue Imperial agents. [[Chief Regional Auditor]] [[Cygnus Anton]], a servant of the Empire who had secretly joined Crimson Dawn, was killed when Vader destroyed his [[All Terrain Scout Transport]] with his Advanced's lasers.<ref name="Vader 18">[[Darth Vader (2020) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 18]]</ref>
