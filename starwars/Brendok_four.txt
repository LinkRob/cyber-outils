The '''Brendok four'''<ref name="Challenges">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-jedi-order|text=How ''The Acolyte'' Challenges How We See Some Members of the Jedi}}</ref> was a team consisting of four [[Jedi]]: [[Jedi Master|Masters]] [[Kelnacca]], [[Sol]], [[Indara]], and her [[Padawan]] [[Torbin]]. They were stationed on [[Brendok]]<ref name="Choice" /> in [[148 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> about a [[Standard century|century]] after the [[Emergences]] which had seemingly left the [[planet]] lifeless. However, they were dispatched to the planet to discover how it had seemingly returned to life.<ref name="Choice" />
