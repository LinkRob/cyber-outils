The '''Commissars of Grizmallt''' were a shipbuilding organisation loyal to the [[Galactic Republic]] that shared their name<ref name="CV New">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> with the [[planet]] [[Grizmallt]].<ref name="Naboo">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/crisis-on-naboo-episode-guide|text="Crisis on Naboo" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> Following the Republic's use of vessels manufactured secretly in the [[Kuat shipyards|shipyards]] of [[Kuat Drive Yards]] during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]<ref name="CV New" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Commissars ordered countless industrial [[Spy|spies]] and [[starship]] designers to reduce their competitor's competitive lead for the Republic's new contracts as the [[Clone Wars]] began. The [[Arch-Provost]] of [[Rendili]]&ndash;another competitor to the Commissars&ndash;did similar, and due to Rendili's extensive system of smaller [[shipyard]]s across the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid]] and [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], they had a strong advantage with the potential to grant them temporary ascendancy.<ref name="CV New" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Commissars of Grizmallt were first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> from the [[2002]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', which was written by [[Curtis Saxton]] and marked the [[Commissars of Grizmallt/Legends|Commissars']] first mention in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Cross-Sections">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
