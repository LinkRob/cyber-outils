The '''Alderaanian delegation''' was a group of [[diplomat]]s that represented the [[planet]] of [[Alderaan]] in the [[Galactic Senate|Congress]] of the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="VE" /> and later in the [[Imperial Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="ANH" />

Included in the delegation was [[Bail Prestor Organa]], [[Bail Antilles|Agrippa Aldrete,]] and later [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo]].
