'''Balaans''' was a two-player card game that lent itself to [[gambling]]. Around [[18 BBY]], balaans was played by the patrons of a [[Unidentified cantina (Lau)|cantina]] on the [[planet]] [[Lau]]. The disguised [[Cloning|clone]] [[Omega]] partook to the game, gambling in hopes of earning herself quick [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and fleeing the planet along with her [[lurca hound]] [[Batcher]] and fellow clone [[Crosshair]].

==Cards==
A balaans deck had 30 cards in total.<ref name="Deck details">{{SW|url=a-different-approach-concept-art-gallery-star-wars-the-bad-batch|text="A Different Approach" Concept Art Gallery &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|Slide 8-12}}</ref> The deck consisted of five [[Color|blue]] and five red cards,<ref name="Faulty Trivia">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/a-different-approach-episode-guide|text="A Different Approach" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> as well as ten "spell" cards,<ref name="Deck details" /> consisting of two "destroy," two "deflect," two "buff," two "steal," and four "block" cards.<ref name="Faulty Trivia" /> The spell cards had their own symbol in the top corner of the card, as well as the card name in [[Aurebesh]] text. The spell cards also featured various artwork.<ref name="Deck details" />

==Gameplay==
Balaans was a two-player game, with each player having their own draw pile that would be ''dealt'' to them and which they would draw from. Each player maintained a hand of several cards and laid out cards in five face-up stacks. Players won by having the winning hand face down and revealed.<ref name="A Different Approach" /> The [[Three Eastern Stars]] was a nearly unbeatable hand.<ref name="Faulty Trivia" />

==History==
During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="TBBS3 Date">{{TBBDate|304}}</ref> while on the run from the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[planet]] [[Lau]], the [[Cloning|clones]] [[Omega]] and [[Crosshair]], along with their [[lurca hound]] [[Batcher]], entered a [[Unidentified cantina (Lau)|cantina]] with the hopes of earning enough [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to bribe a [[Unidentified ticketing agent|ticketing agent]] for passage offworld. To secure funds, Omega played balaans against a [[Unidentified Trandoshan (Lau)|Trandoshan]], winning several hands against them. When the local [[Imperial officer]], [[Mann]], faced off against Omega, he was overconfident in his chances until Omega laid down the Three Eastern Stars, taking twenty thousand credits off of him.<ref name="A Different Approach" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The card game came out of several conversations and Andre Kirk, our art director, and one of his designers helped build a real card game with real rules. We play-tested it. It's pretty fun. It's actually really cool. It's kind of like a combination of Uno and Magic the Gathering.|Brad Rau|<ref name="Finale Interview">{{SW|url=news/the-bad-batch-series-end-interview|text=Brad Rau and Jennifer Corbett Look Back on ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch''}}</ref>}}
Balaans was a game designed for "[[A Different Approach]]," the fourth episode of the [[The Bad Batch Season 3|third season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''.<ref name="A Different Approach" /> The production team designed a complete rule set for the game,<ref name="Slide 4">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/a-different-approach-episode-guide|text="A Different Approach" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> with supervising director [[Brad Rau]] describing the game as a cross between [[Wikipedia:Uno (card game)|Uno]] and [[Wikipedia:Magic: The Gathering|''Magic: The Gathering'']]<ref name="Finale Interview" /> and even play-tested several rounds of it.<ref name="Slide 4" />
