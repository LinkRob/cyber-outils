'''Derek Arnold''' is a puppeteer and actor who worked as a creature and droid performer in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. He also portrayed [[Lanever Villecham]], the [[Luggabeast]] (along with [[Tom Wilton]]), and [[Vober Dand]] in ''The Force Awakens'', and [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek|Pao]] and the [[Bor Gullet]] in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|anthology film]], ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. Arnold returned to the screen to play a young [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] in the [[Day (episode)|fourth episode]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''.
