'''Free Comic Book Day 2002 Special''' is a one-shot issue of the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars Tales]]''. It was published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] in [[May]] [[2002]] as a [[Free Comic Book Day]] title. It features the story "[[A Jedi's Weapon]]," which was reprinted in [[Star Wars Tales 12|''Star Wars Tales'' 12]] the following month.
