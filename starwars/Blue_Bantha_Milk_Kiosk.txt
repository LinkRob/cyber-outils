The '''Blue Bantha Milk Kiosk''' was a small drink vendor found within the [[Dune Sea Outpost]] battlefield of the [[Grand Arena]], utilized by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. It was owned by the [[Blue Bantha (milk distributor)|Blue Bantha]] [[milk]] distributor, who also operated the much larger [[Blue Bantha Refreshments]] milk bar found in the [[Mos Espa Waystation]] battlefield.<ref name="Hunters" />

They were staffed by at least one specially equipped, [[Color|blue]] and white&ndash;clad [[R5-series astromech droid]]. The Blue Bantha Milk Kiosk's menu included both [[Blue milk|blue]] and [[green milk]] for four [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] each, as well as an item simply called "[[Aurebesh]]" listed for fifteen credits.<ref name="Hunters" />
