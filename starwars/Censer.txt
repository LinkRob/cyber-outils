A '''censer''',<ref name="ROUVG" /> or '''incense burner''',<ref name="CL" /> was a small vessel used to carry burning [[incense]]. Some members of the [[Brotherhood of the Beatific Countenance]], such as [[Toshdor Ni]], carried staff-mounted censers filled with [[Fire|burning]] [[Qatameric incense]] whose smell permeated through their heavy [[robe]]s and masked their natural [[Lorrdian]] scent.<ref name="ROUVG" /> The ancient [[Sith]] also used censers such as the [[Sith Chalice]] during their [[meditation]] rituals.<ref name="CL" />
