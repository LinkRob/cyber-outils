'''Fabrin''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="BMF72" /> located in the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Circarpous sector]], within the [[Fabrin system]]. It was situated in grid square O-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> in [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> Fabrin was the headquarters of the company [[Fabritech]], a leading manufacturer of [[sensor]] and communications devices.<ref name="BMF72" /> By [[34 ABY]], Fabrin was defended by the [[Resistance]]'s [[Cobalt Squadron]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' establishes that the [[Resistance]]'s [[Cobalt Squadron]] defended Fabrin prior to the [[Battle of D'Qar]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Fabrin was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 72|seventy-second issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine series,<ref name="BMF72" /> published around [[May 18]], [[2016]].<ref name="Date">{{BMFdates|72}}</ref> The [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="FDCR">[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny Core Rulebook|''Star Wars: Force and Destiny'' Core Rulebook]]</ref> [[Fabrin/Legends|Fabrin]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in [[W. Haden Blackman]]'s [[2004]] reference book, ''[[The New Essential Guide to Weapons and Technology]]''.<ref name="NEGTW">''[[The New Essential Guide to Weapons and Technology]]''</ref>
