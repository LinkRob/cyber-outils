==Events==
*The ''[[Radiant VII]]'' enters the service of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Diplomatic Corps|Diplomatic Corps]].<ref name="Radiant VII">According to ''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]'', when the ''[[Radiant VII]]'' was dispatched to [[Naboo]] during the [[Trade Federation]] [[blockade]] of the [[planet]], it was a veteran of 34 [[Standard year|years]] in the [[Republic Diplomatic Corps]]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states that the Trade Federation's [[Invasion of Naboo]] took place in [[32 BBY]], the ''Radiant VII''{{'s}} service with the Republic Diplomatic Corps began in 66 BBY.</ref>
*The ''[[Libertine]]'' is constructed by [[Guild d'Lanseaux]].<ref name="Libertine" />
*[[Shmi Skywalker Lars|Shmi Skywalker]] and [[Skywalker family|her family]] are [[Slavery|enslaved]] by [[pirate]]s.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Births==
