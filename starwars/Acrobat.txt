An '''acrobat''' was a type of [[Entertainer|performer]]. [[Preigo's Traveling World of Wonder]] consisted of many [[Twi'lek]] acrobats.<ref name="DB Preigo">{{Databank|preigo|Preigo}}</ref> A group of [[Jedi]] [[youngling]]s consisting of [[Petro]], [[Katooni]], [[Byph]], [[Zatt]], and [[Gungi]] pretended to be acrobats in order to join the [[circus]] while.<ref name="Bound for Rescue">{{TCW|Bound for Rescue}}</ref>
