'''Dante Basco''' (born [[August 29]], [[1975]])<ref name="SWYT 27-08-2020" /> is an actor who provides the voice for [[Jai Kell]] in ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episodes "[[Breaking Ranks]]"<ref name="Breaking Ranks">{{Rebels|Breaking Ranks}}</ref> and "[[The Occupation]]."<ref name="The Occupation">{{Rebels|The Occupation}}</ref> He has also served as narrator for all the episodes of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''.<ref name="Daily Dot">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dailydot.com/parsec/star-wars-galaxy-of-adventures|text=New 'Star Wars' animated shorts bring big movie moments to life|quote=Dante Basco (Hook, Avatar: The Last Airbender) is also on hand to do some narration.|archivedate=20181201020515}}</ref>
