The '''Alazmec''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]]. The [[Alazmec of Winsit]] were [[Sith cultist]]s who honored [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] by protecting the ruins of his [[Fortress Vader|castle]] and a nearby bog, [[Corvax Fen]]. [[Loriach]] was a Alazmec [[sniper]] who was a member in the [[Assassin's Guild]].<ref name="Vader 18">[[Darth Vader (2020) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 18]]</ref>
