'''Dung worms''' were edible animals on [[Tatooine]]. Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="BoBF Date">{{BoBFDate}}</ref> [[Peli Motto]] supplied dung worms for [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] to eat.<ref name="Name of Honor" />

==Behind the scenes==
Within the canonical ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Canon|continuity]], dung worms first appeared in "[[Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor]]," the seventh episode of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'', which was written by [[Jon Favreau]], directed by [[Robert Rodriguez]], and aired on [[Disney+]] on [[February 9]], [[2022]].<ref name="Name of Honor" />

[[Dung worm/Legends|Dung worms]] were originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (video game)|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'' video game]]''.<ref name="video game">[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (video game)|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'' video game]]</ref>
