'''Alakatha''' was a terrestrial astronomical object known for its [[beach]]es<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> that was located in the [[Alakatha system]] within the [[Var Hagen sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. It was situated in grid square M-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> while playing the game ''[[Who? What? Where?]]'' with other [[New Republic]] [[New Republic pilot|pilots]] aboard the [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate|frigate]] ''[[Hellion's Dare]]'', the [[pilot]] [[Rep Boy]] claimed that he wished to live until old age and [[Death|die]] of heatstroke on the beaches of Alakatha. However, Rep Boy's wish was not fulfilled, as he was killed shortly afterwards by the forces of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] during an [[Attack on the Hellion's Dare|attack]] on the ''Hellion's Dare''.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alakatha was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], a [[2019]] novel written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first volume in the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Alakatha/Legends|Alakatha]] was a resort [[Moon/Legends|moon]], and appeared in the novel ''[[I, Jedi]]'', written by [[Michael A. Stackpole]] and published in [[1998]].<ref name="I, Jedi">''[[I, Jedi]]''</ref>
