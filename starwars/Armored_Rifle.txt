The '''Armored Rifle''' was a modified [[blaster rifle]] utilized by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Firestorm Rifle|standard rifle]]. This [[blaster]] [[rifle]] featured a [[Color|silver]] and black frame, with blue and red decals, and sandy-colored weathering, resembling the [[armor]] worn by the [[Gen'Dai]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Durge]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Armored Rifle was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Imara Vex's Firestorm Rifle, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.20.0  on [[March 28]], [[2024]] during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Fortified Armor]]. This wrap could be unlocked by completing "Part 5" of the game's "Welcome to the Arena!" introductory Quest.<ref name="Hunters" />
