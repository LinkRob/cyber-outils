'''Daryl Mandryk''' is a [[Wikipedia:Canadian|Canadian]] video game concept artist based in [[Wikipedia:Vancouver, British Columbia|Vancouver]]. He is most notable for illustrating the covers of [[Knights of the Old Republic 39|''Knights of the Old Republic'' 39]] and [[Knights of the Old Republic 40|''Knights of the Old Republic'' 40]]. He also illustrated the paperback cover of ''[[Choices of One]]''.{{Mediacat|imagecat=images by Daryl Mandryk}}
