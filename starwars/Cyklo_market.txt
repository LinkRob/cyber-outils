The '''Cyklo market''' was a [[Slavery|slave]] market on the [[planet]] [[Chalcedon]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], the criminal [[Black Sun]] [[Unidentified Black Sun slavemonger|slavemonger]] traded the [[Smuggler's Guide]] to the [[Varcinius Auction and Artifact Collective]] there.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cyklo market was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and released in [[2018]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
