'''Ebullism''' was the profuse evaporation of one's bodily [[Water|moisture]] when exposed to the [[vacuum]] of [[Realspace|space]]. [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] suffered from it when she was flung into space after the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] was destroyed by the [[First Order]].<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
