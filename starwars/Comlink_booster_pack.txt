A '''comlink booster pack''' was a piece of technology worn on the backsides of most [[B1-series battle droid]]s. It varied in design and usage for various [[battle droid]]s, differing by B1 [[droid]] sub-models, the droids' military position and affiliation, and specific groups of droids.

==Description==
===Manufacture and design===
[[B1-series battle droid]]s with [[Color|rust-brown]] plating at the [[Geonosis primary droid foundry]]'s assembly lines were each installed with a [[comlink]] booster pack.<ref name="Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> The booster pack was a piece of technology that most B1-series [[droid]]s wore on the back of their torso.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

====Design differences====
The shape and appearance of the pack differed between those worn by B1-series droids produced for the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]] and B1 droids manufactured for the [[Separatist Droid Army]].<ref name="TPM" /><ref name="The Last Battle" /> Two antennae of differing length extended from a [[Trade Federation]] droid's booster pack when the battle droid was activated by a central [[computer]],<ref name="TPM" /> whereas some [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] droids' packs featured only one antenna.<ref name="Ambush" /> However, Separatist B1-series battle droids present at the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], which were manufactured by the [[Geonosian|natives]] of [[Geonosis]], had two antennae of differing length.<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref>

The booster pack was fit over twin railings on the backs of Separatist battle droids,<ref name="The Last Battle" /> and Federation droids' packs featured numerical markings identifying the droid wearing it.<ref name="USW" /> Whilst a battle droid without the pack holstered their standard [[E-5 blaster rifle]] on one of two railings installed on the back of their torso,<ref name="The Last Battle" /> a Federation battle droid equipped with the booster pack placed their [[blaster rifle]] alongside the pack,<ref name="TPM" /> with booster pack&ndash;equipped Separatist droids of the [[Agamar garrison]]&mdash;who remained on the [[planet]] [[Agamar]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] after crash-landing on the world during the [[Clone Wars]]&mdash;instead storing their blaster rifles at the back of the pack.<ref name="The Last Battle" />

==Usage==
The antenna was not extended for Separatist battle droids aboard the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of a [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]] participating at the [[Battle of Quell]].<ref name="Jedi Crash">{{TCW|Jedi Crash}}</ref> Some droids stationed aboard the bridges of the Confederacy ''Munificent''-class [[frigate]]s [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Cad Bane]] at the [[Battle of Devaron]] lacked the booster pack entirely.<ref name="Cargo of Doom">{{TCW|Cargo of Doom}}</ref>

Of the [[OOM-series battle droid]] line, some [[OOM command battle droid]]s wore a booster pack.<ref name="TPM" /> Some Separatist OOM command battle droids, such as [[224]], had a yellow, circular marking on their booster pack, although it lacked the numerical markings that identified the droid.<ref name="Ambush" /> [[OOM-9]], the Trade Federation field commander of the [[Invasion of Naboo|Battle of Naboo]] against the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Gungan]] population, lacked a comlink booster pack, although the OOM command battle droid had the pack's two antennae installed on the back of his torso.<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I really wanted to give the droids jetpacks because I thought, you know, they'd be really cool flying around. That idea didn't quite work for [writer and director] George [Lucas], so the jetpack just became your regular utility backpack.|Doug Chiang|<ref name="Evolution">{{SWYouTube|uYK1OXlm7Us|Doug Chiang: The Evolution of Star Wars Design - Designing Episode I Live Panel at SWCC 2019}}</ref>}}
Comlink booster packs evolved from [[Doug Chiang]]'s concept of B1-series battle droids wearing [[jetpack]]s while working for the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']],<ref name="Evolution" /> in which the booster packs first appeared.<ref name="TPM" />
