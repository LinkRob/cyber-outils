'''Blue Milk Cooler''' was an [[alcohol]]ic beverage sold at the [[Milk Stand]] in [[Batuu]]'s [[Black Spire Outpost]]. The drink was based on the original non-alcoholic frozen [[blue milk]] drink available at the stand, which used [[milk]] farmed locally from [[Gender|female]] [[bantha]]s, but with the addition of [[rum]].<ref name="Milk Stand" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Milk and [[Green milk|Green Milk]] drinks were available at the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] Milk Stand in both [[Disneyland]] and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] on opening day, but only the Hollywood Studios version included alcoholic "cooler" variations of the beverages. The menu's description of the Blue Milk Cooler reads, "Plant-based blend of Coconut and Rice Milk with alluring fruity characteristics featuring Bacardi Rum."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/milk-stand/menus|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Milk Stand Menu}}</ref>
