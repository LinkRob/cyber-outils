The '''Echo Base Defenders Army Box''' ('''SWQ148''') is a starter set for the refreshed version of the ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' tabletop game by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. It is an updated version of the [[Echo Base Defenders Battle Force Starter Set]].<ref name="Asmodee" />

{{Game-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
===Online summary===
''Regrouping after the battle of Yavin, the Rebel Alliance established Echo Base on planet Hoth. Fortified and well armed, Echo Base was a defensible position that Hoth’s heavy snowfall gave the converted Wampa den natural camouflage.''

''However, the Empire’s Probe Droid located Echo Base. The Empire’s attack was relentless and Echo Base would not hold. To keep the rebellion alive, the brave Echo Base defenders pushed back against the empire for as long as they could to insure as many Rebels could escape as possible.''

''This Echo Base Defenders Special Edition Starter Set contains a 600-point Recon-level army, including a Special Edition alternate sculpt General Leia Organa.'' 

===Back blurb summary===
''Fortified and well armed, Echo Base was a defensible position that Hoth's heavy snowfall gave the converted Wampa den natural camouflage. However, the Empire's Probe Droids located Echo Base. The Empire's attack was relentless and Echo Base would not hold. To keep the rebellion alive, the brave Echo Base Defenders pushed back against the Empire for as long as they could to insure [sic] as many Rebels could escape as possible. This Special Edition Army Box includes enough units and miniatures to build a 600 point Recon army using the Echo Base Defenders Battle Force rules.''

==Contents==
*27 plastic miniatures
**1 [[C-3PO]]
**1 [[Leia Organa]]
**1 [[R2-D2]]
**2 [[Mark II EWHB-10|Mark II Medium Blaster]] troopers
**2 [[Tauntaun]] riders
**20 [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance]] veterans
*26 cards
