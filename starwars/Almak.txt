'''Almak''' was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Leffingite]] [[species]]<ref name="Alien Archive" /> and [[Almakian apple]]s.<ref name="BV" /> It was located in the [[Almak system]] within the [[Almak sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western" /> It was situated in grid square J-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

The Leffingite [[Magaloof]] was from Almak,<ref name="Alien Archive" /> although by [[22 BBY]],<ref name="AOTCDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}, in which [[Magaloof]] appears on [[Coruscant]], to [[22 BBY]].</ref> he was working as a criminal on the planet [[Coruscant]], where [[Almakian apple pie]] from Almak had made its way onto the menu of the [[Restaurant|eatery]] [[Dex's Diner]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], many Leffingites came under attack from the government and were forced to flee Almak. Almak was mentioned in an entry on Leffingites in an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]], which was restored, expanded, and displayed by the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="Alien Archive" /> at some point no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Magaloof's homeworld, Almak, is just random letters that sounded good together.|Gregory Walker|<ref name="Blog">{{Blog|narbflick|7|Magaloof: The Story Behind the Story}}</ref>}}
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Almak was first mentioned in an entry on Magaloof on the current [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]],<ref name="DB">{{Databank|magaloof|Magaloof}}</ref> which had been published by [[February 8]], [[2016]].<ref name="DBOldest">{{Databank|magaloof|Magaloof|oldversion=20160208191550}}</ref> The [[Almak/Legends|world]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity,<ref name="OldDB">{{DB|character|magaloof|Magaloof}}</ref> where it was created by [[Gregory Walker]], writing as "NarbFlick,"<ref name="DaD">{{Hyperspace|url=fans/hyperspace/source/dexdiner/index.html|text=Dining at Dex's|int=Dining at Dex's}}</ref> as part of the backstory for [[Magaloof/Legends|Magaloof]],<ref name="OldDB">{{DB|character|magaloof|Magaloof}}</ref>  submitted through the StarWars.com [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] feature "[[What's The Story?]]." The entry was added to the site's original [[Databank (original)|Databank]] on [[August 12]], [[2005]].<ref name="What's The Story">{{SWArchive|url=databank/updates/news20050812.html|text=Hyperspace Presents: What's the Story, Round 1}}</ref> Walker later claimed that Almak's name was simply derived from "random letters that sounded good together."<ref name="Blog" />
