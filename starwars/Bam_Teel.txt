==Behind the scenes==
Bam Teel first appeared in the 2015 [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'',<ref name="GoH" /> however Teel was unnamed until "Version Update [[May 23|5/23]]/[[2018]]" where Teel gave players tutorials and quests for the [[holotable]]s.<ref name="EA-Version Update 5/23/2018">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/170303/version-update-5-23-2018|text=Version Update 5/23/2018}}</ref> On [[April 27]], [[2026]], the cantina was completely replaced and Teel's tutorials were reassigned.<ref name="NewCantina" />
