The '''''Falfa''''' was the personal [[Rainhawk-class transport|''Rainhawk''-class transport]]<ref name="TVE" /> of [[Baron]] [[Notluwiski Papanoida]] of the [[Pantoran Assembly]]. During the [[Clone Wars]] Papanoida and his son [[Ion Papanoida|Ion]] used the [[Starship|ship]] to [[Rescue in Chalmun's Cantina|rescue]] his daughter [[Che Amanwe Papanoida]] who was being held hostage by the [[Trade Federation]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Sphere" />
