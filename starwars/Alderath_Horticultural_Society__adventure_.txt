"'''Alderath Horticultural Society'''" is an adventure seed contained in the ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]''. A group of [[Spacer/Legends|spacers]] are hired to transport a valuable plant to a [[Crime lord/Legends|crime lord]]. Possible alternate storylines that could be adapted by a gamemaster were also included. It was written by [[Bill Olmesdahl]].
