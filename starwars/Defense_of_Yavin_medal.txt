The '''Defense of Yavin medal''' was the first major campaign [[medal]] that was issued by the [[Alliance Military]]. The medal was awarded to all [[Alliance Army|army]] and [[Rebel Alliance Navy|naval]] personnel who was present at the [[Battle of Yavin]]. The medal was characterized by a [[bronze]]-[[color]]ed disc with diagonal [[copper]] and [[gold]] bands that depicted the [[planet]] [[Yavin Prime]] and was suspended from a long, tapering gold [[ribbon]] with red and brown stripes. The rack ribbon that was issued with the medal was a horizontal gold bar with red and brown stripes at the ends. It was often worn alongside the [[Death Star medal]].<ref name="Lead" />
