The '''Dermos system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Belsmuth sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It fell within grid square O-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the [[planet]] [[Dermos]], which a [[Unidentified insurgent group|group]] of insurgents planned to raid before they were arrested by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Dermos system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
