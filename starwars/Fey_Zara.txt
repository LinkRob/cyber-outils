'''Fey Zara''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Bartender|keeper]] of<ref name="GoH" /> [[Fey's Cantina]],<ref name="NewCantina" /> where she assisted new patrons at the [[holotable]]s and offered people cold [[Foodstuff|refreshments]] as well as tutorials and quests for the [[holotable]]s. She was assisted by the [[protocol droid]] [[B7-N5]].<ref name="GoH" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:FeyZara-GoH.png|thumb|left|200px|Fey Zara's original appearance in ''Galaxy of Heroes'']]
Fey Zara first appeared in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'', though she was unnamed until<ref name="GoH" /> "Version Update [[May 23|5/23]]/[[2018]]",<ref name="EA-Version Update 5/23/2018">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/170303/version-update-5-23-2018|text=Version Update 5/23/2018}}</ref> after which<ref name="Quests Summary">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/170302/quests-summary-from-a-cg-developer|text=Quests Summary from a CG Developer|archivedate=20190917193924|nolive=1}}</ref> she gave quests to players. Players can also earn a portrait of her as a quest reward.<ref name="GoH" /> Voice actor [[Martha Marion]] stated that she at one point provided voice work for a Twi'lek for the game.<ref name="Martha Marion">{{WebCite|url=http://www.marthamarionactor.com/resume/|text=Resume|work=[[Martha Marion]]'s website|archivedate=20190220151452|nolive=1}}</ref> On [[April 27]], [[2026]], the cantina was completely replaced and Zara's model was updated and she now greet the player with spoken dialogue.<ref name="NewCantina" />
