'''Aran''' was an [[Elomin]] who was a secret informant for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Soon after been picked up by [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] during the [[Dragon Void Run]],<ref name="Han Solo 4" /> he was [[Death|murdered]] by the traitor informant [[Bot]], seemingly compelled by the [[Galactic Empire]] after being compromised.<ref name="Han Solo 5" />
