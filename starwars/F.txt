'''F''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. She [[Jedi training|trained]] under the Jedi [[Zero|Shad-Rah Varcanella]], who was [[Death|killed]] in [[22 BBY]] at the [[First Battle of Geonosis|Battle of Geonosis]]. After her master's death, F was traumatized and began wearing a mask to isolate herself. Between [[19 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]], during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], F met with the [[Scout|explorer]] [[Valco]] on the [[planet]] [[Keelia]]. There, Valco showed F to where the Keelia residents [[Haru]] and [[Asu]] were performing a [[purification ceremony]]. That [[Local day|night]], F and Valco attended Asu and Haru's [[Wedding of Asu and Haru|wedding]] in [[Meevai Village]]. At the [[Marriage|wedding]], F learned that Haru would be giving herself up as a hostage to a [[Izuma's raiders|group]] of [[Pirate|raiders]] the next [[Standard day|morning]]. After the wedding, F [[Meditation|meditated]] in the [[forest]] and spoke with Haru, who explained her choice to give herself to the raiders. 

The next morning, F cut her [[Padawan braid]] and removed her mask. [[Lord]] [[Izuma]], the leader of the raiders, [[Attack on Meevai Village|arrived]] outside the [[City|village]] to take Haru and revealed that his forces had captured Haru's sister, Saku, after she had tried to resist him. He attempted to execute her, but F [[Force Stasis|stopped]] his [[blaster]] bolt with [[the Force]] and sent it back toward him. After Valco deactivated Izuma's [[B1-series battle droid|battle droids]], Izuma grabbed Haru and held a blaster to her. F ignited her [[F's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and propelled herself toward Izuma, [[Dismemberment|cutting off]] his [[Limb|arm]] and saving Haru. She later departed the planet in her [[F's ship|ship]].

Less than a [[Standard month|month]] later, F was present on the planet [[Inoli]] when a [[groundquake]] occurred, trapping the humans [[Ron]] and [[Theo (Inoli)|Theo]] inside a building. F rescued them and then joined them on [[Ron's Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|Ron's shuttle]] as clouds of [[carbonite]] released from abandoned [[Mining|mines]] by the quake engulfed the [[Unidentified city (Inoli)|town]]. The [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|shuttle]] landed in a [[Unidentified refugee ship|refugee ship]], where F visited a [[Joona's clinic|clinic]] run by the [[Besalisk]] [[Joona]]. F gave advice to a group of [[Youngling|children]] asking her questions, and after, Joona repaired her [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] leg. The [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] [[Oppressor (Imperial-class)|''Oppressor'']] exited [[hyperspace]] near the [[refugee]] ship, and its [[Unidentified Imperial admiral (Oppressor)|admiral]] demanded F surrender herself. F went with Ron on his [[shuttle]] and landed in the [[Star Destroyer]]'s [[hangar]], with Ron pretending he had captured the Jedi. Aboard the ''Oppressor'', F was shocked to meet a [[Resurrection|resurrected]] Varcanella, now known as Zero. He invited F to join him, and the two [[Duel on the Oppressor|dueled]] when she refused. When F leaped above him to attack, Zero cut off her feet and right arm. However, F placed a capsule of carbonite on him, [[Carbon-freezing|freezing]] him moments later. The ''Oppressor'' began to fall toward Inoli due to explosions set off by Ron, and F escaped with him after he helped her to his shuttle.

===Observing a ritual===
{{Quote|People of this world have a deep respect for nature. Their rituals allow them to live in harmony with it.''"<br />"''Is that what you wanted to show me?''"<br />"''Only because an old friend of ours had roots on this planet.|Valco and F|<ref name="Bride" />}}
[[File:ValcoAndF-VillageBride.jpg|thumb|left|295px|F and Valco observe the purification ceremony]]
During the Empire's [[Imperial Era|reign]],<ref name="Bride" /> between 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[1 BBY]],<ref name="The Village Bride IU Date">{{VisionsDate|104}}</ref> F received a message from the [[Scout|explorer]] [[Valco]],<ref name="Bride" /> an old ally<ref name="Main Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-visions/episode-guide-the-village-bride|text="The Village Bride" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars: Visions}}</ref> and a friend of her master.<ref name="Art of Visions">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]''</ref> At<ref name="Bride" /> his<ref name="Art of Visions" /> request, she traveled to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet<ref name="Bride" /> [[Keelia]],<ref name="Meet the Heroes">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-visions-meet-the-heroes|text=Inside ''Star Wars: Visions'': Meet the Heroes}}</ref> where she found him using his [[Valco's helmet|helmet]] and other devices to explore a [[forest]]. F reminded Valco of her need to be cautious, though Valco assured her that the Empire would not reach her there. After noting his interest in the planet and telling her that he wanted to show her something, Valco commented that F's mask did not suit her. F looked away and noticed the Keelia resident [[Asu]] carrying<ref name="Bride" /> his<ref name="Art of Visions" /> [[Marriage|bride]], [[Haru]], on a [[Bridal chair|chair]] up a nearby [[mountain]].<ref name="Bride" />

Valco then showed F to the edge of a cliff, where they observed Asu and Haru perform<ref name="Bride" /> a [[purification ceremony]]<ref name="Art of Visions" /> in the valley below. A circle of<ref name="Bride" /> blue energy<ref name="Bride AD">{{Visions|The Village Bride}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> covered the valley, and Haru and Asu saw themselves playing in a [[Water|river]] as children. F and Valco also witnessed the [[Force vision|vision]], and F realized they were seeing the planet's memories. As the vision ended, the valley appeared to collapse. Valco told F of the deep respect the people of Keelia had for nature and explained he had shown her the ritual because of her master's roots on the planet. Later, F and Valco discussed how the Separatists had exploited Keelia and stripped it of its resources. When Valco commented on the loss that war brought, F remembered her master's death.<ref name="Bride" />

===A wedding of mourning===
{{Quote|I understand the grief of the village runs deep. Imagine being imprisoned by invaders to save your grandpa.|Valco|<ref name="Bride" />}}
[[File:F-the-village-bride.jpg|thumb|right|300px|F and Valco attend the wedding]]
That [[Local day|night]], F and Valco attended Asu and Haru's [[Wedding of Asu and Haru|wedding]] in [[Meevai Village]], where they sat outside at low tables. F noted that it seemed wrong, but Valco told her celebrations were meant to be shared with a large group. A [[Unidentified villager (Valco)|villager]] offered them both [[Alcohol|drinks]], which they accepted. Valco and the villager discussed the circumstances surrounding the wedding, explaining that [[Izuma's raiders|raiders]] with reprogramed Separatist [[B1-series battle droid|battle droids]] had demanded the [[Unidentified village chief (Meevai Village)|village chief]] as collateral, but that Haru had volunteered to go in his place and would be taken the next [[Standard day|morning]]. Another [[Unidentified brown-haired villager|villager]] apologized for the villager bothering them, though Valco said he understood.<ref name="Bride" />

Haru's younger sister [[Saku]] then stood and shouted at Haru, attempting to persuade her to fight back. When Haru refused to allow them to, Saku and a group of other villagers who supported her left the wedding. As they did, Valco commented to F that Saku reminded him of her. F responded she knew what he was trying to do, which Valco replied to by claiming he did not care about her [[Jedi Code|code]].<ref name="Bride" />

===Making a choice===
{{Quote|I have a bad feeling about this.''"<br />"''You think I'm being reckless?|F and Haru|<ref name="Bride" />}}
[[File:IzumaSaku-VillageBride.png|thumb|left|270px|F freezes Izuma's blaster bolt, saving Saku]]
Later that night, F stood in the forest and<ref name="Bride" /> [[Meditation|meditated]] by [[Force power|using]] the Force to [[Telekinesis|levitate]] small rocks.<ref name="Episode Gallery Slide 9">{{SW|url=the-village-bride-episode-gallery|text="The Village Bride" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref> She was approached by Haru, who noticed F could also feel the Magina, the local name for the Force. F expressed doubts about Haru's decision to give herself to the raiders, but Haru remained resolute and explained that not doing so would put the [[City|village]] at risk. Asu arrived, and Haru added that she would not be alone before leaving with her husband. After they departed, F saw a brief vision of a red [[Darth Sidious' lightsabers|lightsaber]] and heard Sidious scream.<ref name="Bride" />

As the [[Star|sun]] rose the next morning, F used a [[knife]] to cut her [[Padawan braid]] and removed her mask, letting both fall to the ground. Just outside the village, its inhabitants gathered as the raiders [[Attack on Meevai Village|arrived]] to take Haru, who approached them along with Asu to give herself up. [[Lord]] [[Izuma]], the leader of the raiders, then revealed that<ref name="Bride" /> his<ref name="Art of Visions" /> forces had captured Saku and other villagers who had attempted to resist. He drew his [[KYD-21 blaster pistol|blaster pistol]] and aimed it at Saku, intending to execute her for resisting. Izuma fired at point-blank range, but F, who by that point had arrived behind Asu and Haru, used the Force to [[Force Stasis|freeze]] the [[blaster]] bolt before it could reach Saku. Izuma, as well as Asu and Haru, looked at F with surprise, and the Jedi sent the bolt back into Izuma's hand, causing him to fall to the ground and drop his [[blaster pistol]]. F stepped in front of the couple and spoke about the way the people of Keelia lived in harmony with nature and the Force.<ref name="Bride" />

===Magina rises===
{{Quote|What are you? Some kind of a monster?''"<br />"''I am a Jedi.|Izuma and F|<ref name="Bride" />}}
[[File:FvsIzuma-VillageBride.jpg|thumb|right|400px|F faces off against Izuma]]
Izuma ordered his forces to fire, and Valco began shooting at them from atop a nearby cliff. After running out of ammunition, Valco threw his [[Armor|helmet]] to the ground below. It propelled itself into [[Izuma's starship]] and caused it to explode, deactivating Izuma's [[battle droid]]s and causing his remaining forces to flee. Izuma scrambled to pick up a blaster from the ground, then grabbed Haru and held it to her while screaming at F. She responded by declaring herself to be a Jedi and igniting her [[F's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. After a few moments, F used a device in her shoe to propel herself toward Izuma<ref name="Bride" /> with super-human speed<ref name="Episode Gallery Slide 16">{{SW|url=the-village-bride-episode-gallery|text="The Village Bride" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 16}}</ref> and [[Dismemberment|cut off]] his blaster arm before he could react. The raider collapsed as F remained crouched behind him.<ref name="Bride" />

Later, a group of villagers gathered as F shook hands with Haru. F then departed in her [[F's ship|ship]], and as she [[pilot]]ed it away, the villagers waved at her and Valco removed his helmet to look up at it as she flew overhead. After leaving the planet, F jumped to [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Bride" />

===Carbonite crisis===
{{Quote|What happened to this planet?''"<br />"''Hmm. They used it for carbonite mining, but during the war it was abandoned and left to waste away. It's leaking in gas form, being released in quakes like the last one.|F and Ron|<ref name="Lost Ones" />}}
[[File:Freaching-TheLostOnes.jpg|thumb|left|300px|F offers her hand to Ron and Theo]]
Less than a [[Standard month|month]] later,<ref name="Lost Ones Date">{{VisionsDate|306}}</ref> F landed her ship in a valley on the planet<ref name="Lost Ones" /> [[Inoli]].<ref name="Focus">{{SWYouTube|series=Star Wars: Visions Filmmaker Focus|text=THE LOST ONES|video=vFN_YL-Vseg}}</ref> Smoke billowed from the [[starfighter]], and she leapt out and pulled<ref name="Lost Ones" /> a conduit<ref name="Lost Ones AD" /> loose from a section of its fuselage missing its external paneling. She then stepped to the edge of a cliff to look through her [[macrobinoculars]] at a group of humans walking through a [[Unidentified city (Inoli)|city]]. A short time later, a [[groundquake]] occurred, causing an apartment to collapse with two members of the group&mdash;[[Ron]] and [[Theo (Inoli)|Theo]]&mdash;inside, trapping them under the rubble. F arrived at the site of the collapse and removed a large slab, then helped Ron and Theo out of the building.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

After rescuing them, F and the other members of the group boarded [[Ron's Sheathipede-class transport shuttle]], which took off as the town was engulfed by large clouds of [[carbonite]] released from abandoned [[Mining|mines]] by the quake. Ron told F about the carbonite leaks, which by that point had resulted in a third of the planet being frozen, and showed her a capsule of concentrated carbonite gas they were researching to take back the planet. The [[droid]] [[Lulu (droid)|Lulu]] then rolled into the room and bumped into F's leg. F recognized the droid and greeted it, causing Ron to realize F knew its owner, the [[Besalisk]] [[Joona]], as well.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

===Offering wisdom===
{{Quote|There's more to reality than what we see. And not everything seen is true. On the other hand, there are some things you can believe in, even if you can't see them. Evil takes advantage of people's ignorance and conflict, fosters ignorance. Suspicion eats away at people, and eventually leaves wounds on one's heart.|F|<ref name="Lost Ones" />}}
[[File:FandChildren-TheLostOnes.jpg|thumb|right|350px|F speaking with children aboard the refugee ship]]
The [[shuttle]] landed aboard a large [[Unidentified refugee ship|refugee ship]], and F visited a [[Joona's clinic|clinic]] run by Joona. While she was there, a group of children ran up to her and began asking her questions. F did not answer them, but did kneel down to greet the child [[Mei (Inoli)|Mei's]] [[tooka doll]] [[Imly]] when the<ref name="Lost Ones" /> girl<ref name="Lost Ones AD" /> showed him to F. The children then began asking about the Jedi, wondering if they were real and had actually started the Clone Wars, with [[Unidentified Inoli boy 2|one]] saying they wanted to join the Empire as an adult.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

F gave the children advice, suggesting they gather many experiences and make their own observations to find the answers to their questions, adding that they would be able to notice things they had not before, including the feelings of others. Hearing this, Ron's younger brother [[Leon]] ran to greet [[Unidentified Inoli boy 4|another child]] standing alone nearby. F further warned the children against ignorance, conflict, and suspicion, prompting Mei to touch the scar on F's face and ask if Joona would heal it. F replied that it was a wound she needed to heal by herself and mentally recalled seeing the hand of Varcanella's dead body.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

Later, F sat in Joona's clinic. After the Besalisk had repaired F's [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] left leg, a black coating formed to conceal it, and she thanked Joona, who warned her to be more careful. Noticing the effect F's advice had had on the children, Joona asked her if she had considered teaching, believing that like Varcanella, she would be good at it. F admitted she was unsure and remembered sparring with her master.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

===Pretend prisoner===
{{Quote|If you go, they'll kill you.''"<br />"''If I don't go, they'll kill everyone.|Ron and F|<ref name="Lost Ones" />}}
[[File:RonCapturesF-TheLostOnes.png|thumb|left|300px|F pretends to be a captive of Ron]]
The lights on the ship later dimmed, and F bundled her possessions, including a [[Jedi holocron]] and her mask, while sitting in the clinic. Joona entered and asked F to stay longer, but she declined and promised to visit Joona's planet in the future. An [[Alarm system|alarm]] blared, and the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Oppressor (Imperial-class)|''Oppressor'']] exited hyperspace near the [[refugee]] ship. It fired a warning shot at the ship, and the ''Oppressor''{{'s}} [[Unidentified Imperial admiral (Oppressor)|admiral]] broadcast a message demanding the Jedi aboard surrender. Ron found F and attempted to convince her not to turn herself in, but she insisted on doing so to protect the refugees aboard.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

F boarded Ron's shuttle and instructed Lulu to navigate it back to the refugee ship once she arrived on the Star Destroyer, but before they took off, Ron arrived as well. When the shuttle landed in the ''Oppressor''{{'s}} [[hangar]], F and Ron exited, with Ron holding a blaster and F wearing [[binder]]s. Pretending to have captured F, Ron asked for a reward and handed her lightsaber to an [[Unidentified Imperial officer (Oppressor)|Imperial officer]]. At the [[Imperial officer|officer's]] command, a pair of [[stormtrooper]]s escorted F to a [[Zero's room|room]]<ref name="Lost Ones" /> that appeared like an endless open field<ref name="Lost Ones AD" /> with the floor covered in red flower beds.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

===Ghost of the past===
{{Quote|Hear me, Padawan. No, you're a proper Jedi now, aren't you? I'm giving you a chance to become my apprentice once again. A chance to correct your misguided ways. Your choice. Will you eliminate the Jedi with me? Or will you die as a Jedi?|Zero, to F|<ref name="Lost Ones" />}}
[[File:ZeroHoldingFlower-TheLostOnes.png|thumb|right|250px|F was shocked to see her master awaiting her aboard the ''Oppressor''.]]
Awaiting F in the room was a [[Resurrection|resurrected]] Varcanella, who now went by the name Zero. F stared at Zero in shock, and he noted the flower he was holding was the same as the one she had left at his grave, then removed her binders with the Force. F asked how he was alive, and Zero responded that he had died but then returned. Between them, a pillar emerged from the floor and revealed F's lightsaber. Zero invited F to rejoin him as his apprentice, explaining his belief that the Jedi were misguided and must be destroyed. Zero acknowledged that F was now a proper Jedi and no longer a Padawan. He then demanded F take her lightsaber and either join him or die, but F refused to do either.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

Zero accused F of running, reminding her of how she had run from the Clone Wars and from being a Jedi. Zero then stated he hated the war and the Jedi who had caused it, as well as F, who had run after he had died to save her. He ignited his [[Shad-Rah's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and [[Duel on the Oppressor|charged]] at F, who pulled her own lightsaber to her in time to block his strike. Zero swung his lightsaber at F several times, but she evaded each strike without attacking back and then [[Force jump|jumped]] backward<ref name="Lost Ones" /> with the Force, putting distance between them.<ref name="Lost Ones AD" />

===Path of the Jedi===
{{Quote|It is by supporting each other that peace can be born. My master followed the path of the Jedi and lost his life. And so&hellip;I will continue to live as that master taught me.|F, to Zero|<ref name="Lost Ones" />}}
F acknowledged that Zero had saved her life and stated she would continue to protect people as he had taught her. Zero responded that she had been captured because of those she protected. As<ref name="Lost Ones" /> the [[hologram]]<ref name="Lost Ones AD" /> around the room's windows fell away, revealing the room was surrounded by [[viewport]]s showing space, F resolved to continue living by the teachings of her master, and assumed a stance with her lightsaber held behind her. F's boot opened, and she and Zero dashed toward each other.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

[[File:FJumpAttack-TheLostOnes.png|thumb|left|400px|F leaps to attack Zero]]
F performed a flip and leapt above Zero's head to strike at him,<ref name="Lost Ones" /> but he anticipated the attack<ref name="Lost Ones AD" /> and cut off her right arm at the elbow, then cut off her feet with another slash. She pushed off his shoulder in mid-air, placing a capsule of carbonite on him, and Zero slashed at her again as she fell to the floor. Zero taunted her, telling F her skills were not enough to defeat him, but the carbonite began to spread across him, [[Carbon-freezing|freezing]] him. F looked at Zero and admitted she lacked the lightsaber skills to defeat him, but told him she could still stop him. As the carbonite spread across him, Zero complemented F's growth, and she called out to him.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

At the same time, Ron set off explosions aboard the ''Oppressor'', which began to fall toward Inoli, allowing the refugee ship to escape into hyperspace. F used her remaining arm to flip herself over to look up through the room's ceiling and remembered the occasion on which she waited for Varcanella by a tree and greeted him. A tear formed in F's eye, and she closed her eyes as the ''Oppressor'' fell toward Inoli. Ron ran into the room and brought her aboard his shuttle, which flew away from the Star Destroyer. While sitting in the shuttle, F thanked Ron, who apologized for causing trouble, but F reiterated that she was grateful and offered her hand to Ron. He shook it, and the shuttle jumped to hyperspace.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Know something? She reminds me of you.|Valco, comparing Saku to F|<ref name="Bride" />}}
[[File:F-TheLostOnes.jpg|thumb|right|250px|F was a stoic and guarded Jedi.]]
F was a young<ref name="Episode Gallery Slide 2" /> human with light skin, dark blue eyes, and black hair, which was longer when she was a child. She had a scar on her face and a cybernetic left leg.<ref name="Lost Ones" /> F was traumatized after her master's death and was left without guidance or a purpose,<ref name="Art of Visions" /> losing both her pride and self-confidence.<ref name="Backstory" /> She struggled with her identity and was unsure of which side of the Force she would fall.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> F was cautious<ref name="Bride" /> and isolated herself behind her mask.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> When F arrived on Keelia, she was drawn into conflict and forced to decide whether to reveal herself.<ref name="Meet the Heroes" /> Valco gently guided her without pushing her,<ref name="Art of Visions" /> and after making her choice she was resolute. She cut her Padawan [[braid]], which marked her as a student, indicating she was ready to become the master of her destiny.<ref name="Highlights">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-highlights-the-village-bride|text=''Visions'' Revisited: 5 Highlights from "The Village Bride"}}</ref> Valco compared F to<ref name="Bride" /> the headstrong<ref name="Art of Visions" /> Saku,<ref name="Bride" /> and F saw something of herself in her as well.<ref name="Art of Visions" />

Stoic and guarded, F sought out people to help despite the danger involved.<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> When F was asked questions by the children aboard the refugee ship, she did not answer them and instead gave them advice. She did, however, greet Mei's [[tooka]] [[doll]]. F willingly surrendered herself to the Empire to protect the refugees aboard the ship. She refused to join Zero, instead choosing to live by his past teachings.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

==Powers and abilities==
{{Quote|Can you feel the Magina as well?|Haru, to F|<ref name="Bride" />}}
As a Jedi, F was [[Force-sensitive]]. She used telekinesis while meditating and used Force stasis to save Saku.<ref name="Bride" /> While dueling Zero, she used telekinesis to pull her lightsaber to her and used the Force to jump backward. She lacked the lightsaber skills to directly defeat Zero in lightsaber combat, but was still able to stop him using a capsule of carbonite.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|It doesn't suit you, that mask.|Valco, to F|<ref name="Bride" />}}
As a child, F wore brown [[Jedi apparel]] over a white [[Clothing|shirt]] with a blue symbol. She had blue sleeves and socks, with green pants and brown shoes. She utilized a stick while training with Varcanella.<ref name="Lost Ones" /> While older, F wielded a yellow [[lightsaber]] with a thin blade that curved to a point. She wore white [[robe]]s under a black [[cloak]] and wore blue clothing over her legs. She wore a mask to cover her face, a gray ring on her finger, and a Padawan braid. Her boots were gray and contained a device she used to propel herself forward. F piloted a [[clone Z-95 starfighter]].<ref name="Bride" /> She used macrobinoculars on Inoli, and while on the refugee ship, she used her cloak to bundle her possessions, including her mask and a Jedi [[holocron]].<ref name="Lost Ones" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Overview===
{{Quote|F brings a sense of serene calm to this piece. ‘The Village Bride’ is as much about the natural world, customs, traditions, and a new and unexpected way people might worship and wield the Force. It’s a meditative story, almost a tone poem, as F reflects on what is happening in front of her, but also on her own tragic past.|Josh Rimes|<ref name="Meet the Heroes" />}}
[[File:The_Village_Bride_poster.jpg|thumb|right|270px|F first appeared in "The Village Bride."]]
F first appeared in "[[The Village Bride]]," a [[Canon|non-canon]] short film directed by [[Hitoshi Haga]], written by Haga and [[Takahito Oonishi]], and released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|first volume ]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kinema Citrus Co.|Kinema Citrus]], was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Bride" /> on [[September 22]], [[2021]].<ref name="Stunning Trailer">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-trailer-and-cast|text=Stunning New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Trailer Debuts}}</ref> Prior to the short's release, F was first pictured in a video uploaded to the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[July 3]], 2021,<ref name="Special Look">{{SWYouTube|b9Gr9gKFxpE|STAR WARS: VISIONS &#124; SPECIAL LOOK &#124; DISNEY+|sw_url=video/special-look-star-wars-visions}}</ref> and she was first mentioned, albeit indirectly, in a [[StarWars.com]] article the same day.<ref name="20 Things">{{SW|url=news/anime-expo-lite-star-wars-visions|text=Anime Expo Lite: 20 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: Visions'' Panel}}</ref> F was then identified in another StarWars.com article published on [[August 17]] of that year.<ref name="Stunning Trailer" /> F also appeared in "[[The Lost Ones]]," a sequel to "The Village Bride" written and directed by Haga, produced by Kinema Citrus,<ref name="Lost Ones" /> and released on Disney+ on [[October 29]], 2025 as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of ''Visions''.<ref name="Focus" /> F is voiced by [[Asami Seto]] in the original [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] and by [[Karen Fukuhara]] in the [[Wikipedia:English language|English]] dub.<ref name="Stunning Trailer" />

Haga complimented Seto's performance in "The Village Bride" and was sorry she had so few lines. He described F and Haru and their chemistry as the key of the story and said they ended up taking over the story about nature he wanted to do.<ref name="Special Program">{{DisneyPlusYouTube|video=7wv95d_OX4Q|text=【特別番組】スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ 全世界配信記念 &#124; Disney+ (ディズニープラス)|jp=1}}</ref> Seto said F was not a talkative person and carried a burden from her past. She also thought that although F appeared to be isolated, she had actually been protected by Valco.<ref name="Visions Times">{{BookCite|author=Yamazaki, Gigan; Shide, Hidekuni; Ishii, Makoto|container=Visions Times|title=[[Wikipedia:Newtype|Newtype]]|publisher=[[Wikipedia:Kadokawa Corporation|Kadokawa]]|date=[[November]] [[2021]]|pages=insert poster|language=[[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]}}</ref> Oonishi said that in ''Star Wars'', individuals could often embody both good and evil or light and dark, which is the struggle he wanted for F.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> Executive producer [[Josh Rimes]] described F as mysterious and said she brought a sense of serene calm to the short, comparing it to a [[Wikipedia:Symphonic poem|tone poem]] as F reflected on her past as well as what was occurring in front of her. Rimes also explained that F's mask and cloak both literally and figuratively hid her from her past trauma.<ref name="Meet the Heroes" /> 

Haga explained that when F took off her mask, she separated from the unpleasant memories of her past and removed the sign of her being a Padawan, allowing her to rise up as a Jedi in her own way.<ref name="Backstory" /> In "The Lost Ones," Haga wanted to show the choices F had to make to overcome her past and move forward.<ref name="Key Tradition">{{WebCite|url=https://www.awn.com/animationworld/sequels-star-wars-visions-volume-3-carry-key-franchise-tradition|text=The Sequels of ‘Star Wars: Visions’ Volume 3 Carry on a Key Franchise Tradition|date=2025-12-02|work=[https://www.awn.com awn.com]|archivedate=20251202195416}}</ref> Haga said that when Zero acknowledged F as a Jedi, it was in a sense her greatest wish being finally fulfilled.<ref name="Sequel Directors">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-sequel-directors|text=Meet the Directors Behind the ''Star Wars: Visions'' Sequels}}</ref> 

===Development===
{{Quote|I thought it would be easy to imagine what happens next. The injured Padawan would understand her own purpose and rise up. However, I thought viewers might not accept that, so we brought it to a proper conclusion.|Hitoshi Haga|<ref name="Backstory" />}}
[[File:F_concept_art.jpg|thumb|left|270px|Concept art by Shiori Tani for "The Village Bride"]]
In "The Village Bride," F became an observer and guest at Asu and Haru's wedding after happening upon them in the forest, which was inspired by [[Wikipedia:Akira Kurosawa|Akira Kurosawa's]]<ref name="Art of Visions" /> [[1990]]<ref name="Dreams">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/biography/Kurosawa-Akira|text=Kurosawa Akira|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica, Inc.|Encyclopædia Britannica]]|archivedate=20250318103719}}</ref> film [[Wikipedia:Dreams (1990 film)|''Dreams'']].<ref name="Art of Visions" /> Originally, Haga planned for "The Village Bride" to end when F ignited her lightsaber blade, as he believed it would be easy to imagine what happened next. However, he worried viewers would not accept that and decided to show the confrontation's conclusion. When F landed the blow with her lightsaber, the film cuts to black, as rather than just depicting the action, Haga wanted to symbolically depict the event and F's incredible speed.<ref name="Backstory" /> In the scene in which F cut her Padawan braid, Haga experimented with the strap of his own glasses to determine how to achieve a natural movement. F was constantly feeling fear about having escaped to an unfamiliar planet, and the lines of her eyes slightly shifted to express her feelings. Because F was suppressing her fear and looking straight ahead, her eyes moved before her head.<ref name="Celebration Blog">{{WebCite|url=https://swgm1138.livedoor.blog/archives/36602229.html|text=&#91;セレブレーション2日目&#93;サイン会5連発！カイミール祭り&原点の推しK-2SOとの邂逅&「ビジョンズ」イベントなどなどなどでまたもや時間が濃密過ぎる|date=2025-04-19|work=[https://swgm1138.livedoor.blog swgm1138.livedoor.blog]|language=Japanese|archivedate=20251010184548}}</ref>

Haga wanted to express a contrast between F and [[Ahsoka Tano]], another Order 66 survivor. Unlike Tano, F continued to be a Jedi, which Haga thought meant she could play a different role than Tano did in spinoff works.<ref name="Animate Times">{{WebCite|url=https://www.animatetimes.com/news/details.php?id=1762831026|text=アソーカとは異なる未来に繋ぐ、もう一人の“パダワン”の物語――『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』Volume3『彷徨う者たち』垪和等監督インタビュー|date=2025-11-13|work=[https://www.animatetimes.com animatetimes.com]|language=Japanese|archivedate=20251114010008}}</ref> In "The Lost Ones," Haga wanted to display F's feelings. One of his favorite scenes was F's conversation with the children on the refugee ship. The flashback scenes of F's memories were vague to show that F was reminiscing.<ref name="VFX Voice">{{WebCite|url=https://vfxvoice.com/star-wars-visions-vol-3-expands-the-saga-with-the-duel-payback-the-ninth-jedi-child-of-hope-the-lost-ones/|text=Star Wars: Visions Vol. 3 Expands the Saga With The Duel: Payback, The Ninth Jedi: Child of Hope, & The Lost Ones|date=2026-03-10|work=[https://vfxvoice.com VFX Voice]|archivedate=20260318182136}}</ref> In the short, Haga wanted the audience to feel that F was unable to defeat Zero, but still had to stop him. According to Haga, the most difficult shot was when F jumped over Zero during their duel and was cut up.<ref name="Key Tradition" /> At the beginning of their duel, F avoided Zero's attacks, but her movement changed when she realized he was now her enemy. Haga described F allowing Zero to cut her as tiny steps she took to calculate how to go beyond his power and inject the carbonite into his body.<ref name="VFX Voice" />

F was originally a temporary name used during production, but it was kept due to Kinema Citrus staff preferring to use the name. In "The Lost Ones," Haga considered having F's real name be revealed. However, this did not occur, and Haga stated that it remained undecided whether F was an alias or the character's real name.<ref name="Instagram Interview">{{Instagram|username=starwarsjapan|url=reel/DQjX8TxEc2K|name=Star Wars Japan|date=2025-11-02|quote=トークセッション「彷徨う者たち」キネマシトラス／垪和等監督【前編】|archiveurl=https://archive.org/details/the-lost-ones-instagram-live-2025-11-02-part-1}}</ref> Haga created a backstory for F so that the filmmakers could determine how she would react during the events of "The Village Bride," although only a few details from it actually appeared in the short. In the backstory, F received her scar from Count Dooku at the Battle of Geonosis, where she also lost her master.<ref name="Backstory" /> ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]'', a [[2022]] reference book by [[Zack Davisson]], instead stated F lost her master during Order 66.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> "The Lost Ones" later referenced additional elements of Haga's original backstory, including Varcanella's death on Geonosis.<ref name="Lost Ones" /><ref name="Backstory" />

===Design===
{{Quote|I was highly conscious of what F would wear. Is F Japanese or not? Is F a man or a woman? I wanted to keep it ambiguous.|Shiori Tani|<ref name="Art of Visions" />}}
[[File:FConcept-TaniSide8.jpg|thumb|right|270px|Concept art by Shiori Tani for "The Lost Ones"]]
F was designed by [[Shiori Tani]].<ref name="Special Program" /> While visualizing her, Tani was highly conscious of what she would wear, and wanted to keep it ambiguous as to whether F was [[Wikipedia:Japanese people|Japanese]] or not, and whether she was a man or woman. F's clothes were based on traditional [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] [[Wikipedia:Hakama|''hakama'']], and her mask was based on [[Wikipedia:Men-yoroi|''menpō'']] jaw armor.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> During the process of creating the character, the staff at Kinema Citrus dressed up like F to test her costume design. Producer [[Kanako Shirasaki]] loved the combination of F's ''hakama'' and boots,<ref name="Meet the Heroes" /> and Haga also praised Tani's design.<ref name="Special Program" /> Concept art of F was created by Tani for both "The Village Bride"<ref name="Art of Visions" /> and "The Lost Ones,"<ref name="Lost Ones Gallery Slide 5">{{SW|url=the-lost-ones-concept-art-gallery-star-wars-visions-volume-3|text="The Lost Ones" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars: Visions Volume 3}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> and [[Anna Okamatsu]] also created a piece of concept art for "The Lost Ones."<ref name="Lost Ones Gallery Slide 6">{{SW|url=the-lost-ones-concept-art-gallery-star-wars-visions-volume-3|text="The Lost Ones" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars: Visions Volume 3}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref>

Early in development of "The Village Bride," Haga wanted to give F a classic send-off in a heroic ship such as an [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]]. However, because of the time period the short took place in, traditional X-wings could not be used, and [[Matt Martin]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] instead suggested that F's ship be a Z-95.<ref name="Meet the Ships">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-visions-meet-the-ships-and-vehicles|text=Inside ''Star Wars: Visions'': Meet the Ships and Vehicles}}</ref> F's lightsaber was designed by [[Takeshi Takakura]] and has a distinct [[Wikipedia:Japanese sword|Japanese sword]] motif, similar to the [[Mandalorian]] [[Darksaber]]. Unlike traditional lightsabers, it has a thin blade like a [[Wikipedia:Katana|katana]].<ref name="Art of Visions" /> Haga thought F was in a similar position to [[Jedi Temple Guard]]s,<ref name="IGN Japan">{{WebCite|url=https://jp.ign.com/star-wars-visions/54787/interview|text=『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』監督インタビュー！キネマシトラス垪和等監督『村の花嫁』|date=2021-09-29|work=IGN Japan|language=Japanese|archivedate=20250322214529}}</ref> who often used yellow-bladed lightsabers,<ref name="Art of Visions" /> and kept their role as special guardians in mind while designing F, which led to his decision for her to use a yellow lightsaber.<ref name="Animate Times" />

In "The Village Bride," Haga had not yet decided if F's leg was prosthetic,<ref name="VFX Voice" /> and it was made to be deliberately vague so that fans would wonder if that was the case, which was confirmed in "The Lost Ones". In addition to the scar on her face, Haga wanted F's body to also be wounded, as he felt ''Star Wars'' has a theme of moving forward despite physical imperfections, which was the case with<ref name="Animate Times" /> the Jedi<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]]. According to Haga, this was also the reason why F continued to wander space and help people.<ref name="Animate Times" /> Visually, Haga wanted Zero's design to be the complete opposite of F.<ref name="Key Tradition" />
