'''Ahn Krantarium''' was a terrestrial astronomical object in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim's]] [[Ahn Krantarium system]]. Long before the [[Imperial Era]], it was overrun by ferocious [[droid]]s who continued to rebuild themselves to be [[Death|deadlier]]. Its settlements were left in ruin, inhabited only by the droids, who were not prevented from upgrading themselves further. Around [[2 BBY]], [[Governor]] [[Ahr]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] eventually led an [[Attack on Ahn Krantarium|attack]] on the droids of Ahn Krantarium in which he sent out [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] alone, hoping that the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] would [[Death|perish]]. However, Vader succeeded in destroying the droids and survived.

==Description==
A terrestrial astronomical object,<ref name="Age of Rebellion" /> Ahn Krantarium was located in the [[Esuain sector]]'s [[Ahn Krantarium system]], which was in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square P-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Galaxy" /> Ahn Krantarium had a rocky surface and an [[atmosphere]] filled with smoke.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />

==History==
{{Quote|It'd be a ''suicide run''.''"<br />"''I see&hellip; Send ''Vader''.|An [[Imperial officer]] and Governor Ahr|<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />}}
[[File:VaderOnAhnKrantarium-AORDarthVader1.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Darth Vader destroys the droids on Ahn Krantarium.]]
Ahn Krantarium was once inhabited, with settlements built on its surface. Several [[Standard decade|decades]] before the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Ahn Krantarium was overrun by murderous [[droid]]s, who destroyed its civilization. The droids were left alone, allowing them to rebuild themselves until they had become far more dangerous to handle than regular [[security droid]]s.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />

Around [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Age of Rebellion - Darth Vader 1|''Age of Rebellion - Darth Vader'' 1]] to around [[2 BBY]].</ref> [[Ahr]], the Imperial [[governor]] of the [[sector]] in which Ahn Krantarium was located, was assigned the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] during the [[Pacification of the Esuain sector|pacification of the sector]]. After Vader disobeyed Ahr's orders during an [[Attack on Namzor|attack]] against [[Namzor insurgents|insurgents]] on the astromical object [[Namzor]], the Emperor demanded that Vader follow every order Ahr gave him.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />

Ahr subsequently traveled to Ahn Krantarium in his [[Ahr's Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], where he [[Attack on Ahn Krantarium|deployed]] a [[stormtrooper]] [[Scout|patrol]] to the surface. The patrol came under attack from the droids and was declared to be lost. Upon hearing from his [[Ahr's subordinate|subordinate]] that fighting the droids would be a futile effort, Ahr sent Vader to deal with them, in hopes of getting the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Death|killed]]. To Ahr's surprise, Vader destroyed the droids, returning to the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]].<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />

==Inhabitants==
Ahn Krantarium was formerly inhabited, but it was taken over by murderous droids who killed any [[Species|organic beings]] that attempted to land on the astronomical object. The droids were allowed to run wild on Ahn Krantarium for some [[time]], before being neutralized during the Imperial Era.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />

==Locations==
Settlements had once been constructed on Ahn Krantarium, though they had fallen into ruin by the time that Governor Ahr sent [[Darth]] Vader to destroy the wild droids.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
