'''Dowuta''', also known as '''Dowutinese''', was the [[language]] spoken by members of [[Dowutin]] [[species]],<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> including [[Grummgar]],<ref name="Every Language">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref> primarily on their [[homeworld]] [[Dowut]]. It consisted of deep, booming vocalizations and was considered difficult for members of other species to emulate. If a weaker being were to come to Dowut, they were expected to find a way to communicate&mdash;as Dowutin believed there was little need to learn [[Galactic Basic Standard]].<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
