During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], shortly after the [[Battle of Crait]], [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] assigned [[Wedge Antilles]] and his team, dubbed '''Corellian Team Two''', on a [[Mission to Corellia|mission]] to [[Coronet City]] on [[Corellia]], to rescue political [[prisoner]]s being held in secret by the [[First Order]]. Among the prisoners included a former [[New Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Ransolm Casterfo]], a long time friend to Organa, whom she thought was [[Death|killed]] many [[Standard year|years]] ago.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />
