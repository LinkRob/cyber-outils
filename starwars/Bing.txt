'''Bing''' was a [[Color|fair]]-skinned [[humanoid]] [[bounty hunter]] affiliated with the [[Haxion Brood]] crime syndicate during the [[Imperial Era]]. They wore yellow and white [[armor]] and [[helmet]], yellow [[boot]]s, a brown [[belt]], and a tan [[Clothing|shirt]] and pants. Bing had access to several tools, including a [[jetpack]], a [[flamethrower]] and [[missile launcher]] from the armor's [[gauntlet]]s, and an [[A280C blaster rifle]]. The [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] had several [[cybernetics|cybernetic]] enhancements, including a right [[mechno-arm]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFO Date" /> Bing fought the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]]. However, the hunter was defeated and destroyed by the Jedi.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bing appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', published by [[Respawn Entertainment]]. In the game, after the player attempts to return to their [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'', after visiting the [[Zeffonian]] [[Tomb of Miktrull]] on the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]], the player may be attacked and captured by any of the Haxion Brood bounty hunters, including Bing.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

After Kestis wakes up in a cell on the Haxion Brood homebase of [[Ordo Eris]], if the bounty hunter to capture him was Bing, he will fight and destroy the bounty hunter within the [[Haxion Fight Pit]]. If Bing is not the bounty hunter to capture Kestis, the player may encounter Bing in a variety of locations after the Ordo Eris chapter of the story. The player may choose to kill Bing and scan the corpse for the "Haxion Brood Bounty Hunter" tactical guide entry if not already previously obtained.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> In the [[2026]] [[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, Vol. 2|manga adaptation]], the hunter who captures Cal Kestis is [[Atticus Rex]].<ref name="Manga">[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, Vol. 2|''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'', Vol. 2]]</ref>
