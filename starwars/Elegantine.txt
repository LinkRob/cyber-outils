The '''''Elegantine''''' was a [[limousine]] used by [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] the [[Hutt]] in [[Nar Shaddaa]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The craft was considered to be the jewel of [[Sorbee Manufacturing]]'s line of luxury [[airspeeder|cruisers]]. Its interior was customized for Grakkus's large form and teemed with amenities.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />
