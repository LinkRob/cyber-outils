The '''Emperor's personal zoo''' was a [[zoo]] holding several intergalactic [[species]] for [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] personal viewing, many of which were rare or exotic animals. At some point, numerous species, including an [[argora]], an [[acklay]],<ref name="EndangeredPt1" /> a [[mordon]], a [[dianoga]],<ref name="EndangeredPt2">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=8|story=Endangered, Part 2}}</ref> and multiple [[tooka]]s were being transported to the zoo. However, [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] boarded the [[freighter]] carrying the animals, and released all of them in an attempt to locate the argora. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] cell known as the [[Spectres]] later arrived,<ref name="EndangeredPt1" /> and rescued all of the animals from the freighter while returning the argora to its home planet, saving them from captivity in the zoo.<ref name="EndangeredPt2" />
