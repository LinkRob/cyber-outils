The "'''Corvax descendant'''"<ref name="Inside Intel">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-inside-intel-mustafar|text=''Star Wars'' Inside Intel: Mustafar|int=Star Wars Inside Intel}}</ref> was a [[human]] [[smuggler]] who was the [[captain]] of the [[starship]] ''[[Windfall]]'', accompanied by the [[droid]] [[ZO-E3]].<ref name="Vader Immortal Ep I" /> The [[smuggler]] was unknowingly [[Force-sensitive]]<ref name="Vader Immortal Ep II" /> and was a descendant of [[Lady]] [[Corvax]], an ancient resident of the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]]. Seeking out the descendants of Corvax, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] had [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Admiral]] [[Gable Karius]] use an [[Interdictor]] vessel to pull the ''Windfall'' out of [[hyperspace]] above [[Mustafar]] and capture the smugglers, taking them to his [[Fortress Vader|fortress]]. With the help of the [[Mustafarian]] [[Vylip F'alma]], the smuggler and ZO-E3 escaped, discovering [[Corvax Fortress|Corvax's sanctum]] in the [[cave]]rns of the planet.<ref name="Vader Immortal Ep I" />

Eventually taking command of the [[Marching Horde]], the smuggler helped lead an [[Battle on Mustafar|attack]] on [[Fortress]] Vader. The [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] was attempting to activate the [[Bright Star]], hoping it would resurrect his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. The smuggler stopped Vader and destroyed the artifact, which meant Mustafar would be able to heal from its hellish state. Escaping the planet on the ''Windfall'', the smuggler and ZO-E3, after they placed a totem into [[Lord Corvax's Lightsaber]], realized they now had what seemed to be a treasure map.<ref name="Vader Immortal III" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|In earlier versions, you were part of the Empire, like an envoy. This droid would be your yeoman or your military secretary. As the story evolved, you weren't part of the Empire, you were more of a rogue adventurer-type character. Zoe became more of a sidekick, and we had a lot more freedom to play around.|Aaron McBride|<ref name="ILMXLAB">{{ILMxLAB|url=news/exploring-mustafar-intro|text=Exploring Mustafar – Introduction & Characters|archivedate=20240501211508|nolive=1}}</ref>}}
[[File:Smuggler_Vader_Immortal.png|thumb|right|200px|The smuggler's depiction in concept art for ''Vader Immortal – Episode III'']]
The smuggler serves as the player character of ''[[Vader Immortal: A Star Wars VR Series]]'', a three-part virtual reality series created by [[ILM Immersive|ILMxLAB]], first appearing in ''[[Vader Immortal – Episode I]]'', released on [[May 21]], [[2019]].<ref name="Revealed">{{SW|url=news/vader-immortal|text=''Vader Immortal: A Star Wars VR Series - Episode I'' Revealed}}</ref> As ''Vader Immortal'' is played in the first person, and the character has no voice lines, the smuggler's gender is undetermined. The smuggler's name is not given, referred to only as "Captain" by ZO-E3.<ref name="Vader Immortal Ep I" />

The player character was originally meant to be part of the Galactic Empire in a position like an envoy, but was changed to a "rogue adventurer-type character" as the story evolved.<ref name="ILMXLAB" />
