'''Clan Rook''' was a [[Mandalorian clan]] that was active during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" /> and was a vassal of [[House Kast]].<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Chronology Note">{{RebelsDate|401}}</ref> after a [[Mission to Mandalore|mission]] undertaken by [[Clan Wren]], [[Clan Kryze]], and members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] that took place during the [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|Mandalorian Civil War]], a [[Unidentified Clan Rook warrior|member]] of the clan pledged the clan to [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] [[Mandalorian resistance|along]] with other clans, joining the [[Mandalorian resistance]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />

Clan Rook consisted of Mandalorian warriors that donned [[Mandalorian armor]] with [[Color|purple]], yellow, and silver color combinations. Their [[signet]] was<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" /> that of House Kast, representing a coiled [[vexis]].<ref name="The Star Wars Book" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clan Rook first appeared in the premiere of [[Star Wars Rebels Season Four|the fourth season]] of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', titled ''[[Heroes of Mandalore|Star Wars Rebels: Heroes of Mandalore]]'', which aired in [[2017]].<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" /> Mandalorian [[Rook Kast]], appearing in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' series<ref name="OFNF">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> and tie-in material,<ref name="DMSOD1">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 1|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 1]]</ref> shares her first name with Clan Rook, but no connection has been made between them.
