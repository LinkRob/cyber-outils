'''Dark Aura''' was a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Force power]] and a form of [[Force lightning]]. [[Darth Sidious]], [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] of the [[Sith]] and [[Galactic Emperor|ruler]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], employed this technique.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I am eternal.|Palpatine, upon using the ability in-game|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
[[File:Dark aura Sidious.png|thumb|left|250px|Dark Aura as it appears in matches]]
Dark Aura appeared as an ability for Emperor Palpatine in the [[2017]] video game ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'', in which Palpatine appears as a [[w:c:battlefront:Heroes and Villains|hero-class character]]. In game, the ability would slowly damage enemies and slow their movement, but this was later changed in future updates. The ability also appears as a dark cloud around Palpatine. As the ability has only appeared in the form of an in-game ability at this time, the canonicity of these effects is unknown.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
