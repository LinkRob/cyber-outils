The '''Eirriss family''' was a family that operated the arms manufacturer [[Eirriss Ryloth Defense Tech]] and were supporters of the [[Twi'lek Resistance]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Battle of Ryloth|occupation of Ryloth]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. After the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] the Eirriss family continued to support Twi'lek Resistance leader [[Cham Syndulla]] and his continuing [[Free Ryloth Movement]] to free the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] from Imperial oppression. Consequently, weapons manufactured by the family's company, such as the [[Blurrg-1120 holdout blaster]], were symbols of rebellion against the empire.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Eirriss family was first mentioned in the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The family and the company they operate share their name with the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] character [[Cesi Eirriss]], who first appeared in the [[1997]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] one-shot [[X-Wing Rogue Squadron ½|''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' ½]] by [[Michael A. Stackpole]]. Eirriss was a [[Twi'lek/Legends|Twi'lek]] [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]] who flew with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="X-Wing Rogue Squadron">[[X-Wing Rogue Squadron ½|''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' ½]]</ref>
