A '''Banchii survivalist belt''' was a type of resistant and insulated [[belt]] that was made on<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Banchii]]. The [[Clothing|belt]] was [[Color|brown]] and connected in the front by a silver-colored buckle. On the right side it had a [[holster]] that could carry a [[blaster pistol]], and the front had holders for [[blaster module]]s. The back of the belt had holders for [[bacta vial]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a Banchii survivalist belt was for sale in [[Wesla's]], a shop on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Kijimi]] that was operated by the [[Twi'lek]] [[tailor]] [[Lani Wesla]]. A [[Banchii survivalist jacket]] and [[Banchii survivalist pants]] were also stocked by Wesla.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] purchased a Banchii survivalist belt from Wesla for 700 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. While wearing the belt, Vess attached her [[Hintaro grappling hook]] to it.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A Banchii survivalist belt appeared in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', a [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The belt allows the player to carry an additional Bacta Vial when equipped.<ref name="Outlaws" />
