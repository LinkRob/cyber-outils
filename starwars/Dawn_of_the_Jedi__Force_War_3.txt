'''''Dawn of the Jedi: Force War'' 3''' is the third issue in ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force War]]'', the third story arc miniseries of the ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series by [[John Ostrander]] and [[Jan Duursema]]. ''Force War 3'' was published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[January 15]], [[2014]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Je'daii generals Daegen Lok and Xesh are captured by Xesh's former Rakatan masters, and Xesh learns the truth behind his arrival on Tython. Half-mad with regret, he is forced to give the invaders the knowledge they need to defeat Xesh's Je'daii friends&mdash;and enslave the entire planet!''
