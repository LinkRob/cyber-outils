'''Coruscant: Center of the Empire''' was a four-part online supplement released by [[Wizards of the Coast]] for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''. It was written by [[Cory J. Herndon]] and published on [[Wizards.com]] in April [[2003]] as part of the ''[[Planet Hoppers]]'' series.

==Contents==
===Part 1: Bright Lights, Very Big City===
''&hellip;a promising young [[Vune Willic|officer]] visits [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] for the first time and is inducted into the [[Imperial Intelligence/Legends|Intelligence]] division.''<ref name="Coruscant" />

===Part 2: Heart of Ice===
''&hellip;an [[Imperial Security Bureau/Legends|ISB]] recruit meets his new boss, [[Director of Imperial Intelligence|Intelligence Director]] [[Ysanne Isard]].''<ref name="Coruscant" />

===Part 3: Dangerous Pawns===
''&hellip;insurgents carry out an [[Assassin/Legends|assassination]] plot against the [[Palpatine/Legends|Emperor]] himself -- a plot that comes disturbingly close to success.''<ref name="Coruscant" />

===Part 4: Eyes of Red and Blue===
''&hellip;the new [[YI-5 Surveillance/Interrogation droid|YI-5 surveillance/interrogation droid]] gets a test run on an unwilling subject.''<ref name="Coruscant" />
