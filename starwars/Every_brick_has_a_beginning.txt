"'''Every brick has a beginning'''" is the tenth in a series of comics on the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] website. It is the conclusion to the story arc started in [[Always trust Secret Strategy 3|"Always trust Secret Strategy #3"]]. LEGO first placed the comic on their Star Wars website in [[March]] [[2009]]. It was later added to the [[StarWars.com|official Star Wars site]] on [[October 23]], 2009.

==Publisher's summary==
''"And every webcomic has a finale!"<ref name="StarWars.com">{{SWArchive|url=video/view/000893.html|text=LEGO Webcomic #10: Every brick has a beginning...}}</ref>''

==Plot summary==
"Every brick has a beginning"

The comic begins with the two [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry/Legends|gunships]], the [[Twilight/Legends|''Twilight'']], and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker's]] [[Anakin Skywalker's Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor/Legends|personal starfighter]] crashed on [[Felucia/Legends|Felucia]]. [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] along with some [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]] disembark form one of the gunships while [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] and [[Ahsoka Tano/Legends|Ahsoka Tano]] get out of the starfighter and the ''Twilight'' respectively. Skywalker is soon heard by the group calling down from a rancor skeleton's rib. [[Asajj Ventress/Legends|Asajj Ventress]] arrives at that time and cuts through the rib holding up Anakin with her [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]]. She then orders an army of [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]] to attack the group. Obi-Wan, Anakin, and Ahsoka are joined by [[Clone Commander/Legends|Commander]] [[Cody/Legends|Cody]] and his troops and together they charge at the droids. The party does well against the battle droids but they are halted by a [[OG-9 homing spider droid/Legends|homing spider droid]]. Suddenly, [[Plo Koon/Legends|Plo Koon]] arrives with the clone troops from the second gunship. Plo immediately jumps upon the spider droid and stabs it with his lightsaber, bringing the droid down. With the battle won, Plo Koon is hailed as a hero. Later, Anakin and Ahsoka find [[Rotta/Legends|Rotta]] in the ''Twilight'' while one clone is given medical care. Meanwhile, Cody informs Kenobi that there is no sign of Ventress as a [[Nu-class attack shuttle/Legends|''Nu''-class attack shuttle]] sets down. All then gather as Koon receives a medal and [[Fireworks/Legends|fireworks]] light up the sky. Elsewhere, on an unknown ship vessel, Ventress informs [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]] via hologram that the Death Brick is safe. Dooku responds stating the Jedi do not know of its importance. The Death Brick is then shown to be one of many used in the construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]].

"THE END...?"

==Continuity==
In the course of the comic, Ahsoka and Plo Koon's lightsabers switch between having a blue blade and having a green blade.
