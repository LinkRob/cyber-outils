The '''Fringe Territories''' was a [[Sector/Legends|sector]] of [[Realspace/Legends|space]].<ref name="Twinkle" />

==Behind the scenes==

{{Quote|The Fringe Territories was named as a sector for the often mentioned "fringe of Imperial space" and placed to provide a counterbalance to the Outer Rim Territories.|[[Glen Allison]]|<ref name="Twinkle" />}}
Glen Allison created the Fringe Territories sector as a counterbalance to his Outer Rim Territory sector. In ''[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]]'' however, the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]] is a region not sector.<ref name="GG1">''[[Galaxy Guide 1: A New Hope]]''</ref><ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> Early [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] works applied the demonyms "[[Fringer/Legends|fringer]]", to denizens of the [[The fringe|Fringe]], and "[[Rimmer/Legends|rimmer]]" to those of the [[Rim]], which included the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]]. Other than [[Branth system]], all other systems in this sector have since been placed elsewhere by the ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''.<ref name="TEA" />
