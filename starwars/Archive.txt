The '''Archive''' was the most secure and valuable building on the [[planet]] [[Vardos]]. It was located in the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Kestro City|Kestro]], to where all the roads in the city led.<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> At its base was a statue of [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]] who was responsible for aligning the planet with the [[Galactic Empire]], and was named the "Hero of Vardos."<ref name="Battlefront II" />
