A '''blumbush''' was a bush that grew on [[Ohma-D'un/Legends|Ohma-D'un]]. It produced orange fruits called [[Blumfruit/Legends|blumfruit]]. The fruit was often used by the [[Gungan/Legends|Gungans]] to produce a fermented juice that was used as an adhesive and also as a disinfectant.
