A '''AT-ST puppet''' was a toy puppet version of an [[All Terrain Scout Transport]] that was used by children on<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|at-st-puppet|AT-ST puppet}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Mina-Rau]].<ref name="General Store">{{Databank|mina-rau-general-store|Mina-Rau General Store}}</ref> While [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] AT-STs are viewed as terrors in other areas of [[the galaxy]], the lack of Imperial oversight on Mina-Rau led them to being used for toys in [[4 BBY]]. At least one of these puppets was at a communal gathering between local [[farmer]]s and [[Technician|mechanics]] in 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], where it was played with by a [[human]] [[Unidentified AT-ST kid|kid]].<ref name="Harvest" />

==Behind the scenes==
An AT-ST puppet appeared in the episode "[[Harvest]]" of the [[Disney+]] live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' released on [[April 22]], [[2025]].<ref name="Harvest" /> An entry about the puppet was added to the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] site on the same.<ref name="Databank" /> On [[April 24]], [[StarWars.com]] posted an article titled "Fight the Empire with an ''Andor''-Inspired AT-ST Puppet" which detailed how fans could create their own version.<ref name="Craft puppet AT-ST Puppet">{{SW|url=news/andor-craft-at-st-puppet|text=Fight the Empire with an ''Andor''-Inspired AT-ST Puppet}}</ref>
