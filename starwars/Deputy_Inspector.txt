{{Quote|I was a good Deputy Inspector! I was very good. I solved a double murder and found the killer in two days. I was overly ambitious, yes, but time was slipping away and the opportunity was real.|Syril Karn|<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref>}}
'''Deputy Inspector''' was a title used by an [[officer]] in the [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation]], serving as part of the [[Pre-Mor Security Inspection team]] for the [[Morlani system]]. [[Syril Karn]] was a deputy inspector during the [[Imperial Era]], and defied orders from [[Chief Inspector]] [[Hyne]] regarding a case of two [[Death|murdered]] employees.<ref name="Episode 1" /> Deputy Inspector Karn led a contingent of twelve [[Tac-Corpo|Corpos]] and [[Sergeant]] [[Linus Mosk]] in an [[ambush on Ferrix]].<ref name="Episode 3">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref>
