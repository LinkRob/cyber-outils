'''Duraslug Rolls''' were a type of savory [[sandwich]] made using the [[Foodstuff|meat]] of the [[duracrete slug]]. Occupants of the [[Core Worlds]] [[ecumenopolis]] [[Coruscant]]'s [[Coruscant Underworld|lower levels]] originally [[Diet|ate]] the [[slug]]s out of necessity, but after [[Coruscanti|citizens]] living in the [[planet]]'s upper levels quickly learned of the creature's culinary uses, they created the Duraslug Roll, which became popular with denizens of both Coruscant's upper and lower levels.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

After making a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for Duraslug Rolls in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Duraslug Rolls were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the rolls makes use of [[Wikipedia:Mayonnaise|mayonnaise]], [[Wikipedia:Lemon#Juice|lemon juice]] [[Wikipedia:Basil|basil]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic salt|garlic salt]], [[Wikipedia:Celery#Seeds|celery seeds]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], [[Wikipedia:Lobster#As food|lobster meat]], [[Wikipedia:Brioche|brioche buns]], [[Wikipedia:Butter|unsalted butter]], chopped [[Wikipedia:Chives|chives]], and [[Wikipedia:Lemon|lemon wedges]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
