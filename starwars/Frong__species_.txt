The '''Frong''' were a [[species]] hailing from a [[planet]] [[Frong|of the same name]]. Tightly-knit and clannish, the Frong had little to offer [[the galaxy]], although looked to the [[New Republic]] and criminal organizations such as [[Black Sun]] and the [[Red Key Raiders]] for protection during the events leading to the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Biology and appearance==
The Frong had face-tubules which trembled and twitched in stressful situations, and curled inwards with fear. They had big, glossy [[Color|black]] eyes, and long, blue forearms lined with contracting suckers. The species was capable of speaking [[Galactic Basic Standard]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Society and culture==
The Frong hailed from the [[planet]] [[Frong]], located in a fringe [[star system]] orbiting a dim [[star]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The [[species]] was insular and clannish, and remained tightly-knit through bloodlines that verged on the incestuous. The Frong became intimately familiar with concepts such as shame and exile.<ref name="Empire's End" />

The Frong had little to offer the wider galaxy except for [[fruit]], [[spice]], and clean water. The species had no navy or defense fleet, and looked to governments such as the [[New Republic]] for protection.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==History==
{{Quote|Th-the New Republic hasn't yet secured the Outer Rim. Frong is v-vulnerable. By giving my vote, I'm earning protection for my planet and my people.|Senator Rethalow|<ref name="Empire's End" />}}
During the [[High Republic Era]], a Frong named [[Xanaven]] was a [[Tempest Runner]] of the [[Nihil]].<ref name="The High Republic: Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref>

[[Senator]] [[Rethalow]] represented his [[homeworld]] in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] of the New Republic during the lead-up to the [[Battle of Jakku]]. He entered into a trade deal with [[Black Sun]] and the [[Red Key Raiders]], giving over his vote in the decision to attack the [[Galactic Empire]] at [[Jakku]] in order to protect his people. He was kidnapped from a [[deprivation chamber]] in [[Quarrow]]'s [[poma-club]] by [[Jom Barell]], and interrogated aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' by [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] and his cohorts.<ref name="Empire's End" />
