The '''Dug Racer''' was a costume owned by [[Dizzy (Ugnaught)|Dizzy]], the [[Ugnaught]] member of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]], that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Wild Racer|standard costume]]. It was intended to resemble the racing uniform commonly worn by the [[Dug]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Sebulba]], with a color scheme consisting of varying shades of gray, including striped components, as well as a [[Hat|cap]] and circular [[goggles]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dug Racer was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dug Paintwork]].<ref name="Hunters" />
