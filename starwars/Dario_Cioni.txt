'''Dario Cioni''' was the second assistant director for the Italian crew during the filming of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].

Other notable work of Dario's includes Megiddo: The Omega Code Two[http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0263728/], on which he was the first assistant director.
