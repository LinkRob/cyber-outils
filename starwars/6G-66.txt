'''6G-66''', nicknamed "'''Sixer'''," was an [[astromech droid]] in service to the [[Resistance]] during its [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] with the [[First Order]]. 6G-66 flew in the [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] of [[Jessika Pava]] during [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron's]] [[Mission to the Outer Rim|mission]] to gather allies and bring new [[Star system|systems]] into the Resistance. Though Pava noted Sixer was less than thrilled about it,<ref name="Poe Dameron 29" /> due to Pava being known as the "Great Destroyer" by the astromechs at the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]],<ref name="Poe Dameron 20">[[Poe Dameron 20|''Poe Dameron'' 20]]</ref> she added there was no way she would fly the mission without an onboard astromech as it was too important.<ref name="Poe Dameron 29" />
