'''Ben Zweifel''' is an artist who has provided illustrations in some capacity for every ''[[Star Wars]]''&ndash;related game produced by [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. When Fantasy Flight Games developed the [[Raider-class corvette/Legends|''Raider''-class corvette]] for the [[Star Wars: X-Wing Miniatures Game|''X-Wing Miniatures Game'']], Zweifel was commissioned to design the look of the brand-new starship.<ref>{{Twitter|pixelgarbage|status/546007375619297281|[[Ben Zweifel]]|quote=Box art for the new Imperial Raider that I designed! It was just announced.|archivefile=File:Ben Zweifel Raider Creator.png}}</ref>
