'''Borgin Castle''' was a location on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Serenno]].<ref name="BMF 702" /> It shared its name with [[House Borgin]], one of the seven [[Great Houses]] of Serenno.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
This location was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 70]] Magazine.<ref name="BMF 702" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Borgin Castle/Legends|Borgin Castle]] first appeared in the [[Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets 2|second issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets|Hard Targets]]'' story arc in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]] ''[[Star Wars: Agent of the Empire]]'', written by [[John Ostrander]].<ref name="Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets 2">[[Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets 2|''Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets'' 2]]</ref> The name "Borgin Castle" was first coined for the ''Star Wars'' Legends location by [[Wookieepedia]].<ref name="Wook">{{WookCite|url=Borgin_Castle/Legends?oldid=5003968|text=Borgin Castle}}</ref>
