'''Domir''' was the [[Star|sun]] of the [[Dathomir system]] in the [[Quelii sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]]. The star was orbited by the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]] and its 4 [[moon]]s.<ref name="BMF 49" /> The sun was a [[red dwarf]]<ref name="Battle Scars">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]''</ref> and produced [[Color|red]] sunlight.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The sun of Dathomir first appeared in "[[Revenge (episode)|Revenge]],"<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> the twenty-second episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated television series, which first aired on [[March 14]], [[2012]].<ref name="TCWEG">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref>

In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the sun's name was revealed in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 49|fourty-ninth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine, published by [[De Agostini]] on [[December 9]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF 49" /> In ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' the sun's name was first revealed in
''[[Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds]]'', a sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] on [[March 24]], [[2004]].<ref name="GatORW">''[[Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds]]''</ref>
