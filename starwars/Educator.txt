An '''educator''' was a position the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] once sought.<ref name="Beaumont Kin DB" /> However, Kin&mdash;who was deemed "useless" by his [[parent]]s<ref name="SW Adventures 27">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=27|story=Ghosts of Kashyyyk, Part 1}}</ref> though was to become one of the youngest professors at [[Lerct Historical Institute]] after studying at [[Lerct]]'s [[Arhul Hextrophon School of Embedded Journalism]]<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>&mdash;abandoned this career route when the [[Hosnian system]] was [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroyed]]. Instead, the [[Scholar|academic]] joined the ranks of the [[Resistance]], putting his knowledge to use on the field<ref name="Beaumont Kin DB" /> against the [[First Order]] and the [[Sith]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
