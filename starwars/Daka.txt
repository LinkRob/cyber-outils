'''Daka''', also known as '''Old Daka''', was an elderly [[Gender|female]] [[Dathomirian]] [[witch]] and member of the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Dathomiri clan|coven]] of witches who lived in the [[Nightsister lair]] on her [[homeworld]] [[Dathomir]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The oldest and wisest of the Nightsisters, her vast knowledge of arcane [[magick]]s was unprecedented. As the elder of [[Mother Talzin's clan|her clan]], Daka passed down her expertise to the other Nightsisters. This lended her the reputation of being the most powerful member of her clan. The clan's leader, [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]], regarded Daka as so in particular.

One such ability Daka possessed was the ability to [[Chant of Resurrection|resurrect]] the [[Death|dead]]. The skill was notably used during the [[Battle of Dathomir]], where the sisters stood against the vengeful [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The sisterhood's victory was not to be, however, as Daka was stabbed by one of [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander of the Droid Army]] [[General]] [[Grievous]]' [[lightsaber]]s. Before her death however, Daka was able to direct the distressed witch of no clan [[Falta]] to the location of her daughter, who had previously joined the Nightsisters.

==Biography==
Born on the<ref name="SWE" /> [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Dathomir]],<ref name="SWE" /> Daka served as a member of the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Dathomiri clan|coven]] of [[Dathomirian]] [[witch]]es.<ref name="Massacre" /> The oldest and wisest,<ref name="SWE" /> Daka served as an elder in [[Mother Talzin's clan|her clan]]<ref name="BTCTG">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> as well as a healer.<ref name="GOH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> As an elder, Daka passed down spellcraft and had a vast knowledge on the history of the clan.<ref name="BTCTG" />

===Battle of Dathomir===
{{Quote|Choono slalem denni tay'lori olee-ay. Lucheno vadem klavlane. Blenay vedi nalem koreem. Blenay vedi nalem koreem. Villos susko kono lamal! Vlemon tagoo!|Old Daka reciting the Chant of Ressurection|audio=Chant_of_Resurrection.flac|<ref name="Massacre" />}}
[[File:Ancient_Magicks_SoH.png|thumb|left|200px|Old Daka performing ancient magicks]]
In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> in retaliation of reborn Nightsister [[Asajj Ventress]]' betrayal and plots against him, the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] leader [[Count]] [[Dooku]] dispatched<ref name="Massacre" /> the [[Kaleesh]] [[warlord]]<ref name="GrievousSWE">{{SWE|characters|generalgrievous|General Grievous}}</ref> [[General]] [[Grievous]] and the [[Separatist Droid Army]] to completely wipe out the Nightsisters so they could prove no further threat. When Separatist forces landed and the battle began, Ventress declared to leader [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] that the Nightsisters fighting at the frontier would need reinforcements to survive the onslaught brought on by the [[droid]] forces.<ref name="Massacre" />

Listening to Ventress, Talzin journeyed to the [[Hidden Cave]], a hidden room in the [[Nightsister lair]] which could be accessed through the use of [[magick]],<ref name="Massacre" /> a use of [[the Force]] exclusive to the witches of Dathomir.<ref name="Bug">{{StoryCite|story=Bug (short story)|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref> Talzin came before Old Daka who agreed to use a spell to unleash the [[Nightsister Zombie|undead Nightsisters']] [[Army of the Dead]] to help fight the droids. Daka began to enchant and recite the [[Chant of Resurrection]], a spell which would raise the [[Death|undead]]. When the undead army awoke and crawled out of their [[burial pod]]s, they aided the living Nightsisters in fighting the droids. However, despite the support from the [[zombie]]s, the Nightsisters were outmatched by the droid army, which pushed forward.<ref name="Massacre" />

[[File:DeathOldDaka.jpg|thumb|right|250px|General Grievous driving one of his lightsabers into Daka's chest, wounding her.]]
Dooku sent Grievous directly to Talzin in order to kill her before she could complete a [[voodoo]] spell to take the Count's life. Following the source of the magick, Grievous made his way through the fortress, and had a [[BX-series droid commando]] blast through the walls of the Hidden Cave, giving him easy access. Daka was helpless to stop the General, who roughly grabbed her and drove one of his [[lightsaber]]s into her chest, grievously injuring her, and flinging her aside. With Daka's spell broken, the undead army fell to ruin, leading to the swift extermination of the rest of the Nightsisters by overwhelming droid forces.<ref name="Massacre" />

===Death and legacy===
{{Quote|Safe. Further. Off-world|Daka to Falta in her dying breaths|<ref name="Bug" />}}
After the battle ended, [[Falta]], a witch of no clan, arrived to search for her daughter [[Yenna]] who had joind the Nightsisters long before the battle. As Falta entered the Hidden Cave, she noticed Daka lying in the corner. As she was about to lay the old witch to rest, Daka's [[eye]]s opened, having not been dead yet but only unconscious. Before passing away, Daka told her that Yenna had been sent off-world in an [[escape pod]]. This began Falta's search for her daughter, which eventually brought her and her pet [[tooka]], [[Ichor]], to the [[planetoid]] [[Sídi]] where she met the [[Youngling|young]] girl known as "[[Bug (Sídi)|Bug]]." When telling "Bug" about her story, Falta mentioned her encounter with Daka.<ref name="Bug" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Daka, as the oldest and wisest of us, I need you to resurrect our fallen sisters.|Talzin|<ref name="Massacre" />}}
Daka was the wisest of the Nightsisters,<ref name="Massacre" /> and her knowledge of the arcane magicks was unprecedented.<ref name="SWE" /><ref name="Databank">{{Databank|old-daka|Old Daka}}</ref><ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Daka was skilled in spiritual guidance and leadership.<ref name="JtB" />

Daka stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s tall, the equivalent of 6 [[Foot|feet]],<ref name="JtB" /> and had a bent body shape.<ref name="CompleteS4" /> Daka had a deep, raspy voice, and when reciting spells, she spoke in the [[Dathomirian language]]. She had white,<ref name="Massacre" /> [[braid]]ed hair<ref name="CompleteS4">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Four|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Four]]</ref> with red streaks in it, silver eyes,<ref name="Massacre" /> and [[Color|purple]] [[tattoo]]s with aged, mottled skin and shrunken eyes.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref><ref name="Massacre" />

==Powers and abilities==
{{Quote|Ventress will need the aid of the undead army to achieve victory.''"<br />"''Then I will begin the Chant of Ressurection.|Talzin and Daka|<ref name="Massacre" />}}
Daka's mastery of the Nightsister powers was without peer toward the end of her life, as was her knowledge of the clan's abilities. Keeper of the Nightsisters' most dangerous lore, Daka could even perform the Chant of Resurrection, which granted her the ability to raise the dead. Mother Talzin herself could not go about performing the spell, seeking Daka's powers instead.<ref name="Massacre" /> By 20 BBY,<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Daka was the most powerful and mighty of the Nightsister clan, reflected in her status as the clan Elder.<ref name="Dathomir SWE">{{SWE|locations|dathomir|Dathomir}}</ref> Daka was also able to [[Force vision|see into the future]] and acted as a spiritual guide to her clanswomen.<ref name="JtB" />

==Equipment==
When using the Chant of Resurrection<ref name="Massacre" /> in 20 BBY,<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Daka completed the spell using a [[Witches' Orb]]<ref name="Massacre" /> and the [[Talisman of Resurrection]].<ref name="Talisman">{{Destiny|set=Spark of Hope|cardname=Talisman of Resurrection|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/11019}}</ref> Daka wore a headdress of [[bone]]s<ref name="CompleteS4" /> and a [[nightsister robes|decorative garment]] of various colors such as red, purple, and black.<ref name="Massacre" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Daka-FirstConcept.png|thumb|right|250px|Andre Kirk's first illustration of Daka with six variant heads]]
Daka first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|season four]] episode "[[Massacre]]," released on [[February 24]], [[2012]].<ref name="TCWEG">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> She was voiced by actress [[Kathleen Gati]] in the episode.<ref name="Massacre" /> Concept art for Daka was drawn by [[Andre Kirk]] and went through at least six head variations of different shapes and headresses. In the final model for Daka, her mouth was sourced from Talzin's model in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]] episode "[[Nightsisters (episode)|Nightsisters]]," and her fingers were sourced from that of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith/Legends|Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]] with changes in the texture and nail length.<ref name="CompleteS4" />
