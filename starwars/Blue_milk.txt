'''Blue milk''',<ref name="DiD-book">[[Droids in Distress (book)|''Droids in Distress'' chapter book]]</ref> also known as '''bantha milk''',<ref name="TRB">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref> was a rich [[Color|blue]]-colored [[milk]] produced by [[Gender|female]] [[bantha]]s. [[Sentience|Sentients]] drank it,<ref name="AbEverything" /> and also used it in [[bantha butter]],<ref name="BWDHE" /> [[blue bantha buttermilk biscuit]]s,<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> [[blue-milk cheese]],<ref name="Beru Whitesun Lars" /> [[blue milk custard]], [[ice cream]], and [[yogurt]].<ref name="AbEverything" /> It was notably available on [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]s such as [[Batuu]],<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" /> [[Lah'mu]],<ref name="Rogue One" /> [[Lothal]],<ref name="Rise of the Old Masters" /> and [[Tatooine]].<ref name="ANH" /> [[Cid's Parlor]] on [[Ord Mantell]] served a similar beverage that was alcoholic called [[Dark Blue Milk]].<ref name="TBB Rampage">{{TBB|Rampage (episode)}}</ref>

During a [[lightsaber]] training session, the young [[Ezra Bridger]] had to protect himself as the [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] kept lobbing cartons of blue milk at him.<ref name="DiD-book" /> [[Luke Skywalker]] drank blue milk while he discussed the new droids, [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]], with his aunt and uncle.<ref name="ANH" />

Two establishments in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]], served blue milk or blue milk-based concoctions; [[Milk Stand]] sold blue milk and an [[alcohol]]ic [[Blue Milk Cooler]],<ref name="Milk Stand" /> while [[Oga's Cantina]] served the non-alcoholic [[Blue Bantha]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BlueMilkAd.jpg|thumb|left|200px|An advertisement for blue milk. The [[Aurebesh]] text translates as "Blue milk" : "At markets everywhere".]]
Blue milk first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], the first installment in the [[original trilogy]] of ''[[Star Wars]]''.<ref name="ANH" /> Initially, one scene was to have featured [[Beru Whitesun Lars|Beru Lars]], played by [[Shelagh Fraser]], pouring blue milk into a jug in her kitchen. That footage, however, was left on the cutting room floor.<ref name="ANH cut">{{Film|IV}}, deleted scene featured on the Blu-ray edition.</ref> [[Mark Hamill]] stated the blue milk was "[[Wikipedia:Ultra-high-temperature processing|life-long milk]]" with "additives – they put blue food coloring in it – and it was really ghastly. Oily and sweet and euch! Triggered your gag reflex. But I said, 'Look – if they gave me blue milk, you bet I'm going to drink it on camera, because what other chance am I going to get?' So there's an indication that I'm an underrated actor – I gulped it and acted like I liked it without vomiting."<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.radiotimes.com/news/film/2018-04-04/star-wars-last-jedi-mark-hamill-luke-skywalker-green-milk-carrie-fisher-best-moments|text=Mark Hamill teases Luke Skywalker's future after Star Wars The Last Jedi &ndash; and reveals what green milk really tastes like|author=Fullerton, Huw|date=2018-04-04|work=''[[Wikipedia:Radio Times|Radio Times]]''|archivedate=20180415165200}}</ref>

The anthology novel ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]'', celebrating the 40th anniversary of ''A New Hope'', was first teased on social media on [[April 6]], [[2017]], using the hashtag #OperationBlueMilk.<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-from-a-certain-point-of-view-anthology-book-celebrates-40-years-of-a-galaxy-far-far-away|text=''Star Wars: From a Certain Point of View'' Anthology Book Celebrates 40 Years of A Galaxy Far, Far Away}}</ref> The [[In-universe|real-world]] Milk Stand found at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] serves "blue milk", which is actually a frozen plant-based blend of coconut and rice milks that the menu describes as having "alluring fruity characteristics". The [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] location additionally serves an [[alcohol]]ic Blue Milk Cooler.<ref name="Milk Stand" /> The real-world Oga's Cantina at both parks serves Blue Bantha, a non-alcoholic cocktail with a cookie.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />

In the real world, naturally occurring blue items are rarely edible. Writer [[Melissa T. Miller]] dedicated the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' article "[[Got Blue Milk?]]" to analyzing blue ''Star Wars'' items, such as blue milk, and their lack of real world counterparts. In the piece, Miller theorized that blue milk got its color either from its refining process or, drawing on how real world milk can change in hue based on the producer's diet, something eaten by banthas that affected the coloration. Miller specifically pondered if fields of blue wildflowers existed amongst the Tatooine sands.<ref name="Got Blue">{{InsiderCite|227|Got Blue Milk?}}</ref> The [[2020]] [[Canon|non-canon]] video game expansion ''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]'' includes blue milk as a drink sold to the player's [[Wikipedia:The Sims|Sims]] at the Milk Stand vendor in the Black Spire Outpost neighborhood. In the home neighborhood, it can be purchased as a quick meal from refrigerators.<ref name="Sims">''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]''</ref>
