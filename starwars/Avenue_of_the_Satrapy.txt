The '''Avenue of the Satrapy''' was a street near [[Satrap]] [[Isstra Dirus]]' [[Satrap's Palace|palace]] in [[Myrra]], the [[capital]] of [[Akiva]]. During the [[rebellion on Akiva]], a large crowd of citizens marched on the street and proceeded to storm the palace after learning about the [[Imperial Future Council]]'s [[Akiva Summit|presence]] there. Later, [[Temmin Wexley]] was saved from being thrown to his [[death]] from the palace by the same crowd.<ref name="Aftermath" />
