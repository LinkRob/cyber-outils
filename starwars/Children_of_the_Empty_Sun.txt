The '''Children of the Empty Sun''' were a cult that operated on the [[planet]] [[Catadra]] in the [[Cerberon system]], led by [[Let'ij]]. After being stranded in [[Realspace|space]] near Catadra during the [[New Republic]]'s [[Cerberon system campaign|campaign]] to take the Cerberon [[Star system|system]], [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] [[Chass na Chadic]] was rescued by members of the cult. Chadic decided to join the cult, suspecting they had another New Republic individual. She was incorrect, however; the cult had in fact rescued the [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|Imperial pilot]] [[Palal Seedia]]. Chadic and Seedia begrudgingly worked together in effort to escape Catadra, locating the cult's [[starship]]s. Seedia turned on the New Republic pilot and left her behind, though Chadic was able to recover her [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing starfighter]] and escape.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
