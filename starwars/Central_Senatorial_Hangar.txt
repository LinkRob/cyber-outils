The '''Central Senatorial Hangar''' was a large [[hangar]] in [[Imperial City|Galactic City]] on [[Coruscant]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Upon reaching the [[ecumenopolis]] in the aftermath of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], [[Affie Hollow]] tracked down her adoptive [[Parent|mother]], [[Scover Byne]], onboard the [[starship]] ''[[Spiderspun]]'' while it was landed at the hangar.<ref name="ITD" />
