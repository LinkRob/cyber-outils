'''Command Code EBB Vortex Nine''' was a command code utilized to stand down the [[Mon Cala planetary defense services]]. During the [[invasion of Mon Cala]], after learning that he was deceived by [[Ferren Barr]] into leading the [[Mon Calamari monarchy|monarchy]] into open rebellion against the [[Galactic Empire]], [[King of Mon Cala|King]] [[Lee-Char]] implemented Command Code EBB Vortex Nine to order all of his forces to stand down.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />

[[Admiral]] [[Raddus]], upon receiving the order, believed that they could not give up on their efforts, and ignored the command, ordering the [[Mon Cala Mercantile Fleet]] to de-link and escape the world.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />
