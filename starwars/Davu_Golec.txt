'''Davu Golec''' was a [[Mandalorian]]<ref name="Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/duchess-of-mandalore-episode-gallery|text="Duchess of Mandalore" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 12}}</ref> [[human]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and worked in the [[Galactic Republic|Republic's]] [[Republic Ministry of Intelligence|Ministry of Intelligence]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. He was a close friend and associate of [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]], leader of the [[New Mandalorians]] on [[Mandalore]], during the years before and during the Clone Wars. When Duchess Kryze journeyed to Coruscant in order to argue against the Republic's occupation plans for Mandalore, Golec discovered the testimony from [[Deputy Minister]] [[Jerec]]. The testimony stated that the request that the Republic send troops had been falsified. Risking his life and career in order to bring the full version of the testimony to the Duchess, Golec met with her in a shadowy alley on Coruscant.<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" />

However, Kryze had been followed to Coruscant by a [[Unidentified Death Watch assassin|member]] of the radical Mandalorian splinter group, [[Death Watch]]. When the Death Watch [[assassin]] found Kryze meeting with Golec in [[Sector GL5]], he fired a shot from his [[sniper rifle]], killing Golec. Kryze pulled her blaster to fire back, but was then forced to flee the scene, pursued by the [[Coruscant Security Force|Coruscant Police]] and [[Coruscant Guard]] [[Imperial shock trooper|shock troopers]], who believed she had killed Golec. Satine was able to meet with [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in secret and gave him the disk, to be given to [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. Kryze turned herself in to the [[Senate Guard]] while Amidala delivered Deputy Minister Jerec's true message to the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]]. Kryze was vindicated of Golec's murder, and the disk he had provided to Kryze convinced the Senate to vote against sending troops to Mandalore.<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" />
