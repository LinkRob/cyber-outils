"'''A Mon Alone'''" was an adventure for the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] roleplaying campaign. It was written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]].

==Plot summary==
The adventure included a critical event summary that could be sent to the [[RPGA]] before [[September 30]], [[2004]] after being delayed from june 17, 2004 . The summary was to include the final fate of several characters. Following the ''Living Force'' politics, the most popular answers would become [[Star Wars Legends|canon]]. For example, if 50% of the returned event summaries included "[[Phylus Mon]] escaped," then Phylus Mon would be considered at large for further supplements.

This two-round adventure was considerably longer than other Living Force adventures. It proposed the use of soundtracks from [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (soundtrack)|''The Phantom Menace'']] during the first round and [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones (soundtrack)|''Attack of the Clones'']] during the second round as background music.

The adventure used specific rules, including:
*A payment system for qualified, professional characters. Also, a loot summary including the items that characters can keep for further adventures.
*Rules about [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side]] and [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] rage for Wookiee [[The Force/Legends|Force]] users.
*Rules for possession, so that the player characters can possess the "empty" body of a non-player character who has been stripped of his or her "Force essence."
