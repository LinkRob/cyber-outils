The '''Ashas Ree outpost'''<ref name="Guide">{{SW|url=the-relic-raider-concept-art-gallery|text="The Relic Raider" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> was the only populated location on the [[planet]] [[Ashas Ree]]. It was situated next to a [[Jedi]] [[Ashas Ree Temple of the Force|temple of the Force]]. When the ancient [[Sith]] [[Ashas Ree Sith temple|temple]] that had been buried beneath the Jedi temple was raised by [[Mika Grey]], the outpost's population fled, leaving behind messages warning visitors to beware of the ancient evil. The messages were discovered by [[Torra Doza]], [[Freya Fenris]] and [[Kazuda Xiono]] of the [[Colossus]], who had [[Mission to Ashas Ree|visited]] in search of supplies for the station.<ref name="The Relic Raiders" />
