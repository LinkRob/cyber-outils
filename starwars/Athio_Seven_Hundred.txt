{{Quote|You know, you could watch podracing with me instead, I won't tell the Jedi Council. The Athio Seven Hundred is coming up.|Anakin Skywalker, to Obi-Wan Kenobi via comlink|<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref>}}
The '''Athio Seven Hundred''' was a [[Podracing|podrace]]<ref name="Brotherhood" /> held in the vicinity of<ref name="Battlenamia" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Athio system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was held during or after [[22 BBY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]&mdash;who was on a [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Kenobi and Skywalker)|mission]] on the [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]]&mdash;revealed to [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] via [[comlink]] that he had been working with the [[information broker]] [[Dexter Jettster]]. Skywalker replied that Kenobi should instead spend his free [[time]] watching podracing with him, noting that the Athio Seven Hundred was soon and promising not to tell the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Athio Seven Hundred was mentioned in the [[2022]] novel [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']], written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />
