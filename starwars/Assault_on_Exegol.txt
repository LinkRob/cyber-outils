The '''assault on Exegol''',<ref name="Darth Vader 47 solicit">{{WebCite|url=https://aiptcomics.com/2024/03/21/full-june-2024-marvel-comics-solicitations|text=Full June 2024 Marvel Comics solicitations: Ultimates #1 and more Blood Hunt|archivedate=20240321170054}}</ref> otherwise known as the '''razing of Exegol''', was carried out<ref name="Darth Vader 46 solicit" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> by the [[Schism Imperial]] when they attempted to challenge [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] power by attacking the [[Sith Citadel]] on [[Exegol]] where he kept his greatest treasures and secrets. The attack was the Schism and Vader's boldest challenge to Palpatine's rule.<ref name="Darth Vader 46 solicit" />

==Behind the scenes==
