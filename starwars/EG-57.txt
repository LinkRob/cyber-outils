'''EG-57''' was a [[Color|green]]-plated [[GNK-series power droid]] manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]]<ref name="Datapad" /> that in [[10 BBY]]<ref name="SoloDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Raid on Kessel|uprising]] on [[Kessel]] to [[10 BBY]].</ref> witnessed a [[Raid on Kessel|slave and droid uprising]]<ref name="Datapad" /> in one of the [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> During the event it stored an internal data record consisting of an image of the [[Mining|mine's]] control room, showing the freed [[droid]]s<ref name="Datapad" /> [[P6B-LT2]], [[P1-SZE]], [[C00-L05C]], [[P4T-GM]], [[S1D6-SA-5]],<ref name="SoloOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> and [[DV-4D4]].<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2018 Topps Solo: A Star Wars Story|cardname=DV-4D4 - 27|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/162389/cid/11326516/2018-Topps-Solo:-A-Star-Wars-Story-27-DV-4D4}}</ref>

By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> EG-57 had come to serve in the [[Resistance]],<ref name="Datapad" /> and was part of its forces stationed on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] during the world's [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] by the [[First Order]]. EG-57 worked in [[Resistance Supply]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]],<ref name="RS">{{GEAttr|Resistance Supply}}</ref> where it stood storing power for the Resistance next to a long-range [[antenna]] with fellow power droid [[EG-58]].<ref name="Datapad" />

==Behind the scenes==
EG-57 first appeared as a prop within the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="RS" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The prop is capable of emitting various sound effects.<ref name="RS" /> The droid simultaneously appeared in the [[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]] application launched alongside the themed land, where it was first identified as EG-57,<ref name="Datapad" /> although ''Galaxy's Edge'' staff were originally told to refer to it as VD-57.<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|Resistance Supply|store}}</ref> Only the Hollywood Studios datapad confirms that EG-57 is the green GNK droid.<ref name="Datapad" />
