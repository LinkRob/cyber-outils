'''Ashiga''' was a [[queen]] of the [[Ashiga Clan]] who lived in the [[time]] of the [[Imperial Era]]. She ruled the Ashiga for generations and was admired for resisting the [[Galactic Empire]] on [[Kijimi]]. Following the start of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Queen relented and provided the Empire access to the planet's [[rhydonium]] mines in exchange for weapons contracts. Her decision to align with the Empire caused divisions within the ranks of the Ashiga and several members of the clan, including her daughter [[Krisk Ashiga]], formulated plans to overthrow her.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ashiga was created for the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Gameplay Alternatives===

*At the end of the main storyline on Kijimi, the player has the option of siding with [[Crimson Dawn]] or siding with the Ashiga Clan. If the player sides with Crimson Dawn, [[Krisk Ashiga]] will kill Ashiga, leading to Krisk becoming the new leader of the clan and disassociating with the Empire. If the player sides with the Ashiga Clan, Ashiga will remain queen, Krisk dies, and the clan will continue working with the Empire.<ref name="Outlaws" />
