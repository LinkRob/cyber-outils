'''Astriums''' were circular objects created by the ancient civilization of the [[Zeffonian]]s. One of the last remaining astriums was buried within the [[Tomb of Kujet|tomb]] of [[Kujet]] on the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]]. It served as the key to opening the [[Bogano Vault|Zeffo Vault]] on [[Bogano]]. In the Vault, [[Eno Cordova]], a [[Jedi Master]] who studied the Zeffo civilization, hid a [[holocron]] containing a list of [[Force-sensitive]] children that could be used in order to [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|restore]] the [[Jedi Order]] after the [[Great Jedi Purge]].<ref name="JFO" />
