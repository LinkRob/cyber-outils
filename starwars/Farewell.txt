'''Farewell''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] [[Wizards.com|website]] on [[December 15]], [[2005]] and took the form of a message holo-recording made by the [[Oblee]] crimelord [[Nirama]], commemorating the work of the [[Heroes of Cularin]].
