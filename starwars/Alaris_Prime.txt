'''Alaris Prime''' was one of the several [[moon]]s that [[orbit]]ed the [[planet]] [[Alaris]] of the [[Kashyyyk system]], in the [[Mytaranor sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]], and had hosted a colony of [[Wookiee]]s (from the neighboring world of [[Kashyyyk]]) by the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]].

==History==
===High Republic Era===
A [[Duel on Mount Bikja|duel]] took place sometime around [[241 BBY]] atop [[Mount Bikja]], on Alaris Prime; having ascended the [[mountain]] to enter the [[Yallow Shrine|shrine]] of a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] cult (known as the [[Yallow Fellowship]]), the [[Zeltron]] [[Padawan]] [[Klias Teradine]] encountered a [[vitalicron]] that corrupted his mind. In a possessed state, he lashed out against fellow [[Jedi]] student [[Ty Yorrick|Tylera]], who was forced to [[Death|kill]] her friend.<ref name="Temple Peak 4">[[The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak 4|''The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak'' 4]]</ref>

===Clone Wars===
By the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], Alaris Prime became home to a colony of adventurous [[Wookiee]]s from the neighbouring world of [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="Collapse" /> At one point, the moon hid a [[Jedi vault|treasure vault]] of the [[Jedi Order]], which ended up being raided by an audacious [[Defel]] [[thief]] named [[Zaril]].<ref name="RTVC 4" />
