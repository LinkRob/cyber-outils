==Behind the scenes==
The Celegians appeared, albeit unidentified, in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Han Solo 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo]]'', which was written by [[Marjorie Liu]], illustrated by [[Mark Brooks]],<ref name="Han 1" /> and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 15]], [[2016]].<ref name="Release">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52695/han_solo_2016_1|text=Han Solo (2016) #1}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the Celegians originated in [[Tales of the Jedi – The Golden Age of the Sith 0|''Tales of the Jedi – The Golden Age of the Sith'' 0]], written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]] and illustrated by [[Chris Gossett]].<ref name="Origins">[[Tales of the Jedi – The Golden Age of the Sith 0|''Tales of the Jedi – The Golden Age of the Sith'' 0]]</ref> Illustrator Mark Brooks confirmed on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that he included a Celegian in the issue after doing research into various alien races to use.<ref name="MBA Tweet" />
