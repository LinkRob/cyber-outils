The '''Brotherhood of the Beatific Countenance''' was a [[The Force|Force]]-based [[Lorrdian]] [[Religion|religious]] group that was active during the [[Republic Era]] and into the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].

==Description==
[[File:Lorrdian_pilgrims.png|thumb|250px|left|Members wore concealing vermilion robes.]]
Most members of the Brotherhood of the Beatific Countenance were clad in heavy [[Color|vermilion]] [[robe]]s and cowls to represent a physical vow of silence, a counter to their natural physical expressiveness. Some followers carried staff-mounted [[censer]]s filled with [[Fire|burning]] [[Qatameric incense]] that permeated through their heavy fabrics and masked their natural scent. In-keeping with the faith's concealment of individuality, some members went so far as to strip themselves of their own names, such as [[the High Priest]], who was referred to by his followers only by a keening wail of a specific pitch. Some members, such as leaders of a congregation, wore [[Blue Gown of the Sullen Moon|Blue Gowns of the Sullen Moon]] and [[Lorrdian Cowl of Quiescence|Lorrdian Cowls of Quiescence]].<ref name="UVG" /> They favored a hybrid variant of the [[meiloorun fruit]] that had [[Color|purple]] rinds instead of the traditional orange-yellow ones.<ref name="GotW">[[Guardians of the Whills (novel)|''Guardians of the Whills'']]</ref>

==History==
[[File:JedhaRiot-2022THR5.jpg|thumb|250px|right|Members of the Brotherhood respond to riots during the [[Battle of Jedha]]]]
Members of the Brotherhood often went on religious [[pilgrim]]ages to [[Planet|worlds]] of spiritual significance, such as the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[desert]] [[moon]] of [[Jedha]].<ref name="UVG" /> They also dwelt on [[Kijimi]] at some point in their history. They shared the ancient [[Kijimi City]] with the [[Dai Bendu]] monks and lived in the district which would eventually becomes the Thieves Quarter, until both groups of monks were ultimately pushed out by thugs and criminals.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Toshdor Ni]] was a member of the Brotherhood who went on a group pilgrimage to Jedha<ref name="UVG" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="RO date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' in [[1 BBY]]</ref> He and his fellow followers were in the moon's [[Jedha City|Holy City]] shortly before<ref name="UVG" /> it was [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]] during a test of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star's]] [[superlaser]].<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Brotherhood of the Beatific Countenance was a religious organization created for the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Anthology Series|anthology film]], ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="UVG" /> The Brotherhood was clearly inspired by the [[Sisterhood of the Beatific Countenance]], a now-[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[Lorrdian]]-based religion whose members wore similar red robes.<ref name="Seib Nod DB">{{DB|character|seibnod|Nod, Seib}}</ref>
