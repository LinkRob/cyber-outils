The '''Fallen Sanctum''' was a tower on the [[planet]] [[At Achrann]] that was in ruins by the time of the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="No At Attin" />

Sometime before [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> the [[dread pirate]] [[Tak Rennod]] visited the Fallen Sanctum with the [[droid]] [[SM-33]] while looking for the [[galactic coordinates]] of the hidden [[planet]] [[At Attin]]. Once there, Rennod found the coordinates carved into a pillar at the top of the tower and [[death|killed]] all the members of his crew that had seen them. Then, after recording the coordinates for himself, Rennod ordered SM-33 to destroy them and forget everything he knew about At Attin. Then he ordered SM-33 to pull apart anyone who would later come snooping around the sanctum looking for At Attin.<ref name="No At Attin" />

Around [[9 ABY]]<ref name="SCDate" />, the Fallen Sanctum was visited by a [[Skeleton Crew|group]] of [[youngling]]s that were searching for a way back home to At Attin. They were accompanied by the [[pirate]] [[Jod Na Nawood]] and SM-33. After seeing the coordinates destroyed and learning that it was SM-33 that had destroyed them, [[captain]] [[Fern]] ordered a memory override on SM-33, allowing him to remember At Attin, and causing him to attack the children. Fern's companion [[Neel]] was able to distract the droid while Na Nawood was able to deactivate him.<ref name="No At Attin" />
