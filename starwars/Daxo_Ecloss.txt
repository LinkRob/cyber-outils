'''Daxo Ecloss''', also known as '''"Odds"''', was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who was arrested by the [[Canto Bight]] [[Canto Bight Police Department|Police Department]] and locked in one of the cells in [[Canto Bight police headquarters]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. As [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] carries on immediately from the events of ''The Force Awakens'', it can be assumed to take place in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> When the criminal [[DJ]] escaped from his nearby cell using a [[skeleton key]], Ecloss and the other prisoners reached out through the bars of the their cell toward DJ; however, he left without freeing them.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> While in his cell he wore a [[Color|green]] [[Clothing|coat]], red pants and red [[boot]]s, as well as a [[belt]] and a brown [[Armor|helmet]] with a tracking [[sensor]]. Ecloss had fair skin and brown [[hair]].<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Daxo Ecloss first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ" /> which was released on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-viii-to-open-december-15-2017|text=''Star Wars: Episode VIII'' to Open December 15, 2017}}</ref> The character was not named in the film,<ref name="TLJ" /> but received identification in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref>
