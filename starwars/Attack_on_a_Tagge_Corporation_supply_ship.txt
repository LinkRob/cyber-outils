In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[D'Armin Syndicate]] '''attacked''' a [[Unidentified supply ship|supply ship]] of the [[Tagge Corporation]]. News of the attack subsequently spread and left the company with a drop in the [[Hadi system]]. A [[Standard day|day]] after the attack, [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]]&mdash;[[Executive officer|head]] of the corporation&mdash;was informed by her<ref name="Aphra 6" /> [[secretary]]<ref name="WOTBH Boushh">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]]</ref> [[Lapin Tagge]]<ref name="Aphra 28">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 28|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 28]]</ref> of the attack when she inquired about the cause of the company's drop in the Hadi [[Star system|system]]. Following Lapin's report, Domina told<ref name="Aphra 6" /> them<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" /> to have a [[battleship]] sent to cut off the syndicate at the celestial body [[Feriae Junction]], where it was to then take out their [[weapon]]s and [[engine]]s and push them into the [[Star|sun]] the following day.<ref name="Aphra 6" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on a Tagge Corporation [[supply ship]] was mentioned in flashbacks in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|sixth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 6" /> on [[November 25]], 2020. Prior to the release of the comic, the attack was mentioned in a preview article of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[November 18]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-doctor-aphra-6-exclusive-preview|text=There's a New Bounty in Marvel's ''Doctor Aphra'' #6 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
