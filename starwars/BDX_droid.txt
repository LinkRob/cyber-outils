'''BDX droids''',<ref name="Exploring Galaxy Edge">{{Twitter|username=starwars|url=status/1776413380393517073|name=[[Star Wars]]|date=2024-04-05|quote=Playful, curious, and cute. BDX droids are exploring Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge. Similar to other explorer [[droid]] models, such as the [[BD explorer droid|BD unit]], BDX droids were employed for gathering information, such as mapping terrain, geographic analysis, and scanning objects and structures.<ref name="Databank" /> 

At least four BDX droids were present at the [[New Republic]]'s [[Adelphi Base]].<ref name="Databank" /> The BDX droids [[Oskar]], [[Grek (BDX droid)|Grek]], and [[Red (BDX droid)|Red]] were employed by various denizens of [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]], including the [[Gatherers]] and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="SWGE" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Development===
{{Quote|We basically take animation as an input and make sure that our robots know how to best follow these inputs. They learn to not only walk, but to follow the artistic provided motion that differentiates us from the rest of the world.|Moritz Bächer, Associate Lab Director of Disney’s robotics team in Zurich|<ref name="TechRadar">{{WebCite|url=https://www.techradar.com/streaming/entertainment/star-wars-bdx-droids-the-inside-story-on-what-lies-ahead|text='We only build technology in the interest of storytelling' &ndash; Disney’s associate lab director of Robotics on the Star Wars BDX Droids and what lies ahead|archivedate=20250325130409}}</ref>}}
BDX droids were developed as real-world robots by [[The Walt Disney Company]]'s [[Wikipedia:Disney Research|Research]] and [[Walt Disney Imagineering|Imagineering]] groups using [[Wikipedia:Nvidia|NVIDIA]] artificial intelligence technology (AI) as part of a collaboration with NVIDIA and the [[Wikipedia:Google DeepMind|Google DeepMind]] AI laboratory. The initial robots were developed under a year and manually operated with two joysticks&mdash;but they were more complex than Disney's pre-existing repertoire of animatronics as Imagineers used reinforcement learning, based on artists' animations, for the robots to move in imitated motions while navigating various terrain via real-time simulation. Each had two NVIDIA Jetson computers, plus four actuators in the head and neck and five more actuators for each leg. 3D-printed components, sensors, cameras, and LEDs completed each unit.<ref name="TechRadar" /> They were unveiled by Disney Imagineers in [[October]] [[2023]] when three "droids-in-training" roamed the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] themed land in [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|California]]. The "play test" showed that the robots could maneuver on uneven ground, dance without losing their balance, and "interact" with guests with pre-set responses.<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|223|Launchpad}}</ref>

During development, the three BDX units were jokingly named after Disney triplets [[Wikipedia:Huey, Dewey, and Louie|Huey, Dewey, and Louie]].<ref name="Trio">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1712595847450431773|[[Matt Martin]]|date=2023-10-12|quote=We made that same joke internally too.|archivefile=File:MattMartinBDXDroids-3.png}}</ref> After the October play test, [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Matt Martin]] said that he was not yet able to reveal the names<ref name="Names 1">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1712596259918184956|[[Matt Martin]]|date=2023-10-12|quote=You'll have to wait for names.|archivefile=File:MattMartinBDXDroids-2.png}}</ref> of the "sweet babies"<ref name="Matt's take">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1712513169044685089|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=Sweet babies.|archivefile=File:MattMartinBDXDroids-1.png}}</ref> because they were still in development,<ref name="Names 2">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1712596349902860467|[[Matt Martin]]|date=2023-10-12|quote=You'll have to wait for the names. They're still just training.|archivefile=File:MattMartinBDXDroids-2.png}}</ref> though he did reveal the Huey, Dewey, and Louie joke shared behind the scenes.<ref name="Trio" /> Martin credited the talents of the Imagineers with their creation.<ref name="Genius">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1712596082755076403|[[Matt Martin]]|date=2023-10-12|quote=I mean, I had nothing to do with that really. That's the work of genius Imagineers.|archivefile=File:MattMartinBDXDroids-4.png}}</ref> On [[April 5]], [[2024]], it was confirmed the robots would appear as a limited time feature of Galaxy's Edge during the [[Season of the Force]] event, and their [[in-universe]] name was officially revealed to be "BDX droids."<ref name="Exploring Galaxy Edge" /> They were also deployed the [[Wikipedia:Disney Wish|''Disney Wish'']] cruise ship and learned to balance on deck.<ref name="SXSW 2025">{{DisneyCompany|url=disney-sxsw-conference-2025|text=Disney Explores the Future of World-Building at SXSW Conference}}</ref>

BDX units were later featured at the Disney Experience SXSW conference on [[March 8]], [[2025]], where Imagineers explained their development of the robots via reinforcement learning and simulation. [[Jon Favreau]] then revealed that the BDX units would appear in the [[2026]] film ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''. They would also visit [[Disney's Hollywood Studios|Walt Disney World]] in [[Wikipedia:Orlando, Florida|Florida]], [[Disneyland Paris]], and [[Disneyland Paris]] in 2025,<ref name="SXSW 2025" /> with milled parts replacing 3D-printed parts to accommodate Florida's heat.<ref name="TechRadar" /> During NVIDIA's [[Wikipedia:Nvidia GTC|AI conference]] ten days later, on [[March 18]], CEO [[Wikipedia:Jensen Huang|Jensen Huang]] announced that his company was collaborating with Google DeepMind and Disney Research to further develop Newton, an advanced physics engine that would allow AI models to be trained precisely and at high speeds. A BDX unit named "Blue" operating with Newton featured with Huang on stage.<ref name="NVIDIA GTC 2025">{{WebCite|url=https://deadline.com/2025/03/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-ai-disney-next-generation-entertainment-robots-1236329770|text=Nvidia CEO Chats Up Star Wars-Inspired Droid As Chip Maker Partners With Disney, Google And Lays Out The AI Revolution|date=2025-03-18|author=Jill Goldsmith|work=[[Wikipedia:Deadline Hollywood|''Deadline Hollywood'']]|archivedate=20250322064806}}</ref>

===Appearances begin===
Beginning on [[July 16]], [[2025]], the droids made their debut at Hollywood Studios, with a story involving their official names. In an early performance attended by members of the press, [[Red (BDX droid)|Red]], [[Grek (BDX droid)|Grek]], and [[Oskar]] practiced protocols for the crowd, led by the [[Gatherers|Gatherer]] [[Kimbe]], who revealed details of their individual personalities. That initial performance was cut off by rain, resulting in cast members swiftly bagging up the droids to be brought backstage.<ref name="EastDebut" /> Beyond their demonstration with Kimbe, the droids also had two different appearances led by [[Vi Moradi]]. One of these took place in front of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' and the other in front of the [[TIE/es assault shuttle]], in which the droids helped the [[Resistance]] by analyzing [[First Order]] cargo crates.<ref name="East Moradi shows" />

On [[November 8]], 2025, the BDX droids returned to Hollywood Studios for a new play-test during the nighttime ticketed event Jollywood Night. For this play-test, guests had a brief interaction with a single BDX droid which lasted about five minutes.<ref name="JollywoodNights">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fantasylandnews.com/2025/11/09/suprise-bdx-droids-interacting-with-guests-at-jollywood-nights-2025|text=Surprise! BDX Droids Interacting with Guests at Jollywood Nights 2025|date=2025-11-09|work=[https://fanatasylandnews.com Fantasy Land News]|archivedate=20251222164439}}</ref> The interaction included an explanation of BDX history from their handler, a play demonstration, and a photo-op.<ref name="JollywoodVid" /> The droids rotated every 30 minutes during the event.<ref name="JollywoodNights" /> During this play-test, the character handler revealed new lore for the droids, namely that the model went out of production long before the events of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge, but that the ones in [[Black Spire Outpost]] were refurbished by [[Mubo]].<ref name="JollywoodVid" />

In a YouTube video released by Walt Disney Imagineering on [[November 24]], 2025, Imagineers revealed that the next phase of BDX droid development would be to further their autonomy. The video featured Imagineers remotely controlling the droids for a show sequence and then using their prior input to run the show again without human interference. They also showcased developments in BDX droids' ability to track human movements. This video also revealed that [[Bryan Bishop]] has served as creative director for BDX droid shows in the parks.<ref name="Olaf">{{YouTube|video=EoPN02bmzrE|text=NEW Robotic Olaf Revealed! Inside Disney Imagineering R&D &#124; We Call It Imagineering|channel=@waltdisneyimagineering|name=Walt Disney Imagineering|date=2025-11-24}}</ref>

===2026 and onward===
In early [[2026]], the BDX droids continued their world tour, appearing at [[Shanghai Disneyland]] for the first time. In a show called "''Star Wars'': Galactic Encounter with BDX Droids", guests were able to participate in a show involving the droids Red, Grek, and Oskar. An Instagram post from the resort explained the premise of the show as being that an "interstellar explorer and the BDX droids have accidentally arrived in the human world" and that they need the audience's help to get home. The limited-time show ran from [[February 25]]-[[March 1]], [[March 4]]-[[March 8|8]], and [[March 11]]-[[March 17|17]], 2026.<ref name="Shanghai Debut">{{Instagram|username=@shanghaidisneyresort|url=/p/DVLHwZ5Cqdp/|name=[[Shanghai Disneyland|Shanghai Disneyland Resort]]|date=2026-02-25|archivefile=File:BDXArrival-ShanghaiDisneyResort-Insta.jpg}}</ref>

In honor of [[May 4|''Star Wars'' Day]], 2026 and the release of ''Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu'', it was announced that the red, orange, and green BDX droids (not using their in-universe names from Disney's Hollywood Studios) would be available to meet at [[Disneyland Paris]]'s Videopolis Theatre from May 4 through [[May 24]] alongside [[Din Djarin]] and [[Din Grogu|Grogu]],<ref name="DLP26">{{WebCite|url=https://news.disneylandparis.com/en/may-the-4th-be-with-you-on-star-wars-day/|text=May the 4th Be With You on Star Wars Day|archivedate=20260430195755}}</ref> in a character experience called "BDX Droid Mission: An Unexpected Encounter."<ref name="Unexpected Encounter">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneylandparis.com/en-int/entertainment/disneyland-park/bdx-droid-mission-unexpected-encounter|text=BDX Droid Mission: An Unexpected Encounter|archivedate=20260512175102}}</ref> In this brief stage show, presented in mixed English and French, the three participating droids, Oskar, Grek, and Red, were referred to as "Orange", "Green", and "Red", respectively.<ref name="DLP Show">{{YouTube|video=oHq_uWJzrLg|text=🌌 FULL SHOW <nowiki>|</nowiki> BDX Droids Mission: An Unexpected Encounter <nowiki>|</nowiki> Disneyland Paris|channel=@pixie-dustDLP|name=Pixie Dust DLP|date=2026-05-06|archivedate=20260506204829}}</ref> 

For ''Star Wars'' Day 2026, the BDX droids joined the [[Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|''Star Wars'': Droid Factory]] toy line in a pack featuring all four colors of the droids,<ref name="Droid Factory">{{DisneyToyCite|set=Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|pack=BDX Droid|link=bdx-droid-factory-figure-set-star-wars-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-418140526647.html|archivedate=20260506155351}}</ref> as previously previewed at Toy Fair 2026.<ref name="Droid Factory Tease">{{DPB|url=products/new-star-wars-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-merchandise/|text=Discover New ''Star Wars'' Merchandise Inspired by 'The Mandalorian and Grogu'}}</ref>

Despite their active appearances at Disneyland Paris, all four BDX droids made an in-person appearance at the world premiere of ''Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu'' on [[May 14]], 2026 in [[Wikipedia:Los Angeles|Los Angeles, California]], each sporting bow ties.<ref name="MandGWorldPremiere">{{Instagram|username=starwars|url=reel/DYVoY92tsxh/|name=[[Star Wars]]|date=2026-05-14|quote=Everyone's showing off their World Premiere fits.|archivefile=File:BDXatMandoPremier-SWInsta.jpg}}</ref> The following day, it was announced that a souvenir collectible BDX droid popcorn bucket would be arriving at various food vendors across Disney's Hollywood Studios and Disneyland Park.<ref name="Bucket">{{DPB|url=disney-experiences/disney-eats-mandalorian-and-grogu-menus-at-disney-parks|text=Disney Eats: New Mandalorian and Grogu Menus and More at Disney Parks}}</ref> 

On [[May 22]], 2026,<ref name="DL2026">{{DPB|url=disney-experiences/star-wars-at-disneyland-disney-world-and-more/|text=Star Wars at Disneyland, Disney World, and More}}</ref> the day in which BDX droids of all four colors made a cameo appearance in ''Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu'',<ref name="TMAG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref> the BDX droids returned to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge in Disneyland for a new engagement with an unspecified endpoint. Given the same three BDX droids&ndash;Oskar, Grek, and Red&dash;appeared at Disneyland on May 22<ref name="DL2026" /> while still appearing at Disneyland Paris until May 24, it can be deduced that by May 22 there were at least two copies of each color BDX droid in active use.

The character experience at Disneyland differs significantly from their previous appearances at Galaxy's Edge in that there are no visible human handlers or show leaders. Appearing at the [[Black Spire Station]], behind the metal fence, Oskar, Grek, and Red hold a half-hour appearance with occasional prompting from pre-recorded voices of [[Batuuan]]s.<ref name="DL2026Video">{{YouTube|video=6GNA4kMSW2A|text=BDX Droids First Appearance at Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge in 2026 <nowiki>|</nowiki> Disneyland Resort 4K 🌌🤖👣|channel=@Dapsmagic|name=Daps Magic - Disney News and Positive Stuff |date=2025-05-22}}</ref>
