The '''Fryiaa system''' was a [[star system]] positioned in grid square Q-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], in the [[Dail sector]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing Sectors" /> It was [[Homeworld|home]]<ref name="Planetnamia" /> to the [[Fryiaan]], a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]].<ref name="RS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fryiaa system was mentioned in the appendix [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]], published on the [[StarWars.com]] website<ref name="Appendix" /> as part of [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Galaxy">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
