The '''Cerberon system''' was a [[star system]] situated in the [[Deep Core]], centered around the [[black hole]] known as the [[Cerberon singularity]]. It contained several [[planet]]s, including the [[City|urban]] world [[Troithe]], a rival to the [[Galactic Republic]] [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]] [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Imperial Era]]; [[Catadra]], a planet home to numerous [[Religion|religious]] cults and [[orbit]]ed by the [[moon]] [[Narthex]]; and rocky, airless [[Verzan]]. The Cerberon singularity was also orbited by a [[Cerberon debris field|debris field]], containing [[asteroid]]s such as [[Asteroid CER952B|CER952B]] and a [[desert]] [[Unidentified planetoid (Cerberon system)|planetoid]]. During the final stages of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Cerberon system was a valuable location that the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] sought to [[Cerberon system campaign|retake]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It's a bit trivial, but I'm pretty pleased with how the Cerberon system as a whole came together ''[&hellip;]'' So many Star Wars stories (including most of my own) hop from star to star so fast you don't get to feel much of the weight of local history or culture. It was interesting to spend most of the novel in a single system.|Alexander Freed|<ref name="Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://www.syfy.com/syfywire/star-wars-alphabet-squadron-alexander-freed-shadow-fall|text=Yrica Quell and Alphabet Squadron strike back in new ''Star Wars'' novel, ''Shadow Fall''|author=Spry, Jeff|date=2020-06-22|work=[https://www.syfy.com SYFY WIRE]|archivedate=20200622222911}}</ref>}}
The Cerberon system appeared in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> As Freed felt that many stories in ''[[Star Wars]]'' often traveled between locations too quickly to develop the local history or culture, he was interested by having a majority of the novel set in the Cerberon system.<ref name="Interview" />
