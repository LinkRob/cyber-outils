'''Alan Lau''' is one of the cofounders of the animation studio [https://www.ghostbot.com/ Ghostbot]. Along with cofounder [[Roque Ballesteros]], Lau was approached by [[Brad Rau]], director of the animated series ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]'', for help designing the [[Nightwatcher worm]] for the series.<ref name="Brad Rau" />
