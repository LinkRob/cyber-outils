This '''cabin''' was [[Clan Mudhorn]]'s residence on [[Nevarro]], gifted to [[Din Djarin]] by [[High Magistrate]] [[Greef Karga]] as a reward for Djarin's efforts to protect the planet during the [[Pirate siege on Nevarro]] on [[9 ABY]]. It was located just outside of [[Nevarro City]]. Djarin and [[Din Grogu]] spent their time here between their adventures. It has a hitching post.

==History==
After the [[Conflict on Mandalore|Reconquest of Mandalore]], Din Djarin and Din Grogu went back to Nevarro to meet High Magistrate Greef Karga, and Djarin received a deed to the cabin, right before gifting the newly rebuilt [[IG-11]] for Karga to use as the new Marshal of Nevarro. Some time after, Djarin relaxed on the porch as Grogu levitated a frog out of the pond located in front of the cabin.
