'''Authors of the Expanded Universe''' was a recurring feature in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine written by [[Michael Kogge]]. It examined the publishing history of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]], now known as ''Star Wars'' Legends, discussing authors and other contributors.

The feature debuted in [[Star Wars Insider 139|''Star Wars Insider'' 139]] and continued under its recurring title through [[Star Wars Insider 156|issue 156]], with the exception of issues [[Star Wars Insider 144|144]], [[Star Wars Insider 152|152]], and [[Star Wars Insider 155|155]]. Those issues included similar articles on other aspects of ''Star Wars'' Legends, such as its [[Authors of the Expanded Universe|early days]] and [[Gaming the Expanded Universe: Ten Years of Knights of the Old Republic|video games]]. The final article of the series was instead published under the subtitle "Legendary Authors".

Beginning with [[Star Wars Insider 157|''Star Wars Insider'' 157]], the magazine has continued producing feature articles that examine the creation of ''Star Wars'' Legends works, but they have been presented as independent titles, abandoning the recurring "''Legendary Authors''" appellation.
