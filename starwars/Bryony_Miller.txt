'''Bryony Miller''' is a British actress who portrays a [[First Order officer]] in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. In addition to ''Star Wars'', her roles of note include the drama miniseries [[Wikipedia:Patrick Melrose (TV series)|''Patrick Melrose'']], the 2018 historical drama [[Wikipedia:Peterloo (film)|''Peterloo'']],<ref name="Peterloo">{{Twitter|BryonyMiller93|status/991322187482259456|Bryony Miller|archiveurl=https://archive.vn/P6Far}}</ref> the British soap opera [[Wikipedia:Doctors (2000 TV series)|''Doctors'']], and the BBC Three drama series [[Wikipedia:Killed by My Debt|''Killed by My Debt'']]. In 2020, she appeared in three episodes of the [[Netflix]] series [[Wikipedia:Cursed (2020 TV series)|''Cursed'']],<ref name="Cursed">{{Twitter|BryonyMiller93|status/1284057952534630401|Bryony Miller|archiveurl=https://archive.vn/go9jW}}</ref> and co-starred as the main character's maid Clarice in the Netflix film [[Wikipedia:Rebecca (2020 film)|''Rebecca'']].<ref name="Heart">{{WebCite|url=https://www.heart.co.uk/showbiz/tv-movies/netflix-rebecca-cast|text=Who is in the cast of the Netflix Rebecca remake and what else have they been in?|date=2020-10-21|work=[https://www.heart.co.uk Heart]|archivedate=20201023134222}}</ref>
