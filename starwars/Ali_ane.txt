'''Ali'ane''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Lupr'or]] who lived in her [[Home Of Ali'ane|home]] on the [[planet]] [[Luprora]]. They had a [[Ali'ane's great granddaughter|great granddaughter]] who wore a [[necklace]] salvaged from a Lupr'or [[starship]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], Ali'ane's great granddaughter visited them with [[Captain]] [[Phasma]], [[TN-3465]], and [[Dar'en]] to speak about a [[necklace]].

==Biography==
Ali'ane was the oldest living [[Lupr'or]] by the time of the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]] in [[34 ABY]]. They were born in the stars. They gave a necklace salvaged from a Lupr'or [[starship]] to their [[Ali'ane's great granddaughter|great granddaughter]]. During the [[Hunt for Rivas|hunt]] for the [[First Order]] fugitive [[Sol Rivas]], [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] questioned Ali'ane and their great granddaughter about where they had acquired the necklace from.<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" />

Regarding technology as evil, Ali'ane refused to reveal the location of the last Lupr'or starship, claiming that it came from the [[Lupr'or homeworld|"old world"]]. At Phasma's prompting, Ali'ane's great granddaughter claimed that Phasma had been sent by the gods to save them from the [[R'ora]] and the [[Tsw'ells]]. Ali'ane responded that the ancestors had forbidden technology, which descried as the soul killer.<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" />

Despite Ali'ane's efforts, their great granddaughter secretly revealed the location of the Lupr'or starship to Phasma. This allowed her to obtain generators which the Lupr'or used to electrocute the tsw'ells in the lake surrounding the [[R'ora stronghold]].<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" /> While the Lupr'or fought the R'ora, Phasma took the opportunity to kill Rivas, concealing her role in the [[Battle of Starkiller Base|destruction]] of [[Starkiller Base]]. Having outlived their usefulness, Phasma then abandoned the Lupr'or to the R'ora and rising sea levels.<ref name="Captain Phasma 4">[[Captain Phasma 4|''Captain Phasma'' 4]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Ali'ane was an elderly Lupr'or [[Gender|woman]] with green skin, gray hair, and gray eyes. They were the oldest living Lupr'or and had a great granddaughter. Due to their venerable age, Ali'ane was bedridden. Ali'ane regarded technology and starships as a source of evil that belonged to the time before the Lupr'or had settled on Luprora.<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" />
