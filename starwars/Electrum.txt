'''Electrum''' was a type of [[Color|purple]] and blue [[crystal]] found in the [[Anoat sector]].<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> The crystal could be used as a finish to give weaponry and [[armor]] a reflective gold sheen.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:Mace_Windu_Jedi_Champion_EaW.png|thumb|left|300px|Mace Windu's lightsaber (pictured in use by Windu) featured an electrum finish.]]
In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> [[Star|sunlight]] reflected off a [[Jedi symbol]] coated in electrum at the [[Elphrona Outpost]] as [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] prepared for an exercise with his master, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Loden Greatstorm]].<ref name="Light">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Burryaga's lightsaber]] was plated in pure electrum.<ref name="CotJ" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]]'s [[Avar Kriss' lightsaber|lightsaber]] had a single piece of silver-white electrum on it.<ref name="Light" /> The hilt of [[Mace Windu]]'s [[Mace Windu's lightsaber|lightsaber]] possessed a rare electrum finish,<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> as did the [[Darth Sidious' lightsabers|lightsabers]] of [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE" />

The [[Pyre's armor|armor]] and [[Pyre's blaster|personal]] [[F-11D blaster rifle]] of [[First Order]] [[Commander]] [[Pyre]] also had an electrum finishing, giving them a reflective gold sheen.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Electrum first appeared, in the form of the finish of the hilt of Windu's lightsaber, in the [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']],<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref> which was released on [[May 16]], [[2002]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> The material was first previewed in the trailer for ''Attack of the Clones'',<ref name="Trailer">{{SW|url=video/episode-ii-trailer-clone-war|text=Attack of the Clones Trailer: Clone War &#124; Video}}</ref> which released earlier in the year on [[March 10]].<ref name="TrailerRelease">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/article/2002/03/04/sneak-peek-next-episode-ii-trailer|text=A sneak peek at the next <nowiki>''</nowiki>Episode II<nowiki>''</nowiki> trailer|author=Brown, Scott|date=2002-03-04|quote=On March 10, the latest two-and-a-half-minute tease for "Attack of the Clones" will hit screens|archivedate=20231127115801}}</ref> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], electrum was identified in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

[[Electrum/Legends|Electrum]] was initially introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1996]] [[West End Games]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] guide ''[[Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook]]'' by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]].<ref name="Shadows">''[[Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook]]''</ref> It first appeared in [[Barbara Hambly]]'s [[1997]] novel ''[[Planet of Twilight]]''.<ref name="Planet">''[[Planet of Twilight]]''</ref> In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Electrum|electrum]] is a natural alloy of [[Wikipedia:Gold|gold]], [[Wikipedia:Silver|silver]] and other [[Wikipedia:Metal|metals]].<ref name="Adze">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/science/electrum|text=Electrum &#124; Ancient, Precious Metal & Gold-Silver Alloy|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20240206140054}}</ref>

In the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', the player could customize [[Cal Kestis]]' [[Cal Kestis' second lightsaber|lightsaber]] to be electrum-plated.<ref name="FO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> In the [[2023]] sequel ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'', players could customize the lightsaber, [[Cal Kestis's LW-896 blaster pistol|Kestis' blaster pistol]], and Kestis' [[droid]] companion, [[BD-1]], with electrum-plating.<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>
