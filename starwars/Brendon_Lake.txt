'''Brendon Lake''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan who participated in the "Join the Jedi" contest sponsored by [[Hasbro]] During the contest, Lake submitted a [[lightsaber]] training maneuver known as the "[[Whirlwind of Destiny]]." His entry ultimately won the contest and as such, the "Whirlwind of Destiny" was featured as [[Petro]]'s lightsaber maneuver in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[A Test of Strength]]," with Brendon receiving a screen credit for it.<ref name="AToS-TG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/a-test-of-strength-trivia-gallery|text=A Test of Strength Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref>
