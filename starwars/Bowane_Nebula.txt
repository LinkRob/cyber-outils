The '''Bowane Nebula''' was a [[nebula]] located in the [[Tungra sector]]'s [[Fand sector|Fand subsector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square K-18 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />
