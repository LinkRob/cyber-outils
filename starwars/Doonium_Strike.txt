'''Doonium Strike''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[fathier]] that [[Reproduction|mated]] with the female fathier [[Take Flight]], who gave birth to the fathier named [[Mynock Minute]]<ref name="Rose Tico" /> around [[31 ABY]]. By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The Last Jedi: Rose Tico: Resistance Fighter]]'' establishes that [[Mynock Minute]] is three [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of {{Film|VIII}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]], therefore meaning they must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[31 ABY]].</ref> Mynock Minute had competed in a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]] in the [[casino]] [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. Prior to the race, a racing guide was published that listed Doonium Strike as Mynock Minute's [[Parent|sire]]. One of the guides was collected by the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance technician|mechanic]] [[Rose Tico]] while she was on a [[mission to Canto Bight]]<ref name="Rose Tico" /> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> Tico later added the guide to her [[Rose Tico's journal|diary]].<ref name="Rose Tico" />

==Behind the scenes==
Doonium Strike was mentioned in the [[2018]] replica journal ''[[The Last Jedi: Rose Tico: Resistance Fighter]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Rose Tico" />
