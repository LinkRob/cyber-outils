'''Buboicullaar''', or '''Bubo''' for short, was a [[frog-dog]] [[Gender|male]] that was kept as a pet by the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]], who used him as a [[Security guard|guard]] [[dog]]. He lived in Jabba's [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] and foiled many [[assassin]]ation despite most people thinking he was not more than an unintelligent pest. The [[Gran]] [[Ree-Yees]] was assigned to take care of the frog-dog, but on one occasion lost track of him aboard Jabba's [[sail barge]], the ''[[Khetanna]]''. The Gran promised Jabba that he would find his charge and clean the barge, but Buboicullaar avoided his keeper while Ree-Yees searched. Once the Gran believed he had run out of [[time]], Jabba simply summoned Buboicullaar by calling his name.

In [[4 ABY]], Buboicullaar barked at the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] as the [[droid]] entered Jabba's [[throne]] room with the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] to deliver a message to Jabba from the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]]. Later that [[Standard day|day]], the frog-dog was present for a performance of the [[Music|song]] "[[Jedi Rocks]]" by the [[Max Rebo Band]] which ended in the [[Slavery|enslaved]] dancer [[Oola]] being fed to Jabba's pet [[rancor]], [[Pateesa]]. Buboicullaar then witnessed [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], disguised as the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boushh]], threaten Jabba and his court with a [[thermal detonator]] while trying to negotiate over the [[bounty]] on the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]].

==Biography==
===Who let the dog out===
{{Quote|Buubooo! I have to clean because of you! Bubo? Come out if you're in there. I have your favorite! Steaming fecal worms.|Ree-Yees searches for Buboicullaar|<ref name="Dirty">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=11|story=Tales of Villainy: Done Dirty}}</ref>}}
[[File:StealthBubo-SWA11.png|thumb|right|300px|Buboicullaar avoided Ree-Yees on the ''Khetanna'', even after being fed steaming fecal worms.]]
Buboicullaar, also known as just "Bubo," was a [[frog-dog]] [[Gender|male]] that lived in the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] of the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Geektionary" /> He acted as a [[Security guard|guard]] [[dog]] for the crime lord<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> and protected him from many [[assassin]]ation attempts,<ref name="Alien Archive" /> include one incident where he foiled an attempt by eating the detonation link needed for a [[bomb]] to explode.<ref name="New Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> The [[Gran]] [[Ree-Yees]] was tasked with unglamorous job of taking care of Buboicullaar<ref name="CVD" /> but on one occasion lost track of the frog-dog on board Jabba's [[sail barge]],<ref name="Dirty" /> the ''[[Khetanna]]'',<ref name="CVD" /> while it was out traveling on Tatooine.<ref name="Dirty" />

Pleading for his life, the Gran promised to find Buboicullaar while also cleaning the entire sail barge so that his charge would not be tempted to wonder off again. Jabba then gave Ree-Yees the [[time]] it would take for the ''Khetanna'' to return to the palace to make good on his promises. Buboicullaar moved around the barge ignoring Ree-Yees as the Gran cleaned and called out for the frog-dog. When Ree-Yees laid out a plate of [[fecal worm]]s, Buboicullaar's favorite [[Foodstuff|food]], as bait, the guard dog simply ate the [[worm]]s when the Gran's back was turned and continued to avoid him.<ref name="Dirty" />

[[File:BuboReturns.png|thumb|left|200px|Jabba and Buboicullaar]]
Ree-Yees ultimately ended up cleaning the pit on the barge where Jabba kept a [[krayt dragon]], and discovered Buboicullaar's dog tag amongst sewage under a sewage grate in the room. The Gran escaped from the pit assuming that Buboicullaar had been devored, and, upon reaching the top deck, saw that the barge had reached Jabba's Palace. To save Jabba the trouble, Ree-Yees offered to throw himself overboard, but instead of executing the Gran, Jabba simply called out to Buboicullaar. Out the sound of his name, the frog-dog shuffled out onto the deck, much to Ree-Yees' surprise. The Hutt then laughed at the Gran and told him to take better care of Buboicullaar in future.<ref name="Dirty" />

===Barking up the wrong tree===
{{Quote|Arf! Arf!|Buboicullaar barks at C-3PO|<ref name="ROTJ" />}}
[[File:BuboBarking-ROTJ.png|thumb|right|300px|Buboicullaar barked when C-3PO brushed past him.]]
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VI}} to [[4 ABY]].</ref> Buboicullaar was sat in the entryway to Jabba's [[throne]] room when the Hutt's [[majordomo]], [[Bib Fortuna]], and a pair of [[Gamorrean]] guards escorted the [[droid]]s [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]] into the room. When C-3PO brushed past the frog-dog, Buboicullaar began barking loudly, but stopped once the group had passed and turned to listen as they addressed Jabba. R2-D2 projected a [[Hologram|holographic]] message from the droid's master, the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]], in which Skywalker asked to bargain with Jabba for the freedom of the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] and gifted the droids to Jabba as a sign of goodwill. When the message was over, the Hutt declared he had no interest in giving up the imprisoned Solo, who he displayed frozen in [[carbonite]] on the wall of the throne room, and ordered that the droids be taken away to begin work.<ref name="ROTJ" />

Buboicullaar later sat on the floor of the throne room with a [[Jawa]] holding his chain during a performance of the [[Music|song]] "[[Jedi Rocks]]" by the [[Max Rebo Band]]. He bobbed along to the music as the [[Slavery|enslaved]] dancers [[Oola]] and [[Yarna d'al' Gargan]] performed in front of him, but after Jabba called for the song to be repeated, Oola resisted advances made by Jabba during the second performance. The Hutt then dropped the dancer through a trap door into the pit below the throne room, and the court, including the Jawa holding the frog-dog's chain, rushed forward to a grating in front of Buboicullaar to watch her fate. Buboicullaar continued to bob up and down as the crowd cheered while watching Jabba's pet [[rancor]], [[Pateesa]], devour Oola.<ref name="ROTJ" />

===Dog days at the palace===
{{Quote|No need for attitude, do you want the barge repaired?|A quote associated with C2-B9's internal memory record of Buboicullaar|<ref name="Datapad">{{DatapadCite|[[C2-B9]]|C2B9p1-GEDatapad.png|C2B9p2-GEDatapad.png}}</ref>}}
[[File:BoboOnLeash-RotJAVA.png|thumb|left|300px|Buboicullaar acted as Jabba's guard dog.]]
Once the excitement was over, the court's attention was drawn to the court room's entry by the sound of [[blaster]] fire. Buboicullaar turned to look for the source of the sound as courtiers began approaching to investigate, but its origin quickly became apparent when [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], disguised as the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boushh]], entered with the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] as her [[prisoner]]. She dragged Chewbacca to the center of the room in front of the frog-dog and presented her prisoner to Jabba, who offered the twenty-five thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] [[bounty]] he had placed on Wookiee.<ref name="ROTJ" />

Organa demanded double that, revealing an active [[thermal detonator]] as incentive. While most of those present in the room ducked for cover, Buboicullaar began to bob more energetically and Jabba merely laughed, counter offering thirty-five thousand credits instead. Organa accepted and deactivated the detonator, and the band began to play once more as Chewbacca was led away. Organa and Skywalker himself were later captured by Jabba, but ultimately killed the Hutt and much of his court when they were taken to be executed at the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]].<ref name="ROTJ" />

[[File:Bubo-AA.png|thumb|right|300px|Buboicullaar was sketched in an artist's journal.]]
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GEDates">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which includes [[C2-B9]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Datapad]]'' entry, to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the memory banks of the [[astromech droid]] [[C2-B9]] contained an internal data record that showed Buboicullaar in Jabba's Palace alongside the Jawa holding his chain and several other court members seated in a booth. The image was associated with a quote asking if the sail barge should be repaired.<ref name="Datapad" /> An illustration of Buboicullaar and an accompanying description and caption was included in an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]], which was restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="Alien Archives">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> at some point no earlier than 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|The frog-dog acted as a guard and growled at anyone who approached.|An artist's journal describes Buboicullaar|<ref name="Alien Archive" />}}
[[File:Gargan-Laughing-ROTJ.png|thumb|left|300px|Most of Jabba's courtiers assumed Buboicullaar was an unintelligent pest, but in truth he was smarter than most of them.]]
Buboicullaar had a wide [[mouth]] with sharp, protruding [[Tooth|teeth]], giving him an appearance that was simultaneously considered grotesque, ferocious, and ridiculous. His looks meant many in Jabba's Palace assumed that the frog-dog<ref name="Geektionary" /> was just a pest,<ref name="Alien Archive" /> not an intelligent being, where as, in truth, Buboicullaar was considerably smarter then many of the Hutt's other employees.<ref name="Geektionary" /> He growled at anyone who approached him.<ref name="Alien Archive" /> Buboicullaar weighed 35 [[kilogram]]s but could pull up to 119 kilograms (262 [[pound]]s) using his [[tongue]]. He ate [[gorg]]s, but would have prefered to eat Jabba's court [[jester]], the [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]] [[Salacious B. Crumb]].<ref name="SWAEYNTK" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BuboCostume.png|thumb|right|250px|The Buboicullaar costume was worn on the upper half of the performer's body.]]
Buboicullaar was portrayed by performer [[Franki Anderson]], who was six months pregnant at the time,<ref name="Actor">{{Rebelscum|subdomain=forum|url=threads/rotj-history-gerald-homes-creature-info.1069050/post-18520637|text=ROTJ History: Gerald Home's Creature Info.|author=[[Gerald Home]]|date=2010--8-16|quote=Franki Anderson: played Bubo. She was about 6 months pregnant at the time. She operated Bubo from under the set, so we didn't see her much during filming.}}</ref> in the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="ROTJ" /> The creature's costume consisted of a suit worn on the upper half of the performer's body and operated from below the raised throne room set. The costume was based on a maquette of Buboicullaar.<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars Chronicles]]''</ref>

A list of all the creatures being made for the film dated [[November 4]], [[1981]], has the character as number fourteen with the production name "Bubo,"<ref name="Creature History">{{SW|url=news/return-of-the-jedi-creature-history-with-pablo-hidalgo|text=''Return of the Jedi'' Creature History with Pablo Hidalgo|nolive=1}}</ref> which was based on the fact that the frog-dog looked like a blister.<ref name="Archeology">{{YouTube|video=RWw8-s_a0vM|text=Star Wars: Return of the Jedi - Cantina Archeology Part 3: Behind The Scenes of Jabba's Palace|channel=@CredibleNerds|name=The Credible Nerds}}</ref> The palace throne room scenes were filmed between [[January 25]] and [[February 8]], [[1982]] on Stage 8 at [[Elstree Studios]].<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]''</ref>
