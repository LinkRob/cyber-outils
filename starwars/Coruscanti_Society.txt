'''''Coruscanti Society''''' was a periodical publication that featured reports on the social life of [[Coruscant]]'s nobility and elite. The title ran for many [[Standard decade|decades]], from the [[Clone Wars]] to the [[New Republic Era]] and published many individual issues including [[Coruscanti Society 3269|3269]] and [[Coruscanti Society 4117|4117]]. Each issue included several different sections such as the "Profile," which contained either a list of high-status individuals of the time or a detailed report on a particular person. Due to its detailed reports on individuals of historical importance, several issues of ''Coruscanti Society'' were used as sources by [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] in his book ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />
