The '''DH-17 blaster pistol''' was a close-combat [[blaster pistol]] produced by [[BlasTech Industries]].

==Description==
{{Quote|It's a DH-17. Leave the settings the way they are and don't even think of switching it to automatic.|First Sergeant Namir during the [[Mid Rim Retreat]]|<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>}}
[[File:RebelSoldier1-SWBC38.png|thumb|left|200px|Rebel troopers commonly used DH-17 blaster pistols.]]
The DH-17 blaster pistol was a close-combat [[blaster pistol]] commonly used by [[soldier]]s of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] for conflicts aboard starships<ref name="Ultimate" /> or during ground operations.<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> It was also sometimes used by the [[Imperial officer|officers]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], who might carry it instead of the standard [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]].<ref name="Ultimate" /> The [[DH-17 blaster rifle]] was a rifle version that featured a shoulder stock, and a hand-calibrated [[macroscope]]s. The rifles were [[Color|black]] and silver in color.<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

The pistol was dependable on a semiautomatic setting, firing in short bursts. The pistol could also be fired fully automatic, but the power would be drained within ~20 seconds, making fully automatic firing only worth using in panic situations.<ref name="Ultimate" /><ref name="Hoth">{{EA|subdomain=starwars|url=starwars/battlefront/planets/hoth|text=''Star Wars Battlefront'' Planets: Explore Hoth}}</ref> Its high rate of fire and reasonable accuracy made it ideal for medium-range combat.<ref name="Planets Battlefront">{{EA|subdomain=starwars|url=starwars/battlefront/planets|text=''Star Wars'' Battlefront Planets|date=2015-11-05|nolive=1}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:Level5Guards-Narkina5.png|thumb|right|250px|Prison guards on Narkina 5 were armed with DH-17 blasters.]]
Imperial [[prison]] guards stationed at the [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex]] also carried the blaster.<ref name="Andor ep 8">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> The [[Cloud-Riders|Cloud-Rider]] [[Baroosh Pawk]] also wielded a [[vibrobayonet]] attached to his DH-17 blaster pistol.<ref name="Solo The Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> [[Rogue One]] personals, [[Corporal]] [[Rodma Maddel]] wielded a DH-17 as did [[Private]] [[Farsin Kappehl]] used one with an extended magazine and spotting laser.<ref name="“RO:">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], many [[rebel trooper]]s were armed with DH-17 blaster pistols.<ref name="Commander" /> When the Alliance members were on the [[Secret mission to Tatooine|run]] from the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]'' to transmit the [[Battle of Scarif|stolen]] [[Death Star plans]] to [[Alderaan]], their ship, the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Tantive IV]]'', was halted and Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s entered the ship. To stop the enemy boarders, [[Alderaanian consular security|Rebel Fleet soldiers]] attacked with DH-17 [[blaster]]s but were eventually defeated by the Imperial forces.<ref name="ANH" /> [[Bodhi Rook]], who assisted in stealing the plans from [[Scarif]], carried a DH-17 blaster.<ref name="SWGOH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>

The Death Star Troopers that Han Solo, Luke Skywalker, and Chewbacca attacked on the first Death Star were armed with DH-17 blasters, but they were killed when Chewbacca pretended to get loose from Han and Luke and started a chaotic battle in the detention center.<ref name="ANH" /> After the [[Death Star]] destroyed, [[Cylo's marines]] had used the blaster to defend [[Cylo's whale fleet]].<ref name="Darth Vader 5">[[Darth Vader (2015) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 5]]</ref> Approximately three and a half years later, the DH-17 was used by rebel soldiers in the defense of [[Echo Base]] during the [[Battle of Hoth]],<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> including [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa Solo]].<ref name="SWGOH" />

[[File:LandoCalrissian-SWBC18.png|thumb|left|220px|Lando wielding a DH-17 blaster pistol]]
During the [[Battle at Elessia]], rebel troopers armed with DH-17s rushed to defend an [[airlock]] from an Imperial boarding party, but were quickly blown back by the explosive charges used by the [[stormtrooper]]s and Commander [[Ellian Zahra]]. Zahra and her soldiers then fired on any remaining rebels in their path.<ref name="Star Wars 8">[[Star Wars (2020) 8|''Star Wars'' (2020) 8]]</ref> Some of the Alliance troopers fought with DH-17s during the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> including [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]], who was in charge of the Alliance [[fleet]].<ref name="SWGOH" /> The [[N5 sentry droid]]s used these blasters while guarding the prisoners on the [[prison]] [[transport]], the ''[[Bothan-5]]''. During a [[Bothan-Five incident|rescue]], the [[bounty hunter]] known as [[Din Djarin]] used one of the droid's DH-17 and blasted a hole in its head.<ref name="Chapter 6">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 6: The Prisoner}}</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SWH Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Zaina]] had a DH-17 blaster named "[[Fleet Blaster]]" while participating in the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]].<ref name="Fleet Blaster" />

Approximately thirty years after the Battle of Endor, a resident of [[Niima Outpost]] on the planet [[Jakku]] used a refurbished DH-17.<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The DH-17 blaster pistols first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. The pistols were first named as "DH-17"s in the [[canon]] continuity from ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', a reference book published in [[April 28]], [[2015]].<ref name="Ultimate" /> Like the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle|E-11]], the props were based off of the British [[Wikipedia:Sterling submachine gun|Sterling Mk.4 submachine gun]], as well as elements from the [[Wikipedia:BSA experimental model 1949|BSA Machine Carbine]].<ref name="Royal Armouries">{{WebCite|url=https://royalarmouries.org/stories/our-collection/make-believe-star-wars-blasters|text='Star Wars' blasters|author=Jonathan Furguson|language=English|archivedate=20220123041013}}</ref>
