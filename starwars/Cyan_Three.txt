'''Cyan Three''' was a [[callsign]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. During the [[Battle of Vrogas Vas|hunt]] for the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on [[Vrogas Vas]], the [[Rebel pilot]] flying under the callsign Cyan Three attempted to eliminate the Dark Lord, but was instead [[Death|killed]] by the [[Sith]] Lord.<ref name="Vader Down 2" />
