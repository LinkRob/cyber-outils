The '''Astrali system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Var Hagen sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square M-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Astrali]], which housed a [[Temple on Astrali|Jedi temple]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. The [[human]] [[Jedi]] [[Indara]] had become familiar with the [[Jedi temple|temple's]] interior<ref name="Wayseeker" /> by around [[160 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{WayseekerDate}}</ref>
