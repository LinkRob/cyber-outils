"'''''Cult Encounters of the Star Wars Universe'''''" was an RPG article written for an unpublished issue of ''[[Star Wars Gamer]]''. It was written by [[Joseph Bongiorno]] and [[Rich Handley]]. Bongiorno expected for the article to be illustrated by [[Joe Corroney]]. It was to be followed up by ''[[Supernatural Encounters in the Star Wars Universe]]''. It was later published on www.starwarstimeline.net without "of the Star Wars Universe" in its title, RPG statistics, or illustrations.

{{Excerpt|<em>
My article (co-written by [[Rich Handley]]) was "Cult Encounters of the Star
Wars Universe". I'm not sure what clever title the editor had in mind, but
that was essentially the title we had in mind. [[Joe Corroney]] likely would
have supplied the artwork.

It covered:
The [[Guild of Vindicators]] ([[Dark Lord's Conscience|Devilworlds: Dark Lord's Conscience]])
The [[Ancient Order of Pessimists]] (''[[Star Wars: Boba Fett: Enemy of the Empire]]'')
[[Five|Seekers of the Soul Worm]] ([[The Pandora Effect|Devilworlds: The Pandora Effect]])
The [[Order of the Terrible Glare/Legends|Order of the Terrible Glare]] ([[Blind Fury!|Devilworlds: Blind Fury]])

The basic premise was that we provided inside information for the Game
Master, explaining who these cultists were, what beings or principles they
served, what powers they might have had, what their main objectives were,
etc.

The Order of the Terrible Glare portion had some brief dialogue between [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke]]
and [[Bodo Baas]] from the [[Holocron/Legends|Holocron]]. Also, the article on the Ancient Order of
Pessimists was a lighter, more humorous piece (in keeping with their
character) unlike the others which were much darker&hellip;

There was no exact date set for publication (for the Cults piece). It
should have followed up the Alien Encounters by an issue or two (which is
why it was sent to LFL for approval), but Gamer was cancelled before it could be published.<ref name="Lost" /></em>|source=Email<ref name="Lost" />|attrib=[[Joseph Bongiorno]]<ref name="Lost" />|copyright=}}
