The '''Coruscant Revolutionary Battalion''' was a [[battalion]] that was active during the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. It shared its name with the Republic capital [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Republic's successor state, thought of the battalion when speaking to [[Tychon Nulvolio]].<ref name="Mask of Fear" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Coruscant Revolutionary Battalion was mentioned in ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', a [[2025]] novel authored by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Mask of Fear" />
