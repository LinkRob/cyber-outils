'''Dantari crystals''' were a type of [[kyber crystal]] found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Dantooine]]. When Dantooine still housed a prominent [[Jedi Temple (Dantooine)|Jedi Temple]], the [[Color|multihued]] Dantari crystal were extremely popular among the [[Jedi]] seeking to build a [[lightsaber]]. Some Jedi found such crystals within the [[egg]]s of the cave-dwelling [[kinrath]], although how they wound up there remained a mystery.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
