A '''comm station''', also known as a '''comm booth''', a '''communications station''', or a '''comm board''', was a communications station for individuals used to send and receive transmissions.

==Description==
A comm booth was a station where individuals could make transmissions. In at least some cases, these booths were capable of sending transmissions to another [[planet]] in real-[[time]].<ref name="Secret Academy" /> The [[starship]] ''[[Coruscant Dawn]]'' had a comm board that lit up like a [[Solstice tree]].<ref name="TRS">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>

The [[holobooth]] functioned in a similar manner, only featuring an additional [[hologram]] for communications,<ref name="To Catch a Jedi">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref> as did [[communications relay]]s.<ref name="Recon Missions">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Recon Missions]]''</ref>

==History==
Though comm booths existed at the [[Arkanis Academy]] on [[Arkanis]], their use was strictly regulated by its overseer, [[Commandant]] [[Brendol Hux]], who felt that conversation with outsiders was detrimental to a military regimen. As such, he only allowed [[Imperial cadet|cadets]] at the academy a single 15-[[Standard minute|minute]] session every [[Standard month|month]] and monitored the transmissions. When [[Zare Leonis]] desired something more private, he took the advice of his fellow cadet, [[Penn Zarang]], and visited [[Jasko's]] in [[Scaparus Port]]. [[Jasko]], a washout cadet, enjoyed irritating Hux by providing comm services that weren't monitored, though his prices were somewhat expensive.<ref name="Secret Academy" />
