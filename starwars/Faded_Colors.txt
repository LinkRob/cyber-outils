"'''Faded Colors'''" is a short story in the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force campaign]] for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''. It was written by [[August Hahn|August]] and [[Cynthia Hahn]] and was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[February 2]], [[2006]].

==Plot summary==
[[Barnab Chistor]] was traveling to the [[Five Masters Academy|Temple of the Five Masters]] when he saw smoke rising from the forest. Running ahead, he found the Temple aflame, with many students and some of the Masters killed. Inside he found the "[[Master in Violet]]" still alive and [[Destruction of the Five Masters Academy|fighting]] a strange assailant. Shooting his [[Unidentified experimental handgun|unique handgun]] at the creature, Chistor managed to repel it; however, after fleeing, it [[Zombie/Legends|animated]] the corpses of slain students and set them upon Chistor and the Master in Violet.
