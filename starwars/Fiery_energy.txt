A [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Force-sensitive|Force user]] could unleash '''fiery energy''', also known as '''Sith fire''', to attack their enemies. The [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] could use the power, which was created through [[Sith alchemy]]. In [[1 BBY]], he wielded the energy to attack the [[Jedi]] [[Ezra Bridger]] and the [[Force-Sensitive Outcast]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] while the pair were in the [[World Between Worlds]].

==Description==
[[File:AhsokaSavesEzraFromFieryEnergy.png|thumb|right|280px|Fiery energy could be used to grab enemies.]]
Manifesting as bright [[Color|blue]] [[Fire|flames]], fiery energy,<ref name="WBW" /> also known as Sith fire,<ref name="TLI 2">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Lothal Insurgency (2)}}</ref> was a [[Dark side of the Force|dark-sided]] [[Force power]]. Fiery energy had a large range and could be used to attack and also grab a target to draw them closer. The [[Sith Lord]] and [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]] was able to use the power, and could conjure the necessary energy by [[Alchemy|performing]] [[Sith alchemy]] using a ritual altar. After the altar filled with flames, Sidious spoke an [[alien]] [[language]] to deploy the flames and could maneuver the energy from his hands. Fiery energy could be interacted with by physical means or blocked by use of [[the Force]].<ref name="WBW" />

==History==
{{Quote|I can't hold it!|Ahsoka Tano temporarily repels Sidious' fiery energy|<ref name="WBW" />}}
[[File:Ezrafieryenergy.png|thumb|left|280px|Ezra Bridger assisting Ahsoka Tano in deflecting Darth Sidious' fiery energy.]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{RebelsDate|413}}</ref> Sidious used fiery energy to attack the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] and the [[Force-Sensitive Outcast]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] in the [[World Between Worlds]]. From the [[ceremonial chamber]]<ref name="WBW" /> in a [[Inquisitorius Headquarters|tower]] on<ref name="Sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="Comprehensive">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]],<ref name="Sacrifice" /> Sidious was able to send his fiery energy through a [[portal]] into the force realm towards the two. As the [[Sith]] Lord attacked, Tano raised<ref name="WBW" /> her<ref name="Comprehensive" /> palms and used the Force to [[Force deflection|deflect]] the onslaught. With some assistance from Bridger, the two were able to defend themselves, but after the initial wave of fire ended, Tano collapsed, greatly fatigued from the effort.<ref name="WBW" />

The two fled from Sidious' second attack, but as they ran, a strand of flames wrapped around Bridger's [[Limb|leg]] and began to pull him towards Sidious. The connection allowed the Sith Lord to begin physically entering the realm. However, when Tano used her [[Ahsoka Tano's white lightsabers|lightsabers]] to sever the strand of flames, the Sith Lord's hand, which had passed through the portal into the World Between Worlds, was repelled. As Bridger and Tano separated and each ran towards the portal they had entered through, the fiery energy forked and continued to pursue them both. Sidious' attack was unsuccessful, as both [[Light side of the Force|light-siders]] escaped from him and exited the World Between Worlds safely. His fiery energy was unable to pass through the portals that the two had disappeared through.<ref name="WBW" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Ezra_and_Ahsoka_are_attacked_by_Sidious.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Final scene of Ahsoka Tano and Ezra Bridger defending themselves from fiery energy based on initial lighting concepts by Molly Denmark.]]
Fiery energy appeared in "[[A World Between Worlds]],"<ref name="WBW" /> the thirteenth episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Four|fourth season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. The episode premiered on [[February 26]], [[2018]],<ref name="Final">{{SW|url=news/the-final-episodes-of-star-wars-rebels-begin-february-19-on-disney-xd|text=The Final Episodes of ''Star Wars Rebels'' Begin February 19 on Disney XD}}</ref> although fiery energy originally was pictured in the Season Four mid-season trailer on [[January 19]] of that year.<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|-jWIDwq44ZI|Star Wars Rebels Mid-Season 4 Trailer (Official)|sw_url=video/star-wars-rebels-mid-season-4-trailer-official}}</ref> The power was identified in the episode guide released on [[StarWars.com]] after the episode's release.<ref name="Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/a-world-between-worlds-episode-gallery|text="A World Between Worlds" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 8}}</ref> It was later identified as Sith fire in "[[The Lothal Insurgency (2)]]," a reference booklet<ref name="TLI 2" /> written by [[Tim Veekhoven]]<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:Credits-TimVeekhoven-Twitter.png|creator=[[Tim Veekhoven]]}}</ref> and released as part of [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series<ref name="TLI 2" /> on [[August 12]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref>

Concept art of Darth Sidious summoning his fiery energy as well as a lighting concept of Ezra Bridger and Ahsoka Tano being attacked by the fiery energy was done by artist [[Molly Denmark]].<ref name="Showdown">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/a-world-between-worlds-behind-the-scenes-gallery|text="A World Between Worlds" Behind-the-Scenes Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref><ref name="Lighting">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/a-world-between-worlds-behind-the-scenes-gallery|text="A World Between Worlds" Behind-the-Scenes Gallery}} {{C|Slide 10}}</ref> During an episode of ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'' that released on [[March 6]], 2018, [[Lucasfilm Story Group]]'s [[Pablo Hidalgo]] spoke about Sidious using the fiery energy as a means to attempt to enter the World Between Worlds. Hidalgo explained that while Sidious was able to use Sith alchemy to view Bridger's location, the Sith Lord was unable to physically enter the realm. The fiery energy was not only a means of attack, but an attempt to gain a foothold into the realm that he could use to pull himself into it.<ref name="Star Wars Show">{{RebelsRecon|uR8Sn_2VRBA|#4.8: Inside "A Fool's Hope" & "Family Reunion &ndash; and Farewell" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/disneyxd-rebels-recon-inside-a-fool-s-hope-and-family-reunion-and-farewell}}</ref> While anchored to Bridger, Sidious' hand was pulled through the portal, but once the connection broke, his point of entry was severed, leaving him unable to enter the World Between Worlds.<ref name="WBW" />
