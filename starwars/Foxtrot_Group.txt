'''Foxtrot Group''' was an elite [[squad]] of [[clone commando]]s led by [[Clone Captain]] [[Gregor]] serving under Marshal [[Cody|Commander Cody]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|General Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="Missing in Action" /> It operated under the [[Special Operations Brigade]]<ref name="HC21" /> and was attached to the [[212th Attack Battalion]].<ref name="Missing in Action" /> During the second [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the unit took part in the [[Battle of Sarrish]], which ended in disaster.<ref name="HC21" /> Amid the mounting [[Death|casualties]],<ref name="Missing in Action" /> the unit was wiped out with the exception of Gregor, who was injured but able to survive.<ref name="HC21" /> Gregor and his team were reported missing in action,<ref name="Gregor bio 4">{{SW|url=gregor-biography-gallery|text=Gregor Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> Gregor ended up in a [[shuttle]] crash<ref name="HC21" /> on the [[planet]] [[Abafar]], suffering from [[amnesia]].<ref name="Missing in Action" />

==Behind the scenes==
Foxtrot Group was first mentioned in Gregor's military records in "[[Missing in Action]]," the twelfth episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''' [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]]. While the in-episode record notes Foxtrot Group to be a sub-divison of the 212th Attack Battalion,<ref name="Missing in Action" /> which was later supported by Gregor's [[Sideshow Collectibles]] figure,<ref name="Sideshow">{{HotToysCite|set=Television Masterpiece Series|packtype=Sixth Scale Figure|pack=#155: Clone Commando Gregor|link=collectibles/star-wars-clone-commando-gregor-hot-toys-914539}}</ref> [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 21|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 21]] claimed the group was only attached to the 212th for the Battle of Sarrish.<ref name="HC21" /> This article assumes the ''Helmet Collection'' issue is incorrect.
