An '''autothruster''' was a type of [[engine]] installed by the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[TIE/IN interceptor]] and [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]].<ref name="Reinforcements of Renown">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2021/2/15/reinforcements-of-renown|text=Reinforcements of Renown}}</ref> It was later incorporated by the [[First Order]] on their [[TIE/vn space superiority fighter]]s.<ref name="X-Wing">{{FFGXW2|set=First Order Conversion Kit|cardname="Recoil"}}</ref>
