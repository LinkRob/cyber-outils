A [[human]] [[Gender|female]] was the '''mother''' of [[Beilert Valance]]. They lived with Valance and his [[Beilert Valance's father|father]] on<ref name="Bounty Hunters 34" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 18">[[Bounty Hunters 18|''Bounty Hunters'' 18]]</ref> [[Chorin]] where Valance's [[parent]]s worked as [[miner]]s. At one point, Valance's mother fixed his toy [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighter]] after it broke, though Valance's father told them not to as it would only fill his head with [[dream]]s when he was destined to work as a [[miner]]. Valance's mother then told the boy's father to stop being a [[blurrg]] in the mud and that their son would have a childhood.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 34" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beilert Valance's mother first appeared, in flashbacks, in the [[Bounty Hunters 34|thirty-fourth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 34" /> on [[May 17]], [[2022]]. Prior to the release of the comic, the character was revealed in a [https://www.starwars.com/news/marvel-bounty-hunters-34-preview preview article] of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[May 9]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-bounty-hunters-34-preview|text=Iden Versio Finds Her Target in Marvel's ''Star Wars: Bounty Hunters'' #34 &mdash; Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
