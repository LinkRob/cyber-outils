'''Apailana''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Monarch of Naboo|monarch]] who served as the [[Queen]] of [[Naboo]] during the final [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Clone Wars]] and the early [[Imperial Era|days]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Episode III" /> She was one of the youngest rulers to be [[Politics|elected]] by [[Naboo (people)|the Naboo]], succeeding [[Neeyutnee]] at the age of twelve.<ref name="ECUC" />

Shortly after the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars, the young [[queen]] attended the [[Padmé Amidala's funeral|funeral]] of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]],<ref name="Episode III" /> who had supported Apailana's election as the new monarch. Apailana did not believe the official statement that Amidala had been [[assassin]]ated by rogue [[Jedi]].<ref name="ECUC" />

==Biography==
Apailana was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[32 BBY]]<ref name="Birth" /> on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Naboo]].<ref name="DB" /> Towards the end of the [[Clone Wars]] Apailana was successfully [[Democracy|elected]] as the [[queen]] of Naboo,<ref name="Episode III" /> succeeding Queen Neeyutnee. During her campaign to become [[Monarch of Naboo|queen]] she was supported by [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]],<ref name="ECUC" /> who represented the [[Chommell sector]] in the [[Galactic Senate]]<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> and who had also served two terms as queen of Naboo where she had [[Battle of Naboo|defended]] the planet from the [[Trade Federation]].<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref>

Though [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] often elected young girls as leaders, believing that they possessed a form of pure, childlike wisdom that adults lacked,<ref name="SW:AEYNtK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> Apailana was still one of the youngest [[Monarchy|monarchs]] ever elected at age twelve.<ref name="ECUC" />

In [[19 BBY]], following the fall of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Declaration of a New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Apailana, along with thousands of others, attended [[Padmé Amidala's funeral|Amidala's funeral]]. Apailana was part of the procession, along with others such as [[Naberrie family|Amidala's family]] and her former [[handmaiden]]s such as [[Umé]] who walked beside Apailana.<ref name="Episode III" />

Publicly, Amidala's [[death]] was blamed on rogue [[Jedi]]<ref name="ECUC" /> and the alleged [[Jedi Order|Jedi]] rebellion that had triggered [[Order 66]]<ref name="Mon Mothma">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Mon Mothma - Senator of Chandrila}}</ref> but privately Apailana did not believe the official statement.<ref name="ECUC" /> After the funeral, Amidala's former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]], [[Sabé]] admitted to herself that she didn't know how she could help the new queen.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref>

Moving into the [[Imperial Era]], Apailana worked through many challenges. She worked to keep her people united under Imperial reign and to stay true to her morals.<ref name="Topps Women">{{Topps|set=2020 Topps Women of Star Wars|cardname=#62: Queen Apailana|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/216003/cid/14374555/2020-Topps-Women-of-Star-Wars-62-Queen-Apailana|nobackup=1}}</ref> She refused to become a puppet of [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], placing her duty to her people first.<ref name="Force Collection">{{ForceCollection|Queen Apailana|star=4}}</ref>

A [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]] named the ''[[Apailana's Promise]]'' served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during its [[Galactic Civil War|struggle]] against the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
[[File:QueenApailana-2024SWHyperspace.png|thumb|right|250px|Apailana wore a  Shiraya fan headdress in honor of Amadala.]]
Apailana measured 1.57 [[meter]]s and had [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s and light skin. During Amidala's funeral, she wore a [[Clothing|headdress]] in tribute to Amidala,<ref name="ECUC" /> as well as makeup and,<ref name="Episode III" /> as with her predecessors and per Naboo tradition, a [[scar of remembrance]]. She did not believe the lies told by the Empire about the Jedi, specifically that they were responsible for Amidala's tragic death. Apailana had the qualities she sought in Naboo's rulers: her pure heart and absolute devotion to the peaceful values of the planet.<ref name="ECUC" />

==Equipment==
The mourning [[Clothing|dress]] that Apailana wore to Amidala's funeral consisted of a purple underdress under a white-gold funeral gown with a purple trim embossed with the royal emblem. She also wore a feather-crested [[shiraya fan headdress]] with mourning beads hanging from it that symbolized Naboo's tears.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
