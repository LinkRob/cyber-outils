A '''ballistic chaff launcher''' was a type of [[projectile launcher]] that fired [[ballistic chaff]]. [[Axis network]] commander [[Luthen Rael]]'s<ref name="DRVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Luthen Rael's Fondor Haulcraft|Fondor Haulcraft]] was equipped with two such weapons disguised as [[thermal exhaust port]]s<ref name="DOF">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> and modified to fire the chaff powerlessly using [[fuel|chemical propellant]]s invisible to [[sensor]]s.<ref name="DRVG" />
