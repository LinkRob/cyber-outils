During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] launched an '''assault on Vivonah'''. [[Standard year|Years]] later, during the time of peace in the [[New Republic]], [[Senator]] [[Ransolm Casterfo]] used it as an example of the so-called [[terrorist]] tactics used by the Rebels.<ref name="Bloodline" />

==Behind the scenes==
The assault on Vivonah was first mentioned in [[Claudia Gray]]'s [[2016]] canon novel [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']].
