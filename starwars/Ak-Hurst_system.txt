The '''Ak-Hurst system''' was an isolated<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[star system]] located within the [[Tarabba sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], in grid square M-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] to [[19 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the [[Obi-Wan's fleet|fleet]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] used the Ak-Hurst system as a [[staging area]] prior to the [[Battle of Utapau]] while Kenobi scouted ahead on the [[planet]] [[Utapau]] in search of the [[General]] [[Grievous]], the Separatist [[Head of State]] and [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies]]. After successfully locating the Separatist leader on Utapau, Kenobi sent his [[astromech droid]], [[R4-G9]], in his [[starfighter]] back to the Ak-Hurst system to alert the standby [[fleet]] and his [[clone trooper]]s of the [[7th Sky Corps]], resulting in a Republic invasion of Utapau as well as Grievous's [[death]] at Kenobi's hands.<ref name="Battles" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ak-Hurst system was mentioned in the [[2021]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Battles" />
