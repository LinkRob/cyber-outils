The ''' Bilbousa saloon''' was located on the [[planet]] [[Nal Hutta]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] traveled to the saloon to find information on a [[Cad Bane's group|group]] of [[bounty hunter]]s that included [[Cad Bane]], [[Moralo Eval]], and [[Rako Hardeen]], who, unbeknownst to the two [[Jedi]], was [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in disguise. Skywalker forced his way through the [[Gamorrean]] guards posted at the door before interrogating the [[Ithorian]] [[bartender]], using a [[Force choke]] when the Ithorian tried to extort him. The pair left as soon as they learned that the fugitives had recently purchased a [[Starship|ship]] and left.<ref name="FAE" />

The saloon's indoor and outdoor areas were packed with dozens of individuals of a variety of alien [[species]]. In addition to the outdoor seating, the inside of the saloon, although it had very little lighting and featured floors covered with trash, was very lively, with [[Twi'lek]] performers entertaining patrons.<ref name="FAE" />
