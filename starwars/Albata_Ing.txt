'''Albata Ing''' was a terrestrial world located in the third position of the [[Felucia system/Legends|Felucia system]], a [[Star system/Legends|star system]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]]'<ref name="TEA" /> [[Thanium sector/Legends|Thanium sector]]. During the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]], this area of space was contested over by the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]].
