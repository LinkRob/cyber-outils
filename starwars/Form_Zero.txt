'''Form Zero''' was a principle followed by many [[Jedi]], not exactly a form of [[lightsaber combat]] despite its name. The principle of Form Zero was that a Jedi's most important martial skill was to know to only draw one's [[lightsaber]] when absolutely necessary. [[Jedi Master]]s who propagated this belief called it "Form Zero" to highlight it as the basis for all other forms of lightsaber combat, building off the central idea that Jedi were keepers of peace and not warriors.<ref name="KoF">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref>

To practitioners of Form Zero, the idea of Jedi warriors learning additional combat expertise, thus gaining temptation to rush towards lightsaber combat over a peaceful resolution, was considered poor technique at best and moral weakness at worst.<ref name="KoF" />
