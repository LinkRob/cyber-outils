'''CS-Squad''' was an [[infantry]] [[squad]] of the [[First Order military]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="The New Trooper" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{InsiderCite|190|Resistance Revisited|''Resistance'' Revisited}} establishes that the events of [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|''Star Wars Resistance'' Season One]], which includes the episode "[[The New Trooper]]," takes place thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], which corresponds to [[34 ABY]], according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> CS-Squad, led by [[Commander]] [[Pyre]] and [[Sergeant]] [[CS-812]], deployed to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Castilon]] to enforce the [[First Order]]'s [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupation]] of the ''[[Colossus]]'' [[Fuel|refueling]] station. Operating as [[Law enforcement agency|peacekeepers]] in the wake of several [[pirate]] raids on the platform, their presence was welcomed by some civilians such as the [[human]] [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Tamara Ryvora]]. Others, however, opposed the First Order's security measures, leading to a riot among the ''Colossus''{{'}} [[alien]] population.<ref name="The New Trooper" />

In addition to their peacekeeping mission, CS-Squad was tasked with hunting [[Spy|spies]] who worked against the First Order's interests. As such, citizens were regularly subjected to inspection, arrest and interrogation by [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]], inciting to discontent among populace. In the midst of the occupation, the [[Resistance]] agent [[Kazuda Xiono]] [[First Order stormtrooper armor|impersonated]] [[CS-515]], one of the [[trooper]]s in CS-Squad, [[Theft of First Order data rod|in order to spy on the First Order]]. Yet his behavior was considered irregular for a stormtrooper, as noted by Pyre and CS-812, who believed "CS-515" was in need of a [[memory wipe]]. However, Xiono managed to switch places with the true CS-515 which, in turn, led to the [[Prisoner|arrest]] of the real [[soldier]] while the imposter remained at large.<ref name="The New Trooper" />
