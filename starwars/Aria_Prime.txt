'''Aria Prime''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Catalyst" /> in the [[Aria Prime system]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was where [[Lyra Erso]] was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[48 BBY]].<ref name="ROUVG">{{GuideDate|RO|B|47|[[Lyra Erso]]'s [[Reproduction|birth]] on Aria Prime}}</ref> Lyra's [[Lyra Erso's mother|mother]] lived and worked on Aria Prime as an [[artist]]. Due to the [[Clone Wars]] and her [[Parent|mother's]] failing health, Lyra suggested to her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]] that they relocate to Aria Prime from [[Coruscant]] for the duration of the conflict.<ref name="Catalyst" />
