'''Chapel Street''' was a street in the industrial quarter of [[Capital City]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. This street was filled with old warehouses, some built with [[adobe]] and synthetic stone. [[1044 Chapel]] was the address of an old warehouse on this street where [[Yahenna Laxo]] of the [[Gray Syndicate]] would sometimes shelter individuals wanted by the [[Galactic Empire]] in exchange for [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="secret">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref>
