'''''Crimson Reign'' 4''' is the fourth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Crimson Reign]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Steven Cummings]], [[Victor Olazaba]], and [[Guru-eFX]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[April 27]], [[2022]]. The issue contains the story '''Part 4: "The Knights"'''.

==Publisher's summary==
''THE KNIGHTS! THE KNIGHTS OF REN have their role to play in QI'RA'S grand scheme to destabilize THE EMPIRE, and their task is arguably the most important&hellip;Something crucial is locked away in FORTRESS VADER, and the Knights must steal it. A heist on the SITH-controlled furnace world of MUSTAFAR&hellip;with the Galaxy as the prize!''

==Plot summary==
'''The Knights of Ren'''

'''The Outer Rim. [[Varnak]].'''

In the desolate wilds of Varnak, [[Qi'ra|Lady Qi’ra]] meets the infamous [[Knights of Ren]] for the first time. [[Ren (human)|Ren]], intrigued by her reputation, removes his helmet with arrogant flair, hoping to charm her. Qi’ra plays along just long enough to make it clear she hasn’t come for pleasantries. She’s here for power—and for theirs.

Ren warns her that his name carries danger, that most who know it know better than to seek him out. But Qi’ra, guided by information from her trusted Archivist, knows who they are and what they once were. She offers them an alternative to their current life of scavenging, hiding, and wondering when a [[Sith]] might decide they’ve outlived their usefulness. In return for their loyalty and strength, she promises wealth, autonomy, and, most importantly, protection from the Sith. Ren, tempted by the offer, agrees to hear more.

'''Now. [[Night Buzzard|The Night Buzzard]].'''

Hovering in orbit above [[Mustafar]], the Knights of Ren receive their mission: infiltrate [[Fortress Vader]] and steal an artifact known only as the [[Screaming Key]]. Some of the Knights hesitate, knowing that drawing the attention of the Sith is suicide, but Ren dismisses their concerns. He reminds them that the Sith are not their allies, merely stronger predators. Qi’ra’s network has assured them that [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] is away investigating the death of several of [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine’s]] [[Emperor's Royal Guard|royal guards]]. This is their chance.

With plans in hand and confidence in their favor, they descend to the volcanic surface. The lava flows obscure sensor readings, allowing them a direct approach. Using [[the Force]], they part a magma fall that conceals a secret entrance. Inside, the fortress reeks of [[Dark side of the Force|the dark side]]. Sith remnants, corrupted machinery, and ancient traps litter the halls. One Knight fires on a severed head when it twitches to life—an omen of the place they’ve dared enter.

In the fortress's depths, they find a vast [[bacta]] pumping station and desecrate it as a message. But the real objective lies beyond a blast door. Guarded by [[Vaneé]], Vader’s fanatical attendant, they are warned to leave. Ren opens fire, but the bolts glance off Vaneé’s personal shields. The alarm is triggered. [[Stormtroopers]] flood in. A brutal battle ensues.

Ren cuts through the door, entering what seems to be a Sith archive. He listens through the Force and hears the whispering wail of the Screaming Key. He finds it in a containment box and takes it. But something has changed. The troopers are no longer attacking. The silence is not a sign of victory.

'''Darth Vader has returned.'''

He enters without a word, suffocating one of the Knights with a gesture. Ren confronts him directly, engaging in a furious [[lightsaber]] duel. Vader mocks [[Ren (lightsaber)|Ren’s red blade]] and his stolen [[Ren|name]]. Ren insists he earned the saber, taking it from the one who bore the title before him. He claims the name as a legacy of strength. Vader, unimpressed, calls them all pathetic.

Ren is slashed across the torso, bloodied but alive. His crew blasts open a hole in the wall, and he dives through it, using the Force to slow his fall. But the Screaming Key is ripped from his hand mid-flight, seized by Vader’s power. Hovering in midair, the relic seems lost—until the Knights regroup and open fire on Vader, briefly staggering him. Ren snatches the Key back and escapes into the night.

'''The Price of Defiance'''

One Knight lies dead, and the rest are now marked by [[Galactic Empire|the Empire]]. They’ve crossed the line from [[Scavenger|scavengers]] to enemies of the Sith. Whatever the Screaming Key unlocks, it had better be worth it—because the Knights of Ren just made themselves a target.

And Darth Vader never forgets.
