'''Choja''' was a member of the [[Pyke Syndicate]] who was tasked by the [[Pyke]] [[contract broker]] [[Soren Chrephontis]] with handling the sell of a [[Verillian Egg]] aboard the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The [[unidentified Pyke (Verillian Egg)|Pyke]] who was tasked with delivering the egg to Choja was sent a data message that specified that Choja would handle the sell and that they only needed to protect the egg and get it into Choja's hands. However a [[human]] named [[Zana Gatta]] tricked the Pyke, stole the egg and planned to sell it to the [[Crimson Dawn]] [[syndicate]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
While searching for blackmail material on Gatta, a [[gambler]] named [[Kay Vess]] accessed the data message from a [[computer|terminal]] in the Pyke's room aboard the ''[[Morenia]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
