'''Anap Ree-Di''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual who was a [[Jedi Knight]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Ree-Di's connection to [[the Force]] allowed her to have a special relationship to historical objects and the ripples they created in history. During the war, Ree-Di traveled aboard the [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] ''[[Stellar Rise]]'' as they transported artifacts from the [[High Republic Era]]. During the voyage, the [[light cruiser]] was [[Infiltration of the Stellar Rise|infiltrated]] by [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Separatist Supreme Commander]] [[General]] [[Grievous]], who confronted Ree-Di. While Grievous believed he had the upper hand after having taken the bridge with little resistance, Ree-Di was eventually able to lull him into a trap that ejected Grievous from the ship.<ref name="Saber Truth" />
