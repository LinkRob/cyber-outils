"'''100 and Counting'''" is an article written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and published in [[January]] [[2013]]'s [[Star Wars Insider 139|''Star Wars Insider'' 139]]. It looks back at the history of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated series and lists every episode aired in production order. ''[[100 and Counting Expanded]]'', a follow-up to this article, later appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 150|''Star Wars Insider'' 150]].

The article was reprinted in ''[[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2018]]''.
