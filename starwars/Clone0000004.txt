'''Clone0000004''' was the designation of a [[human]] [[clone trooper]] from [[Kamino]] who served the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Clone" /> By [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The [[Kamino]] section in ''[[LEGO Star Wars Yoda's Galaxy Atlas]]'' take place during the [[Clone Wars]] which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the end of to [[19 BBY]].</ref> he posted a five-star review of Kamino in ''[[Yoda's Guide to the Galaxy]]'', a book written by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] during his exile on [[Dagobah]]. In his review, Clone0000004 said that he liked Kamino, however he was unsure whether or not the other clones agreed with his opinion.<ref name="Atlas" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clone0000004 was first mentioned in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Yoda's Galaxy Atlas]]''.<ref name="Atlas" />
