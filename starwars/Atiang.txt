'''Atiang''' were [[Standard year|annual]] hunting awards held on the<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> [[planet]]<ref name="data">{{Databank|rodia|Rodia}}</ref> of [[Rodia]]. The [[Salus Corporation]] [[DF-D1 duo-flechette rifle]], a popular weapon among [[Rodian]] [[big game hunter]]s, was frequently featured at the yearly Atiang.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Atiang was mentioned in the [[2019]] [[canon]] ''[[Fantasy Flight Games]]'' [[roleplaying game]] sourcebook ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
