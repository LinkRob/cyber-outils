'''''Death Star''''' was a canceled ''[[Star Wars]]'' game from [[LucasArts]]. It allowed players to take control of the [[Death Star/Legends|Death Star]] battlestation. It had already gone through QA testing before being canceled.<ref>{{GameSpot|subdomain=au|url=news/canceled-star-wars-games-revealed-6415086|text=Canceled Star Wars games revealed|date=2013-09-27}}</ref>
