'''Erin Kellyman''' was born on [[October 17]], [[1998]]<ref name="This Week Posters" /> in [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="Screen Daily" /> She is an actor known for playing [[Enfys Nest]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="SW.com" /> a film which was released on [[May 25]], [[2018]].<ref name="Solo">{{SW|url=films/solo|text=''Solo: A Star Wars Story''}}</ref> She is also known for playing [[Wikipedia:Éponine|Éponine Thénardier]] in the [[Wikipedia:BBC|BBC]] series ''[[Wikipedia:Les Misérables (British TV series)|Les Misérables]]'',<ref name="Curtis Brown">{{WebCite|url=http://www.curtisbrown.co.uk/client/erin-kellyman|text=Erin Kellyman|work=Curtis Brown|archivedate=20201024175740}}</ref> [[W:c:marvelcinematicuniverse:Flag Smasher|Karli Morgenthau]] in the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Wikipedia:The Falcon and the Winter Soldier|The Falcon and the Winter Soldier]]''<ref name="EW.com">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/tv/falcon-and-the-winter-soldier-erin-kellyman-karli-morgenthau|text=''The Falcon and the Winter Soldier'' star Erin Kellyman on her masked antagonist|author=Coggan, Devan|date=2021-04-02|work=''[[Wikipedia:Entertainment Weekly|Entertainment Weekly]]''|archivedate=20210403232543}}</ref> and [[W:c:willowufgood:Jade Claymore|Jade Claymore]] in the [[Lucasfilm]] television series ''[[Wikipedia:Willow (TV series)|Willow]]''.
