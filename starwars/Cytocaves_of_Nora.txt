{{Quote|I don't think it was engineered. I've worked in the gene farms. This one looks evolved. Too ugly. This one, on the other hand, looks like she was farmed in the Cytocaves of Nora.|Kuiil, about Grogu and Cara Dune|<ref name="Chapter 7">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 7: The Reckoning}}</ref>}}
The '''Cytocaves of Nora''' were a location where [[Sentience|sentient]] beings were [[Bioengineering|engineered]].<ref name="Chapter 7" /> They were located in<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Nora system]].<ref name="Appendix" />

[[File:KuiilCommentsOnDune-TheReckoning.png|thumb|right|300px|Kuiil (leftmost) commented that Cara Dune (second from right) looked like she had been farmed in the Cytocaves of Nora.]]
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|7}}</ref> the [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]], accompanied by<ref name="Chapter 7" /> the [[Alderaanian]] [[human]] ex&ndash;[[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[shock trooper]] [[Carasynthia Dune|Cara Dune]], brought the [[Yoda's species|alien]] [[youngling]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] to the [[Ugnaught]] [[moisture farmer]] [[Kuiil]] on<ref name="Chapter 7" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Arvala-7]].<ref name="SWE" /> Kuiil, who had been employed by the [[Galactic Empire]] as an indentured laborer and had worked in [[gene farm]]s, noted that Grogu, whom<ref name="Chapter 7" /> he<ref name="SWE" /> had met previously, had a too "evolved" and "ugly" appearance to have been [[gene]]tically engineered. He then contrasted the statement by claiming that Dune looked like she had been farmed in the Cytocaves of Nora, to which the ex-shock [[trooper]] responded with a slight smile.<ref name="Chapter 7" />
