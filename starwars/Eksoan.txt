'''Eksoan''' was a company that manufactured [[power generator]]s including the [[Class-4T3 power generator]]<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" /> and the [[Class-5B1 duplex power generator]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Galactic Empire]] used the Eksoan Class-4T3 to power its [[BlasTech Industries]] [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]]s, although the generators were prone to overheat in a spectacular way unless closely monitored.<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />

While living in the wrecked Imperial [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Galactic Civil War)|All Terrain Armored Transport]] ''[[Hellhound Two]]'' on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]] during the [[New Republic Era]], the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] hooked the [[fuel cell]] from the [[All Terrain Armored Transport|walker's]] [[blaster cannon]] [[energizer]] up to an Eksoan power generator that was attached to<ref name="RSG" /> a homemade<ref name="CL">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> grid of [[TIE series]] [[solar collector]]s and other [[fuel]] cells.<ref name="RSG" /> She<ref name="TLJVD" /> mentioned the company on a note she added to a blueprint of the walker included in her [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]]<ref name="RSG" /> by [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' includes an addendum at the end describing some of the events of {{Film|VII}}, written several [[Standard day|days]] after they take place. The addendum is the final entry in the guide and is included chronologically after the entry mentioning the Sitter. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the film to [[34 ABY]].</ref> The Empire's successor, the [[First Order]], used the Eksoan Class-5B1 duplex power generator to power its [[Sonn-Blas Corporation]] [[FWMB-10 repeating blaster]]s.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Eksoan was first mentioned in ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'', a [[2015]] replica journal written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="RSG" /> In the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'', Eksoan is mispelt as "Ekspan."<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> The [[Eksoan/Legends|company]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'', a [[1989]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Greg Gorden]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="ImperialSourcebook">''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]''</ref>
