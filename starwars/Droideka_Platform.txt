'''Droideka Platforms''' were structures utilized by both [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They were designed to support a lone, stationary [[Droideka]], allowing the [[droid]] to operate as a [[turret]] with a comprehensive field of vision. There were two deployable configurations of Droideka Platforms, one featuring a [[Droideka Sentinel]] and the other a [[Droideka Oppressor]], both heavily [[Weapon|armed]] and [[Deflector shield|shielded]].<ref name="Commander" />
