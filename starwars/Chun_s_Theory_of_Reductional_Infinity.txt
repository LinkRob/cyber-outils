'''Chun's Theory of Reductional Infinity''' was a theory formulated by an individual called [[Chun]].<ref name="MedStar I" /> In [[20 BBY/Legends|20 BBY]],<ref name="TERC">''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' dates the events of ''[[MedStar I: Battle Surgeons]]'' to [[20 BBY/Legends|20 BBY]].</ref> the theory had yet to be solved. [[Protocol droid/Legends|Protocol droid]] [[I-5YQ]]&mdash;not occupied with his work at the [[Republic Mobile Surgical Unit 7]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Drongar/Legends|Drongar]]&mdash;thought about using his free [[Droid brain/Legends|cognitive module]] in solving the theory.<ref name="MedStar I" />

==Behind the scenes==
The theory was first mentioned in ''[[MedStar I: Battle Surgeons]]'', the first novel of the [[MedStar Duology]]. Published in [[2004]], ''Battle Surgeons'' was written by [[Steve Perry]] and [[Michael Reaves]].<ref name="MedStar I" />
