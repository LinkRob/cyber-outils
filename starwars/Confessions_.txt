"'''Confessions!'''" is an article published in [[Star Wars Insider 150|''Star Wars Insider'' 150]]. Compiled by [[Jake Devine]], it is a series of behind-the-scenes quotes by various members of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' cast and crew in a quiz where readers can guess who said the quote.
