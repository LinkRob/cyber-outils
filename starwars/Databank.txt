A '''databank''',<ref name="Uprising" /> or '''data bank''',<ref name="Path of the Lightsaber 1">[[Path of the Lightsaber, Vol. 1|''Path of the Lightsaber'', Vol. 1]]</ref> was a type of electronic terminal used for data storage. During the [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]], a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] located a databank on [[Anoat]] for [[Tam Bastion]], a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Uprising" />
