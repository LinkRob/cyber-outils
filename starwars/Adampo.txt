'''Adampo''' was a [[Yarkora]] [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] and a member of the [[Jedi High Council]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

==Biography==
He<ref name="Into the Dark" /> possessed [[Color|blond]] hair<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" /> and long [[Color|white]] whiskers that dangled from his chin, which he occasionally stroked while speaking. When the Council convened to discuss the [[Jedi Order]]'s involvement in [[Galactic Republic]] action against the [[Nihil]], Adampo was present via [[hologram]]. Adampo opposed the idea of military action, insisting that the [[Jedi]] were not a military force. Master [[Oppo Rancisis]] countered his point, stating that the Jedi had been a military force in the past, winning the [[Great Sith War]]. Ultimately, the vote was left with a tie after ten of the eleven Masters present had voted, and the decision was left up to [[Jora Malli]], who chose to involve the Jedi in the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] effort.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

Adampo later [[Death|perished]] during an [[Evacuation of Inad Komesh|evacuation]] of the [[moon]] [[Inad Komesh]] when he was crushed by a collapsing [[Unidentified tower|tower]].<ref name="Into the Light">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Adampo was scholarly and a noted linguist. He backed [[Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]]'s plan to build science vessels for exploration in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] and endorsed putting archives in [[Jedi outpost]] [[Jedi temple|temples]]. He was pacifistic until it became clear that conflict with the Nihil was unavoidable.<ref name="Chronicle">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref>

==Equipment==
Adampo wielded a [[Color|blue]]-bladed [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 1">[[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 1]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Adampo first appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''. Initial versions of the novel referred to the Jedi Master with she/her pronouns.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> However, after ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'' use he/him pronouns for Adampo,<ref name="Into the Dark" /> e-book editions of ''Light of the Jedi'' were updated with the character's correct pronouns.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
