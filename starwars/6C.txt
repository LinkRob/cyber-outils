'''6C''' was the room number of a small apartment that was located in [[Pons Ora]], a small [[City|settlement]] on the remote [[desert]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Abafar]]. It was owned by [[Borkus]], a [[Gender|male]] [[Sullustan]] who was also proprietor of the [[Restaurant|diner]] [[Power Sliders]].<ref name="Missing in Action" />
