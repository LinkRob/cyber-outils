The '''GA-60s double laser cannon''', also known as the '''GA-60s twin rotating blaster cannon''' was a type of [[blaster cannon]] manufacted by [[Taim & Bak]]. The [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] had a GA-60s twin rotating blaster cannon on both of its foldable wings<ref name="BMF22" /> as had ''[[Scythe]]'' [[shuttle]]s.<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>
