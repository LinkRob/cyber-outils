The '''Eighth Division''' was a cell of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]], created following the [[fleet]]'s scatter after the [[Battle of Hoth]]. The Eighth Division regrouped near [[Malastare]], and was later contacted by the [[Fourth Division]]. However, the [[Galactic Empire]] had broken Rebel Alliance codes, and learned the location of both divisions. The Eighth Division was [[Battle near Malastare|completely destroyed]], but a majority of the Fourth Division managed to escape thanks to the assistance of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Star Wars 1" />
