'''''Doctor Aphra'' 31''' is the thirty-first issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 3]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE PARTING GLANCE! Is this the end of the line for former allies APHRA and TOLVAN? DARTH VADER'S least favorite archaeologist must face a cold hard truth that will shake her to her core!!! Can she forgive herself for the chaos she's about to unleash upon the galaxy?''
