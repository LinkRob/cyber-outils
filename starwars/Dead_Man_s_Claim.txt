'''Dead Man's Claim''' was an area of the [[Sayma Nebula]] near [[Hauler's Keep]] in the [[Wild Space]] [[Khepi system]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dead Man's Claim appeared as a labeled area in the Khepi system map in the "A Pirate's Fortune" story expansion to the  [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and released by [[Ubisoft]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> The area had eleven treasures that could be collected.<ref name="Outlaws" />
