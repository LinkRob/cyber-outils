'''Frostberry''' was a type of [[berry]] [[Diet|edible]] by [[Mirialan]]s.<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> as she ate a piece of [[fruit]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Wevo]], the Mirialan [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] considered its taste somewhere between that of frostberry and [[melon]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

==Behind the scenes==
Frostberry was mentioned in the [[2021]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
