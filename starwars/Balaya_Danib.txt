'''Balaya Danib''' was an individual that was on [[Achra Station]], a [[space station]] located in the [[Akiva system]],<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During their time aboard an announcement was broadcast of the station's speaker system in [[Huttese]] to request Danib's presence in the station's Engineering Bay.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Balaya Danib was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. Danib is mentioned in an ambient announcement that players can overhear while on Achra Station.<ref name="Outlaws" />
