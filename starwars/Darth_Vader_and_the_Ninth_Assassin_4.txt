'''''Darth Vader and the Ninth Assassin'' 4''' is the fourth issue of five in the ''[[Star Wars: Darth Vader and the Ninth Assassin]]'' comic mini-series written by [[Tim Siedell]].

==Publisher's summary==
''In the ruins of an ancient moon, Darth Vader encounters an evil people with a dark prophecy and a powerful weapon. They are a threat to the Empire and Vader will force them to serve&mdash;or crush them all! Meanwhile, the assassin hunting Vader still lurks...''
