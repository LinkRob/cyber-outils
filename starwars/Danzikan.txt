'''Danzikans''' were a [[swamp]]-dwelling, two-headed [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[swamp]] world of [[Danzik]]. Similar to the [[Troig]]s, both heads were independent and they used this to their advantage in card games and business deals. They were also often thought of as untrustworthy because of their tendency to cheat.<ref name="Alien Archive" /> [[Lark and Jonk]] were members of this species.<ref name="Official Guide" />
