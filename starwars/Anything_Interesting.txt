"'''Anything Interesting'''" was the title of one of [[Ortolan]] [[musician]] [[Maximilian Rebo|Max Rebo's]] albums. One of [[Maximilian Rebo's human fan|Max Rebo's fans]],<ref name="TSS" /> a [[human]]<ref name="Species">[[Maximilian Rebo's human fan|Max Rebo's human fan]] is nearly identical in appearance to the same model used for [[Taryish Juhden]], which {{Film|VII}} confirms to be [[human]].</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]], was frustrated at being unable to locate the album on the planet, as the fan considered the album the best released by Rebo. The fan expressed this frustration to a traveler in [[Niima Outpost]] and noted that they could only locate what they considered Rebo's worst album&mdash;"[[Junk and Garbage]]."<ref name="TSS" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Anything Interesting" was mentioned in the [[2022]] [[Canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', published by [[TT Games]].<ref name="TSS" />
