The '''debacle on Vrant Tarnum'''<ref name="HRA 8" /> was an incident between the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Galactic Republic]] against the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s. It was sparked in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Grizal]] when [[Jedi Master]] [[Emerick Caphtor]] undertook a mission to [[Vrant Tarnum]] while investigating the mysterious [[death]] of Master [[Loden Greatstorm]]. At the request of [[Jedi High Council]] member [[Stellan Gios]], Caphtor traveled to the [[Kharvashark Ruins|base]] on Vrant Tarnum with a unit of [[Republic Defense Coalition troop|Galactic Republic troopers]]. Most of the pirates stationed at the base fled upon the Republic group's approach, with Caphtor and his [[soldier]]s fighting those Nihil who had not managed to escape, taking as many as they could alive as prisoners. Upon inspecting the ruins with his [[droid]], [[Q-2]], Caphtor discovered a [[cave]] of some significance, with the lyrics of an [[Shrii ka rai ka rai|old nursery rhyme]] he had found to be in some way connected to his investigation carved around it.<ref name="TOS 1" />
