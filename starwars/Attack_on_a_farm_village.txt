During the early [[Imperial Era]], the [[Eleventh Brother]] '''attacked''' a [[Unidentified farm village|farm village]] in response to a report of a [[Jedi]] sighting. The Jedi in question, former [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], had been living and working as "Ashla" in the village. Tano had hidden her true identity until she used her [[Force power]]s to save [[Unidentified village sister|another worker]] from being crushed by falling hay bales. Despite her effort to be covert, the woman Tano saved noticed and, grateful, approached Tano that night and promised to keep her secret, despite Tano's denials. However, the worker's [[Unidentified village brother|brother]], who supported the [[Galactic Empire]], overheard the conversation. Hoping to be rewarded for his loyalty, while his sister and Tano were away the next [[Standard day|day]] delivering the harvest, he reported Tano to the Empire.<ref name="Resolve" />

The Eleventh Brother arrived at the village and, finding no Jedi present, destroyed the settlement, [[Death|killing]] all of its inhabitants save for the brother and an [[Unidentified old man (Ahsoka Tano)|old man]], believing the brother had lied to him. Tano and the sister arrived to find the village in flames, and despite what he had done, Tano saved the brother from the lethal "reward" the Inquisitor attempted to give him before facing the [[Dark side of the Force|dark sider]] herself. The Inquisitor recognized Tano, commenting that she was supposedly dead and that he would be rewarded for killing her. However, Tano was able to quickly disarm the Inquisitor, before killing him with his own [[lightsaber]]. In the aftermath, Tano used a [[comlink]] she had been given by [[Alderaanian]] [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] to call for a lift for herself and the survivors, so they could start a new life elsewhere while she took up Organa's offer to join the fight against the Empire.<ref name="Resolve" />
