'''Cat Staggs''' is an artist who worked on over 130 [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] sketch cards for [[Topps]], as well as the ''Star Wars: 30th Anniversary Trading Card Set''. She has also worked on a [http://www.starwars.com/kids/activity/draw Learn to Draw] tutorial and [http://www.starwars.com/kids/activity/crafts/f20051026/index.html Halloween mask] activity for [[StarWars.com]], in addition to illustrating the short story "[[Fists of Ion]]."

She began her ''[[Star Wars]]'' work after meeting [[Stephen J. Sansweet]], who is Head of Fan Relations at [[Lucasfilm]], at a convention in Chicago.
