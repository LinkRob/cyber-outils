The '''Dismantler Torpedo''' was a model of [[termite torpedo launcher]] manufactured by [[Arakyd Industries]]. It cost 10,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and launched [[termite torpedo]]s, a type of [[torpedo]] containing dozens of [[termite droid]]s.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dismantler Torpedo was first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref>
