The '''Ascada River''' was a [[Water|river]] located in the [[Ascaal district]] of the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> there was unusually high rainfall during a [[hurricane]] and, a [[Standard week|week]] later, this led authorities to predict flooding in the district. [[Botto Fna]]&mdash;a wind report [[Journalist|presenter]] for [[Holonet News Toshara]]&mdash;shared the forecast of flooding in a [[HoloNet News]] report, advising those listeners who planned to travel near the river to seek an alternate route.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ascada River was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
