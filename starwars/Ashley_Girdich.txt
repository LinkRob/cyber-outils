'''Ashley Girdich''' is an executive-technical program manager at [[Walt Disney Imagineering]]'s Research & Development group (WDI R&D). She has worked on multiple iterations of "living characters" based around the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise which have appeared in [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney Parks]] with varying degrees of autonomy. Most notable in ''Star Wars'' are [[J4-K3]], the autonomous [[droid]] who was featured alongside Girdich in the [[2019]] [[Disney+]] documentary [[Wikipedia:One Day at Disney (film)|One Day at Disney]] following a series of play-tests at [[Disneyland]]<ref name="One Day Film">{{VisualMediaCite|title=[[Wikipedia:One Day at Disney (film)|''One Day At Disney'']]|director=Fritz Mitchell|prod=[[Wikipedia:Disney Publishing Worldwide|Disney Publishing Worldwide]] and [[Wikipedia:Fifth Season (company)|Endeavor Content]]|year=[[2019]]}}</ref> and [[Wikipedia:D23 (Disney)|D23]]<ref name="Attractions">{{YouTube|video=skrzmW9opxA|text=Jake the Star Wars Droid roams around Disney Parks and Resorts Pavilion at D23 Expo 2017|channel=@attractionsmagazine|name=Attractions Magazine|date=2017-07-22}}</ref> from [[2016]]<ref name="Lapinig">{{YouTube|video=AQPFnI1XyE8|text=New Jake Droid in Star Wars Launch Bay! (Disneyland)|channel=@keithlapinig|name=Keith Lapinig|date=2016-05-29}}</ref> to [[2017]],<ref name="Disneygeek">{{WebCite|url=https://blog.disneygeek.com/2017/10/06/jake-the-droid-visits-tomorrowland-at-disneyland|text=Jake the Droid visits Tomorrowland at Disneyland|work=Geek's Blog|archivedate=20180327103230}}</ref> and the still-in-development [[BDX droid]]s, which have appeared at Disney Parks around the world, including [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars:'' Galaxy's Edge]] at both Disneyland and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]].<ref name="Olaf" />
