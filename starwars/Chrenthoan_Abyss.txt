The '''Chrenthoan Abyss''' was an area of [[Realspace|space]] in the [[Chrenthoa sector]] within the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Interior]] regions.<ref name="Interior" /> Situated at the coordinates J-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it was controlled by the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}} Furthermore, since the appearance of the Chrenthoan Abyss in ''Victory's Price'' occurs prior to the Battle of Jakku, it must also have taken place that year.</ref> after much of the Empire's territory had been lost to the [[New Republic]], an Imperial presence remained in the Chrenthoan Abyss. The area was marked as one of the largest territories occupied by Imperial [[warlord]]s and opportunists, alongside the [[Faultheen sector]] and the [[Anoat sector]], on a [[Hologram|holographic]] [[map]] displayed during a [[New Republic High Command]] meeting. In that meeting, [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] claimed that territories like the Chrenthoan Abyss were isolated, with only a few posing a serious threat to the New Republic.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chrenthoan Abyss first appeared in a hologram in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the third installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy which was written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
