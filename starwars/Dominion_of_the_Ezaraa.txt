The '''Dominion of the Ezaraa''', or the '''Ezaraa Dominion''', was an organization of war-mongering members of the [[Ezaraa]] [[species]]. They wished to overthrow the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Screaming Citadel 1" /> and take control of the entire [[The galaxy|galaxy]] and wider [[universe]]. However, the dominion only consisted of two [[Ezaraa continents|continents]] on the [[planet]] [[Ezaraa (planet)|Ezaraa]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" />
