{{Quote|&hellip;you didn't see how the people looked at me, Mother. Even friends, like Grandfer Aurin. They've heard rumors&hellip;lies&hellip;|Marda Ro, to the Mother|<ref name="POV">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref>}}
[[Grandfer]] '''Aurin''' was an elderly [[Umbaran]] who ran a used [[droid]] parts [[Aurin's stall|stall]] in the [[City|town]] [[Ferdan]]'s [[Ferdan market|market]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dalna]]. The [[droid-monger]] was friends with the [[Path of the Open Hand]] member [[Marda Ro]] and often allowed her to use the table next to their stall to give gifts to passersby with her group of the Path's [[Littles]]. On one such occasion in [[382 BBY]], the [[Youngling|child]] [[Eff]] sat on Aurin's lap as Ro preached about [[the Force]] to those in the market.

By later the same [[Standard year|year]], Aurin had become wary of the Path due to their incitement of the [[Battle of Jedha]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[moon]]. The [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Ric Farazi]] questioned the Umbaran about the Path due to their friendship with Ro, making them uncomfortable. When Ro later came to visit Aurin, they were unsettled by her and refused to engage in casual conversation, only informing her that her usual table was not available and that they had been questioned by Farazi.

==Biography==
===Ferdan marketplace===
{{Quote|This incident here alters the Force, changes it in ripples, pulls it, directs it, and those changes change other things, out in the galaxy. Who is to say that your tricks here have not set in motion ripples that will result in something dangerous where you cannot see it?''"<br />"''That's not how the Force works.|Marda Ro and Kevmo Zink, as Aurin and others watched with interest|<ref name="POD" />}}
[[File:MardaRo-PathOfDeceit.png|thumb|left|235px|Aurin often saved a table in the marketplace for Marda Ro (pictured) and her group of Littles.]]
[[Grandfer]] Aurin was a<ref name="POD" /> [[Gender|non-binary]]<ref name="Email" /> [[Umbaran]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. They resided on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dalna]]<ref name="POD" /> and [[Merchant|operated]] a [[Aurin's stall|stall]]<ref name="POV" /> selling used [[droid]] parts in the [[Ferdan market|marketplace]] of the [[City|town]] [[Ferdan]]. The [[droid-monger]] became familiar with the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|religious]] group residing on Dalna, although they chose not to join them. At some point, Aurin met Path of the Open Hand member [[Marda Ro]] and enjoyed how she preached about living in harmony with and for [[the Force]]. They began saving a table in the marketplace for Ro and the [[Littles]]&mdash;the Path's [[Youngling|children]]&mdash;that she supervised so they could give gifts to passersby. The [[Klatooinian]] child [[Eff]] became especially fond of Aurin's company.<ref name="POD" />

In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' and ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Ro brought her group of Littles to the marketplace and settled at the table Aurin had saved for them. For the first [[Standard hour|hour]] of their gifting, Eff sat on the Umbaran's lap while Ro preached the Path's beliefs to any passerby who would listen. One market visitor&mdash;[[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kevmo Zink]]&mdash;[[Force power|used]] the Force to [[Telekinesis|lift]] a [[river rose]] in the [[Atmosphere|air]], hoping to delight [[Simi|one]] of the Littles. Ro was horrified and began explaining to Zink why she viewed the action as an abuse of the Force, as Aurin and several others watched them in interest. The group of Littles and their caretaker departed the Ferdan market shortly afterward, leaving the grandfer's table behind. Zink [[Death|died]] not long afterward while [[Mission to Dalna (Macri and Zink)|investigating]] the Path on Dalna,<ref name="POD" /> and Ro began experiencing [[hallucination]]s of him.<ref name="POV" />

===Wary toward the Path===
{{Quote|They ''never'' trusted you. Don't let it get to you. They're not important.|A hallucination of Kevmo Zink, to Marda Ro, about Aurin|<ref name="POV" />}}
[[File:KevmoZink-PoD.png|thumb|right|235px|Mardo Ro experienced a hallucination of Kevmo Zink (pictured) telling her that Aurin had never trusted her.]]
Later the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Battle of Jedha]] erupted on<ref name="POV" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[moon]]<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Jedha]]. The Path's [[Mother (title)|leader]], [[Elecia Zeveron|the Mother]], blamed the group's [[Herald (title)|Herald]]&mdash;[[Werth Plouth]]&mdash;for inciting the incident to deflect accountability from the Path, although many throughout [[the galaxy]] still blamed the religious group as a whole. As a result, Aurin&mdash;and most of Dalna's other residents&mdash;began regarding the Path of the Open Hand and its members with newfound suspicion. The [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Ric Farazi]] traveled to Ferdan and questioned the Umbaran about the religious group due to their friendship with Ro, making them unsettled. When Aurin insisted they did not want to get involved with the incident, Farazi eventually departed for the nearby [[Path compound]].<ref name="POV" />

When Ro visited Aurin not long afterward, the Umbaran was uneasy with her presence and informed her that her usual table was reserved despite it being unoccupied. They briefly told Ro about Farazi's inquiry into the Path but refused to engage in casual chatter with the Path member, fiddling with a [[micro-gyro unit]] and acting busy. Ro took her leave from the Umbaran and was disheartened by her friend's unusually wary response to her visit. As the Path member contemplated the information Aurin had told her, Ro experienced a hallucination of Zink in which the Padawan told her that the Umbaran had never trusted her, but she should brush it off and move on. Ro invited Farazi to reside in the Path compound during her investigation, telling the Mother that doing so would help soothe the fears of Aurin and others who had recently become wary of the Path.<ref name="POV" />

==Personality and traits==
Aurin was friends with Marda Ro and enjoyed the company of her and her group of Littles, allowing one of the children&mdash;Eff&mdash;to sit on their lap. Although they had no wish to join the Path of the Open Hand, they respected the way the group taught about the Force.<ref name="POD" /> Following the Battle of Jedha, Aurin adopted a much warier perception of the Path and was frightened when Ric Farazi questioned them about the religious group. As a result, they limited their interactions with Ro when she came to visit. Ro was disheartened by the interaction, having believed Aurin would be friendly toward her.<ref name="POV" /> By 382 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the Umbaran had an elderly appearance.<ref name="POD" />

==Equipment==
While on Dalna, Aurin operated a stall<ref name="POV" /> selling used droid parts to make a living. Their stall had a table adjacent to it that they often lent out to Marda Ro and her group of Littles for some [[time]].<ref name="POD" /> The Umbaran owned at least one micro-gyro unit that they worked on.<ref name="POV" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Yes, when I use they/them pronouns it's to indicate a non-binary gender for the character. ''[&hellip;]'' For the purposes of wookieepedia ''[''sic'']'' I'm comfortable with nonbinary as a designation for all of them other than male/female.|Tessa Gratton, on the gender of Aurin and several other ''Path of Deceit'' characters|<ref name="Email" />}}
[[File:TheHighRepublic-PathOfDeceit.jpg|thumb|right|230px|Aurin was introduced in ''Path of Deceit''.]]
Aurin first appeared in the [[2022]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and [[Tessa Gratton]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="POD" /> Phase II.<ref name="Phase II">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2023}}</ref> Within the novel, Aurin first debuted in a chapter written by Gratton.<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref> When asked about the usage of singular "they/them" pronouns in the novel for Aurin, Gratton stated that they had intended the usage to indicate a [[Wikipedia:Non-binary gender|non-binary]] gender for the character.<ref name="Email" />

The author added that the character may not personally identify as non-binary and instead may identify with a gender unique to their [[species]] that translates to the usage of "they/them" pronouns in the [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]] [[language]]. For the purposes of the free-to-edit, unofficial online ''[[Star Wars]]'' encyclopedia known as [[Wookieepedia]], Gratton was comfortable with designating Aurin&mdash;as well as several other characters in ''Path of Deceit'' that were not within the binary genders of male and female&mdash;as non-binary.<ref name="Email" /> The author also confirmed that the term "grandfer" was intended to be used a title for Aurin on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]].<ref name="Title">{{Twitter|tessagratton|status/1655588922750115840|[[Tessa Gratton]]|date=2023-05-08|quote=A title!|archivefile=File:Grandfer-TessaGrattonTweet.png}} {{C|In response to: "Hi! I've noticed a non-binary character named Aurin is identified as "Grandfer" in Path of Deceit. Is it their name? Or is it a title like "grandparent"? It seems a British dialect word for "grandfather", and Aurin is pretty old. But Idk if it's just coincidence"}}</ref>
