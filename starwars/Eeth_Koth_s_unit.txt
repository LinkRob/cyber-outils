During the [[Clone Wars]], a [[clone trooper]] '''unit''' was active and led by [[Jedi General]] [[Eeth Koth]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Lock (Clone Captain)|Lock]]. Clone troopers of the unit were equipped with [[Phase I clone trooper armor]], which featured green and yellow markings.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of "[[Landing at Point Rain]]" to [[21 BBY]], and {{HelmetCollectionCite|76|Highlights of the Saga}} dates the [[death]] of [[Ponds]], as seen in the episode "[[Lethal Trackdown]]," to the same [[Standard year|year]]. Since {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}} places "[[Grievous Intrigue]]" between "Landing at Point Rain" and "Lethal Trackdown," its events must also take place in the same year.</ref> the unit was stationed on the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Venator-class)|''Steadfast'']] when it was attacked by [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] aboard [[Grievous's Recusant-class light destroyer|his]] [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class Commerce Guild destroyer]] in the [[Saleucami system]]. Captain Lock and his troops were ordered to evacuate the [[cruiser]] by [[Jedi Master]] Koth, who was captured by Grievous soon after.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />
