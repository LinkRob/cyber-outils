'''Corellians''' were a cultural group of [[human]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. [[Han Solo]], [[captain]] of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', was a Corellian who fought for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Society and culture==
{{Quote|If it has an engine or motor, expect a Corellian to see how fast it can go &mdash; and another Corellian to try and make it go faster.|The ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''|<ref name="Explorer's Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref>}}
Corellians were [[human]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Corellia]],<ref name="Twilight Company" /> a temperate [[Core Worlds|Core World]] of [[forest]]s, [[jungle]]s, and industrial urban environments. The world and its natives were known for their shipbuilding,<ref name="Corellia Databank">{{Databank|corellia|Corellia}}</ref> with the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]<ref name="Rebels VG">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> producing [[starship]]s ranging from [[freighter]]s such as the [[YT-1300 light freighter|YT-1300]]<ref name="TFA ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> to [[cruiser]]s like the [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class]] utilized by the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Ultimate SW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Many notable Corellians were also famed among [[the galaxy]]'s greatest [[pilot]]s; Corellia's luminaries included [[Han Solo]] and [[Wedge Antilles]].<ref name="Corellia Databank" />

Corellians became notable throughout [[the galaxy]] for their wanderlust, only for the planet to lose its importance to the wider [[galaxy]]<ref name="Corellia Databank" /> as younger planets took its power.<ref name="Explorer's Guide" /> Many Corellians had begun to show ideals based upon isolationism during the [[High Republic Era]], only for those opinions to be parodied in the song ''[[Corellia for Corellians]]''. Corellian [[Minister]] [[Chips Fendirfal]] once claimed a brawl was a "very Corellian welcome" to their homeworld.<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref>
