'''District 74''' was located on<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide to Batuu">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Glee Anselm]] and contained a [[Unidentified temple (Glee Anselm)|temple]]. When she was a [[Youngling|child]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5" /> by around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The flashbacks of [[Bounty Hunters 5|''Bounty Hunters'' 5]] which feature [[Nakano Lash]] as a [[Youngling|child]] and include her [[parent]]s' [[death]]s take place before the [[Mission to Corellia (Nakano Lash's crew)|skirmish on Corellia]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to around [[10 BBY]].</ref> the [[Nautolan]] [[Nakano Lash]] lived in district 74 with her [[Nakano Lash's family|family]] and they attended the temple there until her [[parent]]s were [[Death|killed]] by [[Anselmi]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5" />

Afterward, [[Unidentified Nautolan 1|two]] [[Unidentified Nautolan 2|Nautolans]] set out to find the girl's body. As they searched, one of the Nautolans suggested the family should have listened to [[Hologram|holo]] advisories which warned of the Anselmi. In response, the other Nautolan told them to show respect to the family, stating the family were "good temple-going folk" from district 74. Not long after, the two found Lash.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
District 74 was mentioned, in flashbacks, in the [[Bounty Hunters 5|fifth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5" /> on [[September 23]], 2020.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80124/star_wars_bounty_hunters_2020_5|text=Star Wars: Bounty Hunters (2020) #5}}</ref>
