'''Bernd Vollbrecht''' is a voice actor who voiced [[Archyr Beil]] in the audio drama [[Dark Lord (Audio drama)|''Dark Lord'']] and [[Wedge Antilles/Legends|Wedge Antilles]] in the audio dramas [[Erben des Imperiums|''Heir to the Empire'']], [[Die Dunkle Seite Der Macht|''Dark Force Rising'']] and [[Das letzte Kommando|''The Last Command'']].<ref name="Dark Lord" />
