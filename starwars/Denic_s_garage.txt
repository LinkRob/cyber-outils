'''Denic's garage''' was a garage owned by the [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[smuggler]] [[Denic]] in [[Jedha City]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]]. Various crates with [[weapon]]s passed through it,<ref name="GotW" /> and at some point in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Guardians of the Whills (novel)|''Guardians of the Whills]], and as such also its [[Guardians of the Whills: The Manga|manga adaption]], to [[1 BBY]].</ref> it housed a [[landspeeder]].<ref name="Manga">''[[Guardians of the Whills: The Manga]]''</ref> Denic later left Jedha to pilot a stolen [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]] evacuating a [[Gimm sisters' orphanage|group of orphans]] led by [[Kaya Gimm]].<ref name="GotW" />
