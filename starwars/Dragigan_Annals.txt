The '''Dragigan Annals''' were a<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> sacred<ref name="The High Republic 10">[[The High Republic (2022) 10|''The High Republic'' (2022) 10]]</ref> shrine in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] of<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[moon]] [[Jedha]].<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' establishes that the [[Children of the Open Hand]] raided the Dragigan Annals during the [[Battle of Jedha]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the Annals were raided by the [[Path of the Open Hand]]'s [[Children of the Open Hand|Children]], a group of Path members who acquired [[Force artifact]]s on behalf of [[Elecia Zeveron]], the Path's [[Mother (title)|leader]]. The purpose of the raid was to find the [[Rod of Daybreak]], a legendary relic that, when [[Rod of Power|combined]] with the [[Rod of Seasons]] which the Path had already [[Theft of the Rod of Seasons|acquired]], would allow Zeveron to fully control the [[Great Leveler|Leveler]], a creature that [[Diet|fed]] on [[Force-sensitive]]s and [[Death|petrified]] them, without question. The group were unsuccessful in finding the [[Nameless control rods|rod]] in the shrine but later found it in a [[Unidentified vault|vault]] outside the [[city]] inside a [[Jedi Order|Jedi]] [[Final Protector|statue]].<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> In [[1 BBY]], the Holy City was [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]]<ref name="Destruction" /> by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
