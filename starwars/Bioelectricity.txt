'''Bioelectricity''' was a unique feature of [[Parwan]] biology. It allowed Parwans to produce intense electrical fields and caused them to crackle with energy. Parwans, such as the [[bounty hunter]] [[Derrown]], were capable of using their biology to produce bio-electric shocks.<ref name="The Box">{{TCW|The Box}}</ref>

The [[fyrnock]], a ferocious<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> silicon-based [[species]]<ref name="US">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> that attacked its prey with sharp [[Tooth|teeth]] and claws,<ref name="VE" /> fed on bioelectricity and electrical energy.<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref>
