The '''''Chevalier''''' was a [[New Republic]] [[shuttle]] under the command of [[Captain]] [[Krull]]. After a [[Blockade of Substation Grimdock|standoff]] near [[Substation Grimdock]], the ''Chevalier'' was sent by Krull to inspect and board the [[Vermillion (ZV-9 freighter)|''Vermillion'']]. Following a confrontation aboard the ''Vermillion'', Captain Krull by orders of [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], lent the shuttle to [[Taka Jamoreesa]], [[Han Solo]], and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], so they could continue their [[Search for the Phylanx Redux Transmitter|mission]] to find the [[gangster]] [[Fyzen Gor]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
