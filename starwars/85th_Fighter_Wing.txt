The '''85th Fighter Wing''', or simply the '''85th''', was a renowned [[fighter wing]] within the [[Imperial Military|Armed Forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] that was active by [[3 ABY]]. The 85th participated in the [[Local year|annual]] [[Sarapin Flight Show]], impressing millions of spectators and leading to a wave of applications for the [[Imperial Academy]]. In [[4 ABY]], [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]'s [[Mission to the Kudo system|losses]] led [[pilot]]s [[Ganem Kahi]] and [[Jeela Brebtin]] to speculate from which unit their new recruits would be transferring from, incorrectly theorizing they were from the 85th.

==Description==
The 85th Fighter Wing was a unit of the [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]]. As such, [[starfighter]]s<ref name="Outlaws" /> and [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilots]] were part of the [[fighter wing]]'s composition.<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />

==History==
{{Quote|Where do you think they pulled the pilots from?''"<br />"''The 85th, perhaps?|Ganem Kahi and Jeela Brebtin|<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />}}
[[File:SquadronFiveSpeculates-TIEFighter4.png|thumb|right|260px|Jeela Brebtin (right) speculated that Squadron Five's new recruits were from the 85th.]]
The 85th Fighter Wing was active during the [[Imperial Era]] and participated in the annual [[Sarapin Flight Show]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The 85th's fighters performed a display of ultrasonic precision aerobatics, impressing the millions of spectators and causing a spike in applications to the [[Imperial Academy|Imperial Academies]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The 85th's participation in the event was covered later in the [[Standard week|week]] by [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Dorna Parros]] on the [[HoloNet News]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="TIE Fighter 4 date">The events of [[TIE Fighter 4|''TIE Fighter'' 4]] take place between the events of [[TIE Fighter 3|''TIE Fighter'' 3]] and the [[death]] of [[Shakara Nuress]], which are both dated to [[4 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> when [[Squadron Five (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Five]] of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] [[Mission to the Kudo system|lost]] two of their pilots, the Empire assigned [[Imperial cadet]]s [[Bansu Ro]] and [[Rac Syrmo]] to fill the empty places in the [[squadron]]. When the [[cadet]]s arrived on a [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]] used by the 204th, [[Flight officer|Flight Officer]] [[Ganem Kahi]] asked [[Senior Lieutenant]] [[Jeela Brebtin]], both of Squadron Five, where the new members might have been pulled from. Brebtin incorrectly speculated that they might be from the 85th. Squadron Five [[commanding officer]] [[Teso Broosh]] was angered to learn they were cadets instead.<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Double-checked with our resident lore-master associate narrative director @johnbjorling that this is absolutely the case!|Navid Khavari on if the 85th Fighter Wing in ''Outlaws'' was a reference to the 85th from ''TIE Fighter'' 4|<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|Navface|status/1837156969850831108|[[Navid Khavari]]|date=2024-09-20|quote=Double-checked with with our resident lore-master associate narrative director @johnbjorling that this is absolutely the case!|archivedate=20240920164538}}</ref>}}
The 85th was mentioned in the [[TIE Fighter 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2019]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries [[Star Wars: TIE Fighter (comic series)|''Star Wars: TIE Fighter'']]. The issue was written by [[Jody Houser]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> on [[July 17]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/75575/star_wars_tie_fighter_2019_4|text=Star Wars: Tie Fighter (2019) #4}}</ref> The full name of the 85th Fighter Wing was provided in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Navid Khavari]], narrative director for ''Outlaws'', confirmed with associate narrative director [[John Björling]] that the 85th in ''TIE Fighter'' 4 and the 85th Fighter Wing in ''Outlaws'' were the same.<ref name="Tweet" />
