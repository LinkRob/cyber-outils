'''Anla Shou''' was the leader of a small team of rebels on the [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]]. She had a reputation for being a bit of a hothead.<ref name="Legends" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anla Shou first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Assault]]'' companion app ''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'', in the "Aggressive Negotiations" ally encounter.<ref name="Legends" />
