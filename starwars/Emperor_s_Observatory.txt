The '''Emperor's Observatory''', also known as '''Palpatine's observatory''', on the [[planet]] [[Pillio]] was one of several [[Observatory|observatories]] maintained by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] throughout [[the galaxy]], each one being part of his goal to find out what lay beyond the known galaxy. The Pillio observatory contained a number of artifacts from the Emperor's collection.

In [[4 ABY]], after the Emperor's death, the Emperor's Observatory was [[Mission to Pillio|discovered]] by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Galactic Emperor|Imperial]] [[Agent]] [[Del Meeko]] of [[Inferno Squad]]. Meeko's orders were to destroy the observatory, but Skywalker convinced him not to and to avoid inflicting damage to the planet. Skywalker left the Observatory with a [[Jedi star compass|compass]], an artifact he sensed was important.

==Description==
{{Quote|Why would the Emperor have a vault that can only be opened using the Force?|Del Meeko|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
The Emperor's Observatory was a bunker on the [[planet]] [[Pillio]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> It was one of many [[Observatory|observatories]] that [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], also the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], established across [[the galaxy]]. Each observatory looked beyond the [[Galactic Rim|galactic rim]] with the intent of finding what laid beyond the known [[galaxy]], and each had a specific purpose.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> The Pillio observatory contained a number of items the Emperor had collected before his [[death]], such as a [[Jedi star compass|compass]]. The bunker had no locking or unlocking mechanism, and it could only be opened with [[the Force]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

The Pillio observatory also housed a [[Imperialis (Pillio)|replica]] of the Emperor's [[yacht]] ''[[Imperialis]]''.<ref name="Imperialis replica">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' establishes that each [[Observatory]] hosts a functional replica of the Emperor's [[yacht]] ''[[Imperialis]]''.</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|I won't stop you from destroying this vault. But wouldn't you say the Empire has done enough damage to innocent worlds?|Luke Skywalker, to Del Meeko|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
During the [[Clone Wars]], while he was still [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] felt the call of unknown space from beyond the galactic rim. He wanted to explore the [[Unknown Regions]] and galaxies that laid beyond [[The galaxy|his own]],<ref name="Empire's End" /> and even theorized that such unexplored space could be the source of his [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] powers.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> He built observatories, such as the one on Pillio, as part of that exploration and used this one to store several artifacts.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Palpatine&mdash;ruler of the Galactic Empire&mdash;was [[Death|killed]] during the [[Battle of Endor]]. After the battle, [[Inferno Squad]] member [[Del Meeko]] was tasked with leading a contingent of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[trooper]]s to Pillio in order to [[Mission to Pillio|destroy]] the Observatory. At the same time, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] also traveled to Pillio. He felt called to Pillio by the Force, and the arrival of Imperial troops confirmed his suspicions that there was something special on the planet.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

[[File:Luke_opens_the_Emperors_Observatory.png|thumb|left|250px|Luke Skywalker opens the door to the Emperor's Observatory using the Force.]]
After Skywalker destroyed the Imperial forces he encountered and saved Meeko, who was trapped after an encounter with [[scritter]]s who lived in Pillio, the two worked together to find the Emperor's Observatory; neither felt the other was truly their enemy. The [[Jedi]] opened the door with the Force when they arrived, and Meeko realized that the Emperor&mdash;the man who destroyed the [[Jedi Order]]&mdash;could also use the powers of the Force. Once inside, Meeko felt that none of the items were of any value or worth the Emperor protecting, but Skywalker discovered the Emperor's compass. Meeko allowed him to keep it, and Skywalker convinced Meeko not to destroy the Observatory. The Empire, Skywalker said, had already inflicted enough damage on innocent worlds.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Emperor's Observatory on Pillio was introduced in a mission during the single player campaign of ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Observatories and their connection to unknown space were previously introduced in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Empire's End" />
