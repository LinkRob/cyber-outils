'''Fiyaro''' was a [[city]] on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]]. Although it was smaller in size and influence, Fiyaro had similar architectural designs to the [[Carannia|capital]] and also adopted the same anti-[[Galactic Republic|Republic]] stance in the last [[Standard day|days]] of its rule.<ref name="BMF 70" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fiyaro was a location first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 70|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 70]] magazine published by [[De Agostini]]<ref name="BMF 70" /> and released on [[May 4]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|70}}</ref> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the location was first mentioned in the novel ''[[Darth Bane: Rule of Two]]'', although it was spelt with two r's instead of one.<ref name="DB:ROT">''[[Darth Bane: Rule of Two]]''</ref>
