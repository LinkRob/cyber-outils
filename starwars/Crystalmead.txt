'''Crystalmead''' was a type of [[mead]] served at [[Klang's Place]], a cheap [[Cantina|bar]] in [[Canto Bight]]. [[Kedpin Shoklop]] and [[Anglang Lehet]] had some while spending [[time]] at the bar. Unbeknownst to Kedpin, Angland slipped a tiny, ingestible, remote-operated [[nano-detonator]] into his drink, intending to turn the little [[Wermal]] into a living bomb.<ref name="Rules" />
