A '''candle''' was a a cylindrical<ref name="Voices">{{TCW|Voices}}</ref> piece of [[wax]]<ref name="TPW">"[[The Perfect Weapon]]"</ref> with an embedded wick that was burned to produce light.<ref name="Voices" /> A candle that had just been lit emitted timid light that grew in intensity.<ref name="Ezra's Duel with Danger">''[[Ezra's Duel with Danger]]''</ref> In his [[Yoda's quarters|private quarters]] at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] of [[Coruscant]], the [[Grand Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] [[Yoda]] kept several candles. At some point toward the later stages of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Force spirit|Force ghost]] of the late [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] caused Yoda's candles to stir in order to manifest his presence.<ref name="Voices" /> One type of candle was the votive candle, which was the most common offering brought to the [[Tomb of Miktrull]] on [[Zeffo]]. They were often lit as a sign of respect and reverence to [[Zeffo Sage|Sage]] [[Miktrull]]. Generally encased in metal and adorned with carvings, it was said that their flames represented [[Miktrull]]'s unending power in [[the Force]].<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>
