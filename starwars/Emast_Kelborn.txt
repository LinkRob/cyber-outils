'''Emast Kelborn'''<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> was a humanoid [[Mandalorian]] member of a Mandalorian [[Nevarro Mandalorian covert|covert]] on [[Nevarro]].

==History==
[[File:Manga-The Tribe.png|thumb|left|270px|Emast Kelborn, Pen Varad, Paz Vizsla, and other Mandalorians of the Tribe attack the Bounty Hunters' Guild.]]
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|1}}</ref> Kelborn was present when [[Din Djarin]] first entered the covert to meet the [[The Armorer]], during which Djarin presented [[Calamari Flan]] as payment for the forging of a pauldron from a [[beskar]] ingot.<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> Kelborn was later present at the entrance, speaking with another Mandalorian, as Djarin returned with another [[camtono]] of beskar.<ref name="The Sin">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 3: The Sin}}</ref>

Kelborn was present during the [[incident on Nevarro]] when Din Djarin was confronted by members of the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] led by [[Greef Karga]]. Kelborn was among the Mandalorians of the Tribe who descended using jetpacks to aid Djarin, engaging the bounty hunters and helping to force them back. During the confrontation, Kelborn fired upon the Guild members providing covering fire as Djarin retreated to his starship, the ''[[Razor Crest]]''. They also defended the vessel as it prepared for departure, helping ensure Djarin’s escape from Nevarro.<ref name="The Sin" />

Following the Tribe’s intervention, the covert was targeted by the [[Imperial Remnants]], resulting in heavy losses among its members. While some Mandalorians, such as [[Paz Vizsla]], were confirmed to have survived, Kelborn’s fate remained unconfirmed, as it was believed that others may have escaped the planet.<ref name="Redemption">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref>

==Equipment==
[[File:Emast Kelborn helmet.png|thumb|right|140px|Emast Kelborn's helmet]]
Emast Kelborn wore [[Mandalorian armor]] consisting of light [[gray]] and orange plating over a gray [[flight suit]]. Their helmet was light gray, with orange detailing around the visor. Their chest armor was primarily gray, featuring a faceted [[plastron]] designed to better deflect incoming impacts across the torso. They wore orange [[Mandalorian vambrace|vambraces]] on both forearms. They wore gray pauldrons on both shoulders, with their right [[pauldron]] bearing ionization burns on the enamel. Kelborn also wore a gray [[cape]] draped over their armor.<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide" /> During combat situations, they utilized a [[jetpack]] though it was not always worn or deployed.<ref name="Chapter 3">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 3: The Sin}}</ref> The Mandalorian also utilized an unidentified [[blaster rifle]]<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide" /><ref name="Chapter 1"/> and a [[DL-17 blaster rifle]].<ref name="Chapter 3" /> However, in the [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian: The Manga|manga]] they are depicted wielding two [[WESTAR-35 blaster pistol|WESTAR-35 blaster pistols]].<ref name="Manga">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian: The Manga]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Emast Kelborn shares their name with [[Clan Kelborn]], a [[Mandalorian clan]] from [[Star Wars Legends|''Legends'']] continuity; however, no connection has been made.

===Concept and design===
In [[The Art of Star Wars: The Mandalorian (Season One)|''The Art of Star Wars: The Mandalorian'' Season One]], concept artwork depicts a Mandalorian figure with an armor design and color scheme consistent with Emast Kelborn’s later established appearance, along with a similar flightsuit. The concept shares visual similarities with the character’s final depiction in its overall armor styling and coloration. However, it differs in several respects, including the presence of a [[Mandalorian hunting kama]] with large attached pouches and a belt that crosses the torso, both of which are absent from the finalized design.<ref name="ArtofMandoS1">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Mandalorian (Season One)|The Art of Star Wars: The Mandalorian Season One]]''</ref>
