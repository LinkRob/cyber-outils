The '''Chancellor's Podium''' was a podium located in the center of the [[Imperial Senate Building|Galactic Senate Building]]'s [[Galactic Senate Chamber]] that extended from the [[Chancellor's working office]]<ref name="CL" /> directly below.<ref name="Card Trader" />

==Description==
The [[Chancellor]]'s podium was 30 [[meter]]s tall, this was to display authority towards the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]]. When not in use during Senate Meetings it retracts into the [[Chancellor's working office]] which lies beneath the chamber. The podium also featured a data screen that translated dialects and showed the results of votes to the [[Supreme Chancellor]].<ref name="CL" /> The podium has the [[Great Galactic Seal]] that represents the symbol of the senate.<ref name="Episode I" />

The top of the podium was where the Supreme Chancellor stood and addressed the senate alongside his administrative [[aide]] and the [[Vice Chair]]. Slightly below them was where other key individuals sat, these officials included the [[Sergeant-at-Arms]] who was in charge of the senate workers and [[Courier droid|messenger droids]], the [[Journal Clerk]], the Official Reporter who recorded the debates of the senate, and a parliamentarian who advised the chancellor on senatorial procedures.<ref name="CL" />

In the early [[Imperial Era]], the podium had changed and replaced the senate symbol with the [[Imperial crest]].<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy">{{TBB|The Clone Conspiracy}}</ref>
