The '''''Exchequer''''' was a [[starship]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] under the command of [[Moff]] [[Boyse]]. It served as the [[flagship]] of the [[Imperial Navy]]'s [[Scipio blockade|blockade]] of the [[planet]] [[Scipio]] and, along with [[Emperor's adviser|Imperial advisors]], ensured that the [[Core Five]],<ref name="Endless" /> the ruling council of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]],<ref name="Clu Lesser">{{Databank|clu-lesser|Clu Lesser}}</ref> could only loan or allocate [[Currency|funds]] to those who were purely loyal to the Empire.<ref name="Endless" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Exchequer'' was mentioned in ''[[Endless Vigil]]'', a [[2016]] sourcebook for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[roleplaying game]].<ref name="Endless" />
