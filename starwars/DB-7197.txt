'''DB-7197''' was a [[human]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] of the [[First Order]]. He fought in [[Captain]] [[Phasma]]'s [[Attack on Demir|attack]] on the [[iron]] [[planet]] [[Demir]] but was fatally wounded. Fellow [[trooper]] [[KM-8713]] tried to save DB-7197, but when a [[thermal detonator]] threatened Phasma's life, she threw the injured trooper onto the explosive, [[Death|killing]] him while saving herself. KM-8713 and Phasma later discussed DB-7197's worth as a soldier.

==History==
{{Quote|What are you doing? Leave him.''"<br />"''I&hellip;I can bring him. He won't slow us down.''"<br />"''That was an order, KM-8713. Not a ''suggestion''.|Phasm and KM-8713|<ref name="Phasma" />}}
DB-7197 was a<ref name="Phasma" /> [[human]]<ref name="SWE" /> who served the [[First Order]] as a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]]. He went through basic training with fellow [[trooper]] [[KM-8713]].<ref name="Phasma" /> Sometime between [[25 ABY]] and [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Death" /> DB-7197 participated in the [[First Order Army]]'s [[Attack on Demir|assault]] on the [[iron]] [[planet]] [[Demir]], serving under [[Captain]] [[Phasma]]. After DB-7197 received a wound to his chest during the conflict, KM-8713 discovered the fallen trooper. KM-8713 assisted DB-7197 to his [[Limb|feet]] and offered words of encouragement.<ref name="Phasma" />

[[File:DB7197Wounded-AORPhasma1.jpg|250px|left|thumb|DB-7197 was wounded on the planet Demir.]]
Upon witnessing the two, Phasma instructed KM-8713 to leave DB-7197 behind, then clarified it as an order after KM-8713 protested. A [[Demirian]] ambushed the group but was [[Death|killed]] by KM-8713's [[F-11D blaster rifle|blaster]] fire. The fallen Demirian dropped an active [[thermal detonator]], which prompted Phasma to throw DB-7197's body atop the [[grenade]] at the protest of her subordinates, sacrificing DB-7197's life to save Phasma and KM-8713 from the blast. Phasma gave the call to retreat and ordered KM-8713 to report to her [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|command shuttle]] to discuss the events of the [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="Phasma" />

When KM-8713 reported to Phasma within her [[shuttle]], Phasma chastised KM-8713 for disobeying her order to abandon DB-7197 and inquired why they had assisted the fallen [[soldier]]. They described DB-7197 as a good soldier and thought it a waste to leave one behind. Phasma stated he was a dying soldier, but KM-8713 insisted he could have recovered. Phasma relented but argued that helping him had nearly cost their lives, and that it was impossible to help everyone on the battlefield, and that sacrifices must be made.<ref name="Phasma" />
