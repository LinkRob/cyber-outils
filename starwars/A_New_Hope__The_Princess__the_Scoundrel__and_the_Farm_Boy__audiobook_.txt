'''''A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy''''' is the unabridged audio version of the [[canon]] young-readers [[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy|novel of the same name]], written by [[Alexandra Bracken]]. The audiobook was performed by [[Marc Thompson]] and [[Rebecca Soler]], and was released by [[Listening Library]] on [[September 22]], [[2015]].

==Publisher's summary==
"''The galaxy is at war''.

''Although the Rebel Alliance has won a few battles against the Empire, hope is fading. The Empire is about to finish building the greatest weapon the galaxy has ever seen&mdash;the Death Star. The rebels' only chance to defeat it now lies in the unlikely hands of a princess, a scoundrel, and a farm boy. . . . ''

''Acclaimed, ''New York Times'' bestselling author Alexandra Bracken delivers a captivating retelling of ''Star Wars: A New Hope'' like you've never experienced before. Since the premier of the original film, Princess Leia, Han Solo, and Luke Skywalker have become iconic, larger-than-life characters. ''The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy'' takes a deeper look at these three heroes as they join forces to defeat the evil that threatens their entire galaxy.''"<ref name="Penguin-Download" />
