'''Dwoogan''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[chef]] aboard the ''[[Wayward Current]]''. Before she was a chef, she was a pilot for the [[Galactic Republic]].

==Biography==
Dwoogan was a [[pilot]] in the [[Galactic Republic]]. She once went to the [[Dande Donjon]], a [[cantina]] on the Shore of [[Vette Lake]] at [[Xu'hu]], where she met [[Redy]], who told her a conspiracy theory against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Legends of Luke Skywalker" />

In her later life she became a [[chef]] aboard the ''[[Wayward Current]]'' she once told a story to the crew members of the ''[[Wayward Current]]'' about a conspiracy theory that [[Redy]] told her in the [[Dande Donjon]].<ref name="Legends of Luke Skywalker" />

==Personality and traits==
Dwoogan was a tall, muscular woman with scars on her face. She was known on the ''[[Wayward Current]]'' as a someone who could turn the most revolting ingredients into something delicious.<ref name="Legends of Luke Skywalker" />
