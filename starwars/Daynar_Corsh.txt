'''Daynar Corsh''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] active in the [[Anoat sector]] of [[the galaxy]] during shortly after the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. A criminal, he was known to interrogate individuals for gang leaders and [[crime lord]]s, getting information from difficult beings. Preferring the use of a [[blaster pistol]] and to work in stealth, he was also skilled at recovering material.<ref name="SWU" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
[[The Smuggler|A smuggler]] who opposed the Empire recruited Corsh, sending him on various missions in the [[sector]].<ref name="SWU" />{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Daynar Corsh was a crew member obtainable by players of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', by entertainment company [[Kabam]]. He is initially a 2-star Tier-I crew member, upgrading to a maximum 4-star level. His background is criminal, preference is stealth and has the scavenger ability, making him strong in material finder runs. He wears [[Hutt]] [[Security guard|guard]] gear and carries a blaster pistol.<ref name="SWU" />
