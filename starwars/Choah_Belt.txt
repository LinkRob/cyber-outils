The '''Choah Belt''' was an [[asteroid field]] located<ref name="BMF 34" /> in the [[Mortex sector]]'s [[Choah system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] region. It was situated in grid square S-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance Military|forces]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] established a [[Choah Belt fuel refinery|fuel refinery]] among the [[asteroid]]s.<ref name="BMF 34" /> Around [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BuildFalconCite|34|Secrets of Spaceflight}} places the [[Battle of the Choah Belt|battle]] in the Choah Belt shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' establishes as the starting point of [[0 ABY]].</ref> shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Galactic Empire]] discovered the [[refinery]] and destroyed it in a [[Battle of the Choah Belt|battle]], causing critical [[fuel]] shortages among rebel forces throughout the [[sector]]. Sometime after the battle, the Rebellion [[Attack on Black Ice|stole]] the Empire's [[Field Secured Container Vessel]] ''[[Black Ice]]'' refueling [[Starship|ship]] in order to compensate the refinery's loss.<ref name="BMF 34" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Choah Belt was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 34|thirty-fourth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine series,<ref name="BMF 34" /> published around [[August 26]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|34}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Choah Belt/Legends|Choah Belt]] was originally introduced in [[Black Ice (adventure)|''Black Ice'']], a [[1990]] [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' adventure authored by [[Paul Murphy]] and [[Bill Slavicsek]].<ref name="Black Ice">[[Black Ice (adventure)|''Black Ice'']]</ref>
