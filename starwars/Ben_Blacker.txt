'''Ben Blacker''' is an author. Along with [[Ben Acker]], Blacker wrote the books in the ''[[Star Wars: Join the Resistance|Join the Resistance]]'' series of young readers focusing on a group of young [[Resistance]] members in the [[Standard year|years]] after {{Film|VI}}.<ref name="Amazon">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/join-the-resistance|text=Join the Resistance|nolive=1}}</ref> Blacker also created with Acker the program ''Thrilling Adventure Hour''.<ref name="throwdown">{{WebCite|url=https://aux.avclub.com/thrilling-adventure-hour-s-ben-acker-and-ben-blacker-th-1798285223|text=''Thrilling Adventure Hour''{{'s}} Ben Acker and Ben Blacker throw down ''The Martian''|author=Derrick Sanskrit|date=2015-10-10|archivedate=20180621202447}}</ref>

Blacker along with Acker contributed to the compilation volume ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''.<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-from-a-certain-point-of-view-anthology-book-celebrates-40-years-of-a-galaxy-far-far-away|text=''Star Wars: From A Certain Point of View'' Anthology Book Celebrates 40 Years of a Galaxy Far, Far Away}}</ref>
