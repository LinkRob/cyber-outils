During the [[High Republic Era]], a '''battle''' was fought<ref name="HRA 4" /> in the [[Bright Jewel system]]<ref name="Location" /> near [[Ord Mantell]] between a [[Galactic Republic]]-[[Jedi Order]] [[starfighter]] force and a group of [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s. The Nihil force had been sent out by [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] as a diversion, as part of his plans to lure the [[Jedi]] into a trap in the system. As a result, the force was easily defeated, which raised Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]]'s suspicions.<ref name="HRA 4" />
