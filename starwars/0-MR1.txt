'''0-MR1''', more often referred to as '''Omri''', was an [[RA-7 protocol droid]] who served the [[First Order]] during the [[Cold War]].

==Biography==
[[File:C3POsRedArm-SW100Objects.png|thumb|150px|left|C-3PO took and used 0-MR1's arm to honor his friend.]]
A survivor of the ''[[Sterdic Star]]'' [[Mission to Taul|crash]] on the [[planet]] [[Taul]] between [[31 ABY|31]] and [[32 ABY]],<ref name="Year" /> 0-MR1 was taken [[prisoner]] by [[C-3PO]] and the other [[Resistance]] [[droid]]s. Their group was picked off by the planet's dangers in their fight for survival, however, Threepio and Omri befriended one another despite working for opposing sides in the [[Cold War]].<ref name="The Phantom Limb" />

Omri eventually sacrificed himself, [[Death|destroyed]] by Taul's [[Acid rain|acidic rain]] while helping C-3PO get a [[Distress signal|signal]] to the Resistance. As he faded, he noticed red plating under his silver coat. C-3PO, who had lost a [[limb]] during his ordeal on the planet, took Omri's left arm as his own for a [[time]] in memory of those who had fallen.<ref name="The Phantom Limb" />
