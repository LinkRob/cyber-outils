'''Februs Temmt''' was an [[Imperial officer]] who served as [[Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]]'s archivist during an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Akiva Summit|summit]] on [[Akiva]] following the [[Battle of Endor]]. [[Gender|He]] was a small, brittle man. Sloane instructed him to refer to the meeting as the [[Imperial Future Council]]. The summit was interrupted by the [[Rebellion on Akiva]], which ended Imperial rule on the [[planet]].<ref name="Aftermath" />
