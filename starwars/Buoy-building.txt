'''Buoy-buildings''' were a type of floating structure found on<ref name="DoR">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="GM">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Pipada system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Pipada]] by the time of the [[Imperial Era]]. The structures floated above the [[water]] in the planet's lakes. Many of these bobbing buoys featured a variety of shops that were strung together and anchored to downtown buildings.<ref name="DoR" />

==Behind the scenes==
Buoy-buildings were mentioned in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[2018]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] sourcebook published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' game.<ref name="DoR" />
