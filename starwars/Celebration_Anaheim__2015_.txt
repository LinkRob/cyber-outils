'''Celebration Anaheim''' was an official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Celebration]] fan convention held [[April 16]]&ndash;[[April 19|19]], [[2015]], at the [[Wikipedia:Anaheim Convention Center|Anaheim Convention Center]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]], home of [[Disneyland]]. [[Lucasfilm]] formally announced plans for Celebration Anaheim on [[July 28]], [[2013]], at the closing ceremonies of [[Celebration Europe II]] in [[Wikipedia:Essen|Essen]], [[Wikipedia:Germany|Germany]].

[[File:CelebrationAnaheim.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Original Celebration Anaheim starfield logo]]
While previous Celebrations had been distinguished from each other by Roman numerals similar to the live-action films, Lucasfilm decided not to continue this practice. Instead, this and future Celebrations will be designated by the year and location.<ref name="TFNnaming">{{WebCite|url=http://www.theforce.net/story/front/Lucasfilm_Dropping_Numbers_From_Future_Celebrations_153519.asp|text=Lucasfilm Dropping Numbers From Future Celebrations|author=Eric|date=2013-08-01|work=[[TheForce.net]]|archivedate=20190730155652}}</ref>

Celebration Anaheim opened with a panel on [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] and also included a panel on ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="panels">{{WebCite|url=http://www.ocregister.com/articles/wars-657489-star-convention.html|text=Star Wars Celebration: A galaxy far, far away is coming to Anaheim|author=Pimentel, Joseph|date=2015-04-10|work=[http://www.ocregister.com Orange County Register]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/EvmbY}}</ref> There was also a special screening of the story reel for four previously unreleased episodes of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', beginning with "[[The Bad Batch (episode)|The Bad Batch]]."<ref name="Anaheim Announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-bad-batch-4-episode-arc-coming-to-star-wars-celebration|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' "Bad Batch" 4-Episode Arc Coming to ''Star Wars'' Celebration|nolive=1}}</ref>
