'''Electrocrops''' were a type of two-pronged riding crop<ref name="TLJVD" /> that emitted an electrical shock when used. The [[jockey]] [[Arca Yroca]] used a red electrocrop while riding on the [[fathier]] [[Hoth Stuff]] on the [[Canto Bight]] [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|racetrack]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]<ref name="TLJ" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VIII}} to [[34 ABY]].</ref> He applied the shock to the fathier's flank, causing the creature to speed up while letting out a cry of pain. That act of cruelty was [[Mission to Canto Bight|witnessed]] by [[Resistance]] agents [[Rose Tico]] and [[Finn]].<ref name="TLJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
An electrocrop first appeared in the [[2017]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']]<ref name="TLJ" /> and was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> The [[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition|novelization]] of the film written by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="Expanded">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> and the [[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel|junior novel]] written by [[Michael Kogge]]<ref name="Junior">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> both incorrectly described Yroca as using an [[electro-whip]] to strike his fathier rather than an electrocrop.<ref name="Expanded" /><ref name="Junior" />
