'''Bogwings''' were a [[reptavian]] [[species]] native to the [[planet]] [[Naboo]].<ref name="VE" /> During [[Gulliball]] games played by [[Gungan]]s, bogwings were released into the sky to signal halftime. Bogwings were known to be able to carry nine times their own weight.<ref name="AA">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> They were also very territorial.<ref name="SWAEYNTK" /> They could also be found on the [[swamp]]y world of [[Dagobah]],<ref name="Episode V" /> where they made their homes in [[gnarltree]]s.<ref name="VE" /> Other locations where they could be sighted were [[Aleen]],<ref name="MM" /> [[Zygerria]],<ref name="SR" /> [[Onderon]],<ref name="TSW">{{TCW|The Soft War}}</ref> and [[Nal Hutta]].<ref name="FE">{{TCW|Friends and Enemies}}</ref>

There were two kinds of bogwings: the [[Greater Bogwing]] and the [[Lesser Bogwing]].<ref name="NoP" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bogwings were originally supposed to appear in the episode "[[Hunt for Ziro]]" from the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' on the planet Nal Hutta. Concept art of them for the episode was done by [[Jackson Sze]]. At one point they were supposed to be called "Swamp [[Peko-peko|Peko Peko]]".<ref name="Tumblr">{{Tumblr|subdomain=starwars|name=''Star Wars''|url=post/132951414508/artist-of-the-week-jackson-sze|text=Artist of the Week - Jackson Sze|archivedate=20160411203010}}</ref>
