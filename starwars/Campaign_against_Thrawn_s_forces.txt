During the [[New Republic Era]], the [[New Republic]] waged an undeclared war against [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]’s [[Thrawn's forces|forces]].

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|At the time of Thrawn's return, Chalm Plesk&mdash;a historian from the University of Coruscant&mdash;was embedded with the New Republic fleet. His planned work, ''The Last War Against the Galactic Empire: On Deployment with the New Republic Fleet'' was highly anticipated for years, but various military figures refused to permit its publication.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> }}
[[File:Legitimate_Power.jpg|thumb|left|200px|While the Imperial remnant was defeated, the First Order continued its rise.]]
Following the campaign, the New Republic defeated the Imperial remnant. However, it failed to recognize the continued threat of the Imperial remnant until it was too late, repeating its mistake by not acting or joining the [[Resistance]] to destroy the [[First Order]] in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref><ref name="Rise and Fall" /> 

The New Republic kept the campaign and its details about its defense effort classified well until the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]. [[Chalm Plesk]], a [[University of Coruscant]] [[historian]], tried to argue his case before the [[New Republic Senate]], having been blocked from publishing his work, ''[[The Last War Against the Galactic Empire: On Deployment with the New Republic Fleet]]'', by various military figures for [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> 

The campaign was mentioned in historian [[Beaumont Kin]]'s book ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|History is on the move, Captain. Those who cannot keep up will be left behind, to watch from a distance. And those who stand in our way will not watch at all.|Grand Admiral Thrawn, to [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Gilad Pellaeon/Legends|Gilad Pellaeon]] in ''Star Wars'' Legends|<ref name="The Last Command">''[[The Last Command]]''</ref>}}
[[File:ThrawnsCampaigns-TEA.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The Thrawn campaign has its roots within the ''Star Wars'' Legends version of events (a breakdown the Legends Thrawn campaign, pictured).]]
The campaign against Thrawn's forces was first mentioned, albeit indirectly, in "[[Part One: Master and Apprentice]]", the first episode of the ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'' television series<ref name="Part One">{{Ahsoka|Part One: Master and Apprentice}}</ref> that was released on [[August 22]], [[2023]].<ref name="New Release Date">{{SW|url=news/ahsoka-new-premiere-date|text=''Ahsoka'' Premiere Moves Up to Tuesday, August 22|date=2023-08-18|quote=The two-episode premiere of ''Ahsoka'' will now arrive on Tuesday, August 22 at 6 p.m. PT on Disney+}}</ref> More information appeared in the in-universe history book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Doctor [[Chris Kempshall]]<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> and released on [[July 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="DKUK">{{DK|url=uk/book/9780241655047-star-wars-the-rise-and-fall-of-the-galactic-empire|text=Star Wars The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire}}</ref> It is set to be featured in [[Ahsoka Season 2|''Ahsoka Season'' 2]].<ref name="Insider 237">{{InsiderCite|237|Launchpad}}</ref>

The concept of Thrawn leading an Imperial Remnant force against the New Republic after the Battle of Endor originally appeared in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] through the [[Thrawn campaign]]. Originally created by author [[Timothy Zahn]] as the main conflict in his [[1991]] novel ''[[Heir to the Empire]]'', the campaign was first depicted throughout the ''[[Star Wars: The Thrawn Trilogy|The Thrawn Trilogy]]'', which ''Heir to the Empire'' began.<ref name="Heir to the Empire">''[[Heir to the Empire]]''</ref> The conflict was further expanded upon in his [[1992]] novel ''[[Dark Force Rising]]'',<ref name="Dark Force Rising">''[[Dark Force Rising]]''</ref> his [[1993]] novel ''[[The Last Command]]'', and subsequent Legends stories<ref name="IsardRev">''[[X-Wing: Isard's Revenge]]''</ref> and sources.<ref name="Warfare">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref> Very much like canon's campaign,<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> the conflict was depicted as [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn's]] effort to lead a [[Thrawn's confederation|reinvigorated]] [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] against the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]]<ref name="The Last Command" /> in [[9 ABY/Legends|9 ABY]], albeit following his return from the [[Unknown Regions/Legends|Unknown Regions]]<ref name="Warfare" /> instead of a far galaxy.<ref name="Part Eight" /> Thrawn nearly crushed the New Republic until his defeat&mdash;brought about by his [[Assassin/Legends|assassination]] at the [[Limb/Legends|hands]] of his own [[Bodyguard/Legends|bodyguard]], [[Rukh/Legends|Rukh]]&mdash;at the [[Battle of Bilbringi (9 ABY)|Battle of Bilbringi]].<ref name="The Last Command" /> The conflict was first identified as the Thrawn campaign in the [[1999]] novel ''[[X-Wing: Isard's Revenge]]'' by [[Michael A. Stackpole]].<ref name="IsardRev" /> The Legends version of events received a reference within ''Ahsoka'' "[[Part Four: Fallen Jedi]]," in which Tano refers to Thrawn as the "heir to the Empire,"<ref name="Part Four">{{Ahsoka|Part Four: Fallen Jedi}}</ref> the title of Zahn's first Legends novel.<ref name="Heir to the Empire" />
