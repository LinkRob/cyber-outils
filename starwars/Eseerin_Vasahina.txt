"'''Eseerin Vasahina'''" was a [[Music|song]] by the [[band]] [[The Agasar]].<ref name="Survivor" /> By [[9 BBY]],<ref name="SurvivorDate">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> music producer [[Ashe Javi]] and their partner, DJ [[droid]] [[DD-EC]], had acquired a copy of the song.<ref name="Survivor" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Survivor}}
In 9 BBY,<ref name="SurvivorDate" /> after beginning to perform in [[Pyloon's Saloon]] on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]], Javi and DD-EC included "Eseerin Vasahina" in their setlist.<ref name="Survivor" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
"Eseerin Vasahina" was created by [[Wikipedia:Mongols|Mongolian]] band [[The Hu]] for the [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''. If players have recruited Ashe Javi and DD-EC, they can play the song in Pyloon's Saloon.<ref name="Survivor" /> The song was also performed in [https://youtube.com/watch?v=VFIt0Q6VkvA Glastonbury Festival 2023].
