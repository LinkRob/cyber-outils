A '''kingdom''' of the [[Benathy]], a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that resided on the [[planet]] [[Benath]], located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Wild Space]].

==History==
{{Quote|I remember when another like you came here with demands. He was taller. Far more impressive. He killed our last king. But even he could not stand against our god.|Kristoff, to Kylo Ren|<ref name="Age of Resistance" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]], the [[Galactic Empire]] under [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] traveled to [[Benath]] and were able to [[Battle against the Benathy|subdue]] the Benathy by killing [[Unidentified Benathy king|their king]], but could not defeat a [[Zillo Beast]], which they worshiped as their [[Deity|god]]. Thousands on both sides died during the battle.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />

The [[First Order]]'s [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] under Vader's grandson, [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] demanded that the current king, [[Kristoff]] end their [[Benathy expansion|expansion]]. The First Order [[warlord]] also demanded them to submit to the First Order. When Kristoff refused, Ren killed him. The Benathy [[Battle against the Benathy (First Order)|fought]] Ren's forces until he defeated and killed the Zillo Beast. The Benathy bowed to Ren and chose to worship the First Order.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />

==Astrography==
The [[Benathy]] had a large swathe of territory in the [[Wild Space]]. The Betathy under the reign of [[King]] [[Kristoff]] eventually under went an [[Benathy expansion|expansion]] and conquered four [[planet]]s and wiped out all life on them.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />
