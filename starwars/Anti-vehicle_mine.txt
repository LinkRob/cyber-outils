'''Anti-vehicle mines''' were large, heavy [[Bomb|explosive devices]] that were capable of destroying [[walker]]s, [[speeder]]s, and other terrestrial vehicles. When a vehicle passed over a [[mine]], the mine's [[sensor]]s would detect its weight, specific alloys, or [[Repulsor field|repulsorfields]], triggering the device. It would not detonate if [[trooper]]s were to step on one, as their most sensitive settings required far more weight than even that of the most encumbered trooper and a larger repulsorfield than [[Repulsor boots/Canon|repulsor boots]] or [[Repulsor pack|packs]] were capable of generating.<ref name="Gadgets" />

Troopers often camouflaged anti-vehicle mines in large numbers along key roads to block otherwise indefensible approaches to bases and strategic positions. Such defensive minefields were often marked to prevent casualties to allied units.<ref name="Gadgets" />
