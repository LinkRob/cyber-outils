A '''flight''', or '''flight element''', was a type of [[starfighter]] military unit that was smaller than a [[squadron]]. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]] was composed of at least four flights and also maintained [[Red Flight (Rebel Alliance)|Red Flight]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] also utilized flights, with one being [[Delta Flight (Galactic Empire)|Delta Flight]]. The [[First Order]] also utilized flights of [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s.

==Description==
===Overview===
A flight,<ref name="Duty Roster">{{StoryCite|story=Duty Roster|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> or flight element,<ref name="Lead by Example">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> was a small [[starfighter]] military unit, usually making up parts of a starfighter [[squadron]]<ref name="Duty Roster" /> or operating independently.<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref>

The [[Galactic Republic]] deployed flights during the [[Clone Wars]] and organized them within the [[Republic Starfighter Corps]]. A [[flight group]] consisted of several flights, which could number only two craft.<ref name="HC 45">{{HelmetCollectionCite|45|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> Flights were utilized by the [[Galactic Empire]], with four flights also making up a squadron. The [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], along with its six subordinate squadrons, were composed of flights.<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> Flights were also stationed on Imperial [[planet]]s and operated patrols.<ref name="Mercy Mission">{{StoryCite|story=Mercy Mission (short story)|book=The Rise of the Empire}}</ref> Flights<ref name="HC 6">{{HelmetCollectionCite|6|Helmets}}</ref> in the Empire consisted of four starfighters, and could serve as sentries.<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] utilized flights within its [[Starfighter Corps]]. Alliance squadrons were composed of four numbered flights,<ref name="Duty Roster" /> although flights also served independently.<ref name="Battlefront" /> Flights were a part of starfighter unit structure within the [[New Republic]], making up the forces of a squadron within a fighter wing. In the New Republic, five [[bomber]]s formed a flight.<ref name="Victory's Price" /> The [[First Order]] utilized flights within their military organizational structure, and could be composed by five [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s.<ref name="Poe Dameron 31">[[Poe Dameron 31|''Poe Dameron'' 31]]</ref>

==History==
===During the Clone Wars===
Flights were used by the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars. [[Clone Flight Squad Seven]] consisted of several squadrons, of which a bomber flight of two [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]]s were part of. The flight participated in the [[Battle of Umbara]]<ref name="HC 45" /> against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the [[Umbaran Militia]], and bombed a ridge near the [[501st Legion]].<ref name="Darkness on Umbara">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref>

===During the Imperial Era===
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Imperial capital ship ''[[Avenger]]'' participated in an [[attack on a deep space installation]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. A flight of three [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s participated in the battle alongside other rebel fighters and fought against Imperial starfighter forces.<ref name="Amara Kel">{{StoryCite|story=Amara Kel's Rules for TIE Pilot Survival (Probably)|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back|sformatted=Amara Kel's Rules for TIE Pilot Survival (Probably)}}</ref> All four flights of the Alliance [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]]&mdash; [[first flight]], [[second flight]], [[third flight]], and [[fourth flight]]&mdash; participated in the [[Battle of Yavin]] against the Empire's Death Star. Despite the Alliance victory, all members of Red Squadron were killed except [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Duty Roster" /> Following the [[Battle of Hoth]], Red Squadron and its flights participated in an [[attack on an asteroid mining station]] that was operated by [[pirate]]s.<ref name="RPoint">{{StoryCite|story=Rendezvous Point (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>

The Imperial [[Theta Squadron (Galactic Empire)|Theta Squadron]], which unlike other squadrons consisted of twenty-four fighters, consisted of several flights, with [[Amara Kel's flight|one flight]] consisting of six fighters. The flight and the rest of Theta Squadron were launched by the ISD ''[[Avenger]]'' as it hunted the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' in the aftermath of the Battle of [[Hoth]].<ref name="Amara Kel" />

===During the New Republic Era===
During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the First Order participated in the [[Battle of Ikkrukk]], the First Order's invasion of [[Ikkrukk]]. Upon encountering [[Resistance]] starfighter forces, the [[heavy cruiser]] ''[[Fortitude]]'' launched its complement of TIE fighters, including an entire TIE flight. Ultimately, First Order forces were defeated.<ref name="Poe Dameron 31" />
