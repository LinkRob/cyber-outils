{{Quote|Your connection to the Force allows you to see in ways others cannot. If you can see ''yourself'', you will never be ''truly'' blind, Kanan Jarrus, Jedi Knight.|Bendu, to Kanan Jarrus|<ref name="Steps">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>}}
'''Farsight''' (also called '''clairvoyance''', '''far sight''', or '''farseeing''') was a [[The Force|Force]] [[Force power|ability]] that allowed the user to "see" their targets or any event happening without actually needing their eyes, enhancing the bearer's visual and spatial perception even in the dark or behind walls. Users could then acquire information using sight outside the normal range of detection about an object, person, location, or physical event.

==Description==
{{Quote|Concern yourself not with what you see. Feel the Force around you. See inside the land, the trees, the branches. Feel the air. Breathe. Breathe. Good.|Yoda coaches Luke Skywalker|<ref name="SW:FOD:TPA">{{FOD|The Path Ahead}}</ref>}}
Farsight, also known as clairvoyance, far sight, or farseeing, a [[Force power|power]] of [[the Force]] that allowed the [[Force-sensitive|user]] to "see" their targets or any event happening without actually needing their [[eye]]s, enhancing the bearer's visual and spatial perception even in the dark or behind walls. Users could then acquire information using sight outside the normal range of detection about an object, person, location, or physical event.<ref name="TA">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>

==Users==
{{Quote|This time, let go your conscious self and act on instinct.''"<br />"''With the blast shield down, I can't even see. How am I supposed to fight?''"<br />"''Your eyes can deceive you. Don't trust them. ''[&hellip;]'' Stretch out with your feelings.|Obi-Wan Kenobi instructs Luke Skywalker|<ref name="SW:ANH" />}}
In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Acolyte date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> the Jedi Order taught its students to use this power as part of their training.<ref name="Lost/Found">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> The Sith Lord who used the fake identity "[[The Stranger|Qimir]]" wore a [[The Stranger's helmet|helmet]] in this time, which blocked most of his senses and made him resistant to mind-reading, such that he had to use the Force while wearing it.<ref name="Teach/Corrupt">{{Acolyte|Teach / Corrupt}}</ref>

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="SW:GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] could [[Force sense|sense]] what was happening in the room nearby where [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] rested. Anakin arrived just in time to cut two venomous [[kouhun]]s in half, just a few centimeters from the senator's face. Both Jedi could actually sense and identify the danger without being physically in the room, and Anakin managed to eliminate it in the dark right away.<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref>

In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="SW:GA" /> the former [[Dark Acolyte]] [[Asajj Ventress]] led a [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Mission to Serenno (Nightsisters)|assassination attempt]] on the [[Sith Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] in his [[Castle Serenno|castle]] on [[Serenno]]. Before confronting Tyranus, Ventress shot a [[poison dart]] at the [[Sith]] Lord that impaired his sense of sight. Believing his assailants to be Jedi, Dooku used Farsight to discern the location of his attackers and engage them in [[lightsaber combat]]. Ultimately, Dooku survived and the Nightsisters escaped.<ref name="TCW:Nightsisters" />

Some of the Nightsisters, such as [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] and Old [[Daka]] were known for their clairvoyant abilities.<ref name="TCW:CE" />

[[File:DookuNightsisterClash-Nightsisters.png|thumb|left|250px|Dooku uses Farsight to defend himself]]
While assisting in the training of the Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]], [[Ahsoka Tano]] took the [[kyber crystal]] from his [[lightsaber]] and threw it into a field of grass. Hoping to teach the young Padawan a lesson, Ahsoka drew [[Ahsoka Tano's white lightsabers|her lightsaber]] and began swiping at Ezra. Bridger became frustrated, as he was unsure how he was supposed to find his crystal while simultaneously avoiding Tano's attacks. Closing his eyes, Ezra used the Force to view his surroundings, eventually discovering the location of his crystal despite Tano's assault. Retrieving his crystal, Ezra ignited his [[Ezra Bridger's first lightsaber|own lightsaber]] and clashed with Ahsoka, having learnt to trust in the power of the Force.<ref name="SW:FOD:ADL">{{FOD|A Disarming Lesson}}</ref>

[[Kanan Jarrus]] learned to use it full time from the [[Bendu]], augmenting his spatial awareness despite his blindness.<ref name="Steps" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="IV Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> [[Luke Skywalker]] used it to deflect blasts from a remote while training in the ways of the Force with [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] while en route to [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Skywalker once again used Farsight when training with [[Yoda]], who instructed him to sprint through the treetops of [[Dagobah]] with his eyes closed. Having failed previously, Skywalker ran the course and marveled at his success by relying on the Force rather than his own eyesight, describing his ability to perceive his surroundings.<ref name="SW:FOD:TPA" /> Yoda himself could utilize Farsight, as could [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Allies">''[[Allies and Adversaries]]''</ref>

[[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] could detect and locate nearby enemies through the Force, without seeing them with her eyes.<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>
