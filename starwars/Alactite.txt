'''Alactite''' was a smooth, [[Color|blue]] substance that was used as material for the floors of the [[Casino level]] of [[Cloud City]],<ref name="Empire's End" /> a [[mining]] colony on the [[gas giant]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Bespin]].<ref name="Empire's End" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> Cloud City [[Baron Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] visited the Casino level and noticed its alactite floors as he walked.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alactite appeared in ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', a [[2017]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the third installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Empire's End" />
