The '''ECG-series power droid''' was a model of [[power droid]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> with a wide cylindrical body atop two short [[Limb|legs]]. The [[pirate]] [[Phee Genoa]] owned a modified ECG-series power droid named [[MEL]]-221 who was destroyed and rebuilt<ref name="Entombed" /> sometime between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|205}}</ref>

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The ECG-series power droid first appeared in "[[Entombed]]," the fifth episode of the [[The Bad Batch Season 2|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' animated series,<ref name="Entombed" /> which aired on [[January 25]], [[2023]].<ref name="Airdate">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-bad-batch-season-2-trailer-key-art|text=Clone Force 99 Is Back in New ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 2 Trailer}}</ref>
