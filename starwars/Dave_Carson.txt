'''Dave Carson''' has had multiple roles in ''[[Star Wars]]'' productions. He was a key sculptor for the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']],<ref name="ROTJ" /> and an [[Industrial Light & Magic]] visual effects supervisor for the [[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition|Special Edition]] of that film.<ref name="ROTJ: SE" />
