'''Darth Betrius''' was a [[Sith Lord]] and [[Sith alchemist]] who lived in an ancient [[time]] before the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Terror" /> under [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> Scrolls that belonged to [[Darth]] Betrius, [[Dathka Graush]], and Naga Sadow were collected at [[Fortress Vader]] on the [[Volcano|volcanic]] [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Some time between [[1 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Terror Date">{{TerrorOnMustafarDate}}</ref> Vader's aide [[Vaneé]] used their works to further develop a procedure by Imperial [[scientist]] [[Doctor|Dr.]] [[Harper]], which involved implanting parts of a [[droid brain]] into an organic being to force them into becoming an obedient [[spy]] for the Empire.<ref name="Terror" />  

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Betrius was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2026]] audiobook ''[[Terror on Mustafar]]'' which was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and narrated by [[Marc Thompson]].<ref name="Terror" />
