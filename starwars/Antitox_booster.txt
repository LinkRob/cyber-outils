'''Antitox boosters''' were a type of medicine found in [[medpac]]s that could stop the effects of poison. The boosters were injected into patents' necks and worked within a few moments. On [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine Station]], scrapes from poisonous [[Drengir]] vines caused [[Affie Hollow]] to require an antitox booster.<ref name="ITD">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref>
