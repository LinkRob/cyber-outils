The '''''Dalkor Dagger'''''<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|dalkor-dagger|Dalkor Dagger}}</ref> was a [[starship]] owned by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Ax Tagrin]].<ref name="The Missing Agent" />

==History==
During the [[First Order]]-[[Resistance]] War the Dalkor Dagger was used by Ax Tagrin to hunt resistance [[Spy|spies]]. On [[Varkana]] it was parked in the [[Vargo Spaceport]], whilst Ax was hunting [[Norath Kev]]. Kev was captured and brought to the Dagger. After his capture, Ax used his ship as a trap to capture [[Kazuda Xiono]] and his friends. The trap worked and so [[Jarek Yeager]], Kazuda Xiono and [[CB-23]] were trapped along with Norath. CB-23 eventually overloaded the internal systems, causing the ''Dalkor Dagger'' to crash.<ref name="The Missing Agent" />

==Behind the scenes==
According to the [[The Missing Agent|Missing Agent]] [[Bucket's List]] episode, the Dalkor Dagger is shaped like the head of Ax Tagrin.{{Ship-stub}}
