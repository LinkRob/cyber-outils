'''Doni Laro''' was a [[Human]] [[starship]] [[Technician|mechanic]] who ran [[Doni's High Quality Ship Parts and Installments]], a shop built next to the landing pads in the [[City|town]] of [[Jaunta's Hope]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. Laro upgraded ships using provided materials and was capable of upgrading a [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the mechanic wore a [[Color|white]] [[Clothing|shirt]], a brown [[belt]], brown fingerless [[glove]]s, a green apron, gray pants, brown [[boot]]s, a brown [[scarf]], and a brown [[hat]] while at the store. Laro had brown [[eye]]s, light skin, and black [[hair]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Doni Laro appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The player cannot access Laro's services until they have unlocked full access to their ship, the ''[[Trailblazer]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
