'''Catherine Saunders''' is an author and editor who focuses mainly on juvenile literature and movie tie-in books. Apart from writing several [[DK]] Reader Essential Guides, Saunders wrote and edited the notable young readers book [[Star Wars: The Story of Darth Vader (2008)|''Star Wars: The Story of Darth Vader'']] and is also credited as writing its [[Star Wars: The Story of Darth Vader (2015)|2015 reissue]]. She also wrote ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi, Jedi Knight]]'', which was released in [[2012]].

Other non-Star Wars related works include ''Spider-Man: The Amazing Story'', ''High School Musical: The Essential Guide'' and ''WALL-E: The Intergalactic Guide''.
