The '''articles of membership''' were a treaty signed by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Clan Assembly]] joining [[Ryloth]] and other Twi'lek colony planets to the [[Galactic Republic]]. The articles were signed during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Clan Assembly was first mentioned in the [[2021]] [[List of canon audio productions|audio drama]] ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] and performed by a full voice cast.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />
