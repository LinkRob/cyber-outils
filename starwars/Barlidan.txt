'''Barlidan''' was a [[Color|gray]]-skinned [[Gender|male]] [[Kubaz]] who worked  as a [[ferryman]] on the [[Water|ice]]<ref name="Mando1" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|ravinak|ravinak}}</ref> [[Pagodon]]<ref name="Mando1" /> in [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|1}}</ref> the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] approached the ferryman with a [[The Fledgling Mythrol|bounty]] he had captured in a nearby [[Boatman's Reach|cantina]], seeking [[transport]] to his [[ST-70 M-111 Razor Crest series|gunship]], the ''[[Razor Crest]]''.<ref name="Mando1" />

Barlidan summoned one of his newest [[landspeeder]]s by playing the [[flute]], but the Mandalorian rejected it as a [[R2-series astromech droid|droid]] [[pilot]]ed it. After Djarin gave the ferryman extra [[Currency|payment]], Barlidan played the flute once more, summoning an older [[M-335|speeder]] driven by [[Pyan Bosen]], which the Mandalorian accepted. The Kubaz wore a pair of [[goggles]], a brown and gray [[jacket]] with a [[Hair|fur]]-lined hood, brown [[Clothing|pants]], red gloves, and black [[boot]]s. He was able to play the flute.<ref name="Mando1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Kubaz ferryman appeared in the [[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian|first episode]] of the [[2019]] [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="Mando1" /> which aired on [[November 12]], 2019.<ref name="DTCI">{{WebCite|url=https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/the_mandalorian_project_one-sheet_10_ec1e005f.pdf|text=The Mandalorian Media Kit|work=[https://dtcimedia.disney.com Disney Direct-to-Consumer & International]|archivedate=20230315011507}}</ref> He was portrayed by [[Chris Bartlett|Christopher Bartlett]].<ref name="Mando1" /> The character's mask was made for the show by [[Tom Spina]]'s design company, [[Regal Robot]].<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|TomSpinaDesigns|status/1194971072703148032|[[Tom Spina]]|quote=Proud to see the mask we made for #TheMandalorian getting some love :)|archivefile=File:Tom_Spina_Kubaz.png}}</ref> During development, the ferryman was going to be a [[Trandoshan]].<ref name="Art">[[The Art of Star Wars: The Mandalorian (Season One)|''The Art of Star Wars: The Mandalorian'' Season One]]</ref>
