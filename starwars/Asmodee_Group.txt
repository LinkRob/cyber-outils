'''Asmodee Group''' is a tabletop game company based in [[Wikipedia:Guyancourt|Guyancourt]], [[Wikipedia:France|France]]. It is the parent company of [[Atomic Mass Games]], [[Edge Studio]], [[Fantasy Flight Games]],<ref name="About Us" /> [[Space Cowboys]],<ref name="Legal" /> [[Z-Man Games]],<ref name="About Us" /> [[Unexpected Games]],<ref name="Unexpected" /> and [[Days of Wonder]].<ref name="VentureBeat" />
