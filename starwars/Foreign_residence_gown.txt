The '''foreign residence gown''' was a [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[Clothing|royal outfit]] worn by [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] as the [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen of Naboo]] to represent her sadness at being away from her [[homeworld]] during its [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] in [[32 ABY]]. The floor-length, [[Color|black]] [[cyrene silk]] gown had emblems made with antique beads and [[metal]]lic thread. Her hair styled with an intricate foreknot and [[suspensa]]s.

==Description==
The foreign residence gown was a [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[Clothing|royal outfit]] from the [[Padmé Amidala's wardrobe|wardrobe]] owned by [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] when she was the [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen of Naboo]].<ref name="TVE" /> It was made of [[Color|black]] [[cyrene silk]] and had emblems made with antique beads<ref name="TCVD NE" /> and [[metal]]lic thread.<ref name="TVE" /> The floor-length gown concealed her feet and conveyed a sense of effortless movement. Her hair was styled in a wide fan shape along with an intricate foreknot and ornament over her forehead, and she had decorative [[suspensa]]s draped over each shoulder. It was paired with the traditional royal makeup of a whitened face, red beauty marks on her cheeks, and the [[scar of remembrance]] on her lower [[Mouth|lip]]. Worn when the [[queen]] was not in residence on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], the gown represented Amidala's sadness at being away from her [[homeworld]].<ref name="TCVD NE" />

==History==
In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Amidala fled to [[Coruscant]], the [[capital]] planet of the [[Galactic Republic]], to request that the [[Galactic Senate]] end Naboo's [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] by the [[Trade Federation]]. Unaware of [[Senator]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] true identity as the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] who had manipulated the crisis, Amidala visited his [[Palpatine's office|apartment]] following the hearing and wore her foreign residence gown. She had a solemn discussion with the [[Gungan]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] about the events.<ref name="Episode I" />
