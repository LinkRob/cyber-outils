"'''A New Adventure'''" is a short story written by [[Calliope Glass]] with illustrations by [[Katie Cook]] that was published in the young readers book ''[[Star Wars Galactic Storybook]]'' released [[January 6]], [[2021]]. It adapts [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].

==Plot summary==
{{Expandsect}}
A group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]] are fighting back against the evil [[Galactic Empire]] and are working to destroy the [[superweapon]] [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].
