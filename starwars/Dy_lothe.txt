[[Admiral]] '''Dy'lothe''' of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Chiss Defense Force|Defense Force]] was a [[Chiss]] who [[Commanding officer|commanded]] the [[Groundlion man-of-war]] ''[[Venturous]]''. Around [[18 BBY]], during a [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|crisis]] in the Ascendancy, the [[Kilji war cruiser]] ''[[Whetstone]]'' traveled to the [[planet]]s of [[Avidich]] and [[Rhigar]], with the ''Venturous'' journeying to those planets to protect the Ascendancy's people from the [[alien]] [[warship]].

After the [[Chiss]] [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]]'s [[Nikardun blockade frigate]] [[Aelos (frigate)|''Aelos'']] arrived over the planet of [[Sposia]], the ''Venturous'' confronted the [[blockade frigate]], with Dy'lothe attempting to detain the senior captain on orders of the [[Chiss Syndicure]]. However, the ''Aelos'' escaped after partially disabling the admiral's warship with a [[conner net]]. Shortly after, Dy'lothe assessed the aftermath of the [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand|destruction]] of a [[Jixtus's fleet|fleet]] of warships of the [[Grysk]] warlike species over the planet [[Sunrise]].

==Biography==
===After the Kiljis===
{{Quote|Actually, the ''Venturous'' was already en route. ''[&hellip;]'' But the alien was gone when it arrived. ''[&hellip;]'' And as I said, they hadn't been the least bit hostile.|[[Supreme General]] [[Ba'kif]], on the ''Whetstone''|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />|translated=!}}
[[File:Rhigar.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Admiral Dy'lothe was sent to protect the planet Rhigar (pictured) from the Kilji Illumine.]]
Dy'lothe was a [[Gender|male]] [[Chiss]] who,<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> around [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' take place around [[18 BBY]].</ref> was serving as an [[admiral]] in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation's [[Chiss Defense Force]], [[Commanding officer|commanding]], the [[Groundlion man-of-war]] ''[[Venturous]]''. After the [[Kilji war cruiser]] ''[[Whetstone]]'' of the [[Kilji Illumine]] government arrived over the Ascendancy's [[planet]] [[Avidich]], the ''Venturous'' was sent to protect the planet from the [[alien]] [[warship]]. However, the [[war cruiser]] was already gone before the Groundlion arrived. Shortly after, the ''Whetstone'' traveled to the planet [[Rhigar]], and [[Captain]] [[Clarr'os'culry]] [[core name]] "Roscu" met its commanders&mdash;[[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]] and the [[Grysk]] agent "[[Jixtus]]"&mdash;to get the war [[Starship|vessel]] away before Dy'lothe's flagship arrived in three [[Standard hour|hours]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Later, during a [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|crisis]] in the Ascendancy, the ''Venturous'' was assigned to the Chiss world of [[Sposia]], which housed the [[Universal Analysis Group]] "UAG", tasked with safekeeping and studying alien devices such as a [[Vagaari]] [[gravity well projector]]. Before Dy'lothe's arrival, Defense Force [[Supreme Admiral]] [[Ja'fosk]] sent Admiral [[Ar'alani]] and her [[Nightdragon man-of-war]] [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']] to Sposia, as the supreme admiral did not want to leave the UAG unguarded. Moments after the ''Vigilant'' arrived, Dy'lothe reported the presence of a [[Nikardun blockade frigate]], the [[Aelos (frigate)|''Aelos'']], which was crewed by several members of the [[Paccosh]] [[species]] and the Chiss [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn." Tasked with detaining the senior captain by the [[Chiss Syndicure]] ruling body after contacting the Chiss [[capital]] planet of [[Csilla]], the admiral commanded the ''Aelos'' to surrender.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

===Enforcing the Syndicure's wishes===
{{Quote|I'm sorry, Admiral. ''[&hellip;]'' But I can't let you do that.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Excuse me? You can't let me carry out my orders?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Your ''illegal'' orders.|Admirals Ar'alani and Dy'lothe|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />|translated=!}}
[[File:Aralani-Thrawn6.png|thumb|right|300px|Admiral Ar'alani (pictured) confronted Admiral Dy'lothe at Sposia.]]
Ar'alani desired to speak with the senior captain, however, and [[Confrontation between the Aelos and the Venturous|fired]] at the ''Venturous'' to get Dy'lothe's attention. The admiral, who the other believed did not want to carry out his orders, told the ''Vigilant''{{'s}} commander of the Syndicure's order, and engaged in a conversation regarding Thrawn's motives for being at Sposia, which Dy'lothe claimed he did not care about. Meanwhile, the ''Aelos'' moved, sending a [[crippler net]]&mdash;a electronic equipment&ndash;disabling [[weapon]]&mdash;to the ''Venturous''{{'s}} bow [[spectrum laser]] and [[tractor beam]] emplacements. While the [[blockade frigate]] fled, Ar'alani's [[Executive officer|first officer]], Senior Captain [[Kiwu'tro'owmis]] "Wutroow" commented that although it would be useless, Dy'lothe would shoot a [[plasma sphere]] or [[breacher]] [[missile]] at the ''Aelos'', with her commander stating that he would not. Ar'alani then contacted the other admiral, who informed the ''Vigilant''{{'s}} captain that he was leaving for the UAG, where he would drop debris of an [[asteroid missile platform]] that [[Skirmish between the Grayshrike and the Orisson|exploded]] in the [[Ornfra system]] some [[time]] previously.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

[[Standard day|Days]] after Sposia, Dy'lothe arrived in the aftermath of [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand]] over the planet [[Sunrise]], where [[Thrawn's fleet]] of Chiss vessels had destroyed [[Jixtus's fleet|Jixtus's]]. The admiral inquired Ar'alani, who had participated in the conflict, on what he had missed. Some time afterward, Dy'lothe, along with Ja'fosk, Admiral [[Ers'ikaro]], and two other Defense Force admirals, were present in a [[Tribunal of Mitth'raw'nuruodo|tribunal]] where Thrawn was [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] for destroying Jixtus's fleet, with Dy'lothe removing the [[Full dress honor chains|honor chains]] from Thrawn's chest.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
