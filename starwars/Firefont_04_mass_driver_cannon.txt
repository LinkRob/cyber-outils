The '''Firefont 04 mass driver cannon''' was a type of [[mass-driver cannon]] used by the [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer|AT-TE]]<ref name="AT-ACT Deluxe Book" /> and the [[All Terrain Attack Pod|AT-AP]].<ref name="Force Arena">''[[Star Wars: Force Arena]]''</ref> Manufactured by [[Gazac]],<ref name="AT-ACT Deluxe Book" /> the projectile [[cannon]] was [[turret]]-mounted to the  [[walker]]'s dorsal surface.<ref name="AT-TE Walker">{{Databank|at-te-walker|AT-TE Walker}}</ref> The mass-driver cannon 
fired sonic charges, bunker busters, heat-seekers, as well as a variety of other projectile ammunition from its rotating turret.<ref name="AT-ACT Deluxe Book" />
