'''Destroyer-Level Craft Operations''' was a course taught at the [[Royal Imperial Academy]], the [[Coruscant]]-based headquarters of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Academy|academic program]]. It took place in a [[Star Destroyer]] simulator. During their time at the academy, [[Ciena Ree]] and [[Thane Kyrell]] attended that course together.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
