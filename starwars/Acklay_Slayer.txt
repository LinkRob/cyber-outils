The '''Acklay Slayer''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Bounty Hunter (Imara Vex)|standard costume]]. The [[Clothing|outfit]] and [[armor]] resembled an [[acklay]], featuring a [[Color|green]] and tan color scheme, with bladed elements attached to the [[helmet]] and [[gauntlet]]s, a [[Hair|plume]]-like collar, spiky shoulder and knee pads, scale-like patterns on the shin guards, sandy weathering around the legs and feet, and a harness with a large silver buckle connecting the shoulder straps to the belt. The helmet also featured a glowing green visor, and the [[shoulder-mounted armament]] possessed spikes and a large red payload.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Acklay Slayer was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Imara Vex, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.18.0 within the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Acklay Annihilator]].<ref name="Hunters" />
