'''Deej''' was an [[Ewok]] [[Gender|male]]. They and another [[Wicket's mother|Ewok]] were the [[parent]]s of four [[Warrick family|children]]: [[Weechee (Ewok)|Weechee]], [[Widdle Warrick]], [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]], and [[Winda]]. One [[winter]] between [[1 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]], Deej went with a [[hunting]] party and brought essential supplies back to [[Bright Tree tribe]].

==Biography==
[[File:WicketsFamily-2024Ewoks4.png|thumb|right|270px|Deej and their family in Bright Tree Village.]]
Deej was an [[Ewok]] [[Gender|male]] who lived in [[Bright Tree Village]] as part of the [[Bright Tree tribe]] on the [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]].<ref name="Kroolok" /> The Ewok was an accomplished [[hang-glider]] [[pilot]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Deej and another [[Wicket's mother|Ewok]] were the [[parent]]s to four [[Warrick family|children]]: [[Weechee (Ewok)|Weechee]],<ref name="Kroolok" /> [[Widdle Warrick]],<ref name="CE" /> [[Wicket Wystri Warrick|Wicket Warrick]], and [[Winda]]. At some point, Wicket helped his father dig [[Boar-wolf|borra]] pits.<ref name="Kroolok" />

One [[winter]]<ref name="Kroolok" /> between [[1 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="KroolokDate">[[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 4|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 4]] depicts [[Logray]] before he became [[shaman]] of the [[Bright Tree tribe]] and is dated to [[1 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. "[[One Normal Day]]" depicts [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]] as no longer being a [[wokling]] and takes place and occurs concurrent with the events of {{Film|VI}}, which ''Timelines'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. As "[[The Kroolok]]" depicts Logray as a shaman and Warrick as a wokling, it must take place between these two dates.</ref> Deej and a [[hunting]] party left the [[City|village]] in order to obtain essential supplies. Weechee was able to convince his father to let him come with, which made Wicket, who was younger, jealous. Wicket was watching when Deej and the party came back with their spoils, which included a large [[kublag]] and [[Brub berry|brub berries]]. Later that [[Standard day|day]], Weechee told his young brother he felt he took after Deej, noting he was very strong.<ref name="Kroolok" />

Shortly after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Ewoks4">[[Ewoks (2024) 4|''Ewoks'' (2024) 4]]</ref> in 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date2">{{Marvel|url=articles/comics/ewoks-comic-series-steve-orlando-alvaro-lopez-laura-braga|text='Ewoks' Comic Series Reveals What Happened to the Battle of Endor's Most Courageous Warriors}} places [[Star Wars: Ewoks (2024)|''Star Wars: Ewoks'']] directly after the events of {{Film|VI}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> some of the Bright Tree tribe, including Deej, held a gathering with the tribe's neighbors in Bright Tree Village to decide the forest's future. During the gathering, Deej spent time with their family.<ref name="Ewoks4" />

==Personality and traits==
Deej had [[Color|gray]]<ref name="Ewoks4" />-brown [[Hair|fur]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Equipment==
On Endor, Deej donned hooded brown [[clothing]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> and also at one point wore a purple cowl.<ref name="Ewoks4" /> When Weechee and Wicket went hunting, Weechee wore one Deej's old [[cloak]]s. Deej wore [[snowshoe]]s while traveling in the [[Water|snow]].<ref name="Kroolok" />

==Behind the scenes==
Deej first appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[The Kroolok]]," a short story written by [[George Mann]] and [[Cavan Scott]] and published in the [[2021]] short story collection the ''[[Life Day Treasury]]''.<ref name="Kroolok" /> The Ewok was first pictured in "[[Miscellaneous Ships and Vehicles (2)]]," a [[De Agostini]] [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] book<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> published on [[July 29]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Encyclopédie Star Wars}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Deej Warrick|Deej]] was introduced in the [[1984]] television film, ''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]''.<ref name="CoC">''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]''</ref> In Legends, his last name first was confirmed to be "Warrick," akin to his [[Warrick family/Legends|family]] name, in the [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (original)|Databank]].<ref name="DB">{{DB|character|deej|Warrick, Deej}}</ref>
