The '''Bronze Cluster''' was the first embellishment of the [[Kalidor Crescent]], the highest honor of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] awarded only by the [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Alliance Chancellor]] or by the [[Supreme Commander of the Alliance Armed Forces]] for extreme acts of heroism and gallantry against an overwhelming force. Eligibility required a live [[Alliance Military|military]] individual performing a second act of such conduct and was nominated by a superior [[Alliance officer|officer]] or [[Alliance Civil Government|government]] representative.<ref name="Lead" />

The embellishment was a small cluster of [[bronze]] [[star]]s attached to the [[ribbon]] between the medallion and the likened appearance of a [[kalidor]] in flight, an [[Bird|avian]] predator [[species]] from<ref name="Lead" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Davnar II]]. Subsequent embellishments were the [[Silver Talons]], [[Silver Scimitar]], [[Golden Wings]], and [[Diamond Eyes]].<ref name="Lead" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bronze Cluster embellishment was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying]] sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]''.<ref name="Lead" /> The [[Bronze Cluster/Legends|embellishment]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared simultaneously in [[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']], a [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|computer game]] released in [[1993]] by [[Totally Games]]<ref name="X-wing">[[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']]</ref> and the accompanying companion book ''[[The Farlander Papers]]'' for the game's Limited Edition version authored by [[Rusel DeMaria]].<ref name="Farlander">{{StoryCite|story=The Farlander Papers|book=Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)}}</ref>
