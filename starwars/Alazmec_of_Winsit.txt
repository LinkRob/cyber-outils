The '''Alazmec of Winsit''', or '''Alazmec Cultists''', was a [[Sith cult]] formed by members of the [[Alazmec]] [[species]]<ref name="SWCEUAE">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> that followed the [[Sith]] [[religion]] and colonized the [[Volcano|volcanic]] [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] [[Time|sometime]] following the [[death]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. They were the most tenacious of many cult colonists who ventured to the [[Magma|scorched]] Sith world after Vader's [[Battle of Endor|demise]] to venerate his power. They also venerated [[Lady]] [[Corvax]] and [[plant]]ed [[irontree]]s in the bog of [[Corvax Fen]], thought to be drenched in her tears, in a vain attempt to reinvigorate Mustafar's once lush ecosystems.<ref name="TROSVD" /> The Alazmec colonists were [[Mission to Mustafar (First Order)|cut down]] on the grounds of [[Fortress Vader]] by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and his [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] during his [[Mission to Mustafar (First Order)|search]] for the hidden Sith world of [[Exegol]], as the Alazmec of Winsit possessed [[Vader's wayfinder|the]] [[Sith wayfinder]] that once belonged to [[Darth]] Vader.<ref name="TROS" />

The cultists wielded a variety of weapons, including [[CX-55 scatterblaster]]s, various [[melee weapon]]s,<ref name="TROS" /> and [[bow]]s.<ref name="Legacy 1">[[Legacy of Vader 1|''Legacy of Vader'' 1]]</ref> They also wore night-vision lenses.<ref name="SWCEUAE" />
