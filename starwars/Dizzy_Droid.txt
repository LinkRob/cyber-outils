The '''Dizzy Droid''' was a [[gambling]] parlor and bar that operated<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> in the [[Wonto District market]] of [[Mos Eisley]] on [[Tatooine]], a [[desert]] [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Around this time, the [[gambler]]s [[Eodra]], [[Kelel]], and [[Rellra]] frequently played [[Kessel Sabacc]] at the parlor.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|Canon=1}}
During 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] visited the Dizzy Droid while searching for a [[Twi'lek]] named [[Yorn]]. A [[bartender]] at the parlor directed Vess to Yorn's camp in the [[Pillar Forest]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Dizzy Droid first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was one of several locations in the game where the player can play Kessel Sabacc, an optional activity. The name of the parlor was identified in the "Missing Brother" quest.<ref name="Outlaws" />
