'''Darragh O'Farrell''' is the Senior Manager of Audio at [[LucasArts]]. He is credited as the voice-over director for ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: Demolition]]''. He also worked as voice director for ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]'', ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed: Ultimate Sith Edition]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]'', the [[Star Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Sith (video game)|''Star Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Sith'' video game]], ''[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron II: Rogue Leader]]'', and ''[[Soulcalibur IV]]''. O'Farrell was on-staff as voice department manager for [[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (video game)|''Star Wars: Rogue Squadron'']] and ''[[Star Wars Battlefront: Renegade Squadron]]''. In addition, he was creditied as both the voice director and voice producer in ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Mysteries of the Sith]]'', ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing vs. TIE Fighter]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Masters of Teräs Käsi]]'', ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Alliance]]'', the [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace'' video game]], ''[[Star Wars Episode I: The Gungan Frontier]]'', [[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']], ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Super Bombad Racing]]'', and [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (video game)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'']].

He also worked on ''[[Star Wars: DroidWorks]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Force Commander]]'', [[Star Wars: Starfighter (video game)|''Star Wars: Starfighter'']] ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Starfighter]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Racer Revenge]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight II: Jedi Outcast]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Racer Revenge]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunter]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds: Clone Campaigns]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron III: Rebel Strike]]'', ''[[Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided]]'', ''[[Star Wars Galaxies: Jump to Lightspeed]]'', ''[[Star Wars Trilogy: Apprentice of the Force]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Battlefront]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]'', ''[[Star Wars Galaxies: Rage of the Wookiees]]'', ''[[Star Wars Galaxies: The Complete Online Adventures]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Battlefront II]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Lethal Alliance]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War: Forces of Corruption]]'', ''[[LEGO Star Wars II: The Original Trilogy]]'', ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]'', ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Republic Heroes]]'', and was involved with the [[Star Wars Galaxies Trading Card Game|''Star Wars Galaxies'' Trading Card Game]].

He is currently involved in the production of ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.
