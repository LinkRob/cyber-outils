The '''Adrens quarters''' was a quarters that was occupied by the [[Adrens]], [[Joss Adren|Joss]] and [[Pikka Adren]], during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Fate of Eriadu" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the quarters was destroyed by the [[Nihil]] during the [[Battle of Eriadu]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Adrens quarters first appeared in the short story "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: The Fate of Eriadu]]," written by [[Lydia Kang]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Fate of Eriadu" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref> The story was published in the [[Star Wars Insider 229|229th issue]] of the magazine ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' as the sixth installment in the [[The High Republic: Tales from the Occlusion Zone|''Tales from the Occlusion Zone'']] serialized fiction on [[December 10]], [[2024]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/171-star-wars-insider-229|text=Star Wars Insider #229}}</ref>
