A '''franikhad''' was a [[Foodstuff|delicacy]] that was found on nearly every [[desert]] [[planet]] throughout [[the galaxy]]. Local meats, vegetables, and spices would be cooked in a makeshift oven dug into the ground, which was designed to take advantage of the planet's heat and lack of [[water]]. After being cooked, the ingredients would be served in a thin, makeshift [[bread]]-like wrap. Specific variations of the franikhad existed depending on the planet's own meats and culture.<ref name="Brotherhood" />

A vendor on the [[Tatooine]] city of [[Mos Espa]] sold franikhads, a ground oven right next to her stall. [[Anakin Skywalker]] remembered the exact steps to get to her stall, and would get a franikhad as a "bonus" indulgence if his [[Parent|mother]], [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|Shmi Skywalker]], had managed to get extra parts for bartering. Franikhads could also be found on [[Coruscant]]; [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] was told about them by Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa]], and purchased some to eat alongside then-[[Jedi Knight]] Anakin Skywalker after they reminded her of him.<ref name="Brotherhood" />

Fran = our late foodie cat that loved to steal meat. Khad = a pit oven often used by dessert cultures. I saw this years ago when my wife was watching a Gordon Ramsay show.|archivedate=20220517233319|nolive=1}}</ref>
