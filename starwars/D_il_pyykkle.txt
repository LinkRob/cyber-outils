'''D'il pyykkles''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] that could be purchased on the [[planet]] of [[Lothal]].<ref name="FF" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Fighter Flight]]" to [[5 BBY]].</ref> the [[Twi'lek]] [[pilot]] [[Hera Syndulla]] included d'il pyykkles on a shopping list of supplies needed by the crew of her [[VCX-100 light freighter]] the ''[[Ghost]]''. She gave the list to [[Ezra Bridger]] and [[Garazeb Orrelios]]&mdash;two of her crewmates&mdash;who then traveled to the settlement of [[Kothal]] to purchase the items on the list. Orrelios managed to locate and purchase the d'il pyykkles and every other item on the list bar a [[Meiloorun fruit|meiloorun]], which the pair then attempted to [[Attack on Imperial Troop Transports|steal]] from the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="FF" />

==Behind the scenes==
D'il pyykkles were first mentioned in "[[Fighter Flight]]," the fourth episode of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' television show's [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]],<ref name="FF" /> which aired on [[October 13]], [[2014]].<ref name="Release date">{{InsiderCite|156|Star Wars Rebels Recounted|''Star Wars Rebels'' Recounted}}</ref> The list mentioning d'il pyykkles&mdash;which are a clear reference to real life [[Wikipedia:Pickled cucumber|dill pickles]]&mdash;was written using the [[Aurebesh]] alphabet.<ref name="FF" />
