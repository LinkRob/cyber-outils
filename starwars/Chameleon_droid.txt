The '''chameleon droid''' was a type of [[droid]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. At the start of the fourth [[Standard month|month]] of the war, chameleon droids ambushed the [[Jedi]] [[Luminara Unduli]] and [[Barriss Offee]] within the [[Jedi Temple (Ilum)|Jedi Temple]] of [[Ilum]]. The temple was almost completely destroyed and the two Jedi were caught in the rubble, but were saved by [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]].<ref name="Force Users" />
