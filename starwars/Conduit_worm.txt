'''Conduit worms''' were [[Sentience|non-sentient]], [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[worm]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] that [[Diet|fed]] on electricity. They absorbed components as they grew along the [[ecumenopolis]]' [[Power conduit|electrical conduits]], turning them into organs. The worms could grow hundreds of [[meter]]s long, infesting electrical power cable wiring in the [[Coruscant Underworld]] and feeding off [[starship]] power. The name "conduit worm" could also be used as an [[List of phrases and slang|insult]], akin to calling someone a "little parasite."

==Biology and appearance==
[[Sentience|Non-sentient]], conduit worms [[Diet|fed off]] the electricity of buildings and [[starship]]s. These [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[worm]]s absorbed components,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> such as [[Battery|batteries]],<ref name="New Edition" /> as they grew along [[Power conduit|electrical conduits]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> which were then turned into organs. They had [[eye]] threads and resistor segments, capacitor beads to store energy<ref name="New Edition" /> extending from core segments.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> However, the creatures had no discernable head, body, or tail structure.<ref name="BMF26">{{BuildFalconCite|26|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> A conduit worm's feeler threads could grow hundreds of [[meter]]s long, and [[mouth]] cups often grew at their ends. They had [[Color|gray]]-and-brown skin and black eyes.<ref name="New Edition" />

==Behavior==
Conduit worms were known to sneak aboard ships and eat their electrical wiring while the vessel was still in flight.<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> They were considered equally as destructive as [[stone mite]]s. In extreme circumstances, when hungry, a conduit worm could attempt to burrow into the head of a [[humanoid]]<ref name="BMF26" /> through their [[ear]]s<ref name="Coruscant Encylopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Coruscant}}</ref> to feed off its electrical impulses.<ref name="BMF26" />

==History==
{{Quote|That dirty conduit worm.|Merei Spanjaf insults Janus Fhurek|<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref>}}
[[File:MeeburGasconTheZilkin-SWE.png|thumb|left|190px|Borkus compared the appearance of Zilkins (pictured) to that of conduit worms.]]
[[Millennium|Thousands]] of [[Standard year|years]] of [[Ecumenopolis|urbanization]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] led to the evolution of many unique forms of wildlife in its [[Coruscant Underworld|underworld]], including conduit worms. These creatures grew around their [[homeworld]]'s endless power cables and absorbed their components. The possibility of an infestation causing the loss of vital artificial light prompted some<ref name="New Edition" /> worried<ref name="Coruscant Encylopedia" /> underworld residents to wear [[exterminator backpack]]s, which featured probes that detected currents affected by conduit worms.<ref name="New Edition" /> [[Conduit purger]]s were also used as pest control devices to remove the worms from the conduits they had infested,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> as residents could use its prongs to locate and root out the worms before they could grow and move to feed on [[sleep]]ing humanoids.<ref name="Coruscant Encylopedia" />

"Conduit worm" was also used as a [[List of phrases and slang|derogatory term]],<ref name="Missing in Action" /> akin to calling someone a "little parasite."<ref name="Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/missing-in-action-episode-gallery|text="Missing in Action" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="MIA Date">{{TCWcanondates|512}}</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]], [[Borkus]]&mdash;the [[Sullustan]] proprietor of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Abafar]]'s [[Power Sliders]] [[Restaurant|diner]]&mdash;compared [[Meebur Gascon]], a member of the sentient [[Zilkin]] [[species]], to a conduit worm before ejecting him from his business.<ref name="Missing in Action" /> While on the planet [[Lothal]]<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy" /> between [[6 BBY]] and [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Edge Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]'' as taking place from [[6 BBY]] to [[5 BBY]].</ref> the [[Junior Academy of Applied Sciences]] student [[Merei Spanjaf]] called [[Janus Fhurek]], the school's [[athletic director]], a conduit worm after learning of the latter's [[Xenophobia|xenophobic]] actions.<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy" /> By the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]], conduit worms were brought to the Outer Rim planet [[Xorrn]] and lived among several other species there.<ref name="FLT">''[[Friends Like These]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The conduit worm was David ''[West Reynolds]''{{'s}} conception, including the types of details and their function, with the modular eyes, 'batteries' and tentacles. It's meant to come across as a cross between an organic life-form and an electrical circuit.|Robert E. Barnes|<ref name="Archive">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/ref/f20061031/index.html|text=Building a ''Complete Visual Dictionary''}}</ref>}}
[[File:BTMF26.png|thumb|right|215px|Conduit worms were first visually depicted in the current canon in ''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 26.]]
Conduit worms were first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Missing in Action]],"<ref name="Missing in Action" /> the twelfth episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', which originally aired on [[January 5]], [[2013]].<ref name="Episode Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> The creatures were first canonically depicted in "Coruscant Underworld," an article written by [[Mark Newbold]]<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> and published by [[De Agostini]] in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 26|twenty-sixth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF26" /> which was published around [[July 1]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|26}}</ref> Conduit worms were originally created for ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a [[2002]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="Attack of the Clones The Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

The conduit worm was conceived by Reynolds himself and was meant to represent a cross between an organic life-form and an electrical circuit. The model pictured in ''Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary'' was created by concept sculptor [[Robert E. Barnes]] using wire, [[Wikipedia:Sculpey|Sculpey]] polymer clay, urethanes, epoxy, and paint. The conduit worm was intentionally designed to look "pretty nasty" and was built to the scale seen in the book.<ref name="Archive" />

[[File:ConduitwormsBadBatch-LSW.jpg|thumb|left|270px|Omega and the Bad Batch struggled to contain a shipment of conduit worms.]]
Conduit worms appeared in the non-canon [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story "[[Omega Saves the Day]]," released in the [[LEGO Star Wars 115|115th issue]] of the [[Wikipedia:German language|German]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars (magazine)|Lego Star Wars]]'' magazine,<ref name="OSTD">{{LSWCite|115|Omega Saves the Day}}</ref> The story was written by [[Martin Fisher]], illustrated by [[Natascha Römer]] and [[Yanic Leipzig]], and published on [[November 1]], [[2024]].<ref name="jedi-bibliothek">{{WebCite|url=https://jedi-bibliothek.de/datenbank/ausgabe/lego-star-wars-magazin-115|text=LEGO Star Wars Magazin 115|archivedate=20240303173302}}</ref> At some point during or after [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Omega Saves the Day]]" occurs after [[Omega]] meets the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]] in "[[Aftermath]]," which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, "Omega Saves the Day" must have occurred during or after 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]] [[commando]]s and the young [[Cloning|clone]] [[Omega]] picked up a shipment of conduit worms for the [[Trandoshan]] [[Ciddarin Scaleback]] on the planet [[Ettan]], with the latter warning them not to open the crates they acquired. While initially confused about the crates' high energy level, several conduit worms broke out of their crate and rushed toward the group's ship&mdash;the [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Marauder'']]. The group struggled to contain the creatures before they could attach themselves to the ship, fearing the worms would render the ''Marauder'' inoperable. Using power packs, Omega lured the conduit worms away from the ''Marauder'', allowing the Batch Batch member [[Wrecker]] to trap them in their crates. To ensure they would not escape again, Wrecker slept on top of the worms' containers to keep them shut.<ref name="OSTD" />
