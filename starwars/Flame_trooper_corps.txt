The '''flame trooper corps''' was a [[corps]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] that consisted of [[clone flametrooper]]s. Initially, clone flametroopers were outfitted with [[MK I insulated armor]], but was severely flawed in that it did not have sufficient respirator filtration. When the problem was quickly fixed in the [[MK II "Hotspot" Insulated Armor]], it provided a morale boost to the flame trooper corps.<ref name="Rise" /> Members of the flame trooper corps participated in the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], where they served alongside [[special ops clone trooper]]s under [[Jedi General]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Jet]].<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref>
