'''Clarr'ivl'exow''', [[core name]] '''Rivlex''', was a [[Chiss]] [[Gender|male]] who was the [[Patriarch]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Clarr family]]. He resided on the [[planet]] [[Rhigar]], where he had a standing policy that refused landing permission to all non-Chiss vessels. When "[[Jixtus]]," an associate of [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]] who was traveling aboard the [[Kilji war cruiser]] ''[[Whetstone]]'', requested to speak with the Patriarch about a supposed threat from within the Ascendancy, [[Captain]] [[Clarr'os'culry]] "Roscu" wished to judge whether the information was worthy of being passed onto Rivlex; however, as the Patriarch's policy prevented Jixtus from landing on Rhigar, Roscu agreed to meet aboard the ''Whetstone'' instead.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
