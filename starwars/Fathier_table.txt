A '''fathier table'''<ref name="Outlaws" /> also known as a '''holographic fathier-betting module'''<ref name="Low Red Moon">''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]''</ref> was a type of [[betting console]] with integrated [[holoprojector]]s that allowed beings to view and [[gambling|bet]] on [[Fathier racing|fathier races]] broadcast from the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]] on the [[casino]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].

==Description==
Fathier tables<ref name="Art Of Outlaws">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]''</ref> were a type of [[betting console]] that allowed beings to both [[gambling|bet]] on and view remote [[Fathier racing|fathier races]]. The table's shape resembled the oval shape of a [[fathier]] race track with betting [[Computer|terminal]] positioned around the edge. They featured a central [[hologram]] which displayed past race results as well as a close up of ongoing races. During a race, individual holograms synced to the participating fathier's position move around table's center track.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==History==
By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> fathier tables had made their way to [[cantina]]s and [[gambling hall]]s around [[the galaxy]], including on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]], the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planets [[Tatooine]], [[Akiva]], [[Cantonica]], the Outer Rim [[moon]] [[Toshara]] as well as aboard the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''. In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], fathier tables included live broadcasts of fathier races from the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]] in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on Cantonica as part of the [[Canto Cup]] tournament. These broadcasts included commentary transmitted directly from the racetrack in Canto Bight with [[Unidentified Canto Cup announcer (Basic)|one announcer]] speaking in [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]] and [[Unidentified Canto Cup announcer (Huttese)|another announcer]] speaking in [[Huttese]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fathier tables appeared for the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Prior to the game's release, they were shown in  promotional material; first appearing in the game's "Summer Game Fest 2024" teaser trailer which was released on [[June 8]], 2024.<ref name="Official Teaser Summer Game Fest">{{UbisoftYouTube|I6tA_o8uzUI|Star Wars Outlaws: Official Teaser &#124; Summer Game Fest 2024}}</ref> The tables were first identified as "fathier tables" in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]'' The tables were designed for the game by [[Wikipedia:Concept art|concept artist]] [[Adrien Girod]]<ref name="Girod ArtStation">{{ArtStation|subdomain=kurobot|url=projects/4N9BAL|name=[[Adrien Girod]]|text=Sabacc Tables|date=2024-09-30|archiveurl=https://archive.vn/LyuMv}}</ref> and were designed to have a tactile and mechanical design rather than just being screens or holograms.<ref name="Art Of Outlaws" />
