The '''Emperor's wayfinder''' was a [[Sith wayfinder]] belonging to the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] and deposed [[Galactic Emperor]], [[Darth Sidious]], that led to the [[Sith Citadel]] on [[Exegol]]. It was originally located in [[Emperor's Throne Room|his throne room]] in the Imperial Vault on the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. The [[Jedi]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], Sidious' granddaughter, [[Mission to Kef Bir|traveled]] to the [[Death Star ruins|ruins of the Death Star II]] on [[Kef Bir]], after the [[mission to Kijimi]], to obtain the wayfinder. [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] destroyed the device in order to turn Rey to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] and force her to accompany him to Exegol.<ref name="TROS" />
