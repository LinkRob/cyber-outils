'''Elod'al'vumic''', known by her [[core name]] '''Dalvu''', was a [[Gender|female]] [[Chiss]] who served as a [[Mid Commander]] aboard the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[Chiss heavy cruiser|heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

Dalvu was the ''Springhawk''{{'s}} [[sensor officer]]<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> since when [[Ufsa'mak'ro|Samakro]] first commanded the ''Springhawk''. Right before the [[Raid on Rapacc]], Dalvu made a disrespectful remark on [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]'s decision on returning to [[Rapacc]], earning her a stern reprimand from the ''Springhawk''<nowiki/>'s [[Executive officer|first officer]], [[Mid Captain]] [[Ufsa'mak'ro|Samakro]]. Later, during the [[Lioaoin skirmish]], Dalve again made a subtle criticism of Thrawn's tactical assessment of the Lioaoin warships, causing Samakro to admonish her. Despite this, Samakro considered her and the other bridge officers to be capable and trusted them with his life<ref name=":0">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>.

==Biography==
===Battle at Rapacc===
Before the [[Springhawk]] engages in a battle over the [[Rapacc system]], where the Chiss would capture a [[Nikardun]] vessel, Dalvu engages in a conversation with Mid Captain [[Ufsa'mak'ro]] (Samakro). She repeatedly questions Thrawn's capability and authority, causing Samakro to think that Senior Commander [[Plikh'ar'illmorf]] (Kharill) had been spreading his own dissatisfaction upon his fellow officers. Samakro scolds her and tells her to keep her derogatory comments to herself.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Personality and traits==
Mid Captain Samakro believed that Dalvu to not be the type to come up with derogatory opinions about her commanding officer on her own, let alone have the audacity to speak them. He attributed her vocal discontentment towards Thrawn as a result of [[Senior Commander]] [[Plikh'ar'illmorf|Kharill]] spreading his displeasure regarding the new command structure with his fellow officers<ref name="Chaos Rising" />.

Dalvu was friendly with the ''Springhawk''<nowiki/>'s resident sky-walker, [[Che'ri]], offering to play [[Gribblehex]] with the young girl in her spare time. According to Samakro, Dalvu was the most competitive gamer he had ever seen.
