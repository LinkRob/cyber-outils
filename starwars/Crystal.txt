'''Crystals''' were solid substances with various types and uses throughout [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Twilight">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref><ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref> Some crystals such as the [[nectrose crystal]] was used in dishes as a flavor-enhancer,<ref name="">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref> while other crystals such as the [[kyber crystal]] was used by the [[Jedi]]<ref name="datakyber">{{Databank|lightsaber-crystal|Kyber Crystal (Lightsaber Crystal)}}</ref> and the [[Sith]] for their [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Kyber crystal note">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'' states that [[kyber crystal]]s form the [[heart]] of a [[lightsaber]].</ref>

[[Nova crystal]]s were a type of crystal.<ref name="galactic">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref> Some [[planet]]s, such as [[Christophsis]]<ref name="datachris">{{Databank|christophsis|Christophsis}}</ref> and [[NaJedha]] were crystalline in nature.<ref name="Star Wars 38">[[Star Wars (2015) 38|''Star Wars'' (2015) 38]]</ref>

The [[Galactic Empire]] conducted [[Mining|strip mining]] on [[Lothal]] for ore and crystals.<ref name="sketch">''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]''</ref>

The surface of the planet [[Galzez]] was covered in [[Color|orange]] crystals.<ref name="TGOG">"{{RebelsMagCite|20|The Gangsters of Galzez}}</ref> The caverns of the planet [[Quarzite]] were filled with purple crystals.<ref name="Bounty">{{TCW|Bounty (episode)}}</ref> [[Tynzo crystal]]s were only found on the world of [[Isobe]].<ref name="Never Far Behind">{{RebelsMagCite|39|Never Far Behind}}</ref>

{{Substance-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Crystals, albeit unidentified, first appeared in "[[The Gathering (episode)|The Gathering]]," the sixth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Gathering">{{TCW|The Gathering (episode)}}</ref> which aired on [[November 3]], [[2012]].<ref name="The Gathering EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-gathering-episode-guide|text="The Gathering" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> The mineral was then identified as a "crystal" in "[[A Test of Strength]]," the seventh episode of the same season of ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars''.<ref name="A Test of Strength">{{TCW|A Test of Strength}}</ref>
