'''Arratu Station''' was a [[city]] on the [[planet]] [[Parnassos]] created by the [[Con Star Mining Corporation]].

==History==
Following the [[Devastation of Parnassos]] and the abandonment by Con Star, the population fell into extreme divides. Leaders known as [[Arratu]] tried to keep the population under control using the [[Arratu Station arena|arena]]. They also sent out raiding parties to capture new participants.<ref name="Phasma" />

Around [[24 ABY]],<ref name="Phasma date" /> the raiders captured a [[Scyre]] raiding party assisting [[First Order]] forces. The warrior [[Phasma]] dominated in the arena, allowing her to kill the [[Unidentified Arratu|current Arratu]], becoming one herself. She then abandoned the city to continue towards the [[dead lands]] on her quest with [[General]] [[Brendol Hux]] to reach his crashed [[starship]].<ref name="Phasma" />

Before leaving Parnassos with Phasma and the Scyre child [[UV-8855|Frey]], Hux had the [[Finalizer]] destroy Arratu Station from orbit with it's [[Turbolaser]]<nowiki/>s as a demonstration of the First Order's power.<ref name="Phasma" />
