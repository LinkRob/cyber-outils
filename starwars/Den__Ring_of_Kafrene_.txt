The '''Den''' was a base in a [[hangar]]<ref name="EAcom gameplay" /> on the [[Ring of Kafrene]] that was used as a headquarter by the military group [[Zero Company]], led by [[Hawks]], during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Announcement" /> Former [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Runa Blask]] worked as an advisor for the group at the Den.<ref name="PC Gamer 4">{{WebCite|url=https://www.pcgamer.com/games/strategy/star-wars-zero-company-is-more-than-just-star-wars-xcom-it-feels-like-mass-effect-but-with-turn-based-tactics-and-permadeath/|text=Star Wars Zero Company is more than just 'Star Wars XCOM'&ndash;it feels like Mass Effect but with turn-based tactics and permadeath|author=Ted Litchfield|date=2026-03-26|work=[[Wikipedia:PC Gamer|''PC Gamer'']]|archivedate=20260326143803}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:The-Den_Concept_art.png|thumb|Concept art of the Den|left|300x300px]]
The Den will appear in the upcoming 2026 turn-based-tactics [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Zero Company]]'' as the main base of the player.<ref name="Announcement" /> Between operations the Den can be used for talking with the team members and recruiting new ones. It will have an expansive [[Hologram|holographic]] [[map]] of [[the galaxy]] that will be used for planning new operations. Over the course of the game the Den can also be upgraded.<ref name="Celebration">{{SWYouTube|q0trVP56Cy0|Star Wars Celebration LIVE! 2025 - DAY 2}} {{C|c. 6:28:00-7:08:00}}</ref> Inside the Den, the player will control Hawks in a third-person over-the-shoulder perspective.<ref name="PC Gamer 4 de">{{WebCite|url=https://www.pcgames.de/Star-Wars-Zero-Company-Spiel-75171/Specials/Vorschau-Preview-XCOM-Exklusiv-1521249|text=Exklusiv gespielt!
