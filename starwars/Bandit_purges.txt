The '''Bandit purges''' was the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] purge of outlaws in [[the galaxy]]. During the Bandit purges, a [[Unidentified pirate station|pirate station]] was crippled by three [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] and the [[Accresker Penal Legion]] was sent in to board it. After seeing the pirate station destroyed [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] guessed correctly that the raid was part of the purges.<ref name="Aphra 21" />
