A '''capital''' was the seat of a territorial [[Politics|government]].

The [[Core Worlds]] planet of [[Coruscant]] was considered the capital of the entire [[The galaxy|galaxy]] as it was the center of galactic culture and civilization and served as the seat of government for the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Coruscant">{{SWE|locations|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] world of [[Raxus Secundus]] served as the headquarters of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and hosted the [[Separatist Senate]].<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref>

Following the [[Battle of Endor]], [[Chandrila]] became the capital of the [[New Republic]], a nascent government that was created from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> To gain the support of disaffected worlds and prevent the friction that occurred under the Old Republic and the Empire, the New Republic chose not to base its government on Coruscant. Instead, member worlds would host the capital and [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] on a rotational basis through elections. About thirty years after the Battle of Endor, [[Hosnian Prime]] was the capital of the New Republic until it was [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroyed]] by the [[First Order]]'s [[Starkiller Base]] superweapon.<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

[[File:Republic_City_Hosnian_Prime.png|thumb|left|300px|Hosnian Prime served as the capital of the New Republic until its destruction by the First Order.]]
Some planets also had capital cities. [[Theed]] was the capital of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] world of [[Naboo]].<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Mos Espa]] was the de-facto capital of the Outer Rim world of [[Tatooine]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> The planet [[Lothal]] also had a [[Capital City]] which contained the [[Imperial Complex (Lothal)|Imperial Complex]] and the [[Lothal City Capitol Building]].<ref name="Vision of Hope">{{Rebels|Vision of Hope}}</ref>
