'''''Darth Vader'' 26''' is the twenty-sixth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]] and [[Alex Sinclair]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[August 24]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''INTO THE SAND! We all know about Anakin Skywalker's aversion to finely ground particulate matter. But what does sand mean to the Dark Lord of the Sith? When Sabé, Vader's unlikely new ally, goes missing, Vader must confront his own dark heart in the maelstrom of a terrible sandstorm &ndash; while tapping into one of his earliest skills in a wildly unexpected way!''

==Plot summary==
===Riding into the storm===
[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] experiences flashbacks of his previous life as the [[Jedi]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]. On [[Naboo]], he tells [[Padmé Amidala]] that he dislikes sand but appreciates the softness and lushness of the planet. In the present, [[ZED-6-7]] tells Lord Vader that the sandstorm is disrupting comm signals, preventing him from tracing [[Sabé]] or the other villagers. Vader dismisses ZED, telling the droid that he will call upon his assistance when he requires it. Based on the storm's current velocity, ZED predicts that the storm will hit them in four minutes and three seconds. He recommends that everyone except Lord Vader evacuate.

Speaking in [[Rodese/Legends|Rodese]], [[Wald]] believes that Lord Vader is doomed, saying that only Skywalker would be able to drive the machine through such a storm. He adds that even its builder [[Kitster Banai]] had trouble managing it. While firing up [[Kitster's podracer]], Vader experiences flashbacks of his childhood on [[Tatooine]] building [[Anakin Skywalker's Podracer|his podracer]] while his friends and mother [[Shmi Skywalker Lars]] watch. Vader races Kitster's podracer through the forest amidst the storm. Amidst the sandstorm, he experiences a flashback of him and his mother walking through a sandstorm through the deserts of Tatooine. He tells his mother that he is not leaving her.

In another flashback, Skywalker speaks with his mother Skywalker Lars before leaving with [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]. His mother tells him that her place is on Tatooine and that it is his time to go. Skywalker doesn't want things to change but Shmi replies that change is like the [[Tatoo I|twin]] [[Tatoo II|suns]] setting. She tells him to be brave and not to look back. The flashback then moves forward to Skywalker's meeting with the [[Jedi High Council]] where he confides that he misses his mother. Master Yoda warns that fear is the path to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]], leading to anger, hate and suffering. 

The flashback then flashes to Skywalker slaughtering [[Tusken Raider]]s for murdering his mother. Vader then experiences a flashback of Amidala falling out of their [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/infantry gunship]] during the [[First Battle of Geonosis|Battle of Geonosis]]. He argues with Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who urges him to put his Jedi duties above Amidala's well-being and safety.

===The rescue===
Returning to the present, Vader finds Kitster, who is surprised to see Vader riding his podracer. He tells Vader that Sabé's speeder was hit by the tank. He believes that she is trapped underneath the tank. Using [[the Force]], Vader throws the tank aside, freeing Sabé. The three of them are then caught in the middle of an orbital bombardment wave. 

Above, [[Admiral]] [[Firmus Piett]] receives Vader's orders to use the [[Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Executor]]''{{'s}} ventral [[turbolaser]]s to bombard their coordinates. Piett is alarmed that would incinerate everyone in the area including Vader. However, Vader uses the Force to [[Force choke]] Piett into submission. Piett complies and unleashes the turbolaser on [[Governor]] [[Tauntaza]]'s [[Tauntaza's fortress|giant tank]]. Vader along with Kitster and Sabé take shelter and survive the bombardment. He carries the unconscious Sabé. Meanwhile on [[Coruscant]], [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] mocks Lord Vader for failing to save his mother, Amidala and for believing he can save Sabé.
