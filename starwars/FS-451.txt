'''FS-451''',<ref name="The Witness" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> [[Gender|male]] [[stormtrooper]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Along with [[Deena Lorn]], he was selected to escort [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to [[Cloud City]]. FS-451 was a cruel individual, which disturbed fellow stormtrooper, [[Deena Lorn]], who soon defected.<ref name="The Witness" /> FS-451 later served under [[Captain]] [[Enric Pryde]] as part of his [[Enric Pryde's regiment|regiment]] at the [[Battle of Endor]] having become addicted to [[death stick]]s.<ref name="The Steadfast Soldier" />

FS-415 had a bullet-shaped head that was shaven of [[hair]]. Captain Pryde regarded FS-451 as fanatical and dangerous.<ref name="The Steadfast Soldier" />
