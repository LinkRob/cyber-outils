By [[42 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''duel''' was fought on the astronomical object [[Corvair II]] after the [[Wookiee]] [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Krrsish]] attacked fellow [[Trandoshan]] Initiate [[Gheyr]].<ref name="Yoda5" /> When Gheyr's friend, the [[Togorian]] [[Jedi]] [[youngling]] [[Jak'zin]], tried to stop Krrsish, he was harmed and punched by the Wookiee. The fight was eventually interrupted by [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]].<ref name="Yoda6" />

{{Battle-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The duel on Corvair II first appeared in the [[Yoda 5|fifth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Yoda]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series. The comic was authored by [[Jody Houser]], illustrated by [[Luke Ross]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Yoda5" /> on [[March 15]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/96873/star_wars_yoda_2022_5|text=Star Wars: Yoda (2023) #5}}</ref> Prior to the comic's release, it was first mentioned in a post published on [[January 17]] of that year on [[StarWars.com]] that announced the future release of [[Yoda 6|''Yoda'' 6]].<ref name="SW">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-april-2023-covers|text=Inferno Squad Strikes Back in Marvel's April 2023 ''Star Wars'' Comics &ndash; Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
