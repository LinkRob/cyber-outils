'''Faie''' was a [[human]] [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] with the [[military rank]] of [[Clone Commander]]. Serving in the [[Quinlan Vos's unit|unit]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Quinlan Vos]], [[Commander]] Faie's by-the-book and no-nonsense approach aggravated his [[Jedi]] superior. Faie was [[Siege of Saleucami|dispatched]] to the [[planet]] [[Saleucami]] and subsequently to the [[Wookiee]] [[homeworld]], [[Kashyyyk]], to defend it from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]. Faie took control of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[trooper]]s after the disarray of [[Order 66]], a directive calling for the immediate [[Death penalty|execution]] of the Jedi. A wounded Vos struck down Faie during the [[Great Jedi Purge]] before Faie could destroy a Wookiee village.

==Biography==
===Earlier life and service===
[[File:Faie-GG.png|thumb|left|220px|Clone Commander Faie made use of customized clone trooper armor.]]
[[Cloning|Grown]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] from the [[DNA]] of [[Jango Fett]], a [[bounty hunter]] who became the [[Clone template|template]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]],<ref name="Clone Troopers" /> the individual who adopted the name Faie was a clone [[trooper]] with the [[military rank]] of [[Clone Commander]]. Faie was one of many [[commander]]s that were specially trained for combat<ref name="GentleGiant" /> for the pan-[[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]].<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> during the final stages of the Clone Wars, Commander Faie was [[Siege of Saleucami|dispatched]] to [[Saleucami]],<ref name="GentleGiant" /> a planet within the [[Foundry of the Confederacy]],<ref name="Battles" /> alongside [[Clone Marshal Commander]]<ref name="GAR">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Grand Army of the Republic}}</ref> [[Neyo]]<ref name="GentleGiant" /> of the [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]]<ref name="91st Recon FC">{{ForceCollection|Clone Trooper: 91st Reconnaissance Corps|star=3}}</ref> and Clone Marshal Commander<ref name="BFM 21">{{BuildFalconCite|21|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> [[Bly]]<ref name="GentleGiant" /> of the [[327th Star Corps]].<ref name="BFM 21" />

===Kashyyyk and Order 66===
The [[Wookiee]] [[homeworld]], [[Kashyyyk]], located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]], was an attractive target for the Separatists. As such, [[Jedi General]] [[Luminara Unduli]] [[Commanding officer|commanded]] Kashyyyk's defenses, and was joined by [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] and Jedi General [[Quinlan Vos]]<ref name="Battles" /> in 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].<ref name="Atlas" /> Following his [[time]] on Saleucami, Commander Faie was sent to Kashyyyk as well, with both Faie and the [[general]] in whose [[Quinlan Vos's unit|unit]] he served, Vos, fighting a [[Battle of Kashyyyk|vicious campaign]] against [[droid]] armies, keeping the planet securely in Republic control.<ref name="GentleGiant" /> During the Battle of Kashyyyk, [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]], publicly the Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] Sheev Palpatine, contacted [[clone trooper officer]]s throughout the [[galaxy]], directing them to execute [[Order 66]]. The protocol called for the immediate [[Death penalty|execution]] of the Jedi as they were classified as [[Treason|traitors]], thus beginning the [[Great Jedi Purge]].<ref name="Battles" />

[[File:Faie-GG-cloaked.png|thumb|right|240px|Commander Faie utilized a cloak with his clone armor.]]
Republic forces fell into disarray as clone troopers sought to eliminate their Jedi Generals, during which Separatist forces led by the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|ST-series super tactical droid]] General [[Linwodo]] established a beachhead and prepared their attack on the [[city]] [[Kachirho]]. Despite Clone Commander [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]] of the [[41st Elite Corps]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Jek]] of the [[Ranger Corps]] having been [[Death|killed]] attempting to assassinate Master Yoda as part of Order 66, Commander Faie was able to quickly regroup the Republic forces and secure a final victory against the Separatists. Clone operations then adjusted to hunting down lingering Separatists and Jedi fugitives. On Faie's orders, [[clone scout trooper]]s using [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s,<ref name="Battles" /> including the [[41st Ranger Platoon]],<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> searched for Yoda and Vos amid Kashyyyk's bogs and [[forest]]s.<ref name="Battles" />

Although Yoda escaped,<ref name="Battles" /> a wounded Vos struck down Faie before he could destroy a Wookiee village in his hunt for the Jedi.<ref name="GentleGiant" /> Unduli was captured and placed in custody, where she eventually died.<ref name="Battles" /> Vos survived Order 66 and went on to help the [[organization]] [[Hidden Path]] rescue [[Force-sensitive]]s and Jedi survivors.<ref name="Kenobi III">{{Kenobi|Part III}}</ref> Although Vos' fate became largely unknown,<ref name="Battles" /> the [[Inquisitorius]], a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Jedi hunter|Jedi hunting]] organization within the Republic's successor government, the [[Galactic Empire]], presumed Vos to have survived and included him in a list of priority targets with other presumed survivors, such as Yoda.<ref name="Dark Lord 7">[[Darth Vader (2017) 7|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 7]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
As a clone, Commander Faie was a [[human]] who stood 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|ft]]) tall,<ref name="Clone Troopers" /> weighed eighty [[kilogram]]s,<ref name="Weight" /> and had [[Color|tan]] skin.<ref name="Clone Troopers" /> Faie was an ideal [[soldier]] from the [[Kaminoan]] [[engineer]]s that produced him, as he was a by-the-book, no-nonsense soldier. As such, his attitude was the cause of much aggravation to his Jedi General, Quinlan Vos, who was more flexible.<ref name="GentleGiant" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a clone trooper [[officer]], Commander Faie was trained in combat.<ref name="GentleGiant" />

==Equipment==
At [[Reproduction|birth]], like the other clone troopers, Faie was implanted by the Kaminoans with a [[behavioral modification biochip]] to ensure that the trooper would not resist certain [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|orders]].<ref name="Clone Troopers" /> Commander Faie used a set of white customized [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] with circular light brown markings on the [[limb]]s. Faie's [[helmet]] had one such marking as a circle on the right side of the helmet. The helmet consisted of [[Breath mask|respirators]], a [[mouth]]piece, and two small visors for the [[eye]]s. Additionally, a pentagonal accessory was placed on the top section of the helmet, centered above the visors. Faie also wore a white [[cloak]] over his [[armor]], and he was equipped with a [[DC-15A blaster rifle]] as well as a light brown [[utility belt]].<ref name="GentleGiant" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:FullFaie-TCWBRBD1.png|thumb|right|110px|Concept art of Faie for ''Revenge of the Sith'']]
Clone Commander Faie debuted in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in a bust for the character from [[Gentle Giant Studios]] as an exclusive product for Premier Guild members. The hand-made polyresin bust, released in [[2016]], was based on concept art for [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="GentleGiant" /> the final installment of the [[prequel trilogy]],<ref name="Episode III" /> which was released on [[May 19]], [[2005]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> Originally, Faie was meant to be the clone appearing on Saleucami, but after Faie's digital model was replaced by CC-8826's instead, the clone's name was changed to correspond with the alteration. As a result, Faie did not appear in the film. Faie is named after [[Lucasfilm]] Art Department Supervisor [[Fay David]].<ref name="Neyo DB>{{DB|character|commanderneyo|Neyo, Commander}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Faie/Legends|Faie]] debuted in the [[Republic 74|seventy-fourth issue]] of [[Dark Horse Comics]]' ''[[Star Wars: Republic]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book series]],<ref name="Republic 74">[[Republic 74|''Republic'' 74]]</ref> released on [[March 23]], 2005.<ref name="74 Date">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/10-440/Star-Wars-Republic-74|text=Star Wars: Republic #74}}</ref> The comic issue was authored and penciled by [[John Ostrander]] and [[Jan Duursema]], respectively.<ref name="Republic 74" />
