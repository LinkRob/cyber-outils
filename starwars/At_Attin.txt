'''At Attin''' was a [[forest]]ed [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] with an orderly population that was isolated from the wider [[The galaxy|galaxy]] as part of the [[Great Work]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]]. Citizens worked diligently as [[analyst]]s with the help of [[service droid]]s of various types on the world beneath the [[Barrier]], which hid the world and blocked any view of the [[star]]s from the surface. By the [[New Republic Era]], At Attin was known to the wider galaxy as a legendary treasure planet among the [[Jewels of the Old Republic]]. 

The [[Youngling|children]] of the [[Skeleton Crew]] [[Homeworld|hailed]] from At Attin, and their adventures aboard the formerly long-lost [[starship]] ''[[Onyx Cinder]]'' culminated in an [[Invasion of At Attin|invasion of the planet]] by [[Captain]] [[Jod Na Nawood|Silvo]]'s [[Silvo's pirates|pirates]] to plunder the [[At Attin Mint]]. The invasion was foiled by the heroics of Skeleton Crew and the intervention of the [[New Republic]], which was thus made aware of the planet's existence.

==Description==
[[File:AtAttin-SC-RealAdventure.png|thumb|left|250px|The suburbs of At Attin.]]
Orderly, peaceful, and isolated, At Attin was a hilly and [[forest]]ed [[planet]]. Citizens lived in the comfort of a [[Unidentified city (At Attin)|city]] and its suburbs. The [[atmosphere]] was breathable to the citizens and facilitated [[Local day|day-and-night]] cycles, though a [[Barrier]], physically composed of a network of red lights over, [[Color|green]] swirling clouds<ref name="Real Adventure" /> alike [[nebula]]e with blue flashes, blocked access to outer [[Realspace|space]] and [[the galaxy]]-at-large.<ref name="Very Interesting" />

==History==
===The Great Work===
{{Quote|Once you're assigned a career path, you'll become part of our planet At Attin's contribution to the Great Work. Keeping the Republic peaceful and strong.|Undersecretary Fara|<ref name="Real Adventure" />}}
[[Jewels of the Old Republic|At Attin and eight other planets]] were hidden by the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Very Interesting" /> Isolated from the wider galaxy, the planet's populace worked diligently as part of the Republic's Great Work program,<ref name="Real Adventure" /> oblivious to events off-world.<ref name="Very Interesting" /> At Attin's civil society was comprised of [[analyst]]s and statistical accountants and considered itself an important contribution to the Republic. The city and suburbs were serviced by [[droid]]s operating under the [[Office of the Supervisor]] for [[At Attin's government]]<ref name="Real Adventure" /> and its [[Great Work]] program.<ref name="Very Interesting" /> [[Service droid]]s maintained public utilities such as roads, trams, and schools, while [[safety droid]]s patrolled the city to ensure laws were followed.<ref name="Real Adventure" /> Passage through the barrier without government authorization was illegal.<ref name="Very Interesting" /> At some point, the [[starship]] ''[[Onyx Cinder]]'' was buried in the forest near At Attin's city.<ref name="Real Adventure" />

After the glory days of the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> and a period marked by decline and corruption,<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> the [[Democracy|democratic]] Old Republic was faced by the devastating [[Clone Wars]] and then transformed into the [[Galactic Empire]], which was only overthrown after [[Galactic Civil War|war]] and tragedy, such as the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Timelines" /> At some point, the other so-called "Jewels of the Old Republic" were destroyed,<ref name="Very Interesting" /> and off-world, At Attin became a mythical "lost planet of eternal treasure" by the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Way Out" /> [[Youngling|Children]] were told of it in stories, [[pirate]]s sung its name in shanties, and its legend spread far in rumor to those seeking treasure.<ref name="Very Interesting" /> On At Attin, children grew up with heroic tales of the [[Jedi]], and school students studied features of the Republic,<ref name="Real Adventure" /> such as the worlds [[Coruscant]] and Alderaan,<ref name="Very Interesting" /> but focused on mathematics for the [[Career Assessment Test]] in middle school. The exam determined each individual's career path. Some children modified and raced [[hoverbike]]s as a pastime in their neighborhoods.<ref name="Real Adventure" />

===Skeleton Crew===
Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> [[At Attin Middle School Gamma]] students [[Wim]], [[Neel]], [[Fern]], and [[KB]] found the buried starship with a droid named [[SM-33]], along with a [[Snowball|rat]]. Wim activated the ship and the children were unable to stop its course, leaving At Attin and into [[hyperspace]]. The [[Skeleton Crew]] reached [[Borgo Prime|Port Borgo]], where they sought directions home but instead ran into a [[Silvo's pirates|pirate gang]] led by [[Captain]] [[Brutus]].<ref name="Way Out" />

===Invasion of At Attin===
The pirate [[Jod Na Nawood]] used the [[Onyx Cinder]] to get past the Barrier in his attempt to [[Invasion of At Attin|plunder]] the Old Republic Mint on At Attin. However thanks to the efforts of Neel, Wim, KB, Fern, Fara, and Wendle the [[New Republic]] intervened and ultimately defeated the pirates.<ref name="The Real Good Guys" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|Students, tell the Undersecretary how you would like to contribute to the Great Work.''"<br />"''Oh, oh!''"<br />"''Yes, Roona?''"<br />"''I want to be a senior statistical accountant!|A teacher droid and the student [[Roona (individual)|Roona]]|<ref name="Real Adventure" />}}
At Attin was populated by [[human]]s, [[Myykian]]s, [[Ithorian]]s, [[Rodian]]s, and [[Vazooan]]s, who were all aided by droids. Citizens [[Language|spoke]] in [[Galactic Basic Standard]] and [[Currency|traded]] in Republic [[Galactic Credit Standard|dataries]].<ref name="Real Adventure" />

==Locations==
At Attin's city had various intersecting roads and buildings, including [[At Attin Middle School Gamma]]<ref name="Real Adventure" /> and the [[Supervisor's Tower]].<ref name="Can't say">{{SkeletonCrew|Can't Say I Remember No At Attin}}</ref> Further from the city center were suburban houses, some of which had garages for [[hoverbike]]s.<ref name="Real Adventure" />
