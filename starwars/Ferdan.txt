'''Ferdan''' was a town located on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Dalna]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. It had a single small [[Docking yards|spaceport]], with only a [[droid]] managing the [[docking beacon]]. The town also had only two facilities with rooms to let. Ferdan had a [[Ferdan market|central marketplace]], as well as a [[Standard day|day]] of rest that its inhabitants observed. The [[Path compound|commune]] of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] was situated a short distance outside of town. Children of the Path, who were found to be [[Force-sensitive]], were sent there rather than being raised within the commune.<ref name="POD" />
