'''Exodeen''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Colonies]]' [[Exodeen system]], situated along the [[Hydian Way]] and [[Nanth'ri Trade Route]]. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] operative [[Luke Skywalker]] traveled, aboard the yacht ''[[Desert Jewel]]'', to Exodeen from the planet [[Denon]] to take the Nanth'ri Trade Route on his way to the planet Omereth. [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[starship]]s were present in Exodeen's [[Star system|system]] when the ''Jewel'' arrived, prompting its swift departure for the celestial body [[Nanth'ri]].

==Description==
{{Quote|We would have to keep going toward the planet, let the Imperials spot us, and then see if they chase us.|Skywalker, to Nakari Kelen|<ref name="Heir" />}}
Exodeen was a [[planet]]<ref name="Heir" /> in the [[Colonies]]' [[Exodeen system]], situated at the coordinates M-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was along the [[Hydian Way]]<ref name="Map" /> [[super-hyperroute]],<ref name="BMF 48">{{BuildFalconCite|48|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> between the celestial bodies [[Bellassa]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> and [[Suntilla]],<ref name="Map" /> and was the eastern endpoint of the [[Nanth'ri Trade Route]]<ref name="Heir" /> which connected it to the astronomical object [[Caragon-Viner]].<ref name="AVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|They're going to know we're here soon because we can't conceal the reflected light of our entry, but if we leave now we can prevent them from getting a deep scan and confirming three life-forms aboard&mdash;assuming that her decryption is true and they're looking for us.|Kelen, to Skywalker|<ref name="Heir" />}}
In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Heir to the Jedi]]'' to [[0 ABY]].</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] operatives [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Nakari Kelen]] [[Mission on Denon|extracted]] the [[givin]] [[cryptologist]] [[Drusil Bephorin]] from the custody of the [[Galactic Empire]] on the<ref name="Heir" /> [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG" /> planet [[Denon]], planning on reuniting the givin with her family on the<ref name="Heir" /> [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Map" /> planet [[Omereth]]. To get to Omereth, Skywalker, flying the yacht ''[[Desert Jewel]]'', planned to take the Hydian Way from Denon to Exodeen, and then continue on the Nanth'ri Trade Route to the<ref name="Heir" /> celestial body<ref name="Map" /> [[Nanth'ri]].<ref name="Heir" />

However, several Imperial [[starship]]s, including a [[Star Destroyer]] and [[heavy cruiser]], were present in Exodeen's [[Star system|system]] when Skywalker's ship arrived, with word spreading over the [[HoloNet]] communications system about the fugitives. Skywalker quickly had his [[droid]] [[R2-D2]] set a course for Nanth'ri and the ''Jewel'' fled the Exodeen system.<ref name="Heir" />

==Behind the scenes==
Exodeen first appeared in [[Kevin Hearne]]'s [[2014]] novel ''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''.<ref name="Heir" />
