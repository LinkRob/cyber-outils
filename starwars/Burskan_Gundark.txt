"'''Burskan Gundark'''" was an article in the [[Creature Feature]] series that was written by [[Jeff Quick]] and added to [[Wizards.com]] on [[August 7]], [[2003]]. It gave information on [[Burskan gundark]]s, a variant of the [[Gundark/Legends|gundark]] [[Species/Legends|species]] from [[Burska]].<ref name="BG" />
