An '''exosuit''', also known as an '''exoframe''', was a mechanical body that could be worn by individuals with serious injuries, providing the ability to move [[limb]]s and walk. After becoming injured when crashing out of the [[Forty-eighth annual Dragonfire Air Rally|forty-eighth]] annual [[Dragonfire Air Rally]], [[Serennian]] heir [[Ramil]] was forced to wear an exosuit, and continued to wear the exosuit after becoming the [[Count]] of [[Serenno]]. When Ramil organized a [[mercenary]] [[Incident on Serenno|takeover]] of Serenno in order to turn its people away from the [[Galactic Republic]], he was confronted by his brother, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]] used [[Force lightning]] to disable Ramil's [[security droid]] and exosuit, preventing him from moving. With Ramil's mechanical joints frozen in place, Dooku summoned his [[Dooku's lightsaber|lightsaber]], [[Death|killing]] him.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
