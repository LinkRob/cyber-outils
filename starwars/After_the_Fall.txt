"'''After the Fall'''" is a short story written by [[Claudia Gray]] as part of the anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]''. The story was published on [[September 5]], [[2023]].

==Plot summary==
===A new day===
One day after the fall of [[Starlight Beacon]], [[Affie Hollow]] and her fellow survivors [[Leox Gyasi]] and [[Geode]] park their ship, the ''[[Vessel]]'', on a plateau overlooking the coastal [[Eiram]] city of [[Barraza City]].  Gyasi survived the destruction of the space station with a [[parachute]]. Starlight Beacon has fallen into the sea, triggering a tsunami that has damaged Barraza City. Hollow and her crew take the ''Vessel'' into the devastated city to help the local civilians and Starlight Beacon survivors send messages offworld. 

While Geode assists with sending messages offworld, Hollow and Gyasi help [[Marshal]] [[Avar Kriss]] distribute emergency ration kits to refugees and survivors. Hollow is relieved to find her friends [[Pikka Adren]] and [[Joss Adren]] among the survivors. Joss tells Hollow that their ship survived the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|fall]] of Starlight Beacon. While walking through the ruined waterfront, Hollow and Gyasi have a heartfelt reunion with the [[Soikan]] woman [[Velko Jahen]], who has been rescued by a shore patrol craft. 

===A week later===
A week following the fall of Starlight Beacon, Hollow and her crew wait for their ship to receive clearance to travel offworld. While Geode helps with communications, Gyasi joins a repair crew restoring Eiram's patrol craft. An organizer droid [[JJ-5145]] issues assignments, travel information and travel lottery results to spacers. Pikka is wary about divulging tracking information to the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] authorities but Hollow is used to being tracked due to her days with the [[Byne Guild]].

Hollow is confronted by the angry [[Kaleesh]] [[captain]] [[Dumas Mar'Ti]], a former member of the Byne Guild, who resents Hollow for betraying her adoptive mother [[Scover Byne]]. Mar'Ti says that Byne's incarceration has disrupted the guild's operations. Pikka speaks up for Hollow by pointing out that Scover ruined the guild through her own greed. As a gesture of reconciliation, Pikka offers to repair the damaged navigation system of Mar'Ti's ship ''[[Athos]]'', which she accepts. Returning to the ''Vessel'', she confides with Geode about missing her three parents but finding a family in Geode and Gyasi. She also resolves to help other members of the scattered Byne Guild. 

===A month later===
A month later, Hollow and her fellow crew wait for Eiram's security blockade to end. Hollow and Gyasi have learnt about the [[Nihil]]'s [[Stormwall]], a new systems-wide no-fly zone separating Nihil space from the rest of the [[galaxy]]. After receiving clearance from Eiram authorities, they take the ''Vessel'' into [[hyperspace]]. They decide to travel to [[Kennerla]] to patch relations with Captain Mar'Ti and revive the Byne Guild. While Gyasi suggests talking with the other captains first, Hollow decides that winning over Mar'Ti is key to winning over the other guild captains. 

On the planet Kennerla, Hollow and Gyasi visit a damp [[cantina]] known to be a haunt for the Kaleesh captain. While Mar'Ti is still bitter towards Hollow for betraying their leader and her adoptive mother, Hollow is able to convince her to revive the Byne Guild in order to fight the Nihil and continue their shipping operations. She says that the Byne Guild's existence does not need to depend on Scover. Mar'Ti is open to Hollow's ideas and invites her and Gyasi to discuss reviving the guild while having drinks.
