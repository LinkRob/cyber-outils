'''Alderaanian deer''' were a type of [[deer]] [[Homeworld|native]] to<ref name="Star Wars 24" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Star Wars 24" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-marvel|text=SWCE 2023: Marvel Reveals ''Star Wars: Dark Droids'' Horror Event and More}} states that the events of [[Star Wars (2020)|''Star Wars'']], including [[Star Wars (2020) 24|''Star Wars'' (2020) 24]], take place between the events of {{Film|V}} and {{Film|VI}}, which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> as [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[commander]] [[Ellian Zahra]] [[Battle of Panisia|confronted]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], a [[princess]] of Alderaan and [[general]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], in a [[cave]] on the planet [[Panisia]], she told Organa that she intended to [[Death|kill]]<ref name="Star Wars 24" /> her<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and then dress her corpse gutted and splayed like an Alderaanian deer. The commander also informed the [[Gender|princess]] that she had left the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] and [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Pathfinder]] [[Kes Dameron]] alive so that they could discover Organa's corpse and tell the Alliance that she had died like an animal. Zahra was ultimately unsuccessful in her efforts and was instead left for dead by Organa in the cave.<ref name="Star Wars 24" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alderaanian deer were mentioned in the [[Star Wars (2020) 24|twenty-fourth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (2020)|''Star Wars'']]. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 24" /> on [[June 8]], [[2022]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/95844/star_wars_2020_24|text=Star Wars (2020) #24}}</ref>
