'''Fishfruit''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|edible]] [[fruit]] that shared its name with [[fish]], a type of creature. The fruit was available for purchase in the [[Market District (Mirogana)|Market District]] located in the [[city]] of [[Mirogana]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an announcer speaking [[Huttese]] advertised fishfruit alongside fresh [[Gorra berry|gorra berries]] and [[dundle]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fishfruit was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
