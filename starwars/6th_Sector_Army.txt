The '''6th Sector Army''' was one of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s twenty [[Sector Army|Sector Armies]] that was active during the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[army]] served as a reserve command along with the preceding Sector Armies down to the [[1st Sector Army]]. At the start of the war, the Grand Army was organized into ten [[Systems Army|Systems Armies]], with each being split into two Sector Armies and drawn from a specific area of [[the galaxy]]. The Sector Armies were responsible for the defense of their specific areas. Due to the Grand Army's organization, the 6th Sector Army belonged to the same Systems Army as another Sector Army.<ref name="BMF 18" />

Similar to other [[Galactic Republic]] Sector Armies, the 6th Sector Army had one of twenty [[Haven-class medical station|''Haven''-class medical stations]] towed to them.<ref name="Note" /> Being a Sector Army, the unit contained 147,456 [[clone trooper]]s who were divided into four [[corps]].<ref name="CL 2016" /> Like all Sector Armies, it was attached to one of twenty numbered [[fleet]]s of the [[Republic Navy]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The 6th Sector Army was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 18|eighteenth issue]] of the [[De Agostini]] magazine ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'', published in [[2015]].<ref name="BMF 18" /> It first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1996]] novel by [[Michael P. Kube-McDowell]], ''[[Before the Storm]]'', as the [[Sixth Negative Regions|Black Sword Command]].<ref name="Before the Storm">''[[Before the Storm]]''</ref> The Black Sword Command was further clarified to be the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic's]] Sixth Army by the [[2012]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'' by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Paul R. Urquhart]].<ref name="EGW">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
