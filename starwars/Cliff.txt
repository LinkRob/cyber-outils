'''Cliff''' was a location on the [[planet]] [[Polyneus]] where [[New Republic pilot]] [[Wyl Lark]] was [[Reproduction|born]]. Over a [[Standard year|year]] after returning to his [[homeworld]] following the [[Battle of Jakku]], Lark visited Cliff to speak with a [[Sun-Lamas|Sun-Lama]] about treating a [[sur-avka]]'s mangled [[claw]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
