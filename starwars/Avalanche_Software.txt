'''Avalanche Software''' is a company based in Salt Lake City, Utah and owned by Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment. They were founded in [[1995]], and were later acquired in [[2005]] by Buena Vista Games, which was later renamed [[Disney Interactive|Disney Interactive Studios]]. They were the main developer on ''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]'',<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2015/05/05/june-cover-revealed-disney-infinity-3-0.aspx|text=June Cover Revealed – Disney Infinity 3.0|author=Cork, Jeff|date=2015-05-05|archivedate=20180621213608}}</ref> which was the only ''[[Star Wars]]'' project produced by them until their acquisition by Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment in [[2017]].
