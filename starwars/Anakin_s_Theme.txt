"'''Anakin's Theme'''" is a recurring musical theme introduced in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. It was composed by [[John Williams]] as the theme for the young [[Anakin Skywalker]].

Heard throughout ''The Phantom Menace'', "Anakin's Theme" is played when Skywalker first meets [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] in [[Wald's Parts|Watto's junk shop]] on [[Tatooine]]. It is also played as part of the suite accompanying the film's end credits. In [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], the theme appears when Skywalker and Amidala reunite on [[Coruscant]], as well as the film's end credits. "Anakin's Theme" also plays as part of the [[Across the Stars]] theme in the track "Confrontation with Count Dooku and Finale," at 9:39. For [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], the theme plays right after Anakin Skywalker wakes up from his [[Force vision]], where he [[Sleep|dreams]] of Amidala's [[death]] at [[Reproduction|childbirth]].

The theme contains a subtle rendition of "[[The Imperial March]]"&mdash;used as Anakin Skywalker's theme after he becomes Darth Vader&mdash;foreshadowing the boy's eventual fall.

In [[Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace (soundtrack)|''The Phantom Menace Ultimate Edition soundtrack'']], the theme appears at the end of the track "Hail to the Winner, Anakin Skywalker".
