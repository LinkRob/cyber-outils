'''Ashes''' was a [[rib-cat]] owned by the con artist [[Loonoo]]. One of several rib-cats belonging to Loonoo, Ashes resided in his apartment in the [[Corellian Sector (Nar Shaddaa)|Corellian Sector]] on [[Nar Shaddaa]] and subsisted on [[rat]]s while their owner was away on long cons.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
