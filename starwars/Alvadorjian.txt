'''Alvadorjians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="MOF" /> [[Homeworld|native]]<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|species=Alvadorjian|system=[[Alvadorjia system]]}}</ref> to the [[Alvadorjia system]].<ref name="Appendix" /> They were known to the [[Galactic Republic]] as "'''Xivalians'''," but [[Xivalia]] was not their home.<ref name="MOF" />

Some of their physical characteristics included scalp plating that weathered over time and colorful eyespots on the neck and throat that spread with age. They also possessed a biological mutability that made cross-clan breeding difficult, and over time this trait had reduced the species population from 49 clans to seven.<ref name="MOF" />

After what happened to the [[Yehoulta clan]], popular support among youths and academics on [[Coruscant]] and [[Brentaal IV]] coalesced into an activism campaign that propelled an [[Alvadorjian Senate bill]] forward. The piece of legislation discussed funding the genetic repair of Alvadorjians and setting aside land for the clans on newly colonized worlds. [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] co-sponsored the bill, but due to the [[Selonian worker scandal]] and the [[Invasion of Naboo]], public interest in the matter waned, and the bill was eventually tabled before reaching the [[Galactic Senate]] floor.<ref name="MOF" />

The unsuccessful bill, compounded by centuries of unkept Republic promises to aid the Alvadorjians, prompted five of the seven remaining clans to join the cause of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] when offered a genetic cure by [[Tychon Nulvolio]] that reportedly could take effect in five generations.<ref name="MOF" />
