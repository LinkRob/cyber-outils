A group of '''insurgents''' backed by the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate [[Insurrection on Bestine IV|fought]] against the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Bestine IV]]. During their insurrection, the insurgents reportedly hid within a [[Unidentified village (Bestine IV)|village]] on [[Jahnae]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 29" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Bounty Hunters 29|''Bounty Hunters'' 29]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> an Imperial [[transport]] that contained munitions was headed for a [[Imperial naval base]] on Jahnae to be used and crush the insurrection for good. Crimson Dawn hired the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga's crew]] and her [[T'onga's crew|crew]] to intercept the shipment. When they arrived on Bestine, the crew were met by an insurgent, who was underwhelmed by the amount of help that Crimson Dawn had sent them.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 29" />
