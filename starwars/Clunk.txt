'''Clunk''' was a [[Haxion Brood Bounty Droid]] that served the [[Haxion Brood]] crime syndicate during the [[Imperial Era]]. Once a [[Loadlifter droid (model)|loadlifter droid]], the Haxion Brood reprogrammed the [[droid]], who was enhanced with salvaged parts. They had [[Color|yellow]], silver, and red plating, red [[sensor]]s, and a built-in [[wrist blaster]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFO Date" /> Clunk came into contact with and fought the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]]. However, the droid failed to defeat him and was [[Death|destroyed]] by the Jedi.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Clunk appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]]. In the game, after the player attempts to return to their [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'', after [[Second mission to Zeffo|visiting]] the [[Tomb of Miktrull]] on the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]], the player may be attacked and captured by any of the Haxion Brood [[bounty hunter]]s, including Clunk. When Kestis wakes up in a [[Prison|cell]] on the Haxion Brood homebase of [[Ordo Eris]], if the hunter to capture him was Clunk, he will fight and destroy the droid within the [[Haxion Fight Pit]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

However, if Clunk is not the [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] to capture Kestis, the player may encounter the droid in a variety of locations after the Ordo Eris chapter of the story. The player may choose to destroy the droid and scan it for the "Haxion Brood Bounty Droid" tactical guide entry if not already previously obtained.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> In the [[2026]] [[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, Vol. 2|manga adaptation]], the hunter who captures Cal Kestis is [[Atticus Rex]].<ref name="Manga">[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, Vol. 2|''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'', Vol. 2]]</ref>
