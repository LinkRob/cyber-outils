[[Garazeb Orrelios]] once stated that,<ref name="ROR">''[[Rise of the Rebels]]''</ref> by 3272 [[Lothal Calendar|LY]],<ref name="VG">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> his [[Garazeb Orrelios' grandmother|grandmother]] had been living on her [[homeworld]] for three hundred dust seasons.<ref name="ROR" />
