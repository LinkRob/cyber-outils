'''Fralideja''' was the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]].<ref name="AEYNTK" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fralideja was created for the [[2005]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> A mission set on Mustafar in the [[2022]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'' is titled "Fralideja-vu."<ref name="TSS">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''</ref>
