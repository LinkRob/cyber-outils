At some point before the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]] launched an '''assault''' on the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Strike Station Aurek]]. [[Lieutenant]] [[Colvett Valeria|Valeria]] devised a plan to destroy the battle station by dropping a [[proton bomb]] into the base's [[thermal exhaust port]]. Despite some unexpected delays, the rebels managed to destroy Strike Station Aurek with the help of [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] and the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]].<ref name="RSA" />

==Prelude==
After the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], they found work as mechanics servicing the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet's]] [[starfighter]]s.<ref name="Home One">{{FreemakerAdventures|Home One}}</ref> Later, [[Lieutenant]] [[Colvett Valeria|Valeria]] devised an operation to destroy the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] base [[Strike Station Aurek]]. Her plan involved dropping a [[proton bomb]] into the base's [[thermal exhaust port]].<ref name="RSA" /> This tactic had first been used by [[Luke Skywalker]] during the [[Battle of Yavin]] to destroy the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

The rebels experienced an unexpected delay when the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]], who had been temporarily blinded by burning [[sweet-sand cookie]]s, bumped into a stack of containers that Rowan had been standing on. The containers collapsed in a domino wave over [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron's]] starfighters, damaging them. While Rowan's older siblings [[Zander Freemaker|Zander]] and [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] distracted Lieutenant Valeria and her squadron, Rowan and Roger rebuilt the starfighters with the former using [[the Force]].<ref name="RSA" />

Despite repairing the starfighters, Roger built himself into the [[astromech socket]] of Valeria's [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing]]. Rowan was unable to free him and the two became reluctant passengers as Valeria took the Y-wing into space. Valeria's co-pilot and [[astromech droid]] were left behind.<ref name="RSA" />

==The battle==
Upon arriving at Strike Station Aurek, Blue Squadron had to dodge both [[turbolaser]]s and [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. During the battle, Roger panicked and Rowan feigned enthusiasm in order to avoid arousing Valeria's suspicions. Valeria's Y-wing was pursued by two TIEs but Rowan managed to use the Y-wing's rear cannons to shoot them down. One collided with a pinnacle. As the Y-wing approached the thermal exhaust port, Roger fretted that he had not fixed the launch tube. Rowan instead used the Force to detach the bomb, which rolled into the exhaust port. Strike Station Aurek blew up, destroying the Imperial forces. Rowan likened the outcome to the destruction of the Death Star.<ref name="RSA" />

==Aftermath==
After returning to ''Home One'', Rowan used [[Rowan Freemaker's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] to cut himself and Roger free of the Y-wing. Zander and Kordi praised Rowan with Kordi telling her brother that the Force was with him. Valeria meanwhile thanked her bemused co-pilot and astromech droid, not realizing they had been left behind.<ref name="RSA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Assault on Strike Station Aurek serves as the main conflict of the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season Two|Season Two]] animated short "[[Rowan's Secret Adventure]]," which was released on the [[LEGO Star Wars]] website on [[May 4]], [[2017]].
