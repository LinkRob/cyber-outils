'''Carrion serpents''' were a type of [[Snake|serpent]] that lived on the [[Galactic Frontier]] [[planet]] [[Tiikae]] and [[Diet|fed]] on [[Death|carcasses]].<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> after [[Attack on Tiikae|encountering]] [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s on Tiikae, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] asked [[Jordanna Sparkburn]]&mdash;the [[San Tekka clan]] [[deputy]] on the world&mdash;what they would do with the Nihil's dead bodies. In response, Sparkburn told her to leave them for the carrion serpents, before they left for Sparkburn's [[Unidentified settlement (Tiikae)|settlement]].<ref name="Out of the Shadows" />

==Behind the scenes==
Carrion serpents were mentioned in the [[2021]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
