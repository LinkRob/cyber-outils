The '''Farakai''' were a large [[Humanoid|anthropoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. They possessing a unique bonding ability in which two individuals would be joined with a bridge of [[bone]] and muscle. The Farakai were brutal and powerful and a notoriously secretive species that had clandestine rituals of war and [[Reproduction|mating]]. The Farakai [[Chu]] bonded with the [[Rodian]] [[Lok (Rodian)|Lok]] to become [[Chulok]], who would become a [[gangster]] operating on the [[planet]] [[Metagos]].<ref name="The Glass Abyss" />
