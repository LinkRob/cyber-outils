The '''father''' of the [[Gozzo]] [[Flix]] was a [[Gender|male]] individual. Upon returning to the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]'' after a visit to<ref name="Dangerous Business" /> his<ref name="Bucket's List">{{BucketsList|E_DxK2LQKFM|#1.14: "Dangerous Business"|sw_url=video/buckets-list-dangerous-business-star-wars-resistance}}</ref> [[Flix's mother|mother]]<ref name="Dangerous Business" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|1|13}}</ref> Flix claimed to his business partner, [[Orka]], that his [[Parent|father]] was part-[[Gungan]]. Orka rebuffed the claim due to Gungans lacking feathers, inquiring why he insisted on perpetuating untrue information about his [[Flix's flock|parent]].<ref name="Dangerous Business" />
