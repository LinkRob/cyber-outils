'''''Classic Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back''''' is a 24-page book and audio cassette tape adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], released in [[December]] [[1994]]. It uses the same audio and text from the original [[1980]] [[The Empire Strikes Back (book-and-record)|book-and-record adaptation]].<ref name="Amazon" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''The action-packed adventure continues as Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia, and Han Solo struggle against the galactic Empire with the help of Yoda, the Jedi Master. Join the struggle against the evil Darth Vader with this full-color storybook and your Snow Speeder Micro-Machine. It's just like those found in this thrilling Star Wars Adventure.''<ref name="Amazon" />
