'''Farstey''' was an unsettled [[planet]] in the [[Expansion Region]] that became home to a [[Farstey flight school|flight school]] for [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]]'s [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|organized rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. In a message to [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]] between [[4 BBY]] and [[3 BBY]], rebel [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] recommended having the [[flight school]] use [[RZ-1T trainer]]s. Around [[3 ABY]], the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] was suspicious that Farstey was the site of activity from the since-[[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|formalized]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].

==Description==
Farstay was a terrestrial<ref name="Rebel Files" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Scum & Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> located in an eponymous [[Farstey system|star system]] and [[Farstey sector|sector]] in the [[Expansion Region]]. It was situated at the coordinates O-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" />

==History==
{{Quote|Because of reprisals on populated worlds such as Garel and Ghorman, in which civilians took extensive collateral damage during raids, the rebels took to avoiding inhabited worlds, and instead favored unsettled planets.|Report by Andressa Divo|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
By the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Farstey was largely unsettled.<ref name="Scum & Villainy" /> However, it eventually became home to a [[Farstey flight school|flight school]] for an [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|organized rebellion]] against the Empire secretly led by [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Between [[4 BBY]] and [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">In ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', the report mentioning Farstey and its [[Farstey flight school|flight school]] is set between the [[blockade of Ibaar]] and the [[Mission to Ryloth (Phoenix Squadron)|mission to Ryloth]], which are respectively dated to [[4 BBY]] and [[3 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> rebel [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] wrote a message to [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]] detailing how helpful<ref name="Rebel Files" /> he<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> had found the [[RZ-1T trainer]] in certifying [[Pilot|bush pilots]] for [[A-wing starfighter]]s, stating that the [[flight school]]s at Farstey and the astronomical object [[Homon]] could use models of the former if available.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

[[File:Farstey-SV.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Farstey was mapped, albeit in the wrong region, in an Imperial Security Bureau report on suspected Rebel Alliance locations.]]
In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="2 BBY">In ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', the report regarding the rebel locations that included Farstey is set between the introduction of the [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]] and the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance]], which are both dated to [[2 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> a [[Star map|map]] of [[the galaxy]] was produced that marked the rebellion's [[safe world]]s, [[starfighter]] hubs at level five or higher, operations headquarters, [[shadow planet]]s, and deep [[Realspace|space]] caches. Farstey was noted as one of three starfighter hubs of level five or above, alongside Homon and<ref name="Rebel Files" /> the planet<ref name="HC79 Porkins">{{HelmetCollectionCite|79|Helmets}}</ref> [[Tierfon]]. The [[map]] and Sato's message to Dodonna were both eventually collected by [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]], who included them in the compilation of non-electronic documents known as ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> The rebellion was [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|formalized]] as the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] later in 2 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" />

Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' dates both the report on suspected [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] base locations and [[Han Solo]]'s [[Carbon-freezing|encasement]] in [[carbonite]] to the [[Standard year|year]] 7980 [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the latter in [[3 ABY]], the report must also be set around that year.</ref> the Empire grew suspicious of potential rebel activity on Farstey. It was among several unsettled planets identified in an [[Imperial Security Bureau]] (ISB) report that designated targets for [[Viper probe droid]] surveys by cross-referencing areas with a high degree of suspicious activity with the unsettled parts of the [[galaxy]] favored by the Rebel Alliance. The targets were marked on a galactic map in a summary report authored by ISB [[Agent]] [[Andressa Divo]], wherein Farstey was incorrectly placed in the [[Inner Rim Territories]].<ref name="Scum & Villainy" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TheRebelAllianceSourcebook.jpg|thumb|right|250px|In ''Star Wars'' Legends, Farstey originated in ''The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook''.]]
Farstey was introduced into the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via a mention in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', authored by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Originating in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Farstey/Legends|Farstey]] was first mentioned in "[[Extract of Minutes from the 251st Meeting of the Alliance High Command]]," a short companion narrative within the [[1990]] ''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]'', written by [[Paul Murphy]] and published as part of [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Extract">{{WEGCite|story=Extract of Minutes from the 251st Meeting of the Alliance High Command|book=The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook}}</ref>

[[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' designates Farstey as part of the Inner Rim Territories,<ref name="Scum & Villainy" /> while ''Star Wars: The Rebel Files'' places Farstey in the Expansion Region.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> The latter source is backed by the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" of the [[StarWars.com]] article "[[Star Wars Galaxy Map]]"&mdash;&mdash;both being authored by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[June 24]], [[2025]]<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>&mdash;which follows from the ''Star Wars'' Legends map placement of the [[Farstey system]], and therefore Farstey, through the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''{{'s}} [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] on [[StarWars.com]],<ref name="TEA Companion">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> this article treats the placement of Farstey in the ''Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious'' map as erroneous.
