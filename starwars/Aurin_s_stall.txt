[[Grandfer]] [[Aurin]] operated a '''stall'''<ref name="POV" /> selling used [[droid]] parts in the [[Ferdan market|market]] of the [[City|town]] [[Ferdan]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dalna]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], they befriended [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] member [[Marda Ro]], and often saved her a table in the market near them for her and her [[Littles]]&mdash;the Path's [[Youngling|children]]&mdash;to give gifts to passersby.<ref name="POD" />

In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' and ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Ro and her Littles spent [[time]] in the market with Aurin as they sold used droid parts, with the child [[Eff]] sitting on the grandfer's lap.<ref name="POD" /> However, when Ro visited the grandfer at their stall following the Path's involvement in the [[Battle of Jedha]]<ref name="POV" /> later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> they acted cold and wary toward her as they distractedly fiddled with a [[micro-gyro unit]].<ref name="POV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aurin's stall first appeared in the [[2022]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and [[Tessa Gratton]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="POD" /> Phase II.<ref name="Phase II">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2023}}</ref> It was first described as a stall in the [[2023]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="POV" /> and also released in ''Star Wars: The High Republic''{{'s}} [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase II" /> Within ''Path of Deceit'', the stall first debuted in a chapter written by Gratton.<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref>
