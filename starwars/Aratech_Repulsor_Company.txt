'''Aratech Repulsor Company''' was a manufacturer of [[repulsorlift]] vehicles, such as the [[BARC speeder]],<ref name="USW" /> the [[614-AvA speeder bike]],<ref name="VG" /> the [[712-AvA speeder bike]],<ref name="The Mandalorian Handbook" /> and the [[74-Z speeder bike]].<ref name="USW" /> Under the [[Galactic Empire]], it maintained manufacturing facilities in the [[Imperial Armory Complex]] on [[Lothal]].<ref name="Inside Man" />
