The '''''Athylia''''' was a [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigate]] in the service of the [[Jedi Order]]. [[Jedi Master]] [[Kelleran Beq]] often served aboard the [[Starship|vessel]], alongside the [[protocol droid]] [[AD-3]] and the [[astromech droid]] [[LX-R5]].<ref name="JTC-E1" />

==Description==
The ''Athylia'' was a [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigate]] that served as a [[Jedi]] training facility. It had a cockpit that could seat two crew and was equipped with a [[hyperdrive]] and [[navigation computer]].<ref name="JTC-E1" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I think one of my favorite projects that we got a chance to work on&mdash;we designed the spaceship ''Thilia'' ''[sic]'' for 'Jedi Temple Challenge.' I think me and the crew had the most fun doing that one, because it was a chance to do something that hadn't quite been seen before.|Ryan Brett Puckett|<ref name="SWShow">{{StarWarsShow|fKdMtEiHGnM|Ahmed Best and Mary Holland Talk Jedi Temple Challenge, And We Celebrate The Empire Strikes Back!|sw_url=video/ahmed-best-and-mary-holland-talk-jedi-temple-challenge-and-we-celebrate-the-empire-strikes-back}}</ref>}}
[[File:Thilia_reveal.jpg|thumb|right|230px|The ''Athylia'']]
The ''Athylia'' was created for ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Temple Challenge]]'', a game show released on [[StarWarsKids.com]]. The interior of the starship was created by production designer [[Ryan Brett Puckett]]. When the name of the ship was first revealed, it was identified as "''Thilia''."<ref name="SWShow" />

The ship's [[hyperspace]] levers were the same ones used in the touring ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' Experience that accompanied the release of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' in May 2018. According to sound editor [[David W. Collins]], the ''Athylia''{{'s}} sound was a combination of the [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class space cruiser]] ''[[Radiant VII]]'' and [[Yoda]]'s [[E3-standard starship lifeboat|escape pod]]. The ship also incorporated several unused wall panels and props from the theme park ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|Galaxy's Edge]]''.<ref name="20 Easter Eggs">{{SW|url=news/20-jedi-temple-challenge-easter-eggs|text=20 ''Star Wars: Jedi Temple Challenge'' Easter Eggs You Probably Didn't Notice}}</ref>

Contestants who had complete the show's "Strength Trials" would participate in a Knowledge Trial aboard the ''Athylia''. After listening to a story told by [[AD-3]], they would have to split into separate pilot and engineer roles where they had to answer questions from [[Jedi Master]] [[Kelleran Beq]]. The team that was the first to answer all five questions correctly would be admitted to the "Temple Trials."<ref name="JTC-E1" /><ref name="JTC-E10">{{JTC|10}}</ref> The stories the contestants had to listen to were written by Star Wars author [[Cavan Scott]].<ref name="20 Easter Eggs" />
