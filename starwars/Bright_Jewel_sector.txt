The '''Bright Jewel sector''' was a [[sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. It contained the [[Bright Jewel system]],<ref name="USW" /> the [[Aleen system]],<ref name="NoP" /> and the [[Numidian system]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The planet [[Ord Mantell]] in the Bright Jewel [[Star system|system]] served as the sector [[capital]]. The Bright Jewel sector was the capital of the [[Bright Jewel Oversector]], which also contained the [[Yavin system]].<ref name="BMF 48" />
