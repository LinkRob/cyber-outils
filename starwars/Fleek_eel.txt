'''Fleek eels''' were a [[species]] of [[eel]] with [[Color|gray]] skin, a defined dorsal ridge and a pink, fleshy opening to their [[Mouth|buccal tunnel]]. Most species considered it a delicacy.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" /> They could be found in the [[ocean]]s of the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] and were caught by the fishermen of [[Coronet City]], where they were a staple of the [[city]]'s fishmarkets.<ref name="SoloOG" /> They were also fished in the [[Hocekureem Sea]] on [[Falleen]].<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
A fleek eel first appeared in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> and was identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], Fleek eels first appeared in the [[1996]] [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Shadows of the Empire'' novelization]] written by [[Steve Perry]].<ref name="SotE">[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'' novel]]</ref>
