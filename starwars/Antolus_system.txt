The '''Antolus system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Sarka sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square Q-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Antolus system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Antolus system/Legends|Antolus system]] first appeared in "[[Deadly Allies]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic story]] in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic 6.51|fifty-first issue of the sixth volume]] of the [[Star Wars Comic|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Comic]] magazine, which [[Titan Magazines]] published<ref name="Deadly">{{TCWComicCite|6.51|Deadly Allies}}</ref> around [[September 19]], [[2013]].<ref name="Date">[[Star Wars Comic|''Star Wars'' Comic]] was published every four weeks, and the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic 6.21|twenty-first issue of the sixth volume]] was published on [[June 2]], [[2011]] according to {{SWArchive|url=vault/books/whatsnew_may27/index.html|text=What's New This Week: ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic'' #6.21}}. Therefore, the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic 6.51|fifty-first issue of the sixth volume]] must have been published on approximately [[September 19]], [[2013]].</ref> The story was written by [[Mike W. Barr]] and illustrated by [[Andres Ponce]].<ref name="Deadly" /> It was later identified by name in the StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Fry.<ref name="Atlas">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|oldversion=20150701052110}}</ref>
