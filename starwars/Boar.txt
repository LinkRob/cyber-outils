A '''boar''' was a type of creature. The [[Empire (Rei'izu)|Empire]] used [[walker]]s fashioned in the shapes of various creatures, including boars. One type of boar, the [[scaled boar]], was found on the [[planet]] [[Genbara]].<ref name="Ronin">''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]''</ref>
