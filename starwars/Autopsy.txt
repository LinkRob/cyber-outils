An '''autopsy''' was a procedure performed on the [[Death|dead]] to determine the cause of death. In [[232 BBY]], the [[Jedi Order|Jedi]] discovered the wreckage of a [[Hutt Clan|Hutt]] [[starship]] [[Attack in the Kazlin system|attacked]] by the [[Nihil]] in the [[Kazlin system]]. A group of [[Jedi]] were dispatched to investigate, where they discovered the corpse of a [[Unidentified Hutt trader|Hutt]]. After a brief examination the body was brought to the [[Starlight Beacon]] for an autopsy.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref><ref name="THR 2021 2">[[The High Republic (2021) 2|''The High Republic'' (2021) 2]]</ref> After a [[Unidentified settlement (Banchii)|settlement]] on the [[planet]] [[Banchii]] was [[Defense of Banchii|attacked]] by the [[Drengir]], several civilians were turned to [[wood]]. [[Doctor]] [[Silar]] performed an autopsy on the victims and found unusual spores, but was unable to determine if they were for [[Diet|consumption]] or [[reproduction]].<ref name="Balance1" />
