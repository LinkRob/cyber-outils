A '''Dry Cleaner Droid''' was a type of [[cleaning droid]] that could be employed in dry cleaning. By the time of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], it could be found for sale in at least one of the commercial establishments aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|first Death Star]].<ref name="tiny">''[[Star Wars: Tiny Death Star]]''</ref>
