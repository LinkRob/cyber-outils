'''Denta bean buns''' were a [[bread]] [[Foodstuff|dessert]] found on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. The dessert was edible by [[human]]s and featured [[denta bean]]s as a primary ingredient.<ref name="Queen's Peril" />

In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the kitchen staff of the [[Theed Royal Palace]] of Naboo baked a batch of denta bean buns for a [[Chommell sector summit|summit]] hosted by [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]] [[officer]]s [[Quarsh Panaka|Quarsh]] and [[Mariek Panaka]] were about to eat some of the buns when a [[blood-sensitive sensor]] triggered an [[Blood alarm|alarm]] in Amidala's private quarters. After the two discovered that the Queen and most of her [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaidens]] had secretly gone out, Quarsh expressed frustration. Mariek told the remaining handmaiden present, [[Saché]], that Quarsh was merely upset at being called away from eating a fresh denta bean bun. Saché laughed at the idea that the officer would choose dessert over duty.<ref name="Queen's Peril" />

Denta bean buns first appeared in ''[[Queen's Peril]]'', a [[2020]] young-adult novel by [[E. K. Johnston]] as the second installment of ''[[Star Wars: Queen's Series]]''.<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> Johnston noted that in the the first draft of ''Queen's Peril'', Quarsh Panaka ate [[Wikipedia:Adzuki bean|red bean]] buns. The [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] asked if Johnston could make the meal "more ''Star Wars''-y," leading her to use the free-to-edit, unofficial online ''[[Star Wars]]'' encyclopedia known as [[Wookieepedia]] to search for beans unique to ''Star Wars'', eventually finding denta beans.<ref name="Johnstonblog" />
