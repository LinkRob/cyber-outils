'''Drake Lo'gaan''' was an individual whose name was carved on the wall of the facility used by the [[Hidden Path]] on the [[planet]] [[Jabiim]]. The Path was a network that smuggled [[Force-sensitive]]s and [[Jedi]] away from the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Part V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref> in [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Timeline">{{SW|url=news/future-lucasfilm-projects-revealed|text=Future Lucasfilm Projects Revealed}} states that ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'' is set ten [[Standard year|years]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'' must be set in [[9 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] observed Lo'gaan's name among other inscriptions.<ref name="Part V" />
