'''Flame Towers''' were a type of anti-personnel [[turret]] that shot fire at attacking enemy ground forces, such as [[infantry]] or [[tank]]s. While Flame Towers were effective at crowd control against large numbers of relatively weak ground units such as [[B1-series battle droid]]s and [[porg]]s, they could not attack aerial units such as [[Pistoeka sabotage droid|buzz droids]] or [[mynock]]s.<ref name="LSWB">''[[LEGO Star Wars Battles]]''</ref>
