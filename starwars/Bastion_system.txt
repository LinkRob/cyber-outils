The '''Bastion system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Braxant sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> and the [[New Territories]] in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="New Territories" /> The [[Star system|system]] was situated in grid square K-3 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> It contained the industrialized [[planet]] [[Bastion]].<ref name="Battles" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Bastion system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] article on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], it was identified as the [[Bastion system/Legends|Sartinaynian system]] and first appeared in ''[[Specter of the Past]]'', the first book in ''[[Star Wars: The Hand of Thrawn Duology]]'' by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Specter">''[[Specter of the Past]]''</ref>
