{{Quote|Chewie tells me a bunch of Wookiee warriors were passing it around, and he accepted it from Big Quince's bodyguard during that weapons trade on Orelon.|Han Solo, in the ''Smuggler's Guide''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>}}
'''Big Quince''' was an individual who worked with the [[Wookiee]] [[smuggler]] [[Chewbacca]] in a weapons trade on [[Orelon]]. During the weapons trade, he acquired the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]]'s ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' from Big Quince's Wookiee [[Big Quince's bodyguard|bodyguard]] and kept the [[book]] without mentioning it to his parter, [[Han Solo]], for several [[Standard month|months]]. After learning the story, Solo recorded the information of how Chewbacca acquired it in the ''Smuggler's Guide''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Big Quince was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
