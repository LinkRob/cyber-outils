The '''Beskar Buckethead''' was a set of armor worn by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Loyalist Trooper|standard costume]]. According to Sentinel, this [[snowtrooper armor]] was made of [[beskar]],<ref name="Hunters" /> a practice performed by some members of the [[Galactic Empire]] following the [[Great Purge of Mandalore]].<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Beskar Buckethead was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Sentinel, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.20.0 update during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Beskar Destroyer]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching the final rank, Kyber I, within ''Hunter's'' "Ranked" mode during both the early access season it released with, as well as Season 3 following the game's global launch.<ref name="Hunters" />
