"'''Divide and Conquer'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]] title "'''Teile und herrsche'''") is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Alec Worley]], drawn by [[Ruairí Coleman]] and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. The story was published in the 2nd issue of the [[Wikipedia:Germany|German]] ''[[Star Wars Rebels Animation]]'', which was released on [[April 11]], [[2018]]. It was first published in English in the October 2019 issue of the Australian magazine ''K-Zone''.

==Plot summary==
{{Plot|comic}}

==Continuity==
In the final panel of the comic, [[Phoenix Cell]] is represented with [[CR90 corvette]]s, [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvettes]], and a [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigate]]. However, since the comic takes place after the episode "[[The Protector of Concord Dawn]]," the cell's ''Pelta''-class frigate was destroyed in ''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''.
