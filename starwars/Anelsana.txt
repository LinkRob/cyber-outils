'''Anelsana''' was a terrestrial [[Planet|world]]<ref name="LPOA" /> located in the [[Anelsana system]] of the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Farstey sector]], situated in grid square O-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It had several [[City|cities]] on its surface. At some point during the [[Imperial Era]], the world suffered due to a trade embargo enforced by the [[Galactic Empire]] while under its control.<ref name="LPOA" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> [[Viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] gave his [[Gender|daughter]], the [[Alderaanian]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], a list of locations in need of humanitarian aid, which included Anelsana.<ref name="LPOA" />

The princess reflected on the past effects of the embargo affecting Anelsana and surmised that lingering proof of the trade sanctions was most likely to be found within the world's cities. Leia opted to refrain from traveling to Anelsana, citing her [[Parent|father's]] insistence that she only journey to one of the world's rural [[Anelsana continent|continents]] located north of the main cities, and eventually rendered assistance to the world [[Chasmeene]].<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anelsana was mentioned in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="LPOA" />
