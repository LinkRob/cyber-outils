'''Cracek''' was a [[language]] spoken by the [[Esral'sa'Nikto]], a [[Nikto]] [[subspecies]].<ref name="Oplock" /> The traditional Cracek greeting of friendship was a noise similar to a shriek accompanied by the speaker's head fins flaring outward.<ref name="The Platform Classic">{{Resistance|The Platform Classic}}</ref> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|1|9}}</ref> the Esral'sa'Nikto [[Oplock]]<ref name="The Platform Classic" /> cheerfully<ref name="Oplock" /> greeted three [[Technician|mechanics]]&mdash;the [[Kadas'sa'Nikto]] [[Neeku Vozo]] and the [[human]]s [[Kazuda Xiono]] and [[Tamara Ryvora]]&mdash;while aboard the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]''. Though Xiono and Ryvora were frightened by the Cracek greeting, Vozo clarified its meaning to them.<ref name="The Platform Classic" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Jonathan Lipow plays Oplock. He makes the most inhuman voices ever. He's the perfect person to play all these like alien sounds. He's really phenomenal.|Christopher Sean|audio=Cracek-ThePlatformClassicCommentary.flac|<ref name="Commentary">{{HomeVideoCite|set=Star Wars Resistance: Complete Season One|episode=The Platform Classic}}</ref>}}
Cracek first appeared in "[[The Platform Classic]],"<ref name="The Platform Classic" /> the ninth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[November 25]], [[2018]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> In the episode, Oplock was portrayed by [[Jonathan Lipow]].<ref name="The Platform Classic" /> Oplock's facial fins were designed to help him express his emotions while speaking Cracek by flaring outward.<ref name="Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-platform-classic-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 11 Fun Facts from "The Platform Classic" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref> In the audio commentary for the episode, the voice actor of Kazuda Xiono&mdash;[[Christopher Sean]]&mdash;praised Lipow's performance of the language's sounds. Meanwhile, [[Myrna Velasco]]&mdash;the voice actor of [[Ace Squadron]] pilot [[Torra Doza]]&mdash;appreciated the humor in Oplock's Cracek outburst, finding the moment "perfect timing."<ref name="Commentary" />
