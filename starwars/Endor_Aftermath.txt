'''Endor Aftermath''' was a battlefield within the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The battlefield was based the forest [[moon]] [[Endor]] after its [[Battle of Endor|battle]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Reveal" />

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Aftermath first appeared in the [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', which was developed by [[NaturalMotion]]'s [[BossAlien]] and published by [[Zynga]] globally on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Global">{{SWYouTube|4dZcq6m3y40|Star Wars: Hunters Launch Date Reveal Trailer}}</ref> However, the map made its in-game debut through the start of the game's fourth season,<ref name="Reveal" /> released on [[November 26]], 2024.<ref name="Debut">{{ZyngaYouTube|_y5CSNe-ALg|Star Wars: Hunters &#124; New Hunter &#124; Nox Teaser}}</ref>
