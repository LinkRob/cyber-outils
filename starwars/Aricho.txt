'''Aricho''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] behind the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Stormwall]] in the [[Occlusion Zone]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. After the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]]<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]] hid on the planet.<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFS}}</ref> after the aspiring inventor [[Avon Sunvale]] learned that Nihil forces led by<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> the [[Quarren]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> [[Kara Xoo]] intended to set up operations on the planet because of a [[Glaka root|valuable herb]] on Aricho and fearing that Nihil [[General]] [[Abediah Viess]] would also make her way to the world with [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures which [[Diet|fed]] on [[Force-sensitive]]s, Sunvale set out to rescue her friend.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

Later, the [[Hutt]] [[Dahara Devirsivik Norri|Dahara]] conspired with [[President]] [[Orenna Sala]] to set up [[glaka root]] production facilities on Aricho before it was discovered that the planet and its glaka root fields had been afflicted with the [[blight]] and the planet was evacuated.<ref name="A Valiant Vow" />
