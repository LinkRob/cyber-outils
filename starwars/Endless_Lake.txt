The '''Endless Lake''' was a large lake on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. It was once a major settlement named [[Endless Lake Village]] until it was attacked and sunk by [[Orcon]], king of the Underwater Kingdom. <ref name="Ewoks 3" />

After the [[Ewok]] [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]] encountered the lake with a group of [[bounty hunter]]s and [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] he was travelling with, the young Ewok suggested finding a [[Raichling]] to help them cross it, but the Imperials instead forced the group to cross the lake in a makeshift vessel. The group was soon attacked by a group of [[devil serpent]]s led by Orcon and his [[Queen Serpent]], but all managed to survive and reach the other side of the lake.<ref name="Ewoks 3" />
