"'''Coded Transmission: Jan Ors to Mon Mothma'''" is an [[in-universe]] transmission from [[Jan Ors]] to [[Mon Mothma/Legends|Mon Mothma]] released as part of ''[[Dark Forces Manual: Coded Transmissions]]''<ref name="Coded" /> on [[February 15]], [[1995]].<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for ''[[Star Wars: Dark Forces]]''</ref>

==Contents==
<!-- This summary will focus on the details contradicted by the more famous novels -->
[[Kyle Katarn]] is presented as an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] defector who once commanded operations, during which people were killed. He later became an occasional non-aligned freelance [[agent/Legends|agent]] and [[mercenary/Legends|mercenary]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]], but without formally joining (it is suggested that Katarn and [[Jan Ors]] had met [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] in the past). Because both his background and stance raised concerns, Ors sent to [[Mon Mothma/Legends|Mon Mothma]] a coded transmission titled "Reliability of Kyle Katarn," reassuring her that Katarn's experience with the Empire turned him against it, and would eventually bring him closer to the Alliance.<ref name="Coded" />

Ors proceeds with his personal story: Katarn was a farmboy from [[Sulon/Legends|Sulon]], who enrolled to the [[Imperial Academy/Legends|Academy]] in order to follow better his father's trade, discovering his talents in [[cybernetics/Legends|cybernetics]] and information engineering, without noticing the Empire's growing hold on the Academy. In an Imperial cover-up, Kyle learned that both his parents were killed in a Rebel ambush during an Imperial rescue operation on [[Sullust/Legends|Sullust]]. He was devastated when the Academy refused an extension of his leave.<ref name="Coded" />

Fueled by hatred, he accepted a commission in the [[Imperial Special Operations|special operations division]], where he served for [[Standard year/Legends|years]]. While there, he crossed paths with Ors, who was working as a double agent, and could see past his façade a traumatized man, disillusioned with the institution he was serving. The two became friends, and Ors saw an opportunity to pass towards him reports about what really happened on Sullust.<ref name="Coded" />

When Ors's identity was revealed and she was arrested, Katarn was forced to choose his path. He saved her from torture and helped her escape. He deserted his position to return to the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim]] when he met Ors at a Rebel base. There he spent time with smugglers and pirates and learned his unorthodox techniques as a deadly efficient saboteur.<ref name="Coded" />

Ors concluded that his background made him unable to see the ideals of the Alliance, until they could ''win'' his loyalty, but his hatred for the Empire was unquestionable.<ref name="Coded" />

==Continuity==
The manual contained the original version of Kyle Katarn's backstory. This version was later contradicted by ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]'' and ''[[Dark Forces: Soldier for the Empire]]'' (although the writers acknowledged and adapted portions of it). ''[[The Dark Forces Saga]]'' attempted to explain this by stating that between Katarn's defection to the Rebel Alliance and his [[Mission to Danuta|first mission]] for them, Jan Ors was exaggerating about Katarn's history in an attempt to convince Mon Mothma that he was trustworthy.<ref name="The Dark Forces Saga">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20050407b|serieslink=The Dark Forces Saga|text=Part 2: Unsung Heroes of the Light}}</ref>
