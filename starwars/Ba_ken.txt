'''Ba'ken''' was an [[alien]] active on the [[planet]] [[Zenarita]]. At some point during the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[occupation of Zenarita]], he provided [[Engine|thrusters]] for the [[human]] [[Chita]]'s [[Chita's ship|starship]], and later a job that got her in trouble with the Empire. When Ba'ken considered the situation on Zenarita to be dicey, he deactivated Chita's [[Starship|ship's]] systems until she paid double of what they originally agreed upon.

Chita eventually confronted Ba'ken at some point between [[2 BBY]] and [[0 ABY]] in a [[Unidentified cantina (Zenarita)|cantina]] and demanded an explanation. The alien stated his terms and told the outraged human that he would reactivate her ship as soon as he received his payment. The [[Cerean]] former [[Jedi]] [[Gleenu]] overheard the exchange and offered to pay off Chita's debt as long as she could perform a job for the Cerean. Gleenu and Chita made the deal and Chita's ship had its systems reactivated.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Easy there. If you wanna complain, then why don't you take it up with the Empire? What's wrong? You wouldn't happen to be in trouble with the Empire, would you?''"<br/>"''As if you weren't the one who got me that job in the first place.|Ba'ken and Chita|<ref name="Smuggler" />}}
[[File:AngryChita-Smuggler.png|thumb|right|300px|Ba'ken enraged Chita by disabling her starship]]
Ba'ken was active on the [[planet]] [[Zenarita]] during the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Occupation of Zenarita|occupation]] of the world. At some point during the [[Imperial Era]], Ba'ken provided refurbished [[engine|thrusters]] to the [[human]] [[Chita]] and charged a high price for installing them into her [[Chita's ship|ship]], which she paid.<ref name="Smuggler" /> He<ref name="SmugglerAD" /> also gave Chita a job that got her in trouble with the Galactic Empire. After the situation on Zenarita grew more tense<ref name="Smuggler" /> at some point between [[2 BBY]] and [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|307}}</ref> Ba'ken locked Chita out of her [[starship]]'s system.<ref name="Smuggler" />

After realizing her systems were unresponsive, Chita confronted the [[alien]] in a [[Unidentified cantina (Zenarita)|cantina]] on Zenarita. Ba'ken sat alone guarded by his [[Ba'ken's entourage droid|entourage droid]] in a booth with a cup, which he took a sip from as Chita slammed her hands on his table. She demanded that Ba'ken reactivate her ship, and he affirmed he would be happy to do so if Chita paid double for the thrusters, claiming the previous charge was the old price for parts and labor. Infuriated, Chita claimed the original price was already a rip-off and was appalled he was asking for more. Ba'ken stated the prices went up as the situation was "dicey" under the Empire. Chita exclaimed that his explanation was "[[bantha dung]]," and Ba'ken told her to complain to the Empire instead. Chita being speechless, Ba'ken teased her potential trouble with the Empire, to which she replied was his fault. Ba'ken acted clueless and claimed he had not done anything wrong as he took another sip from his drink.<ref name="Smuggler" />

[[File:BakenChitaArgument-Smuggler.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Chita argues with Ba'ken over the change in price for parts and labor]]
Outraged, Chita reached for her [[blaster pistol]], but before she could act, his [[guard droid]] quickly raised its own [[blaster]] to the side of her head. Chita surrendered to the droid and Ba'ken remained calm, informing the human that he had not sold her out to the Empire and stated if she brought him the money that he would enable her ship's systems, suggesting she hurry and adding that it was only a matter of time before the Empire found her. Disgruntled, Chita left the cantina with her [[Limb|arms]] crossed. After overhearing the argument, the [[Cerean]] former [[Jedi]] [[Gleenu]] followed Chita out of the Cantina and offered to pay Ba'ken's price upfront if she could do a job for her. Chita accepted the deal and Gleenu was later informed when the systems were unlocked.<ref name="Smuggler" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Listen, Chita, I haven't sold you out to the Empire because I'm a nice guy and I respect your skills.|Ba'ken, to Chita|<ref name="Smuggler" />}}
Ba'ken was a stout [[Gender|male]] alien with grey and light skin with a brown [[Hair|beard]] and [[Color|yellow]] [[eye]]s. He was a relaxed and sarcastic individual. He denied any involvement with Chita's job that got her in trouble with the Empire, stating whatever the case was, that he did nothing wrong. He considered himself a nice guy and respected Chita's skills. He accepted Chita's debt being paid off by Gleenu.<ref name="Smuggler" />

==Skills and abilities==
The alien was capable of providing labor and parts to individuals capable of paying for the services. He also had the ability to revoke said services if he considered the payment to be outdated or invalid.<ref name="Smuggler" />

==Equipment==
Ba'ken wore a grey [[hat]] and [[Clothing|smock]] over layered grey shorts and tan pants. His clothes contained multiple pockets holding a variety of tools and devices. He wore a [[belt]] with satchels and pockets. While in the cantina he drank from a cup.<ref name="Smuggler" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ba'ken first appeared in "[[The Smuggler (short film)|The Smuggler]]," a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series, produced by [[Trigger (animation studio)|Studio Trigger]] and released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Smuggler" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. Before his appearance in the short, Ba'ken was first revealed during day three of [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]]<ref name="Celebration">{{SWYouTube|83cNchT1gdA|Star Wars Celebration - DAY 3 Replay|livestream=p20GM68wyC4}}</ref> on [[April 20]] of the same year.<ref name="CelebrationDate">{{SW|url=news/visions-presents-the-ninth-jedi|text=Volume 3 Release Date and New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Spin-off Series}}</ref> He was first identified in [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] on labeled concept art at the ''Star Wars: Visions'' release event a [[October 28|day before]] the short's release.<ref name="ReleaseEvent">{{YouTube|video=FUYb52fpIFg|text=【トークノーカット】浪川大輔、ファイルーズあい、黒沢ともよ、監督9名が登壇！『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』Volume3 配信直前イベント|channel=@cinematoday|name=シネマトゥデイ|date=2025-10-28}}</ref> The [[Wikipedia:English language|English]] spelling of his name was later confirmed in the short's credits<ref name="Smuggler" /> and subtitles.<ref name="SmugglerSub">{{Visions|The Smuggler (short film)}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> The alien was voiced by [[Jiro Saito]] in the original Japanese version and by [[Adam Sietz]] in the English dub.<ref name="Smuggler" /> In concept art by [[Tadashi Hiramatsu]],<ref name="Concept8">{{SW|url=the-smuggler-concept-art-gallery-star-wars-visions-volume-3|text="The Smuggler" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars: Visions Volume 3}} {{C|Slide 8}}</ref> the character's name was spelled as "Bakken."<ref name="FilmFocus">{{SWYouTube|series=Star Wars: Visions Filmmaker Focus|text=THE SMUGGLER|video=DeV1njld32k}}</ref>
