The '''Belbullab-22 heavy starfighter''' was a single-[[pilot]] heavy [[starfighter]] model manufactured by [[Feethan Ottraw Scalable Assemblies]]. It was the heaviest fighter in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]] and the only fighter crewed by a pilot.<ref name="Collapse" /> [[General]] [[Grievous]] flew a customized Belbullab-22 named the ''[[Soulless One]]''.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

==Characteristics==
[[File:Belbullab22StarfighterSquadron-SWM37.png|thumb|left|330px|A profile of view of a Belbullab-22 starfighter]]
Produced by the [[Utapau]]-based [[Feethan Ottraw Scalable Assemblies]] for usage by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]], the Belbullab-22 heavy [[starfighter]] was the heaviest starfighter within the Confederacy's inventory and cost 168,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. A rare crewed fighter in the navy, the Belbullab-22 was developed to fill a heavy attack role between the [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class droid starfighter]] and the [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Bomber]].<ref name="Collapse" />

Constructed similarly to a [[landspeeder]] or [[groundcar]] than a starfighter, the Belbullab-22 relied on powerful [[engine]]s and sheer speed to remain aloft. It had a twin-boom design with a long central fuselage flanked by two smaller nacelles. The main fuselage contained the fighter's [[cockpit]], avionics, [[Deflector shield generator|shield generators]], its Class 6 [[hyperdrive]] generator, and main engines. The port and starboard spar-mounted nacelles contained the fighter's weapon systems, auxiliary thrusters, and [[repulsorlift]] systems. The starfighter was also equipped with an [[autopilot]] system,<ref name="SWZ29" /> a backup Class 15 hyperdrive, close-range [[sensor]]s and a [[navigation computer]]. And while the cockpit was cramped, it could be configured from the factory to accommodate nearly any body type.<ref name="Collapse" />

[[File:FeethanOttrawAutopilot-SWZ29.png|thumb|left|330px|A Belbullab-22 approaches Pau City under autopilot.]]
Comparable in both role and armament to the [[Republic Navy]]'s [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter]], the Belbullab-22 was a relatively slow but incredibly tough [[Starship|vessel]]. The fighter's [[armor]] was on par with some smaller [[gunboat]]s, and its sturdy [[hull]] and powerful [[Deflector shield|shields]] allowed it to shrug off damage that would destroy smaller craft. Its armament consisted of a pair of triple light [[laser cannon]]s mounted in retractable pods, and while the fighter lacked ordnance, its [[cannon]]s were more than adequate for anti-fighter and even light anti-ship work.<ref name="Collapse" />

Following the failure of automated Separatist forces in the Clone Wars, it was the only Separatist fighter developed for a single organic pilot, making the way for the future of fighter production for the [[Imperial Era|Imperial era]].<ref name="Ships (II)">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic and Separatist Ships (2)}}</ref>
