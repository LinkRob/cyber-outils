'''Dznori Xam''' was a [[Kallidahin]] [[technician]] who worked on a [[Polis Massa Base|dig site]] run by the [[Archaeological Research Council]] in the [[Polis Massa]] [[Asteroid field|asteroid belt]]. At the end of the [[Clone Wars]] in [[19 BBY]], Xam was part of a team that aided the heavily injured [[human]] [[senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] in giving [[Reproduction|birth]] after she was brought to the dig site. The team successfully delivered the [[twin]]s [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]], but ultimately could not save Amidala's life.

==Biography==
The [[Kallidahin]] Dznori Xam worked as an<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> [[Archaeological Research Council]]<ref name="USW" /> [[technician]] at an [[Archaeologist|archaeological]] [[Polis Massa Base|dig site]] on one of the [[asteroid]]s of the [[Polis Massa]] [[asteroid field]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> The dig site was run by the council<ref name="USW" /> and was established in order to try and ascertain what had caused the destruction of the [[planet]] of Polis Massa and to search for traces of the [[Eellayin]], an extinct [[species]] that the Kallidahins believed to be their ancestors. Dznori Xam shared a surname with [[Selif Xam]], another technician at the base.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" />

At the end of the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|III}} to [[19 BBY]].</ref> the heavily [[Reproduction|pregnant]] [[human]] [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] was brought to the dig site by her allies Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] after sustaining heavy injuries.<ref name="ROTS" /> Dznori was part of a team consisting of two trained [[Doctor|physicians]] and several [[xenobiologist]]s who then began attending to Amidala in order to aid her in delivering the [[twin]]s she was carrying.<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> The team did not even have [[time]] to change out of their [[Clothing|caving jumpsuits]] due to the severity of Amidala's condition.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" />

[[File:Osh_Scal_and_Dznori_Xam.png|thumb|left|250px|Osh Scal and Dznori Xam]]
Amidala was placed in one of the site's medical facilities, although it was designed for treating investigators rather than childbirth.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> While she lay in labor on a table in the center of the room,<ref name="ROTS" /> Dznori and [[Osh Scal]], another technician,<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> stood together by one of the room's monitors while studying and gesturing at the readout. The pair then left the room prior to the actual birth, leaving only Kenobi, a [[GH-7 series medical assistance droid|GH-7 medical droid]], and a [[midwife droid]] to watch over the expectant [[Parent|mother]]. Amidala successfully gave birth to the twins and named them [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]], but [[Death|persihed]] soon after, having lost the will to live following the betrayal of her [[Marriage|husband]], [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="ROTS" />

==Personality and traits==
Dznori Xam and the other Kallidahin technicians at Polis Massa showed discretion when interacting with those accompanying the pregnant Amidala and asked few questions. Due to their limited contact with humans, the Kallidahins knew little about the species' parturition, but were still baffled when they could not save Amidala's life. Dznori had [[Color|purple]]-and-white skin and deep-focus black [[eye]]s.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" />

==Equipment==
While treating Amidala, Dznori Xam wore a form-fitting caving jumpsuit that was blue-and-brown in color and had warming lines down the [[Limb|legs]]. The outfit also included a surgical hood seal and a caver's [[harness]]. The Kallidahin was also equipped with a head-mounted signal light, a [[Droid caller|droid summoner]] and shoulder light mounted on the right shoulder, a [[utility belt]], a voice-activated [[comlink]], and leg warmers.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" />
