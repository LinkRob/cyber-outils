'''Ayo Tam'''<ref name="FX" /><ref name="Concept">{{ArtStation|username=django42|url=artwork/G85bG3|name=[[Dylan Howie-Richardson]]|text=Obi-Wan Kenobi alien concept designs (1)|date=2022-11-28|quote=Work I did for the Obi-Wan Kenobi series on Disney+ designing makeup prosthetics. Featured are designs of aliens for Mos Eisley in Episode 1.}}</ref> was a short [[alien]] individual who could be found on [[Tatooine]] at some point during [[9 BBY]]. He and an [[Aki-Aki]] were seen walking and talking near the [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] when [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] made his way towards an outbound [[Unidentified two-engined passenger transport|passenger transport]] in an attempt to save a young [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]].<ref name="Kenobi I" />

==Equipment==
Ayo Tam donned loose-fitting pants and a shirt which were dark brown and tan respectively, as well as a brown vest and large utility belt. Additionally, he equipped himself with a cybernetic eyepatch-like monocle sporting a dark red lens and three distinct head straps.<ref name="Kenobi I" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Ayo-Tam-Concept-Art-Set-Photo.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Miles Teves head sculpt alongside Dylan Howie-Richardson concept]]
Ayo Tam first appeared, albeit unidentified, in the [[Part I|first episode]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' television series, which aired on [[May 26]], [[2022]].<ref name="Companion">{{InsiderCite|214|Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi Companion|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi'' Companion}}</ref> Originally, make-up effects artist [[Shaun Smith]] and Joe Pepe designed the character's head for an unrelated project. [[Miles Teves]] subsequently sculpted its final, detailed appearance for '''a''' project that was ultimately canceled. [[Shaun Smith|Shaun Smith]]<nowiki/> then repurposed this bust for its eventual use in ''Obi-Wan Kenobi''. Smith asked concept artist [[Dylan Howie-Richardson]] to create an updated design for the character,<ref name="FX" /> which was then implemented as a background character within the series' first episode.<ref name="Concept" /> Ayo Tam was played by actress [[Jo Osmond Morriss]], being one of her many roles as background characters among Star Wars television.<ref name="FX" />
