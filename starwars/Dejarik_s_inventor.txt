The '''inventor of [[dejarik]]''' was an individual who was born many [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="DB Dejarik">{{Databank|dejarik|Dejarik}}</ref> At one point, they invented the popular two-player board game known as dejarik,<ref name="Last Shot" /> also known as holochess.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> They also invented a similar game, [[saigok]], which the [[Twi'lek]] [[Kaasha Bateen]] regarded as a more "badass" version of dejarik. Bateen described the inventor as a "madman."<ref name="Last Shot" />

==Behind the scenes==
The individual responsible for inventing dejarik was first mentioned in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Last Shot]]'' by [[Daniel José Older]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
