'''Delia Leighton''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who operated a [[cantina]] aboard the ''[[Serendipity]]'', a [[1550-LEX yacht]]. She was acquainted with the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]], and helped him and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] member [[Caluan Ematt]] avoid [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] capture and escape the [[planet]] [[Cyrkon]].<ref name="SR" />
