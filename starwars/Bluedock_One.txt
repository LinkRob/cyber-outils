'''Bluedock One''' was a [[bluedock]] [[space station]] used by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet|Expansionary Defense Fleet]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' takes place in [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> following a [[Hoxim incident|battle]] over the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] [[Hoxim]], the [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'' was repaired at Bluedock One. [[Supreme General]] [[Ba'kif]] met with the [[warship]]'s [[commanding officer]]s, [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," and [[Mid Captain]] [[Ufsa'mak'ro]] "Samakro," on the [[Starship|ship]] while it was at the bluedock.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bluedock One appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final volume of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
