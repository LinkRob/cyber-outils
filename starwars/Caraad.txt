'''Caraad''' was a [[planet]] located in [[the Interior]] and [[Colonies]] regions of [[the galaxy]].

==Description==
A cold and [[mountain]]ous [[Planet|world]], Caraad<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|caraad|Caraad}}</ref> was located in [[the Interior]]<ref name="Interior" /> and [[Colonies]] regions of [[the galaxy]] in grid square L-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. It was connected to [[Palanhi system|Palanhi]]<ref name="CLNE" /> and the [[Thrinittik system]]<ref name="Galaxy Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> by the [[Shwuy Exchange]] [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="Shwuy" /> Caraad included a [[Unidentified city (Caraad)|city]]. Built amongst the planet's snowy peaks, the [[city]] had [[speeder]] traffic, residential housing, and markets with vendors selling [[Foodstuff|food]], [[flower]]s, and more. In addition to the cold, the planet was also windy.<ref name="Identity Crisis" />

==History==
During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|310}}</ref> the [[Tarlafar]] [[Ailish]] took their child [[Bayrn]] with them to a market when Bayrn was involved in an incident where multiple citizens witnessed his [[Force-sensitive|Force-sensitivity]]. Hurrying [[Ailish's home|home]], the two left the market, unaware that an [[Unidentified Aqualish (Caraad)|Aqualish]] resident had reported the incident to a tip line for class one [[bounty hunter]]s. That night, the bounty hunter [[Cad Bane]] and his [[butler droid]] [[Todo 360]] traveled to Caraad and kidnapped the child, delivering them to the [[Galactic Empire]] and collecting the [[Midi-chlorian|M-count]] bounty reward for retrieval of Force-sensitive targets.<ref name="Identity Crisis" />

==Inhabitants==
Caraad was home to [[Arcona]], [[Aqualish]], [[Bith]], [[human]]s, [[Pantoran]]s, [[Rodian]]s, and Tarlafar.<ref name="Identity Crisis" />

==Behind the scenes==
Caraad first appeared in "[[Identity Crisis]],"<ref name="Identity Crisis" /> the eleventh episode of the [[The Bad Batch Season 3|third season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'', which premiered on [[April 3]], [[2024]].<ref name="Watch">{{SW|url=news/the-bad-batch-season-3-trailer|text=Watch the ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 3 Trailer}}</ref> Although unnamed in the episode,<ref name="Identity Crisis" /> Caraad was identified via the [[Databank]] on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Databank" />
