'''Elizabeth A. D. Eno''' has been the book designer for many ''[[Star Wars]]'' novels published by the [[Del Rey]] [[Wikipedia:Imprint|imprint]]. She has worked on books in both the former Expanded Universe, now known as [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], and the current [[canon]] continuity.

==Biography==
Elizabeth A. D. Eno, who works for [[Wikipedia:Penguin Random House|Penguin Random House]],<ref name="Book Show" /> has created the book designs for numerous ''[[Star Wars]]'' novels published by the [[Del Rey]] [[Wikipedia:imprint|imprint]] of [[Random House]]; she also designs books published by other Penguin Random House divisions and imprints. Most of her ''Star Wars'' work is in the current canon, including several film novelizations, but she does not exclusively design for ''Star Wars''. She designed [[Crucible (novel)|''Crucible'']] by [[Troy Denning]],<ref name="Crucible">[[Crucible (novel)|''Crucible'']]</ref> chronologically the final novel in the former [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]], as well as the first novel set in the current canon, ''[[A New Dawn]]'' by [[John Jackson Miller]].<ref name="A New Dawn">''[[A New Dawn]]''</ref>

For some authors, Eno has designed more than one of their books. She designed [[James Luceno]]'s [[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']] in the former Expanded Universe,<ref name="Darth Plagueis" /> and Luceno's ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'' in the current canon.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> [[Timothy Zahn]]'s first trilogy in the current canon, [[Star Wars: Thrawn (novel series)|''Star Wars: Thrawn'']], features Eno's book designs,<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref><ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref><ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref> and she began designing Zahn's ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]'' with ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''.<ref name="Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> She created the book designs for [[Alexander Freed]]'s novelization of [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'']]<ref name="Rogue One">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'']]</ref> and ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' series.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref><ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

[[Scott Biel]], senior art director at Penguin Random House,<ref name="Scott Biel bio">{{WebCite|url=http://www.scottbiel.com/bio|text=Bio — Scott Biel|archivedate=20200204005922}}</ref> has been the cover designer for many of Eno's book designs. Eno and Biel's collaborations on [[Paul S. Kemp]]'s ''[[Lords of the Sith]]'' and the script of [[Cavan Scott]]'s [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'']] both featured cover illustrations by [[Aaron McBride]].<ref name="Lords of the Sith">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref><ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'']]</ref>
