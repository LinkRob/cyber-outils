By [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Note" /> a '''blockade of the Yavin system''' was carried out during the [[Galactic Civil War]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[blockade]] was commanded by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Despite being blockaded, [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance]] [[starfighter]]s were able to roam freely.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />
