'''Frogs''' were croaking [[amphibian]]s<ref name="Ep 6">{{Film|VI}}</ref> that could be found on various [[planet]]s including [[Sorgan]],<ref name="Chapter4" /> [[Ossus]],<ref name="FtDCaS">{{BOBF|Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger}}</ref> [[Rodia]],<ref name="Heir to the Jedi">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> and [[Tinnel IV]].<ref name="Attack on Starkiller Base">''[[Join the Resistance: Attack on Starkiller Base]]''</ref> [[Klatooine paddy frog]]s were a type of frogs.<ref name="Ep 6" /> The [[gwerp]]s were similar to frogs in some respects.<ref name="ACGAS">"[[All Creatures Great and Small]]"</ref> The large and predatory [[oggdo]] species, native to [[Bogano]], had features extremely similar to frogs.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

Later in [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]], [[Luke Skywalker]] compared Yoda to a frog when he met the [[Jedi]] Master on [[Dagobah]].<ref name="SYWtBaJ?" />

A large species of [[Unidentified Nal Hutta frog|frog]] lived in a [[swamp]] on [[Nal Hutta]]. During the [[New Republic Era]], one of these frogs attempted to swallow the [[alien]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], before the [[Unidentified species (Grogu)|alien]] [[Gatori]] shot the frog with a [[slingshot]], causing it to spit Grogu out.<ref name="Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[George Lucas]] refused to ever identify [[Yoda's species]], but has jokingly said Yoda was a frog. In the documentary "From Puppets to Pixels," Lucas called Yoda "the illegitimate child of [[Wikipedia:Kermit the Frog|Kermit the Frog]] and [[Wikipedia:Miss Piggy|Miss Piggy]]."<ref name="Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones">DVD bonus disc of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]: "From Puppets to Pixels"</ref>
