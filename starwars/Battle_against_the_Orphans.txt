Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''battle''' was fought on a [[Unidentified planet (Fermata Cage)|planet]] between the [[Orphans]]; made up of [[Chanath Cha]], the [[droid]] [[Ladybright]], [[Sear]], and [[Imara Vex]]; and the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The battle was a culmination of [[Standard year|years]] of training and research conducted by Cha, who led the Orphans and sought revenge on Vader for the [[Mission to Cabarria|deaths]] of her [[parent]]s [[Bhada Cha|Bhada]] and [[Ramat Cha]] two [[Standard decade|decades]] before. It was fought after the Orphans had been sent by [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]], the [[Crime lord|leader]] of the criminal syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]], to rescue her allies, "[[The Archivist]]" and [[Kho Phon Farrus]], as well as the [[Fermata Cage]], which was central to her [[War against the Sith|efforts]] to destroy the Vader and his [[Sith Master]] [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Hidden Empire 3" />

==The battle==
[[File:DarthVader-HiddenEmpire2.png|thumb|left|200px|Darth Vader charges the Orphans]]
Arriving on [[Planet|world]], the [[Orphans]] were able to prevent [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] from [[Death|killing]] "[[the Archivist]] and [[Kho Phon Farrus]]. [[Chanath Cha]] revealed herself to Vader,<ref name="Hidden Empire 2">[[Hidden Empire 2|''Hidden Empire'' 2]]</ref> telling him of her years of studying and training to counter Vader's abilities. When Vader attempted to strike at her with his [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]], Cha had Ladybright damage Vader's vision whilst she threw a ball of [[acid]] at the [[Sith Lord]]. In response, Vader [[Force push]]ed all four Orphans, as well as destroying the [[Kho Phon Farrus' machine|machine]] which housed the Fermata Cage, before he went into the [[ocean]] to neutralize the acid. In the [[time]] Vader was in the ocean, the Orphans and their Crimson Dawn allies managed to retrieve the Cage, and then Cha instructed Vex and Ladybright to get Farrus and the Archivist, as well as the Fermatag Cage, out of Vader's way whilst she and Sear fought him. Vex was reluctant but ultimately followed Cha's orders. As Vader made his way out of the [[water]], he informed Cha that she would not avenge her parents or Qi'ra. The mention of Qi'ra's name surprised Cha, which Vader sensed, and allowed him to realize that she had not been [[Battle against Crimson Dawn|killed]] as he and his Master had previously believed, and he informed Cha that Sidious would be fascinated to hear that fact as he drew his lightsaber.<ref name="Hidden Empire 3" />

As Sear and Cha fought Vader, Vex, who had since returned to Cha's [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Siroto]]'' with Ladybright and the Archivist and Farrus, went to the top of the ''Siroto'', in the hopes of killing Vader from afar. As she made her way up, Vader pushed Sear into the wreckage of Farrus' machine, and she was killed. Vex then shot at Vader, but she missed her shot at his head and instead hit his hand. Vader then swung his [[lightsaber]] to create a turbulence which knocked the ''Siroto'' out of balance, causing Vex to fall into a [[jungle]] below, ending her role in the fight. After stabilizing the ship, Ladybright made contact with Qi'ra, and requested that she send the [[Dawn Fleet]] so that they could rescue Cha from Vader. Qi'ra declined the request, and ordered Ladybright to return to [[Cataract Base|Dawnfall Base]]. Ladybright refused the order, adding that whilst Qi'ra had provided employment, Cha had provided her a home, and turned the ''Siroto'' around to help Cha.<ref name="Hidden Empire 3" />

Vader and Cha saw the ''Siroto'' head towards them, and Cha urged them to go, with Vader commenting that them returning simply meant more wasted lives. The ship then turned away and escaped, and Vader stated to Cha that perhaps she was not as important to them as she had thought. Cha said that the ship going was good, knowing that the Fermata Cage had escaped and believing that Ladybright had also escaped, as she was stabbed in the chest by Vader, killing her. Unbeknownst to Cha, because of her decision to return to help Cha, the Archivist had destroyed Ladybright, and then taken control of the ''Siroto''. As they escaped, Farrus told the Archivist that they could not carry on with the attempts anymore, and that they wanted to be let go, to which the Archivist stated she would drop Farrus off on the way to Dawnfall Base.<ref name="Hidden Empire 3" />

At Dawnfall Base, Qi'ra spoke with [[Cadeliah]] about her decision to not send the Dawn [[Fleet]] and about Cha's mistaken view about her saw her life. Sometime later, the Archivist recorded in a [[The Archivist's Holocron|holocron]] about the first time she had seen Vader. The Archivist stated she was only alive because Cha had saved her, calling Cha very skilled and very brave.<ref name="Hidden Empire 3" />
