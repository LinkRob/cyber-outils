The '''attack''' on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]'s [[Grand Hunt]] took place<ref name="MA" /> in [[40 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> Prior to the event, the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] ordered her [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Aide|attendant]], [[Cady]], to reprogram the hunt's [[prey]], a [[LM-432 crab droid|crab droid]], with the intent to kill the princess. Privately, Fanry hoped that the attack would scare her [[regent]], [[Rael Averross]], into postponing the signage of the upcoming [[Governance Treaty]].<ref name="MA" />

During the event, the [[droid]] acted as intended and fired upon Fanry until the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] destroyed it. Afterward, an investigation was launched into the droid's [[Saboteur|sabotage]]. Despite their best efforts, the pair were unable to procure enough evidence to determine who had reprogrammed the droid.<ref name="MA" />
