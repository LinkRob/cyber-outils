'''Butter pastries''' were a food type served on the cruise ship ''[[Bastion Princess]]'' in [[44 ABY]]. [[Bhindi Drayson]] gave her last pastry to [[Trey Courser]] as a reward for swiftly completing the necessary sabotage work on the ship as part of a [[Wraith Squadron]] ruse.
