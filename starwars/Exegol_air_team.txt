During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], an '''air team''' led by Resistance [[General]] [[Poe Dameron]] was given the task of covering General [[Finn]]'s [[Exegol landing team|ground team]] while they [[Battle of Exegol|destroyed]] the [[navigation tower]] that was capable of making the [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[fleet]], the [[Final Order]], leave the hidden [[Sith]] [[planet]] of [[Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
