The '''artisan colony''' was a [[city]] with 50,000 inhabitants located on the planet [[Kiros]]. It was founded by [[Togruta]] colonists who created a society without weapons, pursuing art and beauty instead of war.

==History==
[[File:GovernorRoshti-TCWCEJtB.png|thumb|left|250px|Gupat Roshti, the governor of the Kiros Artisan Colony]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], the colony was invaded by a contingent of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Separatist Droid Army|forces]] led by [[Zygerrian]] [[Darts D'Nar]], with the aide of Separatist leader, [[Count]] [[Dooku]], who insisted to offer the [[Planet|world]] his protection. In short amount of [[time]], all the colonists were [[Slavery|enslaved]]<ref name="Kidnapped" /> and transported to [[Zygerria]]<ref name="SotR">{{TCW|Slaves of the Republic}}</ref> by Zygerrian slavers.<ref name="Cody Bio Gallery">{{Databank|clone-commander-cody-biography-gallery|Clone Commander Cody Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 18}}</ref> Later, they were taken to a [[Kadavo slave processing facility|slave processing facility]] on [[Kadavo]].<ref name="EfK" /> D'Nar had the [[droid]]s plant [[Thermal annihilator bomb|bombs]] around the [[city]].<ref name="Kidnapped" />

Ten planetary rotations later, [[Jedi]] [[General]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], as well as [[Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and several [[clone trooper]]s, arrived in the city to try to liberate the colonists. Kenobi tried to negotiate with D'Nar in the [[governor's tower]], but D'Nar threatened Kenobi with the bombs, prompting Skywalker, Tano, and the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] to disable them during the [[battle on Kiros]]. D'Nar was ultimately captured,<ref name="Kidnapped" /> and the Jedi trio went on a [[Mission to Zygerria|mission]] to Zygerria to rescue the kidnapped colonists.<ref name="SotR" /> Eventually, all surviving colonists were [[Battle of Kadavo|rescued]], and [[Governor]] [[Gupat Roshti]] announced his people's intention to join the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="EfK" />
