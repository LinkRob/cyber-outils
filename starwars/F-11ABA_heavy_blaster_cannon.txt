The '''F-11ABA heavy blaster cannon''' was a weapon put into service by [[Sith Jetpack Trooper]]s of the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Final Order|fleet]] and [[Sith Eternal army|army]].

==Description==
The F-11ABA was a deadly [[blaster]] rifle which utilized an amplifier barrel to produce a powerful explosive round. A successor to the [[First Order]]'s standard issue [[F-11D blaster rifle]], it was used by Sith jet troopers on [[Exegol]]. It was issued with an adjustable [[J20 electroscope]] which transmitted information from the scope to the user's [[helmet]], allowing for increased accuracy and battlefield awareness.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==History==
[[File:Sith_jet_trooper-Kenner.jpg|thumb|right|150px|A Sith jet trooper holding the F-11ABA.]]
The F-11ABA was deployed with Sith jet troopers on Exegol to repel [[Resistance]] forces attempting to assault the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] to prevent the [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[fleet]], the [[Final Order]], from leaving the [[Star system|system]]. Although unsuccessful, the F-11ABA wielded by the highly trained jet troopers proved to be a highly effective weapon against the boarding [[Resistance trooper]]s, inflicting heavy [[Death|casualties]].<ref name="TROS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The F-11ABA was first seen in [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']],<ref name="TROS" /> but was later identified as the F-11ABA heavy blaster cannon in ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''.
