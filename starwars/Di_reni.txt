'''Di'reni''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] [[bartender]] and owner<ref name="Di'reni" /> of [[Club Lazuli]] in [[Canto Bight]]<ref name="Aphra 9" /> on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Cantonica">{{Databank|cantonica|Cantonica}}</ref> The Twi'lek's club was under the protection of the [[Sixth Kin]] criminal organization, led by [[Wen Delphis]]. After the Sixth Kin [[Lieutenant (criminal)|lieutenant]] [[Ariole Yu]] and the [[sniper]] [[Just Lucky]] were sent by Delphis to collect a [[Currency|debt]] from [[Koz]] in Club Lazuli, Di'Reni watched from behind the bar, speaking with Lucky while Yu fought with several of Koz's associates. When the situation devolved into a shootout, Di'Reni retrieved a [[blaster]] and fired upon Koz's henchmen as the Sixth Kin operatives pursued their target out of the establishment.<ref name="Aphra 9" />

==Behind the scenes==
Di'reni first appeared in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 9|ninth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 9" /> on [[April 21]], [[2021]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/82212/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_9|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #9}}</ref> Wong later confirmed the capitalization of the Twi'lek's name on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]].<ref name="Di'reni" />
