A '''division''' was active within the [[Imperial Army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] following the [[Proclamation of the New Order]] in the early [[Imperial Era]]. The [[division]] was under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Imperial officer]] [[Colonel]] [[Barokki]], though, according to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], demonstrated poor performance. Barokki was summoned before Palpatine and his enforcer, [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], to provide his report, but was [[Force choke]]d by Vader due to his unit's performance. Palpatine ordered Vader to eventually release him, and thanked Barokki for the report, hoping that his division's performance would improve in the [[Standard week|weeks]] to follow.<ref name="Dark Lord 8" />

Colonel Barokki eventually became embroiled in failed [[Assassination attempts on Darth Vader|assassination attempts]] on [[Darth]] Vader. After Vader [[Meditation|meditated]], he remembered Barokki's audience with Palpatine, and later spoke to the Emperor about the conspiracy within the [[Imperial Officer Corps]]. After members of the [[officer corps]] were assembled before Palpatine and Vader, Vader randomly selected five [[officer]]s, including Barokki, and [[Death|killed]] them as a warning to the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="Dark Lord 12">[[Darth Vader (2017) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 12]]</ref>
