The '''chalcedony waves''' was the name of a [[necklace]] that belonged to the women of the [[House of Organa]], the [[Monarchy|royal]] family of [[Alderaan]]. [[Alderaanian monarchy|Queen]] [[Breha Organa]] owned that necklace at some point, before passing it down to her adopted daughter, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. Although Alderaan was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], the chalcedony waves survived the planet's disappearance, as the princess had been offworld herself. After the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Battle of Yavin|defeated]] the Empire over [[Yavin 4]], Leia wore the chalcedony waves during a [[Royal Award Ceremony|ceremony]] honoring the Alliance heroes of [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]]. Shortly thereafter, she offered the necklace to the [[Sullustan]] [[Nien Nunb]], as a reward for his help [[Mission to rescue the survivors of Alderaan|evacuating]] [[Alderaanian]] survivors from [[Sullust]].

==Description==
[[File:LeiaHanMedal-ANH.jpg|thumb|left|Leia Organa wearing the chalcedony waves and their matching bracelet]]
The [[chalcedony]] waves [[necklace]] was made of ten identical square pieces with a carved indentation running diagonally.<ref name="Royal Rebel">''[[Princess Leia: Royal Rebel]]''</ref> All of them had a silvery finish. When worn, the chalcedony waves rested at the top of the collar [[bone]], between its wearer's throat and her cleavage. The necklace came with a chunky cuff [[bracelet]] that matched the waves' style and [[color]].<ref name="ANH" />

==History==
The chalcedony waves belonged to the women of the [[House of Organa]], the [[Monarchy|royal]] family of the [[planet]] of [[Alderaan]]. It notably belonged to [[Alderaanian monarchy|Queen]] [[Breha Organa|Breha]],<ref name="Leia 4" /> who already ruled her [[homeworld]] around the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Revenge of the Sith">{{Film|III}}</ref> The necklace was later passed down to the [[Queen]]'s adopted daughter, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]],<ref name="Leia 4" /> who used to wear it for formal ceremonies.<ref name="Royal Rebel" />

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> the tyrannical government known as the [[Galactic Empire]] used its brand new [[battle station]], the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], to [[Destruction of Alderaan|blow up]] Alderaan. However, having been kept [[prisoner]] by the Empire, Leia survived the destruction of her [[Homeworld|home planet]], and so did the chalcedony waves.<ref name="ANH" />

The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], whose members actively [[Galactic Civil War|opposed]] the Empire, launched an [[Battle of Yavin|assault]] against the [[Death Star]], destroying that planet-killing battle [[Space station|station]] in the [[orbit]] of [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]]. A [[Royal Award Ceremony|ceremony]] honoring the Alliance heroes of Yavin took place, during which Leia wore the chalcedony waves and awarded [[Medal of Bravery|Medals of Bravery]].<ref name="ANH" />

[[File:NunbChalcedonyWaves-PrincessLeia4.png|thumb|Nien Nunb receiving the chalcedony waves from Leia Organa]]
Shortly after the award ceremony, Leia set off on a [[Mission to rescue the survivors of Alderaan|mission]] to reunite the surviving [[Alderaanian]]s who were scattered across [[the galaxy]],<ref name="Leia 1">[[Princess Leia 1|''Princess Leia'' 1]]</ref> and she took the chalcedony waves with her, among other personal effects.<ref name="Leia 4" /> One of those pouches of Alderaanian survivors was located in an [[Alderaan Enclave|enclave]] on [[Sullust]]. However, a [[spy]] in the princess's company revealed their presence on Sullust to the Empire, putting the Alderaanians' life in grave danger. Ultimately, the princess and her people managed to evacuate the enclave with the help of [[Nien Nunb]], a [[Sullustan]] [[smuggler]].<ref name="Leia 3">[[Princess Leia 3|''Princess Leia'' 3]]</ref> Unable to reward Nunb with a precious [[medal]], the princess decided to award him the chalcedony waves instead.<ref name="Leia 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
The chalcedony waves first appeared in the [[1977]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], the first installement in the [[original trilogy]] of ''[[Star Wars]]''. It was worn by Princess Leia, played by actress [[Carrie Fisher]], during the closing scene of the film.<ref name="ANH" /> The name of the necklace was never spoken on-screen, but was referred to as "chalcedony waves" on page 219 of the [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope'' novel]].<ref name="ANHn">[[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope'' novelization]]</ref> That name was later confirmed to be [[canon]] in the [[Princess Leia 4|fourth issue]] of the [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Princess Leia]]''.<ref name="Leia 4" />

The piece of jewelry that was used in ''A New Hope'' was designed by [[Björn Weckström]] of Lapponia Jewelry, one of the most famous jewelry designers in [[Wikipedia:Finland|Finland]].<ref name="starwars.com">{{SWArchive|url=community/event/museum/news20010724.html|text=Museum Tours: Hutchinson & Helsinki}}</ref> In spring [[1976]], the secretary of ''Star Wars'' creator [[George Lucas]] contacted Weckström, requesting a piece of silver jewelry to feature in a movie the name of which could not be revealed at that point. That piece of jewelry was to appear in a scene that was due to be filmed within six weeks. The project seemed interesting to Weckström, who accepted the assignment.<ref name="Lapponia and SW">{{WebCite|url=http://www.lapponia.com/en_en/lapponia-jewelry/history/star-wars|text=Lapponia and Star Wars|work=Lapponia.com|archiveurl=https://archive.is/fwKw8}}</ref>

[[File:Darina_and_Planetoid.jpg|thumb|left|200px|"Planetoid Valleys" and "Darina's Bracelet"]]
As Weckström had started to design a new piece, he was told there had been a change in the filming sequence of the scenes, and the jewelry would be needed in a week's time. Since Weckström was unable to finish his design work on such short notice, it was decided that Princess Leia's jewels would be bought from Lapponia's products that were available in London, where Lucas' movie was being filmed. Consequently, Lucas' agents acquired a necklace and a bracelet from a Lapponia retailer on Bond Street, London. The chosen pieces were the "Planetoid Valleys" necklace and "Darina's Bracelet," which were part of the "Space Silver" collection Weckström had designed in 1969.<ref name="Lapponia and SW" /> Those silver pieces were inspired by the Finnish winter landscape with its frozen lake surfaces.<ref name="Planetoid Valleys">{{WebCite|url=http://www.lapponia.com/se_en/planetoid-valleys-necklace|text=Planetoid Valleys Neckalce|work=Lapponia.com|archivedate=20170709013031}}</ref> The designer was later notified that Lucas thought his jewelry was fantastic, but still did not know in which movie it would be displayed.<ref name="Lapponia and SW" />

Eventually, ''A New Hope'' was released in May 1977, although it only premiered in Finland on December 16. Having seen one of the first screenings of the movie in Finland, a friend of Weckström's recognized his jewelry in the final scene of the movie. Shortly thereafter, Weckström himself went to see the movie, and was impressed to see his own work on the big screen.<ref name="Lapponia and SW" /> Thanks to its appearance at the end of ''A New Hope'', "Planetoid Valleys" became the all-time most well-known piece in the Lapponia Jewelry collection.<ref name="Lapponia">{{WebCite|url=http://www.lapponia.com/eu_en/lapponia-jewelry/history|text=History|work=Lapponia.com|archiveurl=https://archive.is/bNjml}}</ref> As of [[2015]], both "Planetoid Valleys" and "Darina's Bracelet" are still part of Lapponia's catalogue.<ref name="Planetoid Valleys" />
