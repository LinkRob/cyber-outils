'''Ax Tagrin''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Iktotchi]] [[bounty hunter]] active during the [[First Order-Resistance War]]. During the War, Tagrin found work from the [[First Order]] by capturing members of the [[Resistance]] since he believed in getting employed by the winning side.<ref name="The Missing Agent" />

==Biography==
During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[First Order]], [[Iktotchi]] [[bounty hunter]] Ax Tagrin found himself hunting down Resistance [[spy]] [[Norath Kev]] and his droid [[2BR-NTB]] when he got a tip from [[Lechee]], a contact of the First Order who moved their shipments and materials through the channels of the [[black market]]. After a pursuit in [[Vargo Spaceport]] on [[Varkana]], the spy got caught and 2BR-NTB got destroyed. Leeche informed Tagrin caught members of [[Team Colossus|Team ''Colossus'']] fellow Resistance spy [[Kazuda Xiono]], [[Jarek Yeager]], [[Synara San]], and Xiono's droid [[CB-23]] in Kev's apartment but they manage to destroy Kev's [[datapad]] and escape. Later in the same [[spaceport]], Tagrin manage to trap most of them by trapping them in his ship, the ''[[Dalkor Dagger]]''.<ref name="The Missing Agent" />

[[File:AxTagrin-TheMissingAgent.png|thumb|250px|left|Ax Tagrin]]
Due to CB-23 sabotaging one of the ship's [[engine]]s, the ''Dalkor Dagger'' crashed back onto Varkana before Tagrin could turn his [[prisoner]]s over to the First Order. As the Resistance members attempted to make their escape, Kev and Xiono fled on a [[speeder bike]] while San, Yeager, and CB-23 were captured by Tagrin.<ref name="The Missing Agent" /> Tagrin brought them to [[Commander]] [[Pyre]], but because two agents were still at large, he did not receive his [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]]<nowiki/>s.<ref name="Breakout">{{Resistance|Breakout}}</ref>

Tagrin was persistent to catch the spies until he discovered Kev and Xiono disguised as [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] and overheard Xiono mention his [[Star Commuter 2000|shuttle]] parked outside the spaceport. After finding Xiono's shuttle, Tagrin broke in and used signal-tracing to locate the ''[[Colossus]]'', which was hiding in a nearby [[gas giant]], for the First Order to finally get his bounty which was his usual rate. In the meantime, Xiono and Kev helped Yeager and San escape and ran back to his shuttle, where Tagrin was hiding in the back. While Xiono and Kev were starting the shuttle, Tagrin revealed himself and ambushed them. He almost captured Xiono and Kev, but with the help of CB-23, they took out Tagrin and retreat from Varkana.<ref name="Breakout" />

==Skills and abilities==
Tagrin was a formidable bounty hunter. He was skilled in [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]] managing to outmatch Synara San, a skilled pirate. Tagrin managed to fight both San and Yeager. Despite their combined efforts, they were unable to match Tagrin's strength and endurance.<ref name="The Missing Agent" />

==Equipment==
Ax Tagrin wore custom [[armor]], with a removable faceplate and various equipment; a [[C1 personal comlink]], a [[flash grenade]], and a [[homing beacon]] that can listen to conversations from far away. He also had a ship named the ''[[Dalkor Dagger]]''. The ship was heavily fortified and had an impressive arsenal of weaponry. The ship was vulnerable from within the inside, as it has been crashed by CB-23. Tagrin used a [[vibro-ax]] with an extendable arm. He also possessed a double-barreled [[blaster pistol]] that can be wielded with two hands.
