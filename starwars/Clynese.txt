'''Clynese''' was an alphabetic [[writing]] system with 26 letters, suitable for writing [[Galactic Basic Standard]], that originated from the [[Clyne system]] in the [[Core Worlds]].

==Description==
Clynese was made up of straight and curved brush strokes and dots.<ref name="Art Last Jedi">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]''</ref> The letters could be nested together, and could be written vertically or horizontally.<ref name="Millennium Falcon Owner's Manual">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref><ref name="Solo film">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Examples of Clynese throughout the Galaxy==
[[File:DrydensHolocronAndDataplaque-Solo-1h53m14s.png|thumb|right|150px|A dataplaque owned by Dryden Vos, displayed below his [[Qi'ra's Sith holocron|Sith holocron]] on the ''First Light''. The face of the dataplaque is inscribed with words in Clynese and [[Protobesh]].]]
In [[10 BBY]], [[Dryden Vos]] possessed a [[dataplaque]] on which the word<ref name="Solo film" /> "LIFE"<ref name="Art Last Jedi" /><ref name="Solo film" /> was inscribed in Clynese, along with other words in Clynese and [[Protobesh]]. The dataplaque was displayed in [[Dryden's study|his study]] on the ''[[First Light]]''.<ref name="Solo film" /> In [[34 ABY]] the same dataplaque, or a nearly identical one, was displayed in [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]</ref>

Inscribed by hand on at least one of the [[CEC Class-1 escape pod|Model CEC Class-1]] [[escape pod]]s of the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' were the words: "PROPERTY OF [[Han Solo|HAN SOLO]] PLEASE RETURN" in Clynese, which [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] later judged to be poorly written.<ref name="TLJ EE">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Clynese was named after the artist who developed it, [[James Clyne]]. In ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]'', Clyne stated that he and [[Rian Johnson]] discussed that it might be the [[Corellian]] alphabet.<ref name="Art Last Jedi" /> While originally developed for ''The Last Jedi'', released in 2017, Clynese would not ultimately appear in the film. The first visual appearance of Clynese is a split-second shot in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo film" />
