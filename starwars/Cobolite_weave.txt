'''Cobolite weave''' was an experimental ion-enhancing fabric used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] to create [[Test Unit 08-B]]. Test Unit 08-B was a [[clothing|garment]] that produced a weapons amplification field of 2.41 [[meter]]s. The field increased the effectiveness of ion weapons but had a side effect of causing nearby [[battle droid]]s to become violently disorientated. Due to the importance of Battle Droids to the war effort Dr. [[Aiyah Pellorean]], research director on the project, was ordered to halt their work until peace was achieved. The outfit along with its final report was stored in a [[Unidentified Separatist bunker|bunker]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|Canon=1}}
In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}} after tracking down a number of Clone Wars era transmissions </ref> [[Kay Vess]] was able to locate and open the bunker on Akiva. Inside she retrieved the garnet.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cobolite weave was first mentioned on a datapad in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game the outfit made from the weave was made up of three pieces of equipment, the "Disruptor Jacket", the "Disruptor Belt" and the "Disruptor Pants". When all pieces are worn, dealing ion damage to already ionized enemies becomes lethal.<ref name="Outlaws" />
