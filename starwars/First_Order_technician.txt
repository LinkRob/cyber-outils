'''First Order technicians''' were part of the [[First Order]]'s support staff. They were employed to repair technology aboard First Order vessels, including the [[supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Titan]]''. The technicians wore [[Color|gray]] uniforms with the First Order's logo on the sleeve. They were also outfitted with an orange work vest featuring several pockets, black [[glove]]s, and orange-tinted glasses.<ref name="STS" />
