'''Brian Posehn''' (born [[July 6]], [[1960s#1966|1966]]) is an actor who portrayed the [[speeder]] [[pilot]] [[Pyan Bosen]] in the debut episode of the [[Disney+]] live-action TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> According to Posehn, he got the part because [[Jon Favreau]] knew he was a ''Star Wars'' fan and thought that it was something he would want to do. Prior to getting the role, Posehn was known for his stand-up comedy, which included heated rants against both the ''[[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition]]''s and the ''Star Wars'' [[Prequel trilogy]].<ref name="Brian Posehn interview" />
