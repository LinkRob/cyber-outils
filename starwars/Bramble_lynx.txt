'''Bramble lynx''' were a type of creature found on<ref name="Battle Scars" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Dathomir]]. They started out small, a fact the [[Dathomirian]] [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Merrin]] stated was not known to many. A bramble lynx could be kept as a pet and trained to rip things apart in order to protect their owner under any circumstances. Merrin described them as being very cute and good at "[[Death|murders]]."<ref name="Battle Scars" />

By [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Dathomir date">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]'' establishes [[Merrin]] had [[Teeno]] as her pet by the time of the [[Battle of Dathomir]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[20 BBY]].</ref> Merrin had a pet bramble lynx named [[Teeno]], who liked to be scratched under her chin and was fond of [[Lesser Nydak|nydak]] [[blood]].<ref name="Battle Scars" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Dathomir date" /> Merrin's [[Mother Talzin's clan|fellow Nightsisters]] were [[Battle of Dathomir|wiped out]] by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Grievous]], and she did not see Teeno again.<ref name="Battle Scars" /> Between [[12 BBY]] and [[11 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{BattleScarsDate}}</ref> Merrin recalled her pet bramble lynx Teeno when she was asked by the [[Keshiri]] [[Gender|female]] [[Chellwinark Frethylrin]] to tell her something about herself, mentally noting it was still hard to talk about.<ref name="Battle Scars" />
