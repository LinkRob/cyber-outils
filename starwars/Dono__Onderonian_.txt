'''Dono''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] from [[Onderon]] who served the [[Onderon rebels]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Biography==
Dono was a native of [[Iziz]], the [[capital]] of Onderon.<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> Shortly after one of the rebels pulled off a public demonstration, she reported to their leader, [[Steela Gerrera]], that [[King]] [[Sanjay Rash]] was going to execute the former king, [[Ramsis Dendup]], in the [[Yolahn Square|square]] the following [[Standard day|day]]. Later, she watched as Steela's sister [[Saw Gerrera|Saw]] go on a rogue mission to rescue the king the night before his execution. She witnessed him get captured and reported back to Steela. She and a group of Onderon rebels, including Steela and [[Lux Bonteri]] freed Dendup during his execution as well as Saw. Unknown to Dono and the rebels, [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|super tactical droid]] [[General]] [[Kalani]] had called for a squad of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s and when Dono led the group of rebels away, the super battle droids arrived and shot her, [[Death|killing]] Dono instantly. Her death would not be in vain as the rebels eventually succeeded in freeing Onderon.<ref name="The Soft War" />

==Personality and traits==
Dono was a true believer in the rule of King Dendup,<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> willing to fight against the new regime of King Rash.<ref name="The Soft War" />
