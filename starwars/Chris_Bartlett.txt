'''Christopher Bartlett''' (born [[September 3]], [[1970]]<ref name="SW Mando S2" />) is an actor who portrayed several characters in the [[Disney+]] live-action TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]''.<ref name="Stranger">{{BOBF|Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref> including [[Kubaz]] [[Barlidan|ferryman]] in "[[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian]],"<ref name="Chapter 1" /> body double for [[Q9-0]] in "[[Chapter 6: The Prisoner]],"<ref name="Chapter 6: The Prisoner">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 6: The Prisoner}}</ref> an [[RA-7 protocol droid]] [[Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid bartender|bartender]] in "[[Chapter 7: The Reckoning]],"<ref name="Chapter 7: The Reckoning">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 7: The Reckoning}}</ref> a [[bounty hunter]] [[droid]] in "[[Chapter 3: The Sin]]",<ref name="Bartlett-Twitter">{{Twitter|chrisfbartlett|status/1243202413403582465|Christopher Bartlett|quote=For my Star Wars friends in the UK waking up to bingeing The Mandalorian, look for my characters! My favorite is #Q9Zero|archivefile=File:Christopher-Bartlett-Mandalorian-March-26-2020.png}}</ref> a body double for a [[CB-3DU|protocol droid teacher]] in "[[Chapter 12: The Siege]],"<ref name="Chapter 12: The Siege">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 12: The Siege}}</ref> and an [[Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Moff Gideon)|RA-7 droid]] in "[[Chapter 16: The Rescue]].<ref name="Chapter 16: The Rescue">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 16: The Rescue}}</ref>
