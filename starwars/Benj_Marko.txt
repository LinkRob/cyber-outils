'''Benj Marko'''<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> was a [[human]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] during the [[High Republic Era]] who was apprenticed to [[Jedi Master]] [[Helion Volte]]. Marko and Volte were part of [[Galactic Republic]] [[Outer Rim Expedition Eight-Zero-Seven]], an [[Outer Rim Exploration Team]] whose primary mission was establishing a communications network.<ref name="Blade 1" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The present day events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic — The Blade]]'' take place between the [[wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran]] and [[Porter Engle]] [[Siege of Bardotta|earning]] his title the "Blade of Bardotta," both of which are dated to [[382 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> Marko and the team crossed paths with Jedi [[Porter Engle]] and [[Barash Silvain]] at the [[Office of the Frontier]], and Volte offered to drop them off on the [[planet]] [[Gansevor]] since their team was heading in that direction. On the way, Volte asked Engle to spar, explaining that they only got the opportunity to spar with Marko, who was improving but still had less skill because he was a Padawan, causing Marko to make an exclamation of frustration.<ref name="Blade 1" /> Marko was mentioned and pictured in ''[[Chronicles of the Jedi]]'', a document written by Jedi Master [[Harli Cogra]] in the [[Standard week|weeks]] following the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]]<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi" /> in [[230 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Benj Marko was first pictured at the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' panel at [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Star Wars Celebration Anaheim]] in [[2022]].<ref name="Celebration">{{SWYouTube|Mb4ThcRMTf0|Star Wars: The High Republic For Light and Life Panel &#124; Star Wars Celebration Anaheim 2022|sw_url=video/launch-trailer-star-wars-the-high-republic}}</ref> He made his first appearance and was identified in the [[The High Republic — The Blade 1|first issue]] of the [[2022]] comic mini series ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic — The Blade]]'' by [[Charles Soule]].<ref name="Blade 1" /> The character's last name was first revealed in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]'' by [[Cole Horton]].<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi" />
