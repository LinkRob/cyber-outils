{{Quote|We have more than a dozen active battlefronts on Anaxes. And we are losing nearly every one. But if Admiral Trench can no longer anticipate our moves, we now have the opportunity to retake the planet.|Mace Windu|<ref name="UB">{{TCW|Unfinished Business (episode)}}</ref>}}
'''Anaxes''' was a rocky [[planet]] located in the [[Azure sector]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. A majority of its surface was covered with tall, [[Color|red]] [[plant]]-like life. It was a vital world of the [[Galactic Republic]] for its role in protecting the [[Core Worlds]] from attacks along the [[Perlemian Trade Route]] as well as its [[Anaxes shipyards|shipyards]].

In the early years of the [[Imperial Era]], the planet was [[Destruction of Anaxes|destroyed]] in a cataclysm, with the remains becoming an [[Anaxes asteroid belt|asteroid belt]].

==Description==
[[File:AnaxesCanyon.png|thumb|left|The landscape of Anaxes]]
Anaxes was a rocky and [[jungle]] [[planet]]<ref name="Databank" /> located within the [[Azure sector]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> of the Coreward edge of the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> and was the [[homeworld]] of the silicon-based<ref name="USW" /> [[fyrnock]] [[species]]. The jungles were filled with tall, [[Color|red]] [[plant]]-like life that were likely also silicon-based.<ref name="The Bad Batch" /> The planet also possessed hills and grasslands, where agriculture was practiced, and was the origin of the [[Spiral Dumplings]] delicacy.<ref name="Magic Journeys">{{YouTube|video=aVCImNrYPJo|text=We Finally Board Disney's Galactic Starcruiser! A 2 Day Immersive Star Wars Experience! Part One|channel=channel/UCq_88QrBgrNgaeIkJZBC8SQ|name=Magic Journeys}}</ref>

==History==
Anaxes, situated along the [[Perlemian Trade Route]] and on the Coreward edge,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> was an ancient member of the [[Galactic Republic]], hosting a [[Republic Navy|Republic naval base]] protecting the [[Core Worlds]] from attacks along the Perlemian,<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref><ref name="Dawn" /> as well as prominent [[Anaxes shipyards|shipyards]].<ref name="The Bad Batch" /> For [[Republic Era|centuries]], it primarily worked with local [[Planetary defense forces|defense forces]].<ref name="Dawn" />

[[File:Anaxes_UnfinishedBusiness.png|thumb|left|Anaxes from [[Realspace|space]] during the Clone Wars]]
Anaxes returned to [[The galaxy|galactic]] prominence during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Dawn" /> Remaining loyal to the Republic, in the [[19 BBY|last]] [[Standard year|year]] of the war the planet was [[Battle of Anaxes|attacked]] by a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederate]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|fleet]] under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Trench]], as part of the [[Outer Rim Sieges]].<ref name="The Bad Batch" /> The Separatists were initially winning the battle, due to Trench's exploitation of a [[Grand Army of the Republic]] [[Rex's battle strategy algorithm|strategic algorithm]] captured with the [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Echo]]&mdash;who was believed to have [[Death|died]] [[Battle of Lola Sayu|earlier]] in the war. After Echo was [[Mission to Skako Minor (Clone Wars)|rescued]] from the [[Techno Union]] on [[Skako Minor]], [[Clone Force 99|Republic forces]] hacked into the transmissions of Trench's flagship, the ''[[Invulnerable]]'', ordering him to commit his forces to the [[Anaxes assembly complex|assembly complex]]&mdash;where they would encounter [[Jedi General]]s [[Mace Windu]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and the [[212th Attack Battalion]]. After the [[Separatist Droid Army|droid forces]] were neutralized, Trench was killed by [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his flagship destroyed, and the Republic was able to claim victory.<ref name="UB" />

After the Clone Wars, Anaxes was [[Destruction of Anaxes|destroyed]] by a cataclysmic event,<ref name="Databank" /> believed to be the result of a catastrophic [[Reactor|main reactor]] failure detonating a massive stockpile of [[hypermatter]] in the shipyards.<ref name="Dawn" /> All that remained of the planet was an [[Anaxes asteroid belt|asteroid belt]].<ref name="Out of Darkness">{{Rebels|Out of Darkness}}</ref> [[Fort Anaxes]], a [[Republic Military]] base constructed on Anaxes during the Clone Wars, survived the planet's destruction and remained on the [[planetoid]] [[PM-1203]].<ref name="Ezra's Duel with Danger">''[[Ezra's Duel with Danger]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Anaxes_landscape.png|thumb|left|Concept art of Anaxes]]
Anaxes originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, first being mentioned in the [[2003]] sourcebook ''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]'', where it had a long history as one of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic's]] primary military strongholds,<ref name="CatCW">''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]''</ref> and made its first Legends appearance in the [[2006]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]''.<ref name="Empire at War">''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]''</ref>

Before the initial cancellation of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' in [[2013]], a four-part arc featuring Anaxes had been under production. After the series' cancellation, the episodes of the "Bad Batch" arc were shown in story reel form at [[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim]] in April [[2015]].<ref name="Anaheim Announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-bad-batch-4-episode-arc-coming-to-star-wars-celebration|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' "Bad Batch" 4-Episode Arc Coming to ''Star Wars'' Celebration|nolive=1}}</ref> Around the same time, the episodes "[[Out of Darkness]]" and "[[Gathering Forces]]" of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of the follow-up series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' had the [[Spectres]] visit Fort Anaxes, now located in an asteroid field.<ref name="Out of Darkness" /><ref name="GF">{{Rebels|Gathering Forces}}</ref> The official [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] confirmed that the abandoned military base was the same one featured in the Bad Batch arc, and thus that Anaxes had been destroyed in the intervening time.<ref name="Databank" /> Anaxes finally appeared properly when a completed version of the Bad Batch arc aired as part of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|seventh and final season]] of ''The Clone Wars'', which debuted on [[Disney+]] in [[2020]].<ref name="The Bad Batch" />

Despite previous [[canon]] works placing Anaxes in the Core Worlds,<ref name="UltimateNew">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref><ref name="TOoS:AEP">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'' places Anaxes in the Outer Rim,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> and author [[Jason Fry]] confirmed that the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] had its location changed. Fry was allowed to place where the relocated world was on the galactic map and write its entry in ''Battles that Changed the Galaxy'',<ref name="Twitter Anaxes">{{Twitter|jasoncfry|status/1455675104168484873|[[Jason Fry]]|quote=[Bobby Fuller Four plays] I fought Story Group And Story Group won I fought Story Group And Story Group won (But seriously now, love those folks and I got to place relocated Anaxes on the map and write the new entry. It's all good.)|archivedate=20211102231614}}</ref> establishing Anaxes to be on the Coreward edge of the Outer Rim.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> Still, a map within the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel later placed Anaxes in the Core.<ref name="Galactic Starcruiser">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref> The [[2024]] ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' listed Outer Rim for Anaxes and kept the Azure sector placement,<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> but the [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix instead listed the Anaxes system under the [[Antemeridian sector]],<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Azure system|Anaxes system]]</ref> lining up with Anaxes' new placement on the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> as it falls in the part of space where the [[Antemeridian sector/Legends|Antemeridian sector]] was located in Legends.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
