The '''''Darkpath''''' was a [[frigate]] utilized by the [[Black Sun]] criminal syndicate during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Adorned with a [[Color|yellow]] Black Sun logo on the front, the ship was owned by the Black Sun boss [[Lord]] [[Gyuti]]. The ship had a large [[hangar]] that could accommodate many ships. There was a spacious room aboard that included a table and chairs that Lord Gyuti could use to receive guests. [[Droid]] attendants would assist with such visits.<ref name="Crimson Reign 1" />

During her plot to destroy the [[Sith Lord]]s [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], [[Crimson Dawn]] leader [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]] traveled to the ''Darkpath'' in order to speak with Lord Gyuti, and offered the Dawn's services to Black Sun, as well as suggesting that Black Sun make a play to become a partner with the Empire.<ref name="Crimson Reign 1" />
