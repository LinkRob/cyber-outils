'''BB-9E''' was a [[Color|black]]-plated [[BB-series astromech droid|BB-series]] [[astromech droid]] in the service of the [[First Order]]. A top-of-the-line [[astromech droid]], during the [[New Republic Era]], BB-9E served aboard [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]'s [[flagship]], the ''[[Supremacy]]'', keeping machinery operational and upholding security procedures.

During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], BB-9E discovered that the ''Supremacy'' had been [[Battle of Oetchi|infiltrated]] by [[Resistance]] operatives. As a result of the [[droid]]'s actions, [[Captain]] [[Phasma]]'s [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] captured [[Finn]], [[Rose Tico]], and [[DJ]] before they could disable the ''Supremacy''{{'s}} [[hyperspace tracker]].

==Biography==
[[File:BB-9E_SWDL.png|thumb|left|200px|BB-9E was a droid that served aboard the ''Supremacy''.]]
Manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]],<ref name="BB8 Encyclopedia" /> the [[droid]] BB-9E was the property of the [[First Order military]],<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> one of many units that were relied on to keep machinery operational and uphold procedures aboard the [[First Order Navy]]'s [[warship]]s.<ref name="Databank" /> During the [[New Republic Era]], a [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] broke out between the [[Resistance]] and the [[First Order]], as the latter set out to conquer [[the galaxy]] in the wake of the [[New Republic]]'s [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]]. By then,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> BB-9E was assigned to the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]''&mdash;the [[flagship]] of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]<ref name="TLJ VD" />&mdash;where it performed its usual duties by keeping machinery, procedures, and potential intruders in check.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

Following the [[Battle of D'Qar|assault]] on [[D'Qar]], a [[Resistance navy|flotilla]] of Resistance [[starship]]s was [[Hyperspace tracker|tracked]] through [[hyperspace]] and [[Battle of Oetchi|attacked]] by a [[fleet]] of First Order [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers]], led by the Supreme Leader's [[command ship]]. While the attack was in progress, BB-9E located three intruders&mdash;Resistance [[technician]] [[Rose Tico]], the renegade [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn|FN-2187]] "Finn," and the [[Mercenary|freelance]] [[slicer]] "[[DJ]]"&mdash;who infiltrated the ''Supremacy''. BB-9E exposed the would-be saboteurs before they could disable the flagship's advanced [[active tracker]] system, resulting in their capture by [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] and her [[trooper]]s. The Resistance droid [[BB-8]], escaped the First Order's notice, however.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Characteristics==
[[File:BB-series_Fathead.png|thumb|right|250px|Like BB-8, BB-9E was a BB-series astromech droid.]]
BB-9E was a top-of-the-line [[astromech droid]],<ref name="Battlefront II" /> belonging to the [[BB-series astromech droid]] model. The droid was vigilant, utilizing a sharp [[photoreceptor]]&mdash;in every spectrum&mdash;against infiltrators, saboteurs and other threats to Supreme Leader Snoke's flagship.<ref name="Databank" /> Due to the First Order's view on droids, BB-9E was treated simply as a machine, resulting in a cold personality even for a droid that belonged to the normally friendly BB-unit model. Though subjected to regular [[memory wipe]]s in order to remain subservient and docile,<ref name="Phasma">[[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']]</ref> BB-9E had developed a malevolent personality. It was especially malicious when reporting transgressions.<ref name="TLJ VD" />

A dark, gleaming BB astromech unit,<ref name="Databank" /> BB-9E had [[Color|black]]-plating and a red,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> multi-spectrum photoreceptor with [[Telescope|telescoping lens]].<ref name="TLJ VD" /> Standing at 0.6 [[meter]]s in height,<ref name="Extraordinary Droids" /> BB-9E featured a distinctive square dome for a head<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and grilled openings to permit ventilation as well as enhanced [[sensor]] transparency.<ref name="TLJ VD" />

==Equipment==
Boasting the latest technology in the First Order,<ref name="Databank" /> BB-9E was armed with a [[Electroprod|shock prod]] and possessed an array of tools for various purposes, including technical support and combat.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Behind the scenes==
BB-9E was first revealed when several [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] sets' packaging for [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']]-themed sets leaked on [[May 26]], [[2017]].<ref name="Nerdist">{{WebCite|url=http://nerdist.com/star-wars-last-jedi-snoke-lego-nerdist-news|text=New LEGO Leaks Reveal Snoke and THE LAST JEDI Spoilers|author=Marnell, Blair|date=2017-05-30|work=[[Wikipedia:Nerdist News|Nerdist]]|archivedate=20170602024534}}</ref> The droid was officially introduced in a preview for the [[Force Friday]] II products, released on [[August 31]], 2017.<ref name="Force Friday">{{SW|url=news/force-friday-ii-first-look-select-new-star-wars-products|text=Force Friday II: First Look at Select New ''Star Wars'' Products}}</ref> BB-9E also acted as one of the three droids of the "App-Enabled Droids" app AR game by [[Wikipedia:Sphero|Sphero]] alongside [[R2-D2]] and BB-8, with BB-9E in particular giving a tour of the ''Supremacy''.<ref name="Sphero">''[[Star Wars Droids App by Sphero]]''</ref> The droid made his first appearance in the non-[[canon]] ''[[Star Wars Blips]]'' episode "[[Hey You]]," where he had a run in with BB-8.<ref name="Blips">{{Blips|Hey You}}</ref>

BB-9E was nicknamed "BB-H8," as in "hate," on the set of ''The Last Jedi''.<ref name="RianTwitter">{{Twitter|rianjohnson|status/903299927572307968|[[Rian Johnson]]|quote=Just be thankful we didn't stick with his obscenely adorable on set nickname, BB-H8|nobackup=1}}</ref>

During the early development of ''The Last Jedi'', BB-9E went almost three different designs made by [[Luke Fisher]] and [[Jake Lunt Davies]] before the final one which was featured in the finished version of the film.<ref name="Art of TLJ" /><ref name="SW-Designing Star Wars: The Last Jedi, Part 3: Developing BB-9E, a Droid Fit for the First Order">{{SW|url=news/designing-star-wars-the-last-jedi-part-3-bb-9e|text=Designing ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'', Part 3: Developing BB-9E, a Droid Fit for the First Order}}</ref> Every design mostly affected the droid's head. Additionally, its original name was going to be BB-Hate in order, according to droid and creature effects supervisor [[Neal Scanlan]], to demonize BB-8.<ref name="Art of TLJ">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]''</ref>
