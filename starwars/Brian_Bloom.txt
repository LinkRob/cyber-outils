'''Brian Bloom''' is an American actor, voice actor, and screenwriter, known for voicing the male [[Meteor (Havoc Squad)|Trooper]] in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' and its expansions.

Brian Bloom is known for voicing soldiers-type roles in video games. He is a regular voice in the [[Wikipedia:Call of Duty|''Call of Duty'']] video game series, and is responsible for co-writing [[Wikipedia:Call of Duty: Infinite Warfare|''Call of Duty: Infinite Warfare'']] and voicing the main character, Nick Reyes, along with motion capture. He is also known for voicing Kane in [[Wikipedia:Kane & Lynch: Dead Men|''Kane & Lynch'']], Matt Horner in the [[Wikipedia:Starcraft|''Starcraft'']] video game series, Varric Tethras in the [[Wikipedia:Dragon Age|''Dragon Age'']] video game series, and William Blazkowicz in the [[Wikipedia:Wolfenstein|''Wolfenstein'']] video game series.
