The '''Echo systems''' was a [[sector]] located in the intersection of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Interior]]<ref name="Interior" /> in grid square M-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> Among its [[star system]]s<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> was the [[Hillindor system]].<ref name="Systems" /> The [[New Republic pilot]] [[Nath Tensent]] pictured himself retiring to a [[tube-yacht]] in one of the Echo systems.<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> Tensent described his hypothetical [[death]] in the Echo systems during a game of ''[[Who? What? Where?]]'', where the participants named who first heard about their death, what killed them, and where the death took place.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Echo systems were mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the second volume of [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
