'''Exegol''' (also spelled '''Ixigul''' in ancient texts) was a dark [[desert]] [[planet]] located within the [[Unknown Regions]] that, according to legend, was the hidden redoubt world of the [[Sith]]. It was the [[throneworld]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], publicly known as [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Palpatine family|Palpatine]], and the [[Sith Eternal]] by [[35 ABY]]. It was also the [[Homeworld|birthplace]] of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]], whom Sidious created to rule the [[First Order]] while concealing [[Gender|himself]] in the shadows. Exegol was used to hide the [[Final Order]], which was a [[fleet]] amassed by the Sith Eternal, from where they planned to deploy their [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyers]] and establish a new [[Sith Empire]] for [[Darth]] Sidious. Exegol was the site of a [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the First Order and the [[Resistance]], which saw the defeat of the [[Sith Eternal]].

==Description==
[[File:ExegolSystem.png|thumb|left|250px|Kylo Ren approaches Exegol in his TIE whisper]]
Before the [[Sith]] came to Exegol, the [[planet]] was green and fertile, capable of supporting many forms of animal and plant life.<ref name="Dark Side Pocket Expert">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side Pocket Expert]]''</ref> Since its despoiling by the Sith,<ref name="TROS Junior Novel" /> Exegol was a dark, barren, and [[Stone|rocky]] planet with [[desert]] flats. Its dry conditions, combined with the rubbing of [[dust]] particles in its [[atmosphere]], created enormous static discharges that appeared as [[Lightning (phenomenon)|lightning]] strikes from the planet, which made the atmosphere too dangerous to deploy [[deflector shield]]s.<ref name="TROSVD" /> The dead<ref name="217 Launchpad">{{InsiderCite|217|Launchpad}}</ref> and desolate world was covered with enormous fissures that reached deep into its crust. These were excavated by [[Sith cultist|Sith loyalists]] who were attempting to reach a transportative [[vergence]] they believed to lie beneath the surface.<ref name="TROSVD" />

The darkness of Exegol was so extreme that the [[astromech droid]] [[R7-FNG]] was needed to be optimized to work in its environment.<ref name="R7-FNG">{{DisneyToyCite|set=Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|pack=R7-FNG|link=r7-fng-halloween-droid-factory-figure-star-wars-461012872977.html}}</ref> Exegol's nearby [[Realspace|space]] was littered with the remains of megafauna, resulting in the formation of a cloud of red [[gas]] and stardust forming around the world, which posed a navigation barrier known as the [[Red Honeycomb Zone]].<ref name="TROSVD" />

==History==
===Early History===
{{Quote|They are the Despoilers of Worlds, and have Laid to Waste once Fertile Habitats such as Korriban, Ziost, Ixigul, Asog, and Others.|Kli the Elder, ''Rammahgon''|<ref name="TROS Junior Novel" />}}
[[File:ALifeImmortal.jpg|thumb|right|224x224px|According to legend, Darth Noctyss and Darth Sanguis both sought immortality on Exegol]]
As mentioned by the [[Jedi]] writer [[Kli the Elder]] in the ''[[Rammahgon]]'', Exegol (or Ixigul, as Kli wrote it) once had a fertile environment, until the [[Sith]] established their presence there and laid waste to the planet just as they did with other annexed worlds, such as [[Moraband]], [[Ziost]], and [[Asog]].<ref name="TROS Junior Novel" /> Kli also wrote that Exegol was a place of [[Immortality|eternal life]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> Among the changes the Sith made was the creation of massive underground [[Shipyards of Exegol|shipyard]] forges in the [[Forbidden District]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles New" /> Exegol was one of the oldest Sith bastions<ref name="TROSVD" /> and one of their [[throneworld]]s.<ref name="DB Sith">{{Databank|sith|Sith}}</ref> Under the Sith, the [[Sith Citadel]] was built on Exegol for meetings and rituals to be conducted within, and Sith loyalists excavated the world's large and deep planetary fissures in hopes of reaching a transportive [[vergence]] believed to lie beneath the planet surface.<ref name="TROSVD" />

According to legend, two [[Sith Lord]]s, [[Darth Noctyss]] and [[Darth Sanguis]], reached Exegol in their quest for [[immortality]], which the former hoped to learn from the latter. Unknown to Noctyss, Sanguis had achieved immortality by transforming himself into a horrendous creature. Tricking Noctyss by not revealing himself as Darth Sanguis, Sanguis managed to be killed by Noctyss in a ritual to acquire immortality, leading Noctyss to conclude too late that Sanguis had tricked her into becoming a creature like him and freeing him from his miserable existence.<ref name="A Life Immortal" />

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|You can't beat him! He has more power than you can ''imagine''! ''[&hellip;]'' Power beyond ''your'' understanding and ''mine''. Power that only the ''Emperor'' could build.''"<br />"''Build? And where is he ''building'' this power?|Ochi and Darth Vader|<ref name="Darth Vader (2020) 7">[[Darth Vader (2020) 7|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 7]]</ref>}}
Due to its remote location and inherent veil of secrecy, the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Religion|cult]] chose Exegol as its base; a planet unknown to even the most studious of [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[historian]]s, some of whom knew about Moraband and the other Sith worlds.<ref name="TROSVD" />

[[File:SithStarDestroyersBeingConstructed-2020DarthVader11.png|thumb|left|295px|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers under construction by the Sith Eternal during the Galactic Civil War.]]
By the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Darth Sidious]] intended for Exegol to become his permanent seat of power. Seeking to unlock the secrets of immortality, his underlings began to experiment with [[cloning]] technology in a bid to extend his lifespan.<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> Sidious also proceeded to modernize the ancient [[shipyard]]s, making them highly automated<ref name="Complete Vehicles New" /> so the construction of a [[Final Order|fleet]] of [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] was underway by the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Sidious and his [[Sith cultist]]s' genetic machinations led to the creation of several artificial constructs, including [[Tanker (Exegol)|potential replacements]] for his [[Sith apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] also stored several other frail specimens within vats and kept them existing in a state of pain.<ref name="Darth Vader 11" />

These schemes were kept hidden from Vader until shortly after his [[duel on Cloud City]], when he encountered the [[Eye of Webbish Bog]] on [[Mustafar]], who gifted him with a [[Sith wayfinder]] that led to Exegol.<ref name="Vader 8">[[Darth Vader (2020) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 8]]</ref> Seeking answers, Vader forced the [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]] to accompany him on his journey, and together they passed through the [[Red Honeycomb Zone]], eventually crash-landing on the planet's surface.<ref name="Vader 10">[[Darth Vader (2020) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 10]]</ref> Vader fought through Sidious' defenses and spotted a severed hand in the cloning center. Eventually, Vader and Ochi found themselves faced with a mountain-sized [[kyber crystal]] being tormented by their Master's followers so they can [[Bleeding|corrupt]] it to the dark side and have the pieces used to power his fleet's cannons. The power of the crystal was too much for Ochi, whose eyes were burnt by its power, but Vader pressed on, intent to finally take his revenge on Sidious. Nevertheless, the [[Sith Master]] taunted Vader, telling him of his power and how he could only share it if he once again accepted his position as an apprentice. Although Vader received a vision of his son telling him he was destined to kill Sidious, Vader agreed to rejoin his Master.<ref name="Darth Vader 11" />

===Return of Panshard and Sidious===
{{Quote|The might of the Final Order will soon be ready. It will be yours if you do as I ask.|Darth Sidious, to Kylo Ren|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:SteadfastExegol-TROS.jpg|thumb|250x250px|The Sith Star Destroyers emerged from Exegol to deploy across the galaxy, but were stopped by the Resistance.]]
After Sidious' first [[death]] during the [[Battle of Endor]], the Sith loyalists continued to construct the Final Order. With each ''Xyston''-class [[Star Destroyer]] armed with an [[axial superlaser]] capable of destroying entire planets, the Sith fleet would spearhead the Sith reconquest of the galaxy.<ref name="TROSVD" /> Ochi, meanwhile, sought the path to Exegol to heal his eyes. The spirit of Sith [[Viceroy]] [[Exim Panshard]] contained with his [[Mask of Viceroy Exim Panshard|mask]] also sought the path to Exegol to be reborn, creating a darkness in the Force that Skywalker felt. Thus started the [[quest for Exegol]], in which Skywalker and [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] moved to find Ochi and prevent Panshard from bringing about the return of the Sith. While Ochi escaped their grasp, only to die in the [[Forbidden Valley]] of the planet [[Pasaana]], Skywalker was able to destroy Panshard's mask, which he incorrectly believed ended the threat of the Sith once more due to being unaware of Sidious until after he died.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />

As the threat of their return to the galaxy loomed, the [[Resistance]] mustered its forces and fought a desperate [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] against the Sith Eternal. Ultimately, with the help of the [[Citizens' Fleet]], the Resistance destroyed the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith forces]] and killed Darth Sidious.<ref name="Episode IX" /> In the months after the battle, an excavation team under historian [[Beaumont Kin]] was at work [[Exegol Excavation Project|uncovering]] the secrets of the Sith Citadel.<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

==Locations==
[[File:SithCitadel-TROS.jpg|thumb|250x250px|The Sith Citadel, the centerpiece of the Planet.]]
Built on Exegol in ancient times, the Sith Citadel was one of the earliest bastions of the Sith.<ref name="TROSVD" /> Constructed on the surface of the planet, the citadel was both above ground and subterranean in design, with the former section being a large upside-down truncated pyramid-like structure built out of black stone. Below the surface there was a large hall with massive ancient statues<ref name="Episode IX" /> carved in the likenesses of ancient Sith [[Lord]]s.<ref name="TROS Junior Novel" /> There was also an [[Amphitheater of the Sith Eternal|ampitheater]] that housed the [[Throne of the Sith]]. The citadel was destroyed during the Battle of Exegol<ref name="Episode IX" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="TROSdate">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] to one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the events of ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must take place in [[35 ABY]].</ref>
