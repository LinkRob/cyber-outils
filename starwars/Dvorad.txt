'''Dvorad''' was a [[Pantoran]] criminal who operated on the [[planet]] [[Hovun IV]]. [[Tobias Beckett]] and his [[Beckett's gang|crew]] undertook a [[Heist on Hovun IV|heist]] to acquire Dvorad's supply of blank [[Identichip|ID chips]], during which Dvorad was [[Death|killed]] by a [[Unidentified Hovun IV creature|creature]] that attacked his [[Dvorad's ship|ship]].<ref name="Beckett" />
