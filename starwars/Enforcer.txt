The '''''Enforcer''''' was a [[Star Destroyer]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. In [[14 BBY]], it was stationed at the [[planet]] [[Pii]]'s [[Imperial marshaling station Pii|Imperial marshaling station]], along with the ''[[Compliant]]'', another Star Destroyer. When a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] [[Battle near the Gulf of Tatooine|attacked]] an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[convoy]] near the [[Gulf of Tatooine]], the two Star Destroyers were dispatched to the battle. However, by the [[time]] they had arrived, the rebels had already been defeated by [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] on the ''[[Executrix]]''.

==Description==
A [[Star Destroyer]], the ''Enforcer'' was equipped with [[engine]]s and a [[hyperdrive]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

==History==
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Imperial Era]], the ''Enforcer'' was stationed at the [[planet]] [[Pii]]'s [[Imperial marshaling station Pii|Imperial marshaling station]], along with another Star Destroyer, the ''[[Compliant]]''. At that [[time]], [[Berch Teller]] and his [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] launched an [[Battle near the Gulf of Tatooine|attack]] on an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[convoy]] in a [[star system]] [[Core Worlds|Coreward]] of the [[Gulf of Tatooine]]. With the attack, Teller aimed to impede the progress of an Imperial [[battle station]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> later known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]],<ref name="Rogue One guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> which was being constructed over the planet [[Geonosis]] and was the convoy's final destination.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Due to the attack, the ''Enforcer'' and the ''Compliant'' were dispatched from Pii to assist in the battle. However, by the time the two Star Destroyers reached the system through [[hyperspace]], the rebels had just been defeated, as Imperial [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Executrix]]'' had arrived beforehand to protect the convoy. As the ''Compliant'' and ''Enforcer'' arrived, an [[Unidentified Imperial technician (Executrix)|Imperial technician]] on the ''Executrix''{{'s}} [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] informed Tarkin that two marks were reverting from hyperspace. For a moment, Tarkin thought he had fallen into a trap, but the [[technician]] then clarified that the two marks were Star Destroyers dispatched from Pii.<ref name="Tarkin" />
