'''''Cinematic Classics: Star Wars: Attack of the Clones''''' is a hardcover book by [[Tom Huddleston]] published as part of the [[Star Wars Cinematic Classics|''Star Wars'' Cinematic Classics]] series on [[September 18]], [[2025]].<ref name="Amazon" /> The book serves as a visual re-telling of {{Film|II}} and uses concept art to that end. It was released alongside the [[Cinematic Classics: Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'' Cinematic Classics]].<ref name="AmazonESB">{{Amazon|domain=co.uk|url=gp/product/1835871496|text=The Empire Strikes Back (Star Wars Cinematic Classics)|archivedate=20260306194555}}</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
'''''A retelling of ''Star Wars'' Episode II: ''Attack of the Clones'' featuring stunning development art from the original Lucasfilm concept artists.'''''<ref name="Amazon" />

''Relive the excitement of the cinematic classic ''Attack of the Clones.'' Enjoy the hit movie through this all-ages retelling accompanied by concept art from the original artists. Alongside the art, fascinating facts about each piece give an insight into the design process behind iconic characters and settings like Count Dooku and Geonosis.''<ref name="Amazon" />

==Continuity==
In multiple places, the book misspells "[[Zam Wesell]]" as "Zam Wessel".
