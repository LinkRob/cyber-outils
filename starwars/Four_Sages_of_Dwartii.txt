{{Quote|Collectors should keep an eye out for statues of the Four Sages of Dwartii&mdash;&mdash;Sistros, Braata, Faya, and Yanjun ''[sic]''&mdash;&mdash;the ancient philosophers and lawmakers who influenced the earliest laws in the Galactic Republic.|Eloc Throno, ''Traveler's Guide to Batuu''|<ref name="Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>}}
The '''Four Sages of Dwartii –''' [[Sistros Nevet]], [[Braata Danlos]], [[Faya Rodemos]], and [[Yanjon Zelmar]] – were a group of contentious [[philosopher]]s and lawgivers from the early days of the modern [[Galactic Republic]], who had great influence on its early [[Galactic Constitution|constitution]].<ref name="Complete Locations" /> [[Cowus Roont]] was remembered as the 'Fifth' Sage of Dwartii.<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

At some point, when the [[Sith]] dominated [[the galaxy]], a number of statues representing the Four Sages were made.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge">{{GEAttr|Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities}}</ref> [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] kept statues of the Four Sages, coated in [[bronzium]], in his [[Emperor's office|office and chambers]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Complete Locations" /> The statue of [[Sistros Nevet]] was made of [[neuranium]] and housed a hidden compartment where Palpatine hid [[Darth Sidious' lightsabers|his lightsabers]].<ref name="100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> [[Vice Chair]] [[Mas Amedda]] had a statue of Sistros on the top of his [[Speaker's staff]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> When Palpatine became [[Galactic Emperor]] during the aftermath of the [[Clone Wars]], he moved his base of operations to the former [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]], which he refashioned as the Imperial Palace, and took his statue of Sistros with him.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> By [[34 ABY]], statuettes of the Four Sages of Dwartii could be found in [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]], one of the many stores in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" />
