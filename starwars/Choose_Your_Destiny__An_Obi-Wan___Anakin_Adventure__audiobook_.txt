'''''Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure''''' is the audio version of the [[canon]] [[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure|book]] of the same name, written by [[Cavan Scott]] and part of the ''[[Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny]]'' series. The audiobook was released exclusively on [[Wikipedia:Amazon|Amazon]] FreeTime Unlimited in [[2019]]<ref name="Website">{{WebCite|url=https://cavanscott.com/2019/12/20/star-wars-choose-your-destiny-named-one-of-alexas-top-ten-skills-of-2019|text=Star Wars Choose Your Destiny named one of Alexa's top ten skills of 2019|archivedate=20201115001018}}</ref> and eventually added as an Alexa Skill with an Amazon Kids+ subscription.<ref name="Skill" /> An unabridged audiobook adaptation titled '''''Mission Stories: Obi-Wan & Anakin''''', and read by [[Kevin Kemp]] was released exclusively from [https://www.audible.com/ Audible] as part of their ''[[Star Wars Audio Adventures]]'' series on [[February 12]], [[2026]]. 

==Gameplay==
{{Expandsect}}
There is one canon ending and there are 23 non-canon endings. The path to the canon ending is AAABBABABBBAAA,[B,AB],A,[A,BAB],ABABA,[A,BBA],BB,[B,AB],BBBA (the brackets indicate a choice of paths). Therefore there are 16 different paths to the canon ending, taking between 52 to 60 minutes to reach the ending. The total duration of all 73 story segments is approximately 95 minutes and there are 131 total paths possible.
