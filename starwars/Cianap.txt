'''Cianap''' was a terrestrial [[planet]] in [[the galaxy]]. At some point, an advanced society existed on Cianap, but it was destroyed, leaving ruins of [[City|cities]] behind. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a [[Battle above Cianap|battle]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] occurred above Cianap, in which [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was forced to land his [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|TIE Advanced]] due to a system malfunction.<ref name="Dark Visions 1" />
