'''Dwitzel's Rats''' were a [[Mercenary/Legends|mercenary]] unit based on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]].

==History==
The Rats were formed by [[Dwitzel]], a former [[Twi'lek/Legends|Twi'lek]] [[Officer/Legends|officer]] of the [[Republic Army]], after the [[Declaration of a New Order|foundation of the Galactic Empire]]. Dwitzel was seconded by her [[Major/Legends|major]], [[Dorgat]].<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

They were specialized in punctual missions and [[Commando/Legends|commando]] raids, and had a reputation of efficacy and combativeness in all the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], the Rats were used by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]] to locate the base of a local [[Resistance (disambiguation)|resistance]] movement on the planet [[Lyta]]. After Dwitzel's discovered that an old friend, [[Colonel/Legends|Colonel]] [[Goshad]], was held prisoner in this base, she attacked the base, without knowing that it was the Empire which employed [[Goshad]] and that he was already dead, trying to take control of the base. After a brutal assault, the Rats discovered from the guerrilla forces that they were fooled, just a moment before the [[Stormtrooper/Legends|Stormtroopers]] attacked the guerrillas.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

The Rats were also hired by the [[Kuan'Mar]] to organize the resistance against the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]] on the planet [[Trillia (planet)|Trillia]]. Dwitzel dispatched ten specialists.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

==Headquarters==
The Dwitzel's Rats had no true headquarters, but maintained some buildings in the desert of [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], ten kilometers from [[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]]. There was a training facility and some warehouses, protected by advanced security systems.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

Those only served before missions and when Dwitzel convoked her troops for regular physical training.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

==Staff==
There were roughly thirty permanent members in the unit, from any [[Species/Legends|species]]. Based on Tatooine. Nobody knew their true identity, besides the unit's members and [[Scar'dfor]], a [[Sullustan/Legends|Sullustan]] local jurist.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

All the Rats had "civil" professions, which let them blend into the local population. All of them are specialists in their domain, such as explosives or electronics.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

==Equipment==
The Rats kept [[Blaster/Legends|blasters]], [[Grenade/Legends|grenades]], [[Detonite/Legends|detonite]], and more heavy weapons such as [[Repeating blaster/Legends|repeating blasters]], [[Missile launcher/Legends|missile launchers]] and projectile launchers.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

Their defensive equipment consisted of [[Armor/Legends|armor]], hazard and climate suits, and they also had some [[Speeder/Legends|speeders]] and [[Speeder bike/Legends|speeder bikes]],<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

They also had access to a large panel of communicators, [[transponder]]s, [[Sensor/Legends|sensors]] and [[scanner]]s.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

They managed to steal a good amount of material belonging to the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] including two sets of [[Zero-G assault stormtrooper|spacetrooper]] armor, a [[Gamma-class assault shuttle/Legends|''Gamma''-class assault shuttle]], two [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter/Legends|TIE/LN starfighters]], one [[PX-4 Mobile Command Base|Mobile Command Base]], three [[Compact Assault Vehicle/Wheeled PX-10]]s and two [[Swift Assault 5|Hoverscouts]].<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

==Fees and customers==
The hiring generally passed by the jurist [[Scar'dfor]], based in [[Anchorhead/Legends|Anchorhead]], who often negotiated the agreement. If they needed bigger space transports for a mission, the Rats would ask one to their employers.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

Due to the illicit nature of their material, Dwitzel would often bribe some of the controllers at the Mos Eisley Spaceport when traveling.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

They would always refuse any contract with the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] or its allies.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dwitel's Rats were created by [[Jean Balczesak]] for an ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] Gamemaster help which appeared in the French magazine ''[[Casus Belli 67]]''.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />
