'''Doctor Ames''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Scientist/Legends|scientist]] active during the height of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. He eventually came into contact with [[El Borak]] and [[Wilk (wolf)|Wilk]], a [[mutant]] [[Wolf/Legends|Wolf]] that had acquired the ability to speak. Ames recruited the two into helping him complete an insane quest.
