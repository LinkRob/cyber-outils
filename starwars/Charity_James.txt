'''Charity James''' (born [[October 13]], [[1950s#1958|1958]]) is a Canadian voice actress credited as providing additional voices to ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds]]''. While left largely uncredited, she also voiced female Revan (taunts and replies in player's commands) in ''Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic.''

She was born in Thompson, Manitoba, Canada, to a largely Scottish family. She learned to play the guitar starting from the age of eight. Her son is Ralph James, a progressive house DJ known as Maiga. Her first acting role was on [[Wikipedia:General Hostpital|''General Hospital'']] when she was in high school. Her first band, Bad Actor, became popular around this time and it was signed to [[Wikipedia:Metal Blade Records|Metal Blade Records]]. After starring in animated films, she started another group, Only Child, and won a Billboard Songwriter's Award. She has also done a lot of work in comedy, improvisational and scripted.<ref name="bio" /> Her vocals can also be heard on the Red Carpet song ''Alright'' from 2004.
