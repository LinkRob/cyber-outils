A '''fortress''' owned by [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Hego Damask]], the alter ego of [[Darth/Legends|Darth]] Plagueis, was located on the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] of [[Sojourn (moon)|Sojourn]]. It was here that Damask hosted (until [[43 BBY/Legends|43 BBY]]) annual [[Gathering (Sojourn)|Gathering]]s, which were attended by many corrupt officials, senators, criminals and cartels.

The fortress was destroyed in [[33 BBY/Legends|33 BBY]] by a [[Nuclear weapon/Legends|nuclear]] attack ordered by [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] King [[Ars Veruna/Legends|Ars Veruna]] (but masterminded by [[Palpatine/Legends|Darth Sidious]] to eliminate his [[Sith Master/Legends|Sith Master]]). Plagueis barely managed to escape the [[Bombing of Sojourn|bombardment]] and was retrieved by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] in the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt's]] starship [[Star Jewel/Legends|''Star Jewel'']].
