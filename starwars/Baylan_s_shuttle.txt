'''Baylan's shuttle'''<ref name="P2AD">{{Ahsoka|Part Two: Toil and Trouble}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> was an [[Eta-class shuttle|''Eta''-class shuttle]] used by [[Baylan Skoll]] and [[Shin Hati]] during the search for [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Part One">{{Ahsoka|Part One: Master and Apprentice}}</ref> [[Ezra Bridger]] later stole it and used it to return to the New Republic, landing it in the hanger of [[Home One]]<ref name="Part Eight" />
