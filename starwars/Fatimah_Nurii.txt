'''Fatimah Nurii''' was a [[Gender|female]] that served in the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[Captain]] and sub-[[commander]]. Born on the [[planet]] [[Commenor]], she attended the [[Imperial Naval Academy|Imperial Navy Academy]] on [[Prefsbelt IV]], originally aspiring to be a litigator for the [[Imperial Navy]]'s [[office of the Navy's barrister advocate|office of the barrister advocate]]. However, without family connections or contacts to help her get into the office, she was assigned to the Navy's [[Military Police (Imperial Navy)|Military Police]] as a common shore patrol [[officer]] for the [[garrison]] on [[Scarif]]. During her time there, Nurii learned about the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] and became increasingly troubled by the Empire's harsh brutality. She sought to find an excuse to safely leave Scarif and alert [[the galaxy]] of the superweapon.<ref name="Dawn" />

Nurii was a no-nonsense [[Imperial officer|officer]] who had a gift for leadership and was an excellent judge of character.<ref name="Dawn" />
