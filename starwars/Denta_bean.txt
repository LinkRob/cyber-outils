'''Denta beans''' were a [[Foodstuff|food]] edible by [[human]]s. They were used as a major ingredient in [[denta bean bun]]s on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> and in a [[Denta bean beverage|beverage]] that was popular on<ref name="But What Does He Eat?" /> the planet<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="But What Does He Eat?" />

In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="THR Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'', including the mention of denta beans, to [[232 BBY]].</ref> the crew of the [[transport]] [[starship]] ''[[Vessel]]'' transported crates of denta beans to Coruscant. There were so many crates that the ship's [[navigator]], [[Geode]], had trouble navigating the ship's corridors.<ref name="Into the Dark">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="QP Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the kitchen staff of the [[Theed Royal Palace]] of Naboo utilized denta beans to bake a batch of denta bean buns.<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="FACPOV ESB Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|V}} to [[3 ABY]]. As "[[But What Does He Eat?]]" takes place during those events, it must also take place in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> [[Torro Sbazzle]], [[Executive Chef]] of<ref name="But What Does He Eat?" /> the [[tibanna]] [[gas]] [[mining]] colony<ref name="AbEverything" /> [[Cloud City]], and her [[sous-chef]], [[Gersolik]], used denta beans to prepare a beverage that was to be served to the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="But What Does He Eat?" />

Denta beans were first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2020]] young-adult novel ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' by [[E. K. Johnston]] as the second installment of ''[[Star Wars: Queen's Series]]''.<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> According to Johnston, the first draft of the novel featured [[Captain]] [[Quarsh Panaka]] eating red bean buns instead of denta bean buns. However, the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] asked if Johnston could make the meal "more Star Wars-y," leading her to use [[Wookieepedia]],<ref name="Johnstonblog" /> the free-to-edit, unofficial online ''Star Wars'' encyclopedia,<ref name="Wook">{{WookCite|url=Wookieepedia|text=Wookieepedia}}</ref> to search for beans unique to the franchise.<ref name="Johnstonblog" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Denta bean/Legends|denta beans]] were first mentioned in the "[[Galaxywide NewsNets]]" section of the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 3|third issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'', published in [[August]] [[1994]].<ref name="SW Adv Journal">{{JournalCite|3|Galaxywide NewsNets}}</ref> The beans made their first full appearance in [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']],<ref name="SWG">{{SWGcite|exp=AED|type=item|Denta Beans}}</ref> a [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment.<ref name="SWGofficialrelease">{{LucasArts|url=company/release/news20030617.html|text=LucasArts Announces Star Wars Galaxies™: An Empire Divided™ Will Release June 26, 2003}}</ref>
