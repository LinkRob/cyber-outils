The '''''Explorator''''' was a [[Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser|''Dreadnought''-class heavy cruiser]] under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Commodore]] [[Visler Aqorian Korda III]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The ''Explorator'' was shot down during the [[Battle of Jakku]], killing Commodore Korda. Many [[Standard year|years]] later, a [[Mission to the Explorator|mission to the ''Explorator'']] was undertaken by [[scavenger]]s and several [[Resistance]] members to retrieve the contents of its vault.

==Characteristics==
The ''Explorator'' was a [[Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser|''Dreadnought''-class heavy cruiser]],<ref name="Jaws" /> which were manufactured by [[Rendili StarDrive]],<ref name="Starships" /> and as such was a mid-sized [[capital ship]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> The ''Explorator'' additionally had its systems modified with codes that would corrupt any unauthorized [[droid]] who tried to access them. The ''Explorator'' was also outfitted with a vault in its [[hangar]] for storing prototype equipment.<ref name="Jaws" />

==Role==
The ''Explorator'' served as a [[flagship]].<ref name="Jaws" />

==History==
===Wartime service===
[[File:CanonDreadnaughtHeavyCruiser.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Explorator'' was an example of the Empire's ''Dreadnought''-class heavy cruiser.]]
Following the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> during which [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] was [[Death|killed]] and the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] was destroyed, the Empire fragmented into loyalist forces and multiple [[Imperial Remnants]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> [[Commodore]] [[Visler Aqorian Korda III]] created his own [[Visler Korda's faction|faction]] on [[Rekkana]] before eventually possessing the ''Explorator''. Korda became obsessed with experimental weaponry and droids, modifying the ''Explorator'' as he pursued his experiments in an attempt to turn the tide against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Eventually, the ''Explorator'' was deployed to [[Jakku]],<ref name="Jaws" /> where [[Counselor to the Empire]] [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]] and the Imperial loyalist forces had entrenched in a final stand against the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> Korda studied the [[Jakku|Jakkuvian]] [[nightwatcher worm]] and created [[Nightwatcher droid|a prototype]] in its image which he could then control with his mind through a special [[control circlet]]. Before Korda could unleash his [[Unidentified nightwatcher droid|prototype droid]] during the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Jaws" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> he was killed when the ''Explorator'' was shot down and crashed on the surface.<ref name="Jaws" />

===Wrecked on Jakku===
Between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Jaws Date">The events of ''[[The Jaws of Jakku]]'' take place between the events of {{Film|VIII}} and {{Film|IX}}, which corresponds to between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Jakkuvian]] [[scavenger]] [[Velorie Sarcon]] received a contracted to retrieve the contents of the vault within the ''Explorator'', which was wrecked in the sands north of the [[Kelvin Ravine]]. The local [[Leesa Chaffni]] overheard Sacron's mention of the vault and had hoped to secure medical supplies for [[Leesa Chaffni's father|her father]]. She became acquainted with [[Resistance]] paramilitary members [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], [[Finn]] and [[BB-8]] who prevented Sacron from confronting her after Sacron realized Chaffni learned of the vault. The parties then undertook a [[Mission to the Explorator|mission]] to the ''Explorator'', and joined together to reach the vessel's vault. There, they encountered [[First Order officer]] [[Dihun]], who had contracted Sacron, and her [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]].<ref name="Jaws" />

[[File:StarshipGraveyard-CGSWG.png|thumb|right|250px|The ''Explorator'' was one of countless wrecks leftover from the Battle of Jakku that laid upon the surface.]]
During the confrontation, the nightwatcher droid prototype was released from the vault and attacked everyone in the hangar. Rey, using the [[control circlet]], briefly controlled the rogue droid with enough time for them to overwrite Korda's corrupting code and calm the droid, allowing the Resistance members and scavengers to escape the ''Explorator'' while it sank beneath the sand dunes. Chaffni's efforts were not in vain, however, as Sacron retrieved some medical equipment in the ''Explorator''{{'s}} [[medical bay]], allowing them to help Chaffni's father.<ref name="Jaws" />
