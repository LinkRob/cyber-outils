'''Denuo Novo''' is a company that produces officially licensed ''[[Star Wars]]'' helmets and apparel replicas. They are a subsidiary of National Entertainment Collectibles Association (NECA) alongside Rubies II, Kidrobot, Joseph Enterprises, [[WizKids Games]], and LootCrate<ref name="Denuonovo" />
