An '''Akiva thief vest''' was a type of [[Clothing|vest]] that used by [[Thief|thieves]] the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]] to help blend in with the [[jungle]] environment. The vest was [[color|blue]] with a bronze colored circle on its upper-right chest. It included a dark green shirt, a brown [[bracer]], teal arm-wraps and a pair of mismatched [[glove]]s, one blue and one brown.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Pyke Syndicate]] had one of the vests stored behind an [[Ray shield|energy barrier]] at their [[Pyke Crystal Mine|crystal mine]] in Akiva's [[Stormhollow]] region.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] deactivated the barrier by [[slicer|slicing]] into a [[Computer|terminal]] at the crystal [[mine]] and stole the vest.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
An Akiva thief vest appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, the vest awards the player's with a large amount of [[adrenaline]] when they perform stealth takedowns. It also increases the [[stun]] shot recharge amount awarded to the player after they perform melee takedowns or finishers.<ref name="Outlaws" />
