'''Flark''' was a vulgar [[List of phrases and slang|expletive]] used in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Survivors" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">According to ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'', the [[Guardian Protocols]] were implemented in [[229 BBY]]. According to [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|''Shadows of Starlight'' 1]], this happened one week after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places in [[230 BBY]]. The events of ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'', including the [[Expansion of the Stormwall]], take place exactly one year after the Fall of [[Starlight Beacon]]. The events of "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Friend or Foe?]]" and "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Survivors]]" take place one month after the Expansion of the [[Stormwall]], so they must be set in [[228 BBY]].</ref> after being punched in the [[Limb|face]] by the [[human]] [[Joss Adren]], the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] member [[Elping Tsoo]] used the cuss word to explain the pain he felt.<ref name="Survivors" />
