'''Camera Droids''' were [[cam droid]]s used to film and broadcast the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition live. They were deployed during and between matches, and would fly over the arenas, pausing at intervals to slowly pan and capture footage.<ref name="SWH" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
These [[Droid|droids]] first appeared in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', which was developed by [[NaturalMotion]]'s [[BossAlien]]<ref name="SWH" /> and initially published by [[Zynga]] on [[November 16]], [[2021]] as a [[Wikipedia:Soft launch|soft launch]]<ref name="Soft">{{Twitter|ZyngaStarWars|status/1460656552734605317|[[Zynga]] Star Wars|date=2021-11-16|quote=We are excited to announce Star Wars: Hunters is now entering soft launch and is available on Android for players in India, Philippines, Malaysia & Indonesia.|archivedate=20220625084625|archivefile=File:ZyngaStarWars-SoftLaunchDate-Twitter.png}}</ref> before the global release on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Global">{{SWYouTube|4dZcq6m3y40|Star Wars: Hunters Launch Date Reveal Trailer}}</ref> They were designed by concept artist [[Clint Young]].<ref name="Young">{{ArtStation|subdomain=clintyoung|url=projects/wr4loX|name=[[Clint Young]]|text=Star Wars Hunters: Mustafar Vis Dev- exploration|date=2025-05-20|quote=Previs for Star Wars: Hunters-I was Principal Concept Artist on the Natural Motion / Zynga project. Designed and concepted all environment biomes / arenas also characters / weapons and various call outs and POIs.}}</ref>
