The [[Ohnaka Gang]] carried out the '''attack on the ''Steady On''''' during the [[Clone Wars]] to capture the [[starship]] ''[[Steady On]]'' and its cargo. Among the vessel's cargo was a [[Unidentified mammal statuette|mammal statuette]] desired by the [[merchant]] [[Sheb Valaad]]. Requesting six [[pilot]]s to protect the ''Steady On'' in return for payment, the vessel was protected in part by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Asajj Ventress]], who failed to stop the Ohnaka Gang's raid and was left the sole survivor of their attack. She was picked up by the [[Blood Bone Order]], which had been planning its own raid on the ''Steady On'' and thus wanted to [[Skirmish on Florrum (Asajj Ventress)|reclaim its lost target]] upon learning of the Ohnaka Gang's actions.<ref name="Kindred Spirits" />
