The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''233 BBY'''<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]],<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> also known as '''3044 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''217 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7744''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*Just as they have in previous, recent [[Standard year|years]],<ref name="ItD">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref> the [[Nihil]] continue to grow in prominence as they sweep through the [[Outer Rim Territories]] with their [[Path]]s and raids.<ref name="Date note">''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' dates the launch of the [[Starlight Beacon]], depicted in ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'', to 232 [[Standard year|years]] before the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. This corresponds to [[232 BBY]] in the [[Galactic standard calendar|dating system]] used by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. As ''Light of the Jedi'' depicts the [[Dedication of the Starlight Beacon|launch]], all of the novel's events must take place around that year, including [[Marchion Ro]] taking full control of the Nihil, which occurs between the [[Battle of Kur]] and the Beacon's dedication. Furthermore, as ''Light of the Jedi'' notes the Nihil grew in prominence in the months before the launch, they must have grown in prominence between 233 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 232 BBY.</ref>
*The [[Grand Renewal]] of the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]] is spearheaded by the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="LivingForceDate">{{LivingForceDate}} Additionally, the events of the novel coincide with the bicentennial anniversary of the [[Grand Renewal]], which means it must have occurred in 233 BBY.</ref>
