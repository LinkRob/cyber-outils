[[Vice General]] '''Adambo''' was an [[Imperial officer|officer]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|armed forces]] who was captured by a [[Norra Wexley's team|team]] of [[New Republic]] [[Imperial hunter]]s led by [[pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]] sometime between the [[Rebellion on Akiva|rebellion]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]] and the team's [[Capture of Perwin Gedde|mission]] to the planet [[Vorlag]]<ref name="LD" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> After the vice [[general]] was successfully captured, the team put Adambo's image up on a board in their [[SS-54 assault ship|SS-54 gunship]] the ''[[Halo]]'', which included images of all the Imperial targets they had successfully captured.<ref name="LD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Adambo was mentioned in the novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] and released in [[2016]].<ref name="LD" />
