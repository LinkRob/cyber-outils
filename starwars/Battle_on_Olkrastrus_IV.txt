A '''battle''' took place against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' control over the [[planet]] [[Olkrastrus IV]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It ended with the local Separatist civilian government ceding power to the [[Galactic Republic]], which became the [[Galactic Empire]]. However, Separatist military forces retreated into the planet's wilderness and waged war as [[Olkrastrus IV holdouts|holdouts]].<ref name="Mask of Fear" />
