'''Christopher Leith Evans''',<ref name="Chris Evans Website" /> occasionally credited as '''Chris Evans''',<ref name="RotJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> is an [[Wikipedia:American|American]] artist who worked on creating matte paintings for several films,<ref name="Chris Evans Website" /> including [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]<ref name="RotJ" /> and ''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]''.<ref name="Ewoks">''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]''</ref>

==Biography==
Chris Evans was born in [[1950s#1954|1954]] in [[Wikipedia:Bremerton, Washington|Bremerton]], [[Wikipedia:Washington (state)|Washington]]. After moving to Santa Monica, California, he would study at the [[Wikipedia:University of California|University of California]] for several years under artists such as [[Wikipedia:William Brice|William Brice]], [[Wikipedia:James Valerio|James Valerio]], and [[Wikipedia:James Doolin|James Doolin]], graduating with a Master of Fine Arts degree in 1980. He would later find a job as a matte painter for [[Industrial Light & Magic]],<ref name="Chris Evans Website" /> where he would work on [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]<ref name="RotJ" /> and ''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]'',<ref name="Ewoks">''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]''</ref> for which he received an [[Wikipedia:Emmy Awards|Emmy Awards]] in [[1985]] under the category "[[Wikipedia:Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Special Visual Effects|Outstanding Special Visual Effects]]".<ref name="Emmy">{{WebCite|url=https://www.emmys.com/awards/nominees-winners/1985/outstanding-special-visual-effects|text=Outstanding Special Visual Effects Nominees / Winners 1985|work=[[Wikipedia:Academy of Television Arts & Sciences|Television Academy]]'s [https://www.emmys.com official website]|archivedate=20241127094144}}</ref>
