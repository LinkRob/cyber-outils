'''Coalstreak Squadron''' was a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] formed prior to the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]].<ref name="TVD" /> [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Nien Nunb]] was a member of the Coalstreak Squadron where he flew [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s.<ref name="BuildXwing17" /> Nunb wore Coalstreak Squadron stripes on his helmet when he later flew under [[Red Squadron (Resistance)|Red Squadron]] for the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TVD" />
