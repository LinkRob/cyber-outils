'''Chem-receptors''' were a type of [[sensor]]s that gave [[droid]]s the equivalent of a lifeform's oflactory sense. Although a droid could not actually smell, its chem-receptors allowed it to know the overall chemical composition of the air around them.<ref name="Beware the Power of the Dark Side">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref>
