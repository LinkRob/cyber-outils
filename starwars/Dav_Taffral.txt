'''Dav Taffral''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and later became a member of the nascent [[New Republic]]. [[Standard month|Months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], Dav returned to the [[Taffral house]] on the [[planet]] [[Saleucami]] to have a [[Taffral family|family]] dinner with his [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]]-loyalist brother, [[Webb Taffral]], and his [[Parent|father]], [[Glen Taffral]]. Webb gave up his brother's location to [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]], prompting Dav to flee with his father's assistance shortly after [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Starfighter|fighters]] arrived over their home.

==Biography==
{{Quote|You believe in what you're doing, Dav?''"<br />"''I&hellip;do.''"<br />"''Fine. Good enough for me. I hope you're right. Best run now. Go out the back window. Take the speeder bike in the barn.|Glen Taffral and Dav Taffral|audio=RunDavTaffral.flac|<ref name="Aftermath" />}}
[[File:Redstarbird.svg|thumb|left|180px|Dav Taffral served as a member of the Rebel Alliance (emblem pictured).]]
Dav Taffral was a [[Gender|male]] individual who joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] sometime before the [[rebellion on Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> and later became a member of the [[Declaration of a New Republic|newly established]] [[New Republic]]. His [[Parent|father]], [[Glen Taffral]], was a [[farmer]], while his [[Dav and Webb Taffral's mother|mother]] had [[Death|died]] prior to the events on [[Akiva]]. Dav had an older brother, [[Webb Taffral]], who lived on the [[planet]] [[Saleucami]] in the [[Taffral house]] along with their father.<ref name="Aftermath" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], Dav returned to the [[Taffral family|family]] home on Saleucami to have dinner with his father and brother. The three ate in relative silence, which was broken by Dav asking for a [[Foodstuff|food dish]] to be passed to him. The [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]]-loyalist Webb refused to oblige, insulting Dav about his rebel affiliation. The brothers argued, and the ensuing brawl was broken up by their father throwing a [[salad]] bowl at the wall. Glen ordered them to sit and explained that, in the end, the side they should pick is family because governments would come and go.<ref name="Aftermath" />

Glen was soon interrupted by the sound of [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Starfighter|fighters]] arriving over the Taffral residence. Webb reluctantly revealed that he had given up Dav's location to [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]], and his father quickly [[stun]]ned him with a [[blaster]]. Glen asked Dav to affirm his belief in what he was doing. Upon receiving confirmation, he told his son to sneak out the back [[Viewport|window]] and use the [[speeder bike]] stored in the homestead's barn to escape. Dav asked what his father would do, and Glen replied that he would tell the Imperials that Dav had shot him. The older man then stunned himself, and Dav quickly fled while Imperials broke down the home's door.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I'm old enough to remember when the Republic shot itself right in the knee. It wasn't taken over by the Empire. It ''became'' the Empire slowly, surely, not overnight but over years and decades. ''[&hellip;]'' You remember that.''"<br />"''Pop. It won't be like that this time.|Glen Taffral and Dav Taffral|audio=TaffralNR.flac|<ref name="Aftermath" />}}
Dav Taffral had a slightly more scruffy and lean appearance than his brother, Webb. He strongly believed in the principles of the Rebel Alliance and defended the revolutionary organization to Webb, who considered the rebels to be [[terrorist]]s. Dav kept his composure longer than his brother when tensions between their conflicting loyalties ensued, although he was willing to brawl with his brother after several heated exchanges. While his father, Glen, believed that the New Republic could become corrupt just like the [[Galactic Republic|Republic of old]], Dav believed that would not happen.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Equipment==
The rebel fled from Imperial forces using a speeder bike and also took his father's blaster with him for defense.<ref name="Aftermath" />
