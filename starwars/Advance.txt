The '''''Advance''''' was a vehicle used by the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Following his [[time]] serving aboard the vehicle ''[[Mandate]]'', [[Junior Lieutenant|Lieutenant Junior Grade]] [[Gideon Hask]] was posted to the ''Advance'' as a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] in its [[TIE series|TIE]] [[starfighter]] [[squadron]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" />

By the end of his tenure on the vehicle, Hask had served on a [[Star Destroyer]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the section of ''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]'' that includes Hask's induction into [[Inferno Squad]] to [[0 ABY]].</ref> three [[Standard day|days]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]], Hask left the ''Advance'' when he was summoned to<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] by [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]], after which the [[lieutenant]] was drafted into the [[Imperial Special Forces]] group [[Inferno Squad]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Advance'' was mentioned in the [[2017]] novel ''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]'', which was written by [[Christie Golden]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
