'''Black Spire Day''' was a holiday celebrated at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]],<ref name="GEStaff" /> commemorating the founding of the outpost. Locals would decorate the outpost with colorful ribbons and tarps while celebrating with parades and feasts. It would occasionally end with a [[fireworks]] display.<ref name="TG">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Black Spire Day was created for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at Disneyland Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="GEStaff" /> It is the in-universe name for celebrations held at Disney parks on "Star Wars Day" ([[May 4|May the Fourth]]).
