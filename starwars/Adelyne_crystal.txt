'''Adelyne crystals''' were mined by [[Whool (Path of the Open Hand)|Whool]] and his [[Whool's family|family]] on their [[Whool's home planet|homeworld]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="TEDP" /> Sometime before [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Tales of Enlightenment: A Different Perspective]]" take place after the [[Path of the Open Hand]] arrived on [[Jedha]], which ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' establishes was after the [[death]] of [[Kevmo Zink]]. "Tales of Enlightenment: A Different Perspective" also takes place before [[Keth Cerepath]]'s death during the [[Battle of Jedha]]. As ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates both Zink's death and the battle to [[382 BBY]], the events of "Tales of Enlightenment: A Different Perspective" must take place in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> an Adelyne crystal mine collapsed, but Whool's brother [[Grael (Whool)|Grael]] used [[the Force]] to save everyone. Shortly after that, another mine collapsed taking the life of over thirty miners.<ref name="TEDP" />
