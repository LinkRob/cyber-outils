'''Fire lizards''' were a type of [[lizard]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] corralled a bloom of fire lizards on the [[planet]] [[Targusian]].<ref name="Wayseeker" /> Around [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{WayseekerDate}}</ref> while Rwoh was on the planet [[Cerifisis]], she remembered corralling the fire lizards and felt that it was one of the simple acts she had done within the past few [[Standard year|years]] to improve people's lives.<ref name="Wayseeker" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fire lizards were mentioned in ''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]'', a [[2025]] novel written by [[Justina Ireland]].<ref name="Wayseeker" />
