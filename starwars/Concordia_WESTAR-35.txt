The '''Concordia WESTAR-35''' was a pair of [[WESTAR-35 blaster pistol|WESTAR-35]] [[blaster pistol]]s owned by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal MT-97|standard blaster pistols]]. Unlike his usual [[MT-97]]s,<ref name="Hunters" /> these [[WESTAR line|WESTARs]] harkened back to the Mandalorian warriors [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] to Concordia, who would eventually lead the [[Death Watch]].<ref name="The Mandalore Plot">{{TCW|The Mandalore Plot}}</ref>

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Concordia WESTAR-35 was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Aran Tal's Clan Tal MT-97, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Concordia Tradition]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
