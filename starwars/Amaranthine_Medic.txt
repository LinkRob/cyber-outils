The '''Amaranthine Medic''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Zaina]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Rebel Veteran|standard costume]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Amaranthine Medic was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Zaina, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Amaranthine Blaster]].<ref name="Hunters" />
