'''Corulag''' was a [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] that was influential in the [[Galactic Republic]] helping the world [[Eriadu]] out of its debts and putting it onto the [[The galaxy|galactic]]-[[Star map|map]]. Under the [[Galactic Empire]], it was the site of an [[Imperial Academy]], the [[Corulag Military Academy]], which caught some success from its strategic pairing of training instructors, like [[Commandant]]s [[Angor]] and [[Bedgar]].

Corulag was listed at the location of arrest for the criminal "[[Jyn Erso|Liana Hallik]]," who was arrested in the [[Five Points system]] in [[1 BBY]]. Around that [[Standard year|year]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] went to Corulag when investigating the Empire's [[kyber crystal]] shipments, leaving for the [[Ring of Kafrene]] when he was summoned by an [[Tivik|informant]] of his.

After the Empire's [[Battle of Jakku|fall]] in [[5 ABY]], [[New Republic]] veteran [[Chass na Chadic]] resided in the [[city]] of [[Curamelle]] on Corulag, attending therapy for a while. She moved on the planet to [[Crullov]], but her life as a veteran ended when her building was raided by security forces, Chadic eventually moving on to the astronomical object [[Spirana]].

==Description==
Corulag was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Edge" /> located in [[the Interior]] portion<ref name="Interior" /> of the [[Core Worlds]]. Situated at the coordinates L-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it lay along the [[Perlemian Trade Route]]<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> [[super-hyperroute]]<ref name="BMF 48">{{BuildFalconCite|48|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> between the<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> planets<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> [[Chandrila]] and [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Galaxy Map" />

==History==
===High status===
{{Quote|The Imperial Military Academy on Corulag appears to have produced a number of well-regarded Imperial graduates. Much of this appears to be rooted in their tendency to pair training instructors based on their complementary abilities.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:Eriadu-Bad_Batch-The_Summit.png|thumb|left|300px|Corulag was instrumental in the industrial development of Eriadu (pictured).]]
Corulag was a notable member world in the [[Galactic Republic]] in the time after the [[Defeat of the Sith|fall]] of the [[Sith]]<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> in [[1032 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the planet having influence in the Republic's [[Galactic Senate]]. During that time, entrepreneurs from the world saved the settlers of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] planet [[Eriadu]] from their debts to the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]], which had given loans for the development of Eriadu's [[lommite]] [[mining]]. Corulag's senatorial influence also led to the [[Hydian Way]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> a [[Hyperspace route|super-hyperroute]]<ref name="BMF 48" /> that was originally founded in [[3705 BBY]],<ref name="BMF 29">{{BuildFalconCite|29|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> being routed through Eriadu [[Realspace|space]], putting the Outer Rim world on the [[The galaxy|galactic]]-[[Star map|map]]. The Core World entrepreneurs that helped Eriadu in turn forced the planet to increase its lommite supply and demanded much of the profits. This transformed large sections of Eriadu into polluted industrial zones and poverty stricken cities.<ref name="Tarkin" />

The Republic [[Proclamation of the New Order|became]] the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> and Corulag was the site of an [[Imperial Academy]], the [[Corulag Military Academy]]. While considered less prestigious than academies like the [[Raithal Academy]],<ref name="Rebel" /> Corulag still produced a number of well-regarded Imperial graduates. Such success was in part due to their tactic of pairing training instructors based on abilities they had in common. The match-up of [[Commandant]]s [[Angor]] and [[Bedgar]], noted respectively for relevant and robust combat strategy classes and the intricacies of military administration, exemplified this.<ref name="Rise" />

===Seedy activities===
{{Quote|As ordered, I have been investigating reports that the Empire is transporting kyber crystals to Patriim, Horuz, and other remote star systems. Those investigations led me to Corulag and then to Coruscant, where I heard rumors that the Empire was concealing shipments of kyber crystals in convoys of civilian freighters. Further investigation revealed shipments were arriving on Kafrene from the direction of the Unknown Regions before being sent to an unknown destination.|Cassian Andor, in a report to Airen Cracken|<ref name="Rebel Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Erso-arrest-record-SV.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Corulag was listed as the location of arrest for Jyn Erso]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the criminal [[Jyn Erso]], who used the alias "Liana Hallik," was arrested by Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Bethava Rocwyn]] in the [[Five Points system]],<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref> being [[Prisoner|incarcerated]] in the [[Imperial Detention Center & Labor Camp LEG-817|Wobani Labor Colony]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> Her arrest record, designated file / 7976.994.5., listed the location of her arrest to Corulag,<ref name="Scum and Villany">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> her intake record at the Wobani Labor Camp doing the same. The latter file was eventually included in ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', an archive of non-electronic documents compiled by rebel [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

Around that same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="1 BBY">The report mentioning Corulag in ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'' is set shortly before the [[Battle of Scarif]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|intelligence branch]] probed reports that the Empire was transporting [[kyber crystal]]s to the [[Horuz system|Horuz]] and [[Patriim system]]s, [[General]] [[Airen Cracken]] assigning [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] to investigate these. Andor's investigations led him to Coruscant<ref name="Rebel Dossier" /> and to Corulag.<ref name="Rogue One novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> At those worlds, he learned of rumors that the Empire was using convoys of civilian freighters to conceal kyber shipments, some passed through the [[Ring of Kafrene]] [[Merchant|trading]] outpost from the direction of the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Rebel Dossier" />

In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the aspiring [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]] spoke with the [[Force spirit|Force ghost]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] while visiting<ref name="Odyssey" /> the [[swamp]] planet<ref name="DagobahDB">{{Databank|dagobah|Dagobah}}</ref> [[Dagobah]]. In that conversation, Kenobi told Skywalker that the fate of Corulag and many other planets relied on a final confrontation between the latter and the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref>

===Post-Imperial era===
{{Quote|For example, Commandant Angor was noted for his relevant and robust combat strategy classes, and often worked closely with Commandant Bedgar who instructed officer cadets on the intricacies of military administration. Together the pair were able to produce well-rounded officers who could both act in combat and undertake the bureaucracy that was inherent in leadership positions.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:ChassNaChadic-TIEFighter3.png|thumb|left|250px|Chass na Chadic resided for some time on Corulag after the Empire's fall]]
After the [[Battle of Jakku|fall]] of the Empire<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Chass na Chadic]], a veteran [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] of the [[New Republic]], resided in the [[city]] of [[Curamelle]] on Corulag. There, she went to therapy appointments at a [[medcenter]], favouring a [[Unidentified bearded snake-woman therapist|bearded snake-woman therapist]]. However, Chadic gave up on therapy after she missed an appointment and the snake-woman could not reschedule for a [[Standard month|month]]. Remaining on Corulag, she eventually relocated to [[Crullov]], another location on the world, for a cheaper rent, living in a closet sized apartment in a complex rife with [[scrapper]]s and [[death stick]] addicts. One [[Standard day|night]], though, Chadic got [[Alcohol|drunk]] enjoying the bands at the [[Little Orto street fair]] and returned to security forces raiding her building. This brought her life as a New Republic veteran to an end, and she eventually relocated to the astronomical object [[Spirana]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

[[Imperial Archives]] kept a section on the "Corulag Imperial Military Academy," including the file "Combined Officer's Curriculum 27" that covered the pairing of Commandants Angor and Bedgar. Having read the file, [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] summarized the Imperial Academy's curricular success in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[35 ABY]]<ref name="35 ABY">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|When I left Corulag to&mdash;seek my fortune, yeah?&mdash;my parents disowned me. Haven't been back since.|Agias Rikton, to Soran Keize, on leaving Corulag|<ref name="Alphabet">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>}}
Corulag was the [[homeworld]] of [[Baron]] [[Lero Danthe]].<ref name="New Dawn" /> The [[human]] [[Agias Rikton]] was also brought up on the world taking more of a liking to his [[Agias Rikton's grandfather|grandfather]], a Republic veteran, as he struggled to get along with his [[parent]]s, who ultimately cut ties with him when he left to join the Empire.<ref name="Alphabet" /> [[Merei Spanjaf]] played [[Kicker (grav-ball)|kicker]] in the [[grav-ball]] youth bracket on Corulag.<ref name="Edge" /> By the time she left the planet for [[Lothal]], it had been significantly changed by the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Secret" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corulag was first mentioned in [[John Jackson Miller]]'s [[2014]] novel [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']].<ref name="New Dawn" /> It originated in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in [[West End Games]]' [[1992]] roleplaying supplement ''[[Heir to the Empire Sourcebook]]''.<ref name="Heir Sourcebook">''[[Heir to the Empire Sourcebook]]''</ref>

The [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]] establishes that Cassian Andor went to Corulag after Coruscant. While on Corulag, Andor received a message from [[Tivik]], his informant in the extremist rebel cell known as the [[Partisans]], which summoned the rebel operative away to meet with the informant on the Ring of Kafrene.<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> However, ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'' instead stated a different order, that he went to Corulag first and then Coruscant, and didn't contact Tivik until he arrived on Kafrene.<ref name="Rebel Dossier" /> The ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' episode "[[Jedha, Kyber, Erso]]" later established that Andor was on Yavin 4 when he received the message from Tivik.<ref name="Andor">{{Andor|Jedha, Kyber, Erso}}</ref>
