'''Bodach'i''' were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]], pale-skinned [[reptile]]s. They had a tail and scaly skin. Following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Bodach'i showed open defiance towards the regulations established by that [[New Order|new regime]]. Their [[planet]] was invaded by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] and the entire Bodach'i species was [[Slavery|enslaved]] as punishment. Several Bodach'i were forced to work in the [[spice]] mines of [[Kerev Doi]]'s [[Lower-Sea Province]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />
