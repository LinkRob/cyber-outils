The '''Allyuen spaceport''' was a [[spaceport]] on [[Allyuen]],<ref name="Contact" /> a world located in the [[Anoat sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Location">The game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'' allows the player to send members of their crew from within the Anoat sector to Allyuen during a point in the story where no ships can leave the sector due to the Iron Blockade. As such, Allyuen must be located within the Anoat sector for the crew members to reach it.</ref> that was [[garrison]]ed by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Assault" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
During the [[Iron Blockade]], which was established by the Empire in an attempt to prevent news of their crushing defeat by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] at the [[Battle of Endor]] from reaching the Anoat [[sector]]'s populace,<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> an individual contacted [[the Smuggler]] and asked to meet in the Allyuen spaceport. The contact was interested in making a more formal alliance with them, but first needed aid in removing a [[Homing beacon|tracking device]] from their person. The Smuggler sent a crew member to assist the individual,<ref name="Contact" /> also deploying an additional five to directly assault the [[Allyuen Garrison]], which, having seen little action, had grown complacent.<ref name="Assault" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Allyuen spaceport was first mentioned in the [[canon]] mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was developed by [[Kabam]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms and released in [[2015]].<ref name="Uprising" />
