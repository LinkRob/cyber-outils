'''Cloudships''' were a class of [[starship]] utilized by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization. The [[Cloud (rank)|Cloud]] [[Dent (Nihil)|Ultident Margrona]] utilized two Cloudships in a [[Raid on Elphrona|raid]] on [[Elphrona]], one of which was destroyed by [[Jedi Master]] [[Loden Greatstorm]]. The other Cloudship was damaged, but escaped, carrying the [[prisoner]]s [[Ottoh Blythe|Ottoh]] and [[Ronn Blythe]] aboard. [[Jedi]] [[Indeera Stokes]] rescued Ronn, and Greatstorm boarded the damaged Cloudship to rescue Ottoh. The Nihil [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] entered the Cloudship, kidnapping both Greatstorm and Blythe. A number of Cloudships were present at the [[Battle of Kur]] against the [[Galactic Republic]], but were easily destroyed when hit.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
