"'''Divide and Conquer'''" was a [[Starfighter combat|dogfighting]] strategy in which [[starfighter]] [[pilot]]s would take advantage of an enemy pilot's lack of discipline to separate them from the rest of their group and destroy them. [[Fenn Rau]] once warned [[Kay Karno]], a.k.a. [[Phoenix Six]], that her habit of breaking formation might cause her to become a victim of that strategy.<ref name="Divide and Conquer">{{RebelsAnimationCite|2|Divide and Conquer}}</ref>
