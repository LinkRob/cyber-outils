'''2020 Topps Women of ''Star Wars''''' is a set of cards released by [[Topps]] that focuses on the women of ''[[Star Wars]]''. It was released on [[March 25]], [[2020]] and consisted of 100 cards in the base set, along with a variety of inserts.

==Publisher's summary==
''The first trading card release to celebrate the iconic and important women in the Star Wars galaxy''
