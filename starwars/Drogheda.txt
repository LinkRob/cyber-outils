'''Drogheda''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Solo Tales from Vandor" /> located in the [[Drogheda system]], within the [[Teraab sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]] regions.<ref name="Location" /> It was situated in grid square Q-11 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> According to the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], his [[Lando Calrissian's father|father]] had prevented him from [[Marriage|marrying]] into a [[mining]] fortune on Drogheda. After learning Calrissian's story about the celestial body, the [[bartender]] [[Tivoche Bilure]] included the tale in his [[Midnight's book|journal]].<ref name="Solo Tales from Vandor" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drogheda was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] replica journal ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Solo Tales from Vandor" /> The [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Drogheda/Legends|Drogheda]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via the [[Star Wars (1977) 83|eighty-third]] issue of the [[Marvel Comics]] series [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']]. The [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] issue was written by [[Linda Grant]], illustrated by [[Bob McLeod]],<ref name="SW83">[[Star Wars (1977) 83|''Star Wars'' (1977) 83]]</ref> and published on [[February 14]], [[1984]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53873/star_wars_1977_83|text=Star Wars (1977) #83}}</ref>
