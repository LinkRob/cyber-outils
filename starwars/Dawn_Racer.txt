The '''Dawn Racer''' was a costume owned by [[Dizzy (Ugnaught)|Dizzy]], the [[Ugnaught]] member of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]], that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Wild Racer|standard costume]]. It featured a [[Color|blue]], yellow, white, gray, and dark brown color scheme, including yellow triangle designs on the [[boot]]s, as well as yellow, black, and brown [[goggles]]. Dizzy noted that the color scheme for this costume was a bit too cheerful for him.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dawn Racer was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Early Dawn]].<ref name="Hunters" />
