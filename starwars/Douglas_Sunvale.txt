'''Douglas Sunvale''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> who served as the [[marshal]] of the [[Temple Outpost (Port Haileap)|Jedi outpost]] at [[Port Haileap]].<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" />

Sunvale was present in the [[Hetzal system]] in the aftermath of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]. He had a [[Padawan]] learner, the [[human]] [[Imri Cantaros]]. He also worked with the young [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] at Port Haileap. He used the nickname "Vern" to refer to her, although she privately disliked it. At his request, Rwoh brought young inventor [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]], who had been sent there from [[Coruscant]], to accompany a party traveling to the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]]. Starros was the same age as [[Honesty Weft]], the son of the [[Dalnan]] [[Ambassador]] [[Weft]], who was also part of the delegation. Sunvale hoped that the two would become friends and soften the Dalnans' view of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

Sunvale [[Death|died]] during the [[Attack on the Steady Wing|attack on the ''Steady Wing'']], after ensuring that Rwoh, Honesty, Avon, and Cantaros could escape the doomed starship. Cantaros would eventually become apprenticed to Rwoh after the group was rescued from [[Wevo]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" />
