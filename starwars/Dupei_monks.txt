The '''Dupei monks''' were a group of [[monk]]s made up of the [[Dupei]] [[species]]. The Dupei monks were a rare sight in [[the galaxy]], but one group existed on [[Nameel]]. The Dupei monks possessed outlawed weapons that a nearby [[Trandoshan]] [[Vurrsk's tribe|tribe]] attempted to buy, but the monks [[Thief|stole]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] from the tribe. The Trandoshans retaliated, and the [[Jedi Order]] sent [[Eno Cordova]] and [[Cere Junda]] to settle the issue. Cordove spoke with the monks, learning that they had stolen from the tribe.<ref name="Dark Temple 1" />
