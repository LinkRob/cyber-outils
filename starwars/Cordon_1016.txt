'''Cordon 1016''' was located on [[level 11-6]] at the [[Benelex Marshal Service]]'s [[Gulhadar prison|prison]] on the [[planet]] [[Gulhadar III]].<ref name="Han Chewie 7" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]], along with the [[Aqualish]] [[Ponda Baba]] and [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]], had [[dioxis]] [[gas]] canisters moved to the cordon and then leaked in the area as part of [[Phaedra]]'s plan to escape the prison. The leak caused a mass evacuation of all personnel from the [[prison]], and Kanata parted ways from Baba and Evazan so they could all escape.<ref name="Han Chewie 7" />
