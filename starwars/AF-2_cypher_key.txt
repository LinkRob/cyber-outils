The '''AF-2 Cypher Key''', also known as the '''AF-2 cypher''', was an advanced type of anti-security device that could be used to bypass complex security systems or override alarms. The device was a flat octagon with one pair of lengthened sides that each had a circular marking about a third of the way up their length. A line also ran up the center of the device for about two thirds of its length, terminating in another circle.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Rodian]] [[merchant]] [[Namda Eaakee]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]] possessed an AF-2 Cypher Key that they were willing to trade for six units of [[durasteel]], four units of [[polycarbonate]], and two [[helicyclic gear]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] heard that Eeakee was supposedly selling AF-2 Cypher Keys and sort out the Rodian while the merchant was stranded on the island known as the [[Cauldron (Akiva)|Cauldron]] and traded them the desired parts for the device in order to create a [[Slicer|slicing]] kit.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The AF-2 Cypher Key appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The player must obtain the key in order to unlock the optional "Slice Kit" ability.<ref name="Outlaws" />
