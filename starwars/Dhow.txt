'''Dhow''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[senator]] in the [[Imperial Senate]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="The Eye" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' dates the events of "The Eye" to [[5 BBY]].</ref> Dhow advocated together with [[Chandrila]]n senator [[Mon Mothma]] in the Senate for the rights of the [[Ghorman (people)|Ghormans]] who were being persecuted by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Eye" /> Later Dhow attended a party hosted by Mon Mothma at the [[Chandrilan Embassy]], where Dhow and senators Mothma, [[Vivyn]], [[Teenar]] and the individual [[Slorda]] discussed the recently passed [[Public Order Resentencing Directive]].<ref name="Narkina5" />
