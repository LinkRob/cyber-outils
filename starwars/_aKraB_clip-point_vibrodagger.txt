The ''''aKraB clip-point vibrodagger''' was a [[vibrodagger]] created by diehard survival enthusiasts from the [[planet]] [[Toprawa]]. It was engineered to slip in between the [[armor]] plates of large [[Species|animals]], allowing for close-range precision [[Death|kills]]. It could also penetrate thick, armored hides and [[laminate armor]] with ease.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 'aKraB clip-point vibrodagger was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Forged in Battle]]'',<ref name="Forged in Battle" /> a [[2016]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Soldier On!">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/8/17/soldier-on-1|text=Soldier On!}}</ref> The weapon originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in ''[[Luke Skywalker and the Shadows of Mindor]]'', a novel by [[Matthew Stover]], published by [[Del Rey]] on [[February 23]], [[2010]].<ref name="Amazon">{{RandomHouseOld|url=author/results.pperl?authorid=82866|text=Matthew Stover}}</ref>
