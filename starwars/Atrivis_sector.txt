The '''Atrivis sector''' was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and the [[New Territories]], containing many [[star system]]s. [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Dooku]] voyaged to the astronomical object [[Mantooine]] there between [[86 BBY]] and around [[80 BBY]]. The [[planet]] [[Kardoa]] in the [[sector]] saw [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] forces clash in a short [[Skirmish on Kardoa|battle]] around [[19 BBY]].

During its [[Imperial Era|reign]] the [[Galactic Empire]] occupied the planets [[Fest]] and [[Horuz]], and kept a [[Imperial garrison (Mantooine)|garrison]] on Mantooine, in the sector. When [[Crisis on Moltok|fighting]] broke out on the astronomical object [[Moltok]] in [[13 BBY]], the Imperial [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] ''[[Blood Crow]]'' was deployed to de-escalate the situation. The rebel fighter [[Jyn Erso]] was [[Mission to Horuz|sent]] to Horuz in [[7 BBY]] to [[assassin]]ate an [[Dorin Bell|Imperial scientist]], who was ultimately [[Death|killed]] by a failsafe trap from Erso's [[Partisans|rebel cell]].

As an [[Armed rebellion in the Atrivis sector|armed rebellion]] became present in the Atrivus sector by [[5 BBY]], the [[Liberators]] rebel group were [[Battle on Mantooine|crushed]] on Mantooine, while the [[Atrivis Resistance Group]] joined an [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|organized rebellion]] secretly led by [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], later [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|declared]] the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Fest, Mantooine, and the astronomical object [[Generis]] became three rebel operations headquarters in the sector by [[2 BBY]].

Four four [[pilot]]s operating in the Atrivis sector were transferred to bolster the [[MC75 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Profundity]]''{{'s}} [[starfighter]] complement by [[1 BBY]]. The Alliance continued to probe the Empire's presence at Horuz, and [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] was sent to investigate reports of [[kyber crystal]]s being transported there. In [[5 ABY]], as the Empire was falling apart, Horuz remained under the sway of Imperial remnants of criminal organizations.

==Description==
{{Quote|As ordered, I have been investigating reports that the Empire is transporting kyber crystals to Patriim, Horuz, and other remote star systems.|Cassian Andor, in a report to Alliance [[General]] [[Airen Cracken]]|<ref name="Rebel Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref>}}
The Atrivis sector was located within an intersecting portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[New Territories]] regions.<ref name="New Territories" /> It contained the [[Devon system|Devon]], [[Fedje system|Fedje]], [[Fest system|Fest]], [[Generis system|Generis]], [[Gibbela system|Gibbela]], [[Gree Baaker system|Gree Baaker]], [[Hethar system|Hethar]], [[Horuz system|Horuz]], [[Iridium system|Iridium]], [[Kardoa system|Kardoa]], [[Mantooine system|Mantooine]], [[Markbee's Star system|Markbee's Star]], [[Moltok system|Moltok]], [[Nam'ta system|Nam'ta]], [[Shar'Ack system|Shar'Ack]], [[Spefik system|Spefik]], [[Squarr system|Squarr]], and [[Vuchelle system]]s, all situated at the coordinates L-5 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" />

[[File:Moltok-MandoVG.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Moltok and Horuz were located in the Atrivis sector.]]
The Fest system<ref name="Systems" /> included the [[Temperature|cold]] [[mountain]]ous [[planet]] [[Fest]],<ref name="AB Update">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> which was linked by [[hyperspace route]] to the<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Outer Rim<ref name="Systems" /> planet<ref name="AB Update" /> [[Dantooine]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> The Generis system<ref name="Systems" /> included the astronomical object [[Generis]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> the Horuz system<ref name="Systems" /> featured the world of [[Horuz]], home to [[canyon]]s, [[cliff]]s, and [[forest]]s,<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref> and the Kardoa system<ref name="Systems" /> had the rocky planet [[Kardoa]].<ref name="Kanan 8">[[Kanan 8|''Kanan'' 8]]</ref> The terrestrial astronomical objects [[Mantooine]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and [[Moltok]]<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> were located in the Mantooine and Moltok systems respectively.<ref name="Systems" /> Also in the [[sector]] was [[Squarr]], a location<ref name="Rogue One novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> in the Squarr system<ref name="Systems" /> that was notable for its [[hurricane]]s.<ref name="Rogue One novel" />

==History==
{{Quote|After due deliberation, Madame Travia Chan of the Atrivis Resistance Group agrees to the terms of partnership with Senator Mon Mothma of the Alliance. May our factions be stronger together than apart. No more Mantooines! Down with the Empire!|Rebel [[Catta Dionize]], on behalf of Travia Chan, to Mon Mothma|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
By [[5 BBY]],<ref name="5 BBY">In ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', the statements covering the [[armed rebellion in the Atrivis sector]] was set fourteen [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore the reports were set in [[5 BBY]].</ref> an [[Armed rebellion in the Atrivis sector|armed rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]] was present in the Atrivis sector. This included the [[Atrivis Resistance Group]], which operated from [[Generis Station]], and the [[Liberators]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> which was a resistance force for Mantooine,<ref name="Lead">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> where the Empire kept a [[Imperial garrison (Mantooine)|garrison]]. The Liberators went on to [[Battle on Mantooine|seize]] the Imperial [[garrison]], only to soon be crushed by the [[Imperial Navy]] thereafter. As a result, [[Travia Chan]], the leader of the Atrivis Resistance Group, agreed via a message to terms of partnership with a larger [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|organized rebellion]] against the Empire that was secretly led by [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

[[File:Fest-TRF.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Fest, Generis, and Mantooine became rebel operations headquarters by 2 BBY.]]
By [[2 BBY]],<ref name="2 BBY">In ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', the report regarding the rebel locations is set between the introduction of the [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]] and the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance]], which are both dated to [[2 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Fest, Mantooine, and Generis all acted as operations headquarters for Mothma's rebellion. Such was noted on a [[Star map|map]] of rebel [[safe world]]s, [[starfighter]] hubs at level five or higher, operations headquarters, [[shadow planet]]s, and deep [[Realspace|space]] caches sent to Mothma. The [[map]], public statements on the Liberators' fight at the Mantooine garrison, and the message of Travia Chan's agreement with Mothma's terms, were all included in the compilation of non-electronic documents known as ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' by [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]]. Mothma went on to [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|officialize]] the rebellion as the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] later<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in 2 BBY.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="1 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' establishes that four [[rebel pilot]]s were transferred to the ''[[Profundity]]'' by the time of the [[Battle of Scarif]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref> four rebel [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]] [[Rebel pilot|pilots]] operating in the Atrivis sector, including [[Paril Ritta]], were transferred to the Alliance's modified [[MC75 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Profundity]]'' to bolster the vessel's starfighter forces.<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==Locations==
===The Horuz system===
{{Quote|Tatooine. Kerev Doi. Demesel. Horuz. All worlds still in thrall either to some Imperial remnant or to criminal syndicates or gangs.|[[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], to her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Han Solo]]|<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>}}
[[File:JynErsoScarif.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Jyn Erso had her first solo mission for the Partisans on Horuz.]]
The Galactic Empire eventually began a major tactical deployment<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> on Horuz during its [[Imperial Era|reign]], maintaining high protocols and a communications blackouts. The rebel cell known as the [[Partisans]], led by [[Saw Gerrera]], took an interest in the world as a result, calling it a "dark planet" due to the nature of the Empire's presence there despite its apparent insignificance.<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> In [[7 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Gerrera sent [[Jyn Erso]], a fighter that he had taken under his wing, on her first [[Mission to Horuz|solo mission]] to Horuz around the time of her fifteenth [[birthday]]. Erso was dropped on the world to ensure the [[assassin]]ation of the Imperial [[scientist]] [[Dorin Bell]], who was scouting Horuz's southern hemisphere with a small protection detail. While the young fighter tried to [[Sniper|snipe]] her target, the scientist was taken out by a trap that the Partisans had prepared.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

As the Alliance made efforts to analyse the Empire's deployments to Horuz,<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] of [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Alliance Intelligence]] began investigating reports that the Empire was transporting [[kyber crystal]]s to the Horuz system and other remote star systems.<ref name="Rebel Dossier" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> in the late [[Standard day|days]] of the Galactic Civil War, Horuz was one of a number of worlds that remained under the control of an Imperial remnant or criminal organizations.<ref name="Empire's End" />

===Other locations===
{{Quote|Are we allowed to know the nature of this ''mission'', general?''"<br />"''Simple ''recon''&hellip;to confirm or refute repots of a ''Separatist presence'' on Kardoa.|A clone trooper and Depa Billaba|<ref name="Kanan 9">[[Kanan 9|''Kanan'' 9]]</ref>}}
Sometime between [[86 BBY]] and circa [[80 BBY]],<ref name="86-80 BBY">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' establishes that [[Yoda]] took [[Dooku]] to [[Mantooine]] when the latter was the former's [[padawan]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates Dooku's induction as Yoda's padawan to [[86 BBY]] and Dooku's undertaking of his [[Rael Averross|first padawan]] to around [[80 BBY]]. Therefore, the voyage to Mantooine took place between those times.</ref> during the [[Republic Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] took his [[Padawan]] [[Dooku]] on a voyage to Mantooine which Dooku later chronicled in his [[Dooku's journal|journal]].<ref name="Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> At some point by 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]<ref name="1 BBY Troop">In ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', the report mentioning the [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]]'s use at Mantooine is set between the defection of [[Bodhi Rook]] and the [[Liberation of Erso]], both dated to [[1 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the Rebel Alliance used a [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]] as a troop carrier at Mantooine.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

[[File:BillabaVenator-Kanan8.png|thumb|right|300px|Republic forces were deployed to Kardoa.]]
Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="19 BBY">{{HelmetCollectionCite|15|Helmets}} places the [[Third Battle of Mygeeto]] [[Standard month|months]] prior to the [[Fourth Battle of Mygeeto]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places in [[19 BBY]]. As the events on [[Kardoa]] in [[Kanan 9|''Kanan'' 9]] take place shortly prior to the Third Battle of [[Mygeeto]], it must also take place around the same time.</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], Separatist [[General]]s [[Grievous]] and [[Kleeve]] and [[Colonel]] [[Coburn Sear]] were present on Kardoa. Reports of a Separatist presence there reached the Republic, and a [[clone trooper]] [[Depa Billaba's second battalion|battalion]] led by [[Jedi General]] [[Depa Billaba]] was deployed there for confirm or refute the reports. The Separatists there learned of the Republic presence, and Sear was left to deal with the forces, while the two generals went to join respective deployments elsewhere. When forces led by Billaba scouted Kardoa's canyons, Sear's own forces [[Skirmish on Kardoa|ambushed]] them, leading to a short battle between the two. The Separatist forces ultimately withdrew after Billaba and a couple of [[Cloning|clones]] attacked his [[starship]].<ref name="Kanan 9" />

After its [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] in 19 BBY,<ref name="Timelines" /> the Empire occupied the industrial zones on Fest, forcing many [[human]]s there into labor.<ref name="AB Update" /> Fest ultimately became savaged by the Empire, with [[refugee]]s from the world being accepted by the Alliance.<ref name="By Whatever Sun">{{StoryCite|story=By Whatever Sun|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="13 BBY">In [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']], the [[crisis on Moltok]] took place concurrently to the [[Dromedar hijacking]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[13 BBY]].</ref> a [[Unidentified Makurth boss|Makurth boss]] began firing on their local [[Unidentified Ho'Din settlement|Ho'Din settlement]] on Moltok. The Imperial [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] ''[[Blood Crow]]'', under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of Captain [[Filia Rossi]], was sent to de-escalate the [[Crisis on Moltok|crisis]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Atrivis_map.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The Atrivis sector in ''Star Wars'' Legends]]
The Atrivis sector first appeared in the current [[canon]] through the appearance of Kardoa in the [[Kanan 8|eighth issue]] of [[Greg Weisman]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Kanan]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]], illustrated by [[Pepe Larraz]]<ref name="Kanan 8" /> and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 18]], [[2015]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/56102/kanan_2015_8|text=Kanan (2015) #8}}</ref> The sector's name itself was first mentioned in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="ROUVG" /> Kardoa was not confirmed to be in the Atrivis sector until [[June 24]], [[2025]],<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> through "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix released on [[StarWars.com]] alongside the article [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Systems" />&mdash;both works having been authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack" />

The [[Atrivis sector/Legends|Atrivis sector]] originated in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], being introduced in [[Paul Murphy]]'s [[West End Games]] sourcebook, ''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]'', first published in [[1990]].<ref name="TRAS">''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]''</ref> The [[Sector/Legends|sector]] first appeared a mission set in the [[Horuz system/Legends|Horuz system]] in the [[1993]] computer game [[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']], developed by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="X-Wing">[[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']]</ref> The Horuz system was not confirmed to be in the Atrivis sector until [[1998]] when the LucasArts game [[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']] placed<ref name="SWR">[[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']]</ref> the Horuz system [[Planet/Legends|world]] of [[Despayre]]<ref name="X-Wing" /> in the sector.<ref name="SWR" />
