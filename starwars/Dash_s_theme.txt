"'''Dash's theme'''" is a minor theme used in the [[Shadows of the Empire (soundtrack)|''Shadows of the Empire'' soundtrack]] to represent the character [[Dash Rendar/Legends|Dash Rendar]]. It was written by [[Joel McNeely]] and performed by the [[Royal Scottish National Orchestra]] and choir in [[1996]].

It can be heard in the track "Beggar's Canyon Chase", representing the scene where Rendar arrives to [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker's]] rescue from [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba Desilijic Tiure's]] [[Swoop gang/Legends|Swoop gang]].
