'''Cob Tier''' was a [[human]] friend of the [[Bridger family]] who introduced [[Ezra Bridger]] to the [[Rodian]] [[Tseebo]].<ref name="Trivia Gallery" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cob Tier appeared in the original outline of "[[Empire Day (episode)|Empire Day]],"<ref name="Trivia Gallery" /> the sixth episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''{{'}} [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]],<ref name="Empire Day">{{Rebels|Empire Day (episode)}}</ref> which was first released on demand on [[November 10]], [[2014]] and made its broadcast debut the following week on [[Disney XD]] on [[November 17]], 2014,<ref name="Insider 156">{{InsiderCite|156|Star Wars Rebels Recounted|''Star Wars Rebels'' Recounted}}</ref> but the character was eventually combined with Tseebo as the story evolved.<ref name="Trivia Gallery" />
