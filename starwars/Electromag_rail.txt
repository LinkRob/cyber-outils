'''Electromag rails''' were devices that generated a [[Gravity|grav field]] to help reduce the speed of incoming [[starship]]s inside a [[hangar]]. The flight deck of [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]]'s [[Theed Hangar]] was equipped with such devices. They lined the deck beside [[Electrostatic repeller|electrostatic floor grates]].<ref name="CL2016" />
