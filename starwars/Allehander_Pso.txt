'''Allehander Pso''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who owned [[Pso's Palace]] on the [[Five Points]] [[space station]] in the [[Five Points system]]. Pso was a [[gambling]] lord who also hired many individuals to extended work contracts, turning them into indentured servants and essentially [[Slavery|slaves]]. He caught [[Jyn Erso]] when she made fake [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to pay the debt of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[colonel]] [[Lucka Solange]]. After sending his men, including a [[Dowutin]], to apprehend Erso, Pso negotiated with Solange, who agreed for the Empire to buy several of his slaves at a price that would render Solange's debts void. Pso meanwhile found in Erso the chance to make some great business with her talent, trading her passage to [[Rumitaka]] in exchange for her altering the indentured service records to be extended five more [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

==Personality and traits==
Allehander Pso had downy, finger-long, brown and green feathers on his balding head that trailed down his back and underneath his clothing, as well as taloned fingers and jade-green teeth that were pointed and jagged like the edge of a saw blade.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
