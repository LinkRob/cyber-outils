"'''Accessories and Structures'''" is a volume in the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series of reference books published by [[De Agostini]]. It was published on [[May 20]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''The galaxy was characterized by an infinite variety of planets, environments and inhabitants. The resources needed to colonize a world could vary greatly depending on the conditions of its environment, and it was often necessary to erect structures and facilities to facilitate this colonization, or to allow the exploitation of natural resources. The same maxim could be applied to ships and military bases, but also to the field facilities of the various ever-expanding factions in the galaxy. From the carbon dioxide freezing chambers on Bespin to the vaporizers on desert worlds like Tatooine, technology has always been an exceptional ally for species throughout the galaxy.''<ref name="Altaya" />
