'''Anophe Dengue''' was one of the [[Dengue sisters]] who were present in [[Takodana Castle]] in [[34 ABY]], when the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Takodana|attacked]] [[Takodana]].<ref name="AB Update" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dengue may be named after ''Anopheles'', a genus of mosquito and Dengue Fever, a disease spread by mosquitos.
