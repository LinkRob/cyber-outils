'''Aqualish''' were a species of [[Sentience|sentient]] [[humanoid]]s from the [[planet]] [[Ando]], who were identifiable by round black eyes and fur. Some members of this race possessed four eyes, while others only one pair.

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Negola dewaghi wool dugger?''"<br />"''He doesn't like you.|Ponda Baba speaks Aqualish, before being translated to [[Galactic Basic Standard]] by Cornelius Evazan, to Luke Skywalker|audio=HeDoesntLikeYou-ANH.flac|<ref name="Episode IV" />}}
[[File:AqualishArmsDealer-SbtDS.png|thumb|left|250px|An Aqualish [[arms dealer]]]]
Aqualish generally had bald heads and [[Hair|fur]] around their cheeks and two large chelicerae. They appeared to have different skin colors from each other. Aqualish also tended to have either two<ref name="Episode IV" /> or four [[eye]]s,<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> and different kinds of hands. The criminal [[Ponda Baba]] had hairy hands with five digits,<ref name="Episode IV" /> while some Aqualish who were a part of the [[Galactic Republic]] Senate sported hairless hands with three digits.<ref name="Episode I" /> The [[species]] spoke [[Aqualish (language)|Aqualish]]. Aqualish had [[Color|red-colored]] [[blood]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

==History==
The lives of Aqualish revolved around [[water]], but they did not need it to thrive and could settle far from it.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Some Aqualish, like the arms manufacturer [[Amda Wabo]], came from the [[planet]] [[Ando]]. They could be found among the [[The galaxy|galactic]] community by the time of the Galactic Republic, where a number of them were members of the Republic Senate.<ref name="Episode I" />

[[File:Gorothin_Vagger.png|thumb|left|250px|Aqualish politicians]]
However, some Aqualish did not agree with the direction of the Republic, and several Aqualish politicians aligned themselves with the [[Sith]] [[Sith Lord|Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]].<ref name="Sith">{{Film|III}}</ref> The [[Ando Commission]] was composed of three Aqualish and represented Ando within the [[Separatist Senate]].<ref name="AndoComm">{{SWU|set=Secrets of Power|cardname=Ando Commission|link=2642304891|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/3Lqgw}}</ref> Multiple other Aqualish went to work for Dooku's anti-Republic faction, the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During an armed conflict between the Confederacy and the Republic, referred to as the [[Clone Wars]], an Aqualish named [[Pune Zignat]] worked alongside the [[Neimoidian]] [[General]] [[Lok Durd]]. Both were captured by Republic forces on the planet [[Maridun]]. After the Clone Wars, the Republic was dissolved and transformed into the [[Galactic Empire]]. Two of the Aqualish who had aligned themselves with the Confederacy were murdered by the Sith Lord [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on the planet [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Sith" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], some Aqualish turned to [[Pirate|piracy]] and [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunting]], while others decided to join the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]] in order to restore the [[New Republic|Republic]] and regain their seat in the [[New Republic Senate|Senate]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Aqualish in the galaxy==
{{Quote|Ask Mr. Wabo where the shipment is being held.''"<br />"''He said Bay 17.|Sabine Wren incorrectly translates Wabo's response to Minister Tua|<ref name="Droids in Distress" />}}
As well as being [[senator]]s for the Galactic Republic<ref name="Episode I" /> and members of the Confederacy,<ref name="AndoComm" /> some Aqualish have found careers as both criminals and arms dealers. [[Utonk Hid]] was an Aqualish gunman who was a guest at Tiggs Leo's tavern on the planet [[Coruscant]].<ref name="LT">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep222|text="Lethal Trackdown" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}}</ref> During the early [[Imperial Era]], an Aqualish was part of [[Nico Deemis' syndicate]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Janix]], although along with two [[Trandoshan]] [[Enforcer (occupation)|enforcers]] and [[Nico Deemis]], the Aqualish was killed during a [[Skirmish in neutral territory|skirmish in a port on neutral territory]].<ref name="CH1 MSL">{{MSL|Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge}}</ref> Ponda Baba was an Aqualish criminal who, along with his friend [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]], had the [[death]] sentence on twelve [[star system]]s. Baba lost his arm to [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] during a [[cantina]] brawl.<ref name="Episode IV" />

[[File:AqualishAttack-BoBFCE.png|thumb|250px|right|The Aqualish crime family on Tatooine.]]
Amda Wabo was an Aqualish arms dealer who worked out of the planet [[Garel]]. During the reign of the Galactic Empire, Wabo attempted to sell [[T-7 ion disruptor rifle]]s to the Empire through [[Minister]] [[Maketh Tua]]. Their efforts were disrupted by a group of [[Spectres|rebels]] from the planet [[Lothal]], and some of the weapons ended up in the hands of the [[Crime lord|crime boss]] [[Cikatro Vizago]].<ref name="Droids in Distress" /> At least one Aqualish was a member of [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]'s court on the planet [[Tatooine]].<ref name="RotJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

A collective of Aqualish on [[Tatooine]] were members of the crime families in control of the workers district<ref name="Chapter 3">{{BOBF|Chapter 3: The Streets of Mos Espa}}</ref> that [[Boba Fett]] allied with<ref name="Chapter 4">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref> and was then betrayed by. Their leader was assassinated by [[Fennec Shand]] in the coup of the [[Pyke Syndicate]]<ref name="Chapter 7">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="BoBF Date">{{BoBFDate}}</ref>

A group of Aqualish used the former [[Nevarro Mandalorian covert|Mandalorian covert]] of [[the Tribe]] on [[Nevarro]], particularly the [[Armorer's workshop]], as a hideout<ref name="Chapter 12">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 12: The Siege}}</ref> in [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|12}}</ref> where they were intercepted and killed by the [[Marshal]] of Nevarro [[Carasynthia Dune]], prior to the [[second skirmish on Nevarro]].<ref name="Chapter 12" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Aqualish species first appeared through the character of Ponda Baba in the first ''[[Star Wars]]'' film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] in [[1977]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> An Aqualish appeared in ''[[Jabba the Hutt - Galactic Gangster]]'', an episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]'',<ref name="GOA Galactic Gangster">{{GOA|Jabba the Hutt - Galactic Gangster}}</ref> which are a series of shorts that are not supposed to be taken as literal [[canon]] events.<ref name="GoA tweet">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1139593657986588675|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=I think it's just the usual GoA stuff, not meant to be taken literally.|archivefile=File:MattMartinGoA.jpg}}</ref> Within the short, the Aqualish, who had been taken captive by [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]], is brought before Jabba the Hutt. The crime lord proceeded to drop the captive into his [[rancor]] pit, allowing [[Pateesa]] to eat the Aqualish.<ref name="GOA Galactic Gangster" />
