'''Fuel Station Ergo''' was an [[orbit]]al [[space station]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]] for [[Fuel|refueling]] purposes during the [[Imperial Era]],<ref name="BMF 34" /> which lasted from [[19 BBY]] to [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fuel Station Ergo was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 34|thirty-fourth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine series, published by [[De Agostini]]<ref name="BMF 34" /> around [[August 26]], [[2015]].<ref name="Date">{{BMFdates|34}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Fuel Station Ergo/Legends|Fuel Station Ergo]] first appeared in the [[1995]] first-person shooter [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Dark Forces]]'', which was developed by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="Dark Forces">''[[Star Wars: Dark Forces]]''</ref>

The station was excluded from the [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix of [[star system]]s, a follow-up to the<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> [[2009]]<ref name="SW.com">{{SWArchive|url=atlas/index.html|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion}}</ref> [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|''The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion]],<ref name="Substack" /> by author [[Jason Fry]] due to it being a military codename.<ref name="Email">{{EmailCite|email=File:EAOC discussion.jpg|creator=Jason Fry|date=2015-06-30}}</ref>
