'''Argiopid'''<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> was a [[Sentience|sentient]] species that lived in [[the galaxy]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], one member of the [[species]] was present at the [[Auction for Han Solo]] that was hosted by [[Crimson Dawn]], on the<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 2">[[War of the Bounty Hunters 2|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 2]]</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Star Wars 14">[[Star Wars (2020) 14|''Star Wars'' (2020) 14]]</ref> [[Jekara]].<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 2" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TLJ date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], including the appearance of [[Derla Pidys]] in the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]], to the same [[Standard year|year]] as the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[sommelier]] [[Derla Pidys]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|derla-pidys|Derla Pidys}}</ref> was present in the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]] when the [[Resistance]] members [[Finn]] and [[Rose Tico]] [[Mission to Canto Bight|traveled]] to [[Canto Bight]] to get the help of the "[[Master Codebreaker]]" in order to deactivate the [[hyperspace tracker]] device aboard the ''[[Supremacy]],'' the [[flagship]] of [[First Order]] [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]], that was allowing the First Order to track the [[Resistance navy]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />
