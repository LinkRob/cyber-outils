'''File JC-N091FFC74''' was a file claimed to be originated from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] [[Jedi Archives|Archives]]. Among its contents, it recorded a supposed conversation between [[Jedi Master]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Yoda]], [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], and [[Mace Windu]], in which the [[Jedi]] plot to make a deal with [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] leader [[Count]] [[Dooku]] in order to seize control of the [[Galactic Republic]] from [[Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] during in the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>  In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|7}}</ref> [[Bail Prestor Organa]] obtained this file, and doubted its veracity.<ref name="MoF" />
