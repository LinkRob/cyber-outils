'''Arro Basteren''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] who grew up on the [[planet]] [[Ertegas]] and joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in their fight against the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He became a member of the [[Marine Corps]] of the [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces]] and was a [[private]], primarily serving as spotter to the [[sniper]] [[Serchill Rostok]]. Basteren was one of several soldiers recommended by [[General]] [[Davits Draven]] for possessing the fearlessness and self-awareness and considered by the general for a potential mission to raid the headquarters of [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]] on [[Jedha]]. Later, he and his fellow soldiers volunteered for an unsanctioned mission to [[Thief|steal]] the [[Death Star]]'s [[Death Star plans|plans]] from the [[Citadel Tower]] on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]] in [[1 BBY]]. Basteren [[Death|perished]] in the [[Battle of Scarif|battle]] that followed, helping to ensure that the plans were transmitted to the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
Arro Basteren was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] who was born on the<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> [[agriworld]]<ref name="Value of Food">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: "Value of Food"</ref> of [[Ertegas]] in the [[Anoat sector]]. He grew up there, protecting his family's crops from [[woshrike]]s<ref name="ROUVG" /> and other dangerous creatures<ref name="Geektionary" /> which sharpened his [[eye]]sight<ref name="ROUVG" /> and helped him develop the skill of seeking out and destroying the enemy.<ref name="Geektionary" /> He eventually joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in their fight against the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="RO" /> with whom he served as a [[private]] in the [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces]]' [[Marine Corps]],<ref name="Ultimate Sticker Encyclopedia" /> and found a natural place as a [[scout]].<ref name="Geektionary" />

===Rebel Alliance service===
[[File:ArroBasteren-Scarif-SWCT-RODCFR.jpg|thumb|left|215px|Basteren during the Battle of Scarif]]
In the Rebel Alliance, Basteren acted as a spotter for the renowned [[sniper]] [[Corporal]] [[Serchill Rostok]],<ref name="ROUVG" /> uncovering targets using [[Q4-E quadnoculars]] and passing the information on to Rostok.<ref name="Geektionary" /> His primary task was maintaining situational awareness and environmental status in the midst of battle.<ref name="ROUVG" /> During his service, he was recognized by [[General]] [[Davits Draven]] for both his fearlessness and self-awareness. For this reason, when Draven had doubts that the former member of the resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]] [[Jyn Erso]] would successfully connect with Gerrera on the [[moon]] of [[Jedha]], he recommended Basteren and several other [[soldier]]s to Rebellion [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] for a potential raid of the Partisan headquarters in Jedha's [[Catacombs of Cadera]]. The general sent a biography of Basteren to Mothma and suggested they start with those soldiers and build a larger [[squad]] around them.<ref name="The Rebel Files" />

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> once Erso had successfully completed the [[Operation Fracture|mission]] to connect with Gerrera and learned of the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]], Basteren and the same group of Rebel soldiers volunteered to join her in an [[Battle of Scarif|unsanctioned mission]] to [[Thief|steal]] the [[Death Star]]'s [[Death Star plans|plans]] from the [[Citadel Tower]] on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]]. The volunteer [[squad]] took the improvized [[callsign]] "[[Rogue One]]" and infiltrated the planet's [[Shield Gate|defenses]] in the stolen [[Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle|''Zeta''-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle]] [[SW-0608]]. On Scarif, Basteren was among a squad that set out to create a distraction and draw the Empire's forces so that Erso and a small team could sneak into the Citadel and find the plans. He and the others ultimately gave their lives in the fighting to ensure the plans were transmitted to the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]].<ref name="RO" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|Draven was on to something. At Scarif, however, these soldiers weren't assigned. They volunteered for an unsanctioned mission.|Caluan Ematt, on Basteren and his fellow soldiers|<ref name="The Rebel Files" />}}
[[File:ArroBasteren-TheRebelFiles.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Basteren, pictured in Draven's report]]
The intelligence update to Mon Mothma that featured Draven's recommendation and biography of Arro Basteren was eventually included in ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', a collection of documents relating to the Rebel Alliance compiled by the [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]]. When ''The Rebel Files'' were recovered<ref name="The Rebel Files" /> in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="34 ABY date">According to ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' were recovered thirty [[Standard year|years]] after their loss during the [[Bombardment of Durkteel|bombardment]] of [[Durkteel]], which took place at the same time as the [[Battle of Endor]]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states that the Battle of Endor happened in [[4 ABY]], it can be calculated that ''The Rebel Files'' were recovered in [[34 ABY]].</ref> after being lost during the [[Bombardment of Durkteel|bombardment]] of [[Durkteel]]<ref name="The Rebel Files" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Durkteel Date">According to ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', the [[bombardment of Durkteel]] took place at the same time as [[Battle of Endor]]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states that the Battle of Endor was fought in [[4 ABY]], the bombardment of [[Durkteel]] occurred that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the documents were passed on the General [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Resistance]] and several Resistance members read them. Resistance [[officer]] and former Rebel [[Caluan Ematt]] read Draven's recommendation of Basteren and his fellow soldiers and commented that Draven was on to something in selecting them, as evidenced by Basteren's volunteering for the unsactioned mission to Scarif<ref name="The Rebel Files" /> that resulted in his death.<ref name="RO" />

==Personality and traits==
Arro Basteren was a human male with [[Color|light]] skin, brown [[hair]]<ref name="RO" /> and blue eyes.<ref name="ROUVG" />

As member of Rebel Alliance Special Forces, he was a brave and resilient individual.<ref name="Ultimate Sticker Encyclopedia" /> Basteren was among a group of Rebel soldiers that General Davits Draven believed possessed the genuine fearlessness needed to undertake a mission with long odds and the self-awareness needed to survive more than a [[Standard minute|minute]] during such a mission.<ref name="The Rebel Files" />

==Skills and abilities==
Arro Basteren was a first-class rebel soldier.<ref name="Geektionary" /> Basteren had sharp eyesight<ref name="ROUVG" /> and a knack for uncovering and destroying the enemy,<ref name="Geektionary" /> both skills honed by keeping his family's crops protected from woshrikes<ref name="ROUVG" /> and other dangerous creatures on his [[homeworld]].<ref name="Geektionary" />

==Equipment==
[[File:Q4EQuadnoculars-ROVG.png|thumb|left|175px|Basteren holding his Q4-E quadnoculars]]
During the Battle of Scarif Basteren wore a blue vest over a blue [[jacket]], with brown pants and [[boot]]s. The vest had an orange patch on his right lapel<ref name="ROUVG" /> and white and red insignias on the left lapel.<ref name="Ultimate Sticker Encyclopedia" /> These were paired with a black and tan [[helmet]] and a brown colored [[belt]] that contained several containers on it. He also used an [[A300 blaster rifle|A-300 blaster rifle]] and a pair of [[Fabritech]] [[Q4-E quadnoculars]], which possessed an imaging screen surface that captured approaching Imperials. Basteren's [[quadnoculars]] were held up by a blue strap around his neck.<ref name="ROUVG" />
At an earlier occasion Basteren wore a green jacket with a yellow patch below his right shoulder and a bigger tan-colored helmet.<ref name="The Rebel Files" />
