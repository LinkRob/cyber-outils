'''Ezil's Return''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[November 18]], [[2004]] in the form of a monologue by [[Ezil|Old Ezil]] describing his capture by members of the [[Thaereian military|Navy of Thaere]], his detainment, and subsequent escape back to [[Cularin/Legends|Cularin]].
