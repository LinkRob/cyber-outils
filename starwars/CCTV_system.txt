'''CCTV systems''' were a type of surveillance network which utilized extensive systems of [[camera]]s. The [[city]] of [[Ro Station]] on the [[moon]] [[Pantora]] had a CCTV network at the beginning of the [[Imperial Era]], which was hacked by the fugitive [[Cloning|clone]] [[Tech]] after the child clone [[Omega]] was [[Chase on Pantora|targeted]] by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Fennec Shand]].<ref name="Cornered">{{TBB|Cornered}}</ref>
