'''First Order stormtrooper armor''' was the standard [[armor]] of the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] of the [[First Order]], a military junta inspired by the [[New Order|ideals]] of the former [[Galactic Empire]]. Noted for its stark [[Color|white]] appearance, First Order armor was derived from the respective designs of its predecessors; the [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I]] and [[Phase II clone trooper armor|Phase II]] models of [[clone trooper]] [[Clone trooper armor|armor]] and the [[Stormtrooper armor|set of armor]] used by the [[Stormtrooper|Imperial stormtroopers]] of the past.

==Description==
{{Quote|Even a Wookiee can't crush First Order armor.|Captain Phasma|<ref name="TFA novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>}}
[[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] wore stark [[Color|white]] [[armor]], the design of which drew inspiration from that of the foregone [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s of the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="DB Stormtroopers">{{Databank|first-order-stormtroopers|First Order Stormtroopers}}</ref> Like its predecessors, the armor was worn over a black [[body glove]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> The armor was designed for [[human]]s alone.<ref name="Black Spire" /> The First Order, during its [[Cold War|conflict]] with the [[Resistance]] and its supporting [[Politics|government]], the [[New Republic]], sought to have the recognizable and once-feared stormtroopers once again lead the charge against the former enemies of the [[Galaxy|Galactic]] Empire.<ref name="TFAVD" />

[[File:FirstOrderStormtrooper-SW100Objects.png|thumb|left|200px|Pauldrons were added to stormtrooper armor to mark officers.]]
The design of past [[stormtrooper armor]] was improved upon, adding at least greater flexibility through an updated joint design. Individual sets of armor were linked to [[macro scope]] [[Scanner (disambiguation)|scanner]]s.<ref name="The New Trooper">{{Resistance|The New Trooper}}</ref> These scanners can be [[magnet]]ically attached to the back of the armor.<ref name="Art">{{SW|url=the-relic-raider-concept-art-gallery|text="The Relic Raider" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 10}}</ref> One stormtrooper [[captain]], [[Phasma]], wore her own [[Phasma's armor|distinctive stormtrooper armor]] polished in [[chromium]] as a representation of power. Other specialized stormtroopers, such as [[First Order flametrooper|flametroopers]] and [[First Order snowtrooper|snowtroopers]], wore a variant of the First Order stormtrooper armor representing their specializations. To denote [[Military rank|rank]], [[Commanding officer|commanding]] stormtroopers wore color-coded [[pauldron]] armor plates.<ref name="TFAVD" />

The stormtrooper [[helmet]]s had a glossy [[betaplast]] finish, which required constant cleaning. They were also equipped with a smoke filtration system and an external tank hook-up,<ref name="TFAVD" /> although the helmets could not filter [[Poison|toxins]].<ref name="EpVII" /> Aside from providing standard protection for the wearer's head, the stormtrooper helmet had both communication and targeting systems for the foot [[soldier]]s, although [[quadnoculars]] were required for any enhanced imaging. Also within the helmet was a near-constant stream of data projected across the lenses: telemetry, firing solutions, atmospheric conditions, everything up to and including the ammo count for the [[blaster rifle]]. An ID tag would pop up whenever one trooper looked directly at another, the in-suit computer able to read friendly identifications.<ref name="Before the Awakening">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref>

[[File:FOStormtrooperDressing-AORFinn1.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Stormtroopers wore white armor over a black body glove.]]
The same stream of data could also show the exact distance between the wearer and, for instance, their allies or an objective.<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> [[Finn|FN-2187]] once could see that [[FN-2003|Slip]] was exactly 29.3 meters back. The helmets contained polarized lenses and night vision, but lacked advanced imaging gear in order to keep the helmet's weight down.<ref name="TFAVD" /> The armor was immune to [[fire]].<ref name="Age of Resistance">[[Age of Resistance - Finn 1|''Age of Resistance - Finn'' 1]]</ref>

The armor was strong enough to withstand the strength of a [[Wookiee]], according to [[Captain]] [[Phasma]],<ref name="TFA novel" /> the [[commanding officer]] of the First Order's stormtrooper [[legion]]s.<ref name="TFAVD" /> [[Archex]] derived a sense of belonging and purpose from the [[Cardinal's armor|armor]] that he wore as a stormtrooper, prior to [[Treason|deserting]] the [[First Order military]].<ref name="Black Spire" />

==History==
===Development===
The signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]]<ref name="TFAVD" /> in [[5 ABY]]<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> marked the end of the Galactic Civil War with the New Republic claiming [[Battle of Jakku|victory over the Galactic Empire]]. In the aftermath of the war, the Empire was further reduced in power through severe reparations and a disarmament policy imposed by the Republic.<ref name="DB First Order">{{Databank|the-first-order|The First Order}}</ref> As a result, the mobilization of stormtrooper forces and the production of their armor were strictly forbidden by the treaty between the two powers.<ref name="TFAVD" /> In [[the galaxy]]'s [[Unknown Regions]], however, a number of former Imperials regrouped and ultimately formed the First Order. Eager to reclaim the Empire's legacy by challenging the Republic's sovereignty over the galaxy,<ref name="DB First Order" /> the First Order secretly violated the treaty's stipulations and engaged in a vast rearmament program, resulting in the production of a new set of armor for a [[Project Resurrection|new generation of stormtroopers]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> 

[[File:Steadfast-Stormtrooper-trio.png|thumb|left|300px|The First Order inherited one of the galaxy's most enduring symbols of military might&mdash;the white-armored soldier.]]
As a result, the First Order appropriated the tradition of the white-armored soldier that began with the [[Cloning|clones]] who fought for the Republic.<ref name="TROS VD" /> The appearance of First Order stormtroopers, as well as their Imperial precursors, further morphed the [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone army's]] legacy from a symbol of honorable defense into a "faceless" icon of [[fascism]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> The First Order ranks were mostly composed of humans, something publicly claimed to be because their armor was designed with only that species in mind. However, [[Resistance]] [[agent]] [[Vi Moradi]] suspected the real answer was that the First Order did not want to accept non-humans into its ranks out of [[xenophobia]], with the armor excuse simply a way to not openly admit as such.<ref name="Black Spire" />

===Open warfare===
{{Quote|They're my friends! My white helmeted friends!|A brainwashed Seezelslak|<ref name="Last Call">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge – Last Call]]''</ref>}}
Around [[29 ABY]], the same year in which<ref name="29 ABY Note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]] to [[34 ABY]]. According to ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that [[First Order stormtrooper]]s were first publicly deployed about five [[Standard year|years]] before that event, putting their first open deployments to circa [[29 ABY]]. ''The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary'' also states the public formation of the [[First Order]] was five years before the Hosnian Cataclysm, putting that event in 29 ABY.</ref> the First Order was publicly formed via the [[First Order secession|secession]] of [[Centrists|Centrist]] planets, the First Order stormtrooper was officially revealed to the galaxy at large after a secret twenty year long development process. While in truth the First Order ranks were trained to conquer the galaxy, at that time the Stormtrooper Corps was presented merely as a local defense force for the "newly-established" First Order. With the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> of [[34 ABY]],<ref name="29 ABY Note" /> the First Order finally began its conquest of galactic space. In the [[First Order-Resistance War]], stormtroopers wore their armor in battle against enemies of the First Order, chiefly the [[Resistance]], and to enforce its conquest of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

[[File:Kylo-Stormtroopers.png|thumb|right|300px|The faceless ranks of the Stormtrooper Corps marched into the galaxy with the mission of reclaiming Imperial rule.]]
[[Dok-Ondar]], who owned a [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|den of antiquities]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]], also came to acquire a suit of stormtrooper armor through means he refused to elaborate upon. During the First Order's [[Battle of Batuu|operations]] on Batuu, a [[First Order re-education facility]] was established under [[Lieutenant]] [[Gauge]] to brainwash local citizens into obedient servants of the regime, which the stormtroopers saw as a way to counter Resistance propaganda. Made to believe he had signed up to join the regime, the first patient, an [[Azumel]] named [[Seezelslak]], was under the impression he would be made into a stormtrooper with armor of his own, although he suspected he would look odd in armor. When a [[Unidentified droid repair technician|droid repair technician]] infiltrated the facility with Ondar's stolen armor and the help of Seezelslak's other allies, the brainwashed Seezelslak assumed the technician had also joined up and complemented their appearance in stormtrooper armor, only for the technician to break the bar owner out and destroy the base. After the escape, Seezelslak recovered from his re-education with Ondar's help.<ref name="Last Call" />

==Behind the scenes==
For [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], director [[Rian Johnson]] subtly changed the stormtrooper armor seen onscreen from its previous appearance in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''The Force Awakens'']]. The changes included changing the color from a creamy white to a toned-down off-white with a very slight grey tint, and altering the shape of the black "mouth" on the helmets to make them look meaner and more skeletal.<ref name="TLJArt">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]''</ref><ref name="TelegraphArt">{{WebCite|url=https://www.telegraph.co.uk/films/0/art-last-jedi-early-sketch-designs-reveal-movie|text=The art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi –what the early sketch designs reveal about the movie|author=Hawkes=, Rebecca|date=2017-12-20|work=[http://www.telegraph.co.uk The Telegraph]|archivedate=20200205141616}}</ref>
