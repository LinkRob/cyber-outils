A '''sector''' was located in the northeast-zenith of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. It contained the [[Ornfra system]] and the [[planet]] [[Ornfra]]. In [[18 BBY]], two skirmishes occurred in the [[sector]] as part of a [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|crisis]] in the Ascendancy. The first was a brief [[Skirmish between the Grayshrike and the Orisson|confrontation]] in the Ornfra [[Star system|system]] between the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] [[Chiss heavy cruiser|warship]] ''[[Grayshrike]]'', the [[Clarr family]] [[light cruiser]] ''[[Orisson]]'', the warlike [[Grysk]] [[species]], and the [[Dasklo family]]. Shortly afterward, the Grysk [[Shatter-class WarMaster|''Shatter''-class WarMaster]] ''[[FateSpinner]]'' [[Sneak attack by the FateSpinner|ambushed]] the ''Orisson'' as it returned to the system.

==Description==
The [[planet]] [[Ornfra]] and the [[Ornfra system]] occupied a [[sector]] that was situated in the northeast-zenith of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Chiss Space]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> It was situated in grid square F-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Ornfra was an important economic and [[Politics|political]] hub of the sector and, as such, all of the Ascendancy's [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] and seventeen of the forty [[Chiss]] [[Great Families]] which aspired to become a part of the Nine had strongholds on the planet.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==History==
{{Quote|You'll go to Ornfra, wait there until Captain Roscu arrives, and do whatever you can to defuse the situation.|[[Supreme General]] [[Ba'kif]], to [[Senior Captain]] [[Irizi'in'daro]]|translated=1|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
[[File:ChissAscendancyCanonSymbol.svg|thumb|right|300px|The symbol of the Chiss Ascendancy, which the northeast-zenith sector was a part of.]]
At the [[time]] of a [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|crisis]] in the Ascendancy<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> in [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> the sector bore witness to a [[Skirmish between the Grayshrike and the Orisson|skirmish]] between four parties. The participants were the warlike [[Grysk]] [[species]], which attempted to [[Jixtus's plan|incite]] a civil war in the Ascendancy; the [[Clarr family]] [[Light cruiser|warship]] ''[[Orisson]]'', which sought to destroy a Grysk [[asteroid missile platform]] that had been disguised as a [[Mitth family]] [[Space station|espionage station]] and was controlled by a Grysk [[freighter]] in the [[Star system|system]]; the [[Dasklo family]], which defended Ornfra; and the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] [[Chiss heavy cruiser|warship]] ''[[Grayshrike]]'', which was assigned to Ornfra in order to prevent violence between the Clarr and the Dasklo. The skirmish resulted in the Defense Fleet being able to convince the Clarr of the [[asteroid]] [[missile]] platform's true nature. Subsequently, the station self-destructed, destroying the Grysk freighter as well.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Soon afterward, the Chiss [[Patriarch]] [[Clarr'ivl'exow]] "Rivlex" sent [[Captain]] [[Clarr'os'culry]] "Roscu" and the ''Orisson'' to return to the Ornfra system for an investigation: Grysk agent "[[Jixtus]]" claimed that the Dasklo family was manufacturing [[warship]]s disguised as [[Starship|vessels]] of other [[Chiss family|Chiss families]]. As soon as the ''Orisson'' arrived in-system, it was [[Sneak attack by the FateSpinner|ambushed]] by Jixtus's [[flagship]], the [[Shatter-class WarMaster|''Shatter''-class WarMaster]] ''[[FateSpinner]]'', whom he had informed of the Chiss vessel's expected arrival. The ''Orisson'' escaped into [[hyperspace]] following a brief battle.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
