"'''Anvil Leader'''" was a [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] that served in the [[New Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Following the [[Battle of Endor]], they served as [[Anvil Leader|leader]] of [[Anvil Squadron]], where they flew an [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]] in [[Scouring of Mon Cala|defending]] [[Mon Cala]] and the [[Battle of Galitan]] when New Republic forces were attacked by [[Titan Squadron]].<ref name="Squadrons" />
