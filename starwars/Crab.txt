'''Crabs''' were creatures<ref name="ALD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> after which the [[LM-432 crab droid]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|lm-432-muckraker-crab-droid|LM-432 "Muckraker" Crab Droid}}</ref> and the [[Mon Cala crab]] containment device were named.<ref name="Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/prisoners-concept-art-gallery|text="Prisoners" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 8}}</ref> Known [[species]] of crabs included the [[krillcrab]],<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> the [[Ravine crab]],<ref name="The Best Pet">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=5|story=Tales from Wild Space: The Best Pet}}</ref> and the [[caw-crab]].<ref name="ALD" /> [[Reklam Station]]'s [[DTS-series dismantler droid|DTS-series Dismantler Droid]] had [[Limb|legs]] similar to a crab's.<ref name="Dismantler droid">{{Databank|dismantler-droid|Dismantler droid}}</ref>
