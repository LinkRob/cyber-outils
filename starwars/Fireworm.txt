'''Fireworms''' were a type of creature that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Sentinel M2" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Hunters Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]]<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Sentinel]] did an interview<ref name="Sentinel M2" /> for the sporting competitive show [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]]<ref name="SWH">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> and told stories of his [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] service as a [[stormtrooper]]. In his reminisce, Sentinel claimed to have beaten a Fireworm during his deployment on a [[Magma|lava]] [[planet]]&mdash;although he was unsure if it was [[Senna]] or [[Mustafar]]&mdash;by shoving a [[Permafrost crystal]] in the creature's maw.<ref name="Sentinel M2" /> Another competitor of the show,<ref name="SWH" /> [[Imara Vex]], later came to the interview and debunked Sentinel's stories.<ref name="Sentinel M2" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:JASB3.jpg|200px|thumb|right|A fireworm in ''Star Wars'' Legends]]
Fireworm was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via a mention in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', which was developed by [[NaturalMotion]]'s [[BossAlien]]<ref name="SWH" /> and initially published by [[Zynga]] on [[November 16]], [[2021]], as a [[Wikipedia:Soft launch|soft launch]]<ref name="Soft">{{Twitter|ZyngaStarWars|status/1460656552734605317|[[Zynga]] Star Wars|date=2021-11-16|quote=We are excited to announce Star Wars: Hunters is now entering soft launch and is available on Android for players in India, Philippines, Malaysia & Indonesia.|archivedate=20220625084625|archivefile=File:ZyngaStarWars-SoftLaunchDate-Twitter.png}}</ref> before the global release on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Global">{{SWYouTube|4dZcq6m3y40|Star Wars: Hunters Launch Date Reveal Trailer}}</ref> The game was later shut down on [[October 1]], [[2025]].<ref name="Offline">{{Hunters|url=news/sunset-on-vespaara|text=Sunset on Vespaara}}</ref> The mention of the creature was included in a quote as part of the "Sentinel Mastery II" questline.<ref name="Sentinel M2" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Fireworm/Legends|fireworms]] first appeared in the [[1994]] novel ''[[Jedi Search]]'' written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]].<ref name="Jedi Search">''[[Jedi Search]]''</ref> The creature was later depicted in the [[1996]] [[West End Games]] sourcebook ''[[The Jedi Academy Sourcebook]]'' for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="The Jedi Academy Sourcebook">''[[The Jedi Academy Sourcebook]]''</ref>
