"'''Dark Forces: The Villains of ''Star Wars'''''" is an article by [[Daniel Wallace]] that was published in [[Star Wars Insider 113|''Star Wars Insider'' 113]], featuring profiles for several major ''[[Star Wars]]'' villains: [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]], [[Grand Moff/Legends|Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin/Legends|Tarkin]], [[Admiral/Legends|Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]], [[Darth Krayt]], and [[Count]] [[Dooku]].<ref name="SWI 113" />
