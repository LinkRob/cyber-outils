'''Bil''' was a<ref name="Grand Imperial Jubilee" /> [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> [[Stormtrooper Commander]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[2 BBY]]. They spoke with [[Terex]] about their respective plans and future before the latter was taken over by the [[Mask of Lord Momin]]. Eventually, Bil was killed by [[Ellian Zahra]] on [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin's]] orders.<ref name="Grand Imperial Jubilee" />
