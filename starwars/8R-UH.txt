'''8R-UH''' was an [[astromech droid]] who served the [[Resistance]] on the [[planet]] [[Ajan Kloss]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War]]. The [[droid]] was one of five astromechs who worked under [[R5-2JE]] and who were all absent from their jobs at the landing pad one day. 8R-UH was sunbathing atop a nearby cliff, seeking to soak up every last ray of sunshine before returning to work. After being interrupted from sunbathing, the 8R-UH remarked that this had been his "worst tropical vacation ever."<ref name="LEGO saga" />

==Behind the scenes==
8R-UH appeared in the [[2022]] non-canon video game ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', published by [[TT Games]]. His name's spelling resembles the slang word "[[Wiktionary:bruh|bruh]]," which he also used in conversation with the player character.<ref name="LEGO saga" />
