'''Avenger of Jakku''' was a pseudonym used by a [[collector]] of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] artifacts following the [[Galactic Concordance|fall of the Galactic Empire]].<ref name="BL">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> By [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Timeframe">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/652893780236988420|[[Del Rey]]|quote=So excited to have @claudiagray writing a #StarWars novel with us. SW: New Republic: Bloodline coming 2016. Set 6 years before TFA.|archivefile=File:New Republic Bloodline Twitter Post.jpg}} establishes that the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] take place six [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|VII}}. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], so ''Bloodline'' must take place in [[28 ABY]].</ref> fellow Imperial artifact enthusiast [[Senator]] [[Ransolm Casterfo]] had encountered Avenger of [[Jakku]], but considered the collector's moniker ridiculous despite suspecting that it was probably only role-play of a part the collector might play in a reenactment.<ref name="BL" />

==Behind the scenes==
Avenger of Jakku was first mentioned in the novel [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']], which was written by [[Claudia Gray]] and released in [[2016]].
