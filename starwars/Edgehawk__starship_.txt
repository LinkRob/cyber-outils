The '''''Edgehawk''''' was a [[starship]] used by the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] and her [[Marriage|wife]], [[Losha Tarkon]], in their search for [[Cadeliah]], the heir to both the [[Unbroken Clan]] and the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate]] who had the potential to unite both criminal syndicates. The two were accompanied by a [[T'onga's crew|crew]] they had gathered for their task, including the [[Gand]] [[findsman]] [[Zuckuss]], the [[assassin]] [[Tasu Leech]], and the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], the latter of whom the crew rescued on [[Jekara]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 16" /> In the ensuing [[Attack on the Executor|battle]], the crew attempted to save [[Beilert Valance]] from the [[Galactic Empire]], but were thwarted by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 17">[[Bounty Hunters 17|''Bounty Hunters'' 17]]</ref> The ''Edgehawk'' shared its name with an [[Edgehawk|avian creature]] native to the planet [[Lothal]].<ref name="Rebel Journal">''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]''</ref>

After the crew [[Ambush at Depatar|fought]] against the [[Imperial Special Forces]] unit [[Inferno Squad]], who were [[Hunt for Valance|hunting]] the bounty hunter [[Beilert Valance]] to [[Memory wipe|wipe]] his memory of Imperial [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|secrets]], Tarkon and Leech left the crew.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 34">[[Bounty Hunters 34|''Bounty Hunters'' 34]]</ref> In order for her to keep paying the rest of her crew, T'onga was forced to sell the ''Edgehawk''.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35">[[Bounty Hunters 35|''Bounty Hunters'' 35]]</ref>
