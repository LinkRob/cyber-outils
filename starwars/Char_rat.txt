'''Char rat''' were a type of [[rat]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 24" /> [[Time|Sometime]] between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-marvel|text=SWCE 2023: Marvel Reveals ''Star Wars: Dark Droids'' Horror Event and More}} states that the events of ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]'', including [[Bounty Hunters 24|''Bounty Hunters'' 24]], take place between the events of {{Film|V}} and {{Film|VI}}, which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Tasu Leech]]&mdash;a member of the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]]'s [[T'onga's crew|crew]]&mdash;called the [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Dengar]] a "double-crossing char rat" after the latter left the crew on the [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|flagship]] of<ref name="Bounty Hunters 24" /> the criminal syndicate<ref name="Bounty Hunters 25">[[Bounty Hunters 25|''Bounty Hunters'' 25]]</ref> [[Crimson Dawn]] after he had been hired to help them [[Raid on the Vermillion|infiltrate]] the [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 24" />

==Behind the scenes==
Char rat were mentioned in the [[Bounty Hunters 24|twenty-fourth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 24" /> on [[June 15]], [[2022]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/98317/star_wars_bounty_hunters_2020_24|text=Star Wars: Bounty Hunters (2020) #24}}</ref>
