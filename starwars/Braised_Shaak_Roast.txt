'''Braised Shaak Roast''' was a dish made with [[Foodstuff|meat]] from a [[shaak]], a large rounded animal native to the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], which was slowly roasted and braised until juicy and tender.<ref name="Cookbook" /> It was suitable for consumption by multiple species, including [[human]]s.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" />

The [[Latero]] [[pilot]] [[Greez Dritus]] knew how to make the dish<ref name="JFO" /> and in [[14 BBY]]<ref name="JFO date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> claimed to [[Cal Kestis]] that he sometimes became so focused on braising a Shaak Roast that he barely noticed his apron had caught on [[fire]].<ref name="JFO" /> [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for the dish in his [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]. He described his version as hearty and rustic. In his recipe, the shaak meat was braised with local [[Mushroom|fungi]] and [[Plant|greens]], then accompanied by [[noodle]]s.<ref name="Cookbook" /> Tuggs' recipe for Braised Shaak Roast was served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Braised Shaak Roast was introduced to the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via the Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo food stand at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], where it has been available since the opening in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=galaxys-edge-announcements|text=Everything You Need to Know About ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The dish consists of a beef pot roast with cavatelli pasta, wilted kale, and mushrooms. Since its debut, the Galaxy's Edge dish has been renamed to Batuuan Beef Pot Roast.<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref> When that name appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', [[Batuuan Beef Pot Roast]] matched the out-of-universe description of the dish served at Galaxy's Edge, but the [[in-universe]] details changed to be made from [[cow]]s instead of shaak.<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>

A recipe for Braised Shaak Roast is included in the 2019 recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]]. The recipe is made with the following ingredients: [[Wikipedia:Pot roast|pot roast beef round]], [[Wikipedia:Soy sauce|soy sauce]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic]], [[Wikipedia:Cavatelli|cavatelli]] or [[Wikipedia:Orecchiette|orecchiette]] pasta, [[Wikipedia:Olive oil|olive oil]], [[Wikipedia:Pearl onion|pearl onions]], [[Wikipedia:Edible mushroom|mushrooms]], [[Wikipedia:Kale|kale]], rainbow [[Wikipedia:Carrot|carrots]], seasoned [[Wikipedia:Bread crumbs#Panko|Panko breadcrumbs]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], and [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|pepper]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[shaak pot roast]] was a dish served at [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster's]] restaurant on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]].<ref name="Legends">{{Hyperspace|url=fans/hyperspace/source/dexdiner/index.html|text=Dining at Dex's|int=Dining at Dex's}}</ref>
