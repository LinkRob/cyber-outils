'''Demiran corn''' was a type of [[plant]] with tiny [[Color|golden]] kernels grown on the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] of [[Demir]]. In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included the recipe for [[Cup of Gold]] which made use the plant's kernals, in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]''. In the book, chef Tuggs, stated the plant was similar the type of [[corn]] grown by [[Kat Saka]] on [[Batuu]] but that he found the Demiran version to be superior.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:CupOfGold_UltimateCookbook.jpg|thumb|right|200|Cup of Gold from The Ultimate Cookbook]]
Demiran corn was mentioned in a recipe for Cup of Gold in the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The actual name of the plant was not stated in the book, instead it described it as having tiny golden kernels and compared it to corn grown on Batuu. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] used [[Wikipedia:Maize|corn]] as a stand in for the Demiran plant.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
