'''Dear as Crystal''' was a shop in [[Pau City]] on the [[planet]] [[Utapau]]. The shop sold crystals and was run by a [[Unidentified Pau'an shopkeeper|Pau'an shopkeeper]]. At some point, the [[Merchant|shopkeeper]] extended the [[Standard hour|hours]] of their shop, which resulted in it being looted by [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />

{{GameMechanics|LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga}}
During the [[droid]]'s rampage, an individual entered the shop and destroyed all of the battle droids. To show their gratitude, the shopkeeper offered the individual the contents of their safe, in which the individual found a [[kyber brick]].<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
{{EndGame}}
