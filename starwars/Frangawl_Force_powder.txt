'''Force powder''' was a substance created by the [[Frangawl Cult]] on [[Bardotta]] for the use in their [[The Force|Force-extracting]] rituals. A simple [[crystal]]line powder, the substance was charged with the [[Living Force]] extracted from living beings and extracted through arcane magicks. Once charged, the powder glowed a faint [[Color|blue]], and could safely be stored.<ref name="NoP" />
