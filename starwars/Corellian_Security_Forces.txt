'''Corellian Security Forces''', also known simply as '''CorSec''', was a local civilian [[law enforcement agency]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="TOG" /> As [[Gus Talon]], a [[moon]] of Corellia, lacked a substantial security force of its own, CorSec were deployed to the moon in cases of civil unrest.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> After the rise of the [[Galactic Empire]], CorSec served as an extension of the Imperial forces on the [[planet]]. [[Imperial Emigration Officer|Emigration officers]] were part of CorSec, although they became attached to the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]'s [[Surveillance (Imperial Security Bureau)|Surveillance branch]]. They were typically unarmed, and operated in booths with panic buttons that could call upon [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="TOG" /> A few of CorSec [[officer]]s wielded handcrafted limited [[H-7 "Equalizer" blaster pistol]]s.<ref name="Gadgets">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>
