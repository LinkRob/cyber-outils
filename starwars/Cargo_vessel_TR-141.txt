'''Cargo vessel TR-141'''<ref name="Subtitles">[[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance (special)|''Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance'' special]] {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> was the designation of an [[Intersystem Transport Ship]] used by the [[Resistance]] during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]]. It participated in the [[Battle of Batuu|mission to Batuu]], where it was helmed by [[Nien Nunb]] and under the command of [[Lieutenant]] [[Bek]]. It was tasked with bringing recruits from [[Batuu]] to the [[Pacara base|Resistance base]] on [[Pacara]]. Above Batuu however, it was captured by a First Order [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Finalizer]]''.<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />
