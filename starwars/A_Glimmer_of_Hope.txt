"'''A Glimmer of Hope'''" is a short story written by [[Charlene Newcomb]] with illustrations by [[Mike Vilardi]]. It was originally published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 1|''Star Wars Adventure Journal 1'']] in [[February]] [[1994]] and later reprinted in ''[[The Best of the Star Wars Adventure Journal, Issues 1-4]]'', both by [[West End Games]] before being made available online at [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]]. 

==Plot summary==
[[Alexandra Winger]] and [[Doro]], members of the [[Committee of Seven for Garosian Unification|underground resistance]] on [[Garos IV]], secretly monitor an Imperial [[mining center complex]]. The number of [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] personnel has tripled recently and Alex feels there is something critical being done with the [[Ore/Legends|ore]] shipments in the area. Another underground [[Team Two (resistance group)|team]] on the mission is discovered, so Alex and Doro attempt to run. Doro is killed during their escape, but Alex safely returns home to her life in the governor's mansion. Alex is the stepdaughter of the governor, [[Tork Winger]].

Alex has recurring dreams that she feels somehow will come true. In one dream, a [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|man]] with sandy brown hair and blue eyes is with her as she scales the [[Tahika Cliffs]]. At breakfast the following morning, Tork asks Alex to help him host a dinner that evening for Captain [[Brandei]] of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer/Legends|Imperial Star Destroyer]] [[Judicator/Legends|''Judicator'']]. Alex agrees and heads off to her classes at the university.

After her classes, Alex meets with Dr. [[Carl Barzon]] and [[Magir Paca]], two leaders of the resistance movement on Garos IV. She debriefs them on the mission that ended Doro's life and discovers that a cell member, [[Scat]], was captured. Dr. Barzon reveals that he has isolated a component in the local ore that creates natural cloaking abilities. It is imperative that the underground keep this out of the hands of the Empire. Alex promises to get as much information as she can at the dinner and insists on participating in a daring daylight raid on an Imperial ore shipment.

Alex successfully fulfilled her part of resistance mission during the attack, but on her way back home she is discovered by a university classmate, [[Lej Carner]] and taken hostage. On the way to take her to his [[Landspeeder/Legends|landspeeder]], another resistance member, [[Chanceller|Chance]], kills Lej and allows Alex to make it to the dinner at the governor's mansion. While at the dinner, Alex has another vision. This time she is in the landing bay of a starship, sitting in the cockpit of an [[T-65 X-wing starfighter/Legends|X-wing]] seeing [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|man from her dreams]].

After the dinner, Alex participated in a successful rescue of Scat from the Imperial Headquarters. During the rescue, Alex was able to warn other resistance members of sense of danger, when she felt a tingling sensation crawl up her spine not knowing she was [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-sensitive]].

==Continuity==
This marked the first published appearance of [[Alexandra Winger|Alex Winger]], a character who would appear many times in the run of the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]''.<ref name="TERC" /> In a later short story, the reader discovers that Alexandra Winger is [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-sensitive]] and that the sandy brown haired [[Gender/Legends|man]] in her [[Force vision/Legends|visions]] is [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]].

According to [[Charlene Newcomb]]'s blog, ''A Glimmer of Hope'' takes place in [[8 ABY/Legends|8 ABY]].<ref name="Charlene Newcomb's blog">{{WordPress|subdomain=alexwinger|url=2007/11/27/alex-in-the-sw-timeline|name=the alex winger page|text=alex in the sw timeline|author=[[Charlene Newcomb]]|date=2007-11-27|archivedate=20181002231438}}</ref> However, the text clearly states that three [[Standard year/Legends|years]] have passed since the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]], indicating that the story takes place in [[7 ABY/Legends|7 ABY]].<ref name="AJ1" /> However, ''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' places the story in [[6 ABY/Legends|6 ABY]].<ref name="TERC" />
