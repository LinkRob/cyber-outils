The '''EnhanceScan general purpose scanner''', also called the '''EnhanceScan portable scanner''', was a [[sensor]] manufactured by [[CryonCorp]]. It was roughly the size of the [[SE-90 Structural Engineering Scanner]], another product by CryonCorp.<ref name="Fully Operational" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> it was used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Battle of Hoth]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Empire Strikes Back" />

{{Tech-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The EnhanceScan general purpose scanner first appeared, albeit unnamed, in the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="Empire Strikes Back" /> It was later identified in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref> The EnhanceScan general purpose scanner's name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[The Essential Guide to Weapons and Technology]]'', a [[1997]] reference book published by [[Del Rey]].<ref name="Essential Guide">''[[The Essential Guide to Weapons and Technology]]''</ref>
