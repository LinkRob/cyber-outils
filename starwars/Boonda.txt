'''Boonda''' was a reformed [[Gender|male]] [[Hutt]] criminal who held [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] sympathies and operated a shipping business with his [[YT-2000 light freighter|YT-2000 Light Freighter]], the ''[[Vo Granee Droi]]'', after failing to graduate from the [[Corulag Military Academy]]. Using his business, Boonda supplied the Rebel Alliance throughout the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="BMF42" />
