The '''Chadawan navy''' was the [[Navy|naval force]] of the [[Chadawan government]], a remnant of the [[Galactic Empire]] located on [[Chadawa]] and headed by [[Governor]] [[Bordanivaux]]. The Chadawan navy defended the [[Chadawa system]] from both the Empire during [[Operation: Cinder]], and the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] during the [[Attack on Chadawa|Battle at Chadawa]]. At some point, they were joined by the former [[Imperial officer]] [[Colonel]] [[Madrighast]] of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|ISD]] ''[[Unyielding]]'', whom Governor Bordanivaux had offered refuge. Madrighast and [[Madrighast's unit|his unit]] joined the Chadawan navy and assisted in the system's defense.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
