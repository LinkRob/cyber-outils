The '''Alderaan system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Alderaan sector]] in the [[Core Worlds]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> It was home to the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] prior to its [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] by the first [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]<ref name="Episode IV" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Following that event, the star system's [[Realspace|spatial]] mass was slightly reduced and Alderaan's orbit became the site of an [[asteroid field]] known as the [[Alderaanian Graveyard]] that consisted of the planet's remains.<ref name="LD" />
