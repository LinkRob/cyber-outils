'''''FB-88''''' was the designation of a [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigate]] that served within the [[Republic Navy]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. At one point, ''FB-88'' flew by a celestial body along with a contingent of nine [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s. ''FB-88'' was painted in a [[Color|red]] livery on the central ridges and edges, along with the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] and areas in the aft section. Additionally, two golden stripes were painted on the dorsal port and starboard [[hull]]. The frigate was equipped with at least two dual [[laser cannon]] [[turret]]s near the rear by its bridge, at least two frontal [[cannon]]s, and at least one [[sublight engine]].<ref name="Armada" />

==Behind the scenes==
''FB-88'' debuted in a card of the same name in [[Pelta-class Frigate Expansion Pack|Pelta''-class Frigate Expansion Pack'']], a [[2021]] expansion set for the [[Star Wars: Armada|''Star Wars'': Armada]] miniature game from [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Armada" />
