The '''Aing-Tii''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]. Their neural activity resembled the motion and reactions of the [[plasma]] within [[wayfinder]]s, navigational devices used by both ancient [[Jedi]] and [[Sith]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Aing-Tii were first mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Aing-Tii/Legends|Aing-Tii]] first appeared in the story "[[Galaxy's Edge]]," published by [[West End Games]] in [[1996]] as part of ''[[The Kathol Outback]]'', a supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge">{{DarkStryder|adventure=Galaxy's Edge|book=The Kathol Outback}}</ref>
