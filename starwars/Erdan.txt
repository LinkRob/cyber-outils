'''Erdan''' was an [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[human]] that lived in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. A towering individual with [[Color|black]] [[hair]] and tanned skin, his face was marked half in white and half in black as a symbol of his ownership.<ref name="TCVD" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}} to [[32 BBY]].</ref> Erdan was walking through the outskirts of Mos Espa when he passed the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], the [[Gungan]] [[Jar Jar Binks]], and the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] as they entered the city. At the [[time]], Erdan was wearing an [[Clothing|outfit]] of varying shades of brown. It included a pointed [[hat]], a shawl, a shirt, pants, and [[boot]]s.<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Erdan appeared in the [[prequel trilogy]] film,[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']],<ref name="TPM" /> which was released on [[May 19]], [[1999]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> The character's scenes were filmed in [[Oung Jmel|Chott el Gharsa]], [[Tunisia]], between [[July 28]]-[[July 29|29]], [[1997]].<ref name="Archives">''[[The Star Wars Archives: Episodes I–III, 1999–2005]]''</ref>

In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], he was identified in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="TCVD" /> The [[Erdan/Legends|character's]] name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in the reference book [[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary|''Star Wars: Episode I The Visual Dictionary'']], written by West Reynolds<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and released on [[May 26]], 1999.<ref name="Amazon">{{CollectorCite|6|Books with Bite}}</ref>
