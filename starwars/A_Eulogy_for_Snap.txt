"'''A Eulogy for Snap'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story featuring [[Poe Dameron]] that was published in [[Star Wars (2020) 25|''Star Wars'' (2020) 25]]. The story was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Phil Noto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[July 20]], [[2022]].

==Plot summary==
During the [[Battle of Exegol]], [[Resistance]] [[pilot]] [[Temmin Wexley|Snap Wexley]]'s [[starfighter]] is shot down. [[Poe Dameron]] and his friends take a moment to reflect on the recent losses of three other pilots. Dameron praises Wexley's contributions to the Resistance, noting that he was a key part of their victories in the [[Battle of Jakku]], which ended the Empire, and the Battle of Exegol, which ended the [[First Order]].
