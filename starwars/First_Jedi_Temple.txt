The '''first Jedi Temple''', also known as the '''Ahch-To Jedi Temple''',<ref name="Ahch-To Databank">{{Databank|ahch-to|Ahch-To}}</ref> was an ancient building established around [[25,025 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> and maintained by the [[Jedi Order]] on [[Ahch-To]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> which housed a concentration of the [[light side of the Force]].<ref name="Star War Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> It was the first of many [[Jedi temple|temples]] built throughout [[the galaxy]], and one of the few that were not demolished by the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] came to live near the temple as a [[hermit]] after the [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destruction]] of his new generation of [[Jedi]] and his [[Padawan|apprentice]] and nephew, [[Ben Solo]]'s turn to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] of [[the Force]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

{{Struct-stub}}

==Description==
The first Jedi Temple was located on an unnamed rocky [[Temple Island|island]] on the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] of [[Ahch-To]], which would later be named [[Temple Island]].<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Situated atop a high ledge,<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> the [[stone]] structure was crudely carved into a cliffside.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> It consisted of at least one chamber that contained meditation plinths<ref name="TLJ VD" /> and an ancient font<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> with a mosaic affixed to the floor that depicted the [[Prime Jedi]] in a state of balance and [[meditation]].<ref name="TLJ VD" /> A vantage point with a [[meditation ledge]]<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> overlooking the strong [[ocean]]s that covered most of the planet's surface was accessible from the chamber.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The first Jedi Temple was first mentioned in the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], written by [[Lawrence Kasdan]] and [[J.J. Abrams]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> It later debuted in the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], which was written and directed by [[Rian Johnson]] and served as a direct sequel to ''The Force Awakens''.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the first meeting place of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]] was a [[First Jedi Council's meeting cave|cave]] on the planet [[Tython/Legends|Tython]].<ref name="The Old Republic">''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''</ref>
