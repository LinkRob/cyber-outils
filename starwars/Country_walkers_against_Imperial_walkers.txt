'''Country walkers against Imperial walkers''' were a group who opposed the usage of the [[All Terrain Armored Transport]], a vehicle used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Their logo depicted the silhouette of a [[humanoid]] individual walking, about to be stepped on by an All Terrain Armored Transport, with the caption of "don't AT-AT me!" This logo was featured in a [[Unidentified book|vehicle guidebook]] by [[Poe Dameron]].<ref name="Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
Country walkers against Imperial walkers were mentioned in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''.<ref name="Vehicles" />
