'''''Darth Vader'' 22''' is the twenty-second issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[April 13]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''CRIMSON HAVOC!'' With all secrets smashed and all traitors revealed, DARTH VADER unleashes his endgame! But is his
target CRIMSON DAWN &ndash; or the EMPIRE itself? And where does that leave the heroes and assassins who have fought at his side? Plus, a shocking reveal that cuts to the very core of the DARK LORD'S dark heart!''
