'''Anna Francolini''' is an [[Wikipedia:England|English]] [[Wikipedia:Actress|actor]] from [[Wikipedia:Chertsey|Chertsey]],<ref name="WhatsOnStage" /> England<ref name="ChertseyEngland" /> who was born in the early [[1970]]s.<ref name="WhatsOnStage" /> The actor portrayed the [[Imperial Emigration Officer]] [[Falthina Sharest]] in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. While their character remained unnamed in the film,<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Sharest was identified by name in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', the film's accompanying reference book, which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Solo Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
