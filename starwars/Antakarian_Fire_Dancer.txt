'''Antakarian Fire Dancer''' was a [[Color|blue]] colored [[alcohol]]ic beverage<ref name="HNT" /> served in the [[Outlander Club]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]<ref name="ATC" /> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="AOTCDate">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], which includes the appearance of Antakarian Fire Dancer, to [[22 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Antakarian Fire Dancer first appeared in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: How Not to Get Eaten by Ewoks and Other Galactic Survival Skills]]'' by [[Christian Blauvelt]]. ''How Not to Get Eaten by Ewoks and Other Galactic Survival Skills'' revealed that the drink was among the beverages included in the set for the Outlander Club bar in the [[2002]] [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']],<ref name="HNT" /> although the drink was not visible on screen in the film itself.<ref name="ATC" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, Antakarian Fire Dancer was introduced in ''[[Shadows of the Empire Planets Guide]]'', a [[1996]] sourcebook published by [[West End Games]] for their [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|roleplaying game]].<ref name="SEPG">''[[Shadows of the Empire Planets Guide]]''</ref>
