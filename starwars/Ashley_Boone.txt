'''Ashley Boone, Jr.''' was an American marketing and distribution executive in the film industry. As the Vice President of domestic marketing and distribution for [[20th Century Studios|20th Century Fox]] in 1977, he was instrumental in the financial success of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. The film's success led to his promotion to President of distribution and marketing for Fox, making him the first black president of a major Hollywood studio. After leaving Fox in 1979, he started his own consulting firm, where he handled the distribution and marketing of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="THR" />
