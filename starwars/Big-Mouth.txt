'''Big-Mouth''' was the name assumed by a [[Cloning|clone]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]]. [[Gender|He]] served as a [[Clone Corporal|clone corporal]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] in [[Depa Billaba's battalion|the battalion]] under the [[Commanding officer|leadership]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Depa Billaba]]. Big-Mouth was one of four survivors of the [[battalion]] when it suffered ninety percent casualties to [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] during the [[Battle of Haruun Kal|Battle]] of [[Haruun Kal]]. He and the other three survivors of her battalion, [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Grey (clone trooper)|Grey]], [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Styles]], and [[Clone Sergeant|Sergeant]] [[Soot]] joined her [[Depa Billaba's second battalion|new battalion]], and this unit included Billaba's [[Padawan]], [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]], and clone [[trooper]] [[Stance]].

==Biography==
===Disaster on Haruun Kal===
{{Quote|Not a lot of familiar faces, I'm afraid&hellip; the four of us are the only troopers remaining from your last command.|Grey informs Billaba that he, Big-Mouth, Soot, and Styles are the only survivors of Haruun Kal|<ref name="Kanan 9" />}}
[[File:General_Grievous_duing_the_Outer_Rim_Campaign.png|left|thumb|280px|Big-Mouth and the other troopers under Depa Billaba were defeated by General Grievous.]]
"Big-Mouth" was the name<ref name="Kanan 9" /> assumed by one of the many [[Cloning|clones]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] created on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] to serve as a [[clone trooper]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Clone Databank" /> During the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], [[Clone Corporal]] Big-Mouth was assigned to the [[Depa Billaba's battalion|command]] of the [[Jedi General]] [[Depa Billaba]] and [[Battle of Haruun Kal|fought]] alongside her on<ref name="Kanan 9" /> the planet<ref name="Force Users">{{EncyclopediaCite|Mace Windu and Other Users of the Force}}</ref> [[Haruun Kal]] against [[Separatist Droid Army|forces]] led by the [[cyborg]] [[General]] [[Grievous]],<ref name="Kanan 9" /> the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander of the Droid Army]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> The [[battalion]] suffered ninety percent causalities,<ref name="Kanan 7">[[Kanan 7|''Kanan'' 7]]</ref> and all but four of the clone [[trooper]]s serving with Billaba&mdash;Big-Mouth himself, [[Soot]], [[Styles]], and [[Grey (clone trooper)|Grey]]&mdash;were [[Death|killed]].<ref name="Kanan 9" /> The [[Jedi]] [[Gender|herself]] was injured to the point that she fell into a [[coma]].<ref name="Kanan 7" />

===Recon on Kardoa===
{{Quote|Trail's gone. There's no retreat."<br />"Contact the other squads and call in air support."<br />"Roger that.|Big-Mouth reports to Billaba as they are ambushed|<ref name="Kanan 9" />}}
After six [[Standard month|months]], Billaba awoke and took the [[youngling]] [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]] as her [[Padawan]] learner. The pair were assigned a mission to scout the planet [[Kardoa]] for Separatist activity and [[Clone Commander]] Gray re-gathered her four surviving colleagues to serve with her again including Big-Mouth, who held the rank of [[corporal]]. The [[commander]] also managed to have the veteran clones [[Lieutenant]] [[Remo]] and [[Corporal]]s [[Mixx]] and [[Kaylon]] assigned to the [[Depa Billaba's second battalion|battalion]] under Billaba's command, but was otherwise given inexperienced troopers fresh from Kamino to work with.<ref name="Kanan 9" />

Grey relayed this information the the Jedi and her Padawan in a briefing while they camped out on Kardoa's surface. The next morning the operation began and the Republic forces split into five [[squad]]s to search the canyons of the world. Big-Mouth was part of a team led by Billaba that also included Dume and the clone trooper [[Stance|CT-1157]]&mdash;one of the new troopers whom Big-Mouth kept a close [[eye]] on. As they traveled Dume and CT-1157 began joking about the short leash that Billaba and Big-Mouth kept them on respectively, earning them disapproving looks from the general and the corporal.<ref name="Kanan 9" />

[[File:BigMouthOrdersStance-Kanan9.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Big-Mouth ordered for CT-1157 to get back, but this order was ignored by the trooper.]]
As the pair joked, Billaba's squad came under attack from Separatist [[B1-series battle droid]]s that had been waiting in ambush, and Billaba ordered Big-Mouth to contact the other squads and request air support. The corporal tried to comply, but found that their signals were being jammed by the Separatists and informed that general that no help was coming. Billaba then ordered Big-Mouth to remain with Dume and keep the [[battle droid]]s occupied while she slipped away to find the Separatist's command [[Starship|ship]]. The clone did as ordered, but quickly realized that Dume was fighting too far out in front of the rest of the Republic forces, leaving him exposed.<ref name="Kanan 9" />

The corporal tried to call the Padawan back, but was too late, as a series of [[blaster]] bolts struck Dume across the torso and knocked him to the floor. Ignoring Big-Mouth's orders to stay in cover, CT-1157 rushed out to defend his friend, destroying a trio of [[droid]]s approaching the incapacitated Padawan. Billaba meanwhile had managed to locate the Separatist's [[shuttle]] and attacked it, causing the Separatist [[Colonel]] [[Coburn Sear]] to order a retreat off world so that his forces did not become stranded.<ref name="Kanan 9" />

===Battle of Mygeeto===
{{Quote|General Billaba, I've taken the liberty of deploying most of the battalion&hellip; ''Remo, Soot, Big-Mouth, Mixx and Kaylon'' are all leading squads|Grey reports to Billaba after first arriving on Mygeeto|<ref name="Kanan 10">[[Kanan 10|''Kanan'' 10]]</ref>}}
[[File:BigMouthOnMygeeto-Kanan11.png|thumb|left|240px|Big-Mouth visited Billaba in her bacta tank after the third Battle of Mygeeto.]]
The [[battalion]]'s next assignment was to the war-torn world of [[Mygeeto]], where they were tasked with clearing the [[Southern Mesas]] of Separatist forces during the [[Third Battle of Mygeeto]]. Big-Mouth gave Dume one of his trademark [[meiloorun fruit]]s before being assigned a [[squad]] to lead and setting out into the mesas. While he and his men were out in the field, Billaba, Grey, Styles, Dume and CT-1157, who now with the nickname Stance, returned to their camp after being rescued from battle droids by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Skull Squadron]], only to be attacked again this time by Grievous and Sear.<ref name="Kanan 10" />

Big-Mouth and the other clones were called back for support, but before they arrived Sear managed to kill Stance, only to in turn be killed himself by Dume. Billaba sustained injuries while fighting Grievous, but was able to hold the cyborg off until the arriving reinforcements forced him to flee. Following the battle a burial service was held for stance and then the clones and Dume visited Billaba while she recovered in a [[bacta tank]]. Orders then came in for the battalion to head to the planet [[Kaller]] as soon as the general was battle ready.<ref name="Kanan 11">[[Kanan 11|''Kanan'' 11]]</ref>

On Kaller the Republic's forces engaged Separatist forces under the command of [[General]] [[Kleeve]] outside [[Plateau City]], eventually routing them once they came close to the [[city]] walls. Kleeve and the remaining Separatist forces fled the planet, and after meeting with the [[Kalleran]] [[Gamut Key]] in Plateau City, Billaba ordered her men to set up camp for the night in the fields outside the city's walls.<ref name="Kanan 1">[[Kanan 1|''Kanan'' 1]]</ref> After Grievous ordered a major offensive, the [[Kaller system]] was again a target, so Billaba's battalion again fought battle droids, getting help from [[Clone Force 99]]. During the fighting, however, the battalion received [[Order 66]], and Billaba was executed by soldiers who had known her for years.<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You green kids have no business being out here!|CT-1157 impersonates Big-Mouth|<ref name="Kanan 9" />}}
[[File:BigMouthDumeAndSoot-Kanan9.png|thumb|right|250px|Big-Mouth was known for his love of meiloorun.]]
As a clone of Jango Fett, Big-Mouth stood 1.83 [[meter]]s tall and had tan skin, [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]]. By the time he was deployed on Kardoa he supported a large beard, which unlike his hair, was gray. Big-Mouth was known for his habit of constantly eating [[Meiloorun fruit|meilooruns]], often sharing them with his comrades and being able to procure them even on worlds where they were not naturally found. He was unhappy with the fact that many of the clones in Billaba's battalion on Kardoa were so inexperienced, believing they should have more training before entering the field. As such he kept one such clone, CT-1157, on a short leash during their deployment.<ref name="Kanan 9" />

Big-Mouth initially teased Dume for his relative inexperience, but when the Padawan took offense, the clone explained he meant no harm and offered the boy some of his meiloorun. After seeing Dume fight eagerly during the first fighting on Kaldoa, the corporal commented that the boy was doing well, but later had to unsuccessfully try and prevent him from being gunned down while fighting too far from his own troops. Big-Mouth and Dume formed a bond while fighting together on Kardoa, and afterwards frequently offered him meilooruns.<ref name="Kanan 9" />

==Equipment==
Big-Mouth wore [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] with green markings on the helmet during the battle of Kardoa and carried a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]].<ref name="Kanan 9" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:SootAndBigMouthsDeath-Kanan2.png|left|thumb|250x266px|Big-Mouth was killed in ''Kanan'' 2, but the events of the comic were later contradicted by "Aftermath."]]
Big-Mouth was created by writer [[Greg Weisman]] and illustrator [[Pepe Larraz]] for the ''[[Star Wars: Kanan]]'' series of [[Star Wars comic books|comic books]] and first appeared in the [[Kanan 2|second issue]] on [[May 6]], [[2015]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52010/kanan_2015_2|text=Kanan (2015) #2}}</ref> His initial appearance in the series, [[Kanan 2|''Kanan'' 2]], showed his death,<ref name="Kanan 2">[[Kanan 2|''Kanan'' 2]]</ref> but later issues of the comic showed Big-Mouth's earlier actions in the Clone Wars prior to Order 66, including his growing friendship with Caleb Dume.<ref name="Kanan 9" /><ref name="Kanan 10" /><ref name="Kanan 11" />

However, ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' episode "[[Aftermath (episode)|Aftermath]]" depicted Order 66 on Kaller differently, with Big-Mouth not being mentioned. Being the more recent source,<ref name="Aftermath" /> this article assumes "Aftermath" is correct. In ''Kanan'' 2, after darkness had set in, the clones sat around campfires in the fields, with Billaba sat with Dume, Styles and Grey. As they celebrated their victory over Kleeve, they received communication from [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], issuing Order 66, a command to execute all members of the Jedi Order. Despite their friendship with Dume and Billaba, the clones had no choice but to comply due to [[Behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chips]] implanted in them by the Kaminoans on Palpatine's orders. Big-Mouth and the rest of the battalion opened fire on the general and the Padawan, with Billaba killing Kaylon, Remo, and Mixx before Dume could even react. The boy then used his [[lightsaber]] to deflect blaster bolts back into Big-Mouth and Soot, killing them both. Styles and Grey soon managed to kill Billaba, but Dume escaped the clones and went into hiding.<ref name="Kanan 2" />
