The '''Ayoh system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Phelleem sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square S-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Ayoh 7]].<ref name="Origin" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ayoh system was first mentioned in the second iteration of the [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]],<ref name="Appendix" /> an appendix of star systems written by [[Jason Fry]] and released on [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 13]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref>
