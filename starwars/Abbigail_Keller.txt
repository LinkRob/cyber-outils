'''Abbigail Keller''' is a Visual Effects Producer for ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''<ref name="Mandalorian" /> and ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]].''<ref name="Stranger">{{BOBF|Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref> In [[2021]], she was awarded an Outstanding Visual Effects award for her work on the ninth episode of ''The Mandalorian'', "[[Chapter 9: The Marshal]]."<ref name="Insider 208">{{InsiderCite|208|Launchpad}}</ref>
