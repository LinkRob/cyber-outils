'''Barro''' was a [[human]] [[warlord]] of the fallen [[Galactic Empire]] and a member of the [[Shadow Council (Imperial Remnants)|Shadow Council]] for the [[Neo-Imperial forces|Imperial Remnant]] during the [[New Republic Era]], holding the rank of [[commander]] and leading his own [[Barro's Imperial remnant|Imperial forces]] from a [[Water|snowy]] [[planet]].

In 9 ABY, he participated in a meeting of the council. Years later, Barro met with a group of local leaders, during which he was [[Death|assassinated]] by the [[Mandalorian]] [[New Republic]] contractor [[Din Djarin]].

==Biography==

They are already getting sick of this New Republic and its rules and regulations.''"<br />"''And if we give them a show of strength, they will rally behind us.|Another warlord and Barro in a Shadow Council meeting|audio=ThereAreCitizensLoyalToTheEmpire-TheSpies.flac|<ref name="Chapter 23: The Spies" />}}
[[File:Warlord2-TheSpies.png|thumb|210px|left|Barro attended a meeting of the Shadow Council]]
After the fall of the [[Galactic Empire]], Barro was a [[warlord]] leading one of the [[Neo-Imperial forces|neo-Imperialist remnants]] active in the [[New Republic Era]]. He held a seat in the [[Shadow Council (Imperial Remnants)|Shadow Council]], a group of [[Imperial officer]]s who secretly coordinated their [[Imperial Remnants|remnants]] while concealling their true strength.<ref name="Chapter 23: The Spies" /> 

In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|23}}</ref> Barro attended a meeting of the Shadow Council via [[hologram]]. During the meeting, a [[Unidentified Imperial warlord 1|warlord]] on the council stated their belief that citizens loyal to the Empire were present on every planet in [[the galaxy]] and had grown weary of the New Republic and its rules and regulations, to which Barro responded with his belief that by performing a show of strength, they would rally the loyalists behind them. However, [[Captain]] [[Gilad Pellaeon]] replied that such strength should not be wasted and reminded the council that [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] planned return would herald in a resurgence of their [[Imperial Military|military]] and give [[Commandant]] [[Brendol Hux]] enough time to deliver on [[Project Necromancer]]. Later in the meeting, Barro joined his colleagues on the Shadow Council in chanting "[[Long live the Empire]]."<ref name="Chapter 23: The Spies" />

[[File:BarroImperialRemnant-MandalorianAndGrogu.png|thumb|right|290px|Barro during a meeting]]
During or after [[11 ABY]],<ref name="FilmDate" /> he met in person with a group of local leaders at a room guarded by [[cold weather assault trooper]]s located in a mountainous and [[Water|snowy]] location, during which he compelled them to praise imperial rule by chanting the phrase "Long live the Empire." Barro informed them of his decision to raise the tribute they owed him, which caused [[alien]] [[Prefect]] [[Nobah]] to object since they were already operating at a loss and were plagued by [[pirate]]s and thieves. Barro took this as a criticism of his protection and shot Nobah [[Death|dead]] with his [[blaster]]. Upon learning that the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] was attacking, he quickly gave the other five [[blaster pistol]]s before leaving in a [[turbolift]], but the leaders directed the Mandalorian to the turbolift where several snowtroopers open fire and Djarin killed them with a [[grav charge]]. After a warning from Commander [[Fremmet]], he ordered his two [[AT-AT pilot]]s fight the Mandalorian outside the hatch and ordered his [[vehicle commander]] to set the walker to [[autopilot]] and activated the [[Grenade|explosives]]. When Djarin came in he open the inside hatch and found Barro escaping in an escape pod. He was [[Death|killed]] by Djarin, who shot down his [[INT-4 Interceptor]] after hijacking his [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|All Terrain Armored Transport]] before it was blown up with bombs Barro had planted prior to his failed escape.<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" />

==Personality and traits==
Barro was a tan-skinned [[human]] with white hair that was graying by the time of the New Republic Era.<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" /> He was very eager to murder [[Nobah]] for refusing to accept Barro's new higher price for continued protection. Despite his self-proclaimed strength, he was rather cowardly, abandoning his snowtroopers and the locals to flee in a [[turbolift]] when [[Din Djarin]] raided his base.<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" />

==Equipment==
[[File:TMaG-INT-4-Interceptor-taking-off.png|thumb|left|290px|Barro's INT-4 Interceptor]]
Barro wore a dark grey [[Imperial officer's uniform]] with a [[belt]] and a [[rank insignia plaque]] on his [[breast]] with two red tiles on the plaque's top row and two blue tiles on the plaque's bottom row. In addition, the warlord wore a black jacket that held tan fur inside.<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" /> 

While attending a meeting of the Shadow Council, Barro wore black gloves and a [[Imperial kepi]] that both matched his officer's uniform and held an [[officer's disk]].<ref name="Chapter 23: The Spies" /> However, during a later in-person meeting, Barro did not wear the gloves and kepi. At times, he wielded an [[SE-14r light repeating blaster]].<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" />
