The '''attack on Vader'''<ref name="Vader 40" /> occurred between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Scourge crisis|scourge of the droids]] conflict and the [[Force Wave]] incident on<ref name="Vader 39" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Mustafar]], where the [[Scourge]] corrupted an [[octuptarra tri-droid]]<ref name="Vader 39" /> from the abandoned [[Techno Union droid factory]]. Having hijacked [[ZED-6-7]]&mdash;a [[droid]] servant of the Scourge's target, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]&mdash;after a failed attempted to corrupt Vader aboard his [[Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]'',<ref name="Vader 38">[[Darth Vader (2020) 38|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 38]]</ref> the Scourge attacked Vader and his other droid servants with ZED and the octuptarra. Through the skirmish, Vader managed to focus on his hatred for himself and re-establish his [[Force power|control]] over [[the Force]],<ref name="Vader 39" /> which he had struggled to command since the Force Wave returned.<ref name="Vader 33">[[Darth Vader (2020) 33|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 33]]</ref> With his Force powers his to command once more, Vader destroyed the corrupted octuptarra and corrupted ZED.<ref name="Vader 39" /> ZED was later rebuilt.<ref name="Tool of the Empire">{{StoryCite|story=Tool of the Empire|book=Revelations (2023) 1}}</ref>
