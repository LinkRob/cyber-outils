A '''barcode''' was method of representing data in a visual, machine-readable form. Barcodes were sometimes used for encryption and were printed on objects. They could be decrypted using [[datapad]]s.<ref name="SWD">[[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Barcodes (in reality, [[Wikipedia:QR code|QR codes]]) around the [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] can be scanned by guests using the [[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]].
