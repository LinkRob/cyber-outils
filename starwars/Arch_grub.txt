'''Arch grubs''' were a type of [[insect]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]], where they were [[Diet|eaten]] by the native [[Geonosian]]s. The grubs were served as a [[Foodstuff|meal]] during the holiday [[Meckgin]] and were popular as a lightly grilled snack at the [[Petranaki Arena]], where they were served during events in [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]].

==Description==
Arch grubs were a type of small [[insect]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Homeworld|native]] to<ref name="Blog" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Geonosis]], where they were on the lower end of the [[Foodstuff|foodchain]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==History==
Arch grubs were [[Diet|eaten]] by the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Geonosian]] [[species]] also native to the world, and were a popular meal during [[Meckgin]], a Geonosian holiday that celebrated Geonosis's devotion to industry.<ref name="Blog" /> They were also popular as a lightly grilled snack food at the [[Petranaki Arena]], a Geonosian [[gladiator]]ial and [[Death|execution]] arena at the [[Stalgasin hive]].<ref name="AEYNTKUE">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> The smell of the grilling grubs could be potent for non-Geonosisans at the arena.<ref name="Blog" />

[[File:ArchGrubSmoke-CL.png|thumb|right|300px|atl=A high-angle view down onto the Petranaki Arena shows columns of smoke rising from the stands.|Columns of smoke from grilling arch grubs were visible in the Petranaki Arena during the Battle of Geonosis.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="AOTC Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[22 BBY]].</ref> arch grubs were lightly grilled in the walkways of the Petranaki Arena stands when the attempted [[Death penalty|execution]] of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] was held there. The columns of smoke from the grilling grubs were still rising when the executions were interrupted by the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] breaking out in the arena.<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> Arch grubs were also grilled at the [[Eve of Meckgin Festival]] in the arena<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="CatalystDate">The [[Eve of Meckgin Festival]] depicted in ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'' takes place after [[Poggle the Lesser]] is freed from [[Galactic Republic]] custody and prior to his [[death]]. Poggle is depicted still in custody in "[[The Jedi Who Knew Too Much]]" and his [[Mission to Mustafar|death]] is shown in {{Film|III}}, with ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dating the events of both to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, the Eve of Meckgin Festival in ''Catalyst'' must also take place in 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> Their odor was wafted into the [[breath mask]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] in the arena's seats by the [[Limb|wing]]-flapping of Geonosians in the upper-caste galleries following an announcement by [[Archduke]] [[Poggle the Lesser]].<ref name="Catalyst" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], arch grubs were first mentioned in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> The [[Arch grub/Legends|grubs]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in the [[2003]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]'', which was written by [[Curtis Saxton]] and [[Simon Beecroft]].<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]''</ref>
