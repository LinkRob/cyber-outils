'''Chankin''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="The Dark" /> in the [[Chankin system]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim's]] [[Jidlor Marches]] [[sector]].<ref name="Appendix" /> [[Standard year|Years]] prior<ref name="The Dark" /> to [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Dark]]'' to [[18 BBY]].</ref> [[pirate]]s came after the [[Graf family]] of [[cartographer]]s on Chankin until the family's [[Parent|father]], [[Auric Graf]], fired a shot from [[Mu-class shuttle|''Mu''-class shuttle]] ''[[Whisper Bird]]'' while his [[Gender|daughter]] [[Lina Graf]] watched.<ref name="The Dark" />

In 18 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Lina needed to make a similar shot using the weapons of the [[freighter]] ''[[Moveable Feast]]'' against [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces to save the [[Bridger family]]. When her brother [[Milo Graf]] questioned if she had used a ship's weapons before, Lina brought up watching their father do so on Chankin, but Milo countered that the incident had been long ago and the ''Whisper Bird'' likely had different systems to that of the freighter. Despite this, Lina successfully made the shot.<ref name="The Dark" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chankin was mentioned in the [[2016]] novel ''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Dark]]'', the fourth book in [[Tom Huddleston]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Adventures in Wild Space]]'' series.<ref name="The Dark" />
