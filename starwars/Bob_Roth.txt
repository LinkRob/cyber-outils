'''Robert "Bob" Roth''' is the creator and executive producer of the animated television series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' and ''[[LEGO Star Wars: All-Stars]]'' alongside writing partner [[Bill Motz]]. He worked as a script writer for several [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] films including [[Wikipedia:The Lion King II: Simba's Pride|''The Lion King II: Simba's Pride'']], [[Wikipedia:Mickey's Twice Upon a Christmas|''Mickey's Twice Upon a Christmas'']], and [[Wikipedia:The Return of Jafar|''The Return of Jafar'']]. He is the co-creator of Disney's ''[[Wikipedia:The Ghost and Molly McGee|The Ghost and Molly McGee]]''.
