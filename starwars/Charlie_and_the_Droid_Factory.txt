'''''Charlie and the Droid Factory''''' was a famous [[book]], being considered a classic of galactic literature. It told the story of a young [[Jawa/Legends|Jawa]] known as [[Charlie (Jawa)|Charlie]] who wins the chance of touring a [[droid factory]], as well as operating it later on. The story also involved an object known as [[The Golden Widget]].<ref name="bully">''[[Jedi Academy: The Phantom Bully]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The story is a reference to the novel ''[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_and_the_Chocolate_Factory Charlie and the Chocolate Factory]'' by [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roald_Dahl Roald Dahl]. The novel features a poor but kindhearted boy named Charlie, finding a golden ticket in a bar of candy. This allows him to take part in the tour of the fantastic candy factory of eccentric candy maker Willy Wonka, and eventually operates it.
