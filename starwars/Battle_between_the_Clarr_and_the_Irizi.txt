In [[339 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Clarr family]] '''defeated''' a numerically superior [[Irizi family]] force by disabling one of their [[warship]]s' [[engine]]s and [[Comm unit|comm]] and [[Unidentified maneuver (elliptical orbit)|putting]] the [[Starship|vessel]] in a long, elliptical [[orbit]] that took it near the Irizi force.
