'''''Barnes & Noble Collectible Editions''''' is a series of hardcover leather-bound novel collections sold exclusively at [[Wikipedia:Barnes & Noble|Barnes & Noble]] stores. The series reprints a variety novels licensed to Barnes & Noble, including [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novels from publisher [[Del Rey]]. The first ''[[Star Wars]]'' book in the Barnes & Noble Collectible Editions was published on [[September 14]], [[2012]].
