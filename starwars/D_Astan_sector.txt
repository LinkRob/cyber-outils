The '''D'Astan sector''',<ref name="Falcon 70" /> also known as the '''Serenno sector''',<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Scarif and Other Planets in the Outer Rim}}</ref> was a [[sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that contained the [[Serenno system]] and the [[planet]] [[Serenno]]. During the [[Outer Rim Sieges]], the sector became the focus of [[Republic Military|military]] action from the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Falcon 70" />
