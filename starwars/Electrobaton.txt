The '''electrobaton''' was a type of [[baton]] used during the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] era.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order" />

==History==
[[Purge Trooper]]s were trained in the use of single and double electrobatons; these batons could block and parry [[Lightsaber]]s, and were capable of both stunning and killing opponents.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order" /> [[Cyrus Vuundir|Taborr Val-Dorn]]'s [[electrostaff]] could be split into two electrobatons.<ref name="Jankyard Joust">{{YJA|Junkyard Joust}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> The [[electro riot baton]] was a type used by Imperial [[scout trooper]]s<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order" /> and [[KX-series security droid]]s.<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>
