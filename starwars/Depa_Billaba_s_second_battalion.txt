In the final months of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Depa Billaba]] led a '''second battalion''' after her [[Depa Billaba's battalion|previous]] battalion suffered high casualties during a [[Battle of Haruun Kal|battle]] on [[Haruun Kal]]. With the exception of four survivors of Billaba's previous battalion who elected to return and serve under the Jedi Master, the [[clone trooper]] members of the battalion were very young and inexperienced. Depa Billaba's second battalion was deployed to the worlds [[Kardoa]], [[Mygeeto]], and eventually [[Kaller]] where the troops would turn on the two leading Jedi, killing Billaba who sacrificed herself to allow Dume to escape.

==History==
===Age of Republic===
[[File:DepaBillabasSecondBattalion-Kanan9.png|thumb|left|250px|Depa Billaba, Caleb Dume, and their clone soldiers were organized into a battalion.]]
Following Master [[Depa Billaba]]'s recovery from her [[coma]], she, along with her new [[Padawan]], [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]] (later Kanan Jarrus), took command of her new clone trooper battalion. Four of Billaba's officers from her previous battalion, Commander [[Grey (clone trooper)|Grey]], Captain [[Styles]],<ref name="Kanan 8" /> Sergeant [[Soot]], and Corporal [[Big-Mouth]], joined the ranks of her newly formed battalion. Aside from these four troopers, the battalion largely consisted of younger and more inexperienced clone troopers who donned plain or green armor, contrasting with the design of the older troops.<ref name="Kanan 9" />

The battalion was sent to recon [[Kardoa]] for confirmation of a Separatist presence. As the battalion move through several canyons, Billaba divided it into five squads to do their reconnaissance. The battalion came under attack by [[B1-series battle droid|battle droids]] under Colonel [[Coburn Sear]]. The battalion was able to force Sear's forces to retreat from Kardoa. A young trooper, [[Stance|CT-1157]], was given commendation for valor and the nickname "Stance."<ref name="Kanan 9" />

The battalion was sent to Mygeeto. There, the battalion cleared the southern mesas of battle droids. Billaba, Dume, and several of there officers and troopers were surrounded and pinned by the battle droids. They were saved by the [[Fang-class Protectorate fighter|Fang fighters]] of Commander [[Fenn Rau]]'s [[Skull Squadron]] and were able to evacuate the area. While most of the battalion was away from the base camp, Billaba, Dume and Stance came under attack by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] commander, [[General]] [[Grievous]] and Colonel Sear. Grey, Styles, and the rest battalion returned. Despite Billaba's wound by Grievous, the battalion was able to force Grievous to retreat from Mygeeto.<ref name="Kanan 10">[[Kanan 10|''Kanan'' 10]]</ref>

===Order 66===
{{Quote|None of this makes sense, those clones served alongside General Billaba for years. How can they turn on her like that?|Echo wondering why General Billaba was killed|<ref name="Aftermath" />}}
[[File:AllForcesAttack-Kanan1.png|thumb|right|300px|Depa Billaba leads her battalion on Kaller.]]
In what would become its final mission, Billaba's second battalion was sent to Kaller.<ref name="Kanan 10" /> Although the battalion had previously only included four of her previous soldiers,<ref name="Kanan 8" /> by the time of its final mission, a number of clones who had known Billaba for years had joined the unit.<ref name="Aftermath" /><ref name="The Lost Commanders" /> There, it fought against the battle droids under General [[Kleeve]].<ref name="Kanan 10" /> who would be forced to abandon the planet.<ref name="Kanan 1" /> However, Kaller was besieged again in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Coruscant]]. After [[Clone Force 99]] took out a force of droids attacking the battalion, the clones were issued [[Order 66]] by Supreme Chancellor [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. The entirety of Depa Billaba's battalion immediately obeyed the order, opening fire on Billaba and successfully killing her. Dume would escape, fleeing into the surrounding wilderness with the clones in pursuit.<ref name="Aftermath" /> In the following weeks, the battalion would continue their search for Dume as a force of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Kanan 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
The execution of Order 66 differs between "[[Aftermath (episode)|Aftermath]]"<ref name="Aftermath" /> and [[Kanan 2|''Kanan'' 2]]. In the comic, the order occurs at night, with Billaba and Dume cutting down clones [[Kaylon]], [[Remo]], [[Big-Mouth]], [[Mixx]], and [[Soot]]. After her death, Grey orders all clones, including the unit [[Rostu Squad]], to search for Dume.<ref name="Kanan 2">[[Kanan 2|''Kanan'' 2]]</ref>
