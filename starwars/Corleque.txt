'''Corleque''' was a [[human]] who served in the [[Imperial Navy]] with the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[admiral]]. In [[3 ABY]], Corleque and [[Administrator]] [[Sly Moore]] led a force of three [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]] while undertaking a [[Mission to Exegol|mission]] assigned by [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] to [[Death|kill]] the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in [[Red Honeycomb Zone|Red Space]]. Corleque and Moore unleashed hundreds of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] to stop [[Darth]] Vader, but the [[Sith Lord]] managed to destroy several [[Starfighter|fighters]] and to escape in a [[nebula]], where a [[Unidentified summa-verminoth subspecies|subspecies]] of the [[summa-verminoth]] resided.

==Biography==
===Attacking Vader===
{{Quote|Wh&mdash; What is ''that?''<nowiki />|Corleque after seeing the creature that resided in the nebula|<ref name="Vader 10" />}}
[[File:AdmiralCorleque-2020DarthVader10.png|thumb|left|250px|Admiral Corleque and Sly Moore on the bridge of one of his Star Destroyers]]
Corleque was a [[human]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] during its [[Imperial Era|reign]].<ref name="Vader 10" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Darth Vader (2020) 10|''Darth Vader'' 10]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Corleque served in the [[Imperial Navy]] and served under [[Firmus Piett]] in the [[Battle of Comantira]], where Piett delayed the evacuation by four [[second]]s to allow Corleque's late [[shuttle]] a chance to meet up with the escape [[transport]].<ref name="Vader 38" /> Corleque eventually rose to the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[admiral]] and led a force of three [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]]. Corleque and his forces assisted [[Administrator]] [[Sly Moore]] in following [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Mission to Exegol|command]] to [[Death|kill]] the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and the [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]] in [[Red Honeycomb Zone|Red Space]].<ref name="Vader 10" />

Moore warned [[Darth]] Vader that their Star Destroyers would have killed him, but the [[Sith Lord]] replied that, as Ochi had failed in the task, so would they. Corleque was confident that the Star Destroyers' [[Ion cannon|cannons]] would have shot Vader's [[Darth Vader's green Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|interceptor]] down, but Moore ordered several [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] to chase the [[Sith]] Lord's [[starship]]. After Darth Vader destroyed them, Corleque unleashed the rest of his TIEs. In order to escape, Vader flew towards a [[nebula]], where a [[Unidentified summa-verminoth subspecies|creature]], [[subspecies]] of the [[Summa-verminoth]], resided. The admiral asked what the creature was, and Moore replied that it was [[the galaxy]]'s greatest predator. The creature destroyed several TIEs that were following Vader in the nebula, but Vader managed to evade it and escape.<ref name="Vader 10" />

===Attacking the ''Executor''===
As the [[Scourge crisis|Scourge of the droids]] was unknowingly spreading throughout [[the galaxy]], Corleque was in command of five [[Star Destroyer]]s from his [[flagship]],<ref name="Vader 37">[[Darth Vader (2020) 37|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 37]]</ref> the ''[[Unbreakable]]''.<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> When the [[Scourge]] [[Attack on the Executor (Scourge crisis)|infected]] the [[droid]]s of the ''[[Executor]]'', the wider Empire was left unaware of the specifics of how the vessel had been compromised, but [[Mas Amedda]] declared that it could not be allowed to spread: Amedda thus ordered Corleque to destroy the ''Executor'' on the assumption Vader had failed to subdue the Scourge.<ref name="Vader 37" /> However, reasoning that Amedda was more interested in seeing Vader killed and wanting to repay Piett's actions from the Battle of Comantira, Corleque delayed the destruction and dispatched a rescue team. In the end, Vader and his personal droids would succeed in stopping the Scourge from taking the ''Executor''. However, the Scourge managed to spread itself to Corleque's flagship. Piett again gave Corleque four seconds to fix the issue but then ordered the fleet to destroy the compromised vessel.<ref name="Vader 38" /> Corleque was presumed [[Death|dead]] by the Empire,<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> though he survived and joined the [[Schism Imperial]].<ref name="Vader 42" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|And our cannons will catch him in due time."<br />"Let's not wait for the cannons."<br />"Pardon?|Corleque and Moore|<ref name="Vader 10" />}}
[[File:Corleque-2020DarthVader38.png|thumb|right|250px|Admiral Corleque before suffering injuries]]
Corleque had [[Color|black]] [[hair]], brown [[eye]]s, and tan skin.<ref name="Vader 43" /> The admiral would have preferred to let the Star Destroyers' cannons shoot Vader down, but Moore ordered to unleash the TIE fighters.<ref name="Vader 10" /> He also respected Firmus Piett, as he had served under him during the Battle of Comantira, where Piett had waited for four seconds, thereby allowing him to enter the evacuation transport, before it took off. Corleque also cared for his crew and pleaded that Piett think of the thousands at lives at stake when his flagship was compromised, but Piett only gave him four seconds once again before firing.<ref name="Vader 38" />

==Equipment==
Corleque wore a green [[Imperial military uniforms|uniform]], a green [[Hat|kepi cap]], black [[glove]]s, and black [[boot]]s. His uniform also featured a [[rank insignia plaque]] and four [[code cylinder]]s.<ref name="Vader 10" />

The comic, published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Vader 10" /> on [[February 10]], [[2021]],<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-darth-vader-10-exclusive-preview|text=It's Vader Vs. the Empire in Marvel's ''Darth Vader'' #10 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref> was written by [[Greg Pak]] and illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]] and [[Neeraj Menon]].<ref name="Vader 10" />
