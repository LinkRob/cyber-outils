'''Captain of the Line''',<ref name="SWCT" /> also known as a '''Line Captain''', was a [[Navy|naval]] [[Military rank|rank]] that was used by the [[Republic Military]] prior to the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Imperial Officers and Agents" /> When [[Kendal Ozzel]] graduated from a [[Core Worlds|Core World]] naval academy, he was granted that rank.<ref name="SWCT" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Vestae Alcorious]] held the rank while serving the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
