The '''Dentaal system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]]<ref name="Interior" /> that fell within grid square K-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Rimma Trade Route]] connected it to the [[Abregado system]] and the [[Giju system]].<ref name="TFABG" /> It contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Dentaal]].<ref name="LRM" />
