'''Braylen Stramm''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who defected from the Galactic Empire to serve the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] as a [[starfighter]] [[pilot]].

==Biography==
Braylen Stramm began his military career in the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] military stationed at their [[Naboo]] [[Imperial garrison (Naboo)|garrison]] before deserting his post to join the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. In the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]], Stramm reached the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[lieutenant]] and was made deputy wing leader of [[Blade Squadron]], under [[Wing Commander]] [[Adon Fox]]. He had a secret relationship with [[cadet]] [[Gina Moonsong]].<ref name="BS" />

[[File:Braylen_Stramm_ARC-170_XWM.png|thumb|left|200px|Stramm flying an ARC-170 starfighter for the Rebellion.]]
At some point during the Rebellion, Stramm piloted a [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170 starfighter]] during a mission to a jungle planet.<ref name="XWM">{{FFGXW|set=ARC-170 Expansion Pack|cardname=Braylen Stramm}}</ref>

Following the [[Battle of Endor]], Stramm was promoted to [[commander]] and became Blade [[Squadron]]'s leader. He was responsible for planning the squadron's operation during the [[Battle of Malastare (Galactic Civil War)|Battle]] of [[Malastare]]. The other members of Blade Squadron conducted a diversion while Stramm attacked an Imperial [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] and communications center.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{InsiderCite|160|Blade Squadron: Zero Hour}}</ref>

Stramm was promoted again and relieved of flying duties prior to the [[Defense of Kuat|Battle for Kuat Drive Yards]], and was assigned to coordinate the Alliance's starfighter strategy in the assault. When [[Johan Volk]] was injured in combat and excused from flying in the [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] escort section of the squadron, Stramm was substituted in to Volk's slot.<ref name="BS: Kuat" />

Eventually Stramm rejoined the squadron full-time, flying an X-wing at the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="Jakku">{{InsiderCite|172|Blade Squadron: Jakku}}</ref>
