'''Consular Jedi''', also known as '''Jedi Consular''', were [[Jedi|members]] of the [[Jedi Order]] who devoted themselves to the study of [[Diplomat|diplomacy]] or [[Scientist|science]], completely withdrawing from combat and warfare duties. Two such Jedi were [[Doctor]] [[Rig Nema]], who served in the [[Medical Corps]], and Madame [[Jocasta Nu]], [[Chief Librarian]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple's]] [[Jedi Archives|Archives]].<ref name="TVE" />

Although [[Depa Billaba]] had studied to become a Consular Jedi, she was a master of [[Form VII]] [[lightsaber combat]].<ref name="Force Collection">{{ForceCollection|Depa Billaba|star=3}}</ref>
