'''Crown of Corellia Dining Room''' was a [[Restaurant|supper club]] on board the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' that featured entertainment, including performances by a purple-skinned [[Twi'lek]], [[Gaya]]. The dining room offered breakfast and lunch to passengers each day, and in the evening served a lavish multi-course meal.<ref name="DPB Launches">{{DPB|url=2021/05/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-launches-in-2022-at-walt-disney-world-resort|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser Launches in 2022 at Walt Disney World Resort|author=Jeremy Schoolfield|date=2021-05-04}}</ref> Some guests could dine at the exclusive [[Captain's table]] at the center of the club.<ref name="Dining">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/dining|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Dining}}</ref> It was located on Deck 4.<ref name="SWGS Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)|''Star Wars'': Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Crown of Corellia Dining Room first appeared, unnamed, in concept art of the main dining area for guests of [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel. Its name was later revealed on the Disney Parks Blog.<ref name="DPB Launches" /> For an extra fee, guests could eat at the Captain's table.<ref name="Dining" />
