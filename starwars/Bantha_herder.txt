'''Bantha herders''' were individuals who herded [[bantha]]s<ref name="Resistance Rewind">{{SWYouTube|series=Resistance Rewind|text=#1.12: Getting to Know Neeku|video=f4iVFIX12nk|sw_url=video/getting-to-know-neeku-resistance-rewind|date=2019-01-14}}</ref>&mdash;large [[Hair|furry]] [[mammal]]s.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|bantha|Bantha}}</ref> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Path of Deceit date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]]''.</ref> the [[Evereni]] [[Gender|female]] [[Yana Ro]] mentally referred to a [[Unidentified Port Haileap dockmaster|dockmaster]] at the outpost [[Port Haileap]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Haileap]]<ref name="A Test of Courage">''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]''</ref> as a bantha herder while undertaking a [[Mission to Haileap|job]] for<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> [[Elecia Zeveron]]&mdash;the [[Mother (title)|Mother]] of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> During his [[Mission to Metagos|mission]] on the Outer Rim planet [[Metagos]]<ref name="The Glass Abyss">''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]''</ref> in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Glass Abyss date">The events of ''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]'' take place the [[Standard week|week]] after the [[funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] called the [[Iotran]] [[Commander]] [[Nils Slithis]] a bantha herder while being chased by Slithis in a [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="The Glass Abyss" />

The [[Kadas'sa'Nikto]] [[Neeku Vozo]] came from a long [[Neeku Vozo's tribe|line]] of bantha herders,<ref name="Resistance Rewind" /> which included his [[Neeku Vozo's parents|parents]],<ref name="The Engineer" /> on<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> the [[Hutt Space]] planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide to Batuu">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Kintan]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Resistance date">{{ResistanceDate|1|19}}</ref> they sold their most prized bantha calves so their son could become an [[engineer]].<ref name="The Engineer" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Resistance date" /> Vozo mentioned his [[parent]]s' occupation as bantha herders and their selling of the calves to the [[Nikto]] [[Nenavakasa Nalor]].<ref name="The Engineer" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bantha herders were first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the twelfth episode of ''[[Resistance Rewind]]''<ref name="Resistance Rewind" />&mdash;a series of short films that provided behind-the-scenes facts concerning episodes of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''<ref name="Star Wars show">{{StarWarsShow|NJfBB0VLVPs|Behind the Mask of Vader Immortal and a Sneak Peek at Star Wars Resistance|sw_url=video/disneyxd-behind-the-mask-of-vader-immortal-and-a-sneak-peek-at-star-wars-resistance}}</ref>&mdash;which was posted on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[January 14]], [[2019]]. The short accompanied the ''Resistance'' episode "[[Bibo (episode)|Bibo]],"<ref name="Resistance Rewind" /> the twelfth episode of the series' [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]], which aired on [[January 13]] of the same year.<ref name="Air date">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Bantha herder/Legends|bantha herders]] were first mentioned in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'', which was written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Curtis Smith]] and published in [[1987]] for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="The Star Wars Sourcebook">''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]''</ref>
