During the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]] a group of raiders '''attacked''' [[Onyx Squadron]], an Imperial [[squadron]] of [[TIE/rb heavy starfighter]]s. The raiders hoped to steal the advanced new technology used in the [[TIE series|TIE fighters]], which were being [[pilot]]ed by Imperial [[cadet]]s including [[Onyx Two]] [[Lyttan Dree]] and [[Onyx Nine]] [[Han Solo]]. During the attack, Solo saved Dree after Onyx Two became separated from the rest of the squadron, but crashed into a<ref name="SoloE" /> [[Cantwell-class Arrestor Cruiser|''Cantwell''-class Arrestor Cruiser]]<ref name="S&S">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> in the process. For disobeying direct orders from [[Flight officer|Flight Officer]] [[Ubbel]] during the battle, Solo was reassigned to the [[Imperial Army]].<ref name="SoloE" />
