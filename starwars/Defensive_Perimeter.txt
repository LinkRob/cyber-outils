The '''Defensive Perimeter''' was located along the perimeter of [[Fortress Inquisitorius]] on the [[ocean]] [[moon]] of [[Nur]]<ref name="Fallen Order" /> in the [[Mustafar system]].<ref name="Art of Fallen Order">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> [[Airlock 8]] was located within the Defensive Perimeter.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> during his [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|mission]] to restore the [[Jedi Order]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] [[Mission to Nur|infiltrated]] the [[Inquisitorius]] [[fortress]] through the Defensive Perimeter in order to recover a [[holocron]] that was stolen by the [[Inquisitor]] [[Second Sister]] on the planet [[Bogano]] after a brief [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] with one another.<ref name="Fallen Order" />
