'''200 BBY''', or '''200 Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

==Events==
*Towards the end of the High Republic Era, the Jedi Mostima and Sean [[Mission to Begamor|uncover]] the corrupt nature of Begamor's President Jamie Brasson after several years of investigation by a top [[Squawk|Republic agent]].<ref name="Vow" /><ref name="VowDate" />
*By this point, Sean had constructed his [[Sean's lightsaber|lightsaber]] with a [[Ilum crystal|crystal]] that he [[Force-bond|bonded]] with on Ilum.<ref name="Vow" />
*The Jedi Council sends Mostima and Sean to the Aakaash system to [[Mission to Aakaash|investigate]] the murder of Nan Holi, an executive member of the system's [[Land & Sky Corporation|ruling corporation]] who strongly advocated for the Republic against popular separatist tendencies and who made extensive studies of the Force.<ref name="Vow" />
*By this point, legends of Jedi using the Force to destroy entire planets have spread to Nan Holi's star system.<ref name="Vow" />
*The Nihil are recalled by Sean as an illicit organization that once invaded the Republic.<ref name="Vow" />
*At the Nan family villa, Sean is [[Attack on the Nan family villa|separated]] from Master Mostima and is saved from the [[Vermilion Birds]] [[pirate]]s by Li Yu. Li recruits Sean into the ranks of his [[Silver Knights]], a civil mercenary group mainly composed of his [[Currency|debtors]] that procures supplies for their own impoverished community on Wind, a planet of the Aakaash system.<ref name="Vow" />
*Sean is admitted to the Silver Knights' elite [[red apricot squad]]. They [[Mission to Fire|pursue]] a lost ''[[Silver Dawn]]'' shipment of [[Xuanming|enigmatic tenebrae]], which was lost on Fire, the space station.<ref name="Vow" />
*A celebration of the tenth anniversary of the launch of the 74th generation of [[Irregular]] occurs in the city of Fire.<ref name="Vow" />
*After defeating the [[Jade Rats]] on Fire, red apricot [[squad]] traces the enigmatic tenebrae to the [[Blue Dragon]] pirates. The squad realizes that they were manipulated into having to raid the Blue Dragon by the manager of Fire, Zhang Jincheng, though the Knights decide to continue their mission and find a way to place the blame of recent misfortunes surrounding Fire on Zhang afterwards.<ref name="Vow" />
*Red apricot squad forces the Blue Dragon to return the shipment of enigmatic tenebrae by threatening [[Zhang Níngtao]], an official of the [[Security Bureau]] who served as the contact between corrupt ranking members of the Land & Sky Corporation and the Dragon.<ref name="Vow" />
*Sean, determined to save the slaves who were held by Zhang, hires his own squadmates out of his own expense to evacuate the slaves. To do so, red apricot team enlists the help of Zhang, who agrees to do so on the condition that he would be [[Smuggler|smuggled]] out of the Aakaash system to safety by the Knights and a Hutt whose local name is "[[Kong Zhang]]."<ref name="Vow" />
*Zhang helps Sean deduce that the Nan family was responsible for the attack on Nan Holi's villa.<ref name="Vow" />
*The ''Silver Dawn'' ferries Nan Moyou to the Aakaash system's political capital.<ref name="Vow" />
*Nan Yujin returns to the capital from [[Muunilinst]] and helps Nan Moyou in her attempt to become the heir of the Nan family. Determining that there is no justice in the Land & Sky Corporation, they decide to continue using the Silver Knights as allies in winning favor amongst the ruling corporation's [[Land & Sky Corporation board of directors|leadership council]].<ref name="Vow" />
*Sean discovers that Zhuang Yuanying of red apricot squad is a member of the native [[Loongren]], who had been persecuted by the Aakaash system colonists and used as medical supply. Sean thus realizes that the local justice system ensures the suppression of individual rights, and that the Republic is knowingly ignorant of the persecution of the Loongren&mdash;Republic intervention would not solve the issue; not even the dismantling of the Land & Sky Corporation. Prejudice against the native humanoids is deeply ingrained in the culture of the Aakaash system's people.<ref name="Vow" />
