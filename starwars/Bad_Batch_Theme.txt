{{Quote|<nowiki/>''Bad Batch'' is more of an evolution of ''Clone Wars'' than ''Rebels'' was. ''Rebels'' was kind of a reboot and a different way of thinking about things. ''Bad Batch'' comes directly from ''Clone Wars''. They're the weird clones, they're misfit clones with special skills and special crazy personalities. So, the score definitely grows from ''Clone Wars'', just like ''Clone Wars'' grew from or changed throughout its run.|Composer Kevin Kiner|<ref name="ComingSoon">{{WebCite|url=https://www.comingsoon.net/tv/features/1172522-kevin-kiner-star-wars-the-bad-batch-composer|text=Interview: Star Wars: The Bad Batch Composer Kevin Kiner|author=Ames, Jeff|archivedate=20210507040017}}</ref>}}
The "'''Bad Batch Theme'''"<ref name="YT" /> was co-composed by [[Deana Kiner|Deana]] and [[Sean Kiner]]<ref name="Holocron" /> and performed by the [[City of Prague Philharmonic Orchestra]] for the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'s}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|seventh season]]. Initially released on [[February 21]], [[2020]],<ref name="YT" /> the theme is played throughout that season<ref name="TBB TCW" /> and in ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' to represent [[Clone Force 99]]. It is also played during the end credits of the episodes of ''The Bad Batch'', where it is followed by [[Omega's theme]].<ref name="Aftermath TBB">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> [[Dave Filoni]] told Deana, Sean, and [[Kevin Kiner]] that Clone Force 99 were a group of misfits like those in ''[[Wikipedia:The Dirty Dozen|The Dirty Dozen]]'' (1967), and so the composers watched the war films ''The Dirty Dozen'', [[Wikipedia:The Guns of Navarone (film)|''The Guns of Navarone'']] (1961), and [[Wikipedia:Kelly's Heroes|''Kelly's Heroes'']] (1970), and listened to their soundtracks. The composers then took the element of misfits from those films and incorporated it into the theme for ''The Bad Batch''.<ref name="Holocron" /> The first bar of the Bad Batch Theme resembles the main theme of ''The Guns of Navarone'', scored by composer [[Wikipedia:Dimitri Tiomkin|Dimitri Tiomkin]].<ref name="Navarone">{{VisualMediaCite|title=[[Wikipedia:The Guns of Navarone (film)|''The Guns of Navarone'']]|director=[[Wikipedia:J. Lee Thompson|J. Lee Thompson]]|prod=Highroad Productions|year=[[1960s|1961]]}}
