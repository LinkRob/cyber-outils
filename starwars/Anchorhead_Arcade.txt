"'''Anchorhead Arcade'''" was a recurring feature in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' which previewed various games. It replaced the prior [[Gamescape]] section after [[IDG Entertainment]]'s overhaul of the magazine starting with issue 77.

It was later replaced by [[Blaster (department)|Blaster]].
