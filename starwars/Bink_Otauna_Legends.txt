'''Bink Otauna''' was a legendary figure who along with his partner [[Tag Greenly|Tag Greenley]] constituted a mischievous duo. It is possible that the duo was based on actual persons, though [[Historian/Legends|historians]] continued to debate their existence.<ref name=":1">''[[The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

Possibly born on [[Alderaan/Legends|Alderaan]] he had many adventures with Tag. According to the legend, the two witnessed and also affected several important events of the [[Imperial Era/Legends|Imperial Period]] chronicled in the [[Journal of the Whills/Legends|Journal of the Whills]], the exploits of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]], and the lives of the [[Skywalker family/Legends|Skywalkers]] with outrageous and often hilarious results.

==Biography==
===Early Life===
[[File:JediTricksForDummies.jpg|thumb|Bink as a [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Youngling/Legends|youngling]].|left]]

Bink Otauna was born to a [[Alderaan/Legends|Alderaanian]] family on [[Alderaan/Legends|Alderaan]]<ref name=":0">''[[Tag & Bink: Revenge of the Clone Menace]]''</ref> in the last decades before the fall of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]].<ref name=":2">{{Film|III}}</ref> Bink was a [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-sensitive]], an unintended result of [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Darth Plagueis]]' manipulations of [[Midi-chlorian/Legends|midi-chlorians]]. Barely Force sensitive, he was enough so to be accepted into the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi Order]], where he met his friend-to-be [[Tag Greenly|Tag Greenley]], another result of Plagueis' machinations. Both of the boys were weak in [[The Force/Legends|the Force]], but they were able to scrape their way through their training.<ref name=":0" />

===Revenge of the Clone Menace===
[[File:TalkToTheHand.jpg|thumb|Bink trying to study in [[Jedi Archives/Legends|Jedi Archives]].]]
In [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]],<ref>The main events of [[Tag & Bink: Revenge of the Clone Menace|''Tag & Bink: Revenge of the Clone Menace'']] take place during the events of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], which [[The New Essential Chronology|''The New Essential Chronology'']] sets in [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]].</ref> in order to stay afloat, Bink attempted to modify their grades by computer, but in the process accidentally deleted [[Kamino/Legends|Kamino]] from the [[Jedi Archives/Legends|Jedi Archives]]. The two boys, afraid of being caught, fled, and ended up at [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]].<ref name=":0" />

There they tried to figure out what to do next, but were forced to run when [[Jango Fett/Legends|Jango Fett]], upon hearing a waitress' call of "[[Order 66/Legends|Order 66]]!", tried to kill them. The two [[Padawan/Legends|Padawans]] boarded a freighter bound for [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]], where they accidentally ran into [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]],<ref name=":0" /> who was escorting [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Padmé Amidala]] back to her home.<ref name=":3">{{Film|II}}</ref> The two boys cut a deal with him; he would keep secret about their transgressions and help them get back home, and they would help him with his attempts to win Padmé. While this worked for a while, when Anakin sensed that his mother Shmi was worried about him and left Naboo, he left the two boys behind, and they were forced to find an alternate way home.<ref name=":0" />

However, this was just as the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] broke out,<ref name=":3" /> and as a result it took them three years to make their way back to [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].<ref name=":0" /> In [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]]<ref name=":4">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''</ref> they arrived at the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] just in time to find [[Operation: Knightfall/Legends|a massacre]] taking place. Anakin,<ref name=":0" /> now [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]],<ref name=":2" /> refrained from killing them, as they had helped him win Padmé over so many years ago. However, he gave them specific orders: to forget they were ever Jedi and leave forever. The two did so, deciding that [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] would be a perfect place to hide where nothing would happen.<ref name=":0" />

===Tag & Bink Are Dead===
[[File:TagBinkAreDead1-CoverArt-EC.png|left|thumb|376x376px|Tag and Bink survived much of the main events of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].]]
[[0 BBY/Legends|Nineteen years later]],<ref name=":4" /> they had joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebellion]] and were serving aboard the [[Tantive IV/Legends|''Tantive IV'']] when it was captured by the [[Devastator/Legends|''Devastator'']] over Tatooine. The two were taken prisoner by a pair of [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]], but were able to overpower them and don their armor. Disguised, they were taken back onboard the ''Devastator'' and transferred to the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|first Death Star]]. When the Death Star arrived at [[Alderaan/Legends|Alderaan]], they stole a pair of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter/Legends|TIE fighters]] and headed for the planet. Unfortunately for them, this was just as Alderaan was destroyed, and the two found themselves in even more trouble when the [[Millennium Falcon/Legends|''Millennium Falcon'']] arrived. Bink cautioned Tag not to return to the Death Star, but due to the ''Falcon''{{'s}} jamming, Tag misheard and did just that, thus luring the ''Falcon'' to be captured by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]]. Still in disguise, the pair were put in charge of guarding the [[Tractor beam/Legends|tractor beam]] power console that [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] would later disable. They were almost called for duty as Darth Vader's wingmen, but managed to give him the slip and steal [[Grand Moff/Legends|Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin/Legends|Tarkin's]] personal [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle/Legends|shuttle]] [[Tydirium/Legends|''Tydirium'']]. They were leaving the Death Star just as [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] destroyed it, and their [[Hyperdrive/Legends|hyperdrive]] was knocked out, forcing them to make a landing on [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]].<ref name=":5">''[[Star Wars: Tag & Bink Are Dead]]''</ref>

This took them a few months, and by the time they arrived, the Rebels were long gone, the only things left being the leftover trash from the Rebel celebration and a medal reading "[[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewie]]" that had been forgotten. [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] arrived at this point, looking for [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]], but the pair got the drop on him. They tied him up and left for [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City]] to find an old friend of Tag's, [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando Calrissian]]. Fett escaped soon after, and the two were shocked to see [[Slave I/Legends|''Slave I'']] awaiting them. Lando wanted them gone, but when Tag reminded him that Lando had been taking credit for Tag's victory at the [[Battle of Taanab/Legends|Battle of Taanab]], Lando conceded and left to greet [[Han Solo/Legends|his other guest]]. The two hid in a room on [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City]], but when they were surprised by [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]], Bink blasted him as payback for getting them caught on the ''Tantive IV''. As a result, Lando made them escort [[Princess/Legends|Princess]] [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]] and [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] to safety, but first they were taken to the [[Carbon-freezing chamber/Legends|carbon-freezing chamber]], where Chewbacca knocked Tag unconscious. When he came to, the Empire was already taking control of the city, the Princess and [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] had already left, and a crowd of Cloud City's enraged inhabitants were pursuing them. Imperial reinforcements arrived in time, though, and the two were able to escape and get back to [[Mon Mothma/Legends|Mon Mothma]] and [[Gial Ackbar/Legends|Gial Ackbar]], with their captured Imperial shuttle in tow.<ref name=":5" />

===The Return of Tag and Bink===
Some time later, they were sent, again in the disguise of stormtroopers, to assist Lando in meeting a Rebel contact on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]]. When they interrupted his meeting with [[Kannen Doom]], who was supposed to help him get Han Solo back from Boba Fett, he forced them to help instead. They distracted Fett as Lando searched the ship, pretending to be an Imperial customs team, but not only had Han already been taken, but Tag and Bink were caught by Boba, who recognized them. They subdued him, and Tag donned Fett's armor, while Bink disguised himself as [[Taym Dren-garen/Legends|Taym Dren-Garen]], and together the two infiltrated [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba Desilijic Tiure's]] palace. Their first plan was to help Leia get Han out, but when she was captured, they went to their next plan, helping Luke get both of them out. When he failed, they went to the plan after that, helping Luke overpower the guards at the [[Great Pit of Carkoon/Legends|Great Pit of Carkoon]] and escape. Tag, still disguised as Boba Fett, went to help Luke, but the Jedi, mistaking him for the bounty hunter, attacked him. When Han Solo hit his jetpack, Tag sailed into the mouth of the [[Sarlacc/Legends|sarlacc]]. Bink fared little better. When they came to, they were in the custody of Kannen Doom, who had been looking for Boba Fett, mistaking him for his father, who had dumped her. Boba arrived soon after, gunning for the pair, and they escaped to meet their contact, [[Manuel Both-Hanz]]. He had information on a new [[Death Star II/Legends|Imperial space station]], including that [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] would be aboard it. However, he was shot in their escape, and Manny Both-Hanz died bringing the Rebels the information. The two reported as such to Mon Mothma.<ref name=":6">''[[The Return of Tag & Bink: Special Edition]]''</ref>

[[File:Ds2destroyed.png|thumb|250x250px|Bink and Tag died aboard the [[Death Star II/Legends|Death Star II]].]]
She ordered them to take the body to the Empire in their commandeered Imperial shuttle to convince them that the Rebels had not learned the information Manny possessed. They were once again waylaid when the Empire sent them to escort the Emperor to the new Death Star, this time disguised as [[Emperor's Royal Guard/Legends|Royal Guards]]. The two were put on duty in the [[Emperor's throne room]], where they listened as Darth Vader and Palpatine conspired to bring Luke to the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side of the Force]], and later as Luke arrived with Vader. They were surprised to hear that Luke was Vader's son. Palpatine dismissed his guards, and they left, hoping to find an elevator to make their escape in. However, the only elevator was on the other side of the tower they had just came from, and not wanting to look foolish, they waited, watching as Luke fought his father, was tortured by Palpatine, and Palpatine was killed by Luke's redeemed father. When Luke dragged his father into the elevator and left, they came back around and waited for the elevator to return. It was shortly after this that the Death Star exploded.<ref name=":6" />

On the surface of [[Endor/Legends|Endor]], as the Rebels celebrated their victory, Tag and Bink joined them as [[Force ghost/Legends|Force ghosts]], standing beside those of [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] (in fact, ''two'' Anakin Skywalkers, oddly enough, one rather older).<ref name=":6" />

==Behind the scenes==
Tag and Bink are two [[Infinities]] characters, protagonists of the [[Canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars: Tag & Bink|parody comic series]] by [[Kevin Rubio]].

Bink's appearance is based on Kevin Rubio's brother Brian. In [[Star Wars: Tag & Bink Are Dead|''Tag & Bink Are Dead'']], Bink appears significantly darker skinned than Tag; however, according to Kevin Rubio, this was done so as to further differentiate the two.<ref name="Darkhorse">{{DarkHorse|url=boards/viewtopic.php?t=9068&postdays=0&postorder=asc&start=88|text=TAG & BINK SPECIAL EDITION #1 -- Now in 2-D!}}</ref> By ''[[Tag & Bink: Revenge of the Clone Menace]]'', Bink and Tag both have a lighter skin tone.<ref name=":0" />

According to Rubio, during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] Tag and Bink were on [[Malastare/Legends|Malastare]], working as busboys in a local dive bar, and trying to collect enough money to buy transport to [[Alderaan/Legends|Alderaan]].<ref name="CW">{{DarkHorse|url=Help/Boards/viewtopic.php?p=213179#213179|text=The Clone Wars TV SHOW on Cartoon Network: WOW [spoilers?]}}</ref>

The exploits of Tag and Bink were canonized by ''[[The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia]]'' as legend. The book also hints that the characters may, themselves, have existed in canon.<ref name=":1" />

In the book, ''[[Tag & Bink: Revenge of the Clone Menace]]'', Bink is identified as "Bink Atauna".<ref name="Comicvine">{{GameSpot|subdomain=comicvine|url=images/1300-1549497|text=Loading your precious image&hellip;}}</ref> The mistake would be fixed in future editions.<ref name="Tag & Bink: Revenge of the Clone Menace">''[[Tag & Bink: Revenge of the Clone Menace]]'' (reprints)</ref>

Screenwriter [[Jon Kasdan]] and first assistant director [[Toby Hefferman]] portrayed Tag Greenley and Bink Otauna in the [[2018]] [[canon]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Deadline">{{WebCite|url=http://deadline.com/2017/10/star-wars-ron-howard-tag-and-bink-dark-horse-comic-book-han-solo-movie-spinoff-1202188549|text='Star Wars': Ron Howard Teases Fan Favorite Characters For Han Solo Spinoff|author=Ramos, Dino-Ray|date=2017-10-14|archiveurl=https://archive.is/RpiEn}}</ref> though their scene was ultimately cut.<ref name="WhytheCut">{{WebCite|url=https://comicbook.com/starwars/2018/05/26/solo-a-star-wars-story-deleted-scene-tag-and-bink|text=Why Tag and Bink Were Cut from Solo|author=Schmidt, JK|date=2018-05-26|work=ComicBook|archivedate=20181116082332}}</ref>
