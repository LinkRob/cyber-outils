'''Brenna''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[colonel]] in command of a [[Brenna's brigade|brigade]] in the [[Imperial Military|military]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. She believed that her brigade was superior to those stationed on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Biography==
===Galactic Civil War===
{{Quote|Civilian casualties are acceptable.|Colonel Brenna to her troops|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
{{GameMechanics}}
Throughout the [[Galactic Civil War]], Brenna and her troops engaged in multiple skirmishes with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], frequently against Alliance [[Major]] [[Shin]] and his forces.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], as the Rebels were evacuating [[Great Temple|their base]] on [[Yavin 4]], Brenna and her troops landed and attacked the base, intending to capture data drives belonging to the Rebellion.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

On [[Tatooine]], the Imperials became privy to an elite Rebel cell operating out of [[Mos Eisley]]. Brenna led her troops in an assault on the city, attempting to prevent an evacuating Rebel [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wing]] from taking off. Unlike the opposing commander, Brenna did not care about civilian causalities, letting her troops know that they were "acceptable".<ref name="Battlefront II" />

While at the [[Fondor Shipyards|Shipyards]] orbiting the [[planet]] [[Fondor]], Brenna was forced to command Imperial fighters when they came under attack by Rebel ships who attempted to destroy an [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] docked for repairs.

When the Empire discovered the Rebellion's [[Echo Base|secret base]] on the remote world of [[Hoth]], they launched a full assault against them with the intent of finishing the Rebels for good. During [[Battle of Hoth|the battle]], [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]] successfully destroyed the base's power generator, allowing Imperial troops to land.<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref> As the Rebels scrambled to evacuate what they could, Brenna and her troops attacked the base's east and west hangars, trying to cut the fuel lines so the Rebel ships would be unable to take off.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

Later, during the [[Battle of Endor]], Brenna was stationed at [[Command Sector (North)|Command Center North]] on the under construction [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. During the battle, a Rebel [[CR90 corvette]] crashed into one of the [[Battle station]]'s hangars. The surviving Rebel passengers engaged Brenna's troops in a skirmish with the main objective being to sabotage the station and escape.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

After the Death Star II's destruction, Brenna commanded a lone ''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer and several Imperial fighters in an attack against an [[MC80 Star Cruiser]]. The battle took place in the debris of the destroyed Death Star.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

On the moon of [[Endor]], Brenna was present at [[Research Station 9]] while they evacuated. The station and a nearby outpost came under attack by Rebel troops who tried to steal an [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] to use in their assault. Brenna and her troops mobilized and attempted to defend the base.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
{{EndGame}}
