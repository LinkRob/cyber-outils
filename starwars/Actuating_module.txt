An '''actuating module''' was a modification that could be added to a [[blaster]] for 500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. [[Mercenary|Mercenaries]], [[bounty hunter]]s, and others used the modules because it provided extra power to a weapon, although it also increased the weapon's recoil.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] used actuating modules to modify the firing rate and power of her [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Actuating modules were first mentioned in the [[canon]] continuity in ''[[Special Modifications]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]].<ref name="SM">''[[Special Modifications]]''</ref> They first appeared as blaster upgrade components in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />

The modules first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends]] continuity in ''[[Rescue at Glare Peak]]'', a roleplaying adventure module for [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] published by Fantasy Flight Games in [[2014]].<ref name="Glare Peak">''[[Rescue at Glare Peak]]''</ref>
