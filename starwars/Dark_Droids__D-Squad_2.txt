'''''Dark Droids: D-Squad'' 2''' is the second issue of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Dark Droids: D-Squad]]''. The issue was written by [[Marc Guggenheim]], illustrated by [[Salva Espín]] and [[Israel Silva]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 25]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE BATTLE OF THE CIRCUITRY! R2 VS. BT! 000 VS. IG-88! A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! ARTOO'S adventure across the galaxy to rescue his friend SEE-THREEPIO continues! But does Artoo need rescuing himself? Guest-starring 000, IG-88 and 4-LOM!''

|text=The SPARK ETERNAL has merged with an ancient droid intelligence to become something
new and terrifying.

This SCOURGE is corrupting droids on both sides of the GALACTIC CIVIL WAR.

Among them is the protocol droid SEE-THREEPIO. Under the control of the Scourge,
Threepio attacked his friend and counterpart ARTOO-DETOO and jettisoned him from
the Rebel flagship, ''Home One''.

Following rumors of a "homicidal protocol droid," Artoo has traveled to the planet of
Gallios&mdash;only to find the murderous droid TRIPLE-ZERO&hellip;.}}
