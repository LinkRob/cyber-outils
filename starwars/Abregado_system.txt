The '''Abregado system''' was a [[star system]]<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> located in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Beginner Game" /> The system was the setting for the [[Battle of Abregado]], an early battle of the [[Clone Wars]], which saw a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[fleet]] under the command of [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Plo Koon]] destroyed by a new [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] weapon, the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]''.

The system contained the star [[Anza]],<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> the orbiting planet [[Abregado-rae]]<ref name="Beginner Game" /> and, following the Battle of Abregado, a [[Abregado debris field|debris field]].<ref name="Concept Art Gallery" />

==History==
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> early in the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Jedi Council]], concerned by growing rumors of a mystery [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] weapon sowing chaos across the galaxy, sent [[Jedi Master]] [[Plo Koon]] to investigate.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

[[File:Abregado_SWCW_RM2.png|thumb|left|200px||The ''Malevolence'' fires one of its ion cannons]]
Koon tracked the weapon, revealed to be the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]'' commanded by [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]], to the Abregado [[Star system|system]]. Before his [[Plo Koon's fleet|fleet]], consisting of Koon's flagship the ''[[Triumphant]]'' and two accompanying [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]], engaged the ship, Koon contacted [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], who were stationed in the nearby [[Bith system]].<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

Told that they were unable to reinforce his position, and with their communications disrupted by the ''Malevolence'', the [[Kel Dor]] [[Jedi]] moved to engage. Before Koon's fleet was in firing range, however, the ''Malevolence'' fired one of its [[ion cannon]]s, sending out a massive shockwave of ion energy that left the three [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] cruisers without power and defenseless. The two accompanying cruisers were quickly torn apart by the Separatist ship's [[turbolaser]]s, and Koon ordered his crew onboard the ''Triumphant'' to evacuate.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

[[File:Abregado_SWCW_RM3.png|thumb|right|250px|A pod hunter latches onto Plo Koon's escape pod]]
Four [[escape pod]]s managed to escape the ship before it was destroyed, with one pod striking a passing piece of debris and detonating immediately. Koon, [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]], and [[clone trooper]]s [[Boost]] and [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Sinker]] manned one of the three remaining pods.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

Eager to keep the position of the ''Malevolence'' a secret, Dooku sent a [[Droch-class boarding ship|pod hunter]] to the newly created [[Abregado debris field|debris field]], with the aim of hunting down any survivors. The pod hunters mercilessly hunted down two of the pods, killing those within. Soon after, Koon's pod was spotted, and Koon decided to take the fight to the droids, heading outside the pod with Sinker and Boost, while Wolffe tried to keep the pod's [[distress signal]] active.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

Despite sustaining damage to their pod, Koon, Boost and Sinker managed to wipe out the pod hunters and destroy their vessel &ndash; though this alerted the ''Malevolence'' that not only were there survivors, but that they were fighting back.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

[[File:Abregado_SWCW_RM4.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Twilight'' flees a shockwave sent from an ion cannon of the ''Malevolence'']]
Soon after, the group were rescued by Skywalker and Tano in the ''[[Twilight]]'', who had defied orders from both the Jedi Council and [[Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] to look for survivors. With the clones being tended to in the cargo bay by a [[2-1B surgical droid|medical droid]], Koon, Skywalker and Tano were alarmed to see the ''Malevolence'' sweeping the debris field. Despite frantically shutting down the systems of both the ''Twilight'' and the accompanying [[R2-D2]], their position was given away by the presence of the medical droid.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

The ''Malevolence'' once more fired its ion cannon in an attempt to strand the ''Twilight'', but the [[G9 Rigger-class light freighter|freighter]] was able to escape the debris field and flee to [[hyperspace]] ahead of the onrushing ion shockwave. Dooku and Grievous were furious, knowing that their secret weapon would now be revealed to the Republic. Upon their return to Skywalker's fleet in the Bith system, Koon, Skywalker and Tano reported the news from their engagement with the ''Malevolence'' to the Jedi Council.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Abregado system first appeared in ''[[Heir to the Empire]]'', a [[1991]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel written by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the first entry in [[Star Wars: The Thrawn Trilogy|''The Thrawn Trilogy'']].<ref name="Heir to the Empire">''[[Heir to the Empire]]''</ref> It became [[canon]] when it appeared in the opening flashbacks of "[[Ambush]],"<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> the first episode of the canon animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]], which originally aired on [[October 3]], [[2008]].<ref name="Ambush Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode001.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Ambush}}</ref> It was first identified as the Abregado system in "[[Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence'']]," the second episode of ''The Clone Wars''{{'}} first season,<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> which also aired on October 3, 2008.<ref name="Rising Malevolence Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode002.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref>

The name "Abregado" may be derived from the Spanish word "Abrigado," meaning "warm" or "snug."
