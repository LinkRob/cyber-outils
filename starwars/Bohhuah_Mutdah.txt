'''Bohhuah Mutdah''' was an individual who craved immense riches. According to a tale told by the [[Mustafarian]] [[Tuttel]], after a [[Welk's owner|Twi'lek girl]] who was led by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] into retrieving the [[Gem of the Luminous]] from an ancient temple was attacked by [[droid]]s designed to ward off intruders, she claimed that being rich beyond Mutdah's dreams was not worth the effort to acquire the artifact.<ref name="Shadow of Vader's Castle" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bohhuah Mutdah was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in a flashback sequence of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] ''[[Star Wars Adventures: Shadow of Vader's Castle]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="Shadow of Vader's Castle" /> on [[November 4]], [[2020]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/shadow-of-vaders-castle-interview|text=In the ''Shadow of Vader's Castle'' with Cavan Scott}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Bohhuah Mutdah/Legends|Bohhuah Mutdah]] originated in the [[1983]] novel ''[[Lando Calrissian and the Flamewind of Oseon]]'', written by [[L. Neil Smith]].<ref name="FlamewindOfOseon">''[[Lando Calrissian and the Flamewind of Oseon]]''</ref>
