A '''theme''' was written by [[John Williams]] for the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. Representing [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], a [[Wikipedia:Figure (music)|figure]] of the [[Wikipedia:Leitmotif|leitmotif]] is also played in "[[The Emperor's Theme]]," and the figure is played backwards in "[[Rey's Theme]]."<ref name="TFA" />

The theme is introduced during Kylo Ren's first appearance in ''The Force Awakens'', during the [[First Order]]'s [[Attack on Tuanul|attack]] on [[Tuanul]]. The theme is played throughout the film, including the tracks "Main Title and The Attack on the Jakku Village" and "Kylo Ren Arrives at the Battle."

In introducing the theme for Kylo Ren, Williams approached it as an extension of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and "[[The Imperial March]]," without being too similar. He wanted the theme to convey Ren's evil power, his strength, and the threat he poses in a way that would be immediately accessible to the audience.<ref name="Sound of a Galaxy" />

In [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] Williams composed light side edition of the theme, which represents the return of Ben Solo to the [[light side of the Force]].
