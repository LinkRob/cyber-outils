A '''club''',<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/trespass-episode-guide|text="Trespass" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> also known as a '''cudgel''',<ref name="Talz">{{SWE|groups|talz|Talz}}</ref> was a type of [[Melee weapon|blunt weapon]] found in [[the galaxy]]. Clubs were used by the [[Talz]] of [[Orto Plutonia]],<ref name="Trespass">{{TCW|Trespass}}</ref> and the [[Tusken Raider]]s of [[Tatooine]] used bladed clubs known as [[gaderffii stick]]s.<ref name="Tusken Raiders">{{Databank|tusken-raiders|Tusken Raiders}}</ref> During the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[Ewoks]] used wooden clubs and other primitive weapons to fight the [[Stormtroopers]].<ref>Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi</ref> The [[Quarren]] also used clubs to attack members of the [[Resistance]] while they were on a [[Mission to Mon Cala|mission]] on [[Mon Cala]], but to no avail.<ref name="Allegiance 2">[[Allegiance 2|''Allegiance'' 2]]</ref>
