The '''Dragon Void Run''', also known simply as the '''Dragon Void''', was an old, dangerous race held in [[the galaxy]]. The race traveled between three [[planet]]s, stopping for mandatory [[Fuel|refueling]] points at each. [[Pilot]]s of the Dragon Void would train specifically for the race, paying an expensive entry fee with the assistance of sponsors. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] entered the race as an undercover agent for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] to rescue Rebel [[Spy|spies]] who would meet him at the fuel markers.<ref name="HS1" /> The [[Loo Re Anno's grandmother|grandmother]] of [[Loo Re Anno]] was a founding member of the Dragon Void Run.<ref name="HS4">[[Han Solo 4|''Han Solo'' 4]]</ref>
