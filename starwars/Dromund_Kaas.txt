{{Quote|It appears, given our preliminary surveys, that Palpatine had spent years, either before or after his 'death' at Endor, gathering up relics from worlds that had previously been thought to be myths like Moraband (or Korriban as it is referred to in Sith texts) and Dromund Kaas.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
'''Dromund Kaas''' was a [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Rise" /> in the [[Dromund Kaas system]] of the [[Sith Worlds]] within the [[Esstran sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It fell within grid square R-5 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Along with the planet [[Moraband]], it was once believed to be a myth.<ref name="Rise" />

During his reign as the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Darth Sidious]] was believed by [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] to have visited this world in search of [[Sith artifact|Sith relics]], based upon findings made by the [[Exegol Excavation Project]] on the [[Sith]]-controlled planet [[Exegol]]. Kin mentioned Dromund Kaas in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Dromund Kaas was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" /> The [[Dromund Kaas/Legends|world]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Mysteries of the Sith]]'', the [[1998]] expansion to<ref name="Mysteries">''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Mysteries of the Sith]]''</ref> the [[1997]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]''.<ref name="DKDF2">''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]''</ref>
