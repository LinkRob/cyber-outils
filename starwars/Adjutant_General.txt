'''Adjutant General''' was a [[military rank]] used by the [[Republic Military|Armed Forces]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Less than a [[Standard year|year]] after his promotion to [[admiral]], [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] was promoted to Adjutant General following his investigation of the [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bombing]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. This allowed him to serve as a prosecutor in the [[trial of Ahsoka Tano]]. The rank of adjutant general encompassed both the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]<ref name="Imperial Officers and Agents" /> and the [[Republic Navy]].<ref name="Catalyst" />
