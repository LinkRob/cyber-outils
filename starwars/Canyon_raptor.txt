'''Canyon raptors'''<ref name="TMVD">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> were a [[species]] of giant [[Diet|predatory]] [[reptavian]]<ref name="Chapter 20" /> native to the "[[Armorer's planet]]."<ref name="SWECG" /> Following the [[rescue of Ragnar Vizsla]], the [[Children of the Watch]] named them '''Shriek-hawks''' after the [[Shriek-hawk Training team]],<ref name="TMVD" /> which themselves shared a name with the ancient [[Mandalore|Mandalorian]] [[bird]] [[Shriek-hawk]]s.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>

==Description==
The canyon raptors were tenacious hunters. They could fly distances up to 5 kilometers at a time, beyond the operational range of [[Mandalorian]] [[jetpack]]s. They protected their young by building inaccessible nests high in the canyon spires. The chicks preferred live food.<ref name="TMVD" />

==History==
[[File:Raptor bird.png|thumb|250px|left|A canyon raptor with its young]]
One raptor frequently caught members of [[the Tribe]] and brought them to its nest, where it fed them to its children. After [[Ragnar Vizsla]] was captured, the [[Shriek-hawk Training team]] had rescued him and killed the hunting raptor.<ref name="Chapter 20" /> The Shriek-Hawks granted their name to the surviving raptor chicks, adopting them to their clan.<ref name="TMVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Canyon raptors first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' episode "[[Chapter 20: The Foundling]]."<ref name="Chapter 20" /> Initially unnamed, they were identified as shriek-hawks by [[Topps]] for ''[[Star Wars: Card Trader]]''.<ref name="Topps">{{ToppsDigital|url=swcardtrader/article/0da4719a-d63c-4ab9-8e33-3c707c7b2bb4|text=Featured Creature continues! Collect A Shriek-Hawk in Week 10!}}</ref> which was repeated in later releases.<ref name="Monstrous">{{SWCT|set=Monstrous|cardname=Shriek-hawk}}</ref><ref name="Topps 2025">{{Topps|set=2025 Topps Star Wars Chrome|cardname=Ragnar Vizsla|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/544247/cid/31223636/2025-Topps-Chrome-Star-Wars-154-Ragnar-Vizsla}}</ref> However, ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'' clarified that the raptors were named Shriek-hawks by the Shriek-Hawk trainers when they were adopted to their clan.<ref name="TMVD" />
