The '''Basilisk system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]]. It was situated at the coordinates L-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and shared a name with the [[Basilisk war droid]].<ref name="Nexus of Power">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Basilisk system was mentioned in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
