'''CT-380'''<ref name="CT-Name">{{SWU|set=Twilight of the Republic (Star Wars: Unlimited)|cardname=Squad Support|link=3292172753|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20241217163744/https://swudb.com/card/TWI/122/squad-support}} depicts this [[clone cadet]] with the number "380" written in [[aurebesh]] on their left [[Limb|leg]]. The [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]] [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]] episode "[[Clone Cadets]]" also depicts [[Cloning|clone]] cadets with numbers on their left legs, which the second image in the episode's [https://www.starwars.com/series/clone-wars/clone-cadets-trivia-gallery trivia gallery] establishes these numbers to be their CT designation. Therefore, this clone's full designation must be CT-380.</ref> was a [[clone cadet]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Squad" /> Created on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] from the [[Clone template|genetic template]] of [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], CT-380 was a [[Cloning|clone]] who shared Fett's<ref name="Clones" /> [[Color|tan]] skin<ref name="Squad" /> and height of 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|ft]]).<ref name="Clones" /> CT-380 weighed 80 [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Weight" /> As a [[cadet]] in a [[squad]], CT-380 participated in a [[Tipoca City Training Facility|training simulation]] where they wielded a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] and dawned green [[Clone training armor|training armor]] marked with the number three, and their designation "380" on their left [[Limb|leg]]. During the training simulation, CT-380 and their squad mates took cover while under [[blaster]]fire and provided support for each other.<ref name="Squad" />

==Behind the scenes==
CT-380 debuted in ''[[Twilight of the Republic (Star Wars: Unlimited)|Twilight of the Republic]]'',<ref name="Squad" /> the third set for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: Unlimited|''Star Wars'': Unlimited]], released on [[November 8]], [[2024]].<ref name="Twilight">{{UnlimitedWeb|url=articles/twilight-of-the-republic|text=Twilight of the Republic}}</ref> CT-380 and their designation were depicted on the card [https://web.archive.org/web/20241217163744/https://swudb.com/card/TWI/122/squad-support Squad Support] illustrated by artist [[Eslam AboShady]].<ref name="Squad" />
