'''Dhandu 3''', often known as simply '''Dhandu''', was the third of three [[moon]]s that orbited the [[planet]] [[Dhandu]]<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> in the [[Dhandu system]] in the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was home to the [[Turlossus]], a massive six-[[Limb|legged]] [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[species]]. Because the creatures were so massive that any structures built on the ground were at risk of being crushed under their feet, inhabitants of the moon built their [[City|cities]] on top of the Turlossus' flat shells. Different parts of the cities were connected by bridges and catwalks that could be raised and lowered. The moon was also home to the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Dhanduese]], a flightless, feathered [[Bird|avian]] species.<ref name="Pirate's Price" />

Sporting [[grass]]y terrain, Dhandu 3 was known for its very low [[gravity]], and visitors to the moon were required to wear [[gravity weight]]s in order to walk safely. After being hired by [[Mahjo Reeloo]] to help steal [[Novian ruby|Novian rubies]] from [[Jayyar Lu-wehs]]' safety deposit box in the [[Undervaults of Gwongdeen|Undervaults]] of [[Gwongdeen]], [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] visited Dhandu 3 in the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. Also accompanied by [[Hondo Ohnaka]], the group came to the moon to steal the key to Lu-wehs' vault.<ref name="Pirate's Price" />
