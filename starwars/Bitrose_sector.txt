The '''Bitrose sector''' was a [[sector]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> It contained the [[space station]] [[Roost]] at least right before its destruction in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /><ref name="Destruction date">{{Topps|set=2023 Topps Star Wars|cardname=Jib Dodger|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/370777/cid/22170344}} confirms that the Roost was destroyed during the events of "[[Chapter 6: The Prisoner]]," which occurs in [[9 ABY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

{{Sec-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Bitrose sector had its first appearance in [[canon]] in "[[Chapter 6: The Prisoner]]," the sixth episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.<ref name="Chapter 6">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 6: The Prisoner}}</ref> However it was first identified as the Bitrose sector in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], it was first mentioned in ''[[Wanted by Cracken (sourcebook)]]'', a paperback supplement to ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Wanted">[[Wanted by Cracken (sourcebook)|''Wanted by Cracken'']]</ref>
