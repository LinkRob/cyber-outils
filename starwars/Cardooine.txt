'''Cardooine''' was an astronomical object in [[the galaxy]] where the criminal [[Zann Consortium]] were active.<ref name="S&S">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> It was located in the [[Cardooine system]] in the [[Jospro sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], in grid square R-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

{{RPGseed}}
During the [[Imperial Era]], the leader of the criminal [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate asked a group of [[spacer]]s to make amends for trouble they had caused the syndicate by eliminating its Zann Consortium rivals on Cardooine.<ref name="S&S" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Cardooine was first mentioned in the [[2020]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement, ''[[Starships and Speeders]]''.<ref name="S&S" /> [[Cardooine/Legends|Cardooine]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was created by [[Timothy Zahn]] for the [[1993]] novel ''[[The Last Command]]''.<ref name="LastCommand">''[[The Last Command]]''</ref>
