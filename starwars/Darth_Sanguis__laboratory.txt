{{Stub}}

==Description==
The laboratory of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sanguis]] was located in the labyrinthine system of meticulously carved tunnels deep below the [[Sith Citadel]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]], a remote world steeped in the rich history of the [[Sith]]. It was home to a population of [[rat-grub]]s. The walls were emblazoned with the oldest of [[Sith alphabet|Sith runes]], and the floor&mdash;covered in a thick layer of dust and detritus&mdash;had once been marked out in a symbolic set of interlocking circles.<ref name="A Life Immortal" />

Bottles and jars filled with unidentifiable tinctures cluttered [[obsidian]] work surfaces, and crumbling [[Book|tomes]], bound in cracked hide of some kind and covered in a thick smear of [[spider]]webs, sat on a crooked [[wood]]en shelf. A [[mirror]] sat at the edge of one of the obsidian workbenches. Another one had a disintegrated [[Unidentified Sith book (Exegol)|book]] that contained the designs that resembled structures on [[Malachor]]. Something that had once been flesh and blood, but had long before putrefied, sat suspended in a filthy tank of some unknown substance.<ref name="A Life Immortal" />

==History==
Deep below the [[Sith Citadel]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Noctyss]] discovered [[Darth Sanguis]]' laboratory and notes on the ritual. After much study, she completed the three-day ritual, sacrificing Sanguis. The ritual then twisted her into a creature much like Sanguis had become, revealing to her that her servant, whom she suddenly understood had been Sanguis, had manipulated her to be freed of his immortal fate.<ref name="A Life Immortal" />
