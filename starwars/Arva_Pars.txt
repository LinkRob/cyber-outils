'''Arva Pars''' was an organization active during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Aphra 6" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 6]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the group's leaders entered into negotiations with the [[Tagge Corporation]] over a sale. During the discussions, Arva Pars' leaders expressed their readiness to negotiate with the Tagge Corporation, which<ref name="Aphra 6" /> [[Lapin Tagge]]<ref name="Aphra 28">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 28|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 28]]</ref>&mdash;the [[secretary]]<ref name="Boushh">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]]</ref> of the Tagge Corporation's [[Executive officer|leader]], [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]]&mdash;informed Domina of. In response, she told Lapin to not send a reply, expecting it to cause Arva Pars to question their offer before they reduced their asking price by twenty percent the following [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="Aphra 6" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arva Pars was mentioned in flashbacks in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|sixth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 6" /> on [[November 25]], 2020. Prior to the release of the comic, the organization was mentioned in a preview article of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[November 18]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-doctor-aphra-6-exclusive-preview|text=There's a New Bounty in Marvel's ''Doctor Aphra'' #6 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
