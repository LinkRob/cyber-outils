'''Code K-one-zero''', sometimes written as '''Plan Kay One Zero''', was an emergency protocol that originated in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]<ref name="TC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> and was later used by the [[New Republic]]<ref name="Empire's End" /> and the [[Resistance]]. It was an order to evacuate a base,<ref name="TFAVD" /> such as the [[Battle of Hoth|evacuation]] of [[Echo Base]].<ref name="TC" />

{{Reb-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Code K-one-zero first appeared in the Decipher Star Wars Customizable Card Game expansion ''Hoth'' on the card ''Fall Back''. It later appeared as "Plan Kay One Zero" in ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', a novel written by [[Alexander Freed]] and published in [[2015]]. Later that year, it was mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]], and was identified as "Code K-one-zero."
