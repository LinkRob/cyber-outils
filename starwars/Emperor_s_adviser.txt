The '''Emperor's advisers''', also known as '''Emperor's advisors''' or '''Imperial advisors''', were high-ranking [[bureaucrat]]s within the [[Galactic Empire]]. They served a variety of roles, including governance and control, as well as more enigmatic ones. The most senior among them formed the [[Imperial Ruling Council]], which was chaired by the [[Grand Vizier]]. They were noted for their sartorial extravagance.

==Organization and duties==
{{Quote|And ''I'' am an emissary of the ''Emperor'' himself.|Rersey|<ref name="Return to Vader's Castle 5">[[Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle 5|''Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle'' 5]]</ref>}}
The [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor's]] [[Advisor|advisers]],<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> also referred to as Emperor's advisors or simply as [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] advisors,<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> were extremely powerful and influential [[bureaucrat]]s whose duty was to oversee details of the Empire's administration. They also conducted covert surveillance on the home [[star system]]s other advisers and high-ranking [[Imperial officer|military officers]]. Typically, there were around one hundred of them.<ref name="Allies and Adversaries">''[[Allies and Adversaries]]''</ref> The most senior among them served on the [[Imperial Ruling Council]], which was chaired by the [[Grand Vizier]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

[[File:AdvisorRersey-ReturnToVadersCastle5.png|thumb|left|250px|The purple-robed Adviser Rersey asserts his authority.]]
Imperial advisers had a large amount of control over large regions of [[Realspace|space]], and had the power to appoint [[Moff]]s. As such, Moffs, who were in control of large [[sector]]s and their corresponding [[Sector Group]]s, reported directly to them and the Emperor. Imperial advisers carried a variety of decrees from the Emperor, and were charged with reporting to him about the state of affairs of government. Some advisers had more mysterious roles that were only discussed as rumors within the highest levels of government,<ref name="Dawn" /> such as scouring [[the galaxy]] for [[Sith artifact]]s.<ref name="Mighty Chewbacca">''[[The Mighty Chewbacca in the Forest of Fear!]]''</ref>

Despite the actual importance of their duties,<ref name="Tarkin" /> the Emperor saw his advisers more as errand boys, as he took advice from no one.<ref name="beware" /> To remind them of who they owed their influence to, Palpatine often manipulated his advisers and played them against each other to keep them from gaining too much personal power.<ref name="Dawn" /> Unaware of<ref name="Kernels and Husks">{{StoryCite|story=Kernels and Husks|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> Palpatine's plans to live forever,<ref name="Secrets of the Sith">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref> Adviser [[Sim Aloo]] believed that he or his daughter [[Alinka Aloo]] had the potential to become the next Galactic Emperor after Palpatine's death.<ref name="Kernels and Husks" />

[[File:YupeTashuinShadow-BattleOfJakkuInsurgencyRising2.png|thumb|right|250px|Yupe Tashu was a shadowy figure who knew Palpatine's true identity as the Dark Lord of the Sith.]]
Imperial advisers [[Clothing|wore]] outlandish garments that were reminiscent of the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[noble]]men's sartorial extravagance,<ref name="Tarkin" /> typically lush, plush, purple robes and flamboyant hats.<ref name="Aftermath" /> Most of them operated on the Imperial [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]]. Even though their duties, tasks, and schemes frequently took them to remote places across the galaxy, they tried not to stay away from the Emperor for too long, for fear of losing influence to a rival or missing important events. While some advisors were well-known public figures,<ref name="Allies and Adversaries" /> walking like [[Deity|gods]] among mortals on their respective [[homeworld]]s,<ref name="beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> others worked behind the scenes. They had sundry personal staff of their own<ref name="Allies and Adversaries" /> and owned posh residences.<ref name="Mighty Chewbacca" /> Presumably, only a very small number of advisers were aware of the Emperor's secret identity as a [[The Force|Force]]-wielding [[Dark Lord of the Sith]].<ref name="Allies and Adversaries" /> One such privileged individual included Adviser [[Yupe Tashu]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> Aloo and [[Janus Greejatus]] attempted to find Sith artifacts for Palpatine.<ref name="Rewind of the Jedi">{{InsiderCite|218|Rewind of the Jedi}}</ref>

==History==
When [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] was still a seemingly unassuming [[senator]] representing [[Naboo]] in the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], a clique formed around him.<ref name="Tarkin" /> After Palpatine was made [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the Republic, at least some of them&mdash;namely Janus Greejatus, [[Ars Dangor]], and [[Sate Pestage]]&mdash;came to work as his [[Emissary|emissaries]].<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> Eventually, Palpatine abolished the Republic and proclaimed the [[Galactic Empire|First Galactic Empire]], anointing himself [[Galactic Emperor]]. Many members of his political clique were promoted Imperial advisers, with some like Greejatus, Dangor, Pestage, and [[Kren Blista-Vanee]] sitting on the Imperial Ruling Council.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Around [[12 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Adviser [[Rersey]] was sent to [[Mustafar]] to check on the Emperor's [[Sith apprentice]], the [[cyborg]] known as [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who had angered his master. Rersey lost his mind at Mustafar after witnessing traumatizing [[Attack on Fortress Vader|events]] at [[Fortress Vader]].<ref name="Return to Vader's Castle 5" />

At the time of the [[Gorse Conflict]], eleven [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Count]] [[Denetrius Vidian]] was one of the Emperor's advisers. Although he was not part of the Ruling Council at the time, the Imperial [[Captain]] [[Rae Sloane]] expected him to to be soon.<ref name="A New Dawn">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref>

In the years that led to the [[Galactic Civil War]], Adviser Sim Aloo and his daughter Alinka collected [[Sith]] artifacts from across the galaxy for the Emperor's collection. Adviser Greejatus also participated in that artifact hunt.<ref name="Mighty Chewbacca" /> Similarly, Adviser [[Veris Hydan]] was a scholar of [[Jedi]] and Sith lore. Upon the discovery of an ancient [[Lothal Jedi Temple|Jedi Temple]] on [[Lothal]], he was charged with leading the excavation.<ref name="Veris Hydan">{{Databank|veris-hydan|Veris Hydan}}</ref>

[[File:GreejatusAndFriends-ROTJ.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Janus Greejatus and Sim Aloo were among the six advisers who accompanied the Emperor on the second Death Star.]]
When the Emperor went to the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] to oversee the destruction of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], six of his advisers &mdash; Janus Greejatus, Sim Aloo, Sate Pestage, Ars Dangor, Kren Blista-Vanee and [[Pebimarus Xorn]]&ndash; accompanied him onboard.<ref name="Kernels and Husks" /> All six [[Death|died]] along with their Imperial master when the Death Star was destroyed by the Alliance during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="HC10">{{HelmetCollectionCite|10|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> Following the Emperor's demise at Endor, Adviser [[Yupe Tashu]] became part of a short-lived group known as the [[Imperial Future Council]].<ref name="Aftermath" />
