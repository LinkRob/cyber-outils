'''Commenor''' was an ancient, conservative [[planet]] located in the [[Colonies]] region of [[the galaxy]]. It was located in the [[Commenor system]] along the [[Trellen Trade Route]], and was an endpoint of the [[Commenor Run]] and the [[Quellor Run]] [[hyperspace route]]s.

==History==
[[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]] hailed from Commenor,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> as did the [[Imperial Officer Corps|Imperial officer]] [[Fatimah Nurii]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the planet supported the [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> and was home to an [[Commenor Underground|underground resistance movement]].<ref name="Rebel Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref>

In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Shattered Empire 4" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Empire [[Attack on Commenor|targeted]] Commenor as part of [[Operation: Cinder]], attempting to destroy the world after the [[death]] of [[Darth Sidious|the Emperor]].<ref name="Shattered Empire 4" /> Civilizations on the world saw devastation at the hands of the Empire's [[Imperial Navy|fleets]] as a result.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> After the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]], which conditionally allowed the remnants of the Empire to peacefully co-exist with the [[New Republic]], Commenor came under control of the [[Inner systems' Imperial remnant]].<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> Thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the Battle of Endor, Commenor was a member world in the New Republic, where it was represented in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] by [[Thanlis Depallo]].<ref name="TFA VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Commenor was first mentioned in [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars: From the Adventures of Luke Skywalker'']], the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] novelization of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].
It was first mentioned in [[canon]] in the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', published in 2015.
