'''Cybernetics'''<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> or '''augs'''<ref name="SC Highlights 106">{{SW|url=news/skeleton-crew-highlights-106|text=''Skeleton Crew'' Explained &#124; Episode 6 - "Zero Friends Again"}}</ref> (short for '''augmentations''')<ref name="SC 105 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> were technological [[Dismemberment|replacements]] or enhancements for organic body parts, including [[eye]]s, [[limb]]s, and, in extreme cases, the majority of an individual's body, including whole new organ systems.<ref name="Aftermath" /> A [[cyborg]] was an organic being who had portions of their body replaced by cybernetics,<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> and a [[cyberneticist]] was a professional who installed, maintained, or researched cybernetics.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 26">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 26|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 26]]</ref> Cybernetics could be covered in [[synthflesh]]<ref name="justice">''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]''</ref> or [[synthskin]].<ref name="A New Dawn">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref>

==History==
In ancient times, the [[Sith]] [[king]] named [[Ommin]] built the mechanical spine called, "[[Ommin harness]]."<ref name="Altaya">{{EncyclopediaCite|Emperor Palpatine}}</ref><ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

[[Darth]] [[Maul]] used three sets of cybernetic legs, to replace the appendages, after his [[Battle of Naboo|duel]] with [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref>

[[File:ArtPrints-General-Grievous.png|thumb|left|200px|The cybernetic warlord Grievous]]
[[General]] [[Grievous]], the cyborg leader of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]], had most of his body replaced by cybernetics.<ref name="GrievousSWE">{{SWE|characters|generalgrievous|General Grievous}}</ref> Grievous also had his [[medical droid]] [[EV-A4-D]] to enhance the body of his pet [[Gor]], covering the [[roggwart]] in thick [[armor]] and two pairs of cybernetic [[limb]]s were attached to his back.<ref name="Build R2-D2 53">{{BuildR2Cite|53|Droid Directory}}</ref>


Due to an [[The accident|accident]] during her [[Challenge of the Body]], [[Breha Organa]]'s [[heart]] and [[lung]]s were replaced with [[pulmonodes]] which emitted a [[gold]]en glow from her chest.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>

[[File:SW25-LukeRoss.png|thumb|right|200px|The Jedi Anakin Skywalker would be reborn as the cybernetic Sith Lord Darth Vader]]
[[Anakin Skywalker]] had a cybernetic right forearm installed to replace the lost appendage after his [[First Battle of Geonosis|duel]] with [[Count]] [[Dooku]], and when he became Darth Vader, he was rebuilt with cybernetic parts by [[medical droid]]s after [[Duel on Mustafar|having]] his left arm and lower legs severed and nearly being burned to death.<ref name="Anakin Skywalker BG">{{Databank|anakin-skywalker-biography-gallery|Anakin Skywalker Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 20}}</ref>

[[Admiral]] [[Trench]] had three cybernetic arms installed, as well as the left side of his head, after losing them to [[tracking torpedo]]s during the [[Battle of Christophsis]].<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref>

The [[bounty hunter]] [[Shahan Alama]] had a cybernetic forearm on his right arm.<ref name="Hostage Crisis">{{TCW|Hostage Crisis}}</ref> [[Cad Bane]] wore removable [[breathing tube]]s<ref name="Deception Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/deception-episode-guide|text="Deception" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> that were designed to counter [[Force choke|Force-choking]].<ref name="Build R2 52">{{BuildR2Cite|52|Droid Directory}}</ref> and later gained a metal headplate.<ref name="Bounty Lost">{{TBB|Bounty Lost}}</ref>

[[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]] had a cybernetic right [[eye]] installed, after losing it to [[Asajj Ventress]], during a [[Battle of Khorm|battle]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

The [[Junker]]s, a [[species]] from the [[planet]] [[Lotho Minor]], regularly augmented their bodies with cybernetic enhancements.<ref name="Databank Junker">{{Databank|junker|Junker}}</ref>

[[Savage Opress]] had a cybernetic left arm installed, to replace the appendage, after his [[Skirmish on Florrum|duel]] with [[Jedi Master]] Obi-Wan Kenobi.<ref name="Revival">{{TCW|Revival (episode)}}</ref>

[[File:EchoNewArmor.png|thumb|left|200px|Echo underwent cybernetic repairs after his injuries.]]
[[Advanced Recon Commando]] [[Echo]] had cybernetic legs, a right socket-forearm, neural and respiratory systems, and a [[AJ^6 cyborg construct]] installed, after suffering a [[Sheathipede-class Type B shuttle|shuttle]] explosion, during a [[Battle of Lola Sayu|battle]].<ref name="Counterattack">{{TCW|Counterattack}}</ref>

Prior to her joining the [[Inquisitorius]], [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Reva Sevander]] (The Third Sister) had cybernetics installed<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> after being impaled by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] during [[Operation: Knightfall]].<ref name="Part V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref> The [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] also obtain cybernetics due to their training under Darth Vader. The [[Ninth Sister]] had a cybernetic left eye installed after losing it to Vader, during a training session.<ref name="Darth Vader 11">[[Darth Vader (2017) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 11]]</ref> A lower right leg was installed<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> after it was severed by the [[Sixth Brother]] during the [[Skirmish in Bel City]].<ref name="Darth Vader 17">[[Darth Vader (2017) 17|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 17]]</ref> She later had her right hand replaced<ref name="JS">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> due to it being severed by [[Cal Kestis]] during the [[Liberation on Kashyyyk]].<ref name="JFO" />

[[Mokko]] had a cybernetic right arm claw, as well as head and back implants.<ref name="Retrieval">{{TBB|Retrieval (episode)}}</ref>

[[Denetrius Vidian]] once thought of getting cybernetic implants that would give him near-total memory recall, but decided against it, afraid of possibly harming any [[brain]] chemistry that gave him his perceived genius.<ref name="A New Dawn" />

[[Greez Dritus]] had a cybernetic right upper forearm installed after [[Mission to Murkhana (Stinger Mantis)|losing]] it to the [[Fifth Brother]].<ref name="JBS">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]''</ref>

[[File:BountyHunters14-headshot.png|thumb|right|200px|The cyborg bounty hunter Beilert Valance.]]
[[Beilert Valance]] received extensive cybernetics, which covered the majority of his body and internal organs, after the [[Mimban Campaign]].<ref name="TV 5">[[Target Vader 5|''Target Vader'' 5]]</ref>

According to [[Spectres|Spectre]] [[Sabine Wren]], "The Empire has been known to implant lower level technicians with cybernetic circuits, personally sacrificed for productivity."<ref name="Empire Day">{{Rebels|Empire Day (episode)}}</ref>

[[Saw Gerrera]] had low-grade cybernetic feet installed after losing them, and a [[Saw Gerrera's suit|pressurized suit]].<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

[[Florn (individual)|Florn]] was a [[pilot]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] who had cybernetics installed after a crash left him severely injured.<ref name="LCZA">{{InsiderCite|156|Last Call at the Zero Angle}}</ref>

[[Sarco Plank]] required the use of cybernetic [[breathing tube]]s.<ref name="The Weapon of a Jedi">''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]''</ref>

[[Hutt]] [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi]] had cybernetic legs installed,<ref name="SW (15) 12">[[Star Wars (2015) 12|''Star Wars'' (2015) 12]]</ref> as did the [[Grakks]].<ref name="SW (15) 9">[[Star Wars (2015) 9|''Star Wars'' (2015) 9]]</ref>

[[Luke Skywalker]] had a cybernetic right hand installed to replace the lost appendage after his [[Duel on Cloud City|duel]] with [[Darth]] Vader.<ref name="Luke Skywalker BG">{{Databank|luke-skywalker-biography-gallery|Luke Skywalker Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 23}}</ref>

The [[assassin]] [[Ochi]] had cybernetic head implants installed, and a visor, after having his eyes burned by a [[kyber crystal]], during the [[Mission to Exegol]].<ref name="Vader 11">[[Darth Vader (2020) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 11]]</ref>

The [[Colony of Kezarat|Colony of ''Kezarat'']] used salvaged [[droid]] parts for prosthetics, such as claws and grappling hooks as replacements for lost hands.<ref name="Star Wars 33">[[Star Wars (2020) 33|''Star Wars'' (2020) 33]]</ref>

[[File:GuswanAskreeth-HNtGEbEaOGSS.png|thumb|left|200px|Guswan Askreeth, a cybernetically altered being of unknown species]]
The [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot]] [[Shen]] suffered multiple injuries during his service. He needed cybernetics to survive and found himself unable to remove his [[helmet]].<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>

At some point after the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Dengar]] underwent cybernetic surgery in an attempt to remain competitive. The surgery left him barely recognizable.<ref name="The Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> Some rumors claimed Dengar in fact became the hunter [[Rothgar Deng]].<ref name="DDaOBHaC">{{EncyclopediaCite|Din Djarin and Other Characters of the Underworld}}</ref>

[[Fennec Shand]] had cybernetics provided by "[[the Modifier]]"<ref name="Storm">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref> implanted in her lower abdomen after she was [[Hunt for Fennec Shand|shot]] by [[Toro Calican]].<ref name="Chapter 5">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 5: The Gunslinger}}</ref>

"The Modifier" had a cybernetic right forearm installed,<ref name="Storm" /> the [[Mods]] had cybernetics installed as well.<ref name="Mos Espa">{{BOBF|Chapter 3: The Streets of Mos Espa}}</ref>

A [[Unidentified Ishi Tib pirate (Silvo's pirates)|Ishi Tib]] that was part of [[Silvo's pirates]], lost an eyestalk during a [[Silvo's pirate raid|raid]] on a [[Unidentified bulk freighter (New Republic Era)|ship]].<ref name="SC 101">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref> He later had it replaced with a cybernetic.<ref name="Way Out Past The Barrier">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past The Barrier}}</ref>

The pirate [[Gunter]] had cybernetics in the right side of his head, over his right eye, and a AJ^6 cyborg construct.<ref name="SC 101">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref>

Due to an accident, [[KB]] had extensive cybernetics installed, covering her ears, a [[Occipital link]], and a visor.<ref name="Zero Friends Again">{{SkeletonCrew|Zero Friends Again}}</ref>

[[Guavian security soldier]]s of the [[Guavian Death Gang]], had cybernetics installed to enhance them, to serve as enforcers.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>

The cyborg "[[Colonel]]" [[Guswan Askreeth]]<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> was present on [[Canto Bight]]<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> and watched a match of [[Fathier racing]].<ref name="TLJ" /> His cybernetics included an arm taken from a [[2-1B surgical droid]], the trunk-like body of a [[FX-8]], and a [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] [[breath mask]].<ref name="TLJVD" />

After [[Darth Sidious]]' first defeat in the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> his spirit has been transferred to the [[Cloning|cloned]] body that wore an Ommin harness.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
