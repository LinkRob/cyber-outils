The '''Boonta Eve Classic''', also known as the '''Boonta Eve Podrace''', the '''Boonta Eve Classic Podrace''' or simply '''Boonta Eve''', was the largest [[Standard year|annual]] [[Podracing|podrace]] in the [[galaxy]]<ref name="CV" /> and cultural event<ref name="Queen's Peril">''[[Queen's Peril]]''</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. It took place in the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena|Grand Arena]] in [[Mos Espa]] and the [[Mos Espa podrace circuit]].<ref name="CL 2016" /> The event was held by the [[Hutt Clan]] to commemorate the holiday [[Boonta Eve]],<ref name="Boonta Eve Classic Podracer Pilots">{{Databank|boonta-eve-classic-podracer-pilots|Boonta Eve Classic Podracer Pilots}}</ref> and was recorded by numerous [[Hologlide J57 cam droid]]s.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> The race had the highest mortality rate of all podrace competitions in the [[galaxy]].<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

[[Boonta Eve Classic (32 BBY)|One such race]], held in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> was presided over by the grandmaster host of the Boonta Eve Classic [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]].<ref name="Episode I" /><ref name="Ultimate" /> This race was attacked by [[Orr'UrRuuR'R]] and [[Orr'UrRuuR'R's tribe|his tribe]], who fired at the [[racer]]s with their [[slugthrower]]s, and the winner of that race was [[Anakin Skywalker]],<ref name="Episode I" /> who finished in 15 [[Standard minute|minutes]] and 42 [[second]]s. Only 6 racers finished, 33% of the initial 18 participants, and one competitor, Ratts Tyerell, was killed in a violent head-on accident at the Laguna Caves on the first lap.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> In the [[Standard year|years]] that followed, Jabba had the number of laps reduced to two, and added [[Dune-Pipes]] to the [[Hutt Flats]] in order to make the race more profitable.<ref name="Podracer Mission">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Podracer Mission]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Boonta Eve Classic appeared in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode I" />

According to concept artist [[Doug Chiang]], director [[George Lucas]] wanted a recognizable but distinct terrain for the race, reason for which Chiang and the production crew mixed and condensed real locations and exaggerated the scale to add that 20% extra terrain that made it unique. Topo maps were made so Lucas could establish the route and configure canyons and plateaus which could constitute into recognizable landmarks. With such map, they timed the race to see how many laps could be completed in eight minutes at speeds of more than 900 kilometers per hour in order to determine the length of the course. Afterwards, they added obstacles in the quieter sections, while others were simplified in areas of greater visual clarity.<ref name="CL 2016" />

In the audio commentary on the Episode I DVD, visual effects supervisor [[John Knoll]] points out that in a shot of the scene of [[Sebulba]] riding just behind [[Anakin Skywalker]], with Anakin's head nearly in the connecting current between the pod engines, it's possible to see that Sebulba isn't present in his pod.<ref name="Episode I DVD Audio Commentary">{{Film|I}} DVD audio commentary</ref>
