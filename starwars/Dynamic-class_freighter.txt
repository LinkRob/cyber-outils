The '''''Dynamic''-class freighter''' was a model of [[starship]] which was considered ancient<ref name="Brotherhood" /> by [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Date">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] is set between the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] and the [[Battle of Christophsis]], both of which are dated to [[22 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> The [[Rokura's family|family]] of the [[Gharal]] [[Rokura]], who lived on the [[mining]] [[planet]] [[Langston]], owned one such [[freighter]], the ''[[Norriker]]'', which they used to transport select cargoes of the planet's finest [[ore]]s. After the older generations were [[Death|killed]] during an [[Attack on Langston|attack]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]], Rokura loaned the ''Norriker'' to [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] in exchange for supplies.<ref name="Brotherhood" />
