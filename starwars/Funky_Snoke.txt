==Behind the scenes==
Funky Snoke first appeared in the [[2022]] [[canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', in the side mission "Attack of the Clones (Again)".<ref name="TSS" />
