The '''Below the Belt trilogy''' was a trilogy of adventures in year two of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign that were released in [[2002]]. The first two, ''[[Below the Belt: Philanthropy]]'' and ''[[Below the Belt: Philosophy]]'', were written by [[August Hahn]] and focused on the [[Confederation Rock Rush]], the exploration of the [[Cularin asteroid belt]]. The third adventure, ''[[Below the Belt: Philology]]'', was written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] and [[Christina Nichols]] for use at Gencom 2002; in it, [[Len Markus]] stole the [[Darkstaff]] from the asteroid belt, which resulted in the [[Cularin system/Legends|Cularin system]] disappearing for ten years and so moved the campaign to the time of the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].
