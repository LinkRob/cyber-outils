An '''airdome''', also known as an '''airshield''', was the [[oxygen]] field that surrounded the [[Galactic Empire]] [[wreckage-prison]] [[Accresker Jail]].<ref name="Aphra 20" /> When the [[prison]] was engaging in battle, the airdome would be extended so the [[Accresker Penal Legion]] could fight in a larger area. After the combat scenario had ended, the airdome would retract to its regular perimeter.<ref name="Aphra 22">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 22|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 22]]</ref>
