The '''sun''' of the [[Unknown Regions]]' [[Dassal system]] was [[orbit]]ed by several celestial bodies, including the [[planet]] [[Najra-Va]], which had an inhabited [[Unidentified moon (Najra-Va)|moon]]. At some point by [[34 ABY]], the [[First Order]] moved its [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]] into the [[Star system|system]]. The [[weapon]] was charged up by draining all energy from the [[star]], and subsequently it performed test shots that cored the other objects in the system. The star's disappearance was eventually [[Mission to the Dassal system|discovered]] by the [[Resistance]] operatives [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Kazuda Xiono]].

==Description==
A [[star]] was at one point located in the [[Dassal system]], a part of [[Sector Epsilon 51-3]] of the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="BattleStations" /> It was situated withing grid square H-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="GalaxyMap" /> and was [[orbit]]ed by several [[planet]]s<ref name="Intel" /> and [[planetoid]]s,<ref name="Timelines" /> including the planet [[Najra-Va]] and its [[Unidentified moon (Najra-Va)|moon]].<ref name="BattleStations" /> The latter at one point became inhabited by [[Sentience|sentient]] beings who lived at a [[Unidentified village (Dassal system)|village]] there.<ref name="The Core Problem" />

==History==
{{Quote|OK, that's not right.''"<br />"''What? I'm not detecting anything.''"<br />"''Exactly. Looks like there was a system here, but there's no star.|Poe Dameron and Kazuda Xiono|audio=ArrivingDassalSystem-TheCoreProblem.flac|<ref name="The Core Problem" />}}
[[File:DassalSystemCored-TheCoreProblem.png|thumb|right|300px|The First Order extinguished the Dassal system's sun in order to core its orbiting bodies (pictured).]]
At some point, the [[First Order]] moved the [[Starkiller Base]], its mobile [[superweapon]] built into the planet [[Ilum]], to the Dassal [[Star system|system]]. With the system being used as testing grounds, the [[weapon]] was charged up by draining the energy from the star,<ref name="BattleStations" /> completely extinguishing the latter. Several of the smaller celestial bodies within the system were then cored<ref name="The Core Problem" /> by test shots of the superweapon,<ref name="BattleStations" /> with the system becoming strewn with planetary debris as a result. When the [[Resistance]] operatives [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Kazuda Xiono]] [[Mission to the Dassal system|arrived]] in the Dassal system<ref name="The Core Problem" /> in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> in order to investigate First Order activity, Dameron immediately pointed out to Xiono the disappearance of the system's star.<ref name="The Core Problem" />

==Behind the scenes==
The sun of the Dassal system was introduced via a pair of mentions in "[[The Core Problem]],"<ref name="The Core Problem" /> the seventeenth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'',<ref name="Insider190">{{InsiderCite|190|Resistance Revisited|''Resistance'' Revisited}}</ref> which aired on [[February 17]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> Prior to the episode's release, one of the mentions of the star was featured in a mid-season trailer published on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [https://www.youtube.com/@StarWars YouTube channel] on [[January 9]] of the same year.<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|m7Pls-vYWwM|Star Wars Resistance Season 1 - Mid-Season Trailer (Official)|sw_url=video/season-1-mid-season-trailer-star-wars-resistance|date=2019-01-09}}</ref>
