'''"Best Friends"'''<ref name="Qobuz">{{WebCite|url=https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/album/best-friends-star-wars-young-jedi-adventures-cast/st5x76v73he2a|text=Best Friends (From "Disney Junior Music: Star Wars &ndash; Young Jedi Adventures")|work=[https://www.qobuz.com Qobuz]|archivedate=20260201012850}}</ref> was a song written and performed by the [[band]] the [[Ku-Bops]], who were based in the [[city|town]] of [[Kublop Springs]] on the [[planet]] [[Tenoo]]. The song included vocals in [[Galactic Basic Standard]] performed by all three members of the band: [[Metz]], [[Bruff]], and [[K1-T]]. Although Metz was the lead singer of the band, Bruff sang lead vocals on "Best Friends". During a performance of the song at the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple]], young local [[pilot]] and fan of the Ku-Bops [[Nash Durango]] joined the band on stage while they performed, getting to play a tambourine.<ref name="The Show Must Go On" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Best Friends" is a diegetic composition which first appeared in the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]] episode of ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'', "[[The Show Must Go On]]". Although the song is not explicitly named in the episode, it was released as a [[In-universe|real-world]] single for streaming on [[August 2]], [[2023]] alongside the episode.<ref name="Qobuz" /> Given the diegetic nature of the song and that the lyrics are in [[Galactic Basic Standard]], featuring "best friends" as a prominent lyric, this article assumes that the in-universe name of the song matches its real-world release title.
