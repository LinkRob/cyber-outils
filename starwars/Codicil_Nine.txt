'''Codicil Nine''' was a subsection of [[Order 66]]<ref name="Crimson Reign 3" />&mdash;an [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|order]] to [[Death penalty|execute]] the [[Jedi Order]] as [[Treason|traitors]] to the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>&mdash;which forbade the keeping of [[Force artifact]]s by citizens.<ref name="Crimson Reign 3" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Han Solo & Chewbacca 3|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 3]] to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]]. As the flashbacks in [[Crimson Reign 3|''Crimson Reign'' 3]] that take place on [[Antillion]] occur concurrently with the events of ''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 3, the flashbacks must also take place during the same [[time]].</ref> former [[University of Bar'leth]] [[Sava]] [[The Archivist|Madelin Sun]] was nearly arrested by three [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s for violating the codicil after they discovered<ref name="Crimson Reign 3" /> she<ref name="Han Chewie 3">[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 3|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 3]]</ref> was in possession of [[Unidentified dark side artifact|one]] such [[Dark side artifact|artifact]] in<ref name="Crimson Reign 3" /> her [[Madelin Sun's home|home]] on the [[planet]] [[Antillion]].<ref name="Han Chewie 3" /> The [[Unidentified Stormtrooper Commander (Madelin Sun)|stormtrooper commander]] present informed Sun they would be making to a report to the [[D'Astan sector|sector's]] [[Unidentified Imperial governor (Madelin Sun)|Imperial governor]] about the discovery, however, the [[Stormtrooper Commander|commander]] and the two other [[trooper]]s were [[Death|killed]] by [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]]&mdash;the [[Crime lord|leader]] of the criminal syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]]&mdash;before they could.<ref name="Crimson Reign 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
Codicil Nine was mentioned in a flashback in the [[Crimson Reign 3|third issue]] of the [[2021]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Crimson Reign]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Crimson Reign 3" /> on [[March 9]], [[2022]]. Prior to the release of the issue, the codicil was mentioned in a preview article of the issue released on [[StarWars.com]] on [[March 2]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-crimson-reign-3-exclusive-preview|text=The Odyssey of the Archivist in Marvel's ''Crimson Reign'' #3 &ndash; Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
