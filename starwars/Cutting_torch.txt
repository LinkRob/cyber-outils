A '''cutting torch''' was a device used to cut through certain materials. [[B1-series rocket battle droid|Rocket battle droids]] were equipped with cutting torches.<ref name="Rising">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|102}}</ref> they were used by a group of rocket [[battle droid]]s to cut open [[escape pod]]s when they hunted down survivors of the ''[[Malevolence]]'' attack during the [[Battle of Abregado]].<ref name="Rising" />
