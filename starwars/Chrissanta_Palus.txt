'''Chrissanta Palus''' was a [[human]] [[merchant]] who resided on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]]<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> They operated from a camp near [[Typhon's Rock]] in the [[Lost Steppe]] region of Toshara. Their inventory included [[robuma leather]], [[slivian iron]], [[durasteel]], [[bacta vial]]s and [[copalroot bead]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> The same year,<ref name="Outlaws Date" /> they provided special [[tailor]]ing supplies to [[Berek Keehro]], a tailor who operated [[Sirei's Stall]] in the Tosharan [[city]] [[Mirogana]]. Palus had [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s, black [[hair]] and tan skin. They wore yellow [[clothing|coat]] over a brown shirt, yellow pants, brown [[boot]]s. They also wore an orange [[belt]] over a teal waist [[sash]] and teal arm wraps. A piece of [[amberine]] hung from a string connected to their belt.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] paid Keehro 200 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to learn Palus's location and she purchased a [[copalroot]] bead from Palus for 1000 credits.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Chrissanta Palus appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, they are part of the "Special Tailoring Supplies" intel quest which players can begin any time after they complete the "Underworld" main quest by purchasing the associated intel from Berek Keehro.<ref name="Outlaws" />
