'''Clan Markona''' was a clan that referred to the community on [[Hubin]] in which [[Thane]] [[Markona]] and his daughter [[Tula Markona|Tula]] lived.<ref name="SW 57" />

==History==
Following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[death]] of his [[parent]]s, [[Markona]] left [[Hubin]] and started a [[mercenary]] company. Markona eventually met a [[Markona's wife|field technician]], [[Marriage|married]] her, and had a daughter, [[Tula Markona|Tula]]. Following his wife's death, Markona read a message from her, asking him to give himself, Tula, and the clan a better life. When the clan's members got older, they decided to go into retirement. They agreed to carry out one last mission<ref name="Star Wars 58">[[Star Wars (2015) 58|''Star Wars'' (2015) 58]]</ref> for the Empire by gathering data from a secure location.<ref name="SW 60" /> After completing their mission, the clan was given Hubin as their private property, with Markona as their [[Thane]]. Clan Markona agreed to pay tithes to the Empire in order to be left alone due to Hubin not being useful.<ref name="SW 57" />

Following the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] devastating [[Battle of Mako-Ta|defeat]] at the [[Mako-Ta Space Docks]], Markona rescued [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Captain]] [[Han Solo]], and [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]] from a [[thanrax]]. They were brought to [[Thane Markona's Estate]].<ref name="SW 56">[[Star Wars (2015) 56|''Star Wars'' (2015) 56]]</ref> Markona and his clan's trackers were able to drive the thanrax pack away from the village. After entertaining [[Heroes of Yavin|Organa's team]], Markona and Tula gave Skywalker a tour of the village and his family's grave plot. Markona and Luke then fought a a pack of thanrax.<ref name="SW 57" /> Together, they were able to defeat and drive the thanrax away from the village.<ref name="Star Wars 58" /> The clan later watched a shoot out between Solo and one of their own. After Solo [[stun]]ned his opponent, the clan stood down.<ref name="Star Wars 59">[[Star Wars (2015) 59|''Star Wars'' (2015) 59]]</ref>

Markona then welcomed [[Sergeant]] [[Kreel]] and his [[Task Force 99|SCAR squadron]] to his estate. Despite denying the presence of the rebels, SCAR Squadron confirmed the rebels were there due to their interrogation of their [[prisoner]], [[Sana Starros]]. As SCAR Squadron and Organa's team prepared to fight each other, Markona ordered his daughter to stun Solo and Organa. Markona threatened to call in the rest of the clan if Kreel did not agree to [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] Skywalker. Both Kreel and Skywalker [[Skirmish on Hubin|agreed]] to it.<ref name="Star Wars 59" /> After Skywalker escaped from the [[Lightsaber combat|duel]], Markona agreed to help the [[Special Commando Advanced Recon Trooper|SCAR troopers]] track Skywalker. During this, Tula stunned [[Aero]], freed Starros and revived Solo and Organa. As Tula and the others left, [[MK-1]] sounded the alarm to gather the clan. Makona then fought SCAR Squadron until he was killed.<ref name="SW 60" />

Tula, the clan, and Organa's team stole SCAR squadron's [[shuttle]] and left the moon as the clan agreed to join the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="SW 60" /> The clan and Organa's team went to the [[Imperial Impound Yard]] on [[Novka]] where they [[Attack on an Imperial Impound Yard|reclaimed]] Starros' ''[[Volt Cobra]]'' and a [[Unidentified YT-2400 light freighter (Clan Markona)|YT-2400 light freighter]]. Afterwards, they traveled to [[Brentaal IV]], and after the rebels had met with [[Qensog]], Skywalker told them they could join up with [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] on the [[Geist (cruiser)|''Geist'']].<ref name="SW 61">[[Star Wars (2015) 61|''Star Wars'' (2015) 61]]</ref>''
