'''Baradas''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Victory's Price" /> located in the [[Baradas system]] of the [[Colonies]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[rebel pilot]] and [[squadron commander]]<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> [[Nath Tensent]] experienced a bad landing on Baradas in his first [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing starfighter]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> sometime after his defection to the Alliance from the Empire<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> around [[1 BBY]].<ref name="0 BBY">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']] establishes that [[Nath Tensent]]'s [[squadron]] defected to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] shortly before the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]]. Tensent's squadron must have therefore defected around 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> The incident resulted in the [[starfighter]]'s underside and one of its nacelles being torn off, the remains of the Y-wing being irreparable.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baradas was mentioned in ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the final novel in [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy which was published in [[2021]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]], the similarly named [[Baradas II]] was first mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 10: Bounty Hunters]]'', a supplement to [[West End Games]]' [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]] which was written by [[Rick D. Stuart]] and released in [[1994]].<ref name="GG10">''[[Galaxy Guide 10: Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>
