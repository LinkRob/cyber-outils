The '''Festival of Bounty''' was a [[Wookiee]] festival that was celebrated on [[Kashyyyk]]. It was a celebration of the harvest, and lasted for longer than three [[Standard week|weeks]]. No [[nut]]s, [[fruit]]s, or [[mushbloom]]s could not be exported during the holiday as it was considered bad luck.<ref name="Vader's Castle" /> Sometime between [[4 ABY]] and [[5 ABY]],<ref name="VC Date">{{VadersCastleDate}}</ref> [[Lepi]] [[smuggler]] [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] had a recurring nightmare in which he was hired to retrieve a shipment of [[saava]] [[Berry|berries]] from Kashyyyk. However he was stopped by a [[Unidentified Wookiee (Jaxxon T. Tumperakki)|Wookiee]] who would not allow him to take the berries due to the festival.<ref name="Vader's Castle" />
