'''10114''' was a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] that was assigned to [[Fuel Station Visavi]] while it was orbiting the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> after it was found to have faulty power line it was shipped from the station to another location. A maintenance report left on a [[datapad]] at the station detained the transfer however the destination was redacted by the [[Gallusian]] [[Bosnok]] who was a [[prisoner]] [[accountant]] aboard the station during this time.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
10114 was first mentioned on a [[datapad]] in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
