'''Dent''' was a [[human]] who lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Star Wars 26" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars (2020) 26|''Star Wars'' (2020) 26]] 26 take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> Dent worked aboard the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] during its construction along with [[Bevelyn]]. After finishing their shift for the [[Standard day|day]], Dent asked Bevelyn if they would see her in the [[Cantina|bar]] later that evening, to which Bev responded that she was unsure, though unless she had been thrown out of an [[Airlock|air lock]] for insubordination it would be a safe bet she would be there.<ref name="Star Wars 26" />
