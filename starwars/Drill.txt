'''Drills''' were machines used to bore through hard surfaces, usually the ground for [[mining]] purposes. Drills could also be used as weaponry and [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident''-class assault ships]] were equipped with [[drill spike]]s that could be used to drill into structures or other [[starship]]s.<ref name="EoSaOV" />
