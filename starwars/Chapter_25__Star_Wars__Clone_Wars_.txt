"'''Chapter 25'''" is the twenty-fifth and final episode of the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' animated television series, and the fifth and final episode in [[Clone Wars: Season Three|Season Three]] and [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume Two|Volume Two]], leading directly into the events of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']]. The episode originally aired on [[Cartoon Network]] on [[March 25]], [[2005]].

==Plot summary==
===Target: Palpatine===
[[File:YodaMaceCoruscant.jpg|thumb|left|250px|[[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] and [[Mace Windu/Legends|Mace Windu]] determine the reason for the Separatist [[Battle of Coruscant/Legends|attack]].]]

[[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] and [[Mace Windu/Legends|Mace Windu]] continue to cut a swath through the invading [[Separatist Droid Army/Legends|droid army]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. They begin to wonder why such a massive invasion has not attempted to attack the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] or the [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Galactic Senate]], and realize that the invasion is a distraction from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy's]] real target&mdash;[[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]]. Windu jumps aboard an overflying [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/Legends|Republic gunship]], and orders the [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]] to turn around.

Palpatine and his three [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] protectors reach a hover capsule in a tunnel, and [[Shaak Ti/Legends|Shaak Ti]] orders the others to get to the bunker while she holds off their pursuers. She ignites her [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] as a group of [[IG-100 MagnaGuard/Legends|MagnaGuards]] troop out of the darkness.

===The Nelvaan conflict===
On [[Nelvaan/Legends|Nelvaan]], [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] tries to fight off the Nelvaan [[mutant]]s without hurting them. The latest subject manages to burst free of his restraints, smashes the tank and rips the [[Cybernetics/Legends|cybernetic]] chestplates off two of the Nelvaanians. Skywalker soon gets the idea and cuts the rest off using his lightsaber. The half-mutated Nelvaan points to a [[Crystal/Legends|crystal]] in the middle of the energy chamber which is causing the damage to the [[Planet/Legends|planet]].

===Mauling the MagnaGuards===
As Shaak Ti fights the MagnaGuards, she is deprived of her lightsaber and nearly overwhelmed. But she manages to turn the tables by obtaining an [[Electrostaff/Legends|electrostaff]] and destroy several of them with her new weapon. She then manages to reclaim her lightsaber and masterfully wields it in conjunction with the electrostaff.

===The Ghost Hand of Nelvaan===
[[File:AnakinKills.jpg|thumb|250px|[[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Skywalker]] strangles a [[Techno Union/Legends|Techno Union]] [[Techno Union scientist|leader]] as the mutated [[Nelvaanian/Legends|Nelvaanians]] bear witness.]]
The Nelvaan warriors are rampaging through the facility, tearing [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]] apart with their bare hands, and freeing their comrades from captivity. Skywalker leaps to the top of the generator, then lowers himself into the crystal chamber. Screaming in agony, he reaches his prosthetic [[Limb/Legends|arm]] into the energy sphere, uses [[The Force/Legends|the Force]] to pull the crystal from its socket, and then shatters it into a thousand shards. The energy surge causes the generator to explode, and Skywalker leaps to safety, his arm destroyed. The Nelvaan warriors reach the planet's surface, where the waiting [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]] are destroyed as the planet's ice age comes to an end.

An enraged Skywalker clambers to the surface, where he kills the [[Techno Union/Legends|Techno Union]] [[Scientist/Legends|scientists]] trying to flee in their ship. Seeing Skywalker's ravaged arm, the Nelvaan mutants worship him as "holt kezed"&mdash;"ghost hand"&mdash;their savior, and in solidarity they tear the implanted weapons from their own arms. Skywalker and the Nelvaanians return to the village. The women are at first horrified by the mutants, but when a child recognizes her [[Parent/Legends|father]], the familial bonds are restored.

===Grievous's victory===
On Coruscant, [[Foul Moudama]] and [[Roron Corobb/Legends|Roron Corobb]] manage to get Palpatine into his secure bunker. Once they are locked inside, they discover that [[General/Legends|General]] [[Grievous/Legends|Grievous]] has beaten them to it, and the two Jedi leap forward to defend their leader, who sinks, seemingly unconcerned by the danger, into the shadows. As Moudama and Corobb manage to get Grievous in a saber-lock, his arms split along their lengths into four. His extra two arms then grab the remaining two lightsabers on his waist, and Grievous lunges forward.

Shaak Ti continues to fight off Grievous's MagnaGuards, but when the [[Droid/Legends|droids]] suddenly retreat, she realizes they've been keeping her busy while Grievous reaches the others. As she [[Force speed/Legends|races]] to the bunker, Grievous finishes off the other two Jedi, before turning to and threatening Palpatine. Palpatine calmly replies that Grievous wouldn't dare hurt him, for fear of his master's wrath. Grievous venomously replies that Palpatine is lucky he's wanted alive.

[[File:Mace_Windu_using_Force_Crush.jpg|thumb|left|250px|[[Mace Windu/Legends|Mace Windu]] using [[Telekinesis/Legends#Force Crush|Force Crush]] on [[Grievous/Legends|Grievous]].]]

Just then, Shaak Ti arrives. Horrified to see the bodies of her comrades, she charges Grievous. Grievous easily grabs the exhausted Jedi by the throat, relieves her of her lightsaber, and activates snaking cables from his wrist, announcing he has other plans for her.

The droids on Grievous's ship open fire on the gunship with Mace Windu on board. As the vessel falls from the sky, Windu jumps to safety. Grievous turns to face him, igniting four lightsabers, but Windu uses the Force to crush the chest plates protecting the General's organs. Grievous turns and flees on board, coughing furiously. Windu tries to leap after the ascending ship, but the vessel's [[Blaster/Legends|blaster]] barrage forces him to defend himself, thus robbing him of the power behind the leap, and the ship blasts into space. He rushes into the bunker to find the two dead Jedi, with Shaak Ti trussed and tied to the ceiling, devastated by her failure to protect the Chancellor.
