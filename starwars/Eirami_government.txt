The '''Eirami government'''<ref name="Haunted Starlight">''[[The High Republic: Haunted Starlight]]''</ref> was a [[Monarchy|monarchial]] government that ruled the [[planet]] [[Eiram]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. During the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]], the government was led by [[Queen]] [[Adrialla]] and her [[consort]] [[Odelia]]. The military at the time consisted of a planetary defense fleet and ground troops.<ref name="Convergence" /> By [[232 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the main events of ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> the government was led by Queen [[Dima]]. Her consort, Queen [[Thandeka]], assisted in government affairs, especially their dealings with the [[Jedi Order|Jedi]].<ref name="Into the Dark" /> Although Eiram did not possess a [[fleet]] in 232 BBY, it did possess a [[Eiram medical cruiser|medical cruiser]]. A [[defense minister]] reported to Queen Dima. Eiram's resources were mobilized when the [[Galactic Republic]]'s and the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[space station]], the [[Starlight Beacon]], was destroyed by the [[Nihil]] and crashed off the coast of [[Barraza City|Barraza]] [[city]].<ref name="The Fallen Star" />
