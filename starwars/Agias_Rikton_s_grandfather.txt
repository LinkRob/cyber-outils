The '''grandfather''' of the [[engineer]] [[Agias Rikton]] served alongside [[clone trooper]]s by the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. He recounted it to Rikton during the engineer's upbringing on<ref name="Alphabet" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="New Dawn">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref> [[Corulag]], stating that it taught him what mattered was [[heart]]. Sometime after his grandfather [[Death|died]], Rikton told another engineer that went by the name [[Soran Keize|Devon Lhent]] about his [[Parent|grandfather]] a number of [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Alphabet" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>
