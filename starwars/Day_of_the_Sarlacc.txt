'''''[[Standard day|Day]] of the [[Sarlacc]]''''' was a [[Painting|poster]] available in [[the galaxy]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It showed a [[Mandalorian]] dressed in [[Color|green]] [[Mandalorian armor|armor]], running from a monster.<ref name="Missing" />

==Behind the scenes==
The poster was painted by [[Amy Beth Christenson]], and was developed for [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars'']] episode "[[Missing in Action]]." It is based on the classic [[LucasArts]] [[Wikipedia:Graphic adventure game|graphic adventure game]] [[Wikipedia:Day of the Tentacle|''Day of the Tentacle'']].<ref name="SW">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/missing-in-action-trivia-gallery|text="Missing in Action" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 11}}</ref> The [[Mandalorian]] on the poster wears [[Mandalorian armor|armor]] identical to [[Boba Fett's armor|the one]] worn by [[Boba Fett]], who is eaten by a [[sarlacc]] in the film {{Film|6}}.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>
