The '''Faers''' were a family of [[Evereni]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. The family had several members, including a [[Gender|female]] [[Vika and Velya Faer's grandmother|member]] and their grandchildren [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]]. At some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]], Vika and Velya met the Evereni [[pirate]] leader [[Marda Ro]] during her [[Marda Ro's hunt|hunt]] for other Evereni. During their stay with Ro, Vika [[Death|killed]] the pirate's [[Aide|assistant]] [[Alirya]], which caused Ro to shoot Vika in retaliation. Afterward, Ro convinced Vika's brother to join her. Ro later chronicled her encounter with the Faers in [[Marda Ro's journal|messages]] she recorded for her [[Ro family|descendants]], which her great-great-grandson [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to by [[252 BBY]].

==Description==
{{Quote|''[Faer]'' means 'to know.'|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
The Faers were a family of [[Evereni]] whose name meant "to know" in the Evereni's [[Evereni (language)|language]], as the [[species]] named its families for actions. They owned at least two [[Starship|ships]]. One [[Unidentified Faer ship|ship]] housed several of the family's members<ref name="Closed Fist" /> by some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]],<ref name="FaerDate">The segment of "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" depicting a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi's]] [[death]] onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' takes place around [[379 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:ClosedFistJediDeathDate|here]]. [[Marda Ro]] met the Faers after the [[Jedi]]'s death; therefore, she must have met them no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> and the other [[Vika and Velya Faer's ship|ship]] belonged to the [[twin]]s [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]].<ref name="Closed Fist" />

==History==
{{Quote|Be my guests for a while. I've been looking for you.|Marda Ro, to Vika and Velya Faer|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
[[File:MardaRo-PathOfDeceit.png|thumb|right|220px|Vika and Velya Faer met Marda Ro (pictured).]]
The Faers were active during the [[High Republic Era]] with a [[Gender|female]] [[Vika and Velya Faer's grandmother|member]] of the family maintaining a list of Evereni names going back to<ref name="Closed Fist" /> at least [[500 BBY]]<ref name="EveronDate">"[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" established that the list contained names of Evereni from before their [[Everon exodus|exodus]] from their [[homeworld]] of [[Everon]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[500 BBY]].</ref> by some point no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].<ref name="FaerDate" /> By around [[397 BBY]],<ref name="Birth">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' states that members of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] receive full membership at the age of fifteen, and also mentions that [[Marda Ro]] recently became a full member. As ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''Path of Deceit'' to [[382 BBY]], Ro must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[397 BBY]]. "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" establishes Vika and Velya Faer are older than her, therefore they must have been born by around 397 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> they had at least two grandchildren, the siblings Vika and Velya Faer, who had grown up together. While most of the other six Faers alive<ref name="Closed Fist" /> by the point no earlier than 379 BBY<ref name="FaerDate" /> lived on one ship together, Vika and Velya had their own ship.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

At that time,<ref name="FaerDate" /> the siblings were intercepted by [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the Evereni<ref name="Closed Fist" /> founder of a group of [[pirate]]s who would eventually become the [[Nihil]]<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref>&mdash;during her<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> [[Marda Ro's hunt|search]] for other Evereni. The two Faers then met with Ro in the [[throne hall]] of her ship, the ''[[Gaze Electric]]''. Ro had grown up isolated from her people, so the siblings told her more about the Evereni and about their own family. During the siblings' time with Ro, her [[Aide|assistant]] [[Alirya]] stabbed Vika from behind with a [[dagger]] in an attempt to [[Death|kill]] her to protect Alirya's place by Ro's side. Vika reacted by killing Alirya but was, in turn, shot by Ro for her action. While Velya was enraged by his sister's death, Ro calmed him down and convinced him to stay with her.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Later, Ro discussed her encounter with the Faers in a [[Marda Ro's journal|recording]] she made for her descendants.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during his youth, Ro's great-great-grandson [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to her journal, including the entries where Faers were mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="TemptationDate">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> while listening to the journal again, Ro recalled that the journal's contents chronicled his great-great-grandmother's hunt for Evereni, including the Faer twins.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I realized, as I was building the story, that it would be just much more dynamic for there to be two of them.|Tessa Gratton, discussing the creation of Vika Faer|<ref name="GrattonInterview" />}}
[[File:TalesOfLightAndLife-Cover.png|thumb|right|180px|Faers were created for "A Closed Fist Has No Claws," published in ''Tales of Light and Life'' (cover pictured).]]
The Faers appeared in flashbacks in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> In an interview, Gratton revealed that<ref name="GrattonInterview" /> they<ref name="GrattonPronouns">{{Bluesky|username=tessagratton.bsky.social|name=[[Tessa Gratton]]|quote=she/he/they|archivedate=20251206043541}}</ref> find the lack of naming conventions in ''[[Star Wars]]'' frustrating, which led to her deciding to give Evereni a naming convention as one of the first parts of the story he created.<ref name="GrattonInterview" />

Gratton initially planned on having Marda Ro meet two Evereni, one of whom rejects her, while the other embraces her.<ref name="GrattonInterview" /> In the final story, those ended up being the elder Evereni "[[Isren]]" and Velya Faer respectively.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> However, while building the story, they realized that it would be much more dynamic for there to be a pair of Evereni with a duality similar to the one that Ro had with her<ref name="GrattonInterview" /> cousin<ref name="Closed Fist" /> [[Yana Ro]], which led her to create Vika Faer, a process he described as complicated.<ref name="GrattonInterview" />
