The '''Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor''', also known as the '''Eta-2 Actis Jedi Interceptor''', '''Eta-2 light interceptor''', '''Eta-2 interceptor''' or simply '''Actis''', was a [[starfighter]] used by the [[Jedi]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Their popularity with the [[Jedi Order]] during the conflict resulted in the Actis being known as the '''Jedi starfighter'''. Designed to replace the [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7 ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]], the Eta-2 interceptor was a smaller vessel but possessed superior firepower.

==Characteristics==
[[File:Anakins_Eta-2_SWCT.png|thumb|left|250px|Anakin Skywalker's custom yellow Eta-2]]
Measuring 5.47 [[meter]]s in length,<ref name="SWJB" /> the Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light [[interceptor]] was a highly maneuverable combat-oriented [[starfighter]] utilized by the [[Jedi Order]] to lead troops in battle during the final days of the [[Clone Wars]]. As with earlier [[Jedi]] starfighters, the ship lacked a [[hyperdrive]] and relied on an external [[hyperspace transport ring]] to reach [[hyperspace]]. Despite this, it was faster than its [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|''Aethersprite'']] predecessor, with [[ion engine]]s providing a maximum acceleration of 5,200G and a max speed of 1,500 kph. The Eta-2 featured wings with upper and lower radiator panels, [[S-foils]], that were opened to shed excess heat from the ship's [[engine]]s. The radiator wings were primarily opened during intense combat.<ref name="Ships of the Galaxy" />

The ship was piloted by a single [[pilot]], with the assistance of a single externally mounted [[astromech droid]] for navigation and repair assistance. For offense, the ship featured two [[laser cannon]]s and two [[ion cannon]]s. Unlike laser cannons, the ship's [[ion cannon]]s fired bursts of [[plasma]] that caused temporary electrical disruptions to their target upon impact.<ref name="Ships of the Galaxy" />

==History==
[[File:JediGeneral-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|The Eta-2 ''Actis''-class interceptor used by a Jedi General]]

===Clone Wars===
The Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor was designed by [[Kuat Systems Engineering]] and was introduced sometime prior to [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Sabotage">''{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> It was intended to be a successor to the [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7 ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] and its [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B variant]], which had been used by the Jedi Order before and during the [[Clone Wars]]. The Eta-2 saw extensive use during the final year of the Clone Wars, most notably by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]].<ref name="Ships of the Galaxy" />

Skywalker and his [[padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] both flew in their new Eta-2s in the [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|Battle of Cato Neimoidia]], where they were both intercepted by [[droid tri-fighter]]s. They released [[Pistoeka sabotage droid|Pistoeka sabtoage droids]] onto Skywalker's starfighter, which began shutting down the interceptor's systems. The droids drilled underneath the craft, resulting in an explosion that caused Skywalker to fall unconscious. Tano ordered [[R2-D2]] to land the ship on a nearby mountain so that she could jump to the fighter to rescue him. She succeeded just before Skywalker's starfighter went over the cliff, but not before R2 escaped.<ref name="Sabotage" />

Grand Master Yoda used a [[Yoda's fighter|modified]] Eta-2, alongside R2-D2, on his journey to discover the secrets of [[Force spirit|eternal consciousness]].<ref name="Voices">{{TCW|Voices}}</ref>   

Skywalker and Kenobi both flew their Eta-2s during the [[Battle of Coruscant]], to rescue [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] who was being held hostage aboard the ''[[Invisible Hand]]''. Kenobi's starfighter was heavily damaged by buzz droids during the approach and his [[astromech droid]] [[R4-P17]] was destroyed. Skywalker used his fighter to disable the ''Invisible Hand''<nowiki/>'s shields and the two crash-landed their starfighters inside the [[hangar]] bay. The starfighters were both destroyed when the ''Invisible Hand''<nowiki/>'s aft section broke apart from the rest of the ship as they entered [[Coruscant]]'s atmosphere.<ref name="ROTS" />

Kenobi flew in a [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's blue Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|blue Eta-2]] with [[R4-G9]] on his mission to hunt down [[Grievous|General Grievous]] on [[Utapau]].<ref name="ROTS" />

===Imperial Era===
The newly christened Darth Vader would use a [[Darth Vader's green Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|green Eta-2]] on his [[Mission to Mustafar|mission]] to kill the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Executive Separatist Council|leaders]] on [[Mustafar]].<ref name="ROTS" /> 

Vader would continue to use a modified black [[Darth Vader's black Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|variant]] of the starfighter for several years following the conclusion of the Clone Wars.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> 

Five years after the end of the war, a damaged Jedi interceptor was found on the junkyard planet [[Bracca]] by two [[Scrapper Guild]] members, [[Prauf]] and [[Cal Kestis]]. Unknown to Prauf, the ship's discovery caused Kestis to recall his past, as he was a Jedi survivor of the purge that shattered the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> 

A grey Eta-2 was hung as one of the many exhibits in the [[Gallery of Peace]] in the [[Coalition for Progress headquarters]], which was located on the third [[moon]] of [[Coruscant]], [[Centax-3]].<ref name="Aphra 35">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 35|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 35]]</ref>

[[File:VaderBattlesTIEs-2020DarthVader10.png|thumb|Vader fighting his way to Exegol in his green Eta-2.|307x307px]]
In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>, Vader reused his green Eta-2 on a mission to travel through the [[Red Honeycomb Zone]] to find the [[Sith]] planet [[Exegol]].<ref name="Darth Vader 9">[[Darth Vader (2020) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 9]]</ref> Alongside [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]] in an [[escape pod]], Vader was intercepted by [[Imperial Navy|Imperial forces]] sent to stop him, and he used the Eta-2 to take out the attacking [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. The pair also encountered a [[Unidentified summa-verminoth subspecies|subspecies of summa-verminoth]]. Upon crashing on Exegol, Vader used the Force to tame the creature.<ref name="Darth Vader 10">[[Darth Vader (2020) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 10]]</ref>  

===Later use===
Between [[32 ABY]] and [[34 ABY]], one Eta-2 was piloted by [[Temmin Wexley]] of the [[Resistance]]'s [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] during a [[Heist of the Alderaanian data archive|mission]] to obtain a [[Archive of the Great Library of Alderaan|data archive]] containing all that was left of the [[Royal Library of Alderaan|Royal Library]] of [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Annual 2">[[Poe Dameron Annual 2|''Poe Dameron Annual'' 2]]</ref>
