The '''Bedlam Raiders''' were a group of [[Pirate|marauders]] based on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]] during the [[Imperial Era]], although they shared their name<ref name="Survivor" /> with the mysterious planet [[Bedlam]].<ref name="GEstaff">{{GEAttr|Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities}} has backstory elements that, though not directly published for the public, are officially [[canon]] details that can be obtained verbally from cast members working in the store.</ref> They were led by the [[Gen'Dai]] [[Rayvis]] and the [[Dark side of the Force|fallen]] [[Jedi]] [[Dagan Gera]].<ref name="Survivor" />

The image used by the Raiders, was [[Forest Array|from]] [[Koboh Observatory|three]] [[Shattered Moon|arrays]] [[Alignment Control Center|creating]] a safe passage through the [[Koboh Abyss]].<ref name="Survivor" />

==Description==
{{Quote|Monk and I picked it up from the previous owner through a - well, a - well..."<br />"He caught a fatal case of blaster poisoning courtesy of the Bedlam Raiders.|[[Greez Dritus]] and [[M-6NK]] "Monk"|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
[[File:TagueLouesh.png|thumb|left|200px|A lieutenant of the Bedlam Raiders armed with a lightsaber.]]
The Bedlam Raiders were originally formed of many disparate bands of bandits and guns-for-hire, brought together by the [[Gen'Dai]] [[Rayvis]] through violence and persuasive diplomacy. With an army of [[battle droid]]s under their control, the Bedlam Raiders were a powerful group of [[Pirate|marauders]] with a lot of firepower and feared by the inhabitants of the [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. Many [[lieutenant]]s of the Bedlam Raiders were given [[lightsaber]]s by Rayvis, who collected several from the [[Jedi]] he killed. Due to their great power and many resources, aided by Koboh's little importance to the [[Galactic Empire]], the Bedlam Raiders were able to impose their will on the world without facing much opposition. Jedi Knight Cal Kestis, having fought in the [[Clone Wars]] and knowing the capabilities of battle droids, stated that the Bedlam Raiders possessed enough power to conquer a planet.<ref name="Survivor" />

==History==
===Claiming a droid army===
{{Quote|The Raider base is in the swamp. They gutted an old Lucrehulk battleship from the Clone Wars."<br />"We've seen it. Means they've got a lot of firepower.|[[Mosey Cimmaron]] and Cal Kestis|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
[[File:Rayvis-CGSWG.png|thumb|right|200px|Created and led by the Gen'Dai warrior Rayvis, the Bedlam Raiders were feared throughout Koboh.]]
As of [[9 BBY]], the Bedlam Raiders' main base of operations was a crashed [[core ship]] of a [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|''Lucrehulk''-class LH-3210 cargo freighter]], ''[[Primacy]]'', on [[Koboh]], although they also had a presence on the planet's [[Shattered Moon]]. The raiders commanded an [[army]] of [[B1-series battle droid]]s, [[B2-series super battle droid]]s, [[BX-series droid commando]]s, [[droideka]]s, [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s, [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]], and [[HMP droid gunship]]s in stasis, from the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Survivor" />

[[File:ThePrimacyExterior.png|left|thumb|200px|Primacy was the main base of the Bedlam Raiders, where they acquired an army of Clone Wars-era battle droids.]]
In 9 BBY,<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> surviving [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] crash-landed on Koboh and quickly learned about the nearby [[Koboh Abyss]] and its potential access to [[Tanalorr]], which seemed like a perfect safe haven for fugitives from the [[Galactic Empire]]. In his quest to find a passage through the deadly [[nebula]] Koboh Abyss, Cal decimated the Bedlam Raiders and [[Death|killed]] Rayvis, as well as the [[Dark side of the Force|fallen]] [[Jedi]] [[Dagan Gera]], whom Rayvis served and took command of after the Gen'Dai's death.

===An alliance for revenge===
{{Quote|Have the Bedlam Raiders been cleared out?''"<br />"''Actually, this is the fun part. Sorc's champion has cut a deal with what's left of the Raiders.''"<br />"''They're working together? That's not good."<br />"''All to bag you.|Cal Kestis and [[Caij Vanda]]|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
Soon after, the surviving Bedlam Raiders and their remaining [[battle droid]]s allied with [[Jo (bounty droid)|Jo the Cannibal]] and the [[Haxion Brood]] to [[Death|kill]] Cal and avenge their leaders, but Cal succeeded in killing them, including Jo. Although it already had a presence on Koboh, the Empire avoided the planet, as it thought the world was not valuable enough to justify facing the might of the Raiders, but the power vacuum resulting from their [[Battle at the Koboh Observatory|decimation]] was quickly filled by the Empire, which increased its military presence on the planet.<ref name="Survivor" />
