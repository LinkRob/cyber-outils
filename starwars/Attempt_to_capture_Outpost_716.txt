In [[22 BBY]] during the [[Clone Wars]], [[General]] [[Grievous]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] '''attempted to capture''' [[Outpost 716]] from the [[Galactic Republic]]. In the end, Grievous's attack failed. As the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lords]] [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]], the puppet masters of the war, still wanted the outpost to fall under Separatist control, Tyranus later [[Capture of Outpost 716|dispatched]] his [[assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] on her own mission to Outpost 716, which ended with Ventress successfully destroying the command center.

==The attempt==
[[File:Grievous-DestroyMalevolence.png|thumb|left|300px|General Grievous (pictured) commanded the effort to take Outpost 716.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the [[Galactic Republic]], [[General]] [[Grievous]]<ref name="HS 5" />&mdash;a brilliant tactician, powerful combatant, and effective [[Jedi hunter|Jedi killer]] who served as the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander of the Separatist Droid Army]]<ref name="SWBook">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>&mdash;attempted to capture [[Outpost 716]], a posting and command center used by the Republic. The well-defended command center was staffed by a group of [[clone trooper]]s and operated under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Mayanka Catrine]], who was joined by her [[Padawan]],<ref name="HS 5" /> [[Jedi Commander]]<ref name="Jedi Commander">[[Hyperspace Stories 5|''Hyperspace Stories'' 5]] establishes that [[Agustin]] was a [[Padawan]] who served in the [[Republic Military]]. According to ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', Padawans in such a position served as [[Jedi Commander]]s.</ref> [[Agustin]]. However, the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lords]] [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]]<ref name="HS 5" />&mdash;who controlled the war in their respective public personas of [[Supreme Chancellor]] Sheev Palpatine of the Republic and Confederate [[Head of State]] [[Count of Serenno|Count]] Dooku<ref name="SWBook" />&mdash;wanted to see the outpost taken out of service via a Separatist attack. To that end, Grievous launched his effort against Outpost 716. However, despite all of Grievous's skills, the assault ultimately failed.<ref name="HS 5" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|I defeated their plans. Now you shall grant me the title of ''Sith Lord''!''"<br />"No. ''You destroyed their outpost but you did not defeat ''them''. You must ''prove'' yourself worthy.''"<br />"''I have ''more'' than done so. Even Grievous couldn't&mdash;''"<br />"I will tell you when you have done so.|Asajj Ventress and Darth Tyranus after her successful plan|<ref name="HS 5" />}}
[[File:AsajjVentressattacks-HyperspaceStories5.png|thumb|right|330px|Count Dooku dispatched his personal assassin Asajj Ventress to capture Outpost 716 after General Grievous's attack failed.]]
Due to her own skills and reasoning that she was expendable, Tyranus later dispatched his personal [[assassin]] and [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], [[Asajj Ventress]], in another [[Capture of Outpost 716|attempt]] to capture Outpost 716. Although Sidious doubted her ability to take a base even Grievous could not capture, Dooku promised that the command center would soon fall in a [[Hologram|holographic communication]] with Sidious aboard his [[Count Dooku's solar sailer|''Punworcca 116''-class interstellar sloop]]. In fact, Ventress had already captured and destroyed the base thanks to a deceptive plan: she allowed herself to be captured and only struck when she was certain the [[Jedi]] had departed for another [[Unidentified mission (Mayanka_Catrine)|mission]].<ref name="HS 5" />

Ventress returned to Tyranus immediately after his discussion with Sidious, demanding to be anointed a [[Sith Lord]] by citing that she had succeeded where even Grievous had failed. Instead, Tyranus ordered her to [[Hunt for a Jedi artifact|find]] a [[Unidentified Jedi artifact|Jedi artifact]] to prove herself. During her [[Ambush on the moon of Staggec|investigation]] on the [[Moon of Staggec]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], she learned that, once more, Grievous had been Tyranus's [[Mission to the moon of Staggec|first choice]] in carrying out the mission, as the general had been previously tasked with locating the item.<ref name="HS 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
Grievous's attempt to capture Outpost 716 was mentioned in the [[Hyperspace Stories 5|fifth issue]] of the [[2022]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories]]''. The issue was written by [[Amanda Deibert]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="HS 5" /> on [[May 24]], [[2023]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3010-785/Star-Wars-Hyperspace-Stories-5|text=Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories #5}}</ref>
