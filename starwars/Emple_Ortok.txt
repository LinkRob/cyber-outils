'''Emple Ortok''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served in the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization.<ref name="CtM" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Ortok was on their balcony of their [[Emple Ortok's apartment|apartment]] in [[Eriadu City]] on the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]] when the former [[Lucem]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ruu]] landing on the balcony. As Ortok stood in shock at the man and demanded a name, Ruu exclaimed while igniting his [[lightsaber]] that he was one of them before striking down Ortok. Ruu proceeded to steal the Ortok's [[identichip]] and left them on the balcony while heading to a [[Unidentified public shipyard|public shipyard]] to their private [[Unidentified vessel|vessel]]. Ruu later used the identichip and voice of Ortok to travel pass the [[Stormwall]].<ref name="CtM" />

==Behind the scenes==
Emple Ortok first appeared in "[[Chronicles of Corellia: Cracking the Mask]]," the second installment of the ''[[The High Republic: Chronicles of Corellia]]'' short stories. The story was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' [[Star Wars Insider 228|228th issue]]<ref name="CtM" /> on [[October 29]], [[2024]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/167-star-wars-insider-228|text=Star Wars Insider #228}}</ref>
