The '''''Facet''''' was a [[Cieran]]-made [[Nivex-class starfighter|''Nivex''-class starfighter]] owned by the [[Thief|thieves]] [[Pax Maripher]] and [[Rahara Wick]], who modified it and stored it in the lower cargo hold aboard their [[freighter]] [[Meryx (starship)|''Meryx'']]. It had a coppery surface.<ref name="Master & Apprentice" />

Around [[40 BBY]], as Maripher and Wick went to [[Pijal]] on an crystal-stealing operation, they became involved in a [[Jedi]] [[Mission to Pijal|mission]] led by [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Wick took it to engage in a firefight with the [[blackguard]]s and [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]]. She was shot down captured. Kenobi and Maripher repaired it. As the crisis came to a tipping point, Kenobi was aboard the ''Facet'' and accidentally engaged the [[autopursuit]], taking the starfighter through the corridors of the cruiser ''[[Leverage]]'' and crashing into the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]].<ref name="Master & Apprentice" />
