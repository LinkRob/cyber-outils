'''Baylan Skoll's theme''' was composed by [[Deana Kiner|Deana]], [[Sean Kiner|Sean]], and [[Kevin Kiner]] for the [[2023]] television series [[Star Wars: Ahsoka|''Ahsoka'']]. The theme was initially introduced in piano, in the style of [[Wikipedia:Sergei Rachmaninoff|Rachmaninoff]]. Sometimes played as "big bombastic Baylan brass," it also interacts with [[Shin Hati's theme]], which the Kiners enjoyed playing with.
