During the [[Galactic Civil War]] in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[planet]] [[Fedovoi End]] was '''destroyed''' by the loyalist [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], led by [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]], as part of [[Operation: Cinder]]. The destruction resulted in the [[death]]s of half a million [[Trooper|troops]] and their families, loyal to the [[Yomo Council]], as well as a military outpost on the planet's surface.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
