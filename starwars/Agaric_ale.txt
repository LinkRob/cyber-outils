'''Agaric ale''' was a type of [[ale]] that could be [[Diet|consumed]] by [[Neimoidian]]s and found on the [[Colonies]] [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]]. During her duties following the [[Cadesura disaster]]<ref name="Brotherhood" /> of [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] to [[22 BBY]].</ref> [[Neimoidian Royal Guard]] [[Ruug Quarnom]] instructed her stressed partner [[Ketar Nor]] to go home and relax, potentially to enjoy an agaric ale.<ref name="Brotherhood" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Agaric ale was just research of any previously established lore related to Neimoidian culture. I used Wook to find nuggets like that!|Mike Chen|<ref name="Email">{{EmailCite|email=File:MikeChenEmail2.png|creator=[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|date=2023-12-13}}</ref>}}
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], agaric ale was mentioned in the [[2022]] novel [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']], written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="Brotherhood" /> While drafting ''Brotherhood'', Chen included the ale after researching already-established lore about Neimoidian culture on the free-to-edit, unofficial online ''Star Wars'' encyclopedia known as [[Wookieepedia]].<ref name="Email" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Agaric ale/Legends|drink]] first appeared in the [[2001]] novel ''[[Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter]]'' by [[Michael Reaves]].<ref name="Shadow Hunter">''[[Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter]]''</ref>
