'''Alluv Eyam''' was a [[Neimoidian]] [[Politics|politician]] who served as [[Cato Neimoidia]]'s [[minister of defense]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[22 BBY]], following the [[Cadesura disaster]] in the [[capital]] [[city]] [[Zarra]], Eyam negotiated terms with [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] to allow a [[Galactic Republic]]-led [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Kenobi and Skywalker)|diplomatic investigation]] into the incident. Since Cato Neimoidia was officially neutral in the war, Eyam also welcomed an emissary of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], [[Asajj Ventress]], who attempted to persuade the minister that Kenobi and the Republic could not be trusted.<ref name="Brotherhood" />
