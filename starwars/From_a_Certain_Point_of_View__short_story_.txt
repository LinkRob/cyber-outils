"'''From a Certain Point of View'''" is a short story featuring [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] that was written by [[Alex Jennings]] as part of the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''. The story was published on [[August 29]], [[2023]].
