'''Falthina Sharest''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Imperial Emigration Officer|emigration officer]] in the [[Corellian Security Forces]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], CorSec became an extension of the Imperial forces on [[Corellia]], and she served as [[Lead Transport Security Officer]] in [[Coronet City Spaceport|Coronet Spaceport]].

==Biography==
Falthina Sharest had at least one sibling, a [[Falthina Sharest's sister|sister]].<ref name="Solo Novel" /> By [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on [[Corellia]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to six years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]], therefore the events on Corellia in ''Solo'' must take place in [[13 BBY]].</ref> Sharest worked in [[Coronet City Spaceport|Coronet Spaceport]] booth at the planet [[Corellia]]. Her<ref name="Solo Novel" /> work consisted on opening security gates to travelers to let them depart from Corellia or visit it.<ref name="Solo" /> Sharest hated her job because, as [[Falthina Sharest's grandmother|her grandmother]] used to say, it was as dull as an old [[milk]] [[knife]]. She dreamed to leave Corellia in order to visit other planets, only coming back to Corellia to visit her sister.<ref name="Solo Novel" />

[[File:OfficerSharestBribe-SASWS.png|thumb|300px|left|Qi'ra offered Sharest a bribe of coaxium.]]
One day, two criminals known as [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]] approached and offered to bribe her with a vial of [[coaxium]] in exchange for passage through the security gate. While Sharest mentioned that they could be arrested for just having the vial, Han replied that it would not do anyone good then. She agreed to allow them through, provided they handed the coaxium over prior to the gate being opened. Qi'ra did not agree to this, and instead suggested a simultaneous exchange. However, because the [[White Worms]] gang members were approaching, Han encouraged her to hand it over. Qi'ra gave up the coaxium, and Sharest opened the gate. However, as Han passed through, Qi'ra was grabbed by members of the White Worms gang led by the [[Grindalid]] thug [[Moloch]].<ref name="Solo" />

Meanwhile, Sharest took the opportunity to issue a security alert; this action promptly broke her agreement to let the young couple pass quietly. Officer Sharest then locked down the gate which separated Han and Qi'ra. Despite Han's pleas for her to open the passage again, she ignored him and continued to alert the spaceport staff about an unauthorized traveler.<ref name="Solo" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:FalthinaSharest.jpg|thumb|200px|right|Sharest was opportunistic and apathetic towards most travelers she encountered.]]
Falthina Sharest was a human female who stood 1.63 meters tall<ref name="Sharest Databank">{{Databank|officer-sharest|Officer Sharest}}</ref> and had [[Color|brown]] eyes. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Sharest had dark [[hair]].<ref name="Solo" /> A [[Lead Transport Security Officer]] in Coronet Spaceport, Sharest seized and sometimes accepted contraband from unlucky and foolish travelers if its value meant a big payday for her, such as when Han and Qi'ra brought her a vial of valuable coaxium,<ref name="Sharest Databank" /> given that she hated her job.<ref name="Solo Novel" />

Sharest's personality changed depending in the circumstances. She showed concern when Han and Qi'ra bribed her with a vial of coaxium, as they could have been detained for having it. She opened the gates out of Corellia for them, but when Moloch and the White Worms began to drag Qi'ra to their [[Den of the White Worms|den]] to make her pay for her treason, Sharest apathetically left the young woman to her fate and closed the door, reporting that Qi'ra was an unauthorized traveler and ignoring Han's pleas to reopen the gate.<ref name="Solo" /> Despite these negative traits, Sharest was not uncaring at all, as at least she loved her family, planning to depart Corellia to explore [[the galaxy]] but only coming back to visit her sister.<ref name="Solo Novel" />

==Behind the scenes==
Falthina Sharest was portrayed by [[Anna Francolini]] in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Credits">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' credits</ref> and was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TOG" />

In the [[Solo: A Star Wars Story Adaptation 1|first issue]] of the [[Marvel Comics]]' [[Solo: A Star Wars Story Adaptation|comic book adaptation]] of the film, Sharest's dialogue is shortened and she does not express concern for Han and Qi'ra nor does she report Qi'ra to the spaceport's authorities as an unauthorized traveler.<ref name="SA1">[[Solo: A Star Wars Story Adaptation 1|''Solo: A Star Wars Story Adaptation'' 1]]</ref>
