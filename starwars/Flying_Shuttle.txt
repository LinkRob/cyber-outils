'''Flying Shuttle''' was a company that produced seasonings during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="TVoSD" /> In [[200 BBY]],<ref name="Vow Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Sean]] inspected the [[Presidential manor|estate]] of [[President]] [[Jamie Brasson]] on the [[planet]] [[Begamor]]. While visiting the kitchen of the [[Latero]] [[Chef|head chef]] [[Marino Pomana]], Sean noticed that about half of Pomana's products, including all the [[Galaxy's Choice]] and Flying Shuttle&ndash;branded [[foodstuff]]s and [[Rouge Beauty Company]]&ndash;branded beauty products, were counterfeits made by the local business [[Opera Trade]].<ref name="TVoSD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flying Shuttle was mentioned in the [[Wikipedia:Written Chinese|Chinese]] web novel ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''<ref name="TVoSD" /> written by "[[His Majesty the King]]," within its fifth chapter, which was released on [[December 18]], [[2020]]. The company's name was written in [[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|simplified Chinese]] as "飞梭" (fēi suō).<ref name="Chapter 5">{{WebCite|url=https://www.qidian.com/chapter/1015950219/623147707|text=第5章 独门秘方 _《星球大战：白银誓约》小说在线阅读|author=[[His Majesty the King]]|date=2020-12-18|language=[[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|Simplified Chinese]]|archivedate=20260514141239}}</ref>
