'''Buzzz''' was a foamy substance<ref name="Must-Try Drinks">{{DPB|url=2020/01/must-try-drinks-at-ogas-cantina-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Must-Try Drinks at Oga's Cantina in ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> that caused a tingling sensation in the [[Mouth|lips]] when consumed. It was an ingredient in the [[Fuzzy Tauntaun]], a [[fruit]]y [[alcohol]]ic [[cocktail]] served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina">{{GEAttr|Oga's Cantina}}</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events of [[Oga's Cantina]] within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Buzzz appears in [[In-universe|real-world]] version of the Fuzzy Tauntaun served at Oga's Cantina in the themed attraction [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. The menu's description of the cocktail reads, "[[Wikipedia:Cîroc Vodka|Cîroc Peach Vodka]], [[Wikipedia:Bols (brand)|Bols]] [[Wikipedia:Peach|Peach]] [[Wikipedia:Schnapps|Schnapps]], and [[Wikipedia:Simply Orange Juice Company|Simply Orange&reg;]] topped with [[Wikipedia:Tangerine|Tangerine]], [[Wikipedia:Sucrose#Types|Pure Cane Sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Acmella oleracea|'Buzzz']] Foam."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/dinner|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Dinner Menu}}</ref>
