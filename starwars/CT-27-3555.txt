'''CT-27-3555''' was a [[clone cadet]] who served in [[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]], a training unit on [[Kamino]], during the [[Clone Wars]]. They were marked with a five on their [[clone training armor]].

==Biography==
CT-27-3555 was a [[Cloning|clone]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] who was [[Reproduction|born]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] to serve in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. Early during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], CT-27-3555 was part of [[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]], a [[squad]] of [[clone cadet]]s training on Kamino. During their training, Bravo [[Squad]] was able to successfully complete the practice test in record [[time]]. When [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Colt]] of the [[Rancor Battalion]] arrived to oversee the final exams, Bravo Squad, along with [[Domino Squad]] and two other units, were summoned to a [[hangar]] in [[Tipoca City Military Complex]] for Colt's speech and his introduction by [[Master Chief]] [[Bric]]. During his speech, Colt selected Bravo Squad for the final test first due to their record time in completing the practice test.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

Starting [[Citadel Challenge]] version THX variable 1138, [[CT-66-3232]] and [[CT-66-3233]] took the left flank while [[CT-66-3238]], [[CT-66-3339]], and CT-27-3555 took the right flank. CT-66-3232 directed CT-66-3238 and CT-66-3339 to move forward and engage the training [[battle droid]]s while they followed close behind and the others provided cover fire. The squad moved up, reaching the base of the citadel and eventually CT-66-3339 told CT-66-3238 to move forward to the citadel. CT-66-3238 was able to rappel up and capture the light post, completing the exam. While exiting the arena, CT-66-3555 and their teammates passed the incoming Domino Squad, while CT-66-3233 mocked them.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Personality and traits==
A clone of Jango Fett, CT-27-3555 was born on Kamino, was 1.83 [[meter]]s tall, had [[Color|black]] [[hair]], tan skin, and was a [[human]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Skills and abilities==
During training, CT-27-3555 demonstrated that they had teamwork skills and effectively coordinated with the rest of Bravo Squad.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Equipment==
CT-27-3555 was equipped with [[clone training armor]] which contained the red coloring of Bravo Squad, the number five on their chest plate and left shoulder [[armor]], had their designation on their left leg, and utilized a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] with non-lethal bolts and an [[ascension cable]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Behind the scenes==
This Bravo Squad cadet's identification number was first depicted in the episode "[[Clone Cadets]]" of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''. It says 27-3555, and the prefix CT- was predetermined. This way of determining identification numbers was proved with the troopers in [[Domino Squad]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />
