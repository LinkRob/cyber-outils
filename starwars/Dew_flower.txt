'''Dew flowers''' were a type of [[flower]] that was found on the [[planet]] [[Siline]]. It was edible for [[Silinese]] and [[human]]s.<ref name="TCC" /> In [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> during the [[survival level]] of the [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|Crowning Convocation]] on Siline, the human [[Lionine Graf|Lio Graf]] gathered some dew flowers for a fruit [[salad]].<ref name="TCC" />
