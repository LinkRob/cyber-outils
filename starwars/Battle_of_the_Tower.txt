The '''Battle of the Tower''' was fought on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] [[Crucival]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] nineteen [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Clone Wars]]. The Empire armed the civilians to help them defend the [[Crucival transmitter tower]]. However, soon after the battle began, they abandoned the armed civilians and hid under their shield, using the civilians as fodder for the rebels.<ref name="Battlefront" />

After decimating the civilian mercenary force, the 61st Company shredded through the tower's defense. After the battle and the death of his friend [[Pira Ten|Pira]], one of the civilian mercenaries named [[Hazram Namir]] joined Twilight Company. He was recruited by the [[Besalisk]] [[Gadren]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
