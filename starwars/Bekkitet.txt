'''Bekkitet''' was a [[Quarren]] individual who served as a [[major]] in the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] aboard the [[Emissary-class Republic Cruiser|''Emissary''-class Republic Cruiser]] ''[[Third Horizon]]'' during the [[Battle of Eriadu]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 3" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bekkitet first appeared in the [[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 3|third issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi]]'', released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 3" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Marika Cresta]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[April 30]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/122249/star_wars_the_high_republic_-_fear_of_the_jedi_2025_3|text=Star Wars: The High Republic - Fear of the Jedi (2025) #3}}</ref> Bekkitet is referred to as a [[Lieutenant Commander]] in ''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]'',<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> but as a major in ''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 3.<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 3" />
