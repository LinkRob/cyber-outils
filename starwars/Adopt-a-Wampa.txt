'''Adopt-a-Wampa''' was a business in [[Pons Ora]], a [[City|settlement]] on the [[planet]] [[Abafar]]. It was marked with a sign reading "ADOPT-A-[[Wampa|WAMPA]]" in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="MIA" />
