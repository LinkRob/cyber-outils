'''Dantoo Town''' was a town located on the [[planet]] [[Dantooine]]. Around the [[time]] of [[Destruction of Alderaan|the Disaster]], [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] was in Dantoo Town trying to reactivate and upgrade old [[droideka]]s. After being captured by and subsequently escaping from [[General]] [[Cassio Tagge]]'s expedition to the deserted [[Dantooine base|rebel base]] on the planet, she was forced to abandon the unfinished droids in the settlement.<ref name="The Trigger" />
