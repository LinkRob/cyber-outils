The '''Accresker Penal Legion''' was a unit of criminals deployed by the [[Galactic Empire]] as an expendable fighting force. They were based on [[Accresker Jail]], a mash of warships held together with an [[attractor node]] and hauled about by a [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]]. [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] became a part of the legion<ref name="Aphra 20" /> following her arrest in an [[Second attack on the Son-tuul Pride|attack on the Son-tuul Pride]].<ref name="Aphra 19">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 19|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 19]]</ref> She attempted to escape by making her [[Unidentified hubdroid|hubdroid]] malfunction and explode before having a rebellious [[hubdroid]] named [[Dek-Nil]] transfer her and [[Cornelius Evazan|Lopset Yas]] into his squad. They attempted to then use an escape pod to escape but Dek-Nil's restraining bolt was moved and took control of him, halting their plan.<ref name="Aphra 20" />

==Organization==
[[File:HubdroidRations-2016DoctorAphra20.jpg|thumb|left|200px|A hubdroid distributes rations]]
The Accresker Penal Legion was made to be an expendable fighting force as its entirety of [[soldier]]s were [[Prisoner|convicts]]. The criminals were kept in line by [[hubdroid]]s using micro implants that [[Detonator|detonated]] if the prisoner was more than ten [[meter]]s away from the droid. The [[Droid|hubdroids]] led [[squad]]s of at least half a dozen convicts into battle and supplied them with the needed [[Foodstuff|food]] and [[weapon]]s. If the hubdroid happened to die in battle or other circumstances, its squad's implants would detonate all together to prevent any possible escape attempts without a hubdroid around. Squad members could not be transferred to another squad.<ref name="Aphra 20" />

The Accresker Penal Legion operated solely from the [[Accresker Jail]], a [[wreckage-prison]] held together by an [[attractor node]] and pulled by an [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]]. The wreckage prison deployed the many convicts into battle by undergoing a controlled collision and latching onto the target. The prison also had an [[airdome]] to keep a survivable environment for convicts to live in and fight in when the wreckage-prison was latched onto something.<ref name="Aphra 20" />
