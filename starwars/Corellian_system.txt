The '''Corellian system''',<ref name="No Disintegrations">[[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']]</ref> also known as the '''Corellia system''',<ref name="Tarkin" /> was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]] that contained the [[planet]]s [[Corellia]],<ref name="Most Wanted" /> [[Selonia]], [[Talus]] and [[Tralus]],<ref name="GG" /> as well as the [[star]] [[Corell]].<ref name="Most Wanted" /> The system was one of the founding systems of the [[Galactic Republic]]. It opposed the [[Military Creation Act]] before the [[Clone Wars]], and even closed its borders, but without declaring its independence.<ref name="Palpatine" />
