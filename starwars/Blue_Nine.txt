'''Blue Nine''' was the [[callsign]] assigned to the ninth [[pilot]] of [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]]. [[Calum Gram]] flew as Blue Nine during the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="SWROUVG" />

{{Reb-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Blue Nine callsign made its first appearance in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'', which was published by [[Electronic Arts]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
