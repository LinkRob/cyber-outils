The '''''Dynamo''''' was a [[starship]] owned by the [[Kuranu]] inventor [[Mantessa Chekkat]] and her daughter, [[Klerin Chekkat|Klerin]]. As Kuranu had a pathological fear of [[germ]]s, the ''Dynamo'' was kept clean and sanitized by an army of small [[droid]]s. The Chekkats utilized the vessel to travel to the [[Republic Fair]] on the [[planet]] [[Valo]], where the elder Chekkat hoped to sell a [[47 lightsaber nullifier|four-seven nullifier]] that Klerin had created to the [[Galactic Republic]] [[senator]] [[Tia Toon]]. However, Toon declined to purchase the device, and the ''Dynamo'' was later destroyed by [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] when they [[Valo Atrocity|attacked]] the Republic Fair.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
