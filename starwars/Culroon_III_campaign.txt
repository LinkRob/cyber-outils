Shortly before the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="Ships" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas" /> a '''campaign''' occurred<ref name="GEG">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref> on the astronomical object [[Culroon III]]. The conflict involved the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Culroonian]]s, the inhabitants of Culroon III,<ref name="So" /> and occurred as a result of a revolt there.<ref name="Helmet" /> The [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Maximilian Veers]] participated in the campaign<ref name="GEG" /> on behalf of the Empire, where he demonstrated his courage, cunning,<ref name="So" /> and leadership<ref name="Ships" /> and secured victory for the Empire through new tactics he had developed.<ref name="Helmet" /> Veers' actions led to his eventual promotion to the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[general]] in the [[Imperial Army]],<ref name="So" /> and he was recognized by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who requested Veers' assignment to his own [[Death Squadron]].<ref name="Helmet" /> The campaign was acknowledged in Veers' profile in the ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]'' [[author]]ed by the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] and his [[RA-7 protocol droid|protocol droid]] [[DK-RA-43]] after the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]]<ref name="GEG" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Atlas" />

==Behind the scenes==
The conflict on Culroon III was given an indirect mention in [[canon]] in [[Adam Gidwitz]]'s [[2015]] children's book [[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?|''So You Want to Be a Jedi?'']], a retelling of the<ref name="So" /> [[1980]]<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="So" /> It was referred to as a campaign in ''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]'', a [[2019]] reference book by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="GEG" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Insurrection on Culroon III]] involved the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]] and the native [[Culroon]] of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Culroon III/Legends|Culroon III]]. It appeared in a short story called "[[The Promotion of Lieutenant Veers]]," published in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'' by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Curtis Smith]] in [[1987]]. In the story, [[Maximilian Veers/Legends|Maximilian Veers]] received a promotion from [[Lieutenant/Legends|lieutenant]] to [[Major/Legends|major]] after securing an Imperial victory.<ref name="Promotion">{{WEGCite|story=The Promotion of Lieutenant Veers|book=The Star Wars Sourcebook}}</ref> The event was identified as the insurrection on Culroon III in Veers' [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (original)|databank]] entry, published by [[2001]].<ref name="Databank">{{DB|character|generalmaximilianveers|Veers, General Maximilian}}</ref>
