The '''''Centennial Peregrine''''' was a [[starship]] [[Thief|stolen]] and crashed by [[Hondo Ohnaka]], a [[pirate]] operating throughout [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], Ohnaka stole the vessel following a night of drinking [[Sarlacc Juice]] and firing [[blaster]] bolts at an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[garrison]] [[commander]]'s [[Foot|feet]], though he was hazy on the details. After stealing the vessel, Ohnaka was quickly pursued by two Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. He tried to escape to the [[planet]] [[Galagolos V]], where he was shot down and escaped into the planet's [[Galagolos spaceport|spaceport]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" />

Shortly afterward, Ohnaka stowed away aboard the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', hoping to steal the vessel. However, after overhearing a [[Gender|woman]] named [[Mahjo Reeloo]] pleading with the ship's owner, the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]], to aid in her quest to find [[Novian ruby|Novian rubies]], he decided to reveal himself, surprising Solo. He described the ship as very unusual, asking for its name, and was told it. In response, he playfully referred to the vessel he had stolen as the ''Centennial Peregrine''. Solo objected, but Ohnaka replied that he could not prove otherwise. He went on to state that having stolen and crashed it in the same day, it was a small thing to name it as well, asking if Solo wanted it to have [[Death|died]] without a name. Solo retorted that whoever Ohnaka stole it from likely had their own name for it, which he replied was a good point.<ref name="Pirate's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Centennial Peregrine'' appeared briefly in ''[[Pirate's Price]]'', a junior novel written by [[Lou Anders]], published in [[2019]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" />
