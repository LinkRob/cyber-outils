'''Freerago's Satellite Diner and Motel''' was a satellite with both a diner and motel in [[orbit]] of [[Hosnian Prime]], where many met to consort and mingle. It was owned by [[Freerago]] and frequented by many different [[species]], as well as [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]]. Sometime before the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] were there on a stakeout.<ref name="Last Shot" />
