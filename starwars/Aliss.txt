'''Aliss''' was a [[Pantoran]] who served as the [[chief communications officer]] of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] ''[[Shattersong]]'' during the [[High Republic Era]]. She was stationed aboard the ''Shattersong'' when the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] attempted to [[Assault on the Stormwall|breach]] the [[Stormwall]] barrier to the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization's [[Occlusion Zone]] territory, giving commands to the other [[starship]]s of the gathered [[fleet]].<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />
