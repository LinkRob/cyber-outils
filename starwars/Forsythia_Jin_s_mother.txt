The '''mother''' of [[Forsythia Jin]] was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who at some point served as the [[queen]] of [[Hynestia Prime|Hynestia]]. After her [[death]], the [[crown]] was left in dispute and Jin did all she could to ensure her claim to the [[throne]] was the only one. According to stories she killed each of her twelve siblings, with the help of a [[bounty hunter]], and killed them with a ruthless precision that left onlookers stunned.<ref name="Lando's Luck" />
