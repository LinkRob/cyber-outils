A '''free trader''' was a type of [[Merchant]] in [[The galaxy]]. On [[Coruscant]], the vast tunnels that connected the surface levels to [[Hangar|docking bays]] in the [[Coruscant Underworld]] gave free traders, along with [[Bounty hunter]]s and [[Smuggler]]s, quick and easy access to [[Imperial City|Galactic City]]'s lower levels.<ref name="Falcon 26">{{BuildFalconCite|26|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> A publication published during the [[Imperial Era]] known as the [[Free Trader's Gazette]] shared it's name with the type of merchant, as did [[Free Trader|a]] [[Gender|male]] [[fathier]] that took part in the [[Canto Cup]] [[Fathier racing|fathier race]] in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

==Behind the Scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Free traders were mentioned in "Coruscant Underworld,"<ref name="Falcon 26" /> an article written by [[Mark Newbold]]<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> and published in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 26|twenty-sixth]] issue of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="Falcon 26" /> which released around [[July 1]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|26}}</ref>
