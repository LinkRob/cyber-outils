The '''Dragon Kite''' was a flight maneuver used by [[New Republic pilot|pilots]] of the [[New Republic]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]. Four years after the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]], pilots of the [[Temperance battle group|''Temperance'' battle group]] were warned to only perform this maneuver in open space, along with the [[Antilles Intercept]] and most of all, the [[Enlightened Bantha]].<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dragon Kite was first mentioned in [[Star Wars]] [[Canon]] in the [[2020]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''.<ref name="Squadrons" /> It was originally referenced in [[Star Wars Legends]] in the [[1995]] comic [[X-Wing Rogue Squadron 1|''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' 1]].<ref name="XwingRO1">[[X-Wing Rogue Squadron 1|''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' 1]]</ref>
