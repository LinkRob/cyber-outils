'''Stormtrooper snipers''' were a [[Sniper|specialized]] variant of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] who served in the [[First Order military|military]] of the [[First Order]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War]].

==History==
The [[First Order]]'s predecessor, the [[Galactic Empire]], had [[stormtrooper sniper]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> When the Empire [[Imperial Instruments of Surrender|surrendered]] at the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> many Imperials fled into the [[Unknown Regions]]<ref name="Empire's End" /> and joined the First Order. The First Order created their own [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]], and trained [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[sniper]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Equipment==
First Order stormtrooper snipers wore typical [[First Order stormtrooper armor|stormtrooper armor]] and primarily carried [[DLT-19D heavy blaster rifle]]s, though they were also able to wield [[IQA-11 Blaster Rifle]]s, [[A280 blaster rifle]]s, [[NT-242]]s, and [[Cycler rifle]]s. [[Sergeant]]s wore [[Color|black]] [[Pauldron]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Behind the scenes==
First Order stormtrooper sniper Sergeants appeared in multiplayer gamemodes in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' as the Specialist class for the First Order.
