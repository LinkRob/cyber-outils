'''Ash-rabbits''' were a [[species]] of [[rabbit]] found throughout [[the galaxy]]. One type of ash-rabbit was the [[Sullustan ash-rabbit]], which was native to [[Sullust]]. They were small and [[Diet|herbivorous]], eating [[Foodstuff|food]] such as [[marsh-fruit]]. While on an [[Graf-World|uncharted swampworld]], [[Milo Graf]] attempted to capture an ash-rabbit, although he feared that his sister [[Lina Graf|Lina's]] [[happabore]]-like blundering would scare it away.<ref name="The Escape" />
