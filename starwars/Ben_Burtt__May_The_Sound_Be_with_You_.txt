"'''Ben Burtt: May The Sound Be with You!'''" is an article appearing in [[Star Wars Insider 99|''Star Wars Insider'' 99]]. It was written by [[Pat Jankiewicz|Patrick Jankiewicz]]. The article offers an inside look on the sound effects used on the films and features commentary from sound designer [[Ben Burtt]].
