'''2J-3PO''' was a [[protocol droid]] that was active during the [[Clone Wars]] on [[Tatooine]] and acted as a courier for the [[Vosadii/Canon|Vosadii]].<ref name="Rise" />

{{RPGseed}}
At one point, the droid was carrying a message from the [[Hutt Clan]] Vosadii to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] when the convoy was attacked by [[Tusken Raider]]s. After two days of wandering the desert, the droid encounters a group of agents and offers to split the reward for their help. They must then recover the message.<ref name="Rise" />
