'''Brub berries''' were a type of [[berry]] that grew on [[brub-brub bush]]es found on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]], where the berries were eaten by the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Ewok]] [[species]]. During a particularly bleak [[winter]] on Endor, a [[Bright Tree tribe]] hunting party collected an overflowing basket of brub berries while collecting essential supplies. The Ewok [[Weechee (Ewok)|Weechee]] carried the basket back to [[Bright Tree Village]], where he was spotted by his younger brother [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]], who was pleased to see Weechee relegated to the back of the party carrying the berries rather than the [[kublag]] the party had caught.<ref name="Kroolok" />

Warrick assumed Weechee would exaggerate how hard it had been in order to collect the berries, imagining that he would claim the thorns on the brub-brub bush were as big as a [[kradak]]'s [[Tooth|fang]]. When Weechee later belittled Warrick, the younger brother sarcastically mocked him for only picking berries on the hunt, to which Weechee responded that Warrick would not have been able to even reach the berries due to his short height.<ref name="Kroolok" />

By [[34 ABY]]<ref name="GE date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref>, brub berries had been incorporated into various [[foodstuff|culinary items]] found in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. The berries were incorporated into the [[alcohol]]ic beverage [[Nysillin and Bubbles with Brub Berry Essence]] served at [[Oga's Cantina]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina">{{GEAttr|Oga's Cantina}}</ref> and into the [[Brub Berry Muffin]] served at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]].<ref name="KSK" />
