'''Full Monty Catering''' is a catering company headed by [[Nick Chapman]] that provided craft services for the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. Catering the scenes shot in [[Wikipedia:Abu Dhabi|Abu Dhabi]], [[Wikipedia:United Arab Emirates|United Arab Emirates]], Chapman provided a liquid-based menu to keep the crew hydrated.

==History==
{{Quote|That was challenging. The chefs in the kitchen are working in 40 degrees even with air conditioning. The closest supermarket was two hours away.|Nick Chapman, on filming ''The Force Awakens''|<ref name="Interview" />}}
[[File:FullMontyCraftServices.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The craft services table for ''The Force Awakens'']]
Full Monty Catering is a catering company founded in [[2008]]<ref name="Website">{{WebCite|url=http://www.fullmontydubai.com|text=Welcome to Full Monty|work=[http://www.fullmontydubai.com www.fullmontydubai.com]|archivedate=20161010155358|nolive=1}}</ref> that provided the food services for the cast and crew of the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], at the scenes shot in [[Wikipedia:Abu Dhabi|Abu Dhabi]], [[Wikipedia:United Arab Emirates|United Arab Emirates]]. The company is run by [[Nick Chapman]], who explained in an interview that the company provided a craft services table that included [[Wikipedia:Sandwich|sandwiches]], [[Wikipedia:Wrap|wraps]], and quick bites. Their menu for the film was very much liquid-based; they provided [[Wikipedia:Protein|protein]] [[Wikipedia:Milkshake|shakes]], [[Wikipedia:Smoothie|smoothies]], and [[Wikipedia:Slushee|slushees]] to keep the crew hydrated.<ref name="Interview" />

Chapman called their [[Wikipedia:Energy bar|energy bars]] and [[Wikipedia:Iced tea|lemon iced tea]] the most popular items on the menu. The shoot was particularly challenging due to the heat of the middle of the [[Wikipedia:Summer|summer]]&mdash;chefs were forced to work in conditions that were uncomfortably hot, even with the help of air conditioning. Deliveries from a two-hour-away supermarket came daily in order to ensure that fresh food was always at hand; Chapman was committed to making sure that everything was cooked on-site and made-to-order.<ref name="Interview" />

Chapman stated that he loved working on the set and that it was enjoyable to work with director [[J.J. Abrams]] as well as producer [[Kathleen Kennedy]].<ref name="Interview" /> Full Monty Catering teased the production of the film in [[2014]] by posting an image of the food for ''The Force Awakens'' on their [[Wikipedia:Facebook|Facebook]] page with the caption "May the Craft Be with you !!," a play on<ref name="Facebook">{{Facebook|noprofile=1|url=1551905148358951/photos/a.1551910951691704/1551910831691716|name=Full Monty Catering|archiveurl=https://archive.is/NO2uz}}</ref> the ''Star Wars'' term "[[May the Force be with you]]."<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> While the company itself went uncredited, Chapman was credited as "Craft Supervisor."<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> Full Monty later called Episode VII a difficult secret to keep.<ref name="Website" /> As of [[{{CURRENTYEAR}}]], both Full Monty Catering's website and Facebook page have gone inactive.<ref name="Website" /><ref name="Facebook2">{{Facebook|username=Full-Monty-Catering-Services-1551905148358951|name=Full Monty Catering|archivedate=20211223000000}}</ref>
