'''Clejo''' was [[Gender|female]] individual who lived during the [[Rise of the Empire era]]. She was the aunt of [[Imperial Intelligence]] operative [[Chemish Runazha]].<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|7}}</ref> she was unemployed, and had damage to her [[lung]]s that medics assured were not permanent. Chemish later that year would feel guilty about not helping Clejo fix her neighbor's [[airspeeder]].<ref name="MoF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clejo was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
