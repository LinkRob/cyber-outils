==Description==
The Brotherhood of Darkness, also named the Sith Brotherhood, Sith Empire, or Sith army,<ref name="Note" /> was an [[Politics#Empire|empire]] of the [[Sith]].<ref name="Nexus">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref> It consisted of many [[Soldier|warriors]] of the Sith including the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Bane]].<ref name="Dark Side" /> The empire was active on the [[planet]] [[Auratera]] until its demise.<ref name="Nexus" />

==History==
A millennium before the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Brotherhood of Darkness<ref name="Note" /> rose to power, becoming an empire of the Sith. The empire was active on the planet Auratera during this time, and the Sith eventually participated in the [[Jedi-Sith War]] in [[1032 BBY]] against the [[Jedi Order]]. During the war, the greed of the Sith eventually resulted in infighting, allowing the [[Jedi]] and the [[Galactic Republic]] to defeat the Empire, becoming the last empire of the Sith at the time. The inhabitants of Auratera coordinated with the Jedi to secretly alter official Republic records to misclassify the system as uninhabitable. As such, the inhabitants and the planet became nearly closed off to the rest of [[the galaxy]], and eventually cut ties with the few remaining trading partners that they had when the Sith Empire fell.<ref name="Nexus" />

[[Darth]] Bane was the sole survivor of the Brotherhood, and established the [[Rule of Two]] that served as a doctrine for the surviving Sith for the next thousand years.<ref name="Dark Side" /> Eventually, the Dark Lord of the Sith [[Darth Sidious]], who followed the Rule of Two, destroyed the Republic from within during the [[Clone Wars]] after [[Supreme Chancellor election, 32 BBY|becoming]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] and commenced the [[Great Jedi Purge]] that destroyed most of the Jedi Order. The [[Galactic Empire]] rose from the Republic, cementing Sith rule throughout the galaxy.<ref name="Episode III" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BrotherhoodOfDarkness-BoS.jpg|thumb|left|125px|The Brotherhood of Darkness as depicted in ''Star Wars'' Legends]]
The Brotherhood of Darkness was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] as the "Sith army" in [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 28|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 28]].<ref name="HC 28" /> The Brotherhood was mentioned in a [[datascreen]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Cat and Mouse]],"<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref> though [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Matt Martin]] has stated he would consider it more of an [[Easter egg (virtual)|easter egg]].<ref name="Matt tweet">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1067139158987104256|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=@CharlyPyle I'd consider it more of an easter egg.|archivefile=File:Matt-Cat-and-Mouse.png}}</ref>

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the [[Brotherhood of Darkness/Legends|Brotherhood of Darkness]] first appeared in the ''[[Dark Forces: Jedi Knight]]'' comic book that was based on the [[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II|video game of the same name]].<ref name="Jedi Knight">''[[Dark Forces: Jedi Knight]]''</ref> It was an organization of [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] that was created by the [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Skere Kaan]].<ref name="TEGTW">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
