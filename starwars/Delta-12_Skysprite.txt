The '''Delta-12 Skysprite''' was a type of [[starfighter]] utilized by the [[Jedi Order]]. The craft was known for its agility and speed. [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] used a Delta-12 Skysprite when embarking on a [[Mission to Metagos|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Metagos]].<ref name="The Glass Abyss" />
