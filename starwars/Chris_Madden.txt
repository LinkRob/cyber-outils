'''Chris Madden''' is a concept artist who provided conceptual artwork for ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'', where he created concept art for [[Fennec Shand]], [[Ro Station]], [[Outcast|Fennec's ship]],<ref name="Cornered EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/cornered-episode-guide|text="Cornered" Episode Guide}}</ref> and the bottles lined along the wall of [[Cid's Parlor]].<ref name="Rampage EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/rampage-episode-guide|text="Rampage" Episode Guide}}</ref>
