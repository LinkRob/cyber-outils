'''Dominic Pace''' (born [[September 21]], [[1975]]) is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] actor who portrayed a [[Unidentified bounty hunter (Nevarro)|bounty hunter]] in the [[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian|first]] and [[Chapter 3: The Sin|third chapters]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''. 

Pace's makeup as the [[bounty hunter]] character was designed and applied by makeup artist [[Brian Sipe]], who used the name "Gekko" for the character.<ref name="Sipe Instagram">{{Instagram|username=briansipemakeup|url=p/CCAaT2fgnI4|name=[[Brian Sipe]]|date=2020-06-29|quote=The bounty hunter, Gekko was played by Dominic Pace. The makeup was designed and initial test by Brian Sipe.|archiveurl=https://archive.vn/pChja}}</ref> Pace said he was able to select his own weapon during production and chose a large, unique heavy [[blaster]].<ref name="Blaster">{{WebCite|url=http://www.yodasnews.com/10-questions-with-dominic-pace|text=10 Questions with Dominic Pace|author=Picirilli, Mark|date=2020-02-03|archivedate=20200318025112}}</ref> He views his character as ruthless, unapologetic, imposing, and intense.<ref name="Podcast">{{WebCite|url=https://soundcloud.com/user-925932928/episode-39-dominc-pace-the|text=Episode 39 - Dominic Pace, The Mandalorian|author=Fletzer, Pete|date=2019-11-11|work=[https://soundcloud.com/user-925932928 Around the Galaxy]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/uAdX1}}</ref>

Pace was accepted as an honorary member of the fan organizations the [[501st Legion (fan organization)|501st Legion]]<ref name="Pace 3">{{Twitter|DominicPPace|status/1212717459548991488|Dominic Pace|quote=Thank you to the @501stLegion . Amazing company on that list. ❤️ https://www.501st.com/honorary.php|archivedate=20200103160359}}</ref> and the [[Rebel Legion]].<ref name="Pace twitter">{{Twitter|DominicPPace|status/1219280707320594433|Dominic Pace|quote=Thank you to the @rebellegion for this honor. May we continue to pay it forward to help others in need. Grateful for all of you and your contributions to your communities. ❤️|archivedate=20200121223050}}</ref> The mayor of [[Wikipedia:Sioux City, Iowa|Sioux City, Iowa]] officially named July 18, 2020 as Gekko the Bounty Hunter aka Dominic Pace Day.<ref name="Pace twitter 2">{{Twitter|DominicPPace|status/1284620499801210880|DominicPPace|quote=Deeply honored and grateful today in Sioux City. Thank you to the Mayor of Sioux City for naming July 19th, 2020, Lair of the Gekko Dominic Pace Day. Honored to share this with #BrianSipeMakeup @dave_filoni and @Jon_Favreau $1,000 earned for @TheWookieeRoars|archivedate=20200719025903}}</ref> On July 26, 2020, Pace was given a key to the city in [[Wikipedia:Cleveland, Tennessee|Cleveland, Tennessee]].<ref>{{Twitter|DominicPPace|status/1287513973420699653|DominicPPace|quote=Grateful to Nancy McCann of Comics Unlimited , along with the Mayor of Cleveland TN. Key to the City was such a humbling honor. Privileged to be a small ambassador to a franchise that gives so much happiness to so many.|archivedate=20200726222506}}</ref>

Pace is a lifelong ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan and described his time on set and in costume as one of the coolest experiences of his lifetime.<ref name="Your Money Geek">{{WebCite|url=https://yourmoneygeek.com/dominic-pace-star-wars-appearance|text=Actor Dominic Pace: Star Wars Fan Lands a Dream Appearance|author=Dinich, Michael|date=2019-11-11|archivedate=20200103161142}}</ref>
