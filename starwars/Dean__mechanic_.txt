'''Dean''' was a young Force-sensitive [[human]] [[Gender|male]] whose family owned a shop on [[Arkanis]]. As a [[Youngling|boy]], he worked as a [[Technician|mechanic]] for the [[Hutt]] [[Merchant|entrepenur]] [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]] on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] when the Hutt was attempting to develop a hotel within [[Fortress Vader]], the former domain of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] known as [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Dean was known to be plucky and courageous.<ref name="Tales" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dean appeared in the [[2021]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short film ''[[LEGO Star Wars Terrifying Tales]]''. He was voiced by [[Raphael Alejandro]].<ref name="Tales" />
