{{Quote|Forgery of Imperial documents, possession of stolen property, aggravated assault, resisting arrest.|A list of the crimes committed by [[Jyn Erso]], listed by [[General]] [[Davits Draven]]|<ref name="Rogue One: A Star Wars Story">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>}}
A '''forger''' was an individual who profited by forging and selling fake documents or [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. [[Gwellis Bagnoro]] was a [[Gender|male]] [[Onodone]] who specialized in forging transit documents.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

Before joining the [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens]], [[Rabé Tonsort|Rabene Tonsort]] had run a forgery ring at her school that created and sold forged artwork to unwary offworlders, a fact that [[Captain]] [[Quarsh Panaka]] attempted to hold over her head as a threat when he recruited her.<ref name="Queen's Peril">''[[Queen's Peril]]''</ref>

The [[Vanguard Axis]] was a criminal cartel that engaged in forgery as part of their [[Smuggler|smuggling]] business. [[Clone Force 99]] were hired to deliver [[chain code]]s for them.<ref name="Tribe">{{TBB|Tribe}}</ref>
