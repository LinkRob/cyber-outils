'''Euwood Gor''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] born on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] who served as one of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Pathfinders]] before leaving the Alliance while [[Onderon insurgency|fighting]] on the planet [[Onderon]], where he abandoned much and "went native," joining the [[Partisans]] movement of the rebel leader [[Saw Gerrera]].<ref name="ROUVG" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas" /> he and the rest of the Partisans were based on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] where he was part of a group that waited with the [[Tognath]] [[Benthic]] for delivery of the defected [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[pilot]] [[Bodhi Rook]]. The Partisans later captured a group including Gerrera's adoptive daughter [[Jyn Erso]] in [[Jedha City]]. Shortly after the capture, the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]] the [[city]], forcing the Partisan forces to evacuate their base in the nearby [[Catacombs of Cadera]] before it was destroyed by the blast.<ref name="RO" />

==Behind the scenes==
Euwood Gor first appeared in the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which was released in North America on [[December 16]], [[2016]]. He was not named in the film, but received identification in ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in conjunction with the film.
