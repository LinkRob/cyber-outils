'''Chertek''' was an opportunistic [[Geonosian]] who served as an [[ace]] [[pilot]] within the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="Strings" /> which lasted from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> At one point during the war, they flew a [[Nantex-class territorial defense starfighter|''Nantex''-class starfighter]] on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]] and attacked a Republic [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="Strings" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chertek's [[starfighter]] appeared as a card in the [[Nantex-class Starfighter Expansion Pack|''Nantex''-class Starfighter Expansion Pack]] for ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'', a ship-to-ship combat game by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Strings" /> The expansion pack was released on [[September 13]], [[2019]].<ref name="Available Now">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/9/13/available-now-september-13|text=Available Now &ndash; September 13}}</ref> Before the pack's release, Chertek's card was first seen, albeit obscured, in a news post on Fantasy Flight Games' [https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/ official website] announcing multiple expansion packs on [[April 19]], 2019.<ref name="Fate">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/4/13/the-fate-of-the-galaxy|text=The Fate of the Galaxy}}</ref> The card was later fully revealed in a post on the website previewing the expansion on [[August 13]] of the same year.<ref name="Strings" /> The art used on Chertek's card was designed by [[Robert Laskey]].<ref name="Laskey">{{WebCite|url=https://www.robertlaskey.com/droidsvehicles.html#|text=Droids/Vehicles|work=[[Robert Laskey]]'s [https://robertlaskey.com official website]|archivedate=20251212032238}}</ref>
