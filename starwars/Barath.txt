'''Barath''' was a [[human]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] who flew as part of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. As part of a [[squadron]] led by [[Captain]] [[Nosteen]], Barath took part in a [[Destruction of Nacronis|genocide]] on the [[planet]] [[Nacronis]]. While defending [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bombers]] from [[New Republic]] [[X-wing starfighter]]s, the [[pilot]] was [[Death|killed]] by a [[missile]]. Nosteen's second-in-[[Commanding officer|command]], [[Yrica Quell]], defected from the Empire after and lied to the New Republic that Barath had died trying to join her.

==Biography==
A [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]], Barath flew with the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] as a part of a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] [[squadron]] led by [[Captain]] [[Nosteen]]. Two [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor]] and the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]<ref name="Alphabet" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the 204th was tasked with the [[Destruction of Nacronis|destruction]] of all life on the [[planet]] [[Nacronis]] as a part of the [[Emperor]]'s [[contingency]] plan [[Operation: Cinder]]. Barath and the rest of the [[pilot]]'s squadron was assigned to protect [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bombers]] carrying [[vortex detonator]]s. However, as enemy [[X-wing starfighter]]s engaged the [[TIE series|TIE]] squadron, Barath was killed by a surface-to-air [[missile]].<ref name="Alphabet" />

The vortex detonators successfully devastated Nacronis by stoking [[siltstorm]]s. Following the genocide, [[Lieutenant]] [[Yrica Quell]], Nosteen's second-in-[[Commanding officer|command]], defected to the [[New Republic]] and told one of it's [[IT-O|interrogation droids]] an alternate story of her defection where she, Barath and the pilot [[Tonas]] defected together but Barath and Tonas were killed in the process.<ref name="Alphabet" /> In [[11 ABY]],<ref name="11 ABY">The section of ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' where [[Yrica Quell]] thinks about Barath is set 6 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Galactic Civil War]], which ends with the [[Battle of Jakku]] in [[5 ABY]], according to ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' respectively. The section must therefore be set in [[11 ABY]].</ref> Quell thought about Barath when she flew a [[Helotek Loadhauler]] [[shuttle]].<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Barath was first mentioned in the [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], the first volume in the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' series, written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Alphabet" />
