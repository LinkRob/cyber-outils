The '''Corporate Sector''', also known as '''CorpSec''',<ref name="BMF 18">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> was a [[sector]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]] that was governed by the independent [[Corporate Sector Authority]]. It was described as a "fiefdom" where corporate entities were given free rein to govern their own territory.<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==History==
===Fall of the Republic===
Despite being shut down, the same organizations and market forces which created and destroyed the [[Expansionist Oligarchy]] resulted in the foundation of the Corporate Sector some two centuries later. Compromises between the Republic and these corporate interests fueled the division in the [[Galactic Republic]] and its [[Galactic Senate]], pushing the Senate away from the interests of the citizens, and leaned more towards the interests of the companies.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> [[LeisureMech Enterprises]] was based within the Corporate Sector.<ref name="Protocol and Service Droids">{{EncyclopediaCite|Protocol and Service Droids}}</ref> Notable corporations with membership inside the Corporate Sector included the [[Trade Federation]], [[Tagge Corporation]], the [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation]], [[Kuat Drive Yards]], [[Chiewab Amalgamated Pharmaceuticals]] and [[Cybot Galactica]].<ref name="SWTATVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> Even if it was not yet a single, unified region,<ref name="Catalyst" /> a form of governing body and security enforcement known as the [[Corporate Sector Authority]] (CSA)<ref name="SWTATVG" /> had come into existence by [[132 BBY]].<ref name="AcolyteVGDate">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' establishes that the Corporate Sector Authority existed by the events of ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which occur in [[132 BBY]] per the reasoning [[TheAcolyteDate|here]].</ref>

Prior to the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], the Corporate Sector was made up of a series of loosely-aligned autonomous [[star system]]s, independent from the Galactic Republic, which were home to numerous corporations. Furthermore, in the [[Salient system]], tax evaders, arms merchants, and [[pirate]]s called at least the edge of the sector home.<ref name="Catalyst" /> Although they largely supported the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="BMF 18" /> the majority of these systems remained neutral during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Catalyst" /> of [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> It also gaining a reputation for harboring war profiteers.<ref name="Catalyst" /> The war ended with the fall of the CIS, but the Corporate Sector remained a hotbed for Separatist sentiments, with hardline [[Separatist holdouts]] finding a home in the region.<ref name="Tarkin" />

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|Salient's resources, in whatever state we inherit them, are trivial against the necessity of sending a message to other systems in this sector that autonomy is at the pleasure of the Empire, and that that privilege can be revoked whenever the Emperor sees fit.|Moff Wilhuff Tarkin|<ref name="Catalyst" />}}
[[File:CorporateSector-BMF18.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The Corporate Sector]]
By [[18 BBY]], these star systems were considered to be slowly coalescing into a unified entity. Fearful of this opposition, [[Imperial Military|forces]] of the newly-formed [[Galactic Empire]] under [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] [[Salient Campaign|invaded]] and occupied the [[Salient system]] on the edge of the sector, intending to send a message to defiant systems and gain a staging area for further military incursions into Corporate space. The prolonged campaign drew in [[smuggler]]s and [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] from across the sector as well as from other [[sector]]s as far away as the [[Tingel Arm]], including [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]], but the Empire eventually proved victorious.<ref name="Catalyst" /> Shortly after the end of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] led a [[Campaign in the Corporate Sector|campaign]] against the [[pirate]]s who were launching raids into the Corporate Sector.<ref name="BMF 18" />

Despite this, the Corporate Sector would remain in existence, and its systems operated under the CSA.<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> The Empire viewed it as a haven for former Separatists. Indeed, even five [[Standard year|years]] after the end of the war, technologies that had previously belonged to the former Confederacy as well as the [[Trade Federation]] were still appearing in the sector. Moff Tarkin suspected that the [[Berch Teller's warship|warship]] that [[Attack on Sentinel Base|attacked]] [[Sentinel Base]] originated in the Corporate Sector. When the Moff later traveled to [[Coruscant]], some speculated he was going to be sent on a mission to the sector to hunt down Separatist recidivists. [[Berch Teller]], the leader of the [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] that was responsible for the ensuing [[Berch Teller campaign|series of attacks]], suggested his fellow rebels flee to the Corporate Sector to avoid Imperial capture.<ref name="Tarkin" />

===New Republic Era===
During the [[New Republic Era]], the Corporate Sector was still controlled by the Corporate Sector Authority. This bought the attention of the [[First Order]], who used it as a way to bribe [[New Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Erudo Ro-Kiintor]], who received credits through shells and third-party corporations with the connivance of the CSA.<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> It served as a haven for war profiteers who sold munitions to both sides in the [[Cold War]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary" />

===Rise of the First Order===
[[File:Cantonica_system.png|thumb|right|300px|The Cantonica System, in the Corporate Sector, during the First Order-Resistance War.]]
During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the Corporate Sector world of [[Cantonica]] was a destination for wealthy tourists and war profiteers, who visited the casinos and racing tracks of [[Canto Bight]]. Following the [[Battle of D'Qar|Evacuation of D'Qar]], the [[Resistance]] operatives [[Finn]], [[Rose Tico]], and [[BB-8]] traveled there on a [[Mission to Canto Bight|mission]] to recruit the [[Master Codebreaker]] to disable the First Order's [[hyperspace tracker]]. Though the mission went awry, Finn and Rose managed to recruit the [[slicer]] [[DJ]], free several [[fathier]]s, and inspire hope in several stable hands including [[Temiri Blagg]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Corporate Sector was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> It was originally created for ''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]'', a [[1979]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel written by [[Brian Daley]].<ref name="Han Solo at Stars' End">''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]''</ref>
