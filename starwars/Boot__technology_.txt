'''Boots''' were a type of device used by the [[Galactic Empire]] in the early [[Imperial Era]] to lock down impounded [[starship]]s. They incorporated [[magnetic clamp]]s and included a socket which could be interfaced with by a [[scomp link]]. Despite the locks, boots could be removed through sufficient application of brute force. The Empire used them to secure seized spacecraft on [[Saleucami]] in the [[Standard day|days]] after its [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]].<ref name="Cut and Run" />
