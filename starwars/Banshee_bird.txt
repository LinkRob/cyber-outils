The '''banshee bird''' was a type of [[Limb|winged]] [[Bird|avian]]. A [[brass]] banshee bird with its wings outstretched was mounted on the cross section of the [[Corellian Cross]], a [[medal]] made to commemorate the contributions of the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] that was awarded as the Alliance's second highest honor.<ref name="Lead" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Corellian_cross.png|thumb|right|The Legends Corellian Cross]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the banshee bird was mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]] for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Lead" /> The [[Banshee bird/Legends|Corellian banshee bird]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2001]] novel ''[[Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter]]'', which was written by [[Michael Reaves]].<ref name="ShadowHunter">''[[Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter]]''</ref> ''Lead by Example'' then [[Retroactive continuity|retconned]] the bird depicted on the [[Corellian Cross/Legends|Corellian Cross]],<ref name="Lead" /> which in Legends first appeared in the [[1993]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']],<ref name="X-Wing">[[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']]</ref> into being a banshee bird.<ref name="Lead" />
