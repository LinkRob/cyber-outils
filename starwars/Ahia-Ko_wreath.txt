An '''Ahia-Ko wreath''' was a traditional [[Flower|floral]] wreath used to celebrate [[spring]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. It shared its name with the [[Ahia-Ko]], a civilization that lived on Akiva<ref name="Outlaws" /> long before [[3 ABY]]. By that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[human]] [[merchant]] [[Berek Keehro]] had at least one Ahia-Ko wreath while operating a [[Sirei's Stall|shop]] in [[Mirogana]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the Outer Rim [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] traded four trinkets that [[Nix]], her [[merqaal]] companion, found on Akiva to Keehro in exchange for an Ahia-Ko wreath.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
