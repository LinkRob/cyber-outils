'''Dorzek Senn''' was an [[alien]] who hosted a broadcast of a [[droid]] battle from the settlement of [[Galma]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. Senn co-hosted the broadcast with [[Blom Groffitch]] and [[Haz Bakkel]], and it was played on radio station [[BSO 401.72]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on Batuu during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="GEStaff" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dorzek Senn was first created for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at Disneyland Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="GEStaff" />
