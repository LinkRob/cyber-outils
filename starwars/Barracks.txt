A '''barracks''' was a type of building that was dedicated to housing [[soldier]]s. Certain [[starship]]s, like the [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigate]], were equipped with barracks for any embarked [[troop]]s.<ref name="Brain Invaders">{{TCW|Brain Invaders}}</ref>

The first [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] was known to have communal barracks, but there were enough private bunks that most people could expect to receive one within three to six [[Standard month|months]] of arrival.<ref name="LS">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
