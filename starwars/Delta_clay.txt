'''Delta clay''' was a type of sticky [[clay]] found on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. Around five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Mandalorian]] [[artist]] and demolitions expert [[Sabine Wren]] came to possess a pile of delta clay, which she kept in the corner of her cabin on board the [[starship]] ''[[Ghost]]'' while trying to decide what to use it for. Wren managed to get some of the clay on her [[nose]] and cheeks, where it dried and remained for two [[Standard day|days]]. Wren wrote about the clay in her [[Sabine Wren's journal|journal]], where she claimed to love having the dried clay on her face.<ref name="SMRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Delta clay was first mentioned in ''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and published in [[2015]].
