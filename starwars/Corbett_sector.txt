==Behind the scenes==
The comic story "[[Droid Dilemma]]" placed [[Reamma]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Droid Dilemma">{{StoryCite|story=Droid Dilemma|book=Star Wars (2015) 25|reprint=1}}</ref> but the [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix of [[star system]]s placed the [[Reamma system]] in the Corbett sector. While the appendix did list Outer Rim for the system, it is the only system in the Corbett sector to do so, as all others are listed as Mid Rim<ref name="Appendix" /> consistent with the [[Corbett sector/Legends|Corbett sector]]'s original definition in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as being located on the border of the Mid and Outer Rims.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> The [[October 13]] iteration of the appendix corrected this error.<ref name="Substack 2">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref>
