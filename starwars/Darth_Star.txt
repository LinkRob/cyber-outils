The '''Darth Star''' was a massive [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Battle station/Legends|battle station]]/[[Superweapon/Legends|superweapon]] which was built by the Empire shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]] to replace the original [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]] destroyed over [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]]. Envisioned by [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]], who bought the necessary materials for its construction, it was built and named by the [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]], who named it after the [[Darth/Legends|Darth]] concept of the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]]. Both Palpatine and Maul used [[The Force/Legends|The Force]] to build the station, though Palpatine just did some work before letting Maul to finish the rest. This proved wrong for Palpatine, as Maul set aside the manual to build the new [[Death/Legends|Death]] Star at his whim, modeling it to resemble his face, with his mouth being where the [[Superlaser/Legends|superlaser]] was placed. In response, Palpatine was furiously reprimanded Maul for revising the appearance of the superlaser. However, the weapon never saw use in the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]] as it was accidentally destroyed by [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] while trying to escape into [[Hyperspace/Legends|hyperspace]] from [[Gender/Legends|his]] [[Luke Skywalker's fangirls|fangirls]].<ref name="TESO" />

==Description==
{{Quote|Now, it's a Darth Star.''"<br />"''No, no, no you Maul-head. I can't terrorize the galaxy with that. I would be the laughing-stock of the Empire.|Darth Maul and Emperor Palpatine|<ref name="TESO" />}}
The Darth Star was assembled by Darth Maul and the Emperor using the Force. Although intended by Palpatine to be similar in design to the first Death Star, Maul instead remade the weapon in his own image.<ref name="TESO" />
