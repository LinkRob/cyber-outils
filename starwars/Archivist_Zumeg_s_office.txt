'''Archivist Zumeg's office'''<ref name="The High Republic 4">[[The High Republic (2022) 4|''The High Republic'' (2022) 4]]</ref> was a room in the [[Temple of the Kyber]] in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] on [[Jedha]]<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> that was the office of [[Archivist]] [[Zumeg]].<ref name="The High Republic 4" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[The High Republic (2022) 3|''The High Republic'' (2022) 3]] take place between [[Vildar Mac]]'s arrival on [[Jedha]] and the [[Battle of Jedha]]. As ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates both events to [[382 BBY]], ''The High Republic'' 3 must take place in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> archivists Zumeg and [[Feric Oranalli]] visited the office along with the [[Jedi]] [[Vildar Mac]] and [[Matthea Cathley]] to check the logs of the temple's [[security camera]]s for unauthorized visitors to the temple, which they hoped would tell them the identity of who had stoledn the [[Firebird of Colsassa]] from the temple. At the same [[time]], the former [[Guardians of the Whills|Guardian of the Whill]] traveled to the temple with the same idea of checking the logs, though when he arrived at the office, he discovered a [[bomb]] outside the office. As he picked up the explosive, Mac had sensed someone was present and turned to find Sirrek with the explosive. Mac then [[Force push|pushed]] the bomb from Sirrek's hand,<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> which caused it to [[Bombing at the Temple of the Kyber|explode]] and tear a hole in the wall of the temple. After the detonation of the bomb, Mac [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to protect the two archivists from the explosion, though Zumeg later [[Death|passed away]], and Mac suffered injuries.<ref name="The High Republic 4" />
