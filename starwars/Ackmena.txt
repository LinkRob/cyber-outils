'''Ackmena''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who worked as the nighttime [[bartender]] in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in the [[Tatooine]] [[spaceport]] of [[Mos Eisley]]. She operated a "side hustle" with two local [[Thief|pickpockets]], [[Kabe]] and [[Muftak]].

==Biography==
Ackmena, a [[human]]<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> [[Gender|female]], lived on the [[Delkin Ridge]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Tatooine]], along with her wife, [[Sorschi]].<ref name="We Don't Serve Their Kind Here" /> During the night, she worked as [[bartender]] in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]],<ref name="CL 2016" /> a [[cantina]] in the [[spaceport]] of [[Mos Eisley]] owned by the [[Wookiee]] [[Chalmun]].<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> She shared this job with [[Wuher]], who tended the bar during the daytime.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> However, she possessed a friendly manner, and this made her more popular than Wuher.<ref name="CVD" />

As part of her own "side hustle," Ackmena charged "rent" to [[Kabe]] and [[Muftak]], two [[Thief|pickpockets]] that lived in the cavernous stone pipeways of the cantina's underground and that often made their earnings off inebriated regulars of the bar. She would forward the pay to Chalmun, the proprietor, while keeping some of the proceeds for herself.<ref name="kloohorn">{{StoryCite|story=The Kloo Horn Cantina Caper|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

[[File:Ackmena_and_Chalmun.png|thumb|left|250px|Ackmena in Chalmun's office]]
Despite their racket, the [[Talz]] and [[Chadra-Fan]] duo often fell behind on their payments, eliciting sympathy from Ackmena but also the threat that Chalmun would take the opportunity of removing the "infestation in his pipes." This happened on one [[Standard day|day]] in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="ANH Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> and Ackmena warned the duo that she would need the money by that night, hinting at the consequences.<ref name="kloohorn" /> That same day, Ackmena was present in Chalmun's office, requesting better working [[Standard hour|hours]].<ref name="CL 2016" /> Later that night, after taking over the bar from Wuher, she received the money from Kabe and Muftak, who had sold the [[kloo horn]] of [[Lirin Car'n]], a member of the [[Figrin D'an and the Modal Nodes|in-house cantina band]], and then proceeded to serve Muftak several drinks with his remaining earnings until finally cutting him off.<ref name="We Don't Serve Their Kind Here" /><ref name="kloohorn" />

==Personality and traits==
Ackmena was respected by many, even by the gruff and unapproachable Wuher, as a "tough old broad."<ref name="We Don't Serve Their Kind Here" /> However, even when confronted with Kabe and Muftak's bankruptcy, she seemed sympathetic to their plight, despite the unspoken knowledge that Chalmun would be very unhappy, and allowed them a day of respite for them to accrue the money to pay off their debt.<ref name="kloohorn" /> She had a friendly manner, and this made her more popular than Wuher.<ref name="CVD" /> Ackmena was attracted to women, and took a wife named [[Sorschi]], with whom she lived with out on the [[Delkin Ridge]].<ref name="We Don't Serve Their Kind Here" />

==Behind the scenes==
The character of Ackmena first appeared in [[1978]], during ''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]'', where she was played by [[Bea Arthur]].<ref name="TSWHS">''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]''</ref> Her first mention in [[canon]] was in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']],<ref name="CL 2016" /> and she eventually made an appearance in the short story "[[The Kloo Horn Cantina Caper]]" from the [[2017]] short-story anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''.<ref name="kloohorn" />
