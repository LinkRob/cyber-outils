The '''Climbsuit''' was a type of lightweight [[armor]] manufactured by [[Ayelixe/Krongbing Textiles]] for climbing and rappelling. It cost 1,450 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and was built from light, flexible fabrics, and had impact-absorbing reinforcements at its important areas. The suit was composed of a pair of [[glove]]s, [[boot]]s, and a [[helmet]] with a built-in polarized visor. It was equipped with a built-in climbing harness, load-bearing straps wrapped around the waist and [[Limb|legs]], equipment buckles, and a 300-[[meter]] retractable spool of high-tensile climbing line. The suit was coated in [[FrictionGrip]], a special coating composed of thousands of micro­scopic filaments designed to grip surfaces in a manner similar to [[Spider|arachnids]]. All of the armor's systems combined to help the user stay firmly attached to a surface, or to help them survive a fall.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />
