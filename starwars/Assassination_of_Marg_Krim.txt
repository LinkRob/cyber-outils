During the early [[Imperial Era]], the former [[Sith Lord]] turned [[crime lord]], [[Maul]], assassinated Illustrious Imperator [[Marg Krim]] of the [[Pyke Syndicate]] in the [[Pyke Palace]] on [[Oba Diah]], in revenge for [[Attempt to assassinate Maul|betraying him]] and his [[Shadow Collective]] crime [[syndicate]] at the end of the [[Clone Wars]]. 

Maul had [[Raid on the Janix Civil Defense HQ|kidnapped]] another betrayer of his on [[Janix]], [[Looti Vario]], and used the [[Aleena]]'s [[Vario's syndicate|syndicate]]'s connections to the Pykes to disrupt their operations, then had Vario offer to personally deliver restitution to Krim. When Vario arrived on the Pyke's [[homeworld]], he was met by a squad of Pykes lead by [[Captain of the Guard]] [[Kalt]]. The Aleena nervoursly tried to reveal Maul's presence, only for a hooded [[Zabrak]] to emerge from the [[starship]] and begin slaying the Pykes with a red-bladed [[lightsaber]]. Vario used a [[pistol]] to [[stun]] the attacker, and Kalt and the remaining guards escorted the pair to see Krim in the Pyke stronghold's throne room. 

There, the crime lord admonished Vario for thinking he was unaware of Maul's return on Janix, and took [[Maul's lightsaber]] from Kalt before pulling the hood off the captive Zabrak. Doing so revealed the assailant to be [[Icarus]], a [[Nightbrothers|Nightbrother]] underling of Maul's, who at that moment entered the throne room and used [[the Force]] to activate his saber, telekinetically slashing it across Krim's chest. As the crime lord collapsed, [[death|dead]], Maul cut his way through the Pyke guards, pausing when only three, including Kalt, remained. They surrendered, and Maul lectured Krim's corpse for betraying him, before appointing Kalt as the late Pyke's successor on the condition of his loyalty. With his vengeance achieved and control over the Pyke Syndicate reasserted, Maul returned to his base of operations on Janix.

==Prelude==
===Betrayal===
{{Main|Attempt to assassinate Maul}}
{{Quote|I was alone and weak after the tragic events on Mandalore, and I came here to you, my old ally, seeking safe haven. But, you tried to kill me. You failed.|Maul, to Marg Krim's corpse|<ref name="Chapter 4" />}}
[[File:TogetherMargKrimFifeDebate.png|thumb|right|300px|Marg Krim was a syndicate leader in the Shadow Collective but grew annoyed with the limitations and threats that came with life under its rule.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Maul]], a former [[Sith Lord]]-turned-[[crime lord]], bought [[the galaxy]]'s most powerful criminal syndicates, including the [[Spice]] [[Spice dealer|dealing]] [[Pyke Syndicate]], together into an organization called the [[Shadow Collective]]. When [[syndicate]] leader [[Lom Pyke]], who had joined up willingly,<ref name="EM">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref> was [[Mission to Oba Diah (Clone Wars)|killed]],<ref name="TLO">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref> Illustrious Imperator [[Marg Krim]] replaced him.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> Krim was less enthusiastic about the partnership, but remained in it for fear of Maul.<ref name="Together Again">{{TCW|Together Again}}</ref> At the end of the war, Maul [[Siege of Mandalore|suffered a significant defeat]] on the planet [[Mandalore]], ending with his capture by the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> as the Shadow Collective crumbled.<ref name="BH14">[[Bounty Hunters 14|''Bounty Hunters'' 14]]</ref>

Before his capture, Maul issued a command to Krim and his other allies in positions of leadership among his syndicates that they were to go into hiding.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref> During passage to the Republic capital world of [[Coruscant]], however, [[Order 66]] was issued and the Republic [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] into the [[Galactic Empire]], allowing Maul to [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|escape in the chaos]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Alone and weak, Maul sought refuge on the Pyke's world of [[Oba Diah]], only for Krim to unsuccessfully try to kill him. Krim's [[attempt to assassinate Maul]], being one of a number of betrayals by syndicates previously loyal to Maul, prompted the [[dark side adept]] to [[Campaign to rebuild the Shadow Collective|seek revenge]] during the early [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Chapter 1">{{MSL|Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge}}</ref>

===Sinister schemes===
{{Quote|What about the Pykes, huh? If you're after me, you've gotta be after them! I know how to get to Krim!''"<br/>"''Take him alive... for now.|Vario bargains for his life from Maul|<ref name="Chapter 1" />}}
Maul reestablished himself, bolstered by a small contingent of loyal [[Mandalorian super commando]]s, lead by [[Rook Kast]], a [[spy droid]] named [[Spybot]], and the [[Nightbrothers]] [[Icarus]] and [[Scorn]], on the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet|world]] of [[Janix]].<ref name="SoM2">[[Shadow of Maul 2|''Shadow of Maul'' 2]]</ref> There, he orchestrated the demise of one of the local crime lords who had betrayed him, [[Nico Deemis]], and planned a [[raid on the Janix Civil Defense HQ]] to kill another betrayer who was imprisoned there, Looti Vario. However, when Maul came to slay the Aleena, Vario bargained for his life by offering to facilitate access to Marg Krim, who he knew the Zabrak would also be seeking revenge against.<ref name="Chapter 1" />

[[File:Vario and Krim.png|thumb|left|350px|Vario contacts Krim via hologram, offering restitution for the Pyke Syndicate's lost cargo]]
Maul kidnapped Vario and forced him to reveal details about his syndicate's dealings with the Pykes. Using that information, Maul was able to [[Ambush on Janix's moon|intercept a Pyke Syndicate ship]] before it could jump into hyperspace, and slaughter all the crew, as his [[Mandalorian]]s took the cargo of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] blocks.<ref name="Chapter 2">{{MSL|Chapter 2: Sinister Schemes}}</ref> The Shadow Collective later forced Vario to give up the location of the Pyke's next shipment, [[Attack on a Pyke Syndicate shipment|ambushing]] them at the [[Janix Spaceport]]. The Pykes then sought to contact Vario via [[hologram]], who, under duress from the Shadow Collective, blamed the disruption to business on lieutenants loyal to Deemis. He claimed that these rogue elements and their former boss had been dealt with, and offered to make amends by personally delivering the lost payments to Krim plus the value of the lost cargo.<ref name="Chapter 3" />

==The assassination==
===A stunning turn of events===
{{Quote|Uh...Um...I didn't have a choice.''"<br/>"''What are you saying?''"<br/>"''He's here!|Looti Vario tries to warn Kalt of Maul's presence|<ref name="Chapter 4" />}}
[[File:Vario warns Kalt.png|thumb|right|350px|Vario tries to warn Kalt of Maul's presence]]
Looti Vario traveled in a [[starship]] to the Pyke [[homeworld]] of Oba Diah to deliver the restitution. When he approached the planet, he spoke over the intercom, identifying himself, and was promptly given permission to land. As the ship approached the [[Pyke Palace]], Vario concealed a small [[blaster]] pistol on his person. When the starship landed, two of [[Vario's guard droid]]s exited the boarding ramp with crates full of credits, and Kalt, the Pyke Captain of the Guard, approached with a contingent of armed Pykes. He ordered his men to count the money, and Vario nervously tried to warn them that Maul was there.<ref name="Chapter 4" />

Suspicious, Kalt ordered the ship checked out, only for a hooded Zabrak to suddenly exit it, and begin cutting down the Pyke enforcers and two of the [[droid]]s with a red-bladed [[lightsaber]]. Vario, hiding behind one of the crates, worked up the courage to shoot the assailant, stunning them. He proudly declared to Kalt to tell his master Marg Krim that he was the one who had captured "Maul." The Pykes then escorted Vario and their unconscious captive to the throne room of the Pyke stronghold, when Krim awaited.<ref name="Chapter 4" />

===Krim killed===
{{Quote|That's not Maul! Aaaaarh...|Marg Krim realises he's been fooled, before being killed by the real Maul|<ref name="Chapter 4" />}}
[[File:Kalt surrenders to Maul.png|thumb|left|300px|Kalt and the remaining Pyke guards surrender to Maul, the corpses of their slain comrades littering the throne room hall behind them]]
In the throne room, the Pyke crime lord admonished Vario for thinking he was unaware of Maul's return and demanded that the Aleena explain himself. Vario explained that he had been taken prisoner by Maul and forced him to do his bidding, asserting that he had, however, taken care of their mutual threatener. Kalt handed Krim the assailant's lightsaber, and the Pyke leader ordered the Zabrak prisoner brought forward, in order to inspect him. Pulling back the captive's hood, Krim realized that it was not Maul, but one of his Zabrak underlings.<ref name="Chapter 4" />

As he made the revelation, the throne room's doors whooshed open, leaving the room's occupants to look up and come face-to-face with the real Maul. The former Sith used [[the force]] to ignite [[Maul's lightsaber|his lightaber]] in Krim's hand, slashing it across the crime lord's chest and killing him. He then telekinetically summoned the saber and advanced though the ranks of guards, slaying them and reflecting the blaster bolts they fired at him in vain. Within [[Standard second|seconds]], very few Pykes remained alive. After slashing another Pyke, Maul paused, allowing the remaining three, including Kalt, to exchange a look and drop their weapons, submitting to his rule.<ref name="Chapter 4" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|You are the Captain of the Guard?''"<br/>"''Uh... yes, my Lord.''"<br/>"''What is your name?''"<br/>"''Kalt, Lord Maul.''"<br/>"''You are in charge now, Kalt. But you answer to me.|Maul and Kalt|<ref name="Chapter 4" />}}
[[File:Maul laments Krims betrayal.png|thumb|right|350px|Maul laments Krim's betrayal of him]]
With the remaining three Pykes having surrendered, Maul knelt over the corpse of his former ally, and admonished him for the attempted betrayal after the events of Mandalore. He then stood, declaring that Krim's fate was what awaited all those who betrayed him. Maul singled out Kalt, confirming his occupation and name, before appointing the obeisant Pyke as Krim's successor. The vengeful crime lord then gestured to the vacant throne and warned Kalt that his new post was contingent upon his loyalty.<ref name="Chapter 4" /> Upon their return to Janix, Maul granted Vario his request to be freed, accepting his reasoning that his side of the deal to deliver Krim had been upheld. However, the recent arrival of the Galactic Empire prompted the Aleena to remain with the Shadow Collective in [[Maul's new safe house]] deep below [[Janix city]] anyway.<ref name="Chapter 5">{{MSL|Chapter 5: Inquisition}}</ref>
