The '''ninth episode''' of the animated web series ''[[Grogu Cutest In The Galaxy]]'' released on [[May 20]], [[2025]]. It was posted by ''[[Star Wars]]'' Japan on [[Wikipedia:Instagram Reels|Instagram Reels]],<ref name="Instagram" /> and later on [[Wikipedia:Facebook|Facebook]].<ref name="Facebook">{{Facebook|username=StarWars|url=reel/1399205851197873|name=[[Star Wars]]|quote=The cutest and hungriest in the galaxy 🪐|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/Cze60}}</ref>

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
[[Din Grogu|Grogu]], while sitting on a rock with his eyes closed, spots an incoming [[Arges frog]]. He then uses [[the Force]] to catch the frog while it leapt, and has the creature swirl around mid-air. [[Luke Skywalker]] says, "Grogu," prompting the baby to drop the frog, who escapes, to Grogu's chagrin.
