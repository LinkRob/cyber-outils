'''Dark yellow blood''' was a substance rumored to grant [[immortality]]. This was an exaggeration, but it could prolong life. It was manufactured by extracting it from the [[Loongren]] [[Species]]. [[Xia Moist Pharmaceuticals]] was its manufacturer. It was used by the [[Aakaash system]]'s elite and even exported for the use of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s elite.<ref name="Vow">''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dark yellow blood was introduced in the Chinese webnovel ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' as "'''玄黄血'''."<ref name="Vow" /> Its English translation was confirmed by the [[2023]] reference title ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
