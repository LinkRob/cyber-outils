The '''Chiss heavy cruiser''',<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> also known as the '''Chiss warship''',<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> was a class of [[heavy cruiser]]<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> utilized by the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet|Expansionary Defense Fleet]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] that when uncloaked glistened with lights and was covered in weaponry.<ref name="Thrawn" /> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] noticed that it was not as big as a [[Star Destroyer]], although after seeing its flankside weaponry he believed it would be a formidable opponent.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> One such [[starship]], the [[Steadfast (Chiss heavy cruiser)|''Steadfast'']]<ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref> was used by [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] to rendezvous with [[Commander]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] of the [[Imperial Navy|naval branch]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] in the aftermath of the [[Batonn insurgency]],<ref name="Thrawn" /> and was again used to gather and return kidnapped [[Force-sensitive]] [[Gender|female]] Chiss children after a [[Battle against Grysk forces|battle]] between the [[Grysk]] and the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chiss warship first appeared, albeit unnamed, in the [[2017]] [[canon]] novel by [[Timothy Zahn]], [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']],<ref name="Thrawn" /> and was later pictured in [[Thrawn 6|''Thrawn'' 6]], the sixth part of a comic adaptation by [[Jody Houser]] of the novel that was released in [[2018]], with illustrations by [[Luke Ross]].<ref name="Thrawn VI">[[Thrawn 6|''Thrawn'' 6]]</ref> Chiss heavy cruisers were first identified in Zahn's [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''.<ref name="Greater Good" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, Chiss heavy cruisers were known as "[[Cruiser/Legends|Chiss assault cruisers]]"<ref name="Dark Nest 3">''[[Dark Nest III: The Swarm War]]''</ref> or "Chiss attack cruisers"<ref name="Outbound Flight">[[Outbound Flight (novel)|''Outbound Flight'']]</ref> and first appeared in [[Troy Denning]]'s [[2005]] novel ''[[Dark Nest III: The Swarm War]]''.<ref name="Dark Nest 3 />
