An '''artisan''' was a skilled craftsperson who made quality, beautiful objects of various kinds, such as musical instruments or chests.<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> Some [[time]] after its initial discovery, the [[Jedi Order]] dispatched artisans to the [[planet]] [[Ilum]] to secure it for themselves.<ref name="NoP">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>
