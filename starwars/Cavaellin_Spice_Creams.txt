'''Cavaellin Spice Creams''', also known as '''Cavaellin creams''', were [[spice]]d sandwich [[cookie]]s that consisted of a pair of [[Color|brown]]-colored cookies that had a brightly-hued [[fruit]]-and-[[cream]] filling between them. The cookies [[Homeworld|originated]] in the [[Cavaell system]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

The [[Artiodac]] [[Strono Tuggs]] formerly worked as head [[chef]] for [[Maz Kanata]],<ref name="Cookbook" /> who operated a [[Cantina|tavern]] out of her [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]].<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Around [[146 BBY]],<ref name="RecipeDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[A Recipe for Death]]" to around [[146 BBY]].</ref> Tuggs' kitchen crew included many [[droid]]s, some of which contained recipe databases that he utilized; he acquired a recipe for Cavaellin creams from an old [[434 unit]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Cookbook date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> [[Standard century|centuries]] later and after the castle's [[Battle of Takodana|destruction]],<ref name="RecipeDate" /> Tuggs published the recipe in his [[Book|cookbook]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''. His description of the cookies expressed his appreciation of their spicy zing.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cavaellin Spice Creams were introduced to the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] ingredients for the cookies are [[Wikipedia:Butter|butter]], [[Wikipedia:Sucrose|granulated sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Brown sugar|brown sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Egg as food|egg]], [[Wikipedia:Molasses|molasses]], [[Wikipedia:Sodium bicarbonate|baking soda]], [[Wikipedia:Cinnamon|ground cinnamon]], [[Wikipedia:Ginger|ground ginger]], [[Wikipedia:Five-spice powder|five-spice powder]], and [[Wikipedia:Flour|all-purpose flour]]. The filling is made with [[Wikipedia:Cream cheese|cream cheese]], [[Wikipedia:Opuntia|desert pear]] syrup or [[Wikipedia:Orange (fruit)|orange]] extract, [[Wikipedia:Powdered sugar|powdered sugar]], and [[Wikipedia:Cream|heavy cream]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

[[Cavaellin Creams/Legends|Cavaellin Creams]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in ''[[Galladinium's Fantastic Technology]]'', a [[1995]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplement to ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Galladinium's Fantastic Technology">''[[Galladinium's Fantastic Technology]]''</ref> In that continuity, however, they were depicted as a [[Candy/Legends|candy]] rather than a [[Cookie/Legends|cookie]] when they first appeared in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']].<ref name="SWG">{{SWGcite|exp=AED|type=item|Cavaellin Creams}}</ref>
