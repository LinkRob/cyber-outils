'''Endente''' was a [[Sugi]] [[arms dealer]] who at some point made a deal with [[Governor]] [[Torul Blom]] and [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to provide the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] with a giant [[kyber crystal]].

==Biography==
Endente was the leader of a [[Endente's arms dealing group|group]] of [[Sugi]] [[arms dealer]]s during the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[19 BBY]], he negotiated with [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]], [[Head of State|leader]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], to sell him a large [[kyber crystal]].<ref name="In Search of the Crystal" /> He had also allied himself with the corrupt [[Governor]] [[Torul Blom]] by the time of his attempts to sell the prized possession.<ref name="Crisis" />

Endente and his right-hand, [[Chong]], later captured [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] and prepared to deliver the crystal to their Separatist clients in [[Pau City]]. [[Count]] Dooku also made it clear that if the arms dealer failed, he would not only lose his money but his life as well. Endente assured the impatient [[Sith Lord]] that the merchandise would be delivered on schedule, to which Dooku ominously promised to give him what he "deserves". The greedy Sugi assumed that the [[Serennian]] count would pay him triple.

However, Endente ultimately lost the [[transport]] with the crystal which was captured by Kenobi and Skywalker, but still got to the [[city]] on his speeder. Shortly after arriving, he proceeded to Governor Blom's office. As he was about to speak Endente realized that he and the [[Pau'an]] were not alone and saw Count Dooku's [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|enforcer]], [[General]] [[Grievous]]. The [[cyborg]] [[warlord]] was just as displeased as his master and asked the arms dealer where the crystal was. The Sugi attempted to cover up his failure by claiming that the merchandise would be delivered on time. Grievous sarcastically asked again if the [[Jedi]] were also part of the plan after which he revealed that he knew that Endente had lost the crystal. The [[droid]] general then stated that he had no more business to do with him. Grievous grabbed the fearful arms dealer and [[Death|decapitated]] him, after which he threw Endente's body to the other side of the room. Grievous then went to deal with the two Jedi himself.<ref name="Crisis" />

==Behind the scenes==
Endente first appeared in an unfinished episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', "[[In Search of the Crystal]]," and was voiced by [[James Hong]], who also voiced [[Azmorigan]] in ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.
