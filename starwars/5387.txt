'''5387''' was the designation of a [[stormtrooper]] who served as part of a [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|remnant]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]], being stationed at a [[Nevarro Imperial base|base]] on the [[Volcano|volcanic]] [[planet]] [[Nevarro]].<ref name="Chapter 12" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|12|the appearance of 5387}}</ref> a group of infiltrators [[Second skirmish on Nevarro|sabotaged]] the base's cooling system, which prevented the control of Nevarro's [[Magma|lava]]. 5387 and fellow stormtrooper [[5389]] were called upon by one of their stormtrooper comrades to follow the [[trooper]]. The pair followed, ignorant to the fact that the infiltrators were watching from afar.<ref name="Chapter 12" />

==Behind the scenes==
5387 appeared in "[[Chapter 12: The Siege]]" of [[Jon Favreau]]'s [[2019]] [[Disney+]] television series [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian|''The Mandalorian'']], directed by [[Carl Weathers]]<ref name="Chapter 12" /> and released on [[November 20]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-november-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in November 2020}}</ref>
