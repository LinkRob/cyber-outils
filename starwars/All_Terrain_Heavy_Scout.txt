The '''All Terrain Heavy Scout (AT-HS)''' was a model of [[walker]] fielded by the [[First Order]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary" />

{{Veh-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:All_Terrain_Heavy_Scout.png|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of the All Terrain Heavy Scout by James Clyne.]]
The All Terrain Heavy Scout was created as concept art for the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], but was ultimately not used in the final cut of the film.<ref name="Art TLJ">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]''</ref> It was nonetheless mentioned in, and canonized by, the ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> A LEGO set for the walker was also released,<ref name="LEGO">{{LEGOCite|num=75177|name=First Order Heavy Scout Walker}}</ref> and it has appeared in several [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] media as a result.<ref name="DK">[[LEGO Star Wars: The Last Jedi|''LEGO Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' DK Reader]]</ref>

A card in the [[2017 Topps Star Wars: The Last Jedi|2017 Topps ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'']] trading card set incorrectly labeled the [[First Order All Terrain Scout Transport]] as the All Terrain Heavy Scout walker;<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2017 Topps Star Wars: The Last Jedi|cardname=#61 - First Order Walker}}</ref> a similar card in the ''[[Star Wars: Card Trader]]'' app similarly mislabels the First Order AT-ST as the AT-HS.<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Star Wars: The Last Jedi - Vehicles & Ships|cardname=AT-HS}}</ref>
