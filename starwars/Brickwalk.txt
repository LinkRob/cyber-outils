'''Brickwalk''' was a region of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] planet [[Celwis]]. The [[Xodlak family]]'s [[Patriel]] [[Xodlak'oo'nifis]] managed the family's affairs on a planetary scale from Brickwalk.<ref name="Greater Good" />

Brickwalk was located across the [[planet]] from [[Redhill province]], where [[Councilor (Chiss Ascendancy)|Councilor]] [[Xodlak'uvi'vil]] was stationed, though it was less than a day's transport. It had a landing field.<ref name="Greater Good" />
