'''Corpse-leeches''' were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[Diet|carnivores]] native to [[Carnelion IV]]. They were distinguished by their six [[eye]]s, [[Color|red]] skin, and wings.

==Description==
Corpse-Leechers were [[humanoid]] like carnivores that inhabited the open mountains on Carnelion IV. They often attacked both the Open and the Closed and forced them to move to their mountain bases and to use [[Airship]]s for travel.<ref name="Part II" />

==History==
Corpse leeches were not native to Carnelion IV prior to the war between the [[Open]]s and the [[Closed]]. Due to the toxic gases and bio weapons that destroyed the world, many [[human]]s were mutated into Corpse-Leeches.<ref name="Obi-Wan and Anakin 2">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 2|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 2]]</ref>

While on Carnelion IV, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] , his [[Padawan]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Grecker]], [[Pran]], and [[Kolara]] were attacked by corpse-leeches. Kenobi ordered Anakin to merely redirect the creatures instead of killing them, saying that they should not die simply for being in their way. However, Skywalker struggled to push them back and [[Death|slew]] several creatures with [[Anakin Skywalker's first lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Part II" />
