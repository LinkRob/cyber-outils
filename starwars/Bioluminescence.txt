[[File:ZabrakSpines.png|thumb|right|250px|Zabrak Spines were a bioluminescent plant found of the planet Umbara]]
'''Bioluminescence''' was the production of light by certain living organisms.<ref name="Inferno Squad">''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''</ref> Typically, those organisms thrived in environments where light was limited, such as deep [[Ocean|sea]] beds or caverns. Bioluminescence was used for a variety of purposes, including attracting pollinators and prey.<ref name="EV">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

Because the [[planet]] [[Umbara]] was constantly submerged in darkness, bioluminescent [[plant]]s like the [[Zabrak Spine]] were common there. Such plants were also common in the darkest depths of [[Mon Cala]]'s oceans,<ref name="EV" /> as exemplified by the [[Mon Cal microplankton]].<ref name="SMRS">''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]''</ref>

A [[species]] of bioluminescent [[orchid]] grew in the [[Forest|rain forests]] of [[Yavin 4]],<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> and bioluminescent [[lichen]] could be found in [[cave]]s on [[Alpinn]].<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>

The [[gangster]] "Big" [[Sturg Ganna]] had a suite with fountains which included bioluminescent [[algae]] which cast a bluish glow.<ref name="nothing">{{StoryCite|story=Hear Nothing, See Nothing, Say Nothing|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref>
