'''Corn''' was an ingredient. Kernels of corn were used in making [[Kat's Kettle Corn]].<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref> On one occasion, [[Unkar Plutt]] used corn clusters from a [[ration pack]] to lure [[ripper-raptor]]s into attacking the [[Zendiat Collective]] after they abducted him.<ref name="Better the Devil You Know, Part II">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=2|story=Better the Devil You Know, Part II}}</ref>
