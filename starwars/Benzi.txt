'''Benzi''' was a [[human]] individual who served as an [[ISB tactical agent]] [[sergeant]] within the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Who Else" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Benzi led an [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[ISB Tactical|Tactical]] [[Team-Two|team]] to arrest and detain the individual [[Kleya Marki]]. However, while doing so, Benzi was<ref name="Who Else" /> knocked unconscious<ref name="Who Else AD" /> and ultimately [[Death|died]]<ref name="Insider 236">{{InsiderCite|236|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> due to the actions of the [[KX-series security droid|KX security droid]] [[K-2SO]], being left in an [[Turbolift|elevator]] following their brief encounter.<ref name="Who Else" />

==Behind the scenes==
Benzi was portrayed by [[Louis Martin (actor)|Louis Martin]] in the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', first appearing in "[[Who Else Knows?]]" and "[[Jedha, Kyber, Erso]]," the eleventh and twelfth episodes of the show's [[Andor Season 2|second season]],<ref name="Who Else" /><ref name="JKE">{{Andor|Jedha, Kyber, Erso}}</ref> both of which aired on [[May 14]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref> The character was misspelled as "Bezni" in the subtitles for Disney+<ref name="Who Else Sub">{{Andor|Who Else Knows?}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> along with the subtitles for another [[soldier]] who [[Death|perished]] before him<ref name="Who Else AD" /> being inserted for the sergeant.<ref name="Who Else Sub" /> Martin shared a behind the scenes look into his filming via social media.<ref name="TacticalBTS">{{Instagram|username=louismartin|url=p/DJquT1zoekb|name=[[Louis Martin (actor)|Louis Martin]]|date=2025-05-15|quote=Andor BTS #starwars #andor #actor #studio #bts|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/i30aq}}</ref>
