The '''Blue Tooka''' was a [[Cantina|tavern]] in the [[city]] [[Parantha]] on<ref name="A Valiant Vow" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Defy the Storm">''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Aricho]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. It was owned by [[Kol Gresh]] and [[Lowl Gresh]]. It was named for the multitude of blue [[Tooka|tooka cats]] that lived in the tavern.<ref name="A Valiant Vow" />
