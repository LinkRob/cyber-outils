The '''Annihilation''', known in [[Mando'a]] as '''Dral'Han''',<ref name="OSWM">{{GermanFanCite|104|Database}}</ref> was a battle in the final war of the larger, ancient [[Mandalorian-Jedi War|Mandalorian conflict]], between the [[Jedi]]<ref name="Creating Mandalore" /> and the [[Mandalorian]] [[warlord]]s<ref name="Mandalorian super commando" /> caused a cataclysm<ref name="Creating Mandalore" /> that scorched<ref name="Mandalorian super commando" /> much<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know" /> of the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]]'s surface into lifeless [[Color|white]] [[desert]],<ref name="Gauntlet fighter">{{SWE|technology|gauntlet-fighter|Gauntlet fighter}}</ref> uninhabitable outside of hermetically sealed [[Domed city|dome]] [[City|cities]].<ref name="Mandalorian super commando" />

The [[Standard century|centuries]] of war that consumed Mandalore also helped destroy its landscape.<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know" /> The grasslands that survived into [[Satine Kryze]]'s early life<ref name="Kenobi's Shadow">{{StoryCite|story=Kenobi's Shadow|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref> were destroyed during the wars she lived to see.<ref name="The Mandalore Plot">{{TCW|The Mandalore Plot}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Did they fight against the Jedi? Yes, they absolutely did. Is there a big battle where there's a cataclysm between them and the Jedi? Yes. Have we pushed that idea that the cataclysm was so great that the surface of Mandalore was laid to waste? Absolutely, we did.|[[Dave Filoni]]|<ref name="Creating Mandalore" />}}
The concept of a cataclysm devastating Mandalore was created as part of the backstory of the Mandalorian people as depicted in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated series.<ref name="Creating Mandalore" /> Within [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], this event was identified as the [[Mandalorian Excision]],<ref name="Warfare">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref> though this information is no longer considered [[canon]].
