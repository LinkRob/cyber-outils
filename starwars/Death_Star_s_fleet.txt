The [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station's]] '''fleet''' was a fully equipped [[Imperial Navy]] [[fleet]] that defended the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[planet]]-[[Death|killing]] [[battle station]]. The fleet consisted of hundreds of [[Starship|vessels]] that ranged from [[dreadnought]]s to [[assault gunboat]]s, and included tens of thousands of [[TIE series|TIE-series]] craft. The fleet was part of the [[Death Star's Imperial Navy garrison|garrison]] that was deployed as part of the military forces of the [[Death Star]]. Due to the size of the fleet and the [[garrison]] on the Death Star, personnel and equipment took up a large amount of space within the battle [[Space station|station]], including [[hangar]]s and magazines.<ref name="Dawn" />

The fleet, along with the garrison, were led by [[Chief of the Imperial Navy]] [[Admiral]] [[Conan Antonio Motti]], who was described as a veteran [[Navy|naval]] [[commander]] and one of the highest decorated [[officer]]s in the Imperial Navy. While the fleet existed,<ref name="Dawn" /> [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], the commander of the Death Star, decided not to deploy any naval escort with the battle station during the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref>
