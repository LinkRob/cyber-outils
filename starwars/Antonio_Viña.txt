'''Antonio Viña''' is an actor who portrayed [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Kassa]] in the ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series.

==Biography==
Antonio Viña is an actor who played a young [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], who was known as Kassa on his [[homeworld]] [[Kenari]].<ref name="Kassa" /> [[Diego Luna]], the actor who played adult Andor, found it very important for young Andor to be played by a [[Wikipedia:Mexicans|Mexican]] actor to show that the two actors shared something.<ref name="EW" /> Viña, Luna, and [[Fiona Shaw]], the actress who played [[Maarva Carassi Andor|Maarva Andor]], collaborated to shape Maarva's journey as an adoptive mother.<ref name="Daughter">{{SW|url=news/fiona-shaw-andor-interview|text=Daughter of Ferrix: ''Andor'''s Fiona Shaw on the Role of Cassian's Mother, Maarva}}</ref> When Viña flew to [[Wikipedia:London|London]] for his last session, Luna made sure that he was the right actor for the role, and they spent time together. The two actors were shooting at the same time, so Luna was able to welcome Viña and make him feel part of the team. Luna thought that Viña was special because he does a lot with his eyes, which Luna thought was very important for the role. Luna also liked that Viña has a "spark" in his eyes.<ref name="EW" />
