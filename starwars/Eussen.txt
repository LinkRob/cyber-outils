'''Eussen''' was a thinker and book author. Eussen's [[Lemma]], particularly its fifth book, was known to the [[Narkis Anchorite]]s for exploring the antinomy of enlightenment, understood to be a paradox of the inevitability of cycles.<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> By [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> Eussen's Lemma had confounded scholars for centuries, and was understood by some anchorites to mean that one could not reason their way to true enlightenment, a view rejected by others based on [[the Canons]].<ref name="Survivor" />
