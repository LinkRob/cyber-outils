The '''Copul system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Pallis sector]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. It lay in grid square N-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The system was the origin point<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Copulan shadow-warrior]]s|system=the Copul [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> of the [[Copulan shadow-warrior]]s, which were known to [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]]<ref name="Glass">''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]''</ref> by around [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]'' take place one [[Standard week|week]] after the [[Funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn|funeral]] of [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]].</ref>
