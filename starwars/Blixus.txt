'''Blixus'''<ref name="V7">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> were rare, half-shelled [[Amphibian|semi-aquatic]] creatures native to [[Sljee]].<ref name="Bestiary" /> 

==Biology and appearance==
They possessed five tentacles and six sharp legs.<ref name="Databank" /> They shared a common ancestor with the [[rathtar]], [[vixus]], and [[sarlacc]], all possessing a large maw, tentacles, and a voracious appetite. Each was native to different worlds, and the different species had adapted separately to become great hunters in their specific environments.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==History==
They could be found on [[Scarif]]<ref name="AB Update">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> and other watery worlds,<ref name="Bestiary" /> where they dragged insufficiently vigilant individuals into lagoons to eat them.<ref name="AB Update" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Commander]] and [[Zygerrian]] [[enslaver]] [[Darts D'Nar]] owned a blixus which he kept aboard his [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Tecora]]''. When D'Nar was forced to escape from [[Kiros]] aboard the ''Tecora'', he unleashed his pet blixus upon [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. It attacked, wrapping its tentacles around Skywalker's torso and coiling one around Tano's foot. Tano was able to cut it off and escape, but Skywalker's arms were trapped, making it difficult for him to use the [[lightsaber]]. However, after some struggle, Skywalker was able to [[Death|kill]] it with [[Skywalker lightsaber|his lightsaber]] after the blixus opened its mouth to eat him.<ref name="Kidnapped" />
