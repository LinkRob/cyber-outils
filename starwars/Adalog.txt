'''Adalog''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> located in the [[Adalog system]]<ref name="Systems" /> within [[the Slice]]<ref name="Slice" /> portion of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. Situated in the [[Mytaranor sector]], it lay at the coordinates Q-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Intelligence officer|operative]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] visited<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> by [[1 BBY]].<ref name="1 BBY">The events of the [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]] are dated to [[1 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> However, the lead about a [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|weapon]] being built by the [[Galactic Empire]] that Andor was given on Adalog turned out to be useless.<ref name="Rogue One novel" />

==Behind the scenes==
Adalog was mentioned in the [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]], written by [[Alexander Freed]] and published in [[2016]].<ref name="Rogue One novel" />
