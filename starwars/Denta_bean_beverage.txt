A '''beverage''' primarily consisting of [[denta bean]]s was popular on<ref name="BWDHE?" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]]. The [[Foodstuff|drink]] featured [[whilk milk]] and [[thalassa seed]]s as secondary ingredients. The resulting brew had a bitter flavor, smooth texture, and was guaranteed to energize the individual who consumed it.<ref name="BWDHE?" />

To make a serving of the beverage, one had to roast ten cans of denta beans and then steep them in boiled whilk [[milk]] and thalassa [[seed]]s. [[Time]] and care was needed to brew the beverage with a smooth texture.<ref name="BWDHE?" />

The drink could also be found around<ref name="BWDHE?" /> the [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]].<ref name="GA" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] in [[3 ABY]]. Since "[[But What Does He Eat?]]" takes place during those events, its events must also take place in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> [[Torro Sbazzle]], [[Executive Chef]] of<ref name="BWDHE?" /> the [[tibanna]] [[gas]] [[mining]] colony<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> [[Cloud City]] instructed her [[sous-chef]], [[Gersolik]], to prepare the beverage. It was to be served to the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] during a banquet with [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], the [[city]]'s [[Baron Administrator]].<ref name="BWDHE?" />

==Behind the scenes==
The beverage was mentioned in the short story "[[But What Does He Eat?]]" by [[S.A. Chakraborty]]. The story was published in the [[2020]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="BWDHE?" />
