'''''Coffret Star Wars 2 : Les Ennemis''''' is a box set containing two [[Wikipedia:French language|French]] young-readers books, ''[[Les Sith]]'' and [[Dark Vador (children's book)|''Dark Vador'']], that were written by [[Sabine Boccador]]. It was published on [[October 14]], [[2016]] by [[Fleurus]] as part of the series ''[[La grande imagerie Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Amazon" />

==Publisher's summary==
''In this collection devoted to the heroes of Star Wars, children will discover the followers of the dark side of the Force, the Sith and their allies. A closer look at the different characters and situations will help them better understand the history of this famous saga.<ref name="Amazon" />''
