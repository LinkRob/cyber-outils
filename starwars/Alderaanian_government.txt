The [[Omwati]] [[Qeris Lar]] was a member of the government until he came to the belief that the [[House of Organa|royal family of Alderaan]] would not take a strong enough stand against the [[Galactic Empire]] and left the government to fund anti-Imperial operations.<ref name="Battle Scars">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]''</ref>
