'''Calladan's field''' was a place on the planet [[Gorse]] that was near the settlement of [[Gorse City]]. During the [[Gorse Conflict]], the [[Galactic Empire]] rerouted all of [[Moonglow Polychemical]]'s personnel transports to Calladan's field. This created a traffic jam for the returning [[miner]]s and [[Okadiah Garson]] was forced to take a [[hovercab]] back to his [[cantina]] [[The Asteroid Belt]].<ref name="AND" />
