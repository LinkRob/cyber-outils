In [[33 BBY/Legends|33 BBY]], an '''attack on Supreme Chancellor [[Finis Valorum/Legends|Finis Valorum]]''' at the hands of the [[Nebula Front/Legends|Nebula Front]] terrorist organization occurred on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].

==Prelude==
[[Qui-Gon Jinn/Legends|Qui-Gon Jinn]] had reason to believe that Captain [[Arwen Cohl]] had survived the [[Dorvalla Incident]] and aired his concerns to fellow [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Adi Gallia/Legends|Adi Gallia]] who suggested the two confer with Supreme Chancellor Valorum.

==The attack==
The two Jedi arrived to spot Chancellor Valorum engulfed in a crowd of media correspondents and his personal aides. As they approached the crowd, Jinn sensed that something was not right, and that moment was when the [[Nebula Front/Legends|Nebula Front]] arrived in an airspeeder to attack Valorum. Jinn and Gallia fended them off but one succeeded in wounding Valorum.

==Aftermath==
In the moments following the attack, Valorum was rushed to hospital. Some believed that it was in retaliation of the Front not being included in the recently proposed [[Eriadu Trade Summit]]. Master Jin eventually found a Front base at [[Asmeru/Legends|Asmeru]] in the [[Senex sector/Legends|Senex sector]] and headed there with his [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] apprentice, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and five other [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]].
