'''Brissa''' was an [[Ikkrukkian]] who was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] as part of the [[Magna Tolvan's crew|crew]] led by [[Captain]] [[Magna Tolvan]]. The crew was called by [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to an undisclosed [[Rebel Base (Leia Organa)|rebel base]] after taking down an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] outpost. They were tasked with [[Search for the Silver Arrow|searching]] for the missing Rebellion [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Silver Arrow]]'', which the entity known as the [[Scourge]] [[Scourge crisis|took over]].

When the team eventually found the lost vessel, it was still intact and showed no sign of damage. However, once Brissa and the rest entered the ship, they discovered the corpses of all crewmembers. They questioned the whereabouts of the ship's [[droid]]s, though it did not take long for the crew to get their answer. The missing droids, taken over by the Scourge, acted in unison and fought to capture the crew. They put up a fight against the droids, though the crew was overwhelmed by the sheer quantity of them. Having no use for pure organics, the Scourge [[Death|disposed]] of Brissa, the [[Devaronian]] [[Sheyo]], and the [[Caamasi]] [[Pilok]], while Tolvan and Kelb were taken for further experimentation due to their [[cybernetics]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|She mentioned that it was an urgent matter.|Magna Tolvan, on Leia Organa's request for the crew's arrival|<ref name="Aphra 36" />}}
[[File:LeiaTolvanCrewBriefing-2020DoctorAphra36.png|thumb|left|245px|Leia Organa briefs Brissa and the crew about the ''Silver Arrow'' situation.]]
Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Aphra Date" /> the [[Ikkrukkian]] Brissa<ref name="Aphra 37" /> was part of a [[Magna Tolvan's crew|crew]] led by the [[human]] [[cyborg]] [[Captain]] [[Magna Tolvan]] under the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. The crew took down an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] outpost before getting called to an undisclosed [[Rebel Base (Leia Organa)|rebel base]] by [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] for an urgent matter. The crew later landed their [[starship|ship]], the ''[[Hawk's End]]'', and met up with the general as planned. In the briefing room, Organa informed Brissa and the rest that one of the Rebellion's most reliable supply-run ships, the ''[[Silver Arrow]]'', went missing a [[Standard week|week]] prior. The only clue they had was a recording of a [[Distress signal|distress call]] from the ship before the [[Comm unit|comms]] cut out. Unable to perform a full search effort, Organa tasked the crew to [[Search for the Silver Arrow|search]] for the missing vessel alone, considering the crew's past performances to be miracles.<ref name="Aphra 36" /> Unbeknownst to the crew, the missing vessel was [[Scourge crisis|taken over]] by the technological entity<ref name="Aphra 37" /> known as the [[Scourge]].<ref name="Aphra 38">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 38|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 38]]</ref>

As the crew traveled through the [[Outer Rim Territories]] aboard the ''Hawk's End'', they listened to the recorded distress call from the ''Silver Arrow''. Other members of the crew, including the [[Caamasi]]<ref name="Aphra 36" /> [[Pilok]]<ref name="Aphra 37" /> and the [[Devaronian]]<ref name="Aphra 36" /> [[Sheyo]]<ref name="Aphra 37" /> offered ideas about what could happen aboard the missing vessel. Given that the only lead they had were week-old [[Galactic coordinates|coordinates]] from the ship's log, doubts about fulfilling their search started to show among the crew. Despite their pessimistic remarks, the crew pulled through with encouragement from Tolvan before they set up new coordinates and jumped into [[hyperspace]]. After jumping out of hyperspace, still deep in the Outer Rim, they found the ''Silver Arrow''. Brissa and the human cyborg [[navigator]] [[Kelb]] inspected the ship and confirmed that it appeared to be intact and undamaged. Tolvan attempted to make contact with the ship but was unable to receive any response from it. Unsure of what lay ahead, she reminded everyone to keep their wits.<ref name="Aphra 36" />

[[File:ScourgeOfTheDroids-2020DoctorAphra37.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Scourge attacks Brissa and their crew.]]
The crew then boarded the ''Silver Arrow'' and inspected the [[hull]], where they witnessed many [[Death|lifeless]] bodies lying around to their horror. Tolvan warned the rest to be ready in case the murderer was still somewhere aboard the ship, with Kelb deducing that the people were already dead for a few [[Standard day|days]] and noted that one of the bodies was stabbed with precision. As they inspected the bodies, Kelb noticed everyone except the [[droid]]s were there. They soon found themselves surrounded as the missing droids ambushed the crew. The droids, controlled by the Scourge, talked and acted as if they were under a single hivemind, which Brissa told the rest to ignore. Tolvan instructed the crew to head back to the ship, though they were overwhelmed by the droids before they could do anything. As the rebels struggled to break free of the droids' grasps, Tolvan told Brissa to run, though the Ikkrukkian could not move and was in tears. Having [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] enhancements, Tolvan and Kelb were then taken away for further experimentation by the droids,<ref name="Aphra 37" /> while Brissa, Sheyo, and Pilok were killed<ref name="Death Twitter" /> and disposed of due to them being pure organics.<ref name="Aphra 37" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|D&ndash;Don't listen to them!|Brissa|<ref name="Aphra 37" />}}
[[File:SilverArrowCorpses-2020DoctorAphra37.png|thumb|left|275px|The crew discovers many corpses lying around the ''Silver Arrow''.]]
Brissa was an Ikkrukkian with [[Color|white]] skin, orange [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]].<ref name="Aphra 36" /> Similar to other members of their [[species]], Brissa had two protruding bulges on their [[Bone|skull]].<ref name="Ikkrukkian">[[The High Republic — The Blade 1|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 1]] showcases another [[Ikkrukkian]], [[Porter Engle]], with two protruding bulges on their [[Bone|skull]]; similar to Brissa's seen in [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 36|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 36]].</ref> Even though the Ikkrukkian did not talk much, they were cheerful and excited when returning to the rebel base after a successful mission.<ref name="Aphra 36" /> Their smile would later turn, as Brissa was apprehended by the infected droids aboard the ''Silver Arrow'', crying helplessly.<ref name="Aphra 37" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|We're stretched too thin for a full search effort, but your team performs miracles.|Leia Organa, on the crew's performance|<ref name="Aphra 36" />}}
Brissa was part of Magna Tolvan's crew, who Organa considered to be miracle workers, and were able to take down an Imperial outpost.<ref name="Aphra 36" /> The Ikkrukkian was also able to use a [[blaster]] and spoke [[Galactic Basic Standard]] to their colleagues.<ref name="Aphra 37" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Weapons at the ready. Whoever ''murdered'' the ''Silver Arrow's'' crew may still be aboard.|Magna Tolvan, ordering her crew|<ref name="Aphra 37" />}}
Brissa wore a green [[Clothing|shirt]] with a brown sleeveless vest over. During their mission to the ''Silver Arrow'', Brissa wielded a single blaster<ref name="Aphra 37" /> and had an active tracker embedded in their clothes, sending out a signal to the rest of the Rebellion; however, the tracker became inactive shortly after<ref name="Aphra 38" /> their demise.<ref name="Death Twitter" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brissa first appeared, albeit unidentified, in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 36|thirty-sixth issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'' (2020)]], which was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Jethro Morales]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 36" /> on [[September 27]], [[2023]].<ref name="Aphra 36 Release">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103081/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_36|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #36}}</ref> They were then identified by name in the series' [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 37|thirty-seventh issue]],<ref name="Aphra 37" /> also written by Wong and published by Marvel on [[October 25]], 2023.<ref name="Aphra 37 Release">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103082/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_37|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #37}}</ref> On [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], Wong confirmed Brissa to be the Ikkrukkian in the issues.<ref name="Name ID">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1748357814827819346|[[Alyssa Wong]]|date=2024-01-19|quote=I just checked the script to be sure, and that's right!|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/wS3eA|archivefile=File:AlyssaWong-MagnasCrewID-Twitter.png}} {{C|In response to: "Hi @crashwong, I recently reread Aphra 37 for identification of the rest of Magna's crew. This panel made it seem like Pilok is the Caamasi, Sheyo the Devaronian, and Brissa the Ikkrukian [''sic'']. Not sure if it was the intention, but I would love to get a confirmation on it if possible."}}</ref> Additionally, Wong also confirmed that Brissa died after their encounter with the Scourge, which the comic issue did not show.<ref name="Death Twitter" />
