'''Dendada XVII''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Aphra 15" /> in the [[Dendada system]] in the [[Seswenna sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was the location of the [[Imperial Internal Affairs Outpost]] utilized by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Internal Affairs]]. [[Lieutenant]] [[Magna Tolvan]] was taken to the outpost to be [[Death|executed]] for incompetence of command.<ref name="Aphra 15" />
