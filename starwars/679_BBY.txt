'''679 BBY''', or '''679<ref name="Date" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> Around that year,<ref name="Date" /> a [[Unidentified Faer ship|starship]] that would eventually fall into the hands of the family of the [[Evereni]] [[species]] known as [[Faers]] was constructed.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

==Behind the scenes==
679 BBY was indirectly mentioned in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" />
