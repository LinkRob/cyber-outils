A '''flatharp''' was a twenty-stringed [[harp]]. A [[Rodian]] [[Jedi]] playing the flatharp was depicted on a [[Jedi mural|mural]] in the [[Valo Temple|Jedi temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Valo]]. On the painting, [[music]] was depicted swirling around the Jedi while they played the instrument.<ref name="EFV" />

In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''The High Republic: Escape from Valo'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> Valo was trapped behind the [[Stormwall]] in the [[Nihil]] [[Occlusion Zone]]. [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] used the Valo Jedi temple as a secret base to hide from the Nihil. While looking at [[Ram Jomaram's lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]] after coming back to the temple, Jomaram noticed the painted Jedi and their flatharp on the ceiling.<ref name="EFV" />

==Behind the scenes==
A flatharp appeared as a painting in ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo|Escape from Valo]]'', a junior novel written by [[Daniel José Older]] and [[Alyssa Wong]] and published in [[2024]] as part of the [[Star Wars: The High Republic]] multimedia campaign's<ref name="EFV" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref>
