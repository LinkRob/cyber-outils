'''Ecorbs''' were a [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]] that inhabited the [[Detral Pits]]. They had coiled attachments called [[hampa]]s on their gelatinous heads and possessed [[Color/Legends|red]], pulsating [[blup sack]]s. Ecorbs were said to have grown hampas after excessively [[Diet/Legends|feeding]] themselves with a particular species of radiant [[Orange and blue flower|orange and blue]] [[Flower/Legends|flower]], a trait that was supposedly hereditarily transmitted to following generations.

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Ow! My head is killing me!''"<br />"''Get it off! Get it off!|Two ecorbs crying after hampas suddenly sprout on their heads|<ref name="MAGL" />}}
Ecorbs were dark yellow-[[Color/Legends|colored]], scaly-skinned [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] social beings that hatched from [[Egg/Legends|eggs]] in nests. Each ecorb possessed a gelatinous head with a painfully constricting, coiled shell-like [[hampa]] on top and a large red [[blup sack]] that pulsated rhythmically when the individual was happy. The [[Species/Legends|species]] was capable of [[Dream/Legends|dreaming]] while sleeping and was known to have nightmares on occasion. Ecorbs had sharp [[Tooth/Legends|teeth]] and amber-colored [[Eye/Legends|eyes]], as well as dark patterns on their backs. They were able to speak and possessed at least two [[Gender/Legends|sexes]], male and female. Older ecorbs were known to produce spit from their [[Nose/Legends|nostrils]]. They inhabited a location known as the [[Detral Pits]], and they freely and unhurriedly slid across the region.<ref name="MAGL" />

==History==
{{Quote|If you eat this tantalizing creature, things will change forever!''"<br />"''Things are going to change anyway.|Exchange between two ecorbs, deciding whether or not they should eat the orange and blue flowers|<ref name="MAGL" />}}
According to a story known to every ecorb, their species did not always have hampas on their heads. During a period referred to as "the [[Mercurial and Long Time Away From Here Times]]," they lived happily and slid freely and lazily across the Detral Pits until one night, when an [[orange and blue flower]] grew from a slimy rock. The ecorbs, having never before seen a [[Flower/Legends|flower]], were tantalized. All of them gathered around it, puzzled by its appearance. They then discussed what it could be and what they should do with it, eventually deciding on [[Diet/Legends|eating]] the flower. An [[Unidentified elder ecorb|elder ecorb]] warned against this, but he was ultimately ignored. The flower petals were divided among the ecorbs and eaten.<ref name="MAGL" />

The plant had a psychotropic effect on the ecorbs, who felt giddy, laughed, and danced. After they went to sleep, however, they had nightmares of disembodied eyes and [[Blood/Legends|bloody]] teeth. When the [[Local day/Legends|morning]] came, hundreds of orange and blue flowers blossomed, surrounding the ecorbs. They decided to eat all of the flowers, against the advice of the older ecorb, who eventually proved to be right: the overconsumption of the plants made them feel very sick. Painfully, hampas then suddenly started to sprout atop of their heads, which made the ecorbs feel constantly miserable from that point on, while the flowers themselves had all but disappeared, never to be seen again. According to the story, hampa growth became a hereditary trait passed down to new generations due to this incident.<ref name="MAGL" />
