'''Arel Trem''' was a corrupt [[Customs official|Customs Officer]] of the [[New Mandalorians|New Mandalorian]] government. During the [[Clone Wars]], Arel Trem was part of [[New Mandalorian customs department|Mandalore Customs]] and was to accept fifty crates of [[Corellian Run MultiMeal]]s in exchange for giving 200 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] per case.<ref name="Collapse" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arel Trem was mentioned in ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Collapse" />
