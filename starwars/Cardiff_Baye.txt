'''Cardiff Baye''' was a [[human]] [[Jedi Knight]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Baye held the [[military rank]] of [[Jedi General]] and led [[Cardiff Baye's battalion|a battalion]] during a [[battle on Covenanon]]. Afterwards, he and his unit were assigned on [[Invasion of Katou|a mission]] to [[Katou]]. There, Baye [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] with [[General]] [[Grievous]] and was eventually defeated by the warlord, who took his [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Grievous" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Powers and abilities==
Cardiff Baye was proficient in [[lightsaber combat]] and [[telekinesis]]. He could make [[Force jump|acrobatic leaps]] and [[Taming beasts|control]] a large creature using [[the Force]].<ref name="Grievous" />

==Equipment==
Cardiff Baye wore [[armor]] and wielded a blue [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Grievous" />
