"'''Building the Galaxy'''" is a department within the ''[[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2]]'' magazine published by [[De Agostini]]. Each issue contains the department, which covers details on the creation of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' universe.<ref name="BuildR21">[[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 1|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 1]]</ref>

{{Magazine-stub|article}}

==Publisher's summary==
''This fascinating section lets you look behind the scenes to see the techniques used to construct and film the droids, ships, sets and creatures of all the ''Star Wars'' movies. Explore their development from the earliest concept to the final stunning prop models, photographed in minute detail.''<ref name="Model-space">{{WebCite|url=https://www.model-space.com/gb/r2-d2-full-kit.html|text=Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2|archivedate=20201203183846}}</ref>
