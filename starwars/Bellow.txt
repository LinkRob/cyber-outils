'''Bellow''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[501st Legion]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Bellow served under [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] during a [[Mission to Benglor|mission]] to investigate [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] activity on the [[planet]] [[Benglor]] in [[22 BBY]]. During the mission, Bellow discovered parts of a [[B1-series battle droid]] while trekking through [[jungle]] with Skywalker and several other [[Cloning|clones]]. After searching for more clues, Bellow was attacked by a [[Unidentified quadruped (Benglor)|creature]] that caught and [[Death|ate]] him.

==Biography==
===Republic duty===
{{Quote|I've got you beat, Hardcase! Clanker head. Found it in the sand mound back there.|Bellow discovers a droid part|<ref name="Date" />}}
[[File:BellowDroidHead-TCWBattleTales1.png|thumb|left|250px|Bellow discovered a droid head on Benglor.]]
The [[clone trooper]] who adopted the name Bellow served in the [[501st Legion]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Battle Tales 1" /> Like all of the Republic's [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s, he was created and trained on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] as a clone of the [[human]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]].<ref name="Databank" /> During the Clone Wars<ref name="Battle Tales 1" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> he served under [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] on a [[Mission to Benglor|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Benglor]] in order to investigate Separatist activity there.<ref name="Battle Tales 1" />

Bellow trekked through the hot [[jungle]] of Benglor with Skywalker, Rex, [[Hardcase]], and another clone. As their search for the Separatists was proving unfruitful, Skywalker began considering ending the mission due to the extreme heat. Then, Hardcase discovered a green-[[color]]ed slime on one of the nearby [[tree]]s, which he alerted the others to as a potential clue.<ref name="Battle Tales 1" />

===Benglor breakfast===
{{Quote|We can't fall back! This thing swallowed Bellow!|Hardcase refuses to retreat|<ref name="Date" />}}
[[File:BenglorQuadruped-TCWBattleTales1.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Bellow was eaten by a creature on Benglor.]]
Bellow then discovered a [[B1-series battle droid]] head in a sand mound, teasing Hardcase that he had found better evidence than him. As the clones discovered more parts of the [[droid]], Skywalker realized the pieces had been purposefully left as a trail for them to find. However, before he could warn the others of the potential trap, Bellow discovered more of the slime and called out to the others.<ref name="Battle Tales 1" />

Skywalker yelled out to Bellow as a large [[Unidentified quadruped (Benglor)|creature]] emerged from the undergrowth, attacking swiftly. Bellow had [[time]] only to turn and cry out in horror as the creature wrapped its [[tongue]] around him and then swallowed him. When Skywalker then ordered the others to retreat, Hardcase refused and attacked the creature, wanting to avenge Bellow. The [[Jedi]] was then forced to save Hardcase, and ultimately slew the creature with aid from Rex. Rex later told [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody|CC-2224]] the tale of Bellow's [[death]] during the [[Siege of Hisseen|Separatist siege]] of the planet [[Hisseen]].<ref name="Battle Tales 1" />

==Personality and traits==
As a clone of Jango Fett, Bellow had tan skin, [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]] that he wore in a mohawk.<ref name="Battle Tales 1" /> He stood 1.83 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Databank" />

==Equipment==
Bellow wore [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] with the blue stripes of the 501st on each [[Limb|arm]].<ref name="Battle Tales 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bellow appeared in a flashback in the [[2020]] comic book [[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 1|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 1]], which was written by [[Michael Moreci]] and illustrated by [[Arianna Florean]].<ref name="Battle Tales 1" />
