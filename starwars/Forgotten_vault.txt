The '''forgotten vault''' was a [[Miyuki Trade League]] vault located on the north shore of [[Lake Amari]] in the [[Great Basin]] region on [[Akiva]], a [[jungle]] [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Sometime before [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Jet Kordo]] left a message about how much they enjoyed a previous job on [[Nar Shaddaa]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|Canon=1}}
During 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> [[Kay Vess]] located the vault using [[Jet Kordo's Holotracker]] and a [[hologram disc]] that she retrieved from a [[hutt]] enforcer in the [[Hunter's Canopy]] area of Akiva. While in the vault Vess recovered the audio message from Kordo as well as a pair of [[Clothing|pants]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The forgotten vault appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was one of several vaults that the player can find in the game as part of the "Jet Kordo's Legacy" quest.<ref name="Outlaws" />
