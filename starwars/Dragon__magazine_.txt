'''''Dragon''''' was a [[roleplaying game]] magazine that ran for 430 issues, from [[1976]] to [[2013]]. It was one of two official magazines providing source material for the [[Wikipedia:Dungeons & Dragons|''Dungeons & Dragons'']] role-playing game, the other being [[Wikipedia:Dungeon (magazine)|''Dungeon'']]. During its history the magazine was also known as '''''The Dragon''''' and '''''Dragon Magazine''''', and was released by three different publishers: [[TSR, Inc.]], [[Wizards of the Coast]], and [[Paizo Publishing]].

''Dragon Magazine'' issues [[Dragon Magazine 157|157]] ([[May]] [[1990]]) and [[Dragon Magazine 200|200]] ([[December]] [[1993]]) provided supplementary material for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. However, these articles were released outside of the [[Lucasfilm]] licensing process.

Issue 157 included the article "[[For the Greater Good]]" by [[Carl Sargent]], while issue 200 included the adventure "[[The Galaxy Dragon and crew|The ''Galaxy Dragon'' and crew]]" by [[Bill Slavicsek]].
