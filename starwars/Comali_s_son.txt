This [[Gender|male]] individual was the '''son''' of [[Lady]] [[Comali]], and served as a guard for [[Monarch of Shu-Torun|Queen]] [[Trios]] of [[Shu-Torun]]. At some point, he [[Death|died]] protecting Trios. During the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Mission to Shu-Torun (Rebel Alliance)|mission to Shu-Torun]], Trios crossed paths with Lady Comali at a party in the [[Abyssal Rooms]] as she was leaving, and mentioned Comali's son to her, and that she understood she was still mourning for him.<ref name="Star Wars 64" />
