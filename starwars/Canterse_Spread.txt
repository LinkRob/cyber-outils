The '''Canterse Spread''' was an [[Asteroid field/Legends|asteroid belt]] rotating around the [[Star/Legends|star]] [[Julevia]] in its third [[Orbit/Legends|orbit]]. Positioned between the [[Planet/Legends|planets]] [[Proxida]] and [[Gologry]], it was located in the [[Julevian system]], in the [[Dalonbian sector/Legends|Dalonbian sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim]].
