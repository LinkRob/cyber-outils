A '''battle''' took place at [[Devaron]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and both the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Trade Federation]]. The Separatist and Federation fleet was led by the [[Duros]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] on behalf of the [[Sith]], who abducted the [[Jedi Master]] [[Bolla Ropal]], whom he wanted to unlock the [[holocron]] that Bane had [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|stolen]] from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] so the device could read the [[kyber memory crystal]] in Ropal's possession. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], sent after Bane, arrived in the [[Devaron system]] aboard the ''[[Resolute]]'' shortly after Ropal's abduction.

While the [[frigate]]s escorting [[Cad Bane's Munificent-class star frigate|Bane's flagship]] were destroyed by the attacking [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], Bane [[torture]]d Ropal to [[death]] in an unsuccessful attempt to force him to open the stolen [[Jedi holocron]]. When Skywalker, Tano and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] executed an impromptu boarding of Bane's [[flagship]] to capture him, the bounty hunter changed his plans, managing to capture Tano to force Skywalker to open the holocron. Subsequently, Bane's flagship was destroyed, with the Jedi presuming Bane to have been killed by [[clone trooper]] [[Denal]], and the holocron lost. However, Bane had actually killed Denal and used his [[armor]] to get onboard the escape [[shuttle]], subsequently escaping the ''Resolute'' and prompting another hunt for him.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Intruders, there will be. In the Temple.|Yoda|<ref name="Holocron Heist" />}}
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> during the [[The galaxy|galaxy-spanning]] [[Clone Wars]], [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]] hired the [[Duros]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] to [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|infiltrate]] the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] in order to steal a [[Jedi holocron]]. Knowing the difficulty of such a job, Bane hired [[Clawdite]] bounty hunter [[Cato Parasitti]] to serve as an inside woman on the job to assist him and his [[techno-service droid]] [[Todo 360]]. [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] foresaw that there would be intruders within the temple, but the Jedi assumed that any intruders would be after military intelligence from the [[Jedi Temple communication center|communication centre]]. While Jedi Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his former apprentice [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] kept a lookout, Skywalker's [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] was on guard duty at the Archives, assigned as a punishment for disobeying orders to retreat during the [[First Battle of Felucia]].<ref name="Holocron Heist" />

Parasitti, shapeshifted into the form of [[Ord Enisence]], a [[Skrilling]] Jedi Master whose recent death was still unknown to the [[Jedi Order]], easily gained access to the Temple and entered the archives. A bored Tano eagerly offered "Enisence" her help, but Parasitti ended up brusquely ordering her to leave in an attempt to be left alone. After Parasitti had accessed a computer terminal, she guided Bane and Todo through a weak point in the Temple's [[deflector shield]]s and through the vents to the entrance to the [[Holocron Vault]]. Informed by Yoda that the intruders were present, Kenobi and Skywalker set out to track Bane while the Temple was placed under lockdown. Chief librarian [[Jocasta Nu]], spotting "Enisence" at the computer, attempted to inform the impostor about the lockdown, but Parasitti, taking the old woman by surprise, knocked her unconscious and shifted into her form. As Skywalker and Kenobi closed in on Bane's position, the bounty hunter was attempting to hack open the door to the vault. Parasitti was attempting to assist Bane when she was discovered by Tano, who swiftly realized she was dealing with an impostor and subdued the shapeshifter after a brief scuffle.<ref name="Holocron Heist" />

With no more help forthcoming, Bane had Todo cut a hole in the wall and head for the communication centre, while he placed a small explosive on the door control. Blowing up the controls, Bane was able to open the vault door. When Kenobi and Skywalker emerged from the vents in the Vault hallway, they saw the hole cut by Todo and followed, not noticing the partially open vault door. Bane then blew up Todo with a bomb he had planted in the droid earlier after the droid had reached the communication centre. Stealing a [[holocron]], Bane then snuck out of the Temple without being caught. Only after Bane had escaped did the Jedi realize the burglary's true motive. Parasitti, angry about being left behind by Bane, informed the Jedi that [[Rodian]] Jedi Master [[Bolla Ropal]] was the Duros' next target. The Jedi were alarmed, as Ropal was the keeper of the [[kyber memory crystal]], which contained a list of every [[Force-sensitive]] child known to the Order and could only be read by a holocron. Skywalker and Tano set out in pursuit of the bounty hunter.<ref name="Holocron Heist" />

==The battle==
In order to capture Ropal, who was commanding [[Cloning|clone]] forces on the [[planet]] [[Devaron]], Sidious, who was secretly in charge of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], ordered [[Trade Federation]] [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] to give Bane command of a [[fleet]] of five [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]]. Bane's forces arrived in the [[Devaron system]] and conducted an assault on the [[Galactic Republic]] outpost where the Rodian Jedi was stationed, capturing him. The remaining forces at the outpost were under heavy attack.<ref name="Cargo of Doom" />

[[File:FiveStar-Cad-Bane.png|thumb|left|250px|Cad Bane during the battle]]
In hot pursuit of the bounty hunter, Skywalker arrived in the [[Star system|system]] aboard his [[flagship]], the ''[[Resolute]]'', accompanied by Tano. The Jedi learned from a [[Unidentified clone trooper (Devaron)|clone trooper]] on the planet below that Ropal had been taken and that there was no possibility of evacuating the forces remaining there, after which the transmitter was destroyed at the source. Skywalker ordered [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] to attack Bane's fleet. The swift attack quickly brought down the frigates escorting [[Cad Bane's Munificent-class star frigate|Bane's flagship]], followed by fire that crippled the [[hyperdrive]] of the remaining [[frigate]], preventing Bane from escaping. The bounty hunter paid the attack little heed, however, as in the detention block he had [[battle droid]]s [[torture]] Ropal to try and induce him to open the stolen holocron. Ropal refused, and when Bane continued the interrogation, ignoring the presiding droid's warnings that the Jedi Master could not take much more, the Rodian died. Turning his attention to the Republic attackers, Bane noted that whatever Jedi was commanding the enemy [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] was clearly a bold strategist, and came up with a new plan for opening the holocron.<ref name="Cargo of Doom" />

Skywalker resolved to board Bane's frigate and retrieve Master Ropal, despite Yularen, Tano and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]]'s skepticism since the ''Resolute'' was not carrying boarding craft, as their initial plan had been to land on the surface. Noting the [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s stored in the [[hangar]] bay, Skywalker came up with a plan to use them as impromptu boarding craft. While Yularen remained incredulous, Tano and Rex quickly came around, and they boarded three [[walker]]s with three brigades of clone troopers. The walkers were then dropped onto Bane's crippled [[starship]] from above, with Skywalker and Tano wearing [[Environment suit|spacesuits]]. A brief firefight with enemy [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] and battle droids atop the frigate ensued, but the Republic forces quickly gained entry to the frigate. As Skywalker and his team made their way to the frigate's [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], Bane had the droid bridge crew slave all of the ship's functions to his wrist [[computer]], before escaping into the ventilation just before the Jedi and clones arrived.<ref name="Cargo of Doom" />
