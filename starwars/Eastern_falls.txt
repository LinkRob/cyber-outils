The '''eastern falls''' was a series of waterfalls located in the southeast area of [[Grazing Fields]] region on [[Toshara]], a [[wind]]-swept [[moon]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. By [[3 ABY]], a hideout called [[Shaska's Hold]] had been build in a set of rocks near the falls.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The eastern falls first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It is identified in the game by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] communications about the player's last seen location while they have a wanted level.<ref name="Outlaws" />
