'''Ars Veruna'''<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|47|Starship Fact File}}</ref> was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as the [[Monarch of Naboo|king]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Naboo]] prior to the reign of [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="SWCEUE" /> During his reign, Veruna established a vast royal library collection in a [[Veruna's tower|tower]] at the back of the [[Theed Royal Palace]].<ref name="SWCL" /> Veruna was considered a corrupt [[king]],<ref name="HC2">{{HelmetCollectionCite|2|Databank A-Z}}</ref> and was [[Death|assassinated]] prior to Amidala's [[Democracy|election]] as queen<ref name="HC 54">{{HelmetCollectionCite|54|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Death Note" />

He shared his name with [[Veruna]], one<ref name="SW:TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> of the three<ref name="T:A">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> [[moon]]s of Naboo.<ref name="SW:TVE" /> The [[Verunas|Veruna family]] overall would be remembered as one of Naboo's ruling families.<ref name="Legacy4" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Development===
[[File:VerunaTheInteresting.jpg|thumb|right|130px|Ars Veruna in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]]
Ars Veruna was first [[canon]]ically mentioned in the [[2014]] magazine [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 2|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 2]].<ref name="HC2" /> He was later mentioned in in Sio Bibble's entry from the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]. The text mentioning Veruna is reprinted from<ref name="SWCEUE" /> the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia]]'', written by [[Simon Beecroft]] and published in [[2011]].<ref name="SWCE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

In the Legends continuity, Veruna was first mentioned in the [[1999]] novel [[Star Wars Journal: Queen Amidala|''Star Wars Episode I Journal: Queen Amidala'']], written by [[Jude Watson]].<ref name="EpIJQA">''[[Star Wars Journal: Queen Amidala]]''</ref> He later made his first appearance in the [[2000]] short [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] "[[A Summer's Dream]]," published in [[Star Wars Tales 5|''Star Wars Tales'' 5]] by [[Dark Horse Comics]].<ref name="A Summer's Dream">{{Tales|5|A Summer's Dream}}</ref> Veruna was eventually given the first name Ars in the novel ''Darth Plagueis'', in which he has a prominent role.<ref name="Darth Plagueis" />

===Contradictions===
{{Quote|Even on my own Naboo, the king finds himself embroiled with the Trade Federation and off-world bankers with regard to our plasma exports.|Senator Sheev Palpatine, in flashback in ''Tarkin''|<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>}}
In 2016, ''Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded'' stated that Veruna was Amidala's predecessor.<ref name="SWCEUE" /> Other reference materials published in that year stated that his reign lasted thirteen [[Standard year|years]],<ref name="SWCL" /> and ended when she defeated him in an election.<ref name="HC2" /> Moving forward, mentions of Veruna are inconsistent with material published during and after [[2018]]. The [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 54|fifty-fourth]] issue of the magazine ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'', published that year, [[Retroactive continuity|retconned]] the previous information that Amidala had defeated Veruna in an election by instead stating that he had been [[assassin]]ated before she was elected,<ref name="HC 54" /> which is a plot point carried over from the [[2012]] Legends novel [[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']].<ref name="Darth Plagueis">[[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']]</ref>

The [[2019]] [[canon]] novel ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'', written by [[E. K. Johnston]], instead established that Naboo monarchs can only serve for a maximum of four years, or two terms lasting two years each.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref> The 2019 reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' placed Bibble's election to the position of Governor during Veruna's reign, but did not maintain Veruna as Amidala's immediate predecessor, or specify the duration of his reign.<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> The [[Database]] section of [[Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin 96|''Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin'' 96]], published that year, placed Veruna as being Amidala's predecessor, but being king after [[Réillata]] and her successor [[Ekay]].<ref name="OSWM96">{{GermanFanCite|96|Database}}</ref> Johnston's [[2020]] novel ''[[Queen's Peril]]'',<ref name="Queen's Peril">''[[Queen's Peril]]''</ref> which is set in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="QP date 1">The events of ''Queen's Peril'' take place immediately before and concurrently with the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]].</ref> stated that Amidala's immediate predecessor was [[Sanandrassa]], a single-term queen who was elected when Queen [[Réillata]] retired after one term.<ref name="Queen's Peril" />

''Queen's Peril'' placed the beginning of Réillata's first term in [[36 BBY]], and Sanandrassa's in [[34 BBY]].<ref name="QP date 2">Since ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' is set in [[32 BBY]], and ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'' establishes that one term for the [[Monarch of Naboo]] is two [[Standard year|years]], it can therefore be calculated that the two prior elections took place in [[34 BBY]] and [[36 BBY]].</ref> This dating is inconsistent with [[James Luceno]]'s [[2014]] canon novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']],<ref name="Tarkin" /> which is set in [[14 BBY]], and has [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] remember a conversation from "twenty years earlier," 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]]. The flashback takes place twenty years prior to the main narrative; if this is exactly rather than approximately twenty years, it can be calculated to [[34 BBY]].</ref> in which [[Senator]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] briefly talked about the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] king who ruled at that time: "''Even on my own Naboo, the king finds himself embroiled with the Trade Federation and off-world bankers with regard to our plasma exports.''"<ref name="Tarkin" /> The quote superficially describes events previously seen in ''Darth Plagueis'', which was also written by Luceno.<ref name="Darth Plagueis" /> However, it has not yet been confirmed in canon whether this king is indeed Veruna.<ref name="Tarkin" /> ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' later provided a list of Monarchs of Naboo, confirming Réillata ended her term and Sanandrassa started her term in 34 BBY, with no mention of Veruna.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[2022]]'s [[Star Wars Starships & Vehicles 21|''Star Wars Starships & Vehicles'' 21]], however, repeated the claim that Amidala succeeded Veruna, whom it claimed had abdicated his throne after a series of political scandals spurred on by his foreign policy decisions.<ref name="Ships21">{{ShipsandVehiclesCite|21|Pilots and Crew Members}}</ref>

''Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded'' and ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition]]''{{'s}} reference to Sio Bibble being elected under Veruna<ref name="SWCEUE" /><ref name="Character Encyclopedia New Edition">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition]]''</ref> was removed in the later ''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]'', which just states Bibble was elected under Amidala's predecessor,<ref name="Updated Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> who would have been [[Sanandrassa]].<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> Veruna's mention in ''Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition''<ref name="USWNE" /> was not present in its updated version, ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''. However it does maintain there were multiple monarchs between Bibble's appointment and Amidala's reign, meaning he was not elected under Sanandrassa.<ref name="Comprehensive Guide to the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Also, [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] mentions Veruna in the context of his [[Veruna's tower|royal library]],<ref name="SWCL" /> however the newer publication ''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]'' removes the mention of Veruna.<ref name="CLNE">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> Author [[Jason Fry]] described Veruna's existence in canon having "become a little tangled," and removing him from ''Complete Locations'' was deliberate.<ref name="Fry Interview">{{WookYouTube|-nqSqVOvfM4|Wookieepedia Interviews - Emily Shkoukani & Jason Fry}}</ref>
