The '''''Build My Droid Contest''''', sometimes just referred to as '''''Build My Droid''''', was a contest hosted as a part of [[Lucasfilm]]'s [[Wikipedia:Star Wars: Force for Change|Star Wars: Force For Change]] initiative in collaboration with [[Wikipedia:For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology|FIRST]], created with the express goal of inspiring innovators in [[Wikipedia:Science, technology, engineering, and mathematics|STEM]] and the creative arts.<ref name="Build My Droid">{{SW|url=news/kp-1-obi-wan-kenobi|text=How a ''Star Wars'' Fan Created a New Droid for Disney+'s ''Obi-Wan Kenobi''}}</ref>

Starting in [[2019]], [[The Walt Disney Company]] and Lucasfilm partnered with ''FIRST'', providing them over $2 million dollars in grant funding to boost accessibility to FIRST programs in underserved and underrepresented communities. This contribution was projected to increase access to ''FIRST'' programs by 15% internationally, reaching 658,000 youth worldwide, with the goal of inspiring the next generation of heroes, innovators, and storytellers, as well as bolster diversity within the STEM talent pipeline, leveraging ''[[Star Wars]]'' to excite and engage new audiences with the power of technological innovation and creativity. This contest was born from this partnership, with the added purpose of promoting the then upcoming film, ''[[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="Shorty Awards">{{WebCite|url=https://shortyawards.com/12th/first-star-wars-force-for-change-build-my-droid-contest|text=FIRST & Star Wars: Force for Change: Build My Droid Contest|work=[https://shortyawards.com Shorty Awards]|archivedate=20260528181919}}</ref>

Entrance was opened to individuals 16 years or older, with sketches, drawings, or paintings being acceptable artistic mediums. The contest ran for less than a month, accepting entries from [[October 22]] until [[November 13]], 2019, and was available to citizens of only ten countries: The [[Wikipedia:United States|United States of America]], [[Wikipedia:Canada|Canada]] (excluding [[Wikipedia:Quebec|Quebec]]), [[Wikipedia:Great Britain|Great Britain]] ([[Wikipedia:England|England]], [[Wikipedia:Scotland|Scotland]], and [[Wikipedia:Wales|Wales]] only), [[Wikipedia:France|France]], [[Wikipedia:Spain|Spain]], [[Wikipedia:Belgium|Belgium]], [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japan]], and [[Wikipedia:Mexico|Mexico]]. The panel of 5 judges included special make-up effects specialist Neal Scanlan, then Lucasfilm vice president [[John Swartz]], [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Pablo Hidalgo]], celebrity ''Star Wars super'' fan and STEM advocate [[Mayim Bialik]], and ''FIRST'' founder [[Dean Kamen]], who was also the inventor of the [[Luke Skywalker]]-inspired "LUKE Arm" prosthetic device.<ref name="Announcement" /><ref name="Official Site">{{WebCite|url=https://www.buildmydroidcontest.com|text=Build My Droid Contest|work=[[Build My Droid Contest]]|archivedate=20191212201422|nolive=1}}</ref>

Over 3,000 submissions were entered into the contest, with digital promotions regarding the competition receiving a collective 2.8 million views as of [[December 12]], 2019. Three winners were selected, one Grand Prize Winner and two Runner Up Winners, with the latter both receiving official 3D models of their [[droid]]s, a poster signed by the main cast of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'', and a personal letter of congratulations from Neal Scanlan. The Grand Prize Winner, [[Camille Manet]], would instead be invited to the official ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' world premiere, where their artwork was showcased on the red carpet.<ref name="Shorty Awards" />

Their droid design would also be fully realized by Scanlan and department, promised to appear in a future project that had not yet been announced at that time.<ref name="Official Site" /> It would later be revealed that Manet's submission, [[KP-1]], was featured in the [[Part V|fifth episode]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' television series,<ref name="Kenobi V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref> which was released on [[June 15]], [[2022]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-june-2022|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in June 2022}}</ref>

==Winners==
*Grand Prize Winner: [[KP-1]], by [[Camille Manet]]
*Runner Up Winner: [[T0-J4]], by [[Raye Yesaki]]
*Runner Up Winner: [[QT-5]], by [[Ana Gabriela Lopez Espinosa]]

Other entries were also publicly displayed on the contest's now defunct website for a time under a dedicated tab, though this page has not been preserved.<ref name="Official Site" /><ref name="View All Submissions">{{WebCite|url=https://www.buildmydroidcontest.com/gallery.html|text=View All Submissions|work=[[Build My Droid Contest]]|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref>
