'''Almakian apples''' were a type of [[apple]] from the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Almak]]. They were filling and could be used to make [[Almakian apple pie]]. The [[Prae'tus]] [[Volo Bolus]] and the [[Twi'lek]] [[Sornah Bocc]] kept several sacks of Almakian apples in their [[Foodstuff|food]] supply at [[Sornah's Garage]] on the planet [[Malastare]], although by around [[10 BBY]] Bolus had become sick of [[Diet|eating]] them.

==Description==
{{Quote|Almakian apples&hellip; cheap and filling&hellip; but mainly cheap.|Volo Bolus|<ref name="BV" />}}
Almakian apples were a type of [[Color|red]] and green [[apple]] from [[Almak]],<ref name="BV" /> [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Almak system]]</ref> [[homeworld]] of the [[Leffingite]] [[species]].<ref name="Archive" /> They were cheap and filling<ref name="BV" /> and were used to make [[Almakian apple pie]], a dish served in the [[Restaurant|eatery]] known as [[Dex's Diner]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Archive" />

==History==
{{Quote|I'm so sick of eating these apples. I'd give anything for a ronto wrap.|Volo Bolus|<ref name="BV" />}}
[[File:AlmakianApples-BeyondVictory.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=A sack of Almakian apples lies on the ground, spilling its contents onto the ground of Malastare.|Volo Bolus and Sornah Bocc had a supply of Almakian apples on Malastare.]]
Around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> the [[Twi'lek]] [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Sornah Bocc]] and the [[Prae'tus]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Volo Bolus]] had Almakian apples among their [[Foodstuff|food]] supplies at Bocc's [[Sornah's Garage|garage]] on the planet [[Malastare]]. Bolus [[Diet|ate]] enough of the apples, including as dinners, to become sick of them, longing for a [[Ronto Wrap]] instead. A small bowl full of apples was placed by a chair outside [[Sornah's studio]] where she sat for inspiration.<ref name="BV" />

After they lost a race on the [[Hydian-Hutta Podracing Circuit]], Bolus returned to the garage to find the sacks of Almakian apples from their supply half-eaten by a local [[Ba'zhu|vermin]] on the ground outside the garage. The rotten apples had been left alone, but the rest were more slobber than apple, which Bolus found disgusting despite the fact they had not wanted to eat the apples for another meal anyway. ==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Almakian apples were first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]'', which was written by [[Nicole Clubb]] and [[Katrina Pallant]]. The book only mentioned the apples as part of the name of the dish Almakian apple pie and did not establish them as a separate entity.<ref name="Archive" /> The apples then appeared independent of the pie in the  [[Industrial Light & Magic]] [[Star Wars video games|virtual reality game]] ''[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset]]''.<ref name="BV" /> The [[Almakian apple|apples]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were mentioned in "[[Dining at Dex's]]," a [[2009]] [[StarWars.com]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] exclusive article written by [[Gregory Walker]].<ref name="DaD">{{Hyperspace|url=fans/hyperspace/source/dexdiner/index.html|text=Dining at Dex's|int=Dining at Dex's}}</ref>
