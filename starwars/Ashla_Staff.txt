An '''Ashla Staff'''<ref name="UP" /> was a type of [[staff]],<ref name="LotL" /> often created from [[wood]]en branches gifted by [[Tholothian]] dignitaries, used by the [[Revered Masters]]<ref name="UP" /> of [[Lasat]] culture. These staffs held a spiritual purpose in Lasat culture, and had a special connection to [[the Force]]. A survivor of the [[Siege of Lasan]] named [[Chava]] carried one such staff on her journeys to return to [[Lira San]].<ref name="LotL" />

During the Siege of [[Lasan]], these staffs were plundered by the [[Galactic Empire]]&mdash;and some of those who survived sold the staffs.<ref name="UP" />
