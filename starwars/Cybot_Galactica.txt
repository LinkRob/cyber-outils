'''Cybot Galactica''' was a [[droid]] manufacturing company that most notably produced the [[3PO-series protocol droid|3PO]] [[protocol droid]]s<ref name="Rebels VG" /> on the factory [[Planet|world]] of [[Affa]].<ref name="USWNE" /> The famous protocol unit [[C-3PO]] was built by [[Anakin Skywalker]] out of scrap parts<ref name="Bio Gallery">{{Databank|c-3po-biography-gallery|C-3PO Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> from Cybot Galactica droids<ref name="Rebels Facebook 10-17">{{Facebook|username=starwarsrebels|url=starwarsrebels/photos/a.230754950414469.1073741828.180356405454324/367716480051648|name=''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''|date=2014-10-17|archivefile=File:StarWarsRebels-C-3PO-Facebook.png}}</ref> collected in a junk pile on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Bio Gallery" /> Cybot Galactica also manufactured several [[medical droid]]s such as the [[2R-series medical droid|2R-series]] and the [[AZ-series surgical assistant droid|AZ-series]] as well as analysis droids such as the [[JN-66 analysis droid|JN-66]] and the [[SP-4 analysis droid|SP-4]].

==Description==
[[File:TranLang_III_communication_module.png|left|thumb|294x294px|Cybot Galactica's greatest invention, the TranLang III communications module included in their 3PO-series droids]]
Cybot Galactica was one of the biggest [[droid]] manufacturers in the entire [[galaxy]] along with [[Industrial Automaton]]. It became extremely successful after its introduction of the [[3PO-series protocol droid|3PO series]] of [[protocol droid]]<ref name="Rebels VG" /> which revolutionized galactic affairs with their [[TranLang III communications module|TranLang III communication module]]<ref name="Rebels Facebook 10-17" /> which made them fluent in over 6 million forms of [[language|communication]] -including speech<ref name="TFA:VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and vocalizations.<ref name="SwKTW">{{StoryCite|story=She Will Keep Them Warm|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> Operating from the [[factory]] [[planet]] of [[Affa]], where 3PO units were constructed,<ref name="HC12">{{HelmetCollectionCite|12|Databank A-Z}}</ref> Cybot Galactica went on to design a variety of droids to aid the [[Galactic Republic]] in the [[Clone Wars|war effort]], including the [[IM-6 Battlefield Medical Droid]]<ref name="Protocol and Service Droids">{{EncyclopediaCite|Protocol and Service Droids}}</ref> and the [[IW-37 pincer loader droid]] used by the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 Actis-class interceptors]].<ref name="BR2D2">[[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 54|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 54]]</ref> After the formation of the [[Galactic Empire]], CG continued designing a variety of droids for them including the [[AC1-series surveillance droid]]<ref name="Cyphers">''[[Cyphers and Masks]]''</ref> and the [[WSW Mk III "Harrier" Combat Remote]].<ref name="Knights of Fate">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref> [[Setag Developmental Engineering]] was the research division for Cybot Galactica.<ref name="Master of Evil" />

==Products==
===Protocol droids===
[[File:C-3PO_EP3.png|thumb|250x250px|A 3PO-series protocol droid]]
Cybot Galactica's oldest -and most famous- product was protocol droids which they pioneered with their [[TC-series protocol droid|TC-series]],<ref name="Protocol and Service Droids">{{EncyclopediaCite|Protocol and Service Droids}}</ref> introduced to the galactic market before [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Introduction date">According to [[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]], [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace]] takes place during that date, and TC and 3PO-series droids already exist during the film</ref> These functioned as assistants and butlers to high-ranking officials, including [[Trade Federation]] [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]], whose personal assistant [[TC-14]] was one such model.<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> CG perfected the design with the 3PO-series, which had the same overall appearance but featured one of the most capable communications modules ever constructed, the TranLang III.<ref name="Rebels Facebook 10-17" /> 3PO droids also had more personality than their TC counterparts, being pleasant -if occasionally tiresome- companions to their masters.<ref name="Protocol and Service Droids" /> Ultimately and despite Industrial Automaton and [[Arakyd Industries]]'s attempts to corner them by introducing specialized models such as the [[BD10-series assay support droid]]<ref name="Disciples of Harmony">''[[Disciples of Harmony]]''</ref> and the [[RA-7 protocol droid]], Cybot Galactica dominated the protocol droid market in the galaxy.<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11">{{BuildR2Cite|11|Droid Directory}}</ref>

===Medical droids===
[[File:AZ-345211896246498721347.png|left|thumb|284x284px|An AZ-series surgical assistant droid, one of Cybot Galactica's medical droids used by the GAR]]
Despite being primarily known as a protocol droid manufacturer, Cybot Galactica also pioneered the field of [[medical droid]]s. Their products included the [[AZ-series surgical assistant droid]],<ref name="TCW:CE">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> which was used as an emergency response medtech in the Galactic Republic's [[Kamino]]an [[Tipoca City Military Complex|military facilities]].<ref name="Conspiracy">{{TCW|Conspiracy}}</ref> These droids boasted the pleasant if curious attitude of most CG products<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> and had a variety of useful tools such as [[fusioncutter]]s and anesthesia inducers for operations,<ref name="Conspiracy" /> [[scomp link]]s for [[computer]] navigation<ref name="Kamino Lost">{{TBB|Kamino Lost}}</ref> and the option to transform into a [[repulsorcraft]] for quick transportation.<ref name="Fugitive">{{TCW|Fugitive}}</ref> Another medical droid model manufactured by CG and used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] was the IM-6 battlefield medical droid,<ref name="Protocol and Service Droids" /> which was among the standard emergency equipment carried by [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]]s<ref name="Complete Vehicles">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> and [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s to attend to combat-caused injuries.<ref name="IncrediBuilds">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: AT-ACT Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]''</ref> Finally, CG manufactured the [[2R-series medical droid]]; this particular unit proved to be a failure leading CG to re-use most perts for its significantly more successful [[RH-series research droid]] used in bulk by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Fully Operational">[[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']]</ref>

===Labor and worker droids===
[[File:PKseriesWorkerDroid-NEGTD.png|thumb|311x311px|The PK-series worker droid, a typical example of Cybot Galactica's labor units]]
Apart from their more intelligent droid models, Cybot Galactica also specialized in the manufacture of [[labor droid]]s, autonomous drones which worked in jobs requiring manual labor on military and civilian industries. In particular the [[CLL-series]] saw great use by the GAR during the Clone Wars, with the [[CLL-M2 binary loadlifter]] model being used in military and naval facilities to load cargo on vehicles and [[transport]] [[starship]]s,<ref name="BR2D2 54S" /> while the IW-37 pincer loader was used to load more sensitive cargo in tight spaces, including Jedi officers' Eta-class starfighters.<ref name="SWVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Doctor]] [[Sionver Boll]] also modified some CLL units to study the [[Zillo Beast]] in its containment in [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Zillo">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast Strikes Back}}</ref> Later, CG began manufacturing [[WED Treadwell repair droid]]s,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> a more specialized maintenance droid to repair the turrets and combat units of the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Force Arena">''[[Star Wars: Force Arena]]''</ref>

For the private sector, CG manufactured [[PK-series worker droid|PK-]]<ref name="BR2D2 38">[[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 38|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 38]]</ref> and [[SE-2 worker droid]]s, with the former seeing use in the Trade Federation's [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter]]s<ref name="TPM" /> and later having most support and repair tasks in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]] relegated to them.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> The SE-2 on the other hand saw more use in remote settlements were it was used as more or less a general [[service droid]], earning the nickname "farm droid".<ref name="UVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

===Analysis droids===
[[File:SP-4AnalysisDroid-FFp55.png|thumb|356x356px|An SP-4 analysis droid used in the Jedi Temple Analysis Rooms|left]]
One of Cybot Galactica's more clandestine sectors of operation was the production of [[analysis droid]]s, exotic units specialized in analyzing objects and artifacts and tracing them back to an owner or manufacturer.<ref name="Protocol and Service Droids" /> These droids proved to be very popular with [[law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] and military organizations such as the [[Coruscant Security Force]] and even the [[Jedi Order]], the latter of which used both [[SP-4 analysis droid|SP-4]] and [[JN-66 analysis droid]]s in its [[Analysis Rooms]].<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> The Order even modified several of their SP-4 units to [[SP-4 ISC crime scene analysis droid|SP-4 ISCs]]<ref name="R2-D2 47">{{BuildR2Cite|47|Droid Directory}}</ref> to assist [[Jedi investigator]]s on their cases due to the [[Dark side of the Force]] clouding their prescient senses. One of these modified droids, [[Russo-ISC]], helped [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] with finding the perpetrator of the [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar]]<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

===Combat droids===
Following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the Galactic Empire, Cybot Galactica continued manufacturing military droids for the new government, including the [[AC1-series surveillance droid]], an intelligent [[spy droid]] programed to report directly to [[Imperial Officer Corps|Imperial officers]]<ref name="Cyphers" /> and the [[WSW Mk III "Harrier" Combat Remote]] which was equipped with twin repeating [[blaster]]s and could be extremely deadly when deployed in large numbers.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />

==Unofficial variants==
Due to Cybot Galactica's prominence in the galactic droid market, a number of custom units were built based on their designs. Most notable of these was [[0-0-0]], result of the combination of the [[Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix]] and a 3PO-series model's chassis. When the combination was correctly executed and the matrix was bonded with the host body, it modified the droid's hardware to include deadly weapons -including subdermal electric pulsers in the palms<ref name="Darth Vader 3">[[Darth Vader (2015) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 3]]</ref> and a [[proton charge]] in the chest-,<ref name="Misadventures">{{StoryCite|story=The Misadventures of Triple-Zero and Beetee|book=Darth Vader (2015) 20}}</ref> turning it into a sadistic [[assassin droid]]. 0-0-0 had multiple incarnations throughout the years, taking control of various droids' bodies and wreaking havoc from the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]]<ref name="Darth Vader 20">[[Darth Vader (2015) 20|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 20]]</ref> to the [[Imperial Era|Imperial era]].<ref name="Darth Vader 3" />

==Business model==
Cybot Galactica acted both as a manufacturer and a distributor of its droid models. For example, the 3PO-series was available for purchase directly from the firm, but CG also distributed ready-for-assembly kits for hobbyists who wanted to build their own droid at home or further customize it, leading to many parts being re-usable.<ref name="100objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> This particular trait of Cybot Galactica's products allowed young [[Anakin Skywalker]] to assemble a complete and fully functional 3PO, [[C-3PO]]<ref name="Bio Gallery">{{Databank|c-3po-biography-gallery|C-3PO Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> from spare parts of several CG droids<ref name="Rebels Facebook 10-17" /> lying in a junk pile in [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Bio Gallery" />

==Behind the scenes==
The reference booklet "[[Labor, Repair and Miscellaneous Droids]]" claims that Cybot Galactica suffered under Imperial nationalization after the Clone Wars, impacting its sales.<ref name="LaborRepair">{{EncyclopediaCite|Labor, Repair and Miscellaneous Droids}}</ref> However, the [[2025]] novel ''[[Master of Evil]]'' later revealed Cybot Galactica was actually a corporate partner to the Empire and benefited from its rule. Instead of being nationalized, it sold droids to the new regime and was even permitted control over former Separatist corporate assets.<ref name="Master of Evil" />
