'''Frax''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Kajain'sa'Nikto]] who lived in [[the galaxy]]. While being chased by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] on the [[planet]] [[Odona]], [[Han Solo]] left him for [[Death|dead]] in the [[Cordaxian Sea]] because the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was holding two [[Metric ton|tons]] of [[Cordaxian ore]]. Solo dumped Frax in the Cordaxian [[Ocean|Sea]] along with the [[ore]]. The [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] later enslaved Frax and forced him into labor on the planet.

On a shore by the Cordaxian Sea, he was eventually found by a [[Unidentified rebel (Odona)|member]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] after the Rebellion had bombed the shore to take out Imperial forces. Frax killed the Rebel and journeyed across the sea for four months. With hired mercenaries [[Selt]] and a [[Unidentified droid (Odona)|droid]], he tried to kill Solo and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] during their [[mission to Odona]], but failed. Frax murdered Selt and destroyed the droid when the two said they would leave. He left the cave he was in and went after Han and Leia, and died in a [[Water|snowstorm]], his frozen body poised to shoot Organa.<ref name="Annual 3" />
