'''Adrian Dunbar''' was cast to play [[Bail Prestor Organa/Legends|Bail Prestor Organa]] in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''The Phantom Menace'']], but during the production his character was changed to that of [[Bail Antilles]]. Ultimately the scene in which he appeared was cut from the final version of the film. In his [[2001]] novel ''[[Cloak of Deception]]'', author [[James Luceno]] described Dunbar's appearance when writing the character of Bail Antilles. Bail Organa was eventually played by [[Jimmy Smits]].<ref name="dbOrg">{{DB|character|bailorgana|Organa, Bail}}</ref>
