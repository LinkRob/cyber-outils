'''Davo Sculdun's party'''<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=news/andor-trivia-guide-week-2|text=Secrets from the Set of ''Andor'' Season 2: Week 2 Trivia}}</ref> was a party hosted by the [[Chandrilan]] businessman [[Davo Sculdun]] at [[Sculdun Tower|his office]] on [[Coruscant]] in celebration of [[Senate Investiture]]<ref name="What a Festive Evening" /> during [[3 BBY]].<ref name="Episode Date" />

[[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] was left as senator [[Mon Mothma]] and her [[Marriage|spouse]], [[Perrin Fertha]] arrived at the party. [[Major]] [[Lio Partagaz]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] was invited to the party but ultimately passed his invitation to two of his subordinates, [[ISB Supervisor]]s [[Lonni Jung]] and [[Avril Heert]]. During the party, Sculdun invited a select group of party guests to tour [[Davo Sculdun's gallery|his art collection]]. During the tour, two members of the group, [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] and senator Mon Mothma traded barbs over the [[Battle of Carmeen]]. At the same time as the tour, [[Kleya Marki]] was able to remove a [[listening device]] she had previously planted in the [[Tinian Codex]], an artifact in Sculdun's collection.<ref name="What a Festive Evening" />

==Behind the scenes==
The party appeared in the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''. It was first mentioned in the [[I Have Friends Everywhere|fifth episode]] of the season<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere">{{Andor|I Have Friends Everywhere}}</ref> and later appeared in the [[What a Festive Evening|sixth episode]],<ref name="What a Festive Evening" /> both of which aired on [[Disney+]] on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref>
