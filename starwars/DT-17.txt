The '''DT-17''' was a model of [[Battle droid|attack droid]]. They were floating, orb-shaped [[droid]]s which utilized a [[laser turret]].

==Description==
DT-17 [[Battle droid|attack droids]] were orb-shaped and floated above the ground. They were equipped with a [[laser turret]] with precise aim. The [[droid]]s could be summoned via a remote control device.<ref name="HNJ" />

==History==
The [[Kubaz]] [[bank manager]] [[Rikard Lovas]] used a DT-17 to attack [[Deputy|Deputies]] [[Drego]] and [[Streehn]] when they uncovered his plot to steal the electronic [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] of the citizens of [[Niima Outpost]] on [[Jakku]]. The DT-17 injured Streehn and shot a [[blaster]] out of Drego's hand before [[Constable]] [[Zuvio]] destroyed the droid.<ref name="HNJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
A DT-17 attack droid first appeared in the [[2015]] short story "[[High Noon on Jakku]]," which was written by [[Landry Q. Walker]].<ref name="HNJ" />
