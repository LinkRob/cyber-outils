A '''flame jar'''<ref name="Who Are You AD">{{Andor|Who Are You?}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> was an improvised [[weapon]] that consisted of a jar or bottle half-filled with liquid and capped with a cloth. In [[2 BBY]], the [[Ghorman Front]] member [[Dilan]] handed out several flame jars during the protest in [[Ghorman]]'s [[Palmo Plaza]] that would become known as the [[Ghorman Massacre]]. After the [[Galactic Empire]] opened [[Blaster|fire]] on the largely unarmed crowd, Dilan threw a flame jar at a group of Imperials that exploded in [[Fire|flames]], and the rebel was shot in the gut immediately afterward.<ref name="Who Are You" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flame jars appeared in the [[Who Are You?|eighth episode]] of [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''<ref name="Who Are You" /> on [[May 6]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref> They were identified as flame jars by the episode's [[Wikipedia:English language|English language]] [[Wikipedia:Audio description|audio description]] released on Disney+. In the real world, they are known as [[Wikipedia:Molotov cocktail|Molotov cocktails]].
