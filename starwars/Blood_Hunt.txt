The '''Blood Hunt''' was a coming-of-age ritual performed by the beings of<ref name="Beware the Nameless">''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''</ref> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Cathar system]]</ref> astronomical object<ref name="Darth Vader 10">[[Darth Vader (2017) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 10]]</ref> [[Cathar (location)|Cathar]] by the time of the [[High Republic Era]]. The [[Hutt]] [[Gender|male]] [[Churo]], who resided on the [[Hutt Space]] [[planet]] [[Nal Hutta]], was familiar with the ritual.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|BTN}}</ref> Churo mentally used the Blood Hunt as an example of coming-of-age rituals beyond those of the Hutts, considering himself lucky that he did not have to undertake it, while feeling pressured to prove himself to his [[Devirsivik Clan|family]].<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TheHighRepublic-BewaretheNameless-cover.jpg|thumb|right|200px|''The High Republic: Beware the Nameless'' marked the introduction of the Blood Hunt in the current ''Star Wars'' canon.]]
The Blood Hunt was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2024]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Blood Hunt/Legends|Blood Hunt]]<ref name="Redemption 4">[[Tales of the Jedi – Redemption 4|''Tales of the Jedi – Redemption'' 4]]</ref> first appeared in the [[Tales of the Jedi – Redemption 3|third issue]] of the [[1998]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi – Redemption]]''. The issue was written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]], illustrated by [[Chris Gossett]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Redemption 3">[[Tales of the Jedi – Redemption 3|''Tales of the Jedi – Redemption'' 3]]</ref> on [[September 23]], 1998.<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/97-376/Star-Wars-Tales-of-the-Jedi---Redemption-3-of-5|text=Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi – Redemption 3}}</ref> The capitalization of the ritual's name was provided in the lower case form of "blood hunt" within the series' [[Tales of the Jedi – Redemption 4|fourth issue]], also written by Anderson, illustrated by Gossett, and published by Dark Horse<ref name="Redemption 4">[[Tales of the Jedi – Redemption 4|''Tales of the Jedi – Redemption'' 4]]</ref> on [[October 28]] of the same year.<ref name="Dark Horse 2">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/97-388/Star-Wars-Tales-of-the-Jedi---Redemption-4-of-5|text=Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi – Redemption 4}}</ref> The upper-case capitalization of the event's name in "Blood Hunt" was given in the [[2009]] novel ''[[Coruscant Nights II: Street of Shadows]]'', the second installment in the ''[[Star Wars: Coruscant Nights]]'' trilogy, which was written by [[Michael Reaves]].<ref name="Street of Shadows">''[[Coruscant Nights II: Street of Shadows]]''</ref>
