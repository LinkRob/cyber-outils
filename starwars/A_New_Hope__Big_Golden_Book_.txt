The '''''A New Hope''''' [[Golden Books|Big Golden Book]] is a [[canon]] retelling of the [[original trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. It was adapted by [[Geof Smith]], with illustrations by [[Caleb Meurer]] and [[Micky Rose]]. The book was published on [[April 11]], [[2017]], in commemoration of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise's fortieth anniversary.<ref name="Penguin" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Star Wars: A New Hope is now back as a Big Golden Book&ndash;just in time for the 40th Anniversary of the film's release!''

''On May 25th, 1977, Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope was released in theaters. The groundbreaking film introduced the world to an epic battle between good and evil that took place a long time ago in a galaxy far, far away...''

''Luke Skywalker sets off on an important mission with Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi, smuggler Han Solo, Chewbacca the Wookiee, and the two droids R2-D2 and C-3PO. Will the heroes be able to rescue Princess Leia from Darth Vader and destroy the evil galactic Empire's ultimate weapon&mdash;the Death Star? Featuring stunning retro stylized illustrations, this book is perfect for Star Wars&ndash;and Big Golden Book&mdash;fans of all ages!''<ref name="Penguin" />
