'''Donovia''' was a [[Water|rainy]] [[planet]]<ref name="BMF50">{{BuildFalconCite|50|Starship Fact File}}</ref> located in the [[Donovia system]] of the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Hali sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where [[Technician|mechanics]] known as Donovian [[Rainman|rainmen]] maintained signal towers. During the [[Separatist Crisis]], some rainmen who had left Donovia became [[freighter tramper]]s and traveled on board the [[AA-9 Freighter-Liner]] ''[[Jendirian Valley]]'' as it carried refugees fleeing the crisis from the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] to the planet [[Naboo]]<ref name="BMF50" /> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], which includes the ''[[Jendirian Valley]]'s'' journey from [[Coruscant]] to [[Naboo]], to [[22 BBY]].</ref> The planet was situated in grid square O-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />
