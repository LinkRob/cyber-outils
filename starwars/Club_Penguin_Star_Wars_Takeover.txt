'''''Club Penguin Star Wars'' Takeover''' was an event held within [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] web-based children's [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online game|MMO]] [[Wikipedia:Virtual world|virtual world]], ''[[Club Penguin]]''. It began on [[July 25]] and ended on [[August 15]], [[2013]].<ref name="TSM" /> The event was initially announced in [[June 20]], 2013 on ClubPenguin.com, with [[StarWars.com]] posting an official cinematic trailer on their website in early [[July]].

The event featured many characters from ''[[Star Wars]]''; However, they didn't appear as actual characters. Some characters like [[Princess/Legends|Princess]] [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia]] and [[Darth/Legends|Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Vader]] appeared as costumes that were worn by ''Club Penguin'' characters, while other characters like [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] appeared on promotional materials, but did not play a part during the event itself. The story was largely a parody of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].

==Gameplay==
During this event, many special rooms were added that was based on ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Planet/Legends|planets]] and other familiar locations. The [[Original trilogy]], mainly {{Film|IV}} was the focus of the event, so special rooms based on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]], and the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|first Death Star]] were added, while the ''[[Club Penguin]]'' island was lightly decorated for the event.

''Club Penguin'' characters such as [[W:c:clubpenguin:Cadence|Cadence]], [[W:c:clubpenguin:Sensei|Sensei]], and [[W:c:clubpenguin:Herbert P. Bear|Herbert P. Bear]] portrayed [[Princess/Legends|Princess]] [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and [[Darth/Legends|Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Vader]], respectively. They were the only major characters that played a part in the event.

Despite the absent of most characters in the event, ''Club Penguin'' added a special ''Star Wars'' catalog where players could purchase iconic costumes and wear them. The catalog had costumes of [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]], [[Tusken Raider/Legends|Tusken Raider]], [[Greedo/Legends|Greedo]], and many more available. However, only players with an active membership subscription could purchase most of the costume. Players without a membership could only buy a [[Bandfill/Legends|bandfill]] and a [[Kloo horn/Legends|kloo horn]]. A costume of [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] was also added, but it was exclusive to purchase via the ''Club Penguin'' App.<ref name="Boba Fett">{{ClubPenguin|url=blog/2013/07/new-ipad-exclusive-star-wars-takeover-costume|text=New iPad-Exclusive Star Wars Takeover Costume!|nolive=1}}</ref>

[[File:STAR_WARS_TAKEOVER_PARTY_INTERFACE.png|thumb|250x250px|The party's interface.]]
Each week, penguins traveled to different parts of the galaxy and played a minigame to save the galaxy from Darth Herbert and the Galactic Empire.

During week one, players had to travel to Tatooine and defeat the enemy in the Defeat Stormtroopers minigame to receive a Stormtrooper Helmet. Playing more as a member gave players the Stormtrooper costume and Sandtrooper Pauldrons. During week two, players were required to travel to the Death Star and complete challenges to receive a Rebel Trooper Helmet. During week three, players needed to travel to the Rebel Base on Yavin 4 to participate in battle flying an X-wing and receive a X-Wing Pilot Helmet.

Members could buy more than twenty costumes from the Star Wars Catalog and had the chance to become Jedi Masters. To do this, they needed to equip Anakin's Lightsaber and duel with other penguins. Winning a battle would award an item; winning thirty battles would unlock more items.

==Continuity==
Despite their appearances in multiple promotional images and videos, characters like [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] only appeared in-game as costumes for players to purchase and wear.
