{{Quote|Ah, like beautiful candied nightjar petals as bookends to either side of me.|Xylan Graf, to Jordanna Sparkburn and Sylvestri Yarrow|<ref name="Defy the Storm" />}}
'''Candied nightjar petals''' were a type of [[candied petal]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFS}}</ref> the former [[deputy]] [[Jordanna Sparkburn]] and her [[Gender|girlfriend]], [[Sylvestri Yarrow]], kidnapped the [[scientist]] [[Xylan Graf]] while he was attending a party at the [[Starros family homestead|Starros Manor]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]]. When Sparkburn and Yarrow stood on either side of Graf to escort him, he told them that they were like beautiful candied nightjar petals.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

==Behind the scenes==
Candied nightjar petals were mentioned in the young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and [[Justina Ireland]] and published in [[2024]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref> Within the novel, candied nightjar petals appeared within a chapter written by Gratton.<ref name="ChapterAuthor">{{Bluesky|tessagratton.bsky.social|post/3lzypgmy75s2y|[[Tessa Gratton]]|date=2025-09-29|quote=Hi!
