'''Flob''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who worked in bureaucratic record keeping at the [[Imperial Bureau of Standards]] [[Imperial Bureau of Standards Coruscant office|office]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. In [[5 BBY]], he spoke with the disgraced [[Tac-Corpo|corpo]] [[Syril Karn]] in an interview arranged by Karn's influential uncle [[Harlo]], and after hearing about Karn's fall from grace in the [[Ambush on Ferrix|pursuit]] of the fugitive [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], Flob offered him a new start and a job.

After Karn used his new role to [[Hunt for Cassian Andor|investigate]] Andor further, Flob escorted members of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] to Karn's cubicle to take him away for questioning. Karn later returned and received a promotion.

==Biography==
===Offering a fresh start===
{{Quote|Morlana One.''"<br />"''Yes, uh&hellip; There's a rebel murderer running free because of corruption and laxity in the corporate authority. I was punished for trying to uphold the law. Do my job. Maintain public safety. Two men dead. Co-workers.|Flob and Syril Karn|audio=MorlanaOne-Flob-Karn-Announcement.flac|<ref name="Announcement" />}}
[[File:FlobKarn-Announcement.png|thumb|left|300px|Flob interviewed Syril Karn at the Bureau of Standards.]]
Flob was a [[human]]<ref name="DB" /> [[Gender|male]] corporate<ref name="Announcement AD" /> functionary within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Bureau of Standards]] who worked on bureaucratic recordkeeping for topics such as weights, gauges, redundancies, and verification.<ref name="DB" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' dates the events of "[[Announcement]]" to [[5 BBY]].</ref> he<ref name="Announcement AD" /> spoke with the influential individual [[Harlo]], who arranged for his nephew, the disgraced [[Tac-Corpo|corpo]] [[Syril Karn]], to attend a job interview at the bureau. Flob then met with Karn in the bureau's [[Imperial Bureau of Standards Coruscant office|office]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], [[capital]] of the Empire.<ref name="Announcement" />

As the pair walked across an<ref name="Announcement" /> elevated walkway within a sprawling warehouse-like office bullpen,<ref name="Announcement AD" /> Flob spoke about the different types of work available within the bureau. Looking down at his [[datapad]], Flob then stopped as he came across information about Karn's [[time]] on [[Morlana One]], the location of Karn's former employment before he oversaw a [[Ambush on Ferrix|disastrous attempt]] to arrest the fugitive [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]]. Karn quickly explained that he had been trying to uphold the law and maintain public safety by going after Andor, who had [[Death|killed]] two of Karn's colleagues, and that it was due to corruption and laxity within the [[Corporate Sector Authority]] that he had been punished.<ref name="Announcement" />

Once Karn stated that he planned to exonerate himself and have his record expunged, Flob smiled and offered to start the process by editing Karn's record before signing him in, noting that Harlo would likely approve. Flob then brought Karn to an empty cubicle pod and offered him a fresh start, revealing that he could fill an immediate opening in [[Fuel Purity]].<ref name="Announcement" />

===A visit from the Imperial Security Bureau===
[[File:FlobISB-Narkina5.png|thumb|right|300px|Flob (left) escorted the ISB to Syril Karn's desk when they wanted to question him.]]
Karn accepted<ref name="Narkina5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> the mediocre office job<ref name="Space Moms">{{InsiderCite|221|Space Moms}}</ref> and began working, but in less than a [[Standard month|month]] he had attracted the attention of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] after filing six false reports in an attempt to [[Hunt for Cassian Andor|investigate]] Andor further. When [[ISB Attendant|Attendant]]s [[Corv]] and [[Avril Heert]] of the Imperial Security Bureau came to the Bureau of Standards to bring Karn in for questioning, Flob accompanied the pair as they followed an [[MSE-6 series repair droid]] along the elevated walkway to Karn's cubicle.<ref name="Narkina5" />

Karn was then taken to the [[ISB Central Office]] for interrogation. After Karn proved useful to the ISB, [[ISB Supervisor|Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]] informed the Bureau of Standards that he had been of use to the Empire<ref name="Narkina5" /> and gave him a "clean bill of health," resulting in Karn's promotion.<ref name="Listening">{{Andor|Nobody's Listening!}}</ref> An image of Flob was included in the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]]'s [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was published at some point after the [[Battle of Exegol]] in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Fresh start, eh? New beginnings. We just happen to have an immediate opening in Fuel Purity. I'd hate to see you miss your chance.|Flob|audio=FuelPurity-Flob-Announcement.flac|<ref name="Announcement" />}}
[[File:FlobSmile-Announcement.png|thumb|left|300px|Flob helped Syril Karn find a fresh start on Coruscant.]]
When talking to Karn, Flob stated that there were no small jobs within the Imperial Bureau of Standards and claimed that any lifetime employee would agree with him, despite how much more interesting the [[Weights and Gauges]] sector might appear compared to [[Redundancy]] or [[Verification]]. After hearing Karn's story about Andor, the functionary quickly offered to help him despite the former corpo's past failures and claimed that he would hate to see Karn miss his chance at a new beginning. Flob had light skin and [[Color|graying]] brown [[hair]].<ref name="Announcement" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|I believe that we have laws for a reason. I fully intend to clear my name and have my record expunged.''"<br />"''Why don't we start that process now. Probably best for everyone to just edit this a bit before signing you in. I'm sure your uncle would approve.|Syril Karn and Flob|audio=Expunged-Karn-Flob-Announcement.flac|<ref name="Announcement" />}}
While at the bureau, Flob wore a [[suit]] consisting of a dark gray [[jacket]] and matching [[Clothing|pants]] over a light gray shirt with a black tie and black shoes. He used a datapad while interviewing Karn and had a white and red information tag on the left lapel of his jacket.<ref name="Announcement" />
