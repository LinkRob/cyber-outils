'''Burnium Ro''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[warlord]] who operated out of a [[Burnium Ro's stronghold|stronghold]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Ikkrukk]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. He used rhetoric and imagery associated with the [[Nihil]] raiders of old. The threat Ro posed led [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to send his protégé [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Ellian Zahra]] to [[Mission to Ikkrukk|eliminate]] Ro, lending her his [[corvette]], the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', for the mission.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> He shared his surname with [[Marchion Ro]], who was [[Eye of the Nihil]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Villains">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-the-high-republic-meet-the-villains|text=Inside ''Star Wars: The High Republic'': Meet the Villains}}</ref>
