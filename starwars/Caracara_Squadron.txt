'''Caracara Squadron''' was a [[mercenary]] [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] led by [[Zulay Ulor]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. Ulor and her squadron offered defense services to [[star system]]s that could not afford their own [[fleet]]s.<ref name="TROS VD" />
