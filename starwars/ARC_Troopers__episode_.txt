"'''ARC Troopers'''" is the second episode of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series' [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]]. It aired on [[September 17]], [[2010]] as one half of the season's hour-long premiere. It serves as a sequel to the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] episode "[[Rookies (episode)|''Rookies'']]," though it does not immediately follow the episode. It chronologically takes place in between [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|Season Three's]] seventh episode, "[[Assassin (episode)|Assassin]]" and fourth episode "[[Sphere of Influence]]."

==Official description==
''The Republic learns of an impending Separatist attack on Kamino, and Anakin and Obi-Wan hurry to the planet. After the Republic repulses the Separatists' diversionary assault, the real attack begins. Asajj Ventress, General Grievous and an army of droids rise out of the oceans, bent on destroying Kamino's clone production facilities. Rex, Cody, Fives, and Echo lead the clones in a desperate defense of their home planet''.

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|Echo, Fives, you're both officially being made ARC Troopers.|Commander Cody|<ref name="ARC Troopers" />}}
Forewarned by the destruction of [[Rishi Station]] earlier in the [[Clone Wars]], and a new intercepted transmission between [[Asajj Ventress]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]], [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] travel to [[Kamino]] to warn [[Shaak Ti]] and [[Lama Su]] of an upcoming Separatist attack, with clone troopers [[Rex|Captain Rex]], [[Cody|Commander Cody]], [[Broadside]], [[Echo]], and [[Fives]] of the [[501st Legion]] in their company. While Echo and Fives are walking down a hallway full of young clone cadets, remembering their own time as cadets, they run into [[Clone 99|99]]. 99 welcomes them back, but is saddened to hear that [[Hevy]], who gave him his [[Graduation medal|medal]], has not made it.

[[File:KaminoBlockade-ARCT.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Republic blockade of Kamino]]
Shortly, a Separatist fleet arrives and a space battle ensues. Anakin leads the Republic space forces against the attackers, scoring several serious hits against the enemy―or so it seems. Suspicious, Obi-Wan travels underwater, where he discovers that the pieces of debris falling into the Kaminoan ocean are actually components for [[Trident-class assault ship|attack craft]] being assembled by [[AQ-series battle droid]]s. The droids discover and attack him, and Obi-Wan barely manages to get away astride an [[aiwha]].

The finished attack craft begin to assault [[Tipoca City]], breaking through the walls and depositing their droid load inside, along with Grievous and Ventress. After a bit of bickering about which one of them is superior to the other, the two leaders proceed to attack two key targets: Grievous is to attack the barracks to kill all the clones he can find there. After a short battle, Colt is the only clone guarding the DNA chamber. Before he can fight, Ventress force chokes him and impales him with her lightsaber. Ventress heads for the main DNA storage to secure the prime DNA sample of the clones' template, [[Jango Fett]]. However, Obi-Wan has managed to return and recall Anakin to the city; while Obi-Wan faces off against Grievous, Anakin engages Ventress in combat. Grievous escapes when a Trident unit crushes the platform they are on; Obi-Wan seemingly falls to his death, but is saved again by the aiwha. With the aid of a [[clone trooper]] platoon, Anakin secures the DNA sample from Ventress, but she manages to hop onto Grievous' escape pod and get away.

Meanwhile, Fives and Echo destroy attacking Aqua Droids from a sniping position, where they are soon joined by 99 bringing them ammunition and a group of lost youngsters. They retreat into the barracks, where Cody and Rex also appear. Although their chances do not look good, Fives and Echo succeed in raising the morale of the young cadets, and 99 shows them the way to a nearby armory, where they re-equip themselves. A platoon of [[B1-series battle droid|battle droids]] soon attack the barracks, and by having the cadets strike from ambush, the troopers succeed in destroying all of their attackers, but 99 is killed as he tries to get more ammunition for his brothers. After the battle, Cody, Rex, Echo, and Fives mourn 99's death, and honor him as a true soldier. Soon afterward, both Echo and Fives are promoted to [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC troopers]] for their initiative and bravery.

==Continuity==
[[File:VentressKissColt-ARCT.png|thumb|250px|The first scene of the series to be censored by Cartoon Network]]
The scene where Asajj Ventress kills Commander Colt originally had Ventress kissing the ARC trooper just after she impaled him on her lightsaber. The scene was cut when [[Cartoon Network]] insisted on taking it out at [[Celebration V]].<ref name="BigShinyRobot">{{WebCite|url=http://www.bigshinyrobot.com/16255/did-clone-wars-get-censored|text=Did Clone Wars Get Censored?|author=Swank-mo-tron|date=2010-09-11|archivedate=20150608025712}}</ref> However, the scene was still part of a dataclip summary of "ARC Troopers" on Cartoon Network's own site.<ref name="Dataclip">{{CN|url=tv_shows/starwars/?episodeID=8a250ab02b058b7b012b21ac33e70271|text=Dataclip: ARC Troopers|na=video file}}</ref> In later reruns of this episode, Colt is not shown being impaled at all; rather, it is merely implied. The [[Star Wars home video releases|DVD/Blu-ray]], [[Netflix]], and also the [[Disney+]] release of the episode shows both Colt's death and the kiss, however, the iTunes release of the episode cuts the kiss.

An inconsistency in the episode was that the [[bridge]] of Grievous' command ship, which was a [[Providence-class Dreadnought|Separatist dreadnaught]], was depicted in scenes where it was shown as resembling the bridge of a [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]].

The holotable in Tipoca City shows the Separatist fleet having three [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleships]], however, these are absent in the scenes showing Grievous' ships.
