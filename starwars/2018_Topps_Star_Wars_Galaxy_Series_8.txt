'''2018 Topps ''Star Wars Galaxy'' Series 8''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] on [[December 9]], [[2018]]. It is eighth in the ''Star Wars'' Galaxy series of trading cards produced by Topps, which features ''[[Star Wars]]'' characters using artwork from a wide variety of artists.
