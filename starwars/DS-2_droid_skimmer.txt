The '''DS-2 droid skimmer''' was a skimmer operated by droids in the wake of the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Epic">{{KennerCite|set=The Epic Continues}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The DS-2 was a concept for a vehicle that would have been part of a proposed but ultimately rejected extension of [[Kenner Products|Kenner's]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' toy line, ''[[The Epic Continues]]''.<ref name="SWCA">{{WebCite|url=http://theswca.com/index.php?action=disp_item&item_id=50963|text=1985-86 Line Extension Presentation Binder Page 4 - Presentation Notes|work=The Star Wars Collectors Database|archivedate=20161118020546}}</ref> A presentation binder for the toy line mentioned but did not picture this vehicle, but a sketch was later published in the ''Hasbro Design Sketchbook Volume 2'' from [[2009]].<ref name="Evans">{{Instagram|username=mrstevie18|url=p/B2ZWM_bgFU8|name=Steve Evans|nobackup=1}}</ref>
