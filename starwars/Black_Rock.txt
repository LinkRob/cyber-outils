'''Black Rock''' was the first of the [[planet]] [[Varl]]'s [[Moons of Varl|three]] [[moon]]s. It was connected to the two other moons by [[Hutt]] pilgrimage tunnels in which the splinter faction of the [[Pyke Syndicate]] led by [[Baron]]<ref name="Pride" /> [[Grott]]<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|username=bryanQmiller|url=status/1836930481520050328|name=[[Bryan Q. Miller]]|date=2024-09-20|quote=Kayfoundo Kapa Barba Dim Grott Torrick Score (from Skor)|archivedate=2024-09-20|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/JEBs9}}</ref> built the [[Varl Circuit]]. They also created a [[Unidentified Black Rock city|makeshift city]] on Black Rock from which Grott ran the [[Varl Tournament]] while secretly [[Reproduction|breeding]]<ref name="Pride" /> [[Kayfoundo Kapa]].<ref name="Twitter" /> The [[Pyke]]s eventually abandoned the moon when it was overrun by Kayfoundo Kapa<ref name="Pride" /> in [[29 BBY]].<ref name="PrideDate">{{PadawansPrideDate}}</ref>
