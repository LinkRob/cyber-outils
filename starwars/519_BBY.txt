The following events took place in '''519 BBY'''.

==Events==
*[[Kallidah]]'s [[Archaeological Research Council]] establishes a [[Polis Massa Base|Research Base]] on one of the largest fragments of ancient [[Polis Massa]]. [[Kallidahin]] [[archaeologist]]s start surveying and excavating the whole of the Polis Massa [[asteroid]] belt for information on their [[Eellayin]] ancestors as well as other [[alien]] [[species]] via [[cloning]] skills they had learned from the [[Kaminoan]]s.<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref><ref name="Dating">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] states that the surveys of [[Polis Massa]] had begun for 500 [[Standard year|years]] by the [[time]] of [[Luke Skywalker]]'s [[Reproduction|birth]]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates his birth to [[19 BBY]], the Kallidahin surveys began in 519 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>
