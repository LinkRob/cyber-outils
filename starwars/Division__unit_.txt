A '''division''' was a measurement of [[time]]. Divisions made up [[local day]]s and were longer than fragments. On the [[planet]] of [[Vallt]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], one local day contained at least 18 divisions and one division contained at least 6.23 [[fragment]]s.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>
