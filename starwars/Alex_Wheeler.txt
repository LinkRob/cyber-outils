'''Alex Wheeler''' is the author of ''[[Star Wars: Rebel Force]]'', a series of six young readers novels published between [[2008]] and [[2010]] by [[Scholastic]], with book covers illustrated by [[Randy Martinez]]. Set in the aftermath of the events of the [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], ''Rebel Force'' focuses on the films protagonists&mdash;[[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]], [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]], and is one of the few [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novels set during the timeframe of the original trilogy that draws knowledge from the [[prequel trilogy]] films, which concluded in [[2005]].<ref name="TERC">''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]''</ref>

The novels also expand on story threads from the works of [[Jude Watson]], another ''Star Wars'' young readers author. In particular, the characters [[Ferus Olin]] and [[Lune Oddo Divinian]] from the ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Quest]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: The Last of the Jedi]]'' series of novels<ref name="TERC" /> were incorporated as major point-of-view characters.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Rebel Force: Uprising]]''</ref> Despite these connections, Alex Wheeler and Jude Watson are separate individuals.<ref name="Ratts">{{Twitter|username=judymblundell|url=status/1286827368464744450|name=[[Jude Watson|Judy Blundell]]|quote=Not true, sorry! Thank you for asking.|archivedate=20210407221011}} {{C|replying to: "@judymblundell Hi there!
