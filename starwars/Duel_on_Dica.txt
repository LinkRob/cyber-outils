A '''duel''' took place on the [[Acid/Legends|acidic]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Dica]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederate]] [[General/Legends|General]] [[Grievous/Legends|Grievous]] and [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]] [[Twin/Legends|twins]] [[Kaa (Dark Jedi)|Kaa]] and [[Lii]]. When one of the twins stole Grievous's [[Sith lightsaber/Legends|Sith lightsaber]] collection, the General followed her and confronted her and the other sister on Dica. A [[Lightsaber duel/Legends|duel]] ensued, during which Grievous, who was using his only remaining [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]], struck the stolen lightsabers out of the Dark [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Limb/Legends|hands]], accidentally causing the weapons to land on an acidic lake, destroying them. After the Dark Jedi twins quickly withdrew from the duel and fled with their lives, Grievous left the planet, resolving to start gathering Jedi lightsabers to replace the destroyed collection.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|It's true, sir! We believe they were taken by that Dark Jedi.''"<br />"''Yes, she was acting suspiciously.|An [[IG-100 MagnaGuard/Legends|IG-100 MagnaGuard]] and Grievous|<ref name="DarkJedi" />}}
In hopes of becoming [[Sith/Legends|Sith]], two [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]] [[Twin/Legends|twin]] sisters, [[Kaa (Dark Jedi)|Kaa]] and [[Lii]], decided to steal the [[Sith lightsaber/Legends|Sith lightsaber]] collection of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederate]] [[General/Legends|General]] [[Grievous/Legends|Grievous]]. After one of the twins discovered the location of Grievous and his collection, which consisted of four Sith [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsabers]], she successfully entered Grievous's [[Starship/Legends|spacecraft]] and partially convinced the General that she was on his side. Even though the Dark [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] raised Grievous's suspicions, she was able to steal the lightsaber collection and escape from the ship, after which she headed to the [[Acid/Legends|acidic]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Dica]]. However, the theft was quickly revealed, and Grievous pursued the Dark Jedi on Dica with his [[Soulless One/Legends|personal starfighter]].<ref name="DarkJedi" />

==The duel==
{{Quote|You're too slow, Grievous!|Lii|<ref name="DarkJedi" />}}
After landing on Dica, the General quickly spotted Kaa standing on a cliff over an acidic lake. When Grievous confronted her, Lii revealed herself, and the Dark Jedi duo raised the stolen Sith lightsabers. Though slightly surprised, Grievous remained confident that he would defeat his enemies in [[Lightsaber combat/Legends|combat]]. Armed with his sole remaining lightsaber, the General leaped onto the cliff. Grievous first grabbed both of Kaa's lightsabers with one of his [[Cybernetics/Legends|cybernetic hands]], and when Lii tried to attack him from behind, he grabbed her sabers with his other [[Limb/Legends|hand]], locking the twins in their positions. However, the twins quickly broke free from the lock and severed a part of Grievous's mask with a swift combined swing. Grievous jumped down from the cliff, attaching himself just below it with his legs, and declared that tricks would not save the Dark Jedi from [[Death/Legends|death]]. He jumped back on the cliff and disarmed the twins with one swipe of his saber.<ref name="DarkJedi" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Now begone! It will take more than a Sith weapon to make you a Sith!|Grievous|<ref name="DarkJedi" />}}
Much to the chagrin of the three combatants, the Sith lightsabers fell off from the cliff, right into the acidic lake, where they quickly disintegrated. With his Sith lightsabers now destroyed, Grievous was outraged, and the now-weaponless sisters chose to flee with their lives. As he prepared to leave the planet, Grievous resolved that he would have to restart his collection of lightsabers, only this time with Jedi lightsabers.<ref name="DarkJedi" />

==Behind the scenes==
The [[Lightsaber duel/Legends|duel]] first appeared in [[Dark Jedi (comic story)|''Dark Jedi'']], a short [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story that was included in [[Tokyopop's Star Wars Manga|Tokyopop's]] ''[[Star Wars Manga: Silver]]'' anthology. The original [[2006]] publication of the work was labeled as "[[Infinities]]," marking the stories contained within as non-[[canon]].<ref name="DarkJedi" /> However, comments made by [[Leland Chee]], keeper of [[Lucasfilm]]'s [[Holocron continuity database]], on [[StarWars.com]] later described the continuity of these stories as "a bit fuzzy," indicating their canonicity was ambiguous.<ref name="forums">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=980#15895005|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-11-06|time=6:03 PM|quote=The continuity of the manga comics is a bit fuzzy.}}</ref> In [[2008]], a French publication of the comic again labeled the stories as Infinities, thus securing their current status as non-canon tales.<ref name="French">{{WebCite|url=http://manga.bdfugue.com/media/catalog/product/cache/3/thumbnail/17f82f742ffe127f42dca9de82fb58b1/9/7/9782756012780_4_75.jpg|text=star wars manga ; silver et black|work=Librairie BDfugue.com|language=French|nobackup=1}} &mdash; Back cover of ''Star Wars Manga: Silver & Black'' French publication; note Infinities label in lower right corner</ref>
