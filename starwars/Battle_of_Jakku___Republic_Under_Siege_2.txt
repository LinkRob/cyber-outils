'''''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 2''' is the second issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege]]''. The issue was written by [[Alex Segura]], illustrated by [[Stefano Raffaele]] and [[Alex Sinclair]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 27]], 2024.

==Publisher's summary==
''LUKE AND DOCTOR APHRA JOIN FORCES TO FIGHT OFF A SURPRISE ATTACK AS THE REMAINING IMPERIALS PLOT THEIR NEXT ATTACK! LUKE SKYWALKER must battle his way through a gang of Force-focused zealots! A faction of Imperial survivors plot to return the Empire to its past glory &ndash; and hint at a huge struggle to come! Can LUKE survive against a giant&hellip;swamp rat?! As the defiant Imperial consolidates his power and allegiances &ndash; he discovers a traitor!''

==Plot summary==
On the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[swamp]] [[planet]] [[Lehanis]], rogue [[archaeologist]] Dr. [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] finds herself confronted by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[New Republic]] [[pilot]] [[Rynn Zenat]] after she stole a [[Jedi artifact]] from them. She quips about Luke's potential hard feelings after their [[Mission to Ktath'atn|previous meeting]] at the [[Citadel of Ktath'atn]] and tosses Luke the [[Ky Narec's Compendium|Compendium]]. Curious why she stole the artifact in the first place, he introduces Aphra to Zenat. Aprha then tells Luke she came to warn him. Before she can explain further, the trio is confronted by a massive [[Klyff'wat|beast]]. 

Elsewhere in the Outer Rim, over the planet [[Sauwecer|Souwecer]], the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Shadow Council]] meets to strategize. [[Counselor to the Empire|Counselor]] [[Gallius Rax]] addresses the problem to their larger plans being posed by [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] in the [[Anoat sector]]. Adelhard has been going rogue by circumventing the Shadow Council and consolidating his power, even expanding his [[Iron Blockade]] beyond the Anoat sector. [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] concurs, recognizing that in order to enact the [[death|late]] [[Emperor|Emperor's]] [[Contingency|protocols]], they must stop Adelhard. [[Grand General]] [[Kenner Loring]], however, secretly coerced into subservience to Adelhard by way of threats, attempts to pose a question to protect Adelhard. He asks if the plan is not to stop the New Republic from forming, what the true plan is. Rax shuts down the question and accurately suggests that Loring is in league with Adelhard. When Loring professes his loyalty to the Empire, Rax reminds all that they are in a new era and that alliances and loyalties must be reforged. He informs the others that the plan is to adapt and react, including to deal with the problematic Adelhard. Rax subsequently orders Grand Moff [[Randd]] to activate their agent in the Anoat sector and announces that the upcoming [[Battle of Jakku]] will bury the past and open the door to the future.

Back on Lehanis, Skywalker, Aphra, and Zenat run from the klyff'wat. Luke asks the others to hide behind cover in a nearby clearing, and Luke reluctantly slays the massive creature, hoping he can soon get back to where he is needed most. Now in safety, he asks Aphra what her warning is. The archaeologist tells Luke that she wanted to pay back a favor after their time at the [[Sason Temple]]. She informs Luke that the Compendium is a fake, and wonders what the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] are trying to distract Luke from. She reminds Luke that the Acolytes are not loyal to anyone but themselves, and that their collaboration with Adelhard is likely some sort of ruse. 

Just outside the Anoat sector, [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] fights in his [[Personal Luxury Yacht 3000]] along with [[Preeti Delmin]] in her [[T-65 X-wing starfighter|X-wing]]. Their forces decimated by Adelhard's assault, the two decide to escape. As they prepare for their [[hyperspace]] jump, Lando ponders Adelhard's uncharacteristic brutality; he is not behaving like an Imperial, even collaborating with the criminal underworld. This behavior makes him dangerous. Lando and Delmin jump away. 

On Adelhard's [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] [[Scepter (Imperial-class)|''Scepter'']], the Grand Moff is unbothered by the fleeing enemy, hoping they will share the story of his power. In discussing the ruse on Lehanis, the mystic [[Reyna Oskure]] admonishes Adelhard for his crass disregard of their alliance. Adelhard shifts the conversation to a discussion about an issue among their rank: a potential [[spy]]. He aims to handle that in the shadows. In the meantime, Adelhard has other plans. He bemoans what he sees as incompetence from the Shadow Council and decides to start gutting the Republic using his non-traditional approaches, such as the use of [[bounty hunter]]s. 

On [[Kewwil]], a [[Kel Dor]] [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] had recently recruited to join the New Republic, [[Mirim Kess]], runs through a [[forest]] from a frightening [[Trandoshan]] bounty hunter by the name of [[Kranor]]. The Senator eventually trips and is murdered by the bounty hunter. Kranor then informs [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] member [[Tomasso]] he will be heading to the next target. 

On Lehanis, Luke feels overwhelmed by the recent events and tries to rush to [[Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)|his X-wing]]. Zenat slows him down, hoping to discuss what is going on. Luke talks down to her, stressed about the many threats to their friends and their nascent government. He wishes he confirmed Adelhard's death after their recent fight, and is tired of being played. Ultimately, he fears it may be too late to stop Adelhard. 

==Continuity==
''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 2 features [[Gallius Rax]] using the title of [[Counselor]] as a member of the [[Shadow Council]] alongside [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]].<ref name="Under Siege 2">[[Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege 2|''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 2]]</ref> However, the novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' establishes that Rax only names himself [[Counselor to the Empire]] after parting ways with Sloane, with [[General]] [[Hodnar Borrum]] stating the title counselor had not existed in Imperial records before that point.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> Furthermore, the issue sees Sloane refer to Rax as "Counselor Rax"<ref name="Under Siege 2" /> despite her saying she has never heard him called that in ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>
