'''1MSE-KUP24''' was a [[KUP24 messenger droid]] that was [[Slavery|forced to work]] in the criminal [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s [[Spice mines of Kessel|spices mines]] on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. In [[10 BBY]], 1MSE-KUP24 was present in the [[Mining|mines']] control room when it was [[Raid on Kessel|taken over]] by a [[Beckett's gang|group of criminals]] who started a revolution against the [[Pyke]]s. Joyful at his liberation, 1MSE-KUP24 joined the revolt.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Follow me, compatriots!|L3-37 calls out to 1MSE-KUP and the other droids in the control room|audio=L3-Compatriots.flac|<ref name="Solo: A Star Wars Story" />}}
[[File:1MSE-KUP24_control_room.png|thumb|left|250px|1MSE-KUP24 (bottom) was one of many droids that worked in the spice mines of Kessel.]]
1MSE-KUP24 was the designation<ref name="SoloOG" /> of a [[KUP24 messenger droid]]<ref name="Kids" />that was one of many [[droid]]s [[Slavery|forced]] to work in the [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] controlled by the criminal [[Pyke Syndicate]] on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events on [[Kessel]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref> the [[Courier droid|messenger droid]] was present in the [[Mining|mines']] control center while a [[Beckett's gang|group]] of [[Crimson Dawn]]&ndash;affiliated criminals disguised as [[enslaver]]s met there with [[Pyke]] [[Administrator]] [[Quay Tolsite]] in an attempt to [[Raid on Kessel|steal]] a shipment of [[coaxium]].<ref name="Solo: A Star Wars Story" />

During the meeting, the criminal [[Qi'ra]] [[Death|killed]] Tolsite while her accomplice [[Tobias Beckett]] shot each of the [[Pyke sentinel]]s and [[Mining Guild]] supervisors present in the room. With the room now under the infiltrators' control, the criminal [[Pilot droid|droid]] [[L3-37]] removed the [[restraining bolt]] from the enslaved droid [[DD-BD]], who in turn began removing the restraining bolts from the other droids in the room. 1MSE-KUP24 whizzed around the floor of the control room<ref name="Solo: A Star Wars Story" /> at top speed<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> as the droids were freed,<ref name="Solo: A Star Wars Story" /> after which he joined the other droids and the organic slaves in revolting against the Pykes,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> creating a distraction that allowed the criminals to escape with the coaxium.<ref name="Solo: A Star Wars Story" />

==Characteristics==
1MSE-KUP24 had [[Color|gray]] and black plating with orange detailing and a black [[sensor]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> He was modified in order to work in the mines on Kessel and showed all the joy that a mechanical being of his type was capable of expressing when liberated on Kessel.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
1MSE-KUP24 first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo: A Star Wars Story" /> The droid was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" />
