The '''Aquilaris Classic''' was a [[podracing]] [[Racetrack|track]] that partly shared its name<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> with the [[Aquilaris system]] and [[Aquilaris Minor system]], both in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> By the [[time]] they participated in the [[Hydian-Hutta Podracing Circuit]]<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> the [[Neidorian]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Paghee'Dhe Dahglyn]] had won a race on the track, which was recorded on their downloadable [[Racer|racing]] profile.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Aquilaris Classic was mentioned in the [[2025]] [[Industrial Light & Magic]] [[Star Wars video games|virtual reality game]] [[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]].<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where the [[Aquilaris Classic/Legends|track]] first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']], a [[1999]] video game released by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="Racer">[[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']]</ref>
