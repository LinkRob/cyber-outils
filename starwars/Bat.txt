'''Bat''' were a type of creatures whose characteristics included wings,<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> a flat nose, and elongated ears.<ref name="KaleeshDB">{{Databank|kaleesh|Kaleesh}}</ref> Known [[species]] of bats included the [[chirodactyl]],<ref name="Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> the [[jogan-bat]],<ref name="Aftermath" /> the [[kinor bat]],<ref name="The Escape">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Escape]]''</ref> the [[Loth-bat]],<ref name="Battle to the End">''[[Battle to the End]]''</ref> the [[snow shredder bat]],<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> the [[Sand bat|sandbat]], and the [[Spintiri glowbat]].<ref name="Savage Spirits">''[[Savage Spirits]]''</ref> The [[silicon]]-based [[parasite]]s called [[mynock]]s were bat-like creatures.<ref name="Everything to Know" />
