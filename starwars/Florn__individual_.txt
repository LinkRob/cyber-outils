'''Florn''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] affiliated with the [[Galactic Empire]]. Originally a [[pilot]] with the Empire, his flying career was interrupted after a crash that left him severely injured. As a consequence of that accident, Florn was turned into a [[cyborg]], and he went on to serve as a [[Bartender|barman]] at the [[Zero Angle]], the pilots' [[cantina]] of the [[Bright Jewel Oversector Flight Base]].<ref name="LCZA" />
