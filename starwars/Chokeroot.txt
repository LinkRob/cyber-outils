'''Chokeroot''' was a type of [[Plant|root]] [[vegetable]] that could be consumed by [[human]]s, [[Artiodac]]s, and [[Togruta]]s. The root was an ingredient in the dish [[mashed chokeroot]], which was a component of the entrée [[Fried Endorian Tip-Yip]]. Chokeroot could be found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]s [[Batuu]] and [[Saleucami]], and it was known to be a family standard on some worlds.

The Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] felt that chokeroot was unsuitable for his cooking until he discovered a recipe for mashed chokeroot on Saleucami that he considered stellar. The revelation prompted him to reevaluate his opinions about the root, and he began serving mashed chokeroot and Fried Endorian Tip-Yip in his traveling [[Restaurant|diner]], [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|Tuggs' Grub]], while it was stationed on Batuu.

==Description==
{{Quote|Is this single stellar recipe enough to change my opinion about an ingredient I once despised? I dunno. Ask me after I scarf down another bowl or two&hellip;|Strono Tuggs, about chokeroot|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
Chokeroot was a type of [[Diet|edible]] [[Plant|root]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> [[vegetable]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> that was consumable by [[human]]s,<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" /> [[Artiodac]]s,<ref name="Cookbook" /> and [[Togruta]]s.<ref name="Edible" /> The root was relatively lacking in flavor on its own, despite being known as a family standard on some worlds. Chokeroot could take on a sweeter taste when combined with [[spice]]s and [[falumpaset cheese]] in the recipe for [[mashed chokeroot]], a dish that was a component of the entrée [[Fried Endorian Tip-Yip]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

==History==
{{Quote|You should have seen how packed this place was when he first arrived. I might have snuck off work early just to get an order of fried Endorian tip-yip or seven.|Julen Rakab, about Tuggs' Grub|audio=RakabTipYip.flac|<ref name="A Crash of Fate">''[[A Crash of Fate]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Mashed_chokeroot.jpg|thumb|left|230px|Mashed chokeroot was a dish that utilized chokeroot as an ingredient.]]
Mashed chokeroot was served during a [[Taffral family]] dinner in the [[Taffral house]] on<ref name="Aftermath" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Saleucami<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GEBSDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Fried Endorian Tip-Yip was served to [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] in the traveling [[Restaurant|diner]] [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|Tuggs' Grub]] while it was visiting the Outer Rim planet [[Batuu]].<ref name="Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref> The same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="ACOFDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[farmer]] [[Julen Rakab]] and his friends, the [[smuggler]] [[Izal Garsea]] and the [[musician]] [[Neelo]], consumed dishes of Fried Endorian Tip-Yip while at the same establishment.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

Until around the [[time]] that the [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] stationed his traveling restaurant, Tuggs' Grub,<ref name="Cookbook" /> in [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]]<ref name="Black Spire" /> on Batuu, he considered chokeroot to be an ingredient lacking in taste and deemed it unsuitable for use in his cooking. However, the chef eventually visited Saleucami and discovered a creamy mashed chokeroot recipe that he considered stellar and thus reevaluated his opinion of the root. Tuggs included the recipes for mashed chokeroot and Fried Endorian Tip-Yip in his [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was authored sometime after the [[Battle of Takodana]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The adventurer [[Eloc Throno]] mentioned mashed chokeroot as an ingredient in Tuggs' recipe in his book titled ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'',<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> also released that year.<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> Around the same time, Tuggs included his cookbook's mashed chokeroot recipe in a small pamphlet of recipes meant to help a Batuu visitor learn more about the planet.<ref name="Treasures">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Treasures from Batuu]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Chokeroot first appeared as an ingredient within the dish mashed chokeroot in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> Mashed chokeroot is a component of the entrée Fried Endorian Tip-Yip,<ref name="Cookbook" /> both of which were first made available to guests in the restaurant Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], a themed attraction that opened at [[Disneyland]] on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref>

The real-life recipe in [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]'s 2019 cookbook, ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', uses the [[Wikipedia:List of root vegetables|root vegetable]] of [[Wikipedia:Parsnip|parsnip]] as an ingredient in mashed chokeroot. The cookbook's recipe for Fried Endorian Tip-Yip also allows for [[Wikipedia:Mashed potato|mashed potatoes]] to be used as a substitute for mashed chokeroot.<ref name="Cookbook" />
