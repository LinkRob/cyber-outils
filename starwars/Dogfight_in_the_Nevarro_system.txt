During the [[New Republic Era]], a '''dogfight''' occurred in an [[Nevarro system asteroid fields|asteroid belt]] at the edge of [[Nevarro system|Nevarro's system]] around the [[Standard year|year]] [[9 ABY]].

==The battle==
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[High Magistrate]] [[Greef Karga]] had a [[Showdown on Nevarro|showdown]] with [[Vane]] and [[pirate]]s from [[Gorian Shard's pirate crew|Gorian Shard's pirate gang]] on [[Nevarro]]. Later, while traveling through the space above [[Nevarro]], Djarin told Grogu that there was more being a Mandalorian than fighting. He added that navigating was useful since no one never knew where one was heading next. Djarin showed Grogu a hyperspace map and told him he could determine his range by looking at the fuel gauge. The ship's proximity alert rang, warning Djarin about the presence of pirates starfighters.<ref name="Chapter 17" />

Vane sought to avenge the deaths of four of his brothers and reminded Djarin that they served the Pirate King Gorian Shard. Djarin responded that Shard should stick to hijacking and ransoming before pulling the accelerator. Djarin led the pirate starfighters on a chase through an asteroid field. One of the pirate ships crashed into an asteroid, but the others continued their chase. Djarin gained the upper hand and ran circles around his pursuers, shooting several starfighters down. One of the pirate ships crashed into a mining platform on an asteroid.<ref name="Chapter 17" />

Despite these losses, Vane drove Djarin's starfighter towards Captain Shard's warship. Pirate gunners aimed their weapons at Djarin's N-1 starfighter. Shard warned Djarin that he had guns trained on him. Djarin responded that he had no quarrel with him, but Shard reminded Djarin that he had just destroyed four of his starfighters. Shard demanded that Djarin surrender in return for sparing his life. Djarin told Grogu to never trust a pirate before pushing the accelerator, causing the N-1 starfighter to shoot past the bridge of Shard's warship. Shard growled in rage.<ref name="Chapter 17" />
