The '''Fighting Corps''' was a subgroup of the [[Children of the Watch]]. [[Din Djarin]] was rescued by members of the [[Death Watch]] from a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Battle on Aq Vetina|attack]] during the [[Clone Wars]], after which he was raised as a [[Mandalorian]] [[foundling]] in the Fighting Corps.<ref name="Chapter 8" />
