The '''Atrisian Emperor''',<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> also referred to as the '''Emperor of Kitel Phard''',<ref name="SWE" /> was the title of rulers who [[Politics|governed]]<ref name="CVD" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="WorldsTFA">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Kitel Phard system]]</ref> celestial body<ref name="CVD" /> [[Atrisia]]<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-09-15.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-09-15}}</ref> long before the [[First Order-Resistance War|armed conflict]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]<ref name="CVD" /> that began in<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> They shared their name with the [[Atrisian Basic]] [[language]].<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> The name of the [[Praetorian Guard]],<ref name="SWE" /> a personal [[bodyguard]] unit of mysterious origins<ref name="CVD" /> that was originally affiliated with the [[Imperial Remnants]]<ref name="SWE" /> and was active<ref name="Chapter 23">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 23: The Spies}}</ref> by [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoDate|23}}</ref> dated back to the reign of the [[14th Atrisian Emperor]].<ref name="CVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TheLastJedi-TheVisualDictionary.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Atrisian Emperors were introduced in the current ''Star Wars'' canon in ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary''.]]
The title "Atrisian Emperor" was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''.<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The [[2019]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' subsequently introduced the alternate name "Emperor of Kitel Phard."<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>

The title [[Atrisian Emperor/Legends|"Emperor of Atrisia"]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention in [[Greg Gorden]]'s [[1989]] ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'' that was published by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="ISB">''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]''</ref> In [[August]] [[1997]], the article "[[From the Files of Corellia Antilles]]," which was authored by [[Timothy S. O'Brien|Timothy O'Brien]] and published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 14|fourteenth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'' magazine, identified the title as the Atrisian Emperor.<ref name="Files">{{JournalCite|14|From the Files of Corellia Antilles}}</ref> The [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' featured the first illustration of an Atrisian Emperor&mdash;[[Uueg Tching]]&mdash;which was done by [[Chris Trevas]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
