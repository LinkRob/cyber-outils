The '''First Order Navy''', also referred to as the '''First Order fleet''', was the [[Navy|naval]] branch of the [[First Order]] [[First Order military|military]]. Consisting primarily of [[Star Destroyer]]s and [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]], the First Order fleet proved to be paramount during the regime's gradual conquest and colonization of the [[Unknown Regions]].

Following the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|annihilation]] of the [[New Republic]] in [[34 ABY]], the naval forces of the First Order were deployed from within the Unknown Regions in an attempt to seize military control of [[the galaxy]]. One year later, following [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren's]] [[Battle of Oetchi|ascension]] to the position of [[Supreme Leader]], the fleet was on track to be bolstered by the might of the [[Final Order]], a massive armada of [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] built by the [[Sith Eternal]].

==History==
===Origins===
[[File:ExecutorEscort-BTMF27.png|thumb|left|250px|The Imperial Navy was the naval arm of the Galactic Empire as well as the precursor of the First Order Navy.]]
Following the Galactic Empire's decisive defeat at the [[Battle of Jakku]] and its subsequent surrender to the [[New Republic]] with the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]], the Empire reformed into a military junta known as the [[First Order]] which would eventually cement its political sphere in the galactic frontier known as the Unknown Regions. There, away from prying New Republic eyes, the First Order would rapidly expand its military and research some of the former Empire's greatest military secrets in a bid to return to power. Via [[Project Resurrection]], the First Order Navy would end up constructing hundreds of vessels within the Unknown Regions, including the [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought]]. The First Order possessed enough capital ships to reclaim control of the entire galaxy as of the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

About [[21 ABY|thirteen years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[Hosnian system]],<ref name=" Bloodline note">In [[Claudia Gray]]'s [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']], it is mentioned that the First Order had begun channeling funds into [[Rinnrivin Di's cartel]] seven years before the events of the novel, which takes place six years before the events of {{Film|VII}}</ref> the First Order began channeling billions of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] into the hitherto minor [[Nikto]] [[crime lord]] [[Rinnrivin Di]]'s [[Rinnrivin Di's cartel|cartel]] through a militia group called the [[Amaxine warriors (New Republic Era)|Amaxine warriors]]. Rinnrivin's cartel was involved in smuggling and gambling activities in New Republic space with the Amaxines serving as their financiers and partners. In addition, Rinnrivin's cartel and the Amaxines raided legitimate commercial shipping and extorted protection money from [[merchant]]s. The proceeds were funneled to shadow corporations in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and [[Centrists|Centrist]] sources like [[Lady]] [[Carise Sindian]], who transferred the funds back to the First Order, which used it to rebuild the Old Imperial fleet, which became the basis for the First Order's fleet.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>

Although impressive on its own terms, the First Order Navy was nevertheless a mere fraction of that of the [[Imperial Navy]] that acted as its basis.<ref name="TROS VD" />

===Road to war===
{{Quote|You will see how the First Order has created state-of-the-art TIE fighters, attack craft, and new classes of Star Destroyers that not only enforce its will in First Order space, but also threaten neutral worlds in the galactic borderlands.|[[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]|<ref name="The Last Jedi: Bomber Command">''[[The Last Jedi: Bomber Command]]''</ref>}}
[[File:TFA_FirstOrder_Star_Destroyer.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer served as the mainstay warship of the First Order Navy.]]
Roughly thirty years after Endor, the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] would represent the Order's newest vessel, however older models such as the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class]] continued to see service in the First Order's Navy, and would be maintained by unmanned repair drones.<ref name="Before the Awakening" />

During the [[Cold War]], First Order forces engaged in skirmishes with both the New Republic and the [[Resistance]], a private paramilitary force that had been established by [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to keep an eye on the First Order's movements. In [[34 ABY|33 ABY]], a First Order boarding party backed by [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] [[Suraz engagement|captured]] the freighter ''[[Yissira Zyde]]'' near [[Suraz 4]]. The New Republic [[Rapier Squadron]] led by [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] responded but were unable to stop the heist. Later, Dameron undertook a [[Skirmish in OR-Kappa-2722|rogue mission]] to find the ''Yissira Zyde'' and encountered several First Order capital ships including three [[Star Destroyer]]s, four [[Lancer-class frigate|''Lancer''-class frigates]], two [[Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser|''Maxima-A'' class heavy cruisers]], and one [[Dissident-class light cruiser|''Dissident''-class light cruiser]].<ref name="Before the Awakening" />

In response to [[Operation: Sabre Strike]], the First Order fleet deployed several ships including ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer and a [[Nebulon-K frigate]] to prevent the Resistance from capturing [[Senator]] [[Erudo Ro-Kiintor]]'s luxury yacht ''[[Hevurion Grace]]'', which carried evidence of the senator's collusion with the First Order. Despite their efforts, Dameron managed to escape with the ship back to the Resistance. As a result, the Resistance obtained information about the explorer [[Lor San Tekka]], who held the key to finding [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Before the Awakening" />

During the [[attack on Demir]], First Order starfighter forces engaged in battle against the [[Demirian]] in support of First Order ground forces. After [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] lured the Demerians out of their [[Demirian stronghold|stronghold]] by using her forces as bait, a starfighter assault commenced and destroyed all forces.<ref name="Age of Resistance - Phasma">[[Age of Resistance - Captain Phasma 1|''Age of Resistance - Captain Phasma'' 1]]</ref>

[[File:FO_Navy_SWCT.png|thumb|left|220px|The TIE line survived the Empire's demise, spawning next-generation starfighters more advanced than their Imperial precursors.]]
During the First Order's activities, naval forces [[Blockade of the Otomok system|blockaded]] the [[Otomok system]] and at least three ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers were sent to [[Hays Minor]] to take over the [[planet]]'s mines and [[OreDigger]]s were deployed. During the occupation, rebels including the [[Tico family]] fought back against the First Order, although the planet was ultimately destroyed.<ref name="Age of Resistance - Rose">[[Age of Resistance - Rose Tico 1|''Age of Resistance - Rose Tico'' 1]]</ref>

During the [[hunt for Lor San Tekka]], the First Order fleet sent the ''Maxima A''-class heavy cruiser ''[[Ravenous]]'' to assist [[First Order Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Terex]] in finding the explorer first. The ''Ravenous'' and its TIE fighters clashed with Poe's Black Squadron in a [[Mission to Ovanis|dogfight]] above Ovanis.<ref name="PD 2">[[Poe Dameron 2|''Poe Dameron'' 2]]</ref> After Black Squadron left with information from the [[Unidentified Crèche elder|Crèche elder]], Terex and his surviving troops were allowed to radio the ''Ravenous'' for a pick-up. The Resistance fighters allowed them to leave in order to avoid provoking a way between the First Order and the New Republic.<ref name="Poe Dameron 3">[[Poe Dameron 3|''Poe Dameron'' 3]]</ref>

In [[33 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Kylo Ren led an invasion on [[Benath]] to stop their [[Benathy expansion|expansion]] in the region. [[Unidentified First Order admiral|An admiral]] participated with Ren, while a First Order starship supported ground troops. The battle ultimately resulted in a First Order victory.<ref name="Age of Resistance - Kylo Ren">[[Age of Resistance - Kylo Ren 1|''Age of Resistance - Kylo Ren'' 1]]</ref>

Later, a Maxima-A class heavy cruiser led by Commander [[Malarus]] was sent to chastise Agent Terex, for embarking on an errant campaign to destroy the Resistance. Malarus' cruiser emerged towards the end of Terex's [[Battle over a desert planet|battle]] with Poe's Black Squadron and destroyed Terex's starship ''[[Carrion Spike]]''. The heavy cruiser's TIE fighters then wiped out the remainder of the [[Ranc Gang]]'s "[[Ugly|Uglies]]" before dispatching a landing party to apprehend Terex.<ref name="Poe Dameron 13">[[Poe Dameron 13|''Poe Dameron'' 13]]</ref>

Prior to the Hosnian Cataclysm, the ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer ''[[Finalizer]]'' traveled to [[Jakku]] to [[Attack on Tuanul|attack]] [[Tuanul]] village, where Poe was meeting Lor San Tekka. After Poe escaped with the defecting stormtrooper [[Finn]], the ''Finalizer'' engaged in a brief dogfight with their stolen [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]], which crashed on Jakku. Despite sending a landing party, Finn managed to escape with the scavenger [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and the [[BB-series astromech droid]] [[BB-8]], who was carrying the [[map to Skywalker]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

===Counterattack against the Resistance===
{{Quote|The New Republic's home fleet is destroyed, and its surviving senators have dissolved the remaining task forces to protect their homeworlds. Their division makes them defenseless. No power in the galaxy can stand against us, Supreme Leader.|Armitage Hux, to Snoke|<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Mandator_IV-class_Siege_Dreadnought.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Following the destruction of Starkiller Base, the First Order deployed a fleet against the Resistance at D'Qar.]]
Shortly after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]], the Star Destroyer ''[[Retribution]]'' participated in the [[Battle of Kestro City|Battle of Kestro]] along with its starfighter complement, although the ''Retribution'' was [[Skirmish on the Retribution|infiltrated]] by the Resistance and eventually forced out of hyperspace near the Battle of [[Starkiller Base]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> After the destruction of Starkiller, surviving First Order elements regrouped near a terrestrial body, including the ''Finalizer'',<ref name="Captain Phasma, Part IV">[[Captain Phasma 4|''Captain Phasma'' 4]]</ref> and the First Order Navy began a full [[First Order-Resistance War|invasion]] of the galaxy with Rey estimating that the First Order would control all major systems within weeks.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Around this time, the ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer ''[[Thunderer]]'' was [[Battle of Castilon|sent]] to the [[planet]] [[Castilon]] to secure the ''[[Colossus]]'' after Resistance sympathizers managed to overthrow the First Order [[Colossus garrison|garrison]] there. Ultimately, the ''Colossus'' escaped into [[hyperspace]] with Resistance members onboard.<ref name="No Escape: Part 2">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 2}}</ref>

Before the Resistance could fully [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuate]] [[D'Qar]], a First Order fleet of three ''Resurgent''-class Battlecruisers under General [[Armitage Hux]] entered the [[Ileenium system|system]], and were joined by the ''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought ''[[Fulminatrix]]'', which destroyed the [[Resistance base]]. During the battle, the ''Fulminatrix'' was destroyed by the Resistance [[Cobalt Squadron]], although the squadron was destroyed in the processes. Though the Resistance fled into [[hyperspace]], the First Order was able to use a [[hyperspace tracker]] to track them down.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:Supremacy_out_of_hyperspace.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A fleet of First Order Star Destroyers accompanied the ''Supremacy'' as it hunted the Resistance through hyperspace.]]
A larger First Order fleet consisting of thirty<ref name="TLJ VD" /> ''Resurgent''-class ships led by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]'s [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]'' ambushed the Resistance fleet. Launching an [[Battle of Oetchi|attack]] on the fleet, the First Order reduced the Resistance forces to three capital ships and dispatched [[Kylo Ren's personal squadron]], whose attack run killed several command crew, including [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]]. For the next eighteen [[Standard hour|hours]], the First Order pursued the Resistance through [[Realspace|space]], waiting for them to run out of [[fuel]]. The Resistance fighters evacuated on a fleet of thirty [[U-55 orbital loadlifter]]s to the planet [[Crait]]. However, the [[slicer]] [[DJ]] exposed the Resistance's plan and the First Order bombarded the loadlifters, destroying all but six.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:HoldoManeuver-TLJ.png|thumb|right|250px|The ''Raddus'' crashed through the ''Supremacy'' at lightspeed, destroying the First Order's mobile capital in the process.]]
In response, [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] ploughed the [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']], the Resistance [[flagship]], into the ''Supremacy'', seriously damaging the vast warship and destroying twenty other ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers. Meanwhile, Snoke was killed by his disciple [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], who claimed the title of Supreme Leader. Ren then led First Order ground forces against the Resistance during the [[Battle of Crait]] but failed to destroy the rebels due to Luke's intervention.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Conquering the galaxy===
{{Quote|Are those?''"''<br />''"''Capital ships.''"''<br />''"''Enough to reclaim control over the entire galaxy.|Shriv Suurgav, Zay Versio, and Iden Veriso|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
[[File:FirstOrderOverMonCala-Allegiance4.png|thumb|left|220px|Entire worlds fell to the First Order as its navy conducted a campaign of conquest across the galaxy.]]
Despite the loss of the contingent of ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers and the ''Supremacy'',<ref name="Episode VIII" /> the ''Finalizer'' was intact.<ref name="Allegiance 1">[[Allegiance 1|''Allegiance'' 1]]</ref> The First Order launched an offensive on the galaxy, with the Maxima-A class [[heavy cruiser]] ''[[Fortitude]]'' participating in the [[Battle of Ikkrukk]], although it was destroyed.<ref name="Poe Dameron 30">[[Poe Dameron 30|''Poe Dameron'' 30]]</ref> The ''Finalizer'' was later part of a [[flotilla]] consisting of at least seventeen ''Resurgent''-class ships and a ''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought that chased the remnants of the Resistance. Evidence was found that the Resistance received aide on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet of [[Tah'Nuhna]], and as such, the flotilla made its way there and destroyed the planet via bombardment by starships and [[TIE/se Bomber]]s, with [[First Order snowtrooper|snowtroopers]] slaughtering civilians.<ref name="Allegiance 1" />

A short time later, a fleet of at least twelve ''Resurgent''-class warships arrived over [[Mon Cala]] acting on intelligence that the Resistance was there. After killing [[General]] [[Nossor Ri]], a fleet of Mon Cala warships led by [[Aftab Ackbar]] managed to escape the planet's surface from the far side and jump to hyperspace, although the First Order managed to destroy a Nebulon-C escort frigate. Now knowing that Mon Cala supported the Resistance, the First Order prepared to make an example of Mon Cala.<ref name="Allegiance 4">[[Allegiance 4|''Allegiance'' 4]]</ref>

Around some time after, the First Order [[light cruiser]] ''[[Ladara Vex]]'' was [[Mission to Minfar|dispatched]] to [[Minfar]] in order to obtain the [[Echo Horn]]. Despite receiving reinforcements via a ''Resurgent''-class, the ''Ladara Vex'' was destroyed by Resistance members and Resistance sympathizers while a fleet of starships sent by Aftab arrived and destroyed the Star Destroyer.<ref name="Spark of the Resistance">''[[Spark of the Resistance]]''</ref> Some time after, the ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer ''Finalizer'' was [[Battle of Batuu|attacked]] by a Resistance fleet that consisted of various [[capital ship]]s and starfighters over the planet [[Batuu]].<ref name="Rise of the Resistance">''[[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance]]''</ref> After the ''Finalizer'' sustained serious damage during the battle, the [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] became the Supreme Leader's new flagship.<ref name="TROS VD" />

[[File:AeosBombardment-TheEscape.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The First Order Navy attacked Aeos Prime for sheltering members of the Resistance.]]
During the conflict with the Resistance, the ''[[Thunderer]]''&mdash;a ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer under the command of [[Commander]] [[Pyre]] and [[Agent]] [[Tierny]]&mdash;was dispatched by Captain Phasma to [[Hunt for the Colossus|hunt down]] the mobile refueling platform ''[[Colossus]]''.<ref name="Into the Unknown">{{Resistance|Into the Unknown}}</ref> The inhabitants of the ''Colossus'' led by [[Jarek Yeager]] and [[Captain]] [[Imanuel Doza]] evaded First Order fleet forces on several occasions at various locations including the [[Ileenium system]] and [[Aeos Prime]].<ref name="AQSR">{{Resistance|A Quick Salvage Run}}</ref><ref name="The Engineer">{{Resistance|The Engineer}}</ref><ref name="Rendezvous Point">{{Resistance|Rendezvous Point (episode)}}</ref><ref name="No Place Safe">{{Resistance|No Place Safe}}</ref> A sizable First Order fleet later devastated the surface of the planet Aeos Prime to punish the indigenous [[Aeosian]]s for sheltering the ''Colossus'', prompting [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|First Order TIE Fighter Pilot]] [[Tamara Ryvora]] to defect to [[Team Colossus|Team ''Colossus'']], who had by then joined the Resistance. Pyre and Tierny's Star Destroyer and forces were [[Battle of Barabesh|annihilated]] by the combined forces of the ''Colossus'' and Resistance in the [[Barabesh system]].<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref>

===The Final Order===
{{Quote|Forgive me, sir, but these allies on Exegol&hellip; they sound like a cult. Conjurers and soothsayers.''"<br />"''They've conjured legions of Star Destroyers. The Sith fleet will increase our resources ten-thousandfold.|Domaric Quinn and Enric Pryde|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:Xyston-engines.png|thumb|left|250px|The Final Order was a vast armada composed of ''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers, planet-destroying warships meant to force the galaxy into submission.]]
In the year [[35 ABY]], First Order TIE/sf and [[TIE/wi Interceptor|TIE/wis]] were deployed in action against a Resistance team led by Poe Dameron that obtained invaluable intelligence from the Resistance informant [[Boolio]] at the [[Sinta Glacier Colony]], who had obtained them from the disaffected General [[Armitage Hux]]. First Order forces failed to prevent the Resistance team from escaping back to the Resistance base on [[Ajan Kloss]], sustaining several casualties. As a result of this intelligence leak, the Resistance learned that [[Darth Sidious]] had returned from the dead with a Sith armada known as the [[Final Order]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

Supreme Leader Kylo Ren confronted the Emperor on [[Exegol]], where he discovered the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] that comprised the Final Order. Sidious offered to make Ren his successor, granting him control of the fleet in exchange for killing Rey. While some within the First Order welcomed the opportunity to augment their forces, others distrusted the [[Sith Eternal]], a group of [[Sith cultist]]s who served the Emperor and created the Final Order. General [[Domaric Quinn]], a member of the [[First Order Supreme Council]], objected to affiliating the First Order with a [[Sith cult]], whereas [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]] favored the additional resources that their allies would bring to the First Order.<ref name="TROS VD" /> Ren regarded the Sith fleet as an opportunity to transform the First Order into a true empire,<ref name="Episode IX" /> thereby restoring military rule to the galaxy.<ref name="TROS VD" />

[[File:Uprising_on_Cloud_City.png|thumb|250px|The Sith Eternal's destruction inspired the galaxy to rise up against the remaining forces of the First Order.]]
Following Ren's redemption, the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]], along with Pryde, were defeated by the Resistance and the [[Citizens' Fleet]] at the [[Battle of Exegol]]. In the wake of the Emperor's second death, various worlds [[Uprising against the First Order|rose]] [[Uprising on Cloud City|up]] [[Uprising on Endor|against]] [[Uprising on Jakku|the First Order]]. First Order warships were downed on [[Endor]], [[Jakku]], and [[Bespin]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Personnel==
[[File:FOTIEPilot-Matchless.png|thumb|left|180px|First Order TIE fighter pilots (pictured) were not as expendable as their Imperial predecessors]]
In order to operate their vessels, the First Order Navy utilized personnel ranging from TIE fighter pilots to [[First Order fleet gunner]]s.<ref name="Episode VII" /> First Order TIE Fighter pilots were treated as important parts of the First Order's war machine, with potential pilots being identified as [[Youngling|children]] and given rigorous training, much of the time within the corridors of warships where they would then be tested ruthlessly throughout their careers to ensure their reflexes, visual acuity and coordination measure up.<ref name="First Order TIE Fighter Pilots">{{Databank|first-order-tie-fighter-pilots|First Order TIE Fighter Pilots}}</ref> The First Order was also open to recruiting adults to become TIE pilots.<ref name="Into the Unknown" /> Red markings on TIE pilot helmets signified their [[First Order Special Forces TIE fighter pilot|place]] in the [[First Order Special Forces]]. Special Forces pilots were directly answerable to the upper levels of the Starkiller operation.<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" />

First Order fleet gunners served aboard the navy's Star Destroyers,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> including Snoke's flagship, the ''Supremacy''. They wore helmets with a face visor displaying targeting data.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> Other support staff in the navy included [[First Order technician]]s.<ref name="Station to Station" />

First Order warships such as [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer]]s and [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought]]s carried large numbers of [[First Order officer|officers]], enlisted personnel and stormtroopers.<ref name="TFA ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref><ref name="TLJ ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> Resurgent Star Destroyers carried 19,000 officers, 55,000 enlisted and 8,000 stormtroopers. This crew included two [[fighter wing]]s.<ref name="TFA ICS" /> Dreadnoughts had a larger crew consisting of 53,000 officers, 140,000 enlisted and 22,000 stormtroopers.<ref name="TLJ ICS" />

==Organization==
===Administration===
The First Order Navy was led by [[First Order High Command]], a council of leaders which included General Hux and the Supreme Leader.<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> The fleet was composed of at least two [[corps]], including the [[First Order starfighter corps]] which served as the [[Starfighter Corps]], and the [[Transport Corps]], a unit which was also composed of [[pilot]]s but flew [[shuttle]]s instead of [[starfighter]]s.<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

===Order of battle===
Consisting of various ranks including [[lieutenant]]s and [[captain]]s, officers who held the rank of general<ref name="Episode VIII" /> or [[colonel]] could also command naval assets.<ref name="Poe Dameron 30" /> At its height, the First Order Navy had constructed hundreds of capital ships.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Starships of the First Order Navy==
===Starkiller Base and space stations===
The headquarters of the First Order along with the ''Supremacy'', Starkiller Base was a devastating [[superweapon]] that could destroy entire [[star system]]s.<ref name="Episode VII" />

The First Order used [[Station Theta Black]] to [[Mining|mine]] [[dedlanite]] for [[blaster]]s.<ref name="Station Theta Black">{{Resistance|Station Theta Black (episode)}}</ref> The First Order briefly [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupied]] the ''[[Colossus]]''<ref name="TFFO">{{Resistance|The First Order Occupation}}</ref> and used the other [[supertanker fuel depot]] the ''[[Titan]]''.<ref name="Station to Station">{{Resistance|Station to Station}}</ref> The First Order also seized control of old [[Fondor Shipyards|Imperial shipyards]] above [[Fondor]].<ref name="Allegiance 2">[[Allegiance 2|''Allegiance'' 2]]</ref>

===Capital ships===
The First Order Navy employed a variety of [[capital ship]]s, ranging from Star Destroyers to [[Star Dreadnought]]s. The flagship of the First Order, the ''Supremacy'', was the sole ''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought<ref name="TLJVD" /><ref name="TLJ ICS" /> of the navy.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> The navy was also bolstered by [[Siege Dreadnought]]s, specifically ''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnoughts<ref name="Battlefront II" /> of the [[Mandator line|''Mandator'' line]].<ref name="TLJ ICS" /> Star Destroyers included ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers,<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" /> as well ''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers of the old Empire.<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> The First Order's naval forces also included Maxima-A class heavy cruisers.<ref name="PD 2" />

===Smaller craft===
The First Order Navy was equipped with a large degree of smaller [[warship]]s, including [[frigate]]s, [[light cruiser]]s, and more. Frigate models included ''Lancer''-class frigate and Nebulon-Ks, while light cruiser models included ''Dissident''-class light cruisers<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> [[First Order Light Cruiser]]s,<ref name="Poe Dameron 19">[[Poe Dameron 19|''Poe Dameron'' 19]]</ref> and the old [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|Imperial ''Gozanti''-class cruiser]].<ref name="SWZ">{{FFGXW2|set=Huge Ship Conversion Kit|cardname=First Order Sympathizers}}</ref> Other craft included [[First Order landing platform]]s,<ref name="PD 1">[[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]]</ref> a [[Phelarion-class fuel tanker|First Order tanker]],<ref name="Poe Dameron 15">[[Poe Dameron 15|''Poe Dameron'' 15]]</ref> [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|First Order bomber]],<ref name="Bomber Command">''[[The Last Jedi: Bomber Command]]''</ref> and the modified [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette]], ''Carrion Spike''.<ref name="PD 2" />

During the [[blockade of the Otomok system]], the First Order repurposed [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17]] into "[[OreDigger]]s," where they were used for [[mining]] purposes.<ref name="Age of Resistance - Rose Tico 1">[[Age of Resistance - Rose Tico 1|''Age of Resistance - Rose Tico'' 1]]</ref> A [[Unidentified starship (First Order)|starship model]] was used by the First Order during the [[Battle against the Benathy (First Order)|battle against Benathy]].<ref name="Kylo Ren 1">[[Age of Resistance - Kylo Ren 1|''Age of Resistance - Kylo Ren'' 1]]</ref>

===Transports===
The First Order Navy was composed of an array of [[transport]]s and shuttles. Transports included [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport]]s<ref name="Episode VII" /> and [[First Order dropship]]s,<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> while shuttles included [[Griffin-class light shuttle|''Griffin''-class light shuttles]],<ref name="Attack on Starkiller Base">''[[Join the Resistance: Attack on Starkiller Base]]''</ref> [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|''Upsilon''-class command shuttles]],<ref name="DB">{{Databank|kylo-rens-command-shuttle|Kylo Ren's Command Shuttle}}</ref> [[Xi-class light shuttle|''Xi''-class light shuttles]],<ref name="Poe Dameron 19" /> and [[TIE/es assault shuttle]]s.<ref name="27 Things">{{SW|url=news/27-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=27 Things We Learned About ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> During the [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Resistance)|mission to Cato Neimoidia]], the First Order stole [[Leia Organa's ship]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 20">[[Poe Dameron 20|''Poe Dameron'' 20]]</ref>

===Starfighters===
[[File:First_Order_TIE_Fighter_2017_Q1_Tournament_Kit_Core_Prize_Card.png|thumb|left|250px|Three TIE/fo space superiority fighters.]]
The First Order's composition of starfighters include standard [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s,<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" /> [[First Order Special Forces]] [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s,<ref name="Episode VII" /> [[TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor]]s,<ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref> [[TIE/se Bomber]]s,<ref name="Allegiance 1" /> [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|First Order bombers]],<ref name="Bomber Command">''[[The Last Jedi: Bomber Command]]''</ref> [[First Order TIE/rb heavy starfighter]]s,<ref name="Smuggler's Run">{{GEAttr|Millennium Falcon: Smuggler's Run}}</ref> [[TIE/wi Interceptor]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[TIE/wi modified interceptor]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> and the elite [[TIE/vn space superiority fighter]]s.<ref name="TLJ ICS" /> During the mission of Cato Neimoidia, the First Order also stole the [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] ''[[Black One]]''.<ref name="Poe Dameron 20" />

Starfighters were organized into both squadrons and wings. At least one fighter wing, Tarkin's Revenge, was given a non-numerical designation. It was based at [[Starkiller Base]].<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
