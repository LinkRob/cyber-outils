The '''ARC-40b Scout-Reconnaissance Fighter''' was a class of medium light-weight [[starfighter]] that was used by the [[Republic Starfighter Corps]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]. It carried a crew of three. After the war, many surviving ARC-40bs were scrapped by the [[Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], with some still seeing life in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] in [[mercenary]] units or with [[Scout|surveyors]].<ref name="SaS" />
