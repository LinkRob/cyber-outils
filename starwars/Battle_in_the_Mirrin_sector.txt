During the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]] a '''battle''' took place between [[Rapier Squadron]] and a [[pirate]] group in the [[Mirrin sector]]. Rapier [[Squadron]] was [[Rapier Leader|lead]] by [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]]. The squadron took out many of the [[Unidentified pirate starfighter|pirate starfighters]] but Dameron and his [[droid]] [[BB-8]] were captured by the pirates. While Dameron was being interrogated, BB-8 alerted the rest of Rapier Squadron who came to their rescue.<ref name="Kidnapped" />
