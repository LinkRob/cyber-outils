In [[232 BBY]]<ref name="Date" />, explorer [[droid]] [[0G-LC]] had an excavation site on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Tenoo]] to unearth the [[skeleton|fossil]] remains of [[Raka (creature)|rakas]], ancient creatures on Tenoo which climbed [[Tenoo tree|trees]] using their well-adapted [[claw]]s. The droid brought Tenoo-based [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], and [[Nubs]] to the site to showcase his work and get their assistance in painstakingly brushing off the dirt covering the fossils. However, the site was soon overtaken by the real estate developer [[Ozon Nimbee]], who aimed to build a resort there. Though he burst in with his [[scoop droids]] and a two-armed [[bulldozer]], his plan was foiled by the [[Jedi]], ending his construction initiative. Nimbee fled Tenoo in [[Ozon's ship|his starship]], and his scoop droids were reconfigured by 0G-LC to help clean the site for the excavation of the many fossils.<ref name="Fossil Hunt" />
