An '''entropy field''' was a physical field used to place an object in [[suspended animation]]. In his [[Dryden's study|personal museum]] onboard the [[Yacht|star yacht]] ''[[First Light]]'', the [[gangster]] [[Dryden Vos]] kept rare creatures in jars sitting upon entropy field-generating pedestals, which preserved their contents.<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
