'''Eelia''' was an individual who served aboard the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] [[Scout vessel|scout ship]] ''[[Spotrunner]]'' during the [[Starship/Legends|vessel's]] [[Deep Charting Expedition]]. [[Covan]], who also served aboard the ''Spotrunner'', and Eelia were infected and [[Death/Legends|killed]] by the creature known as [[Mnggal-Mnggal]]. An [[Unidentified individual (Spotrunner)|individual]] recorded the pair's death in the ''Spotrunner''{{'s}} log.<ref name="Endnotes" />
