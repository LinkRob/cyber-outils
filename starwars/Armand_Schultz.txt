'''Armand Schultz''' is an [[Wikipedia:Actor|theater actor]] and [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|audiobook narrator]] who performed the The Scot Harvath Series audiobooks<ref name="Simon and Schuster" /> Schultz who voiced [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] in the [[1995]] ''[[The Mixed-Up Droid]]'' audio drama.<ref name="Mixed-Up Droid" /> Schultz returned to the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise in [[2011]] to provided additional voices in the''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]].<ref name="TOR credits">{{TORcite|type=other|Credits}}</ref>
