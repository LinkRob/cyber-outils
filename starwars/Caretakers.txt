The '''Caretakers''' were [[Gender|female]] [[Lanai]] who lived on [[Ahch-To]] and attended to the ancient [[Jedi village]] on the [[planet]], which housed the [[first Jedi Temple]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> The females considered it their religious duty to maintain the [[Temple Island]]'s ancient structures and to provide care for those who traveled to their isolated world seeking truth.<ref name="Databank" /> They led simple, unassuming lives and wore [[Color|white]] [[robe]]s with matching headdresses.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Their [[Caretakers' religion|religion]] had analogues for the [[Jedi Order]]'s concepts of the [[Light side of the Force|light side]] and the [[dark side of the Force]] as expressed by the weather of the island. Their ideal was balance in tranquility.<ref name="TLJVD" />

[[File:CaretakerVillage-SoH.png|thumb|left|250px|Caretakers attend to their duties in their village.]]
The Caretakers were led by their matron, [[Alcida-Auka]].<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> They tolerated the presence of former [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] as his impact on them was minimal and courteous, but were less than enamored with the visiting [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], who they found detrimental to their attempts to keep the island in good shape. After Rey blasted a hole in the [[Rey's hut|hut]] she was staying in, two Caretakers fussed over trying to repair the damage and lambasted the young woman in their native [[language]].<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> The Caretakers reminded Rey of the [[Anchorite sect|anchorites]] on [[Jakku]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

Later, Alcida-Auka and another Caretaker were pushing a wheelbarrow along a cliff path when the cart was obliterated by a falling rock pillar which Rey had sliced off with her [[Skywalker lightsaber|lightsaber]], leaving the matron holding the wheelbarrow's handles. Guessing who must have been responsible, the two Lanais glared up at her exasperatedly, and she replied with a sheepish smile.<ref name="TLJ" />
