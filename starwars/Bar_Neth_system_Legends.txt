The '''Bar Neth system'''<ref name="EAOC" /> was a quinary<ref name="Star Tours">[[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']]</ref> [[Star system/Legends|star system]] located in the [[Albarrio sector/Legends|Albarrio sector]] of [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="EAOC" /> that contained the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Bar Neth]].<ref name="Twitter" /> By the [[Standard year/Legends|year]] [[4 ABY/Legends|4.3 ABY]],<ref name="KOTHB">{{Blog|holocron|7|Death Star timeline}}</ref> the [[Star Tours/Legends|Star Tours]] travel agency facilitated transit to the system through its [[Frontier Vacation Package]].<ref name="Star Tours" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bar Neth system was established in [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|''The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion]], which placed the system in grid square K-5.<ref name="EAOC" />
