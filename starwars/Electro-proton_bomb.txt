The '''electro-proton bomb''' was a type of [[bomb]] designed to emit an electromagnetic field that could take out the Separatist [[droid]] forces.<ref name="TZB" />

==History==
[[File:Electro-proton_explosion.png|thumb|left|250px|The ion pulse created by the bomb on Malastare]]
The electro-proton bomb was designed by [[Doctor]] [[Sionver Boll]]<ref name="databoll" /> for use by the [[Galactic Republic]], but the bomb's use had unintended consequences. While it was successful at [[Battle of Malastare|taking out droids]] in service of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] on [[Malastare]], it also upset the [[planet]]'s ecosystem, awakening an ancient and powerful [[Unidentified Zillo Beast|creature]] known as the [[Zillo Beast]].<ref name="TZB" />

A weapon derived of the bomb, designated as the [[Electro-Proton Bomb/Defoliator]], was rumored to be developed by the [[Galactic Empire]]. It combined the electronic elements of the electro-proton bomb and elements of the [[defoliator]], a Separatist creation, to create a bomb that could devastate robotics and organics.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
