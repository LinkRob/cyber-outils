'''BG-81''' was an [[astromech droid]] who served the [[Mon Calamari]] [[engineer]] [[Quarrie]] in the [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]] and during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Biography==
===Project Shantipole===
[[File:BG-81.png|thumb|left|175px|BG-81]]
BG-81 lived on the [[planet]] [[Shantipole]], where it assisted the [[Mon Calamari]] [[starship]] [[engineer]] [[Quarrie]] with the construction of his [[Prototype B6]], which later became the basis for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-Wing]]. BG-81 was present when the [[Spectres]] [[Hera Syndulla]], [[Sabine Wren]], and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] arrived on Shantipole to test the new [[starfighter]].<ref name="Wings of the Master" />

The [[astromech droid]] was present when Hera flew Prototype B-6 for a test flight above the canyons of Shantipole. During the test flight, Quarrie had BG-81 upgrade the rebels' [[shuttle]], the ''[[Phantom]]'', with a [[hyperdrive]] and astromech socket. Later, the [[droid]] and Quarrie were aboard the ''Phantom'' when Hera used the prototype starfighter to break through the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Blockade of Ibaar|blockade]] of [[Ibaar]]. Following the skirmish, BG-81 left the rebel fleet with Quarrie in order to oversee the production of more B-Wings for the rebel cause.<ref name="Wings of the Master" />

===Joining the Rebellion===
BG-81 and Quarrie eventually joined the Rebel Alliance aboard the [[flagship]] ''[[Home One]]''. While Quarrie and the [[Freemakers]] spent time building, the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR]] told stories of the past to BG-81, during which the latter droid spent much of that time in sleep mode.<ref name="Droidography" />

==Characteristics==
BG-81 was a [[Lothal astromech droid]]<ref name="Wings of the Master" /> who served the Mon Calamari starship engineer Quarrie. It aided Quarrie with the construction of the experimental Prototype B6 starfighter. BG-81 was present both during the starship's test flight and the first field operation.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|bg-81|BG-81}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
BG-81 first premiered in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' [[Star Wars Rebels Season Two|Season Two]] episode "[[Wings of the Master]]," which premiered on [[Disney XD]] on [[November 11]], [[2015]]. He is named after [[Industrial Light & Magic|ILM]] veteran [[Bill George]], who designed the B-wing fighter for {{Film|VI}} and its color scheme mirrors an 1981 photo of George wearing a striped blue T-shirt<ref name="Wings of the Master Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=wings-of-the-master-trivia-gallery-2|text="Wings of the Master" Trivia Gallery}}</ref>. He makes cameo appearances in several ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season Two|Season Two]] episodes. In the series, BG-81 still serves Quarrie, who now works for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] aboard their flagship ''[[Home One]]''.<ref name="Trouble on Tibalt">{{FreemakerAdventures|Trouble on Tibalt}}</ref> Later, BG-81 plays back images of various starships but none match [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker's]] [[Force vision]] of the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']].<ref name="The Tower of Alistan Nor">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Tower of Alistan Nor}}</ref> BG-81 also assists [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker's]] mission to steal an Imperial list of worlds with [[kyber crystal]]s from an Imperial base during Rowan's [[Quest to build the Arrowhead|Quest to build the ''Arrowhead'']].<ref name="TLCOQ">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Lost Crystals of Qalydon}}</ref> Later, BG-81 awoke Quarrie so that Rowan could show him his [[Ugly#Behind the scenes|"Ugly" starship]].<ref name="A Perilous Rescue">{{FreemakerAdventures|A Perilous Rescue}}</ref>
