The northeastern [[factory]] '''Belts''' were the primary living quarters for the inhabitants of [[Foundry Four]] on the [[planet]] [[Xorrn]]. While the main walkway to the landing shaft was kept clear for traffic and the transportation of finished products, the former assembly lines were used to support housing. Prefabricated habitation cubes were among the first things [[Jans Coorsa]] ordered to be constructed upon the foundry's reactivation. The identical, modest cubes generally contained four rooms, and they rested in long rows along the former assembly lines, and while only a few hundred people lived on the planet, most worked in either of the two factories or the [[Ferra Sector Shipyard]], or are too young or old to do so.<ref name="Friends Like These" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Belts were introduced in the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] adventure book ''[[Friends Like These]]'' as part of the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]].<ref name="Friends Like These" />
