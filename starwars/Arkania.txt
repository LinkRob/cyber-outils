'''Arkania''' was a [[Temperature|cold]] [[planet]] located in the [[Arkania system]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Colonies]] region, positioned at the coordinates M-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. It was the [[homeworld]] of [[Arkanian]] [[species]].

==Decription==
The [[Temperature|cold]] [[planet]] Arkania<ref name="DoH" /> sat within the [[Arkania system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Colonies]] region of [[the galaxy]] at coordinates M-8<ref name="TFABG" /> on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Rebel Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref> The [[Arkanian]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|hailed]] from Arkania.<ref name="DoH" /> As a result of life on the frigid planet, the Arkanians would develop infrared vision over the course of their evolution. Root vegetables were a staple crop on Arkania. The world<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref> shared its name with the [[Species|creatures]] known as [[Arkanian dragon]]s.<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>

==History==
By the year [[228 BBY]],<ref name="228 Date">According to ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'', the [[Guardian Protocols]] were implemented in [[229 BBY]]. According to [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|''Shadows of Starlight'' 1]], this happened one week after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places in [[230 BBY]]. The events of ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'', including the [[Expansion of the Stormwall]], take place exactly one year after the Fall of [[Starlight Beacon]]. The events of "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Friend or Foe?]]" and "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Survivors]]" take place one month after the Expansion of the [[Stormwall]], so they must be set in [[228 BBY]].</ref> the [[Tarkos family]] had been, for [[Standard century|centuries]] by that point, among the most illustrious families who called Arkania home. Members of the clan arose to positions like [[general]], [[governor]], that of top [[scientist]], and even [[Jedi]].<ref name="Survivors" /> In the [[Standard week|weeks]] following the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[New Republic]] was involved in a [[battle on Arkania]] that included ground-level fighting.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Arkania was first mentioned in ''[[Savage Spirits]]'', published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]].<ref name="SS">''[[Savage Spirits]]''</ref>
In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Arkania/Legends|Arkania]] first appeared in [[Tales of the Jedi 1|''Tales of the Jedi'' 1]], a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] published in [[1993]].<ref name="Tales of the Jedi 1">[[Tales of the Jedi 1|''Tales of the Jedi'' 1]]</ref>
