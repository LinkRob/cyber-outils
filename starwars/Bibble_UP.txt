'''Bibble UP''' was a [[Foodstuff/Legends|beverage]] that shared its name with<ref name="MenaceSith" /> the [[Governor/Legends|governor]] of [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]], [[Sio Bibble/Legends|Sio Bibble]].<ref name="ff">{{FactFile|34|BIB 1-2|Sio Bibble}}</ref> The [[Cloning/Legends|cloning]] device referred to as a "[[New and improved Clone-amatic|new and improved version]]" of the [[Clone-amatic 11-38]]<ref name="MenaceSith" /> on the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser/Legends|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]]<ref name="CampaignGuide">''[[The Clone Wars Campaign Guide]]''</ref> [[Malevolence/Legends|''Malevolence'']] was capable of dispensing Bibble UP. The beverage was stored in an [[Color/Legends|orange]] and silver can with white stripes and a white label reading "Bibble UP" in the [[High Galactic/Legends|High Galactic]].<ref name="MenaceSith" />

In [[21 BBY/Legends|21 BBY]],<ref name="Yoda Chronicles Book">The [[LEGO Star Wars: The Yoda Chronicles (book)|''LEGO Star Wars: The Yoda Chronicles'']] book places the [[Reproduction/Legends|birth]] of [[Jek-14/Legends|Jek-14]]&mdash;which takes place during the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Yoda Chronicles]]'' episode "[[The Phantom Clone]]"&mdash;in [[21 BBY/Legends|21 BBY]]. As "[[Menace of the Sith]]" occurs immediately after the events of "The Phantom Clone," it must have occurred around 21 [[Galactic Standard Calendar|BBY]].</ref> [[General/Legends|General]] [[Grievous/Legends|Grievous]] accidentally dispensed Bibble UP from the new and improved Clone-amatic while attemping to clone the [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-sensitive]] clone [[Jek-14/Legends|Jek-14]] due to having pressed the wrong button. When the [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Count/Legends|Count]] [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]] reacted with contempt, Grievous chuckled before tossing the can aside.<ref name="MenaceSith" />
