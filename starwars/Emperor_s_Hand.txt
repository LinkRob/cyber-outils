'''Emperor's Hands''' were secretive operatives of the [[Galactic Empire]] that conducted their covert operations from [[VT-49 Decimator]]s. At one point during the [[Imperial Era]], a group of individuals were tasked with rescuing a prominent and outspoken celebrity and transport them to safety. When the individuals approached the celebrity, a VT-49 Decimator carrying one of the Emperor's Hands arrived and were tasked with ending the trouble-making subversive permanently.<ref name="Starships" />

In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date note" /> Imperial [[Viceroy]] and [[Governor]]<ref name="Gar Saxon">{{Databank|gar-saxon|Gar Saxon}}</ref> of [[Mandalore]] [[Gar Saxon]] was an Emperor's Hand.<ref name="Legacy of Mandalore" /> Saxon led the [[Imperial Super Commando]]s, a group of elite [[Mandalorian warrior]]s loyal to the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Imperial Supercommandos">{{Rebels|Imperial Supercommandos}}</ref> Saxon was eventually [[Death|killed]] during a [[skirmish on Krownest]] against Mandalorian [[Clan Wren]].<ref name="Legacy of Mandalore" /> The [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the [[Sith apprentice]] and enforcer of Palpatine,<ref name="RiseFall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> was also identified as the Emperor's Hand<ref name="Emperor's Hand">{{Acme|url=collections/star-wars-artwork/products/the-emperors-hand-brandon-kenney|text=The Emperor's Hand}}</ref> and [[Emperor's Fist|Fist]].<ref name="Vader 1">[[Age of Rebellion - Darth Vader 1|''Age of Rebellion - Darth Vader'' 1]]</ref>
