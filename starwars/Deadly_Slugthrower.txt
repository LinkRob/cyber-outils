The '''Deadly Slugthrower''' was a [[slugthrower rifle]] owned by the [[Miraluka]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Diago Velaar]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Frontier Slugthrower|standard slugthrower]]. It featured a sleek [[Color|gold]], silver, red, and black color scheme.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Deadly Slugthrower was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Diago's Frontier Slugthrower, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 5.0.0 alongside Season 5 on [[January 27]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Deadly Sharpshooter]]. This wrap could be unlocked by reaching Tier 61 on the Season 5 Arena Pass' premium track.<ref name="Hunters" />
