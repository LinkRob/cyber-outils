The '''CR56 corvette''' was a class of [[corvette]] manufactured by [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] during the [[Republic Era]]. One CR56, ''[[Assurance]]'', served in the [[Republic Diplomatic Fleet]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Judicial Forces]] and operated in [[the Slice]] against criminal elements such as the [[Riftwalkers]]. ''Assurance'' was led by [[Captain]] [[Pell Baylo]], and served with [[Jedi Master]]s [[Mace Windu]] and [[Depa Billaba]].<ref name="TLF" />
