The '''Black Sun smash-processing lab''' was operated by [[Black Sun]] in the mountains of [[Dantooine]]. A formula was developed there which made the drug [[smash]] more potent and more addictive to a wider array of [[species]]. Built into the mountain itself, the facility was crewed by scientists and included a significant amount of armed guards of many different species for the sake of security. Several [[transport]] [[Starship|ships]] were kept at the facility. At some point after the [[Escape from Cloud City]], [[Chanath Cha]] and the [[Orphans]] [[Attack on the Black Sun smash-processing lab|attacked]] the facility, leaving it burned to ash. After the attack, footage of the facility in flames made its way onto the [[Shadowfeed]]s.<ref name="Crimson Reign 1" />
