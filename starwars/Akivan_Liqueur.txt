'''Akivan Liqueur''' was a [[Color|purple]] [[liqueur]] from [[Akiva]] that tasted like [[honey]] and [[lavender]]. The drink was consumed by the [[human]] [[Sinjir Rath Velus]], and by [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]], who purchased it from the [[Ithorian]] [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|antiquities dealer]] [[Dok-Ondar]]. Due to the liqueur's rarity and expense, Tuggs created a substitute recipe for his [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]].

==Description==
Akivan Liqueur was a rare, [[Color|purple]]-colored [[liqueur]] from the [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. Its front note had the lingering [[syrup]]y sweetness<ref name="Cookbook" /> of [[honey]], followed by a finish of [[lavender]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> [[Flower|floral]] notes and a hint of pepper<ref name="Cookbook" /> or [[copper]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

==History==
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date: Aftermath">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that [[Temmin Wexley]] and [[Norra Wexley]] were reunited on [[Akiva]] 30 [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore their reunion in ''Aftermath'' took place in [[4 ABY]].</ref> ex-[[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[loyalty officer]] [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] had gone into hiding in [[Myrra]], the [[capital]] of Akiva, after deserting the Empire during the [[Battle of Endor]]. The [[human]] [[Gender|male]] drank the [[alcohol]]ic beverage while denying to [[Jas Emari]] that he was a drunk; however, his inebriation led to former [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]] overhearing him discuss his former loyalties.<ref name="Aftermath" />

By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date: Cookbook">The [[in-universe]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'' was published after the [[Battle of Takodana]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the cookbook was published during or after that [[standard year]], and the anecdotes within occured during or prior to it.</ref> [[Dok-Ondar]],<ref name="Cookbook" /> the [[Ithorian]] [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|antiquities dealer]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]],<ref name="GE 1">[[Galaxy's Edge 1|''Galaxy's Edge'' 1]]</ref> was known to acquire the liqueur and sell it at an expensive price. After [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] repeatedly experienced difficulties in collecting sufficient [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to purchase it from the Ithorian, Tuggs created a recipe for a substitute. He included the recipe for imitation Akivan Liqueur in his [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]], and although he compared the liqueur to honey for its rarity and expense in the Outer Rim, he nevertheless used honey to sweeten his substitute.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Akivan Liqueur first appeared in the [[2015]] novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]], as an unidentified liquor with honey and lavender flavors.<ref name="Aftermath" /> The drink was first identified as a recipe in the [[2019]] cookbook ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The ingredients are [[Wikipedia:Honey|honey]], dried [[Wikipedia:Lavandula#Culinary_use|culinary lavender]] flowers, [[Wikipedia:Sucrose|sugar]], water, whole [[Wikipedia:Clove|cloves]], grated [[Wikipedia:Orange (fruit)|orange zest]], cracked [[Wikipedia:Aframomum melegueta|grains of paradise]], [[Wikipedia:Vodka|vodka]], and [[Wikipedia:Lavender (color)|lavender]]-colored [[Wikipedia:Food coloring|gel food coloring]].<ref name="Cookbook" />
