'''Corellian sting-gnats''' were a type of [[gnat]] that shared their name with [[Corellia]],<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> a [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]].<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[starship]] known as the ''[[Vessel]]'' traveled to the [[Echerta system]]<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> in an attempt to make a faster journey to the [[Galactic Republic]] [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]].<ref name="The Fallen Star" />

After arriving in the Echerta [[Star system|system]], the ''Vessel'' came under [[Attack in the Echerta system|attack]] by starships of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s. [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]]&mdash;who was traveling aboard the ''Vessel''&mdash;went to its [[cockpit]] to determine how much trouble they were in. Following his arrival into the cockpit, Mann looked outside of the ''Vessel'' and immediately regretted his decision to as he saw that the system was flooded with Nihil ships and that they swooped in each direction. He then mentally noted that they were like clouds of Corellian sting-gnats.<ref name="The Fallen Star" />
