'''27th generation droidspeak''' was a compressed variant of standard [[Binary|droidspeak]].<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[BB-series astromech droid|BB-series]] [[astromech droid]]s like [[BB-8]] spoke it.<ref name="AB Update">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>

==Known phrases==

*''Bwa-woop?''&mdash;"For who?"<ref name="AB Update" />
*''Wooooo.''&mdash;"Oh."<ref name="AB Update" />
*''Bloopy boo!''&mdash;"That's right!"<ref name="AB Update" />
*''Blip! Blip! Blip!''&mdash;"Okay!"<ref name="AB Update" />
*''Bleep brrro beep!''&mdash;"Oh no you don't!"<ref name="AB Update" />
*''Bee-boorp!''&mdash;"That's classified!"<ref name="AB Update" />
