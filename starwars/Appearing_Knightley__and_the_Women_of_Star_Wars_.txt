"'''Appearing Knightley'''" is an article that first appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 75|''Star Wars Insider'' 75]]. It features interviews with several women of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' cast and crew:
*[[Keira Knightley]] ([[Sabé/Legends|Sabé]])
*[[Alethea McGrath]] ([[Jocasta Nu/Legends|Jocasta Nu]])
*[[Rena Owen]] ([[Nee Alavar/Legends|Nee Alavar]] and voice of [[Taun We/Legends|Taun We]])
*[[Amy Allen]] ([[Aayla Secura/Legends|Aayla Secura]])
*[[Ayesha Dharker]] ([[Queen/Legends|Queen]] [[Jamillia/Legends|Jamillia]])
*[[Bai Ling]] ([[Bana Breemu/Legends|Bana Breemu]])
*[[Caroline Blakiston]] ([[Mon Mothma/Legends|Mon Mothma]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|Episode VI]])
*[[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|Episode III]] hair stylists [[Tracey Reeby]] and [[Annette Miles]]
