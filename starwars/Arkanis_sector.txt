The '''Arkanis sector''' was a [[sector]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Within it was the [[Arkanis system]],<ref name="Life Debt" /> along with the [[Geonosis system]], the [[Tatoo system]],<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> and the [[Sentinel Base system|system]] in which [[Sentinel Base]] was located.<ref name="Tarkin" /> During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Saw Gerrera]] [[Attacks in the Arkanis sector|won]] a number of scores in the Arkanis sector, making life miserable for the [[Unidentified territorial moff|territorial moff]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
