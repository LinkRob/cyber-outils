'''Fambaa horns''' were a type of [[Horn/Legends|horn]]<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> used by the [[Gungan]]s of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]].<ref name="TPM" /> Members of the [[Gungan Grand Army]] used these to signal troops prior to and during the [[Battle of Naboo]]. They shared a namesake with [[fambaa]]s, quadruped [[amphibian]]s<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> native to Naboo<ref name="DB">{{Databank|fambaa|Fambaa}}</ref> that the Gungans used to carry to carry [[deflector shield generator]]s into battle.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" />
