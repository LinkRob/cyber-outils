'''Bleary''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[pirate]] who worked in a crew led by [[Maliss]] and [[Sorath]], hired to collect components stolen from [[Project Stardust]] for [[Grand Admiral]] [[Balanhai Savit]]. The [[Unidentified mobile way station|mobile way station]] the pirates operated from was [[Attack on a mobile way station|attacked]] by the [[Grysk]]s, who captured the survivors, including Bleary. The [[prisoner]]s were rescued by Grand Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] and [[Commodore]] [[Karyn Faro]] of the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]], who questioned Bleary before sending him and the other pirates to be prosecuted.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
