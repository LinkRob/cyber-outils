'''Fixer's Power Units & Astroparts''' was a company with locations within both the [[Dune Sea Outpost]] and [[Mos Espa Waystation]] battlefields of the [[Grand Arena]], utilized by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. Owned by an individual known as "[[Laze Loneozner|Fixer]]," the company's branding was simple, their horizontally rectangular signage displaying the words "Fixer's Power Units & Astroparts" written in [[Aurebesh]], with varying shades of [[Color|brown]] and orange throughout the design. The company dealt in [[Fusion furnace|Power units]] and [[astromech droid]] parts.<ref name="Hunters" />
