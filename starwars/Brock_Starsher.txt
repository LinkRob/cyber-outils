'''Brock Starsher''' served as a guard for the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], working on board one of his [[Bantha-II cargo skiff]]s at the Hutt's [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] in [[4 ABY]]. When Jabba sentenced his [[prisoner]]s [[Han Solo]], [[Luke Skywalker]], and [[Chewbacca]] to be fed to the [[sarlacc]] in the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]], Starsher manned one of the two [[skiff]]s that made the journey to the pit. At the pit, Skywalker managed to free himself and [[Rescue of Han Solo|attack]] the guards, boarding Starsher's skiff and knocking the guard into the [[Mouth|maw]] of the sarlacc below.

==Biography==
===Sailing to the sarlacc===
[[File:Second_skiff.png|thumb|left|300px|Brock Starsher manned a skiff above the Pit of Carkoon when Luke Skywalker and his allies were to be executed.]]
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Brock Starsher worked as a guard<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=Star Wars Force Attax: The Force Awakens|cardname=Brock Starsher|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/122192/cid/8287800/2016-Topps-Star-Wars-Force-Attax-The-Force-Awakens-72-Brock-Starsher}}</ref> for the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], serving on board one of the [[Bantha-II cargo skiff]]s kept at [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. When the Hutt declared that three of his [[prisoner]]s&mdash;the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]], his [[Pilot|co-pilot]] [[Chewbacca]], and the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]]&mdash;were to be fed to the [[sarlacc]] that lived in the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]], Starsher flew to the pit on board<ref name="ROTJ" /> an escort [[skiff]]<ref name="NovelJr">[[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (paperback novel)|''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'' junior novelization]]</ref> alongside several other guards<ref name="ROTJ" /> including [[Barada]], [[Lathe]], [[Yotts Oren]], and their pilot [[Pote Snitkin]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Battle of Hoth and the Second Death Star}}</ref> The prisoners traveled on a second skiff while Jabba and his court rode aboard the [[sail barge]] ''[[Khetanna]]''.<ref name="ROTJ" />

===Battle at the great pit===
Upon reaching the pit, the [[Repulsorcraft|vehicle]] carrying Starsher began circling around the sarlacc while the skiff carrying the prisoners positioned itself above the creature's [[Mouth|maw]]. When Skywalker was forced over the edge of his skiff to be [[Death|executed]], he flipped back onboard and caught his [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|lightsaber]], which had been launched to him by the [[R2-series astromech droid|astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] from the sail barge's deck. Skywalker and his allies then quickly defeated the guards aboard the vehicle carrying them, at which point Starsher's skiff approached so that its occupants could attack.<ref name="ROTJ" />

[[File:Starsher_overboard.png|thumb|right|300px|Brock Starsher was knocked into the mouth of the sarlacc.]]
Starsher stood ready at the very front of the skiff, so when Skywalker leaped onboard, the guard was the closest to him. At first, Starsher did not engage the [[Jedi]], but after Skywalker deflected a [[blaster]] bolt fired by one of the other guards, Starsher moved forward to attack. Skywalker slashed at Starsher with his [[lightsaber]] and caused the guard to tumble forward over the rail of the vehicle into the sarlacc's pit below,<ref name="ROTJ" /> killing Starsher and then a second guard before the other guards on the skiff even fully realized the Jedi was on board.<ref name="Darkside">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> Skywalker and his allies then defeated the rest of Jabba's men and killed the Hutt before escaping.<ref name="ROTJ" />

==Personality and traits==
Starsher had [[Color|fair]] skin.<ref name="ROTJ" />

==Equipment==
Starsher wore a white [[Clothing|jumpsuit]] with a black [[Armor|helmet]], black [[glove]]s, a black [[utility belt]], and brown [[boot]]s while on the skiff. Starsher carried<ref name="ROTJ" /> a [[BD-1 Cutter vibro-ax]] and a [[DL-18 blaster pistol]].<ref name="Hasbro">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Vintage Collection|packtype=Special Action Figure Set|pack=#152-154: Skiff Guard 3-Pack (Vadain, Vizam, & Brock Staraher)|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/Special-3-Action-Figures-Set-The-Vintage-Collection.asp}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BrockStarsherHelmet-SWCOT.png|thumb|right|250px|Helmet worn by both Brock Starsher and Doallyn]]
Brock Starsher was portrayed by an uncredited extra in the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="ROTJ" /> The scenes on Starsher's skiff were filmed in the [[Yuma]] desert in [[Wikipedia:Arizona|Arizona]] between [[April 12]] and [[April 24]], [[1982]], with the skiffs then being moved to [[Industrial Light & Magic]] in [[Wikipedia:California|California]] for blue screen shots that were filmed between [[May 10]] and [[May 14|14]].<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]''</ref> The face plate of Starsher's helmet,<ref name="Costume">''[[Star Wars Costumes: The Original Trilogy]]''</ref> which was also worn in the film by the character<ref name="ROTJ" /> [[Doallyn]],<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> was made from the knee guard of a [[scout trooper]] costume.<ref name="Costume" />

In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Starsher's name was first used on a card in the [[Star Wars Force Attax: The Force Awakens|''2015 Topps Star Wars Force Attax The Force Awakens'']] trading card set released by [[Topps|The Topps Company, Inc.]]<ref name="Topps" /> in [[December]] [[2015]].<ref name="ToppsDate">{{WebCite|url=https://www.toppsdirect.com/star-wars-force-awakens-tarding-card-collection.html|text=Star Wars Force Attax &ndash; Force Awakens Trading Card Collection|date=2016-02-19|work=[[Wikipedia:Topps|Topps Direct]]|archivedate=20170202082007}}</ref> The name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|143|Rogues Gallery|Rogues Gallery: Jabba's Henchmen!}}</ref> on [[July 23]], [[2013]]<ref name="InsiderDate">{{TitanMagazines|url=t/star-wars-insider/us/143|text=''Star Wars Insider'' 143}}</ref> in the article "[[Rogues Gallery (department)|Rogues Gallery: Jabba's Henchmen!]]," which was written by [[Leland Chee]] and published in the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine's [[Star Wars Insider 143|143rd issue]].<ref name="Insider" />
