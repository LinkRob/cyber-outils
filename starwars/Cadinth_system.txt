The '''Cadinth system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Allied Tion sector]], a subsector of<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[Tion Cluster]].<ref name="TFABG" /> It was situated in the regions known as the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> Located in grid square S-6 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> the Cadinth system was situated along a [[Lianna–Jaminere hyperlane|hyperspace route]] which connected it to the astronomical object [[Lianna]] and the [[Jaminere system]].<ref name="TFABG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cadinth system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] boxed set for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="TFABG" /> The system was later identified as the "Cadinth system" in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]],"<ref name="Appendix" /> an appendix accompanying the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]], an article on [[StarWars.com]] featuring a map of the galaxy.<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]] and posted on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Fry">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map...|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Cadinth system/Legends|Cadinth system]] was introduced in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> The StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to ''The Essential Atlas'' placed<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> the [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="Cadinth">{{GalaxyCite|2|The Battle of Cadinth}}</ref> [[Cadinth]] in the system,<ref name="EAOC" /> thus making the world's first appearance&mdash;"[[The Battle of Cadinth]]," a short story authored by [[Bill Smith]] and published in the [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 2|second issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine]]'' series released by [[Topps|The Topps Company, Inc.]]<ref name="Cadinth">{{GalaxyCite|2|The Battle of Cadinth}}</ref> in [[January]] [[1995]],<ref name="GalaxyMagDate">[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 5|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 5]] indicates it was published in winter [[1995]], and as issues [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 3|three]] and [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 7|seven]] of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine]]'' were published in [[April]] 1995 and [[1996]] respectively&mdash;according to {{GalaxyCite|2|Rebel Report}} and {{GalaxyCite|6|Around the Galaxy}} respectively&mdash;issue two must have been published in [[January]] 1995.</ref> [[Retroactive continuity|retroactively]] the system's first appearance.<ref name="EAOC" />
