The '''Belsallian Sea''' was an [[ocean]] located on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] of [[Serenno]]. The planetary [[capital]] of [[Carannia]] was located upon its shoreline.<ref name="BMF 70" /> It shared its name with the [[Belsallian Cantina]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
This location was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 70]] magazine published by [[De Agostini]]<ref name="BMF 70" /> and released on [[May 4]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|70}}</ref> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the location first appeared in the [[Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets 1|sixth]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Agent of the Empire]]'' comic series published by [[Dark Horse Comics]].<ref name="AotE - HT1">[[Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets 1|''Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets'' 1]]</ref>
