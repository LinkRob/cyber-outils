'''Corell''' was the [[Color|yellow]] [[Star#Types of stars|G-class]] main sequence [[star]]<ref name="BTM" /> of the [[Corellian system|Corellia system]], which included the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. When traveling into [[Realspace|space]] aboard a [[shuttle]], [[Han Solo|Han]] noted that Corell didn't seem any smaller, and that it was just whiter and maybe even brighter than Corellia, which became a tiny shining dot.<ref name="Most Wanted" />
