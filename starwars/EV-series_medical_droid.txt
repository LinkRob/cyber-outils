The '''EV-series medical droid''',<ref name="EV-AD-4" /> also known as the '''A-series medical droid''',<ref name="BMF 75">{{BuildFalconCite|75|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> or the '''A4 laboratory assistant droid''',<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> was a model of [[medical droid]]<ref name="EV-AD-4" /> based on the [[EV-series]] chassis that was manufactured by [[MerenData]].<ref name="R2-D2 20" /> Beginning their operational lives as [[EV-series supervisor droid]]s, the company refitted them<ref name="R2-D2 53" /> as cheap medical units<ref name="R2-D2 20" /> in an attempt to salvage the EV-series.<ref name="R2-D2 53" /> However, their [[Skeleton|skeletal]] frames hardly encouraged trust in most patients, and the model was largely a failure.<ref name="R2-D2 20" /> The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] purchased and retrofitted an EV-series medical [[droid]] designated [[EV-A4-D]],<ref name="R2-D2 53" /> gifting him to [[General]] [[Grievous]] to serve as the [[cyborg]]'s [[Doctor|surgeon]] and [[cybernetics]] [[Cyberneticist|repair technician]].<ref name="R2-D2 20" />

==Behind the scenes==
The EV-series medical droid first appeared in "[[Lair of Grievous]]," the tenth episode of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]],<ref name="Lair of Grievous">{{TCW|Lair of Grievous}}</ref> which originally aired on [[December 12]], [[2008]].<ref name="The Clone Wars: Episode Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> It was designed by [[Atsushi Takeuchi]]<ref name="The Art of The Clone Wars">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''</ref> of [[Production IG]], who also designed the show's incarnation of Grievous<ref name="The Lair of Takeuchi">{{SWArchive|subdomain=starwarsblog|url=index.php/2008/07/20/celebration-japan-the-lair-of-takeuchi|text=Celebration Japan: The Lair of Takeuchi}}</ref> and directed "Lair of Grievous."<ref name="Lair of Grievous" /> The EV-series medical droid was first identified in EV-A4-D's [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="EV-AD-4" />

In the original [[The Clone Wars Episode Guides|episode guide]] for "Lair of Grievous," EV-A4-D was described as a modified supervisor droid.<ref name="Lair of Grievous Episode Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode010.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Lair of Grievous}}</ref> However, his Databank entry identified him as an medical droid.<ref name="EV-AD-4" /> [[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 20|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 20]] would later establish that the EV-series medical droid was based on the chassis of the EV-series supervisor droid,<ref name="R2-D2 20" /> with [[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 53|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 53]] further explaining that the medical droids were supervisor droids before being refitted.<ref name="R2-D2 53" />
