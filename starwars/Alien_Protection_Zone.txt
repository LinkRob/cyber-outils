The '''Alien Protection Zone''' was a walled neighborhood on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. It was walled-off from the central regions of [[Imperial City]]. In keeping with the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[High Human|humanocentric]] ideals, it arranged regular [[stormtrooper]] patrols and the inhabitants were harassed by their armored guards.<ref name="BMF 53" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Alien Protection Zone was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 53|fifty-third]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 53" /> published on [[January 6]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|53}}</ref>
