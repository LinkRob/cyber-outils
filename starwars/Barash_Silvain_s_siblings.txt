At least two [[Kage]] '''individuals''' from<ref name="The Blade 3" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Quarzite]]<ref name="Quarzite" /> were [[Reproduction|born]] to [[Barash Silvain's parents|parents]]<ref name="The Blade 3" /> by [[407 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> They were [[Barash Silvain's family|siblings]] to the Kage [[Gender|female]] [[Barash Silvain]]<ref name="The Blade 3" /> and possessed [[Eidetic memory|eidetic memories]] like all Kage.<ref name="Eidetic memory">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Kage]]</ref> By that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> their [[Youngling|infant]] sister's [[Force-sensitive|Force-sensitivity]] was discovered, and Silvain<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was taken to be [[Jedi training|trained]] as a [[Jedi]] at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="The Blade 3" /> the [[Core Worlds]] planet<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> [[Coruscant]] by the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="The Blade 3" />

In [[397 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">The present day events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic — The Blade]]'' take place between the [[wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran]] and [[Porter Engle]] [[Siege of Bardotta|earning]] his title the "Blade of [[Bardotta]]," both of which are dated to [[382 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As the flashbacks in [[The High Republic — The Blade 3|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 3]] take place fifteen [[Standard year|years]] before the present day events, they must take place in [[397 BBY]].</ref> Silvain confessed to [[Porter Engle]]&mdash;an [[Ikkrukkian]] Jedi who was<ref name="The Blade 3" /> her<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> close friend&mdash;that as she had been found by the Order at an older age than usual, and due to her<ref name="The Blade 3" /> eidetic memory,<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> she had continued to remember her siblings and family. She noted she recalled how it felt to be with her siblings, and despite not regretting being brought to the [[Jedi temple|temple]], she remembered what she had lost. After telling Engle she had found family again with him, Silvain asked him if he would be her brother, which the Ikkrukkian accepted.<ref name="The Blade 3" />
