The '''Celes IV militia''' was a [[Planetary defense forces|planetary defense force]] located on [[Celes IV]] which existed during the [[Clone Wars]] and was aligned with the [[Galactic Republic]] during the conflict. At some point during the war, forces from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] which opposed the Republic established a [[Separatist fortress|fortress]] on Celes IV which was too shielded to destroy from the planet's orbit, prompting a ground assault from the Celes IV militia led by a [[Unidentified Jedi (Altadan Igar)|member]] of the [[Jedi Order]], who served as [[General]]s in the [[Republic Military]]. One [[soldier]] from the Celes IV militia who participated in the assault, [[Altadan Igar]], would join the Republic's successor state, the [[Galactic Empire]], following the Clone Wars' conclusion, later recounting his experience serving in the militia and fighting alongside the [[Jedi]] to a captive [[Jedi Knight]], [[Luke Skywalker]], after arresting him on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]].<ref name="TMWCLS" />
