"'''Fall Of The Republic'''" is a one-minute [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] [[LEGO Star Wars Movie Short|Mini Movie]] that is connected to ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Yoda Chronicles]]'' animated TV series. It is the fourth video and was released on YouTube on [[May 21]], [[2014]].<ref name="Fall of the Republic" />

==Plot summary==
During the [[Great Jedi Purge/Legends|Great Jedi Purge]], [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine's]] [[Clone trooper/Legends|Clone troopers]] attack the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. As the Jedi Purge rages, Palpatine remarks that this is his best birthday ever. On [[Kashyyyk/Legends|Kashyyyk]], [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] and the [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiees]] flee several [[Clone scout trooper/Legends|Clone scout troopers]] intent on executing [[Order 66/Legends|Order 66]]. Yoda manages to commandeer an [[All Terrain Recon Transport/Legends|AT-RT walker]] and warns [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] that the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Clone army]] has turned on them.

Kenobi warns Yoda about the fall of the Jedi Temple while riding the [[Varactyl/Legends|varactyl]] [[Boga/Legends|Boga]] in the sinkholes of [[Utapau]]. Yoda orders Kenobi to return to Coruscant and retrieve the [[Holocron/Legends|holocorons]]. However, due to the fighting, Kenobi misunderstands Yoda's command and thinks that Yoda will be returning to the Temple to retrieve the holocrons himself. Meanwhile, Emperor Palpatine and two of his [[Emperor's Royal Guard/Legends|Royal Guards]] enter the [[Holocron Vault/Legends|Holocron Vaults]] to view the holocrons. The Emperor does a victory dance.
