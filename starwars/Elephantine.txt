==Behind the scenes==
During widespread debates over the status of the [[Myykian|Neel's species]] - before the reveal of them being Myykians - as potentially just being the [[Ortolan]]s, [[Leland Chee]] shared an image on [[Wikipedia:Instagram|Instagram]] showcasing nine distinct species he labeled as being "elephantine." Among these included [[Canon]]-exclusive species such as the [[Onodone]]s, the [[Pakiphantos]], and the previously unidentified [[Andraven (species)|Andraven]], as well as one [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] only [[pachydermoid]], [[Bothel's species|Trud and Bothel's species]].<ref name="Elephantine" />

In the comments of said post, he revealed he chooses to classify these species as "elephantine" rather than the previously utilized terms "pachyderm/pachydermoid" as that would also include species inspired by real-world animals such as rhinoceroses and hippopotamuses, while the former phrase simply encompasses species with elephant-like features such as trunks. He also stated that, despite being previously classified as both [[insectoid]] and [[mammal]]ian (specifically [[anteater]]) inspired, the [[Kubaz]] would have been a worthy inclusion for his graphic as well, given their trunks.<ref name="Elephantine" />

Additionally, within the aforementioned comment section, he responded to a comment about the [[Chevin]] [[Ephant Mon]] species, stating he had "waffled on it," though did not deny its status,<ref name="Elephantine" /> When later asked for clarification on the process of determining an elephantine, Chee stated "I’d rather not make a strict ruling either way. I don’t mind leaving room for a bit a subjectivity." and "Maybe today I rule out [[Hoover]] because of size. Maybe tomorrow I wouldn’t." though he did reiterate that possessing a trunk and being generally elephantine,<ref name="Discord" /> defined as "of or relating to an elephant" by the Merriam-Webster Dictionary,<ref name="Definition">{{WebCite|url=https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/elephantine|text=elephantine adjective|work=[https://www.merriam-webster.com Merriam-Webster]]|archivedate=20250826215014}}</ref> were the primary factors in his decisions making. He also further revealed that no official entry for the term elephantine existed within the [[Holocron continuity database]] until after his initial Instagram post about the topic, with the term previously relegated simply to a key word.<ref name="Discord" />
