'''Candied cactus flower''' was a lush type of [[Foodstuff|food]] made of [[cactus flower]]s that was [[Diet|edible]] by [[Quarren]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], it was a rare and expensive delicacy on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]. While on the world<ref name="Cataclysm" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the Quarren [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Orlen Mollo]] was given a bowl of candied [[cactus]] [[flower]] while visiting E'ronoh's [[Monarch of E'ronoh|Monarch]] [[A'lbaran]]. However, Mollo disliked being pampered with fine dining in a lavish setting, instead wishing to converse frankly with the [[Monarchy|monarch]].<ref name="Cataclysm" />
