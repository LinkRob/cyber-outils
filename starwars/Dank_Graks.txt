The '''Dank Graks''' were a group of criminals whose members were mostly rejects from [[dark side of the Force]] [[Religion|cults]],<ref name="THRA 1" /><ref name="Celebration 2022">{{SWYouTube|swclPDFHKNk|Star Wars Celebration LIVE! – DAY 1|nolive=1}}</ref> active in [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic Adventures (2022) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2022) 1]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Led by the [[force-sensitive]] [[Geonosian]] [[Arkik Von]], their goal was to take over the [[planet]] [[Takodana]] by ridding it of the [[Jedi]] and the [[Pirate Queen|pirate queen]] [[Maz Kanata]].<ref name="THRA 1" />

{{Company-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DankGraks-OlderConcept.png|thumb|right|200px|Concept art by Daniel José Older]]
The Dank Graks were created for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project and were first revealed in concept art by [[Daniel José Older]] showcased during a panel at [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim]] on [[May 26]], [[2022]].<ref name="Celebration 2022" />
