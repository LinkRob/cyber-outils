'''Corvettes''' were a type of small [[starship]] used during the final days of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Alderaan Cruiser">{{Databank|alderaan-cruiser|Alderaan Cruiser}}</ref> and the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]]. They typically measured around 100-200 [[meter]]s in length, and were considered the smallest type of [[capital ship]].<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>

The ''[[Assurance]]'' was a [[CR56 corvette]] commanded by [[captain]] [[Pell Baylo]].<ref name="The Living Force">''[[The Living Force]]''</ref> The ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', a custom ship owned by [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], was a corvette;<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> as was the ''[[Tantive IV]]'', a [[CR90 corvette]] manufactured by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]<ref name="Encyclopedia Starfighters">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> and owned by [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]].<ref name="Alderaan Cruiser" /> The [[Corvus (Raider II-class)|''Corvus'']] was a [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvette]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Inferno Squad]].<ref name="BFII">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>
