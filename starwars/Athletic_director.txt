An '''athletic director''' was somebody who oversaw the athletic activities, such as sports, within an organization like a school or academy. [[Janus Fhurek]] was the athletic director at the [[Junior Academy of Applied Sciences]] on [[Lothal]]. Due to the strong emphasis placed on sports, such as [[grav-ball]], at this academy, the position carried heavy weight and influence, possibly exceeding that of even the academy's [[headmaster]].<ref name="edge" />
