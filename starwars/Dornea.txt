'''Dornea''', located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Lothal sector]], was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Dornean]]s, who designed the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunships]] to protest their homeworld against the Empire. Some of the [[gunship]]s saw further action in a [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|growing rebellion]] against the Empire, especially after Dornea joined the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|officialized]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Dornea continued to use the ''Braha'tok''-class gunships through [[34 ABY]].

==Description==
Dornea was an astronomical object<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> located in the [[Lothal sector]]'s [[Dornea system]] within an intersecting portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]] regions.<ref name="Slice" /> It was situated at the coordinates U-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" />

==History==
[[File:Brahatok-classGunship-Fathead.png|thumb|left|300px|The ''Braha'tok''-class gunship was designed to protect Dornea]]
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Dornea's [[Homeworld|native]] [[Dornean]]s<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> designed the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]],<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> which were manufactured by the [[Dornean Braha'ket Fleetworks Conglomerate]] to protect Dornea from the Empire<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> and patrol the Dornea system. Following a [[Dornean treaty]],<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> some of these gunships saw service with a [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|growing rebellion]] against the Empire<ref name="Rebel Files" /> by around [[4 BBY]].<ref name="Date">The section of ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' that first mentions the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunships]] being part of the rebellion is set after the [[Blockade of Ibaar]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 BBY]].</ref> The rebellion became [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|officialized]] the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] in [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> and after the Alliance secured a decisive victory at the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> Dornea joined their cause and brought more ''Braha'tok''-class gunships with them.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

The [[gunship]]s continued to protect the Dornean homeworld after the [[Battle of Jakku|fall]] of the Empire.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date 2">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' establishes that ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' were buried on the same [[Standard day|day]] as the [[Battle of Endor]], which takes place in [[4 ABY]], according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Since ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' also establishes that the files were rediscovered thirty [[Standard year|years]] later, they must have been [[Excavation on Durkteel|uncovered]] in [[34 ABY]].</ref> the archive of non-electronic documents known as the ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' were uncovered during an [[Excavation on Durkteel|excavation]] on the [[planet]] [[Durkteel]] and eventually reached the [[Resistance]], a private military with roots to the Rebel Alliance. Resistance [[Admiral]] [[Ushos O. Statura]] questioned wrote a note in a section on the ''Braha'tok''-class gunship, questioning who still used them. Resistance Admiral [[Gial Ackbar]] left a note reply that Dornea still used the vessels.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dornea was introduced into the current [[canon]] when it was mentioned in the [[2017]] boxset and reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Dornea/Legends|Dornea]] was first mentioned in the [[1996]] novel ''[[Before the Storm]]'', written by [[Michael P. Kube-McDowell]].<ref name="Before the Storm">''[[Before the Storm]]''</ref>
