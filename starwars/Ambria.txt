'''Ambria''' was an unpleasant [[desert]] [[planet]], in tune with the [[dark side of the Force]], that was located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]. At some point, a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] was seen beside a [[Water|lake]] on Ambria. It was rumored that [[Darth Bane]], creator of the [[Rule of Two]] and the [[Grand Plan|Great Plan]], was [[Death|killed]] by his [[Sith apprentice]] [[Darth Zannah]] in a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] on Ambria.<ref name="HC 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ambria was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 47|forty-seventh issue]] of the [[De Agostini]] magazine ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'',<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|47|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> published around [[November 25]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdates">{{BMFdates|47}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Ambria/Legends|Ambria]] first appeared in the [[Tales of the Jedi 3|third issue]] of the [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (miniseries)|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries, written by [[Tom Veitch]], penciled by [[Janine Johnston]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="TotJ3">[[Tales of the Jedi 3|''Tales of the Jedi'' 3]]</ref> on [[December 21]], [[1993]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54568/star_wars_tales_of_the_jedi_1993_3|text=Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (1993) #3}}</ref> The planet was first pictured in the [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 2|second issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'' magazine series, published in [[2016]]. The issue used a comic image featuring a woman beside a lake<ref name="HC 2" /> originally printed in ''Tales of the Jedi'' 3<ref name="TotJ3" /> to depict the planet.<ref name="HC 2" /> In the comic, the woman and the lake in question were identified as [[Nomi Sunrider]] and [[Lake Natth]] respectively<ref name="TotJ3" /> but this is not confirmed to also be the case in canon.
