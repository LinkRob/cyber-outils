'''Culia''' was a substance that could be used to flavor [[water]] and was [[Diet|consumable]] by [[human]]s.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|TOTF}}</ref> [[Nihil]] [[Minister of Information (Nihil)|Minister of Information]] [[Ghirra Starros]] met with her [[Starros Clan|daughter]] [[Avon Sunvale]] in her [[Ghirra Starros' second office|office]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and poured them both a glass of culia-flavored water from a carafe.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

==Behind the scenes==
Culia appeared in the [[2024]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
