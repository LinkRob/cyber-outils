The '''Sector Group''' for the [[Anoat sector]] was one of the combined assets of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|Military]] and [[Imperial Security Bureau|intelligence]] for the [[sector]]<ref name="Dawn" /> under the command of [[Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]. The [[Sector Group]]'s [[Imperial Army|ground forces]] were lead by [[Commander]] [[Bragh]]<ref name="IR 1" /> and included [[Purge Trooper (Anoat sector)|Purge Troopers]]<ref name="Uprising" /> standard [[Stormtrooper]]s,<ref name="Star Wars 3">[[Star Wars (2020) 3|''Star Wars'' (2020) 3]]</ref> while the [[Adelhard's fleet|Sector fleet]], lead by Adelhard himself,<ref name="IR 1" /> was comprised of at least Twenty-four [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]].<ref name="BMF 20">{{BuildFalconCite|20|Starship Fact File}}</ref> The Sector Group's intelligence assets were lead by Imperial Security Bureau [[Lieutenant]] [[Kith Alaytia]].<ref name="IR 1" /> Shortly after the [[Battle of Endor]], [[Governor]] Adelhard reformed the Sector Group into an [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]].<ref name="IR 1" />
