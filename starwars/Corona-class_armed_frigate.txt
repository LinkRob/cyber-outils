The '''''Corona''-class armed frigate''', also known as the '''pirate saucer mothership''', was a large saucer-shaped combat [[frigate]] model used by the [[Ohnaka Gang]] of [[Florrum]], led by [[pirate]] leader [[Hondo Ohnaka]].

==Characteristics==
The ''Corona''-class armed frigate was heavily armed with offensive weaponry such as [[missile]]s and [[laser cannon]]s, as well the ability to capture and board smaller ships using [[tractor beam]]s and grappling harpoons. It was also able to stow and deploy smaller [[Flarestar-class attack shuttle|''Flarestar''-class attack shuttles]] for transportation purposes.<ref name="SWE Pirate saucer mothership" />

==History==
The Ohnaka Gang used ''Corona''-class armed frigates throughout the [[Clone Wars]], such as during a [[Raid on the Crucible|raid]] on the [[Jedi]] [[Paladin-class corvette|training ship]] ''[[Crucible]]''.<ref name="A Test of Strength">{{TCW|A Test of Strength}}</ref>

Years later, during the height of the [[Galactic Civil War]] and the Hunt for [[Han Solo]], the bounty hunters [[Dengar]] and [[Beilert Valance]] came into possession of one frigate, but it was heavily damaged by fellow bounty hunter [[Zuckuss]] and his [[starfighter]] the ''[[Mist Hunter]]''. The pair abandoned the damaged frigate on [[Nar Shaddaa]] due to concerns that it was being watched for their return, and made their way to [[Canto Bight]] on a commercial transport.<ref name="BH 12">[[Bounty Hunters 12|''Bounty Hunters'' 12]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Corona''-class armed frigate was derived from [[1950s]]-era film concepts of [[Wikipedia:Flying saucer|flying saucers]] (UFOs), such as those in the [[1950s#1956|1956]] film [[Wikipedia:Earth vs. the Flying Saucers|''Earth vs. The Flying Saucers'']]. The commentary of "[[Dooku Captured]]" revealed that the production crew had planned on using a flying-saucer type spacecraft in the [[prequel trilogy]] era since the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], either as [[Alderaanian]] vessels or [[Trade Federation]] spacecraft, but it wasn't until the production of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series that they got the chance to implement the idea.<ref name="Dooku Captured Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode011.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Dooku Captured}}</ref>
