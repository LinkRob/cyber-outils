***'''''[[5-Minute Star Wars Villain Stories]]''''', a 2017 reissue featuring only stories about ''Star Wars'' antagonists
**[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories (second edition)|'''''5-Minute Star Wars Stories''''']], a refresh of the 2015 title, published in [[2021]]
***'''''[[Star Wars 5-Minute Stories Audio Collection: Volume 1]]''''', an audiobook adaptation of the 2021 book
***'''''[[Star Wars 5-Minute Stories Audio Collection: Volume 2]]''''', an audiobook adaptation of the 2021 book
**'''''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories Strike Back]]''''', the sequel to the 2015 title
***'''''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories 2]]''''', a German audiobook adaptation
