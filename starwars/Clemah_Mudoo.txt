'''Clemah Mudoo''' was a [[Sullustan]] who worked as [[contract broker]] for the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] crime syndicate<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> they they offered contract jobs to independent criminals who had a good relationship with the [[Hutt]]s from the [[Desilijic Complex]] in the [[city]] [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. Mudoo had light skin, [[color|black]] [[eye]]s, and spoke [[Huttese]]. While operating from the Desilijic Complex in the [[Hutt Cartel District (Mos Eisley)|Hutt Cartel district]], Mudoo wore a grey [[clothing|vest]], of a brown shirt, brown pants, brown [[glove]]s and a brown [[hat]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
The [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] accepted contracts from Mudoo<ref name="Outlaws" /> during 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Clemah Mudoo appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. Accepting contracts from Mudoo is an optional activity which rewards the players with valuables, materials, [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], and increases the player's reputation with the Hutt Cartel.<ref name="Outlaws" />
