The '''Celto Slush''' was a frozen, plant-based beverage made from [[Celto/Canon|Celto]] sold at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="SWGE" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] and [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]]. As this beverage was sold at Galaxy's Edge, it was therefore sold in 34 ABY.</ref>

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Celto Slush was sold at [[Disneyland]]'s version of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] during the [[2024]] [[Season of the Force]] event.<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2024/03/disney-eats-season-of-the-force-foodie-guide-for-disneyland-resort-2024|text=Disney Eats: Season of the Force Foodie Guide for Disneyland Resort 2024}}</ref> It was sold at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] for $6.49<ref name="SWGE" /> from [[April 5]] to [[June 2]] of 2024.<ref name="DPB" /> It was a plant-based drink inspired by [[Celto]], a leafy green introduced in the [[The Star Wars Holiday Special|''Star Wars Holiday Special'']] released in [[1978]].<ref name="SWcom">{{SW|url=news/season-of-the-force-2024-food-and-beverage|text=Feast Your Eyes Upon the Galactic Treats Coming to Season of the Force at Disneyland Park - Exclusive}}</ref> The beverage was described as "Plant-based pandan-flavored horchata topped with cold brew."<ref name="DPB" />
