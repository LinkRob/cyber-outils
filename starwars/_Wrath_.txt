"'''Wrath'''" was the [[callsign]] of a<ref name="Wrath" /> [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> [[pilot]] for the [[First Order]]. They piloted a [[TIE/wi modified interceptor]]. "Wrath" once engaged [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s in their [[starfighter]].<ref name="Wrath" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Wrath" premiered in ''[[Fury of the First Order Squadron Pack]]'',<ref name="Wrath" /> an expansion for [[Atomic Mass Games]]'s ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'' miniatures game that released on [[September 24]], [[2021]],<ref name="Release">{{WebCite|url=https://www.wargamer.com/star-wars-x-wing/fury-of-the-first-order-release-date|text=Star Wars: X-Wing's Fury of the First Order and Resistance Y-Wings revealed|author=Callum Bains|date=2021-07-28|work=[[Wikipedia:Wargamer (website)|''Wargamer'']]|archivedate=20230929133137}}</ref> which included a card representing them.<ref name="Wrath" /> The pilot was originally previewed by Atomic Mass Games on Twitter earlier in the year on [[July 28]].<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|username=atomicmassgames|url=status/1420459103818850306|name=[[Atomic Mass Games]]|date=2021-07-28|quote=The TIE/wi Whisper Modified is a new addition to the First Order, bringing aspects of other TIE variants together into a potent combination of maneuverability and firepower. #StarWars #StarWarsXwing #Xwing #miniatures|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/BMkCj}}</ref>
