The '''Crimson E-Web''' was an [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]] owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Prototype E-Web|standard E-Web]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Crimson E-Web was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Sentinel's Prototype E-Web, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 4.0.0 update alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Crimson Vanguard]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing it within the Hunters in-game shop,<ref name="Hunters" /> or in the Crimson Vanguard Pack on the official Nintendo website.<ref name="Nintendo Store">{{WebCite|url=https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/star-wars-hunters-crimson-vanguard-pack-switch|text=STAR WARS: Hunters™ - Crimson Vanguard Pack|work=[https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/ My Nintendo Store]|archivedate=20250322014616}}</ref>
