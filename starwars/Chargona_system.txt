The '''Chargona system''' was located in the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Citlik sector]] at the coordinates O-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> The [[Chargona campaign]], a deployment involving the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]], took place in the vicinity of the [[star system]]<ref name="BTC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> no later than [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate|the mention of the [[Chargona campaign]]}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Chargona system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published on [[StarWars.com]] alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]].<ref name="Systems" /> Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]] and released on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
