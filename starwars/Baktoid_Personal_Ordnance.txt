'''Baktoid Personal Ordnance''' was a subsidiary of [[Baktoid Armor Workshop]]. It produced the [[BKX-4 "Shockwave" Grenade]], which was available during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baktoid Personal Ordnance was first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']], a [[2018]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']].<ref name="Fully Operational" />
