The '''Agriculture Hub''' was a [[city]] located on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Kamil]] in [[Wild Space]]. A popular activity in the city was the auctioning of [[Species|livestock]], especially [[runyip]]s, [[nuna]]s, [[moof]]s, and [[roba]]s. Various vendors were located in the Agriculture Hub including, the [[Livestock]], along with [[Restaurant|dining establishments]], like [[The Lucky Whistle]]. During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], [[Finn]] and [[Poe Dameron]] of the Resistance were deployed to the planet to negotiate a deal for livestock to feed the Resistance.<ref name="HS3" />
