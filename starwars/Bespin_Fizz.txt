'''Bespin Fizz''' was a fizzy<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> [[alcohol]]ic beverage that was [[Color|red]] in color and served in a tall and thin glass with a cloud of steam.<ref name="SW.com" /> It was suitable for [[human]] consumption, and shared its name with the planet [[Bespin]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> The [[cocktail]] was served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]]. During that year,<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[Chadra-Fan]] [[Gender|female]] [[Kriki]] drank the beverage during a visit to the [[cantina]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bespin Fizz is one of the alcoholic beverages that is sold in the [[In-universe|real-world]] [[Oga's Cantina]] bar at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] only.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> It was first revealed as part of a large promotional event held on [[February 26]], [[2019]].<ref name="Nerdist">{{WebCite|url=https://nerdist.com/article/star-wars-galaxys-edge-food-drinks-cocktails-menu-disney-parks|text=What You Can Eat and Drink in STAR WARS: Galaxy's Edge|work=[[Wikipedia:Nerdist Industries|Nerdist]]|archivedate=20190228074254|author=[[Amy Ratcliffe]]|date=2019-02-27}}</ref> The menu's description reads, "Bacardi Superior Rum, yuzu purée, pomegranate juice, white cranberry juice, and a cloud swirl."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/ogas-cantina/menus/dinner|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Oga's Cantina: Dinner Menu}}</ref>

The [[2020]] [[Canon|non-canon]] video game expansion ''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]'' includes the Bespin Fizz as a drink sold to the player's [[Wikipedia:The Sims|Sims]] at the Oga's Cantina vendor in the Black Spire Outpost neighborhood. Upon consuming the drink for the first time, the Sim automatically learns the recipe and can prepare it at mixology bars in their home neighborhood.<ref name="Sims">''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]''</ref>
