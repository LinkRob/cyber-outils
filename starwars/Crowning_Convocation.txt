'''Crowning Convocations''' were championships held on the [[planet]] [[Siline]] between the planet's youth every seven to ten planetary [[Standard year|year]]s when [[thermal vent]]s in the continental crust of Siline busted and spewed red [[Metal|metallic]] vapor into the [[atmosphere]]. They were made out of three portions meant to test the strengths and abilities of the combatants&mdash;a multi stage [[dueling tournament]], a six day and five nights long [[survival level]] in the wilderness and the [[great hunt]], where the combatants hunted the [[ericadi]], the planet's most dangerous predator. If multiple winners emerged, they would duel until only one remained. In earlier times these competitions had been nationalized competitions meant to test the abilities of the nations' future leaders and had been deadly, but during around the [[High Republic Era]], Siline became [[Silinese government|unified]] and the Crowning Convocations became international and more civilized. As such the artificially constructed [[Survival arena]] reflected all of Siline's biomes, the duels ended after "first blood" and the ericadi were tagged instead of killed. The winners of these Crowning Convocations received the [[crystal]] [[Convocation crown]] and had high chances of becoming future government officials, with the members of the [[Silinese War Council]] all having had an exceptional showing in the Convocations. Though almost all participants were [[Silinese]]s, inhabitants of other [[species]] between the ages of fifteen and twenty-five could also participate, though they were few. During the [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|Crowning Convocation]]<ref name="TCC" /> in [[134 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s [[Jecki Lon]] and [[Yord Fandar]] also participated and [[Mission to Siline|managed to stop]] a conspiracy during the great hunt.<ref name="TCC" />
