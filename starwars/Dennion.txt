'''Dennion''' was a [[human]] [[spy]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. They were conscripted by the Empire to fight against insurgents on their [[homeworld]], initially believing they needed to conform in order to survive. However, after the [[death]]s of friends who stood against the Empire, Dennion left to join the Rebellion. Dennion led a [[Rescue of Asara Deyn|mission]] in [[Canto Bight]] to rescue fellow rebel [[Asara Deyn]] from the [[Westhill Palace|mansion]] of [[Sliro Barsha]] of the [[Zerek Besh]] syndicate, recruiting the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] to break into the building. After Vess demanded payment upfront following Deyn's rescue, unconvinced by the rebels' cause, they [[stun]]ned her and left her behind.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Biography==

===Imperial service and defection===
During the Imperial Era, Dennion was conscripted into the [[Galactic Empire]] to fight against insurgents on their [[homeworld]]. While Dennion initially believed they needed to conform for the sake of survival, their beliefs changed after the deaths of their friends, who stood up to the Empire. Following this, Dennion defected and joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Rescuing Asara Deyn===
In [[3 ABY]], months after the [[Battle of Hoth]], Dennion was coordinating a rescue of [[Twi'lek]] and fellow rebel [[Asara Deyn]], who was locked in the vault of [[Westhill Palace]], a mansion owned by the [[Zerek Besh]] crime lord and [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[director]] [[Sliro Barsha]]. Aided by several other rebels and enlisting the aid of [[Human]] [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]], they [[Rescue of Asara Deyn|attacked]] Westhill Palace. Assaulting the outer walls, the distraction allowed Kay Vess, armed with the codes to unlock the vault, to enter the palace and fight her way to the vault.<ref name="Outlaws" />

Kay successfully unlocked the vault, but was under the belief that the goal was to steal [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and was unaware that Dennion was a rebel. Kay was surprised when the vault the codes opened held Asara. Dennion and the other rebels soon arrived to meet Dennion, but Kay held them at gunpoint and demanded payment. While Asara tried to convince Kay to join the Alliance, Dennion, believing there wasn't any time for that, decided to [[stun]] Kay before the rebels fled the palace.<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Later encounters===
Dennion would later encounter Kay Vess again when the latter accidentally discovered their [[Akiva production plant|hidden Rebel base]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]] while searching for parts to repair her damaged [[BX-series droid commando|BX-series commando droid]], [[ND-5]]. Dennion apologized to Kay, saying their first take on her was mistaken. Kay, however, said she wasn't interested in talking about it, saying things may have been different if Dennion had been truthful about the goal of the heist.<ref name="Outlaws" />

When Kay Vess, her [[Kay Vess' crew|crew]], and Asara were later [[Battle of the Revelator|escaping]] from the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Revelator]]'', Asara attempted to contact Dennion and the base, warning them that the Empire knew where they were and telling them they needed to leave. After witnessing this, Kay was convinced not to flee the battle aboard the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', and instead turn around again to fight the ''Revelator'', while sending out a [[distress beacon]] to call for aid from a crime syndicate she was in good standing with. Dennion would speak to Vess and many others throughout the battle. These efforts were successful, and with the aid of syndicate [[starfighter]]s summoned by Kay and Rebel [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s called in by Dennion, the ''Revelator'' was destroyed, saving the Akiva base.<ref name="Outlaws" />
