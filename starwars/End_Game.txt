"'''End Game'''" is a short story written by [[James Luceno]]. It was published in the new edition of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|''Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace'']] from [[Del Rey]] on [[January 31]], [[2012]].

==Plot summary==
Taking place after [[Darth/Legends|Darth]] [[Darth Maul/Legends|Maul's]] first [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber combat/Legends|duel]] with [[Qui-Gon Jinn/Legends|Qui-Gon Jinn]] on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], the story begins as Maul contemplates the mistakes he made because of the actions he took on the planet, since he failed his mission to kill Jinn and his [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and he failed to capture [[Queen/Legends|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Padmé Amidala]] as a result. But in spite of his failure, Maul's [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] [[Sith Master/Legends|Master]], [[Palpatine/Legends|Darth Sidious]], assigns him to overlook the rest of the [[Trade Federation/Legends|Trade Federation's]] occupation of [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]].

On Naboo, Maul takes the position as chief commander over the Trade Federation's occupying droid army, and he is disgusted over the idiocy and incompetence from the likes of the Federation's leaders in [[Viceroy/Legends|Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray/Legends|Nute Gunray]] and [[Rune Haako/Legends|Rune Haako]]. Nevertheless, Maul carries on with the mission assigned to him by Sidious as he commands the droid army to begin looking for the [[Gungan/Legends|Gungans]] and eliminate them as a threat. During his venture, though, Maul learns that Naboo's previous monarch, [[King/Legends|King]] [[Ars Veruna/Legends|Ars Veruna]], had been killed by a [[Muun/Legends|Muun]] named [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Hego Damask]] prior to the Federation's occupation of Naboo. The significance of this to Maul strikes him when he learns that Damask is actually working with Naboo's representative [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] to the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]], Palpatine, who is Darth Sidious's alter ego, in orchestrating the Trade Federation's blockade of Naboo.

From this, Maul hypothesizes that Damask may very well be a Sith Lord controlling Palpatine, and that Palpatine himself is disobeying the [[Rule of Two/Legends|Rule of Two]] by making Maul into a Sith Lord, even though Maul figures that he isn't really much of a Sith Lord so much as he is a weapon. However, Maul still carries on with his duties even as the [[Battle of Naboo/Legends|Battle of Naboo]] commences. The story ends just as Obi-Wan Kenobi defeats Maul by bisecting him across the waist, but Maul survives this through his sheer will, power, and hatred, fueled by the dark side of the Force, against the Jedi, especially Kenobi.
