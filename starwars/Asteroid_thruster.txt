'''Asteroid thrusters''' were a type of [[Engine|thruster]] which could be attached to [[asteroid]]s and used to move them. The owner of [[Osisis Station]], [[Galus Vez]], had an asteroid thruster hidden on an asteroid in his personal race track, [[Fool's Run]]. He used the thruster to drive the asteroid into competitor's vehicles will he raced, being able to activate it remotely from his [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|Gauntlet fighter]] ''[[Splendour]]''. Vez deployed the thruster during a race against the [[Twi'lek]] [[pilot]] [[Hera Syndulla]], hitting her [[starship]] ''[[Ghost]]'' and damaging its [[stabilizer]]s. Syndulla was still able to beat Vez in the race despite the damage, as two of her crew members used [[the Force]] to drive asteroids into the ''Splendour'' as well.<ref name="Ring Race" />

==Behind the scenes==
Asteroid thrusters first appeared in [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] "[[Ring Race]]" which was written by [[Martin Fisher]] and illustrated by [[Bob Molesworth]]. The strip was published in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]] series, which was released on [[January 28]], [[2015]].
