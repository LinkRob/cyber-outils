'''Abar-12''' was the location of [[Vemu]], which in turn was the site of [[Cylinder 14]]. When the [[Lawyer/Legends|attorneys]] [[Lilac Nani]], [[Medypsh]], and [[Zot Effervescence]] sent a letter to their partner, [[The Wavering Oeklass]], it was addressed to the latter at Cylinder 14, Abar-12.<ref name="Monsters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Abar-12 was mentioned in the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that combines creature designs and photographs from various projects of [[George Lucas]], creator of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, with original text by [[Bob Carrau]].<ref name="Monsters" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in ''Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas'' is [[Canon|non-canonical]]<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref> with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> However, the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' and its [[StarWars.com]] [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] built upon the mention of Abar-12 by introducing the [[Abar system/Legends|Abar system]], which was situated in the [[Tragan Cluster/Legends|Tragan Cluster]],<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> a part of the [[New Territories/Legends|New Territories]] portion of<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="EAOC" />
