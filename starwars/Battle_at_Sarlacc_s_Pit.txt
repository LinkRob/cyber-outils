'''''Battle at Sarlacc's Pit''''' was a board game released by [[Parker Brothers]]<ref name="Collectibles" /> in [[June]] [[1983]],<ref name="Cargo Bay" /> based on the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].

{{Game-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Your favorite ''Star Wars'' heroes need your help! Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia Organa, Han Solo, and Chewbacca are being held captive by the evil Jabba the Hutt. They're on his Sail Barge, high above the dangerous sandpit where the monster Sarlacc lives. To escape, you must defeat the Gamorrean Guards &ndash; and the powerful Nikto and Boba Fett &ndash; by pushing them overboard into Sarlacc's waiting jaws. Collect Jedi points for each guard you defeat. Then try to overpower Jabba the Hutt. The one with the most Jedi points at the end of the game is the winner!''<ref name="BGG" />
