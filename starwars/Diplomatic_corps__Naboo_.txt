The '''diplomatic corps''' of<ref name="QH" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Naboo]] was formed<ref name="QH" /> by [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> At some point, the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[Tepoh]] worked for the [[diplomat]]ic organization as an [[Aide|attendant]] and as part of a protective detail. By the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] [[Dormé]] monitored every member of the diplomatic [[corps]] and was notified when someone with a skill set suitable for the retinue of her employer, [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], was working with the organization.<ref name="QH" /> In 22 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Tepoh caught Dormé's [[eye]], and zhe was invited for an interview, though the latter later passed zher information to her former colleague, [[Representative]] [[Saché]]. After the representative hired Tepoh, Saché explained to zher that zhe had been initially interviewed by Dormé because she flagged zher skillset from working for the diplomatic corps.<ref name="QH" />
