'''Chiata''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Jedi Master]] who served the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Clone Wars]], training the [[Padawan]] [[Marseph]]. During a [[Battle against a Separatist dreadnought|battle against]] a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Providence-class Dreadnought|dreadnought]], Chiata's [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Crash Site|crashed]] onto the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]]. Chiata and Marseph both survived the crash, along with some of their men, but were separated. Chiata was found by her clone commander, but [[Order 66]] was executed just as he did and she was forced to kill him in self-defense before being attacked by more [[clone trooper]]s. Though she reunited with Marseph and mounted a brief defense, Chiata was fatally wounded, [[Death|dying]] of her injuries. Marseph then buried her among the wreckage before dying of his own wounds.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> She wielded two [[Color|blue]]-bladed [[lightsaber]]s.<ref name="GoH" />
