The '''128th Fighter Group''' served the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Starfighter Corps|starfighter corps]] utilizing [[TIE/IN interceptor]]s. The [[Flight group|fighter group]] was an elite Imperial unit alongside the [[181st Imperial Fighter Wing]].<ref name="BMF 33" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 128th Fighter Group was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 33|thirty-third issue]] of the [[De Agostini]] magazine ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'', published in [[2015]].<ref name="BMF 33" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the unit first appeared as the [[128th TIE Interceptor Squadron]] in the [[1998]] [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (video game)|''Star Wars: Rogue Squadron'']].<ref name="Rogue">[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (video game)|''Star Wars: Rogue Squadron'']]</ref>
