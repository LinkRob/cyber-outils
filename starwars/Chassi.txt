'''Chassi''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|dish]] that was available on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it was listed on the lunch menu of the [[Hot Pot]] [[Restaurant|eatery]] in the [[Market District (Mirogana)|market]] of the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] and costed three [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] for a small serving or four credits for a large.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chassi was mentioned on a menu in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
