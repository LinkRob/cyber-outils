'''Blick''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[lieutenant]] in the [[New Republic]]'s [[New Republic Defense Force|military]] during its [[New Republic Era|reign]].<ref name="Mando&Grogu" /> During or after [[11 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoDate|MandalorianAndGrogu}}</ref> Blick was stationed at [[Adelphi Base]],<ref name="Mando&Grogu" /> a New Republic base on the [[planet]] [[Adelphi]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="MandoVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> The lieutenant was sat at a table with a group of [[New Republic pilot]]s in the base's [[cantina]],<ref name="Mando&Grogu" /> [[the Pilots' Rest]],<ref name="MandoVG" /> when the [[Mandalorian]] [[mercenary]] [[Din Djarin]] and his [[Gender|son]], [[Din Grogu]], arrived with the [[Prisoner|captive]] [[Imperial Remnant]] [[warlord]] [[Commander]] [[Janu Coin]]. The seated group turned and watched as the pair marched Coin over to [[Colonel]] [[Ward]], the base's [[Commanding officer|commander]], to turn him in. Blick had [[Color|tan]] skin, brown [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]].<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blick was portrayed by [[Doug Chiang]] in the [[2026]] film ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''. The character's name was not spoken in the film but appeared in the credits.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />
