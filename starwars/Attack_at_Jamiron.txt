As part of the [[Grysk]] "[[Jixtus]]"'s attempts to lead the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] into its destruction, an '''attack''' was carried out at the [[Chiss]] [[planet]] of [[Jamiron]]. Jixtus had several falsified [[patrol ship]]s, supposedly from the [[Erighal family|Erighal]] and [[Xodlak family|Xodlak families]], attack a [[Clarr family]] [[convoy]] carrying valuable technology. Though the [[Freighter|Clarr freighters]] were highly damaged, their escort [[cruiser]], the ''[[Orisson]]'', commanded by [[Captain]] [[Clarr'os'culry]] "Roscu," as well as local Clarr patrol ships, fought back. The [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Grayshrike]]'', under the command of [[Senior Captain]] [[Irizi'in'daro]] "Ziinda," which had been posted to defend at Jamiron, also stepped in to assist in the battle. After two of the false patrol ships were destroyed, the others self-destructed, leaving nothing intact to be captured. However, from analyzing the wreckage, both Ziinda and Roscu determined that the patrol ships had been fake, though the latter assumed that it was the rival [[Dasklo family]] that had been responsible.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
