The '''Cassadrean Matrix''' was an artifact in the collection of the [[Jedi Archives]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Rising Storm" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> it was one of a number of artifacts sent to the [[Valo Republic Fair]] for display in the [[Starlight Pavilion]] as part of the ''[[Secrets of the Jedi]]'' exhibit curated by [[Jedi archivist]] [[OrbaLin]]. After the opening of the [[Republic Fair|fair]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] escaped from a conversation with Delegate [[Retar]] of the [[Ardennian Guild of Technicians]] by asking OrbaLin to show him around the exhibit, suggesting that Retar would find the Cassadrean Matrix particularly interesting.<ref name="Rising Storm" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cassadrean Matrix was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Rising Storm" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
