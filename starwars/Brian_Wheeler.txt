'''Brian Wheeler''' is an actor who played two roles in [[1983]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|Return of the Jedi]]''&mdash;an [[Ewok]] and a [[Jawa]].<ref name="Autographs" /> In [[2012]], ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine named the Jawa [[Snipp Nkik]].<ref name="YouTube Interview" /> <ref name="Rogues Gallery">{{InsiderCite|134|Rogues Gallery|Rogues Gallery: An Audience with Jabba the Hutt|reprint=1}}</ref>

Wheeler spent about two weeks filming in the [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] set, and one of his scenes was ultimately cut from the movie: when [[Luke Skywalker]] was hanging onto the grid of the [[Pateesa|rancor]] pit, Wheeler and [[Kalit|another Jawa]]<ref name="YouTube Interview" /> (played by [[Andy Herd]])<ref name="Andy Herd">{{WebCite|url=https://graphs.let-the-wookie-win.com/picture/Brian%20Wheeler_Andy%20Herd%202|text=Brian Wheeler Andy Herd|work=My Star Wars Autograph Collection|author=
Dave Louis Oldbury|date=2020-03-22|archivedate=20240522020131}}</ref> banged on his hands to knock him down. As their Jawa costumes reduced their visibility, however, they kept falling down the holes themselves.<ref name="YouTube Interview" />

Wheeler has also appeared in such projects as ''[[Wikipedia:The NeverEnding Story (film)|The NeverEnding Story]]'', several movies in the [[Wikipedia:Harry Potter (film series)|''Harry Potter'' series]], and [[Wikipedia:BBC|BBC's]] [[Wikipedia:The Chronicles of Narnia (TV series)|''The Chronicles of Narnia'' television adaptation]].<ref name="Autographs" />
