'''AD-97R''' was a [[Viper probe droid]] who resided in the [[Anoat sector]] at the time of the [[Iron Blockade]]. He was reprogrammed by scavengers to seek pillage on [[Hoth]], AD-97R had developed an extreme attachment to and fondness for its controllers.<ref name="Aydee" />
