'''''Forces of Destiny'': Season One''' is the first season of the animated micro-series ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]''. It was announced on [[April 13]], [[2017]] and on [[April 14]] at [[Celebration Orlando]] the episode "[[BB-8 Bandits]]" was premiered. The series debuted on [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] on [[July 3]].
