'''''Clone Wars'': Season Three''' was the third season of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]''. It originally aired from [[March 21]] to [[March 25]], [[2005]] (Chapters 21&ndash;25), and comprises [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume Two|Volume Two]]. Season Three was based on early plot outlines of the novel ''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]'', by [[James Luceno]]. It thus follows the basic plot of that book. However, since it was made separately from the book, there were some [[Star Wars Legends|continuity]] issues between the two.
