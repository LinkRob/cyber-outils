'''''Captain Phasma'' 2''' is the second issue in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Captain Phasma]]''. The comic focuses on [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] following the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. ''Captain Phasma'' 2 was written by [[Kelly Thompson]], illustrated by [[Marco Checchetto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 20]], [[2017]] under the [[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'']] publishing label.

==Publisher's summary==
''As the excitement ramps up for this December's Star Wars: The Last Jedi, come back with us to the final moments of The Force Awakens and the destruction of Starkiller Base to learn the fate of Captain Phasma! Captured by the Resistance and thrown into a garbage masher, we follow the chrome-adorned warrior from the site of the First Order's biggest defeat to the doorstep of this holiday season's biggest blockbuster! Written by Kelly Thompson (Hawkeye, Star Wars Annual 2016) and drawn by Marco Checchetto (Star Wars: Shattered Empire, Star Wars: Screaming Citadel), Captain Phasma will recondition you to love Star Wars again!''<ref name="Marvel" />

==Plot summary==
Flying their [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]] through [[Realspace|space]], [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] and [[First Order]] [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] [[TN-3465]] pursue the fugitive [[Lieutenant]] [[Sol Rivas]]. Phasma orders TN-3465 to close in on Rivas' TIE fighter so that she can shoot him only to discover that the [[droid]] [[BB-K8]] had failed to complete the reapirs to the [[laser cannon]]. Phamsa kicks BB-K8 in frustration before ordering TN-3465 to keep a distance from Rivas' fighter.

Phasma and TN-3465 follow Rivas to [[Luprora]], an [[Unknown Regions]] world which the First Order reported as having unsuitable environmental conditions. After landing their TIE fighter on the rocky, oceanic world, the two track Rivas' [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]] to the east. While navigating the planet, Phasma instructs TN-3465 to carry BB-K8 since the globular droid is unable to navigate the rocky, mountainous terrain. Phasma finds the atmosphere breathable but thinks the planet brings back ill memories of her [[Parnassos|former homeworld]].

They approach Rivas' fighter but find the fugitive has escaped. After disabling the communications, navigation, and distress beacon, Phasma deduces that he just ran out of [[fuel]]. Phasma also discovers evidence that Rivas was attacked by some kind of [[alien]] creature. The two take refuge in an abandoned dwelling. Using the gun-sight in her [[Phasma's blaster rifle|blaster rifle]], Phasma discovers a nearby village.

To avoid alarming the locals, the two change into mufti clothes. Phasma also takes care to hide her face by donning a helmet with a red visor. TN-3465 also changes out of her uniform, revealing herself as a young [[human]] woman with a black bob haircut. To avoid revealing their First Order identities, Captain Phasma tells TN-3465 to identify herself as Pilot while Phasma calls herself Captain. While wading through a lake, Phasma is attacked by a giant tentacled monster known as the [[Tsw'ells]], which grabs her in its tentacles. Following a fierce fight, Phasma manages to cut herself free and kill the creature with a large [[knife]].

Upon surfacing to the other side of the lake, Phasma and TN-3465 encounter a humanoid people with pale, greenish skin called the [[Lupr'or]]. Phasma realizes that the Lupr'or speak a variation of [[Lupre'en]]. A black-haired woman named [[Dar'en]] tells the visitors that the Lupr'or leader [[Jair'i]] wants to meet Phasma for killing a Tsw'ell, a beast of mighty strength and destruction. Jai'ri tells the visitors the history of his people while Phasma translates for TN-3465.

Phasma and TN-3465 learn that the Lupr'or escaped from their planed many years ago. Regarding technology as an evil that spoiled that former homeworld, they destroyed their [[starship]]s and adopted a primitive lifestyle based on fishing and farming. However, Luprora's climate deteriorated with rising sea levels and unpredictable waves eliminating much of the land they had settled and farmed. The Lupr'or also came under attack from Tsw'ells and a race of creatures known as the [[R'ora]]. The remaining Lupr'or had retreated to the safety of their [[Lupr'or Village|remaining city]].

Phasma learns from Jai'ri that Rivas was taken by the R'ora. While TN-3465 thinks that it is the end of the fugitive, Phasma claims that Rivas is her responsibility and cannot be left as a loose end. Ja'iri sends Dar'en to escort the offworlders to the [[R'ora stronghold]] in an attempt to show them how futile the task of "rescuing" R'ora is.
