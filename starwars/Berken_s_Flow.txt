Located on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Mustafar]], '''Berken's Flow''' was the largest [[Magma|lavafall]] in [[the galaxy]], as well as one of the few constant features of the lava planet.<ref name="BMF17" />

==Behind the scenes==
Berken's Flow was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 17|seventeenth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF17" /> published around [[April 29]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdates">{{BMFdates|17}}</ref> [[Berken's Flow/Legends|Berken's Flow]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it appeared in the [[2003]] [[Sony]] Online Entertainment [[Star Wars video games|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]], [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']], in its [[2005]] expansion, [[Star Wars Galaxies: Trials of Obi-Wan|''Trials of Obi-Wan'']].<ref name="SWG">{{SWGcite|exp=ToOW|type=location|[[Berken's Flow/Legends|Berken's Flow]]|[[Mustafar/Legends|Mustafar]]}}</ref>
