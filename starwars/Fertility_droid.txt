'''Fertility droids''' were a type of [[droid]]. Before [[Luke Skywalker]] was brought to them by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Beru Whitesun Lars|Beru]] and [[Owen Lars]] learned that they would not be able to have children of their own and considered their only chance of having kids was to start seeing one of the fertility droids in [[Mos Eisley]].<ref name="Beru Whitesun Lars">{{StoryCite|story=Beru Whitesun Lars (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>
