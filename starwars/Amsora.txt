'''Amsora''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[witch]] who served as a handmaid for the [[Brendok coven]] on the [[planet]] [[Brendok]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. [[Jala]] and Amsora prepared [[Mae-ho Aniseya|Mae Aniseya]] and [[Verosha Aniseya|Osha Aniseya]] for the Ascension ceremony. They cared for the girls together, as they were almost always together. Amsora cared for Osha.<ref name="Destiny" />

Amsora died in 148 BBY as a result of the arrival of the [[Jedi]] on Brendok and their dispute with the coven members. A massive fire broke out, claiming the lives of many members.<ref name="Destiny" />
