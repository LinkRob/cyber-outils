'''Aynaboni'''<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|aynaboni|Aynaboni}}</ref> was a tropical [[planet]]<ref name="Panel" /> located in [[the galaxy]]. Covered largely in [[ocean]] that housed some sea life, the planet also had at least one [[jungle]] island and was inhabited by [[aggrocrab]]s. During the early [[Imperial Era]], the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]] took a [[Crab Heist|job]] retrieving a crate from an island on the planet for the [[pirate]] [[Phee Genoa]].<ref name="Spoils of War" />

{{Planet-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aynaboni first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' episode "[[Spoils of War]]."<ref name="Spoils of War" /> The planet was named after the famous pirate [[Wikipedia:Anne Bonny|Anne Bonny]], because of the many pirate elements found in the episode.<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/spoils-of-war-episode-guide|text="Spoils of War" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref>
