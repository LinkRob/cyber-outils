The '''Amloch system'''<ref name="POD" /> was located in the [[Rayter sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]'<ref name="Appendix" /> [[Galactic Frontier]]<ref name="POD" /> and the [[Western Reaches]],<ref name="WesternReaches" /> situated in grid square J-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The system contained the [[ocean moon of Amloch]].<ref name="POD" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Zallah Macri]] and her [[Padawan]], [[Kevmo Zink]], rescued a [[prospector]] team from a superstorm in the [[ocean]]s of the [[moon]]. After successfully doing so, the pair stayed for an additional [[Standard day|day]] in the [[Star system|system]], which allowed them to be ready to help Jedi Knight [[Azlin Rell]] with a mission on the [[planet]] [[Tiikae]].<ref name="POD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Amloch system was first mentioned in [[The High Republic: Path of Deceit|''Path of Deceit'']], a young-adult novel written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and [[Justina Ireland]] and published in [[2022]]<ref name="POD" /> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase II">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
