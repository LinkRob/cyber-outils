The '''Army of Light''' was an [[army]] of [[Jedi]] within the [[Jedi Order]] that fought in several [[Jedi-Sith War|crusades]] against the [[Brotherhood of Darkness]]. According to old records, the ancient [[Jedi Master]] [[Morrit Ch'gally]] served within the Army of Light as a veteran of the crusades, and created the discipline of the [[Jedi Sentinel]], whilst a member of the [[Acquisition Division]].<ref name="Endless" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Hoth_and_Farfalla.jpg|Hoth and Farfalla.jpg|thumb|right|180px|The Army of Light as depicted in ''Star Wars'' Legends.]]
The Army of Light was reintroduced into ''[[Star Wars]]'' canon in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Endless Vigil]]''.<ref name="Endless" />

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the [[Army of Light/Legends|Army of Light]] was an army formed by [[Jedi Lord]] [[Hoth (Jedi Lord)|Hoth]] to combat the [[Brotherhood of Darkness/Legends|Brotherhood of Darkness]] during the [[New Sith Wars]].<ref name="EGTW">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref> It made its first appearance in ''Star Wars'' Legends in the [[Jedi vs. Sith 1|first issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Jedi vs. Sith]]'' series of comics, which was released on [[April 18]], [[2001]].<ref name="DH">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/99-222/Star-Wars-Jedi-vs-Sith-1-of-6|text=Star Wars: Jedi vs. Sith #1 (of 6)}}</ref>
