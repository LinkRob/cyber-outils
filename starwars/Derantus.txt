'''Derantus''' was an individual who served as a [[governor]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. At one point during the [[Imperial Era]], Derantus hired the [[Trandoshan]] [[Feresk Tssat]] to retrieve a piece of artwork, but when Derantus showed [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], it was discovered to be faux.<ref name="Light You Bring" /> As a result, Derantus put a [[death mark]] on Tssat.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>
