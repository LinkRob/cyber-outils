A '''BT-2X charge controller''' was a [[blaster]] component that allowed for a controlled overcharge of [[power cell]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the possession of a charge controller was a crime in the [[Galactic Empire]]. A group of [[slicer]]s had stored a BT-2X charge controller at a [[Slicer's Shelter|hideout]] in the [[South Jundland Wastes]] region of the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] heard about the BT-2X charge controller at the slicer's hideout. She later visited the hideout, recovered and used the charge controller to upgrade her [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]]'s power module.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A BT-2X charge controller appeared as an upgrade item in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The player must obtain the charge controller in order upgrade their blaser's bolt power module to level four.<ref name="Outlaws" />
