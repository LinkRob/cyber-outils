The '''Endor Gate''' was a [[black hole]]<ref name="Starships">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> located in the [[Moddell sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was located just off<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> the [[Bakura Trace]] [[hyperspace route]]<ref name="BMF 18">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> between the nearby [[Endor system]] and the celestial body [[Sanyassa]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> One of many spatial anomalies found in the area, the black hole could easily destroy a [[starship]] or throw it off course way past Endor, out into [[Wild Space]] or the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="BMF 38">{{BuildFalconCite|38|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>

During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[University of Bar'leth]] chartered a scientific expedition to study the Endor Gate. However, within [[Standard hour|hours]] of the expedition's first observations, its members were attacked by the [[Imperial Navy]], hinting that the Empire was hiding something in the Endor system.<ref name="Starships" />
