A '''''Providence''-class Dreadnought''' served in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]'s [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] and served as the [[flagship]] of a Separatist [[Aut-O's fleet|fleet]] commanded by the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] [[Commander]] [[Aut-O]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[20 BBY]], the [[Providence-class Dreadnought|dreadnought]] was [[Mission to steal a Separatist encryption module|infiltrated]] by a [[D-Squad|squad]] of [[Galactic Republic]] [[droid]]s, who had been sent to retrieve an [[encryption module]] located aboard. The [[squad]] later escaped the [[Starship|vessel]] with the encryption module, but not before Aut-O was destroyed in a brief incursion with the droids.

==Description==
As a [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]], the [[Starship|vessel]] had a main [[Hangar]] bay and a command [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]]. The [[dreadnought]] was also equipped with a [[tractor beam projector]], and had a passenger complement of [[B1-series battle droid]]s and [[B2-series super battle droid]]s.<ref name="Secret Weapons" />

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]] in [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">{{TCWcanondates|510}}</ref> the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] [[Aut-O]] was in command of a large [[Aut-O's fleet|fleet]] of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[warship]]s in an area of [[Realspace|space]]. The dreadnought which [[Flagship|flagged]] the [[fleet]] became [[Mission to steal a Separatist encryption module|targeted]] by a [[D-Squad|squad]] of [[Galactic Republic]] [[droid]]s led by the [[Zilkin]] [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]], who were sent by the Republic to board the dreadnought and steal an [[encryption module]] located aboard it. The encryption module would allow the Republic to decipher an intercepted and encoded transmission of Separatist General Grievous and discover what the Confederacy was planning.<ref name="Secret Weapons" />

After arriving at the fleet's location aboard a [[D-Squad's Maxillipede shuttle|stolen]] [[Maxillipede shuttle]], the [[DUM-series pit droid]] [[WAC-47]] set the squad's [[shuttle]] on a collision course with the dreadnought. However, the dreadnought, alerted by their arrival, instead pulled the shuttle into its main hangar bay with a [[tractor beam]], where several B1 [[battle droid]]s proceeded to search the vessel.<ref name="Secret Weapons" />
