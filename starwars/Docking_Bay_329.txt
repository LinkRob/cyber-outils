'''Docking Bay 329''' was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] constructed in the western hemisphere of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]], falling under the control of [[Station Control West]].<ref name="EOW" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> the [[YT-1300 light freighter]]<ref name="EOW" /> known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was pulled by [[tractor beam]]<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> into [[Docking Bay 327]], the bay was put on lockdown and all its traffic was diverted into [[Docking Bay 328|Docking Bays 328]] and 329, causing a seven [[Standard minute|minute]] delay.<ref name="EOW" /> Shortly after the ''Falcon'' [[Rescue of Princess Leia|escaped]], the docking bay was destroyed along with the rest of the Death Star during the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="ANH" />

==Behind the scenes==
