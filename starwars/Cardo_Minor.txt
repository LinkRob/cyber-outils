{{Quote|I re-upped my guild pilot's license, so it looks like I'm flying some locals to Cardo Minor for some sightseeing, but once we're out of Akivan space I'll disable the trackers and the ship is ours.|Norra Wexley|<ref name="Resistance Reborn">''[[Resistance Reborn]]''</ref>}}
'''Cardo Minor''' was a location<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> in the [[Cardo Minor system]] within the [[Meerian sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It lay at [[Standard Galactic Grid]] square O-6,<ref name="Appendix" /> which was also within the [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Resistance Reborn]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> when renting a leisure [[shuttle]] in the [[city]] [[Myrra]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]], the [[pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]] altered her guild pilot's license to appear as if she would be using the [[starship]] to transport Akivan locals to Cardo Minor for sightseeing purposes. In truth, Wexley planned to leave Akivan [[Realspace|space]], turn off the shuttle's trackers, and rendezvous with [[Resistance]] forces on the planet [[Ryloth]].<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />
