The '''DLT-19x targeting blaster''' was a [[blaster rifle]], [[longblaster]], and a variation of the [[DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle]]. Unlike the DLT-19, the DLT-19x released all of its power in one shot before it would need to cool down. The DLT-19x was an incredibly powerful weapon and could take down most enemies in one shot. The weapon had a scope that could be zoomed in farther than most scopes. This targeting blaster was silent and effective, and as such, was a weapon of choice for many snipers, and the primary weapon for some [[Stormtrooper sniper|Imperial sharpshooters]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> The [[LOM-series protocol droid|LOM-series]] [[bounty hunter]] [[4-LOM]] also wielded a DLT-19x,<ref name="Allies">''[[Allies and Adversaries]]''</ref> as did agent [[Del Meeko]] of the [[Inferno Squad]], who used it during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />
