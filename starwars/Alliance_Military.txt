The '''Alliance Military''', also referred to as '''Alliance Armed Forces''', the '''Rebel military''', or the '''Rebel Alliance Military''', comprised the paramilitary forces of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. It consisted of the [[Alliance Army]], the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]], and the fleet's [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] was the [[Commander-in-Chief]] of the military.<ref name="UVG" /> High ranking positions included the [[Supreme Commander of the Alliance Armed Forces]]<ref name="Lead" /> and [[Supreme fleet commander|supreme commander of the Alliance fleet]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" />

The Alliance military was rather large; by the time of the [[Mid Rim Retreat|Mid Rim Offensive]], it included hundreds of battle groups.<ref name="Twilight">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> During the final [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Rebel Alliance defeated the [[Galactic Empire]] at the [[Battle of Endor]], which led to the Alliance [[Declaration of a New Republic|reorganizing]] into the [[New Republic]] . The Alliance Military was therefore transformed into the [[New Republic Defense Force]].<ref name="Aftermath" />
