'''Drell''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] who was part of the [[Warbird gang]]. He was contacted by one of their spies, [[Synara San]] when she wished to speak with their leader, [[Kragan Gorr]]. San later helped smuggle Drell and fellow pirate [[Valik]] onto the ''[[Colossus]]'' to kidnap [[Torra Doza]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Synara&hellip;how goes the mission? Enjoying your new home?|Drell|<ref name="Synaras Score" />}}
[[File:DrellHologram-SynarasScore.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Drell is contacted by Synara San]]
By the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]], the [[Weequay]] Drell was a high-ranking member of the [[Warbird gang]], a [[pirate]] gang led by the [[Quarren]] [[Kragan Gorr]].<ref name="Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-8-fun-facts-from-synaras-score-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 8 Fun Facts from "Synara's Score" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref>

===The daylight raid===
During that year, [[Synara San]], a member of the gang operating as a [[spy]] on the ''[[Colossus]]'' platform, reached Drell when she contacted the rest of the gang. He mockingly asked her how she was enjoying her new home, and she demanded to speak to Gorr immediately. Gorr was soon able to reach her and was given the go-ahead to lead the pirates into an [[Raid on the Colossus|attack]] on the ''Colossus''.<ref name="Synaras Score" />

===Kidnapping Torra Doza===
Later, Drell and the [[Palliduvan]] [[Valik]] were sent to the ''Colossus'' to [[Torra Doza's abduction|kidnap]] [[Torra Doza]], the daughter of the refueling stations [[captain]], [[Imanuel Doza]]. The pair were smuggled into the [[Colossus loading docks|''Colossus'' loading docks]] in a container and San led them to [[Doza Tower]], where the Doza's lived. After San let them in, Drell and Valik then left her.<ref name="Doza Dilemma" />

When Synara learned about the true nature of their mission, she warned her friend [[Kazuda Xiono]] and Captain Doza. After Drell took out the security droids with an [[Electromagnetic pulse grenade|Electro Magnetic Pulse grenade]], he and Valik managed to capture Torra and bring her back to their ship, where Vonreg later betrayed them and shot Drell and the [[Trandoshan]] [[Skreek]].<ref name="Doza Dilemma" />

===Picking up Synara===
As Synara escaped from the ''Colossus'' during [[Occupation of the Colossus|its occupation]] by the First Order [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]], Kragan and Drell picked Synara up by using the skiff.<ref name="The First Order Occupation">{{Resistance|The First Order Occupation}}</ref> 

===Kaz's appeal===
Later, onboard the ''Galleon'', Drell heard from Valik intercepted an encoded transmission sent from the ''Colossus'', which she brought to Kragan. When she decoded the message, it turned out to be a transmission sent to the [[Resistance]] by Kazuda Xiono, calling for help.<ref name="Descent">{{Resistance|Descent (episode)}}</ref>

===Salvage the dreadnought===
Kragan, Drell and Valik are laughing that Kazuda Xiono want to help them to [[Skirmish over D'Qar|salvage]] the [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought]] ''[[Fulminatrix]]'' to get the [[coaxium]], as he tells Kaz they lackeys only take orders from one tyrant, Captain Kragan. As what Kaz said that ''Colossus'' can't jump into hyperspace, they agreed.<ref name="A Quick Salvage Run">{{Resistance|A Quick Salvage Run}}</ref> 

===Hunt the jakoosk===
Drell was surprised about the people of ''Colossus'', as he thinks that Doza's "got a real mess on his hands", Kragan said, "Which means an opportunity for us". Drell with Kragan to [[Aunt Z's Tavern]] as Kragan tells [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza|Aunt Z]] that he and his crew will go on a [[Mission to Celsor 3|hunt]] for [[jakoosk]]. At the [[Water|ice]] [[moon]] [[Celsor 3]], he and Snarl are on of [[skiff]]s. So Snarl use the [[thermal detonator]]s to get the jakoosk out of its hiding, until the it came. When it failed as their skiff was destroyed, they're stranded until Tora and Kaz come back with [[blaster cannon]] and the jakoosk woke up, they stole [[Blue Ace|both]] [[Fireball|of their ships]]. After the jakoosk have been defeated by Kaz with the big laser cannon, Synara pulled Drell's right arm while Snarl walks with him as she tells them to apologize for stealing their ships, and being stranded on the ice moon even for [[Buggles]]. After that, they headed to the service of jakoosk meat.<ref name="Hunt on Celsor 3">{{Resistance|Hunt on Celsor 3}}</ref>

===Selling super battle droids===
Not too long before the [[Battle of Exegol]],<ref name="TROS:TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> his sail barge the ''Galleon'' came up to the [[Unidentified icy asteroid|rendezvous point]] with ''[[Meson Martinet]]'' led by [[Sidon Ithano]] of [[Sidon Ithano's pirate crew|his crew]]. Drell carried a case of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to give Sidon to pay him. Kragan was so pleased to see a [[B1's B2-series super battle droid battalion|battalion]] of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s, so he ordered Drell to give the credits for the Crimson Corsair and with the battle droids can help his plan to [[Mutiny on the Colossus|take over the ''Colossus'']].<ref name="The Mutiny">{{Resistance|The Mutiny}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Drell was a [[Gender|male]] [[Weequay]] with [[Color|brown]] skin, black [[hair]], and gray eyes.<ref name="Doza Dilemma" /> He had an attitude problem<ref name="Bucket's List" /> and had sense of joy from wreaking havoc in the galaxy and shooting down enemies. Gorr trusted him with important missions.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|drell|Drell}}</ref> Drell had no scruples about kidnapping Torra Doza for the financial benefit of the Warbird gang, who worked as proxies for the [[First Order]].<ref name="Doza Dilemma" />

==Equipment==
Drell usually wore [[stormtrooper]] armor and was armed with [[thermal detonator]]s. He also armed with a [[DL-44 heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="Bucket's List" /> He carried an [[E-5 blaster rifle]] when trying to take over the ''Colossus''.<ref name="The Mutiny" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drell first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' episode "[[Synara's Score]]." He is voiced by [[David Shaughnessy]].<ref name="Synaras Score" />
