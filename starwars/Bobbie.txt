'''Bobbie''' was a [[WA-7 waitress droid]] who worked aboard the [[Serendipity|''Miss Fortune'']], a [[Cantina|bar]]/[[starship]] based on [[Cyrkon]]. She featured a built-in order transmitter that kept her in touch with the kitchen at all times, and a paired set of [[Repulsorlift|repulsor]] stabilizers helped her maintain balance as she went around her customers.

==Biography==
Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[WA-7 waitress droid]] Bobbie was serving customers upon the [[Starship|ship]] [[Serendipity|''Miss Fortune'']] when [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] spoke with the bartender. She approached and placed an order of two [[Juri juice|juri]], an [[incandescent]], and a bottle of [[bost]]. When the order filled, she picked up the tray and quickly delivered it. Later, four customers arrived and prompted to speak with the bartender, and Bobbie questioned them, unaware of the situation playing out around her.<ref name="SR" />
