'''''Expose, Pursue, Destroy''''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] created by [[Dasita Lyros]]. It depicted a regular [[stormtrooper]], a [[scout trooper]], and a [[Cold weather assault trooper|cold weather assault stormtrooper]] adopting idealized, hieratic poses, as well as the words "[[Galactic Emperor|Your Emperor]] commands you: Expose - Pursue - Destroy - Those who would resist us" in [[Color|red]] type (and in the case of the final words, black type on a red banner) using the [[High Galactic]] alphabet.<ref name="Propaganda" />

As open combat between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]] and Empire sent [[the galaxy]] into a state of [[Galactic Civil War|civil war]], the soldier art that depicted Imperials as sentries and vanguards became more dynamic. Troops were now depicted in action, and the sense of glory infused in the images evoked the more rousing posters of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Expose, Pursue, Destroy'' is an [[in-universe]] artwork in the [[canon]] art book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''. It was illustrated by [[Russell Walks]].<ref name="Propaganda" /> It had previously appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] sourcebook ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide]]''. Although its appearance there was largely similar to in ''Propaganda'', the final phrase on the poster in the former was "Those who would resist us!" instead of "Those who would destroy us!" Something similar existed with [[Strength & Obedience|Strength &]] [[Security in Strength & Order in Obedience|Obedience]] and [[COMPNOR Recruitment|COMPNOR]] [[Loyal? Prove It!|Recruitment]].<ref name="IHB">''[[Star Wars: Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide]]''</ref>
