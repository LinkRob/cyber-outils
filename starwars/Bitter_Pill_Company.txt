'''Bitter Pill Company''' was an [[infantry]] [[company]] in the [[Alliance Army|army]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] that was deployed during the [[Mid Rim Retreat]]. The company was sent to a [[planet]] which its members considered to be a "trash heap"<ref name="Twilight" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> as part of a final effort to distract the [[Imperial Military|forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Bitter Pill Company lost its [[troop transport]] on the planet but secured a replacement. News of the company's actions soon reached the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]], and one of its members, [[Sergeant]] [[Maximian Ajax]], briefly recounted the story to [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]].<ref name="Twilight" /> [[Captain]] [[Fennro Drogan]], an expert tactician and [[technician]], was a member of the company who later served at the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="GOH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Bitter Pill Company was first mentioned in [[Alexander Freed]]'s [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''.<ref name="Twilight" /> The company later appeared in the form of Fennro Drogan, a playable character in the 2015 mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''<ref name="GOH" /> who was made unlockable through a marquee event on [[August 31]], [[2023]].<ref name="EA">{{EA|url=games/starwars/galaxy-of-heroes/news/kit-reveal-captain-drogan|text=Kit Reveal - Captain Drogan|nolive=1}}</ref>
