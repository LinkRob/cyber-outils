The '''Capture of Theed''', also known as the '''fall of Theed''' or the '''Battle of Theed''', was when the [[Naboo]] [[capital]] of [[Theed]] was taken over during the early stages of the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo]] in [[32 BBY]]. The [[Trade Federation Droid Army]], lead by [[OOM command battle droid]] [[OOM-9]], moved its forces into the capital, consisting of [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tanks]], [[Multi-Troop Transport]]s, [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]], and thousands of [[B1-series battle droid]]s. In addition, battle droids flying [[Single Trooper Aerial Platform|STAPs]] and groups of [[OOM-series security droid]]s were in Theed as well. When [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and other captives were being transported, [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his apprentice [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], fought of the droid escort and lead them to the [[Theed Royal Palace]]'s [[Theed Hangar|hangar]], where the group escaped in a [[Naboo Royal Starship]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|This turn of events is unfortunate. We must accelerate our plans. Begin landing your troops.|[[Darth Sidious]], to [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]]|<ref name="Episode I" />}}
[[File:TF_Battleship.png|thumb|left|250px|The blockade of Naboo.]]
In retaliation to the taxation of the [[Free Trade Zone]] voted by the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Galactic Senate]],<ref name="SWCT">[[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]]</ref> the [[Trade Federation]], an interstellar shipping and trade conglomerate, [[blockade]]d the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], in an attempt to force [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]]'s hand in the matter. The Federation's actions were guided secretly by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]]. Valorum dispatched [[ambassador]]s from [[Coruscant]] on the [[Judicial Department]] [[Consular-class light cruiser|Consular-class space cruiser]] ''[[Radiant VII]]'' to the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Lucrehulk-class Battleship]] ''[[Saak'ak]]'' over Naboo. The ambassadors were secretly two [[Jedi Knight]]s, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], sent to negotiate the ending of the blockade with the [[Viceroy of the Trade Federation|Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]]. After Sidious ordered Gunray to begin the invasion of Naboo, the viceroy was skeptical, as he was concerned over the legality of invading Naboo. Sidious assured the Viceroy he would "make it legal" and told Gunray, upon being inquired as to what to do with the Jedi, to [[Escape from the Saak'ak|kill]] them. Gunray then tried to kill the Jedi with [[dioxis]] gas but failed.<ref name="Episode I" />

==Capture of Theed==
{{Quote|Viceroy, we have captured the Queen."<br />"Ah, victory.|OOM-9 and Viceroy [[Nute Gunray]]|audio=AhVictory-TPM.flac|<ref name="Episode I" />}}
<div style="float:right">[[File:Trade_Federation_Droid_Army.png|thumb|250px|The Trade Federation Droid Army advances on Theed.]]</div>
Gunray then spoke with [[Queen]] of Naboo [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Amidala]] where the viceroy reassured her no action would take place with the approval of the Senate and denied any ambassadors were sent. Amidala told Gunray he had gone too far before ending the transmission. He then promptly ordered a blackout of all communication arrays on the planet. At the behest of Sidious, the Federation began their [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] of Naboo.<ref name="Episode I" />

Meanwhile [[Senator]] Palpatine with the [[Naboo Royal Advisory Council|Royal Advisory Council]] in the [[Theed Royal Palace]] only to lose communication. The [[Governor of Naboo]], [[Sio Bibble]] was one of the first to suspect it was the start of an invasion. While [[Quarsh Panaka]] recognized the significant threat and that the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]] would not be able to defend the world against the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]]. However, Queen Amidala refused to condone any action that would lead them to war and believed the Federation would not dare go that far.<ref name="Episode I" />

Instructed by their control ship, the ''[[Vuutun Palaa]]'', several [[C-9979 landing craft]]s departed from the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleships]] to Naboo and landed in a [[forest]] on the planet's surface, where the two Jedi had stowed away aboard who planned to warn the Naboo that the invasion was coming. After learning the Jedi had escaped, Gunray contacted the invasion [[commander]], the [[OOM command battle droid]] [[OOM-9]], and warned him of the Jedi's presence among them and to not underestimate them. Once on the surface, Jinn rendezvous with Kenobi where he met a [[Gungan]] named [[Jar Jar Binks]] in the chaos of the Federation invasion. After saving Binks from a [[Multi-Troop Transport|troop carrier]], he swore a [[life debt]] to Jinn for saving him, rendezvoused with Kenobi and planned to find shelter, which Binks offered to give them in the [[Otoh Gunga|Gungan city]] beneath the [[ocean]] to avoid the approaching Federation forces.<ref name="Episode I" />

On the surface, Bibble's suspicions proved correct and upon realizing an invasion was underway, Amidala soon switched places with her decoy [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|Handmaiden]] [[Sabé]].<ref name="Episode I" /> Within [[Standard minute|minutes]],<ref name="Altaya Naboo">{{EncyclopediaCite|Naboo}}</ref> the droid armies consisting of [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tanks]], Multi-Troop Transports and [[Single Trooper Aerial Platform]]s took Theed, subduing Naboo's small security forces and leaving the planet completely under Federation control. The [[Royal House of Naboo]] capitulated and surrendered.<ref name="Episode I" />

[[File:QueenCaptured.png|thumb|left|330x330px|Arriving once the city was secure, Nute Gunray moved to assume control.]]
Arriving in the ''[[Lapiz Cutter]]'',<ref name="BMF 54" /> the viceroy then landed in Theed and assumed control from Queen Amidala's own throne room. After Amidala refused to sign a treaty legalizing the occupation, she and the rest of the prisoners were to be processed into [[Camp Four]]. However unexpected resistance was met by the escaped Jedi Knights who freed the queen and her party after a brief skirmish against the escorting droids, Bibble and several handmaidens decided to stay while the Queen went to [[Coruscant]] to plead for help in the [[Galactic Senate]]. The party then retook the [[Theed Hangar]] from the battle [[droid]]s stationed there, entered Amidala's [[Naboo Royal Starship|Royal Starship]], and blasted away from Naboo. However, in trying to slip through the blockade, the [[Starship|ship's]] [[hyperdrive]] [[engine]] took heavy damage under fire from the ''Saak'ak'', and the Royal House of Naboo had gone into exile.<ref name="Episode I" />

After the Queen and her escorts had escaped, Governor Bibble and two of the Queen's handmaidens, [[Saché]] and [[Yané]] were captured by battle droids. Bibble was escorted away by the droids while the handmaidens were given a blood test and imprisoned in the tent for Camp Four alongside [[Mariek Panaka]] and [[Tonra]]. During their incarceration, Yané used her skills as a [[weaver]] to create strips of cloth mapping out the prison camp and detailing the droids shift changes using the fabric from her and Saché's [[Flame-colored robes|outfits]] and utilizing the underside of their cot as a make-shift loom. Over the course of their incarceration, the inhabitants of the tent passed out the pieces of fabric to other prisoners who were instructed to memorize the information contained on them and then destroy them. The information passed along to the prisoners via these cloths helped the prisoners more successfully revolt against their captors when the Naboo reclaimed Theed.<ref name="Queen's Peril" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|This invasion will gain you nothing. We're a democracy. The people have decided&hellip;|Governor [[Sio Bibble]] to Gunray|<ref name="Episode I" />}}
[[File:Sith_Lords_Trade_Federation.png|thumb|right|250px|Viceroy Gunray and Rune Haako conferring with Darth Sidious and Darth Maul on an update on the invasion.]]
The fall of Naboo's capital city was remembered as the "Capture of Theed"<ref name="Complete Vehicles">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> and the "Battle of Theed."<ref name="AmidalaSeries1">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Padmé Naberrie - Handmaiden}}</ref> When Gunray informed Sidious of Amidala's failure to sign the treaty and that the queen had escaped, the livid [[Sith Lord]] introduced them to [[Maul|Darth Maul]], whom he dispatched to find and recapture Amidala. Due to Amidala's ship sustaining damage in the escape, the queen opted to land on the obscure desert world of [[Tatooine]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], that was controlled by the [[Hutt Clan|Hutts]], to repair the ship. Maul managed to ascertain the queen's location, but he failed to kill the Jedi or retrieve Amidala.<ref name="Episode I" />

The Trade Federation's [[droid]] army succeeded in occupying Naboo with little resistance. The viceroy and his [[Settlement Officer]], [[Rune Haako]] presided over the occupation of Naboo. On [[Coruscant]], the [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] Queen Amidala requested the [[Galactic Senate]] intervention, however the Federation's Senator [[Lott Dod]] managed to block Senator Palpatine's efforts. Meanwhile, Naboo's population was forced into camps where they experienced starvation and hardship. In Amidala's absence, the viceroy attempted to force [[Governor]] [[Sio Bibble]] to sign the treaty with the Trade Federation but the governor refused to back down.<ref name="Episode I" />

The [[OOM command battle droid]] [[Commander]] [[OOM-9]] meanwhile has [[Attack on Otoh Gunga|sent]] [[troop]]s to [[Attack on Otoh Langua|investigate]] the various rumored [[Gungan]] underwater [[City|villages]] on the planet.<ref name="Episode I" /> The [[Battle of Naboo|Second Battle of Theed]],<ref name="Qui-Gon Card">{{Topps|set=2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|subset=Jedi Temple Archives|cardname=Qui-Gon Jinn|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/114595/cid/8022614/2015-Topps-Chrome-Star-Wars-Perspectives-Jedi-vs.-Sith-10-J-Qui-Gon-Jinn}}</ref> which was also known under the name of the "Battle of Theed"<ref name="Battle of Theed">{{ForceCollection|Padmé Amidala [Battle of Theed]|star=4}}</ref> like the city's capture,<ref name="AmidalaSeries1" /> would later occur as part of Amidala's effort to liberate her planet.<ref name="Episode I" />
