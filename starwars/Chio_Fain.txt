'''Chio Fain''' was an [[Ardennian]] [[Gender|male]] [[slicer]] who operated during the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. After a failed job, Fain [[Death|lost]] his partner, [[Rone]], at the hands of the Empire, which made him eager for revenge. Fain was hired into a [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]], led by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]], working to [[Hunt for Vader|hunt down]] the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] for the [[Hidden Hand]] crime syndicate. When given the chance to face Vader in a skirmish on [[Heva]], Fain attacked the Empire's enforcer to avenge Rone's death. Fain managed to fend off [[Darth]] Vader for some [[time]], but was eventually overpowered and killed by the [[Sith Lord]].

==Biography==
===Slicer work===
{{Quote|I'll buy you time, Chio. Make the jump to lightspeed. Go!''"<br />"''Rone, wait&mdash;''"<br />"''I love you, Chio. Always and forever. Always and forev&mdash;|Rone and Chio Fain|<ref name="Target Vader 5">[[Target Vader 5|''Target Vader'' 5]]</ref>}}
[[File:ChioFainRone-TargetVader4.jpg|thumb|left|270px|Chio Fain watches as Rone is killed by the Empire.]]
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Chio Fain worked as a [[slicer]] with his partner, [[Rone]].<ref name="Target Vader 4">[[Target Vader 4|''Target Vader'' 4]]</ref> In [[6 BBY]],<ref name="Flashback">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Target Vader 4|''Target Vader'' 4]] to [[1 BBY]]. As ''Target Vader'' 4 establishes that Chio Fain's flashback takes place five [[Standard year|years]] before, it take place in [[6 BBY]].</ref> they undertook a job that involved slicing into property of the Empire, and Fain was skeptical of whether it was a good idea. The two slicers were eventually caught, and were pursued by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] above [[Ryarten]]. Rone began to fall behind, and chose to let the [[Starfighter|fighters]] catch up, allowing Fain to escape into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Target Vader 4" />

Fain attempted to protest, but Rone was [[Death|killed]], and Fain reluctantly made his escape. Due to Rone's death at the hands of the Empire, Fain held a hatred for the regime, particularly wishing to kill the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the enforcer at the forefront of the Empire.<ref name="Target Vader 4" />

===Hunt for Darth Vader===
====The hunt begins====
{{Quote|This ''is'' a crazy plan. But hey&hellip;at least we'll die doin' what we love.|Chio Fain|<ref name="Target Vader 2" />}}
Five [[Standard year|years]] later,<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date 3">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Target Vader 1|''Target Vader'' 1]] to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Fain was hired by [[Gwi]], a messenger for the [[Hidden Hand]] crime syndicate. The slicer joined a [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] of [[bounty hunter]]s and [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] whose goal was to kill [[Darth]] Vader. The team was led by the [[cyborg]] bounty hunter [[Beilert Valance]], and included the bounty hunter [[Dengar]], the [[Gamorrean]] tracker [[Honnah]], the [[droid]] and self-proclaimed [[Jedi hunter]] [[Arr-Nine-Nineteen]], and a [[sniper]] known as [[Gita|Urrr'k]]. Valance immediately destroyed Arr-Nine-Nineteen due to his dislike of droids, learning he held a [[Homing beacon|tracker]] for the [[Hutt Clan]]. With their team assembled, Valance was ready to begin their [[Hunt for Vader|hunt]].<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

[[File:ChioFainSlicing-TargetVader2.jpg|thumb|right|310px|Chio Fain slices into Fetya's databanks on Arvina.]]
The team traveled aboard Valance's [[starship]], the ''[[Broken Wing]]''. After Valance destroyed several [[TIE series|TIE]] fighters, Dengar was concerned that the Empire would be alerted, but Fain reassured him, having assumed Valance jammed the fighters' communications. However, Valance had only amplified the signals to purposefully lead the Empire to follow them. The team landed on [[Arvina]] to meet with the [[arms dealer]] [[Fetya]] in the town of [[Fetya's Hollow]]. While Valance spoke with Fetya, Fain sliced into her [[databank]]s, learning the locations of Hidden Hand outposts. Fetya turned on Valance due to the failure of a previous job, but an Imperial [[Star Destroyer]] arrived above Arvina, destroying the town. The crew escaped aboard the ''Broken Wing'', narrowly avoiding Vader, who instead [[torture]]d Fetya for information on the Hidden Hand.<ref name="Target Vader 2" />

====Facing Vader====
{{Quote|I've waited to kill this monster for five years, Honnah. I'm ''done'' waiting&hellip;always and forever, Rone&hellip;always and forever&hellip;|Chio Fain, before attacking Darth Vader|<ref name="Target Vader 4" />}}
The crew's next stop was a [[Heva Hidden Hand outpost|Hidden Hand outpost]] on [[Heva]], where they met with [[Wef]], an arms dealer whose stock included [[bot-drone]]s. As Fain sliced into the [[drone]]s' command center, the Star Destroyer<ref name="Target Vader 3">[[Target Vader 3|''Target Vader'' 3]]</ref> ''[[Formidable]]''<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> arrived above Heva, with Darth Vader on board. Valance attacked the Star Destroyer in the ''Broken Wing'', while Fain remotely [[pilot]]ed the bot-drones alongside the [[cruiser]]. Vader flew [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|his]] [[TIE Advanced x1]] in a skirmish against the bounty hunters, destroying several bot-drones. On Valance's command, Fain merged the drones together and used them to destroy the [[Conning tower|command tower]] of the ''Formidable'', causing the Star Destroyer to crash. The ''Broken Wing'' was damaged and also crashed, while Vader landed on Heva in pursuit of the bounty hunters.<ref name="Target Vader 3" />

With Vader on the ground, Valance prepared to advance his plan to use an [[electromagnetic pulse]] device to disable Vader's [[Darth Vader's armor|armor]]. Dengar, impatient, attempted to contact the other hunters, but Fain silenced him, not wishing for their positions to be given away. However, Fain was also impatient to kill Vader, having wished to do so ever since the death of Rone, and jumped down to fight the [[Sith Lord]]. Vader attacked him from behind, deflecting Fain's [[blaster]] bolts with his [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. Fain was hit, but refused to surrender, dodging Vader's attack and continuing to fire.<ref name="Target Vader 4" />

[[File:ChioDeath-TargetVader4.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Chio Fain is killed by Darth Vader.]]
Vader overpowered Fain, [[Dismemberment|slicing]] three of his [[Limb|arms]] off. Fain was saved by Honnah, who attacked Vader with her [[Gamorrean war ax|Gamorrean battle-ax]]. The two hunters, working together, managed to damage Vader slightly, but it had no effect on the Sith Lord. Vader used [[the Force]] to lift Fain, pushing him into the blade of Honnah's [[Axe|ax]]. Fain fell to the ground, fatally wounded, and whispered Rone's name before dying.<ref name="Target Vader 4" />

Following Fain's death, Vader killed Honnah, and Dengar revealed himself to be a [[spy]] for the Empire, betraying Valance.<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> Valance was captured and interrogated, but Vader ultimately left him alive, claiming to require his service in the Empire.<ref name="Target Vader 5" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Of ''course'' Valance is insane. Personally, I like that in a captain.|Chio Fain|<ref name="Target Vader 2" />}}
An [[Ardennian]]<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> [[Gender|male]], Chio Fain had [[Color|orange]] [[eye]]s,<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> gray [[hair]], and skin that was gray and tan in color.<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> The death of his partner, Rone, at the hands of the Empire made Fain eager to take revenge.<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> When introducing Fain to Valance, Gwi described the Ardennian as insane.<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> Fain lived up to his description, claiming to like being led by an insane [[captain]]. Even though Valance's plan was risky and had a high possibility of failure, Fain did not mind, as long as he got to die doing what he loved.<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> Despite his insanity, Fain claimed that he was not crazy enough to get close to Vader,<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> though later did just that in an attempt to avenge the death of Rone. Fain was confident in his ability to defeat Darth Vader, even after losing three of his four arms.<ref name="Target Vader 4" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|What the hell is that Ardennian doing?|Dengar, while Chio Fain slices into Fetya's databanks|<ref name="Target Vader 2" />}}
[[File:ChioAttack-TargetVader4.jpg|thumb|right|320px|Chio Fain uses his blasters in battle against Darth Vader.]]
Having carried out slicing jobs for a number of years,<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> Fain was an expert slicer, referred to by Gwi as the best in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> He was knowledgeable in the field of technology, claiming to notice a customized communications jammer made by [[Grenyt]] in the [[cockpit]] of the ''Broken Wing''.<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> Fain piloted bot-drones in a skirmish against the Empire during the hunt for Vader, and used them to cause the destruction of a Star Destroyer.<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> He could also fight with [[blaster]]s, able to hold off Vader for some time, but was eventually defeated.<ref name="Target Vader 4" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Okay, fine. You're pretty good against blasters.|Chio Fain, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Target Vader 4" />}}
In combat, Fain utilized up to four blasters of various models; one was a [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|DC-17]], which he was able to hold in his four arms. He lost three of his blasters when his arms were cut off by Darth Vader, but continued to fight Vader with a blaster held by his remaining arm.<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> During Valance's hunt for Vader, Fain utilized remotely-piloted bot-drones.<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> Fain wore a black and orange [[flight suit]] and had [[goggles]] on his forehead.<ref name="Target Vader 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chio Fain first appeared in [[Target Vader 1|''Target Vader'' 1]], a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] written by [[Robbie Thompson]], illustrated by [[Marc Laming]] and [[Cris Bolson]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> on [[July 3]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/77664/star_wars_target_vader_2019_1|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #1}}</ref> In ''Target Vader 1'', Fain is portrayed with green eyes,<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> though the rest of the series shows him having orange eyes.<ref name="Target Vader 2" />
