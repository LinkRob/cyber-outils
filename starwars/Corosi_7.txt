'''Corosi 7''' was a terrestrial [[moon]] with a rocky surface.<ref name="Star Wars 55" /> It was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and the [[New Territories]]' [[Corosi sector]], within the [[Corosi system]]. Corosi 7 was situated in grid square N-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

In [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars (2015) 55|''Star Wars'' (2015) 55]] to [[1 ABY]].</ref> [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] heroes [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Han Solo]] escaped the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Battle of Mako-Ta|attack]] on their base at the [[Mako-Ta Space Docks]] in a [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]], landing the [[Starship|ship]] on Corosi 7, where they received a transmission from [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]]. As the [[carrier]] was severely damaged, Solo took them to<ref name="Star Wars 55" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="Star Wars 13">[[Star Wars (2020) 13|''Star Wars'' (2020) 13]]</ref> [[Nar Shaddaa]], so that he could ask fellow [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] to transport them in her ship, the ''[[Volt Cobra]]''.<ref name="Star Wars 55" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corosi 7 appeared in the [[Star Wars (2015) 55|fifty-fifth issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series, which was written by [[Kieron Gillen]], illustrated by [[Salvador Larroca]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 55" /> on [[October 3]], [[2018]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/71181/star_wars_2015_55|text=Star Wars (2015) #55}}</ref>
