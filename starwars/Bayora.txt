'''Bayora''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Battlefront II" /> in the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where at some point in time, [[Lor San Tekka]] took a [[Map to Skywalker|map]] that led to [[Luke Skywalker]]. While [[Mind probe (Force power)|probing]] the mind of [[Del Meeko]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] [[Mission to Pillio (First Order)|learned]] that Tekka had taken the map to Bayora.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
