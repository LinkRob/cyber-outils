'''''Fly Fast and Die Young!''''' was a memoir written by a [[Unidentified retired ace|retired ace]]. [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]] read the book while he was a [[Youngling|child]] on<ref name="Rendezvous Point" /> the [[Core Worlds|Core World]]<ref name="Corellia">{{Databank|corellia|Corellia}}</ref> of [[Corellia]]. Antilles chose to read ''Fly Fast and Die Young!'' because he was interested in becoming a pilot, although he would later find out that the memoir and other media that described a pilot's life focused heavily on [[Gravity|high-g]] maneuvers and defiant messages sent via [[Comlink|comm]], while neglecting other key aspects, such as pilots' inability to [[sleep]] for long periods of [[time]] while flying.<ref name="Rendezvous Point" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Fly Fast and Die Young!'' was mentioned in the short story "[[Rendezvous Point (short story)|Rendezvous Point]]," written by [[Jason Fry]] and published in the [[2020]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="Rendezvous Point" />
