'''Fett Code''' was a [[language]]<ref name="YouTube">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref> spoken by the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] and his [[Cloning|clone]] son [[Boba Fett]]. When the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] visited the pair in their suite in [[Tipoca City]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]]<ref name="AOTC" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Jango spoke to Boba in Fett Code while conversing with Kenobi, after which Boba quickly shut the door to a room containing Jango's [[Mandalorian armor]].<ref name="AOTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fett Code first appeared in the [[2002]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'' Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="AOTC" /> It was first identified by name in the video "Every Language in ''Star Wars''" which was released in [[2019]] on the ''Star Wars'' Kids [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel.<ref name="YouTube" />
