'''''Darth Vader'' 5''' is the fifth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. It was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 16]], [[2020]]. It features the story "'''Dark Heart of the Sith Part V'''," in which [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] search is concluded at [[Polis Massa]], the birthplace of [[Luke Skywalker]].

==Publisher's summary==
''A QUEST FOR TRUTH&hellip;A JOURNEY OF DESTRUCTION! DARTH VADER'S search for revenge against those who hid his son Luke reaches a terrible climax &ndash; at the tomb of PADMÉ AMIDALA! What secrets does he learn? And who will pay as a result? All the answers are coming &ndash; in the ruins of POLIS MASSA &ndash; the birthplace of Luke Skywalker!''
