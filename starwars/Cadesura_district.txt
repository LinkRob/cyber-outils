The '''Cadesura district''', also known simply as '''Cadesura''', was a district of [[Zarra]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]]. A cultured region of the [[bridge city]], Cadesura contained an [[arts district]] and a [[Trade Federation licensing office|licensing office]] of the [[Trade Federation]].<ref name="Brotherhood" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> shortly after the beginning of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the Cadesura district was [[Cadesura disaster|destroyed]] in a bombing for which blame was pointed at both sides of the conflict.<ref name="Brotherhood" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ruins of the Cadesura district appeared in the [[2022]] novel [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']], written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="Brotherhood" /> The name of the district came from Cade Sura, the protagonist of fellow ''[[Star Wars]]'' author [[Michael Moreci]]'s original novels ''Black Star Renegades'' and ''We Are Mayhem''.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|mikechenwriter|status/1526631290555932672|[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]] - LIGHT YEARS FROM HOME is out now!|quote=#2: The Cadesura district of Cato Neimoidia's capital is named after Cade Sura, the protagonist of Star Wars Adventures writer @MichaelMoreci, from his excellent space opera books BLACK STAR RENEGADES / WE ARE MAYHEM.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/bwYPN|nolive=1}}</ref>
