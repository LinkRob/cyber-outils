'''Forthe''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] member of the [[House of Tagge]] and an employee of [[Tagge Corporation]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Forthe's [[Parent|mother]] was TaggeCo's Head of Talent Acquisition [[Layla Tagge-Faulken]].<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Forthe was present on the ''[[Acquisitor]]'' during a session in which Tagge Co's leader [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]] tested the [[Tagge protoblade]]. Domina remarked that, despite the weapon's efficacy, the [[Galactic Empire]] would not pay for it. Forthe asked her if that was because the weapon would be cont-ineffective to deploy at mass scale, which she confirmed. Domina addressed Forthe by name, telling the young man that young Tagges like him were the future of the company. Forthe beamed with excitement at the compliment, and even the fact that Lady Domina knew his name.<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" />

After leaving the testing area where Forthe was present, Domina confirmed with her secretary<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" /> [[Lapin Tagge]]<ref name="Aphra 28">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 28|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 28]]</ref> that Forthe was the son of Layla. When Lapin confirmed that, Domina implicitly threatened Forthe by remarking that Layla would be on her "best behavior" or something "unfortunate" could happen to her son.<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" />
