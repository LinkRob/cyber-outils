'''Back to Canto''' was a type of [[alcohol]]ic beverage that was listed in [[Aurebesh]] on a [[cocktail]] menu aboard the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> According to the listing, it cost thirteen [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Back to Canto was mentioned on a menu in the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref> In the game, it is only mentioned on an Aurebesh sign that players can find while aboard the ''Morenia'' during the story quest titled "The Game."<ref name="Outlaws" />
