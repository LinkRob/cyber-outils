'''Billow''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Neimoidian]] [[pirate]]. One of his stolen shipments has been intercepted by [[Poe Dameron]]. Billow caught up to him on [[Takodana]], but Dameron defeated him with the help of [[BB-8]]. He piloted a [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|V-wing starfighter]].<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
Billow first appeared in "[[Trouble on Takodana]]," a comic featured in the magazine [[LEGO Star Wars 55|''LEGO Star Wars'' 55]]. In the [[Wikipedia:German language|German]] version of the comic, he is spelled "Bilow".<ref name="LEGO" />
