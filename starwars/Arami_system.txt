The '''Arami system''' was located in the [[Galov sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was situated in grid square T-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and lay on the [[hyperspace route]] known as the [[Triellus Trade Route]],<ref name="Triellus" /> which linked it to the [[Gamorr system|Gamorr]] and [[Syvris system]]s.<ref name="TFABG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Arami [[Star system|system]] was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' boxed set.<ref name="TFABG" /> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Arami system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> ''[[The Clone Wars: Act on Instinct]]'', a [[Star Wars comic books|webcomic]] authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on StarWars.com<ref name="AoI">''[[The Clone Wars: Act on Instinct]]''</ref> over the span of the 2009<ref name="AoI announcement">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/news20091001comics/index.html|text=New ''Clone Wars'' Web Comic Tells Original Tale}}</ref>&ndash;[[2010]]<ref name="Lethal Trackdown Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode222.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Lethal Trackdown}}</ref> [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series,<ref name="AoI announcement" /> featured the first appearance of the Arami [[Star system/Legends|system]].<ref name="AoI" />
