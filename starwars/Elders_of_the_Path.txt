The '''Elders of the Path''' was a religious group which existed during the [[High Republic Era]] that was a descendant of the [[Path of the Open Hand]].<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> Divided into multiple communes, members were either [[Elder (Elders of the Path)|elders]] or acolytes within the group.<ref name="HRA 1" /> They were lead by the [[Council of Elders]].<ref name="HRA 3" /> One Elder, [[Barbatash]],<ref name="RtCT">''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]''</ref> had been an [[Elder (Path of the Open Hand)|Elder]] of the Path of the Open Hand but abandoned it as it disintegrated with the emergence of the [[Path of the Closed Fist]] and [[Night of Sorrow]].<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref> Another Open Hand member, the [[Gran]] [[Tromak]], had been one of the cult's [[Littles]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> before growing into an Elder of the Elders of the Path.<ref name="HRA 1" />

The Elders preached that [[the Force]] was like "[[fire]]" and that it should not be sensed by any living being, instead believing it should be appreciated from a distance. Relationships between elders and acolytes were considered far more important than those between [[parent]]s and children. The [[Force-sensitive]] [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]] and her parents were members of the group, as was Mrala's best friend<ref name="HRA 1" /> [[Krix Kamerat]].<ref name="HRA 2">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 2|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 2]]</ref> The commune Mrala and Krix were part of moved around from [[planet]] to planet until Mrala was ten [[Standard year|years]] old, at which point it settled on [[Trymant IV]] for what seemed a permanent basis. The [[Eye of the Nihil]], [[Marchion Ro]], captured Tromak to help him find a half to a [[Nameless control rods|Nameless control rod]],<ref name="HRA 1" /> which the order had hidden years prior after realizing its danger. After the [[Trymant IV disaster]], the Path's resources were in decline.<ref name="RtCT" />
