An '''extended hilt''' was a [[lightsaber]] modification that extended the length of the weapon's hilt, making the weapon easier to wield with two hands. The modification cost 3,800 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and was often used by beings with uncommon sizes or body shapes.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Extended hilts were mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
