The '''''Aka'jor''-class shuttle''', or simply the '''Mandalorian shuttle''', was a model of [[shuttle]] used by the [[New Mandalorians]] of [[Mandalore]]. It was produced by [[MandalMotors]] and saw use during the [[Clone Wars]] on Mandalore.

==Characteristics==
[[File:Mandalorian_shuttle2.png|thumb|left|150px|An ''Aka'jor''-class shuttle in landing mode]]
The ''Aka'jor''-class [[shuttle]],<ref name="Starfighters" /> also referred to simply as the Mandalorian shuttle,<ref name="Episode Guide" /> was a type of shuttle manufactured by [[MandalMotors]] during the [[Clone Wars]] for the [[New Mandalorians]]. The ''Aka'jor''-class was not equipped with a [[hyperdrive]],<ref name="Starfighters" /> measured at a length of 17.97 [[meter]]s,<ref name="AEYNK" /> and could reach speeds of 900 [[Kilometer per hour|kilometers per hour]]. Although unarmed, several owners modified their shuttles to carry weapons.<ref name="Starfighters" />

The shuttles had two rotating wings, one on each side, that changed position when landing, and acting as part of the landing gear. The wings were also decorated with Mandalorian designs. The front of the shuttle also had a landing gear [[Limb|leg]], and a platform with a ramp that lowered from the rear of the ship for entry and exit.<ref name="Starfighters" /> The shuttle could accommodate at least four passengers, and was crewed by at least two [[pilot]]s.<ref name="Mandalore Plot" />

==Role==
The ''Aka'jor''-class was intended for peaceful transportations between [[Mandalore]] and other [[planet]]s. They were often used to ferry individuals between Mandalore and its [[moon]], [[Concordia]]. The [[Mandalorian Guard]] also utilized the shuttles to conduct criminal investigations.<ref name="Starfighters" />

==History==
[[File:AkajorClassShuttle-DuchessOfMandalore.jpg|thumb|right|250px|An ''Aka'jor''-class shuttle landing in Sundari]]
Developed by MandalMotors for [[Mandalorian]]s, the ''Aka'jor''-class [[shuttle]], was a type of shuttle that saw use during the [[Clone Wars]] by the [[New Mandalorians]].<ref name="Starfighters" /> After the [[Attack on the Memorial Shrine|bombing]] of the [[Memorial Shrine]] in the capital [[city]] of [[Sundari]] on Mandalore saw the [[death]] of the [[Death Watch]] [[Death Watch bomber|bomber]], a [[Concordian]], [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] utilized an ''Aka'jor''-class shuttle to transport the body to Concordia.<ref name="Mandalore Plot" />

[[Time|Sometime]] later, a member of the [[Mandalorian Guard]] used an ''Aka'jor''-class to travel to Mandalore after [[scout]]ing Death Watch movements, and observed that they were preparing for an invasion of Mandalore. The scout reported to [[Prime Minister]] [[Almec]] of his findings.<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore">{{TCW|Duchess of Mandalore}}</ref>

''Aka'jor''-class shuttles saw continued use during the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars, with several shuttle present when [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] arrived on Sundari during a [[Mandalorian black market conspiracy|black market conspiracy]],<ref name="Corruption">{{TCW|Corruption (episode)}}</ref> and when [[Ahsoka Tano]] arrived to root out the conspiracy.<ref name="Academy">{{TCW|The Academy}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Aka'jor''-class shuttle debuted in the twelfth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the animated TV-series, ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', [[The Mandalore Plot]], released in [[2010]].<ref name="Mandalore Plot" /> It was identified as the Mandalorian shuttle in the accompanying episode guide,<ref name="Episode Guide" /> and later named as the ''Aka'jor''-class shuttle in the [[2018]] [[canon]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]'' written by [[Landry Q. Walker]].<ref name="Starfighters" />
