'''Christopher West''' is an illustrator whose work has appeared in several different ''[[Star Wars]]'' books and magazines. He specializes in cartography, and has illustrated many [[Starship/Legends|starship]] deck plans and other maps for the [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game|''Star Wars'']] [[roleplaying game]], ''[[Star Wars Gamer]]'', and various other products. He wrote and illustrated a ''[[Polyhedron]]'' feature article that officially explored the history of the [[Millennium Falcon/Legends|''Millennium Falcon'']], and continues to illustrate poster maps for the ''Star Wars'' [[Star Wars Miniatures|Miniatures Game]].
