The '''2R-series medical droid''' was a [[medical droid]] manufactured by [[Cybot Galactica]]. The [[RH-series research droid]], a [[science droid]] also produced by Cybot Galactica, was a variant of the 2R-series medical droid.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 2R-series medical droid was mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']], a [[2018]] sourcebook for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />
