'''Dathomiri''' is a term used to describe several things:

==[[Canon]]==
*'''[[Dathomirian]]''', the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] also referred to as Dathomiri.
*'''[[Dathomiri clan]]''', the type of communal structure in the society of [[Dathomir]]{{'s}} [[witch]]es
*'''[[Dathomiri spider-silk]]''', a type of [[silk]].

==[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]]==
*'''[[Dathomirian/Legends|Dathomirian]]''', the [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]] also referred to as Dathomiri.
*'''[[Witches of Dathomir]]''', the culture of [[Dathomir/Legends|Dathomir]] [[Witch/Legends|witches]] often referred to as Dathomiri.
*'''[[Dathomiri (language)|Dathomiri]]''', the [[Language/Legends|language]].
*'''[[Dathomiri boar]]''', the type of [[Swine|pig]]-[[Rodent/Legends|rodent]] native to Dathomir.
*'''[[Dathomiri Shamanism]]''', the religion practiced by the Witches of Dathomir.
*'''[[Dathomiri mystic symbols]]''', glyphs used by the Witches of Dathomir.
*'''[[Dathomiri spider silk]]''', a type of [[Silk/Legends|silk]].
