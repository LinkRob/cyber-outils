'''CIS Special Operations''' was a [[special forces]] branch active as part of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Confederacy military|military]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a [[The galaxy|galactic]] conflict between the CIS and [[Galactic Republic]].

==Description==
As part of the [[Confederacy military]], CIS Special Operations supported the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] government and greater military apparatus.<ref name="Outlaws" /> While the Separatist military was overwhelming composed of [[droid]]s, it did employ a limited number of [[Species|organic]] personnel.<ref name="DarkDisciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> Indeed, organic [[Agent|operatives]], who could answer to [[Military rank|ranks]] such as [[lieutenant]], existed amongst the special operations personnel.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==History==
[[File:BXSeriesDroidCommandosStrikeTeam-2025SepUnit.png|thumb|right|300px|BX-series droid commandos were special forces units active during the Clone Wars.]]
CIS Special Operations was active during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> a [[The galaxy|galaxy-wide]] war between the Confederacy of Independent Systems and [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="DarkDisciple" /> that raged from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Special forces units deployed by the Confederacy included the [[BX-series droid commando]],<ref name="TCWRookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref> which was deployed both into large-scale battle alongside other [[Separatist Droid Army]] units<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref> and into smaller-scale [[commando]] missions,<ref name="TCWRookies" /> and organic operatives.<ref name="Outlaws" /> For example, elite [[Commando (Techno Union)|Skakoan commandos]] from the [[Techno Union]] served the Separatist war machine despite the Union's alleged neutrality in the Clone Wars.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

The Clone Wars would [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] in 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> with the collapse of the CIS<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> and [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformation]] of the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]]. The Confederacy military was defeated with the state,<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> resulting in the dissolution of CIS Special Operations.<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Standard decade|Decades]] after the war<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Outlaws date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]], the [[human]] criminal [[Kay Vess]] discovered the still-functioning but heavily damaged BX unit [[MT-7]], who failed to realize the war was over and remained steadfast in completing his mission. At the suggestion of her partner-in-crime and fellow BX unit [[ND-5]], Vess claimed she was part of CIS Special Operations to gain MT-7's trust. Afterward, Vess&mdash;whose alleged rank in the special operations branch was lieutenant&mdash;and ND-5 completed MT-7's final mission to give the damaged BX [[droid]] peace, with ND-5 then introducing himself to the fallen commando as a [[commander]] and allowing MT-7 to stand down at long last. Afterward, ND-5 and Vess talked about what it would have been like for Vess to have served under ND-5's command during the war.<ref name="Outlaws" />
