This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. [[Ayo Tam]] was a member of this species.<ref name="Kenobi I" />

==Biology and appearance==
This diminutive [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] was distinguished by their [[Color|purple]] and mottled blue skin, as well as solid black [[eye]]s.<ref name="Kenobi I" />

==Species in the galaxy==
In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Kenobi date">{{KenobiDate|I}}</ref> a member of this species, [[Ayo Tam]], walked and talked with a small [[Aki-Aki]] near the [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] when [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] made his way towards an outbound [[Unidentified two-engined passenger transport|passenger transport]] in an attempt to save a young [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]].<ref name="Kenobi I" />

==Behind the scenes==
This species first appeared in the [[Part I|first episode]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' limited television series, directed by [[Deborah Chow]]<ref name="Kenobi I" />and aired on [[May 26]], [[2022]].<ref name="Companion">{{InsiderCite|214|Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi Companion|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi'' Companion}}</ref> The Ayo Tam bust, originally sculpted by [[Miles Teves]], was a collaborative design by [[Shaun Smith]] and [[Joe Pepe]] for a project that was ultimately scrapped. Years later, make-up effects artist Shaun Smith repurposed the detailed bust for use in ''Obi-Wan Kenobi''. Smith asked concept artist [[Dylan Howie-Richardson]] to create an updated design for the species,<ref name="FX">{{Facebook|username=Smith-FX|url=photo/?fbid=875445987918169&set=pb.100063583262385.-2207520000|name=''[[Shaun Smith]]''|date=2023-12-27|quote=Ayo Tam was one of several background characters we did on the Obi-Wan series. This was originally sculpted by Miles Teves for a completely different project that did not come to fruition. I had Dylan Howie-Richardson sketch out an alien based on the sculpture and thus Ayo Tam was born into the colorful Star Wars universe! He only appears in one scene of the series, walking in the background - let me know if you spot him!|archivedate=20240516152129}}</ref> which was then implemented as a background character within the series' first episode.<ref name="Concept">{{ArtStation|username=django42|url=artwork/G85bG3|name=[[Dylan Howie-Richardson]]|text=Obi-Wan Kenobi alien concept designs (1)|date=2022-11-28|quote=Work I did for the Obi-Wan Kenobi series on Disney+ designing makeup prosthetics. Featured are designs of aliens for Mos Eisley in Episode 1.}}</ref>
