The '''Axis droid leader''' was a reprogrammed [[K3 security droid]] that led the [[Vanguard Axis]] criminal cartel,<ref name="SWECG" /> an offshoot of the [[Droid Gotra]],<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> during the early [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Era|Era]].<ref name="Tribe" /> Between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> it encountered the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]] when they delivered a case of forged [[chain code]] chips to the<ref name="Tribe" /> [[Axis Base]] on [[Ordo Tera Spaceport]] [[space station]].<ref name="SWECG" /> After [[Omega]] discovered the Axis droids were trafficking the [[Wookiee]] [[Padawan]] [[Gungi]] and tried to free him, the Axis Leader intervened but was shot by [[Hunter]].<ref name="Tribe" /> The Axis droid leader was a blue and gold plated droid, with red and blue [[sensor]]s. It wore a [[bandolier]] with four [[thermal detonator]]s and [[Gungi's lightsaber]] on the side. It also carried a [[blaster pistol]] for combat.<ref name="Tribe" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Axis droid leader first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' [[The Bad Batch Season 2|season two]] episode "[[Tribe]]",<ref name="Tribe" /> released on [[February 1]], [[2023]].<ref name="BB S2">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-bad-batch-season-2-trailer-key-art|text=Clone Force 99 Is Back in New ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 2 Trailer}}</ref> In the episode the droid was voiced by [[Noshir Dalal]] and was credited as "Axis Leader".<ref name="Tribe" /> Prior to the episode's release, it was first pictured in the second trailer for the season.<ref name="Season 2 Trailer">{{SWYouTube|R15uYFpeBG0|Star Wars: The Bad Batch Season 2 &#124; Official Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/season-2-official-trailer-star-wars-the-bad-batch}}</ref> It was later first identified as "Axis droid leader" in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''.<ref name="SWECG" />
