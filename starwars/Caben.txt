'''Caben''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[krill farmer]] who resided in a [[Unidentified human tribe|village]] on the [[planet]] [[Sorgan]]. He, along with fellow [[farmer]] [[Stoke]], attempted to hire the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] to deal with raiders stealing their harvest. The pair offered to pay the bounty hunter with a monetary reward gathered from the members of the village.<ref name="Chapter 4" />

==Biography==
Cabin is a Krill farmer on one of Sorgan's villages but was being threatened by raiders who come to steal their harvest every week. Then one day, he and his villagers spotted an [[Razor Crest]|a ship]] in the sky and landed somewhere outside the village. He and his fellow farmer: Stoke took off to go find the stranger and hired him to help them. After a long journey, Caben and Stoke located the Bounty Hunter known as Din Djarin to help them with the raiders. Despite that it wasn't enough, Djarin decided to help and had to take some of their supplies off the ship, Djarin also recruited [[Carasynthia Dune]] to help them.<ref name="Chapter 4" />
