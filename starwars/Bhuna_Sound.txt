'''Bhuna Sound''' was a location<ref name="Vandor" /> in the [[Bhuna Sound system]] in the [[Yushan sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where the criminal [[Beckett's gang|Beckett gang]] pulled off a [[Bhuna Sound Heist|heist]]<ref name="Vandor" /> at some point prior to [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]'' states that the [[Beckett's gang|Beckett gang]] carry out the [[Bhuna Sound Heist]] and so it must take place prior to their [[death]]s, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[10 BBY]].</ref> The [[Corellian]] [[Tivoche Bilure]] mentioned Bhuna Sound and the heist in a section on the gang in the [[Midnight's book|book]] he wrote about his [[time]] working at [[the Lodge]] in [[Fort Ypso]] on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]].<ref name="Vandor" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Bhuna Sound was mentioned in the [[2018]] replica journal ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Vandor" /> The [[Bhuna Sound/Legends|location]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in "[[Lando's Commandos: On Eagles' Wings]]," a story written by [[Ian Edginton]] and published in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Star Wars Tales 5|''Star Wars Tales'' 5]], which was published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Wings">{{Tales|5|Lando's Commandos: On Eagles' Wings}}</ref> on [[September 27]], [[2000]]<ref name="DarkHorse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/00-110/Star-Wars-Tales-5-photo-cover|text=Star Wars Tales 5}}</ref>
