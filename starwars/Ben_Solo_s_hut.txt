[[Padawan]] [[Ben Solo]] resided in a '''hut''' at the [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|Jedi temple]] where his uncle, [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Jedi training|trained]] a [[Jedi Order|new generation of Jedi]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. It was destroyed during a confrontation between the two [[Skywalker family|Skywalkers]], precipitating the [[destruction of the Jedi Temple]] in [[28 ABY]].

==Construction==
[[File:BenSoloCabin.png|thumb|left|250px|Ben Solo's hut was located near Luke Skywalker's Jedi Temple on Ossus.]]
Located on the [[Jedi temple|temple]] grounds of the [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker]] on [[Ossus]],<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> [[Ben Solo]]'s hut was a simple dwelling composed of wood<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and stone.<ref name="TLJ EE">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> It contained [[Ben Solo's calligraphy set]]<ref name="Episode VIII" />&mdash;a set of pens, a box with perforations in the lid which served as pen sockets, and parchment scrolls with [[Jedi]] insights<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>&mdash;that was placed on a table near Solo's bed.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> The hut's entrance was covered by a curtain, and its design was similar to several other huts near the temple.<ref name="Rise of Kylo Ren 3" />

==History==
{{Quote|And the last thing I saw were the eyes of a frightened boy whose master had failed him.|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:MasterAndApprentice-2020StarWars50.png|thumb|right|250px|Solo lived in a hut on the Ossus temple grounds during his time as Skywalker's Jedi apprentice.]]
This hut served as the chambers of [[Padawan]] Ben Solo during his [[Jedi training]] under his uncle, [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]],<ref name="TLJ EE" /> who endeavored to rebuild the [[Jedi Order]] by teaching the ways of [[the Force]] to a new generation of [[Force-sensitive]] apprentices.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Solo was still living in the hut<ref name="TLJ EE" /> by [[28 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> at the age of twenty-three [[standard year]]s and was torn between<ref name="TLJ EE" /> the [[Light side of the Force|light]] within himself<ref name="Kylo Ren DB">{{Databank|kylo-ren|Supreme Leader Kylo Ren}}</ref> and the temptation of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]. Having grown to suspect the dark side's influence over his nephew, Skywalker decided to enter Solo's hut while he was asleep. He used the Force to look inside Solo's mind, and [[Force vision|foresaw]] that Solo would destroy everything that he cared about. Skywalker believed that what he saw would come to pass, aware of Solo's potential as a scion of the [[Skywalker family|Skywalker bloodline]]. Driven by pure instinct, he ignited [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|his lightsaber]] to strike down Solo, only to hesitate and realize that he could not [[Death|kill]] the son of his [[twin]] sister, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="TLJ EE" />

[[File:Wakey_wakey_Ben.png|thumb|left|250px|Solo awoke in his hut to see Skywalker standing over him with a lightsaber, an incident that precipitated his fall to the dark side of the Force.]]
Though Skywalker felt shame for contemplating this action,<ref name="TLJ EE" /> it was an unforgiveable act of betrayal to Solo,<ref name="TROS VD" /> who was surprised and alarmed to see that his master had come into his chambers, at night, armed with a [[lightsaber]]. Through the Force, Solo sensed that he was in danger, and while he saw the expression of regret on Skywalker's face, he believed that his uncle had gone too far to hesitate or falter in striking down his nephew. Solo [[Telekinesis|summoned]] [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|his own lightsaber]], which had been resting beside his calligraphy set, out of desperation, attempting to block what he saw as a killing blow from his master.<ref name="TLJ EE" />

Using the brief reprieve he gained from their blades locking, Solo telekinetically pulled the ceiling down, causing the stones to come crashing down on his uncle.<ref name="TLJ EE" /> Solo was the first to emerge from the wreckage of his hut; filled with rage, he inadvertently created a powerful lightning storm above the [[Jedi temple|temple]], resulting in the facility's [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destruction]] along with the apprentices inside.<ref name="Rise of Kylo Ren 1">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 1|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1]]</ref> Skywalker also survived, but by the time he emerged from the ruins of his nephew's hut, Solo had disappeared and the the temple was in [[Fire|flames]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />
