The '''Attack on the ''Executor'''''<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> was a battle between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> fought for the ''[[Executor]]'', the [[flagship]]<ref name="Vader 38" /> of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] after it was targeted by [[droid]]s that had been [[Scourge crisis|corrupted]] by the [[Scourge]] in order for the Scourge to target Vader himself,<ref name="Vader 37" /> who had defeated its [[Scourge attack on Fortress Vader|previous attempt]].<ref name="Vader 39" /> The droids, many of whom were part of the [[droid uprising]] [[Battle with the droid uprising|recently]] reprogrammed to serve Vader's will, took over the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|vessel]], compromising it, leading to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] requesting, via [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]], that Vader resecure it. During the battle, the [[Sith Lord]] was forced to fight the droids affected by the Scourge. Meanwhile,<ref name="Vader 37" /> a hostile force of five [[Star Destroyer]]s surrounded the ''Executor'',<ref name="Vader 38" /> which Amedda deemed a lost cause and ordered destroyed to contain the Scourge,<ref name="Vader 37" /> in a battle formation. However, [[Admiral]] [[Corleque]] delayed the order, giving Vader's own droids long enough to shut down the ''Executor''{{'s}} mechanical compliment. However, the Scourge had managed to seed itself onto Corleque's flagship,<ref name="Vader 38" /> the ''[[Unbreakable]]'',<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> which Admiral [[Firmus Piett]] had destroyed after giving his fellow admiral a mere four [[Standard second|seconds]] to contain the infection.<ref name="Vader 38" />
