'''Chal Hudda''' was a [[Swamp|marsh]]-covered [[planet]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that had a soupy [[Color|green]] [[atmosphere]]. Because its natural resources held use for almost no life-forms except the native [[Chalhuddan]]s, that world was of interest to virtually no one else.<ref name="Leia" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] birth in [[19 BBY]]. The events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' commence just after Organa's sixteenth [[birthday]], which places the events of the novel in [[3 BBY]].</ref> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] traveled to Chal Hudda on a mercy mission.<ref name="Leia" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chal Hudda first appeared in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]] that was released as part of the [[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi|Journey to ''Star Wars'': ''The Last Jedi'']] publishing program.<ref name="Leia" />
