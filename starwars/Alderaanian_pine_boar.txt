An '''Alderaanian pine boar''' was a creature that sweated profusely and partially shared its name with [[Alderaan]]&mdash;a [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> [[planet]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while [[Jedi training|training]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Dalna]], the [[Pantoran]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kevmo Zink]] and his [[Jedi Master]]&mdash;the [[Soikan]] [[Zallah Macri]]&mdash;stripped down to their [[Clothing|undershirts]] and trousers due to the [[Temperature|heat]] from Dalna's [[Binary star system|two]] [[Star|suns]]. After they had finished, Zink [[Brain|mentally]] noted that unlike Macri who did not look flushed at all, he was sweating like an Alderaanian pine boar.<ref name="Path of Deceit" />

==Behind the scenes==
An Alderaanian pine boar was mentioned in the [[2022]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and [[Tessa Gratton]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase II">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
