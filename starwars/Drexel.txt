'''Drexel''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Adventures Annual 2019" /> located in the [[Drexel system]] of the [[Lol sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing" /> It was situated in grid square Q-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The celestial body was associated with individuals known as [[Drexelian shell-digger]]s, who often dressed in ragged [[clothing]].<ref name="MA" />

In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' to [[40 BBY]].</ref> when meeting the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Rael Averross]], who was serving as the [[lord]] [[regent]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] inwardly compared his clothing to that of a Drexelian shell-digger.<ref name="MA" /> Between [[4 ABY]] and [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date2">"[[Hare-Brained Heist]]" takes place after the [[Battle of Endor]] but before [[Han Solo]] replaced the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''{{'s}} [[rectenna]]. As the ''Millennium Falcon'' has a new rectenna in ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', "Hare-Brained Heist" must take place beforehand. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]]. Therefore "Hare-Brained Heist" takes place between those two [[Standard year|years]].</ref> while attempting to infiltrate the [[Palpatine Archive]] [[space station]] disguised as an [[Imperial officer|officer]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] operative [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki|Jaxxon]] claimed to be bringing artifacts from places such as Drexel or [[Deltron]] when questioned by an Imperial [[droid]].<ref name="Adventures Annual 2019" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drexel was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' boxed set.<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Drexel/Legends|Drexel]] first appeared in the [[Star Wars (1977) 9|ninth issue]] of the [[1977]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']]. The issue was written by [[Roy Thomas]], penciled by [[Howard Chaykin]],<ref name="SW9">[[Star Wars (1977) 9|''Star Wars'' (1977) 9]]</ref> and published on [[December 13]], 1977.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53799/star_wars_1977_9|text=Star Wars (1977) #9}} {{C|On-sale date is noted in backup link.}}</ref>
