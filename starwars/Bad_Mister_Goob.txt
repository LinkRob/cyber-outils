'''Bad Mister Goob''' was a toy owned by [[Jyn Erso]] during her childhood on her [[Erso family|family's]] [[Erso homestead|homestead]] on the [[planet]] [[Lah'mu]], where she lived with her [[parent]]s, [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen]] and [[Lyra Erso]]. Jyn generally kept Bad Mister Goob in her toybox, but on the [[Standard day|day]] in [[13 BBY]]<ref name="ROUVG" /> that the [[Galactic Empire]] arrived on the homestead in search of her father, the toy was out with several others on her bed. Bad Mister Goob was made to look like a [[Color|white]]-[[Hair|furred]] bipedal creature with blue lips and a black face and [[eye]]s. It wore a small brown [[Clothing|robe]].<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Bad Mister Goob first appeared as a prop in the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which was released in North America on [[December 16]], [[2016]]. The prop was not identified in the film, but received a name in the ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released alongside the film.
