The '''Don Bies Archive Tour''' was a special feature on ''[[Star Wars Trilogy: The Definitive Collection]]'', the [[1993]] [[Wikipedia:Laserdisc|laserdisc]] release of the original [[Original trilogy|''Star Wars'' trilogy]]. It featured special effects artist [[Don Bies]] giving a tour of the [[Lucasfilm]] archives. One of the props seen was [[Lumpawaroo/Legends|Lumpy's]] mask, as worn by [[Patty Maloney]] in [[1978]]'s ''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]''.
