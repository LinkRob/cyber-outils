The '''Cataract of Moons system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Tungra sector]]'s [[Fand sector|Fand subsector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square K-18 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Cataract of Moons]].<ref name="Origin" />
