The '''291st Legion''' was a [[legion]] in the [[Imperial Army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. At some point, the 291st Legion used the newly released [[All Terrain Defense Pod|AT-DP]] [[walker]]s to clear out a nest of [[Thalassian slavers]] on [[Galpos II]]. The 291st Legion subsequently used the AT-DP walkers as the vanguard in urban fighting on [[Mendavi]].<ref name="Edge" />
