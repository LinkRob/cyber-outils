'''Darth Vader''' was the name of a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Human/Legends|Human]] who served the [[Galactic Empire (The Star Wars)|New Galactic Empire]] as the [[General/Legends|General]] of its [[Imperial Space Force|armed forces]]. A highly decorated [[Officer/Legends|officer]], he possessed severe scarring on his face, and also had his left eye replaced with a cybernetic, giving off a deep red hue.<ref name="TSW 1" />

Alongside [[Governor/Legends|Governor]] [[Crispin Hoedaack]] and their ally in the [[Supreme Tribunal]], [[Vantos Coll]], General Vader had plotted to overthrow [[Emperor (The Star Wars)|Emperor]] [[Cos Dashit]], with their move depending on the outcome of the [[Aquilaean campaign]]. Aside from command over the Imperial Space Force and the [[Space Fortress]], a deadly [[Battle station/Legends|battle station]], he was apparently granted carte blanche to use the [[Knights of Sith|Knights of the Sith]] to eliminate any [[Jedi-Bendu]] involved. Suspecting that General Luke Skywalker had survived the self-destruction of the rebel command post, General Vader tasked [[Prince/Legends|Prince]] [[Valorum (The Star Wars)|Valorum]] with tracking him down, providing the Knight of Sith with full access to Imperial resources for his assignment.<ref name="TSW 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Vader was a character in the original 1974 draft for [[George Lucas]]'s ''Star Wars''. Unlike later incarnations of the cyborg [[Commander/Legends|commander]], he was neither related to the main protagonist nor was he [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-sensitive]], although he did to some extent command the Knights of Sith.<ref name="TSW 1" />
