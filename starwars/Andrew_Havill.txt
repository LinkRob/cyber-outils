'''Andrew Havill''' is a [[Wikipedia:British people|British]] actor who portrays a [[Unidentified First Order officer (Exegol)|First Order officer]] in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. Other roles of note include Johns Hotchkiss in the 2012 science fiction film ''[[Wikipedia:Cloud Atlas (film)|Cloud Atlas]]'',<ref name="Film">{{WebCite|url=http://www.andrewhavill.com/film|text=Film|archiveurl=https://archive.is/cjMs4}}</ref> the head of the BBC Sound Department in the 2010 historical drama ''[[Wikipedia:The King's Speech|The King's Speech]]'',<ref name="Film" /> [[Wikipedia:Helena Bonham Carter|Helena Bonham Carter's]] fiancé in the 2002 British drama film ''[[Wikipedia:The Heart of Me|The Heart of Me]]'',<ref name="Film" /> The Equerry in the [[Wikipedia:Sherlock (TV series)|''Sherlock'']]<ref name="TV">{{WebCite|url=http://www.andrewhavill.com/television|text=Television|archiveurl=https://archive.is/IEhOz}}</ref> episode "[[Wikipedia:A Scandal in Belgravia|A Scandal in Belgravia]],"<ref name="WhatCulture">{{WebCite|url=https://whatculture.com/tv/20-doctor-who-actors-who-appeared-in-sherlock?page=9|text=20 Doctor Who Actors Who Appeared in Sherlock|quote=In A Scandal in Belgravia, Havill plays the Royal Equerry, Harry, the representative of the Royal Family and acquaintance of Mycroft's who brings Sherlock and John to Buckingham Palace to investigate the threat of Irene Adler and her incriminating photographs.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/YNj4P}}</ref> and a reverend in the ''[[Wikipedia:Midsomer Murders|Midsomer Murders]]''<ref name="TV" /> episode "[[W:c:midsomermurders:The Night of the Stag|The Night of the Stag]]."<ref name="TVMaze">{{WebCite|url=https://www.tvmaze.com/episodes/31841/midsomer-murders-14x06-the-night-of-the-stag|text=The Night of the Stag|quote=Andrew Havill as Rev.
