'''Free Draw''' was a type of [[Shift Token]] that was used in the card game [[Kessel Sabacc]]. It allowed the players to draw a card without having to pay a chip from their stock.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] won a Free Draw token from the [[Pyke Syndicate District (Mirogana)|Pyke Syndicate district's]] cardroom in [[Mirogana]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. Kess also won another copy from the [[Pyke Syndicate District (Myrra)|Pyke Syndicate district's]] cardroom in [[Myrra]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Shift tokens were created for Kessel Sabacc which first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. They are one of several Shift Tokens that could be collected and used in Kessel Sabacc, an optional activity in the game.<ref name="Outlaws" />
