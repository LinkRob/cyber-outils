The '''Beloved Paintwork''' was the standard body configuration utilized by the [[droideka]] [[Team Slingshot droideka|member]] of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]] during the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Beloved Paintwork was a "Base" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added when the game's early access went live via update 0.11.0 on [[November 16]], [[2021]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Wild Racer]]. This costume was unlocked by default when Slingshot became playable.<ref name="Hunters" />
