A '''build-a-space-center set''' was a [[Youngling|children's]] toy that was made up of many small pieces, which were used to create a toy space center. [[Han Solo]] once tried to replace his young son [[Ben Solo]]'s [[play blaster]] with a build-a-space-center set, but was stopped by his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], as it was a choking hazard.<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref>
