'''''Destroy Death Star''''' was a board game released by [[Palitoy]] in the United Kingdom in [[1978]].<ref name="Copyright" /> It shared its name with a [[Destroy Death Star (1977)|game]] released a [[1977|year earlier]] in the United States.<ref name="9 Classic">{{SW|url=news/the-vintage-vault-9-classic-star-wars-board-games|text=The Vintage Vault: 9 Classic ''Star Wars'' Board Games|nolive=1}}</ref>

==Gameplay==
''Destroy Death Star'' was a battery operated game in which players moved [[Starfighter/Legends|fighter]] [[Squadron/Legends|squadrons]] (shaped pieces of transparent brightly colored day-glo perspex) across a blue translucent plastic game board. Players had the chance to destroy enemy targets (and the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]] itself) by estimating the target co-ordinates from their position using two rotational knobs (one for horizontal, one for vertical) and then pressing the firing button. This operated a small bulb underneath the game board, and if the light was directly on a target and illuminated by the bulb, the target was destroyed and removed from the playing board. If it was not directly on a target, it was considered a miss.<ref name="BGG" />

The game was entirely based on the Palitoy Parker games named ''Salvo!'' which was released in [[1976]].<ref name="SWI 195">{{InsiderCite|195|Destroy the Death Star}}</ref> Cosmetic changes were made to ''Salvo!'' to transform it from a battleship-based game to a space arena.<ref name="BGG-Salvo">{{WebCite|url=https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/94074/salvo|text=Salvo! (1976)|archivedate=20211016214226}}</ref>
