'''Attacker Four''' was the designation given to a [[heavy cruiser]] by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. The [[heavy cruiser]], along with three others, was sent to [[Attack at Primea|attack]] the [[Chiss]] [[warship]] [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']] by the [[Lioaoin Regime]], under orders from the [[Nikardun Destiny]], when the ''Vigilant'' attempted to collect [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] from the [[Vak]] [[homeworld]] of [[Primea]]. Thrawn, aboard a [[Thrawn's patrol boat|commandeered]] [[Patrol ship|Vak patrol boat]], fired on Attacker Four, managing to cause significant damage while the [[Lioaoi]] were confused by the sight of a neutral Vak [[starship]] attacking. The ''Vigilant'' proceeded to escape in Four's shadow, preventing the other [[cruiser]]s from firing.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
