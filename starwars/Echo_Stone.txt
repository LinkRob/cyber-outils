The '''Echo Stones''' were powerful [[Force artifact]]s that originated from the mysterious [[Sophros|Planet X]] that acted as conduits for both the [[Light side of the Force|light side]] and the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] of [[the Force]]. Echo Stones could amplify one's connection to [[the Force]] and allow it to be wielded in unpredictable ways. The [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Ravi]] sought the power of the Echo Stones. The [[Tolemite]] [[Species|people]] placed Echo Stones in [[Nameless control rods|three rods]], which gave them the power to control the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]], creatures who fed on the Force and also originated from Planet X.

During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Barnabas Vim]] and [[Padawan]] [[Vix Fonnick]] [[Mission to Angcord|traveled]] to Angcord in search of artifacts, coming across an Echo Stone. The artifact was shattered by the Jedi and the shards were scattered across [[the galaxy]]. After their mission, Vim dedicated his life to studying the Echo Stones and discovered one in the possession of Jedi Master on Petris Major. Vim then attempted to return both of of the Echo Stones in his possession to Planet X, but his ship was damaged in the [[planet]]'s [[The Veil (Sophros)|Veil]] and he was never heard from again.

==Description==
[[File:FonnickShatterStone-THRAQOTJ.png|thumb|left|150px|Fonnick shatters the Echo Stone]]
The Echo Stones had the ability to enhance and amplify connections to [[the Force]]. The longer that an Echo Stone was connected to an individual, the greater their [[Force power|abilities]] became. The Echo Stone on [[Angcord]] had an extremely powerful connection to the [[Living Force]], being able to influence the well-being of an entire planet. The stone acted as a conduit of the Force, allowing Angcord to be supported by the [[Light side of the Force|light]] but also poisoned by the [[Dark side of the Force|darkness]]. The stone could allow its connections to gain immense amounts of power in the Force, as evidenced by the [[Lady of Angcord]] and the [[pilgrims of Angcord]]. While the Echo Stones could promote life and growth, they could also channel energy from the dark side of the Force into those connected to it.<ref name="Quest of the Jedi" />

==History==
===Ancient times===
[[File:RodOfSeasonsPathOfDeceit.png|thumb|right|150px|The Rod of Seasons and Rod of Daybreak, two of the three rods the Tolemites placed Echo Stones into]]
The Echo Stones originated from [[Sophros|Planet X]], a mysterious paradise [[Planet|world]] with a powerful connection to the Force.<ref name="Echoes of Fear 2" /> Over ten [[Millennium|millennia]] before [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date 1">''[[The High Republic: Tears of the Nameless]]'' establishes that the [[Tolemite]] civilization that created the Nameless control rods existed over ten [[Millennium|millennia]] before its events, which take place in [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:THRContent228BBY|here]].</ref> the [[Tolemite]] people placed Echo Stones into [[Nameless control rods|three rods]], giving them the power to control the [[species]] known as the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]], who originated from Planet X and fed on the Living Force. The Tolemites had brought the Nameless to their [[homeworld]] [[Tolis]], but their people were ravaged by the Nameless feeding on the Force within them, and a [[blight]] came in the Nameless's wake. The [[Three Ancients]], the Tolemite leaders, used the three rods containing Echo Stones to lead the Nameless back to Planet X in the hopes of saving their people and stopping the blight.<ref name="Tears of the Nameless">''[[The High Republic: Tears of the Nameless]]''</ref>

Sometime long before [[382 BBY]], an Echo Stone was hidden on the world of [[Angcord]] to hide it from the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Ravi]]. The people of Angcord used the stone to amplify their force connection and grow more powerful.<ref name="Quest of the Jedi" /> According to legend, Darth Ravi found an Echo Stone on the [[Dead Moon of Gothol]]. However, he was not strong enough to wield its power, destroying himself and the stone when he tried to channel it.<ref name="Echoes of Fear 1" />

===High Republic Era===
{{Quote|Because this is the place the Force showed the Mother during the flood. She was meditating deeply and saw this chunk of ice and an artifact known as Jedi Tears, a necklace made with a rare stone that reacts to the Force. That's our next liberation.|[[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Children of the Open Hand|member]] [[Cincey]], to fellow Path member [[Yana Ro]]|<ref name="Path of Deceit" />}}
In around [[482 BBY]], <ref name="Angcord mission Date">The present-day events of ''[[The High Republic Adventures: Quest of the Jedi]]'' take place between the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' and ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'', both of which are dated to [[382 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. The flashbacks of ''The High Republic Adventures: Quest of the Jedi'' take place around a [[Standard century|century]] before its present-day events, equating to around 482 BBY.</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Barnabas Vim]] and his [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Vix Fonnick]] [[Mission to Angcord|traveled]] to Angcord in search of [[Force artifact]]s. The [[Lady of Angcord]], as well as the [[pilgrims of Angcord]], drew power from the Echo Stone which corrupted them and the planet itself. Master Vim had sensed the [[Dark side of the Force|darkness]] growing on Angcord and warned the [[Lady]] of Angcord of the danger surrounding the Echo Stone. The Lady of Angcord, as well as the [[First Pilgrim]], refused to listen to Vim's concerns. Following a dream that revealed the true danger of the Echo Stone, Vim and his apprentice made a move to destroy it. The Echo Stone was effectively shattered, freeing the Lady of Angcord from her dark, raging state. Vim and Fonnick then took the fractured shards of the stone to different parts of [[the galaxy]] to create [[The Force|balance]] on Angcord.<ref name="Quest of the Jedi" />

[[File:TarakBorran-THRAEchoesFear2.png|thumb|left|200px|Tar'ak Borran with his Echo Stone on Petris Major]]
Vim then dedicated himself to studying the Echo Stones in the hopes of mitigating their danger and preventing them from being used as weapons. He eventually discovered that his old friend Jedi [[Wayseeker]] [[Tar'ak Borran]] was in possession of an Echo Stone on the planet [[Petris Major]]. Vim took a long journey to locate Borran on the planet, discovering that Borran's Echo Stone was slowly consuming him and making him ill. Borran then requested Vim's help to save an [[Oolorian settlement]] from a massive wave that threatened to destroy it. Working together, Vim and Borran channeled the power of the stone to raise a massive stone wall from the ground and hold back the wave. Afterward, Borran&mdash;who was injured by using the stone&mdash;agreed to give the stone to Vim for safe keeping. Borran revealed the orgin of the Echo Stones as Planet X.<ref name="Echoes of Fear 2" />

Vim then traveled to Planet X to attempt to return Borran's Echo Stone and his Echo stone shard from Angcord to the planet. While navigating through the planet's hazardous and tempestuous [[The Veil (Sophros)|Veil]], Vim left a final message to Fonnick explaining what he was doing. However, Vim's ship was damaged by the Veil and he was never heard from again.<ref name="Echoes of Fear 2" />

Around 382 BBY, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Azlin Rell]] searched the [[Jedi Archives]] at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] for information about the Echo Stone.<ref name="Quest of the Jedi" /> One was used in the [[Jedi Tears]] [[necklace]].<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref>
