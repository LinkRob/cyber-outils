'''Ardee''' was an [[astromech droid]] in service to a [[human]] [[Unidentified Luke Skywalker impostor|man]] who claimed to be the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[pilot]] [[Luke Skywalker]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Star Wars 5" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars (2020) 5|''Star Wars'' (2020) 5]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the man boasted in a [[Cantina|bar]] that he had [[Battle of Yavin|destroyed]] the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], claiming that Ardee had handled his [[targeting computer]] during the event. The droid's master was then approached by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who executed him to send a message to anyone else who thought of impersonating Skywalker.<ref name="Star Wars 5" />
