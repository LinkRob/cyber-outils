A '''speeder''' belonged to the [[Factory Workshop]] on a [[Unidentified planet (Screecher's Reach)|planet]]. The factory worker [[Baython]] stole the bike with the help of [[Quinn]] to travel to [[Screecher's Reach]] with their friend [[Daal]].<ref name="Screecher" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Development===
Baython's speeder appeared in "[[Screecher's Reach (short film)|Screecher's Reach]]," a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series, written by [[Will Collins]] and [[Jason Tammemägi]] and directed by [[Paul Young]]. The animated short, produced by [[Cartoon Saloon]], was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Screecher" /> on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Release Date, Studios Revealed}}</ref> [[Federico Pirovano]]<ref name="PirovanoWebsite">{{WebCite|url=https://federicopirovano.com/star-wars-visions-season-2|text=Star Wars Visions Season 2 &mdash; Federico Pirovano|archiveurl=https://archive.is/3AoTY}}</ref> and [[Leo Weiss]] created concept art for Baython's speeder alongside pieces for the other speeders featured in the short.<ref name="WeissWebsite">{{WebCite|url=https://leo-weiss.com/screechers-reach|text=Screecher's Reach &mdash; Leo Weiss|archiveurl=https://archive.is/g1WsJ}}</ref>
