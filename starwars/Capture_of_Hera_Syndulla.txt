Sometime after the [[Battle of Yavin]], a crew of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] led by [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] traveled to the ''[[Lucrehulk Prime]]'' to '''capture''' the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]. When they arrived, the [[Alliance officer]] [[Bini]] thought they were recruits and led them to the control core to meet Syndulla. Syndulla shot Aphra's [[Tooka]] when it ran into the control room and [[Tam Posla]] revealed that they weren't recruits, leading her to realize they were mercenaries.

Syndulla had the group locked up in the [[brig]] and [[Magna Tolvan]], who was disguised as the mercenary [[Glahst Ombra]], entered Aphra's cell. She revealed her true identity and [[Dek-Nil]] stunned her, making her shoot the [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] and freeing the mercenaries. However, the rebels found them still in the cells and Syndulla offered to let them leave. The mercenary [[Sister Six]] then stunned Syndulla and the surrounding rebels and the mercenaries escaped with Syndulla.
