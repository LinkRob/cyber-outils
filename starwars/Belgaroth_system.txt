The '''Belgaroth system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]] that contained the [[Belgaroth asteroid field]]. It was situated at the coordinates K-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Belgaroth system was mentioned in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> It previously appeared in the episode "[[The Mines of Graballa]]" of the non-canonical [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''.<ref name="Freemaker">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Mines of Graballa}}</ref>
