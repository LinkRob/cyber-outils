'''Burundang''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Crash and Burn" /> in the [[Burundang system]] in the [[Harron sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where [[Han Solo]] bought a [[play blaster]] for his son [[Ben Solo]]. However, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] did not approve of the purchase, telling Solo that the blaster was too violent to be an appropriate children's toy.<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Burundang was first mentioned in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Last Shot]]'', written by [[Daniel José Older]].<ref name="Last Shot" /> The original solicitation for the [[2021]] comic book [[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 5]], also written by Older, stated that the issue's plot involved a [[Nihil]] attack on Burundang.<ref name="Solicits">{{WebCite|url=https://blog.gocollect.com/idw-publishing-june-2021-solicitations|text=IDW Publishing June 2021 Solicitations|work=[https://blog.gocollect.com GoCollect]|archivedate=20210319022332}}</ref> However, the final issue takes place largely on [[Quantxi]], a [[moon]] of [[Ord Mantell]], and has no mention of Burundang.<ref name="HRA 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 5]]</ref>
