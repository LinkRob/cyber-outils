'''Firrerre''' was a [[Planet|world]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Sugai sector]], a subsector of the [[Zuma sector]]. It was the site of a [[New Republic Space Ministry Firrerre Office|consular office]] for [[New Republic Space Ministry]] that issued the [[METOSP]] titled [[558 ModSuFi]], warning [[spacer]]s to avoid the planet [[Jakku]]. 

==Description==
Firrerre was a terrestrial<ref name="RSG" /> [[Planet|world]]<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|38|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> located in the [[Firrerre system]] of the [[Sugai sector]], a sub-[[sector]] of the [[Zuma sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Falling within grid square H-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> it was considered local to the [[moon]] [[Endor]],<ref name="BMF" /> which was located in the same grid square in the [[Moddell sector]], another subsector of the Zuma sector.<ref name="Endor">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Endor system]]</ref>

==History==
[[File:New_Republic_canon.svg|thumb|right|300px|alt=The emblem of the New Republic, consisting of a blue Rebel Alliance starbird with yellow points radiating from it.|Firrerre housed a New Republic consular office.]]
Until the [[Galactic Empire]] created the [[Sanctuary Pipeline]] [[hyperspace route]] to Endor during the [[Imperial Era]], the inhabitants of Firrerre and other local worlds like [[Bakura]] and [[Trenwyth]] largely ignored the moon due to its proximity to the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="BMF" /> The [[New Republic Space Ministry]] had a [[New Republic Space Ministry Firrerre Office|consular office]] on Firrerre that was considered to be the closest [[New Republic]] authority for [[spacer]]s passing in and around the planet [[Jakku]]<ref name="RSG" /> which was located in grid square I-13 in the [[Inner Rim Territories]].<ref name="Jakku">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Jakku system]]</ref> The office issued a red level [[METOSP]] titled [[558 ModSuFi]] warning concerning the Jakku via its [[Moddell Node]] and [[Sugai Subnode]].<ref name="RSG" />

The warning advised spacers to avoid approaching or landing on Jakku due to the hazardous environment on and around the world, as well as the minimal services, trade, and [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] provided by the residents. Neither traveler's assistance nor the ability to request such assistance was available to trailing on Firrerre, and if assistance was required by travelers on Jakku they were advised to report to the Firrerre office in person.<ref name="RSG" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' includes an addendum at the end describing some of the events of {{Film|VII}}, written several [[Standard day|days]] after they take place. The addendum is the final entry in the guide and is included chronologically after the entry mentioning Firrerre. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the film to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] discovered a copy of the office's warning on the [[Pilgrim's Road]] on Jakku and, believing it to be good advice, included it in a [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]] she compiled while living on Jakku.<ref name="RSG" /> In 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Starcruiserdate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Firrerre's position was marked on several [[Viewscreen|screens]] aboard [[Chandrila Star Line]]'s [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' that displayed a partial [[Star map|map]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="GS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Firrerre-TEGTPAM.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=An image of Firrerre shows its surface covered with a mixture of landmasses and oceans. A vertical section of the world is removed from the sphere to show a cross section of its internal layers. Part of one of the world's landmasses is highlighted and a close-up view of the surface there is provided beneath the image of the planet, showing dots representing the Fierrerre captial and a spaceport.|Firrerre originated in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity.]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Firrerre was first mentioned in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" section of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 38|thirty-eighth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine series,<ref name="BMF" /> which was written by [[Mark Newbold]]<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> and published around [[September 23]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|38}}</ref> It then first appeared on several maps viewable in [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]], an immersive two-day roleplaying experience at [[W:c:disney:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]] in Florida<ref name="GS" /> that opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]]<ref name="31 Things">{{SW|url=news/31-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=31 Things We Learned in an Exclusive Preview of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}}</ref> and was launched for the final time on [[September 28]], [[2023]].<ref name="GalacticStarcruiserClosure">{{WebCite|url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/travel/2023/05/18/star-wars-hotel-closing|text=Disney to close its $5,000 Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser hotel|author=Hannah Sampson|date=2023-05-18|work=[[Wikipedia:The Washington Post|''The Washington Post'']]|archivedate=20230519064024}}</ref>

The [[Firrerre/Legends|world]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in ''[[The Crystal Star]]'', a [[1994]] novel written by [[Vonda N. McIntyre]].<ref name="Vonda">''[[The Crystal Star]]''</ref> It was then depicted for the first time in ''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]'', a [[1998]] reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and illustrated by [[Brandon McKinney]] and [[Scott Kolins]].<ref name="TEGtPaM">''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]''</ref>
