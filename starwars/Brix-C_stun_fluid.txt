'''Brix-C stun fluid''' was a [[poison]] created by the arms manufacturing company [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]]. Brix-C stun fluid worked when ingested or inhaled by the victim and could cause nausea, impaired vision, breathing difficulties, burning pains, and unconsciousness. It was used in the Merr-Sonn [[R-88 "Suppressor" riot rifle]].<ref name="Gadgets" />
