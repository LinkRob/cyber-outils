The '''Burning Plains''' was a region of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Magma|lava]] [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] which boasted heavy [[Volcano|volcanic]] activity. The area was to the north of Mustafar's giant [[central volcano]], and it was one of the few contant features of the planet.<ref name="BMF17" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Burning Plains was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 17|seventeenth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF17" /> published around [[April 29]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdates">{{BMFdates|17}}</ref> The [[Burning Plains/Legends|plains]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it appeared in the [[2005]] [[Star Wars Galaxies: Trials of Obi-Wan|''Trials of Obi-Wan'']] expansion to the [[2003]] [[Sony]] Online Entertainment [[Star Wars video games|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]], [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']].<ref name="SWG">{{SWGcite|exp=ToOW|type=location|[[Burning Plains/Legends|Burning Plains]]|[[Mustafar/Legends|Mustafar]]}}</ref>
