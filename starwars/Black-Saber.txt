'''''Black-Saber''''' was a code name for a [[Data-tape|data tape]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Scarif vault|data vault]] on [[Scarif]]. [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] member [[Jyn Erso]] mentioned it while searching for the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star's]] [[Death Star plans|plans]].<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> [[Tenzigo Weems]] found a mention of it in an intercepted data packet that was pulled from the [[Balmorra]] relay. He reported it to [[Davits Draven]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
''Black-Saber'' was first mentioned in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Anthology Series]]'' film, ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', directed by [[Gareth Edwards]].<ref name="Rogue One" />
