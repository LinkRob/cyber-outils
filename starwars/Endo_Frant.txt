'''Endo Frant''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who [[Homeworld|hailed]] from the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] [[Reproduction|born]] at least twenty [[Standard year|years]] after [[64 BBY]]. Frant moved to [[Coruscant]] for his education, eventually serving in the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[Imperial weapons technician|gunner]] on board the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. By the [[time]] the Death Star commenced a simulation firing on [[Rango Tan]], Frant held the [[Assignment|position]] of [[Chief Gunner]]. He was [[Death|killed]] when [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] jettisoned him and several other gunners into [[Realspace|space]] after [[bio-tracker]]s indicated they hesitated when they were ordered to [[Destruction of Alderaan|fire on Alderaan]].<ref name="Grand Moff" />
