A '''flotilla''' was a [[Navy|naval]] [[List of phrases and slang|term]] that denoted a formation of [[starship]]s. The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] maintained a [[Bulwark flotilla|flotilla]] of [[Bulwark-class battle cruiser|''Bulwark''-class battle cruisers]] in its [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval branch]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> During the [[Resistance]]'s [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation of D'Qar]], [[Gial Ackbar|Admiral Ackbar]] stated that the [[First Order]] had a "flotilla" of starships.<ref name="The Last Jedi Adaptation 1">[[The Last Jedi Adaptation 1|''The Last Jedi Adaptation'' 1]]</ref>
