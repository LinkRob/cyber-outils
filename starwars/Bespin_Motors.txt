'''Bespin Motors''' was a vehicle manufacturing company that produced the [[Storm IV Twin-Pod cloud car|Storm IV Twin-Pod]] model of [[cloud car]]. The company was named for the [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]] where their vehicles were frequently used by the security forces of [[Cloud City]].<ref name="USW" /> They also manufactured [[Carbonite storage repulsor sled|repulsor sleds]] used for the safe transport of [[Carbon-freezing|carbon-frozen]] exotic gasses.<ref name="CL 2016" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bespin Motors was originally created by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Curtis Smith]] for ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'', a ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' reference book released in [[1987]]. The company was confirmed to be [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which was released in [[2015]].
