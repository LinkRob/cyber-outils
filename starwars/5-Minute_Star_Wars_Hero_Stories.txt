'''''5-Minute Star Wars Hero Stories''''' is a hardcover title collecting five heroic stories from [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]'s previously published ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]''. It was first available in [[September]] [[2017]] exclusively from retailer [[Wikipedia:Kohl's|Kohl's]], which donated 100 percent of the book's net profits to its Kohl's Cares charitable initiative. A companion title, ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Villain Stories]]'', was also available.

{{Book-stub}}

==Contents==
*"[[Destroy the Death Star!]]," written by [[Trey King]] and illustrated by [[Pilot Studio]]
*"[[A Jedi, You Must Become]]," written by [[Andy Schmidt]] and illustrated by [[Stéphane Roux]] and Pilot Studio
*"[[The Ewoks Join the Fight (short story)|The Ewoks Join the Fight]]," written by [[Brooke Vitale|Brooke Dworkin]] and illustrated by Pilot Studio
*"[[A Friend for Rey]]," written by [[Elizabeth Schaefer]] and illustrated by Pilot Studio
*"[[Rathtars on the Loose!]]," written by Elizabeth Schaefer and illustrated by Pilot Studio
