An '''Emergency Work Order''' was a special pass that allowed an individual passage to different councils on [[Mina-Rau]] to work on something urgent within that council. One was given by [[Kellen]] to [[Brasso]] for [[Eight Hundred Block]] to allow him along with [[Bix Caleen]] and [[Wilmon Paak]] to get past the Imperial Inspection.<ref name="Harvest">{{Andor|Harvest}}</ref>
