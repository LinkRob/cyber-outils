'''Daisy Ridley''' (born [[April 10]], [[1992]]) is a [[Wikipedia:British people|British]] actress who stars in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], and [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] as the character [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]].

==Biography==
[[File:Rey-TROSBase.png|thumb|left|300px|Daisy Ridley as Rey]]
Ridley's involvement with the [[Star Wars saga]] was first announced on [[April 29]], [[2014]].<ref name="Cast">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-vii-cast-announced|text=Star Wars: Episode VII Cast Announced}}</ref> She also voiced her character in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]]s ''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]'',<ref name="USA Today">{{WebCite|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/tech/gaming/2015/08/28/star-wars-inside-out-disney-infinity-video-game/32381221|text='Star Wars,' 'Inside Out' add to expanding 'Disney Infinity' universe|author=Brian Truitt|date=2015-08-28|work=[[Wikipedia:USA Today|''USA Today'']]|archivedate=20200508130717}}</ref> ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'',<ref name="Hollywood Reporter">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/heat-vision/lego-star-wars-force-awakens-890283|text='Lego Star Wars: The Force Awakens' Writer Talks New Mythology and Celebrity Voices|author=Graeme McMillan|date=2016-05-04|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20190529200452}}</ref> and ''[[Star Wars: Battlefront II]]'' as well as doing the voice in the television series ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]''.<ref name="Entertainment Weekly">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/movies/2017/04/13/star-wars-female-heroes-forces-of-destiny-stories|text=''Star Wars'' highlights female heroes in ''Forces of Destiny'' — first look|author=Anthony Breznican|date=2017-04-13|work=''[[Entertainment Weekly]]''|archivedate=20200402231832}}</ref>

On [[August 7]], 2014, it was reported that Ridley would reprise her role as Rey in both Episodes [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|VIII]] and [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|IX]].<ref name="Deadline">{{WebCite|url=https://deadline.com/2014/08/uta-signs-star-wars-lead-daisy-ridley-815881|text=UTA Signs 'Star Wars' Lead Daisy Ridley|author=Mike Fleming Jr|date=2014-08-07|work=[[Wikipedia:Deadline Hollywood|''Deadline'']]|archivedate=20200109060138}}</ref>

Ridley won [[Wikipedia:MTV Movie Award for Best Breakthrough Performance|Best Breakthrough Performance]] for her portrayal of Rey at the [[Wikipedia:2016 MTV Movie Awards|2016 MTV Movie Awards]].<ref name="CBS News">{{WebCite|url=http://www.cbsnews.com/news/mtv-movie-awards-2016-highlights-and-winners|text="The Force Awakens" wins big at MTV Movie Awards 2016|author=Andrea Park|date=2016-04-10|work=[[Wikipedia:CBS News|CBS News]]|archivedate=20160703061616}}</ref>

''[[Star Wars]]'' composer [[John Williams]] cited Ridley's "enchanting performance" as Rey as a factor in his decision to return for Episode VIII, stating that he would not want another composer scoring her scenes.<ref name="MassLive">{{WebCite|url=https://www.masslive.com/entertainment/2016/08/john_williams_brings_star_wars.html|text=Review: John Williams, Boston Pops bring 'Star Wars' universe to stormy Tanglewood|author=Ray Kelly|date=2016-08-14|work=[[Wikipedia:The Republican (Springfield, Massachusetts)|''MassLive'']]|archivedate=20190904181508}}</ref>

In 2019, as [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] opened in both [[Disneyland]] and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]], Ridley reprised her role in the [[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance|''Rise of the Resistance'']] attraction in the form of a holographic message played to guests before the mission portrayed in the ride.<ref name="EW Rise">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/movies/2019/02/28/star-wars-actors-will-appear-in-disneys-rise-of-the-resistance-ride|text=Star Wars actors bring their characters to life in Disney theme parks' Rise of the Resistance ride|author=Anthony Breznican|date=2019-02-27|work=''[[Entertainment Weekly]]''|archivedate=20190914091306}}</ref>

At [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe 2023]], it was announced that Ridley would reprise her role as Rey in an [[Untitled Star Wars film (Sharmeen Obaid-Chinoy)|Untitled ''Star Wars'' film]] directed by [[Sharmeen Obaid-Chinoy]] set fifteen years after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="New movies" />
