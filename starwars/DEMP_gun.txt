A '''DEMP gun''' was a type of short-barreled [[ion blaster]], manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]], which fired an [[electromagnetic pulse]] to disable [[droid]]s. It was available in [[Destructive electromagnetic pulse ion carbine|blaster carbine form]] and [[DEMP pistol|blaster pistol form]].<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" /> In [[0 ABY]], a DEMP gun was used by [[Sergeant]] [[Torrent|TX-828]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]] to incapacitate [[Captivator (droid)|Captivator]], a droid [[bounty hunter]].<ref name="Smuggler's Run" />

On its highest setting, a DEMP gun could destroy all electronic security devices protecting a large house with one shot.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
