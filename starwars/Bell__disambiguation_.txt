'''Bell''' is a surname for a number of real world people, as well as the [[Corellian/Legends|Corellian]] named [[Areta Bell]], and the Jedi Padawan [[Bell Zettifar]].
This name is not to be confused with [[Bel/Legends|Bel]], the clone trooper.
