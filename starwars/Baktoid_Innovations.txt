'''Baktoid Innovations''' was a manufacturing company active around the [[19 BBY|final year]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. A subsidiary of the [[Techno Union]] corporation, the company constructed the [[S/D Decimator droid|Decimator]] [[droid]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baktoid Innovations was mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'', a [[2021]] reference book authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />
