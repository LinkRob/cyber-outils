The '''A-A5m Emergency Response Vehicle''' was a [[speeder truck]] purpose-built as an emergency response vehicle. Developed based on the high success of the [[A-A5 Speeder Truck]] and manufactured by [[Trast Heavy Transports]], the A-A5m was designed to operate in harsh, inhospitable scenarios, including inclement weather or terrible disasters. The most reliable vehicle available when time was of the essence and lives were on the line, the A-A5m came equipped with the latest medical technology and an expanded, long-range sensor suite to find those in need despite chaotic situations and deliver lifesaving aid, capable of saving lives and salvaging entire settlements no matter the circumstances.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony" />

The A-A5 could transport six passengers and had a limited cargo capacity with the option for additional external cargo modules. Staffed by one [[pilot]], one [[medic]] and/or [[medical droid]], and one liaison officer, medical and rescue personnel and relief workers around [[the galaxy]] had made the speeder truck synonymous with salvation for [[Standard year|years]] as of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Sold during the conflict, the A-A5 cost 10,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and was available with a fully compatible medical droid for a nominal price increase. The emergency response vehicle was unarmed.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony" />
