The '''Echo Base''' was the facility established by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] on the frigid [[planet]] of [[Hoth]] in the wake of the [[Battle of Yavin]], so named due to its strange acoustics.<ref name="CL 2016" /> Within a [[Standard month|month]] of its establishment, the base was discovered by the [[Galactic Empire]], which quickly [[Battle of Hoth|invaded]] Hoth, forcing the Rebels to evacuate.

==Description==
{{Quote|Now let's get you warmer and then to work. It never gets comfortable here, and you don't quite get used to it, but there are ways to make it livable.|General [[Philap Bygar]]|<ref name="Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Echo_Base_Defenses_DICE.png|thumb|left|250px|Echo Base was protected by many ground-based defenses, as well as an ion cannon that could fire into orbit]]
The den of a [[Unidentified wampa's clan|wampa clan]] before the arrival of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]],<ref name="Hunger">{{StoryCite|story=Hunger (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> Echo Base was carved out of a glacier and connected by artificial corridors linked together with structural supports, while natural caverns were expanded to suit the Alliance's needs.<ref name="sweHO" /> It took the [[Rebel Alliance Corps of Engineers|Alliance Engineering Corps]] eighteen months to build, with two full months to install the equipment needed to make the base functional and survivable.<ref name="HC32" /> It was protected from [[orbital bombardment]]s by a massive [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] (because Hoth was one of the few planets small enough to be fully protected by an energy shield)<ref name="Absolutely Everything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> and a [[V-150 Planet Defender|v-150 ion cannon]], which would help the Alliance evacuate the base in case of an attack.<ref name="sweHO" />

Owing to slap-dash construction methods, a single faulty element in the base's electrical system could deny heat to half the base. Nevertheless, [[soldier]]s of the outpost were better equipped and displayed better discipline than those stationed elsewhere.<ref name="Twilight Company" /> In its cavernous ice hangars, the base hosted a contingent of [[T-47 airspeeder|speeders]], which were specially modified to operate in the harsh conditions and which the Rebels used for patrol and defense of the outpost.<ref name="sweT47">{{SWE|technology|snowspeeder|snowspeeder}}</ref><ref name="databank">{{Databank|echo-base|Echo Base}}</ref> As the [[Rebel technician|technicians]] had trouble adapting their [[airspeeder]]s to the subzero temperatures of Hoth, the base also held a herd of native [[tauntaun]] creatures for the patrols and recon missions.<ref name="sweTau">{{SWE|characters|tauntaun|tauntaun}}</ref>

Echo Base also provided a desperately needed respite from the freezing temperatures of Hoth's surface, which were particularly nasty during nighttime. After Hoth's sun set, temperatures outside could dip down to -60 degrees celsius, low enough to kill even the [[Tauntaun]]s that rebel soldiers rode.<ref name="Absolutely Everything" />

Several sentry posts were stationed around the base, including [[Echo Station 3-T-8]], [[Echo Station 5-7]], [[Perimeter Outpost Delta]], and [[Outpost Beta]]. Outpost Delta stood far to the northwest of Echo Base, while it was a mere hundred [[meter]]s outside the base's energy shield.<ref name="Twilight Company" />

[[File:Echo-Base-cutaway-SWCL.png|thumb|right|250px|Cutaway of Echo Base]]
Owing to the base's top secret nature, many soldiers fighting across the galaxy were withheld information regarding its location, while rumors of it being on an asteroid or an underwater city spread like wildfire among the troops of the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]]. Ships en route to the base were sent on a random path through hyperspace in which they would be transmitted new jump coordinates in an effort to shake any possible Imperial pursuers.<ref name="Twilight Company" />

==Equipment==
The interior of Echo Base extended seven levels below the surface, with the lower levels for barracks, rec rooms, maintenance hangars and fuel stores. The mid-level housed the command rooms, med center, and two large [[hangar]]s&mdash;north and south. The huge south hangar held the transports, squadrons of [[X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] and [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|Y-wings]], and a flight of snowspeeders. The smaller north hangar housed starfighters, snowspeeders, and the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="HC32" />

[[File:SnowspeederLaunchBay-TCGtBoH.png|thumb|left|300px|Snowspeeders launch from an Echo Base hangar bay]]
This was where Rebel technicians maintained the numerous speeders that the Alliance had at its disposal. The hangar was open to the outside with a large door, which could be closed to protect the base from the frigid wastes.<ref name="Episode V" />

The medical bay contained a [[bacta tank]] used for healing wounded Rebels, which was overseen by a [[medical droid]]; the [[bacta]] was used to heal [[Luke Skywalker]] after he was wounded in a [[wampa]] attack, and&mdash;according to [[Captain]] [[Han Solo]]&mdash;made him look strong enough to pull the ears off of a [[gundark]]. Additional medical facilities in the base included a morgue, which took advantage of the planet's natural temperature to preserve bodies, and an insulated room in which vaccines were stored.<ref name="CL 2016" />

The base held thirty [[Gallofree Yards, Inc.|Gallofree Yards]] [[GR-75 medium transport]]s and at least sixty starfighters, which allowed two fighters to be assigned to each transport during the evacuation.<ref name="Episode V" /> There were many hangars in the base, including [[Hangar 7]]. The base also held at least eleven snowspeeders.<ref name="CL 2016" />

==Personnel==
[[File:Unlimited-EchoBaseDefender.png|thumb|right|300px|An Echo Base-based Cold-weather Soldier]]
The base was occupied by 7,500 combat personnel and 350 [[Doctor|medical personnel]],<ref name="CL 2016" /> and contained a command center where the Rebel leadership coordinated Rebel activities, including the eventual evacuation from Hoth.<ref name="Episode V" /> Most of the troops present slept in enormous bunkers with several hundred others, and often had scant rations.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>

Additionally, many droids could be found around Echo Base, such as [[2-1B]], [[K-3PO]], [[R-3PO]], [[EG-4]], [[R5-M2]], and many other droids that helped the Rebel Alliance in some way.<ref name="Absolutely Everything" />

==Threats==
Besides the possibility of the Galactic Empire eventually finding Echo Base, there were other threats to the encampment. Hoth was continually pelted by meteors from its asteroid belt.<ref name="Absolutely Everything" /> Designed to be evacuated at a moment's notice, the base was hastily constructed, and often as not, [[wampa]] [[Water|ice]] [[Species|creatures]] would sneak into the base at night, forcing the closures of various corridors. Additionally, a specially reinforced chamber was constructed to hold multiple wampas.<ref name="CL 2016" /> During the [[battle of Hoth]], several [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]] ignored the sign and went into the room anyway, only to be slain by the icy creatures.<ref name="Absolutely Everything" />

==History==
===Establishment and use===
[[File:Falconinechobase.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The ''Millennium Falcon'' received repairs in Echo Base's main hangar prior to the Battle of Hoth]]
Early in the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Rebel Alliance base made its home in the [[Great Temple]] on [[Yavin 4]], the fourth [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] tracked the Rebels to Yavin 4 and attempted to destroy the moon with the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], a deep-space mobile [[battle station]] capable of destroying entire planets.<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> The Rebels launched the [[Battle of Yavin]] in a successful attempt to destroy the Death Star, an event that was carried out by [[Rebel pilot]] [[Luke Skywalker]]. Though the Death Star was destroyed,<ref name="Episode IV" /> the Rebels [[Evacuation of Yavin 4|evacuated]] the Great Temple as the Empire still knew where the Alliance was housed. The initial search for a new base was conducted by [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]] during the evacuation from Yavin 4.<ref name="Leia 1">[[Princess Leia 1|''Princess Leia'' 1]]</ref> The Rebels eventually decided to establish their new base of operations on the ice planet Hoth<ref name="Lost Stars" /> in [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Chosen date">{{InsiderCite|203|A Certain Point of View}} states that [[Hoth]] was chosen as the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] base one [[Standard year|year]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states that ''A New Hope'' ends in [[0 ABY]], Hoth must have been chosen as the base in [[1 ABY]].</ref> with the Rebellion then spending the next two years building what became Echo Base.<ref name="Insider 203">{{InsiderCite|203|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> Over those two years, rebel engineers expanded ice caverns<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> in a feat of engineering work.<ref name="Insider 203" /> Once the area was finally carved and expanded, completing the base for use<ref name="Lost Stars" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the Rebellion inhabited Echo Base for less than a [[Standard month|month]] before the Empire discovered it.<ref name="Lost Stars" /> The base's South Corridor and many others were built by [[Major]] [[Kem Monnon]]'s [[Rebel Alliance Corps of Engineers|engineers]].<ref name="CL 2016" />

After the Battle of Yavin, the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], searched for Skywalker<ref name="Episode V" />&mdash;who he [[Duel in Ben Kenobi's home|had learned]] was his son&mdash;across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="SW 6">[[Star Wars (2015) 6|''Star Wars'' (2015) 6]]</ref> In 3 ABY,<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> less than a month after the Rebels had constructed Echo Base,<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Vader's fleet sent [[Viper probe droid]]s to all corners of the galaxy, [[XJ9-CS14|one]] of which was sent to Hoth. Vader believed that Hoth was the location of the Rebel base and that Skywalker was there, so he ordered [[Death Squadron|his fleet]] to the snowy planet. Once the Rebel Alliance realized that the Empire had discovered their location, [[General]] [[Carlist Rieekan]] ordered [[Code K-one-zero|plan kay one zero]] be taken into immediate effect, calling for Echo Base be evacuated, despite only having inhabited it for less than a month.<ref name="Lost Stars" /> With a strategy conference having been held on the planet not too long before,<ref name="Twilight Company" /> several key members such as Princess [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and General Carlist Rieekan were still stationed on the world, while the Rebels soon set about to pack up their base and escape on transports, hoping that they would have enough time before the Empire arrived.<ref name="Episode V" />

===Battle of Hoth and abandonment===
{{Quote|Imperial troops have entered the base! Imperial troops have entered&mdash;|An unidentified Rebel on the base's loudspeaker during the Battle of Hoth|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
[[File:ATATsHoth-Databank.png|thumb|right|300px|The Empire, using AT-ATs, attacks Echo Base during the Battle of Hoth]]
The Empire's arrival led to the [[Battle of Hoth]]. Imperial forces landed [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s at the [[Moorsh Moraine]] to begin the ground assault on the base's shield generator.<ref name="GD">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref> The Imperials were first spotted on the ground by Outpost Beta, with it being the first destroyed, while the [[walker]]s were spotted by Rebel [[scout]]s at [[Echo Station 3-T-8]], who relayed the message to the rest of Echo Base.<ref name="Twilight Company" /> While [[Rogue Squadron]] fought the AT-ATs, the Rebels launched their [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing]]-protected transports to carry personnel off-world. The transports escaped and the shield generator was destroyed, allowing Vader and his [[stormtrooper]]s to enter Echo Base and destroy it. Although Skywalker was on Hoth, he escaped before Vader could capture him.<ref name="Episode V" />

[[Commander]] [[Mercos]] of [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces|Alliance Special Forces]] was left behind and survived for weeks on Hoth.<ref name="Mercos">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Commander Mercos"</ref> General [[Octavion Sorin|Sorin]] was dispatched to find any rebels who had been left behind in the wake of the evacuation.<ref name="Legends">''[[Legends of the Alliance]]''</ref> Sometime after the battle, Imperial forces identified as the [[Hoth detachment]] remained on the planet, waiting to see if the Alliance would return despite the unlikelyhood of that. One snowtrooper suspected their unit was placed on Hoth more as a punishment for their [[Unidentified Imperial lieutenant (Inspectorate)|lieutenant]] failing to prevent the Alliance's [[Assault on Coruscant|strike on Coruscant]]. Months after the Battle of Hoth, [[Chewbacca]], Organa, Skywalker, and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] would return to Echo Base in order to [[Mission to Hoth (Rebel Alliance)|rescue]] personnel left behind from the battle. The rebels would face resistance from Imperials but were able to free their companions.<ref name="FCBD" />
