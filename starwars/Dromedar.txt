The '''''Dromedar''''' was a [[freighter]] carrying a cargo of static-locked [[tibanna]] [[gas]] that during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] was found derelict in [[Realspace|space]] by the [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] ''[[Blood Crow]]''. [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] and other crew members were dispatched to investigate it. They encountered [[Nevil Cygni]] who told the incoming team that his [[Culoss|crew]] had been kidnapped by [[pirate]]s. Later, Cygni and his crew revealed that they were in fact the pirates and took Thrawn and his crew as [[prisoner]]s, while the real crew of the [[Starship|vessel]] were hidden away and imprisoned.<ref name="Thrawn" />
