The '''Battle on Epikonia'''<ref name="Star Wars 41" /> occurred around [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> when the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] undertook a mission to the [[Planet|world]] [[Epikonia]] to rescue the [[cyborg]] [[Lobot]], who had been taken by the [[Scourge]] during a prior [[Mission to Jabba's Palace (Lando Calrissian)|mission]] to [[Tatooine]] that had been undertaken by Lobot and his friend [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]].<ref name="Star Wars 40" />
