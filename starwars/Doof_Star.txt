The '''Doof Star''' was the scale model of a device invented by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[scientist]] [[Heinz Darthenshmirtz]]<ref name="PaF:SW" /> by [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{PnFDate|1}}</ref> Darthenshmirtz originally conceived his creation to work as a nutcracker, using it to shoot a [[laser]] beam on any nuts to break them as he showed to his [[3PO-series protocol droid]] [[Norm-3PO]]. Unfortunately for Darthenshmirtz, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] stole his design and scaled it up significantly until turning what started like a harmless appliance into the Imperial [[superweapon]] known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. The Empire did go to hire Darthenshmirtz, but never gave him credit for the idea.<ref name="PaF:SW" />
