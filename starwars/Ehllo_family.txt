{{Quote|There is nothing an Ehllo can't do, my dear. I told you my father knows the ''Emperor''.|Cerasus Ehllo, to Aryssha|<ref name="Sana Starros 1" />}}
The [[human]] [[Imperial officer]] [[Cerasus Ehllo]] was a member of a '''family'''. According to Ehllo, his [[Cerasus Ehllo's father|father]] knew [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Sana Starros 1" /> By between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SanaStarrosDate}}</ref> Cerasus had [[Marriage|married]] [[Aryssha Starros|Aryssha]] of the [[Starros Clan]], and she became [[Reproduction|pregnant]] with [[twin]]s. After Cerasus [[Attack on the Starros family homestead|retrieved]] Aryssha from the [[Starros family homestead]] so she could have their [[Youngling|children]] in [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] care, members of the Starros Clan set out to rescue her. During a [[Capture of the King's Ransom|capture]] of Cerasus' [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[King's Ransom]]'', by the Starros Clan and members of the [[Genetian Collective]], Aryssha gave birth to her's and Cerasus' children, two [[Gender|girls]] who she named [[Desana]] and [[Thevera]]. Cerasus, meanwhile, was boarded into an [[escape pod]] and left in the [[Hynestian sector]], swearing to [[Phel Starros]] he would get his family back.<ref name="Sana Starros 5">[[Sana Starros 5|''Sana Starros'' 5]]</ref>
