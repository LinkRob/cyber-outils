'''Cryo-cycle stasis pods''' were devices in which items or life forms could be preserved in [[cryo-cycle stasis]]. Toward the end of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[clone trooper]] [[Kix|CT-6116]] was put in one such pod by [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. The pod was discovered and reopened by [[Sidon Ithano]] and his [[Sidon Ithano's pirate crew|crew]] several decades later.<ref name="Lost Treasure">"[[The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku]]"</ref>
