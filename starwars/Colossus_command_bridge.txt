The '''command bridge'''<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-resistance/into-the-unknown-episode-guide|text="Into the Unknown" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> of the ''[[Colossus]]'' platform was located on the top floor of [[Doza Tower]], above the [[Doza's office|office]] of the station's owner, [[Captain]] [[Imanuel Doza]]. After the platform was launched back into space to [[Battle of Castilon|escape]] [[Castilon]] and the forces of the [[First Order]], Doza re-opened the command deck to direct the navigation of the platform. The deck was staffed by Doza, [[Jarek Yeager]], and [[4D-M1N]], as well as multiple other [[droid]]s.<ref name="Into the Unknown" /> The droids were the same model as 4D, although more stripped down, and programmed to answer to her. They had been shut down with the bridge during its period of disuse.<ref name="Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-into-the-unknown-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 8 Fun Facts from "Into The Unknown" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref>
