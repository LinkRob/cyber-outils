'''Echo-3''' was an attack formation utilized in [[Space warfare|naval warfare]], which prioritized the defense of a [[command ship]]. As such, the formation required the surrendering of other [[starship]]s within a [[fleet]].<ref name="ARC Troopers" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], [[Kaleesh]] Separatist [[General]] [[Grievous]] issued Echo-3 during the initial phases of his [[Battle of Kamino|attack]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]].<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

While utilizing the formation, Grievous sacrificed his [[transport]]s to the Republic [[Kamino blockade|blockade]] in order for the debris from the [[warship]]s to plummet into the [[ocean]] on the planet's surface. The debris concealed several [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident''-class assault ships]], which were assembled in the ocean and deployed against [[Tipoca City]]. However, the battle ultimately ended in victory for the Republic.<ref name="ARC Troopers" />
