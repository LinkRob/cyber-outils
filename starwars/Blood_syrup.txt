'''Blood syrup''' was a type of heavy and sweet [[syrup]].<ref name="ARFD" /> Around [[146 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[A Recipe for Death]]" to around [[146 BBY]].</ref> the [[Devaronian]] [[chef]] [[Sama Macoy]] served a double toasted [[seallia sandwich]] with [[blood]] syrup to<ref name="ARFD" /> the head chef at [[Takodana Castle]]<ref name="ARFD" /> on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]],<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[Strono Tuggs]], also known by the nickname "Cookie."<ref name="ARFD" />

Macoy prepared this blood syrup-topped [[Foodstuff|dish]] while competing with three other members of the castle's kitchen staff in an attempt to become Tuggs' newest [[sous-chef]] following the [[Death|murder]] of the previous one, the [[Volpai]] [[Robbs Ely]], though unbeknownst to her, the competition was secretly an attempt by the head chef to identify Ely's killer.<ref name="ARFD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blood syrup appeared in the short story "[[A Recipe for Death]]," written by [[Landry Q. Walker]] and included in the [[2016]] anthology book ''[[Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I]]''.<ref name="ARFD" />
