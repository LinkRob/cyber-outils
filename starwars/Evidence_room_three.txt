'''Evidence room three''' was a room in the [[security tower]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]]. The remains of a [[Ceeril's bodyguard droid|bodyguard droid]] belonging to [[Ambassador]] [[Ceeril]] were placed in the room after the [[droid]] was destroyed during an [[assassin]]ation attempt on the diplomat. [[Administrator]] [[Velko Jahen]] and [[KC-78]] later visited the room to examine the droid's remains for evidence.<ref name="First Duty" />
