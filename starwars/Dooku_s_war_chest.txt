'''Count Dooku's war chest'''<ref name="Guides">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-bad-batch-season-2-episode-guides|text=All ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch ''Season 2 Episode Guides Now Available!}}</ref> was a massive cache of valuable goods and artifacts stored in [[Castle Serenno]] on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]]. Throughout the [[Clone Wars]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]] amassed this cache through worlds pillaged by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Spoils of War">{{TBB|Spoils of War}}</ref> as well as the [[Serennian|inhabitants]] of Serenno itself.<ref name="Ruins of War">{{TBB|Ruins of War}}</ref> Between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|202}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] carried out an operation to ship the cache off-world, led by [[Cloning|clone]] [[stormtrooper]] [[Captain]] [[Wilco]]. Though the operation was largely a success, the unexpected intervention of [[Clone Force 99]]&mdash;who were attempting to steal a small portion of the cache to maintain enough wealth to remain free from the Empire&mdash;resulted in approximately 15% of the cache being scattered across Serenno.<ref name="Ruins of War" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|In the opening episode of Season 2, we wanted to do a crossroads mission for the Batch where they could either walk away from it or take a risk to get this war chest and hope maybe their wildest dreams could come true.|Jennifer Corbett|<ref name="Interview">{{SW|url=news/the-bad-batch-interview-jennifer-corbett-brad-rau|text="The Empire Is Everywhere": The Creators of ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' on The Long Road Ahead in Season 2}}</ref>}}
Dooku's war chest first appeared in the first episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'s'' [[The Bad Batch Season 2|second season]],<ref name="BB S2">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-bad-batch-season-2-trailer-key-art|text=Clone Force 99 Is Back in New ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 2 Trailer}}</ref> "[[Spoils of War]],"<ref name="Spoils of War" /> which premiered on [[January 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="BB S2" /> Head writer [[Jennifer Corbett]] wanted the episode to have a crossroads mission in which the Bad Batch could walk away or obtain the war chest to make their dreams come true. The writers decided to use Dooku's palace because they knew it had a lot of treasure.<ref name="Interview" />
