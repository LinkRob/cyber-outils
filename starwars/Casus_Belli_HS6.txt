'''''Casus Belli'' HS6''' was a French roleplaying magazine from February of [[1993]]. It contained many roleplaying articles, including one adventure for the [[West End Games]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|D6]] system. The article was called "[[Le faucon mandalais]]" (in English "The Mandalese Falcon") written by [[Jean Balczesak]].
