'''Chanceuxi''' was a celestial body<ref name="USWNE" /> located in the [[Chanceuxi system]] within the [[Kanz sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It was situated in grid square N-4 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] and was connected to the astronomical object [[Danzik]] and the [[Atterra system]] via the [[hyperspace route]]<ref name="Galaxy Map">{{GalaxyMapPoster}}</ref> known as the [[Veragi Trade Route]].<ref name="VeragiTradeRoute" /> Chanceuxi was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Suerton]] [[gambler]] brothers [[Dodibin]], [[Thodibin]], and [[Wodibin]].<ref name="USWNE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chanceuxi was first mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''.<ref name="USWNE" />
