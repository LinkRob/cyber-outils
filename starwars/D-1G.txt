'''D-1G''',<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Tobias Beckett and Other Characters of the Underworld}}</ref> or '''Dee-Onegee,''' was an [[astromech droid]] that served as a [[pilot droid]] for the ''[[Rampart]]''. [[Tobias Beckett]] spliced into the console of the ''Rampart''. The [[droid]] was aboard the ''Rampart'' when the [[Starship|ship]] was destroyed by Beckett on the [[space station]] [[Munt Ontdal]].<ref name="Beckett" />
