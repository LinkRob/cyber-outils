The '''Boss Meetup''' was the name given to the gathering of [[Gungan]]s at their [[Gungan Sacred Place|Sacred Place]] following [[Attack on Otoh Gunga|attacks]] on Gungan [[City|cities]] by the [[Trade Federation]] during the [[Invasion of Naboo]]. The Gungans planned to organize their counterattacks against the Trade Federation, but their meeting was interupted by the arrival of the [[Human]] [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]]. [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] shed her [[handmaiden]] disguise in a plea for an alliance between the two [[species]], which impressed Nass and led to the two groups uniting for the [[Battle of Naboo]].<ref name="TPM" />
