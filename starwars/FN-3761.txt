'''FN-3761''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] in the [[First Order]]'s [[FN Trooper Corps]]. Along with [[Finn|FN-2187]], FN-3761 was assigned to [[janitor]]ial duties on [[Starkiller Base]]. Following reports of an infestation in one of the construction areas, [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] sent FN-3761 and FN-2187 to join the [[Bray's cleaning crew|cleaning crew]] led by Captain [[Bray (stormtrooper)|Bray]] on [[Deck Nine]].<ref name="Age of Resistance" />
