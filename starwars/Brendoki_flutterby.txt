'''Brendoki flutterby'''<ref name="Getty">{{Getty|url=thewaltdisneystudios/allaccess/#!asset/gpkknkjj7thhg8rk5z7pbk4b|text=PDX-FF-002700|date=2024-07-15}}</ref> were a [[species]] of [[butterfly]]-like [[insect]]<ref name="SW">{{SW|url=news/ilm-the-acolyte-vfx-interview|text=Lightsabers & Magic: Visual Effects Supervisor Julian Foddy on <i>The Acolyte</i>'s Creatures and Weapons of the Jedi}}</ref> that were found on the [[planet]] [[Brendok]]. It was translucent and flew with its two diaphanous [[Color|red]] [[Limb|wings]].<ref name="Destiny" /> In [[148 BBY]],<ref name="DestinyTimeline">{{TheAcolyteDate}} "[[Destiny (The Acolyte)|Destiny]]" establishes the events of the episode to take place 16 years earlier, placing the events of the episode to [[148 BBY]].</ref> [[Verosha Aniseya|Verosha]] and [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] used [[the Force]] on one insect by a [[bunta tree]] on Brendok, [[Force Stasis|holding it in place]].<ref name="Destiny" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brendoki flutterby first appeared, albeit unnamed, in the live-action [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'' in the episode "[[Destiny (The Acolyte)|Destiny]]".<ref name="Destiny" /> The species was identified in official promotional assets linked to the show released by [[The Walt Disney Company|Walt Disney Studios]] via [[Wikipedia:Getty Images|Getty Images]].<ref name="Getty" /> It was inspired by real-world [[Wikipedia:Sea angel|sea angels]].<ref name="DestinyEG">{{SW|url=series/the-acolyte/destiny-episode-guide|text="Destiny" Episode Guide &#124; The Acolyte}}</ref>
