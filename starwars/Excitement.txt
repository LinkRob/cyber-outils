"'''Excitement'''" is the second episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Sounds]]'', an inside look at the sound design of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' films and series.<ref name="D+" /> It premiered on [[September 29]], [[2021]] on [[Disney+]].<ref name="Announcement" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Official description==
''Embrace the sounds of excitement in galaxy ''[''sic''] far, far away through chases, duels, and battles.''
