The '''''Dark Syndicate''''' was a [[War Cruiser]] utilized by the [[Son-tuul Pride]] criminal syndicate during the [[Imperial Era]]. The [[cruiser]], commanded by a [[Unidentified Son-tuul Pride member (Jekara system)|member]] of the syndicate, was present above the [[planet]] [[Jekara]] during [[Crimson Dawn]]'s [[Auction for Han Solo|auction]] of the [[carbonite]]-frozen [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]]. The [[rebel pilot]] [[Luke Skywalker]] flew his [[Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)|X-wing starfighter]] over the ''Dark Syndicate'' after he was pursued by [[Galactic Empire]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] from the nearby [[Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Executor]]'', hoping that the Imperials would mistakenly fire upon the Son-tuul Pride cruiser and provoke them. Skywalker's tactic was successful; after the ''Dark Syndicate'' was hit by Imperial fire, its commander retaliated, and Skywalker was able to escape the [[TIE series|TIEs]]. However, the ''Executor'' subsequently opened fire on the ''Dark Syndicate'', destroying the cruiser.<ref name="Star Wars 16" />
