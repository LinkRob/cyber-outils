'''Diana Lee Inosanto''' is an actress and martial artist who portrayed [[Morgan Elsbeth]] in the [[2020]] [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''<ref name="Chapter 13">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref> and the [[2023]] [[Ahsoka Season 1|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'' television series.<ref name="Part One">{{Ahsoka|Part One: Master and Apprentice}}</ref> She reprised the character in the [[2024]] animation television series ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Empire]]''.<ref name="TOTE">{{SW|url=news/tales-of-the-empire-trailer|text=''Star Wars: Tales of the Empire'' Trailer Revealed}}</ref> Her father is martial arts expert [[Wikipedia:Dan Inosanto|Dan Inosanto]] and her uncle and godfather was the legendary [[Wikipedia:Hong Kong Americans|Hong Kong&ndash;American]] martial artist and actor [[Wikipedia:Bruce Lee|Bruce Lee]], and both of them passed down their arts and philosophies.<ref name="Magistrate Excerpt" />

Following her ancestry, Diana Lee Inosanto had Bruce Lee's [[Wikipedia:Jeet Kune Do|Jeet Kune Do]] as her foundation and combined various martial arts schools, becoming attached to Filipino martial arts while supplementing them with Japanese and Chinese martial arts, with weapons like the spear as part of such flexibility. She and her father watched the [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|first ''Star Wars'' film]] during its release in [[1977]] and were excited by the possibility of combining Filipino martial arts with the fictional [[lightsaber]]. Indeed, in the [[2020]] ''The Mandalorian'' episode "[[Chapter 13: The Jedi]]," Diana Lee Inosanto made her debut as [[Magistrate]] Morgan Elsbeth, wielding a [[beskar spear]] against the [[Ahsoka Tano's white lightsabers|lightsabers]] of the former [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Magistrate Excerpt" /> Inosanto went onto describe herself as a professional "late bloomer" who was at long last given the role of a lifetime through Elsbeth.<ref name="Lifetime Role">{{Twitter|TheRealDLI|status/1722883018740940955|[[Phil Szostak]]|quote=Read Full Letter on Instagram "For Dathomir" or "For Ahsoka". Professionally, I'm a late bloomer in my career as an actress, who for years was a struggling mother of a son with Autism, who was finally given the role of a lifetime. A miracle. Thank you @ahsokaofficial @starwars|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/dUK2T}}</ref>

The initial fight choreography in ''The Mandalorian'' involves complex moves by Morgan Elsbeth. Much of the stunt team had trained under Diana Lee Inosanto's father and she recognized some of the moves. However, she thought that Elsbeth's actions should be simple as the character was analyzing Ahsoka Tano's abilities, similar in spirit to the samurai in [[Wikipedia:Akira Kurosawa|Akira Kurosawa's]] films that showrunner [[Dave Filoni]] said he admires. The actress also improvised some of Elsbeth's moves and was surprised when they were included in the final cut. For the ''Ahsoka'' series, Lee Inosanto worked with [[Wikipedia:Wushu (sport)|wushu]] master and stuntwoman [[Ming Qiu]], reminding the actress of her wushu roots when she incorporated it as a stuntwoman in the television series [[Wikipedia:Buffy the Vampire Slayer|''Buffy the Vampire Slayer'']]. Lee Inosanto described her training and choreography with Mind Qiu as "pretty intense," and the two became good friends and "[[Wikipedia:Dim sum|dim sum]] buddies."<ref name="Wook">{{WookYouTube|JhfZ2yhlkGE|The WookieeCast Episode 10 - Sitting down with Diana Lee Inosanto|date=2024-03-27|old=1}}</ref>
