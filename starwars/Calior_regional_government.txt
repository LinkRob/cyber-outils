A '''regional government''' held power on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Calior]]. At some point, the regional government employed residents of the planet to construct its [[southern water works]].<ref name="Vader 18" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsComicDate|Darth Vader (2020) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 18}}</ref> [[Cygnus Anton]], [[Chief Regional Auditor]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], visited Calior to claim the [[water]] works as Imperial [[Colonist|colonial]] property, citing that they had been built by the [[parent]]s of Calior's inhabitants under the employment of the regional government.<ref name="Vader 18" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Calior regional government was mentioned in the [[Darth Vader (2020) 18|eighteenth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Vader 18" /> on [[December 1]], [[2021]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/89724/star_wars_darth_vader_2020_18|text=Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020) #18}}</ref>
