A '''barista''' was a person employed in a restaurant, whose durties included the preparation and serving of [[caf]] drinks and other beverages.<ref name="PnF">''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]''</ref> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{PnFDate|1}}</ref> several baristas were employed at the ''[[Death Star]]'' space station. Shortly before its destruction in the [[Battle of Yavin]], they were evacuated by the [[Firestar Girls]] squadron.<ref name="PnF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The occupation of barista was first mentioned in the non-canonical television special ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'', which aired in 2014.
