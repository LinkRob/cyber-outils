The '''''Conquest''''' was an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] that saw service during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. It served in [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] personal [[fleet]], [[Death Squadron]], when the unit was stationed in the [[Juris sector]] shortly prior to the [[Battle of Hoth]]. The ''Conquest'' was accompanied by the ISDs ''[[Avenger]]'', ''[[Devastator]]'', ''[[Stalker]]'', ''[[Tyrant]]'', and the [[flagship]] ''[[Executor]]''. The ''Conquest'' was destroyed along with the ''[[Ultimatum]]'' while pursuing the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.

==Description==
As an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]], the ''Conquest'' was 1,600 [[meter]]s long.<ref name="Length" /> It was additionally equipped with two [[deflector shield generator dome]]s on the [[Conning tower|command tower]], a [[communications tower]] between them, three [[Destroyer-I ion engine]]s, and four secondary [[Gemon-4 ion engine]]s.<ref name="Equipment" />

==History==
The ''Conquest'' served in the [[Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Episode V" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Project Swarm]] was initiated by the Imperial [[Death Squadron]] to locate the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Echo Base|main base]]. When a [[Viper probe droid|probe droid]] discovered the base on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Hoth]], Death Squadron was located in the near [[Qeimet system|Qeimet]] in the [[Juris sector]]. The ''Conquest'' was part of the [[squadron]], alongside the Star Destroyers ''[[Avenger]]'', ''Devastator'', ''[[Stalker]]'', and ''[[Tyrant]]'', which were all led by Darth Vader and [[Admiral]] [[Kendal Ozzel]] from their [[flagship]], the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]''.<ref name="Odyssey" /> The squadron was then deployed to the [[Hoth system]], with the ''Avenger'',<ref name="Episode V" /> ''Devastator'', ''Stalker'', ''Tyrant'',<ref name="Odyssey" /> and the Star Destroyer ''[[Ultimatum]]''<ref name="Final Order">{{StoryCite|story=The Final Order|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> participating in the [[Battle of Hoth]] and its direct aftermath, which included the squadron chasing the ''Millennium Falcon'' through the [[Hoth asteroid field|Hoth asteroid belt]]. The pursuit resulted in destruction of the ''Conquest'', as well as the ISD ''[[Ultimatum]]'',<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> and the ''Falcon'' successfully escaping to the nearby world of [[Bespin]].<ref name="Episode V" />
