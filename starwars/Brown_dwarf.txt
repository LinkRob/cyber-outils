A '''brown dwarf''' was a type of [[star]] that was in the process of a formation. Their mass was too small to maintain [[hydrogen]] [[fusion]] reaction and they were sometimes considered to be large [[gas giant]]s. Brown dwarfs made up a very small fraction of [[the galaxy]]'s stars and were considered unlikely to support life.<ref name="BMF 34">{{BuildFalconCite|34|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>

By the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[sector]] of [[Realspace|space]] that separated the [[planet]]s [[Kupoh]] and [[Gamorr]] was largely unexplored, and [[Luke Skywalker]] suspected it might contain uncharted brown dwarfs that could become a hazard for [[hyperspace]] travel.<ref name="Heir to the Jedi">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Doornik-1142]] was a brown dwarf star.<ref name="BTM">{{BuildFalconCite|34|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>
