The '''Ando Prime Centrum''' was a [[podracing]] [[Racetrack|course]] located on the [[Water|icy]]<ref name="Complete Locations" /> [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Ando system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Ando Prime]]. The [[Grand Trophy]] originated from a race on the course and,<ref name="Complete Locations" /> by [[32 BBY]],<ref name="TPMDate">The depiction of [[Watto's junkshop]] in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], where the [[Grand Trophy]] is shown, is set during {{Film|I}}, the events of which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]]. As such, [[Watto]] must have taken possession of the Grand Trophy by 32 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> had found its way into the [[Toydarian]] [[junk dealer]] [[Watto]]'s [[Watto's junkshop|junkshop]] on the planet [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Complete Locations" /> The twin [[Rodian]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracers]] [[Staka]] and [[Raka]] had won a race on the Ando Prime Centrum by the [[time]] they competed on the [[Hydian-Hutta Podracing Circuit]]<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> by around [[10 BBY]].<ref name="BVDate">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> The win was recorded on their downloadable [[Racer|racing]] profile.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ando Prime Centrum was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], in a portion focusing on locations from the<ref name="Complete Locations" /> [[1999]]<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[prequel trilogy]] film {{Film|I}},<ref name="Complete Locations" /> which was originally written by [[Kristin Lund]] for her [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book released in [[2000]], ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''.<ref name="Inside Worlds Ep I">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref> The Ando Prime Centrum was first introduced in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity via [[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']], a 1999 [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]].<ref name="EI Racer">[[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']]</ref>
