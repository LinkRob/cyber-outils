'''''Blue One''''' was a [[T-65 X-wing starfighter]] that was used by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] [[General]] [[Antoc Merrick]]. Merrick notably used ''Blue One'' during the [[Battle of Scarif]], where he was killed when his fighter was shot down.<ref name="Rogue One" />
