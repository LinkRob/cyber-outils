'''Egmont''' is an [[Wikipedia:England|English]] children's book publisher, which also publishes official ''Star Wars'' publications in Poland. Its ''[[Star Wars]]'' publications include the UK editions of various young reader novels, such as ''[[Lost Stars]]'', plus other young readers books like the ''[[Star Wars Joke Book]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: Where's the Wookiee? Colouring Book]]''. They also publish many different ''Star Wars'' magazines across the UK, which include the ongoing ''[[Star Wars Rebels Magazine]]'', [[Star Wars Adventures (magazine)|''Star Wars Adventures'' magazine]] and the ''[[LEGO Star Wars (magazine)|LEGO Star Wars Magazine]]''.
