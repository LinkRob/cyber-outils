The '''Enhanced AT-MP Walker''' was a model of [[All Terrain Missile Platform]] [[walker]] used by the [[Imperial Army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. It had been outfitted with enhanced munition firing systems, which were capable of delivering heavier and more explosive payloads into battle. The performance enhancements were built around existing systems, which visibly differentiated the Enhanced AT-MP from standard All Terrain Missile Platform walkers, with one such difference being dark [[color]]ed [[armor]] plates.<ref name="Commander" />
