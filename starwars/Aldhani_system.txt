The '''Aldhani system''' was a [[star system]] that contained the [[planet]] [[Aldhani]],<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> home to the [[Dhani]],<ref name="The Axe Forgets" /> a group of [[human]]s.<ref name="Aldhani" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], in [[5 BBY]], a [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|group]] of [[Axis network|rebels]] undertook a [[Aldhani heist|heist]] of a [[Galactic Empire]] payroll on Aldhani.
