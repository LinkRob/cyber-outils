'''373-CR''' was a [[Customs official|customs agent]] of the [[Rampa II Port Authority]] based in [[Port Hoshim]] on the world [[Rampa II]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> Between [[10 BBY]] and [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The entry in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' that mentions 373-CR takes place after [[Han Solo]] has completed the [[Kessel Run]], but before he encounters a [[bounty hunter]] on [[Ord Mantell]], which is mentioned as having occurred prior to the [[Battle of Hoth]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' establishes that Solo completes the [[Kessel]] Run nine [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]], meaning Solo's Kessel Run must take place in [[10 BBY]]. The ''Atlas'' also dates the Battle of [[Hoth]] to [[3 ABY]]; therefore, the entry in the [[in-universe]] [[book]] ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' must have been written between 10 BBY and 3 ABY.</ref> 373-CR accepted a bribe paid in [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] from the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]], who wished to claim the belongings of her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Han Solo]] and his co-[[pilot]] [[Chewbacca]] while the pair were imprisoned on Rampa II. 373-CR approved Starros's merchandise claim form, allowing her to take the gear, which included the [[book]] known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', in which Starros wrote about bribing the agent.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
373-CR was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
