'''A Long Time Ago....''' is a regular feature of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine that selects one year in the history of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise and discusses various product releases and events related to the franchise. Additionally, it notes some entertainment news outside the scope of the ''Star Wars'' franchise and notable events in the world that occurred during that year. "A Long Time Ago&hellip;." debuted in [[Star Wars Insider 102|''Star Wars Insider'' 102]].
