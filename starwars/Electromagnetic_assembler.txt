'''Electromagnetic assemblers''' were one of several systems used in [[self-repair system]]s, a type of [[armor]] modification used to maintain a piece of armor. Other systems used alongside the assembler included smart materials and [[laminate extruder]]s.<ref name="SM" />

==Behind the scenes==
Electromagnetic assemblers were introduced in ''[[Special Modifications]]'', a [[2016]] roleplaying supplement for [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']], which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="SM" />
