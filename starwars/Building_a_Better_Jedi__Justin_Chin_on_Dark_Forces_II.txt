"'''Building a Better Jedi: Justin Chin on ''Dark Forces II'''''" is an article published in [[Star Wars Insider 31|''Star Wars Insider'' 31]], Fall [[1996]]. It discussed the process of designing characters for the [[Wikipedia:Personal computer|computer]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]''.
