'''Floral tea''' was a type of [[human]]-[[Diet|consumable]] [[tea]] that could be served steaming [[Temperature|hot]]. While in her [[Greylark residence|residence]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]<ref name="Cataclysm" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[protocol droid]] [[C-04L]] served the human [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Kyong Greylark]] a steaming cup of floral tea on a tray. However, moments afterward, Greylark left the room in haste after receiving a recorded message that demanded her attention.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Behind the scenes==
