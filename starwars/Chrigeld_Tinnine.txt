'''Chrigeld Tinnine''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[artist]] based on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] who volunteered his art to the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]] including the [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] ''[[Report Sedition]]'', which encouraged Imperial citizens to report suspicious activity or individuals in their neighborhoods. The poster was altered before its distribution, with the [[Republic crest|emblem]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] used in [[graffiti]] in the poster being swapped out for the [[Separatist hex icon]]. Ultimately, Tinnine was pushed out by younger artists and retired back to Alderaan, where he was [[Death|killed]] by the Empire when they [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] the planet<ref name="Propaganda" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="SWGA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chrigeld Tinnine was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on [[October 25]], [[2016]].
