'''Allya''' was, according to a commonly told story, a [[Gender|female]] [[Jedi]] whom the [[Jedi Council]] sent into [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] on [[Dathomir]],<ref name="Collapse" /> in [[600 BBY]].<ref name="BMF 59" /> There, she allegedly trained other women in the ways of [[the Force]], ultimately giving birth to the [[Dathomirian]]s,<ref name="Collapse" /> who eventually splintered into [[Dathomiri clan|clans]] of [[witch]]es.<ref name="BMF 59" /> However, the Jedi Council had no such records, and the Dathomirians had other, contradicting stories about their origins.<ref name="Collapse" /> Allya wrote the [[Book of Law]], a book which portrayed the [[Jedi Order]] in a bad light, causing the [[Nightsisters]] to have little respect for the Order.<ref name="BMF 59" />

==Behind the scenes==
Allya was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 59|59th issue]] of the [[2016]] magazine, ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]''. She initially appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]'', a [[1994]] novel written by [[Dave Wolverton]] and published by [[Bantam Spectra]].<ref name="Courtship">''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]''</ref>
