The '''Frejukes''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that possessed [[Temperature|heat]]-retaining blubber. [[Wolentic Dudge]], a resident of [[Kijimi City]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]], was a Frejuke whose blubber made him a good fit for the world's snowy climate.<ref name="CGSWG" />

{{Species-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Frejuke, albeit unidentified, first appeared in the theatrical film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> which released on [[December 20]], [[2019]].<ref name="Jedi Redeemed">{{InsiderCite|196|The Jedi Redeemed: Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker|The Jedi Redeemed: ''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker''}}</ref> The species was designed by concept artist [[Ivan Manzella]].<ref name="Manzella">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]''</ref> The species later got identified in the [[2024]] [[Dorling Kindersley]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'', which was authored by [[Dan Brooks]], [[Megan Crouse]], [[Amy Richau]], [[Amy Ratcliffe]], [[Brandon Wainerdi]], [[Dan Zehr]], and [[Kelly Knox]].<ref name="CGSWG" />
