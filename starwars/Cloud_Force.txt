'''Cloud Force''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[August 4]] [[2005]] and took the form of an internal memo from the [[Cularin Militia]]. It gave information on the creation of [[Division Cloud Force]], a military unit specialized for operations on the [[Gas giant/Legends|gas]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Genarius]].
