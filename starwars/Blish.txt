'''Blish''' was an [[Ishi Tib]] volunteer member in the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. They were one of ten RDC personnel who embarked on a Republic and [[Jedi Order]] [[Mission to Angoth|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Angoth]] to capture a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature from the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]. The Ishi Tib possessed [[Color|green]] skin.<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />
