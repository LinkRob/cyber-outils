'''Art Group''' ('''Art''') was a sub-bureau of [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order|COMPNOR's]] [[Coalition for Progress]] responsible for reporting on the suitability of artistic expression within the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was noted for its austere tenets and inflexible criteria.<ref name="Propaganda" />

==History==
In Art Group's early [[Standard year|years]] following the [[Clone Wars]], when an Art agent had a scathing report on a piece of art, a distinctive [[Color|red]] [[Hologram|holo-label]] was affixed to the work in question. Among the underground, this act was referred to as "drawing a scarlet," and was viewed as a laudable achievement among counterculture artists chafing under [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] restrictions.<ref name="Propaganda" />

Art Group was eventually given the authority to call in [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[agent]]s when artistic violations were egregious. The ISB was known for its ability to make troublesome artists disappear.<ref name="Propaganda" />
