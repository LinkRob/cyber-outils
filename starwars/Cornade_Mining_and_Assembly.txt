'''Cornade Mining and Assembly''' was a company founded by [[Arvik Cornade]], who also served as its chief executive officer<ref name="MoF" /> by [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|20}}</ref> Around that time, Cornade Mining and Assembly may have been the most profitable company in the past century.<ref name="MoF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cornade Mining and Assembly was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
