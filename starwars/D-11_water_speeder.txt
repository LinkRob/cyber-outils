The '''D-11 water speeder''' was a 7-[[meter]] long pleasure [[speeder]] designed by the [[Theed Palace Space Vessel Engineering Corps]]. Although designed to navigate on [[water]] rather than [[Realspace|space]], it shared many similarities with the [[N-1 starfighter]], which was also build by the Engineering Corps. Such speeders were built specially for the [[Monarch of Naboo|monarch]] of [[Naboo]] and their entourage, and were kept in a section of the [[royal boathouse]] of [[Theed]].<ref name="Starfighters" />
