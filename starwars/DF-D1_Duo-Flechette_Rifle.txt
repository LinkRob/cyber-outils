The '''DF-D1 Duo-Flechette Rifle''' was a type of [[flechette launcher]] produced by [[Salus Corporation]], who took inspiration from [[Golan Arms]]' [[FC-1 flechette launcher]]. Salus's goal for designing the DF-D1 was to reduce the FC-1 to a [[rifle]]-sized platform. As a result, the DF-D1 possessed twin canisters of needlelike [[flechette]]s, which would detonate and create a cloud of fast-moving razor-sharp debris.<ref name="Gadgets" />

However, before Salus could put it for sale, the DF-D1 was banned due to its lethality. Despite being banned, it found use among [[Rodian]] big game [[hunter]]s who hunted [[nexu]], [[dragonsnake]]s, or [[rathtar]]s, and was featured in [[Rodia]]'s annual [[Atiang]] hunting awards. There were also a number of [[bounty hunter]]s and [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] who wielded the DF-D1.<ref name="Gadgets" />
