'''07''' was one of several [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[BX-series droid commando]]s who participated in the [[Defense of Rishi Station]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Clones!|07's final remark before they were destroyed|<ref name="Rookies" />}}
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> 07<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/rookies-episode-guide|text="Rookies" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> and other [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[BX-series droid commando]]s were tasked with [[Defense of Rishi Station|infiltrating]] the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Rishi Station]] on the [[Rishi Moon]] and activate the "all-clear" signal, allowing for a surprise attack on the key Republic [[Planet|world]] of [[Kamino]]. 07 and the other [[droid]]s used [[Droch-class boarding ship|''Droch''-class boarding ships]] and a [[meteor shower]] to land near the base. After sneaking in and killing several [[clone trooper]]s, the droids hard-wired the "all-clear" signal, despite several clones managing to escape.<ref name="Rookies" />

[[Clone Captain]] [[Rex|CT-7567]] "Rex" and [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody|CC-2224]] "Cody" arrived for a standard inspection, a droid disguised as a clone approached them in an effort to cancel the inspection. When Hevy alerted the clone officers with a [[droid attack flare]], Rex shot and destroyed the disguised commando droid. They were subsequently ambushed and driven off the platform, with the Separatist droids believing that they were pulverized. Afterwards, 07 and two other droids, [[08]] and [[09]], guarded the entrance to the base when Rex tricked 07 into opening the doors for [[Unit 26]], a droid that was previously destroyed. When the doors opened, 07 and the other droids were destroyed, and the clones succeeded in recapturing the base, but not before a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|fleet]] of Separatist [[warship]]s arrived overhead and deployed reinforcements to retake the base.<ref name="Rookies" />
