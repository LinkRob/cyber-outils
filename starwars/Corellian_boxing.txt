'''Corellian boxing''' was a combat sport from the [[planet]] of [[Corellia]]. [[Crix Madine]], a former member of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[commando]] team who ended up defecting to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], was a known practitioner of that sport.<ref name="TFA head to head">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Head-to-Head]]''</ref>
