'''Blue Bantha''' was a non-[[alcohol]]ic beverage served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> The drink consisted of a glass of [[blue milk]] with a [[cookie]] on top decorated with the image of a [[bantha]] horn.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> The [[milk]] was the same [[Bubo Wamba Family Farms|locally-farmed]] bantha milk found at the [[Milk Stand]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> The cookie and the [[Color|blue]] milk were both suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Bantha is one of the non-alcoholic beverages sold to customers at the Oga's Cantina bar in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> It was first revealed as part of a large promotional event held on [[February 26]], [[2019]].<ref name="Nerdist">{{WebCite|url=https://nerdist.com/article/star-wars-galaxys-edge-food-drinks-cocktails-menu-disney-parks|text=What You Can Eat and Drink in STAR WARS: Galaxy's Edge|author=[[Amy Ratcliffe]]|date=2019-02-27|work=Nerdist|archivedate=20190228074254}}</ref> The menu's description reads, "Blue Milk served chilled with Bantha-inspired Vanilla-Butter Sugar Cookie."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/breakfast|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Breakfast Menu}}</ref>
