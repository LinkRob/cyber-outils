'''Cloaking coats''' were used by many [[slicer]]s to bypass automated detection measures. A skilled slicer typically considered avoiding detection by [[sensor]]s and [[Probe droid|probes]] the most crucial aspect of breaching security, while [[Spy|spies]], [[Thief|thieves]], and [[saboteur]]s preferred more expensive personal stealth fields. These heavy, full-body [[suit]]s could be built or purchased and were outfitted with [[Jammer|jamming]] suites designed to mask the wearer's presence from electronic detection. A cloaking coat’s automated systems kept the wearer hidden by constantly broadcasting an obscuring cloud of junk data. This specialized array did nothing to prevent visual recognition but could baffle almost any [[scanner]]. A skilled slicer could even modify the signal on the fly to confuse [[droid brain]]s into disregarding the wearer.<ref name="Gadgets" />
