'''AJ LoCascio''' is a voice actor who has voiced a number of characters, including [[Han Solo]] in ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]'',<ref name="Han Solo">{{Twitter|Tcann13|status/914713441180553216|Tracy Cannobbio|quote=Welcome @AJLoCascio and @kiffvh as younger/older Han Solo's in #ForcesofDestiny!!|archivefile=File:TracyCannobbio-Voice-October-2-2017.png}}</ref> ''[[The LEGO Star Wars Holiday Special]]'',<ref name="LEGO Holiday Special">''[[The LEGO Star Wars Holiday Special]]''</ref> ''[[LEGO Star Wars Terrifying Tales]]'',<ref name="Terrifying Tales">''[[LEGO Star Wars Terrifying Tales]]''</ref> and ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'',<ref name="Skywalker Saga">{{YouTube|video=F1z-DhaUfHs&t=38s|text=AJ LoCascio On Voicing HAN SOLO In LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga, Meeting Harrison Ford, & More|channel=@LunarSquadron|name=Lunar Squadron|date=2022-05-01|quote=(0:53-1:03) ...where do I begin I mean I voice Han uh young Han and then silver fox Han and uh in Lego Star Wars and also a random Sith Trooper...}}</ref> [[Cad Bane]] in ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Underworld]]'',<ref name="Good Life">{{TOTU|The Good Life}}</ref> and the [[Inquisitor]] [[Marrok]] in ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]''.<ref name="MSL 105">{{MSL|Chapter 5: Inquisition}}</ref>
