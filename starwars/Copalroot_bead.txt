'''Copalroot beads''' were a type bead that was worn for vitality. They were made from the resilient [[copalroot]] that grew on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]], where the survivalist [[Chrissanta Palus]] had such a bead available for sale for one thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A copalroot bead appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The item is used as a minor charm in the game and increases the player's health if equipped.<ref name="Outlaws" />
