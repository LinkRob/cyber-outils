The '''Belnar system''' was situated at the [[Standard Galactic Grid|coordinates]] M-9 within an intersecting portion of the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Interior]] regions.<ref name="Interior" /> Located within the [[Belnar sector]],<ref name="Systems" /> it lay in the vicinity of the [[Belnar Insurrections]],<ref name="TC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> which took place at some point by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate|the mention of the [[Belnar Insurrections]]}}</ref> During the Belnar Insurrections, [[Everi Chalis]], an [[emissary]] to the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Ruling Council|ruling council]], punished the crew of the [[starship]] ''[[Mandible]]'' over an incident, [[Death penalty|executing]] those responsible.<ref name="TC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Belnar system was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via a mention of the Belnar Insurrections in the novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]]<ref name="TC" /> and published on [[November 3]], [[2015]].<ref name="Penguin-Hardcover">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/7821/battlefront-twilight-company-star-wars-by-alexander-freed/9780345511218|text=Battlefront: Twilight Company (Star Wars)}} {{C|Hardcover}}</ref> It was first introduced as a location by the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 52|fifty-second issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s magazine series ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'',<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|52|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> in an article written by [[Mark Newbold]],<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> released around [[December 30]] of the same year.<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|52}}</ref> Its classification as a [[star system]] was then established in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]].<ref name="Systems" /> Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Belnar system/Legends|Belnar system]] was originally introduced in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by Jason Fry and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
