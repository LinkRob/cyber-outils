'''DD-1A''',<ref name="The Edge of Balance V2">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 2|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 2]]</ref> also known as '''Deedee-Wunay''', or simply as '''Deedee''', was a [[droid]] that served the [[Jedi Order]] on the [[planet]] [[Banchii]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Deedee assisted the [[Jedi]] stationed on Banchii, [[Jedi Master]] [[Arkoff]], [[Jedi archivist|Archivist]] [[Ru-Ru]], [[Padawan]] [[Keerin Fionn]], and the [[Jedi Initiate|younglings]] [[Nima Allets|Nima]] and [[Viv'Nia Nia'Viv|Viv'nia]].<ref name="The Edge of Balance V1" /> The droid helped in monitoring the [[forest]] around the Banchii [[Jedi temple (Banchii)|Jedi temple]], maintaining a map of the area.<ref name="The Banchiians">{{StoryCite|story=The Banchiians|book=The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 1|bformatted=''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 1}}</ref>
