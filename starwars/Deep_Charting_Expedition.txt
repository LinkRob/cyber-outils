The '''Deep Charting Expedition''' was an expedition made by the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] scout [[Starship/Legends|ship]] ''[[Spotrunner]]'', leading into the depths of the [[Unknown Regions/Legends|Unknown Regions]]. At some point a [[Mnggal-Mnggal]] outbreak occurred on the ship. According to a log recording by an [[Unidentified Spotrunner's crew member|unidentified crew member]], a man called [[Covan]] was the first victim, followed by a person called [[Eelia]]. Also the lone surviving crew member stated that he would commit suicide in order to prevent this plague from infesting him. Nothing more has been heard from the expedition since.

{{Event-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Deep Charting Expedition was originally meant to appear in the Unknown Regions book, but it was not included in the final version.
