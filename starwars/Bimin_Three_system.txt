The '''Bimin Three system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Majoor sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing" /> It was situated at the coordinates N-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Bimin Three system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Bimin Three system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2001]] young readers novel ''[[Jedi Apprentice Special Edition: Deceptions]]'', which was written by [[Jude Watson]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Apprentice]]'' series.<ref name="Deceptions">''[[Jedi Apprentice Special Edition: Deceptions]]''</ref>
