'''Azudo Chanch''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who was an able [[Technician|mechanic]] and served as a [[pilot]] for the [[Cavern Angels]] [[squadron]], part of the resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[partisans]]. In [[1 BBY]], Chanch was stationed in the [[Catacombs of Cadera]], Gerrera's headquarters on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]].

When the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[prisoner]] [[Jyn Erso]] was brought to the catacombs, Chanch glared at her shortly before the structure was [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]].

==Biography==
[[File:AzudoChanch-DoRVG.png|thumb|left|300px|Azudo Chanch was an able mechanic who served in the partisans' Cavern Angels squadron.]]
Azudo Chanch<ref name="Guide" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="RO" /> [[Gender|male]]<ref name="RO AD" /> who served as a [[pilot]] in the [[Cavern Angels]], a [[squadron]] of eight [[T-65 X-wing starfighter]]s used by the resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[partisans]] during their [[Campaigns of Saw Gerrera's Partisans|campaigns]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Guide" /> By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Chanch and the Cavern Angels were based in<ref name="RO" /> the [[Catacombs of Cadera]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]],<ref name="Guide" /> Gerrera's headquarters, while he [[Jedha insurgency|disrupted]] the Empire's [[kyber crystal]] [[mining]] operations.<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Chanch was stood<ref name="RO" /> in the [[armory]]<ref name="RO AD" /> of the catacombs when the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[prisoner]] [[Jyn Erso]] was escorted past by Chanch's compatriot<ref name="RO" /> [[Javroth Meers]]<ref name="Guide" /> and another partisan. Stood at a table with an illuminated surface,<ref name="RO" /> Chanch glared at Erso as she passed.<ref name="RO AD" /> Soon after, [[Jedha City]] was [[Destruction of Jedha City|obliterated]] when the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]] fired its [[Mk I Superlaser|superlaser]] at Jedha, creating a shockwave heading toward the catacombs. The partisans inside attempted to flee, and while Gerrera<ref name="RO" /> and many others [[Death|perished]] when the catacombs were destroyed, others did manage to escape the blast.<ref name="SW39">[[Star Wars (2015) 39|''Star Wars'' (2015) 39]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Azudo Chanch had light skin and [[Color|white]] [[hair]] including a beard.<ref name="RO" />

==Skills and abilities==
Chanch was an able [[Technician|mechanic]].<ref name="Guide" />

==Equipment==
Chanch wore a cobbled-together kit that included a brown, padded [[jacket]] with a brown [[belt]], black [[glove]]s, and a gray [[gauntlet]] on the right [[Limb|arm]]. The pilot also had a white [[pauldron]] on the jacket's left shoulder and the white [[Scout trooper armor|helmet]] of an Imperial [[scout trooper]].<ref name="Guide" />
