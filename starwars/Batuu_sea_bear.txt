'''Batuu sea bears''' were [[bear]]s that shared their name with the [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="Quest" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The mention of the Batuu sea bear in ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' takes place in between the [[Battle of Jedha]] and the [[Night of Sorrow]], both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> a [[crystal]]ized [[Bone|skull]] of an ancient Batuu sea bear was one of many artifacts in the [[artifact chamber]] of the [[Batuu Jedi temple]], with a [[Unidentified Jedi compass|compass]] tucked behind it.<ref name="Quest" />

==Behind the scenes==
A Batuu sea bear's skull appeared in [[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X|''Quest for Planet X'']], a junior novel written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in [[2023]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia campaign's<ref name="Quest" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2022-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=NYCC 2022: 10 Things We Learned From the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref> Prior to the book's release, the mention of the Batuu sea bear was included in an online preview.<ref name="Preview">{{Kobo|url=gb/en/ebook/quest-for-planet-x|text=Quest for Planet X}}</ref>
