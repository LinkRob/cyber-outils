"'''Dans la nuit des temps'''" was a small roleplaying adventure in the French gaming magazine [[Casus Belli (magazine)|''Casus Belli'']], written by [[Jean Balczesak]]. Not designed as a complete and detailed scenario, it was essentially a base for the gamemaster.
