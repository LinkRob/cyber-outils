'''Ersos' apartment''' was the residence of the [[Doctor]] [[Galen Walton Erso]], his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Lyra Erso]], and their [[daughter]] [[Jyn Erso]]. It was situated in an elevated residential area of the [[Project Celestial Power complex]] in the former [[B'ankor Refuge]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Catalyst" />
