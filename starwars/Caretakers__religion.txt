[[File:CaretakerVillage-SoH.png|thumb|right|250px|The Caretakers]]
The [[Gender|female]] [[Lanai]] [[Caretakers]] followed a '''religion''' that idealized a balance in tranquility. Despite having maintained the [[Temple Island]] and the [[First Jedi Temple]] for millennia, the Caretakers did not adhere to the [[Jedi]] path. Even so, the [[Light side of the Force|light side]] and the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] of [[the Force]] had analogs in their religion, expressed by the changing weather of the [[planet]] [[Ahch-To]].<ref name="TLJ:VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The [[religion]] of the [[Caretakers]] was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'' ([[2017]]) by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TLJ:VD" />
