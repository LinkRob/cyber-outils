{{Quote|Each of the sires marked on this map contains a buried vault armed with an antitamper sub-space alarm. To date, no such alarm has ever sounded.|Maz Kanata describes the alarms protecting her treasures|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
'''Antitamper sub-space alarms''' were a type of alarm that could be used to indicate when an object had been tampered with. The [[Pirate]] [[King|Queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] hid the spoils of her reign in twelve treasure caches throughout [[the galaxy]], each protected by an antitamper sub-space alarm. The caches were located on [[Iskalon]], [[Takodana]], [[Descopose Farmark]], [[Graddus Rex system|Graddus Rex]], [[Seeratter]], [[Ponemah Terminal|Ponemah]], [[Adrathorpe]], [[Lost Murvey]], [[Pashvi]], "[[Graceful Promising]]," [[Nantoon]], and [[Moldour]]. As of the [[time]] prior to or during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] that Kanata began writing the book that would become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', none of her dozen alarms had sounded.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Antitamper sub-space alarms were first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
