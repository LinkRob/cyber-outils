'''''Attack of the Clones''''' ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]]: '''''Angriff Der Klonkrieger''''') is an original German-language audio drama adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones]]. It contains dramatized passages from the film script performed by a cast in German translation along with narration by [[Joachim Kerzel]]. The CD was released by the German label [[WortArt]] in 2005 as part of a series of six single-disc audio adaptations of the original and prequel trilogies.
