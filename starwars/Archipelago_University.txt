'''Archipelago University''' was a [[university]] that operated during the [[Imperial Era]]. By that time, an [[Alderaanian]] [[Gender|woman]] who was the [[Kier Domadi's mother|mother]] of the [[Kier Domadi's family|boy]] [[Kier Domadi]] taught [[Politics|political]] science as a [[professor]] there.<ref name="LPOA" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> Domadi became an [[Apprentice Legislator]] for the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] alongside the Alderaanian [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], serving in the [[Apprentice Legislature]]. When Organa told her [[House of Organa|father]], [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], about her fellow Apprentice Legislators, Bail mentioned Domadi and his [[Parent|mother's]] position as a professor at Archipelago University. Not long after, when Domadi was conversing with Leia on a [[pathfinding]] excursion, the former told the latter that he hoped to someday teach at a university to follow in his mother's footsteps.<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Archipelago University was first mentioned in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="LPOA" />
