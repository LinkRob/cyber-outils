This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="AOTC" /> [[Chon Actrion]] was a member of this species.<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==Biology and appearance==
This [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] was distinguished by their their wrinkled skin and sunken faces, with pointed chins, high-set [[eye]]s, naturally downturned [[mouth]]s, wide, upturned [[Nose|nostrils]], and large three-ridged head crest that strongly resembled those belonging to the [[Besalisk]] species, appearing hard and segmented, running up and over from their foreheads.<ref name="AOTC" />

==Species in the galaxy==
Sometime prior to the start of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> a member of this species, [[Chon Actrion]], served as a member of the [[Jedi Order]], even reaching the rank of [[Jedi Master]]. As a legendary [[Jedi]] who earned the title "'''Architect of Freedom''',"<ref name="TVE" /> they were remembered via [[bronzium]] busts displayed throughout the [[Jedi Archives]] in the [[Imperial Palace|temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]<ref name="AOTC" /> up until the [[Great Jedi Purge|fall of the Order]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The species made their onscreen debut via busts in the [[2002]] film {{Film|II}}, directed by [[George Lucas]].<ref name="AOTC" />
