'''Castor''' was a gardener on the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]].<ref name="Hidden Danger" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Starlight: Hidden Danger]]" to [[231 BBY]].</ref> [[Administrator]] [[Velko Jahen]] of Starlight asked Castor to force the station's [[garden]]s to bloom for the visiting [[Galactic Agricultural Alliance]], which she felt was a small ask seeing as the gardens were on an artificial schedule. When [[Head of Security]] [[Ghal Tarpfen]] pointed out that the gardens were blooming, Jahen noted that she would have to thank Castor.<ref name="Hidden Danger" />

==Behind the scenes==
Castor was mentioned in the first installment of the short story "[[Starlight: Hidden Danger]]," which was written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published in the [[Star Wars Insider 203|203th]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine as part of the [[The High Republic: Starlight|''Star Wars: The High Republic: Starlight'']] serialized fiction<ref name="Hidden Danger" /> on [[June 22]], [[2021]].<ref name="TitanComics">{{TitanComics|url=m/76-star-wars-insider-203|text=Star Wars Insider #203}}</ref>
