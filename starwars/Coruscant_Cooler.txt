The '''Coruscant Cooler''' was a [[cocktail]] that originated on [[Coruscant]],<ref name="Cookbook" /> a [[city]]-[[Ecumenopolis|covered]] [[planet]] in the [[Core Worlds]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> when it was seen as [[the galaxy]]'s cultural center prior to the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]]. Based on another classic cocktail, the [[Color|red]]-colored beverage was a [[fruit]]y mix of several [[alcohol]]s and juices that was served over [[Water|ice]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> It was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="Ronto Roasters" />

The drink was served at [[Oga's Cantina]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> and at [[Ronto Roasters]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]]<ref name="Ronto Roasters" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE Date">The events at [[Oga's Cantina]] and [[Ronto Roasters]] within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Sometime after the [[Battle of Takodana]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] published a recipe for the beverage in his [[Book|cookbook]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''. He described its flavors as "sophisticated," and considered the cocktail a reflection of the intermingled cultures and customs of the nearly one trillion inhabitants of Coruscant. In Tuggs' cookbook, the beverage was pictured with a fruit garnish and served with a large ice cube.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
On [[August 1]], [[2019]], an article on the official [[Wikipedia:Disney Parks, Experiences and Products|Disney Parks]] [[Wikipedia:Blog|Blog]] announced that Coruscant Coolers would be available at the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] as a drink exclusive to [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]]. The drink debuted on [[August 29]] with the opening of the Disney's Hollywood Studios version of Ronto Roasters,<ref name="Sip and Savor">{{DPB|url=2019/08/sip-and-savor-intergalactic-food-beverage-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge-at-walt-disney-world-resort-opening-august-29|text=Sip and Savor Intergalactic Food & Beverage at ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge at Walt Disney World Resort, Opening August 29}}</ref> which has no alcoholic options in its [[Disneyland]] Park incarnation. The drink was later added to Disneyland's version of Oga's Cantina, which serves alcohol.<ref name="DL Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/lunch|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Lunch Menu}}</ref> The menu description of the drink says: "[[Wikipedia:Maker's Mark|Maker's Mark]] [[Wikipedia:Bourbon whiskey|Bourbon]], [[Wikipedia:Girolamo Luxardo|Luxardo]] [[Wikipedia:Maraschino|Maraschino]] [[Wikipedia:Liqueur|Liqueur]], [[Wikipedia:Fratelli Branca|Carpano Antica]] [[Wikipedia:Vermouth|Sweet Vermouth]], [[Wikipedia:Cranberry|Cranberry]], [[Wikipedia:Lime (fruit)#Uses|Lime Juice]]."<ref name="WDW Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/ronto-roasters/menus/breakfast|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Ronto Roasters: Breakfast Menu}}</ref>

A recipe for the beverage is included in the 2019 cookbook ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. It is made with the ingredients [[Wikipedia:Cranberry juice|cranberry juice]], bourbon, lime juice, sweet vermouth, and [[Wikipedia:Prunus avium|dark cherry]] puree; the drink is then garnished with a lime wedge and served over a single large ice cube.<ref name="Cookbook" /> In an interview, Sumerak noted Coruscant Coolers as a favorite of his from the recipe collection.<ref name="ForceNet">{{WebCite|url=https://forcecast.net/story/home/The_ForceCast_Author_Series_With_Chelsea_MonroeCassel_And_Marc_Sumerak_185657.asp|text=The ForceCast Author Series With Chelsea Monroe-Cassel And Marc Sumerak|author=Steve|date=2019-11-15|format=[[Wikipedia:Podcast|Podcast]]|work=[https://forcecast.net/ ForceCast.net]|archivedate=20240327213404}}</ref> [[Coruscant Cooler/Legends|Coruscant Coolers]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity with the [[2004]] novel ''[[MedStar I: Battle Surgeons]]'', written by [[Michael Reaves]] and [[Steve Perry]].<ref name="MedStar I">''[[MedStar I: Battle Surgeons]]''</ref>
