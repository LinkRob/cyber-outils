We've bombed the shipyards at Kuat and the supply base on Imdaar. ''[&hellip;]'' I'm not sure what else we could possibly be doing.|Leia Organa and Gial Ackbar|<ref name="Star Wars 4" />}}
In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and [[Galactic Empire]], the Alliance '''bombed''' the [[Kuat shipyards|shipyards]] at<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> the [[Core Worlds|Core]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Planet|world]] [[Kuat]]. The [[shipyard]]'s [[bomb]]ing, which was carried out after the Rebel Alliance had [[Battle of Yavin|destroyed]] the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> [[superweapon]],<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> was one of over a dozen attacks undertaken to exploit a damaged Empire.<ref name="Star Wars 4" />

The Kuat shipyards bombing was recalled by [[Admiral]]<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> [[Gial Ackbar]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> as an example of the Rebel Alliance's actions after the [[Death Star]]'s destruction during a meeting of [[Alliance High Command|Alliance leaders]]<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> later that [[Standard year|year]]<ref name="Date" /> after [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] noted her belief the Rebel Alliance was not doing enough in their fight against the Empire. After<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> his<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> mention of the attacks, Ackbar stated he did not know what more the Alliance could be doing.<ref name="Star Wars 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bombing of the Kuat shipyards was mentioned in the [[Star Wars (2015) 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']]. The issue was written by [[Jason Aaron]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> on [[April 22]], 2015.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52655/star_wars_2015_4|text=Star Wars (2015) #4}}</ref>
