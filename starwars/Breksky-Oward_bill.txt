The '''Breksky-Oward bill''' was a proposed piece of legislation regarding [[droid rights]], which offered protections for advanced intelligence life.<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="date">{{MOFdate|14}}</ref> [[Senator]] [[Nadrian DuQuosenne]]'s [[DuQuosenne's protocol droid|protocol droid]] negotiated with [[Mon Mothma]] the inclusion of Breksky-Oward protections in her proposed [[Imperial Rebirth Act]].<ref name="MoF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Breksky-Oward bill was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
