According to Abda, the [[Marriage|wedding]]&mdash;which took place on the [[Planet|world]] [[Quarzite]]&mdash;was one of the biggest her [[Abda's clan|clan]] had hosted.<ref name="Convergence" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Abda&mdash;now a member of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]]&mdash;mentally recalled her brother's wedding while infiltrating a pre-[[Wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran|wedding]] party hosted for the [[E'roni]] [[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]].<ref name="Convergence" />
