'''Chadkol Gee''' was a [[Quarren]] [[advisor]] of [[General]] [[Nossor Ri]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. Gee was fiercely isolationist, and was among a contingent of government personnel who confronted Resistance General [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] when she arrived on [[Mon Cala]].<ref name="Allegiance 1" />

[[Chadkol Gee's family|Chadkol's family]] were tortured and killed while under the yoke of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Occupation of Mon Cala|oppression]]. Chadkol blamed Leia Organa for the occupation because the orders were given by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], her father. Because of this, he contacted General [[Armitage Hux]] to inform the First Order that Leia and her co-conspirators were on Mon Cala.<ref name="Allegiance 2" />

After an incident between the Resistance members and Quarren on the landing platform that was orchestrated by Chadkol and Nossor Ri, during a trial presided over by [[King]] [[Ech-Char]], Chadkol suggested that the [[Ancient Rite of Challenge]] be used to determine their fate, using [[RK-9|Arkay-Nine]] as his champion. After violating the rules of the Rite, Leia's contingent was ordered to leave Mon Cala.<ref name="Allegiance 3">[[Allegiance 3|''Allegiance'' 3]]</ref>

One of Chadkol and Ri's [[Chadkol Gee's partner|attendants]] tried to sabotage the Resistance ship and was captured by [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]. At this time, a First Order fleet arrived, so Leia confronted Chadkol, denying his allegations that she brought the fleet to Mon Cala, and presenting the captured attendant to testify against Chadkol. Ech-Char denounced Chadkol for [[treason]]. Ech-Char allowed a fleet of Mon Cala ships to go with the Resistance and the First Order took revenge on Mon Cala, leaving Chadkol to repeat "I warned them that they would bring war to Mon Cala again... I warned them..."<ref name="Allegiance 4">[[Allegiance 4|''Allegiance'' 4]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Chadkol Gee first appeared in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Allegiance 1|''Allegiance'' 1]], although the Quarren's name was first revealed in the comic's Behind the Scenes section. Here, Gee is scripted to have said Nossor Ri's dialogue, though this was not the case in the actual comic.<ref name="Allegiance 1" />
