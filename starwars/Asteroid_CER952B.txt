'''Asteroid CER952B''' was an [[asteroid]] located in the [[Cerberon debris field]], within the [[Cerberon system]] of the [[Deep Core]]. The [[New Republic]] attempted to set a trap for the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] involving asteroid CER952B, though the [[fighter wing]] ignored the asteroid.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
