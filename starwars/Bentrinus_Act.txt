The '''Bentrinus Act''' was an act implemented by the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Galactic Senate]],<ref name="Republic" /> which disallowed the renting of the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]] for private events. However, the act had not been enforced for [[Standard year|years]]<ref name="MOF" /> by [[19 BBY]]. That year,<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|6}} ''Star Wars: Timelines'' also dates the Empire's [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] to that year.</ref> following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformation]] of the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]], a private fundraiser was held in the Senate Building to fund the campaigns of six [[senator]]s from the [[Colonies]] region. [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] attended, at one point telling another guest that hosting the fundraiser at the Senate Building was disallowed per the Bentrinus Act, though the law was largely unenforced.<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bentrinus Act was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
