'''Flyleaf trees''' were a type of [[tree]] that could be found in the [[Skygarden]], a floating botanical [[garden]] on<ref name="ALD" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Chandrila]].<ref name="ALD" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> several members of the [[New Republic pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]]'s [[Norra Wexley's team|team]] visited the garden and waited for their leader under a copse of flyleaf [[Plant|trees]]. When Wexley arrived, they saw her and approached from under the trees' canopy.<ref name="ALD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flyleaf trees appeared in ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', a [[2016]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the second installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="ALD" />
