'''Anvil Squadron''' was a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] of the [[New Republic]] active following the [[Battle of Endor]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Squadron were stationed aboard the ''[[Silver Coronet]]'', were often [[Scouring of Mon Cala|deployed]] to vulnerable [[planet]]s such as [[Mon Cala]], in order to defend civilians against [[Galactic Empire]] attacks. They also participated at the [[Battle of Galitan]].

==Description==
Anvil Squadron was part of the [[New Republic]] made up [[starfighter]] [[New Republic pilot|pilots]] and [[astromech droid]]s, and was under the command of an individual with the [[callsign]] [[Anvil Leader]]. In one battle, they were joined by [[Commander]] [[Lindon Javes]], who handled their tactics personally.<ref name="Squadrons" />

Anvil Squadron was attached to a [[fleet]] that consisted of their flagship and headquarters [[MC75 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Silver Coronet]]'', [[CR90 corvette]]s, and at least one [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate|Nebulon-B]] [[Hospital ship|medical frigate]]. The Squadron utilized [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|BTL-A4 Y-wing starfighters]], [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s, [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s, and [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]]. The size of the squadron expanded well beyond the usual 12 starfighters estimating to be at least 30 active at one time. Their starfighters were marked with [[Color|yellow]] stripes.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==History==
===Before Mon Cala===
{{Quote|Anvil Squadron. Been a while. They shot me down, once.|Titan Squadron pilot Shen, prior to Mon Cala|<ref name="Squadrons" />}}
Sometime during the [[Galactic Civil War]], Anvil Squadron were involved in an engagement with [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces, that resulted in shooting down at least one [[TIE pilot]] named [[Shen]].<ref name="Squadrons" />

===Mon Cala===
{{Quote|Imperial invaders, this is Anvil Squadron! Leave Mon Cala immediately or face the consequences!|Anvil Leader, at Mon Cala|<ref name="Squadrons" />}}
Four years after the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]], Anvil Squadron responded to an Imperial [[Scouring of Mon Cala|strike]] on the New Republic's fuel depot above [[Mon Cala]], led by [[Titan Squadron]] who were using hit-and-run tactics to cause chaos. Anvil deployed many A-wings, U-wings, X-wings, and Y-wings, but suffered heavy losses during the chaos, including their flagship ''Silver Coronet''.<ref name="Squadrons" />

===Galitan===
{{Quote|The rebels managed to call in reinforcements of their own--Anvil Squadron, to be precise. Lindon Javes is handling Anvil Squadron's tactics personally. Anvil took heavy losses at Mon Cala, but they still have starfighters--and a grudge.|Captain Terisa Kerrill, briefs Titan Squadron prior to the battle of Galitan|<ref name="Squadrons" />}}
Later, Anvil Squadron responded to a distress call from the [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] and Commander Lindon Javes, who's [[Nadiri fleet|fleet]] was badly wounded and trapped by Imperial forces over the nearby [[moon]] [[Galitan]], located within the [[Ringali Nebula]]. Led personally by Javes, Anvil [[Battle of Galitan|fought]] to protect the heavily damaged ''[[Starhawk]]'' from a final strike by Titan Squadron, but they were unable to prevent Titan from damaging the ''Starhawk''{{'s}} [[tractor beam]] core, which would later result in its catastrophic overload. Anvil suffered heavy losses, but were able to destroy an [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] with Javes' help. Javes' X-wing was shot down during a dogfight with one of Titan's [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Titan Three (Flight Baron)|pilots]], however he did survive and later helped [[Vanguard Squadron]] protect the ''Starhawk'' from waves of TIEs, while it was towed into the moon's exposed core. The ''Starhawk''{{'s}} unstable core exploded as it impacted the moon and caused a massive explosion of molten rocks and a shockwave that destroyed all enemy ships in range.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anvil Squadron appeared in the single player campaign and as a multiplayer starfighter cosmetic option in the [[2020]] game ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''.<ref name="Squadrons" />
