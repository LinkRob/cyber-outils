"'''Batuu Bonanza'''" is the second part of the [[The New Droid Friends / Batuu Bonanza|first episode]] of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Three|third season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''. It was released on [[Disney+]] on [[December 8]], [[2025]].

==Official description==
''[[Kai Brightstar|Kai]] loses a package on [[Batuu]]''.<ref name="Batuu Bonanza" />

==Plot summary==
===Mission to Batuu===
[[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]] and [[Nubs]] travel to [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]] with their new [[astromech droid]] friends [[Dozer]], [[Gigi (droid)|Gigi]] and [[Beepers]] on a mission to deliver a package to [[Jedi Master]] [[Carver Drow]]. The three droids are curious and excited about their surroundings, and start exploring. 

Dozer accidentally knocks over a bowl of [[fruit]], upsetting a [[Mon Calamari]] merchant. At the [[Milk Stand]], Gigi startles a [[human]] [[Gender|girl]] and a vendor by detaching her drone and toppling some jars. Dozer runs circles around a [[labor droid]], causing it to go dizzy. Brightstar remarks that the droids are already distracted while Solay thinks the droids are just excited to see Batuu. Brightstar reminds his friends to stay focused, and Solay tries to remind the three astromechs to focus on their mission. 

===The lost package===
However, all six are soon distracted by a green-skinned street busker juggling balls. The busker tosses Brightstar a few balls to juggle, causing him to put down Master Drow's package near a trash can. While Brightstar is distracted juggling, a [[Unidentified trash collector droid|trash collector droid]] arrives in a [[trash hauler]] to empty the trash can. He accidentally picks up the blue package that Brightstar was carrying. After returning the balls to the vendor, Brightstar and the others discover that the package is missing. 

Solay deduces that someone must have mistaken it for trash, and Brightstar says this is why they need to stay focus on the mission. He decides that they should find the trash collector in order to retrieve the package. Solay suggests they split up to cover more ground. The group splits in three pairs. Dozer and Nubs scout near a trash can in [[Merchant Row]]. While waiting for a trash collector, Dozer and Nubs get distracted by a floating pink [[flower]]. Due to their distraction, the two of them miss the trash collector droid emptying the bin. They spot the package in the trash hauler just as it leaves. The two run after the vehicle.

A frantic Nubs contacts Solay via [[comlink]], telling her they got distracted and missed the trash hauler. Solay says that she and Gigi will keep an eye out for the trash hauler and take positions on the opposite sites of a bridge. Gigi detaches her drone for a better view, but she gets distracted by a four-winged [[kroa bird]]. Solay also spots the bird and is excited because they can only be found on Batuu. While the two are distracted, the trash hauler passes under the bridge. The two are upset they were too distracted, but Solay reassures Gigi that they can still count on Brightstar and Beepers. 

Meanwhile, Brightstar and Beepers take an interest in a rare white and blue [[starship]] from [[Variax]]. Solay contacts Brightstar and warns him that the trash hauler is coming their way. The two spot the trash hauler passing through the street below and follow it on foot through the [[Black Spire Outpost Market]]. They nearly bump into a [[loader droid]] and in the recovery, Brightstar runs into Nubs and Dozer, causing Dozer to hit the trash hauler. 

===The kindly trash collector droid===
The concerned trash collector droid exits the trash hauler and checks on Dozer, who is unharmed. Brightstar and Solay explain that he may have accidentally picked up a package in his trash hauler. The trash collector droid recalls encountering such a package but says that it may have taken it to the dump already. He explains that everything that goes into the dump gets recycled. He apologizes, but Brightstar apologizes for not looking after the package. Dozer apologizes on behalf of his droid friends. Nubs says they got distracted, and Solay says that they got excited about visiting Batuu. 

The trash collector droid agrees that Batuu is a beautiful place. Brightstar asks the droid if he could take them to the dump so that they can search for the package and deliver it to Master Drow. The kindly trash collector droid takes them to the [[Black Spire Outpost dump]] where they see a magnetic crane sifting metal objects which are then crushed and compressed by other machinery. A claw stacks the trash cubes into piles. 

===Junkyard adventure===
Working together, the younglings and droids resume their search for the package. Gigi spots the package but is unable to stop it from being caught by the magnetic crane, which lifts the object onto the conveyer belt towards the smasher. Beeper offers to fly Brightstar to the conveyer belt. While flying, Beeper is caught by the magnet while Brightstar lands on the conveyer belt system. Solay gets Nubs and Dozer to clear a path to the control panel by moving crates. 

Solay fiddles with the foreign controls, causing the magnet to spin. She presses another button which causes the mechanical claw to wave. Solay spots a [[dataport]] and convinces Gigi to connect to the computer in order to switch off the magnet. Brightstar runs on the conveyer belt after Beepers. Solay tasks him with retrieving the package while Dozer is tasked with reversing the conveyer belt before the package gets to the smasher. The lever reverses the wrong belt on the conveyer system, though, slowing Brightstar down.

Brightstar reaches the package just as it approaches the smashing machine. Gigi manages to deactivate the magnet, allowing Beepers to race towards Brightstar. While the others watch tensely, Brightstar rides on Beepers and manages to retrieve the package just seconds before it would be crushed by the smashing machine. Brightstar and Beepers circle before landing near the others, who congratulate them. Brightstar remarks that Black Spire Outpost is exciting while Beeper whistles. 

===Better late than never===
Later that evening, Master Drow waits beside a stall while eating a [[Ronto Wrap]]. He is relieved to see the younglings with the package. Solay hands him the package while Brightstar apologizes that they got distracted and that the package accidentally got lost. Drow is pleased the younglings succeeded with their mission and learned a valuable lesson along the way. 

Nubs is curious about the package and Drow reveals that it is a [[Tenoo tree]] sprout which Master [[Zia Zaldor Zanna]] wanted to give him for his experiments. Drow explains that they are growing all sorts of plants from across [[the galaxy]] on Batuu. As Master Drow heads back to the [[Batuu Jedi temple]], the younglings take the droids on a tour of Black Spire Outpost. Solay and Brightstar decide to show them the town's spires while recounting their adventure with [[Ozon Nimbee]].
