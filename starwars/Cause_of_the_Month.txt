'''Cause of the Month''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[August 19]], [[2004]] and took the form of an interview by [[Yara Grugara]] with [[San Herrera]] and [[Nia Reston]] on their newly published paper "[[Droid Rights]]."
