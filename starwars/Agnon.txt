'''Agnon''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[First Order officer|officer]] who held the [[Military rank|rank]]<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" /> of [[First Lieutenant]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> in the [[First Order military|armed forces]] of the [[First Order]]. He used a [[TIE/es assault shuttle|TIE echelon]] to travel to [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] during the First Order's [[First Order-Resistance War|conflict]] with the [[Resistance]], and was accompanied by a [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper sergeant|sergeant]] and several [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] of the [[709th Legion]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Agnon was created as part of the backstory for the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] theme park land,<ref name="GEStaff" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Opening Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-opening-dates-revealed|text=''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge Opening Dates Revealed}}</ref> He first appeared in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Galaxy's Edge 1|''Galaxy's Edge'' 1]], written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Will Sliney]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" /> on [[April 24]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/75139/star_wars_galaxys_edge_2019_1|text=Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge (2019) #1}}</ref> Since 2023, the role of Agnon has been open for casting of any gender.<ref name="GEStaff" />
