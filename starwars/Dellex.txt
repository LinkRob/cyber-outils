'''Dellex''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[cyborg]] who served as a [[Storm (rank)|Storm]] for the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization during the [[High Republic Era]]. She always thought of herself as ugly and because of this, spent all of her money on shiny mechanical upgrades to make herself more beautiful. She had worked under [[Kassav Milliko]] and his [[Kassav's Tempest|Tempest]] for a long time, with Kassav even being attracted to her at one point despite her perceived ugliness. He noted that her upgrades did not do her personality any favors and regarded her as a dangerous person.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

She served in [[Kassav's Tempest|Milliko's Tempest]] on several occasions<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> including a mission at the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]] in an attempt to extort their government by saving them from the [[Eriadu system Emergence|Emergence]] of [[hyperspace]] debris from the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]. However, they accidentally allowed the Emergence to contact the planet, causing tremendous casualties. The Eriaduans swore revenge on Milliko and his crew. Dellex would later participate in the [[Battle of Kur]], facing a [[Emergences task force|joint task force]] sent by the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Jedi Order]], in addition to a [[Eriaduan flotilla|flotilla]] of [[Eriaduan cruiser]]s. Although the Nihil employed a devastating array of cruel tactics at the battle, they were ultimately routed by the Republic forces. She was one of the last survivors aboard Milliko's [[flagship]], the ''[[New Elite]]'', when it was boarded by [[Eriaduan]] soldiers, who took their revenge by killing all aboard.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
