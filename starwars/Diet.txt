An organism's '''diet''' referred to what forms of [[Foodstuff|food]] it chose to eat. Forms of diet included herbivore and carnivore.<ref name="JFS">[[Star Wars: Journey Through Space (2015)|''Star Wars: Journey Through Space'']]</ref>

Carnivores were flesh-eaters. They included non-sentients such as the [[krayt dragon]],<ref name="DB Tat">{{Databank|tatooine|Tatooine}}</ref> and the [[colo claw fish]] from [[Naboo]],<ref name="DB Colo">{{Databank|colo-claw-fish|Colo Claw Fish}}</ref> but also sentient species such as the [[Zabrak]]s<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> and the [[Pau'an]]s.<ref name="VG">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> Carnivorous diets were common in cold environments, due to the lack of [[plant]]life.<ref name="EV">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Carnivorous plant]]s such as the [[reeksa]] were unusual plant [[species]] that evolved to capture and consume live prey in order to make up for the lack of nutrients in their soil.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

Herbivorous lifeforms such as the [[bantha]], the [[ikopi]], the [[happabore]], and the [[shaak]] all fed on plants.<ref name="EV" />

Omnivores were organisms that fed on both plant and animal matter. [[Reek]]s were large, muscular omnivores native to the [[planet]] [[Ylesia]] that, while herbivorous by nature, could survive on meat.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> The [[Vulptex|vulptices]] of [[Crait]] dug the [[salt]] crust of their world for small [[mammal]]s and [[tuber]]s.<ref name="RT:RF">''[[The Last Jedi: Rose Tico: Resistance Fighter]]''</ref>

Some species, like certain [[bird]]s on [[Jelucan]],<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> and the [[crab glider]]s native to the [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]], were carrion-eaters, feeding on refuse and other decaying organic matter.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

Other creatures were practitioners of geophagia, subsiding off of inorganic material. The [[fire rat]]s of [[Mustafar]] were one example of this phenomenon; they were known to gnaw on the [[lava crystal]]s of the [[Chaos|hellish]] planet.<ref name="Return 1">[[Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle 1|''Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle'' 1]]</ref>

Some species were energy-eaters. The [[conduit worm]]s fed off the electrical wiring of [[starship]]s,<ref name="AEYNK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> and the [[mynock]]s absorbed electrical, stellar, and electromagnetic energy using their mouths.<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Mynock - Creature}}</ref>
