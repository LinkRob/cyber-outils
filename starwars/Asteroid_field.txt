An '''asteroid field''' was composed of [[asteroid]] clusters of varying sizes that was found throughout [[the galaxy]]. Because of their unpredictability, asteroid fields were almost impossible to navigate through safely,<ref name="Asteroid field DB">{{Databank|asteroid-field|Asteroid Field}}</ref> and most [[pilot]]s did not dare flying through them.<ref name="Asteroid Field 2020">[[Star Wars Day-at-a-Time Calendar 2020|''Star Wars'' Day-at-a-Time Calendar 2020]]</ref> Certain living creatures, such as the [[exogorth]] of [[cantina]] legends<ref name="Space Slug">{{Databank|space-slug|Space Slug}}</ref> and the dangerous [[fyrnock]] were known to inhabit asteroid fields such as [[Fort Anaxes]].<ref name="Fyrnock">{{Databank|fyrnock|Fyrnock}}</ref> The exogorth lived within asteroid caves, and subsided on minerals, [[mynock]]s, and passing [[starship]]s.<ref name="Space Slug 2020">[[Star Wars Day-at-a-Time Calendar 2020|''Star Wars'' Day-at-a-Time Calendar 2020]]</ref>

In the lead-up to the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] employed a tactic of hiding his small [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7 ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] behind an asteroid within an asteroid field to evade detection and pursuit of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], who was piloting ''[[Slave I]]'' with the witness of his [[Parent|son]], [[Boba Fett]].<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref> The latter later anticipated a similar maneuver by the [[smuggler]] pilot [[Han Solo]], who hid his [[Millennium Falcon|craft]] from the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Death Squadron]] by attaching it to the back of an [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]].<ref name="Boba Fett DB">{{Databank|boba-fett|Boba Fett}}</ref> When the [[Star Destroyer]] ejected its waste just before entering [[hyperspace]] per standard procedure, Solo had his [[freighter]] fly amongst the resulting debris field, escaping undetected save from Boba Fett, who had become an experienced [[bounty]] hunter himself.<ref name="ESB" />
