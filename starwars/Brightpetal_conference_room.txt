The '''Brightpetal conference room''' was a room in the upper levels of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. It had a long oval table and a dozen slim chairs and a window looking out on the Coruscant skyline. During the late [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Sol]] met with another [[Jedi]] and later his [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] in the Brightpetal conference room to speak about their upcoming [[Mission to Siline]].<ref name="TCC" />
