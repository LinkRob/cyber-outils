'''Canto Bight''' was a coastal [[city]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. The city was a destination for wealthy individuals, as it was filled with [[casino]]s and [[racetrack]]s. During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[First Order]], Resistance members [[Finn]] and [[Rose Tico]] [[Mission to Canto Bight|traveled]] there.

==Description==
{{Quote|So! Canto Bight! Playground of the most glamorous beings in the galaxy! Big-money card games and high-stakes fathier races! The galaxy's biggest artificial ocean! And the fanciest food this side of Coruscant.|[[Kedpin Shoklop]]|<ref name="Rules of the Game" />}}
[[File:CantoCasino-CantoBight-SoH.png|thumb|left|350px|The Canto Casino was a main attraction of Canto Bight.]]
Canto Bight was the [[capital]]<ref name="TLJ Junior novel">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> [[city]] of [[Cantonica]], situated on the shores of the [[Sea of Cantonica]], the largest artificial [[ocean]] in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Rules of the Game" /> Canto Bight was a destination for wealthy tourists, [[Gambling|gamblers]], and war profiteers who visited its lavish casinos and racetracks; child workers [[Fathier racing|rode]] quadruped creatures called [[fathier]]s at the races. The [[Canto Bight Police Department]] were the primary [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] of the city.<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The city's [[South Stretch Worker's District]], home to the [[Sixth Kin]],<ref name="Aphra 7" /> was a vibrant district where residents and opportunists made a living off tourism. Canto Bight constantly evolved, with new distractions constructed onto the city's core every cycle.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|It's a terrible place filled with the worst people in the galaxy.|Rose Tico|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
Canto Bight was a hedonistic tourism and gambling destination on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] world of Cantonica, which was part of a corporate fiefdom known as the [[Corporate Sector]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> The city was built for the sole purpose of offering the greatest, most exclusive, and expensive entertainment in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> A young [[Binnot Ullo]] and [[Axel Greylark]] once swam in the oceans offshore of Canto Bight, after which they stole pies to eat under the sunshine. During the [[Night of Sorrow|Battle of Dalna]], Ullo recalled the memory as a way to try and trick Greylark into letting him escape, promising his former friend that they could do it again and become [[prince]]s of [[the galaxy]]. While he seemed initially open to rekindling their old brotherhood, Greylark was unable to forgive Ullo for destroying his beloved [[droid]] [[QN-1]].<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref>

At some point, the smuggler [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] played a card game in the city with a gambler named [[Brushaun]], the latter losing two thousand credits when Lando won the game.<ref name="Double or Nothing 1">[[Lando - Double or Nothing 1|''Lando - Double or Nothing'' 1]]</ref> The [[Tonnika sisters]] pulled a job in the casino on one occasion.<ref name="You Owe Me a Ride">{{StoryCite|story=You Owe Me a Ride|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>. The scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] was also born and raised there until she left at twenty two years old to run from both the [[Sixth Kin]] and [[Zerek Besh]] of whom she fell foul of because of two botched heists.<ref name="Outlaws" />

[[File:CantoBightProfilePromo-Outlaws.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Canto Bight during the Galactic Civil War]]
At the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]], Canto Bight was already being referred to as the home of the most luxurious casino around by certain individuals.<ref name="TEOL">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|annual=2018|story=The Lost Eggs of Livorno}}</ref> [[Ronen Tagge]] had a [[Ronen Tagge's Private Penthouse|penthouse]] in the city where he would hold parties.<ref name="Aphra3">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 3|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 3]]</ref>

During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the [[Resistance]] operatives Finn, Rose Tico and [[BB-8]] traveled to Canto Bight as part of a [[Mission to Canto Bight|mission]] to recruit the [[Master Codebreaker]] in order to disable the [[First Order]]'s [[hyperspace tracker]]; Finn and Rose<ref name="Episode VIII" /> crashed<ref name="Captured">{{StoryCite|story=Captured in Canto Bight|book=5-Minute Star Wars Stories Strike Back}}</ref> their [[Resistance transport pod|ship]] on a public beach, leading the [[Abednedo]] businessman [[Slowen Lo]] to alert the Canto Bight Police Department.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

They tried to make contact with the Master Codebreaker, but were instead arrested by Canto Bight police for a traffic violation. While being detained at the [[Canto Bight police headquarters]], the two met the [[slicer]] [[DJ]], who offered to help them disable the hyperspace tracker in return for their freedom. BB-8 also tied-up the guards, allowing Finn, Rose and DJ to escape the jail. While fleeing through the racetrack, Rose convinced a child worker named [[Temiri Blagg]] to help them escape, in return for giving him her Resistance ring. Blagg released a herd of fathiers, which stampeded through the streets of Canto Bight, causing the town's businesses significant damages.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Finn and Rose escaped on the back of a fathier to Canto Bight's outskirts, where they were picked up by DJ and BB-8, who had stolen the luxury yacht ''[[Libertine]]''. The group then traveled to the First Order [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]'' in an attempt to disable the hyperspace tracker.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Behind the scenes==
Canto Bight was created for [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], and it was described by director [[Rian Johnson]] as "a ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:Monte Carlo|Monte Carlo]]&ndash;type environment, a little [[Wikipedia:James Bond|James Bond]]&ndash;ish, a little [[Wikipedia:To Catch a Thief|''To Catch a Thief'']]." Canto Bight is also themed after the American city of [[Wikipedia:Las Vegas|Las Vegas]], due to its debauchery and number of casinos.<ref name="Vanity Fair: The Last Jedi Preview">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2017/05/star-wars-the-last-jedi-cover-portfolio|text=Cover Story: Star Wars: The Last Jedi, the Definitive Preview|author=Kamp, David|date=2017-05-24|work=Vanity Fair|archivedate=20200221133741}}</ref>

The city and its casino design and look were heavily inspired by the city of [[Wikipedia:Dubrovnik|Dubrovnik]] in [[Wikipedia:Croatia|Croatia]]. Parts of ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' (Canto Bight scenes) were filmed in Dubrovnik in March 2016. Canto Bight chase scenes with Finn and Rose are filmed on the city main street [[Wikipedia:Stradun (street)|Stradun]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.croatiaweek.com/video-star-wars-episode-viii-starts-shooting-in-dubrovnik-this-week|text=Star Wars Episode VIII Starts Shooting in Dubrovnik this Week|date=2016-03-08|work=Croatia Week|archivedate=20200221133913}}</ref><ref>{{WebCite|url=https://balkaninsight.com/2016/02/15/star-wars-bringing-global-prestige-for-dubrovnik-02-12-2016|text=Star Wars Adds Shine to Croatian 'Pearl' Dubrovnik|author=Milekic, Sven|date=2016-02-15|archivedate=20200221133839}}</ref>

During the late design process of ''The Last Jedi'', [[Lucasfilm]] president [[Kathleen Kennedy]] said that Canto Bight's design didn't feel enough "''Star Wars''" to her, reason for which the creative team scrapped many of their original ideas for Canto Bight and ultimately drew inspiration from early drawings by [[Ralph McQuarrie]] for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt's]] [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] during the development of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] in the [[1980]]s.<ref name="Art TLJ">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]''</ref>
