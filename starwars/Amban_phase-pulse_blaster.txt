The '''Amban phase-pulse blaster''', also known as an '''Amban sniper rifle''' or '''Notimo-6 phase-pulse disruptor rifle''', was a type of [[disruptor]] [[sniper rifle]] used by the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]]. The weapon was capable of electrocuting opponents as well as completely disintegrating targets. It was also capable of listening to conversations at least a house away. Djarin used the deadly weapon to great effect during his bounty hunting career.

==Description==
[[File:AmbanPowerCellPort-SW100Objects.png|thumb|left|300px|The power cell port on an Amban snipe rifle]]
{{Quote|That's one impressive weapon.|Greef Karga|<ref name="Chapter 3" />}}
Manufactured by [[Amban Arms Corporation]],<ref name="sw100" /> the Amban phase-pulse blaster was a modified [[sniper rifle]]<ref name="SWCT">{{Topps|set=2020 Topps Star Wars: The Mandalorian Season 1|subset=Tools of the Bounty Hunter|cardname=Amban Rifle}}</ref> with a long barrel and a [[Color|brown]] stock.<ref name="Chapter 1" /> It was tipped with a fork-shaped [[Ion-prod electro-bayonet|resonator]] and a specially-calibrated visual [[scope]]. It could deliver a paralyzing electrical pulse at close range, and at long range, it could disintegrate organic matter on contact.<ref name="SWCT" />

The firearm was calibrated to fire a shock of anywhere from 50,000 to 500,000 [[volt]]s. It could incapacitate most species. Used against more massive creatures, the stun feature was little more than a painful annoyance, like a sudden pinching sensation. On its highest setting, one blast would send a ionic pulse reverberating through a lifeform's essential systems. Contact with living matter vibrated the molecules so violently that the jolt and subsequent burst of released energy tore the target to shreds.<ref name="sw100" />

[[File:AmbanPhasePulseRifleBlast-TMCh2.png|thumb|right|300px|The target of an Amban phase-pulse blaster would be disintegrated (a Trandoshan falls to Din Djarin's Amban, pictured).]]
A shower of sparks, charred flesh, and shreds of clothing were frequently all that was left, making victim-identification extremely difficult. For this reason, bounty hunters only used the rifle's highest setting as a last resort when hunting fugitives, as they could not claim success without at least the trophy of the intact head of their prey.<ref name="sw100" />

==History==
===From Pagodon to Nevarro===
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date Note">{{MandoDate}}</ref> the [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] used this rifle on several missions. On his expedition to claim the [[bounty]] on [[The Fledgling Mythrol|the Mythrol]] on the [[planet]] [[Pagodon]], he used the taser function on a [[Ravinak]] that attacked his ship, the ''[[Razor Crest]]''.<ref name="Chapter 1" />

[[File:AmbanPhasePulseRifle-TMCh2.png|thumb|left|300px|Djarin aims his Amban phase-pulse rifle]]
After being [[Mission to Arvala-7|commissioned]] by [[the Client]] to acquire an asset known as [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] on the planet [[Arvala-7]],<ref name="Chapter 1" /> Djarin used it as a scouting scope upon his arrival and wielded the rifle later to combat several [[Trandoshan]] bounty hunters who were seeking to claim his bounty. When Djarin discovered that [[Jawa]]s had stripped his [[Starship|ship]] for parts, he fired on them from a distance, disintegrating several members of their group. When Djarin was forced to retrieve a [[mudhorn]] [[Mudhorn egg|egg]] in return for parts to repair his ship, his [[disruptor]] rifle became inundated with mud which rendered it useless against the large creature.<ref name="Chapter 2">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 2: The Child}}</ref>

Before rescuing Grogu, Djarin used his pulse rifle to listen in on a conversation between the Client and Dr. Pershing. Djarin later used the weapon's taser function on an [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] [[stormtrooper]] while [[Incident on Nevarro|rescuing]] Grogu from their [[Imperial Remnant safe house|compound]]. However, after crossing the Client, [[Greef Karga]] and members of the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] came after him, ambushing him in the middle to the street. When Djarin was pinned down, several hunters advanced on him, but Djarin used the weapon to disintegrate several of them, causing the survivors to take cover. Djarin continued to fire before escaping with the help of [[the Tribe]].<ref name="Chapter 3" />

===Djarin's continued adventures===
[[File:DinDjarinsArmor2-CGSWG.png|thumb|right|300px|Over the course of his journey with Grogu, Din Djarin continued to make use of his Amban phase-pulse blaster until its destruction.]]
While defending a [[Unidentified human tribe|human tribe's]] [[Unidentified village (Sorgan)|village]] on [[Sorgan]], Djarin used the weapon against an invading [[Klatooinian raiders|Klatooinian tribe]]. When the [[Klatooinian]] [[AT-ST Raider]] failed to activate their trap, he gave the rifle to his new comrade, the former [[shock trooper]] [[Carasynthia Dune]], who then jumped into the pond and fired at the walker in order to bait it into their trap.<ref name="Chapter 4" />

Djarin also utilized the taser function of the weapon to electrocute the insides of a [[krayt dragon]], effectively forcing it to open its mouth after engulfing Djarin, allowing him to escape from the stomach of the beast.<ref name="Chapter 9" />

The scouting scope was used by Djarin on the woods of [[Corvus]] while seeking for [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Chapter 13" />

The rifle was aboard the ''Razor Crest'' on [[Tython]] while Djarin took Grogu to an [[Force-henge|ancient stone temple]] of the [[Jedi]]. During a subsequent encounter with [[Gideon|Moff Gideon's]] remnant Imperial forces, Gideon shot Djarin's ship with an orbital laser blast from his [[Gideon's light cruiser|light cruiser]], destroying the ship and the rifle.<ref name="Chapter 14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref>

Several Amban phase-pulse blasters were on a self in a [[TR-RL3 Terrapin-class bulk tanker]] owned by [[Unidentified gunrunner droid|a]] [[droid]] [[Smuggler|gunrunner]]. After recovering from the [[dragonsnake]] [[poison]] and fighting several [[Amani]] warriors, [[Din Djarin]] found one and decided to use it to kill the [[The Twins|Hutt Twins]]. After crashing the ship at [[Hutt Palace|their palace]], Djarin used his new Amban rifle to damage [[Arges (droid)|Arges]] and [[Bullethead]]. He was about to use his new Amban rifle when [[Keibu]] interfered and he ended up fighting [[Embo]] in [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]]. Despite Embo having the upper hand, Djarin used the prongs to [[stun]] Embo unconscious before using the rifle to destroy several [[Droid Gotra]]'s [[sentry droid]]s who were breaking into the throne room, including Arges. Soon Djarin used his new rifle to blast open a hole in the wall and escape with his new rifle when a squad of [[Adelphi Rangers]] lead by [[Colonel]] [[Ward]] came to rescue him and [[Garazeb Orrelios|Zeb]] flew the [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-Wing]] to pick up him, Grogu, and [[Rotta]].<ref name="The Mandalorian and Grogu">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>

===Cold War era===
By the time of the [[Cold War]], a rifle was known to have come into the possession of [[Dok-Ondar]] and was displayed alongside a Mandalorian helmet in [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]].<ref name="SWGE">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:PaarsIchthyodont-SWHS.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Amban rifle first originated in ''The Star Wars Holiday Special''.]]
The Amban sniper rifle first appeared in "[[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian]]," the debut episode of the [[2019]] [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="Chapter 1" /> which aired on [[November 12]], 2019.<ref name="DTCI">{{WebCite|url=https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/the_mandalorian_project_one-sheet_10_ec1e005f.pdf|text=The Mandalorian Media Kit|work=[https://dtcimedia.disney.com Disney Direct-to-Consumer & International]|archivedate=20230315011507}}</ref> However, it traces its design back to the [[Amban phase-pulse blaster/Legends|weapon]] of [[Boba Fett]] in the [[The Story of the Faithful Wookiee|animated segment]] of [[1978]]'s ''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]'', the first ''[[Star Wars]]'' television property and the first appearance of [[Mandalorian armor]].<ref name="Holiday Special">''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]''</ref> Its initial appearance in ''The Mandalorian'' pays homage to the ''Holiday Special'', being used to ward off a subterranean beast (a ravinak in ''The Mandalorian'' versus a [[Panna dragon]] in the ''Holiday Special'') attempting to devour a [[BTL Y-wing starfighter/Legends|Y-Wing]] starship with the protagonists on board.<ref name="Chapter 1" /><ref name="Holiday Special" />
