'''Chylaroo''' was a small [[rodent]]-like creature native to the [[planet]] [[Yamradi]].<ref name="Creature Safari" />

== Biology ==
Chylaroo has two big feet, a fuzzy brown head, brown fur that looks like blades of grass, a thin tail, a pair of big ears and a snout.
