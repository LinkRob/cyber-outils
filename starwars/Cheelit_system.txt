The '''Cheelit system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Semagi sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]] that fell within grid square I-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It contained [[Cheelit]], an astronomical object that was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[brain crawler]] [[worm]].<ref name="RSG" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Cheelit system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Cheelit system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in "[[Dark Lord's Conscience]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Alan Moore]], illustrated by [[John Stokes]], and first published in [[The Empire Strikes Back Monthly 155|''The Empire Strikes Back Monthly'' 155]] in [[April]] [[1982]].<ref name="DLC">{{StoryCite|story=Dark Lord's Conscience|book=The Empire Strikes Back Monthly 155}}</ref> The system was then identified for the first time in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', which was written by Fry and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
