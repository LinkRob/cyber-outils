'''Force binding''', also known as '''Force containment''', was a [[Force power]] of the [[light side of the Force]] that gave its [[Force-sensitive|users]] the ability to contain the influence that an [[Force artifact|artifact]] of the [[dark side of the Force]] had on its surroundings. Members of the [[Jedi Order]] used binding rituals on artifacts so that they could be transported safely.<ref name="Into the Dark" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Cohmac Vitus]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Orla Jareni]], and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Reath Silas]] used Force binding on a set of four [[Binding statues|idols]] they discovered on the [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine space station]].<ref name="Into the Dark" />
