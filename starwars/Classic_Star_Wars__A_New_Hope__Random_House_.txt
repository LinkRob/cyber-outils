'''''Classic Star Wars: A New Hope''''' is a [[Random House]] adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. A part of the "Bullseye Book" series, it was adapted by [[Larry Weinberg]] and released in [[1995]]. As a young readers novel, it condenses many of the scenes and puts [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] as a key player. A new version was released in [[2004]].
