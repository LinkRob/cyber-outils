'''Daxiong''' ('''Guo Jing Xiong''') is an illustrator, artist and founder of Flag Studios. He provided the art for two of [[Dark Horse Comics]]' [[Star Wars Adventures (Dark Horse Comics)|''Star Wars Adventures'']]: [[Star Wars Adventures: Luke Skywalker and the Treasure of the Dragonsnakes|''Luke Skywalker and the Treasure of the Dragonsnakes'']] and [[Star Wars Adventures: Boba Fett and the Ship of Fear|''Boba Fett and the Ship of Fear'']].
