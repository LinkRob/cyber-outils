The '''Aethersprite Saber Design''' was a custom [[lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] owned by the [[droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[J-3DI]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Jedi Saber Design|standard lightsaber]]. It was designed in a way that was meant to invoke the aesthetics of the [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor]]s used by [[Jedi|members]] of the [[Jedi Order]] shortly before its collapse.<ref name="SWH" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aethersprite Saber Design was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter J-3DI's Jedi Saber Design, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Aethersprite (J-3DI)|Aethersprite]].<ref name="SWH" />
