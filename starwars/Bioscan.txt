A '''bioscan''' was a device similar to a [[mediscan unit]]. It observed an individual's biological make-up, origin, age, and other factors. A bioscan could detect anomalies and health problems in an individual.<ref name="CFOWM">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>
