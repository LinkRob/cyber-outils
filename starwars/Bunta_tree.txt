The '''bunta tree''' was a type of [[tree]] that grew on the [[planet]] [[Brendok]]. Its [[Color|yellow]] leaves were used to create a dark [[poison]] sharing its [[Bunta|name]].<ref name="Destiny" /> A bunta tree near [[Brendok fortress]] also had bright blue fungal growths and the ancient tree stood out in Brendok's mossy forests.<ref name="DB">{{Databank|bunta-tree|Bunta tree}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Storywise, the script called for a 'waterfall/special place' where the girls could go to be away from the fortress I looked for waterfalls and other places and none of them said, 'why would you go there?' So in looking around locations, there was this forest that was all pine trees but had one dead oak tree in the middle of it. I kind of got interested in that and wondered if we might make a 'space willow tree' out of it. And so that tree is actually there, and the yellow is there, but we put it all on it. So technically we made that tree.|Production designer [[Kevin Jenkins]]|<ref name="LOL">{{Lucasfilm|url=news/lucasfilm-on-location-the-acolyte|text=Lucasfilm on Location: ''The Acolyte''}}</ref>}}
A dead oak tree in the Cadlan Woods of the [[Wikipedia:Brecon Beacons National Park|Bannau Brycheiniog National Park]] (Brecon Beacons) in [[Wikipedia:Wales|Wales]] was decorated with yellow-leafed branches for the willow-like effect of the bunta tree.<ref name="LOL" />
