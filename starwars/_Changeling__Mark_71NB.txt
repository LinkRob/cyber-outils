The '''"Changeling" Mark 71NB''' was a type of [[racing starfighter]]. [[Ace Squadron]] [[pilot]] [[Bo Keevil]] flew one, the ''[[Yellow Ace]]'', during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="USW, NE" />

{{Ship-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The "Changeling" Mark 71NB first appeared in part one of the two-part episode "[[The Recruit]],"<ref name="The Recruit" /> the first episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[October 7]], [[2018]].<ref name="Disney release">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> The model was identified in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''.<ref name="USW, NE" />

The model was named by [[Adam Bray]] in reference to [[Mark Newbold]], with "71" referencing his birth year and "NB" referencing "Newbold."<ref name="Fantha Tracks">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fanthatracks.com/news/literature/ultimate-star-wars-find-the-hidden-fantha-tracker|text=Ultimate Star Wars: Find the hidden Fantha Tracker|date=2019-10-14|author=[[Mark Newbold]]|archivedate=20191014193923}}</ref>
