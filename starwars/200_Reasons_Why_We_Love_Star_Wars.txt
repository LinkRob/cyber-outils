"'''200 Reasons Why We Love ''Star Wars'''''" is an article featured in [[Star Wars Insider 200|''Star Wars Insider'' 200]].<ref name="SWI" /> Published [[February 9]], [[2021]],<ref name="Titan" /> it celebrates two-hundred issues of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' with two-hundred reasons to love ''[[Star Wars]]'' from various people who have contributed to ''Star Wars'' over the years. It mistakenly says that [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] was released in [[1999]] as opposed to [[2015]].<ref name="SWI" />
