The '''Aquilaris system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Sanbra sector]] that was part of both the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing" /> It was situated in grid square O-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. The system partly shared its name with the [[Aquilaris Minor system]], which was located in the [[Grumani sector]] of the Outer Rim. Both systems also partly shared a name<ref name="Appendix" /> with the [[Aquilaris Classic]] [[podracing]] [[Racetrack|track]], which the [[Neidorian]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Paghee'Dhe Dahglyn]] had won a race on by the [[time]] they took part in the [[Hydian-Hutta Podracing Circuit]]<ref name="BV">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> around [[10 BBY]].<ref name="BVDate">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Aquilaris system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Aquilaris system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']], a [[1999]] video game released by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="Racer">[[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']]</ref> It was first identified as the Aquilaris [[Star system/Legends|system]] in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Fry.<ref name="EA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
