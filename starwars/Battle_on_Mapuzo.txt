The '''battle on Mapuzo'''<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|214|Decoding Darth Vader}}</ref> was a skirmish fought on the [[mining]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Mapuzo]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Part III" /> in [[9 BBY]]<ref name="Timeline" /> as part of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Hunt for Obi-Wan|hunt]] for [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who arrived on Mapuzo with [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]],<ref name="Part III" /> whom he had [[Search for Princess Leia|recently]] rescued from the planet [[Daiyu]], on instructions about a contact from the criminal [[Haja Estree]].<ref name="Part II" /> Although Kenobi came to doubt Estree, he found the man had been telling the truth upon meeting [[Tala Durith]], an [[Imperial officer]] who had defected from the Empire to join [[Hidden Path|the Path]] that had helped other [[Jedi]] in the past. However, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] himself arrived on Mapuzo with the aim of confronting his former master, defeating Kenobi in [[Lightsaber combat|combat]] but staying back as he was saved by Durith and the [[loader droid]] [[NED-B]].<ref name="Part III" />

==Prelude==
Traveling to the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Mapuzo]] via a [[TR-286 transport|TR-286]] designated [[transport eight]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] snuck off the [[starship]] and attempted to rendezvous with a contact from the [[Hidden Path]] operating there. Unable to locate them, Kenobi and Leia met [[Freck]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] sympathizer who was traveling in his [[speeder truck]]. Developing a cover, Kenobi and Leia were able to convince Freck to take them to a spaceport. On the way, local [[stormtrooper]]s were picked up by Freck and taken to an Imperial facility nearby, until they arrived at an Imperial checkpoint.<ref name="Part III" />

After questioning Kenobi and Leia's identities, Kenobi attacked the stormtroopers with his [[blaster]], [[Death|killing]] multiple [[stormtrooper]]s. [[Imperial officer]] [[Tala Durith]] arrived with three other stormtroopers on an [[CSL-430 hover transport|Imperial ground troop transport]], forcing Kenobi to surrender. Durith, however, shot and killed the three troopers by firing into their backs. From there, the three escaped to a local [[Mapuzo mining village|village]]. The [[Inquisitorius]] received information that Kenobi was found on Mapuzo, and ordered the Imperial officers with them to mobilize their [[battalion]]s and shut down every port.<ref name="Part III" />

==The battle==
{{Quote|What have you become?''"<br />"''I am what you made me.|Obi-Wan Kenobi meeting Darth Vader|<ref name="Part III" />}}
[[File:Kenobi_and_Vader_duel_after_10_Years.png|thumb|left|260px|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Darth Vader have their second duel after 10 years.]]
As Kenobi fled into a nearby quarry, he encountered [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]], who followed him. After the [[Sith Lord]] ignites [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|his lightsaber]], Kenobi ran away immediately but was followed by Vader, who taunted the Jedi Master before he ignited his [[lightsaber]] as the two stood face to face. Kenobi asked his former [[Padawan|apprentice]] what has he become only for Vader to respond that he became what Kenobi [[Duel on Mustafar|made]] him.<ref name="Part III" />

While Kenobi continued to try and avoid Vader by walking through the quarry, the Sith Lord emerged from the darkness and swung his lightsaber at Kenobi, who reflected it with his [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber|own lightsaber]]. A brief [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] between the two former friends ensued, with Vader quickly overpowering his former Master, who had grown inept after [[Standard year|years]] of not using [[the Force]].<ref name="Part III" />

[[File:Vader-won-this-round.png|thumb|right|260px|Obi-Wan Kenobi moments before being burned by Vader.]]
As the Sith Lord forced Kenobi to run away, Vader knocked down a storage container filled with flammable rocks while he seized Obi-Wan with the Force. After igniting the flammable rocks with his lightsaber, Vader forced Kenobi into the [[Fire|flames]], severely burning him while claiming that his pain has only just begun. The Sith Lord then ordered his stormtroopers to seize Kenobi, but Durith arrived and re-ignited the rocks with a [[blaster]] bolt, cutting Kenobi off from Vader and his [[troop]]s while she rescued the Jedi Master.<ref name="Part III" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|What was key about that sequence and Vader at this point is really feeling the Anakin under it all, the range and the anger, and articulating that within the sequence. It was about finding ways to feel the rivalry that exists after everything they've gone through and what are the opportunities within the fight so that it's not just slug, slug, slug. It was about finding a moment to understand the depth of the relationship between the two of them.|Joby Harold discussing the duel between Obi-Wan Kenobi and Darth Vader|<ref name="Leia Secret">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/obi-wan-kenobi-leia-disney-1235160356|text='Obi-Wan Kenobi' Writer Joby Harold Relieved Leia Was Kept Secret|author=Davids, Brian|date=2022-06-07|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20220607180506}}</ref>}}
The Battle on Mapuzo was first pictured in the teaser trailer for the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' television series, which was released on [[March 9]], [[2022]].<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|TWTfhyvzTx0|Obi-Wan Kenobi &#124; Teaser Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/teaser-trailer-obi-wan-kenobi}}</ref> It first appeared in the [[Part III|third episode]] of the series, which was directed by [[Deborah Chow]]<ref name="Part III" /> and released on [[June 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-june-2022|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in June 2022}}</ref>

Writer [[Joby Harold]] had Kenobi and Vader duel in the series because he found that there was no line in a [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] that said they could not duel. When Harold was writing fight scenes for the series, such as the duel on Mapuzo, he tried to find a way for character to come through action. He wrote the choreography from a character point of view, which got handed off to stunt coordinator [[Jonathan (Jojo) Eusebio|Jonathan Eusebio]] who developed the fight sequence with Chow. Harold said the key to the sequence and Vader is feeling and articulating the range and anger under it all. He also wanted to find ways to feel the rivalry between the characters and to define the opportunities that are present in the fight.<ref name="Leia Secret" />
