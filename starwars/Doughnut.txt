'''Doughnuts''' were a type of [[foodstuff]] eaten as a treat. [[Doughnut dispenser droid]]s could serve doughnuts, and were found in [[Ashton Stadium]] on [[the Wheel]].<ref name="Obi-Wan and Anakin" />

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Doughnuts first appeared in the [[canon]] junior novel ''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure]]'', which was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Obi-Wan and Anakin" />

===Non-canon history===
{{Quote|GO AWAY!''"<br />"''But we brought doughnuts.''"<br />"''That was a cheap move.|Luke Skywalker and Rey|<ref name="Rey and Pals">''[[Rey and Pals]]''</ref>}}
Doughnuts also appear in the non-canon picture book ''[[Rey and Pals]]'', by [[Jeffrey Brown]]. When [[Luke Skywalker]] was hiding in his [[Luke's hut|hut]] on [[Ahch-To]], [[Chewbacca]] and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] attempted to bribe him to come out with doughnuts, which he described as a "cheap move."<ref name="Rey and Pals" />
