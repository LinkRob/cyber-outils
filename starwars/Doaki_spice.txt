'''Doaki spice''' was a type of [[spice]] that was green when fresh. During a brawl on<ref name="Aftermath" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Corellia]], the veteran [[bounty hunter]] [[Dengar]] claimed his opponent, the [[bounty]] hunter [[Mercurial Swift]], was an amateur by taunting that he was as green as fresh doaki spice.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Doaki spice was mentioned in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
