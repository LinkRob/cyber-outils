'''Echo Stations''' were a series of small scouting outposts on the icy [[planet]] [[Hoth]] and were used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] to keep an eye out for any dangers. Most notable Echo Stations included [[Echo Station 5-7]] and [[Echo Station 3-T-8]].<ref name="ESB" />
