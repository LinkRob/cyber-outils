"'''Elusive'''" is an [[adventure]] for [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition|''Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game,'' 2nd ed.]] It was written by [[Chris Hind]] and published as part of the book [[No Disintegrations (West End Games)|''No Disintegrations'']] in [[1997]]. "Elusive" is the fifth and final adventure in the book, all of which are aimed at [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] characters. The adventure takes place during the [[Rebellion era]].<ref name="ND 2">[[No Disintegrations (West End Games)|''No Disintegrations'']], p. 2.</ref>

==Plot summary==
The player characters are [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]] hired to capture a [[Herglic/Legends|Herglic]] [[Gambling/Legends|gambler]] named [[Narloch]] of [[Giju/Legends|Giju]]<ref name="ND 72">[[No Disintegrations (West End Games)|''No Disintegrations'']], p. 72.</ref> by an anonymous client (unbeknownst them them, a [[Noble/Legends|noble]] named Lord [[Haakon]], who lost a game of [[Sabacc/Legends|sabacc]] to the Herglic). The bounty is private (and thus illegal).<ref name="ND 74">[[No Disintegrations (West End Games)|''No Disintegrations'']], p. 74.</ref> The adventure follows a non-linear structure, allowing the players to accomplish its objectives in the order of their choosing.<ref name="ND 72" />

The characters are pursuing a small-time [[Bounty/Legends|bounty]] on the [[Hurt Vectors]] [[Swoop gang/Legends|swoop gang]]. They confront the gang in their hideout in [[Vandalar City]] on [[Reaper's World/Legends|Reaper's World]], subdue them, and turn them into the [[Vandalar City police station|local police station]] for their reward.<ref name="ND 74" /> They then receive a strange message on a [[Comlink/Legends|comlink]] from the [[Information broker/Legends|informant]] [[Vox (Defel)|Vox]]. She promises them a new bounty when they return to their room, and sure enough, they find a [[Datafile/Legends|datafile]] there detailing the bounty on [[Narloch]] of [[Giju/Legends|Giju]]. Asking around Vandalar City yields several leads:<ref name="ND 75">[[No Disintegrations (West End Games)|''No Disintegrations'']], p. 75.</ref> Narloch has interests in several casinos in the sector, has left for Emmer, and has ties to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba the Hutt]].<ref name="ND 77">[[No Disintegrations (West End Games)|''No Disintegrations'']], p. 77.</ref> The bounty hunters eventually catch up to Narloch and capture him, at which point they learn that he has struck a deal with the [[Ubese/Legends|Ubese]] bounty hunter [[Boushh/Legends|Boushh]], who had also been seeking his head.<ref name="ND 78">[[No Disintegrations (West End Games)|''No Disintegrations'']], p. 78.</ref>

==Continuity==
Once the player characters start on Narloch of Giju's trail, the adventure is open ended and allows for the possibility of a capture at any point. It outlines Narloch's schedule and leaves it up to the gamemaster to determine when the player characters encounter their target.<ref name="ND 78" />
