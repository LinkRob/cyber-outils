A '''double-bladed spinning lightsaber''' was utilized by the [[Eleventh Brother]] during his [[time]] as an Inquisitor of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was similar in appearance to other [[double-bladed spinning lightsaber]]s wielded by [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]].

==Description==
{{Listen3|EleventhBrotherLightsaber-Resolve.flac|the lightsaber's blade being waved}}
The [[double-bladed spinning lightsaber]] was used by the [[Eleventh Brother]], a [[Inquisitor|member]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Inquisitorius]]. It could switch between crescent mode, for a single [[Lightsaber|blade]], or disk mode, for one or two [[Double-bladed lightsaber|blades]] and the ability to spin. The [[weapon]] had a cylindrical [[hilt]] with an [[emitter ring]], forming a disc-hilt, with filigree near both emitters.<ref name="Oblivion" /> It contained two [[kyber crystal]]s<ref name="SecretsJedi" /> that emitted [[Color|red]] blades.<ref name="Oblivion" />

==History==
===Hunting Maul===
Sometime between [[17 BBY]] and [[13 BBY]],<ref name="ShadowLordDate">{{ShadowLordDate}}</ref> the Eleventh [[Brother (Inquisitorius)|Brother]] journeyed to the [[planet]] [[Janix]] to assist the [[Marrok|First Brother]] in the [[Hunt for Maul|hunt]] for the [[Zabrak]] [[Maul]], a former [[Sith]]. The two Inquisitors traveled aboard an Imperial [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]] to locate Maul. Upon cutting the former Sith's path off on a facility bridge, the Eleventh Brother leaped from the [[transport]], alongside<ref name="Oblivion" /> his<ref name="Companion">{{InsiderCite|230|Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi Companion|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'' Companion}}</ref> comrade, with his lightsaber in [[Limb|hand]]. Maul ignited his [[Maul's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and charged the two Inquisitors, and the Eleventh Brother responded in kind with the ignition of both his blades. After the Inquisitors [[Lightsaber duel|deflected]] Maul's strikes, the Zabrak jumped to a broken bridge below with the First Brother and Eleventh Brother following suit, while Maul's [[Shadow Collective|associates]] engaged in battle against Imperial [[Stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Oblivion" />

On the bridge Maul drove the Inquisitors back, forcing them to retreat to a nearby roof. The Eleventh Brother blocked Maul's<ref name="Oblivion" /> ferocious<ref name="OblivionAD">{{MSL|Chapter 7: Call to Oblivion}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> attacks, after the Zabrak followed them. The masked Inquisitor went on the offensive, but Maul blocked his attacks as well and then pushed his hilt against the Inquisitor's, shoving him back. Maul retreated into a [[cave]] behind a [[Water|waterfall]], where the pair followed. As the Inquisitors searched for the Zabrak, the Eleventh Brother<ref name="Oblivion" /> anticipated<ref name="OblivionAD" /> an attack from behind, whipping around and blocking Maul's strike. The two clashed blades, with Maul rolling to dodge one of the Eleventh Brother's swings, before eventually the Zabrak [[Force push]]ed the Inquisitor away, with the First Brother taking his place in the duel.<ref name="Oblivion" />

===Cornering Maul===
[[File:TheCrowAndMarrokDuellingMaul.png|thumb|250px|left|The Eleventh Brother damaged Maul's legs during their duel]]
After the First Brother [[Telekinesis|Force pushed]] Maul into a [[Stone|stalagmite]], the former Sith buckled as his [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] leg fritzed. The Eleventh Brother took the opportunity to strike, but Maul was able to dodge the attack. On the bridge, a member of Maul's team, [[Scorn]],<ref name="Oblivion" /> noticed the red flashes of the lightsaber duel and ran towards the cave. The duel continued as the Inquisitors exchanged clashes with Maul, until the Eleventh Brother's lightsaber made contact with Maul's cybernetic thigh, forcing the former Sith to the ground. The Inquisitor switched his lightsaber to disk mode and began spinning the blades around their hilt as he approached his enemy. As he raised the lightsaber to strike, Scorn entered the cave and rammed the Eleventh Brother aside. The First Brother quickly destroyed the loyalist's [[weapon]], and as Scorn swung a fist at the First Brother, the Eleventh Brother impaled his chest from behind.<ref name="Oblivion" />

The Eleventh Brother shoved the loyalist off his blade before [[The Force|force]] pushing<ref name="Oblivion" /> him<ref name="OblivionAD" /> out of the cave and to his [[death]]. The two Inquisitors, lightsabers in hand, then encroached on Maul,<ref name="Oblivion" /> forcing<ref name="OblivionAD" /> him to jump to the ground below. The pair followed after and flanked the Zabrak. Once Maul was cornered again, the First Brother approached to kill him. However, Maul reached out with the force and caused a cave-in, forcing the Inquisitors to retreat.<ref name="Oblivion" />

===Jedi do not attack the unarmed===
{{Listen3|EleventhBrotherLightsaberDrag-Resolve.flac|the lightsaber being dragged through the ground}}
Sometime between [[17 BBY]] and [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Death">The Eleventh Brother is featured in ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'', which takes place between [[17 BBY]] and around [[13 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:ShadowLordDate|here]], therefore meaning his [[death]] in "[[Resolve (episode)|Resolve]]" must occur no earlier than [[17 BBY]]. The end of "Resolve" depicts [[Ahsoka Tano]] becoming a part of the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], meaning it must be before the events of "[[Out of Darkness]]," which ''Timelines'' dates to [[5 BBY]], where she is working with the rebellion. Therefore, the events of "Resolve" must take place between 17 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 5 BBY.</ref> the Eleventh Brother traveled to a [[Unidentified farm village|remote village]] to investigate a report of a [[Jedi]] being there.<ref name="Resolve">{{TOTJ|Resolve (episode)}}</ref> He set the [[City|village]] on [[fire]] to find the Jedi.<ref name="Who">{{SW|url=news/who-is-ahsoka-tano|text=Who Is Ahsoka Tano?|author=[[Kelly Knox|Knox, Kelly]]|date=2023-08-15}}</ref> After failing to find the Jedi, the Inquisitor dragged his lightsaber in the ground as he paced around two hostages, an old [[Unidentified old man (Ahsoka Tano)|man]] and a [[Unidentified village brother|boy]] that reported the Jedi. He leveled his lightsaber to the informant's throat as he questioned the truth of the his claims.<ref name="Resolve" />

The exhibit was available from [[July 24]] to [[July 27]], 2025.<ref name="Sketch" />

The episode "Resolve" includes an animation error with the lightsaber, specifically during a close-up of the Eleventh Brother threatening the informant where the weapon is in crescent mode, in comparison to the lightsaber being depicted in disk mode in the scenes immediately before and after the moment.<ref name="Resolve" /> The [[2016]] young-adult novel [[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']], which was written by [[E. K. Johnston]], features Ahsoka Tano battling and killing an Inquisitor known as the [[Sixth Brother]] during an [[Raada Uprising|uprising]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Raada]] and then [[Purification|purifying]] his [[Sixth Brother's lightsaber|lightsaber's]] kyber crystals.<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> After the release of ''Tales of the Jedi'', [[Dave Filoni]]<ref name="Nerdist" />&mdash;supervising director<ref name="TOTJ Is Here">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-tales-of-the-jedi-is-here|text=''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'' Is Here!|date=2022-10-26}}</ref>&mdash;confirmed that "Resolve" was based on the same outline provided for the ''Ahsoka'' novel, and was intended to be the same story.<ref name="Nerdist" /> This article assumes the events of ''Ahsoka'' and "Resolve" are separate.
