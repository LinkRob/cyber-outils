'''Daal''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who lived on a [[Unidentified planet (Screecher's Reach)|planet]] where she worked in an [[Factory Workshop|Imperial workhouse]] as an orphan. At some point, she received an [[Daal's medal|amulet]] that allowed for communication to a [[Sith Lord]] known as the [[Sith Mother]]. The [[Sith]] instructed Daal to travel to the [[mountain]] known as [[Screecher's Reach]] and promised a new life if she passed a test. Eventually, she decided that it was time to go and stormed out of the workshop. Daal and her friends [[Baython]], [[Keena]], and [[Quinn]] stole [[speeder]]s from the workshop to travel to the mountain. They eventually arrived at a [[tree]]line and spoke about a [[Ghost (folklore)|ghost]] known as the [[Screecher (Sith)|Screecher]] that supposedly lived in the [[cave]]. Baython and Daal briefly talked about their lives, and he told Daal that if she had the opportunity to pursue a better life, then she should take it and not look back.

Eventually, the [[Youngling|children]] arrived at the mountain and entered its cave. They reached a large clearing within and encountered the Screecher. The cave crumbled around the children, and Baython led Quinn and Keena out while Daal stayed behind. Alone, the Screecher attacked Daal with a [[Color|red]]-bladed [[Screecher's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. Daal attempted to escape through an opening in the cave wall and reached out to it, using [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|pull]] a large rock down, which knocked down both herself and the Screecher to the cave ground. Regaining her senses, Daal gained sight of the Screecher's true identity&mdash;a real old woman. After a brief struggle over the woman's [[lightsaber]], Daal ignited the [[weapon]] and [[Death|killed]] her.

Daal exited the cave through the opening and reunited with her friends as she reflected on the Screecher being a real person. Baython questioned Daal as to what happened, and she replied that her amulet gave her strength. Suddenly, the Sith Mother's voice emanated from the pendant, telling Daal it was time for them to meet. A [[Sith Mother's ship|starship]] approached the group, landing before them, and a door opened as the Sith Mother stepped out to meet Daal. After she confirmed that the girl had passed the test and killed the Screecher, Daal joined the Sith on the [[Starship|ship]], leaving her friends behind.

==Biography==
===Leaving the workshop===
{{Quote|quotestart=11|''[Daal sighs]'' "I'm going to Screecher's Reach|Daal admits her plan|audio=GoingScreecher-Screecher.mp3|<ref name="Screecher" />}}
[[File:FactoryWorkers-SR.png|thumb|right|300px|Daal (second from top right) worked in an Imperial workhouse.]]
Daal was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]]<ref name="Screecher" /> orphan<ref name="Orphan">{{WebCite|url=https://www.space.com/star-wars-visions-vol-2-creator-interview|text='Star Wars: Visions Vol. 2' director tells a ghost story in the galaxy far, far away (exclusive)|work=[[Wikipedia:Space.com|Space.com]]|archivedate=20250417173747}}</ref> who lived on a [[Unidentified planet (Screecher's Reach)|planet]] in [[the galaxy]]. While living on the [[planet]], she was a laborer tasked with turning wheels on a pipe to direct steam at the [[Factory Workshop]],<ref name="Screecher" /> an Imperial workhouse<ref name="Interview">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-screechers-reach-interview|text=Meet the Visionaries: Paul Young on the Irish Folklore-Inspired Tale of "Screecher's Reach"}}</ref> with challenging conditions.<ref name="StoryGuide1">{{SW|url=screechers-reach-story-gallery|text="Screecher's Reach" Story Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> She sought reprieve from her days there,<ref name="SWCE23">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-visions|text=SWCE 2023: ''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Trailer, Key Art, and Cast Revealed}}</ref> feeling she was destined for greater things.<ref name="5Highlights">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-highlights-screechers-reach|text=''Visions'' Revisited: 5 Highlights from "Screecher's Reach"}}</ref>  At some point she obtained a [[gold]]en [[Daal's medal|medal]] that allowed communication between her and the [[Sith Lord]] known as the [[Sith Mother]],<ref name="Screecher" /> who had recognized that Daal had great potential.<ref name="Focus">{{VisionsFocus|Screecher's Reach}}</ref> The Sith Mother told Daal that she needed to travel to [[Screecher's Reach]], a [[mountain]] rumored to be haunted by a [[Ghost (folklore)|ghost]] known as the [[Screecher (Sith)|Screecher]], and would need to pass a test within her mind. The [[Sith]] had promised Daal a new life if she passed. Daal wore the medal around her neck as a [[necklace]].<ref name="Screecher" />

One [[Standard day|day]], Daal was working when she began turning a wheel too quickly, causing a pipe to burst and steam to erupt from it. Frustrated, she exited the workhouse and ripped her [[glove]]s off of her [[Limb|hands]] and grunted as she tore off her protective [[Clothing|head covering]], revealing her face, red from the steam. She panted and unzipped her protective suit.<ref name="Screecher" /> In the quiet of the outside, she dreamed of escape.<ref name="StoryGuide1" /> Two friends of Daal's&mdash;[[Baython]] and [[Quinn]]&mdash;ran after her as she exited. Baython asked Daal what she was doing, and Daal confessed her plan of leaving the workhouse. Quinn celebrated the idea of leaving and asked where they would be heading, to which Daal answered she was going to Screecher's Reach. Baython noted that the walk to the mountain would take three days, but Daal dismissed his reply.<ref name="Screecher" />

[[File:TheJourney-SR.png|thumb|left|300px|The children traveled to Screecher's Reach on speeders.]]
Quinn suggested the trio steal some [[speeder]]s instead of walking. [[Keena]], another worker from the factory, approached them and asked what was happening, to which Baython smiled and asked if she wanted to see a ghost. With Keena joining the group, Daal led them to the speeders, where Quinn broke the chains securing the vehicles using a handheld device. Keena sat with Daal on her [[Daal's speeder|speeder]] while the others got on theirs and began traveling to the mountain, knocking over two workers controlling small [[walker]]s. They passed over a body of [[water]] and through a field. As they approached a large section of [[tree]]s, the group sped up their speeders.<ref name="Screecher" />

===Confliction===
{{Quote|Daal, you've always wanted something more. And if you ever get the chance to find out what that is, take it. Don't look back.|Baython, encouraging Daal|audio=DontLookBack-Screecher.mp3|<ref name="Screecher" />}}
[[File:DaalFire-SR.png|thumb|right|300px|Daal sits by a fire, thinking about the test ahead.]]
After reaching the treeline, the group sat around a [[Fire|campfire]] as Baython told stories about the haunted [[cave]]<ref name="Screecher" /> and Daal contemplated her destiny,<ref name="5Highlights" /> full of doubt.<ref name="Focus" /> Keena was scared and stated they were just stories, asking Daal for confirmation. Daal held her amulet in her hands and told the others<ref name="Screecher" /> solemnly<ref name="5Highlights" /> that she believed the ghost was not real, only something that one could see in their mind. Upon hearing this, Quinn stated that in his mind he was "kick'n her up the [[List of phrases and slang#A|arse]]!" Keena giggled and repeated what he said before kicking him. Quinn chased her around as they both laughed. Daal briefly smiled, watching them play together.<ref name="Screecher" />

Baython shared a laugh before sitting next to Daal and asking if she was also having fun. He then nudged her arm, saying they were going to see the ghost and that it beat being at the workhouse. Frowning again, Daal pulled her legs back and turned toward the [[star]]lit sky. She asked Baython what he would do if he did not have to go back to the workhouse or the farms and if he would leave if he could. Baython told her that there were worse lives. Daal, however, believed there were also better lives. She pulled her legs to her chest and wrapped her arms around them, burying her face in her knees. Baython looked forward at Quinn&mdash;who was hitting his face against a tree root while Keena laughed&mdash;and asked who would take care of Quinn, referring to him as an "eejit." The comment caused Daal to smile as a [[fruit]] landed on Keena's head, causing the children to laugh and Daal to smile wider and let out a giggle.<ref name="Screecher" />

[[File:StarryDaal-SR.png|thumb|left|300px|Daal ponders a better life away from the factory and farms.]]
Baython put his hand on Daal's shoulder and acknowledged that she wanted more than what the planet had to offer and told her that if she found out what it was, then she should take it without looking back. Baython then stood up and walked to the children. Daal smiled up at him before returning to a frown. She looked at the medal in her hand and put it back around her neck. Keena ran up to Daal, still giggling, invoking a smile back onto Daal. Keena asked if they were taking the speeders. Daal then plucked the piece of fruit off of Keena's head and picked her up to place her on Baython's shoulders, saying that now she would be traveling in style and to hold on tight. The friends then made their way through the trees to Screecher's Reach.<ref name="Screecher" />

===Strength and courage===
{{Dialogue|Baython|Daal! Come on, Daal.|Keena|Why isn't she coming?|Daal|I'll be right behind you. Go!|attr=The friends as the cave began to crumble|audio=ComeOnDaal-Screecher.mp3|src=<ref name="Screecher" />}}
[[File:ABadPlace-SR.png|thumb|right|300px|The children stand at the entrance to Screecher's Reach.]]
Eventually, the group found themselves at the entrance to the cave. Holding her medal, Daal said the name of Screecher's Reach, and Keena commented that it was a bad place while grabbing Daal's [[cape]]. Staring into the cave, Daal closed her [[eye]]s and raised the amulet to her [[mouth]], sighed, and said "I follow your path with strength and courage."<ref name="Screecher" /> Resolute,<ref name="5Highlights" /> she opened her eyes and let the amulet rest around her neck. She then proceeded into the cave ahead of her friends, stating that some bad places were there to test them. While exploring the cave, Daal ventured ahead of the group, leaving them behind as Baython called out to her. She made her way into a section of the cave where a thick layer of fog rested on the ground as Baython and the others caught up with her.<ref name="Screecher" />

Narrowing her eyes, she raised a finger to her mouth and outstretched her hand back, gesturing for them to stop and saying to stay quiet. They heard a faint moaning in the cave as they looked around. Baython, Keena, and Quinn huddled together as a dark figure dashed past the group. As Daal began to back up to her friends and turned around to look at them, Baython's lantern flickered and the disturbed Screecher rose behind Daal, with rocks floating around her as two [[Bone|bony]] hands rose behind her head. The children's eyes widened. Baython struggled to speak, trying to tell Daal that the Screecher was behind her.<ref name="Screecher" />

[[File:ScreecherRises-SR.png|thumb|left|300px|The Screecher rises behind Daal.]]
Daal slowly turned around to see the towering, moaning ghost. The rocks continued rising, and the Screecher let out a loud shriek, throwing the children backwards and knocking them to the ground. The cave's large rocks then began crumbling around them. Daal stood up, looking for the ghost as Baython grabbed Quinn to try to escape the cave. Keena ran to Daal and grabbed her arm to get her to leave with them, but Daal refused and told Keena to retreat with the others. The ghost continued shrieking as Keena ran to the others, and Baython made a final yell to Daal for her to come with them. Daal yelled out for them to leave and that she would be right behind them. Reluctantly, they ran, and rocks covered the exit to the rest of the cave and trapped Daal, filling the cave with dust.<ref name="Screecher" />

===The test===
{{Quote|Please. Give me strength.|Daal, desperately trying to escape and using the Force|audio=GiveMeStrength-Screecher.mp3|<ref name="Screecher" />}}
[[File:Screecher-SR.png|thumb|right|350px|The Screecher readies her saber to cut down Daal.]]
Now in silence, breathing shakily, Daal grabbed on to the amulet.<ref name="Screecher" /> Believing this was a test of will,<ref name="5Highlights" /> she closed her eyes, recited the saying once more, and assured herself that the ghost was only in her mind. Rocks crumbled through the cave as she opened her eyes, staring forward into the darkness. She told the amulet to give her strength. The ghost again revealed itself, gently groaning and reaching a hand out toward the young girl. Daal repeatedly repeated the saying and breathed heavily. A [[Color|crimson red]] blade of a [[Screecher's lightsaber|lightsaber]] ignited, reflecting in Daal's eyes.<ref name="Screecher" />

The Screecher held the [[Sith lightsaber]] above her head horizontally and then swung it at Daal. She screamed and narrowly dodged the attack, falling to the ground as the [[lightsaber]] burnt her arm. A hole began to form at the top of the cave, letting light bleed into the mountain. Realizing the ghost was real, Daal began to run to the exit but tripped on a sharp rock on the ground of the cave. The Screecher then threw her lightsaber and spun it toward the fallen girl&mdash;Daal ducked and the saber struck the rock wall. Standing back up, she looked at the glowing blade before the Screecher pulled it back into the darkness.<ref name="Screecher" />

===Escape===
[[File:GiveMeStrength-SR.png|thumb|left|350px|Daal uses the Force to pull a jagged rock from a ledge in the cave.]]
Daal began to climb the side of the cave, trying to reach the opening at its top to escape. The Screecher approached her, stabbing her lightsaber into the wall and climbing after Daal. Daal frantically climbed but slightly slipped at the end. In desperation, she extended her hand toward a large jagged rock near the bright opening, yet the ghost grabbed her ankle, trying to drag her down. The amulet scraped against the wall as Daal once again raised her hand toward the rock, begging for the medal to give her strength. Suddenly, Daal began to [[Telekinesis|pull]] the rock using [[the Force]]. The rock was slowly dragged over the edge before breaking free and tumbling down the side of the wall alongside Daal, and it struck the Screecher as she shrieked.<ref name="Screecher" />

Daal awakened as the dust settled, upon which she kicked herself back to press against the wall, panting heavily. She looked forward and laid her eyes on the true identity of the "ghost," seeing it was a real<ref name="Screecher" /> [[Gender|woman]].<ref name="ScreecherAD" /> The Screecher gasped and tried to use the Force to pull her lightsaber&mdash;which was knocked out of her hands&mdash;back to her grasp. Instead, Daal leapt for the saber and grabbed it. The woman reached out her hand in an attempt to pry the [[weapon]] from Daal's hands using the Force, shrieking louder, causing the cave to crumble again, and pulling Daal ever closer. However, Daal ignited the lightsaber and swung its blade into the woman, [[Death|killing]] her.<ref name="Screecher" />

===Don't look back===
{{Quote|You said the test was in my mind.''"<br />"''The true test is always in your mind. And you did what you had to do, right?|Daal confronting the Sith Mother|audio=ManipulativeMother-Screecher.mp3|<ref name="Screecher" />}}
[[File:Aftermath-SR.png|thumb|right|300px|Daal reunites with her friends and reflects on what she has done.]]
Staring blankly, Daal emerged from the cave, holding the lightsaber in one hand and her amulet in the other. Her friends spotted her and quickly ran to her. The stunned Daal spoke quietly, reflecting that the Screecher was a real person<ref name="Screecher" /> but expressing her belief that she did what she had to do to survive.<ref name="StoryGuide8">{{SW|url=screechers-reach-story-gallery|text="Screecher's Reach" Story Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 1}} {{C|Slide 8}}</ref> Baython lowered his eyebrows and asked her what she had done, and Daal replied that she was strong. She gazed upon her amulet and spoke to it, saying it gave her strength. The indentations in the medal pulsed with red light as the Sith Mother's voice emitted from it, telling Daal that it was time for them to meet. Baython raised his voice, asking Daal what it was that she was holding.<ref name="Screecher" />

With a rumble, the [[Sith Mother's ship]] emerged from a large glowing light. Daal looked up as the [[starship]] blew her cape and [[hair]] around while landing before the group. The ship's entrance opened, and the Sith Mother walked down its steps. Daal walked up to the tall woman, stating that the Sith had told her that the test was in her mind. The Sith Mother removed her hood and knelt down to the girl, replying that the true test was always in her mind and that she had done what she had to. Holding the deactivated lightsaber in her hands, Daal asked the towering Sith if she passed. She confirmed that Daal had indeed passed and pressed the lightsaber to Daal's chest&mdash;the saber belonged to Daal.<ref name="Screecher" />

[[File:DaalAndMother-Screecher.png|thumb|left|300px|Daal joins the Sith Mother as her new Sith Apprentice.]]
The Sith Mother told Daal to come with her. The girl gasped and turned towards her friends, asking if they would join. She was quickly met with a "no" as the Sith Mother explained that the opportunity was only for Daal as her new [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]]. Keena then asked Daal who the woman was, though Baython grabbed Keena and hugged her close. Daal then told her friends that the Sith Mother promised her a new life, a way off the planet. With tears in her eyes, Daal said she had to try. The Sith's eyes narrowed behind Daal as she told her apprentice to make her choice. Keena began to cry as Quinn and Baython stared at Daal disapprovingly. Daal offered a meek smile and repeated Baython's past words about not looking back when presented with a new opportunity. He closed his eyes and gently nodded his head once. Sobbing, Daal grabbed hold of the Sith Mother's hand and let herself be led into the starship, joining the [[Sith Mother's faction]]. As the ship's entrance shut behind Daal and ascended, she looked back at her friends one more time.<ref name="Screecher" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Come child.''"<br />"''And my friends too?''"<br />"''No. This was always for you, my young apprentice. Only you.|The Sith Mother and Daal, regarding her friends|audio=OnlyYou-Screecher.mp3|<ref name="Screecher" />}}
[[File:DaalCares-SR.png|thumb|right|300px|Daal cared for her friends.]]
Daal was a female human with light skin and red hair. She always wanted there to be more to her life.<ref name="Screecher" /> Daal had no knowledge of what the [[Jedi]] or Sith were.<ref name="Focus" /> When she burst a pipe in the workhouse, she became frustrated and stormed out of the factory, throwing her gloves and helmet off. She was determined to travel to Screecher's Reach, even if it meant a three-day walk. When Baython attempted to reassure Daal that there were worse lives, she still understood that there were also better lives. Daal cared deeply for her friends, and seeing Quinn and Keena playing together around the campfire made her happy. She spoke to her medal when scared or nervous, telling it to give her strength.<ref name="Screecher" />

Daal was scared of the Screecher, but when they were pinned beneath a boulder, Daal retrieved the woman's lightsaber and killed her with it.<ref name="Screecher" /> When leaving the cave, she was in a daze.<ref name="ScreecherAD" /> After reuniting with her friends, she reflected on the fact the Screecher was a real person, not a ghost, and that her amulet gave her strength to kill her. As Daal was leaving her world with the Sith Mother, she asked if her friends could join her, and when Daal was prompted to make her choice, she sobbed and decided to leave her friends behind in pursuit of a better life. However, Daal looked back at her friends as she departed.<ref name="Screecher" />

==Powers and abilities==
Daal turned wheels on pipes at the workshop and was capable of piloting a speeder stolen from the workshop. She was able to use telekinesis through the Force to pull a large rock from a ledge in Screecher's Reach.<ref name="Screecher" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|So, did I pass?''"<br />"''You passed. That's yours now.|Daal and the Sith Mother, regarding the Screecher and her blade|audio=YouPassed-Screecher.mp3|<ref name="Screecher" />}}
[[File:ScreecherSaber-Screecher.png|thumb|left|300px|After killing the Screecher, Daal claimed her saber.]]
As a worker, Daal donned a black protective suit with a zipper down the middle. The suit was worn with [[boot]]s, gloves, and a helmet with a narrow slit for her eyes. Outside the workshop, Daal dressed in a black long-sleeved shirt with waist-high tan pants and a purple cape. Daal also wore her golden pendant around her neck to communicate with the Sith Mother. After killing the Screecher with her own crimson-bladed lightsaber, Daal kept it as her own. The saber had a smooth white handle with a bulbed bottom, and the emitter was surrounded by two petal-like protrusions. One side had an activation switch while the other had a red streak running down from the petals.<ref name="Screecher" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Development===
{{Quote|I never would've imagined I'd get to do something like this. Getting to play this cool, strong character and being part of a ''Star Wars'' thing. That's so cool.|Eva Whittaker, on her role as Daal|<ref name="Focus" />}}
[[File:MothersEmbrace-RedondoArt.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Almu Redondo's illustration for director Paul Young, titled "Three ages of Sith"]]
Daal appeared in "[[Screecher's Reach (short film)|Screecher's Reach]]," a non-[[canon]] short film directed by [[Paul Young]] in the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. Produced by<ref name="Screecher" /> the [[Wikipedia:Ireland|Ireland]]-based<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Release Date, Studios Revealed}}</ref> studio [[Cartoon Saloon]], the animated short was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Screecher" /> on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="Release" /> Prior to Daal's appearance in the short film, she was revealed in the official trailer for ''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 shown at the ''Visions'' panel at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]] on [[April 10]], 2023.<ref name="Celebration">{{SWYouTube|vf7D4MWJofM|Star Wars Celebration LIVE! 2023 - DAY 4}}</ref>

[[Eva Whittaker]] voiced Daal. In an interview, Whittaker revealed that she never thought she could play a ''[[Star Wars]]'' character such as Daal, whom she considered to be strong and cool. During the scene at the camp site, Daal was framed in a circle of light<ref name="Focus" /> that represented friendship and bonding,<ref name="RedondoWebsite">{{WebCite|url=https://www.almuredondo.com/starwarsvisions|text=Art Direction for Cartoon Saloon's Star Wars "Screecher's Reach" short film|work=[[Almu Redondo]]'s [https://www.almuredondo.com official website]|archivedate=20250913122705}}</ref> but when doubting and asking Baython if he would leave, she is framed out of the light. In the final scene, the same symbology was used with the circle of light that is closing on Daal and cutting off the attachment she had with her friends<ref name="Focus" /> and welcoming her to her new family.<ref name="RedondoWebsite" /> Young believed that Daal possibly began to regret her decision as the Sith Mother's ship's doors closed, unsure if it was the right thing to do.<ref name="Focus" />

Daal's interaction with the Screecher in the cave was based on a scene from the<ref name="Interview" /> [[1980]]<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> film {{Film|V}}<ref name="Interview" /> in which the Jedi [[Luke Skywalker]] fought a vision of the Sith Lord [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in the [[Cave of Evil]],<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> except the threat in Screecher's Reach is physical. Daal's adventure with her friends was tied to the writers' own childhoods.<ref name="Interview" /> Young thought of his own childhood when writing Daal's character, having grown up in a small town and reflecting that he was fortunate to go to an art college&mdash;he was leaving his friends and did not want to gloat about it to them.<ref name="Focus" /> [[Lucasfilm]] executive producer [[James Waugh]] referred to Daal's journey as "a subverted Luke Skywalker story."<ref name="Insider231">{{InsiderCite|231|Vision Quest}}</ref>

===Art and conceptualization===
[[File:InitialDaal-PirovanoConcept.jpg|thumb|left|300px|An initial concept of Daal by Federico Pirovano]]
Concept artist [[Federico Pirovano]] was responsible for all character designs in the short as well as defining their visual style from initial concepts into the final delivery. An early design of Daal gave her a larger build, and she wore thick clothes and a scarf. She also held a scrawny blue creature with four legs on a leash. Pirovano created multiple pieces of concept art refining Daal's appearance.<ref name="PirovanoWebsite">{{WebCite|url=https://federicopirovano.com/star-wars-visions-season-2|text=Star Wars Visions Season 2|work=[[Federico Pirovano]]'s [https://federicopirovano.com official website]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/3AoTY}}</ref> Art director<ref name="Screecher" /> [[Almu Redondo]] and Pirovano worked together to create a lineup sheet featuring the main characters of the short, including Daal.<ref name="Concept4">{{SW|url=screechers-reach-concept-art-gallery|text="Screecher's Reach" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> Pirovano also worked with [[Maria Madelaire]] to create concept art of Daal and her medal.<ref name="Concept3">{{SW|url=screechers-reach-concept-art-gallery|text="Screecher's Reach" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref>

[[Leo Weiss]] created concept art pieces of scenes wherein Daal and the other kids walked through the Factory Workshop that were not included in the final film. Weiss also created multiple storyboards for the short's major scenes and various environmental concepts of the planet and cave that included Daal.<ref name="WeissWebsite">{{WebCite|url=https://leo-weiss.com/screechers-reach|text=Screecher's Reach|work=[[Leo Weiss]]' [https://leo-weiss.com official website]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/g1WsJ}}</ref> Redondo also made various art pieces of scenes from the short and color scripts to establish the final tone and design.<ref name="RedondoWebsite" /> Redondo, Weiss, and Pirovano all made concept art of Daal and the children on their speeders.<ref name="Concept5">{{SW|url=screechers-reach-concept-art-gallery|text="Screecher's Reach" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> Redondo additionally painted a piece featuring Daal, titled "Three ages of Sith", for Paul Young on the last day of production, sharing it online via [[Wikipedia:Instagram|Instagram]] on [[January 15]], [[2024]] to celebrate "Screecher's Reach" winning an [[Wikipedia:Emmy Awards|Emmy Award]]<ref name="RedondoInstagram">{{Instagram|username=almu_redondo|url=p/C2HNwJrxnb-|name=[[Almu Redondo]]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/vCEHq}}</ref> for production design.<ref name="Emmy">{{WebCite|url=https://www.televisionacademy.com/features/news/awards-news/75th-juried-winners-231206|text=Juried Winners for 75th Emmy Awards Announced|date=2023-12-06|work=[[Wikipedia:Academy of Television Arts & Sciences|Television Academy]]|archivedate=20250320185852}}</ref>
