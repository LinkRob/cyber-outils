'''Deblindrix IV''',<ref name="Premonition" /> also know simply as '''Deblindrix''',<ref name="Edge of Balance Vol 4">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 4|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 4]]</ref> was a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] owned by the [[Nihil]] [[scientist]] [[Niv Drendow Apruk]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. During the [[mission to Banchii]], Apruk was instructed Deblindrix IV to [[Hunting|hunt]] the [[Jedi]], unsuccessfully chasing after [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] and the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Nima Allets]] and [[Viv'Nia Nia'Viv]].<ref name="Premonition" />
