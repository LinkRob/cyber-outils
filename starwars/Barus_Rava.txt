'''Barus Rava''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] who served as an [[Admiral/Legends|admiral]] in the [[Republic Navy/Legends|Republic Navy]] of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]]. Around [[3625 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TOR_updates|7.0}}</ref> Rava led a [[Fleet/Legends|fleet]] to assist the Republic efforts during the [[Invasion of Manaan]] against the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Sith Empire]].

==Biography==
Rava was born into a military family and was granted a proper education and training to prepare him for his long career. In his [[Platoon/Legends|platoon's]] first deployment, he and his unit were captured by the forces of the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Sith Empire]] due to an error of [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] intel.<ref name="CX">{{TORcite|exp=lots|type=codex|Admiral Barus Rava}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
During the 14 [[Standard month/Legends|months]] of his imprisonment, he was left scarred and his optimistic view for negotiating with the Republic's opponents was shattered. Since his release, he sought to eradicate the Empire's presence from [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]], which drove him into conflict with other Republic leaders. His controversial ways made him unpopular within the Republic ranks, but he was greatly sought after when decisive action was required.<ref name="CX" />
