'''Ferb Fletcher''', formerly known briefly as '''Darth Ferb''', was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who lived on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. He was a [[moisture farmer]] alongside his stepbrother [[Phineas Flynn]].<ref name="Phineas" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Biography==
===Early life===
Ferb Fletcher met his stepmother [[Linda Flynn-Fletcher]] and stepbrother [[Phineas Flynn]] when his father [[Lawrence Fletcher]] got married.<ref name="Phineas" />

===Summer on Tatooine===
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{PnFDate|1}}</ref> Ferb Fletcher along with his stepbrother were enjoying their summer on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Phineas" />
