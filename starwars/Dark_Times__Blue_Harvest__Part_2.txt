'''''Dark Times: Blue Harvest'', Part 2''' is the second of the two [[Dark Horse Comics]] special mini-issues of ''[[Star Wars: Dark Times]]'', which serve as a lead-up to the [[Star Wars: Dark Times: Blue Harvest|''Dark Times: Blue Harvest'']] story arc. It was published in the February 2009 release of Myspace Dark Horse Presents.
