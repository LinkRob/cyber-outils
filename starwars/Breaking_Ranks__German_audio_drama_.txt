"'''Breaking Ranks'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]]: "'''Ezra undercover'''") is an episode of the [[German Rebels audio dramas]] adapting the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Breaking Ranks]]". It was released on [[May 8]], [[2015]] by [[Universal Music Group]] as part of the album [[Star Wars Rebels Episode 2 - Rise of the Old Masters / Breaking Ranks]]. The episode was written by [[Gabriele Bingenheimer]], directed by [[Marian Szymczyk]] and includes narration by [[Hanns Jörg Krumpholz]].<ref name="Kiddinx" />
