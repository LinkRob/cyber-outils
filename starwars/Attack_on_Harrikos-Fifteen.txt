The '''attack on Harrikos Fifteen''' was an engagement between [[New Republic Intelligence]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] in the [[Harrikos system]] at the [[Harrikos-Fifteen Research Station]]. New Republic Intelligence [[Officer]] [[Caern Adan]] believed that the [[Attack on the Hellion's Dare|attack]] on the ''[[Hellion's Dare]]'' was linked to an elite Imperial fighter wing, the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], also known as Shadow Wing. To find where the [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] was in the [[Oridol Cluster|Oridal Cluster]], Adan sought to find a forward base to locate and extract the frigate. The Republic [[pilot]]s initially tried to trick the Imperials, but were forced to open fire and board the research station.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>

==Prelude==
[[New Republic Intelligence]] [[Officer]] [[Caern Adan]] believed that the recent disappearance of the ''[[Hellion's Dare]]'' was a cause of [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing|Shadow Wing]]. The [[New Republic]] needed a station from which to conduct search and extraction of the frigate in the [[Oridol Cluster]]. Adan believed that seizing the [[Harrikos-Fifteen Research Station]] was a perfect opportunity for this, as it would allow them to utilize the science station's facilities.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

==The attack==
The New Republic's plan involved deceiving the station's hangar crew into allowing [[Yrica Quell]] to land with her [[X-wing starfighter]] and secure it. Then, the marines on the ''[[Buried Treasure]]'' would board the station and seize control of it. As the squadron emerged from hyperspace, Quell asked for permission to land using [[Galactic Imperial]] access codes, claiming she had stolen the X-wing after her squadron had been destroyed. The request was denied by an automated machine, and [[Nath Tensent]] improvised by pretending to shoot her down as a hostage. A crew member came on the radio and again denied her request to land, but the station's laser turrets fired at Tensent instead of her. Quell flew down onto the landing strip and opened fire with her [[laser cannon]]s. Simultaneously, Tensent made several bombing runs on the station. The ''Buried Treasure'' landed and secured the station after two hours.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

==Aftermath==
Despite their victory, New Republic Intelligence was not able to locate the ''Hellion's Dare'' due to it being destroyed by Shadow Wing. However, two pilots, [[Chass na Chadic]] and [[Wyl Lark]], survived the defeat and were able to escape to the station. There, they notified the Republic Intelligence of their story. Both pilots would eventually join the new [[Alphabet Squadron]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
