'''Crik''',<ref name="Han Chewie 10">[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 10|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 10]]</ref> '''crikk''' or '''crikking''' was a vulgar [[List of phrases and slang|expletive]]. It was once used by a [[Unidentified Besalisk (Jedha)|Besalisk]] on [[Jedha]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="THR1" /> It was still used during the [[Imperial Era]], once said by [[Scout trooper]] [[Dooze]] to his [[Imperial officer|commanding officer]] [[Altadan Igar]].<ref name="TMWCLS">{{StoryCite|story=The Man Who Captured Luke Skywalker|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> As [[Phaedra]] maneuvered the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' through a tumultuous dogfight in the skies, her limited [[pilot]]ing experience became evident when she exclaimed, "Crik... I am in deep [[poodoo]] here," indicating her awareness of the trouble she was in.<ref name="Han Chewie 10" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Crik(k)"<ref name="THR1" /><ref name="Han Chewie 10" /> and its variant "crikking"<ref name="THR1" /> are phonetically similar to the real-world noun "frick"<ref name="Frick">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dictionary.com/browse/frick|text=frick|quote=a euphemism for the word ''f*ck''.|work=[[Wikipedia:Dictionary.com|''Dictionary.com'']]|archivedate=20250105164244}}</ref> and the corresponding intensifier "fricking,"<ref name="Fricking">{{WebCite|url=https://www.thefreedictionary.com/fricking|text=fricking|quote=''adj slang'' (intensifier): ''surrounded by fricking idiots.'' [C20: euphemism for f*cking]|work=[[Wikipedia:The Free Dictionary|''The Free Dictionary'']]|archivedate=20250105165300}}</ref> which are euphemisms for the swear words "f*ck"<ref name="Frick" /> and "f*cking."<ref name="Fricking" />
