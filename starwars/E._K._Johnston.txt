'''Emily Kate Johnston'''<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|username=ek_johnston|name=Emily Kate Johnston|archivedate=20130829220234}}</ref> (born [[May 19]], [[1984]]) is an author who has written multiple young-adult novels in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]]. Her novels notably feature many LGBTQ+ characters.

==Biography==
Johnston is best known as the author of the young-adult novel [[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']], which was released on [[October 11]], [[2016]].<ref name="SWC Ahsoka">{{SW|url=news/swce-2016-ahsokas-untold-tales-panel-liveblog|text=SWCE 2016: Ahsoka's Untold Tales Panel Liveblog}}</ref> She later wrote the story "[[By Whatever Sun]]" for the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]'', which focuses on the character [[Miara Larte]] created by Johnston for ''Ahsoka'', showing the character's actions during the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']].

Johnston later wrote the novels in the ''[[Star Wars: Queen's Series]]'', released between [[2019]] and [[2022]], focusing on [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] as [[senator]] and [[Queen]] of [[Naboo]] and [[Sabé]] and the other [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens]].<ref name="Queen's Hope">{{SW|url=news/queens-hope-exclusive-reveal|text=In the New Book ''Queen's Hope'', Padmé Grapples with the Clone Wars &ndash; Exclusive Reveal}}</ref>

She also wrote the [[2023]] novel ''[[Crimson Climb]]'', which focuses on the character [[Qi'ra]] from the film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Crimson Climb">{{SW|url=news/e-k-johnston-crimson-climb|text=5 Behind-the-Pages Secrets of ''Star Wars: Crimson Climb''}}</ref>

Johnston has stated she keeps ''[[Star Wars]]'' close to her soul. As such, she has stated ''Star Wars'' is a major influence on her writing and that influence can be seen in her works she cares deeply about.<ref name="EK Soul">{{Bluesky|username=ekjohnston.bsky.social|url=post/3lveq4twifk27|name=[[E. K. Johnston]]|date=2025-08-01|quote=The Aether Saga is where I keep my soul, so that's why there's a lot of Star Wars in it. But basically: the marketing budget is tiny, and marketing is how you get attention. So I *deeply* appreciate it every time one of you says something.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/4kK4e|archivefile=File:EKJohnston-StarWarsSoul-Bluesky.png}} {{C|In response to: "Just finished Sky on fire by @ekjohnston.bsky.social. It's the second book in the series and I loved it. I don't know why this series isn't getting more attention. I got into reading @ekjohnston.bsky.social from her Star Wars books and you can see the Star Wars influence here"}}</ref>

In [[2025]], fellow author, [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]] teased that he consulted with Johnston for one of the characters in his novel ''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]''.<ref name="Chen 11">{{Bluesky|mikechenwriter.bsky.social|post/3lusyh2rivs2p|[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|date=2025-07-25|quote=I think I can tease that I pulled supporting cast from all areas of SW media. And several were pie-in-the-sky "no way they'll let me do this" requests. Different fans are going to be VERY happy for different reasons. (I consulted @ekjohnston.bsky.social for one, make of that what you will!)|archivefile=File:MikeChen-LowRedMoonThread-Bluesky.png}}</ref>
