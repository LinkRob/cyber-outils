A '''Corruption Node''' was a source of [[Corruption (virus)|corruption]], a [[Computer virus|virus]] that plagued the [[Observatory (The Island)|Observatory]], found within the [[Observatory HoloSimulator]] connected to the Observatory. Destroying one would cleanse the nearby corruption, helping limit the virus' spread.<ref name="Castaways">''[[LEGO Star Wars: Castaways]]''</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Corruption Nodes first appeared and were identified in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Castaways]]'',<ref name="Castaways" /> released on [[November 19]], [[2021]]. Prior to the game's release, they were first pictured in the game's article<ref name="GamePage">{{SW|url=games-apps/lego-star-wars-castaways|text=LEGO Star Wars: Castaways}}</ref> and the news post reveal article of the game,<ref name="News">{{SW|url=news/lego-star-wars-castaways|text=''LEGO Star Wars: Castaways'' Coming to Apple Arcade &ndash; Exclusive Reveal}}</ref> both posted on [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 19]] of that year.<ref name="GamePage" /><ref name="News" />
