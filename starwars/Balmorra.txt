'''Balmorra''' was a rocky wasteland [[factory world]] located in the [[Colonies]]' [[Balmorra system]]. It joined the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] during the [[Clone Wars]] and housed [[droid]] [[Factory|factories]] for the faction. The [[Galactic Empire]] used the factory sites after the war [[Mission to Mustafar|ended]], and an [[Balmorra Imperial outpost|Imperial facility]] there held [[clone trooper]]s [[prisoner]], siphoning them off to [[Mount Tantiss|another facility]]. When [[transport]]ed off Balmorra around [[18 BBY]], the [[Cloning|clone]] prisoner [[Howzer]] and his two remaining [[trooper]]s were [[Attack on the prisoner transport ship|rescued]] by a [[Clone Underground|strike team]] of rogue clones.

Later on, the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] went to Balmorra with the [[Forger (profession)|forger]] [[Bar the Rotter]] to recruit the [[Gender|woman]] known as the [[Marquess]]. In [[1 BBY]], a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Balmorra relay|relay]] at Balmorra intercepted a communication mentioning top secret Imperial projects, which the rebels were able to partially extract from the relay before a [[hyperstorm]] arrived. While the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] led a [[Operation Ringbreaker|campaign]] that targeted the [[Kuat Drive Yards]] in [[3 ABY]], Alliance [[General]] [[Crix Madine]] considered Balmorra a possible target should the 61st be successful.

==Description==
{{Quote|Ah, Balmorra. It is an ugly place coated in a sticky film.|Hondo Ohnaka, ''Smuggler's Guide''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
[[File:BalmorraLandscape-TBBs2e14.png|thumb|left|350px|Balmorra featured a barren mountainous landscape.]]
Balmorra was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Balmorra DB" /> located in the [[Balmorra system]]<ref name="Rookies" /> within an intersecting portion of the [[Colonies]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and [[the Slice]] regions.<ref name="Slice" /> Situated in grid square M-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Coords" /> it was very close to the [[Kuat system|Kuat]] and [[Neimoidia system]]s, the three [[star system]]s being connected by a [[Fedalle–Kuat hyperlane|hyperspace route]] to the [[Fedalle system]].<ref name="CLNE" />

Balmorra was [[orbit]]ed by a [[Balmorra's moon|moon]]. Its surface was rocky and barren, with [[mountain]]s and gorges extending across the landscape.<ref name="Tipping Point" /> The world's Type I [[atmosphere]]<ref name="Atmosphere" />&mdash;which was breathable to [[human]]s<ref name="Tipping Point" /> and other [[alien]] [[species]]<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />&mdash;was filled with [[cloud]]s, and [[Limb|winged]] creatures inhabited the world.<ref name="Tipping Point" /> The planet shared its name with [[Balmorran Arms]], a [[droid]] manufacturer;<ref name="Disciples of Harmony">''[[Disciples of Harmony]]''</ref> [[Balmorran Heavyworks]], a [[starship]] manufacturer that notably produced [[Freighter|hauler]] models;<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> and the [[Balmorra Run]], a [[smuggler]] route through the [[Kaliida Nebula]].<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence">{{TCW|Shadow of Malevolence|Shadow of ''Malevolence''}}</ref>

==History==
===Uncertain allegiances===
During the [[Republic Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], Balmorra was an<ref name="HC 4" /> isolated<ref name="Balmorra DB" /> [[factory world]] with a strong sense of independence.<ref name="HC 4" /> With a strong reputation for manufacturing,<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> it was slow to join the Republic despite its proximity to the [[Core Worlds]],<ref name="HC 4" /> where the Republic [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]] was located.<ref name="Timelines" /> During the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]],<ref name="HC 4" /> which began in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Balmorra left the Republic and joined the Separatists,<ref name="HC 4" /> which installed droid [[Factory|factories]] on the planet.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> However, the Separatists were ultimately [[Mission to Mustafar|defeated]] in [[19 BBY]], ending the war.<ref name="Timelines" />

===Under the Empire's leash===
{{Quote|Doctor&mdash;one of our transport ships was attacked departing Balmorra. The prisoners aboard escaped.|Medical officer [[Scalder]], to Royce Hemlock|<ref name="Tipping Point" />}}
The Republic [[Proclamation of the New Order|transitioned]] into the [[Galactic Empire]] at the end of the Clone Wars,<ref name="Timelines" /> with the latter government making use of Balmorra's factory sites<ref name="Balmorra DB" /> for both [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] production<ref name="HC 4" /> and [[prison]] camps.<ref name="Balmorra DB" /> An isolated [[Balmorra Imperial outpost|Imperial outpost]] on Balmorra held [[clone trooper]]s<ref name="Tipping Point SG1">{{SW|url=tipping-point-story-gallery|text="Tipping Point" Story Gallery &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> that had questioned or disobeyed orders. Many were eventually transported offworld to [[Mount Tantiss]],<ref name="Tipping Point" /> an Imperial facility on the planet [[Wayland]],<ref name="Kamino Lost">{{TBB|Kamino Lost}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> where they were subjected to experiments by [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]] of the Empire's [[Advanced Science Division]].<ref name="Plan 99">{{TBB|Plan 99 (episode)}}</ref> Among the detained [[Cloning|clones]] was [[Captain]] [[Howzer]] and eight of [[Howzer's squad|his men]],<ref name="Tipping Point" /> who had defied orders while posted on the planet [[Ryloth]]<ref name="Rescue on Ryloth">{{TBB|Rescue on Ryloth (episode)}}</ref> that [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="Timelines" />

[[File:BalmorraImperialBase-TBBs2e14.png|thumb|right|350px|Howzer and his men being escorted through the base's hangar]]
At some point no earlier than [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' episode "[[Tipping Point]]" take place not long after those of "[[Truth and Consequences]]," which ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' dates to [[18 BBY]].</ref> Howzer and his remaining two [[trooper]]s were taken aboard an [[Unidentified Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser (Balmorra)|Imperial ''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by Captain [[Pearce]] to be transported to Mount Tantiss. As it left Balmorra the [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] was [[Attack on the prisoner transport ship|attacked]] and boarded by a [[Clone Underground|strike team]] of renegade clones. The team rescued Howzer and his troopers and extracted some of the [[prisoner]] [[transport]]'s data before escaping as a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] that had arrived out of [[hyperspace]] attempted to intercept.<ref name="Tipping Point" />

By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="10 BBY">The entry in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' where Balmorra is depicted on a [[Star map|map]] as an outlaw garage is placed [[Time|chronologically]] prior to an entry written by [[Tobias Beckett]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates Beckett's [[Showdown on Savareen|death]] to [[10 BBY]], therefore his entry in the guide and those chronologically before it must all be set at some point by that date.</ref> former [[Pirate Queen|pirate queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] went to Balmorra and made contact with the underworld there.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> She marked the world as the location of an outlaw garage on a [[Star map|map]] that she included in the [[book]] that later became known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''. The ''Smuggler's Guide'' changed hands multiple times before it fell into the possession of the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]], who had the [[Forger (profession)|forger]] [[Bar the Rotter]] create three counterfeit copies to auction off. The pair traveled to Balmorra to meet with the [[Gender|woman]] known as the [[Marquess]], whom he wanted to muster buyers for the auction via the [[Undervine]] network. Ohnaka found the Marquess in Balmorra's [[Swellbottom market]], with the woman not being happy to see him but ultimately agreeing to join him after negotiating a thirty percent cut of the profits. Subsequently, the pair left to host the auctions elsewhere.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

===Rebel operations===
{{Quote|It's only headers. The deep copy is behind another layer still, and I think it might actually be unrecoverable no matter what we do because of the hyperstorms that were going on when we cut this packet out of the Balmorra relay.|Tenzigo Weems, in a report addressed to Davits Draven|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
Between [[2 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]],<ref name="2-1 BBY">In ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', the report mentioning the [[Balmorra relay]] is set between the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Desertion|defection]] of [[Bodhi Rook]], which are respectively dated to [[2 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] maintained a [[Balmorra relay|relay]] around Balmorra for surveillance purposes. That year, the relay picked up communications that discussed six secret Imperial projects: [[Black-Saber]], [[Cluster-Prism]], [[Ion Ring]], [[Pax Aurora]], [[Stellar Sphere]], and [[Project War-Mantle|War Mantle]]. The rebels managed to cut some of the data package out of the relay but were interrupted when a [[hyperstorm]] came in. Rebel [[Private]] [[Tenzigo Weems]] then worked on decrypting the findings and sent what he could decrypt in a report for [[General]] [[Davits Draven]], who further relayed it to the Alliance [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

[[File:Xa-Fel-RF.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Balmorra was a major industrial center for the Galactic Empire.]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the Rebel Alliance, Balmorra was among the former's major industrial sites.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] attacked a number of lightly defended Imperial logistical hubs to cause redeployments from the [[Kuat Drive Yards]], which the [[company]] intended to attack and sabotage.<ref name="BTC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> The campaign, dubbed [[Operation Ringbreaker]], was vetted from afar by the [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Alliance Intelligence]], and General [[Crix Madine]] produced a [[map]] denoting other major Imperial [[shipyard]]s, drydocks, and key [[mining]] centers that the Alliance could possibly target if the 61st was successful in its campaign.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

Balmorra was mentioned on the map with the same tag as<ref name="Rebel Files" /> the planet<ref name="BTC" /> [[Kuat]] and its [[Navy|naval]] [[Kuat shipyards|shipyards]], the tag being designated as a high-defended, high-priority target. The map was included in a document sent by Madine to Mothma. The document, as well as the report about the Balmorra relay findings, were eventually acquired by the [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]], who included it in a non-electronic archive called ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> The 61st ultimately abandoned its campaign before it reached Kuat.<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Balmorra-TippingPoint-concept.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Concept art for Balmorra in "Tipping Point" by Molly Denmark]]
Balmorra was introduced into the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] when it was mentioned in the [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 4|fourth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s magazine series ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'',<ref name="HC 4" /> first published on [[September 30]], [[2014]]<ref name="France">{{HCdatesFR|4}}</ref> and written by [[Iain Lowson]].<ref name="HC-Credits">{{EmailCite|email=File:HCSV-Email-2025-07-26.png|author=[[Iain Lowson]]|date=2025-07-26}}</ref> The world later appeared in "[[Tipping Point]],"<ref name="Tipping Point" /> the fourteenth episode of the [[The Bad Batch Season 2|second season]] of the [[Disney+]] animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''. The episode aired on [[March 22]], [[2023]].<ref name="BB S2">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-bad-batch-season-2-trailer-key-art|text=Clone Force 99 Is Back in New ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 2 Trailer}}</ref> Concept art for Balmorra in that episode was drawn by artist [[Molly Denmark]].<ref name="Concept Art Gallery S7">{{SW|url=tipping-point-concept-art-gallery|text="Tipping Point" Concept Art Gallery &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|Slide 7}}</ref> Balmorra originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, first appearing in the [[Dark Empire II 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Dark Empire II]]'', written by [[Tom Veitch]], illustrated by [[Cam Kennedy]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="DE21">[[Dark Empire II 1|''Dark Empire II'' 1]]</ref> on [[December 20]], [[1994]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54063/star_wars_dark_empire_ii_1994_1|text=Star Wars: Dark Empire II (1994) #1}}</ref>
