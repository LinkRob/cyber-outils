'''Erkeere Ridge''' was a thin, jagged [[mountain]] range located on the [[planet]] [[Auratera]]. It ran down the middle of Auratera's largest continent, meandering uninterrupted from the planet's north to south pole and resembled a maw of teeth from space. The tallest mountain in the range was [[Dran's Peak]], which was used as a ritualistic site by the [[Sith Empire]] visiting the nearby [[vergence]] at [[Acablas]].<ref name="NoP" />
