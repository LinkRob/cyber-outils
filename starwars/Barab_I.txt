'''Barab I''',<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> also known simply as '''Barab''',<ref name="TFABG" /> was an [[Radiation|irradiated]] [[planet]]<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> in the [[Barab system]] in the [[Albanin sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was located along the [[Triellus Trade Route]] between [[Teth]] and [[Dubrava system|Dubrava]], in grid square U-12<ref name="TFABG" /> on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Rebel Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref> and was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Barabel]] [[species]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
