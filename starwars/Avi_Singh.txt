'''Avi Singh''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[senator]] who served in the [[Separatist Senate|Senate]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and resided on its [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Raxus Secundus]] after the [[Mission to Mustafar|collapse]] of the Separatist state before fleeing into hiding. Originally a senator in the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Galactic Senate|Senate]], his political skills ensured that the capital of the Confederacy of Independent Systems and its Senate would be located on Raxus Secundus.

==Biography==
===Early life===
{{Quote|The Separatist Senate may not exist any longer, but our desire for true independence has not faded|Avi Singh|<ref name="Infiltration">{{TBB|Infiltration (episode)}}</ref>}}
During the [[Republic Era]], Avi Singh was a [[senator]] on the [[Galactic Senate]] and an outspoken advocate of self-determination from the [[Galactic Republic]]. As a political powerhouse based on Raxus, Singh and his colleagues turned [[Raxus Secundus]] into the capital of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Dawn" /> In reward for his years of service, Senator Singh was gifted an [[Avi Singh's vase|antique vase]]. However, though his [[RA-7 protocol droid]] [[GS-8]] claimed the vase was a possession that was prized by its owner, Singh himself later admitted to have never liked the vase.<ref name="Common Ground" />

===Under new authority===
After the [[Mission to Mustafar|collapse]] of the Confederacy,<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] began its [[Occupation of Raxus Secundus|occupation of his planet]], [[Captain]] [[Bragg]] prefaced a public announcement by telling the citizens that the occupation was for their benefit as long as they complied and gave the Empire their allegiance. Senator Singh told his droid, GS-8, that he could not go through with endorsing the occupation and would not become an Imperial pawn. When it was his turn to speak and endorse the Imperial presence, he instead spoke out, calling the occupation unjust and saying that he and the people would not bow to their acts of intimidation. He was dragged away as the crowd cheered for his actions, but the masses of citizens were soon cleared out by Imperial [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer|walkers]] and Singh was [[Prison|imprisoned]]. GS-8 contacted [[Ciddarin Scaleback|Cid]], who sent [[Clone Force 99]] to extract Singh. Meanwhile, Bragg prepared to torture Singh to comply. After being rescued, he helped the group escape by directing [[Tech]] towards a secret passage through which they escaped the pursuing [[clone trooper]]s and retreated to the [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Marauder'']].<ref name="Common Ground" />

[[File:ChuchiSingh-TBB-Infiltration.png|thumb|left|275px|Singh meeting with Senator Chuchi]]
Singh would go into hiding following the occupation but would later emerge and visit the planet [[Pantora]] following an invitation from Senator [[Riyo Chuchi]]. Accompanied by GS-8 as well as members of the [[Clone Underground]], Singh and Chuchi had a [[Riyo Chuchi and Avi Singh's secret meeting|secret meeting]] in a [[Pantora tea parlor|tea parlor]] discussed their growing discontent of the Empire from formerly Republic worlds as well as formerly Separatist ones. As they sat and drank [[tea]], they were attacked by [[Clone X trooper|clone assassin]] [[CX-1]], who attempted to kill them both. Thanks to the efforts of Captain Rex, the attempt was unsuccessful. Following their meeting, senator Singh and Chuchi assumed low profiles in response to the attack. After encrypting a [[Holopuck|data puck]] from the assassin, the clones learned that Singh was among one of targets on the list.<ref name="Infiltration" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|This is what it's come to? I must be a puppet for the Empire?|Avi Singh|<ref name="Common Ground" />}}
Avi Singh was a human male with white hair, light brown eyes and dark skin. The senator was completely bald and had mutton chops.<ref name="Common Ground" />

A good-natured person, Senator Avi Singh acted in the best interests of his people. Despite warnings from his droid GS-8, Singh refused to endorse the Imperial occupation of Raxus and instead spoke out against it. While these actions resulted in him being arrested and tortured to comply, his disposition about the occupation went unchanged. After being rescued by the Bad Batch, Singh realized he could not abandon his people to the Empire. With encouragement from Echo, the Senator decided to make the decision and go into hiding. His passion for bringing true independence to his people and Raxus, however, did not dissipate.{{Fact}}
