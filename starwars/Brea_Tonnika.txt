'''Brea Tonnika'''<ref name="Lightsaber Lost">{{TCW|Lightsaber Lost}}</ref> was a [[Gender|woman]] who was one of the [[Tonnika sisters]], alongside her sister [[Senni Tonnika|Senni]].<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

==Biography==
By the time of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" /> Brea was wanted for murder, having killed a Guard while trying to break an associate out of prison,<ref name="You Owe Me a Ride">{{StoryCite|story=You Owe Me a Ride|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> and her mugshot was one of several pulled up by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Chief Librarian]] [[Jocasta Nu]] while they searched the [[Jedi Archives]] databases to find clues to the identity of the [[thief]] who had stolen [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|Tano's]] [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" />

Brea and Senni eventually came to meet [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and trashed their apartment on [[Coruscant]] to be on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. They also met [[Han Solo]]; however, he later betrayed and humiliated the sisters.<ref name="You Owe Me a Ride" />

By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]], Tonnika and her sister were living on [[Tatooine]], but when [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] put a bounty on Solo, they go to [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]], a [[Cantina|drinking establishment]] located in [[Mos Eisley]]. She and her sister witnessed [[hermit]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] defending his [[Padawan|apprentice]] named [[Luke Skywalker]] and fight off two wanted crooks, wounding [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]] and [[Dismemberment|dismembering]] [[Ponda Baba]]'s [[Limb|right arm]] before they decided not to collect Jabba's bounty, but to steal the ''Millennium Falcon'' so they could leave the planet, overhearing the ship was in [[Docking Bay 94]]. They go there, only to have to hide quickly when they found out that Jabba was there as well. They snuck out of the docking bay, and decided to steal another ship.<ref name="You Owe Me a Ride" />

==Personality and traits==
Brea Tonnika had black [[hair]], green [[eye]]s, and fair skin. She put her hair into many braids, similar to that of her sister, She and her sister both wore similar catsuits.<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" />
