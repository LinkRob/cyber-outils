'''Akray''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Gender|female]] [[Nazzar]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. When she was captured by [[Baron]] [[Boolan]] and brought to [[Shingcu Station]], her brother [[Krrl]] hired the [[human]] [[Fallanassi]] [[bounty hunter]] [[H'Tar]] to free her. When H'tar realized that a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] was on the station and attempted to abort the rescue mission, Krrl betrayed her, giving her up to Boolan in exchange for Akray's freedom.<ref name="Brothers and Sisters" />
