A '''bodyguard droid''' with [[Gender|masculine programming]] was owned by [[Ambassador]] [[Ceeril]] of [[Rion]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. While being treated in the [[Starlight Beacon's medcenter|medcenter]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]], Ceeril used his [[bodyguard droid]] to stage an [[assassin]]ation attempt against himself, hoping to frame two [[Hassarian]]s also being treated onboard as their [[species]] were long-time rivals of his own, the [[Skembo]]. The [[droid]] was destroyed in the process, but [[Administrator]] [[Velko Jahen]] and the [[astromech droid]] [[KC-78]] discovered evidence in its remains which exonerated the Hassarians and proved Ceeril's guilt.<ref name="First Duty" />
