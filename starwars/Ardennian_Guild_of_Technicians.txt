The '''Ardennian Guild of Technicians''' was a [[technician]]s' guild active during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="TRS" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the guild's delegate&mdash;the [[Ardennian]] [[Retar]]&mdash;attended the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]. Retar engaged [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] in conversation about [[starship]] repairs for some [[time]] before eventually being led away by [[Jedi archivist]] [[OrbaLin]] to view the ''[[Secrets of the Jedi]]'' exhibition at the [[Republic Fair|fair]].<ref name="TRS" />
