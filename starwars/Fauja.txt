'''Fauja''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Aleena]] [[merchant]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Saleucami]] at the beginning of the [[Imperial Era]]. [[Cut Lawquane]], a [[Cloning|clone]] deserter from the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], attempted to purchase passage for himself and his [[Lawquane family|family]] on a [[shuttle]] offworld, but Fauja told him and [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Hunter]] that his [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] were useless due to the recent introduction of [[Imperial credit]]s, and that the [[Galactic Empire]] was requiring anyone who wanted to exchange their currency to register for a [[chain code]].<ref name="Cut and Run" />
