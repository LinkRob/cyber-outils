'''Cyrus's workshop''' was located on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Vuundalla]]<ref name="The Prince and the Pirate" /> during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Date">{{YJADate}}</ref> The workshop served as the [[pirate]] [[Cyrus Vuundir|Taborr Val Dorn]]'s base of operations, being where he stored his [[Taborr's helmet|pirate gear]] when not in use. He also used the workshop to train in his fighting abilities.<ref name="Unmasked">{{YJA|Unmasked}}</ref>
