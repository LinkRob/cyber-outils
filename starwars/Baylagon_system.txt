{{Quote|I have acknowledged that a data clerk on Aldraig mistakenly routed the shipment through Baylagon, but one extra layover is barely an inconvenience for a competent cargo pilot.|Senator Bail Organa, in a letter to ISB Investigator Axvering|<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>}}
The '''Baylagon system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> In an attempt to cover up a theft of [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wing transports]] during the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]], [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] had an [[Aldraig]] data clerk mistakenly route a shipment of U-wings to [[Coruscant]] through Baylagon.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

After the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Security Bureau]] launched an inquiry led by [[Chief Investigator]] [[Axvering]], Senator Organa acknowledged the data clerk's mistake, but believed that another layover would not have been an inconvenience to a competent [[pilot]]. The U-wing [[transport]]s eventually reached the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baylagon was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', a [[canon]] [[2017]] boxed set and reference book authored by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
