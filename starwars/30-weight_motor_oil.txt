'''30-weight motor oil''' was a [[Oil|substance]] that could be consumed by [[droid]]s, including [[B1-series battle droid]]s and [[WA-7 waitress droid]]s in a process similar to drinking. [[R0-GR]], a B1 battle droid, mentioned the substance in his [[book]], ''[[Droidography]]'', wherein he described it as "the good stuff."<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref>
