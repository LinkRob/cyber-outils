'''Coromon Besh''' was an island located in the [[Coromon Islands]], an island chain on the [[Core Worlds/Legends|Core World]] of [[Fresia/Legends|Fresia]]. The Islands contained a number of [[Factory/Legends|manufacturing plants]]. Most of the [[Human/Legends|Human]] staffers and designers for those factories lived on Coromon Besh.<ref name="CatCW" />
