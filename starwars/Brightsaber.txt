The '''''Brightsaber''''' was a [[heavy cruiser]] that served within the [[New Republic Defense Fleet|fleet]] of the [[New Republic]]. It took part in the New Republic's [[Battle of Jakku|attack]] on the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|forces]] at the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] in [[5 ABY]], where it was targeted and destroyed by the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. After investigating the loss of contact with the [[cruiser]], the New Republic [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] was able to discover the 204th's actions above Jakku.

==History==
{{Quote|They're using the radiation to hide from scanners and sever our ships' communications. After they blind and deafen their target, we can assume they hit hard and fast; we've already lost three heavy cruisers.|Hera Syndulla describes how the ''Brightsaber'' was lost|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[heavy cruiser]] ''Brightsaber'' served in the [[New Republic]]'s [[New Republic Defense Fleet]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> it was among the many New Republic [[starship]]s that took part in a large [[Battle of Jakku|attack]] on the loyalist [[Imperial Military|forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] at the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. During the fighting, the ''Brightsaber'' was positioned at the fringe of the battle and exchanged coordinates and vectors with the New Republic's refitted [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'' before it was targeted by the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

The 204th produced a particle [[radiation]] effect to hide from scanners and sever the ''Brightsaber''{{'s}} communications, allowing the [[fighter wing]] to silently destroy the heavy [[cruiser]]. The cruisers loss in communication was noticed by [[Comm/scan processor|comscan]] [[officer]] [[Dhina]] aboard the ''Deliverance''. [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] had Dhina play the last communications with the ''Brightsaber'' after learning that it was one of three heavy cruisers located at the fringe of the battle, including the ''[[Hunting Hound]]'' and ''[[Serenade]]'', that had lost communication. Hearing the static fill the channel, Syndulla deduced that the 204th was responsible for the losses of the cruisers.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
