'''Flashflies''' were a type of creature capable of flight.<ref name="CR" /> Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' take place around [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Chiss]] [[sky-walker]] [[Che'ri]] drew pictures of flashflies and [[bird]]s with markers. Based on this and the fact that Che'ri drew landscapes as seen from above, [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] deduced that the sky-walker wanted to learn how to fly. Che'ri claimed that Thrawn was wrong, remarking that [[Youngling|children]] drew flashflies because they were pretty, but ultimately admitted she wanted to fly.<ref name="CR" /> Fireflies hatch from cocoons.<ref name="LE">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Flashflies were first mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', which was written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="CR" />
