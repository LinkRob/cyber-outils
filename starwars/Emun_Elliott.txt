'''Emun Elliott''' (born [[November 28]], [[1983]]) is a [[Wikipedia:Scottish people|Scottish]] actor who portrayed [[Lieutenant]] [[Taslin Brance]] in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] ([[2015]]).<ref name="Fletcher">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gamesradar.com/well-known-actors-bit-parts-star-wars-force-awakens|text=8 well-known actors you probably missed in Star Wars: The Force Awakens|author=Fletcher, Rosie|work=[[Wikipedia:GamesRadar+|GamesRadar+]]|archivedate=20210225094139}}</ref> He is also known for playing the pilot [[W:c:avp:Emun Chance|Chance]] in the [[2012]] science fiction film [[Wikipedia:Prometheus (2012 film)|''Prometheus'']],<ref name="Fletcher" /> and for playing leading roles in several television shows, including Dr. Christian King in the BBC One sci-fi police drama [[Wikipedia:Paradox (TV series)|''Paradox'']], [[W:c:gameofthrones:Marillion|Marillion]] in [[Wikipedia:Game of Thrones|''Game of Thrones'']], Jay Adams in [[Wikipedia:Lip Service (TV series)|''Lip Service'']], and Richie Valentine in [[Wikipedia:Threesome (TV series)|''Threesome'']].<ref name="Edinburgh">{{WebCite|url=http://www.edinburghnews.scotsman.com/what-s-on/theatre/emun-elliott-enjoying-life-in-paradise-1-3226988|text=Emun Elliott enjoying life in Paradise|author=Rudden, Liam|date=2013-12-10|work=[https://www.edinburghnews.scotsman.com Edinburgh News]|archivedate=20131213012400|nolive=1}}</ref>
