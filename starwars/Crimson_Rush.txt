While playing kirgatz with [[Barpa]], [[Sev Cataban]] and [[Zo Ryda]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] played a Crimson Rush, but lost to Ryda's Damakian Tide.<ref name="Last Shot" />
