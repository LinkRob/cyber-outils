The '''Felvath Kurs Arena''' was a combat stadium located in [[Mos Eisley]], [[Tatooine]]. Named in honor of the famed pit-fighter [[Felvath Kurs]], it included a private box for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], Tatooine's resident [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]].<ref name="CL 2016" />
