{{Quote|Information partially recovered from the databanks of the ISD ''Deliberator'' near the Kessel system in 6 ABY.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
The '''''Deliberator''''' was an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] used by the [[Imperial Navy]]. The [[Star Destroyer]] contained [[databank]]s, which included a file titled "[[Pyke Syndicate]]" from the "[[Kessel]]" section of the [[Imperial Archives]]. This file detailed the [[Galactic Empire]]'s deal with the criminal Pyke Syndicate, whereby the Empire [[Mining|excavated]] the addictive substance [[spice]] from the [[Spice mines of Kessel|mines]] on<ref name="Rise" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Kessel before the [[Pyke]]s sold the drug on the [[black market]]. The file also noted that although the Empire received a substantial share of the profits, it ultimately assumed full control of the spice output.<ref name="Rise" />

In [[6 ABY]], the ''Deliberator'' was present near the [[Kessel system]], and the "Pyke Syndicate" file was partially recovered from the [[Starship|vessel's]] databanks.<ref name="Rise" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] used the file as a source in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', noting the file's recovery from the ''Deliberator''.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Deliberator'' was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
