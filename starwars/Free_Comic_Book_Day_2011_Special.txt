'''Free Comic Book Day 2011 Special''' was a comic published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[May 7]], [[2011]] as part of the annual [[Free Comic Book Day]] promotion. It contained the story "[[Opress Unleashed]]," and was bundled as a flip cover with ''Avatar: The Last Airbender''.
