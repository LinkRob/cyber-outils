The '''clone trooper doll''', also known as a '''clone trooper toy''',<ref name="Cornered EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/cornered-episode-guide|text="Cornered" Episode Guide - The Bad Batch}}</ref> was a type of [[doll]] that begun to be sold by the dawn of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Doll DB">{{Databank|clone-trooper-doll|Clone Trooper doll}}</ref> On the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]]<ref name="DBPantora">{{Databank|pantora|Pantora}}</ref> [[Pantora]], at least two of these dolls were sold at a [[Unidentified trading post|trading post]] in the [[central marketplace]] of [[Ro Station]]. Shortly after the Empire's [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]], one of those dolls was stolen by the [[Youngling|young]] [[Cloning|clone]] [[Omega]] after she was distracted by a crate full of [[voorpak]]s.<ref name="Cornered" /> Omega considered the toy trooper to be a female like herself, and later decorated it to look like a member of [[Clone Force 99]], declaring her to now be a "Bad Batcher."<ref name="Rampage" />

==Behind the scenes==
The clone trooper doll is similar to the [[Stormie|stormtrooper toy]] owned by [[Jyn Erso]] in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Cornered EG" />
