"'''Comic Marvels'''" is a 10-page reference article appearing in [[Star Wars Insider 91|''Star Wars Insider'' 91]]. It was written by [[Jason Fry]], and features an interview with [[Mary Jo Duffy]].

The article contains a brief overview of the [[Star Wars (1977)|Marvel Star Wars]] comic book series, with descriptions of the characters that featured, as well as recommendations for best and worst stories. In addition, the article draws attention to the continuity that was established in these early comics.
