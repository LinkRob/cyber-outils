{{Quote|I've ridden aryx. Big bipedal birds native to Cerea. Do those count?|Kidi Aleri, to Leia Organa|<ref name="Moving Target" />}}
'''Aryx''' were large [[Limb|bipedal]] [[bird]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to<ref name="Moving Target" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Location">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Cerea]]. The [[Cerean]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Kidi Aleri]] rode the birds at some point in her life prior to [[Operation Yellow Moon]]<ref name="Moving Target" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{HelmetCollectionCite|53|Highlights of the Saga}} dates [[Operation Yellow Moon]], during which [[Kidi Aleri]] described riding aryx, to [[4 ABY]].</ref>

While participating in the operation&mdash;an Alliance mission designed to distract [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces&mdash;Alliance [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] asked Aleri and the other members of the team taking part in the mission if they knew how to ride. In response, Aleri described the birds to Organa, stating that she was unsure if having ridden them would prepare her to ride the mounts that Organa planned to rent on the [[Corva sector]] planet [[Jaresh]].<ref name="Moving Target" />

==Behind the scenes==
The aryx was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Cecil Castellucci]].<ref name="Moving Target" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Aryx/Legends|aryx]] first appeared in the [[Star Wars (1998) 1|first issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Republic]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series, written by [[Jan Strnad]], pencilled by [[Anthony Winn]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="SW1">[[Star Wars (1998) 1|''Star Wars'' (1998) 1]]</ref> on [[December 16]], [[1998]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/98-022/Star-Wars-1Prelude-to-Rebellion-Part-1-of-6|text=Star Wars 1 Prelude to Rebellion (Part 1 of 6)}}</ref>
