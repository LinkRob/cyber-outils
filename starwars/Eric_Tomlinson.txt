'''Eric Tomlinson''' was a recording engineer. He is credited as working on all movies in the [[Original trilogy]]<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref><ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref><ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> as well as the [[Indiana Jones]] series. His music was used on the [[Star Wars: A Musical Journey]] Feature contained with the [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] DVD.
