"'''Quickdraw'''" was the nickname of a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the [[First Order]].<ref name="Starfighter Missions" /> She served as a [[First Order Special Forces TIE fighter pilot|Special Forces pilot]] who flew one of the specialized [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s.<ref name="Note" /> Quickdraw had light skin and dirty [[Color|blonde]] [[hair]]. She was equipped with the First Order pilot uniform<ref name="Starfighter Missions" /> and was described as a defiant duelist. At one point, Quickdraw flew in a debris field and fired her [[starfighter]]'s [[laser cannon]]s.<ref name="X-wing">{{FFGXW2|set=First Order Conversion Kit|cardname="Quickdraw"}}</ref>
