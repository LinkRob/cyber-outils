'''Drego''' was a [[Kyuzo]] who worked as a [[deputy]] with his fellow partner [[Streehn]] in [[Niima Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. When the [[Kubaz]] banker [[Rikard Lovas]]'s banking ship exploded, Drego and [[Streehn]] stayed at their cousin [[Constable]] [[Zuvio]]'s [[Office of the Constable|office]] interrogating people who were possible suspects to the explosion.<ref name="High Noon" />
