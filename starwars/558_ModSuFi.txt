'''558 ModSuFi''' was the title of a red-level [[METOSP]] issued by the [[New Republic Space Ministry Firrerre Office|consular office]] of the [[New Republic]]'s [[New Republic Space Ministry|Space Ministry]] on<ref name="RSG" /> the [[Planet|world]]<ref name="BMF38">{{BuildFalconCite|38|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> [[Firrerre]] via the [[Moddell Node]] and [[Sugai Subnode]].<ref name="RSG" />

The document warned [[spacer]]s of the dangers of approaching or landing on the [[desert]] planet [[Jakku]], listing the hostile environment of the planet and surrounding [[Realspace|space]], lack of services and [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]], presence of criminal elements, and distance to the nearest New Republic office on Firrerre as hazards. It also warned them that New Republic assistance was not available on Jakku and had to be requested in person on Firrerre. The [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] found a physical copy of 558 ModSuFi on the [[Pilgrim's Road]] on Jakku and believed it offered good advice, including it in her [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]]<ref name="RSG" /> by [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' includes an addendum at the end describing some of the events of {{Film|VII}}, written several [[Standard day|days]] after they take place. The addendum is the final entry in the guide and is included chronologically after the entry mentioning 558 ModSuFi. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the film to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
558 ModSuFi was created for ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'', a [[2015]] replica journal written by [[Jason Fry]]. In the British release of the book, the document is included as a double-sided fold out page<ref name="RSG" /> but the version release in the United States included only a single side of the document with large amounts of the text removed, including the document's name.<ref name="USA">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' {{C|US version}}</ref>
