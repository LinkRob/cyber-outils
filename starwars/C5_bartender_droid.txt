The '''C5 bartender droid''' was a model of [[bartender droid]] manufactured by [[LeisureMech Enterprises]],<ref name="Thrawn" /> being one of the company's only notable products alongside the [[BD-3000 luxury droid]] and [[RH-series CardShark droid]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Protocol and Service Droids}}</ref> C5s possessed two limbs and two [[Color|white]] [[photoreceptor]]s. The [[droid]]s' plating was white.<ref name="Thrawn3" />

By the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]], the model was regarded as a classic. Several suites at the [[Alisandre Hotel]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] featured restored C5 units, including the suite of Imperial [[Moff]] [[Ghadi]].<ref name="Thrawn" /> Circa [[13 BBY]],<ref name="date">Per the novel [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']], [[Arihnda Pryce]] encountered a C5 [[bartender droid]] at the [[Alisandre Hotel]] shortly after she also met [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] there. ''Thrawn'' establishes that the pair met there approximately two [[Standard year|years]] prior to Thrawn's promotion to [[commodore]], which occurs nearly four years after the start of the novel. As noted [[Template:ThrawnNovelStartDate|here]], the novel begins around [[15 BBY]]. Therefore, Thrawn's promotion to commodore comes around [[11 BBY]], thereby placing Thrawn and Pryce's meeting to circa 13 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> Ghadi allowed [[Arihnda Pryce]], [[aide]] to [[Lothal]]ite [[Senator]] [[Domus Renking]], to get a [[Alcohol|drink]] from a C5 when she visited his suite.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the C5 bartender droid appeared in the [[2017]] novel [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']], authored by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the first installment of the [[Star Wars: The Imperial Trilogy|''Star Wars: Thrawn'']] trilogy.<ref name="Thrawn" /> It was pictured the [[Thrawn 3|third issue]] of the [[Star Wars: Thrawn (comic series)|comic adaptation]] of ''Thrawn''. The issue was written by [[Jody Houser]], illustrated by [[Luke Ross]], and released on [[April 11]], [[2018]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/66795/star_wars_thrawn_2018_3|text=Star Wars: Thrawn (2018) #3}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the droid was mentioned in the short story "[[A Bad Feeling: The Tale of EV-9D9]]," authored by [[Judith Reeves-Stevens|Judith]] and [[Garfield Reeves-Stevens]] in [[1995]] as part of the ''[[Tales from Jabba's Palace]]'' anthology.<ref name="Leg">{{StoryCite|story=A Bad Feeling: The Tale of EV-9D9|book=Tales from Jabba's Palace}}</ref>
