The '''Cabarka Union''' was a group that used [[Verazeen stones]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Temple Peak 1">[[The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak 1|''The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak'' 1]]</ref> Around [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]]. ''The Rising Storm'' states that the events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak]]'' occurred several [[Standard month|months]] prior, dating it to approximately the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the [[archaeologist]] [[Tapel]] was surprised to see former [[Jedi]] [[Ty Yorrick]] use Verazeen stones, as he thought no one outside the Cabarka Union used them.<ref name="Temple Peak 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cabarka Union was mentioned in the [[The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic miniseries]] ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak]]'', which was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[IDW Publishing|IDW]]<ref name="Temple Peak 1" /> on [[August 11]], [[2021]].<ref name="Comixology">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-The-Monster-of-Temple-Peak-1-of-4/digital-comic/946350|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures&mdash;The Monster of Temple Peak #1 (of 4)}}</ref>
