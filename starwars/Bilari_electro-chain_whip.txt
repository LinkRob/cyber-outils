The '''Bilari electro-chain whip''' was a weapon used by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]'s [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guard]]. It remained in the shape of a long [[Rapier/Canon|rapier]]-like weapon until snapped by its user into a whip-like weapon. In its whip form, an electro-plasma current ran through it. It could be wound around an opponent like a chain to hold him or her still, or could strike someone like a traditional whip, and was resistant to [[lightsaber]] strikes.

==Description==
[[File:BilariElectroChainWhip-CineFex157.png|thumb|300px|left|A Praetorian Guard activating the electro-plasma current on their whip.]]
The Bilari electro-chain whip was one of the most unique weapons in the [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guard]] arsenal, able to function as a [[sword]]-like [[melee weapon]] with an electro-[[plasma]] current or be shifted into a [[Whip (disambiguation)|whip]] depending on combat style. It could also be used to ensnare an opponent's weapon.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==History==
One of the three Imperial Praetorian Guards, assigned to Moff Gideon to aid in the [[Conflict on Mandalore|battle for Mandalore]], stabbed the [[Heavy Infantry Mandalorian]] [[Paz Vizsla]] with a Bilari electro-chain whip first before the [[Mandalorian warrior]] succumb to his death.<ref name="Chapter 23" />

[[File:ElitePraetorianGuardBilaryWhip.gif|thumb|right|A Bilari electro-chain whip in sword form (both deactivated and activated status depicted)]]
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. As [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] carries on immediately from the events of ''The Force Awakens'', it can be assumed to take place in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> two members of the Elite Praetorian Guard carried their Bilari electro-chain whips aboard [[Snoke]]'s [[flagship]], the ''[[Supremacy]]'', when [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] and [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] of the [[First Order]] went to talk with Snoke about their failure. Sometime later, when Ren took the [[Force-sensitive]] [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] to Snoke, the Praetorian Guards used their whips along with their other weapons to combat Rey and Ren after the latter [[Death|killed]] Snoke. Their whips were no match for [[Skywalker lightsaber|Rey's]] and [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|Kylo's]] [[lightsaber]]s, resulting in the deaths of all the guards. One of the Bilari whip-wielding guards nearly overwhelmed Rey by ensnaring her lightsaber blade and drawing her in, but she freed herself and killed the guard before swinging his weapon with her blade toward the red banner causing it to light a fire, while Ren beheaded the other after a brief duel.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>
