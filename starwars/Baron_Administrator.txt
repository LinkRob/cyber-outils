'''Baron Administrator''',<ref name="Databank Lando" /> also spelled '''Baron-Administrator''',<ref name="Databank Cloud City">{{SWE|locations|cloudcity|Cloud City}}</ref> was a title borne by the individual in charge of certain facilities, including [[Cloud City]], a floating [[City|metropolis]] suspended in the clouds of [[Bespin]],<ref name="Databank Lando" /> and the [[space station]] best known as [[the Wheel]].<ref name="Obi-Wan and Anakin">''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure]]''</ref>

By the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Hoth]], Cloud City's Baron Administrator was [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]],<ref name="Databank Lando" /> a position he won in a game of cards.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> To his regret, Calrissian was forced<ref name="Star Wars 1">[[Star Wars (2020) 1|''Star Wars'' (2020) 1]]</ref> to abandon the title during the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Escape from Cloud City|occupation]] of Cloud City, though he later reclaimed the title following the fall of the [[Iron Blockade]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> By [[7 ABY]],<ref name="Last Shot Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Han Solo]]'s pursuit of [[Fyzen Gor]], as seen in ''Last Shot'', to twenty-seven [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Hosnian Cataclysm to [[34 ABY]], meaning that the events of ''Last Shot'' took place in [[7 ABY]].</ref> Calrissian was no longer the Baron Administrator.<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref>
