During the [[Cold War]], a '''battle''' broke out in the [[Fjor system]] between the [[Resistance]], the [[First Order]], and the [[Ranc Gang]]. In an attempt to prevent the First Order from finding the [[D'Qar|location]] of the [[Resistance base]], the Resistance [[starfighter]] [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] exited [[hyperspace]] above a [[Unidentified desert planet|remote desert planet]]. Poe was followed by the [[First Order Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Terex]] and his Ranc Gang. After Poe crash-landed his [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] on the planet, a skirmish broke out between the Ranc Gang and the Resistance's [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]]. Terex's Ranc Gang was subsequently wiped out by First Order forces under [[Commander]] [[Malarus]], who then apprehended Terex for insubordination.

==Prelude==
[[File:Poe-and-Nunzix-headed-to-DQar-PD-11.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Poe Dameron, taking the Resistance spy, N1-ZX ("Nunzix"), back to the Resistance base]]
During the later stages of the [[Cold War]], the [[Resistance]] operatives [[Poe Dameron]], [[BB-8]], [[C-3PO]], and [[Oddy Muva]] undertook a [[Mission to Kaddak|mission]] to the planet [[Kaddak]] to recover the Resistance spy droid<ref name="PD 8">[[Poe Dameron 8|''Poe Dameron'' 8]]</ref> [[N1-ZX]] ("Nunzix"),<ref name="PD 10">[[Poe Dameron 10|''Poe Dameron'' 10]]</ref> who had purportedly recovered valuable information about the [[First Order]] including the location of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]].<ref name="PD 8" /> Poe and the droids managed to escape Kaddak on Poe's [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] ''[[Black One]]'' but were separated from Oddy, who stowed aboard Terex's ship ''[[Carrion Spike]]''.<ref name="PD 10" /> Oddy wanted to rescue his wife [[Sowa Chuan]] from Terex,<ref name="PD 12" /> who had blackmailed him into spying on Poe's [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]].<ref name="PD 11" />

Terex had allowed Poe to escape Kaddak in order to track down the location of the [[Resistance base]]. Terex wanted to use the Ranc Gang to destroy the Resistance and curry favor with the First Order. Terex's plans went against the Supreme Leader's orders for First Order forces to avoid open hostility with forces aligned with the New Republic. Despite [[Captain]] [[Phasma]]'s objections, Terex was confident that his plan would succeed.<ref name="PD 11" /> Terex used the ''Carrion Spike''{{'s}} advance sensors to home in on Poe's X-wing. He also assembled a fleet of [[Ugly|"Uglies"]], starships cobbled up from old starships and other vehicles.<ref name="PD 10" />

However, Poe realized that the information that N1-ZX was carrying was a trick used by Terex to uncover the Resistance base. After consulting with BB-8 and C-3PO, Poe decided to abort their return trip to the Resistance base. Poe instead exited hyperspace above a [[Unidentified desert planet|remote desert planet]]. Terex and his fleet followed suit. Despite failing to reach the Resistance base, Terex ordered his fleet to destroy Poe instead. Meanwhile, Oddy Muva overpowered and forced Terex's henchman [[Zumgi]]<ref name="PD 11" /> to take him to his wife Sowa.<ref name="PD 12" />

==The battle==
===Hide and seek===
[[File:PoeCrashLanding-PoeDameron11.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Poe was forced to crash his X-Wing and escape on foot]]
Poe managed to shoot down at least two "Uglies" before one of his X-wing's engines was hit by enemy fire and crashed on the desert planet. Poe and the droids were largely unhurt but N1-ZX's right arm was severed. The droids escaped Terex's "Uglies" by fleeing into a cave. Meanwhile, Oddy Muva freed his wife and the other slaves. He sought revenge against Terex only to learn that Terex had disembarked from the ''Carrion Spike'' with a landing party to hunt down Poe and the droids. Terex left another lieutenant [[Brrang]] in charge of the ''Carrion Spike'' and the "Uglies" fleet.<ref name="PD 12" />

In an attempt to slow down Terex and his gang, BB-8 collapsed an ice stalactite on top of the gangsters. One was crushed by the heavy object. However, Terex managed to use his [[blaster]] to shoot down BB-8's chain, causing the droid to fall. C-3PO then decided to stay behind to slow down Terex so that Poe Dameron and Nunzix could travel deeper into the cave. When Terex and the Ranc Gang arrived, Poe spoke to the native [[fjor-bat]]s and claimed that the gangsters wanted to steal their [[youngling]]s. While the Ranc Gang fought the fjor-bats, Terex confronted C-3PO. He deactivated the droid by stealing his memory unit.<ref name="PD 12" />

Meanwhile, Poe tried to get N1-ZX to fight since the droid was a [[BX-series droid commando]]. Nunzix refused due to his self-preservation software and his self-centered streak. Shortly later, Poe received contact from his fellow Black Squadron pilot [[Temmin Wexley]] that the squadron had arrived to rescue him. Terex's lieutenant Brrang believed that the Ranc Gang's "Uglies" could beat Black Squadron due to their superior numbers and ordered his fleet to destroy Poe's comrades.<ref name="PD 12" />

===Black Squadron vs Rancs===
[[File:BlackSquadronRancGang-PoeDameron13.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Black Squadron vs the Ranc fleet]]
While Black Squadron fought, Poe tried to convince Nunzix to fight. Fortunately for Poe, Temmin transmitted his droid [[Mister Bones]]' droid personality template onto Poe's [[comlink]]. After tapping N1-ZX's transponder, N1-ZX assumed Mister Bones' personality and slaughtered Terex's landing party. Meanwhile, Oddy Muva managed to reprogram the ''Carrion Spike''{{'s}} battle computers to target any ship without a Resistance transponder, resulting in several "Uglies" being wiped out. He then evacuated his wife Sowa Chuan and the other slaves in four [[escape pod]]s. Oddy managed to contact the Resistance pilot [[Karé Kun]] and convince her squadron to escort their escape pods to safety.<ref name="PD 13" />

Meanwhile, Terex managed to shoot and decapitate N1-ZX. Poe then attacked him and forced him to the ground with a blaster. Back in space, Black Squadron escorted the escape pods to the desert planet while under fire from the "Uglies." One of the Ranc fighters shot down an escape pod. The Resistance pilot [[L'ulo L'ampar]] retaliated by shooting that fighter. However, he was then shot down by another "Ugly." Back in the cave, Terex managed to disarm Poe by throwing a knife into his hand. Before he could finish off Poe, BB-8 electrocuted him, knocking the First Order agent and [[crime lord]] unconscious.<ref name="PD 13" />

Elsewhere, Oddy and the escape pods safely entered the desert planet's atmosphere. Unwilling to let L'ulo die in vain, the rest of Black Squadron attacked the ''Carrion Spike'' and the Ranc fighters. Shortly later, a First Order [[Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser|Maxima-A class heavy cruiser]] emerged from hyperspace and disgorged a swarm of [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]. To the surprise of Black Squadron, the First Order heavy cruiser wiped out the Ranc "Uglies" and destroyed Terex's flagship ''Carrion Spike''. The cruiser also dispatched an [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport|Atmospheric Assault Lander]] and several TIEs to the desert planet's surface. These forces had been dispatched by the First Order to arrest Terex for insubordination.<ref name="PD 13" />

==Aftermath==
[[File:TerexArrested-PoeDameron13.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Terex was handed over to the First Order]]
The battle ended both the Ranc Gang and Agent Terex's ambitions of destroying the Resistance. The First Order [[Commander]] [[Malarus]] and a contingent of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] landed on the desert planet and met with Poe Dameron. After being assured of their peaceful intentions, Poe allowed them to arrest Terex. Before handing Terex over, he taunted Terex about the destruction of the Ranc Gang and his flagship ''Carrion Spike''. Terex countered that Poe had lost one of his pilots. However, Poe responded that Terex had lost everything while Poe had only lost one of his friends.<ref name="PD 13" />
