The '''''Destrier''''' was a [[starship]] that was destroyed during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[human]] [[Soran Keize]]'s first [[Standard year|years]] in the [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]]. Keize looked back on the destruction as one of the events that taught him about his responsibility to protect the lives of his cohorts in order to protect even more.<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Destrier'' was mentioned in [[canon]] in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Destrier/Legends|''Destrier'']] was mentioned in [[2012]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Paul R. Urquhart]].<ref name="Warfare">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
