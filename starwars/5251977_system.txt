The '''5251977 system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Quence sector]], which was situated in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and was in the [[Standard Galactic Grid|grid square]] P-18.<ref name="GMA" /> [[5251977]] was a small orbiting [[planet]]<ref name="Lost Stars" /> in the star system.<ref name="GMA" /> During the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] occupied the orbiting planet following their defeat at [[Echo Base]] on [[Hoth]].<ref name="Lost Stars />

==Behind the scenes==
The 5251977 system was first seen in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' novel ''[[Lost Stars]]'' through the planet 5251977.<ref name="GMA" /> The novel was written by [[Claudia Gray]] and published on [[September 4]], [[2015]].<ref name="Disney">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/lost-stars|text=Lost Stars}}</ref> The system was later named through the "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="GMA" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Jason">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map...|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
