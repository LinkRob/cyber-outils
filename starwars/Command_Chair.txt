The '''Command Chair''' was a type of specialized [[repulsorlift chair]] developed by the rookie [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[medic]], [[programmer]], and tinkerer [[Sprocket]] to assist him within the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] hosted within [[Grand Arena]] on [[Vespaara]]. A console and other controls were added to help Sprocket command his various [[droid]]s. The Command Chair was highly customizable, and also featured a fold out cup holder.<ref name="Hunters" />
