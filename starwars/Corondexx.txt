'''Corondexx''' were a company that manufacturered the [[VES-700 pulse rifle]], which was available during the [[Galactic Civil War]], although hard to find.<ref name="G&G" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Corondexx was mentioned in the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="G&G" /> The [[Corondexx/Legends|company]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 10: Bounty Hunters]]'', a [[1994]] supplement written by [[Rick D. Stuart]] for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]''.<ref name="GG10">''[[Galaxy Guide 10: Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>
