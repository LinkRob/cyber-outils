==Behind the scenes==
Chadian rum was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Queen's Hope]]'', a young-adult novel written by [[E. K. Johnston]].<ref name="Queen's Hope" /> The novel was initially released on [[January 25]], [[2022]] in [[Wikipedia:German|German]],<ref name="German">{{Panini|shop=1|url=star-wars-tv-und-film-science-fiction-buecher/star-wars-hoffnung-der-koenigin-ydswya007|text=Hoffnung der Königin|language=[[Wikipedia:German|German]]}}</ref> and was later published on [[April 5]] of the same year in [[Wikipedia:English language|English]].<ref name="English">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/queens-hope|text=Queen's Hope}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, Chadian rum first appeared in "[[A Credit for Your Thoughts]]," a short story written by [[Tish Eggleston Pahl]] and [[Chris Cassidy]] that was published in [[Star Wars Gamer 2|''Star Wars Gamer'' 2]] in [[February]] [[2001]].<ref name="Gamer 2">{{SWArchive|url=gaming/other/rpg/news20010118.html|text=''Star Wars Gamer'' #2}}</ref>
