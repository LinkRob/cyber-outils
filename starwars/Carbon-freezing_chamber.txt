A '''carbon-freezing chamber''' was used to [[Carbon-freezing|encase]] objects, and in some cases individuals, in [[carbonite]]. The [[gas-processing vane]]s of the floating [[city]] [[Cloud City]], located on the [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]], each contained a carbon-freeze chamber, which used a mixture of carbonite and Bespin-mined [[tibanna]] gas for flash-freeze preservation.<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> Ships such as ''[[Slave I]]''<ref name="IG-88 1">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – IG-88 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – IG-88'' 1]]</ref> and the ''[[Razor Crest]]'' had these chambers installed in their cargo holds.<ref name="Ch. 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:Acme-LurkingLineage.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Vader waits for Luke Skywalker on Bespin]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi]] [[Jedi General|General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] had himself, his master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and their team of [[clone trooper]]s encased in carbonite at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. They were able to smuggle themselves past [[Lola Sayu]]'s [[Lola Sayu defense fleet|defense fleet]] in order to [[Battle of Lola Sayu|infiltrate]] the [[Citadel]] to rescue Jedi Master [[Even Piell]] and Captain [[Wilhuff Tarkin]].<ref name="The Citadel" />

A [[Figg and Associates]] [[Class-3 CFC]]<ref name="Fascinating Facts" /> in Cloud City was used by the [[Galactic Empire]] to encase the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] after the [[Battle of Hoth]], whose frozen form was then handed off to the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]]. [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] later tried to capture [[Luke Skywalker]] in carbonite, having tested the equipment on Solo, but was unsuccessful.<ref name="Episode V" /> With Bespin under Imperial occupation, the Empire began following Vader's example of freezing prisoners in carbonite.<ref name="Star Wars 3">[[Star Wars (2020) 3|''Star Wars'' (2020) 3]]</ref>

During the [[Mission to Cloud City|return mission to Cloud City]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] would be frozen in carbonite herself.<ref name="Star Wars 3" /> However, she and numerous other frozen prisoners were rescued when Skywalker used the Force to activate the unfreezing controls.<ref name="Star Wars 4">[[Star Wars (2020) 4|''Star Wars'' (2020) 4]]</ref>
