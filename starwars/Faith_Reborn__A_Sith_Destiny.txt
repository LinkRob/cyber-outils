'''Faith Reborn: A Sith Destiny''' is an unpublished short story written by [[Abel G. Peña]]. The story would have focused on [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader's]] final [[Sith apprentice/Legends|apprentice]] [[Lumiya]] and her [[Yuuzhan Vong]] [[Sith familiar]] [[Vongerella]], who would have received a real name in this story. It was intended to be a follow-up [[Insider Online Supplement|Hyperspace tie-in]] to the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' article ''[[Vader's Legacy]]'' until both were cancelled by [[October]] [[2009]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.njoe.com/2009/10/13/njoe-interviews-abel-g-pena|text=NJOE Interviews Abel G Peña&hellip;|date=2009-10-13|archivedate=20091119054755}}</ref> Peña allegedly sent a copy to author [[Joseph Bongiorno|Joe Bongiorno]] for research. Bongiorno released Faith Reborn and Vader's Legacy on his [https://www.starwarstimeline.net/ website] in [[2025]].<ref name="FR">{{WebCite|url=https://www.starwarstimeline.net/The%20Archives.htm|text="Faith Reborn: A Sith Destiny"|author=[[Abel G. Peña|Abel G. Peña]]|work=[http://www.starwarstimeline.net Star Wars Expanded Universe Timeline]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/OkzqG}}</ref>
