'''Deesh Crozli''' was a [[Color|black]]-skinned [[Deesh Crozli's species|alien]] who left her [[Deesh Crozli's homeworld|homeworld]] and ended up on the [[planet]] [[Ueda]], where she kept her ear to the ground for information to [[Information broker|trade]]. As Crozli's soft tissue needed to be immersed in an [[Atmosphere|atmospheric]] mixture that matched that of her [[homeworld]] periodically, she wore a black [[pressure-mask]] with red lenses while on Ueda, hiding her face. She also wore black [[Clothing|robes]], a brown [[Currency|money]] [[belt]] that she kept well-secured, and a gray [[gas mixture monitor]] on her chest.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> Crozli was sitting by the gate into<ref name="LostFound" /> [[Point Ueda]], the [[capital]] of Ueda,<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> when the [[Sith assassin]] [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] [[Mae's quest|approached]] her and produced two<ref name="LostFound" /> [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="LostFoundAD">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> Aniseya asked where the local [[Jedi]], [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Indara]], was located and Crozli pointed into the city and spoke in an [[alien]] [[language]]. In response, Aniseya placed the [[coin]]s in a basket on Crozli's lap. The [[assassin]] then proceeded to<ref name="LostFound" /> the [[Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop]],<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> where she [[Indara's murder|murdered]] Indara.<ref name="LostFound" />

==Behind the scenes==
Deesh Crozli first appeared in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="LostFound" /> the first episode of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which aired on [[Disney+]] on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> Concept art of the character was titled "A Spy" was created by [[Ivan Manzella]],<ref name="ArtOf">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''</ref> who also produced a render of Crozli's full head.<ref name="Manzella">{{WebCite|url=https://www.krop.com/ivanmanzella|text=Ivan Manzella Creatures & Character Designs|author=Manzella, Ivan|work=[https://www.krop.com Krop]|archivedate=20190121223617}}</ref> The character is also visible in concept art of the Ueda Village Gate created by [[Kevin Jenkins]] and [[Stephen Tappin]].<ref name="ArtOf" /> The Ueda gate scene was shot at [[Shinfield Studios]],<ref name="Fantha Part I">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fanthatracks.com/news/the-acolyte-a-journey-to-the-filming-locations-part-1-madeira|text=The Acolyte: A journey to the filming locations Part 1: Madeira|archivedate=20241204220147}}</ref> [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="Julian Foddy">{{Lucasfilm|url=news/julian-foddy-acolyte|text=''The Acolyte'' Visual Effects Supervisor Julian Foddy}}</ref> Prior to the release of the episode, Crozli was revealed in a trailer exclusively shown at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{CelebrationTrailer|''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''|Celebration Europe (2023)}}</ref> during the [[Lucasfilm]] Studio Showcase on [[April 7]], [[2023]].<ref name="CelebrationDate">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-lucasfilm-studio-showcase|text=SWCE 2023: 11 Highlights from Lucasfilm's Studio Showcase}}</ref> She was first identified in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
