'''Braxen''' was an [[E'roni]] [[soldier]] from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]] who served in his [[homeworld]]'s [[E'ronoh's military|military]] for several [[Standard year|years]] until resigning to labor as a quarry [[miner]] when the world was at peace. When E'ronoh restarted their [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|war]] with their neighbor&mdash;the Outer Rim planet [[Eiram]]&mdash;in [[387 BBY]], Braxen reenlisted and joined [[E'ronoh's fleet]] as a [[drill ship]] [[pilot]]. On one occasion, the soldier participated in an [[Attack on Eiram's western isles|attack]] on [[Eiram's western isles]], following orders without hesitation to rain destruction on E'ronoh's enemies.

While aboard the [[Alif-class Longbeam|''Alif''-class Longbeam]] ''[[Paxion]]'' for a [[Summit aboard the Paxion|peace summit]] in [[382 BBY]], Braxen served as one of [[Monarch of E'ronoh|Monarch]] [[A'lbaran]]'s [[Security guard|guards]]. During the summit, the E'roni [[Viceroy]] [[Ferrol]] ordered the soldier to eject himself from one of the ''Paxion''{{'s}} [[airlock]]s, claiming his actions would save his homeworld from ruin. Braxen loyally complied, [[Death|sacrificing]] himself and [[Death|dying]] in [[Realspace|space]]. Meanwhile, the viceroy framed the guard for killing [[Captain]] [[Jerrod Segaru]]&mdash;a crime actually committed by his [[Ferrols|son]], [[Rev Ferrol]], in an attempt to disrupt the peace negotiations. Braxen's body was later recovered from space by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]] and [[Padawan]] [[Enya Keen]], and the truth behind his framing was eventually confirmed by [[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]] [[Standard week|weeks]] later.

==Biography==
===Military service===
{{Quote|You, ''you'' are a true child of E'ronoh.|Viceroy Ferrol, to Braxen|<ref name="Convergence" />}}
[[File:Eronoh-THRShow.png|thumb|left|300px|Braxen hailed from E'ronoh and served in his homeworld's military.]]
The [[E'roni]] [[soldier]] Braxen lived during the [[High Republic Era]] and [[Homeworld|hailed]] from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]] in the [[Eiram system]]. During his youth, he enlisted in [[E'ronoh's military]] to fight against the E'ronoh's neighboring world&mdash;Outer Rim planet [[Eiram]]&mdash;and served for several [[Standard year|years]]. Eventually, when the worlds made peace, Braxen left military service to work as a quarry [[miner]]. However, following the [[death]] of E'roni [[Prince]] [[Niko A'lbaran]] during a [[Diplomatic mission to Eiram|diplomatic mission]] to Eiram<ref name="Convergence" /> in [[387 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the start of the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]] to [[387 BBY]]. As ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' establishes that the [[diplomatic mission to Eiram]]'s failure was the inciting event of the conflict, it must have occurred that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> [[Monarch of E'ronoh|Monarch]] [[A'lbaran]] reignited E'ronoh's [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|conflict]] with their neighboring planet and blamed them for murdering his [[E'ronoh royal family|heir]]. Braxen reenlisted in E'ronoh's military&mdash;as part of their [[E'ronoh's fleet|fleet]]&mdash;to [[pilot]] a [[drill ship]], a [[Starship|ship]] once used for [[mining]] that had been retrofitted into a destructive [[weapon]] of war.<ref name="Convergence" />

Over the following five years, Braxen grew comfortable with his position as a pilot, preferring the rattling of a drill ship over silence. The pilot engaged in several endeavors against Eiram, struggling alongside his colleagues as the war intensified at the same [[time]] as the drought on E'ronoh raged. Braxen [[Diet|consumed]] [[siltgrog]] [[ale]], often brewed on the world in dry [[season]]s, during the drought, becoming familiar with its taste. At some point, he became acquainted with several members of the E'ronoh [[fleet]]'s [[Thylefire Squadron]], including E'ronoh's [[Gender|princess]], [[Captain]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]]; [[Lieutenant]] [[Jerrod Segaru]]; and the pilots [[Kinni]], [[Bly Tevin]], and [[Rev Ferrol]]. Braxen participated in a [[Mission to Eiram's western isles|mission]] to [[Eiram's western isles]] during the conflict, using his drill ship to rain destruction down on his homeworld's enemies.<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the man returned to [[the Rook]]&mdash;E'ronoh's [[capital]] [[city]]&mdash;after a mission and learned a cease-fire was brokered between Eiram and E'ronoh by Xiri. Though he wished to stay stationed as a drill ship pilot, [[medic]]s did not clear him for active duty.<ref name="Convergence" />

===Death and legacy===
{{Quote|You framed the guard.''"<br />"''Me. Well, I had help. My father has such a way with words.''"<br />"''Viceroy Ferrol. Why?''"<br />"''Jerrod was a good soldier. The best, honestly. But he'd always be loyal to you, and you are compromised, Xiri.|Xiri A'lbaran and Rev Ferrol, discussing Braxen's framing for Jerrod Segaru's death|<ref name="Convergence" />}}
[[File:EiramandEronohsystem.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Braxen boarded the ''Paxion'' as one of Monarch A'lbaran's guards for a peace summit in the Eiram system.]]
Instead, Braxen was assigned to be one of [[Monarchy|Monarch]] A'lbaran's [[Security guard|guards]] and traveled to the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Orlen Mollo]]'s [[Alif-class Longbeam|''Alif''-class Longbeam]] ''[[Paxion]]'' for a [[Summit aboard the Paxion|peace summit]] between Eiram and E'ronoh, aimed at ending the war between them. The soldier traveled with his world's retinue, boarding the ''Paxion'' while it was stationed in the Eiram [[Star system|system]]. During the negotiations, when the Monarch again blamed Niko's death on [[Adrialla]]&mdash;Eiram's [[queen]]&mdash;Xiri told those present that her brother had drowned and that none of the fighting in the last five years would bring him back. That evening, after finishing an evening meal aboard the ''Paxion'', Braxen returned to his post guarding Monarch A'lbaran's quarters. As he stood outside the rooms, the man reflected on how full he felt, comparing it to his last several [[Standard month|months]] of hunger. E'roni [[Viceroy]] [[Ferrol]]&mdash;who was secretly organizing the [[Children of E'ronoh]] dissident group intent on prolonging the war&mdash;approached the drill ship pilot, lying to him that Xiri had been affected by Adrialla's secret weapons. After the viceroy's explanation, Braxen agreed to assist him in any way he required, listening to Ferrol's claims that he&mdash;despite only being a guard&mdash;was the only one not compromised within the E'roni delegation.<ref name="Convergence" />

[[File:GellaNattai.png|thumb|left|220px|Gella Nattai (pictured) was one of the Jedi who recovered Braxen's body.]]
When Braxen suggested that Segaru was still loyal, Ferrol indicated that the newly appointed captain had also been compromised and assured the soldier that he was a true child of E'ronoh. With the viceroy's words ringing in his [[ear]]s, the guard moved in a stupor to the ''Paxion''{{'s}} [[hangar]]. As he walked, the pilot briefly grappled with his internal unrest about the given objective but felt serving E'ronoh one last time at Ferrol's request was worth it. Without hesitation, Braxen [[Death|ejected himself]] from the vessel's [[airlock]] and died in [[Realspace|space]], giving one final shout of loyalty for E'ronoh in his dying breath. Around the same time, elsewhere on the ''Paxion'', Ferrol's [[Ferrols|son]]&mdash;Rev&mdash;murdered Segaru, considering him too loyal to Xiri's quest to restore peace between E'ronoh and Eiram. Meanwhile, the [[Jedi]] traveling aboard the ''Paxion'' felt a faint [[disturbance in the Force]] upon Braxen's passing but decided not to investigate.<ref name="Convergence" />

After discovering Segaru's body the next [[Standard day|day]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]] and [[Padawan]] [[Enya Keen]] borrowed the ''[[Eventide]]'' starship and flew around the immediate vicinity of the ''Paxion''. The pair found Braxen's body floating in space and transported it to the ''Paxion''{{'s}} morgue. Afterward, everyone aboard the ''Alif''-class Longbeam assumed the guard had killed Segaru and considered the incident unfortunate, rendering Ferrol's coverup of his son's actions complete. [[Standard week|Weeks]] later, when Rev and a group of his [[Parent|father's]] Children of E'ronoh took Xiri hostage, she reflected on the blame assigned to Braxen for Segaru's death to give the captain's [[Segaru clan|family]] closure. After confronting her captor for framing the guard, he confirmed participating and revealed he was helped by his father.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|For E'ronoh!|Braxen's last words|<ref name="Convergence" />}}
[[File:Eronoh.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Braxen deeply cared for his homeworld, E'ronoh.]]
The E'roni man was extremely loyal to his homeworld and Monarch, willing to keep fighting for his homeworld despite the rough conditions of military service. He felt strongly that E'ronoh's enemies deserved no mercy and executed orders his superiors gave without hesitation, including launching vicious attacks on Eiram when piloting his drill ship. Braxen was well-acquainted with his peers during his service and had several friends he felt compelled to protect.<ref name="Convergence" />

Although the pilot felt uneasy when receiving his final orders from Ferrol, he hid his emotions out of loyalty to his superiors and completed his last given objective&mdash;to exit the ''Paxion''{{'s}} airlock and sacrifice himself&mdash;without complaint, having been told his actions would save E'ronoh from ruin. Having spent much time in his ship during the war, Braxen despised being in environments he felt were too quiet.<ref name="Convergence" /> By 382 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the soldier had an aged appearance.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a drill ship operator, Braxen was competent in maneuvering the vessels during military engagements and efficiently eliminating targets. By the time of his death, he was a seasoned and experienced soldier who had spent a large portion of his life fighting.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Equipment==
Like every E'roni soldier, Braxen owned a ceremonial [[bane blade]] he kept strapped at his hip. While part of E'ronoh's fleet, he also donned the standard [[Color|red]] [[Clothing|uniform]] worn by his world's military and operated a drill ship during missions.<ref name="Convergence" />
