The '''double-bladed lightsaber''', also called a '''saberstaff''' or '''lightstaff''', was a [[lightsaber]] variant that featured a blade emitted from both ends. Several variants on the type existed, including the [[hinged double-bladed lightsaber]], the [[split saber]], the [[lightsaber pike]], and the [[double-bladed spinning lightsaber]]. According to the mythical [[Qel-Droma Epics]], [[Exar Kun's lightsaber|double-bladed lightsabers]] dated back to a [[Sith Lord]] named [[Exar Kun]].

==Description==
{{Quote|This long, staff-like weapon has a blade on both ends and contains two kyber crystals and twice the amount of internal mechanisms.|[[Luke Skywalker]]|<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>}}
[[File:VentressSabers-TLC.png|thumb|left|250px|Asajj Ventress' dual curved-hilt lightsabers joined together to form a double-bladed saber.]]The double-bladed [[lightsaber]] was, simply, a lightsaber with [[blade emitter]]s on both ends. Such a weapon could be wielded like a typical single-bladed lightsaber if only one blade was ignited.<ref name="Episode I" /> This lightsaber style had several variants: [[hinged double-bladed lightsaber]]s effectively consisted of two single-bladed lightsabers joined with a hinge at the ends. Notable wielders of this saber style included [[Pong Krell]]<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> and the [[Jedi Temple Guard]].<ref name="Shroud of Darkness">{{Rebels|Shroud of Darkness}}</ref> Temple Guards also used [[lightsaber pike]]s, which had a much longer handle and shorter blades compared to typical double-bladed lightsabers.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref>

[[Split saber]]s had handles that detached, allowing their owners to fight with either one double-bladed weapon or two single-bladed weapons as they pleased.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref><ref name="TCW movie">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>

The [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Inquisitorius]] used distinctive [[double-bladed spinning lightsaber]]s, which had relatively short handles and a ring around which the active blades could spin, allowing their wielders to even fly in certain locations.<ref name="TOTA">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref> However, the ring was also a weakness, as cutting it would instantly destroy the weapon. This weakness was used against [[the Grand Inquisitor]] on two occasions, the [[Action at Mustafar|second]] leading to his [[death]].<ref name="DVDLOTS 6">[[Darth Vader (2017) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 6]]</ref><ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:The_High_Republic_8_unlettered_cover.png|thumb|left|220px|Orla Jareni and Keeve Trennis, Jedi of the High Republic who wielded double-bladed lightsabers]]
The existence of [[Exar Kun's lightsaber|double-bladed lightsabers]] dated back to the mythical [[Sith Lord]] [[Exar Kun]] of the [[Qel-Droma Epics]].<ref name="G&G">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>

During the [[High Republic Era]], the Jedi Master and [[Wayseeker]] [[Orla Jareni]] wielded a hinged double-bladed lightsaber that produced white blades.<ref name="The High Republic: Into the Dark">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref> Meanwhile, Jedi Knight [[Keeve Trennis]] wielded a green [[Keeve Trennis' lightsaber|double-bladed lightsaber]] of her own.<ref name="The High Republic (2021) 1">[[The High Republic (2021) 1|''The High Republic'' (2021) 1]]</ref>

The [[Dathomirian]] [[Zabrak]] [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] [[Maul]] wielded a [[Color|red]] [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|double-bladed lightsaber]] he created himself.<ref name="Episode I" /><ref name="Maul Lightsaber DB">{{Databank|darth-mauls-lightsaber|Darth Maul's Lightsaber}}</ref> Maul's lightsaber was damaged when he was defeated by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] during the [[Battle of Naboo]].<ref name="Episode I" /> [[Savage Opress]], Maul's brother, also wielded a red [[Savage Opress's lightsaber|double-bladed lightsaber]] he received from [[Count]] [[Dooku]] during his brief stint as the Sith Lord's [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]].<ref name="Witches of the Mist">{{TCW|Witches of the Mist}}</ref> When Opress recovered Maul from the junk [[planet]] [[Lotho Minor]], Maul only had one half of his old lightsaber. He wielded it as a single-bladed lightsaber for a time,<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> but by the time of the [[Siege of Mandalore]], he had rebuilt it into a double-bladed weapon. However, he lost the saber when he was captured by [[Ahsoka Tano]] during the Siege.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref> Maul would recover his weapon and by his time on [[Janix]], the lightsaber was modified<ref name="Shadow Lord Trivia">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-trivia|text=Inside the Making of ''Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord''}}</ref> to also separate into two weapons.<ref name="Whispers in the Unknown">{{MSL|Chapter 3: Whispers in the Unknown}}</ref>

A weapon that previously belonged to a [[Unidentified Padawan (Dooku)|different apprentice]],<ref name="LaJE">{{EncyclopediaCite|Lightsabers and Jedi Equipment}}</ref> Count Dooku's personal [[assassin]] and apprentice [[Asajj Ventress]] had a [[Asajj Ventress's lightsabers|pair]] of curved-hilt lightsabers that could be joined together at the hilt into a single weapon by a locking mechanism, becoming a double-bladed lightsaber. However, she rarely wielded her sabers in that fashion.<ref name="TCW Film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> This was a configuration known as a split saber, one that was also fielded by [[Jedi]] such as [[Cal Kestis]],<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> who built a [[Cal Kestis' second lightsaber|double-bladed lightsaber]] from the pieces of his master [[Jaro Tapal]]'s [[Jaro Tapal's lightsaber|broken double-bladed lightsaber]], which Kestis had used as a single-bladed weapon after Tapal's death and the loss of his own lightsaber, combining what was salvageable from Tapal's weapon with pieces of his new master [[Cere Junda]]'s lightsaber.

[[File:PongKrellsLightsaber-CM.png|thumb|right|220px|Pong Krell wielding dual hinged double-bladed lightsabers.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Besalisk]] [[Jedi Master]] Pong Krell wielded [[Pong Krell's lightsabers|dual-hinged double-bladed lightsabers]], one blue-bladed and one green-bladed, which could fold at the middle.<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> The Lasat Jedi Master [[Jaro Tapal]] also made use of a double-bladed lightsaber throughout the Clone Wars and to defend himself and his Padawan during the execution of Order 66, though the weapon was sundered in half by clone blasterfire.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order" /> The Jedi Temple Guard wielded a similar weapon, the yellow double-bladed lightsaber pike.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" /> A yellow double-bladed lightsaber was used by the [[Zygerrian]] Jedi Master [[Tosan]].<ref name="Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> A blue double-bladed lightsaber was also used by a [[Unidentified human Jedi (Zondula)|human Jedi]] that was present for the [[Massacre on Zondula]].<ref name="Inquisitors 4">[[Inquisitors 4|''Inquisitors'' 4]]</ref>

The Imperial [[Inquisitor]]s, [[dark side adept]]s tasked at identifying and hunting down [[Jedi]] following [[Order 66]], fought using their unique version of the weapon. Their red double-bladed spinning lightsabers could also be used as regular single-bladed lightsabers.<ref name="Rebels Visual Guide" />

[[File:DarkReyLightsaberIgnited-TLC.png|thumb|left|300px|The hinged double-bladed lightsaber of Rey's vision]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], Maul used [[Maul's lightsaber|another double-bladed lightsaber]], which could be disguised as a [[Sabercane|cane]] and bore strong visual similarities to the lightsabers wielded by members of the [[Inquisitorius]].<ref name="TOTA">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref> Maul wielded this lightsaber until his [[death]] on Tatooine at the hands of his old enemy Kenobi a few years before the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Twin Suns">{{Rebels|Twin Suns (episode)}}</ref>

While searching for the [[wayfinder]] on [[Kef Bir]] in [[35 ABY]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] fought a [[Force vision|vision]] of herself as a Sith. In the vision, her dark side counterpart wielded a hinged double-bladed lightsaber with red blades.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>


==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DarthMaulLightsaber-SW100Objects.png|thumb|right|300px|The prop of Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber used in ''The Phantom Menace''.]]
The double-bladed lightsaber first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Episode I" /> [[Double-bladed lightsaber/Legends|It]] was originally conceived by writer [[Tom Veitch]] and artist [[Chris Gossett]] for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (comic series)|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'']].<ref name="TotJ:TSW3:TToUQ-D">[[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 3|''Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War'' 3]]</ref> Gossett has said he thought of [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsabers]] as the personal "honor weapons" of the Jedi, and would be more customized to suit personal preference rather than standardized for all.<ref name="CGinterview">[[Wookieepedia:Interview/Christian Gossett|Interview with Christian Gossett]]</ref>

During the development of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], screenwriter [[Michael Arndt]] became really interested in the motif of "fire and ice" and wanted to have one character wielding a double-bladed lightsaber, with one side red and the other blue.<ref name="TAoSW:TFA">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>
