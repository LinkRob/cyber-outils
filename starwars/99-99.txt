'''99-99''',<ref name="Name">{{SW|url=the-ninth-jedi-concept-art-gallery|text="The Ninth Jedi" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> nicknamed '''Four-Nines''', was a [[droid]] companion to [[Lah Kara]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Hy Izlan]]. The droid was capable of self-propelled flight and of firing blaster bolts that Kara could deflect with a [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Ninth Jedi" />
