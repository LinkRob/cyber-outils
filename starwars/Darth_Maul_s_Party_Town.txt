'''Darth Maul's Party Town''' was a spot set up<ref name="LSW:DMM" /> in [[32 BBY/Legends|32 BBY]]<ref name="ERC" /> in the outskirts of [[Mos Espa/Legends|Mos Espa]], a [[City/Legends|city]] on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]]. The [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]] established his Party Town next to [[Monarch of Naboo/Legends|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Amidala's]] [[J-type 327 Nubian royal starship/Legends|Royal Starship]]. When [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn/Legends|Qui-Gon Jinn]] arrived, he was distracted by the scene, as his [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was lounging, the young [[Gender/Legends|boy]] [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] was playing in the sand, and the Queen was playing a game. The [[Droid/Legends|droid]] [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] served drinks for everyone. Maul himself was using his [[Darth Maul's lightsaber|lightsaber]] as a limbo bar for [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Quarsh Panaka/Legends|Quarsh Panaka]]. After Jinn arrived, Maul launched into a duel with the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]].<ref name="LSW:DMM" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Maul's Party Town appears in the [[2012]] young readers book, ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Darth Maul's Mission]]'', a humorous re-telling of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]''.
