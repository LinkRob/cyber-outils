The '''Duros''' were a [[humanoid]] [[species]] common in the [[galaxy]] native to the [[planet]] [[Duro]]. Notable Duros included [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] who operated during the [[Clone Wars]] and [[Shriv Suurgav]], a Duros who was a member of the [[New Republic]] [[Inferno Squad]], and later the [[Resistance]] [[Inferno Squad (Resistance)|Inferno Squad]].

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:CadBane-BoBFADVG.png|thumb|left|180px|Cad Bane, a Duros bounty hunter active in the galaxy for decades.]]
The Duros were a [[humanoid]] [[species]]<ref name="Duros">{{Databank|duros|Duros}}</ref> [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Duro]],<ref name="Duro" /> a [[planet]]<ref name="Cad Bane">{{Databank|cad-bane|Cad Bane}}</ref> located in the [[Duro sector]]<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Duro" /> They had smooth [[Color|blue]]-green skin, red [[eye]]s, lipless [[mouth]]s, and long thin [[nose]]less faces<ref name="Duros" /> with green [[blood]].<ref name="Children of the Force">{{TCW|Children of the Force}}</ref>
When the Duros experienced high amounts of strain, a kind of oil&mdash;which had a highly irritating smell and bad taste&mdash;gathered in the sacs along their ribs. This was an evolutionary throwback and defense mechanism, remaining from when the Duros were liable to be eaten by a number of entities roaming Duro.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
They were related to the similar-looking [[Neimoidian]] species, having sufficiently diverged in physical appearance by [[15,000 BBY]] to be distinguished.<ref name="BMF 52">{{BuildFalconCite|52|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>

==History==
The Duros were one of the first cultures to develop [[hyperdrive]]-equipped [[Starship|ships]], and as such, could be found all over [[the galaxy]] and were described as adventurous.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> A colonial species, they had migrated and adapted to dominate many worlds.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> "Let us become [[spear]]s" was a ritual phrase within Duros culture.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> After the [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Duro became one of the first planets in the Core Worlds to be under Imperial control. Many Duros would become fugitives from their homeworld, and many joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Duros in the galaxy==
[[File:OhwunChachiDeMaal-SWCT.png|thumb|right|250px|Ohwun and Chachi De Maal in Chalmun's cantina on Tatooine]]
[[Cad Bane]] was a [[Gender|male]] Duros [[bounty hunter]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Holocron Heist">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref> who was regarded as one of the best bounty hunters in the [[galaxy]] following the [[death]] of [[Jango Fett]].<ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref> He grew up as an impovrished orpahn on the streets of<ref name="Good Life">{{TOTU|The Good Life}}</ref> Duro<ref name="DurounderworldID">{{SWCT|set=widevision 2025|cardname=Duro}}</ref> together with his friend [[Niro]].<ref name="Good Life" /> Bane would continue his work as a bounty hunter into and after the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Reunion">{{TBB|Reunion}}</ref><ref name="From the Desert Comes a Stranger">{{BOBF|Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger}}</ref>

Multiple [[Force-sensitive]] Duros joined the [[Jedi Order]], including [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] [[Jedi Master|member]] [[Rana Kant]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Te'Ami]],<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />, [[Jedi Master]] [[Wishan]]<ref name="JTC-E1">{{JTC|1}}</ref> and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Fvorn]].<ref name="Inquisitor">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref> Another Force-sensitive Duros joined the [[Sith]] and became a [[Sith Lord]] named [[Darth Caldoth]].<ref name="Gaze of Stone" />

[[Ohwun De Maal]] and his wife, [[Chachi De Maal|Chachi]], resided in [[Mos Eisley]] on [[Tatooine]]. They owned several [[Hangar|docking bays]] in the spaceport including [[Docking Bay 94]], where [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] berthed the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''<ref name="SWFC">''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]''</ref> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>

Other Duros joined the Alliance to Restore the Republic including [[Shriv Suurgav]], who led [[Danger Squadron]] and would later join [[Inferno Squad]] as part of the [[New Republic]] and [[Resistance]]<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Another Duros in the Alliance to Restore the Republic was [[L'ulo L'ampar]], a member of [[Starlight Squadron]]<ref name="Star Wars 10">[[Star Wars (2020) 10|''Star Wars'' (2020) 10]]</ref> and [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]],<ref name="Shattered Empire 1">[[Shattered Empire 1|''Shattered Empire'' 1]]</ref> who would later join [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] in the Resistance.<ref name="Poe Dameron 1">[[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]]</ref>
[[Norath Kev]] was a Duros who joined the Resistance as a [[spy]].<ref name="The Missing Agent">{{Resistance|The Missing Agent}}</ref>

The [[Lost King of Duro]]'s hand was mummified. [[Standard year|Years]] later [[Dryden Vos]] kept it as part of his [[Dryden's study|artifact collection]] onboard his personal [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]''.<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
