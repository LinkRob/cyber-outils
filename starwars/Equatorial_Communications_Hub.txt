The '''Equatorial Communications Hub''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] communications station on the [[planet]]
[[Ryloth]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The Hub was commanded by [[Major]] [[Steen Borkas]] in [[14 BBY]]. During a [[Mission to Ryloth|plot]] to [[assassin]]ate [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], members of the [[Free Ryloth Movement]] including [[Isval]] bombed the station's [[satellite]] dishes in an attempt to hamper Imperial rescue efforts. Despite this, Borkas and his men managed to repair the station's dishes. One satellite, [[Dish 3]], survived the assault. This enabled [[Moff]] [[Delian Mors]]' forces to reinforce the Emperor and Vader before the Free Ryloth movement could [[Death|kill]] them.<ref name="LOTS" />
