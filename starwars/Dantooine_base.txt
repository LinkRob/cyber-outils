The '''Dantooine base''' was a base operated in the jungles of [[Dantooine]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]]. By the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Scarif]], the base had been abandoned.

==Description==
The base was built in a dense [[forest]] on the far side of [[Dantooine]] from [[Dantoo Town]]. It was disguised as a [[sap]] [[farm]].<ref name="The Trigger">{{StoryCite|story=The Trigger|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> The first structures erected were prefabricated [[Barracks|living modules]], which were soon supplemented with defenses including weapons stolen from [[smuggler]] outposts and an [[Deflector shield|energy shield]] taken from an Imperial prison.<ref name="BMF 37">{{BuildFalconCite|37|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> The base contained a vast enclosure that could house dozens of small [[starfighter]]s, dozens of [[droid]]-charging ports, advanced communications technology, and dozens of [[bunk]]s for several hundred [[troop]]s. At one point, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance]] troops planned to expand the base, starting to dig underground extensively.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>

[[General]] [[Antoc Merrick]] was in overall command of the starfighter squadrons on Dantooine.<ref name="BuildXwing10">{{BuildXWingCite|10|Starfighter Aces}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Our scout ships have reached Dantooine. They found the remains of a Rebel base, but they estimate that it has been deserted for some time. They're now conducting an extensive search of the surrounding systems.|Officer [[Siward Cass]]|audio=Cass-ANH.flac|<ref name="ANH" />}}
The Dantooine base was the first rebel base established by the Alliance to Restore the Republic.<ref name="Dantooine Databank">{{Databank|dantooine|Dantooine}}</ref> [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] was based there before being transferred to the Massassi Temple on Yavin following the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance]] by [[Mon Mothma]]<ref name="BuildXwing40">{{BuildXWingCite|40|Starfighter Aces}}</ref> in [[2 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

Although the base was originally intended to be only temporary, more and more facilities were constructed as the Alliance grew. At some point prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]], the pilot [[Ryle Torsyn]] found an Imperial tracking device hidden in an equipment delivery. Not taking any risks, the device was destroyed and Dantooine base was evacuated,<ref name="BMF 37" /> with the headquarters moving to an [[Great Temple|ancient temple]] on [[Yavin 4]], a base already used by [[Jan Dodonna]] and his [[Massassi Group|rebel cell]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> Furthermore, organizers [[Bail Prestor Organa]] and Mothma had already intended for the Yavin base to be the Alliance's eventual headquarters<ref name="Welcome">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref> despite the usage of the Dantooine base. Despite the tracker, the former base on Dantooine was still unknown to the Empire.<ref name="BMF 37 />

Some time later, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] was captured by [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]], who kept her [[prisoner]] on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. When Tarkin threatened the princess with destroying her [[homeworld]] if she did not give up the location of the Alliance's base, Organa attempted to fool the Grand Moff by naming Dantooine. Tarkin, however, ordered [[Destruction of Alderaan|the destruction]] of Alderaan anyway.<ref name="ANH" />

An Imperial survey team<ref name="Lost Stars" /> consisting of several [[scout ship]]s<ref name="ANH" /> and a [[Star Destroyer]], led by [[Cassio Tagge]],<ref name="The Trigger" /> was dispatched to Dantooine to investigate the princess' claim. Although the Imperials did find the remains of the Alliance base, they rightly estimated that it had been evacuated some time ago and began to scout surrounding [[star system]]s.<ref name="ANH" /> However, they did capture [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], who was searching the base for salvage. Believing her to be trouble, Tagge ordered her execution, but she escaped.<ref name="The Trigger" />
