'''Cibaba''' was an [[Azumel]] [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Stationed on [[Alaris Prime]], Cibaba trained the [[Tholothian]] [[Padawan]] [[Ty Yorrick|Tylera Yorrick]], as well as her friend [[Klias Teradine]]. Yorrick left the Jedi Order after an incident with Teradine at the [[Yallow Shrine]], but continued to remember her old master.

==Biography==
===Training Ty Yorrick===
Cibaba was an [[Azumel]] who served as a [[Jedi Master]] in the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. At one point, he took the [[Tholothian]] [[Ty Yorrick|Tylera Yorrick]] as his [[Padawan]].<ref name="MoTP 1" /> During her apprenticeship, Cibaba taught Yorrick how to construct her own [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Rising Storm" /> Cibaba, along with Yorrick, were stationed at the [[Jedi temple]] on [[Alaris Prime]].<ref name="MoTP 1" />

Yorrick was friends with the [[Zeltron]] Padawan [[Klias Teradine]], who was known to be arrogant and prideful. Cibaba, aware of what Teradine thought of himself, attempted to remind him that Jedi were supposed to show humility. On one occasion<ref name="MoTP 1" /> in [[241 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashback events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak]]'' to [[241 BBY]].</ref> Cibaba found Yorrick and Teradine just after Yorrick had won a sparring bout against her friend. In response to Teradine commenting that it looked like Yorrick was picking up some of his brilliance, Cibaba pointedly asked Teradine to remember what he had been taught about Jedi humility, but Teradine responded boastfully that he was exceptionally good at it. Yorrick told Cibaba that Teradine was kidding, but quietly asked him to tell the Master that. Cibaba reassured Yorrick that he knew what Teradine thought of himself, and told her that she needed to find her own path instead of imitating someone else's.<ref name="MoTP 1" />

Teradine later discovered the location of<ref name="MoTP 1" /> the [[Yallow Shrine]],<ref name="Rising Storm" /> a sanctum of the [[Yallow Fellowship]], an extinct sect of [[Dark side of the Force|darksiders]], and attempted to persuade Yorrick to come investigate it with him. Yorrick was uncertain, knowing that Cibaba would not approve, but Teradine persuaded her to come along by in part suggesting that she was too scared to do so, and further suggesting that Cibaba did not need to know.<ref name="MoTP 1" />

The incident at the shrine led to Yorrick leaving the Jedi Order and becoming a [[mercenary]] and monster hunter known as Ty Yorrick. About a decade after leaving the Order,<ref name="Rising Storm" /> Yorrick was reminded of Cibaba after her [[Administrative droid|admin unit]], [[KL-03]], made a remark to her that reminded her of Teradine.<ref name="MoTP 1" /> During a hunt on [[Safrifa]], Yorrick was reminded of her old master when she thought that they would not have approved of the spikes she had added to her lightsaber hilt during her time as a mercenary. When the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Valo Atrocity|attacked]] the [[Republic Fair]] on [[Valo]], Yorrick found herself teaming up with the Jedi Master [[Elzar Mann]]. Sharing memories in order to establish trust, Mann [[Force vision|saw]] Cibaba and Teradine briefly, learning their names.<ref name="Rising Storm" />

===Child of the Storm===
Eventually, Cibaba became a [[Jedi seeker]].<ref name="Saber for Hire 2">[[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 2|''The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire'' 2]]</ref> At some point Cibaba was captured by the Nihil and brought to [[Boolan]], who tortured him<ref name="Saber for Hire 4" /> and, by [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire]] take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> Cibaba had become a member of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Children of the Storm]],<ref name="Saber for Hire 3" /> [[Jedi hunter]]s who captured [[Force-sensitive]]s for [[Ithorian]] [[scientist]] [[Baron]] [[Boolan]].<ref name="Boolan Databank">{{Databank|baron-boolan|Baron Boolan}}</ref> That year,<ref name="Date" /> Cibaba attempted to capture the [[Altiri]] [[Lene Kostana]], and encountered his former Padawan, Ty Yorrick.<ref name="Saber for Hire 3" /> Cibaba fought his old Padawan and defeated her, but when he was about to kill her, [[Lene Kostana]] came forward, and revealed that Ty had run away after [[Klias Teradine]]'s death because she was afraid of Cibaba's judgement. Cibaba saw in Lene his old Padawan and, overcome by shame, let Ty go and asked her to kill him. Ty refused and instead brought him back to [[Coruscant]] so that he could get the help he needed.<ref name="Saber for Hire 4" />

===Rehabilitation===
Cibaba was eventually rehabilitated and was sent to join the [[Stormwall Defense Fleet]]<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 1" /> but due to the trauma he suffered as a [[Children of the Storm|Child of the Storm]] [[Keeve Trennis]] was reluctant to have him back in the field so soon.<ref name= "Fear of the Jedi 2">[[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 2|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 2]]</ref> When [[H'Tar]] used her abilities to project the fears of the members of the fleet Cibaba used his connection with her to sense that she was inside of a [[Fear Engine]].<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 4">[[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 4|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 4]]</ref> Cibaba convinced her to give up her location and wanted to rescue her but [[Lourna Dee]] took the opportunity and destroyed the ship she was on.<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 5" />

==Personality and traits==
Cibaba frequently lectured Klias Teradine on his lack of humility, considering it unbefitting of a Jedi. They also encouraged their apprentice, Tylera Yorrick, to find her own path instead of trying to be someone she was not.<ref name="MoTP 1" /> Ty Yorrick later felt that Cibaba would not have approved of the spikes she added to her lightsaber hilt during her time as a mercenary.<ref name="Rising Storm" /> When Cibaba crossed paths with Tylera years later, he expressed curiosity about the capabilities of the individual that she had taken on as her own student, [[Drewen Qweebjillan]].<ref name="Saber for Hire 4" /> He was an Azumel with [[Color|yellow]] skin and green [[eye]]s.<ref name="MoTP 1" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a former Jedi Master Cibaba was trained in the art of lightsaber construction, teaching their apprentice to build her own weapon.<ref name="Rising Storm" /> He was able to form a connection with his Padawan, [[Ty Yorrick]], that allowed them to see each other's thoughts.<ref name="Saber for Hire 2" /> He also possessed strong telekinetic abilities that he used to torture [[Lene Kostana]]'s parents.<ref name="Saber for Hire 3" /> He was also a proficient lightsaber duelist able to match his former Padawan, [[Ty Yorrick]].<ref name="Saber for Hire 4" /> Cibaba was linked to the other members of the [[Children of the Storm]] and was able to sense them and their feelings through the Force.<ref name= "Fear of the Jedi 4" />
