==Development==
Almost two years passed from ''Ahsoka'' Season 1's announcement in 2020 to its beginning of production in 2022, resulting in what some call a "lengthy preproduction phase."<ref name="Cinema Blend">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cinemablend.com/star-wars/star-wars-ahsoka-series-celebrates-filming-start-with-first-behind-the-scenes-pic-and-its-thankfully-not-on-tatooine|text=Star Wars' Ahsoka Series Celebrates Filming Start With First Behind The Scenes Pic, And It's Thankfully Not On Tatooine|author=Adam Holmes|date=2022-05-09|work=[https://www.cinemablend.com Cinema Blend]|archivedate=20240419222547}}</ref> Casting began as early as 2021,<ref name="Comic Book Resources">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cbr.com/ahsoka-rosario-dawson-confirm-ezra-thrawn-cast|text=Ahsoka: Rosario Dawson Confirms Ezra, Thrawn Casting Reports|author=Samuel Gelman|date=2021-07-28|work=[[Wikipedia:Comic Book Resources|''Comic Book Resources'']]|archivedate=20220707063719}}</ref> but the full cast was not confirmed until 2023.<ref name="USA Today">{{WebCite|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/tv/2023/08/20/ahsoka-release-date-time-cast/70612249007|text=How to watch 'Ahsoka' premiere: new release date, start time; see cast of 'Star Wars' show|author=Francisco Guzman|date=2023-08-20|work=[[Wikipedia:USA Today|''USA Today'']]|archivedate=20240203182524}}</ref>

In developing stories for Season 1, co-creator Dave Filoni drew from ''The Lord of the Rings'' for inspiration. He wanted Ahsoka to go through a Gandalf-like transformation such as: meandering through the universe, finding her place in it by experiencing a metaphorical death, and finally coming back even stronger.<ref name="Screen Rant">{{WebCite|url=https://screenrant.com/why-ahsoka-is-star-wars-gandalf-according-to-dave-filoni|text=Why Ahsoka Is Star Wars' Gandalf (According To Dave Filoni)|author=Marcelo Leite|date=2023-01-05|work=[[Wikipedia:Screen Rant|''Screen Rant'']]|archivedate=20230730213858}}</ref>
