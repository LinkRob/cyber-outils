"'''A Most Deadly Hunt'''" is a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure outline in the [[September]] [[1996]] ''[[Shadows of the Empire Planets Guide]]'', published by [[West End Games]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' as part of the ''[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire]]'' multimedia project. As a sequel to the preceding two outlines, ''[[A Case of Mistaken Identities]]'' and ''[[An Invitation to Defect]]'', the adventure tasks [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] players with capturing two wild [[krak'jya]] beasts so that a [[Bothan/Legends|Bothan]] [[clan]] can add them to their zoo and thus increase their status within Bothan society.
