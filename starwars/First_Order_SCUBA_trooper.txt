'''First Order SCUBA troopers''',<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|first-order-scuba-troopers|First Order SCUBA troopers}}</ref> also known as '''First Order underwater stormtroopers'''<ref name="No escape part 1 Bucket's list">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-no-escape-part-1-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 8 Fun Facts from "No Escape: Part 1" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref> or simply '''SCUBA troopers''',<ref name="Databank" /> were [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] of the [[First Order]] that were specialized to operate in [[Water|aquatic]] environments. SCUBA troopers were deployed to the ''[[Colossus]]'' and subsequently defeated during the First Order's [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupation]]. They were armed with a type of underwater [[blaster]] that had a flashlight on the end.<ref name="No Escape 1" />

==Equipment==
These troopers were equipped with flippers, a breathing apparatus, and underwater blaster rifles that look similar to [[DC-12U beam rifle]].<ref name="Databank" />

==Behind the scenes==
First Order scuba troopers first appeared in the second half of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated TV series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', in the episode "[[No Escape: Part 1]]." They were first seen in the season one mid-season trailer released on YouTube.<ref name="Mid-season trailer">{{SWYouTube|m7Pls-vYWwM|Star Wars Resistance Season 1 - Mid-Season Trailer (Official)|sw_url=video/season-1-mid-season-trailer-star-wars-resistance}}</ref>
