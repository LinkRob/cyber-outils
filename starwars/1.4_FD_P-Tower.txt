The '''1.4 FD P-Tower''', also known simply as a '''P-Tower''' was a light fixed anti-vehicle weapon emplacement manufactured by the [[Atgar SpaceDefense Corporation]].<ref name="Ultimate" /> It held enough power to disable an [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]], and its rugged dependability meant it could function in even the harshest environments.<ref name="Planets Battlefront">{{EA|subdomain=starwars|url=starwars/battlefront/planets|text=''Star Wars'' Battlefront Planets|nolive=1}}</ref>

The tower functioned by having its single [[laser cannon]] fire from the center of the energy dish, which had 16 micropower routers evenly spaced along the edge of eight power conversion cells along the interior. Despite its ability to offer soldiers a 360° field of fire due to a rotating base, along with operational capability in temperatures ranging from -73°C to 49°C, it was inexpensive, quick and easy to produce.<ref name="Ultimate" />

==History==
[[File:Atgar_Turret_DICE.png|thumb|left|200px|1.4 FD P-Towers were used during the Battle of Hoth.]]
The P-Tower was used throughout the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Star Wars: Galactic Defense">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref> and the [[Galactic Civil War]] on [[Planet|worlds]] such as [[Hoth]], [[Tatooine]], and [[Endor]].<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref> They were used by [[Alliance gunner]]s of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Battle of Hoth]], though they proved ineffective at penetrating the armor of the [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Episode V" /> The emplacement was eventually withdrawn from service.<ref name="EHWaUE" />
