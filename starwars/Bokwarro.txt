'''Bokwarro''' was a [[Wookiee]] [[Gender|male]] [[pirate]] active during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] who was a member of the [[Starhowl Pirate]]s. His preferred weapon of choice was a [[bowcaster]]. He was [[Death|killed]] in an encounter with [[The Smuggler]] who was sent by [[Shelish]] to "incapacitate" the Starhowl Pirates.<ref name="Uprising" />
