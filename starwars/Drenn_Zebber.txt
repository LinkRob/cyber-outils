'''Drenn Zebber''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Twi'lek/Legends|Twi'lek]] who stayed on [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yaivn 4]] during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="AED" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drenn Zebber appears in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']]. According to these files, he would have been on [[W:c:swg:Yavin IV|Yavin 4]]. However, he is not known to have appeared on the [[Star Wars video games|video game]]'s live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]]. Nevertheless, an emulated depiction pulled from this character's Interchange File Format reveals what he would have looked like if he were added to the game.<ref name="AED" />
