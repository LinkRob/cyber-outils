'''Concussive absorbing material''' was a type of physical product that could be included in clothes. In [[32 BBY]], some of [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s wardrobe was made of the material; during the [[Invasion of Naboo]] one of the [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens]] used it to create headbands for themselves as protection.<ref name="Queen's Peril">''[[Queen's Peril]]''</ref>
