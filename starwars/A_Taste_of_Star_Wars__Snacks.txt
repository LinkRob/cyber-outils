'''''A Taste of Star Wars: Snacks''''' is a cookbook of ''[[Star Wars]]''-inspired baking recipes that was released on [[May 6]], [[2025]] published by [[Insight Editions]].<ref name="Edelweiss" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Experience a taste of a galaxy far, far away with this pocket-sized cookbook filled with the most delicious and iconic snack recipes from Insight Editions’ bestselling Star Wars cookbooks.''

''Celebrate your love of all things Star Wars when you make and enjoy snacks from this bite-sized cookbook! Fans of all ages, appetites, and dietary needs will find new favorites among these recipes inspired by the most lovable heroes, fearsome villains, and majestic planets of the Star Wars galaxy.''

''BITE-SIZE AND DELICIOUS: Twenty-six scrumptious snacks will take your taste buds on a mini tour of the galaxy from Bespin to Mon Cala to Endor and more, visiting beloved characters such as Grogu, Darth Maul, and Ahsoka along the way. Celebrate Life Day over Wookiee-Ookiees, pay tribute to Darth Vader with some hot Mustafarian Lava Buns, fuel up for your next mission with Omega’s Popcorn Mix, and so many more tasty treasures from a galaxy far, far away.''

''GREAT GIFT: These sweet and savory snacks are perfect for holidays, parties, birthdays, movie marathons, study sessions, and more. Quick and easy recipes can be rustled up for any casual gathering, while more challenging showstoppers will really wow your guests. This handy guide for Star Wars-loving cooks and foodies is a delight to give and to receive.''

''BEAUTIFUL AND CUTE: Just like Grogu, this fully licensed, bite-sized cookbook is short and sweet, the perfect size to carry around everywhere. It also features mouth-watering photography of delicious recipes.''<ref name="Edelweiss" />
