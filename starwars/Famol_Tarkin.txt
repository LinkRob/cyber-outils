[[Lord]] '''Famol Tarkin''' was a [[Gender|male]] member of the [[Tarkin family]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Famol&mdash;along with [[Grand Dame]] [[Veermol Tarkin]]&mdash;had two [[human]] [[Youngling|children]], the [[twin]]s [[Navaj Tarkin|Navaj]] and [[Sevran Tarkin]].<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" />

In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 9|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 9]]|one [[Standard year|year]] and a few [[Standard month|months]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> Sevran mentioned her [[Parent|father]] when she attempted to recognize the [[Zygerrian]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Farzala Tarabal]] to address<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 10">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 10|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 10]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Eriadu]]'s ruling council&mdash;the [[Quintad]]&mdash;in the [[Seswenna Hall]] in [[Eriadu City]]. As Sevran had been previously declared [[Death|dead]] and the process to restore her status in the family was still ongoing, however, the endorsement proved meaningless. Not long later, Navaj arrived at the hall and mentioned his father during his successful recognition of Tarabal to speak to the Quintad.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" />

==Behind the scenes==
Famol Tarkin was mentioned in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 9|ninth issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" /> on [[August 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-802/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-9|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #9}}</ref>
