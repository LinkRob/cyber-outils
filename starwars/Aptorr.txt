'''Aptorr''' was a [[Yarkora]] [[Gender|male]] who owned [[Aptorr Industries]] on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]]. He lived with his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Varralis]], who was also his business partner. Aptorr began training a [[Wookiee]], [[Gartabba]], to operate his [[Aptorr's factory|plant]]&mdash;keeping his actions secret as his products were advertised as Yarkora-made&mdash;and his frequent absence caused Varralis to feel neglected. When Varralis approached the [[Jedi Master]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] on [[Langdam Key]], Aptorr assumed the [[Jedi]] was interfering in his relationship and confronted him. However, Ki-Adi-Mundi was able to handle the disagreement between the couple, leading Aptorr to reveal the truth.<ref name="The Living Force" />
