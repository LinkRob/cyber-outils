"'''A Change Of Fortune'''" is an adventure seed contained in the ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]''. After winning big on the [[Gambling/Legends|gambling]] tables aboard a [[Luxury liner/Legends|luxury liner]], a group of [[Spacer/Legends|spacers]] have to survive long enough to get off the ship with their winnings. Possible alternate storylines that could be adapted by a gamemaster were also included. It was written by [[Bill Olmesdahl]].
