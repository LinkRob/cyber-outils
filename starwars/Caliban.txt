{{Quote|Your bridge will allow us to freely move weapons and troops across this planet&mdash;another victory for the Separatist cause. You ''lose'', Kenobi. And now you and your men will die here, alone on this frozen tundra.|Grievous on Caliban|<ref name="BattleTales5" />}}
'''Caliban''' was an [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] with a [[Temperature|frigid]] climate. Its [[Water|snow]]-covered frozen tundra, [[mountain]]s, and ravines were [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Qubey]] [[species]]. In [[22 BBY]], during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the [[Galactic Republic]], Caliban was the site of a [[Battle on Caliban|battle]], in which the Separatist [[General]] [[Grievous]] captured [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his [[clone trooper]]s and took the [[Jedi]]'s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber|lightsaber]].

To comply with the Separatists' demands, the Jedi and his [[212th Attack Battalion|forces]] began rebuilding a [[lunar ice bridge]] that Grievous had destroyed during his [[Invasion of Caliban|invasion]] of the moon. However, with the help of the Qubey, Kenobi reacquired his [[lightsaber]] from the [[cyborg]]. After the bridge's completion, Kenobi confronted Grievous in a [[lightsaber duel]] and [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to bury the Separatist in an avalanche. As he and his [[trooper]]s left the moon on a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|gunship]], the Jedi ordered the destruction of Caliban's lunar ice bridge so their enemies could not benefit from it.

==Description==
{{Quote|&hellip;don't even mention the word ''cold''. It's hard enough keeping my mind off of how freezing I am.|Clone trooper [[Gearshift]] on Caliban|<ref name="BattleTales5" />}}
Caliban was a [[Temperature|frigid]] [[moon]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]'<ref name="BattleTales5" /> [[Savareen sector]]. The moon was situated in the [[Caliban system]], which was within grid square Q-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Caliban's terrain consisted of vast frozen tundra stretches, [[Color|gray]] [[stone]] [[mountain]]s covered in [[Water|ice]] and snow, and deep ravines. A [[Star|sun]] was visible from Caliban's surface, and its light made the [[cloud]]s in the moon's sky appear orange.<ref name="BattleTales5" /> The moon featured a Type I [[atmosphere]]<ref name="Atmosphere" /> breathable to multiple [[species]].<ref name="BattleTales5" />

==History==
===A chilly engagement===
{{Quote|You are my ''prisoners''. And if you care to live for even one more day, you'll do exactly as ''I'' command. You will get to work immediately building a lunar ice bridge, and if you complete my task, perhaps you'll one day leave this frozen wasteland.|Grievous, to Obi-Wan Kenobi and his clone troopers|<ref name="BattleTales5" />}}
[[File:Caliban1-TCWBattleTales5.png|thumb|left|270px|Grievous captured Obi-Wan Kenobi and his clone troopers on Caliban.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the main events of [[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 5|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 5]] and its flashbacks to [[22 BBY]].</ref> Caliban was the site of a [[Battle on Caliban|battle]] of the [[Clone Wars]], fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. While [[Invasion of Caliban|invading]] the moon, the Separatist [[General]] [[Grievous]] destroyed the moon's [[lunar ice bridge]] that connected one side of the moon to the other, which left one [[Unidentified Qubey|Qubey]] [[Homeworld|native]] separated from the rest of their group. Eventually, Grievous captured the Republic's [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and several of his [[clone trooper]]s in the conflict, taking the [[Jedi]]'s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber|lightsaber]] and having [[B1-series battle droid]]s surround the group. Grievous then ordered Kenobi and the [[trooper]]s to reconstruct the lunar ice bridge that [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]] had destroyed. When Grievous threatened to [[Death|kill]] them if they did not comply, the Jedi countered that forcing [[prisoner]]s to work violated the [[Galactic Accord of Systems|Galactic Accords]],<ref name="BattleTales5" /> an established [[The galaxy|galactic]] convention dictating rules of engagement in conflicts.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> In response, however, Grievous only hit Kenobi and insulted him, ignoring his complaint.<ref name="BattleTales5" />

[[File:Caliban2-TCWBattleTales5.png|thumb|right|270px|Kenobi and his troopers begin constructing the lunar bridge at Grievous's demand.]]
Guided by a [[Hologram|holographic]] diagram of the structure they needed to build, the Jedi and his troopers used the provided materials to begin reconstructing the lunar ice bridge over a deep ravine. The laboring prisoners devised a plan to [[Saboteur|sabotage]] the bridge and trap Grievous and his forces on one side after escaping across it. However, Kenobi discovered shortly afterward that one of the moon's [[Qubey]] had been separated from the rest and remained unable to reunite with their family. Kenobi promised the Qubey they would rebuild the bridge better than before, and the native hugged him. The Qubeys on the other side of the ravine drew a message in the snow for Kenobi to see, indicating they would help attack Grievous upon the bridge's completion.<ref name="BattleTales5" />

===Confrontation===
{{Quote|General Kenobi, I've got visual of the Qubeys on the surface.''"<br />"''Are they all gathered together on the one side as we planned?''"<br />"''Indeed they are, sir.''"<br />"''Excellent. Fire when ready, Cody.|Cody and Obi-Wan Kenobi, aboard a gunship|<ref name="BattleTales5" />}}
[[File:QubeysAttackB1s-TCWBattleTales5.png|thumb|left|180px|The Qubey attacked Grievous and stole Kenobi's lightsaber from the cyborg.]]
After coordinating with the Qubey a plan to thwart the Separatists, the Jedi and his troopers continued building the lunar ice bridge. Grievous eventually became frustrated with the slow pace of their progress and confronted Kenobi about it. However, the Qubey then surrounded the Separatist general and latched onto him, distracting the [[cyborg]] long enough to [[Thief|steal]] Kenobi's [[lightsaber]] from him. Grievous broke away from the natives in disgust, leaving the site with disdain. Afterward, Kenobi thanked the group of Qubey for acquiring his [[weapon]] for him.<ref name="BattleTales5" />

After the bridge's completion, Grievous gloated about how it would allow the Separatists to conquer the moon easily. After the Separatist general revealed that he was planning to abandon Kenobi and his troopers to die in Caliban's frozen tundra, the Jedi signaled his Qubey allies to attack. The Separatists scrambled to oppose the combatants and assemble reinforcements as Kenobi engaged in a [[lightsaber duel]] with Grievous. In the ensuing chaos, the [[Cloning|clone]] troopers stole the [[E-5 blaster rifle]]s from Grievous' [[battle droid]]s and made their way to a nearby [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|gunship]] in order to flee the moon.<ref name="BattleTales5" />

[[File:Caliban3-TCWBattleTales5.png|thumb|right|260px|As they departed Caliban, Kenobi and his forces destroyed the moon's lunar ice bridge.]]
Meanwhile, Kenobi continued [[Lightsaber combat|dueling]] Grievous and maneuvered the cyborg to near the base of a mountain. The Jedi then [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to cause an avalanche that buried Grievous and the rest of his forces. Though [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] suggested attempting to take the cyborg into custody for the Republic, Kenobi opted to leave the moon before Separatist reinforcements arrived. As they flew away in their [[gunship]], the Jedi ensured their Qubey allies were safely gathered on one side of the ravine before destroying the lunar ice bridge so the Separatists could not use it.<ref name="BattleTales5" /> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Cody recounted Kenobi's actions on Caliban to his colleagues, [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] and Clone [[Commander]] [[Wolffe]], while on a [[Siege of Hisseen|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Hisseen]].<ref name="BattleTales5" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|Looks like one of the planet's natives&mdash;a Qubey, I believe.|Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="BattleTales5" />}}
Caliban was home to the Qubey, a species of small [[Sentience|sentients]] that spoke an [[Qubey language|alien language]].<ref name="BattleTales5" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|I expected this bridge to be complete by now!''"<br />"''General, my men and I are working almost around the clock. It isn't easy rebuilding what you destroyed.|Grievous and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="BattleTales5" />}}
During the Clone Wars, a lunar ice bridge spanned a ravine on Caliban's surface and served as the only way to traverse from one side of the moon to the other. The bridge was destroyed by Grievous, briefly rebuilt by Kenobi and his [[212th Attack Battalion|forces]], then destroyed again upon the [[Republic Military|Republic forces']] departure from Caliban.<ref name="BattleTales5" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I'm still pretty awed I got to design an ''[sic]'' species and planet in the Star Wars universe&hellip;|Philip Murphy, about his work on ''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 5|<ref name="Insta">{{Instagram|username=crashtesterx|url=p/C7Xoe6uPbNv|name=[[Philip Murphy]]|date=2022-01-17|quote=I'm still pretty awed I got to design an species and planet in the Star Wars universe&hellip; #StarWars|archivefile=File:PhilipMurphy-DesignCW5-Instagram.png|nolive=1}}</ref>}}
[[File:GrievousKenobi-CW5Murphy.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Prior to its release, Philip Murphy shared a work-in-progess panel of the Caliban flashback featured in ''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 5.]]
Caliban first appeared in flashbacks in the [[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 5|fifth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series. The issue was written by [[Michael Moreci]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="BattleTales5" /> on [[September 30]], [[2020]].<ref name="ReleaseDate">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-Clone-Wars-5-of-5/digital-comic/838786|text=Star Wars Adventures: Clone Wars #5 (of 5)|nolive=1}}</ref> The flashback depicting Caliban was drawn by [[Philip Murphy]]. The comic alternates between referring to Caliban as a moon and a planet. This article assumes the former is correct due to its initial introduction as an Outer Rim moon and the presence of a lunar ice bridge on its surface.<ref name="BattleTales5" />

Murphy first announced his involvement with the project on [[January 23]], 2020, expressing his excitement at being able to draw for the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise again in one of his favorite time periods, the Clone Wars.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|crashtesterX|status/1220439619755479042|[[Philip Murphy]]|date=2020-01-23|quote=Super excited to announce this, it's always amazing getting the chance to draw the Star Wars universe and in one of my favourite eras The Clone Wars &hellip; @IDWPublishing|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/kTZmT|archivefile=File:PhilipMurphy-Announce-Twitter.png}}</ref> Prior to the issue's release, in two posts on [[February 22]]<ref name="Insta2">{{Instagram|username=crashtesterx|url=p/B84LQ09h33b|name=[[Philip Murphy]]|date=2020-02-22|quote=On the drawing board &hellip; So cool to get the chance to work on more #StarWars #CloneWars #Grievous @idwpublishing|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/ENzuS|archivefile=File:PhilipMurphy-PreviewClipCW5-Instagram.png}}</ref> and [[March 6]] of the same year,<ref name="Insta3">{{Instagram|username=crashtesterx|url=p/B9ZVLq-BTyY|name=[[Philip Murphy]]|date=2020-03-06|quote=On the drawing board &hellip; #Kenobi #Grievous #StarWars #CloneWars #IDW|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/LDuII|archivefile=File:PhilipMurphy-PreviewClip2CW5-Instagram.png}}</ref> the artist shared two colorless, unfinished panels of the comic&mdash;set on Caliban&mdash;on [[Wikipedia:Instagram|Instagram]].<ref name="Insta2" /><ref name="Insta3" />

Murphy was proud of having designed Caliban and its native Qubey for the comic<ref name="Insta" /> and shared several uncolored pages featuring the planet on his portfolio website<ref name="Portfolio">{{WebCite|url=https://philmurphycartoonist.carrd.co|text=Portfolio of Phil Murphy|author=[[Philip Murphy]]|work=[https://philmurphycartoonist.carrd.co Phillip Murphy's official website]|archivedate=20230413043758}}</ref> and on Instagram.<ref name="Insta4">{{Instagram|username=crashtesterx|url=p/CHeX_Hfh165|name=[[Philip Murphy]]|date=2020-11-11|quote=Some Star Wars The Clone Wars pages ......|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/k7W2c|archivefile=File:PhilipMurphy-UncoloredPagesCW5-Instagram.png}}</ref><ref name="Insta5">{{Instagram|username=crashtesterx|url=p/COdN9Myhwal|name=[[Philip Murphy]]|date=2021-05-04|quote=#MayThe4thBeWithYou It was an amazing privilege to draw in the Galaxy Far Far Away...|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/cZ2bK|archivefile=File:PhilipMurphy-UncoloredPages2CW5-Instagram.png}}</ref> While working together, Moreci and Murphy had the idea of the Qubey having descended from [[Ewok]]s hailing from the [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]], having evolved to have thicker white [[Hair|fur]] and blue [[eye]]s.<ref name="WookInterview">{{WookCite|url=p/4400000000003931839|text=Interview with Philip Murphy|forum=1}}</ref> Though Caliban shares its name with a [[Wikipedia:Caliban|character]] from [[Wikipedia:William Shakespeare|William Shakespeare's]] play [[Wikipedia:The Tempest|''The Tempest'']], Moreci indicated that he had named the planet Caliban for a different reason, possibly relating to the [[Wikipedia:Aliens|''Aliens'']] franchise. The events on Caliban were based on [[Wikipedia:The Bridge on the River Kwai|''The Bridge on the River Kwai'']], a [[1950s#1957|1957]] film in which [[Alec Guinness]]&mdash;who portrayed Kenobi in ''Star Wars''&mdash;is forced to build a bridge as a prisoner of war.<ref name="MoreciInterview">{{WookYouTube|qszf2DVccqI|Wookieepedia Interviews - Michael Moreci}}</ref>
