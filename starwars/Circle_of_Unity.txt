A '''Circle of Unity''' was a rare event in which the [[Patriarch]]s of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] came together on the [[Chiss]] [[capital]] world of [[Csilla]]. Around the end of the [[Clone Wars]], two Circles of Unity&mdash;the first in many years&mdash;were held. The first granted permission for the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] to travel to the [[planet]] [[Sunrise]], where a group of Chiss warships had illegally gathered, while the second determined that [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], who had been in large part responsible for the gathering, would be [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]].<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>

Each Patriarch was permitted to bring a single aide, usually the [[Senior Aide]] to the Patriarch or the [[Chiss family|family's]] [[Speaker (Chiss Ascendancy)|Speaker]] in the [[Chiss Syndicure|Syndicure]]. However, sometimes the Patriarch decided to bring other members of their family like [[Syndic Prime]] [[Irizi'stal'mustro]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
