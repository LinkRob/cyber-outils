"'''Frank Oz: Blizzard of Oz'''" is an article covering an interview with [[Frank Oz]], the man who portrayed Yoda, regarding among other things the characters' return in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. It was written by [[Scott Chernoff]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 42|''Star Wars Insider'' 42]].
