'''''Droids'' 6''' is the sixth issue of [[Star Wars: Droids (1994)|''Star Wars: Droids'' (1994)]], a [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series starring [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]]. It was published in September 1994 by [[Dark Horse Comics]].

==Publisher's summary==
''In the conclusion to the adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO in the Kalarba system, a power core rupture on Hosk forces all life forms to be evacuated, but life forms doesn't include droids! As Olag Greck makes his final stand, R2-D2 and C-3PO race against time to save the industrial moon&hellip; but first they'll have to survive Greck's droid armada!''
