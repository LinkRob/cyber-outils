'''Aquasuits'''<ref name="CotR" />, or '''scuba suits'''<ref name="Prisoners Episode Guide">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep403|text="Prisoners" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}}</ref> were a type of [[suit]] made for [[Water|underwater]] operation by non-aquatic [[species]], allowing the user to go hundreds of [[meter]]s beneath the [[ocean]] surface. The suits were pressurized, yet flexible and airtight due to the natural memory of the used materials. The user's natural senses are enhanced by integrated [[helmet]] optics and audio feeds. An aquasuit would provide six [[Standard hour|hours]] of atmosphere to an [[Breath mask|air breather]]. The suit also gives the wearer fresh water, nutrition supplements, and other biological necessities.<ref name="CotR" /> [[Jedi]] at the [[Battle of Mon Cala]] like [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]], wore aquasuits.<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref>
