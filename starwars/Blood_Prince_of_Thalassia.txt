The '''Blood Prince of Thalassia''' was a [[Monarchy|monarchical]] title in the [[Thalassian]] society.<ref name="Friends">''[[Friends Like These]]''</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The title of Blood Prince of Thalassia was mentioned in ''[[Friends Like These]]'', a [[2016]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying]] adventure published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for use with the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Friends" /> The Blood Prince title originated with the [[1996]] novel ''[[Shield of Lies]]'', which first mentioned a [[Blood Prince of Thassalia]], ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' later established the [[Thassalia system]] and the similarly-named [[Thalassia/Legends|Thalassia]] as separate locations, and ''Friends Like These'' used Thalassia as part of the title instead of the original Thassalia.
