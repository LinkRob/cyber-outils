'''Bad Robot Productions''' is an American film production company co-founded by producer, writer and director [[J.J. Abrams]] and producer [[Bryan Burk]]. Under the leadership of Abrams and Burk, Bad Robot produced the films [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].
