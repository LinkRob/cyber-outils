'''''Darth Vader and Son / Vader's Little Princess'' Deluxe Box Set''' is compilation of the [[Canon|non-canon]] books ''[[Darth Vader and Son]]'' and ''[[Vader's Little Princess]]'' by [[Jeffrey Brown]]. The title was published on [[September 8]], [[2015]] by [[Chronicle Books]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Jeffrey Brown's beloved reimagining of the'' Star Wars ''universe featuring Darth Vader as a devoted dad to young Luke and Leia has charmed and captivated fans of all ages, in books that have become galactic bestsellers and classics in their own right. This deluxe gift box set reproduces the first two books in the series in a large format—perfect for sharing with young Jedi&mdash;that showcases Brown's funny, colorful work. The slipcase edition also includes two unique, frameable art prints created just for this set as well as custom artwork for the slipcase box.''<ref name="Edelweiss" />
