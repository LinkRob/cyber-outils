"'''A Very Special Meal'''" was a short narrative written by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]], and was published in the ''[[Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook]]''.

==Plot summary==
[[Master Chef]] [[Tavvar Va'ran]], the second-best [[Kubaz/Legends|Kubaz]] chef in the galaxy, is ordered by [[Guri]], part owner of the exclusive [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscanti]] restaurant [[Manarai]] and [[Black Sun/Legends|Black Sun]] [[Assassin/Legends|assassin]], to poison the food of [[Imperial Advisor/Legends|Imperial Advisor]] [[Fendrilon Koozar]]. Va'ran complies with the instructions, resulting in Koozar's death.
