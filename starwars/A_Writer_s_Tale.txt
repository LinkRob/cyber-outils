"'''A Writer's Tale'''" is an interview with ''[[Star Wars]]'' writer [[Katie Lucas]] about ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|Season Three]]. The interview was conducted by [[Jonathan Wilkins]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 125|''Star Wars Insider'' 125]], a magazine published in [[April]] of [[2011]].<ref name="sw125" />
