A '''CC-voice modulator''' was a model of [[Speech scrambler|voice modulator]] that was used in [[protocol droid]]s to allow them to speak.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a CC-voice modulator was valued at twenty two [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
CC-voice modulators appeared as an item in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, the modulator could only be sold for credits, and did not have another function.<ref name="Outlaws" />
