The '''Antilles Maneuver''' was a [[starfighter]] tactic named after the maneuver [[Rebel pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]]' used to save [[Luke Skywalker]] from a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] during the [[Battle of Yavin]]. The maneuver involved flying directly at an enemy starfighter an ally couldn't shake and flying through the resulting explosion once the target was destroyed.<ref name="RRaRS">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic, Rebel, and Resistance Starfighters}}</ref>
