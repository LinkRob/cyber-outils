'''B7-N5''', nicknamed '''Bev''',<ref name="NewCantina">{{EA|subdomain=forums|url=blog/swgoh-game-info-hub-en/upcoming-cantina-update--more/13363839|text=Upcoming Cantina Update + More}}</ref> was a [[protocol droid]] who assisted [[Fey Zara]] in running [[Fey's Cantina|her cantina]], where she offered individuals tutorials and quests for [[holotable]]s.<ref name="GoH" />

==Behind the scenes==
B7-N5 first appeared in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'', though she was unnamed until<ref name="GoH" /> "Version Update [[May 23|5/23]]/[[2018]]",<ref name="EA-Version Update 5/23/2018">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/170303/version-update-5-23-2018|text=Version Update 5/23/2018}}</ref> after which<ref name="Quests Summary">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/170302/quests-summary-from-a-cg-developer|text=Quests Summary from a CG Developer|archivedate=20190917193924|nolive=1}}</ref> she gave quests to players. Players can also earn a portrait of her as a quest reward.<ref name="GoH" />  On [[April 27]], [[2026]], the cantina was completely replaced and B7-N5 received an updated design and the nickname Bev.<ref name="NewCantina" />
