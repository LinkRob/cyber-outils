An '''energy binder''',<ref name="Episode 1" /> (more commonly known as a [[power coupling]])<ref name="Episode 21">{{Film|II}}</ref> was a device that coupled two or more [[podracer]] engines/turbines via electrical beam. Many of the racing machines used for [[Podracing|podraces]] combined multiple large [[engine]]s with energy binders. Energy binders prevented engines in the system from naturally straying from one another, which could lead to catastrophic accidents. A notable energy-binder failure was during the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] podrace, when [[Ben Quadinaros]]' binders failed, sending his engines flying in multiple directions.<ref name="Episode 1" />
