'''1980 Topps ''Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back'' Series 3''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] in [[1980]].<ref name="Volume Two" />

The set was based on [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], and featured stills from the film, promotional photos, and behind the scenes photos all within a yellow and green border. Most cards had "Star Quotes" on the back with dialogue from both films in the series. "Space Paintings" cards featured concept art by [[Ralph McQuarrie]], and "Movie Facts" cards included production photos on the front and information on the back. A sub-set of sticker cards were included, the backs of which included a portion of a larger image that could be pieced together with other cards like a puzzle.<ref name="Volume Two" />

Card numbering in the set continued from the [[1980 Topps Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back Series 2|previous series]].<ref name="Volume Two" />

==Continuity==
Card 286 shows [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] standing in front of a labeled door in [[Echo Base/Legends|Echo Base]] which was part of the film's deleted subplot showing [[Wampa/Legends|wampas]] being held inside that room.<ref name="Blu-Ray">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''</ref>
