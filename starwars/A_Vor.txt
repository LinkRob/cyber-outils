'''A'Vor''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Tusken Raider]] who was a member of [[A'Yark]]'s [[A'Yark's tribe|tribe]]. He had a [[A'Vor's twin|twin brother]], and was the cousin of [[A'Koba]].<ref name="Rites" />

==Biography==
A'Vor<ref name="Rites" /> lived on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Tatooine]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> as part of [[Chief]]tain [[A'Yark]]'s [[A'Yark's tribe|tribe]].<ref name="Rites" /> Shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="Episode IV" /> A'Vor and his [[A'Vor's twin|twin brother]], joined by his cousin, [[A'Koba]], [[Death|killed]] a juvenile [[krayt dragon]] as a rite of passage into adulthood. He then claimed to A'Yark that he would be able to kill the adult krayts as well.<ref name="Rites" />

After A'Koba knocked Luke Skywalker unconscious, he and his twin brother<ref name="Episode IV" /> and their cousin<ref name="Rites" /> A'Koba began searching Skywalker's [[X-34 landspeeder|landspeeder]]. They were scared off by [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who mimicked the call of a krayt dragon to spook A'Vor and his fellow Tusken Raiders into fleeing.<ref name="Episode IV" />
