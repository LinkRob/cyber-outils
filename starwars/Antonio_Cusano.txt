'''Antonio Cusano''' designed the "Harkov's Defection" scenario that was one of the winners of [[LucasArts]]'s "Design Your Own Mission" contest following the release of ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds]]''. The scenario, along with the other winning levels, was included with the [[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds: Clone Campaigns|''Clone Campaigns'']] expansion pack and made available for download on the LucasArts website.
