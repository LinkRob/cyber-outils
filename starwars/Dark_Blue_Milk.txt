'''Dark Blue Milk''' was a beverage [[Diet|potable]] by [[human]]s. It was served at various locations during the [[Imperial Era]], including at [[Cid's Parlor]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Ord Mantell]], [[Tanda's Cantina]] on the [[Suria space station]], and [[cantina]]s on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planets [[Lau]], [[Serolonis]], and [[Varkana]].

Around [[10 BBY]], while her [[Nakano Lash's crew|crew]] was at the Varkana [[Losha Tarkon's cantina|cantina]], the human [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] went to order a round of drinks for the crew, one of which was a Dark Blue Milk for her [[twin]] [[T'onga's family|brother]] [[T'ongor]]. After T'onga became distracted, T'ongor went to see why she was taking so long, and upon realizing why, the [[Gender|brother]] said he would have to get his own drink.

==Description==
Dark Blue Milk was a beverage [[Diet|potable]] by [[human]]s<ref name="Bounty Hunters 30" /> that shared its named with the beverage [[blue milk]].<ref name="Droids in Distress">[[Droids in Distress (book)|''Droids in Distress'' chapter book]]</ref> It was stored in a dark [[Color|blue]] bottle with a grey top and a lighter blue label with the drink's name written in<ref name="Concept Art gallery">{{SW|url=rampage-concept-art-gallery|text="Rampage" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> [[Aurebesh]] [[Writing|text]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|So. What's got you so distracted? ''[Sees that T'onga is looking at Losha]'' Oh I get it&hellip;I guess I'll be getting my own drink, then. See you back at the booth.|T'ongor, to T'onga|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 30" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era]], Dark Blue Milk was served at the [[Cantina|bar]] [[Cid's Parlor]]<ref name="Rampage" /> in [[Ord Mantell City]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[planet]] [[Ord Mantell]]<ref name="Rampage" /> by [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Season 1 Date">{{TBBDate|S1}}</ref> By the time between 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Season 2 Date">{{TBBDate|204}}</ref> the beverage was available in the cantina at the [[Safa Toma Speedway]] on<ref name="Faster" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Serolonis">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Serolonis system]]</ref> planet [[Serolonis]].<ref name="Faster Trivia">{{SW|url=faster-trivia-gallery|text="Faster" Trivia Gallery &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> A [[Unidentified cantina (Lau)|cantina]] on<ref name="A Different Approach" /> the Outer Rim<ref name="Lau">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Lau system]]</ref> planet<ref name="Lau Databank">{{Databank|lau|Lau}}</ref> [[Lau]]<ref name="A Different Approach" /> and [[Tanda's Cantina]] on<ref name="Bad Territory" /> the [[Suria space station]]<ref name="Suria Databank">{{Databank|suria-space-station|Suria space station}}</ref> both served Dark Blue Milk<ref name="A Different Approach" /><ref name="Bad Territory" /> during or after 18 BBY.<ref name="Season 3 Date">{{TBBDate|S3}}</ref>

[[File:TongaTongorVarkanaBar-BountyHuntres30.png|thumb|right|300px|While on Varkana, T'onga (right) was to order a Dark Blue Milk for her brother T'ongor (left) but became distracted.]]
By around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 30 Date">The flashback events of [[Bounty Hunters 30|''Bounty Hunters'' 30]] that take place on [[Varkana]] occur between [[Beilert Valance]]'s discharge from the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Mission to Corellia (Nakano Lash's crew)|skirmish on Corellia]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places in [[10 BBY]] and around 10 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], respectively. The events on Varkana must therefore also take place around that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> Dark Blue Milk was served at a [[Losha Tarkon's cantina|cantina]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 30" /> owned by the human<ref name="Bounty Hunters 42">[[Bounty Hunters 42|''Bounty Hunters'' 42]]</ref> [[Losha Tarkon]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> on the Outer Rim planet [[Varkana]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 30" /> Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 30 Date" /> the [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunting]] [[Nakano Lash's crew|crew]] of the [[Nautolan]] [[Nakano Lash]] visited the cantina. During the crew's time at the cantina, the human [[T'onga]] visited its bar to get a round of drinks for the crew, including a Dark Blue Milk for<ref name="Bounty Hunters 30" /> her human [[twin]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[T'onga's family|brother]] [[T'ongor]]. T'onga, however, became distracted by a [[bartender]]&mdash;Tarkon&mdash;and ignored two others who approached her, resulting in it taking a long enough [[time]] to get the drinks that T'ongor went to see what was taking her so long.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 30" />

When he arrived at the bar, T'ongor noted to T'onga he still did not have his Dark Blue Milk, to which she replied that she was good for her round. After noting that he needed a drink before they were kicked out for their fellow crewmate [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] fighting with other patrons, T'ongor realized why his [[Gender|sister]] had been taking so long and then stated he would have to get his own drink before heading back to their crew's booth.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 30" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dark Blue Milk first appeared in "[[Rampage (episode)|Rampage]],"<ref name="Rampage" /> the fifth episode in the [[The Bad Batch Season 1|first season]] of the [[Disney+]] animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'', which aired on [[May 28]], [[2021]].<ref name="Companion">{{InsiderCite|206|Star Wars: The Bad Batch Season One Companion|''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season One Companion}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Animation]] artist [[Chris Madden]] created a concept-art illustration of the beverage's bottle design for the episode on [[August 16]], [[2019]], which labeled it as "Bottle K."<ref name="Concept Art gallery" />
