'''Bonipa''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who served the [[House of Organa]] during the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], and was stationed aboard the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' under [[Captain]] [[Raymus Antilles]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Caibol, Bonipa&mdash;get these droids out of my ''sight''. I want ''both'' their memories wiped before we make the jump to ''lightspeed''.|Raymus Antilles, on R2-D2 and C-3PO|<ref name="Trouble Again" />}}
Bonipa accompanied Antilles and the crew of the ''Tantive IV'' to help obtain cargo from the [[Gran]] [[Garsa]]. She used a [[hoversled]] to load cargo boxes while Antilles conversed with Garsa and the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]]. His counterpart, the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]], attempted to alert Bonipa to the actions of the [[Prowler 1000 seeker droid]] [[PTZ-1K]] as it tried to access the ship's communication grid to send a message to the Empire. She ignored the astro-droid and the crew returned to the ship to leave the [[Unidentified planet (Garsa)|planet]].<ref name="Trouble Again" />

R2 continued to cause trouble aboard the ship, and Bonipa backed up Antilles alongside [[Caibol]] in confronting the droids in the cargo hold. Antilles ordered them to have the droids' [[Memory wipe|memories wiped]], and the pair led the two troublesome units away. However, R2 released an oil slick, causing Bonipa and Caibol to skid and C-3PO to land on the pair, giving R2 the chance to flee. Bonipa realized the droid had a plan, and followed R2 back to the cargo hold to fill Antilles in. The four were able to catch PTZ-1K as the unit attempted to send a transmission to the Empire, stalled by C-3PO's ingenuity, and Bonipa helped Caibol grab the spy droid at [[blaster]]-point. The crew decided to have the seeker droid's memory wiped, putting an end to the incident.<ref name="Trouble Again" />

==Equipment==
As a crew member of the ''Tantive IV'', Bonipa wore a [[Color|blue]] [[jacket]] with a fur collar, a gray undershirt, beige pants with a [[holster]] strapped to her thigh, and dark [[boot]]s. She carried a [[DH-17 blaster pistol|DH-17]] [[blaster pistol]].<ref name="Trouble Again" />
