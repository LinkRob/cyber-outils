A '''Partisan cell''' led by the [[Devaronian]] [[Blue (Devaronian)|Blue]] was active in the [[Five Points system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The group consisted of Blue, the [[Labbo]] junk trader [[Risi Amps]], [[Shawburn]], a [[Krish (species)|Krish]] who served as the group's [[pilot]], the mechanic [[Burta]], [[Bardbee]], the [[Ma'cella]] [[Dwindun]], and his brother. Together, the group crewed a [[XO1 yacht]]. Bardbee was at one point captured by the [[Galactic Empire]] and was eventually [[torture]]d to [[death]]. At some point after, Amps hired [[Jyn Erso]] as [[forger]] to forge cargo manifests and clearance codes, allowing the cell to bypass [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[blockade]]s.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Bethava Rocwyn]] had Amps and Erso arrested for forgery and blackmailed Erso into infiltrating the cell, promising amnesty in exchange for Erso's espionage. However, Admiral Rocwyn broke her promise to Erso, arresting her along with the remainder of the group. Rocwyn stated that Blue would be [[Death penalty|executed]] for [[treason]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
