An '''unidentified''' '''Dark Acolyte''' was in the service of [[Count]] [[Dooku]] by the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]]. During the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], the [[Dark Acolyte|Acolyte]], alongside [[Sev'rance Tann|Sev'rance Taan]], helped to defend<ref name="Prelude" /> a hidden<ref name="Dooku 5">{{Databank|url=count-dooku-biography-gallery|title=Count Dooku Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> [[Dooku's hangar|hangar]] where Dooku's personal [[Count Dooku's solar sailer|solar sailor]] was docked. The two engaged [[Mace Windu]] in a struggle on the valley floor below the [[hangar]], preventing Windu from coming to [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s aid against Dooku.<ref name="Prelude" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Star_Wars_the_Clone_Wars_Dark_Acolytes.png|thumb|300px|The Dark Acolyte Trio made their debut in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity.]]
The Dark Acolyte was mentioned in the [[De Agostini]] [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] release "[[Prologue to the Clone Wars and the First Battle of Geonosis]],"<ref name="Prelude" /> which was first released in [[April 22]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, a [[Dark Acolyte Trio|trio of Dark Acolytes]] were present on Geonosis in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (video game)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' video game]].<ref name="SW:TCW">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (video game)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' video game]]</ref>
