A '''funeral''' was a ceremony for the [[burial]] or [[cremation]] of the [[Death|deceased]]. Mourners usually wore dark or modest clothing to reflect their sadness and mourning of the person's death, as did [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[parent]]s and [[Sola Naberrie|sister]] during her [[Padmé Amidala's funeral|funeral]]<ref name="SW3">{{Film|III}}</ref> and non-Jedi guests during Knights' and Masters' funerals.

==Jedi funerals==
[[File:FauxFuneral-Deception.png|thumb|right|300px|A funeral in the Jedi Temple]]
Members of the [[Jedi Order]] had humble funerals which were held in the Jedi Temple, as they did not fear death or value worldly possessions. [[Jedi]] attendees wore their [[Jedi apparel|usual daily attire]], while non-Jedi guests, including honorable ones, wore modest clothing out of respect.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The deceased's body would be placed in a casket which was then lowered and sealed in a slot underneath the floor. A bright light would then emanate from a circular opening in the seal, signifying the dead's ascendance.<ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref>

==Ferrix traditional funerals==
[[File:The_band_with_brothers_and_sisters.png|left|thumb|290x290px|An honor guard band and members of the Daughters would accompany upstanding Ferrixians' funeral processions]]
In [[Ferrix]], upstanding citizens as well as members of the [[Daughters of Ferrix]] would receive a public funeral which was held in the streets of the [[Gavalox Communications|city]]. Before the ceremony, their body would be cremated, its ashes mixed with mortar and Ferrix dust and pressed into a [[funerary stone]] which was then inscribed with their name as well as [[date]] of [[Reproduction|birth]] and [[death]] in [[Bazeese script]]. In the day of the ceremony, the stone would be received by a person close to the deceased, usually a family member or close friend if one wasn't available. This trustee would then along with Daughters of Ferrix, [[Honor guard (Ferrix)|Honor Guards]] and the Honor Guard band lead a parade of Ferrix citizens from the deceased's home to [[Rix Road]] all the way to [[Fountain Square]] where their holorecorded "Truth" would be broadcast for the crowd.<ref name="Rix Road">{{Andor|Rix Road (episode)}}</ref> Movements such as "Forming Up", "Unto Stone We Are" and "To The Sky" would be played by the band during the parade,<ref name="Music">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e12-trivia-gallery|text="Rix Road" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> while passers by would stop and stand to pay their respects. Upon reaching the Square, the procession would form around the [[holoprojector]] broadcasting the Truth while chanting "Stone and Sky"; silence then befell upon the crowd as the deceased's word "lifted" them. After the ceremony, the funerary stone was placed in a city wall,<ref name="Rix Road" /> as the deceased metaphorically "gave their body to support those who remain".<ref name="Stone">{{Databank|ferrix-funerary-stone|Ferrix Funerary Stone}}</ref>
