'''Bio-hexacrypt''', or '''biohexacrypt code''', was an incredibly secure form of code encryption,<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> developed by [[Kupohan]]s.<ref name="SG">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>

Used during the [[New Republic era]] and most prominently by the [[First Order]], bio-hexacrypt was used to secure valuable data, such as data networks and clearance codes.<ref name="TLJVD" /> Notably, the ''[[Supremacy]]''{{'s}} clearance codes were bio-hexaencrypted, and re-scrambled every hour; leading [[Finn]] and [[Rose Tico]] to seek out the [[Master Codebreaker]] to gain access.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

[[DJ]] was capable of handcrafting custom [[computer spike]]s and [[Key bypass|bypass key]]s capable of befuddling bio-hexacryption, albeit only temporarily.<ref name="TLJVD" />

The Master Codebreaker kept his real identity a secret, but posted his personal data on a public access node wrapped in a quantum-spread bio-hexacrypt code. Anyone capable of cracking the code was welcome to claim his title of Master Codebreaker; though, as of the [[Mission to Canto Bight]], no one had even come close.<ref name="TLJVD" />
