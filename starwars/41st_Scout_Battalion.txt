The '''41st Scout Battalion''' was an elite [[infantry]] and [[Tank|armor]] [[battalion]] within the [[41st Elite Corps]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] that participated in the [[Clone Wars]], a pan-[[Galaxy|galactic]] conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. In [[19 BBY]], it was decided that [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Yoda]] would lead a battalion to support the [[Wookiee]]s on [[Kashyyyk]]. The 41st Scout Battalion was then dispatched to the [[planet]], where they participated in the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]] and later the implementation of [[Order 66]], as part of the [[Great Jedi Purge]].

The battalion was characterized by the [[Color|green]] camouflage [[Phase II ARF armor]] they wore, and utilized [[All Terrain Attack Pod]]s, [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s, [[BARC speeder]]s, [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]]s, and [[Infantry Support Platform]]s. The battalion was under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of Jedi General Yoda, as well as Jedi General [[Luminara Unduli]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]].

==Description==
The 41st [[Scout]] [[Battalion]]<ref name="Battlefront II" /> was an elite<ref name="BC 3" /> [[infantry]] and [[Tank|armor]] subunit of the [[41st Elite Corps]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> It was under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Yoda]], [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Luminara Unduli]], and [[Clone Commander]] [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]].<ref name="Episode III" /> As a battalion of the Grand Army,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> it was composed of four [[Company|companies]] of 144 [[trooper]]s each.<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'' establishes that a [[battalion]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] is made up of four [[Company|companies]]. [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] furthermore establishes that a company in the Grand [[Army]] of the Republic is composed of 144 [[clone trooper]]s.</ref>

The battalion was composed largely of [[clone scout trooper]]s in [[Color|green]] camouflaged [[clone trooper armor]].<ref name="Episode III" /> The 41st Scout specialized in reconnaissance and warfare over difficult terrain, including overgrown floral locations.<ref name="BFII Info">{{EA|subdomain=battlefront-forums|url=discussion/137644/community-transmission-where-are-those-droidekas|text=Community Transmission &ndash; Where Are Those Droidekas}}</ref> As such, members of the unit wore [[Phase II ARF armor]] for greater mobility and flexibility. Their [[helmet]] featured a sunshield, a wider visor, and its overall shape helped to secure wider vision while traversing [[jungle]]-[[Forest|rainforest]] environments.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> The battalion also utilized [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]]s, [[All Terrain Attack Pod]]s, [[Infantry Support Platform]]s, and [[BARC speeder]]s. When the battalion was deployed to the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]], they were transported there via a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="Episode III" />

==History==
{{Quote|What about the droid attack on the Wookiees?''"<br />"''It is critical we send an attack group there immediately.|Ki-Adi-Mundi and Mace Windu, during a Jedi High Council meeting|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:KashyyykStagingArea-ROTS.png|thumb|left|300px|The battalion participated in the Battle of Kashyyyk.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] following the [[Battle of Coruscant]]<ref name="Episode III" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the [[Battle of Coruscant]] in [[19 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi High Council]] settled on sending an attack group to the<ref name="Episode III" /> planet<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Kashyyyk to reinforce the [[Wookiee Army]] against a Separatist invasion. It was decided that the 41st Scout Battalion of the 41st Elite [[Corps]] would be dispatched, and shortly after, the corps assembled at a [[Coruscant shipyard|staging area]] on [[Coruscant]] as equipment and vehicles were loaded onto a ''Venator''-class [[Star Destroyer]] and Jedi General Yoda arrived to [[Commanding officer|command]] the battalion.<ref name="Episode III" />

The 41st Scout arrived on Kashyyyk and took defensive positions in<ref name="Episode III" /> [[Kachirho]], the capital [[city]] of Kashyyyk,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and unloaded vehicles and equipment alongside the [[Kachirho Wookiee Militia]]. While on Kashyyyk, the battalion was led by Jedi Generals Yoda, Luminara Unduli, and [[Cloning|Clone]] [[Commander]] CC-1004 "Gree." Eventually, the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]] began and the battalion defended the beachhead against [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]]. Members of the battalion positioned themselves on the beach itself as well as city structures and the large [[tree]]s surrounding the city. During the battle, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] of the Republic, secretly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious, initiated the [[Great Jedi Purge]] by activating [[Order 66]], a directive that labeled the Jedi as [[Treason|traitors]].<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:BoK-ROTS.png|thumb|right|300px|Members of the 41st Scout Battalion during the Battle of Kashyyyk.]]
Commander Gree received the message from Palpatine in the command center, and he and [[Clone Captain]] [[Jek]] moved into position to [[Death|kill]] Yoda.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> Yoda, however, sensed the betrayal of Palpatine and the deaths of his fellow Jedi across [[the galaxy]], and beheaded Gree and Jek before they could fire their [[blaster]]s. The two [[Wookiee]]s beside Yoda, [[Chewbacca]] and General [[Tarfful]], then helped Yoda leave the city, failing to stop him from escaping the planet in an [[E3-standard starship lifeboat|escape pod]].<ref name="Episode III" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The 41st Scout Battalion was a group of clones from the 41st Elite Corps who specialized in reconnaissance and warfare over difficult terrain. In the final days of the war, Commander Gree and Grand Master Yoda deployed the 41st Scout Battalion to the beaches of Kashyyyk to fend off a droid attack on the Wookiees.|Description from ''Star Wars Battlefront II''|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
The 41st Scout Battalion first appeared, albeit unnamed, in the theatrical [[2005]] [[Star Wars saga|Skywalker saga]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Episode III" /> The name of the organization was first revealed to be the 41st Scout Battalion in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''<ref name="Battlefront II" /> with an update on [[June 26]], [[2019]], but was first announced in a community transmissions post for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] on [[May 23]].<ref name="BFII Info" /> Like the [[41st Ranger Platoon]], the 41st Scout Battalion shares the number of its parent unit, the 41st Elite Corps.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
