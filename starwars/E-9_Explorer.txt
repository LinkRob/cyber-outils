The '''E-9 Explorer'''<ref name="Chris Menezes" /> was a [[transport]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during its fight against the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. It was deployed by the [[Tatooine rebel cell]] during the [[Battle for Tatooine]], operating under [[Colonel]] [[Jennica Pierce]], to transport troops and vehicles to battle.<ref name="Commander" />
