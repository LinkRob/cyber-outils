"'''Assassin, Security, and Other Droids of War'''" is a volume in the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series of reference books published by [[De Agostini]]. It was published on [[May 2]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Droids were mechanical creatures capable of performing a myriad of tasks, but one of the most necessary was undoubtedly the exercise of some form of violence, either in self-defense or to assault living beings. Security and guard droids were extremely popular throughout the galaxy, and it didn't take much to turn one of these units into a killing machine. The lack of personality and emotion in many of these droids was an added benefit: the result was an efficient, ruthless killer who asked no questions. However, these same droids were often the source of other types of problems for their owners…''<ref name="Altaya" />
