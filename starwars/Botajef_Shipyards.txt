'''Botajef Shipyards''' was a small [[starship]] manufacturer based on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] world of [[Botajef]].<ref name="Life Debt" /><ref name="KoF" /> The company was first brought to galactic prominence with the [[AA-9 Freighter-Liner]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles" /><ref name="KoF" /> Shortly after, they attempted to enter the military market with the production of the [[SS-54 assault ship]].<ref name="Life Debt" /><ref name="KoF" /> Pylon-mounted engines were a hallmark of Botajef designs, including their contributions to the [[ST-70 assault ship]].<ref name="MVG" />
