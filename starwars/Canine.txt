'''Canines''', also known as '''canids''', were a subgroup of primarily [[mammal]]ian [[species]]. Different types of canines included the [[Corellian hound]]s, [[Vulptex|vulptices]], [[charhound]]s, and [[howler]]s. The [[anooba]]s were stated to be canine-like.<ref name="Bestiary">''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]''</ref>
