The '''destruction of Burnin Konn'''<ref name="Insider 228">{{InsiderCite|228|Making History: Five Years of The Mandalorian|Making History: Five Years of ''The Mandalorian''}}</ref> took place three [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="Shattered Empire 4" /> when [[Operation: Cinder]] was carried out on the [[Planet|world]] of [[Burnin Konn]] was by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Shattered Empire 4" /> During the attack, [[General]] [[Valin Hess]]'s forces planted bombs on the geothermal reactors within the cities which generated power from the planet's magma shifts. The [[city]] [[Chakonnis]]<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> and its entire population were destroyed within moments. Former Imperial [[Sniper|sharpshooter]] [[Migs Mayfeld]] was present on Burnin Konn during the attack as part of a [[Valin Hess's division|division]] which was lost, an event that seemed to mark his split from the Empire.<ref name="Chapter 15" />

Some time after the event, while undercover with [[Din Djarin]], Mayfeld was confronted by his old [[commanding officer]] during the battle, Valin Hess. During a tense drink that followed, Mayfeld pointedly reminded Hess of the battle and the slaughter that took place, to which Hess responded with derision and indifference. Overwhelmed with the traumatizing memories of the battle as well as Hess' callousness, Mayfeld shot him dead.<ref name="Chapter 15" />
