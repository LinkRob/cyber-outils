'''Anton Lesser''' is an actor who played [[Major]] [[Lio Partagaz]] in the [[2022]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|j5UX1Adanis|Andor &#124; Teaser Trailer &#124; Disney+}}</ref> Lesser has been a theatre actor with the [[Wikipedia:Royal Shakespeare Company|Royal Shakespeare Company]] who featured in numerous film, television, and [[Wikipedia:BBC Radio|BBC Radio]] productions. For his television drama roles, Lesser received a [[Wikipedia:British Academy Television Award for Best Supporting Actor|BAFTA]] nomination for playing [[Wikipedia:Thomas More|Sir Thomas More]] in [[Wikipedia:Wolf Hall (TV series)|''Wolf Hall'']] and two [[Wikipedia:Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by an Ensemble in a Drama Series|SAG Award]] nominations for playing [[W:c:gameofthrones:Qyburn|Qyburn]] and Prime Minister [[Wikipedia:Harold Macmillan|Harold Macmillan]] in [[Wikipedia:Game of Thrones|''Game of Thrones'']] and [[Wikipedia:The Crown (TV series)|''The Crown'']] respectively.<ref name="Bio" />
