{{Top|legends=FX-8 medical droid}}
'''FX-8''' was a type of [[droid]] with a trunk-like body. The [[cyborg]] [[Guswan Askreeth]] had everything below the waist replaced with a customized black FX-8 with hidden casters.<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
FX-8 was first mentioned in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'' written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], FX-8 droids were created for the [[2006]] reference book ''[[The New Essential Guide to Droids]]'' written by [[Daniel Wallace]].
