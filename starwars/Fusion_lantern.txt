'''Fusion lanterns''' were a type of illumination device that were a combination power source, light, and area heater. They were extremely durable and maintained a reputation for reliability, and could, with the correct connecting cables, power all manner of electronics. The cost of a fusion lanterns was 150 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets" />
