'''Dobak''' was an entity that the [[Dobak leg]] [[Foodstuff|dish]] was named for, which was available for purchase at [[Market District (Mirogana)|Market District]] of [[Mirogana]], the [[capital]] [[city]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dobak was first mentioned in [[canon]] in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' as part of the name of the dish that shares its name.<ref name="Outlaws" />
