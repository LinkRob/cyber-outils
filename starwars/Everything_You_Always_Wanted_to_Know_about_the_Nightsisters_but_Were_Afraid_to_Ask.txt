"'''Everything You Always Wanted to Know about the Nightsisters but Were Afraid to Ask'''" is an article written by [[Leland Chee]] that appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 122|''Star Wars Insider'' 122]]. The article delves into the history of the [[Nightsisters/Legends|Nightsisters]], and their continued appearances throughout the [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]]. Chee also explains the new additions of the [[Nightbrothers/Legends|Nightbrothers]] and [[Asajj Ventress/Legends|Asajj Ventress]]' new history with the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Dathomir/Legends|Dathomir]] in regards to the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series.<ref name="SW122" /> This article was later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2014|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2014]].<ref name="SWSE2014" />
