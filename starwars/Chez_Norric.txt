'''''Chez Norric''''' was one of the many eating and drinking establishments in [[Doom City]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].<ref name="CB">{{CasusBelliCite|HS24|Il faut sauver Miss Pickalho}}</ref>

{{stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
''Chez Norric'' is mentioned in ''[[Il faut sauver Miss Pickalho]]'', a French-language scenario for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' which appeared in ''[[Casus Belli]]'' magazine in March 1999. As this material was not approved through the [[Lucas Licensing]] process, it is considered non-canon.

In contrast with the [[Black Hole (cantina)|Black Hole]], ''Chez Norric'' is not directly described as a [[Cantina/Legends|cantina]].
