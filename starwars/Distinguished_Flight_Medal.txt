The '''Distinguished Flight Medal''' was an award granted by the [[Galactic Empire]] to [[Lieutenant]] [[Sparda]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> after they led an [[Operation to rescue Imperial service members|operation]] to rescue over forty [[Imperial Military|Imperial service members]]. The awarding of the medal was covered on the [[HoloNet News]] by [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Dorna Parros]], who claimed that the recipient demonstrated the Empire's courage and dedication to its members.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Distinguished Flight Medal was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" /> The [[Distinguished Flight Medal/Legends|medal]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'', a [[1987]] rulebook for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Curtis Smith]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="SWSourcebook">''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]''</ref>
