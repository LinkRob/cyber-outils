The '''5x5 Puzzle Cube''' was a cube-shaped combination puzzle. It was notoriously more difficult than other similar games, like the [[Cerean Puzzle Cube]], and posed a challenge to even the most technically-minded.<ref name="5x5" />

{{GameMechanics}}
During the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Iron Blockade|blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]], a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] from [[Burnin Konn]] asked a member of their crew to solve a 5x5 Puzzle Cube.<ref name="5x5" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The 5x5 Puzzle Cube was first mentioned in an optional Crew Run mission of the same name available in ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', a [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] developed by [[Kabam]], [[Disney Interactive]], and [[Lucasfilm]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms. The Crew Run required a single crew member to attempt, with a bonus for those with a technical background. If successful, players were rewarded with a high amount of experience for that crew member.<ref name="5x5" /> The game was released on [[September 10]], [[2015]],<ref name="SWNews">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-uprising-available-now|text=''Star Wars: Uprising'' Available Now}}</ref> and shut down on [[November 17]], [[2016]].<ref name="Shutting down">{{Kabam|subdomain=community|url=forums/showthread.php?675751-Star-Wars-Uprising-is-Shutting-Down|text=Discussion Thread: Star Wars Uprising is Shutting Down|date=2016-09-22}}</ref>
