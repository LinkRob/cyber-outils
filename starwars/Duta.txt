'''Duta''' was a [[Tholothian]] [[Jedi Master]] who served on the [[Jedi Council]]<ref name="JDH" /> at some point by [[1032 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|205|the [[Unidentified Jedi–Sith war|Jedi–Sith war]]}}</ref> While in a [[Jedi Council]] meeting, the group were approached by [[Ara]], a pilot from the planet [[Dolgarak]] who could see prophetic images on rocks when it rained. Ara's culture had two statues attuned to the force, representing the light and dark, and she proposed a mission to cut off the head of the Dark Side statue to influence the outcome of the Jedi's current [[Unidentified Jedi–Sith war|war]] with the [[Sith]]. While Duta's peer, Jedi Master [[Leesagum]], was skeptical of the idea, Duta was more sympathetic and assigned [[Toul]] to accompany Ara on their [[Mission to Dolgarak|mission]].<ref name="JDH" />

==Behind the scenes==
Duta appeared in "[[Journey to the Dark Head]]," the fifth episode of ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]{{'}}'' [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second volume]], which premiered on [[Disney+]] on [[May 4]], [[2023]]. They were voiced by [[Choi Soo Min]] in the Korean voice cast and [[Jonella Landry]] in the English voice cast.<ref name="SWCE 2023">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-visions|text=SWCE 2023: ''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Trailer, Key Art, and Cast Revealed}}</ref>
