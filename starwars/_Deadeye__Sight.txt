The '''"Deadeye" Sight''' was a model of [[blaster sight]] manufactured by [[Neuro-Saav Corp.|Neuro-Saav]]. It allowed its user to know where to aim when equipped to an energy weapon.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Deadeye" Sight was first mentioned in ''[[Forged in Battle]]'',<ref name="Forged in Battle" /> a [[2016]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Soldier On!">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/8/17/soldier-on-1|text=Soldier On!}}</ref>
