'''Eglyn Valmor''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] from the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] who came to serve as the [[Regent Administrator|regent administrator]] of the [[Alderaan Flotilla]]; a collection of [[starship]]s that carried the surviving natives of her [[homeworld]] after it was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]. In the [[Standard year|year]] [[5 ABY]], she attended a meeting to discuss her peoples' future on board the flotilla's [[Alderaanian escort frigate]] ''[[Sunspire]]'' while the flotilla was floating amongst the remains of Alderaan in the [[Alderaan system]]. At the meeting, Valmor argued that the flotilla should remain where their home had once been, while others argued that they should leave to resettle another world. The argument ended when several [[New Republic]] vessels arrived in the system to deliver the remains of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]], which Valmor declared could be used to build a new [[space station]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|We could build a whole space station of our own.|Valmor begins making plans for the Death Star remains|<ref name="Life Debt" />}}
Eglyn Valmor was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] native of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] who survived the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of her [[homeworld]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]<ref name="Life Debt" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' establishes that the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]] occurs in [[1 BBY]].</ref> Following Alderaan's destruction, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of Alderaan and the [[Rebel pilot]] [[Evaan Verlaine]] gathered together the surviving [[Alderaanian]]s and formed the [[Alderaan Flotilla]].<ref name="Life Debt" />

By the year [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> Valmor had been elected as [[regent Administrator]] of the flotilla, the [[starship]]s of which were positioned amongst the remains of Alderaan in the [[Alderaan system]]. Valmor attended a meeting on board the [[Alderaanian escort frigate]] ''[[Sunspire]]'' to discuss the future of the flotilla where she argued that they should remain where their homeworld had once been. Others at the meeting including the former [[teacher]] [[Icar Orliss]] claimed that they should instead find a new world to settle on.<ref name="Life Debt" />

This argument continued until Valmor and the others were made aware of the arrival of several [[New Republic]] vessels in the Alderaan [[Star system|system]] by [[communications officer]] [[Rickert Beagle]]. The vessels were hauling the remains of the Death Star with them, as Organa and Verlaine had worked with flotilla member [[Teven Gale]] to secure them as a resource for the Alderaanians. The regent administrator was initially shocked, but then stated they should use the remains to forge a [[space station]] of their own. Orliss still disagreed, but others began to concur with her plan.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|This is our home. ''This'' patch of sky is ours. Our world was here. And the diaspora has returned us to this place. We are home and I will not leave it.|Eglyn Valmor|<ref name="Life Debt" />}}
While arguing on the ''Sunspire'', Valmor paced up and down and fiddled with a loose braid of her [[Color|ice-blonde]] [[hair]]. Teven Gale considered her young, but believed she possessed vital [[heart]]. She was extremely pleased about the flotilla coming to possess the Death Star wreckage, staring at it with child-like wonderment.<ref name="Life Debt" />
