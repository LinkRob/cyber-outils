'''Eriaduan crustacean soup''' was a type of [[soup]] filled with [[crustacean]]s that [[Homeworld|originated]] from the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]]. It was [[Diet|edible]] by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[species]].<ref name="Fate of Eriadu" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: The Fate of Eriadu]]" take place after the [[expansion of the Stormwall]] and concludes with the beginning of the [[Battle of Eriadu]], both of which occur in [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:THRContent228BBY|here]].</ref> after being severely replenished of sustenance while trapped aboard a [[prison ship]],<ref name="Family Ties">{{InsiderCite|226|Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Family Ties}}</ref> the Mon Calamari [[Ghal Tarpfen]] had reverted to consuming the crustacean soup on Eriadu along with supplements of vitamins as part of a Mon Calamari diet to recover in her health.<ref name="Fate of Eriadu" />

Later, [[Republic Defense Coalition]] personnel [[Elping Tsoo]] and [[Visma Tarko]] confronted [[Joss Adren|Joss]] and [[Pikka Adren]] at their [[Adrens quarters|quarters]] on Eriadu.<ref name="Fate of Eriadu" /> All of them had saved Tarpfen's life aboard the [[prison]] [[Starship|ship]],<ref name="Family Ties" /> and they discussed several topics at the [[Adrens]]' quarters, including how Tarpfen was digesting the soup and vitamins to feel better.<ref name="Fate of Eriadu" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eriaduan crustacean soup was mentioned in the short story "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: The Fate of Eriadu]]," written by [[Lydia Kang]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Fate of Eriadu" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref> The story was published in the [[Star Wars Insider 229|229th issue]] of the magazine ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' as the sixth installment in the [[The High Republic: Tales from the Occlusion Zone|''Tales from the Occlusion Zone'']] serialized fiction on [[December 10]], [[2024]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/171-star-wars-insider-229|text=Star Wars Insider #229}}</ref>
