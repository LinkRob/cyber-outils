'''Ashaftans''' were a diminutive [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that had wooly [[Hair|fur]].<ref name="BL" /> In [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Timeframe">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/652893780236988420|[[Del Rey]]|quote=So excited to have @claudiagray writing a #StarWars novel with us. SW: New Republic: Bloodline coming 2016. Set 6 years before TFA.|archivefile=File:New Republic Bloodline Twitter Post.jpg}} establishes that the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] take place six [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|VII}}. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], so ''Bloodline'' must take place in [[28 ABY]].</ref> the Ashaftans were [[Senator|represented]] in the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]].<ref name="BL" />
