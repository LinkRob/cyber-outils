'''Fambola''', nicknamed "'''Fambola the Noble'''," was an elder [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Galactic Bake-Off Spectacular" />

Fambola was on the ''[[Voyager Dawn]]'' for a mediation retreat for elder Jedi when the ''[[Legacy Run]]'' split apart in hyperspace, causing fragments of the ship to crash through the [[Hetzal system]] at incredible speeds in an event known as the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]. The ''Voyager Dawn'' took significant damage during the Great Disaster and needed an emergency evacuation of all passengers. Jedi Master [[Kantam Sy]] responded to their call and assisted Fambola to the docking bay, believing Fambola to be the last evacuee. Fambola quickly informed the Jedi that Master [[Griswal]] had been left behind, pleading the others to find her. Thankfully, Ky turned back and successfully retrieved Griswal with the aid of Jedi Master [[Torban Buck]].<ref name="Galactic Bake-Off Spectacular" />
