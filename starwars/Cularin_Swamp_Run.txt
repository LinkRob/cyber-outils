The '''Cularin Swamp Run''' was a [[podracing]] [[Racetrack|course]] that shared its name<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> with the astronomical object [[Cularin]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> in the [[Cularin system]] of the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Thaere sector]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Cularin system]]</ref> The [[Dobatan]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Weebahn]] had won a race on the course prior to his disappearance while taking part in the [[Hydian-Hutta Podracing Circuit]]<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> around [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> The win was recorded on his downloadable [[Racer|racing]] profile.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Cularin Swamp Run was mentioned in the [[2025]] [[Industrial Light & Magic]] [[Star Wars video games|virtual reality game]] [[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]].<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where the [[Cularin Swamp Run/Legends|course]] appeared in "[[Peaces]]," a [[2002]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' adventure published by [[RPGA]] for the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign.<ref name="LivingForce">{{LivingForce|Peaces}}</ref>
