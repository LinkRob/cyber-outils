'''''Disney Gallery: The Mandalorian''''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' documentary series streaming on [[Disney+]] centered on the making of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''. It premiered on [[May 4]], [[2020]] alongside the [[Victory and Death|series finale]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' and the Disney+ debut of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="SW-Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker Coming to Disney+ on May the 4th">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker-coming-to-disney-on-may-the-4th|text=''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker'' Coming to Disney+ on May the 4th}}</ref>
