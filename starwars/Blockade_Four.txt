'''Blockade Four''' was one of four [[Nikardun blockade frigate]]s stationed by the forces of the [[Nikardun Destiny]] to blockade the [[Rapacc system]] during the [[Nikardun conquests]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

Like other [[Nikardun]] [[warship]]s, Blockade Four was distinguished with a very large [[viewport]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
