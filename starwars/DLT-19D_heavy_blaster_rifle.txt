The '''DLT-19D heavy blaster rifle''' was a long-range [[heavy blaster rifle]] and [[longblaster]] that was a variant of the [[DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle]].

==Description==
A variant of the [[DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle]]<ref name="Bandai" /> produced by [[BlasTech Industries]] and sold for 3,100 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]],<ref name="Dawn" /> the DLT-19D heavy blaster rifle<ref name="Bandai" /> was a model of long-range<ref name="SC">{{WebCite|url=https://www.sideshow.com/collectibles/star-wars-death-trooper-specialist-sideshow-collectibles-300530?var=3005301|text=Star Wars Death Trooper Specialist Premium Format|archivedate=20200110161904}}</ref> [[heavy blaster rifle]]<ref name="Bandai" /> that also served as a [[longblaster]]. It was also able to act as a [[sniper rifle]].<ref name="BF2" /> A unique galven pattern with auxiliary barrels that tapped into an expanded central energy chamber provided higher power output and an increased rate of fire. The weapon featured an [[scope|optical telescopic sight]] to improve long-range accuracy<ref name="Dawn" /> for [[Sniper|sniping]] purposes<ref name="HC80">{{HelmetCollectionCite|80|Helmets}}</ref> and two underbarrel devices, one of which was a [[glowrod]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

==History==
===Capture of Galen Erso===
====Overview and landing on Lah'mu====
[[File:Displate-NightWatch.png|thumb|left|270px|The DLT-19D was part of the standard equipment kit for the elite death troopers of the Galactic Empire.]]
The DLT-19D was part of the standard equipment kit available to the [[Galactic Empire]]'s elite [[death trooper]]s,<ref name="CharacterNew">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition]]''</ref> resulting in some members of the [[death trooper division]] wielding the [[heavy blaster rifle]] while on duty, such as in the field or while on guard duty,<ref name="Rogue One" /> as those death troopers preferred it to the [[E-11D blaster carbine]]<ref name="HC80" /> also present within their standard kit.<ref name="CharacterNew" /> Those death troopers who used the rifle were designated as DLT-19D Troopers. During one incident in a rocky environment, a DLT-19-equipped death trooper took aim with the rifle as [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter|TIE strikers]] flew overhead.<ref name="DLTTrooper">{{Legion|set=Imperial Death Troopers Unit Expansion|cardname=DLT-19D Trooper}}</ref> The DLT-19D was used by some of the death troopers within [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]]'s [[Krennic's guard squadron|guard squadron]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> it was wielded by three of the six troopers who [[Capture of Galen Erso|accompanied]] the director across the plains of<ref name="Rogue One" /> the planet [[Lah'mu]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="UVG" /> where the former Imperial [[scientist]] [[Galen Walton Erso]] had taken to hiding with his [[Erso family|family]]. The other three death troopers arrived with E-11Ds.<ref name="Rogue One" /> Beyond serving in their typical capacity as Krennic's guardians,<ref name="CharacterNew" /> the gathered death troopers acted as a death squad<ref name="R1Novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> and the muscle who would force Erso to come back to the Empire,<ref name="CharacterNew" /> under which Erso had previously helped to develop [[Project Stardust]] until discovering the damage his work could do. As Krennic greeted Erso and tried to convince his former friend to return of his own free will, the death troopers stood by<ref name="R1Novel" /> silently, all while wielding their E-11Ds and DLT-19Ds.<ref name="Rogue One" /> The rifles, as well as the [[blaster pistol]]s, of each soldier were noticed by Erso, who believed the weaponry granted each death trooper the appearance of being ready for war.<ref name="R1Novel" />

====Target: Lyra Erso====
{{Quote|Do it.|Director Krennic gives the command to fire on Lyra Erso|<ref name="Rogue One" />}}
As he spoke with Erso, Krennic dispatched four death troopers&mdash;including two armed with DLT-19Ds&mdash;to search the [[Erso homestead]], only for Erso's wife [[Lyra Erso|Lyra]] to reveal herself with a [[blaster pistol]], threatening Krennic as the two remaining death troopers, including the remaining DLT-19D-equipped trooper, took notice<ref name="Rogue One" /> after Krennic pointed out her arrival.<ref name="R1Novel" /> While the DLT-19D-equipped trooper focused on and aimed at Lyra, the other soldier, armed with an [[E-11D blaster carbine]], forced Galen to stay in place via their blaster.<ref name="Rogue One" /> At Krennic's order, the trooper did not fire on Lyra<ref name="R1Novel" /> but kept their weapon raised at the woman,<ref name="Rogue One" /> who herself did not look at the death trooper but instead directly at Krennic.<ref name="R1Novel" /> While both Ersos were kept at blaster-point,<ref name="Rogue One" /> even if Lyra noticed they were holding their blasters low due to Krennic's order,<ref name="R1Novel" /> Krennic gave the Ersos a chance for come with him as "heroes of the Empire,"<ref name="Rogue One" /> a term that hid the life awaiting them as well-treated [[prisoner]]s.<ref name="RebelDossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref> Ultimately accepting Lyra's refusal, he then ordered the DLT-19D-equipped trooper to shoot her.<ref name="Rogue One" />

[[File:Death_troopers.png|thumb|right|300px|Death troopers armed with DLT-19Ds on Lah'mu in 13 BBY.]]
Before the command, both death troopers were,<ref name="Rogue One" /> despite their full armor covering their features,<ref name="Rogue One" /> clearly tense, which was noticed by Lyra, who knew all she had to do was properly twitch to fire on Krennic, even if she herself was shot.<ref name="R1Novel" /> Nonetheless, the soldier quickly complied with Krennic's command by firing the DLT-19D and killed Lyra, whose own blast<ref name="Rogue One" />&mdash;fired as soon as Krennic gave the command to kill her<ref name="R1Novel" />&mdash;only managed to injure Krennic with a shot to the shoulder;<ref name="Rogue One" /> realizing she had underestimated Krennic, Lyra had nonetheless found strength in the knowledge that her and Galen's daughter, [[Jyn Erso|Jyn]], would be safe and attempted to kill Krennic with her blaster shot to save her husband, but the death trooper's shot made her own blast go wide. She only heard<ref name="R1Novel" /> the DLT-19D's shot<ref name="Rogue One" /> after feeling the pain of the blaster bolt spread across her body. Struggling through his own pain,<ref name="R1Novel" /> Krennic revealed Jyn's existence to his death troopers and ordered them to find her, leaving the soldiers, including those with the DLT-19D, to search across the area for the young girl.<ref name="Rogue One" />

Unknown to all, Jyn had left her hiding place and watched her mother's execution<ref name="Rogue One" /> in horror,<ref name="RogueOneJunior">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> quickly then choosing to flee back to safety inside a cave. One death trooper armed with a DLT-19D joined another trooper in searching through that nearby cave but quickly departed, believing there was no trace of the girl and unaware that the young Erso was watching from both from behind cover. Despite the failure to find Jyn, the death troopers departed from Lah'mu with Krennic, who had successfully captured Galen and could force him back to work on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> In Krennic's mind, delaying the death troopers' response to Lyra's presence had been an act of selflessness and a chance for her to come with them for Galen's benefit. To Krennic, he had ignored what he thought was the more tactically sound option of allowing the death troopers to kill her outright, as he knew the soldiers would have preferred, in order to give her the chance to stand down.<ref name="R1Novel" />

===Galactic Civil War and beyond===
{{Quote|I'm going!|Rebel Taidu Sefla, immediately before being shot with a DLT-19D|<ref name="Rogue One" />}}
[[File:FirstOrderDLT19D-GalaxysEdge4.png|thumb|left|250px|A DLT-19D used by the First Order]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> two death troopers armed with DLT-19Ds stood guard outside of Krennic's [[Delta-class T-3c shuttle|''Delta''-class T-3c shuttle]] ''[[ST-149]]'' on<ref name="Rogue One" />  the Outer Rim planet<ref name="UVG" /> [[Eadu]] shortly before a [[Mission to Eadu|rebel raid]]. Shortly thereafter, members of Krennic's guard continued to use the heavy [[blaster rifle]] during the [[Battle of Scarif]], which marked the formal start of open war between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the Empire. A shot from a DLT-19D was used to kill [[Chirrut Îmwe]] and wound [[Baze Malbus]], members of the [[Guardians of the Whills]] who had joined with the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Rogue One" />

[[Standard year|Years]] later, the DLT-19D was used by [[Sniper|specialist]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] within the [[First Order]], the successor state to the Empire. Those specialists utilized the weapon as a [[sniper rifle]] and used DLT-19s in the same color scheme as the Empire.<ref name="BF2" /> Meanwhile, a DLT-19D rifle in service to a stormtrooper of the [[709th Legion]] incorporated white details seen on most other [[First Order Army]] weapons. As part of the First Order's [[Battle of Batuu|growing presence]] on the planet [[Batuu]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War]]<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 4">[[Galaxy's Edge 4|''Galaxy's Edge'' 4]]</ref> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> that 709th stormtrooper armed with a DLT-19D was deployed to [[Black Spire Outpost]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I worked as weapon artist on Star Wars BattleFront II. This is the DLT-19D. I was able to reuse a lot of the high poly from the original DLT-19 from Battlefront 2015, made by Erik Hallberg.|Joel Dabrosin, on the DLT-19D in ''Battlefront II''|<ref name="ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=joeldabrosin|url=artwork/19rE8|name=[[Joel Dabrosin]]|archivedate=20201121203333}}</ref>}}
The DLT-19D heavy blaster rifle was first seen in the [[2016]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Rogue One" /> The weapon reappeared in the video game ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'', with [[DICE]] weapon artist [[Joel Dabrosin]] designing its in-game model. Dabrosin was able to pull much of the high polygon work from the standard DLT-19, which had already been designed by artist [[Erik Hallberg]] for [[2015]]'s ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''.<ref name="ArtStation" />

[[File:RogueOne-DeathTroopersaim.png|thumb|right|300px|A death trooper aims a DLT-19D at Lyra Erso in ''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story''.]]
The DLT-19D is never outright named in the [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One'' novelization]] by [[Alexander Freed]], but the book's novelization of scenes<ref name="R1Novel" /> that feature the DLT-19D in the film<ref name="Rogue One" /> confirms its appearance within, even if it is only called a blaster or rifle in the text alone.<ref name="Rogue One" /> The same is true for the [[Star Wars: Rogue One: A Junior Novel|junior novelization]] by [[Matt Forbeck]].<ref name="RogueOneJunior" /> The Freed's novelization suggests that<ref name="R1Novel" /> the two<ref name="Rogue One" /> death troopers standing with Krennic when Lyra Erso revealed herself on Lah'mu aimed at the woman, culminating in her being shot multiple times.<ref name="R1Novel" /> As seen in the film, however, only the death trooper with the DLT-19D aimed at Lyra, whereas the E-11D-equipped trooper kept it trained on Galen Erso, and Lyra is only shot once by the DLT-19D. In the film, the trooper also raises their weapon at Lyra off-camera between shots,<ref name="Rogue One" /> whereas the novelization sees the troopers raise their blasters at Lyra almost immediately after she shows herself.<ref name="R1Novel" /> A similar mistake is seen in the junior novelization, which sees multiple death troopers aim at Lyra as she appears and later fire on her,<ref name="RogueOneJunior" /> as opposed to a single death trooper taking the shot.<ref name="Rogue One" /> This article sides with the film's depiction of events.
