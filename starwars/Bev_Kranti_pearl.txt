'''Bev Kranti pearls''' were a type of [[pearl]] produced by [[Bev Kranti clam]]s from the [[planet]] [[Chandel]]. Bev Kranti [[clam]]s took 150 [[Standard year|years]] to produce several pearls at once which were considered quite valuable. The peals had an iridescent [[color]] that was almost invisible to [[human]] [[eye]]s, however [[Mon Calamari]] could see the vibrant pulsing and shifting color of the pearl. In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a pearl could sell for 2000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

During 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] was tasked with retrieving a Bev Kranti clam from the [[Pyke Syndicate]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. After aquiring the clam, Vess brought it to her [[contract broker]] [[Danka Orsato]] who proceeded to sell it to an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] officer. However Vess was able to palm a pearl from the clam before the Imperial officer took possession of it. After the exchange Danka requested Vess meet her at [[Makal's Gambling Parlor]] in the [[capital]] [[city]] [[Mirogana]]. When Vess arrived, Danka revealed that she knew that Vess had taken one of the pearls and she offered to broker the sale of it to Crimson Dawn or the Ashiga Clan.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Bev Kranti clams first appeared in [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' which was published by [[Ubisoft]] and developed by [[Massive Entertainment]]. The quest involving the clam has two choices for the player to decide which changes how things progress. The first is whether to pay a bride to the imperial officer. If the player doesn't pay it they will be assigned a wanted level of four and will have to evade the Empire. The second is what to do with the pearl, they can choose to keep the pearl which sells for 2000 credits, or give it to one of the two syndicates in exchange for increased reputation with the chosen syndicate.<ref name="Outlaws" />
