'''Black Squadron''' was the name of a number of fighter [[squadron]]s throughout [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]]:

==[[Canon]]==
*'''[[Black Squadron (Galactic Empire)|Black Squadron]]''', an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] [[squadron]] that participated in the [[Battle of Yavin]].
*'''[[Black Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Black Squadron]]''', a Rebel [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|fighter]] [[squadron]].
*'''[[Black Squadron (TIE striker)|Black Squadron]]''', an Imperial [[atmospheric fighter]] squadron of [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter]]s.
*'''[[Black Squadron (First Order)|Black Squadron]]''', a [[First Order]] [[starfighter]] squadron active during the [[Cold War|conflict]] against the [[Resistance]].
*'''[[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]]''', a [[Resistance]] starfighter squadron active during the conflict against the First Order. <br />

==[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]]==
*'''[[Black Squadron (Galactic Empire)/Legends|Black Squadron]]''', an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter/Legends|TIE fighter]] [[Squadron/Legends|squadron]] that participated in the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]].
