'''Essovin''' was a [[Trandoshan]] [[Gender|female]] [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] who flew in the [[New Republic]]'s [[Flare Squadron]] as [[Flare Leader]]. Along with the rest of the [[squadron]], Essovin accompanied [[Commander]] [[Wyl Lark]] to investigate a damaged [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[convoy]]. Essovin later participated at the [[Battle of Jakku]] but was killed.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
