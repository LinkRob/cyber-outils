The '''Ferocious Fighter''' was a costume sometimes worn by the [[Wookiee]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Grozz]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Mighty Champion|standard costume]]. It featured a number of fancy elements, including [[Color|gold]] and silver coatings, a [[helmet]], a large golden chain, and both [[Limb|forearm]] and knee guards.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ferocious Fighter was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Grozz, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 5.0.0 alongside Season 5 on [[January 27]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Ferocious Hammers]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching the rank Kyber I within ''Hunter's'' "Ranked" mode during the game's fifth season.<ref name="Hunters" />
