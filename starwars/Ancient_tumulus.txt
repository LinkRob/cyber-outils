An '''ancient tumulus''' dating back to ancient times was discovered on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] at the time of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]] by archeologist [[Schardak]]. According to Schardak, this tomb was built roughly 100,000 years before the Imperial Era, and its builders were the first civilization to have lived on the planet.<ref name="Dieu mort" />

An ancient [[Mummy/Legends|mummy]] wrapped in metallic cloth was discovered in the tumulus. Analysis revealed this mummy was cursed by ancient [[Magic/Legends|magic]] and was in fact a monster whose aim was to rule [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] by investing [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine's]] body.<ref name="Dieu mort" />

==Behind the scenes==
This mysterious tumulus was created by [[Jean Balczesak]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] scenario "[[Le dieu mort]]" which appeared in the French magazine [[Casus Belli 66|''Casus Belli'' 66]].

The builders of the tumulus might have been the [[Kumumgah/Legends|Kumumgah]], as they once were a technologically-advanced race of Tatooine in the pre-Republic era.<ref name="KOTOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref><ref name="DOTJ1">[[Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm 1|''Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm'' 1]]</ref>
