'''Code one-six-three''' was a military code used by the [[Galactic Empire]] during its [[Imperial Era|reign]] of [[the galaxy]]. It related to prioritizing fugitives.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|option=1}}
In [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Imperial dispatcher]] reported a code one-six-three to prioritize the arrest of the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]], after she was involved in multiple Imperial [[death]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Code one-six-three. The fugitive is your highest priority.|An Imperial dispatcher invoking Code one-six-three|audio=CodeOneSixThree-Outlaws.flac|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
Code one-six-three is identified by the Imperial dispatcher comm messages as part of the wanted system in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
