The '''AS-M12-series droid''' was a model of bespoke and familiar [[Courier droid|messenger droid]] used by [[Imperial Intelligence]] services of the [[Galactic Empire]] such as the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]. Because they used the AS-M12-series and [[MSE series]] messenger droids, the Imperial intelligence services rarely used [[Cybot Galactica]]'s popular [[M4-series Messenger Droid]].<ref name="C&M" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the AS-M12-series droid was mentioned in the [[2018]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Cyphers and Masks]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="C&M" />
