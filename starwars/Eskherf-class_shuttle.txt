The '''''Eskherf''-class shuttle''' was a model of [[transport]] manufactured by [[Sublight Products Corporation]]. The [[shuttle]] could accommodate a small crew, and had a [[Color|silver]]-colored [[hull]] with four [[wing]]s pointing downward from the [[starship]]'s body. A modified ''Eskherf''-class named the [[Edgehawk (starship)|''Edgehawk'']] was used by [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] and [[T'onga's crew|her crew]] during their missions<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> until she was forced to sell the ship in order to continue paying her crew.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35">[[Bounty Hunters 35|''Bounty Hunters'' 35]]</ref>
