The '''Broken Chain Clan''' were a group of [[pirate]]s whose membership consisted of ex-prisoners and criminals that had banded together. They attacked [[starship]]s to take their supplies and cargo. During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], the Broken Chain Clan were enemies of the [[Ithorian]] [[antiques dealer]] [[Dok-Ondar]].<ref name="GEStaff" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Broken Chain Clan was first created for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at Disneyland Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="GEStaff" />
