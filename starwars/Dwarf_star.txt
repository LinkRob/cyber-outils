A '''dwarf star''' was a type of [[star]]. There were many dwarf stars in [[the galaxy]] including, [[white dwarf]]s, [[brown dwarf]]s, and [[red dwarf]]s. When [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was helping ''[[Chimaera]]'' to navigate through space using [[the Force]], he realized a white dwarf ahead and changed the ship's course.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>
