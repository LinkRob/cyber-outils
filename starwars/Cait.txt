'''Cait''' was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. She served as a [[Forward Air Controller]] for the [[company]], directing their [[X-wing starfighter]]s to their targets.<ref name="Inbrief" />

At some point between [[2 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the 61st was deployed onto the [[planet]] [[Allst Prime]] to [[Battle of Allst Prime|attack]] the [[Allst Prime Imperial outpost|Imperial outpost]] there. Cait was [[Death|killed]] during the battle, and [[Micha Evon]], the [[captain]] of the 61st, took her receiver and began yelling orders to the X-wings. At the same [[time]], the [[bounty hunter]] [[Brand]] saw Cait's body from afar and examined the signs of trauma on her.<ref name="Inbrief" />
