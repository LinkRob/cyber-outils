'''Ailee''' was an [[Eirami]] [[port commander]] who worked in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Eiram]]'s [[capital]] [[city]], [[Erasmus Capital City|Erasmus]], as part of [[Eiram's military]]. In [[382 BBY]], the commander ordered [[Captain]] [[Plana Van]] to [[Klytobacter incident|investigate]] the explosion of a downed [[Unidentified transport (Path of the Open Hand)|ship]] on Eiram and the Outer Rim planet [[E'ronoh]]'s shared [[moon]], [[Eirie]]. As the situation unfolded, Ailee reported to Eiram's [[Queen]] [[Adrialla]] and the [[Eirami royalty|royal]] court on the issue, indicating that Van was boarding the [[Starship|vessel]] under her directive.

After the captain discovered the [[Bioweapon|biological]] [[weapon]] [[klytobacter]] aboard the damaged Eirie vessel, Ailee relayed Adrialla's [[advisor]]s' orders to destroy the ship. As news of the failed [[Summit on Jedha|peace talks]] with E'ronoh came through the [[Comm unit|comms]], she further instructed Van's crew to destroy the [[communications buoy]]s being used by the [[E'roni]] to call their [[E'ronoh's military|forces]] home to fight Eiram. After the latter successfully destroyed the devices, helped by E'roni [[pilot]]s also seeking to break down communications, the captain reported the crew's success to Ailee.

==Biography==
===A lunar situation===
{{Quote|Captain Van. I'm ordering you to land on the moon and check for casualties.''"<br />"''But there's no evidence&mdash;''"<br />"''Captain Van. You may be a hauler, but as reserve military, you still obey my orders as your port's commanding officer.''"<br />"''Yes, Commander.|Ailee and Plana Van|<ref name="Cataclysm" />}}
[[File:Eiram-THRShow.png|thumb|left|270px|Ailee worked on Eiram as a port commander.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Eirami]] Ailee resided on their [[homeworld]], the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Eiram]], and worked as a [[port commander]] for [[Eiram's military]] in the world's [[capital]] [[city]], [[Erasmus Capital City|Erasmus]]. During a cease-fire in the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]]<ref name="Cataclysm" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> a downed [[Unidentified transport (Path of the Open Hand)|ship]] malfunctioned and [[Klytobacter incident|exploded]] on [[Eirie]], the [[moon]] between the planet and its [[Double planet|twin world]], the Outer Rim planet [[E'ronoh]]. From Eiram's surface, [[sensor]]s picked up evidence of the incident, causing concern among port officials monitoring the situation. Ailee contacted the crew of a [[Plana Van's transport|transport ship]] returning to Eiram that was near Eirie. The [[commander]] asked for a status update from the [[Starship|vessel's]] [[captain]], [[Plana Van]], and the latter assured the commander that their vessel was unaffected. However, Van's crew began speculating on the incident's possible causes, which Ailee overheard over her open [[Comm unit|comms]].<ref name="Cataclysm" />

Despite the cease-fire with E'ronoh, she insisted that the crew remain prepared for an attack by the [[E'roni]]. The commander further instructed Van to investigate the explosion on Eirie despite her reservations, making the directive an explicit order. The captain led their vessel closer to the moon and was informed by [[Lieutenant]] [[Gunnaw]] of the [[E'ronoh's military]] [[Thylefire Squadron]] that the malfunctioning ship had been looted by [[pirate]]s and landed in neutral territory to make repairs. However, when Gunnaw was reluctant to provide more details, Van contacted Ailee to apprise them of the situation. The port commander felt the incident was suspicious, indicating that she would send four [[crescent fighter]]s to assist the captain's crew in searching the downed vessel to ensure the occupants' safety. Ailee urged the group to hurry, indicating that more E'roni ships would arrive soon, but also indicated that no shots should be fired unless necessary, as Eiram and E'ronoh's [[ambassador]]s may have already [[Summit on Jedha|signed]] a [[Eiram–E'ronoh peace treaty|peace treaty]] on the moon [[Jedha]] by that point.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

===Ship boarded===
{{Dialogue|Phan-tu Zenn|We have an incoming transmission. Speak, Commander Ailee.|Ailee|Our pilot is boarding the ship.|Adrialla|On whose authority?|Ailee|Mine. There could be Eirami citizens on board. They're in neutral territory.|attr=Phan-tu Zenn, Ailee, and Adrialla about the downed ship on Eirie|src=<ref name="Cataclysm" />}}
[[File:EiramandEronohsystem.jpg|thumb|right|280px|Ailee assisted Van over comm as their vessel boarded a downed ship on Eirie (bottom right).]]
News of the explosion quickly reached the [[Eiram palace]], where an [[advisor]] informed the Eirami [[Queen]] [[Adrialla]] that Ailee wished for the passengers of the damaged vessel to be questioned. The port commander then spoke to the queen and Eiram's [[Eirami royalty|royal]] court via transmission, telling those present that Van was boarding the ship under her orders. Although Eiram's [[prince]], [[Phan-tu Zenn]], protested the boarding as a breach of E'ronoh's trust, Ailee informed the court that the captain had already done so. At the same [[time]], Van discovered the [[Bioweapon|biological]] [[weapon]] [[klytobacter]] aboard the downed ship, which was operated by the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] members [[Binnot Ullo]] and [[Goi Ganok]]&mdash;who both denied knowledge of the weapon. However, the pair had actually been secretly instructed by the cult [[Mother (title)|leader]], [[Elecia Zeveron|the Mother]], to sow distrust between Eiram and E'ronoh to shatter the worlds' fragile peace by bringing such a dangerous substance into the [[Eiram system|system]]. Van apprised Ailee of the situation and Gunnaw's role in attempting to conceal it, of which she then informed Adrialla's court.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

The majority of the queen's advisors ordered the vessel be destroyed, which the port commander told Van's ship to carry out. Moments later, the comms flooded with the news of Eiram and E'ronoh's failed peace talks on Jedha, cutting off Van's reply to the port commander. Before the captain's ship could destroy the bioweapon containers, the Eirami crescent [[Starfighter|fighters]] with their ship fired on Gunnaw and the vessel on Eirie's surface. The port commander ordered Van to capture Ullo and Ganok as the pair fled the wreckage of their vessel, as the pair needed to be questioned on the klytobacter's origins. However, as Van's crew moved to do so, Ailee then urgently indicated to all nearby Eirami ships over [[Comlink|comm]] that the crew needed to destroy the [[communications buoy]]s calling E'roni reinforcements back to their homeworld, or Eiram would be inundated by enemy ships. After a brief skirmish, Van's crew eliminated the necessary [[buoy]]s&mdash;aided by E'roni [[pilot]]s also seeking to limit offworld communications by destroying the devices&mdash;and informed an exhausted Ailee of their success. During the investigation afterward, Ullo and Ganok's true intentions to disrupt peace were discovered.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You have it on my authority to fire on that cargo ship. It's carrying an outlawed bioweapon. E'ronoh has broken the pact of the cease-fire.|Ailee, to Plana Van|<ref name="Cataclysm" />}}
Ailee was fiercely loyal to Eiram and deeply suspicious of enemies of her world, choosing to remain wary of most E'roni. By the time they served in Eiram's military, Ailee had risen to the [[Assignment|position]] of port commander and felt comfortable issuing orders to those in Eiram's reserve forces. Despite Van's objections, the port commander felt the Eirie incident was suspicious and ordered the captain to investigate. She kept a calm and professional demeanor when reporting to Adrialla and her advisors, reporting on the situation to them and also coordinating several Eirami ships' destruction of communications buoys. Ailee was willing to follow the orders of their superiors, passing on the message to destroy the vessel landed on Eirie due to the klytobacter onboard.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|E'ronoh is sending out a distress call to their out-of-sector pilots. Both planets sent a large contingent to Jedha, but we're having trouble contacting ours. If those E'roni reinforcements come in&mdash;we'll be inundated. We have to stop the message.|Ailee, over comms to several Eirami ships|<ref name="Cataclysm" />}}
The port commander was an efficient leader, capable of giving orders in high stress situations. To keep Eiram safe, Ailee sought to use her skills and position to coordinate the most beneficial outcome for their homeworld.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Equipment==
While working as a port commander, Ailee had access to communications equipment that could be used to contact Eiram's palace and any Eirami ships near Eiram. They also wielded authority over a number of Eirami crescent fighters, which the commander deployed to assist Van.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I think I vaguely had her in mind to be nonbinary, but to be honest, I can't remember.|Lydia Kang, about Ailee|<ref name="Email">{{EmailCite|email=File:LydiaKangEmailAilee4VOTG.png|creator=[[Lydia Kang]]|date=2023-11-22}}</ref>}}
Ailee's voice appeared in [[The High Republic: Cataclysm|''Cataclysm'']], a novel written by [[Lydia Kang]] and published in [[2023]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Cataclysm" /> Phase II.<ref name="Phase II">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-cataclysm-excerpt|text=The Jedi Arrive at Dalna in ''The High Republic: Cataclysm'' - Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref> When asked about the character's gender identity, Kang indicated that due to the port commander's limited role in ''Cataclysm'', she had not put much thought into it. The author also stated that she may have vaguely had Ailee in mind to be [[Wikipedia:Non-binary gender|non-binary]], but ultimately could not remember clearly.<ref name="Email" />
