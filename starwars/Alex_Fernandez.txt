'''Alex Fernandez''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] actor. He voiced [[Addy Ingrol]], [[Rickard Organa]], [[Shar-Da]], [[Velan-Raz]], [[Velasu Graege]], and additional voices in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.

Fernandez has also appeared in several television series including [[Wikipedia:Commander in Chief (TV series)|''Commander in Chief'']], [[Wikipedia:Without a Trace|''Without a Trace'']], [[Wikipedia:Devious Maids|''Devious Maids'']], and [[Wikipedia:Killer Women|''Killer Women'']].
