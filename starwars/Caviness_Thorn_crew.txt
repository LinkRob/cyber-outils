The '''Caviness Thorn crew''' was a [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunting]] crew that operated during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 12" /> Around [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashbacks of [[Bounty Hunters 12|''Bounty Hunters'' 12]] to around [[5 BBY]].</ref> the crew took a [[bounty]] for a [[smuggler]] and traveled to the<ref name="Bounty Hunters 12" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Abregado-rae|Abregado-Rae]] where they were to [[Death|take out]] the target. Whilst on Abregado-Rae, Thorn allowed the [[cyborg]] bounty hunter [[Beilert Valance]] to join the crew for the job as they were down a gun for the hit. As the crew made their way to the [[Abregado-Rae Spaceport]] where the target was and got themselves into position, Valance saw who the target was, his old friend [[Han Solo]] and attempted to stop Thorn from killing him. In the ensuing fight, the other two members of the crew were killed, which left Thorn to attempt to claim the bounty alone, but was stopped again by Valance. Shortly after, Solo left the [[spaceport]] aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 12" />
