'''Boba Fett's motif''' is a piece written by [[John Williams]] for [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back Original Soundtrack|The Empire Strikes Back soundtrack]] and was performed by the [[London Symphony Orchestra]] in [[1980]]. It represents the character [[Boba Fett]] and is heard twice during the track "Departure of Boba Fett", firstly, when Fett escorts the carbon-frozen [[Han Solo]] through [[Cloud City]], and secondly, when he orders Solo placed aboard his ship.

Another motif associated with Boba Fett is heard during the tracks "Imperial Starfleet", "City in the Clouds" and "Betrayal at Bespin", when Fett is hired by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], stealthily pursues the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', and reveals himself on Cloud City, respectively.
