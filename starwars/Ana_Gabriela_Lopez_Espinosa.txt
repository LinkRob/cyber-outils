'''Ana Gabriela Lopez Espinosa''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan who designed the [[droid]] [[T0-J4]] for the [[Build My Droid Contest]]. Lopez Espinosa was a Runner Up Winner,<ref name="Winners">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-build-my-droid-contest-winner|text=Meet KP-1, Winner of the ''Star Wars'' 'Build My Droid' Contest}}</ref> earning her an official 3D models of their droid.<ref name="Rewards">{{WebCite|url=https://shortyawards.com/12th/first-star-wars-force-for-change-build-my-droid-contest|work=[https://shortyawards.com Shorty Awards]|text=FIRST & Star Wars: Force for Change: Build My Droid Contest|archivedate=20260528181919}}</ref>
