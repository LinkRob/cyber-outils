'''2010 Topps ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Rise of the Bounty Hunters''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] in [[2010]]. It was based on [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|season two]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Topps site" /> which was marketed under the name "Rise of the Bounty Hunters."<ref name="The Rise of the Bounty Hunters Begins">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/news20090928/index.html|text=The Rise of the Bounty Hunters Begins!}}</ref> The base set contains 90 cards and was advertised as having four cards from each episode of the season.<ref name="Topps site" />
