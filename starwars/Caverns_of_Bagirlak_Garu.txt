The '''Caverns of Bagirlak Garu''' were a system of deep mazelike caves on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. [[Niima]] the [[Hutt]] controlled all traffic that moved through the caverns and the narrow [[Yiulong Canyons]] of Jakku. The [[Valley of the Eremite]] was located beyond Niima's canyons and caverns.<ref name="Empire's End" />
