'''Cerveteri Slane''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served in [[Crimson Dawn]].<ref name="CC" /> Around [[13 BBY]],<ref name="CCDate2">The chapters of ''[[Crimson Climb]]'' in which Cerveteri Slane works on [[Corellia]] take place in the [[Standard week|weeks]] following [[Han Solo|Han's]] escape from the [[planet]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[13 BBY]].</ref> Slane worked on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] under the [[Galactic Empire]], going under the alias of Manager '''Veteri'''. While trying to obtain control boards for Crimson Dawn, a [[Heist on a Corellian shipyard|raid]] occurred on the shipyard by the [[White Worms]], a criminal gang who operated on Corellia. Later, [[Qi'ra]], a participant in the raid, entered Crimson Dawn and became well known and respected amongst peers, which angered Slane, who tried to [[Death|kill]] Qi'ra. The attempt ultimately failed after Qi'ra used [[Teräs Käsi]] on Slane, who submitted to Qi'ra. While one of many selected to go on a mission to retrieve a [[Jedi]] artifact, Slane was killed by Qi'ra when the temple's roof they were in started to descend, crushing Slane after Qi'ra used Teräs Käsi, breaking Slane's knees and leaving her to be crushed by the roof.<ref name="CC" />
