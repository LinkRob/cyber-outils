'''Docking Bay 3''' was a docking bay located along [[Merchant Row (Batuu)|Merchant Row]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. It was Batuu's primary landing site for [[Black Spire Outpost transport|transports]] going to and from the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''.<ref name="SWGS Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)|''Star Wars'': Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)]]</ref>
