'''Director''' was a title referring to the head of an organization.

==History==
[[Aiyah Pellorean]] worked as a [[research director]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and developed [[Test Unit 08-B]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Armand Isard]] was Director of the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> [[Sliro Barsha]] served as the Director of the bureau's [[Zerek Besh]] undercover division and later briefly his brother [[Jaylen Vrax]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> Under the title of [[Director of Advanced Weapons Research]],<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> [[Orson Callan Krennic]] served as Director of [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]] prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]]. He was in charge of [[Project Stardust]], which was the code name for the [[Death Star]].<ref name="SW:R1:TUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[General]]<ref name="Chaos Agent 3">[[Doctor Aphra - Chaos Agent 3|''Doctor Aphra - Chaos Agent'' 3]]</ref> [[Meteora Gray]] served as director of the [[New Republic]]'s [[Department of Artifacts and Archival Goods Retrieval]].<ref name="Chaos Agent 1" /> [[Orlok (First Order)|Orlok]] served as the director of the [[First Order Security Bureau]].<ref name="SAD,2" />
