'''Chagar IX''',<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> also known as '''Chagar''',<ref name="Map" /> was a [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Helmet Collection 34">{{HelmetCollectionCite|34|Databank A-Z}}</ref> located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was the homeworld of [[stormtrooper]] [[Sergeant]] [[Kreel]], and fell under the control of the [[Galactic Empire]] in Kreel's youth.

==History==
{{Quote|I was doomed to die in the arena, just like my father had. Just like my mother and everyone I'd ever known. Until the stormtroopers came. Until the Empire made life worth living.|Kreel|<ref name="Star Wars 21" />}}
The culture of Chagar IX primarily revolved around its [[fighting pit]]s, which saw those too poor to afford seats in the stands forced to fight in the pits themselves as [[gladiator]]s. Many individuals [[Death|died]] in the arena, including [[Kreel]]'s [[Kreel's father|father]] and [[Kreel's mother|mother]], and many of his acquaintances. Kreel himself survived once [[stormtrooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> [[501st Legion]]<ref name="Star Wars 37" /> arrived on Chagar IX. The Empire toppled the overlords of the pits in a [[Standard day|day]], and went on to close the arenas down and give jobs to the population. Kreel ultimately joined the [[Imperial Academy]] and the [[Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="Star Wars 21" />
