'''''3 Tales of Adventure''''' is a compendium title that collects three previously published ''[[Star Wars]]'' young-readers stories in the [[World of Reading]] series: [[Death Star Battle (book)|''Death Star Battle'']], ''[[Use the Force!]]'', and [[Rescue from Jabba's Palace (World of Reading)|''Rescue from Jabba's Palace]]. It was released by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[September 6]], [[2016]], and includes a companion CD to help readers listen along with the story.

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Learn to use the Force with Luke, rescue Han from Jabba's Palace, and witness the battle for the Death Star! This level two Star Wars reader bind up features three stories (Death Star Battle, Use the Force! and Rescue From Jabba's Palace) and comes with an accompanying audio CD featuring word-for-word narration of the text.''
