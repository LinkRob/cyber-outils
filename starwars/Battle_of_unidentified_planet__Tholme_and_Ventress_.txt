In [[22 BBY]], [[Standard month|months]] before the [[Battle of Christophsis]],<ref name="Timeline" /> a '''battle''' took place on a [[Unidentified planet (Tholme and Ventress)|planet]] early in the [[Clone Wars]]. A major campaign, it saw [[Jedi Master]] [[Tholme]] lead hundreds of [[clone trooper]]s against a detachment of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[battle droid]]s under the command of [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], [[Asajj Ventress]]. As the tide of the battle turned in the Confederacy's favor, Ventress [[Lightsaber combat|engaged]] Tholme in a [[lightsaber duel]] and [[Death|struck him down]]; according to Dooku, as part of a later attempt to turn Tholme's former [[Padawan]] [[Quinlan Vos]] against Ventress, her murder of Tholme had violated Dooku's orders.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />
