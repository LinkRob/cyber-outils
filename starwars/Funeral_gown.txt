A '''funeral gown''' was worn by [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], the [[Senator]] and former [[queen]] of [[Naboo]] during her [[Padmé Amidala's funeral|funeral]] procession through the streets of [[Theed]] as her open casket was pulled by four [[gualaar]].<ref name="ROTS" />

The gown was [[Color|blue]] and long<ref name="ROTS" /> as it was designed to have the appearance of floating in [[water]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref> Amidala also had white [[Flower|blossoms]] and [[ribbon]]s in her flowing hair and in her hands she clutched the [[Japor snippet]] that her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] had made for her shortly after their first meeting.<ref name="ROTS" />
