'''''Doctor Aphra'' 13''' is the thirteenth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']], and is part of the ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'' event. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]], [[Victor Olazaba]], and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[August 25]], [[2021]].

==Publisher's summary==
''GUEST OF HONOR DOCTOR APHRA finds herself face-to-face with the one person she hoped she'd never see again! For once she might not be able to talk her way out&hellip;Meanwhile, JUST LUCKY and ARIOLE confront a familiar presence of their own!''

the most dangerous place in the galaxy-an auction hosted by resurgent crime syndicate Crimson Dawn!

Meanwhile, Just Lucky and Ariole Yu's old mentor has betrayed the Sixth Kin syndicate, and
they've been tasked with assassinating him right under Crimson Dawn's nose.

With all of these crime syndicates and gangsters in one place, what could possibly go wrong?}}
