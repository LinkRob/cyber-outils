'''Bulu''' was an individual who stashed a [[Canto Pass]] game piece called [[the Messenger]] in a container within a locked building in the [[City|town]] [[Wayfar]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> A [[keycard]] which unlocked the building was left on a set of scaffolding on the side of the building<ref name="Outlaws" /> by the same time.<ref name="OutlawsDate" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Merqaal]] [[Nix]] retrieved the forgotten [[keycard]] for his companion, the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]], who later used it to unlock the door and steal the stash.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bulu was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. Their stash contains one of the collectible trinkets that can be used to customize the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', the player's [[EML-850 light freighter]], and to customize the player's [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
