'''Cady''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Slavery|enslaved]] by the [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] during the [[Republic Era]]. At some point, the company brought her into the service of the [[Pijali monarchy]] on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]. By [[40 BBY]], Cady had become a key [[Aide|attendant]] and trusted confidante of the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]]. Cady helped Fanry plot a [[Blackguard campaign|revolution]] against the Czerka Corporation, which included devising a fake [[assassin]]ation attempt on the princess with a [[slicer dart]] to instill fear in Pijal's [[lord]] [[regent]], the [[Jedi]] [[Rael Averross]]. Cady accompanied Fanry on the ruler's [[Pijali religion|ceremonial]] [[Fanry's Grand Hunt|Grand Hunt]], [[Saboteur|sabotaging]] a [[LM-432 crab droid|crab droid]] to attack her princess as part of their scheme to frame the [[Opposition]], a [[Politics|political]] performance troupe, for increasingly violent activities.

Cady helped the princess prepare for her [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]], an event that ultimately ended in the pair escaping the [[Pijali palace]]'s [[Celestial Chalice]] after Fanry's violent seizure of absolute power resulted in the injury of [[Deren]], the ruler's loyal [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] [[captain]]. Cady initially believed that Fanry would cease the practice of slavery on Pijal after becoming [[queen]]. However, she quickly realized that Fanry only cared about her own power when the ruler did not agree to help free the enslaved workers [[Revolt on the Leverage|revolting]] on the ''[[Leverage]]'', a Czerka Corporation [[cruiser]]. After Cady helped depose the recently [[crown]]ed queen, Pijal installed a new [[Democracy|democratic]] [[Assembly (Pijal)|Assembly]] to govern the planet, headed by [[Governor]] [[Orth]]. Cady accepted Orth's offer to enroll in the school of her choice, selecting a leadership academy on the [[Core Worlds]] planet [[Alderaan]].

==Biography==
===Service on Pijal===
{{Quote|You there&mdash;Cady, isn't it?''"<br />"''Yes, Lord Regent.|Lord Regent Rael Averross and Cady|audio=Cady2.mp3|<ref name="MA" />}}
Cady, a [[human]] [[Gender|female]], was [[Reproduction|born]]<ref name="MA" /> around [[56 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> and eventually became [[Slavery|enslaved]] by the [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]]. During the [[Republic Era]], the company brought her into the service of the [[Pijali monarchy]] on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]. Under Pijal's [[lord]] [[regent]], the [[Jedi]] [[Rael Averross]], Cady performed cleaning duties on Czerka [[Starship|ships]] and attended to any other requests made of her.<ref name="MA" /> By [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Cady had become a key [[Aide|attendant]] and trusted confidante of the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]].<ref name="MA" />

Fanry privately loathed Averross's close association with and support of the Czerka Corporation, a company she felt was exploiting Pijal. As a result, the princess conscripted Cady's assistance in staging a revolution against Averross's aspiration to make her a constitutional [[Monarchy|monarch]] with little to no power. To gather information about Averross's activities, Fanry ordered Cady to stay in his presence or her own most of the [[time]]. Cady was made aware of Fanry's plans to frame the [[Opposition]], a [[Politics|political]] performance troupe, for increasingly violent [[Blackguard campaign|attacks]] against Czerka facilities that were actually committed by [[blackguard]]s, a secret division of [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guards]] loyal only to the princess.<ref name="MA" />

===Active subterfuge===
{{Quote|What did they want to do? Make me go mad, just before the ceremony?''"<br />"''They could've been aiming at me, Your Serene Highness. Hoping I'd assassinate you.|Princess Fanry and Cady, about a slicer dart|audio=Cady3.mp3|<ref name="MA" />}}
As Fanry's blackguards continued executing attacks on Czerka, [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], were [[Mission to Pijal|sent to Pijal]] to investigate. Now operating under the watchful [[eye]]s of the Jedi, Fanry devised a fake [[assassin]]ation attempt on herself to frame the Opposition for its first violent initiative against the Pijali monarchy, which Cady carried out. The attendant acquired a [[slicer dart]], a device capable of temporary mind control, and planted it in the [[Viewport|windowsill]] of her princess's [[Pijali palace]] [[Royal chamber|quarters]].<ref name="MA" />

[[File:ObiWanPadawanLenahra.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn's Padawan, Obi-Wan Kenobi]]
When Cady tripped the palace's alarms, Fanry's screams hurriedly summoned Jinn, Kenobi, and Averross to the princess's chambers. The ruler asked Cady if she had seen anything, prompting her attendant to present the [[slicer]] dart to Fanry and Averross, claiming she had found it lodged in the windowsill after the attack. Cady suggested to Fanry and the Jedi that the attack may have been aimed at her so that the dart's [[nanotechnology]] would force her to assassinate the ruler. Believing the fabricated attack to be legitimate, Averross began to worry about Fanry's safety, growing more fearful over time.<ref name="MA" />

At some point before Pijal's [[Pijali religion|ceremonial]] [[Fanry's Grand Hunt|Grand Hunt]], Cady, under Fanry's orders, [[Saboteur|sabotaged]] the event's [[Hunting|prey]], a [[LM-432 crab droid|crab droid]], by giving it orders to [[Death|kill]] the princess. Cady later attended the [[Grand Hunt]] with Fanry, carrying a basket of luxuries for her ruler. During the event, the [[droid]] Cady had sabotaged attacked Fanry, further leading Averross to believe that the Opposition was becoming increasingly violent. After the trying event, Cady escorted the princess back to the palace and ensured that she had not been harmed. The next [[Standard day|day]], under heavy security, Cady attended a rally with Fanry in Pijal's [[Pijal's capital city|capital city]] [[Capital dome|dome]].<ref name="MA" />

===Revolutions and freedom===
{{Quote|Your Majesty. The people inside&mdash;they're trying to get away! We could help them!''"<br />"''And let Czerka go? Hardly. We have sworn to show them no mercy, and we shall not.|Cady and Queen Fanry, aboard the ''Righteous''|audio=Cady4.mp3|<ref name="MA" />}}
Cady helped Fanry prepare for her upcoming [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]], offering reassurance that the event would result in positive change. During the coronation, which was held in the palace's [[Celestial Chalice]], the princess stabbed the [[Unidentified Skykeeper|Skykeeper]], Pijal's [[Religion|religious]] leader, with the [[Pijali ceremonial sword|ceremonial sword]] used in the ceremony. After completing her seizure of power as an absolute monarch, the newly [[crown]]ed [[Queen]] Fanry publicly decried [[democracy]]. With the crowd stirred into an excited turmoil, Cady, Fanry, and [[Deren]], Fanry's loyal royal guard [[captain]], activated [[kohlen crystal shield]]s around themselves. However, using his [[kohlen crystal]]&ndash;powered [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's first lightsaber|lightsaber]], Kenobi breached Deren's [[Personal energy shield|shield]], cutting the man open. The sight of the injured guard prompted Cady and Fanry to escape through the Chalice's roof by using [[Ascension cable|ascending cables]] to climb into a royal [[troop]] [[dropship]], leaving Deren, the injured [[Skykeeper]], and Pijal's [[Unidentified court jeweler|court jeweler]] behind.<ref name="MA" />

[[File:Qui-Gon-Jinn-Databank.jpg|thumb|right|290px|Qui-Gon Jinn contacted the ''Righteous'' with news of the ''Leverage'' revolt, prompting Cady to betray Fanry.]]
After Cady and Fanry boarded the [[Heavenly Sphere|''Righteous'']], the recently renamed [[flagship]] of Pijal's [[royal fleet]], a [[2-1B surgical droid]] removed Cady's slave-tracking chip. Cady then informed Fanry that the Czerka Corporation's [[Sector Supervisor]], [[Meritt Col]], still held [[Commanding officer|command]] of the ''[[Leverage]]'', a Czerka Corporation [[cruiser]]. Although Fanry was displeased to learn that Deren had failed to kill Col at the coronation, she and Cady agreed to rectify the error. Not long after, Fanry accepted a [[hologram]] call from Jinn on the ''Righteous'', which Cady attended with her princess. Fanry, who had been intending to attack the ''Leverage'' using her flagship, was surprised to learn from Jinn that the enslaved workers stationed aboard the Czerka cruiser were [[Revolt on the Leverage|revolting]] against their handlers.<ref name="MA" />

Cady initially believed that Fanry would cease the practice of slavery on Pijal after becoming queen. However, Cady quickly realized that Fanry only cared about her own power when the ruler insisted on commencing her assault on the ''Leverage'', despite the innocent lives still on board. She helped depose Fanry from her position by holding the recently coronated queen at [[blaster]]-point until she surrendered. Afterward, Pijal installed a new democratic [[Assembly (Pijal)|Assembly]] to govern the planet, headed by [[Minister]] [[Orth]], who took the post of [[governor]]. Cady accepted Governor Orth's offer to enroll in the school of her choice, selecting a leadership academy on the [[Core Worlds]] planet [[Alderaan]].<ref name="MA" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I regret to inform Her Majesty that Meritt Col survived the ceremony.''"<br />"''What? Deren. He spared her, didn't he?''"<br />"''He was a good man. He just didn't understand that these Czerka scum have to die.''"<br />"''But we do.|Cady and Queen Fanry, aboard the ''Righteous''|audio=Cady5.mp3|<ref name="MA" />}}
[[File:PijalCover.jpg|200px|thumb|left|Cady was enslaved by Czerka Corporation, which operated on Pijal (pictured).]]
As an enslaved Czerka Corporation servant, Cady held great contempt for her handlers. She held hope that Princess Fanry's revolution against Czerka and Pijal's lord regent, Rael Averross, would end the practice of slavery on Pijal. Outwardly, Cady presented herself as a dutiful and loyal attendant, becoming Fanry's close confidante. Cady was willing to engage in deception to pursue her goal of ending slavery, including lying to Averross and instigating violent plots conceived by her princess. She also cared for Fanry's needs, believing that the monarch's work would result in her own freedom.<ref name="MA" />

Although Cady was thrilled to be freed from Czerka after Fanry's coronation, the attendant's loyalty to the princess evaporated upon realizing the ruler only cared for her own power, and she helped depose the recently crowned queen. After the revolution, she exercised her newfound freedom to attend the school of her choosing. Governor Orth considered Cady to be an intelligent individual and hoped that she would return to Pijal someday. Cady had long, [[Color|dark-brown]] [[hair]] that fell past her shoulders. During her time as an enslaved worker on Pijal, she possessed a youthful appearance.<ref name="MA" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|Did you see anything?''"<br />"''No, Your Serene Highness. But I found this lodged in the windowsill.|Cady and Princess Fanry, carrying out a faux assassination attempt|audio=Cady6.mp3|<ref name="MA" />}}
On Pijal, Cady usually performed simple cleaning tasks and attended to Princess Fanry's welfare. However, she was also was skilled in subterfuge. Cady carried out several of Fanry's plans to frame the Opposition for violence against the Pijali monarchy and the Czerka Corporation, including fabricating an assassination attempt on the princess with a slicer dart and convincing Rael Averross of the attack's legitimacy. Having some technical knowledge, Cady proved able to sabotage the [[crab]] droid used in the Grand Hunt, remaining undetected despite the heavy security in the area.<ref name="MA" />

==Equipment==
While owned by the Czerka Corporation on Pijal, Cady wore gray [[Clothing|coveralls]] bearing the symbol of the Pijali monarchy. She kept her hair tied back with a practical piece of cloth while attending to cleaning duties. As a slave, Cady had a tracking chip embedded under the skin of her [[Limb|hand]]. In the palace, she proved able to procure a slicer dart to plant in Fanry's windowsill for use in one of the princess's revolutionary plots.<ref name="MA" />

At Queen Fanry's coronation, to escape from the Celestial Chalice, Cady used an ascending cable attached to a harness to ascend into a dropship. After the coronation, while aboard the ''Righteous'', Cady's slave chip was surgically removed by a droid, and she donned a black-colored Pijali blackguard uniform. Cady also acquired a blaster, which she later used to help depose Fanry from her position as queen.<ref name="MA" />
