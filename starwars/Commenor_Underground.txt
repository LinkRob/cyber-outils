The '''Commenor Underground''' was a rebel cell that fought against the [[Galactic Empire]] during its [[Imperial Era|reign]]. Around [[10 BBY]], it briefly worked with [[Saw Gerrera]]'s extremist [[Partisans]]. [[Arhul Nemo]] was a member of this group.<ref name="Rebel Dossier" /> During his twenties, [[Caluan Ematt]] had dealings with the Commenor Underground.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
