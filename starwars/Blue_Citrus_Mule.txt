'''Blue Citrus Mule''' was an [[alcohol]]ic [[Foodstuff|beverage]] served at [[Oga's Cantina]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The drink was served in honor of the cantina's [[Blutopian]] proprietor [[Oga Garra]]'s [[birthday]].<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2022/07/whats-cooking-at-disneyland-resort-this-july|text=What's Cooking at Disneyland Resort In July|author=Nicole Cantore|date=2022-07-11}}</ref> It was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Citrus Mule first appeared as a beverage available to guests at the [[In-universe|real-world]] Oga's Cantina in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]] from [[July 18]] to [[July 20]], [[2022]] as part of the park's celebration of Oga Garra's birthday, along with several other limited-time offerings across the land. The menu described it as "[[Wikipedia:Citrus|citrus]] [[Wikipedia:Vodka|vodka]], [[Color|blue]] [[Wikipedia:Curaçao (liqueur)|curaçao]], pure [[Sugar|cane]] [[syrup]], [[Wikipedia:Moscow mule|mule mix]], and [[Wikipedia:Club soda|club soda]] garnished with a [[Wikipedia:Lime (fruit)|lime]] wheel and candied [[Ginger (ingredient)|ginger]]."<ref name="DPB" />
