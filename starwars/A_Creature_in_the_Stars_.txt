"'''A Creature in the Stars!'''" was a comic originally published in [[Star Wars Weekly 112|''Star Wars Weekly'' 112]]. It was later reprinted in ''[[Star Wars 2: World of Fire]]'' as part of the story ''[[The Word for World is Death!]]''.
