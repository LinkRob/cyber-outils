'''Fireball''' was a rogue [[clone trooper]] who was a member of the [[Clone Underground]] network that was created by [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] early in the [[Imperial Era]]. Fireball was part of a mission that was involved in an [[attack on the prisoner transport ship]] alongside [[Gregor]], [[Echo]], and [[Nemec]] that rescued [[Howzer]] and two other clones.<ref name="Tipping Point" /> He<ref name="Infiltration AD" /> would eventually be killed while defending the Underground's base on the planet [[Teth]] from an Imperial [[Clone X trooper|clone assassin]], [[CX-2]].<ref name="Infiltration" />

==Biography==
===Clone Wars===
Fireball<ref name="Tipping Point" /> was created on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] as a [[Cloning|clone]] of the [[human]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]]<ref name="Clone Data" /> to serve as a [[clone trooper]] in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone army]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]].<ref name="Clone troopers DB" /> At some point after the end of the Clone Wars and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]], Fireball went rogue and joined Rex's Clone Underground.<ref name="Tipping Point" />

===The attack on the prisoner transport===
During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|214}}</ref> Fireball took part in an [[Attack on the prisoner transport ship|attack]] on an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] that was transporting disobedient clones from [[Balmorra]] to an unknown [[Mount Tantiss|location]]. After boarding the [[Unidentified Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser (Balmorra)|vessel]], Fireball was ordered by [[Echo]] to free [[Howzer]] and his men while he and [[Nemec]] secured the ship's bridge. After freeing and arming the clone prisoners, Fireball and the raiding party fled the ship upon the arrival of an Imperial [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]. As the prisoner transport ship was strafed and destroyed by a squadron of [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|V-wing starfighters]], Fireball and the other rogue clones jumped to [[hyperspace]] in their [[Remora|ship]].<ref name="Tipping Point" />

===Death===
At some point,<ref name="TBBS3Date" /> Fireball was present at the Clone Underground Hideout at the [[B'omarr Order Monastery]] on the planet Teth when a brainwashed Imperial [[Clone X trooper|clone assassin]], CX-1, was captured and brought in for interrogation. Fireball encrypted CX-1's [[Holopuck|data puck]] which he found out that it contained a target list that included [[Senator]] [[Avi Singh]] and [[Omega]], forcing Rex to call Clone Force 99. When Clone Force 99 arrived to help with the interrogation, Fireball offered to cook for the group. When the clone assassin [[CX-2]] attacked the Clone Underground [[B'omarr Order Monastery|base]] in order to kill CX-1 for failing his mission, Fireball grabbed a flamethrower in an attempt to throw off the assassin's aim and cover the others retreat, but he was shot in the shoulder, causing him to drop his weapon and collapse to the ground. The flamethrower ignited a nearby thermal detonator, which triggered several other detonators and caused a massive explosion that killed Fireball and buried the outpost in rubble.<ref name="Infiltration" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Trooper-Fireball.png|thumb|right|250px|Fireball's unique armor.]]
As a clone of [[Jango Fett]], Fireball stood 1.83 [[meter]]s tall and had [[Color|tan-colored]] skin. Fireball was extremely proficient with blasters, taking out multiple stormtroopers with ease. He was also shown to be a very capable pilot. He showed selflessness and bravery when their base on Teth was attacked, grabbing a flamethrower and jumping in the line of fire to cover the others when he saw that Nemec was downed. At the Clone Underground base on Teth, Fireball was able to cook for the troops. When the Bad Batch visited the base, he cooked food that was of Gregor's personal recipe, with his own spicy modifications added.<ref name="Infiltration" />

==Equipment==
Fireball was equipped in standard [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] that was dyed a tint of [[Color|dark green]]. The visor of Fireball's helmet was also green in coloration.<ref name="Tipping Point" />
