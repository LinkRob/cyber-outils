'''Beard suppressants''' were substances that prevented the growth of [[Hair|beards]]. [[Thane Kyrell]] was known to have used such substances as an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial cadet|cadet]] as well as during his service. Upon deserting the Empire on the [[planet]] [[Kerev Doi]], Kyrell ran out of beard suppressant and, unwilling to spend [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to purchase more, began to grow stubble.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
