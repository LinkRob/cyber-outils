The '''Flacharia system''' was located in [[Wild Space]] in grid square H-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Flacharia [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Flacharia system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention in the [[2004]] novel ''[[Survivor's Quest]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="SQ">''[[Survivor's Quest]]''</ref>
