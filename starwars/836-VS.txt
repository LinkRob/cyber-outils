'''836-VS''' was a [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]] that was stolen by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and used for a [[Mission to recover Imperial codes|mission]] to the [[moon]] [[Killun 71]]. Crewed by members of [[Phoenix Cell]], namely the former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]], the former Imperial [[inventory droid]] [[AP-5]], and the [[C1-series astromech droid]] [[C1-10P]] "Chopper," 836-VS arrived at [[Killun Station]], where they attempted to steal Imperial clearance codes.<ref name="DAD" />

Following the mission, 836-VS docked with the modified [[VCX-100 light freighter]] ''[[Ghost]]''. Soon after, Chopper, who had been reprogrammed by Imperial personnel, trapped Antilles, AP-5, and the fellow rebels [[Hera Syndulla]], [[Ezra Bridger]], and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] in the ship's cargo bay. Then, he detached the ''Ghost'' from 836-VS and flew away, leaving the stolen Imperial shuttle floating in [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="DAD" />
