'''Caibol''' was a [[human]] who served [[Captain]] [[Raymus Antilles]] aboard the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' during the [[Imperial Era]]. When Antilles received supplies from the [[Gran]] [[Garsa]], Caibol helped to collect the cargo. After the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] attempted to attack the [[probe droid]] [[PTZ-1K]], who had been attempting to send a message to the [[Galactic Empire]], Antilles sent Caibol and [[Bonipa]] to take both R2-D2 and [[C-3PO]] for a [[memory wipe]]. However, R2-D2 escaped and returned to Antilles, informing him of the [[droid]] [[spy]]. When PTZ-1K attempted to send a transmission to the Empire, it was tricked by C-3PO into using an incompatible [[binary]] [[language]], Caibol and Bonipa captured the droid.<ref name="Trouble Again" />
