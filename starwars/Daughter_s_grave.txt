This '''grave''' was the [[Daughter]]'s [[Burial|tomb]] on the [[planet]] [[Mortis]]. After the Daughter sacrificed her life to save the [[Father (Mortis)|Father]], she was buried alongside the [[Dagger of Mortis]]. Later, the [[Son]] would enter the tomb, rolling away the stone that had sealed the entrance, to retrieve the dagger and admit that he had loved his sister despite being the one to have [[Death|killed]] her.<ref name="Ghosts of Mortis" />
