'''Cartoon Saloon''' is an [[Wikipedia:Republic of Ireland|Irish]] animation studio, known for multiple [[Wikipedia:Academy Award|Academy Award]]&ndash;nominated films such as [[Wikipedia:The Secret of Kells|''The Secret of Kells'']] and [[Wikipedia:Wolfwalkers|''Wolfwalkers'']]. It was founded by [[Paul Young]], [[Tomm Moore]], and [[Nora Twomey]] in 1999.<ref name="About" /> On [[February 2]], [[2023]], it was announced on [[StarWars.com]] that Cartoon Saloon would contribute a short film to [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|Volume 2]] of the [[Disney+]] anthology series ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]''. Young directed the episode, which was titled "[[Screecher's Reach (short film)|Screecher's Reach]]" and served as the second episode of the anthology. ''Visions'' Volume 2 was released online on [[May 4]], 2023.<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Release Date, Studios Revealed}}</ref>
