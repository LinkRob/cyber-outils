The '''finance ministry''' was the government department of the [[Galactic Empire]] which dealt with financial issues and headquartered in the [[Federal District]] of [[Coruscant]], the Imperial throneworld. [[Lieutenant]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] of the [[Imperial Navy]], and his [[aide]] [[Ensign]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]], discovered that the Empire had made use of much more [[doonium]] than they required for [[starship]] construction. Thrawn argued that this must be a result of a large, unannounced [[Project Stardust|project]] which was kept secret even from the [[Imperial Senate]] and the finance ministry. He would later find out that this secret project was in fact the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]],<ref name="Thrawn" /> a [[moon]]-sized deep-[[Realspace|space]] mobile [[battle station]] with the ability to destroy an entire [[planet]].<ref name="Death Star DB">{{Databank|death-star|Death Star}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The finance ministry of the Galactic Empire was first mentioned in the [[canon]] [[2017]] novel [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']], written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Thrawn" />
