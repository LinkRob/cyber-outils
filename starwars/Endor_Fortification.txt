The '''Endor Fortification''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Bounty Hunter (Imara Vex)|standard costume]]. The [[Clothing|outfit]] and [[armor]] resembled the uniforms worn by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[AT-ST driver]], consisting of [[Color|brown]] boots and gloves, a gray jumpsuit underneath a brown harness and dark belt and hip holster, both featuring silver buckles, and a grayish-green [[helmet]], [[gauntlet]], and kneepads. The helmet featured a glowing green visor, and the costume also included a circular gray [[shoulder-mounted armament]] housing a dark red payload.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Fortification was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Imara Vex, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Endor A280C Custom]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching Tier 40 on the Season 1 Arena Pass' premium track.<ref name="Hunters" />
