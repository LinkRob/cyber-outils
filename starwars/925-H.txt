'''925-H''' was an entity called to [[transport]] control by an announcer in<ref name="Rogue" /> the [[Imperial Center of Military Research]]<ref name="Geek">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]]<ref name="Rogue" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="RogueDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Shortly after the call went out, the [[Battle of Scarif]] broke out between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], resulting in the destruction of the center by the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] at [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s demand.<ref name="Rogue" />
