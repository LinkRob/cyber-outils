'''Animal Instincts''' was the name given to the [[Jedi Initiate|younglings]] who joined [[Preigo]]'s [[Preigo's Traveling World of Wonder|circus]] to infiltrate the [[Ohnaka Gang]] and [[Rescue on Florrum|rescue]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] on [[Florrum]]. The [[youngling]]s claimed to be acrobats and were presented to the [[pirate]] leader [[Hondo Ohnaka]].<ref name="Bound for Rescue" /> [[Petro]] was dressed as a [[gundark]],<ref name="Slide 3">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/bound-for-rescue-trivia-gallery|text="Bound for Rescue" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> [[Zatt]] was a [[rancor]],<ref name="Slide 4">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/bound-for-rescue-trivia-gallery|text="Bound for Rescue" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> and [[Gungi]] was a [[narglatch]].<ref name="Slide 5">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/bound-for-rescue-trivia-gallery|text="Bound for Rescue" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> Despite their group name, [[Katooni]] and [[Byph]] were not dressed as animals, although the latter was adorned with [[tree]] blossoms from [[Carlac]].<ref name="Slide 6">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/bound-for-rescue-trivia-gallery|text="Bound for Rescue" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref>
