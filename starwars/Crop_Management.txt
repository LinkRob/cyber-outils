'''Crop Management''' was a compulsory subject at the [[Junior Academy of Applied Sciences]] at [[Lothal]]'s [[Capital City]]. Subjects covered by the class included the use of [[enzyme seeding]] on clouds to raise crop yields, as well nutrient outputs and types of [[fertilizer]]. [[Zare Leonis]] considered the subject extremely dull and worried that he would get his first [[demerit]] at the academy by falling [[Sleep|asleep]] in the class. [[Merei Spanjaf]], however, found the subject fascinating as she was able to think in a way that allowed to see how the information gained in the subject could provide power that could be used in other areas.<ref name="edge" />
