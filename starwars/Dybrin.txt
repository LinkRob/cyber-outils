'''Dybrin''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> located in the [[Dybrin system]],<ref name="Appendix" /> in the [[Deep Core]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> and [[the Interior]] region,<ref name="Location" /> in grid square L-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> [[historian]] and [[author]] [[Eloc Throno]] included a [[map]] with the location of Dybrin in his [[book]] ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', a guide that detailed the planet [[Batuu]] and its [[Batuuan|inhabitants]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> Dybrin was located in the same [[star system]] as the [[moon]] [[Dybrin 12]],<ref name="Appendix" /> [[homeworld]] of the [[Dybrinthe]] [[species]].<ref name="AOTG">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dybrin was mentioned in the [[2020]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', written by [[Cole Horton]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />
