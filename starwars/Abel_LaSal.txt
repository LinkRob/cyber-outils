'''Abel LaSal''' was an individual who served as a [[commander]] in the [[Imperial Navy's officer corps]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> LaSal was deployed aboard the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Sovereign]]''. While the [[Starship|vessel]] was docked at [[Fondor]], someone stole the commander's identity by using a forged [[code cylinder]] and [[officer's disk]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

At some point, the LaSal came to [[Commanding officer|command]] the [[Star Destroyer]]. However, this was no longer the case<ref name="Epic Battles">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Visual Guide: Epic Battles]]''</ref> by [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Rebels date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Fire Across the Galaxy]]" to [[4 BBY]].</ref> when the ''Sovereign'' had become [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s [[flagship]], with [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]] reassigned to become Tarkin's second-in-command aboard the starship.<ref name="Epic Battles" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Rebels date" /> the Star Destroyer was [[Action at Mustafar|destroyed]] over<ref name="Epic Battles" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Mustafar]] by the [[Spectres|Spectre]] rebel cell.<ref name="Epic Battles" />
