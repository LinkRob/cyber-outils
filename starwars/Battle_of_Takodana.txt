The '''Battle of Takodana''', also known as the '''Battle on Takodana''', the '''Clash on Takodana''', the '''attack on Takodana''', the '''invasion of Takodana''', or '''raid on Takodana''', was a battle that took place in the immediate aftermath of the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]] during the early stages of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the forces of the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Takodana]].

==Prelude==
[[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] of the [[First Order]] commanded [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]],<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> and [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] to capture or destroy the [[Map to Skywalker|map]] leading to the missing [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]], which was contained in the [[droid]] [[BB-8]] after [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] [[Attack on Tuanul|retrieved]] it from<ref name="Episode VII" /> [[Lor San Tekka]].<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> BB-8 was in the company of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] deserter [[Finn]], [[Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca]], and a [[scavenger]] named [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], who sought to return the droid, and thus the map, to the [[Resistance]] led by General [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. In order to have the droid delivered to General Organa, the four stopped at the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of [[Maz Kanata]] on [[Takodana]]. At the castle, both a [[Resistance spy droid network|Resistance spy]] and a [[Bazine Netal|First Order informant]] identified BB-8 and informed their respective allies, drawing both forces to Takodana to recover BB-8 and the map.<ref name="Episode VII" />

==The battle==
===Castle assault===
{{Quote|Those beasts! They're here!|Maz Kanata realizes her castle is under attack|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:HanNoLookee-TFA.png|thumb|left|300px|Han Solo blasts a stormtrooper without looking]]
During the battle, [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] was attacked by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] after they discovered that the [[droid]] [[BB-8]] was at the castle. Flying in with [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s and [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport|Atmospheric Assault Landers]], the First Order swiftly laid siege to the castle and leveled it, [[Death|killing]] many of its inhabitants. BB-8 followed [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] into the forests surrounding the castle, and the First Order was unable to locate the droid until a stormtrooper [[officer]] reported to Ren that the droid had been sighted in the company of a [[Gender|girl]]. Ren deployed stormtroopers to the woods before heading into the woods himself, hunting Rey down and eventually finding her. Frightened, Rey attempted to shoot him, but Ren reacted quickly. He deflected her blasts with his lightsaber, and proceeded to disarm his target with the Force. Ren proceeded to corner and capture her.<ref name="Episode VII" />

As the surviving denizens of the castle attempted to defend themselves throughout the ruin, stormtroopers surrounded and overran them as fighters delivered fire from above. During the fighting at the castle, [[FN-2199]] in [[First Order riot control stormtrooper|riot control gear]] called out to [[Finn]]. He called the former [[trooper]] a traitor and challenged him to a fight.<ref name="TR-8R" /> The two fought down throughout the castles rubble, and the stormtrooper eventually overpowered Finn. FN-2199 was stopped from killing Finn however, when he was shot by Han Solo. Despite killing multiple stormtroopers, [[Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca]], and Finn were forced to surrender as full attention was placed on them when the last of the castle residents had been dealt with.<ref name="Episode VII" />

===The Resistance arrives===
[[File:PoeTakodanaBattle-PoeDameron27.png|thumb|right|300px|Poe Dameron's path during the battle]]
The stormtroopers had bigger problems to worry about however,<ref name="Episode VII" /> as eleven [[Resistance]] [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> led by [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] soon arrived to fight the First Order. A fierce dogfight ensued and resulted in the destruction of many First Order TIEs and the deaths of many stormtroopers from X-wing strafing runs.<ref name="Episode VII" /> In an act that awed even himself, Dameron took out nine TIE fighters and multiple ground targets in a single run.<ref name="Poe Dameron 27" /> 

Back in the woods, Kylo Ren was notified of the Resistance attack by another stormtrooper officer, who asked him to call in more troops. Ren, however, ordered the First Order [[Kylo Ren's division|divison]] to pull out, saying they had what they came for. As the battle raged on above the castle, the remaining First Order troops boarded their ships and withdrew, taking the captured and unconscious Rey along with them. The battle turned out to be a failure for the First Order, as Ren failed to get the information he wanted from Rey, and the BB-8 droid ended up in the hands of the Resistance.<ref name="Episode VII" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|First Order scum. The nerve to show up here. How far in the galaxy do I have to go to get away from them?''"<br />"''I might regret asking this, Cookie, but what did they do to you?''"<br />"''They blew up the last place I worked. Maz Kanata's castle. Shame. Such a shame. That's why I took my show on the road. Lucky for you lot.|Strono Tuggs and [[Izal Garsea]]|<ref name="ACoF">''[[A Crash of Fate]]''</ref>}}
When the battle concluded, the denizens of Kanata's castle had suffered casualties. However, the stormtroopers and TIEs had suffered heavy casualties from the Resistance starfighters.<ref name="Episode VII" /> The battle was also a major [[propaganda]] win for the Resistance: the battle represented its first struggle against the First Order in clear view of civilians, which helped show the Resistance was a force that aimed to protect the innocent from the First Order.<ref name="Poe Dameron 27" /> With Kanata's castle destroyed after one thousand years in operation,<ref name="Episode VII" /> chef [[Strono Tuggs]] took his career elsewhere and eventually traveled to the planet [[Batuu]].<ref name="ACoF" />
