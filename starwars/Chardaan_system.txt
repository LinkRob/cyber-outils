The '''Chardaan system''' was a [[star system]]<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> located in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] at the intersection<ref name="TFABG" /> of the [[Shipwrights' Trace]]<ref name="Shipwright" /> and [[Hydian Way]] [[hyperspace route]]s.<ref name="TFABG" /> The system contained [[Chardaan]]<ref name="WorldsOfTFA" /> and its [[Chardaan Orbital Yards|shipyards]]. A [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] cell operated out of the system and helped hide the [[freighter]] [[pilot]] [[Lantra Tan]] as she attempted to escape the [[bounty]] on her head.<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />
