A '''battle''' occurred on [[Tkoku Island]] on the [[planet]] [[Olcatz]] when the [[Crusaders (The Grand Master)|Crusaders]],<ref name="Payback" /> a radical [[Jedi]] sect<ref name="ProductionBrief">{{Getty|subdomain=assets.dam|url=s75j56ckkw879rfqfnxr49s/v/1291165528/original/VISIONS%20SEASON%203%20Final%20Production%20Brief.pdf?Expires=1761418361&KeyName=gcs-bfldr-prod&Signature=yVNCOpLKX0CT4Ufu0FjQLOANhQs=|text=VISIONS SEASON 3 Final Production Brief|date=2025-10-22}}</ref> led by [[the Grand Master]], were lured there by the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] and the associates of the [[Anzellan]] [[Que-Dama]]. The Crusaders were defeated, with the Ronin and Que-Dama's [[bodyguard]] [[Aneé-san]] working together to [[Death|kill]] the Grand Master.<ref name="Payback" />
