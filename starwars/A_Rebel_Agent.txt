"'''A Rebel Agent'''" is an adventure in ''[[Galaxy Guide 7: Mos Eisley]]''.

==Plot summary==
The characters are investigated by [[Lieutenant/Legends|Lieutenant]] [[Harburik]] under suspicion of being [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] agents. They must find some way to prove they are innocent of the charges.
