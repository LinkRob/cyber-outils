'''Empire Day''',<ref name="Empire Day" /> also known as '''Imperial Unity Day''',<ref name="SW 2020 26">[[Star Wars (2020) 26|''Star Wars'' (2020) 26]]</ref><ref name="SW 2020 36">[[Star Wars (2020) 36|''Star Wars'' (2020) 36]]</ref> was the [[Standard day|day]] that [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|declared]] himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] of the First [[Galactic Empire]], bringing an end to the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Empire Day" /> as well as the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Siege RR">{{RebelsRecon|mTkd0MKEm2I|#2.01: Inside "The Siege of Lothal" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-the-siege-of-lothal}}</ref> Following that [[Standard year|year]], Empire Day became a holiday marking the anniversary of the [[Imperial Era|rise]] of the Empire. Festivities of the holiday included a [[parade]] on various Imperial-controlled [[planet]]s, the playing of the Imperial anthem, "[[Glory of the Empire]]," as well as the airing of Emperor Palpatine with two [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guards]] at the [[Imperial Senate]], but the footage used made the Emperor seem younger and more physically appealing than he actually was at the [[time]] of the speech.<ref name="Empire Day" /> Attendance to the festivities was mandatory.<ref name="Absolutely">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> On [[Coruscant]], the holiday was the cap to a [[Standard week|week]] of festivities known as [[Ascension Week]].<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>

It was also the day [[Ezra Bridger]] was [[Reproduction|born]],<ref name="Empire Day" /> and two days before the births of [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Skywalker]] on [[Polis Massa]].<ref name="Siege RR" />

Unity Day also celebrated the camaraderie of its citizenry. Festivities of the event included grand [[Imperial Military|military]] parades that showcased the might of the Empire's [[Imperial Army|army]] and [[Imperial Navy|navy]]. Following the [[Battle of Hoth]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] led an [[Unity Day attack|attack]] on a Unity Day parade on the [[Core Worlds|Galactic Core]] planet [[Bar'leth]]. The strike was a calculated move by the Alliance, as the attack harmed no civilians and was witnessed by beings across [[the galaxy]] via the [[HoloNet]]; [[Alliance High Command]] hoped that their actions would gather more allies to the weakened Rebellion.<ref name="SW 2020 26" />

==Behind the scenes==
The real-world [[Wikipedia:Commonwealth Day|Empire Day]] was a holiday of the [[Wikipedia:British Empire|British Empire]] that was celebrated on [[May 24]], the birthday of [[Wikipedia:Queen Victoria|Queen Victoria]], and is rebranded as "Commonwealth Day" in the [[Wikipedia:United Kingdom|United Kingdom]] and the [[Wikipedia:Commonwealth of Nations|Commonwealth of Nations]].<ref name="Old Vic">{{WebCite|url=http://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/commonwealth-day|text=Commonwealth Day|work=[https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com Oxford Learner's Dictionary]|archivedate=20171009195025}}</ref> The premiere of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] in the British capital, [[Wikipedia:London|London]], was held on [[May 20]], [[1980]], and the term "Empire Day" was used in the event's marketing.<ref name="Empire Day real">{{SW|url=news/an-empire-day-to-remember|text=An 'Empire Day' to Remember}}</ref> As a royal charity premiere, it was attended by [[Wikipedia:Princess Margaret, Countess of Snowdon|Princess Margaret]] and benefited children's charities.<ref name="Evening News">{{WebCite|url=https://www.tiefighters.com/post/102938129372/an-empire-day-to-remember-back-in-1980-when-the|text=IT'S EMPIRE DAY TODAY|author=Mottram, Terry|date=1980-05-20|work=[[Wikipedia:The Evening News (London newspaper)|''Evening News'']], via [https://www.tiefighters.com TieFighters]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/zewnS}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the holiday of Empire Day was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (novelization)|novelization]] of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="ROTS novel">[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (novelization)|''Star Wars'': ''Revenge of the Sith'' novelization]]</ref>
