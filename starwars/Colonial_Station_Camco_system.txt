The '''Colonial Station Camco system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], which was situated in the [[Unknown Regions]], and was in the [[Standard Galactic Grid|grid square]] F-8.<ref name="GMA" /> An orbiting [[planet]] of the system, [[Colonial Station Camco]], was [[homeworld|home]] to the [[Chiss]] [[Caregiver]] [[Mitth'ali'astov]], [[core name]] "Thalias."<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Colonial Station Camco system first appeared in [[Canon]] in the novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> by [[Timothy Zahn]] on [[September 1]], [[2020]].<ref name="Penguin-Hardcover">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/622990/star-wars-thrawn-ascendancy-book-i-chaos-rising-by-timothy-zahn/9780593157688|text=Star Wars: Thrawn Ascendancy (Book I: Chaos Rising)}} {{C|Hardcover}}</ref> It was later named in the "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix posted on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="GMA" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Jason">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map...|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the Colonial Station Camco system was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]] as "Colonial Station Cam'co system."<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
