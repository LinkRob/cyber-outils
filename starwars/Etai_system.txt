The '''Etai system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Druess sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square O-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the [[planet]] [[Etai]].<ref name="Origin" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Etai system was first mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
