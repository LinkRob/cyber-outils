'''Blackstone's Retreat''' was a small outpost built near a [[water|waterfall]] on the slopes of [[Mount Selasa]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] tool a [[jarwal tree]] flower from a container at the outpost.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Blackstone's Retreat first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The location contained a "Jarwal bloom" major charm which increases [[adrenaline]] gained from [[blaster]] headshots and allows players to hit an additional target when using their "Adrenaline Rush" ability.<ref name="Outlaws" />
