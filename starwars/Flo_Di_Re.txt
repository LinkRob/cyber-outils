'''Florence "Flo" Di Re''' (died [[May 8]], [[2024]])<ref name="Death" /> was an actress who voices [[Jocasta Nu]], an ex-[[Jedi Researcher]] and [[Chief Librarian]] of the [[Jedi Archives/Legends|Jedi Archives]]. She is the official "audio double" of the character played by [[Alethea McGrath]]. She appears in four episodes of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, one episode produced in [[2009]], two in [[2010]] and one in [[2014]].
