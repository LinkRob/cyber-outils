"'''BB-8 and the Jungle Adventure - Chapter 3'''" is the third episode of the animated shorts series ''[[Star Wars Roll Out]]''. It was first released [[August 23]], [[2019]] on YouTube.<ref name="YT" />

==Official description==
''Old friends and new allies team up to help BB-8 escape!''<ref name="YT" />

==Plot summary==
In the opening credits, [[BB-8]] and a Star Wars ball lead the way followed by [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], [[Maz Kanata]], [[Finn]], [[Captain]] [[Phasma]], two [[stormtrooper]]s, [[Ben Solo]], [[Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca]], and three [[porg]]s.

Stuck in a web of a [[Unidentified spider-like creature|spider-like creature]] alongside a [[Unidentified shape-shifting creature|shape-shifter]], BB-8 has cecently met, the two are only rescued by another member of the [[changeling]]'s species. Their celebration is shrort-lived however, when the arachnid comes back to find them free of its trap. The three attempt to run, only to get pushed into a corner. Fortunately, BB-8 comes up with a brilliant idea to use his [[welding torch]] to scare the spider off. It works, and using their morphing abilities, BB-8's new friends also adopt the same tactic.

Now that the tables have turned, the shape-shifters turn into more [[BB-series astromech droid|BB units]] and after BB-8's example, they tie all of the predator's eight spindly legs.

The procession of droids set to find BB-8 in [[D'Qar]] dense [[jungle]], where they had previously lost him, now finds that there are three of their round friend, demobilizing the spider. They join in the fun, tugging on the creature's legs, until it catapults it high up in the sky. There the creature lands in the nest of [[Unidentified D'Qar bird|long-necked]] [[bird]]s, which peck at its feeble body.

When it comes to BB-8's rescuers meeting his new changeling friends, they turn out to be a little too scary for the droids, which run home screaming in terror.
