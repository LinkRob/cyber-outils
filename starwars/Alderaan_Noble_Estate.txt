The '''Alderaan Noble Estate''' was a residence of the [[Eternal Alliance]]'s [[Alliance Commander|Commander]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Alderaan/Legends|Alderaan]].

==Behind the scenes==
The Alderaan Noble Estate is the Alderaan stronghold introduced in update 6.1 of ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Onslaught]]''.
