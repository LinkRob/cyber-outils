The '''ATA pulse-wave blaster''' was a vicious short-ranged [[plasma]] [[disruptor pistol]] capable of maiming or [[Death|disintegrating]] targets. It was produced by [[Greff-Timms Industrial]] and was considered state-of-the-art [[Millennium|millennia]] before the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The [[weapon]] functioned on the same basic principle as modern [[blaster]]s during the war but fell under the auspices of [[disruptor]] weapon bans and control acts.<ref name="G&G" />

Whenever ancient ATA pulse-wave blasters were newly discovered during the war they required only some basic maintenance and a fresh [[Power cell|power pack]] to operate like new but were confiscated and destroyed by authorities as quickly as possible. Because of this, the weapons were worth a large amount on the black market at 750 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] per weapon. News of new pulse-wave blaster caches discoveries traveled fast, with criminals trying to beat authorities to claim them from the source or liberating entire shipments destined for destruction.<ref name="G&G" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ATA pulse-wave blaster appeared in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[2019]] role-playing supplement released by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="G&G" /> The weapon originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Tales of the Jedi Companion]]'', a [[1996]] supplement to ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[George R. Strayton]].<ref name="TOTJc">''[[Tales of the Jedi Companion]]''</ref>
