'''Folimar''' was a [[commander]] who commented on the [[Imperial Navy]]'s [[Battle in the Altyr system|destruction]] of [[Unidentified rebel capital ship|one]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[capital ship]]s in the [[Altyr system]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> calling it a brisk reminder of the Empire's superior force. The commander was quoted on a broadcast of the [[HoloNet News]] covering the battle by [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Dorna Parros]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Folimar was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
