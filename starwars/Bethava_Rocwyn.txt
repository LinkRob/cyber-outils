'''Bethava Rocwyn'''<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[admiral]] who served in the [[Five Points system]] aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Authority]]'' during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="1 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rebel Rising]]'' involving Admiral Rocwyn's capture of [[Blue's partisans]] to [[1 BBY]].</ref> after the human [[Jyn Erso]] agreed to work for the Empire upon being threatened, she obliged to help Rocwyn locate a [[Blue's partisans|rebel team]] that had been operating in the [[Five Points system]]. When Erso found them, Rocwyn arrested all of them, along with Erso.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
