'''Blue cabbage''' was a smelly type of [[cabbage]] that was thrown at a [[Gamorrean]] named [[Tan]] by [[Jayko]]. Jayko thew five cabbages at Tan but all of them bounced off of him. One of the cabbages bounced into a pot of [[red dewback broth]] resulting to it spilling on the floor.<ref name="JTC-E10" />
