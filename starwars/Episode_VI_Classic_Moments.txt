'''Episode VI Classic Moments''' was an image archive on [[StarWars.com]] that provided concept art and behind the scenes images from production of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. Accompanied by quotes from production staff, many of these rare images, which were first published in this feature, have yet to appear elsewhere.
