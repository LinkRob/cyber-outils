'''Acantha''' was a [[Nephran]] youth who was a member of the criminal [[Norikus gang]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. In around [[232 BBY]], she, a [[Lepi]], and the gang's leader, [[Norikus]], attempted to rob the [[thief]] [[Nittel Karkson]] on [[level four thousand]] of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]], but after being confronted by [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]], Acantha fled.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Is that it? We wanted more!|Norikus expresses he and Acantha's disappointment at the contents of Nittel Karkson's pockets|<ref name="A Coruscant Solstice" />}}
Acantha was a [[Nephran]] member of the criminal [[Norikus gang]], which was led by the [[Octeroid]] [[Norikus]] and operated on the lower levels of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. On [[Solstice Tide]]<ref name="ACS" /> around [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Reveal">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[A Coruscant Solstice]]" to around [[232 BBY]].</ref> Norikus, Acantha, and a [[Lepi]] member of the gang attempted to rob the young [[thief]] [[Nittel Karkson]] in a litter strewn alleyway on [[level four thousand]] of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]]. Acantha snapped her [[Limb|pincers]] ominously and then lifted Karkson&mdash;who had been pickpocketing on [[Republic Avenue]]&mdash;by his [[Clothing|jacket]] and emptied his pockets out onto the ground while growling in [[Nepotisian]].<ref name="ACS" />

Norikus complained the boy had not gathered enough, but the group were then interrupted by the arrival of [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]]. Acantha whirled round to face the [[Jedi]], dropping Karkson in surprise, but upon seeing his activated [[Stellan Gios's lightsaber|lightsaber]] she fled along with the Lepi, leaving Norikus to face him alone. Gios let Karkson go, but when he later visited the boy's [[Nittel Karkson's hovel|hovel]], the thief heard him approach and thought it might be Acantha or Norikus come to confront him and so grabbed a ladle for defense, despite knowing it would have been useless against Acantha's pincers.<ref name="ACS" />

==Personality and traits==
Acantha was a youth when she encountered Stellan Gios, but still loomed over Karkson and was at least twice his height. Her pincers were [[tattoo]]ed.<ref name="ACS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Acantha first appeared in "[[A Coruscant Solstice]]," a short story written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in the [[2021]] ''[[Life Day Treasury]]'' anthology.<ref name="ACS" />
