An '''attack''' was carried out by [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]] against [[Center One]], a [[Galactic Empire]] reeducation facility<ref name="MoF" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> 

While [[Karama]] kept watch, in wait for the previous 40 hours, [[Nankry]] planted an explosive in the complex. Gerrera then signalled to Karama, who triggered the detonation. At a later point, when asked by [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], Gerrera confirmed his group had authored the attack. The rationale given was that, sooner or later, insurgents such as himself would be locked in facilities such as Center One, so the explosions were meant as a recruiting tool for the Partisans.<ref name="MoF" />
