The Corellian '''1D servo turret''' was a type of [[laser turret]] found on stock [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] [[YT-2400 light freighter]]s.<ref name="Manual">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> The YT-2400 had two double [[laser cannon]]s<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> mounted on dorsal and ventral 1D servo turrets.<ref name="Manual" />
