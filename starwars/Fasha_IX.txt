'''Fasha IX''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Abyss" /> in the [[Fasha system]] in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was the site of the [[Heptathlon]], a series of seven contests. The [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] once participated in the contest while incognito, and dominated every event without using [[Force power]]s but allowed himself to be beaten by the best non-augmented competitors.<ref name="Abyss" />
