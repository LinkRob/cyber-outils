A [[Chiss]] '''aide''' served [[Supreme General]] [[Ba'kif]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet|Expansionary Defense Fleet]],<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' takes place im [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Under Ba'kif's orders, he located [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," on the [[planet]] [[Naporar]] and informed the supreme general that Thrawn would be available to serve at Ba'kif's convenience. When preparing for the journey to Naporar, the [[aide]] asked Ba'kif if the trip was to be officially logged or if it would constitute as a private trip, to which the supreme general affirmed the former.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ba'kif's aide appeared in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
