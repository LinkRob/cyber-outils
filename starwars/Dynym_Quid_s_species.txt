A '''sentient species''' of [[cephalopod]]-like [[humanoid]]s lived in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> In [[132 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref>, a member of this species was present at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] during a conversation between [[Jedi Master]]s [[Sol]] and [[Vernestra Rwoh]].  During the [[Great Jedi Purge]], a member of this species was among the refugees on [[Jabiim]] who were given passage to [[Tessen]] aboard [[Kawlan Roken]]'s [[JT-731 Broadhorn transport|Jabiim freight transport]]. Another member was [[Dynym Quid]], who spent time at [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Casino]] in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="TROSDate">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], which includes the appearance of Dynym Quid, to the same [[Standard year|year]] as the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref>
