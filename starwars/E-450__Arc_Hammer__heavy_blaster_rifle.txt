The '''E-450 "Arc Hammer" heavy blaster rifle''' was a type of [[heavy blaster rifle]] that was utilized by the [[Phase-III Dark Trooper|Phase III Dark Troopers]] commanded by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]].

==History==
The Phase-III Dark Troopers were created by the [[Imperial Department of Military Research]], and a [[Gideon's Dark Trooper platoon|contingent]] of the [[Battle droid|battle droids]] were used by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] warlord [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]<ref name="Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> by the time of [[9 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|16}}</ref>. When [[Din Djarin]] launched an [[Second rescue of Grogu|assault]] on [[Gideon's light cruiser|Gideon's Light Cruiser]] to rescue the [[force-sensitive]] child [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], the Dark Trooper platoon was activated to fight the intruders, wielding E-450 blaster rifles. Before infiltrating the captured bridge of the cruiser, all the troopers were destroyed by the [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]], neutralizing Gideon's Dark Trooper threat.<ref name="Chapter 16" />

==Behind the Scenes==
The designation "Arc Hammer" is a reference to the [[Arc Hammer]] vessel that was the deployment ship for the Dark Troopers in [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]]
