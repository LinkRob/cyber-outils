'''Furball'''<ref name="Bounty Hunters 13">[[Bounty Hunters 13|''Bounty Hunters'' 13]]</ref> was a [[Gender|female]] [[nexu]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" /> that was owned and trained by a [[Unidentified human (Ruusan)|human]] resident of the [[planet]] [[Ruusan]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 9">[[Bounty Hunters 9|''Bounty Hunters'' 9]]</ref> When the [[bounty hunter]]s [[Beilert Valance]] and [[T'onga]] visited Ruusan in search of Valance's former mentor, [[Nakano Lash]], the nexu and several residents attacked them to defend Lash. Valance fought back against the nexu, but was stopped by her trainer, who, upon learning Valance's identity, directed them to Lash's location.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" /> After the nexu's owner rescued T'onga, who had been shot and nearly [[Death|killed]], he sent a message to the [[Hunting|hunter's]] wife, [[Losha Tarkon]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 9" />

After Tarkon arrived on Ruusan,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 9" /> T'onga regained consciousness. She intended to embark on a mission<ref name="Bounty Hunters 10" /> to put an end to the crime war between the [[Unbroken Clan]] and the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 13" /> wishing for Tarkon to accompany her. The nexu was given to T'onga and Tarkon by her owner to assist them in their quest.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 10" /> Tarkon later took to calling the nexu Furball.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 13" />
