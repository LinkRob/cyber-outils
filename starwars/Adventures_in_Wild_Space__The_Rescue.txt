'''''Adventures in Wild Space: The Rescue''''' is a book by [[Tom Huddleston]] and the sixth entry in the ''[[Star Wars: Adventures in Wild Space]]'' young-readers novel series. The book was published by [[Egmont]] UK Ltd on [[May 4]], [[2017]] and is comprised of 192 pages. [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] published a U.S. edition of the title on [[April 10]], [[2018]].<ref name="Disney" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Milo and Lina Graf have located the mysterious Wild Space planet where their parents are being held. They must infiltrate a secret base to free them. With an entire legion of Imperial troops to contend with, can the children at last succeed in THE RESCUE?''<ref name="Rescue" />
