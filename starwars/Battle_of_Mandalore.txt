The '''Battle of Mandalore'''<ref name="Insider 208">{{InsiderCite|208|Bo-Katan Kryze: Leader in Waiting}}</ref> was fought in [[Sundari]], the [[capital]] of the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]], during the [[Clone Wars]]. The battle marked the beginning of a [[Mandalorian Civil War (19 BBY)|civil war]] between the [[Mandalorian]]s who secretly followed the renegade [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]], and those who followed [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]], [[Gender|sister]] of the [[Death|murdered]] [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]].<ref name="The Lawless" />

Although the battle was mainly between those two factions of Mandalorians, the [[Mandalorian super commando]]s and the [[Mandalore resistance]], Maul's former [[Sith Master]], [[Darth Sidious]], also arrived, killed [[Savage Opress]], and captured Maul. The battle started when Obi-Wan went to save Satine.<ref name="The Lawless" />
