The '''Coruscant slider''' was one of the basic [[slider]]s served at [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]], a restaurant owned and operated by a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Besalisk/Legends|Besalisk]] named [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], before<ref name="Menu" /> its destruction in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]].<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The Last of the Jedi: Underworld]]''</ref>

==Overview==
The recipe for each basic slider was devised by Jettster himself with inspiration from his adventures on one of several worlds and given an eponymous name, and this dish was influenced by his time on the [[The galaxy/Legends|galactic]] [[Capital/Legends|capital]]. Served on a trans-shipped medium-density [[foodboard]], the slider was based around one [[Mongo Beefhead patty]], served grilled, with the other ingredients settled around it. These items were a slice of [[Gonzo yellow cheese]], some [[Revwien lettuce]], [[Topato/Legends|topato]] slices, grilled [[Ojomian onion]]s, and [[B'omarr-style pickle]]s. Like all sliders served at his diner, the Coruscant slider came with Jettster's distinctive "[[special sauce]]" as a garnish. The meal cost 6.5 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]], and for an additional charge of .5 credits a customer could have another meat patty with it. It could also be bought alongside another basic slider as a [[Talus and Tralus slider]] meal. A customer could request both [[Sernpidalian mayo-ketchup]] and [[Bimm mustard]] to go with the meal at no charge.<ref name="Menu" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Coruscant slider is mentioned in a [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]]-exclusive release, "[[Dining at Dex's]]" by [[Gregory Walker]], which represents the [[in-universe]] menu of Dex's Diner.
