The '''Aubreeyan Cluster''' was a [[star cluster]] in the [[Eidoloni sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[planet]] [[Polaar]], where the former [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] member [[Komat]] lived in [[Exile (punishment)|exile]], was located in the [[Polaar system]] in the Aubreeyan Cluster.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> In [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] traveled to Polaar to speak with Komat as she could help them [[Hyperwave signal interception|track]] the location of the [[luxury yacht]] ''[[Goldstone]]'', which was being used by a [[Palpatine family|family]] the two sought.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
