The '''Alderaanian monarchy''' was the hereditary [[monarchy]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. The last monarch was [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]], who [[Death|perished]] along with her [[homeworld]] when the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] Alderaan with the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] in [[1 BBY]]. Although the Alderaanian diaspora regrouped, they had yet to reform their monarchy by [[5 ABY]].

==History==
{{Quote|We're lucky on Alderaan. We are loved by our people. We have their loyalty. That's because we love them and are loyal to them in turn. If we ever cease to appreciate those around us&mdash;from the highest lord to the humblest laborer&mdash;we'll lose that loyalty. We'll ''deserve'' to lose it.|Bail Organa, to his daughter Leia|<ref name="Leia" />}}
The monarchy on Alderaan already existed a [[millennium]] before the [[Imperial Era|rule]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. When the monarchy was established, its traditions involving the succession were also established, such as the heir's [[Day of Demand]] and their three challenges.<ref name="Leia" /> In the distant past, belligerence did exist on Alderaan,<ref name="SW PL2" /> and [[prince]]s and princesses who made their claim to the crown sometimes had to fight their way to the [[throne]] room on their Day of Demand.<ref name="Leia" /> Over time, however, a pacifist culture focused on creativity, love, and life developed on the planet, and the monarch was made the guarantor of that philosophy.<ref name="SW PL2" /> Because the monarchs of Alderaan proved loyal and compassionate to their people, regardless of their social background, they, in turn, enjoyed the loyalty and love of the Alderaanians.<ref name="Leia" />

When [[Breha Organa]] was sixteen, she [[The accident|fell]] from [[Appenza Peak]] during her [[Challenge of the Body]]. Her injuries almost killed her; she was only saved by the quick action of the [[Alderaan Guard|guards]] watching from a distance. As a result, Breha's lungs and heart had to be replaced with [[pulmonodes]], though she chose not to go through the [[bacta]] sessions necessary to grow new flesh and skin around them later, telling her daughter that it reminded her that she lived and that she cannot be stopped so easily. The accident, however, did lead to her and her [[Marriage|husband's]] decision to adopt rather than further strain Breha's body by having biological children.<ref name="Leia" />

[[File:BrehaOrgana-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|200px|Breha Organa, the last Queen of Alderaan]]
At some point before the end of the [[Clone Wars]], Breha took up the mantle of queen<ref name="Revenge of the Sith">{{Film|III}}</ref> succeeding [[Breha Organa's mother|her mother]].<ref name="Leia" /> During Breha's reign and through her husband and consort [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa's]] efforts in the Senate, Alderaan advocated ending the Clone Wars between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], due to the planet's pacifist beliefs,<ref name="DB Ald">{{Databank|alderaan|Alderaan}}</ref> and hosted a [[Alderaan Refugee Conference|conference]] to assist [[refugee]]s displaced by [[The galaxy|the galactic]] conflict.<ref name="Assassin">{{TCW|Assassin (episode)}}</ref> Many refugees from across the Republic came to Alderaan to find their way again or to stay on Alderaan. During the Clone Wars, Breha and her husband sent aid to planets which had been ravaged by the Separatists in the form of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], supplies, [[doctor]]s, [[teacher]]s, [[architect]]s, builders, [[medic]]s, and [[droid]]s. Breha also sent her people to dangerous places where they were needed, and though some of them never came back to their families, they didn't blame her as they knew she had sacrificed and would again if she had to.<ref name="Queen's Hope">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref>

Breha also witnessed the fall of the [[Democracy|democratic]] [[Galactic Republic]] and its replacement by the [[Dictatorship|dictatorial]] Galactic Empire, and, along with her consort and [[viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]], played a major part in the rise of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Leia" /> She remained on the throne until her [[death]]<ref name="Eclipse">{{StoryCite|story=Eclipse (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> which occurred during the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of Alderaan by the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]],<ref name="Eclipse" /> a mobile, spherical [[battle station]] the size of a [[Class IV moon|Class IV]] [[moon]].<ref name="ANH paperback novel">[[Star Wars: A New Hope (paperback novel)|''Star Wars: A New Hope'' junior novelization]]</ref> However, Crown Princess [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who was on a [[Secret mission to Tatooine|secret mission]] to [[Tatooine]], survived the destruction of her homeworld.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[Viscount (title)|Viscount]] [[Rabael Dir Glorio]], 26th in line to the throne and last scion of [[House Glorio]], also survived the cataclysm.<ref name="Aphra 15">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 15|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 15]]</ref>

Although Alderaan no longer existed, some surviving Alderaanians like [[Evaan Verlaine]], a former pupil of Breha Organa, considered Princess Leia Organa to be their new queen.<ref name="SW PL1">[[Princess Leia 1|''Princess Leia'' 1]]</ref>

After the [[Battle of Endor]], the surviving Alderaanians lived together aboard a seven-[[starship]] [[Alderaan Flotilla|flotilla]] and planned to build themselves a new home from the remains of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. However, they still had not chosen a monarch, and were ruled by [[Regent Administrator]] [[Eglyn Valmor]].<ref name="LD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

On [[Chandrila]] in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Birth date">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' establishes that Ben Solo was [[Reproduction|born]] on [[Chandrila]] the [[Standard day|day]] the [[Galactic Concordance]] was signed. ''Aftermath: Empire's End'' also states that the [[The galaxy|Galactic]] Concordance was signed during the [[Battle of Jakku]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[5 ABY]]. Therefore, Solo was born on Chandrila in 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> former princess of Alderaan Leia Organa gave birth to her son, [[Ben Solo]]. As Leia's son, Ben would have been given the title of prince of Alderaan if the planet and the [[House of Organa]] had still existed when he was born.<ref name="Skywalker: A Family at War">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]''</ref> However, upon his birth he was considered a member of the [[Elder Houses]].<ref name="Bloodline" />

By the [[time]] of the [[Cold War|conflict]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], Leia Organa was still considered the last princess of Alderaan. However, that status commanded little authority,<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> since almost nobody took the concept of hereditary nobility seriously any longer,<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> and Organa never wore any symbol of royalty unless tradition demanded it.<ref name="TFA VD" />

==Role==
{{Quote|Our ruler&mdash;one day, you&mdash;must struggle to keep the culture focused on creativity, love and life. Whatever happens, Leia&hellip; you must keep Alderaan alive.|Bail Organa, to his daughter Leia|<ref name="SW PL2">[[Princess Leia 2|''Princess Leia'' 2]]</ref>}}
The [[Monarchy|monarch]] of [[Alderaan]] (otherwise referred to as "the [[King]]" if [[Gender|male]], and "the [[Queen]]" if female) both ruled and governed the [[planet]], with the help of [[minister]]s and [[advisor]]s. In addition, he or she had charge of the royal books, balancing the many accounts and personally overseeing funding of all public works on Alderaan.<ref name="Leia" /> In some cases, the monarch even assumed a ministerial portfolio.<ref name="Backstories Leia">''[[Princess Leia: Royal Rebel]]''</ref> While the monarch worked for harmony on Alderaan,<ref name="SW PL2" /> the planet's dealings with the [[galaxy]] at large were entrusted to his or her [[consort]] (referred to as [[viceroy]] if male).<ref name="Leia" />

==Succession==
{{Quote|The crown of Alderaan is not merely inherited. It must be earned. The heir must prove herself worthy in body, heart, and mind. Are you prepared to do so?|Queen Breha, asking her daughter a ritual question|<ref name="Leia" />}}
[[File:RhindonSword-LeiaManga.jpg|thumb|right|200px|The Rhindon Sword which was used as part of the Day of Demand ceremony]]
A monarch of Alderaan was meant to be succeeded by his or her eldest child.<ref name="Leia" /> Like all the other [[Elder Houses]],<ref name="Bloodline" /> the Alderaanian monarchy did not adjudicate succession through strict bloodline inheritance, meaning that adopted children, such as Leia Organa, could ascend to the throne.<ref name="Leia" />

A new member of the royal family would make their first public appearance on his or hers [[Name Day]] where the child's birth or adoption was officially announced in the palace throne room. The Name Day ceremony usually took place of the day of the child's birth but in the case of adoption it usually took place a few days after at which point they would officially be considered a prince or princess and member of the royal family.<ref name="Leia" />

The heir apparent&mdash;whose succession appeared certain&mdash;was not made Crown Prince or Crown Princess until the age of sixteen, or in the case of adoptive members on the sixteenth anniversary of their original Name Day, at which point he or she would have to undergo a series of rituals to prove their worth.<ref name="Leia" />

The first step was the [[Day of Demand]], which took place sixteen years after the heir's original Name Day, on which the heir apparent carried the [[Rhindon Sword]] though the palace throne room, where they would speak the ritual dialogue and would publicly claim his or her right to the crown, before announcing three self-imposed challenges, that of the Body, [[Challenge of the Mind|that of the Mind]], and [[Challenge of the Heart|that of the Heart]]. When the heir apparent was adopted "it is known" was added to the ritual dialogue. The Challenge of the Body traditionally was the heir climbing the famous Alderaanian mountain Appenza Peak, though they could bring along a companion if they wished. Upon fulfilling those challenges, he or she was invested with the [[Heir's Crown]] during a [[Coronation of the Alderaanian heir|coronation ceremony]]. Many heirs gave up their [[keepsake chest]]s at the point of investiture, but the ritual didn't demand it.<ref name="Leia" />

==Residences==
{{Quote|I've studied the palace&mdash;even wrote a paper on it once. It's the whole history of Alderaanian architecture in one massive building.|[[Kier Domadi]]|<ref name="Leia" />}}
[[File:Aldera_webcomic.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Royal Palace of Alderaan]]
The monarch's official residence was the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan]], also known as the Mountain Palace,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> in the planetary [[capital]] of [[Aldera]]<ref name="Leia" /> and near a large [[Forest|wood]].<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref> It was the site of many official ceremonies, festivities, and banquets and was the work of more than a millennium<ref name="Leia" /> by [[3 BBY]].<ref name="Palace Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] birth in [[19 BBY]]. The events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' commence just after Organa's sixteenth [[birthday]], which places the events of the novel in [[3 BBY]].</ref> The palace embodied the whole history and evolution of Alderaanian architecture,<ref name="Leia" /> as well as the opulence of royalty,<ref name="Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> with ancient rooms made of [[stone]] in a large labyrinth alongside more modern rooms such as data centers and holochambers. The palace also housed the Rhindon Sword<ref name="Leia" /> and a [[Unidentified Appenza Peak painting|landscape painting of Appenza Peak]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref>

At the time of Queen Breha and Viceroy Bail Organa, the royal family also possessed a [[Organa Ranch|ranch]] where they grew [[emerald grape]]s used to make high-quality [[emerald wine]], which was greatly liked by [[Hiram Zataire]],<ref name="rebellion">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref> and a penthouse suite on [[Coruscant]] called [[Cantham House]] that was considered relatively modest by either royal or senatorial standards,<ref name="Leia" /> though the former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] [[Sabé]] noted that the Alderaanian [[plant]]s and pieces of art that incorporated wind or [[water]] that were on the roof of Cantham House must have cost a fortune.<ref name="Queen's Hope">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref>

==Style==
{{Quote|Yes, Your Highnessness?|Han Solo, mocking Leia Organa's royal title|<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref>}}
Despite being a hereditary monarchy, the Alderaanian monarchy was less formal than the elective [[Royal House of Naboo|Naboo monarchy]], something that greatly shocked [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] when she first visited Alderaan.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />

As egalitarian as the Alderaanian monarchy was, people were normally expected to follow certain rules when conversing with royals. During her reign, Queen Breha was properly heralded and referred to as "Her Majesty Breha Organa" or "Her Majesty Queen Breha," and addressed as "Your Majesty." Her viceroy and consort Bail Organa was addressed simply as "Viceroy." Their heiress Princess Leia was variously addressed as "Your Highness," "Princess Leia (Organa) of Alderaan," "Princess Leia," or just "Princess." She would introduce herself as "Leia Organa, princess of Alderaan," "Princess Leia Organa of Alderaan," "Leia Organa, princess of the ruling house of Alderaan, heir to the crown," or "Princess Leia of the royal House of Organa, future queen of Alderaan."<ref name="Leia" />

Princess Leia's eventual husband, [[Corellian]] [[Han Solo]], often mocked Leia's royal title, referring to her as "Your Worship," "Your Highnessness"<ref name="Episode V" /> and "Her Royal Annoyance."<ref name="Smuggler's Run">''[[Smuggler's Run: A Han Solo & Chewbacca Adventure]]''</ref>

==Regalia and emblems==
{{Quote|Ow. That pulls.''"<br />"''And it's going to keep pulling. Today of all days, you must look your best.''"<br />"''You say that every day.''"<br />"''It's true every day. Standards are even higher for special occasions!|Princess Leia Organa and WA-2V, preparing for her Day of Demand|<ref name="Leia" />}}
[[File:Crest_of_Alderaan.svg|thumb|250px|The Crest of Alderaan]]
An emblem used by the royals was the [[Crest of Alderaan]], an upside-down triangle made of interlacing curves. It was notably featured on one of Viceroy Bail Organa's belts,<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" /> and Princess Leia had a hair clasp shaped like it.<ref name="A Princess on Lothal">{{Rebels|A Princess on Lothal}}</ref>

Alderaan's traditions called for the monarch to wear [[braid]]s and Alderaan's monarchy tended to [[Clothing|dress]] modestly, especially compared to the fatuous, bright garbs of the [[Monarch of Naboo|monarchs]] of [[Naboo]]. In fact, they favored relatively simple designs with muted [[color]]s with a viceroy's apparel of office included a long coat. Queen Breha notably owned a blue-and-[[bronze]] slash-sleeved dress that mirrored her planet's [[ocean]]s and valleys. Her state wardrobe also included a dress of bronze [[silk]], which she wore with her [[hair]] piled high atop her head in braids woven through with strings of beads, a green dress with wide skirts, and a deep red silk [[robe]]. In informal contexts, she wore simple [[velvet]] robes and let her hair down.<ref name="Leia" />

Simple white outfits also played a significant role in the monarchy. For example, both the monarch and his or her consort wore them during the investment ceremony of their heir. Also, Princess Leia was fond of simple white gowns that suited most occasions. The princess' simplistic style often caused her to clash with her attendant droid [[WA-2V]] who was programmed to ensure that the princess was always presented in grand style for every occasion.<ref name="Leia" /> During the [[Royal Award Ceremony]], she wore [[Delva Racine original hanging sleeve dress|one]] with long draping sleeves and a low scoop neckline.<ref name="ANH" /> created by fashion designer [[Delva Racine]].<ref name="Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

Leia Organa once noted that had [[Wedding of Han Solo and Leia Organa|her wedding]] been on Alderaan, in the [[Aldera cathedral|cathedral]] located in Aldera, she would have worn a customary lace dress. There also would have been an [[Standard hour|hour]] dedicated to the bell service, three different traditional [[Foodstuff|meals]] with guests of rank and more. Breha Organa also would have attached the Rhindon Sword to Leia's waist before going to meet her husband (or wife).<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>

[[File:CrownofAlderaan-LeiaManga.jpg|thumb|250px|The Crown of Alderaan]]
While there was a [[Crown of Alderaan]] it was usually kept in a [[museum]], only taken out on special occasions such as the heir's Day of Demand. The Heir's Crown was decorated with jewels.<ref name="Leia" /> There were also [[Alderaanian crown jewels|crown jewels]], which [[Brea Tonnika|Brea]] and [[Senni Tonnika]] once broke into the Royal Palace to steal; their attempt failed, forcing them to hide in a dumpster for two [[Standard day|nights]].<ref name="You Owe Me a Ride">{{StoryCite|story=You Owe Me a Ride|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

The thrones of the Alderaanian monarch and their consort were golden and were kept in the throne room of the Royal Palace of Alderaan on a dais.<ref name="Leia" />
