The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''12 BBY''',<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3265 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7965''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Ephraim Bridger|Ephraim]] and [[Mira Bridger]] are captured by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Gathering Forces" />
*[[Hera Syndulla]] leaves [[Ryloth]] to fight against the Empire.<ref name="Galactic Atlas" />
*[[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] becomes [[Executive officer|first officer]] of the [[Thunder Wasp]] under [[Commander]] [[Alfren Cheno]], then [[Captain]] after Cheno was forced out.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
*[[K-2SO]] is constructed on [[Vulpter]].<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
*[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] moves in to his newly constructed [[Fortress Vader|fortress]]<ref name="BSI note" /> after the [[Battle of Fortress Vader]]<ref name="Dark Lord 24">[[Darth Vader (2017) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 24]]</ref> and failing to resurrect [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Dark Lord 25" />
*[[Darth Sidious]]'s unnamed [[Cloning|clone]] "[[Reproduction|son]]," who later took on the name [[Dathan]], is created on [[Exegol]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref><ref name="Dathan birth date">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' establishes that [[Dathan]] is thirty-three [[Standard year|years]] old. ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' places the events of ''Shadow of the Sith'' thirteen [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, ''Shadow of the Sith'' takes place in [[21 ABY]], and Dathan must have been [[Cloning|created]] on [[Exegol]] in 12 BBY.</ref>
*[[Lieutenant]] [[Gorn]] is first stationed on the [[planet]] [[Aldhani]]<ref name="Gorn">{{Databank|lt-gorn|Lt. Gorn}}</ref> and begins serving under [[Commandant]] [[Jayhold Beehaz]].<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref><ref name="Andor date">According to "[[The Eye]]," [[Gorn]] has served under [[Jayhold Beehaz]] for seven [[Standard year|years]]. "The Eye" takes place in between "[[Kassa (episode)|Kassa]]," which dates itself to [[5 BBY]], and "[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]]," which {{SW|url=andor-s1-e8-trivia-gallery|text="Narkina 5" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 6}} dates to 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. Therefore, "The Eye" must take place in 5 BBY, seven years after 12 BBY.</ref>
