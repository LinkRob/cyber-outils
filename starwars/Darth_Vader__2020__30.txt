'''''Darth Vader'' 30''' is the thirtieth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Luke Ross]] and [[Federico Blee]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 11]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''ALL HER SHADOWS! During their terrible battle at Padmé's tomb on Naboo, the Queen's most devoted handmaidens were no match for the fury of Darth Vader. So as they are about to face the Dark Lord again, the handmaidens have prepared. But so has Vader. Who will triumph in the rematch? And what dark price will that victory exact?''

==Development==
On [[August 24]], [[2022]], writer [[Greg Pak]] tweeted that he had recently turned in the script for the issue.<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|gregpak|status/1562481344336105474|[[Greg Pak]]|quote=I just recently turned in the script to DARTH VADER #30 and am working on #31 right now, and it's hard to express how excited I am by the current storyline and all of the incredible creative collaborators working on this book. ==Continuity==
The opening crawl of the issue states that [[Sabé]] had been sent on a mission to [[Skako Minor]] to [[Death|assassinate]] [[Jul Tambor]], even though the issue itself, and the prior issue, [[Darth Vader (2020) 29|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 29]], depicted Sabé's [[Mission to Brentaal IV (Sabé)|mission]] as taking place on [[Brentaal IV]].<ref name="Vader 29">[[Darth Vader (2020) 29|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 29]]</ref>

On the first page, [[Saché]] refers to [[Eirtaé]] as "Saché."
