'''Feyn Gospic''' was a [[human]] who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], holding the [[military rank]] of [[colonel]] within the [[Massassi Group]]. After the rebel [[droid]] [[R2-D2]] arrived at the [[Great Temple|rebel headquarters]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]], Gospic and others immediately began to examine the [[Death Star plans]] stored within the droid. In the subsequent [[Battle of Yavin]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], the colonel used a [[Computer monitor|monitor]] within the war room of the headquarters alongside other Alliance leaders. The Alliance was ultimately able to destroy the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] during the battle.

==Biography==
[[File:BusyWarRoom-ANH.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=The command center in the Great Temple on Yavin 4 with various rebels working at consoles and tacitcal displays. Princess Leia Organa and C-3PO stand at a central table.|Feyn Gospic served in the rebel war room during the Battle of Yavin.]]
The [[human]]<ref name="Episode IV" /> Feyn Gospic held the [[military rank]] of [[colonel]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Rise of the Rebellion" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> Gospic was stationed in the [[Great Temple]] headquarters<ref name="Rise of the Rebellion" /> of the Alliance's [[Massassi Group]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]]<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> when the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' arrived carrying the [[Death Star plans|plans]] to the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]. Gospic, [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]], [[Major]] [[Lajaie]], and others then immediately began examining the plans, which had been stored in the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]], in order to find a way to destroy the [[battle station]].<ref name="Rise of the Rebellion" />

The Alliance soon discovered a [[Thermal exhaust port|vulnerability]] in the [[Death Star]]'s design that had been purposefully created by the [[scientist]] [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]]<ref name="Rise of the Rebellion" /> and would allow the [[weapon]] to be destroyed. The Death Star then arrived in the [[Yavin system]] to destroy the Alliance, and several [[squadron]]s of [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps|Alliance starfighters]] were deployed to eliminate the [[Space station|station]] before it could use its [[Mk I Superlaser|superlaser]]. During the ensuing [[Battle of Yavin|battle]], Gospic worked at a [[Computer monitor|monitor]] station within<ref name="Episode IV" /> the war room<ref name="AD">{{Film|IV}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> of the Great Temple where the Alliance's strategists were providing tactical support to the [[starfighter]] squadrons. Ultimately, the Alliance successfully destroyed the Death Star<ref name="Episode IV" /> and then completely [[Evacuation of Yavin 4|evacuated]] its Yavin base.<ref name="Rise of the Rebellion" />

==Personality and traits==
Feyn Gospic had light skin and [[Color|brown]] [[hair]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

During the Battle of Yavin, Gospic wore a gray [[jumpsuit]] and a [[headset]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Feyn Gospic first appeared in the [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="Episode IV" /> Gospic's scenes were filmed on H Stage at [[Shepperton Studios]] between [[May 13]] and [[May 18]], [[1976]].<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character was identified in "[[The Rise of the Rebellion and the Battle of Yavin]]," a part of the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series published by [[De Agostini]]<ref name="Rise of the Rebellion" /> on [[March 9]], [[2021]].<ref name="De Agostini Mexico">{{DeAgostini|lang=es|url=libros-comics/enciclopedia-star-wars|text=Enciclopedia Star Wars}}</ref> The name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used on a card in the [[1998]] "[[Special Edition Limited]]" set of the [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]] produced by [[Decipher]]<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Special Edition Limited|cardname=Colonel Feyn Gospic}}</ref>
