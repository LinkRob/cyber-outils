'''Bullethead''' was a member of the [[Droid Gotra]] who provided security for [[the Twins]], a pair of [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]]s, alongside [[Arges (droid)|Arges]] in their [[Hutt Palace]] on the [[planet]] [[Nal Hutta]]. During or after [[11 ABY]], the [[Mandalorian]] [[mercenary]] [[Din Djarin]] and his [[Gender|son]], [[Din Grogu]], [[Battle of Nal Hutta (Din Djarin)|fought]] Bullethead and Arges in order to reach the Hutts after they had unsuccessfully tried to [[Death|execute]] Djarin.

During the battle, Grogu managed to damage Arges' wiring enough to cause it to unintentionally shoot Bullethead with a [[blaster]]. This provided Djarin an opportunity to activate several [[grenade]]s on Bullethead's [[belt]], which then detonated and destroyed the droid.

==Biography==
===Silent guardian===
Bullethead<ref name="Tested">{{YouTube|video=ijk4XMe7n_c|text=Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu Set Visit!|channel=channel/UCiDJtJKMICpb9B1qf7qjEOA|name=Adam Savage’s Tested}}</ref> was a gigantic member of the [[Droid Gotra]] who was among the forces hired to provide security for the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]]s known as [[the Twins]] in the [[Hutt Palace]] on the [[planet]] [[Nal Hutta]] during the [[New Republic Era|reign]] of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Mando&Grogu" /> During or after [[11 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate" /> Bullethead guarded the doorway to the palace's throneroom alongside the similarly scaled [[droid]] [[Arges (droid)|Arges]] and both were knelt, still and silent, to the sides of the throneroom doorway when the [[Mandalorian]] [[mercenary]] [[Din Djarin]] and his [[Gender|son]] [[Din Grogu]] arrived for an audience with the Hutts to discuss him [[Mission to Shakari|bringing]] their nephew [[Rotta]] back to them safely.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

After Djarin elected not to honor his agreement with the Hutts, he was later [[Ambush on the Clan Mudhorn cabin|brought back]] before them as a [[prisoner]] by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Embo]], who marched the Mandalorian past Bullethead and Arges into the throneroom in [[Stuncuffs|binders]]. Once again, the droids did not move or acknowledge the arrivals. After Djarin [[Rescue of Din Djarin|escaped]] from an attempted [[Death|execution]] by the Twins, the mercenary returned to the throne room's entryway a third and final [[time]], having [[Battle of Nal Hutta (Din Djarin)|fought]] through many other members of the Droid Gotra with Grogu to get there. This time, Bullethead and Arges did acknowledge the mercenary and his son, standing to do battle.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

===2v2===
During the fight, Bullethead made use of [[grenade]]s stored on its [[belt]] and a club while Arges fired with a [[blaster]]; however, Grogu managed to climb inside Arges and began severing the droid's circuitry. This meant that as Djarin and Bullethead battled, Arges' aim was severely impacted, causing the droid to miss Djarin multiple times and eventually leading to one of the bolts hitting Bullethead and bringing the droid to its knees.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

This distraction gave Djarin time to rush over to Bullethead and activate the remaining grenades on the droid's belt. Bullethead had time only to lift one of the flashing devices up to look at before they detonated and destroyed the droid. Djarin and Grogu then defeated Arges as well and went on to aid Rotta in besting the Twins and causing their deaths, after which [[Adelphi Rangers|New Republic forces]] destroyed the entire palace where Bullethead had fallen.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

==Characteristics==
Bullethead was a large bipedal, [[Color|gray]]-plated droid with four [[Limb|arms]] and red [[sensor]]s in a tapered, pointed head. It wore a belt of grenades and used a [[club]] in combat.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Bullethead-Tested.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=The stop-motion model of Bullethead with its club raised.|Phil Tippet built the stop-motion model for Bullethead.]]
{{Quote|This is the actual stop-motion models from Tippett. So, they're next level. And this was a very ambitious stop-motion sequence. So, because we had the time to plan and we had people like John Knoll coordinating all of it, we're able to engage with Tippet and have them do a&mdash;and they're actually&mdash;you saw Mando's scale's about here. So, these are giant droids.|Jon Favreau|<ref name="Tested" />}}
Bullethead first appeared in the film ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]'',<ref name="Mando&Grogu" /> which was released on [[May 22]], [[2026]]. Prior to the film's release, the character was revealed in the film's first trailer, which aired on [[September 22]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|_pa1KLXuW0Y|The Mandalorian and Grogu &#124; Official Trailer &#124; In Theaters May 22, 2026}}</ref>

The droid was a stop-motion model created by [[Phil Tippett]], who director [[Jon Favreau]] was able to work with on the ambitious stop-motion fight sequence due to the length of time they had to produce the film and the involvement of people like [[John Knoll]] as a coordinator. The droid's name was first revealed in a video where Favreau discusses the stop-motion droids with [[Adam Savage]] that was posted to the [https://www.youtube.com/@tested Adam Savage's Tested] [[Wikipedia:Youtube|YouTube]] channel on [[April 16]], 2026.<ref name="Tested" />
