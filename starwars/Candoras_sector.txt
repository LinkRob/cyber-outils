The '''Candoras sector''' was a [[Freestanding subsectors|freestanding subsector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> a term used for unincorporated [[sector]]s and regions whose inclusion into the [[Galactic Republic]] or [[Galactic Empire]] was not yet ratified,<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> on the edges of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It contained the [[Spillwater system]] and the [[Poln system]].<ref name="Appendix" />
