'''Deep Core''' was a type of dark [[lum]]<ref name="Faster" /> found at [[Cid's Parlor]] in [[Ord Mantell City]] on [[Ord Mantell]],<ref name="Rampage" /> in the [[cantina]] at the [[Safa Toma Speedway]] on [[Serolonis]],<ref name="Faster" /> in [[Mokko]]'s quarters in his [[Mokkotown|town]] on [[Ipsidon]]<ref name="Retrieval" /> and in [[Tanda's Cantina]] on [[Suria space station]].<ref name="Bad Territory" /> The [[Alcohol|beverage]] came in a dark [[Color|purple]] bottle with a dark label, with the drink's name written in [[Aurebesh]], and "DARK LUM" written in smaller letters underneath.<ref name="Faster" />
