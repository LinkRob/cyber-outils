The '''B2-RP battle droid''', also known as the '''B2-RP super battle droid''', '''rocket droid''' or '''jetpack droid''', was a [[B2-series super battle droid]] model used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Engineered by the [[Techno Union]],<ref name="B2DB">{{Databank|b2-rp-battle-droid|B2-RP battle droid}}</ref> they were equipped with [[jetpack]]s which incorporated compact [[repulsorlift]]s,<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> but were otherwise were the same as standard B2s. They were created as the Separatists response to [[clone jetpack trooper]]s.<ref name="Official Magazine 118">{{GermanFanCite|118|Database}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
B2-RP super battle droids augmented the power of the normal [[B2-series super battle droid]]s with their built-in [[jetpack]]s,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> which incorporated compact [[repulsorlift]]s,<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> creating a formidable aerial threat that the [[Galactic Republic|Republic's]] ground [[troop]]s, fighters and [[gunship]]s had to deal with.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Their jetpacks enabled them to fly and they were also capable infiltrating enemy [[Starship|ships]].<ref name="Jedi Crash" /> Like the standard B2s,<ref name="JtB corresponding">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[B2-series super battle droid]]s</ref> the B2-RP [[battle droid]]s were 1.93 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]], 4 inches) tall, had [[Gender|masculine programming]] and [[Color|red]] [[photoreceptor]]s.<ref name="Jedi Crash" /> The [[droid]]s also had thick [[armor]], aggressive programming,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> as well as [[wrist blaster]]s on each [[Limb|arm]].<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

==History==
[[File:Battle_of_Quell.png|thumb|left|250px|B2-RPs attacking Secura's last cruiser.]]
At the [[Battle of Quell]], a large force of B2-RPs was deployed to attack [[Aayla Secura]]'s [[Aayla Secura's task force|task force]], swarming the last remaining [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|Republic cruiser]]. They landed and quickly boarded the ship, advancing down the corridors of the doomed vessel and forcing Secura and her forces to abandon ship.<ref name="Jedi Crash" /> A month prior to the [[mission to Batuu]], the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] was damaged by a bolt fired from an B2-RP.<ref name="Thrawn">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>

Later in the war, an upgraded B2-RP known as the [[Super battle droid rocket trooper|B2 super rocket trooper]] was introduced.<ref name="Ultimate SW" /><ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref>

Later, an entry on B2-RPs written by [[Clone Commander]] [[Bly]] was included in "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]," a survival compendium sent out by Commander [[Neyo]] that listed notable battle droids.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />
