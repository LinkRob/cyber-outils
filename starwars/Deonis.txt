'''Deonis''' was a [[human]] [[gambler]] who resided on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During which they played [[sabacc]] against a [[Gallusian]] named [[Bosnok]] and [[Trandoshan]] named [[Bliziu]] at [[Kaslo's Sabacc Parlor]] in [[Mirogana]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of Toshara. They had light skin, [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s and brown [[hair]]. While playing at [[Kaslo]]'s, Deonis wore a [[clothing|green]] shirt, dark pants, black pants and a beaded [[necklace]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> a gambler named [[Kay Vess]] played a game of [[Kessel Sabacc]] against Deonis, Bosnok, and Bliziu. During which Vess won six hundred [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and a [[Canto Pass]] game piece called [[The Score]] that Bosnok had added to the prize pot.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Deonis appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The game the player plays against Deonis was part of the "High-Stakes Showdown" quest. After completing the player wins the game for the quest they can continue to play sabacc against the group to win additional credits.<ref name="Outlaws" />
