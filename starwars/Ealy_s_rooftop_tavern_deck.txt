'''Ealy's rooftop tavern deck''', or simply '''Ealy's''', was a [[Cantina|tavern]] located in a five-story building in a [[Unidentified city (Reena)|city]] on the [[Colonies]] [[planet]] [[Reena]]. The tavern was frequented by [[Axel Greylark]], a student of [[Reena University]]. During the [[Queen's Bloom Festival]] around [[396 BBY]], Greylark and his friends almost celebrated the festival at Ealy's before going to [[Gorag Tower]] instead.

==Description==
{{Quote|Really? A tavern?|Kozmo Sundrel IV|<ref name="QueensBloom" />}}
Ealy's rooftop tavern deck, also known as just Ealy's, was located inside a five-story building in a [[Unidentified city (Reena)|city]] on the [[Colonies]] [[planet]] [[Reena]]. The establishment had a fountain overflowing with a [[Foodstuff|beverage]] the owner referred to as "[[giddy tonic]]."<ref name="QueensBloom" />

==History==
{{Quote|I have a better idea for premier views.|Leyli Romero, suggesting Gorag Tower instead of Ealy's|<ref name="QueensBloom" />}}
[[File:Axel-Greylark-headshot.png|thumb|right|240px|Axel Greylark (pictured) frequented Ealy's.]]
Ealy's was the first place discovered by [[Axel Greylark]] during his tenure as a student at [[Reena University]], and he listened to [[Merchant|traders]] and [[pilot]]s tell stories about evading [[pirate]]s on the [[Caloria Run]]<ref name="QueensBloom" /> [[hyperspace route]]<ref name="Knights of Fate">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref> at the tavern&mdash;an activity he found more exciting than his [[Political Ideology in the Outer Rim]] class.<ref name="QueensBloom" /> Around [[396 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' establishes that [[Axel Greylark]] was eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old thirteen years prior to its events. As ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''Convergence'' to [[382 BBY]], he must have been [[Reproduction|born]] in [[413 BBY]]. Greylark is seventeen years old in "[[The Queen's Bloom]]," dating its events to around [[396 BBY]].</ref> Greylark and fellow students [[Leyli Romero]] and [[Kozmo Sundrel IV]] flouted their responsibilities at the [[Queen's Bloom Festival]] and arrived at the building Ealy's was in to observe the Queen's Bloom. After Sundrel reacted poorly to the tavern, Romero convinced them to sneak into a penthouse in [[Gorag Tower]] instead. After nearly encountering his [[Parent|mother]]&mdash;the [[Senator]] [[Kyong Greylark]]&mdash;at the party in Gorag Tower, Axel remarked that they should have gone to Ealy's instead.<ref name="QueensBloom" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ealy's rooftop tavern deck was mentioned in the short story "[[The Queen's Bloom]]," written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="QueensBloom" />
