'''Comlinks''', also known as '''communicators''', were standard handheld communication devices, fit with microphones and receivers, and were used throughout [[the galaxy]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] employed the [[C1 personal comlink]], manufactured by [[SoroSuub Corporation]].<ref name="datacom" />

During the [[Cold War|conflict]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], [[Bazine Netal]] used a comlink to inform the First Order that [[BB-8]] had been located at [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on [[Takodana]].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> Comlinks during that era could also be fitted with [[holoprojector]]s.<ref name="Signal from Sector Six">{{Resistance|Signal from Sector Six}}</ref>
