'''Falshoi''' was a [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> [[Gender|female]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] who held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[lieutenant]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> Around [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> the [[fighter wing]] [[Attack on Cerberon|fought]] a [[New Republic]] [[Barma Battle Group|battle group]] at the [[Cerberon system]]. Falshoi took part in the attack, which saw the devastation of the [[battle group]]'s [[New Republic Defense Fleet|naval forces]] above the [[planet]] [[Troithe]]. With the 204th's [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="New Villain">{{SW|url=news/shadow-fall-novel-first-look-at-new-villain-and-more|text=''Shadow Fall'' Novel: First Look at New Villain and More}}</ref> having been lost, the [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s and [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bombers]] of the 204th were ordered by [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]] to attack New Republic positions on the surface of Troithe.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

Falshoi soon landed her [[TIE series|TIE]] at Troithe's [[Raddakkia Plaza]], which the fighter wing used as a staging ground to repair and refuel their [[Starfighter|fighters]]. Her injuries from the prior fighting required her to wear a sling for her [[Limb|arm]]. After [[Second Lieutenant Commander]] [[Teso Broosh]] and his [[squadron]], [[Squadron Five (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Five]], made a supply run and set up a makeshift galley, Falshoi approached Keize and offered to get [[Foodstuff|food]] for him. The colonel accepted the lieutenant's offer and thanked her for it.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
