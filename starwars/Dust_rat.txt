'''Dust rats''' were a type of [[rodent]] that lived on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], where they were commonly found across all of the world's [[City|settlements]]. They relied entirely upon off the leftovers and trash of the world's population and were widely considered pests due to the severe amount of damage they could cause to [[farm]]s and homes.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dust rats appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
