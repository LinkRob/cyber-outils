'''Elbriade''' was a [[Planet/Legends|planet]] where the narcotic [[Blue (narcotic)|Blue]] could be found. It was possible that a drug trafficking ring headed by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] could be traced there. The planet was annexed into the Empire during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. During this time, a group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel investigators]] went under cover on [[Sanod]] to find the source of the drug trafficking.

==Behind the scenes==
Elbriade was created, along with [[Sanod]] and the planet [[KX-112]] as part of the French [[Roleplaying game|role-playing game]] supplement in the magazine [[Casus Belli (magazine)|''Casus Belli'']].

This planet is not mentioned in the ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''.
