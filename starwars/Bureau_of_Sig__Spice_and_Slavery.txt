The '''Bureau of Sig, Spice and Slavery''', shortened to '''S3''', was an intelligence and analysis [[law enforcement agency]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] which was formed [[Standard century|centuries]] prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. S3 was responsible for the examination of weaponry, [[spice]], and [[Slavery|slaves]] that were recovered from arrests made by the [[Republic Security Force]] and traced their history to create a map of criminal records. Due to corruption within the [[Galactic Senate]], S3 suffered a cut in staff members, budget, and facilities for [[Standard decade|decades]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bureau of Sig, Spice and Slavery was mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Rise" />
