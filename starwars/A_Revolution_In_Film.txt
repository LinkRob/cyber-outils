"'''A Revolution In Film'''" is a behind-the-scenes section of ''[[Star Wars: The Official Starships & Vehicles Collection]]'' magazine. It provides short, broad accounts of the unique creative processes behind the ''[[Star Wars]]'' films. The feature of this section is sometimes related to the craft or character on the issue's cover.
