'''Danite''' was a type of [[crystal]]. Either a danite or [[Kathracite crystal|kathracite]] crystal was present in each of the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[training lightsaber]]s&mdash;a non-[[Death|lethal]] [[lightsaber]] variant that utilized an ultra-low-output [[power generator]] and created a blade that did not cause lasting damage to an opponent.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Danite was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''.<ref name="Rise" /> The [[Danite/Legends|crystal]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in the [[1996]] [[roleplaying game]] sourcebook ''[[Tales of the Jedi Companion]]'', written by [[George R. Strayton]] and published by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="TotJC">''[[Tales of the Jedi Companion]]''</ref>
