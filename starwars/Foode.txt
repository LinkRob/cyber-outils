'''Foode''' was located on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Put up in the street were three vertical neon signs in different colors, blue, pink and red. The text was written in [[Aurebesh]], but the blue sign was reversed like a mirror image. The two other ones were presented normally.<ref name="Chapter 23" />
