The '''Eedit Road''' was a road that ran straight through the thick vine jungles of the [[planet]] [[Devaron]]. Joining the [[City|town]] of [[Tikaroo]] and the [[Temple of Eedit]], the road was built by the [[Jedi Order]] and made of red stone.<ref name="NoP" />

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Savage Opress]] traveled on Eedit Road when he [[Devaron massacre|stormed]] the Temple of Eedit, on the orders of his new master, [[Count of Serenno|Serenno]] [[Dooku]].<ref name="Monster">{{TCW|Monster}}</ref>
