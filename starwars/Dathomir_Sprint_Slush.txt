The '''Dathomir Sprint Slush''' was a cold, sweet [[Foodstuff|beverage]] available at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> The drink was [[Color|pink]] and white in color. The beverage, whose name is shared with the planet [[Dathomir]], was consumable by [[human]]s.<ref name="Kat Saka" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dathomir Sprint Slush was added to the menu of Kat Saka's Kettle at [[Disneyland]]'s [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in summer, [[2024]]. After a hiatus, it returned in [[January]], [[2025]]. The menu description is "[[Wikipedia:Watermelon|Watermelon]] and [[Wikipedia:Lemon|Lemon]]-[[Wikipedia:Lime (fruit)|Lime]] Slush, [[Wikipedia:Popping boba|Popping Spheres]], [[Wikipedia:Strawberry|Strawberry]] [[Cream]]."<ref name="DL Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/kat-sakas-kettle/menus|text=Disneyland: Kat Saka's Kettle Snack Menu}}</ref>
