'''Dors Urtya''' was a [[Mon Calamari]] who served as [[Grand Admiral]] of the [[Mon Cala Mercantile Fleet]], [[Regent]] of [[Mon Cala]], [[Dual Protector of the Two People]], and [[Warden]] of the [[King]] in [[Exile (punishment)|exile]]. Some [[time]] following the [[battle for Jedha]], Urtya met with [[Gial Ackbar]], [[Han Solo]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Luke Skywalker]], [[R2-D2]], and [[C-3PO]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in order to secure a coalition.

==Biography==
Dors Urtya<ref name="BTCTG">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> was a [[Mon Calamari]]. By the time of the [[mutiny on Mon Cala]], he served as the [[Warden]] of the [[King]] in [[Exile (punishment)|Exile]], [[Regent]], [[Grand Admiral]] of the [[Mon Cala Mercantile Fleet]], and bore the title of [[Dual Protector of the Two People]]. At that time, he agreed to meet with agents of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] in secret on Mon Cala. He declined to join in open rebellion, as the risk to Mon Cala was too great.<ref name="Star Wars 44" />

After [[Heroes of Yavin|Leia Organa's team]] recorded King [[Lee-Char]]'s final message, Urtya took the recording. He ultimately decided to play it planet-wide, knowing the greater good it would spark. It sparked an uprising. In response, the [[Galactic Empire]] fired on his headquarters, killing Urtya and his [[Urtya's guards|droid guards]].<ref name="Star Wars 49" />

==Personality and traits==
Urtya was a Mon Calamari with [[Color|silvery]] skin and blue [[eye]]s. He often wore regal blue-and-gold armor as well as a blue cape. He possessed many titles, but was not fond of them, considering them empty and believing that Mon Cala truly belonged to [[Moff]] [[Tan Hubi]]. He was fiercely protective of his people,<ref name="Star Wars 44" /> and believed that the King's message was solely a Mon Calamari matter.<ref name="Star Wars 49" />
