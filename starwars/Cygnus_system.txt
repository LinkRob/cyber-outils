The '''Cygnus system''' was located in the [[Kontahr sector]], a part of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square R-11 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cygnus [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Cygnus B system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention in "[[Cantina Communications]]," a one-page vignette authored by [[John Chesterman]] and included in the [[Star Wars Official Poster Monthly 16|sixteenth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Official Poster Monthly]]'' magazine, published in [[1979]] by [[Paradise Press]].<ref name="Poster">{{StoryCite|story=Cantina Communications|book=Star Wars Official Poster Monthly 16}}</ref> The [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry subsequently provided the shortened designation "Cygnus system."<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
