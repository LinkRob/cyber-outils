The '''first season''' of the animated [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'' television series]] premiered on [[September 7]], 1985, with the airing of the episode "[[The Cries of the Trees]]" on [[ABC Television Network|ABC]]. The [[Ewoks (song)|theme song]] was unique to this season, as a [[Friends Together, Friends Forever|new one]] was introduced for the [[Ewoks: Season Two|second season]].
