The '''equinox''' was a [[time]] of celebration on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. Various festivities would be held, including a gala at the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan|Royal Palace]] which demanded formal dress.<ref name="Eclipse" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> preparations for the equinox festivities in three [[Standard week|weeks]] were well underway when the planet was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]].<ref name="Eclipse" />
