'''''Entertainment Weekly's Ultimate Guide to Rogue One''''' is a standalone magazine published by [[Entertainment Weekly Books|''Entertainment Weekly'']] that gave in-depth coverage to ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. It was released on [[December 9]], [[2016]], a week before the film itself premieres. Included in the book is a foreword by Director [[Gareth Edwards]], a map of [[the galaxy]], coverage on the characters such as [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]], [[Mon Mothma]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], and the [[stormtrooper]]s, plus some focus on the [[Death Star]], as well as coverage on the new creatures, [[droid]]s, and coverage on some old favorites, plus production photos for the film, interviews with the cast and crew, presentation of upcoming toy figures to promote the film, as well as a timeline for the ''[[Star Wars]]'' series thus far.<ref name="Amazon" />

==Continuity==
The map provided in the magazine contains several errors. On the map, after leaving its origin world of [[Geonosis]], the [[Death Star]] is shown to have visited [[Scarif]] and [[Alderaan]] before meeting its destruction at [[Yavin 4]], without ever visiting [[Jedha]],<ref name="Entertainment Weekly's Ultimate Guide to Rogue One">''Entertainment Weekly's Ultimate Guide to Rogue One''</ref> as seen in the feature film, ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> [[Iego]] is misspelled as "Lego." [[Kessel]] is put within [[Hutt Space]]<ref name="Entertainment Weekly's Ultimate Guide to Rogue One" /> instead of being located right outside its borders.<ref name="Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> The map also lists ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' as a source,<ref name="Entertainment Weekly's Ultimate Guide to Rogue One" /> although the book had not yet been released at the time of the magazine's publishing.<ref name="Amazon" /><ref name="Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide date">{{DK|url=us/9781465452634-star-wars-rogue-one-the-ultimate-visual-guide|text=Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide|nolive=1}}</ref>
