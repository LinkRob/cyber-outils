'''Alex Ross''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] artist who has drawn covers for the first four issues of [[Dark Horse Comics]]' [[Star Wars (Dark Horse Comics 2013)|''Star Wars'']] comic series.<ref name="SWI" /> He has also provided variant covers for the first issues of [[Marvel Comics]]' [[2015]] [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']],<ref name="Marvel.com">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/article/2014/10/20/star-wars-cover-alex-ross-marvel|text='Star Wars' exclusive: Check out this Alex Ross cover for Marvel's 'Star Wars #1'|author=Franich, Darren|date=2014-10-20|work=''[[Entertainment Weekly]]''|archivedate=20200630205833}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2015)|''Darth Vader'']],<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-darth-vader-1-alex-ross-variant-cover-exclusive-reveal|text=''Star Wars: Darth Vader'' #1 Alex Ross Variant Cover &ndash; Exclusive Reveal!}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Princess Leia|''Princess Leia'']],<ref name="PREVIEWSworld">{{Previews|url=Catalog/JAN150730|text=PRINCESS LEIA #1 (OF 5) ROSS VAR}}</ref> and [[Star Wars: Lando (comic series)|''Lando'']] series.<ref name="PREVIEWSworld2">{{Previews|url=Catalog/MAY150769|text=STAR WARS LANDO #1 (OF 5) ROSS VAR}}</ref>

Ross did a piece for the [[2010]] art book ''[[Star Wars Art: Visions]]'' titled ''Empire of Style''.<ref name="OldSW.com">{{SWArchive|url=vault/books/swvisions_alexross/index.html|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' -- Empire of Style}}</ref> His artwork for the book was later used as a cover for the French magazine ''Star Wars &ndash; La Saga en BD'' #38 published by [[Delcourt]].<ref name="Delcourt">{{WebCite|url=http://www.editions-delcourt.fr/catalogue/comics/star_wars_la_saga_en_bd_38|text=Star Wars &ndash; La Saga en BD #38|archivedate=20130627230054}}</ref>

He was one of the artists providing exclusive artwork at [[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim's]] Art Show.<ref name="SW.com2">{{SW|url=news/j-j-abrams-and-kathleen-kennedy-to-kick-off-star-wars-celebration|text=J.J. Abrams and Kathleen Kennedy to Kick Off ''Star Wars'' Celebration}}</ref> His piece, titled ''Han/Chewie Wanted'',<ref name="CAnaheim">{{Celebration|url=Events/Events-A-H/Art-Show/#Chris-Trevas|text=Art Show &ndash; Star Wars Celebration|celebration=[[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim]]|nolive=1}}</ref> pays homage to [[Gil Kane]] and [[Tony DeZuniga]]'s cover for [[Star Wars (1977) 7|''Star Wars'' (1977) 7]].<ref name="CBR">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cbr.com/exclusive-alex-ross-pays-homage-to-tony-dezuniga-in-star-wars-celebration-lithograph|text=EXCLUSIVE: Alex Ross Pays Homage to Tony DeZuniga in Star Wars Celebration Lithograph|author=Albert Ching|date=2015-02-13|work=[https://www.cbr.com Comic Book Resources]|archivedate=20200819102116}}</ref> It was later used as a variant cover for the [[Chewbacca (2015) 1|first issue]] of Marvel's [[Star Wars: Chewbacca (2015)|''Chewbacca'']] miniseries.<ref name="CBR2">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cbr.com/chewbacca-1-unlettered|text=Preview: Chewbacca #1 (Unlettered)|date=2015-09-15|work=[https://www.cbr.com Comic Book Resources]|archivedate=20200819102357}}</ref>
