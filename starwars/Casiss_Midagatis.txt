'''Casiss Midagatis'''<ref name="Join the Battle" /> was a [[Gender|male]] [[Felucian]] who was the elder of a [[nysillin]] [[farm]]ing village of [[Akira]] on the [[planet]] [[Felucia]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], his village was threatened by the [[Ohnaka Gang]] who wanted a portion of the nysillin. Midagatis hired four [[bounty hunter]]s&mdash;[[Sugi (bounty hunter)|Sugi]], [[Embo]], [[Rumi Paramita]], and [[Seripas]]&mdash;to fight the [[pirate]]s, and later, the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Ahsoka Tano]] also agreed to help after their ship crash-landed nearby. After helping the villagers learn how to fight and defeat the [[Ohnaka Gang]], Midagatis thanked the Jedi and bounty hunters before setting off to oversee the rebuilding of the village.<ref name="Bounty Hunters" />

==Personality and traits==
Midagatis was 1.35 [[meter]]s (4 [[Foot|feet]], 5 inches) tall Felucian.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> He had [[Color|red]] [[eye]]s and green skin. Unable to protect themselves from [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s pirates, he hired four bounty hunters and later convinced the hired [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] to work alongside the Jedi to defend Akira and the nysillin crops.<ref name="Bounty Hunters" />

==Skills and abilities==
As the elder of the nysillin farming village of Akira, Midagatis was an effective leader and an experienced farmer.<ref name="Join the Battle" />
