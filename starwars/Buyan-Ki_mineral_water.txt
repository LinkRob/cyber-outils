'''Buyan-Ki mineral water''' was a type of [[mineral]] [[water]] used by the [[Koori]] [[Foodstuff|street food]] stall to make [[Duradan Soup]] in [[Kijimi City]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] and her [[merqaal]] companion [[Nix]] shared a meal at Koori which included Duradan Soup made with Buyan-Ki mineral water.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Buyan-Ki mineral water was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was mentioned in the description of Duradan Soup, one of the "Nix treat" galactic street foods featured in the game.<ref name="Outlaws" />
