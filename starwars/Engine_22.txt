'''Engine 22''' was a [[HFY-3920 shuttle ferry|shuttle ferry]] used by [[Axis network|rebel]] [[Luthen Rael]] to get to the [[Ferrix]] [[Ferrix City|city center]] in [[5 BBY]]. Local man [[Willi]] also rode the same ferry and warned Rael about pickpockets.<ref name="Reckoning">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref>
