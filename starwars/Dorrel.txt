'''Dorrel''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Bith/Legends|Bith]] [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] stationed on [[Hoth/Legends|Hoth]] during the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]] between the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|reconstituted Sith Empire]].<ref name="TOR" />

==Behind the scenes==
