At some point between [[230 BBY]] and [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s repeatedly '''bombed''' the caldera in the north of the [[Planet|world]] [[Isolt]]. The [[bomb]]ings occurred as some Nihil wanted to know if they could cause a [[Volcano|volcanic]] super eruption similar to the [[Destruction of Dalna|one]] the Nihil had carried out on<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> [[Dalna]]. The Nihil were ultimately successful in causing at least one such eruption, as well as destroying most of the life on Isolt, leaving it a quiet world.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
