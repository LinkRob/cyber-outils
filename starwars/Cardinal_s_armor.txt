A set of [[Color|red]] personalized '''armor''' was worn by the [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] CD-0922, later known as [[Archex|Cardinal]]. The armor was given to him by [[General]] [[Brendol Hux]] at a ceremony in front of thousands of other stormtroopers. It was colored red by Hux because he believed it was the color of power. It also had a traditional First Order [[captain]]'s [[cape]].<ref name="Phasma" />
