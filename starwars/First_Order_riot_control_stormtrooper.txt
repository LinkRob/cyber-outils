The '''First Order riot control stormtrooper''', also called simply the '''riot control stormtrooper''', was a specialized variant of the standard [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] of the [[First Order]]. Assigned to keep order on worlds within First Order territory, they were trained in riot-control tactics and given non-lethal equipment,<ref name="DB" /> such as lightweight composite [[betaplast]] ballistic [[riot shield]]s and [[Z6 riot control baton]]s<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> They also carried lethal weapons, such as the [[Sonn-Blas Corporation|Sonn-Blas]] [[F-11D blaster rifle]], in case situations spiraled out of control.<ref name="DB" /><ref name="TFAVD" /> Indeed, riot control stormtroopers could be fielded into active battlefields as specialized soldiers, during which they would wield any of the lethal or non-lethal weapons on their person depending on what the battlefield called for.<ref name="Episode VII" />

During the [[Battle of Takodana]], [[FN-2199]], armed with a Z6 riot control baton, fought his former brother-in-arms [[Finn]] in a [[lightsaber]] duel, besting Finn until [[Han Solo]] shot him with [[Chewbacca]]'s [[bowcaster]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

Riot control stormtroopers were later present aboard the ''[[Supremacy]]'' when [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] ordered the executions of both Finn and [[Resistance]] [[technician]] [[Rose Tico]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />
