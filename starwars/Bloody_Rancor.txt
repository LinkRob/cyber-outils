'''Bloody Rancors''' were [[alcohol]]ic beverages available on the [[planet]]s [[Batuu]], [[Tatooine]], and [[Takodana]]. On the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] world of Batuu, the drink was served in [[Oga's Cantina]] and was a dark [[Color|brown]] liquid served in a [[glass]] with an [[Diet|edible]] [[bone]]-shaped garnish on top. [[Oga Garra]], the proprietor of the [[cantina]], had the beverage imported from Tatooine.

==Description==
{{Quote|Where's the bone for my Bloody Rancor?|translated=1|Oga Garra angrily demands a bone garnish in [[Huttese]]|<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />}}
A Bloody Rancor was a beverage that was dark brown in [[color]] and was served in a short [[glass]] with an [[Diet|edible]] [[bone]]-shaped garnish on top. The drink, which shared its name with the [[rancor]] [[species]], was [[alcohol]]ic and suitable for [[human]],<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> [[Latero]],<ref name="Fallen Order" /> and [[Wookiee]] consumption.<ref name="Black Spire" /> At [[Oga's Cantina]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]], the [[Cantina|bar's]] proprietor, [[Oga Garra]], served the drink on a coaster that read "Bloody Rancor" and "imported from [[Tatooine]]"<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />&mdash;a planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>&mdash;in [[Aurebesh]], alongside a depiction of a rancor. Also served at Oga's was a variation on the Bloody Rancor called the [[Angry Bloody Rancor]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />

==History==
Bloody Rancors were available to patrons of a castle on<ref name="Fallen Order" /> the planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> [[Takodana]], which the Latero [[captain]] [[Greez Dritus]] praised as the best in [[the galaxy]] to the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]]<ref name="Fallen Order" /> in [[14 BBY]].<ref name="JFO Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> It was also served at Oga's Cantina in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on Batuu sometime during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Black Spire date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the Wookiee [[N'arrghela]] drank a Bloody Rancor in the cantina and picked her [[Tooth|teeth]] with the bone from her drink.<ref name="Black Spire" /> The same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Garra had a Bloody Rancor that was missing the bone garnish on top, which she angrily demanded to know the location of from her workers.<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|Oga's Cantina|bar}}</ref> The [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] included a photo of a Bloody Rancor in his book&mdash;''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''&mdash;in a section detailing his recommendations for Batuu newcomers visiting Oga's Cantina, which was published<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Bloody Rancor first appeared as an alcoholic beverage sold to guests at the Oga's Cantina bar in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The beverage was first revealed as part of a media preview event held on [[February 26]], 2019 and is a morning drink similar to a [[Wikipedia:Bloody Mary|Bloody Mary]].<ref name="Nerdist">{{WebCite|url=https://nerdist.com/article/star-wars-galaxys-edge-food-drinks-cocktails-menu-disney-parks|text=What You Can Eat and Drink in STAR WARS: Galaxy's Edge|author=[[Amy Ratcliffe]]|date=2019-02-27|work=[https://nerdist.com Nerdist]|archivedate=20190228074254}}</ref> The cantina's menu description reads, "Tito's Handmade [[Wikipedia:Vodka|Vodka]], Ancho Reyes Chile [[Wikipedia:Liqueur|Liqueur]], Spicy Bloody Mary Mix, and Rancor Bone," and the beverage is only sold during breakfast hours.<ref name="Disneyland.com">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/breakfast|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Breakfast Menu|oldversion=20240621041405}}</ref> On [[November 18]], [[2024]], the bone was swapped out for spicy candied [[Wikipedia:Bacon|bacon]] at Disney's Hollywood Studios,<ref name="BlogMickey">{{WebCite|url=https://blogmickey.com/ogas-cantina-review-2024-new-menu-drinks-food|text=Oga’s Cantina Review: November 2024 Menu Refresh Brings New Drinks & Food|date=2024-11-19|work=[https://blogmickey.com/ BlogMickey]|archivedate=20241209190557}}</ref> while the drink was retired completely from the Disneyland version's menu sometime after [[June 21]], 2024.<ref name="Disneyland.com" />
