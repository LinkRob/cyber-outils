Late in the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[cruiser]] ''[[Obrexta III]]'' was '''attacked''' in a [[sector]] of [[the galaxy]] by [[Galactic Republic]] forces en route to deliver [[clone trooper]] [[Kix|CT-6116 "Kix"]], who had been captured on [[Coruscant]] by Separatist forces, to Confederate leader [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[Castle Serenno|palace]] on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]]. [[B1-CC14]], a [[battle droid]] aboard the ''Obrexta III'', input random [[hyperspace]] coordinates into the [[Starship|ship]] to escape the Republic forces, resulting in the ship crash-landing into the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Ponemah Terminal|Ponemah]], where it sat undisturbed for the next several [[Standard decade|decades]].

==Prelude==
===Fives sets the stage===
During the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]], [[clone trooper]] [[Tup]] of the [[501st Legion]] abruptly shot and [[Death|killed]] [[Jedi General]] [[Tiplar]] in the middle of battle, prompting a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] retreat.<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref> The cause was due to a malfunction in Tup's [[behavioral modification biochip]], which had been implanted in all of the clones to ensure their compliance with carrying out [[Order 66]] and wiping out the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Conspiracy">{{TCW|Conspiracy}}</ref> The incident was cause for concern for [[Sith Lord]]s [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and [[Darth Sidious]], as this could lead the Jedi to discover their plans.<ref name="The Unknown" /> [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]], Tup's best friend in the 501st, decided to personally investigate what had happened to his friend, and discovered the existence of the chips and that they were in all of the clones.<ref name="Fugitive">{{TCW|Fugitive}}</ref>

However, when Fives was allowed to plead his case to [[Supreme Chancellor]] Sheev Palpatine&mdash;who was in actuality Sidious&mdash;he was framed for attacking the Chancellor and made a fugitive, eventually being shot and killed by the [[Coruscant Guard]] while attempting to tell his commanding officers [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] about the chips. Due to the effects of a drug Fives had secretly been administered which made him appear insane, Skywalker and Rex disbelieved his claims,<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref> although Rex made sure to submit a grievance report cataloging what Fives had told him about the chips in case there was something wrong.<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref>

===Kix investigates===
[[File:Kix_and_fives.png|thumb|left|300px|ARC trooper Fives (right) informed clone medic and fellow 501st member Kix (left) about a conspiracy he had uncovered, one which Kix would continue the investigation of after Fives' death.]]
During the events involving the near-exposure of Order 66, clone [[medic]] [[Kix]], another trooper of the 501st, fought at [[Ringo Vinda]], performing the initial examination of Tup after his mysterious breakdown.<ref name="The Unknown" /> Later, after the legion had returned to [[Coruscant]], Kix and some of his comrades went to the clone bar [[79's]], where Kix was confronted by the renegade Fives in the [[refresher]] and given the coordinates where Fives wanted to meet with Skywalker and Rex.<ref name="Orders" /> Kix became suspicious about the circumstances of Fives' and Tup's deaths, which had been publicly attributed to a [[parasite]] the two had picked up on Ringo Vinda which had made their "inhibitor" chips, supposedly there to keep the clones mentally stable, break down.<ref name="Orders" /><ref name="The Crimson Corsair" />

In the aftermath of Fives's death, Kix decided to do his own investigating about what had happened. However, like Fives before him, Kix drew the attention of the [[Sith]]. Kix found out more about the control chips and Order 66 than Fives had, including that Palpatine was not only behind the conspiracy but was himself a Sith Lord. Before he could tell anyone else what he knew, Kix was hunted down and captured by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] forces on the orders of Dooku<ref name="The Crimson Corsair" /> at some point after the [[Battle of Anaxes]]<ref name="Chronology Note" /> of the [[Outer Rim Sieges]].<ref name="A Distant Echo">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref> Not believing Kix's claims that he had not told anyone else about his discoveries, the clone trooper was placed in a [[cryo-cycle stasis pod]] on Dooku's orders for transport to [[Serenno]] to be interrogated personally by the Count. Kix's pod was shipped aboard the [[cruiser]] ''[[Obrexta III]]''.<ref name="The Crimson Corsair" />

==The attack==
On the way to Serenno, the ''Obrexta III'' was in an unidentified [[sector]] of [[the galaxy]] when it was attacked by Republic forces, including multiple [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT gunships]]. With orders to protect its cargo, who was of great value to the Sith's plans, [[battle droid]] [[B1-CC14]] input random [[hyperspace]] coordinates into the computers of the damaged [[starship]], sending the ''Obrexta III'' hurtling to an unknown region of space.<ref name="The Crimson Corsair" />

==Aftermath==
The ''Obrexta III'' ended up at the remote [[planet]] [[Ponemah Terminal|Ponemah]], in the [[Western Reaches]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. It subsequently crashed into the planet's dangerous [[Sea of Sand|southern polar region]], full of ionic dust storms and giant [[sandworm]]s. There it lay for over fifty [[Standard year|years]], during which Kix's stasis pod continued to keep him alive.<ref name="The Crimson Corsair" />

During that [[time]], the ''Obrexta III'' acquired a reputation as having apparently carried a legendarily valuable lost treasure, as Dooku had been known as the richest man in the galaxy during his lifetime, although no one knew the ship's cargo at the time of its loss had been valuable primarily for what Kix knew and could reveal about the Sith's plans. Eventually, during the [[New Republic era]], an ionic storm reactivated the cruiser's [[distress beacon]], where it was picked up by numerous bands of [[pirate]]s on the planet. A [[Hunt for Dooku's Treasure|race]] ensued between the various bands to try and gain the treasure.<ref name="The Crimson Corsair" />

When [[Delphidian]] pirate leader [[Sidon Ithano]] and his [[Sidon Ithano's pirate crew|crew]] made it to the treasure, they were surprised that it was a person instead of valuables. As the ship was in danger of being destroyed, Ithano had the rest of his crew, as well as the unconscious Kix, leave in an [[escape pod]], despite there seeming to be no means of escape. Several weeks later, Ithano walked into the bar his crew frequented with the ''Obrexta III''{{'s}} memory core, which contained the locations of some hidden Separatist bases that still had not been plundered. Kix joined Ithano's crew as he had nowhere else to go.<ref name="The Crimson Corsair" />
