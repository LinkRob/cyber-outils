'''Brakkenback stew''' was a type of thick [[stew]] served at [[Dex's Diner]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. While holding several patrons of the diner hostage in [[22 BBY]], the [[Weequay]] [[Jarl]] demanded [[Dexter Jettster]], the [[Besalisk]] proprietor of the diner, serve him a bowl of the stew. Jettster obliged before attacking the Weequay and throwing a pan of the stew at the [[protocol droid]] [[T7]].

==Description==
{{Quote|I tell you what. I'm not an unreasonable man. How about you bring me a bowl of that fine smelling stew, while Servit here considers his options.''"<br />"''One bowl of brakkenback stew coming up.|Jarl and Dexter Jettster|<ref name="Inheritance" />}}
Brakkenback stew was a type of thick [[stew]] that was edible to [[Besalisk]]s, [[Mirialan]]s, and [[Weequay]].<ref name="Inheritance" />

==History==
{{Quote|Come on. We'd better get Tee-Seven down to the workshop before his circuits are gummed up for good by that stinking stew.|Jarl|<ref name="Inheritance" />}}
[[File:Dexs_Diner.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Brakkenback stew was served at Dex's Diner (pictured).]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="InheritanceDate">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-author-roundtable|text=The High Republic Author Roundtable: "The End of the Beginning"}} establishes the events of "[[Galactic Tales: Inheritance]]" take place during the events of {{Film|II}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> brakkenback stew was served at [[Dex's Diner]]<ref name="Inheritance" /> on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Coruscant" /> When the Mirialan [[Servit]] visited the [[Restaurant|diner]] to meet with [[Dexter Jettster]], the Besalisk proprietor, Jettster asked the Mirialan if he wanted something to eat for lunch, informing him that that [[Standard day|day's]] batch of brakkenback stew was particularly good. Admitting that he wished he could sit down for lunch, Servit instead asked Jettster to speak with him privately behind the diner.<ref name="Inheritance" />

Their discussion was interrupted by the Weequay criminal [[Jarl]] and his [[Jarl's crew|crew]], who had taken several patrons of the diner hostage in order to force Servit to hand over an [[EX communication droid|EX droid]] in his possession that was carrying valuable information. When Sevrit and Jettster confronted the criminals, Jarl demanded a serving of the brakkenback stew, as he thought it smelled good. The Besalisk then ladled a portion of stew from the large cooking pan he had been preparing it in into a bowl, which he placed before the Weequay. When Jarl then put down his [[blaster]] and reached for a spoon in order to eat the stew, Jettster hit the criminal around the back of the head with a [[frying pan]], causing him to slump forward and tip the stew all over his lap.<ref name="Inheritance" />

[[File:Dex_in_the_kitchen.png|thumb|left|300px|Dexter Jettster (pictured) threw a batch of brakkenback stew on the droid T7.]]
The Besalisk then threw the rest of the pan of stew at [[T7]], a [[protocol droid]] working with Jarl, causing the [[droid]] to curse as the thick [[sauce]] seeped into its inner workings. Jarl soon recovered, but after discovering that the information held by the droid was in fact worthless, the Weequay left with T7 to ensure that the stew did not permanently damage the droid. Servit then asked for a [[dewback burger]] for lunch, assuming that no stew was left.<ref name="Inheritance" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brakkenback stew appeared in "[[Galactic Tales: Inheritance]]," an installment of the ''[[Galactic Tales]]'' series of short stories. The story was written by [[George Mann]] and published in the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine's [[Star Wars Insider 211|211th issue]]<ref name="Inheritance" /> on [[June 7]], [[2022]].<ref name="Date">{{TitanComics|url=m/102-star-wars-insider-211|text=Star Wars Insider #211}}</ref>
