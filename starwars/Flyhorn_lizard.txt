'''Flyhorn lizards''', also simply known as '''flyhorns''', were a type of [[lizard]] found on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], where they lived solitary lives out in the world's [[desert]]s. While staying cool in the sand, the creatures would use their horns and wide fan-like [[Limb|tails]] to collect [[Water|condensation]] and catch smaller [[insect]]s to [[Diet|eat]]. Some [[moisture farmer]]s habitually estimated their yields by observing flyhorn lizards near their [[Moisture farm|farms]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flyhorn lizards were created for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which released on [[August 30]], [[2024]]. Prior to the game's release, the creatures were revealed in a gameplay preview released on [[June 10]], 2024.<ref name="Gameplay">{{UbisoftYouTube|490LVhVGAqo|Star Wars Outlaws Official Gameplay: All the Star Wars Lore, Details & Easter Eggs &#124; Ubisoft Forward}}</ref>
