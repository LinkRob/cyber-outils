'''Cactus flowers''' were a [[plant]] found on the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]] that were [[Diet|edible]] by [[Quarren]]. [[Candied cactus flower]] was a rare and expensive delicacy on the world.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The appearance of [[cactus]] [[flower]]s in ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' take place in between the [[Battle of Jedha]] and the [[Night of Sorrow]], both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> [[Galactic Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Orlen Mollo]] ate lush, candied cactus flower while in the E'roni [[capital]] [[city]] of [[The Rook]] to discuss the planet's [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|conflict]] with its neighboring world of [[Eiram]].<ref name="Cataclysm" />
