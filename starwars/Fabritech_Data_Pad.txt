The '''Fabritech Data Pad''' was a model of [[datapad]] manufactured by [[Fabritech]]. [[ArmaTek]] also manufactured [[ArmaTek Data Pad|a datapad]] that was visually identical.<ref name="GoH" />

{{Tech-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Fabritech Data Pad first appeared in [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'', developed by [[EA Capital Games]]. In the game they are used as "Gear" to upgrade characters. There exist ten tiers of the Fabritech Data Pad, designated Mk 1 to Mk 9, and an additional tier called "Mk 12 ArmaTek Data Pad", as well as "Salvage", "Prototype" and "Component" versions.<ref name="GoH" /> Like the other gear items, it was modeled by [[Brittany Hermann]],<ref name="HermannArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=naastika|name=[[Brittany Hermann]]|url=artwork/L3BvNv|text=Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes - Gear Icons|date=2021-01-13}}</ref> with additional help by<ref name="HermannForums1">{{EA|subdomain=forums|url=discussions/swgoh-strategy-and-tips-en/everything-you-never-needed-to-know-about-gear/4039897/replies/4039908|text=Reply|author=[[Brittany Hermann]]|date=2017-03-07}}</ref> [[Paul Martin]]<ref name="Martin1">{{ArtStation|username=morepaul|name=[[Paul Martin]]|url=artwork/8wwOxx|text=Star Wars Galaxy of Heroes - Gear Icons|date=2021-12-06}}</ref> and another artist.<ref name="HermannForums1" /> Hermann said that she used the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[Versafunction88 datapad]] as a reference.<ref name="HermannForums1" />
