'''1978 Topps ''Star Wars'' Series 5''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]]<ref name="Volume One" /> in [[1978]].<ref name="Copyright" />

The set was based on [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], and featured stills from the film, promotional photos and behind the scenes photos, all within an orange border. Backs of the cards featured a "Movie Facts" theme or included a portion of a larger image that could be pieced together with other cards like a puzzle. A sub-set of sticker cards was included.<ref name="Volume One" />

Card numbering in the set continued from the [[1978 Topps Star Wars Series 4|previous series]].<ref name="Volume One" />
