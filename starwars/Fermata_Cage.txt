The '''Fermata Cage''' was a weapon of the [[dark side of the Force]] that was ancient in the [[Imperial Era]]. Designed by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Momin]], it could use the dark side to freeze individual moments and suspend people and places in time. The individual or place could then be recalled to the present as if no time had passed.

==Description==
The Fermata Cage was a pre-[[Ascendant]] artifact<ref name="Hidden Empire 2" /> designed by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Momin]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> It was considered ancient by the [[Imperial Era]]. The cage resembled an hourglass, with its mechanism containing miniature [[black hole]]s held in place by a [[Dark side of the Force|dark-side]] matrix surrounding the [[glass]] surface of the cylinder. This gave it the ability to warp and trap [[time]],<ref name="Hidden Empire 2" /> freezing individual people and places inside<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" /> which could then be recalled to the present by use of concentrated dark-side energy applied directly to it, as if no time had passed.<ref name="Hidden Empire 2" /> To open, the Cage required an immense amount of dark-side energy, either applied directly by a powerful [[The Force|Force]] user,<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" /> or, in their absence, collected by the life energy of [[Species|organic]] lifeforms in its immediate vicinity.<ref name="Hidden Empire 2" />

==History==
===Pre-Empire===
The Fermata Cage was designed and constructed long before the Imperial Era by the dark-side artist<ref name="Hidden Empire 2" /> Darth Momin.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> The Cage was never used lightly. It was used to lock away a disc-like<ref name="Hidden Empire 5">[[Hidden Empire 5|''Hidden Empire'' 5]]</ref> [[Unidentified droid (Fermata Cage)|droid being]].<ref name="Dark Droids 1">[[Dark Droids 1|''Dark Droids'' 1]]</ref> The Cage was eventually stored inside a temple in a [[dark side hellscape]]<ref name="Crimson Reign 5" /> Momin knew of,<ref name="Darth Vader 22">[[Darth Vader (2017) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 22]]</ref> leaving the Cage lost to [[The galaxy|the greater galaxy]].<ref name="Crimson Reign 5" />

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|Qi'ra's planning to lure in Vader and the Emperor with rumors of this false Sith&mdash;so she can trap them with the Fermata Cage. If that fails? With the Spark Eternal's help, she'll kill whoever's left standing.|Kho Phon Farrus on Qi'ra's plans for the Fermata Cage|<ref name="Aphra 29">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 29|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 29]]</ref>}}
Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date 2" /> [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]], the [[Crime lord|leader]] of [[Crimson Dawn]], [[War against the Sith|plotted]] to destroy the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lords]] [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] by having them trapped inside the Fermata Cage.<ref name="Aphra 29" /> In order to achieve this, she had the Cage [[Mission to a dark side hellscape|recovered]] by "[[the Archivist]]" and the [[Knights of Ren]], who had also [[Heist on Vader's Castle|stolen]] the [[Screaming Key]] from [[Fortress Vader]].<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" /> To lure Vader and Sidious into a confrontation and then trap them in the Cage, Qi'ra created a rumor that a [[Unidentified Sith Lord (Fermata Cage)|Sith Lord]] had been trapped inside the Cage.<ref name="Aphra 29" />

The Archivist was then tasked with unlocking the Cage. The task proved to be extremely difficult, and the Archivist called in [[Kho Phon Farrus]], an [[archaeologist]] knowledgeable in the ways of the sect of the Ascendant to assist her. The duo tried a variety of methods to unlock the cage, to no avail. The result of their experiments was a complex machine which included at least four dissected [[humanoid]]s, a [[cyborg]] and two [[GNK-series power droid]]s as part of its mechanism, with the Fermata Cage on its core.<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" />

===War against the Crimson Dawn===
{{Quote|Qi'ra believes she can use this ''ancient Sith artifact'', the ''Fermata Cage'', to destroy Palpatine. It's one of the ''most wizard'' things I've ever seen, and I'm pretty sure it's going to kill us all. You'd ''love'' it. When we turned it on, it caused this&hellip;''ripple'' in the Force. I couldn't feel it myself, but it spooked the ''Knights of Ren'' so badly they left. [&hellip;] I think the Fermata Cage holds the secret to ''harnessing'' the ''Spark Eternal''. I just need to figure it out and ''not die'' in the process.|Kho Phon Farrus contacts Detta Yao|<ref name="Aphra 27" />}}
During a [[battle against Crimson Dawn]], Qi'ra told Sidious her<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" /> rumor<ref name="Aphra 29" /> about the Sith Lord being trapped in the Feramata Cage, and her intent to release it to hunt Sidious and his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] Vader. Qi'ra had Farrus and the Archivist activate their machine, which they accomplished by strapping the [[Knights of Ren]] to and having them use their dark-side [[Force power|powers]] to their fullest extent. With the machine at full power, the Knights were lifted to the air by their necks, and the Cage's containment unit exploded, creating a massive [[disturbance in the Force]] which was felt across [[the galaxy]] all the way to the [[Imperial Palace]] on [[Coruscant]] and [[Fortress Vader]] on [[Mustafar]]. Its power was so great that it caused Palpatine to jump from his [[throne]] in fear, demanding to know Qi'ra's secret. Nevertheless, the disturbance was temporary, and with the Archivist and Farrus' machine destroyed the Cage was once again inert.<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" />

Despite the success in identifying the Cage's dormant potential, the Knights of Ren abandoned the Dawn, frustrated by the Archivist and Farrus' using them as test subjects<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" /> and scared of the ripple in the Force the test had set out.<ref name="Aphra 27" /> Despite the setback in losing their Force-sensitive test subjects, the duo confirmed that Farrus' Ascendant tech replicated the Knights' Force power and acted as a viable replacement to prime the Cage.<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" /> Following the Knights' departure, while setting up a new test, Farrus took a break to contact [[Detta Yao]] after their frustrations and fears about the project and Palpatine's inevitable response began to boil over. Contacting Yao, Kho revealed their theory that the Cage was the key to unlocking the Ascendant's artificial intelligence known as the [[Spark Eternal]] before returning to work.<ref name="Aphra 27">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 27|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 27]]</ref>

As Palpatine pinpointed where they had conducted their experiment, they packed their gear and left for another location, where they built yet another machine with the Cage at its center. This one was designed to collect dark-side energy from its surroundings, as it, like the [[Light side of the Force|light side]], existed in abundance in a natural state, and then focus it on the Cage like a lens. After installing it, Farrus activated it, but they, like the Knights, were swapped in the Vortex, their life powers sucked in by the Cage through the device. At the same time, [[Yoda]], [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Darth]] Vader, and Palpatine sensed the disturbance caused by the priming from different sides of the galaxy, with Palpatine also pinpointing Farrus and the Archivist through the dark-side energy unleashed there.<ref name="Hidden Empire 2" />

Farrus and the Archivist were forced to disable the machine before it could collect enough energy to unlock the Cage, as they later reported to a distressed Qi'ra, who prompted them to try again. Just as the Archivist objected, warning Qi'ra of the danger the Cage posed, Vader arrived in orbit aboard his [[flagship]], the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]''. Upon reaching the surface aboard a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]], Vader found the exhausted Archivist hiding with Farrus underneath parts of their device, having barely had any time to even disconnect the Cage from it.<ref name="Hidden Empire 2" /> However, [[Chanath Cha]]'s [[Orphans]] intervened, engaging Vader and buying the Archivist and Farrus the time they needed to escape with the Cage, perishing in the process.<ref name="Hidden Empire 4">[[Hidden Empire 4|''Hidden Empire'' 4]]</ref>

===Final chance===
With yet another test failed, the Archivist, abandoned by a terrified Farrus, decided that the best course of action would be to take the Cage to a place that would be both full of dark side energy and rich in life. She concluded that the [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine space station]] would be the best candidate, being rich in [[Drengir]] plants that could channel the dark side and would make for the perfect "nourishment" for the cage. After she arrived and set up her equipment, she activated the matrix, resulting in the cage immediately consuming most of the plants in its vicinity and beginning to open. [[Galactic Emperor]] Sidious immediately sensed it and arrived to crush her once and for all aboard the Executor along with Darth Vader. This time, however, they were met with Qi'ra's full Crimson fleet who disabled their long range [[turbolaser]]s, forcing them to land to the planet themselves accompanied by [[Emperor's Royal Guard|royal guards]] and [[death trooper]]s, slaughtering the Dawn fighters and making their way towards the Cage.<ref name="Hidden Empire 4" />

Farrus feared Qi'ra's latest plan would end in disaster. Aboard the [[space station]], Qi'ra met with the Spark Eternal&ndash;possessed [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] near the Cage<ref name="Aphra 28">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 28|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 28]]</ref> before she rejoined her fleet. After Vader defeated the Spark and removed it from Aphra's body through a [[Force push]],<ref name="Hidden Empire 5" /> with Farrus leading Aphra's allies to the station to free her from the Spark's attempts to bond with her once more,<ref name="Aphra (2020) 30">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 30|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 30]]</ref> Vader and Sidious faced down the Cage as the Archivist remotely opened it. The small disc-like droid fell from the Cage,<ref name="Hidden Empire 5" /> marking it as finally being released.<ref name="Dark Droids 1" /> Unaware of its true nature, the disc only confirmed to Sidious Qi'ra had been lying about the supposed Sith Lord since he felt no connection between the object and the Force. With both Sith in its range, Qi'ra ordered the Archivist to seal the Sith within the Cage so they could throw them into a sun.<ref name="Hidden Empire 5" />

However, the Fermata Cage was destroyed, which resulted in a [[Force Wave]] being sent out across the galaxy,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 32">[[Bounty Hunters 32|''Bounty Hunters'' 32]]</ref> when the Knights of Ren arrived in the battle and fired on it. Believing Sidious would escape whatever became of him and hoping that, by saving the Sith, they would save themselves from their wrath, the Knights' intervention spared both Sith Lords from being trapped in the Cage, leaving Crimson Dawn's efforts to crumble around them as the Empire overwhelmed their fleet. Vader collected<ref name="Hidden Empire 5">[[Hidden Empire 5|''Hidden Empire'' 5]]</ref> the destroyed<ref name="Bounty Hunters 32" /> but still intact Cage and departed with Sidious, who tortured the Knights with [[Force lightning]] but promised they would be put to use. The Spark Eternal, meanwhile, fled into the disc-droid dropped by the Cage<ref name="Hidden Empire 5" /> and merged with it, becoming the [[Scourge]].<ref name="Dark Droids 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fermata Cage was first indirectly mentioned in [[Crimson Reign 4|''Crimson Reign'' 4]].<ref name="Crimson Reign 4">[[Crimson Reign 4|''Crimson Reign'' 4]]</ref> It made its first appearance in [[Crimson Reign 5|''Crimson Reign'' 5]],<ref name="Crimson Reign 5" /> and was later identified in [[Hidden Empire 1|''Hidden Empire'' 1]].<ref name="Hidden Empire 1" />

In the real world, a [[Wikipedia:Fermata|fermata]] is a musical direction to hold a note longer than its usual length, and a [[Wikipedia:Faraday cage|Faraday cage]] is an enclosure to shield objects from electromagnetic fields.
