"'''Drone Alone'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story by [[Christopher Cooper]] featured in the [[Star Wars Comic 7.10|tenth issue]] of [[Star Wars Comic|''Star Wars'' Comic]] magazine, released in [[September]] of [[2014]]. The story, set shortly after [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], features [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], [[Wedge Antilles/Legends|Wedge Antilles]], [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] and [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando Calrissian]] on a [[Mission to steal hyperdrive components|mission]] to steal [[Hyperdrive/Legends|hyperdrive]] components from the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]].

==Plot summary==
Not long after the [[Occupation of Bespin]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel spies]] uncovered a [[Convoy/Legends|convoy]] of [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[drone freighter]]s carrying [[Hyperdrive/Legends|hyperdrive]] components. [[Commander/Legends|Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], [[Wedge Antilles/Legends|Wedge Antilles]], [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]], and [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando Calrissian]], [[Mission to steal hyperdrive components|operating]] as [[Raptor Squad]], took an [[Nu-class attack shuttle/Legends|assault ship]] to intercept. Antilles took them out of [[Hyperspace/Legends|lightspeed]] right on top of one of them. They were able to board without triggering the escorts.

Once aboard, they split up. However, the Imperials picked up their ship near [[Drone vessel 171]], and realize that their trap had been tripped. The [[Unidentified Imperial captain (escort frigate)|captain]] orders his crew to transmit the command signal.

The Imperials on the freighter get out of their cargo boxes and prepare to take the Rebels prisoner. Meanwhile, Calrissian was having trouble with the junction terminal, and Chewbacca suggests cutting through from the deck above.

When Calrissian goes to do so, he discovers the [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] and warns the squad. The troops order Skywalker to halt, and he has a brief moment of doubt.

On the other deck, Calrissian knocks out [[Traneel]] and steals his uniform. He orders a pair of stormtroopers to go to the docking bay. They protest that they have orders from the [[Imperial officer/Legends|officer]], but Calrissian threatens them.

Skywalker uses [[The Force/Legends|The Force]] to distract the troopers and knocks them out in unarmed combat.

He then met back up with Calrissian, who tells him he locked up the other troopers. Skywalker uses a [[Mind trick/Legends|mind trick]] on them and convinces them the ship was empty and they were in danger, and they abandoned ship in the [[Escape pod/Legends|escape pods]]. The Imperial Commander angrily orders their frigate's weapons to be charged.

As it closes in, Chewbacca finishes the sabotage, giving them manual control. They escape into lightspeed as the Imperial Commander orders a torpedo strike.
