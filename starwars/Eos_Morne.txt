'''Eos Morne''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] who partied on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. In [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]], they visited the [[Outlander Club/Legends|Outlander Club]] in Coruscant's [[Uscru Entertainment District/Legends|Uscru Entertainment District]] when [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell/Legends|Zam Wesell]] ran into the club to [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala/Legends|escape]] from the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]]. 

==Biography==
Eos Morne was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] participant in<ref name="Rogues Gallery" /> the [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant's]] party scene.<ref name="Rogues Gallery" /> During a [[Local day/Legends|night]] in<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}} to [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]].</ref> Morne visited the [[Outlander Club/Legends|Outlander Club]],<ref name="Episode II" /> a [[Nightclub/Legends|nightclub]] and [[Gambling/Legends|gambling]] hall in the [[Uscru Entertainment District/Legends|Uscru Entertainment District]] of Coruscant.<ref name="CSWE">{{CSWECite|II|431|Outlander Club}}</ref> The partier stood near the club's entrance with the fellow Human<ref name="Episode II" /> [[Kell Borean]]<ref name="Rogues Gallery" /> as the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] entered searching for the [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell/Legends|Zam Wesell]] after her [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala/Legends|assassination attempt]] on [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Episode II" /> 

Morne later moved to the club's bar, where they talked with another Human up until Kenobi sliced off Wesell's [[Limb/Legends|arm]] with his [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] when she attempted to ambush the Jedi. Skywalker told the clubgoers to go back to their drinks and the Jedi, with Wesell, left.<ref name="Episode II" />  

==Personality and traits==
The partygoer had [[Color/Legends|light]] skin, brown eyes, and black [[Hair/Legends|hair]] worn in an updo.<ref name="Rogues Gallery" />

==Equipment==
When they visited the Outlander Club, Morne wore a white [[Clothing/Legends|outfit]] with heeled light yellow [[Boot/Legends|boots]].<ref name="Rogues Gallery" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eos Morne first appeared an illustration in the [[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Movie Storybook|storybook]] for the then upcoming [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. The book was authored by [[Jane Mason]] and [[Sarah Hines Stephens]],<ref name="Storybook">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Movie Storybook]]''</ref> and released on [[April 23]], [[2002]],<ref name="Amazon">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/young/news20020225.html|text=Book Preview: Episode II Children's Books}}</ref> ahead of the film's [[May 16]], 2002 release date.<ref name="Year2">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> The Outlander Club scenes including Morne, portrayed by an uncredited extra,<ref name="Episode II" /> were shot at [[Fox Studios, Sydney]] on [[July 17]], [[2000]].<ref name="Mythmaking">''[[Mythmaking: Behind the Scenes of Attack of the Clones]]''</ref> The character was later revealed on [[August 29]], [[2000]]<ref name="Archive">{{SWArchive|url=episode-ii/bts/onloc/archive.html|text=''on location'' archive}}</ref> when the extra playing Morne, in costume, introduced "The AD Stare," the fifth episode of the Episode II behind-the-scenes video series [[On location|''on location'']].<ref name="On location">{{SWArchive|url=episode-ii/bts/onloc/5.html|series=On Location|text=Part 5 - The AD Stare}}</ref>
