'''Bina''' was a [[Gender|female]]<ref name="GEStaff" /> [[Amani]] from [[Utapau]] who ran the [[Creature Stall]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]], which she filled with [[Species|creatures]] that she collected from around [[the galaxy]] and brought to [[Batuu]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> She also wrote a [[Bina's Guide to the Creatures of Batuu|guidebook]] to Black Spire's creatures for [[youngling]]s to encourage them to explore the outpost and discover and track some of its resident creatures.<ref name="Bina's Guide">''[[Bina's Guide to the Creatures of Batuu]]''</ref>

==Biography==
While most Amani are warriors, Bina left her home planet to learn and explore [[the galaxy]], and after some time, she made Black Spire her homebase.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> Years before [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Bina gave a young [[Salju]] and her pet [[therii]], [[Elee]], advice on how to brush Elee's teeth and her preferred diet.<ref name="Elee" /> As a part of her exotic species collection and sale, she began breeding [[Kowakian monkey-lizard|Kowakian monkey-lizards]] to produce a variety of colors and patterns.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />According to local gossip that [[Vi Moradi]] heard, [[Mubo]] of the [[Droid Depot]] allegedly had a crush on Bina.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref>
[[File:Binas Guide.jpg|right|thumb|Bina's Guide|201x201px]]

During 34 ABY, Bina procured and attempted to sell a collection of [[Loralora bird]]s, but [[Izal Garsea]] and [[Julen Rakab]] accidentally set them free as a distraction.<ref name="A Crash of Fate">''[[A Crash of Fate]]''</ref> Moradi, a [[spy]] and recruiter for the [[Resistance]], did not consider Bina a viable candidate for the movement due to her entrepreneurial endeavors.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> While Bina may not support the Resistance, she does work to educate the children that come through the [[Black Spire Outpost Market]]. She wrote a small workbook called [[Bina's Guide to the Creatures of Batuu]] filled with pictures and details about the local fauna that call Batuu their home. The guide was available for all younglings who stopped by her storefront.<ref name="GEStaff" /> Bina was not present when [[Eloc Throno]] visited Black Spire Outpost as she had left for one of her personal adventures.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bina is one of many shopkeeper characters created for the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] theme park land.<ref name="27 Things">{{SW|url=news/27-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=27 Things We Learned About ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> In February 2020, the Creature Stall began offering a free scavenger-hunt activity book, ''Bina's Guide to the Creatures of Batuu'', for children ages 10 and under. After stamping the books at each location, the player would be rewarded with a special coaster token.<ref name="WDW">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2020/02/photos-kids-can-earn-special-token-by-completing-batuu-scavenger-hunt-at-disneyland-resort|text=PHOTOS: New "Bina's Guide to the Creatures of Batuu" Scavenger Hunt Available in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge at Disneyland|date=2020-02-03|work=[https://wdwnt.com WDW News Network]|archivedate=20201128094214}}</ref>
