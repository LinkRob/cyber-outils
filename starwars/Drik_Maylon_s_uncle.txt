The '''uncle''' of the [[Gender|boy]] [[Drik Maylon]] told him stories about actions that the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] had supposedly taken on [[Kupoh]],<ref name="Terror" /> a [[planet]] that was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Kupohan]] [[species]].<ref name="HttJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> Between [[1 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{TerrorOnMustafarDate}}</ref> Maylon and the rest of his [[Vardos Star Scouts]] troupe witnessed Vader arriving at their [[Unidentified barracks (Kestro City)|barracks]] in [[Kestro City]] on the planet [[Vardos]]. The boy whispered to his fellow [[scout]]s [[Zan Videx]] and [[Deeta Kardon]] that they should avoid drawing the [[Sith Lord]]'s attention based on the stories he had heard from his uncle.<ref name="Terror" />
