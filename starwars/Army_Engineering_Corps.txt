The '''Army Engineering Corps''' was an [[engineer]]ing [[corps]] that was part of the [[Imperial Army|ground forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], and as such, was responsible for building Imperial infrastructure and destroying enemy fortifications.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Army Engineering Corps was mentioned in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2018]].<ref name="Dawn" />
