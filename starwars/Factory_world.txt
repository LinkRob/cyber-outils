A '''factory world''' was a type of [[planet]]. The [[AJ-23 concussive rifle]] manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] often saw use on [[factory]] worlds with exploited work forces that regularly rioted.<ref name="Gadgets">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref> The planets [[Xorrn]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Friends">''[[Friends Like These]]''</ref> and [[Troithe]] in the [[Deep Core]] were factory worlds.<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

Many of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' factory worlds were located in a region of [[the galaxy]] known as the [[Foundry of the Confederacy]].<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>
