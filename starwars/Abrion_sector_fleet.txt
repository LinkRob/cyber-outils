A '''sector fleet''' of the [[Republic Navy]] was active in the [[Abrion sector]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. When the [[Rishi Station]] was destroyed during a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[Defense of Rishi Station|incursion]], an alert was sent to the [[sector fleet]], and a larger force was assembled to repel the attack,<ref name="Mustafar SWE" /> the larger force being the [[fleet]] under [[High Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]].<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref>
