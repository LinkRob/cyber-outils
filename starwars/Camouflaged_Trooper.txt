The '''Camouflaged Trooper''' was a set of armor worn by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Loyalist Trooper|standard costume]]. This unique [[armor]] consisted of a lightly armored appearance from the neck down, sporting a [[Snowtrooper armor|showtrooper chest plate]], as well as exposed pants around the thighs, all covered in a subtle [[Color|green]] and brown rural camouflaged pattern. A hood was seemingly worn over the entire face, featuring a white design reminiscent of [[General]] [[Grievous]]' face over the nose and mouth. On top of this hood included [[Imperial Army trooper]] [[Imperial Army trooper armor|goggles]], an [[Imperial military uniforms|Imperial officer cap]], and bulky headphones. According to Sentinel, this armor worked quite well for stealth operations.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Camouflaged Trooper was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Sentinel, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically within the 3.0.0 update alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Camouflaged E-Web]]. This costume could be unlocked as one of two grand prizes within the "Disguised Trooper Vault."<ref name="Hunters" />
