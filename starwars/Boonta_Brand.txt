'''Boonta Brand''' was a company that advertised and sold branded [[podracer]] [[fuel]]. One advertisement for the brand was collected by [[Poe Dameron]] in a [[Unidentified book|vehicle guidebook]]. It shared a name with<ref name="Vehicles" /> the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]],<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> and the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] astronomical object [[Boonta]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref>
