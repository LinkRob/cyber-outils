The '''Battle of Yavin''', also known as the '''Battle of the Death Star''' or the '''Miracle of Yavin''', was a major and decisive battle of the [[Galactic Civil War]], in which the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] was able to destroy the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] and was seen as one of the first major victories over the [[Galactic Empire]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|He put a fuse in the middle of your machine, and I've just told the entire galaxy how to light it.|Jyn Erso, to Imperial Director Krennic|<ref name="R1">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>}}
During the [[Battle of Scarif]], [[Jyn Erso]] and [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] managed to steal detailed [[Death Star plans|plans]] and design specifications for the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[battle station]] known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] and transmit them to the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] above; these plans soon made their way to [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="R1" /> Imperial forces under the command of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], soon captured Princess Leia and the crew of the ''[[Tantive IV]]''.<ref name="ANH" />

Princess Leia was [[Secret mission to Tatooine|taken]] [[prisoner]] aboard the [[Death]] Star by [[Lord]] Vader. The Princess was [[Rescue of Princess Leia|rescued]] from [[Detention Level|captivity]] by an unlikely alliance of the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]], former [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], a young farmboy from [[Tatooine]] named [[Luke Skywalker]], and Solo's [[First Mate]], the [[Wookiee]] named [[Chewbacca]]. The group, which also included [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[droid]]s [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]], in possession of the Death Star plans, fled to the [[Great Temple|Rebel base]] on [[Yavin 4]]&mdash;closely pursued by the battle [[Space station|station]].<ref name="ANH" />

Before Organa arrived, [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] was evacuated aboard a [[shuttle]], as [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna|Dodonna]] considered her the core of the rebellion. During her [[time]] away, she composed a contingency plan: surrender to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Contingency Plan">{{StoryCite|story=Contingency Plan|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

[[File:PlanofAttack-CM.png|thumb|left|250px|General Dodonna gives a briefing on the battle plan.]]
General Jan Dodonna briefed the [[rebel pilot]]s on the plan. The Death Star's defenses were designed for a direct, large-scale assault. By using small, one-man [[starfighter]]s, the Rebels would slip through the Death Star's main defenses, maneuver down a [[Meridian trench|narrow trench]] leading to the exhaust port, and fire [[proton torpedo]]es into the exhaust port. An analysis of the plans identified a weakness in the battle station,<ref name="ANH" /> placed there by Imperial [[scientist]] [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]]:<ref name="R1" /> a small [[thermal exhaust port]] leading to the main [[reactor]]. A [[proton torpedo]] fired into the port would cause a chain reaction that would destroy the battle station.<ref name="ANH" />

==The battle==
===The approach===
{{Quote|Lock S-foils in attack position.|Red Leader Garven Dreis|<ref name="ANH" />}}
With the Death Star approaching, [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wings]] from [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] and [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] from [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]] and [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]]<ref name="Sparks" /> were dispatched to carry out the attack plan. A total of 30 [[Starship|ships]] were deployed in the assault on the Death Star: 8 [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|Y-wings]] from Gold [[Squadron]] as well as 22 [[X-wing starfighter|X-wings]], 12 from [[Color|Red]] Squadron and the other 10 from Green Squadron.<ref name="Grounded" /> The starfighters weaved through the [[turret]] emplacements, destroying many as they passed. [[Red Six]] ([[Jek Tono Porkins]]) was the first to be destroyed in the volley of turret fire. The deployment of Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], including one piloted by [[Lieutenant]] [[Iden Versio]],<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> devastated the Rebel attackers, destroying them far faster and more efficiently than the station's weapon emplacements had been able to. [[Red Four]] ([[John D. Branon]]) was the first to be shot down by the incoming [[TIE series|TIEs]].<ref name="ANH" />

[[File:Battle_of_Yavin.png|thumb|right|270px|Rebel X-wings and Y-wings approach the Death Star.]]
During the battle, Rebels in the [[Great Temple|temple]], including pilots who were left behind watched the battle from the command center,<ref name="Duty Roster" /> along with Fighter [[Boss]] [[Nera Kase]]. She tallied the Rebel starfighter losses as they occurred during the battle.<ref name="Grounded" />

Some time into the battle, [[Imperial officer]] [[Jude Edivon]] aboard the Death Star identified the threat of the Rebels' attack strategy, and attempted to warn her superiors of the danger. However, [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] ignored the warning and refused to evacuate.<ref name="LS" /> Informed by [[Lieutenant]] [[Tanbris]]<ref name="Spacecraft Origami">''[[Star Wars Origami: 11 Amazing Paper Folding Projects from a Galaxy Far, Far Away.... Spacecraft]]''</ref> that the Rebels were avoiding the turbolaser fire, Vader himself decided to deploy a [[Black Squadron (Galactic Empire)|squadron]] of TIE fighters to destroy the remaining Rebel fighters.<ref name="ANH" /> This would be the only Imperial squadron launched during the battle.<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> Vader himself would join the battle, entering the fray aboard his personal [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|TIE Advanced]]<ref name="ANH" /> as [[Black Leader]].<ref name="AdvancedVader">{{FFGXW2|set=TIE Advanced x1 Expansion Pack|cardname=Darth Vader}}</ref> He was joined by two other members of Black Squadron, the TIE pilot<ref name="ANH" /> aces<ref name="TIE Fighter">''[[TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> [["Backstabber"]]<ref name="BackstabberFFG">{{FFGXW2|set=Battle of Yavin Scenario Pack|cardname="Backstabber"}}</ref> and [[Black Two]] [[Mithel]],<ref name="MaulerXWing">{{FFGXW2|set=TIE/ln Fighter Expansion Pack|cardname="Mauler" Mithel}}</ref> as his hand-picked<ref name="TIE Fighter" /> wingmen.<ref name="ANH" />

===Trench run===
{{Quote|We've analyzed their attack, sir, and there is a danger. Should I have your ship standing by?''"<br />"Evacuate? ''In our moment of ''triumph?'' I think you overestimate their chances!|Moradmin Bast and Wilhuff Tarkin|audio=BastTarkin.flac|<ref name="ANH" />}}
[[File:XX-9TurbolaserTurretFires.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Turbolasers defend the Death Star during the Battle of Yavin]]
The first attack run was performed by a trio of Y-wings from Gold Squadron. The three Y-wings, led by [[Gold Leader]] ([[Jon Vander]]) with [[Gold Two]] ([[Dex Tiree]]) and [[Gold Five]] ([[Davish Krail]]) escorting, attempted to make a shot on the exhaust port but were shot down by Vader. A second trench run was performed by a trio of X-wings from Red Squadron flown by [[Red Leader]] ([[Garven Dreis]]), [[Red Ten]] ([[Theron Nett]]), and [[Red Twelve]] ([[Puck Naeco]]). [[Darth]] Vader picked off Nett and Naeco, but Dreis was able to fire off a shot at the exhaust port. However, his proton torpedoes missed their mark, only scratching the surface. Before being shot down by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]], Dreis ordered another attack run.<ref name="ANH" />

Following the final command of Red Leader, [[Red Five]] (Luke Skywalker) led the final attempt with [[Red Two]] ([[Wedge Antilles]]) and [[Red Three]], Luke's old friend, ([[Biggs Darklighter]]), as [[Wingmate|wingmen]]. Rushing down the trench at full throttle, they found themselves being tailed by Vader and his fighters. [["Backstabber"|One]] of Vader's wingmen damaged Antilles' fighter, forcing him to disengage, whilst Vader personally destroyed Darklighter's X-wing, forcing Skywalker to attempt the shot on his own. In this dire moment the Rebel leaders were concerned when the young [[pilot]] deactivated his [[Targeting computer|targeting system]] for the attempt, but Skywalker assured them that nothing was wrong; he was advised by the [[Force spirit|spirit]] of Kenobi to rely on [[the Force]] instead for his attack.<ref name="ANH" />

[[File:JasonRaish-StarWarsTrenchRun.png|thumb|right|300px|Luke Skywalker flies as Red Five down the trench while pursued by Darth Vader in his TIE Advanced.]]
For his part, Vader sensed this power himself and found firing on the fighter unusually difficult, with his single hit only putting R2-D2, who was serving as the fighter's [[astromech droid]], out of commission. When the Dark Lord prepared to finally shoot down the enemy, his wingman Backstabber was suddenly shot down from above beside him. The source of that attack was the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' diving out of the nearby star's light. Han Solo and Chewbacca, believed to have abandoned the Rebels prior to the assault, unexpectedly returned to assist Skywalker in his most desperate moment.<ref name="ANH" /> Chewbacca had been urging Solo to return, with Solo's conscience finally getting the better of him when he decided he needed to help Skywalker, whom he had befriended.<ref name="Solo Journey">{{InsiderCite|158|Solo Journey}}</ref>

===Destruction of the Death Star===
{{Quote|Great shot, kid! That was one in a million!|Han Solo to Luke Skywalker|<ref name="ANH" />}}
Momentarily distracted by the intervention, the Dark Lord's remaining wingman [[Mithel]] collided with his ship, and both spiraled out of control. The [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] flew into the side of the trench and knocked Vader away from the Death Star, and this allowed Skywalker to fire his shot. With the aid of the Force, the torpedoes shot along the trench and entered the shaft perfectly, as the two surviving X-wings, the lone surviving Y-wing and the aging [[freighter]] made for Yavin 4. The surviving rebel pilots included Skywalker, Antilles,<ref name="ANH" /> and [[Gold Three]] ([[Evaan Verlaine]]).<ref name="Leia 1" />

[[File:Exds.png|thumb|left|250px|The destruction of the First Death Star]]
On the [[Overbridge (Death Star I)|Overbridge]], unaware of his impending doom, [[Governor]] Tarkin continued to examine the battle as the [[technician]]s counted down the moments until firing commenced. Mere seconds before the [[Mk I Superlaser|superlaser]] could obliterate the moon, the torpedoes struck the reactor and the station exploded, killing all aboard and saving Yavin 4.<ref name="ANH" /> The explosion of the Death Star could be seen from the ground of [[Yavin 8]], lighting up the entire sky and leaving debris within Yavin 8's [[atmosphere]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> 

Shortly before the explosion, while on the Death Star, [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Ellian Zahra]] punched a [[stormtrooper]] and stole a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]], with which she planned to continue her hunt for the [[warlord]] [[Burnium Ro]]. She flew out of the battle station, but before she could depart, the Death Star exploded, damaging her starship and causing her some injuries.<ref name="SW7">[[Star Wars (2020) 7|''Star Wars'' (2020) 7]]</ref> After the destruction of the battle station, the ''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer known as [[Tarkin's Will|''Fortitude'']] was hit by a piece of Death Star wreckage that heavily damaged its [[hull]].<ref name="Star Wars 3">[[Star Wars (2020) 3|''Star Wars'' (2020) 3]]</ref>

==Aftermath==
===Rebel Alliance===
{{Quote|Thanks to your courage, we have delivered a telling blow to the Empire with the destruction of their Death Star.|Leia Organa, to the assembled Alliance members|<ref name="Leia 1" />}}
[[File:Rebels_Heroes_AaA.png|thumb|right|250px|Skywalker returned to a hero's welcome.]]
Skywalker, Solo, and the surviving Rebel fighters returned to Yavin, where they held a [[Royal Award Ceremony|celebration ceremony]] and awarded the pair with [[Medal of Bravery|medals]] for their achievement in saving the Rebellion.<ref name="ANH" /> Mothma, having heard of the victory, permanently discarded her contingency plan to surrender to Palpatine.<ref name="Contingency Plan" /> Antilles' move with which he saved Skywalker's life during the battle would be remembered as the [[Antilles Maneuver]].<ref name="RRaRS">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic, Rebel, and Resistance Starfighters}}</ref>

Shortly after the battle, the Rebels were forced to [[Evacuation of Yavin 4|leave]] the site of their victory, fleeing the Empire once again.<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> According to the [[Wild Space]] [[cartographer]] [[Emil Graf]], in the [[Standard day|days]] after the battle, the rebels' evacuation was briefly interrupted by an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] which threatened to destroy the base before the evacuation could be completed. The rebels managed to disable the crew of the [[Star Destroyer]], however, they soon discovered that its weapons were primed to fire at the base within minutes. Thanks to Verlaine's efforts, the ship misfired at its intended target, with the evacuation being resumed. Some time later, the Imperials regained control of the [[Destroyer]].<ref name="FMOY">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=2|story=Tales from Wild Space: The Flat Mountain of Yavin}}</ref>

To capitalize on the Empire's weakness following the destruction of the Death Star, the Rebels began to conduct dozens of raids against Imperial targets to weaken the now-vulnerable Empire. In addition to [[Bombing of the Kuat shipyards|attacks]] [[Bombing of the Imdaar supply base|carried]] out on the [[Kuat shipyards|shipyard]]s of [[Kuat]] and a [[Imdaar supply base|supply base]] on [[Imdaar]], a Rebel team composed of Leia Organa, Han Solo and Luke Skywalker [[Assault on Cymoon|assaulted and destroyed]] the Empire's [[Weapons Factory Alpha|largest armament factory]] on [[Cymoon 1]], severely undermining Imperial military production.<ref name="Star Wars 4">[[Star Wars (2015) 4|''Star Wars'' (2015) 4]]</ref>

===Galactic Empire===
{{Quote|Countless loyal Imperial subjects died that day. ''People''. Murdered by ''Leia Organa's'' traitorous insurgency.|Commander Zahra, on the Battle of Yavin|<ref name="Star Wars 3" />}}
[[File:FortitudeDuringYavin-2020StarWars3.jpg|thumb|left|270px|The ''Fortitude'' was heavily damaged during the Battle of Yavin.]]
The Star Destroyer [[Tarkin's Will|''Fortitude'']] was caught in the ensuing blast of the Death Star's destruction. Despite surviving the blast, the ship incurred significant damage and loss of life. In spite of the damage, the ship was never brought to a [[shipyard]] for repairs, with the damaged sections and the bodies of the fallen Imperials being retained as a sort of memorial while the ship continued its service to the Empire under the new name ''Tarkin's Will''.<ref name="Star Wars 21">[[Star Wars (2020) 21|''Star Wars'' (2020) 21]]</ref> Lord Vader, one of the few notable Imperials who survived the battle, was recovered in his damaged [[TIE Advanced x1]] by Imperial pilots [[Ciena Ree]] and [[Berisse Sai]].<ref name="LS" /> Iden Versio, who was outside of the Death Star's explosion, managed to pilot her damaged TIE Fighter to Yavin 4, where she ejected before it crashed into the jungle. There, she patched her wounds and stole a [[transport]] from the Rebel Base during the festivities.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />

Nearly all blame on the defeat was placed on Tarkin and his overconfidence. He had assumed that the station would not need an escort, and had launched no TIEs. The only group had been the single [[squadron]] Vader had launched on his own authority. Regardless, the Grand Moff was no longer alive to defend himself, and the Empire would never engage the rebels without exploiting their larger numbers compared to their opponent again. The destruction of the Death Star was a major blow to the Empire and represented the threat that was the Alliance to Restore the Republic.<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> [[Cassio Tagge]] was given the rank of [[Grand General]], as Emperor Palpatine had taken note of his argument against using the Death Star as the sole weapon of the Empire, and his awareness of the degree of the rebel threat. Vader was chastised and personally reminded by Palpatine about how he alone was alive to bear the blame of the destruction and failure of the Death Star. He was then ordered to act upon the will of Tagge.<ref name="Darth Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2015) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 1]]</ref>

[[File:DebrisFieldDS.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Death Star's debris field]]
[[Thane Kyrell]] and the other Imperials stationed at the [[Dantooine base|abandoned Rebel base]] on [[Dantooine]] during the battle were cut off from communication with [[Coruscant]] after the Death Star was destroyed. Unconfirmed rumors spread through the [[Military rank|ranks]] that the new weapon was gone. Early on, these rumors were dismissed as an Imperial trap to lure the Rebels out of hiding. Meanwhile, the destruction of the Death Star caused chaos within the Imperial ranks, leading to deliberate oversights on internal investigations and the rapid promotion of many young [[officer]]s and [[lieutenant]]s such as Ciena Ree to replace the higher-ranked officers killed on the Death Star.<ref name="LS" />

Additionally, according to [[Everi Chalis]], Palpatine was convinced that the incompetence of high-ranking Imperials was responsible for allowing the Death Star to be destroyed, which led to a culling of officers including [[Moff]] [[Coovern]] and [[Minister]] [[Khemt]]. Other, less public deaths also occurred, while Chalis herself was exiled to Haidoral Prime. Darth Vader carried out at least some of the killing personally.<ref name="Twlight" /> Though the Death Star and its purpose had disgusted Ciena Ree, who was horrified when she discovered the Empire was in the process of constructing the battle station's replacement, its destruction also cost the life of Jude Edivon, one of Ree's close friends, and Ciena, though she would grow to understand why her lover, Thane Kyrell, had chosen to abandon the Empire, nevertheless she doubted she would ever forgive the Rebel Alliance for its role in Jude's demise.<ref name="LS" />

===Further influence===
{{Quote|The loss of the Death Star made the galaxy think they could rebel. This has shown that the rebellion can ''never win''.''"<br />"''Hmph. It is&hellip; satisfactory.|Darth Vader and Emperor Palpatine after the Battle of Mako-Ta|<ref name="SW 2015 55">[[Star Wars (2015) 55|''Star Wars'' (2015) 55]]</ref>}}
[[File:VaderReports-2015StarWars55.png|thumb|left|300px|Darth Vader argues that his "master stroke," the Battle of Mako-Ta, has made up for the loss of the Death Star.]]
In [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Vader sprung a major trap against the rebellion in the [[Battle of Mako-Ta]], which he described as an attack on the Alliance's hope itself to Admiral [[Kendal Ozzel]].<ref name="SW 2015 50">[[Star Wars (2015) 50|''Star Wars'' (2015) 50]]</ref> The battle would end with major rebel leaders dead, over half of the cruisers in the Alliance Navy destroyed, and over ninety percent of the [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps]] lost. In his report to Palpatine, Vader argued his "master stroke," as Palpatine called it, was a success despite some rebel forces managing to escape: whereas the Battle of Yavin had shown weakness in the Empire, the Battle of Mako-Ta did the same for the rebellion, with Vader declaring the galaxy would see it as proof the Alliance would never succeed in its crusade.<ref name="SW 2015 55" /> Ultimately, however, galactic sentiment continued to turn against the Empire as the war continued.<ref name="For the Love">{{StoryCite|story=For the Love of the Empire|book=A New Legacy 1}}</ref>

Even years after the Battle of Yavin,<ref name="Circa 2 Date">{{Marvel|url=articles/comics/star-wars-a-new-legacy-1-marvel-comics-history|text=Marvel Comics Celebrates Their History of Star Wars Comic Book Storytelling in 'Star Wars: A New Legacy' #1}} establishes the events of the story "[[For the Love of the Empire]]" take place after [[Task Force 99]]'s last appearance in [[Star Wars (2015) 61|''Star Wars'' (2015) 61]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of to around [[2 ABY]]. As such, the events of the comic occur around 2 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] at the absolute earliest, which would put it to around two [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]] per ''Star Wars: Timelines''.</ref> Imperial supply lines remained thinly stretched after to the rebellion's victory at Yavin and as the Galactic Civil War continued. Due to those thinly stretched supply lines, the elite stormtroopers of [[Task Force 99]] found themselves left to their own devices while performing their duties. The squad's leader, [[Sergeant]] [[Kreel]], truly believed in the Empire and thought its victory in the Galactic Civil War was inevitable even despite the loss of the Death Star; as he reasoned, the Empire would be able to build twenty more battle stations to make up for the loss of one, whereas the rebels lacked such firepower and could not match Imperial numbers. However, upon landing on the once-Imperial loyal world of [[Horford]]<ref name="For the Love" /> at some point during or after [[2 ABY]]<ref name="Circa 2 Date" /> and seeing how its populous had turned on the regime, Kreel came to believe the war was lost: in seeing people flee from his squad in fear and watching children play as the Heroes of Yavin, he believed the hearts and minds of the galactic populous were what mattered, instead of weapons like the Death Star, and realized stormtroopers like himself had hurt the Empire's reputation. Consumed by fear that they would be remembered as villains once the histories of the war were written, the squadron [[Massacre on Horford|turned its weapons on the population]].<ref name="For the Love" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars (2020) 3|''Star Wars'' (2020) 3]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> [[Lieutenant]] [[Gorr]] of the ''Tarkin's Will'', formerly known as ''Fortitude'', proposed to [[Commander]] Ellian Zahra to execute repairs on the Star Destroyer's hull. Zahra replied that the ship represented a memorial to the destruction of the Death Star and told the lieutenant the ship's history during the Battle of Yavin. The commander also mentioned that when Vader offered her any vessel in the [[Imperial Navy|fleet]] to hunt for the rebel remnants, she chose the ''Fortitude'' because of what it represented. Zahra continued, saying that she picked the ship's crew among those who had lost someone close to them during the Battle of Yavin. After hearing the story, Gorr asked Zahra if she had lost someone during the battle as well and offered his sympathies to her.<ref name="Star Wars 3" /> Death Star debris could still be found in the atmosphere of Yavin 8<ref name="Outlaws" /> around that same year<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> according to the [[Unidentified Quarren spacer's cousin|cousin]] of a [[Unidentified Quarren spacer|Quarren spacer]], who heard from their cousin what it was like to see the Death Star's destruction and later shared that story to an [[engineer]] in the [[Bestine Cantina]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> around 3 ABY. Around the same time,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> an announcement honoring the memory of the Imperials killed in the battle was broadcast in the [[Myrra Spaceport]] on the Outer Rim planet [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

===New Republic Era===
Six years before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[Hosnian system]], an unofficial political faction in the [[New Republic]]'s [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] known to admire the Galactic Empire's rule, the [[Centrists]], questioned the official New Republic historical accounts of the Battle of Yavin and the [[Galaxy|Galactic]] Civil War. They believed that the Death Star was destroyed by an Imperial [[Treason|traitor]] and not by Luke Skywalker and the Rebel Alliance. Another Centrist, Senator Ransolm Casterfo, would later remind Leia Organa how the destruction of the Death Star had cost thousand of fairly-innocent workers and employees their lives while listing the flaws of the Rebellion.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>

Some time during the [[New Republic Era]], the [[Ithorian]] cartographer [[Gammit Chond]] created a collection of maps that used a [[Galactic standard calendar|calendar system]] dating all events in reference to the Battle of Yavin.<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Yavin first appeared in the [[1977]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH" />

The timeline of ''Star Wars'' is most often based upon this event, where events are dated as happening so many years "Before the Battle of Yavin" (BBY) or "After the Battle of Yavin" (ABY).<ref name="GA" />

The short stories in ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]'' are inconsistent regarding some elements of the battle. "[[Sparks (short story)|Sparks]]" establishes that [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]] fought at the Battle of Yavin,<ref name="Sparks" /> whereas "[[Grounded]]" states that only [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]] and [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] fought at the battle.<ref name="Grounded" /> This article assumes "Sparks" is correct, because "Grounded" additionally establishes that there were 22 [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s that fought in the battle, and "[[Duty Roster]]" states that Red Squadron had twelve pilots at the Battle of Yavin.<ref name="Duty Roster" /> "Grounded" additionally established that 5 X-wings, 1 Y-wing and 1 U-Wing were left behind at the Massassi base because there was no available pilots,<ref name="Grounded" /> whereas "Duty Roster" stated that several pilots stayed behind at the base during the battle.<ref name="Duty Roster" /> This article assumes that Duty Roster is correct.

The reference book ''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]'' says a Red Squadron pilot flying as [[Red 16]] took part in the battle,<ref name="SG">''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]''</ref> which cannot be possible if only twelve Red Squadron pilots were involved.<ref name="Duty Roster" /> Additionally, the ''[[Battle of Yavin Scenario Pack]]'' released by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] claimed that the [[TIE/IN interceptor]]s of [[Sigma Squadron]] took part in the battle.<ref name="Yavin FFG">{{FFGXW2|set=Battle of Yavin Scenario Pack}}</ref> However, other sources confirmed Tarkin refused to send out reinforcements beyond the group of TIE fighters, [[Black Squadron (Galactic Empire)|Black Squadron]], Vader ordered into battle.<ref name="Battles" /><ref name="On the Front Lines" />
