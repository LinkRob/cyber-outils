'''Airlock 8''' was a location at the edge of [[Fortress Inquisitorius]]' [[Defensive Perimeter|defense perimeter]]. A spaceous chamber, the [[airlock]] contained a body of [[water]] upon which laid a balcony housing [[Computer|consoles]], by which were stationed [[stormtrooper]]s. The balcony had a [[blast door]] leading into the heart of the Inquisitorius headquarters through a corridor, as well as an access walkway reaching halfway through the airlock.<ref name="JFO" />

Around [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> during his [[secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] [[Mission to Nur|infiltrated]] the Inquisitorius [[fortress]] through Airlock 8, entering from underwater into the flooded airlock. After defeating several stormtroopers and [[scout trooper]]s led by a [[Unidentified Scout Trooper Commander|Scout Trooper Commander]] at Airlock 8, the [[Jedi]] was assisted by his [[droid]] companion, [[BD-1]], in [[Slicer|slicing]] open the balcony blast door, revealing a [[KX-series security droid]] in the corridor beyond. Kestis fought his way through, and proceeded towards the heart of the Imperial facility.<ref name="JFO" />
