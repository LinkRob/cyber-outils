'''Dooku's journal''' was an old [[leather]]-bound [[datapad]] that documented [[Dooku]]'s time in the [[Jedi Order]] and which chronicled his rise in the Order until his departure. The journal contained his lessons with [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], voyages to [[Mantooine]], [[Lahsbane]] and [[Kashyyyk]], diplomatic encounters, rescues, mercy missions, and a time when Dooku and his master liberated a star system from the [[Unidentified necrotic queen|necrotic queen]]. The journal was kept on [[Serenno]] but later given to [[Asajj Ventress]]. The [[assassin]] reviewed its contents while on her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|mission]] to locate and free Dooku's sister [[Jenza]] who had been kidnapped; she had hoped to understand more about Dooku through the journal.<ref name="JL" />

The journal was incinerated after Jenza's [[death]] with Dooku ordering his sister's death be covered up; Ventress doused the location in gas before lighting it and sending the journal into the blaze as well.<ref name="JL" />
