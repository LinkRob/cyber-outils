A '''meditation chamber''' was located in the [[Batuu Jedi temple]]. Around [[379 BBY]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] [[Meditation|meditated]] in the [[Meditation chamber|chamber]] after being unable to shake a feeling of anticipation. Just after Nitani lost her concentration, her [[Jedi Master|Master]], [[Silandra Sho]], entered the chamber and told Nitani that it was time for her to undergo her [[Rooper Nitani's Jedi Trials|Jedi Trials]]. After completing her [[Jedi Trials|trials]], Nitani returned to the chamber to speak with Sho.

==Description==
A [[meditation chamber]] was located in the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Batuu Jedi temple]] on<ref name="SotJ" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Planet|world]] [[Batuu]]. There was a mat on the floor of the room that [[Jedi]] could sit on while [[Meditation|meditating]]. The chamber had a doorway that led to a corridor.<ref name="SotJ" />

==History==
{{Quote|It's time.''"<br />"''Time for what?''"<br />"''For your trials. For you to become a Jedi Knight.|Silandra Sho and Rooper Nitani|<ref name="SotJ" />}}
[[File:BatuuJediTemple-YJAS1-ShortSpire.png|250px|thumb|right|The meditation chamber was located in the Batuu Jedi temple.]]
Around [[379 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Shield of the Jedi]]" establishes that [[Rooper Nitani]] was around eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old during its events. In ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of to [[382 BBY]], Nitani was fifteen years old. Therefore, "Shield of the Jedi" must take place around [[379 BBY]].</ref> Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] went to the chamber to meditate after she was unable to shake a feeling of anticipation. While meditating, she reached out with [[the Force]], perceiving the life around her as glowing [[color]]s and [[Force sense|sensing]] other Jedi in the [[Jedi temple|temple]], including [[Petrish Imglor]], [[Evat Seth]], and her [[Jedi Master|Master]], [[Silandra Sho]]. She then reached out further, and sensed a flock of [[skylar-bird]]s, a herd of [[horned stoan]]s, and the [[forest]]s surrounding the temple, before losing her concentration.<ref name="SotJ" /> 

Just as Nitani was considering seeking counsel from her Master, Sho entered the room with the [[church droid]] [[P3-7A]] and explained that the anticipation Nitani was sensing was actually her own. When Nitani questioned her, Sho told her that it was now time for Nitani to undergo her [[Rooper Nitani's Jedi Trials|Jedi Trials]] to become a [[Jedi Knight]]. Sho explained that she had hidden her [[Silandra Sho's shield|shield]], which Nitani would have to find, and also told her to take P3-7A with her, although the [[droid]] knew nothing about the [[Plasma shield|shield's]] location. Nitani then left the room with P3-7A to begin searching.<ref name="SotJ" />

After Nitani found the [[Shield (physical armor)|shield]], she returned to the meditation chamber, where Sho was waiting. Nitani told her about what had happened during her [[Jedi Trials|trials]], and they agreed to talk to the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] the next [[Local day|day]]. As Sho left the room, Nitani attempted to return the shield to her, but Sho told her to keep it and explained that she wanted to honor the tradition of passing down the shield from Master to Padawan.<ref name="SotJ" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The Batuu Jedi temple meditation chamber appeared in the short story "[[Shield of the Jedi]]," written by [[George Mann]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="SotJ" />
