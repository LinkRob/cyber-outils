"'''Darth Vader - Might of the Empire'''" is the sixth episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com]]<ref name="Star Wars Kids" /> and the [[The Walt Disney Company|DisneyNOW]]<ref name="Disney Now">{{DisneyNOW|url=shows/star-wars-galaxy-of-adventures|text=Galaxy of Adventures}}</ref> websites on [[November 30]], [[2018]].<ref name="YouTube" />

==Plot summary==
[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] walks down the ramp of a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class shuttle]] as smoke and foam rises. The narrator explains that Darth Vader commands the might of the [[Galactic Empire]] as he walks past two rows of [[stormtrooper]]s, several [[Imperial officer]]s, and [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] and [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT walkers]] in the [[hangar]] bay of the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Executor]]''. [[AT-ST driver]]s and [[AT-AT pilot]]s stand at attention from the hatches of their vehicles. Vader then walks onto the bridge of the ''Executor'' where he watches the assembled [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]] and [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter/Legends|TIE fighters]] of [[Death Squadron]]. As the ships fly in formation, Lord Vader says that this will be a day long remembered.
