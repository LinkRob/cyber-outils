"'''Best Friends / Happy Trails, Nubs'''" is the twenty-first episode of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''.<ref name="dgepress" /> It will be released on [[Disney+]] on [[February 14]], [[2024]].<ref name="Futon" />

==Official description==
''Kai and Nubs team up for a training exercise. Lys and Kai must rescue Nubs from the Ganguls.''
