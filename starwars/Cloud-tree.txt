The '''cloud-tree''' was a type of [[tree]] that was found on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[swamp]] [[planet]] [[Dagobah]]. The [[Yoda's species|alien]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] made a [[Cloud-tree root tea|tea]] from the [[Plant|roots]] of the tree during his [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] on the planet<ref name="Atlas" /> from [[19 BBY]] to [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The cloud-tree was mentioned in the [[2021]] [[Canon|non-canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Yoda's Galaxy Atlas]]''.<ref name="Atlas" />
