The '''Arc of Eden''' was a location<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> in the [[Arc of Eden system]] within the [[Immalia sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="The Interior" /> It was situated in grid square K-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

According to the former [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[general]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], the Arc of Eden served the best [[hot chocolate]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> In [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> when he had a hot chocolate at the [[Komat homestead|homestead]] of the former [[Acolytes of the Beyond|Acolyte of the Beyond]] [[Komat]] on<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Polaar">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Polaar system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Polaar]] Calrissian [[Toast (ritual)|raised]] his drink in salute to Komat for the quality of the beverage being the finest outside of the Arc of Eden, which made Komat smile.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
