'''Dara Vish''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Bartender|proprietor]] [[Homeworld|hailing]] from the [[planet]] [[Sorgan]] who worked at the [[Sorgan Common House|Common House]] on the world. Around [[9 ABY]], she served [[grinjer]] [[bone broth]] and [[spotchka]] to the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] and his [[Youngling|young]] companion, [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], the former of whom bribed Vish so that she would speed up his order. Djarin also inquired Vish about a local named [[Carasynthia Dune|Cara Dune]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Welcome, travelers. Can I interest you in anything?|Dara Vish, to Din Djarin|audio=WelcomeTravelers.flac|<ref name="Chapter 4" />}}
[[File:DaraVish.png|thumb|left|300px|Dara Vish accepted the Mandalorian's bribe.]]
Dara Vish<ref name="Handbook" /> was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Chapter 4" /> [[Homeworld|hailing]] from the [[planet]] [[Sorgan]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> On Sorgan, Vish owned and [[Bartender|bartended]] the [[Sorgan Common House|Common House]],<ref name="Chapter 4" /> working to create a welcoming establishment for her patrons.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|S1}}</ref> she<ref name="Handbook" /> had [[Death|taken down]] a creature known as a [[grinjer]], which provided for plenty of [[bone broth]] for the Common House. Shortly afterward, the [[Mandalorian]]<ref name="Chapter 4" /> [[Din Djarin]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> and the [[Youngling|infant]]<ref name="Chapter 4" /> [[Din Grogu|Grogu]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> visited the planet and then the house, where Vish welcomed them and took Djarin's order: [[bone]] [[Soup|broth]] for Grogu. She offered the Mandalorian broth as well, but he declined.<ref name="Chapter 4" />

He then took interest in the nearby human [[Carasynthia Dune|Cara Dune]], inquiring about how long she had been there and what she was doing; Vish thought that she had seen Dune for around a [[Standard week|week]] and then chuckled, for there was not much business on Sorgan. Djarin then slipped her some [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], prompting Vish to assure the Mandalorian that his meal would come as soon as possible and that she would additionally serve him a flagon of [[spotchka]]. After noticing that Dune had left her seat, Djarin walked outside, tossing an additional [[Currency|coin]] to Vish in exchange for her watching Grogu for him.<ref name="Chapter 4" /> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Vish was present in the building when Dune fought the [[Zabrak]] fighter<ref name="Chapter 7">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 7: The Reckoning}}</ref> [[Taur Deeves]],<ref name="MandoVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> standing behind the bar and watching the tussle.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I will get that broth to you as soon as possible, and I will throw in a flagon of spotchka just for good measure.|Dara Vish accepts the Mandalorian's bribe|audio=GoodMeasure.flac|<ref name="Chapter 4" />}}
Dara Vish was willing to accept bribes in exchange for better service, assuring Djarin that his [[Foodstuff|food]] would come as soon as possible after he gave [[Currency|credits]] to her and even adding free spotchka to the order.<ref name="Chapter 4" /> She considered all who visited her tavern a friend, with many of her regular customers happy to travel for her hospitality.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Vish had [[Color|black]] [[hair]], light skin, and hazel-colored [[eye]]s.<ref name="Chapter 4" />

==Equipment==
While on Sorgan, Vish wore brown, lightly patterned [[clothing]].<ref name="Chapter 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dara Vish first appeared in "[[Chapter 4: Sanctuary]],"<ref name="Chapter 4" /> the fourth episode of the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''{{'s}} [[The Mandalorian Season One|first season]]. The episode aired on [[November 29]], [[2019]].<ref name="Mando Date">{{InsiderCite|201|Star Wars: The Mandalorian Season One Companion|''Star Wars: The Mandalorian'' Season One Companion}}</ref> In the episode, she was portrayed by [[Ida Darvish]] and was identified in the closing credits only as "Common House Proprietor."<ref name="Chapter 4" /> Her name of "Dara Vish," a [[tuckerization]] of Darvish's name, was given in the [[2021]] young readers reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Handbook]]'' by [[Matt Jones]].<ref name="Handbook" />
