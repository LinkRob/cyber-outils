An astronomical object was the original '''homeworld''' of the [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[fathier]] creatures.<ref name="Bestiary" /> No earlier than [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BestiaryVol1Date}}</ref> [[Ardis San Tekka]], the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]]'s [[Director of Creature Cartography]], noted in her [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]] that although fathiers were found on [[planet]]s across [[the galaxy]], the location of their [[homeworld]] was unknown.<ref name="Bestiary" />

==Behind the scenes==
The fathier homeworld was mentioned in ''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]'', a [[2024]] reference book written by [[S.T. Bende]].<ref name="Bestiary" />
