'''Bantha Burger''' was an eating establishment in the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|DS-1 Orbital Battle Station]] that served [[Bantha burger/Legends|Bantha burgers]], [[Krayt bacon burger]]s and [[sithwich]]es.<ref name="Tiny">''[[Star Wars: Tiny Death Star]]''</ref> [[Astromech droid/Legends|Astromech droids]] often acted as servers, wearing [[Hat/Legends|hats]] with the [[Restaurant/Legends|restaurant's]] name on them.<ref name="R2">''[[How to Speak Droid with R2-D2: A Communication Manual]]''</ref>
