'''Fikari''' was a terrestrial world in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] searched for information regarding a [[Hunt for Vader|plot]] to assassinate him on the [[Hidden Hand]] crime syndicate on the world.

==History==
During the [[Imperial Era]], a number of [[Hidden Hand]] members were present on Fikari. Two members, [[Holaq]] and [[Uro (Aqualish)|Uro]], discussed the criminal syndicate and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] attacks on them. Vader, who had arrived on the world after learning of a [[Hunt for Vader|plot]] to assassinate him, eliminated most of the Hidden Hand members and interrogated Holaq. Unsatisfied with his response, Holaq was slain by the [[Sith Lord]]. He later contacted the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]], who expressed his disappointment and stated that the syndicate's elimination was of utmost importance. Unbeknownst to Vader, he was spied on by [[Gwi]], another Hidden Hand associate.<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fikari made its debut in the [[Target Vader 1|first issue]] of the [[2019]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book series]] ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]'', written by [[Robbie Thompson]].<ref name="Target Vader 1" />
