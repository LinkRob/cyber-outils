A '''Dacdanite''' was an entity associated with [[death]] that was known to the [[alien]] [[Dacna]].<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 1" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 1|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 1]] are set between the events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone]]'' and [[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 2|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 2]] which includes the [[Battle of Eriadu]]. ''Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone'' and the Battle of Eriadu are both dated to [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:THRContent228BBY|here]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], Dacna and their companion [[Morka]] were traveling aboard their [[Freighter|cargo ship]] ''[[Marshskimmer]]'' when it was attacked by a pair of [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] [[cloudship]]s.<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 1" />

As Morka attempted to [[Distress signal|call]] for help, Dacna told them they were wasting their time as no one would arrive to help them, to which Morka stated that Dacna did not know that for sure. In response, Dacna noted they did know and added that the Nihil would not stop until they were "dead as a Dacdanite." Before the Nihil could kill them, however, the two were saved by the arrival of [[Jedi Master]]s [[Sskeer]] and [[Keeve Trennis]], with Sskeer destroying one of the Nihil cloudships and the other fleeing into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 1" />
