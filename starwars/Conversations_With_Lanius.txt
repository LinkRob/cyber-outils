'''Conversations With Lanius''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] [[Wizards.com|website]] on [[October 23]], [[2003]] and took the form of an interview of [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Lanius Qel-Bertuk]] by [[Yara Grugara]] and another reporter. In the interview, Qel-Bertuk responded to criticisms that the Jedi were not doing enough in the Cularin system and argued that Jedi are not as different from non-Force sensitives as many believe; they search for meaning in their lives just as others do.

In [[2005]], [[Conversations With Lanius (second article)|another article]] with the title ''Conversations With Lanius'' was released on Wizards.com.
