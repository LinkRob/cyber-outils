"'''Art Attack'''" is the second of four prelude shorts<ref name="Article">{{WebCite|url=http://www.ign.com/articles/2014/08/08/star-wars-rebels-exclusive-clip-spotlights-conflict-in-space|text=IGN Machine in the ''Ghost'' article|archivedate=20140808181444}}</ref> released in promotion of the animated series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. The three-minute short aired online and on [[Disney XD]] [[August 18]], [[2014]]. The remaining two shorts premiered in the same time slot over the next two weeks.<ref name="Article" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Sabine shows off both her artistic and explosive skills against a squadron of stormtroopers.<ref name="StarWars.com">{{SW|url=video/star-wars-rebels-art-attack-short|text=Star Wars Rebels: "Art Attack" Short}}</ref>''

==Plot summary==
On [[Lothal]], a citizen is in a restricted [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] area and is threatened by a [[Unidentified stormtrooper commander (Lothal)|stormtrooper]], who commands the citizen to leave the area. As the stormtrooper moves along his patrol, [[Sabine Wren]] appears on a rooftop and makes her way toward a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] landing platform. Once on the outer wall of the platform, Wren contacts [[Hera Syndulla]] on the ''[[Ghost]]'', alerting the [[Twi'lek]] [[pilot]] that Wren is in place in order to create a diversion that will allow the ''Ghost'' to fly away.

[[File:Sabine_Wren_Symbol_TIE_Fighter.png|thumb|left|250px|Wren paints her symbol onto the wing of a TIE fighter.]]
Wren sneaks through the landing platform and begins painting her call sign in [[graffiti]] on the side of a TIE fighter, when she is spotted by two stormtroopers who overheard the sound of her painting. They question the [[Mandalorian]] as to what she is doing and command her to stand down, or else they will shoot. Wren goads them to shoot. The stormtroopers, staring at each other in bemusement prepare their [[blaster]]s only to find that she had disappeared. The troopers chase her through the platform, with Wren taunting them as she moved. One of the troopers, [[TK-626]], calls for reinforcements.

When the reinforcements arrive, the two troopers describe Wren as being still at large and wearing "[[Mandalorian armor|Mando gear]]." Wren returns to her graffiti and adds an explosive device to it before running off. The troopers notice the explosive, but not before it is too late. The TIE fighter explodes, and the ''Ghost'' uses the distraction as a diversion to fly off. The groaning stormtroopers survived, covered in paint, while Wren marvels at her explosive artwork.

==Continuity==
This short was adapted into a short story by [[Michael Kogge]] in the [[Star Wars Rebels chapter books|chapter book]] ''[[Rise of the Rebels]]'' as well as the [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] young reader's book ''[[Sabine's Art Attack]]'' by [[Jennifer Heddle]]. Another [[Art Attack (book)|version]] by [[Brooke Vitale]] was one of eight titles in ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Storybook Library]]''.

Sabine's attack is mentioned by the stormtroopers guarding the TIE fighters in "[[Fire Across the Galaxy]]."
