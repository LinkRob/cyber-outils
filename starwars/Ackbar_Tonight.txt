'''''Ackbar Tonight''''' was a [[holotelevision]] nighttime talk show hosted by [[Gial Ackbar/Legends|Gial Ackbar]], aired on [[OR4-TV]]. Once, when [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Grand Master/Legends|Grand Master]] [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] was making a [[Hologram/Legends|holographic]] transmission to the [[Jedi High Council/Legends|Jedi High Council]], a young [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] ("Ian") got bored and briefly interrupted the transmission with a series of holotevision programs, one of which was this one.
