'''Fel Ix''' was a [[Kessarine]] [[Gender|male]] who was a member of the [[Path of the Open Hand]]. He was one of [[Ferize]]'s partners along with [[Er Dal]],<ref name="POD" /> and in [[382 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> they hatched five [[Youngling|children]] who were then the subject of a [[Naming ceremony]] that the Path performed, with one of the leaders of the Path, [[Elecia Zeveron|the Mother]], blessing them and declaring them part of the Path.<ref name="POD" />

[[Marda Ro]] was a member of the Path who was friends with Fel Ix and his family and helped both with the Naming ceremony and caring for their children. The same year as the Naming ceremony, she visited the family to say goobye as she was leaving the [[Path compound]] on [[Dalna]]. Fel Ix comforted her when she said they were her family too, which he agreed with and recognized. Fel Ix and Er Dal then gathered their children while Ferize and Ro went to investigate a strange sound.<ref name="POD" />
