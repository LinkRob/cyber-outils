'''Brock Peters''' ([[1920s|1927]] &ndash; [[August 23]], [[2005]]) was an [[Wikipedia:American|American]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]], [[Wikipedia:Singer|singer]], and [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice artist]].<ref name="NYTimes" />

Peters voiced [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] in the [[Star Wars radio dramas|radio drama]] adaptations of ''[[Star Wars (radio)|Star Wars]]'', ''[[The Empire Strikes Back (radio)|The Empire Strikes Back]]'', and ''[[Return of the Jedi (radio)|Return of the Jedi]]'' in [[1981]], [[1983]], and [[1996]] respectively.<ref name="SW Radio">[[Star Wars radio dramas]]</ref> In [[1997]], Peters voiced Darth Vader in the read-along story books of the ''[[Original trilogy]]''.<ref name="ANH">''[[A New Hope Read-Along]]''</ref> The same recordings were later used in the [[2015]] read-along story books, a decade after Peters' passing.<ref name="ANH Storybook">''[[A New Hope Read-Along Storybook and CD]]''</ref>
