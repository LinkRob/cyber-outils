'''Converter Station''' was a business at [[Stobar Spaceport]] during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Brothers" /> and [[Havoc Outpost]]<ref name="Sisters" /> and [[Horizon Base]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Forgotten" /> During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], an advertisement for the company was seen on the exterior of [[Vranki's Hotel and Casino]], a [[space station]] in the [[Voxx Cluster]].<ref name="Voxx">{{Resistance|The Voxx Vortex 5000}}</ref> 

The business featured a sign with the business' name written in a casual, handwritten [[Outer Rim Basic]] font, with a cable representing a converter, extended vertically across the title.<ref name="Forgotten" /> At least two different [[color]] palettes were used, with the sign from the Clone Wars displaying the name of the business and the cable in an orange-yellow hue set against a vivid blue backdrop,<ref name="Brothers" /> while later on, the signage adopted a pure white shade for the name and cable.<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/blood-sisters-concept-art-gallery|text="Blood Sisters" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref>
