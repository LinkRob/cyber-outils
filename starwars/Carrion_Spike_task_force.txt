During the [[Berch Teller campaign]], an [[Imperial Navy]] '''task force''' was assembled to hunt down the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'',<ref name="Tarkin" /> a modified [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette]],<ref name="Ships">{{EncyclopediaCite|Starships of the Empire and the Rebellion}}</ref> which was stolen from [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] by the rebel [[Berch Teller]]. The [[task force]], initially led by Moff Tarkin, consisted of the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Executrix]]'', the [[Imperial escort carrier]] ''[[Goliath]]'', a [[CC-7700 frigate]], a [[Detainer CC-2200]], an [[Immobilizer 418 cruiser]], and several [[picket ship]]s along with other [[starship]]s. Eventually, Moff Tarkin was replaced by [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]] of the [[Naval Intelligence Agency]] to lead the task force.<ref name="Tarkin" />
