'''Darth Voren''', originally known as '''Voren Renstaal''', was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Human/Legends|Human]] who served [[Darth/Legends|Darth]] [[Revan/Legends|Revan's]] [[Darth Revan's Sith Empire|Sith Empire]] as a [[Sith apprentice/Legends|Sith apprentice]]. Renstaal was originally a member of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]], and he fought alongside the [[Revanchist]]s during the [[Mandalorian Wars]], a conflict that erupted in [[3976 BBY]] when the [[Mandalorian/Legends|Mandalorian]] [[Mandalorian Neo-Crusaders/Legends|Neo–Crusaders]] began an invasion of the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]].

Following the conclusion of the Mandalorian Wars in [[3960 BBY]], the Revanchists reformed to become Revan's Sith Empire. Renstaal became an apprentice and took up the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] title "[[Darth/Legends|Darth]]," renaming himself as Darth Voren. The Empire began an [[Jedi Civil War|invasion]] of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]], and Voren was assigned to serve aboard [[Darth Revan's flagship|Revan's flagship]]. In [[3957 BBY]], the ship was [[Capture of Darth Revan|attacked]] by the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]], and Voren encountered a Republic strike team in the ship's Security Command center.

==Biography==
The [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] Voren Renstaal was [[Reproduction/Legends|born]] during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]]. He joined the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]] and completed his [[Jedi training/Legends|Jedi training]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Ossus/Legends|Ossus]] under the tutelage of a [[Voren Renstaal's Jedi Master|Jedi Master]].<ref name="TBODR" /> In [[3976 BBY]], the [[Mandalorian/Legends|Mandalorian]] [[Mandalorian Neo-Crusaders/Legends|Neo–Crusaders]] began attacking worlds in the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]], beginning the [[Mandalorian Wars]].<ref name="KOTORCG">''[[Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide]]''</ref> Renstaal fought alongside his [[Jedi Master/Legends|Master]] during the early stages of the conflict until his Master was [[Death/Legends|killed]] during the [[First battle of Onderon (Mandalorian Wars)|First Battle of Onderon]]. Afterward, Renstaal joined the [[Revanchist]]s,<ref name="TBODR" /> a renegade faction of Jedi led by the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Knight]] [[Revan/Legends|Revan]], and fought in the Mandalorian Wars against the wishes of the [[Jedi High Council/Legends|Jedi High Council]]. Following the [[Battle of Malachor V|defeat]] of the Mandalorians in [[3960 BBY]], the Revanchists traveled to the [[Unknown Regions/Legends|Unknown Regions]],<ref name="KOTOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref> and Renstaal accompanied them. There, the Revanchists adopted the teachings of the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] and reformed themselves to become [[Darth Revan's Sith Empire]]. Renstaal took up the Sith title of "[[Darth/Legends|Darth]]," renaming himself "Darth Voren." He became a [[Sith apprentice/Legends|Sith apprentice]] and trained under a [[Unidentified Sith Knight|Sith Knight]], who was under the direct command of the newly-christened "Darth" Revan.<ref name="TBODR" />

The Sith Empire began an [[Jedi Civil War|invasion]] of the Republic, and during the conflict, Voren was appointed to serve aboard [[Darth Revan's flagship|Revan's flagship]], where he helped to control the [[Starship/Legends|ship's]] security forces. In [[3957 BBY]], Voren was aboard the [[Flagship/Legends|flagship]] when it was [[Capture of Darth Revan|attacked]] by Republic forces, who planned to capture Darth Revan. During the attack, a team of Republic agents was sent to the ship's Security Command, or "SecCom," center, from where they planned to order all of the ship's security teams to go to the aft side of the flagship, allowing a team of Jedi to make their way to the bridge and capture Revan. The strike team was made up of the [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knights]] [[Aldren Dayos]] and [[Anyara]]; the [[Scout/Legends|scout]] [[Haden Vazzar]]; the [[Soldier/Legends|soldier]] [[Kagen Brendel]]; the [[Spy/Legends|spy]] [[Mara Davos]]; and the intelligence officer [[Samar]].<ref name="TBODR" />

The group made their way to the Security Command center, where the Republic [[Squad/Legends|squad]] found Voren standing in the middle of the center with fifteen [[Sith trooper (Sith Empire)|Sith troopers]] and several [[Sith Marauder]]s, who were all under his command. The Sith troopers attacked the strike team, while Voren and the Sith Marauders ignited their [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsabers]] and deflected any [[Blaster/Legends|blaster]] shots that were fired at them. The Republic team was eventually able to obtain the codes required to access the SecCom computer system, and they used them to order the Sith security teams to move to the aft side of the ship, eventually capturing Revan.<ref name="TBODR" />

==Personality and traits==
Voren was ambitious and hoped to be able to rise quickly through the ranks of the Sith Empire. To achieve this, he strove to complete his Sith training and hoped to remove at least one of his superiors to quicken his ascent. He wore dark-colored robes and a featureless black-tinted faceplate that obscured his facial features. He also wielded a lightsaber, which he had constructed himself.<ref name="TBODR" />

==Powers and abilities==
Voren knew how to use the Force to [[Telekinesis/Legends|move objects]], [[Mind trick/Legends|influence]] the minds of others, and [[Energy resistance|create]] an energy-resistant field around himself. He could also manipulate the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side of the Force]], using [[Force lightning/Legends|Force lightning]] and [[Force Slow]] to directly attack others, or to employ [[Force Enrage|Force rage]] to enhance the rest of his powers. Voren was bilingual, able to speak both [[Galactic Basic Standard/Legends|Basic]] and the [[Sith (language)|Sith]].<ref name="TBODR" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Voren was created by [[Patrick Stutzman]] in ''[[The Betrayal of Darth Revan]]'', a supplement to the [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]] that was released in [[2008]]. In the supplement, the players role-play as the Republic agents that Voren encounters and their encounter with him can go a number of ways, depending upon the actions of the player characters. If the players kill more than half of the Sith troopers in the SecCom center, Voren and the Sith Marauders directly attack the player characters and will fight to the death, rather than surrender.<ref name="TBODR" />

The players can speak to Voren and try and get him to tell them the code for the SecCom computer system, which they can do by either interrogating him or by trying to persuade him that it is his best interest to help them. If they tell him that the aim of their mission is to capture Darth Revan, then Voren becomes more likely to help them, as he believes that removing Revan would help him to rise through the ranks of the Sith. However, if either of the Jedi player characters are still conscious, Voren will not voluntarily help the players and remains hostile. If the players do succeed in gaining Voren's favor, he enters the code into the computer system and orders the security teams to move aft. However, if the player characters then decide to alter the ship's systems, for example by disabling the weapons or lowering the shields, Voren becomes hostile to them again. When the players leave the SecCom center, if Voren is still assisting them, he remains behind to ensure that the Sith security teams stay away from the flagship's bridge. If the players fail to obtain the code from Voren, they can get it by interrogating any surviving Sith troopers.<ref name="TBODR" />
