The '''First Order Department of Military Research''' was an organization within the [[First Order]] dedicated to military research and the development of weaponry and [[armor]] for the First Order. The Department developed [[First Order stormtrooper armor]], modelled after [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper armor]]. They also created the [[helmet]]s worn by [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|First Order TIE Fighter Pilot]] and the [[riot shield]]s used by [[First Order riot control stormtrooper|riot control stormtroopers]]. Weapons created by the First Order Department of Military Research included the [[BL-155 Laser ax]], the [[D-93 Incinerator flamethrower]] and the [[Superlaser siege cannon]]. In addition to weapons and armor, the department also created [[IT-000 interrogator droid]], a new generation of [[Interrogation droid|interrogator droid]] based on the old Imperial [[IT-O interrogation droid|IT-O Interrogator]] that was used for [[torture]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

==Behind the scenes==
