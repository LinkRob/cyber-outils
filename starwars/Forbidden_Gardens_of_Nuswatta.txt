{{Quote|Have you ever seen the Forbidden Gardens of Nuswatta? Or perhaps heard the beautiful music of the Cathedral of Winds?|An excerpt from Maz Kanata's journal|<ref name="Hope & Courage">''[[Forces of Destiny: Tales of Hope & Courage]]''</ref>}}
The '''Forbidden Gardens of Nuswatta''' was a point of interest<ref name="Hope & Courage" /> located in<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Nuswatta system]] of the [[Cronese Mandate]] [[sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In her [[Maz Kanata's Journal|journal]], [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]] wrote that she had visited the Forbidden Gardens of Nuswatta and that she considered it, alongside the [[Cathedral of Winds]], as one of the most wondrous things she had seen in her life.<ref name="Hope & Courage" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Forbidden Gardens of Nuswatta were first mentioned in the [[2017]] [[canon]] replica journal book ''[[Forces of Destiny: Tales of Hope & Courage]]'', written by [[Elizabeth Schaefer]].<ref name="Hope & Courage" /> The [[Forbidden Gardens of Nuswatta/Legends|Forbidden Gardens of Nuswatta]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in [[Dark Horse Comics]] one-shot ''[[Jabba the Hutt: Betrayal]]'', written by [[Jim Woodring]], illustrated by [[Art Wetherell]],<ref name="Betrayal">''[[Jabba the Hutt: Betrayal]]''</ref> and published on [[February 1]], [[1996]].<ref name="DH-Link">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/95-178/Star-Wars-Jabba-the-Hutt---Betrayal|text=Star Wars: Jabba the Hutt - Betrayal}}</ref>
