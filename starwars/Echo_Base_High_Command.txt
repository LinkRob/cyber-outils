'''Echo Base High Command''' was the command unit<ref name="HC 9" /> of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Alliance High Command|High Command]] that oversaw Alliance forces on [[Echo Base]] on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Hoth]].<ref name="Episode V" /> Echo Base High Command was confident that Hoth's harsh climate played in their favor when selecting a planet for a new headquarters.<ref name="HC 9" />
