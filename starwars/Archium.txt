The '''Archium''' was a structure in [[Upper Pabu]], the older section of the [[Pabu island|remote island]] on [[Pabu]]. It housed artifacts from all over [[the galaxy]], as well as an early warning system, which alerted the inhabitants of Pabu when the island was at risk of a [[Tsunami|sea surge]]. A [[weeping maya tree]] stood just outside the Archium.<ref name="Pabu" />

An archive for displaying artifacts from various places, the Archium was a place for the residents of Pabu, who had come from a variety of [[homeworld]]s, to learn about the cultures of their neighbors. Artifacts were also repaired inside the Archium.<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>
