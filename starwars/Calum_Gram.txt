'''Calum Gram''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Rebel pilot]] who served in the [[Galactic Civil War]]. A former search and rescue [[pilot]] for the [[Wilderness Preserve service]] on [[Atrisia]], Gram developed precision flying skills by weaving and landing an [[airspeeder]] through the [[planet]]'s dense brush. He flew as [[Blue Nine]] during the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="SWROUVG" />

==Equipment==
[[File:C_Gram_helmet.jpg|thumb|left|150px|Calum Gram's flight helmet]]
As a pilot of Blue Squadron, Calum Gram wore a blue [[flight suit]] and [[Flight helmet|helmet]] similar in design to those worn by [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wing]] pilots.<ref name="SWROUVG" />
