"'''Clash on Cloud City'''" is the fiftieth episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com|''Star Wars Kids'']] YouTube channel on [[June 12]], [[2020]]. It was later compiled into an omnibus YouTube video called "The Empire Strikes Back 40th Anniversary", that was released on [[June 19]], [[2020]].<ref name="TESB">{{SWKidsYouTube|n2IF0DMFKxg|The Empire Strikes Back 40th Anniversary &#124; Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
[[Luke Skywalker]] and [[R2-D2]] arrive on [[Bespin]], hoping to save his friends from the clutches of the [[Galactic Empire]] but instead finds [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The two engage in a [[lightsaber duel]] with Vader hurling Skywalker out of a window into the depths of [[Cloud City]]. Lord Vader corners Skywalker on a bridge and the two parry.

As Vader drives Skywalker to the edge of the bridge, he tries to tempt Skywalker into embracing the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] and joining him. Skywalker replies that he will never join him and the two continue fighting. Vader slices off Luke's right hand with his lightsaber, causing Luke's blade to drop into the depths of Cloud City. Luke screams in pain and shock.

As Luke clings onto the edge of the platform, Lord Vader invites Skywalker to work together with him to bring order to [[the galaxy]]. He implores Luke to come with him. Luke refuses Vader's offer and lets go off his left hand. He descends into the bowels of Cloud City, clinging onto a cross-shaped platform. Through [[the Force]], Luke calls out to his sister [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who convinces [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] that they have to go back for her brother. Leia, Lando and [[Chewbacca]] turn the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' around and rescue Luke.

Back at the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]], Lando prepares to depart on the ''Falcon'' with Chewbacca. He tells a recovering Skywalker and [[Prince]]ss Leia that he promises to bring Han back. Luke wishes Lando "[[May the Force be with you]]." The [[Tom Kane|narrator]] says that together our heroes will fight on, the adventure will continue, and the Jedi will return.
