'''Alex Lindsay''' was an [[Industrial Light & Magic]] digital effects artist that worked on [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> In that movie he also played [[Rum Sleg]].<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|alexlindsay|1389579359577526273|Alex Lindsay|quote=In 1998, I was working at ILM's "Rebel Mac Unit". One day, someone came in and said "Come with me"... we walked over to Windward Stage where I was given essentially a scuba suit with helmet and accessories. Here's how it ended up... May the Fourth be with you.|archivedate=20210505073505}}</ref> Also, he voiced the adult [[Wicket Wystri Warrick/Legends|Wicket Wystri Warrick]] in ''[[Tales from the Endor Woods]]'' and [[Mungo Baobab]] in the ''[[Treasure of the Hidden Planet]]'' prologue.
