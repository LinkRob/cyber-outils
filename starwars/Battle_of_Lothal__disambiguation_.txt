There have been several '''battles''' taking place on or around the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] throughout galactic history, including the following:

==Between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]]==
*'''[[Takeover of Lothal]]'''&mdash;The [[Galactic Empire]]'s arrival on [[Lothal]] begins between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]].

==Between [[18 BBY]] and [[5 BBY]]==
*'''[[Insurgency on Lothal]]'''&mdash;A series of mass protests and insurgent attacks on Lothal [[Time|sometime]] between 18 BBY and [[5 BBY]].

==[[5 BBY]]==
*'''[[Mission in Capital City]]'''&mdash;A heist by the [[Spectres]] in Lothal's [[Capital City]].

==[[4 BBY]]==
*'''[[Rescue of Tseebo]]'''&mdash;A rescue of the [[Rodian]] [[Tseebo]] by the Spectres after he was wanted by the Empire.
*'''[[Trap in the Old Republic Senate Building]]'''&mdash;An ambush by the Galactic Empire on the Spectres in the [[Lothal City Capitol Building]].
*'''[[Raid near Jalath]]'''&mdash;A raid near [[Jalath]] by the Spectres.
*'''[[Raid on the communications tower]]'''&mdash;A raid on the [[Imperial Communications Center]] on Lothal by the Spectres that resulted in [[Ezra's broadcast]].
*'''[[Action at Mustafar]]'''&mdash;A mission to rescue [[Kanan Jarrus]] by the Spectres initially undertaken on Lothal and subsequently continued above [[Mustafar]].
*'''[[Siege of Lothal]]'''&mdash;A siege imposed on Lothal by the Empire to root out the Spectres.
*'''[[Attack on Phoenix Squadron (4 BBY)|Attack on Phoenix Squadron]]'''&mdash;An attack on the Spectres and [[Phoenix Cell]] in the [[Lothal sector]].

==[[2 BBY]]&mdash;[[1 BBY]]==
*'''[[Lothal campaign]]'''&mdash;[[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] campaign against Phoenix Cell and the Spectres in the Lothal sector taking place from [[2 BBY]] to [[1 BBY]].
**'''[[Raid on a suspected rebel cell]]'''&mdash;A raid on a suspected rebel cell on Lothal.
**'''[[Attack on Lothal]]''', also known as the '''Battle of Lothal'''&mdash;A failed attack by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] on Lothal.
**'''[[Liberation of Lothal]]''', also known as the '''Battle of Lothal'''&mdash;A battle that liberated Lothal from the Galactic Empire.

==[[35 ABY]]==
*'''[[Uprising on Lothal]]'''&mdash;The rebellion against the forces of the [[First Order]].
