Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[droid]]s of the [[Second Revelation]] '''attacked''' the [[Scourge]] [[Starship|ship]] [[Scourge 01|''Scourge'' 01]] while the Scourge was [[Scourge attack on Fortress Vader|attempting]] to corrupt the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. During the battle, a number of the Second Revelation's droids were almost themselves infected by the Scourge, but they destroyed themselves to prevent their minds from falling into its hands. [[Ajax Sigma]], the leader of the Revelation, was forced to escape having failed to [[Death|destroy]] the Scourge.<ref name="Dark Droids 3" />
