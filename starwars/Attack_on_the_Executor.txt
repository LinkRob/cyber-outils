The '''Attack on the ''Executor'''''<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> was an attack launched by the [[Hutt Clan]] against the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Executor]]''<ref name="WOTBH 5" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> in the [[Jekara system]] over the<ref name="WOTBH 5" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Star Wars 14">[[Star Wars (2020) 14|''Star Wars'' (2020) 14]]</ref> [[Jekara]]. The attack began after [[Lord]] [[Bokku]] of the [[Grand Hutt Council]] attempted to recover the [[carbonite]]-frozen [[Han Solo]], who had been won by the [[Hutt]]s in an [[Auction for Han Solo|auction]] hosted by [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]] of [[Crimson Dawn]] before being turned over to the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] by the Council's leader, [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], in an attempt to maintain relations between the Empire and the Hutts. Insulted, Bokku had an<ref name="WOTBH 4" /> [[Fleet|armada]] of [[starship]]s surround the [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] [[ST-718]], which was [[transport]]ing Solo back to the ''Executor'', Vader's [[flagship]].<ref name="WOTBH 5" />

After the Hutts gained control of ST-718 via [[Magnetic clamp|magclamps]] and tried to bring it aboard the [[war barge of the Hutt Council]], the Empire were able to recover the [[shuttle]] and eventually brought it back aboard the ''Executor''. At the same [[time]], Vader, who had been chasing his [[Skywalker family|son]] [[Luke Skywalker]], in [[starfighter]]s above Jekara, was informed by Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Firmus Piett]] that he had been requested by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] to end the Hutt threat personally. Vader followed his [[Sith Master|Master's]] orders and boarded the Hutt's war barge, where he [[Death|killed]] the Council members.<ref name="WOTBH 5" />

Whilst this was taking place, the [[bounty hunter]]s [[Boba Fett]] and [[Beilert Valance]] boarded the ''Executor'' as part of their own attempts to recover Solo. The pair traversed the ship unnoticed until they were spotted by a [[KX-series security droid]]. After killing the [[droid]], Fett betrayed Valance and threw a [[thermal detonator]] onto the [[cyborg]], leaving him for dead. Fett eventually made his way to the [[Prison|detention level]] where Solo was being held, though he encountered members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] led by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who were also attempting to recover Solo. A [[missile]] hit the ship, causing the carbonite block containing Solo to be sucked into [[Realspace|space]]. Fett used his [[Z-6 jetpack|jetpack]] to follow Solo to the planet's surface and caught him just he as he entered an [[ocean]]. With his [[bounty]] secured, Fett returned to [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on [[Tatooine]] to deliver him to Jabba.<ref name="WOTBH 5" />

The death of the Hutt Council left Jabba as the sole leader of the Hutt Clan, lost Crimson Dawn valuable allies, but strained the relationship between the Hutts and the Empire,<ref name="WOTBH 5" /> which Qi'ra took advantage of by sparking a [[Syndicate War]] between the criminal factions of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Crimson Reign 1" />
