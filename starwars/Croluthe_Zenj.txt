'''Croluthe Zenj''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> in the [[Croluthe Zenj system]] in [[Lannik Space]], a [[sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]], in grid square R-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> [[Hologram|Holographic]] footage was captured by [[Law enforcement agency|private security]] forces of the [[Guavian Death Gang]] during their operations on Croluthe Zenj.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />
