The '''3PO-series protocol droid''', also known as the '''3PO-series protocol unit''', was a model of [[protocol droid]] produced by [[Cybot Galactica]] sometime prior to the [[Invasion of Naboo]]. The successor to the similar-looking [[TC-series protocol droid]]s, they were equipped with a [[TranLang III communications module]], and as a result were fluent in over 6 million forms of [[Language|communication]]. [[C-3PO]] was an example of this model, though he was rebuilt out of spare parts.

==Description==
{{Quote|I am programmed to understand humans!|C-3PO|<ref name="AOTC" />}}
[[File:ProtocolDroidHead-SW100Objects.png|thumb|left|200px|The 3-PO units head housed its AA-1 VerboBrain.]]
The 3PO-series protocol droid,<ref name="C&M">''[[Cyphers and Masks]]''</ref> also known as the 3PO-series protocol unit,<ref name="Build R2 7">{{BuildR2Cite|7|Droid Directory}}</ref> was a [[class three droid]]<ref name="EV" /> manufactured by [[Cybot Galactica]].<ref name="Rebels Visual Guide" /> Stood at 1.7 [[meter]]s tall<ref name="PD DB" /> and equipped with a [[TranLang III communications module]], the [[protocol droid]] model was fluent in over 6 million forms of [[Language|communication]].<ref name="Rebels Facebook 10-17" /> The 3PO-series was programmed to understand [[human]] beings<ref name="AOTC" /> as per its "human-[[cyborg]] relations" duties.<ref name="ANH" />

The [[TC-series protocol droid]] was a predecessor to the 3PO-series,<ref name="Master of Evil">''[[Master of Evil]]''</ref> and had a similar appearance. Constructed by a young [[Anakin Skywalker]] from spare parts in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], [[C-3PO]] was a 3PO-series droid.<ref name="TPM" /> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="SWT">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> 3PO-series protocol units operating for the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] had [[Color|yellow]] [[photoreceptor]]s and bore blue-[[color]]ed plating with white accents, echoing the color scheme of the [[Senate Guard]]'s [[armor]].<ref name="HoBS">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref> The droid could be purchased from the manufacturer, but a consumer could buy a kit to build it themselves.<ref name="100objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:RebellionAppreciatesYourService-2020StarWars11.png|thumb|right|250px|The 3PO-series droid C-3PO met a predecessor to his line, an Autonomous Translator Module, during the Imperial Era but found the unit to be very rude.]]
3PO-series droids were a modern model of protocol droid that succeeded the likes of the ancient [[Autonomous Translator Module, Mark II]].<ref name="Star Wars 2020 9">[[Star Wars (2020) 9|''Star Wars'' (2020) 9]]</ref> Despite being used as a protocol droid,<ref name="Star Wars 2020 10">[[Star Wars (2020) 10|''Star Wars'' (2020) 10]]</ref> the talky lacked the diplomacy modules seen in later protocol droids. While he knew the talky to be a predecessor to his own model, 3PO unit [[C-3PO]] regarded the talky as an inelegant, blocky design. Indeed, by the time of the 3PO-series, droid design had been streamlined instead of remaining like that of the talky.<ref name="Star Wars 2020 9" /> The [[TC-series protocol droid]] was also a predecessor to the 3PO model.<ref name="Master of Evil" />

3PO droids in the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]] of the [[capital]] planet [[Coruscant]] served as [[aide]]s, carrying drinks for [[senator]]s,<ref name="Senate Murders">{{TCW|Senate Murders}}</ref> including the [[Supreme Chancellor]].<ref name="HoBS" /> During the [[Clone Wars]]' [[Senate hostage crisis]], one such droid occupied the Office Building's [[power control room]], which was the operations center for not only power to the main structure itself, but also the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]]. This crucial waypoint for power supply was targeted by a group of [[bounty hunter]]s led by the infamous [[Cad Bane]]. One of the group's [[IG-86 sentinel droid]] unit, [[HELIOS-3E]], shot the Senatorial protocol droid as they stormed the control room.<ref name="Hostage Crisis">{{TCW|Hostage Crisis}}</ref>

A Senatorial protocol droid trailed after Senator [[Dantum Roohd]] aboard [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]]'s [[starship]], the ''[[Coronet]]'', as it ferried a delegation led by Kryze from the world of [[Mandalore]] to Coruscant.<ref name="D of Mandalore">{{TCW|Duchess of Mandalore}}</ref> At another point, a blue-white model followed Senator [[Mee Deechi]] through the corridors of the Senate Office Building.<ref name="Senate Murders" /> During a meeting with Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] about the [[Confederate–Republic peace initiative]], Supreme Chancellor [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] was supplied a beverage by a Senatorial protocol droid at the [[Emperor's office|Chancellor's Suite]]. [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Vice Chair]] [[Mas Amedda]] were also present, although none consumed the drinks offered.<ref name="HoBS" />
