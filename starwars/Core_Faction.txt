The '''Core Faction''' was a split [[Politics|political]] faction that was active within the [[Galactic Senate|legislative]] assembly of the [[Galactic Republic]] prior to the [[Raxus Address|outbreak]] of the [[Separatist Crisis]], which was organized by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Its members advocated for an increase in centralized government power, such as the establishment of tax-funded programs, which prioritized the Republic civilization's expansion and elevation in a way that organized the rest of [[the galaxy]] similarly to the [[Core Worlds]] region.<ref name="Rise" />

However, the Core Faction's ideals were diametrically opposed by the Republic Senate's [[Rim Faction]], whose members voted against taxation and centralized power in favor of [[star system]]s' rights. Although opposed by the Rim Faction, the Core often saw support from the third [[Militarists]] group, which later became focused on restoring a centralized military for the Republic.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Core Faction was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Core Faction/Legends|Core Faction]] was introduced in ''[[Cloak of Deception]]'', a [[2001]] novel authored by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Cloak">''[[Cloak of Deception]]''</ref> It was later identified in Luceno's [[2012]] novel, [[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']].<ref name="Plagueis">[[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']]</ref>
