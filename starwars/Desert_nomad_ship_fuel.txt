'''Desert nomad ship fuel''' was a type of fuel that could be used for [[starship]]s. It produced a [[Color|pink and blue]] color from the engines of the ship using it. The ship's trail was meant to blend in with the natural shades of a desert [[planet]], making it harder to spot.<ref name="Outlaws" />
