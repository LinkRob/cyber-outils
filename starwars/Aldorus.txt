'''Aldorus''' was an old, brilliant [[Color/Legends|blue]] [[Star/Legends|star]] in the [[Aldorus system]]. Seven [[Planet/Legends|planets]] were in [[Orbit/Legends|orbit]] around it, but only the [[Aldorus IV|fourth]] was inhabited.<ref name="CasusBelli" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aldorus was created by [[Jean Balczesak]] for the ambiguously-[[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] scenario ''[[L'écume de l'espace]]'' which appeared in the French magazine [[Casus Belli 44|''Casus Belli'' 44]].<ref name="CasusBelli" />
