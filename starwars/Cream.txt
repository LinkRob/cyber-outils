'''Cream''' was a [[Foodstuff|dairy]] product which could be added to beverages like [[caf]], [[mocoa]], or [[tea]], or used as an ingredient in [[sauce]]s or baked goods. Beings such as the [[human]]s [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Luke Skywalker]] and the [[Gand]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Zuckuss]] drank it in beverages, while [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] additionally included it in his [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]] recipes. Some beverages served in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]] were topped with flavored whipped cream.

==Description==
Cream was a [[Foodstuff|dairy]] product and food additive which could be used as an ingredient in other foods,<ref name="Cookbook" /> or added to beverages such as [[mocoa]],<ref name="Leia" /> [[tea]],<ref name="Cookbook" /> or [[caf]].<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" />

==History==
In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date: Leia">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] [[Reproduction|birth]] in [[19 BBY]]. The events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' commence just after Organa's sixteenth [[birthday]], which places the events of the novel in [[3 BBY]].</ref> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] and her [[pathfinding]] class visited a [[Pathfinding class chalet|chalet]] after hiking in the [[mountain]]s, and the young [[human]]s in the group enjoyed warming up with oversized mugs of mocoa topped with foamy cream. Citing a saying about muffins always landing with the cream side down, [[Amilyn Holdo]] voiced her own musings about covering a muffin with cream to make an analogy about [[Senator]] [[Winmey Lenz]] ensuring he would benefit from any outcome of a [[quadanium steel]] conflict.<ref name="Leia" />

When the [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]] made an unpalatable batch of caf with the [[automatic brewer]] installed onboard [[Nakari Kelen]]'s ''[[Desert Jewel]]'' [[yacht]], he tried to disguise the taste by adding large amounts of [[sugar]] and cream.<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" /> The [[Gand]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Zuckuss]] ordered a caf with extra cream during an interview with [[Parazeen Parapa]].<ref name="STET!" />

[[Numian cream]] was a [[sauce]] served with [[mealbread]] and meat in the mess halls of [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]]. [[Finn|FN-2187]] thought it tasted like chalk.<ref name="Before the Awakening">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref>

In ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'' by [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]], cream was an ingredient in multiple recipes. It was used in the filling of [[Cavaellin Spice Creams]] and the sauce on top of [[Gormaanda]]'s [[glowblue noodles]]. Tuggs created [[Sunrise Caf]] using fresh cream supplied by the [[Aqualish]] dairy [[farmer]] [[Bubo Wamba]] who farmed [[bantha]]s on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

When made according to Tuggs' recipe, [[Moogan Tea]] was topped with flavored whipped cream.<ref name="Cookbook" /> [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] served the tea that way, along with their version of [[Spiran caf]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina">{{GEAttr|Oga's Cantina}}</ref> [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] and [[Ronto Roasters]] used a [[Color|purple]]-colored version when they served [[Black Spire Hot Chocolate]].<ref name="Docking Bay 7">{{GEAttr|Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo}}</ref><ref name="Ronto Roasters">{{GEAttr|Ronto Roasters}}</ref>
