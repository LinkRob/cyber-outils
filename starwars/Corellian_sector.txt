The '''Corellian sector'''<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> was a rich, industrialized [[sector]]<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> It notably contained the [[Corellian system|Corellia system]] and the [[planet]] [[Neral]].<ref name="Lando's Luck">''[[Lando's Luck]]''</ref> In the peaceful [[Standard century|centuries]] that preceded the [[Clone Wars]], many large and powerful [[warship]]s were built to defend that sector.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the fleets of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] fought above a terrestrial object in the sector. The Rebel fleet consisted of an [[MC80 Liberty Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80 ''Liberty'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]], two [[Assault Frigate Mark II]]s, and two [[MC30c frigate]]s, while the Imperial fleet was composed of an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]], a [[Gladiator-class Star Destroyer|''Gladiator''-class Star Destroyer]], six [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruisers]], and seven [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvettes]].<ref name="Corellian Conflict">{{Armada|set=The Corellian Conflict}}</ref>
