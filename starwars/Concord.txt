The '''''Concord''''' was a [[Starhawk-class Battleship Mark I|''Starhawk''-class Battleship Mark I]] that served in the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] in [[5 ABY]]. It was commanded by [[Commodore]] [[Kyrsta Agate]]. The ship saw service in a [[Skirmish near Wild Space|skirmish]] in [[Wild Space]], the [[Defense of Kuat|Battle for Kuat Drive Yards]], and was incredibly important to [[New Republic]]'s efforts in the [[Battle of Jakku]].

==Description==
The ''Concord'' was the first [[Starhawk-class Battleship Mark I|''Starhawk''-class Battleship Mark I]] that was built by [[Nadiri Dockyards]] following the [[Battle of Endor]]. The ship had a hatchet-shaped front and was built from recycled Imperial warships. The ''Concord'' was classed as a [[battleship]]<ref name="Life Debt" /> and was equipped with a powerful [[tractor beam]] that relied on [[magnite]] crystals as a power source. The magnite crystals amplified the tractor's beam strength and allowed her to drag and carry vessels ten times the ''Concord''{{'s}} size.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==History==
[[File:NadiriDockyardsConceptArt.jpg|thumb|left|210px|''Concord'' was the first Starhawk constructed at the hidden Nadiri Dockyards following the Battle of Endor.]]
Following the Battle of [[Endor]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] transitioned into the New Republic. The Republic's [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] commissioned the building of new warships using disassembled Imperial warships during the last stages of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The ''Concord'' was the first Starhawk built by Nadiri Dockyards and was captained by [[Commodore]] [[Kyrsta Agate]], who had been promoted for her role during the [[rebellion on Akiva]]. The ''Concord'' had white spacious interiors and bright docking bays.<ref name="Life Debt" />

The ''Concord'' and the [[Alderaanian escort frigate]] [[Sunspire (New Republic)|''Sunspire'']] destroyed [[Commander]] [[Valent]]'s [[Star Destroyer]] [[Scythe (Star Destroyer)|''Scythe'']] during a [[Skirmish near Wild Space|skirmish]] near [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Life Debt" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> Commodore Agate then brought the New Republic pilot [[Norra Wexley]] and [[Captain]] [[Wedge Antilles]] aboard the ''Concord'' to reprimand them for embarking on an unauthorized mission to find [[Han Solo]]. Later, the ''Concord'' took part in the New Republic's [[Defense of Kuat|capture]] of the planet [[Kuat]] and [[Kuat Drive Yards]].<ref name="Life Debt" />

[[File:StarhawkRavagerCharge-BattleOfJakkuLastStand4.jpg|thumb|right|220px|''Concord'' charges against the ''Ravager'' above Jakku.]]
During the climactic [[Battle of Jakku]], ''Concord'' served alongside her sister ships ''[[Amity]]'' and [[Unity (Starhawk-class)|''Unity'']] against the Empire above [[Jakku]]. During the battle, after a long New Republic effort, the Imperial starship defensive perimeter around the ''Ravager'' was broken, giving Commodore Agate an opening. In a decisive move, and after a discussion with [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]],<ref name="LastStand4">[[Battle of Jakku — Last Stand 4|''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand'' 4]]</ref> Agate evacuated as many of ''Concord''{{'s}} personnel as possible, and used the battleship to attack [[Counselor to the Empire|Counselor]] [[Gallius Rax]]'s flagship ''[[Ravager]]''. While the ''Concord'' was stricken during an all-out assault on the [[Super Star Destroyer]], her captain Agate managed to use the ship's powerful [[tractor beam projector]]s to drag the ''Ravager'' down to Jakku's surface with it, turning the tide of the battle in favor of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Empire's End" /><ref name="LastStand4" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Concord'' first appeared in [[Chuck Wendig]]'s [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' and its [[2017]] sequel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''.
