A '''cortosis gauntlet''' was a protective [[gauntlet]] made with [[cortosis]] [[metal]]. A [[Sith]] known as "[[the Stranger]]" during the [[High Republic Era]] wore one such gauntlet on his left [[Limb|arm]].<ref name="Stranger DB" /> He utilized this gauntlet in a [[Mission to Khofar|battle]] on Khofar, where it protected him from lightsaber strikes. The gauntlet was later destroyed by [[Yord Fandar]] when the young [[Jedi]] sliced through it with his lightsaber.<ref name="Night">{{Acolyte|Night}}</ref>
