'''2-1B Delta''' was a [[2-1B surgical droid]] who initially belonged to the [[Galactic Empire]]. While working throughout the military [[prison]] system, he developed an aversion to the damage done to organic flesh by Imperial weapons, which eventually prompted him to flee. Once free, the droid took on the "Delta" moniker to show he was not merely a cog in the system.<ref name="Delta" />

==Biography==
2-1B Delta was a [[2-1B surgical droid]]<ref name="Delta" /> manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]].<ref name="HC58" /> The [[medical droid]] was one of millions used by the [[Galactic Empire]], working throughout its military [[prison]] system. The [[droid]] eventually fled from the Empire<ref name="Delta" /> while working in the [[Anoat sector]]'s prison system.<ref name="HC58" /> Taking on the Delta moniker, he<ref name="Delta" /> worked with active rebel cells in the [[sector]] following the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="HC58" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> During this period, the Anoat sector was locked down by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] in the [[Iron Blockade]] in order to prevent the occupants from learning of the Empire's defeat at [[Endor]] and the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Delta" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
Several [[Standard month|months]] into the [[blockade]], which ended in [[5 ABY]], 2-1B Delta joined the crew of [[the Smuggler]], a young member of an [[Uprising]] forming in the sector. The [[smuggler]] dispatched the medical droid on a variety of missions throughout the sector.<ref name="Delta" />
{{EndGame}}

==Characteristics==
2-1B Delta had [[Color|gray]] plating, yellow [[sensor]]s,<ref name="Delta" /> and stood 1.77 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="charencupdated" /> The droid developed a severe distaste for the damage done to organic flesh by the Empire's weapons while working in the prison system, causing him to flee. He took on the Delta moniker in order to show he was more than just another worker drone in the system.<ref name="Delta" />

==Behind the scenes==
2-1B Delta was created for the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was developed by [[Kabam]].<ref name="Delta" />
