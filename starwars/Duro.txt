'''Duro''' was a temperate [[planet]] in the [[Duro system]] of the [[Core Worlds]]'s [[Duro sector]]. The planet was located in grid square M-11 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], and was situated along the [[Corellian Trade Spine]]. The planet was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Duros]] [[species]], including the duro [[Bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]], who spent his childhood in a crime-ridden [[Unidentified city (Cad Bane)|city]] on Duro with his friend [[Niro]].

Duro was a [[Temperature|temperate]]<ref name="Duro" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Cad Bane">{{Databank|cad-bane|Cad Bane}}</ref> located in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Duro" /> It was positioned at the [[Galactic coordinates|coordinates]] M-11<ref name="TFABG" /> of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Rebel Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref> located along the [[Corellian Trade Spine]].<ref name="TFABG" /> Duro had at least one [[Unidentified city (Cad Bane)|city]], where the [[Bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] and [[gender|his]] friend [[Niro]] grew up.<ref name="The Good Life" /><ref name="DurounderworldID" />

==History==
{{Quote|The legal system is what it is but none of that means he's coming here."<br />"You sure about that? 'Cause there's a shuttle from Torano due at noon. If I were Bane, I know where I'd be headed.|[[Deputy]] [[Vance]] and [[Marshal]] Niro, at the [[Unidentified police station|local police station]]|<ref name="One Good Deed" />}}
[[File:Tinnel-SWT.jpg|left|250px|thumb|Duro was located along the Corellian Trade Spine]]
At some point before [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref> the [[Lost King of Duro]] ruled as [[king]] of Duro.<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> It shared its name with the Core Worlds [[Duro sector]].<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>

Sometime during the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Pablo-Jill]] had an intense [[Duel over Duro|duel]] with [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] in a collapsing [[Unidentified satellite city|satellite city]] over Duro. The [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] left Pablo-Jill injured, and Grievous claimed [[Pablo-Jill's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] from the debris.<ref name="Lightsabers" /> At another point during the war, [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] informed her [[Wedding of Anakin Skywalker and Padmé Amidala|husband]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] that she was traveling to Duro, when in reality she was [[Mission to Clabron|traveling]] to the neutral world of [[Clabron]] to try and bring them into the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Age of Republic" />

After the new rule of the [[Galactic Empire]] was [[Proclamation of the New Order|declared]], Duro was one of the first Core Worlds to fall under Imperial control. Many [[Duros]] joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] after finding themselves as fugitives from their [[homeworld]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Inhabitants==
Duro was the homeworld of the Duros [[species]],<ref name="Duro" /> including the infamous [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]], who was active during the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars.<ref name="Cad Bane" /> Thanks to the predators on Duro, the Duros evolved so that a foul smelling oil with an awful taste would fill up along their ribs during times of great strain.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

==Locations==
A [[Satellite city|satellite]] [[city]] was located in [[orbit]] of Duro.<ref name="Lightsabers" />

==Behind the scenes==
Duro was first mentioned in the [[Hostage Crisis]] featurette in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season One|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season One]] blu-ray and DVD boxed set released in [[2009]].<ref name="Feature">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season One|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season One]]</ref> Duro eventually first appeared, albeit unnamed, in "[[The Good Life]]," the fourth episode of the [[Disney+]] animated anthology series ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Underworld]]'',<ref name="The Good Life" /> which aired on [[May 4]], [[2025]].<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/tales-of-the-underworld-announce|text=Two Favorite ''Star Wars'' Bounty Hunters are Back for ''Tales of the Underworld''|date=2025-04-02}}</ref> It simultaneously appeared in the following episodes, "[[A Good Turn]]"<ref name="A Good Turn">{{TOTU|A Good Turn}}</ref> and "[[One Good Deed]],"<ref name="One Good Deed" /> and was identified as Duro in [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]].<ref name="DurounderworldID" />
