A '''holocron''' was kept in the [[Jedi Archives]] and contained a series of fragmentary recordings made by [[Jedi Master]] [[Barnabas Vim]] for his [[Padawan]], [[Vix Fonnick]] after he left to search for the origin of the [[Echo Stone]]s. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Reath Silas]] and Padawan [[Amadeo Azzazzo]] watched the recordings in the [[holocron]] while they were searching for the [[Sophros|origin]] of the Echo Stones.<ref name="Echoes of Fear 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
The holocron appeared in the [[The High Republic Adventures – Echoes of Fear 2|second issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Echoes of Fear]]'', written by [[George Mann]], illustrated by [[Vincenzo Federici]] and [[Juan Samu]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Echoes of Fear 2" /> on [[October 9]], [[2024]]<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3014-601/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures--Echoes-of-Fear-2|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures--Echoes of Fear #2}}</ref> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref>
