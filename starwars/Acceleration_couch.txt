'''Acceleration couches''', also known as '''flight couches '''or '''g-couches''' were a type of furniture that were built into some [[starship]]s and [[escape pod]]s. They were made to help lessen the effects of gravity on the user when they were strapped into one. The [[freighter]] ''[[Moonglow-72]]'' had an acceleration couch installed facing the [[airlock]] in its main cabin; however, the [[Bartender|barkeeper]] and frequent passenger of the vessel [[Okadiah Garson]] preferred to sleep on shelving further back in the vessel, as it was quieter during flights.<ref name="AND">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref> [[Nien Nunb]]'s space [[yacht]] ''[[Mellcrawler]]'' had an acceleration couch that [[Lokmarcha]] and [[Kidi Aleri]] cuddled on during their trip to [[Yellow Moon]].<ref name="MT">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref>

The [[YT-1300 light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was also equipped with an acceleration couch in its cabin.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
