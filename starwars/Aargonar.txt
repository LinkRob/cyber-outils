'''Aargonar''' was a [[planet]] located in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Timelines" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Mina Bonteri's husband|husband]] of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Senator]] [[Mina Bonteri]] went there to set up a [[Aargonar Separatist base|base]] for the Separatists, but was [[Death|killed]] when [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s [[Attack on Aargonar|attacked]].<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> The planet was also the [[homeworld]] of the [[human]] [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] [[TK-14057]].<ref name="SWROUVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aargonar was first mentioned in the ''[[Rebellion Era Sourcebook]]'', a [[2001]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] supplement that was written by [[Bill Slavicsek]], [[Steve Miller]], and [[Owen K.C. Stephens]] for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Rebellion Era Sourcebook">''[[Rebellion Era Sourcebook]]''</ref> It became [[canon]] when it was mentioned in "[[Heroes on Both Sides]]," the tenth episode of the canon animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]],<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> which originally aired on [[November 19]], [[2010]].<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides air date">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode310.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref>
