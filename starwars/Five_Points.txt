'''Five Points''' was a [[space station]] located in the [[Five Points system]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It sat in the [[Star system|system's]] center between the [[planet]]s [[Rumitaka]] and [[Satotai]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> Around [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Mission to Tamsye Prime]] to [[5 BBY]]. Around one year, [[Jyn Erso]] ended up at Five Points. Thus, this occurred around [[4 BBY]].</ref> the station had an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] presence maintained by [[Commander]] [[Lucka Solange]]. Despite this, the Empire saw nothing truly of value there, and local [[gambling]] lords such as [[Allehander Pso]] maintained much of the influence on the station.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

Five Points was a tourist destination, filled with gambling halls as well as other locations of decadence. It also had residential cubes, an inn, a park, public restrooms, a bodega, and other shops, including a shop operated by a [[Unidentified Kath shopkeeper|Kath]] that sold and repaired weapons. It had a thriving black market and was welcoming to [[bounty hunter]]s looking for jobs. In contrast to the overflowing wealth of some of the gambling lords, there remained many beggars and homeless residents.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

The station operated in a twelve-[[Standard hour|hour]] cycle, during which work shifts would change. The end of a cycle would be indicated by the lights of the station all blinking.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
