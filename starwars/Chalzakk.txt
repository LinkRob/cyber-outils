'''Chalzakk''' was an individual on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> At one point during the year, the [[human]] [[Ju'syl]] gossiped about Chalzakk being in deep with the [[Pyke Syndicate]] to their fellow gamblers [[Avrar]] and [[Torai]] while playing [[Kessel Sabacc]] in the [[Crimson Dawn District (Kijimi)|Crimson Dawn district]] in [[Kijimi City]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chalzakk was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
