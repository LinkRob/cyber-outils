'''Chespea''' was a [[forest]] [[Planet|world]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Rayter sector]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], it was the location of a [[Chespea Temple|Jedi temple]]. In [[230 BBY]], the [[Nihil]] [[Attack on the Jedi temple at Chespea|attacked]] the [[Jedi temple|temple]], destroying it and all the [[Jedi]] within and shocking the [[Jedi Order]] with their blunt anti-Jedi tactics. The event, coordinated with an [[Attack on Banchii|attack]] on the planet [[Banchii]], drew [[Galactic Republic]] forces away from their [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] and caused [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] to realize that the Nihil still posed a serious threat. During the [[Imperial Era]], Chespea was mentioned on a list of Jedi locations in the possession of [[Jedi training|aspiring]] Jedi [[Luke Skywalker]].

==Description==
Chespea was a thickly [[forest]]ed, terrestrial [[Galactic Republic]] [[planet]]<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> located in the [[Berenge sector]] [[Sector|subsector]] of the [[Rayter sector]]'s [[Chespea system]], which was in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="WesternReaches" /> It was situated in grid square J-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Chespea was positioned several [[hyperspace]] jumps away from<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Eiram system]].<ref name="The Fallen Star" />

==History==
===Nihil attack===
{{Quote|We need to set aide the question of Chespea for now.|Elzar Mann|<ref name="The Fallen Star" />}}
In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Chespea Temple|temple]] on Chespea was headed by [[Jedi Master]] [[Imgree]]. The [[Jedi Knight]]s of the [[Jedi temple|temple]] were more independent than on other worlds; Imgree had never contacted the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the planet [[Coruscant]] in her life. However, [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] arrived above the planet to [[Attack on the Jedi temple at Chespea|destroy]] the Chespea Temple below in order to send a message to the [[Jedi Order]] that they would show no mercy toward them.<ref name="The Fallen Star" />

[[File:Lina-Soh-HS-THR.png|thumb|left|280px|The attack on Chespea dismayed Chancellor Soh (pictured).]]
The attack was coordinated along with a similar [[Attack on Banchii|attack]] on<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> the planet<ref name="Chronicles" /> [[Banchii]], with the Nihil trying to draw support away from the Republic's [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] as the worlds were several hyperspace jumps away from it. Imgree had her [[Imgree's Padawan|Padawan]] contact Coruscant for the first [[time]] to inform them not of the danger but of their own demise, barely getting it done before the temple was destroyed<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> and all [[Jedi]] present were [[Death|killed]].<ref name="Chronicles" /> Around one hundred other beings who lived on Chespea saw the Nihil [[starship]]s arrive above them, but since the Nihil had no interest in them, they were spared.<ref name="The Fallen Star" />

The news of Chespea reached the desk of [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]], who was dismayed and realized that the Nihil were still a serious threat as a result. The [[Jedi High Council]] responded by sending Jedi to Chespea to investigate the destruction, and by making sure that other temples were aware of the attack and were prepared with defenses in case they were next. [[Stellan Gios]], the Jedi [[marshal]] of Starlight Beacon later learned that there was coordination between the attacks on Chespea and Banchii, grasping that the choice of worlds was not random. Shortly after, Jedi Master [[Elzar Mann]] was forced to deprioritize Chespea, setting aside those who had fallen to instead focus on taking care of Starlight Beacon's issues: he felt that they should focus on [[refugee]]s from Nihil attacks who were staying on the station, while [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] was more concerned with a [[Shrikarai|disturbance]] in [[the Force]] that the Jedi had been [[Force sense|sensing]].<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> When discussing Nihil attacks within the publication ''[[Chronicles of the Jedi]]''{{'s}} seventh chapter in a section dedicated to the [[Eiram cyclone disaster|crisis]] at the world [[Eiram]] and abroad<ref name="Chronicles" /> in [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Chronicles Date">{{ChroniclesoftheJediDate}}</ref> Jedi Master [[Harli Cogra]] mentioned the attack on Chespea.<ref name="Chronicles" />

===Skywalker's search===
{{Quote|This is a list the ''Imperials'' made. It's hard to think there would be anything left to find at ''any'' of these places. They'd have destroyed them or taken everything worth taking.|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="SW19">[[Star Wars (2020) 19|''Star Wars'' (2020) 19]]</ref>}}
[[Standard century|Centuries]] later, at some point between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">[[Star Wars (2020) 19|''Star Wars'' (2020) 19]] takes place in between {{Film|V}} and {{Film|VI}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively.</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] and [[Jedi training|aspiring]] Jedi [[Luke Skywalker]] had acquired an [[Aurebesh]] list of possible Jedi locations from the [[Galactic Empire]], whom he assumed had destroyed and ransacked all of them. The list included Chespea, which was crossed out.<ref name="SW19" />

==Inhabitants==
The Chespea Temple housed Jedi Knights and at least one Jedi Master and Padawan. An additional one hundred individuals of various [[species]] lived nearby, having arrived to the world via battered craft. [[Bird]]s also lived on Chespea.<ref name="The Fallen Star" />

==Locations==
Chespea was home to the Jedi Order's Chespea Temple.<ref name="The Fallen Star" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chespea was first mentioned in a [[hologram]] in the [[Star Wars (2020) 19|nineteenth issue]] of [[Marvel Comics]]' [[Star Wars (2020)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]], which was written by [[Charles Soule]]<ref name="SW19" /> and published on [[December 8]], [[2021]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/89714/star_wars_2020_19|text=Star Wars (2020) #19}}</ref> The planet later made its first appearance in the [[2022]] novel [[The High Republic: The Fallen Star|''The Fallen Star'']], authored by [[Claudia Gray]]<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
