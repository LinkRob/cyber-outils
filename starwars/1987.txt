A list of events in '''1987''' related to ''Star Wars''.

==January==
*[[January 9|09]] &ndash; [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] opens at [[Disneyland]].
*[[January 10|10]] &ndash; [[Battle for the Sunstar|The last episode]] of [[Ewoks (television series)|''Star Wars: Ewoks'']] aired.
*[[January 13|13]] &ndash; [[Droids (1986) 7|''Droids'' 7]] published.
*[[January 18|18]] &ndash; [[Ingvild Deila]] is born.
*[[January 30|30]] &ndash; [[Valene Kane]] is born.

*[[March 31|31]] &ndash; [[Ewoks (1985) 14|''Ewoks'' (1985) 14]] published.

==April==
*[[April 7|07]] &ndash; [[Ismael Cruz Cordova]] is born.
*[[April 17|17]] &ndash; [[Griffin McElroy]] is born.

==May==
*[[May 7|07]] &ndash; [[Maya Erskine]] is born.
*[[May 12|12]] &ndash; ''[[The Star Wars Trilogy]]'' published.
*[[May 23|23]] &ndash; [[Starlog Salutes Star Wars|Star Wars 10th anniversary convention]] held in Los Angeles, CA.

==June==
*[[June 6|06]] &ndash; [[Daniel Logan]] is born.
*[[June 7|07]] &ndash; [[Daniel Scheinert]] is born.
*[[June 24|24]] &ndash; ''[[Star Wars]]'' parody ''[[Wikipedia:Spaceballs|Spaceballs]]'' is released.
*[[June 26|26]] &ndash; [[Zoraida Córdova]] is born.

==July==

*11 &ndash; [[Cristina Valenzuela]] is born.

==August==
*[[August 5|05]] &ndash; [[Adrian Petriw]] is born.

==September==
*[[September 4|04]] &ndash; [[Richard Marquand]] dies.
*[[September 23|23]] &ndash; [[Joe Kennard]] is born.
*[[September 24|24]] &ndash; ''[[Ewoks: The Ewok Fun Colouring Book]]'' published.
*24 &ndash; ''[[Sun Star Against Shadow Stone]]'' published.
*24 &ndash; [[To Save Deej (book)|''To Save Deej'']] published.
*24 &ndash; [[Wicket's Wagon (book)|''Wicket's Wagon'']] published.

==October==
*[[October 1|01]] &ndash; [[Michaela Coel]] is born.
*[[October 3|03]] &ndash; [[Mousy McCallum]] is born.
*?? &ndash; [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game (first edition)|''Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game'']] is published by [[West End Games]].
*?? &ndash; [[Star Warriors: Starfighter Combat in the Star Wars Universe|''Star Warriors'']], a board game of starfighter-to-starfighter dogfighting in the ''Star Wars'' universe, is published by [[West End Games]].

==November==
*[[November 7|07]] &ndash; [[C.B. Lee]] is born.
*?? &ndash; ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'' is published for the first time by [[West End Games]].
