'''Cybotaro waxen glass''' was a type of [[glass]] used in the making of [[Sith wayfinder]]s.<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> It was a pliable form of glass that allowed data cables from a [[navigation computer]] to be inserted through it at specific points.<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
