'''Fire Team Three''' was a [[Coruscant Rescue Ops]] [[Squad|team]] that was active during the [[Battle of Coruscant]]. When the ''[[Invisible Hand]]'' plummeted towards [[Coruscant]]'s surface, Fire Team Three was ordered to intercept the wrecked hull with their [[tractor beam]]s and extinguish the flames.<ref name="HC 28" />
