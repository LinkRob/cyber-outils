'''Aayla Secura''' {{Pronounced|file=AaylaSecuraJediCrash.flac|'eɪlʌ sə'kʊrə}}, a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Rutian]] [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]], was a [[Jedi Master]] during the waning years of the [[Galactic Republic]], serving with distinction as a [[Jedi General]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Prior to the start of the war, Secura learned the ways of [[the Force]] as the [[Padawan]] of [[Jedi]] Master [[Quinlan Vos]], being awarded the rank of [[Jedi Knight]] sometime later. With the outbreak of civil war between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in [[22 BBY]], Secura and her fellow Jedi supported the Republic by becoming [[officer]]s within the newly-formed [[Grand Army of the Republic]]; Secura received the rank of [[general]] and took command of the [[327th Star Corps]], [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Bly]] serving as her second-in-command. 

Throughout the conflict, Secura fought alongside her [[soldier]]s in multiple battles across [[the galaxy]], from [[First Battle of Geonosis|Geonosis]] to [[Battle of Quell|Quell]], [[Battle of Coruscant|Coruscant]], and [[Battle of Felucia (Outer Rim Sieges)|Felucia]]. As the war drew to an end, the clones were instructed by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], secretly the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious, to execute [[Order 66]], resulting in a sudden [[Great Jedi Purge|purge]] of the [[Jedi Order]] at the hands of their own [[trooper]]s. As a result, Secura was among the first casualties of the purge, having been shot in the back repeatedly by Bly and several other clone troopers.

The [[Death|demise]] of Secura and many other Jedi was sensed through the Force by [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], who survived the purge and ultimately escaped into [[Exile (punishment)|exile]], while the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|rose to power]] in place of the Republic. Secura's death was also witnessed in a [[Force vision]] by [[Depa Billaba]] and her apprentice, [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]], on [[Kaller]]. Decades after her [[death]], Secura's voice joined those of her fellow fallen Jedi offering inspiration to [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] in her climactic [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] against her grandfather, [[Darth Sidious]], on [[Exegol]].

==Biography==
===Early years===

[[File:AnzatiAaylaSecura-HelmetCollection3.png|left|thumb|250px|Aayla Secura once had an encounter with an Anzati.]]
Aayla Secura was a [[Rutian]] [[Twi'lek]] who was [[Reproduction|born]] on the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] during the [[Republic Era]]. Secura came from the disreputable side of her [[homeworld]], until she was rescued by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Quinlan Vos]] who brought the [[Force-sensitive]] [[youngling]] to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. Vos personally saw to Secura's [[Jedi training|training]] in the [[Jedi Order]], taking her as his [[Padawan]].<ref name="Aayla SWE" /> Secura was close with her mentor during their [[time]] together<ref name="JC" /> and under Vos' guidance, she ascended to the rank of [[Jedi]] Knight.<ref name="Aayla SWE" /> She was also an associate of [[Kit Fisto]] and [[Tholme]],<ref name="Aayla Jedi Hunt">{{Topps|set=2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|subset=Jedi Hunt|cardname=Aayla Secura|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/117713/cid/8023098/2015-Topps-Chrome-Star-Wars-Perspectives-Jedi-vs.-Sith---Jedi-Hunt-5-Aayla-Secura}}</ref> who had trained Vos.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> Secura was also considered a [[Jedi Vanguard]].<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>

At some point, Secura's [[Aayla Secura's uncle|uncle]] was [[Death|murdered]]. In rage, Secura nearly fell to the [[dark side of the Force]] by launching a hunt for the man who killed her uncle. The guidance of [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] and her own courage, however,<ref name="Aayla Jedi Card">{{Topps|set=2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|subset=Jedi Temple Archives|cardname=Aayla Secura|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/114595/cid/8022611/2015-Topps-Chrome-Star-Wars-Perspectives-Jedi-vs.-Sith-7-J-Aayla-Secura}}</ref> led her back to the [[light side of the Force]].<ref name="Aayla Sith Card">{{Topps|set=2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|subset=Sith Information Guide|cardname=Aayla Secura|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/114595/cid/8022661/2015-Topps-Chrome-Star-Wars-Perspectives-Jedi-vs.-Sith-7-S-Aayla-Secura}}</ref> Secura once encountered an [[Anzati]] who was dressed in robes<ref name="HC 3">{{HelmetCollectionCite|3|Databank A-Z}}</ref> that resembled those of a Jedi or Sith.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> The Anzati took hold of her head and neck,<ref name="HC 3" /> but she survived.<ref name="Episode III" /> At some point, Secura trained with the [[Mirialan]] who later became the [[Seventh Sister]].<ref name="Rise Blade">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref>

===Clone Wars===
====Battle of Geonosis====
[[File:AaylaSecura-TSWB.png|thumb|right|200px|Secura during the Battle of Geonosis]]
Secura was on Coruscant during the last [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Separatist Crisis]] in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Jedi Knight [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] walked past her as he made his way to a class of [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]], intending to confer with their instructor, [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], about his investigation into the [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|plot to assassinate]] [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. Following Kenobi's discovery of the [[Separatist Droid Army]] on [[Geonosis]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] assembled a [[Jedi assault team|strike team]] of all available Jedi, including Secura. The Jedi infiltrated the [[Petranaki Arena]] during the attempted execution of Kenobi as well as his apprentice, [[Anakin Skywalker]], and Senator Amidala.<ref name="Episode II" />

Igniting her [[Aayla Secura's lightsaber|blue-bladed lightsaber]], Secura and her fellow Jedi fought against waves of [[battle droid]]s in the [[Geonosian]] arena. Though the Jedi succeeded in destroying many [[droid]]s, with Secura dispatching a [[B2-series super battle droid]], the Jedi were overwhelmed while trying to defend the sheer number of droids and [[blaster]] fire. Secura and her comrades were herded into the center of the arena where they formed a defensive circle. [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]], a former Jedi who assumed command of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], signaled his forces to [[Death|kill]] the remaining Jedi. However, with the arrival of Yoda and the newly-formed [[Grand Army of the Republic]], Secura and her allies managed to escape aboard several [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]]s before their execution could be carried out.<ref name="Episode II" />

[[File:Aayla-Secura-unit.png|thumb|left|300px|Secura led her own unit of clone troopers during the battle.]]
The [[First Battle of Geonosis]] commenced with the [[Army|armies]] of the Confederacy and the [[Galactic Republic]] pitted against each other on the field of Geonosis.<ref name="Episode II" /> Given charge of her own [[Aayla Secura's unit|unit]],<ref name="Ahsoka and Others" /> Secura fought on the front lines along with Windu, [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], [[Kit Fisto]], and others and led [[clone trooper]]s in a direct assault that ultimately forced the [[Confederacy military]] into full retreat. While the efforts of Secura, her fellow Jedi, and the [[Cloning|clones]] secured the first victory for the Republic, Yoda sensed the rise of the [[dark side of the Force]], noting that the [[Clone Wars]] had begun.<ref name="Episode II" /> Due to Secura's efforts on Geonosis, Secura was given her own clone corps, the [[327th Star Corps]], as well as her own [[Aayla Secura's task force|fleet group]], Clone Commander [[Bly]] assigned as her second-in-command.<ref name="HC 73">{{HelmetCollectionCite|73|Helmets}}</ref> She helped bring about several sneak attacks that the Confederacy claimed made neutral peoples hate her, with the Confederacy painting Secura as a violent war criminal in a citizen's alert.<ref name="Aayla Jedi Hunt" />

====Mission to Escander====
During the [[Clone Wars]], in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> after Quinlan Vos discovered intelligence about a new [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[droid]], Secura, Bly, and the [[327th Star Corps]] were sent to the muddy [[planet]] of [[Escander]], where the Separatists had a base. After arriving, the droids attacked them resulting in Bly being wounded, because of his injuries another clone trooper took command of the unit. Secura ordered the clone to stay put, and she went alone to the Separatist base. After a long radio silence, the clone contacted her, but accidentally caused the droids to detect her. The clone, having ignored the command, went into the base looking to help Secura. During the search he found evidence Secura's capture in a research lab. Eventually the clone discovered Secura being interrogated by [[Asajj Ventress]], who revealed that they leaked the intelligence to lure and capture [[Jedi]]. At the end, the clone distracted Ventress by activating the remaining droids and freed Secura. The two escaped the base safely.<ref name="Clone Trooper Mission">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Clone Trooper Mission]]''</ref>

====Battle of Quell====
{{Quote|Are all Jedi this reckless?"<br />"Just the good ones.|Admiral Yularen and Aayla Secura during the battle of Quell|<ref name="JC" />}}
[[FIle:Aayla Secura SWZ Jake Murray.png|thumb|250px|right|Secura was in command of the Republic Forces at Quell.]]
Later the same year,<ref name="Timelines" /> Secura and her [[Aayla Secura's task force|fleet]] came under [[Battle of Quell|attack at Quell]]. Damaged in the battle, her flagship, the [[Liberty (Venator-class)|''Liberty'']], began falling through the atmosphere. The situation was made worse when [[B2-RP battle droid|rocket droids]] started boarding her ship. She and Bly managed to fight them off until [[Anakin Skywalker]], his apprentice [[Ahsoka Tano]], and [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Rex]] arrived to rescue her and Bly. Secura and the others then fought their way to Skywalker's frigate. However, en route, Secura and the others were pushed out of the way of an explosion by Skywalker, who tried to hold it back with the Force. However, it was too strong for him and he was flung against the bulkhead and badly injured. Secura then helped Tano get Skywalker aboard the frigate. As they tried to dock with the ''[[Resolute]]'', the hyperdrive was activated when the ship was struck by laser fire and Secura ordered [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen|Yularen]] to detach. She then ordered Tano to assist Bly in flying the frigate before it could collide with one of the stars. Secura then ordered the power to be turned off, despite the risk to Skywalker's life. However, during the chaos, she was pushed away from the switch but was able to [[Telekinesis|use the Force]] to turn the power back on. The ship then crashed on the planet [[Maridun]].<ref name="JC" />

====Defense of Maridun====
{{Quote|I am sure you are aware that the Jedi did not initiate the Clone Wars. Our only intention is to end it and restore peace to our galaxy.''"<br />"''What difference does it make who ''started'' the war and who only wants to ''end'' it?|Aayla Secura and Tee Watt Kaa|<ref name="JC" />}}
[[File:Epguide014.png|thumb|left|250px|Secura fighting on Maridun]]
After the crash, Secura then decided to find help and ordered Tano to accompany her, Bly, [[Cameron]], [[Lucky]], and [[Flash]] to do so. En route, Secura began sensing Tano's doubt and confusion. Worried about her master, she started questioning the Jedi philosophy concerning attachments. Secura assured her that it was nothing to be ashamed of as she went through the same thing with her own master, but in time she learnt to let go. Secura and her group then came under attack by [[Mastiff phalone]]s that killed three of her troopers. She, Tano, and Bly then found a [[Lurmen village|village]] of [[Lurmen]]. There, Secura asked the village elder [[Tee Watt Kaa]] for aid but he refused to help them. However, upon hearing of their friends' plight, he allowed his son to go with Tano and Bly to treat them as long as Secura stayed at the village. Secura and Tee Watt Kaa began discussing the Jedi's involvement in the war and how she believed it was their intention not to spark conflict but to restore peace to the galaxy.<ref name="JC" />

[[File:Jedi_and_clones.png|thumb|right|200px|Aayla Secura with Ahsoka Tano and the clone troopers.]]
Secura, Skywalker, Tano, Rex, and Bly were then forced to leave the village when Separatist General [[Lok Durd]] arrived and searched the village for contraband. Secura destroyed a [[Ringneck recon droid|scout droid]] and found Durd's base. There she witnessed Durd's secret weapon, the [[Defoliator]] [[Defoliator Deployment Tank|tank]], and rescued Bly from its effects. Despite Tee Watt Kaa's wishes, Secura and Skywalker decide to steal Durd's shuttle and stop him from using it on the Lurmen village. Secura and Tano snuck into the base, destroyed the droids and took the shield generators. Secura and the others made their way back to the village and set up a barrier and shield around its periphery, despite Tee Watt Kaa's objections. She and the others fought against Durd's Squad until Skywalker disabled Durd and his weapon. With the Lurmen finally deciding to defend their village, they along with the remainder of the republic forces were able to destroy the rest of the Separatist droid army. Secura then took Durd and the Defoliator's gunner into custody aboard their shuttle just as the Republic fleet arrived.<ref name="DoP" />

====Later conflicts====
{{Quote|We aren't having much of an effect!|Secura regarding her efforts to distract the Zillo Beast|<ref name="TZBSB">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast Strikes Back}}</ref>}}
[[File:SecuraYodaZilloBeast.png|thumb|left|250px|Secura and Yoda distract the Zillo Beast]]
Later in the year,<ref name="Timelines" /> Secura accepted a post on Coruscant besides high-ranking Jedi members such as Master Yoda and Windu.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Secura was in the Jedi Temple's communications center when Cad Bane [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|infiltrated the Temple]] to steal a Holocron.<ref name="HH">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref> Secura then aided Yoda in distracting the [[Zillo Beast]] while Skywalker worked on a plan to save himself, the Chancellor, Senator Amidala, C-3PO, and R2-D2. After realizing that their efforts weren't amounting to much, Yoda and Secura leapt off the beast and joined the others as it was assailed with gas bombs, holding the cloud back with the Force to protect themselves.<ref name="TZBSB" />

At some point, Secura joined Luminara Unduli on [[Alzoc III]] in a [[Confrontation on Alzoc III|confrontation]] with the [[Sith assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]].<ref name="Force Users">{{EncyclopediaCite|Mace Windu and Other Users of the Force}}</ref> Later, in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Secura and her [[astromech droid]] [[QT-KT]] were sent on a [[Mission to the Secret Research Facility|mission]] to the [[Secret Research Facility]] on an [[Unidentified unmapped moon|unmapped moon]]. According to a Separatist source, the Geonosians were involved in "[[Death Star|something big]]" at the facility. She quickly defeated several battle droids in the facility, before stealthily jumping down to a monitoring platform. QT-KT extracted the monitor's data in a disc, but the disc was stolen by Asajj Ventress, who then Force pulled the astromech towards her. Secura attempted to pull the droid back to her, resulting in QT-KT's head disattaching. Ventress fled, activating the facility's self-destruct beam, before engaging with Secura in a [[Lightsaber combat|lightsaber duel]]. Ventress mocked Secura's adherence to the [[Jedi Code]], stating that they kept Secura from truly matching her. As facility debris started to fall onto the Geonosian workers, Secura disengaged from the duel and held the debris up with the Force, telling the workers to flee to the tunnels. Ventress mocked Secura's empathy, stating that it made her as vulnerable as a baby [[tooka]], but refrained from killing her, as she needed the Jedi to report back to the [[Jedi Council|Council]] that the facility was destroyed. Secura managed to escape the facility with QT-KT's head. Through [[hologram]], she reported the mission to Masters [[Yoda]] and [[Mace Windu]], lamenting that Ventress had stolen the data. However, both Masters reassured her that she fought with honor and saved lives.<ref name="SWA12">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=12|story=Tales of Villainy: Give & Take}}</ref>

Sometime during the war, Secura and [[Jedi High Council]] member [[Shaak Ti]] partnered with one another to [[Prisoner|seal away]] a [[Starweird]], an ancient creature, in the [[Jedi]] [[prison]] known as the [[Sason Temple]] on [[Sason]].<ref name="Aphra 33">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 33|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 33]]</ref> Between [[20 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Quell card date note">{{Destiny|set=Way of the Force|cardname=Quell|link=http://swdestinydb.com/card/07150}} shows the [[327th Star Corps]] with [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], which makes its first chronological appearance in "[[Water War]]," while [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] establishes that [[armor]] was used for the rest of the [[Clone Wars]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of "Water War" to [[20 BBY]] and those of ''Revenge of the Sith'' to [[19 BBY]], the events pictured on the Quell card must occur between those two [[Standard year|years]].</ref> Secura returned to Quell with the 327th and led them into a fight on the planet's surface.<ref name="Quell card">{{Destiny|set=Way of the Force|cardname=Quell|link=http://swdestinydb.com/card/07150}}</ref> Secura's astromech droid was chosen to join [[D-Squad]] for a special mission. She dropped off QT for the mission briefing.<ref name="Secret Weapons">{{TCW|Secret Weapons}}</ref>

Secura attended the [[Funeral of the Jedi Temple bombing victims|funeral service]] for the victims of the Jedi Temple's [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|hangar bombing]] in 19 BBY.<ref name="Timelines" /><ref name="TJWKTM">{{TCW|The Jedi Who Knew Too Much}}</ref>

[[File:SecuraMandalorianHelmet-SoD3.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Secura's discovery of a Mandalorian helmet on Ord Mantell]]
Later the same year,<ref name="Timelines" /> Secura went with Masters Windu, Kenobi, [[Tiplee]], and [[Commander]] [[Wolffe]] to investigate a recent [[Battle on Ord Mantell|battle]] on [[Ord Mantell]]. Secura informed her fellow Jedi Masters that Wolffe had discovered a damaged [[Mandalorian]] helmet at the battle site. She and Master Windu later arrived on a [[Vizsla Keep 09|Mandalorian asteroid outpost]] to assist Masters Kenobi and Tiplee in [[Assault on Vizsla Keep 09|fighting]] [[Maul]] and [[Count]] Dooku. Maul kicked Secura as [[Mandalorian super commando]]s [[Gar Saxon]] and [[Rook Kast]] approached the battle. Saxon ordered Kast to shoot a rocket at Secura, knocking both her and Windu out while Maul escaped. As Secura and Windu recovered, the latter received a message from Chancellor Palpatine.<ref name="SoD 3">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 3|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 3]]</ref>

Towards the end of the Clone Wars, Secura had achieved the rank of Jedi Master.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

====Battle of Felucia and death====
{{Quote|Execute Order 66.|Darth Sidious ordering the deaths of the Jedi, including Aayla Secura|audio=ExecuteOrder66.flac|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:AaylaSecuraEta2-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|Secura pilots her Eta-2 interceptor against a HMP droid gunship.]]
During the final days of the war, in 19 BBY,<ref name="Timelines" /> Secura and Clone Commander Bly fought against the Separatist forces on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet of [[Felucia]].<ref name="Episode III" /> Initially, Secura was to receive reinforcements from Jedi Master [[Stass Allie]], who was preparing to leave the capital on a mission to fulfill, but she was transferred to oversee [[Battle of Saleucami|clean-up operations]] on the planet [[Saleucami]].<ref name="Yoda and Other Users">{{EncyclopediaCite|Yoda and Other Users of the Force}}</ref> During her time posted there, Secura attended a meeting in the [[Temple's war room]] via hologram along with Windu, [[Yoda]], Skywalker and Mundi, where they learned of Master Kenobi's [[Battle of Utapau|engagement]] with [[General]] [[Grievous]] on [[Utapau]] and discussed [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] abuse of [[Emergency Powers Act|emergency powers]], with Mundi advocating removing him from office if he did not yield his powers upon the destruction of Grievous, with Secura remaining silent.<ref name="Episode III" /> The meeting was then joined by Ahsoka Tano, who had left the Order after being wrongfully accused of the [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|Temple hangar bombing]] and who reported on the [[Siege of Mandalore|successful capture]] of Maul on [[Mandalore]]. Secura acknowledged Tano with a quick glance but again remained silent during the meeting before signing off to resume her [[Battle of Felucia (Outer Rim Sieges)|campaign]] on Felucia.<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref>

As she and her troops prepared to engage Separatist spider tanks, Sheev Palpatine, who had secretly been the Sith Lord [[Darth]] Sidious all along, issued [[Order 66]], calling for the extermination of all Jedi throughout the galaxy. Bly, having received the orders, ordered his fellow clones to open fire on the Twi'lek Jedi. Secura, who was distracted at the time by a [[bird]] suddenly taking flight, was unable to even draw her lightsaber before being shot twice in the back by the same clones she had trusted and even risked her life for.<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:AaylaSecuraDead-SWBC53.png|thumb|right|250px|Secura is killed on Felucia.]]
As she crumpled beneath a large fungal [[tree]], the clones continued to hammer her body with [[blaster]] fire, ensuring that she was killed. Yoda felt the death of Secura and many other fellow Jedi while on [[Kashyyyk]], which allowed him to defend himself and escape.<ref name="Episode III" /> Master [[Depa Billaba]], who was [[Meditation|meditating]] at the time, witnessed Secura as one of those killed in her [[Force vision]] which she shared with her Padawan [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]] on [[Kaller]]. She sacrificed herself soon after so Dume could survive.<ref name="Kanan 2">[[Kanan 2|''Kanan'' 2]]</ref>

====Legacy====
{{Quote|Let it guide you.|Aayla Secura, to Rey|<ref name="Episode 9">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>}}
After Secura's death, her droid [[QT-KT]] went to live with Secura's friend, [[Loris Cona]], on Ryloth.<ref name="Dark Droids: D-Squad 3">[[Dark Droids: D-Squad 3|''Dark Droids: D-Squad'' 3]]</ref> Around 3 ABY,<ref name="Date">The flashbacks of [[Star Wars (2020) 50|''Star Wars'' (2020) 50]] take place approximately twenty-two [[Standard year|years]] after [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|ascension]] to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]], meaning they must occur around [[3 ABY]]. The flashbacks also take place after the events of [[Star Wars (2020) 43|''Star Wars'' (2020) 43]], which occur after the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Droids]]'' crossover event, an event {{SW|url=news/marvel-dark-droids-interview|text=Charles Soule Unleashes ''Dark Droids''}} places after ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'', which is dated to 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] by ''Timelines''. As ''Dark Droids: D-Squad'' 3 is part of the ''Dark Droids'' event, its events must take place around 3 ABY.</ref> [[R2-D2]] and several other droids visited QT-KT to reassemble the [[D-Squad]]. QT-KT informed the droids of her master's death during Order 66.<ref name="Dark Droids: D-Squad 3" />

In [[35 ABY]], during the [[Battle of Exegol]], Secura's voice was heard by [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] among the voices of many Jedi of the past who aided her with killing the resurrected Darth Sidious. She advised Rey to let the Force guide her.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Don't lose a thousand lives just to save one.|Aayla Secura|<ref name="JC" />}}
[[File:AaylaSecura-SWBC53.png|thumb|left|250px|Aayla Secura was a compassionate Jedi, yet also a capable warrior.]]
An athletic and agile<ref name="Pocket" /> [[Rutian]] [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]],<ref name="Aayla DB" /> Aayla Secura had [[Color|blue]] skin, hazel [[eye]]s,<ref name="Episode II" /> and stood at 1.7 [[meter]]s<ref name="Aayla DB" /> (5 [[foot]], 7 inches).<ref name="Pocket" /> Intelligent and occasionally mischievous,<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> Secura was also compassionate and sympathetic<ref name="JC" /> despite what Confederate [[propaganda]] would have its citizens believe.<ref name="Aayla Jedi Hunt" /> She displayed her compassion when talking with Tano about her bond with her master.<ref name="JC" /> She compared it to her own bond with her own master, Quinlan Vos, who she saw as a father figure.<ref name="Women of the Galaxy" /> However, knowing she had to let go of him for the greater good, Secura told Tano that she would have to let go of Skywalker for the same reason. In addition, Secura prioritized the safety of the majority over the few, warning Tano to not jeopardize a thousand lives in order to save one individual.<ref name="JC" /> Secura also ensured her personal feelings did not get in the way of missions, qualities that her Clone Commander, Bly, came to respect. Her grit in battle and her sense of duty also were traits that her clone commander came to respect her for possessing.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> However, Secura was also willing to sacrifice her mission to save lives, as she let Asajj Ventress escape with vital data in order to rescue several Geonosian workers.<ref name="SWA12" />

Like any Jedi, Secura grieved over her homeworld, Ryloth, when it fell under Separatist occupation. However, she was raised at the Jedi Temple on Coruscant from a young age, and as such, lacked a deep connection to her home planet. As a member of the Jedi Order, Secura felt duty-bound to serve not just Ryloth but all worlds within the Galactic Republic.<ref name="Aayla SWE" />

==Powers and abilities==
[[File:AaylaSecuraLightsaber-SWTLC.jpg|thumb|Aayla Secura's lightsaber]]
Secura as a Jedi could utilize the Force. While buffeted around during the crash landing to [[Maridun]], she used her power to [[Telekinesis|flip a power switch without touching it]], saving the ship; later she rearmed herself by pulling her saber into her hand. After the crash, she empathetically [[Force sense|felt]] how sad and afraid Ahsoka was for Anakin.<ref name="JC" /> Fighting the invading CIS forces who were running weapons tests, she [[Force jump|sprang around and up a tree]] with the Force and did the same maneuver to get into the enemy base.<ref name="DoP" /> With Yoda and Anakin, she conjured a [[Force barrier]] to halt a poison gas used to destroy the Zilo Beast.<ref name="TZBSB" />

Hailed as one of the Jedi Order's most skilled fighters during her time, Secura possessed skills in [[Unarmed combat|martial arts]] and was a capable lightsaber duelist<ref name="Join the Battle" /> who was a master of [[Form IV]]. Her style of fighting included combining her exceptional athletic skills and lightsaber skills to outwit her opponents.<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> Secura was also known to use the nexu-stance evasive roll.<ref name="Head to Head">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Head-to-Head]]''</ref> On Maridun, she demonstrated herself to be hardy,<ref name="JC" /> and able to hack apart droids and do acrobatic swings on vines to save others, at one point sneaking into a Separatist stronghold and wiping out their wall guards. She and Ahsoka Tano looted a ship and flew back, then alongside Rex, Bly, and a wounded Anakin, the pair destroyed many battle droids.<ref name="DoP" /> With the aid of Mace Windu, Secura was able to hold against the rogue Sith Lord Maul<ref name="SoD 3" /> and she was capable of going toe-to-toe against [[Asajj Ventress]] on her own.<ref name="SWA12" /> She was also known for her ability to meditate.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
[[File:QTKT-AS.png|thumb|left|200px|Secura owned a pink astromech droid named QT-KT]]
She wielded a [[Aayla Secura's lightsaber|blue-bladed lightsaber]] during her time as a Jedi Knight<ref name="Episode II" /> and later Jedi Master.<ref name="Episode III" />The lightsaber had a [[durasteel]] hilt. The hilt itself was designed for durability during long missions and so that its inner workings were easily accessible if Secura needed to repair it in the field. The hilt's upper portion sported a ridged grip section and controls for adjusting the blade's length and intensity.<ref name="Lightsabers">''[[Star Wars Lightsabers: A Guide to Weapons of the Force]]''</ref>

Secura had her own [[Aayla Secura's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]], which was blue in color. She flew it during the Battle of Felucia.<ref name="Eta-2 Card">{{FFGXW2|set=Eta-2 Actis Expansion Pack|cardname=Aayla Secura}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:SWPicsMissFisto.jpg|thumb|right|Early, unused concept art by Dermot Power, of a fish-like Jedi that would evolve into Kit Fisto.]]
Aayla Secura was portrayed by [[Industrial Light & Magic]] Production Assistant [[Amy Allen]] in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] and the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Old databank">{{DB|character|aaylasecura|Secura, Aayla}}</ref> She was voiced by [[Jennifer Hale]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' TV series. Series director [[Dave Filoni]] had Hale perform the role with a French accent in homage to the French resistance fighters of World War II.<ref name="Hale">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/news20090123b.html|text=Jennifer Hale: Twi'lek Time}}</ref> According to Filoni, [[George Lucas]] specifically requested Secura to have a French-inspired accent.<ref name="Much to learn">{{SW|url=news/much-to-learn-you-still-have-7-things-you-might-not-know-about-twileks|text=Much to Learn You Still Have: 7 Things You Might Not Know About Twi'leks}}</ref> Hale reprised the role for a vocal cameo in [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] as one of many voices that give Rey encouragement.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Unlike many Jedi Knights from the [[prequel trilogy]], Secura wasn't originally created for the films. [[Aayla Secura/Legends|She]] was created by artist [[Jan Duursema]] and writer [[John Ostrander]] for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Republic]]''.<ref name="Star Wars 19">[[Star Wars (1998) 19|''Star Wars'' (1998) 19]]</ref>

In early concept art for the character by artist [[Dermot Power]], she was a [[fish]]-like creature rather than a Twi'lek, but the
concept later evolved into the character of [[Kit Fisto]].<ref name="fish girl">{{Facebook|username=jan.duursema.7|url=photo.php?fbid=10151868261798993&set=a.10150421871483993.411075.703558992&type=1&relevant_count=1&ref=nf|name=[[Jan Duursema]]|date=2013-08-19|quote=Original concept art for Quinlan Vos and Aayla Secura--yes, Aayla was almost a little fish girl...|archivefile=File:JanDuursema-AaylaSecura-August-19-2013.png}}</ref> After seeing a piece of comic book cover art of the final character, George Lucas decided to place her in ''Attack of the Clones''.<ref name="DWW:EotTK">{{SW|url=news/die-wanna-wanga-encounters-of-the-twilek-kind|text=Die Wanna Wanga: Encounters of The Twi'lek Kind|nolive=1}}</ref>

Secura's cone-shaped hearing organs were made from [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foam_rubber foam rubber] so that they would be malleable in a way similar to earlobes.<ref name="Much to learn" />

[[File:Aayla_bellybutton.jpg|thumb|right|150px|A close-up of Amy Allen's floral navel jewelry from her costumed photo shoot during production of ''Revenge of the Sith''.]]
During the [[Order 66]] scene in ''Revenge of the Sith'', Secura can be very briefly seen sporting a floral navel piercing that was absent in her appearances in ''Attack of the Clones'' and the costumed reference photos taken during that film's production. This detail has been ignored in [[canon]] media, possibly due to it being Amy Allen's personal jewelry that had been overlooked in production, and not an intended part of Secura's in-universe attire. This detail was, however, highlighted and canonized in [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity, and also appeared on a [[Sideshow Collectibles]] [[Sideshow Collectibles|''Premium Format Figures'']] statue released in 2012, since the piercing is clearly visible in the costumed reference photos taken for ''Revenge of the Sith''.<ref name="Visions">''[[Star Wars Art: Visions]]''</ref>

While Aayla Secura made non-speaking appearances in the films, the [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith Illustrated Screenplay|illustrated screenplay]] of ''Revenge of the Sith'' shows that Secura was meant to have at least two lines of dialogue during the Order 66 sequence, where she assured her troops of the stability of the area before asking Commander Bly if he thought there were hostiles nearby, only to be shot at by Bly and his troops moments after.<ref name="Ep. III Illustrated Screenplay">[[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith Illustrated Screenplay|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith Illustrated Screenplay'']]</ref>

On May 4, 2015, a costume of Aayla Secura was added to ''[[The Sims 4]]'' for playable Sims to wear in-game. The costume is purely cosmetic and offers no gameplay changes.<ref name="Sims 4">{{EA|url=games/the-sims/the-sims-4/news/star-wars-day|text=CELEBRATE STAR WARS™ DAY WITH NEW COSTUMES IN THE SIMS 4|nolive=1}}</ref>

Aayla Secura was introduced as a playable character of the same name in the 2015 mobile game ''Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes'' in 2015, which features characters and ships from both the canon and Legends continuities.<ref name="GoH" />
