'''Chuppa Chok''' was a [[scrivener]] working for the [[Varcinius Agglomeration]]. He was responsible for recording the [[Smuggler's Guide]] entry into the [[Varcinius Repository]] on [[Clerion]].<ref name="Smugglers Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
