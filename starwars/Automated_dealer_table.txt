An '''automated dealer table''' was a type of furniture used to play the card game [[Kessel Sabacc]]. The tables had an integrated [[dealer droid]] which guaranteed that players could not interfere with each other's cards.<ref name="Art Of Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the tables could be found on worlds around the galaxy, including on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]], the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planets [[Tatooine]] and [[Akiva]], the Outer Rim [[moon]] [[Toshara]] as well as aboard the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Automated dealer tables appeared for the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Prior to the game's release, they were shown in  promotional material; first appearing in the game's "Summer Game Fest 2024" teaser trailer which was released on [[June 8]], 2024.<ref name="Official Teaser Summer Game Fest">{{UbisoftYouTube|I6tA_o8uzUI|Star Wars Outlaws: Official Teaser &#124; Summer Game Fest 2024}}</ref> The tables were first identified as "automated dealer tables" in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Art Of Outlaws" /> The design for the tables was done by Concept Artist [[Adrien Girod]]<ref name="Girod ArtStation">{{ArtStation|subdomain=kurobot|url=projects/oJbmEL|name=[[Adrien Girod]]|text=Sabacc Tables|date=2024-09-30|archiveurl=https://archive.vn/xZgIm}}</ref> with the modeling, texturing and skinning done by the Level Artist [[Boris Kolimaga]].<ref name="Kolimaga Sabacc ArtStation">{{ArtStation|subdomain=boriskolimaga|url=projects/lGD5bO|name=[[Boris Kolimaga]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws - Kessel Sabacc|quote=I had the immense pleasure to be part of the team to bring the famous Kessel Sabacc card game to life in Star Wars Outlaws.|date=2024-09-30|archiveurl=https://archive.vn/NUKn9}}</ref>
