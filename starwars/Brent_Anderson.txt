'''Brent Eric Anderson''' is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] illustrator who drew an issue cover in the [[Star Wars (1977)|''Marvel Star Wars'']] comic series, and he pencilled the first issue of [[Star Wars: Chewbacca (2000)|''Star Wars: Chewbacca'']] for [[Dark Horse Comics]].
