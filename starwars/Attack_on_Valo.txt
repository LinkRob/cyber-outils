During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s '''attacked''' the [[planet]] [[Valo]]. During the attack, the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]] witnessed his [[Jedi Master]],<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> [[Aebal]],<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> being [[Death|killed]] by a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]], though he managed to escape and lived in the ruins of the [[Lonisa City Zoo]] for [[Standard month|months]] along with fellow [[youngling]]s [[Kildo]] and [[Tep Tep]].<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> As a result of the attack, Valo was consumed by the Nihil and placed behind its [[Stormwall]]<ref name="Databank" /> in the [[Occlusion Zone]].<ref name="New Heroes">{{SW|url=news/inside-the-high-republic-phase-3-new-heroes|text=Inside ''Star Wars: The High Republic'': Meet the New Heroes of Phase III}}</ref>
