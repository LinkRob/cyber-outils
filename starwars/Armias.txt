[[Brother]] '''Armias''' was a [[Narkis Anchorite]] who operated on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]]. He was a known associate of [[Jedi Master]] [[Cere Junda]], and held the contact codes for the [[Hidden Path]] network, which protected [[Force-sensitive]] beings and their families from the [[Inquisitorius]]. As the [[Galactic Empire]] closed in on the [[Pilgrim's Sanctuary]] safe house in [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Survivor Date" /> Armias entrusted the contact codes to [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]], who Armias recognized from Junda's stories. Shortly after handing over the codes, Armias was inadvertently crushed by a [[metal]] arm of a [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident''-class assault ship]], killing him.<ref name="Survivor" />
