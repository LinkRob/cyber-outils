The '''ACP Repeater Gun''' was a [[heavy repeating blaster]] produced by [[Arakyd Industries]] and sold for 1,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. Initially designed for [[planetary defense forces]] who deemed it too expensive and underpowered, it found popularity with [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] and criminals as an effective suppression weapon. Among mercenaries and [[enslaver]]s, the [[Repeating blaster|repeater]] became known as the '''Trandoshan Chopper''', while members of the [[Gank]] [[species]] called it the '''Nar Shaddaa Grinder'''.

==Description==
The ACP Repeater Gun was a [[heavy repeating blaster]] with medium range and auto-fire and [[stun]] settings. It had a compact frame with a blocky body and short, thin [[barrel]], below which a thin handle curved beneath the weapon, pointing back toward the handle and trigger. The [[Repeating blaster|repeater]] had a high energy output and rate of fire, making it an effective suppression weapon.<ref name="G&G" />

==History==
The ACP Repeater Gun was designed by [[Arakyd Industries]] prior to the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="G&G" /> which began in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The company also made the similarly-named ACP Array Gun, which was fully titled the [[Accelerated Charged Particle Array Gun]].<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> It was intended for use by local [[planetary defense forces]]; however, the purchasing agents for most [[planet]]s decided that it was both too underpowered to act as a primary [[City|urban]] combat weapon and, at one thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] a [[blaster]], too expensive. Limited purchases were made for [[special forces]] units who liked the weapon's compact frame.<ref name="G&G" />

[[File:NarShaddaa-2015StarWars8.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Viewed from orbit, the dark surface of Nar Shaddaa is criss-crossed by lines of artificial light from its city.|The ACP Repeater Gun was also known as the Nar Shaddaa Grinder, referencing the moon Nar Shaddaa (pictured).]]
Eventually, the repeater found a home on the secondary market, where it became popular with [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] and criminals due to its energy output and fire rate. Criminals favored how its suppression abilities could be used to force pursuers behind cover, allowing a clean getaway, and how quickly crowds scattered upon hearing a short burst from an ACP Repeater Gun fired into the air. The weapon earned the nickname of Trandoshan Chopper among mercenaries and [[enslaver]]s,<ref name="G&G" /> referencing the [[Trandoshan]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the planet [[Trandosha]],<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> and was called the Nar Shaddaa Grinder by [[Death|killers]] among the [[Gank]] species,<ref name="G&G" /> referencing the [[moon]] [[Nar Shaddaa]], a hotbed of criminal activity.<ref name="CGttSWG" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the ACP Repeater Gun was mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="G&G" /> The weapon originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]'',<ref name="RC">''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]''</ref> which was released on [[February 28]], [[2005]].<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|Special Edition 2022|Sith Unseen: A Retrospective}}</ref> The weapon was then identified as the [[Accelerated Charged Particle Repeater Gun]] in the official [[Prima]] [[Star Wars: Republic Commando: Prima Official Game Guide|game guide]] for ''Republic Commando'', which was written by [[Michael Knight]]<ref name="Prima">[[Star Wars: Republic Commando: Prima Official Game Guide|''Star Wars: Republic Commando'': Prima Official Game Guide]]</ref> and published on [[March 8]], 2005.<ref name="Amazon">{{Amazon|url=Republic-Commando-Prima-Official-Guide/dp/076154710X|text=Star Wars Republic Commando (Prima Official Game Guide)}}</ref>
