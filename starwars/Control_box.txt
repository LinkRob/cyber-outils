A '''control box'''<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>  also known as an '''Imperial pilot chestbox'''<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> was a component of the [[Environment suit|atmospheric suits]] worn by [[TIE series|TIE fighter]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilots]].<ref name="Bloodline" /> The device featured multiple buttons and switches and controlled the air being fed into the suit's [[Armor|helmet]].

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the criminal [[Pyke Syndicate]] had collected at least one control box from Imperial shipwrecks on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Later that [[Standard year|year]], the [[syndicate]] gifted a control box to the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] after she gained an excellent reputation with the organization.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}
