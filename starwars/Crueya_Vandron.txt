'''Crueya Vandron''' was an individual who served both under the [[Galactic Republic]] and its [[Proclamation of the New Order|successor]] state, the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], he was one of the primary driving figures of the [[Commission for the Protection of the Republic]], or COMPOR, which issued [[propaganda]] to support the goverment against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. After the war ended and the Republic transitioned into the Empire, COMPOR became the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]], or COMPNOR. Vandron took an active leadership in the revamped commission, sitting on its [[Select Committee]].<ref name="Rise" />
