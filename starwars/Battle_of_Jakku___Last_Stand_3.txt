'''''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand'' 3''' is the third issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] limited series ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Last Stand]]''. The issue was written by [[Alex Segura]], illustrated by [[Leonard Kirk]] and [[Jim Campbell]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 15]], [[2025]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE BATTLE OF JAKKU UNLEASHED! LUKE must enter the underworld to strike a deadly bargain! THE SPICE RUNNERS OF KIJIMI play a deadly wild card &mdash; with shocking consequences! As unseen forces move to disrupt the BATTLE OF JAKKU, the NEW REPUBLIC must fight a war on two fronts! LUKE takes on the new threat directly &mdash; putting everything at risk!''

==Plot summary==
As [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces gather at the [[planet]] [[Jakku]], leaders of the [[New Republic]] strategize on [[Chandrila]]. The mysterious informant "The Operator" has confirmed New Republic intel and [[Agent]] [[Bastion]] leads the briefing. [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] reminds the group, including [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and [[Han Solo]], that if the Empire uses the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Ravager]]'' as their bulwark, the New Republic will have a very small margin for error in the coming [[Battle of Jakku|battle]]. 

Meanwhile, the Operator, truly [[Counselor to the Empire|Counselor]] [[Gallius Rax]] feeding the New Republic intel that would lure them to Jakku, speaks via [[hologram]] with [[Grand Moff]] [[Randd]]. He informs Randd of the Republic's imminent arrival at Jakku. After the call, Rax delivers a rallying speech to all of the Imperial forces at Jakku. He denigrates the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] so-called government, blames them for starting the [[Galactic Civil War|war]], and calls for the creation of a better, stronger Empire by ending the tyranny of the New Republic. During the speech, [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] realizes Rax's true intention to betray the Empire by destroying both sides' forces, Randd positions [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]] to surround the ''Ravager'', and New Republic [[General]] [[Tyben]] informs Ackbar of the menacing [[blockade]] around Jakku. 

Gial Ackbar speaks with the holograms of Leia Organa and Mon Mothma, reiterating the necessity of executing their plans flawlessly. The women, however, warn Ackbar of the wild card presented by the rogue Imperial Grand Moff [[Ubrik Adelhard]], whose [[Adelhard coalition|coalition]] is acting as a third party in this conflict. Their hope lies in Luke Skywalker and [[pilot]] [[Rynn Zenat]] to find a way to stop Adelhard. The battle then commences. 

Admiral Ackbar directs various [[squadron]]s of New Republic [[starfighter]]s to try to break the ''Ravager's'' blockade and make it to Jakku's surface. One [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wing]] pilot, [[Odavia Anaru]], finds herself outgunned by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and on the brink of destruction. She is saved by the few surviving members of [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian's]] forces who made it out of the [[Anoat sector]]. Calrissian, [[Jarek Yeager]], and [[Preeti Delmin]] clear out some TIEs, allowing Anaru to sneak through an opening into Jakku's [[atmosphere]]. She is tailed by two TIEs and cannot shake them as she races to the surface. She finds the Empire's ground forces waiting. Anaru makes a daring u-turn to head back up, but an [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]] targets her with a surface-to-air [[missile]], exploding her ship. 

Back on Chandrila, Luke and Rynn welcome the New Republic [[spy]] [[Kith Alaytia]] and the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] [[assassin]] [[Kranor]]. Luke is hesitant to trust a Spice Runner, whose group is aligned with Adelhard as part of his coalition, and especially one who attempted to assassinate him in the past. In fact, Luke is most concerned about getting to Jakku to help the New Republic win the battle. Rynn convinces him to trust her and the new arrivals. She confesses that she is a Spice Runner, and that she and Kranor believe that the entire Adelhard coalition is fragile enough to break if they work together. Luke decides to trust his friend. 

As the New Republic further realizes they were led into a trap. The ''Raveger's'' blockade is considerable and keeps the New Republic from properly forming up. On the surface, Anaru survives the crash and finds herself surrounded by [[stormtrooper]]s. The odds are not in the New Republic's favor. 

As Moff Adelhard's forces arrive to Jakku, he holds his attack for the right moment. He plans to bombard both Imperial and New Republic targets so he can wreak the most destruction. The [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] mystic [[Reyna Oskure]] tries to tempt Adelhard one more time. He refuses and prepares to give a command to the Acolytes when their ship is struck by a TIE fighter's fire. It is the New Republic agent [[Therisa Alern]] who commandeered the starfighter.

On [[Sauwecer]], Rynn Zenat confesses to Luke Skywalker that her adopted [[parent|father]] is high-ranking Spice Runner [[Tomasso]]. Instead of joining her father's world, Rynn decided to join the Rebellion. The group meets with Tomasso, and they ask him to have the Spice Runners pull back from Jakku. Tomasso tells them how their leader, [[Zeva Bliss]] had always been planning ahead. She canvassed the underworld before joining Adelhard, recognizing who his adversaries would be. That is, the Spice Runners have already been activating allies to help turn on Anoat coalition. 

At Jakku, Alern continues her assault, dodging fire from Imperial and Spice Runner forces. Within the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the [[Scepter (Imperial-class)|''Scepter'']], Adelhard and his [[officer]]s detect [[Liberty's Misrule|a new Star Destroyer]] approaching. They receive an ominous [[Comm unit|comm]] from the [[pirate]] [[Eleodie Maracavanya]], who has arrived to fight against Adelhard. The distraction allows Alern to escape.
