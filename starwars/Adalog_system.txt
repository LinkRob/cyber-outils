The '''Adalog system''' was located in the [[Mytaranor sector]] within an intersecting portion of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> Situated in grid square Q-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Adalog]]. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Intelligence officer|operative]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] visited Adalog<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> by [[1 BBY]]<ref name="1 BBY">The events of the [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]] are dated to [[1 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> to investigate a lead about a [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|weapon]] being [[Project Stardust|built]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Andor's lead ultimately proved to be useless for him.<ref name="Rogue One novel" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Adalog [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix released alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Systems" />&mdash;both works having been written by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
