The '''destruction of Jedha City''',<ref name="RO junior">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> also referred to as the '''destruction of the holy city''', or as the '''massacre at Jedha''',<ref name="Rebel Files" /> occurred during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] when the Empire, in an effort to test the [[Mk I Superlaser|superlaser]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], used the [[battle station]] to destroy the [[Jedha City|holy city]] of the [[moon]] [[Jedha]]. Operating on a single [[reactor]] for the superlaser's power, the Empire [[Protocol 13|evacuated]] the Imperial occupation force before moving the Death Star into [[orbit]], targeting the holy [[city]], and destroying it. The blast caused not only an enormous explosion but also significant damage to the moon, throwing fire, smoke, and dirt into the upper reaches of the atmosphere. On the ground, the shockwave caused Jedha's surface to begin peeling back, destroying everything that it touched. The Empire subsequently fabricated a cover story for the [[Imperial Senate]] that Jedha was destroyed in a [[mining]] disaster.<ref name="Rogue One" />

By the time of the [[Cold War]], some dismissed the destruction of the city as mere Imperial [[propaganda]] capitalizing on the "actual" mining accident.<ref name="The Legends of Luke Skywalker">''[[The Legends of Luke Skywalker]]''</ref>

==Prelude==
As the Galactic Empire completed the Death Star, one of its scientists, [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]] convinced Imperial cargo pilot [[Bodhi Rook]] to defect and take a message to Saw Gerrera and his partisans on Jedha. Rook returned to his homeworld of [[Jedha]]. There, he surrendered to the partisans and was taken right to Gerrera. During this time, [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Intelligence]] officer, Captain [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] learned of the message from [[Tivik]] on the [[Ring of Kafrene]].<ref name="Rogue One 1">[[Rogue One Adaptation 1|''Rogue One Adaptation'' 1]]</ref><ref name="Rogue One" />

==The destruction==
{{Quote|Oh, it's beautiful...|Orson Krennic|audio=OhItsBeautiful.flac|<ref name="Rogue One" />}}
[[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]] Director [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] watched the Death Star's completion aboard the ''[[Executrix]]''. Tarkin learned that Krennic had a defector loose in Jedha City and was decided to test the weapon to prevent more systems from joining the Alliance. The Alliance sent Captain Andor, [[K-2SO]] and Professor Erso's daughter, [[Jyn Erso]] to verify the pilot's claim and find Professor Erso.<ref name="Rogue One 1" /> Andor, Erso, [[Chirrut Îmwe]], and [[Baze Malbus]] were captured by the partisans and taken to the [[Catacombs of Cadera]]. There, Gerrera gave Jyn the message from her father.<ref name="Rogue One 2">[[Rogue One Adaptation 2|''Rogue One Adaptation'' 2]]</ref><ref name="Rogue One" />

[[File:DestructionofJedhaCity-RO.png|thumb|left|270px|The Holy City's destruction seen from the Catacombs of Cadera]]
Despite Krennic wishing to destroy Jedha in its entirety, Tarkin decided that they did not require a pronouncement of the Death Star's power, and chose to destroy [[Jedha City]] only. He also ensured that Lord Vader and the Emperor would not be there in case of any embarrassment. After [[Protocol 13]] evacuated all Imperial forces from the city, Krennic gave the order and destroyed the city. The city was destroyed in a matter of seconds<ref name="Rogue One 2" /> and the shockwave moved the catacombs. At least a few Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s failed to evacuate and perished in the ensuing blast.<ref name="novelization">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref>  Some of the partisans, except Gerrera made their escape.<ref name="Rogue One" /> In his last moment alive, Gerrera simply said "[[Steela Gerrera|Steela]],"<ref name="Rogue One 3">[[Rogue One Adaptation 3|''Rogue One Adaptation'' 3]]</ref> the name of his sister, who had died during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Tipping Points">{{TCW|Tipping Points}}</ref> Andor, Erso, [[Chirrut Îmwe|Îmwe]], Malbus, and Rook were able to make their escape and meet up with K2 aboard the [[LMTR-20]] U-wing before the shockwave destroyed the Catacombs.<ref name="Rogue One 3" /><ref name="Rogue One" /> [[Gretta]] lost her family in the Death Star's attack.<ref name="Star Wars 34">[[Star Wars (2020) 34|''Star Wars'' (2020) 34]]</ref>

==Aftermath==
===Immediate aftermath===
{{Quote|There is no Death Star. We're informing the Senate that Jedha was destroyed in a mining disaster. I expect you to not rest until you can assure the Emperor that Erso has not compromised the weapon.|Darth Vader speaking with Krennic's on the Death Star's "problems"|<ref name="Rogue One 4">[[Rogue One Adaptation 4|''Rogue One Adaptation'' 4]]</ref>}}
[[File:Acme-TheEscape.png|thumb|right|250px|Jyn Erso and her allies were able to escape the destruction unleashed by the Death Star via the U-wing LMTR-20.]]
Pleased with the test, Tarkin apologized to Krennic and decided to take command of the Death Star. Despite Krennic's objections, Tarkin told him that he needed to find and eliminate the defector's contact at the [[Eadu Energy Conversion Laboratory|kyber refinery]] on [[Eadu]]. Andor and his friends continued with their mission and went to Eadu to rescue Professor Erso. In truth, Andor was under orders to eliminate Erso. Krennic arrived and demanded to know who supplied Rook with information on the Death Star. Professor Erso revealed himself as the traitor, but Krennic killed the other scientists.<ref name="Rogue One 3" /><ref name="Rogue One" /> At the same time, the Alliance sent in [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]] to bomb the facility.<ref name="Rogue One 3" /> Erso was mortally wounded in the attack, but was comforted by his daughter before she, Andor, and their friends could escape.<ref name="Rogue One 4" />

Lord Vader learned of Jedha City's destruction and the Alliance's attack on Eadu. He summoned Director Krennic to his [[Fortress Vader|Fortress]] on [[Mustafar]] to explain the problems concerning the Death Star and its use in destroying the city. Vader told Krennic that there was no Death Star and that the [[Imperial Senate]] was told that Jedha City was destroyed in a "mining accident". Angered by the incompetence of Krennic in keeping the battle station, a significant investment of time and resources, under wraps, Vader instructed Krennic to ensure that Erso did not compromise the weapon, unaware that the engineer had already [[Thermal exhaust port|done so]].<ref name="Rogue One 4" />

===Ruins of Jedha===
{{Quote|I thought I'd be used to it by now&hellip;that's still the worst thing I've ever seen. It feels so wrong."<br />"Yes. A holy moon, maimed, dying. I call the fiery rain Jedha's tears. I weep along with her.|Luke Skywalker and Chulco Gi, looking upon the remains of Jedha|<ref name="Star Wars 38">[[Star Wars (2015) 38|''Star Wars'' (2015) 38]]</ref>}}
[[File:Jedha-2015StarWars38.png|thumb|left|250px|The ruins of Jedha]]
Jedha was reduced to a horrible state after the Death Star fired upon it<ref name="Star Wars 38" /> despite the battle station only using a minor blast.<ref name="Rogue One" /> While the Death Star would be destroyed in the [[Battle of Yavin]], Jedha was left in decay. Surviving Partisans under [[Benthic]] continued the fight against the Empire on Jedha<ref name="Star Wars 38" /> and intended to continue on elsewhere after the "death" of the moon. Imperial forces arrived to recover whatever kyber crystals were left on Jedha, only to be countered by the Partisans and several members of the Alliance proper in the [[battle for Jedha]].<ref name="Star Wars 43">[[Star Wars (2015) 43|''Star Wars'' (2015) 43]]</ref> After taking part in the [[Mission to Shu-Torun (Rebel Alliance)|mission to Shu-Torun]], Benthic resolved to change and decided to bring the Partisans to [[Salobea]] instead of returning to Jedha.<ref name="Star Wars 67">[[Star Wars (2015) 67|''Star Wars'' (2015) 67]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The destruction of Jedha City was depicted in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology film]] ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. The first footage of the event was shown in the first ''Rogue One'' trailer on [[August 11]], 2016.<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|frdj1zb9sMY|Rogue One: A Star Wars Story Trailer (Official)|sw_url=video/rogue-one-a-star-wars-story-trailer}}</ref>

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the event where the Death Star tested its [[Superweapon/Legends|superweapon]] was the [[destruction of Despayre]].<ref name="DS">[[Death Star (novel)|''Death Star'']]</ref> Similar to how the kyber crystals of Jedha were harvested for use in the Death Star project, the planet Despayre hosted the [[Slavery/Legends|slave labor]] utilized to construct the battle station.
