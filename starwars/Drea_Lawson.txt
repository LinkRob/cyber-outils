'''Drea Lawson''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who was the mother of [[Rylee Lawson]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Around the period between [[17 BBY]] to [[13 BBY]], Lawson was working for the [[Galactic Empire]].

==History==
{{Quote|I talk to Mom.''"<br />[Brander Lawson sighs]<br />"''She, uh, wants me to come visit. Said she&mdash;''"<br />"''Rylee, just&hellip; Wait until I can go with you.|Rylee Lawson and Brander Lawson|<ref name="Chapter2">{{MSL|Chapter 2: Sinister Schemes}}</ref>}}
At some point prior to [[17 BBY]],<ref name="17BBY">{{ShadowLordDate}}</ref> Drea Lawson began working for the [[Galactic Empire]], married [[human]] [[Brander Lawson]]<ref name="DreaDB" /> and had their son, human [[Rylee Lawson]].<ref name="RyleeDB" /> She<ref name="Chapter2" /> later divorced Brander and moved out of the Lawson [[Lawson's apartment home|family apartment]]<ref name="Chapter2" /> on the [[planet]] [[Janix]],<ref name="Chapter2" /> leaving Rylee with Brander.<ref name="RyleeDB" />

[[File:LawsonFamily-SinisterSchemes.png|thumb|300px|left|Rylee Lawson observes a photo of himself beside his parents, Drea and Brander Lawson.]]
Some time between 17 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[13 BBY]],<ref name="ShadowLordDate">{{ShadowLordDate}}</ref> Brander Lawson, a [[detective]] for the [[Janix Civil Defense]], kept a photo of Drea, him, and their son on his desk at the [[Janix Civil Defense HQ]].<ref name="DreaDB" /> Rylee displayed the same photo on a [[computer]]<ref name="Chapter2AD">{{MSL|Chapter 2: Sinister Schemes}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> in the family's apartment as well.<ref name="Chapter2" /> During that time, Drea spoke to Rylee and expressed a desire for<ref name="Chapter2" /> him<ref name="RyleeDB" /> to visit her. When Rylee relayed this request to Brander, the [[Parent|father]] requested Rylee to wait until he was free to accompany him,<ref name="Chapter2" /> much to Rylee's disappointment.<ref name="Chapter2AD" /> After Brander left, Rylee observed the family photo on his computer.<ref name="Chapter2" /> Later, Brander and his partner, the [[droid]] [[2B0T]], debated if the Empire should be contacted to help with criminal activity organized by the [[fugitive]] [[Maul]]. Brander refused, which 2B0T inquired if the decision was "personal" because of Drea's involvement with the Empire, to which Brander denied.<ref name="Chapter3">{{MSL|Chapter 3: Whispers in the Unknown}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Lawson had [[Color|black]] [[hair]] and tan skin.<ref name="DreaDB" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drea Lawson first appeared through the form of a photograph in "[[Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge]],"<ref name="DreaDB" /> the first episode of the [[2026]] [[Disney+]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'', which released [[April 6]], 2026.<ref name="Release">{{Twitter|username=starwars|url=status/2033951658485682588|name=[[Star Wars]]|date=2026-03-17|archivefile=File:MaulShadowLord-EpisodeTitles-Twitter.jpg}}</ref> She was voiced by [[Tamlyn Tomita]].<ref name="Chapter5">{{MSL|Chapter 5: Inquisition}}</ref> She was identified as "Drea Lawson" in Rylee Lawson's [[Databank (2014-)|databank]] entry, which was posted on [[StarWars.com]] the day of the premiere.<ref name="RyleeDB" />
