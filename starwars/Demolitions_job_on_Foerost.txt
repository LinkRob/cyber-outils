The '''demolitions job on Foerost''' was a mission undertaken by the [[Drabatan]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[corporal]] [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek]]<ref name="Heroes" /> by [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> The mission consisted of destroying a [[Foerost communications tower|communications tower]] on the astronomical object [[Foerost]], which he successfully did.<ref name="Heroes" />

In 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> after the rebel group [[Rogue One]] arrived on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]], [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[pilot]]-[[Desertion|turned]]-rebel [[Bodhi Rook]] pointed out the [[Citadel Tower]], noting that all communications in and out of the [[Imperial Center of Military Research|base]]&mdash;which they planned to [[Battle of Scarif|infiltrate]]&mdash;passed through it. [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] vaguely recalled Pao's demolitions mission to destroy the [[Comm tower|tower]] on Foerost&mdash;noting it was similar to the one on Scarif&mdash;and made a note to ask the Drabatan about it.<ref name="Rogue One novel" />

==Behind the scenes==
The demolitions job on Foerost was first mentioned in [[Alexander Freed]]'s [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|novelization]] of the [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' released in [[2016]].<ref name="Rogue One novel" />
