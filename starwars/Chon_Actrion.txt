'''Chon Actrion''', also known as the "'''Architect of Freedom'''," was a famous [[Jedi Master]] at some point in the history of the [[Galactic Republic]]. A [[bronzium]] bust of Actrion was displayed together with other busts of Jedi Masters that were deemed important to be remembered in the [[Jedi Archives]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Temple]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="SWcom">{{SW|url=news/hidden-knowledge-5-fascinating-facts-about-the-jedi-temple|text=Hidden Knowledge: 5 Fascinating Facts about the Jedi Temple|nolive=1}}</ref>
