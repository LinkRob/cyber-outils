The '''Aquillian Rangers''' were a paramilitary force affiliated with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]]. They wore a uniform, a breath mask, and carried [[Lightsaber/Legends|laser swords]]. Small hovering and flying chrome [[Seeker|balls]] were part of their arsenal, which exploded with considerable force. The Rangers appear to have been able to [[Telekinesis/Legends|telekinetically]] move and set off these devices. Though they carried laser swords and some had [[The Force/Legends|Force]] abilities, they were distinct from the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]]. [[Deacon|Deak Starkiller]] and [[Bail Antilles/Legends|Bail Antilles]] were Aquillian Rangers.

==Behind the scenes==
The Aquillian Rangers first appeared in the second draft to the film that would become [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']], and were present with the Rebels on [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]] in the [[The Star Wars: From The Adventures of Luke Starkiller|third draft]]. They were the sole surviving reference to the planet [[Aquilae (The Star Wars)|Aquilae]] that appeared in the [[The Star Wars rough draft|rough]] and [[The Star Wars first draft|first draft]]. In later drafts, they became ordinary members of the Rebel Alliance. It is interesting, however, to compare them to the [[Antarian Rangers/Legends|Antarian Rangers]] from the [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]].

As part of his conceptual art for the second draft, [[Ralph McQuarrie]] illustrated Deak Starkiller's laser sword duel with [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] on board the Rebel fighter.
