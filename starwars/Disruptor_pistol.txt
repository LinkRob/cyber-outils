While once dominant across the battlefields of [[the galaxy]], use of [[disruptor]] weaponry was banned for several generations. As a result, '''disruptor pistols''' were manufactured only under close [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] scrutiny, and the vast majority of disruptor weaponry in the galaxy was salvaged from ancient battlefields, preserved by happenstance.<ref name="Gadgets">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>

Despite their highly restricted status, modern designs were appealing to [[assassin]]s and [[Spy|spies]] as a result of how they could destroy any evidence of their crimes completely. The [[Tenloss Syndicate]] served most of the market, employing [[Outlaw Techs]] to design and manufacture their own line of disruptor pistols.<ref name="Gadgets" />

Disruptor pistols were often bulky and had very limited ranges, though it was unclear if this was a result of the weapons' ancient designs Outlaw Techs replicated, rather than some inherent limitation on disruptor weaponry.<ref name="Gadgets" />
