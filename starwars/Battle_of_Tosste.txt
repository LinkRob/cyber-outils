A '''battle''' occurred near the [[planet]] [[Tosste]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was fought between the [[Iska pirates]], having [[Battle of Fanha|retreated]] from [[Fanha]], and the forces of the [[Imperial Navy]], commanded by [[Admiral]] [[Terrinald Screed]]. Screed led the Empire to victory, decisively defeating the [[pirate]] group.<ref name="TSA" />
