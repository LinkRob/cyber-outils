During the [[Operations on Tatooine]], a secondary [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] outpost being used as a [[Second Imperial Alloy Processing Facility (Tatooine)|Alloy Processing Facility]] was '''attacked''' by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] forces shortly following an [[Attack on Imperial Alloy Processing Facility|earlier attack]] on a [[Imperial Alloy Processing Facility (Tatooine)|Imperial Alloy Processing Facility]]. Under the guidance of [[Colonel]] [[Jennica Pierce]], the rebels emerged victorious, destroying the facility.<ref name="Commander" />
