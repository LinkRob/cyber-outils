The '''Contagion of Tynna''' was an event that occurred on<ref name="MOF" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> [[Tynna]]<ref name="MOF" /> by [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> During the event, the [[Galactic Republic]] imposed a quarantine, which was broken through by the [[Politics|political]] figure [[Nermani Ulekt]] to deliver aid. As a result, Ulekt was [[Prisoner|imprisoned]].<ref name="MOF" /> 

In 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> after the Republic was [[Proclamation of the New Order|reorganized]] into the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] was detained and interrogated by Imperial [[security officer]]s on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. While detained, Mothma recalled several Republic political figures she believed were wrongly [[Prisoner|imprisoned]], including Ulekt, and thought of the Contagion of Tynna.<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Contagion of Tynna was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
