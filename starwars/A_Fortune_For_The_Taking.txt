"'''A Fortune For The Taking'''" is an adventure seed contained in the ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]''. A group of [[Spacer/Legends|spacers]] come across a new world where they help a member of the [[Planet/Legends|planet's]] [[Unidentified sentient avian species|native species]]. In gratitude, they reward the spacers with sacks of [[hezar stone]]s worth thousands of [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|Galactic Credit Standards]]. The spacers are left with the dilemma of becoming exceedingly wealth at the cost of the species' peaceful existence. Possible alternate storylines that could be adapted by a gamemaster were also included. It was written by [[Bill Olmesdahl]].
