The '''DC-12U beam rifle''', also known as the '''SCUBA trooper rifle''', was a specialized [[blaster rifle]] that could operate [[Water|underwater]]. It was a double-[[barrel]]ed [[blaster]] that came equipped with a [[Glowrod|glowlight]] attachment to provide visibility in dark [[ocean]] depths.

==Description==
Designed by [[BlasTech Industries]] for the [[Clone SCUBA trooper|clone dive troopers]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army of the Galactic Republic]], the DC-12U beam [[rifle]] was a heavy, double-barreled weapon developed from an older BlasTech [[blaster rifle]] built for use in [[Water|wet]], humid, or [[swamp]]-like environments. Equipped with a collapsible stock and an integrated under-[[barrel]] mount, the DC-12U fired two low-output [[blaster]] bolts with one pull of the trigger and was devastating in the proper hands. The under-barrel mount could also typically be equipped with a high-powered [[Glowrod|flashlight]] that provided visibility in the darkest depths of the various aquatic [[planet]]s' [[ocean]]s.<ref name="Collapse" />

As a result of its particular mix of [[blaster gas]]es and its special focusing elements that were used to optimize underwater operations, the DC-12U fired [[Color|blue]]-colored blaster bolts as opposed to the typical red bolts emitted by most weapons. One DC-12U cost 1,600 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]<ref name="Collapse" />

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], a detachment of [[clone SCUBA trooper]]s led by [[Clone Commander]] [[Monnk]] were equipped with these [[rifle]]s during the [[Battle of Mon Cala]] in [[20 BBY]].<ref name="Water War" /> DC-12Us were carried by the SCUBA [[troop]]ers' successors, the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[seatrooper]]s, during the [[invasion of Mon Cala]].<ref name="Darth Vader 15" />
