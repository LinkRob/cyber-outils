The '''battle of the Glass Abyss''' took place in the [[Glass Abyss]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Metagos]] some time following the [[Invasion of Naboo]]. The conflict took place between the alliance formed by the [[gangster]] [[Chulok]] and [[Queen]] [[Sybil]], and the inhabitants of the [[city]] of [[New Xaxxis]], who were spurred into action by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]].<ref name="The Glass Abyss">''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]''</ref>
