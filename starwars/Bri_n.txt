'''Bri'n''' was an [[Forest|arboreal]] [[planet]] with a breathable [[atmosphere]] and various [[Plant|flora]] and [[Species|fauna]]. A horned [[humanoid]] [[Th'er's species|species]] lived on the planet. In [[37 BBY]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], were sent to Bri'n to assist with [[Conflict on Bri'n|negotiations]] between [[Priestess of Wood|Priestess]] [[Th'er]] and the [[Metal Clan]], a group that opposed her and wished to destroy the [[tree]]s present on the planet. Once the negotiations failed, the [[Jedi]] were forced to bring Th'er to safety on the planet [[Coruscant]], but advised her to seek offworld allies.

==Description==
{{Quote|More outsiders to strip our world of everything we value, you mean.|Th'er, to Qui-Gon Jinn|<ref name="Age" />}}
Bri'n was a terrestrial [[Forest|arboreal]] [[planet]]<ref name="Age" /> located in the [[Lothal sector]]'s [[Bri'n system]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and [[the Slice]], situated in grid square U-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Galaxy" /> The world had a Type I [[atmosphere]]<ref name="Atmosphere" /> breathable to [[human]]s and its [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Th'er's species|resident species]]. Its [[Species|fauna]] included [[lizard]]s and [[reptavian]]s&mdash;two species with [[Reptile|reptilian]] traits. In addition to [[skyscraper]]s on its surface, Bri'n had [[tree]]s which were valued by its inhabitants.<ref name="Age" />

==History==
{{Quote|It sounds like the negotiations didn't end well?''"<br />"''There is little middle ground between cutting down a tree and not cutting down a tree.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Qui-Gon Jinn, on the failed negotiations between Th'er and the Metal Clan|<ref name="Age" />}}
[[File:EscapeFromBrin-AORQuiGonJinn1.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Qui-Gon Jinn flees from the Metal Clan with Th'er.]]
In [[37 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> a [[Conflict on Bri'n|conflict]] arose between [[Priestess of Wood]] [[Th'er]], whose supporters defended the planet's trees, and the [[Metal Clan]], which sought to destroy the trees. The [[Jedi Order]] subsequently sent [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], to Bri'n with the intention of finding a peaceful solution to the conflict.<ref name="Age" />

The negotiations failed, and the Metal Clan pursued the [[priestess]] and Jinn through a forest, where they were rescued by an [[Eta-class shuttle|''Eta''-class shuttle]] [[pilot]]ed by Kenobi. Jinn was forced to bring Th'er to safety on<ref name="Age" /> the planet<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]], despite her complaints, as she did not want to abandon her trees to the Metal Clan. Although Th'er accused the Jedi of cowardice for leaving the planet, [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] later assured Jinn that it had been a necessary decision. While on Coruscant, Jinn advised Th'er to find allies to back her against the Metal Clan, which had already acquired its own powerful off-world allies. Even though the priestess was hesitant, the Jedi Master was able to convince her that it was for the good of her world.<ref name="Age" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|Your word or your blood, mistress Th'er. The trees will fall with one or the other.|The leader of the Metal Clan, to Priestess Th'er|<ref name="Age" />}}
[[File:MetalClan-AORQuiGonJinn1.png|thumb|right|320px|Many members of the Metal Clan belonged to a horned species.]]
A horned [[humanoid]] species resided on Bri'n, members of which included Th'er and several members of the Metal Clan, including its [[Metal Clan leader|leader]]. Th'er believed that the planet and its flora were to be preserved as they were, while the Metal Clan wanted to destroy Bri'n's trees. The clan claimed that they would do so regardless of Th'er's protests and threatened her with [[death]].<ref name="Age" />
