The '''Donderbus blaster pistol''' was a model of [[blaster pistol]] favored by members of the [[Kanjiklub]].<ref name="Across the Galaxy" /> However, members of the criminal organization often preferred rifles due to the rifles' superior firepower. As a result, this was not the primary weapon of choice for the members of Kanjiklub who confronted [[Han Solo]]<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==History==
[[Kanjiklub]] [[Topboss]] [[Tasu Leech]] owned a Donderbus blaster that had a bell mounted on the blaster to increase the bolt spread, a [[flexorcord]] assembly, and [[kintan strider]] on the handle.<ref name="TFAVD" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Donderbus blaster pistol first appeared in {{Film|VII}} holstered on the belt of one of the members of Kanjiklub when they confronted [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] aboard the ''[[Eravana]]''.<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> It was first identified in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]'' released in [[2017]].<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref>
