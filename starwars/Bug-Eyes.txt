'''Bug-Eyes''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]] belonging to the [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Unidentified Xantar species|species]] native to the planet [[Xantar]]. He met [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] after the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] officer destroyed an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Cloning/Legends|cloning]] facility. The pilot brought Skywalker to Xantar aboard his [[Unidentified Xantar starship|starship]]. There, he introduced the young Rebel to [[Indiana Jones]], whose [[Earth/Legends|planet]] the pilot had visited some time earlier.<ref name="Star Wars: Yoda Stories" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:IndianaJonesAndTheStoriesOfYoda.jpg|thumb|The pilot introduces Luke and Indy.]]
This character is meant to resemble the common image of aliens believed to visit Earth, known as [[Wikipedia:Greys|Greys]]. He originally appeared at the end of the ''Indiana Jones and His Desktop Adventures'' video game, taking Indy away in his flying saucer. He and Indiana Jones later appeared in ''Yoda Stories'' as a reference to that earlier game.
