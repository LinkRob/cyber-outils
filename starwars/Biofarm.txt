A '''biofarm''' was a type of scientific facility. One such facility was located on the astronomical object [[Thannt]],<ref name="Aphra 22" /> where at least one [[pluripleq]], a rare [[Bioengineering|bioengineered]] [[Changeling|shape-shifting]] [[Species|creature]], was kept.<ref name="Aphra 25">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 25|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 25]]</ref> Sometime between [[1 BBY]] and around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 22|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 22]] establishes that [[Cornelius Evazan]]'s infiltration of the biofarm on [[Thannt]] takes place after his scuffle on [[Tatooine]], which is depicted in {{Film|IV}}. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 22 to around [[1 ABY]], and ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the [[secret mission to Tatooine]] to [[1 BBY]]. Therefore, Evazan's infiltration must have occurred between 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and around 1 ABY.</ref> the notorious [[doctor]] and [[smuggler]]<ref name="DB">{{Databank|doctor-cornelius-evazan|Doctor Cornelius Evazan}}</ref> [[Cornelius Evazan]] infiltrated the biofarm on Thannt,<ref name="Aphra 22" /> [[Thief|stealing]] a pluripleq, which he used to disguise himself as a shapeshifter named "Lopset Yas" to avoid his death sentences.<ref name="Aphra 25" />

Eventually, the [[bounty hunter]] [[Tam Posla]], who was [[hunting]] Evazan, learned about the infiltration of the biofarm, and, on the 421st [[Standard day|day]] of his hunt, recounted it among the events he knew Evazan had been involved in. He added that Evazan's trail had been cold for a [[Standard month|month]] some time after the infiltration.<ref name="Aphra 22" />

==Behind the scenes==
The biofarm was first mentioned in the [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 22|twenty-second issue]] of the [[2016]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Simon Spurrier]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 22" /> on [[July 25]], [[2018]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/69596/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_22|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #22}}</ref>
