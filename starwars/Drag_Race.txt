"'''Drag Race'''" is an adventure seed contained in the ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]''. A group of [[Spacer/Legends|spacers]] are forced to race for ownership of their [[Starship/Legends|starship]]. Possible alternate storylines that could be adapted by a gamemaster were also included. It was written by [[Bill Olmesdahl]].
