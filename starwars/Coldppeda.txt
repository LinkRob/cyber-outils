'''Coldppedas''' were fifteen-[[Limb/Legends|footed]] domestic animals that lived on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Coonee/Legends|Coonee]]. A teenage [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] herded them for three [[Standard year/Legends|years]].

==Behind the scenes==
They were created by [[George Lucas]] shortly after the release of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']] in [[1977]] as part of the back-story of [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]]. Coonee, or coldppedas, were never incorporated into Han's biography, despite the fact that Lucas developed them for the use of licensees in the [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]].
