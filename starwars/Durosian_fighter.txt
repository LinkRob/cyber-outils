The '''Durosian fighter''' was a type of [[starfighter]] active during the [[First Order-Resistance War]]. One visible Durosian fighter, along with a few others, were part of the [[Citizens' Fleet]] led by [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] during the [[Battle of Exegol]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[Sith Eternal]].<ref name="TROS Junior Novel" />
