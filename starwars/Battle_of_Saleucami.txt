The '''Battle of Saleucami''' was fought on [[Saleucami]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. It took place following Confederate [[General]] [[Grievous]]'s [[Capture of Eeth Koth|capture]] of [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] member [[Eeth Koth]].

==Prelude==
[[File:Make_Demands_SWD.png|left|thumb|220x220px|Prior to the battle, Grievous captured Jedi Council member Koth.]]
After the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] received a message from [[General]] [[Grievous]] about his [[Capture of Eeth Koth|capture]] of [[Jedi]] Council member [[Eeth Koth]], [[Clone Commander|Commander]] "[[Wolffe]]" found a hidden message from Koth. The message said that Grievous was going to the [[Saleucami system]]. Thus, [[Jedi Master]]s, [[Adi Gallia]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his former [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] decided to rescue Koth from Grievous.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

==The battle==
===Space battle===
Kenobi took command of several [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[warship]]s. The three Jedi decided that Kenobi would distract General Grievous and lure him into the ''[[Surrogator]]'' whilst Skywalker and Gallia would sneak onto [[Grievous's Recusant-class light destroyer|Grievous's ''Recusant''-class light destroyer]] and rescue Koth, alongside [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] and several other [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

[[File:Grievous-Intrigue-old-thumbnail.png|left|thumb|238x238px|Generals Kenobi and Grievous dueled over Saleucami.]]
However, Grievous anticipated their plans, leaving several [[BX-series droid commando]]s on the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] with the [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] [[TV-94]] and the captured Koth. Grievous then boarded Kenobi's ship with several BX-series [[droid]] [[commando]]s and two [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s. He then [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] with Kenobi on the ship's bridge, who grew very close to defeating him after taking one of the Magnaguard's [[electrostaff]]s to use alongside [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. However, Grievous fled the bridge, and Kenobi contacted Skywalker, who was with Gallia and Koth, having defeated the commando droids. Gallia went to help Kenobi, whilst Anakin took Koth back to the [[Eta-class shuttle|''Eta''-class shuttle]] that they had boarded Grievous' ship with.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

Gallia engaged Grievous just as the [[docking tube]] started to break. With the help of Gallia, Kenobi and two other [[Cloning|clones]] were able to escape onto Grievous' ship just as the Republic ship exploded, [[Death|killing]] everyone on it. Skywalker then picked up the two clones, Kenobi and Gallia in the [[shuttle]] before flying back to the ''[[Resolute]]''. Grievous then prepared to land his forces, but debris from Kenobi's ship crashed into the [[C-9979 landing craft]] as they attempted to land on Saleucami. Grievous and what remained of his forces took to the [[escape pod]]s. Thus, Kenobi took Rex, [[Commander]] [[Cody]], and other forces to pursue Grievous while Skywalker would continue fighting the [[Space warfare|space battle]] against the Separatist forces.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

===Tracking Grievous===
After his [[escape pod]] landed, Grievous and his forces then attempted to send a distress signal to what remained of their [[fleet]] in orbit. However, his escape pod's [[transmitter]] was destroyed and Kenobi's forces landed near them. Thus Grievous and his forces searched for an escape pod with a working transmitter.<ref name="The Deserter" />

[[File:YouStoleMyFace-TD.png|left|thumb|233x233px|Clone Captain Rex met deserter Cut Lawquane.]]
Kenobi then divided his forces; Rex would take several of his [[trooper]]s on [[BARC speeder]] through the wetlands while Cody and [[Crys]] would investigate the crash site. As Rex and his troopers went through the wetlands, Rex was shot by a pair of commando droid [[sniper]]s. Clone medic [[Kix]] was able to patch up Rex, but was forced to leave him at the [[Lawquane's Farm|Lawquane's farmstead]]. Rex then left [[Jesse]] in charge and ordered him to continue tracking Grievous down. Later, Rex discovered that the [[farmer]], [[Cut Lawquane]], was a deserter from the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="The Deserter" />

[[File:Grievous and B1 battle droid on Saleucami.png|thumb|right|300px|Grievous reunites with his forces at the final escape pod.]]
General Grievous, riding a [[Reek]], made his way to an escape pod that contained a working transmitter. However, his [[battle droid]]s were running low on power. They asked Grievous to let them recharge, but the General refused to do so and continued while droids died. Kenobi, Cody and Crys then discovered the locations of the escape pods and continue their search for Grievous.<ref name="The Deserter" />

Following a dinner with Cut's family, Rex and Cut came under attack by commando droids. Together, they were able to defeat the droids. Grievous was able to find a pod with a working transmitter and was able to send a signal to the fleet. However, Kenobi, Cody, Jesse, and their forces were able to find him and attempted to stop his escape. Kenobi then confronted Grievous, and Kenobi briefly dueled with him before he made his escape.<ref name="The Deserter" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Five agonizing months!|R0-GR|<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref>}}
[[File:RexLawquaneAndFamily.jpg|left|thumb|220x220px|Captain Rex parted ways with Lawquane.]]
Following Grievous's escape, Kenobi and his forces prepared to leave. Rex decided to allow Cut to remain with his family and not report him. Rex then left on an [[eopie]] to rendezvous with Kenobi's forces.<ref name="The Deserter" /> With Grievous having abandoned his forces during his escape,<ref name="The Deserter Episode Gallery image 15">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-deserter-episode-gallery|text="The Deserter" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Image 15 caption}}</ref> the B1 battle droid [[R0-GR]] found himself trapped on Saleucami for the next five [[Standard month|months]] before he had a chance to return to the war.<ref name="Droidography" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Saleucami was first identified in ''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]'', but the book itself did not link the battle R0-GR fought in<ref name="Droidography" /> to the battle depicted in "[[Grievous Intrigue]]"<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> and "[[The Deserter]]."<ref name="The Deserter" /> However, ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' later identified the battle depicted in these episodes as the "Battle of Saleucami."<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New Edition">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Given that the battle mentioned in ''Droidography'' and the battle in the episodes share the same name, this article assumes they are the same.
