'''180 BBY''', or '''180<ref name="Date" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]]. Around that year,<ref name="Date" /> the [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Sol]] was born.<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
180 BBY was indirectly mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and published in [[2025]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
