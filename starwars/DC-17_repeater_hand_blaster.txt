The '''DC-17 repeater hand blaster''',<ref name="SWCEUEE">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> also known more simply as the '''DC-17 hand blaster'''<ref name="Ultimate" /> or '''DC-17 blaster pistol''',<ref name="Epic Battles">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Visual Guide: Epic Battles]]''</ref> was a model of [[heavy blaster pistol]] and the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s standard-issue sidearm. Produced by [[BlasTech Industries]], the DC-17 was usually given to weapon teams, the crews of vehicles, and [[officer]]s. The weapon was one of the most reliable and toughest models of [[blaster pistol]]s created, as well as having remarkable stopping power in its small design. The weapon was also well balanced, possessed a respectable rate of fire, and lightweight.<ref name="Rise" /> 

==History==
[[File:RebelRex_USWNE.png|thumb|left|250px|Clone Captain Rex used two DC-17s and continued to long after the Clone Wars.]]
Of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s who used the [[blaster]] during the [[Clone Wars]], high-[[Military rank|ranking]] [[Clone Commander|commanders]], [[Clone Captain|captains]],<ref name="TCW film" /> and [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC troopers]] were all users.<ref name="arc">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref> During the conflict, ARC troopers, as well as other [[special forces]] units like [[Clone Force 99]] within the [[Special Operations Brigade]], used the DC-17 for close-quarters fighting and as a backup weapon.<ref name="Rise" />

[[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], a notable [[clone trooper officer]]<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> who served in the elite [[501st Legion]],<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> preferred to use two DC-17s at once.<ref name="Ultimate" /> After the Clone Wars, clone stormtroopers such as [[Clone Captain|Captains]] [[Howzer]]<ref name="Devil's Deal" /> and [[Wilco]]<ref name="Spoils">{{TBB|Spoils of War}}</ref> continued to make use of the blaster.

[[Greez Dritus]] wielded four of them to protect the [[Stinger Mantis]], during the [[Raid on the Archive]].<ref name="Survivor" /> In the years leading up to the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Twi'lek]] rebels [[Numa]] and [[Gobi Glie]] also used these blasters.<ref name="Homecoming">{{Rebels|Homecoming}}</ref> [[Lieutenant]] [[Dicer]] of the [[Phoenix Cell]] also used this model when she was attacked by [[krykna]], [[spider]]-like creatures native to the planet [[Atollon]].<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base">{{Rebels|The Mystery of Chopper Base}}</ref>
