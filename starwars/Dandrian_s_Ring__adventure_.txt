"'''Dandrian's Ring'''" was an adventure for the [[West End Games]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|D6]] system, written by [[Chris Hind]]. It was published in the magazine ''[[Challenge 49]]'' in [[1991]]. Like most RPG material from [[Challenge (magazine)|''Challenge'']], its characters and events are ambiguously canon.
