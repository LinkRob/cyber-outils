'''80-HD''' was a [[Protocol droid/Legends|protocol droid]] who was malfunctioning<ref name="Twitter" /> at some point by the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Imperial Era/Legends|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Empire">{{WebCite|url=https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/star-wars-1313-will-be-the-product-of-an-evolved-lucasarts|text=Star Wars 1313 will be the product of an evolved LucasArts|author=Adam Rosenberg|date=2012-07-28|work=[[Wikipedia:Digital Trends|''Digital Trends'']]|archivedate=20190928050240|nolive=1}} revealed that ''[[Star Wars: 1313]]'', which would have featured 80-HD per {{Twitter|oogew|status/858046604053557249|[[Jesse Harlin]]|date=2017-04-28|quote=At LucasArts working on Star Wars 1313, I was writing hip-hop for a malfunctioning protocol droid named 80-HD. He probably looked like this.|archivefile=File:JesseHarlin-80-HD-April-28-2017.png|nolive=1}}, would have been set approximately five to ten [[Standard year/Legends|years]] prior to the events of {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to between approximately [[5 BBY/Legends|5 BBY]] and [[10 BBY/Legends|10 BBY]] and during the [[Imperial Era/Legends|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]], according to ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
As revealed on [[April 28]], [[2017]] in a [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] post by composer [[Jesse Harlin]], 80-HD would have been featured in the [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: 1313]]'',<ref name="Twitter" /> which was announced in [[June]] [[2012]]<ref name="gamespot">{{GameSpot|url=articles/star-wars-1313-revealed/1100-6379758|text=Star Wars: 1313 revealed|author=Laura Parker|date=2012-06-01}}</ref> and later cancelled on [[April 3]], [[2013]].<ref name="kotaku">{{WebCite|url=https://kotaku.com/disney-shuts-down-lucasarts-468473749|text=Disney Shuts Down LucasArts, Cancels Star Wars 1313 And Star Wars: First Assault|author=[[Wikipedia:Jason Schreier|Jason Schreier]]|date=2013-04-03|archivedate=20200301083454}}</ref> Harlin, replying to a post of fan art depicting a [[3PO-series protocol droid/Legends|3PO-series]] protocol [[Droid/Legends|droid]] wearing [[Clothing/Legends|clothing]], stated that 80-HD "probably looked like [that]" and also revealed that they had been writing [[Wikipedia:Hip hop music|hip-hop]] for him.<ref name="Twitter" /> The droid shares its name with a fictional rapper, portrayed by C. J. Nord, in previous LucasArts titles [[Wikipedia:Thrillville|''Thrillville'']] and [[Wikipedia:Thrillville: Off the Rails|''Thrillville: Off the Rails'']]. The soundtracks to both were composed by Harlin and [[David W. Collins]], who also wrote all of the games' original songs.<ref name="Thrillville">{{VisualMediaCite|title=[[Wikipedia:Thrillville|''Thrillville'']]|prod=[[Wikipedia:Frontier Developments|Frontier Developments]]|year=[[2006]]}}</ref><ref name="Off the Rails">{{VisualMediaCite|title=[[Wikipedia:Thrillville: Off the Rails|''Thrillville: Off the Rails'']]|prod=[[Wikipedia:Frontier Developments|Frontier Developments]]|year=[[2007]]}}</ref>
