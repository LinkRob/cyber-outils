A '''force bridge''' was a type of [[force field]] generated to provide a pathway across open space, similar in function and appearance to a [[plasma bridge]]. The [[Citadel of Rur]] featured a force bridge that provided access to the fortress's central computer core which held the records of the [[Ordu Aspectu]].<ref name="Aphra 5" />

[[Chelli Lona Aphra]] activated the force bridge in the Citadel while exploring its ruins with her [[Parent|father]] [[Korin Aphra]] in [[0 ABY]], allowing the pair to reach the computer core, though she destroyed the control panel, deactivating the bridge, to prevent [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtrooper]] squads from pursuing.<ref name="Aphra 5" /> The bridge was reactivated to allow [[Captain]] [[Magna Tolvan]] and her troops across, and deactivated once more to cut off pursuing [[droid]]s.<ref name="Aphra 6">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 6|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 6]]</ref>
