'''Alaspin''' was a location where a faintly [[poison]]ous [[Alaspin wine|variety]] of [[wine]] was produced.<ref name="Wine in Dreams">{{StoryCite|story=The Wine in Dreams|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref> It was located in the [[Alaspin system]] of the [[Suolriep sector]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]],<ref name="Slice" /> falling within grid square S-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/4-reasons-canto-bight-is-an-essential-read|text=4 Reasons ''Canto Bight'' Is an Essential Read}} states that the stories of [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']] take place a few [[Standard day|days]] prior to the events of {{Film|VIII}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[sommelier]] [[Derla Pidys]] sold the wine from Alaspin.<ref name="Wine in Dreams" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alaspin was mentioned in "[[The Wine in Dreams]]," a novella written by [[Mira Grant]] and published in the [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']] anthology in [[2017]].<ref name="Wine in Dreams" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Alaspin system/Legends|Alaspin system]] was a [[Star system/Legends|star system]] in the [[Suolriep sector/Legends|Suolriep sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> and [[The Slice/Legends|the Slice]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
