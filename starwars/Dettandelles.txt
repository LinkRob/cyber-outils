'''Dettandelles''' were a [[species]] that could wilt. In the first draft of his [[holobook]], "[[Hondo's Memoirs]]", the [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] used the phrase "shrink like wilting dettandelles" to describe an individual being cowardly.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
