'''Fil''', designated '''2720''', was a [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].<ref name="By any other name" /> As a [[Gender|male]] [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] of [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett/Legends|Jango Fett]], 2720 was born on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Kamino/Legends|Kamino]],<ref name="db" /> had [[Color/Legends|tan]] skin,<ref name="Skin" /> and stood at 1.83 [[Meter/Legends|meters]] tall.<ref name="db" /> 2720 was acquainted with fellow [[Trooper/Legends|troopers]] [[1137]] and [[1139]]. When 1139 wanted a name rather than being referred to by a designation number, 1139 came up with the names "Fil" and "Byl." 1137 then stated that those names were already taken by the troopers who they knew as 2720 and 0037 last [[Standard week/Legends|week]].<ref name="By any other name" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fil was mentioned in the animated comic [[A clone by any other name]] on the [[LEGO Star Wars]] website, released on [[August 21]], [[2009]].<ref name="By any other name" />
