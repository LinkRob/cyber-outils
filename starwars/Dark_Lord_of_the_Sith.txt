'''Dark Lord of the Sith''' was a title used by members of the [[Sith]], also known as [[Sith Lord]]s. Under the [[Rule of Two]], it was applied to both the [[Sith Master]] and the [[Sith apprentice]].

[[Darth Plagueis]]' apprentice, [[Darth Sidious]], conspired to restore the Sith to power roughly a [[millennium]] after the [[death]] of [[Darth Bane]], the Dark [[Lord]] who reformed the Sith. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Maul|Darth Maul]] and [[Savage Opress]] claimed the mantle of Dark Lord despite the presence of Sidious and [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]]. Sidious killed Opress and chastised Maul, reminding his former apprentice that there could only be two Sith Lords at any given [[time]] in the galaxy.

After the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|fell]], [[the galaxy]] was ruled by two Dark Lords&mdash;[[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth]] Sidious and his apprentice, the fallen [[Jedi]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]&mdash;during the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Their reign ended when Vader [[Battle of Endor|sacrificed]] himself by destroying his master, rendering the Sith extinct through the [[death]]s of the final Dark Lords.

Despite the Sith's [[Chosen One|prophesied destruction]], their legacy endured through Sidious, who was revived through [[cloning]] and the [[dark side of the Force]], as well as [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], Vader's [[Skywalker family|grandson]]. During the [[First Order]]'s [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]], Ren aspired to rule all the [[galaxy]] as the new [[Emperor]] and Dark Lord of the Sith. Following Ren's [[Light side of the Force|redemption]], Sidious perished along with his [[Sith Eternal|followers]] in the [[Battle of Exegol]] as the very last Dark Lord of the Sith remaining.

==History==
===Early history===
{{Quote|The Sith killed each other. Victims of their own greed. But from the ashes of destruction, I was the last survivor.|Darth Bane's specter|audio=GhostBane01-TCW.flac|<ref name="Sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:DarthBane-GoH.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Bane was the Dark Lord of the Sith who created the Rule of Two after the culling of the Sith Order.]]
The Dark [[Lord]] of the [[Sith]], alternatively known as a [[Sith Lord]], was a title in the Sith Order.<ref name="Episode II" /> The title "[[Darth]]" used by all Sith Lords roughly translated into "Dark Lord."<ref name="SWEasy">''[[Star Wars Made Easy]]''</ref>

The [[Valley of the Dark Lords]] on the [[planet]] [[Moraband|Korriban]] contained crypts of the deceased Dark Lords of the Sith.<ref name="Moraband DB">{{Databank|moraband|Moraband}}</ref> The planet became a sacred place of ceremonial rituals for the Sith. The [[burial]] ceremony of a Dark Lord of the Sith would be accompanied by hundreds of Sith warriors carrying the body of the fallen master. The corpse was stored in the tomb along with precious objects, and an escort of slaves accompanied the deceased on his journey to the spirit realm. The Sith then left the planet until the next burial.<ref name="Mustafar and Other Outer Rim Planets">{{EncyclopediaCite|Mustafar and Other Planets in the Outer Rim}}</ref>

The Sith later fell victim to infighting because of their greed for power, weakening the Sith Order to the point where the [[Jedi Order]] defeated and nearly [[Fall of the Old Republic|destroyed]] their ancient rivals.<ref name="SOTS">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref> However, the ancient Sith Order had one surviving member: [[Darth Bane]]. Bane assumed the mantle of Dark Lord and established a philosophy known as the [[Rule of Two]], declaring that only two Sith Lords could exist at any [[time]]&mdash;a [[Sith Master]] and a [[Sith apprentice]].<ref name="Bane DB">{{Databank|darth-bane|Darth Bane}}</ref> After his death, Bane was the last Dark Lord to be buried at Moraband's Valley of the Dark Lords.<ref name="Bane SWE">{{SWE|characters|darth-bane|Darth Bane}}</ref>

===Fall of the Republic===
[[File:DarthSidious-Villains2024.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Sidious concealed his nature as the Dark Lord of the Sith behind the facade of Sheev Palpatine.]]
{{Quote|Hundreds of senators are now under the influence&hellip;of a Sith lord called Darth Sidious.''"<br />"''I don't believe you.''"<br />"''The viceroy of the Trade Federation&hellip;was once in league with this Darth Sidious, but he was betrayed ten years ago by the dark lord<!--decapitalization of "dark lord" is intentional, since the film's captions format it that way-->.|Darth Tyranus and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
The Dark Lords that succeeded [[Darth]] Bane would adhere to his teachings, maintaining the Sith Order's ranks at two members throughout the [[millennium]]-long [[Republic Era|reign]] of the modern [[Galactic Republic]]. [[Darth Sidious]], publicly known as Sheev Palpatine from [[Naboo]], became the Dark Lord of The Sith after betraying and [[Death|murdering]] his Sith Master [[Darth Plagueis]]. Sidious was the Dark Lord of the Sith during the [[Republic Era|last years]] of the Republic and the [[Imperial Era|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

At the time of the [[Clone Wars]], Sidious' former apprentice [[Maul|Darth Maul]] sought to reclaim his status as a Sith Lord while training his brother, [[Savage Opress]]. Having replaced Maul with [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]], Sidious confronted the [[Zabrak]] Sith claimants on [[Mandalore]]. There, he killed Opress and subdued Maul. The Dark Lord castigated Maul, informing the [[Dathomirian]] that he had been replaced in an Order that permitted only two Sith Lords. However, Sidious informed Maul that he still had [[Talzin|uses]] for his former apprentice,<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref> using him to [[Campaign against the Shadow Collective|draw out]] [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]].<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part One">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 1|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 1]]</ref>

===Age of the Empire===
{{Quote|I am a lord of the Sith. They are the ones who should be running.|Darth Vader|<ref name="Vader Down 1">[[Vader Down 1|''Vader Down'' 1]]</ref>}}
[[File:Battlefront_Vader.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The dark side of the Force transformed Anakin Skywalker into the Dark Lord of the Sith known as Darth Vader.]]
Sidious would later betray Tyranus and have him killed by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], as he desired to make Skywalker his apprentice. After the death of Tyranus, Skywalker fell to the dark side and became Darth Vader, Sidious' third apprentice. Sidious [[Proclamation of the New Order|proclaimed]] himself [[Galactic Emperor]] after [[Order 66|destroying]] the Jedi Order.<ref name="Episode III" /> The Dark Lords ruled [[the galaxy]] for a generation<ref name="Sith DB">{{Databank|sith|Sith}}</ref> until the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> when Sidious betrayed Vader and tried to make [[Luke Skywalker]] his new apprentice.<ref name="Episode VI" /> Previously, Vader had tried to do the same, but he gave up on his quest to make his son his apprentice after the [[mission to Exegol]],<ref name="Darth Vader 11">[[Darth Vader (2020) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 11]]</ref> only to resume during his alliance with the [[Schism Imperial]].<ref name="Darth Vader 48">[[Darth Vader (2020) 48|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 48]]</ref>

During the Battle of [[Endor]], Luke refused to kill his [[Parent|father]], however, declaring his allegiance to the [[Jedi]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> Enraged by Luke's refusal to turn to the dark side, Sidious [[torture]]d the Jedi Knight with [[Force lightning]]. Luke begged Vader to save him; unwilling to see his son die, Vader sacrificed his life<ref name="Sith DB" /> by destroying the [[Emperor]].<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>

===Return of the Dark Lord===
{{Quote|At last the work of generations is complete. The great error is corrected. The day of victory is at hand. The day of revenge. The day of the Sith.|Darth Sidious|audio=PalpatinesBroadcast.flac|<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>}}
The deaths of Sidious and Vader marked the end of the lineage of Dark Lords going back to Darth Bane,<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and the beginning of the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Sith DB" /> Vader died as Anakin Skywalker<ref name="Anakin DB">{{Databank|anakin-skywalker|Anakin Skywalker}}</ref> having returned to the light side<ref name="Obi-Wan DB">{{Databank|obi-wan-kenobi|Obi-Wan Kenobi}}</ref> through an act of selflessness and sacrifice,<ref name="Anakin DB" /> which allowed Yoda to preserve Anakin's identity when he became one with the Force. As a result, Anakin joined Yoda and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in death as a [[Force spirit]]. With his death, Anakin brought balance to the Force<ref name="Yoda DB">{{Databank|yoda|Yoda}}</ref> and his destiny as the [[Chosen One]] was fulfilled.<ref name="Beware the Power">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref>

[[File:Sidious_triumphant.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Resurrected on Exegol, Darth Sidious endeavored to reclaim the galaxy as Emperor and Dark Lord of the Sith.]]
Unbeknownst to the galaxy at large, Sidious was revived when he used the dark side to move his spirit to a [[Cloning|cloned]] body.<ref name="TSWB" /> Aided by his [[Sith cultist|followers]] on [[Exegol]], the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Religion|cult]], the fallen Emperor [[Bioengineering|engineered]] the [[Strand-Cast|strandcast]] [[Snoke]]<ref name="TROS EE" /> to groom Vader's grandson, [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], into an heir that was worthy of inheriting the Sith legacy.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Ren, who coveted the power of his Sith grandfather,<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> sought to rule all the galaxy as the new Emperor<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> and Dark Lord of the Sith.<ref name="TROS JN">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>

Sidious' plan to restore his Order through the creation of a new [[Sith Empire]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> ended at the [[Battle of Exegol]]. Kylo Ren had returned to the [[light side of the Force]], renouncing the dark side and his claim to the Sith mantle, while Sidious' [[Palpatine family|granddaughter]], the [[Padawan|Jedi apprentice]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] rejected the Emperor's entreaty to succeed him. Ultimately, Rey used the power from all the past deceased Jedi to permanently destroy Sidious and Sith for good.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the story of the non-[[canon]] ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' short "[[THE TWINS]]," the [[Force-sensitive]] [[twin]]s [[Am]] and [[Karre]] both held the title "[[Dark Lord]]."<ref name="Art of Visions">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]''</ref> In that capacity, both wielded power over a [[TRIGGER Empire|Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Twins">{{Visions|THE TWINS}}</ref> that emerged after the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="IGN">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ign.com/articles/star-wars-visions-anime-jedi-rock-opera|text=Star Wars Anime Visions Will Include What Became of the Jedi, a Rock Opera, and More|author=Daniel Robson|work=''[[Wikipedia:IGN|IGN]]''|archivedate=20210922124827}}</ref> The twins' empire hoped to claim control over the galaxy, only for Karre to rebel and abandon the regime.<ref name="Twins" />
