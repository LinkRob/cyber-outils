'''Boga''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[varactyl]], a large lizard-like creature. She dwelled on the [[planet]] [[Utapau]], and, in the [[Outer Rim Sieges|final days]] of the [[Clone Wars]], was used by [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] as a mount as he engaged [[General]] [[Grievous]]'s [[Separatist Droid Army|forces]] during the [[Battle of Utapau]].

==Description==
A [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Databank Boga" /> [[varactyl]],<ref name="DB Vara" /> Boga had iridescent [[Color|green]] pebbled skin with a blue sheen. She was fifteen meters long and measured about four [[meter]]s tall at her shoulder. Her head and neck were fringed with richly colored feathers. Like other varactyls, she possessed strong [[limb]]s with five-toed feet featuring curled claws and a long tail that helped her to balance.<ref name="Databank Boga" />

==History==
[[File:BogaROTS-SWE.png|left|thumb|Boga was ridden by Obi-Wan Kenobi during the Battle of Utapau.]]
During his [[Battle of Utapau|search]] for [[General]] [[Grievous]] on [[Utapau]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] found himself in a need of a mount. He visited an area of pens where the [[Pau'an]] natives kept varactyls and [[dactillion]]s that they had trained for use as transports. Here, he encountered, and chose the varactyl Boga as his mount<ref name="Episode III" /> after using [[the Force]] to [[Mind trick|influence]] the mind of a [[Utai]] wrangler.<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|40|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> She aided him in his fight against General Grievous,<ref name="Episode III" /> but was later [[Death|killed]] on the orders of<ref name="Death" /> [[Clone Marshal Commander|Commander]] [[Cody]] upon the [[Great Jedi Purge|execution]] of [[Order 66]] in an attempt to kill Kenobi.<ref name="Episode III" />
