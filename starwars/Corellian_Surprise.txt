'''Corellian Surprise''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[fathier]] with [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] and black [[eye]]s<ref name="Outlaws" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[0 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> they were 289 [[centimeter]]s tall and weighed 957 [[kilogram]]s when they took part in the [[Canto Cup]] [[Fathier racing|fathier race]] that was held in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. Just before the start of the race, a Canto Cup [[Unidentified Canto Cup announcer (Basic)|announcer]] commented that the fathier looked eager to begin the race.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Corellian Surprise shared their name with<ref name="Outlaws" /> the planet [[Corellia]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian Surprise appeared in a hologram in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They are one of the fathiers that can randomly feature in the optional Canto Fathier Racing activity. In most races, the six fathiers are assigned a random [[jockey]] and a random symbol. As such, this article does not assume one pairing or symbol color to be [[canon]]ical.<ref name="Outlaws" />
