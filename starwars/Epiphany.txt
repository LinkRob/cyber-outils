'''Epiphany''' was the outermost of three [[planet]]s that [[orbit]]ed a [[Salient's red dwarf|red dwarf]] [[star]] in the [[Salient system]],<ref name="Catalyst" /> in the [[Aparo sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Its [[Epiphany moon|moon]] was the location of the [[Zerpen Industries headquarters|headquarters]] of [[Zerpen Industries]].<ref name="Catalyst" /> The [[Second Battle of Epiphany]] took place during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and the early [[Standard year|years]] of [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] [[Soran Keize]]'s service in the [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]]. Having been involved in the battle, Keize reflected on it as one of the events which taught him his responsibility to protect those close to him in order to protect the lives of even more.<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Epiphany first appeared in the [[2016]] novel ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'', written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Catalyst" />
