"'''Count to Three'''" is a short story written by [[Joanna Berry]] featuring the characters [[Shen]] and [[Havina Vonreg]] from ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''. It was released on the [[Electronic Arts|EA Games]] website on [[October 13]], [[2020]]. It has since been delisted.
