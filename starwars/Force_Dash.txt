'''Force Dash''',<ref name="Battlefront" /> also known as '''Force Speed''',<ref name="Destiny">''[[Star Wars: Destiny Two-Player Game]]''</ref> or '''speed burst''' was an [[Force power|ability]] of [[the Force]] that allowed its user to increase their speed for a brief [[time]].<ref name="Episode I" /> The increased speed of the [[Force-sensitive|Force-user]] enabled the individual to perceive and react to the world and the entities around them in slow motion, allowing them to dodge attacks easily and attack more quickly with greater accuracy.<ref name="Fallen Order" /><ref name="M&A">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> While powerful in short bursts, overexertion of the ability could quickly drain the Force-user of their energy for a brief period of [[time]].<ref name="Brotherhood" /> With sufficient skill in Force Dash, users were able to run on vertical surfaces for brief periods of time, an ability used by [[Jedi]] such as [[Cal Kestis]]<ref name="Fallen Order" /> and [[Jocasta Nu]].<ref name="Dark Lord 9">[[Darth Vader (2017) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 9]]</ref>

While aboard the [[Trade Federation]] [[starship]] ''[[Saak'ak]]'', [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] used Force Dash to escape from [[droideka]]s.<ref name="Episode I" /> Kenobi could also use Force Speed to increase his reaction time, an ability he used to quickly assess a situation and briefly escape capture while undergoing [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Kenobi and Skywalker)|business on Cato Neimoidia]].<ref name="Brotherhood" /> [[Darth Sidious]], a [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], was also capable of utilizing Force Dash.<ref name="Battlefront" /> [[Second Sister|The Second Sister]] could move to greater speeds, and she was able to dodge incoming blaster bolts.<ref name="Fallen Order" /> [[Luke Skywalker]] utilized Force Speed during his training on [[Dagobah]],<ref name="Covert Missions">{{Destiny|set=Covert Missions|cardname=Jedi Trials|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/12065}}</ref> and later to slice through [[Boba Fett]]’s [[blaster carbine]] with [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Spirit of Rebellion">{{Destiny|set=Spirit of Rebellion|cardname=Force Speed|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/02055}}</ref>
