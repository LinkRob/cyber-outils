'''Chass na Chadic''' was a [[Theelin]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a pilot for multiple groups, starting with the [[Cavern Angels]], and eventually joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Hound Squadron]], where she flew a [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]], during the [[Imperial Era]]. Chadic continued to serve with Hound Squadron after the [[Battle of Endor]] until it was destroyed. Chadic then joined the [[New Republic]]'s [[Alphabet Squadron]] and continued to fly a B-wing.

==Biography==
===Finding herself===
[[Reproduction|Born]] around [[21 BBY|21]] and [[16 BBY]],<ref name="Chass age" /> Chass na Chadic was a [[Theelin]] [[Gender|female]] who was a [[pilot]] for the [[New Republic]]. Growing up in the [[Galactic Empire]], who were adamantly [[Xenophobia|xenophobic]], Chadic struggled to carve out an identity as a Theelin without any guidance. Throughout her life, Chadic had seen her heroes [[Death|die]] while fighting for freedom, while she had been left behind.<ref name="Chadic 1">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/1131561469844361217|[[Del Rey]]|quote=Chass na Chadic has watched her heroes fall one after another in the name of freedom, leaving her behind; now, as the tide turns, Chass is adrift. Alphabet's mission may be her chance to find meaning in her life—if she can be convinced not to seek it in death. #AlphabetSquadron|archiveurl=https://archive.is/XppCl}}</ref> At one point, Chadic working on the planet [[Uchinao]] in the [[Five Points system]] when she was attacked by a street gang. However, before they could rob her, Chadic was saved by a woman that called herself Liana Hallik. A year later, when Chadic was on [[Koiogra]], she realized the true identity of her savior when she robbed a black-market shop that sold [[holovid]]s stealing [[music]], which she began a collection of music to listen and sing along with during battle, and a rebel [[propaganda]] video. Upon viewing it, she realized who that she had met [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] hero [[Jyn Erso]], and began to idolize her.<ref name="Chadic 2">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/1131561471371108354|[[Del Rey]]|quote=Fun facts about Chass: She keeps a collection of music in her B-wing to listen to (and sing-a-long with) during battle. One of her fallen heroes? Jyn Erso|archiveurl=https://archive.is/aPwXJ}}</ref> Before joining the Rebel Alliance, Chadic traveled to [[Jedha]] and flew with the [[Cavern Angels]] and at one point, was associated with the [[Unignited Stars]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

At one point, Chadic joined [[Hound Squadron]] and began to fly a [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]]. Chadic once stole fellow Hound Squadron member [[Yeprexi]]'s ship and crashed it into a bog, but fortunately, Yeprexi never told any of their superiors about it.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

Chadic later flew with Hound Squadron on a recon mission aboard the ''[[Hellion's Dare]]'', which was stationed on the [[planet]] [[Jiruus]]. The ''Hellion's Dare'' received a transmission from a scout that caused the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] to pursue them. Chadic and Hound Squadron flew alongside the [[RZ-2 A-wing interceptor]]s of [[Riot Squadron]] to defend the frigate. When Riot Squadron pilot [[Nasi Moreno]] was seen surrounded and never turned up after the jump to hyperspace, Chadic became furious when Riot Squadron's leader, [[Rununja]], declared Moreno killed in action. As the ''Hellion's Dare'' flew into the [[Oridol Cluster]], the squadrons were pursued and their pilots were methodically picked off one by one. Chadic scored the first kill and was ironically dubbed the "Queen of Starfighters" by Riot Squadron pilot [[Sata Neek]]. In the next battle, Chadic's close friend [[Fadime]] was killed, and despite previously thinking the funerals were silly, Chadic was reduced to tears at Fadime's.

After numerous attacks, the squadrons reformed into one group under the leadership of Rununja. Chadic's callsign changed to [[Riot Ten]]. When [[Kreskian]], the captain of the ''Hellion's Dare'' ordered the new squadron to attack the 204th's [[Aerie|Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier]], Chadic flew alongside two other former Hound Squadron pilots [[Merish]] and [[Glothe]]. When Glothe was killed, Chadic and Merish successfully damaged the enemy cruiser-carrier, but returned to the ''Hellion's Dare'' and saw their ship's engines in flames and hyperdrive disabled. The new squadron was eventually decimated in multiple ensuing confrontations, leaving only Chadic and [[Wyl Lark]] to flee. They were rescued by a working group led by [[Agent]] [[Caern Adan]], and they later melded into [[Alphabet Squadron]] under the command of [[Lieutenant]] and so-called [[Alphabet Leader]] [[Yrica Quell]] with [[Nath Tensent]] and [[Kairos]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> 

===Serving Alphabet Squadron===
Chadic, still angry with Lark and not entirely trusting of any of her new squadron members, remained hard to reach as the squadron did exercises in the [[Gobreton minefield]] and in the [[flight simulator]]s onboard the ''[[Lodestar]]''. At one point, Chadic skipped out on a squadron briefing and volunteered herself to join a group of [[New Republic Special Forces]] on a mission. When Quell learned of her behavior, she lost her composure and shoved Chadic into a wall.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

[[File:YricaCoverTextless.png|thumb|left|250px|Yrica Quell (pictured) was Chass na Chadic's commanding officer and eventual lover]]
When Alphabet Squadron was sent on a reconnaissance [[Mission to Abednedo|mission]] to the planet [[Abednedo (planet)|Abednedo]], Chadic landed on the planet with Quell and Kairos while Lark and Tensent stayed in orbit. Chadic and Quell advanced into [[Neshorino]] and met with a [[Verpine]] [[Unidentified Verpine smuggler|smuggler]], who showed them a group of Imperial prisoners. When Chadic escorted one of the prisoners, a stormtrooper sergeant, back to Kairos, she was attacked by a group of stormtroopers. Using the prisoner as a shield, she made her way to Kairos, who was dispatching the troopers with her [[Bowcaster]]. Quell then ordered Chadic and Kairos to take the prisoner into orbit. Chadic watched as the freighter escorting the other prisoners was destroyed, and returned with the squadron to the ''Lodestar'' with the sergeant.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

Eventually, Alphabet Squadron received its next mission and was sent to a [[Unidentified moon (Harkrova I)|moon]] of the planet [[Harkrova]] for a salvage mission. On the hike down from the mountain where they landed to the supply cache, Chadic took the time to thank Kairos for her help in saving her during the mission to Abednedo, which Kairos silently acknowledged. Chadic continued to ignore Lark, who she still held responsible for interfering with her efforts in the Oridol Cluster. However, when Lark called upon her to tell a story as Tensent, Kairos, and himself had already done, Chadic told the story of when she met Jyn Erso.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

===After the war===
After the [[Battle of Jakku]] ended the Galactic Civil War, Chadic would find her post-military career pension brought down to a single year's worth due to record of disciplinary actions, which left her in desperate need of money eleven months after the battle as the final pension month approached. She also attempted to go to therapy on the planet [[Corulag]], where she enjoyed sessions with a [[Unidentified bearded snake-woman therapist|snake-woman]] but ultimately found the whole process frustrating. After she missed an appointment due to a broken down tram and learning that woman was unable to reschedule for a month, she dropped therapy altogether rather than continue with a droid. Finding she had nowhere else to go, however, she stayed on Corulag.<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

By [[11 ABY]],<ref name="LastDate">The final subchapter of ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' states that it has been six [[Standard year|years]] since the end of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Since the [[Battle of Jakku|end of the war]] was in [[5 ABY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', that last subchapter must take place in [[11 ABY]].</ref> she had reunited with Quell and began a relationship with her.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Personality and traits==
Chadic grew up struggling with her identity as a Theelin and felt the xenophobic influence of the Empire. After realizing she met Jyn Erso, she was inspired to do something great and went on her own sort of pilgrimage by joining the Cavern Angels and eventually the Rebel Alliance. She held a grudge after Wyl Lark's actions in the Oridol Cluster and took her time in forgiving him. During the [[Battle of Pandem Nai|assault on Pandem Nai]], she almost rammed a [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|cruiser]] as she regretted that she lived while many of her heroes had died. She had a contentious relationship with fellow pilot Lark, who Chadic felt had taken away her agency when he forced them to retreat from the Cluster. Chadic often played music when flying, listening to genres that included [[Herglic rage-metal]], [[deva]] pop, and [[glimmik]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|In other cases, it was more of a 'Well, B-Wings are ridiculous and aggressive. What kind of character is going to be in there?' That felt like a natural fit for Chass [na Chadic], who is our born Rebel&ndash; our very aggressive, easily offended, and often brutal Theelin character.|Alexander Freed|<ref name="Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://www.laughingplace.com/w/articles/2020/06/26/interview-star-wars-shadow-fall-an-alphabet-squadron-novel-author-alexander-freed|text=Interview &ndash; "Star Wars: Shadow Fall &ndash; An Alphabet Squadron Novel" Author Alexander Freed|author=Celestino, Mike|date=2020-06-26|archivedate=20210219075341}}</ref>}}
Chass na Chadic first appeared in the [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> Freed assigned Chadic to the B-wing of Alphabet Squadron, feeling that her traits matched the starfighter's nature. His original plans for the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy included Chadic's interactions with the [[Children of the Empty Sun]] cult in the second installment, ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', and her character's change as a result.<ref name="Interview" />
