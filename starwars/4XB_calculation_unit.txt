A '''4XB Calculation Unit''', was a part used in [[starship]] weapons and systems. Specific weapons that used the unit were [[C-5B plasburst cannon]]s, [[M-72 concussion missile]] launchers and [[M-IO8 ion missile]] launchers. The units were also used in the [[S30B deflector shield]] system.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
4XB Calculation Units first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' that was published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game it was used as an ship upgrade material to improve the ''[[Trailblazer]]'s'' weapons and shields.<ref name="Outlaws" />
