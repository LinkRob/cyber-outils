'''Ansiv Garmuth''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[First Order officer|officer]] who served in the [[First Order Security Bureau]]. Holding the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[colonel]], he served aboard the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]'', the [[flagship]] of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] and headquarters of the [[First Order]].<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> [[Resistance]] members [[Finn]] and [[Rose Tico]], while undercover aboard the [[Starship|vessel]], had a brief run-in with Garmuth.<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|His actual part was cut, it'll be in the deleted scenes. He's in the finished movie for half a second, blink and you'll miss him when F&R are caught at the tracker.|Rian Johnson|<ref name="Rian Johnson">{{WebCite|url=https://heroichollywood.com/last-jedi-director-cut-game-thrones-star|text='The Last Jedi' Director Reveals Cut Scene Involving 'Game of Thrones' Star|author=Dayani, Aahil|date=2018-01-13|work=Heroic Hollywood|archivedate=20180122062220}}</ref>}}
In [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], Colonel Garmuth was played by British actor [[Ralph Ineson]].<ref name="The Last Jedi">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> According to director [[Rian Johnson]], the character originally played a bigger part in the story, but most of his footage ended up being cut from the final movie.<ref name="Rian Johnson" /> The cut material is available in a deleted scene on the [[2018]] Blu-ray release of the film.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://io9.gizmodo.com/all-the-new-things-we-learned-from-the-star-wars-the-l-1823756546|text=All the New Things We Learned From the ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Blu-Ray|author=Lussier, Germain|date=2018-03-14|quote=The home release has the extended version of the "Mega Destroyer Incursion" by Finn, Rose, and DJ. It includes a lot more tension, more humor, a visual homage to the classic '50s romantic dramedy ''The Apartment'', and even a cameo by Ralph Ineson (''The Witch'') as a First Order officer who's onto their games.|archivedate=20200316142412}}</ref>
