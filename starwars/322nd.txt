The '''322nd''' was a [[clone cadet]] unit within the [[Clone Youth Brigade]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] active during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Death Trap" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Death Trap]]" to [[21 BBY]].</ref> [[Boba Fett]] infiltrated the [[Clone Youth Brigade]], and when asked by fellow [[Cloning|clone]] [[cadet]] [[Whiplash (Clone Youth Brigade)|Whiplash]], used the cover that he was transferred from the 322nd. As a result of his infiltration, Fett was able to [[Sabotage of the Endurance|sabotage]] the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Endurance]]'' in an attempt to [[assassin]]ate [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] for the murder of his father, [[Jango Fett]].<ref name="Death Trap" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 322nd was first mentioned in "[[Death Trap]]," the twentieth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the TV series, ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' that was released in [[2010]].<ref name="Death Trap" />
