The '''communications tower''' was a feature of [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]]<ref name="Shattered Empire, Part III">[[Shattered Empire 3|''Shattered Empire'' 3]]</ref> and upgraded<ref name="BS">{{InsiderCite|issue1=149|issue2=150|Blade Squadron (short story)}}</ref> [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyers]].<ref name="UltimateSW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Located on top of the [[conning tower]], it sat between two [[deflector shield]] [[Deflector shield generator|generator]] globes,<ref name="SWCT">[[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]]</ref> replacing the [[tractor beam targeting array]] that was in the same position on ''Imperial I''-classes.<ref name="DiD-book">[[Droids in Distress (book)|''Droids in Distress'' chapter book]]</ref>
