'''Bistro De Lothal''' was a [[restaurant]] built on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] which was in business during the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]]. For 125 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], the Bistro served a four-course [[Foodstuff|meal]] of the [[Standard day|day]] which consisted of [[crystal mushroom soup]], sautéed [[spring]] greens from five planets, [[groat chop]] with fresh [[candleberry reduction sauce]], and [[soufflé a la Lothal]] with [[jogan fruit drizzle]]. The meal of the day menu was included on [[Painting|fliers]] which the eatery handed out, one of which the street urchin [[Ezra Bridger]] received and placed in his [[Rebel Journal|journal]].<ref name="RJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bistro De Lothal was first mentioned in ''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]'', a young readers book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and released in [[2014]].
