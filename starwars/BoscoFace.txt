'''BoscoFace''' was a [[Lasat]] who appeared on a [[BoscoFace (poster)|decorative poster]], three [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Their name was printed on the poster, in [[Aurebesh]] characters. The poster, along with other illustrations, was used to decorate one of the crates at [[Zeb's Hideout]], a [[cargo container]] at [[Chopper Base]] on [[Atollon]], where fellow Lasat [[Garazeb Orrelios]] relaxed.<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base" />

Later, when the [[E-XD-series infiltrator droid|infiltrator droid]] [[EXD-9]] scanned Orrelios, the scan resulted in "[[Species|SPECIES]]: LASAT" and "NON BOSCO FACE".<ref name="Warhead">{{Rebels|Warhead (episode)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The name "BoscoFace" is most likely a reference to [[Bosco Ng]], who directed the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episodes "[[The Mystery of Chopper Base]]"<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base" /> and "[[Warhead (episode)|Warhead]],"<ref name="Warhead" /> in which the character's name appears.
