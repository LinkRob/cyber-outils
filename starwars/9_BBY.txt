The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''9 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3268 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7968''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] is moved to [[Scarif]].<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
**To cover up the existence of the Death Star, the [[Galactic Empire]] launches the [[sterilization of Geonosis]],<ref name="Visual Guide" /> bringing its multi&ndash;[[Standard year|year]] [[Second occupation of Geonosis|occupation]] to an end. {{Circa}}<ref name="Geonosis note" />
*The [[Inquisitor]]s' [[hunt for Obi-Wan]] Kenobi kicks off after ten years of no progress.<ref name="Part I" />
**Young [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] is taken hostage in a plot created by Reva Sevander, the [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]], to find [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="Part I" /> Her goal is to draw Kenobi out to get closer to the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and kill him to avenge her fellow [[Jedi Initiate]]s, whom Vader slew during [[Operation: Knightfall|the Siege of the Jedi Temple]].<ref name="Part V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref>
**Kenobi regains his Force connection while rescuing Organa on the planet [[Daiyu]] and learns from the Third Sister that Vader is alive.<ref name="Part II">{{Kenobi|Part II}}</ref>
**Vader [[Battle on Mapuzo|finds and pursues]] Kenobi on the planet [[Mapuzo]]. However, before Vader can exact revenge on his former Jedi Master, Kenobi is escorted to safety by members of the [[Hidden Path]]. At the same time, Organa is captured by the Third Sister<ref name="Part III">{{Kenobi|Part III}}</ref> and brought to the [[Fortress Inquisitorius]] on the [[moon]] of [[Nur]].<ref name="Part IV">{{Kenobi|Part IV}}</ref>
**Kenobi and members of the Path successfully retrieve Organa from Nur, but the Third Sister hides a [[homing beacon]]<ref name="Part IV" /> on Organa's [[toy droid]] [[L0-LA59]] "Lola," leading the Empire to the Path's base on [[Jabiim]].<ref name="Part IV">{{Kenobi|Part IV}}</ref>
**Despite the Empire launching an [[Attack on Jabiim|all-out attack on Jabiim]], Kenobi's knowledge of Vader's tactics and the Third Sister's treason ensure the Path can escape. Vader impales the Third Sister with her [[Reva's lightsaber|own lightsaber]] and leaves her for dead<ref name="Part V" /> to continue the hunt for Kenobi.<ref name="Part VI" />
**Alerted to the existence of [[Luke Skywalker]] by an [[imagecaster]] left on Jabiim,<ref name="Part V" /> Reva travels to [[Tatooine]] to kill Vader's son.<ref name="Part VI" />

[[File:TheConfrontation-KarlFitzgerald.png|thumb|right|250px|Kenobi and Vader's rematch on a barren moon]]
**Vader and Kenobi engage in a [[Fight on a barren moon|lightsaber duel]] on a [[Unidentified barren moon|barren moon]], which Kenobi ultimately wins. When Vader declares it was he who "killed" Anakin Skywalker, Kenobi believes his old friend is dead and departs the moon.<ref name="Part VI" /> 
**After fighting past [[Beru Whitesun Lars|Beru]] and [[Owen Lars]], Reva is unable to strike Luke down after cornering him in [[Beggar's Canyon]] and returns him safely. Kenobi, who flew straight to Tatooine after sensing Skywalker was in trouble, assures Reva that she honored her friends by not giving in to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] and encourages her to take a new path forward.<ref name="Part VI" />
**Organa is returned home to [[Alderaan]] and reunites with Kenobi, who urges her to keep their connection a secret.<ref name="Part VI" />
**Kenobi formally meets Luke Skywalker for the first time and reunites with the [[Force spirit]] of [[Qui-Gon Jinn]].<ref name="Part VI" />
*[[Cal Kestis]] and [[Stinger Mantis crew|his crew]] locates the planet [[Tanalorr]], hidden in the [[Koboh Abyss]].
**The majority of Kestis' crew is killed during the [[mission on Coruscant]].<ref name="Survivor" />
**Kestis crashes the [[Stinger Mantis|Mantis]] on the Outer Rim planet [[Koboh]], reuniting with his old friend, [[Greez Dritus]], in an effort to repair the Mantis.<ref name="Survivor" />
**[[Dagan Gera]] awakens on Koboh and turns to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]], teaming up with the [[Bedlam Raiders]].
**Kestis reunites the crew of the Mantis - Dritus, [[Cere Junda]], and [[Merrin]] - as well as Master [[Eno Cordova]]. He discovers the [[Hidden Path]], and begins to consider the prospect of using Tanalorr as a safe haven for the Path.<ref name="Survivor" />
**[[Rayvis]], a [[Gen'Dai]] member of the Bedlam Raiders, is killed by Kestis on the [[Shattered Moon]] of Koboh.
**Gera is killed by Kestis and [[Bode Akuna]] on Koboh.
**Bode gives the location of the [[Hidden Path]] on Jedha to the Empire and kills [[Eno Cordova]]'''.'''
**[[Cere Junda]] is killed by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on Jedha.
**Bode and [[Kata Akuna|his daughter]] escape to Tanalorr and are followed by Kestis and Merrin, where Bode is killed in combat.
*[[Miara Larte]] is promoted to the rank of [[captain]] at a ceremony on Alderaan.<ref name="By Whatever Sun">{{StoryCite|story=By Whatever Sun|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref><ref name="Miara Rank">In "[[By Whatever Sun]]," which takes place at the [[Royal Award Ceremony]], [[Miara Larte]] reminisces that her promotion to [[captain]] took place nine years before. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states that the ceremony takes place in [[0 ABY]], thus dating Miara Larte's promotion to 9 BBY.</ref>
*[[4-LOM]] and [[Zuckuss]] attempt to capture [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]],<ref name="Free2018">{{HelmetCollectionCite|78|Highlights of the Saga}} places the events of ''[[Star Wars Adventures Free Comic Book Day 2018]]'' in 9 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> before crashing onto a [[Unidentified planet (droid uprising)|planet]] full of [[scorpion-droid]]s.<ref name="PoweredDown">''[[Powered Down, Part 1]]'' and ''[[Powered Down, Part 2|II]]'' takes place immediately after ''[[Star Wars Adventures Free Comic Book Day 2018]]'', which {{HelmetCollectionCite|78|Highlights of the Saga}} places in [[Galactic standard calendar|9 BBY]].</ref>
