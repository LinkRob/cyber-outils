'''BG01-1''' was a [[BG01-1 farming droid]] programmed for [[Agrirobot|farming]] and [[Security droid|security]] in [[Habo village]] on the [[planet]] [[Habo]]. It was capable of moving back and forth on a [[repulsorlift]], automatically collecting [[Plant|weeds]] and monitoring the fields. It was conical in shape and had a [[Color|black]] [[photoreceptor]] on its grey, white, and green outer shell. The top of the [[droid]] had an [[antenna]] and was able to rise from the base, revealing a second black [[sensor]] and a red sensor. It also had three retractable [[Limb|arms]] with two prongs each for plucking weeds.<ref name="Art" />

==Behind the scenes==
BG01-1 was created for the "[[The Elder (short film)|The Elder]]," a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|first volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Trigger (animation studio)|Trigger]], was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="The Elder" /> on [[September 22]], [[2021]].<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/anime-expo-lite-star-wars-visions|text=Anime Expo Lite: 20 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: Visions'' Panel}}</ref> The droid, however, did not appear in the episode.<ref name="The Elder">{{Visions|The Elder (short film)}}</ref> Concept art for the droid appeared in [[Zack Davisson]]'s [[2022]] reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]''. Its design was based on traditional [[Wikipedia:Asian conical hat|Asian conical hats]].<ref name="Art" />
