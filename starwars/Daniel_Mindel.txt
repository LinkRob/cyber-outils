'''Daniel Mindel''' is a [[Wikipedia:South Africa|South African]]-[[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Wikipedia:Cinematographer|cinematographer]] who has frequently worked with film directors [[Wikipedia:Ridley Scott|Ridley Scott]], [[Wikipedia:Tony Scott|Tony Scott]] and [[J.J. Abrams]]. On [[August 22]], [[2013]], Mindel confirmed that he would serve as Abrams' cinematographer on [[2015]]'s [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. The announcement came one day before cinematographer [[Gilbert Taylor]], who originally filmed [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], passed away. Mindel returned as cinematographer for [[2019]]'s [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref>{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-ix-cast-announced|text=''Star Wars'': Episode IX Cast Announced}}</ref>
