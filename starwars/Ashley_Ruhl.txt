'''Ashley Ruhl''' is a [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developer who worked at [[BioWare]] on the game ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' and its extensions. She originally worked as a cinematic designer and later became the game's narrative director.<ref name="GUSWTOR" />
