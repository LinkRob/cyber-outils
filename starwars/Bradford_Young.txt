'''Bradford Young''' (born [[July 6]], [[1977]]<ref name="SWYT 2020-07-09" />) is an [[Wikipedia:Academy Awards|Academy Award]]&ndash;nominated American cinematographer notable for his work on the [[2016]] film [[Wikipedia:Arrival (film)|''Arrival'']]. He served as the Director of Photography for the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Han Solo" />
