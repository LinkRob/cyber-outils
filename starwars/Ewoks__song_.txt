"'''Ewoks'''" is the opening theme from the [[Ewoks: Season One|first season]] of the [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']] animated television series. The song was written and performed by [[Wikipedia:Taj Mahal (musician)|Taj Mahal and Inshira Mahal]].
