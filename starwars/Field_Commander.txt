'''Field Commander''' was a position held by [[commanding officer]]s on the ground during battle. The [[Stormtrooper Commander]] [[Atty]] held the position during the [[Galactic Empire]]'s pacification of [[Ontotho]].<ref name="Dark Temple 1" /> For the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo]], [[OOM command battle droid]] [[OOM-9]] served as the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]]'s Field Commander during the [[Great Grass Plains]] [[Battle of Naboo|battle]] on [[Naboo]].<ref name="BTCTG" /> The [[Kilji]] [[species]]' battle [[fleet]], the [[Illumine Kilhorde]], had several field commanders.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
