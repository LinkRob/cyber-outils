'''Aglis flyers''' were winged creatures [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[moon]] [[Toshara]], where the locals referred to them colloquially as just '''flyers'''. The creatures were notorious for [[Thief|stealing]] any item they could lay their [[Limb|hands]] on, hoarding what they took in their remote nests. They were known to migrate across the moon, often passing through the narrow river canyon known colloquially as [[Mirogana Gate|The Gate]]. The [[Foodstuff|meat]] of flyers was popular and, despite the creatures' agility and highly developed sense of sight, the Tosharan's [[wind-fishing net]]s were effective at capturing them.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aglis flyers were created for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which released on [[August 30]], [[2024]]. Prior to the game's release, the creatures were revealed in a gameplay preview released on [[June 10]], 2024.<ref name="Gameplay">{{UbisoftYouTube|490LVhVGAqo|Star Wars Outlaws Official Gameplay: All the Star Wars Lore, Details & Easter Eggs &#124; Ubisoft Forward}}</ref>
