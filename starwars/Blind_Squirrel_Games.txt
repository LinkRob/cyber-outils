'''Blind Squirrel Games''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] development studio originally based out of [[Wikipedia:Santa Ana, California|Santa Ana]], [[Wikipedia:California|California]].<ref name="Web About">{{WebCite|url=https://blindsquirrelentertainment.com/about|text=About Blind Squirrel Games|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/XZOZY}}</ref> The studio was founded in [[2010]] by [[Brad Hendricks]],<ref name="LinkedIn About" /> who serves as President and CEO of both the studio, as well as their parent company, [[Wikipedia:Blind Squirrel Games|Blind Squirrel Entertainment, Inc]],<ref name="CEO" /> which Blind Squirrel Games was incorporated into as of company-wide a restructuring in [[March]] of [[2016]].<ref name="Parent" /> Since their founding, they have established their three major studio locations, one in [[Wikipedia:Irvine, California|Irvine]], [[Wikipedia:Orange County, California|Orange County]], California, one in [[Wikipedia:Austin, Texas|Austin]], [[Wikipedia:Texas|Texas]], and one in [[Wikipedia:Auckland|Auckland]], [[Wikipedia:New Zealand|New Zealand]], with remote employees across a total of 18 states and 3 countries.<ref name="Web About" />

The gaming studio specializes primarily in all forms of console gaming, including new IPs, as well as popular ports and remasters. Partnering with [[The Walt Disney Company]], [[Lucasfilm]], and [[Lenovo]], Blind Squirrel Games were one of two major game developers, the other being [[Schell Games]], who were tasked with designing the gameplay component of [[2017]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Challenges]]'' Virtual/Augmented Reality (VR/AR) experience. Working closely with [[Caleb Arseneaux]], a Managing Producer at Disney at the time who served as managing producer for the ''Jedi Challenges'' project,<ref name="The Last Jedi BTS">{{SW|url=news/5-behind-the-scenes-details-of-jedi-challenges-the-last-jedi-update|text=5 Behind-The-Scenes Details of ''Jedi Challenges' The Last Jedi'' Update}}</ref> employees at Blind Squirrel Games would create various game assets,<ref name="Mak">{{ArtStation|username=tricyclecaptain|url=artwork/yaEE3|name=[[Danny Mak]]|text=STAR WARS: Jedi Challenges &#124; Strategic Combat 2|quote=I had the pleasure of working on countless characters, environments/weapons and ships for Disney's Star Wars: Jedi Challanges &#124; Strategic Combat. An AR game for mobile. Here are some assets I can show from the game. They're all hand painted textures. Characters/weapons were 512x512 while some of the ships/vehicles are 1k. Used 3DCoat to bake textures and paint. Learned a lot on this project. Specifically pushing the amount of detail that I could fit onto a 515x512. Special thanks to Adam Schuman for helping establish a style.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/5bt5T}}</ref> level designs,<ref name="Caron">{{LinkedIn|url=in/kurtcaronkloster|text=Kurt Caron|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/A8jvB}}</ref> visual effects,<ref name="McNine">{{ArtStation|username=ericmcnine|url=artwork/1JBA8|name=[[Eric McNine]]|text=Star Wars: Jedi Challenges|quote=This was a mobile AR game I had the pleasure of working on. I created the effects for the strategic combat mode. Being a mobile game, we were limited on many factors like polycount and rendering features. In addition, it was augmented reality which presented its own unique challenges, such as making things clear and easy to see against a variety of backgrounds and in a number of lighting conditions.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/SI4TL}}</ref> and gameplay modes,<ref name="The Last Jedi BTS" /> while Lenovo developed the associated hardware.<ref name="Lenovo">{{WebCite|url=https://news.lenovo.com/pressroom/press-releases/star-wars-jedi-challenges-a-new-smartphone-powered-augmented-reality-experience-launching-this-holiday|text=Star Wars™: Jedi Challenges, A New Smartphone-Powered Augmented Reality Experience Launching this Holiday|archivedate=20231208102453}}</ref>

They would continue working on the game post-launch, helping to develop three major expansions: The "[[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''The Last Jedi'']] Update,"<ref name="The Last Jedi Update">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@disneygames|name=Disney Games|video=cG2mDrNld-c|text=Star Wars: Jedi Challenges &ndash; The Making of The Last Jedi AR Content|archivedate=20211021091025}}</ref> the "[[Dejarik|Holochess]] Update,"<ref name="The Holochess Update">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theverge.com/2018/4/18/17251802/star-wars-holochess-dejarik-arkit-augmented-reality-jedi-challenges-ios-app|text=Star Wars holochess comes to the iPhone with ARKit|archivedate=20230528184321}}</ref> and the "[[Dark side of the Force|Dark Side]] Expansion."<ref name="The Dark Side Expansion">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-jedi-challenges-dark-side-expansion|text=Star Wars: ''Jedi Challenges'' Turns to the Dark Side}}</ref> Employees at Blind Squirrel Games were some of the first outside of Lucasfilm to learn of the existence of the [[All Terrain MegaCaliber Six]], as they were assigned with developing the [[Crait]] strategic combat arena for the ''The Last Jedi'' tie-in expansion. In an interview with [[StarWars.com]], Arseneaux explained, "I said, 'Listen, I can't tell you guys a lot. But here's where the AT-AT comes up to me,' and I pointed to my thigh. 'And here's where the AT-M6 needs to be on me,' and I pointed to my head. And they were like, 'Ohhh. Okay.'"<ref name="The Last Jedi BTS" />

At the [[Wikipedia:2018 Webby Awards|2018 Webby Awards]], ''Star Wars: Jedi Challenges'' earned Blind Squirrel Games and their partners awards in four categories, including the "Official categories" of "Technical Achievement" and "Best Use of Augmented Reality," as well as the "People's Voice categories" of "Technical Achievement" and "Best Use of Augmented Reality."<ref name="Webby">{{WebCite|url=https://winners.webbyawards.com/2018/games/features/best-use-of-augmented-reality/54582/star-wars-jedi-challenges|text=Star Wars: Jedi Challenges|work=[[Wikipedia:Webby Awards|Webby Awards]]|archivedate=20230205103838}}</ref> The game was eventually retired on [[June 1]], [[2022]], with no future content in the works, support ending completely only a month later on [[July 6]].<ref name="Retirement">{{SW|subdomain=support|url=hc/en-us/articles/6689295979156-Star-Wars-Jedi-Challenges-has-been-retired|text=Star Wars™: Jedi Challenges has been retired}}</ref> Blind Squirrel Games also contributed to the creation of ''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]'' and the ''[[Star Wars]]'' characters and settings introduced within it.<ref name="CEO" />
