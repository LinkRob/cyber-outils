'''Copper''' was a metal,<ref name="BL" /> with the chemical symbol '''Cu''',<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> that had a [[Color|reddish-brown]] tint comparable to the color of [[Mon Mothma]]'s [[hair]].<ref name="R1 novel" /> It had a smell<ref name="Inferno Squad">''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''</ref> and a taste resembling those of [[human]] [[blood]].<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> The presence of copper in compounds like grains of sand gave them a blue coloration.

Copper could be used in [[lightsaber]] construction.<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' takes place five [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Great Jedi Purge]], which is dated to [[19 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'' is set in [[14 BBY]].</ref> the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] acquired enough of it to fashion a lightsaber hilt.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], copper was used as a component in certain [[starship]] fuels.<ref name="Survival" /> Other uses of copper included [[droid]] plating.<ref name="BL" />
