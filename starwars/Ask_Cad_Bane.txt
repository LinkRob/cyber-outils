"'''Ask Cad Bane'''" is an article that appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 114|''Star Wars Insider'' 114]]. It is a change of style from the usual [[Ask Lobot]] series. In it, [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars'']] character [[Cad Bane/Legends|Cad Bane]] takes over the series from [[Lobot/Legends|Lobot]], and lists ten irreconcilable continuity errors in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' universe.<ref name="sw114" />
