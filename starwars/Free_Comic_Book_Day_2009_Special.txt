'''Free Comic Book Day 2009 Special''' is a [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]], which features the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (comic series)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'']] comic story "[[The Gauntlet of Death]]."

==Publisher's summary==
''This special issue features the greatest heroes of The Clone Wars, plus Emily the Strange, Usagi Yojimbo, and others. Packed with 28 pages of story, the book bears two covers, one with the Star Wars character, and another cover celebrating Dark Horse's best all-ages characters.<ref name="Dark Horse" />''
