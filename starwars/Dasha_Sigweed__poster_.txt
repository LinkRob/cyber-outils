'''''Dasha Sigweed''''' was a [[Painting|poster]] used to advertise a [[Dasha Sigweed|person]] of the same name on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It featured a blue, white and greenish-blue [[color]] palette and was written in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="Lethal" /> It could be found in a [[Unidentified massage parlor|massage parlor]] in [[Slum district G17]],<ref name="Lost">{{TCW|Lightsaber Lost}}</ref> in [[Terminal 24]], the [[Moshi Bar]],<ref name="Lethal" /> an [[Unidentified Coruscant cantina|unidentified cantina]],<ref name="Peace">{{TCW|Pursuit of Peace}}</ref> and the [[Refresher|latrine]] in the [[79's]] cantina.<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref>

Fifteen [[Standard year|years]] after the Clone Wars ended,<ref name="Day">{{Rebels|Empire Day (episode)}}</ref> the same poster was used to decorate the walls of the ''[[Ghost]]''.<ref name="Spark">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref>
