'''Bellassan peppers''' were a type of [[pepper]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> that shared its name with the celestial body [[Bellassa]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> They were [[Diet|edible]] by [[Chagrian]]s and were sometimes [[Temperature|chilled]] before serving.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' dates the interview with [[Mas Amedda]] to twenty-two [[Standard year|years]] after the [[proclamation of the New Order]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' places in [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, the interview must have taken place in [[3 ABY]].</ref> during an interview with<ref name="Rebel Files" /> [[journalist]]<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> [[Tracene Kane]] for [[HoloNet]] news' [[Queen of the Core Network]], senior [[Emperor's adviser|Imperial advisor]] [[Mas Amedda]] ate chilled Bellassan peppers, and as the interview progressed, servers cleared plates of the peppers away from the advisor's table.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bellassan peppers were introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via mention in ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', a [[2017]] reference book by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Bellassan pepper/Legends|Bellassan peppers]] were mentioned in the [[2009]] [[StarWars.com]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] article "[[Dining at Dex's]]," written by [[Gregory Walker]] as an [[in-universe]] menu.<ref name="DaD">{{Hyperspace|url=fans/hyperspace/source/dexdiner/index.html|text=Dining at Dex's|int=Dining at Dex's}}</ref>
