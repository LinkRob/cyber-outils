'''225''' was an [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]] owned by a [[Gran]] [[Unidentified Gran trader|trader]] on [[Pantora]]. Along with fellow astromech [[droid]] [[DK-3]] and the [[LE-series droid]] [[Clink]], 225 was supervised by the [[protocol droid]] [[CG-67]]. 225 and its fellow droids assisted the [[Cloning|clones]] of the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]] in repairing their [[starship]], the [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Marauder'']].<ref name="Cornered" />
