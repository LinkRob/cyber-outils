'''Emperor Palpatine's cane''' was, as its name suggested, a walking aid used by [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] during his reign as [[Galactic Emperor|ruler]] of the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="DK preview">{{DK|url=us/9781465459626-star-wars-visual-encyclopedia|text=''Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia'' preview|nobackup=1|nolive=1}}</ref> despite the fact that he was perfectly capable of walking without it.<ref name="RotJ" /> Twisted and gnarled, the cane had a glossy [[Color|black]] finish.<ref name="DK preview" /> The Emperor brought it with him when he visited the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]<ref name="RotJ" /> in [[4 ABY]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> in order to lure the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] into a trap. Shortly thereafter, however, the Death Star was destroyed during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="RotJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Emperor's cane first appeared in the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], the third and final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]].<ref name="RotJ" /> According to the reference book ''[[From Star Wars to Indiana Jones: The Best of the Lucasfilm Archives]]'', the original prop measured 20&times;94&times;3 centimeters.<ref name="From Star Wars to Indiana Jones: The Best of the Lucasfilm Archives">''[[From Star Wars to Indiana Jones: The Best of the Lucasfilm Archives]]''</ref>
