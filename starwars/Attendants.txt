The '''Attendants''' were a group of mute [[alien]] [[navigator]]s<ref name="TLJTVD" /> from an [[Unidentified insectoid species|ancient]] [[insectoid]] [[species]]<ref name="Helmet Collection 61">{{HelmetCollectionCite|61|Databank A-Z}}</ref> that was used by various factions in the [[Unknown Regions]], where they originated.<ref name="TLJTVD" /> They were active in the [[Imperial–Grysk conflict]] during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], as well as during the time of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], serving under First Order [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]].

They had a unique method of communication, where they used inaudible pulses of energy in wavelengths that were not perceptible to [[human]] [[eye]]s.<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>

==History==
The [[Grysk Hegemony]] made use of Attendants as their [[hyperspace]] [[navigator]]s through the dangerous [[Unknown Regions]]. The [[Grysk]] "[[Jixtus]]" even had a pair in his personal service. One of his pawns, the [[Agbui]] [[Haplif]], was disturbed by the beings, having never seen any other [[species]] like them and being unaware of where they had originated. Additionally unsettled by their eerie eye lenses, [[Color|purple]] [[robe]]s, and constant silence, he figured they came from somewhere far out within the Unknown Regions. Haplif and his fellow [[Agbui]] nonetheless used an Attendant as their navigator until Jixtus deployed the [[Pathfinders (Navigators' Guild)|Pathfinder]] [[Qilori]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

The Attendants were active in the [[Imperial Era]], during which time they still served Grysks. Under the Hegemony, the Attendants trained other [[alien]] [[species]] in [[Wookieepedia:Welcome, newcomers|navigation]] so that Grysk [[starship]]s could travel through the Unknown Regions. After an [[attack on a Grysk warship]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Chiss Ascendancy]], allied forces rescued [[Un'hee]], a [[Chiss]] [[Force-sensitive]]. Imperial Chiss [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] and Ascendancy [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] questioned Un'hee if there were other Chiss aboard the Grysk vessels that the allied forces were combating, which they denied as there were only other species that had been trained by the Attendants.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

Following the fall of the Galactic Empire at the [[Battle of Jakku]], what was left of the Empire fled to the Unknown Regions in order to escape from the [[New Republic]] and plan their revenge. Although most of the surviving [[Imperial officer]]s would have likely [[Death|died]] while crossing the uncharted Regions, the ancient [[hyperspace]] trails blazed by the Attendants helped them to stay there, where they eventually formed the [[First Order]]. The Attendants served the First Order's [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]],<ref name="TLJTVD" /> a genetically engineered [[Strand-Cast|strandcast]] created by the resurrected [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], after his first demise at the [[Battle of Endor]], who unwittingly and obliviously served as his puppet and proxy in power over the First Order.<ref name="The Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> At least two Attendants were aboard Snoke's [[flagship]], the ''[[Supremacy]]'', after the [[Battle of D'Qar|Evacuation of D'Qar]] in [[34 ABY]], where they had designed and operated the [[Oculus (device)|oculus]] viewing scope in Snoke's [[Snoke's throne room|throne room]].<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

Two Attendants<ref name="TLJ" /> were in Snoke's throne room when [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] entered the chamber after the battle over [[D'Qar]]. As Hux and Snoke conversed, the Attendants carried on with their mysterious work at the oculus viewing scope.<ref name="TLJEE">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Later, as [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] arrived, the Attendants slowly turned to look over to Snoke before turning back to the oculus. As Ren knelt before his master, the Attendant closest to the [[throne]] turned and slowly looked to Ren and kept its gaze on him while the other Attendant's attention remained on the oculus. As Snoke moved from his throne to Ren, the Attendant turned back to the oculus as the second Attendant moved to watch the interaction between Snoke and Ren.<ref name="TLJ" />

Both watched and did not react as the Supreme Leader struck Ren with [[Force lightning]] when he attempted to attack Snoke in a fit of anger. They continued to remain still as Snoke returned to his throne.<ref name="TLJ" /> Sometime later, Hux entered the throne room to find it in [[Battle of Oetchi|shambles]] and on [[fire]]. Looking around he saw that the Attendants had fled before seeing Snoke [[Death|dead]] in a crumpled heap before his throne.<ref name="TLJEE" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Snoke's aides were an Ivan Manzella design from ''The Force Awakens'', quite close to being used. They were also quite close to being used in ''Rogue One''. But no one seemed to find the place for them. And then suddenly, 'Bang!' It was Michael Kaplan that really drove the costume and their final look, more than anything&mdash;even the color of their faces. They were tailored toward that sort of servile, quiet, almost religious-type feel.|[[Neal Scanlan]] on the Attendants|<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]''</ref>}}
The Attendants first appeared in the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars:'' Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="TLJ" />

They were based on concept art by [[Ivan Manzella]] originally for [[J.J. Abrams]]' [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars:'' Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] and were considered for [[Gareth Edwards]]' [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars Anthology'']] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', before being finalized by the [[Lucasfilm]] artists for ''The Last Jedi'' by recommendation of [[Michael Kaplan]].<ref name="Art" />
