'''Fried Endorian chicken''' was a type of crispy meal served at [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. It consisted of four pieces of [[Tip-yip/Legends|Endorian chicken]] meat dipped in seasoned [[pom]] seed flour, then flash-fried in hot [[aola]]. This dish cost 7.9 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]].
