'''Fleximetal''' was a material from which [[Armor|cuirasses]] were made. Although it could keep [[blaster]] bolts from burning through its wearer's chest, a fleximetal cuirass could not reduce the power of their impact. [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] wore a fleximetal cuirass to protect his upper torso.<ref name="TRB">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref>
