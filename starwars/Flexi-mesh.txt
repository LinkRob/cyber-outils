'''Flexi-mesh''' was a type of lightweight, armor-grade material used in [[Blast armor]]. [[Syril Karn]] wore a [[Mk 35 lightweight blast vest]] made out of flexi-mesh on his mission to arrest [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] on [[Ferrix]].<ref name="DRVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>
