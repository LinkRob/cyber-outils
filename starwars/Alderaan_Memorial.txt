The '''Alderaan Memorial''' was a structure on [[Coruscant]] designed to preserve the memory of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]], which had been [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Its archives notably contained a [[Breha Organa Collection|collection]] of artifacts pertaining to [[Alderaanian monarchy|Queen]] [[Breha Organa]], which included a [[Letter from Bail Organa to Breha Organa|message]] her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] wrote to her to announce the [[death]] of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], and his upcoming arrival with the senator's orphaned newborn, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Amidala Skywalker]].<ref name="Backstories" />
