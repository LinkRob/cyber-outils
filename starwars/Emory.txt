'''Emory''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual and the [[Marriage|husband]] of [[Varko Grey]], [[Titan Leader|leader]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Titan Squadron]]. Despite the dangers faced in the [[Galactic Civil War]], Grey managed to survive battle after battle, much to Emory's relief.<ref name="Grey DB" /> Emory worked on [[Kuat]].<ref name="SW Pride">''{{SW|url=news/8-star-wars-characters-to-celebrate-pride-month|text=8 ''Star Wars'' Characters to Celebrate Pride Month}}''</ref>
