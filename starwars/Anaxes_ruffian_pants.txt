'''Anaxes ruffian pants''' were a type of [[Color|grey]] [[Clothing|trousers]] worn by common outlaws<ref name="Outlaws" />  on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|fort-anaxes|Fort Anaxes}}</ref> [[Anaxes]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> They included dark grey leg wraps and protective [[boot]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Ugnaught]] [[smuggler]] [[Jet Kordo]] had heard about the [[Pyke Syndicate]] stashing a pair of the pants at their [[Pyke Syndicate workshop|workshop]] in the [[Mirage (Toshara)|Mirage]] region of [[Toshara]], a [[moon]] also located in the Outer Rim. Kordo included the pants in a note he had wrote about the important of getting high quality gear while operating as a smuggler and outlaw. A copy of the note was stored on a [[datapad]] that in-turn was stored on the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', his [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" />  the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] [[Westhill Palace robbery|stole]] the ''Trailblazer'' from [[Sliro Barsha|Sliro Rudback]], the [[Crime lord|leader]] of the [[Zerek Besh]] [[syndicate]], who had acquired it after Kordo. She subsequently crash landed on Toshara and had the [[starship]] repaired by the [[Rodian]] [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Waka]] who found the datapad with Kordo's information on the pants.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Vess eventually snuck into the workshop and stole a pair of Anaxes ruffian pants.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A pair of Anaxes ruffian pants appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When worn, the pants reduced the damage taken by the player while they were sprinting.<ref name="Outlaws" />
