'''Bilder''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|male]] who was a member of [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]]. He typically worked in a crew with [[Xosad Hozem]] and [[Jari]]. He joined Gerrera, Hozem, Jari, and [[Jyn Erso]] on a mission to raid a [[CCR-class pinnace|civilian cruiser]] that the [[Galactic Empire]] was using for clandestine operations in the [[Western Reaches]]. The mission was a success, with Bilder securing a stack of Imperial [[datapad]]s to analyze. Bilder was later [[Death|killed]] in action.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
