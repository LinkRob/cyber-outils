'''Adjustments''' was a branch of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Intelligence]] organization. The elite secret agents that were part of Adjustments received critical assignments, and thus were allowed extreme flexibility to implement those missions. The orders given to the agents were usually unwritten.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Adjustments branch was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] through the [[2018]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplement ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Dawn" /> The [[Adjustments/Legends|branch]] also existed in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, being first mentioned in the [[1989]] ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'', which was authored by [[Greg Gorden]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="IS">''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]''</ref> The group performed a similar role of using field agents to gather information.<ref name="Imperial Handbook">''[[Star Wars: Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide]]''</ref>
