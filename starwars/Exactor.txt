The '''''Exactor''''' was a [[Star Destroyer]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Supposedly, the [[Imperial officer]] [[Alger Denholm]] served aboard the [[Starship|vessel]]. After Denholm's superior, [[Lieutenant]] [[Marsden]], was [[Death|killed]] by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], Denholm was promoted, but began to go mad, seeing visions of Marsden's death.<ref name="The Predecessor" />
