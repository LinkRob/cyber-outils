'''Airo''', originally known only by the number '''AR-9771''', was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. He was [[Death|killed]] in [[Battle of Abrion Bridge|battle]] while fighting against the [[Separatist Droid Army]] on [[Abrion Major]]<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> around the [[Standard year|year]] [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> At the [[time]] of his death, Airo had just adopted his name and was serving in the [[212th Attack Battalion]] under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody|CC-2224]] "Cody." Kenobi attempted to save the [[Cloning|clone]], but Airo implored his [[general]] to leave him and survive. He tried to say "''[[May the Force be with you]]''" to Kenobi, but died before he could finish the [[Jedi]] phrase.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />
