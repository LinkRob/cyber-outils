'''Corellian nog''' was a type of [[Nog (beverage)|nog]] that shared its name with the<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" /> [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Corellia">{{Databank|corellia|Corellia}}</ref> [[Corellia]].<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" /> Between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Darth Maul – Black, White & Red 3|''Darth Maul – Black, White & Red'' 3]] take place during [[Maul|Darth Maul's]] [[time]] as the [[Sith apprentice]] of [[Darth Sidious]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]].</ref> while on the [[moon of Dawk]], a [[Duros]] [[Unidentified Duros (moon of Dawk)|criminal]] suggested to the [[Devaronian]] [[Coir Cion]] that he digest such a nog to calm his anxiety after his acquaintance, [[Aro]], disappeared not long after being [[Mission to the moon of Dawk|dispatched]] by Cion to [[Death|eliminate]] whoever landed on the [[moon]]. Coir Cion, along with the Duros and other patrons at a [[Korts Cantina|cantina]], were shortly killed by the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Darth]] [[Maul]], the person responsible for the disappearance of Aro.<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian nog was first mentioned in the [[Darth Maul – Black, White & Red 3|third issue]] of the [[2024]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] anthology miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Darth Maul – Black, White & Red]]''. The issue was written by [[Erica Schultz]], illustrated by [[Leonard Kirk]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" /> on [[June 26]], 2024.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/110340/star_wars_darth_maul_-_black_white_red_2024_3|text=Star Wars: Darth Maul &ndash; Black, White & Red (2024) #3}}</ref>
