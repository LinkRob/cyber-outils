{{Quote|The Endor system? Where the last war ended?|[[Finn]]|<ref name="Rise" />}}
The '''Endor system'''<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> was a [[Binary star system|binary]]<ref name="AEYNtK" /> [[star system]] located in the [[Moddell sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> It notably contained the [[gas giant]] [[Endor (planet)|Endor]] and its nine [[moon]]s.<ref name="CL 2016" /> Following the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[New Republic]] restricted travel to the system in the fear that amateur [[historian]]s and sightseers might wander into the [[sector]]'s notorious [[Hyperspace anomaly|hyperspace anomalies]], unwittingly set off unexploded ordnance, or collect unclaimed [[Galactic Civil War|war]] materials.<ref name="TROS VD" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' as taking place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must take place in [[35 ABY]].</ref> the system was guarded by a [[First Order]] [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]].<ref name="TROS Junior Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>
