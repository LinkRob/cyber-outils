The '''holding room''' of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] was a waiting area that was connected to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Emperor's Throne Room|throne room]] by an [[Turbolift|elevator]]. The [[Imperial officer|officers]], dignitaries, and [[Imperial Ruling Council]] members waiting for an audience with Palpatine had to remain in that room until their master was willing to see them. At one point, even [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the Emperor's right hand, was forced to wait in the holding room, much to his frustration.<ref name="Beware" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DS2_holding_room.png|thumb|250px|Jerjerrod and two Royal Guards barring entrance to the Emperor's throne room]]
Initially, the holding room of the Emperor's throne room was featured in a brief scene in the [[1983]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. In said scene, [[Moff]] [[Tiaan Jerjerrod|Jerjerrod]] and two [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guards]] bar entrance to the turbolift as Darth Vader attempts to enter it. Although the scene was ultimately left on the cutting room's floor,<ref name="Insider 67">{{InsiderCite|67|The Ultimate Return of the Jedi Insider's Guide|The Ultimate ''Return of the Jedi'' Insider's Guide}}</ref> it was included in the ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]'' Blu-ray set.<ref name="Blu-ray">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''</ref>
