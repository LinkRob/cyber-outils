The '''Endor Reclaimers''' was an outfit owned by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Salvagers|standard costume]]. This garb resembled the outfits worn by [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Rebel Pathfinders]] during the [[Battle of Endor]], including [[robe]]s with the same [[Color|coloration]] and camouflage patterns, as well as [[helmet]] and other accessories. On their back was a brown [[Backpack|satchel]] with glowing yellow components and both the head and hand of a silver and bronze [[3PO-series protocol droid]] poking out.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Reclaimers was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Utooni, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.18.0 during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Endor Set]].<ref name="Hunters" />
