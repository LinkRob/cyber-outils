"'''Brothers'''" is a short story published in the developer blog for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' on [[July 28]], [[2015]]. It is set during the events depicted in the cinematic trailer [[Sacrifice (cinematic trailer)|''Sacrifice'']], and depicts from [[Thexan]]'s perspective the night after [[Arcann]] was injured in battle.
