'''Bon''' was an [[Bon's species|alien]] who, along with the [[human]] [[Niki]], was proprietor of the [[Niki-Bon Travel Agency]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. In [[3 BBY]], the pair were in the agency's back room when [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] of the [[Axis network]] arrived at the building. Niki emerged first to speak with Andor but called for Bon after he asked to travel to the planet [[Ghorman]] and used the phrase "I have friends everywhere." Andor later left and traveled to Ghorman.

[[File:Bon-Niki-FriendsEverywhere.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Bon, a large alien with gray skin, emerges from a beaded curtain behind Niki in the Niki-Bon Travel Agency.|Bon and Niki were co-proprietors of the Niki-Bon Travel Agency.]]
Bon was the [[Bon's species|alien]]<ref name="Friends" /> proprietor of the eponymous [[Niki-Bon Travel Agency]] on an upper level of<ref name="DB">{{Databank|niki-bon-travel-agency|Niki-Bon travel agency}}</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Friends" /> He<ref name="FriendsAD" /> resided with<ref name="DB" /> [[Niki]], his [[human]] co-proprietor, in a room in the back of the office of the agency where<ref name="Friends" /> in [[3 BBY]],<ref name="GhormanDate">{{AndorDate|205}}</ref> the pair were laughing together when the rebel [[Axis network]] member [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] arrived at the building and knocked on the [[Viewport|window]]. Hearing Andor, Niki told Bon to stop what he was doing and then left the back room to let Andor in.<ref name="Friends" />

Bon remained in the back room while she spoke with Andor, but after the rebel asked about transport to<ref name="Friends" /> the [[Colonies]]<ref name="GalaxyMap">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> planet<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Ghorman]] and used the phrase "I have friends everywhere"<ref name="Friends" />&mdash;which was used by members of the network to identify allies<ref name="Evening">{{Andor|What a Festive Evening}}</ref>&mdash;Niki called to Bon and asked what they had for Ghorman. Bon then emerged shirtless from the back room and grunted while looking at Andor. The rebel later left the travel agency with a new piece of luggage and traveled to Ghorman via<ref name="Friends" /> the [[Coruscant Eastern Spaceport]].<ref name="CGttSWG" />

==Personality and traits==
For the right price, Niki and Bon were willing to help a potential customer at any [[Standard hour|hour]].<ref name="DB" /> He had white [[hair]], gray skin, pale [[eye]]s with no eyebrows,<ref name="Friends" /> and was giant in terms of height.<ref name="FriendsAD" />

==Skills and abilities==
Bon understood [[Galactic Basic Standard]], but while they were in the back room together Niki used another [[language]] while speaking to him.<ref name="Friends" />

==Equipment==
When Andor visited the travel agency, Bon wore black [[Clothing|pants]] and an open brown jacket with red and gray stripes but no shirt.<ref name="Friends" />
