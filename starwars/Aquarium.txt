An '''aquarium''' was a [[water]] tank designed to hold aquatic creatures. Aquariums could vary widely in size, from tanks small enough to keep in an office or other indoor room<ref name="The Academy">{{TCW|The Academy}}</ref> to the conversion of half the space of a large [[freighter]].<ref name="Light">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

One of the voyages of the [[starship]] ''[[Legacy Run]]'', during the [[High Republic Era]], involved half of the vessel's cargo space being converted into an aquarium to transport an enormous [[saberfish]] from [[Tibrin]] to a private aquarium on [[Abregado-rae]]. When the creature became ill, the [[Unidentified countess|countess]] who had ordered the creature paid for it to be returned to Tibrin.<ref name="Light" />

[[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] of [[Mandalore]] had an aquarium installed in her private quarters in the city of [[Sundari]], which was destroyed when [[Prime Minister]] [[Almec]] attempted to stage a coup against her. Kryze's nephew [[Korkie Kryze|Korkie]] and his friends found the body of one of Kryze's bodyguards lying in what remained of the tank, with water all over the room.<ref name="The Academy" />

[[Senator]] [[Christo]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] also had an aquarium installed in his house on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="POP">{{TCW|Pursuit of Peace}}</ref> [[Alderaanian]] [[viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] had one installed in his office in the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan]] to keep his adopted daughter [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]] amused when she was a toddler.<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref>

An aquarium was located in [[Pyloon's Saloon]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Koboh]]. Eventually it housed various aquatic animals and [[plant]]s and was maintained by the [[Sakavian]] [[Skoova Stev]].<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Aquariums first appeared in [[canon]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season 3]] episode "[[The Academy]]."<ref name="The Academy" />
