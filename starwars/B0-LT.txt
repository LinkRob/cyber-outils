Manufactured by [[Veril Line Systems]],<ref name="BuildR2" /> '''B0-LT''', nicknamed "'''Bolts'''," was an [[EGL power droid]] from the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. During the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the planet<ref name="BBH" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GEDates">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> there was a 525-[[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] [[bounty]] on B0-LT for reprogramming, evading arrest, and illegal self-modification with a [[blast shield]]. The bounty also stated that [[bounty hunter]]s should not attempt to blast the [[Power droid|droid]] and requested that B0-LT be returned whole or in parts to [[Mubo]],<ref name="BBH" /> proprietor of the [[Droid Depot]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on Batuu.<ref name="Batuu">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>

B0-LT hid in one of the outpost's buildings but was located with a [[thermal scanner]] by a visitor to the outpost who had been assigned the droid's bounty by the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]]. The visitor then reported the [[droid]]'s location to the local [[Guildmaster's Hideout]] in order to claim the reward.<ref name="BBH" />

==Behind the scenes==
