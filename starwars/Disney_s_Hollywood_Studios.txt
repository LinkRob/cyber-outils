'''Disney's Hollywood Studios''' is a real-world theme park located at [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World|Walt Disney World]] in [[Wikipedia:Orlando, Florida|Orlando, Florida]]. Originally opened as '''Disney-MGM Studios''' on [[May 1]]st, [[1989]] and renamed in [[2008]],<ref name="MGM A to Z">{{D23|url=a-to-z/disney-mgm-studios|text=Disney A to Z: Disney-MGM Studios}}</ref> it has featured various ''[[Star Wars]]''-themed attractions and events over the years, starting with the opening of [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] third [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] attraction months after the park's opening.<ref name="SWI 104">{{InsiderCite|104|Star Tours: Taking Off on a Star Tour}}</ref> The long-running annual [[Star Wars Weekends|''Star Wars'' Weekends]] were held in the park until [[2015]]. It eventually became home to the second [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] themed land.<ref name="Now Open">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-now-open-at-walt-disney-world-resort|text=''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge is Now Open at Walt Disney World Resort!}}</ref>

[[Lucasfilm]]'s ''[[Indiana Jones (franchise)|Indiana Jones]]'' franchise is also represented at the park by the ''[[Wikipedia:Indiana Jones Epic Stunt Spectacular!|Indiana Jones Epic Stunt Spectacular]]'' show.<ref name="DHS official site">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=destinations/hollywood-studios|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios}}</ref>
