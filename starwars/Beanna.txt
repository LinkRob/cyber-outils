[[Torchbearer]] '''Beanna''' was a [[Varovian]] leader who served in a high-ranking position in the [[Varovian Order of Seers]] faction of the [[Varovian Civil War]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. [[Standard decade|Decades]] into the war, when a [[Republic Pathfinder]] [[Atticus Cale's Pathfinder team|team]] led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Atticus Cale]] came to [[Varovia]] in search of evidence of a missing [[Barnabas Vim|colleague's]] whereabouts, Beanna told them about the [[Rod of Ages|Sacred Rod]], the focal point of the ongoing war. As a Seer, Beanna believed that the Sacred Rod was too dangerous to be wielded by the Varovians.<ref name="Echoes of Fear 3" />
