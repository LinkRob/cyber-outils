The '''''Empire's End'' audio drama''' was a full cast audio adaption of [[Dark Horse Comics]]' ''[[Star Wars: Empire's End]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series. It was adapted by [[John Whitman]] from the source material by [[Tom Veitch]]. It was produced and directed by [[Arthur G. Insana]] and featured parts of composer [[John Williams]]'s classic score along with authentic stereo sound effects. The adaptation was released exclusively by [[Time Warner AudioBooks]] as a bonus compact disc as part of the ''[[Dark Empire: The Collector's Edition]]'' boxed set also containing the [[Dark Empire (audio drama)|previous]] [[Dark Empire II (audio drama)|two]] audio dramas.<ref name="Amazon" />

==Publisher's summary==
''This exclusive bonus CD features the final confrontation between Luke Skywalker and the Emperor in a battle to decide the fate of the galaxy! ''<ref name="Amazon" />
