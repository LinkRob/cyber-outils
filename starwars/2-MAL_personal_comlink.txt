The '''2-MAL personal comlink''' was a [[comlink]] model manufactured by [[Crozo Industrial Products]]. A cheap and simple, yet efficient, communications device, it was commonly used by the soldiers of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. [[Jyn Erso]] notably carried one at the time of [[Operation Fracture]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Crozo 2-MAL comlink first appeared in the [[2016]] movie ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. It is similar in name to the [[3-MAL personal comlink]], which was created for [[Bill Smith]]'s [[1997]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Weapons and Technology]]''.
