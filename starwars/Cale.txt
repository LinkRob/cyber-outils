'''Cale''' was a [[Pau'an]] [[Gender|male]] [[Nihil]] who served as a [[technician]] aboard the [[flagship]] of [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]], the ''[[Gaze Electric]]''.<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Cale was sent alongside fellow techs [[Werrera]] and [[Leyel]] to carry out the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|sabotage]] of [[Starlight Beacon]] due to the threes loyalty. The trio were successful in their sabotage of the [[space station]], though Cale was [[Death|killed]] by [[Nan Hague|Nan]] during a brief firefight aboard the station.<ref name="The Fallen Star" />
