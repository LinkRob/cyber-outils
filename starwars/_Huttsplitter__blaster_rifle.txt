The '''"Huttsplitter" blaster rifle''' was the [[Limb|hand]]-crafted personal weapon of [[Tasu Leech]], [[topboss]] of the criminal gang [[Kanjiklub]]. Leech brought it with him while trying to reclaim money owed to him by [[Han Solo]], owner of the [[Baleen-class heavy freighter|''Baleen''-class heavy freighter]] ''[[Eravana]]''. He then used the weapon to defend himself from [[rathtar]]s that attacked him and his men onboard Solo's [[Starship|vessel]].

==Description==
The "Huttsplitter" [[blaster rifle]] consisted of a [[Limb|hand]]-crafted [[blaster]] body with a grip made from [[bone]]s belonging to a [[gundark]]<ref name="TFAVD" /> a vicious [[species]] native to the [[planet]] [[Vanqor]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Its [[barrel]] was equipped with oversized [[amplifier]]s that increased the charge of blaster bolts within the barrel. A tusk belonging to a member of the planet [[Orto Plutonia]]'s [[narglatch]] species was converted into a [[vibro-spike]] attached to the end of the "Huttsplitter's" barrel.<ref name="TFAVD" />

==History==
[[File:Tasu_Leech.jpg|thumb|left|175px|Tasu Leech with a Huttsplitter aboard the ''[[Eravana]]'']]
[[Tasu Leech]],<ref name="TFAVD" /> [[topboss]]<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> of the criminal [[Kanjiklub]] organization, carried the "Huttsplitter" as his personal weapon around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]. As with all of Kanjiklub's weapons, the "Huttsplitter" was maintained and upgraded by Leech's [[lieutenant]] and tech-expert [[Razoo Qin-Fee]]. Its name referred to the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Hutt]] species, who had controlled Leech's [[homeworld]] of [[Nar Kanji]] and [[Slavery|enslaved]] its people before being overthrown in a revolt by the [[human]] [[colonist]]s.<ref name="TFAVD" />

At around this [[time]], Leech brought the blaster rifle with him when boarding the [[Baleen-class heavy freighter|''Baleen''-class heavy freighter]] ''[[Eravana]]'' in order to reclaim a debt of 50000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] from the [[Starship|vessel's]] owner [[Han Solo]], who had borrowed it from Kanjiklub in order to [[Hunting|capture]] several [[rathtar]]s. Onboard, he and his men were [[Skirmish aboard the Eravana|attacked]] by the rathtars and Leech managed to discourage one of the beasts from chasing him by firing upon it with the "Huttsplitter" before fleeing.<ref name="TFA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Huttsplitter" blaster rifle was created as a prop for the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], but was first revealed under just the name "Huttsplitter blaster" on [[November 19]], [[2015]] in a gallery on the website [[Wikipedia:Wired.com|Wired.com]] displaying a range of props from the upcoming film.<ref name="Wired">{{WebCite|url=https://www.wired.com/2015/11/star-wars-force-awakens-props|text=''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''{{'}} Arsenal of Battle Props|work=Wired|archivedate=20170705195328}}</ref> It then appeared in the film itself<ref name="TFA" /> when it was released on [[December 18]] later that year,<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-vii-to-open-december-18-2015|text=Star Wars: Episode VII to Open December 18, 2015}}</ref> and was further identified and detailed in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released alongside Episode VII.<ref name="New Books">{{SW|url=news/a-galaxy-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens-books-coming-december-18-first-look|text=A Galaxy of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Books Coming December 18 - UPDATED!}}</ref>
