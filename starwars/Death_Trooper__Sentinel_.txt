The '''Death Trooper''' was a set of armor worn by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Loyalist Trooper|standard costume]]. This [[armor]] was actually an old set of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[death trooper armor]] with visible damage and repairs, as well as a set of three [[C-25 fragmentation grenade]]s attached to the front.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Death Trooper was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Sentinel, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 2.0.0 update alongside Season 2 on [[July 30]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Death E-Web]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching Tier 15 on the Season 2 Arena Pass' premium track.<ref name="Hunters" />
