'''Clak'dor V''' was a location<ref name="Chapter 14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref> in the [[Clak'dor system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Mayagil sector]], in grid square M-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was where the convicted [[human]] criminal [[Pretts Rulato]] served a 50 [[Standard year|year]] sentence for manslaughter and industrial negligence. The sentence was listed on Rulato's profile, which was among a number of profiles seen on a [[New Republic]] database by [[Marshal]] [[Carasynthia Dune|Cara Dune]] when she surveyed for that of the convicted criminal [[Migs Mayfeld]]<ref name="Chapter 14" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|14}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Clak'dor V was mentioned on a screen in "[[Chapter 14: The Tragedy]]" of [[Jon Favreau]]'s [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', directed by [[Robert Rodriguez]]<ref name="Chapter 14" /> and released on [[December 4]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-december-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in December 2020}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Clak'dor V/Legends|Clak'dor V]] was a [[Prison/Legends|prison]] colony in the [[Colu system]] -- the same system as the [[Bith/Legends|Bith]] [[Homeworld/Legends|homeworld]] of [[Clak'dor VII/Legends|Clak'dor VII]] -- and was first mentioned in the [[2004]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' sourcebook ''[[Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds]]''.<ref name="GatORW">''[[Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds]]''</ref>
