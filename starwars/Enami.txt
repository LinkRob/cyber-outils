The '''Enami''' were a [[species]] of [[Hair|furry]] [[humanoid]]s native to the [[planet]] [[Ena]]. Their fur could range in [[Color|shades]] of brown with speckles of color which could lose its hue or turn white with old age. After the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], the Enami were subjugated by the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] led by [[Tregarra]] the Hutt. Sometime around [[229 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{SWYouTube|Qi-b2c0RY7I|Star Wars Celebration LIVE! 2023 &ndash; DAY 2|nolive=1}} dates "Light in the Darkness" right before ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' Phase III, which is also placed a [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which corresponds to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>, [[Jedi Master]] [[Keeve Trennis]] arrived on the planet with [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] forces to aid in the liberation of the Enami.<ref name="Light in Darkness" />
