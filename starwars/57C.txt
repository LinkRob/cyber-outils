The '''57C''' was a model of security camera<ref name="Outlaws" /> used in [[the galaxy]]. It consisted of six rectangular parts with black screen-like surfaces at the front.<ref name="ANH" />

By [[19 BBY]],<ref name="TBB Cornered Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of [[The Bad Batch Season 1|''The Bad Batch'' Season 1]] in [[19 BBY]].</ref> [[Ro Station]], a [[city]] on the [[moon]] [[Pantora]], had central security network which utilized the model of security [[camera]]. That [[Standard year|year]], the enhanced [[clone commando]], [[Tech]], [[slicer|sliced]] in to the city's security system and used it to located the whereabouts of the missing [[Cloning|clone]] [[youngling]] [[Omega]].<ref name="Cornered">{{TBB|Cornered}}</ref>

The [[Galactic Empire]] used the model of security camera at its facilities around the [[galaxy]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> including on the [[Detention Level]] on the [[Death Star]]. During the [[Rescue of Princess Leia]] from the Death Star's [[Detention Block AA-23]], [[Han Solo]], [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Chewbacca]] shot multiple of the security cameras.<ref name="ANH" />
