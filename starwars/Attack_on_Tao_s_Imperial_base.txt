An [[Imperial base (Tao)|Imperial base]] on [[Tao]] was '''attacked''' at least seven [[Standard year|years]] into the [[Imperial Era]]. Tao, a resource-rich but undeveloped [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], accepted the Empire's rule in exchange for assistance developing. Over time, discontent spread among Tao's inhabitants as the Empire oppressed its inhabitants. As a result, some of the local population attacked the Imperial base in a local [[Unidentified town (Tao)|town]].<ref name="L&O" />
