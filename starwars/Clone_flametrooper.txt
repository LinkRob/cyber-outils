'''Clone flametroopers''' were [[clone trooper]]s of the [[flame trooper corps]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].

==Description==
Clone flametroopers were a group of specialized [[clone trooper]]s<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> who used [[BT X-42 heavy flame projector]]s<ref name="Rise" /> to clear enclosed places. They were trained to resist their natural instinct to retreat from [[fire]]. Clone flametroopers were skilled in armed and [[unarmed combat]], as well as heavy [[weapon]]ry.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

Initially, clone flametroopers were outfitted with [[MK I insulated armor]], but that armor was severely flawed in that it did not have sufficient respirator filtration. The problem was quickly fixed in the [[MK II "Hotspot" Insulated Armor]] and they switched to that armor. They were equipped with BT X-42 heavy flame projectors.<ref name="Rise" /> Clone flametroopers relied on their insulated [[body glove]]s, coolant reservoirs, and their fireproof equipment to prevail in tackling dangerous objectives.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Like most clone troopers,<ref name="clonetroopers">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref> clone flametroopers stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s, or 6 [[Foot|feet]] tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==History==
[[File:CloneFlametrooper-TCWCEJtB.png|thumb|left|250px|A flametrooper in Mk II armor]]
A group of flame [[trooper]]s under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]]<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" /> and [[Clone Commander]] [[Jet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{SWE|characters|clone-commander-jet|Clone Commander Jet}}</ref> were highly effective against the [[Geonosian]]s during the [[Second Battle of Geonosis|Second Battle]] of [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" />
