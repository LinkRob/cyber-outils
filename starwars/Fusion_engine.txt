A '''fusion engine''' was a type of [[engine]] that was used by [[sandcrawler]]s. The engines consisted of several components such as a [[Fusion engine core|power core]] and a [[stellaract unit]] that to confined [[plasma]] within the engine.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] traded ten units of [[coarseweave]], six [[accu-accelerator]]s and five [[MG-2 power exceeder]]s to the [[Jawa]] [[merchant]] [[Pinota Pakinota]] in exchange for a fusion engine core. She later traded that engine core to [[Entip]] in exchange for a [[BT-P7 ion accelerator]] to upgrade her [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fusion engines were mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The player must obtain a fusion engine core to as part of the process for upgrading their [[blaster]]'s ion surge module to level four. Fusion engines are also mentioned in the description of the stellaract unit item.<ref name="Outlaws" />
