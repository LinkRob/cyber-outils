'''Dewback eggs''' were the eggs produced by the [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[dewback]] [[species]] found in the [[desert]]s of the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. Although the [[Diet|omnivorous]] dewbacks were near the top of the world's [[Foodstuff|foodchain]], their eggs were vulnerable to predators. To compensate for this and keep the species' population sustainable, dewback [[Gender|females]] produced between fifty and eighty-five eggs per [[Local year|year]] and buried them beneath the sand. [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] [[Director of Creature Cartography]] [[Ardis San Tekka]] mentioned dewback eggs when writing about dewbacks in her [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]]<ref name="Bestiary" /> at some point after the [[Battle of Exegol]] in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]'' describes the [[Cloning|clone]] of [[Darth Sidious]] as having [[Death|perished]] prior to its writing. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the clone's death at the [[Battle of Exegol]] to [[35 ABY]], it must take place after this date.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], dewback eggs were mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[S.T. Bende]].<ref name="Bestiary" /> The [[Dewback egg/Legends|eggs]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 7: Mos Eisley]]'', a [[1993]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Martin Wixted]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="GG7">''[[Galaxy Guide 7: Mos Eisley]]''</ref>
