'''49AX3''' was a [[Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser|''Dreadnought''-class heavy cruiser]] that served the [[Imperial Navy|navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Age of Rebellion" /> Some [[time]] between [[3 ABY|3]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">[[Age of Rebellion - Luke Skywalker 1|''Age of Rebellion - Luke Skywalker'' 1]] is set between [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], the events of which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]] respectively by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. ''Age of Rebellion - Luke Skywalker 1'' must therefore take place during one of those [[Standard year|years]].</ref> the [[Starship|vessel]] was captured by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], who used it to [[Raid at Imperial Refining Platform M36|raid]] [[Imperial Refining Platform M36]], a refinery in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Assisted by [[Luke Skywalker]], the team of rebels managed to [[Thief|steal]] [[fuel]] from the refinery, but their escape was blocked by an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]]. Skywalker landed his [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] in the vessel's [[hangar]], where he and the rebel [[Unidentified Rebel major (Pau'an)|major]] used a [[shuttle]] to draw the attention of the Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] away. The tactic worked, and the rebels aboard 49AX3 were able to evacuate, escaping to a nearby world.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
