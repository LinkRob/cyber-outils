'''Free Trader''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[fathier]] with [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] and black [[eye]]s<ref name="Outlaws" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[0 ABY]]. In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> they took part in the [[Canto Cup]], a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> that was held in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. By the time of the race, Free Trader was 323 [[centimeter]]s tall and weighed 962 [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> Free Trader competed in a race that the [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] watched via [[hologram]] and [[Gambling|bet]] on. Beforehand, an [[Unidentified Canto Cup announcer (Basic)|announcer]] on the broadcast stated that Free Trader was focused entirely on the race track.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Free Trader appeared in a hologram in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They are one of the fathiers that can randomly feature in the optional Canto Fathier Racing activity. In most races, the six fathiers are assigned a random [[jockey]] and a random symbol. As such, this article does not assume one pairing or symbol color to be [[canon]]ical.<ref name="Outlaws" />
