'''''Casus Belli'' 76''' was a French roleplaying magazine. It contained many roleplaying articles, including a significant amount of material for the [[West End Games]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|D6]] system.

This issue (76 from [[June]] [[1993]]) contained an adventure scenario, titled "[[La légende des cristaux]]" an adventure scenario by [[Christophe Debien]] and [[Patrick Bousquet]].
