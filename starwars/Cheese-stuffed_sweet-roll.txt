'''Cheese-stuffed sweet-rolls''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|roll]] stuffed with [[cheese]].<ref name="EFV" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> the aspiring [[pirate]] [[Zyle Keem]] mentally reflected that their [[Scarlet Skulls|crew]] member&mdash;the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]]&mdash;harbored romantic feelings for his fellow [[Jedi]] [[youngling]] [[Kildo]] and often looked at the other [[Gender|boy]] as if he had created [[Star|sunshine]] and cheese-stuffed sweet-rolls.<ref name="EFV" />

==Behind the scenes==
