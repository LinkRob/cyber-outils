The '''Fikzwaa system''' was located in the intersection of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]]<ref name="Trailing" /> in grid square N-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> It contained the astronomical object [[Fikzwaa]], which was [[Homeworld|home]] to a multiple-billion population of a [[Unidentified Fikzwaa species|diminutive species]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] controlled Fikzwaa until shortly after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> of [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> when the natives drove them out.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fikzwaa [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published on [[StarWars.com]] alongside the article [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Systems" />&mdash;both works having been authored by [[Jason Fry]] and released on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
