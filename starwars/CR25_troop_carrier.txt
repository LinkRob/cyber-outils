The '''CR25 troop carrier'''<ref name="Chris Menezes" /> was a [[troop transport]] utilized by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. It was able to carry newly constructed vehicles from their factories and ferry them to a nearby landing pad for deployment. The [[Tatooine rebel cell]] under the command of [[Colonel]] [[Jennica Pierce]], [[Saponza]], and [[Saponza's partner]] utilized CR25 troop carriers during the [[Battle for Tatooine]].<ref name="Commander" />

==Behind the scenes==
The CR25 troop carrier appears in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Canon]] in the [[2014]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''. It is based off of the [[CR25 troop carrier/Legends|CR25 troop carrier]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' ''Legends'']] video game ''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]''.
