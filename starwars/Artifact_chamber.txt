The '''artifact chamber''' was a room in the [[Batuu Jedi temple archive|archive]] of the [[Batuu Jedi temple|Jedi temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. It contained a collection of artifacts from the [[Outer Rim Territories]], including a [[crystal]]ized [[Bone|skull]] of an ancient [[Batuu sea bear]] and a small [[Unidentified Jedi compass|compass]].<ref name="QFPX" /> 

In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">According to ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'', [[Rooper Nitani]]'s [[time]] in the [[Batuu Jedi temple archive]] takes place between the outbreak of the [[Battle of Jedha]] and the [[Night of Sorrow]], both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> on her second [[Standard day|day]] in the archive, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] studied the many artifacts in the chamber and their unique shapes while practicing mentally organizing them into classes. Eventually, she heard her name whispered despite being there alone. Because of this, Nitani reached out into [[the Force]] and discovered that while everything in the chamber was connected to the Force, the [[compass]] particularly called to her. After finding it tucked behind the [[bear]] skull and picking it up, Nitani felt calm. She explained this experience to [[Jedi Master]] [[Merak (Jedi)|Merak]], the [[Jedi archivist|archivist]] of the temple, who allowed her to keep the compass so that she could keep following the [[Cosmic Force|will of the Force]].<ref name="QFPX" />

==Behind the scenes==
The artifact chamber appeared in the junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' by [[Tessa Gratton]], which released in [[2023]]<ref name="QFPX" /> as part of the second phase of the multimedia initiative ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]''.<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2022-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=NYCC 2022: 10 Things We Learned From the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref> The artifact chamber appeared in an online preview prior to the book's release.<ref name="Preview">{{Kobo|url=gb/en/ebook/quest-for-planet-x|text=Quest for Planet X}}</ref>
