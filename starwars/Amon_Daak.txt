{{Quote|To end this evening, we will have a recital from the poet-playwright Moobian Tax! Specifically, the grand romance of ''Amon Daak''.|Tunga Arpagion|<ref name="Star Wars 48" />}}
'''''Amon Daak''''' was a three-[[Standard hour|hour]]-long romance play written by the [[poet]]-playwright [[Moobian Tax]], featuring the characters of [[Arthoni]], a warrior-princess, and the demagogue [[Panai]]. While needing to stall for time when posing as the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Moff]] [[Tan Hubi]] at the [[Moncaladrome]], [[Tunga Arpagion]] decided to perform the unabridged version of ''Amon Daak'' with the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]]. Arpagion took the role of Arthoni, while C-3PO played Panai. Their performance was interrupted midway by [[stormtrooper]]s, who had been alerted that the Moff was an impersonator.<ref name="Star Wars 48" />
