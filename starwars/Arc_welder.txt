'''Arc welders''' were a tool that included a tiny blowtorch and could be used for repairs and cutting into objects. Some models of [[astromech droid]] were equipped with arc welders, including [[R2-series astromech droid|R2-series]], [[BB-series astromech droid|BB-series]], and [[C1-series astromech droid]]s.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> Though other types of droids included them as well, such as [[toy droid]]s.<ref name="Kenobi V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref>

During a [[Silvo's pirate raid|pirate raid]], [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Jod Na Nawood|Silvo]] ordered his [[Silvo's pirates|crew]] to use an giant arc welder to bypass the [[Magnetic seal|magnetically sealed]] vault.<ref name="A Real Adventure">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref>
