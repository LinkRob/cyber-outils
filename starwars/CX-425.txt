'''CX-425''' was a member of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] who was situated on the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] of [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]] during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. Despite CX-425's allegiance to the Empire, the individual possessed an arsenal of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] weapons.<ref name="JTL" />

==Biography==

Isn't that overkill?"<br />"They're probably all Rebel sympathizers, so they deserve to die. [[File:CX425-SWG.jpg|250px|thumb|Sergeant CX-425, stationed at a small base near the Massassi Temple on Yavin 4]]
A [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] bearing the designation CX-425 served as a [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtrooper]] [[Sergeant/Legends|sergeant]] in the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] [[Stormtrooper Corps/Legends|Stormtrooper Corps]] during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. By the [[Time/Legends|time]] following the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]]<ref name="TRE" /> in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]],<ref name="NEC">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''</ref> CX-425 was stationed at a small Imperial outpost near the recently subjugated [[Great Temple/Legends|Massassi Temple]] on the [[Jungle/Legends|jungle]] [[Moon/Legends|moon]] of [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]]. When [[Medic/Legends|medics]] at an independent supply depot were spotted issuing supplies to a known Rebel, CX-425 desired to see the depot destroyed in response to their treachery. In accomplishing this objective, the sergeant employed the services of an Imperial [[Soldier/Legends|soldier]]. Although the soldier expressed their belief that destroying an entire medical supply depot for helping one Rebel was "overkill," the dutiful soldier nevertheless carried out the mission. In addition to a thug and two medics, the depot was indeed defended by individuals loyal to the Rebellion, including a [[Sergeant major/Legends|sergeant major]] and an [[Recruitment Agent|Alliance Recruitment Agent]], all of whom were [[Death/Legends|killed]] by the soldier dispatched by CX-425.<ref name="TRE" />

===Rebel Kliknik Lair===
{{Quote|We've located a Rebel transmission relay. Seems to be hidden in a lair of klikniks, some kind of native bugs. Destroy the lair and that should take out the transmitter."<br />"Why destroy a whole lair of innocent creatures instead of just the transmitter?"<br />"Saves time. Bugs are bugs. Do you want the job or not?|CX-425 and an Imperial soldier|<ref name="TRE" />}}
CX-425 later learned of the location of a Rebel transmission relay hidden in a lair of [[kliknik]]s,<ref name="TRE" /> a [[Species/Legends|species]] of [[Bug (creature)|buglike]] [[Insectoid/Legends|insectoids]] native to the jungles of Yavin 4.<ref name="AED" /> To take out the [[Transmitter/Legends|transmitter]], CX-425 requested that the entire lair be destroyed by the Imperial soldier who had helped him previously. Once again, the soldier expressed at least some measure of hesitation. When asked why an entire lair of innocent creatures needed to be eradicated in order to destroy a single transmitter, the sergeant simply remarked that it would "save time," and that "bugs were bugs." This was apparently enough of an explanation to satisfy the soldier's sensibilities, who then went on to destroy the entire lair, guarded by a group of five thugs. All the thugs were killed, and when the soldier returned to CX-425 to report news of this success, the sergeant simply remarked "good job," and that he had no further duties for the soldier to carry out.<ref name="TRE" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Good job. That's all for now.|CX-425 to an Imperial soldier, after they successfully attacked an alleged Rebel kliknik lair|<ref name="TRE" />}}
As a stormtrooper sergeant for the Galactic Empire, CX-425 exhibited a ruthlessly unforgiving demeanor. When a medical supply depot was spotted giving supplies to a single Rebel, CX-425 assumed that everyone at the depot was a Rebel sympathizer, and therefore deserved to die. He also believed that anyone who did not have the [[Stomach/Legends|stomach]] to destroy the depot, was not worthy of being called a soldier. CX-425 exhibited a similar ruthless, and uncaring disposition when it came to destroying a hidden Rebel transmission relay. When he learned that the transmitter was hidden in a lair of klikniks, CX-425 requested that the entire lair be destroyed altogether. When asked by the soldier tasked with this responsibility why this was necessary, CX-425 simply remarked that it would save time. He also expressed something of a flippantly [[Speciesism|xenophobic]] attitude towards the creatures, claiming that "bugs were bugs." Whenever his missions were dutifully accomplished by the Imperial soldier, CX-425 would express a curt measure of gratitude. For instance, after successfully attacking the suspected Rebel kliknik lair, CX-425 told the soldier "good job."<ref name="TRE" />

==Equipment==
As a member of the Imperial Stormtrooper Corps, CX-425 wore standard [[Color/Legends|white-colored]] [[Stormtrooper armor/Legends|stormtrooper armor]]. His [[Armor/Legends|armor]] was also complimented by an orange [[Pauldron/Legends|pauldron]].<ref name="TRE" />
{{Cutend}}
Despite being aligned with the Empire, CX-425 possessed an arsenal of weapons typically carried by members of the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="JTL" />

==Behind the scenes==
CX-425 is listed as a non-player character in the [[2004]] ''[[Star Wars Galaxies: Jump to Lightspeed: Prima Official Game Guide]]''.<ref name="JTL" /> The reference guide was created for the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']], a [[Roleplaying game|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment and published by [[LucasArts]], prior to its closure on [[December 15]], [[2011]].<ref name="AED">{{GalaxiesAED}}</ref><ref name="Shutdown">{{SonyCite|url=playerinfo|text=IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT ''STAR WARS'' GALAXIES™}}</ref> In the guide, CX-425 is listed a member of the [[W:c:swg:Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[W:c:swg:Faction|faction]] located on [[W:c:swg:Yavin IV|Yavin 4]]. The guide also indicates that CX-425 possessed "[[W:c:swg:Rebel Alliance|Rebel]] [[W:c:swg:Weapon|weapons]]."<ref name="JTL" />

===Cut content===
Despite being listed in the aforementioned reference guide, CX-425 is not definitively known to have appeared on the video game's live [[W:c:swg:Server|servers]] as a proper non-player character.<ref name="AED" /> If CX-425 was ever included as an NPC, then the character was removed from the game before its existence could be properly verified. According to an archived 2003 [[W:c:swg:Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies'']] forum thread, a user named AcowebaAbi indicated that CX-425 could be found at an Imperial base on Yavin 4.<ref name="PartialList">{{SonyForumsCite|url=swg/board/message?board.id=game_guides&message.id=33801.html|text=partial list of quest npc}} {{C|"username/password: swganh"}}</ref> In another archived 2003 thread, a user named RayBothan presumed a similar possibility.<ref name="Unexplored">{{SonyForumsCite|url=swg/board/message?board.id=jedi&message.id=50193.html|text=Yavin 4: Unexplored?}} {{C|"username/password: swganh"}}</ref>

The game's directory tree files and source data place CX-425 among the many NPCs who gave players simple kill, delivery, and escort [[W:c:swg:Quest|quests]]. The files contain information for two of these quests, one of which would have required the player to destroy a "Medical Supply Depot" and another which would have required the player to destroy an alleged "Rebel Kliknik Lair." Each of these quests would have awarded the player 200 Imperial [[W:c:swg:Faction point|faction points]]. At least one emulator project incorporates CX-425 and his quests, however, as emulator projects are not officially licensed, this does not constitute a definitive appearance. Because of the ambiguity surrounding CX-425's actual inclusion to ''Star Wars Galaxies'', this article assumes the gameplay information to be cut,<ref name="TRE" /> and that only the information contained in the ''Star Wars Galaxies: Jump to Lightspeed: Prima Official Game Guide'' is [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="JTL" />
