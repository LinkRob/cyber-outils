The '''Battle of Khorm''' was fought during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> at some point between [[22 BBY]] and [[21 BBY]]<ref name="Dating" /> over the natural resources of [[Khorm]].<ref name="OF84">{{GermanFanCite|84|Database}}</ref> The [[Republic Military|Republic forces]]&mdash;[[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]]&mdash;and the Confederate [[Separatist Droid Army|troops]], led by<ref name="FC" /> [[Commander]]<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Asajj Ventress]],<ref name="FC" /> a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[assassin]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> clashed at Khorm during the battle.<ref name="FC" />

In the midst of the fighting, Ventress and Wolffe had a close encounter, during which the latter lost his right [[eye]]<ref name="WolffeDB" /> and received a distinctive facial scar,<ref name="HC 64">{{HelmetCollectionCite|64|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> but ultimately survived due to his exceptional combat abilities. Proudly carrying the scars from that fight,<ref name="Helmets in Pictures">{{HelmetCollectionCite|70|Helmets}}</ref> Wolffe continued to serve the Republic, but he began using a [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] implant in order to replace his missing eye.<ref name="WolffeDB" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Introduction to canon===
{{Quote|Stories with large casts and different planets tend to be difficult to do on the show.|Henry Gilroy, on why the Battle of Khorm was not featured in ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars''|<ref name="Blaster">{{InsiderCite|111|Blaster}}</ref>}}
[[File:Wolffe_on_Aleen.png|thumb|left|275px|The Battle of Khorm served as the backstory for Commander Wolffe's missing eye.]]
The Battle of Khorm was first indirectly mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Cloning|Clone]] [[Commander]] Wolffe's [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|Encyclopedia]] entry on [[StarWars.com]]. One of the slides in the entry's biography gallery depicted a scene<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|characters|clonecommanderwolffe|Clone Commander Wolffe}}</ref> from "[[Holocron Heist]],"<ref name="Heist">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref> an episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series.<ref name="HH guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/holocron-heist-episode-guide|text="Holocron Heist" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> The accompanying caption addressed Wolffe's appearance with a missing eye in the episode, stating that he had lost his eye "on the battlefield."<ref name="SWE" /> That battle was eventually identified by an official name in the [[2015]] sourcebook ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

[[Henry Gilroy]], writer for ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'', at one point considered the Battle of Khorm as a potential premise for episodes of the series. However, that idea never materialized due to the show's budget constraints.<ref name="Blaster" />

===Portrayal in ''Star Wars'' Legends===
{{Quote|Commander Wolffe returns sporting a scar over his right eye, an event that was shown in the comics.|[[Leland Chee]], regarding the Legends depiction of the Battle of Khorm|<ref name="Chronicling the Clone Wars">{{SW|url=news/chronicling-the-clone-wars-7|text=Chronicling ''The Clone Wars'' #7|int=Chronicling The Clone Wars|date=2013-02-01}}</ref>}}
[[File:Wolffe_scarred.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Asajj Ventress slashes at Wolffe's eye as she escapes from Republic forces in the Legends portrayal of the Battle of Khorm.]]
Gilroy's ideas for the battle were instead realized in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where the [[Battle of Khorm/Legends|Battle of Khorm]] was featured in a [[2009]] story arc of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (comic series)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' monthly comic series]], entitled [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: In Service of the Republic|''In Service of the Republic'']], written by Gilroy and [[Steven Melching]], and illustrated by [[Scott Hepburn]].<ref name="Blaster" /> In the [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]], the battle was fought for control of the [[Planet/Legends|planet's]] valuable [[Agrocite/Legends|agrocite]] [[Mineral/Legends|mineral]],<ref name="ISotR 1">[[The Clone Wars 7|''The Clone Wars'' 7]]</ref> and it ended in a [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] victory, while depicting [[Asajj Ventress/Legends|Asajj Ventress]] inflicting the [[Eye/Legends|eye]] injury on [[Wolffe/Legends|Wolffe]].<ref name="ISotR 3">[[The Clone Wars 9|''The Clone Wars'' 9]]</ref>

However, after ''The Clone Wars'' comics were relegated to the non-canon Legends continuity,<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> canon sources provided different information on the Battle of Khorm. Namely, the ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' digital card game established Anakin Skywalker's participation in the battle,<ref name="FC" /> which did not occur in the comic series.<ref name="ISotR 1" />
