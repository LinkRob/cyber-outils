'''Adana''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Helmet Collection 67">{{HelmetCollectionCite|67|Databank A-Z}}</ref> in the [[Deep Core]]'s [[Adana system]], situated in [[Standard Galactic Grid]] square L-10.<ref name="Appendix" /> Adana was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Faust]] [[scientist]] [[Nuvo Vindi]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Adana was first mentioned in the [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure "[[Tanith: the Guest-Star Wars]]," written by [[Olivier Frot]] and published in the [[March]] [[1990]] [[Graal 24|twenty-fourth issue]] of the French-language magazine [[Graal (magazine)|''Graal'']] for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Graal24">{{GraalCite|24|Tanith: the Guest-Star Wars}}</ref> The release was considered [[Canon|noncanonical]] with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity,<ref name="Unlicensed">{{UnlicensedStatus}}</ref> and Adana was not considered canon with respect to that continuity until its mention in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Character Encyclopedia]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="SW:TCWCE">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> and released on [[June 21]], [[2010]].<ref name="TCWCE DK">{{DK|url=us/9780756663087-star-wars-the-clone-wars-character-encyclopedia|text=Star Wars: The Clone Wars Character Encyclopedia|nolive=1}}</ref>

Adana was confirmed to be [[canon]] in the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'',<ref name="USW" /> which was released on [[April 28]], [[2015]].<ref name="Amazon">{{DK|url=us/book/9781465436016-ultimate-star-wars|text=Ultimate Star Wars|nolive=1}}</ref>
