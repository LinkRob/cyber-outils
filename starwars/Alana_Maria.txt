'''Alana Maria''' is a [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice artist]] and [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]]  who provided additional voices in the [[2017]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> Maria later voiced the [[human]] [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' developed and published by [[Zynga]].<ref name="Spotlight" />
