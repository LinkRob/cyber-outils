'''Cold Weather AT-MP Mark IIIs''' were modified [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[AT-MP Mark III]]s that had its internal systems retrofitted, allowing for optimal performance in sub-zero temperatures. It had light [[armor]] and needed protection when deployed, but could launch a volley of [[missile]]s to enemy targets.<ref name="SWC" />
