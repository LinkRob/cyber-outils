The '''Awalia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Raioballo sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square L-4 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Awalian newt]].<ref name="The Legends of Luke Skywalker" />
