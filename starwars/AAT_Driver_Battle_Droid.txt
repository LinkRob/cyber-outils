'''AAT Driver Battle Droids''',<ref name="LEGO">{{LEGOCite|num=75283|name=Armored Assault Tank (AAT)}}</ref> also known as '''AAT Driver Droids''',<ref name="Join the Battle" /> '''jungle battle droids''',<ref name="BFII">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> and as '''Kashyyyk Battle Droids''',<ref name="Droid Gunship">{{LEGOCite|num=75233|name=Droid Gunship}}</ref> were a variant of the [[B1-series battle droid]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. They had tactical camouflage markings that both allowed them to blend into [[jungle]]s<ref name="BFII" /> and designated their role as tank operators. [[Droid]]s of this model were deployed in [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tanks]].<ref name="Ambush" />

==Characteristics==
AAT driver [[droid]]s were modified [[B1-series battle droid]]s, designed to pilot [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tanks]]. They were equipped with specialized programming that improved their reaction [[time]]s and helped them detect enemies and obstacles more quickly than the standard B1 units. The driver droids were also given combat programming. Driver droids had [[Color|green]] flares so they could be quickly identified.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The AAT Driver battle droid first appeared in "[[Ambush]],"<ref name="Ambush" /> the first episode of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]], which originally aired on [[October 3]], [[2008]].<ref name="Ambush EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/ambush-episode-guide|text="Ambush" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> They appeared as "jungle battle droids" in DLC for ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''.<ref name="BFII" /> "[[Battle Droids and Other Droids of War]]" later confirmed that AAT Drivers and jungle battle droids were one and the same.<ref name="Battle Droids and War Droids">{{EncyclopediaCite|Battle Droids and Other Droids of War}}</ref>
