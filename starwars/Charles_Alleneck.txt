'''Charles Alleneck''' is an [[Industrial Light & Magic]] animator. He was the physical model for [[Poggle the Lesser]] for [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. He used a PVC pipe to imitate Poggle's cane and practice the way he felt the character would walk.<ref name="Behind the magic" />
