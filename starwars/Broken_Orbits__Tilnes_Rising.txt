"'''Broken Orbits: Tilnes Rising'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was Episode III of the [[Broken Orbits trilogy]] and was written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]].

The events in this adventure provided some of the background for the later adventure [[Metatheran Caution: Caaried Away|''Caaried Away'']].
