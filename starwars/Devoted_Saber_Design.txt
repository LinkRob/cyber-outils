The '''Devoted Saber Design''' was a custom [[lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] owned by the [[droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[J-3DI]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Jedi Saber Design|standard lightsaber]].<ref name="SWH" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Devoted Saber Design was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter J-3DI's Jedi Saber Design, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Devoted Guardian]]. This wrap could be unlocked by reaching J-3DI Hunter Fame level 22.<ref name="SWH" />
