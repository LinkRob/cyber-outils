'''Archaidan''' was a jungle [[planet]] in [[the galaxy]] that hosted a [[Jedi]] outpost during the [[High Republic Era]]. [[Standard century|Centuries]] later, during the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]], the outpost was converted into a training facility for the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]], who erected a statue of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. After the fall of the Empire, former Guardsmen [[Kastor Taizen]] retreated to Archaidan with his son [[Tobue Taizen]], who he trained in the ways of the Guard until the [[Knights of Ren]] discovered him. Taizen refused to serve the Knights' leader [[Kylo Ren]] and the [[First Order]], viewing them as pretenders to the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]]'s name, and was [[Death|killed]] in battle as a result. Tobue survived in hiding and remained on Archaidan until around [[34 ABY]], when [[Nioka Nastar]] and [[Barun]] visited Archaidan in search of any remaining Jedi.
