'''Alliance gunners''' were weapons operators of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Serving in the [[Alliance Military]], [[gunner]]s operated weapons emplacements, and succumbed to large numbers of fatalities. As a result, gunners turned to using [[remote]]s such as the [[Atgar Artillery Remote]] to operate emplacements behind cover.<ref name="Forged in Battle" /> Several Alliance gunners participated in the [[Battle of Hoth]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], with at least one operating a [[1.4 FD P-Tower]].<ref name="Episode V" />
