'''Figrin D'an and the Modal Nodes''' was a seven-member [[band]] that performed at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Episode IV" /> and the [[H'unn Cabaret Pit]] on [[Nar Kaaga]] during the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 8" /> The band had several popular [[Music|songs]], one of which was titled "[[Mad About Me]]."<ref name="AEYNTK" />

{{Music-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
In [[1977]]'s [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], seven [[Bith]] can be seen performing on stage in the cantina. An eighth Bith sits at a nearby booth.<ref name="Episode IV" /> The [[2011]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] source ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia]]'' originally established that [[Sun'il Ei'de]] was the seventh Bith on stage during the film.<ref name="SWCE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
