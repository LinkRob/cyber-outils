'''Brooks''' was a [[Dyplotid]] [[mayor]] of a [[nerf herder|nerf herding]] town on an [[Unidentified astronomical object (Brooks)|astronomical object]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The Rustler Roundup" />

He had become friends with the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]], who returned to Brooks' town with the [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Kai Brightstar]] on a mission given by [[Zia Zaldor Zanna]] to help the townfolk. Just as the Jedi arrived, Brooks was in the process of riling up the citizens to go fight the local Nerf rustlers, who had recently stolen the towns' Nerf herd. However, on Zettifar's request, the Jedi was allowed a day to handle the problem peacefully. Zettifar and Brightstar successfully released the herd during the night, but the thieves caught up with them as they were heading back to town. Brooks and the townfolk arrived at that time, and fighting between the two groups startles the nerf herd who ran towards a cliff. The rustlers calmed down the beasts, and explained their heritage to the mayor, being descended from townsfolk who once left the village and were promised a part of the herd, but never got it. The dispute was settled and peace ensured when Brooks decided to hand over the herd to the rustlers.<ref name="The Rustler Roundup" />

==Behind the scenes==
Mayor Brooks is a character that was developed for the second season of the [[Disney Junior]] and [[Disney+]] animated television series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''. Mayor Brooks is voiced by [[David Shaughnessy]].<ref name="The Rustler Roundup" /> The character was designed by [[Chris Blackstock]].<ref name="Blackstock">{{WebCite|url=https://blackstockart.com/young-jedi-adventures|text=Young Jedi Adventures|work=[[Chris Blackstock]]'s [https://blackstockart.com official website]|archivedate=20241210073436}}</ref>
