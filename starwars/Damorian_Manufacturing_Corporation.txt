The '''Damorian Manufacturing Corporation''' was a small [[starship]] manufacturing corporation that had a reputation for producing quality starships. They manufactured the [[Carrack-class light cruiser|''Carrack''-class light cruiser]] on the [[planet]] [[Esseles]] that was used by the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="BMF 52" /> and the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>
