'''Corusca gems''', also known as '''Corusca stones''',<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> were an extremely rare and [[Currency|valuable]] type of [[gemstone]]<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> found on the [[planet]] [[Sarka]].<ref name="Mind You Manners" /> [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] gave away her corusca gem [[necklace]] to a [[Gender|man]] named [[Huga]] on [[Mokivj]] so he and his companions would not hand her over to [[Duke]] [[Solha]].<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" /> Corusca stones were among the profitable cargoes recommended to fellow [[smuggler]]s by [[Platt Okeefe]] in the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]]'s ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', along with [[beskar]], [[Quella gem/Canon|quella gems]], and [[prismatic crystal]]s.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Corusca gems were first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] book ''[[Galaxy Guide 2: Yavin and Bespin]]'' ([[1989]]), and their first appearance was in the novel [[Dark Apprentice (novel)|''Dark Apprentice'']] ([[1994]]). The first [[canon]]ical appearance of Corusca gems was in the [[2014]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile-game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]'', in which they are used as in-game currency. Additionally, it was heavily featured in the IGN Mobile Game of the Year, ''[[Star Wars: Puzzle Droids]]''.
