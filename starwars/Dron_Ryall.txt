'''Dron Ryall''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]]. He was one of the leading designers of [[Sienar Fleet Systems]], a manufacturing company affiliated with the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Secrets" />

== History ==
At some point following the [[Siege of Lothal]], he worked at [[Sienar Fleet Systems factory|Sienar's factory]] on [[Absanz]]. One day, Ryall stumbled upon [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Garazeb Orrelios]], two notorious [[Spectres|enemies]] of the Empire, who were searching the facility for intelligence on Sienar's most recent prototypes. In spite of Ryall's claim that he was "just a humble employee," the intruders realized who he really was, and they decided to capture him. Although the factory was full of [[stormtrooper]]s, Jarrus and Orrelios did manage to steal a [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]] and take the designer on board. The craft was barely in the air when an Imperial [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] attacked, causing Ryall to fall through the boarding hatch, but Orrelios caught his hand just in time. The designer, however, convinced the rebel to let him go, arguing that many lives would be lost in the Empire's pursuit of him. Against the instructions of Jarrus, Orrelios finally let go of Ryall, who crashed safely on a railing below.<ref name="Secrets" />

== Personality and traits ==
Ryall was a human male with blond hair, blue eyes and fair skin. He kept his hair long and shaggy and had a blond beard. He wore a brown hat with goggles and an orange jumpsuit with a utility belt. He had a family and was desensitized to the destruction his designs caused, as long as they were taken care of.<ref name="Secrets" />

Dron Ryall was named after Chris Ryall,<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|tronprogram2|status/853703197583605761|[[Martin Fisher]]|quote=You guys saw the IDW SW comic announcement? You should know that the character of Ryall in a Rebels comic was named after @chris_ryall|archivedate=20201206174634}}</ref> Chief Creative Officer and Editor-in-Chief of [[IDW Publishing]].<ref name="IDW">{{IDW|url=creators/chris-ryall|text=Chris Ryall}}</ref> The character was introduced in the comic "[[Secrets of Sienar]]",<ref name="Secrets" /> published in [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 13]], and reappeared in the non-canon comic "[[The Ryall Defection]]"<ref name="LEGO">{{LSWCite|38|The Ryall Defection}}</ref> from [[LEGO Star Wars 38]]. Both stories were written by [[Martin Fisher]]. In "[[The Ryall Defection]]" Ryall defects from the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]], escaping from an imperial base on [[Mertes]] with the help of [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Chewbacca]], and joins the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].
