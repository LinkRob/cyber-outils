'''Arkiin Smir''' was the head of the secret service of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Alderaan/Legends|Alderaan]]. After the [[Destruction of Alderaan/Legends|destruction of the planet]], Arkiin Smir was dismissed from his functions due to psychological disorders. He founded the [[Luminous Knives]] group, with the goal to eliminate all [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] forces.<ref name="LCL" />
