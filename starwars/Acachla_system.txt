The '''Acachla system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was the origin of the [[Acachlan suit]].<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref> It was situated at the coordinates N-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Acachla system was mentioned in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The system name derives from the [[Acachlan suit]] which first appeared in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Last Shot]]'', written by [[Daniel José Older]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
