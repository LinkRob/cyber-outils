The '''Droideka Interceptor''' was a bodily configuration owned by the [[droideka]] [[Team Slingshot droideka|member]] of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]] that she could swap for her standard [[Beloved Paintwork|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This configuration vaguely resembled the appearance of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/IN interceptor]], featuring a [[Color|gray]] and black appearance, with a simulated, circular, split viewport on top of the head, as well as faux pointed wings extending forward from the shoulders and [[Limb|arm]] [[cannon]]s, which featuring glowing red components.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Droideka Interceptor was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[TIE Racer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
