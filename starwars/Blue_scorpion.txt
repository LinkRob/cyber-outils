'''Blue scorpions''' were a type of [[scorpion]] native to [[Eiram]]. They were mostly kept out of [[Erasmus Capital City]] by the protection dome, although smaller ones could sometimes slip through. These scorpions became poisonous once they reached maturity.<ref name="Convergence" />

{{Creature-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Blue scorpion first appeared in the [[2022]] novel [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" />
