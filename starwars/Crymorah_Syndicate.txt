The '''Crymorah Syndicate''' was a set of [[Crime lord|crime families]]&mdash;the [[Baldamiro Family]], the [[Rang Clan]], the [[Wandering Star]], the [[Droid Gotra]], and [[envoy]]s of the [[Hutt Clan]]&mdash;who operated during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].

==History==
{{Quote|As odd as it sounds, my lord, we have also been receiving intelligence from the Crymorah syndicate, which apparently shared some nefarious affiliation with criminal subcontractors who operated Phindar's refueling station.|[[Harus Ison]], [[Deputy Director]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], to Emperor [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
[[File:AORJabbaTheHutt-textless.png|thumb|left|250px|Jabba the Hutt (pictured) was affiliated with the Crymorah Syndicate and used it to his advantage]]
At some point, [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] became affiliated with the Crymorah Syndicate. As a result of his collaboration with the Crymorah, he gained a position on the [[Grand Hutt Council]].<ref name="Jabba Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Jabba the Hutt and Other Characters of the Underworld}}</ref> As one of the most powerful syndicates in [[the galaxy]], the Crymorah Syndicate was a member of the [[Five Crime Syndicates]]<ref name="The Living Force">''[[The Living Force]]''</ref> by [[33 BBY]].<ref name="The Living Date">{{LivingForceDate}}</ref> The Crymorah itself was composed of five smaller syndicates at that time.<ref name="The Living Force" />

Five years after the end of the [[Clone Wars]], the syndicate provided the [[Galactic Empire]] with intelligence during their attempts to [[Berch Teller campaign|capture]] [[Berch Teller]] and his [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]]. [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] explained to [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] that the Empire and the Cyrmorah had reached an "accommodation," but he did not go into details.<ref name="Tarkin" /> In [[12 BBY]],<ref name="CCDate">The chapter of ''[[Crimson Climb]]'' in which [[Qi'ra]] works for [[Crimson Dawn]] is said to have taken almost a [[Standard year|year]] after she left the [[White Worms]]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Han Solo|Han]] and Qi'ra escaping [[Corellia]] to [[13 BBY]], in which Qi'ra is still working for the White Worms, the events must be in around [[12 BBY]].</ref> members of the Crymorah attended a meeting of the Five Crime Syndicates on [[Nightsend]].<ref name="CC">''[[Crimson Climb]]''</ref>

In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Yavin]] and the destruction of the Empire's [[superweapon]], the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] sent Vader to secure a deal with the [[Hutt]]s to gain new raw materials for [[Imperial Military]] production. After the [[Assault on Cymoon|destruction of Weapons Factory Alpha]] on [[Cymoon 1]] and the failure of negotiations with the Hutts, the Emperor ruled out opening negotiations with the Crymorah to secure resources and instead ordered Vader to go to [[Tatooine]] to negotiate directly with [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]].<ref name="Darth Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2015) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 1]]</ref>

After [[Attack on an unidentified pirate base|destroying]] a [[Unidentified pirate space station|base]] of [[Crymorah-backed pirates|pirates]] who had been raiding the Empire's supply lines, Vader discovered that the pirates were being backed by the Crymorah and their allies, the [[Droid Gotra]], a band of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]]-era [[battle droid]]s who were not deactivated and who were acting against the Empire. Several of these droids were upgraded due to the efforts of droid [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] such as two [[droideka]]s who were equipped with [[missile launcher]]s.<ref name="Darth Vader 2">[[Darth Vader (2015) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 2]]</ref><ref name="Darth Vader 3">[[Darth Vader (2015) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 3]]</ref>

Later, it was reported by [[Cynabar's InfoNet]] that the Crymorah and the [[Son-tuul Pride]] had become the latest casualties in [[Grand General]] [[Cassio Tagge]]'s undeclared war on the underworld,<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> though by [[4 ABY]] it had become active in the galaxy again.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>
