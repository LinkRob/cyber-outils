"'''Down Underworld: Leeanna Walsman'''" is an article written by [[Michael G. Ryan]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 57|''Star Wars Insider'' 57]]. It is an interview with [[Leeanna Walsman]], the actress who played [[Zam Wesell]] in {{Film|II}}.
