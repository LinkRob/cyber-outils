In the last [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], the '''Free Trade Zones''' were trading areas untaxed by the Republic. The [[Trade Federation]] exploited loopholes in the trade legislation to establish an economic monopoly over the zones.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>

Around [[32 BBY]], the [[Galactic Senate]] passed [[Prop 31-814D]], making the Free Trade zones eligible for taxation.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> The Trade Federation retaliated by [[Invasion of Naboo|invading]] the remote [[planet]] of [[Naboo]] with its private [[Trade Federation Droid Army|droid force]], secretly backed up and manipulated by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Lott Dod - Senator}}</ref>
