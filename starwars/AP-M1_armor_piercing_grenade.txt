The '''AP-M1 armor piercing grenade''' was a model of [[grenade]] manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]]. It was designed to provide light anti-[[armor]] capabilities to basic foot [[soldier]]s, detonating in a highly focused blast that can crack open even the toughest armor. The grenade cost two hundred [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and was the size of a [[human]] [[Limb|fist]] and consisted of a small shaped [[Charge Core|charge core]] encased in a [[plasteel]] casing.<ref name="Rise" />

It could be thrown like a standard grenade or placed and held in position by a integral chemical adhesive system, making them well suited for precision demolition work as well as anti-vehicle and anti-armor use. The grenades were commonly used by the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The AP-M1 armor piercing grenade was mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" />
