An '''Ewok Engineer''' belonged to the [[Bright Tree tribe]] on the forest [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. They rebuilt the debris from the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] into another [[space station]], calling it the ''Doom Ball 3''.<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ewok Engineer first appeared in the [[2022]] [[canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', in the side mission "Doom Ball III".<ref name="LEGO" />
