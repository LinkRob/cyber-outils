'''David Oyelowo''' (born [[April 1]], [[1976]]) is a British-Nigerian actor who voices [[Alexsandr Kallus]] in ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. He first became involved with [[Lucasfilm]] when he played the character Joe "Lightning" Little in [[Wikipedia:Red Tails|''Red Tails'']].
