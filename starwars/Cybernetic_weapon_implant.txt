'''Cybernetic weapon implants''' were a type of [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] implant, and were popular among [[bounty hunter]]s, [[assassin]]s, and clandestine operatives. Such implants guaranteed that an individual was never unarmed no matter the situation. The artificial forearm housed a small [[blaster]] that emerged from a hidden compartment and delivered a hail of blaster fire before most enemies were aware they were in danger. The cost of a cybernetic weapon implant was 4,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cybernetic weapon implants were mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets" />
