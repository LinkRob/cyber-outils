'''Burl Ives''' ([[June 14]], [[1900s|1909]] &ndash; [[April 14]], [[1995]]) was an actor who narrated [[John Korty]]'s ''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]''.

==Biography==
===Birth===
'''Burl Icle Ivanhoe Ives'''<ref name="amug">{{WebCite|url=https://www.allmusic.com/artist/burl-ives-mn0000536754/biography|text=Burl Ives &#124; Biography & History|author=William Ruhlmann|work=[[Wikipedia:AllMusic|AllMusic]]|archivedate=20200409172910}}</ref> was born in [[Wikipedia:Hunt City Township, Jasper County, Illinois|Hunt City Township]] in [[Wikipedia:Jasper County, Illinois|Jasper County]], [[Wikipedia:Illinois|Illinois]], on [[June 14]], [[1900s|1909]];<ref name="Spartacus Educational" /><ref name="den">{{WebCite|url=https://thekeep.eiu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1010&context=den_1995_apr|text=Eastern mourns Burl Ives' death|author=Betsy Cole|date=1995-04-17|format=[[Wikipedia:PDF|PDF]]|work=[[Wikipedia:The Daily Eastern News|The Daily Eastern News]]|pages=[https://thekeep.eiu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1010&context=den_1995_apr#page=2 p. 2]|archivedate=20200409183758}}</ref> the youngest of six children.<ref name="Obit">{{WebCite|url=http://www.nytimes.com/1995/04/15/obituaries/burl-ives-the-folk-singer-whose-imposing-acting-won-an-oscar-dies-at-85.html|text=Burl Ives, the Folk Singer Whose Imposing Acting Won an Oscar, Dies at 85|author=Richard Severo|date=1995-04-15|work=[[Wikipedia:The New York Times|The New York Times]]|archivedate=20090909131556}}</ref>

===''Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure''===
{{Quote|Our story begins in a time long, long ago, deep in an enchanted forest, on the distant moon of Endor.|Burl Ives as the Narrator|audio=BurlDamnYou.flac|<ref name="CC" />}}
In [[1984]], Ives came out of retirement to provide narration for [[John Korty]]'s ''[[Star Wars]]'' telemovie, ''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]''. Ives' narration in the film's opening scenes explained the actions of a group of [[Ewok/Legends|Ewoks]] who spoke only [[Ewokese/Legends|Ewokese]]; as the narration continued throughout the course of the film, it provided context to a plot in which a [[Mace Towani|brother]] and [[Cindel Towani|sister]] had to save their [[Towani family|parents]] from a [[Gorax/Legends|giant monster]].<ref name="CC" />

===Death===
[[File:EruditeIves.jpg|thumb|right|120px|An elderly Ives]]
{{Quote|Burl Ives, whose sweet, strong, mournful way with folk ballads made him an international singing star in the 1940's and whose earthy acting won him an Academy Award in the 1950's, died yesterday at his home in Anacortes, Wash. He was 85.|''The New York Times'' obituary, by Richard Severo|<ref name="Obit" />}}
Ives died on [[April 14]], [[1995]] in [[Wikipedia:Anacortes, Washington|Anacortes]], Washington due to [[Wikipedia:Oral cancer|oral cancer]].<ref name="amug" /><ref name="NYPL">{{WebCite|url=http://archives.nypl.org/uploads/collection/pdf_finding_aid/theives.pdf|text=Guide to the Burl Ives Papers, 1913-1975|author=Burl Ives|format=[[Wikipedia:PDF|PDF]]|work=[http://archives.nypl.org New York Public Library Archives & Manuscripts]|archivedate=20191018022046}}</ref>
