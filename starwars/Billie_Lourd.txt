'''Billie Lourd''' (born [[July 17]], [[1992]]<ref name="SWYT 2020-07-16" />) is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] film and television actress. She is the daughter of actress [[Carrie Fisher]], who portrayed [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. Lourd played [[Lieutenant]] [[Kaydel Ko Connix]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']]'','' and [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. On [[Wikipedia:The Ellen DeGeneres Show|''The Ellen DeGeneres Show'']] in [[2017]], she said that she originally auditioned for the lead role of [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], which ultimately went to [[Daisy Ridley]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://ew.com/movies/2017/09/13/billie-lourd-rey-star-wars-carrie-fisher|text=Carrie Fisher's Daughter, Billie Lourd, Auditioned for ''Star Wars'' Rey Role|author=Holub, Christian|date=2017-09-13|work=Entertainment Weekly|archiveurl=https://archive.is/xMKa1}}</ref> Lourd also played her mother's character in a flashback for Episode IX.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker-visual-effects-secrets-revealed-200000081.html|text=Billie Lourd played Leia Organa and other visual effects secrets from 'Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker'|author=Alter, Ethan|date=2020-01-07|work=Yahoo Entertainment|archivedate=20200318224824}}</ref>

Outside of ''[[Star Wars]]'', she has a starring role in the television series [[Wikipedia:Scream Queens (2015 TV series)|''Scream Queens'']] on FOX, as well as the television series [[Wikipedia:American Horror Story|''American Horror Story'']] on FX.
