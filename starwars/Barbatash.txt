{{Quote|The Jedi have been our sworn enemy for many ages, yes, but were they our sworn enemies when they saved so many of us during the disaster?|Barbatash|<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower" />}}
'''Barbatash''' was an individual who, by the [[time]] of the [[Night of Sorrow]]<ref name="Cataclysm" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> was an [[Elder (Path of the Open Hand)|Elder]] in the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]]. During the Night of Sorrow, Barbatash escaped from the [[Path compound]] on [[Dalna]] in a [[shuttle]].<ref name="Cataclysm" /> By [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]'' take place concurrently to ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' which take in [[231 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Barbatash was an [[Elder (Elders of the Path)|Elder]] in the community known as the [[Elders of the Path]],<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower" /> a group that was a descendant of the Path of the Open Hand.<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> By this time he was considered an ancient being, keeping his community alive until finding them a safe haven on the [[planet]] [[Trymant IV]]. Towering over [[human]]s, Barbatash possessed four long-lashed [[eye]]s and long, shaggy [[Limb|arms]] on which he leaned forward. After Trymant IV was reduced to an inhospitable [[desert]] following an [[Trymant IV disaster|Emergence]] of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], Barbatash remained on the planet, though the community's numbers and resources continued to dwindle.<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower" />
