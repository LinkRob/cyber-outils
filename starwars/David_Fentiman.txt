'''David Fentiman''' is an author of ''[[Star Wars]]'' young-readers titles. Along with [[Sadie Smith]], he wrote and edited the young-readers activity book ''[[Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars]]''</ref> He is the author of the children's [[canon]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Amazing Sticker Adventures]]'',<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/journey-to-star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker-publishing-program|text=Lucasfilm Unveils Complete 'Journey To ''Star Wars: The Rise Of Skywalker''{{'}} Publishing Program &ndash; Exclusive}}</ref> which was published on [[October 4]], [[2019]] as part of the [[Force Friday|Triple Force Friday]] merchandising event.<ref name="Penguin">{{Penguin|url=books/607923/star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker-amazing-sticker-adventures-by-dk/9781465479044|text=Star Wars The Rise of Skywalker Amazing Sticker Adventures by DK}}</ref> Fentiman provided editorial help for ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''.<ref name="Solo Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
