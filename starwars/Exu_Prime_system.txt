The '''Exu Prime system''' was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Tolonda sector]] and contained the astronomical object [[Exu Prime]]. In [[230 BBY]], the [[Star system|system]] became part of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]]&ndash;controlled region of [[Realspace|space]] known as the [[Occlusion Zone]] and was therefore stranded behind the Nihil's [[Stormwall]] [[hyperspace]] barrier.

Between 230 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[229 BBY]], [[Ikkrukkian]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Porter Engle]] [[Ambush on Exu Prime|stole]] a [[Unidentified Nihil patrol (Exu Prime)|Nihil patrol's]] [[Torment (shuttle)|shuttle]] on Exu Prime. In [[228 BBY]], the [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|Stormwall's fall]] dissolved the Occlusion Zone.

==Description==
The Exu Prime system was a [[star system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> containing the terrestrial astronomical object [[Exu Prime]]<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> that was located in the [[Tolonda sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing Sectors" /> It was situated in grid square R-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==History==
[[File:Porter-PhaseIII.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Porter Engle ambushed a Nihil patrol on Exu Prime.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], a section of the Outer Rim was designated as the [[Galactic Frontier]] by the [[Galactic Republic]], which contained locations&mdash;including the Exu Prime system&mdash;considered removed far enough from [[the galaxy]]'s center for the Republic to send [[Republic Pathfinder|explorers]] there.<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi" /> After the [[Deployment of the stormseeds|formation]] of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders']] [[Occlusion Zone]] region of [[Realspace|space]]<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'' establishes that the [[ambush on Exu Prime]] took place between the [[Deployment of the stormseeds|formation]] of the [[Occlusion Zone]] and the one-[[Standard year|year]] anniversary of the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to between [[230 BBY]] and [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> the Exu Prime system became trapped behind the [[Stormwall]],<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> a barrier preventing [[hyperspace]] travel into Nihil space.<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> As one of the systems in the Occlusion Zone, the Exu Prime system<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> was cut off from the rest of Republic space, and the worlds within it were forced to [[Currency|pay]] tributes to the Nihil.<ref name="Eye of Darkness" />

Between 230 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Ikkrukkian]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Porter Engle]] [[Ambush on Exu Prime|ambushed]] a [[Unidentified Nihil patrol (Exu Prime)|Nihil patrol]] on Exu Prime and stole their [[Torment (shuttle)|shuttle]].<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="TrialsDate">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|23|the [[Nihil]]'s surrender}}</ref> the Nihil surrendered to the Republic following the [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|Stormwall's fall]], which dissolved the Occlusion Zone. The worlds of the Occlusion Zone,<ref name="Trials of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref> which included those in the Exu Prime system,<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> were welcomed back into the Republic if they wished to rejoin. However, some instead opted to retain their independence while others remained under the control of individual Nihil [[warlord]]s.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
