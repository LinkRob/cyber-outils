The '''Divo family''' was a [[human]] bloodline of [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] personnel, whose most notorious member was the [[Coruscanti]] [[Law enforcement agency|police]] [[inspector]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo]], who served the [[Coruscant Security Force]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Through his grandson, [[Hosnian Prime]] police [[Commissioner]] [[Exantor Divo]], seventeen generations of the family served in law enforcement.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />
