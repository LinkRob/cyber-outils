'''Brooke Roberts''', sometimes credited as '''Broke Eikmeier''', wrote the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' episode "[[The Crossing]]." She also wrote several episodes of the superhero series ''[[Wikipedia:The Flash (2014 TV series)|The Flash]]'', and was the co-executive producer of the crime television series ''[[Wikipedia:FBI: International|FBI: International]]''.
