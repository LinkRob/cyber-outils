'''Fatulaine''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who served as a [[captain]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]. At some point in his service, he was promoted to oversee activities in the [[Chroman Labor Pits]]; three [[Standard day|days]] into his position, Fatulaine secretly began to free prisoners. He continued to counter Imperial actions, and during the [[Iron Blockade]], he joined the crew of a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]].<ref name="Captain" />

==Behind the scenes==
Captain Fatulaine first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the multiplayer [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', created by the [[Kabam]] company and released on [[September 10]], [[2015]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms. Within the scope of the game, Fatulaine acts as a potential crewmate for the player character, able to be tasked with carrying out timed quests to recover valuable experience points and loot.
