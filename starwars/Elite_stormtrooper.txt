The [[First Order]] maintained '''elite stormtroopers''', a specialized variant of the standard [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]], that were part of its [[First Order Special Forces|special forces]]. Armed with [[SE-44C blaster pistol]]s, they also utilized [[elite speeder bike]]s that were employed for high-risk missions. They wore [[Color|black]] [[First Order stormtrooper armor|stormtrooper armor]], featuring two red stripes on the left side of the chest-plate, a color scheme that matched that of their [[speeder bike]]s.<ref name="Hasbro" />

==Behind the scenes==
Elite stormtroopers and their accompanying speeder bikes first appeared in a [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] toy pack, released as a part of the film's [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (toy line)|marketing campaign]]. However, neither the stormtrooper type nor their bikes actually appeared in the movie itself. They have continued to be absent from further ''[[Star Wars]]'' media or marketing, and have yet to receive an official name.<ref name="Hasbro" />
