'''Beta 3''' was a [[callsign]] used by a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Beta Squadron]]. During a chase for a [[Unidentified rebel spy (Bellicose)|rebel spy]], [[Commander]] [[Tylux]] ordered Beta 3 to disable but not destroy the [[spy]]'s [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|ship]] as they needed it alive.<ref name="Vader - Dark Visions 2" />
