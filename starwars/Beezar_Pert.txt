'''Beezar Pert''' was a [[Gungan]] bandleader of the [[Great Municipal Band]] alongside [[Augara Jowil]].<ref name="OM90" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}}, which includes Beezar Pert's appearance, to [[32 BBY]].</ref> after the [[Battle of Naboo]], Beezar and Augara led the Great Municipal Band through the streets of Naboo's [[capital]], [[Theed]], playing [[Music|songs]] including the "[[Symponik Nabooalla/Canon|Symponik Nabooalla]]."<ref name="OM90" />
