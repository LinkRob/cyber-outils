A '''Bomber Trap''' was a type of [[Starship Trap]] utilized by both the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] to defend their territory on [[planet]]s like [[Tatooine]]. These circular devices serve as a [[proximity sensor]] when initially set, switching to a [[homing beacon]] once their sensor has been tripped. Faction [[bomber]]s were then called to blanket the area surrounding the trap with [[bomb]]s for a short period of time, obliterating enemies. Bomber Traps also featured a [[holoprojector]], displaying the type of [[starship]] they would call in, with Alliance traps contacting [[BTL Y-wing starfighter]]s while Imperial traps contacted [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bombers]].<ref name="Commander" />
