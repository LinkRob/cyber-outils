The '''Cantonarive crown coin''' was an expensive [[coin]] that originated from [[Canto Bight]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], the coin was owned by [[Baron Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] of [[Cloud City]]. Calrissian planned to give the coin to a [[Unidentified grand slicer|grand slicer]] who would, in return, protect Cloud City from being taken over by the [[Galactic Empire]]. However, three Cloud City [[Youngling|children]], [[Moxxin]], [[Traff]], and [[Vanraya]], [[Thief|pickpocketed]] the coin from Calrissian, intending to give it to the [[Crime lord|crime boss]] [[Wonn Ionstrike]] for a reward. Calrissian managed to convince the children that his deal with the [[slicer]] was worth more than anything Ionstrike would give them for the coin, and they changed their minds about giving it to Ionstrike, instead returning it to the Baron Administrator.<ref name="The Coin" />
