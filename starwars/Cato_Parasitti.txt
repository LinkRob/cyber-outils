'''Cato Parasitti''' was a [[Clawdite]] [[Gender|female]] [[bounty hunter]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], she was hired by [[Cad Bane]] to [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|steal]] a valuable [[holocron]] from the [[Jedi Archives|archives]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]], which she managed by impersonating the [[Death|deceased]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Ord Enisence]] and later [[Jocasta Nu]].

==Biography==
===Early life and career===
A [[Clawdite]] [[Gender|female]], Cato Parasitti was [[Reproduction|born]] on the desolate [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Zolan]] prior to start of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="HC 39">{{HelmetCollectionCite|39|Highlights of the Saga}}</ref> During her career as a [[bounty hunter]], she gained a reputation as an incredibly lethal assassin.<ref name="topps2021">{{Topps|set=2021 Topps Star Wars Bounty Hunters|cardname=Cato Parasitti|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/263568/cid/18708432}}</ref> After the death of legendary bounty hunter [[Jango Fett]],<ref name="HC 39" /> Cato worked with his protégé [[Cad Bane]] on a number of occasions.<ref name="HC 44">{{HelmetCollectionCite|44|Databank A-Z}}</ref>
===Infiltration of the Jedi Temple===
{{Quote|You should be able to walk into the Archives and monitor us without a problem.''"<br />"''Now that I am a Jedi, I can do that.|Cad Bane and Cato Parasitti, as the latter disguises herself as Ord Enisence|<ref name="heist" />}}
[[File:CatoAndCad-HH.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Parasitti worked closely with Cad Bane.]]
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="HC 39" /> Cato was hired by Cad Bane to help [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|steal]] a [[Holocron]] from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] for the [[Sith]] [[Sith Lord|Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]]. Together, Cato and Bane used a security chip provided by Bane's [[Techno-service droid|techno-service droid]] [[Todo 360]], and schematics of the temple provided by the Sith Lord, to plan the heist. Bane presented Parasitti with the [[Death|corpse]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Ord Enisence]], whom he had recently kidnapped and murdered. Using her abilities as a [[Changeling|changeling]], she transformed into the [[Jedi]], and her [[Holographic disguise matrix|holographic disguise matrix]] emulated his robes.<ref name="heist" /> 

When she entered the [[Jedi Archives]] disguised as Enisence, Parasitti was stopped by [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. To avoid distraction, Cato harshly dismissed the young Jedi and proceeded to a terminal, where she remotely assisted Bane and Todo in bypassing security. The intrusion was discovered, and the temple was placed on high alert. [[Chief Librarian]] [[Jocasta Nu]] questioned why Enisence was still working. Parasitti knocked the [[Librarian|librarian]] unconscious, hid her body, and assumed her identity. Meanwhile, Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] deduced that an infiltrator disguised as a Jedi was aiding the intruders and contacted Tano.<ref name="heist" /> 

[[File:Cato_Parasitti_-_Databank.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Parasitti defeated by Tano.]]
Tano found Cato speaking with her accomplice over an [[Earbud comlink|earbud comlink]], ignited [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|her lightsaber]], and demanded to know what had happened to Madame Jocasta. Parasitti drew the [[Jocasta Nu's lightsaber|librarian's saber]] and fought back, but Tano, aided by [[The Force|the Force]], quickly subdued her. After she realized that she had been abandoned by Bane, Parasitti revealed that his target was the [[Holocron Vault]]. After [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] failed to stop Bane from stealing the Holocron, Cato informed them that his next target would be Jedi Master [[Bolla Ropal]],<ref name="heist" /> hoping to reduce her inevitable prison sentence.<ref name="HC 39" /> 

===Disrupting negotiations===
{{Quote|Is she on the guest list?''"<br />"''Uh, no.|Ahsoka Tano and Padmé Amidala about Parasitti|<ref name="FoD">{{FOD|The Imposter Inside}}</ref>}}
[[File:KarteImpersonated-FODAhsokaPadme.png|thumb|left|160px|Parasitti disguised as Karté.]]
Later in 21 BBY,<ref name="ImposterDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "The Imposter Inside" to [[21 BBY]].</ref> after Parasitti left prison,<ref name="ComprehensiveGuide">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> the planet [[Arthuria]] was considering joining the [[Galactic Republic]] after the [[Arthurian|Arthurians]] felt disrespected by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Disguised as [[Karté]], one of her [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|royal handmaidens]], Parasitti [[Attempt to bomb a diplomatic reception|infiltrated]] [[Naboo]] [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala]]'s [[Padmé's Apartment|apartment]] to disrupt negotiations that were to be hosted there with the [[Arthurian delegates]].

While she set the dining table, Cato planted a [[thermal detonator]] underneath. As she made her escape, the senator and her Jedi companion Ahsoka called out to her, asking why the table was set with [[Fork|utensils]] when the Arthurians were a species that did not eat with them. Cato pulled a [[Blaster pistol|blaster pistol]] and fired at Amidala and the Padawan, who flipped the table and hid behind it. Tano ignited her sabers and deflected a shot back into Cato's arm. Cato fell to the floor, dropping her disguise and revealing her true Clawdite form.<ref name="FoD" />
[[File:Cato_Parasitti_smashed.png|thumb|right|200px|Parasitti's plot to disrupt the negotiations was foiled.]]
With her plan foiled, Cato attempted to detonate the previously planted bomb to kill Amidala. Padmé tossed the bomb to Ahsoka, who then used the Force to throw it from the apartment where it went off harmlessly in the air. While she attempted to flee, Padmé hit Cato with a vase and knocked her unconscious.<ref name="FoD" />

===Chalmun's Cantina===
In [[20 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|420}}</ref> Cato was a patron at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Desert|desert]] planet [[Tatooine]]. She once joined other patrons in laughter after former [[Sith acolyte]] [[Asajj Ventress]] killed a bounty hunter who attempted to flirt with her.<ref name="Bounty">{{TCW|Bounty (episode)}}</ref> Later that same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date2">{{TCWcanondates|422}}</ref> she was served a drink by the Cantina's [[BU-series bartender droid]] while Ventress accepted a bounty on the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Savage Oppress]].<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref>

===Legacy===
[[Standard decade|Decades]] later, in [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SecretsoftheBountyHunters Date">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]'' establishes that the in-universe book was written after the [[war against the Pykes]], but before the [[statue of IG-11]] was taken down in "[[Chapter 17: The Apostate]]." ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the war against the [[Pyke]]s to [[9 ABY]], and "Chapter 17: The Apostate" takes place in 9 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] per the reasoning [[Template:MandoDate|here]]. Therefore, ''The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters'' must have been created in that [[Standard year|year]] as well.</ref> Parasitti was mentioned in ''[[The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]'', a [[book]] written by the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]], where he noted that she might still be alive, undetected due to her changeling abilities.<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Who are you, and what have you done with Madame Jocasta?''"<br />"''The same thing I'm going to do to you!|Ahsoka Tano to Cato Parasitti, disguised as Jocasta Nu|<ref name="heist" />}}
Cato Parasitti, a Clawdite [[Thief|thief]], [[Con artist|con artist]], bounty hunter, and assassin, went wherever the money was. Her chest plate bore a scorch mark from a blaster bolt, which she refused to clean to show off how tough she was.<ref name="HC 39" /> Though she initially shared Cad Bane's belief that infiltrating the Jedi Temple was impossible, the chance to disguise herself as a Jedi Master and move undetected among them proved too tempting to resist.<ref name="heist" /> 

==Powers and abilities==
[[File:Skrilling_jedi_changeling.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Parasitti impersonates Ord Enisence to infiltrate the Jedi Archives.]]
Cato Parasitti's Clawdite biology enabled her to excel at shape-shifting, allowing her to pose as [[Human|humans]] such as the Naboo Royal Handmaiden Karté<ref name="FoD" /> or as the [[Skrilling]] Jedi Ord Enisence.<ref name="heist" /> She was highly agile and skilled in infiltration, surveillance, armed and unarmed combat, and [[Slicer|data slicing]].<ref name="Join the Battle" /> She supplemented her fighting with acrobatic somersaults, but her lack of lightsaber training left her at a severe disadvantage against Ahsoka Tano. In their duel, Parasitti relied on defensive maneuvers, and her untrained attacks ultimately led to her defeat by the Padawan.<ref name="heist" />

==Equipment==
Parasitti wore an outfit that stretched to fit any shape she took on.<ref name="HC 39" /> Her armor incorporated a holographic disguise matrix that allowed her to appear in anyone's clothing. She carried a [[Weequay blaster lance|long-barreled blaster rifle]]<ref name="heist" /> and an [[LL-30 blaster pistol]].<ref name="Cato Parasitti" /> She wore a helmet with a flip-down lensed visor, which she even wore when she wasn't on a mission or facing any danger.<ref name="Revenge" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It was very important that Cato Parasitti as Jocasta Nu does not really have lightsaber skills. So we actually went through a large amount of effort to make most of the impostor's attacks defensive, and when offensive, incredibly wild and overdone.|Dave Filoni|<ref name="Holocron Heist Comment">{{SWArchive|url=watch/holocronheist_commentary.html|text=Episode Commentary #2.1 &ndash; Holocron Heist}}</ref>}}
Cato Parasitti was created for the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated television series. She first appeared in the [[2009]] episode "[[Holocron Heist]]," the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] premiere, where was voiced by [[Gwendoline Yeo]].<ref name="heist" /> In [[2017]] she appeared in the [[Forces of Destiny: Season One|first season]] of the animated micro-series ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]'' in the episode "[[The Imposter Inside]]" where she was voiced by [[Gina Torres]].<ref name="FoD" />
