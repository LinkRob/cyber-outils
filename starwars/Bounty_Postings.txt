'''Bounty Postings''' is a series of articles published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website. Written by [[Eric Cagle]], they provide gamesmasters with [[Bounty/Legends|bounty]] targets to use with the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game Saga Edition Core Rulebook|Saga Edition]]''.
