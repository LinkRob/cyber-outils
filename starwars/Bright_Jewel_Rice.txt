'''Bright Jewel Rice''' was a [[Diet|vegetarian]] fried brown [[rice]] [[Foodstuff|dish]] that was made with a variety of [[Color|red]] and green [[fruit]]s, [[vegetable]], and exotic [[Spice (seasoning)|spices]] from across [[the galaxy]]. The dish was a combination of flavors unique to the culturally diverse [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Ord Mantell]] and was named for the world's [[star]], [[Bright Jewel]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

During the culinary tour of the [[galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] ate Bright Jewel Rice while in [[Worlport]], the [[capital]] of Ord Mantell. The dish was Tuggs' favorite among those that he tried in the [[city]] and was one of the highlights of his entire tour. He included a recipe for Bright Jewel Rice in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bright Jewel Rice was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the rice makes use of [[Wikipedia:Vegetable oil|vegetable oil]], diced [[Wikipedia:Onion|onion]], diced [[Wikipedia:Bell pepper|red pepper]], minced [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic cloves]], minced fresh [[Wikipedia:Ginger|ginger]], leftover cooked [[Wikipedia:Rice as food|rice]], beaten [[Wikipedia:Eggs as food|eggs]], [[Wikipedia:Oyster sauce|oyster sauce]], [[Wikipedia:Soy sauce#Varieties|tamari]], [[Wikipedia:Sesame oil|sesame oil]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper#White pepper|white pepper]], crushed [[Wikipedia:Pineapple|pineapple]], [[Wikipedia:Pea|green peas]], and [[Wikipedia:Scallion|green onions]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
