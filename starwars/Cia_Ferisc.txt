'''Cia Ferisc''' was a [[Silinese]] warrior who,<ref name="TCC" /> in [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> when she was between the ages of fifteen and twenty-five, participated in [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|that year's]] [[Crowning Convocation]]. During the [[survival level]] of the competition, Ferisc snuck up on [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Yord Fandar]], a fellow participant. Fandar was distracted while planning a way to get [[Uavin fruit|fruit]] from a [[uavin tree]] without being able to use [[the Force]]. 

Posing as a potential ally, the Silinese tricked Fandar into letting his guard down under the false pretense of helping one another get fruit. She then [[thief|stole]] his makeshift [[staff]] and fled. As Fandar pursued her through the [[forest]], he was soon joined by his friend [[Sitia Arthyst]]. The latter eventually caught Ferisc, tackling her. Ferisc lost the resulting fight, effectively ending her prospects in the competition. Her supplies were subsequently looted by Fandar and Arthyst. This upset Ferisc, and she threw scathing words at Arthyst, though her insults had minimal effect on the other Silinese.<ref name="TCC" />
