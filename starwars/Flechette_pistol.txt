'''Flechette pistols''' were a class of pistol that fired needle-like projectiles called [[flechette]]s. [[Malaxan Firepower Incorporated]]'s [[FWG-5 smart pistol|FWG-5 flechette pistol]] was one such weapon.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Flechette pistols were first mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Knights of Fate]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2017]].<ref name="Knights of Fate">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref>
