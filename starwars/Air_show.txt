An '''air show''' was a public event where [[pilot]]s performed complex maneuvers for the entertainment of their spectators. They were often performed during ceremonies, celebrations and holidays, such as the [[Bail Organa statue-dedication ceremony|statue-dedication ceremony]] in honor of [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], and [[Equinox Day]] on [[Hosnian Prime]].<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>
