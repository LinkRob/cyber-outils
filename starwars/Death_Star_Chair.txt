The '''Death Star Chair''' was the collective name of a custom [[Repulsorlift chair|repulsorlift]] [[Command Chair]] and a pair of [[Blaster Remote]]s owned by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Command Chair (Sprocket)|standard chair and remote set]]. The chair resembled a miniature [[Death Star]], with a [[Color|black]] and reddish-brown seat mounted on top, and a white light attached to the front side of the connected control panel, as well as various smaller lights on the spherical base as well. The [[remote]]s, instead, resembled a pair of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], with faux [[solar collector]]s attached to both sides of each.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Death Star Chair was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Sprocket's Command Chair, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Star Programmer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
