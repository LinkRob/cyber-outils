'''Cyanoxis D-570''' was a [[poison]] manufactured by the company [[Toxico]], originally used to [[Death|kill]] [[rodent]]s as a rodenticide. The poison was an anticoagulant and led to internal hemorrhaging after a creature ingested it, eventually causing death. The chemical makeup of Cyanoxis D-570 was kept a closely-guarded secret by Toxico and it was deadly to numerous [[species]] around [[the galaxy]], which led [[Spy|spies]] and [[assassin]]s to use it.<ref name="CAM" />

D-570 was typically administered via [[Foodstuff|food]] and drink, and takes about a [[Standard week|week]] to break down in an individual's circulatory system and cause internal [[Blood|bleeding]]. Symptoms of D-570 included fatigue, bruises, and bleeding from the [[nose]], [[mouth]], and [[ear]]s. Death occurred in around ten [[Standard day|days]] or fewer, making the cause of an individual's death difficult to determine, and allowing time for the killer to escape.<ref name="CAM" />

[[Zlarb's]], a poisons shop on [[Coruscant]], carried Cyanoxis D-570.<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-hidden-empire|text=''Hidden Empire'' Revealed: Charles Soule on Marvel's Next ''Star Wars'' Epic &ndash; Exclusive}} establishes that the events of ''[[Star Wars: Crimson Reign]]'', including [[Crimson Reign 2|''Crimson Reign'' 2]], take place between the events of {{Film|V}} and {{Film|VI}}, which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Master Assassin]]<ref name="Vader 22">[[Darth Vader (2020) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 22]]</ref> [[Ochi]] of [[Bestoon]] purchased a dose of D-570 from the owner, [[Zlarb]], to aid in his [[Assassination of the Emperor's Royal Guard|assassination]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]'s [[Emperor's Royal Guard|royal guard.]] Ochi charged the fee to his employer, [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]] of the [[criminal|crime]] syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]].<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" />
