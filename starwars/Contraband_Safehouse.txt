'''Contraband Safehouses''' were structures utilized by both the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in association with the [[Hutt Clan]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They were used to store contraband delivered via [[smuggler]]s at [[Contraband Trade Port]]s, and were commonly guarded by at least one armed [[Weequay]].<ref name="Commander" />
