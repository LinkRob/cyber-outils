A '''Chain Sickle''' was a type of [[melee weapon]] used by the [[Dathomirian|Dathomiri]] [[Nightsisters]] and [[Nightbrothers]] of the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]].

==Description==
A [[Death|deadly]] two-handed weapon, the Chain Sickle consisted of a razor-sharp, scythe-shaped blade attached to a cylindrical handle by a long, flexible cord. A second hand grip was positioned approximately one-third of the way between the primary handle and the blade. The cord between the second handle and the blade was covered in small, sharp spikes.<ref name="Monster" /><ref name="AtG">{{Destiny|set=Across the Galaxy|cardname=Chain Sickle|link=http://swdestinydb.com/card/08015}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:Ventress_Chain_Sickle.png|thumb|left|250px|Asajj Ventress wielding a Chain Sickle during the Selection.]]
During her [[Clone Wars]], the Nightsister [[Asajj Ventress]] journeyed to the [[Nightbrother village]] on Dathomir to take part in the [[Selection]] to choose a champion from among the Nightbrothers. In the [[Trial of Blindness|second]] of three trials, Ventress wielded a Chain Sickle against four Nightbrothers.<ref name="Monster" />

Later, while investigating the origin of [[Savage Opress]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] traveled to Dathomir. Upon landing their [[T-6 shuttle|shuttle]] at the Nightbrothers' village, the Jedi disembarked and were surrounded by a group of Nightbrothers soon after entering the village. One of the Nightbrothers who attacked Kenobi and Skywalker brandished a Chain Sickle.<ref name="WotM" />
