'''Container ships''' were [[starship]]s owned by [[Eriadu Mining and Shipping]]. They were most commonly used for carrying [[ore]]s like [[lommite]]. They traveled in [[convoy]]s and were [[Slavery|slave]] rigged to shepherd vessels to save the cost of hiring crew members and adding [[life support]]. During the [[Greater Seswenna raids]] many of the unprotected convoys were raided by [[Q'anah's Marauders]] until [[Outland Regions Security Force]] [[Commander]] [[Ranulph Tarkin]] came up with an idea to add a virus to the Container ship's [[navigation computer]] which would get onto the [[pirate]] ships and send them to new jump coordinates where the Outlands Security [[Fleet]] was waiting. With the threat gone, the container ships could continue without the threat of being attacked.<ref name="Tarkin" />
