A '''destroyer''' was a type of [[warship]] used by multiple factions in [[the galaxy]]. There were multiple examples of destroyers, used by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], and other groups.

==Description==
===Overview===
A destroyer was a type of [[warship]] used by various factions throughout [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> As a [[capital ship]], the [[Anaxes War College System]] defined a destroyer as a warship that measured approximately 1,000 to 2,000 [[meter]]s in length, placing it after a [[heavy cruiser]] and before a [[battlecruiser]]. The system introduced the destroyer class in<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> what the [[Chiss]] and other Unknown Regions groups refer to as Lesser Space, the galaxy beyond the Unknown Regions.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

===Models===
[[File:Star_Destroyer.png|thumb|left|300px|An example of a Star Destroyer, the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class]]]]
There were multiple destroyer models in service. The [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Ruling Families]] and [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] used the [[Chiss destroyer]]<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> and the [[Destroyer|Clarr destroyer]].<ref name="Lesser Evil">{{WebCite|url=https://www.polygon.com/22770279/star-wars-thrawn-ascendancy-book-3-lesser-evil-timothy-zahn-interview-excerpt|text=Timothy Zahn prepares us for the end of the ''Star Wars: Thrawn Ascendancy trilogy''|author=Patches, Matt|date=2021-11-09|archivedate=20211109165826}}</ref> The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] used multiple models, including the [[Corporate Alliance]]'s [[Fantail-class destroyer|''Fantail''-class destroyer]] and the [[Commerce Guild]]'s [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class Light Destroyer]]<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> and [[Recusant-class Support Destroyer|''Recusant''-class Support Destroyer]].<ref name="Support">{{Armada|set=Recusant-class Destroyer Expansion Pack|cardname=''Recusant''-class Support Destroyer}}</ref> The [[Nikardun destroyer]] was deployed by the [[Nikardun Destiny]] during their [[Nikardun conquests|conquests]] in the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> [[Star Destroyer]]s were used by factions like the [[Galactic Republic]], [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> [[Imperial Remnants]],<ref name="Part Six">{{Ahsoka|Part Six: Far, Far Away}}</ref> [[First Order]], and [[Sith Eternal]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==History==
Although the classification of destroyer was used in the Unknown Regions,<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> the Anaxes War College System expanded the nomenclature during the [[Clone Wars]] to include three new classes; the destroyer, the [[battlecruiser]], and the [[dreadnought]], despite the earlier [[dreadnought era]] existing during the [[Alsakan Conflicts]]. Destroyers saw use in the Clone Wars, with the rise of the [[Star Destroyer]] design family and the destroyers used by the Separatists. Star Destroyers continued service during the [[Imperial Era]] after the Clone Wars, as well as during the rise and fall of the [[First Order]] several [[Standard decade|decades]] later.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" />
