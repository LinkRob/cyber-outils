The '''Chakham Army Base''' was a [[Galactic Federation of Free Alliances|Galactic Alliance]] military base on [[Kuratooine]] named after [[General/Legends|General]] [[Chakham]]. It was built sometime around when [[General/Legends|General]] [[Stavin Thaal]] assumed command of the [[Galactic Alliance Army]]. In [[44 ABY]], the [[89th]], five companies of [[Pop-Dogs]] army unit, was stationed on the base.<ref name="MK" />
