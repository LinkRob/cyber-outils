'''Canyon adders''' were a type of venomous [[snake]]. While on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[Gender|woman]] [[Esmelle]] criticized her [[Wexley family|sister]], the [[New Republic pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]], for abandoning her [[Temmin Wexley|son]] to join the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Wexley was infuriated by her sister's words, mentally comparing her to a venomous canyon adder.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Canyon adders were mentioned in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
