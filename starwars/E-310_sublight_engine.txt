The '''E-310 sublight engine''' was a [[Color|gray]] model of [[sublight engine]]. Upgrading an [[EML-850 light freighter]] previously equipped with the slower [[E-210 sublight engine]]s to E-310s cost seven hundred and fifty [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and required ten units of [[Quadanium steel|quadanium]], and four enhanced [[durasteel]] [[hull]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
During [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] paid for the services of a [[starship]] [[Technician|mechanic]] to upgrade her EML-850 [[light freighter]] ''[[Trailblazer]]'s'' E-210 engines to E-310 sublight engines, providing the necessary parts.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}
