'''Dunstig Pterro''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[captain]] in the [[Galactic Empire]], who served as [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]]'s personal aide in [[1 BBY]]. This position had him [[pilot]]ing [[ST-149|''ST 149'']], a [[Delta-class T-3c shuttle|''Delta''-class T-3c shuttle]] that was used as a [[transport]] for the Director and [[Krennic's guard squadron|his]] [[death trooper]] unit. Whilst Krennic viewed the craft as a tool, Pterro held a secret affection for the [[Starship|ship]], even nicknaming it ''Pteradon''.<ref name="ROVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==Biography==
{{Quote|Sir, we're in position, ready to..."''<br/>''"Fire!"''<br/>''"Commence primary ignition.|Dunstig Pterro and Orson Krennic, before the Death Star first test on Jedha City|<ref name="Rogue One" />}}
Pterro accompanied Krennic and the death troopers when the former went aboard [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s [[Star Destroyer]], ''[[Executrix]]'', glancing around disinterestedly as the group went onto the bridge to talk to Tarkin. He later accompanied them to the [[Overbridge (Death Star I)|overbridge]] of the [[Death Star]] and gave the order to prepare the battle station's [[superlaser]] at single reactor ignition. When told that they were prepared, Krennic snapped at him angrily to fire, and a bored Pterro relayed the order to a [[Death Star Trooper]] who in turn relayed it to the station's gunners. He then watched as [[Jedha City]] was destroyed on the [[Jedha|planet's]] surface below.<ref name="Rogue One" />

Following this, Pterro flew Krennic and the death troopers to [[Eadu]], landing the ship on the narrow landing platform jutting over a chasm. Donning a raincoat, he accompanied Krennic and the troopers to greet [[Galen Walton Erso]] and stood by impassively as the [[Tarkin Initiative]] scientists were gunned down as punishment for Erso's betrayal. He survived the ensuing attack by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] starfighters, and yelled to Krennic that they needed to escape, before the two men and the surviving death troopers scurried aboard the shuttle, and Pterro flew them to safety, informing his superior officer that they'd been instructed to fly to [[Mustafar]]. At Mustafar, Pterro remained aboard the ship with the death troopers when Krennic went inside [[Fortress Vader]] to meet with [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

At some point, Pterro returned to the Death Star and did not accompany Krennic or the shuttle to [[Scarif]], as he was on the Death Star's overbridge with Tarkin and General [[Hurst Romodi]] throughout the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Rogue One" />
