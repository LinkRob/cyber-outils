'''Command Sector North''' was an area along the equatorial trench within the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

==Description==
The command sector's area ran along the Death Star II's equatorial trench. It consisted of hangars, an armory, a conference room, access to the shafts of one of the [[superlaser]]'s [[tributary laser stream]]s, and control stations for the station's tractor beam along with the sector's weapons systems. The hangars were used to dock [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttles]]. The command sector also had several [[Super Blaster 920 laser cannon]]s and [["Tulip" style Imperial work station]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Its southern counterpart was [[Command Sector (South)|Command Sector South]].<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Blueprints|cardname=Death Star II}}</ref>

==History==
The command sector was constructed between [[0 ABY|0]] and [[4 ABY]], and was located along the Death Star II's equatorial trench.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

During the [[Battle of Endor]] in 4 ABY, when the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] attacked the Death Star II, a [[Unidentified CR90 corvette (Command Sector North)|Rebel]] [[CR90 corvette]] captained by [[Major]] [[Shin]] would be shot down and crash-land in one of the command sector's hangars. After the crash, Major Shin led the surviving Rebel passengers in a fight through the command center to sabotage its systems and commandeer a shuttle to escape the battle station, in which they were opposed by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces [[Brenna's brigade|under the command]] of [[Colonel]] [[Brenna]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> [[R2-Q5]], an Imperial [[astromech droid]] stationed in the sector, [[desertion|deserted]] and assisted the rebels in their raid.<ref name="GalacticFiles">{{Topps|set=2018 Topps Star Wars Galactic Files|cardname=ROTJ-20 - R2-Q5|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/170092/cid/11689258/2018-Topps-Star-Wars:-Galactic-Files-ROTJ-20-R2-Q5}}</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
The rebels first gained ground by overriding the battle station's tractor beam generator and control stations. They prioritized sabotaging the controls to the sector's [[turbolaser]] turrets and [[tributary laser stream]], assuring that even if they were not to escape, they could at least lighten the enemy fire upon the rest of the Rebellion. Overriding controls to another hangar and its shuttle bay elevator, Shin and his troops successfully secured a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle]] and escaped. Brenna would engage with Shin's reorganized forces at [[Endor]], both in orbit and at [[Research Station 9]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />
{{EndGame}}

The command sector would be destroyed shortly after; the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' and [[Red Leader]] attacked the Death Star II's [[hypermatter reactor]] and caused it to explode, destroying the entire battle station.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:CSN-ConceptArt.jpg|thumb|200px|Concept art for Command Sector North in ''Star Wars Battlefront II'']]
Command Sector North appears in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' as a map for Galactic Assault, Heroes vs. Villains, Hero Showdown, Supremacy, Co-Op Missions, Blast, Instant Action, and Arcade. Its appearance in Galactic Assault features an overall story in which during the [[Battle of Endor]], a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[CR90 corvette]] crash-landed in Command Sector North. After the crash, the surviving Rebel passengers led by [[Major]] [[Shin]] engaged in a skirmish with [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces under the command of [[Colonel]] [[Brenna]]. The first phase of the game involves the Rebels overriding the battle station's tractor beam generator and control stations. The second phase then includes shutting down the Death Star II's main weapons and superlaser controls, while the third phase consists of overriding controls to a hangar and its shuttle bay elevator in order to secure a shuttle to escape with. The Imperial players are tasked with stopping the Rebels while they attempt to take these objectives. If the Rebels win, they are shown flying away in a ''Lambda''-class shuttle while the Death Star explodes behind them. If the Imperials win, they are shown capturing the Rebels and securing the command sector.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

The droid [[R2-Q5]] cameos in the Command Sector North map.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> His [[2018 Topps Star Wars Galactic Files]] card states he "perform[ed] duties for the Rebel Alliance during the second Death Star raid",<ref name="GalacticFiles" /> affirming that the general events of the Rebel skirmish through the command sector seen in Galactic Assault and his presence there are canonical.

===Continuity===
In ''Battlefront II'', Command Sector North is shown to be along the equatorial trench,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> however, in the [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]] app, it is shown on the Death Star II blue prints to be towards the top of battlestation.<ref name="SWCT" />
