"'''Conversation in Shadow'''" is an article written by [[August Hahn]] and [[Cynthia Hahn]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[July 29]], [[2004]] and described a visit by [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu/Legends|Mace Windu]] to the [[Almas Academy]]. He had been asked to come by [[Lanius Qel-Bertuk]] who had some concerns over his new staff member [[Darrus Jeht/Legends|Darrus Jeht]]. Windu told Qel-Berturk the particular history of Jeht and then departed for [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].

In the context of the Living Force campaign, following this article, Jedi player characters faced an increasingly chance that their mentor or a close Jedi friend would be killed during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].
