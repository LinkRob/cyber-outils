'''Freerago''', nicknamed '''Free''',<ref name="Last Shot" /> was a [[Gamorrean]]<ref name="FACPOV" /> [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> and the owner of [[Freerago's Satellite Diner and Motel]].<ref name="Last Shot" /> He was a friend of the [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]], who thought Freerago's diner served the best [[Fritzle Fries]] in [[the galaxy]]. Tuggs assumed that Freerago's establishment was destroyed by the [[First Order]] in the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]].<ref name="Cookbook" />
