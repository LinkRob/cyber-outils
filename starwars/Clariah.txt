'''Clariah''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Nautolan]] and old acquaintance of [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. She had a son, named [[Jiandy]].

==Biography==
Clariah came to visit Lando Calrissian while he was the leader of [[Cloud City]], bringing her son Jiandy with her. She sat down to talk with Lando, and explained to him that Jiandy's recent behavior was concerning her: he had been skipping school, spending time with suspicious people, and had expressed a desire to someday lead a criminal empire like [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]]. Clariah revealed that she had one of Lando's favorite [[cape]]s, which he thought he had lost, and promised she would give it back to him if he helped Jiandy see the error of his ways. At first, Lando refused, but when he saw Jiandy cheat at a [[Gambling|game of cards]] and then trick his opponents into fighting each other, he changed his mind and agreed to do it.<ref name="Family Affair, Part 1" />

After Lando successfully convinced Jiandy to give up his dreams of being a gangster, Clariah came to talk to him again. As promised, she gave Lando his cape back, but expressed her concern that kids like Jiandy would continue to be tempted to a life of crime.<ref name="Family Affair, Part 2">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=11|story=Tales from Wild Space: Family Affair, Part 2}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Clariah was a [[Gender|female]] [[Nautolan]] with [[Color|blue]] skin and large black eyes. She had circles of darker blue around her eyes and bands of lighter blue on the tendrils on her head.<ref name="Family Affair, Part 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clariah first appeared in the short comic story "[[Tales from Wild Space: Family Affair, Part 1]]," written by [[Elsa Charretier]] and [[Pierrick Colinet]] and illustrated by Charretier. The story was published in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 10|tenth issue]] of the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'' series]],<ref name="Family Affair, Part 1" /> which was released on [[May 9]], [[2018]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2017-2020-10/digital-comic/652799|text=Star Wars Adventures #10|nolive=1}}</ref> Charretier stated on Twitter that Clariah's design was based on the actress [[Anjelica Huston]].<ref name="Clariah">{{Twitter|e_charretier|status/1005125821588295681|[[Elsa Charretier]]|quote=Clariah's design, one of the characters from our STAR WARS ADVENTURES #11 Lando story was based on Anjelica Huston 😬 !|date=2018-06-09|archivedate=20230523123136}}</ref>
