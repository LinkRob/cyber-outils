The '''Atrivis Resistance Group''' ('''ARG'''), informally known as the '''Atrivis cell''', was an early resistance group against the [[Galactic Empire]] led by [[Travia Chan]], and was active in the [[Atrivis sector]]. After the [[Battle on Mantooine|defeat]] of the [[Liberators]] in [[5 BBY]], [[Catta Dionize]], on behalf of Chan, wrote to [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] that the group decided to join her in what she named "the Alliance," becoming one of the first units of the later established [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Their successful integration into the Rebel Alliance [[Alliance Military|military]] led Mothma to believe that the [[Alliance High Command]] hierarchical structure was broadly functional.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
