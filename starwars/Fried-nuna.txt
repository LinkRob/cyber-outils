'''Fried-nuna''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] made from [[nuna]]s,<ref name="MA" /> [[Diet|omnivore]] [[bird]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Naboo]].<ref name="ABEUE">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> Around [[56 BBY]],<ref name="MA Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]]. [[Rahara Wick]] is established to be twenty-nine years old in the novel, and to have absconded from [[Slavery|enslavement]] when she was thirteen years old, placing her escape around [[56 BBY]].</ref> [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] worker [[Rahara Wick]] escaped her [[Slavery|enslavers]] by stealing a [[knife]] from a fried-nuna [[Merchant|vendor]] to cut out her slave chip while on<ref name="MA" /> the planet<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Ord Mantell]]. [[Standard year|Years]] later, she would recall the experience and remember the pain of the knife's cut and felt no regret.<ref name="MA" /> Throughout [[the galaxy]], fried-nuna was considered to be a popular dish.<ref name="Lists">''[[Star Wars: Book of Lists]]''</ref> Nuna could also be deep fried to create meals like the [[Nuna drumstick]].<ref name="Voyage of Temptation">{{TCW|Voyage of Temptation}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
