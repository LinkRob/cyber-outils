'''Dirk Ullodin''' was a [[Mandalorian]] who [[pilot]]ed a [[Fang-class Protectorate fighter|''Fang''-class]] [[starfighter]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. A member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], Dirk participated in a battle in [[Realspace|space]] and shot down a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]. At that [[time]], Dirk aspired to become a [[commando]].<ref name="Pride of Mandalore" />
