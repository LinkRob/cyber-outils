'''Char Siu Chicken Tip-Yip Drumsticks''' were a [[tip-yip]] meal served at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Kat Saka's Kettle" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> They were consumable by [[human]]s.<ref name="Kat Saka's Kettle" />

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Char Siu Chicken Tip-Yip Drumsticks was an offering exclusively available at Kat Saka's Kettle in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]] for a limited time in fall of [[2022]]. The menu described the seasonal offering as "[[Wikipedia:Char siu|Char Siu]]-glazed [[Chicken]] [[Wikipedia:Drumstick (poultry)|Drumsticks]]."<ref name="DLP Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/kat-sakas-kettle/menus|text=Disneyland: Kat Saka's Kettle Snack Menu}}</ref>
