'''''Disney Die-Cut Classics''''' is a series of hardcover illustrated storybooks published by [[Studio Fun International]]. Each book in the series features a glossy outer hardcover with a circle cut out of the center, revealing an illustration on the page underneath.<ref name="Die Cut Announce" />

The series started with storybooks retelling classic [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] animated films in [[2019]],<ref name="Die Cut Announce" /> but later branched out to include ''[[Star Wars]]'' films and other properties owned by Disney. The first three ''Star Wars'' books in the series were published on [[May 5]], [[2020]].
