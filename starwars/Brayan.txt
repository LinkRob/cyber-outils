'''Brayans''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] from the [[planet]] [[Bray]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], a [[Unidentified village (Bray)|village]] on Bray was terrorized by [[Lord]] [[Ravna]], leading to the local Brayan population to ask the [[Jedi Council]] for help. The [[Jedi Master]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Adi Gallia]] were then [[Mission to Bray|sent]] to the planet, and the [[Jedi]] successfully defeated both Ravna and [[Count]] [[Dooku]], freeing the Brayans.<ref name="TFVC 2" />
