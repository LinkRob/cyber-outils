The '''''Arbitrator''''' was an [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] and one of thirteen [[Super Star Destroyer]]s in the [[Galactic Empire|Empire's]] service prior to the [[Battle of Endor]]. Around the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] considered it to be one of the Empire's most critical ''Executor''-class [[Starship|vessels]]. Between [[4 ABY]] and [[5 ABY]], the ''Arbitrator'' was pursued by several [[New Republic]] starships. Attempting to escape them, the [[dreadnought]] jumped to erroneous [[hyperspace]] coordinates, which resulted in the destruction of the vessel after it was pulled into a [[black hole]].

==Description==
{{Quote|These dreadnoughts, or Super Star Destroyers, are more akin to mobile command centers than proper warships, yet they can annihilate an armada through concentrated arms alone. Five thousand turbolasters&hellip;what could withstand such a broadside?| Gial Ackbar, Rebel Alliance document "RI 043-N1"|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
As an [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]], the ''Arbitrator'' measured 19,000 [[meter]]s long and was armed with 5,000 [[turbolaser]]s. [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] believed that a [[Starship|ship]] of the ''Executor''-class could destroy an [[Fleet|armada]] through concentrated firepower alone.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> The ''Arbitrator'' was also equipped with [[engine]]s and a [[hyperdrive]]. The [[command ship]] transmitted tracking data to the [[Hall of Imperial Register]] on the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], allowing for a [[black box]] recording of information so it could be discerned what occurred before the ship was destroyed, captured, or surrendered.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==History==
{{Quote|Following are the Empire's most critical SSDs, both those currently in service and those still under construction: ''[&hellip;] Arbitrator''|Gial Ackbar, Rebel Alliance document "RI 043-N1"|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
[[File:CrseihBlackHole-BountyHunters27.jpg|thumb|right|275px|A black hole (one pictured) evaporated the ''Arbitrator''.]]
The ''Arbitrator'' served in the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. At some point, Gial Ackbar wrote a classified document titled "RI 043-N1" that detailed the Empire's most critical ''Executor''-class Star Dreadnoughts&mdash;both those in service and under construction&mdash;which included the ''Arbitrator''. The document contained a technical specification of the class and was sent to Alliance [[Commander]] [[Mon Mothma]]. Rebel [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]] later included the list in ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', a compilation of information, reports, and accounts belonging to the Alliance, in the "Alliance Chron 3" section,<ref name="Rebel Files" /> spanning approximately between [[0 ABY]] and [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Rebel Files date">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' states that ''[[The Rebel Files]]''{{'}} "[[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance]] Chron 2" section, which mentions the ''Arbitrator'', spans 19&ndash;22 [[Imperial calendar|AFE]], or nineteen to twenty-two [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, the section spans approximately between [[0 ABY]] and [[3 ABY]].</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

Prior to the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Life Debt" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the ''Arbitrator'' was one of the Empire's thirteen in-service [[Super Star Destroyer]]s,<ref name="Life Debt" /> a title given to several classes of [[capital ship]]s.<ref name="Complete Locations">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> After the battle, which saw the Rebel Alliance defeat the Empire after [[Darth Sidious|the Emperor]] and the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] were destroyed, the Empire fractured, and the Rebel Alliance transitioned into the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Life Debt" /> Sometime after the battle, between 4 ABY and [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the ''Arbitrator'' was pursued by forces of the [[New Republic Defense Fleet|New Republic's fleet]]. In an attempt to escape, the ship made an erroneous [[hyperspace]] calculation, causing it to be sucked into a<ref name="Life Debt" /> [[black hole]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and evaporated.<ref name="Life Debt" />

In 5 ABY,<ref name="Date" /> after the vessel's destruction, Imperial [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] of the ''Executor''-class ''[[Ravager]]'' visited Coruscant to research [[Gallius Rax]], her mysterious superior. While at the Hall of Imperial Register, Sloane discovered that eleven of the thirteen [[dreadnought]]s originally in the Empire's service, including the ''Arbitrator'', had been destroyed, captured, or surrendered, with the only two remaining being the ''Ravager'' and the [[Eclipse (Executor-class)|''Eclipse'']].<ref name="Life Debt" /> ''The Rebel Files'' was subsequently discovered and annotated by the leadership of the [[Resistance]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> a military movement founded in [[28 ABY]] to oppose the [[First Order]], the successor of the Empire.<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Ushos O. Statura]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> an admiral in the Resistance,<ref name="TFA">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> noted on "RI 043-N1" that New Republic forces had chased the ''Arbitrator'' into a black hole.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Arbitrator'' was first mentioned in the [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', the second entry in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]'', written by [[Chuck Wendig]].<ref name="Life Debt" />
