The '''Canto Grand''' was a [[casino]] that operated in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Sometime before [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> two locals, a [[Rodian]] named [[Gedeek Obaz]] and a [[human]] named [[Kay Vess]], used a [[Freedom Spike|customized data spike]] to slice in and steal from the [[slot machine]]s at the Canto Grand.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Canto Grand was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
