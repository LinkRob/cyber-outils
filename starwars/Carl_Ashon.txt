'''Carl Ashon''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who served in the [[Galactic Empire]] as a garbage worker on the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Avenger]]''. He attempted to make a deal with the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]], but he was betrayed and locked in a damaged [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]] that was ejected into space along with the waste from the [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="FACPOV" />
