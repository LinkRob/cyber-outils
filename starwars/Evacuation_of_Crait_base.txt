The '''evacuation of Crait base''' occurred<ref name="Rebel Files" /> at some point before the [[Battle of Scarif]]<ref name="SWROUVG" /> when the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] evacuated their [[Crait outpost|base]] on the [[planet]] [[Crait]]. In a message to [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Heff Tobber]], who participated in the evacuation, noted that despite evacuating the base in a hurry, they left most of the facilities intact in the hopes of returning at a later date with heavy transport equipment.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
