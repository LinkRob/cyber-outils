'''Amira Ghazalla''' is an actress<ref name="Cast" /> who portrayed [[Goneril]], a [[First Order]] [[First Order officer|officer]], in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2017/12/star-wars-the-last-jedi-cameos-who-is-joseph-gordon-levitt-james-corden-mark-hamill-playing|text=32 Delightful ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Cameos You Might Have Missed|author=Robinson, Joanna|date=2017-12-17|work=[[Wikipedia:Vanity Fair (magazine)|''Vanity Fair'']]|quote=...'''Amira Ghazalla''' (Dothraki Crone) as Captain Canady's First Order Commander...|archivedate=20171217232113}}</ref> She is also notable for roles such as a [[W:c:gameofthrones:Dothraki crone|Dothraki crone]] in the ''[[Wikipedia:Game of Thrones|Game of Thrones]]'' episode "[[Wikipedia:A Golden Crown|A Golden Crown]]"<ref name="Cast" /> (where she acts opposite fellow ''Star Wars'' actress [[Emilia Clarke]]), [[W:c:Sense8:River El-Saadawi|River El-Saadawi]] in the ''[[Wikipedia:Sense8|Sense8]]''<ref name="Cast" /> episode "[[W:c:Sense8:Amor Vincit Omnia|Amor Vincit Omnia]]," and the recurring role of [[W:c:Carnival-Row:Mima Roosan|Mima Rosan]] in the neo-noir fantasy series ''[[Wikipedia:Carnival Row|Carnival Row]]''.
