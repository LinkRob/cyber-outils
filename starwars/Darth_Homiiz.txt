'''Darth Homiiz''' was a [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] with a rotund appearance, featuring [[Color/Legends|tan]] skin covered in [[Tattoo/Legends|tattoos]] ranging from tan to purple coloring. On Homiiz's face, there were blue markings between the [[Eye/Legends|eyes]], and an [[Eye patch/Legends|eye patch]] covering the [[Sith/Legends|Sith's]] right eye. Homizz also had a black [[Hair/Legends|beard]] and tail.<ref name="HOON" />

Homiiz carried a [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] and was clothed in a gray-brown [[Cloak/Legends|cloak]]. The cloak featured two red stripes below the waist and was worn open to expose the Sith Lord's torso. Golden [[Ring/Legends|rings]] were present on Homiiz's left hand and nose.<ref name="HOON" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Homiiz appeared in concept art on the website of [[HOON]], the illustrator of the [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] ''[[Jedi: Yoda]]'',<ref name="HOON" /> published on [[July 21]], [[2004]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54285/star_wars_jedi_-_yoda_2004_1|text=Star Wars: Jedi - Yoda (2004) #1}}</ref> In the concept art, Homiiz is standing next to [[Pix (Jedi)|Pix]], a [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] character from the comic. While Pix appeared in the comic, the Sith was cut from the title.<ref name="HOON" />
