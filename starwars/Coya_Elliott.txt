'''Coya Elliott''' is credited as an assistant voice editor for ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Mysteries of the Sith]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Rebel Assault II: The Hidden Empire]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Masters of Teräs Käsi]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]''. She was also a dialouge editor for [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (video game)|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'' video game]], a voice editor for ''[[Star Wars Episode I: The Gungan Frontier]]'', as well as the voice department manager for [[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (video game)|''Star Wars: Rogue Squadron'']].

Additionally, Elliott worked on [[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']], ''[[Star Wars: DroidWorks]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron 3D]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Force Commander]]'', [[Star Wars: Starfighter (video game)|''Star Wars: Starfighter'']], and ''[[Star Wars: Episode I Jedi Power Battles]]''.

Coya Elliott was the supervising assistant sound editor for [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] and the supervising assistant editor for [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].
