'''File JC-G052F9B38''' was a file claimed to be originated from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] [[Jedi Archives|Archives]]. Among its contents, it featured a recording of a supposed conversation between [[Jedi Master]]s [[Yoda]] and [[Shaak Ti]], in which Yoda insists [[Padawan]] [[youngling]]s fight in the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>  In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|7}}</ref> [[Bail Prestor Organa]] obtained this file, and doubted its veracity.<ref name="MoF" />
