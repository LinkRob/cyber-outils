'''Chasen Piian''' was a [[Politics|politician]] who served as [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Catalyst" /> Around [[1300 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Piian granted a piece of [[grass]] land on [[Coruscant]] to the sole surviving members of the [[B'ankora]] [[species]]. Though the land had originally been acquired by the Republic to only serve as a intermediate sanctuary for the B'ankora after [[B'ankora homeworld|their homeworld]] [[B'ankora homeworld cataclysm|had been destroyed by a cataclysmic collision]] with a [[meteor]], Piian granted the group the rights to use it in perpetuity so they could resume their sedentary lifestyle with humble homes, fields, and trails, with the land eventually becoming known as the [[B'ankor Refuge]]. Because of this action, Piian was often seen as benevolent.<ref name="Catalyst" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chasen Piian was fist mentioned in ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'', written by [[James Luceno]]<ref name="Catalyst" /> and published on [[November 15]], [[2016]]<ref name="Penguin-Hardcover">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/7879/catalyst-star-wars-by-james-luceno/9780345511492|text=Catalyst (Star Wars)}}</ref> as a prequel to the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Catalyst" />

The 2016 reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]], originally dated the B'ankor being granted the B'ankor Refuge, and as such the tenure of Chasen Piian as Supreme Chancellor, to [[131 BBY]],<ref name="Rebel Dossier date">According to ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'' the [[B'ankora]] were granted the [[B'ankor Refuge]] 112 [[Standard year|years]] before the end of [[Proclamation of the New Order|the end]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. As such it could be deduced that the B'ankor Refuge was established in [[131 BBY]].</ref> but in ''Catalyst'' it is stated that the B'ankora had lived in the B'ankor Refuge "for more than fifty generations" and had planted a copse of gargantuan [[tree]]s "centuries earlier" by the time of the novel.<ref name="Catalyst" /> [[2023]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' later instead placed the event to [[1300 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Because ''Timelines'' is the newer source and it's date lines up better with what is established in ''Catalyst'', this article assumes it to be correct.
