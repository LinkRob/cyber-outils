'''Ashcan Reach''' was a region of [[The galaxy|space]] that was raided by part of the [[Blazing Claw]]'s fleet under the instructions of the [[Pirate King|Pirate queen]] [[Gunda Mabin]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Ashcan Reach was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and released in [[2018]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
