The '''Delrakkin''' were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]] predatory [[reptile]]s<ref name="MOF" /> from the [[Delrakkin system]].<ref name="Planetnamia" /> They were covered in scales which could be dun in [[color]], and had claws. [[Senator]] [[Ta'am Khlaides]], a Delrakkin [[Gender|woman]], served in the the [[Imperial Senate]] in the early days of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="MOF" /> They originated from the [[Delrakkin system]].<ref name="Planetnamia" />
