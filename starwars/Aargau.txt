{{Quote|Oh, you mean ''Aargau'', home to the wealthiest crust of society? These people have every imaginable need taken care of, so what currency do you think they deal in? Secrets, of course.|[[Chelli Lona Aphra]], to [[Luke Skywalker]]|<ref name="Chaos Agent 2" />}}
'''Aargau''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Chaos Agent 2" /> in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Core Worlds]], in grid square L-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Beginner Game" /> that was headquarters of the [[Bank of Aargau]].<ref name="Uprising" /> Colossal [[seventh-stage bank-courier repulsor-truck]]s were used on Aargau.<ref name="CVNE">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> It was considered one of the financial centers of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="OSWM 121">{{GermanFanCite|121|Database}}</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="BOBF Date">{{BoBFDate}}</ref> Aargau was mentioned on [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] given to [[Boba Fett]].<ref name="Chapter 1">{{BOBF|Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Aargau was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', a reference book written by [[Tricia Barr|Patricia Barr]], [[Adam Bray]], [[Daniel Wallace]], and [[Ryder Windham]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> and released on [[April 28]], [[2015]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars release date">{{DK|url=us/book/9781465436016-ultimate-star-wars|text=Ultimate Star Wars|nolive=1}}</ref> It was originally created for [[Star Wars (1977) 48|''Star Wars'' (1977) 48]], the forty-eighth issue of [[Marvel Comics]]' earlier [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']] comic series,<ref name="The Third Law">[[Star Wars (1977) 48|''Star Wars'' (1977) 48]]</ref> which was first published on [[March 24]], [[1981]].<ref name="The Third Law release date">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53838/star_wars_1977_48|text=Star Wars (1977) #48}}</ref>
