'''Deckmaster''' was a title used by a member of the [[Rokana Raiders]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> named [[Offo]]. Offo oversaw the landing bay of the raider's base in the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Sayma Nebula]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Deckmaster was mentioned in a datapad entry in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', which released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirates Fortune">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune|text=Steal a Look at ''Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune'', Available May 15}}</ref>
