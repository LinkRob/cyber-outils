'''A4-J4''' was an [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]] [[Pilot|co-pilot]] aboard a [[Unidentified smuggler (Imperial-class Star Destroyer)|smuggler's]] [[YT-2400 light freighter]]. At one point, the [[Light freighter|starship]] was intercepted by a [[Galactic Empire]] [[Unidentified Imperial-class Star Destroyer (smuggler)|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]], with the [[smuggler]] and the astromech both being incarcerated in detention cells aboard the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|warship]]. After A4-J4's partner eventually broke out of their cell and also freed the [[droid]], she in turn helped by [[Slicer|slicing]] open the cargo bay [[Blast door|doors]] separating them from their impounded expensive and illegal cargo. Ultimately, A4-J4 and her fellow former detainees escaped from the [[Star Destroyer]] aboard their [[freighter]] and fled into [[hyperspace]].

==Biography==
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], A4-J4 was the [[droid]] [[Pilot|co-pilot]] of a [[Unidentified smuggler (Imperial-class Star Destroyer)|smuggler]] aboard their [[YT-2400 light freighter]]. The duo operated in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], dealing with danger and surprises on a [[Standard day|daily]] basis. At one point, while transporting expensive illegal cargo for<ref name="Unlock" /> the [[crime lord]]<ref name="Tatooine">{{Databank|tatooine|Tatooine}}</ref> [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]], the [[Smuggler|smuggling]] [[Light freighter|starship]] was captured by an [[Unidentified Imperial-class Star Destroyer (smuggler)|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] of the Empire. The [[freighter]] was impounded, its cargo confiscated, and the smuggler and A4-J4 were placed in different cells in the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer's]] detention center&mdash;with the [[astromech droid]] occupying cell 3.<ref name="Unlock" />

[[File:A4J4slicing-Unlock.jpg|thumb|right|300px|A4-J4 slicing into the cargo bay doors of the ''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]]
With the assistance of the [[techno-service droid]] [[Todo 515]] and the similarly detained smuggler [[Nuri Gleb]], A4-J4's partner eventually broke out of their cell. The smuggler heard the astromech's familiar beeps and chirps coming from inside her cell; however, the cell's door did not respond to the [[code cylinder]] the escapee had retrieved from their own cell's incapacitated [[stormtrooper]] guard, forcing them to search for another way to liberate the droid. Eventually, the smuggler navigated Todo 515 through the [[Star Destroyer]]'s [[Atmosphere|air]] duct system to A4-J4's cell. Working together, the two droids unlocked the room's door, and the astromech rejoined her partner with a series of happy beeps and whistles.<ref name="Unlock" />

The smuggler then tasked A4-J4 with [[Slicer|slicing]] into the control system of the sealed cargo bay [[Blast door|doors]] separating them from their confiscated cargo, which the astromech successfully did. Ultimately, A4-J4 and her companions all boarded the impounded YT-2400, which then broke out of the Star Destroyer's [[hangar]] bay. After evading pursuit by the [[warship]]'s [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], A4-J4's partner instructed her to prepare their [[Starship|ship]] for a jump into [[hyperspace]], which then allowed the craft to escape the Imperials. The light freighter subsequently traveled at high speed to<ref name="Unlock" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Tatooine" /> [[Tatooine]] in order to deliver its cargo to Jabba.<ref name="Unlock" />

==Characteristics==
A4-J4 was an [[R-series]] astromech droid manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]].<ref name="Astromech" /> She had [[Color|white]], orange, and black plating and a black [[photoreceptor]]. The droid was equipped with a [[scomp link]] that allowed her to slice into door systems.<ref name="Unlock" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:A4J4app-Unlock.png|thumb|right|250px|In one of the scenarios of ''Unlock!: Star Wars Escape Game'', a player uses the game's mobile app to reply to the other players' questions solely via using A4-J4's beeps and whistles.]]
A4-J4 appeared in "An Unforeseen Delay," a scenario of [[Space Cowboys]]' [[2020]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile app]]&ndash;driven card game ''[[Unlock!: Star Wars Escape Game]]''. The game's two illustrations of the astromech droid present an inconsistency in her appearance&mdash;one of them depicts her with scorch marks on her body plating, which are absent from the other image.<ref name="Unlock" />

In "An Unforeseen Delay," the players have to assist A4-J4 in her efforts to slice into the control system of the ''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer's cargo bay doors. This is realized game-mechanically via one player playing the part of the droid in the game's accompanying app and the other players asking the "droid" questions in order to determine the correct numerical code needed to progress the game.<ref name="Unlock" />

The code corresponds to a pictogram, with only the player controlling A4-J4 seeing the latter and only the other players having knowledge of the possible code options. Furthermore, the player controlling the droid can only use the app-provided three sets of beeps and whistles&mdash;corresponding to the answers "yes," "maybe," and "no"&mdash;to answer the others' questions. This article assumes that the exact specifics of A4-J4's slicing into the door system are purely game-mechanical.<ref name="Unlock" />
