'''Athex''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[spy]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Sometime prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]], Athex led a rebel intelligence [[Mission to Mustafar (Rebel Alliance)|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] with the [[droid]] [[K-2SO]]. Although the mission was ultimately a success, Athex was [[Death|killed]] by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Secrets of the Empire" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Athex was a character created for the virtual reality experience ''[[Star Wars: Secrets of the Empire]]'', in which he was voiced by ''[[Star Wars]]'' veteran [[Sam Witwer]]. Athex wore the armor of an [[Imperial Transport Pilot]],<ref name="Secrets of the Empire" /> which was first identified in the [[2018]] ''LEGO Star Wars Darth Vader's Castle 75251'' set.<ref name="LEGO">{{LEGOCite|num=75251|name=Darth Vader's Castle}}</ref>
