'''Cellia Moten''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[humanoid]] who operated out of her [[Moten estate|estate]] on the [[planet]] [[Dallow]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Sanctuary" />

==Personality and traits==
Raised by the values of the self-important Moten family, Cellia Moten was a cruel, ruthless and power hungry individual. To the public, she maintained the image of a coy woman. All her power and influence led her to develop a god complex, which contributed to her obsession with acquiring religious artifacts. While she would hire treasure hunters to acquire these items, Moten would end up double crossing, executing and burying them in the groves estate, claiming it was a service to the galaxy.<ref name="Sanctuary" />

Moten also fancied herself a close, personal friend of Emperor Sheev Palpatine, and would frequently refer to him by first name, throwing his name around Imperials as if it were to bend them to her will. Her behavior was similar to other Imperials seeking the Emperor's favor.<ref name="Sanctuary" />

However, deep down, Moten was a complete coward whenever she was thrust into situations where she was at a complete disadvantage and would frantically say anything to save herself. She would also be prone to child-like tantrums if she was ever so humiliated.<ref name="Sanctuary" />
