'''Amanda Noar''' is an English theatre actress who has also had occasional film roles. She played the dancer [[Jess/Legends|Jess]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], and also appeared in the unreleased short film ''[[Return of the Ewok]]''.
