The '''dwarf probe droid''', also known as an '''Imperial probe droid'''<ref name="Epic Battles" /> or '''Viper probot''', was a model of [[probe droid]] manufactured by [[Arakyd Industries]].<ref name="FB Cite" /> It was fitted with a pair of [[blaster]] barrels, which it could fire with deadly accuracy.<ref name="Epic Battles" />

==History==
After the rebel group known as the [[Spectres]] were responsible for a [[Raid near Jalath|raid]] near the town of [[Jalath]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]], dwarf probe droids were deployed to spy on the group and reported their movements to [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]]. One probe caught an image of the rebels near the [[Imperial Communications Center]], where [[Ezra Bridger]] disable the probe by summon a [[loth-cat]] to attack the probe. Despite being damaged, the droid still managed to transmit footage of the rebels to [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]].<ref name="Call to Action" />

Kallus later dispatched a probe to the planet [[Seelos]], in order to spy on the former [[Galactic Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s and the [[Ghost]] [[Spectres|crew]]. The probe was caught in the [[Phantom]] by [[Sabine Wren]] and manage to damage the [[engine]]s before [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] shot the probe with a [[DC-15A blaster rifle]].<ref name="The Lost Commanders">{{Rebels|The Lost Commanders}}</ref>

A probe was in the [[Concord Dawn system|Concord Dawn System]], after the [[Journeyman Protector]]s were wiped out. The probe spotted Bridger, Wren, and [[Fenn Rau]], but thanks to Bridger using [[the Force]] to restrain the probe, Wren manage to shoot the droid with her [[WESTAR-35 blaster pistol]] but not before transmitting it's information to [[Gar Saxon]].<ref name="Imperial Supercommandos">{{Rebels|Imperial Supercommandos}}</ref>

A [[Death trooper]], [[DT-L21]] deployed two probes down a sewer system at [[Old Jho's Pit Stop]] to chase after the Spectres. The Ghost crew manage to destroy both probes but ran into more as the Imperials used them to find them in the sewers.<ref name="The Occupation">{{Rebels|The Occupation}}</ref>

A probe was guarding the [[TIE/D Defender Elite]], when Wren infiltrated a base to steal the [[flight data recorder]]. After [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] activated the [[starfighter]]'s kill switch, several probes were sent searching the crash site after the Defender crashed.<ref name="Flight of the Defender">{{Rebels|Flight of the Defender}}</ref>
