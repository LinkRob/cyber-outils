'''Forsyth Wry''' was a [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the Jedi Master was part of [[Missions across the Stormwall|missions]] carried out by the [[Jedi]] and [[Republic Defense Coalition]] to cross the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Stormwall]] [[hyperspace]] barrier using the [[Sunvale device]] to rescue [[refugee]]s from the Nihil's [[Occlusion Zone]] region of [[Realspace|space]] and bring them to [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] space. For Wry's mission, they were sent to rescue five refugees alongside a [[Maz Kanata's pilot|pilot]] of<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Pirate Queen|pirate queen]]<ref name="Escape from Valo">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref> [[Maz Kanata]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

While [[transport]]ing the refugees back to Republic space, however, the Jedi Master, the [[pilot]], and the five refugees were all [[Death|killed]]. Before she undertook one such mission of her own<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> later that same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Jedi Master [[Avar Kriss]] mentally recalled Wry's death, noting that their mission had been the second failed one that had been undertaken.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

==Behind the scenes==
Forsyth Wry was mentioned in the [[2024]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref>
