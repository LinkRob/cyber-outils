The '''Adrathorpe system''' was located in [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="The Interior" /> It was situated in grid square H-11 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and lay on the [[Burke's Trailing]] [[smuggler]] [[hyperspace route]], which linked it to the [[Jakku system]]<ref name="Smuggler" /> of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Jedha system]]<ref name="Smuggler" /> of the [[Mid Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> At one point, the [[Pirate Queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] hid a treasure vault in the Adrathorpe [[Star system|system]]. She subsequently noted the system's location on a [[map]] included in the [[Book|logbook]] that would come to be known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Adrathorpe system was first mentioned in [[Daniel Wallace]]'s [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler" /> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> Although "Star Systems of the Galaxy" initially placed the Adrathorpe system in grid square I-11,<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[October 14]], 2025<ref name="Oct14">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-14}}</ref> update to the appendix changed its grid square to H-11.<ref name="Appendix" />

When Fry was asked about the possibility of the Adrathorpe system's name being meant to instead refer to<ref name="Email">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-09-15.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-09-15}}</ref> the Mid Rim<ref name="AppendixAdratharpe">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Adratharpe system]]</ref> celestial body [[Adratharpe 7]]<ref name="HC">{{HelmetCollectionCite|59|Helmets}}</ref>&mdash;which was also mentioned in ''Smuggler's Guide''<ref name="Smuggler" />&mdash;the author confirmed that it was a probable case of misspelling but that it was decided to list both Adrathorpe and the [[Adratharpe system]] in the appendix, further noting that the situation presented comic storytelling possibilities involving mixups by smugglers, referencing<ref name="Email" /> the humorous scene involving the names of the [[Alderaanian/Legends|Alderaanians]] and the [[Aldereenian]]s from [[Dave Wolverton]]'s [[1994]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity novel ''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]''.<ref name="Courtship">''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]''</ref>
