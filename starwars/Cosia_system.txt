The '''Cosia system''' was a [[star system]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Deep Core]],<ref name="DoH" /> within [[Cosian Space]].<ref name="USW" /> The system was home to a [[Cosia's star|single]] [[star]], as well as at least one [[planet]], the only habitable planet in the system, [[Cosia]]. Cosia was home to the [[Cosian]] [[species]] and had two [[moon]]s, [[Tabu]] and [[Tebu]], [[orbit]]ing it.<ref name="DoH" />
