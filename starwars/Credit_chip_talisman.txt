A '''credit chip talisman''' was a type of charm that consisted of a worn [[credit chip]] and was believed my [[smuggler]]s to bring luck while committing illicit activities.<ref name="Outlaws" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a group of [[windfisher]]s on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]] owned a number of charms, including a credit chip talisman. That [[Standard year|year]], the windfishers had a difficult season and were unable to pay off a group a [[pirate]]s with their usual [[aglis flyer]]s and instead the pirates took took all of their belongings, including their charms. Afterwards a one of the [[Unidentified windfisher (Stolen Charms)|windfishers]] began surveying the [[unidentified pirate camp (Mirogana Valley)|pirate's camp]] in [[Mirogana Valley]] from the balcony of [[Makal's Gambling Parlor]] in the [[city]] [[Mirogana]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Later that year, [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] approached the [[human]] windfisher on the balcony and asked what they were looking at. After hearing the story, Vess offered to sneak in and steal back the charms. Vess later recovered the charms from the pirate camp and returned to the windfisher who rewarded her with 150 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] as well as the credit chip talisman. <ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A credit chip talisman appeared as part of the "Stolen Charms" optional intel quest in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The item is used as a minor charm in the game and gives the player a chance find extra credits when looting a container. During the quest, if the player is spotted while recovering the charms, they will still get the credit chip talisman but will not received the reward money.<ref name="Outlaws" />
