'''''Bravo One''''' was a [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|UT-60D]] [[transport]]/[[gunship]] that served the [[Extraction Team Bravo]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Under the command of [[General]] [[Davits Draven]],<ref name="Battlefront VR" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="RO Date">{{FilmDate|RO}}</ref> the Bravo Team took a successful mission to [[Liberation of Erso|break out]] [[Jyn Erso]]<ref name="Battlefront VR" />&ndash;a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] criminal who would later become a key figure in the [[Battle of Scarif|theft]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[Death Star plans|plans]]&ndash;from a [[Imperial Detention Center & Labor Camp LEG-817|labor camp]] that was used by the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[planet]] [[Wobani]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

After a successful extraction on the planet surface, ''Bravo One'' encountered a minor technical setback and was unable to jump to [[hyperspace]]. The team took refuge in an [[asteroid field]] in the [[Wobani system]] and a priority transmission to all available Rebel vessels was sent by [[K-2SO]]<ref name="Battlefront VR" />&ndash;a [[KX-series security droid]] who formerly served the Empire.<ref name="Rogue One" />
