'''Fulcrum''' was a title used by former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] [[New Republic Era|during]] the [[New Republic]], and was used by [[agent]]s and [[Spy|spies]] early in the [[Galactic Civil War]], with the purpose to gather and distribute intelligence, and recruit new members to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel cause]].

"Fulcrum" was originally the name of a subspace communications frequency used by [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] during the [[Clone Wars]], and later proposed to reuse as a title by Ahsoka Tano during the [[Imperial Era]], who became a most notable Fulcrum [[agent]] during the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]]. In the [[Standard year|years]] leading up to the [[Battle of Yavin]], former [[Imperial Security Bureau]] agent [[Alexsandr Kallus]] became another Fulcrum after becoming disillusioned with the [[Galactic Empire]] and joining the Rebel Alliance, while [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] was a Fulcrum recruitment agent in the [[Albarrio sector]].

==Description==
{{Quote|You want to recruit people?''"<br />"''Not exactly. Though if I found good people, I would certainly try to bring them in. I want to take your recruits and find missions for them. I want to be the one who listens to what people need, who finds out what people can do and then helps them do it.|Bail Organa and Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="Ahsoka" />}}
The title of Fulcrum was used by [[Spy|spies]] in the rebel network. Their purpose was to gather and distribute intelligence, and recruit new members to the rebel cause. Fulcrums were supposed to keep their identities as secret as possible, and to that aim, usually modulated their voices on their transmissions.<ref name="OOD">{{Rebels|Out of Darkness}}</ref> They also used avatars when speaking via [[hologram]].<ref name="Rebel Resolve">{{Rebels|Rebel Resolve}}</ref> The most common avatar was that of the Fulcrum symbol,<ref name="TAE" /><ref name="By Whatever Sun">{{StoryCite|story=By Whatever Sun|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> a white design based on [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s forehead markings. As the title's creator, she was also known to use an avatar of a cloaked figure.<ref name="Rebel Resolve" />

Fulcrums were both [[agent]]s and [[commander]]s, as they investigated Imperial activity and gave missions to their subordinates, they also directed forces and oversaw associated cells. The use of Fulcrum as a single codename allowed for the confusion of Imperial intelligence operations if an individual agent was uncovered.<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref>

==History==
===Origins===
{{Quote|What am I going to call you, if I can't call you Ahsoka? You'll need a code name at the least, so you can deal with other operatives.''"<br />"''Fulcrum. You can call me Fulcrum.''"<br />"''Then welcome to the Rebellion.|Bail Organa and Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="Ahsoka" />}}
[[File:Fulcrumisborn-DoR.png|thumb|left|200px|Ahsoka Tano shows Bail Organa a hologram of the Fulcrum symbol.]]
"Fulcrum" was originally the name of a subspace communications frequency used by [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Among those aware of the frequency were Skywalker's former [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]], [[Onderonian]] freedom fighter [[Saw Gerrera]], and Skywalker's former [[Padawan]] Ahsoka Tano. When Tano and [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] used the frequency to contact Skywalker and Kenobi during the [[Battle of Yerbana]], Skywalker initially assumed that Gerrera was the one attempting to get in touch, regarding the ongoing [[Siege at Onderon|siege]] at [[Onderon]].<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" />

Following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Tano, having survived [[Order 66]], took over [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa's]] [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|intelligence network]], which was part of the early rebel network. [[Gender|She]] adopted the codename Fulcrum,<ref name="Ahsoka" /> which later became the standard alias for other rebel operatives within the network.<ref name="TAE" /> By [[9 BBY]], Tano and the other Fulcrums were using a symbol derived from her forehead markings. Most people did not know the significance of the symbol, but [[rebel pilot]] [[Miara Larte]], who had once been [[Raada Uprising|rescued]] by Tano, did. She knew to keep the secret, however, even from her sister [[Kaeden Larte|Kaeden]], who had also known Tano.<ref name="By Whatever Sun" />

===The Spectres===
{{Quote|Fulcrum.''"<br />"''Ahsoka. My name is Ahsoka Tano.|Ahsoka Tano introduces herself to the [[Spectres]]|<ref name="FATG">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref>}}
By [[5 BBY]], the [[Spectres]] rebel cell leader [[Hera Syndulla]] kept in touch with Fulcrum, who supplied her cell with information about Imperial targets.<ref name="OOD" /> While Syndulla was somewhat privy to Fulcrum's true identity, although not her real name,<ref name="FATG" /> she kept this information a secret from the rest of her team members, including the [[Mandalorian]] weapons expert [[Sabine Wren]]. Following an [[attack on an Imperial convoy]], Wren demanded to know Fulcrum's identity and insisted on accompanying Syndulla on a supply mission to [[Fort Anaxes]] on the [[asteroid]] [[PM-1203]].<ref name="OOD" />

[[File:ChopperCallsFulcrum-FAtG.png|thumb|right|250px|Chopper contacting "Fulcrum"]]
After the Spectres [[Rescue of Tseebo|rescued]] the Imperial defector [[Tseebo]] from [[Lothal]] in [[4 BBY]], Syndulla arranged for him to be spirited away into hiding aboard one of [[Ahsoka Tano's corvette|Fulcrum's]] [[CR90 corvette]]s.<ref name="Gathering Forces">{{Rebels|Gathering Forces}}</ref> Following the [[Raid on the communications tower|capture]] of the Spectres rebel and Jedi Padawan [[Kanan Jarrus]],<ref name="Call to Action">{{Rebels|Call to Action}}</ref> Fulcrum contacted Syndulla by hologram and counseled her not to rescue Jarrus in order to avoid exposing the rebellion's existence to the Empire, warning her that further notice from the Empire could put the Spectres — including the Jedi apprentice [[Ezra Bridger]] — in greater danger.<ref name="Rebel Resolve" /> However, despite these urgings, the Spectres persisted with their [[Action at Mustafar|rescue mission]], destroying [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s [[Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Sovereign]]'', in the process. After the Spectres' [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] contacted her, Fulcrum dispatched members of [[Phoenix Cell]] to rescue the rebels. It was also at this point that she finally chose to reveal herself to the rest of Syndulla's team, including her real name.<ref name="FATG" />

===Building a rebellion===
Although Tano was presumed [[Death|dead]] after her [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] with [[Darth]] Vader on [[Malachor]],<ref name="TOTA">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref> her codename survived and was still in use by several other rebel [[Spy|spies]] and operatives, including the disillusioned [[Imperial Security Bureau]] Agent [[Alexsandr Kallus]]<ref name="An Inside Man" /> and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] recruiting agent [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> Another rebel recruiting agent, former [[Partisans|Partisan]] [[Xosad Hozem]], knew of the Fulcrum agents, and upon encountering fellow ex-Partisan [[Jyn Erso]], attempted to discern if she was aware of them. As Erso had never heard of the Fulcrums, she did not know what he was talking about.<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref>

Between [[2 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]], Cassian Andor used the Fulcrum symbol when transmitting information to and later receiving information from agents sent on the [[Mission to Mustafar (Rebel Alliance)|Mission to Mustafar]].<ref name="Star Wars: Secrets of the Empire">''[[Star Wars: Secrets of the Empire]]''</ref>

[[File:KallusIsFulcrum-TAA.png|thumb|left|250px|Fulcrum helps Sabine extract defectors from Skystrike Academy.]]
In 2 BBY, Kallus used his Fulcrum pseudonym to inform Phoenix [[Squadron]] about the presence of [[Imperial cadet]]s seeking to defect to the rebellion at [[Skystrike Academy]]. As a result, Wren mounted a [[Escape from Skystrike Academy|rescue mission]] and managed to recruit the [[starfighter]] [[pilot]]s [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Derek Klivian]]. During the mission, Kallus actively helped the trio escape from the [[Imperial Academy|academy]] by telling them which levels to avoid and which [[hangar]] bay they could get a [[Starship|ship]] from. When Wren, not suspecting that he was their new Fulcrum, demanded to know why they should trust him, he simply told her to "Tell [[Garazeb Orrelios]], we're even."<ref name="TAE" /> Orrelios, a member of the Spectres, had unwittingly gotten Kallus to reconsider his allegiance to the Empire after saving his life during an incident with an [[escape pod]] on the [[Geonosian]] [[moon]] [[Bahryn]].<ref name="The Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref>

Kallus later tipped off Phoenix Squadron that the Empire was planning to place the [[planet]] [[Mykapo]] under martial law, prompting the rebels, including the Spectres, to go there to evacuate all of the rebels and rebel sympathizers.<ref name="Iron Squadron">{{Rebels|Iron Squadron (episode)}}</ref> Some [[time]] after, when Bridger, Jarrus, and Chopper undertook an [[Infiltration of the Imperial Armory Complex|undercover mission]] to infiltrate and steal plans for [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter|TIE Defender]] initiative, the rebels were aided by Kallus. He identified himself as a Fulcrum operative using the code phrase "by the light of Lothal's moons," and helped the rebels escape with the plans. After studying the rebel infiltration, Thrawn realized that the Empire had a spy within their ranks who was leaking information to the rebels. Due to their many past encounters with Kallus, most of the Spectres were skeptical that his change of allegiance was genuine.<ref name="An Inside Man" />

[[File:FulcrumInfiltratorIntel-Warhead.png|thumb|right|250px|Fulcrum warns Chopper Base about Thrawn's infiltrator droids.]]
Kallus later used his Fulcrum pseudonym to aid the Phoenix rebels by leaking intelligence about Thrawn's [[E-XD-series infiltrator droid]]s. Using this information, the rebels Orrelios, Chopper, and [[AP-5]] overpowered [[EXD-9]] before it could reveal [[Chopper Base]]'s location to the Empire. They then reprogrammed the [[droid]] into an improvised [[Proton warhead|warhead]] and used it to destroy a Star Destroyer. Fulcrum later contacted the Spectres to thank them for their creativity in tackling the infiltrator droids. However, Thrawn was able to use this incident to narrow down his search for Phoenix Squadron's base to 94 [[Star system|systems]].<ref name="Warhead">{{Rebels|Warhead (episode)}}</ref>

Afterwards, Thrawn and [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] intercepted one of Kallus' Fulcrum transmissions and realized that Fulcrum was the code name of the mole in the [[Lothal sector]] command staff. They monitored his next transmission to learn his identity. Because only a highly-ranked Imperial could access the classified information Kallus sent in the transmission, they realized that the Fulcrum in the Lothal [[sector]] was not only a spy for the rebel network, but also a traitor to the Empire as well, and solicited the help of the head of the ISB [[Colonel]] [[Wullf Yularen]] in hunting down and identifying Fulcrum by screening staff from [[Imperial Sector Command|Sector Command]]. Thrawn and Yularen believed that the spy was an [[Imperial officer]] who was highly placed enough to have had access to classified information, and low enough to send his transmissions to the rebel network without the Empire's knowledge. After learning that Thrawn was monitoring Fulcrum's transmissions, the Spectres mounted a [[Rescue of Alexsandr Kallus|mission]] to rescue Kallus. During the course of the mission, the rebels also deleted [[Atollon]] from Thrawn's star chart. Kallus managed to stall the investigation by implicating [[Lieutenant]] [[Yogar Lyste]] as Fulcrum. However, Thrawn privately deduced that Kallus was actually Fulcrum because he had concealed the arrest of Bridger. The Grand Admiral decided to keep this secret, as he felt that Kallus would be more useful as a spy than he ever was when he was still loyal.<ref name="TIE">{{Rebels|Through Imperial Eyes}}</ref>

On the eve of Phoenix Cell's long-planned attack on Lothal's Imperial [[Factory|factories]], Thrawn arrived on the planet to warn Pryce of the impending attack. Kallus, who was not invited to the meeting, used a [[MSE-6 series repair droid|mouse droid]] to spy on Thrawn from the ventilation system. At the meeting, Thrawn informed Pryce, [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]] and Tarkin, who was attending by hologram, of the impending attack and that Phoenix Squadron was being reinforced by the [[Massassi Group|fleet]] of [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]]. Via the [[mouse]] droid, Kallus overheard everything, and set out for an [[LothalNet comm tower E-272|old communications tower]] outside of [[Capital City|Lothal City]]. When he tried to transmit a message, he found that his signal was being blocked, and was shortly ambushed by Thrawn. Kallus managed to destroy the Grand Admiral's jammer, letting the partial message be sent, before Thrawn and his [[death trooper]]s arrested him. Thrawn explained to Kallus that the rogue agent's message was the final piece of the puzzle he needed to locate the rebel base, as its trajectory intersected with that of Dodonna's [[fleet]]. The art of the ancient people of the Lothal sector showed a star system at those coordinates, which was not on Imperial charts.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

[[File:KallusCaptured-ZHp1.png|thumb|left|250px|Kallus was held captive by Thrawn during the assault on Atollon.]]
At Chopper Base on Atollon, the rebel commanders received Fulcrum's warning in enough time for them to place all personnel on alert before the arrival of the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]]. During the [[Battle of Atollon|battle]] that followed, Kallus was held [[prisoner]] on the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the ''[[Chimaera]]'' and forced to watch. After the rebels were forced to retreat back to the planet, and the [[orbital bombardment]] that followed, Thrawn left Pryce in charge of the [[Imperial Navy|fleet]] while he led a ground assault. One of the rebel ships, Bridger's [[Nightbrother (Gauntlet fighter)|''Gauntlet'']], had escaped since [[Jun Sato]] ram his [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|cruiser-carrier]] command ship the ''[[Phoenix Nest]]'' into the [[Kassius Konstantine's Interdictor|first Interdictor]] where Admiral Konstantine commands, and returned with [[Clan Wren|reinforcements]] to destroy the Imperial fleet's remaining [[Interdictor-class heavy cruiser|Interdictor]], the other having been destroyed during the first stage of the battle. As the remaining rebel ships raced towards the blockade, Kallus took advantage of Pryce's reaction to the [[Constrainer|second]] [[Interdictor]]'s destruction to taunt her about the mess she was making of Thrawn's fleet, causing her to order him thrown out of the [[airlock]]. He neutralized his [[stormtrooper]] guards in the [[turbolift]] and escaped the ''Chimaera'' in an escape pod, transmitting his coordinates to the ''[[Ghost]]'', which picked him up before the remaining rebels escaped into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Zero Hour" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fulcrum title first appeared in "[[Out of Darkness]]," a [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first-season]] episode of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="OOD" /> Ahsoka Tano was not revealed to be the person behind it until the first season finale, "[[Fire Across the Galaxy]]."<ref name="FATG" /> The novel [[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']] revealed the circumstances under which she created the title,<ref name="Ahsoka" /> while [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars'']] [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|season 7]] episode "[[Old Friends Not Forgotten]]" revealed Tano's inspiration for the title.<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> The [[Star Wars Rebels Season Three|third season]] episode "[[The Antilles Extraction]]" later revealed that the title had come to be used by multiple agents.<ref name="TAE" />
