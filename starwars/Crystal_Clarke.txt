'''Crystal Clarke''' is an American actress who played [[Pammich Nerro Goode]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. She reprised the role in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].

In 2022, she appeared in Sam Mendes' ''City of Light'' alongside [[Tanya Moodie]].
