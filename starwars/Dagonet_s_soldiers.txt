The [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Dagonet]] commanded his own personal unit of '''soldiers''', whom he utilized when his [[Uriel Dagonet|son]] was kidnapped by the citizens he represented in the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]].

==History==
During the [[Mission to rescue Dagonet's son]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] were discussing with [[Uriel Dagonet|Dagonet's Son]] and the villagers who kidnapped him. The [[Senator]]'s son said he'd been treated well and sympathized with the plight of the people, claiming he did not know of their hardship beforehand as he was usually on [[Coruscant]] or in<ref name="Justice" /> the [[planet]] [[Wayyl]]'s<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> capital. The discussion is interrupted by word that [[Dagonet]] had come in person, with<ref name="Justice" /> a [[garrison]]<ref name="Insider230">{{InsiderCite|230|Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi Companion|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'' Companion}}</ref> of his [[soldier]]s, despite Dooku insisting that he had no prior knowledge of their visit.<ref name="Justice" />

The villagers armed themselves and left the barn with the Jedi. Dooku reasoned with Dagonet to stand down, noting that his people had good reason to be angry with him. The Senator refused however and ordered his soldiers to fire upon the kidnappers. The Jedi defended the villagers, but several were struck down by the soldiers. As the [[death]]s mounted, Dagonet ordered his soldiers to hold their fire and spoke to the Jedi, gloating in victory. In response, Dooku [[Force choke]]d the Senator, throwing Jinn away with [[the Force]] when he tried to stop him. As a result, the soldiers resumed fire upon Dooku and their remaining ranks were also pushed away. Jinn went back into the barn to free Dagonet's son from his [[Stuncuffs|binders]], telling him to save his father, now defenceless.<ref name="Justice" />

The son's intervention lead Dooku to release the Senator, as the son expressed disappointment in his father. After the skirmish, as father and son made preparations to leave the [[Wayyl village|village]] by soldier-chauffeured [[skiff]], many of the soldiers stayed to help repair the damage.<ref name="Justice" />

==Equipment==
The soldiers wore a [[Color|blue]] uniform with gold trim and wielded [[blaster rifle]]s, with sights and a shoulder [[stock]]. They wore [[helmet]]s which had three sections, each protecting different parts of the head: the back of the neck, the chin and mouth, and the top of the head. They also wore [[goggles]] as [[eye]] protection. Some soldiers preferred to fight without the added [[eye]] protection, while some fought with the [[armor]] in its entirety, concealing their whole face and head.<ref name="Justice" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dagonet's soldiers first appeared in the episode "[[Justice (episode)|Justice]]" of the series [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (television series)|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'']] released in October 26, 2022.
