'''G-2''', also called '''Xiri's starfighter''' or just '''Xiri's fighter''', was a [[Forward ReCon fighter]] [[starfighter]] that [[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]] and her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Phan-tu Zenn]] discovered in a [[hangar]] north of the [[Path compound]] on the [[planet]] [[Dalna]] during the [[Night of Sorrow]]<ref name="Cataclysm" /> in [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> After using it to fight in the battle, they fled Dalna in it to try and contact their planets, [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]], for help.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
