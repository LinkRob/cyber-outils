'''Emily O'Brien''' is an actress who voiced [[Scout Three]] in ''[[Rogue One: Recon A Star Wars 360 Experience]]''. Additionally Emily narrated the [[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance (audiobook)|audiobook]] for ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''.<ref name="Path of Vengeance(Amazon)">{{Amazon|url=Star-Wars-Join-Resistance-Books/dp/B07HY6SL1F|text=Star Wars Join the Resistance: Books 1-3: The Star Wars Join the Resistance Series, Books 1-3|archivedate=20201114201332}}</ref>
