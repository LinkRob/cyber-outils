'''Force wielders''',<ref name="Overlords" /> or '''Force-wielders''', were an enigmatic [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="UltimateSW" /> of highly [[Force-sensitive]] beings with [[Changeling|the power to alter their forms]].<ref name="Overlords" /> The Force wielders were known for their affinity to [[the Force]] and it was believed they were [[Deity|god]]-like beings.<ref name="DB Mortis">{{Databank|mortis|Mortis}}</ref>

A family of Force wielders, collectively known as [[the Ones]]<ref name="UltimateSW" /> and the Mortis gods,<ref name="Wolves and a Door">{{Rebels|Wolves and a Door}}</ref> took sanctuary on the [[Planet|realm-world]] of [[Mortis]],<ref name="Overlords" /> which some believed was where the Force itself originated from,<ref name="UltimateSW" /> where they lived as anchorites. The three familial beings&mdash;the [[Father (Mortis)|Father]], the [[Daughter]], and the [[Son]]&mdash;withdrew from the temporal realm when their power threatened to disrupt [[the galaxy]]. The Father acted as the balance between the Daughter and Son, who embodied the [[Light side of the Force|light]] and [[Dark side of the Force|dark sides]] of the Force, respectively.<ref name="Overlords" /> The siblings were [[Immortality|immortal]],<ref name="UltimateSW" /> although only as long as the Father remained alive.<ref name="Ghosts" />

Despite little knowledge remaining of the existence of the powerful Force wielder beings, the Father believed that the [[Sith]] sought to exploit his family's power for their own benefit.<ref name="Overlords" /> The Father also believed that if the Son, who had fully given into the dark side during [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s [[mission to Mortis]],<ref name="Altar" /> was allowed to leave Mortis, he would bring doom across the [[galaxy]] and the [[universe]] itself.<ref name="Ghosts" />

==Biology and appearance==
The Force wielders were a powerful<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> highly intelligent [[species]] that was so in tuned with [[the Force]] that some believed them to be [[god]]s. They had [[Changeling|no fixed form]] and could manifest themselves as any species they chose. They were from the [[planet]] [[Mortis]], which was rumored to be the origin of the Force.<ref name="AA">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:Force_Gods_of_Mortis.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A painting of the Daughter, the Father and the Son at Lothal's Jedi Temple.]]
[[The Ones|A family]] of Force wielders known as the [[Father (Mortis)|Father]], the [[Son]] and the [[Daughter]] appeared throughout the [[Jedi Order]]'s recorded history with the family depicted on a mural of at least [[Lothal Jedi Temple|one]] Jedi Temple.<ref name="A World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref>

Prior to the [[Clone Wars]], the Father realized that he was [[Death|dying]]. When the Father learned that the [[Chosen One]] had been found, he knew that only the Chosen One could maintain the balance between his [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]-aligned Son<ref name="DB Son" /> and [[Light side of the Force|light side]]-aligned Daughter.<ref name="DB Daughter" /> Thus in the [[20 BBY|second year]] of the Clone Wars,<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> a transmission was sent out using a 2,000 year old Jedi [[Distress signal|distress code]] and was received by [[Admiral]] [[Nils Tenant]] in the [[Chrelythiumn system]]. The [[Jedi High Council]] then sent [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and his [[Padawan|apprentice]], [[Ahsoka Tano]] to investigate; upon their arrival, they were pulled towards the mysterious [[Mortis monolith]]. This transported them to the [[planet]] of [[Mortis]] where they met the Ones. Skywalker was separated from Kenobi and Tano who attempted to return to [[Jedi shuttle 634|their]] [[Eta-class shuttle|shuttle]]. Upon seeing its disappearance, Tano and Kenobi met the Son, whom they believed was Sith. The two of them took shelter cave where Kenobi was visited by an apparition of his late master, [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], who told them that the Ones, like him, believed Anakin to be the Chosen One. Tano was visited by a future apparition of herself, who warned her that she would be in danger if she continued to be Skywalker's apprentice.<ref name="Overlords" />

[[File:Father_Anakin_in_Arena.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Father tests Anakin Skywalker to see if he truly is the Chosen One.]]
There, Skywalker met with the Father at [[Mortis monastery|his monastery]]. Skywalker was then confronted by the Son, who had taken on the form of Skywalker's late mother, [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|Shmi]]. After Skywalker saw through the deception, he confronted the Father, who demonstrated his powers by handling the end of Skywalker's [[Skywalker lightsaber|lightsaber]]. The Father then told Skywalker of his belief that the Chosen One was him. The Father then decided to test him by having the Son and Daughter kill Kenobi and Ahsoka. Skywalker then proved himself the Chosen by controlling the Son and Daughter and force them to let Kenobi and Ahsoka go. The Father then told Skywalker that he was dying and that he must replace him. However, Skywalker refused and attempted to leave Mortis.<ref name="Overlords" />

The Son attempted to convince Skywalker to join him, but he refused. The Son was able to board the shuttle and take Tano to his [[Son's cathedral|cathedral]]. There, the Son corrupted Tano into giving into the dark side. The Son visited his father, where his father disproved of him giving into the dark side. The Son, in anger, attacked his Father. The Daughter placed the Father in a bed to recuperate. Knowing that her brother was growing more powerful, she agreed to help the Jedi defeat her brother. The Daughter showed Kenobi an [[Altar of Mortis|Altar]] where a powerful [[Dagger of Mortis|Dagger]] was kept. Kenobi took it.<ref name="Altar" />

[[File:TheOnesDaughterDeath-AOM.png|thumb|left|250px|The Daughter sacrifices herself in order to save the Father.]]
At the Son's cathedral, Skywalker attempted to rescue his apprentice, but was forced to fight. Kenobi and the Daughter arrived and attempted to stop the Son giving in to the dark side. As Kenobi assisted Skywalker in fighting Tano, the Son and Daughter fought each other. The father sensed this and traveled to the cathedral to end their fight. Kenobi attempted to give Skywalker the dagger, but Tano took it and gave it to the son. With no further use for her, the Son ended Tano's life and attempted to kill his Father. At the last moment, the Daughter stepped between them and was killed by the Son with the dagger. The Son left in anguish and the Father mourned for his Daughter. Skywalker begged for the Father's help in saving his apprentice. The Daughter allowed her father to transfer her life essence to Tano. Using Skywalker as a conduit, the Daughter was able to resurrect Tano before she died. The father urged them to leave before the Son could use their shuttle to escape.<ref name="Altar" />

[[File:NowISlayMeDownToSleep-GOM.png|thumb|right|250px|The Father stabbing himself]]
After the Father buried his Daughter, Skywalker visited him. Skywalker offered to assist the Father in dealing with the son. Skywalker, after speaking with an apparition of Master Jinn, ventured to the [[Well of the Dark Side]] to confront the Son. However, the Son was able to convince Skywalker to join him, by showing him his future. Upon seeing these terrible visions, Skywalker abandoned Kenobi and decided to join the Son, and attempted to take the shuttle. However, Tano prevented them from escaping. The Son, knowing Kenobi would seek out the father, decided to retrieve the dagger from his [[Daughter's grave|sister's grave]]. The Father, despite the pain Skywalker caused, erased Skywalker's memories of his future visions. Skywalker and the father were joined by Kenobi and Tano at the monastery. As the Son arrived, the Father took the dagger and stabbed himself. Shaken by remorse, Son embraces and makes peace with his father, just before he was stabbed by Anakin Skywalker with his lightsaber. As the Father died, he proclaimed Skywalker as the Chosen One, but warned him to be aware of his heart. The Father then vanished and the planet disappeared.<ref name="Ghosts" />

[[File:HydanAndMortisGodsPainting-SWRS04E13.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Minister Veris Hydan seeing a picture of the Mortis gods.]]
In 1 BBY, following [[Sith Lord]] Darth Vader [[Fifth Brother|and]] [[Seventh Sister|his]] [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]]' discovery of a Jedi Temple on [[Lothal]], the [[Mining Guild]] and Imperial forces under [[Minister]] [[Veris Hydan]] discovered a [[Painting of the Mortis gods|Painting]] on the Temple's wall. Hydan identified the figures as the Father, Son, and Daughter. The painting was able to open a [[portal]] to the [[World Between Worlds]]. When Rebel [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Ezra Bridger]] attempted to open the portal with Sabine Wren's help, the painting moved in which the daughter spoke to him and opened the portal. The Daughter and Father pointed towards the portal. As they pointed to the portal, [[Loth-wolf|Loth-wolves]] at the base of the painting moved to guide to the portal opening. Ezra used the portal to enter and while there he saved Tano from being killed by Darth Vader by pulling her into the World Between Worlds.<ref name="Wolves and a Door" /> The [[Sith]] [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]] [[Mission to the Lothal Jedi Temple|tried to enter]] World Between Worlds, but fails. Ezra returns and exits the portal to Lothal while Tano moves in another direction. Ezra then sealed the portal using the figure of the son to do so. The painting moved in which the Son spoke to him and closed the portal. The Father's hand moved away from the portal, the Son's right fist on his left hand while looking at the Father. The figures of all three begin moving down and the temple begins to collapse. The Loth-wolves move back to the painting and the Father claps his hands and the temple collapses completely.<ref name="A World Between Worlds" />

In [[9 ABY]], the former [[Jedi General]] and a [[mercenary]] [[Baylan Skoll]] traveled to the [[Monument to the Ones|statues]] of the Force wielders in [[Peridea]].<ref name="Part Eight">{{Ahsoka|Part Eight: The Jedi, the Witch, and the Warlord}}</ref>
