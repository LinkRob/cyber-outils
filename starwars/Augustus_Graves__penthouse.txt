A '''penthouse''' was owned by [[Augustus Graves]], the [[Chief Executive Officer|CEO]] of the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]], in the [[Pearl District]] of [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. The penthouse contained a [[Locris 1-42-08]] safe in the master bedroom.<ref name="Han Chewie 1" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] stole a [[Datacard|passcard]] from Graves in order to [[Heist on Corellia|break into]] the penthouse and recover an [[Unidentified urn|urn]] that contained the ashes of [[Krestrel D'Naran]], a rival of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]. When Solo reached the bedroom, he discovered the safe and was forced to leave in order to come up with a plan to break into the safe.<ref name="Han Chewie 1" />
