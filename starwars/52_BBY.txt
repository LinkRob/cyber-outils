The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''52 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3225 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''33 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7925''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] elected to [[Senator|represent]] [[Naboo]] in the [[Galactic Senate]], being aided by the influence of his Sith Master [[Darth Plagueis]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Births==
*[[Maneeli Tuun]] on [[Polis Massa]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Maneeli Tuun">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[First Order]]'s [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of [[Batuu]] to [[34 ABY]]. {{DatapadCite|Flight Crews Wanted|FlightCrewsWanted-IDIntro.png|WazellmanProfile-GEDatapad.jpg}} states that [[Maneeli Tuun]] is age 86 during the occupation, meaning he was [[Reproduction|born]] around 52 BBY.</ref>
*[[Rokura]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Rokura">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] to [[22 BBY]]. The novel establishes that [[Rokura]] is thirty [[Standard year|years]] old, therefore placing her [[Reproduction|birth]] around 52 BBY.</ref>
*[[Orson Callan Krennic]] on [[Lexrul]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Krennic">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' dates [[Orson Callan Krennic]]'s [[Reproduction|birth]] to 51 [[Standard year|years]] before the the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the the events of ''Rogue One'' to [[1 BBY]], the event must have taken place around 52 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref><ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
