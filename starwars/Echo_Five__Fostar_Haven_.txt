"'''Echo Five'''" was a [[pilot]] who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. After the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]], they served under the [[callsign]] [[Echo Five (Echo Squadron)|Echo Five]] for [[Echo Squadron]] and participated in a [[Battle at Fostar Haven|battle]] at [[Fostar Haven]], flying an [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]]. During the initial stages of the battle, they were killed when Echo Squadron had to fly under the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|''Vigilance'']] to get to the stranded [[Alderaanian refugee convoy]].<ref name="Squadrons" />
