'''Amand''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who was the [[king]] of the [[planet]] [[Molos]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], King Amand led an [[uprising on Molos]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Molos garrison|garrison]], and joined with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. In response, the Alliance dispatched [[Ral Garas]] and [[Sergeant]] [[Trask (Rebel)|Trask]] to advise the king, while bolstering [[Molos militia|his forces]], creating an [[Molos army|army]]. After debating what strategy to use when the Empire brought reinforcements to reconquer the world, King Amand devised a new strategy, and led his forces from the front during the [[Battle of Molopolis]]. The battle resulted in an Imperial defeat, which drove them away from the world.<ref name="Lead" />
