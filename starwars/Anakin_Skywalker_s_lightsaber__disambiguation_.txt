[[Anakin Skywalker]] is known to have used several [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsabers]] over his career as both a [[Jedi Knight]] and [[Sith Lord]]:

==[[Canon]]==
*'''[[Anakin Skywalker's first lightsaber]]''', used prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis]].
*The '''[[Skywalker lightsaber]]''', used after the First Battle of Geonosis and inherited by [[Luke Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan|apprentice]], [[Rey Skywalker]].
*'''[[Kirak Infil'a's lightsaber|Darth Vader's first red lightsaber]]''', a green lightsaber stolen from [[Kirak Infil'a]].
*'''[[Darth Vader's lightsaber]]''', used after the [[Clone Wars]] and during the [[Imperial Era]].

==[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]]==
*'''[[Anakin Skywalker's first lightsaber/Legends|Anakin Skywalker's first lightsaber]]''', used prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis/Legends|First Battle of Geonosis]].
*'''[[Anakin Skywalker's second lightsaber/Legends|Anakin Skywalker's second lightsaber]]''', used after the First Battle of Geonosis and inherited by his son [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and his later his daughter-in-law [[Mara Jade Skywalker]].
*'''[[Darth Vader's lightsaber/Legends|Darth Vader's lightsaber]]''', used after the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] and during the [[Imperial Era/Legends|Imperial Period]].
