A '''Cholganna survivalist belt''' was a type of highly resistant [[belt]] that was made on<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Nexu GoC">{{GoC|Nexu}}</ref> [[Cholganna]]. The [[Color|black]] belt connected in the front by a gold&ndash;colored buckle with a [[holster]] that could carry a [[blaster pistol]] attached to its right side. The belt featured space to store [[blaster module]] on its front and [[bacta vial]]s on its back along side an attached pouch.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a Cholganna survivalist belt was for sale at [[Wesla's]], a shore on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Kijimi]], that was operated by the [[Twi'lek]] [[tailor]] [[Lani Wesla]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] purchased a Cholganna survivalist belt from Wesla for 1750 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
An Cholganna survivalist belt appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The belt allows the player to carry an additional [[Bacta|Bacta Vial]] when equipped as well as fully restoring the player's health when they take lethal damage.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Iuliia Misiul]] created [[Wikipedia:Concept art|concept art]] for the Cholganna survivalist belt and other outfits used by Vess.<ref name="Art Station">{{ArtStation|username=Iuliia Misiul|url=/artwork/4N9Ve4|name=[[Iuliia Misiul]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws &mdash; Kay's Gearsets}}</ref>
