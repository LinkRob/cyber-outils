The '''Emperor's Fist''' was a title or moniker held by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], a [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] and [[Supreme commander of the Imperial fleet|supreme commander of the Imperial Fleet]].<ref name="Rise2">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> Throughout the [[Imperial Era]], [[Lord]] Vader acted as [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]'s enforcer, being tasked with hunting down surviving [[Jedi Knight|Jedi Knights]], crushing [[rebel]] cells and eradicating any threats to his [[Sith Master|master]]'s reign.

[[Darth]] Vader's reputation as the [[Emperor]]'s Fist was well known among his peers and subordinates within the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Vader" /> but also to criminals.<ref name="FACPOV ESB">{{StoryCite|story=Fake It Till You Make It|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> During the [[attack on Namzor]] by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] headed by [[Governor]] [[Ahr]], the [[Sith]] [[warlord]] ignored his demands and pushed on to eliminate the [[Namzor insurgents]], which also resulted in the destruction of a [[coaxium]] storage. The infuriated [[Moff]]'s [[Ahr's subordinate|subordinate]] informed him that it was the Emperor's Fist who was responsible for [[bomb]]ing the area.<ref name="Vader" /> The [[Medal of the Emperor's Fist]] was a rare award given to some Imperial [[pilot]]s.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>
