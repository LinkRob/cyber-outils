An '''alternating polarizer''',<ref name="UltimateSW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> also known as a '''polarizer sphere'''<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> or '''polarizing orb''',<ref name="UltimateSW" /> was a type of magnetic device used to energize metal [[blaster]] projectiles known as [[quarrel]]s in [[bowcaster]]s. A bowcaster contained a positive-default and negative-default alternating polarizer, along with a conductive bowstring,<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> balanced on each end of the weapon.<ref name="UltimateSW" /> The polarizing orbs would charge a quarrel and envelope it in [[plasma]]<ref name="DB Bowcaster">{{Databank|bowcaster|Bowcaster}}</ref> energy before firing a destructive blaster bolt from the barrel of the bowcaster,<ref name="TFAVD" /> boosting its power with its [[magnetic field]].<ref name="UltimateSW" />
