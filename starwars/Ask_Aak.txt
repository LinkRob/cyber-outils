'''Ask Aak''' was a [[Gran]] [[Gender|male]] [[senator]] who represented the [[planet]] [[Malastare]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] during the final [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]]. At the [[time]] of the [[Separatist Crisis]] Aak was a member of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] [[Loyalist Committee]]. A proponent of military action against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the senator advocated the use of the [[clone trooper]] [[army]] in order to defend the Republic. The result was the establishment of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] and the subsequent [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]].

==Biography==
===Separatist Crisis===
[[File:BailOrganaAlderaanianSenator-SoH.png|thumb|left|250px|Ask Aak with Senator Organa and Mas Amedda in the Chancellor's office]]
During the [[Separatist Crisis|Secessionist Movement]], a period of [[Politics|political]] tension between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Episode II" /> Ask Aak [[Senator|represented]] the [[planet]] [[Malastare]] in the [[Galactic Senate]],<ref name="Ask Aak" /> replacing the [[assassin]]ated [[Aks Moe]].<ref name="SWHC1" /> He was a part of the [[Loyalist Committee|inner circle]] of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]&mdash;secretly [[Darth]] Sidious, the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] who was manipulating the political crisis. Aak was present in [[Emperor's office|Palpatine's office]] when [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] sent a [[Hologram|holographic]] message to [[Coruscant]] warning that the [[Commerce Guild]] and the [[Corporate Alliance]] had formed an alliance with [[Count]] [[Dooku]], a [[Sith Lord]] and political leader of the Separatists. Aak stated that the debate over military action was over and that the Republic needed the [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone army]] that Kenobi [[Mission to Kamino (Separatist Crisis)|discovered]] had been created in secret on [[Kamino]]. Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] disagreed that the debate was over, believing that the Senate would not approve the use of the [[Clone trooper|clones]] prior to a Separatist attack.<ref name="Episode II" />

===Clone Wars===
[[File:AskAak-SWCT.png|thumb|right|200px|Senator Ask Aak]]
After the meeting in the Chancellor's office, [[Junior Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] of [[Naboo]] proposed that Palpatine be given emergency executive power in order to approve the use of the [[Cloning|clone]] [[army]]. The Senate approved the measure, and the clone [[trooper]]s became the Grand Army of the Republic and were put into action during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]. Aak was later with Palpatine and other Senators, who watched as<ref name="Episode II" /> tens of thousands<ref name="Attack of the Clones story gallery">{{SW|url=films/star-wars-episode-2-attack-of-the-clones-story-gallery|text=''Star Wars: Attack of the Clones'' Story Gallery}}</ref> of clones departed for more battles against the Separatists during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode II" />

When Palpatine was rescued from General Grievous at the Battle of Coruscant in [[19 BBY]], Ask was among the Senators who greeted him on the landing platform. Glad to be rescued, Palpatine stopped in second place but was still quick to get in. He was later in the Senate when Palpatine proclaimed the Galactic Empire, a government that replaced the Republic.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith">{{Film|III}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Ask Aak was a [[Gran]] with [[Color|orange-brown]] skin and black [[eye]]s, of which Aak had three.<ref name="Episode II" /> Aak claimed to be patriotic to the Republic and was a steady supporter of Palpatine and the war, questioning or threatening any who did not support the conflict.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Senator Ask Aak first appeared in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. The character was portrayed by [[Steven Boyle]].<ref name="Episode II" /> He later appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], portrayed by [[Paul Spence]].
