The '''Doctrine of the Dyad''' was a concept centered on the [[Force dyad]], a pairing of two [[Force-sensitive]] beings linked by a unique [[Force-bond]] that was unbreakable''.'' It was the predecessor of the [[Rule of Two]], a decree developed by [[Darth Bane]] to limit the [[Sith]] Order's ranks to two [[Sith Lord]]s&mdash;a [[Sith Master]] and a [[Sith apprentice]]&mdash;at any given [[time]]. Although the Rule of Two ensured the survival of the Sith for a [[millennium]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]] called it a "pale imitation" of the Doctrine of the Dyad.<ref name="SotS" />

The Doctrine of the Dyad was etched into the walls of the [[Sith Citadel]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]], where it served as a reminder of its importance to the Sith Order and the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cult|cult]].<ref name="SotS" /> For a millennium under the Rule of Two, the Sith unsuccessfully tried to create a dyad through force of will. [[Darth Plagueis]] failed to form a dyad with [[Darth]] Sidious, his apprentice, who in turn failed to create a dyad with his own apprentice, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The Sith Eternal, a group of [[Sith cultist]]s that followed the Sith [[religion]],<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> believed that the dyad was the future of the Sith&mdash;the key to unlocking the full potential of the [[dark side of the Force]].<ref name="SotS" /> A prophecy in the Sith Eternal's lore foretold the birth of a Force dyad.<ref name="TROS VD" />

Entire generations passed before a dyad was born in [[Ben Solo]], the [[Skywalker family|grandson]] of Darth Vader, and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], the [[Palpatine family|granddaughter]] of Darth Sidious, during the [[New Republic Era]]. Linked together through a rare and powerful Force connection,<ref name="TROS VD" /> their bond as a dyad allowed Solo and Rey to communicate across time and [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="SotS" /> During the [[Battle of Exegol]], Sidious discovered their nature as a dyad and [[Force drain|drained]] the combined life force of Rey and Solo to heal his weakened [[Cloning|clone]] body. After Sidious' destruction, Solo transferred his life force to Rey, resurrecting her at the cost of his life.<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Doctrine of the Dyad was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]'', the [[2020]] novelization of the [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|film]] of the same name,<ref name="TROS EE" /> and was first identified as such in the [[2021]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''.<ref name="SotS" /> 

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, in the [[2011]] novel [[Knight Errant (novel)|''Knight Errant'']], written by [[John Jackson Miller]], the [[Dyarchy]] [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] principality was ruled with a similar [[Force-bond/Legends|Force bond]] that existed between the Sith [[Twin/Legends|twins]] [[Quillan (Sith)|Quillan]] and [[Dromika]] during the [[Republic Dark Age]], who were described as "separated in body, but conjoined through the Force" and "one being, that no power in science or [[Sith alchemy/Legends|Sith alchemy]] could separate."<ref name="Knight Errant">[[Knight Errant (novel)|''Knight Errant'']]</ref>
