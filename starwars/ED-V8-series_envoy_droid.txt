The '''ED-V8-series envoy droid''' was a model of [[protocol droid]] designed by the [[Rseikharhl Droid Group]], for [[diplomat]]s working with non-[[humanoid]] [[species]]. It was built around the chassis of an ancient [[Krath war droid]], using [[cybernetics]] to alter the appearance of its body. They were programmed to be nonthreatening, having a high level of autonomy.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony" />
