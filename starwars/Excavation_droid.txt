'''Excavation droids''' or '''demo-droids''' were a type of [[droid]] utilized on [[Archaeology|archaeological]] expeditions. According to legend, [[HCT-10]] and [[RF-U5]] were two such droids that were part of a crew led by the [[Scout|explorer]] [[Fionn Tucat]] during an [[Mission to Lupal|expedition]] to the [[moon]] of [[Lupal]].<ref name="Blood Moon">{{StoryCite|story=Blood Moon|book=Dark Legends}}</ref> Demo-droids were used by the [[Alderaan Flotilla]] to dig for lost artifacts, remains or fragments from [[Alderaan]] in the [[Alderaanian Graveyard]].<ref name="ALD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>
