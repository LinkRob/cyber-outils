This '''[[Turbolift/Legends|turbolift]]''' was in use during the initial stages of construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|DS-1 Orbital Battle Station]], better known as Death Star I. It could fit at least one [[Human/Legends|Human]] adult. During the expansion of the [[Space station/Legends|space station]], it may have been superseded by faster lift tube models such as [[Infini-lift Lightspeed]], [[Insta-Lift 2000 SE]], [[NB200 Commercial Lift]] or [[Ultra-Lift 3000 Extreme]].
