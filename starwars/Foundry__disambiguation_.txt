'''Foundry''' can refer to several things:

=='''[[Canon]]'''==
*[[Foundry]], a location in the galaxy controlled by the [[Galactic Empire]]
*[[Foundry (Burnin Konn)]], a [[chromium]] [[mining]] site on the [[planet]] [[Burnin Konn]]
*[[Foundry (Corellia)]], an underground bunker on the planet [[Corellia]]
*[[Foundry Four]], an underground warren of droid factories on the planet [[Xorrn]]
*[[Foundry of the Confederacy]], a region in the [[Mid Rim Territories]] and [[Outer Rim Territories]] that served as the location of several [[factory world]]s of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]
*[[Orbital Foundry 7]], a [[space station]] located in [[orbit]] of the planet [[Geonosis]] during the [[Imperial Era]]

==[[Star Wars Legends|'''Legends''']]==
*[[Foundry/Legends|Foundry]], a [[Planet/Legends|planet]] located in the [[Colonies/Legends|Colonies]]
*[[Foundry system/Legends|Foundry system]], the [[Star system/Legends|star system]] of the planet Foundry
*[[Foundry Four/Legends|Foundry Four]], a warren of droid factories on the planet [[Xorrn/Legends|Xorrn]]
*[[Foundry of the Confederacy/Legends|Foundry of the Confederacy]], a region of industrial worlds controlled by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]] located in the [[Mid Rim/Legends|Mid Rim]] and [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]]
*[[Foundry Uprising]], an event that took place prior to [[3678 BBY]]
*[[Foundry (space station)]], a [[Space station/Legends|space station]] located in the [[Nanth'ri system/Legends|Nanth'ri system]]
*[[Battle of the Foundry]], a battle that took place on the Foundry space station during the [[Galactic War]]
*[[Mission to Foundry]], a mission to the planet Foundry during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]
