'''Blackvine''' was a type of [[Color|green]] [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[Plant|vine]] that grew on the [[planet]] [[Dagobah]]. In [[Dragonsnake Bog]], blackvine had formed natural bridges over portions of the [[swamp]] close to the [[Yoda's Hut|hut]] of the [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] [[Jedi]] [[Yoda]]<ref name="CL" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|V}} to [[3 ABY]]. The [[map]] depicting blackvine in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] also depicts events that take place in ''The Empire Strikes Back'' and so the image must also be set in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> Blackvine also grew on top of the nearby [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Cave of Evil]], which thought of itself as a trap beneath the blackvine leaves.<ref name="FACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=Vergence (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> When Yoda [[Death|died]] in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[dragonsnake]] he named [[Bright-Eyes]] felt [[Dark side of the Force|darkness]] pulsing from the cave and came to investigate. She found a mess of blackvine surrounding the cave mouth and slowly moving as it stretched alongside the opening, fed by the dark energy spilling from within.<ref name="ROTJFACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=The Light That Falls|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi|audiobook=1}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Blackvine was first mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], in a section written by [[James Luceno]] and illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]].<ref name="CL" /> The location originally appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2004]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars Trilogy]]'', which was also written by Luceno, with illustrations by Chasemore and Jenssen.<ref name="OTWorlds">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars Trilogy]]''</ref>
