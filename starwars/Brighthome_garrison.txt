In the aftermath of the [[Clone Wars]] and [[Order 66]]<ref name="Dark Lord 2" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''garrison''' of [[Clone trooper|clone]] [[stormtrooper]]s was established at the former [[Jedi]] [[space station]] ''[[Brighthome]]''. However, the entire [[garrison]] was wiped out when the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Attack on Brighthome|arrived]] at the station. Instead of simply transmitting his clearance codes, Vader elected to simply wipe out the [[soldier]]s.<ref name="X-Wing 54" />

==Description==
The garrison of [[Cloning|clones]] was stationed on ''Brighthome'', a Jedi space station in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid-Rim]], and was comprised of numerous clone stormtroopers and a small squadron of four [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter]]s. The garrison was under the command of a [[Unidentified clone trooper (Brighthome)|clone officer]].

==History==
===Attack on ''Brighthome''===
Sometime following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and the initial aftermath of Order 66, a garrison of clone troopers, now serving as Imperial stormtroopers, was established on ''Brighthome'', a Mid-Rim former Jedi outpost. The [[trooper]]s were tasked with collecting any Jedi artifacts stored at the [[space station]], and to prepare them to be shipped back to [[Coruscant]]. As troopers [[Ding]] and [[Kicker]] discussed how menial the mission was and their place as [[soldier]]s in the Empire, the two were reprimanded by their commanding officer and alerted to the presence of an [[Vader's starship|approaching vessel]].

On the vessel was newly-christened [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who refused to respond to the warnings issued by the garrison's Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter squadron. While Vader's [[Darth Vader's droid|personal droid]] insisted on transferring clearance codes to the clones, Vader decided to wipe out the garrison. After destroying the starfighters in a brief dogfight, Vader docked in the space station's [[hangar]] and engaged the stormtroopers stationed inside. Utilizing a Jedi [[Lightsaber]] and [[The Force]], Vader annihilated the garrison before accessing ''Brighthome''<nowiki/>'s [[databank]] for the locations of any living Jedi. Suddenly, Vader was ambushed by Ding and Kicker, who had survived the initial onslaught. After seizing the two troopers with The Force, Vader snapped their necks before fleeing the station with his personal droid as a thermal detonator went off, destroying a portion of the station.{{Stub}}
