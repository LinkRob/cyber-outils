'''Dragon of the Desert''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[July 8]] [[2002]] and describes the formidable [[K'kayeh dragon]] designed by [[Darth Rivan]].
