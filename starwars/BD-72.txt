'''BD-72'''<ref name="Mandalorian Experience">{{YouTube|video=BKDLvemhY_k|text=The Mandalorian Experience, Full Tour at Star Wars Celebration 2022|channel=attractionsmagazine|name=Attractions Magazine}}</ref> was owned by [[Peli Motto]] on [[Tatooine]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. The droid assisted [[Din Djarin]] and [[Peli Motto]] with the construction of [[Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter]].<ref name="Chapter 5" />

==Equipment==
The droid was equipped with various tools, including a [[spotlight]] and a [[holoprojector]]. He used both of these tools to aid in building Din Djarin's customized [[N-1 starfighter]].<ref name="Chapter 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
The droid first appeared in "[[Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian]]," the fifth episode of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]''.<ref name="Chapter 5" /> It was first identified as BD-72 in ''The Mandalorian Experience'' exhibition in [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|''Celebration Anaheim'']] in [[2022]].<ref name="Mandalorian Experience" />
