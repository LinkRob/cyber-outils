The '''C-series''' was a series of [[astromech droid]]<ref name="SWTVE" /> manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and employed as [[Technician|mechanics]] or co-[[pilot]]s on [[starship]]s of all sizes. By the time of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]], they were outdated by most standards.<ref name="SWTVE" /> One model within the line was the [[C1-series astromech droid]].<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>
