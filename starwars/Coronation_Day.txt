'''Coronation Day''' was an event celebrated on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. It was also celebrated by the [[Alderaanian]] residents of [[Level 3204]] on the planet [[Coruscant]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], despite the fact many of them rarely visited their [[homeworld]].<ref name="OTLD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coronation Day was mentioned in "[[One Thousand Levels Down]]," a short story written by [[Alexander Freed]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 151|''Star Wars Insider'' 151]]<ref name="OTLD" /> on [[July 22]], [[2014]].<ref name="Titan Magazines">{{TitanMagazines|url=t/star-wars-insider/us/151|text=Star Wars Insider #151}}</ref>
