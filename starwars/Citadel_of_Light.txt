The '''Citadel of Light''' was an ancient fortress on the [[planet]] [[Bray]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], it was home to [[Lord]] [[Ravna]], who began to infect and convert members of the native [[Brayan]] population. [[Count]] [[Dooku]] also resided in the Citadel, planning to use Ravna's powers to help the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] win the war. The Citadel was destroyed after [[Jedi Master]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Adi Gallia]] visited the planet and discovered Ravna's presence.<ref name="TFVC 2" />
