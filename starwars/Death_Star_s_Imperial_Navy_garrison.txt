The '''Death Star's Imperial Navy garrison''' was the sizable [[garrison]] of the [[Imperial Navy|naval branch]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] that was stationed on board the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] and was fully equipped with a [[Death Star's fleet|fleet]] that included [[assault gunboat]]s, [[dreadnought]]s, and [[Star Destroyer]]s. Due to the size of the garrison, Navy personnel took up a large amount of room aboard the Death Star, annoying the [[Death Star's Imperial Army garrison]] and [[Battle Station Operations]] personnel. The entire garrison and [[fleet]] were led by [[Chief of the Imperial Navy]] [[Admiral]] [[Conan Antonio Motti]].<ref name="Dawn" />
