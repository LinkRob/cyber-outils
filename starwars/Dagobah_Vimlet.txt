'''Dagobah Vimlet''' was a [[Color|green]] [[alcohol]]ic speciality [[cocktail]] served [[Water|iced]] with sliced [[fruit]]. It was potable to [[human]]s and served on board the ''[[Halcyon]]'', an [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]],<ref name="Cruiser" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> On board the [[starship]], the beverage was served in the [[Sublight Lounge]]<ref name="Cruiser" /> and was also available during breakfast, lunch, and dinner in the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]].<ref name="Crown" />

While the dining room served the drink in a standard cocktail glass,<ref name="Crown" /> the lounge served it in a specific type of souvenir glass that guests could then keep after purchasing the beverage. The glass had a transparent bowl on top of a golden stem with the [[Chandrila Star Line]] logo on the base. The drink shared its name<ref name="Cruiser" /> with the [[swamp]] [[planet]] [[Dagobah]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dagobah Vimlet was an alcoholic beverage available at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] beverage is a [[Wikipedia:Vodka gimlet|vodka gimlet]] made with [[Wikipedia:Vodka|vodka]], [[Wikipedia:Triple sec|Herbaceous Orange liqueur]], [[Wikipedia:Lime|lime]], and [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|peppercorn]].<ref name="Cruiser" /> Dagobah Vimlet was last served at the hotel during the [[September 28]][[September 29|&ndash;]][[September 30|30]] voyage of [[2023]], the final voyage before the closure of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser.<ref name="Official website">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref>
