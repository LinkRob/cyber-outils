The '''decrepit starport''' was a [[spaceport]] built in a rock formation in the [[Western Dune Sea]] region of the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]] the spaceport had fallen into disrepair and was controlled by a [[pirate]] group.<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> 
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate" />, [[Kay Vess]] used her [[fusioncutter]] to cut through a seal area of starport and steal the materials required to apply a [[color|sandstorm finish]] to the ''Trailblazer''<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The decrepit starport first appeared in the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' that was published in [[2024]] by [[Ubisoft]]. <ref name="Outlaws" />
