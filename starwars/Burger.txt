'''Burgers''' were a type of [[sandwich]].<ref name="Stop, Thief!" /> A variety of burger, the [[bantha burger]], was made using the meat of a [[bantha]].<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> The [[restaurant]] [[Buffo's Blurrg Burgers]] served burgers made from [[blurrg]] meat, and displayed an [[Aurebesh]] sign reading "THE BEST BLURRGER IN TOWN".<ref name="99 Trivia" /> Blurrg burgers also seemed to be available in [[Tanda's Cantina]] on [[Suria space station]], where an ad showed an illustration of a blurrg between two pieces of [[bread]].<ref name="Bad Territory" />
