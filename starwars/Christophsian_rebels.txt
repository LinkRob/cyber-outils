The '''Christophsian rebels''' were a resistance group active during the [[Clone Wars]] on the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]]. The rebels combated the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance's]] [[Assault on Christophsis|invasion]] of the planet. By the [[time]] the [[Galactic Republic]] arrived, the rebels had been nearly wiped out and had been pushed back to the [[eastern exurbs]].<ref name="OTFL" /> The rebels were equipped with [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s.<ref name="Hidden Enemy">{{TCW|The Hidden Enemy}}</ref>
