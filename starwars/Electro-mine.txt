'''Electro-mines'''<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> (also spelled '''electromines'''<ref name="Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-citadel-episode-gallery|text="The Citadel" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref>) were demolition devices that were electrified. The [[Citadel]] [[prison]] on [[Lola Sayu]] had its exterior walls armed with electromines to prevent the wall from being scaled. During the [[Clone Wars]], when the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Battle of Lola Sayu|sent]] a rescue [[squadron]] to extract the [[prisoner]] [[Even Piell]], the [[clone trooper]] nicknamed "[[Charger]]" fell from the prison wall and hit an electromine&mdash;[[Death|killing]] him&mdash;causing an explosion that alerted the prison to the Republic's presence.<ref name="The Citadel">{{TCW|The Citadel (episode)}}</ref>
