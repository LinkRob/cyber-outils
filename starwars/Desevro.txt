'''Desevro''' was an astronomical object located in the [[Tion Cluster]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was also located on the [[Perlemian Trade Route]].<ref name="TFABG" /> It was contested territory during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Squadrons" />

==History==
Four years after [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]], a [[Battle above Desevro|battle]] occurred above the planet, when the [[Galactic Empire]] attacked a [[New Republic]] convoy trying to sneak through the debris field on its way to [[Project Starhawk]]'s secret base. [[Titan Squadron]] were to follow the convoy and allow them to jump to [[hyperspace]] to calculate all possible destinations along their trajectory. However, the mission was foiled when a local [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Tormentor]]'', arrived and attacked the convoy. Titan Squadron gained the sector coordinates, but all New Republic forces as well as the ''Tormentor'' were destroyed.<ref name="Squadrons" />
