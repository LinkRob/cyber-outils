'''Dugar dugar''' were a [[species]] of [[deer]]like creature [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. They were described as slender, with long silky fur that was "the color of mushrooms."<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> Their calls could be heard from within [[Black Spire Outpost]]. Some residents of Batuu raised dugar dugar to become pets or for other reasons.<ref name="GEStaff">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] has official backstory elements that, though not directly published for the public, are officially canon details that can be obtained verbally from cast members working in the land.</ref>

{{Creature-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dugar dugar were created for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] on [[May 31]], [[2019]]. While not physically present in the park, sound clips of the creatures' calls can be heard in certain areas.<ref name="GEStaff" />
