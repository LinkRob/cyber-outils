'''Fiumarians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="Free Fall" /> from<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Fiumar system]] in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> with [[Color|pale pink]] skin, pupilless [[eye]]s, a sloping forehead, and yellow blood. They were deceptively fast and could remain neutral in heated negotiations, making them adept [[diplomat]]s or [[smuggler]]s.<ref name="Free Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fiumarians first appeared in the [[canon]] young-adult novel ''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]'', which was authored by [[Alex Segura]]<ref name="Free Fall" /> and published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[August 4]], [[2020]].<ref name="Free Fall Amazon">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/poe-dameron-free-fall|text=Poe Dameron: Free Fall}}</ref> The species was later depicted in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] one-shot [[Return of the Jedi – The Rebellion 1|''Return of the Jedi – The Rebellion'' 1]], which was written by Segura, illustrated by [[Matt Horak]], [[Brent Peeples]], and [[Rafael Pimentel]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[July 19]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103246/star_wars_return_of_the_jedi_-_the_rebellion_1_2023_1|text=Star Wars: Return Of The Jedi &ndash; The Rebellion (2023) #1}}</ref>
