'''Bubble wort extract''' was a product used in the construction of [[Otoh Gunga]]'s bubble-like cities on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] world of [[Naboo]],<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> and was mixed with plasma to grow the [[Gungan]] bubble-like buildings.
