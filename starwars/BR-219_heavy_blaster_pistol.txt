The '''BR-219 heavy blaster pistol''' was a model of [[heavy blaster pistol]] that was considered one of the most [[Death|lethal]] [[blaster pistol]]s on the market. The weapon was essentially a [[pistol]] conversion of the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]], but used a notoriously unstable mix of [[blaster gas]]es that could cause it to explode.

==Description==
The BR-219 heavy blaster pistol was a model of short-ranged [[heavy blaster pistol]] that was essentially a [[pistol]] conversion of the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] made by [[BlasTech Industries]]. The weapon had a tubular frame with a wide body and a broad muzzle. The [[barrel]] was shortened, which necessitated reconfiguration of the weapon's [[XCiter]] and [[Galven-pattern]] in order for the weapon to maintain the bare minimum of beam cohesion.<ref name="G&G" />

The overpowered [[blaster pistol]] used a proprietary mix of [[blaster gas]]es which were notoriously unstable. Over [[time]], this mix melted the weapons Galven circuity, which could lead to the weapon exploding. The BR-219 emitted a notoriously bright muzzle flash and loud cracking noise upon firing, abandoning any sense of subtlety.<ref name="G&G" />

==History==
The BR-219 was considered one of the [[Death|deadliest]] [[blaster pistol]]s available during the [[Galactic Civil War]] and was available for purchase only directly from the factory, with any prospective buyer requiring an intense background check from the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Security Bureau]]. The weapon was considered rare and cost 625 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="G&G" />
