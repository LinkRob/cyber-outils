'''Dendro IV'''<ref name="Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-escape-part-1-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 5 Fun Facts from "The Escape - Part 1" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2020-01-27}}</ref> was a [[planet]] that appeared [[Color|green]] from [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Concept Art" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY">{{ResistanceDate|2|18}}</ref> the [[First Order]] attacked the planet,<ref name="Bucket's List" /> causing a region of its surface to become devastated and [[Fire|burned]].<ref name="Concept Art" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dendro IV was meant to appear in the opening sequence of part one of the two-part episode "[[The Escape (episode)|The Escape]],"<ref name="Bucket's List" /> the finale of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[January 26]], [[2020]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> Dendro IV's role was changed to the<ref name="Bucket's List" /> planet [[Aeos Prime]]<ref name="NPS">{{Resistance|No Place Safe}}</ref> once work began on episodes involving the [[Aeosian]]s,<ref name="Bucket's List" />&mdash;"[[The New World]]"<ref name="The New World">{{Resistance|The New World}}</ref> and "[[No Place Safe]],"<ref name="NPS" /> the fifteenth and sixteenth episodes of Season Two that aired [[January 12]], 2020<ref name="Disney Press" />&mdash;to better illustrate the danger of the First Order.<ref name="Bucket's List" />

While Dendro IV was removed from the episode, it was mentioned in the episode guide for the second half of "The Escape"<ref name="Concept Art" /> and the article "Bucket's List Extra: 5 Fun Facts from 'The Escape &ndash; Part 1' &ndash; ''Star Wars Resistance'',"<ref name="Bucket's List" /> both of which were posted on [[StarWars.com]] on [[January 27]], 2020.<ref name="Concept Art" /><ref name="Bucket's List" /> [[Jim Moore]] created concept art for the planet on [[October 15]], [[2018]] that was included in the episode guide. While the caption of the concept art identified the image as "Aeos,"<ref name="Concept Art" /> the world depicted appears different from the [[Waterworld|ocean planet]]<ref name="The New World" /> and is labeled "planetballDendroDstrd."<ref name="Concept Art" />
