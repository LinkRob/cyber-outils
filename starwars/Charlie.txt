'''Charlie''' was a [[clone trooper]] in service to the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. At some point in the war, Charlie deserted his [[squad]], but was returned to [[Kamino]], where he was assigned a [[Heater's squad|squad]] with several other [[Cloning|clone]] deserters led by "[[Heater (clone trooper)|Heater]]." Charlie believed that his desertion had been a mistake, and that he could redeem himself to the Republic. He and his fellow former deserters were sent on a [[Assault on a Separatist weapons depot|mission]] to destroy a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Separatist weapons depot|weapons depot]], after which the unit chose to return to Republic service.<ref name="Battle Tales 4" />
