'''Bene''' was a rare [[mineral]]. Early [[prospector]]s on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] believed there to be bene deposits in [[Plibene Rock]].<ref name="SW:CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], bene was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. The caption mentioning bene was reprinted<ref name="SW:CL" /> from the mineral's first mention in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was created for the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]].<ref name="InsideTPM">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
