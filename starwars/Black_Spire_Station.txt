'''Black Spire Station''' was a business location in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet [[Batuu]] that sold various [[droid]] parts.<ref name="WDWNewsToday" /> People searching for parts at the station could look to [[Salju]] for assistance. Several droids, including an R2 unit, an R3 unit, an R4 unit, a G2 droid, and two box droids, were also found near the location alongside at least one [[speeder bike]] and two [[landspeeder]]s. Amongst the machinery, [[Tooka|Tooka cats]], could occasionally be found as Salju has a soft spot for the creatures. Fuel and repairs for vehicles were available at this location, and it was almost always open.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==History==
In [[34 ABY]], the station's owner, [[Salju]] met the young girl, [[Izal Garsea]], when she landed her ship, the [[Meridian (light freighter)|''Meridian'']], nearby the station. The mechanic provided her services to repair the small ship amidst other orders.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Black_Spire_Station_card.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Trading card art of the station]]
Black Spire Station was first unveiled during the December 16, 2017 "Galactic Nights" session as part of the map side of several [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]-themed trading cards that were intended to promote the ride. The map cards contained locations for the planet [[Batuu]], as well as a [[Old Batuuan ballad|poem in Aurebesh]] along the border of the card. In Black Spire Station's case, the poem line was "THOSE BROKEN AND TOSSED". According to the Walt Disney World news page, Black Spire Station is the place where droid parts could be purchased.<ref name="WDWNewsToday" /> The catwalk above the station is a location where [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], [[Chewbacca]], [[Vi Moradi]] or [[First Order stormtrooper]]s may speak to visitors. Rey and [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] fought a lightsaber duel on the catwalk during the media event on the opening day of [[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance|''Star Wars'': Rise of the Resistance]] at Hollywood Studios.<ref name="YouTube-Star Wars Lightsaber Battle and Stunt Show in Galaxy's Edge during media event &#124; Attractions Magazine">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@attractionsmagazine|name=Attractions Magazine|video=UGUsYWmVV2I|text=Star Wars Lightsaber Battle and Stunt Show in Galaxy's Edge during media event &#124; Attractions Magazine|date=2019-12-04}}</ref> The Disneyland version of the catwalk was the site of another duel between Rey and Kylo Ren at the conclusion of the [[Blaze Your Path]] special event for Make-A-Wish children on 11 September 2024.<ref>{{YouTube|video=sQpRXpmMzHs|text=Star Wars Galaxy's Edge Stunt Show Blaze Your Path: The Finale in Disneyland|channel=@touringplans|name=TouringPlans|date=2024-09-12}}</ref>
