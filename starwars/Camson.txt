'''Camson''' was a [[desert]] [[planet]]<ref name="Senate Perspective" /> in the [[Camson system]] in the [[Abrion sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="SWSystems" /> that had at least one [[Unidentified city (Camson)|city]]. 

== Description ==
Camson was a rocky [[desert]] planet located in [[Abrion sector]] the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="Senate Perspective" /><ref name="SWSystems" /> The planet had at least one large city with a [[spaceport]]. The planet had a breathable atmosphere with clouds.<ref name="Senate Perspective" /> 

== History ==
Following a [[Liberation of Nadea Tural|skirmish]] with [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Commander]] [[Earll]] in [[4 BBY]] the [[Spectres]] transported [[Senator]] [[Nadea Tural]] to safety on Camson.<ref name="Senate Perspective" />
