'''Conduit 398-GX14''' was a power system conduit on the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]], located behind an access hatch near the entrance to the station's [[Starlight Beacon's Jedi temple|Jedi temple]]. Shortly after the station's official completion, a badly wired [[resistor hub]] inside the conduit nearly caused a station-wide power outage, but [[technician]]s [[Joss Adren|Joss]] and [[Pikka Adren]] located and fixed the problem before any major issues could occur.<ref name="Starlight" />
