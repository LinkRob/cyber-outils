{{Quote|The Agbui. Creatures of loud arrogance and silent uselessness, able to persuade a target merely into doing what he already wanted to do.|General Yiv the Benevolent|<ref name="Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>}}
The '''Agbui''',<ref name="Greater Good" /> nicknamed '''Scratchlings''',<ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref> were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that resided within the [[Unknown Regions]]. Their members possessed a form ability of [[telepathy]], able to read a being's emotions by touch. They had faces of [[Color|dark red]] and off-white folds, with black [[eye]]s and lipless slits for [[mouth]]s. The Agbui [[Haplif]] was a servant of the [[Grysk]] "[[Jixtus]]" who was skilled in reading cultures and societies. Under orders from Jixtus, Haplif attempted to [[Jixtus's plan|manipulate]] the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] into a civil war, and a number of Agbui assisted in the scheme.<ref name="Greater Good" />

By the near-completion of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first Death Star]], the Agbui were a client species of the [[Grysk Hegemony]] and helped their overlords [[Imperial–Grysk conflict|prepare to invade]] the [[Galactic Empire]]. At that time, they became known to the [[Chiss]] and [[human]]s by the exonym "Scratchlings" because of the scratchy sound of their voices.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

==Biology and appearance==
Agbui were sentient beings with dark red and off-white skin. The colors of their faces manifested in seemingly random patterns that were distinct between individuals.<ref name="Greater Good" /> The Agbui had long fingers compared with other sentient species, and their native language sounded scratchy to [[Chiss]] and [[human]] observers.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> Adult Agbui typically ranged from 1.5-2 [[meter]]s in height. Agbui's mouths were slits with no lips.<ref name="Greater Good" />

Agbui had a limited form of [[telepathy]] based on touching other sentients. Direct physical contact with the skin of other beings allowed them to sense that person's emotions and desires. The more prolonged, the more directly, and the more frequently an Agbui touched another person, the more they understood that individual's goals and desires. [[Haplif]] used this ability to manipulate many [[Chiss]] during his travels through the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], particularly on [[Celwis]]. Haplif claimed to be able to understand how to manipulate entire species after using his telepathy on sufficiently many members of that species.<ref name="Greater Good" />

The Agbui did not disclose their telepathic abilities to most beings they interacted with. One exception was the [[Grysk Hegemony]] agent "[[Jixtus]]," who hired Agbui specifically for those abilities. Some Agbui, such as Haplif and [[Shimkif]], became irritated or even angry when people would not allow them to touch them. Lack of direct physical contact meant the Agbui could not manipulate that person as well, which led Haplif to become angry at [[Yomie]] and [[Xodlak'phr'ooa|Lakphro]]. The Agbui's body structure was very different to that of [[The Magys's people]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

==History==
===Helping the Grysks conquer the Unknown Regions===
{{SeeAlso|Grysk conquests}}
{{Quote|Why in the world are these Agbui stealing jewelry from the Magys's people and passing it off as their own? What does that gain them?|Mid Captain [[Ufsa'mak'ro|Samakro]]|<ref name="Greater Good" />}}
A group of Agbui led by [[Haplif]] engineered a disastrous [[Sunrise's civil war|civil war]] on the planet known as [[Sunrise]], killing most of the population and subsequently [[mining]] that world for [[nyix]]. The Grysk "[[Jixtus]]" then tasked them to execute a [[Jixtus's plan|plot to destroy the Chiss Ascendancy]] by convincing several [[Great Families]] to fight over a [[Hoxim|planet]] supposedly rich with nyix.<ref name="Greater Good" />

During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], some Agbui continued to serve the Grysks, including managing captured Chiss ''[[Sky-walker|ozyly-esehembo]]'' [[navigator]] [[Youngling|children]] on behalf of their Grysk overlords. The young girl [[Un'hee]] was a captive of the Agbui, whom she nicknamed Scratchlings due to the sound of their voices. Several members of the species were assigned to watch over Un'hee during her captivity aboard a Grysk [[Grysk observation post|observation post]], but were [[Death|killed]] when the Grysks realized the post was about to be captured by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

===Subverting the Empire===
{{Quote|Did they utilize body scanners or other measurement devices?''"<br />"''I don't think so. ''[...]'' They didn't need anything. ''[...]'' Screechy voices, long fingers, crazy eyes. They'd have two or three of those things standing behind us the whole time, those fingers wrapped around our heads and the back of our necks. Creepiest thing I ever saw. They'd ask questions, and the screechers would just stand there hanging on to us. Then, after it was all over, they'd all sit down together and talk about the whole thing.|Grand Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] and smuggler [[Bleary]] discuss Bleary's captivity under the Agbui|<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />}}
Shortly before the [[Liberation of Lothal]], the Agbui assisted the [[Grysk Hegemony]]'s attempts to infiltrate, subvert, and invade the Galactic Empire. Two slaved Agbui [[starship]]s were present at a Grysk forward base in Imperial territory, where they formed a [[communications triad]] with a [[cloak]]ed Grysk warship. After the ''[[Chimaera]]'' destroyed the Grysk warship, a squad of [[stormtrooper]]s and [[Commodore]] [[Karyn Faro]] boarded one of the Agbui ships, where they encountered six Agbui holding several human and [[Dashade]] [[prison]]ers.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

The Dashades and humans were part of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Balanhai Savit]]'s [[Anti-piracy campaign in the Esaga sector|anti-piracy campaign]], which was secretly a means for Savit to pilfer [[Project Stardust]] materiel to other parts of the [[Imperial Navy]]. The Agbui had taken the smugglers prisoner and used their [[telepathy]] to divine the fears, wishes, desires, and ambitions of their captives. They planned to use that information to assist the Grysks subvert, sabotage, and/or invade the Empire; and managed to convince [[Yimmer]] and [[Westerli]] that they should work for the Grysks.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

One of the Agbui aboard spoke the [[trade language]] [[Meese Caulf]], which Grand Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] translated for Commodore Faro's benefit. That Agbui said he feared for his and his companions' lives and needed a face-to-face guarantee of safety from the Imperials. This was a ruse intended to allow them to [[Self-destruct mechanism|suicide bomb]] and take as many Imperials as possible with them, and thus maintain the good graces of the Grysk. Thrawn deduced as much, and ordered the stormtroopers to kill the Agbui aboard before they could detonate.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

==Society and culture==
{{Quote|These Agbui pop in out of nowhere, they just ''happen'' to land in your province, they just ''happen'' to have a nyix mine that no one else in the Chaos has ever heard of, and it just ''happens'' to be completely open and undefended? That doesn't sound insane to you?|[[Patriel]] [[Xodlak'oo'nifis]] of [[Celwis]]|<ref name="Greater Good" />}}
The Agbui had myths involving demons of some type, which they used to threaten children to behave. Haplif had never seen Jixtus without his cloak, hood and veil, and once wondered whether Jixtus might be a demon from Agbui mythology.<ref name="Greater Good" />

Agbui could eat the same foods as the Chiss. Some Agbui learned to understand the Chiss language [[Cheunh]], and some were known to speak the [[trade language]]s [[Minnisiat]]<ref name="Greater Good" /> and [[Meese Caulf]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

==Behind the scenes==
Agbui first appeared in the [[2019]] novel ''[[Thrawn: Treason]]'', which described them as having skin colored dark red and dirty white, with a lipless slit of a mouth tucked away amid skin folds, and with bright black eyes.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> The species was first identified as the Agbui in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', with the same description.<ref name="Greater Good" /> In both novels, the Agbui that appear served the [[Grysk Hegemony]].
