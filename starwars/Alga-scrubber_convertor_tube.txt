'''Alga-scrubber convertor tubes''' were a replaceable component of the unit attached to [[meknek helmet]]s during the [[High Republic Era]]. Using for scrubbing [[algae]], the tube was white and cylindrical with black bands circling each end and a ribbed portion around the middle.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> the mekneks [[Fillik]] and [[Verosha Aniseya]] had alga-scrubber convertor tubes in the units attached to their meknek [[helmet]]s while they worked on the [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] on the exterior of<ref name="Lost Found">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> the [[Trade Federation]] [[WD-2550 Whydah-class Yield Conveyer|WD-2550 ''Whydah''-class yield conveyer]] ''[[Fallon]]''.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alga-scrubber convertor tubes appeared in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="Lost Found" /> the first episode of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which aired [[June 4]], [[2024]] on [[Disney+]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> The tubes were first identified in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
