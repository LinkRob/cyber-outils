"'''Boba Fett: Mystery Man in Not-So-Shining Armor'''" was an eight-page fold-out poster included in [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 1|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 1]] featuring art by [[Markus Harrison]]. It included an article by [[Andy Mangels]] and a field guide diagram of [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett's]] [[Mandalorian armor/Legends|Mandalorian armor]].
