'''Dashi''' was a type of [[foodstuff]] edible by [[Twi'lek]]s.  While onboard the [[light freighter]] ''[[Poor Crow]]'', the Twi'lek [[Yuehiro]] made a simple [[soup]] consisting of [[miso paste]] melted in dashi with rehydrated [[vegetable]]s, sharing a bowl with it with the [[pilot]] [[Ekiya]], who liked it.<ref name="Ronin" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dashi appeared in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] novel ''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]'', by [[Emma Mieko Candon]].<ref name="Ronin" /> In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Dashi|dashi]] is a [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] [[Wikipedia:Soup|soup]] [[Wikipedia:Stock (food)|stock]] made from [[Wikipedia:Fish|fish]] and [[Wikipedia:Kelp|kelp]].<ref name="Jisho">{{WebCite|url=https://jisho.org/word/%E5%87%BA%E3%81%97|text=出し|work=Jisho.org|archivedate=20220701200012}}</ref>
