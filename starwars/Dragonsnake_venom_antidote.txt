During the [[New Republic Era]], the [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] was critically injured and rendered unconscious on the [[planet]] [[Nal Hutta]] by [[Dragonsnake venom]] after a battle with a [[Hutt’s Dragonsnake|dragonsnake]] owned by the [[The Twins]]. Eventually, the [[Unidentified species (Grogu)|alien]] [[Gatori]] created an [[antidote]] by mixing local [[plant]]s for Djarin's adoptive son, [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], to give him after learning from the bounty hunter [[Embo]] that Djarin had been poisoned by dragonsnake venom. Gatori warned Gorgu that it may be too late to save Djarin, but the antidote worked, causing Djarin to awaken from his coma after Grogu fed it to him.<ref name="Mandalorian and Grogu">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>
