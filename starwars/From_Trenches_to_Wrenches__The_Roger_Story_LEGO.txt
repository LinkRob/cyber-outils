'''''From Trenches to Wrenches: The Roger Story''''' was a memoir written by the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]], who worked for the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]], a family of scavengers. It was considered by [[Wick Cooper]], a Freemaker customer, to be tedious and boring.<ref name="Zander's Joyride" /> According to Roger, it was for sale on the Holonet for "an extremely reasonable price."<ref name="What Goes Up">{{All-Stars|What Goes Up... (episode)}}</ref> The [[book]] consisted of at least 482 chapters.<ref name="The Power at Jakku">{{All-Stars|The Power at Jakku}}</ref>

The first chapter began with Roger's creation at the [[Geonosis droid factories|Geonosian droid factories]] followed by his subsequent service during the [[Battle of Naboo]] and the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Lo, I am Manufactured">{{All-Stars|Lo, I am Manufactured}}</ref> In Chapter 18, Roger and his master [[Pace Freemaker/LEGO|Pace Freemaker]] escaped [[Dathomir]] on an [[Ugly]].<ref name="It Gets Ugly">{{All-Stars|It Gets Ugly}}</ref> In Chapter 48, Roger encountered [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] operatives [[K-2SO]] and [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] while picking up some [[30 weight oil]].<ref name="The Good Stuff">{{All-Stars|The Good Stuff!}}</ref> In Chapter 129, Roger was kidnapped by several [[Jawa]]s, but escaped during a job to service [[Ben Quadinaros]]' [[podracer]].<ref name="TPOT">{{All-Stars|The Prisoner of Tatooine}}</ref>

In Chapter 202, Roger and [[C1-10P|Chopper]] rescued several decommissioned droids being sent to be melted down by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Droid Rescue Gambit">{{All-Stars|The Droid Rescue Gambit}}</ref> In Chapter 299, Roger learned about persistence from a young [[Ewok]].<ref name="Yub Nub!">{{All-Stars|Yub Nub!}}</ref> In Chapter 317, Roger accidentally wreaked havoc in [[Takodana Castle]] when he lost control of his [[rocket pack]].<ref name="What Goes Up" /> In Chapter 482, Roger helped the Freemakers repair [[Lieutenant]] [[Colvett Valeria]]'s [[T-47 airspeeder]] during the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="The Power at Jakku" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{SeeAlso|From Trenches to Wrenches: The Roger Story}}
From Trenches to Wrenches: The Roger Story was first mentioned in the [[Zander's Joyride|third episode]] of the TV series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''.<ref name="Zander's Joyride" /> Several chapters from Roger's story would later be told as flashbacks in eight short episodes, which were later compiled as the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: All-Stars]]'' episode "[[From Trenches to Wrenches: The Roger Story (episode)|From Trenches to Wrenches]]," which premiered on [[November 10]], [[2018]].<ref name="From Trenches to Wrenches">{{All-Stars|From Trenches to Wrenches: The Roger Story (episode)}}</ref>
