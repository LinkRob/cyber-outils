'''Frog-dogs''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] of lizard that, at first glance, were usually thought to be non-sentient<ref name="Alien Archive" /> and were often mistaken for pests.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> They often used this fact to their advantage, allowing prosperous masters to keep them as pets to secure themselves shelter and a constant supply of [[Foodstuff|food]]. They were often ignored, and could therefore sneak unnoticed into palaces or [[Starship|ships]] and complete secretive tasks.<ref name="Alien Archive" /> The [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] kept the Frog-dog known as [[Buboicullaar]] as a pet, despite his sentience, and used him to [[spy]] on his many courtiers.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> Buboicullaar protected the Hutt from many assassination attempts.<ref name="Alien Archive" /> Frog-dogs like Bubo could pull 119 kilograms, or 262 pounds, with their tongue.<ref name="AEYNTK" />
