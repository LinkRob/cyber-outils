'''Flitterbats''' were a type of [[bat]] with broad [[Limb|wings]]. They could be found on the [[ecumenopolis]] [[Coruscant]] and in the first [[Standard year|year]] of the Empire's [[Imperial Era|reign]] after its [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]]<ref name="MoF" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] saw several broad-winged creatures that she thought might be flitterbats clinging to the underside of a pedestrian bridge in the [[Federal District]] of the [[planet]] and tucking themselves away for [[Local day|morning]]. Moments later, Mothma caused the detonation of a [[speeder]] near the bridge in order to [[Death|kill]] the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[assassin]] [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]]. As the blast knocked her to the ground, her final thoughts before falling unconcious were hopes that she had caused the explosion swiftly enough to spare the flitterbats and pedestrians on the bridge from its effects.<ref name="MoF" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], flitterbats were mentioned and potentially appeared in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', which was written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first instalment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" /> The [[Flitterbat/Legends|bats]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they appeared in "[[Lure of the Lost]]," a [[2015]] bonus adventure for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] that was written by [[Jordan Goldfarb]], with [[Daniel Lovat Clark]] and [[Chris Gerber]].<ref name="LotL">{{FFG|story=Lure of the Lost|book=Star Wars: Force and Destiny}}</ref>
