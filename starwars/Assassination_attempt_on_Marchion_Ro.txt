In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> an '''assassination attempt''' against [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] was carried out. The attempt was unsuccessful, but resulted in the [[death]] of [[Arathab Fal]]&mdash;a member of<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> Ro's loyal [[bodyguard]]s<ref name="Eye of Darkness">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref> known as the [[She'ar]]. As a result of Fal's death, the Eye began to see less value in the group. The event eventually became known to the [[Nihil]] [[spy]] [[Nan Hague|Nan]] and the Nihil [[Minister of Information (Nihil)|Minister of Information]] [[Ghirra Starros]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

In 228 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> when Starros questioned Nan about Ro's location, the spy informed her that she did not know. The [[minister]] then asked Nan if he had taken any of the She'ar with him, to which the spy told Starros of the Eye's waning opinion of the group after Fal's death. The minister then recalled the assassination attempt and mentally considered that a lot of her problems would be solved if Ro managed to have himself killed.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

==Behind the scenes==
The assassination attempt on Marchion Ro was first mentioned in the novel ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]] on [[June 11]], [[2024]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref> The event later appeared in ''[[The High Republic Adventures: Crash and Burn]]'', a one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] published as part of ''The High Republic''{{'s}} Phase III. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Nick Brokenshire]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Crash and Burn" /> on [[September 4]] of the same year.<ref name="Phase" />
