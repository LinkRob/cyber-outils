'''''Doctor Aphra'' 28''' is the twenty-eighth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Simon Spurrier]], illustrated by [[Emilio Laiso]], and published on [[January 30]], [[2019]] by [[Marvel Comics]]. The issue contains the story "'''Worst Among Equals Part III'''," in which [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] and [[0-0-0|Triple-Zero]] are pursued by the [[Death|undead]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Tam Posla]], the [[Hunting|hunters]] [[Winloss]] and [[Nokk]], and the population of [[Milvayne]].

==Plot summary==
In the underworld of [[Milvayne City]], Dr. [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and [[0-0-0|Triple-Zero]] are confronted by the reanimated bounty hunter [[Tam Posla]] and a hostile, newly reassembled [[BT-1]]. Their escape is complicated by the sudden arrival of monster hunters [[Winloss]] and [[Nokk]], who have a score to settle with Aphra. They use the ensuing chaos, as the hunters engage BT-1, to flee, keenly aware of the ticking clock on the bombs implanted within them. Unseen, the sadistic [[Cornelius Evazan]] monitors the events. He decides to broadcast the chase across the entire city, turning Aphra and Triple-Zero into public fugitives with a bounty on their heads.

The duo is briefly rescued by the scavenger girl [[Vulaada Klam]], who arrives on a smog-collecting [[bloatbarge]]. During a moment of reflection, Aphra admits to Vulaada her own selfish, survival-at-all-costs nature, revealing her vulnerability as she looks at a hologram of her lost love, [[Magna Tolvan]]. The respite is short-lived as the undead Posla and his soldiers corner them. Triple-Zero predictably takes Vulaada hostage, but the plan backfires. Motivated by the city-wide bounty, Vulaada shoots Triple-Zero to free herself, declares her intention to collect the reward, and knocks Aphra unconscious.
