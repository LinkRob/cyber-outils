A '''civilian robe''' was worn by civilian<ref name="Day" /> allies<ref name="Bazil">{{Databank|bazil|Bazil}}</ref> of the [[Jedi Order]] on field missions during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Day" />

==Design==
The civilian robe was a set of fatigues<ref name="Costumes Feature">{{SW|url=news/dressing-the-acolyte-costumes-feature|text=Dressing ''The Acolyte'': The Stranger and an Assassin Hunt the Jedi of the High Republic &mdash; Updated}}</ref> that consisted of a frumpy<ref name="Day AD">{{Acolyte|Day (episode)}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> quilted poncho with a kangaroo pocket<ref name="Costumes Feature" /> over a long-sleeved shirt and trousers.<ref name="Day" />

==History==
In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> the [[Tynnan]] tracker [[Bazil]] and the [[human]] ex-[[Padawan]] [[Verosha Aniseya]] both donned civilian robes when accompanying a [[Jedi]] team on a [[Mission to Khofar|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Khofar]]. Bazil wore his fatigues with a helmet and a visor that could drop down to cover his eyes, while Verosha wore her own belt over the poncho with a pouch to carry her droid [[Pip]], as well as wearing her own [[backpack]].<ref name="Day" /> Verosha's [[twin]] sister [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] would later steal her robe in order to disguise as her.<ref name="Night">{{Acolyte|Night}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The civilian robe first appeared in "[[Day (episode)|Day]]," the fourth episode of the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''.<ref name="Day" />
