'''Dasoor''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> located in the [[Dasoor system]] in the [[Agarix sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was known as a world overrun by [[Thief|thieves]], and gangs of [[enslaver]]s. Some months after the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] and his [[Wookiee]] co-[[pilot]] [[Chewbacca]] were headed to this planet, before changing course to the Wookiee's [[Homeworld|home planet]] of [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="Aftermath" />
