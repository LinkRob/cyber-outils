An '''arc array''' was a device that was composed of nodes, gates, [[actuator]]s, and [[vibration crystal]]s.<ref name="Aphra 21" /> Around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{Aphra2016Date|21}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Captain-Inspector]] [[Magna Tolvan]] pretended to interrogate the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] while informing the latter of a rescue plan to help her flee the [[wreckage-prison]] [[Accresker Jail]]. During their conversation, Tolvan made Aphra recount the serial composition of an arc array.<ref name="Aphra 21" />

==Behind the scenes==
An arc array was mentioned in the [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 21|twenty-first issue]] of the [[2016]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Simon Spurrier]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 21" /> on [[June 27]], [[2018]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/69595/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_21|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #21}}</ref>
