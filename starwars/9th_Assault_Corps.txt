The '''9th Assault Corps''' was a [[corps]] within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] that was active during the [[Clone Wars]] and was under the command of [[Clone Commander]] [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]]. The [[41st Elite Corps]], at [[time]]s referred to as the 41st Elite Legion, was organized under the 9th Assault Corps. [[Jedi General]] [[Luminara Unduli]] repeatedly led the 9th Assault Corps' 41st Elite thanks to her composure and diplomacy. Together, [[General]] Unduli and [[Commander]] Gree traveled on long journeys to distant worlds, and aided vulnerable [[species]] on several [[planet]]s against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] forces that invaded.<ref name="Mace Windu" />
