{{Quote|The air trembles with the whine of their engines. It's like an approaching storm of can-cells. And one of them has a stinger I'm especially worried about.|[[Brander Lawson]], on [[podracer]]s|<ref name="Shadow of Maul 4">[[Shadow of Maul 4|''Shadow of Maul'' 4]]</ref>}}
'''Can-cells''' were a [[species]] of flying [[insect]] found of a number of [[planet]]s across [[the galaxy]], including [[Batuu]],<ref name="guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Kashyyyk]],<ref name="ROTS" /> [[Aleen]],<ref name="MM">{{TCW|Mercy Mission (episode)}}</ref> [[Ryloth]],<ref name="IOR">{{TCW|Innocents of Ryloth}}</ref> [[Rodia]],<ref name="BJ">{{TCW|Bombad Jedi}}</ref> [[Taul]],<ref name="C-3PO">[[Star Wars Special: C-3PO 1|''Star Wars Special: C-3PO'' 1]]</ref> and [[Teth]].<ref name="TCWF">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> They had quad wings and large [[eye]]s,<ref name="guide" /> and were capable of growing to over three [[meter]]s in length.<ref name="VE" />

While watching the [[Star Gauntlet]] [[Podracing|podrace]] on [[Janix]], [[Brander Lawson]] compared the approaching [[podracer]]s to a storm of can-cells, and referred to one, [[Tor Braxis]], as having a worrisome stinger.<ref name="Shadow of Maul 4" /> The [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Wookiee]]s of Kashyyyk believed seeing a can-cell brought the viewer good luck and based the design of their [[Raddaugh Gnasp fluttercraft|''Raddaugh Gnasp'' fluttercraft]] on the odd-looking creatures. Can-cell were attracted to the buzzing sound made by Wookiees' [[Oevvaor jet catamaran|Oevvaor Jet Catamarans]] and would often congregate at landing pads where the vehicles could be found.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> Some residents of the planet Batuu raised native can-cell to become pets or for other reasons.<ref name="GEStaff">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] has official backstory elements that, though not directly published for the public, are officially canon details that can be obtained verbally from cast members working in the land.</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]] rode one on Teth to escape [[Asajj Ventress]] and reach the ''[[Twilight]]''.<ref name="TCWF" />
