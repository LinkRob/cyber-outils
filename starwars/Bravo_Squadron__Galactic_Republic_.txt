'''Bravo Squadron''' was a [[Grand Army of the Republic]] [[squadron]] that served in the [[Clone Wars]] under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]. [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[General]] [[Whorm Loathsom]], who was increasingly frustrated by the strong [[Galactic Republic]] resistance during the [[Battle of Christophsis]], set up a [[deflector shield]] in anticipation for his next attack, and sent his [[Whorm Loathsom's army|army]] in two groups. One group attacked [[Chaleydonia]] from the south while another group attacked from the east. [[High Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] managed to repel [[droid]] forces from the south, but Skywalker's [[Bravo Squadron (Republic)|Bravo Squadron]] were unable to stop the Separatist advance.<ref name="The Separatists" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bravo Squadron was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2022]] [[De Agostini]] [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] magazine "[[The Separatists (reference book)|The Separatists]]."<ref name="The Separatists" /> Bravo Squadron was previously introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as [[Bravo Squad (Galactic Republic)|Bravo Squad]], which was mentioned in the Nintendo DS version of the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Republic Heroes]]''. The game launched on [[October 9]], [[2009]].<ref name="Republic Heroes">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Republic Heroes]]''</ref>
