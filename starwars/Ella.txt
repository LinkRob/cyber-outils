'''Ella'''<ref name="Witness" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> [[stormtrooper]] in the [[501st Legion]],<ref name="Witness" /> the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] personal [[legion]].<ref name="BTC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> The [[trooper]] served in a [[Deena Lorn's fireteam|fireteam]] alongside fellow stormtroopers [[Riccarn]], [[Tig]], [[Xander]] and [[Deena Lorn]]. The fireteam members made it through many missions together, and were thus assigned with a [[Deena's squad|squad]] aboard<ref name="Witness" /> the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> ''[[Executor]]''.<ref name="Witness" />

Aboard the [[Super Star Destroyer]], Ella spent some nights with their [[squad]]mates, aside from trooper [[FS-451]], sat in a corner in the [[destroyer]]'s mess hall, notably sipping [[alcohol]] illegally distilled by Xander and wondering why 451 was still only a stormtrooper.<ref name="Witness" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[The Witness]]" coincides with the events of {{Film|V}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> when the ''Executor'' traveled to the [[tibanna]] [[mining]] colony [[Cloud City]], Ella, Riccarn and Xander remained stationed on the [[Starship|vessel]] while Lorn, Tig and FS-451 went down to the [[city]] with Vader.<ref name="Witness" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ella was mentioned in the short story "[[The Witness]]," which was written by [[Adam Christopher]] and released as part of the [[2020]] short story collection ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="Witness" />
