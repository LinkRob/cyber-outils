An '''atmospheric fighter''', or '''air superiority fighter''', was a [[starfighter]] variant designed specifically for flight within the [[atmosphere]] of a [[planet]]. The [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter]], or TIE striker, was a notable example.

==Description==
An atmospheric fighter was best deployed in low to high atmosphere, although was capable of suborbital flight. The [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter|TIE striker]] was streamlined for air superiority, featuring a solid stabilizing mass in its central hull, framed by tilting wings where a standard [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]'s large hexagonal panels were vulnerable to crosswinds, making the vessels difficult to steer outside of a vacuum. The TIE striker was fitted with [[twin ion engine]]s for forward thrust, although also utilized specialized [[repulsorlift]]s for its atmospheric operations.<ref name="RO UVG" /> The TIE striker was designed for atmospheric patrols over [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] ground installations.<ref name="TIE striker DB">{{Databank|tie-striker|TIE striker}}</ref>
