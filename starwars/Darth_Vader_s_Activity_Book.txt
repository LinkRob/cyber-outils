'''''Darth Vader's Activity Book''''' is a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] activity book that was written by [[James Razzi]] and illustrated by [[Patricia Wynne]]. It was published by [[Random House]] in [[March]], [[1979]] alongside ''[[Artoo Detoo's Activity Book]]'', ''[[Chewbacca's Activity Book]]'', and ''[[Luke Skywalker's Activity Book]]''. It was one of the few works licensed by the short-lived [[Lucasfilm]] subsidiary [[Black Falcon]]

==Publisher's summary==
''Join forces against the evil Darth Vader! See if you can crack his secret code, escape from his tricky mazes &mdash; and destroy the Imperial Battle Station!''

''Blast off for hours of ''Star Wars'' fun with these and other exciting games, puzzles, and out-of-this-world activities.''
