The '''BX-series sniper rifle''' was a type of [[sniper rifle]] used by [[BX-series droid commando|BX-series droid commandos]]. In [[21 BBY]], during the [[Battle of Saleucami]], [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] was sniped while leading his men through the [[Saleucami]] wetlands.<ref name="The Deserter" /> A [[Unidentified Death Watch assassin|Death Watch assassin]] sent by [[Pre Vizsla]], the leader of the [[Death Watch]], used a BX-series sniper rifle to assassinate [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]]. Although the weapon was destroyed by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], using [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]] to cut the weapon in half.<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" />
