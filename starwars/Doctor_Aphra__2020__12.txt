'''''Doctor Aphra'' 12''' is the twelfth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[July 14]], [[2021]].

==Publisher's summary==
''"PARTY TRICKS" A MYSTERIOUS INVITATION leads DOCTOR APHRA and SANA STARROS to an exclusive party&mdash;teeming with scum and villainy! Will Aphra and Sana be able to survive the underworld social event of the century? Or will their cover be blown by A FAMILIAR FACE with an agenda of their own?''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=War of the Bounty Hunters PARTY TRICKS
|text=Rogue archeologist Doctor Aphra and smuggler Sana Starros are working for Domina Tagge. The job: infiltrate

Meanwhile, Just Lucky and Ariole Yu's old mentor has betrayed the Sixth Kin syndicate, and
they've been tasked with assassinating him right under Crimson Dawn's nose.

With all of these crime syndicates and gangsters in one place, what could possibly go wrong?}}
