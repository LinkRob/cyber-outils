'''Chorgo''' was an individual that was on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During which Chorgo approached a [[Rodian]] [[gambler]] named [[Grrikke]] multiple times to ask for [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. Grrikke later mentioned the interaction<ref name="Outlaws" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> while playing [[Kessel Sabacc]] with a [[Quarren]] named [[Kurluk]] and a [[human]] named [[Markin]] at a [[Hutt Cardroom|cardroom]] in the [[Hutt Cartel District (Mos Eisley)|Hutt Cartel district]] of the [[city]] of [[Mos Eisley]] on Tatooine.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chorgo was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They were mentioned in ambient dialogue that players could overhear while at the cardroom.<ref name="Outlaws" />
