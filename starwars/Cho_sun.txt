'''''Cho sun''''' was a [[lightsaber combat]] technique that could be used to disable and disarm enemies in battle.<ref name="JMM2">[[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 2|''Star Wars Jedi Master'' Magazine 2]]</ref> Around [[52 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' states that [[Dooku]] was born in [[102 BBY]]. ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' states that [[Dooku]] was about fifty [[Standard year|years]] old when he taught ''cho sun'' to a group of [[Jedi Initiate]]s, placing the event around [[52 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]] trained a group of [[Jedi Initiate]]s to perform the technique against a [[Hologram|holographic]] opponent.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cho sun first appeared in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in a flashback sequence in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, ''[[Cho sun/Legends|cho sun]]'' was the specific technique of [[Dismemberment/Legends|cutting off]] an opponent's weapon arm.<ref name="Jedi vs. Sith">[[Jedi vs. Sith: The Essential Guide to the Force (real-life book)|''Jedi vs. Sith: The Essential Guide to the Force'']]</ref>
