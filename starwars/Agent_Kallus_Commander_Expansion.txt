The '''Agent Kallus Commander Expansion''' ('''SWL80''') is an expansion to the ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' [[infantry]] game by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] that adds [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]] as an [[officer]]. It was released on [[March 19]], [[2021]].<ref name="Hunt Continues" />

==Publisher's summary==
===Online summary===
''Hunt your enemies to the far ends of the galaxy with the Agent Kallus Commander Expansion for Star Wars: Legion! Ruthless and cunning, Imperial Security Bureau agent Alensandr Kallus will stop at nothing to suppress dissent and bring order to the galaxy. Now, he can lead your Imperial forces as they hunt any rebel cells.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

''Within this expansion, you'll find a finely detailed, unpainted hard plastic Agent Kallus miniature that you can customize to fit your own style. Beyond this, seven upgrade cards—including his J-19 Bo-Rifle—invite you to outfit Agent Kallus for battle, while his three signature command cards help you explore new strategies as you seek to quell rebellion.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

===Back blurb summary===
''This expansion for STAR WARS: LEGION contains 1 finely sculpted Agent Kallus miniature, enough for one commander unit, along with new unit, upgrade, and command cards to field in battle.''<ref name="Legion Expansion">{{Legion|set=Agent Kallus Commander Expansion}}</ref>

==Contents==
*1 [[Alexsandr Kallus|Agent Kallus]] Miniature
*1 Unit Card
*7 Upgrade Cards
*3 Command Cards
