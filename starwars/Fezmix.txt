'''Fezmix''' was a [[Bonbrak]] [[Gender|male]] who worked with the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] in his [[Ram Jomaram's garage|garage]] in [[Lonisa City]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Jomaram considered him to be rowdy.<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fezmix first appeared in the novel ''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]''.<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower" /> The character was named "Femix" in a preview of the book published in ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]''.<ref name="Preview">''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]''</ref>
