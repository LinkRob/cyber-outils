"'''Among the Clouds: An Audience with Billy Dee Williams'''" is an article which appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 38|''Star Wars Insider'' 38]]. It was written by [[Scott Chernoff]] and focused on the acting career of [[Billy Dee Williams]], who played [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando Calrissian]], and spoke about his feelings on being a part of ''[[Star Wars]]''.
