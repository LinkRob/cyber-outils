'''''Bounty Hunt''''' is a children's activity book that follows the [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] as he pursues a fugitive on [[Tatooine]]. It was first published as "What's Inside" by [[Egmont]] UK Ltd in the United Kingdom on [[April 7]], [[2016]]. [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] released a United States edition on [[June 13]], [[2017]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Lift the flaps to explore the ''Star Wars'' universe! Follow Bossk, the famous bounty hunter, in his pursuit of a fugitive on Tatooine. Discover all about this world and its famous locations, creatures and secrets. On each spread there are various flaps to lift to reveal new information, and find clues to help Bossk find his target.''

==Continuity==
The events of ''Bounty Hunt'' takes place [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{BountyHuntDate}}</ref>
