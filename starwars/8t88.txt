'''8t88''' was a [[Color|silver]]-plated, [[humanoid]]-shaped [[mercenary]] [[droid]] with red, blue, and white sensors. Residing on<ref name="8t88" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="SWbook">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> [[Nar Shaddaa]], the droid was willing to negotiate changing loyalties<ref name="8t88" />&mdash;at some point, 8t88 double-crossed a [[human]] and threatened them with a [[DL-44 heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="DoubleCross" />

==Behind the scenes==
8t88 was introduced to the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] by the [[2018]] ''[[Promise of Power]]'' expansion for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Card Game]]'' with an identifying unit card<ref name="8t88" /> as well as an objective card;<ref name="DoubleCross" /> both were illustrated by [[Martin de Diego Sádaba]].<ref name="8t88" /><ref name="DoubleCross" /> [[8t88/Legends|8t88]] originated in the [[1997]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]''. Voiced by [[Denny Delk]], he was one of several main villains.<ref name="JKDF2">''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]''</ref>
