The '''Core Founders''' were a group of twenty-two [[Planet|worlds]],<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> all located in the [[Core Worlds]] region, which founded the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> around [[25,000 BBY]].<ref name="Date 1">''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' dates the founding of the [[Galactic Republic]] to around 25,000 [[Standard year|years]] before the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], which corresponds to circa [[25,000 BBY]] according to the [['ABY-BBY' dating system|dating system]] used by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> Founder worlds included [[Alderaan]],<ref name="Rising Storm" /> [[Chandrila]],<ref name="HC9">{{HelmetCollectionCite|9|Databank A-Z}}</ref> [[Corellia]], [[Coruscant]], and [[Duro]].<ref name="Inside Intel">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-inside-intel-coruscant|text=''Star Wars'' Inside Intel: Coruscant|int=Star Wars Inside Intel}}</ref> The [[Avenue of the Core Founders]] in front of the [[Imperial Senate Building|Galactic Senate Building]] on Coruscant, the Republic's [[capital]], was named after the group and lined with statues representing the worlds.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref>

The [[Unity Arc]], built for the [[Valo Republic Fair]]<ref name="Rising Storm" /> held in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> was an art display made to honor the Core Founders. It was composed of twenty-two floating spheres, each representing a different Core Founder. [[Jedi Master]] [[Harli Cogra]] mentioned the Unity Arc and the Core Founders in ''[[Chronicles of the Jedi]]'', a document that detailed the Jedi of the High Republic, written<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> in [[229 BBY]].<ref name="Chronicles date">{{ChroniclesoftheJediDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Core Founders were first mentioned indirectly in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']],<ref name="Complete Locations" /> which was published on [[September 27]], [[2016]].<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/545478/star-wars-complete-locations-by-dk|text=Star Wars: Complete Locations}}</ref> They were later identified in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="Propaganda" /> and published on [[October 25]] of that year.<ref name="Harper">{{Harper|url=products/star-wars-propaganda-pablo-hidalgo?variant=32210373345314|text=Star Wars Propaganda}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Core Founder/Legends|Core Founders]] were first mentioned in the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', written by [[Kristin Lund]].<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
