'''Aqualish Military Advisors''' were elite [[Aqualish]] military strategists from the [[planet]] [[Ando]]. Toward the end of the [[Galactic Civil War]], some of them journeyed to the [[Anoat sector]] to trade knowledge and tactics with [[Her Majesty]] and the [[Noble Court]]. After the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] established the [[Iron Blockade]], the Aqualish were trapped in the sector, and they became key assets in the struggle against the governor.<ref name="Aqualish Military Advisor">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Aqualish Military Advisor"</ref>
