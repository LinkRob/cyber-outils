'''Dianogan tea''' was a type of [[tea]]. The [[Muun]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Admiral]] [[Pors Tonith]] possessed a strong predilection for dianogan tea, despite the fact that it stained his [[Tooth|teeth]] a garish [[Color|purple]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dianogan tea was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> [[Dianogan tea/Legends|Dianogan tea]] first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[Jedi Trial]]'', a [[2004]] novel authored by [[David Sherman]] and [[Dan Cragg]].<ref name="Jedi Trial">''[[Jedi Trial]]''</ref>
