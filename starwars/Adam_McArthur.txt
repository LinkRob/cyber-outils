'''Adam McArthur''' is an actor who provided the voice of [[Lee-Char/Legends|Lee-Char]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref> McArthur is also known for his work in the anime industry, with notable roles including Yuji Itadori in Jujutsu Kaisen, Chifuyu Matsuno in Tokyo Revengers, and Koku Hanabata in My Hero Academia.
