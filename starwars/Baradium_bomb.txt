'''Baradium bombs''' also known as '''baradium charges''' were a type of explosive that utilized the compound [[baradium]]. Baradium bombs could be magnetically attached to surfaces.<ref name="Databank" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Val]] employed handmade [[baradium]] bombs during a [[coaxium]] [[Heist on Vandor|heist]] on [[Vandor]]. For the heist, Val keyed the bomb's [[detonator]] to her biological signature.<ref name="Databank" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Kay Vess]] was hired by the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] to infiltrate the [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s [[Pyke poaching station|poaching station]] on [[Toshara]], steal their client list and plant a baradium bomb on the station's generator, there by releasing the caged [[robuma]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baradium bombs first appearead though unidentified in the [[canon]] continuity in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]].''<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> They were not identified in tie-in baradium bomb entry on the ''[[StarWars.com]]'' [[Databank (2014-)|databank encyclopedia]].<ref name="Databank" />

Baradium bombs were originally introduced as part of the [[Star Wars Legends]] continuity in [[2003]] [[Shatterpoint (novel)|''Shatterpoint'']] novel by [[Matthew Stover]].<ref name="Shatterpoint">[[Shatterpoint (novel)|''Shatterpoint'']]</ref>
