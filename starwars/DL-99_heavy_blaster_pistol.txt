The '''DL-99 heavy blaster pistol''' was a type of [[blaster pistol]] in use in the galaxy by [[34 ABY]]. It was very similar to the [[DL-44 heavy blaster pistol]]. It was extremely powerful and accurate, but the power cell didn't contain many shots.<ref name="TALES">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge]]''</ref>
