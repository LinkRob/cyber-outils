In [[5 BBY]], the rebel [[VCX-100 light freighter]] the ''[[Ghost]]'' came under surprise '''attack''' by at least four [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s in a trap set by the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Kanan Jarrus]], one of the rebel leaders and a former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]], came up with a plan to attack what he and the [[Spectres]] believed was an Imperial [[Freighter|cargo]] [[convoy]]. In reality, it was a trap where the [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Starfighter|fighters]] were waiting for them. 

Outnumbered and sustaining damage from the remaining TIEs, Jarrus and rebel [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]] struggled to work together as a communication disruption aboard the ''Ghost'' prevented the two from effectively coordinating their responses to the attack. Their [[C1-series astromech droid|astromech]] [[droid]], [[C1-10P]]&mdash;or "Chopper"&mdash;ultimately saved the day by using the ''Ghost''{{'s}} [[VCX-series auxiliary starfighter|auxiliary ship]], the ''[[Phantom]]'', to destroy the remaining TIE fighters that Jarrus, who had already destroyed two of the fighters with the ''Ghost''{{'s}} [[turret]] [[cannon]], had been unable to finish off. This allowed the Spectres to escape and continue carrying out their activities, part of a growing insurgency against the Empire.

Sometime later, the ''Ghost'' traveled to [[Osisis Station]] for repairs, where Syndulla was forced into a [[Fool's Run|high-stakes race]] against [[Space station|station]] owner [[Galus Vez]] in the hopes of securing the parts needed to repair the damage from the attack. With help from Jarrus and his new Padawan, [[Ezra Bridger]], she won the race and secured the parts needed for the [[Starship|ship's]] repairs.

==The attack==
{{Quote|As I recall, raiding an Imperial convoy was your plan, love.''"<br />"''Well, it made sense at the time.|Syndulla and Jarrus|<ref name="Ghost Machine" />}}
In 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> after a long day of causing chaos against Imperial forces,<ref name="Short Story Rebels Annual">{{StoryCite|story=The Machine in the Ghost (short story)|stext=The Machine in the ''Ghost''|book=Star Wars Rebels Annual 2015}}</ref> the Spectres' [[C1-series astromech droid]] [[C1-10P]], nicknamed "Chopper," deciphered what the ''Ghost'' crew believed was [[Imperial Military]] communications chatter that [[Freighter|cargo ships]] near the ''Ghost'' were transporting [[mineral]]s used to build the Imperial war machine. Not content to ignore an opportunity that could strike a blow against the Empire, Jarrus devised a plan in which he, Syndulla, and Chopper would take the ''Ghost'' on a raid against the supply [[convoy]] in the Outer Rim.<ref name="Rise of the Rebels" />

[[File:Imperial_convoy_raid.png|thumb|300px|left|The ''Ghost'' being pursued by TIE fighters during the surprise attack.]]
In reality, there was no convoy&mdash;the entire operation was a trap to lure unsuspecting rebels into the hands of the Empire. At least four [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s were waiting for the ''Ghost'' when it arrived at the coordinates.<ref name="Rise of the Rebels" /> A [[Starfighter combat|dogfight]] ensued, during which Syndulla piloted the [[Starship|ship]] while Jarrus manned<ref name="Ghost Machine" /> the dorsal<ref name="Short Story Rebels Annual" /> [[turret]] [[cannon]]. During the fight, the [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Starfighter|fighters]] gained the upper hand over the ''Ghost'' crew and managed to inflict damage on the ship's [[deflector shield]]. Despite the damage, as the pursuit progressed, Jarrus managed to shoot down two of the TIE fighters.<ref name="Ghost Machine" />

Syndulla ordered Chopper, who was in the [[cockpit]] with her<ref name="Ghost Machine" /> and unappreciative of her impatience,<ref name="Short Story Rebels Annual" /> to repair the deflector shield, while the TIEs continued their assault and knocked out the ''Ghost''{{'s}} [[Intercom|communication system]]. Unable to contact Jarrus, she redirected<ref name="Ghost Machine" /> a grumbling<ref name="Short Story Rebels Annual" /> Chopper to restore the comms instead and to tell Jarrus to improve his accuracy against the TIE fighters. When Chopper relayed this to Jarrus, the Jedi gave a contradictory order, instructing the droid to continue repairing the shields and to tell Syndulla to "fly better."<ref name="Ghost Machine" /> Chopper, annoyed at being bossed around,<ref name="Short Story Rebels Annual" /> relayed what Jarrus said. Frustrated with Jarrus' contradiction, Syndulla took matters into her own hands, maneuvering the ship to destroy one of the two remaining TIE fighters. She then ordered Chopper to tell Jarrus to take out the last fighter. Frustrated with the back-and-forth, Chopper instead piloted the ''Ghost''{{'s}} [[VCX-series auxiliary starfighter|auxiliary ship]], the ''[[Phantom]]'', and used its [[laser cannon]]s to eliminate the final TIE fighter.<ref name="Ghost Machine" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|If you flew a little bit better we wouldn't have gotten damaged in the first place."<br />"Really? So says the guy whose idea it was to raid an Imperial supply yard and who couldn't hit a TIE fighter ten meters away."<br />"Hey, I got them all&hellip;eventually.|Kanan Jarrus and Hera Syndulla discuss the attack on the ''Ghost''|<ref name="Ring Race">{{RebelsMagCite|1|Ring Race}}</ref>}}
The defeat of the TIE fighters lowered the tension aboard the ''Ghost'', where Syndulla and Jarrus complimented one another for destroying three of the fighters.<ref name="Ghost Machine" /> Chopper, who returned to the ship, was furious that he was not initially thanked as well,<ref name="Short Story Rebels Annual" /> but the two rebels finally thanked him for taking out the last fighter.<ref name="Ghost Machine" /> Syndulla instructed a stunned<ref name="Short Story Rebels Annual" /> Chopper to repair the deflector shields and communication systems as the ''Ghost'' jumped into [[hyperspace]], on their way to continue their resistance against the Empire.<ref name="Ghost Machine" />

Later that year,<ref name="Chronology Note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Ring Race]]" to [[5 BBY]].</ref> the crew of the ''Ghost''&mdash;which had [[Mission in Capital City|come to include]] [[Ezra Bridger]],<ref name="Ring Race" /> a [[Force-sensitive]] orphan from Lothal who became Jarrus' Padawan<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref>&mdash;headed for [[Osisis Station]], a [[space station]] in the [[Regani sector]] owned by a [[Besalisk]] parts dealer named [[Galus Vez]].<ref name="Ring Race" /> Although the ''Ghost'' had taken Imperial fire on other missions,<ref name="Spark of Rebellion" /> their travel to Osisis Station was intended to repair damage suffered during the TIE fighter trap<ref name="Fisher Twitter">{{Twitter|tronprogram2|status/590414161550639104|[[Martin Fisher]]|quote=yes it is :) It was the only reference I had for the show when I wrote the script.|archivefile=File:Martin Fisher supply convoy raid.png}}</ref>&mdash;an encounter that Syndulla, who referred to the intended target as a supply yard, and Jarrus continued to lightly banter back and forth about.<ref name="Ring Race" />

[[File:Splendor_cockpit.png|thumb|right|300px|Galus Vez, aboard the ''Splendour'', races Syndulla and the ''Ghost''.]]
By the time the Spectres arrived at the station, previous encounters with the rebels meant that the Empire had doubled its inspections on Osisis Station. Thus, Vez made the rebels a deal: Syndulla would have to race him in an event called the [[Fool's Run]]. If Vez won, he would take the ''Ghost''. If Syndulla won, however, then the crew could take the parts they needed to repair the ship. The two began the race, but Vez used a [[Engine|thruster]] on an [[asteroid]] to damage the ''Ghost''. Syndulla won the race with help from Jarrus and Bridger, both of whom [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|knock]] an asteroid towards Vez's [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|Gauntlet fighter]], the ''[[Splendour]]''. Vez attempted to attack them for their victory, but the rebels fought back and overwhelmed Vez and his forces. As a result, Vez gave the crew the parts they needed to repair the ''Ghost''.<ref name="Ring Race" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:MachineintheGhost.png|thumb|300px|left|Chopper was the central character of "The Machine in the Ghost"]]
The attack on the ''Ghost'' was the central plot of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' animated television short "[[The Machine in the Ghost]],"<ref name="Ghost Machine" /> which premiered on [[August 11]], [[2014]].<ref name="Insider 156">{{InsiderCite|156|Star Wars Rebels Recounted|''Star Wars Rebels'' Recounted}}</ref> It first appeared in the young readers book ''[[Chopper Saves the Day]]'' by [[Elizabeth Schaefer]]<ref name="Chopper Saves the Day">''[[Chopper Saves the Day]]''</ref> and the [[Star Wars Rebels chapter books|chapter book]] ''[[Rise of the Rebels]]'' by [[Michael Kogge]],<ref name="Rise of the Rebels" /> which were both released on August 5, 2014, ahead of the short's premiere.<ref name="ChopperDate">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/810179/star-wars-rebels-chopper-saves-the-day-by-elizabeth-a-schaefer|text=Star Wars Rebels: Chopper Saves the Day}}</ref><ref name="Disney">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-rebels-rise-of-the-rebels|text=Star Wars Rebels: Rise of the Rebels}}</ref> The ''Rebels'' short, the first in a four-part series, was part of the lead-up to the [[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion|series premiere]] of ''Star Wars Rebels'' on [[September 26]], 2014.<ref name="Insider 156" /> Storyboards as well as footage from the short and the attack first appeared in the promotional video "Meet Chopper, Grumpy Astromech Droid," which introduced the character Chopper on [[January 28]], 2014.<ref name="Meet Chopper">{{SWYouTube|ulIPyDqppfM|Meet Chopper, Grumpy Astromech Droid &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/star-wars-rebels-meet-chopper-grumpy-astromech-droid}}</ref> 

The attack on the ''Ghost'' was later referenced in "[[Ring Race]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Martin Fisher]]<ref name="Ring Race" /> and published on January 28, [[2015]]<ref name="Release">{{Egmont|domain=co.uk|url=egmont-publishing-launch-star-wars-rebels|text=EGMONT PUBLISHING LAUNCH STAR WARS REBELS}}</ref> in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 1|first issue]] of [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]].<ref name="Ring Race" /> When asked on Twitter whether the raid on an Imperial supply yard mentioned in the story was the same as the raid referenced in "The Machine in the Ghost," Fisher stated that the two events were indeed the same and that the event from the short was the only reference he had for the animated series when he wrote the script for the comic.<ref name="Fisher Twitter" />
