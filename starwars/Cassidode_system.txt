The '''Cassidode system''' was a [[star system]] in [[Aparo sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="Slice" /> It fell within grid square R-3 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained [[Cassidode VI]], a terrestrial astronomical object with a tiny, ill-supplied [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Cassidode VI rebel outpost|outpost]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Farns Monsbee]], a [[human]] [[rebel pilot]], served as the sole [[Snub-fighter|snubfighter]] [[pilot]] at the base before transferring to [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]] at some point prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]]<ref name="ROUVG" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Cassidode system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025.
