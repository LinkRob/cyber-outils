'''''Concert Solo''''' was a [[Painting|poster]] used to promote a series of concerts by a [[Unidentified Bith musician (concert solo)|Bith musician]] on [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It showed the [[musician]] performing against a luminous background, with the caption at the bottom written in [[Aurebesh]]. The poster was put up in [[Terminal 24]], in and outside [[Moshi Bar]],<ref name="LT" /> as digital billboards featured on [[hover taxi]]s,<ref name="LT" /><ref name="Catch" /> in an [[Unidentified Coruscant cantina|unidentified cantina]],<ref name="Pursuit of Peace">{{TCW|Pursuit of Peace}}</ref> on a [[holobooth]] on the [[corner of twelfth and G]] on [[Level 1312]],<ref name="Catch">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref><ref name="Wrong" /> and on a wall on Level 1312.<ref name="Wrong">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref>

Shortly after the war ended, a copy of the poster was seen in [[Ord Mantell City]] on the planet [[Ord Mantell]].<ref name="Infested">{{TBB|Infested}}</ref>

Several [[Standard year|years]] later, when [[Ezra Bridger]] visited the [[Lothal Jedi Temple]], he had a vision of his quarters aboard the ''[[Ghost]]'', where the poster was lying on the floor.<ref name="Path">{{Rebels|Path of the Jedi}}</ref> It was also lying there later, when Bridger entered his quarters for real.<ref name="Steps">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>
