The '''Fear Engine''' was a device used by the [[Nihil]] [[Children of the Storm|Child of the Storm]] [[Sister (Children of the Storm)|Sister]] [[H'Tar]]<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 2" /> that boosted her [[Fallanassi]] [[Force power|Force abilities]] and caused individuals within its range to see illusions of their fears.<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 4" /> H'Tar deployed the Fear Engine in the Nihil-Occupied [[Theed Royal Palace]] on [[Naboo]] when members of the [[Tempest Breakers]] guild attempted to infiltrate it.<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 2" /> H'Tar used another Fear Engine, equipped aboard the Nihil [[starship]] ''[[Squall Spider]]'' during the [[Battle of Eriadu]].<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 4" /> 

Use of the Fear Engine by H'Tar was compulsory; despite the immense pain it inflicted upon her, she was physically unable to extricate herself from it nor able to stop it from casting nightmares once activated. While connected to the Fear Engine, H'Tar maintained some autonomy, however, using it and her powers to [[Force projection|project]] an apparition of herself onto the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam]] ''[[Gios]]'' to ask for former Child of the Storm [[Cibaba]] to help her escape.<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 5">[[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 5|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 5]]</ref>
