The '''Baroli system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Baroli sector]]. It was situated in grid square N-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Baroli]].<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> The [[Hydian Way]]<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> [[super-hyperroute]]<ref name="Build the Millennium Falcon 29">{{BuildFalconCite|29|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> connected the Baroli system to the [[Chardaan system|Chardaan]] and [[Pax system]]s.<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" />

The system was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Barolian]] [[species]], while Baroli was the [[homeworld]] of the Barolians [[Jano Brayson]] and [[Teff]], who had become [[pilot]]s in the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> by [[231 BBY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'' establishes [[Jano Brayson]] and [[Teff]] had become [[pilot]]s in the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] by the time of the [[Battle of Galov]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[231 BBY]].</ref> the two reunited on the Republic [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]]<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Eiram system]],<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> with Teff commenting that they were a long way from Baroli.<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Baroli system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' on a [[The galaxy|galaxy]] map authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Christopher Ibbitt]],<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> which was released on [[June 17]], [[2025]].<ref name="Edelweiss">{{DK|url=us/book/9780593958605-star-wars-the-acolyte-visual-guide|text=Star Wars The Acolyte Visual Guide}}</ref> The system was identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]] of the same year&mdash;with both works being authored by Fry.<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Baroli system/Legends|Baroli system]] was introduced in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
