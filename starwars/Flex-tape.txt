'''Flex-tape''' was a material used to fix [[starship]]s.<ref name="Quest" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">Flex-tape's mention in ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' takes place in between the [[Battle of Jedha]] and the [[Night of Sorrow]], both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[human]] [[prospector]] [[Dass Leffbruk]] felt that his [[Spence Leffbruk|father's]] starship, the ''[[Silverstreak]]'', was held together by flex-tape and hope.<ref name="Quest" />
