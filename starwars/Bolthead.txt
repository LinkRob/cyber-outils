'''Bolthead''' was a small hideout built into a cliff on the southern slopes of [[Lake Amari]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[trans-accelerator]] was stored at the hideout.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During the same year,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] used her [[ion blaster]] to open the hideout and steal the trans-accelerator.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bolthead first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. It contained a key item used to upgrade the player's [[blaster]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
