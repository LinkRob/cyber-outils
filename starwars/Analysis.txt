'''Analysis''' was a branch within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Intelligence]] organization. Analysis was responsible for surveying and interpreting an abundance of surveillance data and information acquired [[Standard day|daily]]. Among the data, it monitored [[media]] transmissions, verified communication channels, broke encryptions, and assessed enemy equipment and techniques.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Analysis was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying]] supplement ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Dawn" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Analysis Bureau (Imperial Intelligence)|Analysis Bureau]] was introduced in the [[1989]] ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'' written by [[Greg Gorden]] and published by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="IS">''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]''</ref> It performed a similar role of examining data and information.<ref name="Imperial Handbook">''[[Star Wars: Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide]]''</ref>
