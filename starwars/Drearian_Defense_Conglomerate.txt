'''Drearian Defense Conglomerate''', sometime shortened to '''DDC''', was a respected weapons manufacturing company.<ref name="TLJVD" /> One of their products was the [[Satine's Lament]] model of [[Defender sporting blaster pistol]], which was named for [[Satine Kryze]], the [[Duchess]] of the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The model was popular with nobility and aristocrats, and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] used one during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="USW" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drearian Defense Conglomerate was first mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' rule book ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition]]'' written by [[Bill Smith]] and released in [[1992]] for [[West End Games]]. The company was made [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which was released in [[2015]].
