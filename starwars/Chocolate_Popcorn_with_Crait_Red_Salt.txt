'''Chocolate Popcorn with Crait Red Salt''' was a form of popped [[Surabat grain]] served at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. The kernels were mixed with [[chocolate]] and sprinkled with [[Color|red]] [[salt]] from [[Crait]], an uninhabited mineral world.<ref name="KSK" />

==Behind the scenes==
This popcorn is available exclusively at Kat Saka's Kettle in Disneyland's [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. It was introduced in [[October]] [[2019]] along with other new foods that were not available when the park debuted.<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2019/10/new-bites-and-60-day-advanced-reservations-coming-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge-at-disneyland-resort|text=New Bites and 60-Day Advanced Reservations Coming to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge at Disneyland Resort}}</ref> The menu description is, "Chocolate kettle corn sprinkled with red salt."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/kat-sakas-kettle/menus|text=Disneyland: Kat Saka's Kettle Snack Menu}}</ref>
