'''Bay Two''' was a launch bay located on the wall of [[Hangar Six]] onboard the [[First Order]]'s<ref name="TFA" /> [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]''.<ref name="TFAVD" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[Resistance]] [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] and the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn]] stole<ref name="TFA" /> a [[Unidentified TIE/sf space superiority fighter (Finalizer)|TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]<ref name="TFAVD" /> from Bay Two, but discovered that it was still connected to the bay by a [[fuel]] cable. While Dameron detached the cable, Finn opened fire on First Order forces within the [[hangar]], after which the two escaped from the [[Star Destroyer]]<ref name="TFA" /> and into the [[Jakku system]].<ref name="SWGA" />
