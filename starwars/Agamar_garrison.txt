The '''Agamar garrison'''<ref name="USWNE" /> was a [[Separatist holdouts|Separatist holdout]] led by the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|super tactical droid]] [[General]] [[Kalani]] in the wreckage of a [[Unidentified DH-Omni Support Vessel (Agamar)|downed]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel|supply ship]] on the [[planet]] [[Agamar]] during the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Last Battle" /> In [[2 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' states that the events of "[[The Last Battle]]" occur seventeen [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, the events of "The Last Battle" must take place in [[2 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]], the holdout was [[Mission to Agamar (2 BBY)|visited]] by [[Spectres|members]] of a [[Phoenix Cell|rebel cell]] who fought a last battle of the [[Clone Wars]] with them. The [[droid]]s made peace with the former [[clone trooper]], [[Captain]] [[Rex]], and the rebels and droids fought against the Empire together before fleeing the planet in [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|''Sheathipede''-class transport shuttles]]. Rex and Kalani accepted this as the true end to the Clone Wars, and Kalani and the Rebels went separate ways in their [[shuttle]]s after the droid calculated the rebels had less than a one percent chance to pull off a successful rebellion against the Empire.<ref name="The Last Battle" />
