'''Div''' was a member of the [[Free Ryloth Movement]], a resistance movement that [[Ryloth Insurgency|worked]] to free the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] from the [[Galactic Empire]]. Under the directive of [[Cham Syndulla]]&mdash;the resistance's leader<ref name="LOTS" />&mdash;in [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> they participated in an [[Mission to Ryloth|attempt]] to [[Death|assassinate]] [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] aboard the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Perilous]]''. The group's efforts to destroy the [[Starship|vessel]] after it entered the [[Ryloth system]] failed, with Div ultimately passing away. Syndulla later mentioned Div in a list of every Free Ryloth Movement member who had died in the assassination attempt while rallying his remaining colleagues to attack Palpatine again.<ref name="LOTS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Div was mentioned in ''[[Lords of the Sith]]'', a [[2015]] novel written by [[Paul S. Kemp]].<ref name="LOTS" />
