The '''Blue Bantha Refreshments''' milk bar was a drink vendor establishment found within the [[Mos Espa Waystation]] battlefield of the [[Grand Arena]], utilized by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This circular, open structure was accessed from the upper level of the battlefield, directly across the central raised walkway from the [[Stone Needle Skyhopper Garage]]. Blue Bantha Refreshments was owned by the [[Blue Bantha (milk distributor)|Blue Bantha]] [[milk]] distributor, who also operated the [[Blue Bantha Milk Kiosk]] found in the [[Dune Sea Outpost]] battlefield, both locations selling [[Blue milk|blue]] and [[green milk]]. They were staffed by at least one specially equipped, [[Color|blue]] and white&ndash;clad [[R5-series astromech droid]].<ref name="Hunters" />
