'''Cerosha''' was a [[planet]] home to several [[City|cities]], including [[Mock]] and [[Solace]]. For many [[Standard year|years]], Solace had been peaceful and lively, but some individuals visited Solace for illegal trade. According to legend, Solace was protected by an individual known as [[the Wanderer]], who was first seen on Cerosha when he saved the city from a [[Unidentified pirate group (Solace)|group]] of [[pirate]]s. Several years later, the Wanderer returned to Solace, assisting its citizens with an infestation of [[Species|creatures]] uncovered in a [[mining]] operation. Some [[time]] afterwards, the Wanderer returned to protect Solace when tidal waves from the nearby [[Boralic Sea]] threatened to destroy the city. The Wanderer held back the [[water]] until the waves receded, and departed from Cerosha, and was never seen again.<ref name="The Wanderer" />

At some point after the Wanderer's disappearance, the city of Solace was [[Destruction of Solace|destroyed]] by a figure known only as the [[Anakin Skywalker|Dark Wraith]]. Long after the city's destruction, other inhabitants of Cerosha developed several rumors regarding the reasons behind the event. Citizens of the nearby city of Mock believed that the inhabitants of Solace continued to refuse to follow the Dark Wraith's orders, and, after several incidents, the Dark Wraith punished Solace by destroying the entire city, leaving only a small number of survivors. In the time after Solace's destruction, the land on which it was built was not redeveloped, and the ruins of the city were left as a reminder of what disobedience would lead to.<ref name="The Dark Wraith" />
