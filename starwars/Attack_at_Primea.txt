The '''attack at Primea''', also called the '''Primea incident''', was a skirmish between the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] and forces controlled by the [[Nikardun Destiny]] above the [[Vak]] [[homeworld]] of [[Primea]]. [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] of the [[Chiss]], in his investigations into the [[Nikardun conquests]], traveled aboard a [[Garwian Unity]] [[diplomat]]ic mission to Primea to look into [[General]] [[Yiv]]'s attempts at subjugating the [[planet]]. Yiv intended to orchestrate Thrawn's [[death]] as the [[Garwian]]s left Primea.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

Unplanned for by Yiv, Thrawn and [[caregiver]] [[Mitth'ali'astov]] remained on the planet when the Garwian [[Starship|vessel]] departed. The Chiss Ascendancy sent [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]]'s [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']] to collect them. [[Syndic]] [[Irizi'stal'mustro]], an [[Irizi family]] officia wary of Thrawn's inquisition into [[Nikardun]] activities, insisted on coming along on the mission.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

The ''Vigilant'' arrived three [[Standard day|days]] after the Garwian diplomat's departure, and was confronted by several [[Patrol ship|Vak fighters]]. [[Thrawn's patrol boat|One]] of the [[starfighter]]s had been commandeered by Thrawn, who used [[modulated laserfire]] to make contact with the ''Vigilant'', sending a schematic of the Vak fighters in case Ar'alani needed to disable them without harming the occupants. However, before the Chiss could take action, four [[Heavy cruiser|cruisers]] of the [[Lioaoin Regime]], being sent in place of Nikardun forces by General Yiv, emerged from [[hyperspace]] and opened fire on the ''Vigilant''. Thrawn attacked [[Attacker Four|one]] of the [[cruiser]]s in his Vak fighter, and the [[Lioaoi]]n responded too late, unsure whether to open fire on a Vak ship. The ''Vigilant'' brought Thrawn's fighter aboard, jumping to hyperspace. Following the Lioaoin attack on the ''Vigilant'', the Chiss Ascendancy responded with an [[Lioaoin skirmish|attack]] at the [[Lioaoin heartworld]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Prelude==
Senior Captain Thrawn had met General Yiv the Benevolent at the [[Primea diplomatic reception]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Aftermath==
Bound by law, the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet was required to [[Lioaoin skirmish|attack]] the [[Lioaoin heartworld]] in response to an attack by opponents self-identified as part of the [[Lioaoin military]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
