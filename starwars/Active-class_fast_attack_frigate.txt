The '''''Active''-class fast attack frigate''' was a class of [[frigate]] manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]], eventually serving as the basis for the company's [[Surveyor-class reconnaissance frigate|''Surveyor''-class reconnaissance frigate]] for the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy|naval forces]].<ref name="Lead by Example" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Active''-class fast attack frigate was introduced in the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]] for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Lead by Example" />
