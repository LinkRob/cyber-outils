"'''For the Love of the Empire'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story published in the one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[A New Legacy 1|''A New Legacy'' 1]]. The story was written by [[Jason Aaron]], illustrated by [[Leonard Kirk]] and [[Jay David Ramos]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 29]], [[2025]].

==Plot summary==
On the world of [[Yelsain]], a rebel recruiter attempts to sway a crowd by promising them a chance to fight alongside the galaxy’s "most caring people". The speech is interrupted when Imperial forces, led by a ruthless [[stormtrooper]], execute the recruiter. The trooper mockingly "welcomes" the horrified survivors to the Rebellion before ordering his squad to open fire, illustrating the Empire's shift toward extreme measures to maintain order.

[[Kreel|Sergeant Kreel]] and [[Task Force 99]] (SCAR Squadron) reflect on their declining reputation while pursuing Rebels across the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. Kreel recalls a time on [[Horford]] when they were cheered as liberators; however, returning there weeks after the [[Death Star]]’s destruction, he finds the populace now views them with terror. Kreel concludes that the Empire has already "lost the war" because they have failed to win the hearts and minds of the people. Despite this realization, he vows that SCAR Squadron will continue to fight as "true heroes," even if they must kill every soul in the galaxy to prove it.
