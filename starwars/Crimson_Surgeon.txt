The '''Crimson Surgeon''' was a costume owned by the [[Rodian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Skora]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Underworld Chemist|standard costume]]. It featured a [[Color|red]], brown, and black color scheme, with red paint around the eyes that almost resembled [[blood]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Crimson Surgeon was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Skora, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Crimson Applicator]].<ref name="Hunters" />
