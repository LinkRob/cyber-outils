'''Enza Rylanz''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who resided on the [[planet]] [[Ghorman]]'s capital city of [[Palmo]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. She was the daughter of politician and businessman [[Carro Rylanz]]<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" /> and by [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|204}}</ref> was a member of her father's emerging rebel group known as the [[Ghorman Front]].<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" /> She and her father were members of the [[Twillery]] [[House of Rylanz]], which had been in their family for nineteen generations.<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" /> 

In [[3 BBY]], after the Ghorman Front secretly placed a [[listening device]] in [[Division Chief]] [[Syril Karn]]'s [[Bureau of Standards Ghorman field office|office]], Enza and her fellow rebels listened to his conversations with his [[Eedy Karn|mother]]. When he father later listened to the recordings, they came to the conclusion that Syril was conflicted on the Empire's presence, unaware that Karn was acting under orders of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] to infiltrate the Front.<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" />

Enza was later present at a public meeting where the city councilors, her father included, listened to citizen complaints over the alleged Imperial armory being constructed and the Empire's choking of Ghorman's economy. She, among other Front members, observed Karn, who was invited to the meeting by [[Samm]]. After the meeting was over, Enza introduced Karn to her father and observed as Carro tried to court Karn as a sympathizer.<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" />

Sometime later, Enza and her boyfriend [[Dilan]] continued to monitor Karn's office when a surprise ISB inspection forced their inside agent, [[Leeza]], to signal them to cut the line to keep the bug from being traced. Later, she and Dilan showed her father a dossier on Karn they acquired, revealing that he previously worked for [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces]] as an [[officer]] on [[Morlana One]], but was disgraced by the ISB after a [[Ambush on Ferrix|disasterous mission]]. Believing Karn to be a potential ally, Enza and Carro approached him while he was walking home, claiming to have been worried about him when the ISB invaded his office. With Karn visibly upset, Carro suggested that the ISB was operating a shadow government without Emperor Palpatine's knowledge and asked him to provide information on what cargo the carriers moving through Palmo were carrying.<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" /> 

Enza and Dilan were later part of the protest that would ultimately become the [[Ghorman Massacre]], joining Lezine in singing the Ghorman national anthem in defiance of Imperial orders to disperse. When the Imperial troops began firing upon the protestors, Enza was quick to take up arms in response, firing upon the Imperials with an [[SE-14r light repeating blaster]]. As she and Dilan<ref name="EnzaDatabank" /> broke cover to charge the barricades, the latter was wounded by the Imperial forces. In retaliation, Enza continued to fire her [[blaster]]. As the massacre escalated, several [[KX-series security droid|KX-series security droids]] were deployed, against whom Enza's blaster fire proved ineffective and which only served to draw one droid's attention to her. Accidentally knocked to the ground by a fleeing Ghorman Front member, Enza frantically attempted to scramble away from the advancing droid but was unable to regain her footing in time to escape before the droid grabbed her. As Dilan watched helplessly in horror, the droid hurled Enza high into the air, sending her flying fifty feet across [[Palmo Plaza]] to slam hard against the ground, killing her instantly from the impact.<ref name="Who Are You">{{Andor|Who Are You?}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
While Enza was diligent and detail-minded, she and her boyfriend Dilan grew increasingly impatient and willing to take risks to ensure Ghorman had a future free of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="EnzaDatabank" />
