The '''assassination of Cade''' was an assassination plotted by [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart|Rampart]] to eliminate [[clone trooper]] "[[Cade]]," after the clone threatened Rampart to testify against him in the [[Imperial Senate]] about the [[Attack on Kamino|bombardment]] on [[Tipoca City]]. The assassination was successful, however a fellow clone, [[Slip (clone trooper)|Slip]], escaped to warn [[Senator]] [[Riyo Chuchi]] about Rampart and the assassination.<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" />
