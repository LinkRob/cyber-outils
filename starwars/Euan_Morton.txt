'''Euan Morton''' is a [[Wikipedia:Scottish|Scottish]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]], [[Wikipedia:Singer|singer]]<ref name="Playbill" /> and [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice artist]].<ref name="Audiofile" /> Morton's first contribution to the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise was voicing the [[Gender|male]] [[Darth Nox|Sith Inquisitor]] player character as well as additional voices in the [[2011]] [[BioWare]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''<ref name="TOR credits">{{TORcite|type=other|Credits}}</ref> and its expansions.<ref name="TOR rothc">{{TORcite|exp=rothc|type=other|Credits}}</ref> In [[2014]], Morton narrated the [[Tarkin (audiobook)|audio version]] of [[James Luceno]]'s [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] novel.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (audiobook)|''Tarkin'' audiobook]]</ref> They would go one to voice [[Count]] [[Dooku]] in the [[2019]] ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''<ref name="Dooku Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> and [[Darth Sidious]] in the [[2020]] ''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]'' audio dramas.<ref name="Aphra">''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]''</ref>
