A '''hotel''' was located in at least two different locations in [[Coruscant]]'s [[Coruscant Underworld|Underworld]]: One was at the [[corner of twelfth and G]] on [[Level 1312]], while another one was located next to an [[abandoned munitions warehouse]] on [[Level 1315]]. [[Color|Yellow]] signs outside the building read "[[Hotel|HOTEL]]" in [[Aurebesh]], while a greenish blue Aurebesh sign said "VACANCY" (curiously, the vacancy sign on Level 1315 was upside-down). A [[holobooth]] was located outside the building on Level 1312, which [[Ahsoka Tano]] used to contact her friend [[Barriss Offee]] while on the run.<ref name="Catch" />
