'''Duncan Pow''' (born [[October 8]], [[1977]])<ref name="Duncan BDay" /> is a [[Wikipedia:Scottish people|Scottish]] actor<ref name="ScotsCare" /> who portrayed [[Sergeant]] [[Ruescott Melshi]] in the [[2016]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Rogue One" /> and the [[2022]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> Pow was brought up in [[Wikipedia:Lockerbie|Lockerbie]] in [[Wikipedia:Dumfries and Galloway|Dumfries and Galloway]], [[Wikipedia:Scotland|Scotland]].<ref name="ScotsCare" />
