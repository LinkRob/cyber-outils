The '''Chankin system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Jidlor Marches]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square I-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Chankin]]. [[Standard year|years]] prior<ref name="The Dark" /> to [[18 BBY]],<ref name="TDDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Dark]]'' to [[18 BBY]].</ref> [[pirate]]s came after the [[Graf family]] of [[cartographer]]s on Chankin until the family's [[Parent|father]], [[Auric Graf]], fired a shot from [[Mu-class shuttle|''Mu''-class shuttle]] ''[[Whisper Bird]]'' while his [[Gender|daughter]] [[Lina Graf]] watched.<ref name="The Dark" />
