'''COO-2310''' was one of the supposedly rogue [[droid]]s that attacked [[Constable]] [[Zuvio]] during his investigation of a [[Unidentified Western Reaches Exchange banking ship|banking ship]] robbery in [[Niima Outpost]].

==Biography==
As a part of [[Rikard Lovas]]'s plot to steal the electronic [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] of the citizens of [[Niima Outpost]] on [[Jakku]], COO-2310 and several other [[droid]]s were sent to an [[Unidentified moisture farm (Jakku)|abandoned farmhouse]] to wait for [[Zuvio]] as he tracked the missing [[secretary droid]] [[CZ-1G5]]. COO was destroyed when an [[EG-6 power droid]] caused the farmhouse to explode.<ref name="HNJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
COO-2310 was a character created for the short story "[[High Noon on Jakku]]," which was written by [[Landry Q. Walker]].<ref name="HNJ" />
