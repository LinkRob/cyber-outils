The '''East City Brawlers''' were a [[grav-ball]] team from the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. The team had an intimidating reputation, as it sported two burly [[fullback]]s and the team's coach was known for his unpredictable strategies.<ref name="edge" />

During their match against the [[AppSci SaberCats]], the Brawlers used rough tactics that normally would have been considered fouls, but were ignored by the match's referee. He later ejected one of the SaberCats' players for assaulting one of the Brawlers, even though that player had again initiated the contact.<ref name="edge" />
