The '''eighth episode''' of the animated web series ''[[Grogu Cutest In The Galaxy]]'' released on [[April 15]], [[2025]]. It was posted by ''[[Star Wars]]'' Japan on [[Wikipedia:Instagram Reels|Instagram Reels]].<ref name="Instagram" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
While [[Din Djarin]] pilots the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] tries to grab a lever from the co-pilot's seat. Din picks Grogu up and puts him on the ground. Grogu again tries to reach the lever, prompting Din to unscrew the spherical end of the lever and giving it to Grogu so he can play with it.
