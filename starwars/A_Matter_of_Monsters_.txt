"'''A Matter of Monsters!'''" was the fourth installment of [[The Keeper's World]], published in [[Pizzazz 4]] in January [[1978]]. The entire story arc was reprinted in the UK in ''[[Star Wars Weekly 47]]&ndash;[[Star Wars Weekly 50|50]]'' in [[1978]]&ndash;[[1979|79]], and in the [[1999]] limited-release comic [[Star Wars 0|''Star Wars'' 0]].

==Plot summary==
While stranded after their crash landing on [[Keeper's World]], [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]], [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]], and [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] are attacked by a [[Unidentified creature (Keeper's World)|monster]]. R2-D2 manages to find and awaken the beast's mother, who removes the monster. Just as Leia Organa thinks they are safe, however, an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Unidentified Imperial fighter craft|fighter]] enters the atmosphere.
