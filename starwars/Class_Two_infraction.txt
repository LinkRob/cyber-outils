'''Class Two infractions'''<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> were one of the five categories of crime outlined<ref name="Scum">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> in the [[Imperial Penal Code|Imperial Legal Code]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Build R2 8">{{BuildR2Cite|8|Droid Directory}}</ref> Crimes in this category included the possession of unsanctioned [[weapon]]s, the forgery of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] documents and resisting arrest.<ref name="ROUVG" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jyn Erso]] was arrested on the [[planet]] [[Corulag]] and charged with three Class Two infractions<ref name="Scum" /> which were logged in her case file.<ref name="ROUVG" />
