The '''Cirque Glacier''' was a [[Water|glacier]] located on the ice-covered [[planet]] [[Hoth]]. It was located north of the [[Clabburn Range]] of [[mountain]]s and south of the [[North Ridge]] and [[Lanteel Glacier]]. The [[Hanging Valley]] sat to the west of the Cirque Glacier and the [[Nev Ice Flow]] lay to the east.<ref name="CL" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates [[XJ9-CS14]]'s arrival on [[Hoth]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Viper probe droid]]<ref name="CL" /> [[XJ9-CS14]]<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> passed along the southern edge of the glacier while searching for the secret headquarters of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], diverting south briefly due to a large [[meteorite]] crater. As it reached the western end of the glacier toward the boarder into the Hanging Valley, the [[droid]] detected the energy signature of [[Echo Base]] for the first [[time]], and started moving south toward the Clabburn Range where the base was built.<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cirque Glacier was first mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], in a section written by [[James Luceno]] and illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]].<ref name="CL" /> The location originally appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2004]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars Trilogy]]'', which was also written by Luceno, with illustrations by Chasemore and Jenssen.<ref name="OTWorlds">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars Trilogy]]''</ref>
