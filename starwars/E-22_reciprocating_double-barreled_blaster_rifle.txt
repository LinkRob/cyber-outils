The '''E-22 reciprocating double-barreled blaster rifle''', also known as the '''E-22 blaster rifle''', was a reciprocating, double-barreled [[blaster]] that was bigger and more powerful than the standard [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[stormtrooper]]s. The E-22 saw use during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. It was commonly used by [[coastal defender stormtrooper]]s stationed at the top secret [[Imperial Center of Military Research|Imperial security complex]] on the tropical [[planet]] [[Scarif]]<ref name="Launch Bay" /> and [[Imperial swamp trooper|mudtroopers]] stationed on the planet [[Mimban]].

==History==
[[File:Unlimited-VanguardInfantry.png|thumb|left|330px|A swamp trooper with an E-22 acts as a vanguard for an Imperial force.]]
The E-22 had been introduced by around [[18 BBY]], <ref name="Andor Season 2">Interviews with [[Elizabeth Dulau]] for [[Andor Season 2]] reveal that [[Luthen Rael]] met [[Kleya Marki]] 17 years before [[1 BBY]], most likely equating to [[18 BBY]]. </ref> with the Imperial swamp trooper [[officer]] [[Captain]] [[Amari]] wielding one during a [[Unidentified massacre (Luthen Rael)|massacre]].<ref>''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' - "''[[Make It Stop]]"</ref>

The E-22 later saw use by many of the swamp troopers stationed on the planet Mimban with the [[224th Imperial Armored Division]]<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> in [[10 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> as part of the [[Mimban Campaign]]. Among those who used the rifle was [[Corporal]] [[Wesger Odry]].<ref name="TOG" /> Swamp troopers learned to keep the blaster's recoil sleeve free of mud due to the possibility of the mud causing a weapon malfunction.<ref name="E22-DB">{{Databank|e-22-blaster-rifle|E-22 blaster rifle}}</ref> On one occasion, two swamp troopers armed with E-22s were involved in a battle in a forested location. Deployed alongside at least one other swamp trooper, the E-22 wielding troopers acted as part of the vanguard of Imperial forces, with one firing their blaster in combat at one time during the conflict.<ref name="Vanguard Infantry">{{SWU|set=Spark of Rebellion|cardname=Vanguard Infantry|link=5575681343|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20240407030722/https://swudb.com/card/sor/108/vanguard-infantry}}</ref>

During the [[Battle of Scarif]], the E-22 saw use against [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] forces in the hands of a number of shoretroopers. Ultimately, however, the Galactic Empire's efforts proved ineffective in stopping the Rebel [[squad]] "[[Rogue One]]" from stealing the [[Death Star plans]] from the [[Scarif vault]], and the facility, along with any shoretroopers on the surface, was destroyed by a single reactor blast from the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] ordered by [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] in an unsuccessful attempt at stopping the plans from being transmitted to [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] aboard the ''[[Tantive IV]]'', which proceeded to flee the battle by jumping to [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

[[File:E-22_Fathead.png|thumb|right|250px|Coastal defender Stormtroopers used the E-22 blaster rifle during and after the reign of the Empire.]]
Years after the fall of the Empire, the E-22 was used by [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] forces. [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] encountered and killed the stormtrooper [[TK-7919]],<ref>''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> who was armed with an E-22, whilst [[Incident on Nevarro|retrieving]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] from an [[Imperial Remnant safe house|Imperial compound]].<ref name="Chapter 3">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 3: The Sin}}</ref> Later, Djarin and his allies were [[Clash on Nevarro|trapped]] in the [[Nevarro school|cantina]] on [[Nevarro]] by a force of stormtroopers, some of which were carrying E-22 blaster rifles, and they would continue to be used on [[Trask]] and on [[Gideon's light cruiser]].<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> E-22 rifles were further used by the coastal defender stormtroopers in the [[Morak Imperial remnants]] in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|8|the appearance of the E-22 [[blaster rifle]]}}</ref>
