'''Duke''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and flew under the [[callsign]] [[Phoenix Five]] during the [[Attack on Lothal]]. Duke perished when he was shot down and [[Death|killed]] by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Commander]] [[Vult Skerris]] in his [[TIE/D Defender Elite]] as he was attempting to break through the [[blockade]].

==Biography==
Duke was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]] [[pilot]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. In [[1 BBY]], he was part of [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]'s strike team that attempted to take out the [[Imperial Armory Complex]] and [[Lothal City fuel depot|nearby fuel depot]] on the planet [[Lothal]]. This was part of Syndulla's plan to halt [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]] production programme. During the [[Attack on Lothal|attack]], Duke answered to the [[callsign]] [[Phoenix Five]] and flew with [[Cleat]], who answered to the callsign [[Phoenix Four]].<ref name="Rebel Assault" />

After Hera's strike team exited hyperspace and encountered Thrawn's [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|7th Fleet]], Hera ordered Phoenix Five and Phoenix Four to thin out the approaching [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. Duke and Cleat complied with Hera's orders. When Cleat told him to keep up, he responded affirmatively. As the two were attacking TIE fighters, the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] ace pilot [[Vult Skerris]] entered the dogfight in his [[TIE/D Defender Elite]] behind Duke's X-wing starfighter. Cleat tried to warn him but Skerris shot down his fighter, killing him. Skerris then went on to kill Cleat before being taken out by Syndulla.<ref name="Rebel Assault" />

General Syndulla's strike team was wiped out by Thrawn's strong TIE fighter defense with Hera herself being captured.<ref name="Rebel Assault" /> Despite the failure of the initial assault, Duke's sacrifice was not in vain since the [[Spectres]] subsequently succeeded in knocking out TIE Defender production on Lothal when [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] fired on the Lothal City fuel depot in an attempt to destroy the rebel cell.<ref name="DUME">{{Rebels|DUME}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Duke was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] with light skin. He was a Rebel Alliance T-65B X-wing starfighter pilot who knew how to fly in formation and shoot down TIE fighters, working with his wingmate Cleat. Despite his skills, he was outmatched by the Imperial ace pilot Vult Skerris in his TIE/D Defender Elite.<ref name="Rebel Assault" />

==Behind the scenes==
Duke first appeared in "[[Rebel Assault]]," the ninth episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Four|fourth season]] of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. He was voiced by [[Freddie Prinze Jr.]], who also voices [[Kanan Jarrus]]. He has the same animation model as [[Gold Four (Archeon Nebula)|Gold Four]] from "[[Secret Cargo]]."<ref name="Rebel Assault" />
