'''David Ankrum''' was the voice actor for [[Wedge Antilles]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']],<ref name="Fake Wedge">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-mysteries-hunting-for-the-fake-wedge|text=''Star Wars'' Mysteries: Hunting for the Fake Wedge|int=Star Wars Mysteries}}</ref> and ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Rogue One">{{WebCite|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/rogue-one-wedge-antilles|text='Rogue One': Where Was Wedge Antilles During the Battle of Scarif?|date=2017-01-31|work=[[Wikipedia:/Film|/Film]]|archivedate=20181005094440}}</ref> He overdubbed both [[Denis Lawson]] and [[Colin Higgins]] throughout the former film.<ref name="Fake Wedge" />
