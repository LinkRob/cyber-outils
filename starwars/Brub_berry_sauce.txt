'''Brub berry sauce''' was a [[fruit]]y [[Sauce|compote]] made with the [[Brub berry|brub berries]] of the [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor's]] [[brub-brub bush]]. At some point after publishing his first [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for the sauce in his second [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]''.<ref name="Cookbook" />

Tuggs described the compote's flavor as both sweet and tart and considered it a special sauce, for it could stand out on its own without complementing a dish. Despite that, he did recommend pairing it with main courses such as [[Engine Roasted Tip-Yip]] or [[Glazed Kod'yok]], warning that there was a chance that the reader might just eat a bowl of the brub berry sauce on its own.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brub berry sauce was introduced in the [[2021]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'', authored by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the sauce calls for [[Wikipedia:Blueberry|blueberries]], [[Wikipedia:Brown sugar|brown sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Orange juice|orange juice]], [[Wikipedia:Maple syrup|maple syrup]], [[Wikipedia:Cinnamon|cinnamon]], [[Wikipedia:Nutmeg|nutmeg]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], and [[Wikipedia:Clove|cloves]].<ref name="Cookbook" />
