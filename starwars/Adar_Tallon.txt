==Biography==
Adar Tallon was a [[human]]<ref name="Armada" /> individual who served with distinction in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Military|armed forces]] and later as an [[Imperial officer]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] before he [[Desertion|defected]] to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and became an [[Alliance officer|officer]] there. Following his defection, Tallon [[author]]ed ''[[Treatise on Starfighter Tactics]]'', a [[book]] that explored the rules of [[Starfighter combat|starfighter engagement]] in detail and codified [[starfighter]] techniques. Tallon examined and formulated five important moments in any battle, describing them as Detection, Closing, Attack, Maneuver, and Disengagement; which were collectively referred to as the [[Five Stages of Combat]].<ref name="BMF 29" />

Tallon at one point observed a [[hologram]] of a battle between several rebel [[warship]]s and an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="Armada" /> By [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'' establishes that [[IG-72]] [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destructed]] on [[Tatooine]] prior to the [[Mission to Arvala-7]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[9 ABY]].</ref> the [[IG-series assassin droid]] [[IG-72]] had [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destructed]] while [[Hunt for Adar Tallon|hunting]] for Tallon on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="MandoVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] and [[Resistance]] paramilitary member [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned Adar Tallon in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> book ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''. The book included a citation to the [[Rebel Alliance Archives]], Section: Defections & Debriefings, File: Adar Tallon.<ref name="Rise" />

==Personality and traits==
Adar Tallon had light skin, blue [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]].<ref name="Armada" />

==Skills and abilities==
Beaumont Kin believed Tallon to be a notable example of a skilled [[analyst]] and tactician who left the Empire for the Alliance.<ref name="Rise" />

==Equipment==
In at least one occasion, Tallon wore an [[Alliance military uniform|Alliance officer military uniform]] that included [[Color|black]] [[glove]]s and a beige collared [[Clothing|shirt]] with pins that was worn under a brown military tunic.<ref name="Armada" />

==Behind the scenes==
Adar Tallon was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 29|twenty-ninth]] issue of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' model-building magazine<ref name="BMF 29" /> released around [[July 22]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFDate">{{BMFdates|29}}</ref> Tallon was pictured in the Sector Fleet Rules PDF released by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for ''[[Star Wars: Armada]]'',<ref name="PDF">{{FantasyFlightGames|subdomain=images-cdn|url=filer_public/13/6b/136b6ec9-562a-45e1-9fb3-8bc4e972c293/swm_sector_fleet_rules_eng.pdf|text=Sector Fleet Rules PDF}}</ref> a miniatures [[space warfare]] game.<ref name="Command">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2015/3/27/you-are-in-command-now|text=You Are in Command Now}}</ref>

[[Adar Tallon/Legends|Adar Tallon]] originated from the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where he debuted in the ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' adventure book ''[[Tatooine Manhunt]]'', which was published in [[1988]] and authored by both [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Daniel Greenberg]].<ref name="Tatooine Manhunt">''[[Tatooine Manhunt]]''</ref> Tallon was subsequently pictured in the [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']], which was released in [[1998]].<ref name="Rebellion">[[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']]</ref>
