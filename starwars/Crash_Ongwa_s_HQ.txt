'''Crash Ongwa's HQ''' was a location on the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]] during the [[High Republic Era]] that served as a headquarters for the [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection]] in the lead-up to the [[Battle of Eriadu]].<ref name="DftOZ 2" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFTOZ2}}</ref> members of the crew were present within the rebel encampment in order to discuss a plan in order to [[Infiltration of the Warden's stronghold|infiltrate]] the [[The Warden's stronghold|stronghold]] of the [[Nihil]] [[warlord]], [[the Warden]], to rescue a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Unidentified EX droid (Driggit Parse)|EX droid]] that was taken into the fortress.<ref name="DftOZ 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crash Ongwa's HQ appeared in the [[The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone 2|second issue]] of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone]]''. The comic was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Valeria Favoccia|Valeria "Lux" Favoccia]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="DftOZ 2" /> on [[November 27]], 2024.<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3016-087/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III--Dispatches-from-the-Occlusion-Zone-2|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III--Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone #2}}</ref>
