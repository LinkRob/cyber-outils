'''Darg Neros''' was a [[fathier]] [[jockey]] [[Corellian|from]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Corellia">{{Databank|corellia|Corellia}}</ref> [[Corellia]] who rode in the [[Canto Cup]], a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> that took place in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the planet [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Neros took part in a Canto Cup race that was watched via [[hologram]] and [[Gambling|bet]] upon by the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Darg Neros appeared in a hologram in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' and is one of several jockeys that can be randomly featured in Canto Fathier Racing, an optional activity. For every race in the game the jockeys are assigned a random fathier and a random [[color]] [[Clothing|uniform]] and symbol. As such, this article does not assume one pairing or uniform color to be [[canon]]ical.<ref name="Outlaws" />
