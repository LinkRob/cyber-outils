'''Corporations Road''' was a street in [[Mos Eisley]], [[Tatooine]]. It contained the [[Customs House]].<ref name="CL 2016" /> It was on this street where a [[Jawa]] fell off a startled [[ronto]] during [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s traverse through the [[city]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
