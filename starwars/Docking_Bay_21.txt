'''Docking Bay 21''' was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] located on<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="UltimateSW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Target Vader 1|''Target Vader'' 1]] to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the [[cyborg]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] parked his<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> [[cruiser]],<ref name="Target Vader 3">[[Target Vader 3|''Target Vader'' 3]]</ref> the ''[[Broken Wing]]'', in Docking Bay 21 and traveled to the [[BG-RT's Tavern and Spirits]] [[cantina]] in [[Level 1313]]. Upon returning to the bay following a [[Shootout at BG-RT's Tavern and Spirits|shootout]] at the cantina, he was greeted by<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> the [[Hidden Hand]] criminal syndicate's leader,<ref name="Target Vader 6">[[Target Vader 6|''Target Vader'' 6]]</ref> [[Gwi]], who offered him a [[Hunt for Vader|job]], claiming to be a humble messenger for the organization. Valance initially refused, but accepted the offer after hearing that his target was the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Docking Bay 21 appeared in the [[Target Vader 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]''. It was written by [[Robbie Thompson]], illustrated by [[Marc Laming]] and [[Cris Bolson]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> on [[July 3]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/77664/star_wars_target_vader_2019_1|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #1}}</ref>
