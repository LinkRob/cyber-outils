'''Corellian hounds''', also known as '''Sibian hounds''', were a type of [[Dog|hound]] from the [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> They were quick and nimble canines used by locals for a number of tasks. Due to their keen sense of smell, they were often used for [[hunting]] and tracking. They had a vicious bark and bite, which meant they could be quite effective for protection. Criminal gangs were fond of using the hounds for defense or to attack their rivals. They also had regenerating [[Tooth|teeth]].<ref name="Alien Archive" /> Corellian hounds were also present on the planet [[Ushruu]], where they were known as '''snarlers''',<ref name="Forest of Fear!">''[[The Mighty Chewbacca in the Forest of Fear!]]''</ref> in addition to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet of [[Ferrix]], where they were referred to as '''Ferrix hounds'''.

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:CorellianHound-SoloUSC.png|thumb|left|200px|A profile view of a Corellian hound.]]
Corellian hounds were fast and had an excellent sense of smell. They had a [[bone]]-like ridge along the crest from head to shoulder. Their bites were savage and so they often lost [[Tooth|teeth]], which were regrown.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

==Behavior==
Corellian hounds were prone to fighting.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

==Corellian hounds in the galaxy==
[[File:CorellianHoundSkull-Squadrons-cropped.png|thumb|right|200px|Skull of a Corellian hound, used as an ornament in a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE]] cockpit]]
Corellian hounds were often employed by gangs as guards, attack-dogs, and trackers.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], a group of Corellian 'junkyard' hounds were kept by the [[human]] [[Pegla]], the de facto head of security at [[Zorby's Western Shiplot]]<ref name="DB Pegla">{{Databank|pegla|Pegla}}</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]], to protect the shipyard from trespassers.<ref name="Andor 1" /> At some point on an early morning in [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Andor date">The beginning of {{Andor|Kassa (episode)}} dates it self to 5 BBY.</ref> the dogs were running on the [[Rix Road]], passing<ref name="Andor 1" /> the [[B2 groundmech]]<ref name="DotRVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[B2EMO]], as one of them stopped and [[urine|peed]] on the [[droid]], causing it to be shocked by him.<ref name="Andor 1" />

Around [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GE date">The events of [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]] within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Dok-Ondar]] had the head of a Corellian hound mounted on the wall of his [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|store]] on [[Batuu]]. The plaque the head was mounted on had the words "In memory of [[Yay Jakk|Yay-Jakk]]" painted on it in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="Galaxy'sEdge">{{GEAttr|Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian hounds were featured in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. They were played by four actual dogs named [[Blackie (dog)|Blackie]], [[Boyce (dog)|Boyce]], [[Saxon (dog)|Saxon]], and [[Elsa (dog)|Elsa]]<ref name="Dog Names">{{SW|url=news/creature-feature-5-things-you-might-not-know-about-corellian-hounds|text=Creature Feature: 5 Things You Might Not Know About Corellian Hounds}}</ref> in specially made costumes. Use of the dogs was inspired by [[Mardji]], the [[Wikipedia:Asia elephant|Asian elephant]] who played two [[bantha]]s in the original [[1977]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

According to writer [[Jon Kasdan]], the hounds were his tribute to the Death Dogs from [[Lucasfilm]]'s [[1988]] film [[Willow (film)|''Willow'']], which was also directed by [[Ron Howard]] and starred [[Warwick Davis]].<ref name="Jon Kasdan">{{Twitter|JonKasdan|status/994087163506683904|[[Jon Kasdan]]|quote=In a bizarre TWIST OF FATE, these #CorellianHounds, which were in the script from the very first draft, were 100% a loving homage to the DEATH DOGS in @RealRonHoward 's WILLOW, which terrified me as a kid, particularly when they mauled that poor nursemaid.|archivedate=20200626202127}}</ref> According to director [[Toby Haynes]], the hounds were also played by dogs in the ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series.<ref name="Career Highlight">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/andor-episode-8-toby-haynes-forest-whitaker-stellan-skarsgard-1235250954|text=‘Andor' Director Toby Haynes Talks That Forest Whitaker-Stellan Skarsgard Scene: “It Was a Career Highlight”|author=Davids, Brian|date=2022-10-28|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20221029050743}}</ref>
