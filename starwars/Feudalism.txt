The government of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Toydaria]] was a '''feudal''' [[monarchy]]. In accordance with an ancient feudal system, the lands were ruled by [[Toydarian]] royalty. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[city]] [[Riar]] had been governed by the [[Reeol line]] for about one hundred [[Standard year|years]] and was then both ruled and nominally owned by [[Baron]] [[Reeol]].<ref name="Ghosts of Dathomir">''[[Ghosts of Dathomir]]''</ref>
