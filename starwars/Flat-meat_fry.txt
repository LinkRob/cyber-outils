'''Flat-meat fry''' was a [[sandwich]] served at the [[Foodstuff|food]] pod [[Hugo's]] on the [[moon]] [[Shakari]]<ref name="TMaG" /> during or after [[11 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|MandalorianAndGrogu}}</ref> The [[Ardennian]] [[Chef|cook]] [[Hugo Durant]], who operated Hugo's, once prepared the sandwich for [[Din Grogu]] as [[Din Djarin]] asked questions of the cook about [[Rotta]] the [[Hutt]].<ref name="TMaG" />

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The flat-meat fry first appeared in the [[2026]] film ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]'', directed by [[Jon Favreau]]. After creating the name "flat-meat sandwich" for the film's script, Favreau contacted celebrity chef [[Roy Choi]], who signed on to create the sandwich for the film,<ref name="ChoiVideo">{{SWYouTube|fwqguHvfG2Y&t|Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu &#124; Cooking with Roy Choi &#124; In Theaters May 22}}</ref> serving as the film's "Culinary Creative Consultant".<ref name="TMaG" /> Choi was inspired by Ardennians' four-armed anatomy to design how the sandwich would be prepared and what ingredients made sense in the context of efficient multi-tasking cooking.<ref name="ChoiVideo"/> Choi and Favreau took inspiration from sources including [[Wikipedia:Shawarma|shawarma]], [[Wikipedia:Cuban sandwich|cubanos]], and New York City hotdog vendors to create the aesthetic and culinary language of the flat-meat fry.<ref name="Director's Experience">{{HomeVideoCite|film=TMAG|featurette=The Director's Experience}}</ref> Choi also was informed by concept art of the sandwich designed by the creative team led by [[Doug Chiang]].<ref name="ChoiVideo"/>

Choi wanted Durant to be able to flip the waffle bread, inspired by [[Wikipedia:Taiyaki|taiyaki]], and had the film crew create a rectangular taiyaki press for creating the scaly texture of the waffle bread. The on-screen prop of the dish used sliced onions dyed blue, a pork-loaf [[Wikipedia:Forcemeat|forcemeat]], and a queso hollandaise. The latter was a representation of the contents of an [[in-universe]] mudhorn egg yolk.<ref name="ChoiVideo"/>
