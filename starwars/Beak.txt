'''Beaks''' were a type of body part possessed by some [[Sentience|sentient]] and non-sentient [[species]]. [[Mantellian flutterplume]]s<ref name="AAA">''[[Allies and Adversaries]]''</ref> and [[Hoopaloo]]s had beaks,<ref name="The Rising Storm">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-high-republic-the-rising-storm-exclusive-excerpt|text=Ty Yorrick Tangles with Her Conscience in ''Star Wars: The High Republic: The Rising Storm'' &ndash; Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref> while [[sarlacc]]s had beaked [[tongue]]s.<ref name="Base Series 1">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Sarlacc - Creature}}</ref> The [[Bird|avian]] [[Cygnus Anton]] possessed a beak.<ref name="Vader 18">[[Darth Vader (2020) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 18]]</ref>
