'''Egga''' was a member of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s who held the rank of [[Strike]] in [[Lourna Dee]]'s [[Lourna Dee's Tempest|Tempest]], serving under the [[Cloud (rank)|Cloud]] [[Dent (Nihil)|Ultident Margrona]]. The Cloud and her unit traveled to the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Elphrona]] to [[Raid on Elphrona|kidnap]] the [[Blythe family]] so they could be ransomed to wealthy relatives in the [[Core Worlds]]. During the raid, the [[Jedi]] of the [[Elphrona Outpost]] were alerted to the attack and pursued the pirates. When the pirates became aware of the pursuit, Margrona attempted to arrange an ambush, ordering Egga and [[Rel]] to find good vantage points on either side of the canyon they were travelling through.<ref name="LotJ" />

However, [[Jedi Master]] [[Porter Engle]] sensed the ambush, although his [[steelee]] mount was [[Death|killed]] by one of the pirates, and told [[Loden Greatstorm]] and [[Bell Zettifar]] to continue the pursuit. One of the pirates shot at Engle's back, but he deflected the shots before killing the man who had fired at them by throwing his [[lightsaber]], cutting him and the [[ironstone]] outcropping he had hidden behind in half. Engle then used [[telekinesis]] to deflect a shot fired by the other pirate, who retreated to where they had left their own mount and attempted to escape. When the [[Ikkrukkian]] Jedi caught up to the pirate, he presumed that one was the one who had shot his mount, before injuring them with their own deflected blaster bolt. However, as Engle prepared to take the steelee and rejoin the pursuit, the pirate, who he presumed to be a member of a [[species]] whose [[brain]] was not located in their head, sat up. Engle was saved by the [[charhound]] [[Ember]], a companion of the Jedi, who breathed [[fire]] onto the pirate until he stopped moving. The Jedi Master and charhound then set off to catch up to the others. Ultimately, the Jedi were able to rescue three of the Blythes and kill Margrona and all of her Strikes, but Greatstorm and [[Ottoh Blythe]] were captured by Dee and her [[Tempest]].<ref name="LotJ" />
