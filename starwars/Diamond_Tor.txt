The '''''Diamond Tor''''' was a [[freighter]] [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Captain]] [[Oultovar Misk]] which operated under the protection of the [[Yomo Council]], a group that had broken from the [[Galactic Empire]], in [[5 ABY]]. While the ''Diamond Tor'' operated within a [[convoy]] in the [[Midgor]] [[star system]], it was [[Attack on the Diamond Tor|attacked]] by the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], a unit which served the Empire's loyalist [[Imperial Military|forces]] and was heavily damaged, its escorts dismantled. When a [[New Republic]] [[Deliverance's starfighter wing|starfighter wing]] arrived in the system in search of the 204th, its [[squadron]]s assisted what was left of the ''Diamond Tor'' and its convoy.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Diamond Tor'' appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''. The novel was written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the final installment in his trilogy, ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]''.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
