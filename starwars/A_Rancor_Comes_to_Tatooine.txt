"'''A Rancor Comes to Tatooine'''" is a short narrative story published in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'' in [[1987]]. It recounts the tale of how [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] acquired his [[Rancor/Legends|rancor]]. It was reprinted in the [[The Star Wars Sourcebook (Second Edition)|second edition]] of the book in [[1994]].

==Plot summary==
[[Bidlo Kwerve/Legends|Bidlo Kwerve]], looking for ways to improve his position in [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba]] the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt's]] [[Jabba's criminal empire/Legends|organization]], finds him the perfect gift. However, his plan to impress Jabba does not go quite as Kwerve expects.<ref name="SWS" />
