The '''49-v99 Deflector''' was a military grade [[deflector shield]] [[Deflector shield generator|generator]] developed by [[Kuat Drive Yards]] and the [[Colicoid]] [[species]] for the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was easily portable, allowing to move it from one [[starship]] to another.<ref name="SWDatabank ShieldGenerators" /> In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[Spectres|rebel cell]] of [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Hera Syndulla]] managed to steal six 49-v99 Deflectors from the Empire. The rebels traded three of the deflectors to [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], a [[smuggler]] who agreed to help them get passage off the [[planet]] of [[Lothal]].<ref name="The Siege of Lothal" />
