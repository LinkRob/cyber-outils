'''Denash''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Batonn sector]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], it was governed by [[Governor]] [[Wistran]]. The planet was home to an [[Denash insurgents|insurgent movement]]. During the [[Batonn sector insurgency]], the Denash insurgents were [[Battle of Denash|defeated]] by [[Admiral]] [[Kinshara]].<ref name="Thrawn" />
