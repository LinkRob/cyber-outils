'''''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand'' 1''' is the first issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] limited series ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Last Stand]]''. The issue was written by [[Alex Segura]], illustrated by [[Leonard Kirk]], [[Jethro Morales]], and [[Jim Campbell]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 25]], 2024. The issue also contains the story "[[False Histories]]."

==Publisher's summary==
''A DARK FORCE STRIKES AT THE HEART OF THE JUST-FORMED NEW REPUBLIC AND SERVES AS A REMINDER THAT THE GALAXY IS FAR FROM A SAFE PLACE! Chandrila &mdash; under attack! The rise of a familiar villain &mdash; driven to the edge of sanity! LANDO CALRISSIAN and a ragtag team of pilots are attacked by an unexpected fleet! The return of HAN SOLO?!''

==Plot summary==
On the provisional home of the [[New Republic]], [[Chandrila]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] speaks with New Republic field [[agent]] [[Timra Ariz]] about moving forward after the [[death]] of Agent [[Calder Behrens]]. As they walk through a scenic park, Leia spots a group of hooded [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] descending a nearby staircase. The suicide [[bomb]]ers explode, immediately creating causalities of New Republic citizens. Ariz leaps to protect Leia and her [[Ben Solo|unborn child]]. Both are relatively unscathed by the initial explosion, and Leia springs back into action, hoping to help and stop any further attack, especially after the recent [[Liberation Day (New Republic)|Liberation Day]] [[Attack on Chandrila|attack]]. More smaller explosions ring out, and Leia soon finds a group of armed Acolytes. With no time to call for backup, she pulls out her [[blaster pistol]] to start shooting at the enemy. Ariz is impressed, but her expression soon changes when she is fatally shot in the abdomen by an Acolyte. Leia cradles the fading body of her friend, lamenting how they have fallen so far.

Above the uninhabited [[Anoat sector]] [[planet]] [[Gallowyss]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and New Republic [[pilot]] [[Rynn Zenat]] prepare to investigate a suspicious object they recently saw hurdling from the rogue [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard|Ubrik Adelhard's]] [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] [[Scepter (Imperial-class)|''Scepter'']] towards the planet. Despite the claims of the [[Anzati]] mystic [[Reyna Oskure]] claiming that Adelhard is dead, Luke senses that something was suspicious about that object. As such, he and Zenat borrowed a ship from the [[cyborg]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] to head to the planet's surface, hoping to confirm that Adelhard is truly dead.

Adelhard lives, as do the other passengers of the sabotaged [[escape pod]] launched towards the planet by the [[Trandoshan]] bounty hunter and [[assassin]] [[Kranor]] in an attempt at mercy. He is joined on the planet by [[Commander]] [[Bragh]] and a number of [[stormtrooper]]s loyal to his cause. Adelhard is aware of Skywalker and Zenat's presence and is annoyed by their persistence. Regardless, the [[governor]] has grown disillusioned following his revelation that he was never meant to be anything more than a pawn used by the Empire rather than its heir. With [[Lieutenant]] [[Crathmore]] on his way to retrieve Adelhard and company, the governor calls for Skywalker and Zenat to be brought to him in a show of his might. He is ready to decimate all who have ever tried to defy him.

Aboard the ''Scepter'', Reyna Oskure speaks with the [[hologram]] of [[Counselor to the Empire|Counselor]] [[Gallius Rax]]. He emphasizes his expectation of her loyalty leading up to the eventual [[Battle of Jakku|conflict]] on [[Jakku]]. Oskure had captured the New Republic [[spy]], [[Kith Alaytia]], who was part of Adelhard's inner circle, and she orders Kranor to take her to transport her to the [[brig]]. Along the way, Alaytia reveals that she knows Kranor let Adelhard live, which he justifies by saying it was due to his loyalty to the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]], an ally of Adelhard. Alaytia then evokes the name of [[Tomasso]], a high-level Spice Runner who collaborates with [[New Republic Intelligence]]. She asks Kranor to tell Tomasso about the death of Calder Behrens and to have him warn the Republic about Jakku. She warns him that if Oskure is able to mobilize Adelhard's forces to Jakku, it would spell disaster for everyone. 

Back on Gallowyss, Luke Skywalker and Rynn Zenat fight off stormtroopers who are behaving strangely, as if unsure of the commands they were given. One stormtrooper receives a [[Comm unit|comm]] about Crathmore's [[starship|ship]] arriving. As the troopers retreat, Luke and Rynn pursue but are unable to reach the [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class shuttle]] before Adelhard and his forces take off. Luke and Rynn rush back to their ship, the man realizing Adelhard is out of control, which makes him even more dangerous than before. The two are soon contacted by Leia, who informs them of the attack on Chandrila. She claims that it is Adelhard's doing, that he is powerful enough to enact his revenge even without his ship.

Elsewhere in the Anoat sector, [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] leads a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]]. They receive similar orders from Leia calling for them to return to Chandrila. Before they can, however, [[Therisa Alern]] spots pieces of Adelhard's coalition amassing. They evade the incoming fire and decide to fight instead of flee. 

The reports of incoming ships and the resulting battle surprise Oskure. She can hardly process it before Adelhard comes bursting in. Kranor turns on the Anzati as Adelhard plans to exact his revenge on the traitorous Acolyte.

Far away, near the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Takodana]], [[Han Solo]] receives a message from [[Weazel]]. Instead of following the lead, he realizes his friends need him and prepares to head to their aid.
