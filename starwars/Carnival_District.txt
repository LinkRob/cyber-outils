The '''Carnival District''',<ref name="RtCT">''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]''</ref> also known as the '''Fair park''', was a district of [[Lonisa City]] on [[Valo]] constructed for the [[Valo Republic Fair]]. It was divided into four zones, [[Arts and Culture zone|Arts and Culture]], [[Faith and Life zone|Faith and Life]], [[Sport and Adventure zone|Sport and Adventure]], and [[Technology and Science zone|Technology and Science]]. The district also contained the [[Lonisa City Zoo]].<ref name="Rising Storm" />
