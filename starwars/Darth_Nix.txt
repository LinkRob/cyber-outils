'''Darth Nix''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]]<ref name="ArtStation" /> who was active during the reign of [[Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire]], between [[130 ABY]] and [[138 ABY]].<ref name="Dating note">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/04/09/art-from-the-darth-maul-game-you-39-ll-never-get-to-play.aspx|text=Art From The Darth Maul Game You'll Never Get To Play|author=Andrew Reiner|date=2014-04-09|work=[[Wikipedia:Game Informer|''Game Informer'']]|archivedate=20240223064541|nolive=1}} &mdash; ''[[Battle of the Sith Lords]]'' would have taken place during the reign of [[Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire]], which was founded in [[130 ABY]] according to [[Legacy (2006) 1|''Legacy'' (2006) 1]] and dissolved in [[138 ABY]] according to [[Legacy—War 6|''Legacy—War'' 6]]. Therefore, [[Darth/Legends|Darth]] Nix must have been active between those two dates.</ref> Nix had [[Color/Legends|yellow]] [[Eye/Legends|eyes]], light-colored skin, short black [[Hair/Legends|hair]], and light blue [[Sith tattoo]]s.<ref name="ArtStation" />

Darth Nix was [[Clothing/Legends|clothed]] in a revealing outfit that exposed the majority of their body. Their legs and waist were covered in sheer black material that extended down to black boots with patterned blocks situated on each of their legs. On their head, Nix wore two spiked metal headdresses that were attached near each temple and extended above their head and below her jawline. Additionally, Nix wielded a single pink-bladed [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] with a black hilt.<ref name="ArtStation" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Nix is a character created for [[Red Fly Studio]]'s [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Battle of the Sith Lords]]''. Nix's design was created by concept artist [[Thomas A. Szakolczay]]<ref name="ArtStation" /> at some point prior to the game's cancellation in [[2011]].<ref name="GI article">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/04/09/art-from-the-darth-maul-game-you-39-ll-never-get-to-play.aspx|text=Art From The Darth Maul Game You'll Never Get To Play|author=Andrew Reiner|date=2014-04-09|work=[[Wikipedia:Game Informer|''Game Informer'']]|archivedate=20240223064541|nolive=1}}</ref> Szakolczay described Nix as "brash."<ref name="Instagram">{{Instagram|username=tasmedia|url=p/BPMO5fZgbSD|name=[[Thomas A. Szakolczay]]|date=2017-01-12|archiveurl=https://archive.vn/GP4Sf}}</ref>
