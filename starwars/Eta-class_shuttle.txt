The '''''Eta''-class shuttle''',<ref name="Starfighters" /> also known as the '''Jedi ambassador shuttle''', '''Republic executive shuttle''', or '''Republic ambassadorial shuttle''',<ref name="GI Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/grievous-intrigue-episode-guide|text="Grievous Intrigue" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> was a model of [[shuttle]] manufactured by [[Cygnus Spaceworks]] and used by the [[Galactic Senate]]<ref name="Starfighters" /> and the [[Republic Ambassadorial Corps]]<ref name="Rise" /> for diplomatic missions, and was later utilized by the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Starfighters" /> The [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]] used a modified ''Eta''-class as his [[Darth Sidious' Eta-class shuttle|personal shuttle]] in the later stages of the Clone Wars. He used the ship to travel to the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]] to thwart his former [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] [[Maul]]'s rise to power.<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref>

By the [[New Republic Era]], an [[Baylan's shuttle|''Eta''-class shuttle]] used by former [[Jedi Knight]]-turned-[[mercenary]] [[Baylan Skoll]] and his apprentice [[Shin Hati]] during the search for [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Part One">{{Ahsoka|Part One: Master and Apprentice}}</ref> The shuttle was taken by [[Bokken Jedi]] [[Ezra Bridger]], who landed it in the hangar of ''[[Home One]]''.<ref name="Part Eight">{{Ahsoka|Part Eight: The Jedi, the Witch, and the Warlord}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
[[File:Baylan_Shuttle_PartOne.png|thumb|250px|left|An aft view of an ''Eta''-class shuttle reveals its unique engine configuration.]]
Introduced before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], [[Cygnus Spaceworks]]' ''Eta''-class [[shuttle]] was study in stylish utility. A relatively small shuttlecraft<ref name="Rise" /> at 14.2 [[meter]]s in length,<ref name="Starfighters" /> the ''Eta''-class was initially designed for the [[Republic Ambassadorial Corps]] and the [[Galactic Senate]] as a VIP transport, and cost 100,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. However, with the outbreak of the war on [[Geonosis]], the shuttle were pressed into service by both the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] and the [[Jedi Order]]. Some were outfitted with three hidden triple light [[laser cannon]]s and stealth systems for use as black ops ships.<ref name="Rise" />

''Eta''-class shuttles could carry ten passengers in comfort, along with their baggage of up to 100 [[metric ton]]s for capacity and two [[Standard month|months]] worth of consumables. In addition to their cargo, the ''Eta''-class was sold with a pair of on-board folding [[Undicur-class jumpspeeder|''Undicur''-class jumpspeeders]]. The shuttle was also equipped with short-range [[sensor]]s and a [[navigation computer]], as well as a Class 2 [[hyperdrive]] generator with a Class 12 backup generator,<ref name="Rise" /> while also boasting a maximum [[Atmosphere|atmospheric]] speed of 750 [[Kilometer per hour|kilometers per hour]].<ref name="Starfighters" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Eta''-class shuttle first appeared in "[[Grievous Intrigue]]",<ref name="Grievous Intrigue">{{TCW|Grievous Intrigue}}</ref> the ninth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', which originally aired on [[January 1]], [[2010]].<ref name="GI Old EpGuide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode209.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Grievous Intrigue}}</ref> The shuttle's design was based on original concept art by [[Joe Johnston]] for,<ref name="GI Episode Guide" /> what would ultimately become, the [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]]<ref name="RoJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> for [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars:'' Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="GI Episode Guide" />

The shuttle was first identified in the current [[Canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]'', written by [[Landry Q. Walker]] and published by [[Dorling Kindersley]].<ref name="Starfighters" />
