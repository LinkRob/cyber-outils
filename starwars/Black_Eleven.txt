'''Black Eleven''' was the [[callsign]] of a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the [[Imperial Navy]]'s [[Black Squadron (Galactic Empire)|Black Squadron]]. At some point, "[["Wampa"|Wampa]]" served as Black Eleven and flew a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] in a battle against a [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]] near the [[Death Star]].<ref name="X-Wing" />
