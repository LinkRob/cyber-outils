An '''ambush on a Separatist patrol'''<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> occurred in [[21 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> as part of the [[Twi'lek Resistance]]'s [[Battle of Ryloth|guerrilla campaign]] on the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] against the occupying [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The attack saw fighters under [[General]] [[Cham Syndulla]] himself ambush a [[battle droid]] patrol, who themselves were looking for a lost patrol, among the ruins of Separatist equipment and [[droid]]s. The attack was overheard by [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]], [[clone trooper]] [[Stak]], and [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]] [[Razor]], who were searching for the rebels in order to ally together and finally free Ryloth.<ref name="Liberty" />
