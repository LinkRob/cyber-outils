'''Administrative droids''',<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> also known as '''admin units''',<ref name="TRS">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> were [[droid]]s that were present in the [[Hall of Imperial Register]] on [[Coruscant]] when [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] traveled to the [[planet]] to discover information about the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Star Destroyer]]s and [[Super Star Destroyer]]s.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> [[AD-4M]] was an administrative droid that served the [[Resistance]] at their [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|base]] on [[Ajan Kloss]].<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> At some during the [[High Republic Era]], a admin unit assisted [[Ty Yorrick]] on her missions. She found the admin unit useful, but did not give it a name. In truth, the unit was designated [[KL-03]].<ref name="TRS" />
