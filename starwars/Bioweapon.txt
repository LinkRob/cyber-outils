'''Bioweapons''', or '''biotoxins''' were deadly chemical weapons of mass destruction. Often designed to target specific [[species]], bioweapons were heavily researched by the [[Galactic Empire]], although the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] prioritized the destruction of those facilities, preventing them from using the bioweapons on a large scale.

==History and usage==
===Republic Era===
====Eiram and E'ronoh War====
{{Quote|If the peace talks are over, then why reignite the war with the klytobacter?''"<br />"''A bioweapon against Eiram. Do you not see the irony? After what the queen herself did with her poison? After she backed out of our deal to create an airborne poison and shut down her research facility before that ridiculous sham of a wedding? It's too good to pass up another opportunity to ensure the war continues. E'ronoh will deny it belongs to them, but its very existence speaks to violence in their favor. And so the war will continue. I need conflict to burn like a fire, Binnot. The Path needs it. We need the chaos. It feeds our ability to do more in this galaxy.|Binnot Ullo and the Mother|<ref name="Cataclysm" />}}
[[File:EleciaZeveron-CharactersofTheHighRepublic.png|thumb|left|250px|The Mother (pictured) dreamed of unleashing an Eirami poison on her foes.]]
Bioweapons were a threat throughout many major conflicts.<ref name="TCW:BSV">{{TCW|Blue Shadow Virus (episode)}}</ref> During the [[Republic Era]], the rival [[planet]]s of [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]] were locked into a [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|conflict]] that dragged on across the generations. Nonetheless, the usage of [[poison]] as a weapon was not befitting of [[E'roni]] combat standards. In the incarnation of the conflict that began<ref name="Convergence" /> in [[387 BBY]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Eirami royalty|Eirami Queen]] [[Adrialla]] agreed to aid from the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]], who eventually demanded she create<ref name="Convergence" /> an airborne<ref name="Cataclysm" /> [[Eirami poison|poison]] from her world's [[Thylefire scorpion]]s. While Adrialla initially did as she was asked, creating a lethal but non-airborne strand, she changed her mind, to which cult leader [[Elecia Zeveron|the Mother]] responded by dispatching her agents [[Serrena]], [[Abda]], and [[Axel Greylark]] on a mission to recover the posion for Path purposes.<ref name="Convergence" />

After Serrena attempted to persuade Adrialla into resuming poison production, to which Adrialla refused, Abda used a bit of the poison provided to her by fellow Path member [[Binnot Ullo]] to kill Serrena for her failure. Abda's own mission on Eiram and E'ronoh would end in failure, however, so she was killed with the poison by Greylark, who had a change of heart after suffering from its effects himself and hoped to destroy the poison himself. Meanwhile, the now-united monarchies of Eiram and E'ronoh&mdash;set to be unified by the [[Wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran|marriage]] of royal heirs [[Phan-tu Zenn]] and [[Xiri A'lbaran]]&mdash;decided to keep the last vials of the poison as a last-case defense mechanism for their worlds. After Greylark attempted to destroy all of the [[Erasmus Capital City]] in an effort to destroy the poison once and for all, however, the royals agreed to destroy their remaining samples.<ref name="Convergence" />

After the [[Battle of Jedha]] reopened the Eiram-E'ronoh conflict despite the wedding, the Mother [[Klytobacter incident|dispatched]] Ullo and fellow Path operative [[Goi Ganok]] to the [[Eiram system|Eiram-E'ronoh system]] aboard a [[starship]] carrying a load of [[Klytobacter]]&mdash;which was produced within the [[Path compound]] on the planet [[Dalna]]&mdash;for use as an apparent anti-Eiram biological weapon, which the Mother saw as a fitting irony for Eiram after Adrialla backed out of their own poison agreement. The weapon would look as if it was deployed by E'ronoh no matter what denial the planet offered, which would help prolong the war. In the end, however, the [[Jedi]] discovered the Path's involvement in the scheme. Ullo also made use of a [[Binnot Ullo's poison glove|poison glove]], which he would make use of as conflict ignited between the Path and Jedi until it spelled his own end.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

====Clone Wars====
{{Quote|There have been rumors that Separatists have been trying to develop an anti-clone virus&mdash;biological warfare.|Jedi General [[Tiplee]]|<ref name="Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref>}}
During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], [[Doctor]] [[Nuvo Vindi]] re-created a strain of the dreaded [[Blue Shadow Virus]], although it was destroyed before it could become widespread.<ref name="TCW:BSV" /> Rumors also spread of a Separatist effort to create an [[anti-clone virus]].<ref name="Unknown" />

===Imperial Era===
====Assorted uses====
Imperial [[Vice Admiral]] [[Perwin Gedde]] tested ancient diseases on several worlds, raining sickness from sub-orbital battleships.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref > The [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]] initiated [[Project Blackwing]] , also known as bioweapon 17-A, on the planet [[Dantooine]]. It was a virus capable of turning its host into zombie-like infected.<ref name="Commander" />

====Coyerti====
In the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Galactic Empire researched bioweapons in facilities located on planets such as [[Coyerti]], which was rich with plants and animal life. Coyerti was home to a chemical [[distillery]], which was the target of the [[61st Mobile Infantry]] during the [[Coyerti campaign]] in [[3 ABY]]. The distillery was successfully [[Attack on the Distillery|destroyed]] by a small team of Rebel operatives.<ref name="B:TC" />

====Nakadia====
Although the Distillery on Coyerti was destroyed, its weapons were used against the 61st from Coyerti's stockpiled reserves on the planet [[Nakadia]], causing the agonizing [[death]] of a number of [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldiers]] including [[Maediyu]].<ref name="B:TC" />
