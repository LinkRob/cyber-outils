A '''blurrg''' was the mount and companion for the [[Twi'lek]] freedom fighter [[Cham Syndulla]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Liberty" /> and into the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Heroes">{{Rebels|Hera's Heroes}}</ref> It fought alongside Syndulla during the [[Battle of Ryloth]] in [[21 BBY]]<ref name="Liberty" /> and also during missions on the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Heroes" />

==Biology and appearance==
As a blurrg, Syndulla's blurrg had short arms, powerful legs, sharp teeth, and a large tongue. Unlike other [[blurrg]]s, it had distinct white markings on its head and face.<ref name="Liberty" />
