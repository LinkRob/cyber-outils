'''Aki-Ktura''' was an [[Aki-Aki]] [[farm]]ing community on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Pasaana]]. It had a population of 3,000.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aki-Ktura was first mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
