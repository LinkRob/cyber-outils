'''''Affirmation for Amber Day Six: My Love Flows Ambiently and Infinitely''''' was a piece of work about love written in [[High Galactic alphabet]] by [[Unidentified Whiphid (Affirmation for Amber Day Six)|a Whiphid]].<ref name="MAGL">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
''Affirmation for Amber Day Six: My Love Flows Ambiently and Infinitely'' was mentioned in a segment of the same name, written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that collects concept designs and photographs from various [[George Lucas]] projects.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]].<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM}}</ref>
