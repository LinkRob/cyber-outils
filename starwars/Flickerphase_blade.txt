A '''flickerphase blade''' was a [[lightsaber]] that had been modified to allow its blade to ignite and extinguish at random intervals. Although [[Lightsaber combat|combat]] with flickerphase blades could be more difficult than an ordinary lightsaber, it was challenging for an opponent to effectively block the weapon, a fact which caused the [[Jedi Order]] to generally view them as dishonorable and unsporting. Flickerphase blades cost 5,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] per unit and never saw widespread use.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flickerphase blades were mentioned in ''[[Knights of Fate]]'',<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> a [[2018]]<ref name="Way of the Warrior">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/6/21/way-of-the-warrior|text=Way of the Warrior}}</ref> sourcebook published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for use with its ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[roleplaying game]].<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
