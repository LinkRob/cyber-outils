The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''21 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3256 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7956''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Count]] [[Dooku]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and Anakin Skywalker are captured by the pirate [[Hondo Ohnaka]] on [[Florrum]], but ultimately [[Mission to Florrum|escape]].<ref name="GG">{{TCW|The Gungan General}}</ref>
*Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano help to [[Defense of Maridun|defend]] [[Lurmen]] on [[Maridun]] from [[Trade Federation]] [[General]] [[Lok Durd]].<ref name="DoP">{{TCW|Defenders of Peace}}</ref>
*The native [[Talz]] are discovered on [[Pantora]]'s planet [[Orto Plutonia]], and a [[One-Day War|brief war]] is fought between the [[Pantoran]]s and Talz, which is resolved by the Jedi and Pantoran [[Senator]] [[Riyo Chuchi]].<ref name="Trespass">{{TCW|Trespass}}</ref>
*In an attempt to create anti-Jedi biological weapons,<ref name="Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] hires [[Doctor]] [[Nuvo Vindi]] to recreate the [[Blue Shadow Virus]]. Vindi is given a [[Trade Federation laboratory|laboratory]] on [[Naboo]] as well as [[battle droid]]s to serve as security. Vindi creates an airborne strand of the virus, but is stopped and captured by the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Blue Shadow Virus">{{TCW|Blue Shadow Virus (episode)}}</ref><ref name="GA" />
*[[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic forces]] under [[Jedi General]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] [[First Battle of Felucia|combat]] the [[Separatist Droid Army]] on [[Felucia]]. The [[Galactic Republic]] is forced to flee, however, resulting in a Republic defeat.<ref name="HH">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref>
*The [[Unidentified Zillo Beast|last]] [[Zillo Beast]] is captured by the Republic and presumed dead. [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] orders it to be [[Cloning|cloned]].<ref name="GA" />
*The Confederacy of Independent Systems takes back [[Geonosis]]; however, the Galactic Republic launches a costly [[Second Battle of Geonosis|assault]] and retakes the [[Planet|world]]. Due to their victory, the Republic is able to capture [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] member [[Poggle the Lesser]].<ref name="GA" />
*The [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[Death Star plans|plans]] are first revealed to the [[Strategic Advisory Cell]] by Dr. [[Gubacher]] and [[Professor]] [[Sahali]].<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>
*[[Aurra Sing]] is hired by [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure]] to assassinate [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], targeting the Senator at the [[Alderaan Refugee Conference]]. The [[bounty hunter]] is stopped by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], who accompanied Amidala to the conference after [[Force vision|foreseeing]] the attempt.<ref name="Assassin">{{TCW|Assassin (episode)}}</ref>
*The Confederacy of Independent Systems launches a massive [[Battle of Kamino|attack]] on the [[Clone trooper|clone]] home planet of [[Kamino]].<ref name="GA" />
*The [[Trade Federation]] [[Blockade of Pantora|blockades]] [[Pantora]] in an attempt to force the [[moon]] to join the Separatist Alliance, sending bounty hunters to [[Kidnapping of the Papanoida daughters|kidnap]] [[Pantoran]] [[Supreme Chairman]] [[Notluwiski Papanoida]]'s daughters as well. The daughters are [[Rescue in Chalmun's Cantina|rescued]] from [[Tatooine]] and [[Rescue on the Pantora Droid Control Ship|the Droid Control Ship]] by the Chairman, his son [[Ion Papanoida|Ion]], [[Senator]] [[Riyo Chuchi]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], forcing the blockade to end.<ref name="Sphere of Influence">{{TCW|Sphere of Influence}}</ref>
*[[Cad Bane]] holds the [[Galactic Senate]] [[Senate hostage crisis|hostage]] in order to free [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] the [[Hutt]] from [[prison]].<ref name="HC">{{TCW|Hostage Crisis}}</ref>
*The Separatists notably order more than three million battle droids with financial help from the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]].<ref name="PoP">{{TCW|Pursuit of Peace}}</ref>
