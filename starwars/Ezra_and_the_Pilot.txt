'''''Ezra and the Pilot''''' is a [[World of Reading]] Level 2 young readers book in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' series that was released on [[August 5]], [[2014]]. The story was adapted by [[Lucasfilm]] senior editor [[Jennifer Heddle]] based on the script for the ''Star Wars Rebels'' prequel short "[[Property of Ezra Bridger]]," written by series executive producer [[Simon Kinberg]].

{{Book-stub}}

==Plot summary==
[[Lothal]] orphan [[Ezra Bridger]] witnesses a battle in the skies overhead between the [[VCX-100 light freighter|starship]] ''[[Ghost]]'' and a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. After the TIE fighter is shot down and crashes to the ground, Bridger rushes to the wrecked [[Starship|ship]] and finds that its [[cockpit]] hatch will not open, leaving the TIE [[Starfighter|fighter's]] [[Valen Rudor|pilot]] trapped inside. Bridger eventually opens the hatch, but the [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] shows no gratitude for being saved and instead angrily rebukes Bridger for touching his ship.

To teach the arrogant pilot a lesson, Bridger steals several components from the TIE fighter, including the pilot's [[flight helmet]]. Angered further, the pilot opens fire on Bridger with the TIE fighter's [[laser cannon]]s, but Bridger incapacitates the pilot with his [[energy slingshot]]. The pilot's helmet now belongs to Bridger.

==Continuity==
