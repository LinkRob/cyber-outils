The '''Chiss Expansionary Fleet medcenter''' was located on the [[Unknown Regions]] [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[planet]] [[Naporar]]. Several [[Standard month|months]] before<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' takes place around [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. As the novel states that [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] was injured during the [[Vagaari pirate operations]], which ''Chaos Rising'' states happened [[Standard month|months]] prior to the novel's main events, the operations must have occured months before 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the [[Chiss]] [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," clashed against the [[Vagaari pirates]] during the [[Vagaari pirate operations]] and was injured in the process. Following the conclusion of the operations, Thrawn underwent his last round of physical therapy in the [[medcenter]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Around 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="DateCR">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' takes place around [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] [[Supreme General]] [[Ba'kif]] sent a [[shuttle]] to the [[medic]]al facility to retrieve Thrawn as he was in need of the senior captain's assistance for a [[Attack on the edge of the Dioya system|mission]] to the Ascendancy's [[Dioya system]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chiss Expansionary Fleet medcenter was mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
