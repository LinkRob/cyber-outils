An '''air strike team''' was a sub-unit of the [[planet]] [[Desix]]'s [[Desix droid battalion|droid battalion]]. It was comprised of six [[B1-series battle droid]]s, four of which were armed with [[RPS-6 rocket launcher]]s and two with [[E-5 blaster rifle]]s and led by the [[Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Desix)|T-series tactical droid]] responsible for the entirety of [[Desix City]]'s [[Desix Separatist holdout|forces]].<ref name="Solitary" />
==History==
The air strike team was a group of B1-series battle droids that were reactivated by [[Governor|governor]] [[Tawni Ames]] of [[Desix]] sometime after the end of the [[Clone Wars]] as part of [[Desix Separatist holdout|a Separatist holdout]] that was organized into a [[Desix droid battalion|droid battalion]] that was tasked with maintaining Desix's independence from the newly formed [[Galactic Empire]]. The battalion would be mobilized following the arrival of [[Grotton]] who, accompanied by a squad of [[TK stormtrooper]]s, was sent by the Empire to take Ames' place as governor of Desix only to be captured along with his men. Upon news of this reaching the Imperial authorities, a [[Crosshair and Cody's team|strike team]] of [[Clone trooper|clone stormtroopers]] under the joint-command of [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] and [[Clone commando|clone commando]] [[Crosshair]] was sent to rescue Grotton and install him as Desix's governor.<ref name="Solitary" />

Arriving on [[Imperial Shuttle 995]], the air strike team was deployed and fired upon the [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle]] with their [[RPS-6 rocket launcher]]s and damaged the shuttle with the finishing blow being given by the [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I]] that the battalion possessed in their arsenal and successfully downed the shuttle. While downing the shuttle, the strike team survived and quickly went [[Mission to Desix|into action]] with all clone troopers who weren't injured going into battle and heading for the main gate of [[Desix City]]. After Crosshair took out the AAT, the strike team remained at their post and fired upon the advancing clones with several of them being killed by the team and their rocket launchers, however Crosshair successfully dispatched the droids and quickly eliminated them before they could inflict further casualties and relieved the team, allowing them to advance into the city and engage the rest of the battalion which would be eliminated along with Ames herself by the end of the battle.<ref name="Solitary" />
