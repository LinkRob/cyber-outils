'''Bar the Rotter''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Duros]] [[Forger (profession)|forger]] and member of the [[Church of Infinite Perception]] who had a [[Alcohol|drinking]] problem and lived in the [[Cellar of Penitence]] on the [[planet]] [[Florrum]]. He owned a [[Unidentified polished stone|polished stone]] that he used for [[meditation]] on the church's teachings. He worked with [[Hondo Ohnaka]] and assisted him during an [[First auction of Maz Kanata's Journal|auction]] for a fake [[Smuggler's Guide|Maz Kanata's Journal]].<ref name="SM" />
