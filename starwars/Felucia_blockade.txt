During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] dispatched a '''blockade''' of [[Felucia]] and large amount of [[Separatist Droid Army|ground forces]]. These forces kept the [[Galactic Republic]] at bay from the [[planet]] that was the headquarters of the [[Commerce Guild]], as well as control of the [[Perlemian Trade Route]]. The [[blockade]] resulted in the [[First Battle of Felucia]], which led to a Confederacy victory.<ref name="Collapse" /> The naval complement of the blockade consisted of at least four [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class frigates]], one of which was destroyed.<ref name="Holocron Heist">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref>
