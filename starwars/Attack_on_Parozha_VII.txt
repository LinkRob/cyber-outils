The '''attack on Parozha VII''' was carried out in<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> around [[5 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. The [[fighter wing]]'s [[squadron]]s [[Squadron Two (Shadow Wing)|Two]], [[Squadron Three (Shadow Wing)|Three]] and [[Squadron Four (Shadow Wing)|Four]] swarmed the [[Parozha VII]] supply [[space station]], which formerly supplied the Empire but had since turned to the [[New Republic]], and destroyed the docked [[starship]]s. The cargo bays were then blasted open by the 204th squadrons, suffocating the inhabitants and ejecting the cargo, which was collected by the<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="New Villain">{{SW|url=news/shadow-fall-novel-first-look-at-new-villain-and-more|text=''Shadow Fall'' Novel: First Look at New Villain and More}}</ref> ''[[Edict]]'' through the vessel's [[tractor beam]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on Parozha VII was mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
