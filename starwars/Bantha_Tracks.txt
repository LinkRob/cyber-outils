'''Bantha Tracks''' was a location in the marketplace of [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet [[Batuu]]. [[Blue milk]] was available there for purchase, with the milk being sold to the location beforehand from the [[Saka Homestead]] located just on the outskirts of the outpost.<ref name="Trading Card" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:SakaHomesteadSWGEcard.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Trading card mentioning Bantha Tracks]]
This location was first mentioned on a promotional trading card for [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] park area released during the Galactic Nights event on [[December 16]], [[2017]]. The [[Saka Homestead]] map card states blue milk can be found at Bantha Tracks in the marketplace.<ref name="ITM Twitter">{{Twitter|InsideTheMagic|status/942387264084238337|Inside the Magic|archivedate=20200122020432}}</ref> Another card, called "BLUE MILK DELIVERED FRESH FROM THE FARM," depicted a male human delivering containers of blue milk to a market stand.<ref name="Disney Geek">{{WebCite|url=https://blog.disneygeek.com/2018/01/05/star-wars-galaxys-edge-trading-cards|text=Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge Trading Cards|author=Jason|date=2018-01-05|work=[https://blog.disneygeek.com The Geek's Blog]|archivedate=20210228085631}}</ref>

Though the card suggested a connection between Saka Homestead and the blue milk at Bantha Tracks,<ref name="Trading Card" /> the theme park does not have a location with that name. Blue milk is instead sold at the [[Milk Stand]] which also offers a [[Bubo Wamba Family Farms]] [[Bubo Wamba Family Farms Novelty Drink Vessel|souvenir drink vessel]],<ref name="SWGECite">{{GEAttr|Milk Stand}}</ref> and the stand's [[in-universe]] owner is [[Bubo Wamba]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> The [[:File:Black-spire-outpost-map-1500.jpg|Black Spire Outpost map]] which is available with the Barnes & Noble Exclusive Edition of ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' does not contain the names "Bantha Tracks" or "Saka Homestead," but still includes the illustration of [[bantha]]s.<ref name="GE:BS">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref> It is unknown if these all represent different locations or a change in the naming of the stand and farm before the park's opening.

[[Bantha Tracks (department)|''Bantha Tracks'']] is also the name of a well known ''Star Wars'' fan newsletter.
