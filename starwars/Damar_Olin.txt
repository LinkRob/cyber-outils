'''Damar Olin''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[smuggler]] who, for a [[time]], was the partner of [[Izal Garsea]]. Olin eventually met [[Ana Tolla]] and her [[Ana Tolla's crew|crew]] and joined them.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

==Biography==
===Early life===
Damar was born to a privileged family who was rich enough to afford a chauffer who taught him how to drive. Eventually he joined the [[Smuggler|smuggling]] business and met [[Izal Garsea]]. Both of them tried to survive on their own, but eventually had to join [[Ana Tolla]] [[Ana Tolla's crew|crew]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

===First Order-Resistance War===
After a mission with the crew, Damar was on Actyllon in a polluted city. That day was Izzy's birthday and he had promised to have a fireworks celebration. He forgot. The crew visited a cantina, but after a firefight broke out, he chose to abandon Izzy in favor of them.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

Eventually the crew made their way to [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. There they once again ran into Izzy, who was delivering a [[Pall Gopal's package]] for [[Pall Gopal]]. Ana Tolla told her to stay out of her way and stop following them. Izzy replied that she was doing her own job.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

Damar was eventually sent to [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]] to intimidate [[Julen Rakab]] and Izzy. He offered them a place in the crew, but they both denied.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

===Skirmish at Kat Saka's Farm===
Ana Tolla's job was to plant [[sodilium detonator]]s all across [[Kat Saka]]'s [[farm]] because she refused to sell it to large companies. These would destroy the soil and make it infertile for hundreds of years. Damar teamed up with [[Delta]] and they kidnapped Jules and Izzy. Damar took Jules to the farm where he had him plant the bombs. They waited for Delta to arrive, but realized that she wasn't coming. Delta had actually formed a pact with Izzy and gotten a [[Julen Rakab's Avent100-series light freighter|ship]] with [[Tap]] and [[Volt Vescuso|Volt]] to retake the farm. Just as the ship arrived, Jules broke free and stunned a crew member.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

Later Damar held Jules at gunpoint with his weapon knocked away. A scream echoed in the night and Jules saw his chance. He reached for his blaster and stunned him.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

When Damar woke up, he was being taken away to [[Oga Garra]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Damar Olin appeared in ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', a [[2019]] young-adult novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge (media project)|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge multimedia project]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />
