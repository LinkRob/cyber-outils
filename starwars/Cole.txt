'''Cole''' was a [[Clone Sergeant]] who served the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] before going [[Desertion|rogue]] with three other [[clone trooper]]s, [[Charger (Bad Batch)|Charger]], [[Hank]], and [[Rice (clone trooper)|Rice]]. Cole wore customized [[phase I clone trooper armor]] with [[Color|red]] markings, a [[kama]], a [[pauldron]] and a [[rangefinder]] on their [[helmet]]. The [[Cole's squad|squad]] of rogue [[Cloning|clones]], led by Cole, became [[bounty hunter]]s and took on a [[bounty]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] to locate the [[scientist]] [[Ergin Lazlo]].<ref name="RogueAgents1" />

During their search, the squad crossed paths with another clone squad, [[Clone Force 99]], who were also attempting to locate the scientist, but for the Republic instead of the Separatists. Unknown to be rogue clones, Cole and his men pretended that they had been sent on the same mission for the Republic as Clone Force 99, with Cole inviting the squad onto their [[Starship|ship]] to work together. Clone Force 99's leader, [[Hunter]], was suspicious of Cole and his men, but agreed to come aboard, while having another member of Clone Force 99, [[Tech]], stay back and look into the group of clones. Eventually, Tech discovered that the clones were rogue, but just as he attempted to warn Hunter and the other two clones of Clone Force 99, [[Crosshair]] and [[Wrecker]], Cole had his men shove the clones into a compartment of the ship which housed a [[rancor]]. Clone Force 99 survived the encounter with the rancor by blasting a hole in the side of the ship and escaping into [[Realspace|space]] where Tech picked them up. When Rice suggested destroying Clone Force 99's [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|ship]], Cole allowed them to escape so Clone Force 99 could do the work for them.<ref name="RogueAgents1" />
