'''''Doctor Aphra'' 18''' is the eighteenth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 19]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''"CONJURATIONS" With STRANGE RITUAL MURDERS on the rise, DOCTOR APHRA and SANA STARROS' hunt for HOWLING ARTIFACTS is growing dire! Their leads keep turning up dead, and they're running out of time! They'll have to delve deep into
the secrets of an ANCIENT TECH CULT if they plan to catch the killer!''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=CONJURATIONS
|text=Rogue archeologist Doctor Aphra and smuggler Sana Starros have been put through their paces&ndash;
from encounters with Crimson Dawn, to being betrayed by Just Lucky and Ariole, to surviving an explosive
brush with an ancient piece of Ascendant technology called the Thought Dowser, those two have been busy.

And now, as if things weren't crazy enough, Aphra's old frenemy, Kho Phon Farrus, has returned! Kho plans to
assemble as much Ascendant tech as possible, but not if Aphra has anything to say about it&hellip;.}}
