The '''Dowutin''' were a [[species]] of large [[humanoid]]s that were [[Homeworld|native]] to the frigid [[planet]] [[Dowut]], which was located in the [[Core Worlds]]. Dowutins were known for their great size and strength, facial horns, and long lifespans.

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:DowutinJedi-KoF.png|thumb|left|250px|A Dowutin Jedi]]
The Dowutin [[species]] evolved from a type of [[Diet|omnivorous]] [[Dowutin precursor species|herd animal]] on the [[planet]] [[Dowut]]. They were large, aggressive-looking [[humanoid]]s with chin horns, sharp claws, and thick skin<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> that were commonly brown in [[color]]<ref name="TFAVD" /><ref name="Dark Lord 6">[[Darth Vader (2017) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 6]]</ref> and had poor [[eye]]sight but superior senses of hearing and smell. Dowutins had large fingers with claw-like points at the tip, although these were more comparable to tusks due to their thickness and bony complexion. Dowutin skull ridges were slightly less thick on the sides of their heads, behind their ears.<ref name="Hyperspace Stories">[[Hyperspace Stories—Codebreaker 3|''Hyperspace Stories—Codebreaker'' 3]]</ref> Capable of living [[Standard century|hundreds]] of [[Standard year|years]], Dowutins never stopped growing. Individuals who had lived two decades were about the size of [[Wookiee]]s, and those who lived to their fifth century could weigh up to a metric ton<ref name="KoF" /> and were typically so massive that their loud footsteps and voices warned other species away.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> The largest recorded Dowutin weighed over two tons and stood over four meters tall.<ref name="KoF" />

==Society and culture==
The Dowutin species was native to the [[Temperature|frigid]]<ref name="KoF" /> [[Core Worlds]] planet of Dowut near the [[Unknown Regions]]<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> and spoke the [[Dowuta]] [[language]]. They were often solitary.<ref name="KoF" /> Young Dowutins were forced to fend for themselves from an early age<ref name="Alien Archive" /> upon being thrown out of their nest. Forced to survive in the wild and the colds,<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> those who quickly learned hunting skills survived.<ref name="Alien Archive" /> Their size and strength made them prized as hired muscle, but most Dowutins' pride dissuaded them from such work, as they viewed other species as weak.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

Rumors existed that Dowutins did not ever [[Death|die]] due to old age, instead perishing in battle or greeting death by other means.<ref name="KoF" />

==History==
Dowutins were known for their [[Mercenary|mercenaries]].<ref name="Commander" /> One Dowutin was a part of a [[gladiator]]ial program run by the [[Xonti Brothers]]. They put the Dowutin through harsh training that [[Death|killed]] three of the other members. It faced off with the other remaining member, a [[Wookiee]] named [[Krrsantan]], and was struck down in one punch.<ref name="Aphra Annual">[[Doctor Aphra Annual 1|''Doctor Aphra Annual'' 1]]</ref>

A female Dowutin known as [[Ninth Sister|Masana Tide]]<ref name="Dark Side">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side]]''</ref> was originally a member of the [[Jedi Order]] before she joined [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Inquisitorius]] program and later became known as the Ninth [[Sister (Inquisitorius)|Sister]].<ref name="Dark Lord 6" /> [[Grummgar]], a member of this species, was present at [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] with the [[spy]] [[Bazine Netal]] just before the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Takodana|attacked]] the castle.<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Maz_castle_cross_section.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Patrons of Maz Kanata's castle, including Grummgar]]
The Dowutin were first pictured in a [[Wikipedia:Vanity Fair (magazine)|''Vanity Fair'']] article released on [[May 4]], [[2015]] which included a group photo of characters in Maz Kanata's castle from the production of the then-upcoming film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="Vanity Fair">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2015/05/star-wars-force-awakens-photos|text=Adam Driver and Lupita Nyong'o's Characters Revealed in Exclusive Star Wars: The Force Awakens Photos|author=Leibovitz, Annie|date=2015-05-04|archivedate=20191210223714}}</ref> The species made its first appearance before the release of ''The Force Awakens'' with an expansion added to the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''.<ref name="Commander" />
