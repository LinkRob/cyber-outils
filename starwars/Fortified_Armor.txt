The '''Fortified Armor''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Bounty Hunter (Imara Vex)|standard costume]]. The [[Clothing|outfit]] and [[armor]] resembled the armor commonly worn by the [[Gen'Dai]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Durge]], with a primarily [[Color|gray]], silver, and grayish-blue color scheme, featuring a pink inner vest, dark blue designs on the outer vest and collar wrappings, red arrow-like decals on the kneepads, and pinkish rectangles covering the shins. The helmet also featured a glowing green visor, and a cylindrical [[shoulder-mounted armament]] housing a black payload.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fortified Armor was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Imara Vex, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.20.0  on [[March 28]], [[2024]] during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Armored Rifle]]. This costume could be unlocked by completing "Part 1" of the game's "Welcome to the Arena!" introductory Quest.<ref name="Hunters" />
