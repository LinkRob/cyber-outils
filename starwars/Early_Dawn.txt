The '''Early Dawn''' was a bodily configuration owned by the [[droideka]] [[Team Slingshot droideka|member]] of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]] that she could swap for her standard [[Beloved Paintwork|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This configuration featuring a [[Color|gray]] frame with a glossy blue paint scheme, displaying black and white checkered patterns on the shoulders, as well as complex yellow and white designs on the head plating. Glowing orange [[sensor]]s also extended from the front of the face.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Early Dawn was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dawn Racer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
