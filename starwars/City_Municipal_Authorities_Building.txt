The '''City Municipal Authorities Building''' was a structure located in the [[Federal District]] of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]], the [[ecumenopolis]] that covered the [[Galactic Republic]] [[capital]] [[Coruscant]] in the [[Core Worlds]]. [[Color|Gray]] in color, the top of the building consisted of a spire that emerged above [[Level 5127]], the highest level of Coruscant during the [[Invasion of Naboo|Naboo Crisis]]<ref name="SWCL2016" /> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> A district of seedy [[restaurant]]s and malls was found several levels down from the surface on one side of the building, and the [[Diplomat Hotel]] and [[Galactic Courts of Justice]] were also located nearby.<ref name="SWCL2016" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the City Municipal Authorities Building was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="SWCL2016" /> from the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]], illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]], and marked the first mention of the [[City Municipal Authorities Building/Legends|building]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref> It then appeared for the first time in Legends in ''[[The Clone Wars: Decide Your Destiny: Crisis on Coruscant]]'', a [[2010]] installment in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Decide Your Destiny]]'' book series written by [[Jonathan Green]].<ref name="Destiny">''[[The Clone Wars: Decide Your Destiny: Crisis on Coruscant]]''</ref>
