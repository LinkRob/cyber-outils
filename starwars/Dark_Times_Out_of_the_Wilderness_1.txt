'''''Dark Times—Out of the Wilderness'' 1''' was the eighteenth issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Times]]'' comic series and the first in the [[Star Wars: Dark Times—Out of the Wilderness|''Out of the Wilderness'']] story arc. It was first announced in [[Star Wars Insider 124|''Star Wars Insider'' 124]] and was published in [[August]] of [[2011]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Darth Vader confronts a deadly assassin . . .
. . . the outlaw crew of the Uhumele arrives at the scene of Jedi Dass Jennir's last adventure—but Jennir is already gone . . .
. . . and half a galaxy away, Imperial fighters are shot out of the sky by an "unarmed" pleasure craft . . .''
