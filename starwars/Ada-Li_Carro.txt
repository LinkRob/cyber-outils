'''Ada-Li Carro''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Jedi Master]] who served on the [[Jedi High Council]] during the [[High Republic Era]].

==Biography==
Carro was present in person for a Council vote regarding the participation of the [[Jedi Order]] in action against the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization. After ten of the eleven Masters present voted, the vote came to a tie, which was settled by Master [[Jora Malli]], who resolved to accompany the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[fleet]] in pursuing the marauders.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

==Personality and traits==
Carro was adept at architecture and used her expertise in designing the plans for [[Jedi outpost]]s across the [[galaxy]]. Her designs incorporated modern technology with classical styles. She also came up with a technique to restore and update older buildings.<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi" />

==Equipment==
Carro wore white and gold [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]], and wielded a [[Ada-Li Carro's lightsaber|blue-bladed lightsaber]].<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 1">[[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 1]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Ada-Li Carro was created for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' publishing initiative and first appeared as a [[hologram]] in the novel ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'', written by [[Charles Soule]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> She was first pictured in the fourth episode of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Show]]'' on [[July 28]], [[2021]].<ref name="High Republic Council" /> Younger concepts of Carro were created by concept artist [[Ornella Savarese]] during the character's development, depicting the Jedi with a red brown sash.<ref name="Art of THR">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume I]]''</ref>
