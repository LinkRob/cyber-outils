'''Ansen Strung''' was a [[Dowutin]] [[Gender|male]] [[bounty hunter]] who later worked as a creature tracker during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was hired by the [[human]] [[Raena Zess]] to find her missing pet [[kibbin]] [[Figgles]]. With the help of the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Nubs]], and [[Lys Solay]], Strung managed to return the missing kibbin back to Zess. He later went missing after accepting a job by the [[farmer]] [[Weebo]] to locate a missing [[burowga]], which worried his younger sister [[Senna Strung]].

==Biography==
===A missing pet===
Around [[233 BBY]],<ref name="YJA Date">{{YJADate|107B}}</ref> Ansen Strung was a [[bounty hunter]] who went to the [[forest]] [[planet]] [[Tenoo]] after he was hired by the [[human]] [[Raena Zess]] to find her missing pet [[kibbin]] [[Figgles]]. He searched the town of [[Kublop Springs]], though Strung attracted the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Nubs]], and [[Lys Solay]] after making a mess around the [[Kublop Springs marketplace|marketplace]]. Brightstar introduced himself and his friends to Strung. Strung introduced himself as a [[bounty]] hunter, which confused Nubs, who did not know what that was. Strung explained that a bounty hunter was someone who was paid to find things—usually people. He then told the [[youngling]]s what he had been hired to do: find a missing pet kibbin.  Recognizing the creature, Solay opened a [[datapad]] and showed him the [[Galactic Creature Guide]] of kibbins.<ref name="The Missing Kibbin" />

Strung chased after a [[Faloen hopper]], when he ran into Brightstar, Solay, and Nubs again. He asked them if they had seen the Faloen hopper, and then with their help caught the Faloen hopper. He was then noticed by [[Rhugo Tar]], his old bounty hunter partner. The Jedi introduced themselves and Tar revealed he was after the starship thief known as [[Sty Trune]], who after a chase that damaged both their ships, had came to Tenoo for repairs. Tar said that he had put a tracker on Trune's ship, and asked for Strung to help him. Strung agreed to help, and together they travelled to the planet [[Aludia]]. Strung noticed Trune's ship and began to look for footprints in the area, and despite disapproval from Tar, the Jedi joined in too. When Nubs found Trune's footprints, Strung congratulated him. Tar got upset that his method of Arial surveillance did not work, and argued with Strung, before they continued on in chase of Trune. When they found Trune tied up to a tree, Strung wanted to sneak up on him, but Tar just wanted to go in. Trune taunted Tar as he hung upside down, but when Strung stepped on a twig, Tar got distracted, which allowed Trune to grab Tar's knife and break free. Luckily, the Jedi and Strung surrounded Trune, but Tar got mad at Strung using his creature tracking skills. When Trune realized that the Jedi and Strung were looking for creatures, he faked seeing a [[Aludian trumble pig]], and was able to run away. Tar and Strung began to bicker some more, and while attempting to capture Trune, they missed and caught the Jedi in a net instead. Following some motivation from Solay, Strung and Tar agreed to work together and they freed the Jedi before chasing after Trune, who was escaping in the ship the Jedi, Strung, and Tar arrived in. Together Strung and Tar used a smoke ball, which was a common tool used by both bounty hunters and creature trackers, to force Trune out of the ship, where they then put him in handcuffs and congratulated the team. Back on Tenoo, Strung and Tar exchange a goodbye and offer to help each other whenever they need it.<ref name="Big Brother's Bounty" />
