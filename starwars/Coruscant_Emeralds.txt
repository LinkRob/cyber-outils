The '''Coruscant Emeralds''' were a [[grav-ball]] team that played on the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. [[Edi Myrtaan]], a [[Keeper (grav-ball)|keeper]] for the team, had a significant fan following and appeared in a number of recruitment materials for the [[Galactic Empire]]. After disappearing, Myrtann later resurfaced as a member of a [[Unidentified rebel cell (Sullust)|rebel cell]] on<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> [[Sullust]], after which the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] quickly adapted its [[propaganda]] to make use of him.<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned the Coruscant Emeralds in<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Coruscant Emeralds were first mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise and Fall" />
