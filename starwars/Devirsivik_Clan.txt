The '''Devirsivik Clan''' was a [[Hutt Clan]] active during the [[High Republic Era]]. Members of the family included the [[Hutt]]s [[Churo]] and [[Dahara Devirsivik Norri]], and their [[Churo and Dahara's mother|mother]] and [[Churo and Dahara's father|father]]. Churo and Dahara had a cousin named [[Cedraa]], who owned a [[Cedraa's palace|palace]] on [[Tarnoonga]] and ran an [[Cedraa's animal export business|animal export business]], and another cousin named [[Lorko]]. The clan operated a [[Devirsivik Clan's business|business]] and had a small [[Devirsivik Clan's fleet|fleet]].<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />
