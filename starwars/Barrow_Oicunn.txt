'''Barrow Oicunn''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[lieutenant]] in the [[Republic Military|military]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[19 BBY]], he was a hero of the [[Battle of Coruscant]] and his devotion caught the attention of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].

After the Republic [[Proclamation of the New Order|transitioned]] into the [[Galactic Empire]], Oicunn became an [[Imperial officer|officer]] in the [[Imperial Navy]]. Earning the rank of [[captain]], he was one of the first [[officer]]s selected to serve aboard a [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Death Star)|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] tasked with protecting the [[Project Stardust|construction]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]].

==Biography==
Barrow Oicunn was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served in the [[Republic Military|military forces]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. As a [[lieutenant]], he participated in the [[Battle of Coruscant]]<ref name="Force Collection" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> in which the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Separatist Navy]] launched an attack on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], [[capital]] of the Republic, but were ultimately defeated by the [[Republic Military|Republic forces]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> Oicunn was a hero of the battle, and his devotion caught the [[eye]] of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Force Collection" />

[[File:LtOicunn.png|thumb|left|300px|Barrow Oicunn served in the Imperial Navy.]]
Shortly thereafter, the Confederacy was defeated and the Clone Wars came to an end, after which the Republic [[Proclamation of the New Order|transitioned]] into the [[Galactic Empire]] with Palpatine declared [[Galactic Emperor]].<ref name="ROTS" /> Oicunn became a member of the [[Imperial Navy]]'s [[Imperial Navy's officer corps|officer corps]] and was selected as one of the first [[Imperial officer]]s to serve aboard [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Death Star)|one]] of the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] tasked with protecting the top-secret [[Project Stardust|Imperial project]] to construct the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]<ref name="Force Collection" /> in the [[orbit]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> The vessel upon which Oicunn served<ref name="ROTS" /> was also newly-constructed, coming fresh from the [[Santhe Shipyards]].<ref name="Catalyst" />

The Venator also carried Palpatine, his [[Sith apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], and [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to the [[Death Star]]<ref name="ROTS" /> for an inspection.<ref name="Insider 226">{{InsiderCite|226|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> The presence of such high ranking figures allowed the [[Star Destroyer]] to [[Lightspeed|hyperspace jump]] directly to Geonosis from the [[Core Worlds]] without clearance from [[Sentinel Base]].<ref name="Catalyst" /> During the trio's visit, Oicunn worked at a console in one of the crew pits of the [[Star Destroyer]]'s [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] while Palpatine, Vader, and Tarkin oversaw the Death Star's construction through the bridge's [[viewport]].<ref name="ROTS" /> Oicunn would go on to attain the rank of [[captain]] and at some point [[pilot]]ed a [[VT-49 Decimator]] for the Empire against an [[X-wing starfighter]], destroying the enemy [[Starfighter|fighter]] above a terrestrial body.<ref name="X-wing">{{FFGXW2|set=Galactic Empire Conversion Kit|cardname=Captain Oicunn}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
[[File:CaptainOiccunnVT49Decimator-XWM.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Oicunn piloted a VT-49 Decimator.]]
Although fully committed to the cause of the Republic, Oicunn viewed it as weak. He was loyal above all else and his devotion was enough to be noticed by Palpatine. Oicunn had light skin, [[Color|brown]] [[hair]], and blue [[eye]]s.<ref name="Force Collection" />

==Equipment==
While serving the Empire, Oicunn wore a gray [[Imperial officer's uniform]] including a [[Hat|cap]] with an [[officer's disk]]. The uniform also included black [[boot]]s, black [[glove]]s, and a black [[belt]] also bearing a disk. He bore a [[rank insignia plaque]] with four blue squares upon his left [[breast]].<ref name="Force Collection" /> He piloted a VT-49 Decimator.<ref name="X-wing" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:VenatorBridgeSet-365Days.png|thumb|left|250px|Oicunn's scene was filmed at Fox Studios.]]
Barrow Oicunn first appeared in the [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="ROTS" /> which was released on [[May 19]], [[2005]].<ref name="YbY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> Oicunn's scene was filmed [[September 17]], [[2003]] at [[Fox Studios, Sydney]].<ref name="Making">''[[The Making of Star Wars Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref> Only one of the bridge's two crew pits was created for the set, with a different frame range of the footage of Oicunn and other characters in the pit then being mirrored with some small alterations for the one shot showing the second pit in the film.<ref name="365Days">''[[Creating the Worlds of Star Wars: 365 Days]]''</ref>

In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character was first identified in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile card game]] ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'',<ref name="Force Collection" /> which initially launched in [[2013]]. Although the launch of ''Force Collection'' predates<ref name="Force Collection release">{{SW|url=news/announcing-star-wars-force-collection|text=Announcing Star Wars: Force Collection|nolive=1}}</ref> the ''Star Wars'' canon reset of [[2014]],<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]] confirmed that the game was kept updated to fit with canon.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|holocronkeeper|status/846807700482666496|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=I'm not gonna go through the trouble of changing them and I don't have any reason to believe currently that anyone would. [&hellip;] On the off-chance that we DID have a current need to change any of these, we would have for Force Collection.|archivedate=20210505080831}} &mdash; [[Leland Chee]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] has confirmed that [[in-universe]] information from ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' conforms with ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]].</ref> The name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where the surname Oicunn was first used for the [[Barrow Oicunn/Legends|character]] in ''[[Dressing a Galaxy: The Costumes of Star Wars]]'', a reference book written by [[Trisha Biggar]]<ref name="Dressing">''[[Dressing a Galaxy: The Costumes of Star Wars]]''</ref> and published on [[October 11]], 2005.<ref name="DressingDate">{{WebCite|url=https://www.waterstones.com/book/dressing-a-galaxy-part-1/trisha-biggar/george-lucas/9780810959644|text=Dressing a Galaxy: Part 1: The Costumes of Star Wars (Hardback)|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/mxQvt|archivedate=2022-04-24}}</ref> The first name Barrow was then provided by [[James Floyd]] to [[Daniele F.]] for use in an entry to the [[StarWars.com]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] feature, "[[What's The Story?]]"<ref name="blog">{{Blog|masterdani|61|"What's the story?": My chosen entry}}</ref> which was published on [[November 18]], 2005.<ref name="WTSDate">{{SWArchive|url=databank/updates/news20051118.html|text=What's the Story, Round 3}}</ref>
