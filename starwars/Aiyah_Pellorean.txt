[[Doctor|Dr.]] '''Aiyah Pellorean''' was a [[research director]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] who oversaw the production of [[Test Unit 08-B]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Biography==
During the [[Clone Wars]], a [[galaxy|galactic]] conflict between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> that took place from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' states the [[Clone Wars]] began in [[22 BBY]] and ended in [[19 BBY]].</ref> [[Doctor|Dr.]] Aiyah Pellorean served as a [[research director]] for the Confederacy during which they oversaw the production of [[Test Unit 08-B]], set of experimental [[clothing]] which included a [[Disruptor belt|belt]], [[Disruptor jacket|jacket]], and [[Disruptor pants|pants]]. The outfit produced an amplification field of 2.41 [[meter]]s that increased the effectiveness of [[ion blaster|ion weapons]]. However the outfit also caused nearby [[battle droid]]s to become violently disorientated. Since battle droids were such an important part of the Confederacy's war effort, Pellorean was ordered to halt their work until peace was achieved. Agreeing with the assessment, Pellorean had the outfit stored in a [[Unidentified Separatist bunker|bunker]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. The outfit remained there through the end of the war and into the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|successor]], the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] recovered the sixteen Clone Wars&ndash;era recordings and her companion [[ND-5]], a [[BX-series droid commando]], was decrypt these transmissions which allowed him to locate the bunker where Test Unit 08-B had been store.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Outlaws Date" /> Vess traveled to the bunker where she recovered outfit and read Pellorean's final research log on the project.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}
