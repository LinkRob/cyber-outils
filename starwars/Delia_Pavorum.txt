'''Delia Pavorum''' was a flowering plant found on numerous worlds, including [[Endor/Legends|Endor]]. Though normally yellow, the flowers were extremely sensitive and certain wavelengths of light could cause them to turn blue. Artificial light producers, such as an incorrectly calibrated [[Solar Cycle Optimizer]], could cause the color change.
