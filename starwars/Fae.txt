The '''fae''' were an ancient [[species]]. The myth "[[The Silent Circle]]" tells the story of how the [[Tree (The Silent Circle)|trees]], the fae and the [[Beast (The Silent Circle)|beasts]] once lived in harmony on [[Batuu]].<ref name="Silent Circle" />

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Small and slight, this species grew wings to become a race of fae, flitting among the treetops, chittering about all they had seen out in the wider world.|[[The Silent Circle]]|<ref name="Silent Circle" />}}
The fae had four wings and [[Color|green]] skin.<ref name="Silent Circle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The fae first appeared in the short story [[The Silent Circle (short story)|The Silent Circle]],<ref name="Silent Circle" /> published on [[October 7]], [[2020]]<ref name="Shop Disney">{{ShopDisney|url=star-wars-galaxys-edge-myths-fables-book-477412202085.html|text=Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge Myths & Fables Book|date=2020-10-07}}</ref> in the ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge]]-exclusive edition of the book ''[[Myths & Fables]], written by [[George Mann]]. The art of the story, that included a picture of the fae, was illustrated by [[Grant Griffin]].<ref name="Silent Circle" />
