A '''diagnostic computer''' was a type of [[computer]] that could be found on [[starship]]s to detect the power levels of systems. One was present on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', but was disabled<ref name="Star Wars 17" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars (2020) 16|''Star Wars'' (2020) 16]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> by a [[seismic charge]] fired by [[Boba Fett]]'s [[Slave I|ship]].
