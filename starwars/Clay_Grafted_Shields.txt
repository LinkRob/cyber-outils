The '''Clay Grafted Shields''' were a pair of [[Shield (physical armor)|shields]] owned by the [[KX-series security droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[K-A0S]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her standard [[Grafted Twin Shields]]. This shield resembled elements of the large [[stone guardian]] [[droid]]s utilized by the [[Frangawl Cult]] on the [[moon]] [[Zardossa Stix]].<ref name="The Disappeared">{{TCW|The Disappeared, Part II}}</ref><ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Clay Grafted Shields was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter K-A0S' Grafted Twin Shields, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 2.0.0 alongside Season 2 on [[July 30]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Clay Security Warrior]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing the Super Security Bundle within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
