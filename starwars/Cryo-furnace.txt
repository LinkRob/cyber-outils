A '''cryo-furnace''' was a tool used in the creation of [[Mandalorian armor]]. In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|1}}</ref> [[the Armorer]] used a cryo-furnace to craft [[Mandalorian]] [[armor]] in the traditional way the ancestors of the Mandalorians did.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|amorer-s-equipment|Armorer's equipment}}</ref>
