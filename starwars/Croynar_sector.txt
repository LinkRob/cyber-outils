The '''Croynar sector''' was located far from the [[Core Worlds]] of [[the galaxy]] which included the [[Midgor system]]. In [[5 ABY]], The [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s loyalist forces [[Attack on the Diamond Tor|attacked]] an Imperial [[convoy]] that served the [[Yomo Council]]&mdash;an Imperial group that had broken off from the Empire&mdash;in Midgor during its [[Operation: Cinder|campaign]] of rogue Imperial factions.

Around the same time, [[Barma Battle Group|forces]] of the New Republic received intelligence from the [[Information broker|information merchant]] known as the [[Oracle (Nath Tensent)|Oracle]] that the 204th was in the Croynar sector. With three systems in the [[sector]] considered to have some strategic value, including Midgor, a New Republic [[Deliverance's starfighter wing|starfighter wing]] was deployed to the system, where its [[squadron]]s discovered the ravaged convoy that the 204th had attacked. Calling in the New Republic refitted [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'', the [[fighter wing]] assisted the survivors of the convoy.

==Description==
{{Quote|We've cross-referenced all known worlds under Imperial control with the ''Yadeez''{{'s}} last known heading. They've been weaving through this sector for a while&mdash;we figured their target had to be close. Best guess is Chadawa.|Hera Syndulla, to Alphabet Squadron|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
The Croynar sector was located in an intersecting portion of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing Sectors" /> It contained the [[Ciaox Verith system|Ciaox Verith]], [[Fedovoi End system|Fedovoi End]], [[Ghonoath system|Ghonoath]], [[Kortatka system|Kortatka]], [[Midgor system|Midgor]], and [[Red Yars system]]s at the coordinates M-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], and the [[Chadawa system|Chadawa]] and [[G'Tep'Noi system]]s at the neighbouring coordinates N-14.<ref name="Systems" /> Another [[Croynar sector alphanumeric designated star system|alphanumeric designated star system]] was also located in the sector. The Chadawa, Fedovoi End, and Kortatka systems contained the [[planet]]s [[Chadawa]], [[Fedovoi End]], and [[Kortatka]] respectively.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==History==
===Imperial cannibalism===
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="4 ABY">{{AlphabetSquadronDate}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]], in the midst of a [[Galactic Civil War]] against the emergent [[New Republic]], began splintering following the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> By [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> the forces in control of Chadawa, Fedovoi End, and Kortatka broke away from the Empire's loyalist forces. The [[Yomo Council]] entrenched at Fedovoi End, ignoring orders by Imperial loyalist [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] to send assets to the [[D'Aelgoth sector]] and forming an alliance with the [[Shiortuun Syndicate]]. Meanwhile, [[Governor]] [[Bordanivaux]] oversaw the [[Chadawan navy|forces]] at Chadawa, which were joined by the depleted [[Madrighast's unit|forces]] of [[Colonel]] [[Madrighast]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> that [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="Mad5">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[5 ABY]], establishes that [[Madrighast's unit]] had joined the [[Chadawan navy]] since the events of ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', which ''Timelines'' dates to the same [[Standard year|year]]. This alliance must have therefore formed in 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref>

In a new phase of its genocidal [[Operation: Cinder]], the Imperial loyalists sent the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], led by Colonel [[Soran Keize]], to devastate worlds that had broken from the loyalist faction. The [[fighter wing]] [[Attack on Dybbron III|destroyed]] the planet [[Dybbron III]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in the nearby [[Nythlide Array]]<ref name="Nythlide">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Dybbron system]]</ref> before coming to the Croynar sector and [[Attack on Kortatka|pacifying]] Kortatka. At both instances, they called upon former Imperial subjects to reaffirm loyalties and join them, some recruits being made from this.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

The 204th then turned its eyes the Yomo Council and soon arrived at Fedovoi End. After announcing the world's imminent "retribution," Keize had his forces [[Destruction of Fedovoi End|suffocate]] the planet by bombing open gas pockets in the planet's ice caps, making the [[atmosphere]] toxic. A handful also answered to Keize's call to join his forces and escaped the destruction of Fedovoi End. The 204th also [[Attack on the Diamond Tor|attacked]] a Yomo Council affiliated [[convoy]] led by [[Captain]] [[Oultovar Misk]] in the Midgor system, dismantling the escorts and leaving Misk's [[freighter]], the ''[[Diamond Tor]]'' immobile.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

===Hunters hunted===
Meanwhile, members of [[Alphabet Squadron]], a New Republic working group put together to defeat the 204th, were trying to the fighter wing, joined by a [[Barma Battle Group|battle group]] led by [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]. The [[New Republic pilot|pilots]] [[Captain]] [[Nath Tensent]] and [[Chass na Chadic]] learned from an [[Oracle (Nath Tensent)|information merchant]] that the 204th was in the Croynar sector. Thus, Alphabet Squadron led a [[Deliverance's starfighter wing|starfighter wing]]&mdash;the rest of the wing being formed of [[Flare Squadron]], [[Hail Squadron]], and [[Wild Squadron]]&mdash;to the Midgor system, [[Starfighter Commander]] [[Wyl Lark]] believing it to be one of three [[star system]]s with any strategic value. The starfighter wing found Misk's convoy and came to their aid. From that, they learned about the 204th's path of destruction.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

The 204th continued its journey, Keize setting a course for Chadawa. An [[Palal Seedia|inside source]] in Syndulla's [[flagship]], the repurposed [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'', informed Keize that Syndulla's forces were once again on his tail. The colonel arranged for the 204th's [[sabotage droid]]s to be dropped at the ''Deliverance''{{'s}} next destination. This [[Sabotage of the Deliverance|trap]] successfully stalled the ''Deliverance'' and led to the [[death]]s of two crewmembers. After stopping at an unmapped [[jungle]] location, the 204th kept moving and were attacked by a force of three [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruisers]], which they were able to survive.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

===Cat and mouse===
However, the 204th had their own intelligence issue, as [[Lieutenant]] [[Yrica Quell]]&mdash;Alphabet Squadron's former leader who had rejoined the fighter wing to sabotage them&mdash;had secretly began producing communication package bursts from a refurbished [[transmitter]] within the fighter wing's flagship, the ''[[Yadeez]]''. After [[New Republic Intelligence]] picked these up and identified the point of origin, the ''Deliverance'' followed the trail and encountered the 204th in the Ciaox Verith system. While the Lark and Flare Squadron were able to [[Skirmish in the Ciaox Verith system|skirmish]] with the fighter wing, the Imperials quickly fled the system before the rest of Syndulla's forces could engage.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

As the ''Deliverance'' did a system-by-system search, another comm burst nearly a [[Standard day|day]] later led to them finding the 204th in the Red Yars system. Both side were prepared for the [[Battle in the Red Yars system|encounter]], and the Imperials escaped again before their pursuers could fire any in range shots. Continuing a system-by system search, the ''Deliverance'' found the 204th again just [[Standard hour|hours]] later and latched onto the ''Yadeez'' with a [[tractor beam]]. While the ''Deliverance''{{'s}} starfighter wing attempted to [[Third encounter with the Yadeez|defend]] the [[tractor beam projector]], [[Shymon|one]] 204th fighter made a suicide run into the projector and destroyed it, allowing the Imperials to escape once more.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

Days later, the ''Deliverance'' [[Fourth encounter with the Yadeez|encountered]] the 204th in an unmapped alphanumeric designated system. The New Republic forces delayed the fighter wing's escape by forcing the ''Yadeez'' towards one of the system's [[planetoid]]s, but the New Republic fighters were only able to engage for about six [[Standard minute|minutes]] before their targets escaped again. With more sweeps aboard the ''Yadeez'' for what was giving away its position, Quell sent one more comm burst before destroying the transmitter. From this, the ''Deliverance'' found the 204th in the Ghonoath system, forcing the latter force to seek cover in the [[atmosphere]] of a [[Unidentified radioactive deathworld|radioactive deathworld]]. After another short [[Battle in the Ghonoath system|battle]] ensued, the 204th was able to escape again.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

It was not long before the ''Deliverance'' pursued the 204th again in the G'Tep'Noi system. The battle once again ended in the Imperials escaping, but not before Quell announced herself to the New Republic and threatened them over comms. As the rest of Alphabet Squadron considered the apparent revelation that their ex-commander was with the enemy, Syndulla and her people were suspected that Chadawa was indeed the 204th's next target.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
