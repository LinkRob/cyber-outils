'''Blue wheat''' was a type of [[wheat]] that grew on the [[planet]] [[Dalna]].<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while the [[Path of the Open Hand]] cultist [[Marda Ro]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kevmo Zink]] were walking together on Dalna, Ro stepped off a path near a [[Water|river]] into knee-high [[Color|blue]] wheat. The wheat's bearded [[seed]]s whispered against Ro's [[robe]]s and clung to Zink's [[Jedi apparel|robes]] when he followed her.<ref name="Path of Deceit" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blue wheat appeared in the [[2022]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and [[Tessa Gratton]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase II">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> Within the novel, the wheat appeared in a chapter written by Gratton.<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref>
