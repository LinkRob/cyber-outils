'''Ackbar's task force''' was a [[Rebel Alliance Navy]] [[task force]] under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]].<ref name="Battles" /> The task force was deployed to [[Mon Cala]] following the [[Mutiny on Mon Cala|mutiny]] of the [[Mon Cala Mercantile Fleet|Mon Cala Imperial Trade Fleet]]. The task force consisted of a [[Unidentified Mon Calamari starship|Mon Calamari starship]], [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s, and [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]s.<ref name="SW 49" />
