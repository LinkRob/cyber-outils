The '''''Ace of Staves''''' was a [[Freighter|cargo ship]] owned by the [[pilot]] [[Koley Linn]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Fallen Star" /> Around [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> Linn and his [[starship]] were trapped aboard the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] when the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s enacted plans to [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destroy]] the station. A disreputable individual, Linn was [[Death|killed]] during attempts to escape, leading the pirate [[Nan Hague|Nan]] to steal the abandoned ''Ace of Staves'' for herself while fleeing the remains of the bottom half of the station.<ref name="Fallen Star" />
