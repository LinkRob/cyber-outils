The '''Centrists''', also known as the '''centrist faction''', were one of the two major [[Politics|political]] factions in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] of the [[New Republic]]. Whereas the [[Populists]] favored [[planet]]ary sovereignty over the authority of the central government, the Centrists advocated for a more powerful executive and [[New Republic Defense Force|military]] and valued the rule of law and order; and supported the return of certain [[New Order|aspects]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], such as the centralization of political power and [[The galaxy|galactic]] unity, while disavowing the horrors of the Empire itself.

The Centrists were formed from a [[Inner systems' Imperial remnant|remnant]] of the Galactic Empire composed of [[Planet|worlds]] in the inner [[Star system|systems]] in the [[Battle of Jakku|immediate]] [[Galactic Concordance|aftermath]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]] following the [[Declaration of a New Republic|establishment]] of the New Republic. Paralyzed by internal strife, they quickly disintegrated, leading several of these former Imperial worlds to be admitted into the New Republic, and remerged from politicians who had not been involved in the Galactic Civil War, which evolved into the Centrists, an unofficial faction in the Galactic Senate.

By [[29 ABY]], elements of the Centrists had ties with the First Order, a [[Fascism|fascist]] [[First Order High Command|military junta]] in the [[Unknown Regions]] of the [[galaxy]] and some even aided them as they tried to manipulate the Galactic Senate. The political gridlock that ensued culminated in some Centrist worlds breaking away from the New Republic and [[First Order secession|joining]] with the First Order.

==Policies==
{{Quote|As for political philosophy, all we Centrists want is to take a fair look at what aspects of the Empire actually worked. Centralizing power, creating maximum efficiency, binding the worlds of the galaxy closer together. Can you honestly say it did no good whatsoever?''"<br />"''Whatever good came of the Empire came at too high a price.''"<br />"''I agree completely. But what if we could achieve some of those same benefits without repeating Palpatine's mistakes?|Ransolm Casterfo and Leia Organa|<ref name="Bloodline" />}}
[[File:Starhawk_Mk_II_over_Hosnian_Prime.png|thumb|right|250px|The Centrist adopted a platform emphasizing the aspects of the Old Empire that they believed worked for the greater good of the galaxy, such as strengthening the New Republic Defense Force.]] 
Centrists believed that a strong [[Politics|government]] and military were essential to maintaining peace and stability throughout [[the galaxy]], while their philosophy was largely influenced by the aspects of the [[Galactic Empire]] that actually worked but disavowed its horrors. As such, Centrists advocated in favor of strengthening the [[New Republic Defense Force]] and adopting aspects of the Empire that they believed worked in spite of its horrors. A more extreme wing of the Centrist faction lobbied for the militarization of the New Republic to counteract future security threats. [[Senator]] [[Erudo Ro-Kiintor]] supported an extensive armaments bill to address individual [[planet]]ary defenses, claiming that local security had been neglected by the [[New Republic Senate]]. However, fellow Centrist Senator [[Ransolm Casterfo]] suspected Ro-Kiintor of seeking to earmark government funds toward Centrist worlds.<ref name="Bloodline" />

Overall, the Centrists expressed more authoritarian values, due to their strong beliefs in centralization of executive authority. The Centrists publicly professed that they supported stronger law enforcement, structural hierarchy, and greater centrality of executive authority. Although not a large wing, some of the members privately expressed pro-Imperial sentiments and nostalgia for the "Old Empire," although not all of them necessarily supported its revival. Many of those people simply admired its more authoritarian structure but not the Empire itself or its horrors, and remained faithful to the ideals of the New Republic and [[democracy]].<ref name="Bloodline" /> Those Centrists who longed for a return of the Empire itself were united as supporters of the emerging [[First Order]]<ref name="Propaganda" /> and actively undermined the New Republic in their capacity as its elected representatives.<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> Those First Order-aligned Centrists worked to prepare their party and supporters for life under the First Order.<ref name="Bloodline" />

[[File:CrumblingImperialCrest-RaFotGE.png|thumb|left|250px|While most were faithful to the New Republic, some Centrists privately expressed adulation for the legacy of the Old Empire.]]
Some Centrist senators like [[Ormes Apolin]] and [[Fatil]] admired old [[Imperial military uniforms|Imperial uniforms]] and [[armor]]. Some Centrists believed that planets should be responsible for their own problems and natural disasters and disliked the perceived rampant complaining in the New Republic. Others regarded the New Republic's [[New Republic Military Academy|academies]] as inferior to those on Centrist worlds, which had begun to rebuild the old Imperial programs. Some even questioned the official New Republic [[Timeline of galactic history|historical]] accounts of the [[Battle of Yavin]] and the [[Galactic Civil War]]. At least thirty Centrist senators were known to be avid collectors of old Imperial artifacts and equipment.<ref name="Bloodline" />

While the Centrists supported free trade, they were not as economically liberal as the [[Populists]]. Centrists were more open to central planning, protectionism, interplanetary taxation, and increasing spending to create more centralized programs. Additionally, trade was far more regulated on the remote Centrist world of [[Daxam IV]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Bloodline" />

==History==
===Origins===
{{Quote|The Centrists&mdash;consisting of several former Imperial worlds&mdash;wished to create a new position of authority to steer the government.|Janyor|<ref name="Propaganda" />}}
[[File:AmeddaJediEradicationCeremony-2017DarthVader1.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Centrists originated from several worlds in the New Republic that had once supported the Old Empire.]]
The Centrists were formed during the [[New Republic Era]] by former members of the [[inner systems' Imperial remnant]] of the [[Galaxy|Galactic]] Empire, having unsuccessfully attempted achieve a consensus following the [[Battle of Jakku|conclusion]] of the Galactic Civil War.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> Although [[Jakku Imperial remnant|some forces]] continued to fight on [[Jakku]] while [[Unknown Regions Imperial remnant|other Imperials]] fled to the [[Unknown Regions]], the collapsing Galactic Empire formally capitulated when [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] signed the [[Imperial Instruments of Surrender]], ending the civil war<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

The former [[Imperial Navy]]'s remaining [[Starship|vessels]] were confined within the redrawn boundaries of [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> The inner [[Star system|systems']] Imperial remnant temporarily existed in the [[Core Worlds]], the [[Colonies]], and the [[Inner Rim Territories]] on worlds that held considerable wealth and prestige, its leaders failed reach an agreement on its future,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> which was further hampered by war reparations and disarmament treaties with the New Republic.<ref name="First Order DB">{{Databank|the-first-order|The First Order}}</ref> Unable to achieve cohesion of any sort, the remnant soon disintegrated and its worlds were admitted into the New Republic. In [[time]], its representatives in the Senate formed the Centrist faction,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> led by politicians who had not been involved in the Galactic Civil War.<ref name="Rise" /> In secret, many former Imperials&mdash;united as supporters of the emerging [[First Order]]&mdash;who longed for a return of the Empire quietly bid their time as Centrists<ref name="Propaganda" /> and used their government station to undermine the New Republic in preparation for that return.<ref name="Rise" />

===Senate partisanship===
{{Quote|The conflict between the parties gets worse every day. Most Centrists and Populists are still polite to each other, but barely. Every debate on the Senate floor turns into an endless argument over 'tone' or 'form' and never about issues of substance&mdash;|Leia Organa|<ref name="Bloodline" />}}
[[File:Exantor_Divo_graduation.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Centrists called for a more centralized New Republic without repeating mistakes of the past, coming into disagreement with their Populist counterparts.]]
The Centrists adopted a platform emphasizing the aspects of the former Galactic Empire that they believed worked for the greater good of the galaxy<ref name="Bloodline" /> such as a strong central government and military,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> generating maximum efficiency, and supporting galactic unity. Senator Ransolm Casterfo openly celebrated the Imperial heritage by decorating his office on [[Hosnian Prime]] with [[Imperial Military]] relics and [[Imperial crest|imagery]] despite his condemnation of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Bloodline" />

By [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the Centrists had become a leading political faction in the New Republic's Galactic Senate. Centrist senators typically dressed in rich, extravagant [[clothing]] which contrasted with the simpler, more practical attire worn by their Populist opponents in an effort to show off the wealth of their [[homeworld]]s. As a party that valued law and order, Centrists regarded the Senate as weak and indecisive. Their views and goals were diametrically opposed to that of the Populists, gridlocking the Senate as neither party was inclined to work with the opposition. The partisan environment of the Senate disillusioned [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]; as a senator, Organa sided with the Populist wing of the government and rebuked Ransolm Casterfo's Imperial-influenced Centrist views.<ref name="Bloodline" />

[[File:First_Senator_Leia_poster.png|thumb|right|200px|The Centrists proposed replacing the chancellorship with the office of First Senator.]]
Following Organa and Casterfo's [[Mission to Bastatha|mission]] to [[Bastatha]], the Centrists accused Organa of withholding information from the Senate. During the same session, one of the Centrist senators, [[Lady]] [[Carise Sindian]], announced a motion calling on the Senate to abolish the [[Chancellor (New Republic)|chancellorship]] in favor of the office of [[First Senator]], a new post with greater executive authority over the New Republic's economic and military resources. While the Senate was not swayed by Sindian's arguments, Casterfo managed to win over many independent senators by pointing to the deadlock in the New Republic. As a result, Casterfo's motion was adopted by a slim majority, forcing the Populists to reconsider their strategy.<ref name="Bloodline" />

When Organa accepted the Populist nomination for First Senator, Sindian manipulated Casterfo into exposing her true [[parent]]age as the daughter of the universally reviled [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The public revelation of Organa's [[Skywalker family|secret lineage]] destroyed her political career and weakened the Populists. Sindian, Erudo Ro-Kiintor, and several Centrists accused Organa of being a warmonger due to her position on groups that she saw as threats to the New Republic, such as the [[Amaxine warriors (New Republic Era)|Amaxine warriors]] and [[Rinnrivin Di's cartel]]. In secret, Sindian worked as an [[agent]] for the First Order, which was secretly funding the Amaxines and [[Rinnrivin Di]] in order to invest revenue into rebuilding the old Imperial [[Navy]].<ref name="Bloodline" />

After Casterfo defended Organa's report, Sindian hired the former [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] [[Arliz Hadrassian]] to [[Tai-Lin Garr's assassination|assassinate]] Senator [[Tai-Lin Garr]], whom the Populists nominated for the post of First Senator in the wake of Organa's scandal. She then fabricated evidence framing Casterfo for the deed. Following his arrest, the Centrists officially disowned Casterfo. They also agreed to suspend the upcoming elections for the First Senator in the name of maintaining peace and stability. In secret, Sindian planned to present a motion calling for Centrist worlds to secede from the New Republic and join the First Order.<ref name="Bloodline" />

===Return to legitimate rule===
{{Quote|The New Republic, having been profoundly weaken by the work of Centrist senators who really gave their allegiance to the First Order, was fatally unprepared for what was about to happen.|Beaumont Kin, The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire|<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
The public backlash toward Organa's [[DNA|biological]] relation to [[Darth]] Vader resulted in the [[First Order secession|secession]] of worlds aligned with the Centrist party.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> Disillusioned with the government's lack of cohesion and the collapse of essential services that followed, Centrist worlds grew to doubt the value of their membership in the New Republic, which in turn allowed for a new Separatist movement to take root on the Centrists' platform. Unlike the [[Galactic Republic]] that opposed the [[Separatist Crisis]], the New Republic opted not to prevent the [[star]] systems that advocated a centralized system of government from establishing a separate coalition.<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>

[[File:Legitimate_Power.jpg|thumb|200px|The Centrists seceded from the New Republic and formed the political branch of the First Order.|left]]
While a minority in the Senate opposed the splintering of the New Republic, believing that the Centrists would adopt the [[Dictatorship|authoritarian]] policies that defined the Old Empire, their departure was welcomed by a majority on the galactic [[capital]].<ref name="Propaganda" /> The Centrists therefore gained the power to reform their half of the New Republic according to the standards of Centrist philosophy.<ref name="Skywalker: A Family at War">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]''</ref> They successfully consolidated their territory under a new political structure separate from the Republic proper and ultimately became known as the First Order<ref name="TSWB" /> in [[29 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Covering up its secret, prior existence, with its public formation,<ref name="TROS VD" /> the First Order marketed itself as a legitimate power and heir to the legacy of the Galactic Empire in contrast to the New Republic, which the Order denounced as an illegitimate regime<ref name="Propaganda" /> given to corruption and chaos.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 5">[[Galaxy's Edge 5|''Galaxy's Edge'' 5]]</ref> [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], the son of Leia Organa and apprentice of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]], espoused his belief in the First Order's purpose of restoring stability to galactic civilization.<ref name="TFA novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>

The successful secession of the Centrist systems marked the public formation of the First Order. Unknown to its members, the First Order was compartmentalized into separate branches originating from various aspects of the New Order. The Centrists formed the political bloc of the First Order, serving as its public façade whereas the [[First Order military]] arose from remnants of the Imperial Military that had retreated to the Unknown Regions.<ref name="TROS VD" /> In addition to the political and military components of the First Order, the [[Sith Eternal]] was its [[Religion|spiritual]] branch.<ref name="TSWB" />

[[File:Hux_and_Starkiller_Base.png|thumb|250px|Embracing the tenets of Neo-Imperialism, the First Order set out to conquer the galaxy through military might.]]
The First Order, driven by a political ideology known as [[Neo-Imperialism]],<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> heralded the return of [[fascism]] and militarism in the galaxy.<ref name="TROS VD" /> Following the time of the [[Cold War]], the First Order embarked on a campaign of galactic conquest, [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroying]] Hosnian Prime and overthrowing the New Republic<ref name="TSWB" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GA" /> Its forces subsequently conquered much of the known galaxy in spite of continued opposition from the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> In the last [[Standard day|days]] of the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the First Order and the [[Sith]] Eternal cemented their alliance under the guidance of the [[Transference|resurrected]] Darth Sidious. Despite this, however, the [[Sith Lord]] and his [[Sith cultist]]s were destroyed during the [[Battle of Exegol]]<ref name="TSWB" /> in [[35 ABY]],<ref name="35 ABY note">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' as taking place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must take place in [[35 ABY]].</ref> resulting in the [[Uprising against the First Order|fall of the First Order]] throughout the galaxy.<ref name="TSWB" />

==Centrist worlds==
{{Quote|Now, on Centrist worlds, we're rebuilding the old programs of study. Reestablishing standards. About time, too.|A Centrist senator|<ref name="Bloodline" />}}
During the New Republic Era, several former Imperial worlds comprised the Centrist party's base.<ref name="Propaganda" /> By 28 ABY,<ref name="Date" /> Centrist senators Carise Sindian, [[Arbo]] and [[Orris Madmund]], [[Mortan]], Erudo Ro-Kiintor, Ormes Apolin, Fatil, and Ransolm Casterfo represented [[Arkanis]], Coruscant, [[Comra]], [[Hevurion]], [[Kuat]], [[Orinda]], and [[Riosa]] respectively. Additionally, the Centrist bloc extended to the Outer Rim world of Daxam IV.<ref name="Bloodline" /> When the First Order emerged to claim control of numerous star systems once represented among the Centrists, Arkanis, Orinda and Kuat were among the planets that elected to join it.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

[[File:CoruscantGlobeE1.png|thumb|200px|Coruscant, the former capital of the galaxy, was represented by Centrist senators during the New Republic Era.|left]]
The Centrist planet<ref name="Bloodline" /> Coruscant had the distinction of being the traditional center of galactic politics<ref name="Hosnian Prime DB">{{Databank|hosnian-prime|Hosnian Prime}}</ref> and culture due to its history as the capital of the Galactic Republic and the Galactic Empire that followed.<ref name="Coruscant DB">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref> Additionally, Coruscant was generally accepted as the ancestral homeworld of the [[human]] [[species]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> The status of galactic capital was taken from Coruscant by the New Republic, ending [[Millennium|millennia]] of tradition<ref name="Hosnian Prime DB" /> in order to placate the malcontent systems that attempted to secede from the Galactic Republic in the time leading up to the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The once vibrant [[ecumenopolis]] became riven with chaos and [[Gang war on Coruscant|gang violence]].<ref name="GEG">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref> After occupying the Centrist wing of New Republic politics, Coruscant came under the control of the First Order, but later [[Uprising on Coruscant|rebelled]] against the Order's authority.<ref name="TROS JN">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In real-world political terms, "[[Wikipedia:Centrism|centrist]]" generally means someone who is [[Wikipedia:Moderate|politically moderate]].<ref name="Definition">{{WebCite|url=https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/centrist|text=Definition of Centrist by Merriam-Webster|archivedate=20190620152750}}</ref> The Star Wars use of the term is closer to the concept of "centralism," which refers to the concentration of power and control in the central authority of an organization.<ref name="Centralism">{{WebCite|url=https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/centralism|text=Definition of Centralism by Merriam-Webster|archivedate=20190620152750}}</ref>
