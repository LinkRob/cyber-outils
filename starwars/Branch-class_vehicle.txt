The '''''Branch''-class vehicle''', also known as the '''Czerka Corporation cruiser''', was a class of [[cruiser]] used by the [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]]. The class was long and bulky with a sizable cargo bay, and had a crew of up to 10,000. One such [[Starship|ship]] was the ''[[Leverage]]'', [[Commander|commanded]] by [[Meritt Col]], which was deployed to [[Pijal]] a few years before the [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="Master & Apprentice" />
