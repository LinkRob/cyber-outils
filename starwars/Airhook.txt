An '''airhook''' was a civilian vehicle which provided the design basis for the [[Single Trooper Aerial Platform]] manufactured by [[Baktoid Armor Workshop]]. [[Trade Federation]] [[engineer]]s drew inspiration from the airhook design, and re-engineered the vehicles for greater performance and reliability, as well as to specialize them for [[B1-series battle droid]] [[pilot]]s.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>
