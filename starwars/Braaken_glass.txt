'''Braaken glass''' was a type of [[glass]] that could be sold in shops.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 33" /> Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Bounty Hunters 33|''Bounty Hunters'' 33]] take place before the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Droids]]'' crossover event that {{SW|url=news/marvel-dark-droids-interview|text=Charles Soule Unleashes ''Dark Droids''}} in turn places after ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]''. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''War of the Bounty Hunters'' to [[3 ABY]]. In addition, the flashbacks of [[Star Wars (2020) 50|''Star Wars'' (2020) 50]], which occur after the events of [[Star Wars (2020) 43|''Star Wars'' (2020) 43]] and thereby also after those of ''Dark Droids'', take place approximately twenty-two [[Standard year|years]] after [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|becoming]] [[Galactic Emperor]], which ''Timelines'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore ''Bounty Hunters'' 33 must be set around 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] stated that her friend and fellow [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] was charged in like a [[rathtar]] in a braaken glass shop when she spoke with her fellow [[T'onga's crew|crewmate]] [[4-LOM]] about the volatile nature of their crew.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 33" />

==Behind the scenes==
Braaken glass was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Bounty Hunters 33|thirty-third issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 33" /> on [[April 12]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103053/star_wars_bounty_hunters_2020_33|text=Star Wars: Bounty Hunters (2020) #33}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Braaken glass/Legends|braaken glass]] was mentioned in ''[[Shield of Lies]]'', the second installment in ''[[The Black Fleet Crisis]]'' trilogy, which was written by [[Michael P. Kube-McDowell]] and published in [[1996]].<ref name="Shield of Lies">''[[Shield of Lies]]''</ref>
