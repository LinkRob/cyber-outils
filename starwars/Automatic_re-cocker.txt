An '''automatic re-cocker''' was a device that was used by members of the [[Wookiee]] [[species]] to make it faster and easier to fire a [[bowcaster]]. The devices were not available for sale, but the price of the re-cocker's components was 500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
The automatic re-cocker was mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
