'''Buzz''' was a [[clone trooper]]<ref name="LOT" /> [[squad leader]]<ref name="HC 60" /> who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. During the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], he went with [[Jedi Master]] [[Luminara Unduli]] to search for and capture [[Poggle the Lesser]] to take him into [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] custody. They were able to track him down to a [[Progate Temple|temple]] on their [[speeder]]s, but unbeknownst to them, the temple was crawling with [[brain worm]]s. While scouting the temple, Buzz was [[Death|killed]] by an undead [[Geonosian]] infected with a brain [[worm]].<ref name="LOT" /> His nickname came from the "buzzing" sound of a running Z-6.<ref name="HC 60" />

==Equipment==
Buzz had [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] that was customized with the [[Color|green]] markings of the [[41st Elite Corps]] in addition to other personalized details on his [[helmet]]. He was equipped with a [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon]].<ref name="LOT" />
