'''Buccel Trune''' was a [[human]]<ref name="The Rebellion 1" /> [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Free Fall" /> who fought alongside the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="The Rebellion 1" /> and later became the [[Parent|father]] of the [[New Republic Security Bureau]] [[officer]] [[Sela Trune]].<ref name="Free Fall" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Rebellion Date">{{SW|url=news/the-illustrious-jabba-the-hutt-bids-you-welcome-to-his-new-marvel-comic-jabbas-palace-exclusive-reveal|text=The Illustrious Jabba the Hutt Bids You Welcome to His New Marvel Comic, ''Jabba's Palace'' - Exclusive Reveal}} establishes that [[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (2023)|''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'']] takes place in the era of {{Film|VI}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> Trune alerted the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] about an [[assassin]]ation [[Attempt to assassinate Mon Mothma|attempt]] on [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="The Rebellion 1" /> After the [[Battle of Endor]], Trune witnessed<ref name="BJIR2">[[Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising 2|''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 2]]</ref> and informed [[Agent]] [[Calder Behrens]] that the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] have [[Adelhard coalition|allied]] with [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]].<ref name="BJIR3" /> At some point, Trune later lived at a [[Trune family farm|farm]] on the [[planet]] [[Yungbrii]]. He was often stoic and distant, but Trune felt that he had moments of warmth. Less than a [[Standard month|month]] after Trune joined the [[New Republic]], her [[Parent|father]], along with her [[Sela Trune's mother|mother]] and brother [[Gaithel]], were [[Death|killed]] in the crossfire of a [[Skirmish on Yungbrii|skirmish]] between [[New Republic officer]]s and members of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]].<ref name="Free Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
Buccel Trune was first mentioned, albeit unidentified, in the [[2020]] young adult novel ''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]'', which was written by [[Alex Segura]].<ref name="Free Fall" /> The character later made their first appearance in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] one-shot [[Return of the Jedi – The Rebellion 1|''Return of the Jedi – The Rebellion'' 1]],<ref name="The Rebellion 1" /> also written by Segura and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[July 19]], [[2023]].<ref name="Rebellion Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103246/star_wars_return_of_the_jedi_-_the_rebellion_1_2023_1|text=Star Wars: Return Of The Jedi &ndash; The Rebellion (2023) #1}}</ref> While the character was initially unconfirmed to be the same, Segura clarified that Buccel was intended to be Sela's father.<ref name="Alex">{{Bluesky|alexsegura.bsky.social|post/3lvbbgxp3w22j|[[Alex Segura]]|date=2025-07-31|quote=Yup!|archivedate=20251013050453|archivefile=File:AlexSegura-BuccelTrune-Bluesky.png}} {{C|In response to: "Hey, @alexsegura.bsky.social! In the Rebellion one-shot, we learned that Buccel Trune had a planned retirement on Yungbrii. In Free Fall, we know that Sela Trune's father also happened to retire on Yungbrii. Based on those info, if you're allowed, is it safe to say that Buccel is Sela's father?"}}</ref>
