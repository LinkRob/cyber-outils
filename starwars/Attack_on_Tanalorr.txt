{{Quote|We were invaded. I held them at bay as long as I could, but&mdash; there were too many. The battle was horrific. Still, we weren't beaten. Not yet. Not until the Jedi Council gave the order to abandon Tanalorr and then refused to counterattack.|Dagan Gera|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
The '''attack on Tanalorr''', also known as the '''invasion of Tanalorr''', was a surprise attack by the [[Nihil]] marauders on the [[planet]] [[Tanalorr]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. The attack occurred while a verification was being carried out to decide if Tanalorr would be suitable to house a strong Jedi presence, consisting of the training of [[Jedi Initiate|initiates]] on the world. Despite a spirited defense by the Jedi&mdash;led by renowned [[Jedi Knight]] [[Dagan Gera]] and [[Jedi Council]] member [[Oppo Rancisis]]&mdash;the Nihil overwhelmed the Jedi's defenses.<ref name="Survivor" />

The battle was a victory for the Nihil over the Jedi, as the force with which the marauders attacked and the difficulty of reaching the planet, extending to the impossibility of communication through the [[nebula]] that concealed the world, led the Jedi Council to decide to abandon Tanalorr altogether, which deeply enraged Gera and led to his betrayal of the Order. How the Nihil discovered Tanalorr, however, remained a mystery to the Jedi. Under the belief that the Nihil must have obtained one of the [[Abyss compass|Abyss compasses]]&mdash;the only devices capable of navigating the deadly [[Koboh Abyss]] to reach Tanalorr&mdash;the Jedi Council ordered the destruction of all compasses following the abandonment of Tanalorr.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Like you care about them. What next? Tears for Valo? For Tanalorr?|[[Marchion Ro]], to [[Lourna Dee]]|audio=Tears-For-Valo.mp3|<ref name="Tempest Breaker" />}}
The Nihil attack, besides being a blow to the morale of the Jedi Order, was useful for the marauders in hindering the Jedi advance in the [[Nihil conflict|combat]] against them. The Jedi intended to use Tanalorr as a bastion to repel the threat of the enemies of peace who threatened the [[Galactic Frontier|galactic frontier]] and the Republic. Gera was angered by the Jedi abandoning Tanalorr, culminating in his fall to the [[dark side of the Force]]. Gera's determination not to give up on Tanalorr, his discovery, led him to defy the Jedi Order, resulting in the loss of his right arm to the blade of [[Santari Khri|Santari Khri's]] [[Santari Khri's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. He was placed in a [[bacta]] [[Bacta tank|tank]] for treatment on [[Koboh]], where he remained unconscious until the [[Imperial Era]] due to another action by the Nihil, the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], that led to an [[Emergences|emergence]] that threatened the planet after its [[Shattered Moon|moon]] was shattered, prompting the Republic's evacuation. Upon waking up, he vowed to return to Tanalorr.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on Tanalorr was created for the [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'', where it appears in flashbacks.
