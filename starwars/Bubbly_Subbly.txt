The '''''Bubbly Subbly''''' was an underwater submersible that was owned by the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]], a family of [[scavenger]]s who lived during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The ''Bubbly Subbly'' was built by [[Zander Freemaker]], who was a skilled technician and [[starship]] builder. The Freemakers used the submarine to [[Race to Ningoth/LEGO|recover]] a [[Kyber Saber]] [[Kyber crystal|crystal]] from the depths of the ocean world of [[Ningoth/LEGO|Ningoth]]. The Freemakers obtained the kyber crystal from an [[Opee sea killer]] by exchanging their Snubbly's light for the crystal. However, the kyber crystal was then stolen by the [[Sith]] [[Naare]].<ref name="Crystal Chase" />
