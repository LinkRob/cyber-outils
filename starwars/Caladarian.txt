The '''Caladarians''' were a group represented in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="QP">''[[Queen's Peril]]''</ref> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]]&mdash;while publicly operating as [[Senator]] Palpatine of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]]&mdash;oversaw the proposal of a [[Unidentified taxation bill|taxation bill]] in the Senate, intending for it to fail in order to learn from the Caladarians' reaction. Sidious later reflected on his plan's success&mdash;including how he had learned whom the Caladarians were willing to sacrifice in the process of stopping the initiative&mdash;while speaking to the [[Trade Federation]] [[Viceroy of the Trade Federation|Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]].<ref name="QP" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Caladarians were mentioned in ''[[Queen's Peril]]'', a [[2020]] young-adult novel by [[E. K. Johnston]] as the second installment of ''[[Star Wars: Queen's Series]]''.<ref name="QP" />
