'''Eriadu City''' was the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Eriadu]], a [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> The eruption of the [[Battle of Eriadu]] during the [[Nihil conflict]] brought widespread destruction to Eriadu City, with many buildings reduced to flaming husks of themselves,<ref name="Insider231">{{InsiderCite|231|Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Out with a Bang}}</ref> but it was rebuilt.<ref name="Tarkin" />

The luxurious [[Tarkin family compound|compound]] of the [[Tarkin family]] was located there,<ref name="Tarkin" /> and so was the [[Imperial Academy of Eriadu|local branch]] of the [[Imperial Academy]]. The [[District five|fifth district]] of the city was devoted to the [[Clothing|sartorial]] trade.<ref name="TK-462" />
