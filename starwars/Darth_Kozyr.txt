'''Darth Kozyr''' was an individual<ref name="Revenge">''[[Jedi Academy: Revenge of the Sis]]''</ref> affiliated with the [[Sith]]<ref name="Sith" /> tradition of [[Force-sensitive|Force-users]].<ref name="DB Sith">{{Databank|sith|Sith}}</ref> The [[Twi'lek]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Skia Ro]] famously defeated them in the [[Battle of Jabo]] at some point by<ref name="Revenge" /> approximately [[180 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediAcademyDates|7}}</ref> a fact that was known to Ro's [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Christina Starspeeder]].<ref name="Revenge" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Kozyr was mentioned in [[Jedi Academy: Revenge of the Sis|''Revenge of the Sis'']], the [[2019]] seventh entry in the ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Academy]]'' [[Canon|non-canon]] young-reader book series. The book was written by [[Jarrett J. Krosoczka]] and [[Amy Ignatow]].<ref name="Revenge" />
