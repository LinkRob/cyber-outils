'''Arboreal octopi''' were a type of [[octopus]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[forest]]s of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Cholganna]]. They were hunted by the [[feline]] [[nexu]], but were rarer than the [[bark rat]]s that made up the main part of the predator's [[diet]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the arboreal octopus was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David Reynolds]].<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> The [[Arboreal octopus/Legends|octopi]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in the [[2002]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]'', also written by Reynolds.<ref name="AotC VD">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> They then appeared in ''[[Beyond the Rim]]'', a [[2013]] adventure written by [[Sterling Hershey]] for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]].<ref name="BtR">''[[Beyond the Rim]]''</ref>
