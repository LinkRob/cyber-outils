Several [[Color|black]] '''[[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptors]]''' served as the personal [[starfighter]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]].

==Characteristics==
Particularly distinctive by its lack of [[color]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] third such [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|interceptor]] was accompanied by a silver [[astromech droid]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> Also, as Vader's personal [[Starship|craft]], he was able to prep the [[starfighter]] remotely through his [[Darth Vader's armor|armor]].<ref name="LotS" />

==History==
Fourteen [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], one [[interceptor]] was destroyed when he ejected from its [[cockpit]] and let it collide into a hijacked [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[freighter]] near [[Yaga Minor]]; nearly all of the [[Starship|vehicle]] was vaporized upon impact.<ref name="LotS" />

Shortly after, Vader [[pilot]]ed another interceptor in a [[Mission to Ryloth|mission]] to [[Ryloth]] before returning it to the ''[[Perilous]]'' in an attempt to halt a [[Free Ryloth Movement|group]] of [[saboteur]]s. Ultimately unsuccessful, the ''Perilous'' was destroyed, along with Vader's interceptor.<ref name="LotS" />

During Vader and [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s [[Mission to Murkhana|mission]] to [[Murkhana]], the ''[[Carrion Spike]]''{{'s}} hijacking by [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|insurgents]] prompted the two to contact [[Belderone]] and [[Coruscant]]. They requested the [[Liberator (Venator-class)|''Liberator'']] to be deployed to [[Nam Chorios]], where they boarded the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] and abandoned the temporarily-borrowed ''[[Parsec Predator]]''. Vader and Tarkin then arranged for the ''[[Goliath]]'' to move from its [[deepdock]] at [[Ord Mantell]] and bring Vader's current starfighter as well.<ref name="Tarkin" />

As soon as the ''Goliath'' arrived, Vader left the ''Liberator'' and went to the support [[carrier]]'s starfighter bay in which his personal ship rested to set about repairing several faults caused by the [[hyperspace]] jump from Ord Mantell, assisted by his astromech. When Tarkin successfully predicted the insurgents' next destination, Vader used his Eta-2 ''Actis'' to lead a [[Yellow Squadron (Galactic Empire)|squadron]] of eleven [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighters]] as "Yellow One" and [[Battle in the Phindar system|engaged]] the ''Carrion Spike'' in the [[Phindar system]]&mdash;their priority target being its [[hyperdrive]] generator. Despite Vader and Tarkin's efforts, the fugitive [[corvette]] managed to evade them by jumping into hyperspace. Returning to the ''Goliath'', Vader left his starfighter on the escort carrier and boarded the ''[[Executrix]]'' along with Tarkin.<ref name="Tarkin" />
