The '''Fourth Fleet''' was a numbered [[fleet]] within the [[Republic Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Republic Military]], and was commanded by [[Jedi High Council]] member and [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]].<ref name="Timelines" /> The Fourth Fleet included [[General]] Koon's<ref name="Note 1" /> small [[Plo Koon's fleet|battle group]],<ref name="HC 70">{{HelmetCollectionCite|70|Helmets}}</ref> consisting of three [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]], including Koon's [[flagship]], the ''[[Triumphant]]''.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> The Fourth Fleet was part of the larger [[Open Circle Fleet]].<ref name="Note 2" />

Created in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the Fourth Fleet was one of multiple Republic fleets scrambled to counter the Confederate [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]], the ''[[Malevolence]]''. However, despite its efforts, much of the Republic Fourth Fleet was lost to the ''Malevolence'',<ref name="Battles" /> including Plo Koon's fleet during the [[Battle of Abregado]].<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> The ''Malevolence'' was eventually destroyed during the [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula]] by the Open Circle Fleet.<ref name="Destroy Malevolence">{{TCW|Destroy Malevolence|Destroy ''Malevolence''}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Republic Fourth Fleet was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2021]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Battles" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Fourth Fleet/Legends|Fourth Fleet]] originated via mention in [[2008]]'s ''[[The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="Complete SWE">{{CSWECite|II|280|''Malevolence'' (I)}}</ref>
