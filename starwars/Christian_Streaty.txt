'''Christian Streaty''' is a television writer who wrote [[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]] episodes [[Kai's Bad Day]], [[Mystery of the Opal Cave]], and [[Creature Comforts]]. She was credited as the casting production assistant for fourteen episodes of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'', and as script coordinator for the first season of the television series [[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]].
