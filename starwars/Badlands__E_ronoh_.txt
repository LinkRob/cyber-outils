The '''Badlands''' was a [[desert]] region on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]] that was home to the [[farm]]ing [[City|village]] [[A'ranni]]. [[Iazacal tree]]s were cultivated on [[Iazacal farm|farms]] by the [[E'roni]].<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
