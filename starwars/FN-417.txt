The [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] '''FN-417''' was one of many deployed to the [[planet]] [[Takodana]] in [[34 ABY]] to recover the [[Resistance]] [[astromech droid]] [[BB-8]]. As the First Order's [[First Order military|forces]] [[Battle of Takodana|attacked]] the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]], where the [[droid]] had been sighted, FN-417 moved into the surrounding [[Forest|woods]].

While being ordered to hold position, he was spotted by the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], an ally of BB-8. After Rey failed to fire an initial shot from her [[Rey's blaster|NN-14 blaster pistol]] due to the [[NN-14 blaster pistol|weapon's]] [[safety]] still being on, the stormtrooper fired off one shot before being [[Death|killed]] by the scavenger.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Oh, safety.|Rey realizes the safety of her blaster is still on while trying to shoot FN-417|audio=Rey-Saftey.flac|<ref name="TFA" />}}
[[File:FN-417_killed.jpg|thumb|left|150px|FN-417 was killed on Takodana.]]
FN-417<ref name="TFA" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> who served in the [[FN Trooper Corps|FN Corps]],<ref name="BTA" /> a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] unit in the [[First Order]]'s [[First Order military|military]] during their [[Cold War|conflict]] with the [[New Republic]]&ndash;backed [[Resistance]].<ref name="TFA" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="SWGA" /> he<ref name="TFA AD" /> was amongst many [[trooper]]s deployed to the [[planet]] [[Takodana]] in order to recover the Resistance [[astromech droid]] [[BB-8]], who had been sighted in the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]]. As the [[Battle of Takodana|attack]] on the castle began, FN-417 moved into the surrounding [[forest]] but was ordered to hold his position via radio.<ref name="TFA" />

Overhearing the order from nearby, the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] attempted to shoot the stormtrooper, but failed in her first attempt as the [[safety]] of her<ref name="TFA" /> [[Rey's blaster|NN-14 blaster pistol]]<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> was still on. Before she had another chance to shoot, FN-417 turned and opened fire but missed. Rey then fired off two more shots with her [[NN-14 blaster pistol|pistol]], the second of which hit and [[Death|killed]] him; however, she was captured shortly thereafter by First Order forces.<ref name="TFA" />

==Equipment==
FN-417 wore standard [[First Order stormtrooper armor]] and carried<ref name="TFA" /> an [[F-11D blaster rifle]].<ref name="TFAVD" />
