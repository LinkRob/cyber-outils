'''Aurodium''' was a type of [[Color|metallic yellow-colored]] [[metal]] rarer than [[gold]].<ref name="Bucket List" /> It was [[Ossus hyperspace route rumor|rumored]] that alchemical forges on the [[planet]] [[Ossus]] were used to turn [[lead]] into aurodium.<ref name="NoP">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref> The [[aurodium standard]] for [[currency]] was used by the [[Galactic Republic]] long before the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

==History==
===Imperial Era===
[[File:HanSoloDice-SW100Objects.png|thumb|right|250px|Han Solo's aurodium-plated dice]]
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' takes place five [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Great Jedi Purge]], which is dated to [[19 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'' is set in [[14 BBY]].</ref> the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] acquired enough aurodium to fashion a [[lightsaber]] hilt.<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

[[Han Solo]] was in possession of [[Han Solo's dice|aurodium-plated dice]] during his [[time]] as a [[scrumrat]] on [[Corellia]].<ref name="Solo Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> [[Dryden Vos]], leader of [[Crimson Dawn]], wore an aurodium [[Dryden Vos' signet ring|ring]].<ref name="Solo Novel">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

===New Republic Era===
{{Quote|I'm sure Slingshot would like this aurodium for his droideka, but I earned this.|[[Zaina]]|<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>}}
During the [[Cold War]], the [[pilot]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] won a [[Kaz's lucky trophy|trophy]] made of aurodium from a race.<ref name="Triple Dark" /> After the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] wore a pair of golden aurodium [[Piercing|earrings]].<ref name="TLJ VD" /> The [[Ubdurian]] [[Cratinus]] wore a belt that had a buckle made of aurodium.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

[[Currency]] on [[Cantonica]] was made from aurodium and the standard on the planet rather than [[New Republic]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], which were considered volatile. Anyone looking to spend money in the planet's [[casino]]s had to first exchange their funds for the resort's standard.<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]] wore a ring made from the material.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[Aurodium/Legends|Aurodium]] first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel ''[[Rogue Planet]]'', released on [[May 2]], [[2000]].<ref name="RP">''[[Rogue Planet]]''</ref> It was introduced to ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> Aurodium features in the video game ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'' as the most expensive [[Datacard/Legends|datapack]] type.

The metal's name was inspired by the real chemical element [[Wikipedia:Gold|gold]] and its symbol in the [[Wikipedia:Periodic table|periodic table of elements]]: Au,<ref name="Bucket List" /> which in turn is derived from the Latin ''aurum''.
