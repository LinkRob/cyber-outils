'''Corellian red''' was a type of [[wine]]<ref name="Guide" /> that had a [[Color|pink]] color.<ref name="HNTGE" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="AOTCDate">{{FilmDate|II|the appearance of [[Corellian]] [[Color|red]]}}</ref> Corellian red was being served at the [[Outlander Club]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and was kept in a tall tube on the counter behind the club's central bar.<ref name="HNTGE" /> During the [[Imperial Era]],<ref name="TLR" /> in [[1 BBY]]<ref name="GA">{{FilmDate|IV|1|the appearance of [[Corellian]] [[Color|red]]}}</ref> Corellian red was served on the world [[Tatooine]] in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]].<ref name="TLR" /> It was a favorite among [[smuggler]]s, [[pilot]]s and shipbuilders and was served in [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]].<ref name="Guide" />
