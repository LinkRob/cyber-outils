{{Quote|You say: I cannot name the nameless, Nor praise the unknown, Nor swear upon that I do not understand, When all ends in Death. And I say: Then you are not alive.|Aurek|<ref name="GotW" />}}
'''Aurek''' was a pupil of the [[Central Isopter]] religious group and was quoted speaking about [[death]] in ''[[Collected Poems, Prayers, and Meditations on the Force]]'', a compilation of writings concerning [[the Force]] edited by the [[Disciples of the Whills|Disciple of the Whills]] [[Kozem Pel]].<ref name="GotW" /> The pupil's name was shared with that of a [[Aurek|letter]] in the [[Aurebesh]] alphabet.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Aurek was first mentioned as part of a chapter quote in the novel [[Guardians of the Whills (novel)|''Guardians of the Whills'']], which was written by [[Greg Rucka]]<ref name="GotW" /> and released on [[May 2]], [[2017]].<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/new-novels-rebel-rising-and-guardians-of-the-whills-will-explore-rogue-one-backstories|text=New Novels ''Rebel Rising'' and ''Guardians of the Whills'' Will Explore ''Rogue One'' Backstories}}</ref>
