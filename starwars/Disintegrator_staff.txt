A '''disintegrator staff''' was a type of [[staff]] with a [[Color|brown]] shaft and clubbed ends. The [[Toydarian]] [[junk dealer]] [[Watto]] kept a disintegrator staff with the ends wrapped on the trophy shelf of [[podracing]] memorabilia at his [[Watto's junkshop|junkshop]] in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] when [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] led a group into the store looking for a [[T-14 hyperdrive generator]]<ref name="CL" /> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> The items on the shelf included trophies gifted to him by famous [[Podracer (profession)|podracers]] and pit crews, items stolen from [[museum]]s, and purchases made by his agents at the illegal auctions traditionally held after podraces with spectacular crashes.<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], a disintegrator staff was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="CL" /> from the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]], illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]], and marked the first mention of the [[Disintegrator staff/Legends|device]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
