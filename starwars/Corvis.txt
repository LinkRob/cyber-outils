'''Corvis''' was a [[clone trooper pilot]] in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[104th Battalion]]. In [[22 BBY]], Corvis [[pilot]]ed a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic attack gunship]] during a [[Mission to Quarmendy|mission]] on the [[planet]] [[Quarmendy]]. Corvis flew below the enemy scanners and deployed fellow Wolfpack [[trooper]]s [[Wolffe]], [[Boost]], [[Sinker]], and [[Comet (clone trooper)|Comet]].

==Biography==
[[Reproduction|Created]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]],<ref name="Clones" /> Corvis was a [[clone trooper]] who served as a [[Clone trooper pilot|pilot]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[Republic Navy|navy]], specifically in the [[104th Battalion]], which was under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]]. During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Corvis participated in a [[Mission to Quarmendy|mission]] to the floating [[city]] called the [[Nexus]] on the planet [[Quarmendy]]<ref name="Battle Tales 2" /> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

As Koon and several other [[pilot]]s flying [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170 starfighters]] engaged the [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]], Corvis piloted a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic attack gunship]] that carried [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s Wolffe, [[Boost]], [[Sinker]], and [[Comet (clone trooper)|Comet]], toward the city. Being careful to stay below the designated altitude so as to evade enemy scanners, Corvis flew near the city and allowed the four clones, who were equipped with [[JT-12 jetpack]]s, to deploy from the [[gunship]] so they could finish their approach to the Nexus. The clones were able to rescue a [[Orkle|hostage]] and escape the Nexus before it was destroyed.<ref name="Battle Tales 2" />

==Personality and traits==
As a [[human]] clone of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], Corvis had [[Color|tan]] skin and was 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]]) tall,<ref name="Clones" /> weighing eighty [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Weight" />

==Equipment==
Corvis wore white clone pilot [[Clone trooper armor|armor]]<ref name="Battle Tales 2" /> that was somewhat different from other [[armor]] worn by other clone pilots<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> in that it included a [[helmet]] fin and had tubes protruding from the chest armor<ref name="Battle Tales 2" /> as opposed to running behind the helmet.<ref name="TCW film" /> Like other clone troopers, Corvis was implanted with a [[behavioral modification biochip]] by [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]]s at birth to ensure compliance with certain [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|orders]].<ref name="Clones" />
