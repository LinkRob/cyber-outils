'''Darth Zannah''' was a [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] and the [[Sith apprentice]] of [[Darth Bane]]. Zannah was trained to carry Bane's teachings and the [[Rule of Two]] forward after a [[Jedi-Sith War|war]] between the [[Sith]] and [[Jedi Order]]s.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Lord Bane's own apprentice struck him down to claim Bane's place. And so began a magnificently vicious cycle. Many Sith apprentices&mdash;myself included&mdash;have supplanted their masters in order to ascend.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="Secrets of the Sith">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
[[File:SithOrder-SOTJ.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Darth Bane, the Sith Master of Darth Zannah, reformed the Sith Order by creating the Rule of Two.]]
Approximately [[Millennium|one thousand years]] prior to the [[Invasion of Naboo]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Bane]] enacted the [[Rule of Two]]. This teaching mandated that there would only be two [[Sith]] at any given [[time]]; a [[Sith Master|master]] to embody power, and an [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] to crave it.<ref name="BaneDB">{{Databank|darth-bane|Darth Bane}}</ref> As part of the Rule of Two, Bane took on an apprentice,<ref name="Sacrifice" /> [[Darth]] Zannah,<ref name="HC 2">{{HelmetCollectionCite|2|Databank A-Z}}</ref> to whom he passed on his teachings.<ref name="Sacrifice" />

Zannah escaped the notice of the [[Jedi Order]]. After Bane's defeat, his apprentice carried on the Rule of Two, which allowed the Sith to survive in secret while the [[Jedi]] falsely believed that the Sith had become extinct.<ref name="SWEBane">{{SWE|characters|darth-bane|Darth Bane}}</ref> Although the historical records were not accurate about the identity of Bane's apprentice or how Bane [[Death|died]],<ref name="SW:TDS">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side]]''</ref> he supposedly died in a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] with Zannah on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] world of [[Ambria]], a pattern which would be repeated by almost all Sith thereafter.<ref name="HC 2" />

[[File:Sidious_hologram.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Sidious ascended to power by killing his master, Darth Plagueis, a millennium after Bane was killed by his apprentice.]]
[[Darth Sidious]]' [[book]], ''[[The Secrets of the Sith]]'' recounted the history of the Sith Order, including its reformation under Darth Bane and his apprentice. Sidious praised<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> Zannah's master<ref name="HC 2" /> as a great Sith Lord; he acknowledged Bane as the founder of a Sith line of succession that culminated in his rise to power as [[Galactic Emperor|ruler]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Sidious also credited Bane and his apprentice for starting the Rule of Two, thereby ensuring the Sith Order's survival through a succession of Sith apprentices ascending to the power of the dark side by killing their masters, beginning with Bane's death by the hand of his own pupil. Though not all Sith apprentices were able to supplant their masters, Sidious successfully completed his apprenticeship by killing his master, [[Darth Plagueis]].<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BaneZannah-DoE.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Bane and Darth Zannah in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity.]]
[[Canon]]ically, Darth Zannah was first mentioned, albeit indirectly, in the episode "[[Sacrifice (episode)|Sacrifice]]," the thirteenth and final episode of [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Sacrifice" /> Zannah was first identified by name in the magazine [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 2|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 2]].<ref name="HC 2" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], Darth Bane's apprentice was first mentioned in [[Terry Brooks]]' [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|novelization]] for [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. As the novelization was written before the identity of Zannah was established, it refers to Darth Bane's apprentice as "him."<ref name="TPMNovelization">[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'' novelization]]</ref> Brooks' novelization was based on backstory notes from ''[[Star Wars]]'' creator [[George Lucas]].<ref name="Endnotes for the NEC">
{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/ref/f20051025/indexp2.html|text=Endnotes for the New Essential Chronology}}</ref> The character of [[Darth Zannah/Legends|Zannah]] first appeared in the [[Jedi vs. Sith 1|first issue]] of the comic series ''[[Star Wars: Jedi vs. Sith]]'',<ref name="jvs1">[[Jedi vs. Sith 1|''Jedi vs. Sith'' 1]]</ref> which was published on [[April 18]], [[2001]] by [[Dark Horse Comics]].<ref name="DH">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/99-222/Star-Wars-Jedi-vs-Sith-1-of-6|text=Star Wars: Jedi vs. Sith #1 (of 6)}}</ref>
