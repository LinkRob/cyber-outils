The '''C-25 fragmentation grenade''' was a [[fragmentation grenade]] manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] It consisted of a sphere of [[baradium]]-impregnated [[detonite]] encased in a conductive shell, which was itself housed within a cylinder that was activated with a twist.<ref name="UVG" /> To trigger an explosion, the C-25's user had to turn the cylindrical part of the grenade, which gave them a couple of seconds to move away before it exploded.<ref name="EHWaUE">{{EncyclopediaCite|Explosives, Heavy Weapons, and Power Sources}}</ref> The C-25 fragmentation grenade was visually very similar to the [[Sonic Imploder]].<ref name="Battlefront Scarif">{{EA|subdomain=starwars|url=starwars/battlefront/rogue-one|text=Rogue One: Scarif|date=2016-12-02}}</ref>

The C-25 was used by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[death trooper]]s and [[coastal defender stormtrooper]]s. During the [[Battle of Scarif]], rebels [[Bodhi Rook]] and [[Baze Malbus]] were both killed by C-25 fragmentation grenades.<ref name="Rogue One" />
