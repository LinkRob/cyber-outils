{{Quote|You have an eidetic memory, and I've spoken with your superiors. Apparently, once you see something, you can replicate it, and you've done so on a few occasions previously.|[[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]], to Seyn Marana|<ref name="Inferno Squad">''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''</ref>}}
An '''eidetic memory''' was the ability to remember everything one saw and heard. It was useful in learning multiple [[language]]s. [[Inferno Squad]] member [[Seyn Marana]] possessed an eidetic memory.<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> [[Willard Waylin]]'s eidetic memory and fascination with Imperial design made him an unparalleled mechanic in the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> [[Vi Moradi]] also possessed an eidetic memory and used it to vividly recount the stories told to her by [[Siv]] about [[Phasma]].<ref name="Phasma">[[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']]</ref> One [[Duros]] claimed to Moradi that they had perfect memories.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref> [[Yama Dex]] possessed an eidetic memory, which was why she had been placed in the records department.<ref name="Resistance Reborn">''[[Resistance Reborn]]''</ref> All members of the [[Kage]] [[species]] had an eidetic memory.<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref>
