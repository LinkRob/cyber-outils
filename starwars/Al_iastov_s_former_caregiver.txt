A [[Chiss]] [[Gender|female]] was the former '''caregiver''' of [[sky-walker]] [[navigator]] [[Mitth'ali'astov|Al'iastov]]. At some point they told Al'iastov not to complain about her feelings.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> Around [[39 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Mitth'raw'nuru]] met the [[sky-walker]] [[Mitth'ali'astov|Al'iastov]] around [[39 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:WhenThrassMetThrawnDate|here]].</ref> on Al'iastov's final voyage as a sky-walker on the [[Chiss Defense Force Transport]] ''[[Tomra]]'', she met [[Cadet]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Mitth'raw'nuru]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," who saw her in a state of distress and asked if she was alright. Al'iastov, remembering her former [[caregiver]], told the cadet she was fine.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
This caregiver was mentioned in flashbacks of the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the first entry in his ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
