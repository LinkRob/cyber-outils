The '''Confederacy of Corporate Systems''' was an ambitious<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Politics|government]]<ref name="AEE" /> formed at a point where the [[New Republic]]'s control had been rendered theoretical.<ref name="Battles" /> By [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> as the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Battle of Endor|fracturing]] [[Galactic Empire]] and the New Republic drew to a [[Battle of Jakku|close]], [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Republic Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] had become increasingly unsettled by the arising Confederacy, along with two other ascending governments&mdash;the [[New Separatist Union]] and the [[pirate]]-led [[Sovereign Latitudes of Maracavanya]]. Those worries preoccupied Mothma during a meeting with [[senator]]s of the New Republic, although she conceded her distractions might also be due to her left [[Limb|arm]], which had been previously [[Attack on Chandrila|injured]].<ref name="AEE" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Confederacy of Corporate Systems was first mentioned in the [[2017]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', the final installment of [[Chuck Wendig]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="AEE" />
