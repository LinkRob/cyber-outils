'''Deggen Sont''' was a former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] who defected and freed the [[Wookiee]] [[Drokkatta]] from [[Slavery|enslavement]] at a [[Geonosis]] [[mining]] facility. After freeing the Wookiee, Drokkatta swore a [[life debt]] to Deggen Sont. In an effort to escape the Empire, he joined a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] convoy transporting civilians to a hidden rebel world. The Empire ambushed the convoy, and the transport he was on managed to jump to [[hyperspace]] and escape the battle. Eventually, the [[hyperdrive]] gave out near the planet [[Tatooine]], where the damaged ship crashed. Deggen Sont, along with the other survivors, set up a camp where they stayed before being attacked by [[Captain]] [[Terro]] and his forces.

==Biography==
===Service and desertion===
Deggen Sont was a [[Gender|man]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]]. He attended an [[Imperial Academy]] and eventually went to<ref name="Legends" /> an Imperial [[mining]] facility on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]]. The mining facility utilized the forced labor of [[Wookiee]]s to operate.<ref name="Heart of the Empire">{{SWIA|set=Heart of the Empire|mission=Unfinished Business}}</ref> At the facility, Deggen became acquainted with one of the Wookiee [[Slavery|slaves]] named [[Drokkatta]]. Deggen eventually decided to defect from the Empire, and helped Drokkatta escape enslavement in the process. After freeing the Wookiee and escaping the facility, Drokkatta swore a [[life debt]] to Deggen for his actions.<ref name="Legends" />

===Stuck on Tatooine===
{{GameMechanics|option=1}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="Legends" /> at some point during or before [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Greedo Alive">''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'' has [[Greedo]] alive during this time, so it must take place prior to his death, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Deggen Sont, now a fugitive of the Empire due to his defection, accompanied Drokkatta on a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] convoy transporting civilians to a hidden rebel world. The Empire ambushed the [[convoy]] and a battle ensued. The transport Deggen was on managed to jump to [[hyperspace]] and escape the battle. Eventually, the [[hyperdrive]] gave out near the planet [[Tatooine]], where the damaged ship crashed. Deggen, along with the other survivors, set up a camp where they stayed before being attacked by Imperial forces lead by [[Captain]] [[Terro]].<ref name="Legends" />

A [[Unidentified rebel squad|squad]] of rebels, which included Drokkatta, fought back against Terro's forces as the [[Stormtrooper]] captain attempted to gather the survivors and bring them aboard his [[dropship]]. The rebels were joined by some of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt's]] men and fought together to push back the Imperial forces. During the skirmish, Deggen made sure to cover his face in fear of being recognized as an Imperial defector and eventually ran to safety as the rebel [[squad]] covered his escape. Overwhelmed, Terro fled with some of the survivors he captured, leaving the rest behind, their fait in Jabba's hands. More of Jabba's men then arrived and escorted the survivors aboard [[skiff]]s. While on the skiff, Deggen "thanked" Drokkatta for almost getting him killed, and stated that the life debt was silly after the Wookiee gave him an annoyed glance.<ref name="Legends" />

As the group arrived at [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]], Deggen was separated from Drokkatta. Deggen was sent to the palace's [[prison]] while Drokkatta and the rest of the rebel squad were brought before Jabba. While in the dungeon, one of Jabba's slaves, a [[Gender|woman]] named [[Senza Terro|Senza]], tended to him and the other survivors. Drokkatta was eventually brought down to the prison where the Wookiee checked on Deggen. Deggen stated that he deserved to be behind bars, but he didn't think anything could be worse than an Imperial [[prison]]. After their conversation, Drokkatta left to do a job for Jabba while Deggen stayed behind in the cell.<ref name="Legends" />

===Life debt===
After a successful mission, Drokkatta and the rest of the squad returned to the prison to check on the survivors. Drokkatta checked on Deggen Sont who was in the middle of spitting out his [[Foodstuff|meal]]. Deggen then gave the Wookiee his [[bantha]] meat, stating that he'd just eat the [[nutrient paste]] which reminded him of his time at the academy. Deggen then said that if Drokkatta got him out of this place, then as far as he was concerned, the life debt was paid. After their discussion, Drokkatta then left with the rest of the rebel squad to go on more missions for Jabba while Deggen was left in the prison with the other survivors.<ref name="Legends" />

Eventually, Deggen Sont and three other survivors were brought into Jabba's audience chamber and chained by their necks. The rebel group then attacked the palace, something the Hutt anticipated. When the squad entered the chamber, Jabba stated that they are just in time to watch their friends die. Jabba then pressed a button on his [[Jabba the Hutt's dais|dais]] which opened up a trap door and dropped two of the survivors into a pit which housed a [[rancor]]. Jabba then fled behind a door, using the other two survivors as [[Shield (physical armor)|shields]]. The rebel squad split up, some dropped into the pit to fight the [[Pateesa|rancor]] and the others pursued Jabba before he could get away. The rebels managed to defeat both the rancor and Jabba, freeing Deggen and the rest of the survivors. They left the palace on a [[speeder bike]] and headed towards civilization. On their way there, Deggen told Drokkatta that the Wookiee did a good job. Drokkatta then stated the they were even, to which Deggen said he was no expert in the Wookiees' backwards ways, but he always assumed a life debt was for life. Having escaped Jabba's control, Deggen Sont, Drokkatta, and the other survivors boarded a transport and left Tatooine.<ref name="Legends" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Deggen Sont first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Assault]]'' companion app ''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'', in the "Jabba's Realm" campaign, made available for download in [[2018]].<ref name="Download">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/8/8/the-realm-of-the-crime-lord|text=The Realm of The Crime Lord}}</ref> Deggen Sont has a chance of being one of the eight crash survivors present during the first mission, or guaranteed if the player chooses Drokkatta to be in the [[squad]]. This article assumes a specific path is taken in the campaign, which can very depending on the choices of the players. This article is written from the perspective of having Drokkatta as one of the members in the squad. The dialogue between Deggen Sont and the players differs slightly depending on whether Drokkatta is present in the squad or not.<ref name="Legends" />

===Alternate paths===
Depending upon the actions and success of the player during the missions, Deggen Sont's fate can vary. Deggen Sont can end up being enslaved by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]], eaten by a [[rancor]], left behind at Terro's camp, or successfully have escaped Tatooine with the squad.<ref name="Legends" />

====Terro's camp====
If the players are unsuccessful during the fight when Terro attacks the survivors' camp, then Deggen Sont can end up being one of the survivors taken away on the dropship. If this is the case, then the players don't see Deggen again until later in the story. When the squad attacks the [[Captain Terro's listening post|Imperial listening post]], Deggen has a chance to be the individual in the [[bacta tank]]. If this is the case, then after releasing him from the tank he will explain that Terro took the other survivors to an outpost where he has sheltered and fed them. If a different character is in the [[bacta]] tank, then Deggen is at Terro's camp with the other survivors. If the players choose to make a deal with Terro, then he will bring the group to the outpost where they can find the other survivors, including Deggen. If the players instead choose to kill Terro, then they never go to the outpost and Deggen Sont, along with the other survivors, are left behind there.<ref name="Legends" />

====Prisoner====
If the players fail to complete the first mission Jabba sends them on, then when the characters check on the [[prisoner]]s, Jabba has their food taken away as punishment for their failure. If Deggen Sont is one of the prisoners, then his food will be taken away as well. He also has a chance to demand for the [[Gamorrean Warrior|Gamorrean guard]] to give him his food back. Deggen will ask the Gamorrean if he knows who he is, to which the guard will pause for a moment to think, before ultimately deciding he doesn't know who Deggen is and knocks him unconscious.<ref name="Legends" />

====Rebel help====
When the group obtains the [[code cylinder]]s, they have the option to keep them for the Rebel Alliance and not give them to Jabba. If they choose to keep them for the Alliance, then the group gets picked up by a rebel agent and taken to an outpost. The rebel agents will then rescue the survivors from Jabba's palace, which includes Deggen Sont if he wasn't captured by Terro. The rebels then find an Imperial tracker on their ship, which prompts them to attack the Imperial [[listening post]] on Tatooine. The survivors are left at the outpost, and while the group goes to attack the listening post, Jabba's forces attack the outpost and recapture the survivors, including Deggen.<ref name="Legends" />

====Enslavement====
The finale of the story can end with either the players choosing to storm the palace or trade themselves for the freedom of the survivors. When they trade themselves, the survivors, including Deggen Sont, are freed from their imprisonment. If the player successfully defeat Jabba afterwards, then the rebels meet up with the survivors. When the rebels attack the palace, Deggen has a chance of being one of the four survivors chained in the audience chamber. If Deggen is one of the survivors in the chamber, then he has a chance of either being one of two of the survivors to be dropped into the rancor pit, or one of two of the survivors to be used as a shield by Jabba as he flees. If the players fail to defeat the rancor while Deggen is in the pit, then he is eaten by the rancor. If the players fail to defeat Jabba while Deggen is being used as a shield, then Jabba flees with him and the other survivor and they become enslaved. Deggen also becomes enslaved, along with the rebels and the rest of the survivors, if the players fail to make it to the audience chamber during the attack on the palace.<ref name="Legends" />
