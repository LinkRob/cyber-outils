The '''B1 repeater blaster droid''' was a variant of the [[B1-series battle droid]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in its [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[droid]] used [[repeating blaster]]s, which gave them superior firepower to the standard B1. The droids also had the same markings as the standard B1.<ref name="Galactic Defense" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The B1 repeater blaster droids were first introduced in the mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''. The droid's description in the game was: "Armed with repeater blasters these variants of the B1 droid holster superior firepower capabilities."<ref name="Galactic Defense" />
