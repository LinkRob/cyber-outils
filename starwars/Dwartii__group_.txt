The '''Dwartii''' were a social group that inhabited [[the galaxy]]. [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] of the [[First Order]] had a [[Snoke's ring|ring]] that had [[gold]] etched with the glyphs of the Dwartii.<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> They also shared their name with the [[planet]] [[Dwartii]].<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Dwartii were first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TLJ VD" /> and published on [[December 17]], [[2017]].<ref name="TLJ VD release">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/558520/star-wars-the-last-jedi--the-visual-dictionary-by-pablo-hidalgo|text=Star Wars The Last Jedi The Visual Dictionary}}</ref> When asked on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Matt Martin]] said he assumed the book's mention of "the Dwartii" referred to the [[Four Sages of Dwartii]].<ref name="Martin tweet">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1216920127280537601|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=I assume the former.|archivefile=File:Matt_Martin_the_Dwartii_tweet.png|archivedate=20200117051831}} {{C|In response to: "Hi Matt, not sure if you're able to answer this, but are 'the Dwartii' referenced in the TLJ visual dictionary referring to the Four Sages of Dwartii, or an actual species called the Dwartii?"}}</ref>
