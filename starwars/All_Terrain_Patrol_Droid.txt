I hope to have some armored vehicles of my own when I finally face them.|Major Caluan Ematt in ''The Rebel Files''|<ref name="Star Wars: The Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>}}
The '''All Terrain Patrol Droid (AT-PD)''' was a [[droid]] [[walker]] used by the [[First Order]] during its [[Cold War|conflict]] with the [[Resistance]]. Produced in small numbers, it was deployed across [[Starkiller Base]] to protect critical installations. The walker's two [[Laser cannon|heavy-laser cannons]] were powered by generators located in its "head."<ref name="On the Front Lines" />
