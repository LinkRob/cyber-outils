'''Alderaanian white''' was a light-[[color]]ed [[wine]] from the [[Core Worlds|Core]] [[planet]] of [[Alderaan]]. [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] was known to drink that wine on occasion. He notably poured glasses of it while stationed on [[Agaris]], as part of an attempt to make [[Auric Graf|Auric]] and [[Rhyssa Graf]] reveal everything they knew about the planet and its [[Agarian|inhabitants]].<ref name="The Rescue" />
