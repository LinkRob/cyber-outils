The '''Dune Sea Outpost''',<ref name="SWH" /> or simply called the '''Outpost''', was a battlefield in the [[Grand Arena]] that resembled an [[Unidentified outpost (Tatooine)|outpost]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. It contained [[Dug's Discount Goods]], a [[Joltin's Workshop]] location, and the [[Blue Bantha Milk Kiosk]].<ref name="Hunters" />
