'''Farshore''' was a section of the [[Great Basin]] region of [[Akiva]], a [[jungle]] [[planet]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was situated on the shore of the basin's [[Lake Amari]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|option=1}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] was involved in an incident on Akiva that caused her to be wanted by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Shortly afterwards he was spotted in the Farshore area and an [[Imperial dispatcher]] report the sighting to the Imperial forces who were searching for her.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The suspect has been seen in Farshore. Move in and ensure she is arrested.|An Imperial dispatcher reporting Kay Vess's location in Farshore|audio=SuspectHasBeenSeenInFarshore-Outlaws.flac|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
Farshore appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was only identified by the Imperial dispatcher comm messages which were part of the game's wanted system. As such the exact bounds of Farshore are not clearly defined.<ref name="Outlaws" />
