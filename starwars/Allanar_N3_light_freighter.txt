The '''Allanar N3 light freighter''' was a model of [[light freighter]] that saw use during the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Starship|ship]], which typically required a crew of 4 to 7, boasted a [[hyperdrive]] system,<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> three [[sublight engine]]s,<ref name="APYD" /> and multiple forward facing [[laser cannon]]s.<ref name="FTSM" />

One such ship, [[AL6-KM44]], was among the many freighters used to bring supplies to the [[Kurost transfer point]], where said supplies were transferred to larger freighters and then flown to the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star's]] secret building location.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> another ship was cleared to depart [[Hauler's Keep]], a [[space station]] in the [[Khepi system]] of [[Wild Space]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

In the [[Anoat sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], Allanars were also used by the [[Trade Spine League]],<ref name="APYD" /> a group of [[smuggler]]s and freelance [[Mercenary|mercenaries]].<ref name="OS Factions">{{WebCite|url=https://playstarwarsuprising.com/game-guide/introduction/#factions|text=Factions|work=Star Wars: Uprising Official Website|archivedate=20160624013822}}</ref> Allanars in the League's employ included the ''[[Mother of Pearl]]'',<ref name="APYD" /> the ''[[Cruel Mistress]]'',<ref name="SOTCM">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Story Mission: "Salvage of the ''Cruel Mistress''"</ref> the ''[[Space Manta]]'',<ref name="FTSM" /> and the ''[[Pretty Profit]]''.<ref name="PR">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Story Mission: "Profitable Recovery"</ref>
