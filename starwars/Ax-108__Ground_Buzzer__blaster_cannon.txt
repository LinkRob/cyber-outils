The '''Ax-108 "Ground Buzzer" blaster cannon''' was a [[blaster cannon]] model produced by [[BlasTech Industries]] that [[Han Solo]] mounted on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' for defense while the [[freighter]] was on the ground.<ref name="TFAICS" /> It had a dedicated generator so that it could work even if the ship's electrical system was down. The blaster cannon had a fire rate of 12 blasts per second and had a range of power settings from stun to armor-piercing. The cannon could be controlled manually or by the ship's computer, which would prevent it from hitting friendlies or parts of the ship itself.<ref name="OM">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>
