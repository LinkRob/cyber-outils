'''Excelsior Publications''' was the French publisher responsible for publishing ''[[Jeux et Stratégie]]'' and ''[[Casus Belli (magazine)|Casus Belli]]''.<ref name="CB44">[[Casus Belli 44|''Casus Belli'' #44]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|&hellip;''Star Wars'' est un jeu édité par ''Jeux Descartes''  sous licence ''West End Games''&hellip;|[[Casus Belli 44|''Casus Belli'' #44]]|<ref name="CB44" />}}

Issues of [[Casus Belli 44|''Casus Belli'']] state that "''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|Star Wars]]'' is a game published by ''[[Jeux Descartes]]'' under license from ''[[West End Games]]''",<ref name="CB44" /> however comments by [[Leland Chee]], with regards to [[Lucas Licensing]] and the [[Holocron continuity database]], have resulted in certain materials published prior to [[2003]] to have their licensing status remain undetermined.
