The '''E-50 Landseer''' was a model of repurposed [[CSS-1 Corellian Star Shuttle|Corellian shuttle]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] as ambulance [[Starship|ships]], conveying medical and repair payloads directly on the battlefield. Its hull bore the universal [[red sigil]],<ref name="SWC" /> an identifier for medical equipment, vehicles and personnel.<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Despite being listed as a repurposed Corellian shuttle, the Landseer more closely resembles the crewed section of [[Kuat Systems Engineering/Legends|Kuat Systems Engineering's]] [[Wayfarer-class medium freighter|''Wayfarer''-class medium freighter]].
