An '''eight-leg''' was a creature native to [[Dowut]]. They hid between rocks. When [[Pan Eyta]] was kicked out of his home in around [[423 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]]. Eyta is stated to be around two hundred years old meaning he must have been born there around [[431 BBY]]. He is also stated to be eight years old when he is kicked out of his house. Therefore his encounter with a eight-leg must have taken place in around [[423 BBY]]</ref> he survived on the meat of eight-legs.<ref name="TRS">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>
