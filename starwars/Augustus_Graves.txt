'''Augustus Graves''' was a [[Nimbanel]] [[Gender|male]] who was the [[Chief Executive Officer|CEO]] of the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Han Chewie 1" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> Graves came into [[Unidentified urn|possession]] of the ashes of [[Krestrel D'Naran]], one of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]'s most hated rivals, and kept them within his [[Augustus Graves' penthouse|penthouse]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="Han Chewie 1" />
