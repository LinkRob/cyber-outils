'''Cambylictus trees''' were a type of [[tree]] found in the [[swamp]]y foothills of the [[Gallo Mountains]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. By [[228 BBY]], the [[San Tekka clan|San Tekka family's]] [[San Tekka compound|compound]] used the [[tree]]'s [[wood]] as wall paneling. Several cambylictus trees grew over the statues of the [[Gungan Sacred Place]], where in [[32 BBY]] the [[Gungan]] [[Boss]] [[Rugor Nass]] stood among the roots of one of these trees as he listened to [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] ask for aid amidst the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo]].

==Description==
Cambylictus trees had [[Color|brown]] [[Plant|bark]]; green leaves; and large, tangled roots that lifted the trunk of the [[tree]] above the ground. They could grow in [[swamp]]y terrain.<ref name="CL 2016" /> Its [[wood]] could be polished and used as wall paneling in structures.<ref name="TOTF">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:Nass_on_Sacred_Place.png|thumb|left|250px|alt=Boss Nass stands in a brown robe atop a large statue head partial submerged in the ground and covered in cambylictus tree roots. Behind him, two cowled Gungan High Council Reps also stand on the statue, with another of the statue heads visible in the background with a kaadu-riding militiagung soldier beside it.|Boss Nass stands among the roots of a cambylictus tree.]]
At least three<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> cambylictus trees could be found growing over the stone structures of the ruined temple known as the [[Gungan Sacred Place]], located in the swampy foothills of the [[Gallo Mountains]], north of the [[Lianorm Swamp]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. The trees made up part of the dense foliage that cut off the site from the outside world.<ref name="CL 2016" /> By [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the [[San Tekka compound|compound]] of the wealthy [[San Tekka clan|San Tekka family]] [[Gender|patriarchs]] [[Vellis San Tekka|Vellis]] and [[Marlowe San Tekka|Marlowe]] had an [[observation deck]] room decorated with [[marble]] and polished cambylictus wood paneling.<ref name="TOTF" />

In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}} to [[32 BBY]].</ref> [[Boss]] [[Rugor Nass]] stood among the roots of a cambylictus tree atop one of the Sacred Place's fallen statues as he listened to [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] plead for help in [[Battle of Naboo|fighting]] the [[Trade Federation]] during the [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="TPM" /> At some point after [[34 ABY]], an illustration of a cambylictus tree was included on a map of Naboo thought to have been drawn by the [[artist]] [[Gammit Chond]] based purely on travelers' tales. The map was later restored and published in the ''[[Galactic Atlas]]'' by the [[Graf Archive]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> with a later updated version of the [[book]] also including the map showing the trees.<ref name="AtlasUpdated">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Sacred_place_set.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=A member of the Episode I production crew stands amidst the minatures used for the Gungan sacred place set, including a number of camylictus trees that stand taller then crewmember's head. Miniatures of the statue heads and ruins are also present and a painted forest background stands behind the brightly-lit miniatures.|The cambylictus trees were made in miniature.]]
Cambylictus trees first appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="TPM" /> They were first identified by name in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="CL 2016" /> The [[Cambylictus tree/Legends|cambylictus tree's]] name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, in the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', written by [[Kristin Lund]].<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>

The trees seen in the film were part of a miniature set built by [[Industrial Light & Magic]]. They were included in order to make the scenery of the Gungan Sacred Place a little more exotic than the [[Whippendell Woods]] location, where the rest of the scene was shot.<ref name="365 Days">''[[Creating the Worlds of Star Wars: 365 Days]]''</ref>
