The '''Almakar sector''' included the [[Almakar system/Legends|Almakar system]],<ref name="Twinkle" /> a part of [[The Slice/Legends|the Slice]] portion of the [[Inner Rim/Legends|Inner Rim]].<ref name="TEA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Almakar [[Sector/Legends|sector]] was mentioned in "[[Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star, How I Wonder Where We Are]]," a [[1990]] [[roleplaying game]] source article written by [[Glen Allison]] as a supplement for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' and published in [[Voyages SF 13|''Voyages SF'' 13]]. The article placed the [[Aldo Spachian system|Aldo Spachian]] (spelled as the "Aldo Spachi" [[Star system/Legends|system]]), Almakar, [[Dagobah system/Legends|Dagobah]], [[Kestel system/Legends|Kestel]], [[Mon Torri system/Legends|Mon Torri]], and [[Stic system/Legends|Stic systems]] in the sector.<ref name="Twinkle" />

Since that article was released outside of the [[Lucas Licensing]] process,<ref name="Twinkle">{{VoyagesCite|13|Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star, How I Wonder Where We Are}}</ref> it was considered non-[[canon]]ical with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="UnlicensedStatus">{{UnlicensedStatus}}</ref> The [[StarWars.com]] [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' has since instead placed the Aldo Spachian, Dagobah, Kestel, Mon Torri, and Stic systems in other sectors.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
