'''Forvan''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who lived as a member of the [[Colony of Kezarat|Colony of ''Kezarat'']] in [[No-Space]],<ref name="Star Wars 31" /> where the [[Great Hall of the Nihil|headquarters]] of the [[Nihil]], a group of [[Pirate|marauders]], was once located.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> Forvan was the son of the [[Blythe]], the [[captain]] of the [[convoy]].<ref name="Star Wars 31" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars (2020) 30|''Star Wars'' (2020) 30]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> long after the Nihil had become defunct, the kid encountered<ref name="Star Wars 31" /> an elite [[squad]]<ref name="216 Launchpad">{{InsiderCite|216|Launchpad}}</ref> of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], who had been [[transport]]ed to No-Space by a Nihil [[Path engine]] as they attempted to escape from the [[Galactic Empire]]. The kid asked the rebels if they had any flavors, and in exchange for information about where they were, [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] gave the kid a [[Ration pack|pack]] of [[Ration bar|ration sticks]]. The kid then informed the rebels that the place was once important, but it was the only place he lived. He then added that older individuals who lived in No-Space spoke of another place that had a lot of different stuff, but he had not been there. Before he could finish though, the kid heard a noise and became spooked. He thanked Organa for the food, and told her he had to leave, and informed the rebels they should hide. As the kid ran away, Organa tried to ask him what he was afraid of, and where he was going, but he did not respond.<ref name="Star Wars 30" />

The kid then made his way to an edge, and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] questioned where the kid thought he was going to go. In response, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Luke Skywalker]] stated that he had sensed that the boy was terrified, and that he didn't know where he was going, before adding that he intended to [[Force power|use]] [[the Force]] to stop him before he went over the edge. To the rebels surprise, the kid then jumped off the edge, but quickly activated a [[jetpack]], and left. With the kid gone, the rebels saw what he had been afraid of, a group of [[Killdroid|droids]]. After the rebels had been rescued by a [[Unidentified starship (Colony of Kezarat)|ship]] of the colony, they were requested to throw their weapons out of the [[Starship|ship's]] [[Magnetic field|magnetic shield]] by Blythe. As the rebels complied with the request, the kid appeared and caught their weapons, which he put into a sack. One of the weapons, Skywalker's [[Luke Skywalker's yellow lightsaber|lightsaber]], caught the attention of the kid, who ignited it and noticed it was [[Color|yellow]]. Skywalker told the kid to be careful with the saber, as he could hurt himself, though the kid ignored him and flew away with the saber still ignited.<ref name="Star Wars 30" />
