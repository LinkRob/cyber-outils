The '''death trooper training camp''' was a training camp<ref name="UVG" /> maintained for the recruitment of [[death trooper]]s by the [[Galactic Empire]] on [[Scarif]] prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]]. In a request meant to be sent out through the [[oversector]], preferably to a [[sector admiral]]. [[General]] [[Sotorus Ramda]] requested two [[platoon]]s of [[death]] [[trooper]]s to be returned due to the training camp needing more [[instructor]]s. Ramda preferred veteran death troopers with body augmentations at v1.01 or earlier.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
