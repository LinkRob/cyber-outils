The '''chimaera''' was a three-headed monstrous [[Chimaera (creature)|creature]] used as a symbol of various individuals and organizations during the waning years of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the subsequent reigns of the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[New Republic]].

Originally the symbol of the [[Marocsaa subclan]] of the [[Paccosh]], it came to become the personal insignia of the [[Chiss]] [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]]. As such, it came to be associated with Imperial personnel [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|under Thrawn's command]] and even the namesake and ventral decal of Thrawn's flagship the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|first model]] of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Chimaera]]''.

==Description==
The chimaera symbol represented a monstrous creature with three heads and arc-like [[spider]] [[Limb|legs]].<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref>

==Symbol of the Marocsaa Subclan==
[[File:Seventh_Fleet.svg|thumb|left|200px|This stylized depiction of a chimaera became the symbol of the Galactic Empire's Seventh Fleet.]]
[[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[Core name|better known as]] "Thrawn," originally came across the chimaera as a [[Senior Captain]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> around [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Chaos Rising date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' in [[19 BBY]].</ref> At the time, most of [[the galaxy]] outside the [[Unknown Regions]] was ruled by either the [[Galactic Republic]] or the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]; two parties locked in the pan-galactic [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

While investigating an [[Attack on the edge of the Dioya system|attack on a refugee ship]] at the edge of Chiss space, Thrawn journeyed to the [[Rapacc system]], from where the dead refugees came. There, Thrawn encountered a [[Paccosh]] named [[Uingali foar Marocsaa]] and his [[Unidentified Paccosh spokesman|translator]], who warned the [[Chiss]] that the Paccosh feared extinction at the hands of their conquerers, the [[Nikardun Destiny]]. Fearing the loss of his people's memory in the knowledge of history, Uingali entrusted Thrawn with a [[Marocsaa subclan ring|treasured ring of his clan]] depicting a chimaera, so that the [[Marocsaa subclan]] would be remembered if the worst should happen.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

The next year,<ref name="Greater Good date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> Thrawn returned to [[Rapacc]], finding its people freed of their [[Nikardun]] oppressors. Thrawn met Uingali, by then an official within the [[Paccian Governance]], and saw that his old friend now commanded a captured [[Nikardun blockade frigate]] bearing a chimaera on its underside. Thrawn returned the Marocsaa subclan ring to Uingali,<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> but the Paccosh would eventually send it back to Thrawn as a gift in honor of the aid he had given their people which Thrawn accepted out of respect and was one of the few personal items he took with him upon his [[Exile (punishment)|exile]],<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> later coming to adopt the chimaera symbol as his personal insignia.<ref name="Imperial Eyes">{{Rebels|Through Imperial Eyes}}</ref>

==Thrawn's personal symbol==
[[File:Chimaera_Exp_Pack_box_art_crop.png|thumb|right|300px|The Imperial Star Destroyer ''Chimaera'', which had a chimaera pictured on its underside]]
Senior Captain Thrawn eventually left the Unknown Regions for "[[Outer Rim Territories|Lesser Space]]" to investigate the potential of the young [[Galactic Empire]] to serve as an ally against, and possibly bait for, "evil threats" to his Chiss Ascendancy.<ref name="Thrawn novel">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> Nevertheless, the Chiss retained the chimaera as his personal symbol. As the Chiss rose within the ranks of the [[Imperial Navy]], so too did the symbol of the chimaera.<ref name="Imperial Eyes" />

Upon Thrawn's promotion to [[Commodore]], he was granted command of the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|first model]] of an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial'' Star Destroyer]] named the ''[[Chimaera]]'',<ref name="Thrawn novel" /> which bore a stylized depiction of a chimaera on its ventral surface.<ref name="Thrawn 4">[[Thrawn 4|''Thrawn'' 4]]</ref><ref name="Rebel Recon #3.15">{{RebelsRecon|netG0u3D2F0|#3.15: Inside "Trials of the Darksaber" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-trials-of-the-darksaber}}</ref> The [[Imperial officer|officers]] and [[DT-series sentry droid|sentry droids]] in Thrawn's [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]] had a stylized depiction of a chimaera on their [[pauldron]]s or [[Imperial military uniforms|uniforms]].<ref name="Rebel Recon #3.18">{{RebelsRecon|6Ez3y6BZGrk|#3.18: Inside "Secret Cargo" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-secret-cargo}}</ref>

===Usage by Morgan Elsbeth===
[[File:HK-87_chimaera.png|thumb|left|250px|Morgan Elsbeth's HK-87 assassin droids bore the Seventh Fleet's chimaera symbol on the side of their heads.]]
[[Morgan Elsbeth]] was a woman whose [[Nightsisters|people]] were massacred during the Clone Wars, and who used her fury about that massacre to fuel the construction of the Imperial Starfleet. Elsbeth eventually became a protégé of Thrawn.<ref name="Mando 13">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref> In around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Mando date">{{MandoDate}}</ref> former [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] tracked Elsbeth down and waged a one-woman insurgent campaign against [[Morgan Elsbeth's forces|Morgan Elsbeth's army]] to liberate the oppressed people of [[Corvus]] and to learn the location of Thrawn. During the [[Liberation of Calodan]], Elsbeth's [[HK-87 assassin droid]]s bore a depiction of Thrawn's chimaera on the side of their heads.<ref name="Mando 13" /> At the time of the liberation of Calodan, the [[New Republic]] at least nominally ruled most of the galaxy, having defeated the Empire at the [[battle of Jakku]].<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The chimaera symbol was first depicted in ''Star Wars'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode [[Iron Squadron (episode)|Iron Squadron]], where it appeared on Thrawn's eponymous flagship.<ref name="Iron Squadron">{{Rebels|Iron Squadron (episode)}}</ref> The back-story for Thrawn's introduction to the chimaera as his symbol was first developed in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' by [[Timothy Zahn]],<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Thrawn's original creator.<ref name="HttE">''[[Heir to the Empire]]''</ref>

Previously, the chimaera had been associated with [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity, on Thrawn's [[Chimaera/Legends|''Chimaera'']] in that continuity.<ref name="HttE" />
