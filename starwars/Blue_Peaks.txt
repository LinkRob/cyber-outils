The '''Blue Peaks''' were a location in the southern hemisphere of the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. It was was populated by members of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Bonbrak]] [[species]]. Over a [[Standard century|century]] before<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> the area had a stable population of Bonbrak, but it began to decline as a result of illegal trading and them being [[Diet|eating]] by individuals such as the [[Shani]] [[Musician|singer]] [[Crukibolt Nart Adeen|Crufeela]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

In 230 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> after saving the Bonbrak [[Teetak]] from being eaten by Crufeela, [[Chief Inspector]] [[Deemus Abrus]] of the [[Coronet City Chapter of the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] considered that Teetak might be able to help repopulate the Blue Peaks. Teetak, however, decided that she wanted to accompany the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] and Abrus brought Teetak to Jomaram before he left the planet after [[Battle of the Corellian Shipyards|battling]] the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
