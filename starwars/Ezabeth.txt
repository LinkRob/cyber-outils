'''Ezabeth''' was a individual who, when they were between the ages of fifteen and twenty-five, lived on the [[planet]] [[Siline]] and,<ref name="TCC" /> in [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> participated [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|in that year's]] [[Crowning Convocation]]. Together with [[Larin]], [[Lionine Graf|Lio Graf]] and the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s [[Jecki Lon]] and [[Yord Fandar]], they were one of the only few non-[[Silinese]] contestants. Ezabeth was in attendance during the opening ceremony in the [[Grand banquet hall (Siline)|Grand banquet hall]] and later participated in the first day's dueling tournament.<ref name="TCC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ezabeth first appeared in the [[2025]] novel ''[[The Acolyte: The Crystal Crown]]'' by [[Tessa Gratton]]. In the novel Larin and Ezabeth are identified as a [[Twi'lek]] and a huge broad-shouldered [[Chagrian]], but it is not further specified which of them is which.<ref name="TCC" />

While the Twi'lek isn't mentioned anymore after the opening ceremony, the Chagrian appears again: During the dueling tournament, the Chagrian fought as part of the [[Color|Amethyst]] team and won his first duel, qualifying for the other fights. In the second stage, he dueled Yord Fandar. During the fight, Fandar got the Chagrian turned around and swiped him with the tip of his [[silspar]]'s blade near the neck. This startled the Chagrian, who stumbled back and was kicked off the stage by his opponent.<ref name="TCC" />
