The '''dead lands''' was the centerpoint of the [[nuclear]] accident near [[Calliope Station]] that caused the [[devastation of Parnassos]]. It was a fenced-off area on the [[planet]] [[Parnassos]], where faded signs and the [[Gand]] [[Churkk]] warned visitors of the dangers of entering. Little to no life could survive in the dead lands due to lingering [[radiation]] from the nuclear catastrophe. The lands were visible from [[Arratu Station]].<ref name="Phasma" />

When [[Brendol Hux]]'s [[Palpatine's yacht|ship]] was shot down over the planet by [[Parnassos' automated defense system]], the [[Chromium|chrome]]-plated [[starship]] crashed in the dead lands, while Hux and three [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] ejected in a [[escape pod]] and landed closer to [[Claw territory]].<ref name="Phasma" /> Hux, two of his remaining troopers, [[Phasma]], and several of her [[Scyre]] warriors traveled to the dead lands despite the warnings of a local named [[Churkk]] with the hope of reaching the crashed ship, contacting the First Order, and escaping the planet.
