'''Crullov''' was a location on the [[planet]] [[Corulag]],<ref name="Victory's Price" /> located in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Corulag system]]</ref> Eleven [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Jakku]], where the [[New Republic]] won a decisive final battle against the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> the former [[New Republic pilot]] [[Chass na Chadic]] resided in the city [[Curamelle]] on Corulag. [[Gender|She]] eventually moved to Crullov, where the rent was cheaper, as her New Republic pension was nearing its expiration. Chadic soon found out she would not be able to find a full [[Standard day|night's]] rest in her new accommodations, which offered an apartment the size of a closet in a building full of [[death stick]] addicts.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Crullov was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the final volume of [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Victory's Price" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Crullov City]] was first mentioned in ''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]'', a sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] in [[2003]].<ref name="Leg">''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]''</ref>
