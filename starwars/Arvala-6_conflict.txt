A '''conflict''' took place on [[Arvala-6]] during the [[Imperial Era]], disrupting [[mining]] operations in lands occupied by the [[Galactic Empire]] and causing storage issues amongst displaced people.<ref name="Aldhani" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="5 BBY" /> however, [[Supervisor]] [[Lagret]] informed [[Major]] [[Lio Partagaz]] in a meeting with the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] that the conflict's dimensions had diminished, leading to mining operations resuming. Partagaz admonished Lagret for not having completed a memorandum of recommendations to deal with the storage issue of refugees from the conflict, to which Lagret informed him he was awaiting an assessment from the ministerial director.<ref name="Aldhani" />
