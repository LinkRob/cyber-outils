'''Cularin''' was an astronomical object situated in<ref name="TFABG" /> the [[Thaere sector]]'s<ref name="Appendix" /> [[Cularin system]]<ref name="The Glass Abyss" /> within the regions known as the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="TGEG" /> It was located in grid square P-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="TFABG" /> Cularin was related to a [[Battle of Cularin|battle]] fought<ref name="Lead by Example">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> at some point between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Lead by Example]]'' indicates that the [[Battle of Cularin]] occurred during the [[Clone Wars]], which lasted from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]], according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''.</ref> in which the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] were involved.<ref name="Lead by Example" /> It also shared its name with the [[Cularin Swamp Run]] [[podracing]] course, which had been set up<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> by [[10 BBY]].<ref name="BVDate">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Cularin was introduced in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 52|fifty-second issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine series,<ref name="BMF 52">{{BuildFalconCite|52|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> in an article written by [[Mark Newbold]],<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> published by [[De Agostini]] around [[December 30]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF 52 date">{{BMFdates|52}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Cularin/Legends|Cularin]] first appeared in the roleplaying adventure ''[[Eye of the Sun: A Cularin Presence|A Cularin Presence]]'', the first installment of the [[Eye of the Sun trilogy|''Eye of the Sun'' trilogy]] within the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] roleplaying campaign, written by [[Morrie Mullins]] and published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] in [[2001]].<ref name="A Cularin Presence">{{LivingForce|Eye of the Sun: A Cularin Presence}}</ref>
