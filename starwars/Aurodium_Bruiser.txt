The '''Aurodium Bruiser''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Zaina]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Rebel Veteran|standard costume]]. This dark outfit featured [[Color|golden]] embellishments.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aurodium Bruiser was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Zaina, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 2.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[July 30]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Aurodium Blaster]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching the rank Kyber I within ''Hunter's'' "Ranked" mode during the game's second season.<ref name="Hunters" />
