==Behind the scenes==
FN-2112 first appeared in the video game, ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''. The concept of a stormtrooper with only swimwear and a helmet previously appeared in ''[[LEGO Star Wars II: The Original Trilogy]]'' and ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]'' in the form of [[Beach trooper]]s, along with the Workout [[Clone trooper|Clone Troopers]] from ''[[LEGO Star Wars III: The Clone Wars]]''.
