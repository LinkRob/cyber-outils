During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a '''battle''' took place above [[Cianap]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the battle, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was forced to crash-land on Cianap after his [[TIE Advanced x1]] was damaged. Imperial forces eventually prevailed, and moved to extract Vader from the surface.<ref name="Dark Visions 1" />
