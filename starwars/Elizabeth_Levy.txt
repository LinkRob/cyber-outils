'''Elizabeth Levy''' is a prolific [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Wikipedia:Children's literature|children's author]]. She originally adapted the [[1983]] ''[[Return of the Jedi Step-Up Movie Adventure]]'' book from the [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Return of the Jedi'']] film. The book has since been republished twice.
