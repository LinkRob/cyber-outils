"'''Bogey'''" was the nickname of a [[Kwazel Maw]] that the [[Gungan]] [[Junior Representative|Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] encountered and befriended during a [[mission to Rodia]] in the [[Clone Wars]]. Bogey later played a key role in capturing the [[Trade Federation]] [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]].

==History==
[[File:Good_boy_Boogie.png|thumb|left|250px|Binks and "Bogey" together]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], "Bogey" lived on [[Rodia]] and encountered the [[Galactic Republic]] diplomat [[Jar Jar Binks]] after he escaped from [[battle droid]]s by jumping into the swamp. Bogey attacked and Binks barely managed to escape the beast. Later, however, Binks found himself underwater once more, this time evading a [[missile]] that had been fired by a [[B2-HA super battle droid]]. In the process, Binks came face-to-face again with Bogey, and was swallowed whole by the creature&mdash;only for it to shrug off the missile and then release the [[Gungan]]. With the beast seemingly friendly towards him, Binks affectionately stroked its hide and nicknamed it.<ref name="Bombad Jedi" />

Later, as [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[C-3PO]] were set to be executed by [[Nute Gunray]], Binks distracted the [[droideka]]s and was then assisted by Bogey, who launched out from underwater and destroyed a number of battle droids, with Binks on its back. As Gunray looked to flee amid the confusion, Bogey toppled his [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|shuttle]] off the edge of the [[hangar]], cutting off his escape route. Gunray was then arrested by Amidala and Farr, while Binks was commended for his bravery as Bogey watched on.<ref name="Bombad Jedi" />
