'''Coronet Arms''' was a weapons manufacturer that manufactured a model of [[dueling pistol]]s which was typical of such [[blaster]]s and often ordered in pairs. Coronet Arms [[engineer]]s modelled the [[Mark V "Sand Panther" hunting rifle]], a popular retirement gift among [[Corellian]]s, on an ancient [[rifle]] once used to hunt [[Corellian sand panther]]s. The company shared its name with<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> [[Coronet City]], a [[city]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="Solo TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
