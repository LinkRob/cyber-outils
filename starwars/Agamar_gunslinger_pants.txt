'''Agamar gunslinger pants''' were a type of [[Clothing|pants]] manufactured<ref name="Outlaws" /> on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Agamar]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> They were designed to be simple as to not limit a being's mobility during a [[blaster]] fight. The pants were [[Color|tan]] in color and included a pair of black [[boot]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Twi'lek]] [[merchant]] [[Sato Bizaz]] had at least one pair of Agamar gunslinger pants for sale while operating their [[Bizaz|stall]] in the [[Wonto District]] of [[Mos Eisley]] spaceport on the [[desert]] planet [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] bought a pair of Agamar gunslinger pants from Bizaz for [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Agamar gunslinger pants appeared in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', a [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When equipped by the player, the pants increased the player's accuracy when shooting from the hip or while on the run.<ref name="Outlaws" />
