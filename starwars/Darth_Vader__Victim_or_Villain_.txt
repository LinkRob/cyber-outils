"'''Darth Vader: Victim or Villain?'''" is an [[Star Wars Insider|''Insider'']] article about the character of [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]]. Several ''[[Star Wars]]'' authors and artists, such as [[Timothy Zahn]], [[Aaron Allston]], [[John Jackson Miller]], [[Christie Golden]], [[Michael Reaves]], [[Maya Kaathryn Bohnhoff]], [[Douglas Wheatley]], [[Kevin Hearne]], [[Ryder Windham]], [[Joe Schreiber]], and [[Jason Fry]], were asked if they considered the [[Dark Lord of the Sith/Legends|Dark Lord of the Sith]] as a true villain or a tragic victim, who they thought was truly responsible for Skywalker's fall to the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side]], and if they thought he could really be redeemed. This article was published in [[Star Wars Insider 145|''Star Wars Insider'' 145]] and later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2015|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2015]].
