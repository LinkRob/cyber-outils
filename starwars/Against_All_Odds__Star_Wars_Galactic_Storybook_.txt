"'''Against All Odds'''" is a short story written by [[Calliope Glass]] with illustrations by [[Katie Cook]] that was published in the young readers book ''[[Star Wars Galactic Storybook]]'' released [[January 6]], [[2021]]. It adapts [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].

==Plot summary==
{{Expandsect}}
After [[Han Solo]] was frozen in [[Carbonite]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] led a team to [[Rescue of Han Solo|rescue]] him from [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]]. After defeating Jabba and comlpleting the rescue, the Rebels joined the misison to [[Battle of Endor|destroy]] the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. The team joined with the [[Ewok]]s in knocking out the shield generator. Meanwhile, [[Luke Skywalker]] confronts [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and [[Darth Sidious]]. Skywalker succeeds in redeeming Vader, and the Rebels were victourious, bringing freedom back to the galaxy.
