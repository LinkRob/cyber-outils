'''Brorn''' was an individual who lived above the tram stop in [[Tinker-Town]] on [[Mrinzebon]], along with [[Shonessa]]. Both Brorn and Shonessa were trained by [[Major]] [[Soran Keize]] of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] to fight back against the local criminal gangs when he came to the world under the alias "Devon".<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
