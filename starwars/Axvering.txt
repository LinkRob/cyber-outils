'''Axvering''' was an individual who served as a [[Chief Investigator]] in the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. At one point, [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] wrote a response to an inquiry to Axvering in regards to a missing [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|UT-60D]] shipment.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
