'''Danzik''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="AlienArchive" /> located in the [[Danzik system]], a part of the [[Dalonbian sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Timelines" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It was situated in grid square M-3 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], and the [[hyperspace route]]<ref name="Timelines" /> known as the [[Veragi Trade Route]]<ref name="VeragiTradeRoute" /> connected it to the [[Birgis system|Birgis]] and [[Sernpidal system]]s.<ref name="Timelines" /> Danzik had [[swamp]]s on its surface.<ref name="AlienArchive" />

Danzik was the [[homeworld]] of the two-headed [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Danzikan]] [[species]],<ref name="AlienArchive" /> who spoke the [[Danzikan (language)|Danzikan]] [[language]].<ref name="YouTube" /> It was eventually mentioned in an entry of an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]] dedicated to the Danzikans. The journal was eventually restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="AlienArchive" /> at some point no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Danzik was introduced in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]'' by [[Natalie Clubb]] and [[Katrina Pallant]].<ref name="AlienArchive" />
