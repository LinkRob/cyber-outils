'''Abyssin Outpost''' was an outpost operated by [[Abyssin]] [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] that was located on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Serenno]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> by [[42 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> close to the [[Carannian hills]] and the capital city [[Carannia]]

==History==
In 42 BBY, during the invasion of Serenno, in an attempt to hit the comm tower located in the outpost, Jedi Master Sifo- Dyas used the Force to generate a dust storm as a distraction. While Oster and another Abyssin Guard were distracted by the storm, Jedi Master Dooku killed both guards. Dyas later commandeered a tank that was in the possession of the Abyssin Mercenaries.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
