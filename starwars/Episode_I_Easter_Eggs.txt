"'''Episode I Easter Eggs'''" is an article in [[Star Wars Insider 48|''Star Wars Insider'' 48]] written by [[Mark Cotta Vaz]]. It features various [[Easter egg (virtual)|easter eggs]] found in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] and debunks other easter eggs that some fans claimed they had seen. On the last page is a small article by ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' editor [[Jon Bradley Snyder]], "[[I Was an Episode I Easter Egg!]]," that tells of an Episode I easter egg of his own.
