'''Chris Runco''' was a [[The Walt Disney Company|Walt Disney]] imagineer who worked on the original [[Star Tours/Legends|''Star Tours'']] ride at [[Disneyland]]. Runco designed [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[RX-24/Legends|Rex]], the [[Pilot droid/Legends|pilot droid]] and main character of the attraction. Based on Runco's foam sculpt for the character, fellow imagineers [[Larry Sheldon]] and [[Lance Updyke]] created Rex animatronic puppets from aluminum.<ref name="SWI104 58">{{InsiderCite|104|Star Tours: Taking Off on a Star Tour}}, p. 58</ref>
