'''Fragging''', sometimes used in the form '''frag''', was a [[List of phrases and slang#F|slang term]] that was often used to express rage or disappointment, or simply as a general amplifier, and was seen by some as vulgar. [[Temmin Wexley]] frequently used the phrase, though his [[Norra Wexley|mother]] ridiculed him for it, telling him to watch his language. [[Jom Barell]] also used the word.<ref name="Aftermath" />
