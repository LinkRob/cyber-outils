The '''Erub system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square N-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Erub II]].<ref name="Origin" />
