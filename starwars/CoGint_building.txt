The '''CoGint building''' was a building on<ref name="TCOC" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], located in the same zone as the planet's [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. The [[Tholothian]] [[Alees June]] worked at the CoGint building, where she arranged transfers for officers in a company.<ref name="TCOC" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[The Call of Coruscant]]" takes place concurrently with the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]].</ref> June met [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Amadeo Azzazzo]] while he was exploring the area around the [[Jedi temple|temple]]. She told him about her work at the CoGint building and described it as a "pointed monstrosity."<ref name="TCOC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The CoGint building was mentioned in the short story "[[The Call of Coruscant]]," written by [[Lydia Kang]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="TCOC" />
