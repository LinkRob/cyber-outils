'''Dylan Firshein''' worked as an editor on the television shows ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]''<ref name="Tribes" /> and ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''. Firshein also portrayed a [[Unidentified taxi droid|taxi droid]] in ''The Mandalorian'' episode "[[Chapter 19: The Convert]],"<ref name="Convert" /> which aired on [[March 15]], [[2023]].<ref name="D23 March">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-march-2023|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in March 2023}}</ref>
