The '''Alpha Trawler''' was a [[human]] who, along with a [[Beta Trawler|human]] companion and a [[Quarren Trawler|Quarren]] companion, threatened a [[The Fledgling Mythrol|Mythrol]] in a [[Boatman's Reach|public house]] on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Pagodon]] around [[9 ABY]]. In [[Huttese]], he threatened to sell the [[Mythrol]]'s glands at the port, and commented that the Mythrol's musk must be sweet because of his youth. During this incident, a [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] named [[Din Djarin]] arrived to collect a [[bounty]] on the Mythrol. The human, along with his two companions, threatened Djarin, claiming that he had made the alpha trawler spill his drink. A fight ensued, during which Djarin incapacitated all three of them.

==Biography==
===Harassing a Mythrol===
{{Quote|Look at his glands! I bet we could sell them at the port.|translated=1|The lead trawler points out the Mythrol's gills to the Quarren|<ref name="Chapter 1" />}}
[[File:Trawler_Trio.png|thumb|left|250px|The human harassed a Mythrol with two other trawlers.]]
The [[human]] [[Gender|male]] worked as a trawler on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Pagodon]]<ref name="Chapter 1" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|1}}</ref> Within a [[Boatman's Reach|public house]] at the settlement [[Ferryman's Reach]], he harassed a [[The Fledgling Mythrol|young Mythrol]] alongside a [[Quarren Trawler|Quarren]] and [[Beta Trawler|human]].<ref name="Chapter 1" /> Speaking in [[Huttese]],<ref name="Insider 223">{{InsiderCite|223|Embodying Vizsla}}</ref> the trawler pointed out that they could sell the [[Mythrol]]'s glands a a nearby [[port]], and that the musk he produced would be sweet. Despite the Mythrol surrendering his [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the table, the human prepared to him with a [[knife]] while the [[Quarren]] pulled him to his feet.<ref name="Chapter 1" />

Before they could cut the Mythrol, they were interrupted by the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]]'s sudden entrance. Noticing the spilled drink on the table, he claimed the Mandalorian had spilled it while approaching him at the counter, signaling his companions.<ref name="Chapter 1" />

===Taking on a Mandalorian===
{{Quote|Is that real Beskar Steel?|The trawler, in Huttese, to Djarin|translated=1|audio=Trawler-Beskar.flac|<ref name="Chapter 1" />}}
[[File:Alpha_Trawler_The_Mandalorian.png|thumb|250px|The human was bested by Din Djarin.]]
Continuing to threaten Djarin, the [[Unidentified bartender (Pagodon)|bartender]] translated his threats in [[Galactic Basic Standard|Basic]]. The human then asked if the [[armor]] he was made of real [[beskar]], as the other trawlers surrounded him. As the bartender slid another drink to the human, Djarin grabbed the cup and knocked them back, then slammed it into the other human trawler, knocking him out.<ref name="Chapter 1" />

He proceeded to slam the Quarren's face into the counter, before the human trawler attempted to stab him with his knife. Djarin then grabbed his arm and redirected the blade into his back, incapacitating him. Djarin then caught the fleeing Quarren with his [[whipcord launcher]] and shot the entrance's controls, which promptly activated the door and sliced the trawler in half.<ref name="Chapter 1" />

==Personality and traits==
The human had light skin with a scar on the right side of his face, as well as a bald head and a [[Color|dark brown]] beard,<ref name="Chapter 1" /> and spoke Huttese.<ref name="Insider 223" /> He ignored the Mythrol's offer of credits, as he found the Mythrol's glands to be far more profitable. Upon seeing Djarin, he used the spilled drink on the table as leverage to threaten him.<ref name="Chapter 1" />

==Skills and abilities==
When fighting Djarin, the human attempted to stab him with his knife, but was incapacitated when Djarin redirected the blade to his back instead.<ref name="Chapter 1" />

==Equipment==
The human carried a knife and wore a brown coat, brown pants and black [[glove]]s.<ref name="Chapter 1" />
