{{Quote|The Architects of Vaale were said to be a secret society of ''master artisans''.|[[Detta Yao]]|<ref name="Aphra 2" />}}
The '''Architects of Vaale''' were a secret society of [[artisan]]s that existed long before the [[time]] of the [[Imperial Era]], was long forgotten. They created the [[city]] of [[Vaale]] on the [[planet]] [[Dianth]] out of material that texts called "living stone," as well as several "wonders"<ref name="Aphra 2" /> that included the artifact known as the [[Rings of Vaale]].<ref name="Aphra 1">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 1|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 1]]</ref>

The rings became a target for several individuals during the Imperial Era, including the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] and the [[House of Tagge]] scion [[Ronen Tagge]].<ref name="Aphra 1" /> The group had a workshop where they created the artifacts that would later be discovered by Aphra's crew, who ventured into an underground area that featured colorful iconography that Aphra believed dated back to the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] and Doctor [[Eustacia Okka]] interpreted as warnings about the power of the rings.<ref name="Aphra 2" />
