'''Aggadeen''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who served as an [[Imperial officer|officer]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], he acted as the Imperial [[Overseer]] of [[Weapons Factory Alpha]] on [[Cymoon 1]].

==Biography==
===Rebel assault===
{{Quote|We have an entire Empire to keep armed and ready. We haven't the time or inclination to bargain with every two-bit Hutt or Moff who gets in our way.''"<br />"''And yet&hellip;here we are.|Aggadeen and Han Solo|<ref name="Skywalker Strikes" />}}
A few [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Overseer]] Aggadeen was stationed at [[Weapons Factory Alpha]] on the [[moon]] [[Cymoon 1]], in the [[Corellian Industrial Cluster]].<ref name="Skywalker Strikes" /> While Aggadeen was in charge of the weapons factories on the moon, he had particular responsibly for Factory Alpha.<ref name="SWHC1Ask" /> An envoy was sent from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The envoy was employed by the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]], and was to negotiate terms of shipping raw materials from Hutt controlled [[Realspace|space]] to [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Factory|factories]], to produce more Imperial war machines&mdash;such as [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s and [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] [[walker]]s.<ref name="Skywalker Strikes" />

[[File:AggadeenHanHandshake-2015StarWars1.png|thumb|left|300px|Aggadeen and Solo shake hands upon meeting.]]
Aggadeen and a [[squad]] of [[stormtrooper]]s awaited the [[shuttle]] that carried the Outer Rim envoy on one of the [[Weapons factory|factory's]] landing platforms. The Overseer ordered the stormtroopers to [[Death|kill]] the members of the envoy if they felt in any way suspicious. Three [[human]]s and an [[astromech droid]] emerged, and Aggadeen greeted them in the name of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and asked who he had the honor of addressing. The clear leader of the group called himself the official [[emissary]] of Jabba the Great, Mightiest of all Hutts, but his name was [[Han Solo]]. The pair shook hands as Solo dubbed the Overseer "Aggie." The Imperials' [[Unidentified LOM-series protocol droid (Cymoon-1)|LOM-series protocol droid]] confirmed Solo's identity, and Aggadeen invited them into the factory&mdash;after they had left their weapons outside. The two parties walked through the factory where Solo and "Aggie" conversed about the imminent negotiations. Solo told him that they should be prepared for the negotiations to be vigorous, but Aggadeen revealed to the small [[time]] [[smuggler]] that there would be no negotiations. The negotiator would state the Empire's demands and they would be accepted by the Hutt's envoy&mdash;the Empire was huge, they had no time to negotiate with criminals or anyone who could possibly slow down the Empire. Solo, unimpressed, reminded him that negotiating with criminals from the Outer Rim was not the Empire's style. The [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]] undoubtedly cost billions of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to build&mdash;many [[Standard year|years]] to construct, had been destroyed, running the Empire's resources dry.<ref name="Skywalker Strikes" />

[[File:AggieBeingPunched-2015StarWars1.png|thumb|right|300px|Leia "thanks" Aggadeen with a punch to his jaw.]]
They soon arrived at the room where the negotiations were to be held, Aggadeen invited them inside to await the negotiator. But Solo professed to hold the negotiations on the noisy factory floor. Aggadeen questioned Solo's sanity and asked why. Solo smoothly replied that they were not there to negotiate. Solo's astromech [[droid]], [[R2-D2]] began leaking fluids onto the floor&mdash;the droid then electrocuted the liquid; and the stormtroopers who now found themselves standing in it. Solo and his "[[bodyguard]]s" defeated the remaining stormtroopers in [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]]. Aggadeen had decided that Solo truly was insane, and when he was asked for directions to the main [[Reactor|power core]], Aggadeen refused to tell them, saying that Weapons Factory Alpha was the most heavily guarded base in [[the galaxy]]. Solo ignored him and repeated his question; the proud [[Imperial officer]] refused to cooperate&mdash;until the astromech droid threatened to electrocute him as well. He pointed them in the right direction and was thanked by the [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|female bodyguard]] with right hook across his jaw.<ref name="Skywalker Strikes" />

The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]] moved on and rigged the power core to explode as the negotiator, Darth Vader, arrived and was alerted to the Rebel's presence when he was [[Assault on Cymoon|attacked]] by a [[sniper]]. Vader put the entire moon on alert. The rebels freed several [[Slavery|slaves]] and commandeered an AT-AT walker. They used the walker to wreak havoc in their escape of the factory that was now a bomb. Aggadeen and other Imperials attempted to halt the power core's meltdown&mdash;but the Rebels had done their work well&mdash;the meltdown could not be stopped. Aggadeen contacted [[Lord]] Vader and requested permission to evacuate the facility. Vader vetoed his request and told him "If this factory explodes, you had best explode with it." Aggadeen obeyed and was again cautioned by Vader that further failure would not be tolerated and that the Rebel invaders had to die. Vader did not want [[Luke Skywalker]] dead, however, as the [[Sith Lord]] had other plans for him.<ref name="SW2">[[Star Wars (2015) 2|''Star Wars'' (2015) 2]]</ref>

===The price of failure===
{{Quote|&hellip;Overseer Aggadeen will not fail us again.|Darth Vader, to Darth Sidious|<ref name="Darth Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2015) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 1]]</ref>}}
[[File:AggadeenTortured-2015DarthVader1.png|thumb|left|180px|Aggadeen being tortured by Vader.]]
After the assault ended, Vader returned to the [[Imperial Palace]] on [[Coruscant]], the [[capital]] of the Empire.<ref name="Darth Vader 1" /> The [[Sith Lord]] held Aggadeen responsible for the Rebel's infiltration and destruction of the factory.<ref name="SWHC1Ask" /> He brought Aggadeen to the Palace with him, where the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] had the Overseer [[torture]]d as a consequence for the assault on Cymoon 1. Vader wanted to make sure Aggadeen was not withholding any more information on the Rebel attack that he could possibly use to find Skywalker. Vader brought Emperor Palpatine before the Overseer, and Vader told his [[Sith Master|master]] that Aggadeen would never fail the Empire again.<ref name="Darth Vader 1" /> Ultimately, the Overseer was tortured to death.<ref name="SWHC1Ask" />
