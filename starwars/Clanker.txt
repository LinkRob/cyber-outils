[[File:Strength_in_Numbers_SWDL.png|thumb|right|300px|Battle droids, most notably the B1-series, became infamously known as "clankers" thanks to the "clanking" noise made by their metal joints (marching B1s, pictured).]]
'''Clanker''' was a slang term used to refer to [[droid]]s<ref name="Ambush" /> that existed since at least the days of the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="Tempest Breaker">[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker (script)|''The High Republic: Tempest Breaker'' script]]</ref> because the joints and metal bodies of droids<ref name="Voyage">{{TCW|Voyage of Temptation}}</ref> made noisy<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> "clanking" sounds as they moved.<ref name="Voyage" /> It was commonly associated with and often used by the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Republic Military]], who faced off against the [[battle droid]]s of the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="The Last Battle" /> During the war, "clanker" was often used as a term for battle droids,<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> particularly the [[B1-series battle droid]].<ref name="Aftermath novel">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] was nicknamed the "head clanker" by clones.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence">{{TCW|Shadow of Malevolence}}</ref> Nonetheless, the term was used to denote other [[droid]]s as well.<ref name="The Forgotten Droid">{{Rebels|The Forgotten Droid}}</ref><ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''{{Rebels|Steps Into Shadow}}''</ref> For example, [[Kthoo-Ra]] addressed a damaged [[service droid]] as "dumb clanker."<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 12|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 12]]</ref>

The motifs featured on the monitor of [[Lucky Shot]], a [[slot machine]] found in [[Cid's Parlor]], included an array of B1 battle droid heads. Fittingly enough, "clanker bonus" was written in [[Aurebesh]] above it.<ref name="Decommissioned">{{TBB|Decommissioned}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Oh, the Wookiees aren't going to like this! Just what Kashyyyk needs, a bunch of noisy clankers moving into the neighborhood.|A sarcastic Clone Commander [[Tacks]], in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
The slang term clanker was first used in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] continuity in [[2008]] in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]].<ref name="The Clone Wars film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> The term originated in the [[2005]] [[LucasArts]] [[video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]'' from the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="RC 2005">''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]''</ref> In [[2025]], "clanker" has become an informal term referring to [[artificial intelligence]] (AI) in the real world, functioning as [[Wikipedia:Internet slang|Internet slang]] that originated from online ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan communities and achieving wider significance as a [[Wikipedia:Meme|meme]]&mdash;sometimes as counter-cultural slang, sometimes as a slur.<ref name="RollingStone">{{WebCite|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/clanker-cogsucker-robot-ai-slurs-viral-1235401262|text=AI Slurs Are Taking Over the Internet|author=CT Jones|date=2025-08-06|work=[[Wikipedia:Rolling Stone|''Rolling Stone'']]|archivedate=20250806221734}}</ref>
