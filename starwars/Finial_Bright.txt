'''Finial Bright''' was a [[Nautolan]] who served as a [[captain]] in the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He commanded the [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] ''[[Aurora IX]]''. Bright sacrificed his life preventing [[Solar Array 22-X]] from exploding, buying enough time for [[Petty Officer]] [[Innamin]] and [[Ensign]] [[Peeples]] to escort the surviving crew members onto the ''Aurora IX''.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
