'''Crodit''' was a [[Dwuni]] [[Gender|male]] who was [[Slavery|enslaved]] with his brother [[Blawz]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and served as a [[Mining|miner]] in the [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]]. Due to the brothers' small size they were able to fit into the narrowest parts of the mine where other slaves could not go. The pair oddly seemed to enjoy their work.<ref name="SoloOG" />

In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on [[Kessel]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to nine years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]], therefore the events on Kessel in ''Solo'' must take place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> the brothers were using [[portable drill unit]]s<ref name="SoloOG" /> in the mine when a [[Raid on Kessel|slave revolt]] broke out and overthrew the syndicate's enforcers, allowing many of the miners to flee.

Crodit had [[Color|orange]] skin and black [[eye]]s.<ref name="Solo" /> He wore a set of brown [[Clothing|robes]] stained orange with [[spice]] as well as metal-capped [[boot]]s and a compressor pack.<ref name="SoloOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crodit first appeared in the [[2018]] ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Solo" /> and was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" />
