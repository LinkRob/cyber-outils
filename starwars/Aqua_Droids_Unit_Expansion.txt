The '''Aqua Droids Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ136''') is an expansion pack for the miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' published on [[January 17]], [[2025]] as part of [[Atomic Mass Games]]' overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
===Atomic Mass Games===
''The AQ-series battle droid, or “Aqua Droid” is capable of fighting both on land and underwater. They are highly specialized, furnished with a retractable laser cannon and reliably well armored. When used in large groups Aqua Droids are nearly impossible to stop, persistently advancing towards their target until it is eradicated. Aqua Droids are often seen supporting surprise attacks, wading en masse out of the ocean to assault a critical point, or delivered to the frontline by a Trident-class Assault Ship where the foe is weakest.''

''This expansion for Star Wars™: Legion contains 4 finely sculpted Aqua Droid miniatures, enough for one Heavy unit, along with new unit and upgrade cards ensuring their timely delivery to an aquatic location.''

===Asmodee===
''The Aqua Droid Unit Expansion brings the AQ-series battle droids to the battlefield of Star Wars™: Legion. This expansion contains 4 formidable aqua droid miniatures to form a Heavy support option. This Unit Expansion is part of the Separatist army''
*''Brings aqua droids to Star Wars: Legion, including 4 miniatures for the Separatist faction''
*''Adds new Heavy support option to Separatist faction''
*''1 Aqua Droid Leader, 2 Aqua Droid Troopers, 1 Aqua Droid Heavy''
*''Multi-Language now available for English, French, German, Spanish''

==Contents==
*4 Miniatures
**3 [[AQ-series battle droid|Aqua Droid]]s
**1 [[Heavy Aqua Droid]]

*5 Upgrade Cards
