An '''air taxi''' was a [[Repulsorlift/Legends|repulsorlift]]-powered [[Airspeeder/Legends|airspeeder]] used as a passenger transport on worlds such as [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] and [[Malastare/Legends|Malastare]]. [[Landspeeder/Legends|Landspeeders]] that served as taxis were known as [[taxi speeder]]s or repulsorcabs.

One of the most common types of air taxi was the [[EasyRide passenger airspeeder/Legends|EasyRide passenger airspeeder]], also known as the Coruscant Taxi due to its frequent use on the galactic capital during the time of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]]. Another type of air taxi was the [[Void Spider TX-3 Air Taxi]].
