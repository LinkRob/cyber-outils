'''Axaleem'''<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] in [[the galaxy]]. [[Papa]] [[Toren]] was a member of the species.<ref name="Lando 1" />

{{Species-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Axaleem, albeit unidentified, first appeared in the [[Lando 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Lando (comic series)|''Star Wars: Lando'']], which was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Alex Maleev]],<ref name="Lando 1" /> and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[July 8]], [[2015]].<ref name="Lando 1 Release">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/56328/lando_2015_1|text=Lando (2015) #1}}</ref> The species later got identified in the [[2024]] [[Dorling Kindersley]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'', which was authored by [[Dan Brooks]], [[Megan Crouse]], [[Amy Richau]], [[Amy Ratcliffe]], [[Brandon Wainerdi]], [[Dan Zehr]], and [[Kelly Knox]].<ref name="SWE" />
