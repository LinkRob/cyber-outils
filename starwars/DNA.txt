'''DNA''' was the substance that carried all the information about a life form's appearance, shape, form, and function. For instance, DNA in [[Togruta]] determined such things as what [[color]] the [[lekku]] were.<ref name="Epic Battles">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Visual Guide: Epic Battles]]''</ref> [[Cloning]], the process of making an identical copy of a [[Clone template|given organism]], required manipulation of said organism's DNA.<ref name="Clone Cadets">{{TCW|Clone Cadets}}</ref>

DNA could be used to establish parentage by testing a sample of a suspected [[parent]]'s DNA in comparison with the individual believed to be the [[Youngling|child]]. In considering whether to confirm or deny before the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] of the [[New Republic]] that [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was her father, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] noted to herself that to her knowledge no DNA samples had ever been found labeled either "[[Darth]] Vader" or "Anakin Skywalker."<ref name="bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>
