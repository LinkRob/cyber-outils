'''Alderaan's neo-classical period''' was a cultural period on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. The period produced an architectural style whose typical features included columns and [[flower]]y scrollworks. [[Eastwind Manor]], the [[summer]] residence of the [[noble]] [[House Prestor]], was built in that style.<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>
