The '''Cyclo-Grav''' was a model of [[battle droid]] with [[Color|gray]] plating and a black [[photoreceptor]]. It consisted of the head of a<ref name="Fortnite" /> [[Serv-O-Droid, Inc.]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[DUM-series pit droid]] with the four [[limb]]s and [[repulsorlift]] of an [[ID10 seeker droid]], making it a [[monster droid]]. A group of [[Jawa]]s sold blueprints for Cyclo-Gravs out of a [[Sandcrawler Shop]] that was located near the [[Junkers Cantina]] on<ref name="Fortnite" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Tatooine]]. The default Cyclo-Grav design cost 30,000,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], a golden design cost 120,000,000 credits, a [[diamond]]-colored version cost 240,000,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design cost 480,000,000 credits.<ref name="Fortnite" />
