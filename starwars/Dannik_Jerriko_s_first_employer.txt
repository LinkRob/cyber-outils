The '''first employer''' of [[Dannik Jerriko]] was displeased with the [[Anzat (species)|Anzat]] [[Assassin/Legends|assassin's]] expensive fee. The individual attempted to renegotiate the price of hiring Jerriko, but was [[Death/Legends|killed]] while meeting with the assassin. Despite having knowledge of the incident, [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba]] the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] hired Jerriko for a variety of jobs over a fifty [[Standard year/Legends|year]] span.<ref name="TheAnzati">{{GamerCite|1|The Anzati}}</ref>
