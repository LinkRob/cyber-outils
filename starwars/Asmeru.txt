'''Asmeru''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Trkn" /> located in the [[Asmeru system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Senex sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> part of the [[Senex-Juvex]] region. On the 234th [[Standard day|day]] of the world's sixteenth [[Standard month|month]], the natives at one point celebrated a festival known as [[Q'anah (festival)|Q'anah]] in their [[Unidentified Asmeru language|language]]. The noble [[House Elegin]] were based on the planet, and during the reign of the [[Galactic Republic]] they hired a [[Q'anah's father|man]] from the planet [[Brentaal IV]] to oversee their security. The man's daughter fell in love with a young [[Q'anah's lover|Elegin noble]] who secretly lived as a [[pirate]], and when the daughter too took up piracy she began using the name Q'anah in honor of the Asmeru festival.<ref name="Trkn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Asmeru first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel ''[[Cloak of Deception]]'', which was written by [[James Luceno]] and published in [[2001]].<ref name="cod">''[[Cloak of Deception]]''</ref> The planet was confirmed to be [[canon]] by the novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], which was released in [[2014]] and was also written by Luceno.<ref name="Trkn" />
