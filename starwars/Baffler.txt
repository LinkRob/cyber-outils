'''Bafflers''', nicknamed the "'''Resistance-Cloak''',"<ref name="Bomber Command">''[[The Last Jedi: Bomber Command]]''</ref> were devices that were used to make a [[starship]]'s [[Ion trail|ion exhaust]] hard for another ship's [[sensor]]s to detect. Combining [[engine]] [[Baffle (component)|baffles]], [[emergency shunt]]s, and [[fuel tank]]s, they were invented by [[Rose Tico]] under the patronage of [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War]]. Although bafflers looked "weird," in the words of their inventor, they managed to keep the [[Resistance]]'s [[bomber]]s undetected during [[spy]] missions to the [[Atterra system]]. At the behest of [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]], Tico later stayed aboard the ''[[Ninka]]'' and helped the resident [[List of phrases and slang#B|boffins]] adapt her bafflers for use on other ships.<ref name="RT:RF" />
