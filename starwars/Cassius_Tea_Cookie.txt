{{Quote|This tea biscuit made with the cassius flower's tiny black seeds. I s'pose you could enjoy a steaming mug of cassius tea with out one, but if you're hopin' to experience the true taste of Mandalore, this is the way.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Cassius Tea Cookies''' were a type of [[cookie]] made by [[Mandalorian]]s. It was made with the tiny [[Color|black]] [[seed]]s found in the [[flower]] of the [[cassius tree]]. It could be served with [[Cassius Tea]] which was made brewed using the same flower. After making a culinary tour of the [[galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for the [[Foodstuff|dish]] in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cassius Tea Cookies were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish makes use of [[Wikipedia:Flour|all-purpose flour]], [[Wikipedia:Sesame|black sesame seeds]], [[Wikipedia:Baking soda|baking soda]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], [[Wikipedia:Butter|unsalted butter]], [[Wikipedia:White sugar|granulated sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Brown sugar|brown sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Egg|eggs]], and [[Wikipedia:Vanilla|extract]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
