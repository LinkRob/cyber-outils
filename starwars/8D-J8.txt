'''8D-J8''' was an [[8D-series smelter droid]] manufactured by [[Roche Hive Mechanical Apparatus Design and Construction Activity for Those Who Need the Hive's Machines|Roche Hive]]. At one [[time]], the [[Smelter droid|droid]] was aboard the [[Khetanna|sail barge]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. He was later [[Currency|purchased]] by the butcher [[Bakkar]] and brought to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]], where he was [[Programmer|reprogrammed]] by the [[droid builder]] [[Mubo]] to serve as the [[Pitmaster droid|pitmaster]] turning the spit at Bakkar's [[Ronto Roasters]] [[restaurant]].

In [[34 ABY]], 8D-J8 offered [[Kendoh Gang]] member [[Remex Io]] a [[Ronto Wrap]], which the gangmember declined. He was also turning the spit and mumbling to himself when the [[Batuuan|local]] [[Salju]] brought the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] to the restaurant for [[Foodstuff|food]]. During the [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of Batuu by the [[First Order]], the [[droid]]'s owner complained to Mubo about his programming, and the droid builder reluctantly agreed to reprogram him again.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Who brought this smelter droid? Shouldn't he be with the Jawas?|Ree-Yees and Saelt-Marae have a conversation about 8D-J8|<ref name="Datapad: Profile" />}}
[[File:MerchantRow-GalaxysEdge1.png|thumb|left|300px|8D-J8 offers Remex Io a Ronto Wrap.]]
8D-J8 was an [[8D-series smelter droid]] manufactured by [[Roche Hive Mechanical Apparatus Design and Construction Activity for Those Who Need the Hive's Machines|Roche Hive]],<ref name="Datapad: Profile" /> a [[Verpine]]-controlled [[droid]] manufacturer.<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> At some point, the [[smelter droid]] was on the ''[[Khetanna]]'',<ref name="Datapad: Profile" /> a [[sail barge]] belonging to [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]],<ref name="TVE" /> on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] and witnessed a conversation between<ref name="Datapad: Profile" /> the [[Gran]]<ref name="AEYNTK:UE">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> [[Ree-Yees]] and<ref name="Datapad: Profile" /> the [[Yarkora]]<ref name="AEYNTK:UE" /> [[Saelt-Marae]], who were discussing why the droid was aboard the barge instead of with the local [[Jawa]]s.<ref name="Datapad: Profile" />

At some point by the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'', [[Galaxy's Edge 1|''Galaxy's Edge'' 1]], and [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the droid had been [[Currency|purchased]] by the butcher [[Bakkar]] on Tatooine<ref name="Guide to Batuu" /> and [[Programmer|programmed]] by the [[droid builder]] [[Mubo]] on the planet [[Batuu]] for service at the [[Ronto Roasters]] [[Restaurant|eatery]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]], where he<ref name="Datapad: Droid Problems">{{DatapadCite|Droid Problems|DroidProblems1-GEDatapad.png|DroidProblems2-GEDatapad.png|DroidProblems3-GEDatapad.png}}</ref> turned the spit to cook [[Foodstuff|meat]] for Bakkar, who owned the restaurant.<ref name="Guide to Batuu" /> During that year,<ref name="Timelines" /> the droid offered a [[Ronto Wrap]] to the [[Kendoh Gang]] member [[Remex Io]], who declined the offer, instead requesting a [[meiloorun juice]] for himself and a [[Youngling|young]] kid. 8D-J8 and the gangmember then lamented the presence of the [[First Order]] on Batuu as a [[TIE/es assault shuttle]] flew overhead.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" /> The droid was also turning the spit when [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] was taken to Ronto Roasters by the [[Batuuan|local]] [[Gender|woman]] [[Salju]] for food.<ref name="GE:BS">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref>

Later that year,<ref name="Timelines" /> Bakkar communicated his frustrations with 8D-J8 to Mubo, complaining about how the droid talked non-stop and thought he was a master [[chef]]. To remedy that, Mubo asked if Bakkar had deactivated and reactivated the droid, later exclaiming he had reprogrammed 8D-J8 four to six [[time]]s. Bakkar threatened to put a [[restraining bolt]] on him, causing Mubo to cave in and agree to show up later, fitting the request into his busy schedule.<ref name="Datapad: Droid Problems" /> During the [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of Batuu by the First Order,<ref name="Guide to Batuu" /> also in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] mentioned 8D-J8 in his [[book]] ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''.<ref name="Guide to Batuu" /> The droid was also mentioned by the chef [[Strono Tuggs]] in his cookbook ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'',<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref> published the same year,<ref name="BSOCookbookDate">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> which suggested the [[Pitmaster droid|pitmaster]] be given more credit for the meats at Ronto Roasters than Bakkar himself.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|Of course, most of the credit should probably go to his pitmaster, a former smelter droid named 8D-J8.|Strono Tuggs credits 8D-J8 with the quality of the meat at Ronto Roasters|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
[[File:Bakkar-Cookbook.jpg|thumb|right|295px|8D-J8 was programmed to turn the spit for Bakkar but dreamed of a glamorous life.]]
An 8D-series smelter droid,<ref name="Datapad: Profile" /> 8D-J8 had [[Color|yellow]] [[photoreceptor]]s and tan plating<ref name="GERR" /> and was capable of storing [[Internal Data Recorder|internal data recordings]].<ref name="Datapad: Profile" /> He also talked constantly, much to the annoyance of his owner, Bakkar, and he believed he was a master chef, while in actuality being programmed simply to turn the spit at Ronto Roasters<ref name="Datapad: Droid Problems" /> and ensuring the meats were cooked to perfection. He stayed with Bakkar after leaving Tatooine, and during Throno's visit, the droid never left the side of the grill.<ref name="Guide to Batuu" />

8D-J8 idolized [[DJ (occupation)|DJ]] [[R-3X]] and other droids who managed to make it beyond their programmed duties and managed to keep an upbeat attitude in light of his mundane job, maintaining a dream of one [[Standard day|day]] becoming a droid chef<ref name="Staff">{{GEStaff|Ronto Roasters|restaurant}}</ref> and muttering cheerfully to himself about his prospects for a glamorous future.<ref name="GE:BS" /> According to Tuggs, 8D-J8 was good enough at his job to be credited more for the quality of the grilled meats than Bakkar himself.<ref name="Cookbook" /> The pitmaster showed frustration at the presence of the First Order in Black Spire Outpost, lamenting the potential loss of business he would face and calling them "[[Sithspit]]."<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:8D-J8_Ronto_Roasters.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Concept art of 8D-J8 and Ronto Roasters by Richard Lim and Erik Tiemens]]
8D-J8 was created as an [[Wikipedia:Audio-Animatronics|Audio-Animatronic]] character for the real-life restaurant Ronto Roasters,<ref name="GERR" /> which first opened as part of the theme park land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge|date=2019-05-10}}</ref> The droid was announced prior to the opening on [[February 28]] of that year in a Disney Parks Blog article titled "Building Batuu: New Details Released about ''Star Wars'': Galaxy’s Edge,"<ref name="Building Batuu" /> and he made his first appearance in the [[Galaxy's Edge 1|first issue]] of [[Marvel Comics|Marvel's]] [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge (comic series)|''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries,<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" /> which was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Will Sliney]], and released on [[April 24]], 2019.<ref name="SWI 189">{{InsiderCite|189|Launchpad}}</ref> Prior to the opening, a post on the blog erroneously referred to 8D-J8 as a "former settler droid," rather than a smelter droid.<ref name="DPB" /> Concept art for Ronto Roasters that included 8D-J8 was created by [[Industrial Light & Magic]] concept artist [[Richard Lim]] and [[Lucasfilm]] concept design supervisor [[Erik Tiemens]].<ref name="Art of">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge]]''</ref>

[[File:8D-J8-and-porg.png|thumb|right|300px|The annoyed 8D-J8 waves at a porg as it flies toward grilling meat.]]
8D-J8 was featured in the [[2020]] ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]'' animated short "[[Black Spire Outpost (episode)|Black Spire Outpost]],"<ref name="GoA">{{GOA|Black Spire Outpost (episode)}}</ref> which has been stated by [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Matt Martin]] to be non-literal within ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]].<ref name="Matt tweet GOA">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1139593657986588675|[[Matt Martin]]|date=2019-06-14|quote=I think it's just the usual GoA stuff, not meant to be taken literally.|archivefile=File:MattMartinGoA.jpg}}</ref> In the episode, while on Batuu, the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] chased down a [[porg]] as it flew throughout Black Spire Outpost, eventually being attracted to Ronto Roasters by the smell of the meats being grilled. The porg landed on 8D-J8's head before flying toward the meat as the droid waved annoyedly at it, only to be captured by the Wookiee.<ref name="GoA" />
