"'''Chewbacca - Wookiee Warrior'''" is the twenty-ninth episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com]] website and YouTube on [[May 24]], [[2019]].<ref name="Wookiee Warrior" /><ref name="Star Wars Kids" />

==Plot summary==
The video opens with the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' fleeing pursuing [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] through the [[Maw Cluster]]. One of the TIEs is struck by an [[asteroid]] while the ''Falcon'' collides with a second TIE fighter, destroying it. [[Han Solo]] is flying the ''Falcon'' with [[Qi'ra]]. Han tells [[Chewbacca]] to divert auxiliary power to the rear [[deflector shield]]s. The [[Wookiee]] easily switches the controls, earning the praise of Solo who asks since when did he learn to fly. Chewbacca responds in [[Shyriiwook]] and Solo is amazed to learn that Chewie is 190 [[Standard year|years old]].

In a flashback scene, the narrator explains that the mighty Chewbacca is a legendary warrior. Chewbacca is shown firing a [[bowcaster]] alongside several [[Clone trooper]]s and [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]]. The video then switches to Chewbacca leaping onto a [[NR-N99 Persuader-class droid enforcer]], scattering the [[B1-series battle droid]]s riding the tank droid. Chewbacca plants several [[detonator]]s before grabbing a rope from a passing [[Oevvaor jet catamaran|Oevvaor Jet Catamarans]]. The video then flashes to Chewbacca fighting members of the [[Pyke Syndicate]] in the [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] of [[Kessel]].

Back in the ''Falcon'', Han remarks that Chewbacca looks great for his age while Qi'ra flashes the Wookiee a smile. Qi'ra gives up her seat and tells Chewbacca to get into the co-pilot's seat. Qi'ra stands behind Han and Chewbacca as they fly the ''Falcon'' through the Maw Cluster, completing the [[Kessel Run]].
