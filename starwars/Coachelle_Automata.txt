'''Coachelle Automata''' was a company led by members of the [[Lepi]] [[species]].<ref name="Protocol and Service Droids">{{EncyclopediaCite|Protocol and Service Droids}}</ref> The corporation manufactured the [[LEP-series service droid|LEP servant droid]]. Initially the droid was meant to serve as a [[Youngling|childcare]] unit, but after it failed to find success in this capacity, it was tweaked and relaunched by Coachelle Automata as a [[service droid]]. The droid was manufactured in the same complex as [[Cybot Galactica]]'s [[PK-series worker droid]], which it proved to be only slightly less popular than.<ref name="R2-38">{{BuildR2Cite|38|Droid Directory}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Coachelle Automata was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2017]] [[De Agostini]] magazine [[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 38|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 38]].<ref name="R2-38" /> The [[Coachelle Automata/Legends|company]] originated in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], where it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Visual Guide: Ultimate Battles|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Visual Guide Ultimate Battles'']], which was written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TCWVG">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Visual Guide: Ultimate Battles]]''</ref>
