"'''A Coruscant Solstice'''" is a short story that was published in the ''[[Life Day Treasury]]'' short story collection. The story was authored by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Grant Griffin]], and published on [[September 7]], [[2021]]. The tale revolves around [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] patrolling [[Coruscant]] during [[Solstice Tide]]. Though the story's artwork in the announcement interview was titled "'''Heart of the Jedi'''," the actual collection titles the story "A Coruscant Solstice."
