'''Dosh''',<ref name="Wait for It" /> also known as '''Trandoshan''',<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was the native [[language]] of the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]].<ref name="Wait for It" />

{{Language-stub}}

==Known Words and Phrases==
*''Jolumba!''&mdash; "No!"<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
*''Lucumba!''&mdash; "Move Out!"<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
*''Rek ka luradan'' &mdash; "The Storm of Nightfire"<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
*''fesssellis'' &mdash; "crazy"<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
The non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]'' states that [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|"Bossk"]] means "devour prey" in the language.<ref name="100 scenes">''[[LEGO Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]''</ref>
