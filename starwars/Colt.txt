{{Quote|I want you troopers to remember—we're shoulder-to-shoulder on those front lines. Brothers! And sometimes we may quarrel, but no matter what, we are ''united''. Rule one: we fight together.|Colt|<ref name="Clone Cadets" />}}
'''Colt''' was a [[Clone Commander|Clone ARC Commander]] who served as the [[commanding officer]] of [[Rancor Battalion]], along with [[Commander]]s [[Havoc (ARC trooper)|Havoc]] and [[Blitz]], during the [[Clone Wars]], as part of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. He was [[Death|killed]] by [[Asajj Ventress]] during the [[Battle of Kamino]].

==Biography==
===Early Life===
[[File:Ambush-SWQ133.png|thumb|200px|left|Colt (front right), with other members of Rancor Battalion ambush a column of B2 super battle droids.]]
Colt was, like all [[Cloning|clone]]s, a [[DNA|genetic]] copy of the once renowned [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]],<ref name="Clone troopers" /> and was trained on the water [[planet|world]] of [[Kamino]]. At some point, he was promoted to the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Clone Commander|Clone ARC Commander]] of the [[Rancor Battalion]], serving alongside [[Commander]]s [[Blitz]], [[Havoc (ARC trooper)|Havoc]], and [[Hammer (ARC trooper)|Hammer]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> At some point, Colt&mdash;joined by Havoc, Blitz, and another ARC trooper&mdash;sas serving in a [[forest]]ed environment and prepared an ambush against an incoming group of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s.<ref name="BattleCards">{{Asmodee|subdomain=cdn.svc|domain=net|url=production-amgcom/uploads/2025/04/DOC13_BattleCards-1.pdf|text=''Star Wars: Legion'' 2025 Updated Battle Cards (PDF)}}</ref> Colt once found himself in a conflict in which, while by a [[Viewport|window]], he fired upon a target.<ref name="ARCTwilight">{{SWU|set=Twilight of the Republic (Star Wars: Unlimited)|cardname=Advanced Recon Commando|link=8640210306|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20241102201800/https://swudb.com/card/TWI/093/advanced-recon-commando}}</ref>

===Training of clone cadets===
{{Quote|You broke formation, you disobeyed orders, and you left a man behind. You broke rule number one.|Colt|<ref name="Clone Cadets" />}}
Colt, along with the bounty hunters [[Bric]] and [[El-Les]], oversaw [[clone cadet]]s' training. He selected [[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]] to first perform the final challenge, as the squad's cadets had completed their trial run in record [[Time]], "ARC trooper time," as Colt saw it. From an observation platform overlooking the practice chamber, the commander instructed El-Les to initiate version THX variable [[1138 (easter egg)|1138]], of the [[Citadel Challenge]]. Bravo Squad's performance in the test greatly impressed him, who congratulated the instructors on their training of the cadets.<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> [[File:Arcbricscrubber.png|thumb|right|250px|Commander Colt, Bric, and El-Les observe the progress of clone cadets.]]

Next was the [[Domino Squad]], and Colt instructed El-Les and Bric to not make the Citadel easy for them. As he observed their fighting, he quickly grew disappointed by Domino Squad's "sloppy" and "unorthodox" performance. Colt stopped the test after Domino Squad broke formation, disobeyed orders and had broken rule one, leaving an injured [[Droidbait|CT-00-2010]] behind on the ground. As a result, El-Les had no choice but to give an automatic failure for Domino Squad. Although, the squad did later succeed after they were given a second opportunity to pass the simulation. After Domino Squad had graduated, Colt and his fellow [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC troopers]] saw off the cadets as they shipped out as soldiers for the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

===Battle of Kamino===
[[File:VentressKissColt-ARCT.png|left|thumb|250px|Ventress kissing Colt after killing him]]
Colt and his fellow ARC troopers remained on Kamino. When [[General]] [[Grievous]], the leader of the droid army, and [[assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] launched a surprise attack on Kamino, Colt was assigned to defend [[Tipoca City]], where he encountered Grievous and managed to hold his own against him, accompanied by a small squad of clone troopers. However, soon after, he confronted Ventress, who [[Force choke]]d him. Colt was slammed against the wall, with the impact knocking his [[ARC trooper armor|helmet]] off. Ventress then proceeded to impale him with [[Asajj Ventress's lightsabers|her lightsabers]], while she kissed him on the cheek. She disengaged her lightsaber, allowing Colt's lifeless body to collapse to the floor.<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Colt-CC.png|thumb|right|200px|Colt, in his Phase I ARC trooper armor.]]
As a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] clone of the bounty hunter Jango Fett, Colt stood at a height of 1.83 [[meter]]s<ref name="Clone troopers" /> and weighed eighty [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Weight" /> He had [[Color|black]] [[hair]], brown [[eye]]s, and tan skin.<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

Bravo Squad's performance, specifically, greatly impressed the commander&mdash;the cadets had completed their trial run in record "ARC trooper" time and had performed their final test flawlessly. His evaluation of Domino Squad, conversely, was quite different&mdash;the cadets' shoddy handling of the Citadel Challenge had revealed their disunity and noncompliance, and Colt wasted no time in berating them for their mistakes.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

When faced with a Separatist attack on Tipoca City, Colt and other clones fought against invading [[battle droid]]s despite overwhelming odds. The commander continued to fight even after his comrades were killed, until he himself was struck down in battle.<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

==Skills and abilities==
Colt had received ARC training at one point prior to serving as an ARC trooper and was promoted to the rank of Clone ARC Commander. He served as the commanding officer of the Rancor Battalion<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> and led clone troopers into battle.<ref name="ARC Troopers" /> As an ARC Trooper, Colt was trained in armed and unarmed combat as well as military tactics, survival, reconnaissance and infiltrations.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''&mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s</ref>

==Equipment==
At [[Reproduction|birth]], like the other clone troopers, Colt was implanted by the [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]]s with a [[behavioral modification biochip]] to ensure that the trooper would not resist certain [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|orders]].<ref name="Clone troopers" /> As an ARC trooper, Colt made use of white [[Phase I ARC Trooper Armor]] with red and gray markings, which included a [[helmet]] with a [[rangefinder]]; a [[kama]]; a double [[pauldron]]; additional gray [[armor]] plating on the chest, arms, and legs; a survival pack; and two [[holster]]s for [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster]]s.<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> Colt also had ammunition cartridges on top of chest plates.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Additionally, he was armed with a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]].<ref name="ARC Troopers" />
