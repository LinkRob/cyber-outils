The '''Dalnan evacuation''', also known as the '''Dalnan refugee crisis''', was a rescue mission undertaken by the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> in [[230 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Following an attack on [[Saludad]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Dalna]], by [[Kara Xoo's Tempest]] of the [[pirate]] organization the [[Nihil]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]] organized efforts on [[Starlight Beacon]] to evacuate the [[Dalnan]]s off their planet as it was rocked by [[groundquake]]s and the eruption of [[volcano]]s.<ref name="Mission to Disaster" />
