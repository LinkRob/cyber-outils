The '''Chadawa system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Chadawa]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]] in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' states that it begins eleven [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. The [[Battle of Jakku]], which ''Star Wars: Galactic Atlas'' states takes place in [[5 ABY]], is stated in ''[[Lost Stars]]'' to take place a year and four [[Standard day|days]] after the Battle of [[Endor]]. Additionally, ''[[Before the Awakening]]'' states that [[Kes Dameron]] and [[Shara Bey]] moved to [[Yavin 4]] around six [[Standard month|months]] after the Battle of Endor, and ''Galactic Atlas'' dates their move to 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]. Therefore, ''Victory's Price'', and the [[Attack on Chadawa|battle]] at Chadawa must be set in 5 ABY.</ref> Chadawa was targeted by the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], which sought to punish the world in a continuation of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Operation: Cinder]], but were [[Attack on Chadawa|hindered]] by [[New Republic]] [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] and her forces.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
