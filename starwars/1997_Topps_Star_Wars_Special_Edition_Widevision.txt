'''1997 Topps ''Star Wars'' Special Edition''' is a trading card set covering the events and behind the scenes details of ''[[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition]]''. The set was released by [[Topps]] in [[February]] [[1997]],<ref name="SWGM 10 ad" /> and featured the "Widevision" logo as they were in the same size and format as earlier Widevision sets. The set was released simultaneously with the ''[[1997 Topps Star Wars Trilogy: The Complete Story|Star Wars Trilogy: The Complete Story]]'' card set based on the original versions of the films.<ref name="SWGM 10" />
