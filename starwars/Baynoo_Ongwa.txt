'''Baynoo Ongwa''' was a [[Corellian]] [[human]] [[Gender|female]] retired [[bodyguard]] who previously ran the [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection]] agency.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> Around [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]]. ''Midnight Horizon'' establishes that [[Alys Ongwa]] inherited the leadership of [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection]] the previous [[Standard year|year]], therefore meaning that Baynoo Ongwa retired in approximately [[231 BBY]].</ref> Baynoo retired and turned the business over to her daughter, [[Alys Ongwa|Alys]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
