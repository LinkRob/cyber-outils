{{Quote|Droid Gotra to strike high-value targets on Terminus, Little Petrovi and Corbett Cluster. Position soldiers for intercept.|Translation of [[Bala-Tik]]'s counteracting broadcast in [[binary]], in the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
The '''Corbett Cluster''' was a [[star cluster]] in the [[Corbett sector]]. [[Droid Gotra]] began to transmit orders to all members of Gotra and the [[Cyban Front]] stationed in the Rim Territories, ordering them to strike high-value data caches on [[Terminus]], [[Little Petrovi]], and the Corbett Cluster. The transmission was promptly intercepted by the [[Guavian Death Gang]], who then broadcast their own counteracting broadcast.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
