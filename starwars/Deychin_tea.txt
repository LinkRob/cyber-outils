'''Deychin tea''' was a type of [[tea]] [[Diet|potable]] by [[human]]s, [[Togruta]]s,<ref name="AEE" /> and [[Pkorian]]s.<ref name="Inquisitor" /> When served [[Temperature|hot]], it gave off [[Flower|floral]]-scented [[Water|steam]].<ref name="AEE" /> Following the [[First Battle of Geonosis|Battle of Geonosis]]<ref name="Inquisitor" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Inquisitor date">The chapter in which Deychin [[tea]] is mentioned in ''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'' takes place concurrently with the events of [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Jocasta Nu]] offered [[Jedi Knight]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] the opportunity to return to securing [[Force artifact|artifacts]] for the Jedi Order, noting that the latter's recently-[[Death|deceased]] Master, [[Sember Vey]], had believed she was almost ready to return to her past activities. Though grateful for her Master's faith, Akaris dreaded the proposition and mentally considered haggling over a cup of Deychin tea to secure dusty [[datacron]]s unappealing. Ultimately, she declined Nu's offer.<ref name="Inquisitor" />

In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> the [[advisor]] [[Auxi Kray Korbin]] poured two mugs of hot Deychin tea for herself and her employer, the [[New Republic]] [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]], while in the latter's [[Mon Mothma's office (Chandrila)|office]] on<ref name="AEE" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="TG">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. Koribin suggested that Mothma add some [[alcohol]]ic [[brandy]] to her beverage, though the chancellor declined.<ref name="AEE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Deychin tea appeared in ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', a [[2017]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the third installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="AEE" />
