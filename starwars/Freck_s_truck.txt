'''Freck's truck''' was a [[FMR-385 cargo speeder]] driven by the [[Condluran]] [[Freck]] on the [[planet]] [[Mapuzo]]. An [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] flag is attached to the back of the truck as a sign of Imperial loyalty.<ref name="SWEDK" /> [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and a young [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Princess Leia]] attempted to flee the local Imperials via the [[cargo speeder]], though this attempt ultimately failed, with the truck and its driver being pulled into a brief firefight.<ref name="Part III" />
