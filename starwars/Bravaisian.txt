The '''Bravaisians''' were a short, long-[[Nose|snouted]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that were [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Bravais]]. They had a dexterous [[tongue]] that they often used instead of their fingers. They were nocturnal, due to the intense [[Temperature|heat]] of their homeworld and avoided daylight to protect their small sensitive [[eye]]s. When offworld, most Bravaisians wore [[goggles]] to protect their eyes. They also developed from a [[Bravaisian precursor species|precursor species]]. [[Munduri]] and [[Sonsigo]] were two Bravaisians who were frequent sights at the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of [[Maz Kanata]].

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Sonsigo-BaseSeries4.png|thumb|left|220px|Sonsigo had reddish-brown hair]]
Bravaisians were a [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that stood at a diminutive height, with [[Color|blue]], [[leather]]y skin and two [[Limb|arms]] that ended in four digits. They had snouts that faded to pink at their end. These snouts contained a prehensile [[tongue]] as dexterous as their fingers. Bravaisians would alternate between using their fingers and their tongues.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> Some Bravaisians, such as Sonsigo, had hair growing from the back of their heads.<ref name="Base Series 4">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 4|cardname=Sonsigo}}</ref>

Bravaisians were nocturnal due to the intense [[Temperature|heat]] and lack of life on [[Bravais]] during the [[Standard day|day]]. As a result, they had small [[eye]]s that could easily be overwhelmed by light. For this reason, most members of the species were rarely seen without protective [[goggles]] while offworld. Some even had their eyes surgically removed to combat this problem.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

==Society and culture==
Bravaisians could make a squawking sound if disturbed.<ref name="Aftermath: Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> Due to them being nocturnal, Bravaisians viewed other species' insistence on doing business during daylight hours with a mix of pity and contempt.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

==History==
Bravaisians evolved from [[Bravaisian precursor species|pack hunters]], that used their tough, flexible [[Nose|snouts]] to dig through soil and dirt in order to find their prey, which were tubers and nests of [[insect]]s. They would then suck up these with their long muscular tongues.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

==Bravaisians in the galaxy==
During the [[High Republic Era]], a [[Force-sensitive]] Bravaisian named [[Bibs]] served as a [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="THRA 1">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 1]]</ref> Sometime prior to the [[Battle of Takodana]], a Bravaisian was arguing with [[Gwellis Bagnoro]] in the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] owned by [[Maz Kanata]] on [[Takodana]].<ref name="TFoE">{{StoryCite|story=The Face of Evil|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref>

[[Sonsigo]] and [[Munduri]] were two Bravaisian podmates who were frequent visitors to Kanata's castle. There, they would buy glittering [[Gemstone|gems]] and precious metals, and they would offer top prices for them.<ref name="TFAVD" /> The pair were careful in the appraisal of their wares, which was a process that could involve using their tongues, fingers and an [[electro refractometer]].<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|sonsigo-and-munduri|Sonsigo and Munduri}}</ref> They were both present at Kanata's Castle when the [[astromech droid]] [[BB-8]], carrying a portion of a [[Map to Skywalker|map]] that led to [[Luke Skywalker]], arrived at the castle along with the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]], former [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn]] and [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|They have a great charm about them because they rely very much on the skills of hand puppetry, and they bring a very different life to them than a character that's worn on someone's head.|[[Neal Scanlan]] discusses the Sonsigo and Munduri puppets in the "Creating Creatures" featurette|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
Bravaisians were created for the Takodana sequence of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']],<ref name="Episode VII" /> but were first mentioned in "[[The Face of Evil]]," a short story by [[Landry Q. Walker]],<ref name="TFoE" /> which was released on [[December 1]], [[2015]].<ref name="Amazon eBook">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/the-face-of-evil|text=The Face of Evil}}</ref>

For ''The Force Awakens'', Sonsigo and Munduri were both hand puppets, controlled by their performers from underneath the bench the characters sat on. The performer responsible for Sonsigo was [[Olly Taylor]], and [[Damian Farrell]] was responsible for Munduri. Both men were creature and droid performers. During the production of the film, Munduri and Sonsigo were known as "snooty pirates".<ref name="Episode VII" />
