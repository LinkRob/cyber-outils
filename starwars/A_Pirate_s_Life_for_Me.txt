"'''A Pirate's Life for Me'''" is a magazine article by [[Jens Schleicher]] that interviews [[Vane]] actor [[Marti Matulis]].<ref name="Magazine" />

It was first published in German in [[Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin 117|''Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin'' 117]], as "'''Marti Matulis: Der Mann hinter der Maske'''" ([[Wikipedia:English language|English]]: "'''Marti Matulis: The Man Behind the Mask'''"),<ref name="Magazine" /> on [[March 21]], [[2025]].<ref name="Jedi-Bibliothek" /> It was republished in English in [[Star Wars Insider 233|''Star Wars Insider'' 233]],<ref name="Insider" /> on [[August 19]], 2025.<ref name="Titan" />
