The '''Eastern Wilderness''' was an area of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Ueda]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Clarodian]] [[Gender|brothers]] [[Tianris Mez]] and [[Guanlos Mez]] supervised an old family deep-mantle [[mining]] [[Mez mining operation|operation]] in the Eastern Wilderness.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> Tianris was looking to retire but did not think Guanlos could handle the responsibility of managing the Eastern Wilderness operation.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Eastern Wilderness was mentioned in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
