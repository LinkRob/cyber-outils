The '''Dulvoyinn system''' was located in the [[Deep Core]]<ref name="BMF36" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It was also situated in the grid square K-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dulvoyinn [[Star system|system]] was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "The Deep Core,"<ref name="BMF36" /> an article written by [[Mark Newbold]]<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> and included in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 36|thirty-sixth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine<ref name="BMF36" /> that was published around [[September 9]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF36date">{{BMFdates|36}}</ref> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Dulvoyinn system/Legends|Dulvoyinn system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
