'''Credit Pusher''' was a [[slot machine]] for [[gambling]]<ref name="Rampage" /> found throughout [[the galaxy]]. A Credit Pusher was located in an alley in a [[Unidentified city (Cad Bane)|bustling city]] on [[Duro]] at the [[time]] of [[Cad Bane]]'s childhood,<ref name="The Good Life" /> at [[Cid's Parlor]] in [[Ord Mantell City]],<ref name="Rampage" /> in [[Tanda's Cantina]] on [[Suria space station]],<ref name="Bad Territory" /> and in a [[Unidentified settlement (rocky planet)|settlement]] on an [[unnamed planet]], where [[Inquisitorius]] members [[Fourth Sister]] and [[Barriss Offee]] hunted a [[Unidentified Human Jedi|Jedi]].<ref name="Realization" /> At least two Credit Pusher machines were available at Cid's Parlor,<ref name="BattleScars">{{TBB|Battle Scars}}</ref> with the slogan "GoGoGo!" written in [[Aurebesh]] at the bottom.<ref name="CommonGround" />
