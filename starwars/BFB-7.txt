'''BFB-7''' was a [[droid]] nicknamed "'''Boost-Bot'''," who was active during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. The droid had a [[servomotor]]-equipped riser at its top, and a simplified [[Droid brain|logic center]] on its front.<ref name="TROS VD" />
