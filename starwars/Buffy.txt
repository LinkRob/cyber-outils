'''Buffy''' was the name of a [[CLE-004 window cleaning droid]]<ref name="BuildR2 38 note" /> that worked on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] with<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref> the [[INS-444]] [[Mick]].<ref name="BuildR2 38 note" /> The two [[droid]]s replaced the bedroom window of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[Padmé's Apartment|apartment]] after the original window was destroyed through the actions of [[Zam Wesell]]'s [[ASN-121|assassin droid]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="AOTC" />
