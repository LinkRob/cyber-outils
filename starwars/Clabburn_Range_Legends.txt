'''Clabburn Range''' was a [[Mountain/Legends|mountain]] range bordering the [[Hanging Valley/Legends|Hanging Valley]] and the [[Ceyan Range/Legends|Ceyan Range]] on the icy [[Planet/Legends|world]] of [[Hoth/Legends|Hoth]]. [[Echo Base/Legends|Echo Base]] was built in the mountains of the Clabburn Range, and the [[South Ridge/Legends|South Ridge]] was another smaller part of the Range.
