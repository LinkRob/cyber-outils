"'''Dispatches from Wild Space'''" is a regular section of the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series published by [[IDW Publishing]]. The section contains background information about the characters, behind-the-scenes looks at the creative process, announcements, and messages from readers. It is presented as if it was written by [[Emil Graf]], an [[in-universe]] character.<ref name="SWA 1">[[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 1|''Star Wars Adventures'' (2017) 1]]</ref>
