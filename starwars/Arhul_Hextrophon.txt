'''Arhul Hextrophon''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="anh" /> who served as a [[major]] in the [[Alliance Military|military]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] around the time of the [[Battle of Scarif]]. He and [[Lieutenant]] [[Voren Na'al]] worked on a book titled ''[[Official History of the Rebel Movements, Volume One]]'',<ref name="Rebel Dossier" /> and he created a [[Hextrophon Collection|collection]] of files of historical importance.<ref name="Rogue One novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> Immediately after the Alliance's [[Battle of Yavin|victory]] over the [[Galactic Empire]] at [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]], Hextrophon attended the [[Royal Award Ceremony|ceremony]] held in honor of [[Luke Skywalker]] and the other heroes who helped destroy the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. During the ceremony, he presented a [[Medal of Bravery]] to [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]] before it was awarded to [[Han Solo]] by [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]].<ref name="anh" />

By the time of the [[New Republic]], a [[Arhul Hextrophon School of Embedded Journalism|school]] of embedded journalism on [[Lerct]] was named after him.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
