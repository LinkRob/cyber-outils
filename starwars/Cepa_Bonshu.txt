'''Cepa Bonshu''' was a communications [[artist]] from the [[planet]] [[Riosa]] who was responsible for the creation of the art [[Painting|piece]] ''[[What Will You Do]]'', which protested the [[Galactic Empire]]'s use of forced conscription into their [[Imperial Military|military]] on some [[Planet|worlds]]. The piece used the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] as a central element as he had overseen the [[Subjugation of Riosa|subjugation]] of Riosa and the impressment of its population into labor camps. By the [[time]] of the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]] many [[Standard year|years]] later, Bonshu had retired after a long arts career and began teaching at the [[Ord Canfre Institute for Ethics in Art Criticism]].<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cepa Bonshu was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on [[October 25]], [[2016]].
