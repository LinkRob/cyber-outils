'''Dilanni''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Felucian]] who served as a member of the [[Farmer|farming]] community run by<ref name="Bounty Hunters" /> [[Casiss Midagatis]]<ref name="TCW CE">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> on [[Felucia]]. When [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] was training the farmers to defend themselves against the [[Ohnaka Gang]], Dilanni was pessimistic about their chances and believed that they could not defeat the [[pirate]]s. The farmer later saved [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Sugi (bounty hunter)|Sugi]] from a [[speeder bike]]-riding pirate by throwing his spear at the pirate.<ref name="Bounty Hunters" />
