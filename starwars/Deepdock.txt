'''Deepdocks''' were [[shipyard]]s used by various powers including the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> and the [[First Order]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 9">[[Poe Dameron 9|''Poe Dameron'' 9]]</ref> Prior to the [[Incident in the Obroa-skai system|incident]] in the [[Obroa-skai system]], [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] had a [[CC-7700 frigate]] transferred from a deepdock in the [[Corellian system|Corellia system]] to assist a military operation to recapture Tarkin's [[Mission to Murkhana|stolen]] [[Starship|ship]] ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', which had been captured by [[Berch Teller]]'s [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]]. Other notable Imperial deepdocks included the [[Imperial Navy Deepdock Facility Two]], the [[R/M Facility Four deepdock]], and the [[Ryloth deepdock]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

Following a [[Mission to Megalox Beta|skirmish]] with [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] above [[Megalox Beta]], a First Order [[technician]] repairing [[Agent]] [[Terex]]'s ship ''Carrion Spike'' informed his [[corporal]] that the ship needed a new central [[computer]], an operation that could only be done in a deepdock.<ref name="Poe Dameron 9" />
