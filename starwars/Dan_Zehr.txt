'''Dan Zehr''' is the co-author of ''[[The Star Wars Book]]''<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> and ''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''.<ref name="SWCEUaEE">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> He is also the host and co-creator of ''[[Coffee With Kenobi]]'', a Star Wars fan podcast.<ref name="SW.com" /> Zehr also writes the "[[Ships of the Galaxy]]" column for [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="SOTG">{{SW|url=news/ships-of-the-galaxy-naboo-n-1-starfighter|text=Ships of the Galaxy: Naboo N-1 Starfighter|int=Ships of the Galaxy}}</ref>

Zehr also appeared, in his capacity as an English studies teacher, in a [[2016]] Target commercial promoting the release of [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]].<ref name="Target ">{{YouTube|video=tCMiISI4MNs|text="There's A Rebel In All Of Us" Target Rogue One Commercial|channel=@CoffeeWithKenobi|name=Coffee With Kenobi with Dan Zehr|date=2021-09-02|archivedate=20240731211607}}</ref><ref name="Uproxx">{{WebCite|url=https://uproxx.com/hitfix/reminder-girls-and-women-love-star-wars-just-as-much-as-you-do|text=Rebel ‘Star Wars’ commercial reminds us how inclusive ‘The Force Awakens’ was|author=Jill Pantozzi|date=2016-09-03|work=[[Wikipedia:Uproxx|''Uproxx'']]|archivedate=20240529121456}}</ref>
