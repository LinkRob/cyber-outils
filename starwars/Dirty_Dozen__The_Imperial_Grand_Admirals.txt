"'''Dirty Dozen: The Imperial Grand Admirals'''" was an article by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Abel G. Peña]] for ''[[Star Wars Gamer]]'' magazine. The article included biographies and [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] statistics about the [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admirals]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. After ''Gamer'' was canceled, Wallace and Peña adapted much of the article for the [[Star Wars Insider 66|''Star Wars Insider'' 66]] article ''[[Who's Who: Imperial Grand Admirals]]''.<ref name="JCF">{{JCFCite|url=posts/10611396|board=Literature|thread=Star Wars Insider #66|user=[[Daniel Wallace|Dan Wallace]]|date=2003-02-19|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20200801142406/https://boards.theforce.net/threads/star-wars-insider-66.10415051/page-4}}</ref><ref name="Blog">{{Blog|danwallace|16|Grand Admirals: What Have They Got in their Pocketsses?}}</ref><ref name="Blog2">{{Blog|abelgpena|78|An Old Friend: Star Wars Insider #66}}</ref>
