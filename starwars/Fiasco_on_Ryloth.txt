The [[Black Sun]] criminal syndicate was involved in an '''fiasco on Ryloth'''<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> The incident&mdash;which occurred on<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Ryloth]]&mdash;was considered by the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] to be significant for Black Sun.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while attending an [[Auction for Han Solo|auction]] hosted by the criminal syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]] to gather intel on Crimson Dawn and other competitors of<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12" /> [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]]'s [[Tagge Corporation]],<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 11|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 11]]</ref> Aphra saw Black Sun in attendance, and pointed out the syndicate's<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12" /> Lord<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 2">[[War of the Bounty Hunters 2|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 2]]</ref> [[Gyuti]] to the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]]. The archaeologist then expressed her wonder if Black Sun had ever recovered from the Ryloth fiasco, noting that Gyuti still had his head which suggested to her that the syndicate had.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12" />

==Behind the scenes==
The fiasco on Ryloth was mentioned in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 12|twelfth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12" /> on [[July 14]], [[2021]]. Prior to the release of the comic, the fiasco was mentioned in a [https://www.starwars.com/news/marvel-star-wars-war-of-the-bounty-hunters-2-doctor-aphra-12-preview preview article] of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[July 7]] of the same year<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-war-of-the-bounty-hunters-2-doctor-aphra-12-preview|text=In Marvel's ''Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters'' #2, Boba Fett Heads for the Scoundrel's Ball - Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
