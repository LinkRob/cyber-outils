The '''39th Emergence''',<ref name="Timelines" /> or the '''thirty-ninth Emergence''', was the thirty-ninth of the [[Emergences]], fallout of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] in which fragments of the [[freighter]] ''[[Legacy Run]]'' shot out of [[hyperspace]] seemingly at random. A passenger compartment of the [[starship]] emerged from hyperspace in a deserted section of space and was successfully retrieved by a [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] team which included four [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruisers]], including [[Joss Adren|Joss]] and [[Pikka Adren]]'s ''[[Aurora III]]'', and two [[Jedi Vector]]s piloted by [[Mikkel Sutmani]] and [[Te'Ami]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

The operation was spied upon by the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] in her [[Lourna Dee (cruiser)|cruiser]], as both the Republic's [[Keven Tarr's navidroid array|droid array]] and [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] had predicted that the ''Legacy Run''{{'s}} [[Black box|flight recorder]] might be recovered in the Emergence. However, it did not appear, so most of the rescue team moved on to the imminent [[40th Emergence]], as did Dee, while one Longbeam stayed behind to tend to the rescued passengers. At that subsequent Emergence, the flight recorder appeared, prompting a skirmish which resulted in the device being retrieved by the Republic, but the [[death]]s of Te'Ami and the crew of one Longbeam.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
