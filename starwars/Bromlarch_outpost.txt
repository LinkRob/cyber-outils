The '''Bromlarch outpost''' was an [[Jedi outpost|outpost]] of the [[Jedi Order]] that was located on<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref> [[Bromlarch]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> By [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Bakari]] was stationed at the outpost.<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Valo Atrocity|attacked]] the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] being held on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Valo]] and Bakari was part of the reinforcements that battled against the Nihil. As they [[Pilot|flew]] a [[Jedi Vector]], Bakari noted their presence to [[Jedi High Council]] member [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]], including their station at the Bromlarch outpost.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
