The '''Byron College''' was a prestigious place of learning on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Alderaan/Legends|Alderaan]], before its destruction by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]]. The College was part of the capital city of [[Aldera/Legends|Aldera]]. The famous [[Alderaanian/Legends|Alderaanian]] [[Poet/Legends|poetess]] [[Hari Seldona]] studied ancient litterature at the Byron College.

==Behind the scenes==
The Byron College was created by [[Philippe Rat]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] scenario "[[Prélude à un nouvel espoir]]" which appeared in the French magazine [[Casus Belli 103|''Casus Belli'' 103]].
