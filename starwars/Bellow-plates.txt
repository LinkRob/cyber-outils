'''Bellow-plates''' were a [[clothing]] item worn in order to assist with breathing, worn by [[humanoid]]s on the upper part of their torso.<ref name="TVG" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> the [[Wrosidian]] [[Basko]] wore [[Color|gray]], corrugated bellow-plates while in the [[Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop]] on the [[planet]] [[Ueda]],<ref name="TVG" /> where they were [[Diet|eating]] [[noodle]]s with<ref name="LF">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> the Wrosidian [[Bihun]] and the [[Reshtini]] [[Kesmer Faloodi]].<ref name="TVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bellow-plates first appeared, albeit unidentified, in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="LF" /> the first episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] series [[Star Wars: The Acolyte|''The Acolyte'']], which aired on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="EPG">{{SW|url=series/the-acolyte/lost-found-episode-guide|text="Lost / Found" Episode Guide &#124; The Acolyte}}</ref> Prior to their debut in the episode, they were first depicted in a trailer shown exclusively at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{CelebrationTrailer|''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''|Celebration Europe (2023)}}</ref> during the [[Lucasfilm]] Studio Showcase on [[April 7]], [[2023]].<ref name="Studio Showcase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-lucasfilm-studio-showcase|text=SWCE 2023: 11 Highlights from Lucasfilm's Studio Showcase|date=2023-04-08}}</ref> They were later identified as bellow-plates in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TVG" /> The noodle shop scenes were filmed at [[Shinfield Studios]],<ref name="Fantha Part I">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fanthatracks.com/news/the-acolyte-a-journey-to-the-filming-locations-part-1-madeira|text=The Acolyte: A journey to the filming locations Part 1: Madeira|archivedate=20241204220147}}</ref> [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="Julian Foddy">{{Lucasfilm|url=news/julian-foddy-acolyte|text=''The Acolyte'' Visual Effects Supervisor Julian Foddy}}</ref> Concept art of bellow-plates was created by artist [[Barbra Araujo]] and [[Andre Gilbert]] and was subsequently included in ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', a<ref name="Art of Acolyte">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''</ref> [[2026]]<ref name="Abrams">{{Abrams|url=product/art-of-star-wars-the-acolyte_9781419777349|text=The Art of Star Wars: The Acolyte}}</ref> reference book written by [[Kristin Baver]].<ref name="Art of Acolyte" />
