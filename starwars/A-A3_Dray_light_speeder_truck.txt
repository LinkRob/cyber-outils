The '''A-A3 Dray light speeder truck''' was a model of [[speeder truck]] manufactured by [[Trast Heavy Transports]]. A common sight throughout [[the galaxy]], it was notably used in the [[clone trooper]] training area in [[Tipoca City]], [[Kamino]], at the time of the [[Clone Wars]].

==Characteristics==
Smaller and lighter than the [[A-A5 Speeder Truck|A-A5 speeder truck]], the A-A3 Dray was a model of [[speeder truck]] that featured an enclosed cab that sat three abreast (counting the [[pilot]]) and a long, open bed that could carry cargo or eight passengers.<ref name="Begun" />

==History==
The A-A3 Dray was a common sight throughout [[the galaxy]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], Drays were used in the [[clone trooper]] training area in [[Tipoca City]], [[Kamino]], to move equipment throughout the huge chamber, and were kept in storage garages fitted inside the walls.<ref name="Begun" />
