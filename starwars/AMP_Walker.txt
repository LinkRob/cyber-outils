The '''AMP Walker''' was a model of [[Labor droid|worker droid]] with [[Color|gray]] plating and yellow cabling. A type of [[monster droid]], they had the body of an [[EG-series power droid]],<ref name="Fortnite" /> a [[Veril Line Systems]] product,<ref name="CEUE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> and the legs of an [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]],<ref name="Fortnite" /> a product of [[Industrial Automaton]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> On<ref name="Fortnite" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Tatooine]], the [[Jawa]]-run [[Sandcrawler Shop]] near the [[Junkers Cantina]] sold AMP Walker blueprints. The default design was sold at 5,400,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], a golden design at 21,600,000 credits, a [[diamond]]-colored version at 43,200,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design at 86,400,000 credits.<ref name="Fortnite" />
