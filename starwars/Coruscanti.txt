The '''Coruscanti''' were the inhabitants of [[Coruscant]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> a [[Ecumenopolis|planet-wide city]]<ref name="UltimateSW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> and the [[Timeline of galactic history|historic]] [[capital]] of [[the galaxy]] during the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Imperial Era|age]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="DB Coruscant">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref> The [[homeworld]] of the [[human]] [[species]],<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> individuals native to Coruscant included [[Letta Turmond]],<ref name="DB Letta">{{Databank|letta-turmond|Letta Turmond}}</ref> [[Inspector]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo]] of the [[Coruscant Security Force]],<ref name="DB Divo">{{SWE|characters|tandivo|Tan Divo}}</ref> and [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]].<ref name="UltimateSW" /> At the end of the [[Clone Wars]] in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[millennium]]-Republic was [[Proclamation of the New Order|replaced]] by the Empire; a [[New Order|new system]] that invested absolute power in [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Episode III" /> Millions of Coruscanti citizens were left homeless by the [[Battle of Coruscant]] from the war's end.<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

During the early [[Standard year|years]] of the Empire, most Coruscanti were satisfied with the regime shift, particularly those who lived and worked in the upper tiers of [[Imperial City]]. If for no other reason, the Coruscanti were relieved to see an end of the devastation brought by the Clone Wars. However, the self-anointed Emperor Palpatine made few public appearances, and a number of Coruscanti confessed to having fond memories for Chancellor Valorum.<ref name="Tarkin" /> After the Emperor's [[death]]<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Coruscant descended into a state of [[Coruscant civil war|civil war]] as many Coruscanti took to the streets of the [[city]], [[Riot in Monument Plaza|rioting]] in [[Monument Plaza]] to celebrate the end of Palpatine's reign.<ref name="Episode VI" />

Those who identified themselves as Coruscanti often had what some would consider a superior accent, with it being frequently attributed to [[propaganda]] broadcasts, [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] satire, and the Imperial elite during the reign of the Empire.<ref name="Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> Coruscanti were considered principled, able to verbally parry and weave in debates and politics.<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> According to [[Chief]]tain [[Gruppe]], the human [[Gender|females]] of Coruscant were not known to [[Reproduction|carry or deliver]] their own progeny. Instead, they hired others to do so for them.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>
