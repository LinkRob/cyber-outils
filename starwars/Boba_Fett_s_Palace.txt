'''Boba Fett's Palace''', formerly '''Jabba's Palace''' and briefly '''Bib Fortuna's palace''', and also referred to as '''Daimyo's Palace''', was located at the fringes of the [[Northern Dune Sea]] in [[Mos Espa]] on the [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]]. Originally erected as a monastery for the [[B'omarr Order]], it became a base of operations for the [[Daimyo]] [[Hutt]] crime lord [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]. After his [[death]], it was taken over by his [[majordomo]], the [[Twi'lek]] [[Bib Fortuna]], before he was killed and replaced by the famed [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]].

==Description and layout==
===Overview===
{{Quote|Jabba's Palace is on the far side of the Dune Sea. We'd better hurry if we're gonna make it by morning.|[[Anakin Skywalker]]|<ref name="TCW Movie">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>}}
[[File:Star-wars-jabbas-palace.jpg|thumb|left|320px|Jabba's Palace]]
The [[Daimyo]]'s Palace,<ref name=SWU>{{SWU|set=A Lawless Time|cardname=Daimyo's Palace|link=5043366366|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/YO2ow/image}}</ref> often called [[Boba Fett]]'s Palace<ref name="Boba Fett's Palace Databank">{{Databank|boba-fett-s-palace|Boba Fett's Palace}}</ref> (or Boba's Palace)<ref name="Chapter 2 audio description">{{BOBF|Chapter 2: The Tribes of Tatooine}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> and [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt's]] Palace<ref name="Databank" /> (or Jabba's Palace)<ref name="Episode VI" /> depending on the presiding daimyo,<ref name="Boba Fett's Palace Databank" /> was located in the [[Northern Dune Sea]], a region of the [[Dune Sea]] on [[Tatooine]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and was accessible on land by a single dirt road that stretched through a canyon. The palace, constructed with sandblasted metal and stone, was where Jabba conducted illicit business deals.<ref name="Databank" /> The structure was a fortress, with a defensive design that focused primarily on protection from attack.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The palace included a [[Jabba's Palace garage|garage]].<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref>

===Throne room===
[[File:A-New-Beginning.png|thumb|right|300px|Boba Fett in the throne room.]]
Jabba would entertain guests in a [[Jabba the Hutt's dais|throne room]], which also had a [[Max Rebo Band|live band]] led by [[Maximilian Rebo|Max Rebo]] and overlooked a pit that contained a [[rancor]]<ref name="Databank" /> by the last [[Standard day|days]] of [[Jabba's criminal empire|the Hutt's criminal empire]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> [[Bib Fortuna]] later took over the throne room but eventually lost it to [[Boba Fett]]. Both ruled from the room,<ref name="Rescue">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 16: The Rescue}}</ref> but Fett also adopted a hands on approach to controlling the criminal underworld<ref name="Streets">{{BOBF|Chapter 3: The Streets of Mos Espa}}</ref> instead of remaining locked away in the Palace.<ref name="Name Honor">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref>

===Subterranean complex===
A huge subterranean complex existed under the palace. The complex included Jabba's throne room, workshops, animal pens, the [[sail barge]] ''[[Khetanna]]'', and spice farms.<ref name="CL" />

===Communications tower===
The main tower that rose from the building housed communication, surveillance, and defense systems including anti-aircraft emplacements and shield generators.<ref name="CL" />

==History==
===Origins===
{{Quote|It was their monastery before Jabba moved in. He's adapted much of their architecture since taking over. See this? That used to be a grotto leading to the catacombs.|[[Canno Wix]]|<ref name="Choose Your Destiny: An Escape Mission">''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Escape Mission]]''</ref>}}
[[File:JabbasPalace-TCWfilm.png|thumb|left|250px|Jabba's Palace in [[22 BBY]]]]
Built by the [[monk]]s in a long-forgotten era,<ref name="217 POV">{{InsiderCite|217|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> Jabba's Palace was a monastery that belonged to the [[B'omarr Order]], a religious community who believed in isolating themselves from all physical stimuli to enhance their mental powers.<ref name="CL" /> To fulfill as such, the monks removed their [[brain]]s and placed them into nutrient jars, which were carried around the monastery in [[droid]] [[walker]]s they controlled.<ref name="217 POV" /> The earliest parts of the monastery were underground, and included a mine. At one point, a bandit named [[Alkhara]] appropriated the monastery for himself. He occupied it for 34 years, during which he added battlements, dungeons, and a [[Alkhara's spire|nine-story tower]], and also cut a road from the citadel to the [[Western Dune Sea]] and [[Great Pit of Carkoon]]. Eventually, he was driven off by Jabba.<ref name="CL" />

After taking over the monastery, Jabba had it further fortified by master armorers. After presenting the Hutt with the bill for their services, the said armorers became the first [[prisoners]] of the crime lord. Despite having designed them all, they were never able to escape the thick walls and cruel traps of the palace.<ref name="beware" /> It was said that the area that became the [[rancor]] pit in Jabba's Palace was once a sacred grotto for the B'omarr monks, who continued to roam the palace in their droid forms during Jabba's reign.<ref name="217 POV" />

===Clone Wars===
[[File:Truce-SWDConv.png|thumb|right|250px|[[Ahsoka Tano]] reunited [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] with [[Rotta|his son]]]]
In [[22 BBY]], [[Jedi Master]] and [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] traveled to Jabba's Palace to assure Jabba that the [[Galactic Republic]] would rescue his son [[Rotta]] and to secure a promise from the Hutts for safe passage for Republic ships through the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. After rescuing Rotta from [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] forces, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] brought him back to Jabba's palace. Since they had failed to deliver the kidnapper, Jabba wanted to execute Skywalker and Tano as well. However, [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] managed to save her comrades' lives by exposing Jabba's uncle [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] as the instigator behind Rotta's kidnapping.<ref name="TCW Movie" />

Following the [[Kidnapping of the Papanoida daughters|kidnapping]] of his daughters in [[21 BBY]], Senator [[Notluwiski Papanoida|Papanoida]] and his son [[Ion Papanoida|Ion]] traveled to Jabba's Palace to confront the [[Rodian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Greedo]], who was implicated in the kidnapping of the Papanoida girls. After a confrontation, Greedo agreed to lead Papanoida and Ion to [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[Mos Eisley]] where one of Papanoida's daughters, [[Che Amanwe Papanoida|Che Amanwe]], was rescued and reunited with her family.<ref name="Sphere of Influence">{{TCW|Sphere of Influence}}</ref>

After kidnapping the [[droid]]s [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]], stealing the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building's]] schematics, and [[Memory wipe|wiping their memories]], the [[Duros]] bounty hunter [[Cad Bane]] traveled to Jabba's Palace to deliver the plans to Jabba. There, the crime lord assigned Jabba on a [[Hostage Crisis|mission]] to free Ziro from Republic imprisonment. Ziro had classified information which the [[Grand Hutt Council]] did not want to fall into the hands of the Republic.<ref name="Evil Plans">{{TCW|Evil Plans}}</ref> After assassinating Ziro, the [[Pa'lowick]] singer [[Sy Snootles]] returned with Ziro's holo-diary to Jabba, keeping Ziro's secrets from both the Republic and the Grand Hutt Council.<ref name="Hunt for Ziro">{{TCW|Hunt for Ziro}}</ref>

In [[19 BBY]], former [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul]] and his [[Shadow Collective]] traveled to Jabba's Palace. After killing most of Jabba's guards, the Shadow Collective forced their way into Jabba's throne. Faced with overwhelming force, Jabba and [[Gorga Desilijic Aarrpo]] decided to join Maul's Shadow Collective.<ref name="Eminence" />

===Imperial era===
====Hiring Black Krrsantan====
[[File:Entourage_SWDConv.png|thumb|left|250px|[[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] entourage surrounds [[Jabba the Hutt's dais|his dais]] in the palace throne room.]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], Jabba the Hutt hosted the [[Wookiee]] bounty hunter [[Krrsantan]], whom he hired to hunt down the [[Great Jedi Purge]] survivor Obi-Wan Kenobi, who had attacked his water tax collectors during the [[Great Drought]]. As a display of his fighting prowess, Krssantan single-handedly shot several of Jabba's guards in his throne room.<ref name="SW15">[[Star Wars (2015) 15|''Star Wars'' (2015) 15]]</ref> Krrsantan tracked down Kenobi but failed to kill him. Fearing Jabba's wrath, the Wookiee fled Tatooine.<ref name="SW20">[[Star Wars (2015) 20|''Star Wars'' (2015) 20]]</ref>

====Imperial entanglements====
{{Quote|I do not know about you, my dear Lord of the Sith, but Jabba the Hutt prefers to seal all his business ventures by watching something die.|Jabba the Hutt|<ref name="SW4">[[Star Wars (2015) 4|''Star Wars'' (2015) 4]]</ref>}}
Following the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Darth]] Vader visited Jabba's Palace in [[0 ABY]] to conclude negotiations regarding the transportation of supplies through [[Hutt Space]] to [[Weapons Factory Alpha]] on the [[moon]] [[Cymoon 1]]. Jabba attempted to claim a bounty on Vader's head and had his underlings attack the [[Sith Lord]]. Using his [[The Force|Force]] powers and [[lightsaber]] skills, [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] slaughtered Jabba's guards and [[Force choke]]d the Hutt [[crime lord]].<ref name="Darth Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2015) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 1]]</ref>

To mend bridges, Jabba agreed to provide Vader with two bounty hunters: [[Boba Fett]] and Black Krssantan. Fett was tasked with hunting down [[Luke Skywalker]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] pilot who destroyed the [[Death Star]], while Krssantan was assigned to bring [[Darth Sidious|the Emperor's]] agent [[Cylo]] to him.<ref name="Darth Vader 1" /> The following day, [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] returned to Jabba's palace and concluded negotiations. Jabba agreed to permit the Empire access to Hutt Space in return for financial compensation. Jabba then took Vader on a tour of Tatooine's Dune Sea aboard the [[sail barge]] ''[[Khetanna]]''.<ref name="SW4" />

====Invaded by a thief====
{{Quote|Zerek Besh has a spy in my palace. Break into the Bestine Compound. Bring me a name.|Jabba the Hutt to Kay Vess and Vail Tormin|<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>|translated=1}}
[[File:KayVessInfiltratesJabbasPalace-Outlaws.png|thumb|250x250px|Kay Vess approaches the Palace.]]
When [[Nix]], a creature of the rare [[merqaal]] species, was stolen and ended up in Jabba's possession, criminal [[Kay Vess]] broke into Jabba's Palace to recover her companion. Nix showed aggression towards Jabba by attacking him. After a conflict, Jabba decided to send Vess along with [[Vail Tormin]], a bounty hunter who was after Vess and tried to escape from the Palace with her in a [[WTK-85A interstellar transport]], to discover the identity of a spy that the crime syndicate [[Zerek Besh]] placed in Jabba's Palace. In return, Jabba would forgive the two of them for the confusion at the Palace, return Nix, and allow the two of them to leave Tatooine alive. The mission was a success and the spy was discovered. After Vess obtained compromising information against [[Governor]] [[Thorden]] of the [[moon]] [[Toshara]], at the request of Jabba, who intended to use it to gain an advantage over the [[Pyke Syndicate]], Vess delivered the information to Jabba at his palace.<ref name="Star Wars Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

====Rescuing Han Solo====
[[File:BountyHunters30-textless-Sprouse.png|thumb|220px|C-3PO and R2-D2 approach Jabba's Palace|left]]
[[Luke Skywalker]] and his friends hatched a plan to [[Rescue of Han Solo|rescue]] [[Han Solo]], who was [[Carbon-freezing|frozen]] in [[carbonite]] and kept by Jabba as a wall decoration in the palace.<ref name="Episode VI" /> First, bounty hunter [[Beilert Valance]] [[Assault on Jabba's Palace|openly attacked]] Jabba's Palace to trick the Hutt into unleashing his [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure's Megadroid|Megadroid guardian]]. With the droid destroyed in the ensuing [[Death Match on Tatooine]] by Valance and [[T'onga's crew]], Skywalker and company were set to have an easier time breaking Solo out.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 42">[[Bounty Hunters 42|''Bounty Hunters'' 42]]</ref> In time, a disguised [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] infiltrated the palace<ref name="Episode VI" /> as a new skiff guard. Shortly into his stay at the palace, Calrissian allowed [[Valnir Nai]], a waitresses forced into Jabba's service, to escape his watch with a set of valuable gems stolen from the Hutt's tribute. While they lost the gems in a struggle with the [[Rodian]] enforcer [[Raymo]], Nai and her friend [[Laizhu Bedenn]] both managed to escape the palace for new horizons.<ref name="Valnir and Laizhu">{{InsiderCite|222|Galactic Tales: Valnir and Laizhu}}</ref>

In time, the rebel droids C-3PO and R2-D2 traveled to Jabba's Palace under Luke Skywalker's instructions as part of the rescue plan. Via hologram, Skywalker offered the droids as a gift in an attempt to secure the release of Solo. As expected, Jabba refused and pressed the droids into his service. Later, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] arrived disguised as the bounty hunter [[Boushh]] with the captive Wookiee [[Chewbacca]], Solo's co-pilot.<ref name="Episode VI" />

Leia managed to free Han from carbonite but was captured by Jabba. The following day, Skywalker arrived and reiterated his demand to free Solo and his friends. Jabba refused and threw Skywalker down the trapdoor to the rancor<ref name="Episode VI" /> [[Pateesa]],<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> who also killed the [[Gamorrean]] guard [[Jubnuk]]. Skywalker managed to kill Pateesa by collapsing the dividing door on top of the rancor. Jabba attempted to feed Skywalker, Solo, and Chewbacca to the [[sarlacc]] at the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]]. However, the rebels escaped and killed Jabba and his henchmen, dealing a blow to his criminal empire.<ref name="Episode VI" />

===Beyond Jabba===
[[File:BibsThroneRoom-TMch16.png|thumb|300px|Bib Fortuna took Jabba's place as lord of the palace for a time.]]
Following the death of Jabba, several of his employees including the [[beastmaster]] [[Malakili]] stayed at the palace waiting for a new Hutt to occupy the dais. As the food dwindled and water ran out, many of Jabba's former employees abandoned the castle. Malakili was one of the last to leave. He subsequently joined a community called [[Freetown]], which was led by the lawman and Mayor [[Cobb Vanth]], who enlisted Malakili's services in taming two [[ronto]]s and a Huttlet named [[Borgo]]. The [[Red Key Raiders|Red Key]] crime syndicate had attempted to install the Huttlet in Jabba's palace but Cobb foiled their plan.<ref name="Life Debt" />

The B'omarr Order was known to have reinstated control over the Palace sometime after Jabba's death. They allowed the bounty hunter [[J'Quille]] to join them during that time.<ref name="CE:UaE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>

By [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|16|the [[Attack on Jabba's Palace (Boba Fett)|attack on Jabba's Palace]]}}</ref> [[Bib Fortuna]] had succeeded Jabba as master of the palace. Boba Fett and his partner [[Fennec Shand]] [[Firespray heist|infiltrated]] the palace and retrieved his ship, the ''[[Slave I]]'', which was kept in the garage of the palace.<ref name="GatheringStorm">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref> Later, after [[Second rescue of Grogu|settling]] their debt to [[Din Djarin]], Fett and Shand [[Attack on Jabba's Palace (Boba Fett)|attacked]] the palace again, killing Fortuna and the guards, before Fett took the throne.<ref name="Rescue" />

[[File:Boba_and_fennec_on_the_throne.png|thumb|300px|Boba Fett took the throne at Jabba's Palace after killing Bib Fortuna|left]]
The B'omarr Monks continued to walk about the Palace premises during Fett's reign.<ref name="217 POV" /> Fett, who became the [[Daimyo]] of [[Mos Espa]], received guests and accepted tributes in the throne room with Shand by his side. Fett utilized a [[bacta tank]] in the palace to heal from his scars.<ref name="Stranger" /> After Fett and Shand were [[Ambush outside of the Sanctuary|ambushed]] in Mos Espa by six assassins of the [[Order of the Night Wind]]. Shand captured [[Unidentified Night Wind assassin|one]] of the attackers alive and brought him to the palace. After the assassin refused to give up any information to Fett, Shand dropped the prisoner to the rancor pit. The assassin, believing a rancor was in the pit, succumbed to his fear and claimed that [[Mayor of Mos Espa|Mayor]] [[Mok Shaiz]] hired him. The Night Wind assassin, though, had been fooled, as there was no rancor behind the door.<ref name="Tribes">{{BOBF|Chapter 2: The Tribes of Tatooine}}</ref>

Later on, [[8D8]] gave Fett a presentation on the history of the territories of Jabba the Hutt and Bib Fortuna. As Fett and Shand discussed the assassin attack before, [[Unidentified Gamorrean guard 1|one]] of the Gamorrean guards came with news that one of Fett's vassals was wanting to speak with the Daimyo. Fett let the visitor enter the thrown room. The visitor introduced himself as a [[Water monger|water-monger]] named [[Lortha Peel]]. 8D8 asked what Peel's petition to Fett was, to which Peel claimed that no one respected the new Daimyo. The droid angrily stopped the vendor, but Fett told his assistant to let Peel speak. Peel and the Daimyo then discussed the disorder amongst the residents of Mos Espa and the water-monger asked for Fett to help get rid of the [[Mods]], a group of [[Cybernetics|cybernetically]] enhanced individuals that were stealing from him. Fett visited the mods, but after hearing their situation, he chose to hire the gang members instead.<ref name="Streets" />

Later, the bounty hunter [[Krrsantan]] infiltrated the palace while Fett was in his bacta tank and attempted to [[Assassination attempt on Boba Fett|assassinate]] Fett. Krrsantan failed and was captured.<ref name="Streets" />

==Behind the scenes==
Jabba's card from the digital card game ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' described Jabba's Palace as having been built by Jabba himself using his cleverness and wealth.<ref name="Jabba's Card">{{ForceCollection|Jabba the Hutt|star=3}}</ref> In contrast, the [[2015]] book ''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]'' described Jabba's Palace as having been an old monastery of the B'omarr Order which Jabba fortified and refitted, thus creating a continuity error.<ref name="beware" /> [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], published in 2016, supports the latter history,<ref name="CL" /> as does the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''The Clone Wars'' film]].<ref name="TCW Movie" />
