The '''Comet Runners''' were a [[pirate]] group led by the [[Palliduvan]] [[Corr Taldaan]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Team Up" />

==History==
During the [[High Republic Era]] ,the Comet Runners were led by the [[Palliduvan]] [[Corr Taldaan]]. Other members included the [[Theelin]] [[Gossi]], the [[Nosaurian]] [[Slegg]], and the [[droid]] [[LE-47]]. They were active on the [[planet]] [[Yarrum]].<ref name="Team Up" />

At some point when Taldaan and the Comet Runners were present at [[Yarrum Tower]] they came across the pirate [[Cyrus Vuundir|Taborr Val Dorn]] who was showing off [[Jedi]] artifacts he stole from the droid [[0G-LC]]. The Comet Runners stole the artifacts from Val Dorn and planned to sell them on [[Crescent Island]], although they were thwarted by Val Dorn along with [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], and [[Nubs]].<ref name="Team Up" />

Later, the Comet Runners raided a [[Unidentified village (Vuundalla)|village]] on the planet [[Vuundalla]] and stole the villagers' herd of [[fathier]]s. They encountered opposition from the three Jedi younglings and [[Prince]] [[Cyrus Vuundir]] but managed to get away and capture Brightstar, Solay and Nubs. Unknown to the Jedi and the Comet Runners, Vuundir was the pirate Taborr Val Dorn. Taborr summoned his [[Taborr's gang|gang]] associates [[Pord]] and [[EB-3]]. The two freed the Jedi while Taborr defeated the Comet Runners and warned them that Vuundalla was his "turf."<ref name="Prince of Masks">{{YJA|The Prince of Masks}}</ref>

Taldaan later sought to curry favor with the [[Ganguls]], another pirate gang who had taken over [[Yarrum Tower]]. When the Ganguls' leader [[Sellaccc Orryak]] asked if the Comet Runners had come to pay tribute, Taldaan convinced the Ganguls to raid the Vuundalla royal palace. While the Comet Runners raided the Vuundalla village, the Ganguls raided the palace and stole the jewels. The Comet Runners and Ganguls returned to Yarrum Tower to celebrate. Taborr pursued the pirates to Yarrum and revealed his true identity as Cyrus Vuundir. Despite the revelation of Vuundir's true identity, the Jedi younglings Brightstar, Solay and Nubs were unwilling to give up on him and came to rescue him. The Comet Runners fought the Jedi younglings, who used their [[The Force|Force powers]] to hurl the pirates against the Gangugls' throne. However, Vuundir chose not to escape with his Jedi friends since Brightstar wanted him to drop his pirate ambitions.<ref name="Unmasked">{{YJA|Unmasked}}</ref>

Later, the Comet Runners raided [[Zepher's shop]] on the [[moon]] [[Dedoon]] where they tried to steal a [[coaxium]] package from the Jedi younglings and their friend [[Zepher]]. Following a brief skirmish, the pirates managed to trap their opponents in Zepher's store and flee to their ship. Using the Force to arrange the crates into a stack, the Jedi younglings pursued the pirates to their starship. Followed a brief skirmish, they recovered the coaxium package and escaped.<ref name="Just Like West">{{YJA|Just Like Wes}}</ref>

The Comet Runners joined the Ganguls and other pirate factions including [[Blackbolt]] and [[Blackbolt's crew|his crew]] in attacking the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple]] during the [[Battle of Tenoo]]. Taldaan and his team participated in the space battle. Following the destruction of the Ganguls' flagship ''[[Rathtar's Revenge]]'' by Taborr and [[Padawan|Jedi Padawan]] [[Wes Vinik]], Taldaan and the other pirates abandoned the battle and retreated.<ref name="Battle of Tenoo">{{YJA|The Battle of Tenoo}}</ref>
