'''Crab Puffs''' were [[Foodstuff|puff pastries]] stuffed with living [[crab]]s, originating on<ref name="LDCookbook" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Mon Cala]]. They were [[Diet|eaten]] by the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Mon Calamari]] in order to celebrate [[Life Day]],<ref name="LDCookbook" /> a [[The galaxy|galaxy]]-wide holiday of [[Wookiee]] culture celebrating family, joy, and harmony.<ref name="LDDatabank">{{Databank|life-day|Life Day}}</ref> Sometime before the publication of ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'', which took place after its [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|predecessor's]] release<ref name="LDCookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> [[author]] [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] traveled to Mon Cala in order to fix his [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport]]. While on the planet, he visited a [[Unidentified waterin' hole|watering hole]] where a [[Unidentified Mon Calamari chef|Mon Calamari chef]] gave him the recipe for the dish. The recipe called for live crabs, but Tuggs preferred his crab deep-fried, so he would later publish a deep-fried variation in ''The Life Day Cookbook'' instead of the original.<ref name="LDDatabank" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], crab puffs were mentioned in the recipe book ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'', authored by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] and published in [[2021]]. The book includes an [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the puff-pastry. The ingredients are lumps of [[Wikipedia:Crab meat|crab meat]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic cloves]], [[Wikipedia:Egg as food|egg]], [[Wikipedia:Mayonnaise|mayonnaise]], [[Wikipedia:Parmesan|parmesan cheese]], [[Wikipedia:Lemon|lemon juice]], [[Wikipedia:Onion powder|onion powder]], [[Wikipedia:Paprika|paprika]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], [[Wikipedia:Bread crumbs|bread crumbs]], and [[Wikipedia:Parsley|parsley]].<ref name="LDCookbook" />

[[File:TinyDeathStar-CrabCreampuff.png|thumb|right|70px|alt=|A pixelated yellow circle with a white line through the middle against a gray background.|Crab-stuffed creampuffs as depicted in ''Tiny Death Star'']]
In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[crab-stuffed creampuff]]s appeared in the tenth installment of [[John Whitman]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Fear]]'' young reader series, [[Galaxy of Fear: The Doomsday Ship|''The Doomsday Ship'']], published in [[1998]].<ref name="Doom">''[[Galaxy of Fear: The Doomsday Ship]]''</ref> They were pictured and appeared in the [[2013]] non-canon [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Tiny Death Star]]'', developed by [[Disney Mobile]]. They were depicted as two buns with white filling sandwiched in-between and were served in the "Mon Cala Seafood" food level in the [[Death Star/Legends|Death Star I]] [[Battle station/Legends|battle station]]. They cost 1,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]] and took an hour and forty minutes to prepare.<ref name="TDS">''[[Star Wars: Tiny Death Star]]''</ref>
