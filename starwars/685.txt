'''685''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] that was stationed at a [[Separatist outpost (Orto Plutonia)|base]] on [[Orto Plutonia]] during the [[Clone Wars]] that was destroyed in a [[skirmish on Orto Plutonia]].

==Biography==
685 was a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[B1-series battle droid]] during the [[Clone Wars]] During the early stages of the conflict, the Separatists established a [[Separatist outpost (Orto Plutonia)|base of operations]] on the snowy [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Orto Plutonia]] in the [[Pantora system]], that was orbited by the [[moon]] of [[Pantora]]. The [[Galactic Republic]] also established their own outpost, [[Glid Station]], at the behest of the [[Pantoran Assembly]]. After [[Time|sometime]], the Separatist activity provoked the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Talz]], resulting in a [[Skirmish on Orto Plutonia|skirmish]] that inadvertently resulted in Glid Station being caught in the conflict.<ref name="Trespass" />

[[File:TheDoomThatCameTo685-Trespass.png|thumb|right|220px|685 about to be attacked by a Talz]]
During the skirmish, the Talz attacked the Separatist outpost forcing 685 to contact command that they were being overrun before being destroyed. By the end of the skirmish, both Separatist and Republic forces were annihilated. After the Republic lost communications with Glid Station, a team that consisted of [[clone cold assault trooper]]s and led by [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], and accompanied by members of the Pantoran Assembly led by [[Chairman]] [[Chi Cho]], arrived at Gild Station to investigate.<ref name="Trespass" />

During their investigation, they discovered the Separatist outpost and proceeded to inspect the base. During the analyzation of the base, they discovered a [[hologram]] recording of 685's last moments before being destroyed by a Talz.<ref name="Trespass" />
