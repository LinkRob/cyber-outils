'''Elsa Charretier''' is a [[Wikipedia:France|French]] comic book writer, illustrator and comic book artist who has worked on a number of ''[[Star Wars]]'' projects so far, mainly [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] from [[IDW Publishing]] and its spin-off miniseries [[Star Wars: Forces of Destiny (comic series)|''Star Wars: Forces of Destiny'']].
