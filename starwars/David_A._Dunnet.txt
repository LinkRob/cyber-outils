'''David A. Dunnet''' was the background [[Wikipedia:animation|animation]] supervisor for the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' television series.<ref name="CW1">[[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume One|''Star Wars: Clone Wars'' Volume One]]</ref> He was also a layout design artist for the [[Wikipedia:1960s|1960s]] series [[Wikipedia:The Jetsons|The Jetsons]], and he won a Primetime [[Wikipedia:Emmy|Emmy]] in [[Wikipedia:2004|2004]] for his work as a background designer for the [[Wikipedia:Foster's Home for Imaginary Friends|Foster's Home for Imaginary Friends]] series.
