The '''Aquilaean campaign''' was a conflict between the [[Aquilaean Starforce]] led by [[Luke Skywalker (The Star Wars)|Luke Skywalker]] against the [[Galactic Empire (The Star Wars)|New Galactic Empire]] led by [[Knights of Sith]].

==Behind the scenes==
The Aquilaean campaign was a part of the [[The Star Wars rough draft|first script]] of [[George Lucas]], written in [[1974]].
