'''Falumpaset cheese''' was a type of [[cheese]] used to make the side dish [[mashed chokeroot]], a component of the meal [[Fried Endorian Tip-Yip]]. [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included the recipes in his [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]. The cheese could be found on the [[planet]]s [[Batuu]] and [[Saleucami]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. [[Webb Taffral]] once fell face-first into a bowl of mashed chokeroot. The [[tip-yip]] meal was eaten by the [[human]]s [[Vi Moradi]] and [[Julen Rakab]], by [[Izal Garsea]], and by the [[Togruta]] [[Neelo]].

==Description==
Falumpaset cheese was an ingredient in [[mashed chokeroot]], a side dish that was a component of the entrée [[Fried Endorian Tip-Yip]]; the [[cheese]] and some [[Color|orange]]-colored simple [[spice]]s helped to bring out the sweetness of the otherwise bland [[chokeroot]]. Falumpaset cheese shared its name with<ref name="Cookbook" /> [[falumpaset]]s, a [[species]] of domesticated animals from [[Naboo]].<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|gungan-grand-army|Gungan Grand Army}}</ref> It could be found on<ref name="Cookbook" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]s<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[Batuu]] and [[Saleucami]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

==History==
[[File:Mashed_chokeroot.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Mashed chokeroot was a dish made with falumpaset cheese.]]
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date: Aftermath">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> mashed chokeroot was served at the [[Taffral house]] on Saleucami during a [[Taffral family|family]] dinner. The dinner was interrupted when [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Starfighter|fighters]] flew over the Taffral residence due to [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] loyalist [[Webb Taffral]] alerting [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] to the location of his [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel-aligned]] brother, [[Dav Taffral|Dav]]. Their [[Parent|father]], [[Glen Taffral|Glen]], [[stun]]ned Webb with a [[blaster]], causing him to fall face-first into a bowl of mashed chokeroot.<ref name="Aftermath" />

Sometime after the [[Battle of Takodana]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] had launched his mobile restaurant, [[Tuggs' Grub]], which he operated out of a modified [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|transport shuttle]]. Around that time, chokeroot was considered an old family standard on many worlds, but Tuggs thought the [[Plant|root]] lacked flavor and was not suitable for use in his cooking. Upon a visit to Saleucami, he discovered a [[cream]]y mashed chokeroot recipe that used falumpaset cheese and spices to enhance the underlying sweetness of chokeroot. When Tuggs wrote his [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]], he included the recipe for mashed chokeroot and his entrée that included it, Fried Endorian Tip-Yip.<ref name="Cookbook" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date: GE">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of both ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' and [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Tuggs brought Tuggs' Grub to [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet Batuu and worked as one of the rotating guest chefs at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]].<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> During one of his [[Standard month|monthly]] [[Standard week|week-long]] visits, [[Vi Moradi]], a [[human]] [[Resistance]] [[spy]], ate Fried Endorian Tip-Yip at Tuggs' Grub.<ref name="GE:BS">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref> In the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="ACOFDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[farmer]] [[Julen Rakab]] and the [[smuggler]] [[Izal Garsea]] also ate the meal when Tuggs served it to them. After Garsea left the table, the [[Togruta]] [[musician]] [[Neelo]] helped himself to Rakab's dish.<ref name="A Crash of Fate">''[[A Crash of Fate]]''</ref> In his book ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', the adventurer [[Eloc Throno]] described the meal, but referred to mashed chokeroot as "mashed [[vegetable]]s."<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Falumpaset cheese first appeared, albeit unnamed and retroactively, as an ingredient within the dish mashed chokeroot in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> Mashed chokeroot is a component of the entrée Fried Endorian Tip-Yip,<ref name="Cookbook" /> both of which were first made available to guests in the restaurant Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], a themed attraction that opened at [[Disneyland]] on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref>

Falumpaset cheese was first identified by name in the 2019 cookbook ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', written by [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and [[Marc Sumerak]], which also establishes it as an ingredient in mashed chokeroot. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] mashed chokeroot recipe uses [[Wikipedia:Parmigiano-Reggiano|parmesan cheese]] as an ingredient, and the recipe for Fried Endorian Tip-Yip allows for [[Wikipedia:Mashed potato|mashed potatoes]] to be used as a substitute for mashed chokeroot.<ref name="Cookbook" />
