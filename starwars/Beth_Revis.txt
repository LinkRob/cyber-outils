'''Beth Revis''' is an author of multiple ''[[Star Wars]]'' novels, short stories, and [[Star Wars comic books|comic books]]. In [[2017]], she wrote the novel ''[[Rebel Rising]]''<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> and the short story "[[Fully Operational (short story)|Fully Operational]]," which was published in the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''.<ref name="FACPOV">''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''</ref> She wrote the [[2018]] comic book ''[[Forces of Destiny—Ahsoka & Padmé]]'', an issue of the miniseries [[Star Wars: Forces of Destiny (comic series)|''Star Wars: Forces of Destiny'']].<ref name="FOD: AP">''[[Forces of Destiny—Ahsoka & Padmé]]''</ref> For the [[2020]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]'', she wrote "[[For the Last Time]]."<ref name="FACPOV: TESB">''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''</ref> She is the author of the [[2022]] novel ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''.<ref name="TPATS">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>
