'''Feral''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Dathomirian]] [[Zabrak]] member of the [[Nightbrothers]], a group of [[Soldier|warriors]] subservient to the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Dathomiri clan|clan]] of [[witch]]es [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]]. He was the younger brother of [[Savage Opress]] and [[Darth]] [[Maul]], and a son of Nightsister leader, Mother [[Talzin]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], Feral was among those chosen by Nightsister [[Asajj Ventress]] to become her champion in her campaign of vengeance against her former [[Sith Master]], [[Dooku|Count Dooku]]. Feral survived the tests with Savage's help, but ultimately Savage was chosen as the champion, and soon thereafter as a test of loyalty, Talzin made Savage kill Feral.

==Biography==

[[Reproduction|Born]] on the<ref name="Join the Battle" /> [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="GalaxyMap">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> [[Dathomir]]<ref name="Join the Battle" /> sometime after [[54 BBY]],<ref name="Birth" /> Feral was the son of [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]],<ref name="Mother" /> leader of the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Dathomiri clan|clan]] of [[Dathomirian]] [[witch]]es.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> The youngest of three brothers, Feral's two older brothers were [[Maul]] and [[Savage Opress]].<ref name="DMAODSF">{{EncyclopediaCite|Darth Maul and Other Followers of the Dark Side}}</ref> Around [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Maul was taken from Talzin by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] who originally promised to make Talzin his [[Sith apprentice]].
Talzin swore revenge on the [[Sith]]<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Three">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 3|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 3]]</ref> and Savage grew up without knowledge of his other brother's existence.<ref name="Witches">{{TCW|Witches of the Mist}}</ref> 

Growing up absent from their brother, Savage and Feral passed the necessary rituals to become members of the [[Nightbrothers]], a group of [[Gender|male]] Dathomirian [[Soldier|warriors]] subservient to the Nightsisters, and lived in the [[Nightbrother village]] where they trained.<ref name="DMAODSF" /> Part of the [[Viscus' clan|clan]] led by Brother [[Viscus]],<ref name="Monster" /> Savage eventually obtained a leadership position and shared his responsibilities with Feral,<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Savage Opress - Sith Apprentice}}</ref> who he took care of and protected.<ref name="HC42">{{HelmetCollectionCite|42|Databank A-Z}}</ref>

===Selection and Death===
In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="SWT" /> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Asajj Ventress]] visited the Nightbrothers to find a champion, one who could be given advanced strength and power through [[magick]] to aid her in her quest for vengeance against her former master, [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. A number of Nightbrothers were chosen to participate in the [[Selection|tests]] designed to find Ventress' champion.<ref name="Monster" />

[[File:SavageStranglesFeral-Monster.png|thumb|right|250px|Feral's death at the hands of his brother]]
With Savage's help, Feral survived all three tests. Savage pleaded for mercy for his brother, and Feral was spared despite failing in the final challenge. Savage became the champion and was taken by Ventress and given enhanced strength and power, enough to rival that of a [[Jedi Knight]]. Shortly thereafter, Feral was taken prisoner by the Nightsisters and brought before his brother so Savage could kill him as a test of loyalty and strength.<ref name="Monster" /> Upon seeing Savage's swelled up muscles and rage from Talzin's magicks,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Feral looked up in shock at his brother. He tried to appeal to Savage's love for him, but his brother's mind had become twisted by the Nightsister magick. Savage, disgusted by Feral's weakness, ignored the pleas. At Ventress' command, and with only a moment's hesitation, Savage grabbed Feral and used his newfound strength to break Feral's neck, snuffing out his brother's life and passing his final test.<ref name="Monster" />

==Personality and traits==
Feral was a Dathomirian [[Zabrak]] male,<ref name="Monster" /> who was 1.86 [[meter]]s, or 6 [[Foot|feet]], 1 inch tall,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> with [[Color|yellow]] [[eye]]s and yellow skin with brown [[tattoo]]s. He was the younger brother of Savage Opress and Maul.<ref name="Graphics" /> Feral was often confident and optimistic in spite of his chances, often shrugging off Savage's warnings, believing himself to be a vigilant warrior. However, he quickly learned of his combat faults in his first fight against Asajj Ventress. During the Selection, Savage was able to repeatedly protect Feral, but Feral's reliance on Savage would be his undoing as he was killed by Savage's bare hands after his display of weakness.<ref name="Monster" />

==Skills and abilities==
Feral was a powerful warrior, skilled in martial arts. His agility<ref name="Join the Battle" /> also helped keep him alive for most of the Selection, as he was able to dodge the [[weapon]]s of Ventress and other competitors, as well as move quickly leap across the ledges during the final challenge.<ref name="Monster" />

==Equipment==
Feral wore simple rags matching those of his Nightbrother brethren and wielded crude, archaic weaponry.<ref name="Monster" />

==Behind the scenes==
Feral first appeared in "[[Monster]],"<ref name="Monster" /> the thirteenth episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which released on [[January 14]], [[2011]].<ref name="EpGuide">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> Feral was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="Monster" />
