The '''Colony of ''Kezarat''''', also known as the '''''Kezarat'' Colony''', was a group of individuals who lived amongst the [[Kezarat Convoy|''Kezarat'' Convoy]] located in [[No-Space]]. Members of the colony included its [[captain]] [[Blythe]] and his son [[Forvan]].<ref name="Star Wars 31" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars (2020) 30|''Star Wars'' (2020) 30]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> the colony was encountered by members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], who sought the location of the ''Kezarat'' Convoy so they could use it to [[fuel]] the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Rebel Fleet]].<ref name="Star Wars 30" />
