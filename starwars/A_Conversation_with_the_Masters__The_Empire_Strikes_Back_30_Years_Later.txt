'''A Conversation with the Masters: The Empire Strikes Back 30 Years Later''' (2010, Color, Apx. 25 Minutes) – [[George Lucas]], [[Irvin Kershner]], [[Lawrence Kasdan]] and [[John Williams]] look back on the making of ''The Empire Strikes Back'' in this in-depth retrospective from [[Lucasfilm]] created to help commemorate the 30th anniversary of the movie. The masters discuss and reminisce about one of the most beloved films of all time. It was included in the Blue-ray release of ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''.
