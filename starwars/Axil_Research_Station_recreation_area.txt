The '''Axil Research Station recreation area''' was a section of the [[Axil Research Station]]. It had transparent walls that allowed visitors to see the ocean of [[Tordus]]. It was the site for a meeting between the [[Tordusian Government]] and [[Lady]] [[Hellan Valk]]'s group of protestors.<ref name="TT1" />
