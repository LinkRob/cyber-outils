'''E'roni ironwood''' was a type of [[ironwood]] from the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]] that could be used to make tables.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while visiting the planet, [[Galactic Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Orlen Mollo]] sat at a table made of polished E'roni ironwood with [[Monarch of E'ronoh|Monarch]] [[A'lbaran]] of E'ronoh.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Behind the scenes==
E'roni ironwood appeared in [[The High Republic: Cataclysm|''Cataclysm'']], a novel written by [[Lydia Kang]] and published in [[2023]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Cataclysm" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase II">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-cataclysm-excerpt|text=The Jedi Arrive at Dalna in ''The High Republic: Cataclysm'' - Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
