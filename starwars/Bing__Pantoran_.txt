'''Bing''' was a [[Pantoran]] [[Gender|male]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. He was once a friend of [[Alees June]]. One night he met with Alees and her friends in pub and wanted to give him what she owed.
After Alees said she had given it, he wanted to hit her. But the Jedi Padawan [[Amadeo Azzazzo]] caught his arm and forced him to his knees.<ref name="TCOC" />
