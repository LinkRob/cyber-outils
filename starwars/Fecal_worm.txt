'''Fecal worms''' were a type of [[worm]] with [[Color|pink]] segmented bodies. The worms were one of the favorite [[Foodstuff|foods]] of the [[frog-dog]] [[Buboicullaar]], pet of the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]. When Buboicullaar's carer, [[Ree-Yees]], lost the frog-dog aboard Jabba's [[sail barge]],<ref name="Dirty" /> ''[[Khetanna]]'',<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> at some point prior to the barge's [[Rescue of Han Solo|destruction]] in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> he prepared a plate of steaming fecal worms mixed with [[bone]]s into a green substance in order to try and lure the missing pet out. Buboicullaar then [[Diet|ate]] the plate full of worms when Ree-Yees's back was turned and disappeared again before the carer had noticed.<ref name="Dirty" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fecal worms were created for "[[Tales of Villainy: Done Dirty]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic story]] written by [[Anne Toole]] and illustrated by [[Nick Brokenshire]] that was published in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 11|eleventh issue]] of [[IDW Publishing]]'s [[Star Wars Adventures (2020)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] comic series<ref name="Dirty" /> on [[October 20]], [[2021]].<ref name="PreviewsWorld Cover A">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2020-11/digital-comic/957990|text=Star Wars Adventures (2020-) #11|nolive=1}}</ref>
