'''Bonshyyyr''' was a [[tree]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="HNN">{{HoloNetNewsTumblr|post/92683360686/holonet-news-report-wookiee-revolt-quelled-on|Report: Wookiee Revolt Quelled on Kashyyyk|int=HoloNet News Report: Wookiee Revolt Quelled on Kashyyyk}}</ref> [[forest]] [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]]. The tree grew in the [[mountain]]s of Kashyyyk such as those on which the [[Imperial Refinery]], a large refinery in which the [[Galactic Empire]] harvested [[wroshyr tree]] [[Wroshyr sap|sap]], was located. They had [[Color|green]] leaves.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> when the [[Stinger Mantis crew|crew]] of the [[S-161 "Stinger" XL|luxury yacht]] ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'' undertook a [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|mission]] to rebuild the [[Jedi Order]], crew member and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] [[Liberation on Kashyyyk|infiltrated]] the Imperial Refinery on Kashyyyk. While inside the refinery, Kestis collected a Bonshyyyr [[seed]]. When he returned to the ''Mantis'', he planted the seed in his companion [[Greez Dritus]]' [[terrarium]] aboard the ship. The seed was one of several that Kestis had collected in order to repay Dritus for his assistance during the ''Mantis'' crew's mission.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
{{EndGame}}
