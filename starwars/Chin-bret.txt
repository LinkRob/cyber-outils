'''Chin-bret''' was a sport, played at the [[Junior Academy of Applied Sciences]] on [[Lothal]] during the [[spring]]. It was played every [[Standard week|weekend]]. [[Zare Leonis]] wanted to join the school's chin-bret or track and field squad, but was ultimately prevented from participating in either sport by [[athletic director]] [[Janus Fhurek]], through Coach [[Ramset]], who had given him many [[demerit]]s for nonsensical violations.<ref name="edge">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref>
