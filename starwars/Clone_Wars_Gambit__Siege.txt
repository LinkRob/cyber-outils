'''''Clone Wars Gambit: Siege''''' is a novel tie-in to [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars'']] television series. It was written by [[Karen Miller]] and was released on [[July 6]], [[2010]]. The audio version is narrated by [[Jeff Gurner]]. The book also contains a preview of ''[[The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance]]''.

==Publisher's summary==
'''On the Outer Rim, the planet Lanteeb has no strategic value, no political power, and one enormous problem: it has been invaded by an emboldened Separatist Alliance. To find out why, Jedi Knights Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi have snuck onto Lanteeb &ndash; and now look oblivion in the eye&hellip;'''

''Hiding their lightsabers beneath their dusty disguises, Anakin and Obi-Wan draw on their Jedi skills to stay one step ahead of Lok Durd's droid army on Lanteeb. The Jedi know that a captive scientist has given Durd the keys to a terrifying bioweapon. Durd knows that the Jedi are on his planet. With Yoda calling on the powers of the Jedi Council, with a new Separatist technology jamming the Guardians' communications, and a traitor at the heart of the Republic's government, the wheels of war are turning. But the Separatists have blockaded Lanteeb. The finishing touches are being put on a weapon to destroy whole worlds. And it will be up to the two Jedi Knights and their most trusted comrades to liberate Lanteeb or forever suffer the consequences.''

==Plot summary==
[[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan]] flee into the countryside to the [[damotite]]-[[Mining/Legends|mining]] [[City/Legends|town]] of [[Torbel]]. Still posing as locals, they work in the mines and save the down during an intense [[Radiation/Legends|radiation]] storm. Anakin’s use of the [[The Force/Legends|Force]] in doing so alerts the locals that they are [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]]. Though the townspeople are by their nature wary, the selflessness displayed by the Jedi earns their loyalty. Meanwhile, [[Ahsoka Tano/Legends|Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] [[Taria Damsin]] journey to [[Corellia/Legends|Corellia]] to ensure the safety of [[Bant'ena Fhernan|Fhernan]]’s [[Parent/Legends|mother]]. Similar missions are carried out across the [[The galaxy/Legends|galaxy]], and the Jedi secure the rest of Fhernan’s loved ones. [[Lok Durd/Legends|Durd]], however, continues to pressure her, using [[Hologram/Legends|holographic]] illusions to make her think they are still in jeopardy. Desperate to prove the [[Damotite bioweapon|damotite weapon]]’s effectiveness, Durd impetuously launches and [[Attack on Chandrila (Clone Wars)|attack on Chandrila]] with it, [[Death/Legends|killing]] thousands.

The [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] launches a limited [[Task force/Legends|task force]] of three [[Starship/Legends|vessels]] to [[Lanteeb/Legends|Lanteeb]]. [[Senator/Legends|Senators]] [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Bail Prestor Organa/Legends|Bail Organa]], on their own initiative bolsters this [[Fleet/Legends|fleet]] with volunteer civilian ships. Taria Damsin soars past the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Separatist]] [[Blockade/Legends|blockade]] of Lanteeb to the land on the surface. She contacts Fhernan, revealing the safety of her loved ones. Fhernan destroys the samples of the [[Bioweapon/Legends|bioweapon]], sacrificing herself as penance for the [[Chandrilan/Legends|Chandrilan]] massacre. Durd orders his [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]] to lay siege to Torbel. Anakin jury-rigs the town’s storm [[Deflector shield generator/Legends|shield]] to deflect the enemy [[Droid/Legends|droids]]. Taria reunites with Obi-Wan, and Anakin discovers Obi-Wan has feelings for this Jedi, a close colleague he has known for most of his life. Noting this strong personal bond between them, Anakin can’t help but see hypocrisy in the Jedi strictures against romantic commitment.

The Republic overpowers the Separatist blockade, but Durd escapes with the fleeing Confederacy vessels. With the help of the villagers, the Republic is able to synthetize an [[Antidote/Legends|antidote]] to the bioweapon, nullifying its threat.

==Continuity== 
The [[Outer Rim Sieges/Legends|Outer Rim sieges]] are mentioned in the novels. During the production of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|Episode III]], this campaign – mentioned in the script – was at one point described as starting within six [[Standard month/Legends|months]] of [[Clone Wars/Legends|war]]’s [[Mission to Mustafar/Legends|end]] ([[20 BBY/Legends|20]]-[[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]]). The book seem to take place earlier than that, in [[21 BBY/Legends|21 BBY]], given that Ahsoka is described as only serving under Anakin for months, and she’s hesitant about repairing technology – a skill she possesses in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|animated series]]. Furthermore, Obi-Wan is depicted as not being on the [[Jedi High Council/Legends|Jedi Council]], though he is seen with a Council seat in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second-season]] episode "[[Senate Spy]]". Lok Durd is a villain from the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] episode “[[Defenders of Peace]]”.<ref name="TERC" />
