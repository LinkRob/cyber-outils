{{Quote|This is an electrostaff, Nobody's Padawan. A useful tool&mdash;and one designed to kill Jedi.|Sarco Plank, to Luke Skywalker|<ref name="The Weapon of a Jedi">''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]''</ref>}}
An '''electrostaff''', also known as a '''shock staff''',<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> an '''electro-pole'''<ref name="Epic Battles">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Visual Guide: Epic Battles]]''</ref> or a '''Force Lance''',<ref name="GOH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> was a [[staff]]-like [[melee weapon]] manufactured by [[Baktoid Armor Workshop]] and [[Holowan Mechanicals]]. They were used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> by the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Clone trooper|clone]] [[Imperial shock trooper|shock troopers]] during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Deception" /> and by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Purge Trooper|Purge]], [[Riot control stormtrooper|riot]], and [[Imperial Heavy Trooper|Heavy Troopers]] leading up to and during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Design==
[[File:AhsokaVsMagna_Tatooine.png|thumb|left|300px|An electrostaff was able to counter a Jedi's lightsaber]]
Electrostaffs were designed for single combat against [[lightsaber]]-wielders, such as [[Jedi]], and had the ability to [[Lightsaber combat|block]] lightsaber strikes.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> They were mechanically complicated, double-ended, and able to incapacitate most organic beings, powerful enough to stop a being's heart after five seconds of contact. They could also be used to send bolts of energy along a [[laser]] line.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|electrostaff|Electrostaff}}</ref> Each end of the [[staff]] was usually tipped with either [[Color|purple]]<ref name="Episode III" /> or yellow<ref name="Shadow Warrior">{{TCW|Shadow Warrior}}</ref> electricity<ref name="The Weapon of a Jedi" />, but in some cases like with the variant wielded by the Dugs, the electricity was turquoise,<ref name="The Zillo Beast" /> and the ones utilized by the [[HK-87 assassin droid]]s on [[Corellia]] against Ahsoka Tano had red electricity.<ref name="Master and Apprentice" />

Electrostaffs were often plated in [[phrik]] alloy, which made them resistant to lightsabers. Even with that, however,<ref name="TCW Official Collector's Edition">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars – The Official Collector's Edition|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' – The Official Collector's Edition]]</ref> such electrostaffs were not totally invulnerable to lightsaber strikes, meaning the staff could still be destroyed.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue">{{TCW|Grievous Intrigue}}</ref>

Some variations included only a single electrified tip, and others were capable of being taken apart to wield as 2 batons, similar to how a [[split saber]] works. Another variant had the shaft of the staff hollowed out and functioned as a blaster rifle, that particular electrostaff being wielded by [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Grand Admiral Thrawn's]] personal assassin [[Rukh|Ruhk]]. The [[Lasat]] High Honor Guards wielded Bo Rifles, which similar to Ruhk's electrostaff are a blaster rifle electrostaff hybrid, however they have never been shown in melee combat against a lightsaber, so it's unclear if they can parry lightsaber strikes like a standard electrostaff.

==History==
===High Republic Era===
[[File:TaborrValDorn-CGSWG.png|left|thumb|240x240px|Taborr with his electrostaff]]
In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="YJA Date">{{YJADate|1}}</ref> [[pirate]] [[Cyrus Vuundir|Taborr Val-Dorn]] wielded an electrostaff. It was double sided, but it also had the ability to spilt in half. The electrostaff gave Taborr the opportunity to counter a Jedi's lightsaber, leaving him with a better opportunity to steal without much repercussion.<ref name="YJA Upgraded">{{YJA|Upgraded}}</ref>

===Republic Era===
[[File:MagnaGuards_Tatooine.png|thumb|right|300px|IG-100 MagnaGuards wielding electrostaffs.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] wielded electrostaffs, which they often used against the Jedi.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> Major Separatist leaders like [[Count]] [[Dooku]]<ref name="TCWfilm">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> and [[General]] [[Grievous]] kept electrostaff-wielding IG-100s as [[bodyguard]]s.<ref name="Episode III" /> [[Droid]] mercenary [[AD-W4]] utilized an electrostaff in his duel with [[Mace Windu]].<ref name="Mace Windu 2">[[Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu 2|''Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu'' 2]]</ref> During [[Mission to Jabba's Palace (Clone Wars)|the mission on Tatooine]] while in a fight with three MagnaGuards, [[Ahsoka Tano]], after charging one of the two remaining droids, which knocked it to ground and, taking its electrostaff, rammed the weapon through the other droid's power core before quickly bisecting its head and torso with [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|her lightsaber]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /><ref name="TCWfilm" /> 

[[Pirate King]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] of the [[Ohnaka Gang]] wielded an electrostaff to fight [[Anakin Skywalker]] during the [[skirmish on Felucia]],<ref name="BH" /> [[Ahsoka Tano]] during the [[Raid on the Crucible|Raid on the ''Crucible'']],<ref name="A Test of Strength">{{TCW|A Test of Strength}}</ref> and fought rival [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Lassa Rhayme]].<ref name="Kindred Spirits">{{InsiderCite|159|Kindred Spirits}}</ref>

The [[Dug cavalier]] of [[Malastare]]<ref name="The Zillo Beast">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast}}</ref> and the slavers of [[Zygerria]]<ref name="Escape from Kadavo">{{TCW|Escape from Kadavo}}</ref> made use of a unique single-sided variant that could fold out its end when in use, producing an electrical charge that could harm both droids and living creatures.<ref name="The Zillo Beast" /> [[Captain]] [[Rex]] used one of these to fight off the [[Zygerrian Royal Guard|Zygerrian royal guards]] and to kill [[Keeper]] [[Agruss]].<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" /> 

[[Mandalorian]] [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze|Satine]]'s [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]] also wielded electrostaffs. Due to the pacifist nature of the [[New Mandalorians]], their electrostaffs were non-lethal to deflect blaster bolts and applying [[stun]] pulses that were effective against the [[B2-series super battle droid]]s.<ref name="Voyage of Temptation">{{TCW|Voyage of Temptation}}</ref>

The [[bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing]] utilized several [[IG-series assassin droid]]s as her own [[security droid]]s when she met up with [[Asajj Ventress]] at the [[Doric Prime power plant]] who then gave her a list of [[Galactic Republic]] [[Ghost Agent]]s to kill for her. Sing’s [[IG-series]] security detail wielded electrostaffs and fought [[Clone Force 99]] before Sing contacted the lead [[assassin droid]], [[Elkew-Nine]] and discovered her security droids were destroyed.<ref name="Ghost Agents 3">[[Hyperspace Stories: The Bad Batch—Ghost Agents 3|''Hyperspace Stories: The Bad Batch—Ghost Agents'' 3]]</ref>

The MagnaGuards were able to use their electrostaffs with deadly effects such as when they [[Capture of Eeth Koth|captured]] [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] member [[Eeth Koth]] and [[torture]]d him. Later, after defeating one MagnaGuard after taking its electrostaff, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] temporarily used it to duel Grievous, but he destroyed it, leaving Obi-Wan with only [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber|his lightsaber]].<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> The MagnaGuards also used electrostaffs to activate an [[electroguillotine]] and behead a person. This was used during the execution of [[Ramsis Dendup]].<ref name="SoftWar">{{TCW|The Soft War}}</ref> Grievous used an electrostaff whilst [[Battle of Utapau|fighting]] Obi-Wan Kenobi on his [[TSMEU-6 personal wheel bike]] on [[Utapau]] during the late stages of the war. He later attempted to [[Death|kill]] Kenobi with it, whilst he was hanging on a platform over a [[Pau Sinkhole]], but failed as Kenobi shot him in the torso, instantly killing the [[Kaleesh]] [[cyborg]].<ref name="Episode III" />

===Imperial Era===
[[File:Unlimited-Electrostaff.png|thumb|left|300px|A Purge Trooper wields an electrostaff]]
[[Emperor's Royal Guard]]s who wielded electrostaffs were known as Electrostaff Guards.<ref name="Electrostaff Guard">{{Legion|set=Imperial Royal Guards Unit Expansion|cardname=Electrostaff Guard}}</ref> Some [[Purge Trooper]]s active under the [[Inquisitorius]]'s [[Purge trooper division|Purge division]]<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> during the [[Great Jedi Purge]] carried [[Joopa prod|electrostaffs]], which enabled them to engage the lightsabers of surviving Jedi. During the [[secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order]], former [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] fought several such Purge Troopers.<ref name="Fallen Order" /> Five years later, more electrostaff-wielding Purge Troopers crossed weapons with Kestis during the [[quest for Tanalorr]].<ref name="Survivor" />

Former [[clone commando]] [[Gregor]] handed Ezra Bridger two [[Joopa prod|electrostaffs]] to get the [[joopa]] [[Big Bongo]], who was caught on the laser line.<ref name="The Lost Commanders">{{Rebels|The Lost Commanders}}</ref>

[[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]'s [[assassin]] and [[bodyguard]], [[Rukh]], carried a custom electrostaff. One end had a built-in [[blaster]], which is similar to a [[Lasat]] [[bo-rifle]]. He used this electrostaff to fight against a seasoned [[Ezra Bridger]]<ref name="Kindred">{{Rebels|Kindred}}</ref> and his master [[Kanan Jarrus]].<ref name="Jedi Night">{{Rebels|Jedi Night}}</ref>

The [[scavenger]] [[Sarco Plank]] wielded an electrostaff during the [[Galactic Civil War]], and [[Skirmish in the Temple of Eedit|fought]] [[Luke Skywalker]] with it on [[Devaron]] while the young Jedi was exploring the [[Temple of Eedit]]. Plank proved almost a match for the [[Jedi training|Jedi-in-training]], coming close to killing Skywalker several [[time]]s. Skywalker destroyed Plank's [[control box]], however, and sent him and his electrostaff hurtling into a pit leading to the [[Jedi temple|temple's]] underlevels.<ref name="The Weapon of a Jedi" /> [[Sixth Kin]]-turned-[[Crimson Dawn]] agent [[Gallin Crae]] made use of an electrostaff.<ref name="Aphra 12" />

===New Republic Era===
[[File:Displate-FinalDueloftheDarkSaber.png|thumb|right|300px|Gideon makes use of an electrostaff against the Darksaber]]
During the [[New Republic Era]], the Imperial Remnant [[warlord]] [[Gideon]] owned an electrostaff that he used during the [[Liberation of Mandalore]] against the united [[Mandalorian]] people. He made use of the weapon against the Mandalorian leader [[Bo-Katan Kryze]], who herself wielded the [[Darksaber]]. While Gideon expressed an interest in reclaiming the Darksaber, he ultimately destroyed it, although he was then killed by the battle's end.<ref name="Chapter 24">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 24: The Return}}</ref>

Around the same time, the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Morgan Elsbeth]] had [[HK-87 assassin droid]]s use electrostaffs for melee combat against Ahsoka Tano,<ref name="Master and Apprentice">{{Ahsoka|Part One: Master and Apprentice}}</ref> who by that time was a [[Ronin]]<ref name="Ahsoka comic">[[Ahsoka 2|''Ahsoka'' 2]]</ref> former Jedi<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> looking for leads about the rumored return of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn."<ref name="Master and Apprentice" />
