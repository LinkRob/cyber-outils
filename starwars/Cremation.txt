'''Cremation''' was a [[funeral]] rite that consisted in burning a [[Death|dead]] body to ashes. Following his demise during the [[Invasion of Naboo]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] was cremated in the [[city]] of [[Theed]].<ref name="The Phantom Menace">{{Film|I}}</ref> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Survivor Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> [[Cere Junda]], [[Eno Cordova]], and [[Bode Akuna]] were cremated by the remaining [[Stinger Mantis crew|''Stinger Mantis'' crew]] on [[Tanalorr]].<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> After the [[Jawa]]s who sold the droids [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] were killed by [[Stormtrooper|Imperial Stormtroopers]] on [[Tatooine]], their bodies were burned out of respect by [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Luke Skywalker cremated the [[Darth Vader's armor|armored suit]] of his late [[Parent|father]] [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>
