The '''Bracca resistance''' was a small isolated group of resistance fighters, composed of [[Scrapper Guild]] workers from [[Bracca]], a planet under [[First Order]] occupation. One of its members was [[Puwanini]], sister to [[Resistance]] member [[Pacer Agoyo]], who asked for her help on behalf of [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] in a [[Mission to Bracca|mission]] to steal starfighters from the scrapper planet.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

==History==
Puwanini provided vital information to enable [[Shriv Suurgav]]'s [[Dross Squadron]] to [[Mission to Bracca|infiltrate]] Bracca, disguised as Scrapper Guild workers. But when Dross Squadron was challenged by [[First Order stormtrooper]]s, Puwanini and her Scrapper Guild team came to the rescue and a firefight ensued. Puwanini led Shriv's team to [[Platform thirty-three G]], where a number of ships that were ready to be scrapped were located. These included a [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|T-65B X-wing]], two [[T-70 X-wing starfighter|T-70 X-wings]], and a [[T-85 X-wing starfighter|T-85 X-wing]]. Dross Squadron managed to escape with the X-wings, albeit losing [[Sanrec Stronghammer]] in the firefight.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />
