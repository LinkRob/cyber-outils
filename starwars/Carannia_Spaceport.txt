{{Quote|The place stinks of hyperfuel and desperation. Every spaceport is the same. Yes, they can gild the buildings, play jaunty tunes, even fly holographic flags from the towers, but scratch the surface and you'll find the dregs of the galaxy.|Asajj Ventress|<ref name="Dooku" />}}
The '''Carannia Spaceport''', also known as the '''Serenno Spaceport''', was a [[spaceport]] located in the [[city]] of [[Carannia]] near the [[Trannon district]], on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]].

==Description==
{{Quote|It's easy to see why. Everywhere is noise and bustle. Cargoes unloaded, passengers keen to be on their way. Loved ones are reunited and families torn apart, hope and despair in equal measures. Perfect for those who want to slip beneath the sensor. Thieves and smugglers, cutthroats and bounty hunters, all happy to relieve you of your wallet or your life, most often at the same time.|Asajj Ventress|<ref name="Dooku" />}}
The [[spaceport]] was located in the [[city]] of [[Carannia]]<ref name="BMF 70" /> near the [[Trannon district]],<ref name="Dooku" /> on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]],<ref name="PSFB" /> the [[Homeworld|home]] [[planet]] of the [[noble]] [[House Serenno]].<ref name="DB">{{Databank|serenno|Serenno}}</ref> The main terminal of the spaceport was a domed building next to the control tower.<ref name="Dooku" /> The spaceport itself could house all manners of starship classes. Despite the spaceport being able to accommodate all starship classes, the design of the spaceport was considerate to its natural surroundings.<ref name="BMF 70" />

==History==
The [[Sith apprentice]] [[Asajj Ventress]] visited the spaceport in the ''[[Windrunner]]'' airship while [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|searching]] for [[Jenza]], the sister of her [[Sith Master|Master]], [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]]. Ventress left the ''Windrunner'' without landing the ship, finding herself behind the [[duracrete]] hangar, noting that every spaceport was the same, and headed to the [[Trannon district]].<ref name="Dooku" /> At some point, the [[Corellian]] [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] met a girl named [[Sarla]] in the Carannia Spaceport, and the two had a brief relationship.<ref name="PSFB" />
