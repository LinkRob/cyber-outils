The '''dragonsnake''' (also spelled '''dragon snake''') was a large, predatory [[reptile]] that dwelled in the muddy [[water]]s of certain [[planet]]s, including [[Dagobah]] and [[Nal Hutta]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] had a run-in with a dragonsnake while searching for [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] the [[Hutt]] on Nal Hutta. [[Standard year|Years]] later, [[Luke Skywalker]] crash-landed on Dagobah, where his [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] was nearly eaten by a dragonsnake.

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Dragonsnake-HFZ.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A dragonsnake attacking Obi-Wan Kenobi on Nal Hutta.]]
Living in the murky [[water]]s of [[swamp]]-covered [[planet]]s such as [[Dagobah]]<ref name="DB R2" /> and [[Nal Hutta]],<ref name="DB NH" /> the dragonsnakes were large serpentlike beasts with sharp fangs<ref name="HfZ" /> that could excrete a lethal toxin.<ref name="The Mandalorian and Grogu">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref> Although voracious, they were incapable of digesting metal.<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> The dragonsnake "[[Bright-Eyes]]" displayed a degree of [[sentience]].<ref name="Light Falls">{{StoryCite|story=The Light That Falls|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref>

==Behavior==
Dragonsnakes were [[Diet|predatory]] animals, living off the flesh of other creatures.<ref name="AA">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> When hunting, they hid under the muddy water<ref name="JTS" /> and amongst [[gnarltree]] roots,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> waiting for their next meal to come close enough.<ref name="JTS" /> After snatching its prey, a dragonsnake would then dive back under the water.<ref name="ESB" /> They were amongst the most fearsome reptiles and were considered untameable.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==History==
[[File:Dragonsnake_SM.png|thumb|250px|A dragonsnake lunges at a passing [[jubba bird]].]]
At some point during the pan-[[The galaxy|galactic]] conflict known as the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Jedi Master]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Quinlan Vos]] attempted to track down [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] the [[Hutt]] on Nal Hutta. During the mission, Kenobi was attacked by a local dragonsnake. The creature was [[Death|killed]] when the Jedi Master thrust his ignited [[lightsaber]] in the dragonsnake's head.<ref name="HfZ" />

In the year [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|322}}</ref> a dragonsnake head was kept as a trophy on a [[Ubrikkian Floating Fortress]] by [[Trandoshan]] [[Big game hunter|hunters]]<ref name="Wookiee Hunt">{{TCW|Wookiee Hunt}}</ref> on the [[moon]] of [[Wasskah]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the aspiring Jedi [[Luke Skywalker]] accidentally landed [[Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)|his]] [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] [[X-wing starfighter|starfighter]] in the middle of [[Dragonsnake Bog|watery peat bog]] on Dagobah. A [[Unidentified dragonsnake|dragonsnake]] that hid there snatched Skywalker's [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]], which it attempted to eat. However, the dragonsnake found R2 indigestible and spit him out.<ref name="ESB" /> That incident was also witnessed by another dragonsnake, "Bright-Eyes."<ref name="Light Falls" />

[[File:DinDjarinVsDragonsnake-TMaG.png|thumb|250px|left|Din Djarin faces the Hutt Twin's dragonsnake on Nal Hutta.]]
During the [[New Republic Era]], a [[Hutt's dragonsnake|dragonsnake]] was owned by the [[The Twins|Hutt Twins]] in their palace on [[Nal Hutta]], who attempted to use it to kill [[Din Djarin]] for his betraying their agreement. After Djarin killed a few of their [[Amani]] [[gladiator]]s, the dragonsnake emerged from the depths and began to fight the [[Mandalorian]] from the managed to injure the dragonsnake, but it managed to injure him with [[poison]]. Thanks to [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] and four [[Anzellan|Anzellans]] using a [[grav charge]] to make a hole, Djarin managed to escape. Despite this ,the Twins had the [[Kyuzo]] bounty hunter known as [[Embo]] and his [[anooba]] [[Keibu]] track Djarin, which the pair tracked Grogu to an [[alien]] hut, where Embo informed the [[Unidentified species (Grogu)|local alien]] named [[Gatori]] of Djarin's dragonsnake injury. After Embo left, the local created an [[Dragonsnake venom antidote|antidote]] for the poison using local plants, which helped Djarin recover. After [[Rotta]] destroyed the trapped door, the Twins, Embo, and Keibu were trapped in the pit with the dragonsnake. After Embo and Keibu escaped through the same hole, the Twins were eaten by their dragonsnake.<ref name="The Mandalorian and Grogu" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DragonsnakeConcept-JFO.jpg|thumb|Dragonsnake concept art for ''Jedi: Fallen Order'']]
The reptilian creature first appeared in the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="ESB" /> However, it was first identified as "Dragonsnake" in an episode guide for "[[Hunt for Ziro]]" from the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Name">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/hunt-for-ziro-episode-guide|text="Hunt for Ziro" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

Dragonsnakes were originally going to make an appearance in the [[Star Wars]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' on Dagobah, but it never made it into the final cut.<ref name="Concept JFO">{{ArtStation|username=frizzlefry|url=artwork/EVPE3N|name=Amy Fry|text=Dragon snake}}</ref>
