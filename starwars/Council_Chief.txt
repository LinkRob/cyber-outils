The '''Council Chief''' was the leading figure of the [[Combined Bothan Clans|Bothan Council]], the government of [[Bothawui/Legends|Bothawui]]. The council selected one member to fill the position of Council Chief, who held the power of casting the deciding vote on stalemated issues. As of [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]], [[Savielk Trey'dra]] was the current Council Chief and the de facto ruler of the Bothan people.
