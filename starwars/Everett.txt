'''Everett''' was a company that produced the [[Mark IV Locomotion System]]. The [[R2-series astromech droid|R2 series astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] was equipped with an Everett Mark IV Locomotion System.<ref name="CVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Everett was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''.<ref name="CVD" /> The company originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was created for the [[2006]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary]]''. The company's name is a reference to [[Dave Everett]], founder of the [[R2-D2 Builders Club|Artoo Builders Club]] in [[Wikipedia:Australia|Australia]].<ref name="Archive">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/ref/f20061031/index.html|text=Building a ''Complete Visual Dictionary''}}</ref>
