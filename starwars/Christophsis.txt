'''Christophsis''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Christoph system]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]] where the [[Battle of Christophsis]] was fought during the [[Clone Wars]]. The crystalline planet was invaded by the [[battle droid]] armies of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] but were repelled by the [[Jedi]] and [[clone trooper]]s dispatched by the [[Galactic Republic]]. Later, the planet was the site of a [[Second battle of Christophsis|second battle]] between the Republic and the Separatists. After the transformation of the Galactic Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]], the planet was placed under Imperial control, but nevertheless hosted rebellious elements.

==Description==
{{Quote|Crystal forests cover the surface of Christophsis with tall jagged peaks of turquoise rising from fields of hexagonal "tiles."|The ''Galactic Atlas''|<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>}}
Christophsis's surface was covered in [[forest]]s<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> of naturally occurring, [[Color|turquoise]] [[crystal]] spires, making a dramatic and beautiful landscape.<ref name="The Visual Encyclopedia" /> Those crystal forests arose from what the ''[[Galactic Atlas]]'' described as hexagonally-shaped "tiles," which existed as the world's fields.<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> It had an [[atmosphere]] that was breathable for most [[humanoid]] [[species]]<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> and was [[orbit]]ed by a [[moon]], [[Leesis]].<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />

==History==
===High Republic Era===
During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Dez Rydan]] was involved in a series of exploits on Christophsis that left his former mentor, [[Jedi Master]] [[Jora Malli]], concerned as to whether he had curbed his adventurous nature. Sometime after his time on Christophsis, Rydan left the world and joined the [[Zeitooine mission]], later becoming embroiled in the [[mission on the Amaxine station]]. Following that, he finally sought out a way to find emotional balance through the [[Barash Vow]].<ref name="Into the Dark">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref>

===The Clone Wars===
{{Quote|The only Jedi we can spare are Skywalker and Kenobi. They just captured the planet Christophsis.|[[Mace Windu]], to [[Supreme Chancellor]] Sheev Palpatine|<ref name="TCW film" />}}
During the [[Clone Wars]], Christophsis was the site of several key conflicts between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy]] and the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="The Visual Encyclopedia" /> [[Alderaanian]] [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] visited the world to undertake relief work. However, he and his relief mission was trapped by a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Separatist fleet]] led by [[Admiral]] [[Trench]]. A [[Republic Navy|Republic fleet]] led by [[Jedi General]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and Admiral [[Wullf Yularen]] clashed with Trench's [[fleet]] and managed to defeat it following a [[Battle of Christophsis|lengthy battle]]. This enabled the Republic to deliver vital supplies to Organa and his men.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />

The Republic then established a [[Christophsis Republic base|military base]] on Christophsis and began a campaign to retake the planet from [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]] under [[General]] [[Whorm Loathsom]] and [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[Sith]] [[assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]]. During the fighting, Kenobi and Skywalker hatched a plan to trap [[Separatist Droid Army|Separatist droid forces]] advancing from two towers. However, their plan failed due to the presence of a [[Treason|traitor]] within the ranks of the [[clone trooper]] [[Grand Army of the Republic|forces]]: [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Slick]]. Working together, the [[Cloning|clone]] [[officer]]s [[Cody]] and [[Rex]] managed to capture Slick. Meanwhile, Kenobi and Skywalker narrowly escaped a trap at the [[Christophsis Separatist headquarters|Separatist base]] set by Ventress.<ref name="Hidden Enemy" />

[[File:Rex_on_Christophsis_SWL.png|thumb|left|300px|Clone Captain Rex leads clone troops on Christophsis.]]
Before Slick's capture, the rogue clone [[trooper]] managed to damage most of the Republic's weapons, except for their heavy cannons, which were saved by the clones. The [[Republic Military|Republic forces']] problems were further complicated by the advance of Loathsom's [[Retail Caucus]] [[droid]] forces. Despite being outgunned and outnumbered, the [[Jedi]] and Republic were relieved by a young [[Padawan]] named [[Ahsoka Tano]], who had been sent by [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] to be Skywalker's apprentice. Working together, Skywalker and Tano managed to infiltrate the Separatist lines and shut down their [[deflector shield generator]] while Kenobi stalled the Separatist forces by "negotiating" with Loathsom. This tactic paid off and the Republic succeeded in turning the tables on the Separatist forces and capturing Loathsom.<ref name="TCW film" /> Newsreels at [[Kamino]]'s [[Tipoca City Military Complex|military complex]] carried reports of the Christophsis campaign.<ref name="Clone Cadets">{{TCW|Clone Cadets}}</ref>

About two [[Standard year|years]] after the Christophsis campaign, the Confederacy still maintained a [[Christophsis Separatist tower|military base]] on the planet that was largely manned by [[B1-series battle droid]]s. After fleeing the Republic [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Vigilance (Venator-class)|''Vigilance'']], the Separatist [[Head of State|leader]] Count Dooku, the turncoat Jedi Master [[Quinlan Vos]], and Ventress [[Second battle of Christophsis|crash-landed]] on Christophsis' surface. By this stage, Ventress had been cast out by Dooku and had struck an alliance with the Jedi in return for helping Vos to [[Death|kill]] Dooku. Dooku and Vos were subsequently pursued by a Republic landing force led by Kenobi and Skywalker. Unknown to everyone, Vos secretly planned to get Dooku to lead him to his [[Sith Master|master]] [[Darth Sidious]] so that he could destroy the Sith once and for all.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

After trudging through Christophsis' crystalline landscape, the three travelers reached the Separatist military base. There, Dooku contacted his master Darth Sidious to secure safe passage from Christophsis. Shortly later, the Republic bombarded the Separatist base. In the midst of the bombardment, Dooku attempted to kill Vos with [[Force lightning]] but hit Ventress instead.<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> Ventress' apparent death<ref name="TBB article">{{SW|url=news/the-bad-batch-season-3-trailer|text=Watch the ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 3 Trailer}}</ref> brought Vos back to the [[Light side of the Force|light side]] and he refused to strike down Dooku; realizing that it would only lead him to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]. Dooku managed to escape offworld with the help of Sidious while Kenobi and Skywalker took a penitent Vos into custody.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

===Imperial era===
{{Quote|It is hoped that this action, drastic though it is, has served to quell the radical elements at work on Christophsis, to restore the planet to order, and to deliver a message to all those who would threaten the peace our Emperor has given us: we stand here now, always, and forevermore. No matter what else may come, the Empire will endure.|Wilhuff Tarkin|<ref name="Leia" />}}
Following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformation]] of the Galactic Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]], a rebel cell known as the [[Spectres]] raided an Imperial [[convoy]] en route to Christophsis in [[Realspace|space]]. This was the second in a string of raids that had snared an Imperial convoy to [[Ryloth]]. The rebels then went to raid convoys near [[Kashyyyk]] and [[Toydaria]] before settling down on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] world of [[Lothal]].<ref name="Ghost Raid">{{SW|url=games-apps/star-wars-rebels-ghost-raid|text=''Star Wars Rebels: Ghost Raid''|int=Star Wars Rebels: Ghost Raid|nolive=1}}</ref> The Empire also stole the [[kyber crystal]]s that were holy to the natives and used them for the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

In [[3 BBY]], following the theft of [[Unidentified medical frigate|one]] of the Empire's [[Hospital ship|medical frigates]], the Galactic Empire traced [[Christophsis insurgents|rebellious elements]] to Christophsis and were found to be responsible for the and a [[Arrests on Christophsis|series of arrests]] were conducted, including the [[Unidentified prime minister|planet's]] [[Prime Minister|prime minister]] senators, and territorial supervisors. The senators and territorial supervisors were sent to Imperial work camps and the Prime Minister was executed for treason. The Empire also "pacified" the [[city]] of [[Tophen]], the home town of the prime minister which was believed to be the center of the insurgency, as an example to the populace in the hope of quelling radical elements.<ref name="Leia" /> The capital city of [[Chaleydonia]]<ref name="The Visual Encyclopedia" /> was also attacked by three [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] which opened fire at civilian buildings, starting with the city [[Medcenter|hospitals]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

The news of the arrests<ref name="Leia" /> and subsequent "pacifications"<ref name="Rise" /> were announced to the [[Imperial Senate]] by [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]. Though many of the senators were horrified by the actions they still clapped at the news as they knew a failure to do so may result in the same happening to there own world. Amongst those clapping was Senator Bail Organa and his adopted daughter, the [[Apprentice Legislator]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who later discussed the matter more truthfully with Bail's [[Marriage|wife]], [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]], after they returned home to [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Leia" />

Around the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Jakku]], five [[pilgrim]]s from the [[Church of the Force]]&mdash;the [[MA-B0 cargo lifter droid]] [[Mabo]], [[Addar]] the [[human]], [[Jumon]] the [[Iakaru]], [[Madrammagath]] the [[Elomin]], and [[Uggorda]] the [[Duros]]&mdash;undertook a mission to Christophsis to return several stolen kyber crystals. They had been dispatched by the [[pastor]] and philanthropist [[Brin Izisca]]. Over a thirteen [[Standard day|day]] period, the pilgrims navigated through Christophsis' dangerous terrain. Maddrammagath, Uggorda, and Jumon were killed by several fearsome [[Force-sensitive]] creatures called [[Kyaddak]]. Despite the deaths of their companions, Mabo and Addar manged to return the crystals to a broken crystal wall. Mabo was destroyed when he fell down a hole while Addar was left to fend off a pack of pursuing Kyaddak.<ref name="Empire's End" />
