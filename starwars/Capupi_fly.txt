'''Capupi flies''' were a [[species]] of [[insect]] found on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. They were important pollinators on the planet. Capupi flies would secrete a type of oil after ingesting the flowers of the [[asuka tree]]s that was considered quite valuable for its supposed ability to let people see into the future. Despite asuka tree flowers being their meal of choice the flies could sometimes be found inside of [[kurado fruit]] also found on Akiva.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>, the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] and her [[Merqaal]] companion [[Nix]] share a [[kurado fruit platter]] at the [[Zian (street food stall)|Zian]] [[Restaurant|street food stall]] in [[Myrra]]. When the cooking droid cut open the fruit for the pair, several Capupi flies flew out from the fruit. Nix enjoyed eating the flies.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Capupi flies appeared for the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The flies are described in a conversation the player can have with a researcher in the [[Stormhollow]] region of the planet. They are depicted as part of the optional "Galactic Street Food" activity in [[Myrra]] city. The game uses the same model to depict the capupi flies as the [[Ya-ya fly|ya-ya flies]] despite describing them as unique species of fly.<ref name="Outlaws" />
