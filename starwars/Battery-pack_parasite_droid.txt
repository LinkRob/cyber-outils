'''Battery-pack parasite droids''' were small [[Droid/Legends|droids]] shaped like [[Battery/Legends|battery]] packs for [[Blaster rifle/Legends|blaster rifles]]. They were used by [[Wraith Squadron]] in [[44 ABY]], who planted them on a number of [[Galactic Alliance Army]] blaster rifles during their investigation of [[General/Legends|General]] [[Stavin Thaal]]. They had a very limited amount of charge and also could move independently, as well as subvert other simple droids. The droids were equipped with sensors and burst-transmitters for surveillance.
