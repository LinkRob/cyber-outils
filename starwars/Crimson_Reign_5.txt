'''''Crimson Reign'' 5''' is the fifth and final issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Crimson Reign]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Steven Cummings]], [[Victor Olazaba]], and [[Guru-eFX]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 22]], [[2022]]. The issue contains the story '''Part 5: "The Scarlet Queen"'''.

==Publisher's summary==
''"THE SCARLET QUEEN" LADY QI'RA has used every tool at her disposal to generate chaos in the Galactic Empire, all to further her goal of eradicating the Sith. But as the final phase of Qi'ra's grand plan is revealed, she is about to learn a lesson that the Jedi Order also came to understand. Underestimate EMPEROR PALPATINE and pay the price.''

==Plot summary==
===Discovery of the enemy===
On [[Coruscant]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] meets with [[Darth Sidious|Emperor Palpatine]] in the [[Imperial Palace]]. The Emperor asserts that the recent series of galactic disruptions - including the [[Syndicate War]], the assassination of his [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guards]], and the theft of the [[Screaming Key]] - are not merely attacks on the [[Galactic Empire]], but a coordinated campaign against the [[Sith]]. [[Sliro Barsha|Director Barsha]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] reports that a rumor regarding the Empire's displeasure with the [[Hutts]] originated from [[Crimson Dawn]], successfully baiting the syndicates into a self-destructive civil war.

Vader recounts his previous duel with Lady [[Qi'ra]] noting her mastery of [[Teräs Käsi]] and a fighting style that mirrors Palpatine’s own. The Emperor deduces that Qi'ra was trained by his former apprentice, [[Maul]], as an instrument of revenge. He expresses concern over what other Sith secrets Maul may have shared with her before his death.

===The transactional life===
Aboard the [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']], Qi'ra prepares her inner circle for the next phase of the conflict. She assigns the [[Archivist]] and the [[Knights of Ren]] to locate an ancient chamber. Qi'ra then meets with [[Cadeliah]], the young heir to the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate|Mourner's Wail]] and the [[Unbroken Clan]]. In a moment of frank mentorship, Qi'ra describes her upbringing as a "scumrat" on [[Corellia]] and explains her core philosophy: trust is a failure of leadership. She views every interaction as a transaction based on leverage or threats, citing her relationship with her lieutenant, [[Margo]], as a prime example of turning a potential assassin into a loyal asset through calculated utility.

===The Screaming Key===
The Archivist and the Knights of Ren arrive at a secluded underground cavern filled with giant, frozen monsters. The Archivist explains that the chamber must be "activated" using the Screaming Key and the [[dark side of the Force]]. Ren levitates the key and imbues it with "the Shadow," triggering a deafening metaphysical scream that animates the stone creatures.

A chaotic skirmish ensues as the Knights attempt to hold off the monsters while escorting the Archivist to the centre of the cavern. During the fighting, the Knight [[Bazzra]] is crushed by one of the creatures. The Archivist manages to reach an ancient machine housed within a giant skull and inserts the key, instantly turning the monsters back into stone and securing the site for the next stage of their plan.

===Chaos and the Hidden Empire===
Back on the ''Vermillion'', Qi'ra receives an emergency transmission from her agents embedded within the Imperial Military. She learns that the Emperor has issued a high-priority target designation for Crimson Dawn across the entire fleet. Recognising that the Sith have begun their hunt, Qi'ra sends Cadeliah away with [[Trinia]] for safety.

Qi'ra then broadcasts a final command to her "Hidden Empire"—a vast network of sleeper agents positioned within the Empire, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], and major corporations. She issues a single order: Chaos. She instructs her followers to disrupt and poison the Imperial machine from within, intending to keep the Empire staggered while she prepares a final masterstroke to end the Sith's reign.
