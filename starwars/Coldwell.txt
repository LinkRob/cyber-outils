'''Coldwell''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[captain]] for a [[Cantwell-class Arrestor Cruiser|''Cantwell''-class Arrestor Cruiser]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Boba Fett BWR 1" /> Between [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Boba Fett — Black, White & Red 1|''Boba Fett — Black, White & Red'' 1]] take place after the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|formation]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and before it is [[Declaration of a New Republic|reorganized]] into the [[New Republic]], which corresponds to between [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> they tasked the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] with capturing and delivering the [[Besalisk]] criminal [[Bantan Jabar]] to them alive.<ref name="Boba Fett BWR 1" />
