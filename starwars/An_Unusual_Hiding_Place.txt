"'''An Unusual Hiding Place'''" is a short story published in ''[[The Original Trilogy Stories]]''. It was written by [[Calliope Glass]].<ref name="Original" /> It adapts [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back]] from the [[Millennium Falcon]]'s escape from [[Hoth]] to heading to [[Bespin]].
