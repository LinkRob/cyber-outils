The '''Canary Islands''', known as "Islas Canarias" in [[Wikipedia:Spanish language|Spanish]], is a volcanic archipelago in the [[Wikipedia:Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic Ocean]] composed of seven islands and located at {{Coor title dms|28|0|0|N|16|0|0|W|island}}. Although the archipelago is closer to the [[Wikipedia:Africa|African content]], or the country of [[Wikipedia:Morocco|Morocco]], it belongs to [[Wikipedia:Spain|Spain]] and therefore is a part of the [[Wikipedia:European Union|European Union]]. In [[2019]], the islands had a population of 2,153,389 and a density of 287.39 inhabitants per km2.

Scenes from the ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' movie were filmed in [[Wikipedia:Fuerteventura|Fuerteventura]], one of the eastern islands that make up the archipelago. The [[in-universe]] location of Fuerteventura is the [[planet]] [[Savareen]].
