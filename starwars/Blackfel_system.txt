The '''Blackfel system''' was located in the [[Greater Plooriod sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was situated in grid square M-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

During its [[Imperial Era|reign]], the [[Galactic Empire]] had a presence in the Blackfel [[Star system|system]].<ref name="Star Wars 5" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars (2015) 5|''Star Wars'' (2015) 5]] to [[0 ABY]].</ref> [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] undertook a [[scout]]ing mission for a new rebel base in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Moddell sector]] aboard the stolen Imperial [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class shuttle]] ''[[Invictus]]''. During the mission, their [[shuttle]] was approached by two [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], who instructed them to identify themselves, as they did not have clearance to be in the [[sector]].<ref name="Star Wars 5" />

In response, Solo claimed that the [[Starship|shuttle]] was from the Blackfel system and that they were on a classified scouting mission before Organa transmitted clearance codes to the [[TIE series|TIE fighters]]. As they were about to be cleared, Solo began [[pilot]]ing the shuttle away, forcing the [[Starfighter|TIEs]] to chase after them.<ref name="Star Wars 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Star_Wars_Vol_2_5.jpg|thumb|right|190px|''Star Wars'' (2015) 5 marked the first mention of the Blackfel system in the current ''Star Wars'' canon.]]
The Blackfel system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars (2015) 5|fifth issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']]. The issue was written by [[Jason Aaron]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 5" /> on [[May 20]], 2015.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52656/star_wars_2015_5|text=Star Wars (2015) #5}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Blackfel system/Legends|Blackfel system]] first appeared, albeit unidentified, in the [[Blood Ties: Boba Fett is Dead 2|second issue]] of the [[2012]] comic series ''[[Star Wars: Blood Ties: Boba Fett is Dead]]''. The issue was written by [[Tom Taylor]], illustrated by [[Chris Scalf]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Boba Fett is Dead 2">[[Blood Ties: Boba Fett is Dead 2|''Blood Ties: Boba Fett is Dead'' 2]]</ref> on [[May 30]], 2012.<ref name="Marvel 2">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54020/star_wars_blood_ties_-_boba_fett_is_dead_2012_2|text=Star Wars: Blood Ties - Boba Fett Is Dead (2012) #2}}</ref> The [[Star system/Legends|system]] was later identified in the [[StarWars.com]] [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Essential Atlas Online Companion">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
