'''Egg-seed''' was a substance.<ref name="CL" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the [[Toydarian]] [[Watto]] kept an [[egg-seed dispenser]] by his nest in the top of the [[Watto's junkshop|junkshop]] he ran in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], egg-seed was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="CL" /> [[Egg-seed/Legends|It]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[1999]] reference book [[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary|''Star Wars: Episode I The Visual Dictionary'']], which was written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="TPM VD">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
