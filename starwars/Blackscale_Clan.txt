The '''Blackscale Clan''' was a [[Trandoshan]] [[mercenary]] clan based on the [[species]]'s [[homeworld]], [[Trandosha]]. It had a contract to deliver [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Wookiee]]s to the [[Galactic Empire]], and so the clan's members raided the Wookiee homeworld of [[Kashyyyk]] mercilessly.<ref name="A&A" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Blackscale Clan was first mentioned in the [[2019]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] supplement ''[[Allies and Adversaries]]''.<ref name="A&A" /> The [[Blackscale Clan/Legends|clan]] originated in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], where it first appeared in [[Star Wars Galaxies: Rage of the Wookiees|''Rage of the Wookiees'']], a [[2005]] expansion to the video game [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']].<ref name="Galaxies">''[[Star Wars Galaxies: Rage of the Wookiees]]''</ref>
