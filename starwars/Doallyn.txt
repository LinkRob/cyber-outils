'''Doallyn''' was a [[Geranite]] [[Gender|male]] [[Hunting|hunter]] who [[Jabba's criminal empire|served]] the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]].<ref name="Geektionary" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], which includes Doallyn's appearance, to [[4 ABY]].</ref> Doallyn was present in the court room of [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] when the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Chewbacca]] arrived in the palace, with Organa disguised as the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boushh]]. Doallyn rushed up the stairs leading into the court room to intercept the pair, but got too close to Chewbacca, who threw the hunter against the wall at the base of the stairs. Doallyn then fell to the floor before pulling himself up to sit on the stairs as Organa and Chewbacca stepped past him into the court room to speak with Jabba.<ref name="ROTJ" /> Doallyn was easily identifiable by the large [[Color|black]] [[helmet]] that covered his entire head.<ref name="Geektionary" /> He also wore black [[clothing]] and spoke in a [[language]] other than [[Galactic Basic Standard]].<ref name="ROTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Doallyn first appeared in the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="ROTJ" /> The character first received a name in the current [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]'', although the name Doallyn originated from the [[1996]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] story ''[[Skin Deep: The Fat Dancer's Tale]]'', which was written by [[A. C. Crispin]].<ref name="Skin Deep">{{StoryCite|story=Skin Deep: The Fat Dancer's Tale|book=Tales from Jabba's Palace}}</ref>
