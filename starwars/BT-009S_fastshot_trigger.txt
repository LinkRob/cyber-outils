The '''BT-009S fastshot trigger''' was an [[blaster]] trigger that was illegally modified to reduce the amount of force or movement required to shoot.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a group of [[pirate]]s had stored a fastshot trigger in a cavern inside of [[Typhon's Rock]], a rock mound in the [[Lost Steppe]] region on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]], located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] heard about the fastshot trigger stored at Typhon's Rock. She later went to the cavern and retrieved the trigger and used it to the modify her [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
BT-009S fastshot trigger appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The player must obtain the trigger in order upgrade the light plasma module to level four.<ref name="Outlaws" />
