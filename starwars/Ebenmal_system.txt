The '''Ebenmal system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Irnaj sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western" /> It was situated in grid square K-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Ebenmal system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Ebenmal system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in an update to the StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Fry.<ref name="Essential Atlas Online Companion">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
