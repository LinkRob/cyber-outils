'''''Captured on Cloud City''''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' young-readers title in the [[World of Reading]] Level 2 series. It was written by [[Nate Millici]], illustrated by [[Pilot Studio]], and published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[July 11]], [[2017]]. The story adapts [[Han Solo]]'s [[carbon-freezing]] and [[Luke Skywalker]]'s [[Duel on Cloud City|lightsaber duel]] with [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] from the film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back'']]. ''Captured on Cloud City'' was released exclusively as part of [[World of Reading Star Wars Boxed Set: Level 2|World of Reading ''Star Wars'' Boxed Set: Level 2]].
