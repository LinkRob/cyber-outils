'''Frozen Eeyogurt''' was a frozen [[yogurt]] flavor sold at the [[31 Hundred Flavors]] [[ice cream]] stand on [[Disney Playground Planet]]. It appeared on the shop's Featured Flavors menu, where the [[Merchant|vendor]] offered a broad mix of ice cream creations, such as "[[Thor Ragnarocky Road]]," "[[Mango-lorian]]," and "[[Creamboat Willie]]."<ref name="May12th" />

==Behind the scenes==
Frozen Eeyogurt was mentioned as a menu item in ''[[The Simpsons: May the 12th Be With You]]'',<ref name="May12th" /> an animated crossover short film that premiered on [[Disney+]] on [[May 10]], [[2024]].<ref name="Insta">{{Instagram|username=disneyplus|url=p/C6jkthKCMoe|name=[[Disney+]]|date=2024-05-04|quote="May the 12th Be With You", an all-new short from @TheSimpsons, arrives May 10 only on #DisneyPlus!|archivedate=20240523025125}}</ref> The flavor's name referenced both [[Wikipedia:Frozen yogurt|frozen yogurt]] and the classic [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] character [[Wikipedia:Eeyore|Eeyore]], a favorite of short film writer [[Wikipedia:Al Jean|Al Jean]], who had expressed a desire to include Eeyore cameos in the short film.<ref name="ComicBook">{{WebCite|url=https://screenrant.com/the-simpsons-may-the-12th-be-with-you-al-jean-interview|text=The Simpsons' Al Jean Talks May The 12th Be With You, Disney Easter Eggs & Movie Sequel Chances|author=[https://screenrant.com/author/grant-hermanns Hermanns, Grant]|date=2024-05-14|work=[[Wikipedia:Screen Rant|''Screen Rant'']]|archivedate=20240515031333}}</ref>
