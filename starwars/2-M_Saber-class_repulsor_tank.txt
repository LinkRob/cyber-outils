The '''2-M ''Saber''-class repulsor tank''', or '''2-M Hover Tank''', was a model of [[repulsorlift]] [[tank]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Equipped with powerful [[laser cannon]]s, it excelled against heavily fortified defenses, but its slow speed left it vulnerable to rocket turrets. Thanks to its [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]], the ''Saber''-class had exceptional beam defense for a ground-based vehicle. The ''Saber''-class repulsortank also spawned a [[scout]] variant, the [[Scout-class 2-M repulsor tank|''Scout''-class 2-M repulsor tank]]. It was used [[Battle for Tatooine|in combat]] on [[Tatooine]] shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]].

==Characteristics==
The 2-M ''Saber''-class repulsor tank,<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> or simply the 2-M Hover Tank, was a model of [[hovertank]] deployed by the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was equipped with a [[repulsorlift]] system that allowed it to hover, and was armed with a primary [[laser cannon]] with an [[antenna]] on top. The primary [[hull]] had characteristics<ref name="Commander" /> to the [[TX-130 Saber-class fighter tank|TX-130 ''Saber''-class fighter tank]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> Additionally, the 2-M Hover Tank was equipped with a [[deflector shield generator]] and was crewed by two individuals. The [[Scout-class 2-M repulsor tank|''Scout''-class 2-M repulsor tank]] was a smaller and more lightly armed variant of the 2-M ''Saber''-class.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==Role==
The 2-M Hover Tank was deployed against heavily fortified defenses and units.<ref name="Commander" />

==History==
The 2-M ''Saber''-class repulsor tank was deployed by the [[Imperial Army]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], participating in [[Operations on Tatooine|operations]] on [[Tatooine]], the [[Dandoran Campaign]], the [[Er'Kit Campaign]], a [[campaign on Yavin IV]], and other conflicts shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Commander" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 2-M Hover Tank originally debuted in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]''. It eventually returned to [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''.
