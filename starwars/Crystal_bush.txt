'''Crystal bushes''' were [[Crystal|crystalline]] structures found on the surface of the [[planet]] [[Garasu]]. They came in the [[color]]s blue, pink, and yellow. The crystal bushes had the ability to reflect light, which could impact the vision of [[human]]s and [[B1-series battle droid]]s. This property was utilized by the [[Cloning|clone]] [[Imperial shock trooper|shock trooper]] [[Lock Down]] during a [[Blaster fight|blasterfight]] with [[battle droid]]s, an event that took place during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="LEGO" /> sometime between [[20 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]].<ref name="WarDate">The ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Padawan Lost]]" shows [[clone trooper]]s wearing [[Phase I clone trooper armor]], while the later episode "[[Water War]]" shows clones wearing [[Phase II clone trooper armor]]. As both of these episodes are dated to [[20 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', the switch to Phase II armor must have taken place during 20 BBY. Since clone trooper [[Lock Down]] is shown wearing Phase II armor, the events of the comic must take place sometime after the switch and before the end of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]].</ref> The clone broke off a shard from a crystal bush, and directed the light to shine into the [[Photoreceptor|photoreceptor]] of a B1 battle [[droid]], blinding it. Lock Down then tackled the droid into another bush, sending tiny, sparkling shards into the air.<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crystal bushes appeared in "[[The Lucky Shards]]," a comic written by [[Martin Fisher]] and illustrated by [[Natascha Römer]] that featured in the [[LEGO Star Wars 129|129th issue]] of the ''[[LEGO Star Wars (magazine)|LEGO Star Wars]]'' magazine series,<ref name="LEGO" /> released on [[October 2]], [[2025]].<ref name="jedi-bibliothek">{{WebCite|url=https://jedi-bibliothek.de/datenbank/ausgabe/lego-star-wars-magazin-127|text=LEGO Star Wars Magazin 129|archivedate=20250401131019}}</ref>
