'''Alak''' was a [[human]]<ref name="THRCE" /> [[Gender|male]] [[pirate]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="THRA 1" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic Adventures (2022) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2022) 1]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> he was wanted in about twenty [[Star system|systems]].<ref name="THRA 1" />

On a mission to investigate if the pirate hunters were planning a move on the pirates, Alak fell in love with pirate hunter [[Raf Thatchburn]].<ref name="THRA 2">[[The High Republic Adventures (2022) 2|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2022) 2]]</ref> Alak [[Death|passed away]] long before<ref name="Escape from Valo">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref> [[229 BBY]].<ref name="Date" />

==Behind the scenes==
The [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 20|twentieth issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']] depicts Alak as alive and present on [[Eriadu]] after the end of the [[Nihil conflict]].<ref name="THRA 20">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 20|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 20]]</ref> This, however, conflicts with the novel ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'', which had previously established that Alak had died long before its events,<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> which take place in 229 BBY.<ref name="Date" />
