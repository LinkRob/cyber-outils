'''Doelsor Triggant''' was an individual who served as a [[Moff]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="TOG" /> By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|Solo}}</ref> Triggant had gifted [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]]&mdash;head of the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate&mdash;a [[Taozin]] grub, which Vos kept on display in the [[Dryden's study|study]] of his personal [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]''.<ref name="TOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Doelsor Triggant was first mentioned in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', a [[2018]] reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TOG" />
