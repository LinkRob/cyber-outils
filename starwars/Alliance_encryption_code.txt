'''Alliance encryption codes''' were encrypted codes that the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] used to transmit information. Two such codes were [[Alliance code Peth]] and [[Alliance code Osk]]. Each of the codes was dozens of randomly chosen characters long. Despite this, [[Kidi Aleri]] managed to memorize all of the Alliance's encryption codes.<ref name="MT">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref>
