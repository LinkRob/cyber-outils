"'''An Hour with Howard Kazanjian, Warren Franklin, Richard Marquand, Sidney Ganis and Carrie Fisher'''" was an article published in the [[Bantha Tracks 26|twenty-sixth issue]] of the [[Bantha Tracks (department)|Bantha Tracks]] newsletter, which was published in the Fall of [[1984]]. The article recorded highlights from a panel held at the [[Wikipedia:42nd World Science Fiction Convention|42nd World Science Fiction Convention]] on [[September 2]], 1984, that celebrated the [[original trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']],<ref name="Bantha Tracks 26" /> which was released in the [[1983|previous year]].<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> The panel members included co-producer and [[Lucasfilm]] Vice President [[Howard G. Kazanjian]], director [[Richard Marquand]], executive [[Sidney Ganis]], and actor [[Carrie Fisher]], who played [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="Bantha Tracks 26" />
