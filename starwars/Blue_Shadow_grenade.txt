'''Blue Shadow grenades''' were [[grenade]]s that contained a strain of the [[Blue Shadow Virus]]. The grenades cost 2,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] per unit.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Shadow grenades were first mentioned in ''[[Unlimited Power]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Force and Destiny|''Force and Destiny'']] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' module that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2018]].<ref name="Unlimited">''[[Unlimited Power]]''</ref>
