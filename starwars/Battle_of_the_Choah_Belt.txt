During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a '''battle''' took place within the [[Choah Belt]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], launched by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Targeting the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Choah Belt fuel refinery|fuel refinery]] located in the [[Asteroid field|asteroid belt]], the Empire was able to destroy the [[refinery]] and deplete the Rebellion's [[fuel]] dump. Hoping to compensate their losses, the Alliance [[Attack on Black Ice|attacked]] the Empire's [[Field Secured Container Vessel]] ''[[Black Ice]]'' and stole it.<ref name="BMF 34" />
