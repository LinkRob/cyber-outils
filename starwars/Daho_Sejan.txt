'''Daho Sejan''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Pau'an]] [[senator]] who represented his [[homeworld]] of [[Utapau]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Imperial Era]].

==Biography==
===Background===
{{Quote|Do you think I wanted Utapau to join the Empire? The compromises I made created a future for my people.|Daho Sejan|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
Serving as the [[Envoy|representative]] of [[Utapau]] in the [[Imperial Senate]], [[Senator]] Daho Sejan claimed to have had no fondness for the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Occupation of Utapau|occupation]] of his homeworld. Believing, however, that the [[New Order]] could not be toppled, he instead sought to gain the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor's]] approval, ensuring his own well-being at the cost of being disgraced in the eyes of his own [[Pau'an|people]].<ref name="Survivor" />

At some point, Sejan wrote to a compatriot and likened the Senate to a rigged casino. He stated that as Palpatine always won the political games, and that it seemed pointless to play unless you could also secure your own prizes at the same time.<ref name="TRaFotGE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> By [[9 BBY]], Senator Sejan had come to own a [[T-2 Ramsidian-class yacht|Ramsidian]] [[yacht]] designed by [[Sienar Fleet Systems]].<ref name="Survivor" />

===Hunting Cal Kestis===
Senator Sejan attempted to buy the captive [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] from a group of [[Coruscant Security Force]] officers in order to curry favor directly with the [[Emperor]], and avoid dealing with Palpatine's [[Emperor's Fist|right-hand]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], and the [[Inquisitorius]]. On his orders, [[Level 2046]] was closed and a curfew initiated with any citizens who did not comply being detained and incarcerated.<ref name="Survivor" />

After Kestis was delivered to him, Sejan told Kestis he was aware that the failed coup on [[Norsid]] was his doing. Sejan inspected Kestis' [[Cal Kestis' second lightsaber|lightsaber]] and declared that future generations will look upon the weapon as a thing of myth. He ordered Kestis to provide details of the whereabouts of other rogue Jedi, but unbeknownst to him, the whole affair was a ploy by Kestis and his [[Stinger Mantis crew|allies]] to gain access to Sejan's yacht and the military secrets stored aboard it.<ref name="Survivor" />

===Death===
{{Quote|What took you so long?! While you've been chasing shadows, I have been --"<br />"You have been caught harboring a traitor.|Daho Sejan and the Ninth Sister|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
[[File:How-to-beat-ninth-sister-in-star-wars-jedi-survivor.jpg|left|thumb|250px|Sejan was executed by the Ninth Sister for allegedly committing treason by harboring a traitor.]]
After dispatching Sejan's escort of [[stormtrooper]]s and [[patrol trooper]], Kestis's crew sabotaged the yacht and boarded it. Locking himself inside his personal quarters, Sejan bluffed that he had an elite squad ready for the rebels, but Kestis and Akuna broke in and secured him. Kestis used a [[mind trick]] on Sejan to get him to access the terminal, projecting a holomap of the Empire's expansion across the galaxy, shocking his captors. The senator attempted to cut a "deal" with the [[Jedi]], claiming that the latter's group had no chance against the Empire, but his daunting warnings fell on deaf ears as Akuna reminded Sejan of his own negative reputation back on Utapau. To his disappointment, Kestis defiantly refused and brandished his lightsaber in his face, promising that when his own children see this weapon, they'll know the Jedi never stopped fighting for them. However, when the group tried to escape with the Pau'an representative as their captive to deliver to [[Saw Gerrera]], whom Sejan believed would execute him, they were ambushed by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]]. Just as he was about to complain on the lack of punctuality, the senator was [[Death|stabbed]] in the chest by the [[Inquisitor]] [[Ninth Sister]] for his assumed betrayal.<ref name="Survivor" />

===Legacy===
In [[35 ABY]], Sejan was mentioned in ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', a history book written by [[Beaumont Kin]]. Using information garnered from ''[[Imperial Archives]], Section: Senator Daho Sejan, File: ZE14 - seized records'', Kin noted Sejan's ambivalence towards Emperor Palpatine, and that Sejan had been executed for what he called an "unspecified form of treason".<ref name="TRaFotGE" />

==Personality and traits==
Like most Pau'ans, Daho Sejan had grey wrinkled skin, red eye sockets, pointed teeth, and long clawed fingers. Though professing concern for his people and maintaining he had not wanted Utapau to join the Empire, the Pau'an was truly concerned for his own well-being. Believing there was no chance of standing against the burgeoning Imperial forces, the senator instead sought to increase his standing with the Emperor. Sejan also seemed dismissive of the operatives of the Inquisitorius Program, referring to them as "Vader's dogs". The senator's false sense of security - based solely choice of allegiance -, however, made him blind to the intolerance of treachery typical of his Imperial peers, leading to his demise at the hands of the Ninth Sister.<ref name="Survivor" />

He was also known to torture his captives aboard his yacht, with a dedicated section of the vessel containing tools used specifically for extracting information with violence.<ref name="Survivor" />

Concept art for Senator Sejan was done by [[Theo Stylianides]]. The character's species was decided early on due to their intimidation presence, but they consciously decided to move away from the thin and tall body of prior Pau'an characters and instead chose to make him "stocky" and "solid and intimidating". They then further expanded upon it by giving him an opulent and dark costume and layered [[cape]]. The costume's accessories and jewelry were meant to remind of his wealth and power and the layered collar as a representation of his stature. Other designs featured wider shoulder pieces, a brown orange open collar and same-colored leg-long loincloth, or a copper green collar and purple cape.<ref name="Art of JS">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>
