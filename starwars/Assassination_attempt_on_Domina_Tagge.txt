In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Alpha 28 Date" /> [[Ronen Tagge]],<ref name="Aphra 28" /> the scion of the [[House of Tagge]],<ref name="Aphra 4">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 4|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 4]]</ref> conspired with the criminal syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]] to carry out an '''assassination attempt''' on his aunt [[Domina Tagge]], who served as the [[Executive officer|head]] of the [[Tagge Corporation]], so that he could take over the company himself. The attempt included an attack on the [[Tagge Corporation fleet]] by Crimson Dawn forces and a mutiny by many of Domina's employees and personnel that were secretly Crimson Dawn agents. Ronen hired [[Sana Starros]] and her [[Sana Starros' crew|crew]] to carry out the assassination in the midst of the chaos, but Starros was secretly working for Domina. Ronen's attempt ultimately failed, largely due to the betrayal of Starros and the machinations and actions of [[Lapin Tagge]]. For his actions, which consisted of admitted collaboration with Crimson Dawn, sabotage and destruction of Tagge Corporation property, and the assassination attempt itself, Ronen was killed by Domina.<ref name="Aphra 28" />
