'''B-333''', also known as '''Bee-Triple-Three''' or '''Big Bee''', was a [[droid]] who worked alongside the [[black market]] [[smuggler]] [[Argomon]]. By [[229 BBY]], they joined the droid [[5A-G3]]'s [[Eriadu droid intelligence squad|spy network]] to work against the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] in conjunction with the efforts of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] and the [[Jedi Order]], often [[transport]]ing supplies in their [[Argomon's hauler|hauler]] to the Nihil [[prison]] [[Starship|ship]] ''[[Korvix Vorn]]''. That [[Standard year|year]], B-333 and Argomon planted a [[Homing beacon|tracking device]] on the ''Korvix Vorn'', with the droid remaining behind on the Nihil ship as part of an eventual plot to [[Saboteur|sabotage]] the vessel.

Not long afterward, in [[228 BBY]], the Jedi and [[Republic Defense Coalition]] launched [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|raid]] on the prison ship after the tracking device pinpointed the vessel's location, hoping to rescue the [[prisoner]]s trapped aboard. After 5A-G3 and Argomon helped a boarding party secure the prison ship's topside [[cannon]]s, the pair rigged the ''Korvix Vorn'' to explode before fleeing from the vessel with B-333. After returning to the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]], B-333 enjoyed playing games with several of their colleagues. Later, B-333 attended the [[Mrala-Talisola Wedding|wedding]] of the [[Marriage|wives]] [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] and [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]]. As part of the [[Eriaduan resistance]], the droid helped [[Battle of Eriadu|defend]] Eriadu from the invading Nihil, strategizing with others as the fighting continued. Following the Nihil's defeat, B-333 celebrated with his allies.

==Biography==
===Fighting the Nihil===
{{Quote|This is Bee-Triple-Three. He's been working on a black market hauler for the Nihil, which allows him to get in and out of the Occlusion Zone and keep tabs on things for me.|5A-G3 about B-333, to several colleagues|<ref name="THRA 3" />}}
[[File:B333FarzalaTarabal-2023THRA3.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Farzala Tarabal was B-333's contact on the ''Korvix Vorn''.]]
B-333,<ref name="Name">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1757811877889843337|[[Daniel José Older]]|date=2024-02-14|quote=Yes|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/Q369R|archivefile=File:DanielJoseOlder-B333Name-Twitter.png}} {{C|In response to: "@djolder is Bee-Triple-Three officially B-333?"}}</ref> also known as Bee-Triple-Three or Big Bee, was a [[droid]] who worked alongside the [[black market]] [[smuggler]] [[Argomon]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Following the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]]<ref name="THRA 3" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> B-333 became part of the droid [[5A-G3]]'s [[spy]] [[Eriadu droid intelligence squad|network]] against the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] following the group's formation of the [[Occlusion Zone]],<ref name="THRA 4" /> a region of [[Realspace|space]] sealed behind a seemingly impenetrable barrier called the [[Stormwall]] that only Nihil [[Starship|ships]] could pass through.<ref name="THRA 3" />

As part of this, B-333 and Argomon crossed over the Stormwall several [[time]]s to make supply runs of illicit goods to the Nihil. While operating under the guise of tolerating the marauders, they sometimes [[transport]]ed goods to the [[prison]] ship ''[[Korvix Vorn]]''. B-333 cultivated a contact aboard the vessel<ref name="THRA 3" />&mdash;[[Jedi Knight]]<ref name="THRCE" /> [[Farzala Tarabal]], one of its [[prisoner]]s&mdash;whom he collaborated with to pass information to 5A-G3.<ref name="THRA 3" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date-THRA3">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 3|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 3]] takes place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[space station]]'s fall to [[230 BBY]], the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] must take place in [[229 BBY]].</ref> B-333 learned that the ''Korvix Vorn'' planned to make a supply run outside the Occlusion Zone in the near future and reported the information to 5A-G3 upon returning to the [[Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina|Republic outpost]] on<ref name="THRA 3" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Eriadu]]. After 5A-G3 introduced the droid to several [[Jedi]] and [[Galactic Republic]] colleagues and explained his work, B-333 repeated what he had found out.<ref name="THRA 3" />

===A supply run===
{{Quote|All right, Big Bee&hellip;you're up!''"<br />"''Up? Up?! I thought I didn't have to say anything?! Wha&mdash;''"<br />"''You don't. That's the thing. When you get nervous you never shut up.|Argomon and B-333, as they approached the ''Korvix Vorn''|<ref name="THRA 3" />}}
[[File:B333Argomon-2023THRA3.jpg|thumb|right|310px|B-333 and Argomon operated in a black market hauler that crossed the Stormwall on supply runs.]]
As the others began to converse about the ''Korvix Vorn'', B-333 continued mumbling to himself, repeatedly expressing his sharp dislike for the prison ship&mdash;a vessel he found dilapidated and miserable&mdash;and pleasure at having left it behind. 5A-G3 then dispatched B-333 and Argomon to place a [[Homing beacon|tracking device]] on the ''Korvix Vorn'' to help Republic and Jedi forces pinpoint the next time it left the Occlusion Zone. B-333 remained anxious about the endeavor, panicking as Argomon gleefully accelerated their [[Argomon's hauler|hauler's]] speed. The pilot reassured the droid that the Nihil would always let them across the Stormwall, though the latter remained worried. As they approached the ''Korvix Vorn'', Argomon reminded B-333 of his role in their endeavor, and when he expressed confusion, the pilot clarified that he would not stop talking when he was nervous, which would serve as a distraction.<ref name="THRA 3" />

[[File:B333DeblindrixIII-2023THRA3.jpg|thumb|left|220px|B-333 felt unnerved when Deblindrix III crawled on him.]]
After arriving, Argomon delivered several boxes of regenerative [[mycopram]] substance to the Nihil [[scientist]] [[Niv Drendow Apruk]] while Apruk's young [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature&mdash;[[Deblindrix III]]&mdash;slowly crawled on top of B-333, making him uncomfortable. When the creature moved away from him, the droid repeatedly reiterated his hatred of being on the ''Korvix Vorn'' as he went deeper into the ship. Then, B-333 met with Tarabal, who praised the droid for his perfect timing to start planning the prisoners' escape, though the droid only defeatedly expressed his tiredness at the prospect.<ref name="THRA 3" /> B-333 and Argomon successfully planted a tracking device on the ''Korvix Vorn'' after arrival,<ref name="THRA 4" /> and the latter left the former behind on the ''Korvix Vorn'' as part of 5A-G3's eventual plan to [[Saboteur|sabotage]] the ship<ref name="THRA 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 5]]</ref> to return to Eriadu.<ref name="THRA 4" />

Not long afterward,<ref name="THRA 4" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date-THRA4">{{StarlightDate|[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]|one [[Standard year|year]] and two weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the tracking device on the ''Korvix Vorn'' revealed the vessel's location, prompting the Jedi and the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] to lead a [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|raid]] on the prison ship to rescue its prisoners.<ref name="THRA 4" /> Having assumed the mission objective included destroying the ''Korvix Vorn'' after rescuing its prisoners, 5A-G3 and Argomon worked together to formulate a plan to explode the vessel and reunite with B-333, who was still aboard the Nihil vessel.<ref name="THRA 5" />

===''Korvix Vorn'' destruction and return===
{{Quote|Five-ay&hellip;did you?!''"<br />"''I had to go retrieve my asset! I figured I'd leave some gifts behind courtesy of the Eriadu droid intelligence squad, is all.|Zeen Mrala and 5A-G3, after the latter and Argomon exploded the ''Korvix Vorn'' and reuinited with B-333|<ref name="THRA 5" />}}
[[File:5AG3ArgomonB333-2023THRA5.png|thumb|right|300px|5A-G3, Argomon, and B-333 after exploding the ''Korvix Vorn'']]
During the raid, 5A-G3 and Argomon helped their boarding party secure the ''Korvix Vorn''{{'s}} [[cannon]]s before separating from the group to reunite with B-333. After rigging the prison ship to detonate, they fled aboard their boarding party's ship. As they flew away, ''Korvix Vorn'' exploded in [[Fire|flames]], much to the shock of the Jedi and rescued prisoners aboard. After the Republic operative [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]] inquired, 5A-G3 informed those present that their trio had always intended to destroy the ''Korvix Vorn'', leading everyone to sigh in relief and hug each other. B-333 returned to Eriadu with their colleagues and rescued prisoners, grateful to be headed home.<ref name="THRA 5" />

On Eriadu, B-333 joined several Jedi and Republic colleagues for a game involving scoring pucks into illuminated goals. However,<ref name="THRA 6">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 6|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 6]]</ref> Jedi Master<ref name="Escape From Valo">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref> [[Yoda]] contacted the group via hologram and informed them they needed to evacuate because the Stormwall had [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expanded]] and left Eriadu at the border of Republic space. Moments after, the [[Padawan Academic Cruiser]] ''[[Star Hopper]]''&mdash;commandeered by [[the Warden]], the ''Korvix Vorn''{{'s}} former [[Commanding officer|commander]]&mdash;arrived in Eriadu's [[atmosphere]] for an attack to scare those at the Republic outpost. B-333 stared at the ''Star Hopper'' from Eriadu's surface, watching as several of his colleagues led an attempt to reclaim the Jedi Order's former vessel, though their attempt was ultimately unsuccessful.<ref name="THRA 6" />

===Eriadu invasion===
{{Quote|I'll let Sevran know. She won't be happy.''"<br />"''Neither will Farzala. Didn't sound like he has any extra Jedi laying around.|Emelsine Tarkin and 5A-G3 in a strategy meeting about Bri-Phrang's lava reactor core instability, which B-333 attended|<ref name="THRA 15">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 15|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 15]]</ref>}}
Not long afterward, B-333 attended the [[Mrala-Talisola Wedding|wedding]] of Mrala and<ref name="THRA WS">''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III - The Wedding Spectacular]]''</ref> her<ref name="THRA 6" /> fiancée [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] in Eriadu's Arkorayal Cantina. During the ceremony, as the pair were pronounced spouses, the droid held their [[Marriage|wedding]] cake in his claws. The attendees&mdash;including B-333&mdash;celebrated the couple's nuptials briefly before leaving the cantina to [[Battle of Eriadu|fend off]] the Nihil [[Invasion of Eriadu|invading]] the planet.<ref name="THRA WS" />

[[File:AlliesMeeting-2023THRA15.jpg|thumb|left|275px|B-333 and his colleagues gathered to strategize during the Eriadu invasion.]]
In the streets of Bri-Phrang City, B-333 joined his colleagues as part of the [[Eriaduan resistance]], as they engaged in combat between the buildings lining the city's streets. Later, by the seas near the city, the forces defending Eriadu&mdash;which included the droid&mdash;boarded [[rescue speeder]]s and again clashed with the Nihil under the leadership of Tarabal and Qort. Several Nihil operating [[gunboat]]s under "The Warden"'s command were eventually driven back by the group, with B-333 boarding a Nihil gunboat after its capture. After taking over at least one of the gunboats, the Jedi and droids regrouped under Tarabal's command and rallying cry after Qort was injured, intending to continue rebuffing the remaining Nihil on Eriadu.<ref name="THRA 14" />

Later, B-333 joined several of his colleagues&mdash;including Argomon, [[Tarkin family]] member [[Emelsine Tarkin]], 5A-G3, and a [[Unidentified Tarkin family staffer|staffer]]&mdash;in a building in Bri-Phrang City. As the fighting raged on, they strategized their next move after being informed that the Jedi Master [[Yaddle]] needed more Jedi assistance to stabilize the [[lava reactor core]] under the city's streets.<ref name="THRA 15" /> B-333 eventually boarded a vessel piloted by Argomon and kept combatting the Nihil, with the latter informing Tarabal of their efforts' limited effects. Knowing the Warden would not stop his assault until Eriadu was destroyed, he ordered Argomon to work with Jedi Master [[Tera Sinube]] to clear a path for Mrala and Talisola to attack.<ref name="THRA 18">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 18|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 18]]</ref> Not long after, the Eriaduan resistance defeated the Nihil on Eriadu and the Nihil collectively surrendered to the Republic not long afterward.<ref name="Trials">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref>  B-333 and his colleagues celebrated their victory, while also taking time to reminisce on the allies who had lost their lives during the Nihil conflict.<ref name="THRA 20">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 20|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 20]]</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|Hate this hate this hate it bad bad very bad hate it''"<br />"''Your timing is perfect!''"<br />"''Ha&mdash;''ah!!"<br />"''The plot to break free starts ''now!"<br />"''Great. I hate it.|B-333 and Farzala Tarabal|<ref name="THRA 3" />}}
[[File:B333And5AG3-2023THRA3.jpg|thumb|right|165px|B-333 was part of 5A-G3's anti-Nihil network.]]
B-333 had [[Color|blue]] [[sensor]]s and yellow-and-gray plating. He had an illuminated set of five red, white, and blue buttons on his front and two jointed [[limb]]s affixed with maneuverable claws. B-333 could communicate in [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]].<ref name="THRA 3" />

By the time of the [[Nihil conflict]], B-333 was loyal to 5A-G3's spy network, which worked to assist the Republic and the Jedi. However, he often voiced his dislike for the activities he was involved in, having developed a strong revulsion for the ''Korvix Vorn'', which he and Argomon often visited as part of their supply runs across the Stormwall. Argomon felt that the droid had a tendency to babble, especially when nervous, and the droid also occasionally stuttered over his words.<ref name="THRA 3" /> 5A-G3 and Argomon took care to reunite with B-333 before fleeing with and then destroying the ''Korvix Vorn'', unwilling to leave him behind, with the former considering him an asset to their team.<ref name="THRA 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
B-333 first appeared in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 3|third issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]], was published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="THRA 3" /> Phase III on [[February 14]], [[2024]].<ref name="DH3">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-794/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-3|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #3}}</ref> The droid's was introduced as "Bee-Triple-Three" in the issue,<ref name="THRA 3" /> though Older confirmed that his name was officially B-333 on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]].<ref name="Name" /> [[Kristin Baver]]'s [[2025]] reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]'' included one of Tolibao's unfinished panels from<ref name="Artbook">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref> the one-shot comic ''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III - The Wedding Spectacular]]''<ref name="THRA WS" /> that included B-333.<ref name="Artbook" /> ''The Wedding Spectacular'' was written by Older and released by Dark Horse<ref name="THRA WS" /> on [[December 18]], 2024.<ref name="Dark Horse-Wedding">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-812/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III--The-Wedding-Spectacular|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III--The Wedding Spectacular}}</ref>
