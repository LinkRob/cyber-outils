'''''[[Black One]]''''' was a [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] that was used by [[Resistance]] [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]]. Dameron was usually accompanied by his trusty co-[[pilot]], the spherical [[astromech droid]] [[BB-8]].

==Description==
[[File:Black_One_booster_pod_Fathead.png|thumb|left|220px|''Black One'' equipped with a booster pod affixed to the aft of the fuselage.]]
''Black One'' was a [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] manufactured by the [[Incom-FreiTek Corporation]]. Like all T-70 [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] [[Starfighter|fighters]], ''Black One'' was armed with four [[Taim & Bak]] [[KX12 laser cannon]]s.<ref name="ICS" /> ''Black One'' was also equipped with [[ferrosphere paint]] which was used to jam enemy [[missile]] locks.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

For the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation of D'Qar]],<ref name="Episode VIII" /> ''Black One'' was equipped with an [[Overdrive Thruster|overdrive thruster]].<ref name="Hearalds of Hope" /> It was BB-8's favorite starship.<ref name="Adventures of BB-8">''[[Star Wars: The Adventures of BB-8]]''</ref>

==History==
[[Resistance]] [[pilot]] [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] used the X-wing during the [[Cold War]] with the [[First Order]]. During missions with [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] the fighter became damaged several times: in the [[battle over a desert planet]] Dameron crash-landed ''Black One'',<ref name="PD 11" /> therefore placing it out of commission for several missions<ref name="PD 14">[[Poe Dameron 14|''Poe Dameron'' 14]]</ref> until it was repaired by BB-8 and [[Ivee]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 20">[[Poe Dameron 20|''Poe Dameron'' 20]]</ref> When First Order [[Commander]] [[Malarus]] stole the fighter during the [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Resistance)|mission to Cato Neimoidia]], BB-8 saw himself forced to crash-land ''Black One'' again to stop the enemy officer in the cockpit.<ref name="PD 23">[[Poe Dameron 23|''Poe Dameron'' 23]]</ref> While the fighter was being repaired onboard the [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']]<ref name="PD 24">[[Poe Dameron 24|''Poe Dameron'' 24]]</ref> and on [[D'Qar]], Dameron flew a [[Poe Dameron's blue X-wing|standard T-70]]. BB-8 eventually incorporated one of the [[processor]] units of the destroyed Ivee into the fighter.<ref name="PD 25">[[Poe Dameron 25|''Poe Dameron'' 25]]</ref>

<div style="float: right">[[File:IntegratedSFoilsClosed-SWZ.png|thumb|220px|Poe Dameron piloted ''Black One'' into the thermal oscillator]]</div>
Dameron flew it during the [[Battle of Takodana]] and the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], where he used it to destroy the First Order's [[superweapon]], [[Starkiller Base]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> ''Black One'' saw action during the [[Battle of D'Qar|Evacuation of D'Qar]] where Poe destroyed the ''[[Fulminatrix]]''{{'s}} defense [[turret]]s to clear a path for the Resistance [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|bombers]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

''Black One'' was destroyed by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] during the [[Battle of Oetchi]] when he launched torpedoes into the starboard [[hangar]] of the ''Raddus'' from [[Kylo Ren's TIE silencer|his TIE silencer]].<ref name="Episode VIII" /> By [[35 ABY]], Dameron had named an [[Black One (35 ABY)|orange and white T-70 X-wing]] "Black One" as well,<ref name="Last Order">''[[The Last Order]]''</ref> which he flew in the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
