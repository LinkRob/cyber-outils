"'''Bracchia'''" was the code name of a [[Gender|male]] [[Koorivar]] [[spy]] who worked for both the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], he assisted [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s anti-[[Shadowfeed]] operation on [[Murkhana]]. In [[14 BBY]], Bracchia and his [[Imperial Security Bureau]] case officer [[Stellan]] were tricked by [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]] into reporting a communications cache on Murkhana. This enabled the insurgent leader [[Berch Teller]] to steal Tarkin's ship ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' and embark on an [[Berch Teller campaign|insurgency]].

==Biography==
During the [[Clone Wars]], Bracchia worked as a [[Galactic Republic]] operative, notably assisting [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] in his anti-[[Shadowfeed]] operation on [[Murkhana]]. At the end of the war, Republic [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|proclaimed]] himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]], and [[the galaxy]] entered the [[Imperial Era|age]] of the first [[Galactic Empire]]. Bracchia remained in the service of the new government, becoming an intelligence asset for the [[Imperial Security Bureau]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> ISB [[case officer]] [[Stellan]] tasked Bracchia with investigating a cache located in a [[Corporate Alliance medcenter|disused medcenter]] on Murkhana. The Koorivar [[agent]] found the cache and submitted a preliminary report of his mission, allowing the local Imperial forces to place the cache's location under observation. Later on, he was interrogated by [[Moff]] Tarkin and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the Emperor's enforcer. Bracchia answered their questions. After the insurgent leader [[Berch Teller]] stole Tarkin's corvette ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', he played a role in helping them to procure a replacement [[starship]], the ''[[Parsec Predator]]'', from the [[Sugi]] [[crime lord]] [[Faazah]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

During the course of Teller's [[Berch Teller campaign|insurgency]], both Bracchia and Stellan were interrogated by the Emperor himself, in an attempt to find conspirators within the Imperial security personnel. When questioned about the communications cache on Murkhana, he told the Emperor he had no knowledge about the communications devices but was merely following a directive from [[Coruscant]]. His case officer Stellan told the Emperor that [[Imperial Intelligence|Military Intelligence]] had sent the ISB a [[holovid]] of the communications cache on Murkhana. He then sent Bracchia to confirm the cache. The Emperor realized the holovid was a counterfeit that had been created by someone with access to the Imperial codes and to devices capable of subverting the [[HoloNet]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

Meanwhile, Lord Vader and Tarkin discovered that [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]], the [[Director]] of the [[Naval Intelligence Agency]], had planed the communications cache and was colluding with Teller. Rancit had planted the cache with the intention of luring Tarkin to Murkhana so that Teller and his followers could steal the ''Carrion Spike'' in order to attack Imperial installations across the galaxy. This information enabled the Imperials to neutralize Rancit and Teller's [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Personality and traits==
Bracchia was a tall Koorivar [[Gender|man]] who towered over Wilhuff Tarkin. His cranial horn was of average size for his species. However, his facial ridges were scarred. The Koorivar also wore tattered robes. Bracchia was a useful spy for both the Republic and its successor state, the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Tarkin" />
