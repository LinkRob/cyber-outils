{{Quote|Hi B6, I'm C9-78. My master has given me a day to travel outside of Black Spire and I'm hoping to find someone to travel with me.|translated=1|C9-78 calls into Droid Station Frequency 3-7-6-4-3-7|<ref name="GEStaff" />}}
'''C9-78''' was a [[C-series]]<ref name="GEStaff" /> [[astromech droid]] manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]].<ref name="Stats" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[droid]] had resided in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] for some [[time]], but had never left the outpost.<ref name="GEStaff" />

After C9-78's [[navigation computer]] was updated, its [[C9-78's master|master]] gave it a [[Standard day|day]] to leave the outpost, and in hopes of finding someone who could help it navigate the area and find the coolest locations,<ref name="GEStaff" /> the droid called in to the radio show [[Droid Station Frequency 3-7-6-4-3-7]] on station [[BSO 401.72]]. In [[binary]], it informed the host, [[B6-24]] of its desire for a travel companion,<ref name="GEBSO">{{GEAttr|BSO 401.72}}</ref> stating that it had never visited the [[Surabat River Valley]] but had heard it was beautiful.<ref name="GEStaff" /> B6-24 confirmed that the area was lovely up close and asked C9-78 a little about itself before wishing the droid luck.<ref name="GEBSO" /> C9-78 thanked B6-24<ref name="GEStaff" /> and ended the call.<ref name="GEBSO" />

==Behind the scenes==
C9-78 was created for the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GEBSO" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The droid can be heard speaking in binary on the land's background radio loop,<ref name="GEBSO" /> with a translation of what it is saying available from staff.<ref name="GEStaff" />
