The '''Bar'leth Group''' was a group of the [[Ascendant]] [[Religion|cult]] that was led by [[Miril]], the overall leader of the Ascendant. The group operated from<ref name="Aphra 29" /> the [[Tomb of the Spark Eternal]]<ref name="Aphra 19" /> on [[Bar'leth]].<ref name="Aphra 29" /> The group were eventually [[Slaughter of the Ascendant|slaughtered]] by the [[Sith]] which resulted in the Ascendant's destruction.<ref name="Aphra 22">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 22|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 22]]</ref>
