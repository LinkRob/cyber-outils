'''Binoculars''', also called '''binocs''',<ref name="Aftermath novel">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> were a type of hand-held device used to view objects at a distance. Types of binoculars included [[electrobinoculars]], [[macrobinoculars]],<ref name="Ep IV" /> [[quadnoculars]],<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> and [[optical binoculars]].<ref name="TROS_VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
