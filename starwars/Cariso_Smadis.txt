[[Ensign]] '''Cariso Smadis''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served in the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Rebel Alliance Fleet]]. He was stationed aboard the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Tantive IV]]'', the [[consular ship]] of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], when it fled from the [[planet]] [[Scarif]] with the [[Death Star plans|plans]] to the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] in [[1 BBY]].

The [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]'' pursued the ''Tantive IV'' and disabled it over the planet [[Tatooine]]. Smadis was then among those on board who took position at the port [[airlock]] and engaged Imperials [[stormtrooper]]s there when they boarded the ship. Ultimately the Imperials took control of the vessel, but only after Smadis and the others had bought Organa the [[time]] she needed to get the plans off of the [[Starship|ship]].

==Biography==
Cariso Smadis was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[ensign]] in the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. He was stationed on board the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Tantive IV]]'' to guard [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]],<ref name="Force Collection" /> Alderaan's [[Senator|representative]] in the [[Imperial Senate]], who used the [[starship]] as her [[consular ship]].<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]], during which the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' is assigned to take [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to [[Tatooine]], to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Organa and the ''Tantive IV'' were assigned a [[Secret mission to Tatooine|mission]] to the planet [[Tatooine]], but before the vessel could finish undergoing repairs at the [[Great Temple|rebel headquarters]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]], the Alliance fleet was scrambled to [[Battle of Scarif|support]] [[Rogue One|rebel forces]] attempting to steal the [[Death Star plans|plans]] for the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] from the Empire on the planet [[Scarif]].<ref name="ROn">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref>

Still being repaired, the ''Tantive IV'' traveled to the [[Scarif system]] in one of the hangars of the [[MC75 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Profundity]]''. During the battle at Scarif, the [[Death Star]] plans were transmitted to the ''Profundity'',<ref name="Raymus">{{StoryCite|story=Raymus|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> but when the [[cruiser]] was disabled by the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> ''[[Devastator]]'',<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> the plans were then transferred onto the ''Tantive IV'', which launched from the ''Profundity'' and escaped from the Scarif system.<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> The ''Devastator'' gave pursuit, and was able to catch up to the corvette over Tatooine, where the Imperials were able to disable the vessel.<ref name="ANH" />

As the corvette was being pulled into the hangar of the ''Devastator'' by a [[tractor beam]], the troopers raced through the corridors of the ''Tantive IV'' to take up a defensive position at the port [[airlock]], where the rebels' [[sensor]]s told them the Imperials would board. Smadis was crouched and ready his weapon as a [[TIE boarding craft|TIE boarding shuttle]] then cut through the port airlock,<ref name="Rise">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Rise of the Rebellion and the Battle of Yavin}}</ref> after which Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s began pouring through onto the ''Tantive IV''. Smadis and the others exchanged fire with the Imperials, but were all [[Death|killed]] or forced to retreat from the airlock.<ref name="ANH" /> However, as intended, the troopers had bought enough [[time]]<ref name="Rise" /> for Organa to give the plans to the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] and for the [[droid]] to then escape the ''Tantive IV'' before the Imperials took control of the whole vessel.<ref name="ANH" /> After capturing the ''Tantive IV'', the Empire announced to the media that the vessel had instead been destroyed in a catastrophic [[meteor]] collision that killed everyone on board.<ref name="Scum">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Like all the other rebels on board the ''Tantive IV'', he had sworn allegiance to the planet Alderaan<ref name="Bust20">{{BustCollectionCite|48|Character}}</ref> and served the [[House of Organa]] faithfully, volunteering to give his life if necessary for the rebel cause.<ref name="Rise" /> Smadis had light skin, [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s, and brown [[hair]].<ref name="ANH" />

==Equipment==
Smadis wore<ref name="ANH" /> the black and light blue [[Clothing|uniform]] of the [[Alderaanian consular security]],<ref name="CVD" /> which had been adopted by much of the Alliance. The uniform included a white [[Alderaanian fleet trooper helmet]] with an integrated [[comlink]] and flash visor,<ref name="HC20">{{HelmetCollectionCite|20|Helmets}}</ref> although Smadis did not lower the visor for the battle on the ''Tantive IV''. He used a [[DH-17 blaster pistol]] in combat.<ref name="ANH" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cariso Smadis first appeared in the [[1977]] original trilogy film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH" /> The character's scenes were filmed on Stage 9 at [[Elstree Studios]] between [[June 29]] and [[July 16]], [[1976]].<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] Smadis's name was used in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile card game]] ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'',<ref name="Force Collection" /> which initially launched in [[2013]]. Although the launch of ''Force Collection'' predates<ref name="Force Collection release">{{SW|url=news/announcing-star-wars-force-collection|text=Announcing Star Wars: Force Collection|nolive=1}}</ref> the ''Star Wars'' canon reset of [[2014]],<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]] confirmed that the game was kept updated to fit with canon.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|holocronkeeper|status/846807700482666496|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=I'm not gonna go through the trouble of changing them and I don't have any reason to believe currently that anyone would. [&hellip;] On the off-chance that we DID have a current need to change any of these, we would have for Force Collection.|archivedate=20210505080831}} &mdash; [[Leland Chee]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] has confirmed that [[in-universe]] information from ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' conforms with ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]].</ref> The [[Cariso Smadis/Legends|name]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in the third chapter of [[StarWars.com]]'s [[Visual Guides]] feature, which was published on the [[April 30]], [[2007]].<ref name="Visual Guide">{{SWArchive|url=episode-iv/explore/visualguides/chp03.html|text=Episode IV Visual Guide — Chapter 3: Imperial Boarding Party}}</ref>
