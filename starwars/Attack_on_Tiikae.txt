During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] '''attacked''' [[Tiikae]]. A [[Jedi]] [[starship]] travelling in the area, containing [[Jedi Master]] [[Cohmac Vitus]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] and their [[Padawan]]s, [[Reath Silas]] and [[Imri Cantaros]], diverted to assist in repelling the attack. Rwoh had a [[Force vision]] of the attack while the starship was in [[hyperspace]] before she learned of what was going on, in which she saw an unusual object in the city under attack.<ref name="OOTS" />
