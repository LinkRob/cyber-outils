'''Dedlanite''' was a [[mineral]] used in the manufacture of [[blaster]]s. It could be found in the [[Andelm beetle caverns]] on the [[planet]] [[Andelm IV]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[crime lord]] [[Jaum]] forced [[Mining|miners]] to collect dedlanite, which he sold to the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Part I">[[Chewbacca (2015) 1|''Chewbacca'' (2015) 1]]</ref> [[Standard year|Years]] later,<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[First Order]] used dedlanite to make blasters and mined for it in [[asteroid]]s at [[Station Theta Black]].<ref name="Station Theta Black (episode)" />
