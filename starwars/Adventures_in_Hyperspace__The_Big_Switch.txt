'''''Adventures in Hyperspace: The Big Switch''''' was the planned third volume in the ''[[Star Wars: Adventures in Hyperspace]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] young-reader series, which follows the [[Smuggler/Legends|smugglers]] [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] in the employ of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba the Hutt]] prior to the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. The book was to be written by [[Ryder Windham]], illustrated by [[Jeffery Carlisle]], and published by [[Scholastic]] on [[September 1]], [[2010]].

Windham completed the manuscript for ''The Big Switch'' before he was informed that the third and [[Adventures in Hyperspace: The Last Blade Master|fourth]] ''Adventures in Hyperspace'' books had been canceled. The completed manuscript is now available on author [[Joseph Bongiorno]]'s fan site, [http://www.starwarstimeline.net/AdventuresinHyperspace.htm The ''Star Wars'' Expanded Universe Timeline].

==Publisher's summary==
''A routine job for Jabba goes haywire when Han and Chewie realize the "sculpture" they're supposed to transport is stolen&hellip;and much more than a decorative piece of art!''

''The ''Millennium Falcon'' will have to race its way out of this tight corner—and right into the Blockade Runner's Derby!—if Han and Chewie want to make it back to Tatooine in one piece. Can they win the race and finish the job?''
