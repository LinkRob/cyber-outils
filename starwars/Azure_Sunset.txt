The '''''Azure Sunset''''' was a luxury [[sail barge]] owned by the [[Pantoran]] [[noble]] [[Kyja Cho]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Kyja Cho hosted an event aboard the ''Azure Sunset'', summoning [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]]s and [[Corporate Sector Authority]] executives to view a rare [[krayt dragon pearl]] Cho had in her art collection. Unbeknownst to Cho, an [[Inquisitor]] from the [[Galactic Empire]] infiltrated the sail barge and was posed as an executive of the CSA.<ref name="EV" />
