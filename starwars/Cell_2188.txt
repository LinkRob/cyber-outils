'''Cell 2188''' was a holding cell at a [[Rekkana work camp|work camp]] ran by [[Commodore]] [[Visler Aqorian Korda III]]'s [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Visler Korda's faction|faction]] on the [[planet]] [[Rekkana]].<ref name="Hare-Brained Heist" /> Between [[4 ABY]] and [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[human]] [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] tracked a [[distress signal]] to the work camp, where he snuck inside the camp and opened the cell door, surprised to find the [[Lepi]] smuggler [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki|Jaxxon]] inside. Jaxxon, who was lying down on the bed of the cell, got captured by the Galactic Empire after he was hired to free the [[Rekk]] [[Chancellor]] [[Asera]]. After informing Calrissian that Asera was in the next cell, the Lepi stole [[Lando Calrissian's X-8 Night Sniper|Calrissian's]] [[Lando Calrissian's X-8 Night Sniper|X-8 Night Sniper]] to blast a hole in the cell wall, allowing for the two smugglers to push through the hole and into Asera's cell.<ref name="Hare-Brained Heist" />

Later on, Korda's presentation of the new [[Artificial intelligence|automated]] [[TIE series|TIE fighter series]] [[starfighter]]s, the [[Auto-fighter]] was interrupted when the Auto-fighters opened fire on the work camp. The fighters, which the Rekk had gained control of, destroyed the camp and Cell 2188 along with it.<ref name="Hare-Brained Heist" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cell 2188 appeared in the lead story of [[Star Wars Adventures Annual 2019|''Star Wars Adventures Annual'' 2019]], "[[Hare-Brained Heist]]" which was part of the [[2017]] anthology [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'']]. The story was written by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Charlie Kirchoff]],<ref name="Hare-Brained Heist" /> and published on [[August 14]], [[2019]]<ref name="Annual 2019">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-Annual-2019/digital-comic/760328|text=Star Wars Adventures Annual 2019}}</ref>
