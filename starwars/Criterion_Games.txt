'''Criterion Games''' is a video game developer, known primarily for their work on the ''[[Wikipedia:Burnout (series)|Burnout]]'' series. They developed the [[Endor]] Chase mission in [[2015]]'s ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'' for [[DICE]]<ref name="IGN Galactic Racer">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ign.com/articles/the-big-star-wars-galactic-racer-interview-why-its-not-open-world-how-its-story-mode-works-and-when-its-set|text=The Big Star Wars: Galactic Racer Interview — Why It's Not Open World, How Its Story Mode Works, and When It's Set|author=Tom Phillips|date=2026-02-17|archivedate=20260217144118|work=''[[Wikipedia:IGN|IGN]]''}}</ref> and the game's "''Rogue One'': X-wing VR Mission".<ref name="X-Wing VR announcement">{{EA|subdomain=starwars|url=starwars/battlefront/news/x-wing-vr-mission-announce|text=Star Wars™ Battlefront: X-Wing VR Mission on Target for Release Date this Holiday|nolive=1}}</ref> They were also responsible for the development of vehicle and space combat in [[2017]]'s ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.ign.com/articles/2017/04/15/battlefront-2-multiplayer-preview-bigger-better-and-more-emotional|text=Star Wars Battlefront 2 Multiplayer Preview: Bigger, Better and&hellip; More Emotional|author=Skrebels, Joe|date=2017-04-15|archivedate=20190929154154}}</ref> [[Fuse Games]], the developers of ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Racer]]'', were founded from prior members of Criterion Games.<ref name="Galactic Racer Reveal">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galactic-racer-reveal-trailer|text=We're Going Back to Jakku in the Trailer for ''Star Wars: Galactic Racer'' - Exclusive Interview}}</ref>
