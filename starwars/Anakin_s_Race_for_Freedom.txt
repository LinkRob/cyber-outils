'''''Anakin's Race for Freedom''''' is a children's book written by [[Alice Alfonsi]] and illustrated by [[Jose Miralles]]. It features [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker's]] story during the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. The book, part of the [[Random House]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' Storybook series and includes twelve stickers.

==Publisher's summary==
''Anakin Skywalker'' works as a slave in Watto's junk shop. One day a group of strange travelers comes to the shop in need of help. What happens next will change Anakin's life forever.''
