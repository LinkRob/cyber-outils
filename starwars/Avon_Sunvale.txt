'''Avon Sunvale''',<ref name="Wayseeker" /> also known as<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> and remembered by her family as '''Avon Starros''',<ref name="Sana Starros 2">[[Sana Starros 2|''Sana Starros'' 2]]</ref> was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who was the daughter of [[Galactic Republic]] [[senator]] [[Ghirra Starros]] and a [[Avon Starros' father|member]] of the [[Starros Clan]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> [[Reproduction|Born]] around [[244 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Starros was an aspiring inventor who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. At some point, she was [[Kidnapping of Avon Starros|kidnapped]] but later rescued by her [[Avon Starros' uncles|uncles]], who killed [[Unidentified local group|those]] who had taken her. The incident scared Ghirra and convinced her to keep her daughter close by at all times until she sent Avon to [[Port Haileap]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> When Starros was little more than ten, she visited the [[Jedi Archives|Jedi Temple Archives]] on [[Coruscant]] with her mother, where she found their public section on kyber crystal theory to be lacking.<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> Sometime after the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], Starros, along with her [[protocol droid]] [[J-6]], was supervised by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] on a [[cruiser]] trip to the [[Dedication of the Starlight Beacon|dedication ceremony]] of the new [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]]. However, when [[bomb]]s [[Attack on the Steady Wing|went off]] aboard the cruiser, Starros, J-6, Rwoh, [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]], and [[Honesty Weft]] escaped in a [[shuttle]] and wound up stranded on [[Wevo]], a nearby [[jungle]] [[moon]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

After she was [[Attack on Port Haileap|captured]] by the [[Nihil]],<ref name="Mission to Disaster">''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]''</ref> Avon began to realize there was those in [[the galaxy]] who would use her and her research to create terrible things, and she grew reluctant to share her discoveries with people. Instead, Avon created a [[Avon's journals|series of journals]] in which she documented all of her research on [[crystal]] theory, which she then stored in a [[data cube]], along with a message for how it was to be used by anyone who found it, and then left it in her family's vault. To protect the cube, Avon added a pass code.<ref name="Sana Starros 2" /> During the Nihil's [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], Avon was present on the station alongside Cantaros and J-6. The girl was among those who were assumed to have [[Death|died]],<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> though she had actually survived thanks to her mother who ensured Avon escaped the destruction.<ref name="Databank" />

At some point, Avon went missing, and her descendants were not aware of what happened to her as she was not documented in the family archives. When she was young, [[Thea Starros]] found Avon's data cube and unlocked it, accessing the secrets Avon had left in it. Sometime afterwards, the cube was taken by the [[Galactic Empire]], and<ref name="Sana Starros 2" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Sana date">{{SanaStarrosDate}}</ref> Thea, along with her daughter [[Mevera Starros]], and her nieces [[Sana Starros]] and [[Aryssha Starros|Aryssha]], set out to recover the data cube from the Empire.<ref name="Sana Starros 2" />

After the [[Nihil conflict]], Starros and other scientists fled the most populous parts of the galaxy and traveled to the [[Wild Space]] planet [[Ingae]], co-founding the [[Collective (Ingae)|Collective]], a group who wanted to pursue their experiments for the good of the galaxy, away from those who would like to profit off their breakthroughs. During her time on Ingae, Avon had a child named [[Felix Sunvale]], and taught the girl [[Florinda Jackard]] to build artificial crystal matrices. Eventually, she died and a [[Avon's funeral|funeral]] was held, which was attended by Lompop and Rwoh.<ref name="Wayseeker" />

==Behind the scenes==
Avon Starros first appeared in the junior novel ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> The publisher's summary for ''A Test of Courage'' stated that Avon was eleven years old during the events of the book, whereas Ireland stated in a [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tlshzMnfbiE Comic-Con panel] that Avon was twelve years old. Ireland later clarified that Avon was indeed twelve during the events of ''A Test of Courage''.<ref name="Avon Starros age">{{Twitter|justinaireland|status/1287069113567895559|[[Justina Ireland]]|quote=Avon's twelve! She had a birthday since that was written. 🎂🎊🎉|archivefile=File:Avon-Starros-age.png}}</ref> Ireland stated she considers Starros her favorite character that she created during the multimedia project.<ref name="Justina">{{Bluesky|justinaireland.com|post/3lkcfocuuyc2l|[[Justina Ireland]]|date=2025-03-14|quote=One that I created? Avon Starros. One someone else created? Keeve Trennis.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/bpy3I}}</ref>
