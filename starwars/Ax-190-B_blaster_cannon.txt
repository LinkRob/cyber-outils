The '''Ax-190-B blaster cannon''' was an underslung [[blaster cannon]] produced by [[BlasTech Industries]]. A rotatable Ax-190-B blaster cannon was installed on the undercarriage of the [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] and combined with an automated computer targeting system for rearward defense against enemy fighters.<ref name="RSOWM" />
