An '''astronomer''' was a profession in which an individual [[map]]ped [[the galaxy]] and studied [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Allegiance 1">[[Allegiance 1|''Allegiance'' 1]]</ref> Around [[9 ABY]],<ref>{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> astronomer [[Kh'ymm]]—who worked from the [[Observatory Moon]]—claimed to have in her archives maps of every [[star]] in the galaxy, as well as [[hyperspace]] routes between them.<ref name="SC 103" /> The astronomers of the [[Tah'Nuhna (species)|Tah'Nuhna]] charted the cosmos.<ref name="Allegiance 1" /> One ancient astronomer, [[Harkrova]], discovered a [[star system]], which was named the [[Harkrova system]] after its discoverer.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>
