The '''AD-1S Modular Multi-Role Starfighter''' was a class of [[starfighter]] manufactured by [[Koensayr Manufacturing]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Special Modifications" />

==Characteristics==
[[Koensayr Manufacturing|Koensayr]] sold the [[starfighter]]'s central chassis for 25,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. It was flown by one [[pilot]] with space for two passengers. Although unarmed and without a hyperdrive, it featured a magnetic modular rail system for customizable outfitting.<ref name="Special Modifications" />

==History==
The corporate designers believed that the modular system would be valued by military buyers as they could buy one central design, instead of more specialized variants.<ref name="Special Modifications" />

Ultimately, the cost of customization and proprietary parts reduced interest in the starfighter, though it found life in service to [[Technician|mechanics]] who could customize it to their liking.<ref name="Special Modifications" />

==Behind the scenes==
The AD-1S appears in [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[2016]] [[roleplaying game]] sourcebook ''[[Special Modifications]]''.<ref name="Special Modifications" />
