'''Barbra See Wai Ho''' is an employee of [[Industrial Light & Magic]] who worked on the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]''. Barbra Ho was born in Hong Kong, lived in New Zealand, and is based in Australia where she works for ILM as a digital paint and roto artist.<ref name="Boris">{{WebCite|url=https://blog.borisfx.com/ask-the-artist-with-barbra-ho-digital-paint-roto-artist|text=Ask the Artist with Barbra Ho, Digital Paint & Roto Artist|author=Jessie Electa Petrov|date=2022-01-03|work=[[Wikipedia:Boris FX|Boris FX]]|archivedate=20250115102305}}</ref>
