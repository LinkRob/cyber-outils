The '''57-D delivery speeder''' was a model of [[speeder bike]] manufactured by the [[Aratech Repulsor Company]]. It was equipped with a storage hopper and an Aratech [[repulsorlift generator]] that could propel it to extremely fast speeds in order to swiftly deliver its cargo.<ref name="DRVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|103}}</ref> [[Axis network]] leader [[Luthen Rael]] and local [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] stole a 57-D from an abandoned delivery warehouse in [[Ferrix City]] to escape from pursuing [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces]] [[Tac-Corpo|corpos]].<ref name="Reckoning">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref>
