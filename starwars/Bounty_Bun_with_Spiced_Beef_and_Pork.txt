'''Bounty Bun with Spiced Beef and Pork''' was a [[sandwich]] comprised of [[Foodstuff|beef]], pork, other fillings, and a bun. The dish was served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. It was consumable by [[human]]s.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bounty Bun with Spiced Beef and Pork will be available in the [[in-universe|real world]] at Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disneyland]]. It is set to debut on [[May 21]], [[2026]]. The menu describes it as being served, "with [[Wikipedia:Tomato chutney|tomato chutney]], golden [[Wikipedia:Aioli|aioli]], and sautéed blue [[Wikipedia:Onion|onions]]."<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=disney-experiences/disney-eats-mandalorian-and-grogu-menus-at-disney-parks|text=Disney Eats: New Mandalorian and Grogu Menus and More at Disney Parks}}</ref> 

As the debut of this dish is after the [[April 29]], 2026, shift in timeline of portions of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge at Disneyland from the [[sequel trilogy]] to multiple periods,<ref name="DPB Shift">{{DPB|url=dlr/star-wars-galaxys-edge-timeline-expands-at-disneyland/|text=''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge'' Timeline Soon Expands at Disneyland}}</ref> the timeframe for when this dish was served at the in-universe Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo is unclear. This differs from food and beverages served throughout the land before the timeline shift, which were dated to [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within the original iteration of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>
