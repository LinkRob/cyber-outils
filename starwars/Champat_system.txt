The '''Champat system''' was located within an intersecting area of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Interior]] regions<ref name="Interior" /> in grid square M-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> It contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Champat]], where the [[human]] [[Caluan Ematt]] was [[Reproduction|born]] in [[20 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> An industrial location, Champat was a major resource and production hub for the [[Galactic Empire]] by the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Champat [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Systems" /> Both works were written by [[Jason Fry]] and released on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
