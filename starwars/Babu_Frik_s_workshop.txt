'''Babu Frik's workshop'''<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> was a workshop utilized by the [[Anzellan]] [[Babu Frik]] on [[Kijimi]]. It was here that Frik removed [[C-3PO]]'s programming block during the [[mission to Kijimi]] and allowed him to read the location of the [[Emperor's wayfinder]] as found on [[Blade of Ochi of Bestoon|Ochi's dagger]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
