==Behind the scenes==
The weapon shared its base model, albeit with colors and textures changed, with [[Teroj's blaster]]. [[Lucasfilm Animation]] artist [[Aaron Pham]] created concept art for the blaster.<ref name="ConceptArt">{{SW|url=from-beneath-concept-art-gallery|text="From Beneath" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> Flobb's blaster first appeared in "[[From Beneath]],"<ref name="From Beneath">{{Resistance|From Beneath}}</ref> the sixth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] of the ''Star Wars Resistance'', which aired on [[November 10]], 2019.<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref>
