The '''CS14 "Ghost" light blaster pistol''' was a powerful yet easy-to-conceal light [[blaster pistol]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]].<ref name="Cam" />

==Characteristics==
Small, lightweight, and easily concealed, the CS14 was one of the smallest full-fledged [[blaster pistol]]s on the market, and was considered a premier personal defense blaster.<ref name="Cam" />

Roughly the size and weight of a standard [[hold-out blaster]], the CS14 was built using lightweight [[polycarbonate]]s and [[sensor]]-proof alloys, making the weapon virtually invisible to most [[Weapon detector|weapon scanners]]. As such, on an individual with the right [[holster]] and clothing, it was very difficult to detect without an extremely thorough search.<ref name="Cam" />

==History==
Marketed primarily to [[detective]]s and investigators, the CS14 was adopted by state intelligence agencies across the galaxy as a standard-issue weapon for field agents due to the weapon's size, power, and ease of concealment.<ref name="Cam" />
