'''Chates''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who became a [[scrumrat]] in the [[White Worms]] gang on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] after his [[Chates' father|father]] was [[Death|killed]] by muggers. He looked up to fellow gang member [[Rebolt]] and hoped to impress him by beating up the scrumrat [[Han Solo|Han]], whom he saw escape from the gang's leader, [[Lady]] [[Proxima]].

==Biography==
At a young age, the [[human]] [[Gender|male]] Chates traveled to [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] with his [[Chates' father|father]], who worked as a [[farmer]]. The pair visited a bank in the [[city]], but during the visit, muggers attacked and [[Death|killed]] Chates's [[Parent|father]], leaving him alone in the city. He quickly fell in with the [[White Worms]] gang, who were based out of a [[Den of the White Worms|lair]] in an abandoned industrial complex in the city.<ref name="SoloOG" />

Chates became a [[scrumrat]] within the gang,<ref name="SoloOG" /> and in the [[Standard year|year]] [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' that take place on [[Corellia]] to six years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]]; therefore, the events on Corellia in ''Solo'' must take place in [[13 BBY]].</ref> was present in the den when fellow scrumrat [[Han Solo|Han]] was brought before [[Lady]] [[Proxima]], leader of the gang. Han, who had returned from a trade with no goods for Proxima, claimed to be holding a [[Thermal detonator]] above his head, at which point Chates got down on his knees for cover. However, Han actually only held a rock, which he used to break a shutter behind the gang leader and bathe her in light, which burned her skin. Using the distraction, Han then pushed through the other scrumrats and escaped.<ref name="Solo" />

==Personality and traits==
Chates looked up to [[Rebolt]], a high-ranking thug within the White Worms, and hoped to impress him by beating up Han.<ref name="SoloOG" /> He had dark skin, [[Color|black]] [[hair]], and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="Solo" />

==Equipment==
Chates wore a gray [[Salt|brine]]-stained [[Clothing|coat]] and [[scarf]] over a black shirt with a pair of gray pants and a gray headband. He carried a portable [[HoloNet]] transceiver and used a pipe as a makeshift weapon.<ref name="SoloOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chates first appeared in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> The character was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" />
