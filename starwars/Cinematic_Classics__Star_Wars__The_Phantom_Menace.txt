'''''Cinematic Classics: Star Wars: The Phantom Menace''''' is a hardcover book by [[Tom Huddleston]] published as part of the [[Star Wars Cinematic Classics|''Star Wars'' Cinematic Classics]] on [[August 1]], [[2024]].<ref name="Amazon" /> The book serves as a visual re-telling of the [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]<ref name="Re-Telling Twitter">{{Twitter|username=StudioBooks|url=status/1818981230609641628|name=[[Studio Press]]|date=2024-08-01|quote=Cinematic Classics: Star Wars: The Phantom Menace by @TomHuddleston_ is a visual re-telling of the fourth Star Wars film 25 years after it first hit screens, featuring artwork from the original Lucasfilm concept artists including legendary Doug Chiang.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/SEXiI}}</ref> and uses concept art to that end. The book further celebrates the movie's twenty-fifth anniversary.<ref name="Amazon" /> It was released alongside the [[Cinematic Classics: Star Wars: A New Hope|''A New Hope'' Cinematic Classics]]. Despite that simultaneous release date,<ref name="Amazon Cinematic">{{WebCite|url=https://uk.bookshop.org/p/books/a-new-hope-star-wars-cinematic-classics-tom-huddleston/7664376|text=A New Hope (Star Wars Cinematic Classics)|work=[https://uk.bookshop.org Bookshop.org]|archivedate=20260126192415}}</ref> ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' identified ''The Phantom Menace'': Cinematic Classics as the first book the series.<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|226|Launchpad}}</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
'''''CELEBRATING 25 YEARS OF THE PHANTOM MENACE IN 2024.'''''<ref name="Amazon" />

''A retelling of'' Star Wars ''Episode I:'' The Phantom Menace featuring stunning development art from the original Lucasfilm concept artists.''<ref name="Amazon" />

''Relive the excitement of the cinematic classic The Phantom Menace, the thrilling tale of galactic conquest which begins the epic Skywalker Saga. Two Jedi Knights are dispatched on a mission to the peaceful planet Naboo - only to uncover a shadowy enemy plotting to unleash havoc across the Republic...''<ref name="Amazon" />

''Enjoy the hit movie through this all-ages retelling accompanied by concept art from the original artists. Alongside the art, fascinating facts about each piece give an insight into the design process behind iconic characters and settings like Darth Maul and Tattooine.''<ref name="Amazon" />

==Continuity==
