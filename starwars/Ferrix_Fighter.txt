The '''Ferrix Fighter''' was a costume sometimes worn by the [[Wookiee]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Grozz]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Mighty Champion|standard costume]]. It resembled common [[Ferrixian]] garb worn by individuals such as the [[Time Grappler]] of [[Ferrix City]] on the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]].<ref name="Time Grappler">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}}</ref><ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ferrix Fighter was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Grozz, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Ferrix Clubs]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing the Ferrix Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
