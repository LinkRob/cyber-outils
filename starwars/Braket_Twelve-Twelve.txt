'''Braket Twelve-Twelve''', or '''Braket 12-12''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]], who served [[Fyzen Gor]]'s [[Brotherhood of Wire and Bone]] on the moon [[Grava]]<ref name="Last Shot" /> in [[7 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Last Shot]]'' to [[7 ABY]].</ref> He had a pale face, that was ratcheted with scars, an empty eye socket and a corruption of stitches and filth where his nose should've been. [[Nine-Seven Saquanz]] and he were the first to encounter [[Han Solo]] and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] when they arrived on Grava to find former Imperial Admiral [[Seven-Seven Dirgeos|Ruas Fastent]]. They asked them to hand over their weapons, but as the two scoundrels refused to do so, several members of the cult emerged from the bushes including [[Balthamus]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
