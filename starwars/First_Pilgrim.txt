The '''First Pilgrim''' was an [[Unidentified Angcord species|alien]] individual who led the [[Pilgrim Warriors]] on the [[planet]] [[Angcord]].<ref name="QotJ" /> In [[482 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]'' establishes the flashbacks of ''[[The High Republic Adventures: Quest of the Jedi]]'' take place a [[Standard century|hundred]] years before the events of [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|''The High Republic'' Phase II]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the First Pilgrim served the [[Lady of Angcord]] when she met with [[Jedi Master]] [[Barnabas Vim]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Vix Fonnick]], who were visiting the planet in search of the fabled [[Echo Stone]].<ref name="QotJ" />
