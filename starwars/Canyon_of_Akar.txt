The '''Canyon of Akar''', was a winding serpentine valley on the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> It stretched below [[Mount Selasa]] from a cliff known as the [[Witch's Finger]] in the [[Great Basin]] region towards the [[Karaba Foothills]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> The canyon was inhabited by [[atele]]s, [[clever-bird]]s, [[bladder-bug]]s,<ref name="Aftermath" /> and [[Venga]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Canyon of Akar first appeared in the [[2015]] novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] by [[Chuck Wendig]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> It was later first pictured in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]] though it was not directly identified within the game.<ref name="Outlaws" /> It's location within the game can be deduced by description of the canyon and its position to the [[Witch's Finger]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> which is included in the game.<ref name="Outlaws" />
