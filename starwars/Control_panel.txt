'''Control panels''' were lighted panels that could be hacked and used for surveillance purposes. During the time after the [[Battle of Crait]], control panels were sometimes hacked into by [[First Order]] operatives, while [[Resistance]] agents on the planet would attempt to access these panels to remove the First Order's programming.<ref name="Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]]</ref>
