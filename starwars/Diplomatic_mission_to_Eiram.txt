In [[387 BBY]],<ref name="Date note" /> [[Prince]] [[Niko A'lbaran]] of [[E'ronoh]] traveled to [[Eiram]] for a '''diplomatic mission'''. However, due to an accident, he drowned in Eiram's [[ocean]]s while on the world. Infuriated and blaming Eiram for his heir's death, [[Monarchy|Monarch]] [[A'lbaran]] re-incited the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]].<ref name="Convergence" />
