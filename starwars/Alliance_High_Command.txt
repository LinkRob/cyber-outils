The '''Alliance High Command''', also known as '''Alliance Command''', '''Rebel Command''', '''Rebel Alliance High Command''', '''Rebel Council''', '''Alliance Council''', '''Rebel Alliance's ruling council''', '''Alliance's ruling council''', '''Rebellion Command''', or the '''Rebel Alliance command''', was the leadership council of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. It was headed by [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]]. [[Soldier]]s in the field complained that it attempted to manage the Rebellion as a government, and not a military resistance.

==History==
===Early days===
In the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early days of the rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], various rebel cells began coordinating their efforts to combat the reign of the Empire.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref> A Rebel Command was established that included [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] and [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]]. In [[2 BBY]], Organa and Sato tasked the [[Spectres|crew]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'' with a [[Mission to investigate Geonosis|mission]] to recover a [[Partisans|rebel team]] led by [[Saw Gerrera]] from the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis">{{Rebels|Ghosts of Geonosis}}</ref>

Following the formation of the Rebel Alliance,<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref> the Alliance High Command [[Dantooine base|based]] themselves on [[Dantooine]] before they transferred operations to a new headquarters<ref name="Rebel Massassi Outpost Databank" /> inside the ruins of the [[Great Temple]] on [[Yavin 4]]. It was from there that the Alliance launched their [[Battle of Scarif|assault]] on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]] and saw the acquisition of the [[Death Star plans|technical data]] to the Galactic Empire's new [[superweapon]], the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

===After Yavin===
{{Quote|Oh, Skywalker. You cannot understand the responsibility of high command. It is easy to advise, but the decision is not yours. It is one thing to sacrifice yourself. To sacrifice others is something else entirely&hellip;|Jan Dodonna|<ref name="Star Wars (2015) 54">[[Star Wars (2015) 54|''Star Wars'' (2015) 54]]</ref>}}
Following the [[Evacuation of Yavin 4|evacuation from Yavin]],<ref name="Princess Leia 1">[[Princess Leia 1|''Princess Leia'' 1]]</ref> the Alliance High Command met in the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] to discuss what actions to take next. [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who had just completed a successful [[Assault on Cymoon|assault]] on [[Weapons Factory Alpha]] on [[Cymoon 1]], urged the leadership to allow her [[Heroes of Yavin|team]] to continue pressing the Alliance's advantage against the Empire. [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] told the princess that they could not maintain such an offensive due to still being on the run.<ref name="Star Wars 4" />

===Learning of the second Death Star===
[[File:Death_Star_II_revealed.png||left|200px|thumb|The Alliance High Command learns that the Empire is building a new Death Star.]]
[[4 ABY|Four years]] after the Battle of Yavin,<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the High Command had transferred their headquarters to Admiral Ackbar's [[flagship]] ''[[Home One]]''. When Rebel command learned that the Empire was building a [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] over the [[Forest]] [[Moon]] of [[Endor]], they ordered the Alliance Fleet to assemble near the planet [[Sullust]], where they planned the [[Battle of Endor|assault]] on the [[battle station]].<ref name="Episode VI" />

===Legacy===
With the Galactic Empire's defeat at the Battle of Endor, the Alliance was reorganized into the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> The Alliance High Command eventually transitioned into the [[New Republic High Command]].<ref name="BTA" />
