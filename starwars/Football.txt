'''Football''' was a ball sport that was very popular during the [[Clone Wars]]. During the [[Battle of Ryloth]]<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|121}}</ref> information on a [[Galactic Republic]] display screen stated that another sport was the "second most important sport right after football."<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> The [[Force-sensitive]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], who stood at 0.34 [[meter]]s tall, was only marginally bigger than a football.<ref name="Pocket Expert">''[[Star Wars Jedi Pocket Expert]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Football was first mentioned on a display screen in "[[Liberty on Ryloth]],"<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> the twenty-first episode in [[The Clone Wars: Season One|Season One]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated television series, which premiered on [[March 13]], [[2009]].<ref name="Liberty Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode021.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref> In the real world, the term [[Wikipedia:Football|football]] can refer to multiple different sports, including [[Wikipedia:Association football|association football]] and [[Wikipedia:American football|American football]].<ref name="Football">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/sports/football-the-games|text=football|author=The Editors of Encyclopædia Britannica|date=2024-09-30|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20240920062103}}</ref>
