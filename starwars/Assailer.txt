The '''''Assailer''''' was a [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvette]] that, during the [[Imperial Era]], formed part of the [[Imperial Navy]]. The [[corvette]] boasted [[sublight engine]]s<ref name="Huge Ship Instigator" /> and measured 150 [[meter]]s in length.<ref name="Length" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Notable mentions===
The ''Assailer'' was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a card of the same name in the ''[[Huge Ship Conversion Kit]]'', an expansion for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]''<ref name="Huge Ship Assailer">{{FFGXW2|set=Huge Ship Conversion Kit|cardname=''Assailer''}}</ref> that was released on [[November 8]], [[2019]].<ref name="Huge Ship release">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/11/8/available-now-october-25-1|text=Available Now &ndash; November 8}}</ref>

Artwork of the ''Assailer'' used in the ''Huge Ship Conversion Kit'' was created for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity release [[Imperial Raider Expansion Pack (Star Wars: X-Wing Miniatures Game)|''Imperial Raider Expansion Pack'']], part of the first edition of Fantasy Flight Games' ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Miniatures Game]]''.<ref name="Legends">{{FFGXW|set=Imperial Raider Expansion Pack|cardname=''Assailer''}}</ref> In this continuity, the [[Assailer/Legends|''Assailer'']] was first shown in "Here Comes the Imperial Raider," a preview for the expansion published on [[December 19]], [[2014]]. The [[Corvette/Legends|corvette's]] name was obscured in the preview,<ref name="Here Comes the Imperial Raider">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2014/12/19/here-comes-the-imperial-raider|text=Here Comes the Imperial Raider}}</ref> with the name ''Assailer'' first mentioned in another preview, "The Will of the Empire," published on [[August 10]], [[2015]].<ref name="The Will of the Empire">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2015/8/10/the-will-of-the-empire|text=The Will of the Empire}}</ref>

===Discrepancies and unreleased products===
[[File:Instigator-FFG.png|thumb|left|270px|A card titled "''Assailer''" used artwork of the ''Instigator''.]]
In the ''Huge Ship Conversion Kit'', the card titled "''Assailer''" featured artwork<ref name="Huge Ship Assailer" /> used on all previous depictions the ''Raider''-class corvette ''[[Instigator]]''.<ref name="Armada Raider">{{Armada|set=Imperial Raider Expansion Pack|cardname=''Instigator''}}</ref><ref name="X-Wing Instigator">{{FFGXW|set=Imperial Raider Expansion Pack|cardname=''Instigator''}}</ref> Similarly, the card titled "''Instigator''" featured artwork<ref name="Huge Ship Instigator" /> previously used to depict the ''Assailer'' in the Legends ''Imperial Raider Expansion Pack''.<ref name="Legends" /> The [[2020]] ''[[Upgrade Card Collection]]'' for ''Star Wars Armada'' then reverted to the original artwork of the ''Instigator''.<ref name="Armada Upgrade">{{Armada|set=Upgrade Card Collection|cardname=''Instigator''|bypass=1}}</ref> Since other misprints were reported in the ''Huge Ship Conversion Kit'',<ref name="Misprints">{{YouTube|video=unucsYOsZjg|text=X-Wing - Huge Ship Misprints|channel=@Crabbok|name=Crabbok|date=2019-11-19}}</ref> this article assumes the names of both cards were erroneously swapped.

A card named ''Assailer'' was intended to be released in the [[Imperial Raider Expansion Pack (Second Edition)|''Imperial Raider Expansion Pack'']] for ''X-Wing Second Edition'',<ref name="RaiderProductPage">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/products/x-wing-second-edition/products/x-wing-second-edition-imperial-raider-expansion-pack|text=Imperial Raider Expansion Pack|nolive=1}}</ref> which was announced on [[August 7]], 2019;<ref name="Weight of the Empire">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/8/7/the-weight-of-the-empire|text=The Weight of the Empire|nolive=1}}</ref> however, the expansion was never released.
