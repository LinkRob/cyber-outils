'''Fondar Etzis''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] who served as a [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Republic Era]]. At some point, he was assigned to the [[planet]] [[Mina-Rau]] to deal with [[Quillian bandits]] who had been [[Raids on Mina-Rau|stealing]] the local [[farmer]]s' crop yield. Soon after completing his mission, he was [[Death|killed]] by [[Corlis Rath]] via a [[poison]]. The [[Jedi]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], [[Mace Windu]], and [[Aayla Secura]] were sent to retrieve his body. Etzis wielded a [[Color|blue]]-bladed [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" />
