A '''war''' occurred in the [[far galaxy]], long before the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Darkness Rising" /> The [[Great Mothers]], descendants from the ancient [[Witch Kingdom of the Dathmiri]] on [[Peridea]],<ref name="Part Six" /> were in a hibernation sleep during the war. When they were reawakened<ref name="Darkness Rising" /> by [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]],<ref name="Part Eight">{{Ahsoka|Part Eight: The Jedi, the Witch, and the Warlord}}</ref> they discovered their way of life had been destroyed, and the far galaxy and Peridea were largely dead and barren from the war.<ref name="Darkness Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|There are these characters that have survived in the Great Mothers in some kind of hibernation sleep. And when they wake up, they wake up to a different world where their entire way of life has been destroyed and suppressed. And they live in largely a dead galaxy and a dead planet where war wiped out everything.|Dave Filoni|<ref name="Darkness Rising" />}}
The far galaxy war was mentioned by [[Dave Filoni]] in "[[Darkness Rising]]," a featurette included in ''[[Ahsoka: The Complete First Season]]'', as part of backstory for the [[Star Wars: Ahsoka|series]].<ref name="Darkness Rising" />
