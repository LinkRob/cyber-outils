The '''Bellassa system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained the [[Planet|world]] [[Bellassa]].<ref name="Astrogation" /> The system lay in grid square M-11 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], and the [[Hydian Way]]<ref name="TFABG" /> [[super-hyperroute]]<ref name="BMF 48">{{BuildFalconCite|48|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> connected it to<ref name="TFABG" /> the planets<ref name="Heir">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref><ref name="Shadow">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> [[Exodeen]] and [[Fedalle]]. The Bellassa system shared its name with<ref name="TFABG" /> the [[Bellassan pepper]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> and the [[Bellassan Galaxy Petitionists]] [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebel]] group.<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Starcruiserdate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> [[Chandrila Star Line]]'s [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' passed by the Bellassa system on a cruise from the Core Worlds' [[Chandrila system]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Several [[Viewscreen|screens]] aboard the [[Star Cruiser|starship]] displayed a partial [[Star map|map]] of [[the galaxy]] that indicated the system's position.<ref name="GS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Bellassa system first appeared as a point on a map in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 52|fifty-second issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine<ref name="BMF 52">{{BuildFalconCite|52|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> in an article written by [[Mark Newbold]].<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> The issue was published by [[De Agostini]] around [[December 30]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF52date">{{BMFdates|52}}</ref> The system first appeared [[in-universe]] when it was depicted on a map viewable in [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]], an immersive roleplaying experience at [[W:c:disney:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]] in [[Wikipedia:Florida|Florida]]<ref name="GS" /> that opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]],<ref name="31 Things">{{SW|url=news/31-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=31 Things We Learned in an Exclusive Preview of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}}</ref> and had its final voyage from [[September 28]] to [[September 30|30]], [[2023]].<ref name="StarcruiserClosure">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=destinations/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser}}</ref> The system was identified in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Bellassa system/Legends|Bellassa system]] first appeared in the [[2005]] young reader novel ''[[The Last of the Jedi: The Desperate Mission]]'', written by [[Jude Watson]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: The Last of the Jedi]]'' series.<ref name="Last">''[[The Last of the Jedi: The Desperate Mission]]''</ref> The [[Star system/Legends|system]] was then named in ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', a [[2009]] reference book written by Fry and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> The Bellassa system was first pictured in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2012]] reference book ''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]''.<ref name="TERC">''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]''</ref>
