This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Mando10" /> [[Avrac Kefundee]] was a member of this species.<ref name="Mando14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref>

==Biology and appearance==
This bipedal [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] was distinguished by their [[Color|pink]] skin with dark markings on their faces.<ref name="Mando10" />

==Species in the galaxy==
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|10}}</ref> a [[Unidentified Kajain'sa'Nikto's ally 2|member]] of this species was a part of a small raider gang who attempted to jump the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], though they were knocked out during the ensuing fight.<ref name="Mando10" /> Around that time,<ref name="Date" />, another nearly identical member of this species was serving a 25-year sentence in the [[Wobani Penitentiary]] for aggravated assault of a [[New Republic]] [[officer]].<ref name="Mando14" />

==Behind the scenes==
This species first appeared in the [[Chapter 10: The Passenger|second episode]] of the [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second season]] of the television series, [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian|''The Mandalorian]],'' directed by [[Peyton Reed]].<ref name="Mando10" />
