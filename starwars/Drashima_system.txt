The '''Drashima system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Majoor sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing" /> It was situated in grid square N-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the location [[Drashima III]], where [[senior applications engineer]] [[Jenra]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] discovered a type of [[Poison|toxic]] [[mushroom]]<ref name="MW" /> by [[13 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Most Wanted]]'' to [[13 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Drashima system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
