'''Fusion generators''',<ref name="TFAVD" /> also known as '''fusion reactors''',<ref name="Unfinished Business">{{TCW|Unfinished Business (episode)}}</ref> or '''fusion facilities''',<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> or '''fusion power core'''<ref name="Most Wanted">''[[Most Wanted]]''</ref> were a type of [[power generator|generator]] capable of creating power. [[GNK-series power droid]]s were built with internal fusion generators.<ref name="TFAVD" /> By the time of the early [[Galactic Empire|Empire]], it was considered obsolete for a [[droid]] to be powered with this type of energy.<ref name="Most Wanted" />

Beneath the [[Anaxes assembly complex|assembly complex]] on the [[planet]] [[Anaxes]] was a fusion reactor that, if destroyed, would obliterate much of the world. In pursuing his strategy of total annihilation of [[Galactic Republic]] forces during the [[Battle of Anaxes]] in [[19 BBY]], [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[Admiral]] [[Trench]] nearly detonated the reactor with a [[bomb]] he had pre-installed at the site when [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]], using the disarming sequence for the bomb provided by [[clone trooper]] [[Echo]] and fellow [[Jedi]] General [[Anakin Skywalker]] from the [[orbit]]ing [[warship]] ''[[Invulnerable]]'', safely stopped the countdown.<ref name="Unfinished Business">{{TCW|Unfinished Business (episode)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fusion generators were first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] core rule book for the first edition of the ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', which was released in [[1987]].<ref name="RPG">[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game (first edition)|''Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game'']]</ref> They were confirmed to be [[canon]] under the name fusion reactor when they appeared in "[[Unfinished Business (episode)|Unfinished Business]],"<ref name="Unfinished Business" /> an episode of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series released on [[StarWars.com]] in [[2014]] as part of ''[[The Clone Wars Legacy]]''.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-bad-batch-recon|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' "Bad Batch" Recon|nolive=1}}</ref> The name fusion generator was then also reinstated as canon by the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in [[2015]].<ref name="TFAVD" />
