The '''Alderaan Convention''' was a treaty that established standards for humanitarian treatment in war. The [[Onderonian]] resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]] was known to flout those standards by subjecting [[prisoner]]s to [[torture]]. The convention shared its name<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> with [[Alderaan]], [[planet]] whose [[Alderaanian|inhabitants]] prided themselves on their pacifism.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref>
