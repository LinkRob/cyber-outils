'''Aduba-3''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Annual 2018" /> in the [[Aduba system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Bheriz sector]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[smuggler]] [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki|Jaxxon]] visited Aduba-3 and acquired [[memory chip]]s for his [[Maintenance droid|repair droid]], [[ML-08]]. However, before the chips could be installed, the [[droid]] forgot where they had been placed.<ref name="Annual 2018" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Aduba-3 was first mentioned in the [[2018]] comic strip "[[The Lost Eggs of Livorno]]," written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in [[Star Wars Adventures Annual 2018|''Star Wars Adventures Annual'' 2018]].<ref name="Annual 2018" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, Aduba-3 first appeared in the [[1977]] comic book [[Star Wars (1977) 7|''Star Wars'' (1977) 7]], written by [[Roy Thomas]] and [[Howard Chaykin]].<ref name="Star Wars 7">[[Star Wars (1977) 7|''Star Wars'' (1977) 7]]</ref>
