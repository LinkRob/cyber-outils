{{Quote|Go to the planet Tython. There you will find the ancient ruins of a temple that has a strong connection to the Force.|[[Ahsoka Tano]], to [[Din Djarin]]|<ref name="Chapter 13" />}}
The '''Force-henge''',<ref name="Art of Mando 2">[[The Art of Star Wars: The Mandalorian (Season Two)|''The Art of Star Wars: The Mandalorian'' Season Two]]</ref> also known as the '''old megalith''',<ref name="Handbook">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Handbook]]''</ref> was an ancient temple with a strong connection to [[the Force]] located on top of a [[mountain]]<ref name="Chapter 13" /> in a [[Temperature|temperate]] [[Unidentified temperate region|region]] on the [[planet]] [[Tython]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> By the [[Standard year|year]] [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|13}}</ref> it had fallen into ruin<ref name="Chapter 13" /> and been reduced to a ring of six megaliths surrounding a [[seeing stone]],<ref name="Chapter 14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref> earning it the name of the '''ruined temple'''.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
