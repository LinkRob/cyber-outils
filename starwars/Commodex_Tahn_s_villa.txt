A '''villa''' was owned by the retired [[mortician]] [[Commodex Tahn]] on the idyllic [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. The villa had a wide view of the [[water]] as well as several other buildings and waterfalls.<ref name="Darth Vader 10" />

In [[0 ABY]], [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] and her [[droid]] companions [[0-0-0]] and [[BT-1]] [[Assault on Commodex Tahn's villa|assaulted]] the villa, breaking into Tahn's residence and torturing him for information regarding the late [[Monarch of Naboo]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. The droids collected several valuables to frame their attack as a robbery before 0-0-0 electrocuted Tahn to [[death]]. They left as the villa burned behind them.<ref name="Darth Vader 10" />
