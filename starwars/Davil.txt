'''Davil''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Xot]] who worked for [[Bidvel]] at a [[Bidvel's scrap pile|scrap pile]] on a [[Unidentified planetoid (scrap pile)|remote planetoid]]<ref name="EtSP" /> in [[3 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{Twitter|tronprogram2|status/751843791565381632|[[Martin Fisher]]|quote=@LelalMekha it takes place between The Call and Homecoming|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/wg5Mq}} places "[[Escaping the Scrap Pile]]" between "[[The Call]]" and "[[Homecoming]]." Both of these episodes are dated to [[3 BBY]] in ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

==Biography==
Davil worked for [[Bidvel]] at his [[Bidvel's scrap pile|scrap pile]] on a [[Unidentified planetoid (scrap pile)|remote planetoid]]. There he would assess discarded [[Droid|droids]], inventorying them for parts that could be resold.<ref name="EtSP" /> 

In [[3 BBY]], Davil was brought a new droid to inventory, the [[C1-series astromech droid]], [[C1-10P]] ("Chopper"). The droid had been found floating among space debris. Davil had the droid strapped to a table in his laboratory where he found little of the physical droid to be worth anything, but after going through Chopper's memory banks, found secret rebel files. He showed his findings to Bidvel, who planned to sell them to the [[Galactic Empire]]. Chopper attempted to escape, using his [[Rocket booster|rocket boosters]] to fling himself off the table and into Davil and Bidvel, knocking them into some assorted material scattered around the room.<ref name="EtSP" />

Chopper then fled through an open hatch and managed to steal the activation codes for the other droids in the scrap pile. Bidvel ordered Davil to chase after Chopper, and he got pretty close, but Chopper dropped a bunch of scrap parts on his head. Bidvel eventually recaptured Chopper and had his rocket boosters deactivated. He then had the droid dangled over a molten pit from a crane operated by Davil, now with bandages on his head. However, just before his recapture Chopper had sent out the reactivation codes, awakening all the half-scrapped droids in the pile. They closed on Davil and Bidvel. Bidvel ordered Davil to shoot the droids and Davil did just that, targeting an [[R4 astromech droid]]. Davil was then shot by a [[B2-series super battle droid]] and knocked unconscious.<ref name="EtSP" />

==Personality and traits==
Davil was a male [[Xot]] with gray-blue skin with red markings and green eyes. He wore a red jacket, pants, and boots. He wears a [[blaster]] on his hip. According to Bidvel, Davil was a hard worker. He enjoyed his job and never got tired of doing it.<ref name="EtSP" />
