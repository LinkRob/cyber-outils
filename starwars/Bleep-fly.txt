'''Bleep-flies''' were a type of [[fly]] that could be found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]. While in the [[Lonisa City Zoo]] on Valo<ref name="EFV" /> in [[229 BBY]]<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> the aspiring [[pirate]] [[Zyle Keem]] noticed some bleep-flies singing in the dusk [[Atmosphere|air]] as they used their [[datapad]] to locate the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]].<ref name="EFV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bleep-flies appeared in [[The High Republic: Escape from Valo|''Escape from Valo'']], a [[2024]] junior novel written by [[Daniel José Older]] and [[Alyssa Wong]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="EFV" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref>
