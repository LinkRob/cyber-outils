'''Erashe''' was a [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Senate]].<ref name="MOF" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate}}</ref> [[Hasalia Prederiko]], the [[chief financial officer]] for the company [[General Trade Galactic]] and an agent of the [[Crymorah Syndicate]], offered to temporarily prevent [[Imperial Intelligence]] from monitoring Senator [[Mon Mothma]] if she provided the names of other individuals for them to watch instead. Mothma later accepted the offer, and named Erash, as well as Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], the [[aide]] [[Raave Galdon]], and [[Ungol Ungrave]], Senator [[Palok]]'s [[Chief of Staff|chief of staff]]. Prederiko responded that the names she had listed may not be enough of a distraction, and Mothma provided another name, Senator [[Lud Marroi]].<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Erashe was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
