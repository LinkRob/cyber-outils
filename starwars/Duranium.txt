'''Duranium''' was a tough alloy used in [[Army|military]]-grade plating. It was stronger than [[titanium]] but less resilient than [[impervium]].<ref name="GD">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref> It was notably used in the [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] [[armor]] of [[General]] [[Grievous]].<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> Those same duranium plates that served Grievous successfully were also used in the [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]'s frame to protect their vital areas.<ref name="Build R2-D2 57">{{BuildR2Cite|57|Droid Directory}}</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Barnabas Vim]] wore duranium armor.<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Duranium first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] short story "[[A Boy and His Monster: The Rancor Keeper's Tale]]," which was published as a part of ''[[Tales from Jabba's Palace]]'' in [[1996]].<ref name="Tale">{{StoryCite|story=A Boy and His Monster: The Rancor Keeper's Tale|book=Tales from Jabba's Palace}}</ref> In addition to ''[[Star Wars]]'', duranium has appeared in the science fiction franchise [[Wikipedia:Star_Trek|''Star Trek'']].
