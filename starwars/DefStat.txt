'''DefStat''' was a multilevel security alert protocol carried out in [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] installations such as the [[Imperial Center of Military Research|complex]] on [[Scarif]]. A DefStat Three alert meant that an infiltration had been detected. That level of alert was tripped when [[Jyn Erso]] and [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] entered the complex's [[Scarif vault|vault]].<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
