The '''Battle of Abelard''' was a battle between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel forces]] and the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Abelard]] some time after the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]]. During the conflict, the Rebels managed to steal a stabilized [[ytterbium]] cargo.<ref name="CasusBelli" />
