'''Celmack''' was an individual who served as an officer within the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] during the [[Imperial Era|initial reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. While he occupied the position of a [[colonel]] within the security service, Celmack was named as a friend of the industrialist [[Cellia Moten]], who claimed to have made donations to various causes at his urgings.

==Biography==
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Celmack worked for the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]&mdash;one of the foremost intelligence agencies of the [[New Order]]&mdash;as an individual that reached the [[rank]] of [[colonel]]. In addition to his responsibilities to the Imperial state, the officer also harboured links to the socialite [[Cellia Moten]], who named him as one of her various friends inside the administration. Over the course of their substantial association, Celmack was responsible for cajoling Cellia to provide [[Currency|monetary]] support for causes that the ISB itself sponsored, though when his name was unfamiliar to [[Agent]] [[Sendril Crane]] when the industrialist mentioned it to him<ref name="Sanctuary" /> during or after [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{SanctuaryDate}}</ref>
