"'''Designing Warships'''" was an article written by [[Jason Fry]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 134|''Star Wars Insider'' 134]]. It explored the creation of the new [[Starship/Legends|starships]] models featured in ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'', such as [[Assertor-class Star Dreadnought/Legends|''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought]] and [[Xolochi Dreadnought]], as well as interviewed the two respective artists [[Ansel Hsiao]] and [[Ian Fullwood]] with some details regarding their ships.
