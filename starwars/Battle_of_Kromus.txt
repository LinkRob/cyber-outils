The '''Battle of Kromus''' was a battle<ref name="Lead by Example" /> sometime between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> in the vicinity of<ref name="Battlenamia" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim's]] [[Kromus system]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the battle, the [[Maelstrom-class battle cruiser|''Maelstrom''-class battle cruiser]] [[Maelstrom (Maelstrom-class)|''Maelstrom'']] of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Navy|navy]] served with distinction, with [[Jedi General]] [[Darrus Jeht]] of the [[Jedi Order]] as its [[commanding officer]].<ref name="Lead by Example" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Battle of Kromus was introduced in the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]] for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Lead by Example" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Battle of Kromus/Legends|Battle of Kromus]] first appeared in "[[Dark Soul]]," a short story written by [[August Hahn]] and [[Cynthia Hahn]] as a supplement to [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']], a [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game|roleplaying]] campaign created by [[Wizards of the Coast]]. The story was published on [[Wizards.com]] on [[September 22]], [[2005]].<ref name="Dark Soul">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/livingforce/lf20050922darksoul|text=Dark Soul|int=Dark Soul}}</ref>
