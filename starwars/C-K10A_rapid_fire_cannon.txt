The '''C-K10A rapid fire cannon''' was a model of [[laser cannon]] that could be equipped to [[starship]]s and could fire bursts at multiple targets. Equipping an [[EML-850 light freighter]] with a C-K10A rapid fire cannon cost seven hundred and fifty [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and required ten units of [[quadranium]], and four [[4XB Calculation Unit]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
During [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] paid for the services of a [[starship]] [[Technician|mechanic]] to equip the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her EML-850 [[light freighter]], with a C-K10A rapid fire cannon as well as providing materials to install it.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}
