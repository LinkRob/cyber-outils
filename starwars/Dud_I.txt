A '''Dud I''' was a piece used in the game [[Canto Pass]] which resembled an [[impact grenade]]. A single Dud was worth a negative point with each additional [[Dud piece]] doubling the negative score. One such piece was stored in a container at a [[Unidentified Typhoon Squadron camp|camp]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Typhoon Squadron]] in the [[Myrra Valley]] region of the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] found the container and stole the Dud I piece.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A Dud I Canto Pass piece appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They are one of several Canto Pass pieces that could be collected in the game and used as trinkets to customize the appearance of the player's [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]] and the interior of the player's [[EML-850 light freighter]], the ''[[Trailblazer]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
