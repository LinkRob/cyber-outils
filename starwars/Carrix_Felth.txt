'''Carrix Felth''' was a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]].<ref name="Rise" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Felth held the rank of [[lieutenant]] and flew a [[TIE/IN interceptor]] during the [[Battle of Endor]], in which the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] attacked the Empire's [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II]] [[superweapon]] while it was under construction in the [[Endor system]].<ref name="Rise" />

The [[pilot]] took part in the opening attack wave of [[TIE series|TIEs]] and while flying head on into Alliance [[starfighter]]s, captured an image of the battle with the Interceptor's gun [[camera]] that showed enemy and friendly [[Starship|vessels]] with the [[Death Star]] and the [[moon]] [[Endor]] visible in the background. The Alliance eventually destroyed the Death Star and won the battle.<ref name="Rise" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] included the image from Felth's gun camera in his [[book]], ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Carrix Felth was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
