'''Czerka Secret Weapons Division 7''' was an underground complex located below the [[Dune Sea/Legends|Dune Sea]] on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]]. It was a secret research complex run by [[Czerka Arms/Legends|Czerka Corporation]] to study [[Rakata/Legends|Rakata]] technology discovered on Tatooine, but [[the Imprisoned One]]'s [[mind prison]] took control of the Rakatan artifacts and transformed the personnel into [[Cyborg/Legends|cyborg]] slaves.

==History==
Decades before the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]], the division was established on the planet [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] to investigate a type-seven device which was the designation for a weapon capable of destroying an entire world. The scientists attached to the division had actually uncovered a [[Rakata/Legends|Rakatan]] [[Mind prison]] that contained [[the Imprisoned One]]. [[Grommik Kurthson]] served as a security officer for the division when the relic was activated and killed all the scientists which left Kurthson the only survivor who lived an isolated life in the deserts of Tatooine.

During the Cold War, the existence of the division was eventually discovered by [[Republic Strategic Information Service]] [[Agent/Legends|agent]] [[Fauler]] who later tasked a [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] spacer with uncovering its secrets. At the same time, [[Gayem Leksende]] of Czerka's special enforcement branch intended to recover the secrets of the division for himself.

==Behind the scenes==
The Czerka complex was created for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', a [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] released by [[BioWare]] on [[December 20]], [[2011]]. Originally, the in game facility was much larger, and had a bonus objective which ended with fighting Director [[Antayen]]. However, with the release of ''Knights of the Fallen Empire,'' the map was shrunk down significantly, and Antayen is now a generic enemy who can be fought before entering the phased area.
