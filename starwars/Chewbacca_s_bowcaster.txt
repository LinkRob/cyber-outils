'''Chewbacca's bowcaster''' was a traditionally hand-crafted [[bowcaster]] weapon belonging to the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> Chewbacca built the bowcaster sometime prior to using it during the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]] in the [[Clone Wars]], in which the Wookiees of [[Kashyyyk]] and the [[Galactic Republic]] made a joint effort against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]].<ref name="Episode III" /> After Chewbacca pledged a [[life debt]] to [[Han Solo]] and joined the [[smuggler]],<ref name="TFAVD" /> Chewbacca continued to use his bowcaster throughout the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Episode IV" /><ref name="Episode V" /><ref name="Episode VI" /> having crafted several bowcasters until then.<ref name="UltimateSW" />

The bowcaster required the strength of a Wookiee to fire the weapon comfortably, as it fired destructive metal [[quarrel]]s sheathed in [[plasma]] energy<ref name="DB Bowcaster">{{Databank|bowcaster|Bowcaster}}</ref> that would result in explosive impacts. Thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="TFAVD" /> Chewbacca and Solo were [[Skirmish aboard the Eravana|engaged]] in a conflict on board their [[Baleen-class heavy freighter|heavy freighter]] ''[[Eravana]]'' against the [[Guavian Death Gang]] and [[Kanjiklub]]. There, for the first time, Solo used Chewbacca's bowcaster after the Wookiee was injured in the skirmish and immediately took a liking to the weapon.<ref name="Episode VII" />

Chewbacca's bowcaster had a hand-woven strap made of [[kshyyy-vine]], and he carried a [[bandolier]] that contained nineteen<ref name="Absolutely Everything" /> ammo cases of one to three bowcaster quarrels each. Chewbacca's carry pouch also contained tools to keep his bowcaster operational.<ref name="TFAVD" /> Like all Wookiee gadgets, the bowcaster blended modern technology and traditional materials. Unlike Wookiee tradition, however, Chewbacca's bowcaster had an automatic cocking system for firing the weapon.<ref name="Absolutely Everything" /> In addition, Chewbacca's most recent handcrafted bowcaster made use of the [[Power cell|power pack]] of an [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]], a standard-issue weapon of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="UltimateSW" />

==Behind the scenes==
The static stunt prop version of Chewbacca's bowcaster used in the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] was constructed from dense rubber painted to look metallic with cast rubber balls at the ends of the bow and a metal frontal section to provide structure. The green canvas carrying strap was affixre to the prop with metal clips. The prop measured approximately thirty one inches by thirty two inches by 9.5 inches.<ref name="Auction">{{WebCite|url=https://entertainment.ha.com/itm/movie-tv-memorabilia/props/star-wars-episode-vii-the-force-awakens-disney-2015-peter-mayhew-joonas-suotamo-chewbacca-stunt-bowcaster/a/7456-11052.s|text=''Star Wars: Episode VII - The Force Awakens'' (Disney, 2015), Peter Mayhew/Joonas Suotamo "Chewbacca" Stunt Bowcaster|work=[[Wikipedia:Heritage Auctions|Heritage Auctions]]|archivedate=20260426195156}}</ref>
