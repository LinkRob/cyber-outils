'''CH-83'''<ref name="Wind Up">{{DisneyToyCite|set=Droid Depot (toy line)|packtype=Wind Up Toy|pack=CH-83|altlink=https://wdwnt.com/2021/10/photos-new-wind-up-toys-apparel-and-droideka-figure-at-droid-depot-in-disneys-hollywood-studios}}</ref> was a [[BB-series astromech droid]] with a [[Color|black]] [[sensor]] and green, gray, and white paneling.<ref name="GEDD" /> As a member of the BB-series, it was manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]] and stood 0.67 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Stats" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 BBY]].</ref> during the [[First Order]]'s [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Batuu]], CH-83 was displayed among [[droid]] parts on one of the shelves inside [[Droid Depot]], a store in Batuu's [[Black Spire Outpost]].<ref name="GEDD" />

==Behind the scenes==
CH-83 appeared as a prop within the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GEDD" /> which was opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The droid was first identified in [[2021]]<ref name="ToyDate">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fanthatracks.com/news/collecting/new-wind-up-droids-arrive-at-droid-depot|text=New wind-up droids arrive at Droid Depot|author=[[Mark Newbold|Newbold, Mark]]|date=2021-10-08|archivedate=20220209182602}}</ref> when a wind-up toy of the droid was released as part of the [[Droid Depot (toy line)|Droid Depot]] toy line.<ref name="Wind Up" />
