"'''Clouds of Genarius: Into the Storm Clouds'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the third episode of the [[Clouds of Genarius trilogy]] and was written by [[Jason Nichols]] and [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]].
