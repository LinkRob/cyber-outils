The '''775th Legion''' was an [[stormtrooper]] [[legion]] within the [[Imperial Army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Mando VG" /> Following the Empire's collapse and defeat in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> a number survivors from the legion eventually joined the [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant faction]] of [[warlord]] [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]<ref name="Mando VG" /> by [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Several of these survivors made up a [[Gideon's battalion|battalion]] that was dispatched to [[Nevarro]] and participated in a [[Clash on Nevarro|clash]] against a motley assembly of enemies led by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]].<ref name="Mando 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref>
