'''''Andor'': Season 2 &ndash; Vol. 2 (Episodes 4-6) [Original Score]''' is the soundtrack for the fourth to sixth episodes of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'': "[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]," "[[I Have Friends Everywhere]]," and "[[What a Festive Evening]]." It was composed by [[Brandon Roberts]] and [[Nicholas Britell]] and released for digital streaming on [[May 2]], [[2025]].<ref name="Vol2" />
