'''Elzar Mann&#39;s quarters''' were a set of personal living quarters in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] occupied by [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]].<ref name="TOTF" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''The High Republic: Temptation of the Force''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> Jedi Master [[Avar Kriss]] visited the quarters, bringing [[sourstone mead]] and [[Keldov nut pastry|keldov nut pastries]].<ref name="TOTF" />
