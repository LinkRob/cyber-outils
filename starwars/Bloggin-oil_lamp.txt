'''Bloggin-oil lamps''' were a type of [[lamp]] that burned [[bloggin-oil]] as a [[fuel]] source in order to provide illumination. The residents of the village of [[Tuanul]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]] made their own bloggin-oil lamps, also raising [[bloggin]]s from which the oil could be sourced.<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
A bloggin-oil lamp was first created as a prop for the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which was released on [[December 18]], [[2015]]. The prop was not identified in the film but received a name in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in conjecture with the film.
