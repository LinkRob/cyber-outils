The '''East Spaceport''' was a [[spaceport]] located in the [[Shipping District]] of the [[planet]] [[Keldooine]]'s [[Keldooine's largest city|largest city]]. It contained a [[Regal Voyager facility|hub facility]] of the [[Regal Voyager]] passenger line. The young [[Riftwalkers]] member [[Kylah Lohmata]] [[Thief|stole]] a number of [[Regal Voyager case]]s from the facility as part of a [[Assassinations of the pirate leaders|scheme]] by the gang's leader, [[Zilastra]]. [[Jedi Master]] [[Depa Billaba]], after infiltrating Keldooine's underworld and gaining Lohmata's favor as a [[speeder]] [[thief]] named "Hotwire," assisted her in stealing the final group of cases.<ref name="The Living Force" />
