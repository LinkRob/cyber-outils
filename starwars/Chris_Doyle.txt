'''Chris Doyle''' was an author on several [[West End Games]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]] supplements and adventures, as well as one book for [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s Revised Star Wars Roleplaying Game.

==Star Wars bibliography==
===Books===
*''[[The Kathol Outback]]'' - January 1996
*''[[Shadows of the Empire Planets Guide]]'' - September 1996
*[[Operation: Elrood (book)|''Operation: Elrood'']] - December 1996
*''[[Tapani Sector Instant Adventures]]'' - June 1997
*''[[Lords of the Expanse]]'' - July 1997
*''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]'' - January 2003
