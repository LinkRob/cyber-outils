During the [[Clone Wars]], a '''ceremonial chamber'''<ref name="Sacrifice EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sacrifice-episode-gallery|text="Sacrifice" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 8}}</ref> in the [[Inquisitorius Headquarters|Coruscant tower]] was used by [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]], known publicly as [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] Sheev Palpatine and [[Count]] Dooku, respectively to undertake a [[Dark illusion|ritual]]. The room contained numerous statues and an [[Sith alchemy|altar]] at the center.<ref name="Sacrifice" />
