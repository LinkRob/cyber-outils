'''Cleo Station''' was a former [[Con Star Mining Corporation]] station on the planet [[Parnassos]] used to manufacture mining equipment. It was situated an ocean away from the [[Scyre territory]]. By the [[New Republic Era]], its population had been decimated by a famine. [[Captain]] [[Phasma]], a native of Parnassos, who had joined the [[First Order]] used the station's [[synthicator]] to forge her [[Phasma's armor|chromium armor]].<ref name="Phasma" />
