The '''Cartel Vengeance''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Bounty Hunter (Imara Vex)|standard costume]]. The [[Clothing|outfit]] and [[armor]] were [[Color|dark gray]], pale red, teal, gold, white, and bluish-gray in coloration, with a glowing light blue visor on the helmet, and a cylindrical [[shoulder-mounted armament]] housing a large golden payload.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cartel Vengeance was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Imara Vex, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Vengeance Rifle]].<ref name="Hunters" />
