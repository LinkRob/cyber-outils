'''EGL-21''', also known as "'''AMPS'''" or "'''Amps'''," was a [[Manumission|manumitted]] [[EGL power droid]] based in [[Niima Outpost]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. He welded objects for [[scavenger]]s in exchange for power, spare [[gas]] canisters, interesting metals, or a good joke. The [[droid]] and his mechanical colleague [[GTAW-74]] could sometimes be spotted in the shade trading jokes that their customers had brought them. The scavenger [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] often spent [[time]] thinking up jokes to trade with EGL-21 for his services.

==Biography==
{{Quote|AMPS doesn't eat rations, of course, but he'll weld in exchange for a recharge, spare gas canisters, metals he finds interesting, or a good joke. I spend a lot of time driving across the Goazon trying to think of jokes AMPS will decide are worth a trade.|Rey, writing in her survival guide|<ref name="Survival Guide" />}}
[[File:Amps_in_Niima.jpg|thumb|left|200px|EGL-21 in Niima Outpost]]
EGL-21 was the designation<ref name="Survival Guide" /> of an [[EGL power droid]]<ref name="Kids" /> manufactured by [[Veril Line Systems]]<ref name="BuildR2" /> who was also known by the nicknames "AMPS"<ref name="Survival Guide" /> or "Amps."<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|egl-21-amps-power-droid|EGL-21 "Amps" Power Droid}}</ref> By the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VII}} to [[34 ABY]]. As the final entries in ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' describe events from the film, earlier entries in the journal, including the mention of EGL-21, must have been written no later than 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> he was [[Manumission|manumitted]] and based on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]] in [[Niima Outpost]], a [[Merchant|trading]] center controlled by the [[junk dealer]] [[Unkar Plutt]]. There, the [[droid]] would offer his services as a welder to [[scavenger]]s in exchange for a recharge, spare [[gas]] canisters, interesting metals, or a good joke, as the [[Ration pack|ration portions]] that most of the scavengers traded in were useless to him.<ref name="Survival Guide" />

[[File:EGL-21_sketch.jpg|thumb|right|100px|Rey's sketch of EGL-21]]
The [[welding droid]] [[GTAW-74]] was a colleague of EGL-21, and the pair could sometimes be spotted together in the shade trading jokes that their customers had brought to them.<ref name="Databank" /> The scavenger [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] sometimes traded with EGL-21 and would try and think of jokes he might like while she was traveling across the [[Goazon Badlands]].<ref name="Survival Guide" /> Whenever she did think of a new joke she would seek EGL-21 and GTAW-74 out, as the pair's services could greatly improved the value of pieces of scrap.<ref name="BuildR2" /> At some point, she wrote about the [[power droid]] in a [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]] she was compiling and included a drawing of him.<ref name="Survival Guide" />

In 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> EGL-21 walked through one of the [[tent]]s in Niima Outpost and passed by Rey and the [[BB-series astromech droid|astromech droid]] [[BB-8]] arguing with the former [[First Order stormtrooper]] [[Finn]]. Soon after, the [[First Order]] [[Escape from Jakku|attacked]] Niima Outpost.<ref name="TFA" />

==Characteristics==
EGL-21 was a power droid who enjoyed a good joke.<ref name="Survival Guide" /> He became so quirky with age and lack of [[memory wipe]]s that he formed a genuine friendship with the equally eccentric GTAW-74. Simple survival and the company of others was enough for the pair.<ref name="BuildR2" /> He had [[Color|gray]] plating<ref name="TFA" /> and the capability to weld objects.<ref name="Survival Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
EGL-21 was created for the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. He was not identified in the film<ref name="TFA" /> but was simultaneously named through the books ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which were released in conjunction with the film and written by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Pablo Hidalgo]], respectively.<ref name="Survival Guide" /><ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
