'''Antidar Williams''' was a [[human]] [[officer]] of the [[Judicial Forces]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], training at the [[Judicial Academy]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Between [[40 BBY]] and [[34 BBY]], Williams and [[Captain]] [[Maoi Madakor]] [[pilot]]ed a [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class space cruiser]] while transporting the [[Jedi]] [[Yoda]] and [[Vetna Mooncrest]] to the [[moon]] [[Veeros]] so that they could [[Mission to Veeros|investigate]] experiments taking place there. 

In [[32 BBY]], he and Madakor piloted the [[cruiser]] ''[[Radiant VII]]'' while transporting two Jedi [[ambassador]]s&mdash;[[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]&mdash;to the [[Naboo system]] in order to negotiate an end to the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo|blockade]] of the planet [[Naboo]]. After docking on the Federation [[flagship]], the ''[[Saak'ak]]'', Williams and Madakor waited aboard the ''Radiant VII'' but were [[Death|killed]] when the cruiser was [[Escape from the Saak'ak|ambushed]] and destroyed by a ceiling-mounted [[Droid laser turret (Saak'ak)|droid laser turret]] on the orders of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]].

==Biography==
===Judicial officer and pilot===
{{Quote|Well&hellip; we're on the south side of the palace. Solar activity seems to be ''hiding'' our approach.''"<br />"''Excellent, ''Captain Madakor''. Now&hellip; ''lower'' the ship.|Maoi Madakor and Yoda|<ref name="JK2">[[Jedi Knights 2|''Jedi Knights'' 2]]</ref>}}
Antidar Williams was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Force Collection" /> from the [[planet]] [[Barkhesh]] who served as an [[officer]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Judicial Department]]. After receiving military training at the [[Judicial Academy]] on the planet [[Coruscant]], [[capital]] of the Republic, Williams and other [[Judicial]]s worked together with the [[Galactic Senate]]'s [[Republic Diplomatic Corps|Diplomatic Corps]] and the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Council of Reconciliation]] to maintain peace in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Naboo" />

[[File:MadakorWilliams-JediKnights2.png|thumb|right|300px|Williams and Madakor transported Yoda and Vetna Mooncrest to Veeros.]]
Between [[40 BBY]] and [[34 BBY]],<ref name="JKDate">The events of [[Jedi Knights 2|''Jedi Knights'' 2]] take place between [[40 BBY]] and [[34 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:JediKnightsDate|here]].</ref> Williams and [[Captain]] [[Maoi Madakor]] co-[[pilot]]ed a [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class space cruiser]] used to transport the [[Jedi]] [[Yoda]] and [[Vetna Mooncrest]] to the remote [[moon]] [[Veeros]] when they were [[Mission to Veeros|dispatched]] to investigate dark experiments reported to be happening there. They landed the [[cruiser]] at the [[Veeros palace|palace]] of the moon's leader and sole inhabitant, [[Atha Prime]], where the Jedi disembarked. Yoda and Mooncrest found nothing at the palace and so soon departed in the cruiser again; however, they suspected that the experiments they sought were being hidden and so returned several [[Standard hour|hours]] later south side of the palace.<ref name="JK2" />

Madakor reported to the Jedi in the cruiser's [[cockpit]] that [[Star|solar]] activity was covering their approach, and he ordered the two pilots to lower the ship closer to the moon's surface, allowing the Jedi to leap out. Yoda and Mooncrest then discovered a horde of [[xerexi]] that Prime had created and fought them, although Prime himself escaped from the moon. Yoda and Mooncrest then returned to the cruiser and left Veeros as other Jedi eliminated the remaining xerexi there.<ref name="JK2" />

===Collateral damage===
{{Quote|The chancellor should never have brought them into this. Kill them immediately.''"<br />"''Yes. Yes, my lord, uh, as you wish.|Darth Sidious orders Nute Gunray to kill the Jedi, leading to Antidar Williams's death|audio=SidiousAndGunray-KillThem.flac|<ref name="Episode I" />}}
[[File:RadiantVIIcockpit-SWBC58.png|thumb|left|300px|Antidar Williams piloted the ''Radiant VII''.]]
In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="TPMDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}} to [[32 BBY]].</ref> he was chosen to co-pilot the Diplomatic Corps ''Consular''-class space cruiser ''[[Radiant VII]]'' alongside Madakor<ref name="Naboo" /> while transporting [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], to the [[Naboo system]]. The two Jedi were acting as [[ambassador]]s on behalf of Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]] in the hopes of negotiating an end to the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo|blockade]] of the planet [[Naboo]].<ref name="Episode I" /> Upon reaching the [[blockade]], the ''Radiant VII'' docked in one of the main [[hangar]]s aboard the Trade Federation [[flagship]], the ''[[Saak'ak]]''.<ref name="Naboo" />

Williams and Madakor remained in the cruiser's cockpit while the Jedi left to meet with Federation [[Viceroy of the Trade Federation|Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]]. Learning that the Jedi had been sent to negotiate, Gunray contacted his master, the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], who ordered him to [[Death|kill]] the Republic party.<ref name="Episode I" />

[[File:Radiant_VII_destruction.png|thumb|right|300px|Williams was killed in the destruction of the ''Radiant VII''.]]
Under Gunray's orders, a [[Droid laser turret (Saak'ak)|droid laser turret]] descended from the ceiling in the hangar where the ''Radiant VII'' was parked and opened fire on the [[Starship|vessel]]. Williams<ref name="Episode I" /> immediately<ref name="Naboo" /> saw the attack coming and tried to warn Madakor, who ordered him to activate the cruiser's [[Deflector shield|shields]], but it was too late,<ref name="Episode I" /> and both were instantly killed as the cruiser exploded.<ref name="Naboo" /> The Jedi were able to avoid the Federation's attempt on their lives and [[Escape from the Saak'ak|escaped]] the ''Saak'ak'', ultimately helping to [[Battle of Naboo|defeat]] the Federation after it invaded Naboo.<ref name="Episode I" />

==Personality and traits==
Antidar Williams had black [[hair]], [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s, and light skin. He spoke with an aristocratic accent<ref name="Episode I" /> and stood 1.93 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Naboo" />

==Equipment==
While on the ''Radiant VII'', Williams wore a blue Judicial Forces [[Clothing|uniform]]<ref name="Episode I" /> and was unarmed.<ref name="Naboo" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|But I said to Robin, 'I would really like to appear in the film at some point so people could see my face,' and when the Jedi's pilot came up, they offered it to me.|Silas Carson, on getting the role of Antidar Williams|<ref name="Insider 44" />}}
[[File:NuteDatabank.png|thumb|left|300px|As Nute Gunray, Silas Carson was responsible for the death of Antidar Williams, whom he also portrayed.]]
Antidar Williams was created for the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], in which he was played by [[Silas Carson]].<ref name="Episode I" /> Although Carson initially auditioned for a separate, slightly more prominent pilot role that ended up not making it into the film,<ref name="Insider 44">{{InsiderCite|44|In the Star Wars Universe|In the ''Star Wars'' Universe: Silas Carson}}</ref> casting director [[Robin Gurland]] enjoyed the interview with him and so mentioned him to director [[George Lucas]]. Deciding that Carson looked like an interesting person, Lucas had him come back in for animatics<ref name="Insider 83">{{InsiderCite|83|Multiple Personality}}</ref> and then gave him the part of the Jedi [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] instead.<ref name="Insider 44" />

Carson was later also given the roles of Nute Gunray and [[Senator]] [[Lott Dod]], both of which, along with Mundi, required him to wear prosthetics that covered his face. Carson mentioned to Gurland that he would like for his actual face to appear in the film, so when it came time to cast Williams, Gurland asked him if he would like the role.<ref name="Insider 44" /> All of the scenes in the cockpit of the ''Radiant VII'' were filmed on [[September 8]], [[1997]].<ref name="Worlds">''[[Creating the Worlds of Star Wars: 365 Days]]''</ref>

===Naming===
{{Quote|I tried numerous ways of getting my own name or one of my nicknames in there, but in the end they didn't look right and were kind of an ego-trip anyway.|Ian Tapley, on naming Antidar Williams|<ref name="blog">{{Blog|thefragrantwookiee|46|Lt. Antidar Williams: A 'What's the Story?' Story}}</ref>}}
[[File:Republic_Cruiser_SWE.png|thumb|right|300px|Antidar Williams's first name is an anagram of his ship, the ''Radiant VII''.]]
The Antidar Williams character was first given a name in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] by the [[Star Wars video games|mobile card game]] ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'',<ref name="Force Collection" /> which initially launched in [[2013]]. Although the launch of ''Force Collection'' predates<ref name="Force Collection release">{{SW|url=news/announcing-star-wars-force-collection|text=Announcing Star Wars: Force Collection|nolive=1}}</ref> the ''Star Wars'' canon reset of [[2014]],<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]] confirmed that the game was kept updated to fit with canon.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|holocronkeeper|status/846807700482666496|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=I'm not gonna go through the trouble of changing them and I don't have any reason to believe currently that anyone would. [&hellip;] On the off-chance that we DID have a current need to change any of these, we would have for Force Collection.|archivefile=File:Leland_Chee_Force_Collection.png|archivedate=20190727180426}} &mdash; [[Leland Chee]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] has confirmed that [[in-universe]] information from ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' conforms with ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]].</ref>

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], Williams appeared prior to the film's release in the [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|novelization]] of ''The Phantom Menace'', written by [[Terry Brooks]].<ref name="TPMn">[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'' novelization]]</ref> The character was originally given a surname by the ''[[Coruscant Limited]]'' expansion set of the [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]] in [[2001]].<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Coruscant Limited|cardname=Lieutenant Williams}}</ref> The forename "Antidar" was then created by fan [[Ian Tapley]] for the "[[What's The Story?]]" feature on [[StarWars.com]]. Tapley initially wanted the character's first name to be a [[tuckerization]] of his own name or one of his nicknames, but after being unable to come up with a satisfying result, he settled for an anagram of "radiant"&mdash;a reference to Williams's ship, the ''Radiant VII''.<ref name="blog" />
