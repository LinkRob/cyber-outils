'''Fadime''' was a [[Nikto]] [[Gender|female]] who was a [[pilot]] in the [[New Republic]]'s [[Hound Squadron]]. She flew under the callsign Hound Six. During the [[Attack on the Hellion's Dare|Attack on the ''Hellion's Dare'']], Fadime helped defend the [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate|frigate]] as they fled into the [[Oridol Cluster]]. When [[Hound Leader]] [[Stanislok]] was [[Death|killed]], Fadime assumed some of his duties, including leading the squadron at the tactical board. Fadime was killed shortly after by the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
