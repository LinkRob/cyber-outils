'''Disney&ndash;Lucasfilm Press''', occasionally known as '''Lucasfilm Press''', is an imprint of '''Disney Book Group''' and a subsidiary of [[Wikipedia:Disney Publishing Worldwide|Disney Publishing Worldwide]]. 

==History==
On [[May 2]], [[2025]], [[Random House]] announced that they had acquired the license after negotiations with Disney Publishing,<ref name="RandomHouseArticle">{{PenguinRandomHouse|subdomain=global|url=announcements/penguin-random-house-expands-relationship-with-disney-to-publish-popular-stories|text=Penguin Random House Expands Relationship With Disney to Publish Popular Stories|archivedate=20250516114424}}</ref> and Disney&ndash;Lucasfilm Press was removed from the publisher listing on Random House's website for many ''Star Wars'' books.{{Fact}}
