'''''A Droid Story''''' is a forthcoming animated film scheduled to premiere on [[Disney+]]. The film was announced on [[December 10]], [[2020]] and will feature a new character that is guided by [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]]. Development will be handled by [[Industrial Light & Magic]] as well as [[Lucasfilm Animation]].<ref name="Announcement" /> However, there have not been any official updates since [[February 9]], [[2021]].<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|200|Launchpad}}</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''As Lucasfilm continues to develop new stories, the intersection of animation and visual effects offers new opportunities to explore. Lucasfilm Animation will be teaming up with Lucasfilm's visual effects team, Industrial Light & Magic, to develop a special ''Star Wars'' adventure for Disney+, ''A Droid Story''. This epic journey will introduce us to a new hero, guided by legendary duo R2-D2 and C-3PO.''<ref name="Announcement" />
