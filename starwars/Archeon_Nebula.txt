The '''Archeon Nebula''' was a [[nebula]] in the [[Lothal sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was a [[stellar nursery]] where new [[star]]s were being born. Due to this, its gases could be ignited by [[proton torpedo]]es. The [[Archeon Pass|Archeon pass]], a dangerous smuggling route, traversed the nebula. In [[2 BBY]], [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'', on a [[Operation Handoff|mission]] to transport [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] to [[Dantooine]], decided to use the pass as a shortcut out of the Lothal sector.<ref name="Secret Cargo" />
