{{Quote|We have three small ships that live in the hold of the Gaze now. ''[&hellip;]'' One ''[is]'' a retrofitted exploratory shuttle Esstrop and Kortanio had been using to spread the word of the Path.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
A retrofitted '''exploratory shuttle''' belonging to the [[Kessurian]] partners [[Esstrop]] and [[Kortanio]] was used during the [[High Republic Era]]. They used the [[shuttle]] in their service to the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] to preach its teachings along the [[Galactic Frontier]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> at some point no later than [[382 BBY]].<ref name="PathFall">''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' establishes that the [[Path of the Open Hand]] dissolved in the aftermath of the [[Night of Sorrow]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]]. Therefore, [[Esstrop]] and [[Kortanio]] must have used the [[shuttle]] in service to the Path at some point no later than 382 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> After the cult [[Night of Sorrow|fell apart]], they were recruited by [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the [[Evereni]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> founder of a group of [[pirate]]s who would eventually become the [[Nihil]]<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref>&mdash;into her<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> pirate band and used the [[Starship|ship]] during their attacks.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> At some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]],<ref name="FaerDate">The segment of "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" depicting a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi's]] [[death]] onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' takes place around [[379 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:ClosedFistJediDeathDate|here]]. [[Marda Ro]] met the [[Faers]] after the [[Jedi]]'s death; therefore, she must have met them no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the shuttle was one of the ships sent by Ro to surround the [[Vika and Velya Faer's ship|ship]] of the Evereni [[twin]]s [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]] while she sent the [[Faers]] an invitation to [[Marda Ro's hunt|meet]] with her.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Ro later mentioned the starship in a [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]] she recorded for her [[Ro family|descendants]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during his youth, Ro's [[Gender|great-great-grandson]] [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to her memoirs, including the entries where the shuttle was mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Behind the scenes==
The shuttle was mentioned in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" />
