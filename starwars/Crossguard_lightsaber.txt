The '''crossguard lightsaber''' was an ancient [[lightsaber]] variant with three blades&mdash;a primary blade and two vents just below it called quillons&mdash;or a physical crossguard. It was once common among [[Form II|Makashi]] practitioners by the time of the [[Great Scourge of Malachor]]. The [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Atrius]] wielded a [[Darth Atrius' lightsabers|pair]] of such lightsabers imbued with his anger. Later, during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] wielded a [[Stellan Gios's lightsaber|crossguard lightsaber]] with an additional, folding physical crossguard which supported the quillon blades. [[First Order]] warlord and later [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] wielded a [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|lightsaber]] where the quillons served an additional function as vents to balance out an unstable, cracked [[kyber crystal]].

==Description==
{{Quote|With perpendicular vents to release additional energy generated by unusually powerful or damaged kyber crystals, this cross-shaped lightsaber—based on an ancient design—emits two shorter side-blades from its hilt that not only protect a user's hand but are also deadly in battle.|[[Luke Skywalker]]|<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Kylo-lightsaber-teaser-shot.png|thumb|left|300px|The extra vents of a crossguard lightsaber released additional energy.]]
Crossguard [[lightsaber]]s<ref name="Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/twilight-of-the-apprentice-episode-gallery-2|text="Twilight of the Apprentice" Episode Gallery}}; image #6 {{C|of 51}}</ref> were lightsabers with a single hilt for a main blade that either additionally possessed two small vents below the primary blade, positioned opposite each other on the hilt,<ref name="TFA VD" /> or had a physical crossguard that extended from the tip of the hilt.<ref name="Haul">{{StoryCite|story=The Haul|book=The High Republic Adventures Annual 2021}}</ref> Crossguard lightsaber vents, called quillons, projected two smaller blades and thus could be used to vent out additional energy. The smaller blades were tributaries of the main blade. Quillons vented out the energy using an array of focusing crystal activators to split the energy from the [[kyber crystal]] inside. Emitter shrouds were used to protect the wielder's hands from injury or being cut off from battle.<ref name="TFA VD" />

An ancient design, it was once common among practitioners of the [[lightsaber combat]] [[Form II]], also known as Makashi.<ref name="G&G">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref> During the High Republic Era, another type of crossguard lightsaber was popular among the Jedi Order. These lightsabers featured a physical crossguard made from metal without having any additional energy blades. Known users include [[Avar Kriss]], [[Burryaga Agaburry]], and [[Creighton Sun]].

The crossguard lightsaber was often wielded as a heavier alternative to the single-bladed lightsaber, with users such as [[Cal Kestis]] or Kylo Ren opting to wield it with both hands while attacking in some circumstances. This technique allowed the wielder to break an opponent's guard with ease, or inflict far more grievous wounds in comparison to the more balanced single-bladed lightsaber.<ref name="Star Wars Jedi: Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:StellanGios-SWTLC.png|thumb|During the High Republic Era, Jedi Master Stellan Gios utilized a crossguard lightsaber.]]
The crossguard lightsaber was an ancient lightsaber variant. It proved especially popular among practitioners of the lightsaber combat style known as Makashi, or Form II, and many of them fought at the [[Great Scourge of Malachor]].<ref name="G&G" />

[[Darth Atrius]] owned a [[Darth Atrius' lightsabers|pair]] of crossguard lightsabers during a [[time]] when the [[Sith]] were plentiful and powerful. Imbued with the [[Sith Lord]]'s anger, the lightsabers survived long after the perceived downfall of the Sith, capable of driving their wielders insane with anger. They were eventually destroyed by [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Star Wars Annual 4" />

During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Creighton Sun]] utilized a [[Color|blue]]-[[Creighton Sun's lightsaber|bladed lightsaber]].<ref name="Battle of Jedha">''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]''</ref> Later, Jedi Master [[Stellan Gios]] owned a [[Stellan Gios's lightsaber|lightsaber]] with a retractable physical crossguard, the arms of which would fold up when the weapon was inactive. When the lightsaber was ignited, the arms would fold out and support the two quillon beams, providing an additional layer of hand protection.<ref name="The Lightsaber Collection">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-high-republic-lightsaber|text=An Elegant Weapon: Get an Up-Close Look at a Lightsaber from ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' - Exclusive}}</ref> The [[Pooba]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Barabo]] also had a blue-bladed crossguard lightsaber of his own.<ref name="Nubs's Big Mistake">{{YJA|Nubs's Big Mistake}}</ref>

The [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Burryaga Agaburry]] [[Burryaga's lightsaber|had]] a [[Color|blue]] lightsaber with the [[lightsaber]]'s hilt was plated in<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[meryx]],<ref name="M&A">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> the amber of the [[white wroshyr tree]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> which had been extinct for millennia,<ref name="M&A" /> and was finished off with an [[electrum]] crosspiece.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

[[File:CalKestisSecondLightsaberCrossguard-Survivor.jpg|left|thumb|Cal Kestis modified his second lightsaber with a crossguard design.|250x250px]]
During his [[Stinger Mantis crew's crusade against the Empire|crusade]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] fought against several lieutenants of the [[Bedlam Raiders]], several of whom&mdash;among them [[Drya Thornne]]&mdash;wielded crossguard lightsabers that had been taken from dead Jedi by [[Rayvis]]. Kestis later added a crossguard feature to his blue-bladed [[Cal Kestis' second lightsaber|lightsaber]], using parts salvaged from [[Drya Thornne's lightsaber|Thornne's lightsaber]].<ref name="Survivor" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Ezra Bridger]] found an ancient green-bladed crossguard lightsaber on [[Malachor]]. When he picked it up and activated it, it almost immediately broke and deactivated itself.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice" />

[[File:KyloRenLightsaberHilt-PropShop.png|thumb|300px|Kylo Ren's crossguard lightsaber was uniquely made due to his cracked kyber crystal.]]
Modifying his Jedi lightsaber and [[bleeding]] his kyber [[crystal]],<ref name="Kylo Ren 4">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 4|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 4]]</ref> [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] built [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|his own]] crossguard blade<ref name="TFA VD" /> because his kyber [[crystal]] had cracked in the process.<ref name="Skywalker: A Family at War">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]''</ref> With the extra vents providing an outlet, Ren's blade was unstable but functional.<ref name="TFA VD" /> Ren was one of the few wielders of a lightsaber at that time.<ref name="Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref> Ren's [[Light side of the Force|light side]] counterpart, [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], briefly claimed the lightsaber while confronting [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] during the [[Battle of Oetchi]].<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> At the end of his time as a [[Knights of Ren|Knight of Ren]], Kylo flung his weapon into the sea on the [[moon]] [[Kef Bir]] as a sign of renunciation of his commitment to the [[dark side of the Force]].<ref name="The Rise of Skywalker">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A crossguard lightsaber first appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], the first installment of the ''Star Wars'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> The concept of a [[Crossguard lightsaber/Legends|crossguard lightsaber]] was first introduced in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]]. The lightsaber, which had two beams rather than three, made its first appearance in the [[2004]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Republic 61|''Republic'' 61]].<ref name="Dead Ends">[[Republic 61|''Republic'' 61]]</ref> Crossguard hilted lightsabers with metallic quillons first appeared in [[Star Wars: Jango Fett: Open Seasons|''Jango Fett: Open Seasons'']] Issue #3.<ref name="Jango Fett: Open Seasons 3">[[Jango Fett: Open Seasons 3|''Jango Fett: Open Seasons'' 3]]</ref>
