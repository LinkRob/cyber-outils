The '''Dreamwort''' was a [[plant]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[electromagnetic wind]]&ndash;swept [[planet]] [[Zeffo]]. In [[14 BBY]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] collected a dreamwort [[seed]] to plant in his [[Stinger Mantis crew|crew member]] [[Greez Dritus]]'s [[terrarium]] aboard their [[S-161 "Stinger" XL|luxury yacht]], ''[[Stinger Mantis]]''.

==Description==
Native to the [[electromagnetic wind]]&ndash;swept [[planet]] [[Zeffo]], the dreamwort had [[Color|yellow]] [[flower]]s and grew in cliffs and [[grass]]y patches. The [[plant]] also grew in the [[Water|lakeside]] area that would eventually be known as the [[Crash Site]], a location that housed the wreckage of a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator-class'' Star Destroyer]] that crashed on Zeffo in the aftermath of a [[Battle against a Separatist dreadnought|battle]] against a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[dreadnought]]. The plant had a green stalk and leaves, which grew up the stalk. The yellow flower at the top of the stem was surrounded by pointy, yellow petals.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==History==
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] harvested a Dreamwort [[seed]] from a rocky area within the Crash Site. Kestis planted the seed he had collected in a [[terrarium]] owned by his companion [[Greez Dritus]] aboard the [[S-161 "Stinger" XL|luxury yacht]] ''[[Stinger Mantis]]''. Kestis had been collecting seeds on various planets as a form of reimbursement for Dritus' assistance in the [[Stinger Mantis crew|''Mantis'' crew's]] [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|mission]] to rebuild the [[Jedi Order]]. Kestis stated that the plants reminded him of Greez's [[Hair|fur]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
{{EndGame}}
