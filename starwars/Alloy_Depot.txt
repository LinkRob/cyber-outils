'''Alloy Depots''' were structures utilized by both the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They were used to store [[Metal|alloys]] processed at [[Alloy Refinery|Alloy Refineries]], which were then used during the war effort to enhance various military bases owned by both factions. These depots could be upgraded to increase their defenses, as well as their storage capacity.<ref name="Commander" />
