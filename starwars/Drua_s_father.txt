A [[Gender|male]] individual was the '''father''' of the [[Twi'lek]] girl [[Drua]], a [[Youngling|child]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. However, Drua grew up not knowing her [[Parent|father]], which left her an orphan when her [[Drua's mother|mother]] [[Death|died]]<ref name="LOTS" /> around [[16 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Lords of the Sith]]'' to [[14 BBY]]. As ''Lords of the Sith'' establishes [[Drua's mother]] [[Death|died]] two [[Standard year|years]] before the book's events, they must have passed away around [[16 BBY]].</ref> Afterward, Drua lived with her [[Drua's grandfather|grandfather]] in a [[Drua's Village|village]] on Ryloth.<ref name="LOTS" /> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Drua spoke to the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lords]] [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]&mdash;unaware of their true identities&mdash;and mentioned her parents as she led the pair to her [[City|village]].<ref name="LOTS" />
