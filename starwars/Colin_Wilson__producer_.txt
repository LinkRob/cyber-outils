'''Colin Wilson''' is a film producer/editor who has worked on a number of projects with [[Steven Spielberg]] and [[Kathleen Kennedy]], including the [[Indiana Jones (franchise)|''Indiana Jones'']] franchise. He is credited as a co-producer of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' and as a executive producer of ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]''.
