The '''4L4 fusial thrust engine''' was an [[engine]] produced by the [[Incom Corporation]] for use on their [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s.<ref name="SWCT X-wing" /> T-65Bs had four 4L4 fusial thrust engines, one on each wing.<ref name="ANH" /> A [[4j.4 fusial thrust engine]] could be used in place of a 4L4 fusial thrust engine.<ref name="SWCT X-wing" />
