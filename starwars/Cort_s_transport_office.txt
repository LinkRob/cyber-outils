During the [[Imperial Era]], a '''transport office''' was located in a [[Unidentified desert town|desert town]]. This ticket-selling establishment was run by [[Cort]],<ref name="TOTU 101" /> a [[Gender|female]]<ref name="TOTU 101 AD">{{TOTU|A Way Forward}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> [[human]] who found<ref name="TOTU 101" /> her<ref name="TOTU 101 AD" /> profession unappealing. Cort hired [[Asajj Ventress]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s former [[assassin]], who was tasked with scanning customers' [[datacard]]s and tossing out clients who proved uncooperative.<ref name="TOTU 101" />

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[Cort]]'s transport office debuted in "[[A Way Forward]]," the first episode of the 3D CGI animation series ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Underworld]]'',<ref name="TOTU 101" /> which premiered on [[Disney+]] on [[May 4]], [[2025]]. A snapshot of the establishment had previously been included in the trailer of the series, which was released on April 2, the same year.<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|GkywNANueew|Tales of the Underworld &#124; Official Trailer &#124; Available May 4 on Disney+}}</ref>
