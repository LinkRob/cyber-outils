{{Quote|I accompanied the Inquisitor Ninth Sister to Cabarria to investigate a Jedi sighting. While there, I was attacked by mercenaries. I was the target of a kill order.|Darth Vader, to Emperor Palpatine|<ref name="Dark Lord 12">[[Darth Vader (2017) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 12]]</ref>}}
'''Cabarria''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Colonies]] and [[the Interior]] regions where the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and the [[Inquisitor]] [[Ninth Sister]] [[Mission to Cabarria|traveled]] to investigate a reported [[Jedi]] sighting at a [[Unidentified bar (Cabarria)|bar]] in [[19 BBY]]. However, Vader instead walked into a trap set by the [[Chas]], a [[bounty hunter]] family that had been hired to [[Death|kill]] him. The Chas were able to disarm Vader, but the [[Sith Lord]] instead used patrons as both shields and [[Telekinesis|projectiles]], soon forcing the [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunters]] to flee.

Vader pursued the Chas in a [[speeder]] chase until he was able to ground the family, making the [[parent]]s [[Bhada Cha|Bhada]] and [[Ramat Cha]] help him find out who hired them by threatening the life of their [[Gender|daughter]], [[Chanath Cha]]. Chanath was made to swear to forget the encounter and flee in order to survive while her parents located the origin of the kill order on Vader. Once given the information he needed, Vader slew Bhada and Ramat, Chanath bearing witness from afar, and left for the planet [[Coruscant]].

==Description==
[[File:Cabarria-GalaxyMapPoster.png|thumb|right|250px|Cabarria was situated along the Corellian Trade Spine.]]
Cabarria was a [[planet]]<ref name="Lightsabers">{{EncyclopediaCite|Lightsabers and Jedi Equipment}}</ref> located in the [[Cabarria system]] within an intersecting portion of the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Interior]] regions,<ref name="Interior" /> in grid square M-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. It was situated along the [[Corellian Trade Spine]]<ref name="Galaxy" /> [[super-hyperroute]]<ref name="BMF 48">{{BuildFalconCite|48|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> between the [[Nantoon system|Nantoon]] and [[Corellian system]]s.<ref name="Galaxy" /> [[Cloud]]s and a [[Color|yellow]] celestial body were visible in Cabarria's sky. In the planet's<ref name="Dark Lord 11" /> Type I [[atmosphere]]<ref name="Atmosphere" />&mdash;which was breathable to [[human]]s and other [[species]]&mdash;were floating freeways for [[speeder]]s as well as buildings including a [[Unidentified bar (Cabarria)|bar]].<ref name="Dark Lord 11" />

==History==
===The Chas' trap===
{{Quote|This is the location?''"<br />"''Yeah. This is it. Bar fight in here, few days ago. Someone reported seeing a person use the Force to get out of it. Said they maybe even had a lightsaber. Not many details. This is definitely the spot, though.|Darth Vader and the Ninth Sister|<ref name="Dark Lord 11" />}}
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> during the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Chas]], a family of [[bounty hunter]]s consisting of the couple [[Bhada Cha|Bhada]] and [[Ramat Cha]] and their<ref name="Dark Lord 11" /> twelve-[[Standard year|year]]-old [[Gender|daughter]]<ref name="Hidden Empire 3">[[Hidden Empire 3|''Hidden Empire'' 3]]</ref> [[Chanath Cha]], were [[Assassination attempts on Darth Vader|hired]] through the [[Hunter Net]]<ref name="Dark Lord 11" /> by a pair of high-[[Military rank|ranking]] [[Imperial officer]]s to [[Death|kill]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], a [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] and [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] new enforcer.<ref name="Dark Lord 12" /> The Chas set a [[Mission to Cabarria|trap]] at the [[Cantina|bar]] at Cabarria, with a falsified [[Jedi]] sighting being reported to lure Vader there. While the [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunters]] waited inside the bar, Vader and the [[Inquisitor]] [[Ninth Sister]] arrived at an outside parking area. The Ninth [[Sister (Inquisitorius)|Sister]] questioned why Vader had gone out of his way to pursue a likely false Jedi sighting, claiming that he was eager to fight another Jedi. Vader was dismissive of the claims and had the Inquisitor remain at their speeders while he entered the bar.<ref name="Dark Lord 11" />

[[File:CabarriaTrap-2017DarthVader11.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The Chas attack Darth Vader in the bar.]]
Inside, Vader approached the patrons and demanded information on the supposed Jedi. Awaiting the [[Sith Lord]], whom they thought to be another Jedi, the Chas sprung their trap, Chanath using a [[tractor rifle]] to try and take away [[Kirak Infil'a's lightsaber|Vader's lightsaber]]. Vader accidentally destroyed the [[lightsaber]] while [[Telekinesis|pulling it back]] with [[the Force]], and Ramat used an [[ion grenade]] to disable all [[weapon]]s in the bar sans those of the Chas. Bhada and Ramat used their ion blast as leverage to advise the other patrons not to interfere with their hunt, but Vader instead began lifting and [[Force choke|choking]] the bystanders with the Force, using them as shields and projectiles when the Chas began firing at him from the cover of their [[Personal energy shield|personal shields]]. The Sith Lord also contacted the Ninth Sister for help, but she pretended the [[ion pulse]] had affected her [[comlink]].<ref name="Dark Lord 11" />

===Vader strikes back===
{{Quote|Do you remember ''Cabarria'', Vader? Two decades ago, on a lonely stretch of skyway. A family. A man, a woman and a girl of 12. You killed my mother. Her name was ''Ramat Cha''. You killed my father. His name was ''Bhada Cha''. My name is Chanath Cha, and that day, I vowed to end your life.|Chanath Cha confronts Darth Vader about the events at Cabarria|<ref name="Hidden Empire 3" />}}
As the fighting continued, the Chas chose to retreat as their [[deflector shield]]s were being depleted of power and a growing number of patrons were being killed. They fled for a speeder and held the Ninth Sister at gunpoint as they hijacked one of them. The trio then sped away before Vader exited the bar, accusing the Inquisitor of setting up the trap. When the Ninth Sister denied the claims, the Sith Lord, taking the [[Ninth Sister's lightsaber|Inquisitor's lightsaber]], had her stay at the parking area while he took a speeder to pursue the bounty hunters. Vader quickly caught up with the Chas, who fired back at their pursuer. Deflecting their [[blaster]] bolts with his borrowed [[Double-bladed spinning lightsaber|lightsaber]], Vader used the weapon to steer a large vehicle off-course and onto the Chas' speeder.<ref name="Dark Lord 11" />

[[File:VaderThreatensTheChas-2017DarthVader11.png|thumb|right|350px|Darth Vader threatens the Chas.]]
The Chas used their shields to avoid being crushed by the crashing vehicle, but the crash allowed Vader to reach the three and hold Chanath at saber-point. The Sith Lord demanded to know who hired the bounty hunters, so Ramat offered to [[Slicer|slice]] the Hunter Net, negotiating for Chanath to be released before she could locate the exact origin location of the kill order on Vader.<ref name="Dark Lord 11" />

After Chanath was let go, her [[parent]]s had her swear to forget about their encounter with Vader and flee. With her daughter leaving, Ramat located the origin of the kill order to the top of the [[Imperial Executive Building]] on<ref name="Dark Lord 11" /> the planet<ref name="Coruscant DB">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref> [[Coruscant]]. Having acquired the information,<ref name="Dark Lord 11" /> Vader then killed Bhada and Ramat<ref name="Crimson Reign 1">[[Crimson Reign 1|''Crimson Reign'' 1]]</ref> and left for Coruscant with the Ninth Sister.<ref name="Dark Lord 12" /> Chanath watched her parents' deaths from afar<ref name="Crimson Reign 1" /> and vowed to kill Vader on that [[Standard day|day]]. She eventually [[Battle against the Orphans|confronted]] the Sith Lord<ref name="Hidden Empire 3" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Hidden Empire 3|''Hidden Empire'' 3]] take place after the [[Auction for Han Solo]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> where she brought up the events on Cabarria. Chanath attempted to kill Vader to avenge her parents, but the Sith Lord killed her first.<ref name="Hidden Empire 3" />
