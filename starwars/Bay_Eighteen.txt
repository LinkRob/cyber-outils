'''Bay Eighteen''' was a [[hangar]] bay located on the modified [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser]], the ''[[Home One]]'', the [[flagship]] of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="SW37" /> Between [[3 ABY]] to [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''Star Wars'' 35 take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] visited [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] at the hangar bay to tell her that he would be using the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to go on a [[Mission to Jabba's Palace (Lando Calrissian)|mission]] for [[Lobot]]. Organa questioned Calrissian's legitimacy but decided to trust him with the [[starship]].<ref name="SW37" />
