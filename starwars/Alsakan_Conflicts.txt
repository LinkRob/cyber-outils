The '''Alsakan Conflicts''' were a series of conflicts that saw the first deployment of [[capital ship]]s that matched the scale of those used in the later [[Clone Wars]] and [[Galactic Civil War]]. This "dreadnought era" ended with the development of cutting-edge [[turbolaser]]s that could tear apart [[ray shield]]s, therefore rendering large [[warship]]s obsolete.<ref name="Battles" /> The conflicts shared their name with [[Alsakan]], an astronomical object located in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Alsakan Conflicts were first mentioned in the [[2021]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Battles" /> The [[Alsakan Conflicts/Legends|conflicts]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, in which they were first mentioned in ''[[Han Solo and the Corporate Sector Sourcebook]]'', a [[1993]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Michael Allen Horne]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Corporate">''[[Han Solo and the Corporate Sector Sourcebook]]''</ref>
