The '''Entruvia Conflict''' was a war fought<ref name="Doctor Aphra 6" /> in<ref name="Battlenamia" /> the [[Entruvia system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> long before the foundation of the modern [[Galactic Republic]], when the [[Domancion Accord]] was still employed as an epoch, between the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Ordu Aspectu]], a splinter sect. Eventually, a [[Jedi–Ordu Aspectu treaty|peace treaty]] was signed, which allowed the orthodox Jedi to inspect the Ordu Aspectu's work for signs of dangerous heresy.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 6" />
