'''''ESPN Presents: Never Tell Me The Odds''''' is [[Wikipedia:ESPN|ESPN's]] official ''[[Star Wars]]'' show, which debuted on [[April 26]], [[2023]] and is hosted by [[Wikipedia:Arda Ocal|Arda Öcal]], [[Wikipedia:Ryan McGee|Ryan McGee]], and Clinton Yates. Previous guests of the show include [[Carl Weathers]]<ref name="ESPN" /> and [[Billy Dee Williams]].<ref name="Spotify-CloudCity">{{Spotify|url=episode/7haHe0HlIgQSggGUjNkgtD|text=Cloud City Interior Design - ESPN Presents: Never Tell Me The Odds|quote=The guys sit down to speak with Billy Dee Williams about playing Lando Calrissian, capes, baseball, and his new book, 'What Have We Here?'}}</ref> The show aims to explore and celebrate ''Star Wars'' while simultaneously connecting it to sports<ref name="ESPN" /> and it consists of 13 episodes as of [[July]] [[2024]].<ref name="Apple" />

In [[February]] 2024, the trio did a live show from [[Wikipedia:Amalie Arena|Amalie Arena]] in [[Wikipedia:Tampa, Florida|Tampa, Florida]] during the [[Wikipedia:Tampa Bay Lightning|Tampa Bay Lightning]] [[Wikipedia:National Hockey League|NHL]] team's ''Star Wars'' Night.<ref name="Twitter-Öcal">{{Twitter|Arda|status/1765156244812165379|[[Wikipedia:Arda Ocal|Arda Öcal]]|date=2024-03-06|quote=We talk our amazing experience at @WaltDisneyWorld @starwars Galaxy's Edge before doors opened, our favorite things about the park, plus we do trivia and hang out with @TBLightning fans (and get attacked by @ThunderBugTBL) before Star Wars Night at @AmalieArena and reveal who the biggest Star Wars fan is in the Lightning organization (to the point that he shows up as Yoda to team functions!) Such a great experience, thank you everyone that stopped by!|archivedate=20240428021723}}</ref> Footage from the show's trip to [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] also aired on ESPN TV.<ref name="Twitter-Yates">{{Twitter|clintonyates|status/1761072228722999308|Clinton Yates|date=2024-02-23|quote=work was fun this week, kiddos! nothing like kicking it at @WaltDisneyWorld with my boys @arda & @ESPNMcGee|archivedate=20240428020454}}</ref>

The show's co-host Öcal frequently documents ''Star Wars'' crossovers, such as ''Star Wars'' Nights at sporting events. In 2024, he unveiled [[Dave Filoni]]'s latest jersey design for the [[Wikipedia:Wilkes-Barre/Scranton Penguins|Wilkes-Barre/Scranton Penguins]] hockey team's Annual ''Star Wars'' Night, which was [[Ahsoka Tano]]&ndash;themed.<ref name="Twitter-WBSPenguins">{{Twitter|WBSPenguins|status/1770446690027270355|[[Wikipedia:Wilkes-Barre/Scranton Penguins|x-Wilkes-Barre/Scranton Penguins]]|date=2024-03-20|quote=It's FINALLY time&mdash;@Arda is here to show y'all this year's Star Wars jersey!|archivedate=20240428021724}}</ref> Öcal has also hosted episodes of [[Wikipedia:SportsCenter|''SportsCenter'']] across platforms while dressed up as [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]<ref name="Twitter-Sportscenter">{{Twitter|Arda|status/1719022062126150109|[[Wikipedia:Arda Ocal|Arda Öcal]]|date=2023-10-30|quote=1000 likes and I'll host @SportsCenter like this @dave_filoni @starwars|archivedate=20231030203503}}</ref> and a [[Wikipedia:TED (conference)|TED Talk]] discussing ''Star Wars'' and Thrawn in the final third of the speech.<ref name="TEDTalk">{{YouTube|video=2CJHCBLAeWo|text=I Draw Real World Inspiration from Things I Use to Escape from the Real World &#124; Arda Ocal &#124; TEDxUW|channel=@TEDx|name=TEDx Talks|date=2024-02-12}}</ref>
