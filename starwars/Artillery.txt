'''Artillery''' was a term that referred to medium and heavy weapons systems designed for long-range bombardment against ground [[infantry]] or even [[capital ship]]s.<ref name="MH">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> [[Self-Propelled Heavy Artillery]] was used by the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] to destroy fleeing [[Trade Federation]] ships.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}}</ref>
