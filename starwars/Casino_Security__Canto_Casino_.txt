The '''Casino Security''' of the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Casino]] was the group of plainclothed [[Bouncer (occupation)|bouncers]] who guarded the Canto [[Casino]] in the coastal [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on [[Cantonica]]. They were responsible to avoid any problem within the casino even while the [[Canto Bight Police Department]] could also be called to handle major disturbances in the casino. In case of any disturbances, the Casino Security guards needed to report them to [[Pemmin Brunce]], the head of security.<ref name="TLJ: VD" />
