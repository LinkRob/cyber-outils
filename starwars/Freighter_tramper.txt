'''Freighter trampers,''' or just '''trampers''', were terms used to describe the economic migrants who felt they had no choice but to leave their homes on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] to search for work elsewhere due to the economic impact on Coruscant caused by the [[Separatist Crisis]], making them some of the first victims of the [[Clone Wars]] that followed. Trampers came from many different [[species]] and professions, including talented artisans and [[technician]]s, being united only by the harsh reality in which they found themselves.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> They hopped from one job to another on different planets<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|50|Starship Fact File}}</ref> and when promised work failed to materialize some freighter trampers found themselves constantly on the move, causing their once-pristine work gear to become more and more battered and improvised over time.<ref name="CVD" />

[[AA-9 Freighter-Liner]]s that were refitted to transport [[refugee]]s displaced by the Separatist Crisis off of Corsucant became an economic life line for freighter trampers, who would travel alongside the refugees,<ref name="BMF" /> huddled together carrying what little owned and dreaming of a better life.<ref name="CVD" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], which includes the ''[[Jendirian Valley]]'s'' journey from [[Coruscant]] to [[Naboo]], to [[22 BBY]].</ref> several freighter trampers, including [[Gondrin Upal]], [[Kaycee Kollenz]], [[Nar Hida]], and a group of [[Donovia]]n [[Rainman|rainmen]], traveled on the AA-9 [[freighter]] ''[[Jendirian Valley]]'' from [[Western spaceport|Westport]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] to the planet [[Naboo]]. [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] also traveled onboard the vessel, disguised as refugee<ref name="BMF" /> freighter trampers<ref name="CVD" /> from the [[Thousand Moons system|Thousand Moon system]].<ref name="BMF" />
