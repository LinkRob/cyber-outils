A '''Force power'''<ref name="lair">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/lair-of-grievous-episode-gallery|text="Lair of Grievous" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> was any ability that could be used by a [[Force-sensitive]] individual using the power of [[the Force]]. These powers varied in use, but had applications in war, healing, utility, amusement, and information-harvesting. Some such abilities, such as enhanced reflexes and minor acts of precognition, could be accessed without conscious application. Others, such as [[telekinesis]] or [[mind trick]]s, required acts of will, though with training could be made to seem effortless. In certain cases, attempting to act consciously could actually hinder one's ability to draw upon the Force. Some abilities could only be accessed through the [[light side of the Force]], while others were only accessed through the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]].

Certain abilities, such as [[Force barrier]]s, were neutral and any power in theory could be used by any trained [[Force-sensitive]]. Aside from specific abilities that usually could only be accessed by the dark side, such as many forms [[Force lightning]], or the light side, such the most common means of becoming a [[Force spirit]], there existed neutral Force powers could be aligned to the light or the dark depending on how they were used, such as mind tricks.

==General abilities==
{{Quote|The ability to destroy a planet is insignificant next to the power of the Force.|Darth Vader regarding the power of the Force|<ref name="Episode IV" />}}
[[File:FlowoftheForce-SWQ20.png|thumb|left|300px|Telekinesis (demonstrated by a [[Twi'lek]] Jedi hurling stones at enemy battle droids) was one of the most common uses of the Force.]]
A wide variety of abilities were open to those who were able to use the power of [[the Force]]. One of the most common abilities that were utilized was [[telekinesis]], the ability to move or otherwise manipulate objects using only an application of the Force.<ref name="heir">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> Often this was used by Jedi and Sith to fling away their enemies or hurl items at them.<ref name="Episode III" /> Similarly, an ability known as [[Force Stasis]] allowed a Force user to slow down or completely freeze an object, person, or even pure energy in place, as demonstrated in [[34 ABY]] by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], who froze a blaster bolt in mid-air during the [[Attack on Tuanul]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> Some Jedi, like [[Cere Junda]], were capable of producing [[Force barrier]]s, such as the one used to protect [[Cal Kestis]] from [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="SWJFO" />

Another well-known ability was the [[mind trick]], a power that could be applied to influence the thoughts and actions of another individual, often to the user's advantage.<ref name="Episode IV" /> Notably, mind tricks could only affect those with weak minds.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

[[File:SWTFA4.png|thumb|right|250px|Rey experienced a Force vision upon finding the Skywalker lightsaber]]
[[Force vision]]s were a powerful and mysterious ability that sometimes occurred without conscious application in [[sleep]]<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> or even while awake,<ref name="Episode VII" /> though such visions were not always reliable. Force users were also able to, both consciously and inherently through their connection to the Force, augment their own natural athleticism, allowing for speed, coordination and acrobatic maneuvers beyond natural limits.<ref name="Episode III" /> These included the ability to run on walls.<ref name="SWJFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

A [[Mind probe (Force power)|mind probe]] was a Force power which allowed the user to search the thoughts of others.<ref name="Episode VII" /><ref name="Episode VI" /> It was possible to resist this ability using the Force; powerful Force users could even turn the effects of the probe around and manage a look into the mind of the user.<ref name="Episode VII" /> Some users of the Force could also use their power to sedate, as [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] demonstrated on [[Jar Jar Binks]], or even to induce [[sleep]] and render a person unconscious, as [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] demonstrated on [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and [[Mace Windu]] demonstrated on [[Rako Hardeen]].<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref><ref name="Episode VII" /><ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref>

[[Force sense]] remained the most basic of Force abilities. It could be used to feel another being's feelings, the future, ripples in the Force caused by momentous or traumatic events, impending danger and the presence of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]. Closely related also to ''force sense'' and ''force sight'' was ''[[precognition]]'', the ability to hear direction and whispered promptings from the Force about what lay ahead in the immediate future. Jedi and other [[Force-sensitive|Force-users]] would use this ability to sense impending danger, allowing the Force to warn them of a threat.<ref name="Episode III" /> Jedi could also sense the location of hidden beings or enemies from a distance, and through barriers, allowing them to find, engage or avoid a being.<ref name="BFII" />

Additionally, by using the Force on oneself, combined with the user's strength of mind and will, a Force user could increase his or her resilience to harm, especially to other Force abilities. This typically allowed both strong and skilled practitioners of the Force to defend against another Force-user's attempt to restrain, throw, repel, mentally influence or evaluate, or otherwise affect or harm them with the Force. Most Force users were, therefore, less able to effectively use the Force on each other and tend to rely on physical attacks to break their opponents guard before attempting to use a telekinetic attack. However, those with sufficient enough skill or power in the Force could more easily overwhelm such defenses with less effort as well as make themselves practically immune to having their protection broken by others, making them extremely dangerous in direct combat.<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:Yoda-heartache.png|thumb|left|250px|Yoda felt the effects of Order 66 in the Force.]]
[[Disturbance in the Force|Disturbances in the Force]] were caused by serious events, such as mass death or the presence of powerful Force users, whose signature in the Force was strong. These disturbances could be quite powerful; when nearly all of the [[Jedi]] were killed by [[Order 66]], Yoda felt physical pain, despite the fact that he was on [[Kashyyyk]], thousands of [[light-year]]s away. More minor occurrences could also cause disturbances in the Force; when [[Darth Sidious]] killed [[Mace Windu]], [[Saesee Tiin]], [[Agen Kolar]], and [[Kit Fisto]], Yoda again felt their deaths. It is possible that Yoda's unusual power allowed him to feel such events while others could not, which would explain why few other Jedi felt the deaths of either group in the way that Yoda did,<ref name="Episode III" /> though Jedi like [[Jaro Tapal]] were among those who did, which may have led such Jedi to escape to safety.<ref name="SWJFO" />

The entrance to the [[Lothal Jedi Temple|Jedi Temple]] on [[Lothal]], as well as its gateway to the [[World Between Worlds]], and the [[holocron]]s used by both [[Jedi]] and [[Sith]], could only be opened via the Force, which protected them from outside intrusion. The painting of [[Force wielder|the Ones]] on Lothal, that granted access to the World Between Worlds, could only be opened with the light side of the Force. The only other way of entering was through a bond with a light side user, as demonstrated by Darth Sidious when he used the [[fiery energy]] generated by a Sith ritual to latch onto [[Ezra Bridger]] and gain temporary access to the World Between Worlds.<ref name="Wolves">{{Rebels|Wolves and a Door}}</ref><ref name="AWBW">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref>

[[File:ZeroHour-BenduStorm.png|thumb|right|300px|The Bendu manifests as a storm]]
Extremely powerful Force users and Force entities had access to a variety of uncommon abilities. For example, the [[Parent|Father]], one of [[Force wielder|the Ones]], was so powerful in the Force that he was able to touch a lightsaber blade without suffering any harm,<ref name="Overlords">{{TCW|Overlords}}</ref> while the [[Son]] was able to possess others.<ref name="sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref> Both the Son and the [[Daughter]] were also able to shapeshift.<ref name="Altar">{{TCW|Altar of Mortis (episode)}}</ref> [[Bendu]], "the one in the middle," who was neither light nor dark, could appear and disappear at will. Bendu could appear as and create massive storms with the Force.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

Strong Force users could [[Teleportation (Force power)|teleport]], like the [[Force wielder]]s, the [[Nightsisters]] or [[Dorwin Corvax]].<ref name="SWJFO" /><ref name="Overlords" /> The latter had the ability to suspend [[time]].<ref name="Vader Immortal">{{VaderImmortal|I}}</ref>

Using the Force for intense applications and prolonged amounts of time could tire out the users leaving them exhausted and wounded, or even when unleashed in grand amounts even kill someone overtaxed.<ref name="Episode VIII" /><ref name="The High Republic: Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

==Light side abilities==
{{Quote|All energy from the Living Force, from all things that have ever lived, feeds into the cosmic Force, binding everything and communicating to us through the midi-chlorians. Because of this, I can speak to you now.|The spirit of Qui-Gon Jinn, communing with Yoda|<ref name="Voices">{{TCW|Voices}}</ref>}}
[[File:ThreeJediVisitors-RotJ40-Prouse.png|thumb|left|250px|The Jedi Anakin Skywalker, Yoda, and Obi-Wan Kenobi retained their consciousness after becoming one with the Force.]]
The ability to retain consciousness as [[Force spirit]] after [[death]] was a rare Force power known only to certain orders, such as the [[Force Priestesses]] and a select few [[Jedi]]. Use of this ability required special training, but one who mastered it could continue to have an influence in the realm of the living even upon the death of their physical body.<ref name="sacrifice" /> This influence extended to Force use, as evidenced by Yoda destroying [[Ahch-To]]'s [[uneti tree]] with lightning.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

[[Yoda]], when fighting [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and Darth Sidious, was able to absorb [[Force lightning]] with his hands, and, in his duel with Dooku, deflect it back toward the [[Sith]].<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref><ref name="Episode III" /> It was also possible to deflect Force lightning with a [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Episode III" />

Several Jedi, like [[Quinlan Vos]] and [[Cal Kestis]], had an ability called [[psychometry]], which allowed the user to sense the events associated with an object or location. This ability could be quite vivid, and could include visuals and sounds. The ability was innate, though practice was required to master the skill.<ref name="SWJFO" />

It was possible for Jedi to [[Taming beasts|tame animals]] with the Force.<ref name="Innocents">{{TCW|Innocents of Ryloth}}</ref>

At least one Jedi, [[Sta-Den Eekin]], was able to use his power to cloud the minds of large crowds.<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

[[Luke Skywalker]] believed that the Force manifested itself in [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]] as her strength of will.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

[[Luke Skywalker]] displayed the ability to use [[Force projection]] across [[the galaxy]], sending a lifelike image of himself from [[Ahch-To]] to [[Crait]], [[light-year]]s away. This projection was so realistic that Kylo Ren could not detect any deception until he tried and failed to strike Skywalker down with [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. However, the strain of creating such a projection proved to be fatal for Skywalker, who was still reconnecting with the Force after previously [[Sever Force|severed]] his connection.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> A similar form of projection, used by [[Yoda]], allowed a Force user to communicate with another who was thousands of light-years away.<ref name="Path">{{Rebels|Path of the Jedi}}</ref>

A number of Force users, including [[Yoda]],<ref name="BFII" /> [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], and [[Ben Solo]], demonstrated the ability to use [[Force healing]], an ability that allowed them to heal injuries and remove poisons<ref name="mando7">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 7: The Reckoning}}</ref> by transferring life force from the healer, which could be recovered by resting or meditating.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref><ref name="The Rise of Skywalker (French junior novel)">[[The Rise of Skywalker (French junior novelization)|''The Rise of Skywalker'' French junior novelization]]</ref> In the rare case of a [[Force dyad]], the power could even resurrect the recently deceased, albeit at the cost of the user's own life. Ben Solo used this ability to resurrect Rey in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Dark side abilities==
{{Quote|The dark side of the Force is a pathway to many abilities some consider to be unnatural.|Sheev Palpatine|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:PalpatineLightning-AaA.png|thumb|right|250px|Force lightning was an advanced dark side Force power.]]
Users of the [[dark side of the Force]], such as the [[Sith]], drew upon intense emotions, especially negative feelings, such as fear, hatred, and anger, as well as thoughts of greater power to make use of abilities not often practiced by those such as the Jedi.

[[Darth]] [[Darth Plagueis|Plagueis]] was a powerful [[Sith Lord]] so skilled in the dark side of the Force that he was able to manipulate [[midi-chlorian]]s to create life itself and to prevent [[death]].<ref name="Episode III" /> Other dark side means of [[Force healing|restoring oneself]] included [[Force drain|stealing vitality from others]]<ref name="Episode IX" /> and using dark potions to heal.<ref name=":0" />

[[Force lightning]] was a powerful dark-side ability in which a Force user emitted pure Force energy, in the form of electricity, from their fingers. This lighting had the ability to cause serious pain, permanent damage, and, if a target was exposed to enough, death.<ref name="Episode III" /><ref name="Episode VI" /> The lightning was so powerful that even the caster of lightning was not immune to it.<ref name="Episode III" /> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was never able to use Force lightning because of his injuries in the [[Duel on Mustafar]]. Force Lighting had the ability to be shaped and used in forms other than just lighting bolts. Fallen Jedi [[Azlin Rell]] was able to use the lighting to swing down the branches of a [[wroshyr tree]] during a fight on [[Kashyyyk]]. During the same fight he molded the Lighting to allow him to knife his hand through a Drengir's body and destroy its core.<ref name="The High Republic: Into the Light">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref> Ordinarily, Force lightning was blue, though other colors were possible; for example, the Son's lightning was red.<ref name="Altar" /> Some lightsiders also used lightning, such as Rey<ref name="Episode IX" /> and Yoda.<ref name=":0">''[[Unlimited Power]]''</ref>

[[File:Force_Choke_SWDA.png|thumb|left|200px|Darth Vader frequently used Force choke to punish those who displeased him.]]
Some Sith, including Palpatine, had an ability called Dark Aura, which was a form of Force lightning that formed a miasma that damaged and slowed enemies. Another dark side [[List of battles|battle]] ability was [[focused rage]], which increased the user's aggression and potency while simultaneously decreasing the damage they sustained.<ref name="BFII">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

The dark side was also the source of both Sith and Nightsister [[magick]]. [[Sith sorcery|Sith magic]] (or [[Sith alchemy]]), which could take the form of [[Fiery energy|blue flames]], was used by Palpatine to access the World Between Worlds and to create illusions.<ref name="World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref> Sith alchemy was also used to reinforce [[Kylo Ren's helmet]] when it was rebuilt by [[Albrekh]].<ref name="SWTRSTVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Unlike other Force techniques, Sith alchemy sometimes required the Sith's blood. Nightsister magick generally took the form of green mist and had many uses. For example, it could temporarily reanimate corpses as [[zombie]]s<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref> and was used by Mother [[Talzin]] to show [[Savage Opress]] that his brother [[Maul]] had survived the duel on [[Naboo]]. The Nightsisters also had the ability to [[Teleportation (Force power)|teleport]] themselves and others.<ref name="SWJFO" /> Additionally, their magick could be used as a shield that could repel blaster bolts, to levitate, and even to grant invisibility.<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref>

Palpatine used the dark side of the Force to [[Transference|transfer]] his consciousness into a clone after he was betrayed by Darth Vader.

The Inquisitor known as the [[Second Sister]] was able to use the Force to [[Force Dash|move at incredibly high speeds]] for short periods of time. Although this is also common for light side users.<ref name="SWJFO" />

[[Snoke]] was able to manipulate the [[Force-bond]] between [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and [[Ben Solo]], in which they could see each other (though not their surroundings) over vast distances and were able to converse.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Matter and objects could sometimes pass between individuals in a [[Force dyad]],<ref name="Episode VIII" /><ref name="Episode IX" /> which Rey deliberately used to pass a lightsaber to Ben Solo during the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Force usage in the Unknown Regions==
{{SeeAlso|Navigator}}
Because of a series of long-ago [[supernova]]e, the [[Unknown Regions]] were extremely difficult to navigate, forcing travelers to conduct [[Jump-by-jump|lengthy series of short jumps]] through [[hyperspace]]. However, a number of beings were able to use the Force power of [[precognition]] to navigate, eliminating the need for "jump-by-jump" travel. Many of these [[Force-sensitive]]s, such as [[Qilori]] of Uandualon, were employed by the [[Navigators' Guild]], and were hired by other entities, such as the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> It was also possible for [[human]]s to navigate with the Force. After receiving encouragement from [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was able to learn the skill and guide the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]'' through hyperspace.<ref name="Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>

The [[Chiss]] also had Force-sensitive [[navigator]]s, whom they referred to as ''[[Sky-walker|ozyly-esehembo]]'', [[Cheunh]] for "sky-walkers."<ref name="Alliances" /> However, this fact was kept secret from non-Chiss, and the Ascendancy often hired outside navigators.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Sky-walkers were almost exclusively female, unlike in most species, where Force powers were evenly distributed. Additionally, and likewise unusually, sky-walkers almost always lost their powers by adulthood, except in [[Vah'nya]]'s case. Sky-walkers could use two powers, called [[Telepathy|Second Sight]] and [[Precognition|Third Sight]]. Second Sight referred to telepathy, while Third Sight referred to precognition. The latter was far more common.<ref name="Alliances" />
