"'''Escape from Darth Vader'''" is a short story originally published in ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]'' on [[December 18]], [[2015]]. It adapts the film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="5-Minute Star Wars Stories" />

The story re-used illustrations previously done by [[Stéphane Roux]] in the [[2014]] book of the [[Escape from Darth Vader (World of Reading)|same name]] by [[Michael Siglain]],<ref name="EDV">[[Escape from Darth Vader (World of Reading)|''Escape from Darth Vader'']]</ref> with new text written by [[Brooke Vitale|Brooke Dworkin]].<ref name="5-Minute Star Wars Stories" /> The text and illustrations were slightly changed when it was republished in ''[[Star Wars Galactic Stories: 7 Stories from a Galaxy Far, Far Away....]]''<ref name="Galactic Stories" />
