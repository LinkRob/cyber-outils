'''Endee''' was an [[R1-series astromech droid]] that was present on the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]]<ref name="Dark Lord 14" /> [[18 BBY|one]] [[Standard year|year]] into the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Dark Lord 13">[[Darth Vader (2017) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 13]]</ref> After [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ferren Barr]] revealed to his compatriots that [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] had [[Battle of Dac City|come]] to Mon Cala, he asked Endee to display files four-three-oh to four-five-nine, which contained information on [[Darth Sidious]], [[Order 66]], Skywalker, [[Maul]], [[Darth Plagueis]], [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]], [[Jar Jar Binks]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], [[Sifo-Dyas]], and [[Kamino]].<ref name="Dark Lord 14" />

After the [[Ninth Sister]] tortured [[Lee-Char]] for the location of Ferren Barr, Lee-Char contacted Barr and notified him that Vader and the [[Inquisitor]]s were coming for him. Barr ordered an evacuation, and ordered Endee to [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destruct]] after Farr and his followers left.<ref name="Dark Lord 16">[[Darth Vader (2017) 16|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 16]]</ref>
