'''Extended lariats''' were a weapon used by [[Mandalorian]]s. During the [[New Republic Era]], [[the Armorer]] packed extended lariats for the war party that [[Rescue of Ragnar Vizsla|rescued]] [[Ragnar Vizsla|Ragnar]]. [[Din Djarin]] and [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] used the lariats to free Ragnar from a [[canyon raptor]].<ref name="Chapter 20" />
