The '''Colicoid Creation Nest''', also known simply as '''Colicoid Creation''' was a manufacturing company operated by the [[Colicoid]]s<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> of [[Colla IV]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> it notably produced [[Droideka]]s,<ref name="Build R2 32" /> [[Pistoeka sabotage droid]]s,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> [[Droid tri-fighter]]s,<ref name="X-Wing 2 Tri" /> and [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident''-class assault ships]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />
