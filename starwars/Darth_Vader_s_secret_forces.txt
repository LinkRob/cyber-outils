In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] assembled a group of '''secret forces''' who would be loyal to him alone in order to investigate two matters: Vader sought to learn the identity of the [[Rebel pilot]] who destroyed the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]] at the [[Battle of Yavin]] and the identity of an individual who was conspiring with [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] behind his back.<ref name="Darth Vader 3" /> For these matters, he hired the [[bounty hunter]]s [[Boba Fett]] and [[Krrsantan]],<ref name="Darth Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2015) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 1]]</ref> but he also believed he needed to assemble a private force of [[troop]]s. For that, Vader recruited the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], who provided the [[assassin droid]]s [[0-0-0]] and [[BT-1]]. Next, the group traveled to the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]] to retrieve a [[portable droid factory]], which Vader used to construct<ref name="Darth Vader 3" /> his own army<ref name="DV 2020 35">[[Darth Vader (2020) 35|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 35]]</ref> of [[BX-series droid commando]]s.<ref name="Darth Vader 3" /> After Krrsantan captured the individual who had met with Palpatine, [[Doctor]] [[Cylo]], and 0-0-0 learned the [[scientist]] was working to create replacements for Vader,<ref name="Darth Vader 4">[[Darth Vader (2015) 4|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 4]]</ref> Vader led his [[commando]] [[droid]]s into the [[skirmish in Cylo's research base]].<ref name="Darth Vader 5" />

After learning from Fett that the [[pilot]] who destroyed the [[Death Star]] was named [[Luke Skywalker]], which Vader realized meant the boy was his own son,<ref name="Darth Vader 6">[[Darth Vader (2015) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 6]]</ref> Vader made it his mission to capture the boy and turn him to the [[dark side of the Force]].<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> Vader, Aphra, 0-0-0, and BT-1 all attempted to capture Skywalker during the [[Battle of Vrogas Vas]], but he escaped.<ref name="Vader Down 2">[[Darth Vader (2015) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 13]]</ref> Vader also deployed his commando droids into the [[Shu-Torun War|War on Shu-Torun]].<ref name="Darth Vader 18">[[Darth Vader (2015) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 18]]</ref> When Palpatine learned the extent of Vader's secret army, he was pleased by how his [[Sith apprentice]] had acted in the shadows as it was the way of the [[Sith]].<ref name="Darth Vader 25" />
