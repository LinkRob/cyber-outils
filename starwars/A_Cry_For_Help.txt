"'''A Cry For Help'''" was a [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|roleplaying adventure]] written by [[Matt Maneely]]. It was due to be one of the adventures in ''[[The Kathol Rift]]'', but was cut since the book content was too long. It would have focused on the character of [[Makezh]], and his acquisition of the [[Codex]]. The retrieval of the Codex was subsequently worked into the adventure ''[[Waystation]]''.
