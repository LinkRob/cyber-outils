'''Ebla beer''', also known simply as '''Ebla''' was an [[alcohol]]ic beverage served at the [[Zero Angle]], a [[Cantina|bar]] located in the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Bright Jewel Oversector]] [[Bright Jewel Oversector Flight Base|Flight Base]] on the [[moon]] [[Axxila III]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Huck Trompo]] and [[Sully Olvar]], two [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilots]] in the [[Imperial Army]]'s [[Nashtah Squadron]] drank Eblas while at the bar. After he stopped the pair from singing to antagonise [[Imperial Navy|Navy]] [[pilot]]s, the [[squadron]]'s [[Captain]] [[Sax Hastur]] ordered Trompo an Olvar another Ebla each from the [[bartender]] [[Florn]].<ref name="LCATZA" /> Ebla beer could also be found on [[Lothal]].<ref name="justice">''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Ebla was created by [[Brian Daley]] for the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' novel ''[[Han Solo's Revenge]]'' released in [[1978]], and subsequently appeared in a large number of other legends stories as [[Ebla beer/Legends|Ebla beer]]. The drink's first appearance in [[canon]] was in "[[Last Call at the Zero Angle]]," a short story written by [[Jason Fry]] and published in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' [[Star Wars Insider 156|156]] in [[2015]].
