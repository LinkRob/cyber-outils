"'''Corellian Smuggler'''" was an article written for [[SHADIS Magazine 27|''SHADIS Magazine'' 27]], by [[Dan Verssen]]. The article was an adventure for the [[West End Games]] [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|''Star Wars'' D6]] system. It creates a singleplayer boardgame with several scenarios.
