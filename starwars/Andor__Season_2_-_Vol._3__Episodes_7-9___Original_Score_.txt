'''''Andor'': Season 2 &ndash; Vol. 3 (Episodes 7-9) [Original Score]''' is the soundtrack for the seventh to nineth episodes of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'': "[[Messenger]]", "[[Who Are You?]]," and "[[Welcome to the Rebellion]]." It was composed by [[Brandon Roberts]] and [[Nicholas Britell]] and released for digital streaming on [[May 9]], [[2025]].<ref name="Vol3" />
