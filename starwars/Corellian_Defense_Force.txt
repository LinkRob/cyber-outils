The '''Corellian Defense Force''' was a [[planet]]ary [[Planetary defense forces|defense force]] that was active within the [[Corellian sector]] of [[the galaxy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The [[Type III "Berethron" Personal Modular Armor]] was manufactured for the [[Corellian]] Defense Force as well as the [[Corellian Security Forces]]. The Corellian Defense Force also utilized the [[TX-3 combat flight suit]] for its [[pilot]]s.<ref name="G&G" />
