The '''Dokora'''<ref name="TAVG" /> were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TC" /> [[Cantu Fallum]] was a member of this species.<ref name="TAVG" />

==Biology and appearance==
The Dokora were an [[Amphibian|aquatic]] bipedal [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] who were distinguished by the dark angular spots that covered much of their body excluding their face.<ref name="The Acolyte" /> They had the capacity to operate both on land and in the [[water]].<ref name="TAVG" />

==Species in the galaxy==
During or shortly prior to [[132 BBY]],<ref name="The Acolyte Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> the Dokora [[Jedi]] [[Cantu Fallum]] negotiated a truce between warring [[Aqualish]] factions on the [[planet]] [[Ando]],<ref name="TAVG" /> accompanying [[Vernestra Rwoh]] and [[Mog Adana]] shortly thereafter during their mission to find [[Sol]] and [[the Stranger]] on the planet [[Brendok]].<ref name="The Acolyte" />

==Behind the scenes==
