The '''Batuuan Harvest Festival'''<ref name="Traveller's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> was a holiday celebrated at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="GEStaff" /> It was held in the [[autumn]] to celebrate the harvest season, especially [[Golden Lichen]]. During the celebration, [[Oga Garra]] allowed the lichen to be used in special dishes and drinks. The locals decorated the outpost with golden colored banners and often accentuated their clothing similarly.<ref name="Traveller's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Batuuan Harvest Festival was created for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at Disneyland Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="GEStaff" /> It was first identified by that name in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', which is presented as an in-universe document [[Traveler's Guide to Batuu|of the same name]].<ref name="Traveller's Guide" />
