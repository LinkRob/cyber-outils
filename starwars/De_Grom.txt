'''De Grom''' was an individual who served as a [[Imperial cadet|cadet]] for the [[Galactic Empire]] at the [[Academy for Young Imperials]] on [[Lothal]]. They seemed to have at least some potential as during a particular training exercise, they were one of three cadets at the top of the ranks, along with [[Jai Kell]] and [[Wheeler]]. The cadets were rewarded with an extra dessert ration and a free period.<ref name="ranks" />

==Behind the scenes==
De Grom first appeared in the [[2015]] junior novel ''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]]. The character's name was inspired by [[Wikipedia:Jacob de Grom|Jacob de Grom]],
a [[Wikipedia:Major League Baseball|Major League]] [[Wikipedia:Baseball|Baseball]] [[Wikipedia:Pitcher|pitcher]] for the [[Wikipedia:New York Mets|New York Mets]].<ref name="mets">{{Tumblr|subdomain=jasonfry|url=post/156721905436/notes-rebel-in-the-ranks-pt-2|name=Jason Fry's Dorkery|text=Notes: Rebel in the Ranks, Pt. 2|quote=Baseball fans will recognize that cadets de Grom and Wheeler share last names with a pair of talented Mets pitchers. What can I say? Beyond being a Mets fan, I was running low on evocative names.}}</ref>
