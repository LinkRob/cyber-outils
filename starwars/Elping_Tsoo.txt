'''Elping Tsoo''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[corporal]]<ref name="Survivors" /> and [[scientific officer]]<ref name="Pathogen" /> in the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] who was stranded inside the [[Nihil]] [[Occlusion Zone]] with RDC [[Lieutenant]] [[Visma Tarko]].<ref name="Survivors" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">According to ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'', the [[Guardian Protocols]] were implemented in [[229 BBY]]. According to [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|''Shadows of Starlight'' 1]], this happened one week after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places in [[230 BBY]]. The events of ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'', including the [[Expansion of the Stormwall]], take place exactly one year after the Fall of [[Starlight Beacon]]. The events of "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Friend or Foe?]]" and "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Survivors]]" take place one month after the Expansion of the [[Stormwall]], so they must be set in [[228 BBY]].</ref> Tsoo and Tarko met [[Joss Adren|Joss]] and [[Pikka Adren|Pikka]] [[Adrens|Adren]].<ref name="Survivors" />
