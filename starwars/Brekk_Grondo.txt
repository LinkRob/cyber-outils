'''Brekk Grondo''' was an individual who became a member of the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]] by<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[3 ABY]]. By the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>  Grondo had lost contact with a [[smuggler]] after they entered the [[Pyke Syndicate]] territory on Akiva while traveling on a [[Bala-bala]] [[speeder]]. After learning that the speeder had crashed they wrote a report to other members of the [[Hutt]] Cartel urging it's members to form a search party and recovering the information the smuggler was carrying before it fell into the wrong hands.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] found a [[datapad]] in the [[Hutt Cartel District (Myrra)|Hutt Cartel District]] in the [[capital]] [[city]] [[Myrra]] which contained Grondo's message about the Bala-bala crash. Vess later retrieved the intel and returned it to the Hutt Cartel.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Brekk Grondo was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. They were mentioned on a datapad as part of an optional intel quest. Returning the information to Hutts is one way the player can increase their reputation with the [[syndicate]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
