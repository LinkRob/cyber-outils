The '''Casino District''', was a district of the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] located on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. The district is the main draw for wealthy tourists featuring various luxury hotels, casinos and other entertainment including the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]]. The [[Canto Bight Police Department]] provided strict security for the district.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The district first appeared in the [[2017]] novella collection [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']] as part of the [[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi]].<ref name="Canto Bight">[[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']]</ref> It was first pictured later in the 2017 {{Film|VIII}}<ref name="VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> film. The district was first identified as the Casino District in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
