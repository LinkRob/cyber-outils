The '''68th Legion''' was a [[legion]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Army]]. By the aftermath of the [[Battle of Yavin]], the 68th Legion was headquartered on the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]] as part of the [[Tatooine garrison]]. It was active under the leadership of [[Captain]] [[Kosh]]. Half of the legion was [[Death|killed]] while attacking the [[Saponza's homestead|base]] of an [[Saponza|agent]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], in response to the destruction of [[Fort Sunfire]].<ref name="Commander" />
