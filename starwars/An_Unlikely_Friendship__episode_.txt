"'''An Unlikely Friendship'''" is the forty-fifth episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com|''Star Wars Kids'']] YouTube channel on [[April 24]], [[2020]]. It partially adapts moments from {{Film|VII}}.

==Plot summary==
The [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn|FN-2187]] enters [[Poe Dameron]]'s cell aboard the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]'' and tells him that he can help him escape. Poe asks why he is helping him, and FN-2187 replies that because it is the right thing to do.

In the ''Finalizer''{{'s}} crowded [[hangar]]. Poe and FN-2187 climb into the cockpit of a two-seater [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]. Poe quips that he has always wanted to fly one of those. They fire up the TIE fighter but find that it is still tethered to the hangar. Following a fire fight with [[First Order]] stormtroopers, they blast their way out of the hangar. While taking out the ''Finalizer''{{'s}} [[laser cannon]]s, Poe asks FN-2187 what his name is. FN-2187 gives his trooper designation but Poe refuses to use that and calls him Finn, a name he happily adopts.

Later, Poe and [[Black Squadron]] blast First Order forces during the [[Battle of Takodana]]. Finn joins the battle and shoots down a pair of stormtroopers. While Poe pursues and shoots down a [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]], Finn cheers as Poe races by.

Later, Finn reunites with Poe and tells Dameron that he completed his [[Attack on Tuanul|mission]] to deliver the [[Map to Skywalker]] to the Resistance. Finn wants to return Poe's jacket to him, but Poe tells him to keep it because it suits him so well. The two friends then stand beside Poe's [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] ''[[Black One]]''.
