'''C1-4B'''<ref name="Toy">{{DisneyToyCite|set=Droid Depot (toy line)|packtype=Wind Up Toy|pack=CH-83|altlink=https://wdwnt.com/2021/10/photos-new-wind-up-toys-apparel-and-droideka-figure-at-droid-depot-in-disneys-hollywood-studios}}</ref> was a [[C1-series astromech droid]]<ref name="Guide" /> manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]]<ref name="Stats" /> and owned by the [[Droid Depot]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. During the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="GEDD" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> C1-4B was displayed on a shelf of [[droid]] parts within the depot. The [[C-series]] unit had [[Color|red]], gray and blue paneling and a black [[sensor]]. On the left side of its dome the droid bore the logo<ref name="GEDD" /> of the [[Imperial Press Corps]].<ref name="Logo">{{HoloNetNewsTumblr|post/98160256153/holonet-news-special-report-journalists-across|Special Report: Journalists Across Galaxy Join Press Corps|int=HoloNet News Special Report: Journalists Across Galaxy Join Press Corps}}</ref> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Trav Date">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> the author [[Eloc Throno]] included an image of C1-4B in the [[Droid]] Buyer's Guide section of his [[book]], ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''.<ref name="Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
C1-4B appeared as a prop within the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GEDD" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref>
