'''Battle Station Operations''' ('''BSO''') was a separate entity from the [[Imperial Army]] and [[Imperial Navy]] that was stationed aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]]. In order to effectively and efficiently maintain and operate the [[Death Star]], it had eight major departments: [[engineering]], [[Logistics (Battle Station Operations)|logistics]], [[maintenance]], [[medical]], [[Personnel (Battle Station Operations)|personnel]], [[Security (Battle Station Operations)|security]], [[station defenses]], [[technical support]], followed by various other sub-branches. [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] was the supreme commander of the Death Star's BSO personnel, and all command decisions regarding the battle station ultimately went through him.<ref name="Dawn" />
