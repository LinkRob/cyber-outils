The '''Barter Station''' was an open market area found within the [[Mos Espa Podrace]] battlefield of the [[Grand Arena]], utilized by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. It could be found toward the center of the arena, and was identified by the large sign displayed over one of the entryways. This signage was white, brown, and red, with the large words "Barter Station" in Aurebesh printed alongside a variety of small glyphs underneath and an illustration of arrows surrounding a [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] symbol to the right.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Barter Station first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It was added to the game via the inclusion of the Mos Espa Podrace map.<ref name="Hunters" />
