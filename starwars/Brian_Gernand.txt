'''Brian Gernand''' was a model maker and model-shop supervisor at [[Lucasfilm]] He worked on {{Film|I}}<ref name="SW I" />, {{Film|II}}, and {{Film|III}}.<ref name="SWI 92" /> Gernand also portrayed an extra at the scene at the [[Galaxies Opera House]] which he nicknamed "Eggplant Parmesan".<ref name="Hyp">{{Hyperspace|url=hyperspace/member/postnotes/news20041124.html|series=Episode III Post Notes|text=Part of the Coruscant Elite}}</ref> He was a celebrity guest at [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] ''[[Star Wars Weekends]]'' in [[2005]].<ref name="dswwfacebook">{{Facebook|username=Disneys-Star-Wars-Weekends|url=pages/Disneys-Star-Wars-Weekends/105058056197470?v=info|name=Disney's Star Wars Weekends|date=2012-12-17|archiveurl=https://archive.is/y0GQ}}</ref>
