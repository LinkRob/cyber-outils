The '''E-wing starfighter''', also known as the '''E-wing escort fighter''' or simply the '''E-wing''', was an advanced combat [[starfighter]] utilized by the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] during the [[Standard year|years]] of the [[New Republic Era]].

==Characteristics==
The E-wing escort fighter<ref name="TEB">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Embersteel Blade}}</ref> was an advanced combat [[starfighter]] manufactured by [[FreiTek Incorporated]]<ref name="Comprehensive" /> for the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] in the [[Standard year|years]] following the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Battle of Jakku|fall]].<ref name="E-wing DB">{{Databank|new-republic-e-wings|New Republic E-Wings}}</ref> The E-wing had a crew capacity of one [[pilot]] and an [[astromech droid]].<ref name="Ahsoka P1" /> It was equipped with a Class 2 [[hyperdrive]].<ref name="SecondEdition">[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles'' (second edition)]]</ref>

==History==
During the [[New Republic Era]], E-wings were strategically deployed by the [[New Republic]] across its bases in order to bolster its established fleets. A [[squadron]] was stationed<ref name="Comprehensive" /> to protect the skies of<ref name="225 Launch">{{InsiderCite|225|Launchpad}}</ref> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="SW:T">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Lothal]].<ref name="225 Launch" /> [[Governor]] [[Ryder Azadi]] used the ships to complete patrols around [[Capital City]], ensuring protection of its citizens.<ref name="Comprehensive" />
[[File:Ewing-MaA.png|thumb|300px|left|E-wing starfighters were stationed on Lothal.]]
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Ahsoka series date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> [[Captain]] [[Porter (New Republic)|Porter]] and [[Lieutenant]] [[Callahan]] of the [[New Republic Defense Fleet Starfighter Corps]] flew E-wings with astromech [[droid]]s while pursuing the [[Mandalorian]] [[Sabine Wren]], who was riding a [[speeder bike]] on Lothal.<ref name="Ahsoka P1" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="SW:T" /> [[Baron]] [[Paw Maccon]] owned an E-wing as part of his [[Paw Maccon's collection|collection]], located inside his [[Great Vaults of Cato Neimoidia|palace]] on<ref name="PD23">[[Poe Dameron 23|''Poe Dameron'' 23]]</ref> the [[Colonies]] planet<ref name="SW:T" /> [[Cato Neimoidia]]. During a [[Cold War]] [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Resistance)|mission]] against the [[First Order]], the [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] witnessed the E-wing with Maccon while needing to borrow a [[starship]] from him.<ref name="PD23" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Canonical debut===
{{Quote|A FreiTek E-wing escort fighter?|Zander Freemaker, excited when seeing an E-wing|<ref name="TEB" />}}
[[File:E-wing-TheEmbersteelBlade.png|thumb|right|250px|The E-wing first appeared in the current canon in ''LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures''.]]
A starfighter designated as the "E-wing escort fighter" first appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' continuity in the non-[[canon]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' television series' [[2017]] episode "[[The Embersteel Blade]]," which was directed by [[Michael Hegner]] and [[Jeppe Sandholt]]. It depicts the character [[Zander Freemaker]] being excited when seeing it on sale among other starships at an [[auction on Mygeeto]]<ref name="TEB" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Freemaker date">{{FreemakersDate}}</ref> The episode also established that the starfighter was manufactured by [[FreiTek Incorporated]].<ref name="TEB" />

The E-wing made its canonical debut in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Poe Dameron 23|''Poe Dameron'' 23]], which was written by [[Charles Soule]], penciled by [[Angel Unzueta]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="PD23" /> on [[January 24]], [[2018]].<ref name="PD23 date">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/65929/poe_dameron_2016_23|text=Poe Dameron (2016) #23}}</ref> The starfighter debuted in live action in the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'' television series episode "[[Part One: Master and Apprentice]]," which was directed by [[Dave Filoni]].<ref name="Ahsoka P1" /> The canonical name "E-wing" was first identified in the respective entry of the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]],<ref name="E-wing DB" /> and the accompanying [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set "New Republic E-Wing vs. Shin Hati's Starfighter" includes a buildable E-wing starfighter.<ref name="LEGO">{{LEGOCite|num=75364|name=New Republic E-Wing vs. Shin Hati's Starfighter}}</ref>

===Pre-canon history===
The [[E-7 E-wing multi-role starfighter|E-wing escort starfighter]] first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the comic [[Dark Empire 3|''Dark Empire'' 3]], which was written by [[Tom Veitch]], illustrated by [[Cam Kennedy]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="DE3">[[Dark Empire 3|''Dark Empire'' 3]]</ref> on [[April 21]], [[1992]].<ref name="DE3 date">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54059/star_wars_dark_empire_1991_3|text=Star Wars: Dark Empire (1991) #3}}</ref> Future ''[[Star Wars]]'' concept artist [[Jake Lunt Davies]] also created an "E-wing" with his brother during his childhood, during which time he did not have any larger toys so instead made starships out of cardboard. Davies later reflected on his E-Wing, along with other designs he had made with his brother, as part of his ''Star Wars'' journey.<ref name="Lunt Davies 224">{{InsiderCite|224|Jake Lunt Davies' Star Wars Sketchbook|Jake Lunt Davies' ''Star Wars'' Sketchbook}}</ref>
