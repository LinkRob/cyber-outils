'''Battle Station Command''' ('''BSC'''), or '''Battlestation Command''', was the command agency of [[Battle Station Operations]] and was responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operations of the construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], and to ensure that operations were effective and efficient. [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] was to be the commander of the Death Star's Battle Station Operations' personnel. All command decisions regarding the Death Star would ultimately pass through him.<ref name="Dawn" />
