The '''Bounty Hunter Code''', also known as the '''Bounty Hunters' Code''',<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> the '''code of the Guild''', or the '''Guild Code''', was a set of rules that governed the conduct and behavior of [[bounty hunter]]s under the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]].<ref name="Chapter 3">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 3: The Sin}}</ref>

==Code==
{{Quote|Members of the guild are expected to live by a code, which is intended to keep them from poaching another hunter's bounty, killing another guild member in good standing, or taking a personal interest in their targets once the bounty has been paid. In theory, it is a good system to help regulate some very bad people. However, not every bounty hunter belongs to the guild. And even of those who do, not all adhere strictly to the code.|Hondo Ohnaka|<ref name="TSotBH">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>}}
The Code forbade a [[bounty hunter]] of the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] to slay another guild [[bounty]] [[hunter]]<ref name="Boba Age of Rebellion">[[Age of Rebellion - Boba Fett 1|''Age of Rebellion - Boba Fett'' 1]]</ref> who was in good standing<ref name="TSotBH" /> or steal another hunter's bounty.<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> It also forbade hunters from asking about their bounties once delivered, requiring that the events that transpired from accepting the bounty to its delivery to be immediately forgotten.<ref name="Chapter 3" />

==History==
{{Quote|What are your plans for it?''"<br />"''How uncharacteristic of one of your reputation. You have taken both commission and payment. Is it not the code of the Guild that these events are now forgotten?|Djarin questions the Client regarding his plans for the Child|audio=DjarinClient-Child-TMS1C3.flac|<ref name="Chapter 3" />}}
The [[Trandoshan]] bounty hunter [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] was displeased when two [[Unidentified Ranat bounty hunter|Ranat]] and [[Unidentified Nimbanel bounty hunter|Nimbanel]] bounty hunters that he encountered on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Lothal]] failed to honor the Code and attempted to collect an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] bounty on his head.<ref name="Ezra's Gamble">''[[Ezra's Gamble]]''</ref> [[Beilert Valance]] was well known for flouting the Code,<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> whilst Bossk believed in upholding the Code at any cost.<ref name="Aphra Audiobook">''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]''</ref>

in [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Mando Date">{{MandoDate|3}}</ref> the [[Mandalorian]] bounty hunter [[Din Djarin]] broke the code by rescuing [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] from [[the Client]] after he had retrieved him earlier for a bounty. As a result, the [[Nevarro Hunters]] attacked him, trying to get the bounty back.<ref name="Chapter 3" />
