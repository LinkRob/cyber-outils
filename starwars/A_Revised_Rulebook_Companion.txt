"'''A Revised Rulebook Companion'''" is an article written by [[J.D. Wiker]]. It highlights contained guidelines for converting [[Roleplaying game]] characters and equipment from the rules provided in [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game Core Rulebook]] to the updated rules provided by the [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game Revised Core Rulebook]].
