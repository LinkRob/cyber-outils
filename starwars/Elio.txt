'''Elio''' was a [[Jedi Master]] who served as a member of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="MA" /> Around [[68 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the start of [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]'s [[Jedi training|training]] as [[Dooku]]'s [[Padawan]] to [[68 BBY]]. As ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' establishes that the [[Battle on Shurrupak|battle]] on [[Shurrupak]] occurs four [[Standard month|months]] after Jinn's apprenticeship commenced, the conflict must have taken place around that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the Master was sent to the [[Planet|world]] [[Shurrupak]] to assist in a [[Battle on Shurrupak|battle]] and traveled there in a [[shuttle]] alongside fellow [[Jedi]] Master [[Dooku]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]. The [[Starship|ship]] came under heavy fire while in transit, leading Elio to remark that, despite the turbulence, the world's [[Primus Base]] establishment and its landing site were within a five-[[Standard minute|minute]] flight of the ship's location. Upon landing on Shurrupak's [[beach]], Elio hurriedly left the shuttle with the other Jedi and entered Primus Base through a gate.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Elio appeared in a flashback in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
