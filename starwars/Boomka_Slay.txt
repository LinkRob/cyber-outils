'''Boomka Slay''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Force-sensitive]] individual. According to a ''[[Galactic Enquire]]'' publication<ref name="Launchpad" /> released by [[1 BBY]],<ref name="date">[[Ponda Baba]] loses an [[Limb|arm]] in the [[Secret mission to Tatooine]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]]. As "The Light Side" in the [[Launchpad]] section of [[Star Wars Insider 217|''Star Wars Insider'' 217]] depicts Baba with two arms, the events in the comic must have occurred by that [[standard year]].</ref> Slay was rumored to be the [[Reproduction|son]] of "[[Saelt-Marae|Yak Face]]," a [[Yarkora]]n. The publication was read by the [[Aqualish]] [[Ponda Baba]], who noted this to his companion, the [[Dressellian]] [[Orrimaarko]]. The Dressellian was surprised by this, and Baba noted the lack of resemblance. Orrimaarko clarified he was enquiring if this was "canon" and added, in exasperation, the exclamation "man." Baba misunderstood these statements and pointed to [[Canon-Man]], who was standing nearby.<ref name="Launchpad" />

==Behind the scenes==
Boomka Slay was mentioned in the [[2023]] [[Canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic story]] "The Light Side,"<ref name="Launchpad" /> published on [[March 14]]<ref name="InsiderDate">{{TitanComics|url=m/125-star-wars-insider-217|text=Star Wars Insider #217}}</ref> within the [[Launchpad]] section of [[Star Wars Insider 217|''Star Wars Insider'' 217]]. [[Jamie Cosley]] wrote and illustrated the comic.<ref name="Launchpad" />
