The '''fight on a barren moon'''<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|214|Decoding Darth Vader}}</ref> was a [[lightsaber duel]] fought between [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on a [[Unidentified barren moon|barren moon]],<ref name="Part VI" /> taking place in the moon's [[Unidentified barren moon's dry lands|dry lands]] and a [[labyrinth of rock pinnacles]]<ref name="Sideshow">{{IronStudiosCite|set=Art Scale Battle Diorama Series|packtype=1:10 Scale Statue|pack=Darth Vader|link=collectibles/star-wars-darth-vader-iron-studios-912315}}</ref> during the [[Great Jedi Purge]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|I have to face him, Master. Whether he dies or I do, this ends today.|Obi-Wan Kenobi, attempting to speak to his deceased master, Qui-Gon Jinn before the fight|<ref name="Part VI" />}}
The Jedi Master had lured the [[Sith Lord]] to the moon in a bid to give the [[Hidden Path]] time to escape. Kenobi confronted Vader not far from the Dark Lord's landing site. His former apprentice asked if he was ready to finish their [[Duel on Mustafar|battle]] to which Kenobi ignited his lightsaber and reaffirmed his resolve, assuming a [[Form III|classic defensive stance]]. The Sith Lord replied with murderous intent before igniting his own saber and striking the first blow.<ref name="Part VI" />

==The Duel==
{{Quote|Your strength has returned. But the weakness still remains. [...] And that is why you will always lose.|Darth Vader to Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Part VI" />}}
[[File:Acme-OvercomingThePast.png|thumb|left|400px|Kenobi and Vader's blades clash]]
Both Jedi and Sith initially fought evenly against each other with Vader's strikes, slashes, lunges, and ripostes met by Kenobi's blocks, parries, dodges, and counterstrikes. Kenobi soon sought to end the duel by landing two strong, sudden blows on Vader's blade to knock the Dark Lord off-balance and [[Telekinesis|telekinetically]] dislodge a nearby pillar on top of him but Vader caught it with his own abilities. The Sith Lord acknowledged that his former master had lost none of his power but quickly boasted he was still no match for him. Vader swiftly proceeded to gain the upper hand, pummeling Kenobi with lightsaber strikes, force-guided boulders, and his own armored fist before using his power to collapse the ground beneath him and bury him alive by collapsing the stone columns around them. Declaring his victory, the Sith Lord turned and left for his shuttle.<ref name="Part VI" />

Kenobi had survived but was slowly being crushed by the combined weight of the rubble and his own guilt. However, thoughts of his [[Luke Skywalker|young]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|charges]] allowed him to put his feelings aside and find the strength he needed to blast himself free before setting off to re-engage the Dark Lord. He caught Vader before he reached his shuttle, attempting to cut him down from behind only to meet the Sith Lord's blade instead.<ref name="Part VI" />

Abandoning his customary defense, Kenobi launched a furious lightsaber assault that drove Vader back. The Dark Lord attempted to catch Kenobi's blade with telekinesis but the Jedi Master quickly broke his hold and retaliated by [[Force push|pushing]] him into a distant pillar. Taking advantage of his momentarily dazed opponent, Kenobi used his powers to lift the rocky debris around them and hurl it at Vader in a massive telekinetic onslaught. Vader frantically attempted to retake the offensive but Kenobi soon recovered and renewed his attack.<ref name="Part VI" />

The two former brothers-in-arms traded saber blows and grapples before Kenobi used his lightsaber pommel to smash Vader's chest control panel unit, heavily damaging his [[Darth Vader's armor|armor]]'s life support system. The Sith Lord managed to block a killing strike from the Jedi Master but Kenobi hit him from behind with a piece of force-propelled debris. Desperate, Vader made a overhead strike that Kenobi dodged and immediately countered with a slash that caught the Dark Lord in the back, driving him to his knees. Forcing himself back to his feet, Vader once again struck at Kenobi only to blocked and telekinetically pushed back against a pile of debris. Kenobi then launched himself at the Sith Lord in a running leaping slash, avoiding Vader's counterstrike and slicing open his [[helmet]].<ref name="Part VI" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Anakin.''"<br />"''Anakin is gone. I am what remains.''"<br />"''I'm sorry. I'm sorry, Anakin. For all of it.''"<br />"''I am not your failure, Obi-Wan. You didn't kill Anakin Skywalker. I did. The same way I will destroy you!''"<br />"''Then my friend is truly dead. Goodbye, Darth.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Darth Vader|<ref name="Part VI" />}}
At first, Kenobi moved back and assumed a ready stance but stood down as Vader visibly struggled. The sight of the Sith Lord's scarred face stunned Kenobi into calling him by his old name; he attempted to apologize to his former apprentice for everything that had happened between them. Vader responded by proclaiming he had fully embraced his new identity and reaffirmed his hatred of Kenobi. Upon hearing this, the Jedi Master finally let go of his guilt and departed with the belief that his friend was truly gone, ignoring the Dark Lord's impotent screams of rage.<ref name="Part VI" /> He would later describe Vader as "[[Dark side of the Force|twisted]]" and "[[Cyborg|more machine than man]]" to his new [[Padawan]]: Anakin Skywalker's son, [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The fight on a barren moon first appeared in the [[Part VI|sixth episode]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' television series,<ref name="Part VI" /> which aired on [[June 22]], [[2022]].<ref name="Air Date">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-june-2022|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in June 2022}}</ref> The scenes on the moon were filmed in<ref name="Return">''[[Obi-Wan Kenobi: A Jedi's Return]]''</ref> the virtual LED set<ref name="17 year">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/tv/obi-wan-kenobi-ewan-mcgregor-hayden-christensen-cover-story|text=Inside the 17-year journey to reunite Ewan McGregor and Hayden Christensen for ''Obi-Wan Kenobi''|author=Ross, Dalton|date=2022-03-10|archivedate=20220310151626}}</ref> known as the Volume. [[Ewan McGregor]], the actor who played Kenobi, was suspended with wires when his character sliced off his mask. Vader was attached to wires for when he was [[Force push]]ed by Kenobi.<ref name="Return" />

According to [[Stuart Beattie]], writer for ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'', the idea that Kenobi could confront Vader was based on the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. There is dialogue where [[Luke Skywalker]] tells Vader "I know there is good in you," who responds "Obi-Wan once thought as you do." ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'' [[Retroactive continuity|retroactively]] had this dialogue reference the fight on a barren moon, where Kenobi thinks there is still good in him.<ref name="Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://thedirect.com/article/obi-wan-kenobi-darth-vader-final-battle-exclusive|text=Darth Vader Originally Won Obi-Wan Kenobi's Final Battle (Exclusive)|author=Johnson, Nathan|date=2022-07-06|archivedate=20220706175516}}</ref>
