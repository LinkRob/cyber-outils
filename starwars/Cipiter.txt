'''Cipiter''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Chev]], who was a [[Slavery/Legends|slave]] to his [[Chevin/Legends|Chevin]] master [[Nunk Plaarvin]], and a member of the [[Raff Syndicate]]. He organized auctions on [[Metellos 3]] for his master, when [[Marn Hierogryph]], under alias of "Professor Gryphomarn", tried to run a [[Con artist/Legends|con]] there, using [[Jarael]] and [[Rohlan Dyre]] as his accomplices. Plaarvin, however, had cracked the con and had [[Jarael]] and Rohlan captured and prepared for [[Death/Legends|execution]]. Cipiter expressed his deep regret before Jarael, but admitted that as Chev slave he could not disobey his master. Later that day Marn Hierogryph and his accomplice [[Zayne Carrick]] attempted another con&mdash;this time in an attempt to save their friends. Disguising [[Slyssk]] as a [[Raff Syndicate]] representative, they fooled Plaarvin into thinking that the Syndicate knows about him hiding part of his profits from them. After that they send him to a "rendezvous" with Syndycate on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Jebble]], which, unknown to Plaarvin, was [[Disaster on Jebble|devastated]] several months ago. Pointing to Cipiter and other Chevs that they were now free, Hierogryph suggested them to keep running operations for Syndicate, to which they agreed.
