The '''Cron Drift''' or '''Cron Belt''' was a dense [[asteroid]] field in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Auril sector]], near the [[Perlemian Trade Route]], the [[Salin Corridor]] and the [[Tion Cluster]], that contained the rubble of dozens [[star system]]s. It was estimated to have occurred thousands of years before the [[Imperial Era]]. Once a [[nebula]] where [[star]]s were born, the region became a dangerous [[hyperspace]] hazard filled with gravitational anomalies, asteroids, and [[black hole]].<ref name="NoP" />

==History==
After the [[Umbaran]] [[Sly Moore]] was kidnapped from her [[homeworld]] [[Umbara]] by the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]], she encountered the spirits of long-dead [[Sith Lord]]s in an ancient tomb in the Cron Drift and was spiritually attacked by them.<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|subset=Sith Information Guide|cardname=Sly Moore|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/114595/cid/8022698/2015-Topps-Chrome-Star-Wars-Perspectives-Jedi-vs.-Sith-44-S-Sly-Moore}}</ref>

At some point, the [[Imperial Navy]] restricted the traffic to the Cron Drift.<ref name="NoP" /> The [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] once planned [[shaak]]-breeding scam in the Drift. [[Smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] were once involved in a scam with the criminal [[Big Bunji]] running counterfeit [[Authority cash voucher]]s to the poor people on the far side of the Drift. In the logbook ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', Solo noted that the [[Mining Guild]] had many [[TIE/mg Mining Guild starfighter|Guild TIE fighters]] stationed in the drift, but expressed confidence that he and Chewbacca could use the [[quad laser cannon]]s on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to destroy them.<ref name="SG">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Cron Drift was first [[canon]]ically mentioned in the [[Fantasy Flight Games]] roleplaying manual ''[[Nexus of Power]]''.<ref name="NoP" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the Drift first appeared in the novel ''[[Han Solo and the Lost Legacy]]''<ref name="DA">''[[Han Solo and the Lost Legacy]]''</ref> and was an asteroid field in the [[Auril sector/Legends|Auril sector]] created by the [[Cron Supernova/Legends|Cron Supernova]].<ref name="TOTJ TSW 4">[[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 4|''Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War'' 4]]</ref>
