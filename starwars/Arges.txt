{{Quote|The one-eyed, high-leaping arges frog makes its home in the deserts and forests of several planets, although Arges is believed to be its planet of origin.|From the journal of Ardis San Tekka|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
'''Arges''' was a [[planet]] that was believed to be the [[homeworld]] of the [[arges frog]], a type of [[frog]] that had come to inhabit the [[forest]]s and [[desert]]s of several other worlds, including [[Nevarro]], [[Ossus]], and [[Arvala-7]], by the [[New Republic Era]]. [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] [[Director of Creature Cartography]] [[Ardis San Tekka]] mentioned Arges when writing about arges frogs in her [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]]<ref name="Bestiary" /> at some point after the [[Battle of Exegol]] in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BestiaryVol1Date}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The planet Arges was mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[S.T. Bende]]<ref name="Bestiary" /> and released on [[November 5]], [[2024]].<ref name="Edelweiss">{{Insight|url=products/star-wars-bestiary-vol-1|text=Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1}}</ref> Prior to the book's release, the page mentioning Arges was first revealed in a [[StarWars.com]] article published on [[October 22]], 2024.<ref name="Blog">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-bestiary-art-reveal|text=First Look Inside ''The Star Wars Bestiary, Volume 1: Creatures of the Galaxy''}}</ref>
