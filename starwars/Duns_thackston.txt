'''Dun thackstons''' were a [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[species]] of [[bird]]s that could be found in the [[swamp]]s of [[Vodran]]. Bearing a symbiotic relationship with [[sarlacc]]s, they built their nests out of sticks and mud over sarlacc pits so that wandering animals mistook the hole for solid ground and fell into it. The birds then picked clean the [[bone]]s of whatever the sarlaccs did not finish. Duns thackstons could be identified by the three dark [[Color|purple]] spots on their chests.<ref name="Join the Resistance 1" />
