"'''A Real-Life ''Star Wars'' Hero: Shawn Valdez'''" is an article appearing in [[Star Wars Insider 34|''Star Wars Insider'' 34]], written by [[Stephen J. Sansweet]]. The article describes how ''[[Star Wars]]'' character [[Shawn Valdez]] is named in honor of a young boy who died of leukemia.
