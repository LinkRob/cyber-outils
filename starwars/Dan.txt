'''Dan''' was a [[human]] who worked as an [[Imperial officer|officer]] for the [[Galactic Empire]], and was a friend of their fellow [[officer]] [[Jeff]].<ref name="Phineas" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{PnFDate|1}}</ref> Dan walked in on Jeff with a cup of [[Caf|coffee]] and proceeded to make fun of<ref name="Phineas" /> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]]<ref name="Sith Lord">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Darth Vader - Sith Lord (ROTS)}}</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The officer followed up by pretending to be [[Force choke]]d, spilling coffee all over the place. Jeff quickly got worried which made Dan burst into laughter, tricking the officer. Dan had [[Color|light]] skin, brown [[hair]], and dark brown [[eye]]s. The [[officer]] also wore a standard issued [[Imperial officer's uniform|uniform]], along with a pair of [[glasses]].<ref name="Phineas" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dan appeared in the crossover special ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'',<ref name="Phineas" /> which premiered on [[July 26]], [[2014]].<ref name="Reporter">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/live-feed/phineas-ferbs-star-wars-special-711433|text='Phineas and Ferb's' 'Star Wars' Special Lands Premiere Date|author=Ng, Philiana|date=2014-06-12|work=The Hollywood Reporter|archivedate=20201205100409}}</ref> The officer had facial similarities to [[Dan Povenmire]], the creator of the [[Disney Channel]] animated series [[W:c:PhineasAndFerb:Phineas and Ferb|''Phineas and Ferb'']],<ref name="USA Today">{{WebCite|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/tv/2014/07/23/phineas-and-ferb-star-wars-special/13033037/|text=High jinks strike back in 'Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars'|author=Brian Truitt|date=2014-07-23|work=USA Today|archivedate=20230805163521}}</ref> who also provided the voice for the character.<ref name="Phineas" />
