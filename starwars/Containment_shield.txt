A '''containment shield''' was a device with the ability block off access to an area and isolate it with a [[Force field|field]]. The device could be used on [[starship]]s to cut off a room from the rest of the ship. By around [[379 BBY]], a [[containment field]] was installed on the ship known as the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' and was used by [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the ship's owner&mdash;to hold a [[Shrikarai|beast]] known as the [[Great Leveler]] in the ship's [[Unidentified medical observation room|medical observation room]]. Around that [[Standard year|year]], she deactivated the field to let the Leveler [[Diet|feed]] on a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi]]. Ro later recorded a [[Marda Ro's journal|message]] for her [[Ro family|descendants]] in which she mentioned the containment shield. By [[252 BBY]], her [[Gender|great-great-grandson]] [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to the message mentioning the containment shield.

==Description==
Containment shields were devices that had the ability to generate a [[Force field|field]] that acted as a barrier between areas and blocked off access to the area on the other side of the field. It was possible to use containment shields on [[starship]]s, where they were used to isolate a room from the rest of the ship.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

==History==
{{Quote|Rox dropped the Jedi beside the containment shield, and I turned it off.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
[[File:GazeElectricpaths.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The ''Gaze Electric'' (pictured) was equipped with a containment shield.]]
By around [[379 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" establishes that the section depicting the [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi's]] [[death]] aboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' takes place when [[Marda Ro]] is roughly eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old. ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of to [[382 BBY]], establishes both that members of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] receive full membership at the age of fifteen and that Ro had recently become a full member, meaning that she is around fifteen years old. Therefore, the section of "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" depicting the [[Jedi]]'s death must take place around [[379 BBY]].</ref> a [[containment field]] was installed on the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', a ship belonging to [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the [[Evereni]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> founder of a group of [[pirate]]s who would eventually become the [[Nihil]].<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref> She<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> used the containment field to cage the [[Great Leveler]]&mdash;a [[Shrikarai|monster]] that [[Hunting|preyed]] on the [[Living Force]]&mdash;in the ''Gaze Electric''{{'s}} [[Unidentified medical observation room|medical observation room]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Ro assaulted a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi]] and brought him aboard the ''Gaze''. Her subordinate [[Rox]] dropped the [[Jedi]] next to the containment shield behind which the Leveler lived. Ro then deactivated the shield to allow the Leveler to [[Diet|feed]] on the Jedi and ushered it back into the room once it had turned the Jedi's body into a [[stone]]-like husk. Later, Ro mentioned the shield in a [[Marda Ro's journal|message]] she recorded for her [[Ro family|descendants]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during his youth, Ro's [[Gender|great-great-grandson]] [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to her journal, including the entry where the containment shield was mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Behind the scenes==
A containment shield appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in a flashback in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> The [[Containment shield/Legends|technology]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the short story "[[Of Possible Futures: The Tale of Zuckuss and 4-LOM]]," written by [[M. Shayne Bell]] and published in the [[1996]] short story anthology ''[[Tales of the Bounty Hunters]]''.<ref name="OPF">{{StoryCite|story=Of Possible Futures: The Tale of Zuckuss and 4-LOM|book=Tales of the Bounty Hunters}}</ref>
