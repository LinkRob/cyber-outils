The '''Alnaria system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]. It was located in grid square J-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was where<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=the [[Alnarian Collective]]|system=the Alnaria system}}</ref> the [[Alnarian Collective]] originated.<ref name="Origin">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref>
