The '''Clone Underground''' was an underground resistance network that fought the newly [[Proclamation of the New Order|formed]] [[Galactic Empire]] in the name of the [[Clone Wars|fallen]] [[Galactic Republic]], with their main goal being to free and shelter any disillusioned [[clone trooper]]s that wished to defect from its ranks. Established by former [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] in [[19 BBY]], the Clone Underground operated out of the [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] of [[Teth]]. Rex and [[Echo]], joined by many other disillusioned [[Cloning|clones]], began to strike against the Empire with their companions in order to free imprisoned clones and conduct other anti-Imperial operations.

In [[18 BBY]], after having their base on Teth discovered by an Imperial [[clone X trooper]], the Clone Underground was largely annihilated in a subsequent [[Battle on the B'omarr Order Monastery|assault]] by [[Recovery Strike Team|Imperial forces]]. Rex and his few surviving companions fled the planet after pleading with [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]], who had led the Imperial assault, to which he reluctantly allowed. 

==Description==
===Organization===
{{Quote|Rex and I have built up a small network of clones. Some are in hiding, underground. Others act as our contacts within the Empire.|Echo, to Hunter|<ref name="Tipping Point" />}}
In [[19 BBY]], following the execution of [[Order 66]],<ref name="Date" /> the Clone Underground<ref name="Dawn Visual" /> was established by [[Rex]] as a resistance network.<ref name="Rex Topps Finest 2022" /> The goals of the Clone Underground included helping disillusioned clone troopers escape the [[Imperial Military]]<ref name="Clone Conspiracy">{{TBB|The Clone Conspiracy}}</ref> and fighting the Empire in the name of the fallen [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Battle">{{TBB|Battle Scars}}</ref> By 18 BBY,<ref name="207 Date">{{TBBDate|207}}</ref> the network had several clone contacts within the Empire working as informants, such as [[Slip (clone trooper)|Slip]] until his [[death]]. The group also had a sympathetic [[senator]] in the [[Imperial Senate]] itself, [[Riyo Chuchi]].<ref name="Clone Conspiracy" /> The network managed to rescue multiple clones throughout its history, including [[Gregor]], [[Fireball (clone trooper)|Fireball]], [[Nemec]], [[Captain]] [[Howzer]], and two other clone troopers from [[Howzer's squad|his squad]].<ref name="Tipping Point">{{TBB|Tipping Point}}</ref> Additionally, [[Clone Force 99]] member and former [[Advanced Recon Commando]], [[Echo]], joined the network following the passing of the [[Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill]].<ref name="Truth and Consequences" /> Rex maintained contact with and was occasionally assisted by the rest of Clone Force 99, which included [[Hunter]], [[Omega]], [[Wrecker]], [[Tech]], and [[Crosshair]].<ref name="Infiltration" />

The network possessed a ship known as the ''[[Remora]]'' that was equipped with a detachable [[leech vessel]], as well as several captured [[Rho-class transport shuttle|''Rho''-class transport shuttles]]. The Clone Underground helped rescue clone troopers<ref name="Tipping Point" /> as per their bond as brothers and conducted other actions that harried the spread of the fledgling [[Galactic Empire]]. The network's hideout was on the [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] of [[Teth]] within the ruins of an abandoned [[B'omarr Order Monastery]],<ref name="Infiltration" /> though they also made use of a [[Martez sisters' repair shop|repair shop]] on Coruscant.<ref name="Tipping Point" />

===Members===
{{Quote|Figured you weren't out of the fight just yet."<br />"I spent my life defending the Republic. Can't stop now.|Hunter and Rex|<ref name="Battle Scars">{{TBB|Battle Scars}}</ref>}}
The Clone Underground movement consisted of multiple clone members. Within the movement's ranks were former Clone Commander Rex, who served as the leader of the organization. Other clone [[officer]]s included [[clone commando]] Captain Gregor, [[genetically defective clone|CT-99]] clone commando [[Clone Corporal|Corporal]] Echo and Captain Howzer. Troopers within the Clone Underground included Fireball, [[Greer]], [[Nemec]], [[Samson]], a clone engineer who used [[Phase II clone trooper armor|armor]] with [[Color|orange]] markings, and a clone ship guard who used [[armor]] with red markings.<ref name="Infiltration" /> Rex and the Clone Underground were briefly connected with the Imperial trooper Slip,<ref name="Clone Conspiracy" /> and at times operated with the genetically defective [[Clone Force 99]].<ref name="Infiltration" /> Following her desertion of the Empire, former [[Chief Scientist]] [[Emerie Karr]] agreed to join the Clone Underground, eager to make up for her past misdeeds, along with former clone prisoner [[Vik (clone)|Vik]] and many other clones that were [[Skirmish on Wayland|imprisoned]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Tantiss Base]].<ref name="Cavalry">{{TBB|The Cavalry Has Arrived}}</ref>

==History==
===Liberation over Balmorra===
{{Quote|Free Howzer and the others. Nemec and I will take the bridge.|Echo|<ref name="Tipping Point">{{TBB|Tipping Point}}</ref>}}
Echo, Gregor, Nemec, and Fireball flew the ''Remora'' to [[Attack on the prisoner transport ship|attack]] an [[Unidentified Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser (Balmorra)|Imperial ''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] under the command of [[Captain]] [[Pearce]] over the planet [[Balmorra]]. The ''Remora'' disabled the cruiser's hyperdrive and defenses. Echo and Nemec then led a boarding party that drilled through the upper hull of the cruiser as Gregor docked with the Imperial cruiser. Storming the ship, they freed the prisoners and attacked the bridge. While Echo attempted to retrieve data from the databank, Nemec interrogated Captain Pearce, who committed [[Death|suicide]] by activating a [[Electro capsule|suicide shocker]] implanted in his mouth. Gregor informed Echo and Nemec that they had liberated the prisoners and to retreat as Imperial reinforcements arrived. They boarded the ''Remora'' and fled to [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Tipping Point" />

The clones then went to [[Martez sisters' repair shop|Trace Martez's repair shop]] on [[Coruscant]], where [[Senator]] [[Riyo Chuchi]] met with the rescued three clones, including Howzer. A concerned Chuchi listened to Howzer tell her and Echo what he remembered from the prison.<ref name="Tipping Point">{{TBB|Tipping Point}}</ref>

===Meeting on Pantora===
{{Quote|Everyone down! The assailant's gone underground. We're in pursuit.|Rex|<ref name="Infiltration">{{TBB|Infiltration (episode)}}</ref>}}
Later, Rex, Howzer, Nemec, Samson, and Greer traveled to [[Pantora]] to protect the fugitive Senator [[Avi Singh]] and his [[RA-7 protocol droid]] [[GS-8]]. Rex guided them towards a secret meeting with Senator Chuchi as Howzer kept watch from a balcony, corresponding with Samson and Greer who was security on the inside of the building, with Nemec guarding the two senators along with Rex. Singh and Chuchi discussed the growing dissent against [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] when a [[Clone X trooper]] [[CX-1]] attacked, clandestinely eliminating Greer before throwing a bomb at the senators. Rex grabbed it and tossed it out of their room, causing an explosion that blew a hole in the wall. Rex and Nemec then pursued CX-1 underground and up a tower where Howzer arrived on a speeder bike and stunned the assailant. The clones then traveled on a [[shuttle]] through hyperspace with CX-1. Rex and Howzer discussed the object they have recovered from their prisoner, a [[Holopuck|data puck]]. Rex then told him to make arrangements for Fireball to examine the object at base camp at the [[B'omarr Order Monastery]] on [[Teth]].<ref name="Infiltration" />

===B'Omarr Monastery massacre===
After capturing the [[CX-1|assassin]], the Clone Underground brought him back to the B'Omarr Order Monastery to be interrogated. However, the clone proved to be resistant to interrogation, frustrating the Underground members who considered him the only way to discover the location of Tantiss Base. Increasingly desperate, Howzer insisted that they needed to call in [[Clone Force 99]], a unit of [[clone commando]]s retired to the planet [[Pabu]], since one of their members, [[Crosshair]], was also imprisoned on the Base and Howzer suspected him to know about its location due to his previous Imperial affiliation. However, another [[CX-2|assassin]] was deployed to [[Teth]] in a [[Battle on the B'omarr Order Monastery|mission]] to [[Death|eliminate]] CX-1. He infiltrated the monastery and called for a [[Recovery Strike Team]] to reinforce him after identifying the presence of [[Omega]] in the compound. In the ensuing firefight, the assassin killed many clones, including [[Fireball (clone trooper)|Fireball]], [[Nemec]], [[Samson]], along with a clone who used armor with [[Color|orange]] markings, and another clone who used armor with red markings, and forced the remaining clones (Rex, Howzer, and Clone Force 99) and [[Batcher]] to flee into the [[jungle]], where they were cornered by the Strike Team. Rex, recognizing [[Wolffe]], pleaded with him to realize the harm of his actions. Despite hesitating, Wolffe ordered his troopers to stand down and allowed the clones to board the ''Remora'' which Echo and Gregor had just landed behind them, as a sign of respect to his clone brothers.<ref name="Extraction" />

===Fall of Tantiss===
After the Empire [[Raid on Pabu|recaptured]] Omega, Echo rejoined Clone Force 99 to infiltrate [[Imperial Station 003]] with the aid of former Vice-Admiral Edmon Rampart, whom Clone Force 99 [[Rescue of Edmon Rampart|liberated]] from the [[Imperial labor camp]] on [[Erebus]], in hopes of acquiring the coordinates to [[Tantiss Base]]. However, upon learning that the only scheduled science vessel would only receive its coordinates after departing the station, they were forced to infiltrate the vessel to hitch a ride to Tantiss.<ref name="Juggernaut">{{TBB|Juggernaut (episode)}}</ref><ref name="ITB">{{TBB|Into the Breach}}</ref>

Upon arriving on [[Wayland]], Echo managed to infiltrate Tantiss Base disguised as a stormtrooper, where he made contact with female clone, [[Emerie Karr]], who had grown disillusioned with the Empire.<ref name="Flash Strike}>{{TBB|Flash Strike}}</ref> Despite Dr. [[Royce Hemlock]] unleashed his remaining CX troopers against Clone Force 99, they were able to kill Hemlock and his CX troopers, as well as liberate the clone and young Force-sensitives prisoners. Upon returning to Pabu, Echo declared his intent to get some of the clones settled on Pantora and invited Karr to join him to speak with Chuchi.<ref name="Cavalry">{{TBB|The Cavalry Has Arrived}}</ref>
