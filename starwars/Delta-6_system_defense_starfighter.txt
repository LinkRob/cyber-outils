The '''Delta-6 system defense starfighter''', also known more simply as the '''Delta-6 starfighter''' was a model of venerable [[starfighter]] with aerospace superiority capabilities. It had a long, narrow, arrowhead-shaped fuselage with two light [[laser cannon]]s mounted forward and a small, cramped, bubble-canopied [[cockpit]] mounted far aft over the [[engine]].<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" />

[[Kuat Systems Engineering]]'s [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7 ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] was descended from the Delta-6, but was larger and longer, with more powerful and higher-output engines, more responsive controls for greater agility, a more powerful [[combat avionics suite]], and improved targeting and life support systems compared to the older [[starship]].<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Delta-6 system defense starfighter was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''Star Wars'' roleplaying products.<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" /> It was originally created for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where the it first appeared in the [[2002]] novel ''[[Star Wars Adventures 3: The Hostage Princess]]'', which was written by [[Ryder Windham]].<ref name="SWA3">''[[Star Wars Adventures 3: The Hostage Princess]]''</ref>
