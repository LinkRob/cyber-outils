The '''Federal Station''' was a [[hovertrain]] station located in the [[Imperial City|Galactic City]] on [[Coruscant]] during the [[New Republic era]]. It was a spacious building with a [[glass]] ceiling and functioned as the departure point of trains headed towards the [[Shipyard Depot|shipyard depot]] station.<ref name="Chapter 19" />
