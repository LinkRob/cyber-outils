'''Electrostatic fixative''' was a type of fixative that the [[human]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Yord Fandar]] used to coat the fabric of his [[Jedi]] [[temple robes]] in order to repel dust prior to his [[death]] during the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> in [[132 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
