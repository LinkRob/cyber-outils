The '''Dentamma Nebula''' was located in the [[Varada sector]], a part of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="WesternReaches" /> It was situated in grid square I-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dentamma Nebula was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Dentamma Nebula/Legends|nebula]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention in the [[1993]] [[West End Games]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' sourcebook ''[[Planets of the Galaxy, Volume Three]]'', written by [[John Terra]].<ref name="Planets">''[[Planets of the Galaxy, Volume Three]]''</ref>
