The '''Escape from Jakku''' occurred during the [[Cold War]] when a [[scavenger]] named [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and a defecting [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] named [[Finn]] escaped from the forces of the [[First Order]] on the [[planet]] of [[Jakku]]. They carried with them a [[Resistance]] [[droid]] called [[BB-8]], who carried a vital piece of a map that would reveal the whereabouts of the vanished [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]. To escape from pursuing [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]], the trio stole the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. Finn advised Rey to fly low to confuse the TIEs' tracking. Rey flew it through the [[Starship Graveyard|Graveyard of Giants]] while Finn fired on and destroyed the pursuing TIE fighters. With the TIE fighters destroyed, they escaped into space.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|We need to get as far away from the First Order as we can! We go back to Jakku, we die!|Finn, to Poe Dameron|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:Finn_and_Poe_Escape_Finalizer.png|thumb|left|300px|FN-2187 escorts Poe Dameron, before asking for his help in escaping the ''Finalizer''.]]
Immediately following the [[attack on Tuanul]], [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn|FN-2187]] was stricken with grief upon [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren's]] order to [[Death|execute]] every villager. Meanwhile, [[Poe Dameron]] was [[Torture|interrogated]] on the ''[[Finalizer]]'', continuously being asked for the location of the map that led to [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]. Eventually, Poe cracked down, and told Ren that the map to Skywalker was hidden inside a [[BB-8|BB Unit that fled the village]]. At that moment, Finn had decided to leave the [[First Order]]. Needing a [[pilot]] to escape, FN-2187 freed the [[Prisoner|captured]] Poe Dameron from his cell, and the two [[Escape from the Finalizer|stole]] a [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter|Special Forces TIE fighter]]. FN-2187 manned the guns, killing several of his former comrades in the hangar, and then disabled the ''Finalizer''{{'s}} [[turbolaser]]s on the outside of the vessel. The TIE was then damaged by a missile fired from the ''Finalizer'', sending it spiraling towards [[Jakku]]'s surface.<ref name="Episode VII" />

==The escape==
===Tracking Finn===
{{Quote|FN-2187 reported to my division, was evaluated and sent to reconditioning.''"<br />"''No prior signs of non-conformity?''"<br />"''This was his first offense.|Phasma and Hux|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
Shortly after Finn and Poe´s escape, [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] revealed to [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] that this was his first offense of non-conformity. Realizing that Poe and Finn were going back for BB-8, Hux sent out a squadron of search teams to the [[Goazon Badlands]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

Meanwhile, Finn and Dameron had ejected from the TIE shortly before it crashed, each landing separately some distance from the wreckage. Making his way to the crash, Finn found Poe's [[Resistance fighter jacket|jacket]], but no other sign of the [[Resistance]] [[pilot]], and the fighter sunk into the sand. Believing Dameron to be dead, Finn began wandering through the [[desert]], discarding his [[First Order stormtrooper armor|stormtrooper armor]] along the way and eventually arriving at [[Niima Outpost|the nearest settlement]], thirsting for water.<ref name="Episode VII" />

===Meeting and ambush===
{{Quote|I had a pretty messed up day, alright? So I'd appreciate it if you - stop it!|Finn referring to the escape from the ''Finalizer''|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:Finn_and_Rey_boarding.jpg|thumb|Rey and Finn race aboard the ''Millennium Falcon'' with First Order Stormtroopers and TIE fighters in hot pursuit.]]While Finn staggered to a [[happabore]] trough to desperately slake his thirst, he noticed [[Unkar's thugs|thugs]] attempting to steal a [[BB-series astromech droid|BB unit]] [[droid]], which Finn recognized as BB-8, from a woman, [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], but she fended them off with her [[Quarterstaff|staff]]. BB-8 noticed Finn, wearing his master Poe Dameron's jacket, prompting Rey to chase after him. Rey attacked Finn, believing him to be a [[thief]]. Finn explained that he had met and helped Poe escape the ''Finalizer'', but that Dameron had apparently perished during their crash. At that point, the First Order tracked Finn to the settlement, and launched an airstrike in attempt to capture BB-8 and the map.<ref name="Episode VII" />

===Boarding the ''Falcon''===
{{Quote|What about that ship?''"<br />"''That one's garbage!''"<br />*Quadjumper is destroyed*<br />"''The garbage will do!|Finn and Rey|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:JakkuChase-Fathead.png|thumb|left|220px|The ''Millennium Falcon'' evades TIE fighters within the Graveyard of Giants.]]
As Finn, Rey, and BB-8 were escaping the ambush, the trio came across a [[TUG-b13|Quadrijet transfer spacetug]]. Finn was however, skeptical, and asked if they could use a different ship of [[Unkar Plutt]]'s. Suddenly, two TIE/sf space superiority fighters came out of nowhere, fired and destroyed the Quadjumper, prompting Rey to instead board the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. Rey piloted the ship, while Finn manned the lower [[AG-2G quad laser cannon]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

The Falcon was able to evade the two TIE Fighters as they escaped the outpost. A short time later, two more TIE fighters ambushed the ''Falcon'' as Finn and Rey entered the [[Starship Graveyard|Graveyard of Giants]]. Finn successfully shot one, but the cannon later got stuck in a position where he couldn't move it. Rey then flew the ''Falcon'' into the [[engine]]s of a [[Ravager|crashed Super Star Destroyer]]. When the last TIE Fighter was close to the ''Falcon'', Rey deactivated the engines, giving Finn an advantage to shoot it down. The ''Falcon'' then left the [[planet]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Sir. We were unable to acquire the droid on Jakku. It escaped capture aboard a stolen Corellian YT model freighter.''"<br />"''The droid... stole a freighter?''"<br />"''Not exactly, sir. It had help.|Kylo Ren and Dopheld Mitaka|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
Back aboard the ''Finalizer'', [[Lieutenant]] [[Dopheld Mitaka]] informed Kylo Ren that the droid escaped on a stolen [[freighter]] and that it was aided by the deserter FN-2187. Ren activated his [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and lashed out in a rage-fueled tantrum, slashing at an instrument panel uncontrollably for several minutes before calming down enough to ask Mitaka if there was anything else in his report. Mitaka then told Ren that they also appear to have been helped by a local [[scavenger]] girl. Kylo grew even more livid, using [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|pull]] the [[First Order officer|officer]] by his throat across the room in order to choke him with his bare [[Limb|hands]], and asking him what girl it was.<ref name="Episode VII" />
