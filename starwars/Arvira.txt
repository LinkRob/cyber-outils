'''Arvira''' was an [[Imperial officer]] who served aboard the ''[[Devastator]]'' during the early stages of the [[Galactic Civil War]] as a [[fleet logistics liaison]] of the [[logistics corps]]. As a logistics officer, other officers would often come to Arvira for help with forms, which Arvira used to build a quiet network of influence among the crew of the [[starship]].<ref name="The Sith of Datawork" />
