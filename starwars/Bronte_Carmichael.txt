'''Bronte Carmichael''' is an actress who portrayed [[Leida Mothma]] in ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="The Axe Forgets" /> She is best known for playing [[W:c:disney:Madeline Robin|Madeline Robin]] in the [[2018]] film ''[[Wikipedia:Christopher Robin (film)|Christopher Robin]]'' (daughter of [[Ewan McGregor]]'s [[W:c:disney:Christopher Robin#Christopher Robin|titular character]]) and Skye D'Branin in the TV series ''[[Wikipedia:Nightflyers (TV series)|Nightflyers]]''.
