An '''Arratan wolf''' was a type of [[wolf]]<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> that had gone extinct<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> by [[229 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'' to [[229 BBY]].</ref> During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Marchion Ro]], the [[Eye of the Nihil]] wore the skin of an Arratan wolf around his shoulders in a [[cloak]] that had belonged to his [[Ro family|father]] [[Asgar Ro]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
