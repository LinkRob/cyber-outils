'''Corellian whiskey''' was a type of [[alcohol]]ic beverage from the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]] of the [[Janix]] [[Tactical Defense Force]] ordered a glass of the beverage from a [[Unidentified Dowutin bartender|Dowutin bartender]] during the early days of the [[Imperial Era]], prompting the [[Dowutin]] to comment that he had expensive tastes. Lawson was at the [[Unidentified cantina (Janix)|cantina]] to bust a smuggling operation of [[death|deadly]] [[poison]]ous [[species]], but the bartender knew his identity and poisoned his [[whiskey]] with the venom of a [[Wasber]].<ref name="SOM1" /> A [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Unidentified freighter captain|freighter captain]] once shared a bottle of the whiskey with [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] while both men were stationed at [[Echo Base]] on the planet [[Hoth]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Twilight Company" />
