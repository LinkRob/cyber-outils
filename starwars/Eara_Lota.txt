'''Eara Lota''' was a [[Gender|female]] member of the [[Tetan monarchy|monarchy]] of the [[planet]] [[Empress Teta]]. The niece of [[Emperor]] [[Alor Keto]], Lota was the rightful heir. She often protested the living conditions on the [[prison]] planet [[Ronika]] or the working conditions of [[miner]]s elsewhere in the [[Empress Teta system]], frequently becoming a subject of local [[holovid]]s for doing so.<ref name="Nexus of Power" />
