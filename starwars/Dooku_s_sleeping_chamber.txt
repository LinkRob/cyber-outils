'''Dooku's sleeping chamber''' was the bedroom of [[Dooku]], [[Count of Serenno]], located in the main tower of the [[count]]'s [[Castle Serenno|castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]]. It was very similar to the [[Dooku's throne room|throne room]], to which it was connected by a gangway. During the [[Clone Wars]], Dooku was caught asleep in his bedroom when a group of [[Nightsisters]] led by his former acolyte [[Asajj Ventress]] infiltrated the castle to [[Death|murder]] him.
