'''Concordian Crescent Technologies''', also known as '''WESTAR''', was a [[Mandalorian]] arms manufacturer that produced weapons including the [[WESTAR-34 blaster pistol]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE" /> and eventually the [[WESTAR-35 blaster pistol]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], their product was popular on the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]] where it was used by members of the [[Death Watch]] group and the [[Mandalorian Guard]]. Around five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Mandalorian]] [[Sabine Wren]] used a pair of the company's WESTAR-35s while fighting against the [[Galactic Empire]] on the planet [[Lothal]].<ref name="RVG" /> They also produced a pair of custom fitted [[WESTAR-34 blaster pistol]]s for the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE" /> who bore them in the time leading up to the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="AoTC">{{Film|II}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Concordian Crescent Technologies was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Adam Bray]] and published in [[2014]].<ref name="RVG" /> ''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and published in [[2015]] identifies Wren's weapons as [[Jai'galaar blaster]]s made by [[MandalTech]] instead of WESTAR-35s by Concordian Crescent Technologies.<ref name="SMYS">''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]''</ref> As both the ''Visual Guide'',<ref name="RVG" /> ''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]''&mdash;another 2014 reference book by Wallace<ref name="MRJ">''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]''</ref>&mdash;and the official ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' facebook page<ref name="FB">{{Facebook|username=starwarsrebels|url=starwarsrebels/photos/a.230754950414469.1073741828.180356405454324/337107259779237|name=''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''|date=2014-08-20|quote=Sabine's blasters are a popular model on Mandalore, but like all of Sabine's gear, they're custom painted.|archivedate=20191013221701|archivefile=File:StarWarsRebels-SabinesBlasters-Facebook.jpg}}</ref> identify the weapons as WESTARs this article assumes those sources are correct.

The [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'' listed "WESTAR" as the manufacturer for the WESTAR-34 blaster pistol, but listed Concordian Crescent Technologies for the WESTAR-35; this name was reused in ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'' ([[2019]]) and ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' ([[2020]]) but later sources instead listed Concordian Crescent Technologies for both weapons.
