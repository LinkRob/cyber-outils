'''Borkus''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Sullustan]] who resided on the [[planet]] [[Abafar]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. There, he owned and [[Chef|cooked]] for his diner, [[Power Sliders]], in [[Pons Ora]].

==Biography==
Borkus was a [[Sullustan]] who worked at the diner [[Power Sliders]] in the town of [[Pons Ora]] on the [[planet]] [[Abafar]]. When an [[amnesia]]c [[clone commando]] known as [[Gregor]] became stranded on the planet, the opportunistic Borkus took the [[clone trooper]]'s equipment and gave him the job of a [[dishwasher]] at Power Sliders, providing him with low-quality housing of which he told Gregor his pay could barely afford.<ref name="Missing in Action" />

[[File:BorkusThreatensGascon-AIM.png|thumb|left|250px|Borkus threatening Meebur Gascon with his chef's [[knife]]]]
When a team of [[Galactic Republic]] [[droid]]s known as [[D-Squad]] arrived on the world, the team's leader, [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]], went into Borkus's diner for food and announced that he was from the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. Borkus forced him to leave, threatening the [[Zilkin]] with a [[knife]]. Outside, the [[DUM-series pit droid|pit droid]] [[WAC-47]] led Gascon to the garbage cans behind the establishment to see if there was anything he could eat there. Gascon then stumbled upon Gregor, who was taking out the trash. Gascon knew at once he was a clone trooper, but Gregor did not believe him due to his amnesia. Later, Borkus was asked by Gregor what a clone trooper was, and Borkus, wanting to insulate Gregor from the truth, became angry and warned his employee to never ask about clones again.<ref name="Missing in Action" />

That night, Borkus discovered infiltrators in Power Sliders, as Gregor and D-Squad had broken in to search for Gregor's [[Katarn-class commando armor|clone armor]]. Borkus, who had brought the armor with him, pointed Gregor's own [[DC-17m Interchangeable Weapon System]] at him, but the Sullustan was incapacitated by members of D-Squad. Left tied up on the ground, Borkus shouted that they would never get off the planet and would be forced to work for him forever and burst out laughing as Gregor left with D-Squad.<ref name="Missing in Action" />
