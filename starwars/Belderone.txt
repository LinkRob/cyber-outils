'''Belderone''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Belderone system]], situated along the [[Salin Corridor]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] had a [[Belderone Imperial naval base|naval base]] and an [[R/M Facility Four deepdock]] on the world, but from early into the Empire's [[Imperial Era|reign]], Belderone had a bad reputation as an assignment for [[Imperial Army]] members, influenced by how far it was from the [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]]. It also had a cottaging industry that supported [[Treasure hunting|treasure hunters]] searching for the legendary planet [[Ossus]].

After [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s [[corvette]], the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', was [[Mission to Murkhana|captured]] by [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebels]] in [[14 BBY]], the Imperial [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Liberator (Venator-class)|''Liberator'']] was reassigned from Belderone's [[deepdock]] to the planet [[Nam Chorios]]. In [[3 ABY]], Belderone was marked as a possible target for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] by [[General]] [[Crix Madine]], who was inspired by the campaign [[Operation Ringbreaker]] being carried out by the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Belderone?''"<br />"''Too heavily fortified&mdash;even for your corvette.|Wilhuff Tarkin and Darth Vader|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
Belderone was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> located in the [[Belderone system]] of the [[Belderone sector]] within an intersecting portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> Situated in grid square R-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> the world lay along the [[Salin Corridor]] [[hyperspace route]]<ref name="Salin Corridor" /> between the [[Trogan system|Trogan]] and [[Columex system]]s.<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref>

==History==
===Reputations===
{{Quote|The stormtroopers have a saying, ''Better spaced than based on Belderone'', and we're a far cry from Belderone.|An [[Unidentified Imperial ambassador (Murkhana)|Imperial ambassador]], to Wilhuff Tarkin|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
Beginning during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], an [[Belderone Imperial naval base|Imperial naval base]] and an [[R/M Facility Four deepdock]] were based at Belderone. During that [[time]], members of the [[Imperial Army]], such as [[stormtrooper]]s, were reluctant to be stationed on Belderone, as it was understood that the [[troop]]s stationed the farthest from the Imperial [[capital]], the planet [[Coruscant]], were of the least importance to the Empire's [[New Order|cause]]. As such, virtually any reassignment to the planet was viewed as punishment or demotion.<ref name="Tarkin" />

[[File:Ossus-FromTheDesertComesAStranger.png|thumb|left|300px|Belderone became home to a cottage industry that supported those that were trying to reach Ossus (pictured).]]
The Salin Corridor provided an access point toward the [[Cron Drift]], an [[asteroid field]] and the only known bypass to reach the ancient world of [[Ossus]], which was tied by legends to the origins of the [[Jedi]]. The [[Imperial Navy]] restricted traffic toward the [[asteroid]] field, establishing checkpoints at the best [[Hyperspace jump point|jump points]] from the [[Perlemian Trade Route]]. However, the Salin Corridor and the [[Tion Cluster]] were the less patrolled of those access points, and consequently, Belderone and other worlds such as [[Foran Tutha]] became home to cottage industries that supported [[Treasure hunting|treasure hunters]] searching for Ossus.<ref name="Nexus of Power">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>

===Resource management===
{{Quote|We have additional outposts at Trogan and Jomark. And of course, the naval base and R/M Facility Four deepdock at Belderone.''"<br />"''What do we have parked at R/M, colonel?''"<br />"''Several CR-ninety corvettes, two ''Carrack''-class light cruisers, a couple of Victories, and a ''Venator''-class destroyer&mdash;the ''Liberator''.|An Imperial colonel and Wilhuff Tarkin|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] led by former [[Galactic Republic]] [[Republic Intelligence|Intelligence]] [[Intelligence officer|officer]] [[Berch Teller]] [[Mission to Murkhana|stole]] the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', the personal [[corvette]] of Imperial [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]. After Tarkin and the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] acquired a [[Parsec Predator|replacement ship]], [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] was informed of the situation by several high-[[Military rank|ranking]] [[Imperial officer]]s, who mentioned that Vader and Tarkin were pursuing the captured corvette toward the [[Fial system]]. [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]] further relayed to the [[Emperor]] that the nearest Imperial presence was in the Belderone [[Star system|system]], and thereafter Palpatine ordered that the [[Admiralty (Imperial)|admiralty]] at the Belderone system be instructed to send [[starship]]s to whichever [[star]] systems Vader and Tarkin requested in order to pursue the hijacked ''Carrion Spike''.<ref name="Tarkin" />

[[File:Moff-Tarkin.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Belderone was informed to provide any requested support for Moff Wilhuff Tarkin.]]
After the captured ''Carrion Spike'' was used to [[Attack on Galidraan Station|attack]] [[Galidraan Station]], Tarkin, who had continued to pursue his stolen vessel, liaised with the [[Unidentified Imperial colonel (Galidraan Station)|Imperial colonel]] aboard the [[space station]], soon asking about Imperial resources available near the [[Galidraan system]]. After the [[colonel]] mentioned Belderone's two [[Navy|naval]] facilities, Tarkin questioned what ships there were at the R/M Facility Four [[Deepdock]] there, with the colonel confirming that several [[CR90 corvette]]s, two [[Carrack-class light cruiser|''Carrack''-class light cruisers]], two [[Victory-class Star Destroyer|''Victory''-class Star Destroyers]], and the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Liberator (Venator-class)|''Liberator'']] were stationed at the deepdock.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Tarkin discussed the ''Carrion Spike''{{'s}} next target with Vader, establishing that Belderone was too fortified for the corvette to strike at and instead predicting that the planet [[Nam Chorios]] would be the target. Vader then ordered that Belderone be contacted to have the ''Liberator'' be sent to Nam Chorios, and Tarkin told the colonel to establish contact between him and both Belderone and Coruscant to do so. In another meeting between high-ranking Imperial leaders, [[Deputy Director]] [[Harus Ison]] of the Empire's [[Imperial Security Bureau]] called the withdrawal of ships from Belderone reckless, adding that they were risking the curtailment of pacification campaigns elsewhere. The ''Liberator'' reached Nam Chorios, finding no insurgent attack, and was redeployed to the planet [[Ord Cestus]] afterward.<ref name="Tarkin" />

===Rebel targets===
{{Quote|With Operation Ringbreaker meeting early success, I feel our current angle of attack could provide a broader path to victory. This map notes the locations of other major Imperial shipyards and drydocks, as well as key mining centers for metals and ores. Once we take out Kuat, these should be our next targets.|Crix Madine, in an official document to Mon Mothma|<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Metalorn-TRF.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Belderone was among the Galactic Empire's major shipyard and drydock sites.]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], Belderone's facilities were a major part of the Empire's network of [[shipyard]]s and drydocks.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] led the campaign [[Operation Ringbreaker]] against the [[Kuat Drive Yards]], attacking more lightly guarded industrial hubs to initiate redeployments of the planet [[Kuat]]'s security forces.<ref name="BTC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> While [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Alliance Intelligence]] observed the campaign and its early success, rebel [[General]] [[Crix Madine]] made a [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[map]] of the Empire's major shipyards, drydocks, and [[mining]] centers that were considered possible targets should the Kuat Drive Yards be successfully sabotaged.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

Belderone was marked on the map as a high-priority target with a medium security level. The map was included in an official document sent by Madine to Alliance [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]], eventually being collected by [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]] for the compilation of non-electronic documents known as ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> The 61st ultimately abandoned Operation Ringbreaker before it could reach Kuat Drive Yards.<ref name="BTC" />

During the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[transport]] shuttle designated [[TS428]] traveled from Belderone to the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Kijimi]], where it landed at the [[Kijimi Spaceport|spaceport]] in [[Kijimi City]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Belderone was introduced into the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in a mention in the [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Belderone/Legends|Belderone]] first appeared in the [[Star Wars Annual (1977) 3|third annual issue]] of the [[1977]] [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series, written by [[Mary Jo Duffy]], illustrated by [[Klaus Janson]],<ref name="SW Annual 3">[[Star Wars Annual (1977) 3|''Star Wars Annual'' (1977) 3]]</ref> and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[August 30]], [[1983]].<ref name="Marvel Age 6">[[W:c:Marvel:Marvel Age_Vol 1 6|''Marvel Age'' #6]]</ref>

The short story "[[TK-462 (short story)|TK-462]]," written by [[Sylvain Neuvel]] and originally published in the [[Star Wars Insider 166|166th issue]] of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="TK">{{InsiderCite|166|TK-462 (short story)}}</ref> on [[June 14]], [[2016]],<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/10-highlights-from-star-wars-insider-166-out-now|text=10 Highlights from ''Star Wars Insider'' #166 &ndash; Out Now!|nolive=1}}</ref> is partially set on the planet [[Lothal]].<ref name="TK" /> However, when it was reprinted in the ''[[Star Wars 2017 Del Rey Sampler]]'', this was changed to Belderone.<ref name="2017 Del Rey Sampler">''[[Star Wars 2017 Del Rey Sampler]]''</ref> The change was not present in its most recent reprint in the [[2021]] collection ''[[Star Wars Insider: The Fiction Collection Volume 2]]'',<ref name="Insider Fiction Collection">''[[Star Wars Insider: The Fiction Collection Volume 2]]''</ref> and this article does not interpret it canonically.
