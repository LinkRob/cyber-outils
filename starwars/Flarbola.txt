'''Flarbola''' was an individual who left [[barium]]-laden [[fuel]] in a container as part of a stash in a [[hangar]] on [[Achra Station]], a [[space station]] orbiting the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] discovered the stash and took the fuel.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}
