'''''Dark Horse Comics'' 7''' was a preview [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] issue, published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] in [[February]] of [[1993]]. The cover art depicts soon-to-be [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Nomi Sunrider]], defending her [[Vima Sunrider|daughter]] following the death of her husband, [[Andur Sunrider]]. Included in this issue is an afterword written by ''[[Star Wars]]'' author [[Tom Veitch]], in which he discusses his views on writing [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (comic series)|''Tales of the Jedi'']].

==Contents==
Dark Horse Comics 7 previewed several comic issues which were yet to be released in 1993. Included in this issue were excerpts from:
*[[Tales of the Jedi 3|''The Saga of Nomi Sunrider, Part 1 of 3'']]
*''Mad Dogs, Part 4 of 4''
*''Robocop: Invasions - Part 2 of 4''
*''Predator: Blood Feud - Part 4 of 4''

==Continuity==
Even though the content of the except from ''The Saga of Nomi Sunrider, Part 1 of 3'' is the same as what would eventually be released, a main difference can be seen in the colors of the excerpt in ''Dark Horse Comics 7''. Compared with the colors in the final release of ''The Saga of Nomi Sunrider'', the colors in ''Dark Horse Comics 7'' are much more vibrant and bright, as compared with the final released version which have more of an earth-tone, less vibrant touch to it.
