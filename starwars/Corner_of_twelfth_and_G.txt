The '''corner of twelfth and G''' was an alley in [[Level 1312]] of [[Coruscant]]. [[Asajj Ventress]] moved to an apartment there when she was working as a [[bounty hunter]]. She brought [[Ahsoka Tano]] to use a [[holobooth]] at the corner when she was on the run after being framed for the [[bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar]].<ref name="TCAJ" />

A [[probe droid]] spotted Tano and alerted [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]], who intercepted her at the corner. With Ventress' help, Tano subdued the [[Commander]] and his [[Imperial shock trooper|clone shock troopers]] without [[Death|killing]] them before fleeing.<ref name="TCAJ" />
