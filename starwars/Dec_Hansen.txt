'''Dec Hansen''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[scavenger]] from [[Ques]], a [[swamp]] [[planet]] in [[Hutt Space]]. Hansen was an only child, so [[Dec Hansen's mother|his mother]] built him a "brother," the [[droid]] [[AG-90]]. Hansen grew up with AG, and the two eventually joined the [[Resistance]] against the [[First Order]], serving as [[cadet]]s in [[J-Squadron]]. At the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]], Hansen became reputed as a troublemaker, and in [[34 ABY]], he and a few other members of J-[[Squadron]] were sent on a scavenging [[mission to Vodran]] as a punishment. However, Hansen and fellow cadet [[Sari Nadle]] became separated from the group after being attacked by [[Vodran]]'s [[Species|fauna]], and they were forced to flee the planet.<ref name="Join the Resistance" />

==Behind the scenes==
''[[Star Wars: Join the Resistance]]'' co-author [[Ben Blacker]] confirmed on Twitter that his intention was that Dec was gay, making him the first gay character in a ''Star Wars'' children's book.<ref name="Twit">{{Twitter|BenBlacker|status/1035545827861614592|[[Ben Blacker]]|quote=Yes.|archivefile=File:DecHansen Tweet.png|archivedate=20180907164130}}</ref> This was implied several times throughout the series, but not explicitly stated. In the second book, ''[[Join the Resistance: Escape from Vodran]]'', Dec told [[Harra]] the [[Hutt]] the story of his fears of rejection after realizing it and how he told his brother and [[parent]]s, but only referred to it as "the thing that made me different from everyone else," never specifying what that thing was.<ref name="Escape from Vodran">''[[Join the Resistance: Escape from Vodran]]''</ref> Dec's sexuality was alluded to again in ''[[Join the Resistance: Attack on Starkiller Base]]'' when his closest friend Sari admitted to [[Mattis Banz]] that she had feelings for Dec. Asked by Mattis if she thought Dec liked her back, Sari responded "I've never known him to like a girl. It's just not how he is."<ref name="Attack on Starkiller Base" />
