The '''aid mission to Langston''' was a humanitarian operation carried out by the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] to assist residents of the [[planet]] [[Langston]] in the aftermath of an [[Attack on Langston|attack]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] early in the [[Clone Wars]], which had been repelled by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. The mission was led by newly-promoted [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[D'urban Wen-Hurd]], who were assisted by the [[302nd Battalion]] of [[clone trooper]]s. In addition, five [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] were sent along on the mission as part of an initiative by Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] to get [[youngling]]s and [[Padawan]]s used to the [[Cloning|clones]]. Skywalker, Initiate [[Mill Alibeth]], and the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] departed the mission without warning when Skywalker discovered that his former [[Jedi Master]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], was [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Kenobi and Skywalker)|in danger]] on [[Cato Neimoidia]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />
