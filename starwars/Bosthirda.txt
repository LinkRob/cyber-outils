'''Bosthirda''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Falcon" /> located in the [[Bosthirda system]] in the [[Sith Worlds]], a region of the [[Esstran sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It had a [[Color|brown]] and black surface.<ref name="Falcon" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bosthirda was created for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, was first mentioned in the ''[[Rebellion Era Sourcebook]]''<ref name="Sourcebook">''[[Rebellion Era Sourcebook]]''</ref> and first appeared in ''[[The Old Republic: Revan]]''.<ref name="Revan">''[[The Old Republic: Revan]]''</ref> It was made [[canon]] in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 82|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 82]] magazine.<ref name="Falcon" />
