'''Broadsword Online Games''' is a [[Star Wars video games|video game]] company founded in [[2014]] by former developers of the company [[Wikipedia:Mythic Entertainment|Mythic Entertainment]] following the latter's closure. Like Mythic Entertainment, Broadsword was specialized in [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online role-playing games]]. Upon its creation, the studio took over maintenance and development of the games [[Wikipedia:Dark Age of Camelot|Dark Age of Camelot]] and [[Wikipedia:Ultima Online|Ultima Online]], both previously developed by Mythic Entertainment and published by [[Electronic Arts]].<ref name="DAoC" />

In June [[2023]], [[BioWare]] handed off development and further update of ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' to Broadsword Online Games after almost 12 years under BioWare. The majority of the ''Old Republic'' development team at BioWare was intended to join Broadsword to continue to work on the game.<ref name="Gamespot" /><ref name="TOR">{{TORweb|url=info/news/article/20230627|text=Studio Update - ''Star Wars''™: The Old Republic™}}</ref><ref name="bioware">{{WebCite|url=https://blog.bioware.com/2023/06/27/bright-future-for-swtor|text=The Future is Bright for Star Wars™: The Old Republic™|date=2023-06-27|work=[[BioWare]]'s official website|archivedate=20230627160446}}</ref>
