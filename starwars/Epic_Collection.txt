We'll be bouncing around to different periods of ''Star Wars'' history with each Epic Collection, constructing one huge tapestry, collecting full unbroken runs of all the greatest ''Star Wars comics'' from the past 35 years.|David Gabriel, Marvel Comics SVP of Sales and Marketing|<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/marvel-announces-star-wars-legends-epic-collection|text=Marvel Announces ''Star Wars'' Legends Epic Collection}}</ref>}}
[[File:EpicCollectionLogo.png|right|250px]]
'''Epic Collection''' is an imprint by [[Marvel Comics]] used to reprint large quantities of comics in trade paperback collections. Currently, ongoing [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] Epic Collections are reprinting all the [[Timeline of Legends comics|Legends comics]] put out by Marvel Comics, [[Dark Horse Comics]], and other publishers.<ref name="Announcement" /> Each collection is curated to contain comics in chronological order, including stories from the ''[[Star Wars Tales]]'' line, ''[[Star Wars: Tag & Bink]]'', and the ''[[Star Wars: Visionaries]]'' [[Wikipedia:Graphic novel|graphic novel]], instead of being arranged thematically like the previous ''[[Star Wars Omnibus]]es'' from Dark Horse Comics. Some Epic Collections have been combined to create even larger hardcover [[Marvel Omnibus]]es. To date, the contents of all Epic Collections (and most Marvel Omnibuses featuring Legends material) have been assembled by [[Mike Hansen]].

In May of [[2023]], it was announced that Marvel Comics would begin releasing Star Wars titles under their new ''Modern Era'' Epic Collection imprint. Unlike the previous Legends Epic Collections, these would begin reprinting [[Timeline of canon comics|Canon comics]] put out by Marvel Comics from 2015 onward, the first of which released in the summer of [[2024]].
