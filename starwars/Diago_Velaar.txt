'''Diago Velaar''' was a [[Miraluka]]<ref name="BftA" /> [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Characters" /> and a legendary [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] at the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. As his [[species]] did not have [[eye]]s, he fought only with his hearing. When goons attacked the Arena, intent on causing destruction, Diago found a vantage point to shoot the invaders. He asked his allies to shout out so he knew not to fire upon them.<ref name="BftA" />

His ability to perform trick shots without sight amazed audiences, leading to speculation that his abilities were aided by [[magic]], trickery, or [[the Force]] itself. Diago's [[slugthrower]] fired physical rounds, rather than energy bolts. He had a suave personality and was highly confident.<ref name="Announcement">{{Hunters|url=news/new-hunter-set-to-enter-the-arena|text=Update: New Hunter Set to Enter the Arena}}</ref>

As a Hunter, Velaar was said to come from a [[Farm|ranching]] background, including proudly working as a [[nerf herder]]. He supposedly spent years living in remote regions of [[the galaxy]] enjoying the animals and [[plant]]s of lush planets.<ref name="SWH" />

==Biography==
Eventually a new Hunter joined their ranks, an [[Iridonia]]n [[Zabrak]] named [[Nox]]. During her first few days in the Arena, Nox made it known that she was a solitary woman, with a disdain for friendship and community. Therefore, when Nox began spending time with Rieve, Diago noticed, and commented on it during one instance in the [[chow room]]. Nox surprised Diago with her reaction and stormed out of the Chow Room.<ref name="Chow Room Secrets">{{ArenaNews|url=7f43e105b9294a8e9fc0910d5cb71d7f-en-gb|text=Chow Room Secrets}}</ref>

Diago soon approached Nox to apologize for upsetting her, acknowledging that he hadn't accounted for their different approaches to relationships when he spoke with her in the Chow Room. Nox accepted his apology, also acknowledging that she was unused to having friends. Diago challenged Nox to a friendly Clash and Nox accepted.<ref name="Take Your Best Shot">{{ArenaNews|url=1b881e69f3444c79a14de22c37340d10-en-gb|text=Take Your Best Shot!}}</ref> Diago would eventually win the Clash. In a subsequent interview with [[L-X1]], Diago spoke highly of Nox, and admitted that he was very pleased with his win. He ended the interview calling Nox his friend, and hoping to battle with her again soon.<ref name="Hot Shot Diago">{{ArenaNews|url=35f7e5cbf34340f3884b9f27f0f36468-en-gb|text=Hot Shot Diago}}</ref>

After the Clash, Diago and Nox began training together. In the Training Grounds, Nox awkwardly greeted Rieve, who acknowledged her, but was clearly in a bad mood about something. Suddenly, Balada and Daq Dragus arrived, announcing that Balada's prized tiara had been stolen from her chambers. They offered a reward for the capture of the thief, hinting that this may have something to do with a [[Glikkin|new Hunter]] who would be arriving shortly.<ref name="Training Ground Tensions">{{ArenaNews|url=d93477261edb4791b31b65a08f6eab65-en-gb|text=Training Ground Tensions}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DiagoLogo-Hunters.png|250px|right|thumb|Diago's logo]]
Diago is a playable character in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', produced by [[Zynga]] and [[Lucasfilm Games]]. He was first introduced in the 0.17 update on [[July 18]], [[2023]] during the game's soft launch. In his announcement, he was described as a Hunter preferring to stay back from the fight in order to whittle down opponents' health. His piercing rounds were designed to damage multiple enemies at once, a beneficial quality against enemy tanks.<ref name="Announcement" />

Although he is described as being able to aim just through hearing, in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity [[Miraluka/Legends|Miraluka]] possess a natural form of [[Force sight|Force Sight]] that allows them to perceive their environment without physical vision. This is hinted at through the speculation about his abilities.

When ''Hunters'' launched in [[June 4|June]] of 2024, Diago was the fifth Hunter players could unlock via the Hunter's Path progression. He is considered a level 2 difficulty Damage-class Hunter. His active abilities include Heightened Senses, in which nearby enemies are highlighted even through physical barriers, and a [[proximity mine]] which he can throw at his feet to damage and knock back enemies. Diago has a passive ability in which he can grapple to nearby anchor ledges when jumping or falling. Although considered a passive ability, use of his [[grappling hook]] is triggered by player input. His Ultimate, Piercing Rounds, loads several high-density slugs into his [[rifle]] which cause extra damage and can pass through enemies.<ref name="SWH" />

The character was named after the sound designer Paco Diago, for whom [[BossAlien]] UK leader [[Matthew Hemby]] and ''Star Wars: Hunters'' Creative Director [[William Wallace]] held deep affection. They did not tell Paco Diago about naming the character Diago, instead allowing it to be a surprise.<ref name="NameBTS">{{LinkedIn|url=posts/matthewhemby_star-wars-hunters-trivia-we-named-the-hunter-activity-7207076445855047682-pXFB|text=Matthew Hemby|date=2024-06-13|quote=Star Wars: Hunters Trivia: we named the Hunter "Diago" after Paco Diago without ever telling him!|archivedate=20250122152521}}</ref>
