The '''All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)''', also known as '''AT-AT/P''' or simply '''AT-AT''', was the original model of combat [[walker]] [[transport]] used by the [[Imperial Army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] throughout the [[Imperial Era]]. It was one of several different models produced by [[Kuat Drive Yards]] prior to the release of the standard [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Galactic Civil War)|All Terrain Armored Transport]].

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|Listen. Those walkers double as troop transports so once you get inside... be careful.|Cere Junda, warning Cal Kestis as he embarked to steal an AT-AT/P in active combat|<ref name="JFO" />}}
The All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype), or AT-AT/P, was a prototype model of [[AT-series]] [[walker]].<ref name="DRVG" /> was pitted against rebels during the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Relics Trivia" /> Manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]],<ref name="EB" /> the AT-AT/P was the successor of the [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] (AT-TE) that saw use during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="sweATTE">{{SWE|technology|attewalker|AT-TE walker}}</ref> The Imperial AT-AT/P was armed with two heavy [[laser cannon]] [[turret]]s, four anti-personnel [[blaster]]s, and a dorsal twin [[laser]] turret at the rear.<ref name="EB" /> A [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] was capable of carrying two AT-AT/P.<ref name="A Princess on Lothal">{{Rebels|A Princess on Lothal}}</ref> A single [[walker]] was operated by two [[AT-AT pilot|AT-AT drivers]], who [[pilot]]ed the machinery alongside an optional [[vehicle commander]].<ref name="Relics" />

[[File:ProtoATATCockpit.png|thumb|left|224px|A walker was manned by two AT-AT/P pilots based in the [[cockpit]].]]
Many [[Commanding officer|commanders]] placed the AT-AT/P at the forefront of battle, though some sent [[All Terrain Defense Pod]]s (AT-DPs) to operate alongside the hulking<ref name="Relics" /><ref name="An Inside Man">{{Rebels|An Inside Man}}</ref><ref name="Jedi Night">{{Rebels|Jedi Night}}</ref> combat walker<ref name="Relics Trivia" /> The Imperial [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] had his AT-AT/P walkers flanked by [[K79-S80 Imperial Troop Transport|Imperial Troop Transports]] and [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

The AT-AT/P was larger than the [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Galactic Civil War)|later AT-AT model]] used<ref name="Relics Trivia" /> during the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> which measured twenty- and twenty-two point five [[meter]]s in length and height respectively.<ref name="incredibuilds">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: AT-ACT Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]''</ref><ref name="sweATAT">{{SWE|technology|atatwalker|All Terrain Armored Transport}}</ref> However, as with the later model,<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> its [[Limb|legs]] were attenuated as with other walkers such as the AT-TE or AT-DP,<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> allowing the early walker model to be exploitable by [[lightsaber]] strikes<ref name="Zero Hour" /> and, in one instance,<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> [[magnetic harpoon]]s<ref name="Adventures of Luke Skywalker">''[[The Adventures of Luke Skywalker, Jedi Knight]]''</ref> that tripped the entire walker<ref name="ESB" /> and exposed its vulnerable neck<ref name="Relics" /> to laser [[cannon]]<ref name="Snowspeeder SWE">{{SWE|technology|snowspeeder|snowspeeder}}</ref> or [[Mass-driver cannon|heavy projectile cannon]]<ref name="AT-TE Walker DB">{{Databank|at-te-walker|AT-TE Walker}}</ref> fire that severely damaged the vehicle.<ref name="ESB" /><ref name="Relics" />

==History==
===Development===
The AT-AT/P walker was developed from the Clone Wars-era AT-TE for use in the Imperial [[Army]], the ground forces of the Galactic Empire.<ref name="sweATTE" /> The AT-AT/P constituted a far more imposing example of walker technology than its predecessor;<ref name="sweATAT" /> it featured long legs that lifted the narrow body of the vehicle which towered over the AT-TE<ref name="Relics" /> as well as the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]]'s deep [[Water|rivers]],<ref name="JFO" /> superseding the Clone Wars vehicle's six stubby legs whose movements were hindered by obstacles of a comparable height.<ref name="Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> The walker was in service by the time of the [[Invasion of Mon Cala]] in [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Darth Vader (2017) 14">[[Darth Vader (2017) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 14]]</ref>

===Early rebellion against the Empire===
====Kashyyyk====
{{Quote|Hey! Who are you?''"<br />"''Someone who just brought an AT-AT to the table. Who are you?''"<br />"''Someone making the Empire angry. We're advancing on the Imp landing pad up ahead, wouldn't mind fire support.''"<br />"''Copy that. Let's make 'em even angrier.|Saw Gerrera and Cal Kestis|<ref name="JFO" />}}
[[File:AT-AT-P.Cal_Kestis.jpg|thumb|right|240px|Cal Kestis prepares to infiltrate an AT-AT/P traversing a body of water.]]
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> several AT-AT/Ps were stationed on Kashyyyk and were deployed to assist in defending an [[Imperial Refinery]] against [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[Partisans]]. One found itself collapsed cockpit-first over the edge of the [[Kyyyalstaad Falls]] near the [[Imperial Refinery]]. The [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] successfully infiltrated and hijacked an AT-AT/P, and used it to help Partisans capture a landing area by eliminating Imperial forces along the way to it. The hijacked AT-AT/P fell after Kestis used it to take down a [[Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle|''Zeta''-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle]], which crashed into the AT-AT/P while falling. When Imperial reinforcements arrived, the Partisans were forced to retreat. Gerrera shot down an AT-AT/P during the retreat.<ref name="JFO" />

==== Jedha ====
In [[9 BBY]], several AT-AT/Ps were deployed by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and the [[Inquisitorius]] against the assault on the [[Hidden Path]]'s secret lair on [[Jedha]] after [[Bode Akuna]] leaked ISB Commander [[Lank Denvik]]'s intel when they were about to move the under repair [[Jedi Archives]] to [[Tanalorr]]. They were destroyed by [[Cere Junda]], though she was later killed by Vader in a fierce duel, while the Archives were destroyed by the Sith Lord.<ref>''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

====The Spectres====
=====Warring walkers=====
{{Quote|Those things are practically impervious to blasters, but they have one weak point in their armor, the neck.''"<br />"''One well placed shot from the main gun should destroy it.|Sabine Wren and Clone Commando Gregor|<ref name="Relics" />}}
In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] led three AT-AT/P walkers on an [[Assault on Seelos|assault]] on the desert planet of [[Seelos]] against the [[Spectres]]. The rebel group was meeting with the [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] [[Clone trooper officer|clone officers]] [[Rex]], [[Wolffe]], and [[Gregor]], aboard [[Rex's AT-TE|their]]<ref name="Relics" /> modified All Terrain Tactical Enforcer.<ref name="Modified AT-TE Walker DB">{{Databank|modified-at-te-walker|Modified AT-TE Walker}}</ref> Ultimately, all the Imperial walkers were lost to the rebels,<ref name="Relics" /> and one of them was later repurposed by the [[Cloning|clones]] as [[Joopa Base]], their mobile camp.<ref name="Joopa Base DB">{{Databank|joopa-base|Joopa Base}}</ref>

=====Platform patrol=====
{{Quote|Gravity locks? This is hardly necessary.''"<br />"''I insist. Plus a trooper detachment, and for extra security... Walkers will guard your ships from the rebel insurgency. All for your saftey, miss.''"<br />"''It's ''Your Highness.''|Princess Leia Organa and Imperial Lieutenant Yogar Lyste|<ref name="A Princess on Lothal" />}}
[[File:Gozanti_with_AT-ATs.png|thumb|left|230px|A ''Gozanti''-class cruiser deploys two AT-AT/Ps to patrol the Lothal landing platform.]]
AT-AT/Ps were used again<ref name="A Princess on Lothal" /> in [[3 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> against the Spectres, when [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] arrived on the planet to give the ''[[Ghost]]''{{'s}} crew three [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvettes]]. Two walkers were deployed to patrol the transports' landing platform, which were formally on Lothal to offer supplies to citizens impacted by the rebel efforts. When the rebels [[Mission on Lothal|tried to steal]] the corvettes, the ''Ghost'' started firing on the AT-AT/Ps. Four [[proton torpedo]]s were not powerful enough to destroy one of the walkers, and it was necessary for [[Kanan Jarrus]] to use his [[Kanan Jarrus' lightsaber|lightsaber]] to cut its legs, toppling it. The second one was knocked down by the engines of the last corvette, piloted by [[Ryder Azadi]].<ref name="A Princess on Lothal" />

=====Armory attack=====
{{Quote|Grand Admiral, there is a small band of rebels attacking the east gate. I've deployed two AT-ATs. Should I send backup?''"<br />"''Yes, Lieutenant. Let's play their game.|Imperial Lieutenant Yogar Lyste and Grand Admiral Thrawn|<ref name="An Inside Man" />}}
In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Lieutenant]] [[Yogar Lyste]] deployed two AT-AT/P walkers, [[Walker 271]] and [[Walker 414]], to combat an attack on the east gate of the [[Imperial Armory Complex]] by members of the [[Lothal resistance group]] led by Ryzder Azadi. The rebels Kanan, [[Ezra Bridger]], and [[C1-10P|Chopper]] escaped in the stolen AT-DP [[Walker 216]]. However, Grand [[Admiral]] Thrawn realized that the rebels had hijacked the AT-DP walker and ordered his AT-AT/Ps to crush them. One of the AT-AT/P walkers proceeded to crush the AT-DP under its heavy body but the rebels managed to use their lightsabers to cut their way aboard. [[Marida Sumar]] also fired a rocket which hit one of the walker's legs. After knocking out the AT-AT pilots, the two climbed out and escaped with Ryder's cell into the wilderness.<ref name="An Inside Man" />

=====Battle of Atollon=====
{{Quote|I know that sound.''"<br />"''Yeah. And I hate that sound.''"<br />"''Lousy four-leggers.''"<br />"''Hit 'em with the detonators.|Captain Rex and Garazeb Orrelios|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
[[File:Bendu_lighting_form.jpg|thumb|right|240px|AT-AT/Ps shifted their cockpits upwards to take down the airborne Bendu]]
Thrawn then deployed six of these during the [[Battle of Atollon|battle]] on [[Atollon]]. Jarrus destroyed one with his lightsaber. The [[Force wielder]] [[Bendu]] then destroyed three of the AT-AT/P walkers, though the last two were able to shoot him down from the [[Atmosphere|sky]].<ref name="Zero Hour" />

=====Jedi Knight=====
{{Quote|Governor Pryce, the rebels are headed for the fuel depot.''"<br />"''I'll mobilize walkers, We'll trap them there.|Rukh and Governor Pryce|<ref name="Jedi Night" />}}
The walkers were later used by [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] on Lothal during the [[Rescue of Hera Syndulla (Lothal)|rescue of Hera Syndulla]]. As Kanan Jarrus and [[Hera Syndulla]] were about to be picked up by Bridger and [[Sabine Wren]] atop of the<ref name="Jedi Night" /> [[Lothal City fuel depot]],<ref name="Jedi Night EG">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/jedi-night-episode-guide|text="Jedi Night" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars Rebels}}</ref> two AT-AT/Ps approached, and were commanded by Pryce to open fire on the [[fuel pod]]s. An AT-AT/P pilot questioned this, referring to the highly volatile [[fuel]] contained within the pods. Despite this, Pryce insisted upon her orders; the walkers fired, causing an [[Fire|explosion]], thus forcing Jarrus to use [[the Force]] in an effort to save the other rebels. In sacrifice, the Jedi pushed Syndulla into Bridger and Wren's awaiting [[gunship]], preventing the harm of others as he himself was consumed in the ensuing flames.<ref name="Jedi Night" />

In the aftermath of this incident, Grand Admiral Thrawn viewed Pryce's actions as a mistake as it destroyed the Empire's entire fuel supply on Lothal for his [[TIE/D Defender Elite|TIE Defender project]].<ref name="DUME">{{Rebels|DUME}}</ref> Before the [[Liberation of Lothal]], the ''[[Ghost]]'' visited the walker [[Joopa Base]] on [[Seelos]].<ref name="A Fool's Hope">{{Rebels|A Fool's Hope}}</ref>

===Imperial Remnant===
Despite being obsolete and succeeded by the standard production model by the time of the Galactic Civil War, several AT-AT/Ps remained operational even after the collapse of the Empire. In desperation, [[Neo-Imperial forces]] utilised old and outdated equipment such as these walkers in order to keep fighting. One such example, Imperial [[warlord]] [[Commander]] [[Barro]], had at least three AT-AT/Ps under his command. They [[Attack on Barro's Imperial remnant|fought]] alongside [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]] and the even older [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport|AT-RT]] walkers on an icy mountain world against [[Din Djarin]] and [[Din Grogu|Grogu]]. Despite Walker 3, managing to destroy the smaller walker, Djarin planted two [[grav charge]]s on the legs of the third one before attacking the second one, blowing it up from the hatch above. Barro set the last walker to [[autopilot]] and sets of [[Grenade|explosives]], as they and all of Barro's [[Barro's Imperial remnant|forces]] were destroyed with Barro attempting to escape in a [[INT-4 Interceptor]] but was shot down.<ref name="TMAG Trailer">{{SWYouTube|_pa1KLXuW0Y|The Mandalorian and Grogu &#124; Official Trailer &#124; In Theaters May 22, 2026}}</ref>
