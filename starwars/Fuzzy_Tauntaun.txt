'''Fuzzy Tauntauns''' were [[Color|yellow]], [[fruit]]y, [[alcohol]]ic [[cocktail]]s topped with [[Golden Lichen]] and [[buzzz]], a substance that caused [[Mouth|lip]] tingling when [[Diet|consumed]]. The drink was named after the [[tauntaun]] [[species]] from the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Hoth]] and was served at a very [[Temperature|cold]] temperature.

In [[34 ABY]], the beverage was served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet [[Batuu]]. The [[Weequay]] [[smuggler]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] recommended the cocktail to the [[mercenary]] [[Bazine Netal]], but she said she was not someone who would drink a Fuzzy Tauntaun. The young [[human]]s [[Julen Rakab]] and [[Volt Vescuso]] drank them after Rakab thwarted [[Ana Tolla]]'s [[Skirmish at Kat Saka's farm|attack]] on the [[Saka Homestead]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Yellow, fruity, and topped with foam, the Fuzzy Tauntaun is served cold as Hoth and lovingly named after the snow lizard creatures who live there.|Eloc Throno, ''Traveler's Guide to Batuu''|<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>}}
The Fuzzy Tauntaun was a [[Color|yellow]]-colored, [[alcohol]]ic [[cocktail]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> It was made using [[jogan fruit]]<ref name="EOTE" /> and a substance called [[buzzz]],<ref name="Must-Try Drinks" /> a foam that caused a tingling sensation in the drinker's [[Mouth|lips]] when it was [[Diet|consumed]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> The drink's dusting with [[gold]]-colored flakes of [[Golden Lichen]] made it one of the most expensive [[Foodstuff|drinks]] at [[Oga's Cantina]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> Named after [[tauntaun]]s, a [[snow lizard]] [[species]], Fuzzy Tauntauns were served at very [[Temperature|cold]] temperatures like the creatures' [[homeworld]],<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> the [[Water|ice]] planet [[Hoth]].<ref name="Tauntaun">{{Databank|tauntaun|Tauntaun}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|When something works, go with it. And speaking of something that works, are you sure you wouldn't like a Fuzzy Tauntaun?''"<br />"''Do I look like the kind of person who drinks Fuzzy Tauntauns?|Hondo Ohnaka fails to convince Bazine Netal to try a Fuzzy Tauntaun|<ref name="Pirate's Price" />}}
[[File:FuzzyTauntaun.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Fuzzy Tauntaun at Oga's Cantina]]
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TGDate">The events of [[Oga's Cantina]] within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], the framing story of ''[[Pirate's Price]]'', and the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' all take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Oga's Cantina in Batuu's [[Black Spire Outpost]] served the cocktails.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> The same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Weequay]] [[smuggler]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] personally recommended the drink to the [[human]] [[mercenary]] [[Bazine Netal]] multiple [[time]]s while she questioned him at Oga's Cantina during her search for the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', a [[YT-1300 light freighter]]. She declined each time, and, upon his last attempt, asked him if she looked like the kind of person who drank the beverage; Ohnaka agreed that Netal did not.<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> According to [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]], the former [[First Order stormtrooper]] [[Archex]] was another human who did not seem the type to drink the beverage.<ref name="GE:BS">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref>

Also in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Julen Rakab]] and [[Volt Vescuso]], a pair of humans, drank congratulatory Fuzzy Tauntauns at Oga's Cantina after Rakab successfully thwarted [[Ana Tolla]]'s [[Skirmish at Kat Saka's farm|attack]] on [[Saka Homestead]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> The same year,<ref name="Date">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> [[author]] and explorer [[Eloc Throno]] provided a description of the beverage in his [[book]] ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> During the same time,<ref name="EOTEDate">The events of ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge – Echoes of the Empire]]'' take place while the [[Resistance]] is on [[Batuu]], which corresponds to [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the proprietor [[Oga Garra]] became irate when a [[Unidentified human bartender (Oga Garra)|bartender]] informed her that Oga's Cantina had run out of jogan fruit. Garra threw numerous items at the bartender, shouting that Fuzzy Tauntauns could not be made without the ingredient.<ref name="EOTE" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:FuzzyTauntaun-Sims.png|thumb|left|200px|A Fuzzy Tauntaun in ''The Sims 4'']]
Fuzzy Tauntauns were first mentioned in the junior novel ''[[Pirate's Price]]'', which was written by [[Lou Anders]]<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> and published on [[January 8]], [[2019]].<ref name="Pirate's Price date">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-pirates-price|text=Star Wars: Pirate's Price}}</ref> On [[February 27]] of that year, [[Wikipedia:D23 (Disney)|D23: The Official Disney Fan Club]]<ref name="D23 Everything You Need to Know About SWGE">{{D23|url=galaxys-edge-announcements|text=Everything You Need to Know About ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> and [[StarWars.com]] announced that the beverage, which was first pictured on the latter website, would be sold at the [[In-universe|real-world]] bar Oga's Cantina in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="From Blue Milk to Ronto Wraps">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-food-beverages|text=From Blue Milk to Ronto Wraps: The Food and Beverages of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> a themed land that opened at [[Disneyland]] Park on [[May 31]] and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] on [[August 29]] of the same year.<ref name="Foodie Guide">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The menu's description reads, "[[Wikipedia:Cîroc Vodka|Cîroc Peach Vodka]], [[Wikipedia:Bols (brand)|Bols]] [[Wikipedia:Peach|Peach]] [[Wikipedia:Schnapps|Schnapps]], and [[Wikipedia:Simply Orange Juice Company|Simply Orange&reg;]] topped with [[Wikipedia:Tangerine|Tangerine]], [[Wikipedia:Sucrose#Types|Pure Cane Sugar]], 'Buzzz' Foam."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/dinner|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Dinner Menu}}</ref> The real-world "buzzz" is derived from [[Wikipedia:Acmella oleracea|buzz buttons]].<ref name="Sip and Savor">{{DPB|url=2019/08/sip-and-savor-intergalactic-food-beverage-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge-at-walt-disney-world-resort-opening-august-29|text=Sip and Savor Intergalactic Food & Beverage at ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge at Walt Disney World Resort, Opening August 29}}</ref>

The [[2020]] [[Canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] expansion ''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]'' includes Fuzzy Tauntauns as a drink that the player's [[Wikipedia:The Sims|Sims]] can purchase from the Oga's Cantina vendor in the Black Spire Outpost neighborhood. Its price of sixty [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] makes it the second most expensive item on the menu. Upon consuming the drink for the first time, the Sim automatically learns the recipe and can prepare it at mixology bars in their home neighborhood.<ref name="Sims">''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]''</ref>
