The '''Corvair sector''' was a [[sector]]<ref name="Age of Republic" /> in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Timelines" /> that contained the [[moon]] [[Kudo III]]<ref name="Age of Republic" /> and astronomic object of [[Corvair II]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Early into the [[Clone Wars]], [[Galactic Republic]] [[Grand Army of the Republic|forces]] made advancements into the Corvair sector<ref name="Anakin">{{EncyclopediaCite|Anakin Skywalker}}</ref> and [[Battle of Corvair|battled]] with [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[droid starfighter]]s in the sector, prompting [[Republic Intelligence]] to discover a [[Kudo droid foundry|droid foundry]] on Kudo III. However, the Separatists had allowed the Republic to discover the foundry, hoping that they would destroy it and [[Death|kill]] the [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Kudon]] who worked it.<ref name="Age of Republic" />
