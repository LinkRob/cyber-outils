The '''Five Stages of Combat''' was the name given to the codified [[starfighter]] techniques in [[Adar Tallon]]'s [[Treatise on Starfighter Tactics|''Treatise on Starfighter Tactics'']]. The first stage was detection, the second was closing, the third stage was attack, the fourth being maneuver, and the fifth was disengagement.<ref name="BMF 29">{{BuildFalconCite|29|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref>
