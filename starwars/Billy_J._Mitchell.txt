'''Billy J. Mitchell''' (c. [[1940s#1941|1941]] &mdash; c. [[March]] [[1999]])<ref name="Obituary" /> was an actor who portrayed [[Keir Santage]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="Aveleyman">{{WebCite|url=https://www.aveleyman.com/ActorCredit.aspx?ActorID=21876|text=Billy J. Mitchell|work=[https://www.aveleyman.com Aveleyman.com]|archivedate=20210120032525}}</ref>

Other notable roles include appearances in two Bond films: ''Never Say Never Again'' (alongside [[Max von Sydow]] and [[Andy Bradford]], the latter of whom also appeared in multiple Bond films) and ''GoldenEye'' (alongside [[Derek Lyons]]). He also appeared in ''Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade'', alongside [[Harrison Ford]]; Jones' father in the film was played by Sean Connery, who starred as Bond in ''Never Again''. His final appearance was in the 2005 film ''A Year and a Day''.

Mitchell had a reputation for playing North American characters in British productions, somewhat similar to [[Shane Rimmer]]. He never married.

Mitchell passed away in 1999.
