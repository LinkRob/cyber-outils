During the [[Mid Rim Retreat]], a '''battle''' took place between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] and a [[destroyer]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy|navy]] in a [[Unidentified star system (Metatessu sector)|lifeless star system]] in the [[Metatessu sector]] in [[3 ABY]]. The destroyer, following a [[hypermatter]] trail from the 61st's [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'', ambushed the Alliance [[battle group]] in the [[Star system|system]], but the rebel [[starship]]s were able to escape.

==Prelude==
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Mid Rim Retreat]], the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'', the [[troop carrier]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]], suffered damages to its [[hyperdrive]], leaving a trail of [[hypermatter]].<ref name="TC" />

==The battle==
{{Quote|We have engine problems, because my ship keeps being attacked. And my ship keeps being attacked because we have engine problems.|M2-M5, to Hazram Namir|<ref name="TC" />}}
Discovering the hypermatter trail, [[Imperial officer]]s serving under [[Prelate]] [[Verge]] and [[Captain]] [[Tabor Seitaron]] aboard<ref name="TC" /> the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> ''[[Herald]]'' coordinated with other [[Imperial Navy|Imperial forces]] and an Imperial [[destroyer]] ambushed the 61st while the [[battle group]] was carrying out maintenance on the ''Thunderstrike'' at the edge of a [[Unidentified star system (Metatessu sector)|lifeless star system]] in the [[Metatessu sector]]. As the destroyer moved into firing range, the ''Thunderstrike''{{'s}} escort, the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]] ''[[Apailana's Promise]]'', and its two [[X-wing starfighter]]s swiftly responded to protect the [[corvette]]. The 61st soon fled the [[Star system|system]], jumping into [[hyperspace]], but not before one of the X-wings was crippled by a glancing [[turbolaser]] blow.<ref name="TC" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|That trail was a stroke of fortune, but surely wars are not won through luck?|Verge reflects on tracking the 61st to Tabor Seitaron aboard the ''Herald''|<ref name="TC" />}}
The 61st was attacked two more times in the Metatessu [[sector]] by the Empire, soon discovering how the battle group's [[starship]]s were being tracked. In a following meeting, [[Lieutenant]] [[Sairgon]] reflected that the [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] had only managed to harry the 61st by that point.<ref name="TC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle in the Metatessu sector appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="TC" />
