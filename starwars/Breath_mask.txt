A '''breath mask''',<ref name="The Last Jedi: Bomber Command">''[[The Last Jedi: Bomber Command]]''</ref> also known as an '''oxygen mask''',<ref name="AND">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref> '''breathing mask''',<ref name="Aliens">{{SW|url=news/back-from-the-drawing-board-recycled-star-wars-aliens|text=Back from the Drawing Board: Recycled ''Star Wars'' Aliens|int=Back from the Drawing Board|nolive=1}}</ref> '''respirator''',<ref name="Sketchbook">''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]''</ref> or '''gas mask''',<ref name="Force Collector">''[[Force Collector]]''</ref> was a device that provided breathable air to whoever was wearing it. In some cases, it also acted as a [[life support]] system, especially if the wearers had extensive [[cybernetics]].

A typical [[oxygen]] mask was a small, portable device that allowed an oxygen-breather to survive in low-oxygen [[atmosphere]]s. Although not suitable for use in [[realspace]], these hands-free masks were essential equipment for deep-space travel that might require activity outside of a [[starship]].<ref name="DB" />

==History==
[[File:Benthic_trailer.png|thumb|left|200px|The Tognath Benthic wears a breath mask]]
[[Tognath]] who left their homeworld of [[Yar Togna]],<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> as [[Tognath diaspora|many were forced to]] in the wake of the [[conquest of Yar Togna]],<ref name="SoloGuide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> needed to wear a breath mask.<ref name="Dawn" /> [[Saw Gerrera]] wore a breath mask by the time of the [[destruction of Jedha City]]<ref name="RO" /> that seemed to be an oxygen mask<ref name="Rogue One Junior Novel">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> but was in fact a way for him to breathe in [[rhydonium]].<ref name="TonyGilroy">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-interview-tony-gilroy|text=Making a Revolutionary: Inside ''Andor'' Season 2 with Tony Gilroy}}</ref> Two of his followers were the Tognath [[Edrio]] and [[Benthic]], who both wore breath masks of their own. When remaining behind on [[Jedha]] to face the impending destruction from the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], Gerrera ripped his breath mask off of his [[Saw Gerrera's suit|armor]].<ref name="RO" />

On the [[Occupation of Skako Minor|occupied]] planet [[Skako Minor]], [[stormtrooper]]s wore a type of breath mask attached to their helmet.<ref name="Aphra 15">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 15|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 15]]</ref> The [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the [[Sith]] persona of the fallen [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], wore a [[Darth Vader's armor|suit of cybernetic armor]] that featured a breathing mask. Upon his return to the [[light side of the Force]], he requested the help of his son, [[Luke Skywalker]], in removing his mask, recognizing that he was already dying and that it was his last chance to look upon his son's face without the mask in the way.<ref name="Episode VI" />
