'''Amret Engell''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[general]] in the [[First Order military|armed forces]] of the [[First Order]] and was also a member of [[First Order High Command]]. After [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] was [[Death|killed]] during the [[Battle of Oetchi]], Engell took over Phasma's responsibilities and commanded a large portion of General [[Armitage Hux]]'s [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] training program.<ref name="TROS VD" />

Engell served on the [[First Order Supreme Council]], a cabinet of high-ranking [[First Order officer|officers]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> during the last days of the [[First Order-Resistance War]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> As new [[planet]]s fell under the control of the First Order, Engell saw it as an opportunity to obtain an abundance of [[Youngling|children]] that she could mold into stormtroopers. With the discovery of the [[Sith Eternal]] and its [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer]] [[fleet]], the [[Final Order]], Engell enthusiastically welcomed the addition of [[Sith trooper]]s to the First Order's war effort. Engell admired [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] for his initiative and unquestioned authority, and strove to create an [[army]] that was worthy of his approval.<ref name="TROS VD" />

In a meeting after Ren's [[search for Exegol]],<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> [[Bellava Parnadee]], a fellow Supreme Council member<ref name="TROS VD" /> and general, suggested to Engell that she would need to capture more youth from across the galaxy to fill the ranks of the Final Order fleet. Engell agreed with a nod,<ref name="TROS ExpandedEdition" /> but the fleet was already staffed by [[Darth Sidious]]' followers.<ref name="Episode IX" /> Engell was killed during the [[Battle of Exegol]]<ref name="The First Order" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="TROS date" /> After the battle, an [[uprising against the First Order]] occurred across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
Amret Engell was portrayed by [[Diana Kent]] in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> She was first identified as "Amret" in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
