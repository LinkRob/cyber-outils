'''Abelor survivalist pants''' were a type of durable [[Clothing|pants]] manufactured<ref name="Outlaws" /> on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Abelor]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> The pants were [[Color|tan]] with two black patches on each pocket and grey patches below the knees. A brown piece of material looped around the left leg and attached using two straps. A pair of brown [[boot]]s were included with the pants.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

The [[Twi'lek]] [[merchant]] [[Lalini Ledeno]] had at least one pair of Abelor survivalist pants while he operated from an alley in the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Ledeno used the password "I'm a friend of Arlo" to restrict access to his [[black market]] shop. He further restricted certain stock, including that survivalist pants, as well as offered discounts to members and allies of the criminal [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] learned the "I'm a friend of Arlo" password while eavesdropping on a conversation between two beings in [[Mirogana Market]]; which she later used to gain access to Ledeno's stock and purchase the pants for [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Abelor survivalist pants appeared in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', a [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When equipped by the player, the pants greatly reduce damage taken from explosions while crouched, rolling, or sliding.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Iuliia Misiul]] created concept art of the Abelor survivalist pants and other outfits used by Vess.<ref name="Art Station">{{ArtStation|username=Iuliia Misiul|url=/artwork/4N9Ve4|name=[[Iuliia Misiul]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws &mdash; Kay's Gearsets}}</ref>
