'''Catherine Anne "Cat" Taber''' is an American actress appearing in films, television and video games.

She has done voice work since 2003, participating in several ''[[Star Wars]]'' projects. Her main works included voicing [[Mission Vao]] in ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''<ref name="KotOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref>, [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]] and [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|series]],<ref name="Clone Wars Film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> and ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]'' (a role she reprised in [[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga|Lego ''Star Wars'': The Skywalker Saga]]<ref name="LEGO Skywalker Saga">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''</ref>), [[Vette]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''<ref name="The Old Republic">''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''</ref>, and [[Numa]] in ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. She also voiced a [[Unidentified First Order officer (Starkiller Base)|First Order officer]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>
