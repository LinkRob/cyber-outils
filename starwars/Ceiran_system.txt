The '''Ceiran system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]], situated in grid square N-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It contained [[Ceiran]], a location that the [[Gemstone|jewel]] [[thief]] [[Pax Maripher]] felt was unlike the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]<ref name="MA" /> by [[40 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Ceiran system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
