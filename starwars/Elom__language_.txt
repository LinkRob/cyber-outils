'''Elom''' was the native [[language]] of the [[Elom]] [[species]] from the [[planet]] [[Elom (planet)|Elom]]. Non-Eloms could understand the language, but typically have great difficulty speaking it.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

Most [[Elomin]] struggle to learn the language of their neighbors. The few Elomin who make the effort to learn the language are usually those who end up living with the Eloms after rescue from the mines or seek out Elom enclaves to learn more about them.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />
