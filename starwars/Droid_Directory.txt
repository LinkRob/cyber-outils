"'''Droid Directory'''" is a department within the ''[[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2]]'' magazine published by [[De Agostini]]. Each issue contains one or two articles within the department which cover in-universe details about a [[droid]] model or type. While most information is [[canon]], some articles contain specifically labeled sections of [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] information.<ref name="BuildR21">[[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 1|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 1]]</ref>

{{Magazine-stub|article}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Explore how R2-D2 fits into a ''Star Wars'' universe packed with droids built for every conceivable purpose, from providing medical care to waging war. Beyond their appearance in the movies, discover their functional features through blueprints, specification sheets and a comprehensive technical analysis.''<ref name="Model-space">{{WebCite|url=https://www.model-space.com/gb/r2-d2-full-kit.html|text=Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2|archivedate=20201203183846}}</ref>
