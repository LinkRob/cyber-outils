During the [[Galactic Civil War]] in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]'s [[New Republic]] forces aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'', who had continued their [[Hunt for Shadow Wing|hunt]] for the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], managed to track the [[fighter wing]] and the [[bulk freighter]] ''[[Yadeez]]'', after the 204th had stopped in the [[Ghonoath system]] to permit one of their [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvettes]] to refuel. The ''Yadeez'' and its escorts dropped into the atmosphere of a radioactive deathworld to slow down the New Republic forces and buy time for the ''Raider''. TIE squadrons were launched and a '''battle''' ensued with the New Republic starfighter squadrons. [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bombers]] dropped [[proton bomb]]s without detonator timers to drift like mines in the planet's fierce winds, which managed to destroy two [[X-wing starfighter]]s. [[Squadron Four (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Four]] TIE pilot [[Garl Lykan|Garl "Snapper" Lykan]], known by [[Alphabet Squadron]] for his [[Needle (maneuver)|Needle]] maneuver, was killed by a trio of X-wings that converged on him. The 204th's commanding officer, [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]] found a way out, and so after the TIEs boarded the ''Yadeez'' and exited the planet's atmosphere, they jumped to [[lightspeed]], along with the refueling ''Raider'' and other escorts. The 204th held a funeral for Lykan shortly after.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
