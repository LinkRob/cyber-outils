An open-[[atmosphere|air]] '''hangar''' was located in a back room inside [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on [[Mos Eisley]], [[Tatooine]]. The [[Wookiee]] [[Chalmun]], owner of the Cantina, used the hangar to store his [[utility crane ship]] which he used to pick up [[foodstuff|food]] and drink deliveries from [[orbit]]. The craft was tended to by a [[technician|mechanic]] and kept ready in case Chalmun needed to quickly [[escape pod|escape]] from the Cantina.<ref name="Complete">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
