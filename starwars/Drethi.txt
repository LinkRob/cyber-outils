'''Drethi''' were a type of creature that had [[Limb|wings]]. During the [[Dedication of the Starlight Beacon|dedication]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]]<ref name="TRS" /> in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]] met [[Jedi archivist]] [[OrbaLin]] for the first [[time]] and was left with the impression that the [[archivist]] was capable of boring the wings off a Drethi. Shortly before the start of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]'s [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]]<ref name="TRS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> OrbaLin traveled to the world to attend the [[Republic Fair|festivities]]. As Mann watched his colleague arrive, he remembered his impression of the archivist's capabilities.<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Drethi were mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Drethi/Legends|Drethi]] were introduced as [[Limb/Legends|winged]], [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]]-[[Death/Legends|killing]] beasts in "[[Jedi's Legacy]]," a pick-your-own-path adventure written by [[S. John Ross]] that was included in the [[Star Wars Gamer 10|tenth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Gamer]]'' magazine. The issue was published by [[Wizards of the Coast]]<ref name="Gamer 10">{{GamerCite|10|Jedi's Legacy}}</ref> on [[May 7]], [[2002]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SWArchive|url=gaming/other/rpg/news20020416.html|text=Episode II in Gamer #10}}</ref>
